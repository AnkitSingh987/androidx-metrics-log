Category: Beginner
Title: The Wolf and Fox and the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a wolf and a fox who were very good friends. Every night they would go out together and do something fun. One night, the wolf said, "Let's go out and play!" So they did. They were running around in the forest when they saw the moon. The fox said, "Look, the moon is so big and round!" The wolf replied, "Yes, and it looks so beautiful!" But then, something bad happened. A cloud moved in front of the moon and part of it was hidden. The wolf and the fox were sad that they couldn't see the moon in all of its beauty. The fox said, "It's so bad that this happened, it looks so sad when only part of the moon is hidden. We should always remember to appreciate the beauty of the moon and what we have." The fox and the wolf nodded and continued to marvel at the moon. They understood the importance of being thankful for the things that make us happy.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit who lived in a forest. He was a very charming rabbit and every day he ventured out in search of carrots to eat. One day, the rabbit noticed a deep hole in the ground and went to take a closer look. He peered into the dark depths, and suddenly, he heard a noise from below. It was a frightened kitten! The rabbit quickly jumped down and said, “Don’t worry little kitty, I’ll rescue you.” The rabbit grabbed the kitten and took it out with him. The rabbit fed the kitten a big carrot, and the kitty was very happy. The rabbit stroked her fur and said, “You’re a very charming kitten and I’m sure you’ll be happy here in the forest.” The kitty stayed with the rabbit in the forest and they were so very happy together. The rabbit liked to give the kitten carrots each day, and they spent many happy years living in the forest.

Jill and Ben are friends. They like to play in the yard. They have a ball, a bike and a doll. They are happy. One day, a big dog comes to the yard. It is not their dog. It is a stray dog. It has black fur and sharp teeth. It barks loud. Jill and Ben are scared. "Go away, dog!" Ben shouts. He throws the ball at the dog. The dog does not go away. It runs to the ball and bites it. The ball pops. The dog growls. "Help, help!" Jill cries. She holds the doll tight. She runs to the bike. She tries to get on the bike. The dog chases her. It jumps on the bike. The bike falls. Jill falls. The dog sniffs the doll. The doll squeaks. "Leave her alone, dog!" Ben yells. He picks up a stick. He runs to the dog. He hits the dog with the stick. The dog yelps. It runs away. Ben helps Jill up. He hugs her. "Are you okay, Jill?" Ben asks. "I'm okay, Ben. Thank you," Jill says. She smiles. She hugs him back. They look at the doll. The doll has a hole in her dress. The dog bit her. But she is still cute. Jill and Ben love her. They go inside the house. They tell their mom what happened. Their mom is proud of them. She gives them cookies and milk. She mends the doll's dress. She says they are brave. Jill and Ben are happy. They are friends. They have a ball, a bike and a doll. They are not scared of the dog. They hope the dog finds a home.

One day, a big dog named Max was ready to play. He saw a nice carpet in the living room. Max thought it was a good place to play with his toy ball. He ran to the carpet and started to play. As Max played, he saw a little bug pass by on the carpet. Max stopped playing with his ball and looked at the bug. The bug was very small and moved fast. Max wanted to be friends with the bug, so he followed it. The bug went under the carpet and Max tried to lift it up. But, when he lifted the carpet, he found a big hole under it! Max was very surprised. He looked into the hole and saw a secret room with more toys. Max and the bug went into the secret room and played together all day. The unexpected secret room made Max and the bug very happy.
