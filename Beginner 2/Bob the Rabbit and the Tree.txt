Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Rabbit and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bob. Bob was a careless rabbit. He liked to rush all the time. One day, he rushed to the big tree to see his friend, Tom the turtle. As Bob rushed, he tripped on a rock and fell down. "Ouch!" he cried. Tom heard Bob and came to help him. "Bob, you need to be careful," Tom said. "You always rush and get hurt." Bob looked at his friend and said, "You're right, Tom. I need to slow down and not be so careless." From that day on, Bob learned to take his time and be more careful. And he and Tom played happily together under the big tree.

One day, a girl named Lily had very long hair. She was very good at remembering things. Lily had a friend named Tim. Tim had short hair. They liked to play together. One day, Tim said, "I have a secret! I will tell you tomorrow." Lily was excited and said, "I will remember!" The next day, Lily saw Tim, but he looked different. He had long hair now! Lily was surprised and asked, "How did your hair grow so fast?" Tim laughed and said, "I was playing a trick on you! I put on a wig to look like I have long hair." Lily laughed too and said, "That was a good trick, Tim! I didn't see that coming. But I remembered your secret!" They both laughed and played happily together, with their long hair.

---

Lily and Max were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and make noise. They saw some flowers and bugs and rocks. They were having fun. But then the sky became dark and grey. Lily and Max heard a loud sound. It was thunder. They saw a flash of light. It was lightning. They felt some drops of water. It was rain. "Let's go inside!" Lily said. "The rain is wet and cold!" "Wait!" Max said. "I want to reach the rain!" Max stretched his arms up to the sky. He tried to catch the rain with his hands. He felt the rain on his fingers and palms. He smiled. "Why do you want to reach the rain?" Lily asked. "The rain is ordinary. It comes and goes." "I like the rain," Max said. "The rain is special. It makes the flowers and bugs and rocks shiny. It makes the garden smell good. It makes me happy." Lily looked at Max. She saw his smile. She saw the rain on his face and hair. She thought maybe Max was right. Maybe the rain was special. "Can I reach the rain with you?" Lily asked. "Sure!" Max said. "Come on!" Lily and Max reached the rain together. They laughed and splashed and danced. They did not mind the wet and cold. They were having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tim. Tim was unusual because he could polish things very well. He had a big, red cape and a shiny hat. One day, Tim met a girl named Sally. Sally said, "Hi, Tim! Can you help me? My shoes are dirty." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I can polish your shoes!" So, Tim polished Sally's shoes, and they became very shiny. Sally was so happy, she said, "Thank you, Tim! You are my hero!" Tim smiled and flew away to help more friends. And everyone in the town loved their unusual hero, Tim, who could polish things so well.

One day, a little girl named Lily went with her mom to get the mail. They walked down the street, hand in hand. Lily was a bit sleepy, but she wanted to help her mom. When they got to the mailbox, Lily's mom let her hold the mail. As they walked back, a small dog ran up to them. "Woof, woof!" said the dog. Lily and her mom were surprised. The dog looked at the mail in Lily's hand and jumped up to grab it. "No, no!" said Lily, trying to hold the mail up high. But the dog was too fast. It took the mail from Lily and ran away. Lily and her mom chased the dog, but it was too quick. Just when they thought the mail was gone for good, the dog came back. It dropped the mail at their feet and wagged its tail. It turns out, the dog just wanted to play! So, they all played together and then went home, happy and tired.
