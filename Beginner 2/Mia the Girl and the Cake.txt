Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to bake with her mom. One day, Mia and her mom baked a big, yummy cake. Mia was very happy. While they were baking, the telephone rang. Mia's mom answered it. "Hello," she said. A mysterious voice talked to her. Mia's mom looked sad. She told Mia, "I have to go out for a while." Mia was alone in the house. She wanted to finish the cake. So, she put the cake in the oven. But Mia did not know how long to bake it. The cake burned and turned black. Mia was very sad and cried. The cake was ruined and her mom was not there to help.

Once there was a boy who was restless. He did not like to go to school. He wanted to stay home and play with his toys. The boy did not like his teachers or his classmates. He thought school was so boring. He wished it would be over quickly so he could go home. One day, the boy became so angry that he started hating school. He screamed and shouted and refused to go back again. His parents said that he had to go. He said he would not, no matter what. He was so determined and he really hated school. But in the end, he had to go back. He was so unhappy and he continued to hate school. But nothing changed, and he has to go every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky vest that he loved to wear. He felt happy when he put it on. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore his lucky vest. At the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to observe the tree closely. So, he walked to the tree and looked at it. The tree had many leaves and branches. Tim saw a bird on the tree. The bird was singing a beautiful song. Tim felt lucky to observe the bird. He smiled and danced to the bird's song. Tim had a great day at the park with his lucky vest.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide made of metal. Anna wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, it is fun!" Anna says. "I recommend you try it." Ben shakes his head. "No, it is too high and too fast. And the metal is hot and hard. I do not like it." Anna thinks for a moment. She has an idea. She runs to her backpack and takes out a soft blanket. She brings it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I have a soft blanket. We can use it to slide. It will make the metal feel cooler and softer. And we can hold hands, so we are not afraid. Do you want to try it?" Ben looks at the blanket and at Anna. He smiles a little. He nods. "Okay, Anna, I will try it. But only with you and the blanket." Anna and Ben put the blanket on the slide. They hold hands and sit on the blanket. They slide down together. They laugh and scream. They have fun. They do it again and again. They forget to be scared. They enjoy the slide. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They wanted to have a fun day. Tim saw a popcorn stand and asked his mom, "Can we get popcorn, please?" His mom said yes, and they got a big bag of popcorn to share. As they walked, Tim saw a girl named Sue. She looked sad. Tim wanted to be honest and a good friend, so he asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom looked for the toy. They looked by the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Then, Tim realized the toy was under the bench. He picked it up and gave it to Sue. She was so happy! They all sat down and ate popcorn together. The conflict was over, and they had a great day at the park.
