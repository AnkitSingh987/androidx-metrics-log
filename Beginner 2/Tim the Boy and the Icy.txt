Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Icy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. One day, Tim saw another toy car on the floor. It was icy blue and shiny. Tim wanted the icy blue car, too. Tim picked up the icy blue car and said, "This car belongs to me now." Tim's friend, Sam, heard this and ran over. Sam said, "No, Tim! That car belongs to me!" Tim and Sam both wanted the icy blue car, but there was only one. They didn't know what to do. Then, Tim's mom came to the exit of the room. She saw Tim and Sam arguing. She said, "Boys, why don't you take turns playing with the icy blue car?" Tim and Sam thought this was a good idea. They took turns and played happily together. And they both learned that sharing was the best way to have fun.

Once there was a boy who wanted to build something. He had an arm and he started to plan. He wanted to build a castle with the arm, so he was very busy. He went to get the pieces he needed. He found shiny stones and colorful balls. He also found some blocks and a few sticks. He put all of the pieces together and got to work. He was busy making the castle. He used the arm to place the blocks carefully. The stick was used for the windows and the stones were the turrets. He was so excited when he was finished. The boy was very proud of his plan. He showed all of his friends the castle he built with his arm. Everyone was so amazed! They thought it was the best castle ever.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to work. He liked to sleep all day. One day, Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "Tom, I found some money! Let's go and buy toys!" Tom liked toys, so he got up and went with Max. They went to the store and saw a toy they both wanted. It was a toy car. But it was high up on a shelf. Tom had an idea. He said, "Max, let's pull the shelf down together." Max and Tom pulled and pulled. The shelf came down, and they got the toy car. They were so happy. They played with the toy car all day, and Tom learned that it was fun to work with a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read her big book of stories. Every day, she would sit under a big tree and read her book. One day, while she was reading, she saw a tiny grape on the ground. It was a perfect grape, round and purple. Lily picked up the grape and looked at it. She thought it was the most perfect grape she had ever seen. Lily took the grape home and showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "What a perfect grape you found, Lily!" They put the grape in the fruit bowl and Lily went back to read her big book of stories. And every time Lily saw the perfect grape, she would remember the fun day she found it under the big tree.

One day, a bright sun came up and woke a little girl named Penny. Penny was very happy. She wanted to play outside with her friends. She put on her shoes and ran to the park. At the park, Penny saw her friend Tom. They played with a ball and had fun. Then, they saw a big ice cream truck. Penny and Tom wanted ice cream, but they had no money. They were sad. Penny had an idea. She took Tom's ball and said, "Let's trade the ball for ice cream." Tom was not sure, but Penny said it would be fun. They gave the ball to the ice cream man and got their ice cream. But then, the ice cream fell on the ground. Penny and Tom were very sad. They had no ball and no ice cream. The moral of the story is to not trade what you love for something else.
