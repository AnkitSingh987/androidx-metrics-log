Title: The Sun and Moon Were and the Stars

One day, a sun and a moon were in the sky. The sun was big and bright. It would shine all day. The moon was small and soft. It would shine at night. They both lived in a network of stars. The stars were their friends. The sun and the moon were not happy. They both wanted to shine at the same time. The sun said, "I want to shine when you shine!" The moon said, "No, I want to shine when you shine!" They were wild and loud. All the stars heard them. One star came to help. The star said, "You can both shine at the same time, but not every day. You can take turns." The sun and the moon thought about it. They agreed to take turns. Some days, the sun would shine with the moon. Other days, they would shine alone. They were happy and the stars in their network were happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a great big tree. It lived in a park with many other trees. The great tree was very tall and had many green leaves. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was cold and hungry. The great tree wanted to care for the bird. So, the tree let the bird sit on its branch. The bird was warm and happy. The bird told all its friends about the great tree. Soon, many birds came to the tree. They all lived together and cared for each other. The great tree and the birds were very happy.

One day, a light bug went for a walk. The bug liked to see the trees, the flowers, and the sun. The bug was very happy. The bug saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The bug went up, up, up the tree. But then, the bug started to fall down, down, down. The bug was scared. But, something funny happened. The bug did not hit the ground! The bug turned into a pretty butterfly. The butterfly flew up, up, up and was happy again.

Mummy and little John were in the bathroom. Mummy pointed to the bottle on the shelf and said, "Can you tell me what this is?" Little John looked up at Mummy. She smiled down at him and said, "It's called shampoo." Little John nodded his head and asked, "What does it do?" Mummy explained, "It makes your hair clean and soft. It's like a special kind of soap for your hair, but it's different." she said. Little John smiled. He had young, soft hair, and he liked how it felt when his mummy put the shampoo on it. "Let's try it!", said Mummy. She got out a towel, put some shampoo in her hands and started to rub it into Little John's hair. Little John giggled as it tickled his scalp. "It feels nice!", said Little John. Mummy rinsed the shampoo out of Little John's hair, gave him a big hug and said, "There! All clean and soft now!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy box full of fun things to play with. One day, he wanted to see if all his toys could fit in the box. Tim had a neighbor named Sue. She was a very persistent girl. She always tried to help Tim with his toys. Sue came over to Tim's house to play. Together, they tried to fit all the toys in the box. They put the big toys on the bottom and the small toys on the top. It was hard work, but they did not give up. They were both very happy when all the toys fit in the box. Tim and Sue had a lot of fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable bird named Bobo. Bobo had a special feather that helped him whistle. He loved to whistle all day long. One day, Bobo met a little girl named Mia. Mia said, "Hi Bobo, I like your whistle!" Bobo was happy to meet Mia and they became friends. They played together every day. One day, Bobo lost his special feather. He was sad because he couldn't whistle anymore. Mia saw Bobo was sad and said, "Don't worry, we will find your feather!" They looked and looked, but they couldn't find it. Then, they met a nice dog named Ruff. Ruff had found the special feather! He gave it back to Bobo. Bobo was so happy, he started to whistle again. Mia, Bobo, and Ruff played together and they all lived happily ever after.
