Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shield

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy shield. He would run around the yard and pretend to be a brave knight. One day, Tim met a big dog. The dog was upset and did not want to play. Tim was scared, but he held up his shield to protect himself. The dog saw the shield and stopped barking. Tim thought of an idea. He bowed to the dog like a knight. The dog bowed back and was not upset anymore. They became friends and played together all day. And that is how Tim and the big dog became best friends.

Lily and Tom liked to sail their boats in the pond. They made their boats from paper and sticks. They gave their boats names. Lily's boat was Rose and Tom's boat was Blue. One day, they saw a large duck in the pond. The duck was swimming near their boats. Lily and Tom were happy. They wanted to make friends with the duck. "Hello, duck!" Lily said. "Do you like our boats?" "Quack, quack!" the duck said. It looked at the boats with its black eyes. "Can we play with you?" Tom asked. "We can share our boats with you." The duck nodded. It swam closer to the boats. Lily and Tom smiled. They thought the duck was nice. But the duck was not nice. It was hungry. It opened its big mouth and bit Rose. Lily screamed. She saw her boat torn in half. The duck swallowed it. "Hey, stop that!" Tom shouted. He tried to save Blue. But the duck was faster. It snapped at Blue and broke it too. The duck ate it. Lily and Tom were sad and angry. They cried. They had no boats. They had no friend. They had bad news. The duck quacked. It was full. It swam away. It did not care.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the garden. One day, they saw a big bush with many blueberries on it. They ran to the bush and picked some blueberries to eat. "These are so sweet and juicy!" Lily said. "I love blueberries!" "Me too!" Ben said. "But we should not eat too many. We should share some with Mom and Dad." They put some blueberries in a basket and carried it to the house. On the way, they saw a swing hanging from a tree. They decided to have some fun and swing on it. "Look at me!" Lily said. "I can swing so high!" "Me too!" Ben said. "I can touch the sky!" They laughed and swung for a while. Then they heard Mom calling them. "Lily! Ben! Come inside! It's time for lunch!" They jumped off the swing and ran to the house. They gave Mom the basket of blueberries. "Wow! What a nice surprise!" Mom said. "You are so kind and generous! These blueberries look so rich and tasty!" She hugged them and kissed them. Then she made them some sandwiches and milk. They ate their lunch and enjoyed some blueberries for dessert. They were happy and full. They thanked Mom and said they loved her. They loved blueberries, they loved the swing, and they loved each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big horse there. The horse was all alone. Tim was very happy to see the horse. He wanted to play with it. "Hello, horse! Do you want to play?" Tim asked. The horse nodded its head. Tim was grateful that the horse wanted to play with him. They played together all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The horse started to talk! "Thank you, Tim, for playing with me," said the horse. Tim was very surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend. They played even more and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. The town had many people and animals. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. In the distant sky, there was a big storm coming. The people in the town did not know about the storm. The storm had a big tornado. The tornado was getting closer to the town. The people were scared. They wanted to find a safe place to hide. They asked their friends to provide help. But the tornado was too strong. The people could not find a safe place to hide. The tornado reached the town and destroyed many houses. The people were very sad. The town was not the same after the storm.
