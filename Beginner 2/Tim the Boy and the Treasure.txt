Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Treasure

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an ancient map that his grandpa gave him. The map was very old and special. Tim liked to look at the map and dream about going on adventures. One sunny day, Tim and his best friend, Sally, went to the park to play. Tim took his ancient map with him. They pretended to be explorers, looking for treasure. "Look, Sally! The map says the treasure is by the big tree!" Tim said. But then, Tim lost his map. He started to panic. "Oh no, Sally! I can't find my map!" he cried. Sally saw Tim was sad and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find it together." They looked and looked, and finally found the map under a swing. Tim was so happy, and they continued their adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Ann. Ann liked to play with dough. She made all kinds of shapes with the dough. One day, she made a gray cat out of dough. She loved her gray cat very much. One day, Ann's little brother, Tim, came into her room. He saw the gray cat made of dough and thought it was a toy. He played with it and broke it by accident. Ann was very sad when she saw her broken gray cat. Tim felt bad for breaking Ann's gray cat. He said, "I am sorry, Ann. I didn't know it was your dough cat." Ann was still sad, but she knew Tim didn't mean to break it. She said, "I forgive you, Tim." Then, they both played with the dough and made a new gray cat together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a small box. She was very patient and tried to open it. Inside the box, she found an oat. The oat was very tiny. Lucy wanted to share the oat with her friend, Tim. So, she went to Tim's house and showed him the oat. Tim said, "Wow! Can we fit it in my toy car?" They tried, and the oat fit perfectly in the toy car. Together, they played with the toy car and the oat. They had a lot of fun and laughed. They were very happy that the oat fit in the car. And they learned that being patient and sharing can bring lots of joy.

One day, Max was walking around the park. He was looking at the trees and the flowers. Suddenly he saw a picture on a bench. Max went to the bench and looked at the picture. He was very curious. He asked, "What is this picture?" A busy bee flew by and answered, "It's a beautiful flower." Max smiled. He thanked the bee and continued walking. Max loved looking at the pictures in the park. He answered that the purple flower was his favorite. Then he said goodbye to the bee and ran off. Max spent the rest of the afternoon busy looking at different pictures in the park. By the time he was ready to go home, Max had seen many beautiful pictures. He was happy and content.

Ben and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a pistol in Tom's dad's room. It is shiny and black. They think it is a toy. "Let's play with the pistol," Tom says. "We can pretend to be cowboys." "OK," Ben says. "But be careful. Don't point it at me." They go to the backyard. They run and hide behind the trees. They make noises like guns. "Bang, bang, you're dead," Tom says. Ben laughs. He runs to Tom and tries to grab the pistol. "No, it's mine," Tom says. He pulls the pistol away from Ben. He points it at Ben's chest. "Bang, bang, you're dead for real," he says. He pulls the trigger. The pistol makes a loud noise. Ben falls to the ground. He does not move. He does not laugh. He does not breathe. He has a hole in his chest. Blood comes out. Tom drops the pistol. He screams. He runs to Ben. He shakes him. He cries. He is scared. He does not know what to do. He thinks it was a toy. He thinks it was easy. He thinks it was fun. He is wrong. He is very wrong.
