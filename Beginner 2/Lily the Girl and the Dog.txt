Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily and her dog, Max. They were best friends. Lily was very intelligent. She loved to play with Max all day long. One day, they found a box with a shiny pin inside. Lily thought the pin was very pretty. Lily and Max wanted to play with the pin, but they could not agree on who should get it. They both wanted it very much. This made them feel sad and they started to argue. Max tried to take the pin from Lily, but Lily did not want to give it to him. Lily had an idea. She suggested that they should take turns playing with the pin. Max agreed and they both felt happy again. They played with the pin together and had lots of fun. In the end, Lily and Max learned that sharing was the best way to solve their problem.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly ghost named Boo. Boo lived in a big, old house. He liked to celebrate and play with his friends. One day, Boo was very busy. He was getting ready for a big party. He invited all his friends to come and celebrate with him. He put up balloons and made yummy treats for everyone. Boo's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to the party. She saw Boo and said, "Wow, you are so busy today, Boo!" Boo smiled and said, "Yes, I want to make this party fun for everyone!" They all played games, ate treats, and had a great time. Boo was happy that he could celebrate with his friends, even though he was a ghost. And everyone loved the busy, friendly ghost named Boo.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a wet day, but Tim didn't care. He liked to play in the water. He saw a big jet flying in the sky. Tim looked up and said, "Wow, I admire that jet!" His mom said, "Yes, it's a very big jet. It can go very fast in the sky." Tim wanted to be like the jet. He wanted to go fast and fly high. Tim ran around the park, pretending to be a jet. He went "zoom, zoom" as he ran. He got wet from the water on the ground, but he didn't mind. He was having fun being a jet. His mom watched and smiled, admiring her little boy.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big raft. He was very happy. Tom was a creative boy. He wanted to sit on the raft and play on the water. He pushed the raft into the water and sat on it. Tom saw a big fish in the water. The fish was nice and wanted to play too. The fish jumped high and splashed water on Tom. Tom laughed and splashed water back at the fish. They played for a long time. Then, something unexpected happened. The big fish opened its mouth and said, "Hi, Tom!" Tom was very surprised. The fish could talk! The fish and Tom became good friends. They played and talked every day.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. There is a big slide in the park. Sara and Ben want to go on the slide. "Let's go on the slide!" Sara says. "OK!" Ben says. They run to the slide. Sara goes first. She slides down fast. She is happy. She laughs. "Your turn!" Sara says to Ben. Ben climbs up the slide. He is scared. The slide is high. He sees a mark on the slide. The mark is red. It looks like blood. "Who made this mark?" Ben asks. "I don't know," Sara says. "Maybe someone got hurt." Ben does not want to slide. He wants to go back. But he is too high. He cannot go back. "Come on, Ben!" Sara says. "It's fun!" Ben tries to slide. But he slides too fast. He loses his balance. He falls off the slide. He lands on the ground. He cries. "Ben!" Sara says. She runs to him. She sees his arm. His arm is broken. She is sorry. She hugs him. "I'm sorry, Ben," Sara says. "I didn't know the slide was broken." "It's OK, Sara," Ben says. "It's not your fault." They wait for help. They are still friends. But they do not like the slide anymore.
