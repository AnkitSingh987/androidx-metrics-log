Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Dog

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big, hairy dog. The dog was barking and running. Lily and Ben liked dogs, but this dog was scary. "Let's go away from the dog," Lily said to Ben. "Maybe he is angry or sick." But Ben wanted to pet the dog. He said, "No, he is just happy. He wants to play with us. Look, he has a toy in his mouth." Ben ran towards the dog. The dog ran towards Ben. Lily saw that the toy was not a toy. It was a gun. Lily did not understand what a gun was, but she knew it was bad. She heard her dad say that guns can hurt people. "Ben, stop!" Lily shouted. "That is not a toy! That is a gun! Come back!" But Ben did not hear Lily. He reached for the gun. The dog growled and bit Ben's hand. Ben screamed and dropped the gun. The dog picked up the gun and ran away. Lily ran to Ben. She saw blood on his hand. She was scared and sad. She hugged Ben and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I tried to tell you. The dog was not nice. He had a bad thing. Are you okay?" Ben cried and said, "It hurts, Lily. It hurts a lot. Why did the dog do that? Why did he have a gun?" Lily did not know. She did not understand. She wished the dog was gone. She wished Ben was not hurt. She wished they had a nice dog to play with. A dog that had a ball, not a gun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big bench near the trees. They sat down on the bench to eat their lunch. Tim had a yummy sandwich and his mom had a yummy salad. Tim saw a little bug on the bench. He asked his mom, "What will this bug become?" His mom said, "It will become a pretty butterfly one day." Tim was happy and wanted to see the bug become a butterfly. Suddenly, the bug started to change. But it did not become a pretty butterfly. It became a big, funny frog! The frog jumped off the bench and said, "Thank you, Tim! I was a frog all along, but I was stuck as a bug. You helped me become a frog again!" Tim and his mom were very surprised, but they laughed and clapped. They had a fun day at the park.

One day, a vendor was selling toys in the park. He had a big seat where he sat all day. Many kids came to buy toys from him. They liked his toys very much. A little girl named Lily wanted a toy too. She asked her mom to buy one for her. Her mom said yes and took her to the vendor. Lily was so happy that she jumped and clapped her hands. But Lily was not careful. She fell down and hurt her leg. It was not too bad, but she cried. The vendor saw her and felt sad. He gave her a toy and let her sit on his big seat. Lily stopped crying and smiled. She thanked the vendor and played with her new toy on the seat.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl named Lily. She loved to collect pretty things. One sunny day, Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw many pretty flowers and bright green leaves. She wanted to collect them all! Lily saw a small cell on the ground. She picked it up and put it in her bag. It was a perfect place to keep her collection. She walked around the park and picked up many pretty things. She put them all in her cell. She was very happy. After a while, Lily's cell was full of pretty things. She decided to go home and show her mom. Her mom loved the cute collection Lily had made. They put the cell on the table and looked at it together. They were so happy to have a beautiful collection of pretty things.
