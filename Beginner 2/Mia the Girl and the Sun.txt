Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was a happy girl who loved to play outside. One day, the sun was very hot, and the heat made Mia feel crazy. She wanted to find something fun to do inside her house. Mia went to her room and saw all her toys. She had to choose which toy to play with. There were dolls, cars, and blocks. Mia thought hard and finally chose her blocks. She was excited to build a big tower with her blocks. Mia played with her blocks for a long time. She built a tall tower and then knocked it down. She laughed and clapped her hands. The crazy heat outside didn't bother her anymore. Mia was happy to play with her blocks inside her cool house.

One day, a little dog named Max saw a smooth, big rock. Max wanted to find a way to get on top of the rock. He tried to jump, but it was too high. Max was sad. Max's friend, a bird named Lily, saw Max and asked, "What's wrong, Max?" Max said, "I want to go on the big rock, but I can't." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. Wait here." Lily flew away and came back with a long stick. She put the stick on the rock, making a ramp. Max was happy and ran up the stick. Now, Max could remain on the rock and enjoy the view. Max and Lily played on the rock all day.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play with cars. Ben has a red car and Lily has a blue car. They want to have a race with their cars. "Can I borrow your red car?" Lily asks Ben. "It is faster than my blue car." "OK, you can borrow my red car," Ben says. "But you have to give it back after the race." They go to the park and find a long path. They put their cars on the ground and get ready to race. "Ready, set, go!" they shout and push their cars. The red car and the blue car zoom along the path. They are very close. Lily and Ben run after them. "Come on, red car!" Lily cheers. "Go, go, go, blue car!" Ben yells. The cars reach the end of the path. The red car is a little bit ahead of the blue car. "I win, I win!" Lily says. "The red car is the best!" "Good job, Lily," Ben says. "You are very lucky. But the blue car is also good. It was a close race." They hug and smile. They are happy. "Thank you for letting me borrow your red car, Ben," Lily says. "It was fun to race with you." "You're welcome, Lily," Ben says. "You can borrow my red car anytime. But now I want it back. It is my favorite car." They swap their cars and play some more. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth, round circle. The circle had many friends. They liked to play together. One day, the circle and its friends wanted to play a game. The game was called "hurry and find me." The circle said, "I will count, and you all hide. Then I will hurry to find you." The circle closed its eyes and began to count. All the friends ran to find a place to hide. When the circle finished counting, it opened its eyes and said, "Ready or not, here I come!" The circle hurried to find its friends. It looked behind a tree, under a rock, and in a bush. One by one, the circle found all its friends. They all laughed and clapped. They were happy to play "hurry and find me" together.

Once upon a time, in a small room, there was a big bookshelf. The bookshelf was full of colorful books. A little boy named Tim lived in the house. He loved to read the books on the bookshelf. Tim was a helpful boy. One day, Tim saw a big bug on the bookshelf. The bug wanted to eat the books. Tim did not want the bug to eat the books. He tried to resist the bug. Tim wanted to save the books from the big bug. Tim thought of a plan. He got a jar and put it near the bug. The bug went inside the jar. Tim closed the jar and took the bug outside. He let the bug go far away from the bookshelf. Now, the books were safe. Tim was happy and felt helpful.
