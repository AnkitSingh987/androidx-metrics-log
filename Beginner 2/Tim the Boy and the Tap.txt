Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tap

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a broken toy car. Tim wanted to fix it, so he went to his dad for help. His dad said, "Tim, let's try and gain a new part to fix your car." They went to the store to find a new part. On their way, they saw a tap. The tap was leaking water. Tim said, "Dad, the tap is broken too!" His dad replied, "Yes, Tim. We can fix the tap after we fix your car." Tim and his dad found the part for the car at the store. They went back home and fixed the broken car. Then, they went to the tap and fixed it too. Now, both the car and the tap were not broken anymore. Tim was happy, and he played with his car all day.

One day, a playful cat named Tom saw a big cake on the table. He wanted to eat it. He jumped on the table and took a bite. The cake was very yummy. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came in and saw Tom eating the cake. Max said, "Can I have some cake too?" Tom replied, "Yes, you can have some. It is very good." Max took a bite and they both enjoyed the cake. Suddenly, the cake started to move! Tom and Max were scared. The cake was not a cake at all; it was a big hat! A little mouse was hiding under the hat. The mouse said, "Hi, I am Sam. I was hiding from you two." Tom and Max laughed and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Bobo. He loved to play with his ball and run around the park. One day, while playing, he saw a big red truck. The truck had a sign that said "fuel." Bobo was curious and wanted to see what the truck was doing. Bobo went up to the truck and saw a man. The man was setting up a big machine. Bobo said, "Hi! What are you doing?" The man replied, "Hello, little dog. I am setting up this machine to put fuel in the truck." Bobo was excited and wanted to help the man. As they were working, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind blew the fuel hose away from the truck. The hose started to spray fuel everywhere! Bobo quickly grabbed the hose with his mouth and put it back into the truck. The man was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are such an intelligent dog!" And from that day on, Bobo and the man were good friends.

One day, a happy boy named Tim set out to play. He had a big red ball. Tim liked to play with the ball in the park. He would throw it high and catch it with his hands. Today, the sun was bright and the birds sang. Tim was excited to play. As he played, Tim threw the ball very high. But when he tried to catch it, he missed. The ball hit his thumb. "Ouch!" Tim said. He looked at his thumb. It was red and hurt a lot. Tim was sad and didn't want to play anymore. He sat on the grass and held his thumb. Just then, a little dog came running. The dog saw the red ball and picked it up with its mouth. It wagged its tail and looked at Tim. Tim smiled and forgot about his thumb. He played with the dog and the red ball. The dog was very good at catching the ball. Tim was happy again, and he made a new friend.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play together. One day, they went to the park with their mom. Mom gave them some candy to share. Ben got a red candy and Lily got a yellow candy. "Red is my favorite!" Ben said. He licked his candy happily. "Yellow is my favorite!" Lily said. She bit her candy and smiled. But then, Ben saw a boy with a blue candy. He wanted the blue candy too. He felt jealous of the boy. "Hey, give me your blue candy!" Ben said to the boy. He ran to the boy and tried to grab his candy. "No, it's mine!" the boy said. He held his candy tight and pushed Ben away. Ben was angry. He threw his red candy at the boy. The candy hit the boy's face and made him cry. "Ben, stop that!" Mom said. She saw what Ben did and was very upset. She picked up the red candy and threw it in the trash. Lily saw what happened and felt sorry for the boy. She walked to the boy and gave him her yellow candy. "Here, you can have my candy," she said. "I'm sorry my brother was mean to you." The boy smiled and thanked Lily. He took her candy and shared it with her. Lily and the boy became friends. They played together on the swings and slides. Ben felt sad and lonely. He had no candy and no friend. He wished he had been nice to the boy and his sister. He learned that being jealous and throwing things was not a good way to get what he wanted.
