Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Cat

One day, a big, bossy cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy some food. He saw a display with spaghetti. Tom thought, "I want to eat spaghetti tonight!" Tom met a small, nice dog named Sam at the store. Sam said, "I like spaghetti too! Can we eat together?" Tom said, "Yes, but I will cook. I am the bossy cat." That night, Tom cooked the spaghetti. They both could smell it. It smelled so good. Sam was very excited. Tom was happy too. Then, Tom and Sam ate the spaghetti. It was very yummy. They both liked it a lot. They became good friends. From that day on, Tom and Sam ate spaghetti together every week. They were not bossy to each other anymore. They learned to share and have fun.

One day, a cat named Tom wanted to organize his toys. He had a lot of toys in his room. Tom was a smart cat who liked to keep things neat. Tom found a long wire in his room. He thought it would be a good idea to use the wire to hang his toys. He started to tie the toys to the wire one by one. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him from a distant place. Max thought the wire was a fun thing to play with. He ran to Tom's room and started to pull the wire. The wire broke and all the toys fell down. Tom was sad because his toys were not organized anymore. Max felt bad too, and they both learned that wires are not good for hanging toys.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny boy named Tim. He had a magic whistle. This whistle was very pretty. Tim loved his whistle a lot. One day, Tim blew his whistle. Suddenly, he began to shrink! He became as small as a bug. "Oh no!" Tim said. His friend, Sue, saw him and asked, "What happened, Tim?" Tim told Sue about his magic whistle. Sue had an idea. "Blow the whistle again, maybe you will grow big." Tim blew the whistle and he grew big again. They laughed and played with the whistle. They had so much fun.

Tim and Mia love to go to the sea with their mom and dad. They like to swim and play in the water. One day, they see a big boat with many people on it. The people have masks and fins and tubes. They are going to dive in the sea. "Can we dive too, mom?" Tim asks. "No, Tim, you are too little to dive. You need to learn how to breathe with a tube and how to be safe in the water. Maybe when you are older, you can dive," mom says. "But I want to see the fish and the coral and the dolphins!" Mia says. "Dolphins? What are dolphins?" Tim asks. "Dolphins are like big fish, but they are not fish. They are smart and friendly and they can make sounds. They have a nose on their head and a tail that goes up and down. They are very pretty," Mia says. "Wow, I want to see dolphins too!" Tim says. "Maybe we can see dolphins from the boat. Let's go and ask dad if we can go on the boat," mom says. They go to the boat and dad says they can go on the boat, but they have to wear life jackets and stay with mom. They get on the boat and sit on the edge. They look at the water and hope to see dolphins. Soon, they hear a splash and a whistle. They see a gray and white shape jump out of the water and land with a splash. It is a dolphin! "Look, look, a dolphin!" Tim and Mia shout. They see more dolphins come out of the water and make sounds. They see the dolphins swim close to the boat and look at them with their eyes. The dolphins seem to smile and say hello. "Hello, dolphins!" Tim and Mia say. They wave their hands and clap their hands. The dolphins wave their tails and clap their fins. They play together for a while. Tim and Mia are very happy. They think the dolphins are the best thing they have ever seen. They thank the dolphins and say goodbye. They go back to the shore with mom and dad. They tell them how much they love the dolphins and how they want to dive with them someday. Mom and dad hug them and say they are proud of them. They say the dolphins are very special and not normal. They say they are lucky to see them and to be friends with them. They say they hope to see them again. They say they love them very much.
