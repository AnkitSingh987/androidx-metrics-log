Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Clothes

John and his mom were looking at some clothes in the store. His mom told him they were going to measure the clothes to make sure they fit correctly. She showed him a hanger. "This is what we'll use to measure the clothes," she said. John held the hanger excitedly and said, "It looks funny!" His mom looked at him and started to laugh. "Yes, it's funny-looking, isn't it? But this hanger helps us measure the length of the clothes," she said. John was confused. "What does 'measure' mean, Mommy?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "To measure something is to find out how big or how small it is. We don't know how big or how small these clothes are yet - that's why it's an unknown!" John smiled and said, "Okay! I know how the hanger can help us measure now!" Mom ruffled John's hair and said, "That's right! Let's go find out how big or how small the clothes are!"

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy he loved a lot. One day, his mom gave him a magazine. The magazine had many pictures of toys. Tim saw a toy that looked like his toy. But this toy was dull, not fun like his toy. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the dull toy in the magazine. He said, "I want that toy!" Tim said, "No, trust me, it is not fun. My toy is more fun." Sam did not trust Tim. He still wanted the dull toy. Later, Sam got the dull toy. He played with it, but it was not fun. Sam was sad. He went to Tim's house. Tim shared his fun toy with Sam. They played and had a lot of fun. Sam learned to trust Tim. They were happy friends.

One day, a little baby was in a crib. The baby felt uncomfortable. The baby started to cry. The crib was too small for the baby. Mom heard the cry and came to the baby. She said, "Why are you crying, my little one?" The baby cried louder. Mom knew she had to do something. Mom had an idea. She lifted the baby out of the crib. She put the baby on a soft bed. The baby stopped crying. The baby was happy and comfortable. Mom and baby smiled at each other.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bob. Bob loved to sing all day long. He lived in a big tree near a pretty flower that was about to bloom. One day, Bob saw trouble coming. A big wind blew the tree and the pretty flower. Bob was scared and flew away to find help. He saw a nice bug named Tim who lived on the other side of the tree. Bob asked Tim for help to save the pretty flower. Tim had a secret. He could make the flower bloom! They went back to the tree and Tim touched the flower with his tiny legs. The flower bloomed into the most beautiful flower ever! Bob was so happy and sang a song for Tim. They became best friends and lived happily in the big tree.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the field near the mill. The mill was big and old. It had a big wheel that turned in the water. Sometimes they saw the miller make flour inside. One day, they saw a big dog near the mill. The dog was not normal. It was angry and loud. It barked and ran at them. Lily and Tom were scared. They ran away from the dog. "Help, help!" they shouted. The miller heard them. He came out of the mill with a stick. He saw the dog and the children. "Go away, bad dog!" he said. He waved the stick at the dog. The dog was afraid of the stick. It stopped and ran away. The miller went to Lily and Tom. He hugged them and said, "Are you okay, children? That dog was not nice. I'm sorry he scared you. I wanted to protect you." Lily and Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, miller. You are nice. You protected us." The miller said, "You are welcome, children. You are brave. You can play in the field, but be careful of the dog. He might come back." Lily and Tom said, "Okay, miller. We will be careful. We like to play in the field and see the mill." They waved goodbye to the miller and went back to the field. They played and had fun. They were happy and safe.
