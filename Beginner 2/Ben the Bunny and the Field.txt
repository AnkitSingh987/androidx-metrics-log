Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Field

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben lived near a big field of wheat. Every day, he would hop and play in the wheat field. One sunny day, while Ben was playing, he met a tiny bug. The bug blinked its eyes and said, "Hi, I am Tim. Do you want to play with me?" Ben smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played hide and seek in the wheat field. Then, they found some delicious berries to eat. They laughed and played all day long. Soon, they became best friends. And they lived happily ever after in the wheat field.

---

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with their toy soldiers and cars. They made noises like zoom and bang and boom. They pretended they were in a big war. One day, they found a big hill of snow in the park. It was icy and white and shiny. They wanted to slide down the hill with their cars and soldiers. They thought it would be fun and fast. They took turns to go first. Tom went first. He put his car and soldier on the snow and pushed them hard. They zoomed down the hill very fast. Tom laughed and clapped. He said, "Wow, that was awesome! Your turn, Sam!" Sam went next. He put his car and soldier on the snow and pushed them hard. They zoomed down the hill very fast. But then, something bad happened. Sam's car hit a rock and flipped over. His soldier flew out of the car and landed in a bush. Sam gasped and cried. He said, "Oh no, my soldier! Where is he? I can't see him!" Tom ran down the hill to help Sam. They looked for the soldier in the bush. They could not find him. They were sad and scared. They thought they lost him forever. But then, they heard a noise. It was a bird. It was sitting on a branch above the bush. It had something in its beak. It was Sam's soldier. The bird had taken him. The bird looked at them and said, "Tweet, tweet, tweet!" Tom and Sam were surprised and happy. They said, "Thank you, bird! Thank you for finding our soldier! Can we have him back, please?" The bird nodded and dropped the soldier on the snow. Tom and Sam picked him up and hugged him. They said, "We are so glad you are okay, soldier! You are brave and strong!" They smiled and waved at the bird. The bird smiled and waved back. It said, "Tweet, tweet, tweet!" Tom and Sam went back to their cars and soldiers. They decided to play somewhere else. They said, "Let's go to the sandbox. It is safer and warmer there." They zoomed away with their cars and soldiers. They were happy and careful. They had a lot of fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely tree. It was very tall and had many leaves. One day, a little girl named Amy saw a pretty symbol on the tree. The symbol looked like a happy face. She wanted to touch it, but it was too high. Amy asked her friend Tom for help. "Tom, can you help me reach the happy face on the tree?" Tom said, "Yes, let's try together!" They both tried to reach the symbol, but it was still too high. Then, Amy had an idea. She found a big stick and used it to reach the happy face. With the stick, they could finally touch the symbol. They were so happy and proud. Amy and Tom laughed and played under the lovely tree all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a glass in the kitchen. The glass was from an unknown place. Tim was very curious about this glass. He wanted to know what would happen if he used it. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I use this glass?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. We don't know where it came from." Tim was excited and filled the glass with water. As Tim drank the water from the glass, something strange happened. His voice changed and he could only speak in funny sounds. Tim and his mom laughed and laughed. They had a fun day with the unknown glass, and Tim learned to always be careful with things he didn't know about.
