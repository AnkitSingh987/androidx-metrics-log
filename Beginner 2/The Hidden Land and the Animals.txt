Category: Beginner
Title: The Hidden Land and the Animals

Once upon a time, there was a hidden land. In this land, there were many animals who loved to joke and play. They were all very happy. One day, a big wind came. It was a hurricane! The animals were scared. They did not know what to do. They all hid in their homes and hoped the wind would go away. When the hurricane was gone, the animals came out of their homes. They saw that their hidden land was a mess. They all worked together to clean up. They joked and laughed as they worked. Soon, their hidden land was happy again.

---

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with cars. Yesterday, they went to the park with their moms. They brought their cars with them. Sara had a red car. Tom had a blue car. They drove their cars on the grass. They had fun. But then, Sara saw a big hill. She wanted to drive her car down the hill. She said to Tom, "Let's go to the hill. It will be fun." Tom was scared. He did not want to go to the hill. He said to Sara, "No, thank you. The hill is too high. I might fall. I want to stay here." Sara was not patient. She did not listen to Tom. She said, "You are a baby. I am going to the hill. Bye." She drove her car to the hill. She left Tom alone. Tom was sad. He did not like to play alone. He waited for Sara. He hoped she would come back. Sara drove her car to the top of the hill. She felt proud. She looked down. She saw Tom. He looked small. She said, "Look at me. I am on the hill. You are not. You are a baby." She pushed her car down the hill. She wanted to see it go fast. But she made a mistake. The hill was too steep. The car went too fast. It hit a rock. It flipped over. It broke. Sara was shocked. She ran down the hill. She picked up her car. It was broken. She felt sad. She cried. Tom saw Sara. He felt sorry for her. He ran to her. He said, "Are you okay? I am sorry your car broke." Sara looked at Tom. She felt ashamed. She said, "I am sorry, too. I was mean to you. I did not listen to you. You were right. The hill was too high. I was not patient. I was silly." Tom smiled. He said, "It's okay. We all make mistakes. We can still be friends. Do you want to play with my car? We can share." Sara smiled, too. She said, "Yes, please. Thank you. You are a good friend. You are patient. You are kind." They hugged. They played with Tom's car. They had fun. They learned a lesson. They learned to be patient, to listen, and to share.

Once there was a mouse who lived in a fancy house. The mouse was very happy and spent a lot of time exploring the cobwebs. One day, when the mouse was in a cobweb, it felt a pinch on his tail! He quickly looked around but could not see who or what had pinched him. Later that day he felt the pinch again! The mouse was scared and tried to hide, but still he felt the pinch every time he was in a cobweb. He couldn't understand why he was being pinched. Sadly, the mouse was never able to find out why he kept getting pinched, and never felt safe in the cobwebs again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Ben and a small cat named Tim. They were best friends. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. They were both very excited. Ben was very fast and strong, but Tim was patient. Before the race, Tim's mom taught him to be patient and keep going even when things were hard. Ben thought he would win the race easily, so he didn't listen to his mom when she tried to teach him the same thing. The race began and Ben ran very fast, leaving Tim behind. But soon, Ben got tired and stopped to rest. Tim, being patient, kept going slowly and passed Ben while he was resting. Ben saw Tim pass him and started running again, but he was too tired. In the end, Tim won the race because he was patient and didn't give up. Ben learned that being patient and listening to what his mom tried to teach him was important. From that day on, both Ben and Tim always remembered to be patient and listen to what their moms taught them. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to be patient and listen to the lessons our loved ones teach us.
