Category: Beginner
Title: Remy the Rhinoceros and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big rhinoceros named Remy. Remy loved to spend his days playing with his friends in the jungle. He felt very lucky to have so many friends. One sunny day, Remy and his friends found a big tree with yummy fruit. Remy's friends wanted to eat all the fruit right away, but Remy had an idea. He said, "Let's save some fruit for later. We will be lucky to have more yummy food when we are hungry again." His friends listened to Remy and saved some fruit. A few days later, it rained a lot and there was no food to eat. Remy's friends were very hungry, but then they remembered the fruit they saved. They ate the fruit and were not hungry anymore. Remy's friends were very happy and thanked him. They learned that it is good to save and share, and they felt lucky to have Remy as their friend.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big temple. Many people came to visit the temple. One day, a little boy named Tom went to the temple with his mom. At the temple, Tom saw an old man sitting on the ground. The old man had no shoes. Tom felt sad for the old man. He wanted to help him. So, Tom took off his shoes and lent them to the old man. The old man was very happy and said "thank you" to Tom. As they walked away, Tom's mom told him that the old man was really a kind king in disguise. The king wanted to see who would be nice to him. Because Tom was so kind and lent his shoes to the old man, the king gave Tom a big gift. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, even when you think no one is watching.

One day, a big tree and a small tree were in the yard. They were friends. The big tree said, "Let's discuss our roots." The small tree said, "Okay, I like that." As they talked about their roots, a boy came to the yard. The boy was sorry because he broke his toy. The big tree said, "Don't be sad, we can help you fix it." The small tree said, "Yes, we can do it." The trees used their roots to fix the toy. The boy was very happy. The trees were happy, too. They did not know their roots could fix toys. Now, they were not just friends, but also helpers.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird who loved to soar high in the sky. He had many friends in the forest, but his best friend was a small mouse. The mouse was always in shock when he saw the bird fly so high. He wished he could fly too. One day, the bird had an idea. "Mouse, I can help you fly!" he said. The mouse was very excited. The bird made a plan to find the biggest leaf available. They looked all around the forest and found the perfect leaf. The bird held the leaf in his beak and the mouse climbed on. Then, they soared high up into the sky together. The mouse was so happy and laughed as the wind blew through his fur. They flew all day until the sun began to set. Then, they landed back on the ground, and the mouse thanked his friend for the best day ever.

Lily was a young girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Anna. Anna had a pretty dress and a big bow in her hair. Lily liked to comb Anna's hair and make her bow look nice. One day, Lily and Anna went to the park with Lily's mom. Lily saw a swing and wanted to try it. She asked her mom, "Can I go on the swing, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can, but be careful. Don't drop Anna, okay?" Lily nodded and ran to the swing. She put Anna on her lap and held her tight. She pushed the swing with her feet and felt the wind in her face. She was having fun. But then, a big boy came and pushed Lily's swing harder. Lily was scared and screamed, "Stop it! You are mean!" The boy laughed and pushed again. Lily lost her balance and fell off the swing. She dropped Anna on the ground. Anna's bow came off and flew away. Lily was hurt and sad. She cried and picked up Anna. She saw that Anna's dress was dirty and her hair was messy. She looked for Anna's bow, but she could not find it. Her mom came and hugged her. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. That boy was very rude. Are you okay?" Lily nodded and showed her mom Anna. She said, "Anna is okay, too. But her bow is gone. I can't find it." Her mom looked around and saw the bow on a tree branch. She said, "Look, Lily. There is your bow. It's on the tree. Do you see it?" Lily looked and saw the bow. She smiled and said, "Yes, I see it. Can you get it for me, please?" Her mom said, "Of course, I can. Wait here, I'll be right back." Her mom climbed the tree and got the bow. She gave it to Lily and said, "Here you go, Lily. Now Anna's bow is back. You can fix her hair and make her look pretty again." Lily thanked her mom and put the bow on Anna's hair. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best. I love you." Her mom said, "I love you, too, Lily. You are a brave girl. Now, let's go home and wash Anna's dress. How about that?" Lily agreed and hugged her mom. She said, "Yes, let's go home. I'm tired of the park. I just want to play with Anna." Her mom smiled and took her hand. They walked home together, with Lily holding Anna close. They were happy.
