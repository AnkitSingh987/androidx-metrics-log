Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Kim and the Horse

Tim and Kim are twins who like to play on the farm. They have a big dog named Spot who likes to run and bark. One day, they see a new thing in the stable. It is a big, brown horse with a long mane and tail. "Wow, look at the horse!" Tim says. "Can we pet it?" "I don't know," Kim says. "Maybe we should ask Dad first." They run to find Dad, who is working in the field. They tell him about the horse and ask if they can pet it. "Sure, you can pet the horse," Dad says. "But be careful, he is very old and sick. He has a bad leg that hurts a lot. And he has a sore on his side that is rotting. It smells bad and has flies on it. You don't want to touch that." "Yuck, that's gross," Tim says. "Why do you have a horse that is rotting?" "Well, the horse belongs to Mr. Jones, who lives next door. He can't take care of him anymore, so he asked me to keep him in the stable until he finds a new home for him. I'm trying to help him, but I don't think he will get better. He is too old and weak." "That's sad," Kim says. "Can we give him some water and hay? Maybe that will make him feel better." "Sure, you can do that," Dad says. "But don't get too close to his sore. And wash your hands after you pet him. Come on, I'll show you where the water and hay are." Tim and Kim follow Dad to the stable. They fill a bucket with water and put some hay in a trough. They pet the horse gently on his head and neck. He looks at them with sad eyes and makes a soft noise. "Hello, horse," Tim says. "We are Tim and Kim. We are your friends. We want you to be happy." The horse nods his head and drinks some water. He nibbles on some hay. He seems to like Tim and Kim. They smile and hug him. "Good horse," Kim says. "We hope you find a new home soon. A home where you are not rotting and hurting. A home where you are loved." The horse whinnies and licks their faces. He thanks them for being kind. Tim and Kim giggle and wipe their faces. They say goodbye to the horse and run back to Dad. They tell him they had fun with the horse. Dad smiles and hugs them. "You are good kids," Dad says. "You have big hearts. You made the horse happy today. And you made me proud."

Once there was a little girl called Jane. Jane believed in magic. One day, while walking in the park, she saw something very special. It was a unique rose! Even though the rose was small, it was the most beautiful one Jane had ever seen. She picked it and hugged it, feeling so happy and lucky. Jane believed that the rose was magical and that it could bring her luck and happiness. Every day she would look after the rose and make sure it was healthy. She would talk to it and tell it stories, feeling like it was her best friend. It always made Jane happy when she saw the rose. She believed it was a sign from the universe, telling her that she would always be lucky and blessed. Jane and the rose were together for a very long time and the rose remained unique and beautiful until the end.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. Mia saw a big network of strings on the ground. She felt uncomfortable because she did not know what it was. Mia saw her friend Tom nearby and called him over. "Tom, what is this?" asked Mia. Tom looked at the network and said, "I don't know, but let's play with it!" They started to play with the network, but then something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky and bowed to Mia and Tom. The bird said, "Thank you for finding my network! I lost it when I was flying." Mia and Tom were very surprised, but they were happy to help the bird. They all played together and had a fun day.
