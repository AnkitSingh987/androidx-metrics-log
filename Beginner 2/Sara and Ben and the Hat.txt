Title: Sara and Ben and the Hat

Sara and Ben were playing outside in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two buttons for eyes. "Look, our snowman is so happy!" Sara said. "Yes, he is. But he might get cold soon. The weather is very icy today," Ben said. They heard a loud noise from the street. It was a big truck with a plow. It was clearing the snow from the road. "Oh no, the truck is coming this way!" Sara cried. "It will knock down our snowman!" They ran to their snowman and hugged him. They hoped the truck would stop or turn away. But the truck did not stop or turn away. It came closer and closer. It pushed the snow aside with its plow. It hit the snowman with a thud. The snowman fell apart. His hat and scarf flew away. His carrot nose and buttons rolled on the ground. Sara and Ben were very sad. They looked at their broken snowman. The truck driver saw them. He stopped the truck and got out. He walked to them. "I'm sorry, kids. I didn't see your snowman. I was just doing my job," he said. He picked up the hat and scarf, the carrot nose and the buttons. He handed them to Sara and Ben. "Can you fix him?" Sara asked. "Sure, we can fix him. Let's make him again. But this time, let's make him on the sidewalk, not on the road. That way, he will be safe from the truck," he said. He helped Sara and Ben to make a new snowman. He put the hat and scarf, the carrot nose and the buttons on him. He smiled at them. "There, he's as good as new. And he's happy, too," he said. Sara and Ben smiled, too. They thanked the truck driver. "Thank you for helping us fix our snowman. You are very nice," they said. "You're welcome, kids. Have fun, but be careful. The weather is very icy today," he said. He got back in his truck and drove away. He waved to them. Sara and Ben waved back. They hugged their new snowman. They were happy again.

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went for a walk. They saw a big church. The church was very old and had a tall tower. Tim looked at the church and said, "Mom, can we go inside?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go in and look around." They went inside the church and saw many pretty windows. The windows had pictures of people and animals on them. Tim liked the windows a lot. As they walked around, they found a corner with a big, disgusting mess on the floor. Tim looked at it and said, "Yuck! What is that?" His mom said, "It looks like someone spilled their food. Let's tell the church people so they can clean it up." They told the church people, and they cleaned the mess. Tim and his mom were happy they could help the church.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a pretty flower called a poppy. She loved her poppy very much. One day, Mia saw a big rain cloud. She did not want the rain to spoil her poppy. So, Mia put her poppy in a safe place under a big tree. The rain came and went away. Mia's poppy was safe and did not spoil. Mia was very happy and played with her poppy all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had very long hair. He wanted to get a haircut to look attractive. Tim's mom took him to the haircut place. The haircut man said, "What kind of haircut do you want, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to look nice. Please pick a good haircut for me." The haircut man gave Tim a great haircut. Tim looked in the mirror and said, "Wow! I look so nice now!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you do! You are very attractive, Tim." They went home, and Tim was very happy with his new haircut.
