Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Bridge

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, they have a big idea. They want to make a bridge with blocks and cars. They work hard and put many blocks and cars together. "Look, Lily, I show you my bridge!" Tom says. He is proud of his bridge. He puts a car on top of the bridge and makes it go vroom vroom. "Wow, Tom, your bridge is very good!" Lily says. She likes Tom's bridge. She wants to show him her bridge too. She puts a car on top of her bridge and makes it go beep beep. But then, something bad happens. The bridges are too high and too weak. They fall down and make a big noise. The blocks and cars go everywhere. Lily and Tom are scared and sad. They cry. "Oh no, our bridges are broken!" Lily says. She is sorry for her bridge and Tom's bridge. "I'm sorry too, Lily. We worked hard, but we made a mistake." Tom says. He is sorry for his bridge and Lily's bridge. Their teacher hears the noise and comes to see what happened. She sees the broken bridges and the crying children. She is not angry. She is kind. "Don't cry, Lily and Tom. It's okay. You tried to make something new and fun. That's good. But sometimes, things don't work as we want. That's the result of trying. We can learn from it and try again." She says. She hugs them and helps them clean up. Lily and Tom feel better. They thank their teacher and smile. They still like to play with blocks and cars. But next time, they will make lower and stronger bridges. And they will be more careful. They are happy.

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big dog with a long neck and a brown coat. The dog looked sad and hungry. It had no collar or leash. "Look, a dog!" Ben said, pointing at the animal. "Can we pet it?" Sam asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is friendly. Maybe it is not," Ben said. They walked slowly towards the dog, holding hands. The dog lifted its head and wagged its tail. It seemed to like them. "Hi, dog. What is your name?" Ben said, reaching out his hand. The dog licked Ben's hand and nuzzled his neck. Ben giggled and hugged the dog. "Aw, he likes you, Ben. He is a good dog," Sam said, joining the hug. The dog barked happily and licked Sam's face. "Maybe he is lost. Maybe he has no home," Ben said, looking at the dog's poor coat and thin body. "Maybe we can take him home. Maybe we can be his family," Sam said, looking at Ben with hopeful eyes. They decided to take the dog home and ask their mom if they could keep him. They named him Spot, because he had a big black spot on his back. They hoped their mom would say yes. They did not know that their mom had a surprise for them. She had been looking for a dog to adopt for a long time, and she had just found one that looked exactly like Spot.

Lila and Ben were playing with cubes in the playroom. They liked to build towers and houses with the cubes. They had many cubes of different colors and sizes. "Look, I made a big tower!" Lila said, showing Ben her creation. "It is taller than me!" "That is nice, but I made a better house!" Ben said, pointing to his structure. "It has a door and a window and a roof!" Lila and Ben started to argue about who had the better cube project. They did not use modest words. They used mean words and loud voices. "Your tower is ugly and wobbly!" Ben shouted. "Your house is boring and small!" Lila yelled. They got so angry that they pushed each other's cubes. The cubes fell down and made a big mess. Some of the cubes hit them and hurt them. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lila and Ben cried. Mom heard the noise and came to the playroom. She saw the cubes everywhere and the children crying. She was not happy. "What happened here?" Mom asked. "Lila and Ben were not nice to each other. They fought over the cubes and broke them." A voice said. Mom looked around and saw a small, modest cube on the shelf. It was the only cube that was not on the floor. It was the only cube that could talk. "Who are you?" Mom asked. "I am the magic cube. I can talk and make wishes come true. But only for good children who share and play nicely. Lila and Ben were not good children. They were rude and selfish. So I took away their wishes and made their cubes fall down." The magic cube said. Mom was shocked. She did not know there was a magic cube in the playroom. She did not know what to do. "Lila and Ben, is this true?" Mom asked. Lila and Ben nodded. They felt sorry and ashamed. They wished they had not fought over the cubes. They wished they had been modest and kind. "Can you fix this?" Mom asked the magic cube. "I can, but only if Lila and Ben learn their lesson. They have to say sorry to each other and to me. They have to promise to share and play nicely. And they have to clean up the mess." The magic cube said. Lila and Ben looked at each other. They knew they had to do what the magic cube said. But they did not want to. They did not like to say sorry or to clean up. They did not like the magic cube. They wished they had never played with it. They wished they had never rushed to the playroom.
