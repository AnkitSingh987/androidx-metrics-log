Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Dog

One day, a gentle cat and a funny dog were playing together. They liked to play jokes on each other. The sun was shining, and they were happy. The cat said, "I have a joke for you, dog. I will cover my eyes, and you hide. Then, I will find you." The dog laughed and ran to hide behind a big tree. The cat covered her eyes and started to count. When the cat opened her eyes, she looked for the dog. But the dog was not behind the tree! The cat looked everywhere, but she could not find the dog. She was sad and worried. Then, she heard a giggle behind her. The dog was hiding in a bush! The cat laughed, and they played more jokes together.

A prince wanted to be the most powerful in the land. He did not like to share or be kind. He took things from other people and made them work for him. He had a big castle and many soldiers. One day, a dragon came to the land. The dragon was angry and hungry. He breathed fire and smoke. He burned the fields and the houses. He ate the cows and the sheep. The people were scared and sad. The prince thought he could stop the dragon. He put on his shiny armor and took his sharp sword. He rode his fast horse to the dragon's cave. He shouted at the dragon, "I am the prince and I am powerful. You have to leave or I will fight you." The dragon laughed at the prince. He said, "You are not powerful. You are greedy and mean. You have made the people suffer. You have no friends or love. You are alone and weak. I will not leave. I will eat you." The dragon breathed fire at the prince. The prince tried to run away, but he was too slow. The fire burned his armor and his sword. The fire burned his horse and his skin. The fire burned his bones and his heart. The prince was no more. The dragon flew away. The people cried for their losses. They did not cry for the prince. They hoped for a better ruler. They hoped for peace and happiness. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to jump. One day, he went to the park with his mom. The park was big and had many fun things to play with. Tim saw a big slide, swings, and a sandbox. He was so happy! While he was playing, Tim saw something incredible. It was a big, colorful ball. The ball was bigger than Tim! He thought it was the most fun thing in the park. Tim wanted to play with the big ball, so he ran to it and started to jump on it. As Tim jumped on the ball, something unexpected happened. The big ball started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim! I am a magic ball. You found me, and now I have a surprise for you!" Tim was very surprised. The magic ball took Tim on a fun ride around the park, and they played all day. In the end, Tim made a new friend and had the best day ever at the park.

Once there was a busy ant named Anna. She had a job to do. She had to find food for her ant friends. She walked and walked, but she could not find any food yet. One day, Anna saw a big hill. She thought, "Maybe there is food on the other side!" So, she started to climb the hill. It was very hard, but Anna did not give up. She wanted to help her friends. She managed to get to the top of the hill. When she got there, she saw something she did not expect. There was a big party with lots of food! But it was not for her ant friends. It was for the bees! The bees saw Anna and said, "You can have some food too!" Anna was happy. She took the food back to her ant friends and they all had a big party together.
