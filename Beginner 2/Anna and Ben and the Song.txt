Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Song

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play and sing together. One day, they are in the kitchen with Mom. Mom is making cocoa for them. It is cold outside and cocoa is warm and sweet. "Can we sing a song, Mom?" Anna asks. "Sure, what song do you want to sing?" Mom says. Anna and Ben think for a moment. They like many songs, but they have a favorite one. It is about a snowman who likes to dance and laugh. "Let's sing the snowman song!" Ben says. "Okay, let's sing the snowman song!" Mom agrees. They start to sing the snowman song. They sing loud and happy. They make funny faces and gestures. They have fun. But not everyone likes their singing. In the living room, Dad is working on his laptop. He has a lot of work to do and he needs to concentrate. He hears the singing and he gets angry. "Can you please be quiet?" Dad shouts. "I can't work with all this noise!" Anna, Ben and Mom stop singing. They look at each other. They feel sad and sorry. "We didn't mean to bother you, Dad," Anna says. "We just wanted to sing a song, Dad," Ben says. "I know, I know," Dad says. "But I have a deadline and I need some silence. Can you please play something else?" Anna, Ben and Mom nod. They understand. They love Dad and they don't want to make him angry. "Okay, Dad, we will play something else," Mom says. She gives Anna and Ben their cups of cocoa. They take them and go to their room. They decide to play with their toys and read some books. They still like to sing, but they will sing later, when Dad is done with his work. They hope he will join them and sing the snowman song with them. They think that would make him happy.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a sweet bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat the sweet fruit from the vine. One day, he saw a big bird named Tom on the other side of the garden. Tom also loved the sweet fruit from the vine. Bob and Tom looked at each other. They both wanted the sweet fruit, but there was only a little left on the vine. They did not know what to do. They both wanted the fruit, but they did not want to fight. So, they decide to talk. Bob said, "Let's share the sweet fruit from the vine." Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, that is a good idea. We can both have some of the sweet fruit and be happy." Bob and Tom ate the sweet fruit from the vine together. They were happy that they decide to share. After that day, they became good friends and always shared the sweet fruit from the vine.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty ant. The ant was very strong. One day, the ant saw a big, green cactus. The cactus was very tall and had many sharp points. The mighty ant wanted to attach a red flag on top of the cactus. The ant thought it would be fun. But the other ants said, "No, it is too hard and not safe!" The mighty ant did not listen. The mighty ant tried to climb the cactus. It was not easy. The sharp points hurt the ant, but the ant did not stop. At last, the ant reached the top and attached the red flag. But then, the ant fell down. The mighty ant was hurt and sad. The other ants were right. The end.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big bush with red flowers. Lily wants to wear a flower in her hair. She says to Tom, "Look, how pretty! Can I have one?" Tom says, "Yes, but be careful. The bush has thorns. They can hurt you." He helps Lily pick a flower. He is gentle and does not touch the thorns. Lily smiles and puts the flower in her hair. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." She gives him a hug. Tom says, "You are welcome, Lily. You look very nice with the flower." He hugs her back. They hold hands and run to the swings. They have fun in the park. They are happy.
