Category: Beginner
Title: A Tale of the Cat

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing together. They were very happy. The dog had a belt around his neck. The cat liked the belt very much. The cat wanted to play with the belt, so she tried to slap it with her paw. The dog did not like that. He felt ashamed. He did not want the cat to touch his belt. The dog got mad at the cat. He growled and showed his big teeth. The cat was scared and ran away. The dog chased the cat. They ran and ran. The cat was very tired. The dog caught the cat and bit her. The cat was hurt and cried. The dog was still mad and did not say sorry. The cat and the dog were not friends anymore.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a soft, round bulb in his yard. He did not know what it was, but he liked how it felt in his hand. He took the bulb to his mom and asked her what it was. She smiled and told him it was a special bulb that would grow into a pretty flower if he planted it in the ground. Tim was excited to see the flower, so he decided to plant the bulb in a sunny spot in the yard. A few days later, Tim saw a tiny green plant start to grow where he had planted the bulb. He was very happy and went to tell his mom. She told him that soon he would see a beautiful flower join the little green plant. Tim couldn't wait to see it, so he watched the plant every day. One morning, Tim woke up and ran to the yard to check on his plant. He saw a big, bright flower had joined the little green plant. The flower was the prettiest one he had ever seen. Tim's mom came to see it too, and she was very proud of him for taking care of the bulb. Tim knew that the special bulb had turned into a beautiful flower because he had helped it grow.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a yellow cat named Sunny. Sunny loved to play with her pastel toys. Her favorite toy was a soft, pink ball that she would chase around the house. One day, Sunny's friend, a little boy named Tim, came to visit. Tim picked up the pink ball and threw it across the room. "Fetch, Sunny!" he said. Sunny ran after the ball and brought it back to Tim. He was so happy that he clapped his hands. "Again!" Tim said, and he threw the ball. Sunny loved to repeat this game with Tim. They played fetch with the pastel ball all day long. When it was time for Tim to go home, he gave Sunny a big hug. "Bye, Sunny," he said. "You're the best yellow cat ever!"

One day, Tim and Sue were playing in their room. Tim found a big envelope under his bed. He looked at Sue and said, "Look, I found an envelope!" Sue looked at the envelope and said, "That's silly. What's in it?" They opened the envelope and found a map. The map showed a path to a big tree in their yard. Tim and Sue went outside and followed the map. They looked at the tree and saw a little box. They opened the box and found a note. The note said, "Be kind and share." Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They learned that sharing is important and it makes everyone happy. They went back inside and shared their toys with each other. They had a fun day playing together and being kind.

Tom and Lily went to the park with Mom. They liked to play on the swings and the slide. They saw a big fountain with water that sprayed high in the air. Tom and Lily wanted to go near the fountain and feel the water. "Can we go to the fountain, Mom?" Tom asked. "OK, but be careful. Don't get too wet or too close to the water. It might be cold or strong," Mom said. Tom and Lily ran to the fountain. They laughed and splashed and felt the water spray on their faces and arms. They had fun. But then, Tom had a bad idea. He saw a hose near the fountain. He picked it up and turned it on. He sprayed Lily with the hose. Lily got wet and angry. "Stop it, Tom! You are mean! I don't like you!" Lily shouted. She tried to take the hose from Tom, but he did not let go. He sprayed her more and more. Lily cried and ran to Mom. Mom saw what Tom did and got angry too. "Tom, what are you doing? That is not nice! You are hurting Lily and wasting water! Give me the hose right now!" Mom said. But Tom did not listen. He sprayed Mom with the hose too. Mom got wet and angrier. She grabbed the hose from Tom and turned it off. She scolded Tom and took him and Lily away from the fountain. She said they had to go home and no more park for today. Tom and Lily were sad and sorry. They had a bad day.
