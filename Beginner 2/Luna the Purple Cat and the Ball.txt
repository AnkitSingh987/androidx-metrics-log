Category: Beginner
Title: Luna the Purple Cat and the Ball

One day, a purple cat named Luna went to a new place. This place had many fun things to play with. Luna was so happy to be there. But Luna saw a big sign that said, "Do not destroy anything." Luna met a little dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Luna, let's play with the big ball!" Luna and Max played with the big ball and had lots of fun. But then, Luna saw a big tower made of blocks. She wanted to play with it too. Luna said, "Max, let's play with the blocks!" Max said, "No, Luna, we can't. We will destroy it." But Luna didn't listen. She jumped on the tower and it fell down. All the blocks were all over the place. Luna felt bad for destroying the tower. She said, "I'm sorry, Max. I shouldn't have destroyed it." Max said, "It's okay, Luna. Let's build it again together." So Luna and Max built a new tower with the blocks. They were more careful this time and did not destroy anything else. They had fun and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. The rock could sing a song. One day, the rock met a big bird. The bird was very strong. The rock and the bird became friends. They played together every day. One day, the bird and the rock found a big wall. The wall was blocking their way. The bird had an idea. The bird said, "I can destroy the wall with my strong wings." The rock was happy. The bird started to hit the wall with its wings. But, the wall did not break. The wall was too strong. Then, the rock had an idea. The rock started to sing its song. The wall started to shake. The wall broke into small pieces. The bird and the rock were very happy. They could play together again. The rock's song was more powerful than they thought.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little lion named Leo. Leo had bright eyes and loved to play with his friends. One day, Leo heard a loud noise. It was a big roar! Leo was scared but wanted to find out where the roar came from. Leo looked around with his bright eyes and saw a big cave. He thought the roar might have come from inside the cave. He was scared, but he wanted to be brave like his dad. So, he took a deep breath and went into the cave. Inside the cave, Leo found a big bear. The bear was sad because he had a thorn in his paw. Leo was brave and helped the bear take the thorn out. The bear was so happy that he roared again, but this time it was a happy roar. Leo and the bear became best friends and played together every day.

Once there was a little girl named Sarah. She was very happy and always had a smile on her face. One day, Sarah was playing in her backyard when she spotted a small pass that her mom had made for her. She went over and stood by the pass, excited to use it. That's when she spotted her friend Molly standing on the other side of the pass. Sarah waved and called out to Molly, "Hi Molly!" Molly waved back and shouted, "Hi Sarah!" Sarah was so excited to meet Molly on the other side of the pass, so she jumped up and down and shouted, "Let's play together!" Molly nodded eagerly and they crossed the pass to play together. The moral of the story is that you can be very happy when you stand with friends and welcome them into your life. Sharing and working together is always a better choice than standing alone.

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Tom wanted to go first, but Sam was faster. He climbed up the ladder and sat on the slide. Tom was angry and shouted, "Move, Sam! It's my turn!" Sam did not listen. He smiled and said, "No, Tom. I go first. You are too slow." He pushed his feet and slid down the slide. He laughed and said, "That was fun!" Tom was more angry. He climbed up the ladder and saw Sam waiting at the bottom. He wanted to make Sam fall. He thought, "I will push him hard and he will cry." He did not see the guard who was watching them. The guard saw Tom and knew he was going to be foolish. He said, "Stop, Tom! Don't push your brother. That is not nice. You can hurt him and yourself." He walked to the slide and reached for Tom. But Tom did not stop. He pushed Sam with his hands and said, "Ha, ha, Sam! Take that!" He did not know that Sam was ready. Sam moved to the side and dodged Tom's push. Tom lost his balance and fell off the slide. He landed on the ground and hurt his knee. He cried and said, "Ow, ow, ow!" The guard picked up Tom and said, "See, Tom? That was foolish. You should not push your brother. You should share and be kind. Now you have a boo-boo and you need a band-aid." He took Tom to his mom and gave him a band-aid. Sam felt sorry for Tom. He said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I should not have teased you. You can go first next time." He hugged Tom and said, "Let's play something else. How about the swings?" Tom said, "Okay, Sam. I'm sorry, too. I should not have pushed you. You are my brother and I love you." He hugged Sam and said, "Let's go to the swings. Maybe the guard will push us." They ran to the swings and had fun. The guard smiled and said, "That's better. Good boys."
