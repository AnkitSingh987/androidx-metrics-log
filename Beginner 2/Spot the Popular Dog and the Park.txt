Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Popular Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. He had many friends in the park. Every day, Spot would run and play with his friends. They would chase each other and have fun. One day, while playing with his friends, Spot found a big bone. He picked it up with his mouth and started to shake it. All his friends watched him and wanted the bone too. They all thought the bone was special. But then, something unexpected happened. The bone broke into many little pieces. Spot's friends started to laugh. They thought it was a magic bone, but it was just a normal bone. Spot laughed too, and they all continued playing together.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big pile of clay. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the clay. Tim started to make a clay dog. He was very good at it. Tim noticed a hungry bird in a tree. The bird was looking at the clay dog. The bird flew down and took a piece of the clay. Tim was sad, but then he had an idea. He made some clay food for the hungry bird. The bird ate the clay food and was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird turned into a big, colorful clay bird! The clay bird could talk! It said, "Thank you, Tim, for the food. I was a real bird, but a mean witch turned me into a clay bird. When you fed me the clay food, you broke the spell." Tim and the clay bird became best friends. They played together in the park every day. And they always had lots of fun with the clay.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to write on paper. She had a big box of colorful pens that she used every day. Lily liked to draw pictures and write stories about her friends and family. One sunny day, Lily found a clear, shiny rock near her house. She thought it was very pretty. She wanted to show it to her friends. So, she decided to write a letter and draw a picture of the rock on a piece of paper. She wrote, "Look at my pretty rock. It is clear and shiny. I hope you like it too." Lily put the paper in an envelope and gave it to her friend, Tom. Tom was very happy to get the letter. He thought the clear rock was very pretty too. So, he wrote a letter back to Lily, saying, "Thank you for showing me your pretty rock. I like it a lot." They both smiled and continued to write letters to each other about their favorite things. And they lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a spray bottle filled with water. They liked to spray the flowers and the grass and make them wet. "Look, a bird!" Ben said, pointing to a small brown bird on a branch. "Let's spray it!" Anna shook her head. "No, Ben, that's not nice. The bird is harmless. It doesn't hurt us. It just sings and flies." "But it would be fun!" Ben said. He aimed the spray bottle at the bird and squeezed it hard. The bird felt the water on its feathers. It was cold and wet. It didn't like it. It flew away, making a loud noise. "Ben, you scared the bird!" Anna said. She was angry. "You made it sad. How would you like it if someone sprayed you?" Ben felt sorry. He didn't mean to make the bird sad. He just wanted to play. He looked at Anna and said, "I'm sorry, Anna. You're right. I wouldn't like it. The bird is harmless. It's our friend. Can we be friends again?" Anna smiled. She forgave Ben. She said, "Yes, Ben, we can be friends again. But next time, let's spray something else. Like the fence or the rocks. They don't mind." Ben nodded. He agreed. He said, "OK, Anna, let's do that. And maybe we can give some water to the bird, too. Maybe it will come back and sing for us." Anna and Ben hugged. They were happy. They went to find a bowl and filled it with water. They put it under the tree where the bird was. They hoped the bird would come back and drink from it. They wanted to be kind to the bird. They wanted to hear it sing.
