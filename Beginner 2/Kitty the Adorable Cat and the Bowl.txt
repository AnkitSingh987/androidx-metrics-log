Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Adorable Cat and the Bowl

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there lived an adorable cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to mix things and make tasty food. One day, she found a big jar of jelly. Kitty had an idea to make a yummy snack for her friends. Kitty mixed the jelly with some other things she found in the kitchen. She made a big bowl of jelly snack. She called her friends over to try it. "Come eat this yummy jelly snack I made," said Kitty. Her friends came running to try it. They all took a bite and smiled. They loved Kitty's jelly snack. They ate and ate until the bowl was empty. Kitty's friends said, "Thank you, Kitty! We love your jelly snack!" Kitty was happy that her friends liked her snack. The moral of the story is that when we share and make things for others, it makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple bird. The bird lived in a tree. The tree had many branches. One day, the bird saw a little fish in the pond. The fish liked to splash in the water. The bird said, "Hello, fish! Do you want to play with me?" The fish said, "Yes! I like to splash. Can you splash too?" The bird tried to splash, but it could not. The bird was sad. The fish said, "Do not be sad. You can sit on the branch and watch me splash. We can still be friends." The bird and the fish played together every day. They learned that even if they are different, they can still be friends. The moral of the story is that we can be friends with others even if we are not the same.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to make things with his hands. One day, he found a fragile, pretty stick. He thought it would be fun to make a turn with the stick. He took the stick and waved it in the air. As he made a turn, something strange happened. A big, scary monster came out of nowhere! Tim was very scared. The monster looked at Tim and said, "I am here because of the turn you made with the fragile stick." Tim was sad and scared. The monster stayed with Tim and made everything bad. And so, Tim learned never to play with fragile sticks again.

Lily was a daughter who loved her mom and dad. They lived in a small house with a big garden. Lily liked to play with her toys, read books, and help her mom with chores. She was happy and kind. One day, Lily's mom and dad had to go to the hospital. They said they were sick and needed help from the doctors. They told Lily to stay with her aunt, who lived next door. They said they loved her and would come back soon. Lily was sad and scared. She hugged them and said she loved them too. Lily's aunt was nice and tried to make her feel better. She gave her cookies, read her stories, and played games with her. But Lily missed her mom and dad a lot. She asked her aunt when they would come back. Her aunt said she didn't know, but they were trying their best. A few days later, Lily's aunt got a phone call. She looked very sad and cried. Lily asked her what was wrong. Her aunt said she had bad news. She said Lily's mom and dad were dead. They couldn't fight the sickness and the doctors couldn't save them. She said she was sorry and hugged Lily. Lily didn't understand. She said her mom and dad couldn't be dead. They said they would come back. They said they loved her. She said she wanted to see them. She said she needed them. She cried and cried. Her aunt cried with her. She said she loved her and would take care of her. She said they would always be in her heart. She said they were still her family.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Bob. He had many toys and a big house. One day, Bob found a pair of scissors. He thought it was a fun toy. He showed it to his friend, Tim. "Look, Tim! I found a new toy!" said Bob. Tim looked at the scissors and said, "That's not a toy, Bob. We can use it to make art." They started to cut paper with the scissors. They made many shapes and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the room. The bird saw the paper shapes and thought they were food. The bird tried to eat the paper, but it didn't taste good. Bob and Tim laughed and clapped their hands. They were happy to see the bird. They decided to make a birdhouse with the paper and scissors. The bird loved its new home and stayed with them forever.
