Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, strong tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree. He called his friend, Sam, to come help him. Sam came and they started to climb the tree together. While they were climbing, Tim saw a small bottle. It had a label that said "poison." Tim did not know what it was, but he thought it might be bad. He called Sam to show him the bottle. Sam looked at it and said they should tell a grown-up. They went to find a grown-up and showed them the bottle. The grown-up looked at the bottle and smiled. It was not poison at all! It was just a bottle of juice. The grown-up said it was safe to drink. Tim and Sam laughed and drank the juice together. They were happy it was not poison. Then they went back to climb the strong tree and play in the park.

One day, a warm sun was shining and a little bird was on a cliff. The bird saw a big leaf. The bird wanted to tear the leaf to make a nest. So, the bird went to the leaf and started to tear it. A squirrel came to the bird and said, "Why do you tear the leaf? That is my leaf!" The bird said, "I need it for my nest." The squirrel did not want to share the leaf with the bird. They both wanted the leaf. They pulled and pulled on the leaf. They did not see the edge of the cliff. They fell off the cliff with the leaf. The bird and the squirrel were not happy. They both lost the leaf and got hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a very boring house with no toys or friends to play with. One day, Spot found a small hole in the fence and decided to escape. He wanted to find a place where he could celebrate and have fun. Spot walked and walked until he found a big park. There, he saw other animals playing and having a great time. Spot was so happy! He ran to a friendly cat and said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" The cat said, "Yes, of course! Let's celebrate and have fun together!" So, Spot and the cat played all day long. At the end of the day, Spot realized that he missed his home. He said goodbye to the cat and went back to the boring house. Spot learned that even if his house was not as fun as the park, it was still his home and he loved it. Spot also learned that making friends and having fun is important, but so is being with family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball. He loved to play with the ball every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. Tim and Sue looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in Tim's pocket. But they could not find Sue's toy. Tim had an idea. He thought maybe the toy was in the big box in the room. The box was shut very tight. Tim and Sue worked together to open the box. Inside the box, they found Sue's toy! Sue was so happy. Tim was happy too because he helped his new friend. They played together with their toys all day. They promised to never be rude to each other and to always be kind. Tim and Sue became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always ready to play with his toys. One day, he saw a phone on the table. It was big and shiny. Tim wanted to play with the phone. Tim picked up the phone and started to push the buttons. Suddenly, the phone made a loud noise. Tim began to panic. He did not know what to do. He called out, "Mom, help me!" Mom came running into the room. She saw Tim holding the phone. "Don't worry, Tim," she said. "I will show you how to use the phone." She took the phone and showed Tim how to make it stop making noise. Tim was happy and not scared anymore. They both laughed and played with the phone together.
