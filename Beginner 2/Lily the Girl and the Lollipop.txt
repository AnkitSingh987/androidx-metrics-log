Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Lollipop

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat lollipops. One day, she found a big, red lollipop in her room. Lily was very happy. Lily wanted to attach the lollipop to her shirt, so she could eat it later. Her mom said, "No, Lily. That will make your shirt dirty." Lily was upset, but she put the lollipop in her pocket instead. Later, Lily saw her friend Tim. She wanted to share her lollipop with him. But when she took it out of her pocket, the lollipop was broken. Lily and Tim were both very upset. They could not eat the broken lollipop, and their day was not fun.

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big box on the road. He thought, "I want to play with that thing." So, he went near the box and looked inside. Inside the box, there were many colorful balls. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the balls. But, the box was too small for him to fit in. He tried to push and squeeze, but he could not fit inside the box. Then, Tim had an idea. He took the balls out of the box and played with them on the grass. He had so much fun playing with the balls. He thought, "I don't need to fit in the box to play with these things."

Once upon a time, there was a nosy boy named Tim. He loved to ride his bicycle around the park. One day, while he was riding, he saw a big, red ball in his friend's yard. Tim thought the ball looked fun, so he decided to take it. "Hey, that's my ball!" said his friend, Sam. But Tim didn't listen. He put the ball on his bicycle and rode away very fast. Sam was sad and didn't know what to do. Later, Tim was playing with the ball in the park. Suddenly, the ball bounced away and went into the street. Tim didn't look before he ran after it. He got hit by a car and his bicycle broke into pieces. Tim was hurt and he cried. He knew he shouldn't have taken the ball without asking.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a wild dog running and playing. Tim liked the dog and waved at it. The dog wagged its tail and came to Tim. "Hello, dog!" said Tim. The dog barked happily. Tim's mom smiled and said, "Be nice to the dog, Tim." Tim and the dog played in the park for a while. Then, Tim's mom said, "It's time to go home, Tim." Tim waved goodbye to the dog. The dog barked and wagged its tail. At home, Tim's mom made some coffee for herself. She gave Tim a cup of milk. They sat together and talked about their fun day at the park with the wild dog. Tim said, "I liked waving at the dog, mom." His mom smiled and said, "I liked it too, Tim."

Once upon a time, there was a small white house. Inside lived a small family with a 3 year old daughter named Grace. Each day, Grace would look out the window to watch the cars drive by. She always wondered where the cars were going. One day, Grace asked her mom, "Where do the cars go?" Her mom smiled and replied, "They're going to get gas, sweetheart." Grace thought about this and then made a request. "Can we go get gas too?" Her mom was a bit surprised but said, "Of course we can go, but only this once." Grace was so excited! She quickly got ready and they drove to the gas station. She smiled as they stopped at the gas pump. Grace jumped out of the car and ran up to the big white pump. She reached up with all her strength and said, "Mommy, I want to get gas!" Her mom smiled, stepped out of the car, and said, "Grace, you did it!" She had finally learned how to get gas all by herself.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. Sam was a strong boy who liked to help his mom. One day, his mom asked him to tie a pretty bow on a vase. She wanted to give the vase to her friend as a gift. Sam said, "Mom, I will help you tie the bow on the vase." He took a long ribbon and tied a big, pretty bow around the vase. The bow looked very nice, and the vase was very pretty. Sam's mom said, "Thank you, Sam! You did a great job!" She gave the vase with the pretty bow to her friend. Her friend loved the vase and the bow. Sam felt happy that he could help his mom and make her friend smile.
