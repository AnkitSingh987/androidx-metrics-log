Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a book called a journal. Lucy liked to draw and write in her journal every day. One day, Lucy went to the park with her journal. She saw a frightened little boy sitting on a bench. He was crying. Lucy went to the boy and asked, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I lost my toy car." Lucy wanted to make the boy smile. She had an idea! She opened her journal and drew a toy car. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy car jumped out of the journal and became real! The boy was so happy. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy!" From that day on, Lucy and the boy became best friends. They played with the toy car together and had lots of fun. And Lucy kept drawing more things in her journal to make her friends smile.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to run very fast. One day, Tom found a big jar of powder in his house. The powder was colorful and pretty. Tom wanted to play with the powder. He ran fast with the jar in his hands. But he did not see a rock on the ground. Tom tripped on the rock and fell down. The jar of powder fell too. It broke into many pieces. The powder went everywhere. Tom was sad and his mom was not happy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to write. She wrote on paper, on walls, and even on the ground. One day, Mia found a lime under a big tree. Mia picked up the lime and said, "Hello, lime! I want to write about you!" The lime was very happy. It said, "Thank you, Mia! I want to be in your story!" Mia took the lime home and began to write. She wrote about the lime's adventures, like rolling down hills and playing with other fruits. The lime was so proud to be in Mia's story. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun together.

One day, a big ugly bug was very tired. He wanted to take a nap. He looked for a cozy spot and saw a bin. The bin was full of soft things. The bug thought it was a perfect place to nap. As the bug slept in the bin, a little girl named Sue walked by. Sue saw the big ugly bug and yelled, "Mom, look at the big bug in the bin!" Her mom came to see. She did not like the ugly bug in the bin. She wanted to get it out. Sue's mom got a stick and tried to move the bug. But, the ugly bug woke up and jumped out of the bin. Sue and her mom were surprised! The bug said, "I'm sorry, I just wanted to nap. I didn't mean to scare you." Sue and her mom laughed. They let the bug stay and nap in the bin.

One day, a poor cat named Tim was very thirsty. He saw a big glass of milk on the table. Tim wanted the milk, but he knew it was wrong to steal. He thought, "What should I do?" Tim's friend, a dog named Sam, walked in. He saw Tim looking at the glass of milk. Sam said, "Don't steal, Tim! That's bad!" Tim felt sad, but he knew Sam was right. Just then, a little girl came in. She saw Tim and Sam and smiled. She picked up the glass of milk and said, "This is for you, Tim! I got it for you because you're a good cat!" Tim was so happy and drank the milk. He was glad he didn't steal, and he had a new friend too.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben lived in a peaceful forest with many trees and flowers. He liked to play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, Ben found a big jar of honey. He loved honey so much! He wanted to share the honey with his friends. So, he called all his friends to come and taste the sweet honey. Ben and his friends had a big honey party. They ate the honey and played games. They were so happy and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they all said "thank you" to Ben for sharing the honey. And they lived happily ever after.
