Category: Beginner
Title: The Tidy House and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house near a creek. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his dog, Max. They loved to play near the creek every day. One day, while they were playing, a big frog jumped out of the creek. "Ribbit!" said the frog. Tim and Max were surprised. The frog said, "I am the boss of this creek. You can't play here anymore." Tim and Max did not want to fight the frog, so they asked, "Can we be friends instead?" The frog thought for a moment and then agreed. "Yes, we can be friends. Let's play together!" From that day on, Tim, Max, and the frog played happily near the creek. They had lots of fun and became the best of friends. And the creek was a happy place for everyone.

One day, a little bunny named Tim went out to play. He loved to run on the soft grass. The grass was green and delicate. Tim would hop and run all day long. As Tim was running, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small bird. The bird was sad. Tim hopped over and asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I can't fly. My wing is hurt." Tim wanted to help the bird. He had an idea. He told the bird, "I will find help. Wait here." Tim ran fast to find help. He found a kind squirrel who knew how to fix the bird's wing. The squirrel came and helped the bird. Soon, the bird could fly again. The bird was so happy. He thanked Tim and the squirrel. They all played together on the delicate grass. Tim learned that helping others can make you feel good too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lonely, little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy went to the park to play. She saw a funny path with many zigzags. She walked on the zigzag path and saw a boy named Tom. Tom was laughing. "Hi, Tom! Why are you laughing?" Lucy asked. "Hi, Lucy! I am laughing because my dog, Max, is running in zigzags. It looks funny!" Tom said. Lucy looked at Max and started to laugh too. Max was a very funny dog. Lucy and Tom played together all day. They were not lonely anymore. They learned that when you make friends, you can share laughs and have more fun.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there lived a bird named Blue. Blue loved to fly high in the sky. He had many friends, and they all liked to play together. One day, a new bird came to town. Her name was Red. Red was not very fair when they played games. She would always try to win by breaking the rules. Blue and his friends did not like this. They wanted everyone to follow the law and be nice. One day, Blue said to Red, "Red, we need to talk. When we play, you don't follow the law. We want you to be fair, so we can have fun together." Red thought about it and said, "You are right, Blue. I will try to be fair from now on." From that day on, Red started to follow the law and play fair. Blue and his friends were happy. They all flew high in the sky and played fun games together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once, there was a small dog. The dog had a toy. The toy was his. It was his mine. He loved his mine very much. He played with it every day. It was a warm toy. It made him happy. One day, a big cat came. The cat wanted to eat the dog's mine. The dog was sad. He did not want the cat to eat his mine. He wanted to keep his warm mine. The dog had an idea. He gave the cat some food. The cat liked the food. The cat did not eat the dog's mine. The dog and the cat became friends. They played with the warm mine together. The dog was happy.
