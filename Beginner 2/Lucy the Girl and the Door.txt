Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Door

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing near her dad's office. She saw the door and it was open. Lucy was a bit fearful, but she wanted to see what was inside. So, she went in and looked around. There were many big things, like a chair, a desk, and a computer. Lucy saw a big key on the desk. She thought it was fun to play with the key. She put the key in the door and turned it. "Click!" The door was now locked. Lucy was inside the office and could not get out. Her dad came and saw the door was locked. He asked, "Lucy, are you in there?" Lucy said, "Yes, Daddy, I am here. I locked the door, and I cannot open it." Her dad tried to open the door, but it was still locked. He said, "Do not worry, Lucy. I will find a way to open the door." He went to get help. Lucy's dad came back with a big man who had a tool. They worked together to unlock the door. At last, the door opened, and Lucy was free. She hugged her dad and said, "Thank you, Daddy. I am sorry for locking the door." Her dad smiled and said, "It is okay, Lucy. Just be careful next time."

Mum and Dad suggested to the three year old that it was time to go home. The little one looked around, with a big sad nose, and saw all the other children playing happily. Reluctantly he nodded his head and followed his parents out of the playground. On the way home, Mum and Dad noticed the little one's sadness. They gently asked him why he looked so sad. The little one said that he wanted to stay and play with the other children. Mum and Dad suggested that tomorrow they could return to the playground. The little one brightened up immediately and smiled. At home, he could hardly contain his excitement. With a happy grin on his face and a twitch of his noser, the little one went to bed dreaming of the exciting day ahead.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play at the park. One day, they see a big pond with many ducks. They also see a man with a small boat. The boat has a sail. The sail is red and white. "Look, Ben, look!" Lily says. "The boat is nice. Can we play with it?" Ben nods. He likes the boat too. They run to the man and ask him. The man smiles and says yes. He gives them the boat and a stick. The stick can move the boat. Lily and Ben take the boat and the stick to the pond. They put the boat in the water. They use the stick to push the boat. The boat goes fast. The sail catches the wind. The ducks swim away. Lily and Ben laugh. They have fun. They play with the boat until the sun goes down. Then they give the boat and the stick back to the man. They say thank you. The man says you're welcome. He says they can come back and play again. Lily and Ben wave goodbye. They hold hands and walk home. They are happy. They like the boat with the sail. They like the park. They like each other.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bunny named Bob. He liked to play near the creek. One day, while he was playing, he saw a big stick. Bob wanted to break the stick. Bob said to his friend Cat, "Help me break this stick!" Cat and Bob jumped on the stick. Snap! The stick broke, but it made a very loud noise. They did not know the stick was magic. The loud noise woke up a big, mean bear. The bear was very angry. The bear chased Bob and Cat away from the creek. They could not play there anymore. Bob and Cat were sad, and they learned to be careful with things they did not know.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an incredible whistle that he loved to play with. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and took his whistle with him. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. "I can make my whistle sing!" Tim said. Sue wanted to try too. But when Sue tried to blow the whistle, no sound came out. They tried and tried, but still no sound. Tim was sad and wanted to quit. Just then, a bird flew down and took the whistle from Tim's hand. The bird blew into the whistle, and it made a beautiful sound. Tim and Sue were so surprised! They laughed and played with the bird, and the whistle sang all day.
