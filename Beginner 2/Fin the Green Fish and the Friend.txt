Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Green Fish and the Friend

Once upon a time, there was a little green fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. He had many friends who liked to swim with him. One day, Fin saw a big fish who looked sad. Fin asked the big fish, "Why are you sad?" The big fish said, "I lost my friend. Can you help me find her?" Fin wanted to help, so he said, "I promise I will help you find your friend." Fin and the big fish swam all around the sea. They asked other fish if they had seen the big fish's friend. Finally, they found her near some pretty rocks. The big fish was so happy and said, "Thank you, Fin, for keeping your promise." From that day on, Fin, the big fish, and the friend all swam together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved the park because it had a big tree with lots of peaches. She saw a peach on the ground and picked it up. Mia was very happy. Mia showed the peach to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a peach!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Mia! But remember, we should not steal. We need to ask if it's okay to take the peach." Mia thought about it and said, "Okay, Mom. I will ask." She went to the park lady and asked, "Can I have this peach, please?" The park lady said, "Yes, you can have it. Be safe and have fun!" Mia was very happy that she did not steal the peach. She ate the peach and played at the park with her mom. They had a fun day and Mia learned that it's important to ask before taking something.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big shiny metal thing in his yard. It was enormous! He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was pretty. Tim wanted to keep it and show it to his friends. But then, a big man came to Tim's house. He was looking for his lost metal thing. The big man was sad because he needed it for his work. He asked Tim if he saw the big metal thing. Tim was scared and didn't want to give it back. Tim's mom saw the big man and Tim. She told Tim that it was nice to help others. Tim thought about it and decided to give the big metal thing back to the big man. The big man was so happy! He smiled and thanked Tim for being kind. Tim felt good for helping the big man. He learned that it was important to be nice and help others. Tim's mom was proud of him and they all had a happy day.

One day, a girl named Sue went to a big hotel with her mom and dad. They were going to stay there for a few days. The hotel was full of people who wanted to win a fun game. The game was called "The Best Pillow Fort". Everyone wanted to make the best fort out of pillows and blankets. Sue and her mom started to build their fort. They used many soft pillows and warm blankets. Sue's dad called out, "Are you ready to show your fort?" Sue and her mom said, "Yes!" They were excited to show their fort to everyone. Just as they were about to show their fort, a big dog ran into the room. The dog jumped on the fort and it fell down! Everyone laughed and clapped. The dog was so happy and wagged its tail. Sue's mom said, "We didn't win, but we had fun!" Sue agreed, and they all played with the dog and built a new fort together.

Once upon a time, there was a package. Everyone was very happy when they saw the package because it was clean and smelled nice. But then one day a big bad monster came by and wanted to destroy the package. The monster grabbed the package and started shaking it and growling. The people around were scared, but then a hero came to save the day. The hero was very brave and chased the monster away. The package was safe and the people were happy. The end.
