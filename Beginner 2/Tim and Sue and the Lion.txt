Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sue and the Lion

Tim and Sue were playing in the park. They saw a big tent with many colors. They wanted to go inside and see what was there. "Come on, Tim, let's look!" Sue said. "OK, Sue, but be careful. Maybe there are scary things inside," Tim said. They ran to the tent and lifted the flap. They saw a man with a funny hat and a long whip. He was making a lion jump through a hoop. There were many people watching and clapping. "Wow, Sue, look! It's a circus!" Tim said. "Wow, Tim, look! It's a lion!" Sue said. They were so excited that they did not see the sign that said "Do not enter". The man with the whip saw them and was angry. "Hey, you two, get out of here! This is not a place for children. You are foolish to come here. You could get hurt by the lion," he shouted. He cracked his whip in the air. The lion roared. Tim and Sue were scared. They ran out of the tent as fast as they could. "Sorry, mister, sorry!" they said. They ran back to their mom, who was waiting for them on a bench. She hugged them and asked them what happened. "We saw a circus, mom. But the man with the whip was mad at us. He said we were foolish," Sue said. "We were not foolish, mom. We just wanted to look," Tim said. Mom smiled and kissed them. "I know, my loves. But you have to be careful and follow the rules. The circus is not for children. It is for grown-ups who pay money. Maybe when you are older, we can go and see it together. OK?" she said. "OK, mom," they said. They learned their lesson and played in the park. They did not go near the tent again. They had fun with their mom and each other. They were happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim brought his favorite toy, a long rod. He loved to play with it because he could be very creative. He would pretend the rod was a sword, a wand, or even a fishing pole. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sue, so he walked up to her and asked, "Can I play with you?" Sue said, "Yes! Let's play together!" They were having so much fun playing with the ball and the rod. Suddenly, the ball rolled away and went near a big tree. Tim and Sue ran to get it. But when they got close, they saw a little kitten stuck in the tree. The kitten was scared and yelled, "Meow! Meow!" Tim had an idea. He used his creative mind and the rod to save the kitten. He held the rod up high and the kitten grabbed on. Tim gently pulled the kitten down from the tree. They all became friends and played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a boy named Tom put on a suit. He wanted to look nice for his mom's birthday. The suit was blue and had a big red bow. Tom felt happy in his suit. Tom went outside to play. It was an icy day. He saw his friends and they all played together. They ran and jumped on the icy ground. Tom was having fun, but he forgot about his suit. Tom's friend slipped on the ice and fell. Tom tried to help, but he slipped too. His suit got dirty and wet. He started to cry and shout, "My suit is ruined!" Tom's friends tried to help, but the suit was too dirty. Tom went home, still crying. His mom saw him and hugged him. She was sad that his suit was dirty, but she still loved him. Tom felt bad that he ruined his suit on his mom's birthday. The day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin named Dolly. Dolly lived in the big blue sea. She had lots of fish friends. One day, Dolly saw a thick rope in the water. The rope was not good for the sea or her friends. Dolly wanted to improve the sea for everyone. She had an idea. She called her fish friend, Finny. "Finny, can you help me get rid of the thick rope?" Dolly asked. Finny said, "Yes, Dolly! Let's work together!" Dolly and Finny worked together to pull the thick rope out of the sea. They pulled and pulled until the rope was gone. All the fish friends were happy. The sea was better now. Dolly and Finny were very proud. They knew that working together can improve things for everyone.
