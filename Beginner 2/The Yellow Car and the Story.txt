Category: Beginner
Title: The Yellow Car and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a yellow car. The yellow car needed gas to go. The car's owner, Tom, went to the gas station to provide gas for his car. At the gas station, Tom saw a little girl crying. He asked her, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy." Tom wanted to help, so he looked around and found the toy. He gave it to the little girl, and she was happy. The moral of the story is to always help others when you can. By helping the little girl, Tom made her happy, and he felt good too.

Once, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to wear pretty things. One day, she found some junk in her room. The junk had a fake, shiny necklace. Sue was happy and put it on. Sue went to her friend, Tom. "Look at my necklace," she said. Tom looked at it and said, "It looks fake, but it's nice." Sue felt sad, but she still wore the necklace. Later, Sue and Tom went to play near the lake. The necklace fell into the water. Sue tried to get it, but she fell in too. Tom could not help her. Sue lost the fake necklace and got wet.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little owl named Ollie. Ollie lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, Ollie heard his friend, Bunny, complain. "I am scared of the dark," said Bunny. Ollie wanted to help, so he said, "Don't worry, Bunny, I will show you that the dark is not scary." Ollie took Bunny for a walk at night. They saw the moon and the stars. Bunny started to feel better. They played together in the dark and had fun. In the end, Bunny was not scared anymore. The moral of the story is to help your friends when they are scared or sad. Be brave like Ollie and show them that there is nothing to be afraid of.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Bob. He liked to cut hair. But Bob's haircuts were expensive. People did not want to pay so much. One day, a man named Tom came to Bob's shop. Tom did not have much money. He asked Bob for a haircut. Bob said, "My haircuts are expensive. Can you pay?" Tom said, "No, but I can give you something else." Tom showed Bob a big, shiny rock. It seemed very special. Bob thought about it. He wanted the shiny rock. So, Bob cut Tom's hair and got the shiny rock. But then, people found out about the shiny rock. They wanted one too. They came to Bob's shop and said, "We will give you shiny rocks for haircuts." Bob was happy. He cut their hair and got more shiny rocks. Now, Bob's haircuts were not expensive anymore. People liked to pay with shiny rocks. Bob was happy, and his shop was always busy. Everyone loved their haircuts and their shiny rocks.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his huge bookcase. He wanted to find a good book to read. The bookcase was full of colorful books about animals, cars, and fun things. Tim loved all of his books. He picked a book about a snowman. The snowman was very big and had a big smile. The snowman lived in a cold place with lots of snow. But one day, the sun came out and it got very warm. The snowman started to melt. He got smaller and smaller. Tim felt sad for the snowman. He wanted to help him. So, he put on his boots, hat, and gloves. He went outside and made a new snowman friend. Tim was happy that he could make a new snowman and play with him all day.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and a big bed. Sometimes they make a fort with their pillows and blankets. They pretend they are in a castle or a cave. One day, Anna and Ben are bored. They want to do something new. They see a pair of scissors on the table. They think scissors are fun. They can cut paper and make shapes. They wonder what else they can cut. "Look, Ben, I have an idea!" Anna says. She picks up a pillow and holds it in front of her. "Let's cut the pillow and see what's inside!" Ben thinks that sounds exciting. He grabs the scissors and nods. "OK, Anna, let's do it!" He says. He starts to cut the pillow. He makes a big hole in it. White fluff comes out of the pillow. It is soft and warm. Anna and Ben laugh. They think the fluff is funny. They throw it in the air and catch it. They make a pile of fluff on the floor. They roll in it and hug it. They feel happy and cozy. But then they hear a loud voice. It is their mom. She comes into the room and sees the mess. She is angry and shocked. She sees the scissors and the pillow. She sees the fluff everywhere. "Anna! Ben! What have you done?" She yells. "You cut the pillow! You made a big mess! You could have hurt yourself with the scissors! That was very naughty and dangerous!" Anna and Ben stop laughing. They feel scared and sorry. They look at their mom and the pillow. They realize they did something bad. They don't know what to say. "Mom, we're sorry. We just wanted to play. We didn't mean to cut the pillow. We didn't know it was wrong." Anna says. "Mom, we're sorry. We just wanted to see the fluff. We didn't mean to make a mess. We didn't know it was bad." Ben says. Mom sighs. She is still angry, but she is also sad. She loves her twins, but she is worried about them. She knows they are curious and creative, but they need to learn to be careful and respectful. "Anna, Ben, I know you're sorry, but you need to understand. You can't cut the pillow. It is not a toy. It is for sleeping and resting. You need the pillow to be comfortable and warm. You can't see the fluff. It is not for playing. It is for filling and supporting. You need the fluff to be soft and cozy. And you can't use the scissors. They are not for fun. They are for cutting things that are allowed and safe. You need the scissors to be sharp and handy. But you also need to be gentle and responsible. Do you understand?" Mom says. Anna and Ben nod. They understand. They feel ashamed and sorry. They want to make mom happy and proud. They want to be good and smart. "Yes, mom, we understand. We won't cut the pillow again. We won't play with the fluff again. We won't use the scissors again. We will be careful and respectful. We will be gentle and responsible. We love you, mom." Anna and Ben say. Mom smiles. She hugs her twins. She forgives them. She loves them, too. She helps them clean up the mess. She gives them a new pillow. She tucks them in the bed. She kisses them good night. "Good night, Anna. Good night, Ben. I love you, too. Sleep well and sweet dreams." Mom says. Anna and Ben close their eyes. They feel the new pillow. It is soft and warm. They feel mom's kiss. It is gentle and loving. They feel happy and cozy. They fall asleep and dream.
