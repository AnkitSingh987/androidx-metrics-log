Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Store

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some important things for their home. Lily liked to visit the store because she could see many fun things there. At the store, Lily saw a big, red ball. She wanted to play with it. Her mom said, "We can buy the ball, but first we need to get the important things." So, they went to find the important things together. After they got the important things, Lily and her mom went back to the big, red ball. Lily was happy to take it home. They left the store and went back to their house. Lily had a fun day and a fun visit to the store.

One day, a dog named Max was very excited. He had a new job to deliver things to people. Max had a special vest to help him carry the things. He put on the vest and went outside. Max met a girl named Lily. Lily said, "Hi Max! Can you deliver this toy to my friend?" Max said, "Yes, I can!" He put the toy in his vest and went to find Lily's friend. Max got lost and could not find Lily's friend. He felt sad. He tried to go back to Lily, but he could not find her either. Max was all alone with the toy in his vest. The toy was never delivered, and Max felt bad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to walk on the beach and look for shells. One day, she saw a big, unknown shell. She had never seen a shell like this before. It was very pretty. Sue picked up the shell and saw a small sign on it. The sign said, "Rub me." So, Sue rubbed the shell. Suddenly, a funny little crab came out of the shell. The crab could talk! "Thank you for helping me," said the crab. "I was stuck in that shell for a long time. Now I can go back to my family." Sue was very happy to help the crab. She and the crab became good friends, and they played on the beach together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a weak little boy named Tim. He lived with his mom and dad. Tim was scared of the dark. One night, Tim heard a sound in his room. He saw a big shadow on the wall. Tim was very scared. He called out to his mom, "Mom, help! There is something in my room!" His mom came running in and turned on the light. The shadow was gone. The next day, Tim's toy was missing. He asked his mom, "Did the shadow steal my toy?" His mom smiled and said, "No, Tim. Shadows don't steal toys. Maybe you just misplaced it." Tim looked around and found his toy under his bed. He was happy and not scared of the dark anymore.

Lily wanted to go to the party. She liked parties. She liked cake and games and friends. But she had a problem. She did not have a dress. All her dresses were dirty or too small. She asked her mom for a new dress, but her mom said no. They did not have money for a new dress. Lily was sad. She looked in her closet. She saw a dress that was not hers. It was her sister's dress. Her sister was older and bigger than her. Lily had an idea. She took the dress and put it on. It was too big and too long. She used a belt and some pins to make it fit. She looked in the mirror. She smiled. She thought she looked pretty. She went to the party. She saw her friends. They saw her dress. They laughed. They said her dress was ugly and funny. They said she looked like a clown. Lily felt bad. She felt uncomfortable. She wanted to go home. She ran to the door. She tripped on her dress. She fell down. She hurt her knee. She cried. Her mom came to pick her up. She saw her dress. She saw her knee. She was angry. She asked her why she took her sister's dress. She said it was wrong and naughty. She said she had to say sorry to her sister and to the party. She said she had to go to bed without cake. Lily felt worse. She wished she had not gone to the party. She wished she had not taken the dress. She wished she had a new dress.
