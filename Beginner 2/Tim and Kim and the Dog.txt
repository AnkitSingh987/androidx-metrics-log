Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Kim and the Dog

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big truck with a picture of ice-cream on it. They ran to the truck and asked for ice-cream. The man in the truck gave them each a cone with a scoop of ice-cream on top. Tim and Kim licked their ice-cream and smiled. It was cold and sweet and yummy. They walked to the park to find a bench to sit on. But on the way, they saw a big dog. The dog was black and brown and filthy. It had mud and dirt and bugs on its fur. It barked and ran towards Tim and Kim. Tim and Kim were scared of the dog. They dropped their ice-cream and ran away. The dog chased them and tried to bite them. Tim and Kim screamed and cried for help. They shivered with fear and cold. A lady saw Tim and Kim and the dog. She called the dog and made it stop. She said the dog was her pet and it was friendly. She said she was sorry for the scare. She gave Tim and Kim a hug and a tissue. She said she would buy them new ice-cream. Tim and Kim calmed down and thanked the lady. They went with her to the truck and got new ice-cream. They said goodbye to the dog and the lady and went to the park. They ate their ice-cream and laughed. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty pony named Pat. Sue loved Pat very much. They would play together every day. One day, Sue wanted to complete a big puzzle. It had many pieces. She was very patient, but she needed help. Pat the pony saw that Sue was having a hard time. Pat wanted to help Sue, but he did not know how. Sue tried and tried, but she could not finish the puzzle. Pat the pony had an idea. He used his nose to move the pieces. Together, they worked very hard. They were patient and did not give up. At last, Sue and Pat completed the puzzle. They were so happy! Sue hugged Pat the pony, and they went outside to play. They knew that they could do anything when they worked together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She had a pretty dress that she loved. One day, she wanted to wear her dress to a party. But when she looked in her room, it was a big mess! Lucy knew she had to tidy her room to find her dress. She put her toys away, made her bed, and picked up her clothes. After she cleaned, she saw her pretty dress on a chair. Lucy was so happy to reveal her dress in her tidy room. She put on the dress and went to the party. All her friends liked her pretty dress. They played games, ate cake, and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Lucy went home and put her dress away in her tidy room.

Anna loves her kitten, Max. Max is soft and cute and likes to play with Anna. But sometimes, Max is naughty. He runs away from Anna, he scratches the sofa, and he knocks over the vase. One day, Anna and Max are playing in the garden. Anna throws a ball for Max to catch. Max runs after the ball, but he does not bring it back. He sees a bird on the fence and jumps to catch it. The bird flies away and Max falls off the fence. He lands on the other side, where Anna cannot see him. Anna is worried. She calls for Max, but he does not come. She looks for him, but she cannot find him. She thinks he is lost or hurt. She starts to cry. Max hears Anna crying. He feels sorry. He did not mean to make her sad. He wants to go back to her. He looks for a way to climb over the fence, but it is too high. He sees a hole under the fence, but it is too small. He tries to dig it bigger, but it is too hard. Then, he sees a man walking by. The man has a big dog on a leash. The dog sees Max and barks. Max is scared. He runs away from the dog, but the dog chases him. Max runs faster and faster, but the dog is faster. Max thinks he is going to end. But then, he hears Anna's voice. She is on the other side of the fence. She sees Max and the dog. She shouts for the man to stop the dog. The man hears her and pulls the leash. The dog stops and Max runs to the hole. He squeezes through and jumps into Anna's arms. Anna hugs Max and kisses him. She is happy to see him. She tells him he is a naughty kitten, but she loves him. Max purrs and licks her face. He is happy to see her. He tells her he is sorry, but he loves her. They go back to the house and play with the ball. They do not go near the fence again. The end.
