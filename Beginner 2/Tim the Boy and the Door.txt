Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big building with his mom. They needed to go up to the top floor. They saw a big box with a door. Tim's mom said, "This is an elevator. It will provide us a way to go up." They went inside the elevator and pressed the button for the top floor. The elevator went up, up, up. Tim felt like he was on a crazy ride. He laughed and said, "This is fun, Mom!" When they reached the top floor, the elevator door opened. Tim's mom said, "We are here now, Tim. Let's go do what we came for." Tim liked the elevator and wanted to ride it again. He thought it was a crazy, fun way to go up and down in the big building.

One day, a big, smelly dog named Bob went to play in the park. He saw his friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim was packing a bag with food for a picnic. "Hi, Tim!" said Bob. "Can I help you pack your bag?" "Yes, please, Bob!" said Tim. "I need to put this pipe in the bag too." Bob helped Tim pack the food and the pipe. They walked to a nice spot under a big tree. They sat down and started to eat their food. They were very happy to be together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy missile. One day, he went to the park with his mom and took his toy missile with him. He was so happy to play with it. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. She was also shy. Sue said, "Hi Tim, I like your toy missile." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue. Do you want to play with it?" Sue was happy and they played together. Suddenly, a dog came and took the toy missile. Tim and Sue were sad. They did not recognize the dog. But then, the dog came back with the toy missile and a ball. The dog wanted to play too! Tim, Sue, and the dog played together and had a lot of fun. The shy boy and girl made a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a fragile strip of paper on the ground. She picked it up and saw that there were words on it. She could not read the words, so she went to her friend Tom for help. "Tom, can you read this?" Amy asked. Tom looked at the strip and tried to read the words. "I don't know these words," Tom replied. They both felt sad because they could not read the strip. Amy and Tom went to their mom to ask for help. "Mom, can you read this?" Amy asked. Mom looked at the strip and read the words out loud. "It says, 'Be kind to your friends.'" Amy and Tom smiled and hugged each other. They were happy to know what the strip said and promised to always be kind to each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk on the path near his house every day. He liked to see the trees, the flowers, and the birds. Tim was always ready to go on the path. One day, Tim saw a little girl on the path. She was crying. Tim asked her, "What did you do?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help her. He said, "I will help you find your toy." They looked and looked for the toy. Soon, they found it under a bush. The little girl was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim felt good because he helped her. They became good friends and played together on the path every day. The moral of the story is to always be ready to help others. When you do good things for others, you will feel happy too.

Jimmy was impatient to travel. He looked for his wallet everywhere. His mom said it was in his pocket. But Jimmy couldn’t find it. He looked in his toy box and his shoe. Finally, he felt it in his pocket. He jumped up and down and cheered. Then he went to the park with Mom. He wanted to take an adventure. They hopped in the car and drove away. He could see lots of birds and trees and houses. Mom said they were on a journey. Jimmy got scared when they got to the train station. He was so excited to get on the train. He said goodbye to Mom and waved. The train was so big and fast. He watched the scenery go by out the window. At the end of the journey, he was so happy. He had gone on a long, exciting trip. He saw many new places with Mom. Now, he can’t wait to travel again!
