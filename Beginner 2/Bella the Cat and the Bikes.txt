Category: Beginner
Title: Bella the Cat and the Bikes

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Bella. Bella loved to ride her bike with a big pedal. She would ride it up and down the street all day long. One day, Bella met a new friend named Max. Max was a dog who also liked to ride bikes. Max said, "Hi Bella! Can I ride bikes with you?" Bella was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, Max! Let's ride bikes together!" As they were riding, Bella's pedal fell off her bike. She felt embarrass. Max saw her and said, "Don't worry, Bella! I can help you fix your pedal." Max helped Bella put the pedal back on her bike. They both smiled and continued to ride their bikes together. Bella was happy to have such a good friend like Max.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with an old swing. The tree was ancient, and the swing was too. The swing was in a park with grass all around it. The grass was green and soft, perfect for kids to play on. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily saw the swing and wanted to play on it. Her mom helped her sit on the swing, and then she pushed Lily. Lily felt happy as she went up and down on the swing. After a while, Lily got off the swing and started to play on the grass. She felt the grass tickle her feet as she ran around the tree. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a beautiful day at the park with the ancient tree and the swing.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tiny. Tiny lived in a house with a girl named Sue. Sue was a nice girl who loved to play with Tiny. One day, Sue saw that Tiny was sad. Tiny began to cry. Sue asked, "Tiny, why do you cry?" Tiny said, "I want tea, but the cup is enormous. I can't drink from it." Sue wanted to help Tiny, so she thought of a plan. Sue found a small cup for Tiny. She poured tea into the small cup. Tiny stopped crying and drank the tea. They both smiled and played together. Tiny was happy again, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little pepper named Pep. Pep lived in a garden with many other peppers. Pep was an obedient pepper and always stayed where he was told. One day, Pep saw a big bird fly down and pick up one of his pepper friends. Pep was scared and wanted to escape. Pep said to his friend, "We need to escape or the big bird will get us too!" His friend agreed and they started to think of a plan. They decided to hide under a big leaf so the bird could not see them. They waited and waited, but the bird did not come back. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bird came back, but it was not to get them. The bird put Pep's friend back in the garden! The bird said, "I am sorry I scared you. I just needed help finding my lost baby. Your friend helped me, and now I will leave you alone." Pep and his friend were very happy and stayed in the garden, safe and sound.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the kitchen. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big spoon. They used the spoon to pretend they were cooking, digging, or flying. One day, their mom said, "I need the spoon to make soup. Please give it to me." Ben and Lily did not want to give the spoon. They said, "No, mom, we are playing with it. It is our spoon." Mom said, "You can play with your other toys. The spoon is not a toy. It is a tool. I need it to make food for us. If you share the spoon, I will let you taste the soup later." Ben and Lily thought about it. They liked soup. They wanted to taste it. But they also liked the spoon. They did not want to share it. They looked at each other. They had an idea. They said, "OK, mom, we will share the spoon. But only if you permit us to use it first. We want to do one more thing with it." Mom said, "What do you want to do with it?" Ben and Lily said, "We want to measure how tall we are. We want to see who is taller." Mom smiled. She said, "That is a silly thing to do with a spoon. But OK, you can do it. But hurry up. The soup is almost ready." Ben and Lily took the spoon and stood next to each other. They put the spoon on their heads. They tried to see who was taller. They were surprised. They were the same height. The spoon was the same on both of their heads. They said, "Wow, we are twins. We are the same. We are both tall." They laughed. They felt happy. They gave the spoon to mom. They said, "Thank you, mom, for letting us use the spoon. We are sorry we did not want to share it. We learned something new. We are twins. We are the same. We are both tall. And we love you." Mom hugged them. She said, "I love you too, my tall twins. You are very smart and kind. I am proud of you. Now, let's go taste the soup. It is hot and yummy. And you can use your own spoons to eat it." The end. Moral value: Sharing is caring. We can learn from each other. We are all special and equal.
