Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Box

One day, a boy had a question. He could not find his sock. It was not an ordinary sock. It was his favorite sock. He looked under the bed. He looked in the toy box. But he could not find it. The boy asked his mom, "Where is my sock?" His mom did not know. She said, "Maybe your sister knows." So the boy went to his sister. He asked her the same question. But she did not know either. The boy was sad. He wanted his sock. Then he saw his dog. The dog had something in its mouth. It was the sock! The boy was happy. He got his sock back. Now he could play outside with his favorite sock on.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the color pink. One day, she went to the park with her mommy. In the park, there was a big pink statue. Lily was very happy to see it. Lily played near the pink statue all day. She met a new friend named Tim. Tim liked the pink statue too. They played together and had lots of fun. "Let's play hide and seek," said Tim. Lily agreed and started to hide. She hid behind the pink statue. Tim looked for her but could not find her. "I quit!" he said, laughing. Lily came out from behind the statue and they both laughed. Lily and Tim played with the pink statue until it was time to go home. They said goodbye to each other and the pink statue. Lily couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Ben and Lily were playing hide and seek in their house. Ben was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran fast to find a good spot. She saw a closet in the hall and opened the door. She went inside and closed the door behind her. She hoped Ben would not find her. Ben counted to ten and then shouted, "Ready or not, here I come!" He looked around the rooms and tried to hear Lily's voice. He did not see her under the bed, behind the sofa, or in the bathroom. He wondered where she was. He walked to the hall and saw the closet. He had an idea. He opened the closet door and said, "Boo!" Lily screamed and jumped out of the closet. She was scared and angry. She said, "Ben, that was not fair! You made me scared!" Ben laughed and said, "I found you, I found you! You are the loser!" Lily said, "No, you are the cheater! You can't scare me like that!" They started to argue and push each other. They did not hear their mom coming. She heard the noise and went to the hall. She saw Ben and Lily fighting by the closet. She was not happy. She said, "Stop it, both of you! What is going on here? Why are you making so much noise?" Ben and Lily stopped and looked at their mom. They felt sorry. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We were playing hide and seek and we had a fight." Mom said, "Playing is good, but fighting is bad. You have to be nice to each other and share. Now, say sorry to each other and hug." Ben and Lily said sorry and hugged. They felt better. Mom said, "That's good. Now, how about we have some cookies and milk in the kitchen?" Ben and Lily said, "Yes, please!" They followed their mom to the kitchen. They forgot about the closet and the fight. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small, noisy town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to help people and make them happy. One day, he saw a little boy who was sad. The boy had lost his toy, a sparkly car. Tom wanted to help the boy find his toy. Tom and the little boy looked all around the noisy town for the sparkly car. They looked behind trees, under benches, and in the park. Finally, they found the sparkly car in the hands of a big, mean dog. The dog wanted to keep the toy and would not give it back. Tom thought of a plan. He decided to pay the dog with a big bone to get the sparkly car back. The dog liked the bone better than the toy, so he gave the sparkly car back to the little boy. The boy was so happy, and Tom felt good for helping. The moral of the story is that being kind and helping others can make you feel happy too.
