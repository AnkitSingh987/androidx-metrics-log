Category: Beginner
Title: Look and the Bird

One day, a bird and a bee were playing near a big tree. The bird saw a cobweb and said, "Look, a cobweb! Let's dive and see what's there." The bee said, "Okay, let's go!" They both dived towards the cobweb. As they got closer, they saw a spider on the cobweb. The spider was very loud and said, "Hello, friends! Do you want to play with me?" The bird and the bee were scared but decided to play with the spider. They played a game of tag. The bird, the bee, and the spider ran and flew around the tree. Suddenly, the spider tripped and fell. The cobweb caught the spider and saved him from falling. The bird and the bee cheered, and they all laughed together. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a cat named Max. Max was a different cat because he liked to eat steak. Every day, Sue would care for Max by giving him a small piece of steak. They were very happy together. One day, Sue and Max went to the park. Sue saw her friend Tim. Tim said, "Hi Sue! I like your cat. But why does he eat steak? Cats usually eat fish." Sue said, "Max is different. He loves steak and I care for him, so I give him what he likes." Suddenly, a big dog came running towards them. The dog saw Max's steak and wanted it. But Max did something unexpected. He shared his steak with the dog! The dog was happy and became friends with Max. Sue, Max, and the dog played together in the park all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. The frog lived near a creek. The creek was deep. The frog liked to swim in the creek every day. The frog had many friends who lived in the creek too. One day, the little frog saw a big pot. The pot was by the creek. The frog wanted to cook food for his friends. He thought it would be fun. He put some water and leaves in the pot. He did not know how to cook, but he tried. The little frog put the pot in the deep creek. He thought the water would help cook the food. But the frog made a mistake. The pot began to sink in the deep creek. The frog and his friends tried to save the pot, but they could not. The pot was lost in the deep creek. The little frog was sad. His friends were sad too. The frog learned that he should not cook in the deep creek.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his original football. One day, he asked his mom, "Mom, can you permit me to play outside with my football?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't go too far." Tim went outside and saw his friend, Sam. They played with the football, but it went into the tall grass. Tim and Sam were scared to go get it. They knew they should not go too far away from home. But they wanted the football back. Tim and Sam thought about what to do. They remembered that their moms always said to ask for help when they needed it. So, they went to Tim's mom and told her what happened. She helped them get the football back from the tall grass. Tim and Sam were happy and learned that asking for help is good when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named True. True had a big round belly and loved to play with his friends. They would run and jump in the sun all day long. One day, True's best friend, a little bird, told him that if he believed, he could fly too. True thought about it and wanted to try. He believed he could fly, so he climbed to the top of a small hill and jumped. True did not fly, but he landed softly on the grass. His friends all laughed and cheered for him. True learned that even if he couldn't fly, he could still have fun and believe in himself. And that is what made True the happiest chubby cat in the world.
