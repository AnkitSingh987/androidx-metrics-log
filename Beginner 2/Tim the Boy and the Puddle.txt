Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big round ball and wanted to kick it. So, he ran and gave the ball a big kick. The ball went very high in the sky. Tim was happy and clapped his hands. As the ball came down, it landed in a cold puddle. Tim felt sad because the ball was wet and cold now. He picked it up and walked back to his house. He wanted to make the ball warm and dry again. When he got home, his mom had made a yummy snack for him. Tim was so hungry, he forgot about the ball for a moment. He ate the snack and felt better. Then, he saw the ball was not wet anymore. It was dry and warm from the sun. Tim was surprised and happy. Now, he could play with the ball again.

One day, a hairy dog named Max wanted to have a race. He asked his friend, a cat named Bella, if she wanted to play. Bella said, "Yes, let's race! But first, we need to measure how far we will go." Max and Bella used a long string to measure the race. They put the string from one tree to another tree. Max said, "Okay, that is how far we will race. Are you ready, Bella?" They started the race and ran very fast. But, something unexpected happened! A big bird flew down and picked up the string. Max and Bella stopped and looked at each other. They laughed and said, "Well, that was a funny twist! Let's just play together without the string." So, Max and Bella played together all day. They had so much fun, even without the race. And they learned that sometimes, the best games are the ones that don't go as planned.

Johnny and Mary went to the park. They had a plan to play tag. Johnny shouted, "1,2,3, go!". But Mary didn't hear because she was deaf. Johnny shouted again, but he didn't say the word. He just waved his hands. Johnny felt confused, but then he saw Mary moving away. Suddenly, Johnny ran after her. But Mary was too fast. He tried to catch up, but Mary disappeared. Johnny couldn't find her and he had no plan. Johnny came back to the park alone. He started to cry. His friends tried to comfort him, but they didn't know what had happened. Even if they knew the word, they couldn't understand the situation. Johnny felt helpless and sad. He was sure that he would never see his friend again.

One day, a little cat named Tim went for a walk. The sun was out, and it was a mild day. Tim saw a big pole in the park. He wanted to climb it to see what was on top. "Can you support me?" Tim asked his friend, a dog named Max. Max said, "Yes, I will help you." So, Max held the pole while Tim climbed up. When he got to the top, Tim saw a big, red ball. "Max, there is a big ball up here!" Tim yelled. Max was excited and wanted the ball too. Just then, a bird flew by and took the ball away. Tim and Max were surprised. "Let's go find the bird and get our ball back!" said Max. Tim agreed, and they both went on a new adventure to find the bird and the big, red ball.

One day, a worried boy named Tim was walking in the park. He saw a big, old house. Tim heard a noise and felt scared. He thought there might be a ghost in the house. He wanted to know, so he went inside the house. Inside the house, Tim saw a white thing. It looked like a ghost! Tim said, "Who are you?" The ghost said, "I am a ghost. I am lonely. Will you be my friend?" Tim felt less scared and said, "Yes, I can be your friend." Just then, Tim's mom arrived at the house. She saw the ghost and said, "That is not a ghost! That is a big, white sheet!" She pulled the sheet away, and there was a nice, old lady. The lady said, "I am not a ghost. I am just playing. Thank you for being my friend." Tim and the lady became good friends and played together in the big, old house.
