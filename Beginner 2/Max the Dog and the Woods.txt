Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Woods

One day, a dog named Max was walking in the woods. He saw smoke in the air. Max was confused. He did not know where the smoke was coming from. He wanted to find out. Max saw a rabbit named Lily. He asked Lily, "Do you know where the smoke is coming from?" Lily nodded her head. She said, "I saw a fire near the big tree." Max and Lily decided to go and see the fire. When they got to the big tree, they saw a big fire. They were scared. Suddenly, the wind blew very hard. The fire went out by itself. But then, the big tree fell down on top of Max and Lily. They could not run away in time. The woods were safe from the fire, but Max and Lily were not safe from the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was an honest boy who loved to help his mom. One day, Tom's mom gave him a map. She asked him to go to the store and buy some food for their pet cat. Tom was excited to go on this big trip. Tom followed the map and went to the store. But on the way, he saw a big dog. The dog was hungry and wanted to eat the cat food. Tom did not want the dog to eat the cat food, so he thought of a plan. Tom found some bread on the ground. He decided to feed the dog the bread instead. The dog was happy and ate the bread. Tom saved the cat food and went back home. His mom was proud of him for being honest and helping their cat. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl named Lucy. She was very small and very adorable. She was very happy because she had a good life. Every day, Lucy went to the park to play. She would slide down the slide and swing on the swings. But one day when Lucy asked her mom to go to the park, her mom said no. Lucy was sad and asked her mom why. Her mom said, "The park is too busy and there are too many people there. I refuse to take you there. I want your life to be safe." Lucy was disappointed, but she understood and stayed home. Instead of going to the park, Lucy stayed at home and drew pictures with her crayons. She also wrote stories and sang songs. She had fun and it was still an adorable day! The end.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play in the woods. They found a hut made of sticks and leaves. It looked old and empty. "Let's go inside and see what's there," Ben said. "OK, but be careful. It might fall down," Anna said. They opened the door and went in. It was dark and dusty. They saw a table, a chair, a bed and a chest. "Wow, look at this!" Ben said. He opened the chest and found a blue dress, a blue hat and a blue necklace. "They are so pretty. I want to try them on." He put on the dress, the hat and the necklace. He looked at himself in a mirror. "I look like a princess," he said. Anna laughed. "You look silly. You are a boy, not a girl. Take them off." "No, I like them. They are mine now," Ben said. He did not listen to Anna. He ran out of the hut, wearing the blue things. Anna followed him. She was angry and worried. "Ben, come back. You can't keep them. They belong to someone else. What if they come back and see you?" But Ben did not care. He ran and ran, feeling happy. He did not see the big hole in the ground. He fell in and hurt his leg. He cried and cried. Anna came and helped him out. She took off the blue things and put them back in the chest. She hugged him and said, "Are you OK, Ben?" Ben nodded. He was in pain and scared. "I'm sorry, Anna. I was bad. I should have listened to you. I regret taking the blue things. They were not mine. They were not fun. They were trouble." Anna smiled. "It's OK, Ben. I forgive you. You are my friend. Let's go home and tell our moms what happened. They will make us feel better." They left the hut and walked back to their homes. They learned a lesson. They never went back to the hut again.
