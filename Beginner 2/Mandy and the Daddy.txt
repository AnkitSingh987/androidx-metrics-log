Category: Beginner
Title: Mandy and the Daddy

Mandy couldn't wait for the big day. It was going to be an amazing day. She woke up, got dressed and ran to the living room to find Daddy. "Daddy, where are you?" she asked. "I'm here Mandy," Daddy replied with a big smile on his face. Mandy was so excited. They were going to the playground. It was going to be so much fun. At the playground, they played lots of games and Mandy had the best time. When it was time to go home, Daddy said it was time for Mandy to get some sleep. Mandy was sad to go home, but she knew that sleep was important. As Daddy tucked her in, he said, "Sleep tight Mandy, have an amazing dream." Mandy fell asleep dreaming of all the fun she had with Daddy at the playground.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play outside with her friends. One day, Mia saw a dead tree in the park. She wanted to help the tree, so she went to her mommy for help. Mia's mommy gave her a piece of paper with a picture of a tree on it. She told Mia to put the paper on the dead tree. Mia did what her mommy said and put the paper on the tree. Then, she stumbled on a rock and fell down. But she got up and kept going. After some days, Mia saw that the dead tree started to look better. It grew new leaves and became green again. Mia was happy that she helped the tree. The moral of the story is that even a little help can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a young bird named Tim. Tim lived in a high tree with his family. He loved to look at the sky and dream of flying. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time for you to learn how to fly." Tim was very excited but also a little scared. His mom said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Tim and his mom went to the edge of the tree. Tim looked down and saw the ground far below. His mom said, "You can do it, Tim. Just flap your wings and jump." Tim flapped his wings and jumped. He was flying! He felt so happy and free. Tim flew around the tree with his mom. They laughed and played together in the sky. Tim learned that with practice and help from his mom, he could do anything. From that day on, Tim loved to fly high in the sky and explore the world around him.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who was looking for something to eat. He hopped through the forest, looking for food but he couldn't find anything. He was just about to leave when he noticed a long vine on the ground that had little round things growing on it. The bunny was excited and hopped closer to inspect. He realized the round things were actually fake berries! He was disappointed, until he realized something. He could eat the vine itself! He went up to the vine and started to feed on it. He ate and ate until he was full and content. The little bunny hopped away, satisfied knowing that he had found food.

Tom has a big toy car. He likes to play with it every day. He makes it go fast and loud. He does not let anyone else touch it. He says it is his and he owns it. One day, he goes to the park with his mom. He sees a girl with a small toy car. It is pink and shiny. It is different from his car. He wants to see it. He runs to the girl and tries to grab her car. "Hey, stop!" the girl says. "This is my car. I own it. You can't have it." Tom does not listen. He pulls hard on the car. The girl pulls back. They both fall on the grass. The car breaks in two. They both cry. Mom comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken car and the sad kids. She is not happy. She tells Tom to say sorry to the girl. She tells the girl to say sorry to Tom. She says they can share the car if they fix it. Tom and the girl look at each other. They feel bad. They say sorry. They hug. They try to fix the car. They use tape and glue. They make it look new. They take turns to play with it. They have fun. They smile. They become friends.
