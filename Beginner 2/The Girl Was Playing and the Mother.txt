Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Was Playing and the Mother

One day, a little girl was playing in her backyard. Suddenly, she heard her mother calling her name. She got up and started running to find her mother. As she walked around the corner of the house, she found her mother. The little girl said, “Hi, Mommy.” Her mother said, “Hi, sweetheart. I’m so glad to see you. What have you been doing?” “I was playing with my dolls,” the little girl said. Her mother gave her a big hug. “I’m so glad I found you. Let's go inside now.” The little girl smiled and followed her mom inside.

Lily was a big girl. She had a new baby brother. His name was Ben. Lily liked Ben, but sometimes she was mad. Ben cried a lot. He took mother's time. He made a mess. Lily wanted to play with mother, but mother was busy with Ben. One day, Lily was very mad. She took Ben's toy and threw it away. Ben cried louder. Mother came and saw what Lily did. Mother was not happy. She said, "Lily, that was not nice. You hurt Ben's feelings. You need to share and be kind. Ben is your brother. He loves you. You need to love him too." Lily did not understand. She said, "But mother, I love you. I want you to play with me. Ben is always in the way. He is too small and noisy. He is not fun. He is not my brother. He is a new baby." Mother hugged Lily and said, "Lily, I love you too. You are my big girl. You are fun and smart and brave. But Ben is also my baby. He is your brother. He is new and he needs me. He needs you too. He is learning and growing. He will be fun and smart and brave too. You can help him. You can teach him. You can play with him. He is not in the way. He is part of our family. We are all together. We all love each other." Lily thought about what mother said. She looked at Ben. He stopped crying. He smiled at Lily. He reached for his toy. Lily picked up the toy and gave it to Ben. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I was mean. You can have your toy. You are my brother. I love you. Do you want to play with me?" Ben giggled and nodded. He held Lily's hand. Lily smiled and said, "Come on, Ben. Let's play. Mother, can we play with you too?" Mother smiled and said, "Of course, my loves. Let's play together. I'm so proud of you, Lily. You are a good sister. You are a good daughter. You are a good person. You understand."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, shiny shield. He liked to play with his shield every day. One day, as Tim was playing with his shield, he saw a smelly cat. The cat was very dirty and sad. Tim wanted to help the cat. He said, "Hello, cat! I will help you." Tim took the cat home and gave it a bath. The cat was so happy and not smelly anymore. Tim and the cat played all day with the shield. The next day, Tim's mom had a surprise for him. She brought a new friend for Tim and the cat to play with. It was a little dog! Now Tim, the cat, and the dog all played together with the shield every day. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Amy. She was a happy girl who liked to play outside. She hoped she could find something fun to do. One day, Amy went to the park. She saw a shiny bottle in the grass. She picked it up and opened it. Inside was something spicy! Amy was excited and ran home to tell her mum. Her mum was very happy. She said it was a surprise for dinner and that it would taste yummy. Amy hoped dinner would be ready soon. Later that day, Amy, her mum and dad ate dinner. It was spicy and delicious. Amy ate all of it, she was so happy. The end!
