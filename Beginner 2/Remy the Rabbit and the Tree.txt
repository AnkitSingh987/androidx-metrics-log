Category: Beginner
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Remy and his friends decided to start a new game. They wanted to find the perfect tree to play near. Remy and his friends started to look for the perfect tree. They saw a big tree, but it was too tall. They saw a small tree, but it was too short. Then, they saw a tree that was just right, with a big pass for them to run through. Remy and his friends played near the perfect tree all day long. They ran through the pass, played hide and seek, and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they all went home, excited to play again the next day. And that's how Remy and his friends found the perfect tree to play near.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Kitty. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Kitty met a big dog named Max. Max liked to run and jump, but Kitty liked to stay and watch. "Hi, Kitty!" said Max. "Do you want to play with me?" "No, thank you," said Kitty. "I like to stay here in the sun." So, Max ran and jumped while Kitty stayed in the sun. They both had fun. At the end of the day, they sat together and watched the sun go down. Kitty and Max were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They were good friends. One day, they went for a walk in the park. They saw a shiny jewel on the ground. The cat said, "Look, a pretty jewel!" The little dog was alert. He said, "We should find who it belongs to." They asked the other animals in the park. A squirrel said, "I saw a girl drop it." So, they went to find the girl. They found the girl crying. The big cat said, "Is this your jewel?" The girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you!" The cat and dog enjoyed helping the girl. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved. It was a red car, and Tim thought it was a lucky car. He would race the car all around his house. One day, Tim saw a big file on his dad's desk. Tim thought it would be fun to race his car on the file. So, he put the file on the floor and raced his car on it. But, the car got stuck in the file. Tim was sad. He didn't want his lucky car to be stuck. He tried to get the car out, but it was too hard. Tim's dad came and helped him. He told Tim that racing on the file was not a good idea. Tim learned that it is important to be careful with other people's things. He was happy to have his lucky car back and promised to race it only in the right places.

One day, a fat cat named Tim found a pair of pants. He wanted to attach them to his body, so he could look like people. He asked his friend, a dog named Sam, for help. "Sam, can you help me attach these pants?" Tim said. Sam nodded and helped Tim put on the pants. They both laughed and played, happy to see Tim wearing pants like people. After a while, Tim's mom called them for a snack. Tim tried to eat, but the pants were too tight. He asked Sam to help him take off the pants. Sam helped, and they both enjoyed their snack. They learned that it's more fun to be themselves.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bunny named Binky. Binky loved to dress up in fun clothes. Every day, Binky would put on a new outfit and go outside to play with his friends. One day, Binky found a big hammer in his yard. He picked it up and showed it to his friends. They all thought it was a fun toy. Binky and his friends decided to use the hammer to build a small house for their toys. Binky dressed in his favorite outfit and started to work. His friends helped him too. They hammered and nailed until the house was done. Binky and his friends were very proud of the house they built. They played with their toys in the new house all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.
