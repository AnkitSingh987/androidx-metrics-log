Category: Beginner
Title: Jazz and the Ball

Once upon a time, a messy cat named Jazz liked to play in the mud. He would jump and dance in the wet, brown dirt. After playing, he would feel happy but very dirty. One sunny day, Jazz played in the mud again. He saw a big, red ball and wanted to play with it. But first, he needed to be dry and clean. So, he went to sit in the sun to dry off his muddy fur. As Jazz was drying, a big, loud bird came and took the red ball away. Jazz was sad and still messy. He didn't get to play with the ball, and now he had to find a new toy to play with. The end.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Sarah and Toby. They lived in a beautiful village by the sea and every day they were together. One day, Sarah and Toby went out for a walk and as they were walking, they began to discuss what had happened the week before. Toby explained that they had had a really big flood and their village had been filled with blue water. Sarah listened and then added that everyone had had to go and stay in another village until the water receded. Then, Sarah and Toby decided to go back to the village and help clean up the mess the flood had caused. They spent the day clearing up and helping anyone who had been affected. They even found some blue shells in the mud. After a long day of cleaning, Sarah and Toby said goodbye and hugged. Sarah said “We should always remember to be kind to each other and the environment and help each other when things are hard.” And they did.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a bell that he loved to ring. One day, he went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue who was sad. "Why are you sad?" Tim asked her. "I lost my toy," Sue said with a harsh cry. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. He had an idea. "Let's look for your toy together," Tim said. They looked and looked, but they could not find Sue's toy. Tim had another idea. "Let me ring my bell. Maybe your toy will hear it and come back." Tim rang his bell as loud as he could. Suddenly, they heard a noise in the bushes. Out came Sue's toy, a little dog named Spot. Spot had heard the bell and found his way back to Sue. Sue was happy and thanked Tim. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends, and they always played together at the park.

Sam was restless. He did not want to nap. He wanted to play with his toys. He saw his dad's hammer on the floor. He picked it up and ran to his room. "Sam, what are you doing with that hammer?" his mom asked. She followed him to his room. She saw him hit his bed with the hammer. "Bang, bang, bang!" Sam said. "Sam, stop that! You will break your bed!" his mom said. She took the hammer from him. "This is not a toy. This is for fixing things. You can hurt yourself or make a hole." Sam was sad. He wanted to play with the hammer. He started to cry. "I want the hammer! I want the hammer!" he said. His mom hugged him. She rubbed his back. "Shh, shh, it's okay. I know you are bored. But you need to rest. How about we read a story instead? You can choose one from your shelf." Sam nodded. He wiped his tears. He went to his shelf. He picked a book with a picture of a truck. He liked trucks. He gave the book to his mom. She read it to him. He listened. He felt sleepy. He closed his eyes. He fell asleep. His mom kissed him. She put the hammer away. She smiled. She was glad he was calm.
