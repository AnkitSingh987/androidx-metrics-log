Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bag

One day, a boy named Tim had a big bag. He liked his big bag very much. He put many toys in it. He took the bag everywhere he went. One day, Tim went to the park with his big bag. He played with his friends. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye friends!" His friends said, "Bye Tim!" But Tim did not see that he left his big bag at the park. He walked home without it. When he got home, he looked for his big bag. He could not find it. Tim was very sad. He missed his big bag and all his toys.

Mum and Dad were talking. "We should buy lights for the bedroom!" said Dad. "But they need to be cheap," he added. Mum smiled. "That sounds like a good idea!" On the way home, the little boy was excited. He wanted to touch the lights when they got them. When they arrived home they opened the box. The little boy was amazed - the lights were so bright and beautiful! He wanted to touch them right away. Mum and Dad said it was alright, but he must be gentle. The boy was careful and touched the lights. They were soft and kind - he loved them! The little boy had so much fun touching the lights, and he was very glad that he got such a cheap and wonderful surprise!

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a mild dog who liked to play with his friends. One day, Max wanted to eat his food. He looked for his bowl, but he could not find it. Max saw a spoon on the floor. He had a good idea. Max would use the spoon to eat his food. He went to his food bag and tried to fill the spoon with food. It was hard for Max because he was a dog. Max did not give up. He kept trying to fill the spoon with food. After a while, Max got some food on the spoon. He was very happy. Max ate his food with the spoon and shared some with his friends. They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a grumpy thief. He liked to take things that were not his. People did not like him because he was always in a bad mood and took their toys. One day, a little girl saw the grumpy thief taking a toy from her friend. She felt sad for her friend and decided to care for him. She went to the thief and asked, "Why are you so grumpy? Can I help you?" The thief was surprised. No one had ever asked him that before. He thought about it and said, "I am grumpy because I have no friends. I take toys because I want to play with them, but no one wants to play with me." The little girl felt sorry for the grumpy thief. She said, "If you give back the toys, I will be your friend and play with you." The thief agreed and gave back all the toys he had taken. From that day on, the thief was not grumpy anymore, and he had a new friend who cared for him. They played together every day, and everyone in the town was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat had a lot of friends. They liked to talk and play all day. One day, the rat found a bitter fruit. He did not like it. He wanted to share it with his friends to see if they liked it too. The rat talked to his friends about the bitter fruit. They all tried it and did not like it either. They laughed and played, happy to be together. And they never ate the bitter fruit again.

One day, a gifted bunny named Sam loved to skip in the big park. He would skip all day long with his long arms. He was very happy and always had a big smile on his face. While skipping, Sam met a little girl named Mia. Mia saw Sam skipping and wanted to join him. She asked, "Can I skip with you, Sam?" Sam was happy to have a new friend and said, "Yes, Mia! Let's skip together!" So, Sam and Mia skipped hand in arm, laughing and playing in the park. They were the best of friends and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, they always skipped together in the big park, sharing their love for skipping and their special friendship.
