Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Flowers

Lily liked to print pictures with her crayons. She had many crayons of different colors. She printed flowers, stars, animals and more. She printed on paper, on cardboard, on envelopes and sometimes on the wall. One day, Lily found a huge desk in the basement. It was her daddy's desk. He used it to work on his computer. Lily climbed on the desk and saw many papers and pens. She thought it would be fun to print on them. She took a red crayon and started to print a big heart on a paper. Then she took a blue crayon and printed a smiley face on another paper. She printed and printed until the desk was full of her pictures. She was very happy with her work. She wanted to show it to her daddy. She ran upstairs and called him. "Daddy, daddy, come and see what I printed!" Daddy followed Lily to the basement. He saw the huge desk covered with crayons and papers. He saw Lily's pictures of flowers, stars, animals and more. He was very surprised and a little angry. "Lily, what did you do?" he asked. "This is my work desk. You can't print on my papers. You have to ask me first." Lily looked at her daddy. She saw his face was not happy. She felt sad and sorry. She did not want to make him angry. She wanted to make him happy. "I'm sorry, daddy," she said. "I wanted to print you a gift. I love you." Daddy looked at Lily. He saw her face was sad and sorry. He felt bad and sorry too. He did not want to make her sad. He loved her too. He hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I love you too. Thank you for your gift. But next time, please ask me before you print on my desk. OK?" Lily nodded and said, "OK, daddy. Can I print on your wall?" Daddy laughed and said, "No, Lily. You can print on your own wall. Or better, on your own paper. Come on, let's go and find some."

Lily and Tom like to paint. They have many colors of paint and big papers. They paint flowers and cars and stars. They are happy. But one day, they make a mess. They spill some paint on the floor. The paint is wet and sticky. They try to clean it with a cloth, but it does not work. The paint makes the cloth wet and dirty too. "Oh no, what do we do?" Lily asks Tom. "I don't know, maybe we can hide it," Tom says. They look around the room. They see a big rug. They have an idea. They lay the rug over the paint on the floor. They hope no one will notice. But then, Mom comes in. She sees the rug. She thinks it is strange. "Why is the rug here?" she asks. Lily and Tom look at each other. They do not know what to say. Mom lifts the rug. She sees the paint. She is not happy. "Who did this?" she asks. Lily and Tom feel bad. They know they did something wrong. "We did, Mom. We are sorry. We wanted to paint, but we spilled some paint. We tried to hide it, but we could not," Lily says. Mom sighs. She is not angry, but she is disappointed. "Painting is fun, but you have to be careful. You have to use a mat or a tablecloth to protect the floor. And if you make a mess, you have to tell me. Hiding it is not a good idea. It makes it worse. Do you understand?" she says. Lily and Tom nod. They understand. "We are sorry, Mom. We will not do it again. We will help you clean it," they say. Mom smiles. She hugs them. "I love you, Lily and Tom. But next time, please be more careful. And don't be afraid to tell me the truth. OK?" she says. Lily and Tom hug her back. "OK, Mom. We love you too. And we like to paint. But we will be more careful," they say.

Once upon a time, there was a vendor who sold yummy treats. He had a small cart in the park. Every day, kids would come to buy his treats. They loved the vendor and his treats. The vendor was not rich, but he was happy. One day, a rich man came to the park. He saw the vendor and wanted to buy all the treats. The vendor said, "No, I can't sell all my treats to you. The kids will miss them." The rich man was not happy, but the vendor did not change his mind. The rich man learned that it is good to share and think about others. He started to share his money with people who needed help. The vendor and the kids were happy, and the rich man felt good too. The moral of the story is: it's important to share and care for others.
