Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Parrot

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big cage with a parrot inside. The parrot had bright feathers and a long tail. It could talk and say funny things. "Hello, hello!" the parrot said. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily and Ben nodded. They liked the parrot. They went closer to the cage and said hello back. The parrot smiled and said, "I have a game for you. I will say a word and you have to do it. Ready?" Lily and Ben said yes. They were curious. The parrot said, "Jump!" Lily and Ben jumped up and down. They laughed. The parrot said, "Clap!" Lily and Ben clapped their hands. They smiled. The parrot said, "Kick!" Lily and Ben kicked their feet. They giggled. But then, something bad happened. Ben kicked too hard and hit the cage. The cage shook and the door opened. The parrot flew out of the cage and flew away. "Uh-oh!" Lily and Ben said. They were scared. The parrot flew to a nearby tree and landed on a branch. It looked at Lily and Ben and said, "Bye-bye!" Lily and Ben ran to the cage and tried to close the door. They hoped the parrot would come back. But the parrot did not come back. It saw a man walking by with a bag of chips. The parrot liked chips. It flew to the man and snatched a chip from his hand. "Hey!" the man said. He was angry. The parrot ate the chip and said, "Yum, yum!" But the chip was spicy. The parrot did not like spicy. It made its mouth burn. "Ow, ow!" the parrot said. It was sad. The parrot wished it had stayed in the cage with Lily and Ben. They were nice and played fun games. The parrot wanted to go back. But it was too late. Lily and Ben had left the park. They had gone home with their mom. They were sorry they had lost the parrot. The parrot was alone and unhappy. It had learned a lesson. It should not kick the cage. It should not fly away. It should not eat spicy chips. It should be a good parrot.

One day, a cat with soft fur went to the shop. It was icy outside, so the cat was cold. The cat saw a nice, warm hat. The cat thought, "I want that hat to feel warm." The cat told the shop man, "I want that hat, please." The shop man said, "You need to pay for the hat." The cat did not have any money, so it left the shop sad. The cat went home and found a toy. The cat took the toy to the shop. The shop man said, "I will take the toy and you can have the hat." The cat was happy and warm with the new hat.

One day, a big truck named Tim wanted to reverse. Tim was very hard and strong. He had big lungs to help him go fast. Tim had a friend named Sam, who was a small car. "Beep beep," said Sam. "Can you show me how to reverse, Tim?" "Sure, Sam!" said Tim. "Watch me!" Tim showed Sam how to reverse. They went back and back. Sam tried too, but it was hard at first. Tim helped Sam and they practiced together. In the end, Sam learned how to reverse. They were both happy and played together all day. Sam's lungs got stronger and he became good at reversing too. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy gears. One day, he was careless and lost his favorite gear. Tim was very sad. Tim asked his mom to order a new gear for him. His mom said yes and ordered the gear. Tim was very happy and could not wait for the new gear to come. He played with his other toys while he waited. When the new gear came, Tim was very excited. But he was careless again and lost the new gear too. Tim cried and learned that he needed to be more careful with his toys.
