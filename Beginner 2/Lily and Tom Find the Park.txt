Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Park

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big pile of waste near the bin. It is dirty and smelly. Lily and Tom do not like it. "Who put this waste here?" Lily asks. "It is bad for the park and the animals." "I do not know," Tom says. "Maybe someone was lazy and did not use the bin." Lily and Tom decide to help. They find a big stick and start to push the waste into the bin. It is hard work, but they are strong and brave. "Push, push, push!" Lily and Tom say together. Suddenly, they hear a loud bark. A big dog comes running towards them. It is angry and wants the waste. It shows its teeth and growls. "Run, run, run!" Lily and Tom say together. They drop the stick and run away from the dog. They find a quiet place behind a tree and hug each other. They are scared and sad. "Why did the dog do that?" Lily asks. "We were only trying to help." "I do not know," Tom says. "Maybe it was hungry and did not know better." Lily and Tom wait until the dog is gone. Then they go back to the bin. They see that the waste is still there, but some of it is in the bin. They smile and clap. "We did it!" Lily and Tom say together. "We made the park a little cleaner." They are proud and happy. They go to find their mums and tell them what they did. Their mums are proud and happy too. They give them a hug and a treat. Lily and Tom are good friends. They love the park and the animals.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. It was a hot day and the sun was shining bright. Fin saw something shiny on the sea floor. He swam down to take a look. It was a battery! Fin had never seen a battery before. Fin wanted to show his friend, Sam the Starfish. He swam over to Sam and said, "Look what I found! It's a battery!" Sam looked at the battery and said, "That's cool, but we should not play with it. It can be bad for the sea." Fin didn't listen to Sam. He swam around with the battery, and it started to feel hot. Suddenly, the battery popped and made a big mess. Fin was scared and didn't know what to do. Sam swam over to help Fin clean up the mess. They worked together and made the sea clean again. Fin said, "I'm sorry I didn't listen to you, Sam. I learned my lesson. We should be careful with things we don't know." From that day on, Fin and Sam took care of the sea and helped keep it clean. They learned that it's important to listen to friends and be careful with new things.

Once upon a time, there was a small island. On the island, there lived a kind duck and a worried little bunny. The bunny was always scared and did not like to play with other animals. One sunny day, the duck saw the bunny looking sad. The duck went to the bunny and asked, "Why are you worried, little bunny?" The bunny said, "I am scared to swim. What if the water is too deep?" The kind duck wanted to help the bunny. So, the duck said, "I will teach you to swim. Don't be worried. I am here with you." The duck took the bunny to the water and showed the bunny how to rub its feet in the water. The bunny started to rub its feet in the water, just like the duck. Soon, the bunny was not worried anymore. The duck and the bunny became good friends and played together on the island every day.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they go to the creek. The creek is a small river with rocks and fish. Tom and Mia see a smooth stone. It is round and shiny. They want to touch it. "Look, Mia, a smooth stone!" Tom says. "Can I hold it?" Mia asks. "OK, but be careful. It might be slippery," Tom says. They take turns holding the smooth stone. It feels cold and wet. They hear the water make sounds. Splash, splash, splash. They hear the birds sing. Tweet, tweet, tweet. They hear the wind blow. Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh. They are happy. They smile and laugh. They play with the smooth stone. They throw it in the water and watch it sink. They find another smooth stone and do it again. They have fun at the creek. They are good friends.
