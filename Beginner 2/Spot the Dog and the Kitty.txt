Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Kitty

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a broken toy ball. He loved that ball, but now it was broken. Spot was sad. One day, Spot met a new friend named Kitty. Kitty saw Spot's broken ball and said, "Hi Spot! Welcome to play with me. I have a ball too!" Spot was happy and they played together. While playing, Spot felt an itch on his back. He asked Kitty, "Can you help me with my itch?" Kitty said, "Sure, Spot!" and scratched his back. The itch went away and they continued to play happily.

Once there was a little girl called Jane. Jane was three years old and loved to take walks in the park. But one day, she got a little bit lost. She started to feel scared, so she wandered around until she saw an old man. The old man wore a big hat and had a kind face. He said, "Hello Jane, I think I can help you. I know a spell that will take you back to your home." Jane was so excited! The old man said the spell slowly and it was a bit harsh. Jane said the spell three times and suddenly, she was back at her house. Her parents were so glad to see her safe and happy. From that day on, Jane never got lost in the park again.

Tommy and Lily were playing hide and seek in the park. Tommy was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran behind a big tree and waited for Tommy to find her. She was very quiet and still. Tommy looked around the park and tried to guess where Lily was hiding. He saw a swing, a slide, a bench, and a fountain, but no Lily. He wondered if she was behind the bushes, or under the bridge, or in the sandbox. He decided to check the bushes first. He walked slowly to the bushes and peeked inside. He saw something furry and brown. He thought it was Lily's hair. He shouted, "I found you, Lily!" and pulled the furry thing out of the bushes. But it was not Lily. It was a sleeping dog. The dog woke up and barked loudly. Tommy was scared and let go of the dog. The dog ran away, wagging its tail. Tommy was embarrassed. He felt silly for grabbing the wrong thing. Lily heard the bark and the shout. She came out from behind the tree and saw Tommy. She laughed and said, "That was not me, Tommy. That was a dog. You need to look better." Tommy felt more embarrassed. He said, "Sorry, Lily. I thought it was you. You have good hiding skills." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tommy. You have good seeking skills. But maybe you need a nap. You look tired." Tommy nodded and said, "Maybe you are right, Lily. Let's go home and have a nap. Then we can play again." Lily agreed and they walked home together, holding hands.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird named Pepper. Pepper lived in a big tree with her friends. They all played together and had fun. One day, Pepper saw a big, red ball in the grass. She wanted to play with it, but she was scared. She asked her friends, "Should I play with the ball?" Her friends all nodded and said, "Yes, Pepper, play with the ball. It will be fun!" So, Pepper went down to the grass to play with the big, red ball. Pepper had so much fun playing with the ball! But then, something unexpected happened. The ball bounced too high and got stuck in the tree. Pepper and her friends tried to get it down, but they couldn't. They all felt sad. But then, a kind squirrel came and helped them get the ball down. Pepper and her friends were so happy! They learned that it's always good to help others and that friends can do big things when they work together.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is wild. It has no collar and no leash. It barks and runs at them. "Help!" Lila cries. "The dog is wild!" Ben is brave. He picks up a stick. He waves it at the dog. "Go away, dog!" he shouts. "You are not good!" The dog stops. It looks at Ben and the stick. It growls. It does not like the stick. Lila sees a man. The man is the dog's owner. He runs to the park. He calls the dog. "Rex, come here! Bad dog!" The dog hears the man. It turns and runs to the man. The man puts a leash on the dog. He says sorry to Lila and Ben. "I'm sorry, kids. Rex escaped from the yard. He is not wild, but he is not good either. He needs more training." Lila and Ben nod. They are glad the dog is gone. They are not hurt. They hug each other. They are good friends. They go back to play in the park. They have fun.
