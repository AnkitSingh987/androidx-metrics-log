Category: Beginner
Title: The High Building and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a high building. In this high building, there was a man who had a job to deliver food. He went on a subway to go fast to the people who wanted to eat. One day, the man had to deliver a big cake. He got on the subway with the cake in a big box. But, he did not know that there was a little dog inside the box, not a cake! When the man got to the place to give the cake, he opened the box. Surprise! The little dog jumped out and made everyone happy. The people did not get the cake, but they loved the cute dog more. And they all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a little girl's room. The girl, named Lily, loved to play hide and seek with her friend, Tim. One day, Tim came over to play. Lily said, "Let's play hide and seek! I will hide first, and you must find me!" Tim agreed and started to count. Lily ran to the wardrobe, opened the door, and hid inside. She was very quiet, but she was also a bit careless. The door was not shut all the way. Tim finished counting and started to look for Lily. He looked under the bed, behind the curtains, and even in the toy box. Then, he saw the wardrobe door was not shut tight. He walked over and took a little peek inside. "Found you!" Tim shouted as he saw Lily hiding in the wardrobe. Lily laughed and said, "You found me! Now it's your turn to hide!" They continued to play and had lots of fun, being more careful with their hiding spots.

One day, a big train and a small train were on the same track. The big train said to the small train, "We must separate, or we will get hurt." The small train said, "Okay, let's separate." They went to different tracks. The big train went fast, and the small train went slow. As they went on their separate tracks, they saw a big hill. The big train went up the hill fast, but the small train was too slow. The big train came back to help the small train. They went up the hill together, and they were not hurt. They were happy they could help each other.

One day, a girl named Mia found a rough veil in her play box. She did not know where it came from. It was a pretty veil but felt rough to touch. Mia decided to show it to her mom. "Mom, look what I found! It's a rough veil," Mia said. Her mom looked at the veil and smiled. "Let's reveal what is under the veil," her mom said. Mia felt excited to see what was under the veil. Together, they lifted the rough veil and revealed a beautiful doll. Mia was so happy to see the doll. She played with the doll all day long and even put the rough veil on the doll's head. And from that day on, Mia and the doll became best friends.

Tom was a big boy who liked to wear a suit. He had a blue suit with a tie and a hat. He felt very smart and proud in his suit. He wanted to show his suit to his friends at the park. But when he got to the park, he saw a dog. The dog was big and brown and had sharp teeth. The dog ran to Tom and barked. Tom was scared. He tried to run away, but the dog was faster. The dog jumped on Tom and bit his suit. Tom cried. His suit was torn and dirty. A lady came and took the dog away. She said sorry to Tom and gave him a hug. She said the dog was not mean, just playful. She said she would buy Tom a new suit. Tom stopped crying. He was still sad, but he was also kind. He went to his friends and told them what happened. His friends said they were sorry, too. They said they liked Tom, not his suit. They said they would play with him anyway. Tom smiled. He was happy. He had good friends. He did not need a high suit to have fun.
