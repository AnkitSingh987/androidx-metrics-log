Title: Anna and Ben and the Slide

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Anna wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, it will be fun!" Anna says. "I don't know, Anna, it looks too high," Ben says. "Please, Ben, please. I promise it will be okay. I will go first and then you can follow me," Anna says. "Okay, Anna, I will try. But you have to wait for me at the bottom," Ben says. Anna nods. She gives Ben a hug. "You are very brave, Ben. I am proud of you," she says. Anna climbs up the ladder and sits on the slide. She smiles at Ben and waves. Then she slides down fast and lands on the ground. She laughs and claps. "See, Ben, it was easy. Your turn now," she says. Ben looks at the slide. He feels nervous, but he remembers his promise. He takes a deep breath and climbs up the ladder. He sits on the slide and looks at Anna. She smiles and cheers. "You can do it, Ben. I am here for you," she says. Ben closes his eyes and slides down. He feels the wind in his hair and the sun on his face. He opens his eyes and sees Anna waiting for him. He lands on the ground and jumps up. "I did it, Anna, I did it!" he says. Anna runs to him and gives him a high five. "You did it, Ben, you did it! You are so brave. I am so happy for you," she says. They hug and laugh. They are very happy. They have kept their promise. They have followed their order. They have been brave. They are the best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. One day, Tom had a big sneeze. "Achoo!" he sneezed. Lily looked at Tom and asked, "Are you okay, Tom?" Tom nodded his head, but he still felt grumpy. "Why are you grumpy, Tom?" Lily asked. Tom looked at her and said, "I don't know the truth about why I sneeze so much." Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Let's ask Mom." So, Lily and Tom went to find Lily's mom. "Mom, why does Tom sneeze so much?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Tom is sneezing because he has a little cold. Don't worry, he will feel better soon." Tom felt happy to know the truth. He wasn't grumpy anymore. He knew he would feel better soon. Lily and Tom played together all day, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing outside. She could hear the birds singing and the wind blowing. Mia was wearing her favorite coat, which was red and warm. She loved to play in the leaves and feel the sun on her face. Suddenly, Mia saw her friend Tom. Tom was frustrated because he could not find his coat. "I can't find my coat," Tom said with a sad face. Mia wanted to help her friend, so they looked for Tom's coat together. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and even in the sandbox. Finally, they found Tom's coat hanging on a fence. "Here is your coat, Tom!" Mia said happily. Tom was so happy, and they both laughed and played together for the rest of the day.
