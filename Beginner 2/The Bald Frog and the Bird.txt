Category: Beginner
Title: The Bald Frog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bald frog. The frog lived in a small pond. The pond was his home. He was all alone and wanted a friend. One day, a big bird came to the pond. The bird saw the bald frog and said, "Hello, little frog. Can I be your friend?" The bald frog was very happy. "Yes, please come and be my friend!" said the frog. The big bird and the bald frog played together. They had fun in the sun. But then, the big bird got hungry. The big bird looked at the bald frog and said, "I am hungry. I must eat now." The big bird ate the little bald frog. The end.

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big tree with many flowers. The flowers are white and smell good. "Let's pick some flowers for Mommy!" Lily says. "OK!" Tom says. They run to the tree and pick some flowers. They are happy and sing a song. But then they see a big dog. The dog is dirty and barks loud. The dog runs to them and wants to play. "Go away, dog!" Lily says. "You are dirty and scary!" But the dog does not go away. The dog jumps on them and licks their faces. The dog makes their clothes and flowers dirty. "Help!" Tom says. A man comes and sees them. The man is the dog's owner. He is sorry and calls the dog. "I'm sorry, kids. He is friendly, but he likes to play too much. Are you OK?" the man says. Lily and Tom are not hurt, but they are sad and angry. Their flowers are dirty and ruined. "No, we are not OK. Your dog is bad. He made our flowers dirty. We picked them for Mommy!" Lily says. The man feels bad. He wants to make them happy. He has an idea. "I'm sorry, kids. I have some more flowers in my car. They are clean and pretty. Do you want them?" the man says. Lily and Tom look at the man. They see his car. They see the flowers. The flowers are white and smell good. "Yes, please!" Lily and Tom say. The man gives them the flowers. They are happy and say thank you. They run to their Mommy and give her the flowers. Mommy is happy and hugs them. They tell her the story. Mommy is glad they are OK. She says the dog was not bad, but he needed to learn manners. They agree and laugh. They love their Mommy and their flowers. They are happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Luna. Luna loved to nap all day long. Her favorite place to nap was on a soft strip of grass under a big tree. One sunny day, Luna was napping under the tree when a little boy named Tim came to play. Tim saw Luna and said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" Luna opened her eyes and looked at Tim, but she was too sleepy to play. Tim sat down next to Luna and said, "If you don't want to play, maybe we can nap together." So Tim and Luna both took a long nap on the soft strip of grass under the big tree. When they woke up, they felt happy and rested. From that day on, Tim and Luna became best friends and enjoyed many naps and playtimes together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very careless. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They played near the pond. The pond had many mosquitoes. A mosquito came to Tim and said, "I want to be your friend." Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but you must be nice." The mosquito agreed and they played together. Tim picked up a stick and they played catch. Soon, Tim's friends saw the mosquito and were scared. They told Tim to be careful. But Tim didn't listen. He was careless and kept playing with the mosquito. Then, the mosquito bit Tim. Tim cried and his friends came to help him. They told him, "You should have listened to us. You must be careful when you pick your friends." Tim learned his lesson and promised to be more careful next time. And they all played together happily ever after.
