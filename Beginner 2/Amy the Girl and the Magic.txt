Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Magic

One day, a girl named Amy found a diary. Amy wanted to study the diary, but it was difficult. She asked her friend Tim for help. Tim said, "Let's try to read it together!" They sat down and tried to read the diary. As they read, they saw a funny word. Amy said, "What is this word?" Tim said, "I don't know, let's say it out loud." They said the word and something unexpected happened! When they said the word, a big toy box appeared! They were so happy. They played with the toys all day. The diary was magic! From that day on, they read the diary together and found more magic words.

One day, in a modern town, a big event was happening. All the kids were very excited. They were going to solve a puzzle together. The puzzle was very big and had many pieces. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue were playing with the puzzle. They were trying to solve it. Tim said, "Sue, I can't find the right piece." Sue looked at the pieces and said, "Let's look together, Tim!" But then, two other kids named Sam and Kim came over. They wanted to solve the puzzle too. They said, "We want to play. Can we help?" Tim and Sue didn't want to share the puzzle. They said, "No, we want to solve it by ourselves." Sam and Kim were sad. They wanted to play too. So, they went to the grown-ups and told them what happened. The grown-ups said, "You all need to share and work together to solve the puzzle." So, Tim and Sue let Sam and Kim help. They all worked together, and they solved the puzzle. Everyone was happy, and they had a lot of fun at the big event.

Lily liked to sing songs. She had a toy that could record her voice and play it back. She wanted to record a song for her mom, who was at work. She took the toy to her room and pressed the button. She sang a song about flowers and rainbows. She was happy with her song. She pressed the button again to hear it. But the toy did not play her song. It played a loud noise that sounded like a scale. A scale was something that mom used to weigh herself. Lily did not like the noise. It hurt her ears. She tried to record her song again. But the toy only made the scale noise. She pressed the button many times, but nothing changed. She felt frustrated. She wanted to cry. She ran to the living room, where dad was reading a book. She showed him the toy and told him what happened. "Dad, dad, my toy is broken! It does not record my song. It only makes a scale noise. Listen!" She pressed the button and the toy made the scale noise. Dad looked at the toy and smiled. He saw that Lily had pressed the wrong button. The button that recorded was next to the button that played the scale noise. "Lily, don't worry. Your toy is not broken. You just pressed the wrong button. This button records your voice, and this button plays the scale noise. See?" He pressed the right button and recorded his voice saying "I love you, Lily". Then he pressed the other button and played his voice. Lily heard dad's voice and smiled. She understood her mistake. She hugged dad and said "I love you, too, dad. Thank you for helping me." She took the toy back to her room and recorded her song again. This time, she pressed the right button. She played her song and liked it. She was happy. She waited for mom to come home and hear her song.

Anna and Ben are playing with blocks. They want to build a big tower. They take turns to put blocks on top of each other. They are careful not to make the tower fall. "Look, Anna, I have a red block. It is very pretty. Where can I put it?" Ben asks. "Let me see, Ben. Maybe you can put it here, next to the blue block. It will fit well," Anna says. Ben tries to put the red block next to the blue block. But the red block is too big. It does not fit. It pushes the blue block and makes the tower wobble. "Oh no, Ben, the tower is going to fall! The red block is too big. It does not fit. You have to find a smaller block," Anna says. Ben is sad. He likes the red block. He does not want to find a smaller block. He thinks the red block is the best block. He decides to do something else with the red block. He puts it in his mouth. "Ben, what are you doing? You cannot eat the block. It is not food. It is a toy. It will not taste good. It will taste bad. It will hurt your teeth. Spit it out, Ben, spit it out!" Anna says. Ben spits out the red block. He does not like the taste. It tastes bad. It hurts his teeth. He makes a face. "Yuck, Anna, you are right. The block does not taste good. It tastes bad. It hurts my teeth. I do not want to eat the block. I want to build the tower. But it is difficult to find a block that fits. Can you help me, Anna?" Ben says. "Of course, Ben, I can help you. We can build the tower together. We can find blocks that fit. We can make the tower very high and very strong. It will be fun. Come on, Ben, let's try again," Anna says. Anna and Ben smile. They hug each other. They are friends. They like to play with blocks. They like to help each other. They have fun.
