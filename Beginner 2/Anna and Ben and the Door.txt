Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Door

Anna and Ben are playing in the house. They like to hide and seek. Anna counts to ten and then looks for Ben. She looks in the kitchen, the living room, the bedroom, but she cannot find him. Where is Ben? She hears a noise from the bathroom. She runs to the door and tries to open it. But it is locked. She knocks on the door and says, "Ben, are you there? Come out, I found you!" But Ben does not answer. She hears him cry. "Ben, what is wrong? Why are you crying?" Anna asks. She is worried. She tries to open the door again, but it is still locked. "I want to get out! I want to get out!" Ben cries. "I locked the door and I cannot open it. And the toilet is empty. There is no water. I cannot flush. I made a mess. I am sorry, Anna. I am sorry." Anna feels sorry for Ben. She says, "It is okay, Ben. Do not cry. I will help you. I will get Mommy. She will open the door. She will fix the toilet. She will clean the mess. She will hug you. She loves you. I love you too, Ben. Wait for me, okay?" Anna runs to find Mommy. She tells her what happened. Mommy follows Anna to the bathroom. She has a key. She unlocks the door. She sees Ben. He is sitting on the floor. He is sad. He is dirty. Mommy smiles. She says, "Ben, it is okay. You are not in trouble. You had an accident. It happens. I will help you. Come here, my sweet boy." Mommy picks up Ben. She kisses him. She takes him to the bathtub. She washes him. She dries him. She puts on clean clothes. She hugs him. She says, "You are clean now. You are fine now. You are brave now. I am proud of you. I love you, Ben." Ben smiles. He says, "Thank you, Mommy. I love you too. And I love Anna. She is a good sister. She helped me. She found me. She did not get mad at me." Mommy says, "Yes, she is. She is a good sister. And you are a good brother. You are both good children. And I am a happy Mommy. Come on, let's go and play with Anna. She is waiting for you." Mommy, Ben, and Anna go to the living room. They play with toys. They laugh. They have fun. They are a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Ben. Ben lived in a small ancient house. One day, Ben went to the park to play. He saw a big slide and wanted to slide down it. Ben met a little girl named Lily at the park. Lily was scared to go on the slide. Ben said, "Don't be scared, I will help you!" They both climbed up the slide, and Ben held Lily's hand. Together, they slid down the slide. Lily was happy and said, "Thank you, Ben!" The next day, Ben and Lily went to the park again. This time, Ben was scared to go on the slide. Lily said, "Don't be scared, I will help you!" Lily held Ben's hand, and they slid down the slide together. In the end, Ben and Lily learned that it's good to help each other when someone is scared. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Olive. Olive was very good at tricks. She could jump high, run fast, and catch balls. Olive loved to play with her friends in the park. One sunny day, Olive and her friends were playing with a ball. They took turns throwing the ball and catching it. Olive was very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball went into the woods, and Olive ran to find it. Olive looked and looked for the ball, but she could not find it. She started to shout, "Ball, where are you?" Then, a big bird flew down with the ball in its beak. The bird was Olive's new friend! From that day on, Olive and the bird played together, and they had lots of fun.
