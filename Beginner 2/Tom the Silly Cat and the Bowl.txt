Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Silly Cat and the Bowl

One day, a silly cat named Tom found a big bowl of jelly. He was very hungry, so he thought, "I will eat this jelly all by myself!" Tom took the bowl to his favorite spot under the sun to enjoy his treat. As Tom started to eat the jelly, his friend, a small bird named Sue, flew by. Sue saw the jelly and asked, "Can I have some jelly too?" Tom said, "No, this is my jelly. I found it first!" Sue felt sad and flew away. The sun was very hot that day, and soon, Tom's jelly started to melt. It became a big, sticky mess. Tom was sad because he could not eat the jelly anymore. He thought about his friend Sue and wished he had shared the jelly with her. Just then, Sue came back and saw the melted jelly. She said, "I can help you fix it! Let's put it in the fridge to make it cold again." Tom and Sue put the jelly in the fridge, and soon it was good as new. Tom and Sue shared the jelly and had a great time. Tom learned that sharing with friends is more fun than being greedy. And from that day on, Tom always shared his treats with Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a fast dog. He loved to run and play all day. One day, Max met a new friend, a small cat named Lily. Max said to Lily, "Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, I want to play." So, Max and Lily played together. They ran and played in the sun. They had a lot of fun. After playing, Max and Lily were tired. They sat under a big tree. Max said, "I love playing with you, Lily." Lily said, "I love playing with you too, Max." They hugged, and from that day on, Max and Lily were best friends.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box. They want to make a house with the box. "Can I have the scissors?" Tom asks Mia. "OK, I give you the scissors. Be careful, they are sharp," Mia says. Tom cuts a door and a window in the box. Mia draws flowers and stars on the box. They put a blanket on the top. They have a house! They go inside the house. They pretend to cook and eat. They have fun. But then, a big dog comes. The dog is rough. He barks and runs to the house. He bites the blanket and pulls it off. He scratches the box and tears it apart. Tom and Mia are scared. They run out of the house. They cry. "Go away, bad dog!" Tom shouts. "Leave our house alone!" Mia cries. The dog does not listen. He makes a mess with the house. Tom and Mia are sad. They hug each other. They look for a new place to play.

Lily liked to play with her toys. She had dolls, cars, blocks and books. But her favorite toy was her microscope. She got it from her grandpa, who was a scientist. He told her she could see tiny things with it, like bugs and plants and hairs. One day, Lily wanted to show her friend Max her microscope. She invited him to her room and took out her toy. She put a slide with a flower on it under the lens and turned on the light. "Look, Max, this is a flower, but very small," she said. Max looked through the microscope. He saw the flower, but he did not think it was fun. He thought it was boring. He liked to play with cars and balls and trains. He did not care about tiny things. He yawned and said, "Can we play something else?" Lily was sad. She liked her microscope. She thought it was amazing. She wanted Max to like it too. She asked him, "Don't you want to see more slides? I have bugs and plants and hairs. They are very cool." Max shook his head. He did not want to see more slides. He wanted to play something else. He said, "No, thank you. I think your microscope is boring. Can we play with your cars or your blocks?" Lily nodded. She did not want to make Max unhappy. She put away her microscope and took out her cars and blocks. She played with Max, but she wished he liked her microscope too. She thought maybe he would like it more when he was older. She hoped he would.
