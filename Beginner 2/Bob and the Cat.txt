Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Cat

One sunny day, a busy bee named Bob woke up. He said, "I want to play my guitar today!" Bob loved to play his guitar. He played it every day. Bob went to find his friend, Cat. Cat was sleeping under a tree. Bob said, "Wake up, Cat! Let's play guitar together!" Cat opened her eyes and smiled. She liked playing guitar with Bob. Bob and Cat played their guitars all day. They made happy music. All their friends came to listen. The sun went down, and it was time to sleep. Bob and Cat said, "Goodbye!" They went to bed, ready to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry, so he went to the park to find some food. In the park, he saw a big tree. Tom saw a bird on a branch, so he started to lean on the tree to get closer. The bird saw Tom and said, "Please don't eat me. I promise I will help you find food." Tom thought about it and said, "Okay, I promise not to eat you." The bird flew away and found a big sandwich on a bench. Tom and the bird shared the sandwich and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very bored. He had no friends to play with. One day, Tim found a big box. He had an idea. He would load the box with fun things. Tim put a ball, a toy car, and a note in the box. The note said, "Please play with me." Tim hoped someone would find the box and play with him. He left the box near a big tree. A girl named Sue found the box. She opened it and saw the note. Sue said, "I want to play too!" She went to find Tim. They played with the ball and the toy car. But as they played, it started to rain. The rain was very heavy. Tim and Sue ran home. They were sad because they could not play outside anymore. The box with the note was left in the rain. It got wet and the words were washed away. Tim and Sue never found the box again. They were very sad and bored.

Once upon a time there was a chubby little bird. He loved to fly around and go really really fast. He even loved to zoom around the sky. One day he decided he wanted to find a special herb. So he started looking in all sorts of places. He looked high and low, but he couldn’t find the herb anywhere. But then, he spotted the special herb in a tree up above. He quickly flew up to the tree and grabbed the herb with his beak. Now he had the herb he needed. He zoomed back down to the ground and was so happy he had found it!

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a calm pond with pretty fish. Mia liked the woods and liked to look at the fish. She saw a big mushroom near the pond. The mushroom was red and white. Mia thought it was a pretty mushroom. Mia wanted to take the mushroom home. She picked it up and gave it a kiss. The mushroom started to shake and wiggle. Mia was surprised! She didn't know what was happening. Suddenly, the mushroom turned into a little frog. The frog looked at Mia and said, "Thank you for the kiss!" Mia was very happy to have a new friend. She and the frog played by the pond all day. They had a lot of fun and Mia learned that sometimes, unexpected things can happen when you kiss a mushroom.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to have fun with his friends. They would play all day long in the sun. Tom had a special toy he liked to carry around. It was a small, soft ball. One day, Tom and his friends went to the park. They played games and had lots of fun. Tom carried his ball with him everywhere. He would toss it in the air and catch it with his paws. As the sun started to set, it was time to go home. Tom and his friends said goodbye and went to their homes. Tom was happy to have had so much fun with his friends. He carried his ball back home, ready for more fun the next day.
