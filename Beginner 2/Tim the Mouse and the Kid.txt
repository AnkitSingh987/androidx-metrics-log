Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mouse and the Kid

One day, a little mouse named Tim went out to play. He saw a big cat. The cat looked sad and guilty. Tim wanted to care for the cat. "Hi, cat," Tim said. "Why are you sad?" "I took a toy from a kid," the cat said. "I feel guilty." Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's give the toy back to the kid. Then you won't feel guilty." The cat agreed. They went to the kid's house. The kid was so happy to see the toy. The cat said sorry. The kid forgave the cat. Tim, the cat, and the kid became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little toy train. It loved to run on its tracks all day. One day, the train saw a fake lake near the tracks. It was a big, blue rug! The train thought it was real water. The train wanted to bathe in the lake. It thought it would be fun. But when the train tried to go in, it got stuck on the rug. The train was sad and scared. It wanted to run on its tracks again. A kind little girl saw the train stuck on the rug. She knew it was a fake lake. She picked up the train and put it back on the tracks. The train was so happy! It thanked the girl and went back to running on its tracks all day.

One day, a little dog named Max found a fragile toy. It was a loop with many colors. Max liked the toy very much. He played with it all day. He would shake the loop and watch it move. Max was very happy. But one day, the loop broke. Max was very sad. He did not know what to do. He tried to fix it, but it was too hard. Max was about to give up when something unexpected happened. A tiny bug came and saw Max's broken loop. The bug had magic powers. It wanted to help Max. The bug used its magic to fix the loop. Max was so happy! He thanked the bug and played with his loop again. Now, Max had a new friend to play with too.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy guitar. He would carry it everywhere he went. One day, Spot's guitar got stuck in a tree. Spot was very sad and did not know how to get it back. Spot had an idea. He would whistle for help. He whistled very loud, and his friend, Bird, heard him. Bird flew down to see what was wrong. Spot showed Bird the guitar stuck in the tree. Bird wanted to help Spot get his guitar back. Bird flew up to the tree and tried to get the guitar. It was hard, but Bird did not give up. Finally, Bird got the guitar out of the tree and brought it back to Spot. Spot was very happy and thanked Bird. They played together all day, and Spot learned that friends are always there to help when you need them.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. The turkey lived on a farm. The farm was dry because it did not rain for many days. The turkey was sad because it wanted to play in the mud. One day, the farmer had a big party. The party would include all the animals on the farm. The turkey was very happy. It wanted to play with the other animals at the party. At the party, the turkey played with the cow, the pig, and the horse. They all had fun. Then, it started to rain. The dry farm became wet and muddy. The turkey was very happy because it could play in the mud again.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the city. They needed to take a taxi to go there. They saw a yellow taxi and waved at it. The taxi stopped, and they got in. The driver was a polite man with a big smile. On their way, they saw a girl frowning on the street. She looked lost and sad. Tom's mom asked the driver to stop the taxi. She opened the window and asked the girl, "Are you lost? Can we help you?" The girl said, "Yes, I can't find my mom. I am scared." Tom's mom and the driver decided to help the girl find her mom. They drove around the city until they found the girl's mom. The girl and her mom were so happy to see each other again. Tom felt good about helping the girl. He learned that it is important to help others when they are in need. The taxi driver also felt happy that he could help. And from that day on, Tom always tried to help others and be polite, just like his mom and the taxi driver.
