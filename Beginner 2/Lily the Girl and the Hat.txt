Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Hat

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play dress up. She put on her mom's big, serious hat and her dad's long, funny coat. She looked in the mirror and laughed. She felt like a grown-up. Lily saw a big maze in her backyard. She wanted to play in it. She walked into the maze with her big hat and long coat. She turned left and right, but she could not find the way out. She started to feel scared. Suddenly, her dog, Spot, came running into the maze. He had a big smile on his face. Spot showed Lily the way out of the maze. They ran out together and laughed. It turned out, her dad made the maze for her to play in. Lily and Spot played in the maze all day, and she was not scared anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They found a trail in the woods. The trail was tough, but Tim and Spot were brave. As they walked, they met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "This trail is tough, but I want to see what's at the end." Tim and Spot wanted to see too. So they all walked together. They walked and walked until they reached the end of the trail. There, they found a big, pretty tree. They played under the tree all day. Then, they went home and told their friends about the fun day on the tough trail.

Sara had a new toy. It was a sheep with soft wool. She liked to hug it and play with it. She named it Lily. One day, Sara went to the park with her mom and Lily. She saw a big slide and wanted to try it. She left Lily on a bench and ran to the slide. She had fun going down and up. But when she came back to the bench, Lily was gone. Sara looked around and saw a big dog with Lily in its mouth. The dog was running away with Lily. Sara cried and shouted, "Lily, come back! Come back!" Her mom heard her and ran to help her. She tried to catch the dog, but it was too fast. It ran out of the park and into the street. Sara and her mom followed it, but they could not see it anymore. Sara was very sad. She lost her new toy. She lost her sheep with soft wool. She lost her friend Lily. She wished she had kept Lily safe with her. She wished she had not left Lily alone. She wished she had not gone to the slide. She wished she could hug Lily again.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Quick. Quick loved to run around and play all day. He had many friends in the forest, like Bird, Squirrel, and Mouse. They would all play together and have lots of fun. One day, Quick saw a big box. He didn't know what it was, so he asked his friend Bird to explain. Bird said, "It's a trap, Quick. Don't go near it!" But Quick didn't listen. He thought it would be fun to play with the box. Quick went closer to the box and jumped inside. Suddenly, the box closed, and Quick was stuck! His friends tried to help, but they couldn't open the box. Quick was sad and scared. He wished he had listened to Bird. Now, Quick was trapped in the box, and he couldn't play with his friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an obedient dog named Max. Max loved to jog with his friend, a little boy named Tim. They would jog to the park and play all day. One day, while they were jogging, Max saw a big pit. Max wanted to see what was inside the pit, so he went closer. But Tim called Max and said, "Stay away from the pit, Max!" Max was an obedient dog, so he did not go near the pit. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew out of the pit and landed on Max's head. The bird told Max and Tim that it was lost and needed help. Max and Tim helped the little bird find its way home. The moral of the story is to always listen to your friends and be kind to others, even if they are different from you.
