Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Sam and the Toy

Once upon a time there were two friends - Jack and Sam. Jack was mad at Sam because Sam had taken Jack's toy without asking. Jack was very angry when he saw Sam with his toy. Jack shouted at Sam and said, "Give me back my toy!" But Sam said, "No, it's mine now!" Jack was getting more and more angry. He wanted to show Sam how much he valued his toy by getting it back. So he ran to the sink and started washing the dishes. Once he was done, Sam saw how hard Jack had worked and how much he valued his toy. So Sam decided to give it back. Jack was very happy and the two friends hugged each other to show that even when they get angry, they still value each other.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They saw a big pile of leaves. They wanted to make a fire with the leaves. "Let's pick some leaves and put them in a circle," Ben said. "OK, I will pick the red ones and you pick the yellow ones," Lily said. They picked many leaves and made a circle. They found a stick and a match. Ben tried to light the match, but it did not work. "Let me try," Lily said. She took the match and rubbed it on the box. It made a spark and a flame. "Wow, look at the flame! It is so colorful!" Ben said. They put the match on the leaves. The leaves started to burn. They made a small fire. "Be careful, the fire is hot," Lily said. They sat next to the fire and watched the colorful flame. They felt warm and happy. They sang a song and roasted some marshmallows. They had fun in the garden.

John and his mom were walking in the park. "Mommy, can I count the trees?" asked John. His mom smiled and said, "Sure, go ahead!" John began counting the trees out loud. "One, two, three..." he counted. Soon he had counted all the way to twelve. But then something strange happened. A white veil suddenly appeared and blocked his view of the trees. John couldn't count anymore. He looked up at his mom and asked, "What's that veil doing there?" His mom frowned and said, "That's a selfish veil. It's keeping us from counting the trees. We have to find a way to get it away." Just then, they heard a loud noise in the sky and looked up. It was a giant number 20! "That number will get rid of the veil," said John's mom. Sure enough, the number flew straight down and knocked the veil away. John and his mom cheered. John was able to finish counting all the way to twenty. His mom smiled and said, "See, John, numbers can be very helpful!"

Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play outside with their dad. One day, dad says, "Let's go to the golf place. I have a surprise for you." Lily and Tom are happy. They love surprises. They get in the car and drive to the golf place. Dad gives them each a small stick and a small ball. He says, "This is golf. You hit the ball with the stick and try to make it go in the hole." Lily and Tom think this sounds fun. They follow dad to the green grass. Dad shows them how to hold the stick and swing it. He hits the ball and it flies far away. Lily and Tom clap. They want to try too. Dad helps them hit the ball. Sometimes the ball goes far, sometimes it goes near, sometimes it goes in the wrong direction. Lily and Tom laugh. They have fun. But then, they see something smelly. It is a big pile of cow poop. It is on the grass near the hole. Lily and Tom say, "Ew! Gross! What is that?" Dad says, "That is cow poop. Sometimes cows come here and leave their poop. We have to be careful not to step on it." Lily and Tom think cow poop is funny. They point at it and make silly noises. They say, "Moo! Moo! We are cows! We make poop!" Dad laughs too. He says, "You are silly cows. But don't touch the poop. It is dirty and smelly. Come on, let's go to the next hole. Maybe we will see more surprises." Lily and Tom nod. They follow dad to the next hole. They hope to see more cows. They like golf.
