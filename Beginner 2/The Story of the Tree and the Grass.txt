Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Grass

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was tired and very sleepy. She wanted to play and have some fun. The little girl thought of a great idea. She went outside and found a gum tree. She climbed up the tree and grabbed some gum. Then she came down and sprayed the gum all over the grass. The little girl was very happy. She laughed and sprayed the gum everywhere. Then she lay down on the grass and fell asleep. She was so tired, she slept very soundly. But soon the sun came up and shone brightly. When the sunshine touched the gum, it melted away. When the little girl woke up the gum was gone! The little girl smiled and went to play another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink juice. One day, he found a bottle with a bitter juice inside. Tim did not like the taste, so he gave it to his dog, Max. Max drank the bitter juice and suddenly, he began to talk! "Wow, I can speak!" said Max. Tim was very surprised. They played and talked all day. They were very happy. One day, a police came to Tim's house. The police heard about Max and wanted to see the talking dog. But when Max tried to speak, he couldn't! The police left, and Tim and Max were sad. Then, they found another bottle of the bitter juice. Max drank it, and he could speak again! They promised to keep it a secret and played happily ever after.

Once there was a big bird named Bob. Bob had many pretty feathers. One day, Bob felt very sleepy. He wanted to take a nap. So, he found a soft spot under a tree and lay down. Soon, a little girl named Sue walked by. She saw one of Bob's feathers on the ground. "Wow, a pretty feather!" Sue said. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tim. Tim liked the feather too. But Bob woke up and saw them with his feather. He felt sad. "Please give back my feather," Bob complained. Sue and Tim did not want to make Bob sad. So, they gave the feather back to him. Bob was happy to have his feather back. He thanked Sue and Tim. Then, he went back to his nap. Sue and Tim smiled and walked away, happy to have helped their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow bus. The bus loved to take kids to school. One day, the bus met a baker. The baker liked to bake yummy treats. The bus said to the baker, "Can you bake treats for the kids?" The baker was happy and said, "Yes, I will bake cookies for them." The baker made deep, round cookies. The kids on the bus were very happy. They said, "Thank you, baker!" And they all enjoyed the cookies on their ride to school.

Lily liked to make poems. She used her crayons and paper to write words that sounded nice. She made a poem for her mom and dad. She wanted to give it to them as a surprise. She put her poem under her pillow and waited for them to come home. She heard the door open and ran to hug them. "Hi, Lily, how was your day?" her mom asked. "I had a fine day, mom. I made something for you and dad. Can you guess what it is?" Lily said. Her dad smiled and lifted her up. "Is it a drawing? A song? A cookie?" he guessed. Lily shook her head and giggled. "No, no, no. It is a poem. I put it under my pillow. Come and see!" she said. She led them to her room and showed them her poem. It had words like love, happy, family, and fun. Her mom and dad read it and hugged her. "This is a wonderful poem, Lily. Thank you so much. We love you very much," they said. Lily felt proud and happy. She put her poem on the wall and kissed her mom and dad. "I love you too. You are the best," she said.
