Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ambulance

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big red ambulance. He was glad because he liked big cars. The ambulance had bright lights and went very fast. It made a loud sound. Tim wanted to be a helper like the people in the ambulance. Tim's mom saw that he liked the ambulance. She said, "You can be a helper too, Tim!" She encouraged him to help his friends when they were hurt or sad. Tim liked the idea of being a helper. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, fell down and hurt her knee. She cried because it hurt. Tim remembered what his mom said. He went to Lily and said, "It's okay, Lily. I will help you." Tim helped Lily stand up and took her to her mom. Lily's mom was glad and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good helper." Tim felt happy and knew he could be a helper like the people in the ambulance.

Jill and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to swing, slide and run on the grass. They saw a big rock near a tree. They ran to the rock and climbed on it. "Look, mom, we are on a rock!" Jill shouted. "Be careful, don't fall!" mom said. They pretended the rock was a boat and they were sailing on the sea. They sang a song and waved to the birds. They had fun on the rock. Then they saw a stranger walking towards them. He had a big bag and a hat. He smiled and said hello. "Who are you?" Ben asked. "I'm Tom, the baker. I have some yummy treats for you. Do you want to try?" he said. He opened his bag and showed them some cookies, muffins and pies. They looked yummy. "Can we have some, mom?" Jill asked. Mom looked at Tom and said, "Thank you, but no thank you. We don't take food from strangers. We have our own snacks." She took out some apples and cheese from her backpack and gave them to Jill and Ben. They ate their snacks and said, "These are yummy too!" They thanked Tom and said goodbye. They went back to playing on the rock. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird. The bird had big, colorful wings. It liked to fly high in the sky. One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She looked up and saw the beautiful bird with its big wings. The bird was flying near the trees. Lily was very happy to see the bird. Lily wanted to be friends with the bird. She called out, "Hello, beautiful bird with big wings!" The bird heard Lily and flew down to see her. They played together all day long. Lily and the beautiful bird with big wings were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. Near the lake, lived a gifted frog named Fred. Fred could match colors like no other frog. One day, Fred saw a little girl named Lily by the lake. She was sad because she lost her toy. Fred wanted to help Lily. He said, "Don't be sad. I will find your toy." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Fred." So, Fred jumped into the lake to look for the toy. Fred found many toys in the lake. He matched the colors of the toys to the colors Lily told him. At last, Fred found the right toy and gave it to Lily. Lily was happy and said, "You are the best frog, Fred!" They became good friends and played by the lake every day.

Once upon a time in a small town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved winter. She liked to play in the snow and make snowmen with her friends. She also liked to drink hot cocoa after playing outside. One day, Mia's mom said, "Mia, it's time to bathe. You are very dirty from playing in the snow." Mia did not want to stop playing, but she knew her mom was right. She went inside and took a warm bath. Mia liked the warm water and the bubbles. She played with her toy ducks in the bath. After her bath, Mia put on her great big coat and went back outside. She said to her friends, "I had a nice bath, and now I'm clean and warm. Let's keep playing in the snow!" Her friends agreed, and they all played together until the sun went down. They had a great time in the winter snow.
