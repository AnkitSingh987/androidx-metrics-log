Title: Sara and Ben and the Slide

Sara and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big slide and ran to it. Sara went first and slid down fast. She laughed and said, "That was fun!" Ben wanted to try too. He climbed up the ladder and sat on the slide. But he was scared. He looked down and saw how high he was. He felt his heart beat fast. He said, "I don't want to slide. I want to go back." Sara saw him and said, "Don't be scared, Ben. It's not hard. Just let go and slide. I will catch you at the bottom." Ben looked at Sara. She was his friend. She was cute and kind. He trusted her. He took a deep breath and let go. He slid down the slide and felt the wind in his hair. He smiled and said, "Whee!" Sara caught him at his waist and hugged him. She said, "You did it, Ben! You were brave. I'm proud of you." Ben hugged her back and said, "Thank you, Sara. You helped me. You are a good friend." They both laughed and went to play some more. The moral of the story is: Don't be afraid to try new things. You can overcome your fears with the help of a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and count the number of cars he had. One day, while playing, he saw a funny face on one of his toys. It made him laugh a lot. Tim wanted to show his mom the funny face. He walked to the kitchen, but he tripped and fell. Tim felt embarrassed because he cried in front of his mom. His mom hugged him and told him it was okay. Tim showed his mom the funny face on the toy. She did not laugh. She said it was time to put the toys away and go to bed. Tim was sad because he wanted his mom to laugh too. He went to bed with a bad feeling.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw her friend, Tom. Tom was very proud of his new dance. He wanted to show Lucy his dance. So, Tom started to dance in the park. Lucy clapped and laughed. Tom said, "Lucy, do you want to dance with me?" Lucy said, "Yes, I want to dance too!" They danced and danced, and they were very happy. Then, Lucy saw a big thumb on the ground. She was very surprised. Lucy said, "Look, Tom! A big thumb!" Tom stopped dancing and looked. The big thumb started to dance too! Lucy and Tom laughed and danced with the big thumb. They had a lot of fun that day at the park.

Once there was a little girl who was very eager. She wanted to play a game with her dad. She said, "Let's play hide and seek! You go hide and I will guess where you are!" Her dad smiled and said okay. He hid behind the couch and waited for her to guess where he was. The little girl went outside and looked around. Then she saw a penguin walking in the snow. She thought, "I guess the penguin knows where my dad is hiding!" She asked the penguin. He said, "Yes, I can help you find your dad. But you have to give me your homework for it!" The little girl was so excited and she agreed. The penguin took her homework and said, "Your dad is behind the couch. Now give me a hug!" The little girl hugged him, but the penguin ran away with her homework. She ran inside but it was too late. She never got her homework back and her dad was disappointed in her.
