Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Knee

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, while they were playing, Tim fell down and hurt his knee. It made him very sad. Tim's mom saw him crying and came to help. She told Tim to trust her, and she would make his knee feel better. Tim trusted his mom, and she put a band-aid on his knee. After a little while, Tim's knee felt much better. He was not sad anymore. Tim thanked his mom for helping him and went back to play with his friends. They played happily for the rest of the day.

One day, Tommy was looking through his mum's old box. He saw a map inside and took it out. He held it up and looked carefully. It was ordinary looking, with no drawings or pictures on it. Tommy wanted to taste the map, so he licked it. He made a face and said to no one in particular, "Ew, yuck!" He clearly didn't like the taste of it. Mum came in and laughed when she saw him there. "You don't eat maps, Tommy," she said with a smile. "Maps are for finding your way from one place to another." Tommy smiled and put the map back in the box. He wondered what other surprises the box held. Maybe he could find something more interesting than an ordinary map.

Lila was very sad. She had a birthday party today, but no one came. She waited and waited, but no one rang the bell. She looked at the cake on the table, with six candles on it. She wanted to blow them out and make a wish, but she had no friends to sing to her. She heard a knock on the door. She ran to open it, hoping it was someone for her party. But it was only her neighbor, Mrs. Lee. Mrs. Lee smiled and gave her a card and a present. "Happy birthday, Lila," she said. "I'm sorry I can't stay, but I have to go to work. I hope you have a wonderful day." Lila thanked her and took the card and the present. She opened the card and saw a picture of a cat and a mouse. It said, "You are a special girl, Lila. Don't let anyone make you feel lonely. You have a friend in me. Love, Mrs. Lee." Lila felt a warm feeling in her chest. She opened the present and saw a book of stories. She liked stories very much. She went back to the table and looked at the cake again. She decided to blow out the candles and make a wish. She wished that she could have more friends like Mrs. Lee. She wished that she could be happy and not sad. She wished that she could share her cake and her stories with someone. She cut a slice of cake and put it on a plate. She took the book and the plate and went to her room. She sat on her bed and ate the cake. It was sweet and yummy. She opened the book and read the first story. It was about a cat and a mouse who became friends. She liked the story very much. She heard a knock on the door again. She wondered who it could be. She ran to open it, hoping it was someone for her party. But it was only her mom. Her mom looked tired and sad. She hugged Lila and said, "I'm sorry, honey. I'm sorry I couldn't be here for your party. I had to work late. I know you must be very disappointed. I regret that I missed your special day." Lila hugged her mom back and said, "It's okay, mom. I'm not disappointed. I had a good day. I got a card and a present from Mrs. Lee. She is very nice. She gave me a book of stories. Do you want to read them with me?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to read them with you. Let's go to your room and have some cake and stories. I'm glad you had a good day, Lila. You are a special girl. You have a friend in me. I love you, Lila." Lila smiled and said, "I love you too, mom. You are a special mom. You have a friend in me. Come on, let's go." They went to Lila's room and sat on her bed. They ate the cake and read the stories. They laughed and hugged and talked. They felt happy and not sad. They felt full and not empty. They had each other. They had a friend.
