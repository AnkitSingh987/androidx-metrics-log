Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. It was cold, so she wore a big coat. Mia saw a mysterious big box in her yard. She was very curious and wanted to know what was inside. Mia called her friend Tom to come and see the box. Tom said, "Wow! What is in the mysterious box?" They both decided to open it together. When they opened the box, they found a cute little cat inside. The cat looked scared and cold. Mia and Tom started to panic. They wanted to help the cold cat. They ran inside and got a warm blanket. They wrapped the cat in the blanket and gave it some food. The cat was happy and warm. Mia and Tom felt good because they helped the mysterious cat.

Once there was a three year old boy named Jack. He had a heavy chain. He loved to play with it and clink it together as he walked around his house. One day, his mom asked him to count the numbers on the chain. Jack went to work, counting all the numbers on the chain one by one. “One, two, three, four…,” he said out loud. His mom smiled and asked him if he was done counting. Jack looked up and said, “Yes! I have counted all the numbers on the heavy chain.”

One day, a bird wanted to fly high in the sky. The bird was very quiet and loved to play with tape. The bird had a friend, a cat. The cat liked the tape too. They played with the tape together. One sunny day, the bird and the cat played with the tape outside. They were having so much fun. The bird wanted to fly with the tape, so it held the tape in its beak. The cat held the other end of the tape with its paw. The bird started to fly, but the cat did not let go of the tape. The bird flew higher and higher, and the cat went up with it! The cat was scared, but the bird was happy. They flew together, high in the sky. The cat was not quiet anymore. It was loud and happy, just like the bird. The bird and the cat flew together, and they were the best of friends.

Once there was a boy. The boy was very spoiled. He had many toys and always wanted more. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, he saw a big bug. The bug was on a leaf. The boy wanted to pinch the bug with his fingers. The bug looked scared. The boy's mom saw this and said, "No, don't pinch the bug." The boy listened to his mom. He let the bug go. The bug was happy, and the boy learned to be kind.

Lily liked to fold paper. She could make hats, boats, and animals with paper. She had many colorful papers in her room. She liked to show her paper things to her mom and dad. One day, Lily wanted to fold a big paper. She saw a big paper on the table. It was white and had many words on it. Lily did not know what the words said. She thought it was a paper for folding. She took the paper and went to her room. She folded the paper into a big hat. She put the hat on her head. She felt happy. She wanted to show her hat to her mom and dad. She ran to the living room. But mom and dad were not happy. They were angry. They saw Lily with the paper hat. They said, "Lily, what did you do? That was not a paper for folding. That was a paper for work. We needed that paper. You ruined it. Go to your room. No nap for you today." Lily felt sad. She did not know she did a bad thing. She cried. She took off the paper hat. She went to her room. She did not want to fold paper anymore. She wished she had a nap. She wished mom and dad were not angry. She wished she had a good day.
