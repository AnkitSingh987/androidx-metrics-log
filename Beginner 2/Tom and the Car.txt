Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Car

One day, Tom went for a jog in the park. He liked to jog and feel the sun on his face. The park was big and had many trees and flowers. Tom was happy when he jogged. While jogging, Tom found a card on the ground. The card had a picture of a big, pretty car. The car looked expensive. Tom liked the car on the card very much. Tom took the card home and showed it to his mom. She said, "Wow, that is an expensive car!" Tom put the card on his wall. Now, he could look at the car every day.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see the birds. They held on to the branches and went up. They saw a nest with three eggs. They were blue and shiny. Lily and Tom were happy. They said hello to the eggs. "Hello, eggs. We are Lily and Tom. We are your friends. Do you want to play with us?" they asked. But the eggs did not answer. They were quiet and still. Lily and Tom were sad. They wanted the eggs to talk. Then they heard a loud sneeze. It came from behind the leaves. They looked and saw a big squirrel. He had a twig in his mouth. He was chewing it. He looked at Lily and Tom. He was angry. "Go away, go away. These are my eggs. They are tasty. I will eat them. You are not my friends. You are bad. Shoo, shoo!" he said. Lily and Tom were scared. They did not want the squirrel to eat the eggs. They liked the eggs. They wanted to save them. They grabbed the twig from the squirrel's mouth. They threw it away. They took the eggs in their hands. They climbed down the tree. They ran to the slide. They slid down. They put the eggs on the grass. They hoped the birds would find them. They looked at the squirrel. He was still in the tree. He was sad. He had no twig and no eggs. He wanted to eat. Lily and Tom felt sorry for the squirrel. They had an idea. They went to the bench. They saw a bag of nuts. They belonged to a lady. She was reading a book. She did not see them. Lily and Tom took some nuts. They ran back to the tree. They threw the nuts to the squirrel. He caught them. He ate them. He was happy. He smiled at Lily and Tom. "Thank you, thank you. You are good. You are my friends. I am sorry for being mean. Do you want to play with me?" he asked. Lily and Tom nodded. They climbed the tree again. They played with the squirrel. They had fun. They were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim was a fierce cat. He liked to run and jump and play all day. Tim had a favorite toy, a long string. He would chase the string and catch it with his paws. One day, Tim was playing with his string near a big cup of water. He jumped and accidentally knocked the cup over. Water spilled everywhere! Tim was scared. He didn't know what to do. Tim's friend, a helpful dog named Sam, came to the rescue. Sam saw the mess and knew just what to do. He found a soft cloth and started to wipe the water away. Tim helped Sam wipe the water, and soon the floor was dry. Tim was so happy that Sam helped him. They played with the string together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time there was a popular test. Everyone wanted to pass it. One day, a magical thing happened. The test started shrinking! Everyone who was taking the test got very scared! The people started to run and jump, but nothing worked. The test kept shrinking and shrinking. Then, the people started to shout. They all shouted really loudly, hoping for help. Just then, something magical happened! A fairy appeared! The fairy waved her wand and said a magical spell. The test stopped shrinking and went back to its normal size. Everyone was so happy! They hugged the fairy and thanked her for her help. The people all passed the test, and everyone lived happily ever after.
