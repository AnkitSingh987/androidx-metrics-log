Category: Beginner
Title: Ann the Girl and the Butterfly

There was a little girl named Ann. She loved to play with her colorful toys. One day, she saw a strip of paper on the floor. It was very pretty. Ann had an idea. She thought she could make a toy with the strip. Ann went to her friend, Tim. She showed him the strip. "Look, Tim! I found a pretty strip. Let's make a toy with it," Ann said. Tim was happy. They started to fold the strip. They made a beautiful butterfly. Suddenly, the butterfly began to move! It flew around them. Ann and Tim were so surprised. They didn't think the butterfly would come to life. They laughed and played with their new friend, the colorful butterfly.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was hungry, so he went to his mom and asked for some food. His mom said, "You can have a salad, Tim." Tim did not like salad, but he was very hungry. Tim took the salad and went to sit outside. The sun was shining and it was very hot. The sun was so harsh that it dried the grass and the leaves on the trees. Tim looked at the salad and thought, "Maybe if I put the salad in the sun, it will dry and taste better." Tim left the salad in the sun for a while. When he came back, the salad was dry and the leaves were crunchy. He took a bite of the salad and said, "Yuck! This is not good." The dry salad did not taste better at all. In the end, Tim was still hungry and sad. He learned that drying a salad in the harsh sun did not make it taste better. He wished he had listened to his mom and eaten the salad when it was fresh.

Lily and Tom were playing with a big red ball in the park. They liked to bounce the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they bounced it very high and it went over the fence. Then they had to run and get it back. One day, they bounced the ball too hard and it flew over the fence and into the street. They heard a loud noise and saw a big car hit the ball. The ball popped and went flat. Lily and Tom were very sad. They loved their ball. They ran to the fence and looked at the street. They saw a man get out of the car. He was holding the ball. He looked angry. He walked towards them. Lily and Tom were scared. He was unknown to them. They did not know what he would do. "Hey, you kids!" the man shouted. "Why did you throw this ball in the street? You could have caused an accident. This ball is ruined. You owe me a new one." Lily and Tom did not know what to say. They did not have any money. They did not mean to throw the ball in the street. They were sorry. They felt a lot of worry in their chests. "Please, mister, we are sorry," Lily said. "We did not want to hurt your car. We were just playing. We did not see you coming. We liked our ball. It was our favorite." The man looked at Lily and Tom. He saw their sad faces and their tears. He felt a little sorry for them. He remembered when he was a kid and he liked to play with balls too. He decided to be nice. "OK, OK, I understand," he said. "It was an accident. I'm sorry I yelled at you. Here, take this ball. It's not as big or as red as your old one, but it's still good. You can bounce it and play with it. Just be careful next time, OK?" He gave them a blue ball. It was not as big or as red as their old one, but it was still good. It could bounce and roll. Lily and Tom smiled. They thanked the man. They were happy. They hugged the ball. They did not feel worry anymore. They learned to be careful. They went back to the park and played with their new ball. They had fun.
