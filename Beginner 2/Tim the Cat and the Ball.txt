Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Cat and the Ball

One day, a little cat named Tim was very eager to play. He saw a big ball in the yard. Tim ran to the ball and began to rub his face on it. The ball was soft and fuzzy, just like Tim's fur. He liked how it felt on his face. As Tim was rubbing the ball, a small dog named Max came by. Max saw Tim having fun and wanted to play too. Max gave Tim a friendly wink and Tim knew that they could play together. Tim and Max played with the big ball all day. They took turns rubbing their faces on it and rolling it in the grass. They were very happy and tired by the end of the day. They both knew they had made a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run. One day, Spot saw a big red car. The car was going to drive by. Spot wanted to see the car up close. Spot ran to the car, but he did not see the bone on the ground. Spot stepped on the bone and fell down. His leg hurt a lot. Spot was sad because he could not see the car now. Later, Spot's friend, Max, came to help. Max picked up the bone and gave it to Spot. Spot was happy to have the bone. He forgot about his hurt leg. Spot and Max played with the bone all day.

Once upon a time, there was a tall building. The building was so tall that it could touch the sky. The building was in a big city with many people. One day, a girl named Lily went to the tall building with her mom. She wanted to see the whole city from the top. They went inside and took a ride in a box that went up and up. When they got to the top, Lily looked out the window and saw the whole city. She could see her house, the park, and even the school. Lily will always remember the day she went to the tall building with her mom. She loved seeing the city from so high up. Every time she looked at the building, she thought of the fun day they had together.

One day, a little pink bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a pipe on the ground. The pipe was big and long. The bird was curious and wanted to see what was inside the pipe. The bird went inside the pipe and saw a little mouse. The mouse was sad. The bird said, "Why are you sad, little mouse?" The mouse said, "I want to go outside, but I'm scared." The bird wanted to help, so it encouraged the mouse. "Don't be scared, little mouse. I will help you get outside." The bird and the mouse went through the pipe together. They saw a light at the end of the pipe. But, surprise! It was not the outside. It was a big room full of toys! The bird and the mouse were very happy. They played with the toys all day long. And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big family. They were very nice. One day, Mom asked Dad to give her the ashtray. Dad looked and looked, but he could not find it. He felt sad because he wanted to help Mom. Dad asked his little girl, Amy, to help him find the ashtray. Amy looked under the table and found the ashtray. It was hard and round. She gave it to Dad with a big smile. Dad was very happy and said, "Thank you, Amy!" Dad gave the hard ashtray to Mom. Mom was happy too. They all clapped for Amy. She felt proud of herself. The family was happy and they all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big white ball in his yard. Tim wanted to play with the white ball, so he ran to it. Tim tried to pick up the white ball, but it was too big. He decided to push it instead. As he pushed the white ball, it rolled and got bigger and bigger. Tim was so happy to see the white ball grow. Tim's friend, Sam, saw him playing with the white ball. Sam asked, "Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes! Help me push the white ball." Together, they pushed the white ball until it was very big. Then, they sat down and shared a tasty snack. They both had a fun day playing with the white ball.
