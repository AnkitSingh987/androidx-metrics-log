Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat Was Walking and the Dog

One day, a little cat was walking in the rain. She was wet and cold. She saw an old house and went inside to get dry. In the old house, there was a persistent dog who lived there. The dog said, "Hi, cat! Why are you wet?" The cat said, "It is raining outside. I want to get dry." The dog said, "I can help you. I know how to get dry." The dog shook his body to get dry. The cat saw this and tried to do the same. She shook and shook until she was dry too. They both felt warm and happy. The cat thanked the dog for helping her. From that day on, the cat and the persistent dog became good friends. They played and had fun in the old house. When it rained, they knew how to get dry together. They were always there for each other, no matter the weather.

Once upon a time, there was a new school. In this school, there were many kids. They liked to learn and play together. The kids had a nice teacher. The teacher taught them many things. One day, the teacher taught the kids about math. She showed them how to number things. They learned to count one, two, three, and more. The kids were happy and excited to learn. The kids practiced their math every day. They counted toys, fingers, and even their friends. They loved their new math skills. They were proud to show their teacher and parents. And they all lived happily, learning more each day.

Momma was helping her three-year-old daughter build figures out of Play-Doh, when she asked her little girl, “Do you like learning?” The daughter nodded her head excitedly and said, “Yes! I love learning new things. What do I learn today?” Momma smiled and said, “Today, you will learn how to make tough figures with the Play-Doh. It may be tough, but you can do it!” The daughter began to shape the Play-Doh. She rolled, squeezed and pinched it until she had made a figure. Momma clapped and cheered. The daughter looked up and smiled. “I can learn to make any figure I want,” she said. Momma nodded and said, “You sure can! Learning new things can be tough, but you’re doing a great job!”

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But they did not like Ben. Ben was mean. He always took their toys and pushed them. He made them cry. They hated Ben. One day, Lily and Tom found a ball. It was blue and shiny. They wanted to play with it. They threw it to each other and laughed. They had fun. But Ben saw them. He wanted the ball. He ran to them and grabbed it. He said, "This is my ball now. Go away!" Lily and Tom were sad and angry. They said, "No, Ben, that is our ball. Give it back!" But Ben did not listen. He ran away with the ball. He said, "Ha ha, you can't catch me!" He was rude. Lily and Tom chased Ben. They wanted their ball back. They ran and ran. But Ben was fast. He ran to the road. He did not look. He did not see the car. The car was big and loud. It hit Ben. Ben fell down. He dropped the ball. He did not move. He was hurt. Lily and Tom stopped. They saw Ben. They saw the car. They saw the ball. They were scared and sad. They did not hate Ben anymore. They felt sorry for him. They ran to him and tried to help him. They called for help. They hoped he would be okay. But he was not. He died. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The park was big and had many fun things to play with. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide down the slide. While playing, Tim saw a man dressed up like a skeleton. The man said, "Hi, Tim! I am Mr. Skeleton. I want to show you something." Tim was not scared and said, "Okay, show me!" Mr. Skeleton took Tim to a big empty box. He opened the box and showed Tim many colorful balls inside. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Skeleton!" They played together with the balls and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Tim's mom called him to go home. Tim said goodbye to Mr. Skeleton and gave him a big hug. Tim and his mom went home, and Tim told her all about his fun day with Mr. Skeleton at the park. From that day on, every time Tim went to the park, he looked for Mr. Skeleton to play with. They became very good friends and always had fun together.
