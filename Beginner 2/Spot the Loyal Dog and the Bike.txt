Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Bike

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim got a new bike with pedals. Tim was so happy and wanted to ride it all day long. Spot wanted to help Tim and make his bike look pretty. So, Spot found some paint and decided to decorate the bike. He painted the bike with bright colors and put a big, happy face on it. Tim saw the bike and was very surprised. At first, Tim was upset because he didn't want his bike to be painted. But then, he saw how hard Spot worked to make the bike pretty. Tim realized that Spot was just trying to help and show his love. In the end, Tim and Spot rode the bike together, happy and proud of their new, colorful bike.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce lion named Leo. He lived in a big forest with many friends. Leo loved to sleep under a big tree. One day, Leo's friend, a little bird named Bella, came to visit him. "Leo, let's play!" said Bella. "Okay," said Leo, "but first, I need to sleep. I am very tired." So, Leo slept under the tree while Bella waited. When Leo woke up, they played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. At the end of the day, they were both very happy. "We had a lot of success playing today, Bella!" said Leo. "Yes, we did, Leo!" said Bella. They hugged and said goodbye. Then, Leo went back to sleep under the big tree, dreaming of more fun days with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. She loved to laugh and play with her toys. One day, Mia decided to have a tea party with her teddy bear, Mr. Snuggles. She put on her favorite dress and set the table with cups and plates. "Mr. Snuggles, would you like some tea?" Mia asked her teddy bear. "Yes, please," she said, pretending to be Mr. Snuggles. Mia poured some tea into the cups. It was a special tea that her mommy had made for her. It smelled delicious, and Mia knew it would taste good too. As Mia and Mr. Snuggles enjoyed their tea party, Mia's mommy came into the room. "What a lovely tea party!" she said. "Can I join you?" Mia nodded and poured her mommy a cup of tea. They all laughed and had a great time at the tea party. Mia's mommy told her that she was a wonderful host and that they should have more tea parties in the future.

Lily is a bunny who likes to hop. She hops in the grass, she hops in the park, she hops in the snow. She hops all day long, and she is very happy. One day, Lily finds a big house. She hops to the door and peeks inside. She sees a lot of rooms with many things. She hops into one room that has a big bed, a soft rug, and a light on the wall. She thinks it is a nice room. She hops on the bed and feels the soft pillows. She hops on the rug and feels the warm wool. She hops to the light and tries to touch it. But the light is too high and too hot. She burns her paw and cries out loud. She hears a noise and sees a boy coming into the room. He sees Lily and smiles. He picks her up and hugs her. He says, "Hello, little bunny. Are you lost? Did you hurt yourself? Don't worry, I will help you." He takes Lily to the kitchen and puts some ice on her paw. He gives her some water and some carrots. He tells her his name is Ben and he likes bunnies. He asks her if she wants to be his friend. Lily nods and licks his face. She thinks he is a nice boy. Ben and Lily become friends. He takes her to his room and shows her his toys. He plays with her and makes her laugh. He lets her hop on his bed, his rug, and his chair. He turns off the light and cuddles with her. He says, "Good night, little bunny. I'm glad you came to my house. You can stay with me as long as you want." Lily snuggles with him and feels happy. She thinks this is a nice house. She hops into his arms and falls asleep.
