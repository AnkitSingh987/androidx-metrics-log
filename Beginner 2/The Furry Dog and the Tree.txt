Category: Beginner
Title: The Furry Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog. The dog had a big wing. It was very odd. The dog could fly with the wing. The dog liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the dog saw a big tree. The dog wanted to bite the tree. The dog flew to the tree and bit it. The tree was not a tree. It was a big green monster. The monster was not happy. The monster chased the dog. The dog flew away fast. The monster was too big to fly. The dog was safe. The monster and the dog became friends. They played together every day. The dog never bit the tree again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy because he had a magic toy. This magic toy could talk and play with Tim. Tim loved his magic toy very much. One day, Tim and his magic toy went to the park. They saw a little girl who was sad. She lost her toy and could not find it. Tim and his magic toy wanted to help the little girl. They tried to get her toy back. Tim, the magic toy, and the little girl looked everywhere. At last, they found the lost toy under a big tree. The little girl was so happy to get her toy back. She said "thank you" to Tim and his magic toy. The moral of the story is: it is good to help others when they need it.

One day, a little spider went for a walk. The spider had wide legs that helped it walk fast. It wanted to find a friend to play with. The spider saw a big dog and asked, "Will you play with me?" The dog just looked at the spider and gave a shrug. The spider felt sad and kept walking. Then, the spider saw a tiny ant. The ant said, "Let's play together!" The spider was happy and they played all day. The spider and the ant became best friends.

One day, a little dog went for a walk. The dog liked to bite things. He saw a big ball and bit it. The ball was wet from the rain. The dog liked the wet ball and played with it. The dog walked up a hill. He saw a nice view from the top. He saw many trees, birds, and other dogs playing. The dog was happy to see the view. He wanted to play with the other dogs. As the dog ran down the hill to play, he saw that the other dogs were not dogs at all! They were big, wet frogs! The dog was surprised but still wanted to play. The frogs liked to bite too, so they all played with the wet ball together.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. He liked to tease a little mouse named Jerry. Tom would wink at Jerry and then run after him. They played this game every day. One day, Tom and Jerry were playing their game when they saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play, too. But the dog was not nice like Tom and Jerry. He wanted to catch them both. Tom and Jerry had an idea. Instead of teasing each other, they would tease the dog. They winked at the dog and ran away together. The dog chased them, but he could not catch them. Tom and Jerry were too fast. In the end, Tom and Jerry became friends. They learned that it was more fun to play together than to tease each other. And the big dog? He learned not to mess with Tom and Jerry.

Once upon a time, there was an old house. The house had many cobwebs. In the house, there lived a kind spider. One day, a little girl came to the old house. She looked at the cobwebs and saw the spider. The spider was not scary. The spider was nice. The spider wanted to help the little girl. The spider said, "I can offer you a pretty cobweb." The little girl smiled and said, "Thank you, kind spider." They became good friends and played together in the old house.
