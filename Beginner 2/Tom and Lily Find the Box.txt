Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Box

Tom and Lily liked to play with candles. They liked to watch the flame dance and make shadows on the wall. But they knew they had to be careful, because candles could burn things and hurt them. One day, they found a big candle in the kitchen. It smelled like vanilla and had a pretty picture on it. They wanted to light it and see how it looked. They took a box of matches from the drawer and went to their room. "Let me do it," Tom said. He took a match and struck it on the box. It made a spark and then a flame. He held it close to the candle wick. The candle lit up and smelled nice. "Wow, it's so pretty," Lily said. She leaned closer to see the flame. She touched it with her finger. It was hot and hurt her. She screamed and dropped the match on the bed. The bed caught fire. The fire spread quickly and made smoke and noise. Tom and Lily were scared and ran out of the room. They called for their mom and dad. Mom and dad came and saw the fire. They were angry and sad. They tried to put out the fire with water and a blanket. But it was too late. The fire had burned the bed, the curtains, the toys, and the clothes. The room was ruined. Tom and Lily cried and said they were sorry. They wished they had not played with the candle. They wished they had replaced it with a toy or a book. They learned a hard lesson. They were not successful. They were foolish.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max liked to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Max saw a telephone on the ground. It was ringing! Max barked, "Woof, woof!" to warn his friend, Sam the cat. Sam came running and looked at the telephone. He said, "Meow, what is that?" Max said, "Woof, I don't know, but it's ringing!" Sam the cat was brave. He touched the telephone with his paw. It stopped ringing. Max and Sam looked at each other and laughed. They went back to playing with the ball and running in the park. They were happy, and they always remembered the day they found the ringing telephone.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. It was red and shiny. He rode it every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue. She was very serious. Sue said, "I want to race you on my bike." Tim said, "Okay, let's race!" They lined up their bikes and started to ride. Tim was fast, but Sue was faster. They went up and down hills, and around trees. Tim wanted to win. He pedaled hard and passed Sue. Tim crossed the finish line first. He felt victory. Tim said, "I won!" Sue said, "Good job, Tim. You are fast." They both smiled and rode their bikes home.

Once upon a time, there was a store. This store was not like any other store. It was the first store, the original store. It had toys, food, and many fun things. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw many things he liked. He saw a big red ball, a fluffy teddy bear, and a toy car. Tim was very happy. While Tim was playing with the toys, he felt a sneeze coming. "Achoo!" Tim sneezed very loud. His mom heard him and came running. She gave him a tissue and said, "Bless you, Tim!" Tim smiled, and they went home with a new toy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It was very big and strong. A little bird lived in the tree. The bird had a friend who was a squirrel. They liked to play together. One day, a new neighbor came to live in the tree. It was a small bug. The bug said, "Hello, can I live in the tree too?" The bird and the squirrel looked at the bug. The squirrel said, "I don't know. You are very small. What can you do?" The bug thought for a moment and said, "I can help you find food. I know where all the good seeds are." The bird and the squirrel talked and decided to accept the bug as their new neighbor. From that day on, the bird, the squirrel, and the bug lived happily in the tall tree. They played together and helped each other find food. They were the best of friends, and the tree was a happy home for all of them.
