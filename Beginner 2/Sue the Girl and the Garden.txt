Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to work in her garden. She had a friend named Max. Max was a silly dog who liked to play all day. One day, Max saw a big ball in the garden. Max wanted to play with the ball, but Sue said, "No, Max, we have to work first." Max did not want to work. He wanted to play with the ball. Max thought of a plan. He would hide the ball and play later. Max dug a hole and put the ball in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. Sue saw Max and said, "That was a good idea, Max. Now, let's work." They worked in the garden all day. When they were done, Max went to find his ball. But Max forgot where he hid it! Sue saw Max looking for the ball. She said, "Max, if you had worked with me, you would know where the ball is." Max felt sad. He learned that it is good to work and play together. From that day on, Max always helped Sue in the garden, and they played with the ball after work.

Ben liked to stretch his arms and legs every morning. He felt big and strong when he did that. He also liked to listen to stories that his mom read to him. He had many books with pictures and words that he could look at and learn. One day, Ben saw a new book on the shelf. It had a picture of a big wolf with sharp teeth and red eyes. Ben was curious and wanted to hear the story. He asked his mom to read it to him, but she said no. She said it was a scary story and not for little boys like him. Ben was troubled. He wanted to know what the wolf did and why it was scary. He waited until his mom was busy in the kitchen and then he stretched his arm to reach the book. He pulled it down and opened it. He could not read the words, but he saw more pictures of the wolf. The wolf was chasing a girl in a red hood, a boy in a straw house, and three pigs in a brick house. The wolf looked angry and hungry. Ben felt afraid. He wished he had listened to his mom and left the book alone. He wanted to close it and put it back, but he was too scared to touch it. He started to cry and call for his mom. His mom heard him and came to see what was wrong. She saw the book and the tears on his face. She was angry and sad. She took the book from him and put it away. She scolded him for being naughty and disobedient. She said he could not listen to any stories for a week. She sent him to his room without a hug or a kiss. Ben felt sorry and ashamed. He wished he could say sorry and hug his mom. He wished he could stretch his arms and legs and feel big and strong again. But he could not. He lay on his bed and sobbed. He had a bad day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They liked to play together all day. One day, they were playing in the park and Jack decided to skip. Jill watched as Jack skipped from rock to rock. He hopped over the highest one with ease! After watching Jack, Jill wanted to try too. But she was scared. She looked around, and Jack noticed that she was afraid. He smiled and said, "It's easy, I'll show you." He took one of her hands and placed it on the big rock. He asked her to touch it and to feel how smooth it was. He said, "It's so easy to skip... just try one and see. The sky will be so happy with us!" Together they skipped around the rock, and soon their faces were beaming with joy. Jill remembered the lesson and said, "Friendship makes everything easy." The moral of this story is that with a friend by your side, anything is possible. Friendship makes it easier to do anything, even the things that seem scary!
