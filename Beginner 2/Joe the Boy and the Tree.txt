Category: Beginner
Title: Joe the Boy and the Tree

Once there was a little boy named Joe. He was three years old and very curious. One day, Joe was looking out of the window. He saw a big tree in the garden. It was full of impressive knots. Joe wanted to try and climb the tree. "Mum, can I climb the tree? Please?", asked Joe. "I don't know Joe. It looks a bit high and tricky. But you can sit under the tree if you like. I will help you," said his mum. So Joe sat under the tree. He asked his mum to tie a knot in the rope he was holding. Joe was so excited to have a rope with a knot. The next day, Joe got up early and went to the tree. He took the rope and started to climb it! The rope had the knot he asked his mum to do and it helped him to get to the top of the tree. Joe was so impressed with himself. When Joe reached the top, he looked down and saw his mum looking up with pride. Joe's mum smiled and said, "I knew you could do it, Joe!"

One day, a girl named Mia found an unusual bubble. It was big and shiny. Mia loved to play with bubbles, so she was very happy. She talked to her friend Tom about the bubble. "Look at this big bubble, Tom!" she said. Tom was excited too. Tom and Mia played with the unusual bubble all day. They ran and jumped with it. But then, the bubble popped. Mia and Tom were sad. They didn't know how to fix the bubble. Mia had an idea. "Let's ask Dad to help us repair it!" she said. Dad came and looked at the popped bubble. He knew what to do. He took out a special bubble wand and waved it in the air. Suddenly, the unusual bubble was back! Mia and Tom were so surprised and happy. They thanked Dad and played with the bubble all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. He had so much fun with his friends in the park. One day, Spot was playing with his ball. He threw it very far and it went into the woods. Spot went to look for his ball, but he could not find it. He started to feel sad because he did not want to lose his ball. Then, a smart bird saw Spot looking for his ball. The bird used its brain and helped Spot find his ball. Spot was so happy to have his ball back. From that day on, Spot and the bird became best friends and played together all the time.

Anna likes to jump and run and play. She goes to the gym with her mom and dad. The gym has many things to do. There are mats and balls and ropes and swings. Anna is excited to try them all. At the gym, Anna meets a boy named Ben. Ben is also excited to play. He shows Anna how to swing on the rope. Anna shows Ben how to roll on the mat. They have fun together. They share the balls and help each other. Anna and Ben become friends. They smile and laugh and hug. They say goodbye when the gym is over. They hope to see each other again. Anna and Ben are happy. They like the gym and they like each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind and compassionate man named Tim. Tim loved to help people. One day, he saw a little boy who was very sad. The boy had lost his ticket to the fun fair. Tim wanted to help the boy feel better. Tim said, "Don't worry, little boy. I will help you find your ticket." They looked everywhere, but they could not find the ticket. The little boy started to suffer because he really wanted to go to the fun fair. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "I have an extra ticket. You can have it, and we can go to the fun fair together!" The little boy was so happy, and they had a great time at the fun fair. In the end, the little boy learned that when you are kind and compassionate, like Tim, you can make others happy. And that is the best feeling in the world.
