Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Song

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. Every day she would play with her cell. She would say hello to it and make it sing. One day, her cell gave her a surprise. It sang a special song and gave her a hug. Lucy was so happy! But then something scary happened. A big bug flew into the cell and made a buzzing sound. Lucy was scared and started to cry. But her cell was there to help. It gave her a hug and made the bug go away. Lucy smiled again and her cell sang her a happy song. Lucy knew that she could always rely on her cell. She felt so safe and happy.

One day, a boy named Tim found a marble. It was a good marble. It was very pretty. Tim liked the marble a lot. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim went to Sue's house. "Look at my good marble!" Tim said to Sue. Sue looked at the marble. She liked it too. "Can I play with the marble?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can play with it." Sue took the marble and they played a game. But then, Sue dropped the marble. It rolled far away. Tim and Sue were sad. They looked for the marble together. They found the marble under a big tree. Tim and Sue were happy again. They played with the marble all day. They had lots of fun with their good marble.

One day, a curious little cat named Paws saw a big pile of junk in the yard. Paws liked to play with new things, so he went to the junk to see what he could find. He saw a strange thing with a long hose, but he did not know what it was. Paws played with the hose and found a button on the strange thing. He pressed the button and water started to spray from the hose! Paws was so surprised, but he liked how the water felt on his fur. He played with the hose some more and found he could make the water spray far or close. Later, Paws saw his friend Bunny hopping by. Bunny was very hot from playing in the sun. Paws had an idea to help Bunny cool off. He took the hose and sprayed water on Bunny. Bunny was so happy to be cool and wet. Paws and Bunny played with the hose all day, spraying water and having fun. The strange thing from the junk pile became their favorite new toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear her pretty skirt when she went to the park. One sunny day, she put on her skirt and went to the park to play. At the park, Sue met a big dog. The dog wanted to play with Sue, but he was too rough. He jumped up and grabbed Sue's skirt with his teeth. Sue was scared and didn't know what to do. Sue's mom saw what happened and quickly came to help. She told the dog to let go of Sue's skirt. The dog listened and let go. Sue was happy that her mom saved her. From that day on, Sue learned to be careful around big dogs at the park.

Once there was a girl named Jane who was very hungry. She went on a walk and saw a wreck in a park. She wanted to play with the wreck but was unsure how. Then she saw a boy playing with the wreck. Jane was very excited and asked the boy what he was doing. The boy said he was playing a game and asked if she wanted to join. She said yes and they had lots of fun playing the game. At the end, Jane was very happy and not so hungry anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who really wanted a pet owl. All of his friends had pets, so he really wanted one too. He asked his mom and dad, but they said it was too expensive. Then one day he heard his parents mention a cheap pet owl at the pet store. The little boy was so excited! He asked his mom and dad if he could go to the pet store and buy the cheap owl. They said yes, so the little boy got ready and set off for the pet store. He was so happy! When he arrived at the pet store, the little boy found the cheap owl. But it was very small and scared. The little boy didn't think it seemed very happy. He wanted to make sure the owl was safe, so he asked the pet store worker if he could keep it. The pet store worker said no. He said the owl was too small and it would be better off in the wild. The little boy was very sad and sobbed all the way home. He had to leave the owl at the pet store, and he never got his pet owl.
