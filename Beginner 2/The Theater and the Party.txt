Category: Beginner
Title: The Theater and the Party

Once upon a time, there was a nice little theater in a small town. The theater had many fun shows for kids to watch. One day, a group of friends went to the theater to see a show. They were very excited. In the show, there was a girl named Sally who wanted to manage a big party. She asked her friend Tom, "Can you help me with the party?" Tom said, "Yes, let's make it the best party ever!" Sally and Tom worked together to make the party fun. They had yummy food, games, and music. All their friends came and had a great time. In the end, Sally and Tom managed to make the best party in town. Everyone was happy and they all clapped for Sally and Tom.

One day, a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue wanted to have a race. They each had a kite to fly. Tom had a big, red kite and Sue had a small, blue kite. They went to the park to race their kites. Tom said, "Let's see who can make their kite go up the highest!" Sue thought that was a silly idea, but she agreed to race. They started to run with their kites, and the kites went up in the sky. As they raced, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and mixed up their kites! Tom's red kite and Sue's blue kite got stuck together in the sky. They both stopped running and looked up. Tom said, "Oh no! Our kites are stuck!" Sue laughed and said, "That's so silly!" They both tried to pull their kites down, but they couldn't. The wind was too strong. In the end, Tom and Sue became friends and shared the two kites stuck together. They had fun flying the kites even though they didn't finish the race. They learned that sometimes, silly things can bring people together.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. Lily has a ball and Ben has a stick. Ben wants the ball, but Lily says no. Ben is angry and tries to take the ball from Lily. Lily runs away and Ben chases her. Lily sees a big tree and runs behind it. She thinks Ben cannot find her there. But Ben is persistent and follows her. He sees her hiding behind the tree and swings his stick. He strikes the tree hard and makes a loud noise. Lily is scared and cries out. She drops the ball and comes out from behind the tree. She sees Ben with his stick and is angry. She says, "You are mean, Ben! You hurt the tree and you scared me! You cannot have the ball!" Ben feels sorry and drops his stick. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I was wrong. I should not have struck the tree and scared you. Can we be friends again?" Lily picks up the ball and looks at Ben. She sees that he is sad and sorry. She says, "OK, Ben. We can be friends again. But you have to be nice and share. Do you want to play with the ball with me?" Ben nods and smiles. He says, "Yes, Lily. I want to play with the ball with you. Thank you for being my sister and forgiving me." Lily and Ben hug and play with the ball together. They are happy and forget their fight. They love each other and the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion who lived in a cage at the zoo. He was very big and strong. He had a big mane and sharp teeth. One day, the lion saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The lion wanted to help the bird, but he was stuck in the cage. The little bird tried to fly, but it fell down. The lion felt sad for the bird. He thought of a plan. He used his strong teeth to open the cage. The lion was free! He walked up to the bird and bowed down to it. The bird hopped on the lion's back. The lion took the bird to a nice tree. The bird's friends helped it get better. The bird was very happy. The lion went back to the cage, but the zoo people made it bigger and nicer for him. The lion and the bird became good friends, and they lived happily ever after.
