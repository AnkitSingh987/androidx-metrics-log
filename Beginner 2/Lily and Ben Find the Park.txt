Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Park

Lily and Ben are friends who like to play in the park. One day, they find a cricket on the grass. It is small and green and makes a funny noise. Lily and Ben want to keep the cricket as a pet. But they have a problem. They only have one jar to put the cricket in. Lily says the jar is hers and she saw the cricket first. Ben says the jar is his and he likes the cricket more. They start to fight over the jar. They pull and push and shout. The cricket is scared and jumps out of the jar. Lily and Ben stop fighting. They see the cricket hop away. They feel sad and sorry. They say sorry to each other and hug. They decide to be creative and make a new jar for the cricket. They find an empty bottle and some leaves and sticks. They make a nice home for the cricket and put it in the bottle. They share the bottle and take turns to hold it. They listen to the cricket sing. They are happy and friends again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a crazy game. The game was to match things in the soil. Tim wanted to play the game. He went outside to the garden and started to dig in the soil. He found a big rock and a small rock. He matched them and put them in a line. Tim kept digging and found more things to match. He found a red flower and a blue flower. He matched them too. Then, he found a big stick and a small stick. He matched them and put them in the line. Tim was having fun, but he did not see the angry ants. The ants did not like Tim digging in their home. They wanted to stop him. The ants made a plan. They would bite Tim when he was not looking. So, the ants climbed up Tim's leg and bit him. Tim yelled and ran away. The ants were happy, but Tim was sad. The game was not fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely rocket. The rocket was very sad because it had no friends. It lived in a big space with many stars, but no one wanted to play with it. One day, the lonely rocket saw a man. The man wanted to go to the moon. The rocket thought, "If I help the man, maybe he will be my friend." So, the rocket let the man get inside and took him to the moon. The man was happy and said he would pay the rocket. But when they got to the moon, the man did not pay the rocket. The man found new friends on the moon and forgot about the rocket. The rocket was very, very sad. It went back to its lonely space, and it never found a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a soft toy duck. The duck lived in a bathtub. Every day, the duck would play with a little boy named Tim. Tim loved his soft duck very much. One day, Tim and the duck were playing in the bathtub. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim saw a big spider. The spider wanted to destroy the bathtub. Tim was scared, and the soft duck wanted to help. The soft duck jumped out of the bathtub and chased the spider away. The spider could not destroy the bathtub anymore. Tim was so happy, he hugged the soft duck. From that day on, they played in the bathtub without any worries. And they lived happily ever after.

Ben and Mia are adventurous twins. They like to play outside and explore new things. One day, they go to the beach with their mom and dad. They see the big blue water and the soft yellow sand. They are very happy. "Look, Ben, there is a boat!" Mia says, pointing to a white boat on the water. "It is going very fast. It is speeding!" "Wow, Mia, you are right. The boat is speeding. It looks fun. Do you want to go on the boat?" Ben asks, smiling. "Yes, Ben, I want to go on the boat. But we have to ask mom and dad first. Come on, let's go!" Mia says, grabbing Ben's hand. They run to their mom and dad, who are sitting on a blanket under an umbrella. "Mom, dad, can we go on the boat? Please, please, please!" Ben and Mia say together, jumping up and down. "Which boat, sweeties?" Mom asks, looking at the water. "That boat, mom, the white one. It is speeding. It looks fun. Please, can we go?" Mia says, pointing to the boat. Mom and dad look at the boat. They see that it is a small boat with a friendly man and a cute dog on it. The man is waving at them and the dog is barking. "Sure, sweeties, you can go on the boat. But you have to wear these vests and hold on tight. And listen to the man and the dog. They will take good care of you. OK?" Dad says, giving them orange vests and helping them put them on. "OK, dad, we will. Thank you, thank you, thank you!" Ben and Mia say, hugging their mom and dad. They run to the boat, where the man and the dog are waiting for them. "Hello, little ones. I'm Sam and this is Spot. We are going to take you for a ride on the water. Are you ready?" Sam says, smiling. "Yes, Sam, we are ready. We are Ben and Mia. We are adventurous twins. We like to speed!" Ben and Mia say, climbing on the boat. "Great, Ben and Mia. You are adventurous twins. You will like to speed. Hold on tight, here we go!" Sam says, starting the boat. The boat speeds on the water, making big waves and splashes. Ben and Mia laugh and scream with joy. They feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They see the fish and the birds and the shells. They wave at their mom and dad, who wave back. They pet Spot, who wags his tail. They have a lot of fun. They are very happy.
