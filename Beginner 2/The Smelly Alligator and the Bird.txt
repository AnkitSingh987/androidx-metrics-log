Category: Beginner
Title: The Smelly Alligator and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a smelly alligator. He lived in a pond. He was very big and green. All the animals were scared of him. They did not want to play with him. One day, a little bird came to the pond. The alligator said, "Hello, bird. Do you want to play with me?" The bird was scared, but it did not want to be mean. So, the bird said, "Okay, let's play." They played all day. They splashed in the water and had a lot of fun. But then, the alligator got hungry. He wanted to eat the bird. The bird knew it had to escape. The bird flew up high in the sky. The alligator could not catch it. The bird was safe. The alligator was sad. He learned that he should not try to eat his friends. From that day on, the alligator was nice. He did not scare the other animals. They all played together at the pond. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty bird named Bob. Bob liked to fly around and see things. One day, Bob saw a big tree with a lot of twigs. He thought it would be fun to play with the twigs. Bob took a twig and flew away. He saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy. Bob had an idea. He flew back to the tree and got more twigs. Bob worked hard to make a toy with the twigs for Tim. When he was done, he gave the twig toy to Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked Bob. From that day on, Bob was not naughty anymore. He learned that it was good to help others and be nice.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to sleep at night. He wanted to play with his toys all the time. His mommy and daddy tried to make him sleep, but he would not listen. One night, a friendly ghost came to visit Tim. The ghost said, "Hi Tim, I am a ghost. I can help you sleep if you want." Tim was not scared of the ghost. He thought the ghost was funny. He said, "Okay, Mr. Ghost, let's try." The ghost started to sing a soft song. Tim began to feel sleepy. He tried to fight it, but the ghost's song was too strong. Tim managed to close his eyes and fell asleep. The ghost was happy that he could help Tim. The next morning, Tim woke up feeling good. He told his mommy and daddy about the ghost who helped him sleep. They were happy that Tim had a good night's sleep. From that day on, Tim was not stubborn about bedtime anymore, and the friendly ghost would visit him every night to help him sleep.

One day, a little bird sat on a twig. The bird was sad because its wing hurt. It could not fly and play with its friends. The bird saw a boy walking by and called out, "Help, my wing hurts!" The boy heard the bird and came over. He saw the hurt wing and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you." The boy gently held the bird and fixed its wing with a soft band. The bird felt better and said, "Thank you, kind boy." Just then, a big wind came and blew the twig away. The boy and the bird clapped as the twig flew high into the sky like a magic stick. They laughed and became good friends. From that day on, the bird and the boy played together and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with puzzles. They had many puzzles with animals, cars, and flowers. One day, Lily got a new puzzle with a big castle and a princess. She was very happy and wanted to show Ben. She called Ben and said, "Ben, come to my house. I have a new puzzle. It is very pretty and fun." Ben was curious and said, "OK, I will come. What is your puzzle?" Lily said, "It is a castle with a princess. You will see." Ben went to Lily's house and knocked on the door. Lily opened the door and said, "Hi, Ben. Come in. My puzzle is in the living room." Ben entered the house and followed Lily to the living room. He saw the puzzle on the table. It was very big and colorful. He saw the castle, the princess, and the flowers. He said, "Wow, Lily. Your puzzle is very nice. Can I help you?" Lily said, "Yes, you can. But you have to be careful. The pieces are small and delicate. Don't break them." Ben said, "OK, I will be careful. Where do we start?" Lily said, "Let's start with the castle. It is the hardest part." They started to look for the pieces that matched the castle. They found some and put them together. They were having fun. But then, Ben saw a piece that had the princess's face. He liked the princess. She had a pretty dress and a crown. He wanted to put the piece in the puzzle. He reached for the piece. But Lily saw him and said, "No, Ben. That is my piece. I want to put the princess in the puzzle. She is mine." Ben said, "But I want to help. I like the princess too. Can't we share?" Lily said, "No, we can't. This is my puzzle. You have your own puzzles. Go away." Ben felt sad and angry. He said, "You are mean, Lily. You are not a good friend. You are jealous. I don't want to play with you anymore." He grabbed the piece with the princess's face and ran out of the house. He didn't say goodbye. Lily was shocked and upset. She said, "Ben, come back. Don't take my piece. Give it back." But Ben didn't listen. He ran away with the piece. He didn't care. Lily cried and cried. She looked at her puzzle. It was incomplete. The princess had no face. She felt lonely and sorry. She wished she had shared with Ben. She wished he was still her friend.
