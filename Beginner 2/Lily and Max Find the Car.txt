Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max Find the Car

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play in their bedroom. They have many toys and books and a big bed. One day, they decide to play race cars. They use their toy cars and a long scarf. They tie the scarf to the bed and make a ramp. They take turns to push their cars down the ramp and see who is faster. "Ready, set, go!" Lily says. She pushes her red car down the ramp. It zooms fast and lands on the floor. "Wow, Lily, your car is fast!" Max says. He pushes his blue car down the ramp. It also zooms fast, but it leans to the side and hits the wall. "Oh no, Max, your car is broken!" Lily says. She runs to pick up his car. She sees a dent and a scratch on it. "I'm sorry, Max. Do you want to play something else?" Lily asks. Max looks at his car and frowns. He is sad. He loves his car. He shakes his head. "No, I don't want to play. I want to fix my car." Max says. Lily hugs him and says, "Don't worry, Max. We can fix your car together. We can use some tape and some paint. It will be good as new." Max smiles and says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. I love you." Lily says, "I love you too, Max. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and find some tape and paint." They hold hands and leave their bedroom. They are happy. They are twins.

Tim had a lively hamster named Nibbles. He liked to play with Nibbles and feed him seeds and carrots. Sometimes, he put Nibbles in his pocket and took him to the park. One day, Tim was at the park with Nibbles in his pocket. He saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. He ran to the slide and climbed up the ladder. He forgot about Nibbles in his pocket. Nibbles felt the pocket move and shake. He did not like it. He wanted to get out. He bit the pocket and made a hole. He crawled out of the hole and ran away. He escaped. Tim slid down the slide and had fun. He did not notice that Nibbles was gone. He got off the slide and reached for his pocket. He felt the hole and saw no Nibbles. He was sad and scared. He looked for Nibbles everywhere. Nibbles was scared too. He saw many big people and big dogs and big birds. He did not know where to go. He hid under a bench and hoped Tim would find him. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom helped him look for Nibbles. They asked other people and animals if they saw Nibbles. They did not give up. After a long time, they found Nibbles under the bench. Tim was happy and hugged Nibbles. Nibbles was happy and licked Tim. Tim said sorry to Nibbles and promised to be more careful. He put Nibbles back in his pocket and held it tight. He thanked his mom and everyone who helped him. He learned a lesson. He and Nibbles went home.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a beautiful sunset in the sky. The colors were very pretty. Mia thought it was interesting. Mia met a boy named Tom. Tom said, "Look at the sunset! It's so nice!" Mia agreed, "Yes, it's very interesting!" Mia and Tom wanted to keep the sunset. They asked their mom if they could receive a picture of the sunset. Their mom took a photo and gave it to them. Now, Mia and Tom can always look at the interesting sunset. They were very happy.

One day, Sarah was writing with her crayons. She wrote a big chicken on her paper. It was a fake chicken, not a real one. She was so proud of her chicken! Sarah showed her mom what she wrote. Her mom was so happy for her. She was really impressed with how Sarah wrote the fake chicken so nicely. Her mom gave her a hug and said, “You are a great writer!” Sarah smiled from ear to ear. Sarah kept writing with her crayons. She wrote lots of things, like trees, flowers, and more chickens, but none of them were fake. They were all real. She was so proud of herself and the things she was writing.
