Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat food with sauce. He was very patient when he waited for his mom to make it for him. One day, Tim's mom told him she had a surprise for him in the kitchen. Tim ran to the kitchen and saw three bowls. Each bowl had a different sauce. Tim's mom told him to point at the one he wanted to eat. Tim pointed at the red sauce, and his mom put it on his food. Tim was very happy and started to eat. As Tim ate the red sauce, he made a funny face. It was very spicy! Tim did not expect the sauce to be so hot. He quickly drank some water to cool his mouth. Tim's mom laughed and said, "Surprise! I made a new sauce for you to try." Tim laughed too, and they both enjoyed the rest of their meal.

Lily did not like yellow clothes. She liked pink and purple and blue. But today, her mom gave her a yellow dress to wear. Lily did not want to wear it. She said, "No, no, no! I do not like yellow!" Her mom said, "Lily, you have to wear this dress. It is a gift from your grandma. She made it for you with love. She will be very sad if you do not wear it." Lily did not care. She threw the dress on the floor and ran to her room. She cried and cried. She did not want to wear the yellow dress. Her mom followed her and knocked on the door. She said, "Lily, please open the door. I want to talk to you." Lily opened the door. She saw her mom holding the yellow dress. She said, "Go away! I do not want to see that dress!" Her mom said, "Lily, I know you do not like yellow. But you have to be kind and grateful. Your grandma worked very hard to make this dress for you. She loves you very much. She wants you to be happy. How do you think she will feel if you do not wear it?" Lily thought for a moment. She remembered her grandma. She remembered how she hugged her and kissed her and gave her cookies. She remembered how she read her stories and sang her songs. She loved her grandma very much. She did not want to make her sad. Lily said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was very rude. I will wear the yellow dress. I love grandma. I want to make her happy." Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "I'm proud of you, Lily. You are a good girl. You learned to resist your own feelings and think of others. That is a very important lesson. Come on, let's go and see grandma. She will be very happy to see you in the yellow dress." Lily put on the yellow dress. She looked at herself in the mirror. She still did not like yellow. But she liked her grandma more. She smiled and said, "Let's go, mom. I'm ready."

Anna and Ben were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and bridges and castles with the blocks. They had many colors and shapes of blocks to choose from. Anna wanted to make the tallest tower ever. She stacked the blocks as high as she could, but she needed more. She saw a big box of blocks on the shelf. She asked Ben to help her lift the box. "Ben, can you help me lift the box? I want more blocks for my tower," she said. Ben nodded and went to the shelf. He tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy for him. He pulled and pushed, but the box did not move. He felt frustrated and angry. He decided to use a chair to reach the box. He dragged the chair to the shelf and climbed on it. He grabbed the box and tried to lift it again. But he lost his balance and fell off the chair. The box fell with him and hit the floor. The blocks spilled out of the box and made a loud noise. Anna and Ben were both scared by the noise. They looked at the mess of blocks and the broken box. They heard their mom coming to the living room. They knew they were in trouble. They felt ashamed of what they had done. "What happened here?" their mom asked in a stern voice. She saw the chair, the box, and the blocks. She saw Anna and Ben hiding behind the couch. She was shocked by the scene. "Anna, Ben, come here right now," she said. "You need to explain what you were doing and why you made such a mess. And you need to apologize and clean up." Anna and Ben came out of their hiding place. They looked at their mom with sad eyes. They said they were sorry and that they just wanted to play with the blocks. They said they would help to clean up. Their mom was not happy, but she was also glad that they were not hurt. She hugged them and said she loved them. She said they should not lift things that were too heavy for them. She said they should ask for help and be careful. She said they should not use the chair to climb on the shelf. She said they should respect their toys and not break them. Anna and Ben nodded and said they understood. They said they would not do it again. They helped their mom to pick up the blocks and put them back in the box. They learned their lesson and felt better. They decided to play with something else. They still liked the blocks, but they did not want to lift the box anymore.
