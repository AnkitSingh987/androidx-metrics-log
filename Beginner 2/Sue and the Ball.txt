Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect rocks. One day, she went to the park to find more rocks for her collection. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a ball near a pitch. "Hi, I'm Sue. Do you want to help me find rocks?" she asked. Tim was polite and said, "Sure, I will help you." They looked for rocks near the pitch. But then, a big dog came and took Tim's ball. The dog ran away with the ball and Sue and Tim could not catch it. They were sad because they lost the ball and didn't find any rocks.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She saw a big box with a pretty ribbon. Mia thought it was incredible! She wanted to see what was inside, so she opened the box. Inside, she found some powder. Mia did not know what the powder was for, but she thought it looked interesting. Mia took the powder and went to her friend Tom's house. Tom looked at the powder and said, "Let's use it in our game!" They put some powder on the ground and played a game of hopscotch. But then, the powder started to make everything around it grow big and tall! Mia and Tom looked at each other and knew they had to fix this problem. Mia and Tom quickly found a big broom and swept the powder away. As they did, everything started to go back to normal size. They were happy that they could fix the problem. Mia put the rest of the powder back in the box and hid it so no one else would find it. From that day on, Mia and Tom knew that some things are better left alone.

Once there was a little boy and his name was Tim. Tim had a best friend and her name was Jane. Jane was small and warm. Tim and Jane loved to play together. One day, they wanted to play a special game. The game was called surrender. Jane said:" I surrender". Tim laughed and said:"No, you can't". But Jane was very brave and she put her hands in the air and said:"I surrender, I surrender". Tim laughed and smiled. He could see Jane's eye. It was warm and happy. Tim got happy too and he put his hands in the air and said:"I surrender too!". That was the best game ever played. They laughed and hugged each other. Jane and Tim were happy surrendering. It was a warm moment they will remember forever.

Tom and Lily were playing in the attic. They liked to look at the old things that their grandma kept there. They saw a big box with a lock and a key. They wanted to open it and see what was inside. "Maybe there are toys or books or jewels," Lily said. "Or maybe there are spiders or rats or bones," Tom said. They found the key on a shelf and put it in the lock. It made a click sound and the box opened. They saw a shiny thing inside. It was a wire with a ball at the end. It looked like a star. "What is it?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe it's a magic thing," Tom said. He reached for the wire and touched the ball. It made a zap sound and a spark. Tom felt a shock in his hand and let go of the wire. He cried out loud. "Ow! Ow! Ow! That hurt!" Tom said. Lily ran to him and hugged him. She was scared. "Are you okay, Tom?" Lily asked. Tom nodded. He rubbed his hand. It was red and sore. "I'm okay, Lily. But that was not a magic thing. That was a bad thing," Tom said. They heard footsteps on the stairs. It was their grandma. She came to the attic and saw the box and the wire. "Oh, no! You found my old lamp. That's an ancient thing. It's very dangerous. You should never touch it. It has electricity in it. That can hurt you very much," grandma said. She took the wire and the key and put them back in the box. She locked it and hid it. She checked Tom's hand and gave him a bandage and a kiss. "I'm sorry, Tom. I should have told you about the lamp. I'm glad you're not badly hurt. You're very brave," grandma said. She took Tom and Lily downstairs and gave them some cookies and milk. They felt better and forgot about the wire. They decided to play with their toys instead. They learned to be careful with old things. They loved their grandma very much.
