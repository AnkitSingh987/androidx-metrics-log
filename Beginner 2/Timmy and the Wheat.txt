Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Wheat

Timmy was in a hurry. He had to get the wheat to the market before it was too late. On the way, he ran into his friend Billy. Billy asked, "Where are you going in such a hurry, Timmy?" Timmy replied, "I need to get the wheat to the market before it closes. Do you want to come with me?" Billy said, "Yes! That sounds like fun!" So they both hurried along the path, with Timmy carrying the wheat. When they got there, they were delighted to see how popular their wheat was. Everyone wanted to buy some. In the end, they sold all the wheat and had enough money to buy a treat. It was the perfect way to end a fun day!

Once upon a time, there was a soldier who lived in a small town. He was very strong and flexible. He could bend and twist his body in many ways. All the kids in the town liked to watch him do his tricks. One day, two groups of kids started to fight over a toy. They were yelling and pushing each other. The soldier saw this and decided to help. He went to the kids and showed them his flexible tricks. The kids stopped fighting and watched him in wonder. After his show, the soldier told the kids that fighting is not good. He said that it is better to share and be kind to each other. The kids listened and learned the lesson. They started to share their toys and play together happily. The town became a peaceful place, and everyone thanked the flexible soldier for teaching them the importance of sharing and kindness.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with boats. They have a big box with many boats. Some boats are red, some boats are blue, some boats are yellow. One day, they go to the park. The park has a pond. The pond has water and ducks. Sara and Tom want to play with their boats in the pond. They take out their boats from the box and put them in the water. Sara has a red boat. She likes her red boat. She puts a mark on her boat. The mark is a star. She says, "This is my boat. It has a star. It is the best boat." Tom has a blue boat. He likes his blue boat. He puts a mark on his boat. The mark is a circle. He says, "This is my boat. It has a circle. It is the best boat." They push their boats in the water. They make them go fast and slow. They make them go left and right. They have fun. But there is another boat in the pond. It is a yellow boat. It has no mark. It is shy. It stays in a corner. It does not move. Sara and Tom see the yellow boat. They wonder who it belongs to. They go near the yellow boat. They say, "Hello, yellow boat. Who are you? Do you want to play with us?" The yellow boat does not answer. It is shy. It does not want to talk. Sara and Tom are nice. They want to be friends with the yellow boat. They say, "Don't be shy, yellow boat. We like you. You are a good boat. You can have a mark too. What mark do you want?" The yellow boat thinks. It likes stars and circles. But it wants something different. It says, "I want a heart. A heart is a nice mark. It means love." Sara and Tom smile. They have a heart sticker. They give it to the yellow boat. They put it on the yellow boat. The yellow boat is happy. It has a mark. It has a heart. They say, "Now you have a mark, yellow boat. You have a heart. You are our friend. You can play with us." The yellow boat is not shy anymore. It moves in the water. It plays with the red boat and the blue boat. They have fun. They are friends. They are happy. They have boats. They have marks. They have a port. The port is the pond. The pond is their port.
