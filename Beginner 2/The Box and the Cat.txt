Category: Beginner
Title: The Box and the Cat

Once, there was a big box. A girl named Sue wanted to pack her toys in the box. She put her doll, car, and ball in the box. Sue felt happy because her toys would be safe. One day, Sue saw a dull gray cat. The cat was sad. Sue wanted to make the cat happy. Sue had an idea. She would pack the cat in the box too. Sue put the cat in the box. The cat was not sad anymore. The cat was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped out of the box and all the toys came out too! Sue laughed and played with the cat and her toys. The cat was her new friend.

Anna and Ben were playing hide and seek in their house. Anna was the seeker and Ben was the hider. Anna counted to ten and then looked for Ben. She peeked in the kitchen, the living room, and the bathroom, but she could not find him. "Where are you, Ben?" she called out. "I give up!" Ben did not answer. He was hiding in the hall closet, behind some coats and shoes. He was very quiet and still. He wanted to surprise Anna and make her laugh. But then he heard a loud noise from outside. It sounded like a big dog barking and growling. Ben was frightened. He did not like dogs. He wished Anna would find him and hug him. "Anna, Anna, where are you?" he whispered. "I'm scared!" Anna heard Ben's voice. She ran to the hall and opened the closet door. She saw Ben's face, pale and wet with tears. "Ben, what's wrong?" she asked. "Why are you crying?" Ben pointed to the window. Anna looked and saw a big black dog on their lawn. It was barking and jumping at their fence. "Don't worry, Ben, it's just a dog," she said. "It can't hurt us. We're safe inside." She hugged Ben and rubbed his back. Ben felt better. He hugged Anna back and smiled. "Thank you, Anna," he said. "You're the best sister ever." Anna smiled too. She was happy to help Ben. She forgot about the game of hide and seek. "Let's go play something else," she said. "Something fun and not scary." "Okay," Ben said. "How about we draw some pictures?" "Sure," Anna said. "That sounds good." They left the closet and went to their room. They took out some paper and crayons and started to draw. They forgot about the dog and had a good time. They were not frightened anymore. They were happy.

Once, there was a little toy mouse that wanted to stand. He was very enthusiastic and so he tried his best to stand up. He had been lying down, but he wanted to stand up tall like the other toys. He squirmed and wriggled, but he just couldn't do it. Suddenly, something strange happened. His friends came by and they stood around him. Then, the toy mouse felt a bit of something magical. He felt like he could do it too! He tried again and this time, he managed to stand! He was so happy. The toy mouse felt really proud. He smiled from ear to ear, and all the other toys were so surprised. They were very enthusiastic and they cheered and clapped. Then they all stood up together, and it was the best feeling in the world.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy train. They liked to make it go fast and slow on the tracks. They had a switch that could change the train's direction. It was fun to make the train go where they wanted. But then, their big brother Ben came into the room. He was angry and mean. He wanted to play with the switch. He grabbed it from Tom and pushed him away. He said, "This is mine now. You can't have it. Go away." Tom and Lily were sad and scared. They wanted their switch back. They tried to get it from Ben, but he was too strong and big. He laughed at them and said, "You are silly and weak. I can do what I want with the switch. Watch this." He turned the switch to make the train go the wrong way. The train crashed into a wall and broke into pieces. Tom and Lily cried. They loved their toy train. It was their favorite. Ben had ruined it. He was not sorry. He said, "That was funny. I like to break things. You are stupid and boring. I don't like you." He threw the switch at them and left the room. Tom and Lily were hurt and angry. They wished they could rescue their toy train. But it was too late. It was broken and gone. They had no switch and no fun. They had a bad day.
