Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. Tim liked to play a sport with his friends. They would kick a ball and run around. One day, Tim found a light, shiny rock. He thought it was special. He wanted to keep it safe. So, Tim decided to bury the rock in his backyard. He dug a small hole and put the rock inside. Then, he covered it with dirt. The next day, Tim went to find his rock. He dug in the dirt where he buried it. When he found the rock, it was still light and shiny. Tim was very happy. He played with the rock and his friends all day long.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very sad. She was so sad that she started to cry. Her tears spilled onto the carpet. They made lots and lots of little rivers. Her mommy saw her crying and ran over. She wiped away the girl's tears and hugged her tight. "Why are you so sad?" asked her mommy. The girl shook her head and said, "The carpet is too crazy!" Her mommy smiled and said, "Come on, I will fix it for you." So they grabbed the vacuum and cleaned the carpet. When they were finished, the carpet was nice and smooth. The girl was happy again, and she stopped crying. Her mommy hugged her and said, "There, that's better isn't it?" And the little girl nodded. From then on, the carpet was always nice and calm - and the girl was never sad again!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to skip and wear her big hat. One day, she went for a walk in the park. The sun was bright and warm. Lily was very happy. As Lily skipped, she saw a sweet little cat. The cat had a small hat too. Lily thought the cat was cute. She wanted to play with it. But the cat ran away and hid. Lily was sad. Lily kept skipping in the park. She saw the cat again. This time, the cat was near a big tree. Lily skipped to the tree and found a big pile of hats. The cat had many hats! Lily and the cat played with the hats all day. They had lots of fun, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. The boy had brown hair. He liked to do fun things. One day, the boy went to the park. The park had a big slide. The boy liked the slide a lot. The boy played on the slide. He went up and down. He was very happy. The sun was hot. The sky was blue. The boy saw a big tree. The tree had brown bark. The boy wanted to do something new. The boy tried to climb the tree. He wanted to see the top. The boy went up and up. But then, something unexpected happened. The boy fell down from the tree. The boy was not happy anymore. He was sad and hurt. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little tree. The tree had strong roots that went deep into the ground. The roots were very important because they helped the tree grow big and tall. One day, a small bug came to the tree and wanted to talk. "Hello, tree," said the bug. "I am tired. Can I lie down on your roots and rest?" The tree was kind and said, "Yes, little bug. You can lie down on my roots and rest. They are strong and will keep you safe." The bug was happy and lay down on the tree's roots. The tree and the bug became good friends. They talked and played every day. The tree knew that its roots were important, not just for growing tall, but also for helping its new friend, the bug.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big hill and wanted to go up. Sue started to walk up the hill. On her way, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was gazing at the sky. "Hi, what are you doing?" asked Sue. Tim said, "I am looking at the clouds. They look like animals. Do you want to try?" Sue looked up and saw a cloud that looked like a big cat. They both laughed. As they walked up the hill, they saw a pass. They went through the pass and found a secret garden. In the garden, there were many big, colorful flowers. They were so happy to find this secret place. Suddenly, a big bird came down from the sky. It landed in the garden and began to talk. "Hello, I am the guardian of this garden. You are the first kids to find this place. I have a surprise for you." The big bird gave Sue and Tim a magic seed. They planted the seed in the garden. The seed grew into a big, beautiful tree with many fruits. Sue and Tim were so surprised and happy. They promised to take care of the tree and the secret garden forever.
