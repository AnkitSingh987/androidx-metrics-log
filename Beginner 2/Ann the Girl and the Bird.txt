Category: Beginner
Title: Ann the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. She had a pretty blouse. It was very clean. Ann liked her blouse a lot. She wore it every day. One day, Ann went to the park. She took her favorite book with her. She wanted to read. Ann sat under a big tree. It was nice and cool there. She opened her book and started to read. While Ann was reading, a bird flew by. The bird saw Ann's clean blouse. The bird liked the clean blouse too. The bird sat on Ann's shoulder. Ann and the bird read together. They had a great time at the park.

---

One day, a lazy dog named Bob saw a yummy sausage. It was near a big tree. He wanted to eat it, but it was far away. Bob thought, "I will get it later." He lay down to take a nap. While Bob was napping, a little bird saw the sausage. The bird flew down and picked it up. Bob woke up and saw the bird with the sausage. He wanted it back! He ran after the bird, but he was still sleepy. Bob tried to jump and get the sausage, but he was too slow. He stumbled and fell on the ground. The bird dropped the sausage, and it rolled away. Bob and the bird looked at each other, then they both laughed. They became friends and shared the sausage.

One day, a friendly frog named Fred went for a hop. He hopped through the grass and saw a pair of pants on the ground. The pants were big and blue. Fred had an idea. He wanted to wear the pants. Fred hopped into the pants and started to hop around. He felt very funny in the big blue pants. All the other animals saw him and laughed. Fred did not care. He liked his new pants. Then, something unexpected happened. The pants started to hop too! The pants were magic! Fred and the pants hopped together all day long. They became the best of friends, and all the animals wanted magic pants too.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to measure the roof of his house. He was eager to know how big it was. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam was also eager to help. They went outside and looked at the roof. They saw a bird on the roof. The bird was not happy. It said, "You can't measure the roof. It's my home!" Tim and Sam were sad. They did not want to make the bird mad. Tim had an idea. He said to the bird, "We just want to know how big the roof is. We won't hurt your home." The bird thought about it and then agreed. Tim and Sam measured the roof and the bird was happy. They all became friends and played together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he called X-Ray. X-Ray was an ugly toy, but Tim loved it so much. They played together every day. One sunny day, Tim and X-Ray went to the park. They saw a bird sitting on a tree. The bird could whistle a pretty song. Tim wanted to whistle like the bird. So, he tried and tried, but he could not whistle. Tim felt sad, but X-Ray was there to cheer him up. They played and had fun together. Soon, Tim forgot about whistling like the bird. He was happy to have his ugly toy X-Ray as his best friend. They went home and had a great day.

One day, a boy named Tim got a new scooter. He was very happy. He rode it all day, up and down the street. Tim thought his scooter was fun, but his big sister, Sue, thought it was boring. She did not want to play with him. Tim was sad that Sue did not want to play, so he had an idea. He went to the kitchen and found a big spoon. Tim started to stir his scooter with the big spoon. Sue saw him and laughed. She asked him, "What are you doing?" Tim said, "I am making scooter soup!" Sue thought this was funny. She got a big spoon too and helped Tim stir the scooter. They had fun playing together, and Sue did not think the scooter was boring anymore.
