Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Farm

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw a big pumpkin patch. Tim saw a terrible pumpkin that was very big and round. He wanted to take it home. "Mom, can I have this pumpkin, please?" asked Tim. His mom said, "Yes, you can, but you have to carry it." Tim tried to pick up the pumpkin, but it was too heavy for him. He felt sad. Just then, a nice farmer came by and saw Tim. He waved at Tim and said, "Hello! Do you need help?" Tim nodded his head. The farmer picked up the terrible pumpkin and put it in their car. Tim was so happy and waved goodbye to the farmer as they drove away.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very eager to fly her kite. She ran to the park with her dad. They found a big open space to let the kite go up in the sky. Sue held on tight to the string, and the kite danced in the air. "Look, Dad! The kite is so high!" Sue said with a big smile. Her dad was happy to see her having fun. They played with the kite all day, laughing and running around the park. But then, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind came and split the kite's string. The kite flew away, up and up into the sky. Sue was sad and worried that her kite was gone forever. Just then, a nice boy named Tim saw what happened. He had a long stick and used it to catch the kite as it came down. He gave the kite back to Sue, and they all became friends. From that day on, Sue, Tim, and her dad went to the park together to fly kites and have fun.

Once upon a time a little boy by the name of Tim wanted to visit the forest. He gathered many of his favorite toys and ran to the woods. When he got there, he heard a big, loud noise. He turned around and saw a wolf in the distance. Tim was scared but he still wanted to explore the forest, so he carefully stepped near the wolf. The wolf was very fragile so Tim made sure not to make any sudden movements. Tim said to the wolf, "Hello, my name is Tim. Can I visit your home?" The wolf gently smiled and shook its head yes. Tim was so excited as he began to explore the forest. He picked up twigs and colorful stones while the wolf watched him. They became fast friends. When the sun began to set, Tim said goodbye to the wolf and ran back home with a giant smile on his face. From then on, Tim always visited the woods unafraid because the wolf protected him.

Lily and Ben like to play a game with their old toys. They take turns to drop them from the window and see how they land on the grass. Sometimes they make funny noises or bounce or break. One day, Lily has a new toy. It is a shiny ball that makes music when she shakes it. She wants to show it to Ben and play with it. She runs to the window with the ball in her hand. "Look, Ben, look at my new toy!" she says. "It is so pretty and fun!" Ben looks at the ball and smiles. He likes it too. He wants to touch it and hear the music. He reaches out his hand and asks, "Can I see it, Lily? Can I play with it?" Lily nods and gives him the ball. Ben takes it and shakes it. He hears the music and laughs. He likes the sound and the feeling. He forgets about the game and the old toys. He moves closer to the window and holds the ball up. He wants to see how it looks in the sun. He does not see the edge of the window. He does not feel the ball slip from his hand. He hears a loud thud and a crack. He looks down and sees the ball on the grass. It is broken and silent. He feels sad and scared. He drops the ball, he thinks. He drops Lily's new toy. He drops it and breaks it. He is sorry and he does not know what to say. He looks at Lily and sees her face. She is angry and sad. She cries and shouts, "Ben, you drop my toy! You drop it and break it! You are bad and mean! I do not want to play with you!" She runs away from the window and slams the door. Ben is alone and sad. He wishes he did not drop the ball. He wishes he did not break Lily's toy. He wishes he could say sorry and make her happy. He does not know what to do. He does not want to play the game anymore. He does not want to play with the old toys. He wants to play with Lily and her new toy. He wants to be her friend again.
