Title: The Girl and Her Brother and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a girl and her brother. The girl was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. Her brother was little and liked to play with her. One day, the girl saw a big ball in a tree. She wanted to get it down. She tried to climb the tree, but it was too high. Her brother wanted to help her. He said, "Let me try!" But the girl said, "No, I can do it myself." The girl found a long stick. She tried to reach the ball with the stick. Her brother said, "Be careful!" But the girl did not listen. She pushed the ball with the stick, and it fell down. The girl was happy and said, "Thank you, stick!" But then, the ball bounced away very fast. The girl and her brother tried to catch it, but they couldn't. The ball went into a big river and floated away. The girl and her brother were sad. They lost the ball. The girl wished she had listened to her brother.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird loved to eat peanuts. One day, the bird saw a big peanut on the ground. It was the biggest peanut the bird had ever seen. The bird was very happy. The bird tried to pick up the peanut, but it was too heavy. The bird did not want to leave the peanut. It did not want to share with other birds. The bird refused to let the peanut go. A little boy saw the yellow bird and the big peanut. He wanted to help the bird. The boy picked up the peanut and broke it into small pieces. The bird was now able to eat the peanut. The yellow bird was very thankful to the little boy. They became good friends and shared peanuts every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One day, Tim saw a big dog in the park. The dog was bad and liked to take balls from kids. Tim's mom saw the dog too. She said, "Tim, I warn you, do not go near the bad dog." But Tim did not listen. He wanted to play with his ball in the park. So, he went to the park with his ball. The bad dog saw Tim and his ball. The dog said, "Give me your ball!" Tim was scared, but he said, "No, it is my ball!" The dog got angry and tried to take the ball. Just then, Tim's mom came and chased the bad dog away. Tim learned to listen to his mom's warnings.

One day, a tall man went to a store. He wanted to buy a staff. The man saw a staff he liked. It was very pretty. He asked the store lady, "Can I have this staff?" The store lady said, "No, you can't." The tall man was sad. He really wanted the staff. He asked, "Why can't I have it?" The store lady said, "You need to open a box to get the staff." The tall man looked around. He saw many boxes. He did not know which one to open. He tried to open a big box. It was hard to open. He tried to open a small box. It was easy to open. But the staff was not in the small box. The tall man did not give up. He kept opening boxes. Finally, he opened the right box. The staff was inside! The tall man was happy. He said, "Thank you, store lady!" The store lady smiled and said, "You're welcome! Enjoy your new staff!"

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy was very impatient. He wanted to sail on a leaf boat. He saw a big leaf next to a pond. Buzzy thought it would be fun to sail on the leaf. Buzzy called his friend, Anty the ant. "Anty, come and sail with me on this leaf!" Buzzy said. Anty was excited and ran to the leaf. They both got on the leaf and pushed it into the pond. The leaf boat started to sail slowly. Buzzy and Anty had so much fun. They laughed and played on the leaf. The sun was shining, and they felt very happy. At the end of the day, they sailed back to the edge of the pond. Buzzy and Anty hugged and said goodbye. They knew they would have more fun adventures together soon.
