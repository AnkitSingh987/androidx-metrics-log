Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Sarah and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sarah. She was very naughty and always wanted to push her toys around. One day, she saw a big mound of rice on the table. She was very excited and she wanted to push it. She knew she shouldn't do it, but Sarah was too curious. She pushed and pushed, until all the rice was on the floor. Her mom saw the mess and was very mad. She gave Sarah a sour look. Sarah was sad and knew that she shouldn't have pushed the rice. She cleaned up the mess, but she still felt bad. From then on, Sarah was careful not to push anything else again.

One day, a cat named Tom was very tired. He saw a big ball of yarn and wanted to play. But he was too tired to play. Tom said to his friend, the dog named Sam, "I want to play with the yarn, but I am very tired." Sam saw that Tom was sad. He wanted to help his friend. Sam had an idea. He said to Tom, "I will play with you and the yarn. We can take turns, so you can rest when you are tired." Tom and Sam started to play with the yarn. They took turns and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The yarn rolled away and got lost. Tom and Sam were sad and worried. They did not know what to do. Then, a little bird came and said, "I saw where the yarn went. I can help you find it." Tom, Sam, and the bird went on a journey to find the yarn. They worked together and found the yarn at last. Tom and Sam were very happy. They thanked the little bird for helping them. They all played with the yarn together and had a great time. In the end, Tom was not tired anymore, and they all became good friends.

One day there was a small bear walking down the street. He loved to explore and find hidden treasures. He walked until he came to a big house with lots of lights. He was curious, so he slowly walked up the steps. He saw a big window and the shutters were open. The bear peeked through the window and saw an old man inside. He was wearing a red hat and smoking a pipe. The bear thought he was quite stylish. He waved to the bear but the bear decided to ignore him. The bear looked a little further and found a hidden door. He opened the door and walked inside. It was dark, but then he saw a beautiful chest at the end of the room. He opened the chest and was surprised to find all the treasures inside. But then something bad happened. The old man from outside the window saw the bear and he was very angry. He yelled and shouted and chased the bear out of his house. The small bear ran away and never came back. He had been ignored and the hidden treasures were lost forever.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big ball. It is red and blue and yellow. It looks nice. "Can we play with the ball?" Lily asks her mom. "Sure, but you have to ask the owner first," her mom says. They look around and see a boy sitting on a bench. He is holding a sandwich. He is not looking at the ball. "Hello, can we play with your ball?" Tom asks the boy. The boy nods and smiles. He is nice. "Thank you!" Lily and Tom say. They take the ball and run to the grass. They kick the ball and catch it and roll it. They have fun. But then, a big dog comes. It is black and brown and white. It has sharp teeth and a loud bark. It sees the ball and wants it. It runs to the ball and grabs it with its mouth. It runs away with the ball. "Hey, that's our ball!" Lily and Tom shout. They chase the dog. The boy sees what happens. He drops his sandwich and runs after them. "Stop, dog, stop!" he yells. He is angry. The dog does not stop. It runs to the gate and jumps over it. It leaves the park with the ball. Lily and Tom and the boy stop at the gate. They are sad. They lost the ball. The dog stole the ball. It was not nice. They go back to their moms. They tell them what happened. Their moms hug them and comfort them. They say they are sorry. They say they will buy them a new ball. A nice ball. A ball that no one will steal. They say they still have each other. They are friends. They have the world.
