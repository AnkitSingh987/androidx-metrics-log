Category: Beginner
Title: Abigail the Girl and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abigail. She was very excited about going to the beach for the day. Abigail packed her bag, but when she got home, she realized she had forgotten her beach hat. She panicked and started running around her house. Suddenly, Abigail heard her mom call out, "Abigail, stop panicking! We can just grab the beach hat from your messy room!" Abigail went to her room. It was very messy and she couldn't find the beach hat anywhere. But then she heard her mom again, "Look under the bed!" Abigail crawled under the bed and, sure enough, she found her beach hat. She smiled and quickly put it in her bag. Now she was all set to go to the beach and have a wonderful day!

Once upon a time, in a small house in the west, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a dog named Spot. They were best friends. Tim and Spot loved to stand in the yard and play with a big red ball. One day, a serious-looking man came to their house. He said, "I need your help, Tim and Spot." Tim asked, "What can we do?" The man told them about a big tree that needed to be moved. Tim and Spot wanted to help. They went to the tree and tried to push it. They pushed and pushed, but the tree did not move. They tried and tried, but it was too hard. The man was sad, and Tim and Spot were tired. In the end, they could not move the tree. The man had to leave, and Tim and Spot went back home, feeling sad that they could not help.

One day, a polite dog named Ben went for a walk in the park. Ben loved to walk and play with his friends. In the park, he saw his friend, a cat named Tina. "Hi Tina!" said Ben. "Do you want to come with me for a walk?" Tina said, "Yes, Ben! I want to come too. Let's include our friend, the bird named Sam." So, Ben, Tina, and Sam went for a walk together. They played, laughed, and had a lot of fun. They were very happy to be friends and share their walk in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball in the yard. One day, Tom saw a big lion in the yard. The lion had a loud roar. Tom was scared, but he wanted to make a new friend. Tom said, "Hi, lion! My name is Tom. Do you want to play with me?" The lion looked at Tom and roared again. Tom was still scared, but he wanted to be brave. He thought about his memory of playing with his ball and how happy it made him. The lion said, "I am Leo. I like to roar and play too." So, Tom and Leo played with the ball. They had fun in the yard. They played until the sun went down. But then, Leo got hungry. He looked at Tom and roared loudly. Tom was very scared now. He tried to run away, but Leo was too fast. The lion caught Tom and ate him up. From that day on, the yard was quiet. The ball was left alone, and Tom's memory lived on in the hearts of his family. But they were very sad that their chubby cat was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to paint. She would paint all day. She liked to paint big and small things. One day, she saw a flexible snake. The snake was long and could bend its body. Sue wanted to paint the snake. Sue picked up her paint and brush. She started to paint the snake. But the snake did not like it. It hissed at Sue. Sue was scared, but she wanted to finish her painting. She tried to paint the snake again. The snake got angry. It moved fast like a zoom. It bit Sue's hand. Sue cried and dropped her paint. Her painting was not done. The snake slithered away. Sue was sad and hurt. She learned not to paint things that don't want to be painted.
