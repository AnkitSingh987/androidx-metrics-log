Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Round Wheel and the Mark

Once upon a time, there was a large round wheel. The wheel liked to roll around and play with its friends. It was very happy. One day, the wheel saw a mark on the ground. It was a line. The wheel wanted to make a mark too. So, it rolled over some mud and then rolled on the ground. The wheel made a long line with the mud. It was very proud of its mark. The wheel showed its mark to its friends. They all thought it was fun. So, they all made marks too. They made lines, circles, and squiggles. They all laughed and played together. The large wheel and its friends had a great day making marks.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big tree. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, loved the tree. They would admire the tree every day. One day, Tim said to Max, "I want to touch the tree." They went to the tree and Tim gave it a rub. The tree began to shake and a door opened. "Wow!" said Tim, as he and Max went through the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys. They played and had fun all day. The tree was magic, and they never expected that.

Sara and Ben were playing with scissors and paper. They wanted to make snowflakes and stars. Sara cut a big circle and then cut lines and shapes inside it. Ben cut a small square and then cut triangles and diamonds inside it. They were having fun. But then Ben cut his finger by mistake. He cried out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts!" He showed Sara his finger. It had a red cut and some blood. Sara felt anxious. She did not like to see blood. She wanted to help Ben. She ran to the kitchen and got a paper towel. She wrapped it around Ben's finger. "Don't worry, Ben. It will be okay. The paper will stop the blood." She said. She hugged Ben and tried to make him feel better. Ben still felt some pain, but he was glad that Sara was with him. He hugged her back and said, "Thank you, Sara. You are a good friend. I'm sorry I cut my finger." Sara smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ben. You are a good friend too. I'm sorry you suffer. Let's go and show Mom. She will know what to do." They held hands and walked to the living room, where Mom was reading a book. They hoped Mom would have a band-aid and a kiss for Ben's cut.

Lily and Ben liked to help Mom in the kitchen. They wanted to be mighty helpers. They put the dirty plates and cups in the dishwasher. They put the soap in the right place. They closed the door and pushed the button. "Good job, Lily and Ben!" Mom said. "You are very good at using the dishwasher. Do you want to perform a song for me while we wait for it to finish?" "Yes, Mom!" Lily and Ben said. They loved to sing and dance. They ran to the living room and got their toy instruments. Lily had a drum and Ben had a guitar. They put on their hats and scarves and stood on the rug. "Welcome to our show!" Lily said. "We are the mighty dishwasher band. We will sing a song about how we help Mom in the kitchen. Are you ready, Ben?" "I'm ready, Lily!" Ben said. "Let's rock!" They started to play their instruments and sing. They made up funny words and rhymes. They moved their bodies and made silly faces. Mom clapped and cheered. She was very proud of them. They finished their song and bowed. "Thank you, thank you!" they said. "We hope you liked our show!" "I loved it!" Mom said. "You are very talented and creative. And you are also very helpful. The dishwasher is done. Let's go and put the clean dishes away." Lily and Ben smiled. They ran to the kitchen and followed Mom. They took out the dishes and put them in the right places. They felt happy and mighty. They had fun helping Mom and performing their song. They hugged Mom and said, "We love you, Mom!" "I love you too, Lily and Ben!" Mom said. "You are my mighty dishwasher band!"
