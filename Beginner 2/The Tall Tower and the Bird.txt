Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tower and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a tall tower in a big field. Many people liked to look at the tower. It was very pretty. But there was a stubborn bird who lived on top of the tower. The bird did not like people coming near its home. One day, a little girl named Lily came to see the tower. She wanted to play near it. The stubborn bird saw her and started to worry. The bird did not want Lily to come too close. It thought she might make the tower dirty or break something. The bird flew down and tried to make Lily go away. But Lily did not understand the bird. She thought it wanted to play. The bird worried more and more. Then, Lily saw the bird was sad. She said, "Don't worry, bird. I won't hurt your tower." The bird felt better, and they played together in the field.

One day, a duck named Dolly went to the park. She saw her friends, the birds, playing in the sky. Dolly wished she could soar like them, but she was too scared to try. Her friends laughed and said, "Dolly, come join us!" Dolly felt embarrassed. She wanted to play with her friends, but she was too afraid. She walked away and sat by the pond. She saw a little bug on a leaf, trying to jump into the water. The bug was scared too, but it tried and tried. Finally, the bug jumped and made a big splash! Seeing the brave bug, Dolly felt brave too. She flapped her wings and started to soar. Her friends cheered for her, and she felt so happy. Dolly learned that it's okay to be scared, but trying new things can be fun. Now Dolly loves to soar with her friends in the sky.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with many peaches on it. The peaches were sweet and yummy. Tim wanted to share the peaches with his friends. He picked many peaches and put them in a bag. Tim was very happy. As Tim walked, he saw a distant old man sitting on a bench. The old man looked sad. Tim went up to the old man and said, "Hi, I have some peaches. Do you want one?" The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I love peaches." Tim gave the old man a peach, and they both ate together. Suddenly, a strong wind came and scattered all the peaches from the tree. Tim was sad because there were no more peaches for his friends. But the old man said, "Don't be sad, Tim. You did a kind thing by sharing your peach with me. Kindness is more important than peaches." Tim smiled and learned that sharing and being kind is the best thing to do.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat found a rub and wanted to attach it to the dog's tail. The cat said, "Hey dog, let me attach this rub to your tail. It will be fun!" The dog was not sure. He said, "I don't know, cat. It might be bad." But the cat did not listen. She attached the rub to the dog's tail anyway. The dog tried to be happy, but he felt bad. Soon, the rub got stuck in the dog's tail. The dog tried to get it off, but it hurt. The cat tried to help, but she could not. The dog said, "I told you it was bad!" In the end, the dog had to go to the vet to get the rub off his tail. The cat felt bad for not listening to the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who had a hook. He was a bit upset because he wanted to improve it. So every day, he worked very hard to make it better. He added some shiny blue paint and added some new handles. He even made some decorations with ribbons and glitter. After all his hard work, the hook looked great! The boy was so excited and happy with how the hook turned out. He loved the new sparkles and he was proud of all his hard work. Everyone who had seen it said it was the best hook they had ever seen. He couldn't believe he had improved it so much. He was very happy and smiled every time he looked at it. And from then on, he loved his hook!
