Category: Beginner
Title: The Hill and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a hill. On the hill, there were many thick trees. Animals liked to play there. They had so much fun on the hill. One day, a big bear came to the hill. He wanted to play too. But the animals were scared of the big bear. They did not want to play with him. The big bear was sad. Then a small bird came to the big bear. The small bird said, "Let's play a game. We can weigh things." The big bear liked the idea. The animals saw the big bear was nice. They played together on the hill and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to poke things. One day, he found an unknown box under his bed. Tim was very curious about the box. He opened the box and found a cold, wet ball inside. "What is this?" Tim asked his mom. "It's a snowball from last winter," his mom said. "I saved it for you to play with." Tim took the cold snowball outside. He saw his friend, Sam. "Look at my cold snowball!" Tim said. Sam laughed and poked the snowball. "It's so cold!" Sam said. They played with the snowball until it melted away.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to wander around the world. One day, Fluffy went for a long walk. As Fluffy walked, she saw a big tree. She climbed the tree and saw a small bird. The bird sang a pretty song. Fluffy and the bird became good friends. Fluffy and the bird wandered the world together. They saw many fun things. They played in the sun and slept under the stars. Fluffy and the bird were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big box of pastels. Her favorite pastel was the red one. She used it all the time to draw pretty pictures. One day, Mia's mom told her they would go to a fun party later. Mia was very excited. At the party, Mia saw a big red balloon. It was as red as her favorite pastel. She wanted to draw a picture with her red pastel on the balloon. Mia's mom said, "Be careful, Mia. If you push too hard, the balloon might pop!" Mia was very careful and drew a heart on the red balloon. Then, she gave the balloon a little kiss. Suddenly, the balloon popped! Mia was sad, but her mom gave her a hug and said, "Don't worry, Mia. We can get you another balloon." Mia smiled and enjoyed the rest of the party.

One day, a big shark lived in the sea. The shark was not happy. He was always hungry. He wanted to be wealthy, so he could eat lots of fish. The shark saw a big ship. The ship had many fish. The shark wanted the fish. He swam fast to the ship. But the ship had a door. The door was shut. The shark could not get the fish. The shark was sad. He swam away. He saw a little boat. A kind man was in the boat. The man saw the sad shark. The man gave the shark some fish. The shark was happy. He did not need to be wealthy.

Anna and Ben like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big train. The train has many cars and can make noise. Anna and Ben pretend to be the driver and the passenger. They say "choo choo" and "all aboard". One day, their mom says it is time for lunch. Anna and Ben do not want to stop playing. They say "no, no, we want to play more". Their mom says they have to eat. She says "eating is good for you, it makes you strong and healthy. If you do not eat, you will be thin and weak". Anna and Ben do not like to be thin and weak. They want to be strong and healthy. They say "okay, okay, we will eat". They go to the kitchen and see their lunch. Their mom made them sandwiches and fruit. They say "yum, yum, this looks good". They eat their lunch and drink their juice. After lunch, they feel full and happy. They say "thank you, mom, for the good lunch". Their mom says "you are welcome, my loves. Now you can play again". Anna and Ben smile and run back to their toys. They play with their train until it is time for nap. They say "we love to play, but we also love to eat".
