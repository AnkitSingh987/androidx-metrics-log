Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to play in her big, deep sandbox. One sunny day, she decided to build a sandcastle. She took her bucket and shovel and started to dig. After a minute, Lily heard a tiny voice. She looked around and saw a little green frog. The frog said, "Can I play with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me build my sandcastle!" The frog hopped with joy and went to sit next to Lily. Lily made a seat for the frog in the sand. They both worked together to build the tallest sandcastle they could. The frog helped by bringing water in his mouth to make the sand stick together. When they finished, they were very proud of their sandcastle. Lily and the frog played together in the deep sandbox until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a big bow on it. The bow was red and pretty. One day, a little girl named Sue saw the tree. She liked the bow very much. Sue said, "Wow! What a big bow! I want to play with it." Sue tried to take the bow off the tree. But the tree was too big. She could not reach the bow. Sue was sad. She wanted the bow so much. Sue asked her friend Tom for help. Tom said, "Let's find a way to get the bow." They found a long stick. Sue and Tom used the stick to get the bow. They were happy. They played with the bow all day. But they forgot about the tree. The tree was sad because it lost the bow. The tree started to rot. Sue and Tom saw the tree rot. They felt bad. They said, "We are sorry, tree. We didn't mean to hurt you." They put the bow back on the tree. The tree was happy again. It stopped rotting. Sue and Tom learned to share and care for others.

Once there was a stubborn little dog named Max. He wanted to go to the ocean so badly, but his mom said no. So, he got an idea. He started to work hard to find a way. Max worked and worked, trying to figure out how to get to the ocean. He dug holes and built ramps and made boats out of wood. But nothing worked. Then one day, he jumped in his mom's car and started to drive. He drove and drove, until he saw the ocean! Max was so excited, he ran and ran until his feet hit the water. Max had done it! Even though it was hard work, he was stubborn and he had done it.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and lots of toys. One day, he wanted to relax. So, he went outside to look at the sky. As he lay down, he saw a bright star. The star was very pretty. It twinkled in the night sky. The man felt happy looking at the star. The star made the man feel calm. He forgot about his big house and lots of toys. All he wanted was to look at the star and relax. And so, he did.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Jack. He was 3 years old and loved to play. One day, he found a mysterious board in his living room. He picked it up and began to shake it. Jack could hear rattling noises coming from inside the board. As he kept shaking it, the rattling noises became louder and louder. He got very anxious and wanted to know what was inside it. "What are you doing, Jack?" asked his mum. "I'm shaking this board," said Jack. "I think there's something inside it." His mum smiled and said, "That's nice. Let's open it and see." Jack was very excited. He and his mum carefully opened the board and inside was a toy car! Jack was so happy. He hugged his mum and smiled. His anxiousness was gone. He couldn't wait to play with his toy car.
