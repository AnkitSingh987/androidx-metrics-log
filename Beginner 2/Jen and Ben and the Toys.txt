Category: Beginner
Title: Jen and Ben and the Toys

Jen and Ben were twins. They liked to play in their room. They had many toys and books. But they also liked to help Mom in the kitchen. Mom was a good cook. She made yummy food for them. One day, Mom had to go to the store. She told Jen and Ben to stay in their room and not touch the stove. The stove was hot and dangerous. She said she would be back soon. Jen and Ben said okay. They gave Mom a hug and a kiss. Jen and Ben played with their toys for a while. But then they got bored. They wanted to do something new. They saw Mom's clothes in the closet. They had an idea. They decided to dress up like Mom and Dad. They took Mom's dress and Dad's shirt and hat. They put them on. They looked funny. They laughed. They went to the kitchen. They wanted to pretend to cook like Mom. They saw a pot on the stove. They did not know it was hot. They did not remember what Mom said. They reached for the pot. But then they heard a loud beep. It was Mom. She was back. She saw them in the kitchen. She ran to them. She stopped them from touching the pot. She was scared and angry. She said, "What are you doing? You could have burned yourself! You know you are not supposed to touch the stove!" Jen and Ben felt bad. They said sorry. They said they were just playing. They said they wanted to be like Mom and Dad. Mom hugged them. She said she loved them. She said she was glad they were okay. She said they should never play with the stove again. She said they could dress up, but only in their room. She said she would make them a snack. Jen and Ben said okay. They took off Mom's and Dad's clothes. They went to their room. They learned their lesson. They waited for Mom's snack. They hoped it was something new.

Once upon a time there was a crazy girl who wanted to find a leather bag. She went to her mom and asked if she could find one. Her mom said no, but she was too crazy to listen and she ran away. She looked all over the world and finally found a leather bag. But it was too late. When she opened the bag she found it was full of scary spiders. She screamed and cried, but the spiders wouldn't go away. Finally, she had no choice but to leave the bag behind. She had a bad ending to her journey because she never found what she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cook named Tom. Tom was good at making dessert. One day, he made a big, sweet cake. It was very yummy. Tom wanted to share his cake with his friend, Sue. He put the cake in a box and walked to Sue's house. On the way, he saw a big bug. Tom stopped to examine the bug. It was very interesting. Tom and the bug became friends. He took the bug to Sue's house too. Sue was happy to see Tom, the bug, and the cake. They all ate the dessert together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was a successful helper. She liked to help her mom and dad with jobs around the house. Lily was a happy girl. One day, Lily saw her cat, Mittens, lie down on the floor. Mittens was tired from playing. Lily had an idea. She wanted to help Mittens feel better. Lily went to her room and found a soft blanket. She put the blanket next to Mittens. Mittens liked the blanket and lay on it. Lily was happy she could help her cat. She knew that helping others made her a successful helper.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat. One day, his mom gave him a new food to try. It was green and long. It was called celery. Tim was not sure if he would like it. "Tim, try this great celery," his mom said. "It is good for you." Tim looked at the celery and decided to accept it. He took a bite. It was crunchy and a little bit salty. He liked it! From that day on, Tim loved to eat celery. He would share it with his friends. They all thought it was great too. And they all lived happily, eating celery and having fun.
