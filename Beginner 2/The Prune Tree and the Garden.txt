Category: Beginner
Title: The Prune Tree and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a prune tree in a garden. Every day, the tree would make useful fruits. The people who lived in the house would eat the prunes and be happy. One day, the prune tree started to suffer. Its leaves began to fall, and it could not make any more prunes. The people in the house were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl found a magic seed. She planted the seed next to the prune tree. The seed grew into a new tree. The new tree was a friend to the prune tree. The prune tree felt better and started to make prunes again. The people in the house were happy, and they had two useful trees in their garden.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky owl. He lived in a big tree in the woods. All the other animals liked the owl, but sometimes he would complain. One day, the owl saw a big, shiny star in the sky. He wanted to touch the star. So, he flew up and up, but he could not reach it. The owl started to complain, "I want to touch the star, but it's too high!" Then, something unexpected happened. The star heard the owl and came down from the sky. The star said, "I will help you touch me if you stop complaining." The owl was very happy and stopped complaining. He touched the star and felt very lucky. From that day on, the owl never complained again and all the animals were even happier with him.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bottle of lotion. He did not know what it was. He saw his mom use it sometimes. Tim thought it was a toy. He played with the lotion and made a big mess. Tim felt guilty because he knew he should not play with things that were not his. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not happy. She asked, "Tim, did you make this mess?" Tim looked at the floor and said, "Yes, Mom. I'm sorry." His mom said, "It's okay, but you should ask before using things that are not yours." Tim's mom showed him how to use the lotion. She put some on his hands and said, "Now, rub your hands together." Tim did as she said. His hands felt soft and nice. He liked the lotion. Tim's mom said, "Next time, just call me if you want to use something new." Tim nodded and promised to ask for help next time.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big net on the grass. The net is for catching butterflies. "Look, a net!" Tom says. "Let's catch some butterflies!" "Okay!" Mia says. They run to the net and pick it up. They look for butterflies in the flowers. They see a yellow butterfly. It is very pretty. Tom and Mia run after it with the net. They try to catch it, but it is too fast. "Come here, butterfly!" Tom shouts. But the butterfly does not hear him. The butterfly is deaf. It cannot hear anything. It only sees with its eyes. The butterfly sees the net. It is scared. It flies away from Tom and Mia. It flies to a tree. It hides behind a leaf. Tom and Mia are sad. They cannot catch the butterfly. They put the net down. They sit on the grass. "Maybe we should not chase the butterflies," Mia says. "Maybe they do not like it." "Maybe you are right," Tom says. "Maybe we should just watch them and be quiet." They look at the tree. They see the yellow butterfly. It comes out from behind the leaf. It flaps its wings. It looks happy. Tom and Mia smile. They watch the butterfly. They do not make any noise. They are happy too.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big orange rock. He thought it was very pretty. Tim wanted to take it home, but the rock was too heavy. So, he decided to just look at it and poke it with a stick. As Tim poked the rock, it started to move! A small bug came out from under the rock. The bug looked at Tim and said, "Hi! My name is Buzz. Why did you poke my rock?" Tim was surprised. He said, "I'm sorry, Buzz. I didn't know you lived there. I just wanted to see the pretty orange rock." Buzz smiled and said, "That's okay. Do you want to be friends?" Tim nodded and they played together all day. They had lots of fun and Tim learned that even little bugs can be great friends.
