Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big pink candle that she loved very much. She would look at it every day and want to use it, but her mom said, "No, not yet." One day, Amy's friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the pink candle and said, "I want to play with it!" Amy said, "No, my mom said we can't." But Tom did not listen. He took the pink candle and ran outside. Amy was very sad and ran after Tom. When they were outside, a big wind came and blew the pink candle away. Tom and Amy were both very sad. They did not know what to do. Just then, a nice bird flew by and saw the pink candle. The bird picked up the candle and brought it back to Amy and Tom. They were so happy! They thanked the bird and took the pink candle back inside. From that day on, Amy and Tom learned to listen and not to play with things they should not.

There was a family who loved to pray together. Every morning, they would kneel down and say a prayer before starting their day. One morning, their 3 year old daughter wanted to join in. She didn't know what words to say, but thought it would be fun to pray with her parents. So her mother passed her a book that had original words written in it. She read them carefully, but still couldn't understand them. She tried to repeat the words one by one, but kept confusing them. She had been so eager to join in the praying, but now sat hopelessly. Then her father put his hand on her shoulder. He smiled and said, "It's ok to not know all the words. What matters is that you said them with your heart". He then told her that she could make up her own prayer with her own words. And that's what she did. She smiled and said her own words. It may not have been like her parent's prayer, but that was her way of praying. And even though it was different, it was no less special.

Once upon a time, there lived a chubby bear called Buster. He was always cold and often ignored by the other animals. One day he decided to be brave and decided to make some friends. So, he went up to two squirrels and said, "Hello! Do you want to be my friends?" The squirrels looked at each other and ignored Buster. Buster's heart sank and he walked away slowly, feeling hurt. As he was walking away, he heard a sweet voice say, "Hey, don't worry Buster. I would love to be your friend!" He looked around and saw a fox with a big smile. Buster was overjoyed and quickly nodded. They became best friends and were never cold or ignored again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and even a pink hat. One day, Sue found a small, pink rock. She was so happy! She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a pink rock!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's not just any rock, Sue. That's a special rock called an X-ray rock. It can help people see inside things. Let's go show it to our friend, Dr. Sam." Sue and her mom went to Dr. Sam's office. Sue showed Dr. Sam the pink X-ray rock. Dr. Sam was very excited. He said, "Wow, Sue! This is a very special rock. Let's see what it can do!" Dr. Sam used the X-ray rock to look inside a toy. Sue could see all the parts that made the toy work. She was amazed. Dr. Sam said, "Thank you, Sue. This pink X-ray rock will help me see what's wrong with toys and fix them." Sue was proud that her pink X-ray rock could help Dr. Sam. She knew that the rock was special, just like her. From that day on, Sue and Dr. Sam used the pink X-ray rock to help fix toys for all the children in the town. And everyone lived happily ever after.
