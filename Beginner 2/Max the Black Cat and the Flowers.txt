Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Black Cat and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big black cat named Max. Max lived in a house near a beautiful landscape with trees, flowers, and a river. Max loved to play outside and look at the pretty landscape. One day, Max was playing with his friend, a little bird named Lily. Max and Lily were having fun, running and flying around the trees. They were talking and laughing. Max said, "I love playing with you, Lily!" Lily replied, "Me too, Max! We always have fun!" But then, Max forgot where his house was! He looked around and saw only trees and flowers. Max was sad and scared. Lily saw that Max was upset and said, "Don't worry, Max. I'll help you find your house." So, Max and Lily looked for the house together. They walked and flew around the landscape until they finally found Max's house. Max was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! You're the best friend ever!"

Lena and Tom are friends. They like to play with flags. They have many flags from different countries. They want to display them in their room. "Look, this is the flag of China. It has red and yellow stars," Lena says. She puts the flag on the wall. "And this is the flag of Brazil. It has green, yellow, blue and white. It is very pretty," Tom says. He puts the flag next to Lena's. They display more flags. They have flags of France, India, Canada, Japan and more. Their room is full of colors. "Wow, you have so many flags. You are very smart," Mom says. She comes to see their display. "Thank you, Mom. We like to learn about different countries. They are very interesting," Lena and Tom say. Mom smiles. She is proud of her children. She gives them a hug and a kiss. She says, "You are very smart and very kind. I love you very much."

Tom and Mia were playing in the park. They saw a big balloon in the sky. It was red and yellow and green. It was unusual. They wanted to see it closer. "Look, a balloon!" Tom said. "Let's go there!" Mia said. They ran to the balloon. There was a man with a basket. He smiled at them. "Hello, children. Do you like my balloon?" he asked. "Yes, it's pretty!" Tom said. "How does it fly?" Mia asked. "It flies with hot air. The hot air is lighter than the cold air. Do you want to try?" the man said. He lifted them up and put them in the basket. He turned on a fire. The balloon went up, up, up. "Wow, we are flying!" Tom said. "We can see the whole park!" Mia said. They felt happy and excited. They waved at the people below. They saw the trees, the flowers, the birds, the cars. They saw the blue sky and the white clouds. The man weighed the balloon with a rope. He made it go up and down. He showed them how to steer it. "Thank you for the ride!" Tom and Mia said. "You're welcome. I hope you had fun!" the man said. He brought them back to the ground. They hugged him and said goodbye. They ran back to their mom and dad. They told them about the balloon. They said it was the best day ever.

One day, a little shrimp was in the sea. He was far away from his family. The shrimp was sad because he missed them a lot. He wanted to go back home, but he didn't know how. So, he swam and swam, trying to find his way back home. While the shrimp swam, he saw a big fish. The big fish wanted to eat the little shrimp. The shrimp was scared, so he swam very fast. The big fish chased the shrimp, but the shrimp was able to hide. The shrimp kept swimming and swimming. He got very tired. Then, he saw land. The shrimp thought it was his home, so he swam to the land. But when he got there, he found out it was not his home. The shrimp was on dry land and could not breathe. The little shrimp tried to get back to the water, but he was too tired. In the end, the little shrimp could not make it back to the water, and he was very sad.
