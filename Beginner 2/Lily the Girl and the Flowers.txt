Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Flowers

One day, a little girl named Lily went to gather flowers in the woods. She saw many pretty flowers, but she was a lazy girl. So, she only picked a few. While walking, she met a kind old lady. The old lady said, "Be careful, dear. Some flowers have poison in them." Lily nodded and said, "Thank you, I will be careful." The next day, Lily went to the woods again. She remembered what the old lady said. She saw a very pretty flower. But it looked like it might have poison. So, Lily did not pick it. She felt happy that she listened to the old lady.

Once upon a time, there was a long tree with big palms. It was in a big park. Many kids loved to play near the tree. One sunny day, a little girl named Amy came to the park with her mom. Amy saw the long tree and said to her mom, "Mom, can we decorate the tree with pretty things?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can do that." They found some nice flowers and leaves. They put them on the big palms of the tree. The tree looked very pretty. All the kids in the park came to see the tree. They liked the tree a lot. They played under the tree all day and had a fun time.

Sammy and Jenny were best friends. One day, they went for a walk in the park. They were looking for something to do when they saw a zip. It looked so fun and pretty! Sammy wanted to try it first. So he grabbed the zip tightly and swung himself off the ground. Up he went, higher and higher. Jenny watched and clapped with delight! When Sammy reached the top of the zip, he called down to Jenny, "Come on up!" So Jenny grabbed the zip and followed Sammy. She flew up just like Sammy did. At the top, they laughed and smiled. Sammy led the way and they started to glide down the zip. They couldn't believe how fast they were going. Down they flew, faster and faster. When they reached the ground, Jenny and Sammy hugged each other. They had so much fun flying on the zip! They would definitely come back to the park and zip again.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big truck with a picture of a cone and a cream on it. The truck makes a loud sound. "Ding ding ding!" Anna and Ben run to the truck. They want to buy a cream. "Hello, little ones. What do you want?" the man in the truck asks. He has a big smile and a hat. "I want a chocolate cream, please," Anna says. "I want a strawberry cream, please," Ben says. The man gives them each a cone with a cream on top. Anna and Ben say thank you and pay with their coins. They lick their creams. They are cold and sweet. They sit on a bench and enjoy their creams. They see a cat on the grass. The cat is black and white. It has a bell on its collar. The cat sees a bird on a tree. The cat wants to catch the bird. It jumps and runs to the tree. "Look, Anna, the cat is trying to catch the bird!" Ben says. "Shh, Ben, be quiet. The bird will fly away if you are loud," Anna says. The cat reaches the tree. It climbs up the trunk. It gets closer to the branch where the bird is. The bird sees the cat. It makes a loud sound. "Tweet tweet tweet!" The bird flies away. The cat misses the bird. It falls down from the tree. It lands on the grass. It is not hurt. It shakes its head and walks away. "Wow, that was a silly cat. It could not catch the bird," Ben says. "Yes, that was a silly cat. But it was fun to watch," Anna says. They finish their creams. They throw away their cones. They go back to playing in the park. They are happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, smelly box in his room. He did not know what it was. He asked his mom, "What is this big, smelly box?" His mom said, "It is a computer. You can play games on it. But first, you must sit and wait for me to set it up." Tim was very excited to play games on the computer. He sat and waited for his mom to set it up. While he was waiting, he heard a loud noise from the smelly box. He was scared. Then, the smelly box opened, and a funny-looking dog jumped out! Tim was very surprised. The dog said, "Hello, my name is Bongo. I live in the computer. Let's play games together!" Tim was happy to have a new friend. He and Bongo played games on the computer all day. The smelly box was not so smelly anymore.
