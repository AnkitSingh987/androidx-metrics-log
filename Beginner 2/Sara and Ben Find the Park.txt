Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben Find the Park

Sara and Ben like to play in the park. They run and jump and slide and swing. Sometimes they find junk in the grass. Junk is things that people do not want. Like cans and papers and bottles. Sara and Ben like to pick junk and make things with it. One day, they find a big piece of junk. It is smooth and shiny and round. It has a hole in it. Sara says, "Look, Ben, this is a good junk. We can make a hat with it." Ben says, "Yes, Sara, this is a good junk. Let me try it on." He puts the junk on his head. It is heavy and tight. Sara says, "You look funny, Ben. Can I try it too?" Ben says, "No, Sara, this is my junk. I found it first. You can find another junk." Sara says, "But I want to try it, Ben. Please share with me." Ben says, "No, Sara, go away. This is mine." He pushes Sara. Sara falls and cries. Ben does not care. He likes his junk. He thinks it is a special junk. He does not know that it is a pot. A pot that is very hot. A pot that someone left on the fire. A pot that burns his head. Ben screams and tries to take off the junk. But it is stuck. He runs and runs. He looks for help. But no one is there. The park is empty. Ben is alone. He is hurt. He is scared. He is sorry. He wishes he shared with Sara. He wishes he did not pick the junk. He wishes he played nice. But it is too late. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind dog and a mean cat. They were not friends and would often quarrel. The dog liked to play and have fun, but the cat did not like to share. One day, the dog and the cat saw a big ball. It was a nice sight. They both wanted the ball, but they could not agree on who should have it. They began to quarrel again. The dog wanted to play with the ball, but the cat wanted it for herself. The dog and the cat pulled the ball back and forth. They quarreled so much that the ball broke. The dog was sad, and the cat was angry. They did not get to play with the ball. And so, they both went their separate ways, unhappy and without the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. One day, Lily found a mysterious box in her room. It had pretty colors and shapes on it. Lily wanted to open the box, but it had a lock. She tried to find a key, but there was no key. Then, she saw a pattern on the box. The pattern had shapes like a circle, square, and triangle. She thought maybe if she could recognize the pattern, the box would open. Lily looked at the shapes and tried to remember what they were called. She said the names out loud, "Circle, square, triangle!" And guess what? The mysterious box opened! Inside, there was a beautiful toy for her to play with. Lily was so happy that she could recognize the pattern and open the box.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. Sometimes they share, but sometimes they fight. They are very competitive. One day, they notice a big box in the corner. It is wrapped with shiny paper and has a bow on top. They wonder what is inside. They run to the box and try to open it. "Stop, Ben! It is mine!" Lily says. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Ben says. They pull and tug on the box. They rip the paper and make a mess. They are not happy. Mom comes into the living room. She sees the box and the twins. She is not happy either. "What are you doing, Lily and Ben? That box is not for you. It is a gift for your cousin. He left it here yesterday. He will come back to get it later. You should not touch it. You should say sorry." Lily and Ben feel bad. They did not know it was not their box. They did not mean to ruin it. They look at each other and say sorry. "Sorry, Ben. I was wrong." "Sorry, Lily. I was wrong too." They hug and make up. They help Mom fix the box and clean the mess. They learn to be more careful and respectful. They are happy again.
