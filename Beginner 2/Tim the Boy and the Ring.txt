Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. His favorite cartoon was about a funny cat and a silly dog. Every day, he would sit in front of the empty TV screen and wait for his cartoon to start. One day, while Tim was watching his cartoon, the phone started to ring. He ran to pick it up, but when he answered, there was no one there. Tim was confused, but he went back to watch his cartoon. The phone rang again, so Tim picked it up, but again, no one was there. Tim was getting scared, but then he saw something funny on the TV. The cat and the dog from his cartoon were making the phone ring! They were playing a trick on him. Tim laughed and laughed, and he knew that his cartoon friends were just having fun. From that day on, Tim loved his cartoon even more, and he always smiled when he heard the phone ring.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big truck in the park. The truck has a cage. In the cage, there is a lion. "Wow, look at the lion!" Lily says. "He is so big and yellow!" "Can we go and say hi to the lion?" Tom asks. "I don't know," Lily says. "Maybe he is not nice. Maybe he is unknown." They go closer to the truck. They see a man with a hat. The man is the lion's friend. He takes care of the lion. "Hello, kids," the man says. "Do you want to meet my lion? His name is Leo. He is very friendly. He likes to sing." "Sing?" Lily and Tom say. "How can a lion sing?" The man smiles. He opens the cage. He says something to Leo. Leo comes out of the cage. He walks to the man. He rubs his head on the man's leg. He looks at Lily and Tom. "Leo, can you sing for the kids?" the man asks. Leo nods. He opens his mouth. He makes a loud sound. It sounds like a roar, but also like a song. "Wow, he is singing!" Lily says. "He is amazing!" Tom claps his hands. He says, "He is not unknown. He is cool!" Lily and Tom laugh. They like Leo. They want to be his friends. They sing with him. They have fun in the park.

One day, a lovely cat found a big mug. The cat liked the mug very much. She played with it all day long. She thought, "I will take this mug home and keep it forever." The cat went to her friend, the dog. She showed him the mug. The dog said, "This is not your mug. You must return it to the owner." The cat did not want to give it back. She said, "No, I found it. It is mine now." The sky turned dark and it began to rain. The cat went home with the mug. She felt happy but also scared. She knew she should return the mug, but she did not want to. At night, the cat heard a loud noise. It was the owner of the mug. He was very angry. He took the mug away from the cat. The cat was sad and cried. The cat learned that she should have returned the mug when her friend told her to. Now, she lost the lovely mug and felt bad.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a cat named Kitty. Kitty had very long hair. Sue liked to brush Kitty's hair. Kitty liked it too. One day, Sue and Kitty went to the park. Sue saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. Sue put down the brush and started to climb. Kitty wanted to follow Sue, but it was hard. Kitty felt uncomfortable. Sue saw Kitty and stopped. She got down from the tree and kneeled next to Kitty. Sue picked up the brush and brushed Kitty's hair. Kitty felt better. They both smiled and played under the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Penny and she was three years old. One day, she decided to go outside and explore. She found a bench and decided to sit down, but it was getting dark. She started to worry and wanted to head back home. Suddenly, she heard a voice from behind her. "Don't be scared, it's just me!" It was her friend Tommy. Tommy had been looking for Penny and found her on the bench. He was happy to see her and started to walk back home with Penny. On their way home, Penny and Tommy walked past a big, dark forest. Penny was scared and asked Tommy if they could take another way home. Tommy told her not to worry and said he would protect her. With Tommy by her side, Penny felt much better and found her courage again. They walked past the forest and headed back home, happy and safe.
