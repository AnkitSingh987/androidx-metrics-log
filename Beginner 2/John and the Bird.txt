Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Bird

John loved to explore, he was an adventurous boy! Everyday he would adventure around the backyard looking for new and exciting things. Today he found something special, a pile of stuff! It looked like nice, soft, fluffy stuff so he decided to nap in it. When John awoke, he noticed his feet were starting to feel funny. He couldn't move his feet and they were starting to hurt. He heard a voice behind him,"What are you doing in my nest?". John slowly turned his head and saw a large bird. He was scared but tried to explain,"I was just nap-ping, I'm sorry I didn't know this was your nest!". The bird didn't listen, it just opened his beak wide and grabbed John's feet with its claws. It kept flying until it reached its nest on the top of a tall tree. The bird dropped John in the nest and then flew away. John was stuck on the tall tree and lost all his adventurous spirit. He didn't know how he would ever get out of that nest!

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He was running and jumping and having lots of fun. Today was a very sunny day, and Spot was glad to be outside. While playing, Spot saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, so he ran to it. But when he tried to catch the ball, he missed it. Spot felt a little sad, but he didn't give up. Spot tried again and again to catch the ball. Finally, he caught it! Spot was so glad that he didn't miss the ball today. He played with the ball all day long and had lots of fun.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bell in the park. The bell is shiny and red. It has a rope to pull. "Look, a bell!" Tom says. "Let's pull the rope and make it ring!" "OK!" Mia says. "But we have to be careful. The bell is very high. We have to jump to reach the rope." Tom and Mia run to the bell. They take turns to jump and pull the rope. The bell makes a loud sound. Ding-dong, ding-dong. Tom and Mia laugh and clap. They feel lively and happy. But then, they hear a voice. It is the park keeper. He is angry. He does not like the noise. "Hey, you two! Stop pulling the bell! You are disturbing the birds and the people!" he shouts. "Go away from the bell, or I will call your parents!" Tom and Mia are scared. They let go of the rope. They run away from the bell. They say sorry to the park keeper. They do not want to get in trouble. They find a quiet spot in the park. They sit on a bench. They are still friends. They hug and smile. "Next time, let's play something else," Tom says. "OK," Mia says. "But the bell was fun, right?" "Yes, it was," Tom says. "But maybe too loud." They giggle and nod. They look for a new game to play. They are still lively and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive tree in a small forest. The tree had many logs. One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the forest. She saw the attractive tree and thought it was very pretty. Mia saw a small log near the tree. She picked it up and took it home. At home, Mia's mom was cooking dinner. Mia said, "Mom, I found this log in the forest. Can we cook with it?" Her mom smiled and said, "We can't cook with the log, but we can use it to make a fire to keep us warm." Mia was very happy. They used the log to make a fire, and it kept them warm all night.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very helpful boy. He loved to help his mom and dad with their work. One day, Tim saw a small, helpless bird on the ground. The bird could not fly because it had a broken wing. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, can I help the bird?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can help the bird, but first, you must ask the wise old owl a question." Tim went to the wise old owl and said, "Mr. Owl, how can I help the bird with the broken wing?" The wise old owl thought for a moment and then said, "Tim, you must demand help from your friends. They will know what to do." So, Tim went to his friends, the squirrel, the rabbit, and the mouse. He demanded, "Please help me fix the bird's broken wing." His friends agreed and worked together to help the bird. The squirrel found a stick, the rabbit found some soft leaves, and the mouse found some strong string. They used these things to make the bird's wing better. Soon, the bird could fly again. Tim and his friends were very happy to see the bird fly. They knew that by working together and helping each other, they could do anything. The bird thanked Tim and his friends, and they all became good friends forever.
