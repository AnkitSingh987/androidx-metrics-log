Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Stars

Lily was a little girl who liked to draw. She had many crayons and papers to make pretty pictures. One day, she went to the park with her mom and saw a big contest. There were many people who showed their drawings to a judge. The judge had a big hat and a long nose. He looked at the drawings and gave them stars. Some got one star, some got two stars, some got three stars. Lily wanted to join the contest. She ran to her mom and asked for a paper and a crayon. Her mom gave her a yellow paper and a blue crayon. Lily drew a picture of a flower and a sun. She was very happy with her picture. She ran to the judge and showed him her picture. She hoped he would give her three stars. But the judge did not like Lily's picture. He said it was ugly and simple. He said it had no colors and no shapes. He said it was not a real drawing. He gave Lily no stars. He took her picture and threw it in the trash. Lily was very sad and angry. She cried and ran back to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said she was sorry. She said the judge was mean and wrong. She said Lily's picture was beautiful and bright. She said she was proud of Lily. She took Lily's hand and walked away from the contest. They saw a big tree with many birds. The birds sang and flew in the sky. Lily smiled and said she liked the birds. Her mom said she liked them too. They sat under the tree and watched the birds. They forgot about the judge and the contest. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going to go on vacation with his family. They were going to the beach where they could play in the sand and swim in the sea. One day, while Tim was playing in the sand, he saw a little crab. Tim was scared at first, but then he said, "Hello, Mr. Crab! Do you want to be my friend?" The crab looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I'll be your friend. I'm not scary, I promise!" Together, Tim and the crab played in the sand and had lots of fun. They even went to get ice cream at the ice cream stand. Tim was so happy that he made a new friend on his vacation. And he learned that he didn't need to be scared of new things.

Once upon a time, in a land of pastel colors, there was a lonely cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house near a big tree. She had no friends to play with and felt very sad. One day, Kitty saw a little bird stuck in the tree. The bird cried, "Help, help! I can't get down!" Kitty wanted to rescue the bird, so she climbed up the tree. She got close to the bird and said, "Don't worry, I'll help you." Kitty tried to rescue the bird, but she got stuck in the tree too. They both cried for help, but no one came to save them. The sun went down, and they stayed in the tree all night. The next day, they were still stuck, feeling sad and lonely.

Sara and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They saw a big yellow fruit on the table. Sara wanted to try it. She picked it up and said, "Look, Ben, a yellow ball!" Ben shook his head. "No, Sara, that is not a ball. That is a lemon. It is sour and yucky. You will not like it." Sara did not listen. She was curious. She bit the lemon and chewed it. She made a funny face. The lemon was very sour and bitter. It hurt her mouth and made her eyes water. She spit it out and cried, "Yuck! Yuck! Yuck!" Ben laughed. He said, "I told you, Sara, lemons are not good. They are only for juice or salad. You are silly and messy. Look at your face and the table. You made a big mess." Sara was angry and sad. She threw the lemon at Ben. It hit him in the head. He cried, "Ow! Ow! Ow!" He dropped his toy car and it broke. He was very mad. He said, "You are mean and naughty, Sara. You hurt me and broke my car. I do not want to play with you anymore." Sara and Ben started to fight. They pushed and pulled and shouted. They knocked over the chairs and the plates. They made a bigger mess. Their mom heard the noise and came to the kitchen. She saw the mess and the fight. She was very upset. She said, "Stop it, Sara and Ben. You are both in trouble. You have to clean up the mess and say sorry to each other. And no more lemons for you. Go to your rooms now." Sara and Ben stopped fighting. They were both sorry and ashamed. They said sorry to each other and to their mom. They cleaned up the mess and went to their rooms. They did not have any fun. They wished they had not played with the lemon. They learned their lesson. The end.
