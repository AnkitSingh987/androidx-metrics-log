Category: Beginner
Title: The Fox and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a little fox. This fox was very independent. He liked to do things all by himself. He lived in a big forest with lots of trees and plants. One sunny day, the fox went for a walk. He saw a big rock and thought it would be fun to jump on it. But when he tried to jump on the rock, he did not see the water on it. His foot started to slip, and he fell down. The fox was not hurt, but he was wet and a little sad. He decided to be more careful next time. He went back to his home in the forest, and from that day on, he always looked before he jumped. The little independent fox learned a big lesson and continued to have many fun adventures in the forest.

One morning, Mommy told Tommy to get up. Tommy did not want to get out of bed. Mommy said he needed to get up or the orange alarm would go off. But Tommy just stayed in his bed. Mommy went over to the alarm and set it to go off. It was orange and it said "beep, beep, beep!" Mommy said it was time for Tommy to wake up. But Tommy still did not want to get up. He thought it was too early. He said he needed five more minutes. Mommy smiled and said "Okay, you can have five more minutes. But then you have to get up." She gave Tommy a big hug and he smiled. Tommy listened to the alarm count down the five minutes. When it was finished, he knew he had to get up. He got out of bed and started his day.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted man. He could write very well. Every day, he would sit and write many stories. People loved to read his stories. They would say, "Wow! This man writes so well!" One day, a little boy came to the gifted man. The boy said, "Can you teach me to write? I want to write stories too." The man smiled and said, "Of course! I will help you write." The gifted man and the little boy sat together. They wrote many stories. The boy learned to write very well. Soon, people loved to read the boy's stories too. They would say, "Wow! This boy writes like the gifted man!" The gifted man was very happy. He had helped the little boy. Now, they could both write and share their stories with everyone. They were a great team.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He was a polite boy. Tim wanted to go to a far place with his family. To go, he needed a thing called a passport. But, Tim could not find his passport. He looked in his room, but it was not there. Tim started to struggle. He asked his mom and dad for help. They looked all around the house. They looked in the kitchen, the living room, and even the bathroom. But they could not find the passport anywhere. Time was running out, and they had to leave soon. Tim was very sad. He knew he could not go to the far place without his passport. His family was sad too. They had to go without Tim. In the end, Tim stayed home and did not get to go on the trip.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Amy loved to play with her old red ball. She liked to roll it on the grass and watch it go. As she was playing, Amy saw a boy with a big blue ball. His name was Tim. "Hi, Tim!" said Amy. "Do you want to play with me and my ball?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes! Let's roll our balls and see whose goes the farthest." They rolled their balls and had so much fun. But then, Amy's old red ball hit a rock and got a leak. The air came out and the ball got smaller. Amy was sad. She didn't want her ball to be broken. Tim saw Amy was sad and said, "Don't worry, Amy. We can fix your ball. My dad has a patch to fix leaks." They went to Tim's house and his dad helped fix Amy's ball. Now, it was like new again. Amy and Tim went back to the park and played with their balls all day. They rolled them and laughed together. They became best friends and played at the park every day.
