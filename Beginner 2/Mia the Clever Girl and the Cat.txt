Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Clever Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Mia. She loved to wear her favorite shirt. It was a pretty shirt with a big, happy sun on it. One day, Mia's mom said, "Mia, it's time to wear a new shirt. This one is dirty." But Mia did not want to take off her favorite shirt. She said, "No, I refuse! I love my sun shirt." Mia's mom had an idea. She showed Mia a new shirt with a cute cat on it. Mia thought the cat was very cute. She said, "Okay, I will wear the cat shirt." Mia put on the new cat shirt and felt very happy. She knew she could love more than one shirt. From that day on, Mia enjoyed wearing different shirts with fun pictures on them.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new mattress for Lucy's bed. At the store, they saw many mattresses. There was a delicate one that Lucy liked. It was soft and nice. "Mom, can I have this one?" Lucy asked. Her mom looked at the mattress and said, "Yes, we can get this one." As they were leaving the store, Lucy let out a big yawn. She was very tired. When they got home, Lucy went to her room to take a nap on her new mattress. But when she walked in, she saw something unexpected. There was a big, friendly bear sleeping on her new mattress! The bear looked up and said, "Hello, I was just trying out your new mattress. It's very comfy!" Lucy was surprised but happy to share her new mattress with her new bear friend. They both lay down and took a nice nap together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play soccer with other kids. They want to join the soccer team at school. The soccer team has a big trophy. The trophy is shiny and has a ball on top. One day, the soccer coach comes to the playground. He says, "Who wants to join the soccer team? We have a game next week. We need more players." Anna and Ben raise their hands. They are happy. They want to play soccer and win the trophy. The coach says, "OK, Anna and Ben, you can join the team. But you have to practice hard. The other team is very good. They are fast and strong. You have to be fast and strong too." Anna and Ben nod. They are ready to practice. The next day, Anna and Ben go to the soccer field. They see the other players. They are wearing red shirts. Anna and Ben are wearing blue shirts. The coach gives them a ball. He says, "Kick the ball to each other. Try to pass and score. Don't be slow. Be quick and smart." Anna and Ben kick the ball to each other. They try to pass and score. But they are slow. The other players are faster. They take the ball away. They score many goals. Anna and Ben are sad. They can't keep up. They think they are not good enough. The coach sees them. He comes to them. He says, "Don't be sad, Anna and Ben. You are doing your best. You are new to the team. You need more time to learn. You have to practice more. But you also have to have fun. Soccer is a fun game. You have to enjoy it. Don't worry about the trophy. Worry about having fun." Anna and Ben look at the coach. They look at each other. They smile. They say, "Thank you, coach. You are right. Soccer is a fun game. We want to have fun. We want to learn. We want to be better. We don't care about the trophy. We care about the team." The coach smiles. He says, "That's the spirit, Anna and Ben. You are good friends. You are good teammates. You have a lot of potential. I'm proud of you. Now, let's go and play some more. Let's have some fun." He gives them a high five. They run to the field. They join the team. They play soccer. They have fun.
