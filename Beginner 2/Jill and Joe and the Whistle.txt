Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and Joe and the Whistle

Jill and Joe were walking home from school together. They both wore their new clothes for the first time. “Look at my shirt,” said Joe. It was huge! He waved it around, making it whistle in the wind. “I wish I had a shirt like that,” Jill said. They kept walking and soon saw a huge tree in front of them. “Let’s climb it,” said Joe. Jill agreed, and they started to make their way up the big tree. At the top was a box full of clothes. They lifted the lid and Joe's eyes lit up when he saw the huge shirt inside. It was a perfect fit for him! Jill smiled and said, “You were right. You did find a huge shirt!” Joe grinned and said, “Now we can both whistle in the wind!”

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy. He was very small and his fur was soft and cuddly. He had a big, stuborn personality though, and he liked to run around the garden, barking and playing. One day, the little puppy was running around in the garden, when he heard a big, scary roar. What could it be? The little puppy was scared and hid under a bush. He asked the big dog who lived there, 'What was that roar?' Big Dog kept quiet and didn't answer. The little puppy was very curious and kept asking Big Dog, 'What was that roar? Please tell me!'. Finally, Big Dog said, 'That was a lion. He lives in the jungle. If you want, I can take you to meet him.' The little puppy was so excited. He said 'Yes!' and he followed Big Dog to the jungle. When they got there, the little puppy was amazed. There was a lion! He was huge and his roar was very loud. The little puppy was so scared he hid behind Big Dog. Big Dog smiled, 'See, I told you. Lions are very powerful and stubborn. But don't worry, he won't hurt you.' The little puppy stayed close to Big Dog while they watched the lion. It was a truly amazing sight!

Once upon a time, there was a light bug named Spark. Spark lived in a big tree with his family. They were all very happy. Every night, they would light up the tree and make it look pretty. One day, Spark got lost. He could not find his way back to his tree. He was very sad. As he sat on a leaf, he saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. Spark wanted to help the girl. He used his light to look for the toy. They found it under a bush. The girl was very happy, and she hugged Spark. The girl wanted to help Spark, too. She took him to her home and showed him a big map. They found Spark's tree on the map. The girl took Spark back to his tree, and they said goodbye. Spark's family was so happy to see him. They all lit up the tree together. Spark learned that helping others can also help you.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there were many animals. There was a big, fit horse named Tom. Tom liked to help the farmer fix things. One day, Tom was fixing a fence. He saw a small, sad bunny. The bunny said, "I lost my mom. Can you help me find her?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." Tom and the bunny looked for the mom. They looked in the barn, in the field, and by the pond. Then, they found her. But guess what? The mom was not a bunny, she was a cat! The little bunny was really a kitten. The kitten was happy to be with her mom again. Tom smiled and went back to fixing the fence.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old. She lived on a farm with her family. One day, the girl wanted to measure the length of the path in the farm. She asked her mom, "Mommy, can I measure the path? Please!" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. Let me help you." The mom and the girl went to the path. The mom said, "Now speak up, how long should the measure be?" The little girl said, "It should be really long!" The mom smiled and said, "It sounds like it is a very long and rough path." So the mom and the girl measured the path with a very long and rough measure. The little girl was so proud that she could measure the path. She smiled and said, "Now I know how long the path is!"
