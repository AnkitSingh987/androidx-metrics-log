Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was sad because he could not find his owner, Tim. Spot looked everywhere, but Tim was not available. Spot missed Tim very much. One day, Spot met a big cat named Fluffy. Spot said, "Have you seen my owner, Tim? I miss him." Fluffy thought for a moment and said, "I think I saw Tim at the park." Spot was happy and ran to the park. At the park, Spot found Tim playing with a ball. Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim saw Spot and said, "Spot, I missed you too!" They were both happy to be together again. From that day on, Spot and Tim played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to find different things. One day, he found a big box in his yard. The box had a note on it. The note said, "Fill this box with treasure." Tom thought about what he could put in the box. He found some rocks, sticks, and leaves. He put them in the box. The box was full, but Tom did not feel happy. Tom's mom saw him and said, "Tom, the treasure is not just things. It is being kind and helping others." Tom thought about this. He went to his friend's house and helped him clean up his toys. Then, he helped his mom cook dinner. Tom felt happy now. The moral of the story is that the real treasure is being kind and helpful to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many friends, like birds and squirrels. They all lived happily together. One day, dark clouds came. The wind blew strong. The rain fell hard. The big tree was scared. The birds and squirrels hid in the tree. Then, lightning came. The lightning hit the big tree. The tree was hurt. The birds and squirrels had to leave. They were sad. A wise bird came to examine the big tree. The wise bird said, "The tree was too big and too tall. That is why the lightning hit it." The moral of the story is: sometimes being too big can bring trouble.

Sara and Ben were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were in a castle and the furniture was their throne and their bed. They put a blanket over the couch and made a tent. They had some cookies and juice for their snack. "Let's taste the cookies," Sara said. "They are sweet and crunchy." "OK," Ben said. "But don't make a mess. Mom will be mad if we spill the juice on the furniture." They ate their cookies and drank their juice. They were happy and full. They decided to play some more. They got out their toys and made some noise. But they did not see that the juice had spilled on the floor. It made a big wet spot. It was slippery and sticky. Sara got up and ran to get her doll. She stepped on the wet spot. She slipped and fell. She hit her head on the table. She cried out loud. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Sara said. "It hurts! It hurts!" Ben heard her cry. He ran to help her. He also stepped on the wet spot. He also slipped and fell. He landed on the couch. He knocked over the blanket and the tent. He made a big mess. "Oof! Oof! Oof!" Ben said. "I'm sorry! I'm sorry!" Mom heard the noise. She came to the living room. She saw the wet spot, the spilled juice, the broken cookies, the toys, the blanket, the couch, and the children. She was not happy. "What happened here?" Mom said. "Why is everything wet and dirty?" Sara and Ben looked at each other. They did not know what to say. They felt bad. They knew they had done something wrong. They wished they had been more careful.

One day, a girl named Lily went to play in the park. She saw a soft, green plant with a big root. She wanted to create a new friend from the plant. She pulled the root out of the ground and started to make a face on it. Lily showed her new friend to her mom. "Look, Mom! I made a new friend from this root!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "That's nice, Lily. But be careful, plants need their roots to live." Lily played with her new root friend all day. But when the sun went down, the root friend started to look very sad. Lily didn't know that taking the root out of the ground was bad for the plant. The next day, the root friend was all brown and dry. Lily was sad because her new friend was gone. She learned that plants need their roots to stay alive, and she promised to be more careful in the future.
