Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Bump

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max was not like normal dogs. He had a big bump on his back. Max did not like the bump. It made it hard for him to play with his friends. One day, Max's mom took him to the doctor. The doctor said, "Max needs an operation to make the bump go away." Max was scared, but he wanted to be a normal dog. After the operation, Max felt much better. The bump was gone! Now Max could run and play like a normal dog. He was very happy, and his friends were happy too. They all played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to wear his favorite shirt. It had a big, round moon on it. He loved the moon because it was shiny and bright at night. But when he looked for his shirt, he could not find it. Tim felt sad and did not know what to do. Tim asked his mom for help. She looked all around the house, but the moon shirt was not there. It was cold outside, so they thought maybe it was in the laundry. They looked in the laundry, and there it was! The moon shirt was hiding under a big, warm blanket. Tim was very happy to find his moon shirt. He put it on and felt warm even though it was cold outside. Now, Tim could go outside and play with his friends, wearing his favorite moon shirt. He knew that with his mom's help, they could solve any problem together.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat with a sail. The boat liked to go on the water. One day, the sun was very hot and harsh. The water started to go away. The boat could not sail anymore. The boat was sad because it wanted to sail. The boat's sail was wet, but the hot sun made it dry. The boat did not like the dry sail. It wanted the sail to be wet again. A kind rain cloud saw the sad boat. The rain cloud wanted to help. It rained on the boat and made the sail wet. The rain also made the water come back. The boat was happy and could sail again. The boat and the rain cloud became friends.

Once upon a time, in a high tree, there was a bird named Blue. Blue liked to talk a lot. He talked to all his friends in the tree. One day, Blue heard a story about some shiny gold. Blue wanted to find the gold. He talked to his friends about where the gold could be. His friend, Red, said, "I think the gold is by the big rock." Blue thought this was a good idea. He flew down to the big rock to look for the gold. Blue looked and looked, but he could not find the gold. Then, he remembered a clue from the story. The gold was hidden under a leaf. Blue looked under the leaves by the big rock. And there it was! He found the shiny gold! Blue was so happy. He flew back to his high tree and talked to his friends about his big find.

Once upon a time, there was a cool dog named Max. He had big muscles and loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Max wanted to perform a trick for his friends. He was very excited. Max practiced his trick every day. He would jump high in the air and spin around. His muscles helped him jump very high. All his friends watched him and clapped their hands. They could not wait to see Max perform his trick. The day came for Max to perform his trick. All his friends gathered around to watch. Max took a deep breath, jumped high in the air, and spun around. He landed on his feet and everyone cheered. Max was so happy that his cool trick made his friends smile.

Once upon a time, a wise cat lived in a big house. The cat had a long neck. She loved to do fun things. One day, she saw a big red ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. The wise cat with the long neck tried to push the ball with her nose. But the ball did not move. The cat tried to kick the ball with her paw. But the ball did not move. The cat was sad. She did not know what to do. Then, the wise cat had an idea. She used her long neck to pick up the ball with her teeth. The cat was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball popped! The cat was surprised. She found out that the ball was not a ball. It was a big red balloon!
