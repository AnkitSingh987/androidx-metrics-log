Category: Beginner
Title: The Jeep and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little jeep. The jeep was red and loved to drive around. One day, the jeep went to the park. At the park, the jeep saw a big ice cream truck. The jeep was glad to see the ice cream truck. The jeep went to the ice cream truck and asked for an ice cream. The ice cream man gave the jeep a big ice cream cone. The jeep was very happy. The jeep took a big lick of the ice cream. The jeep could taste the sweet ice cream. It was very yummy. After the jeep finished the ice cream, it was time to go home. The jeep said bye to the ice cream man and drove back home. The jeep was glad it got to taste the yummy ice cream at the park. The jeep went to sleep, dreaming of more ice cream adventures.

Ben and Mia were at the park with their mom. They liked to play on the swings, the seesaw and the slide. The slide was very big and shiny, and they had to climb many steps to reach the top. "Look, mom, I can slide very fast!" Ben shouted as he slid down the slide. He landed on the soft grass and laughed. "Me too, me too!" Mia said. She followed her brother and slid down the slide. She was a little scared, but she liked the feeling of the wind in her hair. They slid down the slide many times, until they saw a man with a cart. The man had many candies in his cart. He had lollipops, gummies, chocolates and more. Ben and Mia loved candies. They ran to the man and asked him for some. "Please, please, can we have some candies?" they said. The man smiled and said, "Sure, you can have one candy each. But you have to pay me one coin for each candy." Ben and Mia looked at their mom. She was sitting on a bench, reading a book. They did not have any coins. They felt sad. "Please, mom, can we have some coins for candies?" they said. Mom looked at them and said, "No, you can't. You already had a snack before we came to the park. You don't need any more candies. They are not good for your teeth and your tummy. Come on, let's go back to the slide. It's more fun than candies." But Ben and Mia did not want to go back to the slide. They wanted candies. They were persistent. They kept asking their mom for coins. They whined and cried and stamped their feet. "Please, mom, please, please, please!" Mom was not happy. She said, "Stop it, Ben and Mia. You are being very naughty and rude. If you don't stop, we will have to go home and you will have no candies and no slide. Is that what you want?" Ben and Mia looked at each other. They did not want to go home. They wanted to stay at the park and play on the slide. They realized that they were being silly and selfish. They said sorry to their mom and to the man. They gave him a hug and a smile. "Sorry, mom, sorry, man. We don't need candies. We have the slide. The slide is better than candies." Mom smiled and said, "That's right, Ben and Mia. The slide is better than candies. I'm proud of you for being good and polite. Now, let's go back to the slide and have some more fun." Ben and Mia nodded and ran back to the slide. They slid down the slide many times, until they were tired and happy. They forgot about the candies. They had the slide. The slide was better than candies.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big bell. The bell would ring every day. When the bell rang, everyone knew it was time to go to the town square. In the town square, there was a kind judge. The judge would help people with their problems. He would listen and then tell them what to do. Everyone liked the judge because he was very fair and nice. One day, a little boy came to the town square. He saw the big bell and the judge. The boy thought the bell was impressive. He wanted to ring the bell too. The judge saw the boy and smiled. He let the boy ring the bell. Everyone in the town was happy and they all clapped. The boy felt proud and the judge was happy to help.
