Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Roof

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a big wing and a small wing. He felt guilty because he could not fly like the other birds. He wanted to find a way to fly with his friends. One day, Tim met a kind man named Bob. Bob liked to drive a big truck. Tim told Bob about his wings and his wish to fly. Bob had an idea to help Tim. Bob put Tim on the roof of his big truck. He started to drive very fast. Tim felt the wind on his wings. He tried to flap his big wing and small wing. With the help of the wind and the truck, Tim was flying. He was so happy and grateful to his new friend Bob.

One day, a bird with a big wing was in a tree. The bird saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her ball. The bird said, "Hi Sue, do you want to play with me?" Sue looked up and saw the bird. She nodded and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" They played together, being very careful not to hurt each other. The bird would fly and catch the ball with its wing, and then drop it for Sue to catch. They had a lot of fun. But, as they played, a big wind came and took Sue's ball away. "Oh no! My ball!" Sue cried. The bird looked at Sue and said, "Don't worry, I will get your ball back." The bird flew high and fast to catch the ball. Just as the bird was about to catch it, a big butterfly came and caught the ball with its wings. The bird was surprised! The butterfly said, "I saw you playing and I wanted to play too!" The bird, Sue, and the butterfly all played together. They were happy and had lots of fun. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

One day, a stubborn cat and a dog went to the sea. The cat wanted to drive a boat. The dog said, "No, I want to drive the boat!" The cat and the dog looked at each other and then they had an idea. The cat said, "Let's both drive the boat together!" So, the cat and the dog got in the boat and started to drive. They were happy and having fun, but then something unexpected happened. A big wave came and pushed the boat back to the shore. The cat and the dog were wet and cold, but they learned that they should not be stubborn. They learned to work together and share. They said to each other, "Next time, we will listen and help each other." And from that day on, they always did.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a card on the floor. The card had a pretty picture of a dog on it. She showed it to her friend, Tom. Tom liked the card too, but he wanted it for himself. Sue did not want to give the card to Tom. Tom said, "I promise I will give you something if you give me the card." Sue thought about it and said, "Okay, I will give you the card, but you need to give me something nice." Tom went to find something for Sue. He looked and looked, but he could not find anything. Then, he saw a big rock. He thought Sue might like it. He picked it up, but it was very heavy. It hurt his foot when he dropped it. Tom went back to Sue and said, "I am sorry, I could not find anything for you. I hurt my foot with a big rock." Sue felt bad for Tom. She gave him the card and said, "I hope this makes you feel better." They both smiled and were happy again.

Mummy and Daddy were angry with Jim. Jim had been naughty and broken some things. Mummy and Daddy said he needed to go out and supply some ash. So Jim went out with his bucket. He knocked on Mrs Smith's door and said, "Mummy and Daddy sent me to supply some ash." Mrs Smith was very kind. She said, "I'm sorry that Mummy and Daddy are angry. Here, take this ash for them," and she gave him a bag full of ash. Jim said thank you and took the bag home. He went into the room where Mummy and Daddy were and showed them the bag of ash. Mummy and Daddy smiled. They were not as angry anymore. Jim was relieved.
