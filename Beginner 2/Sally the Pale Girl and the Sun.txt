Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Pale Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a pale girl named Sally. She liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Sally saw a well near her house. She wanted to look inside the well. Sally went to the well and looked down. She saw water and wanted to wash her hands. She leaned in to get some water, but she fell into the well. Sally was scared and wet. She called for help, but no one heard her. She tried to climb out, but she could not. In the end, Sally stayed in the well and was very sad.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play in the park. They had a favorite tree with a big branch that they could sit on and pretend to be birds. One day, they saw a new boy in the park. He had a shiny bike and a red hat. He looked very cool. "Who is that?" Lily asked Tom. "I don't know. Maybe he wants to play with us," Tom said. They ran over to the new boy and said hello. He smiled and told them his name was Max. He said he liked their tree and asked if he could sit on the branch with them. "Sure, you can sit with us," Tom said. "But you have to promise to be nice and share." "OK, I promise," Max said. They climbed up the tree and sat on the branch. They talked and laughed and had fun. Lily liked Max. He was funny and kind. But Tom felt jealous. He felt like Max was taking his friend away from him. He wanted Lily to play with him, not Max. "Hey, Lily, let's go play on the swings," Tom said. "But I want to stay here with Max," Lily said. "Please, Lily, come with me. I'm your friend, not him," Tom said. "But he's my friend too. He promised to be nice and share," Lily said. Tom got angry and said, "Fine, then. Stay with him. I don't want to play with you anymore." He jumped off the branch and ran away. Lily felt sad and confused. She didn't want to hurt Tom's feelings, but she also liked Max. She didn't know what to do. She looked at Max and said, "I'm sorry. I have to go find Tom. He's my friend and I don't want him to be mad at me." Max nodded and said, "I understand. I'm sorry too. I hope you can be friends again." Lily climbed down the tree and ran after Tom. She hoped she could make him happy and not jealous. She hoped they could all play together and be friends. She hoped Tom would forgive her and keep his promise.

Once upon a time, there was a big metal box. The box was in the park. It had a smile on it. The box seemed nice. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw the metal box. The box was kind and compassionate. It wanted to be her friend. Lily and the metal box played all day. They had so much fun. The metal box was happy. Lily was happy too. They were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her toys and eat yummy food. One day, her mommy made her a big bowl of rice for lunch. Sue thought rice was boring, but she was hungry. Sue started to eat the rice, but she wanted to make it fun. She used her hands to feel the rice in the bowl. It was soft and squishy. Sue giggled as she played with the rice. As Sue played with the rice, she made a big mess. Rice was all over the table and floor. Her mommy saw the mess and said, "Sue, please clean up the rice and finish your lunch." Sue cleaned up the mess, and from then on, she didn't think rice was boring anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a calm cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to sit in the sun and feel the hot rays on her soft fur. One day, while she was sitting outside, a little bird named Chirpy flew down and sat next to her. "Hello, Kitty!" said Chirpy. "Would you like to be my friend?" Kitty thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, Chirpy. I accept. We can be friends." From that day on, Kitty and Chirpy played together every day. They would chase each other around the yard and share yummy treats. The other animals in the town saw how much fun they were having and wanted to join them. Soon, Kitty and Chirpy had many friends. They all played together, laughed, and had a great time. And they all learned that being calm and kind was the best way to make new friends.
