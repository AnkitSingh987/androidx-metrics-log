Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy made of steel. It was a big, strong toy car. Tim loved to play with his steel car every day. One day, Tim saw a fake car on the shelf. It looked like his steel car, but it was not. It was made of plastic. Tim wanted to show his mom the fake car. He ran to her and said, "Mom, look!" He used his finger to point at the fake car on the shelf. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, that is a fake car. It looks like your steel car, but it is not the same." Tim was happy that he could tell the difference between his real steel car and the fake one. He played with his steel car all day and never forgot about the fake car on the shelf.

One day, a fast car went zoom down the road. It was easy for the car to go so fast because it was a race car. The race car driver, named Tim, loved to drive fast and win races. In the town, there was a big store that sold yummy steak. The store owner, named Sue, heard the race car zoom by. She said, "I bet Tim would like a big steak after his race!" Sue had an idea. She went to the race and waited for Tim to finish. When Tim won the race, Sue walked up to him and said, "Great job, Tim! Here is a big steak for you to eat." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! I love steak!" Tim and Sue became good friends. They would always remember the day they met, when the race car went zoom and the yummy steak brought them together.

One day, the sun was shining very bright. A tired wolf was walking in the forest. He was looking for a place to take a nap. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a good place to sleep. As the wolf slept under the tree, a little bird came and sat on the tree. The bird saw the wolf and thought he was a big rock. The bird started to sing a happy song. The wolf woke up and saw the bird. He was hungry and wanted to eat the bird. But when the wolf tried to catch the bird, he found out he was too tired to move fast. The bird saw the wolf and said, "You are not a rock, you are a wolf!" The bird was not scared of the wolf. Instead, the bird and the wolf became friends. They played and sang songs together in the forest. The wolf was not tired anymore, and they both had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry on a fine Tuesday. He wanted to order some food to eat. Tom went to the store and asked for some food. The store lady said, "Sorry, Tom, we don't have any food today." Tom was sad and still hungry. He went to another store to try again. At the other store, Tom tried to order food again. But, the store man said, "No food here, Tom." Tom was very sad and hungry. He went home with no food. That Tuesday was not a good day for Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty thief. He liked to take things that were not his. One day, he saw a big, red ball in a yard. He wanted it very much. So, he went to the yard to take the ball. But, there was a big dog in the yard. The dog saw the thief and started to bark. The thief was scared. He knew he had to delay and wait for the dog to sleep. So, he hid behind a tree and waited for a long time. While the thief was waiting, a little girl came out of the house. She saw the dirty thief and thought he was sad. She did not know he was a thief. The little girl gave the thief a big hug. The hug made the thief feel happy and loved. He did not want to take the ball anymore. The thief said sorry to the little girl and they played with the ball together. The thief learned that it was better to share and be kind.
