Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Tom and the Bull

Sam and Tom were brothers who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big bull in the field next to their house. The bull had horns and a ring in his nose. He looked angry and scary. "Let's go see the bull!" Sam said. He was brave and curious. He wanted to touch the bull and make him run. "No, Sam, that's a bad idea!" Tom said. He was careful and smart. He knew the bull could hurt them. "The bull is not our friend. He is big and strong and mean. We should stay away from him." But Sam did not listen to Tom. He was ignorant and stubborn. He thought he knew better than Tom. He ran to the fence and climbed over it. He zipped towards the bull and shouted, "Hey, bull, look at me!" The bull heard Sam and saw him. He did not like Sam. He felt annoyed and threatened. He lowered his horns and charged at Sam. He was fast and furious. "Sam, run, run!" Tom cried. He was scared and worried. He wanted to help Sam, but he could not reach him. He watched in horror as the bull caught Sam and tossed him in the air. Sam screamed and flew. He felt pain and fear. He landed on the ground and did not move. He was hurt and bleeding. He wished he had listened to Tom. The moral of the story is: Do not be ignorant and stubborn. Listen to your brother who is careful and smart. Stay away from the bull who is big and strong and mean. He is not your friend. He can hurt you.

One day, a boy named Tim found a stick. He picked it up and played with it. He liked the stick a lot. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam was deaf, so Tim used his hands to explain how fun the stick was. While they were playing, a dog came. The dog took the stick from Tim. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted the stick back. They tried to call the dog, but the dog did not hear them. Sam had an idea. He used his hands to explain his plan to Tim. Sam threw a ball far away. The dog saw the ball and ran to get it. When the dog dropped the stick to get the ball, Tim picked up the stick. Tim and Sam were happy again. They learned that working together can solve problems.

Once upon a time, there was a high hill. On top of the hill, there was a big tree. Every day, a little boy named Tim would go up the hill to play. One day, while Tim was playing, a small bird came up to him. The bird said, "Give me food!" The bird was very demanding. Tim was surprised because he did not know birds could talk. He gave the bird some bread from his bag. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird grew bigger and bigger. It was not a bird anymore! It turned into a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Thank you, Tim, for giving me food. I was under a magic spell, and you helped me turn back into a bear." Tim and the bear became best friends and played on the high hill every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a gray doll. The doll was very pretty. Sue wanted to play with the doll, so she took it home. Sue liked to feed her new doll. She gave it pretend food like apples and cake. They played together all day. Sue and the doll were very happy. One day, something unexpected happened. When Sue fed the doll, it started to talk! The doll said, "Thank you for the food!" Sue was very surprised, but now she had a talking friend to play with. They had even more fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, white swan. She lived in a pretty pond. She had many friends like ducks and fish. The swan was very obedient. She always listened to her mom. One day, while the swan was swimming, she saw a small, green frog. The frog was sad. "What is wrong?" asked the swan. The frog said, "I have a big rock on my back. Can you help me remove it?" The swan wanted to help her new friend. The obedient swan gently used her beak to remove the rock from the frog's back. The frog was very happy. "Thank you, swan!" said the frog. They became best friends and played together in the pond every day. And they lived happily ever after.
