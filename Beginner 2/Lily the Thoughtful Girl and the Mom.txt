Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Thoughtful Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to get a jar from the top shelf. Lily said, "Mom, I am too small. Can you allow me to use a chair?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily, but be careful." Lily got the jar and gave it to her mom. They made yummy cookies and shared them with their friends. Everyone was happy, and Lily felt proud of herself.

---

One day, a lovely cat named Tom wanted to cook a big cake. He asked his friend, a little dog named Max, to help him. "Max, will you help me cook a big cake?" Tom said. Max was happy to help, so they went to the kitchen to start cooking. They mixed flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Tom and Max were having fun cooking together. But then, they heard a loud sound from outside. They went to the window and saw a big march of ants coming towards their house. Tom and Max were scared, but they had an idea. They quickly finished the cake and put it outside. The ants saw the cake and started to eat it. Tom and Max watched as the ants ate the cake and then marched away from their house. Tom and Max were happy that the ants left their house. They went back inside and cooked another cake to share. They learned that sometimes, sharing can make things better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called Radio. Radio was a small, filthy car that he played with all the time. The car was dirty because Tim liked to drive it through mud and dirt. One day, Tim was playing with Radio in the park. He saw a big hill and wanted to race Radio down it. As he pushed the car down the hill, it went faster and faster. But then, Radio got stuck in a big puddle of mud. Tim tried to pull Radio out, but the car was stuck. Tim did not want to surrender. He wanted to save his toy. So, he found a big stick and used it to pull Radio out of the mud. Tim was very happy to have his toy back. He decided to clean the filthy car and keep it safe. And from that day on, Tim took better care of Radio and they had many more fun adventures together.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide and a swing. They want to try them. Tom goes first. He climbs up the slide and slides down. He laughs and says, "That was easy and fun!" Mia wants to try too. She climbs up the slide, but she is scared. She does not slide down. She cries and says, "I can't do it! It is too high!" Tom hears Mia cry. He runs to her and says, "Don't cry, Mia. I will help you. You can do it. It is easy and fun!" He holds her hand and slides down with her. Mia stops crying and smiles. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. That was easy and fun!" Their moms see them and clap. They say, "Well done, Tom and Mia! You are brave and kind. We are proud of you. Do you want a treat?" They give them each a piece of chocolate. Tom and Mia say, "Yes, please! We love chocolate!" They eat the chocolate and share it with each other. They are happy and hug their moms. They say, "We love you, mom!" They play some more and have a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a warm sun. The sun loved to dance in the sky. Every day, it would dance and make everyone happy. The sun had a friend, a knot. The knot lived in a big tree. The knot liked to watch the sun dance. One day, the knot wanted to dance too. The knot tried to move but it could not. The knot was sad. It wanted to dance like the sun. The sun saw the knot was sad and wanted to help. The sun had a plan. The sun danced close to the knot. The knot started to feel warm. The knot was happy to feel the sun's warmth. But then, something unexpected happened. The knot became too warm and the tree started to burn. The fire spread and the big tree was gone. The knot was gone too. The sun was sad and stopped dancing. The end.
