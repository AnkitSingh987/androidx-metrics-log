Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Tim's mom was making a spicy soup. Tim wanted to help, so his mom let him serve the soup. Tim carried the big bowl of soup to the table. On the way, he saw his friend, the cat. The cat said, "Meow, what do you have there, Tim?" Tim said, "It's spicy soup, cat. I am helping mom serve it for dinner." Tim put the soup on the table, and everyone sat down to eat. They all loved the spicy soup that Tim helped serve. Tim felt happy that he could help his mom in a big way. From that day on, Tim always helped his mom in the kitchen, and they made many yummy meals together.

Once upon a time, there was a brown bird who lived in a birdcage. The birdcage was in a big tree. The bird liked to sing and play all day. One day, a little boy was walking by the tree. He heard the bird singing. The boy said, "Hello, bird! I like your song. Can I be your friend?" The bird said, "Yes, I want a friend too!" Every day, the boy would pass by the tree and talk to the bird. They became very good friends. The bird was happy to have a friend, and the boy loved listening to the bird sing. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. But there was a big dog that scared them. The dog was ugly. It had black fur and yellow teeth and red eyes. "Go away, dog!" Tom shouted. "You are not nice!" But the dog did not go away. It barked and growled and chased them. Tom and Lily ran to the swings. They hoped the dog would not follow them. "Can we swing, please?" Lily asked a lady who was sitting on a bench. The lady had a book and a hat. The lady looked at them and smiled. "Of course, you can swing," she said. "But be careful of the dog. He is not friendly. He belongs to the man who lives in the house over there. He does not permit anyone to come near his yard." "Thank you, lady," Tom said. He helped Lily get on a swing. He pushed her gently. Lily laughed and kicked her legs. She felt happy. But the dog did not give up. It ran to the swings and jumped at them. It tried to bite their feet and legs. Tom and Lily screamed. They were afraid. "Help, help!" they cried. "The dog is bad!" The lady heard them and put down her book. She got up and walked to the swings. She waved her hand and shouted at the dog. "Stop, dog! Go away! Leave the children alone!" The dog stopped and looked at the lady. It saw that she was angry. It was afraid of her. It turned and ran back to the house. It hid under the fence. Tom and Lily got off the swings. They ran to the lady and hugged her. They were grateful. "Thank you, lady," they said. "You are very kind. You saved us from the dog." The lady hugged them back. She was glad. She checked their feet and legs. They were not hurt. They only had some dirt on their knees. "You are welcome, children," she said. "You are very brave. But you should not play near the dog. He is not safe. He is ugly and mean." Tom and Lily nodded. They agreed. They did not like the dog. They liked the lady. She was nice and sweet. "Can we play with you, please?" they asked. "You have a book and a hat. They are pretty." The lady smiled. She liked the children. They were cute and polite. "Of course, you can play with me," she said. "But first, let me clean your knees. They are dirty." She took a tissue from her bag and wiped their knees. They felt better. They thanked her again. Then they sat on the bench with her. She read them a story from her book. They listened and looked at the pictures. They liked the story. It was funny and happy. They forgot about the dog. They had fun with the lady. They became friends.
