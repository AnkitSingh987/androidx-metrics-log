Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise Owl and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a high tree, there was a wise old owl. This owl had a lot of wisdom. He knew many things. All the animals in the forest would come to ask the owl for help. One day, a little mouse went to the high tree. He said, "Owl, I demand you tell me how to be strong like a lion." The owl thought for a moment and said, "You must find the big red flower in the forest. But be careful, little mouse." The little mouse went to find the big red flower. He didn't listen to the owl's warning. He was too excited. He found the flower and ate it all up. He thought he would be strong like a lion now. But the little mouse didn't become strong. Instead, he got very sick. The other animals in the forest were sad. They knew the wise owl had tried to warn the little mouse. The little mouse learned that he should have listened to the wisdom of the owl.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a noisy toy that she loved to play with all day. One day, she found some money on the floor. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Look, mom!" said Lily. "I found some money!" Her mom examined the money and smiled. "That's great, Lily! Let's save it in your piggy bank." The next day, Lily heard a strange sound coming from her piggy bank. It was making more noise than her noisy toy! She opened it and saw that the money had turned into a tiny, talking bird! "Hello, Lily!" said the bird. "I am a magic bird. I can grant you one wish for finding me." Lily was so surprised and happy. She wished for a new friend to play with. The magic bird turned into a little girl, and they played happily together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to draw. One day, he drew a fierce lion on a big paper. The lion had a big mane and sharp teeth. Tim was very happy with his image. Tim showed his mom the lion. "Wow, Tim! That's a great lion!" his mom said. Tim felt proud of his drawing. Later, Tim went to play outside with his friends. He left the lion on the table. While Tim was outside, the wind blew the lion off the table. The lion started to follow Tim. It went out the door and down the street. The lion wanted to play with Tim too. Tim saw the lion coming and was surprised. "Look, friends! My lion is here!" he said. All the kids laughed and played with the lion. They had a fun day with the fierce lion that followed Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a modest bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat carrots. One day, he hopped to the garden to find a big, yummy carrot. As he was eating, he met a squirrel named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Bob! It's cold today. I think it will freeze tonight." Bob nodded and said, "Yes, Sue. I will hop home before it gets too cold." Bob finished his carrot and hopped home. That night, it did freeze, but Bob was warm and safe in his cozy home. He was happy he had a new friend and a full tummy.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes and find things. Sometimes they found worms, sometimes they found stones, sometimes they found nothing. One day, they found a shiny thing. It was round and red and had a hook on it. Lily said, "It is an ornament. It is for a tree. We can hang it on our tree." Tom said, "No, it is not an ornament. It is a ball. We can play with it. We can throw it and catch it." They both wanted the shiny thing. They started to pull it and tug it. They made angry noises. They did not share. Then, they heard a loud bark. It was Spot, the dog. He saw the shiny thing. He wanted it too. He ran to Lily and Tom. He jumped on them. He grabbed the shiny thing in his mouth. He ran away. Lily and Tom were sad. They lost the shiny thing. They followed Spot. They saw him digging a hole. He put the shiny thing in the hole. He buried it. He wagged his tail. He was happy. Lily and Tom were not happy. They wanted the shiny thing back. They tried to dig the hole. But Spot growled at them. He did not let them. He guarded the hole. Lily and Tom gave up. They went back to the garden. They found another thing. It was yellow and soft and had a peel. Lily said, "It is a banana. It is for eating. We can share it." Tom said, "OK, it is a banana. We can share it. You can have the first bite." Lily peeled the banana. She took a bite. She made a sour face. She spit it out. She said, "Yuck, it is not a banana. It is a lemon. It is sour. I do not like it." Tom laughed. He said, "It is a lemon. It is sour. I like it. I can have it." He took the lemon. He took a bite. He made a sour face too. He spit it out. He said, "Yuck, it is too sour. I do not like it either." They both threw the lemon away. They looked for something else. They hoped to find something nice. They hoped Spot would not take it. They hoped to have fun.
