Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Door

Lily and Tom are playing in the park. They see a big door made of steel. It is shiny and hard. They want to see what is behind the door. They push the door and it opens. They go inside. It is dark and cold. They hear a loud noise. It is a scary noise. They want to go back. They run to the door. But the door is closed. It is locked. They cannot open it. They are trapped. They are scared. They cry for help. A man hears them. He has a key. He comes to the door. He unlocks it. He opens it. He sees Lily and Tom. He is angry. He asks them why they are there. They say they are sorry. They say they were curious. They say they are scared. The man is not so angry. He tells them the door is not for playing. He tells them it is dangerous. He tells them it is a storage room. He tells them to go back to the park. He tells them to be careful. Lily and Tom say thank you. They say they will not do it again. They run back to the park. They hug their mom. They are happy. They are safe.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day in his favorite place, a big soft bed. One day, a little mouse named Jerry moved into Tom's house. Tom and Jerry did not get along. Jerry liked to play tricks on Tom. One day, Jerry thought it would be fun to scare Tom. So, Jerry hid behind a chair and waited for Tom to walk by. When Tom walked by the chair, Jerry jumped out and said, "Boo!" Tom was so scared that he ran away and hid under the bed. Jerry laughed and said, "I got you, Tom!" Tom was not happy, but he learned that he needed to be more careful around Jerry.

Once upon a time, there was a man. The man was anxious. He did not know what to do. He wanted to laugh but he could not. He tried to find ways to laugh. One day, the man went to a park. He saw kids playing and laughing. He tried to laugh with them, but he still could not. He felt sad. The man kept trying to laugh. In the end, the man never laughed. He stayed anxious and sad. The man went home and cried. The man never found a way to laugh. That was the bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. They both love to play and listen to jazz music. In the park, they saw a big box. The box had the word "useful" on it. Tim and Max were very curious. Tim said to Max, "What do you think is in the box?" Max barked and wagged his tail. They opened the box and found a lot of useful things. There were toys, balls, and even a small jazz drum set. Tim and Max were very happy. But then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the box and said, "That's my box! I lost it yesterday." Tim and Max were sad. They didn't want to give the box back. But then, Sue said, "Let's share the box and play together!" Tim, Max, and Sue played with the toys and listened to jazz music. They all became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Bob and Sheila. They both love to ride their vehicles. One day, Bob said to Sheila, "Let's make our vehicles better." Sheila was excited. She smiled and said, "Yes! Let's do it!" So Bob and Sheila went to the garage. Bob took out a screwdriver and said, "We need to tighten the screws on our vehicles". Sheila nodded and quickly followed Bob's instructions. She tightened the screws on her vehicle and Bob tightened the screws on his. Afterwards, Bob said, "There! Our vehicles are now better." Sheila smiled and said, "Yes, they sure look better now!" Bob and Sheila both jumped into their vehicles, eager to put their improved vehicles to the test.
