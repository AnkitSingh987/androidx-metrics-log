Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Coat

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She liked to feed the birds every day. One day, the weather was icy and cold. Lily put on her warm coat and went outside to feed the birds. As Lily was feeding the birds, she heard a big sound. "What is that?" she asked her mom. Her mom said, "That is thunder, my dear. It is very loud, but it cannot hurt you." Lily was scared but her mom held her hand and told her not to worry. Lily continued to feed the birds and soon the thunder stopped. The birds were happy and sang a beautiful song for Lily. She smiled and said, "I am not scared of thunder anymore!" Her mom was proud of her brave little girl.

Lily and Max were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, they found a big box of toys in the park. They were very happy and ran to the box. "Look, Lily, a doll!" Max said. He picked up a pink doll with long hair and a dress. "Look, Max, a car!" Lily said. She picked up a red car with four wheels and a horn. They played with their toys for a while, but then they got bored. They looked at each other's toys and wanted to try them. "Can I play with your car, Lily?" Max asked. "Can I play with your doll, Max?" Lily asked. They nodded and smiled. They decided to share their toys and switch them. They gave each other their toys and said thank you. They played with their new toys for a while, and they had more fun. They made the car go vroom and the doll talk. They laughed and hugged. They were not lonely anymore. They were happy friends who liked to share and switch. They learned that playing together was more fun than playing alone.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play outside. It was a wet day, but she didn't care. She put on her gloves and went to the park. Sue loved to play in the wet grass and splash in the puddles. At the park, Sue saw a big box. She did not know what was inside. She wanted to find out. Sue opened the box and saw a cute little cat. The cat was wet and cold. Sue felt sorry for the cat and wanted to help it. Sue took off her gloves and put them on the cat to keep it warm. Then, her mom arrived at the park. Sue told her mom about the cat. Her mom said, "Surprise! This is your new pet!" Sue was so happy. She named the cat Glove, and they went home to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy telephone that he loved to play with. Tim was a very flexible boy. He could bend and twist in many ways. One day, Tim wanted to play with his friend, Sue. He asked his mom, "Can I play with Sue today?" His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to play with Sue." Tim was so happy. Tim and Sue played together. They talked on the toy telephone. They laughed and had a lot of fun. They were both very flexible, bending and twisting as they played. They had a great day together.

One day, a big red bus was driving down the road. The bus was old and broken. It made a loud noise as it went. People on the street would look and say, "That bus needs to be fixed!" Inside the bus, a little girl named Lily was sitting with her mom. She was scared of the loud noise. Lily said, "Mom, why does the bus scream?" Her mom said, "It's just the broken bus, sweetie. Don't worry, we will get off soon." As the bus went on, the noise got louder and louder. Lily covered her ears and started to cry. Her mom hugged her tight and said, "It's okay, we're almost there." Just then, the bus stopped at their stop. They got off and the noise was gone. Lily smiled and said, "No more screaming bus!" Her mom smiled too, happy that Lily felt better.
