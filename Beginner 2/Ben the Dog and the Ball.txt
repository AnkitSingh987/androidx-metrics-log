Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Dog and the Ball

One day, a little dog named Ben went for a walk. He was very hungry. He looked for food. He saw a big tree. Under the tree, he found a red ball. Ben was happy. He picked up the ball and started to play. Ben threw the ball high in the air. The ball went up and up. Then, the ball started to spin. It spun round and round. Ben watched the ball spin. He felt dizzy. He sat down on the ground. As the ball spun, it made a noise. The noise was loud. Ben was scared. He ran away from the ball. He ran back to his home. Ben's mom gave him food. Now, Ben was not hungry anymore. He was happy and safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a belt that he liked to wear. It was a very nice belt. His mom told him, "Tim, do not take off your belt." Tim said, "Okay, Mom." At the park, Tim played with his friends. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He forgot what his mom told him and took off his belt. He put the belt on a bench and climbed the tree. After playing, Tim's mom brought out some yummy snacks. She asked, "Where is your belt, Tim?" Tim remembered he left it on the bench. They went to look for it, but the belt was gone. Tim felt sad. Then, one of Tim's friends came running with the belt. "I found your belt, Tim!" he said. Tim was happy and put his belt back on. He learned to listen to his mom and not take off his belt. They all enjoyed the yummy snacks and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue went out to play. She saw her shadow on the ground. Sue said, "Hello, shadow! Do you want to play with me?" The shadow did not say anything, but it followed her everywhere she went. Sue thought her shadow was useful because it was always with her. Sue and her shadow started to play a game. They would run and try to catch each other. Sue would run fast, and her shadow would run fast too. They laughed and had fun. Sue said, "You are good at running, shadow!" Then, something unexpected happened. The sun went behind a big cloud, and Sue's shadow disappeared! Sue was sad and said, "Where did you go, shadow? I miss you!" She looked for her shadow, but it was not there. After a little while, the sun came out again, and Sue's shadow came back. Sue was so happy to see her shadow again. They continued to run and play together. Sue knew that her shadow would always be there when the sun was shining.

One day, a big elephant named Elly walked in the forest. She had a very long trunk. She was happy and loved to play with her friends. Elly found a big red ball stuck in a tree. She used her long trunk to get the ball. She got the ball and saw a little boy named Tim. Tim looked guilty because he lost the ball. Elly said, "Is this your ball?" Tim said, "Yes, thank you!" Elly and Tim played with the ball. They had so much fun. But then, Elly's trunk hit the ball too hard. The ball flew away and popped on a sharp rock. Elly and Tim were sad. They did not know what to do. They felt guilty. Just then, a kind man came by. He saw the popped ball and said, "Don't worry! I can fix it!" The man took out a special tool and fixed the ball. Elly and Tim were so happy. They thanked the man and played with the ball all day.

Little Jimmy was in the park playing with his toy gun. He was having a great time making shooting noises and pretending he was a soldier. Then he noticed a strange man in the playground. The man had a thin cut on one of his hands. Jimmy was scared. He asked the man, "What happened to your hand?" The man replied, "I was shooting something and I got a thin cut." Jimmy felt a little better. He smiled and said, "My toy gun makes shooting sounds, too!" The man smiled and said to Jimmy, "Let me show you how to use it safely so you don't get a thin cut like me!" The man showed Jimmy how to hold the gun and showed him the safety rules of shooting. Jimmy had the best time playing with his new friend and the toy gun. Whenever Jimmy goes to the park, he gets to shoot with his new friend and he's always careful not to get a thin cut. And he's never scared anymore!
