Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Park

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. One day, they find a big toy bus in the park. It is red and yellow and has many seats. "Wow, look at this bus!" Sara says. "It is so cute!" "Can we play with it?" Tom asks. "Yes, let's play!" Sara says. "I will be the driver and you will be the passenger." They climb on the bus and pretend to drive around the park. Sara makes honking noises and Tom waves to the birds and the flowers. They have a lot of fun. But then, they hear a loud voice. "Hey, kids, that's my bus!" the voice says. "Give it back to me!" They look and see a man running towards them. He is wearing a blue uniform and a hat. He is the real driver of the bus. "Oh, no, we are sorry!" Sara says. "We did not know it was your bus. We just wanted to play." The driver reaches the bus and looks at them. He is angry at first, but then he sees their scared faces. He softens a bit and smiles. "It's okay, kids, I understand," he says. "But this bus is not a toy. It is my job to drive people around the city. You can't play with it. It is dangerous." He helps them get off the bus and gives them each a sticker. The sticker has a picture of a bus and says "I love buses". "Here, take these stickers," he says. "They are cute, too. And next time, if you want to ride on a bus, you can ask me. I will let you sit in the front and see everything. But only if you promise to be good and listen to me. Deal?" Sara and Tom nod and say "Deal". They thank the driver and apologize again. They stick the stickers on their shirts and wave goodbye to him. They run back to their parents, who are waiting for them. They tell them what happened and show them the stickers. Their parents are glad that they are safe and that they learned a lesson. They hug them and say "We love you". Sara and Tom are happy. They learned something new and made a new friend. They still like to play with cars and trucks, but they also like to ride on buses. They think buses are cute, too.

Anna loved her soft teddy bear. She always hugged him and played with him. He was her best friend. She had many happy memories with him. He made her smile every day. One day, Anna went to the park with her mom and dad. She brought her teddy bear with her. She wanted to show him the flowers and the birds. She put him on a bench while she ran to the swings. She smiled and laughed as she swung high in the air. But when she came back to the bench, her teddy bear was gone. Someone had taken him. Anna looked everywhere, but she could not find him. She cried and cried. She felt very sad and scared. She wanted her teddy bear back. Her mom and dad tried to comfort her. They said they would buy her a new teddy bear. But Anna did not want a new teddy bear. She wanted her old teddy bear. He was special. He had her smell and her love. He had her memories. Anna never smiled again. She missed her teddy bear so much. She wished she had never left him on the bench. She wished she had never gone to the park. She wished she had stayed at home with him. She wished he was still with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a spoiled boy who always wanted to win. He loved to play games with his friends. One day, Tom and his friends had a race. The winner would get a big shiny trophy. The race started, and Tom tried his best to win. He ran very fast, but his friend Tim was faster. Tom did not like that. He thought of a plan. He hid behind a tree and waited for Tim to pass by. When Tim was close, Tom jumped out and scared him. Tim fell, and Tom ran ahead to win the race. At the end of the race, Tom got the big shiny trophy. But his friends were not happy. They saw what Tom did to Tim. They did not want to play with Tom anymore. Tom's trick was revealed, and he felt sad. He learned that being spoiled and cheating was not good. From that day on, Tom decided to play fair and be a better friend.
