Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and a shiny watch. He loved to show off his watch to all his friends. One day, Tom went to the park. He met a little boy named Sam. Sam saw Tom's watch and said, "Wow! That's a pretty watch!" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, it is. I like it a lot." But then, a big wind came and blew the watch off Tom's wrist. Tom tried to catch it, but he could not. A fast dog saw the watch and picked it up in his mouth. Tom and Sam tried to prevent the dog from running away with the watch. They said, "Stop, dog! Give back the watch!" The dog stopped and put the watch down. To their surprise, the dog could talk! The dog said, "I'm sorry. I just wanted to play." Tom and Sam were amazed. They became friends with the talking dog and played together all day.

One day, Henry went out to pick some berries. He had never done it before and he was a bit fearful. He approached a berry bush and saw how pretty the small, dark berries looked. He was excited to taste it. But then, he heard a voice calling: "Hey! Those are my berries! You need to pay if you want to eat them!" Henry was so scared. He looked around and saw a small fairy standing next to the bush. She had wings and a long wand! He said, shaking: "I'm sorry! I don't have money! What can I do?" The fairy smiled. "Don't worry. You don't need to pay with money. Just help me pick all the ripe berries from the bush and you can have one basket for free." Henry happily agreed. He worked very hard and soon there was a big pile of berries. The fairy gave him a basket and said "Thank you for your help! Now enjoy your berries." Henry thanked her and ran home with the basket, filled with fresh berries. He never felt so happy before and he knew he wouldn't forget the lesson he learned that day: always be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, in a wide, wide room, there was a big piano. A little boy named Tim and his cat, Fluffy, liked to play in this room. One day, Tim saw Fluffy climb up on the piano. "Fluffy, be careful!" Tim said. Fluffy looked down at Tim and meowed. Tim wanted to climb, too. He tried and tried, but he could not climb up on the piano. Tim was sad. His mom came into the room and saw him. "Why are you sad, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I can't climb up on the piano like Fluffy." Mom smiled and said, "You don't need to climb up on the piano. You can play it with your hands." Tim tried to play the piano with his hands, and he made beautiful music. He was happy, and Fluffy jumped down to listen. The moral of the story is that we all have our own special ways to do things, and we don't need to copy others to be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a happy boy who loved to play. One day, he found a toy gun in the park. It was a big, red gun. Tom was excited and wanted to play with it. Tom played with the gun all day. He pretended to be a hero, saving everyone. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom's friend, Sue, came to the park and saw the gun. She said, "That gun belongs to my brother, Jim!" Tom was surprised. He didn't know the gun belonged to Jim. He thought about how sad Jim must be without his toy. Tom knew what he had to do. He gave the gun back to Sue and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know it was Jim's." Sue smiled and thanked Tom. She said, "Jim will be so happy to have his gun back!" Tom felt good inside, knowing he did the right thing. And from that day on, Tom, Sue, and Jim played together in the park, sharing their toys and having fun.
