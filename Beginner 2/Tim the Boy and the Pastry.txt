Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pastry

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to try new things. One day, Tim's mom made a healthy pastry for him to eat. She put it on a plate and gave it to Tim. "Here, Tim," said Mom. "Try this healthy pastry I made for you. It is good for you and yummy too." Tim looked at the pastry and thought it looked different. He took a small bite and smiled. It tasted so good! He ate the whole pastry and asked Mom for more. Mom was happy that Tim liked the healthy pastry. She made more for Tim and his friends to eat. They all loved the new treat, and they learned that trying new things can be fun and yummy.

Once there was a grumpy little girl and she did not like to smile. One day, while she was walking outside, she saw a van. It was blue and it had a big smiley face on the side. The grumpy little girl was confused. She stopped and stared at the van. She thought, why would a van have a big smile? She was even more confused. The van started to drive away and the girl wanted to follow it! She started to run after the van and she started to feel happy and excited. To her surprise, when she caught up to the van, the big smile had grown even bigger! The little girl started to smile too. The van had made her so happy that she couldn't help but smile. She waved goodbye to the van as it drove on, and never stopped smiling.

Tom and Jen are twins. They like to play in the yard. Mom and Dad are busy in the house. They tell Tom and Jen to be good and not go near the fire. But Tom and Jen are curious. They see a big fire in the woods. They want to see it closer. They sneak out of the yard and run to the fire. They talk to each other. "Wow, look at the fire! It is so bright and hot!" Tom says. "Can we touch it?" Jen asks. "No, it will hurt us. But we can watch it." Tom says. They sit on a log and watch the fire. They do not see the smoke. They do not hear the sirens. They do not notice the fire getting bigger and closer. Soon, the fire reaches the log. It catches on fire. Tom and Jen scream. They try to run away. But it is too late. The fire is everywhere. They cannot escape. Mom and Dad hear the screams. They run out of the house. They see the fire. They see Tom and Jen. They are too late. They cry. They lose their twins. The fire takes them away.

One day, a big red car went down the road. The car went fast. The sky was dry, and the sun was hot. The car stopped near a tree. A girl named Lily and a boy named Tim came out of the car. They wanted to play under the tree. Lily found a ball and said, "Let's play catch!" Tim shrugged and said, "I don't know how to play." Lily smiled and said, "I will show you." They started to play, but the ball went far away. They both ran to get the ball. As they got close to the ball, they saw a big hole in the ground. They looked at each other, and Lily said, "What is in the hole?" Tim said, "I don't know." They went closer to see. A big frog jumped out of the hole! They were scared and ran back to the car. The frog followed them and said, "Wait! I just want to play too!" Lily and Tim stopped running. They looked at the frog and smiled. They played catch with the frog, and they all had fun. The frog was a new friend, and they were not scared anymore.

One day, a wealthy family had a very special meal. They invited their 3 year old daughter, Lucy, to eat with them. She was so excited! "Do we get cake?" exclaimed Lucy, with a big smile on her face. "Yes, we have a special cake for you," said her father. Lucy was so happy that she ran off to tell her friends. She invited them to come to her house to enjoy the delicious meal. When Lucy's friends arrived, they were amazed by the wealth of food. They all had a great time enjoying the meal with Lucy and her family. Afterwards, they thanked Lucy for inviting them over. The end.
