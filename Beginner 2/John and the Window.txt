Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Window

John looked out his window and saw a huge truck coming closer and closer. He ran to the door and yelped with excitement. His mom opened it and smiled. Mom: What do you think it's for? John: I don't know! The truck came to a stop in front of the house. From inside the truck, two men climbed out. They started to reverse and began to unload a huge stay. John ran outside and waved as the truck drove away. His mom came out and put her arm around him. Mom: You must be so excited to use this stay! John: Yeah! John jumped up and down as he watched the huge stay being rolled into the backyard. He couldn't believe it…he was going to have a stay all for himself! John and his mom looked at the stay. It was huge and it was perfect! John jumped on the stay and started having fun. The stay was just what he needed to make his summer memorable.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Buddy. Buddy was black and loved to play with his friends. One day, Buddy saw a little girl named Lucy crying in the park. Buddy walked up to Lucy and asked, "Why are you sad?" Lucy looked at Buddy and said, "I lost my toy and mommy will punish me if I don't find it." Buddy wanted to help Lucy, so they looked for the toy together. They looked under the trees and in the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near the swings. Lucy was so happy and thanked Buddy for being so kind. They played together in the park and became best friends. From that day on, Buddy and Lucy always played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was gray, and the wind was cold. A little girl named Lily wanted to play outside, but she could not because of the rain. Lily's mommy had an idea. She took out her makeup and said, "Let's move to the living room and play with makeup!" Lily got excited and clapped her hands. They moved to the living room and sat on the soft rug. Lily and her mommy put on makeup and laughed. They made funny faces and took pictures. Soon, the gloomy day was not so gloomy anymore. They had so much fun, and the rain outside did not matter.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy chain that he loved very much. He believed it was the most useful toy he had. He played with the chain every day. One day, Tim's friend Sam came to play. Sam saw the chain and thought it was fun. They played with the chain together. They made it long and short. They made it into a circle and a square. They had a lot of fun with the chain. When it was time for Sam to go home, Tim gave Sam a part of the chain. Now, they both had a useful chain to play with. Tim and Sam believed that their chains were the best toys ever. They played with their chains every day and had lots of fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the subway with his mom. They wanted to go to the park. Tim was very excited. He saw a thick tree near the subway and wanted to climb it. At the subway, they met a nice man. The man had a big bag of candy. He said, "Hi, little boy! Would you like some candy?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" and took some candy. He said, "Thank you!" to the nice man. But then, something unexpected happened. The candy started to talk! It said, "Thank you for saving us from the nice man. He was going to eat us!" Tim was surprised but happy that he saved the candy. He and his mom laughed and went to the park to play.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big sack near the trash can. They want to have fun with it. "Let's wear the sack and pretend we are monsters!" Tom says. "OK, that sounds fun!" Sam says. They take turns to wear the sack and chase each other. They laugh and scream. But the sack is very tight. It is hard to see and breathe. "Help! I can't get out!" Sam says when it is his turn to wear the sack. "Ha ha, you are stuck!" Tom says. He thinks it is funny. "Stop laughing! It is not funny! It hurts!" Sam says. He is angry and scared. "OK, OK, I will help you!" Tom says. He tries to pull the sack off Sam's head. But it is too tight. He can't do it. They start to cry. They need help. A lady sees them and hears them. She comes to them. She is kind and strong. She cuts the sack with scissors. She frees Sam. "Are you OK, boys?" she asks. "Yes, thank you, lady!" they say. They hug each other. They are sorry and happy. They learn a lesson. They will not wear tight sacks again. They will be careful with trash. They will play with safe things.
