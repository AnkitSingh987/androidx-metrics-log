Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Book

One day, a boy named Tim found a big book called a dictionary. He liked the book because it had many words in it. Tim wanted to learn all the words, but his little sister, Sue, thought it was crazy. She did not value the dictionary like Tim did. She wanted to play with her toys instead. "Tim, why do you like that big book? It's crazy!" Sue said. Tim told her, "I like it because it helps me learn new words. I value it a lot!" Sue did not understand why Tim liked the dictionary so much. One day, Sue and Tim had a big fight. Sue wanted to play with her toys, but Tim wanted to read his dictionary. They were both sad and did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Sue, let's play a game. I will find a word in the dictionary and we can act it out with your toys!" Sue liked the idea, and they played together. They both learned to value each other's fun and the dictionary became their favorite book to play with.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. They have a memory game with cards. The cards have pictures of animals, fruits, and toys. Tom and Lily take turns to flip two cards and see if they match. If they match, they keep them. If they do not match, they flip them back. One day, they play the memory game in the park. They find a tall tree with a big branch. They put the cards on the branch and sit on the grass. They start to play. "Look, I found two bananas!" Lily says. She flips two cards with yellow bananas. "Good job, Lily!" Tom says. He claps his hands. He flips two cards with red apples. "Wow, you are good too, Tom!" Lily says. She smiles. They play for a long time. They find many matches. They have fun. But then, a strong wind blows. The wind makes the cards fly away. The cards go up in the air and down to the ground. Tom and Lily are sad. They lose their cards. "Oh no, our cards!" Tom says. He tries to catch them. "Where are they?" Lily says. She looks around. They run after the cards. They find some on the grass, some on the flowers, and some on the swings. They pick them up and put them in a pile. They look at the pile. They see that some cards are missing. They cannot find them. "Maybe the wind took them far away," Tom says. He frowns. "Maybe they are in the tall tree," Lily says. She points to the branch. They look at the branch. They see two cards with blue birds. They are stuck on the branch. "Look, there they are!" Tom says. He jumps. "But they are too high," Lily says. She stretches her arms. They think of a way to get the cards. They see a ladder near the tree. They run to the ladder. They carry it to the tree. They put it against the branch. Tom climbs the ladder. He is brave. He reaches the branch. He grabs the cards. He climbs down. "Yay, you did it!" Lily says. She hugs Tom. "Yay, we have all the cards!" Tom says. He hugs Lily. They are happy. They have their cards. They have their memory game. They have their friend. They play again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty pirate named Lucy. She had a big, shiny boat with many colorful flags. Lucy loved to sail on the blue sea with her friends. They laughed and played all day long. One sunny day, while Lucy and her friends were sailing, they found a big treasure chest. They opened it and found a magic record inside. Lucy was very excited and decided to play the record on her boat. When Lucy played the magic record, it made everyone on the boat dance and sing. They danced and sang all day long. The pretty pirate and her friends had the best time ever. And from that day on, they always played the magic record when they sailed the blue sea.
