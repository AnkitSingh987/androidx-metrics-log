Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends all day long. One day, while playing with his friends, Ben felt very tired. He told his friends that he needed to take a short nap. Ben found a nice soft spot under a big tree and closed his eyes. While Ben was napping, his friend, a little bird named Tim, saw something in the sky. It was big and dark, and it made Tim feel a little bit of shock. Tim knew he had to tell his friends about what he saw. But he didn't want to wake up Ben from his nap, so he decided to wait until Ben woke up. Suddenly, there was a loud noise in the sky, like a big clap. Ben woke up from his nap, and Tim told him about the big dark thing he saw in the sky. All the little animals looked up and saw the big dark clouds. They knew it was going to rain. They all ran to find a safe and dry place to hide. Thanks to Tim's warning, all the little animals stayed safe and dry during the rain. After the rain stopped, they went back to playing and having fun in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play on the soft green grass all day long. One sunny day, while Bob was playing, he met a big, scary wolf. "Hi, Mr. Wolf," said Bob. "Do you want to play with me on the grass?" "No, little bunny," said the wolf, showing his big teeth. "I am hungry and I want to eat you." Bob was scared but didn't quit. He had a plan. "Wait, Mr. Wolf! If you play with me, I will show you where to find more food." The wolf agreed and they played on the grass. But the wolf was not nice. He didn't wait for Bob to show him the food. The wolf caught Bob and ate him. The wolf was happy, but poor little Bob was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. They were very happy. One day, the man said, "Let's make a big cake!" The wife agreed. They went to the kitchen and started to stir the cake mix. The man stirred the mix slowly, and the wife stirred the mix fast. They laughed and had fun while they stirred. When the cake was ready, they shared it with their friends. Everyone loved the cake and had a great time. The man and his wife were very happy to see their friends enjoy the cake they made together.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Lucy. She lived near a lake. She loved to sail her little boat on the water. One sunny day, she went to the lake with her boat. While she was sailing, she saw a pretty vase in the water. Lucy picked up the vase and looked inside. She found a tiny, wet note. The note said, "This vase is magic." Lucy took the vase home and put it in her room. Every day, she would look at the vase and think about the magic inside. She loved her small vase and the fun day when she found it while sailing.

Once upon a time, a sailor named Tom lived near the sea. Tom liked to watch the big boats with their white sails. One day, the sky was gloomy, but Tom still went to the sea. As Tom stood by the water, a little bird came to him. The bird said, "Hi, Sailor Tom! I can fly high and see far. Do you want me to tell you a secret?" Tom nodded, excited to hear the secret. The bird flew high and came back to tell Tom. "There is a big boat coming with a big gift just for you!" Tom smiled and thanked the bird. They both waited for the boat, happy to be friends on this gloomy day.
