Category: Beginner
Title: The Ugly Frog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an ugly frog. This frog was not like other frogs. He had a spark in his eyes that made him special. He had many friends because he was very nice. One day, the ugly frog wanted to play a game with his friends. But he did not have a ball. So, he went to his friend, the duck, to borrow a ball. The duck was happy to help and gave him the ball. The ugly frog and his friends played with the ball all day long. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, the ugly frog gave the ball back to the duck. They all said "thank you" and went to their homes. The ugly frog with the spark in his eyes was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy rod that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim couldn't find his rod. He looked everywhere in his room, but it was not there. Tim felt sad and worried. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about his lost rod. She said, "Stay calm, Tim. Let's look for it together." So, they started searching for the rod in the house. They looked in the living room, the kitchen, and even the backyard. Finally, they found the rod hiding behind a big plant. Tim was so happy and hugged his mom. "Thank you, Mom!" he said with a big smile. Tim learned that when he is calm and asks for help, he can solve his problems. And from that day on, whenever Tim felt worried, he remembered to stay calm and ask for help.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat. The cat wanted to go outside today. The cat put on her hat and went out the door. The cat walked in the sun. She saw a big tree. The cat said, "I want to go up the tree today." She climbed up the tree and saw a bird. The bird said, "Hello, beautiful cat!" The cat and the bird played together. They had fun in the tree. Then the cat went down the tree. She said, "Goodbye, bird. I will go home now." The cat went home and had a nap. She was happy she went outside today.

One day, a small dog named Max was sad. He was sad because he had to take a test. Max did not like tests. He was scared he would not do well. Max's friend, a big cat named Sam, saw Max looking sad. Sam asked, "Max, why are you sad?" Max said, "I have a test, and I am scared." Sam smiled and said, "Don't worry, Max. Trust me, you can do it. I will help you." Sam and Max studied together. They worked hard to get ready for the test. The next day, Max went to take the test. He was still a little scared, but he remembered what Sam said. When Max finished the test, he saw that the test was not what they studied. It was a test about being a good friend! Max was not scared anymore. He knew he could pass this test. Max and Sam were both happy. They knew that trusting each other made them good friends. They passed the test together, and they were not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to read books. One day, her mom gave her a task. She had to go to the store and buy some yummy apples for a pie. On the way to the store, Amy met a friendly dog. The dog said, "Woof! Can I help you?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, please! I need to find yummy apples for my task." The dog wagged its tail and led Amy to the store. They found the apples and bought them. Amy thanked the dog and went home. Her mom made a yummy apple pie, and they all enjoyed it together. Amy felt happy that she finished her task and made a new friend.
