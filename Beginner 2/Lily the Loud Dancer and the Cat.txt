Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Loud Dancer and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a loud dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and make noise with her feet. One day, she went for a walk in the park. In the park, Lily saw a big tree. She heard a sound from behind the tree. "What is that?" she said. She went to look and discovered a little cat. The cat was sad and lost. "Hello, little cat! I am Lily the loud dancer. Why are you sad?" she asked. The cat said, "I am lost and can't find my way home." Lily thought for a moment and said, "I will help you find your home." Lily and the cat walked and danced together, making loud noises with their feet and paws. Soon, they found the cat's home. The cat was happy and thanked Lily. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cat. The cat had a big smile. Every day, the cat would play outside. The sky was clear and blue. The cat liked to jump and run in the sun. One day, the cat saw a big box. The cat went to the box and jumped inside. Inside the box was dark, but the cat was not scared. The cat smiled and played in the box. The cat liked the box a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. The box started to move! The cat was surprised. The box took the cat to a new place. The new place was full of toys and treats. The cat smiled even bigger. The cat played and ate treats all day long.

John was a curious boy who went for a walk in the countryside. As he went along, he saw something shining in the wheat. He reached down and picked it up. It was a golden coin. John was so excited to find it! He decided to keep it in his pocket. As he kept walking, John got lost. He looked around for a way back home, but he couldn't find it. Tears filled his eyes as he realized he could no longer find the way back. John was walking for a long time until he saw a little farm. He went into the farm and saw a nice lady who offered him food and a warm drink. She told him that she was looking for some wheat, so John offered her his golden coin in exchange. The lady was so happy to find the wheat and gave John a big hug. She said he was a brave and curious boy, and showed him the way back home. John was so glad he had not lost his golden coin. As he was walking back, he promised to ask for help the next time he got lost.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lilly. She loved to play in the garden with her friends, the bugs. One day, a new bug came to the garden. The bug was very alert and fast. Lilly wanted to be friends with the new bug. Lilly had an idea. She picked up a pretty flower and went to the new bug. She said, "Do you want to play with me and my friends?" The new bug looked at the flower and then at Lilly. The bug then said, "Yes, I want to play, but I have a secret." The new bug told Lilly and her friends to close their eyes. Then, the bug did something with the flower. When Lilly and her friends opened their eyes, the flower was now many colors! Lilly was so happy. She asked the bug, "How did you do that?" The bug said, "I can change colors. I suggest we play a game. I will change the colors of the flowers and you have to find me." Lilly and her friends played the game and had a lot of fun with the new bug.

Tommy was a boy who liked to play with his furry dog, Spot. Spot was a good dog who loved Tommy very much. They liked to run and jump and fetch in the park. One day, Tommy saw a big cat in the park. The cat was black and white and had a long tail. Tommy thought the cat was pretty and wanted to pet it. He ran towards the cat and tried to grab it. "Meow!" the cat said. It did not like Tommy. It scratched Tommy's hand with its sharp claws. Tommy cried out in pain and let go of the cat. The cat ran away. Tommy's hand was red and sore. It also started to itch. Tommy scratched his hand, but it only made it worse. He ran to his mom, who was sitting on a bench. "Mommy, mommy, look at my hand!" Tommy said. "The cat hurt me and now it itches!" "Oh, Tommy, I'm sorry," his mom said. She looked at his hand and saw the scratch. She took out a band-aid and some cream from her bag. She put the cream on Tommy's hand and covered it with the band-aid. "This will help it heal and stop the itch. But you should not try to pet strange cats, Tommy. They might be scared or angry and hurt you. You should only pet animals that you know and that are friendly, like Spot." Tommy nodded. He felt sorry for what he did. He hugged his mom and said, "Thank you, mommy. I'm sorry I was bad. I love you and Spot. I don't need to pet other animals. Spot is the best furry friend I have." His mom smiled and kissed his cheek. "I love you too, Tommy. And Spot loves you too. He is a good furry friend. Now, let's go and play with him. He is waiting for you." Tommy and his mom walked to where Spot was. Spot wagged his tail and licked Tommy's face. Tommy laughed and hugged Spot. He learned his lesson. He would not try to pet strange cats again. He would only play with his furry dog, Spot. He was happy.
