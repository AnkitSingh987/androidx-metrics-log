Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim found a stick. He thought, "I can make something with this stick." Tim was a thoughtful boy who liked to help others. He saw a bird with a broken wing. The bird said, "Help me, please!" Tim used the stick to make a little bed for the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim's friends saw what he did and they all wanted to help the bird too. They brought food and water for the bird. Soon, the bird was well again. The bird said, "You are all so kind. Thank you!" The children learned that being thoughtful and helping others makes everyone happy.

One day, a happy cat named Tom found a cable. He liked to play with the cable. Tom would run and jump with the cable in his mouth. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him playing. Max wanted to play with the cable too. "Can I play with the cable?" Max asked. Tom said, "Yes, we can play together." They played with the cable all day. They did not complain. They were happy to share and play. They were good friends who liked to have fun.

---

Sam and Lily were playing in the backyard. They liked to dig in the dirt and make mud pies. They did not see Mom hanging the laundry on the line. "Look, Lily, I found a big worm!" Sam said, holding up a slimy thing. "Wow, Sam, let me see!" Lily said, reaching for the worm. But Sam did not want to share. He pulled the worm away from Lily and ran towards the laundry. He thought it would be funny to put the worm on Mom's white shirt. "Sam, no!" Lily shouted, following him. "That's not nice!" But Sam did not listen. He threw the worm on the shirt and laughed. He did not see Mom coming from the house. "Sam, what are you doing?" Mom asked, seeing the worm on her shirt. She was angry and sad. Sam felt ashamed. He knew he did something bad. He looked at Mom and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I was just playing." Mom picked up the worm and the shirt. She said, "Sam, you need to remind yourself to be kind and respectful. This is not playing. This is ruining my laundry. How would you feel if I put a worm on your favorite toy?" Sam thought about his toy truck. He loved his toy truck. He did not want a worm on it. He said, "I would feel sad, Mom. I'm sorry. I won't do it again." Mom hugged Sam and said, "I forgive you, Sam. But you need to help me wash the shirt and the worm. And you need to say sorry to Lily, too. She is your sister and your friend." Sam nodded and said, "Okay, Mom. I'm sorry, Lily. Do you want to play with the worm?" Lily smiled and said, "No, thank you, Sam. But I still love you."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was always so excited to pay for something. One day, Sarah went to a shop. She saw a pretty brush and asked her mom to pay for it. Mom said yes and Sarah was so happy! She took it home and looked at it. She liked the color and the feel of the brush in her hand. The next day, it was cold and icy outside. Sarah wanted to take her brush out. She put on her hat, scarf and coat and went outside. She began to brush the snow away to make a path. After she finished, Sarah smiled. Everything was so pretty and clean now. Sarah was happy that she had paid for the brush. She was now able to do something special with it.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with his friends. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tim was very happy. Soon, his friends arrived. They played with a ball, ran around, and laughed a lot. After some time, they got hungry. Tim's mom had a great idea. She brought out a big bag of snacks for everyone to eat. Inside the bag were pale, round cookies. All the kids loved the snack! They ate the cookies and drank some juice. After that, they felt strong and ready to play again. Tim and his friends had a great day at the park.
