Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his grandma. She lived in a small house with a big yard. Tim loved to play in the yard with his dog, Spot. They would run and jump and have lots of fun. In the yard, there was a cooler. Grandma kept cold drinks and yummy snacks in it. One day, the cooler was closed very tight. Tim tried to open it, but he couldn't. He wanted a cold drink, so he asked Spot to help him. Spot was a fierce dog, but he was also very smart. He pulled and pulled on the cooler, but it wouldn't open. Suddenly, the cooler started to shake and jump! Tim and Spot were scared, but they didn't run away. The cooler opened and out came a funny-looking bird. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me! I was stuck inside!" Tim, Spot, and the bird became good friends and played together in the yard every day.

One day, a team of animals were playing near the river. There were two dogs, a cat, and a duck. They were all very happy. The sun was hot, so they wanted to bathe in the river. The cat said, "I want to bathe first! I am very selfish and don't want to wait." The dogs and the duck did not like this. They wanted to bathe too. But they let the cat go first. While the cat was in the water, a big fish jumped out. The fish scared the cat! The cat ran away from the river. The dogs and the duck laughed. They said, "See, being selfish is not good. We should all bathe together." So, the dogs and the duck went into the river. They had fun and played together. The cat came back and said, "I am sorry for being selfish. Can I join you?" The team let the cat join them, and they all had a great time.

Lily and Anna are sisters. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a doll. They are happy. One day, a big dog comes to the park. It is unknown. It has black fur and sharp teeth. It barks loud. Lily and Anna are scared. The dog runs to them. It wants the ball. It jumps and snatches the ball from Lily's hand. Lily cries. Anna hugs her. "Go away, dog!" Anna shouts. "You are bad! You hurt my sister!" The dog growls. It wants the doll too. It moves closer to Anna and Lily. Anna holds the doll tight. She tries to protect it and her sister. Then, a man comes to the park. He is the dog's owner. He sees the dog and the sisters. He calls the dog. "Rex, come here! Bad dog! Leave the girls alone!" The dog hears the man. It drops the ball and runs to the man. The man puts a leash on the dog. He says sorry to Lily and Anna. "I'm sorry, girls. Rex is a new dog. He doesn't know how to play nice. He likes balls and dolls, but he shouldn't take them from you. Are you okay?" Lily and Anna nod. They are still scared, but they are glad the dog is gone. They take the ball and the doll. They hug each other. "Thank you, man," Anna says. "You are good. You helped us." The man smiles. He walks away with the dog. Lily and Anna go back to their mom. They tell her what happened. Their mom hugs them. "You are brave, girls," she says. "You protected each other. I'm proud of you." Lily and Anna smile. They love their mom. They love their sister. They are happy.

One day, a little girl named Mia went on a tour with her family. They went to see many fun things. Mia loved the tour because it was a perfect day to be outside. While they walked, Mia noticed a pretty butterfly. She saw it fly from one flower to another. Mia liked the butterfly and wanted to show her family. But they were all busy looking at other things. Mia felt sad, but then she had an idea. She took a picture of the butterfly with her toy camera. When the tour was over, she showed the picture to her family. They all smiled and said the butterfly was very pretty. Mia was happy that she could share what she noticed on the perfect tour day.
