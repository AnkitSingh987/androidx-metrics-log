Category: Beginner
Title: The Bee and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a bee. This bee was different. It could talk! The bee lived in a big tree with other bees. They all buzzed and worked, but the talking bee wanted to make friends. One day, the talking bee went to a park. There, it saw a cat. The bee said, "Hi, cat! Let's be friends!" The cat was scared. "A talking bee!" it said, and ran away. The bee felt sad, but it didn't give up. Next, the bee saw a dog. "Hi, dog! Let's be friends!" said the bee. The dog was not scared. It was happy. "A talking bee! How fun!" said the dog. They played and laughed all day. The bee and the dog became best friends, and they had many more fun days together.

One day, a little sheep named Tim was playing in the sun. The sun was shining bright and warm. Tim's wool was soft and fluffy. Tim saw his friend, the horse named Max. Max looked ready to play. Tim said, "Hi Max! The sun is shining. Do you want to play with me?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I like your soft wool. Let's play together in the sun!" They played all day under the shining sun. They were happy and had lots of fun. Tim's wool was shining too, just like the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog who loved to dance. He danced all day and all night. Everyone liked to watch him dance. One day, the dog hurt his leg. He could not dance anymore. The dog was very sad. His friend, a wise cat, said, "I know a doctor who can heal your leg. But first, we must go on a long trip to find her." The dog and the cat went on the trip to find the doctor. They walked and walked. The dog's leg hurt, but he kept going. The cat said, "We are almost there. The doctor will heal your leg soon." When they found the doctor, she looked at the dog's leg. She said, "I can heal your leg, but you must promise me one thing." The dog nodded. The doctor said, "You must teach me how to dance like you." The dog was happy! He did not expect the doctor to want to learn to dance. The doctor healed the dog's leg. They danced together, and the dog taught the doctor all his famous dance moves. The dog and the doctor became good friends, and they danced together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue wardrobe in a room. It was full of pretty clothes. Two friends, Tom and Sue, loved to play dress-up with the clothes inside. One day, Tom and Sue found a new dress in the wardrobe. It was the prettiest dress they had ever seen. Tom wanted to wear the dress, but Sue wanted to wear it too. They both disagreed on who should wear the dress. Tom and Sue were sad because they could not agree. Then, they had an idea. They decided to share the dress. Tom wore the dress first and then Sue wore it after. They both had fun playing dress-up together. The moral of the story is that sharing is important. When we share, everyone can be happy and have fun.

One day, a small mouse named Mimi was walking in the grass. She saw a smooth rock and sat on it. Mimi heard a sound coming from a hole near the rock. She went to see who was making the sound. In the hole, Mimi found a tiny bird named Bobo. Bobo was sad because he could not fly. Mimi wanted to help him. She said, "Don't worry, Bobo. We can find a way to help you fly." Mimi and Bobo tried many things. They jumped, they ran, and they flapped their arms. Nothing worked. But they did not give up. They became good friends and played together every day. And even though Bobo could not fly, they were happy to have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a pink cat. The cat had a friend, a spicy bird. They lived in a big tree. The cat and the bird liked to play together. One day, the spicy bird wanted to provide food for the pink cat. The bird flew high and found a big red apple. The bird carried the apple back to the tree for the cat. The pink cat was so happy! They shared the apple and played all day. They were the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.
