Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Frog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had an unusual pet, a big green frog named Fred. They loved to play in the park, near the pond. Every day, they would go to the park and have fun together. One sunny day, Lily and Fred went to the park. They played hide and seek. Lily hid behind a tree and called out, "Fred, where are you?" Fred jumped out from behind a bush and said, "Here I am!" They laughed and played until the sun started to set in the west. As they were going home, Lily noticed that Fred was very dirty. She said, "Fred, you need a bath." Fred didn't like baths, so he tried to run away. But Lily caught him and said, "If you don't take a bath, I will have to punish you." Fred knew that Lily was right, so he let her give him a bath. After the bath, they both went to bed, happy and clean, ready for another day of fun in the park.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a box. The box had a label on it. The label said "Original Toys." Lily hoped that there were fun toys inside the box. She opened the box and saw many colorful toys. Lily picked up a toy car and said, "Vroom, vroom! I hope you can go fast!" She played with the car and made it race around the room. The car was very fast and Lily was happy. Then, Lily's friend Tim came to play. He saw the box and asked, "What's this?" Lily said, "It's a box of original toys. I hope you like them too!" They played with the toys all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a swing hanging from it. Tim wanted to play on the swing, so he asked his mom, "Can I play on the swing, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful and hold on tight with your hands." As Tim played on the swing, a little girl named Sue came up to him. She didn't know how to swing, so she asked Tim, "How do you play on the swing?" Tim didn't know Sue, and he didn't want to share the swing. So, he said, "I don't know. I can't help you." Sue felt sad and walked away. Later, Tim saw Sue trying to swing by herself, but she couldn't do it. Tim felt bad for not helping her. He went to Sue and said, "I'm sorry for not helping you before. Let me show you how to swing." Tim held Sue's hand and showed her how to swing. They both had fun playing together. The unexpected thing was that Tim and Sue became good friends. The moral of the story is that we should always help others when they need it, even if we don't know them.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bag. The bag lived in a big store with many other bags. But this bag was different. It was not pretty like the other bags. It felt very sad. One day, a nice girl came to the store. She saw the lonely bag and liked it a lot. She asked her mom if she could take the bag home. Her mom said yes. The bag was very happy. The girl took the bag everywhere. She put her toys and snacks in it. She thanked the bag for being her friend. The bag was not lonely anymore. They were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree near a house. In the house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy and Bob were friends. One day, Amy had some jam. She wanted to share it with Bob. She called out, "Bob, come here! I have some jam for you!" Bob heard her and flew to her. He was so happy. Bob and Amy sat and ate the jam together. They talked and laughed. Soon, the jam was all gone. They had eaten it all. They both felt full and happy. Then, it was time for Bob to go back to his tree. He said, "Thank you, Amy. I had fun." And with that, their fun day came to an end.
