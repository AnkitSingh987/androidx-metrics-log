Category: Beginner
Title: The Wet Pond and the Duck

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big wet pond. In the pond lived a happy duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to swim and play all day long. One day, Ducky met a new friend named Froggy. Froggy liked to jump and play near the pond. Ducky warned Froggy to be careful near the water. "Don't jump too close or you might fall in!" said Ducky. Froggy knew the law of the pond was to be safe. So, Froggy and Ducky played together, away from the wet water. They had lots of fun and became best friends. And they always remembered to follow the law and be careful near the pond.

One day, a small cat named Kitty found a big ball. The ball had a funny texture. It was not soft like her toys. It was hard and dry. Kitty wanted to play with the ball, but it was difficult to move. Kitty pushed the ball with her nose. She tried to pick it up with her mouth. But the ball was too big and heavy. Kitty was sad. She wanted to play with the ball, but it was too difficult. Just then, her friend Dog came to help. Dog was big and strong. He pushed the ball with his nose, too. Together, Kitty and Dog moved the ball. They played and had fun. Kitty was happy that her friend Dog helped her with the difficult ball.

Anna and Ben are friends who like to play in the park. They like to swing, climb and slide on the big slide. One day, they see a new slide that is very high and long. It looks like fun, but also scary. Anna says, "Let's try the new slide!" Ben says, "OK, but be careful. It is very fast and steep." They climb up the ladder and wait for their turn. Anna goes first. She sits on the slide and pushes off. She slides down very fast and feels the wind in her hair. She laughs and says, "Whee! This is fun!" Ben goes next. He sits on the slide and pushes off too. But he is careless. He does not hold on to the sides. He slides down very fast and feels the wind in his face. He does not laugh. He screams and says, "Help! This is scary!" Ben slides so fast that he flies off the end of the slide. He goes up in the air and does not come down. He keeps going up and up and up. He sees the sky, the clouds, the sun and the moon. He sees the stars and the planets. He sees a big blue planet that looks like his home. He says, "Oh no! I slid off the earth!" Anna sees Ben fly off the slide. She is shocked and worried. She runs to the end of the slide and looks up. She sees Ben in the sky. She says, "Ben! Come back! You slid too far!" But Ben does not come back. He keeps going up and up and up. He slides through space and wonders what will happen to him. He wishes he was not careless. He wishes he was back on the slide with Anna. He says, "Sorry, Anna. I miss you."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to draw with his big box of colors. One day, he drew a big, happy sun on a piece of paper. But then, he made a mistake. He felt sad. Tim asked his friend, Sue, for help. "Sue, I need an eraser please," he said. Sue looked at her eraser, then at Tim. She didn't want to give it to him. "No, it's mine!" she said. Tim was even more sad now. The next day, Tim and Sue were playing with their toys. Sue needed Tim's help. "Tim, give me that toy car," she demanded. Tim thought about the eraser and how Sue didn't help him. He smiled and said, "No, it's mine!" Sue felt sad too. But then, they both learned to share and became happy friends again.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to ride his bicycle. He felt very independent when he rode his bicycle. He loved to feel the wind in his hair as he went fast. As Tim was riding, he saw a big tree. Under the tree, he saw his friend Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! Come relax with me under this tree." Tim got off his bicycle and sat down with Sue. They talked and laughed together. Suddenly, a big bird came and took Tim's bicycle up in the sky! Tim and Sue were very surprised. The bird flew away with the bicycle. Tim was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, we will find a new bicycle for you." They both smiled and went on an adventure to find a new bicycle for Tim.
