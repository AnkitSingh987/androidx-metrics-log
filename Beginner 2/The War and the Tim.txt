Category: Beginner
Title: The War and the Tim

Once, in a land far away, there was a big war. A boy named Tim did not know about the war. The war was harsh and made people sad. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Do you know about the war, Tim?" Tim shook his head. Sue said, "The war is very harsh. Many people are sad." The war became bigger and bigger. Tim and Sue tried to help people. But the war did not stop. The war was too big and harsh. The people stayed sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her stamps. She had a big box full of them. One day, Sue wanted to spread her stamps on the floor to see all of them. While Sue was looking at her stamps, her friend Tim came over. "Hi Sue!" said Tim. "What are you doing?" Sue replied, "I am playing with my stamps. Do you want to play too?" Tim said, "Yes, I love stamps!" They played with the stamps for a long time. They were starting to feel tired. Just then, something unexpected happened. A stamp started to move! Sue and Tim looked at each other, surprised. The stamp grew bigger and turned into a friendly stamp monster. The monster said, "Thank you for playing with me. I was feeling lonely as a stamp." Sue and Tim laughed, and they all played together until it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a hidden room. Inside the room, there was a big box. No one knew what was inside the box. One day, a little boy named Tim found the room. He wanted to see what was inside the box. He opened the box and found a guitar! Tim was so happy. He wanted to play the guitar all day. Tim played the guitar and made loud sounds. The loud sounds disturb his mom and dad. They came to the hidden room and saw Tim playing the guitar. They smiled and said, "We knew you would find the guitar one day." They all played the guitar together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in the park. She saw a small cat stuck in a tree. Lucy wanted to help the cat come down. She tried to climb the tree, but she fell and hurt her knee. Lucy was sad and the cat was still stuck. Lucy's mom saw her crying and came to help. She put a bandage on Lucy's knee to make it feel better. Lucy's mom said, "Let's get some yummy ice cream to help you feel better too." Lucy smiled and they went to get ice cream. While they were eating the yummy ice cream, a kind man came to the park. He saw the cat in the tree and helped it come down. Lucy was happy to see the cat safe. The cat was happy too and they all played together in the park.

Sara and Ben went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. Sara wanted to find a shell for her collection. She walked along the shore and looked at every rock and stick. "Where are the shells?" she asked Mom. "Sometimes they are hard to find, Sara. You have to be patient and keep looking," Mom said. Ben was impatient. He did not want to look for shells. He wanted to build a sandcastle. He walked to a spot where the sand was wet and smooth. He started to dig and pile up the sand. "Come on, Sara, help me build a sandcastle!" he called. Sara did not want to help Ben. She wanted to find a shell. She walked away from Ben and kept looking. She saw something shiny and round in the sand. She ran to it and picked it up. It was a shell! It was big and pink and had stripes on it. Sara was happy. She had found a shell for her collection. "Look, Mom, look what I found!" she shouted. Mom walked over to Sara and smiled. She saw the shell in Sara's hand. "That's a beautiful shell, Sara. Well done! You were patient and you found a shell," Mom said. Sara hugged Mom and showed her the shell. She was proud of her shell. She walked back to Ben and showed him the shell too. "Wow, that's a nice shell, Sara. Can I see it?" Ben asked. Sara nodded and gave Ben the shell. He looked at it and touched it. He liked the shell too. "Can we share the shell, Sara?" he asked. Sara thought for a moment. She liked the shell, but she liked Ben too. He was her brother and he wanted to play with her. "Okay, we can share the shell, Ben. But you have to help me build a sandcastle too," she said. Ben agreed and gave Sara the shell back. They walked to the spot where Ben had started the sandcastle. They worked together and used the shell to make windows and doors. They had fun and made a big and beautiful sandcastle. Mom and Dad came to see their sandcastle and praised them. Sara and Ben were happy. They had found a shell and built a sandcastle. They had a good day at the beach.
