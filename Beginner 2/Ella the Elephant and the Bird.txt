Category: Beginner
Title: Ella the Elephant and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella was a very confused elephant. She did not know where her home was. One day, she met a little bird named Bob. "Hi, Ella!" said Bob. "Why are you so confused?" "I can't find my home," said Ella. "Will you help me?" Bob thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I will help you find your home." Bob and Ella walked and walked. They saw a big tree. "Is that your home?" asked Bob. "No," said Ella. "Let's keep looking." They walked some more and found a big lake. "Is that your home?" asked Bob. "No," said Ella. "But thank you for helping me." Finally, they found a big field with other elephants. Ella was so happy! "This is my home!" she said. "Thank you, Bob!" Ella and Bob became best friends. They played together every day. And Ella was never confused again.

Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim was a very anxious baby. He always worried about things. One day, Tim's mom said, "Let's go to the park!" Tim felt scared, but he went with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tim was scared of the dog. He thought the dog would be mean to him. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog came over to Tim and licked his face. Tim started to laugh. The dog was not mean at all! From that day on, Tim was not so anxious anymore. He learned that sometimes things are not as scary as they seem. Tim and the dog became best friends, and they played together at the park every day. And Tim's mom always said, "Let's go see your friend!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to run fast. One day, Tim saw a big red ball in his yard. He wanted to play with it. He ran fast to the ball. As he ran, he heard a sound. It was a little bird. The little bird told Tim, "Don't kick the ball too hard, or it will break." Tim did not listen to the bird. He wanted to kick the ball very hard and see how far it could go. Tim kicked the ball as hard as he could. The ball went up, up, up into the sky. But then, the ball came down, down, down. It hit a big rock and it broke. Tim was sad. He should have listened to the little bird. Now, his big red ball was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big door in his room. He liked to play jokes on his friends with the door. One day, Tim had an idea for a new joke. Tim put something disgusting on the door handle. He thought it would be funny when his friend, Sam, touched it. Tim called Sam to come over and play. When Sam came, he said, "Let's go to your room, Tim!" As they walked to the room, Tim was excited for his joke. But when Sam touched the door handle, he did not laugh. Sam said, "Ew, what is this disgusting thing on the door?" He was not happy. Tim felt bad for playing the joke. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I thought it would be funny. I won't play jokes like that anymore." Sam forgave Tim, and they played together all day. From then on, Tim only played nice jokes that made his friends laugh.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite toy, a big square block. She loved to play with her square block all day. One day, Sue's mom brought home a new toy. It was an expensive doll with pretty hair. Sue's friend, Jane, saw the doll and wanted to play with it. But Sue wanted to play with her square block. Jane got mad and threw the square block into a puddle of mud. Sue was very sad. Her square block was dirty and wet. Her mom saw Sue's tears and came to help. She took a soft cloth and began to wipe the mud off the square block. Soon, the square block was clean and dry again. Sue hugged her mom and thanked her. She learned to share her toys with Jane. They played with the expensive doll and the square block together. And they all lived happily ever after.
