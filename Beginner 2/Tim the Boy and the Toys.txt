Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box full of fun toys. One day, Tim saw a heavy box on the top shelf in his room. He wanted to see what was inside the box. Tim grabbed a chair to stand on. He climbed up and tried to grab the heavy box. But the box was too heavy for him. Tim tried to use a scale to see how heavy the box was. But he could not read the numbers on the scale. Tim tried again to grab the heavy box. But this time, the box fell on him. Tim was hurt and he cried. His mom came and hugged him. Tim learned not to grab heavy things without help.

Once upon a time there was a big castle. Inside it, there was a beautiful princess with a shiny crown on her head. One day, she decided to take a swim in the castle's moat. It was a tough swim but the princess loved it. She splashed around in the water and laughed. Suddenly, she saw a little fish swimming towards her. "Hi!", said the fish. "I am Cozy and I'd like to welcome you to my home. Can I have a look at your pretty crown?" The princess slowly handed her crown to the fish. Cozy looked at it carefully and then suddenly, he jumped backwards and SPLASHED into the water. The princess screamed and called for help, but it was too late. Cozy was long gone. The princess was so sad that she never returned to the castle's moat again.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat with a sail. The sail was red and white. The boat was very reliable. It could go fast and never broke. One day, the boat wanted to stretch its sail. It went to the sea where the wind blew. The wind helped the boat go fast. The boat was happy. The boat met a little fish. The fish said, "You have a nice sail!" The boat said, "Thank you!" They became friends. The boat and the fish played in the sea every day.

One day, a cheerful girl named Sue went to play in the park. She saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. As she climbed higher and higher, she heard a tiny voice say, "Help me!" Sue looked around but did not recognize who was talking. The tiny voice spoke again, "Down here!" Sue looked down and saw a small bird on a branch. The bird said, "I am scared to fly. Can you help me?" Sue was surprised but wanted to help her new friend. Sue and the bird talked and laughed together. Then, they heard a loud noise. The noise was so loud that there was silence after it. They looked up and saw a big, strong bird flying towards them. The small bird was not scared anymore. It was the small bird's mom! The small bird said, "Thank you, Sue!" and flew away with its mom. Sue was happy she could help and climbed down the tree with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little black puppy named Spot. Spot loved to play all day in the park. One day, he met a big dog named Max. Max could whistle! Spot thought that was very cool. "Max, can you teach me to whistle?" asked Spot. Max smiled and said, "Of course! Just pucker your lips and blow air out." Spot tried and tried, but he couldn't whistle. Spot felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came to the park and saw Spot. She thought he was so cute, even if he couldn't whistle. She took him home and loved him very much. Spot learned that it's okay to be different and that you don't need to whistle to be loved.

Sara and Ben are friends who like to play on the farm. They like to see the animals and feed them hay. One day, they see a new animal in the barn. It is big and brown and has horns. "What is that?" Sara asks. "That is a cow," Ben says. "She gives us milk." "Can we pet her?" Sara asks. Ben nods. "Yes, but we have to be polite. We have to say hello and bow to her first." Sara and Ben walk slowly to the cow. They say hello and bow to her. The cow looks at them and moos. She seems friendly. "Can I give her some hay?" Sara asks. Ben nods. "Yes, but we have to be careful. We have to hold the hay in our hands and let her take it gently." Sara and Ben pick up some hay and hold it in their hands. They offer it to the cow. The cow takes it with her tongue and chews it. She moos again and licks their faces. "Wow, she likes us!" Sara says. Ben smiles. "Yes, she does. She is a nice cow." Sara and Ben pet the cow and hug her. They are happy. They have made a new friend.
