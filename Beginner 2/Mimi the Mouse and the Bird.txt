Category: Beginner
Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big yellow maze, there lived a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to play hide and seek with her friends. They would run and hide in the many twisty paths of the maze. One sunny day, Mimi and her friend Bobo, a small bird, were playing hide and seek. Mimi closed her eyes and counted to ten. Then she opened her eyes and started to look for Bobo. She looked around the yellow maze, but she could not find him. Mimi called out, "Bobo, where are you hiding?" Bobo giggled and said, "You can't find me, Mimi!" Mimi kept looking, and finally, she found Bobo hiding behind a big yellow wall. They laughed and played more hide and seek until the sun went down. The end.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the garden. They liked to make them go fast and make loud noises. But they also liked to make them shiny and clean. They had a cloth and some water to polish their cars. "Look at my car, Lily. It is so shiny and red. It is the best car ever," Ben said. "No, my car is better, Ben. It is shiny and blue. And it has a big horn that goes beep beep," Lily said. They started to argue about whose car was better. They did not want to share their cloth and water. They pulled and pushed each other. "Stop it, Ben. You are making my car dirty. Give me the cloth," Lily said. "No, Lily. You are making my car wet. Give me the water," Ben said. They were so busy fighting that they did not notice the sun. It was very hot and bright. It made their cars hot too. Soon, their cars were too hot to touch. They burned their fingers when they tried to pick them up. "Ow, ow, ow. My car is hot. It hurts," Ben said. "Ow, ow, ow. My car is hot too. It hurts more," Lily said. They dropped their cars and ran to their mom. She was sitting under a tree with a book. She saw their tears and heard their cries. "What happened, Ben and Lily? Why are you sad?" she asked. "Our cars are hot. They burned us. We were fighting over the cloth and the water. We wanted to polish them. But now they are too hot to play with," Ben and Lily said. Mom hugged them and kissed their fingers. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben and Lily. You should not fight over your toys. You should share and be nice to each other. And you should not leave your toys in the sun. They can get hot and hurt you. Next time, you should play in the shade or inside when it is hot. Do you understand?" Ben and Lily nodded. They said, "We understand, Mom. We are sorry. We will share and be nice. And we will not leave our toys in the sun. Can we have some ice cream, please?" Mom smiled. She said, "Yes, you can have some ice cream. But first, let's go and get your cars. Maybe we can polish them together later, when they are cool. How does that sound?" Ben and Lily smiled too. They said, "That sounds good, Mom. Thank you. We love you." They held Mom's hand and went to get their cars. They learned their lesson. They decided to play nicely and safely. And they enjoyed their ice cream. The end.

Once there was a cat called Sally who loved to climb the stairs. Every morning, she would race up the stair and meow to say hello. One day, Sally found the stair to be very dirty. She put her paw on the stair and it came away dirty. Sally was very sad and said, "Who will care for my stair?". Just then, up came Sally's mom. She saw the mess and said, "I will help you clean up your stair, Sally. We can do it together!". Sally was so happy that her mom wanted to help. Together, they worked hard to make the stair clean and shiny. Sally was so proud of her stair. She touched it gently and said, "Mom, thank you for caring for my stair!".
