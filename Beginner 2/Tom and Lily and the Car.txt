Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Car

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw an old bag on a bench. It was brown and dirty and had a hole in it. "Look, a bag!" Tom said. "Maybe there is something inside." He ran to the bench and picked up the bag. He opened it and saw some bread and cheese. "Yum, food!" he said. "Let's eat it." He took out a piece of bread and spread some cheese on it. He gave another piece to Lily. "Thank you, Tom," Lily said. "But where did the bag come from?" She looked around and saw a man sleeping on the grass. He had a beard and a hat and a coat. He looked very tired and sad. "Maybe it is his bag," she said. "Maybe he is hungry too." She felt sorry for the man. She took the bag and walked to him. She tapped him on the shoulder. "Hello, sir," she said. "We found your bag. We are sorry we took some of your food. Here, you can have the rest." The man opened his eyes and saw Lily. He smiled and took the bag. "Thank you, little girl," he said. "You are very kind. I don't have much, but you can have this." He gave Lily a small toy car. It was red and shiny and had four wheels. "Wow, a car!" Lily said. "Thank you, sir. You are very kind too." She hugged the man and ran back to Tom. She showed him the car. "Look, Tom, a car!" she said. "The man gave it to me. He was happy we gave him his bag." Tom and Lily played with the car. They made it go fast and slow and beep and honk. They had a lot of fun. The man watched them and smiled. He ate his bread and cheese. He felt less hungry and less sad. He was glad he met Tom and Lily. They were good children.

Once upon a time, there was a little pigeon named Pete. Pete lived in a hot place where the sun always shined. He had a lot of friends, but he also had a problem. Pete was not very good at keeping things safe. One day, Pete's best friend, a cat named Tom, asked Pete to keep his toy ball safe while he went to take a nap. Pete said, "Okay, I will keep it safe for you." But as soon as Tom went to sleep, Pete started to play with the ball. He was having so much fun that he forgot to keep it safe. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew the ball away. Pete was very sad and scared. He knew he had to find the ball before Tom woke up. So, Pete flew all around the hot place, looking for the ball. Finally, he found it stuck in a tree. Pete tried very hard and got the ball out of the tree. Pete learned his lesson and promised to always keep things safe for his friends. When Tom woke up, Pete gave him the ball back. Tom was very happy, and they played together for the rest of the day. From that day on, Pete became the best at keeping things safe, and all his friends trusted him.

One day, a modest girl named Sue found a camera. She liked to take pictures with it. She took pictures of her cat, her toys, and her friends. She was very happy with her new camera. Sue went to the park with her friend Tim. They played and had fun. Sue said, "Let's take a picture together!" Tim agreed and they smiled for the camera. But then, Sue saw a big mud puddle nearby. She thought it would be fun to jump in it. Sue jumped in the mud puddle and got all dirty. Tim said, "Oh no, now you're all muddy!" Sue looked at her camera and saw that it was dirty too. She tried to wipe the mud off the camera, but it was too late. The camera stopped working, and Sue was very sad. She couldn't take pictures anymore. She wished she hadn't jumped in the mud puddle.

One day, a pirate named Tom found a fine, big chest. He was very happy. "I need to bury this chest so no one can take it," he said to his friend, a parrot named Polly. Tom and Polly went to the beach to bury the chest. "I will dig a big hole, and you will watch for other pirates," Tom told Polly. Polly sat on a tree and looked out for other pirates. Tom dug and dug, and soon, the hole was big enough for the chest. He put the chest in the hole and covered it with sand. "Now, our treasure is safe," said Tom. But Polly saw something in the sky. "Look, Tom, a big storm is coming!" Polly shouted. Tom looked up and saw the dark clouds. They quickly left the beach and went back to their ship. The storm came and washed away the sand, but the chest stayed buried. Tom and Polly were happy because they knew their treasure was safe. And from that day on, they always checked the sky before they buried their treasure.
