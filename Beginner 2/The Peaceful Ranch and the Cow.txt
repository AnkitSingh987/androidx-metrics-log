Category: Beginner
Title: The Peaceful Ranch and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful ranch. On this ranch, there were many animals. There was a big cow, a little pig, and a fast horse. They were all good friends. One day, the little pig lost his toy. He was very sad. The big cow said, "Don't worry, we will search for your toy." So, the big cow, the little pig, and the fast horse searched all over the peaceful ranch. They looked under the trees and behind the rocks. Finally, they found the toy near the pond. The little pig was so happy! He said, "Thank you, friends!" The big cow and the fast horse were happy to help. They all learned that when we help each other, we can find what we are searching for.

---

Lily and Ben were playing in the barn. They liked to pretend they were animals. Lily was a cow and Ben was a horse. They mooed and neighed and ran around the hay. "Let's become something else," Ben said. "What do you want to be?" Lily thought for a moment. She saw a red jar on a shelf. It had a picture of a pepper on it. She was curious. "I want to be a pepper," she said. "A spicy pepper." Ben laughed. "That's silly. How can you be a pepper?" Lily climbed on a stool and reached for the jar. She opened it and smelled it. It made her nose tickle. She took a pinch of the red powder and put it in her mouth. "Wow!" she cried. "It's hot! It's spicy!" She ran to the water bucket and drank some. Ben followed her and tried some too. He made a funny face. "Yuck!" he said. "That's not good. That's not fun." Lily agreed. She spat out the powder. She felt her tongue burn. "Let's not be peppers," she said. "Let's be something else." Ben nodded. He looked around the barn. He saw a basket of eggs. He had an idea. "Let's be chickens," he said. "Chickens are fun. They cluck and lay eggs." Lily liked that. She forgot about the spicy powder. She followed Ben to the basket. They pretended to be chickens. They clucked and flapped their wings and laid eggs. They had fun. They were happy they became chickens. They decided they liked chickens better than peppers. Peppers were too spicy. Chickens were just right.

Ben and Lily are friends who like to play with their dogs. Ben has a big dog named Max, and Lily has a small dog named Coco. One day, they go to the park with their dogs and their moms. "Let's play fetch with the ball!" Ben says. He throws the ball far away and Max runs after it. Max is fast and strong and likes to chase things. "No, let's play tug with the rope!" Lily says. She holds one end of the rope and Coco holds the other end. Coco is smart and cute and likes to pull things. Ben and Lily disagree. They both want to play with their own toys and their own dogs. They start to argue and shout. "Stop it, you two!" Ben's mom says. "You need to share and take turns. How about you switch toys and dogs for a while?" Ben and Lily do not like this idea. They love their own toys and dogs and do not want to give them up. They shake their heads and say no. "Fine, then you need to play nicely with each other. How about you both throw the ball and both pull the rope?" Lily's mom says. "But you have to keep your dogs on the leash. The park has a rule that dogs must be on the leash." Ben and Lily agree to try this. They put the leash on their dogs and walk to the middle of the park. They take turns throwing the ball and pulling the rope. They see that their dogs can play with each other too. Max is gentle and friendly and lets Coco catch the ball sometimes. Coco is brave and playful and tries to tug the rope from Max sometimes. Ben and Lily have fun with their toys and their dogs. They laugh and smile and say sorry for arguing. They learn that it is okay to disagree, but it is better to compromise and cooperate. They also learn that the leash is important to keep their dogs safe and follow the rules. They enjoy the park and their friendship.
