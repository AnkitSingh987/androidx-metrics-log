Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would fly to a clean tree and sing a happy song. The tree was near a big house with a kind family. One day, the bird saw a prune on the ground. The bird picked up the prune and flew to the clean tree. The bird sang a song about the prune, and the kind family heard the song. They came outside and saw the bird with the prune. The family smiled and clapped for the bird's song. The bird was very happy to share the prune with them. They all ate the prune together, and the bird sang more songs for the family. They all became good friends, and the bird sang for them every day.

Tom and Lily wanted to play outside. It was winter and there was snow on the ground. They put on their warm coats, hats, gloves and boots. They asked their mom if they could go to the hill near their house. "OK, but be careful. The hill is icy and slippery. Don't go too fast or too far. And come back before it gets dark," their mom said. Tom and Lily nodded and ran to the hill. They saw other kids with sleds and snowballs. They joined them and had fun. They slid down the hill, made snowmen and threw snowballs at each other. "Look, Lily, I can go faster than you!" Tom said. He pushed his sled hard and went down the hill very fast. He felt the wind in his face and the snow under his sled. He was happy. But he did not see the big rock at the bottom of the hill. He hit the rock and flew off his sled. He landed on the snow with a thud. He hurt his arm and his leg. He cried. "Lily, help me! I'm hurt!" he shouted. Lily heard him and ran to him. She saw his arm and leg were red and swollen. She felt sorry for him. She hugged him and said, "Don't cry, Tom. I'm here. I'll help you. Let's go back to mom. She'll make you feel better." She helped him get up and walk back to their house. She held his hand and his sled. She was a good sister. Their mom saw them and ran to them. She saw Tom's arm and leg and said, "Oh, Tom, what happened? You're hurt. Let me see. We need to go to the doctor. Come on, let's go inside. I'll dress your wounds and give you some hot chocolate." She took them inside and did what she said. She dressed Tom's wounds with bandages and gave him some hot chocolate. She hugged him and said, "You'll be OK, Tom. You're brave. But next time, be more careful on the hill. It's icy and slippery. OK?" Tom nodded and said, "OK, mom. I'm sorry. Thank you. I love you." He smiled and drank his hot chocolate. Lily smiled and drank hers. They were happy. They loved their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to bake with her mom. One day, Amy's mom said, "Let's make dinner for the family." Amy was excited and wanted to help. They started to bake a big, messy pizza. Amy put lots of cheese and sauce on the pizza. It looked so yummy. But, Amy got messy too! She had sauce on her face and cheese in her hair. Amy's mom told her, "It's okay to be messy when we cook, but we must clean up after." When the pizza was done, they all sat down to eat. The family loved the dinner Amy and her mom made. After dinner, Amy helped her mom clean up the messy kitchen. They washed the dishes and wiped the counters. Amy learned that it's fun to bake and make dinner, but it's also important to clean up after. This way, everyone can enjoy a clean and happy home.

Once upon a time there lived a tall man. He liked to read. Every night he would go to the park and sit on the bench. One day while he was reading, he spotted something in the grass. It was a tiny mint! The man picked it up and smiled. He put it in his pocket and continued to read. Suddenly, a small girl came up to him and said, "Hi I'm Molly. Can I have the mint please?". The man smiled and said, "Of course you can!" He handed her the mint and watched as she ran away. Molly was so happy with her mint. She stopped in the park and enjoyed her snack while she read a book. Suddenly, the tall man came up to Molly and said, "You must really like books, huh?" Molly smiled and said, "Yes, I do. I love to read!" The tall man smiled and said, "Me too! Reading is one of the best things in life." Molly smiled and nodded her head. She thanked the tall man for the mint and went home. Molly had a new friend and she had a wonderful mint to enjoy! It was a perfect day.
