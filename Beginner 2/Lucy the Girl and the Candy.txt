Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Candy

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. She saw a big jar of candy on a shelf. Lucy wanted the candy, but her mom said no. Lucy was upset. Lucy thought of a plan. When her mom was not looking, she took some candy and put it in her pocket. Then, she skipped away to hide her candy. Lucy saw her friend Tim and said, "I have candy! Do you want some?" Tim looked at the candy and said, "This candy looks funny. It is not the right color." Lucy did not care. They both ate the candy. Suddenly, their tummies hurt a lot. They were not happy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved Tom very much and gave him a gift. The gift was a big, soft bed for him to sleep in. One day, Tom went outside to play. He ran and jumped in the grass. But then, he started to feel bad. His leg hurt and he could not run anymore. Tom had to suffer because of his hurt leg. The family saw Tom and wanted to help him. They picked him up and put him on his soft bed. They took care of him and made sure he was happy. Soon, Tom felt better and could play again. The family was happy, and Tom was happy too.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a thin ostrich named Ollie. Ollie had a big plan. He wanted to find a new friend to play with. Ollie looked around and saw many trees and a big hill. He thought, "Maybe I can find a friend near the trees or over the hill." The next day, Ollie went to the trees. He looked up and down, but he did not see any new friends. Ollie felt sad, but he did not give up. He remembered his plan and thought, "I will look over the hill tomorrow." On the third day, Ollie walked up the big hill. It was a long walk, but he knew his plan was a good one. When he got to the top, he saw a small pond. And next to the pond was a little duck named Daisy. Ollie went to Daisy and asked, "Will you be my friend?" Daisy said, "Yes, I will be your friend!" Ollie was so happy that his plan worked, and they played together all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to play outside. It was very icy and cold, but they wanted to have fun. They saw a big hill and decided to race to the top. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim. They ran as fast as they could. Tim slipped on the ice, but he got up and kept going. Sue was ahead, but Tim wanted to win. They both reached the top, but Sue got there first. "I win!" she said with a big smile. As they were playing, the mailman came by. He had a lot of mail to deliver. Tim and Sue saw the mailman slip on the ice too. They wanted to help, but they didn't know how. The mailman tried to get up, but he couldn't. The mail got wet and ruined. The mailman was sad, and Tim and Sue felt bad because they couldn't help.

Once upon a time, there was a large wolf. The wolf lived in the woods. He had many friends like the birds, the squirrels, and the rabbits. One day, the wolf met a small mouse. The mouse said, "Hello, Mr. Wolf. Can you help me find my home?" The wolf shrugged, not knowing where the mouse's home was. But he wanted to help his new friend. So, the wolf and the mouse walked together. They looked here and there. At last, they found the mouse's home. The mouse was very happy. The wolf and the mouse became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who loved to play outside. One sunny day, Tim wanted to go to the park. Before he left, Tim's mom said, "Tim, tie your shoes or you will trip!" Tim listened to his mom and tied his shoes. He made sure they were tight so he wouldn't fall. Tim walked to the park, taking big steps. He met his friend, Sam, at the park. They played on the swings and slides all day. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, Sam! Let's play again soon!"
