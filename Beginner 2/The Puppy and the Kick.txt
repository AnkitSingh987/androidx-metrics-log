Category: Beginner
Title: The Puppy and the Kick

Once upon a time there was a playful puppy. He loved to run around and kick things. One day, he found an old pair of pants. He decided to use it as a toy and started to kick it around. "Hey, stop it," said a voice from behind. The puppy jumped and turned around. It was a little girl. "Why are you playing with my pants?" she asked. The puppy only wagged his tail. He didn't know what to say. The girl smiled. "That's okay. I understand," she said. "You can keep playing with it if you want to." The puppy jumped up with joy. He started to kick the pants around in circles. He was very happy and didn't want to stop. The girl laughed. She thought the puppy was very playful and adorable. Soon, they became good friends. The end.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. They had cars, dolls, blocks and books. They made a lot of noise and had a lot of fun. "Ben, look at my doll. She can sing and dance," Lily said, showing her doll to her brother. "Wow, she is pretty. But my car is faster and stronger. It can go up and down the stairs," Ben said, pushing his car along the floor. "Can I try your car?" Lily asked. "OK, but be careful. The stairs are steep and hard," Ben said. Lily took the car and ran to the stairs. She put the car on the first step and pushed it. The car rolled down the stairs and crashed into the wall. Lily gasped and ran after it. "Oh no, your car is broken. I'm sorry, Ben," Lily said, picking up the car. Ben followed her and saw the car. It had a dent and a scratch. He felt sad and angry. "That's not normal. You broke my car. You are a bad sister," Ben said, snatching the car from Lily. Lily felt sorry and sad. She started to cry. "I didn't mean to break it. I just wanted to play. You are a mean brother," Lily said, sobbing. Mom heard the noise and came to the stairs. She saw Ben and Lily crying and holding the car. "What happened here? Why are you crying?" Mom asked. "Lily broke my car. She pushed it down the stairs. It's not normal," Ben said. "I didn't mean to break it. I just wanted to play. Ben is mean," Lily said. Mom sighed and hugged them both. "I know you didn't mean to break it, Lily. And I know you are not mean, Ben. But you need to be careful with your toys and share them nicely. The car is not normal, but it can be fixed. How about we take a nap and then we can fix it together?" Mom said. Ben and Lily nodded and wiped their tears. They followed Mom to their bedroom and lay down on their beds. They closed their eyes and took a nap. They felt better and calmer. They dreamed of playing with their toys again.

One day, a long vine was hanging in the forest. A little monkey named Momo loved to play on the vine. He would swing back and forth, laughing and having fun. One day, Momo's friends came to watch him play on the vine. Momo wanted to show them a big jump. He swung very fast and jumped high in the air. But something unexpected happened. Momo's foot got stuck in the vine and he fell down. Momo felt embarrassed because all his friends saw him fall. But then, Momo's friends started to laugh and clap. They thought it was a funny trick! Momo smiled and realized that it was not bad to make his friends laugh. So, Momo kept playing and falling on the vine, making everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat named Tim. Tim lived in a dry, brown house made of sticks. He loved to play with his friends, the birds and the bugs. Every day, he would go out and have fun with them. One day, Tim needed to borrow a big cup from his friend, the bird. He wanted to fill the cup with water for his dry house. But the bird was not happy. He said, "No, you can't borrow my cup. It is mine." Tim felt sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He said to the bird, "If you let me borrow your cup, I will help you find food." The bird thought about it and finally said, "Okay, you can borrow my cup." Tim was so happy. He filled the cup with water and made his house nice and wet. After that, he helped the bird find food. They all played together and had fun. And from that day on, Tim and the bird were best friends.
