Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Crayon

Lily liked to draw with her red crayon. She drew flowers and hearts and stars on her paper. She was very happy with her work. She wanted to show her mom and dad. She ran to the kitchen with her paper in her hand. She saw mom and dad cooking. They looked busy. Lily wanted to get their attention. She said, "Look, look, I made this for you!" But mom and dad did not hear her. They were talking and chopping and stirring. Lily felt sad. She wanted them to see her work. She thought, maybe if I make a loud noise, they will look at me. She saw a big pot on the stove. It had soup in it. It smelled good. Lily reached for the pot. She wanted to drop it on the floor. She thought, that will make a loud noise. Then mom and dad will look at me. But Lily did not know that the pot was very hot. When she touched it, it burned her hand. She screamed and let go of the pot. The pot fell on the floor. The soup spilled everywhere. It made a big mess. Mom and dad heard Lily scream. They turned around and saw what happened. They ran to Lily. They were scared and angry. They said, "Lily, what did you do? You could have hurt yourself and us. Why did you drop the pot?" Lily cried and said, "I'm sorry, I'm sorry. I just wanted you to see my work. I drew this for you with my red crayon. But you did not look at me. You were busy." Mom and dad hugged Lily and said, "We're sorry, too. We should have looked at you. We know you worked hard on your drawing. It's very beautiful. Thank you for making it for us. But you should never touch the stove or the pots. They are very hot and dangerous. You could have burned yourself or us. You should always ask for help or wait for us to finish. That's the safe and polite thing to do." Lily nodded and said, "I understand. I'm sorry I made a mess. I won't touch the stove or the pots again. I love you, mom and dad." Mom and dad said, "We love you, too, Lily. And we love your work. Let's clean up the mess together. And then we can hang your drawing on the fridge. How does that sound?" Lily smiled and said, "That sounds good. Thank you, mom and dad." The end.

Lila liked to dream. Every night, she closed her eyes and saw many things. She saw animals, flowers, stars, and colors. She heard music, laughter, and stories. She felt happy and free. But Lila was deaf. She could not hear anything in the real world. She could not hear her mom and dad, her brother and sister, or her friends. She could not hear the birds, the wind, or the rain. She felt sad and alone. One day, Lila went to the forest with her family. They wanted to have a picnic and play. Lila loved the forest. She saw many trees, leaves, and mushrooms. She smelled the fresh air and the flowers. She touched the bark, the grass, and the rocks. But Lila could not hear the forest. She could not hear the squirrels, the bees, or the stream. She could not hear her family calling her name, or the car horn beeping. She wandered away from them, and got lost. Lila was scared. She did not know where to go. She cried and shouted, but no one heard her. She wished she could hear, or that someone could hear her. Then, she saw a light. It was a fairy. The fairy was small and bright, and had wings. The fairy flew to Lila, and smiled. She touched Lila's ear, and whispered. Lila gasped. She heard the fairy. She heard a soft, sweet voice. She heard a word. "Hello." Lila smiled. She said the word back. "Hello." The fairy took Lila's hand, and led her to her family. They were happy to see her, and hugged her. They asked her what happened, and how she found them. Lila pointed to the fairy, and said the word again. "Hello." They looked at the fairy, and smiled. They thanked her, and said hello. The fairy nodded, and flew away. Lila felt happy and free. She could hear the forest. She could hear her family. She could hear her dream.
