Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a quiet garden, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play and laugh with her friends. One day, while playing, Lily saw a big red strawberry. She wanted to eat the yummy strawberry, but it was very high up on a tall plant. Lily had an idea. She would ask her friend, the strong ant, to help her. The ant came and used his strong legs to lift Lily up, up, up! Now, Lily could reach the big red strawberry. Together, they picked the strawberry and shared it with all their friends. It was the best strawberry they ever ate. And from that day on, Lily and the ant were the best of friends. They played and laughed in the quiet garden, always helping each other.

Lila and Ben were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw some fish and crabs and shells. They were having fun. "Look, Lila, a shark!" Ben shouted, pointing to a big gray shape in the water. He was scared and ran to his mom. Lila was curious and brave. She wanted to see the shark. She walked closer to the water and looked. The shark was not very big. It had a long nose and small eyes. It was swimming slowly. "Hello, shark," Lila said. "Do you want to play with me?" The shark heard Lila and turned to her. It was a humble shark. It did not want to hurt anyone. It was lonely and bored. "Hello, little girl," the shark said. "I do want to play with you. But I can't come to the sand. And you can't come to the water. We are too different." Lila felt sad. She liked the shark. She wanted to be its friend. "Maybe we can try to play together," Lila said. "We can talk and sing and tell stories. We can share our toys and snacks. We can learn from each other. We can be different and still be friends." The shark smiled. It liked Lila. It wanted to be her friend. "OK, let's try to play together," the shark said. "You are a kind and smart little girl. I am a humble and gentle shark. We can be different and still be friends." Lila and the shark played together for a long time. They talked and sang and told stories. They shared their toys and snacks. They learned from each other. They were different and still friends. Ben and their mom and dad watched them from the sand. They were surprised and happy. They learned something too. They learned that being different is not bad. They learned that being humble is good. They learned that being friends is wonderful.

Once upon a time, there was a man walking in the woods. Suddenly, he heard someone crying! He followed the sound and came to a big pond. He saw a little girl stuck in the mud. The man was brave and went to rescue the girl. He put his hand out and carefully pulled her to safety. The girl said, "Thank you! I was stuck and so scared". The man looked happy and said, "You're welcome, I'm glad I could help". Soon, a bossy lady came running with a towel. She said, "You were saved by a real hero!" She hugged the girl, who smiled. The man said, "I'm glad I could help. Take care of her, bossy lady!" Then, he waved goodbye and walked away. The girl was so thankful for being rescued. She said, "Thank you, Mister! You're my hero".

Once upon a time there was a weak little girl. Her name was Sue. Sue wanted to have some fun, so she asked her mom for help. Mom said, "Let's go to the park. That will be so much fun." So they went to the park. Sue laughed and played in the sunshine. She enjoyed all the fun the park had to offer. Then, all of a sudden, Sue started to feel weak. Her mom asked her if she was ok. Sue said "Yes, I am ok." But then, Sue collapsed to the ground. She didn't receive help in time and died. The end.
