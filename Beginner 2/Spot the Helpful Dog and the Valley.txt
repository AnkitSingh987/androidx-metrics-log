Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Helpful Dog and the Valley

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot lived in a valley with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Sue found a big sack in the valley. She called out to Spot, "Look, I found a sack!" Spot ran over to see the sack. They opened it and found many toys inside. Sue and Spot were very happy. Sue and Spot spent the whole day playing with the toys from the sack. They shared the toys with their friends in the valley. Everyone had a great time. At the end of the day, Sue and Spot put the toys back in the sack and went home together. They knew they could always find fun in their valley.

The sun was shining bright and it was summertime. It was so hot in the sun that the little girl wanted a cool spot to play in. She went to the park and looked around for the perfect spot. There was a purple tree. She liked the color purple, so decided to play under the tree. She sat down and looked all around the park. She could see a lot of different colors in the sky, the grass and the trees. The little girl liked the colors and wished for even more colors. She made a wish and then thanked the summer sky. She said, "Please make my wish come true!" Just then, the sky turned a bright shade of purple. She smiled and said, "Thank you summer sky! You made my wish come true and now I can enjoy the pretty purple sky."

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, round peach. The peach was ready to eat. Tim was very happy. He loved peaches so much. Tim saw a big slide in the park. He wanted to slide down with his peach. He climbed up, up, up to the top of the slide. He held his peach tight and got ready to slide. Tim and the peach went down the slide very fast. Whee! They reached the bottom and Tim laughed. He was so happy. He ate his peach and it was the best peach ever.

One day, in a modern house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. He had a big box of toys, and his favorite toy was a shiny screw. He played with the screw all day. One morning, Tim woke up and could not find his screw. He looked under the bed and in his toy box, but it was not there. Tim was sad, and he asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my screw?" His mom smiled and said, "I think I know where it is. Let's look outside." They went to the backyard and found the screw on the grass. Tim was happy and thanked his mom. From that day on, Tim always remembered to put his screw back in the toy box before going to bed. He played with his screw every day, and he and his mom had many fun days together in their modern house.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Max. Max loved to try new things. One day, he found a gum on the ground. Max thought, "I want to try this gum. It looks fun." So, Max picked up the gum with his mouth and started to chew it. Max met his friend, a little bird named Sky. Sky saw Max chewing the gum and asked, "What is that, Max?" Max told Sky it was gum and that he was trying it. Sky wanted to try the gum too, but Max said, "No, Sky. It's not good for you." Later, Max felt something strange. The gum was stuck in his fur! Max tried to get it out, but it was too hard. Sky saw Max and said, "I can help you, Max." Sky used her beak to get the gum out of Max's fur. Max was happy and thanked Sky. The moral of the story is to always be careful when trying new things and to listen to your friends' advice. Max learned that gum was not good for him, and he was lucky to have a friend like Sky to help him when he needed it.
