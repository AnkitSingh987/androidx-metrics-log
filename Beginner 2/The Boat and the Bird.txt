Category: Beginner
Title: The Boat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat loved to sail on the water. One day, the boat met a silly hanger. The hanger was sad because it could not sail. "Can you help me sail?" asked the hanger. The boat said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's sail together!" So, the boat and the hanger went to the water to sail. They were very happy to be friends. As they sailed, the wind blew hard. The boat and the hanger went very fast. Suddenly, the hanger flew into the air like a bird! The boat and the hanger were surprised. Now, the hanger could sail and fly too. They laughed and played all day long.

Tim and Lily are friends. They like to play in the field near their house. The field has many flowers and bugs and birds. Tim and Lily run and jump and laugh in the field. One day, they see a big red ball in the field. It is shiny and round and bouncy. Tim and Lily want to play with the ball. They run to the ball and touch it. It feels soft and smooth. "Let's zip with the ball!" Tim says. He means to roll and bounce with the ball. He thinks zip is a fun word. "Okay!" Lily says. She likes to zip too. She thinks zip is a silly word. They take turns to zip with the ball. They push the ball and jump on it and roll with it. They have a lot of fun. They zip so fast and so far that they do not see the fence. The fence is around the field. It is to keep the cows in. The cows are big and brown and moo. They eat the grass and the flowers. They do not like the ball. The ball zips over the fence and into the cows. The cows see the ball and get scared. They think the ball is a monster. They run and moo and kick. They are crazy. Tim and Lily hear the cows and stop zipping. They look at the fence and see the ball and the cows. They are scared too. They think the cows are angry. "Uh-oh!" Tim says. He wants the ball back. "Uh-oh!" Lily says. She wants to go home. They run away from the fence and the cows and the ball. They run to their house and tell their mom and dad what happened. Their mom and dad hug them and say it is okay. They say they will get the ball back later. They say they are glad Tim and Lily are safe. Tim and Lily are glad too. They say they are sorry for zipping with the ball. They say they will not zip in the field again. They say they will zip in the yard instead. They say they love their mom and dad. The end.

Once there was a graceful girl. Her name was Zola. Zola lived in a village by the sea. Every day she went to the beach to have fun. One day at the beach, Zola saw a big barrel. It was made of wood and had a curious thing in it – a rope. Zola thought it looked like a swing! So, with a big smile, she leaned into the barrel and grabbed onto the rope. With one big push, she was flying around and around and around. She never felt so free! "Look at me, mama!" Zola shouted to her mother, who was standing nearby. "I'm so graceful!" Mama smiled and said, "You sure are, Zola. You sure are."

One day, a big, ugly bug went to the park. The bug was very hungry. He saw a nice boy named Tim. Tim was holding a big, juicy steak. The ugly bug wanted to eat the steak. "Hi, Tim!" said the ugly bug. "Can I have some of your steak? I am very hungry." Tim looked at the bug and thought for a moment. "Okay, bug," Tim said. "You can have some of my steak. But first, you must hold my hand and walk with me in the park." The ugly bug agreed. He held Tim's hand, and they walked together in the park. They saw pretty flowers and played with other kids. The ugly bug was happy. After they walked, Tim gave the ugly bug a piece of his steak. The bug ate it and felt full. Tim and the bug became good friends. They played together every day at the park, and the ugly bug was not hungry anymore.
