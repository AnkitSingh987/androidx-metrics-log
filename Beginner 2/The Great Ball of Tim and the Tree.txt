Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, at noon, he went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with lots of green leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said, "No, it's too high." Then, Tim saw a man with a big box. The man was selling cheap toys. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have one, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can pick one toy." Tim was very happy. He looked at all the toys and saw a small, red ball. He liked it very much. Tim's mom gave the man some money and the man gave Tim the ball. Tim played with his new ball all day, and when it was time to go home, he said, "Thank you, Mom, for the best day ever!"

Once upon a time, there was a deaf pick who wanted to cook. But she didn't know how. One day, an old lady saw her and said, "What do you want to do?" The deaf pick replied, "I want to cook, but I don't know how." The old lady said, "I will teach you how to cook. First, you must go to the store and buy some ingredients. Then, you must follow my instructions." The deaf pick nodded and excitedly went to the store. She bought the ingredients and returned home to start cooking. With the old lady's help, she made a delicious meal and everyone enjoyed it. The deaf pick was so happy that she knew how to cook and she thanked the old lady. She was now able to cook whenever she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree in a park. Many kids loved to play there. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a cold and icy day, but Tim was excited to play. Tim saw a swing hanging from the oak tree. He ran to the swing and asked his mom to help him sit on it. His mom held his hand and helped him sit on the swing. Tim was very happy. Tim's mom pushed him on the swing. He went up and down, up and down. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He loved the swing under the big oak tree. After a while, they went home, and Tim couldn't wait to come back to the park again.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lucy. She had beautiful curly hair and loved to play and explore. Yesterday, Lucy made a new friend. Her friend's name was Joe. Lucy was so excited when she saw Joe and quickly introduced herself. Joe smiled and said his name was Joe. "It's nice to meet you," Lucy said. Joe nodded his head and said, "It's nice to meet you too." Lucy smiled and said, "Do you want to be friends?" "Yes," Joe said, with a big grin. From that day forward, Lucy and Joe were best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. The end!

Lily and Max were playing with pennies on the floor. They liked to spin them and see how long they would go. Lily had a lovely penny that was shiny and new. Max had an old penny that was dull and dirty. "Can I spin your penny?" Max asked Lily. "No, it's mine!" Lily said. "You have your own penny." "But yours is better!" Max said. He tried to grab Lily's penny, but she held it tight. "Let go!" Lily shouted. "It's not fair!" They pulled and tugged on the penny, but neither of them would give up. The penny slipped from their fingers and rolled away under the sofa. "Oh no!" Lily and Max cried. They ran to the sofa and looked for the penny, but they could not see it. It was too dark and too far. They felt sad and sorry. They had lost their penny and their fun. They looked at each other and said, "I'm sorry." They hugged and made up. They decided to share their pennies and their spins. They found another penny in the jar and took turns to spin it. They laughed and smiled. They had a lovely time.
