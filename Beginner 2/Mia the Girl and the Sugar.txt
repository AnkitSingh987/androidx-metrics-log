Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Sugar

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat sugar. One day, when she was playing in her room, she found an ancient box. The box was very old and seemed special. Mia opened the box and saw lots of sugar. She was so happy! She started to eat the sugar, but then something unexpected happened. The sugar turned into tiny bugs! Mia was scared and didn't know what to do. The tiny bugs started to dance and sing. They were friendly bugs! Mia laughed and danced with them. They played all day long. From that day on, Mia had new friends and didn't want to eat sugar anymore.

Once upon a time there was a purple tire. It was old and tired and wanted to rest. One day it started rolling down the street all by itself. The tire rolled and rolled until it came to a stop in front of a bright blue swimming pool. It jumped in and started to splash around. The tire had never had so much fun! When it got tired, it rolled out of the pool and went back home. From that day on, the purple tire enjoyed playing in the pool every day. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a baby named Tim. Tim had a toy that looked like a baby too, but it was a fake baby. He liked to play with his fake baby all day long. They would laugh and have fun together. One day, Tim and his fake baby were playing near a big tree. They saw a little bird trying to fly. The bird was not very good at flying yet. Tim wanted to help the bird, but he didn't know how. Then, Tim had an idea. He could use his fake baby to prevent the bird from falling. He put the fake baby under the tree. When the bird tried to fly again, it fell on the soft fake baby. The bird was safe and not hurt. Tim was happy he could help the bird with his fake baby. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a cool little bug. The bug had a big horn on its head. It lived in a green park with many trees. One sunny day, the bug went to the park to play. It saw a swing hanging from a tree. The bug wanted to try the swing. It climbed up the tree and sat on the swing. The bug held on tight and began to swing. It swung up and down, back and forth. The bug was so happy. It felt like it could touch the sky. The bug played on the swing all day, and then went back to its home, tired but happy.

One day, a cow named Betsy lived on a farm. Betsy was not a pretty cow. Some animals said she was ugly. Betsy was sad, but she had a big heart. Betsy had a friend, a little bird named Tim. Tim told Betsy, "Show them you are nice, even if they say you are ugly." Betsy thought that was a good idea. She helped the other animals on the farm. She gave them food and water. She was a kind cow. One day, a big storm came. The wind was strong, and the animals were scared. Betsy was brave. She helped all the animals find a safe place. After the storm was over, the animals said, "Thank you, Betsy! You are a good friend!" Betsy was happy. She knew that being nice is more important than being pretty.

Tom and Lily were playing in the sand at the park. They liked to make castles and hills and dig holes. They had a bucket and a shovel and some toys. They saw a big brown dog running towards them. The dog looked angry and hungry. It had sharp teeth and a loud bark. Tom and Lily were scared. They tried to run away, but the dog was faster. "Help! Help!" they cried. "Go away, dog! Leave us alone!" But the dog did not listen. It jumped on them and knocked them down. It bit their arms and legs and made them bleed. It tore their clothes and their toys. It roared like a lion and made a big mess. No one heard their cries. No one came to help them. The dog hurt them very badly. They wished they had stayed at home. They wished they had a nice dog, not a mean one. They wished they could play in the sand again. But they could not. The end.
