Category: Beginner
Title: The Long Tree and the Cat

One day, a little cat saw a long tree. The cat wanted to climb the tree. The tree had a big triangle at the top. The cat thought it was fun to go up high. But the cat was scared. The tree was very tall. The cat did not know if it could climb all the way up. The cat took a deep breath and started to climb. It was hard, but the cat kept going. Finally, the cat reached the triangle at the top. It was so happy! The cat looked down and saw its friends. They were proud of the cat. The cat learned that it was brave and strong. The cat knew that if it tried hard, it could do anything.

One day, a boy and a girl went to play. They saw a pale dog near a big tree. The dog had a cut on its leg. The boy and the girl wanted to help the dog. "Let's find something to help the dog," said the boy. They looked for a big leaf to put on the cut. They found one and put it on the dog's leg. The dog seemed happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the leaf away. The dog's cut was not better. The boy and the girl were sad. The dog went away, and they could not help it. The day had a bad end.

One day, a wild dog saw a grill. The grill was hot and had good food on it. The dog was hungry and wanted to eat the food. He went close to the grill and said, "Yum, I want to eat this!" A nice man was near the grill. He saw the wild dog and said, "Hey dog, do not eat the food on the grill. It is too hot for you. I will pass you some food that is not hot." The wild dog was happy and waited for the food. The nice man passed the dog a big, tasty bone. The wild dog was very happy and said, "Thank you, nice man!" The dog took the bone and ran away. But then, something unexpected happened. The wild dog came back with more wild dogs, and they all wanted food from the nice man. The nice man was surprised but he smiled and passed food to all the wild dogs. They all ate and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big sister and a little sister. The big sister was very proud. She loved to help and serve her family. One day, the big sister said, "I will make a meal for everyone!" The little sister wanted to help too. She asked, "Can I help you, please?" The big sister said, "Yes, you can help me serve the food." They worked together to make a yummy meal. The family was happy and ready to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the house! The bird was hungry and wanted some food too. The big sister and the little sister looked at each other. They decided to serve some food to the bird too. The bird was very happy and ate the food. Then, the bird sang a beautiful song for everyone. The family was surprised and happy. They all clapped and laughed. The big sister and the little sister were very proud. They learned that sharing and helping others can bring joy to everyone.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big black bird on a tree. It was a raven. Anna wanted to look at it closer. She said to Ben, "Let's go and see the raven. Maybe it has something shiny in its beak." Ben was scared of the raven. He said to Anna, "No, let's not go. The raven is mean and ugly. It makes loud noises and eats disgusting things. It might bite us or scratch us with its claws." Anna did not listen to Ben. She said, "You are silly. The raven is not mean or ugly. It is smart and beautiful. It has shiny feathers and a sharp beak. It can fly and sing. I want to be its friend." Anna ran to the tree and climbed up. She reached out her hand to touch the raven. The raven did not like Anna. It did not want to be her friend. It snapped at her hand and made a loud noise. Anna was scared and hurt. She cried and fell down from the tree. Ben ran to Anna and helped her. He said, "Are you okay, Anna? I told you not to go to the raven. The raven is not nice. It is mean and ugly. It does not like us. We should stay away from it." Anna said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You were right. The raven is mean and ugly. It does not like us. It bit me and made me cry. It is not smart and beautiful. It is disgusting. I don't want to be its friend anymore." Ben hugged Anna and said, "It's okay, Anna. I'm glad you are okay. You are my friend. I like you. You are nice and pretty. You are smart and brave. You can play with me and have fun. We don't need the raven." Anna smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are my friend. I like you. You are nice and handsome. You are smart and kind. You can play with me and have fun. We don't need the raven." They went back to their toys and played happily. They learned a lesson. They learned to listen to each other and to be careful of strangers. They learned to appreciate each other and to be happy with what they have. They learned to avoid the raven and to enjoy the park.
