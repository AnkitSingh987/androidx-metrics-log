Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a plan to go to school for the very first time. Sue was so excited, she could not wait to make new friends and learn new things. On her first day of school, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he fell down and got hurt. Sue wanted to help Tim feel better, so she shared her lunch with him. They both smiled and became good friends. One day, while playing, Sue and Tim found a magic box. They opened the box and a big, happy bird came out. The bird could talk and told them that they could have one wish. Sue and Tim thought about what they really wanted. They decided to wish for everyone in their school to be kind and helpful. The magic bird granted their wish, and from that day on, all the kids in the school were kind and helpful to each other. The moral of the story is that it is important to be kind and help others when they are hurt or sad.

One day, a bossy fox went to the park. He saw a little bird on a swing. The fox wanted to play on the swing too. He said to the bird, "Get off the swing! I want to play on it now!" The little bird was sad but got off the swing. The fox started to play on the swing, but then he saw a big bear coming. The bear wanted to play on the swing too. He said to the fox, "Get off the swing! I want to play on it now!" The bossy fox was scared of the big bear. He did not allow the bear to play on the swing. The bear was sad, but then something unexpected happened. The little bird had an idea. She said, "Why don't we all play together? We can take turns on the swing." The fox and the bear liked the idea. They all played together and had a lot of fun. The bossy fox learned to share and be nice to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big dragon. The dragon was very patient. He lived in a cave near a small town. The people in the town were scared of the dragon. They wanted to separate from the dragon. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the dragon's cave. She was brave. She said, "Mr. Dragon, can we be friends?" The dragon looked at her and said, "Yes, I want to be friends too. I am lonely." The girl and the dragon played together. They had fun. The people in the town saw them and were surprised. They did not know that the dragon was patient and kind. Now, the people in the town were not scared anymore. They all lived happily together with the dragon.

One day, a boy named Tim went to school. He had a math class. Tim did not like math. His teacher, Miss Sue, was nice. She would always encourage Tim to try his best. She said, "You can do it, Tim!" One day, Tim saw an expensive toy in a shop. It was a big, shiny car. Tim wanted the car so much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the car?" His mom said, "If you do well in math, I will buy it for you." Tim tried very hard to learn math. He wanted the car. Miss Sue helped him a lot. She encouraged him to keep going. Tim got better at math. His mom was happy. She said, "I will buy the car for you now." But when they went to the shop, the car was gone. Tim was sad. His mom said, "I have a surprise for you." She took Tim to another shop. There, he saw the same car. But this time, it was not expensive. It was on sale! Tim was so happy. He got the car and was good at math too.

One day, a restless dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big can. The can had oil in it. Spot was very curious about the can. He went closer to look at it. Spot thought, "What if I shake the can?" So, he used his strong paw to shake the can. The oil spilled out and made the ground very slippery. Spot slipped and fell down. He was surprised! Just then, a kind cat named Kitty saw what happened. She walked up to Spot and said, "Are you okay?" Spot said, "Yes, I am fine. I did not know the oil would make the ground slippery." Kitty helped Spot get up, and they both laughed. From that day on, Spot and Kitty became best friends, and they learned to be more careful with things they find.
