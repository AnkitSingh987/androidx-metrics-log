Category: Beginner
Title: The Shiny Stone and the Bird

One day, a pale bird saw a shiny stone. The bird liked the stone very much. It wanted to take the stone to its nest. But the stone was in a little girl's hand. The bird flew down and said, "Can I have your stone? It is so pretty!" The little girl said, "No, this is my stone. I found it in the park." The bird was sad, but it really wanted the stone. So, when the girl was not looking, the bird tried to steal the stone. But as the bird took the stone, it changed! The stone was not shiny anymore. It was just a normal stone. The bird was surprised. The little girl looked at the bird and said, "You can have that stone. I have many more shiny stones at home." The bird learned that stealing was not good and became friends with the little girl. They played together and shared the shiny stones.

Tom and Anna like to read stories. They have many books with pictures and words. They have a big shelf for their books. But one day, they cannot find their favorite book. It is a book about a dog and a cat who are friends. "Where is our book?" Tom asks Anna. "Did we lose it?" "I don't know," Anna says. "Maybe we left it in the living room." They go to the living room and look for their book. They look under the sofa, behind the curtains, and on the table. But they cannot find their book. "Maybe we left it in the kitchen," Tom says. They go to the kitchen and look for their book. They look in the drawers, on the chairs, and in the trash. But they cannot find their book. "Maybe we left it in the bedroom," Anna says. They go to the bedroom and look for their book. They look in the closet, under the bed, and on the dresser. But they cannot find their book. They are very sad. They want to read their book. They sit on the floor and cry. "Hey, what's wrong?" Mom says. She comes to the bedroom and sees Tom and Anna crying. "We lost our book," Tom says. "The book about the dog and the cat." "We looked everywhere," Anna says. "But we cannot find it." Mom hugs them and says, "Don't cry. I know where your book is. I saw it in the car this morning. You took it with you when we went to the park. I put it in the glove box for safekeeping. Come on, let's go get it." Tom and Anna stop crying and smile. They follow Mom to the car. Mom opens the glove box and takes out their book. She gives it to Tom and Anna. "Here is your book," Mom says. "I'm sorry you lost it. But I'm glad you found it." "Thank you, Mom," Tom and Anna say. They hug Mom and take their book. They go back to the house and sit on the couch. They open their book and read their story. They read it loud and happy. They love their book. They love their story. They love their Mom.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to play outside. She liked to wander in the big park near her house. The park was full of trees, grass, and many small animals. Mia loved to look at the animals and listen to the birds. Today, the park was very noisy because the birds were singing loudly. As Mia walked, she found a small twig on the ground. She picked it up and started to play with it. She used the twig to draw pictures in the dirt. She drew a sun, a house, and a big smiley face. Mia was having a lot of fun. Just then, a little boy named Tom came to the park. He saw Mia playing with the twig and asked, "Can I play with you?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They took turns drawing pictures with the twig and listening to the noisy birds. They had a great time playing in the park until it was time to go home.
