Category: Beginner
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very alert. He liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim was playing with his ball and it went far away. He ran to get the ball. Tim's mom told him, "Be careful and watch where you go." Tim was a good boy and he always listened to his mom. But this time, he did not see a big rock on the ground. He fell down and felt a lot of pain in his leg. Tim's mom saw him fall and ran to help him. She hugged him and said, "I will not allow you to play near the rocks again." Tim cried a little but he knew his mom was right. From that day on, Tim was more alert when playing and always stayed away from the rocks.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small house made of cheese. He loved his cheese house very much. Every day, he would play outside with his friends and then eat some cheese. One day, while Tim was playing, he met a big cat. The cat looked at Tim and said, "Hi! I'm Cat. Do you want to play with me?" Tim was a little scared, but he thought Cat seemed friendly. So, he said, "Yes, let's play!" Tim and Cat played all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Cat, you are very nice. Do you want to come to my house and eat some cheese?" Cat smiled and said, "I would love that!" They went to Tim's cheese house and shared a big piece of cheese. They were very happy and became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Cat played together every day and always shared their cheese.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue had an interesting toy. The toy was a soft ball. Sue loved to squeeze the ball. One day, Sue found a big box in her room. She thought it was a gift. Sue opened the box and found many balls inside. She was so happy. She started to squeeze each ball. But one ball felt different. When Sue squeezed it, the ball talked! It said, "I can tell you the truth about anything you want to know." Sue was surprised. She asked the ball about her mom's secret cake recipe. The ball told her the truth. Sue was so happy with her talking ball. She never thought that squeezing a ball would change her life. And that was the most interesting and unexpected thing that ever happened to her.

Once upon a time, there was a big pot. The pot was very flexible. It could bend and twist in many ways. One day, the pot met a friend. The friend was a small spoon. The spoon said, "Let's play a game!" The pot agreed. They decided to play a spinning game. They took turns to spin around and around. The pot spun fast and the spoon spun slow. They both had lots of fun. After a while, they got tired from spinning. They sat down and laughed together. The pot and the spoon became best friends. From that day on, they always played together and had so much fun.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with toys and books. But they also like to eat pizza. Pizza is yummy and cheesy. One day, Tom and Mia go to Mia's house. Mia's mom says, "You can play in the living room, but first you have to clean your hands. Pizza is coming soon." Tom and Mia say, "Okay, mom. We will clean our hands." They go to the bathroom and turn on the water. They rub their hands with soap and rinse them. They dry their hands with a towel. They are ready for pizza. They run to the living room and see a big box on the table. It is pizza. Mia's mom says, "Here you go, kids. Enjoy your pizza. But be careful, it is hot." Tom and Mia say, "Thank you, mom. We love pizza." They open the box and see cheese, pepperoni, and olives on the pizza. They each take a slice and bite it. It is hot and delicious. They say, "Mmm, this pizza is great. We are so happy." They eat and eat until they are full. They have some juice to drink. They are very polite and say, "Please" and "Thank you". They are good friends. They finish their pizza and put their plates in the sink. They say, "Mom, can we play now?" Mia's mom says, "Yes, you can. But remember to clean your toys when you are done." Tom and Mia say, "Okay, mom. We will clean our toys." They go back to the living room and play with toys and books. They have fun and laugh. They share and take turns. They are nice to each other. They play until it is time to go home. They clean their toys and put them away. They say, "Bye, mom. Thank you for the pizza. It was great." Mia's mom says, "You are welcome, kids. You are very sweet. Come back soon." Tom and Mia hug and say, "Bye, friend. See you tomorrow." They walk to the door and wave. They are happy. They had a great day.
