Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ghost

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big rock. The rock was in an organized row with other rocks. Tim liked the rocks and played with them. He moved them and made a new row. As Tim played, he heard a voice. It was a ghost! The ghost said, "Hello! I like your rocks. Can I play too?" Tim was not scared. He said, "Yes, you can play with me." So Tim and the ghost played with the rocks. They made new rows and shapes. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, ghost! Let's play again soon." The ghost smiled and said, "Yes, let's play again. Bye, Tim!"

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot had a friend named Tim. They liked to play with a shiny coin. One day, they found a big, sharp rock. Spot said, "Be careful, Tim! The rock is sharp. It can hurt you." Tim nodded and said, "Thank you, Spot. Let's play with the coin instead." They played with the coin all day. They threw it and caught it. Spot and Tim were happy. They knew that they were safe because they stayed away from the sharp rock. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very excited. She was going to the park with her mom, and she couldn't wait! When they arrived at the park, the little girl ran to the playground. She saw a big hoop on the ground and shouted "Mom, what is it?" Her mom replied, "That is a hoop, sweetie. Don't you want to play with it?" The little girl smiled and said yes. She lay down next to the hoop and put her ear to it. To her delight, it made a soft and comforting sound like a whale in the sea. The little girl lay there for a few minutes and listened to the hoop, while her mom watched. Then the little girl got up and said, "Let's go, Mom. I know what I will do with the hoop later." The little girl took the hoop home and that night, she and her mom made a tent in their living room. They used the hoop to hold up the blanket and the little girl was so thrilled. The next day, the little girl and her mom went back to the park. The little girl had brought the hoop with her and happily set it up in the playground. She continued to lay down and listen to the soothing sound it made and then she ran and jumped through it, just like she had imagined!

Grandma was brave. One day she found a little puppy crying. She knew it was suffering and she wanted to help it. Grandma said, "Don't worry puppy, I'll take care of you." She looked the puppy in the eyes and gave it a gentle wink. The puppy was so happy and it followed Grandma home. She gave the puppy food and drink and a warm bed of blankets to rest. Grandma was really brave to take the puppy in and help it. The puppy was so thankful and each time it saw Grandma, it gave her a big wink. That was their special way of saying thank you. Grandma would give the puppy a gentle smile and a pat on the head. She was always happy to see the puppy and what a brave dog it had become!

Once upon a time there was a brave rabbit called Sammy. He was so brave that he was not scared of anything. One day, Sammy went to the park. Suddenly, he heard a threat. It was a tall badger who was trying to scare Sammy away. He shouted, "You small bunny! Leave this park now!" But Sammy was brave, so he didn't run away. He stood tall and said, "No! I won't leave!" The badger laughed, but then he saw that Sammy meant it. He said, "Fine, I will let you stay then." And so, the brave rabbit Sammy stayed in the park and played all day. He was so proud of being brave and standing up to the badger.
