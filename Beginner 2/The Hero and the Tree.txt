Category: Beginner
Title: The Hero and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a bright, sunny land, there lived a hero. The hero had a big, red cape and a strong, happy smile. He liked to help people and make them feel good. One day, the hero met a little girl. The little girl was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the hero. The little girl said, "My toy is stuck in the tree. Can you help me?" The hero looked up and saw the toy. He reached up and got the toy for the little girl. She was very happy. Later, the hero met a little boy. The little boy wanted to play with the hero, but the hero wanted to help more people. The little boy and the hero disagreed. The hero said, "I need to help others, but I can play with you after." The little boy agreed, and they both smiled.

One day, Tim saw a big, noisy truck. The truck went "beep, beep!" as it drove down the road. Tim liked the truck and wanted to see it up close. He ran after the truck, trying to catch it. Tim's friend, Sam, saw him running. "Tim, where are you going?" Sam asked. "I want to see the noisy truck!" Tim said. Sam decided to run with Tim. They both chased the truck, laughing and having fun. As they got closer, the truck stopped at a red light. Tim and Sam blinked their eyes in surprise. They finally caught up to the truck! They looked at the big, noisy truck and smiled. The truck driver saw them and waved. Tim and Sam waved back, happy to have seen the truck up close.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. Everyone was happy and excited. The festival had lots of food, games, and fun. People danced and sang with joy. A hungry little boy named Tim saw a sign for the festival. He went to the festival to find some food. He smelled yummy treats and saw colorful balloons. Tim wanted to join the fun. Tim met a nice lady named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, are you hungry?" Tim nodded his head. Sue gave Tim a big sandwich to eat. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you!" They both enjoyed the festival together.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. "Look, our snowman is so happy!" Lily says. "Yes, he is! But he is also cold. Let's go inside and have some hot cocoa," Ben says. They start to walk back to their house. But Lily slips on the ice and falls down. She hurts her knee and cries. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts!" Lily says. Ben runs to help her. He holds her hand and helps her get up. "Don't cry, Lily. You are brave. You can stand on your leg. See, it's not so bad," Ben says. He encourages her to walk with him. He hugs her and says, "I love you, Lily. You are my best friend." Lily smiles and says, "I love you too, Ben. You are my best friend. Thank you for helping me." They go inside and have some hot cocoa. Their mom kisses their cheeks and says, "You are such good twins. I'm proud of you."

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Mia. Mia loved to pretend and play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a cookie jar full of pretend cookies. One sunny day, Mia took her cookie jar outside to play. She pretended to bake cookies with her friends, Tom and Lily. They had so much fun making pretend cookies in many shapes and sizes. After they made all the pretend cookies, they shared them with their other friends. Everyone loved the pretend cookies and said they were very nice. Mia, Tom, and Lily were so happy that they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dull statue in the park. The statue looked very sad. It was next to a pond where many ducks liked to swim. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw the ducks swimming in the pond. "Quack, quack!" said the ducks. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. She wanted to talk to the ducks. Then, Lily saw the sad statue. "Why are you sad?" she asked the statue. But the statue did not answer. Lily thought the statue wanted to swim like the ducks. Lily had an idea. She ran to her mom and dad. "Mom, Dad, can we make the statue happy?" she asked. They all thought for a moment. Then, they went to the store and bought a big, blue balloon. They tied the balloon to the statue's hand. The balloon looked like it was swimming in the sky. The statue seemed happy now. Lily, her mom, and her dad smiled and played in the park all day.
