Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a big toy truck. Tim wanted to play with the truck too, but Sam said it was difficult to make the truck go in motion. Tim asked Sam, "Can I try to play with your truck?" Sam said, "Okay, but you must promise to be careful." Tim promised to be careful and tried to make the truck go in motion. At first, it was difficult, but Tim kept trying. After a while, Tim learned how to make the truck go in motion. Both friends were happy and played together with the truck and the car. They had a fun day and promised to always share their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing all day long. One day, Blue saw a big tree and thought it would be a good place to sing. The tree had a hole that was just the right fit for Blue. He was very happy. The next day, Blue went to the tree to sing again. But this time, he saw a snake with shiny skin inside the hole. Blue was scared but wanted to sing in his tree. So, he thought of a plan to make the snake go away. Blue found a big stick and pushed it into the hole. The snake with shiny skin didn't like it and left the tree. Blue was happy and could sing in his tree again. From that day on, Blue always checked the hole before he sang.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. One day, they both want to play with the big truck. Tom grabs the truck and runs away. Lily chases him and tries to get the truck back. They run around the room and bump into the table. The table has a lamp on it. The lamp falls down and breaks. The lamp makes a loud noise and a bright flash. Tom and Lily are scared. They drop the truck and start to cry. Mom hears the noise and comes to the room. She sees the broken lamp and the crying twins. She is angry and sad. She tells Tom and Lily to sit on the bed and stay there. She goes to get a broom and a dustpan to clean up the mess. Tom and Lily feel sorry for breaking the lamp. They hug each other and say sorry. They are also sleepy from running and crying. They lie down on the bed and close their eyes. They fall asleep and dream of nice things. Mom comes back and sees the sleeping twins. She is not angry anymore. She is glad they are safe and sorry. She covers them with a blanket and kisses them. She turns off the light and leaves the room. She hopes they will learn to share and be careful. She loves them very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big pine tree. He loved to fly and sing all day long. One day, Tim saw a big cat. The cat was very alert and watched Tim. Tim was scared, so he flew back to his pine tree. He stayed there and did not sing. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She told him, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will help you." Tim's mom went to feed the cat. The cat was happy and full. Now, the cat did not want to catch Tim. Tim was happy again and sang his songs.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, when the sun went to sleep, he would hide under his bed. He was scared of the shadows that danced on his walls. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam knew that Tim was scared of the dark. So, he had a plan to help him. "Let's play a game," said Sam. "We will go outside when it gets dark and I will show you something." That night, Tim and Sam went outside. Tim was scared, but he held Sam's hand. Sam took Tim to a big tree and said, "Look up!" Tim looked up and saw the shining moon and stars. They were beautiful! Sam revealed that the dark could be fun too. From that day on, Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. He loved to look at the moon and stars with Sam. They would go out every night and play under the big tree. The village was happy that Tim was not scared anymore. And they all lived happily ever after.
