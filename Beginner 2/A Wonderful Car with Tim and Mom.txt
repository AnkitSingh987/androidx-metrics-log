Category: Beginner
Title: A Wonderful Car with Tim and Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, he saw a big, expensive toy car in the store. He wanted it so much, but he didn't have enough money. Tim went home and asked his mom, "Can I have the big toy car?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea. You can help me with the post. If you do a good job, I will give you money to buy the toy car." Tim was very happy. He helped his mom put letters in the post box. When they were done, his mom gave him the money. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom!" He went to the store and bought the big, expensive toy car. Tim played with his new toy car all day. He was so proud of himself for helping his mom and earning the money. And his mom was proud of him too, for being such a good helper. They lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a man with a big bag. The man said, "Hi, I am Mr. Smith. I feed the birds here every day. Do you want to help me?" Mia and her mom said yes. Mr. Smith gave Mia some seeds to hold in her palm. Mia held out her hand and the birds came to eat. The seeds felt a little sour on her skin, but she liked helping the birds. When they were done, Mia and her mom said, "Thank you, Mr. Smith!" Mr. Smith smiled and said, "You're welcome! Come back soon to help me feed the birds again!" Mia and her mom went home happy, knowing they had made new friends at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rocket. The rocket liked to fly high in the sky. It was very fast and shiny. The rocket had many friends who liked to watch it fly. One day, a little bird saw the rocket and wanted to follow it. The bird flapped its wings and flew up into the sky. It tried to keep up with the rocket, but the rocket was too fast. The bird felt sad and went back to its nest. The rocket saw the bird and wanted to help. It slowed down so the bird could follow it. The bird was so happy! The bird and the rocket flew together in the sky, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy cat. This cat had a big house and many toys. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat wanted to complete a puzzle. The cat tried to use its paw to put the pieces together. A little mouse saw the cat. The mouse said, "Can I help you with the puzzle?" The cat said, "Yes, please." The mouse and the cat worked together. They used their paws to complete the puzzle. They were very happy. But then, a big dog came into the house. The dog saw the cat and the mouse. The dog wanted to play, too. The dog tried to use its paw on the puzzle. But the dog was too big and not careful. The dog broke the puzzle. The cat and the mouse were sad. The dog said, "I'm sorry," but the puzzle was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Lucky. Lucky loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big, red ball in the yard. He was so happy. He wanted to play with the ball all day. Lucky's friend, a little bird, came to visit. The bird said, "Lucky, let's play with the ball. But first, we must turn it so it goes the right way." Lucky and the bird turned the ball and it started to roll. They played and played until they were tired. As the sun began to set, they saw a beautiful sunset. Lucky said, "Look at the pretty colors in the sky!" The bird replied, "Yes, it's a lovely sunset. We are lucky to see it together." And so, they sat and watched the sunset, happy to have spent the day playing and enjoying each other's company.
