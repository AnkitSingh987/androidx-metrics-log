Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cake

Once upon a time, in a wide, wide world, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his big red ball. One day, Tim saw a big, tall tree with a yummy dessert at the top. It was a big, sweet cake with red and blue icing. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but he did not know how to get it down. Tim had a smart idea. He would use his big red ball to knock the cake down from the tree. He threw the ball at the cake, but it did not work. The ball just bounced away. Tim tried again and again, but the cake would not fall down. Tim was very sad. He wanted the dessert so much, but he could not get it. As he walked away, a big, mean bird came and ate the cake. Tim was very sad that he could not eat the dessert. The bird flew away, and Tim went home with an empty tummy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a humble and happy girl named Jane. She loved playing with colors. One day, her mommy said, "Jane, don't forget your colors!" Jane remembered and said, "Yes, Mommy!" Every day, Jane and her mommy went outside to play in Jane's favorite spot. Jane would always collect all sorts of colors like yellow and blue, and red and purple. But one day, Jane went to play outside but didn't have any colors to collect. She asked her mommy why she didn't have any colors. Her mommy smiled and said, "Oh, Jane, you forgot to bring your colors outside." Jane felt sad, but was grateful that her mommy was with her and suggested that they simply look at all the colors in nature. Jane agreed and they looked at the colors in the sky, the colors of the flowers, and the colors of the trees. Jane was soon filled with awe and delight by all the colors around them!

Lily loved her teddy bear. She hugged him every night and took him everywhere. He was her best friend. But one day, Lily's mom said they had to move to a new house. She said they had to pack all their things in boxes and put them in a big truck. Lily was sad. She did not want to leave her old house and her friends. She did not want to lose her teddy bear. She asked her mom if she could keep him with her. Her mom said yes, but she had to put him in a special bag. She said it was safer that way. Lily did not like the bag. It was dark and tight. She wanted to see her teddy bear. She wanted to hold him and talk to him. She opened the bag and took him out. She whispered to him, "Don't worry, teddy. We will be happy in our new house. You belong with me." But then, Lily's mom saw her. She was angry. She said, "Lily, what are you doing? You have to put your teddy bear back in the bag. We have to go now. The truck is waiting." Lily did not want to. She cried and said, "No, mom. I want to keep him with me. He is my friend. He is scared in the bag." Her mom did not listen. She took the teddy bear from Lily's arms and put him in the bag. She zipped it up and threw it in a box. She said, "Lily, stop being silly. It's just a toy. You can play with him later. Come on, we have to go." Lily was very sad. She felt like her mom did not understand her. She felt like she lost her teddy bear. She followed her mom to the truck, but she did not say a word. When they got to the new house, Lily's mom opened the boxes and put the things in different rooms. She said, "Lily, this is your new room. It's bigger and nicer than your old one. Look, you have a new bed and a new cupboard. You can put your toys and clothes in there." Lily did not care. She did not want a new room or a new cupboard. She wanted her old room and her old teddy bear. She looked for the bag with her teddy bear, but she could not find it. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my teddy bear? Where is the bag?" Her mom looked confused. She said, "I don't know, Lily. I thought I put it in one of the boxes. Maybe it's in the kitchen or the living room. Why don't you go and look for it?" Lily ran to the kitchen and the living room, but she did not see the bag. She checked all the boxes, but she did not find it. She was scared. She thought, "Maybe the bag fell out of the truck. Maybe someone took it. Maybe I will never see my teddy bear again." She ran back to her mom and said, "Mom, I can't find my teddy bear. He is gone. He is lost. I want him back. I miss him so much." Her mom hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sure he is somewhere. We will find him. Don't be sad. He is not lost. He is just hiding." But Lily did not believe her. She felt like her mom lied to her. She felt like her mom did not care about her teddy bear. She felt like she had no friends in the new house. She felt very lonely and sad. She wished she could see her teddy bear again. She wished he could hug her and tell her everything was going to be okay. She wished he could say, "I belong with you."
