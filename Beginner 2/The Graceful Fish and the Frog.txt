Category: Beginner
Title: The Graceful Fish and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a graceful fish. The fish loved to swim in the water all day. One day, the fish saw a big frog. The frog said, "Let's play a game. Who can jump the highest?" The fish tried to jump, but it could not jump as high as the frog. The fish felt embarrass. The frog laughed and said, "You can't jump like me, fish. I am the best jumper." The fish was sad and swam away. It wanted to show the frog it could jump too. So, the fish tried and tried to jump higher. But, the fish didn't see the big net in the water. The fish jumped right into the net. It could not get out. The frog saw the fish in the net and felt bad. The frog tried to help, but it was too late. The fish was taken away by the net, and the frog was left all alone.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were in a room. The room had many cushions. The cat saw an unusual cushion. It was big and red. The cat wanted to sit on it. The little dog said, "No, that is my cushion!" The big cat did not listen. The cat sat on the unusual cushion. The little dog was sad and began to complain. "I want my cushion back!" said the little dog. The big cat looked at the dog and said, "We can share the cushion." They both sat on the unusual cushion and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run in the park. One day, Spot found a big, rough ball. He liked the ball and played with it all day. Spot got very tired and needed a nap. He found a nice, soft spot under a tree and went to sleep. When he woke up, he saw a little girl crying. She was sad because she lost her ball. Spot knew he had to help her. He gave the rough ball to the little girl, and she was very happy. They played together and had fun. Spot even got a taste of her yummy ice cream. The little girl learned that sharing is good, and Spot learned that helping others can make you happy too.

"Hurry, Billy! Come join the race!" Mary called to her brother. Billy was small, but eager to join the game. He loved running and winning. The other kids were in their spots, lined up at the start. Mary had on a long colorful strip that waved in the wind as she stood ready to go. Billy was the last one to get to the start. He looked confused and a little scared. Mary smiled at him and said, "Don't worry, Billy. I'm sure you'll do just fine. You're an intelligent kid!" Billy smiled back and lined up with the others. When the leader shouted, "Go!" everyone started running. Billy ran and ran until he came in first! He was so excited and happy. He had won the race! Mary ran to him and hugged him tight. "You did it! You won!" She said. Billy grinned and waved his strip in the air. He was so proud of himself!

Sara likes to draw with her crayons. She draws a big sun, a blue sky, a green grass and a red house. She draws her family too. She draws Mom, Dad, her baby brother and herself. She is very happy with her drawing. She wants to show her drawing to Mom and Dad. She thinks they will like it too. She looks for scissors. She wants to cut her drawing and put it in a frame. She finds scissors on the table. She picks them up and starts to cut. "Stop, Sara, stop!" Mom says. She runs to Sara and takes the scissors from her hand. "Scissors are not for you. They are sharp and dangerous. You can hurt yourself or the paper. You don't need to cut your drawing. It is beautiful as it is." Sara feels sad. She thinks Mom is angry with her. She thinks Mom does not like her drawing. She starts to cry. Mom hugs Sara and wipes her tears. "I'm not angry with you, Sara. I love you and your drawing. I'm sorry I scared you. I just want you to be safe. Scissors are for grown-ups only. You can use glue or tape to put your drawing in a frame. Do you want to do that?" Sara nods. She smiles. She still wants to put her drawing in a frame. Mom helps her find glue and a frame. They glue the drawing to the frame. They hang it on the wall. Sara is happy again. She shows her drawing to Dad and her baby brother. They all say it is very nice. Sara feels proud. She gives Mom a big hug and a kiss. Mom gives her a hug and a kiss too. They are happy together.
