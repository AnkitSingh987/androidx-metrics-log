Category: Beginner
Title: Sophia and Leo and the Wheel

Sophia and Leo were twins. Sophia was 3 and Leo was 4. Every day the twins played together in their backyard. One day, Sophia found a broken wheel in the grass. She showed it to Leo and said, "Look, Leo! What is this?" Leo answered, "That's a wheel, Sophia. It's broken." Sophia picked up the wheel and asked, "What can we do with this wheel?" Leo thought for a moment and said, "Let's keep it!" So Sophia and Leo kept the wheel and played with it every day. They even put it in their wagon and pulled it around the backyard. Soon, the wheel was no longer broken. Sophia and Leo were very happy they had kept it!

Once upon a time, in a fair land, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a pretty collar with many colors. He liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Spot and his friends were playing with a ball. They were having so much fun. But then, the ball went far away. Spot ran after the ball to bring it back. As Spot got closer to the ball, he saw many tiny things on the ground. They were seeds! Spot decided to scatter the seeds in the field. He thought it would be a fun thing to do. The next day, Spot went back to the field to play with his friends. To his surprise, the seeds had grown into beautiful flowers! The field was now full of color, just like his collar. Spot and his friends played happily in the flower field every day.

One day, a big dog named Max found some stuff in the yard. It was a pile of dry leaves and sticks. Max thought it would be fun to play with the stuff. He ran around the pile, barking and wagging his tail. Max's friend, a little bird named Tim, saw him playing. Tim flew down and said, "Max, be careful! That stuff can burn!" Max didn't listen and kept playing. Suddenly, a strong wind came and the stuff started to burn. The fire grew big and Max was scared. Max felt ashamed that he didn't listen to Tim. He barked for help, and Tim quickly flew to get water. Together, they put out the fire. Max learned to be careful with things that can burn and to listen to his friends. From that day on, Max and Tim played safely and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big mouse. The mouse has a thick tail and sharp teeth. The mouse is hungry. He wants to eat cheese. "Hello, mouse," Lily says. "Do you want to play with us?" The mouse nods. He sees cheese in Lily's bag. He thinks of a plan. He says, "Yes, I want to play. But first, can I have some cheese? I am very hungry." Lily is kind. She gives the mouse some cheese. The mouse eats it fast. He says, "Thank you, Lily. Can I have more cheese?" Lily looks in her bag. She has only one piece of cheese left. She says, "Sorry, mouse. This is my last cheese. I want to share it with Ben." The mouse is greedy. He says, "No, Lily. Give me all the cheese. It is mine." Ben is angry. He says, "No, mouse. You are not nice. You are selfish. You cannot have all the cheese. Lily gave you some cheese. You should be happy." The mouse is mad. He says, "I don't care. I want all the cheese. Give it to me or I will bite you." Lily and Ben are scared. They run away from the mouse. They go to the slide. They slide down fast. They have fun. The mouse follows them. He wants to catch them. He climbs up the slide. He is almost at the top. He sees the cheese in Lily's hand. He jumps to get it. But he jumps too high. He misses the cheese. He falls down the slide. He lands on the ground. He hurts his nose. He cries. Lily and Ben see the mouse. They feel sorry for him. They go to him. They say, "Are you okay, mouse?" The mouse is sad. He says, "No, I am not okay. I hurt my nose. And I have no cheese. And I have no friends." Lily and Ben are kind. They say, "We can be your friends, mouse. But you have to be nice. You have to share. You have to say sorry." The mouse is sorry. He says, "I am sorry, Lily and Ben. I was not nice. I was selfish. I did not share. Can you forgive me?" Lily and Ben are happy. They say, "Yes, we forgive you, mouse. Here, you can have some cheese. But you have to share with us." The mouse is happy. He says, "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are very kind. I will share with you. I will be nice. I will be your friend." They all eat some cheese. They all play together. They all have fun. The end. Moral value: Sharing is caring. Being selfish is bad. Being nice is good. Saying sorry is important. Making friends is fun.
