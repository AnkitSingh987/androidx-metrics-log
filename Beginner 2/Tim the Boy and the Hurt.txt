Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hurt

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play and have fun. But sometimes, Tim was careless. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car. He did not look where he was going. He tripped and fell down. When Tim fell, he hit his head. It hurt a lot. His mom came and said, "Oh no, Tim! You need to be careful. You don't want to hurt your brain." Tim's mom put a band-aid on his head. She gave him a hug and a kiss to make him feel better. After that day, Tim tried to be more careful. He did not want to hurt his brain again. He still played and had fun, but now he looked where he was going. Tim seemed to be a more careful boy now, and his mom was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a naughty dog. He liked to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big red ball on the side of the road. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was too high for him to reach. Spot tried to jump, but he could not jump high enough. Spot had a friend named Sam. Sam was a tall and strong horse. Spot asked Sam for help. Sam had an idea. He told Spot that they could raise the ball together. Spot was happy and excited. They both went to the side of the road where the big red ball was. Sam stood on his back legs and raised his front legs to reach the ball. Spot jumped on Sam's back and pushed the ball with his nose. The big red ball rolled down and Spot could now play with it. Spot and Sam were very happy. They played with the ball all day long. And from that day on, Spot was not a naughty dog anymore. He learned that with the help of friends, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was three years old and loved to laugh. One day, he went on an adventure with his mummy and daddy. They went by a little stream, or "creek". Tim was so excited to see the rushing water and the brightly coloured fish swimming underneath. The creek was deep and Tim wanted to get closer so he could get a better look. But when he bent over to get a closer view, he felt a bit dizzy. He started to giggle and then burst out laughing as he wobbled on his feet. His mummy and daddy laughed too. "Careful, Tim! You nearly fell in!" said Daddy. But Tim wasn't scared. He just kept laughing and watching the fish. What a wonderful adventure it was.

Once upon a time, there was a calm ocean. In this ocean, there lived a big fish named Bob and a little fish named Tim. They were best friends and liked to swim and play all day. One day, Bob and Tim found a small cave. Tim said, "Let's go in!" But Bob was too big to fit. Tim went inside and found a shiny stone. He showed it to Bob and said, "Look what I found!" Just then, the shiny stone started to glow. Suddenly, Bob became small enough to fit in the cave. They both laughed and played inside the cave with the magic stone. They knew that they could always have fun together, no matter how big or small they were.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a fragile tray in the kitchen. He liked the tray because it was shiny and pretty. He wanted to show it to his friend, Tom. Tim went to Tom's house and said, "Look, Tom! I found a fragile tray. Isn't it nice?" Tom looked at the tray and said, "Yes, it's very nice! Can I hold it?" Tim gave the tray to Tom, but he told him, "Be careful, it's fragile." As they walked, Tom tripped on a rock. The tray fell from his hands and it snapped in half. Both Tim and Tom looked at the broken tray, very sad. They knew they couldn't fix the fragile tray. They felt bad because they didn't take care of it.
