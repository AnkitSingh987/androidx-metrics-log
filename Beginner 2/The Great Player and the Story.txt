Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Player and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a great player who liked to zoom around. He always zoomed faster than anyone else, and the other players loved watching him. One day the other players challenged him to see if he could zoom even faster than usual. The great player was confident, so he happily agreed to the challenge. He said, "Let me show you how fast I can go!" Then he started to zoom across the field, faster and faster than before. At first the other players cheered him on, but soon things started to go wrong. As the great player zipped and zagged quickly, suddenly he tripped up and fell to the ground. He had stepped in a gopher hole and twisted his ankle. The other players laughed, and said, "You're not so great now, are you?" The great player sadly shook his head, and the other players stopped cheering him on. The moral of the story is that too much zooming can be dangerous, and you learn a lot more when you slow down and take your time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was John. John had a very big heart Full of love and kind feelings. One day John was walking in the park when he saw a puppy. The puppy was all alone and it looked sad. John kindly said to the pup, "What's wrong little pup? Don't be sad. Please come with me and I'll take care of you." The puppy looked at John with its big eyes and said, "Really? You'll take care of me? That would make me so happy!" John beamed with joy. He nodded eagerly and said, "Yes. Come on, let's go! I'll give you lots of love and hugs." And so they went on their way together, John and the pup, both with big happy hearts.

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play with toys. They had many toys of different colors and shapes. Tom liked to keep his toys organized in boxes and baskets. Mia liked to squeeze her toys and make them talk. She did not care about putting them away. One day, Tom invited Mia to his house to play. He showed her his toys and said, "You can play with any toy you want, but please be careful and put them back where they belong." Mia nodded and smiled. She picked up a soft bear and squeezed it. It made a funny sound. She laughed and threw it on the floor. Then she picked up a truck and squeezed it. It made a loud sound. She laughed and threw it on the floor. She did the same with many other toys. Tom did not like what Mia was doing. He said, "Mia, stop! You are making a mess and hurting my toys. Please put them back in their places." Mia did not listen. She said, "No, I like to squeeze and throw. It is fun. Your toys are boring. They only sit in boxes and baskets." She picked up a rope and squeezed it. It made a knot. She laughed and threw it on the floor. Tom was angry and sad. He said, "Mia, you are not a good friend. You do not respect my toys or my rules. You only care about yourself. You can go home now. I do not want to play with you anymore." Mia was surprised and hurt. She said, "But Tom, I was only having fun. I did not mean to make you mad. Can we still be friends?" Tom shook his head and said, "No, you cannot be my friend. You are too selfish and careless. Go away and do not come back." Mia left Tom's house crying. She realized that she had been wrong and rude. She wished she had listened to Tom and played nicely. She learned that squeezing and throwing toys is not fun. It is mean and wasteful. She also learned that being organized and respectful is important. It makes you a better friend and a happier person.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a generous boy. He liked to share his toys and food. One day, Tim made some toast. He wanted to share his toast with his friends. Tim went to the park to find his friends. He saw a bird with a hurt wing. Tim felt sad for the bird. He wanted to help the bird heal. So, he shared some of his toast with the bird. The bird ate the toast and felt better. The bird's wing started to heal. Tim was so happy to see the bird fly again. He knew that sharing his toast had helped the bird. Tim and the bird became good friends.
