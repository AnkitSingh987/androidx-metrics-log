Category: Beginner
Title: The Mystery of the Cat

Once upon a time there were two old friends, a black cat and a yellow dog. The black cat was always so mysterious and the yellow dog always seemed so happy, but no one was really sure why! One day, the two old friends were out on an adventure together when they came across an old abandoned barn. The black cat said to the yellow dog "I think we should go inside! Who knows what we might find!" The yellow dog nodded his head and the two of them entered the barn. They looked around and eventually came across a black box. The black cat touched it and said "It's locked! It looks like we might need a key to open it!" The yellow dog thought for a moment and then smiled. He stepped forward confidently and said "If you yield, I think it will open!" The black cat looked confused, but did as the dog said. She stepped forward and yielded and to her surprise the box opened! Inside were two sparkly gems, one blue and one green. The two friends were so excited to have found these gems! The black cat was over the moon and said "It's the perfect gift for our adventure!"

Once upon a time there were two friends, Larry and Mandy. One day, Mandy got a big surprise - it was time for her first lecture! She was a bit scared but Larry said he would come with her. The lecture room was big and a lot of grown-ups were there. Mandy and Larry sat down and started to listen. There was a lady at the front and she said some kind things. At the end of the lecture, the lady smiled and said: "Now, can you all say 'hello' to Mandy and Larry?" The grown-ups all smiled and said 'hello'. Mandy and Larry said 'hello' too! After the lecture, Larry gave Mandy a big hug. He said: "That was a very kind lecture. Let’s go home and call our friends to tell them!" Mandy and Larry said goodbye to the grown-ups and then ran off to call their friends. They were so happy that they had gone to the lecture together.

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play with Tim's dog, Max. Max has soft fur and a big tail. He likes to run and jump and lick Tim and Jen. One day, Tim and Jen go to the park with Max. They see a big pond with ducks and fish. Tim and Jen want to feed the ducks. They have some bread in a bag. They go to the edge of the pond and throw some bread to the ducks. Max sees the bread and the ducks. He thinks they are food. He starts to bark and run to the pond. He wants to catch the bread and the ducks. Tim and Jen shout, "No, Max, stop!" But Max does not listen. He jumps into the pond and swims after the bread and the ducks. The ducks are scared of Max. They flap their wings and quack loudly. They try to get away from Max. Some of them fly away. Some of them dive under the water. Max does not catch any of them. He gets wet and tired. The fish are also scared of Max. They swim away from him. But one fish is sleepy. He does not see Max coming. Max sees the fish and opens his mouth. He bites the fish and swallows it. The fish is not good for Max. He feels sick. He coughs and chokes. He tries to spit out the fish, but he can't. He swims back to the shore. He lies down on the grass. He whines and moans. He does not want to play anymore. Tim and Jen are sad and sorry. They run to Max and hug him. They say, "We are sorry, Max. We love you. Please be okay." But Max is not okay. He does not move. He does not breathe. He is gone. Tim and Jen cry. They have lost their friend. They wish they had not fed the ducks. They wish they had watched Max. They wish they could say sorry to the fish. They wish they could have Max back. But they can't. The end.
