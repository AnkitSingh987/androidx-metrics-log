Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and his sister, Sue. They liked to play in the park. One day, they found a big, red ball. Tim tried to lift the ball, but it was too heavy. "Let me help you," said Sue. Together, they lifted the ball. They were so happy. They played with the ball all day. Then, a dog came and took the ball away. Tim was embarrassed. He said, "I'm sorry, sister." Sue smiled and said, "It's okay, we can find a new ball." So, they went to look for a new ball and had fun playing together.

Lily and Sam were friends who liked to play at the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But their favorite thing was to spin on the round seat. They would sit on the seat and push with their feet until they went very fast. One day, they saw a new boy at the park. His name was Ben and he looked sad. Lily and Sam wanted to be nice, so they asked him if he wanted to play with them. Ben nodded and followed them to the round seat. "Sit here, Ben," Lily said. "We will spin you and make you happy." Lily and Sam pushed the seat hard and Ben started to spin. He smiled at first, but then he felt sick. He wanted to stop, but Lily and Sam did not hear him. They thought he was having fun. "Stop, stop, please!" Ben cried. "I feel dizzy. I want to get off." Lily and Sam finally heard him and stopped the seat. Ben stumbled off and ran to a bench. He felt very bad and his face was pale. Lily and Sam felt sorry for Ben. They realized they had been too rough with him. They ran to the bench and apologized to him. "We are sorry, Ben," Sam said. "We did not mean to hurt you. We just wanted to play with you." "We did not know you did not like to spin," Lily said. "We like to spin, but you don't. That's okay. Everyone is different." Ben forgave them and thanked them for being kind. He said he liked to play other things, like catch or tag. Lily and Sam agreed to play those things with him. They became friends and had fun at the park. The moral of the story is: Be careful with your friends and respect their likes and dislikes. Don't force them to do something they don't enjoy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lucy. She had a pair of sunglasses that she loved very much. Every day, she would wear them when the sun would rise. Lucy was an honest girl and always told the truth. One day, Lucy went to the park with her sunglasses. She played with her friends and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, she could not find her sunglasses. She looked and looked, but they were gone. Lucy was very sad. She asked her friends, "Did you see my sunglasses?" Her friends shook their heads. They did not know where the sunglasses were. They all helped Lucy look for them, but they could not find them. Lucy went home feeling sad. The next day, Lucy's friend Tom went to the park. He saw the sunglasses under a big tree. He remembered that Lucy was honest and always told the truth. So, he picked up the sunglasses and brought them to Lucy's house. When Lucy saw Tom with her sunglasses, she was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Tom! You are a good friend." From that day on, Lucy and Tom became best friends, and they always played together at the park when the sun would rise.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a ring for Tim's dad. In the store, they saw many rings, but they found one that was cheap. Tim's mom said, "This ring is good and cheap. Let's buy it for dad." After buying the ring, they went back home. Tim was very tired from walking. He said, "Mom, I want to take a nap." His mom said, "Okay, Tim. You can take a nap on the couch." While Tim was taking a nap, his mom put the ring in a small box. When Tim woke up from his nap, his dad came home from work. Tim ran to his dad and said, "Dad, we got you something!" Tim's dad opened the box and saw the cheap ring. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim and mom. I love my new ring!"
