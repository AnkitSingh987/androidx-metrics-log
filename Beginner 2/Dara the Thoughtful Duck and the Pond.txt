Category: Beginner
Title: Dara the Thoughtful Duck and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful duck named Dara. Dara lived by a pond with clear water. She liked to swim and play with her friends in the water. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the pond. Tim saw Dara and her friends swimming. He wanted to play with them. Tim said, "Dara, can I play in the water with you?" Dara did not mind. She said, "Yes, Tim! You can play with us. But you must be careful near the water." Tim was happy and thanked Dara for letting him play. Tim and Dara played in the water together. They laughed and splashed with their friends. They had a fun day at the pond. From that day on, Tim and Dara became good friends who always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to sing. She would sing all day long. One day, Mia found a mask. The mask was delicate and pretty. She put on the mask and started to sing. As she sang, something unexpected happened. The mask made her voice sound different. Mia was surprised! She took off the mask and her voice was back to normal. Mia put the mask back on and her voice changed again. Mia showed her friends the mask. They all tried it on and sang. Each time, their voices changed too. They laughed and played, singing with the magic mask. The friends had a fun day, and they all loved the special mask that made their voices change.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went outside to play. They were very happy and enthusiastic. They saw a big pile of soil in the garden. Tim urged Spot to dig in the soil with him. "Come on, Spot! Let's dig and find something fun!" said Tim. Spot barked and wagged his tail. They started to dig and dig in the soil. They got very dirty, but they didn't care. They were having so much fun. Then, Tim's mom came outside. She saw Tim and Spot all dirty from the soil. She was not happy. "Tim, you and Spot are so dirty! You need to come inside and take a bath right now!" she said. Tim and Spot were sad. They didn't want to stop playing, but they had to listen to Tim's mom. They went inside and took a bath. The fun day was over, and they were not happy.

Anna likes to feed the fish in the pond. She has a bag of bread that she breaks into small pieces. She throws the pieces into the water and watches the fish come and eat them. She thinks the fish are pretty and funny. One day, she goes to the pond with her brother Tom. Tom is older than Anna and he thinks he knows everything. He tells Anna that she is feeding the fish wrong. He says she should throw bigger pieces of bread and make the fish jump out of the water. He says that is more fun. Anna does not want to do that. She likes to feed the fish gently and see them swim. She tells Tom to stop being mean to the fish. She says the fish are her friends and they do not like to be scared. Tom does not listen to Anna. He grabs the bag of bread from her and throws a big piece into the pond. He laughs when he sees a fish jump out of the water and splash. He says that is how you feed the fish. Anna is angry and sad. She tries to get the bag of bread back from Tom, but he is too strong. He pushes her away and throws another big piece of bread. He does not care about the fish. He only cares about himself. The fish do not like Tom. They are scared and impatient. They do not want to eat his big pieces of bread. They want to eat Anna's small pieces of bread. They want to swim peacefully and be happy. One of the fish has an idea. He swims under the big piece of bread that Tom threw and pushes it up with his nose. The piece of bread flies out of the water and hits Tom in the face. Tom is surprised and drops the bag of bread. Anna picks it up and runs to the other side of the pond. She feeds the fish her small pieces of bread and smiles. The fish smile back and thank her. They are glad that Anna is their friend.
