Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Lily and the Dog

Tim and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big slide that looked very fun. Tim wanted to go first, but Lily said no. She said she was older by two minutes, so she should go first. They started to argue and push each other. "Stop it, you two!" their mom called from a bench. "You need to share and take turns. That is your task today. If you don't, we will go home right now." Tim and Lily did not listen. They kept fighting for the slide. They did not see a little dog that was running around the park. The dog was lost and scared. It saw the slide and thought it was a good place to hide. It ran up the steps and slid down fast. Tim and Lily were surprised when they saw the dog. They stopped fighting and looked at it. The dog was shaking and whimpering. It looked helpless and sad. "Aw, poor doggy," Lily said. "It needs our help. Maybe it lost its family." "Let's be nice to it," Tim said. "Maybe we can find its collar and call its owner." They went to the dog and gently petted it. The dog calmed down and licked their faces. They saw a tag on its collar that had a phone number. They ran to their mom and showed her the dog and the tag. "Good job, kids," their mom said. "You did a kind thing. You helped the dog and made a new friend. Let's call the number and see if we can reunite the dog with its family." They called the number and a happy voice answered. It was the dog's owner. He said he was looking for the dog everywhere. He thanked Tim and Lily for finding it and said he would come to the park right away. Tim and Lily were proud of themselves. They learned that sharing and helping were better than fighting and being selfish. They hugged the dog and waited for its owner. They also decided to share the slide and have fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a good store. People loved to go there because it had many things they liked. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to celebrate his birthday. He asked his mom to take him to the store to buy toys for his party. At the store, Tim saw a big, shiny ball that he wanted for his party. But another little girl named Sue also wanted the same ball. Tim and Sue both held onto the ball and did not want to let go. They were not happy. Tim's mom and Sue's dad saw them and knew they had to help. Tim's mom said, "Why don't we buy two balls, one for each of you?" Sue's dad agreed, and they bought two balls. Tim and Sue both smiled and became friends. They played together at Tim's birthday party and had a great time celebrating.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. But today, mom and dad are busy. They have to go to a boring meeting. They tell Lily and Ben to stay with grandma. "Can we go to the park with grandma?" Lily asks. "Sorry, sweetie, not today. Grandma has a bad leg. She can't walk fast. You have to stay inside and play with your toys," mom says. Lily and Ben are sad. They don't like to play inside. They want to run and swing and slide. They look at their toys. They are old and boring. They have no fun. They look at grandma. She is sitting on the couch. She is knitting a scarf. She has a watch on her wrist. She looks at the watch every few minutes. "What are you doing, grandma?" Ben asks. "I'm knitting a scarf for your mom. It's her birthday soon. And I'm watching the time. Mom and dad will be back soon," grandma says. Lily and Ben think of a plan. They want to make mom and dad come back faster. They want to go to the park. They sneak to the couch. They grab grandma's watch. They run to the kitchen. They find a big pot. They put the watch in the pot. They turn the stove on. They hope the watch will get hot and make a loud noise. They rush back to the living room. They act like nothing happened. They wait for the watch to go boom. But grandma is not silly. She sees them run. She sees them smile. She knows they are up to something. She follows them to the kitchen. She smells something burning. She sees the pot. She sees the watch. She turns the stove off. She takes the watch out. She is angry. "What are you doing, Lily and Ben? You could have started a fire! You could have broken my watch! Why did you do this?" grandma asks. "We wanted to make mom and dad come back faster. We wanted to go to the park. We were bored," Lily and Ben say. Grandma sighs. She understands. She remembers when she was a child. She also liked to play outside. She also hated boring meetings. She hugs Lily and Ben. She says she is sorry. She says she loves them. She says she has an idea. She takes them to the backyard. She shows them a big box. She says it is a magic box. She says they can pretend it is anything they want. They can pretend it is a car, a boat, a rocket, a castle, anything. Lily and Ben are happy. They like to pretend. They thank grandma. They hug her. They go inside the box. They have fun. They pretend they are driving to the park. They pretend they are sailing to an island. They pretend they are flying to the moon. They pretend they are living in a palace. They forget about the watch. They forget about the meeting. They forget about being bored. They have fun until mom and dad come back. Mom and dad are happy to see them. They are happy to see the scarf. They are happy to see the box. They are happy to see grandma. They all go to the park. They all run and swing and slide. They all have fun. They all love each other. The end.
