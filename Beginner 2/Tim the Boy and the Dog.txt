Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

One day, a boy named Tim found a box of marbles. He wanted to sort them by color. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me sort these marbles?" Tim said. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." While they were sorting the marbles, they saw a big, scary shadow. It was a big dog. The dog wanted to play with the marbles too. The dog barked, "Woof! Woof!" Tim and Sam were scared. They did not want the dog to take their marbles. Tim and Sam tried to hide the marbles, but the dog was too fast. The dog took the marbles and ran away. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not sort the marbles anymore. The big, scary dog had taken them all.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called John. He had a special purple vest which he loved to wear. One day, John wanted to go on an adventure. He went to the rocks and began to climb them. John tried his best to get to the top, but it was too hard. He was very persistent, and kept on trying. Suddenly, he lost his footing and fell down. His vest caught on a sharp rock and tore. John was very sad. But John was persistent and kept on trying, and eventually he made it to the top of the rocks. He was very happy and proud that he'd achieved something difficult. John learned a valuable lesson: keep being persistent and don't give up, even when it's hard.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in the park. They see many leaves on the ground. The leaves are red, yellow and brown. They are pretty. Tom picks up a leaf and shows it to Mia. "Look, Mia, this leaf is weird. It has holes in it. Do you know why?" he asks. Mia looks at the leaf and thinks. She remembers what her mom told her. "I know, Tom. The holes are from bugs. The bugs eat the leaf and make holes. They are hungry," she says. Tom nods. He thinks bugs are cool. He wants to find more leaves with holes. He says, "Let's play a game, Mia. Let's see who can find the most leaves with holes. The winner gets a cookie." Mia likes cookies. She agrees to play the game. She says, "OK, Tom, let's play. But we have to be careful. We don't want to step on the bugs or hurt the leaves. They are living things too." Tom and Mia start to look for leaves with holes. They have fun and learn about nature. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a modest dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his bone. He would run, jump, and even fold his legs to roll on the ground with it. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend, a small bird named Tweet. "Hi, Spot!" said Tweet. "Can I play with your bone too?" Spot thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Tweet, but you must be careful. I will show you how to fold your wings and play with it." As they played, a big wind came and blew the bone away. Spot and Tweet looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were very sad. But then, they remembered the fun they had playing together, and they smiled. The next day, Spot and Tweet found a new toy to play with. They learned that sharing and playing together was more important than the toy they had lost. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his red ball. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim was very happy. Tim saw a big, round ice cream stand. He said to his mom, "Mom, can I have some ice cream, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let's go get some ice cream." They walked to the stand and Tim picked a sweet vanilla ice cream. Tim took a big taste of his ice cream. It was so yummy! He played with his red ball, pushing it around the park. He shared his ice cream with a new friend he met. They laughed and played together until the sun began to set. It was time for Tim to go home. Tim and his mom left the park, holding hands and talking about their fun day.
