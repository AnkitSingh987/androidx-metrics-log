Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Gifted Bunny and the Ball

One day, a gifted little bunny named Bobo was playing in the park. He loved to run and speed around the trees. His tiny legs moved so fast, he could feel the wind in his fur. As he was running, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was trying to catch a ball with her hand, but she couldn't. She looked sad. Bobo wanted to help her. He hopped over and said, "Hi, I am Bobo. I can help you catch the ball." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" They played together, and with Bobo's help, Lily caught the ball with her hand. They laughed and had fun. From that day on, Bobo and Lily were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong mechanic named Tom. He worked in a big shop and fixed cars. One day, a little girl named Sally came to the shop with her mom. Sally's mom's car was broken. Tom said, "I will fix your car, don't worry." He worked hard and fixed the car. Sally watched Tom and thought he was very strong. She wanted to give him a gift to say thank you. Sally went home and made a drawing for Tom. The next day, Sally and her mom went back to the shop. Sally gave the drawing to Tom and said, "Thank you for fixing our car. You are very strong." Tom smiled and put the drawing on the wall. From that day on, every time Tom felt tired, he looked at the drawing and felt strong again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. They were not rich, but they were very happy. Tim loved to help his mom and dad with chores around the house. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her pack a bag for a picnic. Tim was excited and said, "Yes, mom! I want to help!" They went to the kitchen and opened the fridge. Tim's mom took out some yummy food like apples, sandwiches, and juice. Tim carefully put the food in the bag. At the picnic, Tim and his mom and dad laughed and ate the yummy food. They played games and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "I loved helping you pack, mom. Can we have another picnic soon?" His mom hugged him and said, "Of course, Tim. We can have more picnics because we have each other, and that makes us rich in love."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an aunt who could make things glow. Lily loved to watch her aunt make things light up. It was like magic! One day, Lily's aunt was frustrated. She could not make a toy glow. Lily asked, "Aunt, why can't you make it glow?" Her aunt said, "I don't know, Lily. It just won't light up." Lily wanted to help her aunt. She took the toy and tried to make it glow, too. She thought really hard and then, the toy started to glow! Lily's aunt was so happy. She hugged Lily and said, "Thank you, Lily. You helped me make it glow!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Max. He was three years old and he loved to play outside. One day, Max was walking through the woods with his mom and he spotted a fancy skull. He was so excited and wanted to take it home. But then he slipped. He was so scared, he started to cry. His mom came over and gave him a hug. He stopped crying and looked at the skull. "It's so fancy!" Max said. "Yes it is," his mom replied. "But we can't take it home with us. We'll have to leave it here in the woods." Max was so sad, but his mom said, "We can come back someday and look at it if we want". Max smiled and thought that was a great idea! He gave the skull one last look and said goodbye. Then, they walked away, holding hands.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill, there was a small house. In the house, lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim's mom asked him to take a basket of apples to his grandma, who lived on the other side of the hill. Tim said, "Yes, mom!" and took the basket. As he walked down the hill, he saw a big, round rock. Tim wanted to play with the rock, so he dropped the basket of apples to roll the rock. The apples rolled down the hill and Tim couldn't find them. He felt very sad. Tim's mom came and saw what happened. She told Tim, "You should always do what you are asked to do first, before playing." Tim learned his lesson and promised to always do his work first. From then on, Tim always took care of his tasks before playing, and he was a very good boy.
