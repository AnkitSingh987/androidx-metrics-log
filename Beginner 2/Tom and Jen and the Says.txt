Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Jen and the Says

Tom and Jen are brother and sister. They like to watch TV after dinner. But they do not like the same shows. Tom likes shows with cars and trucks. Jen likes shows with animals and fairies. One night, they have a big fight. Tom wants to watch a show with a scary monster. Jen does not want to watch it. She is afraid of the monster. She wants to watch a show with a cute bunny. "Let me have the remote!" Tom says. He tries to grab it from Jen. "No, it is my turn!" Jen says. She holds the remote tight. "Stop it, you are a baby!" Tom says. He pushes Jen. "Ow, you are mean!" Jen says. She cries. Mom hears them and comes to the living room. She is not happy. "What is going on here?" Mom says. She looks at Tom and Jen. They both try to talk at the same time. "He wants to watch a scary show!" Jen says. "She won't let me watch what I want!" Tom says. Mom shakes her head. She takes the remote from Jen. "Enough, you two. You need to share and be nice to each other. Or no TV at all." Mom says. She turns off the TV. She tells Tom and Jen to go to their rooms. Tom and Jen are sad and angry. They do not talk to each other. They wish they could watch TV. They wish they could get along.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat. The cat loved to play with a ball. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat wanted to show how good it was with the ball. The cat tried very hard to play well. The cat wanted success. The cat played and played. The cat's friend, a dog, saw the cat play. The dog said, "I value you, cat. You are very good with the ball." The cat was happy and felt success. The mighty cat and the dog played together and had fun.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Harrison. Every week Harrison would kneel down in his bedroom to say his prayers. He would kneel on the floor and close his eyes and talk to God. Even though his prayers were small and modest, he was certain that God was listening. One day, as Harrison knelt on the floor, a beautiful angel appeared next to him. The angel smiled and said, "Harrison, God is pleased with your prayers. He is proud of you." Harrison jumped back in surprise, but the angel gave him a comforting hug and told him not to be afraid. Before she disappeared, the angel said, "Remember to keep up your prayers every week." And so Harrison continued to kneel down every week and pray to God. He was proud that God was proud of him and his little, modest prayers.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fierce dog named Max. Max lived near a port. He liked to watch the boats come and go. One day, the sun was shining very bright. Max saw something shiny in the water. Max wanted the shiny thing. But there was a problem. The shiny thing was far away in the water. Max thought about how to get the shiny thing. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. Then, Max saw a little boat. He had an idea. Max got on the boat and went to the shiny thing. He was so happy! Max took the shiny thing back to the port. Now, Max and the shiny thing can shine together in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a big gym. It was a fun place to play. But sometimes, it was gloomy. The gym had a big door that was always closed. No one knew what was behind the door. The gym's boss did not allow anyone to open it. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the gym. He saw the big door and wanted to know what was behind it. Tim asked his mom if he could open it. His mom said no. She told him that the gym's boss did not allow it. Tim was sad but he still played in the gym. Later that day, the gym's boss saw Tim playing. He saw how much fun Tim was having. The boss decided to open the big door. Behind the door was a room full of toys and games. The boss allowed everyone to play in the new room. The gym was no longer gloomy. All the kids were happy and had lots of fun.
