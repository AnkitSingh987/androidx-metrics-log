Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Cat

Lily was a cat who liked to eat a lot. She ate fish, milk, cheese, and sometimes even cake. She did not like to run or play with other cats. She only liked to sleep and eat. One day, Lily saw a big mouse in the kitchen. She wanted to catch it and eat it. She ran after the mouse, but she was too fat and slow. The mouse was fast and smart. It ran under the table, behind the fridge, and into a hole. Lily could not follow it. She was sad and hungry. Then, Lily saw her friend Tom, who was a thin and fit cat. He liked to eat too, but not too much. He also liked to run and play with other cats. He was happy and healthy. Tom saw Lily and said, "Hi, Lily. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "No, thank you. I am too tired and hungry. I tried to catch a mouse, but I could not." Tom said, "Oh, I see. You know, Lily, you should eat less and exercise more. It is good for you. It will make you faster and stronger. It will also make you happier and nicer. Eating too much is bad for you. It will make you fat and sick. It will also make you lazy and grumpy." Lily did not like what Tom said. She thought he was rude and mean. She said, "Go away, Tom. You are not my friend. You are just a skinny and silly cat. I like to eat what I want and do what I want. I do not need your advice." Tom said, "OK, Lily. I am sorry. I only wanted to help you. I am your friend. I care about you. But you can do what you want. I hope you will be happy." Tom left and went to play with other cats. Lily felt bad. She realized that Tom was right. He was her friend. He cared about her. He wanted to help her. He reminded her of what was good and what was bad. She was wrong. She was not happy. She was fat and sick. She was lazy and grumpy. She wanted to change. She decided to eat less and exercise more. She started to run and play with other cats. She started to catch mice and birds. She started to lose weight and gain energy. She started to smile and laugh. She became a thin and fit cat. She became a happy and healthy cat. She became a better cat. She also decided to apologize to Tom. She found him and said, "Hi, Tom. I am sorry for what I said. You were right. You were my friend. You cared about me. You helped me. You reminded me of what was good and what was bad. Thank you, Tom." Tom said, "Hi, Lily. I am glad to see you. You look great. You are thin and fit. You are happy and healthy. You are a better cat. You are welcome, Lily. I am happy to be your friend. Do you want to play with me?" Lily said, "Yes, please. I would love to play with you." They ran and played together. They had fun. They were friends. The moral of the story is: Listen to your friends who care about you and remind you of what is good and what is bad. Eat less and exercise more. It is good for you. It will make you happier and healthier. It will make you a better cat.

Once upon a time, there was a small train. The train had a worry. It was a hot day, and the train was near a rail that was not safe. The train did not want to go on the rail. The train saw a big tree. The tree was by the rail. The train thought, "If I push the tree on the rail, I will be safe." So, the train pushed the tree with all its might. The tree fell on the rail. The train was happy and went on the rail. But the train did not know that the tree did not make the rail safe. The train went on the rail and it was still not safe. The train fell off the rail and broke. The train could not move anymore. The train was very sad, and the story had a bad ending.
