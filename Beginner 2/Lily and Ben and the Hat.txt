Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Hat

Lily and Ben are playing dress-up in their room. They have many clothes and hats and shoes to try on. Lily likes to wear pretty dresses and shiny jewels. Ben likes to wear funny hats and big boots. "Look at me, Lily!" Ben says. He wears an enormous hat that covers his eyes and ears. He also wears a long coat that drags on the floor. He tries to walk, but he trips and falls. "Ouch!" he says. Lily laughs. She thinks Ben looks silly. She wears a pink dress and a necklace with many beads. She also wears a small hat with a flower on it. She twirls and dances. "Look at me, Ben!" she says. She feels like a princess. Ben looks at Lily. He thinks Lily looks pretty. He wants to try her hat. He gets up and takes off his hat. He reaches for Lily's hat. "Can I wear your hat, Lily?" he asks. Lily shakes her head. She does not want to share her hat. She likes her hat. It is her favorite hat. "No, Ben!" she says. "This is my hat. It is mine. You cannot wear it." Ben feels sad. He likes Lily's hat. He thinks it is a nice hat. He wants to wear it. He does not understand why Lily does not want to share. He says, "But I want to wear your hat, Lily. Please. Just for a little while." Lily looks at Ben. She sees that he is sad. She remembers that Ben is her brother. She loves Ben. She decides to be nice. She says, "Okay, Ben. You can wear my hat. But only for a little while. And you have to be careful. Do not break it." Ben smiles. He is happy. He says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. I will be careful. I will not break it." He puts on Lily's hat. He thinks it is a beautiful hat. He feels like a prince. Lily and Ben hug. They are happy. They wear their hats and play dress-up. They have fun. They are friends.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a bright red slide. They want to go on the slide. "Let's hurry!" Tom says. "The slide looks fun!" They run to the slide. They take turns to slide down. They laugh and shout. They have a good time. But then they see a big squirrel. The squirrel has a peanut in its mouth. The squirrel looks angry. It runs to the slide. It wants to chase Tom and Mia. "Uh-oh!" Mia says. "The squirrel is mad!" They are scared of the squirrel. They do not want to be bitten. They hurry to get off the slide. They run to their moms. "Mama, mama!" Tom says. "There is a bad squirrel on the slide!" "Mama, mama!" Mia says. "The squirrel has a peanut!" Their moms hug them. They are safe. They look at the squirrel. The squirrel is still on the slide. It eats the peanut. It does not care about Tom and Mia. "Maybe the squirrel was hungry," Tom's mom says. "Maybe the peanut was its snack," Mia's mom says. Tom and Mia nod. They understand. They are not mad at the squirrel. They are happy to be with their moms. They decide to play somewhere else. They leave the slide to the squirrel.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Sarah. She loved to spin around in circles. She smiled and laughed as she went round and round. One day Sarah went to a luxury hotel with her family. Inside the hotel was a huge spinning wheel. It had multi-colored lights and it was very sparkly. When Sarah saw the wheel, she wanted to spin on it. Her mom said that she had to wait until morning to try it out. But Sarah was very excited and she couldn't wait. That night Sarah decided to go to the wheel alone. When she got to the spinning wheel, it looked scary. The lights were off and the wheel looked still. Sarah was scared at first, but then she mustered up her courage and started spinning on the wheel. If felt so good to have the wheel turning around so fast. Sarah couldn't help but laugh and smile. She felt like she was spinning amongst the stars. At last, Sarah stopped spinning. She said goodbye to the luxury spinning wheel and went off to sleep. She was so glad that she had been brave enough to get on the scary spinning wheel.
