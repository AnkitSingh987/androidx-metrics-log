Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Lily

Ben likes to play with his cars. He has many cars of different colors and sizes. He makes them zoom and crash on the floor. He does not like to share his cars with his sister, Lily. Lily wants to play with Ben. She asks him nicely, "Can I have a car, please?" But Ben ignores her. He pretends he does not hear her. He keeps playing with his cars. Lily feels sad and angry. Lily sees Ben's pants on the bed. They are blue and have a dinosaur on them. Ben loves his dinosaur pants. Lily has an idea. She takes the pants and runs to the bathroom. She turns on the water and puts the pants in the sink. She laughs and says, "Now you will share your cars with me!" Ben hears the water and sees Lily run. He follows her and sees his pants in the sink. They are wet and soapy. He screams, "Lily, what did you do? You ruined my dinosaur pants!" He tries to grab them, but Lily holds them tight. Mom hears the noise and comes to the bathroom. She sees Ben and Lily fighting over the pants. She is not happy. She says, "Stop it, both of you! Why are you being so mean to each other?" She takes the pants and hangs them to dry. She says, "Ben, you should not ignore your sister when she wants to play. Lily, you should not ruin your brother's pants to get his attention. You need to apologize and make up." Ben and Lily look at each other. They feel sorry for what they did. They say, "I'm sorry" and hug. Mom smiles and says, "That's better. Now, why don't you play together nicely? It's a mild day outside. You can take your cars and play in the yard." Ben and Lily agree. They take their cars and go outside. They share and have fun. They forget about the pants.

Once upon a time, there was a big red van. The van had four round wheels and went very fast. The van had a nice man who drove it around town. The nice man liked to help people and make them happy. One day, the nice man saw a sad dog by the side of the road. The dog was sad because he was lost and could not find his way home. The nice man wanted to help the sad dog, so he let the dog get in the van. They drove around town to look for the dog's home. The nice man and the sad dog looked and looked but could not find the dog's home. The sun went down and it was time for the van to go home. The sad dog had to stay by the side of the road. The nice man felt sad too. The story ends with the dog still lost and the man still sad. The moral of the story is to always keep an eye on your pets, so they do not get lost.

One day, a pretty bird sat on a tree. The bird was very attractive. The bird had a friend, a big bear. The bear was sleeping. The bird wanted to wake the bear. The bird said, "Bear, wake up! Let's play!" But the bear did not wake. The bird tried to replace the bear's pillow with a stick. The bear felt the stick and woke up. The bear was not happy. The bear said, "Why did you replace my pillow with a stick?" The bird said, "I wanted to wake you up to play." The bear said, "Next time, just ask me to wake up." The bird said sorry, and they played together.

Mama and Bob were in the kitchen. Mama was making something in the mixer. She said to Bob, "Can you please remove the mixer for me?" Bob said, "Yes Mama!" He grabbed the mixer and carried it to the sink. Mama looked at Bob and smiled. She said, "Good job!" Then a loud noise came from the mixer. Mama looked inside and saw that the mixer had broken. Mama looked sad and said, "Oh no! I'm sorry, Bob. I should have been more careful. Now I can't finish the recipe." Bob looked at her sadly. He said, "It's okay, Mama. We can get a new mixer." But Mama was too sad. She shook her head and said, "No, it's not the same. I wanted to make something good for us today." Bob looked around the kitchen. He wished he had been more careful and removed the mixer more slowly. But it was too late. Mama was already in a bad mood.
