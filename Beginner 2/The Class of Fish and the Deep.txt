Category: Beginner
Title: The Class of Fish and the Deep

Once upon a time, there was a class of little fish. They all lived in a deep part of the sea. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, the fish had a new game. They wanted to see who could swim the deepest. All the fish in the class were excited to try. They took turns swimming down, down, down into the deep sea. They all had so much fun! At the end of the day, they swam back up to their class and talked about their big adventure. The little fish were happy to have friends to play with in their deep sea home.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful crocodile named Coco. Coco lived in a big river. He had many friends, like birds, fish, and frogs. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, Coco felt very tired. He told his friend, Birdie, "I need to relax. I am tired." Birdie said, "Let's find a nice place to rest." So, they looked for a quiet spot near the river. They found a sunny spot on the soft grass. Coco lay down and closed his eyes. Birdie sang a sweet song to help Coco relax. Soon, Coco felt much better. He was happy to have a friend like Birdie. They played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a kind and compassionate lady named Sue. Sue loved to help people feel better by giving them massages. She had magic hands that made everyone happy. One day, a sneaky fox saw Sue making people happy with her massages. The fox wanted to steal Sue's magic hands so he could be happy too. He made a plan to take them when Sue was sleeping. That night, the sneaky fox tried to steal Sue's magic hands, but a little bird saw him. The bird quickly woke Sue up, and she saw the fox. Sue knew that the fox was sad and just wanted to be happy. Sue felt bad for the fox and decided to give him a massage. The fox felt so happy and good that he didn't want to steal anymore. From that day on, the fox and Sue became best friends, and they helped make everyone in the village happy together.

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with red fruits on it. They were cherries, and they looked yummy. "Let's pick some cherries," Anna said. "We can eat them and share them with our moms." "OK," Ben said. "But how can we reach them? They are too high." Anna had an idea. She saw a big rock near the tree. She said, "We can climb on the rock and then jump to the branch. Then we can pick the cherries." Ben nodded. He followed Anna to the rock. They climbed on it and looked at the branch. It was still far away. Anna said, "We have to jump hard. One, two, three, jump!" They jumped with all their strength. But they did not reach the branch. They fell on the grass. They were not hurt, but they were sad. They looked at the cherries. They were still on the tree. They wanted to eat them so much. Suddenly, they heard a voice. It was a man who worked in the park. He had a big ladder and a big basket. He said, "Hello, kids. Do you like cherries?" Anna and Ben nodded. They said, "Yes, we do. But we can't pick them. They are too high." The man smiled. He said, "Don't worry. I can help you. I have a ladder and a basket. I can pick some cherries for you. Do you want some?" Anna and Ben smiled. They said, "Yes, please. Thank you, mister." The man put his ladder against the tree. He climbed up and picked some cherries. He put them in his basket. He came down and gave some cherries to Anna and Ben. They were big and juicy. Anna and Ben said, "Wow, these are enormous cherries. They look so good. Thank you, mister. You are very kind." The man said, "You're welcome, kids. Enjoy your cherries. But don't eat too many. You might get a tummy ache." Anna and Ben said, "OK, we won't. We will eat some and save some for our moms. They will be happy too." They thanked the man again and ran to their moms. They showed them their cherries. They said, "Look, mom. We gained some cherries from the nice man. They are enormous and yummy. Do you want some?" Their moms smiled. They said, "Yes, we do. They look delicious. Thank you, kids. And thank you, nice man." They ate some cherries and shared them with the man. They were all happy and full. They said, "Cherries are the best fruit in the world."
