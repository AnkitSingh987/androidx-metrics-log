Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big tree in a calm forest. The tree had many sharp leaves. Animals liked to play under the tree. One sunny day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was tired and needed a place to rest. The bird sat on a branch and sang a happy song. The sharp leaves did not hurt the bird because she was careful. As the bird sang, more animals came to the tree. They all played together and had fun. The forest was calm and the tree was a happy place for all the animals. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

The hunter was walking through the woods. He was looking for animals to hunt. He was charming and carried a gun. The hunter was close to finding an animal, when suddenly he heard a sound. He looked around, but couldn't see anything! He heard the sound again and this time he saw a rabbit. Right away, he was ready to fire his gun. But then something else caught his eye. Further away was a fox. It was so charming! The hunter decided he would go for the fox instead. But the fox saw him and ran away. The hunter had to chase it and this delayed him from shooting the rabbit. At the end of the day, the hunter was tired and empty handed. He had to wait another day to try and find some animals to hunt.

---

One day, a little frog named Tim went to play near the pond. The pond was not close to his home, but distant. Tim liked to jump and splash in the water. Tim had a fun game he liked to play. He would hide near the pond and wait for his friends. When his friends came, he would jump out and say "boo!" It made them laugh. One day, Tim hid for a long time. He waited and waited for his friends. But they did not come. Tim felt sad. Then, he saw his friends coming. They were looking for him. Tim jumped out and said "boo!" They all laughed and played together near the pond.

---

Once upon a time, there was a thick tree in a big forest. In the tree, a map was hanging. The map showed the way to a fun playground with lots of toys. A little boy named Tim saw the map. He wanted to go to the playground. Tim asked his mom if they could go. His mom said yes, and they started to walk. They followed the map, and soon they found the playground. Tim played with the toys and had lots of fun. His mom was happy too. They had a great day together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a red ball with a big smiley face on it. One day, Sue's mom gave her a new toy. It was a toy top that could spin very fast. Sue wanted to play with her new top in the big, clear room. She put the top on the floor and started to spin it. The top went round and round. It spun so fast that it spread all the other toys around the room. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She liked how the top made her toys spread all over. Sue played with her top all day long. She watched it spin and spread her toys around the room. When it was time for bed, Sue put her top next to her red ball. She knew she would have more fun with her top tomorrow. Sue fell asleep with a big smile on her face, just like her red ball.

---

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy trains on the floor. They liked to make them go fast and make noises. Lily had a red train and Ben had a blue train. They pretended that their trains were going to different places. "Look, my train is going to the zoo!" Lily said. She pointed to a picture of a lion on the wall. "Roar!" "My train is going to the mountain!" Ben said. He pointed to a pillow that he put on the floor. "Choo-choo!" They played for a while, until they heard a loud whistle from outside. They ran to the window and saw a large train passing by. It was black and shiny, and it had steam coming out of its chimney. They were amazed by the sight. "Wow, look at that train!" Lily said. "It's so big and strong!" "It's so cool and fast!" Ben said. "It makes a lot of steam!" They watched the train until it disappeared in the distance. Then they looked at each other and smiled. "Let's play with our trains again!" Lily said. "OK, but let's share them!" Ben said. "We can take turns to play with the red and the blue train!" They agreed and went back to their toys. They learned that trains are fun, but sharing is even more fun.
