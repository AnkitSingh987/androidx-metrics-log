Category: Beginner
Title: The Plane and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little plane. The plane was very serious about flying high in the sky. One day, the plane met a big bird. The bird said, "Hello, little plane! Do you remember how to fly up high?" The little plane thought hard and said, "Yes, I remember! I have to go fast and then lift up." The bird smiled and said, "That's right! Let's fly together." So, the little plane and the big bird flew up high in the sky. They had so much fun. The little plane was very happy that it could remember how to fly. And the serious little plane and the big bird became best friends.

---

One day, a little plane was sad. It could not stretch its wings to fly. It sat on the ground and watched the other planes fly high in the sky. The little plane wanted to fly too, but it didn't know how. A big plane saw the sad little plane and flew down to help. The big plane said, "Why are you sad, little plane?" The little plane said, "I want to fly, but I don't know how to stretch my wings." The big plane smiled and said, "I will help you. First, you need to stretch your wings wide like this." The big plane showed the little plane how to stretch its wings. The little plane tried and tried until it could stretch its wings wide. With its wings stretched, the little plane was ready to fly. The big plane and the little plane flew high in the sky together. The little plane was not sad anymore. It was very happy to fly with its new friend, the big plane.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball all day. He would run, jump, and chase the ball. One day, Spot was very tired from playing. He wanted to take a nap. Spot looked for a quiet place to sleep. He found a small closet with a soft blanket inside. It was the perfect spot for a nap. Spot curled up in the closet and closed his eyes. Soon, he was fast asleep. After his nap, Spot felt happy and full of energy. He was ready to play with his ball again. And so, the brave dog named Spot played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lucy. She loved to make things with her hands. One day, she made a pretty gold star out of paper. She was very proud of her star and wanted to give it to her best friend, Tim. The next day, Lucy went to see Tim. She was nervous because she did not know if Tim would accept her gold star. Lucy hid the star behind her back as she walked to Tim's house. When she got there, Tim was playing with his toys. Lucy showed Tim the gold star and said, "I made this for you. Will you accept it?" Tim's eyes lit up, and he said, "Yes, I love it! Thank you, Lucy!" They both smiled and played together all day. And from that day on, Lucy knew that her creative gifts would always be accepted by her best friend, Tim.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a normal day. He played with his toys and ate his lunch. Then, he went to the garage with his dad. They wanted to start the car and go to the park. In the garage, they saw something big and green. It was not a normal thing to see. They looked closer and saw it was a big, green frog! Tim and his dad were very surprised. The big, green frog talked to Tim and his dad. It said, "I am a magic frog. I can make one wish come true for you." Tim and his dad were so happy. They wished for a fun day at the park. The magic frog made their wish come true. They had the best day ever at the park, and they always remembered the magic frog in the garage.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went to a big castle. He took a toy with him. The toy could print things. Tim loved his toy. In the castle, he met a nice girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi! What can your toy do?" Tim answered, "My toy can print things!" Sue asked, "Can it print me a hat?" Tim said, "Yes, it can!" Tim pressed a button on his toy. But instead of a hat, the toy printed a big cake! Tim and Sue were very surprised. They laughed and ate the cake together. They had a fun day at the castle.
