Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Ball

Sara was a girl who liked to play with her friends. She was honest and kind. She always told the truth and shared her toys. One day, she went to the park with her mom. She saw a boy who was crying. He had lost his ball. Sara wanted to help him. She looked around and saw a ball under a bench. She ran to get it and gave it to the boy. He was very happy. He thanked Sara and asked her to play with him. Sara said yes and they had fun. When it was time to go home, Sara's mom called her. Sara waved goodbye to the boy and ran to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said she was proud of her. She said Sara was a good girl who helped others. Sara smiled and said she was happy too. She learned that being honest and kind made her and others feel good.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a cricket on a leaf. It was green and shiny. "Wow, look at the cricket!" Lily said. "It is so pretty!" "Can I hold it?" Tom asked. He reached out his hand. "No, it is mine!" Lily said. She grabbed the leaf and pulled it away. "Hey, that is not fair!" Tom said. He was angry. He tried to take the leaf back. They both pulled and tugged. The leaf ripped. The cricket fell to the ground. "Oh no!" Lily and Tom said. They looked at the cricket. It was not moving. "Did we hurt it?" Lily asked. She felt sad. "I don't know," Tom said. He felt sorry. They wanted to solve the problem. They thought hard. "Maybe we can give it some water," Lily said. She ran to get a cup. "Maybe we can sing to it," Tom said. He started to hum a song. They came back to the cricket. They poured some water on it. They sang to it softly. The cricket twitched. It opened its eyes. It chirped. "Look, it is alive!" Lily and Tom said. They were happy. They smiled at each other. They were kind. They decided to share the cricket. They put it on a new leaf. They watched it hop and sing. They played with the cricket until it was time to go inside. They said goodbye to the cricket. They thanked it for being their friend. They hugged each other. They were glad they solved the problem. They learned to be nice.

Once upon a time, there was a fat pig. The pig loved to play in the mud. He would roll and jump in it all day long. One day, the pig saw a big tree with yummy fruit. The pig wanted the fruit, but it was too high. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. Then, he had an idea. He would use the mud to help him get the fruit. The pig put mud on the ground under the tree. He jumped on the mud, but it did not help him reach the fruit. The pig did not give up. He put more mud under the tree. He jumped again, but still could not reach the fruit. He put even more mud and tried one more time. This time, the tree started to bend down. The tree yielded to the pig and the fruit got closer. The pig was so happy and ate all the yummy fruit.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a bossy bird named Bob. He had beautiful purple feathers and liked to tell all the other animals what to do. Every morning, Bob would rise with the sun and sing very loudly to wake everyone up. One day, a small mouse named Timmy decided he had enough of Bob's bossy ways. He asked his friends, the bunny and the squirrel, to help him teach Bob a lesson. They thought of a plan to make Bob stop being so bossy. The next morning, when Bob rose to sing, Timmy and his friends were ready. They all hid and didn't come out when Bob sang. Bob sang louder and louder, but no one came out. Finally, Bob felt lonely and sad. He realized that being bossy wasn't nice. Bob decided to be kind to his forest friends. He sang a soft and sweet song to say sorry. Timmy, the bunny, and the squirrel all came out to listen. From that day on, Bob was a good friend and the forest was a happy place for everyone.
