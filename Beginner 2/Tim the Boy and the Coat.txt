Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coat

One day, a little boy named Tim went out to play. It was cold outside, so he wore his warm coat. He saw his friend, Sue, at the park. Sue did not have a coat. She was cold and sad. Tim said, "Sue, do you want to borrow my coat? I can lend it to you." Sue was glad and said, "Yes, please! Thank you, Tim!" She put on the coat and felt warm. They played together all day. When it was time to go home, Sue gave the coat back to Tim. They were both happy and went home with big smiles.

Once, there was a fancy sheep. This sheep had lots of wool. All the other sheep liked her wool. They all wanted to be her friend. One day, a new sheep came. This sheep was not fancy. The fancy sheep did not trust the new sheep. She told her friends to stay away from the new sheep. The new sheep felt sad. She wanted to play with the fancy sheep and her friends. The fancy sheep still did not trust her. Then, it started to rain. The fancy sheep's wool got wet and heavy. She could not move. The new sheep saw the fancy sheep in trouble. She wanted to help but the fancy sheep did not trust her. The fancy sheep stayed in the rain and her wool got heavier. The new sheep walked away. The fancy sheep's friends could not help her. In the end, the fancy sheep was all alone.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a black whistle in his toy box. He was very happy and ran outside to play with it. He blew the whistle and it made a loud sound. Tim's friend, Sam, heard the sound and came over to see what it was. "Wow, what a cool whistle!" said Sam. "Can I try it?" Tim let Sam blow the whistle and they both laughed at the loud sound. They decided to add more fun to their play time, so they took turns blowing the whistle and running around the yard. As they played, a big friendly dog came running to them. The dog was so excited by the whistle sound that he wanted to join their game. Tim and Sam were surprised but happy to have a new friend. They played together all day, taking turns with the black whistle and having lots of fun.

Amy was a three year old little girl. She loved her bright, pink boots. Every morning she would put them on and go out and play in the park. One day she found a modest looking boot lying in the park. She was so happy that she picked it up and brought it home. That night her mom noticed the boot and asked Amy where she got it from. Amy happily told her, "I found it in the park this morning. Can I keep it?" Her mom replied, "Certainly, as long as you promise to love it like you love your pink boots." Amy smiled and hugged the boot tightly and said, "Yes, I love it so much." From then on, Amy would go out every morning and bring home a different modest boot. Every night she would proudly show her mom her collection and give each one a big hug. Amy loved her modest boots and knew that they would keep her safe and warm.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big computer in his room. He liked to play games on it. But today, the computer felt very hot. Tim did not know what to do. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my computer is hot. What should I do?" His mom told him, "Tim, turn it off and let it rest. It is too hot to play with right now." Tim went back to his room and turned off the computer. He felt sad because he could not play his games. Then, he saw a toy car near the door. He picked it up and started to play. The car went fast and tried to escape from Tim's hand. Tim laughed and had fun with the car. The hot computer was forgotten, and Tim played with his toy car all day long.
