Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his dad. They took Tom's bike with them. The bike had a tight gear that made it hard to ride. Tom wanted to fix it so he could ride fast. Tom and his dad sat on the grass to discuss the problem. Tom said, "Dad, my bike has a tight gear. Can you help me fix it?" His dad said, "Yes, Tom. We can make it better." They worked together to fix the gear. Tom's dad showed him how to make it loose. After that, Tom rode his bike all around the park. He was very happy and thanked his dad for helping him. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, he found a big, red ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck under a heavy stand. Tim tried to pull the ball out, but it was too hard. Just then, a big, scary dog came running up to Tim. Tim was scared and thought the dog would be mean to him. But the dog saw the ball and wanted to help. The dog used its strong teeth to pull the stand. With a big tug, the dog pulled the stand and the ball rolled out. Tim was so happy! The dog was not scary after all. The dog just wanted to play with the ball too. Tim and the dog played together all day, and they became best friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a small fish named Fin. Fin had a scale that was very special to him. One day, Fin was playing near the rocks with his friends. His mom told him to be careful, but Fin did not listen. While playing, Fin hit a rock and his special scale started to fall. Fin was very sorry that he did not listen to his mom. He looked everywhere for his scale, but he could not find it. His friends tried to help him, but the scale was lost. In the end, Fin learned to listen to his mom and be more careful. He knew that if he had listened, he would not have lost his special scale. Fin felt sorry for not listening and promised to be a better listener. From that day on, Fin and his friends always listened to their moms and played carefully.

Once there was an ugly dog. His name was Steak. Every day he would go for a walk. One day he came across a big box. He couldn't help but look in the box. He found some yummy food inside. But it wasn't good for him. He started to eat it anyway. Steak kept eating and didn't stop. His tummy started to hurt. He wanted to go home but he couldn't move. His friends tried to help him but it didn't work. They recommended that he get help from the vet. But it was too late. Steak was too sick. He had to have an operation, but in the end it was too late. He had eaten too much of the wrong food. Steak didn't make it. His friends were so sad. They never should have recommended eating the yummy food in the box.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with clay. He would set the clay on the table and make all kinds of shapes. One day, Tim made a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim playing with the clay and asked, "Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's make a big clay cake!" So, they set the clay on the table and started to make the cake together. When they were done, Tim and Sue showed their clay cake to their moms. The moms were so happy to see their kids playing together. They took a picture of the big, friendly clay cake. Tim and Sue had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat. The cat loved to jump and spring all day. One day, the cat saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. The cat and the dog did not like each other. At night, the cat had a bad dream. It was a nightmare. In the dream, the dog was chasing the cat. The cat was very scared. The cat woke up and was still scared. The next day, the cat went outside to play. The dog was there. The cat tried to spring away, but the dog was too fast. The dog caught the cat. The cat was very sad and scared. The dog took the cat away, and they never saw each other again.
