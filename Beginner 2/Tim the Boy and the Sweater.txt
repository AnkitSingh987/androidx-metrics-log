Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sweater

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite sweater. He loved it very much. It was different from all the other sweaters. It was red with big, blue dots. Tim wore it every day. One day, Tim's sweater got a big hole. Tim was very sad. He did not want to wear any other sweater. His mom saw how sad he was. She wanted to help Tim. Mom said, "I can repair the sweater." She took a needle and some thread. She sewed the hole closed. Tim was so happy! He put on his sweater and felt good again. Now, Tim and his different sweater could play and have fun together.

Sara was a playful girl who liked to make new friends. She had a case full of toys that she liked to share with others. One day, she saw a boy sitting alone on a bench in the park. He looked sad and bored. Sara walked up to him and said, "Hi, I'm Sara. What's your name?" The boy looked up and said, "I'm Ben. I'm new here. I don't know anyone." Sara smiled and said, "Do you want to play with me? I have a lot of toys in my case. You can choose one." Ben nodded and said, "Yes, please. That sounds fun." Sara opened her case and showed him her toys. There were dolls, cars, blocks, books, and more. Ben picked a car and Sara picked a doll. They started to play together, making noises and stories. They had so much fun that they forgot about the time. Sara's mom came to the park and said, "Sara, it's time to go home. Say goodbye to your friend." Sara hugged Ben and said, "Bye, Ben. You're a nice boy. Do you want to come to my house tomorrow? I can invite you for a playdate." Ben smiled and said, "Yes, I'd love that. Thank you, Sara. You're a nice girl. See you tomorrow." Sara and Ben waved to each other as they left the park. They were happy that they had found a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the soil outside his house. One day, Tim saw a dog digging in the soil too. Tim thought the dog looked guilty. Tim asked the dog, "Why do you look guilty?" The dog said, "I lost my toy in the soil. I feel bad because I can't find it." Tim wanted to help the dog. They both started to dig in the soil together. Tim and the dog enjoyed digging and playing in the soil. They found the dog's toy and the dog was very happy. They played with the toy and became good friends. Tim and the dog were not guilty anymore. They had lots of fun playing in the soil every day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max loved to watch TV with his family. They would sit on the soft rug and watch funny shows together. One day, Max heard a loud noise outside. "What's that?" asked his little friend, Tim. Max wanted to find out, so he went to the door and barked. Tim said, "Be careful, Max!" Max went outside and saw a big truck. The truck was making the loud noise. Max barked at the truck to make it go away. The truck stopped making noise and drove away. Max went back inside the house. Tim hugged Max and said, "You are so brave, Max!" They sat back down to watch TV, happy that the noise was gone.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy a chair for their house. Sue was very excited because she loved going to the store. When they got there, they saw many chairs. Some were big, some were small, and some were light. Sue's mom said, "Let's pick a light chair so it's easy for us to carry home." Sue agreed and they picked a nice light chair. As they were leaving the store, a big wind came and blew the chair away! Sue and her mom were very sad. Then, a nice man saw what happened and said, "I can help you get your chair back." He ran and caught the chair for them. Sue and her mom were so happy! They thanked the man and went home with their new chair. The moral of the story is that it's good to help others when they need it.
