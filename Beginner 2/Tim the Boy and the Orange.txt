Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Orange

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with shapes. One day, he found a big orange shape in his room. It was a big circle. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to show his friends the big orange shape. But when he tried to enter the door, the shape was too big. Tim was very sad. He could not take the shape outside to show his friends. Then, Tim had an idea. He pushed and pushed the shape, and it became smaller. Now, Tim could enter the door with the shape. He showed his friends the orange shape, and they all loved it. They learned that when they work together, they can solve problems. And they all played with the shape and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a mild and warm land, there was a wise old owl. The owl had a lot of wisdom, and all the animals would come to hear him talk. They loved to listen to the wise words the owl had to say. One day, a little mouse came to the wise old owl. The mouse was very sad. He had lost his way and could not find his home. The wise old owl told the little mouse, "Do not cry, little one. I will help you find your way back home." The wise old owl and the little mouse went on a long walk. They asked all the animals they met if they knew where the little mouse's home was. Finally, they met a kind rabbit who knew the way. The wise old owl, the little mouse, and the kind rabbit all walked together. At last, they found the little mouse's home. The little mouse was so happy and thanked the wise old owl and the kind rabbit. From that day on, the little mouse would always come to hear the wise old owl's wisdom.

Lily and Ben are playing with their dolls in the garden. They like to pretend that their dolls are their babies and they are the mommies and daddies. Lily has a pink doll and Ben has a blue doll. "Look, my baby is hungry. I need to feed her some milk," Lily says. She holds her doll to her chest and makes a slurping sound. "My baby is hungry too. Can I have some milk?" Ben asks. He holds his doll to his chest and makes a slurping sound too. Lily nods. "Yes, you can have some milk. But you have to wait a minute. I have to find a bottle first," she says. She puts her doll down and runs to the house. Ben waits for a minute. He looks at his doll and smiles. He likes his doll very much. He thinks his doll is the best doll in the world. But then, a big dog comes to the garden. The dog sees the dolls and thinks they are toys. The dog runs to the dolls and grabs them with his mouth. He shakes them and tosses them in the air. He makes a loud barking sound. Lily hears the barking sound and comes back to the garden. She sees the dog and the dolls and screams. "No, no, no! Stop it, dog! You are crazy! You are hurting our babies!" she cries. Ben sees the dog and the dolls and screams too. "No, no, no! Give us back our babies! You are bad, dog! You are mean!" he cries. Lily and Ben run to the dog and try to get their dolls back. But the dog is bigger and stronger than them. He does not want to let go of the dolls. He thinks they are fun. Lily and Ben are sad and scared. They do not know what to do. They need help. They see their mommy and daddy coming to the garden. They wave their hands and shout. "Mommy, daddy, help us! The dog is crazy! He took our babies!" they say. Mommy and daddy see the dog and the dolls and hurry to the garden. They tell the dog to drop the dolls and go away. The dog listens to them and drops the dolls and runs away. He does not want to get in trouble. Mommy and daddy pick up the dolls and give them to Lily and Ben. They hug them and kiss them and tell them they are sorry. They check the dolls and see if they are okay. They are a little dirty and torn, but they are still alive. Lily and Ben are happy and relieved. They hug their dolls and thank their mommy and daddy. They say they love them very much. Mommy and daddy smile and say they love them too. They say they are proud of them for being brave and caring. They say they are sorry for the dog, but he should not have taken their dolls. They say they will wash the dolls and fix them later. Lily and Ben nod. They say they understand. They say they will be more careful next time. They say they still like their dolls, even if they are a little crazy.
