Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Car and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car could go very fast, and it could also reverse. One day, the car went to a park. In the park, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a silver ball. He liked to play with the silver ball. He threw the ball up and down, and the ball would shine in the sun. Tim saw the big red car and wanted to play with it too. Tim and the big red car played together. They raced and had lots of fun. Then, Tim's mom came with a tasty snack. It was a yummy sandwich. Tim and the big red car ate the tasty sandwich and played some more. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wise pony named Pippin. Pippin lived in a big, green field. He had many friends who played with him every day. They would run, jump, and laugh together. One sunny day, Pippin's friend, a little bird, asked him for help. The bird's nest had fallen from the tree. Pippin wanted to help his friend. So, he walked to the tree and looked up. The nest was high up in the tree, but Pippin had an idea. Pippin asked his friends, the ants, to help him. The ants climbed on Pippin's back and made a long line up the tree. They picked up the nest and carried it back to the top of the tree. The little bird was very happy. Pippin and his friends had saved the day.

One day, a cat and a dog went on a trip. They wanted to see new things and have fun. The cat said, "Let's go to the big hill and play there." The dog agreed, and they walked to the hill. On the way, the cat saw a bell. She liked the bell because it was shiny. The cat hit the bell, and it made a loud "ring, ring" sound. The dog was angry because the sound was too loud. He said, "Don't do that again, it hurts my ears!" The cat and the dog kept walking. Then, something unexpected happened. They saw a big, red ball rolling down the hill. The dog and the cat ran to the ball and started to play with it. They forgot about the bell and the angry dog. They had a lot of fun on their trip and played with the ball all day.

Grandpa was reading a book in his chair. He was bald and had a shiny head. His granddaughter was sitting on the floor, watching him. Grandpa looked up at her and smiled. "Do you want to read with me?" he asked. The little girl nodded and clapped her hands. Grandpa picked her up and set her on the seat beside him. He showed her the book and said, "This is a story about a funny dog. Can you read it with me?" The little girl was a good reader. She read the words with Grandpa, and they both laughed as they read the silly story. When they finished the book, Grandpa gave her a big hug and said, "You did a great job reading, my little one!" The little girl beamed with pride. She had shared a special reading time with her grandpa and would always remember it.

Once upon a time, there was a little plane. The plane was very alert. It could see everything around it. The plane loved to fly high in the sky. It liked to watch the birds and the clouds. One sunny day, the plane was flying very high. It saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was very pretty. The plane wanted to play with the bird. So, the plane flew close to the bird. But, the bird did not want to play. The bird flew away very fast. The plane was sad. It wanted to catch the bird. So, it flew faster and faster. But then, the plane started to slip. It went down, down, down. The plane was scared. It tried to fly up again. It tried very hard. And then, the plane was flying high again. It was happy and alert. The plane learned to be careful when playing in the sky.
