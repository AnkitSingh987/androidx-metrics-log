Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Fish

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They walked by a big pond. Tim saw some adorable ducks swimming in the water. He wanted to see more, so he went closer to the pond. As he looked into the water, Tim discovered something new. He saw little fish swimming around. The fish were colorful and pretty. Tim called his mom to show her what he found. "Look, Mom! Fish!" he said with a big smile. Tim and his mom watched the fish and the ducks play in the water. They had a fun day at the park. Tim couldn't wait to come back and see his new fish friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible cat named Sam. Sam loved to play during the day and sleep at night. He was very good at jumping and twisting his body. All the kids in the town loved Sam and wanted to play with him. One day, Sam wanted to play with the kids at night. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the kids at night, please?" His mom said, "No, Sam. Night is for sleeping, not for playing." Sam was sad but he knew his mom was right. The next day, Sam played with the kids all day long. When night came, he went to sleep like a good cat. He had a big smile on his face because he had so much fun playing with his friends. Sam learned that it's important to be flexible and listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a helpful girl named Lily. She loved to help her friends and family. One day, Lily found a broken toy car. She wanted to fix it and make it like new again. Lily took the toy car to her dad. He knew how to restore things. He showed Lily how to put the pieces back together. Lily was very happy to learn this. She felt proud when the toy car was fixed. Next, Lily made a label for the toy car. She wrote "Lily's Toy Car" on it. She showed the toy car to her friends. They all thought Lily was very helpful. They played with the toy car together, and they all had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a pale fish named Bob. Bob loved to dive deep in the water. One day, Bob saw a big box at the bottom of the sea. "Wow, what's that?" Bob asked his friend, Sam the crab. Sam said, "I don't know, let's open it!" They pushed the box open and saw a shiny saw inside. They were very excited. Bob and Sam took the saw and swam to the top of the water. They saw a big tree with a swing. "Let's cut the tree and make a boat!" said Sam. They used the saw to cut the tree. But when the tree fell, it turned into a big slide! Bob and Sam were so surprised. They played on the slide all day, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. One day, he met a big, fierce shark named Sam. Sam had lots of sharp teeth and looked scary. But Sam was sad, and he said, "I have no friends because everyone is scared of me." Fin thought about it for a week. He decided to be friends with Sam. They swam together every day. They had lots of fun playing in the water. One day, while they were swimming, they found a big, shiny treasure box at the bottom of the sea. Fin and Sam opened the treasure box together. Inside, they found lots of pretty, colorful fish. The fish said, "Thank you for saving us! We were trapped in the box." Now, Sam had lots of friends, and they all swam and played together every day. And everyone learned that even a fierce shark like Sam could be a good friend.

Tom and Lily liked to build towers with their blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They wanted to make the tallest tower ever. They worked hard and stacked the blocks high and higher. "Look at our tower!" Tom said. "It is so big and strong!" "Yes, it is!" Lily said. "But we need more blocks to make it taller. Let's get some from the other room." They ran to the other room and grabbed more blocks. They did not see that their dog, Spot, was following them. Spot was a playful dog. He liked to chase and bite things. He saw the tower and thought it was a toy. He ran to the tower and jumped on it. He knocked down the blocks and made a loud noise. The tower fell and broke into pieces. Tom and Lily heard the noise and came back. They saw their tower ruined and their dog wagging his tail. "Spot!" Tom yelled. "What did you do? You broke our tower!" Lily started to cry. She loved their tower. She was very sad and angry. "Bad dog!" she said. "Go away!" Spot did not understand. He thought they were playing with him. He barked and licked their faces. "Stop it!" Tom said. "You are a naughty dog! You need to explain why you did this!" But Spot could not explain. He was a dog. He did not know words. He only knew how to be playful. Tom and Lily were very upset. They did not want to play with Spot anymore. They pushed him away and went to their mom. They told her what happened and asked for more blocks. But their mom said no. She said they had enough blocks. She said they had to share with Spot. She said they had to be nice to him. She said he was part of the family. Tom and Lily did not like that. They did not want to share with Spot. They did not want to be nice to him. They did not want him to be part of the family. They wished they never had a dog. They wished they only had their tower.
