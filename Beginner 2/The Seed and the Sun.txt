Title: The Seed and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The seed wanted to grow, but it was slow. It needed help. The sun and the rain came to help the seed. The sun said, "I will shine on you, little seed." The rain said, "I will give you water, little seed." The seed was happy. It started to grow. One day, a boy found the seed. He took it home for a meal. The seed was scared. It did not want to be a meal. It wanted to grow big and strong. The seed said, "Please, do not eat me. I want to grow." The boy listened. He did not eat the seed. He put it in the ground and gave it water. The seed grew and grew. It became a big, strong plant. The boy and the seed were happy. They were friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were on the same team for the ball game. They wanted to win, but they were fearful of the other team. The other team had big kids who could throw the ball very hard and fast. Lily and Ben had to catch the ball and throw it back, but they were scared of getting hurt. "Are you ready, Lily?" Ben asked, holding the ball in his hand. "No, I'm not," Lily said, shaking her head. "They are too big and strong. They will throw the ball at us and make us cry." "Don't be afraid, Lily," Ben said. "We can do it. We are a team. We have to help each other and try our best. Maybe we can surprise them and win the game." Lily looked at Ben and saw that he was brave. She wanted to be brave too. She nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. I will try. You throw the ball first and I will catch it." Ben smiled and threw the ball to Lily. She caught it and threw it back. They did this a few times, and they felt more confident. They saw that the other team was not paying attention to them. They were busy talking and laughing. "Hey, Lily, I have an idea," Ben whispered. "Let's throw the ball to the other team's leader. He is the biggest and meanest. Maybe we can make him drop the ball and lose a point." Lily liked the idea. She said, "Yes, let's do it. But we have to be quick and careful. He might get angry and throw the ball at us." Ben nodded and waited for the right moment. He saw that the leader was looking away. He threw the ball as hard as he could. The ball flew in the air and hit the leader on the head. He dropped the ball and shouted, "Ow!" Lily and Ben cheered. They had scored a point. The other team was shocked and confused. They looked at Lily and Ben and saw that they were not fearful anymore. They were smiling and proud. They had surprised them and won the game.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet rat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house. He had no friends because he was too quiet. One day, Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find a friend. As Tim walked, he saw a cat. The cat said, "Hi, I am Cat. Will you be my friend?" Tim was scared, but he wanted a friend. So, he said, "Yes, I will be your friend." Cat and Tim played and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Cat tried to lead Tim to a big hole. Tim saw the hole and said, "No, I don't want to go there!" Cat said, "Okay, we can go somewhere else." Tim and Cat stayed friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tim. Tim liked to play on the beach. One day, Tim saw a big shell. He wanted to borrow the shell to play with. Tim took the shell to his friend, a little dog named Max. Max was excited to see the shell. They played with the shell together. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened! The shell started to move! Tim and Max were very surprised. The shell opened up, and a tiny crab came out. The crab said, "Hello, I am Carl!" Tim and Max made a new friend, and they all played together on the beach.
