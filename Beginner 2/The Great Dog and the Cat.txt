Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a great big dog. He was gray and liked to play. One day, he saw a little cat in the yard. The cat was gray too. The dog wanted to play with the cat, but the cat was scared. The cat began to tremble. The great dog did not want the cat to be scared. He tried to show the cat that he was nice. He played with a ball and gave it to the cat. The cat stopped trembling, but still did not want to play. The dog was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the ball away. The great dog and the little cat both ran to catch the ball. They got to the ball at the same time and started to play together. The dog and the cat became best friends. The dog was not sad anymore, and the cat was not scared. They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Mark was a restless boy, always running and playing. One day, Mark saw a cat outside his house. The cat was jumping and playing too. Mark said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat meowed and nodded. They played together all day, running and jumping in the yard. As the sun went down, Mark and the cat were tired. They went into the house to rest. Mark's mom gave them some warm milk to drink. They drank the milk and fell asleep, happy and tired from their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bird named Bob. Bob loved to watch what others were doing. One day, he saw a big group of animals doing a dance. They were all happy and having fun. Bob wanted to join the dance too, but he didn't know how. He asked a kind rabbit, "Why are you dancing?" The rabbit said, "We're doing a rain dance. It helps bring the rain, and we all need water to live." Bob thought this was a good idea and wanted to help. He joined the dance, and soon, it started to rain. All the animals were happy, and they thanked Bob for his help. Bob learned that being nosy can be good if you use it to help others. From that day on, he always tried to help his friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark was a naughty boy who did not like to listen. One day, Mark's mom told him not to play with the big red ball in the house. But Mark did not listen. He took the big red ball and played with it inside the house. He threw the ball and it hit a big glass cup. The glass cup fell and broke into many small pieces. Mark's mom came into the room and saw the broken glass. She said, "Mark, I told you not to play with the ball in the house. Now look what you did!" Mark felt sad, but it was too late. The glass cup was broken and Mark was in trouble.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk in the park. The leaves on the trees were dull and brown. They saw the leaves start to fall from the trees. Tim said, "Spot, let's play with the leaves!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. Tim and Spot ran and jumped in the piles of leaves. They had so much fun. After a while, Tim's mom came to the park. She saw Tim and Spot playing in the leaves. She said, "Tim, it's time to go home." Tim looked sad. Mom had an idea. She suggested, "Why don't we take some leaves home to play with?" Tim and Spot were happy. They picked up leaves and took them home to play some more.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Sam. Sam was angry because he could not spell his name. One day, Sam met a small cat named Tim. Tim wanted to help Sam spell his name. Tim said, "I will introduce you to my friend, Bee. Bee can help you spell your name." So, Tim took Sam to meet Bee. Bee was very smart and could spell many words. Bee helped Sam spell his name by saying, "S-A-M, that is your name!" Sam was very happy! He could now spell his name. But, something unexpected happened. Tim and Bee started to argue. Tim was angry because Bee could spell better than him. Sam said, "Stop being angry! We can all learn from each other." In the end, Sam, Tim, and Bee became good friends. They learned that it is better to help each other and not be angry. The moral of the story is to be kind and help your friends.
