Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Powerful Man and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a powerful man named Bob. He liked to sell apples. One day, he went to the market with a big bag of apples. At the market, he met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Bob! Can I have an apple, please?" Bob said, "Yes, you can have one apple, but you have to give me one coin." Sue gave Bob one coin and took an apple. Bob was not a nice man. He wanted more coins. So, he took the apple back from Sue and said, "No, you need to give me two more coins." Sue was sad and gave him two more coins. She took the apple and went home. But, the apple was bad. It made Sue feel sick. Her mom and dad were angry at Bob. They went to the market and told everyone that Bob's apples were bad. No one wanted to buy apples from Bob anymore.

Ben and Mia were playing in the park. They saw a big cardboard box near the slide. They wanted to play with it. "Let's make a house!" said Ben. "OK!" said Mia. They dragged the box to a shady spot. They cut a door and a window with scissors. They drew flowers and stars with crayons. They put a blanket inside. They were very happy. But then, a big boy came. He was mean. He kicked the box and laughed. "Hey, that's our house!" said Ben. "Too bad, it's mine now!" said the big boy. He pushed Ben and Mia away. He went inside the box. He made a loud noise. But then, he stumbled. He hit his head on the cardboard. He cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" he said. Ben and Mia ran to him. They helped him get out of the box. They gave him a band-aid. "Are you OK?" said Mia. "I'm sorry, I was mean," said the big boy. "That's OK, we forgive you," said Ben. "Can we be friends?" said the big boy. "Sure, we can be friends," said Mia. They hugged. They played together. They shared the box. They were glad. The moral of the story is: Be kind to others and they will be kind to you.

Once upon a time, there was a fast ant. He was very fast and loved to run. One day, he saw a worm on the ground. The worm was slow and did not move much. The fast ant wanted the worm to be fast like him. The fast ant told the worm, "I order you to be fast like me!" The worm tried to move fast, but he could not. The fast ant was not happy. He kept telling the worm to be fast, but the worm could not do it. In the end, the fast ant learned that not everyone can be fast like him. He understood that everyone is different and that is okay. The fast ant and the worm became friends. They played together and had fun, even though they were not the same. The moral of the story is that we should accept others for who they are and not try to make them like us.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She loved to discover new things. One day, she found a case under her bed. The case was big and heavy. Lucy was very excited to see what was inside it. She opened the case and saw many shiny things. There were gold coins, pretty necklaces, and shiny rings. Lucy knew she had found something special. She felt like a wealthy princess with all these treasures. Lucy showed the case to her mom and dad. They were happy and proud of her discovery. They told her that she could keep the treasures and play with them. Lucy played with her new treasures every day, and she felt like the happiest girl in the world.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat was anxious because she did not know the park well. The dog said, "Don't be scared, I will show you around." As they walked, they saw a big box. They wanted to see what was inside. The cat gave the box a little rub with her paw. Suddenly, the box opened with a big surprise! Out came a bird, who was very happy to be free. The cat, dog, and bird became best friends. They played in the park all day, and the cat was not anxious anymore. They all knew that the best surprises came from new friends.
