Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small park, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to roll on the ground and play with his friends. One day, it was icy and cold outside, but Sam still wanted to play. Sam went outside to roll on the icy ground. He slipped and fell, but he did not give up. He tried again and again, until he learned how to roll on the icy ground without falling. His friends watched and cheered for him. Soon, all the squirrels learned how to roll on the icy ground just like Sam. They had lots of fun playing together, even on cold days. The moral of the story is that if you keep trying, you can learn new things and have fun with your friends, no matter the weather.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy boy named Tim. Tim did not like to do any work. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, go outside and play." Tim went outside and saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. But he was lazy, so he just sat under the tree. Then, he heard a noise. "Hello," said a voice. Tim looked up and saw a talking bird! The bird said, "Tim, if you climb the tree, you will find a surprise." Tim thought about it, but he was still lazy. Then, the bird started to sing a magic song. Suddenly, Tim felt strong and not lazy anymore. Tim climbed the tree very fast. When he reached the top, he saw the surprise. It was a big, shiny star! But as he tried to grab it, the star began to fall. Tim held on tight and they both fell to the ground. The talking bird laughed and said, "Tim, you learned that even a lazy boy can do big things with a little help." Tim smiled and thanked the bird. From that day on, Tim was never lazy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Tim wanted to take his ball upstairs to his room. But there was a problem: the stair was difficult for Tim to climb with his ball. Tim tried to understand how he could take his ball up the stair. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. He tried to push the ball up the stair with his hands. But the ball was too big, and it rolled back down. Tim felt sad, but he didn't give up. He tried again, but this time he used his feet to kick the ball up the stair. It was not easy, but Tim kept trying. After a while, Tim finally got his ball to the top of the stair. He was so happy and proud that he could do it, even when it was difficult.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big igloo. He was so happy. The igloo was made of snow. It was cold, but Tim liked it. Tim met a small rabbit near the igloo. The rabbit was very good at spring. It could jump high and far. Tim wanted to be friends with the rabbit. He said, "Hi, rabbit! Can we play together?" The rabbit said, "Yes, let's play!" They played near the igloo all day. They played hide and seek. They played catch. They laughed loud and had fun. Tim and the rabbit were very happy. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat lived on a farm. One day, the goat saw a ball. The ball was big and round. The goat wanted to play with the ball. The goat said to a nearby friend, "Let's throw the ball!" The friend said, "Okay, I will throw the ball to you." The friend threw the ball to the goat. The goat was happy to play with the available ball. But then, the ball went very far away. The goat tried to find the ball, but it was lost. The goat and the friend were sad. They could not play with the ball anymore. The end.
