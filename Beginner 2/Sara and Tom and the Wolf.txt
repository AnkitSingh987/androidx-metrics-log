Title: Sara and Tom and the Wolf

Sara and Tom were happy. They were going to visit their grandma. They liked their grandma. She gave them hugs and cookies. She had a big house with a garden and a swing. They got in the van with their mom and dad. The van was old and noisy. It made funny sounds. Sara and Tom did not mind. They sang songs and played games. They saw cows and trees and houses. But then the van stopped. It made a loud bang. Smoke came out. Mom and dad looked worried. They got out of the van and opened the hood. They tried to fix the van. They could not. "Mom, what's wrong?" Sara asked. "The van is broken, honey. We need help. We have to wait for someone to come," mom said. "Can we still visit grandma?" Tom asked. "I hope so, sweetie. But it might take a long time. I'm sorry," dad said. Sara and Tom felt sad. They wanted to see their grandma. They did not like to wait. They were bored and hungry and cold. They waited and waited and waited. No one came. The sun went down. The sky was dark. The van was dark. Sara and Tom were scared. They heard a noise. It was not a nice noise. It was a growl. They saw eyes. They were not nice eyes. They were yellow and mean. They belonged to a big wolf. The wolf was hungry. He smelled Sara and Tom. He wanted to eat them. He jumped on the van. He scratched the windows. He tried to get in. Sara and Tom screamed. Mom and dad tried to scare the wolf away. They could not. The wolf was too strong. He broke the window. He bit mom and dad. He grabbed Sara and Tom. He dragged them out of the van. He ate them. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. In the barn, there were many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. They all lived happily together. One day, the sun started to rise, and the animals woke up. The rooster said, "Wake up, friends! It's time to play!" All the animals got up and went outside. They played all day, and they had a lot of fun. But then, the little pig saw a big mud puddle. He wanted to jump in, but he was scared. The cow said, "Don't be scared, little pig. We are here to help you." So, the little pig jumped in the mud puddle, and all the animals laughed and played together. At the end of the day, the sun started to set, and the animals went back to the big red barn. They were all very tired, but they were happy. They learned that they should always help each other and be kind. And so, the animals lived happily ever after in their big red barn, always helping and being kind to one another. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and be kind to everyone.

Jack and Jill were on vacation and had gone to the museum. Jack was excited to explore the museum and find out what was inside it. Jill wasn't so sure. She looked around the museum and said, "This looks terrible!" Jack said, "It'll be fun, I promise! We can look at all the cool stuff in the store and maybe even buy something." Jill said, "I don't think I want to buy anything. It all looks terrible!" Jack said, "C'mon, let's just take a look around and see what they have. It might be fun!" So Jack and Jill went into the store and both of them were surprised at how many interesting things were in the store. After a while, they were both having a lot of fun. Jill said, "Wow, this isn't so terrible after all!" Jack smiled and said, "See, I told you it would be fun!"

One day, a chubby cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a pebble on the ground. He liked the pebble, so he picked it up with his paw. Tom walked and walked with his new pebble friend. As Tom walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree with the pebble. But when he tried to climb, he dropped the pebble. Tom let out a big sigh. He was sad that he lost his new friend. Then, something unexpected happened! A small bird flew down and picked up the pebble with its beak. The bird gave the pebble back to Tom. Tom was so happy! He thanked the bird, and they became friends. From that day on, Tom, the bird, and the pebble played together all the time.
