Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cookies

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to get treats. She liked the taste of sweet things. One day, Amy wanted to make cookies. She asked her mom, "Can we make cookies today?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's make cookies together." Amy and her mom got all the things they needed to make cookies. They mixed the dough and put it on a big pan. Amy was very happy. She said, "I can't wait to taste the cookies!" Her mom smiled and said, "Me too!" The cookies were in the oven for a short time. When they were done, Amy and her mom took them out. They were very hot, so they had to wait a little bit. Then, they tasted the cookies. They were successful! The cookies tasted very good. Amy and her mom were happy and ate the cookies together.

One day, a bird named Tim wanted to soar high in the sky. Tim was a careful bird and always looked both ways before flying. But today, he saw something new on the ground. It was a bar of soap. Tim did not know what soap was. He thought it was food. He picked it up with his beak and tried to eat it. But it was very slippery and fell from his beak. The soap slid across the ground and Tim chased after it. As Tim tried to catch the soap, he bumped into another bird named Sam. Sam was not happy and they began to argue. Tim said he was sorry and told Sam about the soap. Sam knew what soap was and explained it to Tim. Together, they put the soap back where it belonged and became friends. Tim learned to be more careful and not to eat things he didn't know.

Sara and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to run and jump and laugh. They see a big puddle of water. They want to splash in it. "Come, Ben, let's splash in the puddle!" Sara says. "OK, Sara, let's splash in the puddle!" Ben says. They run to the puddle and jump in it. They make big waves and wet noises. They are having fun. But then they hear Mom calling them. "Sara! Ben! Come inside now! It's time for lunch!" Mom says. Sara and Ben stop splashing. They look at each other. They are very wet and muddy. They are not clean. "Uh-oh, we are in trouble," Sara says. "Let's go inside and say sorry to Mom," Ben says. They walk to the door and open it. Mom sees them and gasps. "Oh, Sara and Ben, what have you done? You are so wet and muddy! You need to take a bath and change your clothes before lunch!" Mom says. Sara and Ben feel bad. They know they made a mess. "Sorry, Mom, we were playing in the puddle. We didn't think," Sara says. "Sorry, Mom, we were having fun. We didn't mean to," Ben says. Mom sighs. She is not happy, but she is not angry. She loves Sara and Ben. "It's OK, Sara and Ben, I know you were playing. But you need to be careful and listen to me. Playing in the puddle is not good for you. You can get cold and sick. And you can ruin your clothes. Do you understand?" Mom says. Sara and Ben nod. They understand. "Yes, Mom, we understand. We won't play in the puddle again. We will listen to you," Sara and Ben say. Mom smiles. She hugs Sara and Ben. "Good, Sara and Ben, I'm glad you understand. Now, let's go to the bathroom and get you clean. I will rub you with a towel and make you dry. Then we can have lunch together. OK?" Mom says. Sara and Ben smile. They hug Mom back. "OK, Mom, thank you. We love you," Sara and Ben say. They go to the bathroom and take a bath. Mom rubs them with a towel and makes them dry. Then they have lunch together. They are happy. The moral of the story is: Listen to your mom and don't play in the puddle.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Lily went to play outside. They saw a bee flying near a pretty flower. They felt lucky to see the bee so close. Tim and Lily started to discuss the bee. Tim said, "I like the bee. It is nice." Lily said, "Yes, the bee is nice. It makes honey for us." But then, the bee got mad. It flew at Tim and Lily. They tried to run away, but the bee was fast. The bee stung Tim on his arm. Tim cried and Lily was sad. Now, they knew to be careful around bees. They learned that bees can be nice, but they can also hurt if they get mad.
