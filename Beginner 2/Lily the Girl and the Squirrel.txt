Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a clean cap that she loved to wear. One day, she went to play outside with her friends. While they were playing, a big wind came and took Lily's cap away. Lily was very sad. She asked her friends to help find her cap. They looked high and low, but they could not find it. Just then, a kind squirrel saw Lily's cap stuck in a tree. The squirrel climbed the tree and put the cap back on Lily's head. Lily was so happy and thanked the squirrel. She and her friends continued to play, and Lily held on to her cap tightly so it would not fly away again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big tree with lots of fruit. There were red apples, yellow bananas, and green grapes. Lucy felt so happy in her dream. Lucy told her friend Sam about her dream. "I saw a big tree with lots of fruit, Sam!" said Lucy. Sam smiled and said, "That sounds like a fun dream, Lucy! I like fruit too." But then, Lucy started to feel uncomfortable. She wanted to climb the tree and pick the fruit, but she was scared. She asked Sam, "Can you help me climb the tree in my dream?" Sam said, "Of course, Lucy! We can do it together." And they both felt happy and brave.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little girl named Lily. She loved to go to the library and look at all the books. One day, she saw a big, red book on the top shelf. She wanted to read it, but she was too small to reach it. Lily asked her friend, Tom, for help. "Can you help me get the big, red book?" she asked. Tom was tall and could reach the book. But when Tom tried to get the book, he accidentally dropped it on the floor with a loud thud. The librarian, Mrs. Brown, heard the noise and came over. She was not happy. "Who dropped the book?" she asked. Lily and Tom looked at each other, scared. Tom said, "I did, but it was an accident." Mrs. Brown was not happy but understood it was an accident. She told them to be more careful next time. Lily and Tom said sorry and promised to be careful. They sat down together and read the big, red book. They were happy and learned a lot from the book.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a regular dog. The dog was a guard dog, but Tim did not know that. He wanted to play with the dog. "Can I play with the dog, mom?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Okay, but be careful. The dog might be a guard dog." Tim went to the dog and threw a ball. The dog ran to get the ball and brought it back. They played for a while. Then, Tim realized the dog was a guard dog. "Mom, you were right! The dog is a guard dog!" Tim said. But the dog was nice and just wanted to play. So Tim and the guard dog became friends and played every day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a house with a big yard full of green grass. He liked to play in the grass with his ball. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play with him. While they were playing, Sam kicked the ball very hard. The ball went far away and hit a small bird. The bird was hurt and couldn't fly. Tim and Sam felt bad for the bird. They wanted to help it and say "forgive" to the bird. They tried to make the bird feel better, but it was too hurt. The bird didn't get better and it couldn't fly again. Tim and Sam were very sad. They learned to be more careful when they play, so they don't hurt anyone else. But the bird never flew again and stayed hurt.
