Category: Beginner
Title: The Pilot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a pilot. The pilot flew a big plane in the sky. One day, the pilot was very tired. He had been flying the plane for a long time. The pilot saw a pretty place to rest. He landed the plane on the ground. He went outside to play with a big red ball. He had a lot of fun. After playing, the pilot was not tired anymore. He went back to the plane. He started the plane and let it go up in the sky. The pilot released the big red ball. It flew away with a big smile. The pilot was happy and flew the plane back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very eager to play with his friends at the park. He loved to run and jump with them. One day, Max got a message from his friend, a bird named Sam. In the message, Sam said he was sad and needed Max's help. Max started to worry about his friend. He wanted to help Sam and make him happy again. So, Max went to find Sam, who was sitting alone near a tree. Max asked Sam what was wrong, and Sam told him that he lost his favorite toy. Max knew how much Sam loved that toy. Max decided to help Sam look for the toy. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found the toy under a bush. Sam was so happy, and Max was glad he could help his friend. They played together all day, and Max didn't worry anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a snake in the grass. The snake was deaf, so it could not hear Tim coming. Tim wanted to take the snake home to show his mom. Tim said to the snake, "I will take you home to show my mom. She will like you." The snake did not say anything because it was deaf. Tim picked up the snake and took it to his house. When Tim showed the snake to his mom, she was scared. But Tim said, "Don't worry, Mom. The snake is deaf and nice." Tim's mom smiled and let the snake stay. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his red ball. One day, he saw a deep well near his house. Tim was curious and wanted to get closer to the well. As Tim walked closer, his mom saw him. She knew the well was dangerous. She ran to Tim and said, "No, Tim! Don't go near the well. It is dangerous!" Tim listened to his mom and stayed away from the well. From that day on, Tim played with his red ball far away from the well. He knew it was dangerous, and he wanted to stay safe. Tim's mom was happy that he listened and stayed away from the well. They lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toy cars on the grass. They liked to make them zoom and crash and honk. They did not see the big cliff behind them. The cliff was high and steep and had many rocks. A bird was sitting on a branch near the cliff. The bird had a delicate nest with three eggs inside. The bird was happy and proud of her nest. She did not like the noise that Lily and Ben were making. It disturbed her and her eggs. The bird flew down and landed on Lily's head. She pecked at her hair and screeched. Lily was scared and surprised. She dropped her car and cried. "Ow! Ow! Go away, bird! You hurt me!" Lily shouted. Ben saw the bird and ran to help Lily. He waved his arms and tried to shoo the bird away. He did not see the cliff behind him. He tripped on a rock and fell over the edge. "Ben! Ben! Help!" Lily screamed. The bird saw Ben falling and felt sorry. She did not mean to make him fall. She flew after him and caught him by his shirt. She lifted him up and brought him back to the grass. She dropped him gently next to Lily. "Thank you, bird! Thank you!" Ben said. The bird nodded and flew back to her nest. She checked on her eggs and saw that they were safe. She was happy and proud of her nest. She hoped that Lily and Ben would not disturb her again.
