Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Stick

Lily and Tom like to play in the garden. They see many things that grow there. They see flowers, grass, trees and squash. Squash are big and round and have rough skin. Lily and Tom want to touch them. They run to the squash patch and pick one up. It is heavy and hard. They feel the bumps on the skin. They smell the squash. It does not smell like anything. They wonder what is inside. They find a stick and try to poke the squash. The stick does not go in. The squash is too tough. They try to hit the squash with the stick. The stick breaks. The squash does not. They get angry and throw the squash on the ground. The squash cracks open. Lily and Tom stare at it. They see orange flesh and seeds. They see juice and pulp. They see a worm. The worm wiggles out of the squash and crawls away. Lily and Tom scream and run away. They do not like squash anymore. They like flowers, grass, trees and anything else that does not have worms inside. They do not play in the garden for a long time. They play in the house with their toys. They are happy and safe.

---

Lila and Tom were playing in the yard. They had a big box and a radio. They wanted to make a car with the box and the radio. They cut holes for the windows and the wheels. They put the radio inside the box and turned it on. They heard music and voices. "Let's go for a ride!" Tom said. He got in the box and pretended to drive. Lila got in the box too and pretended to be his passenger. They made noises like a car and moved the box around the yard. They had fun. But after a while, they got hungry. They wanted to eat something. They got out of the box and looked for their mom. They saw her in the kitchen. She was making sandwiches and juice. "Mom, we are hungry!" Lila and Tom said. "Can we have some food?" "Of course, my loves," Mom said. "You can have some sandwiches and juice. But first, you have to part with your radio. It is too loud and it bothers the neighbors. You can play with it later, when it is not nap time." Lila and Tom did not want to part with their radio. They liked the music and the voices. But they also liked the sandwiches and the juice. They decided to part with their radio for now. They turned it off and put it back in the box. They went to the kitchen and ate their food. They thanked their mom and gave her a hug. They were not hungry anymore. They were happy. They decided to play with their car and their radio again later, when it was not nap time. They hoped the neighbors would not mind. They loved their car and their radio. They were their favorite toys.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys. Ben has a red motorcycle. Sam has a blue car. They make noises and race them on the floor. One day, Sam wants to play with Ben's motorcycle. He asks Ben, "Can I have your motorcycle, please?" Ben says, "No, it is mine. You have your car. Go away." Sam is sad and angry. He waits for Ben to look away. Then he grabs the motorcycle and runs to his room. Ben sees Sam and shouts, "Hey, give it back! That is not yours!" He chases Sam to his room. Sam tries to close the door, but Ben pushes it open. They fight over the motorcycle. They pull and tug and yell. The motorcycle breaks in two. Ben and Sam are shocked. They look at the broken toy. They start to cry. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken motorcycle and the crying boys. She is very mad. She says, "What have you done? You are both clumsy and naughty. You broke Ben's toy. You did not share. You did not listen. You are in big trouble." She takes away their toys and sends them to bed. Ben and Sam are sorry, but it is too late. They have no toys and no fun. They have a bad day.
