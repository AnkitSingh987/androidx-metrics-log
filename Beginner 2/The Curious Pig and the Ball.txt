Category: Beginner
Title: The Curious Pig and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a curious little pig. The pig lived on a farm with many friends. The pig liked to play and have fun with all the animals. One day, the pig saw a big red ball. The pig wanted to play with the ball. The pig used its nose to point at the ball. The pig's friends, a cow and a duck, saw the pig point at the ball. The cow and the duck went to the pig. They all played with the big red ball together. They were very happy. The curious pig, the cow, and the duck had a fun day on the farm.

One day, a girl named Sally was eager to repair her toy car. She took it to her dad in the kitchen. Sally's dad was a kind man who loved to help. He looked at the toy car and said, "Okay, let's fix it!" In the kitchen, there was a small cell on the table. Sally's dad didn't see the cell. He was too busy fixing the toy car. Sally was very happy to see her dad working on the car. She clapped her hands and said, "Yay, my car will work again!" Then, something unexpected happened. The cell on the table started to grow bigger and bigger. It became as big as a real car! Sally and her dad were so surprised. They looked at each other and then at the big cell. Sally's dad said, "Wow, that was a big twist!" They laughed and played with the big cell and the repaired toy car together.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they decided to be pirates and look for treasure. They put on hats and scarves and took a big box from the garage. They filled the box with shiny things from their rooms, like coins, rings, and necklaces. They called the box their treasure chest. They dragged the box to the backyard and dug a hole with a shovel. They put the box in the hole and covered it with dirt. Then they made a map with crayons and paper. They drew a big X where the treasure was. They hid the map under a rock. "Arrr, matey, we have stored our jewels well," Anna said in a pirate voice. "Aye, captain, no one will find them," Ben said. They went inside to have a snack. But they did not see that their dog, Spot, had followed them to the backyard. Spot smelled the box and dug it up. He opened it with his paws and teeth. He took out the shiny things and played with them. He bit some of them and made them dull. He buried some of them in different places. He left some of them on the grass. When Anna and Ben came back, they saw the mess. They saw the empty box, the dirt, and the dull things. They looked for their map, but it was gone. Spot had taken it and chewed it. "Oh no, our treasure!" Anna cried. "Where is our map?" Ben asked. They saw Spot wagging his tail and holding a necklace in his mouth. He dropped it and barked. "Spot, you bad dog!" Anna said. "Did you take our jewels?" Ben said. Spot licked their faces and wagged his tail more. He did not understand why they were angry. He thought he was playing. Anna and Ben were sad and mad. They tried to find their jewels, but they could not. They learned a lesson. They should not store their jewels in the backyard. They should keep them in a safe place. And they should not let Spot near them.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a small river. One day, he found a pretty twig. Tim thought, "I can use this twig to make my nest even better!" As Tim picked up the twig, he saw a big frog named Fred. Fred was sitting on a rock by the river. Tim said, "Hi, Fred! Look at my new twig. It's so pretty!" Fred looked at the twig and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a great twig. Your nest will be the best!" Tim and Fred became good friends. They played together by the river every day. Tim would rock on the tree branch, and Fred would jump from rock to rock. They were very happy and had lots of fun. And Tim's nest was the best nest in the big tree.
