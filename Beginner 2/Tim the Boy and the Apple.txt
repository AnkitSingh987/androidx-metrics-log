Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very alert boy. He liked to look and listen to everything around him. One day, Tim went for a walk with his mom. They saw a big tree with red and green round things. His mom said, "Tim, these are apples. They taste very good." Tim wanted to try one, so his mom picked a red apple for him. Tim took a bite of the apple. It was sweet and juicy. He liked the taste very much. Tim smiled and said, "Mom, I like apples. They have a good name and a good taste." His mom smiled back and they walked home, happy and alert, with their tasty apples.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an honest boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car. It could go in a zigzag way, which made him very happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his toy car with him. While he played, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam saw the toy car and said, "Wow, your car can go zigzag! Can I play with it, please?" Tim was kind and let Sam play with his toy car. After a while, Tim's mom said it was time to leave the park. Tim told Sam, "I have to go home now. Please give me back my toy car." But Sam did not want to give it back. He ran away with the toy car, and Tim was very sad. Tim's mom tried to catch Sam, but he was too fast. They left the park without the toy car, and Tim cried all the way home.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. The sky was blue and the weather was mild. Lily liked to play on the swings and slide. She also liked to look at the sky and notice the pretty clouds. While Lily was playing, she noticed a big, round cloud in the sky. It was bigger than all the other clouds. Lily thought it was a funny cloud. She told her mom to look at the big cloud too. All of a sudden, the big cloud moved! It was not a cloud at all. It was a big, round balloon! The balloon came down from the sky and landed in the park. Lily and her mom were very surprised. They laughed and played with the big balloon for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Spike. Spike loved to hop and play in the sunny field with his friends. They liked to play hide and seek, and Spike was very good at hiding. One day, Spike found a big, yummy carrot. He paused and looked at it with big, happy eyes. He wanted to share the yummy carrot with his friends. So, he picked up the carrot and hopped back to the sunny field. When Spike got to the field, all his friends were there. He showed them the yummy carrot, and they all cheered. They took turns taking bites of the carrot, and it was the best carrot they had ever tasted. Spike and his friends were very happy and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Jack and Jane. Jane loved eating food and Jack hated food. Every time Jane wanted to go out and eat, Jack would say no and stay home. One day, Jack and Jane went to the park. They saw a hidden area with yummy food! Jane was happy and wanted to eat it, but Jack didn't want to. Jack said, “I hate food! I'm not going to eat it.” Jane cried. “Why don't you like food?” she asked. Jack looked sad. “I don't know,” he said. Jane thought for a while. Then she said, “Let's try a little bit. If you don't like it, you don't have to eat the rest.” Jack thought about it and said, “Ok, I'll try.” He took a bite and, surprisingly, he liked it! He ate all the food and he and Jane both had a happy day together.
