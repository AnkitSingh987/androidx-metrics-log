Category: Beginner
Title: Ben the Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben loved to give hugs to his friends. One day, he met a little bird named Lily. Lily was scared of Ben because he was so big. Ben said, "Don't be scared, Lily. I just want to give you a hug." Lily thought about it and decided to surrender her fear. She let Ben give her a hug. It was the best hug she ever had. They became best friends and played together all the time. One day, while they were playing, they found a magic rock. They touched it and something unexpected happened. Ben became small like Lily and Lily became big like Ben. They laughed and continued to play, now able to give each other even better hugs.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom loved to wear a suit. He looked very nice in his suit. One day, he was walking in the park, feeling nervous. He didn't know why, but he felt like something unexpected would happen. As Tom walked, he saw a little mouse. The mouse was wearing a tiny suit too! Tom was so surprised. He wanted to follow the mouse and see where it was going. So, he started to walk behind the mouse, trying to be quiet. But then, something unexpected happened! The little mouse turned around and said, "Hi, Tom! I like your suit!" Tom was shocked. He didn't know that the mouse could talk. They became friends and walked in the park together, both wearing their suits.

Tom and Sue were brother and sister. They liked to play with their toy cars. They had many cars of different colors and sizes. They pretended to drive them on the floor, on the sofa, on the table. One day, their mom said, "We are going to the park. Get ready and bring your cars if you want." Tom and Sue were happy. They put their favorite cars in their pockets and ran to the car. Mom drove them to the park. At the park, there was a big slide, a swing, a sandbox, and a pond. Tom and Sue played with their cars on the slide. They made them go fast and zoom down. They laughed and had fun. Then, they saw a big dog. The dog was black and hairy. It had sharp teeth and a loud bark. It looked scary. The dog saw their cars and wanted to play too. It ran to them and jumped on the slide. It grabbed Tom's car in its mouth and ran away. "Hey, that's my car!" Tom shouted. He chased the dog. Sue followed him. They ran across the grass, past the swing, to the pond. The dog stopped at the edge of the pond. It dropped the car and drank some water. Tom and Sue reached the dog. They tried to get the car back. But the dog was not happy. It growled and barked at them. It showed its teeth and snapped at them. Tom and Sue were scared. They backed away. "Mom, help!" they cried. Mom heard them and came running. She saw the dog and the car. She picked up a stick and threw it to the dog. The dog saw the stick and forgot the car. It chased the stick and ran away. Mom hugged Tom and Sue. She gave them the car. She said, "Are you okay? That was a scary dog. Next time, be careful and stay away from strange dogs. They might not be friendly." Tom and Sue nodded. They said, "Thank you, Mom. We are sorry. We love you." They hugged Mom back. They put their cars in their pockets and went to play on the swing. They were happy again.

Sam and Bob were playing in the garden. Sam had a spade and Bob had a bucket. Sam dug a big hole with his spade. Suddenly, he slipped and fell into the hole. Bob was deaf, so he didn't hear Sam's cry for help. Bob just kept playing with his bucket. Suddenly, Bob's mum came out of the house and heard Sam's cries. She quickly pulled Sam out of the hole. Sam was happy to be out of the hole, but his spade was still inside. Bob's mum said, "I can get it for you, don't worry!" She went down into the hole to get the spade. In no time she had it, and Sam was able to carry on playing.
