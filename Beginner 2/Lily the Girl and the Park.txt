Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a popcorn stand and Lily's eyes lit up. "Mom, can we get some popcorn?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, we can get some popcorn." Lily was grateful and happy. They walked to the popcorn stand and the nice man gave them a big bag of popcorn. Lily could not wait to touch the popcorn. She put her hand in the bag and felt the warm, fluffy popcorn. Lily and her mom sat on a bench and shared the popcorn. They laughed and talked as they enjoyed their snack. "I love popcorn," said Lily. Her mom smiled and said, "I love popcorn too, and I love spending time with you."

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a hot day, so Tim wore his hat and put on some sun cream. He saw a big ice cream truck and he really wanted an ice cream. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I have an ice cream, please?" His mom said, "Of course, Tim! Let's go get one." As they walked to the ice cream truck, Tim saw a dog. The dog seemed sad and guilty. Tim asked the dog, "Why are you sad?" The dog looked at Tim and barked. Tim's mom told him that the dog might have done something wrong and now it feels guilty. Tim wanted to help the dog feel better. He got his ice cream and gave the dog a lick. The dog wagged its tail and seemed happy. Tim's mom smiled and said, "You did a good job, Tim. The dog is happy now." Tim felt proud and enjoyed the rest of his ice cream with his new friend, the dog.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Sam and Joe. Every day, they would go outside together and explore. One day, they were walking through the meadow when they saw something green out of the corner of their eye. “What is that?” asked Joe. Sam smiled. “Let’s go find out,” he said. They moved closer and saw a clear pond. The water was so still it looked like a mirror. “Wow, let’s go swimming!” Joe said excitedly. Sam nodded and they both jumped in. They moved their arms in circles, making the water ripple and glitter in the sun. After a while, they decided to get out. “What a lovely day this has been,” Sam said to Joe as they walked home. Joe nodded in agreement.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big hole in the ground. They wonder what is inside. "Let's look!" Ben says. He gazes into the hole. He sees something brown and hard. "It's a root!" he says. "Maybe it's from a big tree." Anna wants to see too. She gazes into the hole. She sees something shiny and red. "It's a fire!" she says. "Maybe it's from a dragon." They are both scared. They run away from the hole. They find their mom. She is watering the flowers. "Mom, mom, there's a fire in the hole!" Anna says. "Mom, mom, there's a root in the hole!" Ben says. Mom smiles. She takes their hands. She walks with them to the hole. She gazes into the hole. She sees something metal and round. "It's a pot!" she says. "Maybe it's from a neighbor. They must have lost it." She picks up the pot. It is hot. She puts it on the grass. She explains to Anna and Ben that the pot is not a fire or a root. It is just a thing that people use to cook food. Sometimes, the sun makes it shiny and hot. Anna and Ben are not scared anymore. They are curious. They touch the pot. It is not hot anymore. They look inside. It is empty. "Can we play with the pot?" Anna asks. "Sure, but be careful. It might be dirty," Mom says. Anna and Ben are happy. They take the pot to their sandbox. They pretend it is a treasure. They fill it with sand and stones. They have fun. They forget about the hole.
