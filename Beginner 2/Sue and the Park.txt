Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was an honest girl who always told the truth. One day, Sue went to the park to play. At the park, she saw a nurse who was sad. The nurse had lost her toy. Sue wanted to help the nurse find her toy. Sue looked and looked for the toy. She asked her friends to help too. They looked under the slide, behind the swings, and in the sandbox. Sue reminded her friends to keep looking. The nurse watched and hoped they would find her toy. But then, a big storm came. The wind blew, and the rain fell. Sue, her friends, and the nurse had to go home. They could not find the toy. The nurse was still sad. Sue felt bad because she could not help the nurse. The day had a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He wanted to go outside and play with his ball. He put on his shoes and ran to the door. "Bye, Mom!" he said. "I'm going to play outside!" When Tim got outside, he saw a small cat. The cat was taking a nap under a tree. Tim wanted to play with the cat, but he did not want to wake it up. He walked very slowly, trying not to make a noise. But Tim did not see a rock on the ground. He stumbled on the rock and fell down. "Oh no!" he said. The cat woke up and looked at Tim. Tim was sad, but the cat did not mind. It walked over to him and said, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I do!" So Tim and the cat played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to spend time with her gentle cat, Fluffy. They would play and have fun all day long. One day, Lily and Fluffy found a big box. They wanted to make a house for Fluffy. Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom took a cut and made a door in the box. Fluffy was so happy. Lily and Fluffy spent every day in their new house. They played with toys and took naps together. They were the best of friends, and they loved their gentle, happy times.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink cocoa. Every day after playing, he would ask his mom for a cup of cocoa. One day, while playing outside, Tim felt very thirsty. He ran inside and yelled, "Mom, can I have some cocoa, please?" But his mom was not in the kitchen. Tim felt confused. Tim looked all around the house. Finally, he found his mom in the garden. She was picking flowers. Tim yelled again, "Mom, can I have some cocoa?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim!" They went inside and enjoyed a warm cup of cocoa together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his dad at work. His dad worked at a big place called a university. Tim was very excited to see what his dad did there. When they got there, Tim saw a big room where his dad worked. There were many books and people talking. Tim's dad said, "Tim, I need to do some work now. You can sit here and play with your toy car." Tim played with his car, but he got tired. He wanted to do something else. He saw a long rope hanging from the ceiling. Tim thought it would be fun to stretch and try to reach the rope. Tim stood up and tried to stretch as tall as he could. He jumped up and down, but he could not reach the rope. A man walked by and saw Tim trying to reach the rope. The man said, "You should not play with that rope, Tim. It is not safe." But Tim did not listen to the man. He kept trying to stretch and reach the rope. Finally, he jumped very high and grabbed the rope. But the rope was not strong enough to hold Tim. The rope broke, and Tim fell down on the floor. He got hurt and cried. His dad came running and said, "I told you to stay here and play with your toy car, Tim. You did not listen, and now you are hurt." Tim felt sad and wished he had listened to his dad and the man.
