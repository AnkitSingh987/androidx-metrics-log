Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big box near his house. He was very curious and wanted to see what was inside. So, Tim went to the box and took a little peek. Inside, he saw a funny man with a big hat. The man was a judge. The judge was very careless. He left his big hat outside the box. Tim thought it would be fun to try on the hat. So, he put it on his head. It was so big that it covered his eyes! Tim could not see where he was going and bumped into a tree. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! "Ouch!" said the tree. "Why did you bump into me?" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that trees could talk. The tree was not a tree at all, but a very tall man dressed like a tree. The man was playing a trick on the judge. Tim and the tall man laughed and became friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was a bear called Benny and the other was a sheep called Sally. They liked playing games together. One day, Sally asked Benny: “Can you help me? I need some glue to make a model.” Benny smiled and said yes. He put his paw into his pocket and took out the glue. He gave it to Sally. Sally was so happy. She hugged Benny, then began to make her model. Once she was finished, Benny asked her, “Is it rough?” Sally whispered, “Yes, it’s really rough!" They both laughed. The moral of the story is: it’s always nice to have a friend to help you out.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she lost her favorite toy, a small rocket that could fly to space. Lily was very sad. She looked for her toy everywhere but could not find it. She asked her mom to help her look for the rocket. Her mom was kind too, and she wanted to make Lily happy. Her mom had an idea. She said, "Let's replace your lost rocket with a new one." Lily and her mom went to the store and found a big, shiny rocket. It was even better than the old one. Lily was very happy and played with her new rocket all day, pretending to fly to space.

Once upon a time there was a prince. He was very cheerful and he liked to explore the kingdom. One day he went on an adventure. He ran down the castle steps and he accidentally lost his crown! The prince was very sad. He looked all around the castle but he couldn’t find it. He asked all the castle people if they had seen it, but they didn’t. Soon a fairy appeared. She was very kind and she said she would help the prince. She waved her wand and suddenly the crown was back in the prince’s hand. The prince was so excited and he thanked the fairy. He learned a lesson that day to be more careful so he would never lose his crown again. The prince was very happy and he kept exploring the kingdom. He met lots of friendly people and he was always cheerful. Everyone smiled when they saw him walking around with his shining crown.

Sara was scared of the dark. She always had bad dreams when she slept in her room. She dreamed of big monsters and loud noises that made her cry. She wanted to sleep with Mom and Dad, but they said she was a big girl and had to sleep in her own bed. One night, Sara had a very bad dream. She dreamed that a huge white snake was chasing her in the forest. It had sharp teeth and red eyes. It hissed and slithered behind her. Sara ran and ran, but the snake was faster. She screamed for help, but no one heard her. She woke up with a start. She was sweating and shaking. She felt like the snake was still there. She wanted to go to Mom and Dad, but her door was locked. She did not know how to unlock it. She was trapped in her room with her nightmare. She cried and cried. She banged on the door. She called for Mom and Dad. She hoped they would hear her and come to save her. Mom and Dad heard Sara's cries. They jumped out of bed and ran to her room. They saw that the door was locked. They did not know who locked it. They tried to open it, but it was stuck. "Sara, honey, we're here. Don't be afraid. We'll get you out. Can you unlock the door?" Mom said. "I don't know how. I'm scared. There's a snake in my dream. It wants to eat me." Sara said. "It's okay, sweetie. It was just a dream. There's no snake. You're safe. We love you. We'll break the door if we have to." Dad said. They pushed and pulled on the door. They heard a click. The door was unlocked. They opened it and saw Sara. She was crying and shaking. She ran to them and hugged them. They hugged her back and kissed her. "Shh, shh, it's okay. You're okay. We're here. We're sorry the door was locked. We don't know how that happened. Do you want to sleep with us tonight?" Mom said. "Yes, please. I don't want to sleep here. I don't like the dark. I don't like the snake." Sara said. "Of course, honey. Come on, let's go to our bed. We'll keep you warm and safe. No more nightmares. We promise." Dad said. They carried Sara to their bed. They tucked her in and cuddled with her. They sang her a lullaby and stroked her hair. Sara felt better. She felt loved. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She did not dream of the snake anymore. She dreamed of rainbows and butterflies. She smiled in her sleep.
