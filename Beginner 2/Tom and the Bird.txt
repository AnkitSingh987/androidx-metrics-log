Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Bird

One day, Tom went out to play. His mother told him not to wander too far. So Tom stayed close to home. But then Tom noticed something odd. He saw a cat staring at him from the kitchen window. So Tom went to take a closer look. As Tom approached the kitchen window, he froze. There was a dead bird lying on the kitchen windowsill. Tom couldn't believe it. He stared wide-eyed at the bird. "It's okay," said his mum, coming up behind him. "The bird was just very tired, so he went to sleep." She picked the bird up gently and laid it to rest in the garden, where it could be near the other birds. Then she turned to Tom and gave him a hug. "Let's go back home now and make lunch," she said. Tom nodded, still staring at the garden. Now that he knew the bird was only resting, he felt much better. He couldn't wait to tell his friends all about the dead bird he had found in the kitchen window!

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. He lived near a big blue sea. Tom loved to sail on his little boat. One sunny day, Tom went for a sail on the water. As Tom sailed, he saw something shiny in the water. He stopped his boat and picked it up. It was a pretty jewel! The jewel was red and sparkly. Tom was very happy to find it. Tom sailed back home with his new jewel. He showed it to his friends. They all liked the pretty jewel too. Tom was not bossy anymore. He learned that sharing made his friends happy. And they all played together by the big blue sea.

One day, a little dog and a big cat were playing in the park. They were best friends. The little dog wanted to play with a ball. The big cat wanted to play with a stick. The little dog said, "I want to play with the ball!" The big cat said, "No, I want to play with the stick!" They disagree. The little dog and the big cat started to run around. They were not looking where they were going. The little dog tripped and fell. He hurt his leg. The big cat saw this and felt bad. He stopped playing with the stick and went to help his friend. The big cat found a bandage and wrapped it tight around the little dog's leg. The little dog felt better. He said, "Thank you, big cat!" The big cat smiled and said, "You're welcome, little dog!" They both decided to play with the ball and the stick together. They had a great time and were happy.

One day, a girl named Sue and her dog, Max, went to the park. It was a dull day, but they wanted to play. They saw a big pile of sand and started to dig. Sue and Max were having so much fun in the sand. Suddenly, a big truck came and started to drive towards them. Sue and Max got scared and ran away from the sand. They hid behind a big tree and watched the truck drive closer and closer. But then, something unexpected happened! The truck stopped and a man got out. He took out a big box from the truck and put it in the sand. Sue and Max went to see what was inside the box. To their surprise, it was a big, shiny slide! Now, they had a new toy to play with at the park. They were so happy, and the dull day became a fun day.

One day, a big, strong horse lived in a barn. The horse had many friends. They liked to play and run in the sun. One day, a small mouse came to the barn. The mouse was very scared. The horse said, "Do not be scared, little mouse. I will help you." The mouse said, "Thank you, big horse. You are nice." Then, something unexpected happened. The strong horse got stuck in the mud. The horse was sad and asked the mouse for help. The small mouse ran and got many friends. The mouse and friends pulled the horse out of the mud. The horse said, "Thank you, little mouse. You are strong, too."
