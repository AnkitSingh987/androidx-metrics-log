Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bobo. Bobo had a nice nest in a big tree. He loved his nest very much. One day, Bobo saw a new bird, named Lulu. Lulu was looking for a nest too. Bobo said, "Come, I will help you find a nest." Lulu was happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo!" They looked for a nest together. They found a small nest, but Lulu was not ready for it. Then they found a big nest, and Lulu was ready for it. Lulu said, "I like this nest. It is just right for me." Bobo and Lulu were now friends. They lived in their nests and played every day. They were very happy.

One day, Tom and Mia went to the park with their mom. They saw a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They ran to the slide and took turns going down. "This is fun!" Tom said. "Yes, it is!" Mia agreed. Then they saw a boy sitting on a bench with a big book. He looked sad. Tom and Mia went to him and asked, "What are you doing?" The boy said, "I am reading about planets. They are big balls in the sky. Some are hot, some are cold, some have rings, some have moons. They are very cool." Tom and Mia looked at the book. They saw pictures of different planets. They said, "Wow, they are cool. Can we look at your book?" The boy said, "Sure, you can. But be careful. This is my favorite book. My dad gave it to me. He is an astronaut. He goes to space and sees the planets." Tom and Mia were amazed. They said, "Your dad is an astronaut? That is awesome. Does he use a rocket to go to space?" The boy said, "Yes, he does. He uses a big rocket that makes a loud noise and flies very fast. He wears a special suit and a helmet. He talks to me on the phone sometimes. He tells me stories about the planets. He says they are not like our planet. Our planet is regular. It has water, air, plants, animals, and people. The other planets are different. They have rocks, dust, ice, gas, or fire. They are not good for living." Tom and Mia listened to the boy. They said, "Your dad is very brave. We want to hear more stories about the planets. Can we be your friends?" The boy smiled. He said, "Yes, you can. I am Ben. What are your names?" Tom and Mia said, "We are Tom and Mia. We like planets too. Can we play with you?" Ben said, "Yes, you can. Let's go to the sandbox. We can use the sand to make our own planets. We can pretend we are astronauts too." Tom, Mia, and Ben went to the sandbox. They had fun making planets and playing together. They became good friends.

One day, a big boy named Tim went to the park. He had a whistle. He liked to play with it. Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam had a toy. It was a display toy with many colors. Tim said, "Sam, can I see your toy?" Sam said, "Yes, but can I play with your whistle?" They both agreed to share their toys. As they played, they heard a noise. It was a dog. The dog wanted to play too. Tim blew his whistle. The dog ran fast to them. They all played together and had fun. In the end, the whistle helped them make a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small world, there lived a creative girl named Amy. She loved to draw and paint all day. One day, she saw a big rain cloud coming to her world. Amy did not want the rain to make her world wet. So, she thought of a plan to prevent the rain. She took her paint and brush and painted a big, bright sun in the sky. The sun shone so bright that the rain cloud went away. Amy's friend, Ben, saw what she did and said, "Wow, Amy! You are so creative. You stopped the rain and saved our world!" Amy smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben! We can always use our creative minds to help our world." From that day on, Amy and Ben worked together to keep their world happy and bright.
