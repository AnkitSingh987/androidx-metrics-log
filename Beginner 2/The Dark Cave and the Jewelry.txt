Category: Beginner
Title: The Dark Cave and the Jewelry

Once upon a time, there was a dark cave. In the cave, there was a lot of pretty jewelry. A girl named Lily and a boy named Tom wanted to find the jewelry. They were very brave and went into the cave. As they walked, they saw a big bat. The bat said, "You can't have the jewelry. You must surrender and go away." Lily and Tom were scared but did not want to give up. They thought of a plan. Lily said, "Let's be friends with the bat." They talked to the bat and played with it. The bat was happy and gave them the jewelry. They all became good friends and shared the pretty jewelry.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. The park has a big fence. The fence is brown and hard. Ben and Mia can see the other side of the fence. The other side has a big house and a big garden. The garden has many flowers and trees. One day, Ben and Mia see a girl on the other side of the fence. The girl is wearing a pink dress and a yellow hat. She is holding a ball. She looks sad. Ben and Mia want to play with her. "Hi, girl!" Ben says. "What is your name?" The girl looks at Ben and Mia. She smiles a little. "My name is Lily," she says. "Do you want to play with us?" Mia asks. "We have a swing and a slide and a sandbox." Lily nods. She throws the ball over the fence. Ben catches it. He throws it back to Lily. Lily catches it. She laughs. "Can you come to our side?" Ben asks. "The fence has a gate. We can open it for you." Lily shakes her head. "No, I can't," she says. "My mom says I have to stay in the garden. She says the park is not safe." Ben and Mia feel sorry for Lily. They want to make her happy. They have an idea. "Wait here, Lily," Mia says. "We will be back soon." Ben and Mia run to the gate. They open it. They go to the garden. They pick some flowers and leaves. They make a crown and a necklace. They go back to the fence. They give the crown and the necklace to Lily. "Here, Lily," Ben says. "This is for you. You are our friend. You are welcome to play with us anytime." Lily puts on the crown and the necklace. She looks very pretty. She smiles a big smile. "Thank you, Ben and Mia," she says. "You are very nice. You are my friends too. Maybe one day I can play with you in the park." Ben and Mia smile too. They are happy. They can see Lily clearly. She is not sad anymore. She is happy. They play with the ball and the flowers. They talk and laugh. They have fun. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nice barber. The barber was honest and liked to help people. He cut hair in a small shop. One day, a man came to the barber's shop. The man had very long hair. The barber cut the man's hair and made him look nice. The man was happy and said, "You are the best barber. I value your work." Then, something unexpected happened. The man took off a hat and showed he had more long hair. The barber was surprised. He cut the man's hair again. The man was happy and thanked the barber. The barber learned that some people have surprises, but he still loved his job.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful car named Zoom. Zoom was very fast and loved to hurry. One day, Zoom went to play with his friend, a small car named Beep. As they played, Zoom said, "Beep, let's have a race!" Beep was scared but said, "Okay, Zoom. Let's race to the big tree." They both got ready and started to race. Zoom hurried so fast that he did not see a big rock in the road. He hit the rock and had a big wreck. Beep saw this and stopped. He said, "Oh no, Zoom! Are you okay?" Zoom was sad but said, "Yes, Beep. I am okay. Thank you for helping me." From that day, Zoom learned to be more careful and not to hurry too much.
