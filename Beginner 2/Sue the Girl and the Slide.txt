Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she went to the park with her aunt. They had a lot of fun together. Sue saw a big slide in the park. She was excited and wanted to slide down it. But she was a little scared because it was so high. Her aunt told her, "Don't worry, Sue. I'll slide with you." So, Sue and her aunt went up the slide together. Sue thought her aunt was very tough. As they started to slide down, something unexpected happened. The slide began to shake and twist! Sue and her aunt held onto each other tightly. When they reached the bottom, they found themselves in a magical land full of candy and toys. Sue and her aunt were so surprised and happy. They played there all day and had the best time ever.

Once, there was a boy named Sam. He was 3 years old and he lived on a farm. Every morning, he went for a walk in the field. One morning, Sam saw something strange. He saw smoke coming from the other side of the field. He walked closer and realized that a row of hay was burning. He was so surprised! He wanted to know who started the fire. He asked a neighbor who was walking past. "Did you start the fire?" he asked. The neighbor smiled and said, "No, I did not start it. I think it was the farmer." Sam returned home to tell his father about the warm fire in the field. His father said that he was very glad the fire happened because it meant the farmer could use the field to grow many crops very quickly. Sam was very happy! He had learned something new that day.

One day, a girl named Mia and her mom made soup. Mia thought it was very important to make the best soup ever. They put in lots of good things like carrots, peas, and chicken. Mia was so happy to help her mom. Mia's friend, Tim, came over to play. They laughed and played with their toys. Then, Mia's mom said, "Time to eat the soup!" Mia told Tim, "You will love our soup. It is the best soup ever!" But when Tim tried the soup, he made a funny face. "Yuck! I don't like this soup," he said. Mia was sad and a little mad. She wanted Tim to like the soup too. Mia's mom had an idea. She added some more things to the soup, like corn and a little salt. Tim tried the soup again. This time, he smiled and said, "Now, this is the best soup ever!" Mia laughed and felt so happy. They all enjoyed the soup together. Mia learned that sometimes, even the most important soup can be made better with a little help.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily and her deaf dog, Max. Max loved to play with Lily, and they had a lot of fun together. One day, Lily and Max went outside to gather some sticks for a game of fetch. As they were gathering sticks, Max found a mop in the yard. He picked up the mop with his mouth and brought it to Lily. "Look, Max found a mop!" Lily said. She decided to use the mop to clean their play area outside. Lily started to mop the ground, and Max helped her by holding the mop with his mouth. They worked together, and soon their play area was clean. "Good job, Max!" Lily praised her dog. Max wagged his tail happily, proud of their teamwork.

Lila and Ben are best friends. They like to play with dolls and cars in the park. But one day, Lila has a red spot on her arm. It is very itchy. She scratches it a lot. "Mom, what is this?" Lila asks. "That is a bug bite, honey. You need to put some cream on it and leave it alone. If you scratch it too much, it will get worse," Mom says. Lila does not like the cream. It is cold and sticky. She wants to go to the park and play with Ben. But Mom says she has to stay home until the bite is better. Lila is sad. She misses Ben. She wonders if he misses her too. Ben is in the park. He has his car and his doll. But he does not have Lila. He looks around for her. He does not see her. "Where is Lila?" Ben asks. "She is sick. She has a bug bite. She cannot play today," a girl says. Ben is sad. He misses Lila. He wishes she was here. He sees a big bug on a flower. He thinks it is the bug that bit Lila. He is angry. He wants to make the bug go away. He picks up a stick and hits the bug. The bug falls off the flower. It does not move. Ben feels guilty. He did not mean to hurt the bug. He just wanted to help Lila. He runs to the bug. He says he is sorry. He hopes the bug is okay. He hears a voice. It is Lila. "Ben, what are you doing?" Lila says. She is in the park. She has a bandage on her arm. She sneaked out of the house. She wanted to see Ben. Ben is happy. He hugs Lila. He tells her what he did. Lila is sorry. She says he did not have to do that. She says the bug did not mean to bite her. She says she is not mad at the bug. She says she missed him. He says he missed her too. They play with their dolls and cars. They are best friends. They are happy.
