Category: Beginner
Title: The Judge and the Bug

Once upon a time, there was a big judge who lived in a small house. The judge was very kind and always ready to help others. One day, the judge saw a tiny bug on a leaf. The bug was very sad. The judge asked the bug, "Why are you so sad?" The bug blinked its small eyes and said, "I am lost and cannot find my way home." The judge wanted to help the little bug. So, they started to look for the bug's home together. They walked and walked until they found the bug's family. The bug was so happy and thanked the judge. The judge felt good for helping the bug. From that day on, the judge and the bug were the best of friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was a nervous mouse who lived in a small house. One day, Tim went out to look for some cheese. Tim saw a big cat named Max. Max had a big piece of cheese. Tim wanted to ask Max to give him some cheese, but he was too nervous. Tim's friend, Sam the squirrel, told him to be brave and ask Max for some cheese. Tim took a deep breath and asked Max, "Can you give me some cheese, please?" Max smiled and gave Tim some cheese. Tim said, "Thank you!" and shared the cheese with Sam. They both enjoyed the cheese together. From that day on, Tim learned that being brave and asking for help can lead to good things. And he was not so nervous anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a regular stand where a man was selling toys. Tim's mom said, "You can pick one toy, Tim." Tim looked at the toys and picked a big red car. As Tim played with his car in the park, it got very dirty. Tim said, "Mom, my car is dirty. I need to wash it." His mom gave him a cloth and a small cup of water. Tim started to wash his car. While Tim was washing his car, a little girl came up to him. She said, "Hi, I have a magic cloth. If you wash your car with it, something fun will happen!" Tim took the magic cloth and washed his car. Suddenly, the car started to grow! It became a real car, big enough for Tim and the little girl to ride in. They both laughed and drove around the park, having the best day ever.

Sara liked to play with her dad's files. They were colorful and had many letters and numbers on them. She liked to pretend they were books or cards or puzzles. Sometimes, she would hide them under her bed or in her closet. One day, her dad was looking for a file he needed for work. He asked Sara if she had seen it. It was blue and had the word "report" on it. Sara did not answer. She knew she had taken the file and put it in her dollhouse. "Sara, please listen to me," her dad said. "I need the file. It is very important. Where is it?" Sara felt bad. She did not want to make her dad angry or sad. She liked her dad. He was charming and funny and kind. He always read her stories and played with her. She decided to tell him the truth. She went to her dollhouse and took out the file. She gave it to her dad and said, "I'm sorry, dad. I took the file. I wanted to play with it. It was pretty." Her dad smiled and hugged her. He said, "Thank you, Sara. I'm glad you told me. I'm not angry. But please don't take my files again. They are not toys. They are for work. OK?" Sara nodded and said, "OK, dad. I'm sorry. I love you." Her dad said, "I love you too, Sara. You are a good girl. Now, how about we read a story together?" Sara smiled and said, "Yes, please. Can we read the one with the princess and the dragon?" Her dad said, "Sure. That's a charming story. Let's go to the couch and listen to it."
