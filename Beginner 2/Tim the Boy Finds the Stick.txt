Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Stick

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy box. One day, he decided to unpack his toy box to find his favorite toy, a long stick. Tim took out all his toys from the box. He found the stick at the bottom. He was so happy! He picked it up and ran outside to play. While he was playing, he met his friend, Jane. "Hi Tim!" said Jane. "What are you playing with?" Tim showed her his stick and said, "I found my favorite stick in my toy box! Let's play together!" Jane agreed and they played all day. Later, they felt hungry. Tim's mom called them inside for a snack. She gave them a delicious apple each. They both ate their apples and said, "Thank you, Mom! These apples are so delicious!" After eating, Tim and Jane went back outside to play with the stick. They had lots of fun and played until the sun went down. Then, they went home to rest and get ready for another day of play.

Once upon a time, there was a kind doctor. The doctor had a patient. The patient was a little dog. The dog was very sick, but it was an obedient dog. The doctor liked the dog a lot. One day, the dog wanted to go outside. The doctor did not permit the dog to go outside. The doctor said, "You are sick. You must stay here and get better." The dog was sad but listened to the doctor. The next day, the doctor came to see the dog. The dog was not in the room. The doctor looked outside. The dog was there, but it was not sick anymore! The dog had found a magic flower that made it all better. The dog was happy, and the doctor was happy too. The doctor let the dog go outside and play.

Sam and Mia wanted to make bread with Mom. They helped Mom put flour, water, yeast and salt in a big bowl. Mom said, "Now we have to wait for the dough to rise. It will get bigger and softer." She put the bowl in a warm place and covered it with a cloth. Sam and Mia were bored. They wanted to play with the dough. They sneaked into the kitchen and took the bowl. They took out some dough and made shapes with it. They made a ball, a snake, a star and a heart. They giggled and had fun. Mom came back and saw the mess. She was angry. She said, "What did you do? You ruined the dough! Now we can't make bread. The dough is too small and hard. You have to throw it away." She took the bowl and the shapes and threw them in the trash. Sam and Mia were sad. They said, "We're sorry, Mom. We just wanted to play. We didn't know the dough was important." Mom said, "You should have listened to me. The dough is alive. It needs time and warmth to grow. If you play with it, you kill it. And you waste food. That's not nice." Sam and Mia cried. They wanted to make bread with Mom. They wanted to eat hot and yummy bread. But they couldn't. They had no dough. They had no bread. They had no fun. They had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a mixer. The mixer was very happy. It liked to mix things all day long. One day, the mixer was mixing a big cake. It was very hard work. The mixer was very tired. But it kept mixing, because it wanted to make the best cake ever. Suddenly, the cake mix split! The mixer was very surprised. The mixer looked at the split cake mix. It had turned into two small cakes! The mixer was so happy. It did not have to work so hard now. The two small cakes were just as good as the big cake. The mixer learned that sometimes, when things go wrong, they can still turn out right. And the mixer and the two small cakes lived happily ever after in the tidy little kitchen.
