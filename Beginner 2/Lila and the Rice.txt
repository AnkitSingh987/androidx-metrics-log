Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Rice

Lila liked rice. She liked to eat it with her mom and dad. They cooked it in a big pot with water and salt. Sometimes they added beans or chicken or cheese. Lila liked all of them. One day, Lila saw a new bag of rice in the kitchen. It was big and shiny and had a picture of a panda on it. Lila loved pandas. She wanted to see what the rice looked like. She climbed on a chair and reached for the bag. But the bag was too heavy and it slipped from her hands. It fell on the floor and burst open. Rice spilled everywhere. Lila was scared. She knew she had made a mess. She heard her mom coming from the living room. She hid behind the chair and hoped her mom would not be angry. But her mom saw the rice and the bag and the chair. She was not happy. "Lila, what did you do?" her mom asked in a loud voice. "This is a lot of rice to waste. And it was a special rice from grandma. She sent it from China for us to try. How could you be so careless?" Lila felt bad. She did not mean to waste the rice or make her mom sad. She came out from behind the chair and said sorry. She tried to explain that she liked pandas and wanted to see the rice. But her mom did not listen. She told Lila to go to her room and think about what she had done. She said she would clean up the rice later. Lila went to her room. She was sad and lonely. She wished she had not touched the rice. She wished her mom would hug her and forgive her. She wished they could eat the rice together and be happy. She did not know that her dad had come home from work. He saw the rice and the bag and the chair. He saw his wife cleaning up the mess. He saw his daughter crying in her room. He felt sorry for all of them. He had an idea. He went to the kitchen and took some of the rice. He added some water and salt and put it in a small pot. He turned on the stove and waited for the rice to cook. He smelled the rice and it smelled good. He hoped it would taste good too. He went to Lila's room and knocked on the door. He opened it and saw Lila sitting on her bed. She looked up and saw her dad. She smiled a little. "Hi, Lila," her dad said. "I have something for you. Do you want to try some of the panda rice?" Lila nodded. She liked her dad. He was kind and funny. He always made her feel better. He lifted her up and carried her to the kitchen. He gave her a bowl of rice and a spoon. He sat next to her and took a bite. He smiled. "Mmm, this is good rice," he said. "It is soft and fluffy and yummy. Do you like it?" Lila took a bite. She liked it too. It was different from the rice they usually ate, but it was good. She smiled back. "Yes, I like it," she said. "Thank you, dad." Her dad hugged her and kissed her cheek. He said he was proud of her for being brave and honest. He said he loved her very much. He said they would share some of the rice with mom and make her happy too. Lila felt happy. She loved her dad very much. She loved her mom too. She hoped she would not be mad at her anymore. She hoped they would all eat the rice together and be peaceful.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Toot. Toot was a very soft train. He liked to help his friends. One day, Toot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. Toot asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I want to go to the park, but it is too far." Toot had an idea. "I can take you to the park, Lily. I am a train!" Lily was happy. She got on Toot and they went to the park. They saw many pretty flowers and big trees. Lily and Toot played all day. They enjoyed their time at the park. At the end of the day, Toot took Lily back home. Lily said, "Thank you, Toot! I had fun today." Toot was happy to help. From that day on, Toot and Lily were best friends. They enjoyed many more fun days together.
