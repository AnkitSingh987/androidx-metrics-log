Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Gas and the Spicy

Once there was a little village and every day the people would load up their cars with gas. One day, the gas was extra spicy. Everyone who used it thought it tasted funny. The villagers weren't sure what to do. Then, a brave man named Burk walked into the village. He said he had a plan to fix the problem. First, he said, they should make the gas less spicy. Everyone got to work, mixing the gas and blending it until it had the right taste. When they were done, they all cheered! The spicy gas was gone, and the village was safe. The people thanked Burk for his help. From then on they never had a problem with spicy gas again!

Lily and Sam went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells. They saw a big crab with red claws. It was hiding under a rock. "Look, a crab!" Lily said. She wanted to touch it. "Be careful, Lily. It can pinch you," Sam said. He was older and knew more things. Lily did not listen. She reached out her hand to the crab. The crab saw her and moved fast. It snapped its claws at her. Lily was scared and pulled back her hand. "Ow, ow, ow!" she cried. "The crab hurt me!" Mom and Dad heard her and ran to her. They checked her hand. It had a small cut. They cleaned it and put a band-aid on it. "It's okay, Lily. The crab was just scared too. It did not want to be bothered," Mom said. "I'm sorry, crab. I won't touch you again," Lily said. She blinked her eyes and smiled. Sam gave her a hug. "Don't worry, Lily. You are brave. Let's go find some nice shells instead," he said. They left the crab alone and went to look for shells. They had fun at the beach. They learned to be careful with crabs.

Lily was sad. She had a big boo-boo on her knee. She fell down when she was playing with her bike. It hurt a lot and it was red and wet. She cried and cried. Mommy heard her cry and ran to her. She hugged her and said, "Oh, no, my sweet Lily. What happened? Let me see your boo-boo." She took a cloth and some water and cleaned her knee. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it. "There, there. It will be better soon. You are very brave, Lily." Lily felt a little better, but she was still sad. She said, "Mommy, I'm sorry. I was not careful. I did not look where I was going. I broke my bike." She pointed to her bike, which had a bent wheel and a torn seat. Mommy looked at the bike and said, "Oh, dear. That's not good. But don't worry, Lily. We can fix your bike later. The most important thing is that you are okay. I'm not mad at you, Lily. I love you very much. But you have to be more careful next time, okay? Bikes are fun, but they can also be dangerous. You have to watch out for rocks and cars and other things that can make you fall." Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Mommy. I will be more careful. I love you too, Mommy. Thank you for making me feel better." She hugged Mommy and put her head on her shoulder. Mommy comforted her and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm always here for you. Do you want to hear a story?" Lily said, "Yes, please." Mommy said, "Okay, then. Listen with your ear. I will tell you a story about a little girl who had a magic bike." Lily smiled and listened. She forgot about her boo-boo and her broken bike. She felt happy and safe with Mommy.

One day, a big red bird saw a huge ladder. The bird wanted to climb the ladder. So, the bird went up the ladder, step by step. The bird saw a tiny mouse on the ground. The mouse looked up and said, "Be careful, bird! Don't lose the ladder!" The bird climbed higher and higher. The sky was very blue. The bird looked down and saw the tiny mouse far away. The bird thought about what the mouse said. It was careful not to lose the ladder. At the top, the bird saw a big nest. Inside the nest, there were three eggs. The bird knew these eggs were lost. The bird was happy it did not lose the ladder. It went down the ladder to tell the tiny mouse. The mouse and the bird found the egg's mom. The mom was happy to see her eggs. The bird and the mouse were happy too. They knew they did a good thing by helping the mom find her lost eggs.
