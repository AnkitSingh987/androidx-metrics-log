Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Sam. Sam loved to play with his ball. The ball had a fun texture. It was soft and bumpy. Sam felt happy when he played with his ball. One day, a big cat named Tom came to Sam's yard. Tom saw Sam's ball and wanted it. Tom was not weak. He was strong and fast. Sam didn't want to give his ball to Tom. They started to fight. They both wanted the ball. Sam and Tom fought for a long time. They both got tired. Then, Sam had an idea. They could share the ball and play together. Tom liked this idea. They stopped fighting and became friends. They played with the ball together and had fun.

Lily and Max were twins. They liked to play with their toys and books. But they did not like to go to bed. Every night, they tried to stay up as long as they could. One night, their mom said, "It's time for bed, kids. You have school tomorrow. Come on, zip up your pajamas and brush your teeth." Lily and Max groaned. They did not want to zip up their pajamas. They wanted to play some more. They ran to their room and hid under the bed. "Come out, come out, wherever you are," their mom said, looking for them. "Don't you want to hear a story before you sleep?" Lily and Max loved stories. They peeked out from under the bed. They saw their mom holding a big book. It had a picture of a dragon on the cover. "What's that?" Lily asked. "It's a new book I got for you. It's about a brave knight who fights a dragon. Do you want to hear it?" their mom asked. Lily and Max nodded. They crawled out from under the bed and ran to their mom. They zipped up their pajamas and brushed their teeth. They snuggled with their mom on the couch and listened to the story. The story was exciting and scary. The knight had to face many dangers to reach the dragon's cave. He had a sword and a shield and a horse. The dragon had fire and claws and wings. "Will the knight win?" Max asked. "You'll have to wait and see," their mom said. "But I'll give you a hint. The knight has a secret weapon that the dragon doesn't know about." Lily and Max wondered what the secret weapon was. They tried to guess, but they couldn't figure it out. They listened to the story with wide eyes and open mouths. But soon, their eyes and mouths started to close. They felt sleepy. The story was too long for them. They yawned and stretched and cuddled with their mom. Their mom smiled and kissed them. She closed the book and said, "The end." "What? No, no, no!" Lily and Max said. "What about the secret weapon? What about the dragon? What about the knight?" "Sorry, kids. It's too late. You have to sleep now. Maybe I'll tell you the rest of the story next week. If you behave and go to bed on time," their mom said. Lily and Max pouted. They wanted to know the end of the story. But they were too sleepy to argue. They followed their mom to their room and got into their beds. Their mom tucked them in and turned off the light. "Good night, kids. I love you," their mom said. "Good night, mom. We love you too," Lily and Max said. They closed their eyes and dreamed of dragons and knights and secret weapons. They hoped that next week would come soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Every morning, Tom would wake up and watch the sun come up. He loved to see the sky turn from dark to light. He also liked to watch the birds fly and the flowers open. One day, Tom saw a little bird trying to build a nest. The bird was having a hard time finding useful things to make the nest strong. Tom wanted to help the bird, so he looked around for sticks and leaves. He gave them to the bird and said, "Here, use these to make your nest better." The bird was very happy and thanked Tom. "Thank you, Tom, for helping me. Now my nest will be strong and safe for my babies." Tom felt good about helping the bird and learned that it is important to be kind and useful to others. From that day on, Tom always tried to help his friends and family. He learned that being useful and kind made everyone happy, including himself. And every morning, Tom would still watch the sun come up, knowing that he could make a difference in the world.
