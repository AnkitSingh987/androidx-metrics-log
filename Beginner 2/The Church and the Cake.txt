Category: Beginner
Title: The Church and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big church. The church was having a party to celebrate a special day. Many people came to the church to have fun and play games. Tom, a clumsy boy, went to the church with his mom. Tom was excited to play games and eat cake. He ran around the church, but he was not careful. He bumped into a table and almost knocked over the cake! "Oh no, Tom!" said his mom, "You need to be more careful when you play." Tom felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I'll be more careful." From then on, Tom played carefully and had lots of fun. The church party was a big success, and everyone had a great time.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved to climb. She would climb on chairs, beds, and trees. One day, she saw a big tree with delicate flowers near her house. She wanted to climb the tree and pick the flowers. As she climbed higher and higher, she heard a tiny voice. A small bird said, "Please don't pick the flowers. They are my home." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, little bird. I didn't know." Lily climbed back down to the floor and thought of a new plan. She found a pretty rock and gave it to the bird. They became friends and played together. The bird showed Lily how to make flower crowns without hurting the tree. The moral of the story is to be kind to all living things and to think before you act.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play at the park. One day, they see a big car. It is shiny and has four doors. A man and a woman get out of the car. They wear nice clothes and shiny things. They look rich. Ben and Lily want to say hello. They run to the car and do a wave. The man and the woman do not see them. They walk to a big house. They do not wave back. Ben and Lily feel sad. They go back to the park. They see a dog. It is brown and has a long tail. It runs to them and licks their faces. It is happy to see them. Ben and Lily do a laugh. They do a hug to the dog. The dog does a wag to its tail. Ben and Lily feel happy. They play with the dog. They throw a ball and the dog brings it back. They do a tickle to the dog's belly. The dog does a bark and a roll. They have fun. They do not care about the big car or the rich people. They have a friend. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big pot. It was a very mild day. The pot was in the kitchen. It was a happy pot because it was full of soup. One day, the pot decided to go outside. It wanted to see the sun and the trees. The pot rolled out the door and into the yard. It saw the birds and the flowers. The pot was very happy. Then, the pot decided to go back inside. It rolled back into the kitchen. The soup was still warm. The pot was happy to be home. And everyone who ate the soup was happy too. The end.

One day, a playful cat named Tom found a big cup of milk. He loved milk so much! But there was a problem. The milk was on a high table, and Tom could not reach it. Tom had a smart idea. He would pretend to be a tall giraffe so he could drink the milk. He stood on his back legs and stretched his neck very long. He tried and tried, but he still could not reach the milk. Then, Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came to help. Max was very strong. Max pushed a chair to the table for Tom. Tom climbed on the chair and finally reached the milk. Tom and Max were so happy! They shared the milk and had a fun day together.
