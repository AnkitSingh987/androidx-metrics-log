Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cup

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. The park was very big and bright. There were many kids playing and laughing. Amy saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. Under the tree, Amy found a small, bright cup. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I found a pretty cup!" Amy said. Her mom smiled and told her, "That's a special cup, Amy. It can be used to make new friends." The next day, Amy went back to the park with the bright cup. She saw a little boy playing alone. He looked sad. Amy walked over to him and asked, "Do you want to play with me and my special cup?" The boy's face lit up, and he said, "Yes, I'd love to!" Amy and the boy played together all day, using the bright cup in their games. They became best friends, and from that day on, they always played together at the park. The bright cup had helped Amy make a new friend, just like her mom said it would.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow farm. On the farm, there were many animals. There was a cow, a pig, a horse, and a duck. They all lived together and played all day. One day, the duck lost his toy. He was very sad. The cow wanted to help, so she lent him her toy. The duck was happy again, and they played together with the cow's toy. After a while, the pig found the duck's toy. He gave it back to the duck. The duck was very happy to have his toy again. The cow, the pig, the horse, and the duck played together with both toys. They had a lot of fun on the yellow farm.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bug with incredible big wings. He picked it up and put it on his arm. The bug tickled him, and he laughed. Tim wanted to show the bug to his friend, Lily. "Look, Lily!" Tim said, "I found a bug with big wings!" Lily looked at the bug and smiled. She wanted to kiss the bug to make friends with it. So, she kissed the bug on its head. But something unexpected happened. The bug didn't like the kiss. It got mad and bit Lily's arm. Lily cried and Tim felt bad. They learned that not all bugs like to be kissed.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Tim. They lived in a house with a nice lady. The nice lady loved to cook. One day, she made a yummy sauce for her food. Tom and Tim could smell the sauce, and they both wanted some. Tom and Tim decided to work together to get the sauce. Tom climbed up on the table, and Tim held a spoon. Tom dipped the spoon into the sauce, and they both had a taste. It was so good! But then, Tom started to feel jealous. He wanted all the sauce for himself. Tom tried to take the sauce away from Tim, but the nice lady saw them. She was not happy. She told Tom and Tim to share. Tom and Tim felt bad and said sorry. They learned that it is better to share and work together than to be jealous and fight. The nice lady gave them both a little bit of sauce, and they were happy.

One day, a long cat went for a walk. The cat saw a tall stand with big red balls. The cat wanted a ball to play with, so she jumped up to get one. As the cat jumped, she did not see a small dog. The dog was running fast and hit the stand. The balls began to fall off the stand. The cat was scared and jumped off the stand too. The cat and the dog became friends. They played with the balls that fell. They had fun all day long. The cat was happy she jumped on the stand, even though it was not what she planned.
