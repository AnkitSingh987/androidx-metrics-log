Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a small, fragile bird named Tim. Tim had a tiny head and he liked to stay near his nest. His nest was on a big tree in a nice park. One day, Tim saw a big, red ball near the tree. He wanted to play with it. Tim left his nest and went to the ball. He tried to push the ball with his small head, but it was very hard. Tim did not give up. He pushed and pushed the ball with his head. But then, the ball rolled too fast and hit a rock. Tim's fragile head got hurt and he could not fly back to his nest. Tim was very sad and stayed on the ground, wishing he had stayed near his nest.

Once upon a time, there was a little angel. The angel was sad and worried. She was ashamed because she lost her magic wand. She needed the wand to help people. One day, the angel went to find her wand. She looked in the sky, in the trees, and in the flowers. But she could not find it. The angel felt bad and worried more. The angel asked her friends for help. They looked everywhere too. But they could not find the wand. The angel was very sad. She could not help people anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a dress. The dress lived with its owner, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved her dress very much. It was a regular dress, not too fancy, but very pretty. One day, Sue and the dress went to the park. They saw a dog playing with a ball. Sue put on her dress and said, "Hello, dog! I am Sue. This is my dress. Do you want to play with us?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Sue, the dress, and the dog played together. They ran and laughed, and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Sue said, "Goodbye, dog! We had fun playing with you!" The dog barked and wagged its tail. Sue and her dress went home, happy to have a new friend.

Ben and Lily were playing with their crayons and paper. They liked to draw and write letters. Ben knew how to write his name and Lily knew how to write hers. They were very proud of their letters. But then, Ben saw that Lily had an enormous letter L on her paper. He wanted to have an enormous letter too. He took his red crayon and tried to mark a big B on his paper. But he made a mistake and marked over Lily's L. "Hey, that's my L!" Lily shouted. "You marked over it! That's not nice!" "I'm sorry, Lily," Ben said. "I wanted to have a big letter too. But I messed up. Can I fix it?" Lily was angry and sad. She liked her L and she did not want Ben to mark over it. She did not want to share her paper with him. She said, "No, you can't fix it. You ruined it. Go away!" Ben felt bad. He did not mean to hurt Lily's feelings. He liked Lily and he wanted to play with her. He said, "Please, Lily, don't be mad. I'll give you a new paper and a new crayon. And I'll help you write a new L. Can we be friends again?" Lily looked at Ben. She saw that he was sorry and that he wanted to make up. She thought for a moment. Then she said, "Okay, Ben. I forgive you. You can give me a new paper and a new crayon. And we can write big letters together. But don't mark over my L again, okay?" Ben smiled and nodded. He said, "Okay, Lily. I won't mark over your L again. I promise. Thank you for being my friend." They hugged and then they went back to their crayons and paper. They wrote big letters and drew pictures. They had fun and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant with a long trunk. The elephant had a loyal friend, a small mouse. They liked to play and eat together. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard, and the trees began to tremble. The mouse was scared and ran to the elephant for help. The elephant used his strong trunk to hold onto the trees and keep them from falling. The storm went away, and the sun came out again. The mouse was very happy that his loyal friend the elephant had saved him. They continued to play and eat together, and they were the best of friends.
