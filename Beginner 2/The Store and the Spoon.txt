Category: Beginner
Title: The Store and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a store. In the store, there was a lonely pot. The lonely pot wanted to be used. One day, a little girl named Sue came into the store with her mom. Sue saw the lonely pot and said, "Mom, can we buy this pot?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can use it to stir soup at home." Sue and her mom took the lonely pot home. They made a big pot of soup. Sue used a big spoon to stir the soup in the pot. The pot was happy to be used and not lonely anymore. Sue and her mom enjoyed the soup, and the pot loved being part of their family.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big box of stuff. He loved his stuff. He had toys, balls, and books. Tim liked to play with his stuff every day. It was easy for him to have fun with it. Tim's mom told him that they were going to visit his friend, Sue. Tim was very happy. When Tim got to Sue's house, he saw that she had a small box of stuff. Tim thought that it was not as fun as his big box. They played together, but Tim missed his own stuff. He wanted to go home and play with his big box of stuff. Sue saw Tim was sad and asked him what was wrong. Tim told Sue about his big box of stuff and how it was more fun. Sue had an idea. She told Tim to close his eyes. When he opened them, Sue had a big box too! It was full of fun stuff that Sue had hidden. Tim was so surprised and happy. They played together with both big boxes of stuff. Tim thanked Sue for sharing her big box of stuff with him. They had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a cap that he loved very much. It was an ordinary cap, but it was special to him. One day, Tim went to the park with his cap. He saw a big slide and wanted to play. He put his cap on his head and climbed up the slide. When he got to the top, he looked down and felt happy. Tim slid down the slide with a big smile on his face. His cap flew off his head and landed on the ground. He picked up his cap, put it back on, and went to slide again. Tim played on the slide all day long, laughing and having fun with his ordinary cap.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a high tree with his family. Every day, Tim would fly around and look for food. One day, he found some meat on the ground. Tim was very happy. Tim took the meat back to his family in the high tree. His mom said, "Good job, Tim! This meat will help us grow big and strong." Tim felt proud and his family was happy. Later that day, Tim's sister, Tina, was scared to fly. Tim said, "Don't worry, Tina. I will comfort you." He held her wing and helped her fly. They flew around the high tree together, feeling safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. The bear lived in a big forest. He liked to play with his friends. One day, the bear saw an unknown toy. The toy was very pretty. The bear wanted to play with the toy. The bear went to his friend, the rabbit. He asked, "Can I borrow your toy?" The rabbit said, "No, that toy is not mine. I don't know who it belongs to." The bear was sad. He still wanted to play with the toy. The bear thought for a moment. He decided to take the toy without asking. He played with the toy all day. But, when it was time to go home, the bear lost the toy. He felt very bad. He should have listened to his friend, the rabbit. Now, the toy was gone and the bear was sad. The end.
