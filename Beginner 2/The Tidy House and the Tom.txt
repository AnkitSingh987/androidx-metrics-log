Category: Beginner
Title: The Tidy House and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house. In the house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Tom noticed a knife on the table. He knew it was not safe for him to touch. Tom called his mom and said, "Mom, there is a knife on the table!" His mom came and saw the knife. She was happy that Tom did not touch it. She put the knife away in a safe place. Tom felt good that he helped keep the house tidy and safe. From that day on, Tom always noticed things that needed to be fixed or cleaned. He helped his mom make their home a happy and tidy place. They lived happily ever after, always working together to keep their house nice and clean.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. They have many cars and trucks in their room. They are organized. They put each car and truck in a box with a label. One day, they decide to play outside. They take some of their cars and trucks with them. They find a big hill. They make a road on the hill with dirt and rocks. They race their cars and trucks down the hill. They have fun. After a while, they feel thirsty. They see a water fountain near the hill. They run to the water fountain. They take turns to drink water. They feel better. "Ah, that was good water," Tim says. "Yes, it was," Sam agrees. "Do you want to play some more?" Tim looks at the hill. He sees their cars and trucks scattered on the road. He feels tired. "I want to go back to the room," he says. "I want to rest and organize our cars and trucks." Sam nods. He feels tired too. "OK, let's go back to the room," he says. "We can play again tomorrow." They pick up their cars and trucks. They put them in their backpacks. They walk back to the room. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an ordinary boy who loved to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a big box in his room. He was very excited to see what was inside the box. When Tim opened the box, he found a file with lots of colorful strings. Tim decided to tie the strings together to make a big rope. He thought it would be fun to use the rope to play with his friends. Tim took the rope outside and called his friends. They all played with the rope, tying it to trees and using it to jump over. It was a great day for Tim and his friends, and they all had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful ant. The powerful ant loved to eat apples. One day, he saw a big, red apple on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to eat it. But, the apple was too big for the powerful ant to carry. He asked his ant friends to help him. They all tried to lift the apple, but they could not. They were sad because they could not eat the apple. They decided to delay their plan and think of a new way to eat it. Then, a little girl saw the ants and the big apple. She picked up the apple and saw the ants. She knew the ants wanted the apple. So, she broke the apple into small pieces and gave them to the ants. The ants were very happy and thanked the little girl. The powerful ant and his friends enjoyed the apple and became friends with the little girl.

Once upon a time, in a mild, warm land, there was a flea. This flea lived on a big, soft dog. The dog and the flea were good friends. They played together all day long. One day, the dog said, "Flea, let's go to the big tree and play." The flea was very happy and jumped on the dog's back. They ran and ran until they were close to the big tree. As they played near the tree, a big wind came and blew the flea far away. The dog was sad and said, "Oh no, where is my friend Flea?" The dog looked and looked but could not find the flea. Then, a small bird flew close to the dog and said, "I saw your friend Flea. He is on my back now. I will take him back to you." The dog was very happy and thanked the bird. The bird flew close to the dog and the flea jumped back on his friend. The dog and the flea were happy to be together again. They played and played until the sun went down.
