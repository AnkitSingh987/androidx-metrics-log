Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Magic

One day, a big fish named Bob wanted to dive. He saw a TV in the water. The TV was dead. Bob was sad. He wanted to watch the TV. A crab named Tim saw Bob. Tim wanted to help. Tim had an idea. He found a big shell. Tim put the dead TV in the shell. The TV turned on! Bob and Tim were happy. They watched the TV together. They did not know the shell had magic. The magic shell made the dead TV work. Now, Bob could dive and watch TV with his friend, Tim.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they have a test at school. The test is about colors and shapes. Tim and Sam study hard. They want to do well on the test. The next day, they go to school. They wear their backpacks. They zip them up. They say hello to their teacher. She gives them the test. Tim and Sam look at the test. They see red, blue, yellow, green, circle, square, triangle, star. They know the answers. They write them down. They are happy. They finish the test. They give it to their teacher. She smiles. She says, "Good job, Tim and Sam. You did very well on the test. You are smart." Tim and Sam smile. They are proud. They say, "Thank you, teacher." The teacher says, "You can have a snack now. I have cookies and milk for you. They are delicious." Tim and Sam love cookies and milk. They go to the snack table. They see the cookies and milk. They are happy. They say, "Yummy, yummy." They take a cookie and a cup of milk. They eat and drink. They say, "Mmm, mmm." They enjoy the snack. They share with their friends. They have fun. They are glad they did well on the test. They are glad they have cookies and milk. They are glad they have each other. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a tree. All the animals liked the wise owl. They liked to listen to the owl's stories. One day, a little bird was sad. The bird lost its toy. The wise owl told the bird to remain calm. The owl said, "We will find your toy. You just need to think and look." The little bird and the wise owl looked for the toy. They looked up and down. They looked left and right. At last, they found the toy under a bush. The little bird was happy. The wise owl told the little bird, "When you lose something, remain calm and think. You will find it." The little bird learned to stay calm and think. All the animals learned from the wise owl. They became wise too. And they all lived happily in the big universe.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small, sad bird. The bird had lost her friend, a tiny pebble. The pebble was very special to her. She looked everywhere for the pebble, but she could not find it. The sad bird asked her friends, the squirrel and the rabbit, to help her. They all nodded and said, "Yes, we will help you find your pebble." The squirrel, the rabbit, and the sad bird looked for the pebble all day. They looked under leaves and around the trees. The sun was going down, and they still could not find the pebble. The sad bird was very worried. She thought she would never see her special pebble again. But then, the squirrel saw something shiny by the river. He ran to it and picked it up. It was the pebble! The sad bird was so happy! She thanked her friends for helping her find her special pebble. The squirrel and the rabbit were happy too. They all nodded and went home, knowing that they had done a good thing.

One day, two brothers went to the park. They both loved to play there. One brother happened to find a berry, which he decided to give to the other. When he held it out, the other started to laugh and told him that it was silly. This made the first brother very embarrassed. He dropped the berry and ran away. The brothers never went to the park together again after that.
