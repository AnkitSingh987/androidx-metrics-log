Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big, harmless dog named Spot. They loved to play together. One day, Amy found a big white sheet. She got an idea to play a game. Amy put the sheet on Spot and said, "Now you are a ghost dog!" Spot did not know what to do, so he just sat down. Amy's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the ghost dog and got scared. "Don't be scared," Amy said. "It is just Spot with a sheet." Tim laughed and joined the game. They all played and had fun. Then, Amy's mom came out and saw the mess. "Who did this?" she asked. Amy and Tim looked at each other and said, "We did." They learned it is important to speak the truth and clean up after playing. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to fix a toy. He went to his dad for help. His dad said, "We need dough to fix it." Tom was sad because he did not have any dough. Tom went outside to think. It was an icy day. He saw a big snow hill. Tom got an idea. He took some snow and made it into dough. He was very happy. Tom and his dad used the icy snow dough to fix the toy. It worked! But then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to move and talk! It was a magic toy. Tom and his dad were very surprised and they all played together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to her office. Tim was very excited. He thought the office was a great place. There were big chairs and lots of papers. In the office, Tim saw a little bug. He wanted to catch it. He ran after the bug, but it was very fast. Tim's mom saw him and said, "What are you doing, Tim?" "I want to catch the bug, Mom!" Tim said. His mom smiled and helped him catch the bug. They put it outside, and Tim was happy. He had a great day at the office with his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat peaches. Every day, she would wake up and eat a peach for breakfast. One day, she saw a big peach tree outside her window. Amy ran to the tree and looked up. She saw the biggest peach at the top. "I want that peach!" she said. Amy started to climb the tree. As she climbed higher, she felt dizzy. "Oh no, I need help!" she cried. Just then, her big brother Tom came outside. He heard Amy and looked up. "Don't worry, Amy! I'll help you!" he said. Tom climbed the tree and helped Amy down. They picked the big peach together. From that day on, Amy knew she could always count on her brother Tom.

One day, a strong girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She got a big bowl and put in some flour, sugar, and eggs. Then, she mixed it all up. While she was mixing, her friend Tom came over. Tom saw a skull on the table. He asked, "What is that?" Lily said, "It's a skull. I found it outside. I think it's from a bird." Tom thought it was cool. Lily and Tom baked the cake together. They put it in the oven and waited. When it was done, they took it out and let it cool. Then, they put frosting on it and shared the cake. They were very happy and had a fun day.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to jump on the swings, slide down the slide, and run in the grass. It was a sunny day, but the wind was cold. Lily wore a pink sweater that her grandma made for her. Ben wore a blue sweater that his mom bought for him. "Look, Lily, I can jump very high!" Ben said, as he pushed the swing with his legs. "Wow, Ben, you are so good at jumping!" Lily said, as she watched him fly in the air. "Can you jump too, Lily?" Ben asked, as he slowed down his swing. "I can try, Ben," Lily said, as she got ready to jump. But when Lily jumped, her sweater got caught on the swing. She felt a tug and a rip. She fell on the ground and started to cry. "Oh no, Lily, are you okay?" Ben asked, as he ran to help her. He saw that Lily had a small scratch on her knee, and that her sweater had a big hole in it. "I'm sorry, Lily, your sweater is broken," Ben said, as he hugged her. Lily was very sad and worried. She loved her sweater, and she didn't want grandma to be mad at her. "It's okay, Ben, it's not your fault," Lily said, as she sniffed. "Maybe we can fix it, Lily," Ben said, as he looked at the sweater. "Or maybe we can ask mom or grandma to fix it," Lily said, as she hoped. "Let's go home and show them, Lily," Ben said, as he helped her stand up. They held hands and walked to their houses. They hoped that mom and grandma would not be angry, and that they could still play in the park. They learned to be careful when they jump on the swings.
