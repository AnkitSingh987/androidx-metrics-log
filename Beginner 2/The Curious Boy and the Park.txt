Category: Beginner
Title: The Curious Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy. One day he was walking in the park and saw a price sign. He was excited and wanted to know what it was for. He went over to take a closer look. It was a joke sign! He hadn't seen one before and he thought it was funny and gave it a big smile. He asked his mom what it was and found out that it was a joke. He thought it was so silly and laughed and laughed. He wanted to find some more jokes, so he went to the store. He was so amazed at all the different jokes there were. He found ones with funny words and others with colorful pictures. He was so excited he didn't know which one to choose! His mom said that he could get one for the same price as before. He smiled and was so happy. He couldn't wait to start joking with his friends. He brought his joke home and told it to his family. They all laughed and enjoyed it. He was so proud of himself for finding such a funny joke. Since then he has become more and more curious about jokes and continues to look for new ones.

Sara and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run, jump and slide. They had a lot of fun. But soon they became tired. They wanted to go home. "Mom, can we go home now?" Sara asked. "Yes, my loves, it's time to go home. Let's find your shoes," Mom said. Sara and Ben looked for their shoes. They had taken them off to play in the sand. But they could not find them. They saw many shoes, but none of them were theirs. "Where are our shoes, Mom?" Ben asked. "I don't know, my sweet. Maybe someone took them by mistake. Or maybe they are hiding somewhere. Let's look around," Mom said. They looked around the park. They looked under the benches, behind the trees, and in the trash cans. But they could not find their shoes. They were sad and worried. How could they go home without their shoes? Then they saw a big dog. The dog was carrying something in its mouth. It was a shoe! "Hey, that's my shoe!" Sara shouted. She ran after the dog. The dog ran away. It did not want to give the shoe. "Come back, dog! Give me my shoe!" Sara said. Ben and Mom followed Sara. They chased the dog. The dog was fast and clever. It dodged and weaved. It went into the bushes, across the street, and into a store. The store was a pet store. It had many animals inside. There were cats, birds, fish, and hamsters. The dog entered the store. It dropped the shoe and ran to the other dogs. Sara entered the store too. She saw her shoe on the floor. She picked it up. She was happy. "Mom, I found my shoe!" Sara said. "Good job, Sara! You are very brave," Mom said. Ben entered the store too. He looked around. He saw something shiny. It was his shoe! It was on a shelf, next to a fish tank. Someone had put it there. "Mom, I found my shoe too!" Ben said. "Great, Ben! You are very smart," Mom said. They put on their shoes. They thanked the store owner. They left the store. They walked home. They were tired, but they were also proud. They had found their shoes. They had a big adventure. They hugged Mom. They told her they loved her. Mom hugged them back. She told them she loved them too. She was glad they were safe. She was proud of them. She smiled.

Tommy was a lazy boy who did not like to do his chores. He liked to lie on his bed and dream of being a singer. He wanted to sing in the opera, where people wore fancy clothes and clapped for him. One day, his mother told him to clean his room. She said, "Tommy, you have to tidy up your toys and books. You can't be lazy all the time. If you don't clean your room, you can't watch TV tonight." Tommy did not listen to his mother. He said, "I don't want to clean my room. I want to dream of the opera. I want to be a star." He closed his eyes and imagined himself on a big stage, singing a loud song. But while he was dreaming, his mother came into his room. She saw the mess and was very angry. She said, "Tommy, you are a naughty boy. You did not do what I asked you. You can't watch TV tonight. You have to go to bed early." Tommy opened his eyes and saw his mother. He was sad and scared. He said, "I'm sorry, mother. I was dreaming of the opera. Please don't be mad at me. Please let me watch TV." But his mother did not change her mind. She said, "No, Tommy. You have to learn your lesson. You have to work hard and be good. Maybe then you can dream of the opera. But not now. Now you have to go to sleep." She turned off the light and left the room. Tommy cried and wished he had cleaned his room. He hoped that tomorrow he could watch TV and dream of the opera again.
