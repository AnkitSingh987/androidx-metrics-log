Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tree and the Leaf

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tall tree. A little beetle lived on the tall tree. The beetle liked to climb up and down the tree all day. One day, the beetle saw a big leaf at the top of the tree. The beetle wanted to sit on the leaf. The beetle climbed up the tall tree to reach the big leaf. The wind started to blow. The beetle was scared but wanted to sit on the leaf. The wind blew harder, and the beetle held on tight. The beetle thought about going back down, but it kept going up. Finally, the beetle reached the big leaf. The beetle was so happy to sit on the leaf. But then, the wind blew very hard. The little beetle could not hold on any longer. The beetle started to fall. As the beetle fell, it landed on a soft pile of leaves on the ground. The beetle was safe and learned to be careful when climbing the tall tree.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They saw a big box with a bow on it. It was behind the roses. "Look, a present!" Lily said. "Who is it for?" "I don't know. Maybe it's for us!" Tom said. "Let's open it and see." They ran to the box and pulled the bow. The box was heavy and hard to open. They used their fingers and teeth to tear the paper. Inside the box, there was a shiny vase. It was blue and green and had flowers on it. It looked very pretty. "Wow, a vase!" Lily said. "It's so nice. Maybe it's a reward for being good." "Or maybe it's a reward for finding it," Tom said. "We are very clever." They lifted the vase out of the box. It was fragile and slippery. They held it with both hands. "Be careful, don't drop it," Lily said. "It might break." "OK, I will be careful," Tom said. "Let's show it to Mom and Dad. They will be happy." They walked to the house with the vase. They did not see the cat sleeping on the porch. The cat woke up and saw the vase. It was curious and wanted to play. The cat jumped on the vase and pushed it with its paw. The vase fell from Lily and Tom's hands. It hit the ground and broke into pieces. Lily and Tom gasped. They were sad and scared. The vase was gone. "Uh-oh," Lily said. "We broke the vase. It was not ours. It was a gift for Grandma." "Uh-oh," Tom said. "We are in trouble. Mom and Dad will be mad." They heard Mom and Dad coming. They did not know what to do. They looked at the cat. The cat looked at them. It meowed.

Once upon a time, there was a messy octopus. He lived in the sea. He loved to run with his eight legs. One day, he saw a big fish. The big fish was sad. The octopus wanted to help the big fish. The octopus had an idea. He thought, "I will make the big fish laugh." So, he started to run and dance. The big fish looked at the octopus. The big fish started to smile. The octopus was happy to see the big fish smile. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish was not a fish! It was a big whale! The big whale said, "Thank you, octopus, for making me happy." They became best friends and swam together in the sea. The messy octopus learned that even big surprises can lead to new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Lily. She loved the color pink. One day, she wanted to make a big pink picture for her mom. Lily worked hard to complete her picture. She used pink paint, pink crayons, and pink glitter. Her hands were all pink too! She was very happy with her work. When her mom came home, Lily said, "Mom, look what I made for you!" Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Lily! This is a beautiful pink picture. You are so creative!" Lily felt proud and happy.
