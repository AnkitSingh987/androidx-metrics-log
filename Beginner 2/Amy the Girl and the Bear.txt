Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy named Mr. Bear. Amy loved Mr. Bear very much. One day, Amy and Mr. Bear went to the doctor. The doctor needed to take an X-ray of Amy's arm. Amy was worried. The doctor told Amy, "Don't be worried! I'll just take a quick X-ray and then you can go home." Amy knew the doctor was right, but she still felt scared. She held Mr. Bear tight and hoped everything would be okay. The X-ray was fast and didn't hurt. After the X-ray, the doctor said, "Amy, you are very brave. You and Mr. Bear did a great job!" Amy felt happy and proud. She knew she and Mr. Bear could do anything together. They went home, and Amy told her mom about her day. She learned to value her time with Mr. Bear even more.

Sam and Pat are friends. They like to play with old things. One day, they find a big box of copper in the yard. Copper is a shiny brown metal. They think it is pretty. They take some copper to the street. They want to sell it to people. They make a sign that says "Copper for sale". They put the sign on a stick. They wait for people to come. A man in a black car stops. He has a black hat and a black coat. He looks at the copper. He says "How much do you want for this copper?" Sam and Pat do not know. They say "One dollar". The man laughs. He says "One dollar is too little. This copper is worth a lot. I will give you ten dollars for all of it". Sam and Pat are happy. They say "Yes, please". They give the man the copper. He gives them ten dollars. He puts the copper in his car. He drives away. Sam and Pat are very happy. They have ten dollars. They can buy many things. They run to the store. They buy candy, toys, and books. They share with each other. They say "Selling copper was fun. Maybe we can find more copper tomorrow". They go home. They hug their moms. They tell them about their day. They eat their candy. They play with their toys. They read their books. They go to bed. They dream of copper.

Sam and Lily were going on a trip with their mom and dad. They had big luggage with their clothes and toys. They were very happy and excited. They got in the car and drove to the airport. At the airport, they had to wait in a long line. Sam and Lily were bored and tired. They saw a big fountain with water and lights. They wanted to play with the water. They asked their mom and dad if they could go near the fountain. Mom and dad said no. They said the fountain was not for playing. They said they had to stay with them and watch the luggage. But Sam and Lily did not listen. They ran to the fountain when mom and dad were not looking. They thought it would be fun to splash the water. They splashed the water with their hands and feet. They laughed and screamed. They made a big mess. They got wet and cold. They did not see the sign that said "Do not touch the water. It is very hot and dangerous." The water was very hot and dangerous. It burned their skin and made them dizzy. They cried and shouted for help. Mom and dad heard them and ran to the fountain. They were very scared and angry. They took Sam and Lily out of the water and wrapped them in towels. They called for a doctor. The doctor came and looked at Sam and Lily. He said they had bad burns and needed to go to the hospital. He said they could not go on the trip. He said they had to stay in the hospital for many days. He said they had to be very careful with water. Sam and Lily were very sad and sorry. They wished they had listened to their mom and dad. They wished they had not played with the fountain. They wished they had not splashed the water. They learned a hard lesson. They missed their trip. They had a bad ending.
