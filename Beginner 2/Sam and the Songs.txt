Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Songs

Sam was a famous singer. He liked to sing loud and fast songs. He had many fans who loved his songs. But Sam was not happy. He wanted to sing new songs, songs that were slow and soft. He was bored of his old songs. One day, he had a big show. Many people came to see him sing. But Sam did not sing his old songs. He sang his new songs. The people did not like his new songs. They were too different. They wanted to hear his loud and fast songs. They booed and left. Sam was sad and angry. He went to his car. He saw his phone. It was ringing. It was his manager. He answered the phone. "Sam, what did you do?" his manager asked. "You made a big trouble. The people hated your new songs. They want their money back. You are not famous anymore. You are a flop." Sam felt bad. He realized he made a mistake. He should have listened to his fans. He should have sung his old songs. He should have been grateful for his success. He learned a lesson. He said sorry to his manager. He said he would try to fix his trouble. He said he would sing his old songs again. He hoped his fans would forgive him. He hoped he could be famous again.

Once upon a time there was a prince who owned a very special treasure. Every day he played in his garden while the sun was high. He loved to sit by the pond and watch the fish swim around. One day, he got very sleepy and decided to go lie down in his bed. He looked around his room and saw all the things he owned: his toys, pillow and favourite blanket. He was so cozy that he soon fell asleep. When the prince woke up, he found himself in a new place. He couldn't believe his eyes! There was a castle in the sky and the prince was the king. He had become the prince that owned the sky!

Once upon a time, there was a cool little cat named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big tree in the park. Tim thought it would be fun to climb the tree. He jumped up and grabbed the branch. Up, up, up he went. When he got to the top, he saw a bird's nest. Tim wanted to take a peek. He looked into the nest and saw three baby birds. They were so cute! Tim was happy he got to peek at the baby birds. Then, he climbed back down the tree and went home. And from that day on, Tim loved playing by the big tree in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a lamp. The lamp was very pretty. Tim was happy because he liked the lamp a lot. He put the lamp in his room. When it was dark, the lamp made his room bright. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam saw the lamp and was envious. He wanted a lamp like Tim's. Sam asked Tim where he found the lamp. Tim told Sam that he found the lamp near the big tree. Sam went to the big tree to find a lamp. But he could not find one. He was sad. Then, he saw a wet kitten under the tree. Sam took the kitten home and used a towel to dry it. The kitten was happy and Sam was happy too. Even though Sam did not find a lamp, he found a new friend in the kitten.

Once upon a time there was a friend. She was very proud. She liked going on adventures and having fun. One day she went on a journey all by herself. She thought it would be an exciting experience, but it ended up being a bad one. Soon, she was lost and couldn't find her way home. She asked many people for help but none of them could help her. She was very scared and worried. Finally, she saw a old man. He said he could help her get back home. She followed him but, at the end of it all, she found out he was not a nice person. He tricked her and she was not able to get back home. She was sad and disappointed. Her friend was so proud of her, but her adventure had a bad ending. She never went on an adventure like that again.
