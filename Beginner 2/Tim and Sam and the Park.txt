Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Park

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play soldier in the park. They have toy guns and hats and jackets. They pretend to fight bad guys and save the world. One day, Tim says to Sam, "I have a new game. Let's invite some other kids to play with us. We can be a big army and have more fun." Sam likes the idea. He says, "OK, let's do it. Who should we invite?" Tim thinks for a moment. He says, "How about Lily and Ben? They are nice and they like to play too." Sam nods. He says, "Good idea. Let's go and ask them." They run to the playground, where Lily and Ben are swinging and sliding. They wave and shout, "Hey, Lily, hey, Ben, do you want to play soldier with us?" Lily and Ben stop and look at them. They see their toy guns and hats and jackets. They are curious and excited. They say, "Yes, we do. That sounds fun. What do we do?" Tim and Sam explain the rules. They say, "We are a team. We have to be alert and brave. We have to protect each other and the park. We have to watch out for bad guys and stop them. Are you ready?" Lily and Ben say, "Yes, we are. Let's go." They follow Tim and Sam to the park. They find a good spot to hide and plan. They make noises and signals. They have a lot of fun playing soldier. They are happy they invited each other.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, angry dog. The dog was barking loud. Tim was scared but he wanted to play. He had an idea to make the dog happy. Tim took out his bubble toy. He poured soap and water into the toy. He blew the bubbles into the air. The angry dog saw the bubbles and stopped barking. The dog started to jump and play with the bubbles. Tim was happy to see the dog having fun. Suddenly, the dog spoke, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim was surprised. He did not know dogs could talk. The dog smiled and said, "I am a magic dog. You made me happy, so I will give you a wish." Tim thought for a moment and wished for more bubbles. The magic dog waved his tail and more bubbles filled the air. Tim and the dog laughed and played with the bubbles all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a magic cup. The magic cup could talk. It would whisper secrets to Tim. Tim liked the cup and played with it every day. One day, the cup whispered to Tim, "I can make you dizzy if you want to have fun." Tim said, "Yes, please!" The cup made Tim very dizzy. He laughed and laughed as he spun around. But Tim was too dizzy, and he fell down. He hit his head on the floor. Tim cried and cried. The magic cup felt bad, but it could not help Tim. The magic cup whispered, "I'm sorry, Tim." Tim was sad and hurt, and he never played with the magic cup again.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. He heard a song from far away. Tim liked the song, so he peeked out of his nest to find where it was coming from. He saw a big tree with a lot of birds singing together. Tim wanted to join them and sing too. Tim flew to the big tree and said, "Hi, can I sing with you?" The other birds said, "Sure, come sing with us!" They all sang a happy song together. Tim was having a lot of fun, but then he saw a big, persistent cat sneaking up on them. Tim knew he had to do something to save his new friends. He thought fast and had an idea. Tim started singing a very loud song that scared the cat away. The other birds were surprised and said, "Wow, Tim! You saved us from the cat!" From that day on, Tim and the other birds became best friends. They sang songs together every day, and Tim was always there to protect them from the persistent cat.
