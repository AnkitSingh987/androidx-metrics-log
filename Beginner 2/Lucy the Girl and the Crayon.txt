Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Crayon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a book called a journal. Lucy wanted to improve her writing, so she wrote in her journal every day. She used a cheap pen that her mom gave her. Lucy loved her journal very much. One day, Lucy took her journal to the park. She sat under a big tree and wrote about the pretty flowers and the birds that sang. Lucy's friend, Tommy, saw her writing and wanted to help. Tommy had a cheap crayon, and he drew pictures in the journal. Lucy and Tommy worked together to improve their journal. They wrote and drew about things they saw and did. Their moms were very proud of them. The cheap pen and crayon were all they needed to make their journal special. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom was playing outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he slipped and fell. "Ouch!" Tom cried. He felt hurt and needed help. Tom's friend, a wise owl, heard him cry. Owl flew down and said, "You need medicine to feel better. But you must value it and use it right." Tom nodded and said, "Thank you, Owl." Owl gave Tom the medicine, and he felt better. But Tom was still clumsy. He learned to be more careful when climbing trees. In the end, Tom and Owl played together, happy and safe.

---

One day, a lion was very hungry. He saw a zebra near a lake and wanted to eat it. He ran fast and jumped on the zebra. But the zebra was not weak. He kicked the lion with his legs and ran away. The lion was hurt and angry. He saw a monkey on a tree and wanted to eat it. He climbed the tree and tried to catch the monkey. But the monkey was not weak. He threw a banana at the lion and swung to another tree. The lion fell down and was more hurt and angry. He saw a turtle on the grass and wanted to eat it. He walked slowly and bit the turtle. But the turtle was not weak. He hid in his shell and the lion could not open it. The lion was very hurt and very angry. He saw a mouse on a rock and wanted to eat it. He crawled to the mouse and opened his mouth. But the mouse was not afraid. He said to the lion, "Why are you so mean? You are big and strong, but you are not happy. You should be kind and share with others. Then you will have friends and be happy." The lion was surprised. He said, "But I am hungry and you are small and weak. I can eat you easily." The mouse said, "Maybe you can, but you should not. You should accept what you have and be grateful. Look, there is a staff of honey on that bush. You can eat that and be sweet. And I can help you with your wounds and be your friend." The lion thought for a moment. He said, "You are right. I have been greedy and rude. I am sorry. I will accept the staff of honey and be sweet. And I will be your friend and be kind." The mouse smiled and said, "That is good. Come, I will show you the staff of honey and help you with your wounds." The lion and the mouse walked together to the bush. They ate the staff of honey and licked each other's wounds. They became friends and were happy. The moral of the story is: You should accept what you have and be grateful. You should be kind and share with others. Then you will have friends and be happy.

One day, a boy named True went to play in the park. He saw his friend, Sam, near the big tree. True was happy to see Sam and wanted to play with him. "Hi, Sam!" True shouted. "Do you want to play with me?" Sam looked up and said, "Yes, True! Let's play hide and seek!" They both started to play. True hid behind a bush, and Sam tried to find him. As they were playing, a harsh wind blew. It was so strong that it blew a big branch from the tree. The branch fell down and almost hit Sam. True saw this and shouted, "Sam, watch out!" Sam moved away just in time. Sam was scared, but he was also thankful that True had warned him. "Thank you, True," Sam said. "You saved me!" They hugged and continued to play, but this time they played far away from the big tree.
