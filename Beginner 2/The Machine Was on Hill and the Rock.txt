Category: Beginner
Title: The Machine Was on Hill and the Rock

One day, a big machine was on a hill. The machine was incredible. It could rock back and forth. A boy named Tim saw the machine. He wanted to play with it. Tim went up the hill to the machine. He said, "Hello, machine! Can I play with you?" The machine said, "Yes, Tim! Let's rock together!" So, Tim and the machine rocked back and forth. They had fun. But then, a girl named Sue came. She said, "Stop! The hill is not safe!" Tim and the machine stopped rocking. Sue said, "We must go down the hill." So, Tim, Sue, and the machine went down the hill. They played together in a safe place.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy skeleton named Mr. Bones. Mr. Bones was very useful. He would help Lily clean her room and find her lost toys. They were best friends. One day, Lily could not find her favorite toy car. She looked all around but it was nowhere to be found. So she asked Mr. Bones, "Can you help me find my car?" Mr. Bones nodded his head and started to search with Lily. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Lily let out a sigh. She was sad because they could not find her car. Mr. Bones saw Lily's sad face and knew he had to do something. He looked around one more time and saw the car hiding behind a pillow. "Look, Lily! I found your car!" Mr. Bones said with a big smile. Lily's face lit up with joy and she hugged Mr. Bones. "Thank you, Mr. Bones! You are the best friend ever!" From that day on, Lily and Mr. Bones played together all the time, and Mr. Bones was always there to help his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a nice plane. It had big wings and a long tail. It loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the nice plane saw a little bird. "Hello, little bird," said the nice plane. "Do you want to fly with me?" "Sure!" said the little bird. "I love to fly!" So, the nice plane and the little bird flew high in the sky. They played together and had lots of fun. But then, they saw a big storm coming. "Oh no!" said the little bird. "We should go back home!" But the nice plane wanted to keep flying. It didn't listen to the little bird. The storm came and the wind blew very hard. Suddenly, there was a loud snap! The nice plane's wing broke. It couldn't fly anymore. The nice plane and the little bird fell from the sky. They were very sad because they didn't listen and go home before the storm.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. The toy car was dull. Tim did not like the dull toy car. He wanted to make the toy car shiny. Tim's mom said, "You can polish the toy car to make it shiny." Tim asked, "How do I polish the toy car?" His mom said, "You need a cloth and some water." Tim got a cloth and water. He started to polish the toy car. He rubbed the toy car with the cloth and water. The toy car was not shiny yet. Tim kept polishing the toy car. Finally, the toy car was shiny. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, mom, for helping me polish my toy car!" His mom said, "You're welcome, Tim. Now you can play with your shiny toy car." Tim played with his toy car all day long.

Sam and Mia were twins who loved to play with their toy yacht. They liked to pretend they were sailing on the sea, having adventures and finding treasure. One day, they took their yacht to the park, where there was a big pond. They put their yacht in the water and watched it float. "Look, Mia, our yacht is so fast and pretty!" Sam said. "Yes, Sam, it is! Let's make it go to that island over there!" Mia said, pointing to a patch of grass in the pond. They ran along the edge of the pond, following their yacht. They were having so much fun, they did not see their mom calling them. "Sam! Mia! It's time to go home!" Mom said. But Sam and Mia did not hear her. They were too busy playing with their yacht. Mom waited for a while, then she got worried. She walked around the pond, looking for her twins. She saw them on the other side, near the island. "Sam! Mia! Come here, now!" Mom said, in a harsh voice. Sam and Mia heard her this time. They looked up and saw her. They felt scared. "Sorry, Mom, we were playing with our yacht," Sam said. "We forgot to listen to you," Mia said. Mom was angry, but also relieved. She hugged them and said, "I'm glad you're safe, but you have to listen to me when I call you. You can't forget that. And you have to be careful with your yacht. It could get lost or broken in the pond." Sam and Mia nodded. They said they were sorry again. They took their yacht out of the water and carried it back to Mom. They promised to be more careful and to listen to Mom next time. Mom smiled and said she loved them. They went home together, happy to have their yacht and their mom.
