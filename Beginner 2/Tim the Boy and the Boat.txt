Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy boat in the bathtub. One day, Tim wanted to pull the bathtub to the living room. He thought it would be fun to play with his boat while watching TV. "Mom, can I pull the bathtub to the living room?" Tim asked. His mom said, "No, Tim. That's not a good idea. The water will make a big mess." But Tim did not listen. When his mom was not looking, he started to pull the bathtub. As Tim pulled the bathtub, it made a loud noise. His mom heard it and came running. She saw the mess and was very mad. "Tim, I told you not to pull the bathtub! Now look at this mess!" she said. Tim felt ashamed for not listening to his mom. Tim's mom made him clean up the mess, but as he was cleaning, he slipped on the water and hurt his leg. He cried and his mom came to help him. They both wished he had listened in the first place. The day ended badly for Tim, and he learned to always listen to his mom.

Ben is a painter. He likes to make pictures with colors. He has a big box of paints and brushes. He wants to make a surprise for his mom. He goes to his room and prepares his paints. He puts a big paper on the wall. He thinks of what to paint. He remembers his mom likes flowers. He starts to paint a big flower with red and yellow. He hears his mom coming. He does not want her to see his picture yet. He looks for a place to hide. He sees his bed. He runs to his bed and crawls under it. He is hidden. His mom opens the door. She sees the paper on the wall. She sees the flower. She smiles. She says, "Wow, what a beautiful flower! Who made this?" Ben hears his mom. He is happy. He wants to show her his picture. He comes out from under the bed. He says, "I made it, mom! It is for you!" His mom hugs him. She says, "Thank you, Ben! You are a great painter! I love your picture!"

One day, a boy named Tom was playing with his toy drum. He loved to make noise and find a rhythm with it. But today, he felt restless and could not find a good rhythm. He walked around his house, trying to find a rhythm that would make him happy. Tom saw his cat, Max, and asked, "Max, do you have a rhythm for me?" Max looked at Tom and said, "Meow." Tom tried to copy the rhythm of Max's meow, but it did not seem right for his drum. As Tom kept looking, he found a big box in the corner. He had never seen it before. It had a picture of a big drum on it. He opened the box and found a new, bigger drum inside. Tom was so excited! He picked up the drum and started to play. Suddenly, the drum started to play a rhythm by itself! Tom was surprised and happy. He played along with the magic drum, and they made the best rhythm together. Tom's restlessness was gone, and he played with his new drum all day long, making everyone in the house dance to the beat.

One day, a little cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big tree. The tree had many green leaves. The cat wanted to climb the tree. The cat was very alert. It looked up and down the tree. It blinked its eyes and started to climb. The cat climbed up and up. It was so much fun! At the top of the tree, the cat saw a bird. The bird said, "Hello, cat!" The cat blinked and said, "Hello, bird!" They became friends and played in the tree all day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively cat named Tom. Tom loved to run, jump, and play all day. At night, Tom would dream. In his dreams, he would see a symbol. The symbol was a big red ball. One day, Tom saw the big red ball from his dream in the yard. He was so happy! He ran to the ball and started to play with it. Tom played and played until he was very tired. But then, something unexpected happened. The big red ball started to move on its own! Tom was scared, but he watched the ball. It rolled and rolled until it stopped by a little girl. The girl was the one moving the ball! She wanted to play with Tom. So, Tom and the girl played together, and they became best friends.
