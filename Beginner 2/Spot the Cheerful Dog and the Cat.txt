Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Cheerful Dog and the Cat

One day, a cheerful dog named Spot wanted to join his friends in watching TV. He went to his friend Cat's house. Cat was happy to see Spot. They sat on the floor and turned on the TV. "Look, Spot! It's our favorite show!" said Cat. They watched and laughed together. They were having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The TV made a loud noise and turned off. Spot and Cat were sad. They wanted to watch more TV. They tried to turn it back on, but it would not work. They were not sure what to do. Just then, their friend Bird flew in through the window. "What's wrong?" asked Bird. Spot and Cat told Bird about the TV. Bird had an idea. "Let's play outside instead!" said Bird. Spot, Cat, and Bird went outside and played together. They had even more fun than watching TV. They were happy they could still have fun together, even without the TV.

Tom and Anna like to play in the valley. The valley has many flowers and trees and birds. They run and jump and laugh and sing. They are happy. One day, they see a big dog in the valley. The dog is black and has sharp teeth. The dog is bad. It barks and growls and chases Tom and Anna. They are scared. "Help, help!" Tom and Anna shout. "The bad dog is after us!" They run to a big rock and hide behind it. The dog cannot find them. It sniffs and whines and walks away. Tom and Anna peek from behind the rock. They see the dog is gone. They are safe. "Let's go home," Tom says. "The valley is not fun today." "OK," Anna says. "Maybe we can play in the valley another day, when the bad dog is not there." They hold hands and walk home. They are still friends.

One day, a long octopus named Ollie lived in the sea. He had many friends, like fish and crabs. Ollie loved to play games with his friends. "Let's play 'guess the number'," said Ollie to his friends. "I will think of a number, and you will guess it." The fish and crabs were excited to play the game. "I guess two," said a little fish. "No, I guess four," said a crab. Ollie smiled and said, "No, the number is three!" They all laughed and played more games. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of earthquakes. One day, Tim's mom said, "Don't be scared. I will support you if an earthquake comes." One day, Tim was playing with his toys when the floor started to shake. It was an earthquake! Tim was very scared. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I am scared!" His mom hugged him tight and said, "Don't worry, I am here to support you." The earthquake stopped, and Tim felt better. He knew that his mom would always be there to help him when he was scared.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Allie. Allie was three years old and loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, Allie's dad said to her, "Let's go get the swing!" The swing looked very impressive. Allie ran to it and said, "Daddy, can we swing on it?" Daddy laughed and said, "Of course, my little one! I just need to release it from the tree first." Allie watched as Daddy released the swing from the tree. Once it was free, Allie hopped on and said, "This will be so much fun!" Daddy pushed Allie on the swing, higher and higher until she reached the sky! Allie felt so happy and free as the wind flew through her hair. She felt so impressed by her swing and couldn't believe how lucky she was to have it. When Allie was done swinging, she jumped off and hugged her daddy. She said, "Thank you Daddy, I had so much fun!" Daddy smiled and said, "You're welcome sweetheart, I'm glad you like it!" Allie and Daddy continued to play the rest of the day. Allie spent the rest of her days playing on her impressive swing.
