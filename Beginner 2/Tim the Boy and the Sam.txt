Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw a tiny pebble on the ground. It was round and smooth. Tim picked it up and put it in his pocket. He wanted to show it to his best friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam came out and said, "Hi, Tim! What do you have in your pocket?" Tim showed Sam the pebble and said, "Look at this! I found it in the park." Sam liked the pebble too. They played with it all day. When it was time for Tim to go home, he felt sad. He did not want to leave the pebble at Sam's house. Tim said, "I will miss the pebble. Can I take it home?" Sam felt miserable too. He did not want Tim to be sad. So, Sam said, "Yes, you can take it home. We can play with it again tomorrow." Tim smiled and thanked Sam. They hugged and said goodbye. Tim went home, happy to have the pebble and a good friend like Sam.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his ball. He threw the ball too far and it rolled away. Tim was sad and hoped to find his ball. He looked around but could not see it. Tim saw his friend, Tom, and asked, "Have you seen my ball?" Tom shook his head. "No, but I will help you look for it." They looked near the trees and by the flowers. They even looked on Tim's shoulder, but they could not find the ball. Then, they saw a big dog with the ball in its mouth. The dog was careless and did not know the ball was Tim's. Tim and Tom tried to get the ball back. They said, "Bad dog! Give the ball back!" The dog listened and gave the ball back to Tim. Tim and Tom were happy and played with the ball together.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her mom. It was cold outside, so Sue wore her big, warm coat. They walked to a park where there were many trees and a big slide. At the park, Sue saw a bike with a broken handle. She didn't know what it was, so she asked her mom. Her mom started to explain. "This is a bike handle, Sue. It helps you hold onto the bike and steer it when you ride." Sue listened and looked at the bike handle. She touched it and felt how cold it was. Then, Sue and her mom went to play on the slide and swings. They had a lot of fun at the park, even though it was cold outside.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big statue. The statue was of a man on a horse. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is a statue, Tim. It is here to help us remember someone important." Tim wanted to organize a party for the statue. He thought the statue looked lonely. His mom said, "That is a nice idea, but statues don't need parties." Tim was a bit ignorant, but he still wanted to help. So, he picked some flowers and gave them to the statue. Tim felt happy that he could do something nice for the statue.

Molly was feeling very nervous today. She had to go to grandma's house and she'd never been before. Her mom said she would love it, but Molly wasn't too sure. Mom said, "Let's go, Molly!" Molly paused, but then reluctantly agreed and got in the car. When they arrived, Molly saw a big house with lots of flowers in the garden. Her mom said, "This is Grandma's house! Let's go inside." Molly paused for a few seconds. She was feeling very nervous about meeting Grandma for the first time. Mom said, "It's okay, Molly. Grandma is so excited to meet you!" Molly took a deep breath, then she walked up to the front door. She opened it, and there was Grandma! She was smiling and said, "Hello Molly! I'm so happy to meet you!" Molly felt a little less nervous. She smiled and said, "Hello, Grandma!"
