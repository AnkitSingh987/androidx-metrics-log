Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fox

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a generous boy who liked to help others. One day, Tim saw a big cross near his house. He thought it was pretty and wanted to show it to his friends. While Tim was playing with his friends, a sneaky fox came to the cross. The fox wanted to steal the cross from Tim's yard. The fox took the cross and ran away very fast. Tim and his friends saw the fox steal the cross. They were sad and tried to get it back, but the fox was too fast for them. The cross was gone forever, and Tim and his friends learned to be more careful with their things.

Lila liked to sail her boat in the pond. She made the boat from a big leaf and a stick. She thought her boat was very fashion. She put a flower on it to make it pretty. One day, she saw a boy with a big boat. His boat was red and had a sail. He was not shy. He said, "Look at my boat! It is better than yours. Your boat is small and ugly. You are silly to sail a leaf." Lila felt sad. She said, "No, your boat is not better. My boat is fashion and pretty. You are mean to say that." She wanted to sail away from the boy. But the boy was not nice. He said, "I will show you who is better." He took his boat and sailed it fast. He hit Lila's boat with his boat. Lila's boat broke. The leaf and the stick and the flower fell in the water. Lila cried. She said, "You are bad! You broke my boat! Why did you do that?" She tried to get her boat back. But the water was deep and cold. She fell in the water. She could not swim. She screamed for help. But the boy did not help. He laughed. He said, "That is what you get for sailing a leaf. You are silly and wet. I am better and dry." He sailed away with his boat. Lila was scared. She could not breathe. She sank in the water. She did not see her boat again. She did not see the boy again. She did not see anything again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a doll that she loved very much. One day, Sally was ready to play with her doll. She was so excited! Sally played with her doll all day. But then, she saw her doll was dirty. Sally felt sad. She wanted to make her doll clean again. Sally thought, "Maybe I can reverse this and make my doll like new!" Sally took her doll to her mom. Her mom helped her wash the doll. Soon, the doll was clean again. Sally was so happy! She hugged her doll and said, "Now we can play again!" And they did. Sally learned that with a little help, she could reverse things and make them better.

John was a 3 year old boy who loved to bathe. Whenever his mom said it was time to bathe, John ran to the bathroom right away. John also loved spending time on the porch. He would sit and watch the birds and clouds pass by. One day, he noticed something very useful on the porch. It was a bucket. John grabbed the bucket and ran inside with it. He filled it with water and waited patiently. His mom smiled when he saw what he was doing and filled the bucket to the top. John happily played in the bucket. He splashed and giggled as the bubble's flew in the air. After he was done playing, he was ready for his bath. His mom worked quickly to wash him off in the tub. Afterward, John was as clean as can be. He thanked his mom for his lovely bath, then ran outside and placed his bucket back on the porch. John knew that the bucket would be very useful for his next bath.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Tom. He lived near a calm lake. Every day, he went to the lake to catch fish. One day, Tom saw a big fish in the water. He tried to catch it, but the fish was too strong. Tom started to struggle with the fish. He pulled and pulled on his fishing rod. "Help me!" Tom called to his friend, Sam. Sam came running to help Tom. Together, they pulled on the fishing rod. The big fish came out of the water. Tom and Sam were very happy. They had a big fish to eat for dinner.
