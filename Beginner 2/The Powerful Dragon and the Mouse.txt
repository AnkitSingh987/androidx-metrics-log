Category: Beginner
Title: The Powerful Dragon and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dragon. Every day, he would write a long list of all the things he wanted. One day, he had an idea. He thought he should write a special soup recipe. But, he was stuck and couldn't think of what to do next. He asked his best friend, a tiny mouse, for help. The mouse thought and thought. Then, she said in a tiny voice, "Why don't you add some vegetables and a little bit of salt?" The dragon was so excited. He thanked her and ran off to write his soup recipe. In the end, he wrote the most powerful soup recipe ever! Everyone loved it!

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to wear a big hat. One day, Tom told his friend, a dog named Sam, to go to the bathroom and clean it. Sam did not like to clean, but he did not want Tom to be mad. The next day, Tom went to wear his big hat. But he could not find it! Tom looked everywhere for his hat. He asked Sam if he knew where it was. Sam smiled and said, "I know where your hat is, but you have to help me clean the bathroom." Tom did not like to clean either, but he wanted his hat back. So Tom and Sam went to the bathroom and cleaned it together. When they were done, Sam showed Tom where his hat was. Tom was happy to have his hat back, and he learned that it is not nice to be bossy. From that day on, Tom and Sam shared the work and played together as good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read her big book called a novel. One day, Sue was very troubled. Her novel was lost! She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Sue went to her friend Tom and said, "Tom, I can't find my novel. Can you help me?" Tom was a kind boy and wanted to help Sue. They looked everywhere for the novel, but they still could not find it. Sue was very sad. Then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Sue, let's ask the dog to help us." They went to the dog, and the dog sniffed around. The dog found the novel under a big tree. Sue was so happy! Tom and the dog rescued her novel. Sue thanked them and they all sat down to read the novel together.

One day, a little bug went for a walk. The bug was happy to see the big world. But then, the bug saw a big cat. The cat wanted to play with the bug. The cat tried to squeeze the bug with its paw. The bug was scared and said, "Please don't squeeze me!" The cat felt sorry for the bug. The cat said, "I am sorry, little bug. I will not squeeze you." The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, big cat." The cat and the bug became friends. They played together in the sun. After playing, the cat and the bug were tired. They sat under a tree and talked. The cat said, "I am glad we are friends." The bug said, "Me too." They smiled at each other and knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a nice little stable, there lived a horse named Hope. Hope was a happy horse who liked to play with her friends. She had a big smile and a soft, shiny coat. One day, a little boy named Tim came to visit the stable. He saw Hope and said, "Hi, Hope! You look nice. Do you want to play with me?" Hope nodded her head and let out a happy neigh. Together, Tim and Hope played all day long. They ran around the stable and laughed a lot. When the sun started to set, Tim hugged Hope and said, "Thank you for playing with me, Hope. I had so much fun. I hope to see you again soon." Hope neighed happily, and they both knew they would be the best of friends forever.
