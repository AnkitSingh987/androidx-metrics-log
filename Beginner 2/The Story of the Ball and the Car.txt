Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a small, red ball that lived in a big case. The case was on top of a high shelf, so the ball could see the whole room. The ball had a friend, a blue toy car. They liked to play together. One day, the toy car said, "I want to see the whole room too! Can you help me get up there?" The ball was not sure, but it wanted to help its friend. So, the ball said, "I will trust you. Let's be careful and not be careless." The ball and the toy car tried to climb up the shelf. They were almost at the top when the ball slipped. The toy car tried to hold on, but it was too late. The ball fell down and hit the floor with a loud noise. The toy car was scared and cried, "Oh no! What have we done?" The ball was broken and could not be fixed. The toy car was very sad and wished they had not tried to climb the shelf. They should have listened to the ball and been more careful. The toy car now knew that being careless can lead to bad things happening.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play all day. One day, Kitty found a big box. The box had a button that said "print". Kitty was curious, so she pushed the button. When Kitty pushed the button, the box made a loud noise. Then, out of the box came a pretty picture. The picture was of a big, dark room. Kitty wanted to see the dark room, so she jumped into the picture. Inside the dark room, Kitty could not see anything. But she felt safe because she was still in her picture. She played in the dark room and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Kitty jumped back out of the picture and into her own room. She was very happy with her new adventure.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She liked to play on the swings and slide and see the birds and flowers. She was very happy and had a lot of fun. But then, she saw a stranger. He was a big man with a beard and a hat. He came near her and said, "Hello, little girl. Do you want some candy?" He smiled, but Lily did not like his smile. It was not a nice smile. Lily felt scared. She did not know the stranger. She did not want his candy. She wanted her mom. She looked around and saw her mom on a bench. She ran to her and said, "Mom, mom, there is a stranger. He wants to give me candy. He scares me." Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I am here. You did the right thing. You should never talk to strangers or take anything from them. They might be bad. You are a smart and brave girl." Lily felt safe and loved. She said, "Thank you, mom. I love you. Can we go home now?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can. Let's go home and have some cookies and milk. You are my little star." They held hands and walked away from the park. Lily was happy again. She learned a lesson that day. She learned to be careful of strangers.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to move and play. One day, Tim found a thick book in his room. The book was about math. Tim opened the book and saw many numbers. He wanted to learn math. So, Tim asked his mom to help him. They sat down and started to learn. Tim moved his fingers to count the numbers. He learned how to add and take away. Tim was very happy. He now knew some math. And he loved his thick math book.

One day, Tom the dog had a task. He had to cover his bone so it would be hidden. Tom dug a hole in the yard with his paws. He put his bone in the hole and used his nose to push the dirt on top. The bone was now hidden. Tom's friend, Sam the cat, saw Tom hiding the bone. Sam said, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom replied, "I am hiding my bone. It is a task I must do." Sam wanted to help, so he used his paws to pat the dirt down. The bone was now very hidden. Tom and Sam were happy that they finished the task. They played together in the yard all day. They knew the bone was safe and hidden. At the end of the day, Tom thanked Sam for helping him. They were very good friends.
