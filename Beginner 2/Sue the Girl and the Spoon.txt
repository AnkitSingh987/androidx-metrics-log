Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to have tea with her friends. One day, Sue invited her friends to her house for a tea party. She was very excited and couldn't wait for them to come. Sue set up the table with cups, plates, and spoons. She also made some weak tea for her friends. She put the tea in a big pot. Sue felt something might go wrong, but she didn't know what it could be. When her friends came, they all sat down at the table. They talked and laughed as they drank their tea. Suddenly, the big pot of tea started to shake! Sue began to panic. She didn't know what to do. Then, she remembered she had a magic spoon. She used the spoon to stop the shaking pot. The tea party was saved, and everyone had a great time. Sue was so happy that she was prepared for anything.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark was three years old and very eager to explore the world. One day, his mom asked him if he wanted to help her carry the olives from the farmer's market. Mark was so excited! He quickly grabbed the bag of olives and said, "Okay mom! I can do it!" His mom smiled and said, "Alright, but you have to be careful not to spill any." Mark carefully carried the olives home. Once he was back, his mom said, "Good job, Mark! You were so eager to help with the olives!" Mark smiled and said, "Thanks mom!" And they both had a happy ending to their day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was mighty and strong. He loved to pick up things and show how strong he was. One day, Tim wanted to wear his favorite pants. He thought they made him look very mighty. Tim went to his room to find his pants. He looked in his drawer, under his bed, and even in his toy box. He could not find his pants anywhere. Tim felt sad because he could not wear his favorite pants. Then, Tim's mom came into his room. She saw him looking for his pants. She said, "Tim, I washed your pants. They are hanging outside to dry." Tim went outside and saw his pants hanging on a line. He picked them up and put them on. Now, Tim felt mighty again. He went to play with his friends, showing off his mighty pants.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big toy box with many toys. One day, it was very gloomy outside, and Tim was sad. He wanted to play outside, but he could not because of the rain. Tim decided to play with his toys. As he opened his toy box, he heard a whisper. It was a small toy man. The toy man said, "I can make the rain stop." Tim was very happy and asked the toy man to help. The toy man told Tim to close his eyes and count to ten. Tim did as the toy man said. When he opened his eyes, the rain had stopped. Tim was so happy that he ran outside to play. But, something unexpected happened. The toy man became very big and cross. He did not want Tim to play outside. He wanted Tim to play with him inside the house forever. The toy man grabbed Tim and took him back inside the house. Tim was very sad and scared. He wished he had never listened to the toy man's whisper. Now, Tim could never play outside again.

Lily and Ben are playing in the living room. They have many toys and books to play with. Lily likes to rock her baby doll in her arms. She sings a song to make her doll sleep. Ben likes to make cars and planes with his folder. He cuts and folds the folder to make different shapes. He makes noises to make his cars and planes go fast. "Look at my car, Lily!" Ben says. "It is very fast and cool. It can go zoom, zoom, zoom!" Lily does not look at Ben's car. She is busy rocking her doll. "Shh, Ben!" Lily says. "You are too loud. My doll is sleeping. You have to be quiet." Ben does not listen to Lily. He keeps making noises with his car. He makes it go near Lily and her doll. He wants Lily to play with him. "Come on, Lily!" Ben says. "Let's race our cars. My car is faster than yours. I bet I can win." Lily gets angry at Ben. She does not want to race her cars. She wants to rock her doll. She thinks Ben is rude and mean. She pushes Ben's car away with her hand. "No, Ben!" Lily says. "Go away. You are bothering me and my doll. I don't want to play with you." Ben gets angry at Lily. He thinks Lily is boring and selfish. He wants to play with her and have fun. He does not like that she pushes his car away. He grabs Lily's doll from her arms. "Fine, Lily!" Ben says. "If you don't want to play with me, I will play with your doll. I will make her fly in my plane. She will like it more than rocking with you." Lily screams and cries. She wants her doll back. She does not want Ben to play with her doll. She thinks Ben will hurt her doll. She runs after Ben and tries to get her doll back. "Give me my doll, Ben!" Lily says. "She is mine. You are bad. You are not my friend." Lily and Ben fight over the doll. They pull and tug and shout. They do not share or listen. They make a big mess in the living room. They make their mom very sad and mad.
