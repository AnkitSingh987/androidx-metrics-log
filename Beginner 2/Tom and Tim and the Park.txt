Title: Tom and Tim and the Park

One Sunday, Tom and Tim were playing in the park. They loved to play on the swings and slide. They saw a big ice cream truck. The ice cream truck had many tasty treats. "I want ice cream!" said Tom. "Me too!" said Tim. They both rush to the ice cream truck. But there was a problem. Tom wanted the last tasty chocolate ice cream and Tim wanted it too. "No, I saw it first!" said Tom. "But I want it!" said Tim. They were both sad and didn't know what to do. Then, the ice cream man had an idea. He gave them a big, tasty ice cream to share. Tom and Tim were happy. They ate the ice cream together and went back to play in the park. The Sunday was fun and full of tasty treats.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a shy little mouse named Mimi. Mimi lived in a tiny chamber under the floor. She had a lot of friends, but she was too shy to play with them. One day, Mimi was walking in the garden when she met a big cat named Tom. Tom said, "Hi, Mimi! Do you want to play with me?" Mimi was scared, but she really wanted to play. So she took a big breath and said, "Yes, I hope we can be friends." Mimi and Tom played all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Mimi wasn't shy anymore. She was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Mimi and Tom played together every day and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a calm cat named Tom sat under a big tree. He saw a little bird with a needle in its beak. The bird was trying to make a nest. Tom asked, "What are you doing, bird?" The bird said, "I am making a nest with this needle. I need it to sew leaves together." As they talked, a fast car went by. It went zoom! The car hit a big bump and the needle flew out of the bird's beak. The needle went up, up, up into the sky. Tom and the bird looked up and saw the needle land on the moon. They were sad. They did not know how to get the needle back. Just then, a big friendly bear came by. He had a long ladder. The bear said, "I can help you get the needle back." So, the bear put the ladder up to the moon. Tom and the bird climbed the ladder, got the needle, and came back down. They were very happy and thanked the bear. The bird could now finish its nest, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a new paint set and wanted to make a pretty picture. Amy loved to write and draw with her paint set. She thought it was fun to mix colors and make new ones. One day, Amy's friend, Ben, came to play. "What are you doing?" Ben asked. "I'm making a picture with my new paint," Amy said. "Can I try?" Ben asked. "Of course!" Amy said, and they started to write and paint together. They painted a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. They wrote their names under the picture. They had so much fun making the picture together. When they were done, they showed their moms. Both moms loved the picture and hung it up on the wall. Amy and Ben were very proud of their new painting.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the garage to play. The garage was big and had many things inside. They saw a bright red chair. Tom said, "Let's play a game. We can take turns to seat on the chair." Sue went first and sat on the chair. She felt very happy. Then it was Tom's turn. He was excited to seat on the chair too. But when Tom sat on the chair, something unexpected happened. The bright red chair started to move! It went up and down, and round and round. Tom and Sue were very surprised. They did not know that the chair was a magic chair. They laughed and played on the magic chair all day long.
