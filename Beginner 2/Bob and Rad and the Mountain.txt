Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and Rad and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there were two birds called Bob and Rad. They lived near a mountain of ash. One day, Bob said to Rad, “Do you want to play hide and seek?” Rad smiled and said “yes!” Rad hid behind the mountain of ash. Bob counted to ten and started looking for Rad. He looked around the mountain and he called gently, “Rad… Rad… where are you?” Rad popped out from behind the mountain of ash and put his wings out wide. He said, “Here I am!” Bob was so happy, he flew straight to Rad and gave him a big kiss on the nose. Rad blushed and smiled. Bob felt sorry for not finding Rad earlier so he said, “I'm sorry. I should have found you sooner.” Rad said, “That's ok, let's do it again!” The two birds played many games together, and each time Bob made sure he found Rad really quickly. They were very happy birds and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. He was very good at kicking the ball. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue did not have a ball to play with. She looked sad. Tim wanted to make Sue happy. So, he said, "Sue, do you want to play with my football?" Sue was happy when she heard Tim. She said, "Yes, thank you for lending me your football!" They played together and had lots of fun. They kicked the ball and laughed. Tim and Sue became good friends. They played with the football every day at the park.

Lily and Mom went to the store. Lily wanted a new toy. She saw a big doll with a pink dress and long hair. She liked the doll very much. "Can I have the doll, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom looked at the price tag. It was too much money. "I'm sorry, Lily, but we can't buy the doll today. Maybe another time," Mom said. Lily felt sad. She wanted the doll so bad. She started to cry. Mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I know you like the doll, but we have to save our money for other things. How about we look for something else?" Lily wiped her tears and nodded. She followed Mom to another aisle. There she saw a small teddy bear with a blue bow. It was soft and cute. Lily smiled and picked it up. "Can I have the teddy bear, Mom?" Lily asked. Mom looked at the price tag. It was not too much money. "Yes, you can have the teddy bear, Lily. It's a nice toy," Mom said. Lily hugged the teddy bear and said, "Thank you, Mom. I love the teddy bear. It's my new friend." Mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I love you." They went to the cashier and paid for the teddy bear. Then they walked out of the store. Lily held the teddy bear close to her chest. She was happy. She stepped with Mom to the car. She couldn't wait to play with her new toy at home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of makeup. She loved to play with it and make herself look pretty. One day, her friend, Tim, came over to play. Lily said, "Tim, let's play with my makeup!" Tim was a little shy but said, "Okay, let's try it." They both sat down and started to play with the makeup. Lily put some red on her lips and blue on her eyes. Tim put some green on his cheeks and yellow on his nose. They both looked very funny! After they were done, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw them and laughed. "You two look very cute," she said. Lily and Tim smiled and laughed too. They had so much fun playing with the makeup. From that day on, they loved to play with makeup and make each other look funny.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with plastic toys. They have cars, dolls, blocks and animals. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Lily and Ben go to the park. They see a new girl on the swings. She has a plastic bag with her. Lily and Ben are curious. They go to say hello. "Hi, I'm Lily and this is Ben. What's your name?" Lily asks. "I'm Anna. I just moved here. I don't have any friends yet." Anna says. Lily and Ben smile. They want to be Anna's friends. "Do you want to play with us? We have plastic toys. They are reliable. They don't break easily." Ben says. Anna nods. She opens her plastic bag. She has a plastic train. She likes trains. "Wow, that's a nice train. Can I see it?" Lily says. "Sure, you can. And you can play with it too. I like to share." Anna says. Lily and Ben are happy. They welcome Anna to their group. They take turns playing with the train and their other toys. They have a lot of fun. Anna is happy too. She has made new friends. She likes Lily and Ben. They are reliable. They are kind and generous. They play until it is time to go home. They hug and say goodbye. "See you tomorrow, Anna. We'll play again." Lily and Ben say. "See you tomorrow, Lily and Ben. Thank you for playing with me. You are the best." Anna says. They wave and smile. They are friends.
