Category: Beginner
Title: The King and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there lived a kind king. The king had a big, tall hat that he loved to wear all the time. One day, the king went for a walk with his people. They walked and talked, and everyone was happy. As they walked, the wind started to blow. The king's big, tall hat began to wiggle. The king felt his hat wiggle and tried to hold it down. But the wind blew harder and harder. Suddenly, the hat fell off the king's head and landed on the ground. The king felt embarrassed. He picked up his hat and put it back on his head. The people saw the king's red face and smiled. They knew that sometimes things fall, and it's okay. The king learned that it's okay to feel embarrassed, and everyone still loved him. They all walked back to the castle, happy and laughing together.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to sing and dance. One day, she found a note on the ground. The note said, "Please stop singing and dancing." Sue was sad. Sue's friend, Tim, saw her sad face. He wanted to help her. Tim said, "Let's suggest a new way to have fun. We can still be jolly and not make anyone upset." Sue thought that was a good idea. So, Sue and Tim tried many things. They played with toys, they drew pictures, and they read books. In the end, they found that they could still have fun without singing and dancing. The town was jolly again, and Sue and Tim were happy.

Tom loves to swim. He goes to the pool with his mom and dad every week. He likes to splash and kick and blow bubbles in the water. He has a lot of fun. One day, he sees a big poster on the wall of the pool. It has a picture of a man with a medal and a smile. The poster says he is a famous swimmer. He is coming to the pool to meet the kids and teach them some tips. Tom is very excited. He wants to meet the famous swimmer. The next week, Tom goes to the pool with his mom and dad. He sees the famous swimmer in the water. He is very fast and strong. He waves to the kids and says hello. He asks them to join him in the water. Tom is a little shy, but he follows his mom and dad. They go to the water and meet the famous swimmer. The famous swimmer is very nice. He talks to Tom and the other kids. He shows them how to swim better. He teaches them how to breathe, how to move their arms and legs, and how to turn. He gives them high fives and hugs. He tells them they are doing great. Tom is very happy. He learns a lot from the famous swimmer. Tom thanks the famous swimmer for his help. He asks him for his autograph. The famous swimmer signs his name on Tom's swim cap. He tells Tom he is proud of him. He says he hopes to see him again. He says goodbye and leaves. Tom waves to him and smiles. He feels very special. He has a new friend. He met a famous swimmer.

Once upon a time, there was a tall lamp. It lived in a small room with a little boy named Tim. Tim loved the tall lamp very much. Every night, Tim would say, "Lamp, please give me light!" And the tall lamp would shine bright, so Tim could read his books. One day, while Tim was at school, the tall lamp felt sad. It wanted to do more than just give light. It wanted to play with Tim and be his friend. So, the tall lamp thought and thought. Then, it had an idea! The tall lamp would try to move and play with Tim when he got home. When Tim came home, he saw the tall lamp moving! He was so surprised. "Lamp, you can move!" Tim said. The tall lamp was happy. It could play with Tim now. They played together all day and had lots of fun. But when it was time for bed, Tim said, "Lamp, please give me light." The tall lamp tried to give light, but it couldn't. It had used all its energy to play with Tim. Tim was sad, and the tall lamp felt bad. The next day, the tall lamp decided to mind its job. It would give light to Tim when he needed it. But when Tim was done reading, the tall lamp could still move and play with him. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
