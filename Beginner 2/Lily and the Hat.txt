Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Hat

Lily was playing with her doll in the garden. She liked to make her doll have tea and cake. She put a bright hat on her doll's head. The hat was red and yellow and had flowers on it. But then Lily felt something on her arm. It was a bug. The bug bit Lily and made her arm itch. Lily did not like the bug. She wanted it to go away. She scratched her arm, but it did not help. The itch was still there. Lily ran to her mom, who was sitting on a chair. She showed her mom her arm. She asked her mom to make the itch go away. Her mom looked at her arm and saw the bug bite. She said, "Oh, poor Lily. You have a bug bite. Don't worry, I can help you." Her mom took Lily inside the house. She put some cream on Lily's arm. The cream was cold and soft. It made the itch feel better. Lily smiled and hugged her mom. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are the best." Her mom kissed her and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I love you. Now, do you want to go back to your doll and your tea party?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please. But can I take the cream with me? Just in case the bug comes back." Her mom laughed and said, "Of course you can. But be careful, the bug might like the cream too." Lily giggled and said, "No, the bug can't have the cream. The cream is for me and my doll. The bug can have the bright hat. Maybe it will make him happy and stop biting." Her mom said, "That's a good idea, Lily. Maybe the bug will like the bright hat. But don't forget to share with your doll. She might want the hat too." Lily said, "OK, mom. I will share with my doll. She is my friend. And maybe the bug can be my friend too. If he stops biting." Her mom said, "That's very nice of you, Lily. You are a kind and sweet girl. Now, go and have fun with your doll and your bug." Lily took the cream and the bright hat and ran back to the garden. She put the hat on the bug and said, "Hello, bug. I'm sorry I scratched you. Do you like the hat? It's very pretty. Do you want some tea and cake?" The bug did not answer. He just crawled on the hat and looked at Lily. Lily thought he was smiling. She smiled back and said, "I think you are happy, bug. I'm happy too. Let's be friends."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy cat that she loved very much. The cat had a button eye that was loose, and Mia was scared it would fall off. Mia's mom saw her sad face and said, "Don't worry, I will fix it." She took a needle and some thread. She sewed the button back on the cat's face. Mia blinked her eyes and saw her cat was all better. Mia was so happy and hugged her cat tight. She said, "You are not a stupid cat. You are the best cat ever!" And from that day on, Mia and her cat were always together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray crab. The crab lived near the beach. He had many friends like fish, birds, and turtles. They all liked to play together on the sand. One day, the crab and his friends wanted to have a party. They decided to discuss what they needed for the party. The crab said, "We need food, music, and games." The fish, birds, and turtles agreed. They all worked together to get ready for the party. The gray crab brought food, the fish played music, the birds sang songs, and the turtles played games. The party was fun, and everyone had a great time. The little gray crab was very happy to have such good friends.

Once upon a time there was a big corn field. In the corn field there was a man. He was a farmer. The farmer went to the corn field every day. One day, the farmer heard a loud call. He looked around but he didn't see anyone. The farmer looked around the corn field and he saw a small baby animal. The baby animal was covered in rough fur. It was a little raccoon. He had gotten stuck in the corn. The farmer started to call the baby raccoon. He said, "Come here, little raccoon!" The little raccoon heard him and he slowly made his way towards the farmer. The farmer helped the baby raccoon out of the corn. He was happy to see the little raccoon in good health. He thanked the farmer for his help and went on his way. The little raccoon was very grateful and he never forgot the farmer's kind words. He waved goodbye to the farmer and quickly ran off into the sunset.
