Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy bin that was very dirty. Tim's mom told him, "You need to clean your bin, Tim." Tim did not like cleaning, but he trusted his mom, so he started to clean the bin. As Tim was cleaning the bin, he found a small, old toy car. He had not seen the car in a long, long time. Tim was so happy to find it! He showed his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found my car!" His mom smiled and said, "See, cleaning can be fun!" Just then, the toy car started to move on its own! Tim and his mom were very surprised. They watched as the car drove around the room. The car was magic! Tim learned that when you trust and listen to others, good things can happen. And from that day on, he never had a dirty bin again.

Lily and Tom were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and houses and bridges with the blocks. They had many colors and shapes of blocks to choose from. But Tom was not happy with his tower. He wanted it to be taller and stronger than Lily's. He took some of her blocks and added them to his tower. Lily saw him and said, "No, Tom, those are my blocks. Give them back!" Tom did not listen. He said, "I need them for my tower. It is better than yours. Look how high it is!" He was proud of his tower, but he did not notice that it was wobbly and unstable. Lily was angry and sad. She said, "You are not nice, Tom. You are not sharing. You are taking my blocks and destroying my house. That is not fair!" She tried to pull her blocks back, but Tom held them tight. They both pulled and tugged on the blocks, but they were too hard to break. Instead, they made the tower fall down. The blocks crashed to the floor and scattered everywhere. Some of them hit Tom and Lily on their waists and legs and arms. It hurt a lot. They both cried and said, "Ow, ow, ow!" Mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw the mess and the tears and said, "What happened here? Why are you crying? Why are the blocks everywhere?" Lily and Tom told her what they did. They said they were sorry and that they were wrong. Mom said, "You should not fight over the blocks. You should share and play nicely. You should not destroy each other's work. You should help and praise each other. That is how you make friends and have fun." Lily and Tom nodded and hugged each other. They said, "We are sorry, Mom. We are sorry, Lily. We are sorry, Tom. We will share and play nicely. We will not destroy. We will help and praise." Mom smiled and said, "That is good. I am proud of you. Now, let's clean up the blocks and build something together. What do you want to build?" Lily and Tom said, "We want to build a castle. A big and beautiful castle. With many colors and shapes. And with a flag and a moat and a drawbridge." Mom said, "That sounds wonderful. Let's do it. But first, let me kiss your waists and legs and arms. They must still hurt a little. There, there. All better." Lily and Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom. We love you. We love our blocks. We love our castle." The end.

One day, a cat named Spring went outside to play. The sun was shining, and it was an easy day to have fun. Spring liked to jump and run in the grass. She was very happy. But then, a big storm came. The wind blew and the rain fell. Spring was scared and ran to find a safe place to hide. She found a big tree to stay under. The tree kept her dry from the rain. Spring was not so happy now. While Spring was under the tree, she saw a small bird. The bird was also hiding from the storm. Spring thought the bird was going to be her food. But instead, the bird started to sing a beautiful song. Spring and the bird became friends. They stayed under the tree together until the storm went away. Now, Spring was happy again.
