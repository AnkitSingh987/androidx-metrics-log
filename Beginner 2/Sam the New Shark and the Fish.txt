Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the New Shark and the Fish

Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there was a new shark named Sam. Sam was not like other sharks. He was very nice and loved to help his friends. One day, Sam saw a little fish named Tim who needed help. Tim was trying to carry a big shell to his home. Sam swam to Tim and said, "Let me help you carry the shell." Together, they carried the shell to Tim's home. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" All the other fish saw how nice Sam was and they wanted to be his friend too. From that day on, Sam the new shark had many friends. He carried things for them and played with them. They all had lots of fun in the big blue sea.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her daddy. They saw a broken toy on the ground. "Look, daddy, a broken toy!" said Lily. Daddy picked it up and said, "We can fix it at home, Lily." At home, Lily and her daddy fixed the broken toy. It was a spring toy that could jump high. They played with the toy all day. The toy would jump and Lily would try to catch it. They had so much fun. The next day, they went back to the park with the fixed toy. Suddenly, the toy jumped high and landed in a tree. Lily was sad. But then, a bird in the tree picked up the toy and brought it back to Lily. "Thank you, bird!" Lily said. The bird flew away, and Lily and her daddy played with the toy again.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a fearful dog. He was scared of many things. One day, Spot went for a walk with his friend, Tim. As they walked, they saw a big print on the ground. Spot and Tim looked at the print. They did not know what made the print. They were scared, but they wanted to find out. They followed the print, and they found a big, friendly cat. The cat had made the print! Spot and Tim were not scared anymore. They all played together and became good friends. The end.

One day, a big red jeep went to perform a show. The jeep was very organized. It could do many fun things. The jeep had a friend named Tim. Tim was a small boy who loved to watch the jeep perform. "Jeep, can you show me how to be organized like you?" asked Tim. The jeep smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! First, we must clean your room. Then, we will put your toys in the right place." Tim and the jeep worked together to clean and organize Tim's room. When they were done, Tim's room was very organized, just like the jeep. Tim was happy and thanked the jeep for helping him. From that day on, Tim learned that being organized made his life easier and more fun. He also learned that it was good to ask for help when he needed it. And the jeep was always there to help him.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot had a very mild nature and loved to lend a helping paw to his friends. One sunny day, Spot went to the park to play. He saw his friend, a little bird named Blue, who was sad. "What's wrong, Blue?" asked Spot. "My wing hurts," said Blue, "I can't fly." Spot wanted to help, so he gently gave Blue's wing a rub. After a few rubs, Blue felt better. "Thank you, Spot," said Blue, "you are a good friend." Spot was happy that he could lend a helping paw to his friend. They played together all day long, and from that day on, Spot and Blue were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bob. Bob liked to bite things. One day, Bob saw a big ball. The ball was dull and not very pretty. Bob thought it would be fun to bite the ball. Bob ran to the ball with a lot of motion. He was very fast. When he got to the ball, he opened his mouth to bite it. But something unexpected happened! The ball was not a ball at all. It was a big, round rock. Bob was very surprised. He did not want to bite the rock. It would hurt his teeth. So, Bob decided to find something else to bite. He looked around and saw a soft toy. Bob ran to the toy and bit it. It was fun to bite the toy. In the end, Bob learned that not everything is what it seems. Sometimes, we have to be careful with what we bite. And Bob was happy with his new toy to bite.
