Category: Beginner
Title: The Ancient Spot and the Farmer

Once there was an ancient spot that made the farmer smile. Every day the farmer would feed the ancient spot with the freshest food. One day, the farmer didn't feed the spot, and something bad happened. The spot dried up and the farmer felt so sad. He felt so bad that he never returned to the ancient spot again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toy bunny. One day, Sue saw a shiny penny on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her bunny. "Look, Bunny! I found a penny!" she said. But then, a big boy named Tom saw Sue's penny. Tom wanted the penny too. He walked up to Sue and said, "Give me the penny!" Sue was sad and didn't want to give away her penny. Sue had an idea. She held the penny tight and said, "Let's play a game. If you can spring like my bunny, I will give you the penny." Tom agreed to play the game. He tried to spring like a bunny, but he couldn't do it. Sue's bunny jumped high and Tom couldn't jump as high as the bunny. Sue smiled and said, "You can't spring like my bunny, so I will keep the penny." Tom was sad, but he knew Sue was right. From that day on, Sue and Tom became friends. They played together and shared their toys, and Sue always kept her shiny penny safe with her little bunny.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside, and she always had fun with her friends. One sunny day, Sue saw a big tree with lots of fruit on it. The fruit was called "pick". Sue thought the pick looked very yummy. Sue wanted to eat the pick, but she couldn't reach it. She had a friend named Tim, who was very tall. Sue urged Tim to help her get the pick from the tree. Tim was happy to help, and he picked the pick from the tree for Sue. They both smiled and laughed. Sue and Tim ate the pick together. They found out that the pick was very healthy for them. They felt strong and happy after eating it. From that day on, Sue and Tim always looked for more picks to eat, and they shared them with all their friends. Everyone was healthy and happy.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big balloon. It is red and yellow and blue. It is very fancy. "Wow, look at the balloon!" Anna says. "Can we play with it?" "Let's ask the man," Ben says. They run to the man who has the balloon. He is wearing a hat and a coat. "Hello, sir," Anna says. "Can we play with your balloon?" The man smiles. He has a big nose and a beard. "Sure, you can play with my balloon," he says. "But be careful. The wind is strong today. Hold the string tight." "Thank you, sir," Ben says. He takes the string. He gives it to Anna. They take turns holding the string. The balloon is very high. It dances in the wind. Anna and Ben are happy. They laugh and sing. They do not see the big tree. The tree has many branches. The branches are sharp. The wind blows harder. The balloon goes near the tree. The string gets caught in a branch. The balloon is stuck. "Oh no!" Anna says. "The balloon is stuck!" "Let me try," Ben says. He pulls the string. He pulls hard. The string breaks. The balloon is free. It flies away. It goes higher and higher. Anna and Ben cannot see it anymore. They are sad. They cry. They have lost the fancy balloon. The man comes back. He sees them crying. He is angry. He yells at them. "You broke my balloon!" he says. "You are bad children! Go away!" Anna and Ben are scared. They run away. They do not play outside anymore. They do not meet the man or the balloon again. They do not have fun. They have a bad ending.
