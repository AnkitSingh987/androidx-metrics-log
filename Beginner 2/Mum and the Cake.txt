Category: Beginner
Title: Mum and the Cake

Mum was very glad and hugged her two children tightly. She urged them to go outside and play. Outside, the children explored and laughed together. They found a tree to climb and jumped in every puddle. Their stomachs growled, so they went back home. Mum had made a delicious cake and there was much to eat. The children ate lots of cake and drank a cool glass of juice. They were glad and their stomachs were full. Mum urged them to go and play again, so they did. They ran and laughed until the sun went down and it was time to go to bed.

One day, a nice fairy flew into a big garden. She saw a little girl sitting on the grass. The little girl looked sad. The fairy flew to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I can't do anything right. I want to be good at something." The fairy thought for a moment and said, "I can help you. I will give you a magic clap. When you clap, something good will happen." The little girl was happy and thanked the fairy. She tried to clap, but her hands were too small. The fairy said, "Don't worry, keep trying. You can do it." The little girl tried again and again. Suddenly, she clapped her hands and everyone around started to applaud. The little girl was so happy that she could make people clap. She knew she was good at something after all.

Once upon a time there was a panda. He lived in a big forest. He was very rude and he didn't like to share with anyone. One day, the panda heard a noise. He opened his eyes and saw a little girl. She was standing at the door of the forest with her hands closed. She looked so scared. The panda was very angry. He shouted at the girl and told her to go away. But the girl didn't move. She wanted to be his friend. The panda thought for a moment and then he closed the door. He didn't want the little girl in the forest. But the little girl was sad. She started to cry. The panda felt bad. He opened the door again and let the little girl inside. He was no longer rude and they became good friends. They played in the forest and had lots of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they find a big puddle of mud. They jump in the mud and splash each other. They laugh and have fun. "Look at us!" Tom says. "We are so muddy!" "Yes, we are!" Mia says. "But we are happy!" They run to show their moms. Their moms are not happy. They are angry. "What did you do?" Tom's mom says. "You are so smelly and dirty!" "You need to wash right now!" Mia's mom says. "Or you will get sick!" Tom and Mia are sad. They do not want to wash. They like the mud. "Please, mom, can we play a little more?" Tom says. "No, you can't!" Tom's mom says. "You need to wash!" "Please, mom, can we keep the mud?" Mia says. "No, you can't!" Mia's mom says. "You need to wash!" Tom and Mia have no choice. They go to wash. They use soap and water. They scrub their skin and hair. They rinse and dry. They are clean and shiny. They do not like to wash. They miss the mud. But their moms are happy. They hug and kiss them. "You are so sweet and lovely!" Tom's mom says. "You are so bright and beautiful!" Mia's mom says. Tom and Mia smile. They are happy too. They still like the mud. But they also like their moms.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball that could roll. It was in a tall house with an elevator. The ball had a friend who was a fierce dog. They liked to play together. One day, the ball and the dog went to play near the elevator. The ball rolled into the elevator and the door closed. The dog was sad and scared. He did not know how to help his friend. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog pushed the button and the elevator door opened. The ball rolled out and they were happy again. From that day, the dog and the ball were more careful when they played near the elevator.
