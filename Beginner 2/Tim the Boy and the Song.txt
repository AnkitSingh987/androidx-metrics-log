Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

One day, a little boy named Tim found a tape. He did not know what was on the tape. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sally. Tim went to Sally's house and said, "Look what I found! Let's play it." Sally had a tape player, so they put the tape in and pressed play. They heard music and people talking. It was a play! Tim and Sally wanted to perform the play for their friends. They practiced and practiced until they knew all the words. The day came to perform the play. Tim and Sally stood on a high stage in front of their friends. They started to say the words they had practiced. But then, something unexpected happened. The tape started to play a new, funny song! Tim and Sally looked at each other and laughed. They decided to dance to the funny song instead of performing the play. Their friends laughed and clapped. It was a fun day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there lived an ancient mouse in a big house. Every day, she would take a walk in her backyard. One day, she was walking in the grass, when she came across a trap. She had never seen a trap before. "What is this?" she asked herself. She decided to take a closer look. Suddenly, she heard a noise from behind her. She spun around to see a fox standing there. The fox said, ""What are you doing here? Mixing up trouble?" The mouse was scared, but she decided to be brave. "No, I'm just looking at this trap," she said. The fox smiled. "Oh, I see. Traps are very tricky. Luckily, I know a way to get around them. You see, I know a special mix that can make this trap disappear." The mouse was amazed and she thanked the fox. She watched carefully as the fox prepared the mix. When the fox was done, the trap was gone. The mouse was so happy and thanked the fox again. From that day on, she was very careful when walking in the backyard.

Lily and Ben were playing in the field. They liked to run and jump and roll in the grass. They saw a big red car parked near the fence. It was shiny and new. "Wow, look at that car!" Lily said. "It is so pretty!" "Let's go and see it!" Ben said. He ran towards the car. Lily followed him. They reached the car and touched it. It was smooth and warm. They saw their faces in the door. They smiled. "Hello, kids," a voice said. They turned and saw a man. He was holding a cloth and a bottle. He looked angry. "What are you doing here? This is my car. Go away!" "But we just wanted to see it," Lily said. "It is so shiny. Did you polish it?" "Yes, I did," the man said. "And I don't want you to touch it. You will make it dirty. Go and play somewhere else." "But we like it here," Ben said. "This is our field. We play here every day." "Well, not today," the man said. "This is my field now. I bought it. I'm going to build a house here. And I don't want any kids around. So go away, or I'll call the police." Lily and Ben felt scared. They didn't want to leave their field. They didn't want to make the man more angry. They didn't want to see the police. They looked at each other. They didn't know what to do. They felt sad and angry too. They wished the man would go away. They wished they could keep their field. They wished they could play with the car. They were very impatient.

Mia was sad. She had a bad dream. She saw a big monster with sharp teeth and red eyes. The monster chased her and wanted to eat her. She woke up and cried. "Mama, mama, help me!" she shouted. Mama came to her room. She turned on the light with the switch. She saw Mia crying on her bed. "What's wrong, my sweetie?" she asked. "I had a bad dream, mama. There was a monster. It was scary!" Mia said. Mama hugged Mia and kissed her. She comforted her and said, "It's okay, my sweetie. It was just a dream. There is no monster. You are safe with me. I love you." Mia felt better. She smiled and said, "I love you too, mama. Thank you for comforting me." Mama smiled and said, "You're welcome, my sweetie. Do you want to sleep with me tonight?" Mia nodded and said, "Yes, please, mama." Mama picked up Mia and carried her to her room. She tucked her in and said, "Good night, my sweetie. Have sweet dreams." Mia closed her eyes and said, "Good night, mama. No more monsters." Mama turned off the light with the switch. She cuddled with Mia and said, "No more monsters, my sweetie. Only love." They both fell asleep and had sweet dreams. The end.
