Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Gloves

Sam was walking down the street. He saw a store and wanted to go inside. Sam stepped into the store and started looking around. He found a pair of gloves on the shelf and he was so excited. He picked them up and tried them on. The gloves were tight on his hands but Sam loved them. Suddenly, Sam's mom was there. "Those gloves look nice," she said. "I want to keep them," Sam replied. His mom smiled. "They fit you tight," she said. Sam smiled and hugged his mom. "Can I store them at home?" he asked. His mom nodded. "Yes, you can," she said. So Sam went home with his new gloves, happy and excited to use them.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Ben and Joe. They were very good friends and loved to play together. One day, Ben wanted to share his toy car with Joe but Joe said no. Ben said it was not fair. Joe said he wanted to keep his own toy. Ben felt sad and angry. He said he wanted to share his toy car because it was the right thing to do. Each of them had their own toys but it's good to share. Just then, a bee flew by and gave them an idea. They decided to share the bee instead. They could take turns chasing it and trying to catch it. It was fun for both of them. The idea worked and Ben and Joe were happy. They learned to share and now they could play together with the bee. It was a good lesson for them both.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a big tree with a green lime. Amy wanted to eat the lime, but she was too small to get it. She saw her friend Tom and asked for help. "Tom, can you help me get the lime? I am too small," she said. Tom was a tall boy and he was not selfish. He said, "Yes, I will help you, Amy." So, Tom got the lime and gave it to Amy. Amy was happy, but when she opened the lime, she found a note inside. The note said, "Tell a secret to make a new friend." Amy and Tom were surprised! They told each other a secret, and a new friend, a cute puppy, came to play with them. They all played together and had a fun day at the park.

One day, a little dog named Max was very bored. He wanted to play outside. He saw an open door and thought, "I can escape!" So, Max ran out the door and into the big world. Max saw a big ice cream truck. The ice cream man said, "Hi little dog! Would you like a cone?" Max barked, and the ice cream man gave him a cone. It was so yummy! Max was very happy. As Max walked, he met a new friend, a cat named Lucy. Lucy said, "Max, let's play together!" They played and had fun. At the end of the day, Max went back home. He was not bored anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a yellow house. In the yellow house, there was a family with a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy car and her toy cable. One day, Lily was playing with her car and cable in the living room. She was having so much fun! But, oh no! The cable got stuck under the big chair. Lily tried to pull and pull, but the cable would not come out. She felt sad and did not know what to do. Lily thought hard and had an idea. She went to the kitchen and found a long spoon. She used the spoon to lift the chair up high. The chair began to rise! Lily pulled the cable out from under the chair. She was so happy, and she could play with her car and cable again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a man with a big ball. The man was rude and did not want to share. Tim asked, "Can I play too?" The man said, "No, you need a license to play with this ball." Tim was sad but watched the man strike the ball hard. The ball went very high and far away. The man laughed and said, "See, you can't do that." Tim felt bad and walked away. The rude man kept playing with the ball. But then, the ball hit a tree and broke. The man was sad because he could not play anymore. No one wanted to play with the rude man, and he was all alone.
