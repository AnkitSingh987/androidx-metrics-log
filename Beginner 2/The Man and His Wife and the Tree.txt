Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and His Wife and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near a big tree. The man and his wife were very happy. They liked to play and have fun. One day, the man and his wife wanted to play a game. The man said, "Let's jump and see who can jump the highest!" The wife said, "Yes, that sounds like fun!" So, they went outside where there was lots of available space to play. They stood next to each other and started to jump. They jumped up and down, laughing and having a great time. After jumping for a while, the man and his wife were tired. They sat down under the big tree and smiled. They were happy they had played the jumping game together. The man and his wife loved spending time together and playing games. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. This train had a big rail to follow. The train was very stubborn. It did not like to stop or change the way it went. One day, a bird came to speak to the train. The bird said, "Hello, train! Can you stop for a bit? I want to ride on you." The train did not want to stop. It wanted to keep going on the rail. The bird was sad, but it did not give up. It flew next to the train and said, "Please, train, I am tired. Let me ride with you for a little while." The train thought about it and decided to be kind. It stopped and let the bird ride with it. They both had fun going down the rail together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had very long hair. He needed a haircut. Tim's mom said, "It's time to travel to the haircut place." On the way, they saw a cat. The cat had a bad leg. Tim's mom said, "Oh no, poor cat!" They took the cat to the haircut place with them. At the haircut place, Tim got his haircut. The cat got help for its bad leg. Tim and the cat were happy. They went back home together. Tim said, "Now we both feel better!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy named Mr. Bunny. Mr. Bunny was very soft and Sue loved him a lot. They played together every day. One day, Mr. Bunny got a small hole. Sue's mom said, "Let me fix it." She got a needle and some thread. Sue watched her mom sew the hole. It was hard work, but Sue's mom did it. Now, Mr. Bunny was all better. Sue was happy and gave her mom a big hug. She said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, Sue and Mr. Bunny went to play again. They had lots of fun and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train loved to travel fast on the tracks. It liked to go from one place to another, carrying people and things. One day, the train met a bird with a whistle. The bird could whistle a fun song. The train and the bird became friends. They liked to play together when the train stopped. The train would travel fast, and the bird would fly next to it. The bird would whistle a happy song, and the train would make its "choo-choo" sound. They had so much fun and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a safe world, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to help people. He had a big heart and always made sure everyone was happy. One day, Tom went to the store to buy some food. He saw a little girl who was sad. She wanted to buy a toy, but she did not have enough money. Tom wanted to make the little girl happy, so he decided to pay for the toy. The little girl was so happy and thanked Tom for his help. From that day on, Tom and the little girl became good friends. They played together and had lots of fun. In their safe world, they knew they could always count on each other. And everyone in the world was happy because of Tom's big heart.
