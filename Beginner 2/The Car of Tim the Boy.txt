Category: Beginner
Title: The Car of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One sunny day, Tim played with his car outside. He pushed the car very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The car went down a drain! Tim was sad and scared. Tim wanted to grab his toy car, but the drain was dark and dangerous. He didn't want to get hurt. So, he thought of a plan. Tim went inside his house and got a long stick. He went back to the drain and used the stick to reach for his car. Tim tried and tried, but he couldn't grab his car. Just when he was about to give up, a big dog came by. The dog saw Tim's toy car and grabbed it with its mouth. The dog gave the car back to Tim. Tim was so happy! The dog was his new friend, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable boy named Tom. He liked to help his mom and dad around the house. One day, Tom's mom asked him to cut some paper for her in the room. Tom said, "Yes, mom!" and went to the room to cut the paper. In the room, Tom saw a big, colorful box. He had never seen it before. He was curious and opened the box. Inside, he found a funny-looking hat. Tom put the hat on his head and something unexpected happened. Suddenly, Tom's toy bear started to talk! The bear said, "Hi, Tom! I can talk now because of the magic hat on your head!" Tom was surprised and happy. He played with his talking bear all day, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big country, there lived a thoughtful lion named Leo. Leo loved to play with his friends, but sometimes he would scare them with his big roar. One sunny day, Leo was playing with his friend, a little bird named Blue. They were having fun, but then Leo let out a loud roar. Blue got scared and flew away. Leo felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Blue. I didn't mean to scare you." Blue came back and said, "It's okay, Leo. I know you are a good friend. Just be careful with your roar." From that day on, Leo was more thoughtful when he played with his friends. He didn't roar too loud, and they all had fun together. And they all lived happily in their big country.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little house. In the house, there was a big space. A boy named Tim lived in the house. Tim liked to play in the space with his toys. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam asked, "Can we play in the space?" Tim said, "Yes, we can!" So, they played with the toys and had lots of fun. After a while, Tim's mom came and asked, "Are you having fun?" Tim and Sam both shrugged and said, "Yes, we are!" They all laughed and played some more in the modest little house.

There was a big dog named Max. Max liked to serve his friends. He would bring them toys and food. One day, Max had a party with his friends. They played and ate a lot. Max was happy to serve his friends. At the party, there was a little cat named Lily. Lily was a spoiled cat. She did not want to share. She took all the toys and food for herself. The other animals did not like that. They wanted to play and eat too. Then, all the animals stopped playing. There was silence. Max went to Lily and said, "We need to share. Sharing is fun." Lily listened and started to share. The animals played again and there was no more silence. Max and Lily became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play. He found a big pit near his home. Tim saw a toy boat near the pit. He thought, "I can sail this boat in the pit!" So, he put the boat in the pit and watched it sail. A girl named Sue came by and saw Tim. She said, "Hi, Tim! What are you doing?" Tim said, "I am sailing a boat in this pit. Do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They both played with the boat and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big frog jumped out of the pit. The frog said, "This pit is my home. Please do not play here." Tim and Sue were very surprised. The frog gave them a big, sour fruit to eat. They said sorry to the frog and left the pit. They went to play in another place and became good friends.
