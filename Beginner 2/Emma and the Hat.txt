Category: Beginner
Title: Emma and the Hat

Emma liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had long yellow hair and a pink dress. Emma liked to brush Lily's hair and make her look charming. One day, Emma found a new hat in her mom's closet. It was a big hat with flowers and ribbons. Emma thought it would match Lily's dress. She took the hat and ran to her room. She put the hat on Lily's head and smiled. "Look, Lily, you are so pretty!" Emma said. "Do you like your new hat?" But Lily did not say anything. She just stared at Emma with her blue eyes. Emma felt sad. She wanted Lily to talk to her. "Please, Lily, say something!" Emma begged. "Don't you like me?" Emma did not know that Lily was not a real doll. She was a magic doll. She could talk, but only when she wore the right hat. The hat in mom's closet was not the right hat. It was a hat for another magic doll. The other magic doll was in mom's closet too. She was hiding behind some coats. She had short brown hair and a green dress. She was angry. She wanted her hat back. She did not like Emma or Lily. She waited until Emma left the room. Then she sneaked out of the closet and went to Emma's room. She saw Lily on the bed, wearing her hat. She grabbed the hat and pulled it off Lily's head. "Ha, ha, ha!" she laughed. "This is my hat, not yours! You are a silly doll!" But as soon as she put the hat on her own head, she stopped laughing. She felt a shock. The hat was not her hat either. It was a trick hat. It made her say the opposite of what she wanted to say. "Oh, no, no, no!" she cried. "This is not my hat, it is yours! You are a charming doll!" Lily heard her voice. She opened her mouth and spoke. "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" she said. "This is the right hat for me! You are a nice doll!" Emma came back to the room. She saw the two dolls talking. She was amazed. She clapped her hands and joined them. "Wow, wow, wow!" she said. "You can talk! You are both my friends!" The three of them played happily together. They tried on different hats and matched them with their dresses. They had a lot of fun. Emma was glad she found the new hat. It was a magic hat. It made her dolls come alive.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to pick berries with her mom in the big, green park. One sunny day, they went to the park to find some red, juicy berries. While they were picking berries, Lily heard a noisy bird in a tree. "Mommy, listen to the bird!" she said. "It's so noisy!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, dear, birds can be very noisy sometimes." Lily wanted to see the noisy bird up close, so she ran towards the tree. But, she didn't see the big rock and tripped over it. She fell down and all the berries she picked flew out of her basket. "Oh no, I lost my berries!" she cried. Her mom came over and helped Lily up. "It's okay," her mom said. "We can pick more berries together." So, they picked more berries and had a fun day at the park. And Lily learned to be careful when running near rocks.

One day, a man put on his expensive tie. The tie was red and blue. He looked very nice in it. The man was going to a big party. He wanted to look his best. At the party, there was a sharp pencil on the table. The man did not see the pencil. He was looking at the cake. The cake was big and yummy. The man wanted to eat it. Suddenly, the man's tie fell on the sharp pencil. The pencil made a hole in the tie. The man was sad. His expensive tie was ruined. But then, everyone at the party started to laugh. The man looked down and saw the hole was shaped like a heart. The man smiled too. His tie was now special and everyone loved it.
