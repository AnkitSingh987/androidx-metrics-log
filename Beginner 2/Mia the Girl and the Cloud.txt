Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cloud

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a purple toy for her friend's birthday. Mia was very excited because she loved the color purple. When they got to the store, Mia saw a purple ball. She asked her mom, "Can we buy this ball for my friend?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can buy the purple ball. I think your friend will love it." As they left the store, they saw some mist outside. The mist was like a soft cloud on the ground. Mia and her mom walked through the mist and felt the cool air on their faces. They were happy they found the perfect gift and enjoyed their day together.

Once there was a boy who loved olives. Everywhere he went, he asked for some and ate them with a big smile. But one day he refused to eat olives. His mom asked him why, but he said nothing. She thought it was funny and decided to give him a long look. The boy had found a new kind of olive. It was round and green and it looked delicious. But it was too hard to open. He tried to use a knife, but it wouldn't work. He asked for help, but his mom refused. She said it was too dangerous. The boy was so sad, he wanted to eat the olive. He decided to wait outside until it got dark. Then he tried the long stick he had found. It worked! He opened up the olive and ate it with a big smile. Now he knew olives weren't a problem anymore.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to pretend they were heroes who could protect the world from bad guys. They had a fan that they used as a weapon. It made a loud noise and blew air. "Look, Lily, I can make the fan spin faster!" Tom said. He pushed a button on the fan and it whirred louder. "Wow, Tom, that's cool!" Lily said. "But be careful, don't break it. Mom said it's not a toy." Tom didn't listen. He wanted to see how fast the fan could go. He pushed another button and the fan spun even faster. It made a very loud noise and blew a lot of air. "Tom, stop it, you're making a mess!" Lily said. She saw the fan blowing dirt and leaves all over the yard. "Mom will be mad!" Tom still didn't listen. He pushed the last button and the fan spun so fast that it flew out of his hands. It hit a rock and broke into pieces. The noise stopped and the air stopped. "Tom, look what you did!" Lily said. She was scared and angry. "You broke the fan! Mom will be so mad!" Tom was scared too. He saw the broken fan and the dirty yard. He knew he was in trouble. He tried to pick up the pieces, but it was too late. Mom came out of the house and saw the mess. "Tom, Lily, what happened here?" Mom said. She was very mad. "Did you break the fan? Do you know how much it cost? How could you be so careless?" Tom and Lily looked at each other. They didn't know what to say. They knew they had done something bad. They wished they had listened to Lily and not played with the fan. They wished they had protected the fan instead of breaking it. They wished they could be heroes, but they felt like losers.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot could do many tricks. One day, Spot's owner lost his pants. Spot knew he had to find them. Spot went to the park where he and his owner liked to play. He saw a big tree and a little squirrel. The squirrel had something in its hands. Spot barked and the squirrel dropped it. It was the owner's pants! Spot picked them up and ran back home. Spot passed by many friends on his way home. They all said, "Good job, Spot!" When Spot got home, he gave the pants to his owner. His owner was so happy. Spot was a hero. And from that day on, everyone knew Spot was a very gifted dog.
