Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Toys of Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys and equipment. He had many fine toys like cars, dolls, and balls. Tim was a happy boy. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He took his favorite toy car with him. But when he got there, he saw something strange. A big sheet was covering a big thing. Tim did not know what it was. Tim went to see what was under the sheet. He pulled it off and saw a big, new playground! It had slides, swings, and a sandbox. Tim was so happy! He played on the new playground all day long. And from that day on, the park was his favorite place to play.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Tim. Tim was always working hard, carrying steel to build his ant home. One day, he met a lazy bug named Max. Max did not like to work at all. "Tim, why don't you quit working so hard?" Max asked. "Come play with me instead!" Tim thought about it, but he knew he had to help his ant friends build the home. "No, Max," Tim said. "I can't quit. I have to help my friends." Max was sad, but he went away to play by himself. Later, a big storm came and washed away Max's home. Max had not worked hard like Tim, so his home was not strong. Tim's home, made of steel, was safe and strong. Max learned that being busy and working hard is important.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play sport. His favorite sport was catch. He had a big, red ball that he liked to throw and grab. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was very good at catch too. They played catch every day. Sam was a reliable friend. Tim was happy to have a friend who loved to play catch as much as he did. One sunny day, Tim and Sam were playing catch in the park. Tim threw the red ball high in the sky. Sam jumped up to grab it. He caught the ball and they both laughed. They played catch all day long and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a clever little duck. One day the duck felt like he needed a place to stay. He decided to explore around a harbor to find a safe spot to build his home. The duck brought some big rocks and some dirt and made a cozy little nest. He used some sticks and mud to make a strong wall for his nest. He smiled as he looked at his work because he had made a cozy and safe home. The duck was so clever. He used the harbor’s water to make a pond. He made sure the pond was deep enough that he could splash around in it and keep cool. He also collected some fruit and seeds so he would always have food. The duck was so happy that he had everything he needed. He could have fun playing in the harbor and he had a cozy nest with a pond. He had made a wonderful home. The end!

Ben and Lily were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and cars. But they had a problem. They only had ten blocks, and they both wanted more. "Can I have some of your blocks?" Ben asked Lily. "No, they are mine!" Lily said. "You have five and I have five. That is fair." "But I want to make a big tower," Ben said. "Please, Lily, can I have one more block?" "No, Ben, you can't!" Lily said. "If you take one of mine, then I will have four and you will have six. That is not fair." Ben was sad. He did not want to fight with Lily, but he also wanted to build his tower. He looked around the room and saw a book on the shelf. He had an idea. "Look, Lily, I have a funny book," he said. "Do you want to see it?" Lily loved books. She nodded and smiled. "Yes, Ben, I want to see it." Ben gave her the book and took one of her blocks. He thought he was very clever. He added the block to his tower and made it taller. Lily opened the book and saw the cover. It had a big number zero on it. She did not know what it meant, but she thought it was funny. She laughed and turned the page. But the next page was empty. And the next one. And the next one. The book had no pictures, no words, no colors. It was a book of nothing. Lily was confused. She did not like the book. She looked at Ben and saw his tower. She realized what he had done. He had tricked her. He had given her a bad book and taken her block. Lily was angry. She threw the book at Ben and knocked down his tower. The blocks fell to the floor and made a loud noise. Ben was shocked. He did not expect Lily to do that. He had lost his tower and his book. He felt sorry for what he had done. He had been mean to Lily. They both started to cry. They did not want to play anymore. They wanted to be friends again. Their mom heard the noise and came to the room. She saw the mess and the tears. She hugged them and asked what happened. They told her the story. They said they were sorry. They said they wanted to share. Mom smiled and said she was proud of them. She said sharing was good. She said she had a surprise for them. She brought a big box from the closet. She opened it and showed them what was inside. It was more blocks. Twenty more blocks. They had different shapes and colors and sizes. They were new and shiny and fun. Ben and Lily were happy. They thanked mom and hugged her. They accepted the new blocks and started to play again. They decided to build something together. They decided to build a castle. They learned their lesson. They learned that being fair and kind was better than being greedy and sneaky. They learned that zero was not a funny number. They learned that sharing was fun.
