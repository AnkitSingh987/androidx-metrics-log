Title: The Story of the Park and the Sun

One day, a big red bus arrives at the park. Many kids come to play. They run, jump, and laugh. The sun is shining and the sky is blue. It is a happy day. In the grass, there is a tiny bug. The bug is very alert. It sees the kids and wants to play too. The bug jumps high and lands on a ball. The kids see the bug and are scared. But then, the bug starts to dance. The kids stop being scared and start to laugh. They dance with the bug. Now, they are all friends. The bug and the kids play together, and they have the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a big swamp, there lived a healthy frog named Fred. Fred loved to jump and play all day with his friends. They would hop from one lily pad to another, having lots of fun. One day, a big bird came to the swamp. The bird wanted to play too, but it was too big and strong. When it tried to play, it made big waves and scared all the little frogs. Fred and his friends did not know what to do. Fred had an idea. He asked the big bird to rest and watch them play. The big bird agreed and sat by the swamp, watching the little frogs have fun. After some time, the big bird learned how to play without making big waves. Now, Fred, his friends, and the big bird all played together in the swamp, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pink cat. This cat loved to play with a ball of yarn. The yarn was pink like the cat. The cat's name was Pinky. One day, Pinky found a wire in the house. Pinky liked the wire because it was shiny. Pinky wanted to play with the wire, so she tried to strike the wire with her paw. The wire wiggled and danced when Pinky hit it. Pinky played with the wire all day. She struck it again and again. Pinky was very happy. At the end of the day, Pinky went to sleep with her pink yarn and her new wire friend.
