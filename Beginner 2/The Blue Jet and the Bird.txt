Category: Beginner
Title: The Blue Jet and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a blue jet. The jet could move very fast in the sky. It loved to fly high up with the birds and the clouds. One day, the blue jet saw a little bird who could not move. The bird was sad because it wanted to fly like the other birds. The blue jet wanted to help the little bird. The blue jet moved close to the little bird and said, "Jump on my back, I will help you fly." The little bird was happy and jumped on the blue jet. They flew high in the sky together. The little bird was very happy and thanked the blue jet for helping it fly.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a swing. A little boy named Tim loved to play on the swing every day. One day, while Tim was swinging, he saw a small bird who looked very alert. The bird was jumping around, trying to solve a problem. "Hello, bird," said Tim. "Why are you so alert? Do you need help?" The bird looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I can't find my nest. Can you help me solve this?" Tim stopped swinging and got off the swing. He looked around the park and saw a tree with a small nest. "Is that your nest, bird?" Tim asked. The bird looked at the tree and said, "Yes, that's it! Thank you for helping me solve my problem!" From that day on, Tim and the bird became good friends. They played together on the swing and helped each other solve problems. They were always alert and ready to help each other. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw something unusual on the ground. It was a small, shiny key. Lily decided to pick it up and put it in her pocket. Lily kept walking and found a little door. She thought maybe the key in her pocket would open it. When she put the key in the door, it opened! Lily was very surprised. Inside the door, Lily found a big room full of toys. She thought it was her room, but it was not. It was her friend Tim's room! The door was a secret way to Tim's house. Lily and Tim played with toys and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred liked to hop around the pond all day. One day, he saw a pretty bottle near the pond. It was a bottle of perfume. Fred thought it smelled nice. Fred wanted to keep the perfume, but he knew it was wrong to steal. So, he went to find the owner. He hopped and hopped until he met a little girl named Sue. Sue was looking for her perfume. "Is this your perfume?" Fred asked. "Yes, it is! Thank you for finding it," Sue said with a big smile. Sue was happy that Fred did not steal her perfume, and they became good friends. They played by the pond every day, and the green frog and the little girl were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant. The giant was scared of a small bug. One day, the giant saw the bug and ran away. The giant was so big, but the bug was so small. The giant had a number game he liked to play. The giant would count one, two, three, and then jump. The bug saw the giant play and wanted to play too. So, the bug went to the giant and asked if he could play the number game. But, the giant was still scared of the bug. The bug said, "I am not bad. I want to be your friend." The giant thought about it and said, "Okay, let's play the number game." They played the game and had fun. The giant was not scared of the bug anymore. They became good friends and played together every day. And that was the day the giant learned that even the smallest friend can bring the biggest joy.

Sam and Lily were twins who liked to play in the kitchen. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red truck. They pretended it was a fire truck and they were brave firefighters. One day, Sam and Lily had a fight over the truck. Sam wanted to drive it and Lily wanted to honk the horn. They both pulled and pushed the truck until it broke in two. The wheels came off and the horn stopped working. "Oh no!" Sam cried. "We broke the truck! Mom will be mad at us!" Lily started to weep. She loved the truck and she was sorry she fought with Sam. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to break the truck. Can we fix it?" Sam wiped his tears and looked at the truck. He saw some tape and glue in the kitchen drawer. He had an idea. He said, "Maybe we can fix it with tape and glue. Let's try it." Sam and Lily worked together to put the truck back. They taped the wheels and glued the horn. They tested the truck and it worked again. They were happy and proud. They heard Mom coming into the kitchen. They ran to her and showed her the truck. They said, "Mom, look! We fixed the truck! We are dependable!" Mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "I'm so proud of you, Sam and Lily. You are dependable and kind. You solved your problem and you shared your toy. You are good twins."
