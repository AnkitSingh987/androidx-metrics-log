Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Kid and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim was a selfish kid. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim and his friend Sam were playing with a ball. Sam asked, "Can I play with the ball too, Tim?" Tim said, "No, it's my ball. I don't want to share." Sam was sad. Soon, Tim and Sam started to fight over the ball. They both wanted to play with it. Their mom saw them fighting and said, "Stop fighting, kids! You need to share the ball and play together." Tim and Sam stopped fighting. They learned to share the ball and play together. They had a lot of fun and were not selfish anymore.

Anna and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park near the lake. One day, they saw a big boat with a pink sail. It looked very pretty and fun. "Can we go on the boat?" Anna asked Ben. "I don't know. Maybe we need to ask someone," Ben said. They looked around and saw a man with a hat. He was the boat owner. He smiled at them and said, "Hello, little ones. Do you like my boat?" "Yes, we do. It has a pink sail. Pink is my favorite color," Anna said. "Mine too," Ben said. "Well, you are very lucky. I have a special offer for you. If you promise to be good and listen to me, I will let you ride on my boat for a little while. How does that sound?" the man said. Anna and Ben looked at each other and nodded. They were very excited. They promised to be good and listen to the man. The man helped them get on the boat and gave them life jackets. He showed them how to hold the sail and steer the boat. He told them stories about the lake and the animals that lived there. Anna and Ben had a lot of fun on the boat. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They saw ducks and fish and frogs. They waved at other people on the shore. They thanked the man for letting them ride on his boat. They said they would never forget this day. The man smiled and said, "You are very welcome. You are very good and brave children. I hope you come back and visit me again. Maybe next time, I will have a different color sail for you." Anna and Ben hugged the man and said goodbye. They ran back to their parents, who were waiting for them. They told them all about their adventure on the boat with the pink sail. They said it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his ball. One day, he kicked the ball very hard. It went up, up, up in the sky. Tim was so happy! But then, the ball went down, down, down. It hit a tap near the big tree. The tap started to drip, drip, drip. Tim was sad, but he was an honest boy. He knew he needed to tell his mom and dad about the tap. Tim ran to his mom and dad. He told them all about the ball and the tap. They were proud of their honest boy. They went to the tree and fixed the tap. The water stopped dripping. Tim was happy again. He knew that being honest was the best way to be.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and sometimes he played too rough. One day, he found a big ball in the yard. Spot was very excited and wanted to play with it. Spot pushed the ball with his nose and it rolled fast. It hit a wall and made a loud noise. Spot saw a crack in the wall. He was scared and didn't know what to do. He ran to his mom to explain what happened. His mom was not happy. She told Spot that he should be more careful when playing. Spot felt bad for being naughty. He promised to be more careful next time. From that day on, Spot played gently and never broke anything again.
