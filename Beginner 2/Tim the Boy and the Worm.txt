Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Worm

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the dirt. One day, while playing in the dirt, Tim found a worm. The worm was wiggly and slimy. Tim thought the worm was fun to watch. Tim showed the worm to his friend, Sue. "Look at the worm I found!" said Tim. Sue looked at the worm and said, "Wow, it's so wiggly!" They played with the worm for a while. But then, the worm got tired and stopped moving. Tim and Sue felt sad. They wanted the worm to be happy. Tim had an idea. "Let's replace the tired worm with a new one!" said Tim. Sue agreed. They went back to the dirt and found a new worm. This worm was also wiggly and slimy. Tim and Sue played with the new worm. They were happy again. The first worm got some rest and the new worm was fun to play with. Tim and Sue learned that it's good to replace things when they get tired. And they played with worms all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to move and play with her ball. One day, Sally saw a tasty apple on the ground. She wanted to eat it, but it was too far away. So, she had an idea. Sally called her dog, Spot. "Spot, can you help me get the apple?" she asked. Spot wagged his tail and ran to the apple. He used his nose to move the apple closer to Sally. As a result, Sally could reach the apple. She picked it up and took a big bite. "Yum!" she said. "Thank you, Spot!" Sally and Spot played with the ball and shared the tasty apple. They were very happy.

Mia and Jack were playing in the park one day. They were running around, laughing and having a lot of fun. Suddenly, they found a pigeon flying in circles above them. Jack said, "Look Mia, a pigeon! Let's try to catch him!" Mia looked excited and agreed. They started chasing the pigeon across the park. The pigeon flew away, but the kids kept chasing it, getting closer and closer. Suddenly, they quarreled over who was going to catch it. Jack said “I can catch it!”, but Mia argued, “No, I'm faster”. The quarrel disturbed the peaceful atmosphere of the park. Mia got angry and ran away, leaving Jack all alone. Jack watched the pigeon fly away sadly and he knew it was all his fault. He felt so bad for making Mia run away. Jack ran after Mia and found her sitting by a tree. He said, “I'm sorry Mia. Can we be friends again?” Mia nodded and they hugged. Everything was peaceful once again. They decided to forget about the pigeon and just play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a pale face and loved to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tim enjoyed his youth and loved playing with his friends. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a little shy, but Tim said, "Hi, Sam! Do you want to play with me?" Sam nodded and smiled. They played in the park, kicking a ball and swinging on the swings. They had so much fun together! As the sun began to set, Tim and Sam knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye and promised to play again soon. Tim felt happy that he made a new friend and enjoyed his day. He couldn't wait for more fun days with Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a toy bell. She loved her bell very much. It was useful because it made a loud sound when she shook it. Sue would shake her bell when she was happy or when she wanted her mom and dad to come to her. One day, Sue saw a small, soft ball. She wanted to play with it. She picked it up and gave it a big squeeze. The ball made a squeaky sound. Sue liked the sound it made. She squeezed it again and again, making the ball squeak each time. Sue thought it would be fun to play with both her bell and the ball. She held the bell in one hand and the ball in the other. She shook the bell and squeezed the ball at the same time. The bell and the ball made a fun mix of sounds. Sue was very happy. She played with her bell and ball all day long.
