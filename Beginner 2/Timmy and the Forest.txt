Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy and the Forest

Once there was a little bear called Timmy who had a big dream. He wanted to learn how to fly. So Timmy went off on an adventure to find a way to make his dream come true. When Timmy arrived at the edge of a magical forest, he saw a bright light in the distance. He looked up and saw the source of the light: an old wise owl. When Timmy asked him how to fly, the owl shrugged and said, “Come to the event tonight.” Timmy was curious and followed the owl deep into the forest. He arrived at a clearing where a huge event was taking place. He was so excited and began to look around for the flying lesson he was promised. As he looked around, he spotted a pile of light, fluffy feathers in the corner. Then, he heard a voice. It was the owl. The owl told him to grab the feathers and jump off the edge of the cliff. Timmy took a deep breath and jumped. With the help of the owl and the feathers, Timmy was able to soar through the sky like never before. He had made his dream a reality!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a broken toy car. He wanted to fix it, but he did not know how. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me fix my broken car?" Tim said. His mom said she would try. His mom looked at the car and saw a knot in the string. She tried to untie the knot, but it was too hard. Tim's mom told him, "I can't fix the knot, but maybe your dad can help." So, Tim went to his dad and asked, "Dad, can you help me fix my car?" Tim's dad looked at the car and the knot. He worked on the knot and soon it was untied. Tim was so happy! His dad told him, "Sometimes, we need help from others to solve our problems." Tim learned that it's okay to ask for help when you can't do something on your own. The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sue. Sue had a broken toy too. Tim remembered what his dad said and told Sue, "Let's ask for help together!" They went to Sue's mom, and she helped fix the broken toy. From that day on, Tim and Sue knew that asking for help was a good thing. They learned that when you can't find the answer, it's okay to ask others. And so, they lived happily, always ready to help each other and ask for help when needed.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have a big jeep that they can ride in the garden. The jeep is modern. It has lights and sounds and buttons. One day, Anna and Ben are playing with their jeep. They drive around the garden and pretend they are on an adventure. They see flowers and birds and bugs. They have fun. But then, the jeep gets dirty. It has mud and grass and leaves on it. Anna and Ben are sad. They want their jeep to be clean and shiny. "Mom, can we bathe our jeep?" Anna asks. "Sure, you can bathe your jeep," Mom says. "But first, you have to bathe yourselves. You are also dirty." Anna and Ben nod. They go inside and bathe. They wash their hair and their faces and their bodies. They use soap and water and a towel. They feel fresh and clean. Then, they go outside and bathe their jeep. They use a hose and a sponge and a bucket. They spray and scrub and rinse. They make their jeep clean and shiny. They are happy. They hug their jeep and each other. "Thank you, Mom, for letting us bathe our jeep," Ben says. "You're welcome, my loves," Mom says. "Now, how about a snack?" Anna and Ben smile. They go inside with Mom. They have milk and cookies. They love their jeep and their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was a very fine fish. He liked to swim and play all day. One day, Fin saw a big shark. The shark looked like he wanted to stretch and play too. Fin swam to the shark and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" The shark said, "Yes, I want to play too." So, Fin and the shark started to play. They swam fast, they swam slow, and they stretched their fins. But then, the shark got very hungry. He looked at Fin and thought he would be a good snack. The shark opened his big mouth and ate Fin. The shark was happy, but Fin was gone. The end.
