Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful Plane Was and the Fly

One day, a beautiful plane was in the sky. It was big and blue. It could fly very high. All the kids loved to watch the beautiful plane. A little boy named Tim wanted to fly the beautiful plane. He had a toy plane that he would push on the floor. He would make it go up and down, just like the beautiful plane in the sky. One day, Tim's toy plane started to fly when he pushed it. It went up in the air and flew around the room. Tim was so happy and surprised! He didn't know his toy plane could really fly. Now, Tim could fly his toy plane just like the beautiful plane in the sky.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there lived a busy bee named Ben. Ben loved to fly around and help his friends. One day, Ben saw his friend Sam, the squirrel, trying to carry a big nut up a tree. "Hi Sam!" said Ben. "You look busy. Can I help you?" Sam nodded and said, "Yes, please. I need to take this nut up the tree, but it's too heavy for me." Ben wanted to help, so he tried to lift the nut with Sam. Together, they managed to carry the nut up the tree. The next day, Ben was flying around when he saw a big storm coming. He knew his friends might need help, so he went back to the cabin to get ready. When the storm came, the wind blew and the rain fell. Ben put on his raincoat and went outside to help his friends. He saw Sam again, this time trying to hold onto a branch in the strong wind. "Sam, hold on!" shouted Ben. He flew over and helped Sam hold onto the branch until the wind stopped. After the storm, all the animals were safe and happy. They thanked Ben for being a good friend and helping them when they needed it. And Ben was happy that he could be there for his friends, no matter how busy he was.

One day, a nosy boy named Tim wanted to watch a cartoon. His mom said, "Not now, Tim. Let's go to the store first." Tim was sad, but he went with his mom to the store. He saw a big TV in the store. Tim thought, "Maybe I can watch a cartoon here." At the store, Tim's mom let him pick a toy. He picked a cool car. Tim was happy. He asked his mom if he could watch a cartoon now. His mom said, "Not yet, Tim. Let's go home first." When they got home, Tim's mom said, "Now, let's watch a cartoon together." Tim was so happy that he forgot about the toy car. He and his mom sat and watched the cartoon. They laughed and had fun. In the end, Tim was happy that he waited.

Once, there was a big sheep. This sheep had to pack a bag. The sheep had a hard time. The bag was too small. The sheep tried and tried, but it was very hard. One day, a little sheep came. The little sheep wanted to help. They tried to pack the bag together. It was still hard, but they did not give up. They knew that if they worked together, they could do it. Finally, the bag was packed. The big sheep and the little sheep were happy. They learned that if they help each other, hard things can be done. The big sheep thanked the little sheep. From that day on, they always helped each other when things were hard.

Once upon a time, there was a turkey named Tom. Tom was a popular turkey on the farm. All the other animals liked him. He was nice and always helped his friends. One hot day, Tom and his friends wanted to play in the water. They found a big tub and filled it up. Tom jumped in first and started to soak in the cool water. His friends all cheered and clapped. Soon, all the other animals joined Tom in the tub. They splashed and soaked together. Everyone had fun and laughed. Tom was happy to share his fun with all his friends.
