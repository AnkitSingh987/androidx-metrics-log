Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Lively Cat and the Ball

One day, a lively cat named Kitty went for a walk. Today was sunny and bright. Kitty loved to spring around and play. Kitty saw a big dog named Max. Max looked angry. Kitty wanted to play, so she asked, "Max, do you want to play today?" Max said, "No, I don't want to play. Go away!" Kitty was sad but did not give up. She thought of a plan. She found a ball and showed it to Max. Max saw the ball and started to wag his tail. Kitty threw the ball, and Max ran to get it. They played together all day long. In the end, Kitty and Max became good friends.

One day, a little bird named Tim was sitting on a seat in the park. He looked up at the sky and saw how calm it was. Tim loved to fly when the sky was calm, so he flapped his wings and flew high into the sky. As Tim was flying, he saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He decided to land on a branch and take a rest. While resting, he met a squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "Hello, Tim! The sky is so calm today. It's a great day to play!" Tim agreed and said, "Yes, Sam! Let's play together!" They played all day long, running and jumping from branch to branch. As the sun began to set, they both felt tired and happy. They said goodbye to each other and went back to their homes. That night, Tim sat on his seat in his nest and looked at the calm sky once more. He felt so happy that he made a new friend and had a fun day. Tim closed his eyes and went to sleep, dreaming about the next calm day when he could play with Sam again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy yummy food for a fun picnic. Sue saw a big lemon and asked her mom, "Can we get this lemon for our picnic?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can use the lemon to make a yummy drink." Sue was very happy and put the lemon in the cart. At the picnic, Sue and her mom made a yummy lemon drink. But when they tried to open the lemon, it started to tear! Suddenly, a tiny, friendly frog jumped out of the lemon! Sue and her mom were surprised but they all became friends and had a great picnic together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to help her mom in the kitchen. They would cook, bake, and feed their pet bird, Tweety. One day, Lily's mom said, "We need to cut some paper. Can you find the scissors?" Lily was ready to help, so she went to look for the scissors. Lily looked everywhere for the scissors. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and even in the kitchen. But she could not find them. Then, she saw Tweety playing with something shiny. "That's it!" Lily thought. "Tweety has the scissors!" But when she got closer, she saw that it was not the scissors. It was a shiny key! Lily took the key to her mom. Her mom was surprised and said, "This is the key to a special box!" They opened the box and found the scissors inside. Lily and her mom were so happy. They cut the paper and fed Tweety some birdseed. From that day on, Lily always knew where to find the scissors.

Lily and Tom are playing with blocks. They want to make a big tower. Lily has many blocks. Tom has few blocks. "Can I have some of your blocks?" Tom asks Lily. "No, they are mine. I need them for my tower," Lily says. "But you have so many. I have only a little. Please share with me," Tom says. Lily does not want to share. She puts more blocks on her tower. It is very high. She is happy. Tom is sad. He looks at his small tower. He wants to make it bigger. He sees a fat block on Lily's tower. It is on the top. He thinks Lily will not notice. He reaches for the fat block. He tries to take it. But the tower is too high. He cannot reach it. He stands on his toes. He stretches his finger. He touches the fat block. But the fat block is not stable. It wobbles. It falls. It makes the tower fall too. All the blocks crash to the floor. Lily's tower is gone. Lily is angry. She sees Tom's finger on the fat block. She thinks Tom made her tower fall. She shouts at Tom. "Tom, you are bad! You broke my tower! You took my block! Go away!" Tom is scared. He did not mean to break Lily's tower. He only wanted one block. He says sorry to Lily. "Lily, I am sorry. I did not want to break your tower. I only wanted one block. Can we be friends?" Lily is still angry. She does not want to be friends. She picks up her blocks. She moves away from Tom. She says no to Tom. "No, we are not friends. You are mean. You do not share. You do not play nice. Leave me alone!" Tom is sad. He has no blocks. He has no friend. He needs a hug. He looks for his mom. He runs to his mom. He tells her what happened. His mom listens to him. She hugs him. She tells him what to do. "Tom, I know you are sad. But you made a mistake. You should not take Lily's block. You should ask her first. You should respect her tower. You should say sorry and mean it. You should try to make up with Lily. Maybe she will forgive you. Maybe she will share with you. Maybe you can play together again." Tom thinks about what his mom says. He knows she is right. He wants to be a good friend. He wants to play with Lily. He decides to do what his mom says. He goes back to Lily. He sees her sitting alone. She is not happy. He walks to her. He says sorry to her. "Lily, I am very sorry. I should not take your block. I should respect your tower. I did not mean to break it. Can you forgive me? Can we be friends again?" Lily looks at Tom. She sees his sad face. She hears his sorry words. She remembers their fun times. She feels sorry too. She decides to forgive him. She decides to be friends again. She smiles at Tom. She says yes to him. "Yes, I forgive you. Yes, we can be friends again. Yes, we can play together again. Do you want some of my blocks? We can make a new tower. A bigger tower. A better tower. Together." Tom smiles back at Lily. He says thank you to her. "Thank you, Lily. You are a good friend. You are kind. You are generous. You are fun. I want some of your blocks. We can make a new tower. A bigger tower. A better tower. Together." They hug each other. They share their blocks. They make a new tower. A bigger tower. A better tower. Together. They are happy. They are friends.
