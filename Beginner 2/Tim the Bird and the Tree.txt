Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

One day, a bird named Tim wanted to soar high in the sky. He saw a big tree and wanted to fly to the top. But Tim was scared. He didn't know if he could do it. "Hi Tim," said a nurse who lived near the tree. "Why are you sad?" "I want to soar high, but I'm scared," said Tim. The nurse had an idea. She gave Tim a pair of fake wings. "Put these on," she said. "They will help you feel brave." Tim put on the fake wings and started to fly. He soared high in the sky and reached the top of the tree. But then, something unexpected happened. The fake wings fell off! Tim was scared, but he found out he could still fly without them. "Thank you, nurse!" Tim shouted from the tree. "I can soar high all by myself!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved candy more than anything else. Every day, he would want to eat candy all the time. His mom and dad told him that eating too much candy was not good for him, but Tim did not listen. One sunny day, Tim saw a big candy store. He went inside and saw all the candy he could ever want. Tim was so happy, but he did not have any money to buy the candy. He thought of a foolish idea. He would try to take some candy without anyone seeing him. Tim tried to take some candy, but the store owner saw him. The owner was not happy and called Tim's mom and dad. They came to the store and Tim felt very sad. His mom and dad told him that taking things without asking was wrong. Tim learned his lesson and promised never to be foolish again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big pole. It was long and hard. He thought it was fun to own a pole like this. He went to show his friend, Sue. "Look at my hard pole, Sue!" Tim said. "I own it now!" Sue looked at the pole, and her eyes got big. Just then, a bird flew down and sat on the pole. The bird was very big and had lots of colors. Tim and Sue did not know what to do. They did not think a bird would come to the pole. The bird looked at them and said, "I am a magic bird. I like your pole, Tim. If you let me stay on your pole, I will give you a wish. You can have anything you want." Tim and Sue were very happy. They let the bird stay on the hard pole. They wished for a big toy box full of fun toys to play with. The magic bird made their wish come true. Tim and Sue played with their new toys and were very happy.

One day, a smart dog named Max was very hot. He wanted to find a cooler place to play. He started to walk around the park. Max saw a big tree with lots of shade. "This looks cooler!" he said. But when he got there, he saw a big cat. The cat did not like dogs. Max knew he had to find a new cooler place. Max walked some more and found a pond. "This must be cooler!" he thought. He jumped in and started to play. The water was cooler and Max was happy. The cat from the tree saw Max and said, "You are a smart dog for finding a cooler place to play."

Once upon a time, there was a little purple bird. This bird had big wings. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, something strange happened. The bird saw a big tree with a lot of fruit. The fruit was purple, just like the bird! The bird was very happy and ate some fruit. After eating the fruit, the bird's wings grew even bigger. Now, the bird could fly higher and faster. The bird was very happy with its big purple wings and loved to fly all day long.
