Category: Beginner
Title: The Gray Bird and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a big gray bird. The bird lived near a lake. The bird liked to mind its own work and play near the lake every day. One day, a small fish came to the bird. The fish said, "Please help me, the lake is not safe." But the bird did not mind the fish. The bird thought the fish was not right. The next day, the bird went to the lake to play. The bird saw the lake was now very bad. The bird was sad and could not play. The fish was right. The bird learned to mind others when they need help.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Sam. Sam loved to play with his toys. One day, Sam's owner got him a new toy. It was still in a box. Sam was very happy and excited. Sam wanted to play with his new toy, but he needed to unpack it first. He tried to open the box with his paws, but it was hard. Sam did not give up. He tried and tried, but the box was not open yet. Finally, Sam's owner saw him and helped him unpack the box. The new toy was a soft, colorful ball. Sam played with his new toy all day. He was very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to decorate her home with her mom. They had a big box of fragile ornaments to put on the tree. Sue was so excited to make the tree pretty. Sue picked up a very pretty ornament from the box. She wanted to put it on the tree all by herself. But Sue's little brother, Tim, wanted to help too. Tim said, "Please, let me put the ornament on the tree!" Sue did not want to share. She wanted to do it all by herself. Mom saw Sue and Tim fighting over the ornament. She said, "Sue, please let Tim help. We can all decorate the tree together." Sue thought about it and said, "Okay, Tim can help too." They both put the ornament on the tree and it looked so beautiful. Sue and Tim learned that sharing made everything more fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch his favorite show on TV. But the TV was up high and he could not reach it. He tried to jump and climb, but it was still too high. Tim felt sad and sat down on the dry floor. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him sitting on the floor. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to watch my show, but I can't reach the TV." Sue smiled and said, "I can help you!" Sue reached up and turned on the TV for Tim. They sat down together and watched the show. Tim was very happy. But then, the show stopped and a new show started. It was a show about sharing and being kind to others. Tim and Sue watched and listened. After the show, Tim and Sue went outside to play. They saw a little girl who was sad. She could not reach the ball stuck in the tree. Tim and Sue helped her get the ball down. The little girl was happy and they all played together. The moral of the story is that it is good to help others and share. Sometimes, when we help others, we learn something new and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree in the park. He wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree. He climbed higher and higher. At the top, he found a box. "Wow, a treasure!" Tim said. He opened the box and found a key inside. Just then, a little bird came and said, "That's my key, please give it back." Tim was surprised but gave the key to the bird. The bird thanked Tim and flew away. Tim climbed down the tree and went home, happy to have helped a friend.
