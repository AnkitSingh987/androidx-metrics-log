Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat had a long tail. The tail was very clean. The cat liked to keep its tail clean. It would lick it every day. One day, the cat was playing outside. It saw a ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. It used its tail to strike the ball. The ball went very far. The cat was happy. After playing, the cat went home. It saw that its tail was dirty. The cat did not like that. So, the cat licked its tail until it was clean again. The cat was happy with its clean tail.

Once upon a time, there was a big structure. It was a house made of wood. In this house, there lived a fierce dog named Max. Max had sharp teeth and he loved to play. One day, a little boy named Tim came to visit Max. Tim saw the big structure and said, "Wow, that's a big house!" Max came out to play with Tim. They played with a ball and ran around in the grass. Tim noticed Max's sharp teeth and said, "Max, you have sharp teeth!" Max barked happily. They played all day and became best friends. From that day on, Max was not so fierce and loved to play with Tim.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly weather. It was a sad sight, for there were dark clouds and no sunshine. Suddenly, a voice came from the clouds. It was demanding something! People were scared and asked, "Who is it?". Then, from the clouds a giant appeared. Its voice was loud and strong and it was demanding sunshine! Everyone looked around, but there was no sunshine in sight. They all knew it was impossible to make sunshine. The giant was angry. It made more thunder and lightning. Soon, it started to rain. Everyone was scared and ran for cover. But the giant just stood there, still demanding sunshine! Everyone was sad, but they knew there was nothing they could do. Then, as quickly as it had come, the weather changed. The sun appeared and the sky was blue. Everyone was glad and thanked the giant for having brought the sunshine. From then on, no one refused to answer the giant's demands, for they all knew how the ugly weather could be changed!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to follow the order of his mom and dad. He would always say, "I hate order!" Tim liked to do things his own way. One day, Tim went for a walk and found a big, unknown box. He was very curious about what could be inside. He thought, "Maybe it is a toy or a big cake!" So, he decided to open the box. When Tim opened the unknown box, he found a note inside. The note said, "Do not hate order. It helps you stay safe and happy." Tim thought about it and decided to listen to his mom and dad from then on. He learned that order was not so bad after all.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a screw on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. He wanted to know what number the screw was. He asked his mom, "Mom, what number is this screw?" His mom said, "I don't know, Tim. Let's ask Dad." Tim went to his dad and showed him the screw. His dad looked at it and said, "This is a number two screw, Tim." Tim was glad to know the number of the screw. But then, his sister, Jane, came into the room. She saw the screw and wanted it. "I want the screw," she said. "No, I found it first!" Tim said. They both wanted the screw and did not want to share. Their mom came in and said, "Why don't you both take turns playing with the screw?" Tim and Jane agreed. They took turns and were glad to share. The conflict was over, and they both had fun with the number two screw.

Once upon a time, there was a happy bug named Bob. Bob lived in a big tree. One day, Bob met a new friend named Tim. Tim was a bird. Bob and Tim liked to play together. One day, Bob said, "Let's play a game. I will hide, and you will find me." Tim said, "OK!" So, Bob hid in the tree, and Tim looked for him. Tim looked up and down, but he could not find Bob. Then, Bob had an idea. He started to ring a little bell. Tim heard the bell and found Bob. "I found you!" Tim said. Bob laughed, and they played the game again. From that day on, Bob and Tim were the best of friends.
