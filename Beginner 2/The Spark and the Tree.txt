Category: Beginner
Title: The Spark and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little spark. The spark was very light and small. It lived in a big forest with many trees and animals. One day, the spark met a big tree. "Hello, big tree," said the spark. "Can I be your friend?" The big tree looked at the spark and said, "Yes, spark, we can be friends. But you must be careful not to hurt me or my friends in the forest." The spark was very happy to have a friend. It promised to be careful and not hurt anyone. The spark and the big tree played together every day. They were very happy. One day, the spark saw a small fire in the forest. The spark knew that fire could hurt the trees and animals. So, it went to the big tree and said, "Big tree, there is a fire in the forest. We must help our friends!" The big tree and the spark worked together to stop the fire. They saved the forest and all their friends. The animals and trees were very thankful. From that day on, the spark was even more careful and always helped its friends. The moral of the story is to be careful with our actions and to always help our friends.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. His name was Bill. Bill was very sad because he was not allowed to play with the other boys. One day, Bill saw a cut in a nearby meadow. It was a little cut, and he decided to go explore it. The cut was very small, but inside it was big and full of adventure. Bill could see faraway mountains and rolling hills. He decided to wander through the cut and explore. But still, he was very lonely. At the bottom of the cut, he found a group of ants. Bill introduced himself to the ants and asked if the ants would like to unite with him. The ants said yes and smiled at Bill. So Bill and the ants started to explore the cut together. They met many new animals, from butterflies and ladybugs to snakes and spiders. Together, they enjoyed the little cut and made many beautiful memories. At the end of the day, Bill and the ants said goodbye and continued on their own ways. But they always knew that they would always be friends, united by their love of adventure.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is black and white. The dog has a red collar. "Look, a dog!" Lily says. "Can we pet it?" "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe it is not nice." Lily and Ben go near the dog. The dog wags its tail. It licks Lily's hand. Lily smiles. She pets the dog. "Hello, dog," she says. "You are kind and soft." The dog barks. It jumps on Ben. Ben falls down. He cries. The dog bites Ben's arm. Ben has a cut. It hurts. "Help, help!" Ben calls. "The dog is bad!" Lily runs to Ben. She hugs him. She sees the cut. She is scared. "Let's go to mom," she says. "She can help." Lily and Ben run to mom. Mom is on a bench. She sees the cut. She is worried. "What happened?" she asks. "A dog bit me," Ben says. "It was not kind." Mom takes Ben to the car. She drives to the doctor. The doctor looks at the cut. He cleans it. He puts a bandage on it. "You are brave," he says. "The cut will heal soon." Lily and Ben say thank you. They go back to mom. Mom hugs them. "I'm sorry, Ben," she says. "Are you okay?" "I'm okay," Ben says. "But I don't like dogs anymore." Lily thinks. She remembers the dog. It was kind and soft at first. Then it was bad and hurtful. "Maybe the dog was scared too," she says. "Maybe it did not know us. Maybe it wanted to play, but it played too rough." Ben nods. He understands. "Maybe you are right," he says. "But we should be careful. We should not pet dogs we don't know. We should ask their owners first." Lily and Ben agree. They learn a lesson. They are still friends. They are kind to each other.
