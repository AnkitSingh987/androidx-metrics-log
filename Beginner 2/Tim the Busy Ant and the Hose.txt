Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Busy Ant and the Hose

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Tim. Tim liked to help his friends. He always had a lot to do. One day, Tim found a hose. The hose was very long. Tim had an idea. He thought the hose could help him increase the water for the plants. So, he put the hose near the plants and turned it on. The water came out fast and the plants were happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The hose started to wiggle and dance! It was not a hose, but a big, friendly snake! The snake helped Tim water the plants every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy found a big cardboard box in her room. She thought it would be fun to play with it. So, she took the box outside to show her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom!" said Lucy. "I found a big cardboard box. Let's play in it!" Tom was happy and said, "Yes! Let's pretend it's a house!" They played in the cardboard box all day. They pretended it was an expensive house with many rooms. Lucy said, "Tom, you stay in the kitchen and cook us some food." Tom said, "Okay, Lucy. I will stay here and cook." As the sun went down, Lucy and Tom said goodbye to their cardboard house. They went back to their real homes, excited to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily was very attractive and liked to wear pretty dresses. She had a best friend named Tom. They liked to play together every Sunday. One Sunday, Lily and Tom were playing in the park. Lily started to worry because she couldn't find her favorite toy, a small red ball. She looked everywhere, but it was nowhere to be found. "Tom, I lost my ball!" said Lily. "Don't worry, Lily. We will find it," said Tom. They looked and looked, and suddenly they found a big, shiny ball. It was not Lily's small red ball, but it was even better. Lily and Tom were so happy and played with the big shiny ball all day long. The lost red ball was soon forgotten, and they had the best Sunday ever.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He went to the creek near his house. The creek had water and fish. Tim liked to sit and watch the fish. A poor bird was by the creek too. The bird could not fly. Tim saw the bird and said, "Oh no! Can I help you, bird?" The bird said, "Yes, please. I am poor and need food." Tim wanted to help the bird. He had a book to read about birds. He read the book and found what the bird liked to eat. Tim gave the bird some food. The bird was happy. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tim was happy too. He and the bird played by the creek. They had a fun day together.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big box of toys. They liked to pretend they were kings and queens and princes and princesses. They had crowns and capes and swords and jewels. "Look, I found a prince!" Anna said, holding up a doll with a purple hat and a smile. "He is very funny. He can talk and sing and dance." "Wow, he is funny!" Ben said. "Can I see him?" Anna gave him the doll. Ben pressed a button on his back. The doll said, "Hello, I am Prince Funny. I like to make jokes and have fun. Do you want to be my friend?" "Yes, we want to be your friend, Prince Funny!" Anna and Ben said. They played with the doll for a long time. They made him tell jokes and sing songs and dance with them. They laughed and clapped and had fun. But then, they heard their mom calling them. "Anna, Ben, it's time to come inside. Lunch is ready." "OK, mom, we're coming!" Anna and Ben said. They looked at the doll. They did not want to leave him. They wanted to play with him more. "What should we do with Prince Funny?" Anna asked. "Let's bury him!" Ben said. "We can dig a hole and put him inside. Then he will be safe and we can find him later." "Good idea!" Anna said. They took a shovel and dug a hole in the dirt. They put the doll inside and covered him with dirt. They put a stick on top to mark the spot. "Bye, Prince Funny!" Anna and Ben said. "We will come back for you soon. We love you!" They ran inside to have lunch. They did not see the doll's purple hat sticking out of the dirt. They did not hear the doll say, "Bye, friends! I love you too! See you soon!"
