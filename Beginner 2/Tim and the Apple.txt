Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games and find new things. One day, he went outside to play with his friends. They wanted to play a game where they find things and get a score. The friends all ran around the park, trying to find things to get a score. They found sticks, rocks, and even a bug. But Tim wanted to find something really big to get a high score. He looked and looked, but couldn't find anything. Then, Tim saw something under a bush. It was big and green, and it smelled disgusting. He thought it would give him the best score. But when he picked it up, it was an old, rotten apple. Tim got sick from the disgusting smell and lost the game. His friends went home, and Tim was sad that he didn't win.

One day, a noisy bird was flying under the sun. It was singing a loud song. The bird was very happy. A small girl saw the noisy bird. She frowned because the bird was too loud. She wanted the bird to be quiet. The girl asked the bird to sing a soft song. The bird listened to her and sang softly. Now, the girl was happy too. They both enjoyed the sun and the bird's new soft song.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet named Coby. Coby was a bit confused. He was very far from his friends in the sky. One day, he saw a big star named Stella. Stella was very bright and pretty. "Hi, Stella!" said Coby. "I am lost. Can you help me find my friends?" Stella smiled at Coby. "Of course! Follow me, Coby. Your friends are close by." Coby and Stella went through the sky together. They saw many pretty things. Soon, they found Coby's friends. Coby was so happy. He thanked Stella for helping him. Now, Coby was not confused anymore.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. One day, they find a big box in the living room. It has a red bow on it. "What is this?" Anna asks. "Maybe it is a gift for us!" Ben says. They run to the box and open it. Inside, they see a big teddy bear. It is brown and soft. It has a blue shirt and a yellow hat. It has a tag that says "To Anna and Ben, from Grandma and Grandpa". "Wow, this is impressive!" Anna says. "It is the best teddy bear ever!" Ben says. They hug the teddy bear and take it out of the box. They hold it with both hands and show it to their mom and dad. "Look, look, we have a new friend!" they say. Mom and dad smile and hug them. "That is wonderful news!" mom says. "Grandma and Grandpa love you very much. They sent you this teddy bear to make you happy." "Thank you, Grandma and Grandpa!" Anna and Ben say. They play with the teddy bear all day. They give it a name and a bed. They read stories to it and sing songs to it. They share it and take turns holding it. They are very happy with their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a small shrimp. The shrimp lived in a big, blue sea. The shrimp had a very old friend. This friend was a big, ancient fish. One day, the shrimp and the ancient fish went to play. They swam fast and played hide and seek. The shrimp was good at hiding. The ancient fish could not find the shrimp. The shrimp would keep hiding until the fish found him. After the game, the shrimp and the ancient fish were tired. They went home to rest. They knew they would keep being friends forever. And they lived happily in the big, blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was an old ice cream man. He had a big, colorful truck. The ice cream man was very nice. He loved to give ice cream to all the kids in the town. One sunny day, the ice cream man came to the park. All the kids ran to the truck. They wanted ice cream. The ice cream man gave them ice cream cones. The ice cream had a nice texture. It was smooth and soft. But it was a very hot day. The ice cream started to melt. The kids had to eat it fast. They laughed and played in the park. They were happy. The old ice cream man smiled. He was happy too.
