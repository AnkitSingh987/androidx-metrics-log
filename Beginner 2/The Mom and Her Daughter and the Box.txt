Category: Beginner
Title: The Mom and Her Daughter and the Box

One day, a mom and her daughter went to the store. The mom said, "We need food for our home." The daughter was impatient. She wanted to go play outside. At the store, the mom and daughter put food in the cart. The daughter saw a big toy. She wanted it. The mom said, "No, we need food, not toys." The daughter was sad but listened to her mom. As they left the store, they saw a big box. It was a surprise! Inside the box was the toy the daughter wanted. A man said, "This toy is for you. You were good in the store." The daughter was so happy. The mom smiled too. They went home with their food and the big toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big network of little ants. They were very intelligent ants. They worked together to find food and build their home. They liked to help each other. One day, a little ant went to find food. He found a big cookie. He was so happy! He wanted to share it with his friends. But when he went back to the network, he could not find it. The network seemed to disappear! The little ant was sad. He wanted to find his friends. He called out, "Where are you?" Then, he saw a line of ants. They were all holding hands. They led him back to the network. The little ant was happy again. He shared the cookie with his friends. They all said, "Thank you!"

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty had a long tail. She liked to swing her tail when she played with her friends. One day, Kitty went to the park. She saw a fair. There were many games and fun things to do. At the fair, Kitty met a dog named Max. Max had a short tail. Max saw Kitty swing her tail and said, "I want to swing my tail too." Kitty said, "You can try, Max!" But Max could not swing his tail like Kitty. Max was sad. Kitty wanted to help Max. She thought of a game they could play together. Kitty said, "Let's play a game with a ball." Max liked to play with balls. They played and had fun at the fair. Max was happy again. They both went home as friends, and Max learned that it was okay to have a short tail.

Once upon a time in a small town, there lived a yellow fireman named Bob. He loved to help people and put out fires. Bob had a big red truck and wore a big hat. One day, Bob saw a little girl named Sue who seemed sad. He asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" Bob smiled and said, "Yes, let's look together." They looked high and low, and finally found the toy under a bush. Sue was so happy! She hugged Bob and said, "Thank you, fireman!" Bob felt happy too because he had helped someone. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it, because it can make both of you feel good.

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin. The dolphin was very smelly. He did not mind. He liked to play in the sea. The dolphin had many friends. One day, the dolphin met a new friend. The friend was a fish. The fish did not mind the smelly dolphin. They played together all day. They had a lot of fun. The smelly dolphin and the fish were happy. They did not mind being smelly. They played and swam in the sea every day. They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little bunny named Bop. Bop was very good at jumping. He loved to jump all day long. One day, Bop saw a big loop in the grass. He wanted to jump through the loop, but he was scared. Bop's mommy saw that he was scared. She wanted to help him. She said, "Bop, you can do it! I will encourage you!" Bop felt better. He knew his mommy believed in him. Bop took a deep breath and jumped. He went right through the loop! Bop was so happy. His mommy clapped and said, "Good job, Bop! You are a very gifted bunny!" Bop smiled and knew he could do anything with his mommy's help.
