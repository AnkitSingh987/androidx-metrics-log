Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a healthy boy who loved to play outside. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. As they walked, Tim saw a big box. He was very excited and wanted to open it. "Mom, can I open the box?" Tim asked. His mom nodded and smiled. "Yes, Tim, you can open the box." Tim opened the box and found a soft, fluffy ball inside. He picked it up and threw it up in the air. The ball landed on his mom's shoulder. She laughed and tossed the ball back to Tim. They played with the ball all day long. Tim and his mom had so much fun at the park. They were happy and tired when they went home. Tim loved that day, and he always remembered the fun he had with his mom and the soft, fluffy ball.

Once there was a stubborn old man who had a cane. He walked with the cane every day. He liked to speak to the children in the park. The children liked to hear his stories. One day, the old man lost his cane. He looked for it, but he could not find it. He was very sad. He asked the children to help him find his cane. The children wanted to help, but they could not find it too. The old man was so stubborn, he did not want to get a new cane. He tried to walk without it, but he fell down. He could not get up. The children tried to help him, but they were too small. The old man was very sad and stayed on the ground.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny park, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends and always had a big smile on her face. One day, Lily was playing with her toy crab, and she thought it would be fun to pinch her friends like the crab did. So, Lily went to her friend Tom and pinched him gently. Tom laughed and said, "Hey, that's like your toy crab!" Lily smiled and nodded. Then, she went to her friend Sue and pinched her too. Sue giggled and said, "That's so funny, Lily!" After pinching all her friends, Lily felt happy. She saw that her friends were laughing and smiling too. They all played together in the bright sun, and they had a great day at the park.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big waffle on the table. He wanted to eat it all by himself. He thought it was okay to be selfish. His sister, Sue, saw the waffle too. She wanted some of the waffle. But Tim did not want to share. He said, "No, it's mine!" Sue was sad. She told her mom about Tim being selfish. Mom came and said, "You need to share the waffle, Tim." Tim did not want to share, but mom insisted. She said, "Sharing is good. You both can have some waffle." Tim and Sue cut the waffle in two pieces. They both ate the waffle and were happy. They learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

Once upon a time there was a duck named Dodo. Dodo had no nest and she wanted one very much. Dodo looked around, but she didn't see a nest anywhere. Everywhere she went was dark and cold. Then Dodo walked around a corner and saw something bright and shining. It was a light! Dodo felt happy and followed the light until she found a nest. Dodo was about to hop in when she saw something strange. In the nest, there were two eggs inside. Dodo was puzzled, and then she heard a voice. "Do you want to unite with us and share the nest?" Dodo was so happy. She said, "Yes! I would love to". And so Dodo and the eggs became one big family in their cozy, light nest. The end.

Anna wanted to buy a gift for her mom. She had some money in her piggy bank. She asked her dad to take her to the store. He said yes and they went in the car. At the store, Anna saw many things. She saw toys, books, clothes and flowers. She thought hard. What would her mom like? She saw a pretty scarf. It was red and soft. She liked it. She asked her dad if she had enough money. He said yes and helped her pay. Anna was happy. She wanted to wrap the scarf for her mom. She asked her dad for some paper and tape. He gave her some. She tried to wrap the scarf. It was hard. The paper was too big. The tape was sticky. She asked her dad for help. He said no. He said she could do it. He said she was brave. Anna tried again. She folded the paper. She cut it with scissors. She taped it. She made a bow. She did it! She wrapped the scarf. She was proud. She was brave. She gave the scarf to her dad. He smiled. He said she did a good job. He said her mom would love it. They went back to the car. They drove home. Anna was excited. She wanted to give the scarf to her mom. She hoped she would like it. She hoped she would hug her. She loved her mom. She was happy.
