Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to draw and color. One day, she found a big, deep box under her bed. Inside the box, there were many crayons and a big paper to draw on. Lily was so happy! She started to draw a picture of her family. She drew her mom, dad, and her little brother. She used all the colors to make the picture look nice. Lily was having so much fun. After a while, Lily's picture was complete. She showed her family her beautiful drawing. They all loved it and clapped for her. They put the picture on the fridge for everyone to see. Lily felt proud and happy.

---

One day, a little boy named Tom found a mysterious ball in his yard. He picked it up and saw that it could roll very fast. Tom was very happy and wanted to show his friends his new toy. He called his friend, Lily, and said, "Look at my new ball! It can roll very fast!" Tom and Lily played with the ball all day. They rolled it back and forth to each other. They had so much fun. But then, the wind started to blow very hard. It was a hurricane! Tom and Lily were scared. They ran inside their houses to hide from the wind. After the hurricane was over, they went outside to find the mysterious ball. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find it. Tom was sad. Lily said, "It's okay, Tom. We can still have fun without the ball." They played other games and had a great day. The moral of the story is that even when we lose something, we can still have fun and be happy with our friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big old bull named Bob. Bob lived on a farm with many other animals. He had a friend named Tim, who was a little boy. Tim loved to visit Bob and play with him. One sunny day, Tim came to see Bob with a book in his hand. Tim said, "Bob, I want to read this book to you." Bob was happy and listened carefully as Tim read the book. The book was about a brave bull who saved his friends from danger. When Tim finished reading, Bob felt very proud to be a bull, just like the brave one in the story. Tim and Bob spent the rest of the day playing and having fun together. They knew that they would always be the best of friends.

---

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is black and brown. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. "Let's go and pet the dog!" Tim says. He is brave and likes animals. "No, Tim, the dog is scary!" Sam says. He is afraid and wants to run away. Tim walks to the dog. He holds out his hand. The dog sniffs his hand. Then the dog growls and snaps at Tim. Tim jumps back and cries. "Help, Sam, help!" Tim shouts. Sam runs to Tim. He grabs his hand and pulls him away. They run to a shelter. The shelter is a small house made of wood. It is in the park. They hide inside the shelter. "Are you okay, Tim?" Sam asks. "Yes, Sam, I'm okay. Thank you for saving me," Tim replies. "You're welcome, Tim. I'm sorry I was scared. But you were too brave. The dog was not friendly. We should be careful with dogs we don't know," Sam says. "I'm sorry, too, Sam. I was not smart. I should have listened to you. You are a good brother. I love you," Tim says. They hug each other. They wait until the dog goes away. Then they go home and tell their mom what happened. Their mom is glad they are safe. She gives them a hug and a cookie. She tells them they are both brave and smart. She loves them very much.

Once upon a time, there was a wild little dog named Max. Max loved to run, jump, and play all day long. He had a big yard to play in and he was always happy. One day, Max saw a pretty napkin on the table. It was red and blue and Max wanted to play with it. Max jumped on the table and took the napkin. He ran outside with it in his mouth. Max played with the napkin all day long. But, oh no! The napkin got a big rip in it. Max knew he had to fix it before Mom saw it. Max was worried. Max took the napkin to his friend, Sam the cat. Sam was very smart and knew how to fix things. Sam helped Max fix the napkin with some soft string. Max was so happy that the napkin was fixed. He put it back on the table and Mom never knew. Max learned that he should not play with things that are not his toys.
