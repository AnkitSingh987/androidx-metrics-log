Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to play outside. One day, she saw a path in her yard. Mia was scared, but she wanted to see where it went. Mia walked on the path. She saw a big tree and a small cat. The cat said, "Meow!" Mia said, "Hi, cat! I am scared, but I want to see where the path goes. Will you come with me?" The cat said, "Meow!" and walked with Mia. Mia and the cat walked and walked. They saw a big pond with ducks. Mia said, "Look, cat! Ducks! I love ducks!" The cat said, "Meow!" Mia was not scared anymore. She was happy she found the path and made a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a big sandbox. Tim had a red bucket. He wanted to make a sand castle. Spot was very happy too. They played together. A girl named Sue came to the sandbox. She was careless. She stepped on Tim's sand castle. "Oh no!" Tim said. Sue said, "I want your bucket." Tim did not like that. He did not want to give his bucket. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the sand away. Tim, Sue, and Spot looked at each other. They all laughed. Tim said, "Let's play together." They all played and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a great idea. He wanted to push a big red ball down a hill. He thought it would be fun to watch it roll all the way down. But there was a problem. His friend, Sam, did not like the idea. Sam said, "No, Tim, don't push the ball. It might hit something or someone." But Tim did not listen. He really wanted to push the ball. So, he waited until Sam went away. Then, he gave the big red ball a big push. The ball rolled down the hill very fast. It went faster and faster. Suddenly, it hit a small house at the bottom of the hill. The house broke into many pieces. Tim felt very bad. He knew he should have listened to Sam. Now, the house was gone, and it was all his fault.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to go to the ocean with her mom and dad. They would play in the sand and watch the big waves. One day, Mia saw a small crab walking on the sand. The crab looked hungry. Mia wanted to help the crab. She saw some food by the water and decided to pick it up. Mia said, "Here, little crab, eat this." The crab ate the food and felt happy. Mia's mom and dad saw her being kind to the crab. They smiled and said, "Good job, Mia! You helped the hungry crab." Mia felt proud and continued to play with the crab by the ocean. They all had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, a 3-year-old, named Jack, walked through the woods all alone. He was small and hardly knew which direction to take. Suddenly, he saw an animal. It was big and had sharp teeth. Jack was a bit scared. He said, "Hey, what are you doing here?" The animal said, "I'm looking for something to enjoy. Do you have anything?" Jack thought for a moment and said, "Yes! I have a comb. You can use it and enjoy it." The animal was very happy and said, "Thanks. This is perfect. However, be careful in these woods. They can be dangerous." Jack thanked the animal and went on his way. He was happy to have enjoyed the experience, but was also careful to stay out of danger.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim did not like salt. One day, Tim's mom put salt on his food. Tim said, "Mom, I hate salt! It tastes bad!" His mom said, "Okay, Tim. I won't put salt on your food anymore." The next day, Tim went to play with his friend, Sue. Sue and Tim played with their toys. Sue said, "Tim, let's make a cake!" Tim said, "Okay, but no salt, please. I hate salt." Sue and Tim made the cake. They put in sugar, eggs, and flour. They did not put in salt. The cake was not bad. Tim and Sue ate the cake. They were happy. They did not need salt to have a good time.
