Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Sweet Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys on the couch. One day, she saw a small cat sitting on her couch. The cat was very cute and soft. Lily asked the cat a question, "Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at her and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Lily!" So they played with toys and had so much fun together. After playing, they were both tired. They sat on the couch and shared a sweet snack. They became best friends and played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, mom put on her apron and asked Tim, "Do you want to help me make cookies?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Mom!" Tim put on his small apron too. They mixed the dough and made small balls. They put the balls on a big tray. Mom opened the oven and said, "Be careful, Tim. It's hot!" Tim watched as mom put the tray inside. While they waited, Tim and mom played a game. They pretended to be in space. Tim would shoot his toy rocket and say, "Zoom!" Mom laughed and said, "Great job, Tim!" They played until the cookies were ready. Tim and mom enjoyed the yummy cookies they made together.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sue. Sue's mom had a shop where she would sell toys. Tim loved to see all the toys, but he felt envious because he did not have many toys at home. "Hi, Sue!" said Tim. "I like your mom's toys. I wish I had more toys too." Sue smiled and said, "Don't be sad, Tim. My mom sells toys, so we can play with them together!" They played at the shop all day, and Tim did not feel envious anymore. He was happy to visit Sue and play with the toys. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends, and they always had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine. The machine was very serious. It could make anything. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the machine. Tim wanted to make a toy. "Please, machine, make me a toy," Tim said. The machine wanted to encourage Tim. So, the machine made a toy for Tim. Tim was happy. He played with the toy all day. But, something unexpected happened. The toy became alive! The toy said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was surprised. He had a new friend. Tim and the toy played together every day. They had a lot of fun.

Mia and Sam were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big truck arrive. It had a zip on the back. Mia and Sam were curious. "What is in the truck?" Mia asked. "Let's go and see!" Sam said. They ran to the truck and opened the zip. Inside, they saw many boxes and bags. They wanted to explore. They climbed into the truck and opened some boxes and bags. They found toys, clothes and books. They were happy. But the truck was filthy. It had dirt, dust and bugs. Mia and Sam got filthy too. They did not notice. They were too busy playing. "Hey, you two! What are you doing?" A voice shouted. It was the driver of the truck. He was angry. He saw Mia and Sam in his truck. He saw the mess they made. "Get out of my truck! You are naughty!" He said. Mia and Sam were scared. They dropped the toys, clothes and books. They jumped out of the truck and ran away. They did not say sorry. They ran back to the park. They saw their mom and dad. They were waiting for them. "Where have you been? We were worried!" Mom said. "What have you done? You are filthy!" Dad said. Mia and Sam did not know what to say. They felt bad. They had done something wrong. They wished they had not opened the zip.
