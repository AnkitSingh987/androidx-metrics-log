Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Person and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tim. Tim was a fine person who liked to make plans. One day, he planned to go to the park with his friend, Sam. At the park, they saw a big dog. The big dog wanted to play. Tim said, "Let's play with the dog!" But Sam was scared. "No, I don't want to," said Sam. Tim did not know what to do. Tim had an idea. He found a ball and showed it to Sam. "Look, we can play with the ball and the dog will play too," he said. Sam felt better and they played with the dog. In the end, they all had fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy had a toy that he loved to play with all the time. One day, Buddy saw his friend, a small cat named Kitty, playing with a new toy. Buddy wanted to play with Kitty's toy, but he did not want to give up his own toy. So, he thought of a trade. Buddy asked Kitty if she would like to trade toys for a little while. Kitty thought it was a good idea and agreed to the trade. At first, they were both very happy with their new toys. But after a while, Buddy started to miss his old toy. He began to cry because he wanted his toy back. Kitty saw that Buddy was sad and decided to trade toys back. When they traded back, Buddy was so happy that he stopped crying. Buddy and Kitty played together with their own toys and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly monster named Bob. Bob was sad because no one wanted to be his friend. He wanted to work hard and learn new things, so he went to a place called university. At the university, Bob met a little girl named Sue. Sue was not scared of Bob. She said, "Hi, Bob! Do you want to work with me?" Bob was happy and said, "Yes, I want to work with you!" They went to a big room with many books and started to work together. As they worked, more kids came to the big room. They saw Bob and Sue working together and wanted to join too. Soon, the big, ugly monster had many friends. Bob was happy because he worked hard, learned new things, and made friends at the university.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to run very fast. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing together. While they were at the park, something happened. A big dog came to play with Tim. The dog was very fast too. Tim and the dog had a race to see who was the fastest. They ran and ran, and Tim won the race. The dog was happy to have a new friend. After playing, Tim and the dog were tired. They decided to stay at the park and rest. Tim's mom brought them some water to drink. They sat under a tree and enjoyed the rest of the day. Tim and the dog became best friends and they always played together at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a request. He wanted to make a cake with his mom. His mom said yes and they went to the kitchen. Tim was very happy. First, they needed to measure the flour. Tim tried to do it, but he made a mess. "Oh no, that was stupid," Tim said. His mom just laughed and helped him clean up. Tim and his mom finished making the cake. Tim learned that it's okay to make mistakes. They had fun and the cake was very yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend. He would pretend to be a big, strong lion. One day, Tim's mom made a big, yummy steak for dinner. But Tim did not want to eat it. He said, "I don't like steak. It tastes bad." His mom said, "You should try it, Tim. It's good for you. It will help you grow big and strong like the lion you pretend to be." Tim thought about it and decided to try the steak. Tim took a small bite of the steak. To his surprise, it did not taste bad. It was very yummy! Tim ate all of his steak and felt happy. He learned that it is good to try new things, even if you think you won't like them. And just like his mom said, the steak helped him grow big and strong like the lion he loved to pretend to be.
