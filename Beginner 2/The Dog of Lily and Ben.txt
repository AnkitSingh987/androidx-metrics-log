Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Lily and Ben

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. It was a sunny day, but a bit cold. They wore their coats and hats. "Look, Ben, a big dog!" Lily said, pointing to a furry animal near the fence. "Can we pet it?" Ben nodded. He liked dogs. They walked slowly to the dog. The dog wagged its tail and licked their hands. It was very friendly. "Hello, doggy," Ben said. "What is your name?" The dog barked. It sounded like "Rufus". "Rufus, you are a good dog," Lily said. She hugged the dog. The dog hugged her back. Suddenly, they heard a voice behind them. "Surprise!" the voice said. "I'm Rufus's owner. I saw you playing with him. He likes you very much." Lily and Ben turned around. They saw a man with a smile. He wore a coat and a hat, too. "Hi," Lily said. "We like Rufus, too. He is a big and nice dog." "Thank you," the man said. "My name is Tom. Do you want to play with Rufus some more? I have a ball and a rope." "Yes, please!" Ben said. "That sounds fun." "OK, then," Tom said. "Let's play!" Lily and Ben were happy. They played with Rufus and Tom. They threw the ball and the rope. Rufus ran and fetched them. They laughed and had fun. They made new friends. It was a good day.

Lily and Sam are going to the zoo with their mom. They are very happy and excited. They have to take the train to get there. They walk to the station and wait for the train. "Look, mom, the train is coming!" Sam says, pointing at the tracks. "Yes, it is. It is very fast and loud. We have to be careful and stay behind the yellow line," mom says, holding their hands. The train stops and the doors open. Lily and Sam follow mom inside and find a seat. They look out the window and see the buildings and trees go by. "Mom, when will we see the animals?" Lily asks. "Soon, honey. The zoo is the last stop. We have to listen for the announcement and get off the train," mom says. Lily and Sam nod and watch the window. They see a bridge, a river, a park, and a big sign that says "Zoo". "Mom, mom, we are here!" Sam shouts. "Yes, we are. Come on, let's go see the lions and the monkeys and the elephants," mom says, getting up and taking their bags. Lily and Sam jump off the seat and follow mom to the door. They realize they are at the zoo and they can't wait to have fun. They smile and hold mom's hand as they walk out of the station.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim was a quick dog who loved to run and play. One day, Tim went to the park with his best friend, Sam the cat. They played and had a lot of fun together. As they played, Tim and Sam found a big ball. They wanted to play with it, but when they touched the ball, they started to tremble. They were scared, but they didn't know why. They thought the ball was dead, but it wasn't. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to move and talk! It was not a dead ball, but a new friend named Bob. Tim and Sam were happy they had found a new friend. They all played together in the park and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a shiny steel bridge. Every day, he would fly around and sing happy songs. One day, Tim saw a small, sad bird named Sam. Sam was hungry and tired. Tim wanted to help Sam, so he shared his food with him. Sam was very thankful and they became good friends. As time passed, Tim and Sam helped each other find food and stay safe. They learned that by being kind and working together, they could gain more food and happiness. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we also help ourselves.
