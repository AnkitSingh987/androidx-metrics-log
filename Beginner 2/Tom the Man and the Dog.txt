Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a humble little town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom had a big sack. He liked to fill the sack with food and give it to people who were hungry. One day, Tom saw a hungry dog. The dog was suffering because it had no food. Tom wanted to help the dog, so he gave it some food from his sack. The dog was very happy and wagged its tail. But then, a mean man saw Tom and the dog. The mean man was jealous of Tom's big sack of food. He wanted the food all for himself. So, he took the sack away from Tom and the dog. Tom and the dog were very sad. The mean man went home with the sack, but when he opened it, he found that the food was gone. It had turned into stones. The mean man was sad and hungry, and he had no one to help him. The moral of the story is that being greedy and mean will not make you happy. It is better to be kind and share with others.

Lily and Ben were playing by the pond. They liked to watch the fish and the ducks. Lily saw a big fish with shiny scales. She wanted to catch it. She said to Ben, "Look at that fish! I want to touch it. I will dive in and get it." Ben said, "No, Lily, don't do that. The water is deep and cold. You might get hurt. And the fish might bite you. It is wild. It does not like to be touched. Let it swim free." But Lily did not listen to Ben. She ran to the edge of the pond and jumped in. She felt the water splash on her face and fill her nose. She opened her eyes and saw the fish. She reached out her hand to grab it. But the fish was fast and angry. It swam away and bit Lily's finger. Lily screamed and let go of the fish. She felt pain and blood. She wanted to get out of the water. Ben heard Lily scream and saw her bleed. He was scared and worried. He ran to the pond and helped Lily out. He wrapped her finger with a bandage. He said, "Lily, are you okay? I told you not to dive in. You were silly and brave. But you were also foolish and rude. You should not try to catch a wild fish. You should respect it and leave it alone." Lily felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You were right. I was wrong. I should have listened to you. I should not have dived in. I should have been careful and kind. I learned my lesson. I will not do it again." Ben hugged Lily and said, "It's okay, Lily. I forgive you. I'm glad you are safe. You are my friend. I hope your finger gets better soon. Let's go home and tell mom what happened. Maybe she will give us some cookies and milk." Lily smiled and said, "Okay, Ben. Let's go home. Thank you for helping me. You are a good friend. I love you." The moral value of the story is: Listen to your friends' advice, be careful and kind, and respect the animals.

One day, a brave cat named Tom found a letter on the ground. He picked it up and read it. The letter said, "Warn all the animals in the forest! Something big and scary is coming!" Tom ran to his friend, a little bird named Sue. "Sue, I found a letter!" he said. "It says we need to warn everyone about something big and scary!" Sue and Tom told all their friends in the forest. They were all scared, but they knew they had to be brave. They all waited for the big and scary thing to come. But then, a tiny mouse named Max came out from behind a tree. He was holding a big balloon. "Surprise!" he shouted. "It's my birthday!" All the animals laughed. The big and scary thing was just a fun birthday party! They all had a great time and were happy they had been brave.
