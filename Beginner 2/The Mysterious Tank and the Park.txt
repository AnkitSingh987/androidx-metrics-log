Category: Beginner
Title: The Mysterious Tank and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big tank in the middle of a park. It was very old and no one knew where it came from. The kids in the park would play around it and sometimes talk to it. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw the tank and thought it was very interesting. Tim walked up to the tank and said, "Hello, big tank! Why are you so mysterious?" He noticed that the tank did not say anything back. Tim felt sad because he wanted to be friends with the tank. Just then, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Tim talking to the tank and said, "Hi, Tim! I like the tank too. It's very mysterious." Tim smiled and said, "Hi, Lily! I wish the tank could talk to us." Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Maybe it can't talk, but we can still be friends with it!" Tim and Lily played around the tank, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, harsh rain. It made everyone soak. In a small town, a boy named Tom and his mom were inside their house. Tom asked, "Mom, why does the rain make us soak?" His mom said, "It's because of history. Long ago, the sky would cry when it was sad, and the tears would make people soak." Tom felt bad for the sky. He wanted to help. He said, "Mom, I want to make the sky happy, so it won't cry and make us soak." His mom smiled and said, "That's a good idea, Tom. Let's sing a happy song to make the sky feel better." Tom and his mom sang a happy song. The rain stopped, and the sky was happy. From that day on, Tom and his mom would sing to the sky whenever it cried. And the town was a happier place because of Tom and his mom.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. They loved all the seasons, but their favorite was autumn. They liked to see the leaves change color and fall from the trees. They liked to scatter the leaves and jump in them. One day, they went to the park with their mom. They saw a big pile of leaves and ran towards it. They were about to scatter the leaves when they heard a voice. "Hey, stop that! Those are my leaves!" It was an old man who lived near the park. He looked miserable and angry. He had a rake in his hand and a bag next to him. He was trying to collect the leaves for his compost. "But we just want to play," Lily said. "Too bad. These are not for playing. These are for gardening. Go find your own leaves," the old man said. He shooed them away and went back to his work. Lily and Tom felt sad. They did not understand why the old man was so mean. They looked around and saw that there were no other piles of leaves in the park. All the other trees were still green. They walked back to their mom, who was sitting on a bench. She saw their faces and asked what was wrong. "We wanted to scatter the leaves, but the old man said no. He said they were his leaves. He was very miserable," Tom said. "That's too bad. He must not like to share. Maybe he is lonely and unhappy. Maybe he needs a friend," their mom said. She thought for a moment and then smiled. She had an idea. "Why don't we make him a card and a gift? Maybe that will cheer him up. We can use some of the leaves and make a picture. And we can give him some cookies that we baked this morning. What do you think?" she said. Lily and Tom liked the idea. They thought it was kind and fun. They found some paper and crayons and some leaves. They made a card that said "Happy Autumn" and drew a picture of themselves and the old man. They put some cookies in a bag and tied it with a ribbon. They went back to the old man, who was still raking the leaves. They approached him carefully and said "Hello". He looked up and saw them. He frowned and said "What do you want?" "We want to give you this," Lily said. She handed him the card and the bag. He took them and looked at them. He opened the card and saw the picture. He opened the bag and smelled the cookies. He felt something in his chest. He felt surprised and touched. "Thank you," he said softly. He smiled a little. "You're welcome," Tom said. He smiled back. "Would you like to play with us?" Lily asked. The old man hesitated. He had not played in a long time. He did not know if he could. But he saw the twins' eyes and heard their voices. They sounded friendly and happy. They reminded him of his own grandchildren, who lived far away. "Maybe for a little while," he said. He put down his rake and his bag. He followed them to the pile of leaves. He watched them scatter the leaves and jump in them. He felt a warm breeze and a ray of sun. He felt something else in his chest. He felt joy. He joined them in the fun. He scattered the leaves and jumped in them. He laughed and they laughed. They played until the sun set and the air grew cold. They said goodbye and hugged. They promised to come back soon. The old man went back to his house. He looked at the card and the cookies. He felt grateful and happy. He decided to share his leaves and his compost with the twins. He decided to make some friends in the park. He decided to enjoy the season. He was not miserable anymore. He was glad.
