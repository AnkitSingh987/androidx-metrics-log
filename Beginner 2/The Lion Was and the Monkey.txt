Category: Beginner
Title: The Lion Was and the Monkey

One day, a big lion was in the forest. The lion liked to roar very loud. All the animals in the forest were scared of the lion's roar. But the lion was honest and did not want to hurt anyone. He just liked to play with his friends. In the forest, there was a long rope. The lion and his friends liked to play with the rope. They would jump and swing on the rope, having lots of fun. The lion's best friend was a small monkey. The monkey was very good at playing with the rope. One day, the lion saw the monkey stuck in a hole. The monkey was sad and scared. The lion wanted to help his friend. He remembered the rope they liked to play with. The lion had an idea. The lion found the rope and took it to the hole. He put the rope in the hole and told the monkey to hold on. The lion pulled the rope and the monkey came out of the hole. The monkey was happy and thanked the lion. From that day on, the lion and the monkey played together with the rope. They were always honest and helped each other. And when the lion roared, the animals in the forest knew he was just playing and having fun with his friends.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing hide and seek with her friend, Tom. They both loved to play this game. They would take turns hiding and finding each other. It was Amy's turn to hide. She ran to a big tree and hid behind it. She put her hands on her knees and bent down low. Tom started to count, "1, 2, 3, 4, 5!" Then, he shouted, "Ready or not, here I come!" Tom looked and looked for Amy. He heard a loud noise behind the tree. It was Amy! She was laughing so hard that she could not hide her voice. Tom said, "I found you, Amy! You were hiding by the tree!" Amy said, "Yes, you found me! But next time, I will find a better place to hide!" They both laughed and continued to play their favorite game, hide and seek, all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very restless. He loved to run around and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big pole in the park. He wanted to play with it. Spot ran to the pole and started to point at it. He barked and jumped around. He wanted his friends to come and play with him. But his friends were too far away to hear him. Spot was sad. He tried to play with the pole by himself, but it was not fun. He sat down and cried. His friends never came, and Spot stayed sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day. He did not like to play or run. One day, Tom met a witch. The witch seemed nice. She said, "Tom, I will give you a fun toy if you play with me." Tom was lazy, but he wanted the toy. So, he played with the witch. They played hide and seek. They had lots of fun. At the end of the day, the witch gave Tom the toy. Tom said, "Thank you, witch!" From that day on, Tom was not so lazy. He liked to play with his new toy and his friend, the witch.

Lily and Ben are playing hide and seek in the park. Lily is the seeker and Ben is the hider. Lily counts to ten and then opens her eyes. She starts to search for Ben. She looks behind the trees, under the benches, and in the bushes. She cannot find Ben. She calls out, "Ben, where are you? I cannot see you!" Ben hears Lily and giggles. He is hiding in a big cardboard box near the slide. He thinks he is very clever. He peeks out of the box and sees Lily walking past. He whispers, "Boo!" Lily hears Ben and turns around. She sees the box and runs towards it. She lifts the flap and sees Ben inside. She says, "I found you, Ben! You are in the box!" Ben smiles and says, "You are a good seeker, Lily! You searched everywhere! The box was an interesting hiding place, right?" Lily nods and says, "Yes, it was! But I saw your shoulder sticking out of the flap. That is how I found you!" Ben laughs and says, "Oops! I did not know that! Next time, I will hide better!" Lily and Ben hug and say, "Hide and seek is fun!" They decide to play again and switch roles. Lily goes in the box and Ben counts to ten. He hopes to find Lily soon.
