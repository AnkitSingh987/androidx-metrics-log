Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bird

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They see a big bird with many feathers. The bird is red and blue and green. It is very pretty. "Wow, look at the bird!" Tom says. "I want a feather like that." He runs to the bird and tries to grab a feather. But the bird is not happy. It squawks and flies away. "Tom, that was not nice!" Lily says. "You scared the bird. And you can't take its feathers. They are not for you." Tom is sad. He wants a feather. He sees a man selling toys. The man has a hat with fake feathers on it. The feathers are red and blue and green, like the bird. "Look, Lily, I found a feather!" Tom says. He runs to the man and asks for a hat. The man smiles and gives Tom a hat. Tom puts it on his head. He is very happy. "Thank you, mister!" Tom says. "Now I have a feather like the bird." Lily looks at Tom. She sees his fake feather. She thinks it is silly. "Tom, that is not a real feather," Lily says. "That is a fake feather. It is not soft and smooth. It is hard and plastic. It is not pretty. It is ugly." Tom feels his fake feather. He sees that Lily is right. He is not happy anymore. He takes off his hat and gives it back to the man. "Sorry, mister," Tom says. "I don't want this hat. I want a real feather." The man shakes his head and takes the hat. He says to Tom and Lily, "You can't have a real feather, kids. The bird needs its feathers. They are part of its body. They help it fly and stay warm and look beautiful. You can only look at the bird and admire its feathers. But you can't touch them or take them. That is not fair to the bird." Tom and Lily understand. They say sorry to the man and to the bird. They go back to the park and watch the bird from far away. They see how it flies and sings and shines. They think it is very pretty. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to step and dance all day long. One day, Sue found a pretty piece of cloth with a stitch on it. She was very happy. Sue was impatient to show her friends the cloth with the stitch. She ran to her friends and showed them the pretty stitch. They all liked it and wanted to play with the cloth too. They all took turns stepping and dancing with the cloth. Sue and her friends had a lot of fun playing with the stitch. At the end of the day, they were all very tired but happy.

One day, in a big zoo, there was a zebra named Zee. Zee liked to march around his home. He would march all day, making loud noises with his feet. Zee was happy, but the other animals were not. "Zee, you are too loud!" said the lion. "We cannot sleep with all your noise. Please stop!" But Zee did not stop. He kept marching and making loud noises. One day, a little girl came to the zoo. She saw Zee marching and making loud noises. She clapped her hands and laughed. "I like your marching, Zee!" she said. The other animals saw how happy the little girl was, and they started to march too. Soon, all the animals were marching and making loud noises. The zoo was full of happy sounds. The lion smiled at Zee and said, "Zee, your marching has made us all happy. Thank you for showing us how to have fun." From that day on, the animals in the zoo would march and make loud noises together. They all learned to have fun, and the zoo was a happy place for everyone.

Lila and Sam liked to play on the beach. They made sand castles and looked for shells. Sometimes they went into the water, but not too far. The water was big and strong. It moved up and down. That was the tide. One day, they saw a big fish on the sand. It was not moving. It had a long tail and a shiny skin. Lila and Sam were curious. They went closer to the fish. "Hello, fish," Lila said. "Are you okay?" The fish opened its eyes. It looked sad. It said, "No, I am not okay. I am lost. I was spinning in the water, and the tide took me away from my home. Now I am here, and I cannot breathe." Lila and Sam felt sorry for the fish. They were compassionate. They wanted to help the fish. "Don't worry, fish," Sam said. "We will help you. We will take you back to the water. Then you can spin again and find your home." Lila and Sam used their hands and a bucket to lift the fish. They carried it to the water. They put it gently in the water. The fish smiled. It said, "Thank you, Lila and Sam. You are very kind. You are my friends." The fish swam away. It spun in the water. It waved its tail. Lila and Sam waved back. They were happy. They had helped the fish. They had made a new friend.
