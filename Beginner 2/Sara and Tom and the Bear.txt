Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Bear

Sara and Tom are brother and sister. They share a room and a bed. Sara likes to keep her teddy bear with her when she sleeps. Tom likes to keep his toy car with him when he sleeps. One night, Sara and Tom hear a loud noise outside. They wake up and feel scared. They hug their teddy bear and toy car and whisper to each other. "What is that noise?" Sara asks. "I don't know. Maybe a big dog or a monster," Tom says. They hear the noise again. It sounds like a roar. "Let's go see mom and dad. They will keep us safe," Sara says. They get out of bed and run to their parents' room. They knock on the door and call out. "Mom, dad, can we sleep with you? There is a scary noise outside," they say. Mom and dad open the door and see their children. They smile and hug them. "Of course you can sleep with us. There is nothing to be afraid of. It's just a storm. The noise is thunder," mom says. "Thunder?" Sara and Tom ask. "Yes, thunder. It's when the sky makes a loud sound. It's not a dog or a monster. It's just nature," dad says. He picks them up and carries them to their bed. He tucks them in and kisses them. "Sleep well, my sweet children. We love you," he says. Sara and Tom feel safe and happy. They cuddle with their teddy bear and toy car and close their eyes. They fall asleep and dream of sunny days.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jane. She had a favourite purple dress with lots of pretty ribbons. Every day she would visit her favourite place - the big gym - and spin around and around. At the gym, Jane found lots of friends to play with. They would hold hands together and spin around, faster and faster. Jane felt so happy and free as she spun. “Look everyone!” Jane shouted. “Look how fast I can spin!” The other children laughed and cheered as Jane spun around in her purple dress. She was so proud of herself, and she couldn't stop giggling. Jane loved spinning so much that she kept spinning until she was dizzy. “That was so much fun!” Jane said, as she twirled her way out of the gym. “Who wants to spin with me tomorrow?”

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bob. Bob liked to do different things every day. One day, he saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. Bob went to the ball but he had to wait. He did not like to wait. While he waited, a big cat came and took the ball away. Bob was sad. Bob tried to get the ball back from the cat, but the cat did not want to give it back. The cat ran away with the ball and Bob could not catch it. In the end, Bob did not get to play with the ball and he was very sad.

Lily was excited. Today was her birthday. She had a big cake with four candles. She had many presents. She wanted to open them all. She heard a knock on the door. It was her neighbor, Ben. He was holding a big box with a bow. He smiled and said, "Happy birthday, Lily! I have a present for you." Lily ran to the door and hugged Ben. She said, "Thank you, Ben! You are very nice. Can I open it now?" Ben nodded and said, "Yes, you can. I hope you like it. It is something fun." Lily took the box and opened it. Inside was a beautiful doll with long hair and a pink dress. Lily gasped and said, "Wow! She is so pretty! I love her! What is her name?" Ben said, "You can name her whatever you want. She is your doll. Do you want to play with her?" Lily nodded and said, "Yes, I do. Come on, Ben. Let's go to my room. We can have a tea party with her and my other toys." Lily and Ben went to Lily's room. They played with the doll and had a lot of fun. Lily was very happy. She had the best birthday ever.
