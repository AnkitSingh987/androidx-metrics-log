Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Box

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play and laugh. One day, they find a big box in the park. They wonder what is inside. "Maybe it is a toy," Tom says. "Maybe it is a cake," Sam says. They open the box. But there is nothing inside. Only a note that says "Joke!" Tom and Sam are confused. They do not think it is funny. They think it is mean. "Who did this?" Tom asks. "I don't know," Sam says. They look around. They see a boy hiding behind a tree. He is smiling. He thinks he is clever. "Hey, you!" Tom shouts. "Why did you trick us?" The boy comes out. He says, "I just wanted to make you laugh. It was a joke." Tom and Sam are not happy. They do not like his joke. They think he is rude. "That is not a joke. That is a lie," Tom says. "Yeah, you are not nice. You are mean," Sam says. The boy feels bad. He sees that he hurt their feelings. He says, "I'm sorry. I was wrong. I wanted to be your friend. Can you forgive me?" Tom and Sam think for a moment. They are still angry. But they are also kind. They decide to give him a chance. "Okay, we forgive you. But don't do that again," Tom says. "Yeah, and next time, tell us a real joke. A funny one," Sam says. The boy nods. He says, "Thank you. I will. Do you want to hear one now?" Tom and Sam nod. They say, "Yes, please." The boy says, "What do you call a fish that wears a bow tie?" Tom and Sam think. They say, "We don't know. What?" The boy says, "Sofishticated!" Tom and Sam laugh. They think it is funny. They like his joke. They say, "That is a good one. You are funny." The boy smiles. He says, "Thank you. You are nice." They become friends. They play and laugh. They share more jokes. They have fun. They gain a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a fierce chicken. He liked to count things. One day, he saw a farmer counting some chickens. The farmer was getting closer and closer. The fierce chicken was scared. The farmer said, "Let's count these chickens together." The fierce chicken was too scared to say anything. He ran away as fast as he could and hid in the bushes. The farmer couldn't find him. He was hiding so well. The farmer said, "Where did that fierce chicken go? We can't continue counting without him!" The fierce chicken stayed in the bushes until the farmer was gone. He was relieved that he had dodged being counted. Even so, he knew that the farmer would find him eventually. The fierce chicken felt like he would never be safe.

Lily loves flowers. She likes to look at them, smell them, and touch them. She has a big book with many pictures of flowers. She learns their names and colors. She wants to see them in real life. One day, she goes to the park with her mom. She sees a lot of flowers in the garden. She runs to them and picks one. It is yellow and pretty. She is happy. She shows it to her mom. But her mom is not happy. She says, "Lily, you should not tear flowers from the garden. They are important for the bees and the butterflies. They need them to make honey and to fly. And they are also important for the people who take care of the garden. They work hard to make it beautiful. If you tear flowers, you make them sad." Lily feels sorry. She did not know that flowers are important. She says, "I'm sorry, mom. I just wanted to have a flower. I like them so much." She gives the flower back to her mom. Her mom hugs her and says, "I know, Lily. I like flowers too. But we can enjoy them without tearing them. We can look at them, smell them, and take pictures of them. And we can also grow our own flowers at home. We can plant some seeds in a pot and water them every day. Then we can watch them grow and bloom. And we can share them with our friends and family. How does that sound?" Lily smiles and says, "That sounds good, mom. I want to grow my own flowers. Can we do that today?" Her mom nods and says, "Yes, we can. Let's go to the store and buy some seeds. And let's also buy a new book with more pictures of flowers. Then we can learn more about them and love them more." Lily claps her hands and says, "Yay! Let's go, mom. I love you and I love flowers."
