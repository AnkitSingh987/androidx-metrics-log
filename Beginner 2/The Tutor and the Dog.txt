Category: Beginner
Title: The Tutor and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a nice tutor who lived in a big house. She loved to help kids learn. One day, she got a letter that said a new kid was coming to her house. She was so happy and wanted to welcome the new kid. She got everything ready for the perfect day. She cleaned the house, made yummy food, and put up fun pictures. She could not wait for the new kid to come. She knew they would have a great time learning and playing together. The day came, and the doorbell rang. The tutor opened the door to welcome the new kid, but it was not a kid! It was a little dog with a big bow on its head. The dog was the new kid's gift to the tutor. The tutor was so surprised but happy too. They became best friends and learned together every day. The tutor found out that even a little dog could be the perfect friend.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a large orange ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he picked it up. Tim saw a girl named Sue. He asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to play!" They both smiled and started to play with the large orange ball. Tim kicked the ball to Sue, and Sue kicked the ball back to Tim. They played for a long time, laughing and having fun. The sun was shining, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big box in a room. The box was closed. A little girl named Lily saw the box and wanted to know what was inside. She was very excited. Lily started to unpack the box. She took out a red ball, a blue car, and a yellow teddy bear. She was very happy with her new toys. She played with them all day long. After playing, Lily noticed that the box was now empty. She felt a little sad. But then, she had a great idea! She turned the empty box into a little house for her teddy bear. Now, the teddy bear had a cozy home, and Lily was happy again. They played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

---

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is thin and has no collar. It looks hungry and angry. "Look, a dog!" Tim says. "Can we pet it?" "No, Tim, it is not a nice dog. It may bite us. We should run away," Jen says. But Tim does not listen. He walks to the dog and says, "Hello, doggy. Do you want to play?" The dog growls and shows its teeth. It jumps at Tim and tries to catch him. Tim is scared and screams. "Help, Jen, help!" Jen runs to Tim and grabs his hand. They run to the door of the park. The dog chases them and barks loudly. "Open the door, Jen, open the door!" Tim says. Jen pushes the door and it opens. They run out of the park and slam the door behind them. The dog cannot get out. It scratches the door and whines. Tim and Jen are safe. They hug each other and breathe hard. "That was a bad dog, Tim. You should not go near it. It could have hurt you," Jen says. "I know, Jen. I am sorry. I was silly. Thank you for saving me. You are a good friend," Tim says. They walk home and tell their moms what happened. Their moms are glad they are okay. They give them cookies and milk and tell them to be careful next time. Tim and Jen promise to listen and to stay away from bad dogs. They are happy to have each other.

Today, Ben and Lily are playing with their toys. They have many cars and dolls and blocks. They like to match the toys by color or shape or size. Ben says, "Look, Lily, I have a red car and a red block. They match!" Lily says, "Yes, Ben, they match. And I have a blue doll and a blue car. They match too!" But then, they hear a loud noise outside. They run to the window and see a big truck. The truck is wild. It makes a lot of smoke and noise. It goes very fast and bumps into the trash cans and the fence. Ben and Lily are scared. They say, "Oh no, what is that truck doing? It is wild!" They run to their mom and dad, who are in the kitchen. They tell them about the wild truck. Mom and dad say, "Don't worry, Ben and Lily. The truck is not wild. It is just doing its job. It is a fire truck. It is going to help someone who has a fire. It has to go fast and make noise to get there quickly. It is not trying to hurt anyone or anything." Ben and Lily feel better. They say, "Oh, we see. The truck is not wild. It is a fire truck. It is helping someone. That is good." Mom and dad say, "Yes, that is good. And you know what else is good? You two are very good at matching your toys. That is a fun and smart game. Do you want to show us your matches?" Ben and Lily say, "Yes, we do!" They run back to their toys and show mom and dad their matches. Mom and dad smile and clap. They say, "You are very good at matching, Ben and Lily. We are proud of you!" Ben and Lily are happy. They hug mom and dad and say, "Thank you! We love you!" They keep playing with their toys and matching them. They have a good day.
