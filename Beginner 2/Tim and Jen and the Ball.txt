Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Jen and the Ball

Tim and Jen are playing with their toys in the living room. Tim has a ball and Jen has a doll. Tim likes to throw the ball high in the air and catch it. Jen likes to hug her doll and make it talk. "Hi, I'm Daisy, the doll. Do you want to be my friend?" Jen says in a high voice. "No, I don't. Dolls are boring and disgusting. Balls are fun and cool," Tim says in a low voice. Tim throws the ball again, but this time he throws it too hard. The ball flies across the room and hits Jen's doll. The doll falls to the floor and its head comes off. "Hey, you broke my doll! That's not nice!" Jen cries. "Ha ha, your doll is broken. It looks even more disgusting now," Tim laughs. Jen is very sad and angry. She picks up her doll's head and runs to the exit. She wants to go to her mom and tell her what Tim did. "Mom, mom, look what Tim did to my doll! He threw his ball and broke it. He is mean and rude!" Jen says. Mom hears Jen and comes to the exit. She sees Jen holding her doll's head and Tim holding his ball. She is not happy. "Tim, what did you do to Jen's doll? That's not how you play with your sister. You need to say sorry and share your toys. And Jen, you need to calm down and stop crying. Let's go to the kitchen and fix your doll. And Tim, you need to put your ball away and find something else to play with," Mom says. Mom takes Jen and Tim to the kitchen. She gets some glue and tape and puts Jen's doll back together. Jen hugs her doll and says thank you. Mom gives Tim a cookie and tells him to find a book or a puzzle. Tim says sorry to Jen and takes a bite of his cookie. He thinks maybe dolls are not so disgusting after all.

Once upon a time there was a happy girl. She wanted to go and find a nap. So she went on her shoes to look for one. Eventually she came to a big tree. It was a perfect place for a nap. She laid down and fell asleep. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The girl smiled, she was so happy. When the girl woke up, she put on her shoes and started to run home. It had been the best nap ever! From that day on, the girl was always happy when it was time for a nap.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty skirt that she liked to wear. One day, while playing outside, she got her skirt dirty. Lily felt sorry for getting her skirt dirty. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I am sorry. My skirt is dirty." Her mom looked at her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I will help you clean it." Lily's mom reminded her to be careful next time. Lily said, "I will remember, Mom." They washed the skirt together, and it was clean again. Lily was happy to wear her pretty skirt, and she remembered to be careful when playing outside.

One day, a cute little cat went to play with her friend, the dog. They liked to run and jump in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The cat and dog were very happy. The cat saw a big red ball and said, "Let's play with the ball!" The dog agreed, and they took turns pushing the ball with their noses. They laughed and had lots of fun. But then, the cat tried to jump on the ball and fell down. The dog said, "Are you okay?" The cat felt embarrass, but she got up and said, "Yes, I am okay. Thank you." They gave each other a friendly touch and continued to play with the ball, enjoying their day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Max. Max liked to play outside on Sunday. One Sunday, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because her toy broke. Max went to Lily and said, "Why are you sad?" Lily told Max about her broken toy. Max had an idea! Max could change the broken toy into a new toy. Max said, "Let me change your toy." Max changed the broken toy into a new, cool toy. Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Max!" They played together all day on Sunday. And they became best friends forever.
