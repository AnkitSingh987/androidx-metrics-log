Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and the Park

Sam was restless. He did not want to sit and play with his toys. He wanted to go outside and run and jump and have fun. He asked his mom if he could go to the park. Mom said yes, but they had to include his baby sister, Lily. Sam did not like that. He did not want to share his bike or his ball or his swing with Lily. He wanted to play by himself. Mom put Lily in the stroller and they walked to the park. Sam rode his bike ahead of them. He was fast and he liked to jog. He saw some other kids playing soccer. He wanted to join them, but Mom said he had to stay close to her and Lily. Sam was bored. He did not want to watch Lily in the stroller. He wanted to kick the ball and score a goal. He tried to sneak away, but Mom saw him. She called him back and told him to be nice to his sister. Sam was mad. He did not want to be nice to Lily. He wanted to play soccer. He threw a tantrum and kicked his bike. Mom was not happy. She told him to calm down and apologize. She said they would go home if he did not behave. Sam did not want to go home. He wanted to stay at the park. He realized he was being mean to Mom and Lily. He felt sorry. He said he was sorry and gave them both a hug. He decided to include Lily in his games. He pushed her on the swing and rolled the ball to her. He even let her ride his bike for a little while. He saw that Lily was happy and Mom was proud. He felt better. He was not restless anymore. He was glad he had a mom and a sister who loved him.

Once, there was a dependable jet. It was big and strong. The jet could fly very high in the sky. It had a job to help people go to far places. The jet was very good at its job. One day, the jet was very tired. It wanted to sleep. But it had to fly people to a far place. The jet tried to stay awake, but it was hard. The jet was afraid it could not do its job. The jet asked its friend, the little bird, for help. The little bird was fast and strong too. The little bird helped the jet stay awake by talking and singing to it. The jet became happy and awake. It flew the people to the far place safely. The jet and the little bird were very good friends. They helped each other when they needed it.

Sara and Ben are playing in the yard. They see a big pile of leaves that dad made with the rake. Sara has an idea. She says to Ben, "Let's joke dad. We can hide in the leaves and jump out when he comes back." Ben likes the idea. He says, "OK, let's do it. But we have to be quiet and not move." They run to the pile of leaves and crawl inside. They cover themselves with leaves and wait. Dad comes back with the rake. He does not see Sara and Ben. He thinks the pile of leaves is too small. He says, "I need to rake more leaves. This is hard work." He starts to rake the leaves around the pile. Sara and Ben hear dad. They giggle. They wait for the right moment. When dad is close to the pile, they shout, "Surprise!" and jump out of the leaves. Dad is startled. He drops the rake. He sees Sara and Ben laughing. He smiles. He says, "You got me. That was a good joke. But now you have to help me rake the leaves again." Sara and Ben say, "OK, dad. We will help you. But can we play in the leaves again later?" Dad says, "Sure, you can. But only after we finish the work." They all rake the leaves together. They have fun. They make a bigger pile of leaves. They play in the leaves again. They joke and laugh. They are happy.
