Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Walk

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his friend, Sam. They liked to walk and talk about fun things. They saw a computer on the ground. It was a big computer and it looked very sorry. They wondered who left it there. Tim said, "Let's take the computer and play games on it." Sam agreed, and they carried the computer to Tim's house. They tried to turn it on, but it was broken. They were sad because they couldn't play games. Tim's mom came in and saw the sorry computer. She was not happy. "You should not take things that are not yours," she said. Tim and Sam felt bad. They had to walk back and put the computer where they found it. They were very sorry and didn't play games that day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, deep hole near the train station. Every day, many people walked by the hole on their way to the station. The hole was so deep that no one could see the bottom. One day, a kind man decided to put up a sign near the hole. The sign had a picture of the hole and a big red "X" on it. The man hoped that the sign would help people be careful and not fall into the deep hole. After the sign was up, everyone who walked by the hole saw the sign and knew to be careful. They all thanked the kind man for his help. From that day on, no one fell into the deep hole, and everyone was safe going to the train station. The sign made a big difference, and the kind man was happy he could help.

Sara loves her pony. She calls him Spot, because he has a big black spot on his back. She likes to brush his hair and feed him carrots. Spot is a nice pony. He likes to run and jump with Sara. One day, Sara and Spot go to the park. They see some other kids and ponies there. Sara wants to play with them. She says hello to a girl with a pink pony. The girl looks at Sara and Spot and makes a mean face. "Your pony is ugly," the girl says. "He has a dirty spot. My pony is pretty. She has a shiny coat. You can't play with us." Sara feels sad and angry. She loves Spot. He is not ugly. He is nice. She wants to play with the other kids and ponies. She does not understand why the girl is mean to her. She looks at Spot. He looks sad too. He lowers his head and whines. Sara hugs him and says, "Don't listen to her, Spot. You are the best pony in the world. I love you." She starts to weep. She does not want to stay in the park. She wants to go home with Spot. She turns around and walks away with him. She hopes to find some nice kids and ponies to play with another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and eat yummy food. One day, Tim's mom gave him some broccoli to eat. Tim didn't like the taste of broccoli. It was harsh and yucky. But his mom said it was good for him, so he ate it all up. Later, Tim went to play outside with his friends. They played a fun game of hide and seek. Tim hid behind a big tree. He found a small toy that didn't belong to him. It was a cute, green toy that looked like the broccoli he ate earlier. Tim knew he needed to find the friend that the toy belonged to. He asked all his friends, but no one knew who it belonged to. Finally, he found a little girl named Sally who was sad because she lost her toy. Tim gave the toy back to Sally. She was so happy, and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim didn't think broccoli was so bad after all.

Tommy liked to play with his cars and trucks. He had many of them and he made them zoom and crash on the floor. He pretended he was a driver and a mechanic. He liked to make noises like vroom and beep and honk. But Tommy did not like to take a shower. He thought it was boring and cold and wet. He did not like the soap in his eyes and the shampoo in his hair. He always tried to hide when his mom said it was time for a shower. One day, his mom had a great idea. She said, "Tommy, I have a surprise for you. If you take a shower, you can play with something new and fun. But you have to complete your shower first. Do you want to try?" Tommy was curious. He wondered what the surprise was. He hoped it was a new car or truck. He said, "OK, mom, I will take a shower. But it has to be quick." His mom smiled and took him to the bathroom. She turned on the water and helped him get in. She washed him with soap and shampoo and rinsed him with water. She said, "You are doing great, Tommy. You are almost done. Just a little more." Tommy was impatient. He wanted to see the surprise. He said, "Mom, can I get out now? I want to play." His mom said, "Yes, you can get out now. You completed your shower. Good job, Tommy. Here is your towel. And here is your surprise." She handed him a big, fluffy, yellow duck. It had a red beak and blue eyes. It squeaked when Tommy squeezed it. Tommy was amazed. He had never seen a duck like that. He said, "Wow, mom, thank you. This is a great surprise. I love it." His mom said, "You're welcome, Tommy. I'm glad you like it. You can play with it in the shower or outside. It is a water duck. It likes to swim and splash." Tommy hugged the duck and said, "Can I take it to the shower again?" His mom said, "Sure, you can. But only if you want to. You don't have to." Tommy said, "I want to. I like the shower now. It is fun with the duck. Can we go now?" His mom said, "OK, let's go. But don't forget to dry yourself first." Tommy dried himself and ran to the shower with the duck. He made it swim and splash and squeak. He laughed and smiled. He said, "This is the best shower ever."
