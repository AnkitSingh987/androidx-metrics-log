Category: Beginner
Title: The Cat with Long Tail and the Dog

One day, a nice cat with a long tail went for a walk. The cat saw a small dog. The dog had a short tail. They said "hi" to each other. The cat said, "I like your tail!" The dog said, "Thank you! I like your tail too!" They were happy and played together. They ran and jumped and had fun. Then, the cat saw a big tree. The cat pointed at the tree and said, "Let's climb!" The dog was scared, but the cat helped the dog. When they got to the top, they saw a big bird! The bird had no tail! The cat and the dog were surprised. They helped the bird find a new tail and they all became friends.

One day, a little fish named Fin swam near the shore. Fin was a proud fish who loved to show off his shiny scales. He saw a big crab on the sand and wanted to talk to him. "Hi, crab! I am Fin, the proud fish. Do you want to play with me?" Fin asked. The crab looked at Fin and said, "Yes, I want to play. Let's play a game." The game was to see who could strike the water with their body and make the biggest splash. Fin went first. He jumped high and struck the water with his tail. The water splashed high in the air. The crab clapped his claws and cheered, "Good job, Fin!" Now it was the crab's turn. He went to the edge of the shore and struck the water with his big claw. The water splashed, but not as high as Fin's splash. Fin smiled and said, "Good try, crab. We can play again another day." Fin and the crab became good friends. They played together by the shore every day, and Fin was always proud of his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble bird. The bird loved to sing. Every day, the bird would fly high in the sky and sing a pretty song. One day, while the bird was singing, a little girl heard the song. She looked up at the sky and saw the bird. The girl smiled and started to sing along with the bird. The bird and the girl sang together every day. They were happy. The sky was filled with their beautiful songs. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful place with big trees and pretty flowers. In this place, there was a big rock that all the animals liked to play on. One day, a little bird found a file. The bird did not know what it was, so it showed the file to all its friends. The squirrel, the rabbit, and the frog all looked at the file and thought it was very interesting. The animals decided to keep the file on the big rock so they could all look at it and play with it. They had a lot of fun with their new toy and they all lived happily in their peaceful place.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the beach. The beach was interesting with lots of shells and sand. Tim and Spot liked to play in the water and make sand castles. Tim saw a big shell in the sand. He picked it up and said, "Spot, look at this big shell! It's so interesting!" Spot barked and wagged his tail. They found more shells and put them in a pile. Suddenly, they heard a small voice call, "Help!" They looked around and saw a little crab stuck in the sand. Tim said, "Don't worry, little crab, we will help you!" They dug the crab out and it said, "Thank you, Tim and Spot!" The crab waved goodbye and went back to its home. Tim and Spot were happy they could help and went back to playing on the interesting beach.

Once upon a time, there was a nice teacher. She had many pupils in her class. One day, a new pupil came to the class. The teacher said, "Welcome!" to the new pupil. All the kids were happy to meet the new friend. The new pupil was tired. He had a long walk to school. The teacher said, "Sit down and rest." The new pupil sat down and felt better. The kids played and had fun. They did not know the new pupil had a secret. The new pupil was not a kid. He was a cat! The cat was good at pretending to be a kid. The teacher and the kids were very surprised. They all laughed and played with the cat. The cat was happy to be their new friend.
