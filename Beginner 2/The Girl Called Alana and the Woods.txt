Category: Beginner
Title: The Girl Called Alana and the Woods

Once upon a time there was a girl called Alana who loved to explore. Every day she would look for new places to go. One day Alana spotted something glittering in the woods. It was a glass! She raced to it and decided to take it home with her. When she got home, she showed her mom the glass. But her mom disagreed. She said,"No, Alana. You can't have this glass." Alana was sad and she wanted the glass very much. Each day Alana asked her mom if she could have the glass and each day her mom disagreed. But then one day her mom said,"Alana, if you can find five more glasses like this one, then you can have this one." Alana was so excited! She looked everywhere but she couldn't find five more glasses. They weren't available anywhere. Until one day Alana went to the beach and she saw that the tide had left lots of little glass pieces in the sand! She started collecting them, one by one until she had five more glasses! She was so happy! She showed them to her mom and her mom agreed that it was enough. Alana was so pleased that she could finally have the glass she wanted. She was also so proud of herself for finding the five little pieces of glass that made her dream come true.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a small animal in a bush. It was a kitten, but it was very dirty and thin. It looked sad and scared. "Can we help it?" Lily asked Ben. "Maybe we can give it some food and water," Ben said. "We have some bread and juice in our backpack." They took out their snacks and put them near the bush. The kitten smelled the food and came out slowly. It ate and drank with a hungry sound. Ben and Lily smiled and watched. "Can we take it home?" Lily asked Ben. "I don't know," Ben said. "Mom and Dad might not let us. They already have a dog and a fish." "But the kitten has no home," Lily said. "It will be cold and lonely in the park." Ben thought for a while. He liked the kitten, too, but he knew their parents had rules. He had an idea. "Let's go to the animal shelter," he said. "They can take care of the kitten and find it a good family." Lily nodded. She agreed with Ben. They picked up the kitten gently and put it in their backpack. They walked to the animal shelter and told the lady there what they had done. The lady thanked them and praised them. She said they were very generous and kind. She said the kitten would be happy and healthy soon. She gave them a sticker and a hug. Ben and Lily felt good. They had helped a small animal in need. They learned that sometimes it is better to give than to keep. The end.

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play on the lawn in front of their houses. They had a lot of fun with their toys and balls. But there was a big dog that lived on the other side of the street. The dog was mean and loud. He liked to chase and bite things. Sometimes he ran out of his yard and scared Tim and Sam. One day, Tim and Sam were playing with their new cars on the lawn. They made noises and raced them on the grass. They did not see the dog sneak out of his gate. The dog saw the cars and wanted to catch them. He ran across the street and barked. Tim and Sam heard him and got scared. They dropped their cars and ran to their houses. "Help! Help!" they cried. "The dog is coming!" But the dog was faster. He reached the lawn and grabbed one of the cars in his mouth. He shook it and tore it apart. He was about to get the other car when he heard a loud roar. It was Rex, Tim's big cat. Rex was sleeping on the porch, but he woke up when he heard the noise. He saw the dog hurting the cars and his friends. He did not like that. He jumped off the porch and ran to the lawn. He puffed up his fur and hissed. He showed his claws and teeth. He was not afraid of the dog. "Go away, you bad dog!" he said. "Leave the cars and my friends alone! This is my lawn and I will protect it!" The dog was surprised. He did not expect a cat to stand up to him. He dropped the car and growled. He wanted to fight, but he saw Rex was ready. He thought Rex was brave and strong. He decided he did not want to mess with him. He turned around and ran back to his yard. He did not bother Tim and Sam again. Tim and Sam came out of their houses. They saw Rex on the lawn. They saw their cars. One was broken and one was okay. They felt sad and happy. They hugged Rex and thanked him. "Thank you, Rex!" they said. "You are a good cat! You saved our car and us! You are our hero!" Rex purred and licked them. He was happy too. He liked Tim and Sam. They were his friends. He wanted to protect them. He was a good cat.
