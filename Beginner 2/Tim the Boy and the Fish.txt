Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they decided to pretend to be fishermen. They all had nets to catch fish. Tim had a broken net, but he still wanted to play with his friends. So, he tried to fix it with some tape. His friends were happy that he could play with them. They all went to the park and pretended to catch fish with their nets. They laughed and played all day long, and Tim was happy that his broken net still worked. They had lots of fun pretending to be fishermen together.

---

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Bob. Bob was a regular fish, not too big, not too small. He loved to swim in the deep blue sea with his friends. They would play all day, swimming fast and slow, having fun in the water. One day, Bob and his friends found a big jelly. It was wiggly and jiggly, and they had never seen anything like it before. They all wanted to touch the jelly, but they were scared. They didn't know if it was safe or not. Bob was brave. He decided to swim close to the jelly and touch it. When he did, he found out that the jelly was soft and squishy. It didn't hurt him at all. Bob and his friends all took turns touching the jelly, and they had a great time. From that day on, they knew that jellies could be fun to play with, as long as they were careful.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the cool water all day long. He would swim fast, and he would swim slow. Fin was very happy in his home. One day, Fin saw a big rock. He wanted to raise up and see what was on the other side. So, Fin swam up, up, up, until he was at the top of the rock. From there, he could see the whole pond. It was so big and pretty. Fin saw many new friends to play with. He saw a slow turtle, a fast frog, and a little bug. They all had fun swimming and playing together. And when the sun went down, they all went home to rest. Fin was so happy to have new friends and a big pond to swim in.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park with their bikes. They had a row of keys that they found in a box. They liked to pretend that they were unlocking different things with the keys. "Look, I can unlock this gate!" Lily said, holding a key to the metal bars. "No, you can't. That's not the right key. Let me try." Tom said, taking the key from Lily. They tried different keys, but none of them worked. They were getting bored and hungry. "Let's go home and have a snack." Lily said. "OK, but we have to lock our bikes first." Tom said. They went to the bike rack, where they had left their bikes. But they saw something that made them very alert. Someone was trying to take their bikes! "Hey, stop! Those are our bikes!" Lily shouted. The person looked at them and ran away, leaving the bikes behind. Lily and Tom were very happy that they scared the person away. They locked their bikes with their own keys, and put the row of keys back in the box. "Maybe we can use these keys to unlock something else another day." Tom said. "Maybe. But right now, I want a snack." Lily said. They held hands and walked home, feeling proud and hungry.

Sam and Mia are playing in the park. They see a big metal slide. Sam wants to go on it, but Mia is nervous. She thinks it is too high and too fast. "Come on, Mia, it is fun!" Sam says. "Don't be scared, I will go with you." "No, no, no, I don't want to!" Mia says. "It is too scary, I will fall!" Sam tries to pull Mia, but she holds on to a bench. She shakes her head and cries. Sam feels sorry for Mia. He does not want to scare her. He thinks of a way to make her feel better. "Look, Mia, there is a small slide over there. It is not high and not fast. It is just right for you. Do you want to try it?" Sam says. Mia looks at the small slide. It is yellow and shiny. It has a ladder and a tunnel. It looks fun and safe. "Maybe, maybe I can try it," Mia says. "But you have to go with me." "OK, OK, I will go with you," Sam says. "Let's go together." Sam and Mia hold hands and walk to the small slide. They climb the ladder and go through the tunnel. They slide down and laugh. They are not scared, they are happy. They hug each other and play some more.
