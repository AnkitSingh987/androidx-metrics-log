Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to watch her screen all day. One day, her mom said, "Lily, let's go outside and play. It will excite you." Lily was not sure, but she went outside with her mom. They found a big, pretty tree. It had many original leaves with different shapes and colors. Lily and her mom played under the tree, and they laughed a lot. As they played, Lily forgot about her screen. She realized that playing outside was more fun than watching her screen all day. She learned that it is important to try new things and enjoy the world around her.

Tom and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a big truck and Mia has a doll. Tom likes to make loud noises with his truck and pretend to crash into things. Mia likes to dress up her doll and talk to her. "Tom, stop being so rude!" Mia says. "You are making too much noise and you are hurting my doll!" Tom does not listen to Mia. He thinks his truck is more fun than her doll. He drives his truck closer to Mia and bumps into her doll. Mia's doll falls on the floor and her dress gets dirty. "Tom, you are mean!" Mia cries. "You made my doll fall and you ruined her dress!" Tom laughs and picks up Mia's doll. He sees that the doll has a rubber band around her hair. He pulls the rubber band and snaps it on the doll's head. The doll's hair gets messy and Mia gets more upset. "Tom, give me back my doll!" Mia shouts. "You are hurting her!" Tom runs away with the doll and heads to the door. He wants to hide the doll in his room and make Mia chase him. He thinks it is a funny game. He opens the door and runs outside. But he forgets to close the door behind him. Mia sees the door open and runs after Tom. She wants to get her doll back and tell him to say sorry. She catches up with Tom in the garden and grabs the doll from his hands. Tom tries to hold on to the doll, but Mia is stronger. She pulls the doll away and hugs her. "Tom, you are a bad boy!" Mia says. "You need to say sorry to me and to my doll!" Tom does not want to say sorry. He thinks Mia is boring and he does not like her doll. He sticks out his tongue and makes a face at Mia. He turns around and runs back to the door. He wants to go to his room and play with his truck. He reaches the door and tries to open it. But he cannot. The door is locked. Tom is confused and scared. He does not know why the door is locked. He does not have a key. He bangs on the door and calls for his mom. He hopes she will hear him and open the door. But his mom does not hear him. She is in the kitchen, making lunch. She does not know that Tom and Mia are outside. She thinks they are still playing in the living room. She locked the door to keep them safe and to keep the bugs out. Tom and Mia are stuck outside. They do not know what to do. They are hungry and thirsty and hot. They wish they had not been so rude to each other. They wish they had shared their toys and played nicely. They wish they could go back inside and say sorry to their mom. They wish they had a key.

Once upon a time there were two friends called John and Lucy. They had a big garden with lots of flowers and trees. One day, they were playing hide and seek. Suddenly, they heard a loud trumpet sound! They were very alert and they knew they had to find out where it was coming from. John said, "Let's go and find out who is playing such a loud trumpet!" Lucy said, "OK!". So they followed the noise. When they arrived at a big hill, they found an old man playing a trumpet. The man said, "Hello children! Can you help me find my glasses?" John and Lucy said, "Yes!" But, they didn't see the glasses anywhere. The man said, "Let's look together!" So, John and Lucy looked for the glasses and eventually found them behind a nearby bush. The man was so happy to have his glasses back, he said, "Thank you children! I am so grateful. Here, please have this trumpet as a gift!" John and Lucy were overjoyed with their special gift and they kept it forever. The end!
