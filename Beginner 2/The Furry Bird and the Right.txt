Category: Beginner
Title: The Furry Bird and the Right

Once upon a time, there was a furry little bird. The bird wanted to fly high in the sky. He flapped his wings, but something was not right. The bird said to his friends, "I can't soar." They responded, "But why not?" The furry bird said, "I think it's because I don't have the right structure. I want to fly so high!" His friends nodded and said, "We will help you make the right structure." So they all worked together. They gathered sticks and feathers. After some time, the furry little bird had the right structure. He flapped his wings and soared! His friends cheered, and the little bird kept flying higher and higher into the sky.

Lily and Ben love to play with their toy tank. It is big and green and has a long gun. They like to make noises and pretend they are in a war. One day, they have a new idea. They want to decorate their tank with stickers and paint. They find some stickers in their room. They have stars and hearts and animals. They stick them all over the tank. They think it looks very pretty. Then they go to the kitchen and look for some paint. They see a big can of red paint on the shelf. They want to use it, but it is too high for them to reach. They try to climb on a chair, but it is wobbly. They try to pull the can, but it is heavy. They try to ask for help, but no one is home. They feel helpless. They start to cry. "Mommy! Daddy! Where are you?" they shout. Suddenly, they hear a loud noise. It is their dog, Rex. He comes running into the kitchen. He sees the can of paint and thinks it is a toy. He jumps and bites it. The can falls and breaks. Red paint spills everywhere. It splashes on the floor, the walls, the table, and the tank. Lily and Ben stop crying. They look at the mess. They look at each other. They look at Rex. He is covered in red paint. He wags his tail and licks their faces. They start to laugh. "Wow, Rex, you decorated the tank too!" Lily says. "Yeah, and the whole kitchen!" Ben says. They hug their dog and their tank. They think they are very lucky. They don't feel helpless anymore. They wait for their parents to come home and see their surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue monster. He lived in a small house in the woods. He had a big door with a lock. The blue monster was very sad because he had no friends. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the woods. She saw the blue monster's house and knocked on the door. The blue monster heard her and was scared. He did not know if he should unlock the door. But, he wanted to have a friend. The blue monster unlocked the door and said hello to Lucy. Lucy was not scared. She smiled and said hello back. They played all day and had so much fun. The blue monster was happy to have a friend. The moral of the story is to not be scared of new things. When we try new things, we can find happiness and make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a small bug. The bug loved to crawl. It would crawl all day and all night. One day, it saw a big, big hill. The bug wanted to crawl up the hill. It thought there might be a friend at the top. As the bug crawled up the hill, it saw a big, big castle. The bug was very happy. It thought, "Maybe there is a queen in the castle who will be my friend." The bug crawled and crawled until it reached the castle door. It was very tired, but it was also very excited. The bug knocked on the door, and a mild queen opened it. She had a kind smile on her face. "Hello, little bug," she said. "I saw you crawl up the big, big hill. I knew you would come here, so I waited for you." The queen picked up the bug, and they became good friends. They played together in the big, big castle and had lots of fun.
