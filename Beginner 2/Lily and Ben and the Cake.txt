Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Cake

Lily and Ben are playing in the living room. They have a toy phone and a toy camera. They like to pretend they are making videos and calls. "Hello, this is Lily and Ben. We are going to show you how to make jelly. Jelly is yummy and wobbly. You need water, a pot, a spoon and a packet of jelly. Watch us!" Lily says into the camera. She presses a button to record. Ben holds the packet of jelly and tries to open it. He pulls hard, but it does not open. He pulls harder, but it still does not open. He gets angry and throws the packet on the floor. "Ben, don't do that! You need to be careful. The jelly will spill everywhere. Let me help you." Lily says. She picks up the packet and tears it open. She pours the jelly powder into the pot. "Thank you, Lily. I'm sorry I was mad. Can I stir the water and the jelly?" Ben asks. "Sure, Ben. You can stir. But be gentle. Don't splash." Lily says. She fills the pot with water and puts it on the stove. She turns on the heat and watches the water boil. Ben stirs the water and the jelly with the spoon. He sees the jelly dissolve and change color. He smells the sweet and fruity aroma. He smiles and says, "Look, Lily. The jelly is ready. Can we eat it now?" "Not yet, Ben. We have to wait. The jelly has to cool down and set. We have to put it in the fridge for a while. Then we can eat it." Lily says. She turns off the heat and pours the jelly into a big bowl. She carries the bowl to the fridge and puts it inside. "Okay, Lily. We will wait. But can we watch our video now?" Ben asks. "Sure, Ben. We can watch our video. Let's see how we did." Lily says. She presses another button on the camera and plays the video. They see themselves talking and making jelly. They laugh and clap. "Wow, Lily. We are good at making videos. And we are good at making jelly. I can't wait to eat it. Can you?" Ben asks. "Me too, Ben. I can't wait to eat it. I bet it will be delicious. Do you want to make another video?" Lily asks. "Yes, Lily. I do. What should we make next?" Ben asks. "How about a cake? Or a pizza? Or a salad?" Lily suggests. "Let's make a cake. A cake with chocolate and sprinkles. That sounds yummy." Ben says. "Okay, Ben. Let's make a cake. But first, let's clean up our mess. We don't want to leave the pot and the spoon dirty. And we don't want to forget our jelly in the fridge." Lily says. "Okay, Lily. Let's clean up. And then let's make a cake. And then let's eat our jelly and our cake. And then let's make more videos. We have so much fun." Ben says. They hug and smile. They are happy. They are friends. They love to play. They love to record. They love to make jelly. And they love to eat it. The end.

One day, a big cat saw a fish in a small pond. The fish was swimming and looked ready to play. The big cat wanted to steal the fish and eat it. So, the big cat hid behind a tree and watched the fish. "Hello, fish! Can I play with you?" asked the big cat. The fish stopped swimming and looked at the big cat. "Sure, big cat! But you can't steal me," said the fish with a smile. The big cat jumped into the pond to play with the fish. But, something unexpected happened! The big cat turned into a small fish. Now, the big cat could not steal the fish or eat it. Instead, they became friends and swam together in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a young dog named Max. Max was a gifted dog who could lead his friends to find fun games to play. One day, Max and his friends went to the park. At the park, they saw a big ball. Max wanted to play with the ball, but he saw a little girl crying. Her name was Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her ball. Max knew he had to help Lily. Max led his friends to give the ball back to Lily. Lily was so happy and thanked Max and his friends. They all played together and had a great time. The moral of the story is to always help others and share.
