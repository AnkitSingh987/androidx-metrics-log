Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Car

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together. They have a big toy box with many toys. Sometimes they share, sometimes they fight. One day, they want to play with the same toy car. Anna says, "It is mine! I saw it first!" Ben says, "No, it is mine! I like it more!" They pull the toy car from each other. They are very angry. Then, they hear a loud noise. It is their dog, Spot. Spot is barking and jumping. He has found a ball in the yard. He wants to play with Anna and Ben. He runs to them with the ball in his mouth. But Spot does not see the toy box. He steps on it. The toy box breaks. All the toys fall out. Some toys are broken. Anna and Ben are very sad. They look at their toys. They look at each other. They say, "We are sorry. We should not fight. We should be nice. We are twins. We are dependable. We help each other." They hug and smile. They pick up their toys. They find the toy car. It is not broken. They say, "We can share. We can take turns. We can play together." They give the toy car to Spot. He is happy. He wags his tail. He drops the ball. He says, "Woof! Woof!" Anna and Ben laugh. They play with Spot and the ball. They have fun. They are twins. They are dependable. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. He liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Tom saw a little dog named Tim playing with a ball. Tom wanted the ball, so he went to Tim and said, "Give me the ball now!" Tim was a kind dog and didn't want to fight. So, he gave the ball to Tom. But Tom was not happy. He saw another toy, a match car, and he wanted that too. Tom went to Tim again and said, "I want the match car too!" Tom was very bossy and always wanted more. Tim did not like how Tom was acting. He said, "No, you can't have the match car. You need to learn to share and be nice." Tom was not used to hearing "no" and he was angry. But he knew Tim was right. So, Tom said sorry and they both played with the ball and the match car together. They became good friends and learned to share and be nice to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. The crab was very tired. He lived in a big sea with many friends. The crab liked to play with his friends all day long. But today, he was too tired to play. One day, a fish came to the crab. The fish wanted to show the crab something. The crab was curious, so he followed the fish. They swam together in the big sea. The fish showed the crab a secret place to rest. The secret place was a soft sand bed under a big rock. The tired crab was very happy. He said "thank you" to the fish. The crab took a long nap on the soft sand bed. When he woke up, he was not tired anymore. The crab and the fish played together all day long.

Lily and Sam went to the park with Mom. They saw a vendor with a big cart. The vendor had many colorful balloons. Lily and Sam wanted a balloon. "Mom, can we have a balloon?" they asked. "Sure, you can choose one balloon each. But first, you have to sing a song for the vendor. He likes to hear children sing," Mom said. Lily and Sam nodded. They liked to sing. They sang a song about the sun and the moon. The vendor smiled and clapped. "That was a lovely song. Thank you, children. Now, pick your balloons," the vendor said. Lily picked a pink balloon. Sam picked a blue balloon. They thanked the vendor and waved goodbye. They ran to the swings with their balloons. They had fun at the park.

Once upon a time there was a furry bear. He was walking in the woods one day and spotted a fly. He said to the fly, "Hello fly. I'd like to measure you." The fly said in surprise, "What do you want to measure?" "I'd like to measure your wings," the bear replied. "I'm curious how long they are." The fly was a bit hesitant, but it eventually allowed the bear to measure its wings. The bear carefully measured the wings and smiled when he finished. "You have very long wings," he said to the fly. The fly thanked the bear before flying away. The bear watched the fly until it was out of sight, admiring its long, furry wings. He was so happy to have been able to measure the fly's wings. The end.
