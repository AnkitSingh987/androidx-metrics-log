Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Gentle Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a gentle girl named Lily. She loved to bake yummy treats for her friends. One sunny day, Lily decided to bake a big cake for her best friend, Tim. Lily put on her apron and started to mix the cake. She used eggs, milk, and sugar. When the cake was ready, she put it in the oven. While it baked, Lily made a pretty cardboard box to put the cake in. When the cake was done, Lily gently put it in the cardboard box. She walked to Tim's house and knocked on the door. Tim opened the door and said, "Hi Lily! What do you have there?" Lily smiled and said, "I baked a cake for you, Tim!" Tim was so happy and excited. He opened the box and saw the cake. But, the cake was not a cake at all! It was a big, funny hat made of cake. Lily and Tim laughed and laughed. They put the hat on Tim's head and took a picture. They knew it was a day they would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim wanted to be rich. He wanted a lot of money. One day, Tim saw a tall tree with a shiny box at the top. He thought there must be money in the box. Tim wanted to reach the box. Tim tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard. He asked his friends for help. They made a big ladder. Tim climbed the ladder to reach the box. He was very happy. His friends were happy too. When Tim opened the box, there was no money inside. Instead, there was a note. The note said, "Being rich is not about having money. Being rich is having good friends." Tim learned that having good friends is more important than money. He was happy with his friends, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Bob and Sam. Bob was naughty and often did things he wasn't supposed to. One day, he promised Sam he wouldn't climb the tree in the garden. But when Sam wasn't looking, Bob split the promise and climbed the tree. Sam was very sad when he caught Bob up the tree. He scolded Bob for being so naughty and promised he wouldn't do it again. But this time, Bob kept his promise and stayed on the ground. Bob and Sam remained friends and Bob never again split his promises. They laughed and played together, happy that Bob was no longer naughty. The End.

Sara liked to play with her box of shiny things. She had many things in her box: a ring, a pin, a coin, a button, and a gem. The gem was her favorite. It was blue and sparkly and made her feel happy. One day, Sara took her box of shiny things to the park. She wanted to show them to her friend Tom. Tom liked to play with his truck. He had a red truck that could make noises and move fast. He was proud of his truck. Sara and Tom sat on the grass and opened their toys. Sara showed Tom her shiny things. Tom looked at them and said, "They are pretty, but they are not useful. You can't do anything with them. My truck is useful. I can drive it and carry things and make noises." Sara felt sad. She liked her shiny things. She thought they were useful. She could wear them and pretend they were magic and make stories with them. She said, "My shiny things are useful. They make me happy. And this gem is very special. It can make wishes come true." Tom did not believe her. He said, "That is silly. A gem can't make wishes come true. It is just a rock. Let me see it." He reached for the gem, but Sara pulled it away. She did not want to share it with Tom. She said, "No, it is mine. You can't have it. You don't understand." Tom got angry. He said, "You are mean. You don't want to share. You are not my friend." He grabbed his truck and ran away. Sara was left alone with her box of shiny things. She felt lonely and sorry. She wished she had not been so rude to Tom. She wished he would come back and play with her. She looked at her gem and thought, "Maybe it can make wishes come true. Maybe I can wish for Tom to be my friend again." She held the gem in her hand and closed her eyes. She said, "I wish Tom would come back and play with me. I wish he would like my shiny things. I wish we would be happy." She opened her eyes and waited. She hoped her wish would work. She looked around and saw Tom coming back. He had a smile on his face. He said, "I'm sorry, Sara. I was mean to you. I should not have said your shiny things are not useful. They are very pretty and fun. Can I see your gem again?" Sara smiled too. She said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I was mean to you too. I should have shared my gem with you. It is very special and sparkly. Here, you can hold it." Tom took the gem and looked at it. He said, "Wow, it is beautiful. It seems to glow. Can I make a wish with it?" Sara said, "Yes, you can. But you have to be careful. You have to wish for something good and kind." Tom nodded. He said, "I know what I want to wish for. I wish we would always be friends and play together." Sara said, "That is a good wish. I wish that too." They hugged and laughed. They played with their toys and had fun. They were happy. The gem seemed to smile too. It had made their wishes come true. It was a useful gem.
