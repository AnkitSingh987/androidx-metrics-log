Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Fish and the Box

Once upon a time, there lived a little fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim in the lake each and every day. One day while swimming, he noticed a box floating in the lake. Bob swam closer and saw that the box contained delicious treats. Without hesitation, he opened it up and ate some of the treats. They were so delicious! Bob was about to eat more when he heard a loud noise. He was startled and looked around to see the source of the sound. Suddenly, a big fish appeared and said: "Hey! That box belongs to me! Put it back in the water right now!" Bob felt scared and quickly put the box back in the water. But, before the big fish swam away, it let out a loud laugh and said "You can have one of those treats. You deserve it!" Bob smiled and thanked the big fish. He ate the treat, which was even more delicious than the first one. Bob thanked the big fish again and then swam away. From then on, Bob had a new friend in the lake - the big fish. Every day, the two of them would swim and play together and even share delicious treats.

Tim and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. They have cars, dolls, blocks and books. They are having fun and making noises. "Vroom, vroom, my car is fast!" Tim says. "My doll is pretty, she has a dress and a hat!" Mia says. They hear their mom calling them from upstairs. She says it is time for a nap. "Come on, kids, let's go upstairs and take a nap. You can bring one toy with you," mom says. Tim and Mia do not want to nap. They want to play more. They look at their toys and feel confused. Which toy should they take upstairs? "I want to take my car, but I also want to take my blocks. They are colorful and fun," Tim says. "I want to take my doll, but I also want to take my book. It has pictures and stories," Mia says. They do not know what to do. They look at each other and have an idea. "Let's swap!" Tim says. "OK!" Mia says. They swap their toys. Tim takes Mia's doll and Mia takes Tim's car. They smile and run upstairs with their toys. Mom sees them and feels confused. Why did they swap their toys? "Are you sure you want to take those toys?" mom asks. "Yes, mom, we are sure. We like to try new things," Tim and Mia say. They hug mom and go to their beds. They cuddle with their toys and fall asleep. They dream of cars and dolls and blocks and books. They are happy.

Tommy likes to play with his computer. He has many games and pictures on it. He can make sounds and colors with it. He thinks his computer is fun and smart. One day, Tommy wants to show his computer to his friend Lily. He invites her to his house after school. He says, "Lily, come and see my computer. It is very cool. You will like it." Lily goes to Tommy's house. She sees his computer on his desk. It is not very big or new. It is a modest computer. But Tommy is proud of it. He says, "Look, this is my computer. I can do many things with it. Do you want to play with me?" Lily nods. She likes to play with Tommy. He is a nice friend. He shows her how to use the mouse and the keyboard. He opens a game with animals. He says, "This is my favorite game. You can choose an animal and make it do things. Like this." He clicks on a cat and makes it meow and jump. Lily smiles. She likes cats. She says, "Can I try?" Tommy says, "Sure. Here, you can use the mouse. I will use the keyboard." He gives her the mouse and moves aside. Lily takes the mouse. She tries to move it on the desk. But she is not used to it. She struggles to make the cursor move on the screen. She says, "This is hard. How do you do it?" Tommy says, "You have to move it slowly and gently. Like this." He shows her how to move the mouse with his hand. Lily tries again. She moves the mouse slowly and gently. She sees the cursor move on the screen. She feels happy. She says, "I did it! I did it!" Tommy says, "Good job, Lily. You are smart. Now you can play with me." He opens another game with cars. He says, "This game is fun too. You can drive a car and race with me. Do you want to try?" Lily says, "Yes, please. I like cars too." They play together and have fun. They are happy with their computer.
