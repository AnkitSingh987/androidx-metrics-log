Category: Beginner
Title: Blue the Car and the Red

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Blue. Blue liked to go fast. One day, Blue saw a big car named Red. Red was very fast too. Blue wanted to race Red to see who was the fastest. They went to the open road to race. Blue and Red were both very excited. They said, "Ready, set, go!" and started to race. Blue was going very fast, but Red was going faster. Blue was getting tired, but he did not want to give up. Blue kept going even though he was tired. He wanted to win the race. Red started to slow down because he was tired too. Blue went faster and faster. In the end, Blue won the race. Blue was very happy, and Red said, "Good job, Blue!" They both had fun racing each other.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Zigzag. Zigzag loved to perform tricks for his friends. One day, he met a dog named Spot. Zigzag said to Spot, "Hi, I am Zigzag. I can perform fun tricks. Want to see?" Spot barked, "Yes, I want to see!" Zigzag jumped and did a zigzag dance in the air. Spot clapped and wagged his tail. They played together all day and became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim was excited to play a new sport. He put on his shoes and ran outside. The sun was shining, and it was a perfect day to play. His friend, Sam, was waiting for him in the park. "Let's play!" said Tim. Sam was not excited. He did not like sports. "I don't want to play," said Sam. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He knew that Sam loved to draw, so he said, "Let's draw and play at the same time!" They started to play, and Sam was drawing too. It was fun, but then the sky turned dark. The wind blew hard, and rain started to fall. They had to stop playing and go inside. Tim was sad that the sport had to end. Inside, they sat by the window and watched the rain. Suddenly, they saw a big rainbow in the sky. Tim and Sam were happy to see it. They went outside again and found a big box under the rainbow. Inside the box, there were new toys and games for them to play with. Tim and Sam were so happy that they forgot about the sport. They played with their new toys and games all day long. The rain had brought them a new and unexpected surprise.

Tom and Sue were twins who liked to play together. But sometimes they did not agree on what to play. One day, they found a big box of candles in the closet. Tom wanted to make a castle with the candles. Sue wanted to make a cake with the candles. "I want to make a castle," Tom said. "It will be fun and strong." "No, I want to make a cake," Sue said. "It will be yummy and pretty." They disagreed and started to pull the candles from each other. The candles fell on the floor and rolled away. Some of them broke. "Stop it, stop it!" their mom said. She heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the mess of candles and was not happy. "You are being wild and naughty. You should not play with candles. They are not toys. They are for light and fire. They can hurt you." She picked up the candles and put them back in the box. She took the box away and gave Tom and Sue some books and crayons instead. "Here, you can read and draw with these. They are safe and fun. And you can share them and agree on what to do." Tom and Sue felt sorry for being wild and naughty. They said sorry to their mom and to each other. They decided to read and draw together. They agreed on what to do and had a good time. They learned that candles are not for play and that disagreeing is not nice.
