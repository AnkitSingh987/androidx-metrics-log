Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play outside. He put on his shoes and went to the yard. Tim saw a big tent in the yard. He was very happy. Tim wanted to run and play in the tent. But when he got close, he saw that the tent was broken. Tim was sad. He wanted to fix the tent, but he didn't know how. Tim's big sister, Amy, saw him looking sad. She asked what was wrong. Tim told her about the broken tent. Amy knew how to fix it. They worked together to fix the tent. Now, Tim could run and play in the tent all day.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there lived a kind farmer. He had a big bag of wheat. He wanted to scatter the wheat in the field. So, he went out to the field with his bag. As he started to scatter the wheat, a little bird came by. The bird said, "Hello, can I have some wheat, please?" The kind farmer smiled and gave the bird some wheat. The bird was very happy and flew away. Later, the little bird came back with more birds. They all asked the farmer for wheat. The farmer was happy to share and scattered more wheat for the birds. The birds thanked the farmer and flew away. The farmer felt good that he could help the birds.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tom. Tom loved to give things to his friends. One day, he saw two friends, Sam and Ben, having a fight. Tom felt sad and wanted to help them. Tom had a silly idea. He found a big, red ball and gave it to Sam and Ben. They stopped their fight and looked at the ball. Tom said, "Let's play with the ball together and be happy." Sam and Ben liked the idea. They played with the ball and soon forgot about their fight. They laughed and had fun together. Tom was happy that his silly idea worked. The moral of the story is that sometimes a silly idea can help solve a problem and bring friends together. It is better to share and be kind than to fight.

Tom and Lily like to play in the park. They see a big tree with red balls on it. They are apples. Tom and Lily want to eat them. They pick some apples and take a bite. But the apples are sour. They make their faces funny. They do not like sour apples. They see a wild dog near the tree. The dog is big and black. It has sharp teeth and loud bark. It looks angry. Tom and Lily are scared. They want to go away. But the dog runs to them. It wants to bite them. Tom and Lily throw the sour apples at the dog. The apples hit the dog on the nose and the head. The dog does not like sour apples either. It makes its face funny too. It stops barking and runs away. Tom and Lily are happy. They are safe. They hug each other. They do not want to play with sour apples or wild dogs anymore. They want to play with nice things. They see a swing and a slide. They run to them and have fun. They like the park.

One day, a big chair and a small chair were talking in the room. The big chair said, "I am the best chair because I am big and strong!" The small chair said, "No, I disagree. I am the best chair because I am small and easy to move!" They could not agree on who was the best chair. A clumsy cat came into the room. The cat saw the big chair and the small chair talking. The cat wanted to sit on a chair. The cat tried to sit on the big chair, but it was too high. The cat tried to sit on the small chair, but it was too low. The cat was sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The big chair and the small chair decided to work together. The big chair let the small chair sit on top of it. Now, the cat could sit on the small chair that was on the big chair. The cat was happy and the chairs were happy too. They learned that being different is good and working together is even better.
