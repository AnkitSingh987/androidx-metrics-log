Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Ball

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They like to run and jump on the grass. They see a big ball near the swings. It is red and shiny and heavy. "Let's play with the ball!" Tom says. "OK!" Lily says. They run to the ball and try to roll it. But it is too heavy for them. They push and pull, but the ball does not move. "Help!" Tom says. "The ball is stuck!" Lily sees a man walking his dog. He is big and strong. She runs to him and says, "Please, can you help us? The ball is stuck on the grass and we can't move it." The man smiles and says, "Sure, I can help you. What is your name?" "I'm Lily and this is Tom. We want to play with the ball, but it is too heavy." The man nods and walks to the ball. He lifts it with his hands and puts it on the swing. He says, "There you go. Now you can play with the ball. But be careful, it is heavy. Don't let it fall on you or the grass." "Thank you!" Tom and Lily say. They clap their hands and cheer. They take turns to swing the ball and catch it. They have fun with the ball. They are happy that the man saved the ball for them. They say, "Thank you, thank you, thank you!" to the man and his dog. The man and his dog wave and say, "You're welcome, you're welcome, you're welcome!" and walk away. Tom and Lily keep playing with the ball until it is time to go home.

One day, a little fish named Fin swam in a gloomy pond. The sky was dark, and the water was cold. Fin felt sad and wanted to find a friend to play with. As Fin swam, he saw a big rock. On the rock, he saw a frog named Fred. Fred was trying to balance on one foot. Fin swam up to Fred and said, "Hi, I am Fin. Can I play with you?" Fred said, "Yes, I am trying to balance. Can you help me?" Fin thought for a moment and then had an idea. He swam around the rock and pushed it with his tail. The rock moved a little, and Fred could balance better. They both laughed and played together, and the pond did not feel so gloomy anymore.

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big box with a loop on it. He was very curious about what was inside the box. Tom tried to open the box, but it was very hard to do. A little girl named Sue saw Tom trying to open the box. "Can I help you?" she asked. Tom said, "Yes, please! I want to see what is inside." Sue and Tom pulled on the loop together. With a big "pop", the box opened! They both looked inside the box and saw a big, colorful ball. But it was not a normal ball. It could talk! The ball said, "Thank you for opening the box! I am a magic ball. I will give you both one wish!" Tom and Sue were very surprised and happy. They both made their wishes and thanked the magic ball.

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted to be better. He wanted to improve. So he asked his mom for help. "Mom, how can I improve?" he asked. "You can start by drinking lots of water every day," she said. "It will help you to be strong and wise." The boy started drinking water every day. He felt better everyday. His mom could see that he was getting smarter. One day, the boy saw a man that was being very mean. The man was being ignorant. "Mom why is he being mean like that," he asked. "It is because he is ignorant," she said. "Ignorance makes people act in bad ways. It's important to always be kind and respectful." The boy took his mom's advice. He made a habit of drinking water and being kind to others. He was improving in many ways. He was no longer ignorant and was growing into a kind and wise boy.
