Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Fish

Tim and Sam liked to swim in the sea. They had fun in the water and played with the waves. They saw many fish and shells and rocks. They were happy. One day, they saw something big and gray in the water. It had a fin and a tail and many teeth. It was a shark. The shark was hungry and wanted to eat. It saw Tim and Sam and swam fast to them. Tim and Sam were scared. They tried to swim away, but the shark was faster. It bit Tim's leg and made him bleed. Tim screamed and cried. Sam wanted to help him, but he was too far. The shark bit Sam's arm and made him bleed too. Sam screamed and cried. The shark ate Tim and Sam and felt full. It swam away and looked for more food. It did not care about Tim and Sam. They were gone. The sea was quiet and red. The fish and shells and rocks did not move. They saw what happened and felt sad. Tim and Sam were amazing. They loved the sea and the sea loved them. But the shark did not love them. The shark was bad. The end.

John and his mom were sitting in their living room. Suddenly, john heard a loud noise coming from his bedroom. His mom said, "John, do you know what that sound is?" John was curious and he wanted to find out. He ran to his bedroom, and when he got to the door he heard a strange bouncing sound. John opened the door and saw his mattress. It was jumping up and down! John squealed with delight. He was so happy and very adventurous. He climbed onto the mattress and started jumping with it. He heard his mom laugh in the doorway. She said, "I knew you'd find something to do!" and she gave John a big hug. John and his mattress became the best of friends!

One day, a little boy named Tim was very anxious. He wanted candy, but his mom said, "No candy before dinner." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They played with cars and had lots of fun. Then, Sam said, "I have candy! Let's eat it!" Tim thought about what his mom said and felt worried. Tim told Sam, "Mom said no candy before dinner." Sam smiled and said, "Okay, let's play more!" They played until dinner time. After dinner, Tim's mom let them have candy. They were both very happy.

One day, a red dog with a long tail went to the park. The dog wanted to play with his ball. He saw a cat with a short tail. The cat was sad because she lost her toy mouse. "Can you help me?" asked the cat. The dog said, "Yes, I will help you find your toy mouse." They looked everywhere. Then, something unexpected happened. They found a big box that said "supply." They opened the box, and inside were many toy mice! The cat was so happy. "Thank you for helping me!" said the cat. The dog and the cat played with the toy mice and became best friends.

Bob and Jill were best friends. Every day, they would go for a walk in the park together. Bob had a pet dog and Jill had a pet cat. One day, they went to the vet because Bob's pet dog was feeling sick. The veterinarian said that Bob's pet dog needed to learn how to rest more and be less active. Bob and Jill tried to learn what they needed to do but it was hard to understand. Later on, Bob and Jill were walking in the park when Bob's pet dog started to feel tight. Jill couldn't help but cry. They went back to the vet and the veterinarian told them that Bob's pet dog was very sick and couldn't be helped. Bob was so sad and he hugged his pet dog tightly. Jill and Bob had to say goodbye to Bob's pet dog. Bob learned a tough lesson - it was very sad when someone close to you passes away.
