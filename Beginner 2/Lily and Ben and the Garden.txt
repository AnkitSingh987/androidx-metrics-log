Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Garden

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were explorers, looking for treasure and animals. They had a big map that they drew with crayons and a compass that they made from a paper plate and a magnet. "Let's go to the bush over there," Ben said, pointing to a green and brown spot on the map. "Maybe we'll find a snake or a lizard." "OK," Lily said, following him. She was not afraid of snakes or lizards. She liked to touch them and feel their scales. They walked to the bush and looked inside. It was dark and prickly. They heard a rustle and a hiss. "Wow, a snake!" Ben said, excited. He reached his hand to grab it. "Ben, no!" Lily said, scared. She pulled him back. "It might bite you!" Ben complained. "But I want to see it! It's so cool!" Lily shook her head. "It's not cool, it's dangerous. We have to leave it alone. Come on, let's find something else." She looked at the map and saw a blue spot. "Look, there's a pond. Maybe we'll find a frog or a fish." "OK," Ben said, still sulking. He liked frogs and fish, but he liked snakes more. They walked to the pond and looked inside. It was clear and shiny. They saw a splash and a croak. "Wow, a frog!" Lily said, excited. She reached her hand to catch it. "Lily, no!" Ben said, scared. He pulled her back. "It might jump on you!" Lily complained. "But I want to see it! It's so cute!" Ben shook his head. "It's not cute, it's slimy. We have to leave it alone. Come on, let's find something else." He looked at the map and saw a yellow spot. "Look, there's a hill. Maybe we'll find a flower or a bird." "OK," Lily said, still pouting. She liked flowers and birds, but she liked frogs more. They walked to the hill and looked around. It was bright and breezy. They saw a flash and a tweet. "Wow, a bird!" Ben said, excited. He reached his hand to touch it. "Ben, no!" Lily said, scared. She pulled him back. "It might fly away!" Ben complained. "But I want to see it! It's so pretty!" Lily shook her head. "It's not pretty, it's free. We have to leave it alone. Come on, let's find something else." She looked at the map and saw a red spot. "Look, there's a house. Maybe we'll find a toy or a book." "OK," Ben said, still grumbling. He liked toys and books, but he liked birds more. They walked to the house and looked inside. It was cozy and colorful. They saw a pile and a smile. "Wow, a toy!" Lily said, excited. She reached her hand to grab it. "Lily, no!" Ben said, scared. He pulled her back. "It might be someone else's!" Lily complained. "But I want to see it! It's so fun!" Ben shook his head. "It's not fun, it's rude. We have to leave it alone. Come on, let's find something else." He looked at the map and saw a black spot. "Look, there's a cave. Maybe we'll find a bat or a spider." "OK," Lily said, still whining. She liked bats and spiders, but she liked toys more. They walked to the cave and looked inside. It was dark and spooky. They heard a growl and a roar. "Wow, a bear!" Ben said, terrified. He reached his hand to run away. "Ben, yes!" Lily said, terrified. She pushed him forward. "We have to get out of here!" They ran as fast as they could, back to the garden. They dropped the map and the compass. They didn't care about treasure or animals anymore. They just wanted to be safe and happy. They hugged each other and breathed hard. They looked at each other and laughed. "Wow, that was a creative adventure!" Lily said, smiling. "Yeah, it was!" Ben said, smiling. "But maybe next time, we'll just play with our own toys and books." They nodded and agreed. They learned their lesson. They were still explorers, but they were also respectful and careful. They had fun, but they also had sense. They were happy, and they didn't complain.
