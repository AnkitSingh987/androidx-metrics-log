Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Razor

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy cars. One day, he saw something shiny under his bed. It was a razor. He didn't know what it was, but it looked impressive. Tim showed the razor to his friend, Sam. "Look what I found!" he said. Sam looked at the razor and said, "Wow, that's impressive! What is it?" Tim didn't know, so they decided to ask Tim's mom. They went to the kitchen and peeked around the corner. Tim's mom was making lunch. Tim said, "Mom, look what we found!" She looked at the razor and said, "Oh no! That's not a toy. It's sharp and can hurt you. Let me take it away." Tim's mom took the razor and put it in a safe place. She told the boys to be careful and always ask an adult if they find something they don't know. Tim and Sam learned a lesson and went back to playing with their toy cars.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful baby. It was learning how to take its first steps and giggle with delight. Every day it would take a few steps and stop for a rest. One day, the baby took more steps than before and it didn't want to stop! It kept going and going until it was out of the house! Everyone laughed as the beautiful baby toddled around the garden. Then, it was time to go inside and the baby had to stop playing. Everyone gave the baby lots of cuddles before it was time for bed. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow car. The car loved to go fast. It had a lot of speed. One day, the car raced with a red car. They both wanted to win. As they raced, the yellow car leaned to one side. It went too fast around a turn. The car was scared. It did not want to fall. The red car was close behind. The yellow car slowed down a little. It leaned the right way and did not fall. The red car passed the yellow car. But the yellow car was happy. It learned to be safe and still have fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a hidden toy in his room. It was old and broken. Tim wanted to restore the toy, so he went to his dad for help. "Dad, can you fix this toy?" he asked. His dad smiled and said, "Sure, Tim. Let's do it together." They worked on the toy with care. Tim's dad showed him how to glue the broken parts. They painted the toy with bright colors. Tim was happy to see his toy looking new again. He knew his will to fix it helped make it better. Tim played with his restored toy all day. He thanked his dad for helping him. "Thank you, Dad. I love my toy," he said. His dad hugged him and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm proud of you for never giving up." Tim felt happy and proud, knowing that he could do anything with a little help and will.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. One day, they see a big bowl of salad on the table. It has green leaves, red tomatoes, yellow cheese and brown nuts. "Let's try some salad," Ben says. He climbs on a chair and reaches for the bowl. Anna follows him. "Be careful, Ben," Anna says. "The bowl is cheap. It can break." But Ben does not listen. He grabs the bowl and pulls it hard. The bowl slips from his hand and falls on the floor. It breaks into many pieces. The salad spills everywhere. "Oh no!" Ben cries. "We broke the bowl. Mom will be angry." Anna is scared too. She sees Mom coming from the living room. She hears Mom's footsteps. "Quick, Ben," Anna says. "Let's hide under the table." They crawl under the table and cover themselves with a cloth. They hope Mom will not see them. But Mom sees the mess on the floor. She sees the broken bowl and the salad. She is not happy. "Who did this?" Mom asks. She looks around the kitchen. She sees two bumps under the table. She lifts the cloth and finds Anna and Ben. "Anna, Ben, what are you doing here?" Mom asks. "Did you break the bowl and spill the salad?" Anna and Ben nod. They are sorry. "We wanted to try some salad," Ben says. "But the bowl was cheap and it broke." Mom sighs. She is not angry anymore. She is glad they are not hurt. "I know you were curious," Mom says. "But you should not touch things that are not yours. The bowl was a gift from Grandma. It was not cheap. It was special." Anna and Ben feel bad. They hug Mom and say they are sorry. "It's okay," Mom says. "I forgive you. But next time, ask me first. And be careful with things that can break." She helps them clean up the mess. She throws away the salad and the broken bowl. She makes them some sandwiches and juice. She tells them a story about Grandma and the bowl. Anna and Ben listen and learn. They promise to be good. They love Mom and Grandma. They love salad too. But they will not touch it again.
