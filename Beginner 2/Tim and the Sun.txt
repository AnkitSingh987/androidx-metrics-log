Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Max. Tim loved to play outside with Max. They would run, jump, and have lots of fun. One sunny day, Tim wanted to play outside. But the sun was very bright. Tim's mom gave him sunglasses to wear. "Here, Tim. Wear these sunglasses. They will help you see better outside," she said. Tim put on the sunglasses and felt very cool. Max wagged his tail, happy to see Tim. As they played, Max started to get hot. He began to complain with a soft whine. Tim was alert and heard Max's whine. He knew Max was too hot. "Let's go inside, Max. We can get a drink and cool down," said Tim. They went inside and drank cold water. Tim and Max were happy and loved spending time together.

One day, a big bear and a small rabbit were playing in the forest. They were best friends. The big bear found a stick. He thought it was a weapon. He showed it to the small rabbit. "Look, I found a weapon!" said the big bear. The small rabbit was scared. He did not like weapons. He wanted to play, not fight. The small rabbit said, "No, I do not want to play with that. It is not nice." The big bear was sad. He did not want to make his friend scared. Then, the big bear realized that the stick was not a weapon. It was just a stick. He said, "I am sorry, small rabbit. I did not want to scare you. Let's play with something else." The small rabbit was happy again. They played together and had fun in the forest.

Lily and Sam went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw many things in the water, like fish and shells and seaweed. One day, they saw something new in the water. It was big and round and clear, with long things hanging from it. It looked like a balloon. Lily and Sam wanted to touch it. "Look, Mom, look, Dad, what is that?" they asked. Mom and Dad looked at the water and saw the thing. They knew what it was. It was a jellyfish. "Be careful, Lily and Sam, that is a jellyfish. It can hurt you if you touch it. It has stingers on its long things. They can make you feel bad," Mom and Dad said. Lily and Sam did not want to feel bad. They did not touch the jellyfish. They stayed away from it. They found other things to play with, like a bucket and a shovel and a starfish. They had fun at the beach. They were happy and comfortable. They did not open the jellyfish. They left it alone. They said bye to the jellyfish when they went home.

One day, a little squirrel was playing in the park. He had a long, fluffy tail. He liked to jump and run. The squirrel saw a big tree with lots of nuts. He wanted to eat them. A big bird saw the squirrel. The bird wanted the nuts too. The squirrel and the bird looked at each other. They both wanted the nuts, but there were not enough for both. The squirrel had an idea. He said, "Let's race to the tree. The one who gets there first can have the nuts." The bird agreed. They got ready to race. The squirrel ran very fast. The bird flew in the sky. They both wanted to win. But then, the squirrel saw a little bug in trouble. The bug was stuck in a hole. The squirrel stopped to help the bug. The bird got to the tree first. He was happy to win. But then he saw the squirrel helping the bug. The bird felt bad. He called the squirrel to come. He said, "Let's share the nuts. You are a good friend." The squirrel and the bird shared the nuts. They were both happy. They played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw her friend, a small dog named Max. Lily asked Max, "Do you want to join me?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I will join you!" So, they walked together, taking big and little steps. As they walked, they met a tired old cat named Missy. Missy said, "I'm too tired to walk." Lily and Max wanted to help Missy feel better. They thought of a plan to make her happy. Lily and Max found a big red ball. They asked Missy, "Would you like to play with us?" Missy looked at the ball and smiled. She was not too tired to play after all! They all played together, and had a lot of fun. In the end, the tired old cat was not tired anymore.
