Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Rock

Sara and Ben are playing in the sand. They like to make shapes and towers with the sand. They use buckets and shovels and sticks. They have fun. But then the sun gets very hot. The sand gets very dry. Sara and Ben feel thirsty. They want to drink some water. They look for their water bottles. But they cannot find them. They left them at home. "Oh no, we have no water!" Sara says. "We are very thirsty. What can we do?" Ben sees a big rock. It is white and shiny. He thinks it is a mineral. He has seen minerals in his book. He says, "Look, Sara, a mineral! Maybe it has water inside. Maybe we can break it and drink the water." Sara is not sure. She says, "Are you sure, Ben? How do you know it has water? Maybe it is just a rock." Ben says, "Let's try. Maybe we are lucky. Come on, help me." He picks up a stick and hits the rock. But the rock is very hard. It does not break. It only makes a loud noise. Sara and Ben hit the rock again and again. But nothing happens. The rock is still white and shiny and dry. Sara and Ben are still thirsty and tired. They feel sad. Then they hear a voice. It is their mom. She has come to pick them up. She has brought water and snacks for them. She says, "Sara, Ben, are you ready to go? I have water and snacks for you. You must be hungry and thirsty." Sara and Ben are happy. They run to their mom. They hug her and say, "Thank you, mom. We are very thirsty. We tried to find water in a mineral, but it did not work. It was just a rock." Their mom smiles and says, "A mineral? That's very interesting. But you know, not all minerals have water. Some are just rocks. You have to be careful. Next time, don't forget your water bottles. Now, let's go home and have some water and snacks. You did a good job playing in the sand."

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little animal named Tim. Tim had a long horn on his head. He loved to poke things with his horn. He poked leaves, he poked trees, and sometimes he even poked his friends. One hot, dry day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a small, round bug. Tim said, "Hi, I am Tim. I like to poke things with my horn. Do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to play. But do not poke me, please. I am too small." So, Tim and Sam played together in the dry forest. They poked leaves and trees, but Tim did not poke Sam. They had a lot of fun. And from that day on, Tim and Sam were the best of friends.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big cat sitting on a wall. The cat was very persistent and would not leave. Max wanted to play, but the cat did not want to play with him. Max felt sad and walked away. Max then saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was stuck in a tree. He tried to jump and get it, but he could not reach it. Max was sad again, but he did not give up. He rubbed his body against the tree, trying to make the ball fall down. He kept trying, but the ball did not move. As Max was trying to get the ball, a little girl named Lily came to watch him. She saw Max was sad and wanted to help. Lily had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to hit the ball. The ball fell down and Max was very happy. Max and Lily played with the ball together, and they became best friends. The persistent cat watched them from the wall, and it finally decided to join them. They all played together and had a great day.
