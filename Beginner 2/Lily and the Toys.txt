Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Toys

Lily was a tall girl who liked to write in her diary every day. She wrote about her friends, her toys, her dreams and her feelings. She kept her diary under her pillow and never showed it to anyone. One day, her little brother Ben found her diary and opened it. He saw the pictures and words that Lily had drawn and written. He thought it was funny and wanted to read more. He did not know that it was Lily's secret and that he should not touch it. Lily came back from playing outside and saw Ben with her diary. She was very angry and sad. She shouted at Ben and grabbed her diary from him. She said, "This is mine! You are not allowed to see it! You are a bad brother!" Ben felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to make Lily mad. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I just wanted to see what you wrote. I did not know it was your secret. Please don't be mad at me." Lily looked at Ben and saw that he was crying. She remembered that he was her brother and that she loved him. She also remembered that she had sometimes played with his toys without asking him. She realized that she had been mean to him and that she should share her things with him. She said, "I'm sorry, too, Ben. I should not have shouted at you. I know you were curious. You can look at my diary if you want, but you have to ask me first. And I will ask you before I play with your toys. OK?" Ben nodded and smiled. He said, "OK, Lily. Thank you for letting me see your diary. You are a good sister." Lily hugged Ben and said, "You are welcome, Ben. You are a good brother." They both learned to respect each other's things and to be kind to each other. They became closer and happier. And they both wrote in their diaries about how they made up and how they loved each other. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a long, flexible snake named Mark. Mark lived in a big green park. He liked to play with his friends, the birds, the bugs, and the frogs. One day, Mark saw a big rock with a hole in it. He thought it would be fun to put his long neck into the hole. So, he put his neck into the hole and it went very deep. Mark was very happy because he found a new game. But then, something unexpected happened. Mark tried to pull his neck out of the hole, but it was stuck! He was scared and called for help. His friends, the birds, the bugs, and the frogs came to help him. They all pulled and pulled, but Mark's neck was still stuck. Just when Mark thought he would be stuck forever, a little mouse came by. The mouse saw Mark and his friends and asked what was wrong. They told the mouse that Mark's neck was stuck in the hole. The mouse said, "Don't worry, I can help!" The mouse went into the hole and found that Mark's neck was tied in a knot. The mouse untied the knot and Mark's neck was free! Mark and his friends were so happy and thanked the little mouse. From that day on, Mark was more careful when playing in the park. And he made a new friend, the little mouse.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a thing in the grass. It was a small, dead bug. He looked at it and said, "Oh no! The bug is not moving." Just then, the bug started to blink its eyes. Tim was very surprised! "I thought you were dead!" he said to the bug. The bug looked at him and blinked again. The bug then said, "I was just sleeping, Tim. Thank you for waking me up." Tim was happy that the bug was not dead. They became good friends and played together in the grass.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a friendly cat named Spot. Spot loved to stretch in the sun. Every day, he would go outside to find a hot spot to lie down and stretch. One day, while Spot was lying in a hot spot, he saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with the ball, so he stretched and jumped to catch it. The ball went very high, and Spot jumped very high too. But, Spot jumped too high and got stuck in a tree. He tried to stretch to get down, but he couldn't. Spot was sad and scared. No one came to help Spot, and he stayed in the tree for a long time. The end.
