Category: Beginner
Title: The Pig and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. He lived in a very old house. The house was so old that it would tremble when the wind blew. The little pig was scared of the trembling house. One sunny day, the pig went to play outside with his friends. They played hide and seek. The little pig found a big tree and hid behind it. The wind started to blow, and the tree began to tremble too. The pig was scared, but he remembered his old house. When the pig went back home, he saw the old house trembling in the wind. But now, he was not scared. He knew that just like the big tree, the old house was strong. The little pig felt safe in his old house, and he was happy to call it home.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in the yard. They had a big ball, a swing, and a sandbox. Sometimes they also played with their dog, Spot, who was very clever and friendly. One day, their mom said, "Tom and Lily, you can play in the yard, but you have to stay in the fence. Do not go out of the fence, or you will be in trouble. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, mom, we understand. We will stay in the fence." They ran to the yard and started to play with their ball. They threw it, kicked it, and rolled it. Spot chased the ball and brought it back to them. They had a lot of fun. But then, the ball bounced too high and went over the fence. It landed on the street, where there were cars and bikes and people. Tom and Lily looked at the ball and then at each other. They wanted to get the ball back, but they also remembered what their mom said. "Mom said we cannot go out of the fence," Lily said. "But the ball is out of the fence," Tom said. "What can we do?" Lily asked. Tom thought for a moment. He had an idea. He was very clever. He said, "I know. We can ask Spot to get the ball for us. He can go out of the fence and come back. He is a good dog. He will listen to us." Lily liked the idea. She said, "Yes, let's ask Spot. He is very clever, too." They called Spot and pointed to the ball. They said, "Spot, can you get the ball for us? Please, Spot, get the ball. We cannot go out of the fence, but you can. You are a good dog." Spot looked at the ball and then at Tom and Lily. He wagged his tail and barked. He understood what they wanted. He ran to the fence and jumped over it. He ran to the ball and picked it up with his mouth. He ran back to the fence and jumped over it again. He brought the ball to Tom and Lily and dropped it at their feet. Tom and Lily were very happy. They hugged Spot and said, "Thank you, Spot. You are a very clever and good dog. We love you, Spot." Spot licked their faces and barked. He was happy, too. He loved Tom and Lily. They continued to play with their ball in the yard, in the fence, where their mom permitted them to play. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. It liked to speed down the road. It went very fast all the time. The car was red and shiny. The car was a little spoiled because it always got what it wanted. One day, the car was speeding down the road. It was having so much fun. But then, it hit a big rock. The car felt a lot of pain. It could not go fast anymore. The car was very sad. A nice blue car came to help the red car. The blue car said, "I can fix your pain." The red car was happy. The blue car fixed the red car. The red car learned not to be so spoiled. It did not speed all the time anymore. The red car and blue car became best friends. They had fun together, but they were careful too.
