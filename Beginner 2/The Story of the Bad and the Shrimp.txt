Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bad and the Shrimp

Once upon a time there was a bad boy. He was naughty and made lots of mistakes. His mom was tired of him doing bad things and wanted to punish him. She said, "You are going to eat shrimp for dinner. You must eat all of it! If you don't, it will be a scary punishment!" The bad boy got scared and ate all the shrimp. He didn't want to get in trouble. He never been so scared before. After he finished the shrimp, his mom was very happy. She said, "Good job eating all your shrimp. That was the right thing to do!" The bad boy was very happy too. He learned not to do bad things again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a small unknown bug on a leaf. She did not know what it was. Lily was curious and wanted to learn more. Lily went to her friend Tom and asked, "Do you know what this bug is?" Tom looked at the bug and said, "No, I don't know. Let's ask Will. He knows a lot about bugs." Lily and Tom went to Will's house. They showed him the bug. Will looked at it and said, "I know this bug! It is a ladybug." Lily and Tom were happy to learn something new. They thanked Will and went back to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small dog. They were friends. They liked to play and teach each other new games. One day, they went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a big tree. They wanted to climb the tree. But the dog felt uncomfortable. He was scared of the big tree. The cat said, "Don't worry, I will help you." As they started to climb, something happened. The tree began to shake! The cat and the dog were scared. But then, they saw a big bird come out of the tree. The bird said, "I am the magic bird of the park. I saw you help your friend. That was very nice." The magic bird gave them a wish. They wished to be brave and strong. The bird made their wish come true. The cat and the dog were now brave and strong. They climbed the tree and played all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small modern town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had big eyes that sparkled when he smiled. One day, it began to rain. Tom put on his raincoat and boots and went outside to play. Tom saw a little bird sitting on a tree. The bird was wet from the rain. "Hi, bird! Are you okay?" Tom asked. The bird looked at Tom with its small eyes and said, "Yes, I am fine. Thank you for asking." Tom and the bird became friends. They played in the rain and jumped in puddles. After a while, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Tom and the bird said goodbye and went back to their homes. They knew they would see each other again soon.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her toy bunny in the living room. She saw her big, red armchair and thought it would be fun to jump on it. Her mom saw her and said, "Lucy, be careful! The floor is wet and you might slip." Lucy didn't listen to her mom and kept playing. She ran towards the armchair, but she didn't see the wet spot on the floor. Just like her mom said, Lucy slipped on the wet floor and fell down. She was very sad and started to cry. Her mom came to help her and said, "I told you to be careful, Lucy. Now you have a boo-boo on your knee." Lucy's mom put a band-aid on her knee and gave her a hug. Lucy learned her lesson and promised to listen to her mom next time.

Jimmy was ready for a fun day of playing. He was wearing his favorite shirt and his hat with the big bow, and he had a smile on his face. Jimmy went to the park and saw a big picture on the wall. He wanted to watch it, but as he got closer, he saw that it was broken. He was really sad and didn't know what to do. His mom came over and saw him standing there. She told him not to worry and that she would fix the picture. She said that he should go and play while she worked on the picture. Jimmy went to play with his friends and when he came back, the picture was ready again. He was so happy, and he watched the picture for a long time. He was so glad he didn't have to miss out on it.
