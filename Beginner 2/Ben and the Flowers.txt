Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Flowers

Ben likes to play in the garden. He likes to run and jump and smell the flowers. He likes the big yellow sunflowers best. They are so bright and happy. One day, Ben sees a new sunflower in the garden. But it is not like the others. It is small and brown and droopy. It looks sad and ugly. Ben does not like it. He thinks it is a bad flower. He runs to the ugly sunflower and kicks it hard. He wants it to go away. He kicks it again and again. He hears a snap. The ugly sunflower falls to the ground. Ben feels happy. He thinks he has done a good thing. He looks around for more ugly flowers to kick. But then he hears a cry. It is his mom. She is in the garden too. She sees the ugly sunflower on the ground. She sees Ben's foot. She is very angry and sad. She runs to Ben and picks him up. She says, "Ben, what have you done? That was not a bad flower. That was a baby sunflower. It was just growing. It needed water and sun and love. You hurt it. You broke it. You made it die." Ben feels scared. He does not understand. He thought he was playing. He thought he was helping. He says, "I'm sorry, mom. I didn't know. I didn't mean to." Mom hugs Ben. She says, "I know, Ben. I know. But you have to be careful. You have to be gentle. You have to be kind. Flowers are living things. They have feelings too. They are not toys. They are not for kicking. They are for loving." Ben nods. He feels sorry. He feels sad. He says, "I love you, mom. I love flowers too. Can we fix the baby sunflower? Can we make it happy again?" Mom smiles. She says, "I love you too, Ben. I don't know if we can fix the baby sunflower. But we can try. We can give it water and sun and love. We can hope it will grow again. And we can plant more sunflowers. And we can watch them together. And we can learn from them. And we can be happy too."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together. One day, they saw a different dog. The dog had a soft waist. Tim and Sam wanted to pet the dog. "Let's run to the dog," said Tim. Sam agreed, and they ran to the dog. The dog looked at them and wagged its tail. Tim and Sam were happy. They petted the dog's waist and it licked their hands. Suddenly, the dog's owner came. He was not happy. "You should not run to dogs you don't know," he said. Tim and Sam felt sad. They knew they made a mistake. The owner took the dog away, and they could not play with it anymore. The bad ending made Tim and Sam learn to be careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little plant. The plant was very persistent. It grew and grew, even when it was hard. One day, the plant saw a big ball. The ball was fun to play with. The plant wanted to play with the ball, but it could not move. A little girl came by and saw the plant. She liked the plant and wanted to help it. She picked up the ball and rolled it to the plant. The plant was happy to play with the ball. They played together all day long. But then, the ball started to vanish. The plant and the girl were sad. They looked and looked, but they could not find the ball. The persistent plant did not give up. It kept growing and growing. One day, it found the ball again. The plant and the girl were so happy, and they played together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a modest bird named Blue. Blue loved to go and fly high in the sky. Every day, Blue would spread his wings and go up, up, up into the sky. One day, as Blue was flying, he saw a big cloud. The cloud was so big, it covered the whole sky. Blue wanted to see what was behind the cloud. So, he decided to go and find out. Blue flew and flew until he reached the cloud. He went through the cloud and saw the most beautiful sky he had ever seen. The sky was full of colors and other happy birds. Blue was so happy, he decided to stay and play with his new friends. From that day on, Blue always knew that even a modest bird like him could find amazing things when he decided to go and explore the sky.
