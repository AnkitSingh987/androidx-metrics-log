Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Ben and the Car

Sam and Ben are best friends. They like to play with cars and balls and blocks. They go to the same school and sit next to each other. They have a lot of fun. But one day, Sam is sick. He has a fever and a cough. He has to stay at home and rest. He cannot go to school or play with Ben. He is sad and bored. He misses Ben a lot. Ben is sad too. He does not have Sam to play with. He does not have anyone to share his snacks or jokes or stories. He misses Sam a lot. The next day, Sam feels a little better. He wants to see Ben. He asks his mom if he can go to school. His mom says no, he has to stay at home for one more day. But she has a surprise for him. She gives him a big envelope. She says it is from Ben. Sam opens the envelope. Inside, he finds a drawing of him and Ben playing with cars and balls and blocks. He also finds a small toy car with a zipper on it. He can unzip the car and put things inside. He thinks it is very cool and playful. He smiles. He sees a note from Ben. It says: "Dear Sam, I miss you. I hope you feel better soon. I made this car for you. You can unzip it and put your snacks or jokes or stories inside. Then you can zip it and keep them safe. I can't wait to play with you again. Your friend, Ben." Sam feels happy. He hugs the drawing and the car. He says thank you to his mom. He says thank you to Ben in his heart. He does not feel so bored or sad anymore. He still misses Ben, but he knows Ben misses him too. He knows they will play together soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a dog named Spot. Spot was a naughty dog who loved to play and run around. One day, Tim and Spot went to the beach to play. At the beach, Tim played with his ball and Spot was very excited. Tim wanted to lead Spot to the water, so they could play together. But Spot was naughty and did not want to go. Spot just wanted to dig holes in the sand. Tim tried to lead Spot to the water again, but Spot ran away. Tim was sad and looked for Spot. He found Spot near a big hole in the sand. Tim laughed and forgave Spot for being naughty. Tim and Spot played at the beach all day, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. His favorite cartoon was about a funny dog who could talk. One day, Tim was playing in his room when he thought, "I wish I could imagine my own cartoon." So he closed his eyes and began to imagine. In his mind, he saw a big, happy cat who could fly. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I imagined a cartoon with a flying cat!" His mom smiled and said, "That sounds fun, Tim! Tell me more about your cartoon." Tim told his mom all about the flying cat and how it made him laugh. From that day on, Tim loved to imagine new cartoons with fun animals. He told his friends about his cartoons and they all laughed and played together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fireman named Tom. Tom was very fast. He liked to help people. One day, Tom was walking down the street, and he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally said, "Hi, fireman Tom! I need help. My cat is up in the tree." Tom looked up and saw the cat. He said, "I will help you, Sally. I will get your cat down fast." Tom climbed the tree very fast. When he got to the top, he saw the cat. The cat was scared. Tom said, "Don't worry, cat. I will help you." But then, Tom had to sneeze. "Achoo!" The sneeze was so big, it made the cat jump. The cat jumped right into Tom's arms. Tom climbed down the tree with the cat. He gave the cat back to Sally. Sally said, "Thank you, fireman Tom! You are very fast and very nice." Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sally. I'm always here to help." And they all lived happily ever after.
