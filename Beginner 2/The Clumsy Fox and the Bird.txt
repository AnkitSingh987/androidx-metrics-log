Category: Beginner
Title: The Clumsy Fox and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy fox. He liked to run and play in the forest. One day, the fox was running and he stumbled on a rock. He fell down and hurt his leg. A little bird saw the fox and flew down to help. The bird asked, "Are you okay, Mr. Fox?" The fox said, "I am sad. I hurt my leg when I stumbled on the rock." The bird wanted to help the fox feel better. The bird told the fox, "I will get help for your leg." The bird flew away and came back with a big leaf. The bird and the fox used the leaf to wrap the fox's leg. Soon, the fox felt better. The fox said, "Thank you, little bird." They became good friends and played together in the forest every day.

One night, as Billy went to sleep, he looked around his bedroom. Everything seemed fine and he closed his eyes. Suddenly, a big spider appeared! Billy screamed and ran away, being very scared. His mom heard the noise and came to his room. She asked, "What is the matter, honey?" Billy pointed to where the spider had appeared. His mom stood there with a brave face and said "It's ok, dear. There is nothing to be afraid of. Spiders can be helpful in our garden, so let’s take it out for a walk." So all together, they went to the garden and put the spider carefully outside. The moral of this story is that being brave is not always easy, but sometimes it is important if you want to do the right thing.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a loop. The loop was big and round. It was a harmless loop. Tim liked to play with the loop. Tim took the loop to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "What is this?" Tim said, "It is a loop. It is fun and safe." Sue and Tim played with the loop all day. Sue and Tim looked at the loop. They examined the loop. The loop was fun to look at. They played with the loop until the sun went down. They were happy with their harmless loop.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Beth. Beth loved to climb. One day, she was walking home from school when she saw a drain. She thought it looked like a fun thing to climb, and was very excited. She said, "I think I'll climb it!" She smiled and started to climb the drain. When she reached the top of the drain, she felt very proud of herself. But she was stuck! She couldn't move any further. "Help!" Beth shouted. Soon, a big man came. He said, "Let me help you, young girl!" He lifted Beth down from the drain. They both said goodbye and Beth went on her way home. The next day, Beth remembered her adventure and thought to herself, "I want to climb something else!"

Mom and Dad took Buddy to the beach one morning. The sun was shining, and the waves were crashing in the blue water. Mom and Dad walked over to the docks and saw a big boat with an anchor. They said they were going to buy it. Buddy asked why they were going to buy it. Mom said it was to help the boat stay in one place. If it was too noisy, people wouldn't be able to rest or have fun. So Mom and Dad bought the anchor to keep the boat in one place. Then they took Buddy back to the beach and had a picnic. It was a great day at the beach, and Buddy was so happy that Mom and Dad bought the anchor!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim saw a big freezer. He was glad because the freezer had ice cream inside. His mom said, "We can get ice cream if you help me with work at home." At home, Tim's mom asked him to help put away toys. Tim did not want to do the work. He wanted to eat ice cream. His mom said, "If you do not help, there will be no ice cream." Tim was sad but knew he had to help his mom. Tim worked hard to put away all the toys. When he was done, his mom was very happy. She said, "Good job, Tim! Now we can eat ice cream from the freezer." Tim was glad he helped his mom and enjoyed his ice cream.
