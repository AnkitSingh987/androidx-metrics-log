Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Penguin and the Park

One day, a penguin named Tim went to the park. Tim was a good penguin. He liked to play with his friends. They would run, jump, and slide. Tim was very happy. One day, a big penguin came to the park. He was not nice. He pushed Tim and his friends. "Stop that!" Tim said. The big penguin laughed. "I will not stop," he said. "If you tell, I will punish you." Tim was scared, but he was also brave. Tim went to his mom and told her what happened. His mom was proud of him. She said, "You did the right thing. Now, we will tell the big penguin's mom." They went to the big penguin's house. The big penguin's mom was a dead tree. Tim was surprised. His mom said, "Even trees can be moms. Let's talk to her." They talked to the tree, and the big penguin stopped being mean. Tim and his friends were happy again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Tara. Tara had short brown hair and blue eyes. She was 3 years old. One day, Tara went to the park to play. She saw a big tree with colourful leaves and a friendly blue bird. She reached out her little finger and the bird flew down and landed on her finger. Tara was so excited, but she was a bit scared too. She said to the bird, “Can you stay?” The bird seemed to understand, so Tara smiled and accepted the bird as her new friend. They played hide and seek around the park, and collected shells on the beach. Tara and her blue bird stayed friends forever. They played together all the time, and Tara always accepted her blue bird with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, Lucy and her friends went to the park to play. They saw a big puddle of water. They knew it was wet, but it looked like so much fun. Lucy's friend, Tom, said, "Let's jump in the puddle!" Lucy thought about it for a moment. She remembered her mom told her to stay clean and not get wet. But the puddle looked so fun, and she wanted to play with her friends. So, she jumped in the puddle with them. They all laughed and splashed around. After a while, they got tired and went back to Lucy's house. When they got there, Lucy saw her mom's face. Her mom looked at them and said, "You're all wet and dirty! That's disgusting!" Lucy felt bad for not listening to her mom. She knew she should have remained clean like her mom said. From that day on, Lucy always listened to her mom and stayed away from wet, disgusting puddles.

Sara and Ben are hungry. They want to eat something good. They go to the kitchen and look for food. They see a big cake on the table. It is brown and has white cream on top. It looks yummy. "Can we have some cake, please?" Sara asks Ben. "I don't know. Mom said we have to wait for dinner. But the cake is so big. Maybe we can have a little bit. Mom won't notice," Ben says. They take a knife and cut a small piece of cake. They put it on a plate and share it. They take a bite and taste the cake. It is sweet and soft and delicious. They smile and eat more. But then they hear a noise. It is Mom. She is coming to the kitchen. She sees the cake and the plate and the knife. She sees Sara and Ben with cream on their faces. She is not happy. "Who ate the cake?" Mom asks. Sara and Ben look at each other. They don't know what to say. They feel bad. They ate the cake the wrong way. They should have waited for dinner. They should have asked Mom first. They say sorry to Mom. Mom is still angry, but she loves them. She tells them to wash their faces and hands. She tells them to sit at the table. She tells them they have to eat their dinner first. Then they can have some more cake. But only a little bit. And only if they say please.
