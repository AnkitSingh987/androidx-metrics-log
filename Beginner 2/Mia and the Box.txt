Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia found an interesting box in her room. The box had a pretty wrap on it. She did not know where it came from. Mia asked her mom, "Mom, does this box belong to me?" Her mom looked at the box and said, "Yes, Mia. It is a gift for you. You can open it now." Mia opened the box and found a cute teddy bear inside. She hugged the teddy bear and said, "Thank you, Mom! I love it!" Mia and her teddy bear became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect things. One day, he found a big, red balloon. It made him feel warm and happy. He wanted to show his friend, Sue. Tim went to Sue's house and said, "Look at my red balloon!" But Sue was sad. She said, "I lost my blue balloon. Can you help me find it?" Tim wanted to help his friend, so they went to look for Sue's blue balloon. They looked all over the park, but they could not find the blue balloon. Then, they saw a big tree with many balloons stuck in it. Tim said, "Let's get your blue balloon from the tree!" They worked together and got Sue's blue balloon down. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Now, they both had balloons and they played together all day.

Once, a seal lived in the sea. The seal was light grey and very friendly. The seal liked to play with the fish and swim in the water. One day, the seal saw a big rock. The seal wanted to gain new friends, so it swam to the rock to see who lived there. On the rock, the seal found a bird. The bird was sad because it could not swim in the water. The seal wanted to help the bird. The seal thought hard, and then it had an idea. The seal told the bird to sit on its back. The bird sat on the seal's back, and they swam together in the water. The bird was happy, and so was the seal. They played in the water all day. The bird learned to love the water, and the seal learned to love the sky. The seal and the bird became best friends. They learned that by helping each other, they could gain more fun and love in their lives. And that is how the seal and the bird learned the value of friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with shapes. He had a box full of them. There were long shapes, round shapes, and pointy shapes. Tim liked to build things with his shapes. One day, Tim decided to build a big tower with his shapes. He used the long shapes for the tower's legs. He used the round shapes for the windows. He put the pointy shapes on the top of the tower. Tim was so proud of his tower. Tim's mom saw the tower and smiled. She pointed at the tower and said, "Good job, Tim!" Tim felt so happy. He played with his tower and shapes all day long. And when it was time for bed, Tim knew he would have more fun with his shapes the next day.

"Fold this paper, Mommy!" said Jill, a 3-year-old, as she pointed at a large sheet of paper. Jill's mom carefully folded the paper in half. "But what are we going to do with it?" asked Jill. "We're going to make something fun!" said her Mom. Jill watched intently as her mom made a few folds, until there was a tiny knot in the middle of the paper. "Now it's your turn! Can you try to fold it?" Jill grabbed the ends of the paper and folded it in half. The paper seemed to dance in her hands, making her feel dizzy. "Mom, I did it!" she cried, her face beaming with pride. "It's better than yours!" Her Mom smiled and gave her a hug. "Roll it, now," said her Mom. Jill grabbed the knot and rolled it. Suddenly, a magical paper creature appeared in her hands, making her gasp in amazement. "It's a surprise!" said Mom. Jill squealed with joy, admiring her masterpiece. She had seen the power of folding and she knew she would never forget it.
