Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cartoon

One day, a boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon on TV. He loved this cartoon because it was funny and made him laugh. Tim's mom bought a cheap toy from the store that looked just like the cartoon character. Tim was so happy to have this toy, and he played with it all day. While playing, Tim saw the toy start to move on its own. It was very unexpected! Tim was scared, but then he saw that the toy was just trying to be friends with him. They played together and had a lot of fun. Later, it was time for Tim to go to bed. He was sad to leave his new toy friend, but his mom said it was time to close his eyes and sleep. When Tim woke up the next morning, he found out that his toy friend was really just a dream. But now, every time he watched his favorite cartoon, he remembered his special dream and smiled.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to build houses with twigs and leaves. They found a big pile of twigs near a tree. They wanted to use them for their house. "Look, Ben, we can make a big house with these twigs!" Lily said. "Yes, let's do it!" Ben said. But then a boy came and saw the twigs. He wanted them too. He was bigger than Lily and Ben. He had a mean face. "Hey, these are my twigs!" he said. "Give them to me!" "No, they are not!" Lily said. "We found them first!" "Too bad! I want them!" the boy said. He grabbed some twigs and ran away. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They chased the boy and tried to get their twigs back. "Stop, thief!" Ben shouted. "Give us our twigs!" Lily shouted. The boy laughed and threw some twigs at them. He thought it was fun to make them mad. But then he tripped on a rock and fell down. He dropped all the twigs. He hurt his knee and cried. Lily and Ben caught up with him. They saw him crying. They felt sorry for him. "Are you okay?" Lily asked. "That was not nice, what you did," Ben said. The boy sniffed and looked at them. He felt ashamed. "I'm sorry," he said. "I was mean. I wanted to play with the twigs, but I didn't ask. Can we be friends?" Lily and Ben thought for a moment. They decided to forgive him. "Okay, we can be friends," Lily said. "But you have to pay for the twigs." "Pay? How?" the boy asked. "You have to help us build our house," Ben said. The boy nodded. He agreed. He helped them pick up the twigs and carry them to their spot. He said he was sorry again. They worked together and made a big house with the twigs and leaves. It was difficult, but fun. They played inside and pretended to be a family. They learned to share and be kind. They became good friends. They had a happy day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a new little wolf named Wally. Wally was not like other wolves. He loved to study and learn new things every day. One day, Wally met a big bear named Benny. "Hi, Benny!" said Wally. "Do you want to study with me?" Benny looked at Wally and smiled. "Yes, Wally! I would love to study with you." So, Wally and Benny sat down under a big tree and started to study. They looked at the leaves, the bugs, and the birds. They learned so many new things together. They became very good friends and studied together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big yard, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He had a small spade that he used to dig in the dirt. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play. He took his spade and started to dig a big hole. He was very alert and watched for any bugs that might crawl out. Tim liked bugs. Tim found some mud and decided to mix it with water. He made a big, gooey mess. Tim laughed and played in the mud. It was so much fun! At the end of the day, Tim was very dirty, but he had a great time playing with his spade and mixing mud.
