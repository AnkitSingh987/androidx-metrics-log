Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Lively Squirrel and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a lively little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to climb trees all day. One day, he heard a sound near a creek. He was very curious. Sam climbed down the tree and ran to the creek. He saw a big rock in the water. Sam thought, "I want to climb that rock!" He didn't know that the rock was slippery. Sam tried to climb the big rock. But it was too slippery! He fell into the creek with a big splash. Sam was wet, but he was okay. He learned to be careful when climbing new things.

One day, a dog named Spot and a cat named Puff were playing in the park. Spot liked to run and jump, while Puff liked to climb and hide. They were best friends. Spot saw Puff in a tree and said, "Puff, come down, let's talk!" Puff climbed down and sat next to Spot. They talked about the fun games they played that day. Spot showed Puff his big paw and Puff showed Spot her soft paw. As they talked, an old, deaf dog named Max walked by. Max couldn't hear them talk, but he saw them playing. He wanted to be friends too. Spot and Puff saw Max and waved their paws to say hello. Max came over and they all played together. They had so much fun. Spot and Puff were happy to have a new friend, and Max was happy to play with them, even if he couldn't hear their talks. At the end of the day, Spot, Puff, and Max went home tired but happy. They knew they would play together again soon.

Tim and Kim like to play with cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make roads with blocks and rugs. They zoom their cars on the roads and make noises. Vroom, vroom, beep, beep! One day, they find a big bucket in the yard. It is blue and shiny. They wonder what is inside. They look in the bucket and see water. The water is clear and cold. They touch the water and splash each other. They laugh and giggle. They have an idea. They bring their cars to the bucket. They put some cars in the water and some cars out of the water. They pretend the bucket is a lake and the cars are boats and trucks. They zoom their cars in and out of the lake. They make waves and bubbles. They have fun with the bucket and the cars. Mom sees them playing with the bucket and the cars. She smiles and says, "You are very creative and clever. You made a nice game with the bucket and the cars. I am proud of you." She gives them a hug and a kiss. Tim and Kim are happy. They say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you. We love the bucket and the cars." They keep playing with the bucket and the cars until it is time for lunch. They wash their hands and put away their toys. They are hungry and ready to eat. They have a nice day.

Once upon a time, there was a painter who lived in a small house. The painter loved to paint pretty pictures. One day, while he was painting, he saw a distant hill. He thought it was very beautiful. The painter wanted to paint the distant hill. So, he took his paint and brushes and walked to the hill. On the way, he met a friendly frog. The frog was very nice and liked the painter's pictures. The painter and the frog became good friends. They played together and the painter painted the frog. The frog was so happy that he gave the painter a kiss. They both laughed and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag to pack. He was going to his grandma's house for a sleepover. He was very excited, but he was also a little sad. He had never been away from his mom and dad before. Tim started to pack his bag. He put his favorite toy, his soft cushion, and his clothes in the bag. But the bag was too full. Tim could not close it. This made Tim even more sad. He did not know what to do. Then, Tim's mom came to help. She showed Tim how to pack the bag better. She put the cushion on the bottom and the clothes on top. Tim tried again and this time, the bag closed! Tim was so happy. He hugged his mom and thanked her. Now, Tim was ready for his sleepover at grandma's house.
