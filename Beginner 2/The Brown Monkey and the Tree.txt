Category: Beginner
Title: The Brown Monkey and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a brown monkey. He lived in a big tree. The brown monkey loved to play all day. One day, the brown monkey found a long string. He wanted to learn how to tie it. So, he asked his friend, a big elephant, to help him. The big elephant showed the brown monkey how to make a knot. The brown monkey tried to tie the string. He pulled and twisted it. At last, he made a knot! The brown monkey was happy. He tied the string to the tree and swung on it all day.

Tom and Mia love to play in the pool. The pool is big and blue and has a slide. Tom and Mia like to slide down and splash into the water. They laugh and shout and have fun. One day, Tom has an idea. He says to Mia, "Let's pretend we are birds. We can soar in the air and see the whole pool." Mia nods and smiles. She likes to pretend. They climb up the slide and wait for their turn. When it is their turn, they hold hands and say, "Ready, set, go!" They slide down fast and feel the wind in their hair. They lift their arms and pretend they are wings. They soar in the air for a moment and then land in the water with a big splash. They come up and giggle. They look at each other and say, "That was fun! We are good birds!" They hug and swim to the edge of the pool. They are happy and wet and warm. The sun is mild and bright. They want to soar again.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish. This little fish loved to play near the reef. The reef had many pretty colors and many friends to play with. One day, a big, harsh fish came to the reef. The big fish was not nice. The big fish wanted the little fish to quit playing near the reef. The little fish was sad, but it did not want to go away. The little fish tried to talk to the big, harsh fish. The little fish wanted to be friends. But the big fish did not want to be friends. The big fish chased the little fish away from the reef. The little fish was very sad. It could not play near the reef anymore. The little fish swam away, looking for a new place to play. But it never found a place as fun as the reef.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. In the barn, there lived many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, a foolish duck wanted to bring a surprise to the barn. He found a big, shiny ball near the pond. The duck thought the animals in the barn would love to play with it. The duck pushed the big ball all the way to the barn. When the animals saw the ball, they were very happy. They all played with the ball and had lots of fun. The foolish duck was happy too, because he could bring joy to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her ball and sleep in the sun. She lived in a small house with a nice family. One day, Kitty saw a little bird outside the window. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Kitty wanted to help the bird, so she went outside to offer her help. She brought the bird some food and water. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty. I will give you a reward for helping me." Kitty was excited to get a reward, but she did not know that the bird was a mean bird. The bird told Kitty to follow her to a big tree. Kitty climbed the tree to get her reward, but when she got to the top, the bird pushed her off. Kitty fell from the tree and got hurt. The mean bird flew away, and Kitty was sad.
