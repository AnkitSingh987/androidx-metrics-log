Category: Beginner
Title: The Bug and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little bug. The bug had wide wings. It lived in a big green tree. The bug liked to fly and play with its friends. One day, the bug saw a bright red flower. It wanted to go there. The bug did not look around. It did not see a big cat. The bug started to rush to the flower. The big cat saw the bug. The cat wanted to catch the bug. The cat jumped and caught the bug. The bug was not fast enough. The cat ate the bug. The bug was gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very honest boy. One day, Tim went to play with his toy boat in the bathtub. The bathtub was big and full of water. While playing, Tim met a small fish in the bathtub. The fish said, "Hi, my name is Fin. I am lost. Can you help me go home?" Tim wanted to help Fin, so he said, "Yes, I will help you, Fin." But Tim did not know how to help Fin. He tried to lift Fin out of the bathtub, but Fin slipped and fell back in. Tim tried again and again, but he could not help Fin. In the end, Fin could not go home, and Tim felt very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play by the river. One day, he found a small boat with a sail. He thought it would be fun to let the boat sail on the river. Tim put the boat in the water and said, "Go, boat, go!" The boat started to sail. The wind blew its sail and it moved fast. Tim ran along the river, watching the boat with a big smile. Tim met his friend, Sue, by the river. Sue saw the boat and said, "Wow, Tim! Your boat is fast!" Tim replied, "Yes, it is! I let it sail and the wind makes it go!" They watched the boat together as it sailed with the wind, feeling happy and free.

One day, a sheep was playing near the water. The sheep liked to get wet, so it went close to the water. It saw a big spray of water coming from a hole. The sheep thought it would be fun to play with the spray. The sheep went closer to the spray and said, "Hello, spray! Can I play with you?" The spray said, "Yes, you can play with me, but be careful. I can make you very wet." The sheep was happy and started to play with the spray. The sheep got very wet while playing. It was having fun, but it was also getting cold. The sheep did not want to stop playing, so it continued to play with the spray. The sheep got colder and colder, but it did not want to leave the spray. Finally, the sheep was too cold and too wet. The sheep got sick because it played with the spray for too long. The other sheep told the wet sheep to be more careful next time. The wet sheep was sad because it did not listen to the spray's warning.

One day, a big giant was walking in the forest. He was very careless and did not look where he was going. He stepped on many flowers and broke many trees. The animals in the forest did not like the giant because he made a big mess. A little girl named Lucy was playing in the forest. She saw the giant and stared at him. The giant stared back at Lucy. Lucy was not scared. She said, "Mr. Giant, why are you so careless? You are hurting the forest and the animals." The giant looked sad and said, "I am sorry, little girl. I did not know I was making a mess. I will be more careful." The giant was not bad, he just did not know any better. From that day on, the giant and Lucy became friends. The giant was more careful, and the forest and its animals were happy.
