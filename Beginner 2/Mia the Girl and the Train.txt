Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Train

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to polish her toy train. Her toy train had a shiny rail that went around her room. Mia's train was red, blue, and yellow. One day, Mia's mom made a spicy soup. Mia sniffed the soup and said, "Mom, this smells spicy!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, it's a spicy soup for dinner. Do you want to try it?" Mia nodded and tried the soup. She liked it a lot! Later, Mia went back to her room to play with her train. She polished the rail until it was very shiny. Then, she put her train on the rail and watched it go around her room. Mia was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Tom. Tom was special because he loved to bathe. Most cats do not like water, but Tom did. He liked to be clean and shiny. Tom had a friend named Sam, who was a dog. One day, Tom and Sam were playing near the couch. Tom said, "Sam, I want to bathe today. Will you help me?" Sam wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Tom. I will help you." So, Tom and Sam went to find water. They found a small pool and Tom jumped in. Sam helped Tom wash by splashing water on him. After Tom was done, they went back to the couch. Tom was happy and clean. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time there was a little bird named Blueberry. She was so happy and grateful to be able to soar above the clouds. Everywhere she looked was beauty. But one day, a mean and nasty storm came to the sky. Blueberry was scared, but she still kept soaring. Suddenly, a big gust of wind came and Blueberry was blown far away from her home. She aimlessly flew for hours, but she could not find her way back. “Help! Who can help me get home?” Blueberry called out in fear. But even the kindest people couldn't help her and she continued to soar aimlessly. The storm grew worse and worse, and soon Blueberry was so tired and scared that she could not keep flying anymore. Blueberry fell from the sky and sunk into a pile of snow. There she was, alone and lost. She was so far from her home and she would never be able to find her way back. The little bird was gone forever.

Once there was a little girl named Clara. She was three years old. Every day, Clara would go for a jog with her dad. One day, Clara woke up early with her dad. Her dad said, "Let's go jog, Clara!" Her dad lead her to the park. Clara was so excited. She started jogging around the park. She felt so fair. But just then, a big dog raced towards Clara and growled. Clara was scared but her dad stepped in front of her. He said, "It's okay, I will lead you away". They started walking away. But the big dog followed. Suddenly, the dog bit her dad's leg. It hurt him really bad. Clara screamed and cried. A fair had quickly turned evil. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big metal box. He was nervous because he did not know what was inside. He asked his friend, Sue, "Can you help me solve how to open the box?" Sue said, "Yes, let's try!" They tried to push and pull, but the box would not open. Then, they found a small key on the ground. They put the key in the box and turned it. The box opened! Inside the box, they found a small, talking bird. The bird said, "Hello, I am a magic bird! I can grant you one wish." Tim and Sue were very surprised. They thought hard and wished for a big, fun playground for all their friends to play on. The magic bird made their wish come true, and they played all day long.

One day, a mom and her little boy went to the mall. They walked around and looked at the toys. The little boy saw a big, red ball. He wanted it very much. "Mom, can I have the ball?" he asked. Mom said, "Yes, you can have it." The little boy played with the ball all day. But soon, he started to complain. "Mom, the ball is weak. It doesn't bounce high." Mom looked at the ball and saw that it was not a strong ball. She said, "Let's go back to the mall and find a better one." At the mall, they looked for a new ball. Suddenly, the weak ball started to bounce very high! It went up and up, all the way to the roof. The little boy and mom were very surprised. They laughed and played with the ball. It was not weak after all. They had a fun day at the mall with their new, bouncy ball.
