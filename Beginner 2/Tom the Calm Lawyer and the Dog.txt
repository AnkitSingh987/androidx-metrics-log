Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Calm Lawyer and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a calm lawyer named Tom. He had a big bag that could include many things. One day, he was walking to work when he saw a little dog. The little dog was lost and scared. Tom wanted to help the dog, so he put the dog in his big bag. Tom and the little dog walked to the big building where Tom worked. Tom knew he needed to find the dog's home. When they got to the big building, Tom saw a little girl crying. She was looking for her lost dog. Tom thought maybe this was the dog's home. Tom opened his big bag and the little dog jumped out. The little girl saw the dog and was very happy. She hugged the dog and thanked Tom. The calm lawyer felt good for helping the little girl and her dog. But then, something unexpected happened. The little girl said, "I am not just a little girl, I am a fairy!" She waved her magic wand and turned Tom into a happy dog. Now, Tom and the little dog could play together all day long.

One day, a little child named Tim went for a walk. He liked to wander around the big park near his house. Tim saw many trees and birds. He was very happy. As Tim walked, he saw a distant hill. He wanted to go there. But his mom had told him not to go too far. Tim thought, "I will go just a little bit and then come back." So he walked to the distant hill. When Tim got to the hill, he met another child named Sam. Sam said, "I am lost. Can you help me find my mom?" Tim wanted to help, but he was scared. He knew he was far from home. Tim said, "Let's go back together. We will find our moms." Tim and Sam walked back. They were scared, but they helped each other. Soon, they found their moms. The moms were happy to see them. Tim and Sam learned not to wander too far from home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to drink juice. One day, she saw a dull, old shelf in her room. She thought it would be a good place to keep her juice. Amy called her friend, Tim. "Hi Tim, I found a shelf for my juice. Can you help me put the juice on the shelf?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you, Amy." So, Tim came to Amy's house and they put the juice on the shelf together. Every day, Amy and Tim would drink juice from the shelf. They were happy that they had a special place for their juice. And they always remembered to say "please" and "thank you" when they shared the juice with each other.

One day, a boy named Tim found a shiny coin on the floor. He was very happy. He showed the coin to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a coin!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But we should try to find who lost it." Tim was worried. He didn't want to leave the coin. He wanted to keep it. He and his mom walked around the park, asking people if they lost a coin. No one said yes. Finally, they met an old man. He looked sad. Tim asked him, "Did you lose a coin?" The old man's face lit up. "Yes, I did!" he said. Tim gave the coin to the old man. The old man was very happy and thanked Tim. Tim felt good for helping someone.

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. His name was Dino. Dino liked to jump high. He jumped high every day. One day, Dino wanted to celebrate his birthday. He invited all his friends to his party. They played games and ate cake. Dino was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A small bird flew down and took Dino's cake. Dino and his friends were surprised. They all jumped high to get the cake back. The bird saw them and gave the cake back. Now, Dino and the bird were friends too. They all celebrated together.
