Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Park

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big, hairy dog. The dog is friendly. He wags his tail and licks their faces. "Can we play with the dog?" Lily asks Ben. "Sure, why not?" Ben says. "He looks nice. Maybe he is lonely." They run and throw a ball for the dog. The dog chases the ball and brings it back. He barks and jumps. Lily and Ben laugh and hug the dog. A man comes to the park. He is the dog's owner. He calls the dog's name. The dog runs to the man. "Hello, I'm sorry if my dog bothered you," the man says. "He loves to play with kids. His name is Max." "He didn't bother us," Lily says. "He is fun. We like him. What is your name?" "I'm Tom," the man says. "Do you want to play with Max some more? He likes you too." "Yes, please!" Ben says. "Can we include Max in our games?" "Of course," Tom says. "He would love that. What games do you play?" "We play tag, hide and seek, and wink," Lily says. "Wink? How do you play wink?" Tom asks. Lily explains the rules. She says one person is the winker. The winker closes one eye and looks at someone. That person has to run to the winker and hug them. The others have to catch them. The winker and the hugger are the winners. "That sounds fun," Tom says. "Can I be the winker?" "Sure," Lily says. "But you have to wink at Max too. He is part of our team." "OK," Tom says. He winks at Max. Max wags his tail and runs to Tom. He jumps on him and licks his face. Lily and Ben run after them. They all laugh and hug. They have a good time. They are happy.

One day, a big attractive bird saw a small insect. The insect was in trouble. It was stuck in a spider web. The bird wanted to save the insect. "Help me, please!" said the insect to the bird. The bird tried to save the insect. It pulled and pulled, but the web was too strong. Then something unexpected happened. The spider came out to see what was going on. The spider was very big and scary. It wanted to eat the bird and the insect. The bird was too slow to fly away. The spider ate both the bird and the insect. The end.

Once there was a girl called Mia. She was so excited to go outside and play on the swing. She ran to the garden, found the swing and hopped in. Then, she pushed and pushed. The higher and higher she went. Suddenly, it turned very dark and Mia started to get scared. She heard a voice. "Hello, who’s there?" she said. "My name is Sam," the voice said. "Can you help me come down from the swing? I'm very scared in the dark," Mia said. "Of course I can," Sam said. "But use my hand to push off the swing. That will help you come down." So Mia did just that. She grabbed Sam's hand and pushed off the swing. Before she knew it, her feet were touching the ground. Mia thanked Sam and they both waved goodbye. She was really happy that her new friend had helped her. She got back home safe and sound, and the next day they played together again.

With trembling hands, Sam stepped into the room. She saw a pink chair in the corner with a pile of luggage on top of it. Sam hesitated, before finally kneeling in front of the chair. Suddenly, a voice filled the room. It was sweet and kind, and it said, "Hello Sam". She looked up, and there was her grandpa! Sam had been so nervous to meet him - it had been years! But then, her grandpa gently patted the pink seat beside him and said, "Come, sit with me." Sam felt her nerves settle. She remembered how grandpa used to read stories to her when she was little. He had always made her feel special, and for a moment, she felt like that little girl again. Sam got up, hugged her grandpa, and then happily sat down in the pink seat beside him. She opened up one of the pieces of luggage, and to her delight, it was stuffed full of books! Sam knew then that the day would be special. She had found the thing she loved most - time spent with grandpa.
