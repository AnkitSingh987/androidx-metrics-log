Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a regular boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom bought him a new vest. It was a very nice vest, and Tim liked it a lot. Tim's friend, Sam, saw the vest and wanted one too. Tim told Sam, "I recommend you ask your mom to get you a vest like mine." Sam thought that was a good idea and went to ask his mom. But Sam's mom said no. She did not want to buy a vest for Sam. Sam was very sad and cried. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog. His name was Max. Max loved to sleep. Every day, Max would sleep under the big tree in the park. One day, a police came to the park. The police saw Max sleeping under the tree. The police said, "Hi Max! Are you having a good sleep?" Max woke up and said, "Yes, I love to sleep under the tree." The police smiled and said, "Max, you are very popular. Everyone in the town loves you." Max wagged his tail and said, "I am happy to be popular and make everyone smile." Then Max went back to sleep under the tree, and the police went back to work. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Bob. Bob was a careful fish who always swam close to his home. One day, Bob saw a big, pretty rock. He wanted to swim to the rock, but he was scared. Bob's friend, Tim the turtle, saw him and swam over. Tim wanted to help Bob, so he said, "You can do it, Bob! I will encourage you!" Bob felt happy and brave with Tim's help. Together, they swam to the big, pretty rock. Bob was very careful and stayed close to Tim. When they reached the rock, they were so proud. They played all day and swam back home.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a loyal dog named Max. Max was always with his friend, Sam, who had a supply shop. Sam gave Max food and water every day. Max loved Sam very much. One day, a man came to the shop and asked, "Do you have a license to sell food?" Sam did not understand and said, "I don't know." The man was angry and said, "You need a license to sell food. If you don't have one, you cannot sell food anymore." Sam was sad and did not know what to do. The next day, the man came back and took all the food away. Sam had no food to give Max. Max was hungry and sad too. They both did not know what to do. In the end, Sam had to close his shop because he did not have a license. Max and Sam were very sad. They learned that it is important to know the rules and follow them. If they had known about the license, they could have kept their shop open and been happy.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lucy went to the beach with her mom. She saw the big blue sea and the waves. Lucy loved the waves, they made her feel happy. She wanted to play with the waves, so she asked her mom, "Can I play in the waves, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but stay close to me." Lucy ran to the waves and splashed in the water. She felt dizzy when the waves went up and down. Lucy laughed and played, making a big splash. Then, Lucy saw a little boy named Tom. Tom was playing in the waves too. Lucy said, "Hi! Do you want to play with me?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes! Let's play together!" So Lucy and Tom played in the waves, laughing and having fun. They had a great day at the beach with their new friend.

Ben and Lily like to play with mud. They make mud pies and mud cakes and mud houses. They pretend they are bakers and builders. One day, they have a big idea. "Let's bake our mud pies in the sun!" Ben says. "Then they will be hard like bricks!" "OK!" Lily says. "That sounds fun!" They put their mud pies on a big rock and wait for the sun to bake them. They go and play with their ball and their dolls. They forget about their mud pies. After a while, they remember their mud pies and run back to the rock. They see their mud pies are dry and hard. They try to pick them up, but they are too hot. "Ouch!" Ben says. "They are too hot to touch!" "Let's wait for them to cool down," Lily says. "Then we can eat them!" They sit on the grass and wait for their mud pies to cool down. They talk about what they will do next. They are happy and proud of their mud pies. They think they are the best bakers and builders in the world.
