Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a doll that she loved very much. Her doll had a big red dress and pretty yellow hair. Lily took her doll with her everywhere she went. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and dad. She played with her doll near a big tree. Lily saw a bird soar high in the sky. She wanted her doll to soar like the bird. So, she threw her doll up in the air. But, her doll got stuck in the tree. Lily was very sorry and sad. Lily's dad got a long stick and tried to get the doll down. After a few tries, the doll fell down from the tree. Lily was very happy to have her doll back. She hugged her doll tight and promised never to throw her again. From that day on, Lily learned to be careful with her things and treat them with love.

One day, a big fish and a small fish were swimming in the ocean. The big fish was very jealous of the small fish because the small fish could swim fast and play with other fish. The big fish said, "I wish I could swim fast like you." The small fish told the big fish, "You can relax and play with us too! You don't have to be jealous." So the big fish tried to relax and play with the small fish and their friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish and the small fish found a magic shell. The magic shell said, "I will give you one wish." The big fish wished to swim fast like the small fish. The magic shell made the big fish swim fast. Now, the big fish was not jealous anymore. They all played together in the ocean and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Lily. Lily loved to pretend she was a luxury queen. She would wear a shiny crown and sit on a big, soft pillow. One day, Lily met a little boy named Timmy. Timmy saw Lily in her shiny crown and said, "Wow, you look like a queen!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I am a luxury queen. I love to pretend!" Timmy wanted to pretend too. So, he put on a paper crown and became the king. Together, they had fun ruling their pretend kingdom. They made new friends and played all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks. They built a big house with a roof and a door. They were very proud of their house. But then Sam came. Sam was older and mean. He saw the house and said, "That's a silly house. It's too small and boring. I can make a better house." He took some blocks from Lily and Ben and made a bigger house next to theirs. He put a lot of ice on his house to make it shiny and cold. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They said, "Stop it, Sam! You are not nice. You took our blocks and made us feel bad. Go away!" Sam laughed and said, "No, I won't. My house is better than yours. Your house will melt in the sun. Watch!" He pointed at Lily and Ben's house. The sun was very hot and bright. It made the blocks warm and soft. Lily and Ben's house started to melt. The roof and the door fell down. The blocks became mushy and sticky. Lily and Ben cried. They said, "Our house is gone! You ruined it, Sam! You are very mean!" Sam smiled and said, "See? I told you. My house is better. It won't melt. It's strong and cool." But then he heard a loud crack. He turned around and saw his house. The ice on his house was melting too. It made the blocks wet and slippery. His house started to fall apart. The walls and the windows collapsed. The blocks became soggy and dirty. Sam was shocked and scared. He said, "Oh no! My house is gone too! How did this happen?" Lily and Ben stopped crying and looked at Sam. They said, "The sun is very hot. It melts ice and blocks. Your house was not better. It was worse. You were not smart. You were silly." Sam felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "You are right. I was silly and mean. I'm sorry. Can we be friends?" Lily and Ben thought for a moment. They said, "Maybe. But you have to be nice and share. And help us make a new house. A house that won't melt." Sam nodded and said, "OK. I will. I'm sorry." They hugged and said, "We are friends." They took the blocks and the ice and made a new house together. A house that was big and fun. A house that had a roof and a door and windows and ice cream. A house that they all liked. A house that was happy.
