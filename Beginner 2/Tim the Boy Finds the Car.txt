Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some cheap oil for their car. On the way to the store, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. "Hi Sam! Can I play with you?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes! Let's play catch!" So Tim and Sam played catch with the ball. They had a lot of fun. After a while, Tim's mom came back with the cheap oil. She said, "Tim, it's time to go home now." Tim said goodbye to Sam and went home with his mom. They put the oil in the car, and it worked very well. Tim was happy he got to play with his friend and help his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big bag of marbles. Tim loved to play with his marbles all day long. One day, he found a very dark marble. It was so dark, it looked like the night sky. Tim showed the dark marble to his friend, Sue. "Look at my dark marble," he said. Sue looked at it and said, "Wow, it's so dark! Let's play with it." They played with the dark marble and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The dark marble started to move! It jumped out of Tim's hand and tried to escape. Tim and Sue were so surprised. They chased the dark marble as it rolled away. The dark marble led them to a secret door. Tim and Sue opened the door and found a magical world full of more marbles to play with. They played in the magical world and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit. The rabbit liked to jog every day. He would jog in the sun and in the rain. He was very happy when he jogged. One day, the rabbit met a big, powerful dog. The dog wanted to play with the rabbit. The rabbit was scared. He did not want to play with the big dog. The rabbit had an idea. He asked the powerful dog to jog with him. The dog said yes. They jogged together and became friends. The rabbit was not scared anymore. They played and jogged every day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a large cliff. Many animals lived near the cliff. One day, a little bird wanted to examine the cliff. The bird flew close to the cliff and saw a big hole. The bird saw a squirrel and said, "Hey, squirrel! Look at this big hole in the cliff!" The squirrel came and looked at the hole. "Wow!" said the squirrel, "I want to go inside and see what's there." The bird and the squirrel went inside the hole. It was dark and they could not see well. They walked and walked. They did not know that the hole was very deep. Suddenly, they fell down and could not get back up. The bird and the squirrel were sad because they were stuck in the hole. They cried for help, but no one could hear them. In the end, they could not get out of the hole and had a bad ending. The moral of the story is to be careful when you explore new places. Always tell someone where you are going and do not go alone.

One day, a boy named Tim found an ashtray. It was big and deep. He did not know what it was. He asked his mom, "Mom, what is this?" His mom said, "It is an ashtray, Tim. It is not a toy. Please put it back." Later, Tim saw his dad write a note. Tim wanted to write too. He looked for a place to write. He saw the deep ashtray. He thought it was a good place to write. He took a pen and wrote in the ashtray. It was hard to write in the deep ashtray. His hand got dirty. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, I told you the ashtray is not a toy." Tim learned his lesson. He never played with the ashtray again. He found a paper to write on and was happy.
