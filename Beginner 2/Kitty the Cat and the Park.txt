Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Park

One day, a little cat named Kitty felt miserable. She didn't know what to do. So, she decided to wander around the big park near her home. As she walked, she saw a big, happy dog named Buddy. "Hi, Kitty!" said Buddy. "Why do you look so sad?" Kitty told Buddy that she felt miserable and didn't know what to do. Buddy had an opinion. He thought that playing in the park would make Kitty feel better. "Come play with me, Kitty!" said Buddy. "We can chase each other and have fun!" Kitty played with Buddy in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Kitty felt much better. She was not miserable anymore. She was happy to have a good friend like Buddy.

Once upon a time there was a mommy. Her name was Mommy Sue. She loved to raise trains. Everywhere she went she would see a train and she would raise it into the sky! Some of her trains even became famous because of her work. One day, Mommy Sue had a special train she was raising. She worked extra hard on it and was so proud! When she finished, she showed it to a 3-year old child. The little child smiled and said, "Oh Mommy Sue, that is the most beautiful train I've ever seen!" Mommy Sue beamed. She was so happy that her train was being admired! She hugged the child and said, "Thank you sweetie, I love raising trains!" The little child hugged her back and thanked Mommy Sue. From that day on, Mommy Sue's trains kept getting more and more famous. She kept raising them and loving every minute of it!

One day, a small boy named Tim found an ugly metal toy. It was very heavy. Tim tried to weigh it in his hands. He thought it was the heaviest toy he had ever held. Tim showed the toy to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found this ugly metal toy. It's so heavy!" he said. His mom looked at the toy and smiled. "Yes, it's very heavy, Tim. It must be made of a strong metal," she said. Tim played with the ugly metal toy all day. He made it jump and dance. Even though it was ugly and heavy, Tim loved his new toy. He learned that even ugly things can be fun and special.

Once upon a time, there was a woman. She lived in a little house near the woods. The woman had a big dog. They were good friends. One day, the dog played in the mud. He got very dirty. The woman said, "Oh no! You are a terrible mess!" She knew she had to wash the dog. The woman got a big bucket of water. She washed the dog with a soft cloth. The dog liked the water. Soon, the dog was clean again. The woman was happy. The dog was happy too. They played in the sun and had a great day.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big, organized line of kids waiting to play on the slide. Lily wanted to play too, so she went to the end of the line. She stood close to the boy in front of her. Lily looked at his waist and saw he had a cool belt. "Hi, I like your belt!" said Lily. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you! My mom gave it to me. It helps keep my pants up." Lily thought that was a good idea. She wanted a belt too. When it was Lily's turn, she climbed up the slide and slid down fast. She had so much fun! After playing, Lily went home and asked her mom for a belt. Her mom found one and put it around Lily's waist. Now, Lily felt organized and happy, just like the boy at the park.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big jet in the sky. It makes a loud noise. "Wow, look at the jet!" Tom says. "It is so fast and shiny!" "I want to fly in a jet one day," Sam says. "It must be fun!" They run after the jet. They want to see where it goes. They do not see the rocks on the ground. They stumble and fall. "Ouch!" Tom cries. He hurts his knee. It bleeds a lot. "Are you okay?" Sam asks. He helps Tom get up. "No, I am not okay," Tom says. He is angry and sad. "You made me fall. You are a bad friend!" He pushes Sam away. Sam falls too. He hits his head on a rock. He does not move. "Sam? Sam?" Tom calls. He is scared. He tries to wake Sam up. But Sam does not wake up. He is very still and cold. Tom does not know what to do. He cries and cries. He wishes he did not run after the jet. He wishes he did not push Sam. He wishes Sam was okay. But it is too late. Sam is gone. Tom is alone. He has no friend. He has no jet. He has only a dry mouth and a wet face. He is very sorry. But sorry does not help. Sorry does not change anything. Sorry is a small word for a big mistake.
