Category: Beginner
Title: Paws and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a small, dirty cat named Paws. Paws lived in a big house with a kind girl named Lily. Every day, Paws would go outside and play in the dirt. Paws loved to play in the dirt, but he would get very dirty. One day, Paws saw the pretty sunrise. The sky was pink and orange. Paws wanted to bring the sunrise to Lily. So, Paws tried to catch the sunrise with his paws. But he could not catch it. Paws felt sad. Lily saw Paws and knew he was trying to bring her the sunrise. She hugged the dirty cat and said, "Thank you, Paws. You don't need to catch the sunrise for me. I love you, even if you are dirty." Paws was happy, and they watched the sunrise together.

One day, a little girl named Lisa was playing in her room. She was running fast with her toy car. Her mom was in the next room doing laundry. Lisa was having so much fun with her car, but she wanted her mom to play with her too. Lisa went to her mom and said, "Mom, can you play with me?" Her mom looked at the laundry and said, "I need to finish this first, then we can play together." Lisa went back to her room and played some more. But she was not happy. She wanted her mom to play with her now. So, Lisa yelled, "Mom, please come play with me!" Her mom heard her and said, "Okay, let's play for a little while." Lisa and her mom played with the toy car together. They made it go fast and had lots of fun. The laundry was still there, but they knew they could finish it later. They were happy to spend time together, playing and laughing.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with colorful toys. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. Tom runs to the slide first. He climbs up the ladder. He says, "I am the king of the slide. No one can go on the slide but me." He slides down and laughs. Lily feels sad. She wants to go on the slide too. She says, "Tom, please share the slide. We are friends. Friends share." But Tom does not listen. He climbs up the ladder again. He says, "No, this is my slide. Go away." Lily starts to cry. Her mom sees her and comes to her. She says, "Lily, what is wrong?" Lily tells her mom what Tom did. Her mom says, "That is not nice. Tom should not be selfish. He should let you have a turn." Her mom goes to Tom's mom and tells her what happened. Tom's mom is not happy. She says, "Tom, come here. Why are you being mean to Lily? She is your friend. You should share the slide with her. How would you feel if she did the same to you?" Tom feels ashamed. He knows he was wrong. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I was selfish. You can go on the slide now. I will wait for you." He goes down the slide and gives it to Lily. Lily smiles. She says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I will share the slide with you too." She climbs up the ladder and slides down. She has fun. Tom and Lily take turns on the slide. They laugh and play together. Their moms are proud of them. They applaud them and say, "Well done, Tom and Lily. You learned to share and be kind. That is what friends do."

One day, Jim noticed a steak hovering in the sky. He had never seen anything like it before. He couldn't believe it! It was incredible. Jim called out to his friend Pete. He shouted, "Pete, look! There's a steak in the sky!" Pete replied, "It looks amazing! Can you share it with me?" Jim smiled. He said, "Yes. I can share it with you. We'll both enjoy it together!" The two friends jumped for joy. They couldn't believe how incredible the steak was. They knew it was a special moment and they would never forget it. They each took a bite of the steak and it tasted delicious. Jim and Pete smiled. They had noticed an incredible steak in the sky, and they were glad they had shared it together.
