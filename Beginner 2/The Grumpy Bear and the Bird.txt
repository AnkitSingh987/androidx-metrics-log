Category: Beginner
Title: The Grumpy Bear and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy bear. He lived in a small chamber in the woods. The bear was always grumpy because he did not have any friends. One day, a little bird came to the bear's chamber. The bird wanted to make friends with the grumpy bear. The bear was not sure, but he let the bird stay. As time went by, the bird and the bear became good friends. They played together and had fun. The bird made the bear happy, and he was not grumpy anymore. They lived happily in the small chamber in the woods.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named John and Jack. They lived together in a big igloo. Every day, Jack and John would play together and have lots of fun. One day, John and Jack started to fight. John said he was smarter and wiser than Jack. Jack was mad and said he was smarter too! The fight went on and on. Neither John nor Jack wanted to be wrong. Finally, a wise old penguin stopped by the igloo. He said: "Friends should never fight. Play together and always be nice." John and Jack looked at each other. They smiled and said sorry. From then on, Jack and John were the best of friends. They would always be kind and never fight.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom and his baby brother, Tim. Tom loved his baby brother very much. One day, Tom and Tim were playing in the park. Tim was too small to play on the big slide, so Tom helped him play on the little slide. Tim felt happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Suddenly, a big dog came running into the park. The dog was very big and it scared little Tim. He felt helpless and started to cry. Tom saw how scared his baby brother was and wanted to help him. So, Tom found a big stick and waved it at the dog to make it go away. The dog ran away, and Tim stopped crying. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog came back with a ball in its mouth. It turned out the dog just wanted to play! Tom and Tim laughed and played with the dog. They threw the ball and the dog would bring it back. At the end of the day, Tom and Tim said goodbye to the dog and went home. They had a fun day at the park, and Tim said, "Thank you, Tom, for helping me when I felt helpless."

Tom and Lily liked to play store. They had a big box and some toys and books. They put the box in the yard and made a sign that said "Tom and Lily's Store". They hoped some people would come and buy their things. But no one came. The yard was empty. Tom and Lily felt sad. They wanted to sell their things and make some money. They wondered why no one wanted to buy their things. Then they saw a boy walking by. He had a bike and a helmet. He looked at their sign and stopped. He said, "Hi, I'm Ben. What are you selling?" Tom and Lily smiled. They showed him their toys and books. They said, "We have cars and dolls and puzzles and stories. Do you want to buy something?" Ben looked at their things. He liked the puzzles and the stories. He said, "How much are they?" Tom and Lily thought for a moment. They did not know how to count very well. They said, "One dollar for each thing." Ben nodded. He had some money in his pocket. He gave them two dollars and took a puzzle and a story. He said, "Thank you. These are nice things. I like your store." Tom and Lily thanked him. They were happy. They had sold some things and made some money. They hoped more people would come and buy their things. They said, "Bye, Ben. Come again." Ben waved and rode away on his bike. He was happy too. He had found some new things to play with. He liked Tom and Lily's store. He thought they were nice. He said, "Bye, Tom and Lily. I will come again."
