Category: Beginner
Title: The Pumpkin and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a pumpkin. He was fit and strong like a young tree. He lived in the middle of a field and couldn’t decide what to do. He wasn’t sure if he should stay as a pumpkin forever, or quit and try something new. So he decided to quit being a pumpkin, and instead try being a strong tree. He put out his roots and leaves and stood tall in the field. He enjoyed being a tree far more than he ever had being a pumpkin. But then one day a big storm came. The wind blew very hard and the rain was heavy. Much to his surprise the pumpkin stayed fit while the trees around him were knocked down. The moral of the story is that while it is important to try new things, it is also important to never quit the things you are already good at.

Once upon a time, in a small hidden house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Max. They were good friends and played all day. One day, they found a long cord while playing in the yard. Tom said, "Max, let's play with the cord." Max agreed, and they started playing tug-of-war with the cord. They pulled and pulled, having so much fun together. As they played, a little mouse named Sam came out from behind a tree. Sam saw Tom and Max playing with the cord and wanted to join them. Sam went up to them and said, "Can I play too?" Tom and Max looked at Sam and said, "Sure, let's all play together!" They all took turns pulling the cord and had a great time. After that day, Tom, Max, and Sam became best friends and played together every day. They were happy to meet new friends and share their fun times.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a swing and run to it. Lily gets there first and sits on the swing. Ben is sad. He wants to swing too. "Please, Lily, can I swing?" Ben asks. Lily thinks. She likes to swing, but she is compassionate. She sees Ben's face and feels sorry for him. "Okay, Ben, you can swing. But only for a little bit. Then I want to swing again," Lily says. "Thank you, Lily. You are very nice," Ben says. He sits on the swing and Lily pushes him. He laughs and feels happy. They swing for a while, then they see a top on the ground. It is red and shiny. They both want to play with it. "Look, a top! Can I spin it?" Lily asks. "Me too, me too!" Ben says. They take turns to spin the top. They watch it spin and make a humming sound. They clap and giggle. They have fun with the swing and the top. They are good friends. They share and care for each other. They are compassionate.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Andy and Sally. They went outside and both wanted to have some fun. “Let’s jog!” Andy suggested. Sally agreed and they started running. They ran up and down the street. The trees were tall and the sun was bright. Sally thought it was fun and kept going. Andy was thin and ran even faster. They ran past a pond and saw a fish swimming so quickly. “Wow, that fish is fast!" Andy said. “Let’s try to be even faster!” Sally said. They both laughed and started running faster. They ran for a long time and were so tired but it was so much fun. When they were done, they were happy and sweaty. “That was really fun!” Andy said. “I agree, jogging was a lot of fun,” Sally replied. The two hugged and went inside. They went to get some water and relaxed.

Once upon a time, there was a silly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day. He liked to watch TV too. Spot's favorite thing on TV was a show about a funny cat. The cat made Spot laugh a lot. One day, Spot's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to play. They played with a ball and ran in the yard. Lily wanted to feed Spot a snack. She went inside to get some food for him. Spot saw the TV in the house and wanted to watch his funny cat show. While Lily was getting the snack, Spot went inside to watch TV. He forgot that he was not allowed to go inside the house. Spot jumped on the couch and made a big mess. Lily came back and saw the mess. She was sad and told Spot he was a bad dog. Spot did not get his snack and had to go home.
