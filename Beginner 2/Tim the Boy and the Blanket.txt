Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Blanket

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a small bed that he loved to sleep in every night. One day, when Tim went to bed, he saw something odd under his bed. Tim asked his mom, "What is that under my bed?" His mom looked and saw a big box. She shrugged and said, "I don't know, let's open it and see." They opened the box and found a big, soft, and colorful blanket. Tim and his mom put the blanket on his bed. That night, Tim slept very well. He loved his new blanket that he found under his bed. Now, Tim's ordinary bed was extra cozy and warm.

One day, a white cat named Snow walked in the park. Snow liked to hear the birds sing. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The tree had many branches and was very tall. As Snow climbed the tree, she met a bird named Blue. Blue said, "Hello, Snow! What are you doing?" Snow said, "I like to hear you sing, Blue. Can you sing for me?" Blue was happy to sing for Snow. They became good friends. At one point, Snow saw a big, red ball on the ground. She wanted to play with it. She said, "Blue, look at that ball! Let's play with it!" Blue and Snow played with the ball all day. They had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was flying around looking for a twig to build his nest. He found a big twig on the ground and picked it up with his beak. Tim was very happy with his twig. As Tim was flying back to his tree, he saw a big bear named Bob. Bob looked at Tim and said, "Hi Tim! What do you have in your beak?" Tim replied, "I have a twig to build my nest." Bob asked, "Can you repeat that? I didn't hear you well." Tim said again, "I have a twig to build my nest." Suddenly, the twig began to move! It was not a twig, but a long snake! Tim and Bob were both very surprised. The snake said, "I'm sorry for scaring you. My name is Sam. Can we all be friends?" Tim and Bob smiled and agreed. From that day on, Tim, Bob, and Sam were the best of friends, and they played together in the big open field every day.

One day, a messy boy named Tim wanted to print a picture. He found a pretty picture on the television. Tim asked his mom, "Can I print this picture, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but clean your room first. It is very messy." Tim did not want to clean his room. He wanted to print the picture now. So, he tried to print the picture without cleaning his room. The printer did not work. Tim was sad. He asked his mom for help. His mom said, "Did you clean your room?" Tim said, "No." His mom said, "Clean your room, and I will help you." Tim cleaned his room. It was not messy anymore. His mom helped him print the picture. Tim was happy. He learned that it is good to listen to his mom.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tom saw a big, round onion. He thought it would be good to give to his mom for the dinner. Tom picked up the onion and put it in the cart. But when they got home, Tom saw that the onion was bad. It was not good to eat. Tom was sad. He did not want his mom to be upset. But then, something unexpected happened. The bad onion started to move! It was not a bad onion at all. It was a lost baby turtle! Tom and his mom were very surprised. They gave the baby turtle a new home and named him Onion. Tom was happy that he could give his mom a new friend instead of a bad onion.
