Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Jen and the Park

Tim and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big ashtray near a bench. It is full of dirty things. "Look, Jen, what is this?" Tim asks. "I don't know, Tim. It looks bad. Let's go away," Jen says. But Tim is curious. He wants to touch the ashtray. He reaches his hand to it. "Tim, no! Don't touch it! It is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who smoke. Smoking is bad. It makes you sick and sad," Jen says. But Tim does not listen. He touches the ashtray. He feels something sharp and hot. He screams. He drops the ashtray. It breaks. The dirty things fall on the ground. "Tim, are you okay?" Jen asks. She is scared. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts! It hurts!" Tim cries. He holds his hand. He has a cut and a burn. A lady who works in the park hears them. She runs to them. She sees what happened. "Oh, no! What did you do? You should not play with the ashtray. It is dangerous. Come with me. I will help you," she says. She takes Tim and Jen to her office. She cleans Tim's hand. She puts a bandage on it. She gives him a hug and a candy. "Thank you, lady. I am sorry. I was silly. I will not touch the ashtray again," Tim says. "You are welcome, Tim. I am glad you are okay. But you should listen to your friend. She was right. The ashtray is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who smoke. And smoking is bad. It makes you sick and sad. Do you understand?" she says. "Yes, lady. I understand. Smoking is bad. I will not smoke when I grow up. And I will listen to Jen. She is a good friend," Tim says. "Good, Tim. I am proud of you. And you, Jen, you are a good friend. You tried to stop Tim. You cared for him. You are brave and smart," she says. "Thank you, lady. I am happy. Tim is my friend. I want him to be safe and happy," Jen says. The lady smiles. She gives them both a hug and a candy. She tells them to go home and tell their parents what happened. She tells them to be careful and to have fun. Tim and Jen say goodbye to the lady. They hold hands. They walk home. They are friends. They learned a lesson. They will not play with the ashtray again. They will listen to each other. They will be safe and happy.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had many friends, but they all had different names. One day, Lily had an idea. She wanted to unite all her friends and play together. Lily went to her friends and said, "Let's all unite and play together! We can make a big circle and hold hands." Her friends liked the idea, so they all joined hands and made a big circle. As they played in the circle, they laughed and had fun. They learned that even though they had different names, they could all be friends and play together. From that day on, Lily and her friends always played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful forest. In the forest, there was a big tree. A bird lived in the tree. The bird's name was Blue. Blue had a friend named Bunny. Bunny lived near the tree, in a hole in the ground. One day, Blue lost her egg. She was very sad. Blue said, "Bunny, will you help me search for my egg?" Bunny cared for his friend, so he said, "Yes, Blue, I will help you search for your egg." Blue and Bunny looked all around the peaceful forest. They looked under the leaves and behind the rocks. Finally, they found the egg near the river. Blue was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bunny, for helping me search for my egg. You are a good friend." And they lived happily in the peaceful forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved the color purple. One day, she found a purple box in her room. She was very happy. Lily wanted to reveal what was inside the box. She opened it and saw a tool. The tool was a small shovel. Lily said, "Wow, a purple shovel! I will use it to dig in the dirt." Lily went outside to play with her new purple shovel. She dug a big hole in the dirt. Her friend, Tim, came over and asked, "What are you doing, Lily?" She said, "I am digging with my purple shovel." Tim smiled and they played together all day.
