Category: Beginner
Title: Bella and the Cat

Once upon a time, a graceful bird named Bella lived in a big tree. One day, Bella saw a small cat arrive in her tree. The cat looked sad and lost. "Hi, cat. Why are you sad?" Bella asked. The cat said, "I am lost. I need help to find my home." Bella wanted to help her new friend. She knew it was an emergency. Bella and the cat looked for the cat's home. They walked and walked until they saw a little girl. The girl was happy to see her cat. She said, "Thank you, Bella!" Bella felt good for helping her friend. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was a very dependable girl. She always helped her mom and dad with their work. One day, Sue's mom asked her to pour water into the plants outside. Sue was excited to help her mom. Sue took a big basket and went outside to find the plants. She found a big plant and a small plant. She thought, "I will pour water on the big plant first." So, she poured water on the big plant. Then, she poured water on the small plant. The plants were very happy. Suddenly, the small plant started to talk! It said, "Thank you, Sue, for giving me water. I am a magic plant, and I want to give you a gift." The small plant gave Sue a beautiful flower. Sue was surprised and happy. She ran to her mom and dad and showed them the magic flower. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big classroom. In the classroom, there were many kids. They were eager to learn and play. One day, a new boy came to the classroom. His name was Tim. Tim was a little shy. He did not mind the other kids, but he was scared to talk to them. The kids wanted to play with Tim, but he would not talk. This made the kids sad. They did not know what to do. One day, a girl named Sue had an idea. She gave Tim a toy. Tim smiled and played with Sue. Soon, all the kids played together. They were happy. Tim learned that it is good to talk and play with others. The kids learned to be kind and help others feel welcome. And they all became good friends.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big puddle and thought it would be fun to jump in it. Max jumped and splashed in the puddle, getting all wet. He felt so happy and a little foolish. As Max played, a big frog hopped out of the puddle. The frog looked at Max and said, "Why did you jump in my puddle? You made it all messy!" Max felt bad and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't know it was your puddle." The frog was still mad. He wanted to teach Max a lesson, so he bit Max's tail. Max yelped and ran away from the puddle as fast as he could. He didn't want to play in the puddle anymore. When Max got home, he told his mom about the frog and the bite. His mom was worried and took Max to the doctor. The doctor said, "Oh no, that frog was not a normal frog. It was a magic frog!" Because of the magic frog's bite, Max could never play in puddles again. Every time he tried, he would feel a bite on his tail. Max was sad and wished he had never jumped in the puddle that day.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a ball of thread. One day, Tom saw a small bottle. The bottle had a sign that said "shrink". Tom was curious and wanted to see what would happen if he drank it. Tom opened the bottle and took a sip. Suddenly, Tom started to shrink! He got smaller and smaller until he was the same size as the ball of thread. "Oh no!" Tom cried. "I am so small now. How will I get big again?" Just then, Tom's friend, a wise old owl, flew by. He saw Tom and asked, "Tom, why are you so small?" Tom told the owl about the bottle and the word "shrink". The owl knew how to help Tom. "You must drink from the bottle again, and say the word 'grow'," the owl said. Tom did as the owl said, and he grew back to his normal size. Tom was so happy, and he learned not to be so nosy.
