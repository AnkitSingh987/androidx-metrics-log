Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tree and the Birds

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall tree. The tree was very old and strong. Many birds lived in the tree. They were happy and played all day. One day, a big storm came to the town. The wind blew very hard. The tall tree tried to stay strong, but it fell down. The birds were sad. Their home was now a wreck. They did not know what to do. A little girl saw the birds and felt sad too. She wanted to help them. She gave them hugs and built them a new home. The birds were happy again. They learned that when bad things happen, a hug and a friend can make everything better. And the little girl learned that helping others can make her happy too.

One day, a boy named Tim was watching his favorite show on the television. He was very happy. His mom came in and said, "Tim, it's time to go to bed." Tim was sad and did not want to go to bed. In his room, Tim found a big paper. He had an idea. He used his crayons to draw a television on the paper. He was very proud of his work. But when he tried to hang it on the wall, the paper got a tear. Tim was sad and started to cry. His mom heard him cry and came into the room. She saw the tear in the paper and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can fix it." She took some tape and put it on the tear. The paper was fine again. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Then, Tim went to bed and had sweet dreams about his paper television.

Once upon a time, in a hot summer, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went to the park. They loved to play catch with a red ball. Tim would throw the ball, and Max would run to get it. They were having so much fun. One day, Tim dared Max to go near the big tree. Max was frightened because he heard that a mean cat lived there. But Max wanted to show Tim he was brave, so he went close to the tree. As Max went close to the tree, the mean cat jumped out. Max barked, and the cat hissed. Tim ran to help Max, but he tripped on a rock and fell. The cat ran away, but Tim was sad and hurt. They went home, and Tim's mom put a bandage on his knee. "Next time, we won't dare to go near that tree," Tim said to Max. Max wagged his tail, but they both knew that their fun day had a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her doll. She loved her doll very much. It had a good dress and pretty hair. Lily would put her doll on her shoulder and take it everywhere. One day, while she was playing, her brother Tom came. Tom wanted to play with Lily's doll. He said, "Can I play with your doll, please?" Lily did not want to share her doll. She said, "No, it is my doll!" This made Tom sad and he started to cry. Lily's mom heard the cry and came to see what was wrong. She said, "Lily, you should be nice and share your doll with Tom." Lily thought about it and said, "Okay, Tom, you can play with my doll." Tom was happy, and they played together. From that day on, Lily and Tom always shared their toys and had fun together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was with her parent. They were getting ready for a fun day at the park. Lucy was very excited and felt ready to play. At the park, Lucy saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. Lucy's parent said, "Be careful!" Lucy started to climb the tree. She felt the rough bark as she rubbed her hands on it. Lucy climbed higher and higher. She looked down and saw her parent smiling. When she was done, she climbed back down. Her parent gave her a big hug. They had a great day at the park together.
