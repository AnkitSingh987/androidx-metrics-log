Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play dress-up. She liked to wear unique clothes and make her own fashion. One day, she found a pretty dress in her mom's closet. It was so special, she wanted to wear it to show her friends. Lily put on the dress and went outside to play. She saw a big dog coming near her. The dog was friendly, but Lily was scared. She started to tremble. The dog just wanted to say hello, but Lily ran away. Her pretty dress got caught on a tree, and it ripped. Lily was sad and went back home. Her mom saw the ripped dress and hugged Lily. She told her, "It's okay to be scared, but we should not judge others by how they look. The dog was friendly and just wanted to play." Lily learned that day that it's important to be kind and not judge others. And she also learned to ask before wearing her mom's special clothes.

Once upon a time, there was a huge cactus in the yard. Tim and his mom went to see it. They saw the cactus was very big and tall. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. The cactus has sharp parts." One day, Tim found a pretty rock. He wanted to put the rock on the cactus. Tim's mom said, "No, Tim. The cactus can hurt you." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. Later, Tim's dad came home with a surprise. It was a small toy cactus. Tim's dad said, "Now you can put the rock on this cactus, and it won't hurt you." Tim was very happy. He put the rock on the toy cactus and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird. The bird loved to fly and sing. The bird had a plan to find a new tree to live in. The bird flew left and right, up and down. It looked for the best tree. The bird saw a big tree on the left. The tree had many branches and leaves. The blue bird was happy with the tree on the left. It built a nest and lived there. The bird sang and played every day. The blue bird's plan worked, and it was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a small cabin in the woods. The cabin was old and ugly, but it was home to a kind, friendly bear. The bear loved to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the woods. She saw the ugly cabin and wanted to go inside. The bear saw Lily and insisted, "No, Lily, don't go in. It is not safe for you." But Lily did not listen and went inside the cabin anyway. Inside the cabin, Lily found a pretty toy. She played with the toy and forgot about the bear's warning. The bear came inside and saw Lily playing. He was happy that she was safe. From that day on, Lily and the bear became best friends, and they played together in the woods every day.

One day, a light dove named Lily lay on a big tree. The tree had many green leaves and was very tall. Lily liked to sit on the tree and look at the sky. The sky was blue and pretty. Lily saw a small cat on the ground. The cat was looking up at her. Lily wanted to be friends with the cat. She flew down from the tree to say hello. The cat was nice and they played together. After playing, Lily and the cat were tired. They lay under the tree to rest. The sun was warm and made them feel good. Lily and the cat became best friends and played together every day.

Tim and Sue are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big goose near the pond. The goose has white feathers and a long neck. "Look, a goose!" Tim says. He wants to touch the goose. He crawls to the goose on his hands and knees. "Wait, Tim!" Sue says. She is scared of the goose. She thinks the goose is tough and mean. She crawls after Tim to stop him. The goose sees Tim and Sue. It does not like them. It hisses and flaps its wings. It pecks at Tim's hand. Tim cries out. He crawls back to Sue. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tim says. He holds his hand. He is sad and hurt. "Are you okay, Tim?" Sue says. She hugs Tim. She is sorry for him. The goose walks away. It does not care about Tim and Sue. It looks for more food in the pond. Tim and Sue learn a lesson. They do not crawl to the goose again. They play with other animals that are nice and friendly.
