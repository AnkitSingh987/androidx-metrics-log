Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Tree and the Park

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a tall tree. In the tree, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a patient bird who liked to sit and watch the other animals play. One sunny day, Tim saw a long snake named Sam. Sam loved to stretch out in the sun. Tim thought it would be fun to play a joke on Sam. So, Tim flew down and found a big stick. Tim took the stick and poked Sam while he was stretching. Sam jumped up and saw Tim laughing. Sam knew it was just a joke, so he laughed too. Then, Sam and Tim became good friends and played together in the park every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an impressive toy. It was a big, red fire truck. He loved to play with it all day long. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to cut your hair." Tim did not want to stop playing with his fire truck. So, his mom had an idea. She turned on the television. Tim saw a fun show with a happy dog. He liked the show and sat down to watch. While Tim watched the television, his mom cut his hair. She was very careful. When she was done, Tim looked in the mirror. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom! I like my new hair." His mom smiled back and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Now, you can go play with your fire truck again." Tim was very happy and played with his fire truck all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Bob. Bob liked to play near the big drain in the yard. One day, Bob saw a red ball near the drain. He wanted to play with it. Bob's mom told him, "Bob, be careful near the drain. It can be dangerous." But Bob did not listen. He wanted to play with the red ball. Bob tried to get the ball, but it was too close to the drain. Later that day, the sun was very hot. The grass near the drain started to burn. Bob saw the fire and was scared. He tried to run away, but he was too close to the drain. The fire got bigger and bigger. In the end, Bob could not escape, and the fire reached him. The fat cat Bob should have listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to enter her room and play with her toys. She had a special lamp that she liked a lot. It was a pretty lamp with a picture of a cat on it. One day, Mia was playing with her ball. She threw the ball hard and it hit the lamp. The lamp wobbled and almost fell. Mia's mom saw this and told her to be careful with her ball. Mia said sorry and promised to be more careful next time. A few days later, Mia was playing with her ball again. She remembered her mom's words and tried not to throw the ball too hard. But Mia forgot just for a moment and threw the ball hard again. This time, the ball hit the lamp and it fell on the floor. The lamp broke and Mia was sad. She learned her lesson and promised to always listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was an old goat named Billy. Billy had a big horn on his head. He lived in a small house near the woods. One day, a little girl named Sue came to visit Billy. She said, "Hello, Billy! I like your horn. It is very big!" Billy was very happy to hear this. He said, "Thank you, Sue! I like my horn too." Sue and Billy played together all day. They ran in the grass and played with a ball. When the sun went down, it was time for Sue to go home. Billy said, "Goodbye, Sue! Come play with me again soon." Sue said, "I will, Billy. Goodbye!" And they both went to their homes, feeling very happy.
