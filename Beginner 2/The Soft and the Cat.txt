Category: Beginner
Title: The Soft and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a soft, red velvet chair. A little boy named Tim loved to sit on the chair. One day, Tim saw a tiny, naughty cat on the chair. The cat was so naughty that it scratched the chair. Tim did not want the cat to ruin the chair, so he knelt down to talk to the cat. He said, "Cat, please don't be naughty. I love this chair." The cat looked at Tim and jumped off the chair. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Tim, for being kind to me. I was testing you." The fairy waved her wand, and the chair was fixed. Tim was so happy, and they became good friends.

One day, a cop was walking down the street. He saw a boy point at a cat. The boy said, "Look, a cat!" The cop was ignorant and did not know what a cat was. He asked the boy, "What is a cat?" The boy laughed and said, "A cat is a pet with fur and a tail." The cop wanted to learn more, so he followed the cat. The cat ran into a big house. The cop went inside the house too. He saw many cats and thought the house was a cat party. He was happy and started to dance with the cats. Suddenly, the door opened and a big dog came in. The dog was angry and said, "This is my house! Why are you here?" The cop was scared and ran away. The dog chased the cop and the cats out of the house. The cop learned that he should not go into other people's houses without asking. The boy saw the cop running away and laughed. The cop was sad and felt bad for being ignorant.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was a fit fish. He liked to swim fast and play with his friends in the big tank they lived in. The tank was their home, and they were very happy there. One day, Fin saw a new toy in the tank. It was a small hoop. Fin thought it would be fun to swim through the hoop. He swam back, and with a big push, he went fast and tried to fit through the hoop. But, he was too big! Fin felt sad that he couldn't fit through the hoop like his smaller friends. Fin's friend, Tiny, saw that he was sad. Tiny was a very small fish. She swam up to Fin and said, "Don't be sad, Fin! You may not fit through the hoop, but you are a fast and strong fish. We can still play together and have fun!" Fin smiled, and they swam around the tank, playing and having fun with all their friends. Fin learned that even if he couldn't fit through the hoop, he was still a fit and happy fish.

One day, a girl named Ann was playing in her room. She wore a pretty blouse with flowers on it. Ann liked to scatter her toys all over the floor. She played with her dolls, her cars, and her blocks. Ann's mom would sometimes get frustrated because of the mess. Ann's mom came into the room and said, "Ann, it's time to clean up your toys." Ann picked up her toys and put them away. She felt sad because she wanted to keep playing. When she was done, her mom said, "Good job, Ann. Now let's go outside." Outside, Ann saw her friend Tom. Tom was wearing a blouse just like Ann's! It was very unexpected. Ann laughed and said, "Tom, we are twins!" Tom laughed too. They played together and had lots of fun. Ann was happy that she cleaned up her toys and got to play outside with her friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pencil on the floor. It was a tight pencil, not like the others he had seen before. He picked it up and started to draw on a piece of paper. As he drew, a small car appeared on the paper. Tim was very excited and said, "Wow! Look at the car I drew!" The car suddenly zoomed off the paper and onto the floor! Tim was so surprised and shouted, "Oh no! The car is running away!" Tim chased the car around the room as it zoomed here and there. He tried to catch the car, but it was too fast. Then, the car stopped and looked at Tim. The car said, "I am a magic car from the tight pencil. If you want me to stay, you must promise to take care of me and play with me every day." Tim agreed and they became best friends. From that day on, Tim and the magic car had lots of fun adventures together.
