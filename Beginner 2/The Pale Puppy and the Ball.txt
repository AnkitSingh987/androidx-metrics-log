Category: Beginner
Title: The Pale Puppy and the Ball

Once there was a little pale puppy. He liked to play all day. One day, he saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. But he was very tired. His mommy told him to rest. The puppy did not listen. He kept playing with the big ball. He was so tired, but he did not stop. He wanted to play more and more. The sun was hot, but the puppy did not care. The puppy played too much. He did not rest like his mommy said. The puppy fell down. He did not get up. The mommy was sad. She wished the puppy had listened and rested.

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill, there was an enormous tree. Every day, a little boy named Tim would climb the hill to see the view. He liked to smile when he saw the pretty view from the top. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue at the top of the hill. They both looked at the view together. Tim said, "I like to smile when I see the view. Do you like it too?" Sue nodded and said, "Yes, it makes me smile too." But one day, it rained a lot. The hill was very wet and slippery. Tim and Sue tried to climb the hill to see the view, but they slipped and fell. They were very sad because they could not see the pretty view and smile anymore.

Tom and Lily were two birds who lived in a big tree. They liked to fly and sing and play with their friends. One day, they heard a loud noise and felt the ground shake. It was an earthquake! The earthquake made the tree fall down and broke their nest. Tom and Lily were scared and sad. They did not know where to go. They flew around and looked for a new home. They saw a barn with some hay and a hole in the roof. They thought it would be a good place to stay. They flew inside and made a new nest. They were happy and warm. But then they heard another noise. It was a farmer with a big stick. He did not like birds in his barn. He tried to hit them and chase them away. Tom and Lily were scared and angry. They did not want to leave. They flew out of the hole and saw a big cloud. It was a storm. It had rain and wind and thunder. They did not like the storm. They wished they had a safe home. They saw their old tree. It was still on the ground, but it had some branches and leaves. They flew to the tree and found their old nest. It was still there, but it was wet and dirty. They did not care. They were happy and grateful. They had a home. They learned that sometimes bad things happen, but they can still find good things. They learned to be grateful for what they have and not to be greedy for what they do not have. They learned to be happy and kind.

Once upon a time, there was an ostrich named Molly who liked to go on adventures. She was very helpful to everyone around her in the forest. One day, while out on an adventure, Molly came across a rabbit who looked sad. "Why don't you look happy?" Molly asked, but the rabbit didn't answer. Molly gently nudged the rabbit and asked again. This time, the rabbit looked up and said, "I'm sad because I can't reach the carrots on the high branch." Molly frowned, but then she had an idea. She bent her neck down as far as it would go, and the rabbit was able to grab the carrots. The rabbit was so happy and thanked Molly for her help. From that day on, Molly and the rabbit were good friends. Moral of the story: Being helpful can turn frowns into smiles.

Tom was a boy who liked to draw. He had many colors and papers to make pictures. He drew his family, his dog, his toys and his dreams. He was happy when he drew. One day, he drew a big mark on the wall with a red color. He wanted to make a fire truck. But his mom saw the mark and was angry. She said, "Tom, why did you draw on the wall? That is bad. You have to clean it now." She gave him a cloth and a bucket of water. Tom was sad and scared. He did not want to make his mom angry. He did not know how to clean the wall. He tried to rub the mark with the cloth, but it did not go away. It only made a bigger mark. He started to cry. He was worried that his mom would be more angry. His mom heard him cry and came to see him. She saw the bigger mark and the wet cloth. She sighed and said, "Tom, I know you like to draw, but you have to be careful where you draw. The wall is not for drawing. It is hard to clean. Next time, use your papers and colors, okay?" She hugged him and said, "I'm not angry with you, Tom. I love you. But you have to help me clean the wall. It will take some time and work. Are you ready?" Tom nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I'm sorry. I love you too." He smiled and wiped his tears. They worked together to clean the wall. It was not easy, but they did it. They used soap and water and a sponge. They scrubbed and rinsed and dried. They made the wall white again. They were tired, but they were happy. They sat on the couch and looked at the wall. Tom said, "Mom, can I show you my drawings?" He ran to get his papers and colors. He showed her his family, his dog, his toys and his dreams. She said, "Wow, Tom, you are a great artist. I love your drawings. They are beautiful and colorful. You can hang them on the wall with tape, if you want. But only with tape, okay?" Tom said, "Okay, mom. Thank you. You are the best mom." He hugged her and kissed her. They were happy.
