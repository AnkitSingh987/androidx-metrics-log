Category: Beginner
Title: Finny the Blue Shark and the Fish

One day, a blue shark named Finny lived in the sea. He loved to run fast in the water. He had many friends like fish, turtles, and dolphins. They all liked to play and swim together. One day, while Finny was running fast, he met a little fish named Bubbles. Bubbles said, "Hi Finny! Can you teach me how to run fast like you?" Finny smiled and said, "Of course, Bubbles! Just follow me and I will show you how." Finny and Bubbles started to run in the water. They went up and down, and side to side. Bubbles was getting better at running fast. They both had so much fun. After a while, they stopped to rest. Finny said, "Good job, Bubbles! You are getting fast like me!" Bubbles was very happy and said, "Thank you, Finny! I love running fast with you!" They both laughed and continued to play with their friends in the sea.

One day, a little girl named Sue had a toy. She loved her toy so much. It was a small, stubborn horse that would never fall down. Sue took her toy everywhere she went. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom. She played with her toy on the swings and the slide. Sue's mom said, "Sue, don't forget your toy when we go home." Sue nodded and kept playing. But when it was time to go home, Sue forgot her toy at the park! That night, she was sad and couldn't sleep. The next day, they went back to the park to find the toy. But it was gone! Sue was so sad. Just then, a little boy came up to Sue and said, "Is this your toy? I found it on the swings." Sue was so happy and hugged her toy. She thanked the boy and promised to never forget her toy again. And her stubborn little horse was always by her side.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a pet bird named Angel. Angel had soft white feathers and a sweet song. Lily loved Angel very much. One sunny day, Lily and Angel went to the park. They saw a boy with a big, red ball. The boy said, "Hi Lily, do you want to play?" Lily said, "Yes, but I need to release Angel first." She opened Angel's cage and let her fly up to a tree. While they played, Angel felt hungry. She flew down and found some yummy seeds on the ground. Angel ate the seeds and felt happy. When it was time to go home, Lily called Angel back to her cage. They said goodbye to the boy and went home together. At home, Lily's mom gave her a big hug. She said, "I'm so proud of you for taking care of Angel." Lily smiled and hugged her mom back. She knew that she and Angel would always be best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big backyard where he liked to play. The backyard had trees, flowers, and a pale blue swing. One sunny day, Tim went to the backyard to play. He saw his friends, the birds, the cats, and the dogs. They all wanted to play with him. Tim had to choose who to play with first. First, he chose to play with the cats. They played hide and seek in the trees. Then, Tim chose to play with the dogs. They played catch with a ball. Last, he chose to play with the birds. They sang songs together. After a fun day of playing, Tim went back inside his house. He was happy he had so many friends to play with in his backyard.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red car. The car liked to go fast on the smooth road. The car had a friend named Tom. Tom was a small boy with a big smile. One day, Tom wanted to go to the park. He asked the car, "Will you come with me?" The car said, "Yes, I will come with you!" They were very happy. But the car needed fuel to go. Tom found a fuel can and gave it to the car. They went to the park together, and the car went fast on the smooth road. They had a fun day at the park.
