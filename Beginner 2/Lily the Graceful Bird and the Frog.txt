Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Graceful Bird and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Lily. Lily had a very sharp beak. She used her sharp beak to open seeds and nuts to eat. She loved to fly and sing in the sky. One day, Lily met a big frog named Tom. Tom had a loud voice. He liked to jump and play in the pond. Tom showed Lily how to jump from one lily pad to another. Lily showed Tom how to fly high in the sky. Lily and Tom became best friends. They played and sang together every day. Lily's graceful flying and Tom's loud voice made everyone in the forest happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of filthy boots. He loved to jump in the mud with them. One day, his mom told him to clean his boots. "No, I refuse!" said Tim. He did not want to clean them. He liked his boots just the way they were. His mom said, "If you don't clean your boots, you can't play outside." Tim thought about it and decided to clean his boots. He scrubbed and scrubbed until they were clean. Now, he could play outside again with his clean boots.

Lily and Max were twins. They liked to walk in the park with Mom and Dad. They saw many things: trees, flowers, birds, dogs, and people. Lily was curious. She wanted to know more about everything. Max was not so curious. He liked to play with his ball and run around. One day, they saw a big pond in the park. There were ducks and fish in the pond. Lily wanted to see them closer. She took off her jacket and ran to the edge of the pond. Max followed her, but he kept his jacket on. He was afraid of the water. "Look, Max, look!" Lily said. "The ducks are so cute! And the fish are so shiny! Can we touch them?" "No, Lily, no!" Max said. "We can't touch them. Mom and Dad said so. The water is cold and deep. We might fall in and get wet and sick. Come back, Lily, come back!" But Lily did not listen. She leaned over the pond and reached out her hand. She wanted to touch a duck. The duck did not like that. It quacked loudly and bit Lily's finger. Lily cried out and pulled back her hand. She lost her balance and fell into the pond. The water was cold and deep. She was wet and scared. Max saw what happened. He was scared too. He wanted to help Lily. He threw his ball to her and shouted, "Grab the ball, Lily, grab the ball! I will pull you out!" Lily grabbed the ball and held on tight. Max pulled the ball and Lily out of the pond. He hugged her and said, "Are you okay, Lily, are you okay?" Lily was okay, but she was wet and cold and sorry. She said, "I'm sorry, Max, I'm sorry. I was curious. I wanted to touch the duck. But the duck was mean. And the water was cold. And I was scared. I'm sorry, Max, I'm sorry." Max said, "It's okay, Lily, it's okay. I'm sorry too. I was afraid. I didn't want you to fall in. I'm glad you're okay. I love you, Lily, I love you." They hugged each other and said sorry and I love you. Then they put on their jackets and walked back to Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad were worried. They saw Lily and Max were wet and dirty. They asked them what happened. Lily and Max told them the truth. Mom and Dad were not happy. They said, "You were naughty, Lily and Max, you were naughty. You didn't listen to us. You went too close to the pond. You could have been hurt. You need to be careful, Lily and Max, you need to be careful." Lily and Max said, "We're sorry, Mom and Dad, we're sorry. We were curious and afraid. We won't do it again. We love you, Mom and Dad, we love you." Mom and Dad said, "We love you too, Lily and Max, we love you too. But you need to learn from your mistake. You need to listen to us and follow the rules. You need to be safe, Lily and Max, you need to be safe." They hugged and kissed Lily and Max and said sorry and I love you. Then they walked home together. Lily and Max learned their lesson. They were still curious, but they were also careful. They still liked to walk in the park, but they stayed away from the pond. They played with their ball and watched the ducks and fish from a distance. They were happy, Lily and Max, they were happy.
