Category: Beginner
Title: Anna the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Anna found a fragile bird in her yard. The bird could not fly. Anna felt sad for the bird. She wanted to help it. Anna's mom saw her and said, "What's wrong, Anna?" Anna told her mom about the bird. Her mom said, "Let's call the vet to help the bird." They called the vet, and the vet came to their house. The vet looked at the bird and said, "The bird needs a massage to feel better." Anna and her mom helped the vet give the bird a massage. Soon, the bird felt better and could fly again. Anna was happy to help the bird, and they all waved goodbye as it flew away.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Joe and Terry. Joe had a shiny bow that he wore to school every day. One day, Joe was walking to school with Terry when he spotted a jolly old man. The old man gave a friendly wave and said, "Good morning!" Joe and Terry smiled and walked over to the old man. Terry asked, "Who are you?" The old man said, "I'm Mr. Smith. I'm here to give you a bow like Joe's. I thought you might like it." Joe said, "Thank you, Mr. Smith. This bow is so cool!" Terry gave Joe a big hug and said, "Let's go show our friends." So, off they went to show off Joe's jolly new bow. As they walked, Terry and Joe talked about all the fun they would have with their new bows.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to swim. They went to the pool every week with their moms. They had fun playing with the water and the toys. One day, they saw a big sign at the pool. It said: "Diving Event Today". They did not know what that meant. They asked their moms. Their moms said: "A diving event is when people jump into the water from very high. They do tricks in the air and try to make a splash. It is fun to watch." Tom and Lily wanted to see the diving event. They followed their moms to the other side of the pool. There, they saw a big board that was very high. They saw people climbing up the stairs and standing on the board. They saw people diving into the water. Some did flips, some did twists, some did spins. They made big splashes and loud noises. Tom and Lily clapped and cheered. They also saw a man with a microphone. He said: "Welcome to the diving event. We have some very rich prizes for the best divers. The first prize is a big gold medal. The second prize is a big silver medal. The third prize is a big bronze medal. Who will win the medals?" Tom and Lily did not care about the medals. They just liked to see the divers. They thought they were brave and cool. They wished they could dive like them. They decided to practice diving from the low board. They had fun trying to make splashes and noises. They pretended they were in the diving event. They gave each other medals made of paper. They were happy.

Lily and Tom are hungry. They want to eat something yummy. They ask their mom if they can go to the store with her. "Can we go to the store, mom?" Lily says. "Yes, you can, but you have to be good and stay with me," mom says. They put on their coats and shoes and go to the car. Mom drives them to the store. They see many things in the store. They see fruits, vegetables, bread, cheese, milk, eggs, and more. "Look, mom, apples!" Tom says. He likes apples. He wants to eat one. "OK, Tom, you can have one apple, but you have to pay for it first," mom says. She picks a red apple and puts it in a bag. "Can I have a banana, mom?" Lily says. She likes bananas. She wants to eat one. "OK, Lily, you can have one banana, but you have to pay for it first," mom says. She picks a yellow banana and puts it in the bag. Mom takes the bag to the cashier. She gives him some money. He gives her some change. He smiles and says, "Thank you for shopping with us. Have a nice day." "Thank you, bye," mom says. She takes the bag and the kids and goes back to the car. Lily and Tom are happy. They have their fruits. They peel them and eat them. They are sweet and juicy. "Yum, yum, yum," Lily and Tom say. Mom is happy too. She is proud of her kids. They are good and polite. They are successful shoppers.
