Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Kite

Lily loved to fly her kite on sunny days. She had a big kite with many colors, like a rainbow in the sky. She ran and ran with the kite, and felt happy and free. One day, she saw a dark cloud coming. She was afraid that the cloud would take her kite away. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, can we go home? There is a bad cloud." Mom looked at the sky and smiled. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. The cloud is not bad. It is just bringing some rain. And after the rain, you might see something beautiful." Lily did not understand. She said, "What is beautiful after the rain?" Mom said, "You will see. Let's wait and watch." They waited under a tree, and soon the rain came. It was wet and cold, and Lily hugged her kite. She did not like the rain. But then, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Lily looked up and saw a bright and colorful arc in the sky. It was a rainbow, like her kite, but bigger and more beautiful. Lily gasped and said, "Wow! What is that?" Mom said, "That is a rainbow, Lily. It is a gift from the sun and the rain. They work together to make something wonderful." Lily smiled and said, "I like the rainbow. It is so pretty and graceful. Can I fly my kite with the rainbow?" Mom said, "Of course you can. Let's go and have some fun." Lily ran with her kite, and released it in the air. She watched it fly with the rainbow, and felt happy and free again.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play with boats. They had many toy boats of different colors and sizes. They liked to pretend they were sailors and explorers. One day, they went to the harbor with their mom and dad. The harbor was a big place where real boats came and went. It was very busy and noisy. Ben and Lily saw big boats, small boats, fast boats, slow boats, and even a boat with a sail. "Can we go on a boat, mom?" Ben asked. "Maybe later, honey. First, we have to find a place to eat," mom said. They walked along the harbor, looking for a restaurant. Lily saw a big red boat with a sign that said "Ferry". She wanted to go on it. "Look, Ben, a ferry! Let's go on it!" Lily said, pulling Ben's hand. "No, Lily, we have to stay with mom and dad," Ben said, pulling back. But Lily was too excited. She ran towards the ferry, dragging Ben with her. She did not see the rope that was on the ground. She tripped over it and fell. Ben fell with her. "Ouch!" Lily cried. "Are you okay?" Ben asked. They both struggled to get up. They had scraped their knees and elbows. They felt scared and sorry. Mom and dad saw what happened. They ran to them and hugged them. "Are you hurt, sweeties?" mom asked. "A little," Lily said. "We're sorry, mom. We wanted to go on the ferry," Ben said. "It's okay, honey. But you have to be careful. The harbor is a busy place. You have to watch where you are going and stay with us," dad said. He took out a band-aid and put it on Lily's knee. Mom did the same for Ben. "Thank you, mom. Thank you, dad," Lily and Ben said. They felt better. They smiled and hugged their parents. "Come on, let's go find a place to eat. Maybe we can go on a boat later, if you want," mom said. "Okay, mom. We love you," Lily and Ben said. They held their parents' hands and walked along the harbor. They saw many boats and people. They learned a lot. They had fun. They were happy.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the water. One day, they find a kayak by the lake. It is big and red. "Can we try it?" Sara asks. "OK, but be careful," Ben says. They climb into the kayak and use the paddles to move. The kayak is fast and fun. They see ducks and fish and frogs. They laugh and sing. But then, the water gets rough. The kayak starts to spin. Sara and Ben are scared. "Help! Help!" they shout. Dad hears them. He runs to the lake. He swims to the kayak and grabs it. He stops the spinning. He pulls the kayak to the shore. "Are you OK?" he asks. "Yes, we are OK. But that was scary," Sara says. "Very scary," Ben says. Dad hugs them. He is glad they are safe. "Let's go home and have some soup," he says. "OK, but not spicy soup," Sara says. "No, not spicy soup," Ben says. They walk home with Dad. They are happy and warm. They do not want to spin in the kayak again.
