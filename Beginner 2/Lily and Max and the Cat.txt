Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Cat

Lily and Max liked to play in the backyard. They had a big grill that their dad used to make food. Sometimes, they pretended the grill was a spaceship or a castle. But they knew they had to be careful, because the grill could be hot and hurt them. One day, they saw a shiny red ball under the grill. They wanted to get it, but they were afraid of the heat. Lily had an idea. She whispered to Max, "You go and grab the ball, and I will watch for dad. If he comes, I will say 'boo' and you will run away." Max nodded and crawled under the grill. He reached for the ball, but it was too far. He stretched his arm, but he touched something else. It was dry and rough and made a loud noise. Max screamed and jumped back. It was a cat! The cat was scared too. It ran out of the grill and scratched Max's face. Lily saw the cat and said "boo", but it was too late. Max was crying and bleeding. Lily ran to him and hugged him. She was sorry for her idea. Dad heard the noise and came to the backyard. He saw Max and Lily and the cat and the ball. He was angry and worried. He took Max inside and cleaned his wound. He told Lily to stay away from the grill and the cat. He said they were lucky it was not worse. Lily and Max learned their lesson. They never played with the grill or the cat again. They found other things to do in the backyard. They still liked the shiny red ball, but they left it under the grill. They did not want to get hurt again.

One day, a little dog named Max went to play outside. Max was a patient dog and liked to act funny to make his friends laugh. He would roll on the ground, jump high, and make silly faces. On this day, Max met a new friend, a tiny bird named Lily. Lily liked Max's funny acts and they played all day. They ran in the park, played with a ball, and laughed a lot. As the day ended, Max and Lily said goodbye. They were happy to have a new friend. Max went home and told his family about his fun day with Lily. They all smiled and knew Max would have more fun days with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved very much. The toy car had smooth wheels and went very fast. Tim played with his toy car every day. One day, Tim's toy car lost a wheel. Tim was sad. His mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim showed her the toy car and said, "My car lost a wheel." His mom thought for a moment and said, "Don't worry, we can replace it." Tim and his mom went to the store. They found a new wheel for the toy car. Tim's mom helped him put the new wheel on with her hand. The toy car was as good as new! Tim was very happy. He said to his mom, "Thank you for helping me fix my car!" His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Have fun playing with your car!" And Tim played with his toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to gather sticks in the park. One sunny day, he found a big hole in the ground. It was a calm day, and Spot was very happy. Spot wanted to see what was in the hole, so he put his head in it. Suddenly, he felt a tickle on his nose. He pulled his head out of the hole and saw a tiny frog sitting on his nose! Spot was so surprised! The frog said, "Hello, Spot! I live in this hole. Thank you for visiting me." Spot and the frog became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. And that's how Spot found a new friend in a hole.
