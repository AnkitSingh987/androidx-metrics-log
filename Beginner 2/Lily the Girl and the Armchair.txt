Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Armchair

One day, a little girl named Lily was very eager to buy a new armchair for her room. She saved her money and went to the store with her mom. At the store, Lily saw a big, soft armchair. She was so happy and told her mom that she wanted to buy it. But when they tried to take it home, it was too big for the car! Lily was sad, but then she had an idea. She asked her mom if they could carry the armchair home. Her mom said yes, and they started to walk. On the way, a nice man with a big truck saw them and offered to help. The man put the armchair in his truck and drove them home. When they got home, Lily and her mom thanked the man. They put the armchair in Lily's room, and she was very happy. Now she had a big, soft armchair to sit in and read her favorite books.

One day, Tom and Sue went to the park. They saw a big tree. Tom said, "I bet I can climb to the top." Sue said, "I don't know, Tom. Be careful." Tom started to climb. He felt strong and full of power. He got very high. Sue looked up and shouted, "Tom, I am scared. Please come down now." Tom was too high and felt ashamed. He could not hear Sue. Sue ran to get help. A kind man came and helped Tom come down. Tom was happy to be on the ground. He said to Sue, "Thank you for helping me. I am sorry I scared you." Sue was happy Tom was safe. They went home and never climbed a big tree again.

Anna liked to play in the garden with her doll. She liked to smell the flowers and watch the bees. The bees were nice. They made honey and buzzed around. Anna was not afraid of them. One day, Anna saw a big bee on a red flower. She wanted to see it closer. She walked to the flower and reached out her hand. But the bee did not like that. It stung Anna on her finger. Anna cried out loud. It hurt a lot. Mama heard Anna and ran to her. She took her inside and put some ice on her finger. She hugged Anna and said, "I'm sorry, my love. The bee was scared of you. It thought you wanted to hurt it. It did not mean to hurt you." Anna sniffled and said, "But I like the bees. I just wanted to say hello. Will the bee be okay?" Mama smiled and said, "Yes, the bee will be okay. It flew away. But it will miss its stinger. It can't sting anyone anymore. Maybe it will be more careful next time." Anna felt sorry for the bee. She hoped it would find some honey and some friends. She said, "I miss the bees. Can I go back to the garden?" Mama said, "Yes, you can. But be gentle with the bees. They are nice, but they need their space. And don't touch them. Just watch them and enjoy them." Anna nodded and said, "Okay, Mama. I will be nice to the bees. And I hope they will be nice to me." She kissed Mama and ran back to the garden with her doll. She saw some bees on the yellow flowers. She waved to them and said, "Hello, bees. I'm sorry for before. I hope you have a good day." The bees buzzed and flew around. Anna smiled and played with her doll. She was happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat sausages. One day, Spot saw a big sausage on the table. He wanted to eat it so much! Spot's friend, Cat, saw him looking at the sausage. She wanted to tease him. Cat jumped on the table and said, "Spot, you can't have this sausage. It's too big for you!" Spot felt sad, but he still wanted the sausage. Then, a crazy bird flew into the house. The bird saw the sausage too. He flew down and grabbed it in his beak. The bird flew away with the sausage. Spot and Cat looked at each other and laughed. They knew they could not catch the bird. Spot said, "It's okay, Cat. We can find more sausages to eat." Cat agreed, and they went on an adventure to find more sausages. And they lived happily ever after.
