Category: Beginner
Title: The Rabbit and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there was a small rabbit. The rabbit loved to eat. One day, the rabbit found a big, red apple. It looked delicious. The rabbit wanted to eat the apple, but it was too big to carry. The rabbit had an idea. He would drop the apple down a hill and eat it at the bottom. So, the rabbit pushed the apple and it rolled down the hill. The rabbit ran fast to catch the apple at the bottom. But when the rabbit got to the bottom of the hill, the apple was gone! The rabbit was sad. Then, he saw a big, brown bear. The bear had the apple! The rabbit was scared, but the bear smiled. The bear said, "I saw your apple roll down the hill. Let's share it!" The rabbit and the bear became friends and ate the delicious apple together.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They go to the same school. They wear the same uniform. But they are very different. Tim is always sleepy in the morning. He does not want to get up. He does not want to brush his teeth. He does not want to eat his breakfast. He does not want to wear his uniform. He cries and whines and demands to stay in bed. Sam is always happy in the morning. He jumps out of bed. He brushes his teeth. He eats his breakfast. He wears his uniform. He smiles and sings and helps his mom. One day, their mom is sick. She cannot take them to school. She asks their dad to do it. But their dad is very busy. He has to go to work. He has no time to waste. He goes to Tim's room. He sees Tim still in bed. He says, "Tim, get up. You have to go to school. Put on your uniform. Stop crying. Stop demanding. Be a good boy." He goes to Sam's room. He sees Sam ready to go. He says, "Sam, you are a good boy. You are always ready to go. You wear your uniform. You do not demand. You make me proud." He takes Sam to the car. He leaves Tim in the house. He says, "Tim, you can stay in bed. But you will miss school. You will miss your friends. You will miss your teacher. You will miss your fun. You will be sad. You will be sorry." Tim realizes his mistake. He feels bad. He wishes he was like Sam. He wishes he was not so sleepy. He wishes he did not demand. He wishes he wore his uniform. He wishes he went to school. He learns his lesson. He decides to change. He decides to be a good boy. He decides to be like Sam. The moral of the story is: Do not be sleepy. Do not demand. Wear your uniform. Go to school. Be a good boy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run with speed. He would run and play all day long. But Max was a bit troubled, as he liked to bite things. One day, Max saw a big red ball. He wanted to bite it, so he ran fast and jumped to bite it. But when he bit the ball, it popped! Max was sad and scared because he broke the ball. Max's friend, a wise old cat, saw what happened. The cat told Max, "Biting is not always good. Sometimes, it can make things bad." Max listened and learned from the cat. From that day on, Max only bit his toys and not other things. And he was a happy dog, who played and ran with speed, without any trouble.

Once upon a time, there was a modest tree in a big park. The tree had many big palm leaves. One sunny day, a small boy named Tim came to play near the tree. Tim looked up at the tree and said, "Hello, tree! I like your big leaves." The tree was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You can play under me and have fun." As Tim played, he found a small toy car. He remembered that he lost the car a long time ago. Tim was very happy to find his car again. He said to the tree, "Thank you, tree, for keeping my car safe." The tree smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I will always be here for you to play and remember happy times." From that day on, Tim and the modest tree with big palm leaves became very good friends, and they had lots of fun together.
