Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very careless girl. One day, she went to the market with her mom. Lily saw a big bag of sugar. She wanted it so much. Lily asked her mom, "Can I have the sugar, please?" Her mom said, "No, we have enough sugar at home." Lily was sad but she did not yield. She wanted the sugar more than anything. When her mom was not looking, Lily took the sugar. She was so careless that she dropped it. The sugar spilled all over the ground. Lily's mom saw what happened and said, "Lily, you should have listened to me." From that day on, Lily learned to not be careless and to listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. This cat liked to play with a baby infant. They were best friends. They played with toys and had fun all day. The cat and the baby laughed and smiled together. One day, the cat wanted to become a big cat. The cat thought it would be more fun to play with the baby if it were bigger. So, the cat ate lots of food and took long naps. The cat grew bigger and bigger every day. Now, the black cat was big and strong. It played with the infant all day long. They had even more fun than before. The baby and the cat were very happy. They became best friends forever.

One day, a little toy car named Zip was playing near a big tank of water. Zip was very fast and loved to race around. The tank was full of fish that liked to watch Zip zoom by. Zip had a friend, a small bird named Tweet. Tweet was sad because a bad cat was trying to catch her. Zip wanted to help Tweet, so they made a plan. They would use the tank to make the bad cat go away. Zip told Tweet to fly near the tank. The bad cat followed Tweet, trying to catch her. As the cat got close to the tank, Zip raced towards it. He hit the tank with a big splash! Water went everywhere, and the bad cat got all wet. The bad cat did not like being wet, so it ran away. Zip and Tweet were happy that their plan worked. They played together near the tank, knowing the bad cat would not bother them anymore. And the fish in the tank were safe too, thanks to Zip and Tweet.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to fold paper into shapes. One day, she folded paper into a plant. Mia was so happy with her paper plant. She put it on her window. But the paper plant was not healthy. It needed water and sun like a real plant. Mia was sad. She wanted her paper plant to be healthy and strong. Mia had an idea. She took her paper plant outside. She put it in the ground and gave it water. The next day, a real healthy plant grew in its place. Mia was so happy. She learned that real plants need love and care to grow strong.

Ben is a boy who likes to play. He goes to the park with his mom and dad. He sees a big slide. He wants to go on it. He runs to the slide and climbs up the steps. He looks down and sees a man. The man has a staff. A staff is a long stick that helps him walk. The man is old. He has white hair and a long beard. He smiles at Ben and says hello. Ben says hello back. He asks the man what he is doing. The man says he is looking for something. He says he lost his ring. His ring is very special. It has a star on it. He got it from his wife a long time ago. Ben feels sorry for the man. He wants to help him. He slides down the slide and lands next to the man. He says he will help him find his ring. The man thanks him and gives him a hug. They look around the park. They look under the benches, in the trash cans, and in the flowers. They do not find the ring. Ben is sad. He says he is sorry. The man says it is okay. He says he is happy to have a new friend. They sit on the bench and talk. They share stories and jokes. They laugh and have fun. Ben forgets about the slide. He likes the man. He thinks he is very wise and kind. He gives him his staff and says he can keep it. The man is surprised and happy. He gives him his hat and says he can keep it. The hat is big and floppy. Ben likes it. He puts it on his head. They hug and say goodbye. Ben goes back to his mom and dad. He tells them about the man and the ring and the staff and the hat. They are proud of him. They say he is very brave and nice. They go home and have dinner. Ben puts the staff and the hat in his room. He thinks about the man and hopes he will find his ring. He falls asleep and dreams of stars.
