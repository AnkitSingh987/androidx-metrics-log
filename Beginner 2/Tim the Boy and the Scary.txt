Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scary

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play dress up. He found a big box of costumes in his room. He saw a scary costume and decided to wear it. It was a scary monster costume with big teeth and long claws. Tim walked to the living room where his mom was sitting. "Mom, look at me!" he said. Mom looked up and saw the scary costume. She laughed and said, "Oh, Tim! You look so scary! But it's just pretend, right?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, Mom. It's just pretend." He played around the house, pretending to be a scary monster. After a while, Tim got tired. He went back to his room and took off the scary costume. He put on his soft pajamas and climbed into bed for a nice nap. As Tim napped, he dreamed of more fun costumes to wear and games to play. He knew that when he woke up, he could play dress up again and have even more fun with his costumes.

One day, a creative girl named Amy wanted to put on a play. She told her friend Tom, "Let's make a theater in our yard!" Tom was excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They worked hard to make the theater. They used big boxes and painted them. They made a stage and seats for people to sit. When they were done, they told their friends, "Come see our play at the theater!" The day of the play, many friends and family came to watch. They all sat down and waited. Amy and Tom were a little scared, but they knew their play was good. They had a secret surprise for the end of the play. At the end of the play, a big, fun puppet show started. Everyone was so happy and clapped their hands. The surprise was a big hit! After the play, Amy and Tom smiled and knew their creative theater was a success.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly boat. The friendly boat liked to play in the water. The boat had fun all day long. One day, the boat saw a big cloud. The cloud was very big and gray. The friendly boat wanted to see the cloud up close. So, the boat went near the cloud. The cloud started to cry. The rain came down and it began to soak the friendly boat. The boat got very wet and started to sink. The friendly boat was sad and could not play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red switch on a wall. This switch was very useful. It could do many things when you press it. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the switch and wanted to press it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I press the switch?" His mom said, "No, Tim. That switch is not for you to press." But Tim really wanted to press the switch, so he waited for his mom to leave the room. When his mom left, Tim jumped up and pressed the switch. Suddenly, the room went dark! Tim was scared. He did not know that the switch would do that. He called for his mom, but she did not come. Tim tried to find his way out of the dark room, but he could not see. He bumped into things and fell down. He cried and cried, but his mom still did not come. Tim was very sad and scared. In the end, Tim learned that he should listen to his mom. The useful switch was not for him to press. He wished he had not done it, but it was too late. The room stayed dark, and Tim was all alone.

One day, a little cat named Bob saw a flexible toy on the floor. He did not like it, and he said, "I hate this toy!" Bob's mom heard him and asked, "Why do you hate it, Bob?" Bob told his mom, "It's too fast! When I try to play with it, it goes zoom!" His mom smiled and said, "Let's play with it together. I will show you how to have fun with it." Bob and his mom played with the flexible toy. They made it go slow and fast. They laughed when it went zoom! Bob learned to like the toy, and he did not hate it anymore. He was happy to play with the zooming toy and his mom.
