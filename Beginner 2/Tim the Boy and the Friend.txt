Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Friend

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a computer. He loved to play with the computer. The computer was his best friend. Tim was always eager to learn new things from his friend. One day, the computer started to speak. "Hi, Tim!" it said. Tim was very happy. He had never heard his computer speak before. "Hi, computer!" Tim said back. They talked for a long time. The computer taught Tim about animals, colors, and numbers. Later, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim talking to the computer. "What are you doing, Tim?" she asked. "I'm talking to my computer, Mom. It can speak!" Tim said. Tim's mom smiled. She was happy that Tim was eager to learn from his talking computer friend.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous sunflower. It lay in a big garden. The sunflower loved the sun and grew very tall. Every day, it looked at the sky and smiled. One day, a little bee came to the sunflower. The bee said, "Hello, sunflower! Can I play with you?" The sunflower was happy and said, "Yes, little bee! I would love to play with you." So, the bee and the sunflower played all day. They laughed and had so much fun. The sunflower was very happy to have a friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of junk in his room. His mom told him to clean it up, but Tim did not understand why he had to do it. One day, Tim's mom bought him a new toy. Tim was very happy and wanted to play with his new toy. But, his room was so full of junk that he could not find a place to play. Tim decided to clean up his room. He put away all the junk and made his room nice and clean. Now, Tim had a lot of space to play with his new toy. He understood why his mom wanted him to clean up. Tim and his mom were both very happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a colorful microphone in her toy box. She was so happy and wanted to sing. Sue ran to her mom and said, "Mom, can I please sing with my new microphone?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Sue, you can sing. Please sing a nice song for me." Sue started to sing her favorite song, "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star." She sang so loud and happy. As Sue sang, something unexpected happened. Her toy friends started to dance and sing with her! Sue was so surprised and happy. She kept singing, and her toy friends kept dancing. They all had a fun day singing and dancing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Pup. Pup was a very foolish dog. He liked to play all day and never did what he was told. One day, Pup's mom said, "Pup, you need to be patient and wait for your food." But Pup did not want to wait. He wanted to eat now. Pup saw his mom put the food on the high table. He thought, "I can get the food myself!" Pup jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the table. He was sad and hungry. Pup's mom saw him and said, "Pup, if you are patient, you will get your food." Later, Pup's mom gave him his food. Pup was so happy! He learned that being patient is good. He did not want to be foolish anymore. From that day on, Pup always waited for his food and did what he was told. And Pup and his mom lived happily ever after.

Ben and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big bird with black wings and a red head. It was a crow. Ben wanted to catch the crow and keep it as a pet. He ran after the crow and threw a stick at it. "Stop, Ben!" Sam shouted. "You are hurting the crow! It does not want to be your pet. It wants to fly and be free. You should mind the crow's feelings." But Ben did not listen. He kept chasing the crow and throwing sticks at it. The crow was scared and angry. It flew away from Ben and landed on a branch. Then it saw Sam, who was sitting on a bench. The crow flew down and pecked Sam's ear. "Ow!" Sam cried. "Why did you do that, crow? I did not hurt you. I tried to help you." The crow cawed and said, "You are Ben's brother. You are the same. You do not mind the crow's feelings. You only want to play with the crow. You do not care about the crow's life. The crow is not a toy. The crow is a living being." Sam felt sorry and ashamed. He realized that the crow was right. He had not minded the crow's feelings. He had only wanted to play with the crow. He had not cared about the crow's life. He said, "I am sorry, crow. You are not a toy. You are a living being. I will not bother you again. I will mind your feelings. Please forgive me." The crow looked at Sam and saw that he was sincere. The crow said, "I forgive you, Sam. You are not the same as Ben. You have a good heart. You can learn to mind the crow's feelings. You can be a friend to the crow. But you have to teach Ben to mind the crow's feelings too. He has to learn to respect the crow's life. He has to stop hurting the crow." Sam nodded and said, "I will try, crow. I will try to teach Ben to mind your feelings. I will try to make him respect your life. I will try to make him stop hurting you. Thank you for forgiving me. Thank you for being my friend." The crow smiled and said, "You are welcome, Sam. You are welcome to be my friend. But remember, the crow is not a pet. The crow is a free bird. The crow has black wings and a red head. The crow can fly and be happy." The end. Moral value: We should mind the feelings of other living beings and respect their lives. We should not hurt them or treat them as toys. We should be kind and friendly to them.
