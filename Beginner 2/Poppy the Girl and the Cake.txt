Category: Beginner
Title: Poppy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a girl named Poppy wanted to bake a cake. She went to the kitchen to find yummy things to put in it. Poppy found sugar, eggs, and flour. Poppy mixed all the yummy things in a big bowl. She put the mix in the oven to bake. Poppy was very happy and waited for the cake to be done. But Poppy forgot to turn off the oven. The cake burned and turned black. Poppy was sad because she could not eat the yummy cake. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to write. One day, he found a fragile, old book. It was very thin. It had only one word on each page. Tim wanted to add more words to the book. He asked his mom, "Can I write in this book?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can write in the book. But be careful. The book is fragile." Tim was happy. He wrote many words in the book. He was very careful. But then, something unexpected happened. The words he wrote came to life! A cat jumped out of the book. Then a dog, a bird, and a frog came out too. The animals made a big mess in the house. Tim's mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, you should not have written in the book. Now we have a big mess to clean up." Tim was sad. He did not know that writing in the book would make the animals come to life. He wished he had not written in the fragile book. The bad ending made him learn to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy shop where he would sell toys to other kids. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tim's shop. She was looking for a toy to play with, but she felt helpless because she did not have any money. "Can you help me?" Sue asked Tim. "I want to buy a toy, but I have no money." Tim wanted to help Sue, so he thought of a plan. "You can help me sell toys," he said. "If you help me, I will give you a toy to play with." Sue agreed to help Tim, and they started selling toys together. They sold cars, dolls, and even toys that could sit on their shoulders like little friends. As they worked together, Sue felt happy and not helpless anymore. At the end of the day, Tim gave Sue a toy as a thank you for her help. Sue picked a cute toy that could sit on her shoulder, and they became best friends. Tim and Sue continued to sell toys and help each other, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a mysterious armchair. This armchair was very special. It was big, soft, and had a magic power. One day, a little girl named Lucy found the armchair. She sat down and said, "Wow, this is a nice chair!" When she sat, the armchair began to spread. It became bigger and bigger, just like magic. Lucy's friend Tom came into the room and saw the big armchair. He said, "What happened to the chair?" Lucy smiled and said, "It's a magic chair! When I sit, it spreads and becomes big!" They both laughed and played on the mysterious armchair all day long.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play in the garden with their dog, Spot. One day, they see a big yellow squash growing on a vine. They want to pick it and eat it. "Look, Max, a squash!" Lily says. "It is so big and yellow!" "Can we have it for lunch?" Max asks. "I like squash. It is sweet and soft." They run to the squash and try to pull it off the vine. But it is too heavy and stuck. They tug and tug, but it does not move. "Help, Spot!" Lily calls. "Help us get the squash!" Spot hears Lily and Max. He wakes up from his nap under the tree. He runs to them and barks. He sees the squash and thinks it is a ball. He bites it and shakes it. He pulls it off the vine and runs away with it. "Spot, no!" Lily and Max shout. "That is our squash! Come back!" They chase Spot around the garden. They try to catch him and get the squash. But Spot is too fast and playful. He dodges and jumps and rolls. He makes the squash dirty and wet. "Spot, stop!" Lily and Max say. "You are ruining the squash!" Spot stops and looks at them. He sees their sad faces. He feels sorry. He drops the squash and wags his tail. He licks their hands and faces. "I'm sorry, Lily and Max," Spot says. "I thought it was a ball. I just wanted to play." Lily and Max hug Spot. They forgive him. They look at the squash. It is still big and yellow, but also dirty and wet. "Maybe we can still eat it," Lily says. "We can wash it and cook it." "Or maybe we can make something else with it," Max says. "Like a pumpkin pie or a lantern." They laugh and smile. They pick up the squash and go to the house. They are happy. They love Spot. They love squash. They love each other.
