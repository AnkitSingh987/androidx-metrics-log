Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day long. One day, Fin saw a big land far away. He wanted to see what was there. So, he swam and swam until he reached the land. On the land, Fin met a big, comfortable turtle. The turtle said, "Hello, Fin! What are you doing here?" Fin said, "I wanted to see the land. But I miss swimming in the water." The turtle smiled and said, "I can help you swim again." The turtle carried Fin on his back and went into the water. Together, they swam and swam. Fin was so happy to be back in the water. The turtle and Fin became best friends. They swam and played every day, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big board in his yard. He thought it was silly to have a board just lying there. Tim had an idea to make a fun game with the board. Tim put the board on two rocks and made a seesaw. He called his friend, Lily, to play with him. They took turns sitting on each end of the board and pushing off the ground. They laughed as they went up and down. Lily said, "This is fun, but I want to try something new." Tim insisted, "No, let's keep playing on the board." Lily agreed and they continued to play on the seesaw. They had a great time being silly together on the big board.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cord. It was special because it could squeeze things together. One day, a 3 year old was playing with it. She held it up and said, “Look, mama! I have the famous cord!” Her mother said, “That’s nice, honey. But be careful! Don’t squeeze it too hard.” But the 3 year old didn’t listen. She kept squeezing the cord and suddenly it snapped! “Mama, I broke it!” she cried. Her mother was very disappointed and said, “I told you not to squeeze it too hard. Now the famous cord is gone.” The 3 year old was sad and cried for a long time. She had learned her lesson: always listen to her mother!

Ben and Mia were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big puddle near the swings. They wanted to splash in the water. "Let's go to the puddle!" Ben said. "OK!" Mia said. They ran to the puddle and took off their shoes. They jumped in the water and laughed. The water was cold and wet. They felt happy. But then they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a lot of water coming from the hill. It was a flood! The water was fast and wild. It was coming to the park. "Run!" Ben said. "Help!" Mia said. They tried to run away from the water. But they were too slow. The water caught them and pushed them. They were scared and cold. They cried for help. A man saw them and ran to them. He tried to grab them and pull them out of the water. But the water was too strong. It slapped the man and made him fall. He lost his grip on Ben and Mia. They were swept away by the water. The man got up and shouted for help. But no one came. He was sad and sorry. He could not save Ben and Mia. They were gone.

One day, a mighty lion was in the forest. He liked to shout very loud. All the animals were scared of his big mouth. The lion felt happy when he saw the animals run away. A tiny mouse came to the lion and said, "Please do not shout. It scares us." The lion laughed and said, "I am mighty, and I like to shout. Go away, little mouse." The mouse felt sad but went away. Later, the lion got caught in a net. He tried to shout, but no one came to help. The tiny mouse heard him and came to see. The mouse used his small teeth to free the lion. The lion said, "Thank you, little mouse. I will not shout anymore." From that day, the lion and the mouse were friends, and the forest was a happy place.
