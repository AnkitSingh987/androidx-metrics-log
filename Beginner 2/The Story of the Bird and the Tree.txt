Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird. The bird wanted to create a new home. It looked for a good tree with a tight spot to build a nest. The bird found the perfect tree and started to build its nest. The bird worked very hard to make the nest. It put sticks, leaves, and grass in the tight spot. Soon, the nest had a strong roof to keep the bird safe and dry when it rained. The bird was very happy with its new home. One day, a little, lost squirrel came to the tree. The squirrel was cold and wet from the rain. The bird saw the squirrel and felt sad. The bird invited the squirrel to come under the roof of the nest. They became good friends and lived happily in the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little person named Tim. Tim was a kind and friendly person. He liked to play with his ball in the park. One day, while he was playing, he saw a big, rough rock. The rock looked sad and lonely. Tim had an idea. He wanted to lend his ball to the rock. So, he rolled his ball to the rock and said, "Here, rock, you can play with my ball." The rock did not move, but Tim thought it was happy. Every day, Tim would go to the park and lend his ball to the rock. They became good friends. Tim learned that even a rough rock could be a nice friend. And the rock was happy to have someone to play with.

Once upon a time in a warm, sunny forest, there lived a small squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, jump from tree to tree, and eat nuts. One day, while Sam was looking for nuts, he met a big bear named Ben. "Hello, little squirrel," said Ben. "What are you doing?" "I am trying to gain more nuts for winter," said Sam. "But I can't find any." Ben wanted to help Sam. So, they went on a nut hunt together. They looked under leaves, climbed trees, and searched the ground. Finally, they found a big pile of nuts. "Wow! Look at all these nuts!" said Sam. "We gained so many!" Sam and Ben were very happy. They shared the nuts and became the best of friends. From that day on, they always helped each other and played together in the warm, sunny forest.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little squash. The squash had a friend named Tom. Tom was a small boy who was eager to play with the squash every day. They liked to roll and jump in the sun. One day, while they were playing, a big dog came into the garden. The dog saw the squash and wanted to play too. But the dog played too rough and made the squash scared. The squash yelled, "Help, Tom! Help!" Tom heard the squash yell and came running. He saw the dog and said, "Stop, dog! You are scaring my friend!" The dog stopped and looked at Tom. Tom picked up the squash and hugged it tight. The squash was happy to be safe with Tom again. From that day on, they played together and had lots of fun in the big garden.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big letter on the floor. She picked it up and saw it was for her friend Tom. Lily wanted to give the letter to Tom, so she went to his house. When she got to Tom's house, she saw him playing with a toy that could spin. "Look, Lily!" Tom said, "My toy can spin!" Lily watched the toy spin round and round. She clapped her hands and laughed. Lily then remembered the letter. "Tom, I have a letter for you," she said. Tom took the letter and opened it. Inside, it said, "I'm sorry I can't come to play today." Tom felt a little sad. But then, he smiled and said, "That's okay, Lily. We can still play together!" So Lily and Tom spent the day playing with the spinning toy and having lots of fun.
