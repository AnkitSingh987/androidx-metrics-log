Category: Beginner
Title: The Clean Razor and the Tom

One day, Tom saw a clean razor. He did not know what it was. He thought it was a toy. He wanted to play with it. He took the razor and went to his friend, Ann. Tom said, "Look, Ann! I found a new toy!" Ann looked at the razor and said, "No, Tom! That is not a toy. It is a razor. It can hurt you. You must return it." Tom did not listen to Ann. He wanted to play with the razor. He ran away from Ann and played with the razor. But, the razor hurt Tom's hand. He cried and cried. Ann came to help Tom. She said, "I told you to return the razor. Now, you are hurt." Tom was sad that he did not listen to Ann. He learned that he should always listen to his friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to a park. She saw a pastel pink ball near a tree. Lily stared at the ball and said, "Wow! Pretty ball!" A boy named Tim came and said, "That's my ball, but it's fake." Lily looked at Tim and asked, "What do you mean fake?" Tim said, "It's not a real ball. It can't bounce." Lily and Tim stared at the ball. They kicked it, but it didn't bounce. They were sad. Suddenly, the ball started to grow bigger and bigger. It became a big pastel pink balloon! Lily and Tim looked at each other and smiled. They held the big balloon and played in the park all day. The fake ball was a fun balloon, and they had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a big maze with his mom. They walked and walked for a long time. Tim started to feel tired, but he wanted to find the end of the maze. As they turned a corner, they saw a funny clown. The clown said, "Hello, I am Mr. Giggles! I can help you find the end of the maze if you can make me laugh." Tim thought for a moment and then made a silly face. Mr. Giggles laughed so hard, he fell on the ground. "Thank you, Tim!" said Mr. Giggles. He showed them a secret door in the maze. When they opened the door, they were back at the start of the maze! Tim and his mom laughed and laughed. They had a fun day, even if they didn't find the end of the maze.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He had a very big lake. In the lake he could pick many things. One day he was picking something very special. He thought it was so beautiful. Suddenly, a bad man came. He wanted the boy to give him the special thing he was picking. But the boy said no. The bad man was very angry. He said, "If you don't give it to me I'm going to do something very stupid". The boy was scared. He said, "Please don't do something stupid. I'll give it to you". So he handed the thing to the bad man. The bad man was so happy. He laughed and said, "Ha, ha! Now I can do something very stupid with this". He laughed and went away. The boy was so sad. He had lost the special thing that he had been picking from the lake. He realised that he shouldn't have been so stupid.

John wanted to be rich. So one day he decided to steal from a cave. "John, why are you going to do that?" asked his friend. John replied, "I want to make lots of money, so I'm going to steal from the cave and make myself rich!" His friend was worried and tried to talk him out of it, but John was determined. John set off to the cave early the next morning. But when he finally reached the cave, he encountered a great big dragon! John was very scared. The dragon looked at him angrily and said, "Why are you here? You are not allowed to steal from this cave!" John ran back to his friend as fast as he could. He was very relieved to be back in one piece. John had learned his lesson and knew it was wrong to try and steal. John never tried to make himself rich that way again. He realised that it was better to work hard and earn money in honest ways. He was very pleased that he'd listened to his friend and avoided the dragon.
