Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Magic

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree with purple fruit on it. Sue wanted to pick the fruit, but she was too small. She saw a tall boy named Tom near the tree. Sue asked Tom, "Can you help me pick the purple fruit?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." He reached up and picked a purple fruit for Sue. But when he gave it to her, the fruit started to talk! The fruit said, "Please don't eat me! I can explain why you shouldn't." Sue and Tom were very surprised. They listened to the purple fruit. The fruit told them that it was magic. If they planted the fruit, a special tree would grow. The tree would give them more fruit that could make their wishes come true. Sue and Tom decided to plant the magic fruit. They dug a hole, put the fruit in, and covered it with dirt. They waited and waited. Soon, a new tree grew with lots of purple fruit. Sue and Tom were very happy. They picked the fruit and made their wishes. And all their wishes came true.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved ponies very much. One day, she saw a pony in a field. The pony was very happy and enthusiastic. Lily wanted to say hello to the pony. Lily walked to the pony and said, "Hi, pony! I like you!" The pony looked at Lily and said, "Hi, Lily! I like you too!" They played together in the field. They ran and jumped and had fun. But then, the sky got dark and it started to rain. Lily and the pony got wet and cold. They tried to find a place to hide from the rain, but they couldn't. Lily said, "Bye, pony! I have to go home!" The pony said, "Bye, Lily! I will miss you!" Lily went home and the pony stayed in the field. They both felt sad because they couldn't play together anymore.

Tom and Mia liked to play with blocks. They built a big stand with many blocks. It was impressive. They put cars and animals on the stand. They were happy. But then Ben came. Ben was not nice. He wanted to play with the blocks too. He did not ask Tom and Mia. He just grabbed some blocks and threw them away. He laughed. "Stop it, Ben!" Tom said. "You are destroying our stand!" "No, I am not!" Ben said. "I am making it better!" Ben took more blocks and threw them harder. The stand fell down. The cars and animals fell too. Tom and Mia were sad. They cried. "Look what you did, Ben!" Mia said. "You ruined our stand!" Ben did not care. He ran away. He was mean. Tom and Mia hugged each other. They picked up the blocks and the cars and the animals. They decided to build another stand. They hoped Ben would not come back. They were brave.

One day, a little girl named Lucy and her dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a big, cold cave and decided to enter. Inside the cave, it was very dark and cold. Lucy held Max's paw so they would not get lost. As they walked deeper into the cave, they heard a strange noise. "What is that noise, Max?" Lucy asked, feeling a little scared. Max barked and wagged his tail, letting Lucy know that everything was okay. They followed the noise and found a small, shivering bird. "Hello, bird. Are you cold?" Lucy asked. The bird nodded and made the noise again. Lucy took off her warm scarf and wrapped it around the bird. The bird was happy and flew away. Lucy and Max left the cave and went back home, feeling good about helping their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a fair and sunny land, there was a big oak tree. The oak tree was very tall and strong. Many animals lived in the oak tree, like birds, squirrels, and bugs. One day, a little bird named Timmy was playing with his friends near the oak tree. They were playing a game of catch. Timmy was very fast and could fly high. But, he saw a mean cat coming near them. The cat wanted to catch the birds. Timmy said to his friends, "Quick, let's go to the oak tree! The cat can't catch us there!" So, they all flew to the oak tree and hid in its branches. The cat tried to climb the tree, but it was too hard for him. Timmy's mom saw everything and said, "Timmy, you did a good job. You saved your friends by ordering them to go to the oak tree. I am proud of you." From that day on, Timmy and his friends always played near the oak tree. They knew it was a safe place to be. And they all lived happily in the fair and sunny land.
