Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Toys

Sara and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Sara had a big case full of dolls and dresses. Ben had a small box with cars and trucks. Sara wanted to play with Ben's cars, but Ben did not want to share. He said, "No, these are my cars. You have your dolls. Go away!" Sara was very sad and angry. She was also impatient. She did not want to wait for Ben to change his mind. She thought, "I will take his cars when he is not looking. He will not know. He is so mean and selfish." She waited for Ben to go to the kitchen to get a drink. Then she ran to his box and grabbed some cars. She put them in her case and closed it. Ben came back and saw that some of his cars were gone. He looked around and saw Sara's case. He had a bad feeling. He said, "Sara, did you take my cars? Open your case and show me!" Sara did not want to open her case. She said, "No, I did not take your cars. This is my case. You cannot see it. Go away!" Ben was very sad and angry. He was also impatient. He did not want to listen to Sara. He thought, "She is lying. She took my cars. She is so mean and selfish." He pushed Sara and tried to open her case. Sara pushed him back and tried to keep her case closed. They started to fight and yell. Mom heard the noise and came to the living room. She saw Sara and Ben fighting over the case. She was very upset. She said, "Stop it, both of you! What is going on? Why are you fighting?" Sara and Ben both tried to tell their side of the story. Mom did not believe either of them. She said, "You are both wrong. You are both greedy and rude. You do not know how to share and be kind. You need to learn how to yield and take turns. You need to say sorry and give back what is not yours. If you do not, I will take away all your toys and put them in the closet. Do you understand?" Sara and Ben understood. They felt ashamed and sorry. They said, "Yes, Mom, we understand. We are sorry. We will share and be kind. We will yield and take turns." They opened the case and gave back the cars. They hugged each other and said, "I am sorry. I love you." Mom smiled and said, "That's better. I love you too. Now you can play nicely together. But remember, if you fight again, I will keep my word and take away your toys. Do you promise?" Sara and Ben promised. They learned their lesson. They played nicely together. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a yellow man. He was not like other men. His skin was as bright as the sun. The yellow man lived in a little house with a big door. One day, the yellow man went for a walk. He saw a little girl playing with a ball. The little girl looked at the yellow man and said, "Hi! Why are you yellow?" The yellow man smiled and said, "I was born this way. I like being yellow. It makes me special." The little girl and the yellow man became good friends. They played with the ball all day. When it was time to go home, the yellow man said, "Let's play again tomorrow!" The little girl nodded and said, "Yes, let's!" The yellow man walked to his little house and closed the big door. They were both very happy to have a new friend.

One day, a little dog named Bow went for a walk. Bow was a happy dog who liked to play and run. He saw a big box by the road. The box had mail in it. Bow was very curious about the mail. Bow looked at the mail and saw a picture of a big, scary monster. Bow was frightened by the monster. He thought the monster would come out of the mail and chase him. Bow ran away as fast as he could. Bow ran all the way back to his house. He told his mom about the scary monster. His mom laughed and said, "Don't be scared, Bow. That's just a picture on the mail." Bow felt better and laughed too. He was happy that the monster was not real, and it was just a picture.
