Category: Beginner
Title: Peter and the Sun

Once upon a time there were two friends. One was called Peter and the other one was called Lucy. They always liked to stay together and have fun. One day they went to the beach. It was a mild day and the sun was shining. When they got to the beach they wanted to swim. They both took off their shoes and ran into the water. The sea was so nice and warm. They liked it so much they decided to stay and swim for a long time. They splashed each other and made a lot of noise! Then they had a rest and lay on the sand. They talked and laughed until it started to get dark. The sun went down, but Peter and Lucy stayed and watched the stars. Then Lucy said it was time for them to go home. They gave each other a hug and promised to come back soon. The end!

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big pipe in the grass. The pipe is long and dark. Tom and Lily want to see what is inside. "Let's travel in the pipe!" Tom says. "Maybe we can find a new place." "OK, but be careful," Lily says. "The pipe might be dirty or scary." They hold hands and walk into the pipe. They see some bugs and some trash. They hear some water and some wind. They keep walking until they see some light. They come out of the pipe and see a big house. The house is red and blue and yellow. It has many windows and doors. It looks like a toy house. "Wow, look at that house!" Tom says. "It is so pretty and fun. Can we go inside?" Lily shakes her head. "No, Tom. That house is fake. It is not a real house. It is a house for a movie. See, there are some people with cameras and lights. They are making a movie." Tom feels sad. He wanted to see a real house. He wanted to play in a real house. He says, "Oh, I see. That house is fake. It is not for us. Let's go back to the park." They turn around and walk back to the pipe. They travel in the pipe again. They come out of the pipe and see the park. They see the trees and the flowers and the swings. They see their moms and dads waiting for them. They run to their moms and dads and hug them. They tell them about the pipe and the fake house. Their moms and dads smile and listen. They say, "You had a big adventure today. You are very brave and curious. But next time, be more careful. Don't go into strange pipes. They might be dangerous." Tom and Lily nod. They say, "OK, we will be more careful. But we had fun. We like to travel and see new things." They play in the park some more. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. The sky was dark and the wind was strong. Lily saw a big storm coming. She was scared and wanted to go home. Lily met a kind bird, who said, "Don't be scared. When the storm comes, just turn your back to it." Lily listened to the bird and when the storm came, she turned her back to it. The storm was loud, but Lily was brave. After the storm, the sun came out and Lily saw a beautiful rainbow. She learned that when things are scary, it's okay to turn away and be brave. And after the storm, there is always something beautiful waiting.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing outside. He saw a big, silly bird. The bird had a towel in its beak. Max wanted to play with the towel too. So, he ran after the bird. Max said, "Hey, silly bird! Can I play with the towel too?" The bird stopped and turned to Max. The bird said, "Yes, you can play with the towel. Let's play together!" Max and the bird played with the towel. They took turns running with the towel in their mouths. They had a lot of fun. Then, it was time for Max to go home. Max said, "Thank you, silly bird, for playing with me. I had fun!" The bird said, "You're welcome, Max. Let's play again soon!" And they both went their own way, happy and tired from their fun day.
