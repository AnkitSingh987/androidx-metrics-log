Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Tree

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. They saw a big tree with a hole. They wanted to see what was inside. "Maybe there are birds or squirrels," Lily said. "Or maybe there is treasure," Ben said. They went closer to the hole. They heard a loud buzzing sound. They saw many yellow and black bugs flying out of the hole. They were bees! "Run!" Lily shouted. They ran away from the tree. The bees followed them. They were angry. They wanted to protect their honey. The honey was in the hole. The honey was sweet and sticky. Lily and Ben ran to the house. They closed the door. The bees could not get in. They flew around the window. They made more buzzing sounds. "Are they gone?" Ben asked. "I don't know," Lily said. "They are still outside." They waited for a long time. The sun went down. It was dark. The bees went back to their tree. They were tired. They wanted to sleep. Lily and Ben looked out of the window. They saw the stars. They saw the moon. They saw no bees. "They are gone," Lily said. "Good," Ben said. "Let's not go near the tree again." "OK," Lily said. "But I still wonder what the honey tastes like." The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a small tree with pretty flowers. He wanted to pick some flowers for his mom. So, Tim went to the tree and picked one flower. "This flower is nice, but I want more," he thought. Tim looked around and saw a big flower on a high branch. He knew he could not reach it. "I need help," he thought. Tim went to his dog, Max. Max was an obedient dog. Tim said, "Max, help me get the big flower!" Max barked and wagged his tail. Tim stood on Max's back to get the big flower. He reached up and picked the flower. It was big, pretty, and smelled nice. Tim was happy. He gave the flowers to his mom, and she loved them. Tim knew that with a little help, he could do anything.

One day, a little frog named Tim went to the airport with his mom. Tim was very excited to see the big planes. He liked to watch them go up in the sky. Tim was deaf, so he could not hear the loud noises the planes made. But he loved to see them fly. At the airport, Tim saw a big bird named Bob. Bob was sad because he could not fly like the planes. Tim wanted to help his new friend. "Why don't you try to spring like me?" Tim asked Bob. Bob looked at Tim and thought it was a good idea. Bob tried to spring like Tim, but he still could not fly like the planes. Tim and Bob felt sad. Then, Tim's mom had an idea. She found a big leaf and told Bob to hold on to it. Tim and Bob held the leaf together and started to spring. With each spring, they went higher and higher. Soon, they were flying like the planes. Tim and Bob were very happy. They learned that with a little help, they could do anything.

Sara and Ben are playing in the attic. They find a big box full of old things. They open the box and see many books, toys, and clothes. "Look, Sara, a doll!" Ben says. He picks up a doll with a blue dress and yellow hair. "She is very pretty." Sara nods. She sees a book with a picture of a dragon on the cover. She likes dragons. She takes the book and opens it. "Wow, a story about a dragon!" she says. "Can we read it?" Ben agrees. He puts the doll next to him and looks at the book with Sara. They start to read the story. It is about a dragon who lives in a cave and likes to eat dust. Dust makes him strong and happy. He does not like to eat anything else. He is very original. One day, a knight comes to the cave. He wants to fight the dragon and take his treasure. The dragon does not want to fight. He likes his treasure. He tries to talk to the knight. "Please, go away. I do not want to hurt you. I just want to eat dust and be happy," the dragon says. But the knight does not listen. He draws his sword and attacks the dragon. The dragon has to defend himself. He breathes fire and roars. The knight is scared and runs away. The dragon is sad. He does not like to fight. He wishes he had a friend who would understand him and eat dust with him. Sara and Ben feel sorry for the dragon. They think he is very nice and original. They wish they could meet him and be his friends. They finish the book and hug each other. They decide to keep the book and the doll. They think they are very special and original too. They close the box and go downstairs. They tell their mom about the book and the doll. Their mom smiles and hugs them. She says they can keep them. She says they are very original and she loves them.
