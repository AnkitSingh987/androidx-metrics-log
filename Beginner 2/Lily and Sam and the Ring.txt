Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Sam and the Ring

Lily and Sam were playing in the park. They saw a big fire in a trash can. Smoke and ash came out of the fire. Lily wanted to go closer and see the fire. Sam was scared of the fire. "Let's go see the fire, Sam!" Lily urged. She pulled Sam's hand and ran to the trash can. "No, Lily, no! Fire is hot and bad. It can hurt us. We should stay away from the fire." Sam said. He tried to pull Lily back, but she was stronger. They reached the trash can. The fire was very big and loud. It made crackling noises. Lily saw something shiny in the fire. It was a thin metal ring. Lily liked shiny things. She reached out her hand to grab the ring. "Stop, Lily, stop! Don't touch the fire! It will burn you!" Sam shouted. He pushed Lily away from the fire. Lily fell on the grass. She dropped the ring. The ring was very hot. It made a hissing sound on the grass. Lily started to cry. She was angry and sad. She wanted the shiny ring. She did not like Sam. She did not listen to Sam. Sam was sorry and scared. He wanted to help Lily. He wanted to be her friend. He listened to Sam. He hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I pushed you because I care about you. Fire is dangerous. It can make you sick or hurt. The ring is not worth it. You are more important than the ring. Please don't be mad at me. Please be my friend." Lily stopped crying. She looked at Sam. She saw his kind eyes and his worried face. She felt his warm hug. She realized he was right. Fire was bad. Sam was good. She was silly and brave. Sam was smart and careful. She needed Sam. She liked Sam. She hugged Sam back and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I was wrong. You saved me from the fire. You are a good friend. I'm glad you are here. I don't need the ring. I have you." They smiled at each other and held hands. They walked away from the fire. They saw a firefighter coming to put out the fire. They waved at him and said, "Thank you, firefighter. You are a hero." The firefighter smiled and said, "You're welcome, kids. You are brave and smart. You stayed away from the fire. You helped each other. You are good friends."

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play together. One day, they found a box of matches in the kitchen. They were very curious and wanted to see what would happen if they lit them. "Let's go to the garden and make a fire," Tom whispered to Lily. "But don't tell Mommy, she will be angry." Lily nodded and followed Tom to the garden. They hid behind a big bush and took out some matches. Tom struck one and it made a bright spark. He threw it on some dry leaves and they started to burn. "Wow, look at the fire!" Lily said. "It's so pretty and warm." They lit more matches and threw them on the fire. The fire grew bigger and bigger. They did not see the smoke rising in the air. Soon, Mommy smelled the smoke and ran to the garden. She saw the fire and the twins behind the bush. She was very scared and angry. "Lily! Tom! What are you doing?" she shouted. "You are very naughty! You could have burned the house or hurt yourselves!" She grabbed a bucket of water and poured it on the fire. The fire went out with a hiss. She took the twins by the hand and brought them inside. "I'm sorry, Mommy," Tom said. "We just wanted to play." "We didn't know it was bad," Lily said. Mommy hugged them and said, "I'm glad you are safe, but you should never play with matches or fire. They are very dangerous and can cause a lot of damage. You have to promise me you will never do that again." The twins nodded and said, "We promise, Mommy." Mommy kissed them and said, "I love you, but you have to be careful and listen to me. Now go to your room and think about what you did." The twins went to their room and felt sorry for what they did. They learned their lesson and never played with fire again.
