Category: Beginner
Title: Moony and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a smooth, round ball named Moony. Moony was very special because he looked just like the moon in the sky. He was friends with a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy and Moony were playing in the park. They were having so much fun. Lucy threw Moony up in the air and caught him. "Moony, you're my best friend!" Lucy said. Moony was happy to hear that. But then, Lucy threw Moony too high. She could not catch him. Moony got lost in a big tree. Lucy looked up and saw Moony stuck in the tree. "Oh no!" she cried. "I lost Moony!" Lucy tried to climb the tree, but it was too tall. She asked her mom for help. Her mom got a long stick and carefully got Moony out of the tree. Lucy hugged Moony tight and said, "I'm so glad I found you!" From that day on, Lucy and Moony were always careful when they played. They didn't want to lose each other again. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an honest boy who loved to wander in the woods near his house. One day, he saw a big frog near a pond. The frog told Tim, "I am very sad because there is poison in the pond. All my frog friends are getting sick." Tim wanted to help the frog and his friends, so he thought of a plan. Tim went home and talked to his mom. She gave him a big bucket. Tim took the bucket and went back to the pond. He worked hard to take the poison out of the pond. The frogs were happy and thanked Tim for being a good and honest friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, expensive yacht. It was very pretty. A man named Bob owned the yacht. He loved to sail on the water with his friends. One sunny day, Bob and his friends went on the yacht. They saw a big stick on the water. Bob picked it up and said, "This stick can help us later." His friends laughed. Later, the wind stopped. The yacht could not move. Bob's friends were sad. But Bob had an idea. He took the big stick and put it in the water. He pushed the yacht with the stick. The yacht started to move again. Bob's friends clapped and cheered. The big stick saved the day!

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Lily. Lily loved to run and zoom all around the yard. She liked to chase the birds and play with her ball. One sunny day, Lily saw a big shock in the yard. It was a new dog named Max. Max was big and had a loud bark. Lily was scared, but she wanted to play with Max. Lily slowly walked to Max, and they started to play. They ran and zoomed all over the yard, having fun. Max was not scary anymore. Now, Lily and Max were best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play. He saw a file on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It had a picture of a cat on it. Tim liked cats. Tim showed the file to his friend, Sally. Sally looked at the file too. She said, "I think the cat is fake." Tim looked again and saw that the cat was not real. It was just a drawing. Tim and Sally laughed. They liked the fake cat on the file. They played with the file all day, pretending it was a real cat. They had a lot of fun with their new fake cat friend.

Sara and Ben were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and cars and books. They had a club in the backyard. The club was a big box with a door and a window. They painted the box with many colors and put stickers on it. One day, Sara and Ben had a fight. Sara wanted to play with the pink doll, but Ben wanted to play with it too. They both pulled the doll and it broke. Sara and Ben were very sad and angry. They said mean words to each other and went to their own houses. The next day, Sara and Ben missed each other. They wanted to play in the club again, but they did not know how to say sorry. They were both scared and shy. Then, Sara had a creative idea. She made a card with a heart and a smiley face. She wrote "I forgive you" and "I love you" with her crayons. She put the card in the club and waited. Ben saw the card when he went to the club. He felt happy and sorry. He made a card with a star and a hug. He wrote "I forgive you" and "I love you" with his markers. He put the card in the club and waited. Sara and Ben found each other's cards and smiled. They hugged and said sorry. They played with the dolls and the cars and the books. They were best friends again. They were happy in their club.
