Category: Beginner
Title: The Tough Man and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a tough man. He had a big box. In the box, he had many nails. The tough man was proud to own the nails. One day, the tough man went to his box to get a nail. But when he opened the box, the nails were gone! He was sad and confused. He looked around the house for the nails, but he could not find them. Then, he saw his pet cat. The cat had a shiny nail in its mouth. The tough man laughed. The cat did not eat the nails, it just wanted to play! The tough man was happy to find his nails and played with the cat. They had fun and the tough man was not sad anymore.

---

One day, a wise old cat saw a little bird by a pond. The bird was thirsty and wanted to drink water. The cat wanted to have fun and play with the bird. "Little bird, I will help you drink," said the wise cat. The bird was scared, but the cat looked kind. The bird let the cat help it. The cat put its paw in the water and let the bird drink from it. The bird was happy and they both had fun. But then, a big dog came and saw them. The dog wanted to catch the bird too. The wise cat quickly jumped on the dog's back and said, "Let's play, dog!" The dog was surprised and forgot about the bird. The bird flew away to safety. The cat and the dog played together and became friends. The moral of the story is to be kind and help others, even when it is unexpected.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a small, gentle cat in his closet. The cat was scared and hungry. Tim felt sad for the cat and wanted to help it. He gave the cat some food and water, and they became friends. Tim said to the cat, "Don't be scared, little cat. I will take care of you." The cat looked at Tim and meowed softly. They played together all day and had lots of fun. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam was not gentle with the cat and pulled its tail. The cat was scared and ran away. Tim told Sam, "You should be gentle with animals. They have feelings too." Sam felt bad and said sorry to the cat. He learned to be gentle with animals, and they all played happily together. Moral: Always be gentle with animals and treat them with kindness.

Tom and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with cars and balls and books. One day, they found a big box in the park. It had many old things inside. They saw a hat, a map, a coin, and a medal. "Wow, look at these!" Tom said. "They are from history. They are very old and special. I want them all!" "No, Tom, you can't have them all!" Sam said. "We have to share. Maybe someone else needs them. They are not ours." Tom did not listen. He grabbed the hat, the map, the coin, and the medal. He ran away from Sam. He was very happy. He thought he was successful. But Sam was very sad. He felt lonely and angry. He followed Tom to his house. He knocked on the door. He wanted to talk to Tom. "Tom, please open the door!" Sam said. "We are friends. We have to be nice to each other. You have to accept that you can't have everything. You have to give some things back." Tom heard Sam. He felt sorry. He knew he was wrong. He opened the door. He gave Sam the hat, the map, the coin, and the medal. He said sorry to Sam. "Sam, I am sorry. You are right. I was greedy and mean. I don't need these things. They are from history. They belong to someone else. I accept that I can't have everything. I want to share with you. Can we still be friends?" Sam smiled. He hugged Tom. He said yes. They were friends again. They decided to return the box to the park. They hoped someone would find it and learn from history. They learned that being successful is not about having everything, but about being kind and generous.
