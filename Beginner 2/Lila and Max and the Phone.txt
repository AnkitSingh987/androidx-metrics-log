Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Max and the Phone

Lila and Max were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big box in the garage. It had many old things inside. They saw a hat, a scarf, a book, and a toy phone. "Look, Max, a phone!" Lila said. She picked up the phone and put it to her ear. She pretended to talk to someone. "Hello, this is Lila. How are you?" Max was envious. He wanted the phone too. He tried to grab it from Lila. "No, it's mine!" Lila said. She pulled the phone back. They started to fight over the phone. "Stop it, you two!" their mom said. She heard the noise and came to the garage. She saw them tug and pull the phone. "You have to share. You can take turns with the phone." "But I want it now!" Max said. He was not happy. "How about this?" their mom said. She took the phone and part it in two. She gave one part to Lila and one part to Max. "Now you each have a phone. You can talk to each other." Lila and Max looked at the phone parts. They were not the same. Lila had the part with the ear and the mouth. Max had the part with the dial and the cord. They were not sure how to use them. "Hello, Max, can you hear me?" Lila said. She put the ear part to her ear and the mouth part to her mouth. Max tried to answer. He put the dial part to his ear and the cord part to his mouth. He could not hear anything. He could not say anything. "Max, are you there?" Lila said. She waited for a reply. Max was sad. He did not like his phone part. He wished he had the other part. He looked at Lila. She was smiling. She liked her phone part. He was envious. He decided to trade with Lila. He gave her his phone part and asked for hers. Lila agreed. She gave him her phone part and took his. They swapped the phone parts. "Hello, Lila, can you hear me?" Max said. He put the ear part to his ear and the mouth part to his mouth. "Hello, Max, I can hear you!" Lila said. She put the dial part to her ear and the cord part to her mouth. She could hear Max. She could say something. They were happy. They talked to each other with the phone parts. They did not fight anymore. They learned to share. Their mom was proud of them. She gave them a hug and a kiss. She said they were good twins.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a humble carrot. The carrot lived under the ground, with its roots deep in the soil. It was a very happy carrot. One day, a rabbit came to the garden. The rabbit saw the carrot's green leaves poking out of the ground. "Hello, carrot!" said the rabbit. "I am very hungry. Can I eat you?" The humble carrot did not want to be eaten. It thought about its roots and how it loved living in the garden. "No, rabbit," the carrot said. "I refuse to be eaten. I like my home in the ground." The rabbit was sad but understood the carrot's feelings. It hopped away to find another snack. The carrot was happy that it could stay in its home. The moral of the story is to be kind and understanding of others' feelings, and to stand up for what you love.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy kayak that he loved to play with. One day, he took his kayak to the park to play near the pond. While Tim was playing, he saw a hurt bird. The bird could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he put it in his kayak and took it home. His mom helped him take care of the bird until it was better. When the bird could fly again, Tim and his mom went back to the park. They let the bird go and watched it fly away. Tim was happy to see the bird fly, but he missed it. So, he took his kayak and buried it near the pond. Now, every time Tim goes to the park, he can remember the bird and the fun they had together.
