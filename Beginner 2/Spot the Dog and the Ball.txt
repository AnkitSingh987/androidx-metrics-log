Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a clean, red leash. One day, Spot and his friend, a girl named Sue, went for a walk in the park. As they walked, Spot saw a big, green ball. He wanted to play with it, but he did not recognize it. It looked different from his small, blue ball at home. Spot was curious and wanted to see what it was. Sue let Spot off his leash to play with the big, green ball. Suddenly, the big, green ball started to move! It was not a ball at all, but a big, green turtle! Spot and Sue were very surprised. They laughed and played with the turtle. Then, they put the leash back on Spot and went home, happy with their new friend.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy whip that he loved to play with. He would run around the house, whipping the air and making loud noises. One day, Tom's mom told him to stop playing with the whip inside the house. But Tom did not listen. He kept playing and demanded to keep the whip. His mom warned him that something bad might happen if he didn't stop. Tom ignored his mom's warning and kept playing with the whip. Suddenly, the whip hit a big glass cup on the table. The cup fell and broke into many pieces. Tom's mom was very sad and Tom felt bad. He knew he should have listened to his mom.

Sam and Mia liked to play with the big ball. The ball was round and red and bounced high. They took turns to pass the ball to each other and catch it. They laughed and had fun. One day, they saw a new ball in the park. The ball was a circle and gray and had a hole in it. Sam and Mia were curious and wanted to try it. They ran to the ball and picked it up. "Let's pass this ball," Sam said. "OK," Mia said. "But be careful. It looks strange." Sam threw the ball to Mia. But the ball did not fly straight. It spun and wobbled and made a hissing sound. Mia tried to catch it, but it slipped from her hands and hit her face. Mia screamed and fell to the ground. She felt a sharp pain and saw blood. Sam ran to Mia and saw her nose was bleeding. He was scared and sorry. He did not know the ball was bad. He did not mean to hurt Mia. "Mia, Mia, are you OK?" Sam asked. But Mia did not answer. She was too hurt and angry. She cried and cried and did not want to talk to Sam. She did not want to play with him anymore. The ball was a trap. Someone had put a needle inside it. Someone who did not like children. Someone who wanted to make them sad. Sam and Mia did not know that. They only wanted to have fun. But they found out too late. The ball ruined their game and their friendship. The ball was a bad circle.

Jen and Ben were twins who liked to play in the park. They had a regular ball, a regular bike and a regular kite. But they also had a secret. They had a peanut jar that they hid under a big rock. They loved peanuts, but their mom said they could not eat them because they were bad for them. One day, they went to the park with their mom. They played with their regular toys for a while, then they got bored. They looked at each other and whispered, "Let's go to the peanut jar!" They ran to the big rock and lifted it. They opened the peanut jar and started to eat the peanuts. They were crunchy and salty and yummy. But they did not see the big dog that was watching them. The dog smelled the peanuts and wanted some too. He ran to the twins and barked loudly. Jen and Ben got scared and dropped the peanut jar. The dog grabbed the jar and ran away with it. Jen and Ben cried and ran to their mom. Their mom saw them and asked, "What happened?" Jen and Ben told her the truth. They said they were sorry and they would not eat peanuts again. Their mom was angry and sad. She said, "You know you are allergic to peanuts. You could have gotten very sick. You were very naughty and careless. You have to go home now and no more park for a week." Jen and Ben felt bad. They had lost their peanut jar, their mom was mad at them, and they could not play in the park. They wished they had listened to their mom and not eaten the peanuts. They learned their lesson the hard way. It was a bad day.
