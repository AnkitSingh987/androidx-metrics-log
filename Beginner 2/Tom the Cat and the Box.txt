Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Box

One day, a playful cat named Tom saw a big box. The box was closed. Tom wanted to open the box. He jumped on the box and pushed it with his paws. The box did not open. Tom was sad. He asked his friend, a dog named Max, for help. "Max, can you help me open the box?" Tom asked. Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I will help you!" Max and Tom pushed the box together. The box started to shake. They did not know what was inside. Suddenly, the box opened with a loud "zoom" sound. Max and Tom were surprised. Out of the box came a big, colorful bird. The bird had been hiding in the box. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me! I was stuck in the box!" Tom, Max, and the bird became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a charming cat named Kitty found a newspaper on the floor. Kitty did not know what it was, so she went to her friend, Doggy. "Doggy, can you explain what this is?" Kitty asked, holding the newspaper. Doggy looked at it and said, "This is a newspaper. People read it to know what is happening." Kitty and Doggy sat down and tried to read the newspaper. But they did not understand the words. So, they decided to play with it instead. They had a lot of fun playing and laughing together. And that is how Kitty learned about newspapers.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Zoom. Zoom loved to move fast down the road. One day, Zoom saw a new road with a wire across it. Zoom wanted to move past the wire, but he was scared. He thought something terrible might happen. Zoom told his friend, a blue car named Beep, about the wire. Beep wanted to help Zoom. They thought and thought about what to do. Beep said, "Let's ask our big friend, Truck, to move the wire for us!" Zoom liked the idea, and they went to find Truck. Truck was very big and strong. Zoom and Beep told Truck about the terrible wire. Truck said, "I can help you!" Truck went to the wire and moved it away. Now, the road was safe for Zoom and Beep to drive on. Zoom and Beep were very happy, and they moved fast down the new road together.

One day, a jolly cat named Tom went to the beach. He had a big, soft towel with him. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Sam, playing by the water. Tom and Sam liked to talk and play together. Tom said, "Sam, I have an idea! Let's play a game. I will hide my towel, and you try to find it." Sam wagged his tail and said, "Okay, that sounds fun!" Tom hid the towel under a big pile of sand while Sam closed his eyes. Sam looked and looked for the towel. He dug in the sand with his paws. After a while, Sam found the towel! Tom and Sam laughed and talked about how much fun they had. They played more games and had a great day at the beach.

Tom is a dog. He likes to run and play and bark. He has a big yard and a soft bed. He is happy. One morning, Tom feels something tickle his ear. He shakes his head, but it does not go away. He scratches his ear, but it does not help. He feels more tickles on his back and his legs. He jumps up and looks at his fur. He sees tiny black dots. They are fleas. They bite and itch. He is not happy. Tom runs to his bowl and drinks some water. He hopes the water will make the fleas go away. But it does not. He runs to his bed and rolls on it. He hopes the bed will make the fleas go away. But it does not. He runs to his owner and whines. He hopes his owner will make the fleas go away. But his owner is not there. He is at work. Tom feels sad and lonely. He does not know what to do. He sees a big puddle in the yard. It rained last night. The puddle is wet and muddy. Tom has an idea. He runs to the puddle and jumps in it. He splashes and shakes and rubs his fur. He hopes the puddle will make the fleas go away. And it does. The fleas do not like the water. They fall off Tom and sink in the mud. Tom is happy. Tom gets out of the puddle and shakes his fur. He is wet and dirty, but he does not care. He feels no more tickles or bites or itches. He feels good. He sees his owner coming home. He runs to him and licks his face. His owner is surprised and laughs. He hugs Tom and says, "You are a silly dog. You need a bath. But I love you anyway." Tom wags his tail and barks. He loves his owner too. He is happy.
