Category: Beginner
Title: The Bird and the Cave

Once upon a time, in a big cave, there lived a bird. The bird loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sing happy songs. One day, a little boy found the cave. He heard the bird sing. The boy liked the bird's songs. He wanted the bird to be his friend. The boy gave the bird a cheap toy. The bird liked the toy. They played together and sang songs. They were very happy and became best friends.

Lily and Tom were hungry. They saw a big bowl of grapes on the table. They wanted to eat some grapes. But the bowl was too high for them. They could not reach it. They saw a chair near the table. They had an idea. They pushed the chair to the table. They climbed on the chair. They reached for the grapes. But they were not careful. They knocked over a candle on the table. The candle fell on the tablecloth. The tablecloth started to burn. "Help! Help!" Lily and Tom shouted. "Fire! Fire!" Mom and Dad heard them. They ran to the kitchen. They saw the fire. They grabbed a wet towel. They put out the fire. They were angry and scared. "What were you doing?" Mom asked. "You could have been hurt!" "We wanted some grapes," Lily said. "But the bowl was too high." "You should have asked us for help," Dad said. "You should not climb on the chair. You should not touch the candle. It is dangerous. It can burn you and the house." "We are sorry," Lily and Tom said. "We did not think. We were silly." Mom and Dad hugged them. They were glad they were safe. They gave them some grapes. They taught them a lesson. "Next time, if you want something that is difficult to get, ask for help," Mom said. "Do not do things that can hurt you or others. Be careful and smart." Lily and Tom nodded. They learned their lesson. They ate their grapes. They said thank you. They did not climb on the chair again. They did not touch the candle again. They were careful and smart.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy train for Lily's birthday. Lily was very happy. At the store, they found a toy train with steam. Lily loved the train. Her mom bought the train for her. They went home to play with it. At home, Lily played with her new train. But the steam from the train was too hot. It made the train break. Lily was sad. Her toy train was gone. The bright day did not feel happy anymore.

Sara and Ben love to swim in the pool. They put on their swim suits and jump in the water. They splash and play and have fun. "Let's play a game," Sara says. "I will number to ten and you hide. Then I will find you." "OK," Ben says. He swims to the other side of the pool and hides behind a big float. Sara closes her eyes and numbers. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. Ready or not, here I come!" Sara opens her eyes and looks for Ben. She sees his feet sticking out from the float. She swims over and taps him. "I found you!" she says. Ben laughs and comes out from the float. He hugs Sara. "You are good at finding," he says. "Now it is my turn to number and your turn to hide." Sara nods and swims away. She finds a spot under the slide and waits. She hears Ben number. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten. Ready or not, here I come!" Sara hopes Ben will not find her. She likes to play hide and seek. She hears Ben swim around the pool. He looks under the chairs and behind the plants. He does not see Sara. "Where are you, Sara?" he calls. "I can not find you." Sara smiles and stays quiet. She is glad she found a good hiding spot. She waits for Ben to give up and say she wins. She thinks swimming and playing with Ben is the best.
