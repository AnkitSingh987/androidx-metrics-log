Category: Beginner
Title: The Pony and the Fence

Once upon a time, there was a pony. He wanted to get better at running and jumping. He was so enthusiastic about improving! The pony worked hard and tried his best to do better. But he just couldn't get any better no matter what he did. He was so sad. He wanted to be the best. One day, the pony tried to jump over a fence. He almost made it, but he couldn't quite get over. He tried again and again but he just couldn't make it. He was so disappointed. Sadly, the pony never got better and he had to give up his dream. He was very sad to not be able to improve, but that was the way it was. The End.

Tom and Sue like to play with beans. They have a big jar of beans in many colors. They make pictures and patterns with the beans on the floor. Sometimes they count the beans or sort them by color. One day, Tom wants to make a big bean star. He takes all the yellow beans from the jar. Sue is sad. She wants some yellow beans too. She asks Tom to share the beans. Tom says no. He says he needs all the yellow beans for his star. Sue thinks this is not fair. She waits until Tom is not looking. Then she takes some yellow beans from his star. She puts them in her pocket. She smiles. She thinks she is clever. But Tom sees what Sue did. He is angry. He shouts at Sue. He says she is a thief. He wants his beans back. Sue says no. She says he is greedy. They start to fight. They pull and push each other. The beans fall on the floor. The star is broken. Mom hears the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the beans and the children. She is not happy. She says they are naughty. She says they have to clean up the mess. She says they have to say sorry to each other. She says they have to share the beans. She says that is fair.

Steve was walking along the beach when he saw something yellow in the ocean. It was a big yellow shark! Steve was a brave boy and he wanted to keep the shark as his pet. He said, “Come here, shark! I want to keep you as my pet!” But the shark was scared and he kept swimming away from Steve. Steve ran into the ocean after the shark, but the water was too deep. Soon, Steve was too far away from the shore and he was starting to drown. But the yellow shark didn’t help. He just kept swimming away until Steve was gone. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to sing his song every day. He was a very careful bird and always made sure he was safe in his nest. One day, Tim tried to sing his song, but he began to struggle. He could not sing his song as he used to. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. He wanted to sing his song for all his friends in the forest. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse named Max heard Tim's struggle and came to help. Max said, "I know a new song that I can teach you." Tim was surprised and happy. He learned the new song from Max and sang it for all his friends. The forest was filled with joy and laughter, and Tim and Max became the best of friends.

One day, a clever little boy named Tim found a flashlight under his bed. He was very happy. He wanted to show it to his mom. He ran to her and said, "Mom, look what I found! Can I please play with it?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can play with the flashlight. But please be careful and don't shine it in people's eyes." Tim was so excited. He turned on the flashlight and saw a bright light. He went outside to play with his friends. They played a game called "Find the Light." Tim would shine the flashlight on the ground and his friends would try to catch the light with their hands. As it got darker, the flashlight became more fun to play with. Tim and his friends had a great time. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you, friends, for playing with me. Let's play with the flashlight again soon!" Tim went home and told his mom all about his fun day. Mom was happy that Tim had found a clever way to play with his friends. She told him, "Good job, Tim! You were very clever with the flashlight. Now, please get ready for bed."
