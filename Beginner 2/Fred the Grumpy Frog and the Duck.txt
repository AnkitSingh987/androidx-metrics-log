Category: Beginner
Title: Fred the Grumpy Frog and the Duck

One day, a grumpy frog named Fred lived near a log. He liked to record the sounds of his friends. He had a big, round thing that could listen and save the sounds. Fred's friends were a duck, a cat, and a dog. One day, Fred said to his friends, "Let's play a game. I will record your sounds, and then we can all listen to them." The duck, the cat, and the dog agreed. They all made their sounds. The duck said "quack," the cat said "meow," and the dog said "woof." Fred played the sounds back for his friends. They laughed and laughed. The grumpy frog was not grumpy anymore. They all played together near the log, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her toy cat, Fluffy. They would play all day in the big green park. One day, Fluffy got hurt. Mia was very sad. She wanted to heal Fluffy. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me heal Fluffy?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia. Let's see what we can do." Mia's mom looked at what was available to help. She found a measure of bandages. They put the bandages on Fluffy. Soon, Fluffy was healed and ready to play again. Mia was so happy. She thanked her mom and gave her a big hug. Mia and Fluffy went back to the park to play some more. They had a great day together.

Lily and Tom were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were grown-ups. Lily wore a big hat and a long scarf. Tom wore a tie and a jacket. They had a tea party on the small table. "Would you like some more tea, dear?" Lily asked, holding a toy cup. "Yes, please, darling," Tom said, taking the cup. "And a biscuit, too." They nibbled on the biscuits and sipped the tea. They felt very fancy and happy. On the big table, there was an ashtray. It was round and shiny and had some black stuff inside. Lily and Tom did not know what it was for. They thought it looked interesting. "Look, what is that?" Lily said, pointing to the ashtray. "I don't know. Let's see," Tom said, standing up. He walked to the big table and tried to reach the ashtray. But he was too short. He stretched his arm and stood on his toes. He could not touch it. "Help me, Lily," he said. Lily joined him and tried to help. She pushed a chair to the table and climbed on it. She was a bit taller than Tom. She reached the ashtray and grabbed it. "Got it!" she said, holding it up. But she did not hold it well. It was heavy and slippery. It slipped from her hand and fell to the floor. It broke into pieces and made a loud noise. "Oh no!" Lily and Tom said. They heard footsteps. Their mom came into the room. She saw the mess and the broken ashtray. She was angry. "What have you done?" she said. "That was daddy's ashtray. He liked it very much. It was a gift from his friend. How could you be so naughty?" Lily and Tom felt sorry and scared. They did not mean to break the ashtray. They did not know it was important. They looked at their mom with big eyes and chubby cheeks. "We're sorry, mommy," they said. "We were just playing. We didn't know." Their mom sighed. She was not happy, but she loved them. She knew they were just curious and did not understand. "It's okay, sweeties," she said. "But you have to be more careful. Don't touch things that are not yours. And don't play with the ashtray. It's not a toy. It's bad for you. Do you understand?" Lily and Tom nodded. They hugged their mom and said they were sorry again. They helped her clean up the mess. They learned their lesson. They never played with the ashtray again.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a clever little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends, the animals. One day, she saw a big, round pumpkin. Lily had a fun idea. Lily called her friends and said, "Let's remember to make a fun face on the pumpkin!" So, the clever little girl and her friends found a stick and made a happy face on the pumpkin. They were all very happy. Every day, Lily and her friends would play with the pumpkin. They loved their pumpkin friend. They would remember the fun times they had together. And they all lived happily ever after.
