Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tom went for a walk with his mom. They went to the park to play. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tom!" As Tom climbed the tree, he saw a little bird. The bird was sitting on a branch. Tom said, "Hi, bird! I can see you!" The bird looked at Tom and said, "Hi, Tom! I can see you too!" Tom was very surprised that the bird could talk. Tom pointed at the bird and said, "Mom, look! The bird can talk!" His mom looked up and saw the bird. She said, "Wow, Tom! That is a very special bird!" Tom and his mom were very happy to meet the talking bird. They played in the park with the bird all day.

One day, a bunny named Bob wanted to play. He went to the park and saw a big, dry tree. He thought it would be fun to hop around the tree. So, he hopped and hopped, and he was very happy. As Bob was hopping, he heard a sound. It was a trumpet! He looked around and saw a little boy playing the trumpet. The boy was sad because he could not play it well. Bob hopped to the boy and said, "Why are you sad?" The boy replied, "I want to play the trumpet, but I can't do it right." Bob thought for a moment and then said, "Let's hop and play the trumpet together!" The boy smiled and they both hopped around the dry tree. The boy played the trumpet better and better, and they both had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a pink bunny. The bunny loved to play and hop all day long. One day, the pink bunny saw a big tree with many big roots. The pink bunny wanted to play hide and seek with his friends. He thought it would be fun to hide behind the big tree roots. So, he called his friends, the bird, the squirrel, and the frog, to come and play with him. The bird, the squirrel, and the frog came to play hide and seek with the pink bunny. The pink bunny hid behind the big tree roots, and his friends started to look for him. The pink bunny peeked out from behind the roots to see if his friends could find him. While the pink bunny was peeking, a big, scary wolf saw him. The wolf was very hungry and wanted to eat the pink bunny. The pink bunny saw the wolf and got very scared. He tried to run away, but the wolf was too fast. The wolf caught the pink bunny and ate him up. The bird, the squirrel, and the frog were very sad. They learned that playing near the big tree roots was not safe. From that day on, they never played hide and seek near the big tree roots again.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks. They build towers and houses and bridges. They have fun. One day, they find a big ruler. It is a tool to measure things. Tom and Mia want to measure their blocks. They think it is interesting. But they have a problem. They have only one ruler. Tom wants to measure his tower. Mia wants to measure her house. They both want the ruler. They start to fight. "Give me the ruler!" Tom says. "No, it is mine!" Mia says. They pull and tug. The ruler breaks. They are sad. They look at their blocks. They are still there. They are still fun. They have an idea. They can measure their blocks with their hands. They can compare their hands. They can share their blocks. They smile. They are friends again. They play with blocks. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a nice sister named Emma. They liked to play together in their room. One day, they found a big box of colors. They were very happy. Lily said, "Let's mark the walls with these colors!" Emma thought it was a fun idea, so they started to draw on the walls. They made a big, pretty picture with lots of colors. Just then, their mom came into the room. She saw the marked walls and was very surprised. But instead of being mad, she smiled and said, "That's so pretty! Let's put a big paper on the wall, so you can mark it and keep your art." Lily and Emma were happy that their mom liked their art. They put the big paper on the wall and marked it with more colors. From that day on, they made many nice pictures together.
