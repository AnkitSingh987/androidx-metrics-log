Title: Bob and the Medicine

Bob had been feeling very high all week. He was sure he was sick, so he went to the doctor. The doctor said, "I'm sorry, Bob, you need to buy some medicine." Bob was surprised and asked, "Where can I buy the medicine?" The doctor smiled and said, "You can buy it at the store, my friend." So Bob went to the store and bought some medicine. After taking the medicine, Bob felt much better and was able to play with his friends all day.

Anna and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park near their homes. There was a big oak tree in the park. It had many branches and leaves. Anna and Ben liked to climb the oak tree and pretend they were birds. One day, they found a nest in the oak tree. It had three eggs inside. They were blue and speckled. Anna and Ben were very curious. They wanted to see the eggs up close. But they also knew they should not touch them. The eggs belonged to the mother bird. "Let's just look at them, but not touch them," Anna said. "We can be honest and careful." "OK," Ben agreed. "But we have to be quick. The mother bird might come back soon." They climbed up the oak tree and reached the nest. They peeked inside and saw the eggs. They were very pretty. Anna and Ben smiled. Suddenly, they heard a loud squawk. It was the mother bird. She was angry. She saw Anna and Ben near her nest. She thought they wanted to hurt her eggs. She flew towards them and flapped her wings. "Uh-oh," Anna said. "We have to go. Now!" Anna and Ben were scared. They climbed down the oak tree as fast as they could. They ran away from the park. They did not want to make the mother bird more angry. They reached their homes and hugged each other. They were glad they were safe. They also felt sorry for the mother bird. They did not mean to upset her. "We should not have gone near the nest," Ben said. "It was wrong." "You're right," Anna said. "We should have left the eggs alone. They were not ours." They learned their lesson. They decided to be more honest and respectful. They also decided to be more careful. They did not want to end their friendship over a mistake. They loved each other very much.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to have a race. They were all excited to see who would be the fastest. The race began, and Tim ran as fast as he could. He felt the wind in his hair and his heart raced. But then, Tim tripped on a rock and fell down. He felt a bitter pain in his knee and started to weep. His friends stopped running to help him, but Tim wanted to finish the race. Tim got up and started running again, even though his knee hurt. He ran and ran, and finally, he crossed the finish line. His friends cheered for him, and Tim felt a great victory. He was happy that he didn't give up, and his friends were proud of him too.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He had a red car. The man liked to go fast in his car. One day, the man saw a big hill. He wanted to go up the hill in his car. The man drove his car up the hill. At the top, he saw a toy gun. The toy gun could shoot soft balls. The man picked up the toy gun and played with it. He liked to shoot the soft balls at the trees. The man got back in his car and drove down the hill. He was happy because he had fun with the toy gun. He went home and showed his friends how to shoot the soft balls. They all laughed and had a good time.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy a special crystal for Tim's grandma. Tim's mom said, "We will find a nice gift for grandma." At the store, Tim saw a big crystal. It was very pretty. He asked his mom, "Can we buy this one?" His mom said, "Yes, let's get it." But when they got home, the crystal was gone! Tim felt sorry. He did not know what to do. Then, his little sister came in the room. She had the crystal! She said, "I took it to show my friend. I'm sorry." Tim was happy they found the crystal. Now they could give it to their grandma.
