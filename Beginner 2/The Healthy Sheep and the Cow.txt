Category: Beginner
Title: The Healthy Sheep and the Cow

Once upon a time, there was a nice healthy sheep. Each day, when the sun rose, the sheep would wake up, yawn, and start the day. One day, the sheep was feeling very sleepy. She was just about to take a nap when she heard someone calling her. It was her friend, the cow. "Come on, sheep!" the cow said, "Let's go for a walk!" So, the sheep and the cow enjoyed a nice healthy walk together. As they walked, the sheep began to yawn. "Tired are you?" the cow asked. "Would you like to take a nap right here in the sun?" The sheep nodded yes, and snuggled down into the warm grass. Soon, she was fast asleep. The cow watched over her until the sun set and it was time to go home.

One day, a big plane was at the airport. The plane was getting ready for a long part to a far place. Many people were happy to go on the plane. The plane was very successful at flying. As the plane was flying, something unexpected happened. The plane saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was lost and did not know where to go. The plane wanted to help the big bird find its way home. The plane and the big bird became good friends. They flew together and the plane helped the big bird find its home. In the end, the plane and the big bird were both successful and happy. The people on the plane were happy too, because they saw the big bird and the plane be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved to run, jump, and play all day long. He was the strongest dog in his town, and everyone loved him. One day, Max was playing when he found a broken toy. He wanted to repair the toy, so he took it to his friend, Sam. Sam was very smart and knew how to fix things. Max and Sam worked together to repair the broken toy. When they were done, the toy was as good as new. Max and Sam were very happy that they fixed the toy. They played with it all day long. Everyone in town saw how strong Max was and how smart Sam was. They knew that Max and Sam made a great team. And from that day on, Max and Sam were the best of friends, always helping each other and having fun together.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. One day, they see a big tree with many branches. They want to climb the tree and hang from the branches. "Look, mom, dad, we can hang like monkeys!" Anna says. "Be careful, Anna, Ben, the tree is very high!" mom says. Anna and Ben do not listen. They climb the tree and hang from the branches. They laugh and swing. They think they are very clever. But they are naughty. They do not see the staff who work in the park. The staff are angry. They do not want the children to hurt the tree or fall down. "Hey, you two, get down from the tree right now!" one of the staff shouts. Anna and Ben are scared. They hear the staff. They let go of the branches and slide down the tree. They run to their mom and dad. "Sorry, mom, dad, we were naughty," Anna says. "Sorry, mom, dad, we were naughty," Ben says. Mom and dad are not happy. They hug Anna and Ben. They say, "We love you, but you have to listen to us and the staff. The tree is not a toy. You could have been hurt. Next time, play on the swings or the slide. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben nod. They say, "We understand. We are sorry. We will not do it again." Mom and dad smile. They say, "Good. Now, let's go and have some ice cream. But first, say sorry to the staff." Anna and Ben say sorry to the staff. The staff forgive them. They say, "Thank you for saying sorry. Be good and have fun." Anna and Ben have ice cream. They play on the swings and the slide. They do not climb the tree again. They are good.
