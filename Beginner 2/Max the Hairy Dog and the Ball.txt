Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Hairy Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big, green park, there was a very hairy dog named Max. Max loved to play with his red ball. He would run and kick the ball with his paws. One day, Max met a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Max playing with his red ball. She asked, "Can I play with you, Max?" Max wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" They played together in the park. The wind was blowing the leaves and Max's fur. Max and Lily took turns kicking the red ball. They laughed and had so much fun. After a while, they were both tired and sat down under a big tree. Max and Lily became best friends. They played in the park every day, kicking the red ball and enjoying the wind. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Anna are sisters. They like to play in the park. They have a red ball and a blue ball. They throw the balls and catch them. "Let's race!" Lily says. "Who can run to the big tree first?" "OK!" Anna says. "Ready, set, go!" They run as fast as they can. Lily is faster than Anna. She gets to the big tree first. She is happy. She turns around and sees Anna. "Ha ha, I win!" Lily says. "You are slow!" Anna is sad. She does not like to lose. She does not like Lily's words. She is angry. She picks up the blue ball and throws it at Lily. The ball hits Lily on the head. Lily cries. She is hurt. She is angry. "That's naughty!" Lily says. "You are a bad sister!" They start to fight. They push and pull and shout. They do not play anymore. Their mom sees them. She is not happy. She comes to the park. She stops their fight. "What are you doing?" she asks. "Why are you fighting?" They tell her what happened. She is not pleased. She shakes her head. "That's not nice!" she says. "You should not race to win. You should race to have fun. You should not say mean words. You should say kind words. You should not throw balls. You should share balls. You should not be naughty. You should be good. You are sisters. You should love each other." They feel sorry. They hug each other. They say sorry. "Can we play again?" Lily asks. "Yes, but be nice!" mom says. They nod. They smile. They play again. They have fun. They are sisters. They love each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had black fur and lived in a small house with his best friend, a girl named Lily. Spot loved to play with Lily, but he was envious of the other dogs in the park who could do tricks. Lily told Spot, "Trust me, you can do tricks too." One day, Lily took Spot to the park to play. She had a plan to help Spot learn a trick. When they got to the park, Lily showed Spot a big stick. She said, "I will throw this stick, and you will run and catch it." Spot was excited and trusted Lily. Lily threw the stick, and Spot ran as fast as he could. He caught the stick and brought it back to Lily. Spot was so happy that he could do a trick too. All the other dogs in the park were amazed. Spot was no longer envious, and he knew he could always trust his best friend, Lily.

Once upon a time there was a brave knight. One day, the knight had a big request. He wanted to conquer a fierce witch. So he picked up his sword and set off on an adventure. The knight found the witch living in the village, inside a tall castle. He knew he had to be careful, so he snuck around quietly. Soon, the witch spotted the knight and came out to face him. She was fierce and full of power. The knight stepped forward and boldly made his request. He wanted to defeat the witch. She did not want to be defeated, so she said no. But the knight was brave and he insisted. So the witch used her magic to make the knight fly. He flew higher and higher until he was above the clouds. He flew around for a long time until he finally came back down. The brave knight had conquered the fierce witch! Everyone was so happy and celebrated his victory. The knight lived happily ever after, having fulfilled his request!
