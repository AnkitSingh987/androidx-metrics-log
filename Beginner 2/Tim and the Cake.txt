Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Cake

One day, a little boy named Tim went to class. He was very excited to learn and play with his friends. In class, they were going to make a big cake. Tim was happy because he loved cake. As they started to make the cake, Tim's friend, Sam, found a fake bug. Sam thought it would be funny to put the fake bug in the cake. Tim did not like that idea. He wanted the cake to be yummy, not yucky. Tim told Sam, "No, we should not put the fake bug in the cake." They both started to stir the cake mix. Sam listened to Tim and put the fake bug away. The cake was yummy, and everyone in the class was happy. Tim and Sam learned that it is better to make yummy things, not yucky things.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing with her ball. She liked to pass the ball to her dog, Max. Max would catch the ball and pass it back to Amy. They played together all day. Amy's mom was a nurse. She helped people feel better when they were sick. One day, while Amy and Max were playing, the nurse heard a loud noise. She went outside to see what happened. Amy and Max had kicked the ball into a tree! The ball was stuck and they could not get it down. Amy felt sorry for Max, because he was sad without his ball. The nurse had an idea. She called a friend who had a big ladder. The friend came over and climbed the tree. He got the ball and passed it down to Amy. Max was so happy to have his ball back! Amy said, "Thank you for helping us!" The nurse smiled and said, "You're welcome! Now go and have fun with Max!" And that's what they did, playing and passing the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a row of pretty flowers. The flowers were all different colors: red, blue, yellow, and pink. Mia thought they were perfect. Mia wanted to pick a flower to give to her mom. She tried to reach for the tallest flower in the row. But Mia was too little, and she could not reach it. She tried and tried, but the flower was still too high. Then, Mia had an idea. She found a big rock and stood on it. Now she could reach the tallest flower in the row. Mia picked the perfect flower and gave it to her mom. Her mom was so happy, and they both smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. Tom loved to splash in puddles. One day, it rained a lot, and there were many big puddles outside. Tom put on his boots and went outside to play. Tom saw his friend, Lily. Lily was a crazy duck who also loved to splash. They ran to the biggest puddle they could find. "Let's splash together, Lily!" said Tom. "Quack, quack! Yes, let's splash!" replied Lily. Tom and Lily jumped into the puddle. They splashed and laughed. They had so much fun. The sun came out, and they went home. Tom and Lily were very happy. They could not wait to splash again.

One day, a cute little bird named Tim was flying around. He saw a big tree with many leaves. The wind was blowing, and one leaf fell. Tim saw the leaf and said, "Oh no! The leaf fell!" Tim wanted to care for the leaf, so he picked it up with his beak. He looked around for a safe place to put the leaf. He saw a small hole in the tree. Tim said, "That is a good place for the leaf." Tim put the leaf in the hole and said, "Now you will be safe, leaf." The wind blew again, and more leaves fell. Tim knew he had to care for all the leaves. He worked all day to save the leaves. At the end of the day, the tree was happy, and Tim was tired but happy too.
