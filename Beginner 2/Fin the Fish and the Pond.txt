Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Pond

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a fish named Fin. Fin was a very different fish. He had a big scale on his back that could change colors. It was so pretty! All the other fish loved to see Fin's scale change colors. One day, Fin wanted to move to a new part of the pond. He swam and swam until he found a nice spot. There, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a small fish who liked to play with Fin. They had so much fun together. Fin showed Sam his big scale that could change colors. Sam was very happy to see it. They played together all day long. In the end, Fin and Sam became best friends. They were very happy to have found each other in the big pond.

Once upon a time, there was a big sun in the sky. It was time for the sun to go away. The sun said, "I must go now, so the moon can come out." The sky turned pretty colors as the sun went down. This was called sunset. A little girl named Amy saw the sunset. She was happy to see the pretty colors in the sky. She said, "Welcome, sunset! I like how you make the sky look nice." Amy was not careful and left her toys outside. As the sun went away, it got dark. Amy's mom said, "Amy, you were careless with your toys. Please pick them up before it gets too dark." Amy said, "Okay, mom." She went outside to get her toys. Amy learned to be more careful with her toys. She always picked them up before the sunset. And every day, she would say, "Welcome, sunset!" as the pretty colors filled the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive tree. A little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue loved to play near the tree. They liked to hug the tree and pretend it was the world. They had so much fun together. One day, Tim and Sue had a fight. Tim said, "I want to hug the tree first!" Sue said, "No, I want to hug the tree first!" They both got very mad at each other. They did not want to share the tree. Tim and Sue did not talk to each other anymore. They both hugged the tree, but they did not have fun. They missed playing together. The big, impressive tree was not happy anymore. The tree lost its leaves, and it was very sad. Tim and Sue never became friends again, and the tree stayed sad forever.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and worms. They found a big round thing that looked like a ball. It was brown and had many layers. "What is this?" Anna asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is a treasure," Ben said. They decided to take it to the house and show it to their mom. They ran inside and put the thing on the table. "Mom, look what we found!" Anna said. Mom was cooking in the kitchen. She turned around and saw the thing on the table. She was not happy. "That is not a treasure. That is an onion. Where did you get it?" Mom asked. "We found it in the garden. It was under the dirt," Ben said. Mom shook her head. "That is not your onion. That is Mr. Lee's onion. He grows them in his yard. You took his onion without asking. That is very bad. You have to give it back and say sorry." Anna and Ben felt sad. They did not know it was Mr. Lee's onion. They thought it was a beautiful ball. They did not want to give it back. They wanted to keep it and play with it. They started to cry. Mom took the onion and put it in a bag. She took Anna and Ben by the hand and walked to Mr. Lee's house. She knocked on the door and waited. Mr. Lee opened the door. He saw Mom and the kids and the bag. He was angry. "Hello, Mrs. Smith. What can I do for you?" Mr. Lee asked. "Hello, Mr. Lee. I'm sorry to bother you, but I have something to tell you. My kids took your onion from your garden. They thought it was a ball. They did not mean to hurt you. They are very sorry. Here is your onion," Mom said. She gave the bag to Mr. Lee. He took it and looked at the onion. It was dirty and had a hole in it. He was not happy. "Thank you, Mrs. Smith. I appreciate your honesty. But this onion is ruined. I can't use it. It was a very good onion. I was going to make soup with it. Now I have to throw it away. You owe me a new onion," Mr. Lee said. He closed the door and went inside. Mom and the kids walked back to their house. They felt very bad. They did not have a beautiful ball. They did not have a new onion. They did not have a friend. They only had a problem.
