Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Window

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys, but their favorite was the big window. They liked to look outside and see the birds, the cars, and the people. Sometimes they pretended they were flying or driving or talking to the people. One day, Sara had an original idea. She said to Tom, "Let's play a game. You stand on the window sill and I hold your hand. Then you lean out and see how far you can go. It will be fun!" Tom was not sure. He said, "But Sara, that sounds dangerous. What if we fall? Mom and Dad said we should never go near the window." Sara said, "Don't be scared, Tom. I will not let you go. You can trust me. I am your sister. Come on, it will be fun!" Tom wanted to make Sara happy, so he agreed. He climbed on the window sill and Sara held his hand. He leaned out and saw the street below. He felt a thrill and a fear. He said, "Sara, I don't like this. Let's stop." Sara said, "No, Tom, you can go further. It's not so high. Trust me, Tom, trust me." She pulled his hand and made him lean more. Tom felt his balance slip. He screamed, "Sara, help me!" But Sara did not help him. She let go of his hand. She was angry at Tom for being scared. She wanted to teach him a lesson. She did not think he would fall. She thought he would catch himself. But he did not catch himself. He fell. He fell fast and hard. He hit the ground and did not move. He did not make a sound. Sara looked out the window and saw Tom. She saw the blood and the people. She saw the police and the ambulance. She felt a shock and a guilt. She cried, "Tom, Tom, I'm sorry, I'm sorry!" But Tom did not hear her. He did not hear anything. He was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to race. One day, he decided to have an incredible race with his toy car. Tim went to the bathroom and saw a bottle of shampoo. He thought, "I can use this shampoo to make the race more fun!" So, he put some shampoo on the floor and started to race his car on it. The car went very fast on the slippery shampoo. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He had an incredible time racing his car on the shampoo. And from that day on, Tim always used shampoo to make his races more fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the lake with his dad. They took a kayak to play in the water. Tim was very excited. He had never been in a kayak before. His dad told him, "Tim, be careful not to be clumsy." They got in the kayak and started to push it into the water. Tim was having so much fun. He laughed and splashed the water with his hands. Suddenly, a big fish jumped out of the water and landed in their kayak! Tim and his dad looked at each other and said, "Wow! That was unexpected!" They both laughed as the big fish flopped around in the kayak. Tim's dad picked up the fish and gently put it back in the water. The fish swam away, and Tim and his dad continued to have fun in their kayak.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a purple bow. She wanted to put it in her hair. But she could not reach it. It was too high up on a shelf. She tried to jump, but she could not get it. Sue saw her friend, Tom. She asked him, "Can you help me get the purple bow?" Tom was taller than Sue, so he reached up and got the bow for her. Sue was very happy. She put the bow in her hair and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But then, something unexpected happened. The purple bow started to talk! It said, "Hello, Sue and Tom! I am a magic bow. I can grant you one wish each." Sue and Tom were very surprised, but they made their wishes. And they played together all day, with the magic purple bow in Sue's hair.
