Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Flower

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park with her mom. Sally loved to pick flowers and today, she saw a big pretty flower. She ran to the flower and picked it up. Sally was very happy and showed the flower to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "What a nice flower you picked!" After playing at the park, Sally and her mom went to the ice cream shop. Sally loved ice cream. She picked vanilla cream with colorful sprinkles on top. Her mom picked chocolate cream. They sat down and ate their ice cream together. Sally was very happy. Suddenly, a small puppy came running into the ice cream shop. The puppy jumped on Sally's lap and started to eat her cream. Sally was surprised and a little ashamed because her cream was now gone. But then, the puppy looked at her with big, sweet eyes. Sally smiled and said, "It's okay, puppy. You can have my cream." Sally's mom was proud of her for sharing. And from that day on, Sally and the puppy became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little red ball. The ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce high in the sky and then come back down. The ball had so much fun bouncing around. One day, the ball met a new friend named Tim. Tim was just three years old. Tim and the ball played together every day. They would bounce and laugh all day long. They were very happy. But one day, the ball bounced too high and got stuck in a tree. The ball felt ashamed because it could not come down. Tim was sad too. Then, a kind bird flew up and pushed the ball out of the tree. The ball was so happy to be free again. Tim and the ball said thank you to the bird and went back to bouncing and playing together.

Mark is a boy who likes to draw. He has many colors and papers to make pictures. He draws animals, cars, houses and trees. He is happy when he draws. One day, Mark is drawing in the kitchen. He sees a big bag of rice on the table. He thinks rice is white and boring. He wants to make it colorful. He takes his red color and opens the bag. He pours some rice on the floor and starts to draw on it. He makes red dots and lines and circles. He thinks it looks pretty. But then, his mom comes in. She sees the mess on the floor and the bag. She is angry. She says, "Mark, what did you do? You wasted the rice and made a mess. Rice is not for drawing. Rice is for eating. You are a bad boy. Go to your room now!" Mark is scared and sad. He did not mean to be bad. He just wanted to have fun. He says, "Mom, I am sorry. I did not know. I like to draw. I wanted to make the rice pretty. Please do not be mad at me." Mom sighs. She is still angry, but she sees that Mark is sorry. She says, "Mark, I know you like to draw, but you have to be careful. You cannot draw on everything. Some things are not for drawing. Some things are for other uses. You have to ask me before you use something. Do you understand?" Mark nods. He says, "Yes, mom. I understand. I will not draw on rice again. I will ask you first. I am sorry. Can I hug you?" Mom smiles. She says, "Yes, you can hug me. I love you, Mark. But you still have to go to your room. And you have to help me clean up later." Mark hugs mom. He says, "I love you, too, mom. I will go to my room. And I will help you clean up. I am sorry." Mom hugs Mark back. She says, "It's okay, Mark. Everyone makes mistakes. But you have to learn from them. Now go to your room and think about what you did. And maybe you can draw me a nice picture later." Mark says, "Okay, mom. I will. Thank you, mom." Mark goes to his room. He feels sorry, but he also feels loved. He thinks about what he did. He decides to draw mom a picture of a flower. He hopes she will like it. He still likes to draw, but he will not draw on rice again. He learned his lesson.
