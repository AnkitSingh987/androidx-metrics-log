Category: Beginner
Title: The Fat Cat and the Dust

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a fat cat. The fat cat liked to play and jump all day. One day, the fat cat saw some dust on the floor. The fat cat thought the dust was a toy. The fat cat jumped on the dust to settle it down. But, the dust did not settle. The dust went up in the air and made the fat cat sneeze. The fat cat sneezed so much that it felt very tired. The fat cat wanted to take a nap, but the dust was still in the air. The fat cat sneezed and sneezed, and it could not sleep. The fat cat was sad that it could not play or nap. The fat cat learned that not all things are good to play with.

One day, a tall man went to the park. He saw a big tree with a tap on it. He was very happy because he was thirsty. He turned the tap and water came out. He drank the water and felt good. The tall man saw a little girl with a toy. The toy was a printer. The girl showed the man how to print. They printed many pretty pictures together. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, the tall man said bye to the girl. He walked away from the park with a big smile. He was happy he made a new friend and learned how to print.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toy scale. She would put things on it and watch it go up and down. One day, she found a big, red ball. She wanted to see if her scale could hold it. Amy put the ball on the scale, but it was too heavy. The scale started to bend. "Stop!" said her mom. "That ball is too big for your scale!" Amy felt sad. She wanted to play with her scale and the big ball. Her mom saw that Amy was sad. She said, "Don't worry, Amy. We can find something else for your scale." They looked around the house for something available. They found a small toy car. Amy put the car on the scale, and it worked! She was so happy. She played with her scale and the toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fast dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a little baby in a crib. The baby was crying. Spot wanted to help the baby. He barked and said, "Don't cry, baby. I will provide you a toy." Spot ran fast to his toy box and picked up his favorite ball. He brought the ball to the crib and gave it to the baby. The baby stopped crying and smiled at Spot. The baby said, "Thank you, Spot. You are a good dog." Spot wagged his tail and felt happy. From that day on, Spot and the baby became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a porch. A little girl named Amy lived in the house. She liked to play on the porch with her toys. The porch was near a deep, big forest. Amy did not know what was inside the forest. One sunny day, while Amy played on the porch, a big, funny bear came out of the deep forest. The bear was very nice and wanted to play with Amy. At first, Amy was scared, but then she saw that the bear was friendly. Amy and the bear played all day on the porch. They had so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The bear took off a big, furry coat, and it was Amy's dad! He was playing a trick on her. Amy laughed and hugged her dad. They knew they would remember this fun day forever.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his football. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sam, and they played together. They had so much fun and were very noisy. Then, a big dog came. The dog took their football and ran away. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted their football back. Tim said, "We need to work together to get our football back." They made a plan. Tim and Sam worked together to make a trap. They put a big net near the dog's house. The dog saw the net and got scared. It dropped the football and ran away. Tim and Sam got their football back. They were so happy. They went back to the park and played some more. They laughed and said, "Working together is the best!"
