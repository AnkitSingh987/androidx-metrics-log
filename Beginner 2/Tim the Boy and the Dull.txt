Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dull

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Dull. Mr. Dull was a soft and old toy. Tim loved Mr. Dull very much. They played together every day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to supply food for the party." Tim wanted to help, so he brought Mr. Dull with him. They went to the store and picked out food together. Tim said, "Mr. Dull, do you like these apples?" Mr. Dull didn't say anything, but Tim knew he liked them too. At the party, Tim and Mr. Dull played with the other kids. They laughed and had fun. When it was time to eat, Tim shared his food with Mr. Dull. They both loved the yummy food. Tim gave Mr. Dull a big touch and said, "I'm so happy we could help supply food for the party, Mr. Dull." And Mr. Dull seemed happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cute rabbit named Hop. Hop was a spoiled rabbit because Tim gave Hop lots of love and treats. Hop liked to hop and play in the yard. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with Hop. They played catch and Tim would hold Hop in his arms. Hop liked when Tim held him because it made him feel safe and loved. They laughed and played all day long. As the sun went down, Tim and Hop were tired from playing. Tim held Hop and walked back to their house. They had a fun day together. Tim knew that Hop was a spoiled rabbit, but he loved him very much. And Hop loved Tim too. They were the best of friends.

Tim likes to play with his toy cars. He has many cars of different colors and sizes. He makes them zoom on the floor, on the sofa, on the table. He likes to zoom them fast and loud. One day, Tim sees a big car in the street. It is shiny and red. He wants to zoom it too. He runs to the car and opens the door. He climbs inside and finds a key. He turns the key and the car makes a noise. He pushes a pedal and the car zooms away. Tim is happy. He zooms the car on the road, on the grass, on the sidewalk. He does not see the people and the animals. He does not hear the horns and the sirens. He only sees the car and the zoom. But soon, Tim is in trouble. He zooms the car into a wall. The car makes a big crash. The car is broken. The wall is broken. Tim is scared. He sees smoke and fire. He sees people and police. He hears shouts and cries. Tim is sad. He zooms the car no more. He wastes the car and the wall. He does a bad thing. He is sorry. He wants his toy cars. He wants his mom and dad. He cries and cries.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a plan. He wanted to win a big race at school. The winner would get a shiny medal. Tim wanted that medal so bad. But Tim was not fast. So, he had an idea. He made a fake medal that looked just like the real one. He thought no one would know. On the day of the race, Tim ran as fast as he could. But, something unexpected happened. A little dog joined the race too! The dog was so fast, it won the race. Everyone laughed and cheered for the dog. Tim felt happy for the dog and gave it his fake medal. The dog wagged its tail, and they became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big helicopter in the sky. He was very happy and excited. He waved at the helicopter and jumped up and down. The helicopter was flying very low, and it looked confused. Suddenly, the helicopter landed in Tim's yard. Tim was so surprised! He did not know why the helicopter was there. He walked closer to it, and he saw a sad pilot inside. The pilot was crying because he was lost and didn't know where to go. Tim wanted to help the pilot. He wiped the pilot's tears and said, "Don't cry. I will help you find your way." Tim and the pilot looked at a map together. They found the right path, and the pilot was happy again. The helicopter took off, and Tim waved goodbye. The unexpected visit from the helicopter made Tim's day very special.
