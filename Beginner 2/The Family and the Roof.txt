Category: Beginner
Title: The Family and the Roof

Once upon a time, there was a family that had a leaky roof. Water was dripping into the house and Daddy knew he had to do something fast. Mummy said, "We need a worker to rescue us and fix the leak." Daddy agreed and soon had a worker come to their house. The worker got very busy looking for the leak. After some time, he found it in the corner of the ceiling. He said, "This is an important job and I am going to make sure we fix it quickly." Daddy was happy and said, "Thank you, it is nice to have a rescue." The worker smiled and soon the leak was fixed. As the family looked around, they saw that the home was much drier and lighter. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a grumpy cat. The cat was always cold, so he would sit by the warm fire all day. One day, a little girl came to visit the cat. She asked the cat, "Why are you so grumpy?" The cat did not answer the girl. He just sat by the fire, feeling cold and grumpy. The little girl thought of a way to help the cat. She went to her room and found a soft, warm blanket. She brought it to the cat and wrapped it around him. The cat started to feel warm and cozy. He looked at the little girl and finally answered her. "Thank you for the warm blanket. I am not grumpy anymore." The cat and the little girl became good friends, and they played together every day. And the cat was never cold or grumpy again.

Ben and Jen are twins. They like to go to the mall with Mom. The mall has many shops and toys and people. Ben and Jen like to look at everything and play. One day, Mom says, "We have to go home soon. It is almost time for lunch." Ben and Jen nod, but they do not want to leave. They see a big slide in the middle of the mall. The slide is shiny and red and blue. Ben and Jen run to the slide. They want to go down. Mom calls, "Ben, Jen, come back! The slide is not for you. It is for bigger kids. You might get hurt." But Ben and Jen do not listen. They climb up the stairs to the top of the slide. They see a long and curvy way down. They think it looks fun. Ben goes first. He sits on the slide and pushes himself. He goes fast and faster. He feels the wind in his hair and the sun on his face. He laughs and shouts, "Whee!" He reaches the bottom and jumps off. He looks for Jen. Jen goes second. She sits on the slide and pushes herself. She goes fast and faster. She feels the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She smiles and says, "Whee!" She reaches a twist in the slide. She does not know what to do. She tries to turn, but she slips. She falls off the slide and lands on the floor. She cries and hurts. Mom sees Jen fall. She runs to her and picks her up. She hugs her and says, "Oh, Jen, are you okay? I told you the slide was not for you. You could have been very hurt. Why did you not listen to me?" Jen sobs and says, "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted to have fun. I did not know there was a twist. It was scary." Mom looks at Ben. Ben looks at Jen. He feels bad. He did not know Jen would fall. He says, "I'm sorry, Jen. I did not mean to leave you. I wanted to have fun too. I did not see the twist. Are you okay?" Jen nods and says, "I'm okay, Ben. I'm glad you are here. I was lonely." Mom says, "It's okay, Ben and Jen. I'm glad you are both okay. But you have to listen to me next time. I know what is safe and what is not. I love you both very much. Now let's go home and have some lunch. Maybe we can come back to the mall another day and find something else to play." Ben and Jen say, "Okay, Mom. We love you too." They hug Mom and each other. They leave the mall and go home.
