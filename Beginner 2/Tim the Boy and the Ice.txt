Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream very much. One day, he saw a big red ice-cream truck near his home. Tim was very happy and ran to the truck. The ice-cream man gave Tim a test. He said, "If you can wait and not eat your ice-cream now, I will give you two ice-creams later." Tim thought about it and decided to wait. He went home and put his ice-cream in the freezer. Later, the ice-cream man came back and gave Tim two more ice-creams. Tim was very happy. He learned that waiting can be good and you can get more if you are patient.

Tim and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys and pretend they are in space. They have a big box that they call their rocket. They put stickers on it and draw stars and planets. One day, they decide to go on a mission to explore the universe. They put on their helmets and suits and get inside the box. They count down from ten and then make loud noises like a rocket. "Zoom! Zoom! We are flying to the moon!" Tim says. "Look! There is a big cheese ball!" Mia says. She points to a yellow ball on the floor. "Can we eat it?" Tim asks. Mia nods. They get out of the box and run to the ball. They pick it up and take a bite. "Mmm, this is yummy!" Mia says. She chews the cheese and smiles. "Wait, this is not cheese!" Tim says. He spits out the cheese and makes a face. "This is rubber! It is hard and yucky!" He throws the ball away and rubs his tongue. Mia laughs. She thinks it is funny. "Sorry, Tim. I was joking. That is not the moon. That is our dog's toy. He likes to chew it." She hugs Tim and says sorry. Tim is not mad. He hugs Mia back and says it is okay. They go back to their box and pretend they are in space again. They have fun and forget about the rubber ball. They are happy twins. They love to play and explore the universe.

One Sunday, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to march like a soldier when he walked. Tim was happy because he was going to the park to play. On his way to the park, Tim saw a big ice cream truck. It had many tasty ice creams inside. He wanted to buy one, but he didn't have any money. Tim was sad and marched to the park without an ice cream. When Tim got to the park, he saw a funny clown. The clown was giving away tasty ice creams for free! Tim got his ice cream and marched around the park with a big smile. The day turned out to be great, even though he thought it wouldn't be.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw a big pile of clay near her house. "Wow, this clay is incredible!" she said. She wanted to make something special with it. Lucy started to raise the clay up with her hands. She made a tall tower. Her friend Tom came to see what she was doing. "Look, Tom! I made a tower with the clay!" she said. Tom thought it was very cool. Tom and Lucy played with the clay all day. They made more towers and even a little house. They were very happy with their incredible clay creations.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden with their water guns. They liked to spray each other and laugh. But they had to be careful not to wet the flowers or the grass, because Mom said they needed water to grow. "Can I spray you, Lily?" Tom asked. "OK, but not too much," Lily said. Tom sprayed Lily a little bit, and she giggled. Then she sprayed him back, and he smiled. They had fun with their water guns. But then, Tom noticed that his water gun had a leak. Water was dripping from a hole in the side. He tried to cover it with his hand, but it did not help. "Oh no, my water gun is broken!" Tom said. "Let me see," Lily said. She took his water gun and looked at it. She saw the leak too. "Maybe we can fix it," she said. "Do you have tape?" "I think so," Tom said. "Let's go to the house and ask Mom." They ran to the house with their water guns. They found Mom in the kitchen. "Mom, can we have some tape?" Tom asked. "What do you need tape for?" Mom asked. "My water gun has a leak," Tom said. "We want to fix it." Mom looked at their water guns. She saw that they were wet and dripping. "Did you wet the flowers or the grass?" she asked. "No, Mom, we were careful," Lily said. "That's good," Mom said. "I'm glad you listened to me. OK, you can have some tape. But be careful not to make a mess." She gave them some tape and they thanked her. They went back to the garden and tried to fix the leak. They wrapped the tape around the hole and pressed it hard. "Does it work?" Lily asked. Tom tried to spray his water gun. No water came out. "It works!" Tom said. "The leak is gone!" "Yay!" Lily said. "Now we can play some more!" They hugged each other and ran to the garden. They sprayed each other and laughed. They were happy with their water guns. Mom watched them from the window and smiled. She was proud of them. They were good kids.
