Category: Beginner
Title: The Board and the Rainbow

There was a board. It was big and flat. It seemed special. A little girl picked it up. She was very proud. She carried it around her house. The board felt good in her hands. The little girl had an idea. She put the board on the floor. She asked her mom for some markers. Her mom gave them to her. The little girl drew a big rainbow on the board. She smiled and said "I made this". The little girl felt so proud of herself. She showed the board to her dad. He smiled at her and said "That's very nice". The little girl was so happy. She was happy that she had made something so special. And she was proud of it too!

One day, a zebra named Zeb was walking in the forest. He met his friend, a big lion named Leo. Zeb liked to be useful and help his friends. Zeb asked Leo, "Do you need any help today?" Leo said, "Yes, I can't find my toy. Can you help me look for it?" Zeb and Leo looked all around the forest for the toy. They looked up and down, but they could not find it. Then, they heard a snap sound. It was a little bird with the toy in its beak! The bird had taken the toy to make a nest. Zeb and Leo were surprised. They asked the bird, "Can we have the toy back, please?" The bird said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know it was your toy. I just wanted to make a nest for my eggs." Zeb had an idea. He found some soft grass and gave it to the bird. The bird was happy and gave the toy back to Leo. Zeb and Leo thanked the bird and walked back home. They were happy to have the toy back and to have helped a new friend.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the quiet woods. She loved to listen to the birds and look at the pretty flowers. As she walked, she saw a big rock in her way. Sue tried to go around it, but she did not see another small rock and she stumbled. She fell down and started to cry. A kind squirrel saw Sue and came to help her. "Don't cry, little girl," said the squirrel. "I will help you get up." Sue stopped crying and thanked the squirrel. They walked together and found a shower of pretty, colorful leaves falling from the trees. Sue and the squirrel played and laughed under the shower of leaves. After playing for a while, the squirrel said, "Sue, it's getting late. You should go home now." Sue hugged the squirrel and said, "Thank you for helping me today. I learned that even when we stumble and fall, we can always get up and keep going with the help of our friends." Sue went home with a big smile on her face, and she never forgot the kind squirrel and the fun they had under the shower of leaves.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. A little boy named Tim lived near the tree. One day, Tim saw a big, sour lemon on the tree. He wanted to get the lemon, but it was too high for him. Tim saw a tall man walking by. He said, "Please, help me get the sour lemon." The man stared at the lemon and said, "Okay, I will help you." The tall man reached up and picked the sour lemon for Tim. Tim said, "Thank you!" and took the sour lemon home. He showed it to his mom. She made lemonade with it. Tim and his mom drank the lemonade together. They were happy, and the lemonade was not sour anymore. They shared it with the tall man, too.

Once, a pale boy named Tim had a dream. In his dream, he found a big trumpet. He picked it up and played it. The sound was loud and happy. Tim loved the trumpet and played it all day. One day, while Tim was playing the trumpet, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down from the sky. The bird was big and had many colors. The bird liked the sound of the trumpet. The bird asked Tim if he could play the trumpet too. Tim said yes and gave the bird the trumpet. But when the bird played the trumpet, it made a different sound. It was a soft and sweet sound. Tim was surprised and happy. Now, Tim and the bird played the trumpet together, making both loud and soft sounds. They were the best of friends.
