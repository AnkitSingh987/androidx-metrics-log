Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to clap her hands and dance around the house. One day, while she was dancing in the kitchen, she saw the refrigerator. Lily was hungry, so she opened the refrigerator door. Inside the refrigerator, she found a spicy snack. It was a little red pepper. Lily thought it looked yummy, so she took a big bite. Suddenly, her mouth felt very hot! Lily didn't like the spicy pepper, so she put it back in the refrigerator. Lily's mom saw her and knew she needed something to cool her mouth. She gave Lily a cold glass of milk. Lily drank the milk and felt much better. Then, Lily started to clap and dance again. This time, she danced away from the refrigerator, and she didn't eat any more spicy peppers.

---

Ben and Lily were playing on the beach. They saw a big yacht in the water. It was white and shiny and had a flag on it. "Wow, look at that yacht!" Ben said. "I want to go on it and see what's inside." "Me too!" Lily said. "Maybe there are toys and snacks and books on it." They ran to the edge of the water and waved at the yacht. A man was on the yacht. He saw them and waved back. "Hello, kids!" he shouted. "Do you like my yacht?" "Yes, we do!" Ben and Lily shouted. "Can we come on it, please?" The man smiled and threw a rope to them. "Sure, you can. But be careful, it's slippery. And don't touch anything, okay?" Ben and Lily nodded and grabbed the rope. They climbed on the yacht and looked around. They saw a lot of things they had never seen before. There were buttons and screens and knobs and wheels. There were also chairs and tables and beds and closets. "Wow, this is so cool!" Ben said. "Can I press this button?" "No, don't!" the man said. "That's the horn. It's very loud and it will scare the fish." "Okay, sorry," Ben said. He moved to another button. "What about this one?" "No, don't!" the man said. "That's the anchor. It will make the yacht stop and it will be hard to move again." "Okay, sorry," Ben said. He moved to another button. "What about this one?" "No, don't!" the man said. "That's the ejector seat. It will remove you from the yacht and throw you in the air." "Okay, sorry," Ben said. He moved to another button. "What about this one?" "NO, DON'T!" the man said. "That's the self-destruct. It will make the yacht explode and we will all be gone." "Okay, sorry," Ben said. He moved away from the buttons. He saw a cute teddy bear on a bed. He picked it up and hugged it. "Can I have this?" The man sighed and smiled. "Yes, you can have that. It's my son's. He left it here when he went to school. He won't mind if you take it. He has many more at home." "Thank you!" Ben said. He was happy with the teddy bear. He gave it to Lily. "Here, you can have it. You like teddy bears more than me." "Thank you!" Lily said. She was happy with the teddy bear. She hugged it and kissed it. "It's so soft and cute. I love it." The man was happy to see them happy. He said, "You're welcome, kids. You're very nice and polite. Do you want to stay on the yacht for a while and have some juice and cookies?" "Yes, please!" Ben and Lily said. They followed the man to the kitchen. They had some juice and cookies and talked to the man. They learned his name was Tom and he was a sailor. He told them stories about the sea and the places he had been to. They liked him and his yacht. They had a lot of fun.

One day, a girl named Sue found a veil in her room. She put it on her head and went outside to play. Sue saw her friend Tom and showed him the veil. Tom thought it was very cool. While they played, a poor cat came near them. The cat was hungry and scared. Sue and Tom wanted to help the cat, so they gave it some food. The cat was happy and started to follow them. Suddenly, the cat jumped and took the veil from Sue's head. They tried to catch the cat, but it was too fast. Then, the cat yelled, "Thank you!" Sue and Tom were surprised. The cat could talk! The cat told them it was a magic cat and the veil helped it talk. Now, they had a new magic friend to play with.
