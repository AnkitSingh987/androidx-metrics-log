Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Adventurous Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous dog named Spot. Spot loved to spin around and around in the park. He would spin so fast that he would get dizzy and fall down. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, Spot heard about a big show called "opera." He wanted to go to the opera and spin for everyone to see. Spot thought it would be fun to show off his spinning trick. He was very excited to go on a new adventure. When Spot got to the opera, he started to spin. But the people at the opera did not like it. They said, "Stop spinning, Spot!" Spot was sad that they did not like his trick. He left the opera and went back to the park, feeling very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because he was going to raise a new pet. He had a big space in his room for the pet to play. One day, Tim saw a big box in his room. He knew it was his pet. He opened the box and saw a little green egg. Tim was so happy! He knew he had to take care of the egg until it was ready to hatch. Every day, Tim would watch the egg and wait. He knew something big was going to happen. And one day, the egg started to crack! Tim was so excited to see his pet. But when the egg hatched, out came a little green dinosaur! Tim was so surprised, but he loved his new pet dinosaur even more. They played together in the big space in his room and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish lived in a creek. The fish liked to swim and play all day long. One day, the fish wanted to be successful. The fish wanted to jump high out of the water. But the fish started to worry. The fish thought, "What if I can't jump high?" The fish tried and tried. At first, the fish could not jump high. But the fish did not give up. The fish kept trying. And one day, the fish jumped very high out of the water. The fish was happy and successful.

Tim and Sam were playing with their cars on the floor. They liked to make noises and pretend they were racing. Tim had a red car and Sam had a blue car. They were having fun. But then, Tim saw a shiny toy truck on the shelf. He wanted to play with it. He paused his car and said to Sam, "Look, a truck! Can I have it?" Sam looked at the truck and said, "No, that's mine. You have your car. Play with your car." Tim did not listen. He got up and reached for the truck. He pulled it off the shelf and said, "Ha, ha, I have the truck now!" Sam was angry. He got up and ran after Tim. He tried to grab the truck from him. They started to fight. They pushed and pulled and shouted. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Tim and Sam fighting over the truck. She was not happy. She took the truck from them and said, "Stop it, both of you! You are not sharing. You are not being patient. You are not being kind. You are being naughty. Now, you have to go to your rooms and think about what you did. No more playing today." Tim and Sam felt sad. They had lost their fun. They had lost their truck. They had lost their mom's smile. They wished they had not fought. They wished they had shared. They wished they had been patient and kind. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. Fighting is not. Be patient and kind with your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with an open door. The man and the wife were always happy and they would smile all day long. One sunny day, the man and his wife went for a walk in the woods. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. The tree had a door on it. The man and his wife looked at each other and smiled. They opened the door and went inside. Inside the tree, they found a big room with lots of toys and candy. The man and his wife played with the toys and ate the candy. They smiled and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The toys and candy started to talk! The man and his wife were surprised, but they smiled and played with their new talking friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
