Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Gray Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little gray mouse named Max. Max lived in a small house made of dust. One day, Max saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was looking for a friend to play with. Max said, "I can show you my dust house, Tom. We can play there." Tom was very happy and followed Max to his dust house. They played and had fun together. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard and the dust house was gone! Max and Tom were sad. Tom said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you build a new house." So Max and Tom built a new house together, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. He had a big, pretty gift. He wanted to wrap it for his friend's birthday. Tom was very good at wrapping gifts. All his friends liked his gift wraps. One day, a person named Sam saw Tom wrapping the gift. Sam was envious. He wanted to wrap gifts like Tom. Sam asked Tom to teach him. But Tom said no. He wanted to be the only one who could wrap gifts well. Sam was sad. He tried to wrap a gift by himself. But it did not look nice. The gift fell and broke. Sam cried. Tom saw Sam cry and felt bad. But he still did not help Sam. At the party, Tom gave his friend the pretty wrapped gift. But the friend did not like it. The friend said a gift is not about how it looks, but about the love we put into it. Tom felt sad. He wished he had helped Sam. The moral of the story is to help others and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a black helmet. He wore it all the time. He liked to complete puzzles with his helmet on. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, Tim found a big puzzle. He was excited to complete it. He started to put the pieces together. But something unexpected happened. The puzzle came to life and turned into a little dog! The little dog was friendly. It liked Tim's black helmet. They played together all day. Tim was happy. The little dog was his new best friend. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a big dog named Max put on his suit. He wanted to look nice for his friend's party. Max was a tough dog who loved to jog. Max went to the park to jog before the party. He met another dog named Sam. Sam said, "I like your suit, Max! Let's jog together." So, they jogged and had fun. Max didn't know that Sam liked to play tricks. At the party, Max saw his friend, the birthday dog, named Tim. Tim said, "Wow, Max! Your suit is all dirty!" Max was sad. Then, he saw Sam laughing. Max knew that Sam played a trick on him by making him jog in his nice suit. Max was upset, but he had an idea. He went to the pond and jumped in. The water cleaned his suit. Max went back to the party, and everyone was happy. Sam said, "I'm sorry, Max. I didn't mean to make you sad." Max forgave Sam, and they all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mary. Mary loved to go to the port and watch the boats go by. One day, Mary went to the port with her parents. At the port, Mary saw a giant boat. It was so big, she could barely see the top of it. Mary asked her mom why the boat was so big. Her mom calmly explained that it was a cargo boat, used to transport things all around the world. Mary thought that it was very cool and yelled out "Look at this boat, it's so big!" But her mom told her to calm down and keep her voice down. Mary didn't want to get into trouble so she stayed quiet until they got back home. Mary always remembered the big boat and fondly looked back on when she had gone to the port with her parents.
