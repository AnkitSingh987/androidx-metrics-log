Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Tree

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to look for pebbles of different colors and shapes. They had a small box where they kept their pebbles. They were very proud of their collection. One day, they saw a big pebble that was shiny and smooth. It was under a tree. They ran to get it, but they saw a lock on the tree. The lock had a keyhole. They wondered what was behind the lock. "Maybe there are more pebbles inside the tree," Lila said. "Or maybe there is a treasure," Tom said. They looked around for a key, but they could not find one. They tried to pull the lock, but it was too strong. They felt sad and frustrated. Then, they saw a man walking by. He had a bunch of keys in his hand. He looked friendly and kind. "Excuse me, sir," Lila said. "Do you know how to unlock this lock?" The man smiled and came closer. He looked at the lock and the keyhole. "I think I have the right key for this lock," he said. He took one of his keys and put it in the keyhole. He turned it and the lock opened. Lila and Tom clapped and cheered. They thanked the man and opened the tree. Inside, they saw a lot of pebbles of different colors and shapes. They were so happy and excited. They picked some of the pebbles and put them in their box. They had a very successful day. They had found a new friend and a new pebble. They hugged each other and smiled.

Once upon a time there was a little girl and boy who felt very hungry. So they asked their mum, "Mum, can we have some food?" But their mum said, "No, you can't have any food. You have to wait until dinner." The little girl and boy were so confused and they started to shiver. Then suddenly a big wave came along and swept away all their food! They searched around, but they couldn't find any food. The little girl and boy started to cry. Their mum said, "I told you that you had to wait for dinner!" The little girl and boy had to go hungry and they never ate that day. The End.

Lily and Ben were hungry. They wanted to eat soup for lunch. Mom said she had some soup available in the pot. She told them to wait at the table while she got some bowls and spoons. But Lily and Ben did not wait. They were too curious. They wanted to see the soup. They climbed on the chairs and looked inside the pot. The soup was hot and steamy. It smelled good. They saw some carrots and noodles and chicken in the soup. "Can we have some now?" Lily asked. "Maybe just a little bit," Ben said. They reached for the ladle. But they were not careful. They bumped the pot. The pot tipped over. The soup spilled out. It splashed on the floor and the table and the chairs. It made a big mess. "Oh no!" Lily and Ben cried. Mom heard the noise. She came back to the kitchen. She saw the soup everywhere. She saw Lily and Ben with wet and sticky clothes. She was not happy. "What happened?" Mom asked. "The soup disappeared!" Lily and Ben said.

Ben and Tom were brothers. They liked to play with toys and run in the park. But sometimes they fought over things. One day, they found a shiny watch on the ground. It had a big face and a leather band. It looked very nice. "Wow, look at this watch!" Ben said. "It must be very expensive. I want it!" "No, I saw it first!" Tom said. "It is mine!" They both grabbed the watch and pulled hard. They did not want to share. They shouted and pushed each other. "Let go, you foolish boy!" Ben said. He was angry and slapped Tom on the face. "Ow, that hurts!" Tom said. He was sad and slapped Ben back. They slapped and slapped until the watch broke. The face cracked and the band snapped. The watch was ruined. They both stopped and looked at it. They felt sorry and ashamed. "Look what we did!" Ben said. "We broke the watch. It was so pretty. Now it is ugly." "We were so silly!" Tom said. "We fought over nothing. Now we have nothing." They heard a voice behind them. It was a man. He looked angry and scared. "Hey, you kids!" the man said. "That was my watch! I dropped it when I was running from the police. It was very important. It had a secret chip inside. Now you broke it and they will find me!" The man grabbed the broken watch and ran away. He did not say thank you or sorry. He was a bad man. Ben and Tom looked at each other. They felt very bad. They wished they had not fought over the watch. They wished they had been nice and shared. They wished they had left the watch alone. They learned a hard lesson. They hugged and said sorry. They hoped the bad man would not come back. They hoped the police would catch him. They hoped they would never see the watch again.
