Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and Lily and the Tree

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk with his friend, a little girl named Lily. Spot liked to walk on his heel, because it was easy for him to do. They walked and talked, and Lily was happy to explain things to Spot. "Spot," Lily said, "when you walk on your heel, you are very good. It is easy for you, isn't it?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, Lily! It is easy and fun!" As they walked, they saw a big tree. "What is that?" asked Spot. "That is a tree," Lily explained. "Trees have leaves and can give us shade." Spot liked the tree and decided to rest under it. They sat under the tree and talked some more. Spot asked Lily to explain more things to him. They had a fun day, and Spot learned a lot from his friend Lily. In the end, they walked back home, with Spot walking on his heel, and Lily explaining all the things they saw.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to keep his room very tidy. Every day, he would pick up his toys and put them away. He liked his room to be clean and neat. One day, Tim's mom gave him a cup of yogurt to eat. Tim loved yogurt! He was so excited to eat it. But Tim was worried about making a mess in his tidy room. He thought, "I don't want to spoil my clean room with yogurt." So, Tim went outside to eat his yogurt. He sat under a big tree and ate his yummy yogurt. When he was done, he threw the cup away in the trash. Tim was happy because he did not spoil his tidy room. And he had a fun time eating his yogurt outside.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. The sun was mild, and the sky was blue. Tim was happy to be outside. He saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. While sitting, he heard a sound in the bushes. "Who is there?" Tim asked. Out of the bushes came a small, copper dog. The dog said, "Hi, I am Copper. I lost my ball. Can you help me find it?" Tim said, "Yes, I will help you." So, they both went to look for the ball. As they walked, Tim saw a big rock. He did not see it in time and stumbled. He fell down, but he was okay. Tim got up and saw the ball behind the rock. "I found it!" Tim said. Copper wagged his tail and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They played with the ball and became best friends.

One day, a blue bird named Bob lived in a big tree. Bob loved to sing. He would sing all day long. He had a friend named Tim, who was a guard dog. Tim would sit under the tree and listen to Bob sing. One day, while Bob was singing, a big cat named Max came by. Max wanted to catch Bob. Tim saw Max and said, "Don't touch my friend Bob!" Max got scared and ran away. Tim was a good guard. Later, Bob and Tim were playing near the tree. Suddenly, Max came back with a big hat on his head. He started to sing too! Bob and Tim were surprised. Max said, "I don't want to catch you, Bob. I just want to sing with you." So, Bob, Tim, and Max all sang together and became good friends.

Once there was a kind and compassionate boy named George. He wanted to help everyone he met. One day, George was walking in the woods and he saw a shade in trouble. He noticed that it was burning from the sun heat. George ran over to help. "Shade, I'm here to help you!" he said, his voice full of compassion. The shade was relieved. "Thank you, George! It's so hot. Do you have a way I can cool down?" George thought carefully. Suddenly, he had an idea. He found a big leaf and held it over the shade. Instantly the shade didn't feel so hot any more. "Thank you George!" said the shade. "Your kind heart has saved me!" George was glad. With a cheerful smile, he said, "You're welcome! That's what friends are for!"
