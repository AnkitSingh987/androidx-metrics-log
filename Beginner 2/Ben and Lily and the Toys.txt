Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Toys

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars and dolls. They have a lot of toys in their room. But sometimes they forget to put them away. Then their room is messy and their mom is not happy. One day, mom says, "Ben and Lily, you have to gather your toys and make your room tidy. If you do, you can have a treat. But if you don't, you can't go to the park." Ben and Lily want to go to the park. They love to swing and slide and run. But they have so many toys to gather. They feel tired and bored. They start to whine and cry. Mom hears them and comes to their room. She says, "Ben and Lily, why are you crying? You can gather your toys faster and easier if you work as a team. A team is when you help each other and share the work. Then you can have fun and finish sooner." Ben and Lily think about it. They decide to try to be a team. They pick up their blocks and cars and dolls and put them in their boxes and baskets and shelves. They help each other and say thank you and good job. They make their room tidy and neat. Mom sees their room and smiles. She says, "Ben and Lily, you did a great job! You gathered your toys and made your room tidy. You worked as a team and you were kind and polite. I'm very proud of you. Now you can have a treat and go to the park." Ben and Lily are happy. They hug mom and say thank you. They get their treat and go to the park. They swing and slide and run. They have fun and laugh. They are a good team.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a noisy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and jump all day long. He had a friend named Tom, a little boy who lived next door. Tom and Spot played together every day. One day, Tom and Spot found a big jar of sugar in the kitchen. They wanted to eat the sweet sugar, but Tom's mom said, "No, sugar is not good for you." Tom and Spot were sad, but they listened to Tom's mom. Later, Tom and Spot went outside to play. They ran and jumped and made lots of noise. After a while, they got tired and decided to settle down under a big tree. They sat quietly, listening to the birds sing. And even though they didn't get to eat the sugar, Tom and Spot were still very happy because they had each other.

One day, a cat named Tom found a weird newspaper in the park. Tom looked at the newspaper and shivered. He did not know what it was. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. "Max, look at this weird newspaper I found!" Tom showed Max the newspaper. Max shivered too. "I don't know what it is, Tom," said Max. "Let's ask our friend, the bird." Tom and Max went to the bird's tree. The bird looked at the newspaper and shivered. "I have an idea," said the bird. "Let's open it and read it." They opened the newspaper and found a map. The map showed them a way to a big treasure. They followed the map and found a big box. Tom, Max, and the bird were very excited. They opened the box and saw a big, scary monster. The monster jumped out and chased them away. Tom, Max, and the bird were sad. They did not get the treasure and they were very scared. The weird newspaper was not good after all.

Lily liked to cook with her mom. She helped to stir the soup, cut the bread and wash the salad. She wanted to be a smart cook like her mom when she grew up. One day, Lily's mom had to go to the doctor. She was not feeling well and had a sore throat. She told Lily to stay with her dad and be a good girl. Lily was sad and worried. She wanted to make her mom feel better. She had an idea. She decided to cook a special soup for her mom. She went to the kitchen and looked for the things she needed. She found some carrots, potatoes, chicken and water. She put them all in a big pot and turned on the stove. She did not know how long to cook the soup. She did not know how much salt to add. She did not know how to use the knife safely. She did not hear her dad calling her name. Soon, the soup was boiling and bubbling. It smelled good, but it also spilled over the pot and made a mess. The knife slipped from Lily's hand and cut her finger. She cried out loud. Her dad came running to the kitchen. He saw the soup, the knife and the blood. He was angry and scared. He turned off the stove and took Lily to the sink. He washed her finger and put a band-aid on it. "Lily, what were you doing?" he asked. "You could have burned yourself or hurt yourself badly. You are not supposed to cook alone. You are too little for that." Lily sobbed. "I wanted to cook for mom. I wanted to make her happy. I wanted to be a smart cook like her." Her dad hugged her and wiped her tears. "I know, sweetie. I know you love your mom and you want to help. But cooking is not for little kids. You have to wait until you are older and learn from your mom. She is a smart cook, but she also knows how to be safe and careful. She will be proud of you for trying, but she will also be worried about you. Do you understand?" Lily nodded. She felt sorry for making a mess and hurting herself. She also felt proud for wanting to do something nice for her mom. She hoped her mom would get better soon and come back home. She said to her dad, "I love you, dad. I love mom. Can we make a card for her and say we miss her?" Her dad smiled. "Of course, we can. That is a great idea. Come on, let's go to the living room and find some paper and crayons. And maybe later, we can make some cookies together. How does that sound?" Lily smiled back. She liked cookies. She liked her dad. She liked to cook with her mom. She was a good girl. She was a smart girl.
