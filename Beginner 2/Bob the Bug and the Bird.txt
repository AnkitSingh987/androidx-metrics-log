Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Bug and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Bob. Bob loved to crawl on the ground and look for food. One day, he found a big meal. It was a big pile of crumbs. Bob was very happy. Bob started to eat his meal when he saw another bug named Tim. Tim looked upset. Bob asked, "Why are you upset, Tim?" Tim said, "I'm hungry and I can't find any food." Bob wanted to help Tim, so he shared his meal with him. But, as they were eating, a big bird came down and saw them. The bird was also hungry. The bird ate Bob and Tim's meal and then ate Bob and Tim too. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray cat. The cat liked to sleep all day. When the cat slept, it would dream. In the cat's dream, it saw a big hand. The hand was nice and warm. The cat wanted to touch the hand, so it walked closer. When the cat touched the hand, the hand began to pet the cat. The cat was very happy. It loved the dream and the warm hand. The cat smiled and slept more, dreaming of the hand every day.

One day, a wild cat and a small dog met in the park. The wild cat had a big tooth that was hurting. The small dog saw the wild cat and said, "Why are you sad?" The wild cat told the small dog, "My big tooth hurts. I don't know what to do." The small dog thought for a moment and said, "I know! Let's go see the wise old owl. He will know what to do." So, the wild cat and the small dog went to see the wise old owl. The owl looked at the wild cat's big tooth and said, "You need to shut your mouth and not eat for one day. Then, your tooth will not hurt." The wild cat did not like this idea but agreed to try it. The next day, the wild cat and the small dog met again in the park. The wild cat was very happy. "My tooth does not hurt anymore!" he said. The small dog was glad to help his new friend. They played together all day and became best friends. And the wild cat learned that sometimes, even a small dog can have big ideas.

Once upon a time there was a little kid called Bobby. He was so happy playing in the garden with his friends. But then Bobby saw a spot in the grass. He didn't know what it was so he touched it. Suddenly, Bobby started to weep. His friends were so sad to see him crying. They asked Bobby what was wrong and he explained that when he touched the spot, it made him so sad. Bobby's friends tried to cheer him up. They gave him some of their favourite toys and said they would play together all day. Slowly, Bobby's tears stopped and he smiled. He thanked his friends for being so kind, and they all went to play together. The sun was so warm and the sky was so blue. Bobby was so happy again, he forgot all about the spot in the grass. He laughed and ran around in the sunshine with his friends, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Max and Jay. Max was very thin and Jay was very thick. Every day they would play together and have lots of fun. One day, Max and Jay were walking together. Suddenly, Jay stopped and said, "Look! A lung!" Max nodded to show he saw it. The lung was sitting in the middle of the forest and it looked very strange. Max wanted to touch the lung but Jay said, "No, we don't know what would happen. Let's not touch it". But Max didn't listen and he touched the lung. Suddenly, something terrible happened. Clouds filled the sky, thunder roared and a storm began. Max and Jay tried to run away but the thick winds were blowing them back. Max and Jay were so scared. The storm went on for hours and eventually the friends passed out from exhaustion. When they woke up, they were both very sick. Max had a bad cough and Jay had a tightness in his lung. They knew the storm had been caused by the lung, so they both nodded to each other and were never the same again.
