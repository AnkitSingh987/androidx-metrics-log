Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Wagon

One day, a boy named Tim found a red wagon. The wagon was not high. It was just right for him. He wanted to play with the wagon. He thought of all the fun things he could do with it. Tim saw his friend Sue. "Sue, look at my wagon!" he said. "Let's play together!" Sue liked the wagon too. She wanted to play with Tim. They took turns pulling each other in the wagon. They laughed and had so much fun. Then, they saw a big hill. "Let's go up the hill!" said Tim. Sue agreed. They pushed the wagon up the high hill. At the top, Tim said, "Sue, I will give you a ride down the hill!" Sue was happy. She got in the wagon and Tim pushed her down the hill. They both laughed and played with the wagon all day long.

Once, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big slide and was very excited. He climbed up the steps, but when he looked down, he felt frightened. His stomach felt funny, and he didn't want to slide down anymore. Tim's friend, Sally, saw him and came to help. She said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will slide with you." They held hands, and together they slid down the twisty slide. The twist in the slide made Tim's stomach feel even more funny, but he was not frightened anymore. At the end of the slide, they found a big surprise. There was a big box of toys waiting for them! Tim and Sally played with the toys all day. The twist in the slide had led them to something fun and unexpected. They were so happy they had faced their fears and slid down the twisty slide.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with shapes. They have a box of shapes. They have circles, squares, triangles and stars. One day, they are playing with the shapes on the floor. Lily has a circle. She says, "Look, Ben, I have a circle. It is round and smooth. Do you like circles?" Ben nods. He says, "Yes, I like circles. They are fun to roll and spin. Can I have a circle too?" Lily smiles. She says, "Sure, Ben, you can have a circle. Here, take this one. It is red and shiny. It is a nice circle." Ben takes the circle. He says, "Thank you, Lily. This is a nice circle. It is red like an apple. Do you know what I can do with this circle?" Lily shakes her head. She says, "No, Ben, what can you do with this circle?" Ben grins. He says, "I can eat this circle. Watch me!" He opens his mouth and pretends to bite the circle. He makes a crunching sound. He says, "Mmm, this circle is yummy. It tastes like candy. Do you want to try?" Lily laughs. She says, "No, Ben, you are silly. You cannot eat circles. They are not food. They are toys. You can play with them, but you cannot eat them." Ben giggles. He says, "I know, Lily, I am just joking. I do not really eat circles. I just pretend. It is fun to pretend. Do you want to pretend with me?" Lily nods. She says, "Yes, Ben, I want to pretend with you. You are a fun friend. Let's pretend we are animals. What animal do you want to be?" Ben thinks. He says, "I want to be a lion. Lions are strong and brave. They roar and run. They eat meat. What animal do you want to be?" Lily thinks. She says, "I want to be a bird. Birds are fast and free. They fly and sing. They eat seeds and worms." Ben and Lily are enthusiastic. They start to act like animals. They use the shapes to make their sounds and food. They have a lot of fun. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to listen to her mom read poems. One day, Lily felt very tired. She asked her mom to read her a poem to help her sleep. Her mom read a poem about being kind and helping others. Lily liked the poem a lot. She asked her mom, "What does the poem mean?" Her mom replied, "It means we should always be nice to others and help them when they need it." The next day, Lily saw a boy who was sad. She remembered the poem and wanted to help. She was nice to the boy and they became friends. Lily learned that being kind and helpful made her happy. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others.
