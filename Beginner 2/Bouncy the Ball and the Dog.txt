Category: Beginner
Title: Bouncy the Ball and the Dog

One day, a little ball named Bouncy went outside to play. Bouncy loved to bounce up and down, side to side. It was a cold day, but Bouncy did not care. Bouncy just wanted to play and have fun. While Bouncy was playing, a big dog named Barky came running up. Barky saw Bouncy and wanted to play too. Barky barked, "Hey Bouncy, can I play with you?" Bouncy was scared of the big dog, but said, "Okay, but please be nice." Barky started to play with Bouncy. They bounced up and down, side to side. But Barky was too big and rough. Bouncy got hurt and started to cry. Barky felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Bouncy. I didn't mean to hurt you." Bouncy was sad, but knew Barky was sorry. Bouncy said, "It's okay, Barky. Let's just be more careful when we play." Barky agreed, and they played together gently. Bouncy and Barky became good friends, and they had lots of fun playing together on the cold day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to run and play outside. He liked to look up at the sky and watch the birds fly by. One day, while Tom was running, he saw a big tree with sour fruit. He wanted to taste it. He called his friend, Sam, and said, "Look at that tree! Let's try the sour fruit." Sam said, "No, I don't want to. I heard it's not good." But Tom didn't listen. Tom climbed the tree and picked the sour fruit. He took a big bite and made a funny face. It was very sour! Tom felt sick and couldn't run or play anymore. Sam looked up at the sky and said, "I told you not to eat it. Now you can't play with me." And Sam went to play alone, leaving Tom under the tree feeling sad and sick.

One day, a boy named Tim found a suit. The suit was big and looked foolish. Tim thought it would be fun to wear the suit. So, he put it on and went outside to play. Tim saw his friend Sam. Sam laughed and said, "You look funny in that silly suit!" Tim laughed too. They decided to play a game. They wanted to throw a ball to each other. Tim and Sam took turns throwing the ball. The big, foolish suit made it hard for Tim to catch the ball. But they still had fun playing together. Soon, it was time to go home. "Bye, Tim!" said Sam. "I liked playing with you in your funny suit." Tim smiled and said, "Bye, Sam! I had fun too!" Tim went home, took off the silly suit, and put it away. He knew he would wear it again for more fun with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and his ball. One day, Tim found a long cable in his toy box. He did not know what it was for, but he thought it was fun to play with. Tim decided to supply the cable to his toy cars, so they could go faster. He tied the cable to the back of his toy car and pulled it. The car went fast across the floor, and Tim laughed. He played with the cable and the cars all day long. At the end of the day, Tim's mom saw the cable and told him it was not a toy. She said it was for the TV, but she was glad he had fun. Tim gave the cable back to his mom, and she put it away. Tim went to bed, and he dreamed of playing with the cable and his toy cars again.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow candy. The candy was shiny and new, but the little girl who found it wanted it to be even shinier. So she asked her Mommy if she could polish it up. "Of course you can," Mommy said. She gave the little girl a cloth and taught her how to rub the candy in circles. The little girl smiled and rubbed. She used the cloth to make the candy so shiny that it sparkled in the sunlight. After she was done, the little girl held it up proudly. "Look, Mommy!" she said. "My candy is so yellow and shiny now!" Mommy smiled. "That's wonderful sweetheart," she said. "Now, why don't you go outside and enjoy it with your friends?" The little girl took the candy and skipped outside. She had no idea that the candy would make her friends so happy. But Mommy had a plan. She knew that when the little girl shared the candy, it would bring everyone joy.
