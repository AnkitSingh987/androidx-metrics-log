Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Castle and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a tall castle. In the castle, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big sneeze. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car when he felt a sneeze coming. "Achoo!" Tim sneezed very loud. His friend, Sue, heard the sneeze and came running. "Wow, Tim! That was a big sneeze!" Sue said. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, it was a big sneeze. Let's play together." Tim and Sue played in the tall castle. They had fun running and laughing. Then, Tim felt another sneeze coming. "Achoo!" he sneezed again. This time, the sneeze was so big that it made the tall castle shake. Sue looked at Tim and said, "Your sneeze is very strong, Tim!" Tim laughed and said, "Yes, my sneeze is strong. But we can still play and have fun in our tall castle." So, Tim and Sue played in the tall castle all day. They had lots of fun, even with Tim's big sneezes. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the snow. He liked to make snowballs and throw them at trees. One day, Tim saw a sign near his house. The sign had a picture of a big, fearful dog. Tim did not know what the sign said, but he was scared of the big dog. Tim wanted to play in the snow, but he was afraid of the big, fearful dog. He decided to go outside and play anyway. He put on his warm coat, hat, and gloves. He opened the door and went out into the snow. Tim played in the snow, but he was always looking out for the big dog. As Tim played, he heard a loud bark. The big, fearful dog from the sign was running towards him. Tim was very scared. He tried to run away, but the dog caught up to him. The dog jumped on Tim and knocked him down in the snow. Tim cried and cried, but nobody heard him. The big, fearful dog left Tim alone in the snow, and Tim never wanted to play outside again.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She liked to act like she was a hero. Mia would put on a cape and jump around, saving her toys from danger. It was easy for her to pretend and have fun. In her story, there was a big fire in the toy city. The fire was hot and scary. Mia, the hero, knew she had to save her toy friends. She put on her cape and got ready to help. Mia acted fast and strong. She picked up her toys and moved them away from the fire. The toys were happy and thanked her for being brave. Mia felt proud and knew that being a hero was a good thing to do.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, smooth rock. He liked to play with it. He rolled it, threw it, and even sat on it. Tim had a lot of fun with his rock. He called it his "smooth friend". One sunny day, Tim saw a man on a motorcycle. The man yelled, "Hi, Tim!" Tim yelled back, "Hi!" The man stopped his motorcycle and looked at Tim's smooth rock. He said, "That's a nice rock, Tim. But you should be careful when you play with it. It can hurt someone if you throw it too hard." Tim thought about what the man said. He didn't want to hurt anyone with his smooth rock. So, he decided to play with it more carefully. He didn't throw it as hard, and he made sure no one was close when he rolled it. Soon, other kids saw Tim playing with his smooth rock. They wanted to play with it too. Tim shared his rock and taught them how to play safely. The kids all had fun and no one got hurt. The moral of the story is to be careful when playing with things that can hurt others, and to always share and play safely with friends.
