Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Max and the Car

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play with toys. Lily has a new toy. It is a big red car. She can push it and make it go fast. Max likes the car too. He wants to play with it. "Can I have a turn?" Max asks Lily. "No, it is mine. You have your own toys," Lily says. She holds the car close to her. Max feels jealous. He thinks Lily is not nice. He waits for Lily to look away. Then he grabs the car and runs. Lily sees him and gets angry. She runs after him. "Give it back! Give it back!" Lily shouts. Max does not listen. He runs to the garden. He sees a big hole. He thinks it is a good place to hide the car. He throws the car in the hole. He smiles. He thinks Lily will not find it. But the hole is not empty. It is the home of a big dog. The dog hears the car. He wakes up. He sees the car. He thinks it is a bone. He opens his mouth. He bites the car. He makes a loud noise. Lily and Max hear the noise. They go to the hole. They see the dog. They see the car in his mouth. They are scared. They cry. "Help! Help!" they scream. The dog does not like the noise. He does not like the car. He spits out the car. He barks at Lily and Max. He chases them away. Lily and Max run to the house. They hug each other. They are sorry. They say sorry. "I am sorry I took your car," Max says. "I am sorry I did not share with you," Lily says. They are friends again. They decide to play with other toys. They leave the car alone. They do not want to make the dog angry again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together. Yesterday, they went to a big park. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lucy said, "Let's look in the hole!" Tom said, "Okay, but we must behave." They looked in the hole and saw something unknown. It was small and round. They were scared, but they wanted to know what it was. They picked up the unknown thing and it started to move! It was a little bug. The bug said, "Hello! I am lost. Can you help me?" Lucy and Tom were surprised, but they helped the bug find its way home. They were happy to help the bug and they behaved very well.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside with her friends. They would run, jump, and do exercise together. Amy also loved to hold her mom's hand when they walked to the park. One day, Amy saw a big, sour lemon lying on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her friends. "Look what I found!" she said. Her friend Timmy asked, "What will you do with it?" Amy thought for a moment and said, "I will share it with everyone!" Amy and her friends each took a small bite of the sour lemon. They all made funny faces because it was very sour. Then, Amy's mom came over and said, "It's great that you shared the lemon with your friends, Amy. Sharing is a kind thing to do." From that day on, Amy and her friends learned the importance of sharing. They would always share their toys and treats with each other. They all became even better friends and had lots of fun together. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her friends. One day, they had a race. Sue wanted to win. She ran very fast. Sue won the race. Her friends clapped for her. Sue was happy. Sue's friend, Tom, had a dull toy. It had a stitch that was coming out. Sue saw Tom was sad. She wanted to help. Sue asked her mom for a needle and thread. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sue." Sue took the needle and thread to fix Tom's toy. Sue was very careful. She put the stitch back in the toy. The toy was fixed! Tom was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue felt good helping her friend. The moral of the story is: it is important to help others when they need it.
