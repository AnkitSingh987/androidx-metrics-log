Category: Beginner
Title: John and the Hill

John liked to take walks with his truck. He always wore white shoes that twinkled in the sun. One day, he and his truck went for a long walk. They walked gracefully through the park and up onto the hill. John and his truck stopped to rest at the top of the hill. While they rested, they looked around to see the sights. They saw birds flying gracefully in the sky. They watched bunnies hopping across the grass. John and his truck hopped onto their feet again and kept walking. They passed tall trees and chirping birds. The truck made a loud rumble as they continued their graceful walk. Finally, they stopped. John hugged his truck and he said goodbye. It was time for John to go home. His last, graceful walk with his truck was finished.

---

Ben and Lily liked to go to the farm with their dad. They saw many animals there, like cows, pigs, chickens and horses. But their favorite animal was the mule. The mule was big and brown and had long ears. He was very strong and could carry heavy things. One day, dad gave Ben and Lily a basket of eggs. He said, "These are fragile eggs. They can break easily. You can take them to the mule and he will help you bring them to the barn. But be careful and gentle with the eggs and the mule." Ben and Lily nodded and took the basket. They walked slowly to the mule, who was eating grass. They said hello to the mule and he looked at them with his big eyes. They put the basket on his back and tied it with a rope. They thanked the mule and said, "We will go with you to the barn. You are a good mule." The mule nodded and started to walk. Ben and Lily followed him. They were happy and proud. They talked to the mule and told him stories. The mule listened and made funny noises. He was happy and friendly. They reached the barn and saw dad waiting for them. He smiled and said, "Well done, Ben and Lily. You and the mule did a great job. The eggs are safe and sound. You are very careful and gentle. The mule likes you and you like him. You are good friends." Ben and Lily hugged the mule and said, "We love you, mule. You are the best." The mule nuzzled them and said, "I love you, too. You are the best."

---

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But they did not like to share. They always wanted the same toy or the same spot. One day, they saw a shiny red shoe on the grass. It was big and had a button on the side. Lily ran to the shoe and picked it up. She wanted to try it on. "Give me the shoe!" Ben shouted. He ran after Lily and tried to grab the shoe. "It is mine!" "No, it is mine!" Lily shouted back. She held the shoe tight and pushed Ben away. "You have your own shoes!" They pulled and pushed and yelled. They did not see the man who was looking for his shoe. He was angry and sad. His shoe was broken. The button was gone and the sole was torn. "Hey, you two! What are you doing with my shoe?" the man said. He walked to Lily and Ben and took the shoe from them. "You spoiled my shoe! It was a special shoe. It could make music and light. But now it is broken. You are bad kids!" Lily and Ben felt sorry and scared. They did not know the shoe was special. They did not mean to break it. They looked at each other and said, "We are sorry, mister. We did not know. We just wanted to play." The man sighed and shook his head. He said, "You should not take things that are not yours. You should not fight over toys. You should share and be nice. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Yes, we understand. We will share and be nice. We will not spoil things." The man smiled and said, "That is good. Now, go and play. But be careful and kind. And if you see something that is not yours, leave it alone." Lily and Ben said, "Okay, thank you, mister." They gave the shoe back to the man and ran to the swings. They decided to take turns and have fun. They learned their lesson. They did not want to spoil anything again.
