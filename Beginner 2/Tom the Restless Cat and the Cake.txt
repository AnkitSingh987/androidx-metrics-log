Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Restless Cat and the Cake

One day, a restless cat named Tom wanted to do a job. He wanted to help his friend, Sam the dog, make a big cake. They wanted to make the cake for their friend's birthday. Tom and Sam were very excited to work together. Tom started to stir the cake mix. He stirred and stirred, but something unexpected happened. The cake mix turned into a big, colorful ball. Tom and Sam looked at the ball, and they did not know what to do. Just then, their friend, the birthday bird, came in. She saw the big, colorful ball and was very happy. She said it was the best birthday gift ever. Tom and Sam were surprised but happy too. They had a fun party with the big, colorful ball, and everyone had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cabin in the woods. It was a small and nice place to live. A girl named Lily and her dog, Spot, lived in the cabin. One day, Lily asked Spot, "Do you want to go for a walk?" Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Yes!" So, they left the cabin and walked in the woods. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and happy birds. As they walked, they saw a big, yummy cake. Lily wanted to eat it, but Spot refused. He barked, "No, no!" Lily listened to Spot and they walked back to their beautiful cabin. They played and had fun at home. Lily and Spot were happy and safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw an impressive tall post near his house. He wanted to climb it, but he was too small. Tim felt sad and began to cry. A friendly bird heard Tim crying and flew down to him. The bird said, "Why are you crying, little boy?" Tim looked up and said, "I want to climb that big post, but I can't reach it." The bird wanted to help Tim. It picked up Tim with its strong wings and flew him to the top of the post. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" They both smiled and became good friends.

Jen and Tom are twins. They like to play in the garden. They have a white cat named Snowy. Snowy likes to chase birds and mice. One day, Jen and Tom find a jar of jam in the kitchen. They want to feed Snowy some jam. They think Snowy will like it. They take the jar and a spoon and go to the garden. "Snowy, Snowy, come here!" Jen calls. "We have something yummy for you!" Snowy hears Jen and runs to her. He sees the jar of jam and sniffs it. He does not like the smell. He shakes his head and meows. "No, Snowy, you will like it. It is sweet and red. Try some!" Tom says. He scoops some jam with the spoon and tries to put it in Snowy's mouth. Snowy does not want the jam. He scratches Tom's hand and jumps away. He runs to the fence and climbs it. He sees a bird on a tree. He forgets about the jam and wants to catch the bird. "Ow, Snowy, that hurts!" Tom cries. He drops the spoon and the jar. The jam spills on the grass. "Tom, are you okay?" Jen asks. She runs to Tom and hugs him. She sees the jam on the grass and frowns. "Oh no, we made a mess. Mom will be angry." They hear Mom coming from the house. She sees the jam on the grass and the twins. She is not happy. "Jen, Tom, what have you done? Why did you take the jam? That was for our toast. And why did you try to feed it to Snowy? He does not like jam. He likes fish and milk. You should know that. You are naughty children. Go to your room and think about what you did." Jen and Tom feel sorry. They say sorry to Mom and to Snowy. They go to their room and think about what they did. They learn their lesson. They do not feed jam to Snowy again.

Once upon a time, there was a small, independent cat. The cat lived alone in a big house. One day, the cat found an ashtray in the house. The cat did not know what the ashtray was for. The cat saw a big man walk into the house. The man wanted the ashtray. The cat did not want to give it to him. The cat said, "No, it is mine." The man was not happy. He began to demand the ashtray from the cat. The cat did not want to give it. The man got very mad. He chased the cat around the house. In the end, the man caught the cat and took the ashtray. The cat was sad and scared. The man left the house, and the cat was all alone again. The cat learned that sometimes, it is not good to be too independent.
