Category: Beginner
Title: The Delicate Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a delicate flower. It was spring, and all the flowers started to bloom. The delicate flower was very happy. She loved the warm sun and the soft rain that helped her grow. One day, a little bee came to the delicate flower. The bee said, "Hello, flower! I love spring. Can I get some food from you?" The flower smiled and said, "Yes, little bee! I have sweet food for you. It is called nectar. You can have some." The bee was very happy. He took the nectar from the delicate flower and said, "Thank you, flower! Now, I can make honey for my family." The flower felt good that she could help the bee. They became friends and played together in the garden every day. And they all lived happily in the beautiful spring garden.

Ben and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they see a big map on the wall. The map has many colors and shapes. Ben points to a shape and says, "What is this?" Jen looks at the shape and says, "That is a nation. A nation is a place where many people live. They have a name and a flag and a leader." Ben nods and says, "I see. And what is this color?" Jen says, "That is blue. Blue is a color like the sky and the water. Some nations have blue in their flag." Ben says, "I like blue. It is a clean color. It makes me happy." Jen says, "Me too. Let's find more blue nations on the map." They look at the map and see many blue nations. They say their names and flags. They have fun. But then, a big boy comes to the map. He is mean and angry. He does not like Ben and Jen. He says, "Get away from the map. It is mine. You are stupid and dirty. You do not know anything about nations." Ben and Jen are scared and sad. They say, "No, please. We are just playing. We are not stupid and dirty. We know some things about nations." The big boy does not listen. He pushes Ben and Jen away from the map. He tears the map from the wall. He throws the map on the floor. He steps on the map. He says, "Ha ha ha. Now the map is broken. Now the nations are broken. Now you have nothing to play with." Ben and Jen cry. They see the map on the floor. They see the colors and shapes. They see the blue nations. They are not clean anymore. They are dirty and torn. They are not happy anymore. They are unhappy. The end.

One day, a big cat and a tiny dog were playing in the park. They liked to run and chase each other. The cat would rush after the dog, and the dog would zigzag to get away. They laughed and had lots of fun. Then, a huge bird came down from the sky. It wanted to play too. The cat and the dog looked at the big bird and said, "You can play with us!" The bird was happy and joined their game. But, the bird didn't know how to play well. When it tried to rush and zigzag like the cat and dog, it bumped into a tree. The tree shook and lots of apples fell down. The cat, dog, and bird were surprised. They laughed and had a tasty snack. Now, they had a new game to play together.

One day, a little seal was feeling anxious. He had a big question in his mind. He wanted to ask his mom, but she was not around. So, he went to find his friend, the big fish. "Big fish," the little seal said, "I have a question. I am anxious. Where is my mom?" The big fish thought for a moment. "I don't know, little seal. But don't worry. Let's look for her together." As they swam around, they saw something they did not expect. They saw a big boat. The little seal was scared. But the big fish said, "Don't be scared. Let's get closer." When they got close, they saw the little seal's mom on the boat. She was talking to the people on the boat. They were nice people who wanted to help seals. The little seal was happy to see his mom. His question was answered, and he was not anxious anymore.
