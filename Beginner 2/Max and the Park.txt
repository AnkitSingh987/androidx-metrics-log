Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive dog named Max. Max loved to play and run in the park with his friends. One day, Max found a big bowl of sauce in the park. He was very happy. Max took the sauce and shared it with his friends. They all loved the sauce and ate it all up. After they ate the sauce, they felt very full and tired. Max and his friends found a big tree to sit under. Under the tree, Max and his friends started to sleep. They had a nice, long sleep because the sauce made them very sleepy. When they woke up, they played again and had a fun day. The end.

One day, a big train was going on a trip. It was a sunny day and the train was happy. The train wanted to gather all its friends to go with it. It went to the forest, the beach, and the city to find its friends. In the forest, the train met a bird. The train said, "Bird, come with me on my trip!" The bird said, "Yes, I will come with you!" The train was happy. But the train didn't know that the bird was frustrated. The bird could not find any food in the forest. At the beach, the train met a crab. The train said, "Crab, come with me on my trip!" The crab said, "Yes, I will come with you!" The train was happy. But the train didn't know that the crab was frustrated too. The crab could not find a home on the beach. In the city, the train met a dog. The train said, "Dog, come with me on my trip!" The dog said, "Yes, I will come with you!" The train was happy. But the train didn't know that the dog was frustrated as well. The dog could not find a ball to play with. On the trip, the train saw a big tree with lots of fruits. The bird was happy and ate the fruits. The train saw a big sand castle. The crab was happy and made it his home. The train saw a big park with many balls. The dog was happy and played with the balls. All the friends were happy, and the train was happy too. The train knew that it had helped its friends, and they all had a great trip together.

One day, a girl named Lucy was playing with her toys. She had a big folder with many pretty pictures in it. She liked to look at the pictures and show them to her friends. Lucy wanted to have a fun game with her friends. She thought of a game where they could be competitive. So, she decided to invite her friends to her house. She told them about the game and they were all excited to play. When her friends came, they started the game. They took turns picking a picture from the folder. They had to act like the picture and make others guess what it was. They all laughed and had a great time. In the end, everyone was happy and they promised to play the game again soon.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a man playing golf. Tom thought it was fun and wanted to try it too. Tom asked the man, "Can you provide me a golf stick, please?" The man said, "Sure, but you have to be careful. Golf can be hard for a cat." Tom didn't listen and took the golf stick. Tom tried to hit the ball, but he missed. He tried again and again, but he couldn't hit the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom swung the golf stick so hard that it flew out of his paws and hit a big tree. The tree started to fall down. The man yelled, "Run, Tom, run!" But Tom was too lazy to run fast. The tree fell on Tom and he got hurt. The man felt sad and said, "I told you to be careful, Tom." Tom learned that he should listen to others and not be so lazy.
