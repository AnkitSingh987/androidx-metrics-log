Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Stick

Tom and Lily were playing in the garden. They saw a big hive on a tree. It had many bees inside. "Look, bees!" Tom said. "They make honey. I want some honey." "No, Tom, don't go near the hive!" Lily said. "The bees will sting you. It will hurt a lot." But Tom did not listen. He was tough. He wanted to show Lily he was not afraid. He threw a stick at the hive. He wanted to make the bees come out. The stick hit the hive. The bees were angry. They flew out of the hive. They buzzed loudly. They chased Tom. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom cried. He ran away. He had many stings on his face and arms. He was not tough anymore. He was sorry. Lily ran after him. She helped him go inside the house. She gave him some ice. She was kind. "Tom, why did you do that?" Lily asked. "You know the bees don't like it when you bother them. They only display their honey, they don't share it." "I was silly," Tom said. "I wanted to be tough. But I was not. I was stupid. I'm sorry, Lily. And I'm sorry, bees." Lily hugged him. She said, "It's okay, Tom. You learned your lesson. Next time, be nice to the bees. And don't throw sticks at hives."

Once upon a time, a wealthy man lived on a big hill. He had a pretty view of the town below. He liked to watch the people and the animals down there. One day, he saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and cried for help. The wealthy man wanted to save the cat, so he ran down the hill. When he got to the tree, he saw a little girl. The girl said, "I will save the cat!" She climbed the tree and saved the cat. The wealthy man was surprised but happy. The cat and the little girl became friends, and they all enjoyed the pretty view together.

One day, a furry dog named Max found a big pit in his yard. Max was very happy. He wanted to fill the pit with things he liked. Max ran to find his toys to put in the pit. Max met his friend, a little bird named Tim. Max told Tim, "I found a big pit! I will fill it with my toys." Tim was excited and said, "I will help you, Max!" So, Max and Tim put all of Max's toys in the pit. Soon, Max and Tim saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. Max and Tim looked at the pit full of toys. They decided to give the girl a toy from the pit. The girl was happy and said, "Thank you, Max and Tim!" Max and Tim learned that sharing can make others happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. They all liked to play together. One day, a new animal came to the barn. It was a little duck. The little duck was excited to make new friends. In the barn, there was a big cow. The cow liked to tease the other animals. When the little duck came, the cow teased the duck too. The duck felt sad and didn't want to play with the cow. The other animals saw the duck was sad. They told the cow to stop teasing the duck. The cow said sorry to the duck. Then, they all played together and had fun. The little duck was happy and excited to have new friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very enthusiastic. Each day she wanted to act and have lots of fun. One day she found a big box of chocolates on the floor. She was so excited and started acting silly. She acted like she was hopping around a big cloud of chocolates. Suddenly, she heard a loud voice from behind her. It was her mom who had seen her acting like that. She scolded her daughter for being so naughty. She said that she should not act like that, and that the chocolate was for her brother's birthday. The little girl was so sad. She had been excited to act and to have those chocolates, but it all ended with a bad ending. She learned her lesson not to act without permission and gave her brother the box of chocolates as a present.
