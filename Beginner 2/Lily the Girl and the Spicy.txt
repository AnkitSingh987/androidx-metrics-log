Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Spicy

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play a game. She went to the kitchen and found a spicy red pepper. She thought it would be funny to hide it from her mom. So, she ran to the bathroom and covered the pepper with a small towel. After a while, Lily's mom came into the bathroom. She saw the towel on the floor and picked it up. "Oh no!" she said, as she found the spicy pepper. "Lily, did you hide this here?" Lily giggled and said, "Yes, Mommy! I wanted to surprise you!" Her mom smiled and said, "Well, you sure did! But let's not hide spicy things in the bathroom. It could be dangerous." Lily nodded and promised to be more careful. They both laughed and went back to the kitchen to cook dinner together.

One day, a big bear and a little bunny were playing in the forest. They found a great tree with a big cut. The bear poked the cut with a stick. "Look, Bunny! I can poke the cut!" said the bear. The bunny wanted to try, too. So, the bunny picked up a small stick and poked the cut. They both laughed and played with the tree. As they played, a bird flew down and said, "Hello, Bear and Bunny! Can I play with you?" The bear and the bunny said, "Yes! You can poke the cut, too!" The bird found a twig and poked the cut with her beak. All three friends had a fun day playing and poking the cut in the great tree. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went home to their families. The bear, the bunny, and the bird were very happy.

One day, a little cat with black fur was walking in the woods. She was very hungry. She saw a big tree with many oats growing on it. The black cat wanted to eat the oats but they were too high for her to reach. "Please help me get the oats," the black cat said to a tall bird. The bird did not listen. The bird wanted to eat the oats too. They both wanted the oats, but they did not want to share. The black cat had an idea. "Let's work together," she said to the bird. "You can fly and I can climb. We can both have the oats if we help each other." The bird listened and thought it was a good idea. The bird flew up and picked the oats, then dropped them down to the black cat. They both ate the oats and were happy. They learned that working together is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to whistle. Every day, he sat on a tree branch and whistled a happy song. He had a friend, a big dog, who lived on the other side of the tree. One day, the little bird saw something unusual in the sky. It was a big, colorful balloon. The bird was very excited and wanted the big dog to see it too. So, the little bird whistled very loud to call his friend. The big dog heard the whistle and came running to the tree. He looked up and saw the unusual balloon too. They both watched the balloon until it disappeared into the sky. The little bird and the big dog were happy they saw the unusual balloon together. They played and whistled all day long.

Lily and Max were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a big hose with water. Lily had an idea. "Let's make a fountain!" she said to Max. She turned on the hose and pointed it to the sky. Water came out and made a rainbow. It was beautiful. Max clapped his hands and ran to the hose. He wanted to try too. He took the hose from Lily and pointed it to her. Water came out and made her wet. Lily screamed. She did not like to be wet. She was angry at Max. "Max, that's not nice!" she said. "Give me the hose!" She tried to take the hose from Max, but he did not let go. They pulled and tugged and fought. Water came out and made them both wet. Their mom heard them scream. She came to the garden and saw them. She was not happy. "Stop that, you two!" she said. "You are making a mess!" She took the hose from them and turned it off. She looked at them. They were wet and dirty and sad. She sighed. She loved them, but they were naughty sometimes. She took them to the house and gave them towels. She dried them and hugged them. She smiled. They were adorable, even when they were wet.
