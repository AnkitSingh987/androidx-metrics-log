Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red seat he loved very much. He would sit on it every day. The seat was hard, but Tim did not mind. He liked his seat a lot. One day, Tim's mom told him they had to send the seat away. Tim was sad, but he said okay. They put the seat in a big box and sent it away. Tim missed his seat and felt sad without it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, new, soft seat came to Tim's house. It was a gift from his grandma! Tim loved the new seat even more than the old one. It was so soft and nice to sit on. Tim was happy again, and he knew his grandma loved him very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared to go on a big adventure by himself. One day, his mom gave him a map. She said, "This map will help you find your way, so you don't have to be scared." Tim looked at the map and saw a path to a big tree. He decided to go on an adventure to find the tree. As he walked, he talked to himself. "I am not scared," he said. "I have a map to help me." Finally, Tim found the big tree. He was so happy! He sat down under the tree and settled there for a while. He looked at the map again and saw a path back home. Tim knew he could do it. He was not scared anymore. He said, "Thank you, map, for helping me." With the map in his hand, Tim walked back home. When he got there, his mom was waiting for him. She was so proud of him. Tim was happy and not scared anymore, all thanks to the map and his big adventure.

Lily and Max are twins. They like to play games. Sometimes they play nice. Sometimes they play mean. They are competitive. One morning, Lily and Max wake up early. They see the sun shine through the window. They gaze at the yellow light. They feel happy. They jump out of bed and run to the living room. They see a big box on the table. It has a bow and a card. It is a present. They wonder what is inside. They run to the box and try to open it. They pull and tug and rip. They both want to see first. They are competitive. "Stop it, Max! It is mine!" Lily shouts. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Max shouts back. They fight and push and cry. They make a mess. They are mean. Mom and Dad hear the noise. They come to the living room. They see Lily and Max and the box. They are not happy. "What are you doing, Lily and Max? Why are you fighting?" Mom asks. "It is our present! We want to see what is inside!" Lily and Max say. Mom and Dad look at the card. It says: "To Lily and Max, from Grandma and Grandpa. We love you. Share and enjoy." Mom and Dad smile. They hug Lily and Max. They say: "This present is for both of you. You are twins. You are special. You should be nice to each other. You should share and enjoy." Lily and Max feel sorry. They say: "We are sorry, Mom and Dad. We love you. We love Grandma and Grandpa. We will share and enjoy." They open the box together. They see a big puzzle. It has many pieces. It has a picture of a farm. It has animals and plants and people. It looks fun. They smile and hug. They say: "Thank you, Mom and Dad. Thank you, Grandma and Grandpa. This is a great present. We will play nice. We will help each other. We will have fun." They take the puzzle to the floor. They start to put the pieces together. They gaze at the picture. They feel happy. They are nice.

One day, a nice dog named Sam was playing with his ball. He liked to run and jump to catch it. But then, the ball went into a big bush. Sam was scared to go in the bush. He did not know what to do. Sam's friend, a cat named Max, saw Sam panic. Max asked, "What is wrong, Sam?" Sam said, "My ball is in the bush, and I am scared to go in." Max had an idea. He said, "I will go in the bush and get your ball." Max went into the bush and got the ball. But when he came out, he was all wet! Sam and Max laughed. It turned out there was a small pool of water in the bush. They played with the ball and splashed in the water all day.
