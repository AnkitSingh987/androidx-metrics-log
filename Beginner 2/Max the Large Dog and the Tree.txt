Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Large Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max loved to eat. One day, Max went to play in the park. He saw a big tree with many apples. Max wanted to eat the apples. Max heard a noise near the tree. He saw a little boy named Sam. Sam said, "Hi Max! I can help you get the apples." Max was happy. Sam climbed the tree and picked the apples for Max. Sam and Max sat under the tree. They ate the apples together. Max said, "Thank you, Sam!" They both laughed and played in the park all day. Max and Sam were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Lily and her friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a pretty mist near the tree. The mist was like a soft cloud, and it made the air feel nice and cool. Lily and her friends liked to play in the mist because it made them feel happy. After playing in the mist, Lily and her friends got very thirsty. They went to the little pond and drank some water. The water was cold and yummy. They felt good after they drank the water. Then, they went back to play in the busy little town, and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a delicate tree. The tree was very pretty, but it was not strong. It had many green leaves and beautiful flowers. The tree lived in a park where kids liked to play. One day, a big storm came to the town. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell fast. The delicate tree was scared. It did not want to fall down. The tree needed help to stay up. A kind girl saw the tree and wanted to save it. She asked her friends to help her. They all held hands around the tree. They were strong together. The storm went away, and the tree was safe. The kids were happy they could save the tree. They played under it every day, and the tree grew stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her old cat, Mr. Whiskers. They were very happy together. One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came to play. Tom saw Mr. Whiskers and mentioned, "Your cat is old, but he looks happy!" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, he is old, but he is still very playful." They all played together in the garden, laughing and having fun. Mr. Whiskers chased butterflies and Tom and Lily picked pretty flowers. The sun shone brightly, and they had a wonderful day.

One day, a boy named Tim went on a tour with his mom. They went to see many fun things. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. His mom said, "Be careful, Tim!" But Tim was scared. A girl named Sue saw Tim and wanted to help. She said, "I can encourage you. We can climb the tree together!" Tim felt better with Sue's help. They climbed the tree and saw many pretty things from up high. But then, Tim saw a cheap toy on the ground. He wanted it so badly. Sue said, "No, Tim. It's not yours." Tim was sad, but he knew Sue was right. They climbed down the tree and said goodbye. Tim learned that it's better to be kind and share than to take things that aren't yours.

Once upon a time, in a small gray house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball and throw it all day long. One day, Tim's mom told him not to throw the ball inside the house, but Tim didn't listen. Later, Tim's mom made a big cup of hot coffee and put it on the table. She went to get her book to read. Tim saw the ball on the floor and thought it would be fun to throw it just one more time. He picked up the ball and threw it as hard as he could. The ball flew through the air and hit the cup of coffee. The hot coffee spilled all over the table and floor. Tim's mom was very sad and upset. She had to clean up the big mess, and Tim was not allowed to play with his ball for a whole week. Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom.
