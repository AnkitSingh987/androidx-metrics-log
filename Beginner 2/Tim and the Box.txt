Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Box

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wandered in his yard. He saw a big, powerful truck. The truck had a big box on it. The box was full of oil. Tim asked the truck driver, "Why do you have so much oil?" The driver said, "I need it to make my truck go fast." Tim wanted to help, but the driver said, "No, this is too hard for you." Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He found a little toy truck in his yard. He put some oil in it. The toy truck became powerful too! Tim showed the driver his toy truck. The driver smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Now we both have powerful trucks!" They played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a cat named Tom was feeling confused. He saw a big red bottle with a long nose. He did not know what it was. So, he went to ask his friend, the dog named Max. "Max, what is this?" Tom asked. Max looked at the bottle and said, "It's a spray bottle. It can spray water." Tom wanted to see how it worked. He pressed the top and water came out! He was very happy. He sprayed water on the grass, the flowers, and even on Max. Max was not happy. He said, "Stop, Tom! I don't like it!" Tom thought it was fun, so he kept spraying Max. Max wanted to escape. He ran away as fast as he could. Tom chased him, still spraying water. They ran and ran until they were both tired. They sat down, laughed, and became friends again.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing near the road. Max saw a big vehicle coming. He was scared and started to tremble. He didn't want to get hurt. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw Max near the road. She knew it was not safe. "Max, come here!" she yelled. Max ran to Lily and they both went to the park to play. At the park, they saw another dog. The dog was big and looked mean. Max and Lily were scared, but they didn't want to run away. They wanted to play at the park. Lily said, "We can be brave and play together." Max and Lily played near the big dog, but they were careful not to get hurt. The big dog saw them playing and decided to join them. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Terry was walking through the city and it was very hot. He started to sweat but tried not to let the heat bother him. He was on a mission and wasn't going to be stopped. Suddenly, a big dog appeared. It had a tough look on its face and its fur seemed to hide a ferocious personality. Terry frowned and slowly stepped back. "It's okay," said a kind voice from behind. Terry turned around and saw an old lady. She looked soft and friendly. "Don't be scared," she said. "He's more bark than bite." The old lady made a clicking sound with her tongue and the dog walked away. Terry smiled and thanked her. The old lady nodded and said, "Being tough doesn't mean running away from everything that scares you. Courage means knowing when to stand your ground." Terry nodded and continued his journey, feeling a little bit braver.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a weird thing on the ground. It was a pipe. Tim picked up the pipe and looked at it. It was big and round. He thought it was fun to play with. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and wanted to show her the pipe. He ran to her and said, "Look what I found!" Sue looked at the pipe and thought it was weird too. She said, "What can we do with it?" Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game!" He put the pipe on the ground and they took turns to point at it. They laughed and had fun pointing at the weird pipe. They played all day and had a great time with their new toy.
