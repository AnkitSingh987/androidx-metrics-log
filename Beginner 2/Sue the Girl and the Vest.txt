Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Vest

One day, a little girl named Sue found a purple vest in her room. She did not know where it came from. She put on the vest and went to her mom. "Mom, look at my new vest!" Sue said. Her mom looked at Sue and said, "That's a nice vest, Sue. But it needs something more. Let's add some buttons to your vest." Sue was happy and they added big, shiny buttons to the vest. The next day, Sue wore her purple vest with buttons to the park. While playing, she saw a little boy crying. "Why are you sad?" Sue asked. The boy looked at her and said, "I lost my vest. It's purple and has buttons." Sue was surprised and took off her vest. "Is this your vest?" she asked. The boy smiled and said, "Yes! Thank you!" Sue was happy to help and they became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to gaze at the pretty flowers in her garden. One day, she saw a new flower that she had never seen before. It was very colorful and beautiful. Amy thought it was the prettiest flower in the whole garden. Amy wanted to show the flower to her mom, so she picked it up. But she didn't know that the flower had poison in it. When she touched the flower, her hand started to hurt. Amy felt very sad and cried. Amy's mom heard her cry and came running to help her. She saw the flower and knew it was a foolish thing to touch. She quickly washed Amy's hand with soap and water. Amy's hand felt better soon. From that day on, Amy was careful not to touch any flowers without asking her mom first.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big blue sea. One day, while swimming, he met a friendly crab named Carl. Carl had many legs, but he could not swim like Fin. "Hi Carl," said Fin. "I love to swim. Can you swim too?" "No, Fin," replied Carl. "I can't swim, but I can walk with my legs. They are very reliable." Fin had an idea. "Why don't we play a game? I will swim and you can walk. Let's see who can reach the other side of the sea first." Carl agreed, and they started the race. Fin swam as fast as he could, and Carl walked with his reliable legs. In the end, Fin won the race, but they both had lots of fun. From that day on, Fin and Carl became best friends and loved to play together in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little dove. The dove liked to paint. Every day, the dove painted pretty pictures with lots of colors. One day, the dove met another dove. This dove was competitive. The competitive dove said, "I can paint better than you!" The little dove said, "Let's paint together and have fun!" So, the two doves painted together. They had a great time. They made beautiful pictures with lots of colors. The competitive dove learned that painting was more fun when they did it together. And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with blocks. He had many blocks in many colors. Tim also had a big, hairy dog named Max. One day, Tim wanted to wear a hat made of blocks. He put the blocks on his head, but they fell down. Tim was sad. Max saw Tim and wanted to help. Max took the blocks in his mouth and put them on Tim's head. But the blocks still fell down. Tim and Max tried again and again. Then, Max had an idea. He took a soft, red blanket and put it on Tim's head. Now the blocks stayed on Tim's head! Tim was happy and thanked his hairy friend, Max. They played with the blocks together all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim wakes up early. He wants to play a game with his friends. He puts on his shoes and goes outside. The sun is bright and the birds sing. Tim is happy and ready to play. Tim starts a game with his friends. They run and laugh. But then, a foolish dog comes and takes their ball. The dog runs away with the ball in its mouth. Tim and his friends feel sad. They want their ball back. Then, something unexpected happens. A big bird flies down from the sky. The bird takes the ball from the dog and gives it back to Tim and his friends. They all say "thank you" to the bird. Now, they can play their game again. They are happy and have a fun day.
