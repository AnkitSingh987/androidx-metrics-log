Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Garden

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big spider in a web. The spider is black and has eight legs. "Look, a spider!" Tim says. He wants to touch it. "Be careful, Tim. The spider might bite you," Sam says. He is afraid of spiders. "No, the spider is nice. He is intelligent. He makes a web to catch flies. He is my friend," Tim says. He puts his hand near the spider. The spider moves and makes a sound. He does not like Tim's hand. He wants to be alone. "Tim, stop! The spider is angry. He is not your friend. He is a wild animal. He does not want to play with you," Sam says. He pulls Tim away from the spider. "But I want to keep the spider. He is so cool. Can we keep him, please?" Tim asks. He looks at Sam with big eyes. "No, Tim. We cannot keep the spider. He belongs to the garden. He needs his web and his flies. He will not be happy in a jar or a box. He will be sad and sick. We have to let him go," Sam says. He is wise and kind. "Okay, Sam. You are right. We have to let him go. Bye, spider. Have a good day. Thank you for showing us your web," Tim says. He waves to the spider. The spider waves back with one of his legs. He is glad that Tim and Sam leave him alone. He is intelligent, too. He knows that humans are not his friends. He goes back to his web and waits for his next meal.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big, open field full of pretty flowers and tall trees. In the field, she found a small mushroom. It was red with white dots, just like in her storybook. Mia heard a tiny voice say, "Hello, who are you?" She looked around and saw a small bug sitting on the mushroom. "I am Mia," she said. "Who are you?" The bug said, "I am Bob the bug. I live on this mushroom. It is my home." Mia and Bob talked and played in the open field. Suddenly, Mia felt a tickle in her nose. "Achoo!" she sneezed. The sneeze was so big that it made the mushroom shake. Bob held on tight so he wouldn't fall off. "Wow, that was a big sneeze!" said Bob. Mia said, "I am sorry, I didn't mean to shake your home." Bob smiled and said, "It's okay. I am glad we are friends." From that day on, Mia and Bob the bug played together in the open field, and they were always careful not to sneeze near the mushroom.

One day, a soft bunny named Lily was playing in the forest. She found a long rod on the ground. Lily picked it up and started to play with it. She waved the rod around and said, "I wish my toy would vanish!" Suddenly, her toy did vanish! Lily was very sad and didn't know what to do. She looked around and saw a big tree. A wise owl lived in the tree. Lily went to the owl and said, "Mr. Owl, my toy vanished when I waved this rod. Can you help me find it?" The wise owl looked at the rod and said, "Lily, this is a magic rod. You must be careful with it. To get your toy back, you must wave the rod again and say, 'I wish my toy would come back.'" Lily did as the owl said. She waved the rod and said, "I wish my toy would come back." Just like that, her toy appeared! Lily was very happy and thanked the wise owl. She learned to be careful with the magic rod and never made anything vanish again.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Tim. He was three years old. Tim was very creative and he loved to design things. One day he decided to design a cloud. He got some paper, crayons and glue and started drawing. In no time, he had created a beautiful cloud. However, the cloud was too heavy and it started to fall down. It was so stupid! Tim laughed and kissed his creation on the head before it fell down. He was very proud of what he had designed. The end.
