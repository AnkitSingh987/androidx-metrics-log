Category: Beginner
Title: Al the Alligator and the Pond

One day, a big alligator named Al moved to a new pond. He was new and did not know any of the other animals. Al was big and green, and he had big teeth. He wanted to make friends with the other animals in the pond. Al saw a small fish named Fin. Al said, "Hi Fin, I want to play with you." Fin was scared of Al's big teeth and swam away fast. Al felt sad because he wanted to make friends. The next day, Al saw a big frog named Fred. Al demanded, "Fred, play with me!" Fred was also scared of Al's big teeth, but he had an idea. Fred said, "Al, if you put on this big hat, we can play together." Al put on the big hat, and it covered his big teeth. Now, all the animals in the pond were not scared of Al, and they all played together. Al was happy to have new friends.

One day, a boy named Tom went outside to play. He saw a big puddle in the yard. He wanted to make a bigger puddle. So, he took his toy bucket and filled it with water. He poured the water into the puddle. The puddle grew bigger and bigger. Tom's friend, Sam, came over. He saw the big puddle and wanted to jump in it. Tom said, "Be honest, do you want to make the puddle bigger too?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to help!" They both took their toy buckets and filled them with water. They poured the water into the puddle, making it even bigger. But then, Tom's mom came outside. She saw the big puddle and said, "Boys, you made a mess! Now you must clean it up!" Tom and Sam were sad, but they knew they had to fix it. They took their toy buckets and scooped the water out of the puddle. They worked together until the puddle was gone. Tom's mom was happy, and the boys learned to be more careful when playing with water.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a big cube that he liked to stack and knock down. Lily had a small lock that she liked to open and close. She found the lock in her dad's drawer. She did not know that it was expensive and important. "Look, Tom, I have a lock!" Lily said, holding the lock in her hand. "It can open and close. See?" Tom looked at the lock and wanted to play with it. He reached for it, but Lily pulled it away. "No, Tom, this is my lock!" Lily said. "You have your cube. Go play with your cube!" Tom felt angry and sad. He wanted to see the lock. He thought it was more fun than his cube. He grabbed his cube and threw it at Lily. The cube hit the lock and made a loud noise. "Ow!" Lily cried. "Tom, you are bad! You broke my lock!" Lily looked at the lock and saw that it was bent and cracked. She could not open or close it anymore. She started to cry louder. Dad heard the noise and came to the living room. He saw Lily crying and Tom looking scared. He saw the lock and the cube on the floor. "What happened here?" Dad asked. "Lily, why are you crying? Tom, why did you throw your cube?" Lily pointed at the lock and said, "Tom broke my lock! He threw his cube at me! It was my lock!" Dad picked up the lock and looked at it. He recognized it as his expensive and important lock. He used it to keep his papers safe. He felt angry and worried. "Where did you get this lock, Lily?" Dad asked. "This is not your lock. This is my lock. It is expensive and important. You should not have taken it from my drawer." Lily stopped crying and looked at Dad. She did not know that the lock was his. She did not know that it was expensive and important. She felt sorry and scared. "I'm sorry, Dad," Lily said. "I did not know. I just wanted to play with it. It was shiny and pretty." Dad sighed and hugged Lily. He was not happy, but he knew that Lily did not mean to cause trouble. He also hugged Tom and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I know you were curious about the lock. But you should not have thrown your cube at Lily. That was not nice. You could have hurt her or yourself." Tom nodded and said, "I'm sorry, Dad. I'm sorry, Lily. I was angry and sad. I wanted to see the lock. I did not mean to break it." Dad smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. I understand. But next time, you should ask before you touch something that is not yours. And you should use your words, not your cube, to express your feelings." Tom and Lily said, "Okay, Dad. We will." Dad said, "Good. Now, let's clean up this mess and find some other toys to play with. How about we play with some puzzles or books?" Tom and Lily said, "Yes, Dad. We like puzzles and books." Dad said, "Good. Let's go then." They all went to the playroom and played with some puzzles and books. They forgot about the lock and the cube. They had fun and learned new things. They were happy and sorry. They loved each other. The end.
