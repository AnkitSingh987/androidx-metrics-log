Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Train

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. They have many cars and trucks, but they want more. They see a big box in the garage. The box has a picture of a train. They want to play with the train. They open the box and take out the train. The train has many parts. There are wheels, tracks, and a cable. The cable connects the train to a controller. The controller makes the train go fast or slow, and makes sounds. Tom and Sam are excited. They want to complete the train and play with it. They put the wheels on the train. They snap the tracks together. They plug the cable into the controller. They are ready to play. They turn on the controller and press the buttons. The train starts to move on the tracks. It makes a whistle sound. Tom and Sam are happy. They have a new toy. "Look, Sam, the train is moving!" Tom says. "Yes, Tom, it is very cool!" Sam says. They take turns to control the train. They make it go faster and slower, and make different sounds. They have fun with the train. They think it is the best toy ever. They do not know that the train is very old. It is the original train that Tom's grandpa had when he was a boy. Tom's grandpa gave it to Tom's dad, and Tom's dad gave it to Tom. The train is very special, but Tom and Sam do not care. They just like to play with it.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dog. This dog liked to run and jump all day long. He seemed very happy in the big park. One day, the wild dog saw a big hole in the fence. He thought, "I can escape and have more fun." The wild dog went through the hole and ran away. He found a big road with many cars. The wild dog was scared, but he wanted to have fun. He started to run and jump in the road. He did not know that it was not safe. Suddenly, a big car came very fast. The wild dog did not see it. The car hit the wild dog, and he fell down. He could not run and jump anymore. The wild dog should have stayed in the park. The escape was not fun at all.

Once upon a time, there was a wise driver. He drove a big car that could zoom very fast. The driver loved to zoom down the road and feel the wind on his face. One day, he saw a small cat near the road. The cat looked scared. The driver stopped his car and said to the cat, "Don't worry, little cat. I will help you." The cat looked at the driver and said, "Thank you, wise driver. I am lost and want to go home." The driver smiled and said, "Hop in my car, and I will take you home." As they drove, the driver saw a big hill. He wanted to zoom down the hill very fast. The cat looked scared and said, "Please, wise driver, don't zoom down the hill. It is not safe." But the driver did not listen. He zoomed down the hill very fast. At the bottom of the hill, there was a big tree. The driver could not stop in time, and the car hit the tree. The car was broken, and the wise driver was sad. He should have listened to the little cat.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing in the kitchen. They saw a dishwasher and wanted to count the dishes inside. Tim said, "I want to count the dishes first!" Sue said, "No, I want to count them first!" They could not agree on who should count the dishes first. Tim and Sue's mom heard them and said, "Let's make it fair. You can both count the dishes together." So, Tim and Sue started to count the dishes. "One, two, three," they counted. But when they reached ten, something unexpected happened. The dishwasher door opened, and out jumped a funny-looking frog! The frog said, "Thank you for counting the dishes. Now I can go back to my pond!" Tim and Sue were very surprised but happy to help the frog. They laughed and played with the frog before it went back to its pond. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always counted the dishes together.
