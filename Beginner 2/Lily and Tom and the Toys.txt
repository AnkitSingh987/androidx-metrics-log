Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Toys

Lily and Tom are playing in the garden. They have a big box of toys. They like to dig holes and bury their toys. Then they try to find them again. "Look, I buried my car here," Tom says. He points to a small mound of dirt. "Can you find it?" Lily nods. She takes a shovel and starts to dig. She likes this game. It is funny. She digs and digs until she sees something shiny. "I found it!" she says. She holds up the car. It is red and shiny. "Good job, Lily!" Tom says. He claps his hands. "Now it is my turn. Can you bury something for me?" Lily thinks. She looks at the box of toys. She sees a long, thin toy. It is a snake. It is green and has black spots. Lily smiles. She takes the snake and runs to another spot. She digs a hole and puts the snake in it. She covers it with dirt. She makes a line with her finger on the dirt. "Here, Tom," she says. "Try to find this." Tom runs to the spot. He sees the line. He thinks it is a clue. He follows the line with his shovel. He digs and digs until he feels something soft. He pulls it out. It is the snake. It wiggles in his hand. Tom screams. He drops the snake. He runs away. Lily laughs. She picks up the snake. She runs after Tom. "It is just a toy, Tom," she says. "It is not real. It is funny." Tom stops. He looks at the snake. He sees that it is a toy. He feels silly. He laughs too. "You tricked me, Lily," he says. "That was a good joke. You are funny." Lily and Tom hug. They are friends. They go back to the box of toys. They play some more. They have fun. They bury and find many things. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. Emma was three years old and she loved to try new things. One day, Emma decided to go for a walk and explore the woods near her house. As Emma walked, she saw a large barrel. It was a dull, brown colour and looked like it had been there a while. Excited, Emma ran over to the barrel and said to herself, “I’m going to try to open it!” She tried lifting the lid, but it was too heavy. Suddenly, she heard a strange noise coming from inside the barrel. Then, a little mouse popped his head out. “Oh!” Emma said, “what are you doing in there?” “I’m stuck!” the mouse replied. “Can you help me out?” So Emma worked together with the mouse and finally managed to lift the lid. The mouse thanked Emma and scurried off, leaving Emma with a big smile on her face. The end.

One sunny day, a funny cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big hoop on the ground. Tom wanted to play with the hoop, so he pushed it with his paw. The hoop rolled and rolled, and Tom ran after it. He was having so much fun. Tom saw a little girl with a long string. She was trying to tie the string to a tree. Tom wanted to help the girl, so he used his paw to hold the string. The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Together, they tied the string to the tree. Now, the hoop was hanging from the tree by the string. Tom and the girl took turns jumping through the hoop. They laughed and played all day. It was a fun day at the park for Tom and his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a new blouse. It was not pretty. It was ugly. Sally did not like her ugly blouse. One day, Sally's mom said, "Put on your blouse, Sally. We are going to visit Grandma." Sally put on her ugly blouse. She was sad. But she listened to her mom. At Grandma's house, Sally saw her cousin, Tim. Tim said, "I like your blouse, Sally!" Sally smiled. She learned that being kind is more important than looking pretty. And that made her happy.
