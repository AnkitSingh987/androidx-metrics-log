Category: Beginner
Title: Sam and Ben and the Blanket

Sam and Ben are brothers. They like to play in the living room. They have many toys, but they also like to use the chair. The chair is big and brown. It is tough and strong. One day, Sam and Ben have an idea. They want to make a fort with the chair. They get a blanket and some pillows. They put the blanket over the chair. They put the pillows under the chair. They crawl inside the fort. They feel cozy and safe. "Look, Ben, we have a fort!" Sam says. "We are the kings of the fort!" "Yes, Sam, we are the kings!" Ben says. "No one can get us here!" They giggle and whisper. They pretend they have swords and shields. They have fun in the fort. But then, they hear a noise. It is Mom. She comes into the living room. She sees the chair and the blanket and the pillows. She is not happy. "Sam, Ben, what are you doing?" Mom says. "You made a mess with the chair and the blanket and the pillows!" "Sorry, Mom, we were playing," Sam says. "We made a fort with the chair. The chair is tough and strong." Mom sighs. She loves Sam and Ben, but she does not like the mess. She tells them to clean up. She helps them fold the blanket and fluff the pillows. She puts them back where they belong. She tells them to be careful with the chair. The chair is tough and strong, but it is also old and precious. Sam and Ben say sorry to Mom. They hug her and kiss her. They promise to be good. They still like the chair, but they also love Mom. They know she is tougher and stronger than the chair.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful frog named Fred. Fred loved to ride on the back of his big fish friend, Sam. They lived in a pond with lots of water. Fred and Sam liked to play and swim all day long. One day, the water in the pond started to go away. Fred and Sam were sad because they could not ride and play together. They saw their other friends, the ducks, fly away to find a new pond. Fred and Sam wanted to find a new pond too, but they did not know how. Then, they saw a big bird named Betty. Betty was good at finding water. Fred asked Betty to help them find a new pond. Betty agreed and flew up high in the sky to look for water. After some time, Betty came back and told Fred and Sam about a big pond not far away. Fred and Sam were very happy. They thanked Betty and went to the new pond together. When they got there, they saw their duck friends. The water was back, and they could ride and play together again. Fred, Sam, and all their friends lived happily ever after in their new pond.

Sara and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with dolls and dress up. They also liked to pretend they were grown-ups. Sometimes, they would use their toy cups and spoons to make coffee. They would fill the cups with water and add some sugar and milk. Then they would stir and sip the coffee, making funny faces. One day, they invited Anna to play with them. Anna was new in their class. She was shy and quiet. She did not have many friends. Sara and Lily wanted to be nice to her. They asked her if she wanted some coffee. Anna nodded. She had never tried coffee before. She was curious. Sara and Lily gave her a cup of coffee. They watched her as she took a sip. Anna made a sour face. She did not like the coffee. It was bitter and watery. She wanted to spit it out. But she did not want to be rude. She tried to smile. Sara and Lily saw her face. They thought it was funny. They started to laugh. They teased her. They said, "You don't like coffee? You are a baby! Coffee is for grown-ups. You are not a grown-up. You are a silly girl!" Anna felt hurt. She felt tears in her eyes. She did not like to be teased. She did not want to play with them anymore. She put down the cup and ran away. She wished she had a real friend. Sara and Lily stopped laughing. They realized they had been mean. They felt sorry. They ran after Anna. They said, "Anna, wait! We are sorry. We did not mean to tease you. We were just joking. We don't really like coffee either. It is yucky. We only pretend to like it. We want to be your friend. Please come back and play with us." Anna stopped. She looked at them. She saw they were sincere. She felt a little better. She said, "Really? You don't like coffee either? Why do you pretend to like it?" Sara and Lily said, "We don't know. We think it is cool. We think it makes us popular. But we don't need to pretend. We can be ourselves. We can play with whatever we like. We like dolls and dress up. And we like you. You are nice and smart. You can be our friend. Do you want to be our friend?" Anna smiled. She said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. Thank you for saying sorry. I forgive you. Can we play together?" Sara and Lily said, "Yes, of course. Come on, let's go to the dollhouse. We have a lot of fun things to do. And no more coffee. How about some juice?" Anna said, "Yes, juice sounds good. I like juice. And I like you. You are my friends." They hugged and went to the dollhouse. They played happily. They learned to be kind and honest. They became best friends.
