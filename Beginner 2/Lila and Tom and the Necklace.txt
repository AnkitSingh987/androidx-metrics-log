Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Tom and the Necklace

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big sack on the grass. It was brown and dirty and had a hole in it. "What is in the sack?" Lila asked. "I don't know. Let's look," Tom said. They ran to the sack and opened it. Inside, they saw many things. There were books and toys and clothes and shoes. Some things were new and some things were old. Some things were shiny and some things were dull. "Wow, look at all these things!" Lila said. "Maybe someone left them here. Maybe they are gifts for us," Tom said. They started to take out the things and look at them. They found a ball, a doll, a hat, a car, a book, and a necklace. They were happy and excited. But then, they heard a loud voice. "Hey, what are you doing with my sack?" the voice said. They looked up and saw a man. He was big and angry and had a beard. He was wearing a coat and a hat and had a stick in his hand. He was unknown to them. He was not their friend. They were scared and dropped the things. They tried to run away, but Tom stumbled on the sack and fell down. The man came closer and raised his stick. "Give me back my sack! It's mine!" the man said. Lila ran to Tom and helped him get up. She saw the necklace on the grass. It was shiny and pretty. She liked it. She quickly picked it up and put it in her pocket. Then, she and Tom ran as fast as they could. They ran to the slide and slid down. They ran to the gate and went out. They ran to their mom and dad, who were waiting for them. They hugged them and told them what happened. They showed them the necklace. Their mom and dad were surprised and worried. "Where did you get this necklace?" their mom asked. "It was in the sack. We found it in the park. Can we keep it?" Lila asked. Their dad looked at the necklace. He saw a tag on it. It had a name and a number on it. "No, we can't keep it. It belongs to someone else. It's not ours. We have to give it back," their dad said. He took out his phone and called the number. He talked to someone. He said they found the necklace and wanted to return it. He said they were sorry for taking it. The person on the phone was happy and grateful. He said he was looking for the necklace. He said it was very important to him. He said it was a gift from his grandma, who had died. He said he would come to their house and get it. Lila and Tom felt sorry for the man. They realized they had done something wrong. They wanted to say sorry to him too. They waited for the man to come. They hoped he was not the same man who had the sack and the stick. They hoped he was nice and kind. They hoped he would forgive them.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. Lily was very troubled and wanted to find somewhere to be safe. Everywhere she looked, the world seemed so big and she was so small. So she decided to rush away and look for a safe place to hide. Lily ran and ran, but she couldn't find anywhere to take shelter. Suddenly she heard a quiet sound coming from a nearby cell. She rushed over to take a closer look, and was surprised to find a tiny creature with wings living inside. The creature was friendly and welcomed her in. Lily was so happy to have found somewhere safe and warm where she felt protected. From then on, Lily came back to visit the cell every day. She made a home for herself inside where she was never troubled again.

One day, Tom went to play at the park. He saw a new boy there. Tom said, "Hi, I am Tom. What is your name?" The new boy said, "I am Sam." Tom wanted to introduce Sam to his friends, so they went to find them. Tom and Sam saw Tom's friends playing with a trophy. The trophy was shiny and big. They had won it in a game. Tom said, "Sam, meet my friends. They are nice." Sam said, "Hi, friends. I like your trophy." Tom's friends were very organized. They played games and took turns. Sam liked how they played. They all had fun together at the park. Now, Sam had new friends and they played every day.
