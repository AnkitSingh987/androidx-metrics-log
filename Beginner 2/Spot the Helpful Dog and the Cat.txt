Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Helpful Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his friends. One day, he found a stamp on the ground. The stamp did not belong to him, so he wanted to find the owner. Spot went to his friend, Cat. "Does this stamp belong to you?" he asked. Cat shook her head. "No, it does not belong to me," she said. Spot then went to his friend, Bird. "Does this stamp belong to you?" he asked. Bird also shook her head. "No, it does not belong to me," she said. Spot was sad because he could not find the owner of the stamp. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came running up to Spot. "That stamp belongs to me!" she said with a big smile. Spot was so happy to find the owner. He gave the stamp back to the little girl. The little girl thanked Spot for being so helpful. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big pond with water and mud. They want to go near the pond. But Mom says, "No, no, no. The pond is not for you. There is a hippo in the pond. A hippo is a big animal with a big mouth and big teeth. A hippo can be angry and hurt you. Stay away from the pond." Tim and Sam are sad. They want to see the hippo. They wait for Mom to look away. Then they run to the pond. They are very quiet. They do not want Mom to hear them. They see the hippo in the pond. The hippo is sleepy. He is lying in the water and mud. He is not moving. He is not angry. He is snoring. Tim and Sam are happy. They think the hippo is funny. They want to touch the hippo. They go closer to the pond. They reach out their hands. But the hippo is not sleepy. He is only pretending. He hears Tim and Sam. He opens his big mouth and big teeth. He springs out of the water and mud. He roars at Tim and Sam. Tim and Sam are scared. They scream and run away. They run back to Mom. They are sorry. They do not want to go near the pond again. Mom hugs Tim and Sam. She is angry and worried. She says, "I told you to stay away from the pond. The hippo is not a toy. He is a wild animal. He can be dangerous. You have to listen to me. You have to be careful." Tim and Sam nod. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We will listen to you. We will be careful. We do not like the hippo. He is not funny. He is scary." Mom smiles. She says, "I love you, Tim and Sam. I am glad you are safe. Come on, let's go home. It is time for a nap. You are sleepy." Tim and Sam agree. They are sleepy. They hold Mom's hand. They walk home. They do not look at the pond. They do not look at the hippo. They close their eyes. They dream of nice things.

One day, a little boy named Tim found some dull blocks in his toy box. They were not shiny or bright, but he liked them. He decided to build a tall tower with the blocks. As he stacked them higher and higher, he became very proud of his work. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. When she saw the tall tower of dull blocks, she asked, "What are you making, Tim?" Tim was happy to answer, "I am making a big tower with my blocks!" Sue liked the idea and wanted to help Tim build the tower even taller. Together, Tim and Sue built the tallest tower they could with the dull blocks. They laughed and had fun as they played. When it was time for Sue to go home, they said goodbye and promised to build an even bigger tower next time. Tim was happy that he had found the dull blocks and made a new friend to play with.
