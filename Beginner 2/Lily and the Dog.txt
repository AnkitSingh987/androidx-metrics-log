Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a scary big dog. Lily was scared, but her mom said, "Just ignore the dog, and it will go away." Lily tried to ignore the dog, but she was still scared. Lily and her mom played with a ball. The scary dog came near them. "Go away!" said Lily's mom. But the dog did not listen. It took the ball in its mouth and ran away. Lily was sad and cried. "Let's go home," said Lily's mom. They walked away from the park. The scary dog followed them. Lily's mom tried to make the dog go away, but it did not listen. The dog bit Lily's mom on the body. Lily's mom cried out in pain, and they had to go to the doctor. The day at the park was not fun at all.

One Sunday, a little girl named Lucy was upset. She did not want to go to the park with her mom and dad. They told her to put on her shoes and stand by the door, but she did not want to. Lucy's mom and dad tried to make her feel better. They said, "Lucy, the park is fun! You can play on the swings and slide down the big slide." But Lucy still felt upset. Finally, Lucy's dad had an idea. He said, "Let's take your teddy bear to the park too!" Lucy liked this idea. She stood up, wiped her tears, and went to the park with her teddy bear.

Once upon a time, there was a silly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play and have fun. One day, he went to a theater with his mom and dad. The theater was big and had many seats. Before the show started, Tim wanted to play a game. He asked his mom and dad to kneel down on the floor with him. They played a fun game of "Duck, Duck, Goose" while they waited for the show to start. When the show began, Tim and his family sat in their seats and watched the silly people on stage. They laughed and clapped their hands. Tim had a great time at the theater with his mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and stuff. One day, his mom told him to clean his room. She mentioned that if he didn't clean his room, he would not get a treat. But Tim did not want to clean his room. He thought it was boring to pick up his toys and make his bed. Tim's room was very dirty. There were toys everywhere, and his clothes were on the floor. He played with his toys all day and did not clean his room. His mom came back to check on him, but Tim still did not clean his room. His mom was sad and reminded him that he would not get a treat if his room was dirty. At the end of the day, Tim's mom came back again. She saw that the room was still dirty. She did not give Tim a treat. Tim was sad and cried. He wished he had cleaned his room when his mom mentioned it. Now, he had no treat and a dirty room.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an independent girl who liked to play and explore by herself. One day, while playing in the forest, she heard a voice. The voice was coming from a small bird who was stuck in a tree. Lily asked the bird, "Do you need help?" The bird said, "Yes, please. I believe I can fly, but I am scared." Lily was very kind and told the bird, "Don't be scared. I know you can do it. Just believe in yourself." The bird listened to Lily's voice and tried to fly. With a big flap of its wings, the bird was free from the tree. The bird said, "Thank you, Lily! I can fly now because of you." Lily was happy that she could help the bird. They became friends and played together in the forest every day.
