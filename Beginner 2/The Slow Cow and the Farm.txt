Category: Beginner
Title: The Slow Cow and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a slow cow. The cow lived on a farm with other cows. They all liked to eat grass and play in the sun. The slow cow was very happy with her friends. One day, the slow cow saw something new. It was a big truck with a picture of beef on it. The slow cow did not recognize the picture. She thought it was a friend. So, the slow cow went to say hello to the truck. But, the truck was not a friend. It was there to take the cows away. The slow cow was scared, but she had an idea. She ran fast, faster than she ever ran before. The slow cow was not slow anymore! She led her friends away from the truck and they all hid. The truck left, and the cows were safe. They were very happy and thanked the cow who was not slow anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a very obedient dog named Max. Max loved to play with colors. He had a big box full of colors. One day, Max decided to paint a picture with all his colors. As Max painted, he saw a big flower. The flower was so pretty, he wanted to smell it. But when Max smelled the flower, he started to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo!" Max sneezed so loud that all his friends came to see what happened. Max's friends saw the pretty picture he painted with all the colors. They all laughed and played together. Max learned that even when you sneeze, you can still have fun with your friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. It was a cool day, and the sun was shining. Tim saw a small crab walking on the sand. He thought the crab was very cool and wanted to play with it. Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Crab! Do you want to play with me?" The crab looked at Tim and moved its little claws. Tim thought the crab was saying yes. So, Tim got a stick and started to write his name in the sand. He said, "Look, Mr. Crab, I can write my name!" The crab watched Tim write his name and then started to move its claws. Tim saw that the crab was writing its name too! Tim was so happy to see that the crab could write. He knew they would be best friends. Tim and the crab played all day, writing words in the sand and having fun. As the sun went down, Tim said goodbye to his new friend. He knew he would always remember the cool crab that could write.

Tom and Mia like to play with their toys in the living room. They have a big screen on the wall that shows pictures and sounds. Sometimes they watch the screen with Mom and Dad, but they are not allowed to touch it. One day, Tom and Mia are playing with their cars and dolls, when they hear a loud noise from the screen. They look up and see a scary monster with big teeth and claws. The monster roars and tries to come out of the screen. Tom and Mia are very afraid and run to the door. But the door is locked. Mom and Dad are not home. They forgot to leave the key for Tom and Mia. Tom and Mia cry and hug each other. They do not know what to do. Then they hear a voice from the screen. It is not the monster. It is a friendly man. He says, "Don't be scared, kids. The monster is not real. It is just a movie. I am the man who made the movie. I can make the monster go away. Watch this." The man presses a button on his remote. The screen changes. The monster is gone. Instead, there are flowers and birds and rainbows. The man says, "See? The monster was harmless. It was just a picture. I am sorry I scared you. I was testing my movie. I did not know you were home. Can you forgive me?" Tom and Mia stop crying. They look at the screen. They see the man smiling. He looks nice. They nod and say, "It's okay. We forgive you. But please don't do that again. You made us very scared." The man says, "I promise I won't do that again. I will lock the screen so no one can see my movie until it is finished. And I will tell your Mom and Dad to leave the key for you next time. You are very brave kids. I hope you enjoy the rest of your day." Tom and Mia smile and say, "Thank you, Mr. Man. You are very kind. We hope you make a good movie. Bye-bye." The man says, "Bye-bye, kids. Have fun." He waves and turns off the screen. Tom and Mia hug each other. They are happy. They go back to playing with their toys. They forget about the monster. They are not afraid anymore.
