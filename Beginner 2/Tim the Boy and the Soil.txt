Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Soil

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the soil in his backyard. One day, he found a sharp rock in the soil. He was very excited and wanted to show his mom. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, look at this sharp rock I found in the soil!" His mom looked serious and said, "Tim, be careful with that sharp rock. It can hurt you." Tim listened to his mom and put the sharp rock back in the soil. He learned that sometimes it's better to be safe than sorry. From that day on, Tim always played carefully in the soil and had lots of fun.

Anna and Ben were playing outside with their ball. They liked to kick it and catch it and roll it on the grass. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. They were having fun. But then they heard a loud noise. Boom! It was thunder. They looked up and saw dark clouds. They felt raindrops on their faces. They were scared. "Let's go home!" Ben said. He ran to the house. Anna followed him, but she did not want to leave the ball. She loved the ball. She tried to carry it with her, but it was too big and heavy. She dropped it on the way. They reached the door and knocked. Their mom opened it and hugged them. She saw the ball on the ground. She smiled and said, "Don't worry, I'll get your ball. Come inside, it's cold and wet outside." She gave them towels and hot chocolate. They sat on the couch and watched the rain and the thunder. They felt warm and safe. They thanked their mom for getting their ball. They learned that sometimes they had to leave things behind when they were in danger. They also learned that their mom would always take care of them. They were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a brilliant thing in his room. It was a toy car. The toy car was red and shiny. Tim loved playing with it. He would push it and watch it zoom across the floor. It made him so happy. Tim took the brilliant thing outside to play. He met his friend, Sue, who had a blue toy car. They put the cars on the ground and pushed them. The cars went zoom, zoom, zoom! Tim and Sue laughed and clapped their hands. They raced the cars all day long. As the sun went down, Tim and Sue said goodbye. They put their toy cars away and went home. Tim hugged the brilliant thing and smiled. He knew he would have more fun with it tomorrow. And as he went to bed, he dreamed of the cars going zoom, zoom, zoom.

One sunny day, a little dog named Spot went outside to play. He loved to run and jump in the grass. Spot saw a funny-looking bug and wanted to show it to his friend, Kitty. He called out, "Kitty, come see this funny bug!" Kitty came running over and saw the bug. She thought it was very funny too. They decided to search for more funny bugs to show their friends. They looked under rocks, in the grass, and on the trees. They found more funny bugs and laughed together. But as they searched, they got lost. They were in trouble! Spot and Kitty didn't know how to get back home. They were scared, but they decided to work together. They walked and walked until they saw their house. They were so happy to be home again. From that day on, Spot and Kitty always stayed close to home when they searched for funny bugs.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had many different toys. Tim liked to play with his toys every day. One day, Tim wanted to buy a new toy, but he didn't have enough money. Tim's friend, Sam, saw that Tim was sad. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told Sam that he wanted a new toy but didn't have enough money. Sam wanted to help Tim, so he said, "I can lend you some money, Tim." Tim was very happy that Sam would lend him money. They went to the toy store and Tim bought a new toy. Tim and Sam played with the new toy all day long. They had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends.
