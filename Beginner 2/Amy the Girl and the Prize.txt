Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Prize

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play games with her friends. One day, they played a game with a prize for the winner. The prize was a big, shiny ball. Amy wanted to win the prize very much. Amy played the game with her friends. The game was tough, but Amy did not give up. She tried her best to win the prize. Her friend Tom said, "Amy, you are doing great! Keep going!" Amy smiled and kept playing. At the end of the game, Amy did not lose. She won the prize! She was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom, for cheering for me!" Amy and her friends played with the big, shiny ball together. They all had fun and were happy.

Once upon a time, there lived a joyful girl called Amy. One day, she heard a trumpet playing a clear sound. She loved it so much that she started to dance to the music. She twirled and spun around with a big smile on her face. Suddenly, her happy smile changed to one of tears. She started to weep softly. Her mom came over and asked, "What is wrong, Amy?". Amy looked up with tears in her eyes and said, "I miss the trumpet". Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, I hear the trumpet too. It's playing a clear sound just for you. Now smile and dance again!" Amy smiled and danced to the trumpet's clear sound. She was filled with joy and laughter once again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a troubled boy because he would always forget things. One day, Tim's mom asked him to help her with a task. She said, "Tim, please put these toys in a stack." Tim nodded and started to do what his mom asked. As Tim was stacking the toys, his friend, Sally, came over to play. Sally saw the stack of toys and said, "Wow, Tim! That's a big stack!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I did it for my mom." They played with the toys and had lots of fun. Later, Tim's mom came back and saw the stack of toys. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! You didn't forget this time!" Tim felt happy and proud. From that day on, Tim tried hard not to forget things anymore. And when he did, he just needed to remember the big stack of toys to help him remember.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play or take hugs from anyone. He would sit in the corner and watch the other animals play outside. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the house. She saw Tom and wanted to be his friend. Sue tried to play with Tom, but he was still grumpy. She would take a ball and roll it to him, but he would not play. Sue wanted to give Tom a hug, but he would run away. Then, Sue had an idea. She went to the store and got a soft, warm blanket. When Sue came back, she put the blanket on Tom. He felt warm and cozy. Tom started to feel less grumpy. He walked over to Sue and let her give him a hug. From that day on, Tom was not grumpy anymore. He would play and take hugs from Sue all the time. And they became the best of friends.

One sunny day, a little boy named Tim found a big raft by the river. He was very happy. He wanted to play on the raft. Tim asked his mom, "Can I play on the raft, please?" His mom replied, "Yes, but be careful." Tim jumped on the raft and started to float down the river. He saw a duck. The duck said, "Quack, quack." Tim replied, "Hi, duck! I am on a raft." The duck swam next to the raft. They were both happy. As the sun started to set, Tim and the duck went back to the shore. Tim's mom was waiting for them. She said, "I hope you had fun on the raft with your new friend." Tim and the duck both replied, "Yes, we did!" They all went home, feeling very happy.
