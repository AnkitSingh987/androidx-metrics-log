Category: Beginner
Title: Harry the Hippo and the Frog

One day, a hippo named Harry was walking slow by the river. He saw a big frog on a rock. The frog was sad because his ball had disappeared in the water. "Can you help me find my ball?" the frog asked. Harry the hippo said, "I will try my best." So, Harry went into the water to look for the ball. He looked high, he looked low, but he could not find the ball. The frog started to cry. Just then, a kind fish heard the frog crying and swam up to them. "What's wrong?" the fish asked. The frog told the fish about his lost ball. The fish smiled and said, "I know where your ball is. Follow me." The fish led Harry and the frog to a hidden spot in the river. There, they found the missing ball. The frog was very happy, and they all played together by the river.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big missile in his yard. He did not know what it was, but it looked cool. Tim wanted to show his friend Sam. So, he ran to Sam's house and said, "Sam, come see the big thing in my yard!" Sam went with Tim and saw the missile. Sam said, "Wow, that's big! What is it?" Tim said, "I don't know, but let's play with it." They started to play near the missile, but then they heard a voice. "Stop! Don't touch that!" said a man in a suit. The man told them that the missile was not a toy. The man took the missile away. Tim and Sam were sad, but they knew it was not safe. Then, the man came back with a big box. He said, "I have a surprise for you!" They opened the box and found lots of yummy treats inside. Tim and Sam were so happy. They forgot about the missile and enjoyed the yummy treats together.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was three years old and loved playing outside. One afternoon he went to the park with his mum. He saw an empty football in the grass. He wanted to play with it so his mum said he could. The little boy kicked the football, but it went too far away and he couldn't catch it. Suddenly a kind man met the little boy. He said, "Can I help you?". The kind man gave the little boy a hand and together they brought back the ball. The little boy thanked the man and the two of them played football together, until the sun went down. The little boy and his mum thanked the kind man and they all waved goodbye. The little boy was very happy and knew he would never forget his special day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big sack. He did not know what was in it. He opened the sack and saw a pretty angel. The angel had a calm face and kind eyes. Tim was very happy to meet the angel. "Hello, little boy," the angel said. "I am here to help you." Tim was excited. He asked the angel to play with him. They played all day and had a lot of fun. Tim was very tired but happy. The next day, Tim woke up and saw the sack was closed. He opened it, but the angel was not there. Tim was sad. Then, he heard a soft voice. "I am here, Tim," the angel said. She was now a small toy angel. She could still talk and play with Tim, but now she could go everywhere with him. They were best friends forever.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in the snow. They make a snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two black stones for eyes. "Hello, snowman!" Tom says. "Do you want to play with us?" The snowman does not say anything. He just stands there. Mia feels cold. She shivers. "Tom, I want to go inside. It is too cold here." Tom nods. "Okay, Mia. Let's go inside. We can have some hot chocolate and cookies." They say goodbye to the snowman and run to the house. They take off their boots and coats. They sit on the sofa with a blanket. Mom brings them hot chocolate and cookies. They sip and munch. They feel warm and happy. "Thank you, Mom," they say. Mom smiles. "You're welcome, Tom and Mia. Did you have fun outside?" "Yes, Mom," they say. "We made a snowman. He has a hat and a scarf and a carrot nose and two black eyes." Mom nods. "That sounds nice. But you know, snowmen don't last forever. They melt when the sun comes out." Tom and Mia look sad. They like their snowman. They don't want him to melt. "Can we keep him, Mom?" Tom asks. Mom shakes her head. "No, Tom. You can't keep him. He is made of snow. Snow belongs outside. He would not be happy inside. He would be too hot and wet. He would make a mess on the floor." Tom and Mia understand. They don't want their snowman to be unhappy. They don't want to make a mess. "Okay, Mom," they say. "We will let him go. But can we visit him tomorrow?" Mom smiles. "Of course, you can. As long as there is snow, you can visit your snowman. But remember, he is not a real friend. He is just a snowman. You have each other for real friends. And you have me and Dad and your toys and your books and your games. You have many things to make you happy." Tom and Mia hug Mom. They are happy. They have a snowman. But they have more. They have a family. They have a home. They have a hard sofa and a soft blanket. They have hot chocolate and cookies. They have each other. They are happy.
