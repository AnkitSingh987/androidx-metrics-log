Category: Beginner
Title: The Goose and the Pond

Once there was a goose who lived in a lovely pond. She was always so happy and her feathers were so soft. One day, she decided to explore and feel the ground outside of the pond. As she stepped out of the pond, she felt a strange feeling. Suddenly, she saw a boy! He was so tiny and cheerful. He smiled and waved at the goose. The goose was a bit scared at first, but the boy's smile made her feel calm. He reached out his hand and the goose went closer. The boy petted the goose on her head, and it felt so nice! She was so happy and felt a strong feeling of love for the little boy. The boy kept petting the goose and they felt so connected. It was a lovely moment and they both felt it. From that day on, the boy and the goose were the best of friends.

One day, a gifted dog named Bob could do a fun thing. He could surf on the sea! Bob liked to surf all day. He had many friends who liked to watch him surf. They would clap and cheer for Bob. One sunny day, Bob went to surf with his friends. They were all happy and excited. But, something unexpected happened. A big fish came to surf with Bob! The big fish could surf too. Bob and his friends were surprised. Bob and the big fish surfed together all day. They had so much fun. In the end, Bob and the big fish became good friends. They would surf together every day. Bob's friends would clap and cheer for both of them. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a pink cat went to the park. The cat liked to observe things. She saw a big dog playing with a ball. The cat wanted to play too, but the dog said, "No, you are too small. Go away!" The cat was sad and sat under a tree. Then, a little girl came to the park. She saw the pink cat and the big dog. The girl asked the dog, "Can the cat play with us?" The dog said, "No, the cat is too small." The girl was not happy with the dog. She wanted the cat to play too. The girl had a plan. She took the cat and hid behind a bush. Then, she threw the ball very far. The dog ran to get the ball. While the dog was away, the girl and the pink cat played together. They had so much fun. When the dog came back, he saw them playing and wanted to join. The girl said, "Now we can all play together!" And they did.

---

One day, a big tree had a small leaf. The leaf was green and happy. The tree wanted to teach the leaf how to dance with the wind. "Leaf, I will teach you to dance," said the tree. "When the wind blows, you move like this." The tree showed the leaf how to dance. The leaf tried to dance, but it looked foolish. The tree and the leaf laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird came and sat on the leaf. The bird was heavy, but the leaf did not break. The tree was surprised. The bird said, "Leaf, you are strong. You can dance with me." The leaf danced with the bird, and it was not foolish anymore. The tree was happy, and they all danced together.

Once upon a time, in an ancient forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run, jump, and play all day long. The ground in the forest was soft and perfect for Remy's little paws. One day, Remy was playing with his friends when he saw a big, shiny rock. Remy ran to the rock and paused to look at it. It was the most beautiful rock he had ever seen. Remy wanted to show the rock to his friends, so he picked it up and ran back to them. Remy's friends loved the shiny rock too. They all took turns holding it and admiring it. They played with the rock for hours, until it was time to go home. Remy put the rock back on the ground where he found it, so they could play with it again the next day. And so, Remy and his friends had many more happy days playing in their ancient forest.
