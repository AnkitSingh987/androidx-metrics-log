Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Dog

One day, a pretty girl named Sue and her dog, Max, went for a jog in the park. They saw a boy named Tim playing with a ball. Sue and Max went to play with Tim. "Hi Tim, can we play with you?" Sue asked. Tim said, "Yes, you can act like a dog and catch the ball too!" Sue and Max were happy to play with Tim. They played catch for a long time. Sue acted like a dog and ran fast, just like Max. They all had fun and laughed a lot. After the game, they sat under a big tree and shared a yummy snack. Sue, Max, and Tim became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a big dog named Max and a little cat named Lily were playing in the park. They heard a loud noise. Max and Lily looked around and saw a big truck. The truck was making the noise. Max said, "Let's hurry and see the truck!" Max and Lily ran to the truck. The truck was very big and fine. It was shiny and had many colors. The truck driver saw Max and Lily. He said, "Hi, do you like my fine truck?" Max and Lily nodded their heads. They loved the fine truck. The truck driver said, "I have to go now. Goodbye, Max and Lily!" Max and Lily waved goodbye to the truck driver. They went back to the park and played some more. They were happy they saw the fine truck and would always remember the fun day they had.

Once upon a time, there was a big wolf. The wolf lived in a cold, icy land. He was always hungry and wanted to find food. One day, the wolf saw a little house. He could smell something yummy inside. The wolf thought, "I must rush and get the food before it is gone." So, he ran very fast to the house. When the wolf got to the house, he saw a little girl. The girl was scared and said, "Please, Mr. Wolf, do not eat me!" The wolf felt bad and did not eat the girl. Instead, they shared the yummy food. From that day on, the wolf and the girl were best friends.

Sara and Ben are playing in the yard. They have many toys: balls, cars, dolls, blocks. They like to make noise and have fun. But the sky is getting dark and the wind is blowing hard. They hear a loud boom. "Mommy, what is that?" Sara asks, scared. "That is a storm, sweetie. It is rain and thunder and lightning. We have to go inside now, before it gets wet and dangerous," Mommy says, coming out of the house. "Okay, Mommy. Come on, Ben, let's go," Sara says, grabbing her doll. But Ben does not want to leave. He likes the storm. He thinks it is exciting and cool. He wants to see the lightning and hear the thunder. He does not listen to Mommy and Sara. He runs to the other side of the yard, where his car is. "Ben, come back! It is not safe!" Mommy shouts. But Ben does not hear her. He is too busy playing with his car. He makes it go fast and jump over the rocks. He laughs and smiles. He does not see the big flash of lightning that hits the tree near him. He does not hear the loud crack that makes the tree fall down. He only feels the shock and the pain. "Ben!" Mommy and Sara scream. They run to him. They are scared and worried. They see him lying on the ground, with his car next to him. He is not moving. He is not laughing. He is not smiling. "Ben, are you okay?" Mommy asks, shaking him gently. Ben opens his eyes. He is confused and hurt. He does not know what happened. He sees Mommy and Sara crying. He sees the tree on the ground. He sees his car broken. He feels sorry and sad. "I'm sorry, Mommy. I'm sorry, Sara. I was bad. I did not listen. I wanted to play with the storm. But the storm was not fun. The storm was mean. The storm made a mess," he says, softly. Mommy hugs him. Sara hugs him. They are happy he is alive. They are not angry. They love him. "It's okay, Ben. We are glad you are okay. The storm is over now. We can go inside and get warm and dry. We can fix your car later. We can clean the mess later. We are a family. We stick together," Mommy says, smiling. They go inside. They leave the toys in the yard. They do not care. They have each other. They are safe. They are happy.
