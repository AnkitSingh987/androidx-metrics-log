Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Sue found a regular card on the floor. It was a pretty card with a picture of a cat on it. She wanted to know who it belonged to, so she asked her mom for help. Together, they walked around their house and asked all their friends if they lost a card. But no one said it was theirs. Sue felt sad because she wanted to help someone find their card. Finally, they asked their neighbor, Mr. Brown, who had a big smile on his face. He said, "That's my card! Thank you for your help, Sue!" Sue was so happy that she could help Mr. Brown find his card.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of crayons. He loved to draw with them. Tim had a crazy idea. He wanted to make the whole world colorful. Tim started to draw on the walls. He used red, blue, and green crayons. His mom saw the walls and said, "Tim, you can't draw on the walls!" Tim felt sad. He wanted to make the world colorful, but he didn't want to make his mom sad. Tim's mom cared about him. She gave him a big piece of paper. "You can draw on this paper," she said. Tim was happy. He used his crayons to draw a big, colorful world on the paper. Tim and his mom looked at the drawing and smiled. They both loved the crazy, colorful world Tim had made.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim in the big pond. One day, Fin saw a hedge near the pond. The hedge was green and pretty. Fin wanted to see the hedge up close. He swam closer and closer to the edge of the pond. Fin was so excited to see the hedge, he didn't notice the bad cat watching him. The bad cat jumped and got Fin. Fin could not swim back to the pond. The bad cat took Fin away, and Fin was very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous bird named Bob. Bob loved to soar high in the sky. One day, Bob saw a fence in a big yard. He thought it would be fun to sit on the fence and watch the people. As Bob sat on the fence, a little girl named Sue came up to him. "Hi, Bob!" she said. "Do you want to play with me?" Bob nodded and started to hop along the fence. They played together and had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Bob suddenly changed into a little boy! Sue was very surprised. "Bob, you are not a bird anymore!" she said. Bob smiled and said, "I was a boy who was turned into a bird by a witch. Now, I am free!" They continued to play and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin who loved to swim. He would swim all day with his friends. They had a lot of fun in the water. The penguin was the best swimmer available. One day, the penguin saw a big fish. He wanted to catch the fish for his friends. So, he swam very fast to get the fish. But, the big fish was not a fish. It was a big, friendly whale! The whale said, "Hi, penguin! I like to swim too." The penguin was very surprised. He did not know that whales could be friends too. So, the penguin and the whale swam together. They had even more fun with their new friend.

One day, a cat and a dog sit near a big dish. The dish is dull and not fun to look at. The cat and dog are sad because their dish is not pretty. They want to make the dish look nice, so they can be happy again. The cat and dog go look for things to make the dish pretty. The cat finds a red ball and the dog finds a blue ribbon. They put the ball and ribbon in the dish. The dish looks nice now, but they feel like something is still missing. Just then, a bird flies down and lands on the dish. The bird is very colorful and pretty. The cat and dog are very surprised! The bird starts to sing a happy song. Now, the dish looks and sounds fun. The cat and dog are happy, and they all play together with their pretty dish.
