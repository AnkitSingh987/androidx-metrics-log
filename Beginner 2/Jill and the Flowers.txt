Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and the Flowers

Jill liked to wave at the big trucks that passed by her house. She knew that every Tuesday, the garbage truck would come and make a loud noise. She liked to watch how the men threw the bags into the back of the truck and pushed a button. She thought they were very organized and fast. One day, she decided to make a sign for them. She took a big piece of paper and some crayons and wrote "THANK YOU" in big letters. She also drew some flowers and stars to make it pretty. She was very proud of her sign and wanted to show it to the garbage men. She waited by the window with her sign, holding it up high. She saw the garbage truck coming down the street and got very excited. She ran outside and waved at the men with her sign. They saw her and smiled. They stopped the truck and got out. "Hi, little girl, what's your name?" one of them asked. "I'm Jill, and this is for you," she said, handing them the sign. "Wow, this is very nice of you, Jill. Thank you for making this for us. You are very kind and creative," the other one said. They gave her a high five and a sticker that said "I love garbage trucks". They put her sign on the front of the truck and waved at her as they drove away. Jill was very happy. She waved back at them and ran inside to tell her mom what happened. She put the sticker on her shirt and said, "Mom, I made some new friends today. They are very organized and fast. And they like my sign."

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. They had a big bucket and a small shovel to make sand castles. They worked together and had fun. One day, they saw a new boy in the park. He had a red truck and a blue hat. He came over to Anna and Ben and said, "Hello, can I play with you?" Anna and Ben said, "Yes, you can play with us. We are making sand castles. Do you want to help?" The new boy said, "Yes, I want to help. But I want to use your bucket and shovel. They are better than mine." Anna and Ben did not like that. They said, "No, you cannot use our bucket and shovel. They are ours. You can use your truck and hat." The new boy got angry. He said, "No, I want to use your bucket and shovel. Give them to me now!" He tried to grab the bucket and shovel from Anna and Ben. They held on tight and said, "No, go away!" They started to quarrel. They pulled and pushed and shouted. The sand castles they made were ruined. They were not happy. A lady who worked in the park saw them. She came over and said, "Stop, stop, what are you doing? Why are you quarreling? You should share and be nice to each other." She took the bucket and shovel and the truck and hat. She said, "You cannot play with these toys until you calm down and say sorry. Go and sit on that bench and think about what you did." Anna and Ben and the new boy felt ashamed. They went and sat on the bench. They did not look at each other. They were quiet. After a while, Anna said, "I'm sorry. I did not mean to quarrel with you. I like to play with you. You are my friend." Ben said, "I'm sorry too. I did not mean to quarrel with you. I like to play with you. You are my friend." They looked at the new boy. He said, "I'm sorry too. I did not mean to quarrel with you. I was rude and selfish. I want to play with you. Can you be my friends?" Anna and Ben smiled and said, "Yes, we can be your friends. You can play with us. We can share our toys. We can make new sand castles together." They hugged and said, "We are sorry, lady. We will not quarrel again. We will be nice to each other." The lady smiled and said, "That's good. I'm glad you are friends. You can have your toys back. But remember, be kind and dependable. That's how you make good friends." Anna and Ben and the new boy said, "Thank you, lady. We will remember. We will be kind and dependable." They took their toys and went back to the sand. They played together and had fun. They made new sand castles. They were happy.
