Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a charming boy who loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, Tim saw a big box in the kitchen. It was a new refrigerator! Tim was so happy and excited. "Mom, can I help put food in the refrigerator?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can help me." They started putting food in the refrigerator together. But then, Tim saw his favorite ice cream in the freezer. He wanted to eat it right away. Tim asked his mom, "Can I eat the ice cream now?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We will eat it after dinner." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to wait. He helped his mom finish putting the food in the refrigerator. Later, after dinner, Tim's mom took out the ice cream. She said, "Tim, you were such a good helper today. Now we can eat the ice cream!" Tim was so happy. He knew that waiting was worth it. After they ate the ice cream, his mom and dad both clapped and said, "Good job, Tim!" They all laughed and enjoyed the ice cream together. Tim felt proud and knew that helping and waiting was the right thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy skull. The skull was very smelly. Tim did not like the smell, but he loved the skull. He took it everywhere with him. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and his smelly skull. He saw a big dog. The dog was nice and wanted to play. Tim threw the skull for the dog to catch. The dog was fast and caught the skull. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog did not want to give the skull back to Tim. The dog bit the skull and ran away. Tim was sad and cried. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we will find your skull." They looked for the skull all over the park. Then, they saw the dog again. The dog was playing with another toy. Tim's mom asked the dog's owner if they could have the smelly skull back. The owner said, "Of course!" and gave the skull back to Tim. But the skull was not smelly anymore! The dog's bite had made the smell go away. Tim was so happy to have his skull back and even happier that it was not smelly anymore. He played with the dog and his new friend all day long. And from that day on, Tim, the dog, and the skull were the best of friends.

Anna and Ben love to play in the garden. They like to dig, run, and hide. One day, they find a bright green fruit under a big tree. It is round and small, like a ball. "What is this?" Anna asks, holding the fruit. "I don't know. Let's smell it," Ben says, taking the fruit from Anna. They both sniff the fruit. It has a strong and sour smell. They make funny faces. "Yuck! It smells bad!" Anna says. "Maybe it tastes good. Let's try it," Ben says, biting the fruit. He spits it out right away. It is very sour and bitter. He makes a louder funny face. "Yuck! It tastes worse!" Ben says. Anna laughs at Ben. She does not want to try the fruit. "Let's throw it away. It is not good for us," Anna says. They toss the fruit over the fence. It lands in a trash can. "That was a lime. It is not a fruit for kids. It is a fruit for grown-ups. They use it to make drinks and food," a voice says. They turn around and see their mom. She is smiling. "Mom, how do you know?" Anna asks. "I know because I love limes. They are good for me. They have vitamin C and make me healthy. But they are too sour for you. You need sweeter fruits, like apples and oranges," mom says. She gives them each a juicy apple. They thank her and bite into their apples. They are sweet and crunchy. They love apples. They also love their mom. She knows a lot of things. She is the best.
