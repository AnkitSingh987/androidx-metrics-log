Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lucy's mom told her to be a healthy girl and eat her fruits and veggies. Lucy promised her mom that she would eat them all. Lucy and her friends went to the park to play. They saw a butterfly flying around. Lucy had a net, so she tried to catch the butterfly. She ran and ran, but the butterfly was too fast. Her friends laughed and cheered her on. Finally, Lucy caught the butterfly in her net. She was so happy! She showed her friends the pretty butterfly. Then, Lucy let the butterfly go free. They all clapped and smiled. Lucy remembered her promise to her mom and went home to eat her healthy fruits and veggies.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lawyer named Tom. He was very good at his job, but he was unknown to most people. Tom wanted to help others, so he went to the town center to meet new friends. One day, he met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy. Tom wanted to help Tim find his toy. They looked high and low, near and far. Finally, they found the toy close to a big tree. Tim was so happy, and they became good friends. Now, Tom the lawyer was not unknown anymore. He helped many people in the town and made lots of friends. Everyone loved Tom, and he was happy to be a helpful friend to all.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big book. She opened the book and saw many pages. Sue was very careful with the book. She did not want to hurt the pages. Sue loved the pictures on the pages. Sue saw a picture of a frog on one page. The frog looked sad. Sue said, "Why are you sad, frog?" The frog looked at Sue and said, "I am sad because I need a kiss." Sue was surprised the frog could talk. Sue was brave and gave the frog a kiss. The frog turned into a happy cat! The cat said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue and the cat played and had fun. They were very happy together.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went outside to play. They loved to run and jump in the sun. Max was a dependable dog who always stayed by Tim's side. As they played, they saw dark clouds coming. They knew it was time to go home. "Max, let's go home before the shower comes!" said Tim. They started to walk back, but then they saw a big rainbow in the sky. Tim and Max stopped to gaze at the beautiful colors. As they looked at the rainbow, a small, friendly bird flew down and landed on Max's head. Tim and Max were very surprised. The bird began to talk. "Hello, I am a magic bird. I can grant you one wish." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a sunny day, so we can keep playing outside." The magic bird flapped its wings, and the dark clouds went away. The sun came out, and Tim and Max played happily outside all day long.

Tom and Mia are playing with their toys in the living room. Tom has a car with a zipper on the back. He can open and close the zipper and put things inside the car. Mia has a doll with a dress and a hat. She likes to comb the doll's hair and change her clothes. "Look, Mia, I have a zipper on my car. It is very fun. Do you want to try?" Tom says to Mia. "Yes, please. Can I put my doll in your car?" Mia asks. "Sure, why not? Here, I will open the zipper for you." Tom says. He pulls the zipper down and makes a sound. Zzzzzz. Mia smiles and puts her doll in the car. She tries to close the zipper, but it is hard. She pulls and pulls, but the zipper does not move. "Oh no, I can't close the zipper. It is stuck. What can we do?" Mia says. She is sad and worried. "Don't worry, Mia. I will help you. Maybe the doll's hat is in the way. Let me see." Tom says. He is helpful and kind. He stares at the zipper and sees a bit of the hat poking out. He gently pushes the hat inside the car and then pulls the zipper up. Zzzzzz. "Yay, you did it! Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend." Mia says. She is happy and grateful. "You are welcome, Mia. You are a good friend, too. Do you want to play some more?" Tom says. "Yes, let's play some more. We can take turns with the car and the doll. That will be fun." Mia says. They hug and smile. They play with the car and the doll until it is time for lunch. They are good friends who share and help each other. They have a lot of fun.
