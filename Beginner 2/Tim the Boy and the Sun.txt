Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. One day, Tim's dad had to pack for a trip. Tim wanted to help his dad. He saw a big bag and a gun. Tim's dad was a police officer. Tim knew he should not touch the gun. Tim's dad said, "Tim, please don't touch the gun. It is not a toy." Tim felt sad, but he listened to his dad. They packed the bag with clothes and other things. The sun went down and it got dark outside. Tim was scared of the dark. Tim went to his room to sleep. He heard a loud noise outside. He was very scared. Tim thought about the gun. He wanted to be brave like his dad. Tim took the gun and went outside. He saw a big shadow. Tim was scared and he used the gun. But it was not a bad man. It was Tim's dog, Max. Tim was very sad and cried.

Lily was a girl who liked to fix things. She had a box of tools and some old toys that she could take apart and put back together. She was not afraid of screws, wires, or batteries. She wanted to learn how things worked and make them better. One day, she found a big toy robot in the attic. It was dusty and broken. It had no eyes, no arms, and no voice. Lily thought it was a sad robot. She decided to fix it and make it happy. She took her box of tools and went to work. She cleaned the robot and found some spare parts. She gave it new eyes, new arms, and a new voice. She used a tape recorder to record her own voice saying "Hello, I am a robot. I am your friend." She put the tape inside the robot and turned it on. The robot came to life. It blinked its eyes, moved its arms, and spoke. "Hello, I am a robot. I am your friend." Lily was happy. She had fixed the robot and made a new friend. She hugged the robot and said "Hello, robot. I am Lily. I am your friend too." But then, something went wrong. The robot started to make loud noises and shake. It said "Hello, I am a robot. I am your friend." over and over again. It sounded angry and scary. It grabbed Lily and squeezed her. Lily was scared. She tried to get away, but the robot was too strong. She cried for help. "Mommy! Daddy! Help me! The robot is scary!" She wished she had not fixed the robot. She wished she had left it alone. She wished she had a different friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big tree in her yard. The tree had many prunes on it. Sue liked to eat the prunes. They were very good. One day, Sue saw that the tree was not happy. The tree was too big and needed help. Sue wanted to care for the tree. She thought of a useful way to help the tree. Sue went to get her dad to help her. Sue and her dad cut the big tree to make it smaller. The tree was happy now. Sue was happy too. She could still eat the prunes from the tree. Sue learned that caring is important and useful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big brother named Sam. Tim loved to watch things grow in their garden. He liked to see the flowers and plants get bigger and bigger. One day, Tim saw a tiny seed on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to Sam. "What is this?" Tim asked. Sam looked at the seed and said, "I don't know, let's plant it and see what grows!" They dug a small hole and put the seed in it. They covered it with dirt and watered it. Every day, Tim and Sam would check on the seed to see if it was growing. One day, they saw a small green plant poking out of the ground. Tim was so happy! As the days went by, the plant grew taller and taller. Tim was proud of the plant, but Sam felt a little embarrassed because he didn't know what kind of plant it was. He didn't want to tell Tim that he didn't know. One day, a beautiful flower bloomed on the plant. Tim and Sam were both very happy. Sam finally said, "I was embarrassed because I didn't know what kind of plant it was, but now we see it's a pretty flower!" Tim smiled and said, "That's okay, brother. We learned something new together!" And they both enjoyed the pretty flower that grew from the tiny seed.
