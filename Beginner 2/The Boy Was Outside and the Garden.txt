Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy Was Outside and the Garden

One day, a boy was outside in the garden. He saw something very special in the sky. It was a big icy white cloud. He ran around the garden and he saw a hose. He lifted up the hose and pointed it up at the sky. He squeezed the trigger and began to spray the sky. The cloud moved as the boy sprayed it. He was so excited that he couldn't stop spraying. Soon the sky changed colours. It was now filled with lots of little colourful drops of water. He ran over to the garden and could see ice crystals forming on the food in the garden. The food was now completely icy! The boy was amazed at his work. He had used the hose to spray the sky and the food in the garden was now icy. He was very proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house near a big mountain. Tim loved to play outside and look at the incredible mountain. One day, he wanted to measure how tall the mountain was. Tim asked his friends, the birds, to help him. The birds flew up, up, up to the top of the mountain. They told Tim that the mountain was very, very tall. Tim thought, "I want to climb the mountain and see the top!" Tim started to climb the mountain with the help of the birds. They flew by his side, making sure he was safe. It was a long, hard climb, but Tim did not give up. He knew he could do it if he tried his best. Finally, Tim reached the top of the incredible mountain. He looked down and saw his small house far away. He felt so proud and happy. The birds cheered for him, and they all flew back down together. The moral of the story is that if you work hard and never give up, you can do incredible things. Just like Tim, who was able to climb the big mountain with the help of his friends, the birds.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob had a deep love for carrots. He would hop around in the soft grass, looking for carrots to eat. One day, Bob found a big carrot patch. The carrot patch was in a sunny place, near a big tree. Bob wanted to own all the carrots in the patch. He began to dig with his little paws. He dug and dug, pulling out many carrots. Soon, he had a big pile of carrots. Bob was very happy with his pile of carrots. Bob shared his carrots with his friends. They all came to the carrot patch and ate the yummy carrots together. They played games and had lots of fun. In the end, Bob learned that it was more fun to share his deep love for carrots with his friends than to own all the carrots by himself.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with a useful knot. A little bird lived in the knot. The bird had a friend, a small cat. They played every day. One day, the cat did not come. The bird felt sad and missed her friend. The bird flew to look for the cat. The cat was with a new friend, a dog. The bird felt more sad. The bird went back to the knot. The cat and dog saw the bird was sad. They went to the knot and asked the bird to play. The bird was happy. They all played together. The moral of the story is that friends can be friends with other friends.

Ben and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat something good. They went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. They saw a lot of food inside. "Look, Lily, there is cheese and ham and bread. We can make a sandwich!" Ben said. "Yes, let's do that. And we can have some juice too!" Lily said. They took out the cheese and the ham and the bread and the juice. They put them on the table. They were about to make their sandwich when they smelled something bad. "Yuck, what is that smell?" Ben asked. He looked at the cheese. It was green and fuzzy. It had rot on it. "Ew, the cheese is rot. We can't eat that. It will make us sick!" Lily said. She looked at the ham. It was brown and slimy. It had rot on it too. "Yuck, the ham is rot too. We can't eat that either. It will make us worse!" Lily said. They looked at the bread. It was white and soft. It had no rot on it. "Phew, the bread is good. We can eat that. But we need something else to put on it," Ben said. He looked at the juice. It was orange and sweet. It had no rot on it. "Hey, the juice is good too. We can drink that. But we can't put it on the bread. That would be silly," Lily said. They thought for a moment. They had no cheese and no ham and no other food to put on the bread. They were still hungry. "What can we do?" Ben asked. They heard a voice from the living room. It was their mom. "Ben, Lily, are you in the kitchen? I have a surprise for you!" their mom said. They ran to the living room. They saw their mom holding a big cake. It had frosting and sprinkles and candles on it. "Happy birthday, Ben and Lily! I made you a cake. You can have a big slice each. And you can have some milk too," their mom said. Ben and Lily were happy. They forgot about the rot food in the refrigerator. They hugged their mom and thanked her for the cake. "Mom, you are the best. You are so kind and honest and good. We love you," Ben and Lily said. They ate their cake and drank their milk. They were not hungry anymore. They were full and happy. They had a good birthday.
