Category: Beginner
Title: The Farmer and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who lived in a small house. He had a big farm with many animals. One day, the farmer went for a jog near his farm. While he was jogging, he saw a rude dog. The dog barked loudly at the farmer. The farmer was scared but he said, "Please, don't be rude, dog. I am just jogging." The dog stopped barking and said, "I am sorry, farmer. I was just trying to protect my puppies." The farmer smiled and said, "That's okay, dog. We can be friends." And they jogged together back to the farm. From that day on, the farmer and the dog were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to ride his bike. One day, he saw a big hill and wanted to ride down it. Tim was a little scared. But he wanted to try. He held his bike tight around the waist and started to go down the hill. Tim went fast. Very fast. He could not stop. He hit a big rock and fell off his bike. Tim cried. He was hurt and sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to gain a new toy. He went to his mom and asked, "Mom, can I have a new toy?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, but first you must clean your room." Tim went to his room and started to clean. He found a big chest in the corner. He opened the chest and saw many toys inside. Tim was very happy! He took out the toys and played with them. Tim's dad came into the room and saw Tim playing. He said, "Wow, Tim! You have a strong chest full of toys!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Dad! I gained a lot of toys today!" Tim and his dad played with the toys together, and they had a great day.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a huge orange in her garden. She had never seen such a big orange before. She was very happy and wanted to show her friend, Tim. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Look, Tim! I found a huge orange! What do you think happened?" Tim looked at the orange and said, "Wow! That's the biggest orange I've ever seen! Maybe a magic tree grew it just for you!" Together, they rolled the huge orange back to Lily's garden. They sat under the tree and shared the orange. It was the sweetest orange they had ever tasted. From that day on, Lily and Tim knew that magic could happen in their garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with yarn. One day, she found a fine ball of yarn in her house. It was red and soft. She was very happy. Kitty played with the yarn all day. She rolled it, chased it, and even jumped on it. But then, she heard a loud noise. It made her tremble with fear. She hid behind the couch and peeked out to see what it was. It was just her friend, Doggy. Doggy had come to play with Kitty. They played with the fine yarn together. They had lots of fun and Kitty did not tremble anymore. They played until it was time for bed. And they knew they would have more fun the next day.

One day, a rich man went to the store. He saw a toy that he liked. The toy was very pretty. The rich man wanted to buy it. But he did not have any money with him. So, he thought he could pinch it and take it home. A little girl saw the rich man. She said, "Why do you want to pinch the toy? That is not nice." The rich man felt bad. He said, "You are right. I should not pinch the toy. I will go home and get my money." The rich man went home to search for his money. He looked everywhere. But he could not find it. He felt sad. Then, the little girl came to his house. She said, "I found your money at the store. You dropped it." The rich man was very happy. He thanked the little girl. The rich man and the little girl went back to the store. The rich man bought the toy with his money. He gave the toy to the little girl as a gift. They both learned that it is better to be honest and kind. The moral of the story is: Always be honest and kind, and good things will happen to you.
