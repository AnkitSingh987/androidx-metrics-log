Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Polite Pupil and the Bear

One day, a polite pupil named Tim went to school. He was nice to all his friends. His teacher, Miss Jane, was happy with him. She said, "Tim, you are a good boy." Miss Jane told the class a story about a big bear who did not dare to go into the water. All the other animals laughed at the bear. Tim thought it was a funny story. He did not know that something unexpected would happen later. After school, Tim went to the park with his friends. They saw a big bear by the water. Tim remembered the story and dared the bear to go into the water. The bear looked at Tim and said, "I am not like the bear in the story. I can swim." The bear jumped into the water and swam. Tim and his friends were surprised. They learned not to judge a bear by a story.

One day, a cat named Tom saw a smooth stone. The stone had a symbol on it. Tom did not know what the symbol was. He was very tired, so he let out a big yawn. Tom decided to take the stone home with him. At home, Tom showed the stone to his friend, a dog named Max. Max looked at the symbol and yawned too. They both felt very sleepy. They took a long nap together. When they woke up, Tom and Max were surprised to see that the stone had changed. The symbol was gone, and in its place was a picture of a big, yummy cake. Tom and Max looked at each other and laughed. They knew the stone was magic. From that day on, they played with the magic stone and had lots of fun.

One day, a deaf man went to the park. He liked to sit and watch the kids play. He could not hear them, but he could see them having fun. A little boy came up to the deaf man and asked, "Can you play with me?" The man could not hear the boy, but he could see that the boy wanted to play. So the man smiled and began to play with the boy. They played for a long time. Then, the boy's mom came to the park. She saw the boy playing with the deaf man and was very happy. She said, "Thank you for playing with my son. I am deaf too, and I can't play with him like you can." The man was surprised but happy. In the end, they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red rug. The rug lived in a big room with lots of toys. One day, a little girl named Amy came into the room. She saw the rug and said, "I know you! You are my rug!" Amy sat on the rug and played with her toys. She had a lot of fun. Then, she heard a small voice say, "Hello, Amy!" She looked around but saw no one. "Who said that?" she asked. The voice said, "It's me, the rug!" Amy was very surprised. She did not know that the rug could talk. They became good friends and played together every day. And that is how Amy found her best friend in the bright red rug.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to create things with his hands. One day, he found a long piece of string and had an idea. Tim went to his friend, Lily, and said, "Let's play a game! We can make a line with this string and see who can walk on it the best." Lily liked the idea and they began to play. They took turns walking on the string line, trying not to fall off. As they played, Tim's little sister, Sue, came outside with a sour face. She wanted to play too. Tim and Lily let Sue join in, and they all had fun walking on the line. The more they played, the better they got at it. In the end, they all laughed and had a great time together.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to walk all the way to the park every day. She loved to play on the swings and slides with her friends. One sunny day, Lily saw a big, red ball up in a tree. She wanted to play with it, but it was too high for her to reach. She tried to jump, but she still could not get it. Lily felt sad. Then, a tall boy named Sam came by and saw Lily. He asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily pointed to the ball in the tree and said, "I want to play, but I can't reach it." Sam smiled and said, "I can help." He reached up and got the ball for Lily. They played together all day and became the best of friends.
