Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Print

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big print on the ground. The print was very big and round. Tim was very excited and wanted to know what made the print. He ran to his friend, Sue, to show her the print. "Look, Sue! I found a big print on the ground. Let's find out what made it!" Tim said. Sue was excited too, so they started to follow the print. They saw more and more prints, and they walked a long way. As they walked, they found a big, healthy tree. They were very surprised to see that the tree had legs! The tree was making the prints. "Hello, I am a walking tree," said the tree. Tim and Sue could not believe it. They became friends with the walking tree and had lots of fun together.

Lily was a little girl who liked to dance. She danced in her room, in the garden, and in the park. She danced with her dolls, her dog, and her friends. She danced when she was happy, when she was sad, and when she was bored. One day, Lily was dancing in the park with her friend Mia. They were spinning and jumping and laughing. Lily saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. She ran to the tree and grabbed a branch. She tried to pull herself up, but the branch was too high. She stretched and stretched, but she could not reach it. "Help me, Mia!" Lily called. "I want to climb the tree!" Mia ran to the tree and tried to help Lily. She pushed Lily's feet and lifted her up. Lily felt happy and excited. She reached the branch and swung on it. She smiled at Mia and said, "Thank you, Mia! You are a good friend!" But then, something bad happened. The branch broke and Lily fell to the ground. She landed on her arm and felt a sharp pain. She cried and cried. She could not move her arm. It was swollen and red. Mia was scared and worried. She ran to a grown-up and told him what happened. The grown-up called an ambulance and took Lily and Mia to the hospital. The hospital was a big building with many rooms and people. Lily saw doctors and nurses and machines. She felt scared and sad. A doctor came to see Lily and looked at her arm. He said, "You have a broken arm, Lily. You need a cast to make it heal. It will hurt a little, but it will get better soon. Don't worry, you are brave and strong." The doctor put a white cast on Lily's arm. He asked her what color she liked. Lily said, "Pink." The doctor smiled and gave her a pink marker. He said, "You can draw on your cast and make it pretty. You can also ask your friends and family to sign it and write nice things." Lily liked the idea. She drew a flower and a heart on her cast. She asked Mia to sign it and write, "I love you, Lily. You are my best friend." Mia did and hugged Lily. Lily felt happy and grateful. She said, "Thank you, Mia. You are my best friend too. You helped me a lot today. I'm sorry I made you scared." Mia said, "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you are okay. You are very brave and strong. I hope your arm gets better soon. Can we still dance together?" Lily said, "Yes, we can. We can dance with one arm. We can dance with our feet. We can dance with our smiles. We can dance with our hearts. We can always dance, Mia. We love to dance."

One day, Sam the cat was feeling hot. He was so hot he could hardly stand it. He decided to bathe. He ran over to the lab. When he got to the lab, he was surprised to see a big hairy dog. It was a big Labrador Retriever with long, fluffy fur. Sam could tell it was very hairy. Sam was scared, so he tried to get away. But the big hairy lab blocked him with its big furry body. The lab started to bathe in a big bowl. He splashed around and made a big mess! Sam was so amazed, he decided to bathe too. He hopped in the water and had a good time. He splashed around and made a big mess just like the hairy lab! When they were done, they both ran out of the lab. They were both happy and very clean. The end.
