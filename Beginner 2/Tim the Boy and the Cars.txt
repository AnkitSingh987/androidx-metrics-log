Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cars

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, Tim found a roll of tape in his room. He had a perfect idea! Tim took the tape and made a long road on the floor for his cars. The cars went vroom-vroom on the tape road. Tim's mom came in and saw the road. She said, "Wow, Tim! What a great road for your cars!" Tim was happy and played with his cars all day. When it was time for bed, he felt tired. He put his cars away and got ready to sleep. Tim lay down and settled into his cozy bed, dreaming of more adventures with his cars and tape road.

---

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play hide and seek. One day, they decided to play in the big park near their house. There were many trees and bushes and benches to hide behind. Tom was the seeker and Lily was the hider. Lily ran to find a good spot and Tom counted to ten. "Ready or not, here I come!" Tom shouted and started to look for Lily. He looked behind the swings, under the slide, and in the sandbox, but he could not find her. He wondered where she was. He thought she was very intelligent and good at hiding. He walked around the park and saw a big tent. It was red and white and had a sign that said "Magic Show". Tom was curious and wanted to see what was inside. He walked to the tent and peeked through a small hole. He saw a man in a black hat and a cape. He had a wand and a rabbit. He was talking to some people who were sitting on chairs. "Welcome to the magic show, ladies and gentlemen!" the man said. "I am the great Magico and I will amaze you with my tricks. For my first trick, I will make this rabbit disappear!" Tom watched as the man waved his wand and said some words. He put the rabbit in a box and closed it. Then he opened it again and the rabbit was gone. The people clapped and cheered. Tom was amazed too. He wanted to see more. But then he heard a voice behind him. It was Lily. She had found him. "Gotcha!" she said. "I saw you peeking at the tent. You're not supposed to do that. You're supposed to find me." Tom turned around and saw Lily. She was smiling and holding a balloon. She had found the balloon man and got a balloon for herself. She had been hiding behind the tent. "I'm sorry, Lily," Tom said. "I was looking for you, but then I saw the tent and I wanted to see the magic show. It was very cool. The man made a rabbit disappear." Lily nodded. She understood. She liked magic too. "Maybe we can watch the show together," she said. "But first, let's find Mom and Dad and tell them where we are. They might be worried." Tom agreed. He took Lily's hand and they walked to the tent entrance. They saw a sign that said "Next show in 15 minutes". They decided to wait for the next show. They hoped it would be as cool as the first one. They were happy. They had found each other and a new event to enjoy.

Lily and Sam were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but they always wanted more. One day, their mom said they had to share their toys with their cousin Will, who was coming to visit. "No, no, no!" Lily and Sam said. "We don't want to share! Will is mean and he breaks our toys!" Their mom was not happy. She said, "Lily and Sam, you have to be nice to Will. He is your cousin and he has no toys at home. Sharing is caring. If you share your toys, I will make you a yummy cake for dessert." Lily and Sam thought about the cake. They liked cake very much. It was sweet and soft and had frosting on top. They decided to share their toys with Will, but they still complained. When Will came, he was very happy to see the toys. He said, "Wow, Lily and Sam, you have so many toys! Can I play with them?" Lily and Sam nodded, but they said, "Yes, but you have to be careful. Don't break them or lose them. And you have to give them back when we say so." Will agreed, but he did not listen. He played with the toys very roughly. He threw them, he smashed them, he hid them. Lily and Sam were very angry. They said, "Will, stop it! You are ruining our toys! Give them back to us!" Will did not stop. He said, "No, they are mine now! I like them more than you!" Lily and Sam started to cry. They ran to their mom and told her what Will did. Their mom was very angry too. She said, "Will, that is not nice! You have to share and respect other people's things. Apologize to Lily and Sam and give them back their toys. Or you will have no cake for dessert." Will did not want to apologize or give back the toys. He said, "No, I don't care about the cake. I only care about the toys!" But then he smelled something yummy. He saw the cake on the table. It was big and round and had sprinkles on top. He wanted a slice of the cake. He said, "Wait, can I have some cake?" Their mom said, "No, you can't. You have to say sorry and share first. That is the rule." Will realized he was wrong. He said, "Sorry, Lily and Sam. I was mean and I broke your toys. Can I share them with you?" Lily and Sam were still sad, but they forgave Will. They said, "Yes, you can. But you have to be nice and gentle. And you have to listen to us." Will agreed. He gave back the toys and played with them nicely. He listened to Lily and Sam and had fun with them. They all smiled and hugged. Their mom was proud of them. She said, "Good job, Lily, Sam and Will. You are good cousins and good friends. Now you can have some cake for dessert." They all said, "Yay, cake!" They cut the cake and ate it. It was yummy. They were happy. The end.
