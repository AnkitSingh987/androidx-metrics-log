Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy the Tube and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little tube named Timmy. Timmy was a very foolish tube. He liked to dance all day long. One sunny day, Timmy went outside to play and dance. As Timmy was dancing, he met a friendly cat named Kitty. Kitty saw Timmy dancing and said, "Hi Timmy! Can I dance with you?" Timmy was so happy! "Yes, Kitty! Let's dance together!" And so, Timmy and Kitty danced and played all day long. After a while, the sun started to set, and Timmy and Kitty were very tired. They sat down under a big tree to rest. They looked at each other and smiled. Timmy said, "Kitty, I had so much fun today! Let's dance and play again tomorrow!" Kitty agreed, and they both went home to sleep, excited for another day of fun and dancing.

One evening, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. The sky was gloomy, and it looked like it might rain. Lily wore her yellow raincoat and boots just in case. As she walked, she saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a sign. It had a picture of a cat on it. Lily looked around and saw a small, wet cat sitting by the tree. The cat looked sad. "Hi, cat," Lily said. "Why are you sad?" The cat looked at Lily and said, "I am lost." Lily was surprised! She did not know cats could talk. She decided to help the cat find its home. Together, they followed the signs and found the cat's home. The cat was happy, and so was Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside in the sun. One day, it began to rain. Amy was sad because she could not play outside anymore. Amy's friend, Lily, lived next door. Lily liked the rain. She liked to dance and jump in the puddles. Amy saw Lily having fun and felt envious. She wanted to play in the rain too. So, Amy put on her raincoat and boots. She went outside and joined Lily. They both danced and jumped in the puddles. The rain was fun after all. Amy was happy and not envious anymore. They played until the rain stopped and the sun came out again.

One day, a kangaroo named Kiki was playing in the sun. She was a normal kangaroo, but she had a big wish. Kiki wanted to shrink so she could play with her small friends. Kiki asked her friend, the wise owl, "How can I shrink to play with my friends?" Owl said, "You can try to drink from the magic pond. It might help you shrink!" Kiki hopped to the magic pond and took a big sip. Kiki felt funny and she began to shrink! She became smaller and smaller. Now, Kiki was as small as her friends. They played all day and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Kiki went back to the magic pond and grew big again. She was happy to be a normal kangaroo once more.

Ben and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and trucks. Ben has a big red truck. Sam has a small blue car. They share their toys and have fun. One day, Sam sees a new yellow car in the shop. He wants it very much. He asks his mom to buy it for him, but she says no. She says he has enough toys and he needs to save his money. Sam is sad. He goes to Ben's house and sees his big red truck. He asks Ben if he can lend him his truck for a day. Ben says yes, because he is a good friend. He says he can play with his yellow car instead. Sam is happy. He takes the truck home and plays with it. He makes it go fast and loud. He pretends it is a fire truck and he is a fireman. He has a lot of fun. But he forgets to give the truck back to Ben. He keeps it for two days, then three days, then four days. Ben is sad. He misses his truck. He wants to play with it. He asks Sam to give it back, but Sam says no. He says he likes the truck too much. He says he will give it back later. Ben is angry. He says Sam is not a good friend. He says Sam is fat and greedy. He says he will not play with him anymore. He goes to his mom and tells her what happened. She says she will talk to Sam's mom and get the truck back. She calls Sam's mom and tells her the story. Sam's mom is surprised. She did not know Sam had Ben's truck. She goes to Sam's room and sees the truck. She asks Sam why he did not give it back. Sam says he is sorry. He says he forgot. He says he loves the truck. Sam's mom says he has to give the truck back to Ben. She says he has to say sorry to Ben. She says he has to be a good friend. She says he can save his money and buy his own yellow car. Sam says okay. He takes the truck to Ben's house and knocks on the door. Ben opens the door and sees the truck. He is happy. He takes the truck and hugs it. He says thank you to Sam. Sam says sorry to Ben. He says he will not do it again. He says he wants to be a good friend. Ben says okay. They smile at each other and go to the yard. They play with the truck and the car. They share their toys and have fun. They are friends again. The end.
