Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Compassionate Animal and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate animal named Bobo. Bobo was a big, soft bear. He lived in a green forest with many friends. All the animals loved Bobo because he was very kind and always helped them. One day, a small bird fell from her nest. She was scared and cried for help. Bobo heard the cry and came to see what was wrong. "Don't be afraid," said Bobo to the bird. "I will help you." Bobo carefully picked up the bird and put her back in her nest. The bird was happy and thanked Bobo. "You are the best friend I have," said the bird. Bobo smiled and gave the bird a gentle hug. From that day on, the bird and Bobo were best friends, and they played together every day.

One day Mom and her son went to the beach. The boy loved the sand and began running around, jumping up and down. His Mom couldn't help but laugh. Then, all of a sudden, the boy started picking up handfulsand of sand, and soon he had a huge pile. "What are you doing?" Mom asked. "I'm making sand castles," the boy answered. Mom smiled. She wanted to help him, but she had to quit. She told him "I can't help you today. The sand is too expensive." The boy thought for a minute, then nodded. He said, "Okay, I can make the castles by myself." Mom gave him a hug and kissed him on the head. "That's my brave boy," she said. The boy finished his sand castle and proudly showed it to Mom. She gave him a big hug, and they enjoyed a nice afternoon at the beach.

One day, two friends were walking in the park. Mary had a beautiful blue bow in her hair. Her friend Timmy was feeling very envious. "My bow is so much prettier than yours," he said to Mary. Mary smiled. She knew just what to do. She reached out for Timmy's hand and said, "Let's shake." Timmy smiled and put his hand in Mary's. Together, they started to swing their arms back and forth. After a few minutes, Mary stopped shaking and put one hand on Timmy's shoulder. She reached up and gently untied her blue bow. She placed it in his hand. Timmy's eyes widened. He couldn't believe it. He smiled and thanked Mary for the wonderful gift. From that moment on, they were the best of friends.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a new boy sitting on a bench. He had gray hair and glasses. Lily and Tom thought he looked funny and old. "Let's tease him!" Lily said to Tom. "He is so gray and slow!" They ran to the bench and shouted at the boy. "Hey, gray boy! Why are you so gray? Are you a grandpa? Can you run fast? Can you see well?" The boy looked sad and scared. He did not say anything. He just hugged his book and looked down. Lily and Tom laughed and ran away. They thought they were very clever and funny. But they did not see that a big dog was chasing them. The dog was angry and loud. It barked and snapped at them. Lily and Tom were very anxious. They did not know what to do. They ran and screamed for help. The boy on the bench heard them. He felt sorry for them. He put down his book and stood up. He whistled and called the dog. The dog stopped chasing Lily and Tom. It ran to the boy and licked his face. The boy smiled and petted the dog. "Good boy, Rex!" he said. "You are my best friend!" Lily and Tom were shocked. They realized that the boy was not a grandpa. He was just a boy with gray hair. And he was very brave and kind. They felt ashamed and sorry. They walked to the bench and said to the boy. "Hi, we are Lily and Tom. We are sorry for teasing you. You are not gray and slow. You are smart and nice. Can we be your friends?" The boy looked at them and nodded. He said. "Hi, I am Ben. I have gray hair because I was sick. But I am better now. I like to read books and play with Rex. Yes, you can be my friends." Lily and Tom smiled and said. "Thank you, Ben. We like to read books and play with dogs too. We are sorry for being mean. We learned a lesson. We should not judge people by how they look. We should be kind and friendly to everyone." They all sat on the bench and talked and laughed. They became good friends. And they never teased anyone again.
