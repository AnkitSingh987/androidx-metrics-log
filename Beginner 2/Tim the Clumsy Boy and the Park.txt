Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Clumsy Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim went to the park to play. He liked to run and jump on the grass. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad and began to cry. Tim asked, "Why do you cry, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy." Tim wanted to help Sue. They looked for the toy together. But Tim was clumsy and he fell down. He hurt his knee and started to cry too. Sue and Tim could not find the toy. They both cried and went home with their moms. It was a sad day at the park.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She had a small, clear toy guitar. She loved to play it every day. One sunny day, Ann went to the park. She saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad. "Why are you sad?" Ann asked. "I lost my toy," Tim said. Ann wanted to help. "I will help you find it," she said. They looked and looked. Then, Ann saw the toy by a tree. She jumped and sprang to get it. She gave the toy to Tim. Now, Tim was happy. Ann and Tim played together. They played with the guitar and the toy. They had lots of fun. They learned that helping others can make you happy too. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a rat. He was weak and needed to rest. He walked around looking for a nice place to rest. Suddenly he noticed a really beautiful house. He thought to himself "This will be a great place to restore my strength!" and he knocked on the door. Inside the house, a kind man answered the door. The rat quickly explained to the man that he was very weak and needed a place to rest. The man said "Of course, you can come in and restore your strength." The rat was so happy, he thanked the man and went inside to rest. The rat soon felt much better. He thanked the man and went on his way. From then on, the rat was never weak again!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big wish. She wanted to be a proud helper to her mom and dad. She loved to help them with little jobs around the house. One day, Amy's mom was making a cake. Amy wanted to help. She said, "Mom, let me help you make the cake." Her mom said, "No, Amy, you are too little." But Amy insisted. She said, "Please, mom, I can do it." So, Amy's mom let her help. Amy was very proud. She helped mix the cake, and it was yummy. Everyone loved the cake. Amy's wish came true. She was a proud helper to her mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom was sad because he lost all his hair. One day, he met a nice lady who knew magic. She said she could help him. Tom was very happy. The nice lady said a spell to make Tom's hair grow. But something unexpected happened. Instead of growing hair, Tom began to shrink! He became very small, like a tiny bug. Tom was scared and the nice lady was surprised. The nice lady said another spell to fix her mistake. Tom grew back to his normal size. This time, she tried a different spell. Tom's hair grew back, and he was not bald anymore. Tom was very happy and thanked the nice lady. They both laughed about the shrinking mistake and became good friends.

Ben and Mia like to play with their cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They make roads with their blocks and zoom their cars around. Sometimes they have races and see who is faster. One day, they find a big circle in the garage. It is shiny and has a hole in the middle. Ben says, "What is this? Can we play with it?" Mia says, "I don't know. Maybe it is a wheel for a big car. Let's try it." They roll the circle to the backyard. They take turns pushing it and running after it. The circle is very fast and makes a loud noise. They laugh and have fun. They try to make the circle turn left and right, but it is hard. They have to push it very hard. They see a big tree in the corner of the yard. They decide to make the circle go around the tree. Ben pushes the circle first. He runs with it and tries to make it turn. But the circle is too big and too fast. It hits the tree and bounces back. Ben falls down and hurts his knee. He cries and says, "Ow, ow, ow!" Mia runs to him and says, "Are you okay, Ben? I'm sorry. It was an accident." She helps him get up and hugs him. She says, "Let's go inside and get a band-aid. And some juice. And a cookie. Then we can play with our cars again. Okay?" Ben nods and says, "Okay. Thank you, Mia. You are a good sister." They leave the circle in the yard and go inside. They are happy and forget about the circle. They play with their cars and make open roads with their blocks. They are careful not to make their cars hit the tree. They are good friends.
