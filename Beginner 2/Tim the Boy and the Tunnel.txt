Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tunnel

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a light tunnel in his yard. He began to wonder what was inside. Tim walked into the light tunnel. He saw many colors on the walls. He heard birds singing and felt happy. Tim knew he found a magic place. In the end, Tim went back home. He told his mom and dad about the light tunnel. They all went to see it together. They had fun and played in the magic tunnel all day.

Ben and Lily love to play baseball in the park. They have a big red ball and a small wooden bat. They take turns to hit the ball and run to the bases. They are very excited and happy. One day, they hit the ball very hard and it flies over the fence. They run to the fence and look for the ball, but they cannot see it. They are sad and worried. They do not want to lose their ball. "Where is our ball?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Maybe it is in the bushes or under a car," Lily says. They look around the fence, but they cannot find their ball. They are about to give up when they hear a voice. "Hey, kids, are you looking for this?" the voice says. They turn around and see a man holding their ball. He is smiling and friendly. He is wearing a blue shirt and a hat with a star on it. "Is this your ball?" he asks. "Yes, it is! Thank you, mister!" Ben and Lily say. They run to the man and take their ball. They are very excited and grateful. They hug the ball and hug the man. "You're welcome, kids. I saw your ball land in my yard. It was a good hit. Do you like baseball?" the man asks. "Yes, we do! We love baseball!" Ben and Lily say. "Me too. I used to play baseball when I was young. Do you know who I am?" the man asks. Ben and Lily look at the man and his hat. They see the star and the letters. They are surprised and amazed. "Are you... are you a baseball star?" Ben asks. The man nods and laughs. "Yes, I am. I used to play for the Stars. My name is Joe. Do you want my autograph?" he asks. Ben and Lily nod and smile. "Yes, please!" they say. The man takes a pen and writes his name on their ball. He gives it back to them and says: "Here you go, kids. Keep playing and have fun. Maybe one day you will be baseball stars too." Ben and Lily thank the man again and say goodbye. They run back to the park and continue to play baseball. They are very excited and proud. They have their ball and a new friend. They do not lose their ball. They find a baseball star.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree in a small park. The oak tree was very stubborn and didn't like to share its space with others. One sunny day, a little bird came to the oak tree. The bird wanted to live in the tree and make a nest. The little bird said, "Hello, oak tree! I want to live here with you. Can I make a nest in your branches?" The stubborn oak tree didn't want the bird to live there. But the bird didn't give up. The bird started to whistle a pretty song to make the oak tree happy. The oak tree listened to the bird's song but still didn't want the bird to live there. The oak tree said, "No, you can't live here. I don't want to share my space with anyone." The little bird felt sad and flew away to find another tree to live in. As days went by, the stubborn oak tree stood all alone in the park. No other birds or animals wanted to live with the oak tree because it was not nice to them. The oak tree started to feel lonely and wished it had let the little bird stay. But it was too late. The oak tree had lost its chance to make friends. The oak tree stayed lonely and sad, and no one came to visit it anymore. The stubborn oak tree learned that being kind and sharing is important, but it was too late for the oak tree to change its ways.
