Category: Beginner
Title: The Fairy and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a funny fairy who wanted to cook. So she flew to the kitchen and started to make a delicious cake. "This will be the most yummy cake ever!" she said with a smile. As she was cooking, a big, mean ogre came in and shouted, "No fairies in my kitchen!" The fairy was scared, but bravely stood her ground and said, "Please let me finish my cake." But the ogre did not listen. In a mean voice he said, "If you don't leave my kitchen now, then I'll eat you for dessert!" The fairy shivered with fear. She had no choice but to fly away without her cake. As the fairy flew away, she thought to herself, "I should have known that an ogre and yummy cake don't mix."

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big crack in the wall of his room. He was scared and didn't know what to do. So, he went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a big crack in my room. I don't want it there." His mom looked at the crack and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will pray and ask for help to fix it." They both prayed together, and Tim felt a little better. The next day, a kind man named Jack came to their house with his dull tools. Jack said, "I heard you need help with a crack. I can fix it for you." Tim and his mom were very happy. Jack fixed the crack, and Tim's room was safe again. Tim thanked Jack and knew that his prayers were answered.

Sam and Jen are friends. They like to play in the swamp. The swamp has mud, frogs, and bugs. Sam and Jen have fun in the swamp. One day, Sam finds a big stick. He says, "Look, Jen, I have a stick. I can use it as a sword." He swings the stick in the air. Jen says, "Be careful, Sam. You might hit something or someone." Sam does not listen to Jen. He keeps swinging the stick. He drops the stick on a frog. The frog croaks and jumps away. Sam laughs. Jen does not laugh. She says, "That is not nice, Sam. You hurt the frog. You should say sorry." Sam does not say sorry. He says, "It is just a frog. It does not matter. I want to play with the stick." He picks up the stick again. He swings it at Jen. Jen dodges the stick. She says, "Stop it, Sam. You are not a reliable friend. You are a mean friend. I do not want to play with you." Sam does not stop. He swings the stick harder. He drops the stick on Jen's head. Jen cries. She says, "Ow, Sam, that hurts. You are a bad friend. You are a bully. I do not like you." She runs away from the swamp. Sam is alone in the swamp. He feels sad. He says, "I am sorry, Jen. I did not mean to hurt you. I was just playing. Please come back. I want to be your friend." But Jen does not come back. She is gone. Sam has no one to play with. He has no fun in the swamp.

One day, a lovely bear named Ben went for a walk. He wanted to find some honey. Ben walked and walked until he found a big tree. The tree had a hole in it. Ben looked inside the hole and found some honey. He was very happy. Ben started to eat the honey when a little bee came by. The bee said, "Hey! That is my honey!" Ben felt embarrass. He did not know it was the bee's honey. He said, "I am sorry, little bee. I did not know this was your honey." The little bee thought for a moment. Then, it said, "It is okay. We can share the honey." Ben and the little bee ate the honey together. They became good friends. From that day on, they always shared honey and had fun together. And Ben learned to always ask before taking something that was not his.

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Emma. She loved to look at things with her microscope. She could see tiny things that no one else could see. One day, Emma found a pretty leaf outside. She wanted to look at it with her microscope. So, she went to her friend Tom and asked, "Can you help me cut the leaf? I want to see it better." Tom said, "Yes, I can help you!" He took a pair of scissors and carefully cut the leaf into a small piece. Emma put the piece under her microscope and looked at it. She saw amazing things! The leaf had tiny lines and dots that she had never seen before. Emma and Tom spent the whole day looking at different things under the microscope. They felt so happy and smart. They knew that they could discover new things together. And that's what they did, every day, with their special microscope.
