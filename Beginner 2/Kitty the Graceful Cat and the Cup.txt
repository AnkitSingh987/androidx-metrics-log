Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Graceful Cat and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Kitty. She lived in a house with a lot of furniture. Kitty loved to jump and play on the furniture all day. One day, Kitty was playing on the couch. She saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. Kitty jumped to catch the ball, but she did not see the cup on the table. She hit the cup and it started to drop. The cup fell to the floor and broke into pieces. Kitty felt bad and knew she should be more careful. She learned that playing on the furniture can make things drop and break. From that day on, Kitty played on the floor and did not break anything again. The moral of the story is to be careful and think before you act.

Once upon a time, there was a creative boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy gun and pretend to shoot at things. One day, he found a shiny gem in the garden. "Wow! This gem is so pretty!" said Tim. He showed the gem to his friend, Sam. Sam wanted the gem too, but Tim did not want to share. They both started to argue. Tim's mom saw them fight and said, "Boys, it's better to share and be kind to each other." So Tim and Sam decided to share the gem and play together. They had fun shooting pretend bad guys and finding more gems. They learned that sharing and being kind made them both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall lawyer named Mr. Brown. He lived in a big house with a big yard. Mr. Brown liked to jump and play outside. He had a big spring in his step. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to his house. She saw Mr. Brown jumping and said, "Wow, you can jump so high! Can you help me find my lost toy?" Mr. Brown smiled and said, "Yes, I will help you, Lucy." They looked and looked for the toy. Then, Mr. Brown saw it on a tree branch. He used his tall legs and big spring to jump high and get the toy. Lucy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Brown!" They both played and jumped together for the rest of the day.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to make her mom happy. She thought, "I need to do something nice for her." Sue had an idea. She would write a pretty poem for her mom. Sue sat down with her paper and pen. She thought about what words to use. She wanted her poem to be about the sun, the moon, and the stars. Sue wrote and wrote until her poem was done. When Sue's mom came home, Sue ran to her with the pretty poem. She read it out loud to her mom. Her mom smiled and hugged Sue. She loved the poem very much. They put the poem on the fridge for everyone to see.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a bad dream one night. He called this bad dream a "nightmare". In the nightmare, a big, scary monster chased him. Tim woke up and felt afraid. The next day, Tim told his mom about the nightmare. His mom said, "Remember, it was just a dream. The monster is not real." Tim felt better, but he was still a little ashamed that he was scared. That night, Tim's mom gave him a big hug before bed. "Don't worry," she said, "I will be here if you have another nightmare." Tim felt safe and went to sleep. He did not have any more bad dreams.

One day, a bright red ball of yarn and a bright blue ball of yarn lived in a big box. They were friends who liked to play together. The red yarn said, "Let's have a race today!" The blue yarn agreed, "Yes! Let's see who is the fastest!" They went to the start of the race. The red yarn and the blue yarn started to roll very fast. They raced down the long hallway. The red yarn rolled fast, but the blue yarn rolled faster. They both laughed and had fun as they raced. At the end of the race, the blue yarn won. The red yarn said, "Good job, blue yarn! You are very fast!" The blue yarn smiled and said, "Thank you, red yarn! That was a fun race!" They both went back to their big box, happy and tired from their bright and exciting race.
