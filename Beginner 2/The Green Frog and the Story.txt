Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Frog and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a green frog. The frog had a shiny coin. He loved his coin very much. One day, a little boy saw the frog and said, "Hi frog! Can I see your coin?" The frog replied, "Yes, but you must be careful with it." The little boy took the coin and played with it. He was not careful. He threw the coin high in the air. The coin fell down and went into a deep hole. The frog was very sad. The little boy tried to get the coin, but the hole was too deep. The frog was angry with the little boy. The little boy said, "I am sorry, frog. I did not mean to lose your coin." The frog replied, "It is too late now. My coin is gone." The boy went home, feeling sad. The bad ending of this story teaches us to be careful with other people's things. If we are not careful, we may lose them, and make others sad.

One sunny day, Tom went to the park. He saw a calm cat sitting on a bench. The cat had a hurt knee. Tom felt sad for the cat and wanted to help. "Hi cat, can I help your knee?" Tom asked. The cat looked at him and said, "Yes, please." Tom gently touched the cat's knee and made it feel better. After that, the cat and Tom became good friends. They played in the park every day. The cat's knee was better, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Bob. He was three years old and already smart. One day, he watched cloud shapes in the sky while eating his purple lunch. He saw a big round shape and asked his mum, "What kind of cloud is that?" Mum said, "That is a cumulus cloud. It says a storm is coming." Bob was very excited. He asked, "What kind of storm?" "Maybe a thunderstorm," Mum said as she hugged him. "But don't worry. I'll stay with you." Later that day, there was a lot of wind and thunder roared in the sky. Bob got scared and remained close to his mum. But then, the sky lit up with purple lightning. Bob was amazed and asked, "What is that?" His mum smiled and said, "That is the storm I told you about. But look. The fog is lifting and the sky is clearing. The storm has passed." Bob looked up at the sky and smiled. He knew he and his mum would remain safe together.

Once upon a time, there was a charming frog. He was so happy living in the pond. But one day, he saw a mushroom. He was excited! He wanted to try it. So he hopped over and quickly slapped it. But to his surprise, the mushroom was not to his liking. So he scowled and hopped away. The next morning, he saw another mushroom. He was still curious, so he hopped over and slapped it. But again, it did not taste very good. He felt kind of sad. Soon, a friendly bird came by and saw the frog. The bird said, “I have a special mushroom just for you. Maybe it will be tastier.” The frog was happy and quickly slapped the mushroom. It was delicious! The frog was so happy that he sang and danced. He also thanked the bird for its kindness. From that day on, the frog was always on the lookout for more tasty mushrooms. It was happy and charming. The end.

Jill was a spoiled girl who always wanted more toys. She did not like to share with her brother Jack or her friends. One night, she saw a big box under her bed. She thought it was a new toy for her. She did not wait for morning. She opened the box and tore the paper. Inside the box, she found a teddy bear. It was brown and soft and had a red bow. Jill liked the teddy bear. She hugged it and said, "You are mine, teddy. I will not let anyone touch you." But then she heard a voice. It was Jack. He came into her room and saw the teddy bear. He said, "Hey, that's my teddy bear! Mom and Dad gave it to me for my birthday. You took it from my closet. Give it back!" Jill did not want to give it back. She said, "No, it's mine. I found it under my bed. Go away!" Jack was angry. He tried to grab the teddy bear from Jill. They pulled and tugged and fought. The teddy bear was in the middle. It could not take it anymore. It ripped in half. The stuffing came out. The red bow fell off. Jill and Jack stopped fighting. They looked at the teddy bear. They felt sad. They had ruined it. They had hurt each other. They had been bad. They heard Mom and Dad coming. They knew they were in trouble. They wished they had been nice. They wished they had shared. They wished they had not torn the teddy bear.
