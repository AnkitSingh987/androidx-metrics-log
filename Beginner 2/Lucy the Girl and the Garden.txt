Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a garden in the back where they grew many things. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to go to the garden and pick some celery for dinner. Lucy went to the garden and saw the celery had grown big and tall. She said, "Wow! The celery has increased so much!" She picked the celery with her small hands and took it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Lucy! This celery is very clear and fresh. It will be perfect for our dinner." At dinner, Lucy and her family enjoyed the clear and crunchy celery. They talked about how their garden made the food taste better. Lucy felt happy that she could help her mom and dad. She knew that working together made their family strong and full of love.

One day, a little red car named Zoom went for a ride. Zoom loved to go fast. He would go up and down the hills, and all around the town. Zoom felt very useful because he could help people get to places quickly. Zoom met a big blue truck named Truckie. Truckie was sad. "What's wrong?" asked Zoom. "I can't go fast like you," said Truckie. "I feel slow and not useful." Zoom wanted to help his new friend feel better. Zoom had an idea. "Let's have a race," he said. "But not a fast race, a slow race. The last one to cross the finish line wins!" Truckie agreed and they started the race. Zoom went very slow, and Truckie went slower. All the people in the town came to watch and they all started to applaud. They were so happy to see Truckie doing well. In the end, Truckie won the slow race. He felt so happy and useful. Zoom and Truckie became the best of friends. They learned that everyone is good at something, and that's what makes them special.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, it rained a lot, and the ground was very wet. Spot put on his coat and went outside to play. Spot jumped in the puddles and got very wet. He was having so much fun! His coat started to soak from all the water. But Spot didn't mind. He liked playing in the rain. After a while, Spot's foot started to hurt. He stepped on a small rock in a puddle. Spot was sad, but he knew he needed to go home. He walked home with his wet coat and hurt foot. Spot's mom helped him feel better. She dried his coat and gave him a hug. Spot was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree in a big park. Many kids would come to play under the tree. One sunny day at noon, a little boy named Tim came to play with his ball. While Tim was playing, he heard a snap sound from the tree. He looked up and saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hello Tim! I need help. My wing is hurt." Tim wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't worry, bird. I will help you." Tim found a big stick and put the bird on it. He took the bird to his mom. His mom was a kind lady who knew how to fix the bird's wing. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and mom. Now I can fly again!" Tim and his mom smiled, happy to help their new friend. From that day on, Tim and the bird always played together under the huge tree at noon.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his unique bone. One day, Spot went to the park with his bone. He dug a hole and put the bone in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. Later, Spot wanted to play with his bone again. He went to the hole and dug it up. But guess what? The bone was not there! Spot was sad and didn't know where his bone went. Just then, a bird flew down and dropped a bone right in front of Spot. Spot looked at the bone and saw that it was his unique bone! The bird had taken it by mistake. Spot was happy and wagged his tail. From that day on, Spot and the bird were the best of friends.
