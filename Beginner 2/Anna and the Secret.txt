Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Secret

Anna loved her velvet doll. It was soft and red and had a pretty dress. She got it from her grandma for her birthday. It was her favorite toy. She named it Rose. One night, Anna had a bad dream. She saw a big, scary monster that wanted to take her velvet doll. She woke up and cried. She hugged her doll and whispered, "Don't worry, Rose. I will protect you." She heard a voice from her doll. It said, "Thank you, Anna. You are my best friend. You are very brave and kind. I have a secret to tell you. I am a magic doll. I can rise and fly when you need me." Anna was surprised and happy. She said, "Really? Can you show me?" The doll said, "Yes, but only when it is dark and quiet. Hold my hand and close your eyes. We will go on a unique adventure." Anna did as the doll said. She felt the doll rise and lift her up. She opened her eyes and saw the stars and the moon. They flew over the trees and the houses and the fields. They saw many beautiful things. Anna smiled and said, "This is amazing, Rose. You are the best doll ever."

One sunny day, Tom and Tim went to the park to play. They were very happy. They saw a big ice-cream truck. Tom wanted a red ice-cream and Tim wanted a blue one. They both got their ice-creams and started to eat them. But then, Tom's ice-cream fell on the ground. Tom was sad. He looked at Tim's ice-cream and wanted it. They started to quarrel. Tim did not want to give his ice-cream to Tom. They were not happy anymore. A heavy wind came and blew Tim's ice-cream away too. Now, both ice-creams were gone. Tom and Tim looked at each other and started to laugh. They stopped quarreling and went to play together again. They knew that playing together was more fun than fighting over ice-cream.

One day, a little animal wanted to sail on a big lake. He was very excited. He had a small boat and a big, blue sail. The little animal was a good sailor, but today the wind was not blowing. He felt frustrated. "Wind, please come back!" the little animal said. "I want to sail on the lake today!" He waited and waited, but the wind did not come. The little animal sat in his boat, feeling sad. Then, a big animal came to the lake. He saw the little animal in the boat. "Why are you sad?" the big animal asked. The little animal told him about the wind and how he wanted to sail. "I can help you!" said the big animal. He took a deep breath and blew air onto the sail. The boat started to move. The little animal was so happy. Together, they sailed on the lake all day long.

Once upon a time there was a red thing. Every day the red thing would sigh. One day the red thing wanted to find out why it was always sighing. The red thing asked a very wise owl, "Why do I sigh?" "Well," said the wise owl, "that is a very good question. Let me think about it." The wise owl thought for a very long time, then said, "It's because you are alive. When you are alive, you can feel all sorts of things. Sighing is a way for you to express those feelings." The red thing smiled and said, "Thank you for your wise advice, wise owl." The wise owl smiled too, and said, "You're welcome, my friend". And from that day on, the red thing smiled whenever it sighed.

Jill and Ben love their pet dog, Spot. Spot is big and brown and likes to play. One day, Jill and Ben take Spot to the park. They throw a ball and Spot runs to catch it. "Good boy, Spot!" Jill says. "You are so fast and smart!" But then, a terrible thing happens. A big car comes very close to the park. It makes a loud noise and scares Spot. Spot runs away from the car and the ball. He runs to the street. "Spot, no!" Ben shouts. "Come back!" Jill and Ben run after Spot. They are very scared. They do not want Spot to get hurt by the car. They see Spot on the other side of the street. He looks sad and lost. "Spot, here!" Jill calls. "We are here!" Spot hears Jill and Ben. He sees them and wags his tail. He wants to go to them. But the street is very busy. There are many cars and bikes and people. Spot does not know how to cross the street. "Wait, Spot!" Ben says. "Do not move!" Jill and Ben look for a safe way to cross the street. They see a man with a green vest and a sign. He is a crossing guard. He helps people cross the street. "Excuse me, sir," Jill says. "Can you help us? Our pet dog is over there and we need to get him." The crossing guard sees Spot. He smiles and nods. "Of course, I can help you," he says. "But you have to be careful and listen to me. When I say go, you can cross the street. When I say stop, you have to stop. Do you understand?" Jill and Ben nod. They hold hands and wait for the crossing guard. The crossing guard holds up his sign and stops the cars and bikes and people. He says go. Jill and Ben run to Spot. Spot is very happy to see them. He licks their faces and barks. "Thank you, sir!" Jill and Ben say to the crossing guard. "You are very kind and brave!" The crossing guard smiles and waves. He says you are welcome. He tells them to be careful and close to their pet. He says pets are very special and need love and care. Jill and Ben hug Spot and say they love him. They say they are sorry he was scared. They say they will never let him go. They walk back to the park. They play with Spot and the ball. They have fun and forget the terrible thing. They are happy.
