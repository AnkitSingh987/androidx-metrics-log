One day, a little dog named Max saw a weird bug. The bug had a big nose. Max wanted to be its friend. He said, "Hi, bug! I like your big nose. Do you want to play?" The bug looked sad. It said, "I can't play. I'm stuck in this hole. Can you help me?" Max wanted to rescue the bug. He used his paws to dig and dig. Soon, the hole was big enough for the bug to get out. The bug was very happy. It said, "Thank you, Max! You rescued me. Now we can play together." Max and the bug played all day. They had lots of fun. And Max learned that even weird bugs with big noses can be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pirate named Tom. Tom had a big hat and a big boat. One hot day, Tom saw a big red ball in the water. He wanted to test if the ball was good to play with. Tom said, "Ahoy, friends! Let's test the big red ball!" All the pirates on the boat helped Tom get the ball. They were happy and played with the ball all day. But, the big red ball was not just a ball. It was a big red fish! The big red fish said, "Hello, pirates! I am not a ball. I am a fish!" The pirates were very surprised. They laughed and played with the big red fish. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. He liked to play in the mud and pour water on the sand. One day, while playing outside, Tim saw the mailman coming. "Hi, Mr. Mailman!" said Tim. "Do you have any mail for us?" "Yes, Tim," said the mailman. "Here is a letter for your mom." Tim took the mail and ran inside to give it to his mom. But he was so messy from playing outside, he got mud all over the letter. "Oh no, Tim!" said his mom. "The letter is messy now. Next time, please clean your hands before you touch the mail." From that day on, Tim always made sure to clean his hands before touching the mail. And he still loved to pour water and play in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide city. In the city, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim went for a wander. He walked and walked until he found a big park. In the park, Tim saw a small, green frog. The frog said, "Ribbit! I am a magic frog!" Tim was very surprised. The frog told Tim to make a wish. Tim wished for a big, red ball. The magic frog said, "Ribbit! Your wish will come true!" Tim waited and waited, but nothing happened. Then, Tim looked behind him and saw a big, red ball. He was so happy! But, when he went to grab it, the ball started to bounce away. Tim chased the ball all around the wide city. Finally, Tim caught the ball. He was so tired, but he was very happy. Tim thanked the magic frog and went back home with his big, red ball. And from that day on, Tim and the magic frog became best friends.
