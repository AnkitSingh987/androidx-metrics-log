Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cup

Once there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toy cars. One day, she went to her mom's kitchen. She saw a big cup of coffee on the table. Sue thought it would be fun to add some of her toy cars to the cup. Sue took her red car and dropped it into the cup. It made a loud splash. The coffee spilled all over the table. Sue thought it was funny, so she added more toy cars. The coffee got louder and louder as she added more cars. But then, Sue's mom came into the kitchen. She saw the big mess Sue made. Mom was not happy. She told Sue that she should not play with her coffee. Sue felt bad and had to clean up the mess. She did not get to play with her toy cars again that day.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to walk to the park. She was very impatient because she loved to play on the swings. The sky was very blue and the sun was shining bright. Lily put on her shoes and walked out the door with her mom. On their way to the park, they saw a big dog. The dog was barking loud and it scared Lily. She didn't want to walk past the dog, but her mom told her it was okay. They walked past the dog and Lily felt brave. When they got to the park, Lily played on the swings and had a great time. She was happy that she walked past the dog and didn't let it stop her from having fun. The sky stayed blue and pretty all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a rope. The rope was for kids to play. But, there was a sign. The sign said, "No play. This is mine." A selfish boy put the sign there. He did not want to share. One day, a kind girl saw the rope. She wanted to play. But she saw the sign. She was sad. She wanted to share the rope with everyone. So, she thought of a plan. The kind girl made a new sign. The new sign said, "Let's play together!" She took the selfish sign away. She put the new sign by the tree. All the kids came to play. They were happy. The selfish boy saw this. He learned to share. They all played with the rope and had fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big stadium with his dad. The stadium was very big and had a lot of seats. Tim was very excited to be there. In the stadium, Tim saw a swing. He asked his dad if he could play on the swing. His dad said yes. Tim went to the swing and started swinging back and forth. He felt like he was flying high in the sky. After playing on the swing, Tim and his dad sat down in the big stadium. They watched people play games and had a lot of fun. Tim was happy he got to swing and watch games at the big stadium with his dad.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his toy boat. He loved to sail it in the water. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim was very happy. While playing, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's boat and said, "I want to sail it too!" Tim didn't want to share his boat, so they started to argue. Both kids were sad. Just then, a big wave came. It looked dangerous and was a threat to the boat. Tim and Sue stopped fighting and worked together to save the boat. They pulled it out of the water just in time. They learned that sharing and teamwork are better than fighting.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to take walks with her dog, Max. One day, they walked to the park and saw a beautiful flower garden. The flowers had many colors, like red, yellow, and blue. Lily and Max liked to look at the flowers. "Max, let's take a flower home," said Lily. Max barked, but Lily didn't know that Max was trying to tell her not to take the flowers. So, Lily took a big red flower from the garden. She was happy, but Max was not. When they got home, Lily's mom saw the flower and was sad. "Lily, you should not take flowers from the park," she said. "Now the garden is not as beautiful as before." Lily felt bad and started to cry. She learned that it is not good to take things that are not yours. The moral of the story is: always ask before you take something that does not belong to you.
