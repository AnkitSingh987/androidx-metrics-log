Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Coat

One day, a little girl named Sue saw a bright coat in a shop. She really liked it. Sue said, "I want that coat!" So, she asked her mom if she could get the coat. Her mom said, "Not today, maybe another day." The next day, Sue went back to the shop with her mom. The bright coat was still there. Sue was very happy. She said, "I want that coat now, please!" Her mom said, "Okay, we can get it today." Sue put on the coat and it was very warm. Sue wore her new bright coat to the park. She was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took her coat away. Sue was very sad. She looked for her coat, but it was gone. The wind had taken it far away, and Sue could not find it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was playing in his old, big house. Tom liked to play with his toy train. The train was very fast, and it made steam when it moved. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play with him. Sam saw the train and said, "Can I borrow your train, Tom?" Tom said yes, because he was a good friend. They played with the train together, and watched the steam go up, up, up. After a while, it was time for Sam to go home. Tom let Sam borrow the train for one more day. Sam was very happy. The next day, Sam brought the train back, and they played together again. They had so much fun with the old steam train, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy who loved to play outside. One day, she saw a small spike in the ground. She went closer to examine it. As she looked at it, she heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" Amy was surprised and looked around. She saw a little bug next to the spike. The bug said, "I'm Bob, and this spike is my home. It's very comfortable." Amy smiled and said, "Hi, Bob! Nice to meet you!" Suddenly, the spike started to grow bigger and bigger. Amy and Bob were amazed. The spike turned into a big, soft chair. Bob said, "Wow! Now my home is even more comfortable!" Amy laughed and they both sat on the chair, becoming good friends.

Lily liked to pray every night before she went to sleep. She would kneel by her bed and talk to God. She would thank him for her family, her friends, and her toys. She would also ask him to help her be good and kind. One night, she heard a loud noise outside her window. She got scared and ran to her mom and dad's room. She saw them sleeping in their big bed. She shook her mom and said, "Mom, mom, there is a noisy thing outside. What is it?" Her mom woke up and hugged her. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's just the wind. It's blowing the leaves and the branches. It can't hurt you. Come, let's go back to your room and pray together." They walked back to Lily's room and knelt by her bed. They prayed to God and asked him to keep them safe and calm. They also thanked him for the wind, because it made the air fresh and clean. Then, her mom tucked her in and gave her a kiss. She said, "Good night, Lily. I love you. Do you want a prune for a snack? They are sweet and juicy." Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please. I love prunes. Thank you, mom. I love you too. Good night." Her mom gave her a prune and turned off the light. Lily ate the prune and felt happy. She closed her eyes and fell asleep. She dreamed of flying with the wind and talking to God.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim who liked to play hide and seek. One day, Tim and his friends were playing in the park. Tim found a hidden spot behind a big tree. He thought no one could find him there. As Tim was hiding, he saw something on the ground. He recognized it as ash from a fire. Tim was curious and called his friend, Sam. "Hey Sam, come look at this," said Tim. Sam came over and saw the ash too. They both wondered where it came from. The next day, Tim and Sam went back to the park. They saw a man making a fire to cook food. They realized that the ash they found was from the man's fire. Tim and Sam smiled at each other, happy to have solved the mystery.
