Category: Beginner
Title: Ana and the Bowl

Ana was playing hide and seek with her friends. She went to hide in a big bush. Suddenly, she saw something dangerous! It was a big bowl that was about to drop. She was scared and ran away. She ran very fast, but the bowl dropped right behind her. Ana was very scared and crying. It was a scary sound. Ana saw the bowl. There was a huge crack in the bottom. She felt very sad. She was afraid of the bowl now, so she ran away as quickly as she could. The bowl was very dangerous. Nobody wanted to play near it anymore. Ana was sad and scared. She had a bad day for sure.

One day, a big orange cat named Tim found a box of gear. The gear was for a party. Tim wanted to have a party too. He thought, "I will invite my friends to play." Tim went to his friend, a small blue bird named Sue. He said, "Sue, I have a box of gear. I want to have a party. Will you come and play?" Sue was happy. She said, "Yes, Tim! I will come and play." On the day of the party, Tim and Sue put on the gear. They had hats, and fun toys to play with. All their friends came to play too. They had a big, fun party. Everyone was happy, and they all played together.

---

One day, a big, reliable dog named Max and a little boy named Tim went to the park. They liked to play together. Max would catch the ball and bring it back to Tim. They played for a long time and got very hungry. "Max, do you want some food?" asked Tim. Max wagged his tail and barked. Tim gave Max a bone and Tim ate a sandwich. But Max's stomach did not feel good after eating the bone. He did not want to play anymore. Max refused to play with the ball. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. Then, a nice lady came by and saw Max. She said, "I think your dog needs some water." Tim gave Max some water, and Max felt better. They played together again, and it was a happy day.

---

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big bench under a tree. They wanted to sit on it and rest. But when they got to the bench, they saw a mean boy sitting on it. He had a stick and he was poking a small bird that was on the ground. The bird was hurt and scared. It could not fly away. "Stop it! Stop it!" Lily shouted at the mean boy. "You are bad! Leave the bird alone!" The mean boy looked at Lily and Max. He laughed and said, "No, I won't. This is my bench and my bird. Go away or I will poke you too!" Max was afraid of the mean boy and his stick. He wanted to run away. But he saw Lily was angry and brave. She wanted to help the bird. He wanted to help Lily. He said to Lily, "We can protect the bird. We can make the mean boy go away. We can be strong together." Lily nodded and smiled at Max. They held hands and walked to the bench. They said to the mean boy, "You are not nice. You are not fair. You are not welcome here. Go away and leave the bird alone!" The mean boy was surprised and scared. He saw Lily and Max were not afraid of him. He saw they were friends and they cared for the bird. He felt ashamed of himself. He dropped his stick and ran away. Lily and Max picked up the bird gently. They saw it had a broken wing. They took it to the park ranger. He said he would take care of the bird and make it better. He thanked Lily and Max for being kind and brave. Lily and Max were happy. They had protected the bird. They had made a friend. They had done a good thing. They hugged each other and went back to play.
