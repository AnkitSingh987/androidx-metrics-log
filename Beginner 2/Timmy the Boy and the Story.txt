Category: Beginner
Title: Timmy the Boy and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timmy. Timmy loved to play outside in the mud. One day, he got very filthy. His mom saw him and said, "Oh no, Timmy! You need to change your clothes. They are filthy, and I need to do laundry." Timmy frowned and said, "But Mom, I like my clothes like this." His mom replied, "No, Timmy. Clean clothes are better. Go change now, please." Timmy went to his room and changed his clothes. He put on a clean shirt and pants. Later that day, Timmy went to play with his friend, Sally. Sally saw Timmy's clean clothes and said, "Wow, Timmy! You look nice today." Timmy smiled and said, "Thank you, Sally. My mom made me change my clothes." They played together and had lots of fun. The next day, Timmy remembered what his mom said about clean clothes. He decided to stay clean and not play in the mud. When he saw Sally, she was covered in mud. Sally said, "Timmy, come play in the mud with me!" But Timmy said, "No, thank you. I want to stay clean today." Sally frowned and said, "But mud is fun!" Timmy replied, "Yes, but clean clothes are better. Let's play something else." They played a different game and had even more fun. The moral of the story is that sometimes staying clean can be just as fun as getting dirty.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a match. He did not know what it was, so he showed it to his friend, Sue. Sue said, "That is a match. We can use it to make a small fire, but we must be safe." Tim and Sue went to a safe place to use the match. They found a spot far away from their homes and trees. Tim tried to light the match, but it did not work. They tried and tried, but the match would not light. They were sad and confused. Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird said, "Do not be sad. I can help you." The bird took the match in its beak and flew up high. The bird blew on the match, and it started to glow. The bird flew close to the sun, and the match caught fire! The bird brought the match back to Tim and Sue, and they were happy. They thanked the bird and promised to be safe with the fire.

Lily was playing with her doll in her room. She liked to pretend that her doll was a princess and she was her friend. She made a castle for her doll with pillows and blankets. She used a curtain to make a door for the castle. But then she heard a loud noise from outside. She looked out the window and saw a big truck. The truck was making a lot of smoke and noise. It scared Lily. She wanted to hide in her castle with her doll. She ran to her castle and pulled the curtain behind her. She hugged her doll and closed her eyes. She hoped the truck would go away soon. She did not like the truck. It made her feel hurt in her chest. She remained in her castle for a long time. She did not hear anything else. She wondered if the truck was gone. She opened her eyes and peeked out of the curtain. She saw her mom standing in the doorway. She smiled at Lily and said, "It's okay, sweetie. The truck was just passing by. It's gone now. You can come out of your castle." Lily felt better. She put down her doll and ran to her mom. She gave her a big hug and said, "I love you, mom. Thank you for making me feel safe." Her mom hugged her back and said, "I love you too, Lily. You are very brave. Do you want to show me your castle and your doll?" Lily nodded and took her mom's hand. She led her to her castle and showed her her doll. She was happy again. She forgot about the truck. She only remembered her mom, her doll, and her castle.
