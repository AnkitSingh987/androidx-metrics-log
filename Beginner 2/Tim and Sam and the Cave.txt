Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and Sam and the Cave

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the woods near their homes. One day, they saw a big hole in the ground. It was a cave. "Let's go inside and see what's there," said Tim. "I don't know, Tim. It looks dark and scary," said Sam. "Come on, Sam. Don't be afraid. We are brave. We have a flashlight and a rope. We can show everyone what we find," said Tim. Sam did not want to lose his friend. He followed Tim into the cave. They walked for a long time. They saw bats and spiders and rocks. They heard strange noises. "Tim, I want to go back. I don't like it here," said Sam. "OK, OK. Let's go back. But first, look at this. It's a treasure chest. Maybe it has gold and jewels inside," said Tim. He opened the chest. But instead of gold and jewels, he saw a big snake. It hissed and lunged at him. "Help, help!" shouted Tim. Sam grabbed the rope and pulled Tim away from the snake. They ran out of the cave as fast as they could. They were safe. They hugged each other. "Tim, you were brave to open the chest. But you were also silly. You should not touch things you don't know," said Sam. "Sam, you were brave to save me. But you were also smart. You were right. The cave was dark and scary. I'm sorry I made you go inside," said Tim. They learned their lesson. They never went back to the cave. They played in the woods, but they stayed away from big holes in the ground. They were still best friends.

One Sunday, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore his tight blue shoes that he liked very much. Tim saw a big slide and wanted to play on it. He climbed up and slid down fast. It was so much fun! Tim saw a small girl named Lily standing near the slide. She looked sad and scared. Tim wanted to help her feel better. He walked over to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Why are you sad?" Lily said, "I'm scared to go down the slide. It looks too big." Tim wanted to comfort Lily. He told her, "Don't be scared. I will go down the slide with you. It's fun!" Lily smiled and they both climbed up. Tim held Lily's hand as they slid down together. Lily was not scared anymore. She said, "Thank you, Tim! That was fun!" They played on the slide all day, and Tim and Lily became good friends. They went to the park every Sunday to play together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tim and Jack. They had been best friends since they were babies and were always happy. One day Tim woke up feeling scared and sad. He told Jack he didn't know why. Jack thought and then said, "Let's go explore and make some new friends! That will make you feel better." So, off they went in search of new friends. Everywhere they went, people smiled at them and said hello. Everywhere they went, the friendliness made Tim feel better and better. The more unknown people he met, the more his fear disappeared and his smiles increased. By the end of the day, Tim was feeling happy again. He and Jack knew that kindness and friendship were the best medicine for times when you don't know why you're feeling down. Moral: It is important to make new friends, as the kindness and friendship can help us feel better in unknown situations.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her family. Lily wanted to have more nice things like her friends. She thought that luxury would make her happy. One day, Lily found a deep hole in the ground. She thought it might have nice things inside. She jumped in to look for them. But she got stuck in the hole! She was scared and cried for help. A kind frog heard Lily's cries and came to help her. The frog gave her a big kiss on the cheek. Suddenly, the hole became smaller and Lily could get out. She was so happy and thanked the frog. Lily learned that having nice things is not what makes you happy. It is the love and help from others that makes life better.
