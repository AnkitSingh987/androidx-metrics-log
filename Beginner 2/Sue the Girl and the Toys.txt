Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sue was eager to play with her toys. She had a toy box full of fun things. She wanted to add a new toy to her collection. Sue went to the store with her mom. She saw a toy she liked. It was a toy doctor kit with a bandage inside. Sue asked her mom, "Can I have this toy, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Sue was so happy! Later, Sue was playing with her new toy doctor kit. Her little brother, Tim, wanted to play too. But Sue did not want to share her new toy. Tim was sad and cried. Sue's mom heard the cry and came to see what was wrong. She told Sue to share her toy with Tim. Sue thought about it and realized that sharing was a good thing. She let Tim play with the toy doctor kit, and they both had fun. In the end, they used the bandage to fix their toy bear's boo-boo. They were both happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to hop all day long. One day, while he was hopping, he found a letter on the ground. The letter was cold and wet from the rain. Tim picked up the letter and started to hop back to his home. On his way, he met his friend, the rabbit. The rabbit was also cold and wet from the rain. Tim told the rabbit about the letter he found. Together, Tim and the rabbit went to Tim's home. They warmed up the cold letter near the fire. Then, they opened the letter and read it. It was an invitation to a big party! Tim and the rabbit were so happy. They hopped all the way to the party and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally liked to bake. One day, she decided to make a big cake. She put on her apron with a zip and got to work. She was very serious about making her cake the best. Sally mixed all the things she needed in a big bowl. She put the cake in the oven to bake. While she waited, she played with her toys. She had a lot of fun, but she was still serious about her cake. When the cake was ready, Sally took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! She put some sweet frosting on top. Sally was very happy with her cake. She shared it with her family and friends, and they all said it was the best cake they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep. The sheep had soft wool. She liked to play in the grass. One day, she went for a walk. She wanted to meet new friends. As she walked, she saw a big, angry dog. The dog barked loud. The sheep was scared. She thought the dog would be mean. But then, something unexpected happened. The angry dog stopped barking. He looked at the sheep's soft wool. He touched it with his nose. The dog was not angry anymore. He liked the soft wool. The sheep and the dog became friends. They played in the grass and were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big blue gate. The gate was closed. Tim wanted to go through the gate to see what was on the other side. Tim said, "Hello, gate. Can you please open for me?" The gate opened, and Tim said, "Thank you, gate!" He walked through the gate and saw a big green tree and a small red ball. Tim played with the ball under the tree. He had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said to the gate, "Please open again." The gate opened, and Tim said, "Thank you, gate!" Tim went home and told his mom about the blue gate and the fun he had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty scarf. She loved her scarf so much. She wore it every day. The scarf was long and soft, and it kept her warm. One day, Sue was playing with her friend Tom. They were playing a game of catch. Sue would pass the ball to Tom, and Tom would pass the ball back to Sue. They were having lots of fun. As they played, a big wind came. Sue's scarf blew off her neck. It landed on a dead tree branch. Sue was sad. She could not get her scarf back. Tom saw Sue's sad face and said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will help you get your scarf back." Tom found a long stick and used it to reach the scarf. He passed the stick to Sue, who then pulled her scarf down from the tree. Sue was so happy to have her scarf back. She thanked Tom and gave him a big hug. They continued to play catch, and Sue made sure to hold onto her scarf tightly so it would not blow away again.
