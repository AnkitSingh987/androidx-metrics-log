Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. Lily had a pair of big sunglasses that she loved to wear. She wore them every day, even when it was not sunny outside. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a boy named Tim playing with a ball. Lily wanted to play too, but she was too shy to ask. Tim saw Lily and said, "Hi, I like your sunglasses! Do you want to play with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" They played together all day, and Lily was not shy anymore. At the end of the day, Lily gave Tim a big hug and said, "You are my new friend!" Tim hugged her back and said, "You are my friend too, Lily!" And from that day on, Lily and Tim were best friends.

---

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were spies on a mission. Lily had a pink hat and a pink toy phone. Max had a black cap and a black toy gun. They ran and hid behind the trees and the slides. "Max, I see the bad guy!" Lily whispered into her phone. "He is wearing a red shirt and a blue hat. He has a big weapon in his hand. It looks like a knife!" "OK, Lily, I see him too. We have to stop him before he hurts anyone. Follow me!" Max said into his gun. He crawled towards the bad guy, holding his gun in front of him. Lily followed Max, but she was scared. She did not like the bad guy's weapon. It looked sharp and shiny. She wished they had a real phone to call for help. She wished they were not spies, but just kids. Suddenly, the bad guy turned around and saw them. He smiled and waved his knife. "Hello, little spies. Do you want to play with me?" he said in a mean voice. Max and Lily paused. They did not know what to do. They looked at each other and then at the bad guy. They felt a cold shiver in their backs. "Run!" Max shouted. He grabbed Lily's hand and pulled her away. They ran as fast as they could, leaving their toys behind. They did not stop until they reached the playground gate, where their mom was waiting for them. "Mom, mom, there was a bad guy in the park! He had a weapon and he wanted to hurt us!" Lily cried, hugging her mom. "Max, Lily, what are you talking about? Where are your toys?" mom asked, looking worried. "We left them behind, mom. We had to run. The bad guy was scary!" Max said, panting. Mom looked at the park and saw a man in a red shirt and a blue hat. He was holding a knife and a pink hat and a black gun. He was laughing and waving them in the air. "Oh, no, that's not a bad guy. That's Mr. Jones, the clown. He was doing a show for the kids. He was using fake weapons to make jokes. He was not going to hurt anyone. He was just playing!" mom explained. Max and Lily looked at the man again. They saw that he was wearing a big red nose and big shoes. They saw that the kids were clapping and laughing. They saw that the knife and the gun were made of plastic. They felt silly and confused. They did not know that the bad guy was a clown. They did not know that the weapons were fake. They did not know that it was a show. They looked at each other and then at their mom. They smiled and shrugged. "Oops!" they said.

Once upon a time there was a furry bear who lived in the woods. He was the most friendly bear anyone had ever seen. One day, the bear went out of his cave and saw a tray on the ground. He picked up the tray and brought it back to his cave. The bear was about to eat the food from the tray when a little bird came by. The bird asked the bear if he could have some of the food. The bear was so kind and generous, he shared the food on the tray with the bird and they both enjoyed the meal. After the meal, the bird thanked the bear and said it was a very nice thing of the bear to share the food with him. The bear was so happy that he started to cry. He said to the bird, "you have been so kind to me, can you forgive me for not trusting you before?" The bird said, "Of course I forgive you. We are friends now." The bear was so relieved and happy to have a new friend. He wiped away his tears and smiled. They both went back in the forest, happy to have each other as friends. The end!
