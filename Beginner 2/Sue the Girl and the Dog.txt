Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a new home. She liked her new home a lot. One day, Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree and a little dog. The little dog was sad. Sue wanted to help the little dog. She waved to the dog and said, "Hi, little dog! Do you want to come to my home?" The little dog wagged its tail and followed Sue. They walked and walked until they reached Sue's home. Sue and the little dog played all day. They had so much fun! But then, the little dog's owner came. He was looking for his lost dog. Sue was sad to say goodbye, but she knew the right thing to do was to give the dog back. So, she waved goodbye to the little dog and its owner. The moral of the story is to always do the right thing, even if it makes you sad.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their furry cat, Max. They liked to pet him, feed him, and chase him around the house. One day, they found some scissors and paper in their mom's room. They wanted to make something fun with them. "Let's cut out some shapes and glue them on the paper," Ben said. "OK, I want to cut a star and a heart," Lily said. They started to cut the paper with the scissors. They were careful not to cut themselves or the cat. They made some nice shapes and glued them on the paper. They were happy with their work. "Look, we are painters!" Ben said. "Yes, we are!" Lily said. They showed their paper to Max. He looked at it with his big eyes. He liked the shapes and the colors. He wanted to play with them too. He jumped on the paper and tried to grab the shapes with his paws. He tore some of the paper and made a mess. "Max, no!" Ben and Lily shouted. They tried to stop him, but he was too fast and furry. He ran away with a piece of paper in his mouth. He hid under the bed and chewed on it. "Bad cat!" Ben and Lily said. They were sad and angry. They had worked hard on their paper and Max had ruined it. They went to their mom and told her what happened. She hugged them and said: "I'm sorry, sweeties. Max didn't know that he was hurting your paper. He just wanted to have fun with you. He is a curious cat. Maybe next time you can play with something else that he can't cut or tear. How about some crayons and a coloring book?" Ben and Lily nodded. They liked crayons and coloring books. They decided to forgive Max and play with him later. They went to get their crayons and coloring book from their room. They saw Max peeking out from under the bed. He looked sorry and cute. He meowed softly. "Come on, Max. We still love you," Ben said. They called him and he came out. He rubbed his head against their legs. He was happy and purring. They petted him and gave him a treat. They took him with them to their coloring book. They hoped he wouldn't eat the crayons.

Once there was a girl who had a very special box. It was made out of wood and had a beautiful picture on it. Every day, the girl would take out the box and store her treasures inside it. She kept all kinds of things inside it - tiny toys, feathers and shells. One day, the girl was playing with her box and something happened. The box fell to the ground and it was broken. The girl was so sad and began to cry. Her parents saw what happened and told the girl not to worry. They said they would buy her a new box and she could store her treasures inside it. After a few weeks, the girl's parents returned with a new box and the girl was very happy. She was able to store all her treasures inside it and use it to play with every day.
