Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Troubled Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a troubled boy named Tim. He had a toy car that could reverse. Tim loved to play with his toy car every day at noon when the sun was high in the sky. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Tim's toy car and said, "Wow! Your car can reverse? That's so cool!" Tim smiled and nodded. He liked that Sue liked his car. They played together every day at noon. But one day, while playing, Tim's toy car broke. He felt very sad. Sue tried to help, but the car would not reverse anymore. Tim cried and said, "My car is broken. I can't play with it anymore." Sue felt sad too, and they couldn't fix the toy car. From that day on, their noon playtime wasn't the same without the reversing car.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to organize her toys and play with her friends. One day, she found a big black rock in her yard. She didn't know what it was, so she asked her dad. "Dad, what is this big black rock?" Lily asked. Her dad looked at the rock and said, "That is coal, Lily. We use it to make a warm fire in the fireplace." Lily thought that was very interesting. She decided to gather more coal with her friends, so they could all have a warm fire. They went around the town and found more coal. They put all the coal in a big pile and organized it by size. Lily and her friends were very happy with their work. They took the coal to their homes and made warm fires. Everyone in the town was warm and cozy, thanks to the cheerful girl who loved to organize.

Tom and Mia love to play with their toy sail. It is red and white and has a big flag. They like to make it go on the water in the bath or in the pool. Sometimes they pretend it is a pirate ship or a fairy boat. One day, they take their sail to the park. They find a big pond with ducks and fish. They are very happy and want to play with their sail on the pond. They put it on the water and watch it go. It looks so pretty and fun. But then, a big dog comes. It sees the sail and wants to play too. It runs to the pond and jumps in. It splashes the water and tries to catch the sail. Tom and Mia are scared. They do not want the dog to hurt their sail. They love their sail very much. They run to the pond and shout at the dog. "No, no, bad dog! Leave our sail alone! It is ours, not yours!" They try to protect their sail from the dog. They wave their arms and throw sticks and stones. The dog is confused and scared. It does not understand why they are angry. It just wants to play. But then, a man comes. He is the dog's owner. He sees what is happening and calls the dog. "Rover, come here, boy! Stop that, you silly dog! You are scaring the children and their sail!" He grabs the dog and puts a leash on it. He says sorry to Tom and Mia. "I am so sorry, kids. He did not mean to be bad. He is just playful and curious. He likes toys and water. He thought your sail was a toy for him. He did not know it was yours." Tom and Mia are still upset, but they see that the man and the dog are sorry. They forgive them and say it is okay. They check their sail and see that it is not broken. It is still red and white and has a big flag. It is still adorable. They smile and hug their sail. They are happy that they protected their sail from the dog. They thank the man and the dog and say goodbye. They go back to playing with their sail on the pond. They have fun and forget about the dog. They love their sail very much.
