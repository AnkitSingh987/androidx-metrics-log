Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy dinosaur. One day, Tim and his dinosaur went to the park to play. The park was big and had lots of trees. At the park, Tim saw an icy pond. He wanted to play near the icy pond. Tim threw his dinosaur up in the air and tried to catch it. But the dinosaur fell on the icy pond. Tim was sad and said, "Oh no, my dinosaur!" A nice girl named Lily saw Tim and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you catch your dinosaur." Lily found a long stick and used it to reach the dinosaur on the icy pond. She pulled the dinosaur back to Tim. Tim was so happy to have his dinosaur back. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" They both played together with the dinosaur and became good friends. And from that day on, Tim and Lily always played together at the park.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a show. At the show, people perform tricks. Tim was very happy to see the tricks. He had a new toy car in his pocket. It was red and shiny. He wanted to show it to his friend Sam. At the show, Tim saw Sam. Sam had a toy car too. Tim said, "Look, Sam! I have a new car!" Sam said, "I have a new car too!" They looked at their cars. They were the same! They had a match. They were very happy. Then, a dog came. The dog took Tim's car. Tim was sad. But Sam said, "It's okay, Tim. We can share my car." They played with Sam's car. They were happy again. The dog came back. It had a new car. It was not Tim's car. The dog wanted to play too. Now, they all played with the new cars together.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with cars and trucks. They have many cars and trucks of different colors and sizes. Tom likes big trucks that make loud noises. Lily likes small cars that go fast. One day, they find a new toy in their room. It is a bus. It is yellow and long. It has many windows and seats. It has a driver. The driver is a doll. He has a hat and a uniform. He can hold a wheel and make the bus move. "Wow, look at this bus!" Tom says. He picks up the bus and shakes it. He makes a honk sound. "This is my bus. I am the driver." "No, I want to play with the bus too!" Lily says. She tries to grab the bus from Tom. She wants to hold the driver. She thinks he is cute. "Stop it, Lily! You have many cars. Let me play with the bus!" Tom says. He pulls the bus away from Lily. He does not want to share. They start to fight over the bus. They tug and push and shout. The bus falls to the floor. The driver falls out of the bus. He lands on his head. He does not move. "Oh no, the driver is hurt!" Lily says. She runs to the driver and picks him up. She holds him gently. She feels sorry for him. "Sorry, Tom. I did not mean to break the bus." Lily says. She looks at Tom. She sees that he is sad. "Sorry, Lily. I did not mean to be mean." Tom says. He looks at Lily. He sees that she is kind. They hug and say sorry. They decide to share the bus and the driver. They take turns to hold the driver and make the bus move. They have fun together. They learn that different toys can be fun for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little island in the middle of the sea. Many animals lived on the island. They liked to play and laugh all day long. One day, a big bird came to the island. The bird was very alert and always watched the animals play. The big bird told the animals that a big storm was coming. The animals listened to the bird and started to get ready. They knew they needed to be alert too. The bird helped them find a safe place to hide from the storm. The storm came and the wind blew hard. The rain fell and the sea got big waves. The animals were safe because they had listened to the big bird. After the storm, the sun came out again. The animals were happy and they laughed. They thanked the big bird for helping them. From that day on, the animals and the big bird played and laughed together on the island.
