Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom liked to take naps all day. One day, Tom was playing with his ball when he saw the sun was up high. "Oh no!" he said, "It's noon already! I need to find my bed for my nap." Tom looked everywhere for his bed, but he could not find it. He started to panic. "Where is my bed?" he cried. He asked his friend, Dog, "Have you seen my bed?" Dog shook his head and said, "No, Tom, I have not seen your bed." Tom felt very sad and tired. He looked and looked, but he could not find his bed. Then, he saw his friend, Bird. Bird said, "Tom, I saw your bed near the big tree!" Tom was so happy. He ran to the big tree and found his bed. He jumped into his bed and took a long nap. Tom was not sleepy anymore and he was ready to play again.

Once there was a chubby girl who loved winter. Every day she would go outside and slide. She had a big, shiny sled and she liked to slide down the snowy hill. She would laugh as she rode down and wave her arms in the air. Her chubby cheeks would glow from the cold air. She felt so happy riding down the hill. Whenever the girl would reach the bottom of the hill, she would turn around and do it again. She loved sliding and would do it all day long! When the snow melted, the girl was very sad. She knew she wouldn't be able to slide again until next winter. But then she started counting down the days until winter came again so she could slide with her chubby cheeks in the cold air.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree. One hot day, Tim was very hungry. He saw some food on the ground. But the food was not his. Tim flew down to the ground. He looked at the food. He wanted to eat it. But he knew it was wrong to steal. Tim said to himself, "I should not steal this food." Just then, a kind old lady walked by. She saw Tim looking at the food. She said, "Hello, little bird. Are you hungry?" Tim nodded his head. The old lady picked up the food and gave it to Tim. "Here you go," she said. "You can have this." Tim was very happy. He ate the food and felt better. He knew that it was good not to steal. And he made a new friend in the kind old lady. From that day on, they shared food and played together in the hot sun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a hidden tree with a big trunk. A little girl named Lily lived near the forest. One day, she went for a walk and found the hidden tree. Lily saw a long rope on the ground. She picked it up and decided to tie it around the big trunk. She wanted to make a swing to play on. Lily tied the rope very tight and made a strong swing. Lily played on her swing every day. She loved her swing on the hidden tree with the big trunk. It was her special place, and it made her very happy. And from that day on, Lily and the hidden tree became the best of friends.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they found a stream near their house. The stream had clear water and many stones and fish. Tom and Lily wanted to play in the stream. "Let's make a bridge with the stones," Tom said. "We can cross the stream and see what is on the other side." "OK," Lily said. "But be careful not to fall in the water. It is cold and deep." They started to pile up the stones in the middle of the stream. They worked hard and had fun. They made a bridge that was big enough for them to walk on. "Look, we did it!" Tom said. "Let's go over the bridge and explore." They walked on the bridge, holding hands. But when they reached the middle, they saw something that made them scared. It was an enormous frog that was sitting on the last stone. The frog had big eyes and a wide mouth. It croaked loudly and jumped towards them. "Ribbit! Ribbit! This is my bridge. Go away!" the frog said. Tom and Lily screamed and ran back to the shore. They dropped their hands and fell on the grass. They were wet and dirty and frightened. "Are you OK?" Tom asked Lily. "Yes, I'm OK," Lily said. "But that was a mean frog. He scared us and ruined our bridge." They looked at the stream and saw the frog sitting on the bridge again. He was smiling and croaking. "Ribbit! Ribbit! This is my bridge. I like it. Thank you for making it for me," the frog said. Tom and Lily felt angry and sad. They had worked hard to make the bridge, and they wanted to play on it. But the frog took it from them and did not share. "That is not fair," Tom said. "He should share the bridge with us. We made it together." "Yes, he should," Lily said. "But he is bigger and stronger than us. We can't make him move." They thought for a while. Then they had an idea. "Maybe we can make another bridge," Tom said. "But this time, we can make it with sticks and leaves. The frog won't like it. He will leave it alone." "Good idea," Lily said. "Let's try it." They gathered some sticks and leaves from the ground. They made another bridge with them, next to the stone bridge. The bridge was not as strong or as pretty as the stone bridge, but it was enough for them to cross the stream. They walked on the new bridge, holding hands again. They reached the other side and saw a meadow with flowers and butterflies. They smiled and played in the meadow. They forgot about the frog and the stone bridge. The frog saw them and felt curious. He wondered what was on the other side of the stream. He decided to try the new bridge. He jumped on it, but it was not solid or stable. It broke under his weight and he fell in the water. He splashed and struggled and cried for help. "Help! Help! I can't swim!" the frog said. Tom and Lily heard him and looked back. They saw the frog in the water, and the broken bridge. They felt sorry for him. They remembered that he was a living thing, and that he needed help. "Let's help him," Tom said. "He is in trouble. Maybe he will be nice to us if we help him." "OK," Lily said. "But how can we help him?" They looked around and saw a big leaf floating on the water. They ran to the leaf and pushed it towards the frog. The frog saw the leaf and grabbed it. He climbed on it and floated to the shore. He was wet and cold and ashamed. "Thank you for helping me," the frog said. "I'm sorry for being mean to you. I was selfish and greedy. I should have shared the bridge with you. Can you forgive me?" Tom and Lily looked at each other and nodded. They forgave the frog. They felt happy and proud. They had learned a lesson. "Sharing is caring," Tom said. "We can all play together and have fun." "Yes, sharing is caring," Lily said. "We can be friends and help each other." They invited the frog to join them in the meadow. The frog accepted and smiled. He learned a lesson too. They played in the meadow, with the flowers and the butterflies. They crossed the stream, with the stone bridge and the leaf. They were happy and peaceful. They were friends.
