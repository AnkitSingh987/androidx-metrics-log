Category: Beginner
Title: Vicky the Red Vehicle and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big red vehicle named Vicky. Vicky liked to spread her wheels wide and drive fast. One day, Vicky met a small blue vehicle named Bobby in the park. "Hi, I'm Vicky!" she said. "I like to spread my wheels wide and drive fast. Want to play with me?" Bobby smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together! But my wheels are tight, so I can't spread them like you." Vicky and Bobby played all day, driving around the park. They found out that even though Vicky could spread her wheels wide and Bobby had tight wheels, they could still have lots of fun together. From that day on, they became the best of friends.

Lola and Sam were best friends, and today they were both feeling very glad. Lola looked up at the sky and saw a big, yellow sun shining brightly. She pointed it out to Sam, who smiled and said, "Let's run to the park!". So they did. Lola and Sam ran and ran as fast as they could. Lola laughed and Sam smiled - they felt so happy. Suddenly, they stopped in the middle of the park - right in the yellow grass. Lola and Sam looked around in wonder. Everything was so beautiful - the blue sky, the green trees and the yellow sun. They decided to run around in circles, and when they finished, they were so glad. Lola and Sam hugged and laughed and ran back home, feeling the sunshine on their backs. It was a wonderful day.

One day, a big baby and a little baby were playing outside. The big baby said, "Let's dance!" The little baby smiled and started to dance with the big baby. They danced and danced, laughing and having fun. As they danced, a big ball rolled towards them. The big baby and the little baby stopped dancing and looked at the ball. The ball was shiny and pretty. They had never seen such a big ball before. They decided to play with the ball too. Suddenly, the big ball started to bounce! The big baby and the little baby were surprised. They didn't know the ball could bounce. The big ball bounced higher and higher, and then it started to float away. The big baby and the little baby waved goodbye to the ball and went back to dancing, happy with their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big war between two toy armies. The toy soldiers were fighting in a little boy's room. The boy liked to play with his toys and make fake fights. He had fun watching the toys fight each other. One day, the boy went to sleep and left his toys on the floor. At night, a magic moon rose high in the sky. The moon's light shined through the window and onto the toys. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The toy soldiers came to life and started to move! The toy soldiers were surprised to see each other alive. They decided to stop fighting and become friends. They knew their fights were fake and just for fun. The toy soldiers cleaned up the room and went back to their places before the boy woke up. From that day on, the toy soldiers lived happily together and never fought again.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the living room. Tim has a big jeep that can make noises and move. Sam has a small jeep that is quiet and still. Tim thinks his jeep is better than Sam's jeep. One day, Tim and Sam are playing with their jeeps on the floor. Tim wants to make a race with Sam. He says, "My jeep is faster than your jeep. Let's see who can reach the sofa first." Sam nods and says, "OK, let's go." They put their jeeps on the floor and say, "One, two, three, go!" Tim's jeep zooms ahead of Sam's jeep. It makes loud noises and flashes lights. Sam's jeep follows slowly behind. It is quiet and dim. Tim laughs and says, "Ha ha, I win! My jeep is the best!" Sam feels sad and says, "No fair, your jeep has batteries and mine doesn't." Tim does not care. He picks up his jeep and says, "I'm going to play with my jeep on the table. You can play with your jeep on the floor." He walks to the table and puts his jeep on it. He does not see that the table is messy. There are books, papers, cups, and crayons on the table. Tim pushes his jeep on the table. His jeep bumps into a cup. The cup falls off the table and breaks. The water spills on the floor. Tim's jeep snaps a crayon. The crayon breaks into two pieces. The pieces roll off the table and land on the floor. Tim's jeep knocks over a book. The book falls off the table and hits the floor. The pages fly out of the book. Tim hears a loud noise. He looks at the floor. He sees a big mess. He sees the broken cup, the spilled water, the snapped crayon, and the torn book. He feels scared. He thinks, "Oh no, what have I done?" Sam hears the noise too. He runs to the table and sees the mess. He sees Tim's jeep on the table. He says, "Tim, you made a big mess. You were not careful with your jeep. You should say sorry." Tim does not want to say sorry. He says, "It's not my fault. It's your fault. You gave me a bad jeep. Your jeep is slow and boring. My jeep is fast and fun." Sam feels angry. He says, "No, it's your fault. You were mean and rude. You did not share your jeep. You did not listen to me." Tim and Sam start to argue. They shout at each other. They do not notice that their mom is coming. She hears the noise and sees the mess. She says, "What is going on here? Who made this mess?" Tim and Sam stop arguing. They look at their mom. They feel guilty. They do not know what to say.
