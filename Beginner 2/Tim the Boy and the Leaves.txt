Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a walk with his dog, Max. They went down a wild path with many trees and flowers. Max was very happy and began to scatter leaves all around as he ran. As they walked, they saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was crying. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy on this track and I can't find it." Tim and Max wanted to help Sue. They all looked for the toy together. Max sniffed and scattered more leaves, trying to find the toy. Finally, Max found the toy under a big rock. Sue was so happy and thanked Tim and Max. They all walked back home together, feeling proud of their teamwork.

Lila and Tom were twins who liked to play near the harbor. They liked to watch the big boats and the fish and the birds. But they were fearful of the water, because their mom had told them it was deep and cold and had sharp rocks. One day, they saw a shiny thing on the sand. It looked like a star, but it was hard and pointy. Lila wanted to touch it, but Tom said, "No, Lila, it might be sharp. It might hurt you." Lila did not listen. She ran to the shiny thing and picked it up. But it was not a star, it was a shell. And it was very sharp. It cut Lila's hand and made it bleed. Lila cried out loud and dropped the shell. Tom ran to Lila and hugged her. He saw the blood and was scared. He said, "Lila, Lila, are you okay? We need to go to mom. She will make it better." They ran to their mom, who was sitting on a bench. She saw Lila's hand and said, "Oh, Lila, what happened? Did you touch something sharp?" Lila nodded and said, "Yes, mom, I touched a shell. It looked like a star, but it was not. It was sharp and it hurt me." Mom said, "I'm sorry, Lila, that must have been very painful. But you are brave and strong. Let me clean your wound and put a bandage on it. Then you will feel better." She took out a cloth and some water and cleaned Lila's hand. Then she put a bandage on it and kissed it. She said, "There, now it's better. You are a good girl, Lila. But next time, listen to Tom. He was right. Some things are sharp and dangerous. You have to be careful near the harbor." Lila said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will listen to Tom next time. And I will be careful near the harbor. I love you, mom." Mom said, "I love you too, Lila. And I love you too, Tom. You are a good brother, Tom. You helped Lila and came to me. You are a good boy, Tom." Tom said, "I love you too, mom. And I love you too, Lila. You are a good sister, Lila. But don't touch sharp things again. They are not stars, they are shells." They hugged and smiled. Then they went back to play near the harbor, but they stayed away from the sharp shells. They watched the big boats and the fish and the birds, and they were happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a smelly letter in his garden. He didn't know who it was from. He showed it to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" said Tim. His mom looked at the letter and said, "Let's call your friend, Sam, to see if he knows who it is from." They called Sam and asked, "Sam, do you know who this smelly letter is from?" Sam said, "I don't know, but let's ask our friend, Lily." So, they called Lily and asked her the same question. Lily said, "I think I know who it's from!" Lily, Sam, and Tim met in the garden. They opened the smelly letter together. Inside the letter, there was a map. The map showed them where to find a hidden treasure. They followed the map and found a big, old box. They opened the box and saw many toys and candies inside. They were so happy and decided to share the treasure with all their friends.
