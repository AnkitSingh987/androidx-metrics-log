Category: Beginner
Title: The Secret of the Cat and the Bear

One day, a cat and a dog were playing outside. It was getting dark, and they were getting dirty. The cat said, "Let's go inside and take a shower. We need to get clean." They went inside and turned on the water. The cat and the dog started to mix soap and water to make bubbles. They were having so much fun in the shower. They laughed and played with the bubbles. Suddenly, the lights went out, and it was very dark. The cat and the dog were scared. They heard a noise and saw a big shadow. They thought it was a monster! But then, the lights came back on, and they saw it was just their friend, the bear. He had come to play with them in the shower too. They all laughed and played together, happy that it was not a monster after all.

Once there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They lived in a very organized house and they always had lots of fun. One day they decided to go to the garden. They rushed outside and saw a big radish. Jack and Jill were so excited! They couldn't wait to pick the radish. So they ran to it and started to pull it out. Once they had pulled it out, they rushed back to the house. Jack and Jill wanted to share the radish with their family. They were met with smiles when they arrived back. Everyone was very happy to see the organized radish they had found. They enjoyed dinner that night and ate the radish together. It was the best radish ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim was in his bedroom. The sun began to rise, and the room became bright. Tim was frightened because he saw a big shadow on his wall. He thought it was a big, scary monster. He hid under his bed and said, "Please, don't hurt me, big monster!" His mom heard him and came into the bedroom. She saw the big shadow too. But then, she looked out the window and saw what was making the shadow. She smiled and said, "Tim, it's not a monster. Come out and see what it is." Tim came out from under the bed and looked out the window. He saw a big, friendly dog playing with a ball. The dog was making the big shadow on the wall. Tim laughed and said, "Silly me! It's just a dog, not a monster." Tim and his mom went outside to play with the dog, and they had a fun day together.

One day, a boy named Tim had a big red truck. He loved his truck very much. Tim would play with his truck all day long. He would drive it on the floor, up the walls, and even on the table. One day, Tim was playing with his truck outside. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I like your truck. Can I play with it?" Tim said, "No, it is my own truck." Sue felt sad and walked away. Tim felt embarrassed because he did not want to make Sue sad. Later that day, Tim saw Sue playing with a big blue truck. Tim was surprised! He did not know Sue had her own truck too. Tim went to Sue and said, "I am sorry. Can we play together?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play with our trucks together!" So, Tim and Sue became friends and played with their trucks happily.

One day, a mom and her little girl went to the park. The mom told the girl, "I love you. Stay close to me so you are safe." The girl loved her mom and felt safe when she was near. At the park, the girl saw a big dog. She wanted to play with the dog, but the mom said, "No, it might not be safe." The girl was sad but listened to her mom. Then, something unexpected happened. A nice man came to the park with a small, friendly dog. The girl asked her mom if she could play with this dog. The mom said yes and told the girl, "I love you. Have fun, but be safe." The girl played with the small dog and had lots of fun. But then, the big dog came back. It wanted to play too. The small dog and the big dog started to fight. The girl was scared and ran to her mom. The mom tried to help the small dog, but the big dog was too strong. The small dog got hurt and the nice man was very sad. The girl and her mom left the park, feeling sad and not safe. The mom told the girl, "I love you. We will find a safer place to play next time."
