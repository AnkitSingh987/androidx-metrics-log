Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a mop. It was an incredible mop. Tim liked to take the mop everywhere he went. One day, Tim took his mop to the park. At the park, Tim and his mop played on the swings and the slide. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came to the park. The dog saw Tim's mop and wanted to play, too. The dog took the mop in its mouth and ran away. Tim was sad. He thought he lost his incredible mop. But then, the dog came back with the mop. The dog wanted to be friends. Tim and the dog played with the mop together. They had a lot of fun. In the end, Tim, the dog, and the incredible mop were all best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to go outside. He saw the wide world through his window. He wanted to play with his ball and run in the grass. Tim called his mom, "Mom, can we go out and play?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's put on our shoes and go." Tim was happy and put on his shoes quickly. Mom and Tim went to the door. Mom said, "I will lock the door, so our house is safe." Outside, Tim played with his ball. He kicked it far, and it went wide into the grass. Tim laughed and ran after it. Mom watched him play and smiled. They had a fun day playing outside.

One day, a little girl named Sue got a new toy. She was so happy! She played with her new toy all day. At night, she put the toy in her closet with her other toys. Sue's mom told her, "Sue, don't forget to close the closet door before you sleep." The next day, Sue played with her new toy again. But this time, when it was time to sleep, Sue refused to close the closet door. She said, "Mom, I don't want to close the door. I want to see my new toy when I wake up." Her mom said, "Okay, Sue. But remember to close it tomorrow." That night, Sue had a dream. In her dream, all her toys came out of the closet and played with her. They had so much fun! When Sue woke up, she saw her toys all over her room. She was surprised! Sue remembered her dream and thought maybe it was real. Sue told her mom about her dream and the toys all over her room. Her mom smiled and said, "Sue, maybe it's time to close the closet door at night." Sue agreed and from that day on, she always closed the closet door before she slept. And she still loved her new toy very much.

One day, a kind boy named Tim found a big machine. The machine was old and broken. Tim wanted to make it work again. He tried to fix the machine, but it was very hard. Tim felt sad, but he didn't give up. Tim asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam came and looked at the machine. He said, "This machine is big and old. Can we fix it?" Tim said, "We can try. Let's work together." They worked very hard to fix the machine. It took a long time, but they didn't stop. Finally, the machine started to work again. But it began to tremble and shake. Tim and Sam were scared. They tried to stop the machine, but it was too late. The machine broke again and made a big mess. Tim and Sam were upset. They had worked so hard, but the machine was still broken.

Sam and Mia were friends who liked to play outside. One night, they saw a bright light in the sky. It was a comet, a big rock that flies very fast. "Wow, look at that!" Sam said. "It is so shiny and pretty!" "I wish I could hop on it and see where it goes," Mia said. "Maybe it goes to other planets with funny animals and plants." They ran to the hill and tried to hop as high as they could. They pretended they were astronauts, exploring the space. They made noises like rockets and lasers. "Beep beep, this is Sam. I found a strong alien. He has four arms and two heads. He wants to be my friend," Sam said into his hand. "Beep beep, this is Mia. I found a big flower. It smells like candy and sings a song. It wants to give me a hug," Mia said into her hand. They had so much fun, they did not notice the comet was gone. It had flown away, leaving a trail of sparkles. They looked up and saw the stars. "Bye bye, comet. Thank you for the show," they said together. They held hands and walked back to their homes. They were happy and sleepy. They dreamed of the comet and their space adventures.
