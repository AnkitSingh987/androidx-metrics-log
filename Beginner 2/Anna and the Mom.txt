Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Mom

Anna likes to help her mom with work. She can fold clothes and put them in the drawer. She can also dust the table and water the plants. She thinks work is fun and makes her mom happy. One day, Anna sees her mom sitting on the couch with a big pile of papers. Her mom looks tired and sad. Anna asks, "Mom, what are you doing? Can I help you?" Her mom smiles and says, "I'm doing work for my boss. I have to write a lot of words and numbers. It's hard and boring. You can't help me with this, sweetie. But thank you for asking." Anna feels sorry for her mom. She wants to make her mom happy. She thinks of an idea. She takes some papers from the pile and starts to fold them. She folds them into different shapes, like a hat, a boat, a flower, and a star. She puts them on the table and says, "Look, mom, I made you some gifts. They are for you. I love you." Her mom looks at the paper shapes and laughs. She hugs Anna and says, "You are so clever and kind, Anna. You made me happy. I love you too. Thank you for your gifts. They are beautiful. You are the best helper ever."

One day, a little boy named Timmy went to the park. He wore a brilliant red tie that his mom gave him. Timmy loved to play with his ball. He would kick it very hard and watch it go far. At the park, Timmy met a girl named Sue. Sue saw Timmy's red tie and said, "I like your tie, it is very nice!" Timmy smiled and said, "Thank you! Do you want to play with my ball?" Sue nodded her head and they started to play. They took turns kicking the ball. Timmy kicked it so hard that the ball went into a big tree. The ball got stuck! Timmy and Sue looked at each other. Timmy had an idea. He took off his red tie and threw it at the ball. The tie wrapped around the ball and pulled it out of the tree. Timmy and Sue were so happy that they could play again. They knew that the brilliant red tie was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. He was not strong, he was weak. He lived in a small house. The man wanted to play with the kids, but their mom did not allow it. One day, the weak man with the mustache saw a cat stuck in a tree. He wanted to help the cat, but he was too weak. He tried and tried, but he could not climb the tree. The weak man with the mustache felt very sad. He could not help the cat and could not play with the kids. In the end, the man went back to his small house all alone, feeling very sad.

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the sea. He saw a big shark named Sam. Sam was upset. Tim asked, "Why are you upset, Sam?" Sam said, "I can't stir the water with my tail like other sharks. It makes me sad." Tim wanted to help Sam, so they went to find a friend. They found a big whale named Wally. Wally was good at stirring the water. Wally showed Sam how to stir the water with his tail. Sam tried and tried, but he still couldn't stir the water. Just then, a little crab named Carl came by. Carl said, "I know why you can't stir the water, Sam. You are not a shark, you are a dolphin!" Sam was surprised, but he felt happy. Now he knew why he was different. All the friends swam and played together, and Sam learned to be a great dolphin.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot who lived in a tidy tree house. The parrot loved to jump and play all day long. One day, he saw a cat sitting on a branch near his tree house. The cat looked sad and lonely. "Hi cat! Do you want to jump and play with me?" asked the parrot. The cat looked at the parrot and smiled. "Yes, I would like that," said the cat. So, they played and jumped together, having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to fly! The parrot was very surprised. "Wow! I didn't know cats could fly!" said the parrot. The cat laughed and said, "I am a special cat. Now we can jump and fly together!" And so, the parrot and the flying cat became the best of friends, playing and jumping all day long.
