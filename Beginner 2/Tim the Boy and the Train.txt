Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Train

One day, a little boy named Tim went on an adventurous trip with his mom and dad. They went to see a big steam train. Tim was very excited. He had never seen a steam train before. The train was big, and it made a lot of noise. It went "Choo Choo!" as it moved on the tracks. Tim's dad said, "Look, Tim! The steam comes out of the train when it moves. Isn't that cool?" Tim nodded and smiled. He was so happy to see the train. He wanted to ride on the train and go on an adventure. They got on the train and found their seats. Tim sat by the window, so he could see everything. The train started moving, and Tim could feel the excitement growing inside him. He looked out the window and saw trees, animals, and people as they went by. Tim's mom said, "This is such a fun trip, isn't it, Tim?" Tim agreed and clapped his hands. Tim and his family had a great day on the steam train. They went on an adventure together and saw many new things. Tim couldn't wait to tell his friends about the exciting trip when he went back to school.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a long slide and wanted to play. He climbed up the slide and slid down very fast. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He liked the long slide a lot. Tim saw his friend, Sue, eating yogurt near the slide. She had a big smile on her face. "Hi, Sue!" said Tim. "I like your yogurt. Can I have some?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim! Let's share my yogurt." They sat on the grass and ate the sweet yogurt together. After they ate the yogurt, Tim and Sue played on the long slide again. They took turns sliding down and laughing. They played until the sun went down. Then, they said goodbye and went home. Tim and Sue had a fun day at the park with the long slide and the yummy yogurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree was very proud. It was tall and strong. It lived in a forest with many friends. One day, dark clouds came. A big storm was coming. The storm was very loud. The wind blew hard. The proud tree did not want to bend. It wanted to stay tall and strong. The other trees bent with the wind. They did not want to fall. The storm got worse. The wind blew harder. The proud tree still did not bend. It wanted to show it was the strongest. But then, a big gust of wind came. The proud tree could not stand it anymore. It fell down with a loud crash. The other trees were sad, but they knew the proud tree did not want to bend.

One day, a little bird was flying near a big pine tree. The bird was very tired and wanted to sleep. So, it flew to the pine tree and found a spot to rest. The bird was so tired that it was careless and did not see a big cat sitting near the tree. The big cat saw the bird and said, "Little bird, you look so tired. Why don't you sleep on my back? I promise I won't hurt you." The little bird was so sleepy that it agreed and went to sleep on the cat's back. While the bird was sleeping, something unexpected happened. The big cat started to feel bad about wanting to hurt the little bird. Instead, it decided to protect the bird and let it sleep. When the bird woke up, it was surprised to see the cat still there. The cat told the bird, "I have changed my mind. I want to be your friend." The little bird was very happy and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind man with a big mustache. He lived in a small house with his cat, named Fluffy. The kind man and Fluffy were best friends, and they liked to play and have fun together. One sunny day, the kind man and Fluffy went to the park to play. They saw a little girl who was sad. She was staring at her broken toy. The kind man asked the little girl, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "My toy is broken, and I can't play with it anymore." The kind man wanted to help the little girl. He tried to fix the toy, but it was too hard for him. He felt sad too. Then Fluffy, the cat, had an idea. She found a long stick and brought it to the little girl. The little girl smiled and started to play with the stick. The kind man, Fluffy, and the little girl played together all day long. They became good friends and the little girl was not sad anymore.
