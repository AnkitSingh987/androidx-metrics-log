Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The little dog lived in a shelter. The shelter was big and unknown to him. One day, the little dog wanted to go outside and play. The little dog found a ball. He wanted to get the ball. But, the ball was in a high place. The little dog tried to jump. He struggled to jump high. He tried and tried, but he could not get the ball. A big, kind dog saw the little dog. The big dog wanted to help. The big dog jumped high and got the ball. The little dog was happy. They played with the ball together. The little dog made a new friend in the unknown shelter.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little daisy. The daisy was very kind and compassionate. She loved to help her friends. One day, the daisy saw a small box. She knew that she had to open it. The daisy had a friend, a small bug. The bug was very sad. He lost his toy in the big garden. The daisy wanted to help him. She remembered the small box. She thought that maybe the toy was in the box. The daisy and the bug went to the small box. They tried to open it together. The box opened and the toy was inside! The bug was very happy. The daisy was happy too because she could help her friend. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. He loved to spend time with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Tim found a print on the ground. He showed it to Spot and said, "Look, Spot! What is this?" Spot sniffed the print and wagged his tail. He knew it was from another dog. Tim and Spot spent the day following the prints. They found a lost puppy at the end of the trail. The puppy was scared and alone. Tim said, "Don't worry, little puppy. We'll take you home with us!" They all went back to Tim's house, and the puppy became a part of their family. Spot, Tim, and the new puppy spent every day playing together, and they lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. Today, Anna is going to Ben's house for a play date. She has a big bag of stuff to bring. Anna's mom helps her put on her coat and shoes. She says, "Are you ready to go, Anna?" Anna nods and smiles. She says, "Yes, Mom. I am ready. I want to see Ben and his stuff." They get in the car and drive to Ben's house. Anna looks out the window and sees trees and houses and cars. She says, "Look, Mom. There is a dog. And a bird. And a red ball." Mom says, "Yes, Anna. There are many things to see. We are almost there." They arrive at Ben's house and ring the doorbell. Ben's mom opens the door and says, "Hello, Anna and Mom. Come in, come in. Ben is waiting for you in his room." Anna and Mom go inside and take off their coats and shoes. Anna says, "Thank you, Ben's mom." Ben's mom says, "You're welcome, Anna. Have fun." Anna runs to Ben's room and sees him sitting on the floor with his stuff. He has cars and trains and blocks and animals. He says, "Hi, Anna. I am happy you are here. Do you want to play with me?" Anna says, "Hi, Ben. I am happy too. Yes, I want to play with you. Look, I have stuff too." She opens her bag and shows him her stuff. She has dolls and books and puzzles and stickers. Ben says, "Wow, Anna. You have cool stuff. Can I see them?" Anna says, "Sure, Ben. You can see them. And I can see yours. Let's share and play together." They smile and hug and start to play. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy car that he loved to play with. One day, he went to the park to play with his car. In the park, he saw another boy named Sam. Sam had a fierce-looking toy dinosaur. Tim and Sam started to play together. They had so much fun. But soon, they began to argue about which toy was better. Tim liked his car, but he also wanted the fierce dinosaur. Sam liked his dinosaur, but he also wanted the big car. They could not decide what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked Sam if they could trade toys for a little while. Sam thought it was a good idea, so they traded. Tim played with the fierce dinosaur, and Sam played with the big car. They had even more fun than before. After they were done playing, they traded their toys back and went home happy. They learned that sharing and trading can be fun and make friends happy too.
