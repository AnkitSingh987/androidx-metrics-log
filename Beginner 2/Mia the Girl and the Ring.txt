Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ring

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big, shiny ring. She put it on her finger and went to show her friend Tom. Tom was playing with his toys. Mia said, "Look, I found a ring!" Tom looked at the ring and said, "Wow, it's so pretty!" He wanted to try it on too, but it was stuck on Mia's finger. They tried to pull it off, but it was too tight. Mia's finger started to hurt. She had a little stitch on her finger, and the ring was pushing on it. Mia and Tom went to find Mia's mom for help. She was in the kitchen making lemonade. The lemonade was sour, but it was yummy. Mia's mom saw the ring and the stitch on her finger. She put some soap on Mia's finger, and the ring came off easily. Mia and Tom were happy. They played with the ring and drank the sour lemonade. They promised to be careful with the ring and not to hurt their fingers again.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there lived an ostrich named Ollie. Ollie was very tall and had a long neck. He loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Ollie saw a small bird named Bobby, who was helpless. Bobby was sad because he could not fly. "Hi Bobby," said Ollie. "Why are you sad?" "I miss flying with my friends," said Bobby, "but I can't fly anymore." Ollie wanted to help Bobby. He had an idea. "Climb on my back, Bobby. I will run fast, and it will feel like you are flying." Bobby climbed on Ollie's back, and they started to run. They ran so fast that it felt like they were flying. Bobby was happy, and Ollie was happy to help his new friend. From that day on, they played together every day, and they became best friends.

One day, a little dog named Spot was in the park. He saw a big horn on the ground. Spot picked up the horn with his mouth. He knew the horn could make a loud sound. He wanted to show his friend, Cat. Spot found Cat under a tree. "Look what I found!" said Spot. "I can make a loud sound with this horn." Cat was alert and curious. "Show me," said Cat. Spot blew the horn and it made a very loud sound. They both laughed and played with the horn. Later, they saw a big ball stuck in the tree. They wanted the ball to play with, but they could not reach it. Spot had an idea. He blew the horn as loud as he could. The sound made the ball shake and fall from the tree. Spot and Cat were very happy. They played with the ball and the horn all day long.

One day, Tom and Sam went for a walk. They saw a big mountain. The mountain was very dull. They wanted to go to the top. Tom said, "Let's number our steps as we go up!" Sam agreed, and they started to walk. Tom counted, "One, two, three..." They walked and walked, and the numbers got bigger. As they went up, they saw a big tree. Behind the tree, they found a door! They had never seen a door on a mountain before. Tom and Sam opened the door, and they saw a big room full of toys! They played with the toys and had lots of fun. The dull mountain was not dull anymore. Tom and Sam knew they would remember this day forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rich cat. This cat had soft, pretty fur. The cat lived in a big house with a nice family. The family loved the cat very much. One day, the cat went outside to play. The cat saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it lost its friends. The cat wanted to help the bird. The cat said, "I will include you in my family." The cat took the bird to the big house. The family was happy to see the bird. They gave the bird food and a warm place to sleep. The bird was happy to be included in the cat's family. The cat and the bird became good friends. They played together every day. The cat's fur was soft and warm, so the bird liked to snuggle with the cat. The family was happy to have both the cat and the bird in their home.
