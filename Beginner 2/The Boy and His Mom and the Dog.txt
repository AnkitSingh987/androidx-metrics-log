Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy and His Mom and the Dog

One day, a boy and his mom went to the beach. They saw a big, black dog. The boy said, "Mom, can we play with the dog?" Mom said, "Yes, but be careful." The boy and the dog played with a ball. They had lots of fun. Then, the dog quit playing. The boy was sad. He asked the dog, "Why did you quit?" The black dog said, "I am not a dog. I am a cat!" The boy and his mom were very surprised. They laughed and played with the cat on the beach. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely sailor. He lived on a big boat. He liked to zip up his coat when it was cold outside. One day, the lonely sailor saw a small fish. The fish said, "Hi, sailor! Why are you lonely?" The sailor said, "I have no friends on my boat." The fish wanted to help. "I will be your friend, sailor!" The sailor smiled. He was not lonely anymore. Now, he had a friend to zip his coat with. The sailor and the fish played together every day. They had lots of fun. The sailor was happy. He was not lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big, brown dog. The dog could not hear well. People said the dog was deaf. The dog lived in a yard with a tall fence all around it. One hot day, the dog was very thirsty. He wanted to find water, but his bowl was dry. So, he tried to dig under the fence to find water outside. The fence was very strong, and the dog could not dig under it. The little boy who lived in the house saw the dog trying to dig. He knew the dog was thirsty. The boy filled a big bowl with water and gave it to the dog. The dog was very happy and stopped trying to dig under the fence. The boy and the dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with many apples. The apples were clean and red. There were two friends, a boy and a girl. They liked to play near the tree. One day, the boy and girl wanted to eat the apples. They decided to separate the apples. The boy would take some and the girl would take some. They both wanted to share the apples. The boy and girl picked the clean apples from the tree. They were very happy. They sat under the tree and ate the apples. The apples were tasty, and the friends had a fun day.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toy train in the living room. They liked to make the train go fast and make noises like "choo-choo". But they were not careful and they knocked over a vase of flowers. The water spilled on the floor and the vase broke into pieces. "Oh no!" Lily said. "Mom will be angry. She likes the vase. It was a gift from Grandma." Tom was scared. He did not want to get punished. He had an idea. He ran to the kitchen and got a pot of water. He put it on the stove and turned on the fire. He wanted to make steam. He thought the steam would hide the mess. But Tom did not know how to use the stove. He turned the fire too high and the water boiled too fast. The pot made a loud noise and the steam came out very hot. Tom burned his hand and dropped the pot. The water splashed everywhere and the fire alarm went off. Mom heard the alarm and ran to the kitchen. She saw Tom crying and the pot on the floor. She turned off the stove and took Tom to the sink. She put cold water on his hand and hugged him. "Tom, what were you doing?" Mom asked. "Why did you make steam?" Tom sniffed and told Mom the truth. He said he was sorry for breaking the vase and he wanted to hide it. Mom sighed and took Lily and Tom to the living room. She showed them the broken vase and the wet floor. She said they were both in trouble for being careless and lying. She said they had to clean up the mess and go to their rooms. She said they could not play with the train for a week. Lily and Tom felt bad. They said they were sorry again and they helped Mom clean up. They went to their rooms and did not play. They learned their lesson. They knew they should not play with the stove and they should tell the truth. They hoped Mom would forgive them and let them play with the train again. They also hoped Tom's hand would heal soon. They felt weak and sad.
