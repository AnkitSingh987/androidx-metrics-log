Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bottle

One day, a girl named Lily found a thin bottle. Inside the bottle was a sweet smell. It was perfume! Lily loved the smell and wanted to keep it safe. So, she held the bottle tight in her hands. Lily showed her friend, Tim, the perfume bottle. Tim wanted to hold it too. "Please let me hold it," said Tim. But Lily said "no" because she was scared he might drop it. Tim was sad, and they both did not know what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. She found a soft cloth and put the thin bottle on it. Lily and Tim both held the cloth with the perfume bottle on it. They were happy to share the bottle and smell the sweet perfume together. The conflict was over, and they stayed good friends.

Lily and Tom were playing near the creek. They liked to throw stones and watch the water splash. The creek was clear and cold and made a nice sound. But today, the creek looked troubled. It was muddy and fast and made a loud noise. Lily and Tom saw some big sticks and cans and bottles in the water. They wondered what happened. "Where did all this come from?" Lily asked. "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe someone threw them in the creek. That's not nice. They make the creek dirty and hurt the fish." Lily and Tom felt sad and angry. They wanted to help the creek. They decided to pick up some of the trash and put it in a bag. They also told their mom and dad what they saw. "Good job, Lily and Tom," their mom said. "You are very kind and brave. The creek needs friends like you. But don't drink the water, okay? It might make you sick." Lily and Tom nodded. They hoped the creek would get better soon. They said goodbye to the creek and went home. They drank some juice and ate some cookies. They were happy to have each other and the creek.

Once upon a time, in a quiet room, there was a little cube. The cube was all alone and could not sleep. It tried to close its eyes, but it could not. The cube was sad. One day, the cube met a new friend, a soft bear. The bear liked to sleep a lot. The cube told the bear about its problem. The bear had an idea. He said, "Let's find a way to help you sleep." The cube and the bear looked all around the room. They found a soft, warm blanket. The bear wrapped the cube in the blanket. The cube felt warm and safe. Finally, the cube could sleep. The cube and the bear slept together, happy and quiet.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. Today, they see a big truck in the park. The truck has many books. Lily and Tom are curious. They go to the truck. "Hello, children," a lady says. She is in the truck. "Do you like books?" "Yes, we do," Lily and Tom say. "I have good news for you," the lady says. "This is a book truck. You can borrow books from here and read them at home. Do you want to try?" Lily and Tom are happy. They nod and smile. They climb into the truck. The truck is warm and cozy. There are many books on the shelves. Lily and Tom look at the books. They see books with pictures, stories, animals, and colors. They pick some books they like. "Can we take these books home?" Lily asks. "Of course, you can," the lady says. "But you have to bring them back in two weeks. Can you do that?" "Yes, we can," Tom says. The lady gives them a card. The card has their names and the date. The lady says the card is to remember when to bring the books back. "Thank you, lady," Lily and Tom say. "You're welcome, children," the lady says. "Have fun reading." Lily and Tom take their books and go out of the truck. They sit on a bench in the park. They open their books and start reading. They are very interested in their books. They point at the pictures and say the words. They discuss what they see and what they think. They laugh and learn. They are having a good time. They love books.
