Category: Beginner
Title: The Orange Cat and the Dog

One day, a big orange cat went for a walk. The cat liked to step on the soft grass. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. The cat saw a small bow on the ground. The bow was pretty and red. The cat picked up the bow with its mouth and carried it. The cat met a dog on the walk. The dog said, "I like your bow!" The cat said, "Thank you! I found it on the ground." They both smiled and kept walking. The orange cat and the dog became good friends.

---

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. But then, Lily saw something shiny on the shelf. It was a glass vase that Mom had bought from a shop. Lily wanted to touch it and see how it felt. "Look, Tom, a pretty vase!" Lily said. She climbed on the sofa and reached for the vase. Tom saw what she was doing and got scared. "No, Lily, don't touch it! Mom said it is breakable!" Tom shouted. But it was too late. Lily grabbed the vase and it slipped from her hand. It fell on the floor and shattered into pieces. Lily and Tom heard a loud crash and saw the glass everywhere. "Oh no, oh no, oh no!" Lily cried. She felt bad and scared. She had broken Mom's vase. What would Mom say? Would she be angry? Would she be sad? Lily wanted to fix the vase and make Mom happy again. Tom felt bad and scared too. He wanted to help Lily and make the mess go away. He saw a broom and a dustpan in the corner. He had an idea. "Lily, don't cry. I have a plan. We can restore the vase. We can put the pieces back together. Come, help me sweep the glass." Tom said. He took the broom and the dustpan and started to collect the glass. Lily wiped her tears and joined him. They worked hard and carefully. They put the pieces of the vase on a table and tried to fit them like a puzzle. They were almost done when they heard Mom coming home. They panicked. They hid the vase under a blanket and ran to the sofa. They pretended to be reading a book. "Hello, my sweeties. How was your day?" Mom asked. She smiled and hugged them. She did not see the vase or the glass. "It was good, Mom. We played with our toys and read a book." Lily said. She hoped Mom would not find out. "It was good, Mom. We learned a new word. Restore. It means to make something like before." Tom said. He hoped Mom would be proud. Mom looked puzzled. She did not know what they were talking about. But she was happy to see them. She did not notice anything wrong. "That's wonderful, my darlings. You are so smart and creative. I'm so proud of you. And I love you very much." Mom said. She kissed them and took them to the kitchen for a snack. Lily and Tom felt relieved and grateful. They had restored the vase and their safe. They had not made Mom angry or sad. They had made her happy and proud. They decided to tell her the truth later and say sorry. They knew Mom would forgive them and love them anyway. They hugged Mom and each other. They were happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a huge toy for Tim's friend's birthday. Tim's mom gave him some money to pay for the toy. Tim was very happy. At the store, Tim found the huge toy. He examined it to make sure it was the right one. "Mom, is this the toy we need?" he asked. His mom said, "Yes, that's the one! Good job, Tim!" When they went to pay, the lady at the cash register looked at the money and laughed. "This is not real money!" she said. Tim and his mom were surprised. It turns out, Tim had taken play money from his toy cash register. They all laughed and Tim's mom paid with real money.
