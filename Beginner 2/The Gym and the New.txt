Category: Beginner
Title: The Gym and the New

Once upon a time, there was a gym. In the gym, many kids played games. They ran, jumped, and had fun. One day, a new kid came. He was competitive. He wanted to win all the games. The kids played a game of tag. The new kid wanted to be the best. He ran very fast and caught everyone. He laughed when he won. The other kids did not like it. They talked to the new kid. They told him to be nice and play fair. The new kid did not listen. He still wanted to win. He played too rough. The kids got mad. They did not want to play with him anymore. The new kid was sad. He had no friends. The gym was not fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a shy pirate named Tim. Tim was not like other pirates. He did not like to yell, and he did not like to take things from other people. Tim had a big hat and a little boat. He liked to sail on the sea and look for nice things to do. One day, Tim saw a big ship. The other pirates on the ship were loud and mean. They wanted Tim to join them and take things from people. But Tim was a good pirate, and he refused to do that. He said, "No, I will not be mean like you." Tim sailed away in his little boat. He found a little island with a lot of pretty shells. He made friends with the animals on the island. They liked Tim because he was nice and shy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a dog named Spot and a cat named Fluffy lived in a small house. They were very good friends. Spot had a job to keep the house safe. Fluffy was grateful for Spot's help. One night, Spot heard a strange noise outside. He barked, "Fluffy, do you hear that?" Fluffy listened and said, "Yes, I hear it too. What do we do?" Spot said, "I will go outside and see." Spot went outside and saw a bird. The bird was lost and scared. Spot said, "Do not be scared, bird. We will help you." Fluffy came outside and said, "We can be friends." The bird was happy and grateful. From that day on, they all lived together in the small house, and they were the best of friends.

Anna likes to mail letters to her grandma. She draws pictures of her toys, her dog, and her friends. She puts them in envelopes and sticks stamps on them. Then she walks with her mom to the big blue box on the corner. She puts her letters in the slot and says, "Bye bye, letters!" One day, Anna and her mom go for a walk to the park. They cross a bridge over a river. Anna sees ducks swimming in the water. She says, "Hello, ducks!" The ducks quack back. They are friendly ducks. Anna wants to feed them some bread, but her mom says, "No, bread is not good for ducks. Let's find some seeds instead." They walk to a bench where a man is selling seeds. He gives Anna a handful and says, "Here you go, little girl. You can feed the ducks with these." Anna says, "Thank you, mister!" She runs back to the bridge and throws the seeds to the ducks. The ducks eat them and wag their tails. They are happy ducks. Anna is happy too. She says, "I like ducks. Maybe I can mail them a letter too." Her mom laughs and says, "Maybe you can, Anna. Maybe you can."

Once upon a time, there was a huge room. In the room, there was a bookshelf. The bookshelf was full of books. It was very tall and big. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to read a book. He saw a book on the top of the bookshelf. Tim tried to get the book, but he was too small. He asked his big sister, Sarah, for help. Sarah said, "No, I will not help you." Sarah refused to help Tim. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He found a chair and stood on it. He reached up and got the book. Tim was happy. He read the book and had fun. Sarah saw Tim and smiled. They both read books together and had a great day.
