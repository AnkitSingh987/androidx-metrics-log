Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, harmless dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and run in the park. One day, Spot saw a tall pole. He wanted to play with the pole, but he could not move it. Spot saw a little bird on the pole. The bird said, "Hi, Spot! This pole is not for play. It is old and will rot soon. It is not safe." Spot listened to the bird and decided not to play with the pole. Instead, Spot and the little bird played with a ball. They had a lot of fun in the park. The pole stayed still and they stayed safe. Spot and the little bird became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to read books. Mia had many books in her room. She read them every day. Mia's favorite book was about a big red ball. One day, Mia could not find her favorite book. She looked under her bed, but it was not there. Mia was sad. She wanted to read her favorite book about the big red ball. She asked her mom for help. Mia's mom looked at Mia's fingers. She saw that Mia had a book with her fingers. It was not the book about the big red ball, but it was a new book. Mia's mom said, "Let's read this book." Mia was happy to read the new book. She found out that she liked this book too. Now, Mia had two favorite books to read when they were available.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to sing. He would sing all day long. One day, Tim saw a big tube on the ground. It was a new place for him to play. Tim was a brave bird. He wanted to go inside the tube. He flew to the tube and said, "Hello, tube! Can I play inside you?" The tube did not talk, but Tim went inside anyway. Inside the tube, it was dark and quiet. Tim was not scared. Tim began to sing a happy song inside the tube. His voice echoed and it made the tube feel like a fun place to be. Soon, other little birds heard Tim's song and they came to the tube too. They all sang together, making the tube a happy home for all the brave little birds.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an old toy gun in his room. He did not know where it came from. He was very excited to play with it. Tim ran to his mom and showed her the toy gun. He asked her, "Mom, can I play with this old toy gun?" His mom looked at the toy gun and said, "Yes, you can play with it, but be careful and play nicely." Tim smiled and ran outside to play with his friends. He showed them the old toy gun and they all thought it was very cool. They played a game where they had to ask each other questions. If someone got the question right, they could pretend to shoot the toy gun in the air. They all laughed and had a great time playing with the old toy gun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mouse. The little mouse had a long tail. He liked to play with his tail all day. One day, the little mouse saw something unknown. It was big and round. He was scared but also wanted to play with it. So, he put his tail on the unknown thing. But, oh no! His tail got stuck! The little mouse tried to release his tail. He pulled and pulled. Then, a big, friendly bear came and helped the little mouse. With a gentle tug, the bear released the mouse's tail. The little mouse was so happy and thanked the bear. They became best friends and played together in the big forest every day.

Once upon a time, there was a white goat. The goat was hungry. It went to a boy and said, "I want to eat." The boy looked at the goat and said, "I can help you." He looked around and saw a big green tree. The boy suggested, "Why not try the tree? It has yummy leaves." The white goat went to the tree and started to eat. The goat was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree began to talk! The tree said, "Please, stop eating my leaves!" The goat and the boy were very surprised. They never heard a tree talk before. The goat stopped eating and said, "I'm sorry, tree. I didn't know you could talk." The tree forgave the goat and they all became friends.
