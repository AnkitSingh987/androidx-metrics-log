Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Flea

Lily was a little dog who liked to play in the park. She had a lot of friends there, like Max the cat, Ben the bird and Sam the squirrel. They liked to run, jump and chase each other. One day, Lily felt something tickle her fur. She scratched and scratched, but it did not go away. She looked and saw a tiny black dot. It was a flea! The flea bit her and made her itch. Lily was very angry and tried to catch the flea, but it was too fast and too small. "Help me, help me!" Lily barked. "There is a flea on me and it hurts!" Her friends heard her and came to see what was wrong. They saw the flea and tried to help Lily. Max tried to paw it, Ben tried to peck it and Sam tried to flick it with his tail. But the flea was too clever and jumped from one spot to another. "Ha ha ha!" the flea laughed. "You can't catch me! I am the best flea in the world!" Lily and her friends did not like the flea. They thought he was very rude and mean. They wanted to scare him away. They had an idea. They waited until it was dark and then they hid behind a big tree. They waited for the flea to come out and look for Lily. When the flea came out, he did not see Lily or her friends. He thought they had given up and he was very happy. He started to sing and dance on Lily's fur. "I am the flea, the flea, the flea! I can bite and jump and make you itch! No one can stop me, me, me!" But then, he heard a loud noise. It was Lily and her friends. They jumped out from behind the tree and shouted at the flea. "Boo! Boo! Boo!" they yelled. "We are here to scare you! You are a bad flea, a bad flea, a bad flea! Go away and leave us alone!" The flea was very scared. He had never seen so many angry animals before. He did not like the dark or the noise. He wanted to run away. He jumped off Lily's fur and ran as fast as he could. He did not look back. Lily and her friends were very happy. They had scared the flea away. They hugged and cheered and thanked each other. They did not itch anymore. They went back to play in the park. They hoped they would never see the flea again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his yard when he found a big lime. He tried to lift the lime, but it was too heavy for him. Tim was angry that he could not lift the lime. One day, a nice bird flew down to help Tim. The bird tried to lift the lime too, but it was too heavy for the bird as well. They both tried to lift the lime together, but it still would not move. Tim and the bird were very sad. Then, something unexpected happened! The big lime started to shake and it cracked open. Inside the lime, there was a little toy car! Tim and the bird were so happy. They played with the toy car all day, and they were not angry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. One day, Tim found a piece of paper on the floor. It was a license for a big car. Tim thought the license was pretty, so he kept it. Tim showed the license to his friend, Sue. Sue wanted to play with the license too, but Tim was being selfish. He didn't want to share. Sue was sad and went away. Tim felt bad for not sharing the license with Sue. Tim decided to be a good friend. He found Sue and gave her the license. They both played together and had fun. After playing, the license was dirty. Tim and Sue worked together to wipe the license clean. They learned that sharing was more fun than being selfish.
