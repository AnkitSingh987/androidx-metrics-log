Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Leaves

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big tree with red and yellow leaves. Sara wants to taste a leaf. She thinks it will be sweet like candy. "Look, Ben, a leaf!" Sara says. She picks up a small leaf and puts it in her mouth. "Yuck, Sara, don't do that!" Ben says. He makes a face. He knows leaves are not food. They are for trees. Sara spits out the leaf. It is not sweet. It is bitter and dry. She does not like it. She feels a tickle in her throat. She coughs. "Are you okay, Sara?" Ben asks. He is worried. He loves his sister. Sara nods. She drinks some water from her bottle. She feels better. She smiles at Ben. "Sorry, Ben, I was curious. I wanted to know how leaves taste. But they are not good. They are bad," Sara says. Ben hugs Sara. He is glad she is okay. He is curious too, but he listens to Mom and Dad. They tell him what is good and what is bad to eat. "Let's play with the leaves, Sara. We can make a pile and jump in it. That will be fun," Ben says. Sara agrees. She likes to play with Ben. They forget about the leaf. They run and laugh and jump in the pile of leaves. They feel the fall breeze on their faces. They are happy.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play in the yard. One day, they saw a big truck with a loud horn and a lot of smoke coming out of it. They were curious and wanted to see it closer. "Let's go to the road and wave at the truck!" Tim said. "No, that's not safe!" Kim said. "Mommy told us to stay away from the road. The truck might not see us and hit us." But Tim did not listen to Kim. He ran to the road and tried to get the truck's attention. Kim followed him and shouted, "Tim, come back!" The truck driver saw the twins and was scared. He tried to stop the truck, but it was too fast. He honked his horn and yelled, "Get out of the way!" Tim and Kim heard the horn and the yell. They were afraid and did not know what to do. They froze in the middle of the road. The truck driver could not stop the truck in time. He had to yield to the right and hit a tree. The truck made a loud crash and more smoke came out of it. Tim and Kim were lucky. They were not hurt by the truck, but they were very sorry. They ran to the truck driver and asked, "Are you okay?" The truck driver was angry and hurt. He had a cut on his head and a bruise on his arm. He said, "No, I'm not okay. You two were very foolish and reckless. You could have been killed by the truck. You should never play on the road. You should listen to your mommy and stay in the yard." Tim and Kim felt ashamed and guilty. They said, "We're sorry, mister. We were curious and wanted to see your truck. We didn't mean to cause you trouble. We won't play on the road again. We'll listen to our mommy and stay in the yard." The truck driver sighed and said, "I'm glad you're sorry and learned your lesson. But you should also be grateful that you're alive and not hurt. You should always be careful and respectful of the road and the vehicles on it. They are not toys. They are dangerous and can hurt you and others." Tim and Kim nodded and said, "We understand, mister. We're grateful and respectful. Thank you for telling us." The truck driver smiled and said, "You're welcome. Now go back to your mommy and tell her what happened. And don't forget your promise." Tim and Kim hugged the truck driver and said, "We will, mister. Thank you again. And we hope you feel better soon." They ran back to their mommy and told her everything. Their mommy was shocked and relieved. She hugged them and said, "I'm so glad you're okay. But you were very naughty and disobedient. You could have been hurt or worse. You should never play on the road. You should always listen to me and stay in the yard." Tim and Kim said, "We know, mommy. We're sorry. We won't play on the road again. We'll listen to you and stay in the yard." Their mommy said, "I hope you mean it. And I hope you also learned something from the truck driver. He was very brave and kind to you. He could have been angry and mean to you. He could have sued us for the damage to his truck. But he forgave you and taught you a valuable lesson. You should always be thankful and respectful to him and to others who help you and teach you." Tim and Kim said, "We do, mommy. We're thankful and respectful to him and to others. We learned a lot today." Their mommy said, "That's good. Now go and play in the yard. But be careful and safe. And don't forget to say sorry to the tree too. It also suffered because of your curiosity." Tim and Kim said, "We will, mommy. We love you." They went and played in the yard. They said sorry to the tree and gave it a hug. They were careful and safe. They were not curious about the road anymore. They were happy and wise.
