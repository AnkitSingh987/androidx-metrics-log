Category: Beginner
Title: The Boat and the Sailed

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. It was happy when it sailed. One day, the boat got a hole. It needed a stitch to fix it. A nice man helped the boat. He put a stitch in the hole. The boat was grateful to the nice man. It sailed on the water again. The boat and the man were happy. They sailed together every day.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tom and Tim. They lived in an old, ancient house with their mom and dad. The house was big and had many rooms to play in. One day, mom told Tom and Tim to hurry and get ready for school. The twins put on their clothes quickly and ate their breakfast. They didn't want to be late for school. Tom and Tim walked to school together. They held hands and talked about all the fun things they would do that day. When they got to school, they hugged and went to their classrooms. They knew they would see each other again soon.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Rich. He had a big pumpkin farm. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the farm. He saw the big pumpkins and really wanted one. Tim asked Mr. Rich, "Can I have a pumpkin, please?" Mr. Rich said, "No, I will not let you have one. They are all mine." Tim was sad and angry. He wanted a pumpkin so much. Tim decided to take a pumpkin when Mr. Rich was not looking. He picked the biggest pumpkin and ran away. Mr. Rich saw him and chased after him. Tim tripped and the pumpkin broke. Tim cried, and Mr. Rich was still angry. They both had a bad day.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to take a cup from the cupboard. She wanted to drink some water. The cupboard was very high, so she asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Be careful, Mia. The cups in the cupboard are delicate." Mia nodded and listened to her mom. Mom took a delicate cup and gave it to Mia. Mia held the cup with both hands and drank water. She was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Mia learned to be careful with delicate things like the cup.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the sun. One day, Max saw a light structure in the yard. It was a little pool just for him. Max jumped into the pool and started to soak. He splashed and played all day. Max said, "I love my new pool! It is so much fun!" Max's friend, a cat named Luna, came over to play. Luna saw Max in the pool and said, "Max, can I play in the pool too?" Max said, "Yes, Luna! Come and soak with me!" So they played together in the pool, and had a great day.

Anna likes to jump. She jumps on her bed, on the sofa, on the grass. She jumps with her friends, with her dog, with her doll. She jumps when she is happy, when she is bored, when she is angry. One day, Anna finds a pretty diary in her mom's room. It has a pink cover and a lock. Anna wants to see what is inside. She jumps on the bed and reaches for the diary. She tries to open it, but it is locked. She looks for the key, but she cannot find it. She hears her mom coming. She jumps off the bed and hides the diary under her pillow. She pretends to read a book. "Hi, Anna. What are you doing?" her mom asks. "Reading," Anna says. "Can I see your book?" her mom asks. Anna gives her the book. Her mom smiles and sits on the bed. She feels something under the pillow. She lifts it and sees the diary. "Anna, what is this?" her mom asks. Anna feels scared. She jumps off the bed and runs to the door. "Sorry, mom. I just wanted to see it. It is so pretty. Please don't be mad," Anna says. Her mom sighs and calls her back. She hugs her and says, "Anna, this is my diary. It is very special to me. It has all my secrets and memories. I don't like it when you take my things without asking. Do you understand?" Anna nods and says, "Yes, mom. I understand. I'm sorry. I won't do it again." Her mom smiles and says, "It's okay. I forgive you. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will never jump on the bed again. It is dangerous. You could fall and hurt yourself. Do you promise?" Anna thinks for a moment. She likes to jump on the bed. But she loves her mom more. She says, "Yes, mom. I promise. I will never jump on the bed again." Her mom kisses her and says, "Good girl. I love you, Anna." "I love you, too, mom," Anna says. They hug and laugh. Anna is happy. She doesn't need the diary. She has her mom. And she can still jump on other things.
