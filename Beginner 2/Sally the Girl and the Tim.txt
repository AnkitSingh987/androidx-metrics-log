Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One day, Sally saw her friend Tim playing with a ball. "Can I play too?" Sally asked. Tim said "Yes, but be careful not to strike the ball too hard." They played and had lots of fun. But then, Sally hit the ball too hard and it got dirty. Sally felt guilty because the ball was not hers. She said "I'm sorry, Tim. I will clean it for you." Tim smiled and said "That's okay, Sally. We can clean it together." So, they washed the ball and it was like new again. They played more and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to cook with her mom. They would cook great meals together. One day, Daisy and her mom went to the store. They bought some food to cook a great meal. Daisy was very excited. Daisy and her mom went back home. They started to cook their great meal. Daisy helped her mom cut the food and put it in the pot. They cooked and cooked until it was all done. Daisy could not wait to eat the great meal they made. Finally, it was time to eat. Daisy and her mom sat down at the table. They took a big bite of the great meal they cooked. It was so yummy! Daisy was very happy that she helped her mom cook. From that day on, Daisy knew she wanted to be a great cook just like her mom.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, rough rock. Tim wanted to climb the rock. But, his friend, Sue, said, "No, Tim! It is too high!" Tim did not listen. He climbed the rock. At the top, he saw something. It was a paper with numbers on it. "Look, Sue!" he yelled. "I found a paper with numbers!" Sue climbed up too. They looked at the numbers. They were big and small. Then, something unexpected happened. The numbers started to move! They jumped off the paper and danced in the air. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the dancing numbers. They had never seen such a sight before.

Once upon a time, there was a pale teddy bear named Tim. Tim lived in a big toy box with all his toy friends. One day, Tim saw a new toy in the box. It was a small, blue bird. Tim was happy to see the new toy and wanted to be friends with the bird. Tim went up to the bird and said, "Hi, I am Tim. Do you want to be my friend?" The bird opened its mouth, and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend, Tim. My name is Blue." Tim was so happy to have a new friend. He gave Blue a big hug. Blue hugged Tim back. They played together all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and Blue were the best of friends, and they always gave each other a hug when they were happy or sad.

One day, a rabbit was in the woods. He saw a big, terrible rock. The rabbit was sad. He could not go to his home because the rock was in the way. The rabbit saw a strong bird. The rabbit said, "Please, bird, help me. Lift the terrible rock so I can go to my home." The bird said, "Okay, I will help you." The bird lifted the terrible rock with its strong feet. The rabbit was so happy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" The rabbit went to his home and they were friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small pond in the west, there lived a tough little duck named Tim. Tim loved to splash in the water all day long. He would flap his wings and play with his friends, making big splashes in the pond. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a tiny fish who also loved to splash in the water. Tim said, "Hi, Sam! Do you want to play and splash with me?" Sam replied, "Yes, Tim! I love to splash too!" So, Tim and Sam played and splashed in the pond all day long. They had so much fun together, and they became the best of friends. From that day on, they always played and splashed in the pond, making each other and their friends very happy.
