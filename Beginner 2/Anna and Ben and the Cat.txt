Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Cat

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with yarn. They make shapes and animals with yarn. They have fun. One day, Anna has to go to grandma's house. She says bye to Ben. She takes her yarn with her. She wants to make a gift for grandma. Ben is sad. He misses Anna. He has no one to play with. He looks for his yarn. He cannot find it. He thinks Anna took all the yarn. He goes to Anna's house. He knocks on the door. No one is there. He sees a window. He is nosy. He looks inside. He sees a big cat. The cat is playing with yarn. The cat has a lot of yarn. The cat sees Ben. The cat is angry. The cat hisses at Ben. Ben is scared. He runs away. He thinks the cat is mean. He thinks the cat stole the yarn. He goes home. He waits for Anna. He hopes Anna will come back soon. He wants to play with Anna and yarn. He wants to say sorry to Anna. He wants to be friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a huge dog named Max. Max loved to mix things. One day, he saw a big bowl with many colors. He thought it would be fun to mix all the colors together. Max asked his friend, Cat, "Do you want to mix colors with me?" Cat said, "Yes, let's mix them!" They mixed the colors and made a big mess. They laughed and played, but they did not know what would happen next. The colors they mixed made a magic rainbow. The rainbow grew bigger and bigger, and it changed the town. The town became full of colors, and everyone was surprised. Max and Cat were happy that they made the town more beautiful with their magic rainbow.

One day, a clever cat named Tom found a glowing soap. He had never seen soap glow before. Tom was very excited and wanted to show his friend, a dog named Max. "Max, look at my soap!" said Tom. "It can glow!" Max looked at the soap and was very surprised. He had never seen a soap like that before. "Wow, that is so cool!" Max said. They played with the glowing soap and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The soap started to grow bigger and bigger! Tom and Max were scared. They didn't know what to do. But then, the soap stopped growing and started to talk! "Hello, Tom and Max! I am a magic soap! I can make you glow too if you want!" said the soap. Tom and Max were very happy. They used the magic soap and started to glow just like the soap. They had even more fun playing together with their new glowing selves.

One bright day, a boy named Tim went to the beach. He saw the big blue sea and the waves. Tim smiled and ran to play in the sand. Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my toy in the sand." Tim wanted to help Sue. They both looked for the toy in the sand. As they looked, a big wave came and washed a toy out of the sand. Tim and Sue were surprised! They thought it was Sue's toy, but it was a new toy. They both smiled and played with the new toy together.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, messy landscape. There were many trees, rocks, and dirt. Tim liked to play in the messy landscape. He smiled as he played. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! I like to play here too." Tim smiled and said, "Let's play together!" They played in the messy landscape, having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the messy things away. The landscape was not messy anymore. It was clean and pretty. Tim and Sue looked at each other and smiled. They could still play and have fun in the clean landscape.
