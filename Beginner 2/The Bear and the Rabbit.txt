Category: Beginner
Title: The Bear and the Rabbit

One day, a big bear went for a walk in the woods. He was very alert, looking at all the things around him. The bear saw a big rock and wanted to sit on it. As the bear tried to sit on the rock, he felt a weight on his foot. He looked down and saw a little rabbit. The bear had stumbled on the rabbit by accident. The rabbit was scared, but the bear said, "Sorry, little rabbit, I didn't see you there." The rabbit looked at the bear and said, "That's okay, Mr. Bear. I have a surprise for you." The rabbit pulled out a big, yummy cake from behind a bush. The bear was so happy and they both ate the cake together. The bear and the rabbit became best friends from that day on.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with toys. One day, they saw a big and shiny toy car in the shop window. They both wanted the toy car very much, but it was very expensive. "Mom, can we have the toy car, please?" they asked their mom, who was with them. "I'm sorry, my loves, but we don't have enough money for the toy car. It costs too much. Maybe you can save some money and buy it later," their mom said. Tom and Lily were sad and angry. They did not want to wait. They wanted the toy car now. They thought of a plan. They wrote a note to their dad, who was at work. They wrote: "Dear Dad, we love you very much. Please buy us the toy car from the shop. It is a very nice toy car. Thank you. Love, Tom and Lily." They put the note in their dad's coat pocket, which was hanging on the door. They hoped their dad would see the note and buy them the toy car. But their mom saw the note first. She was not happy. She knew the toy car was too expensive. She took the note and put it in the trash. She called Tom and Lily and said: "What is this note? Did you write this? This is not a nice thing to do. You cannot request things that we cannot afford. You have to be grateful for what you have. And you have to be honest with your dad. He works hard to provide for us. You should not try to trick him." Tom and Lily felt ashamed and sorry. They knew their mom was right. They hugged their mom and said: "We're sorry, mom. We were wrong. We love you and dad very much. We don't need the toy car. We have enough toys. We will be good and patient." Their mom smiled and said: "I forgive you, my loves. I know you are good and kind. And maybe one day, when we have more money, we can buy the toy car. But for now, let's go home and play with what we have." Tom and Lily nodded and said: "Okay, mom. Let's go home and play." They held their mom's hand and walked away from the shop window. They did not look at the toy car anymore. They were happy with their mom and their toys.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to soar high in the sky. He liked to look down and see all the little animals playing below. One day, Bobo saw a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi was looking for food. Bobo flew down and said, "Hi Mimi, do you need help?" Mimi looked up and said, "Yes, I am hungry and cannot find any food." Bobo wanted to help Mimi. He used his big wings to soar high and look for food. He saw some food far away. Bobo told Mimi, "I found food! Follow me!" They went to the food, but it was on the other side of a big river. Mimi tried to cross the river, but the water was too deep. Bobo tried to help, but his wings got wet and he could not soar anymore. They both were very sad and hungry. In the end, they could not get the food and had to go back home with empty bellies.
