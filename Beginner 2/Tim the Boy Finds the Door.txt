Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim was sad because he had no friends to play with. He thought his days were dull. One day, Tim saw a new neighbor moving in next door. He saw a girl his age. Tim's mom said, "Go say hi to her, Tim!" Tim walked to the girl and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play with me?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes! I'm Lily. I don't know anyone here." Tim and Lily played together every day. They shared their toys and helped each other. One day, Lily fell down and started to cry. Tim helped her up and said, "Don't cry, Lily. I'll always be here for you." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. You're a good friend." Tim and Lily learned that being kind and helpful made their days fun and not dull anymore. They knew that having a good friend is important. And they lived happily as the best of friends. The moral of the story is to be kind and helpful to others, and you will never have a dull day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a big tree with pretty leaves. She wanted to wave at the leaves. Lily went to the tree and waved her hand. The leaves waved back. She was very happy. Then, she saw a spicy red pepper hanging from the tree. Lily wanted to taste it. She reached for the spicy pepper and suddenly, she felt a fall. She tripped on a rock. Lily was scared, but she was okay. She looked at the tree and saw the leaves waving again. Lily smiled and waved back.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One of them was called Zara. The other was called Jax. Zara and Jax went out to play in the sun. They saw a big green pool and wanted to explore. Jax was very excited to get closer to the pool. But, when they reached the pool, they realized it was separated in two. There was a thin line at the middle. They both looked puzzled. Zara asked: "What is this thin line?' Jax thought for a moment and then answered "It is separating the pool into two parts". Zara and Jax smiled and went to play in the smaller pools. They had lots of fun!

Sam was a big boy. He liked to do things by himself. He could put on his new shoes, he could brush his teeth, he could even make his bed. But there was one thing he did not like to do. He did not like to take a shower. Every night, his mom would say, "Sam, it's time for your shower. Come on, let's go." And every night, Sam would say, "No, no, no. I don't want to take a shower. I'm not dirty. I'm fine." His mom would try to make him go. She would say, "But Sam, you have to take a shower. It's good for you. It makes you clean and fresh and happy. And you can play with your toys in the shower. Look, I have a new duck for you. Quack, quack, quack." But Sam would not answer. He would hide under his bed, or behind the couch, or in the closet. He would not come out until his mom gave up and said, "Fine, Sam. No shower for you. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that tomorrow, you will take a shower. No more hiding, okay?" Sam would nod and say, "Okay, mom. I promise. Tomorrow, I will take a shower." But he did not mean it. He hoped his mom would forget. But his mom did not forget. The next night, she said, "Sam, it's time for your shower. Remember, you promised me. Come on, let's go." And Sam said, "No, no, no. I don't want to take a shower. I changed my mind. I don't like showers. They are wet and cold and scary." His mom did not try to make him go. She did not say anything. She just smiled and said, "Okay, Sam. No shower for you. But you have to do something else. You have to come with me to the garden. I have a surprise for you." Sam liked surprises. He liked the garden. He thought his mom had a new toy for him, or a new flower, or a new bug. He said, "Okay, mom. I will come with you to the garden. What is the surprise?" His mom took his hand and led him to the garden. There, he saw a big hose with a nozzle. His mom turned on the water and said, "The surprise is this. A new shower for you. A shower in the garden. Isn't it fun?" And before Sam could say anything, his mom sprayed him with the water. Sam screamed and laughed and ran around. He got wet and cold and happy. He played with the hose and the water and the duck. He said, "Mom, this is the best shower ever. I love it. Can we do it again tomorrow?" His mom hugged him and said, "Yes, Sam. We can do it again tomorrow. But only if you take a shower in the bathroom tonight. Deal?" Sam nodded and said, "Deal, mom. I will take a shower in the bathroom tonight. And in the garden tomorrow. And the next day. And the next day. And the next day." And he did. And he liked it. And he was a big boy.
