Category: Beginner
Title: Jen and Tom and the Park

Jen and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big zigzag on the ground. It is yellow and black. It moves fast and makes a loud noise. "What is that?" Jen asks. "It is a snake!" Tom says. "It is scary. We should run away." But Jen is not afraid. She likes the snake. She thinks it is pretty and funny. She wants to dance with it. "Come on, Tom. Let's dance with the snake. It will be fun," Jen says. She runs to the snake and jumps over it. She makes a zigzag with her feet. She laughs and claps her hands. The snake is surprised. It stops moving and looks at Jen. It does not want to hurt her. It likes her dance. It makes a soft noise and wiggles its tail. Tom is scared. He does not want to dance with the snake. He thinks it will bite him. He stays far away and watches Jen. "Jen, come back. The snake is dangerous. It can hurt you," Tom says. But Jen does not listen. She dances with the snake. She is happy. She makes a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy dog, Spot. One day, Tim found a big box in his room. He did not know what was inside. He gave the box a little shake, but it did not make any sound. Tim was very curious. The next day, Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor took a special picture called an "x-ray" of Tim's hand. Tim thought it was so cool to see the bones in his hand. He wanted to see what was inside the big box, too. When Tim got home, he remembered the x-ray. He thought, "If I can see my bones, maybe I can see what's in the box!" So, Tim pretended to take an x-ray of the box. He closed his eyes and imagined what could be inside. When he opened the box, he found a brand new toy car! Tim was so happy, and he played with his new toy car and Spot all day long.

One day, Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They saw a big ladder leaning on the wall. They wanted to climb it and see what was on the other side. "Let's go up the ladder!" Anna said. "OK, but be careful!" Ben said. They took turns to climb the ladder, holding on tight. When they reached the top, they discovered a surprise. There was a big tree with red and yellow apples. The apples looked juicy and sweet. "Wow, look at the apples!" Ben said. "Can we pick some?" Anna asked. They reached out and picked some apples. They bit into them and smiled. They were sweet and crunchy. "These are the best apples ever!" Anna said. "Yum, yum, yum!" Ben said. They ate some more apples and then climbed down the ladder. They took some apples with them to share with their mom and dad. They were happy and proud of their discovery.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help people and was very compassionate. One day, Lily decided to make a big cake for her friends to share. As she was making the cake, a small bird flew into her kitchen. The bird said, "I can't believe I'm inside your house! Can you help me find my way home?" Lily agreed to help the bird and put the cake in the oven. Together, they went outside and looked for the bird's home. The unexpected happened when they found a magic tree that could talk! The tree said, "Thank you for being so compassionate to my bird friend. I will help you both." The tree showed the bird the way home and gave Lily a special cake that never ran out. Lily's friends were so happy, and they all enjoyed the magic cake together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a clumsy boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy chain. One day, Tom had a question for his mom. He asked, "Mom, can I go play with my chain outside?" His mom said, "Yes, Tom, but be careful. Don't lose your chain." Tom was happy and ran outside to play. He swung his chain around and around. But because Tom was clumsy, the chain flew out of his hand and into a big tree. Tom was sad and had another question for his mom. "Mom, can you help me get my chain from the tree?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tom. Let's get a long stick and try to get it down." They found a long stick and used it to get the chain back. Tom was happy again and said, "Thank you, Mom!" From that day on, Tom tried to be less clumsy when playing with his chain.
