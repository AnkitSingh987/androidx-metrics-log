Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sweet things, but he did not like sour things. One day, he saw a big jar of candy on the ceiling. He wanted to eat the candy, but he could not reach it. He thought and thought about how to get the candy down. Tim had an idea. He got a long stick and tried to push the jar off the ceiling. But he did not see that the jar was not full of candy. It was full of sour lemons! The jar fell and broke on the floor. Tim was sad because he wanted candy, but now there were only sour lemons. Then, Tim remembered something. He forgot that he had a bag of candy under his bed. He ran to his room and found the candy. He was so happy that he forgot about the sour lemons. He shared the candy with his friends, and they all had a great day. And from that day on, Tim always remembered to look for candy in his room before trying to get it from the ceiling.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a foolish cat named Tom. Tom loved to see the sun and play with his friends. One day, Tom saw a new cat in town. He was very excited to meet the new cat. Tom went to say welcome to the new cat. He said, "Hi, I am Tom. Welcome to our town!" The new cat said, "Thank you, Tom. My name is Tim." They became friends and played together all day. One day, Tom did a silly thing. He climbed up a tree and could not come down. Tim saw Tom and said, "Oh, Tom, you are so foolish! Let me help you." Tim climbed the tree and helped Tom come down. They both laughed and played together again. From that day, they were always there to help each other.

Mia and Leo were twins who liked to look at the stars at night. They had a big window in their room that showed them the sky. Sometimes they saw the moon, sometimes they saw the clouds, and sometimes they saw the stars. One night, they saw something brilliant in the sky. It was a star that shone brighter than the others. It was yellow and sparkly and made them happy. "Look, Leo, look!" Mia said. "A brilliant star!" "I see it, Mia, I see it!" Leo said. "It is so pretty!" They leaned on the window and tried to touch the star with their fingers. They wished they could fly to the star and play with it. But then, the star moved. It moved across the sky, leaving a trail of light behind it. It was a shooting star! "Wow, wow, wow!" Mia and Leo said. "A shooting star!" They closed their eyes and made a wish. They wished they could see more brilliant stars and have more fun nights. The shooting star faded away, but the twins smiled. They hugged each other and said good night. They dreamed of the brilliant star and the sky.

One day, a boy named Tim walked into a mist. He could not see very well. Tim was scared of the unknown. He wanted to find his way home. Tim saw a big tree. He asked the tree, "Please help me find my way home." The tree did not yield. Tim was sad. He kept walking. Soon, Tim met a kind bird. The bird said, "I will help you go home." They walked together. The bird made the mist go away. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, bird!" Tim and the bird were friends.

Lily and Tom were fish who lived in a big reef. They liked to swim and play with their friends. Sometimes they saw other fish who were big and bright and had many fins. Lily and Tom wanted to be like them. One day, they saw a fish who was very big and bright and had many fins. He was swimming near the reef and talking to himself. He said, "I am the most beautiful fish in the sea. No one can match my colors and my shapes. I am the king of the reef." Lily and Tom swam closer to him and said, "Hello, Mr. Fish. You are very beautiful. Can we be your friends?" The big fish looked at them and laughed. He said, "You want to be my friends? You are too small and dull and have few fins. You are not worthy of my friendship. Go away, you humble fish." Lily and Tom felt sad and hurt. They swam away from the big fish and back to the reef. They met their friend, Nemo, who was a clownfish. He saw their faces and asked, "What's wrong, Lily and Tom? Why are you so sad?" Lily and Tom told him what happened with the big fish. Nemo shook his head and said, "Don't listen to him, Lily and Tom. He is not a nice fish. He is mean and proud and selfish. He thinks he is better than everyone else, but he is not. He is lonely and unhappy. You are nice fish. You are kind and humble and friendly. You have many friends who love you. You are happy fish. You are the best fish in the reef." Lily and Tom smiled and hugged Nemo. They said, "Thank you, Nemo. You are a good friend. You are right. We don't need to be like the big fish. We are happy with who we are. We are happy in the reef." They swam away with Nemo and joined their other friends. They played and laughed and had fun. They forgot about the big fish and enjoyed their life in the reef.
