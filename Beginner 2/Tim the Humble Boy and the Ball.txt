Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Humble Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with dough. He would make all sorts of shapes with it. One day, Tim's mom gave him some dough to play with. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Tim was excited to show her the dough. "Look, Sue! I have dough to play with. Let's make something together," said Tim. Sue was excited too. They both sat down and started to make shapes with the dough. As they played, they decided to part the dough into two pieces. Tim made a big, round ball with his part, and Sue made a long, thin snake with hers. They laughed and had fun playing with the dough. They were very proud of their creations.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a zipper. He liked to go with his zipper everywhere. Tim's zipper was on his favorite bag. He took the bag to the park, to his friend's house, and even to bed. One morning, Tim woke up and felt hungry. He went to the kitchen to find some food. Tim's mom said, "We need to go to the store to buy more food. Take your bag with the zipper, and we will fill it with yummy things." Tim was excited. At the store, Tim saw another boy with a bag just like his. The boy said, "Hey, that's my bag!" Tim said, "No, it's my bag with the zipper!" The two boys were upset. Tim's mom came over and looked at the bags. She saw that Tim's name was on his bag with the zipper. She said, "This bag belongs to Tim, and that bag belongs to you." The boys felt better and went to fill their bags with food.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She was very excited and ready to play. Lily saw a big fountain in the park. The water in the fountain went up and down. It was very pretty. Lily's mom told her to be careful and not go too close to the fountain. But Lily did not listen. She saw a small toy boat near the fountain. She wanted to play with it. Lily went close to the fountain and started to point at the boat. Suddenly, Lily slipped and fell into the water. She got all wet and cold. Her mom came running to help her. Lily was very sad and scared. She should have listened to her mom. Now, they had to go home, and Lily could not play in the park anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a new case. It was a pretty case. The case was red and blue. The case liked to play with his friends, the ball and the doll. They had fun together. One day, the case got dirty. "Oh no!" said the case. "I need to be clean." So, the case went to the sink. The case wanted to wash. The case used water and soap. The case washed and washed until it was clean again. After the case was clean, it went back to play with the ball and the doll. They were happy to see the case. They all played and had fun. The case was happy to be clean and new again. They played all day and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very helpful boy. He always helped his mom and dad with chores. One day, he saw a helpless cat stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat. Tim called out to his friend Sam, "Sam, come help me! The cat is stuck in the tree." Sam came running to help Tim. They tried to reach the cat, but they were too small. They needed a sign to tell other people about the cat. Together, Tim and Sam made a big sign that said, "Help! Cat in tree!" They put the sign near the tree. Soon, a tall man saw the sign and came to help. The man lifted Tim up, and Tim grabbed the cat. The cat was safe now, and everyone was happy. Tim and Sam learned that when you work together, you can help even the most helpless animals. The town was proud of Tim and Sam for their big hearts and helpful hands.
