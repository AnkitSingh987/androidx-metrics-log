Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Mom

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to help their mom in the kitchen. Today, mom is making rice for dinner. She puts some rice and water in a big pot on the stove. "Can we help you, mom?" Lily and Ben ask. "Sure, you can help me. You can watch the pot and tell me when the water boils," mom says. Lily and Ben sit on the floor and look at the pot. They see the water get hot and make bubbles. They see the rice get bigger and bigger. "Mom, mom, the water is boiling!" Lily shouts. "Mom, mom, the rice is increasing!" Ben shouts. Mom comes to the stove and turns down the heat. She smiles at Lily and Ben. "Thank you, my helpful twins. You did a good job. Now the rice is almost ready. Let's set the table and wait for dad to come home," mom says. Lily and Ben are happy. They help mom set the table and put some plates and spoons. They smell the rice and it makes them hungry. They can't wait to eat the rice with mom and dad. They love rice and they love helping mom.

One day, a white cat named Kitty went for a walk. Kitty loved to play outside. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. So, she jumped and climbed up the tree. While Kitty was in the tree, she saw a cup of tea on a branch. She thought it was strange to find tea in a tree. Kitty tried to reach the tea, but she slipped and fell. She was stuck in the tree! A brave boy named Tim saw Kitty in the tree. He wanted to rescue her. Tim climbed the tree and saved Kitty. As they got down, Kitty saw the tea again. This time, Tim reached for the tea and took it down with them. When they got to the ground, Kitty and Tim looked at the tea. They saw a note under the cup. The note said, "For the one who rescues Kitty, this tea is for you." They were surprised! It was a special tea for the rescuer. Tim and Kitty laughed and became friends. They shared the tea and played together. The tea was a gift for being brave and kind. And that's how Kitty and Tim found the unexpected tea in the tree.

Once upon a time, there was a small square. The square was broken, but it was still very happy. It lived in a big park with many friends. The square loved to play with its friends all day long. One day, the square felt very tired. It wanted to take a nap under a big tree. The square said to its friends, "I am tired. I will nap now." Its friends said, "Okay, have a good nap!" So, the square went under the tree and lay down. While the square was napping, its friends played near the tree. They were careful not to wake the square up. After a while, the square woke up from its nap. It felt much better and not tired anymore. The square joined its friends and they all played happily together again.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play outside, but the weather was very hot and dry. She wanted to find a way to make the dry weather better for her and her friends to play. One day, Mia met a kind man named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown saw that the children were tired from the dry weather. He wanted to provide them with something to help. He thought and thought, and then he had a great idea. Mr. Brown said, "Mia, I can provide you and your friends with a big pool of water to play in. It will help you stay cool in this dry weather." Mia was very happy and thanked Mr. Brown. She called all her friends, and they played in the pool all day long. They splashed and laughed, and the dry weather didn't bother them anymore. Mia and her friends were grateful to Mr. Brown for providing them with the pool to play in.
