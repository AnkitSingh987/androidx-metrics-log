Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very enthusiastic. He loved to play with his friends. One day, Max and his friend, a little bird named Sam, made a plan to play a game. Max said, "Sam, let's play on this side of the park." Sam was happy and said, "Yes, Max! That side looks fun!" They ran to the side of the park with the swings and slides. They played and laughed all day. As the sun went down, Max and Sam were very tired. They said goodbye and went to their homes. Max and Sam had so much fun playing on that side of the park. They couldn't wait to play together again.

Anna and Ben were playing on the lawn. They liked to run and jump and roll on the green grass. They also liked to look at the flowers and the bugs and the birds. One day, they saw a big dog on the lawn. The dog was black and brown and had a red collar. He looked troubled. He was barking and growling and scratching at the fence. "Who is that dog?" Anna asked Ben. "I don't know," Ben said. "Maybe he is lost. Maybe he is hungry. Maybe he is scared." Anna and Ben wanted to help the dog. They went closer to the fence. They said, "Hello, dog. We are Anna and Ben. We are your friends. Do you want to play with us?" The dog stopped barking and growling and scratching. He looked at Anna and Ben. He wagged his tail. He licked his lips. He bowed his head. "Look, Ben," Anna said. "He is bowing. He is saying thank you. He is being nice." "Maybe he wants some food," Ben said. "Let's go get some bread and cheese and water for him." Anna and Ben ran to the house. They asked their mom for some bread and cheese and water. They told her about the dog on the lawn. Their mom said, "That dog belongs to our new neighbors. They just moved in yesterday. They are very nice. They will be happy to meet you and share their dog with you." Anna and Ben took the bread and cheese and water to the dog. They gave him some through the fence. The dog ate and drank and wagged his tail. He licked their hands. He bowed his head again. "Good dog," Anna and Ben said. "What is your name?" Just then, they heard a voice from the other side of the fence. It was a girl. She said, "His name is Max. He is my dog. He is very friendly. He likes to play. Do you want to play with us?" Anna and Ben smiled. They said, "Yes, we do. We are Anna and Ben. We are your new friends. We like your dog. He is very nice. He likes to bow." The girl smiled too. She said, "My name is Lily. I am your new neighbor. I like you too. You are very kind. You like to share. Come on, let's play." Anna and Ben and Lily and Max played on the lawn. They ran and jumped and rolled on the green grass. They also looked at the flowers and the bugs and the birds. They had a lot of fun. They became good friends. They were happy.

Once upon a time there lived a clumsy bear. Every single morning he had to add up his honey. He loved counting and adding, so it made him happy. One morning he decided to add up the honey that he had hidden in the forest. In the middle of the adding, he got a shock. He looked around and realised he was not alone in the forest. He had been watched. He got up quickly and started running. But the person in the forest had been faster and caught him by his feet. It was then that the clumsy bear realised he had been tricked. The strange person in the forest tied up the bear and took all of his honey away. The bear had been really clumsy and now his honey was gone. The bear cried and called for help, but no-one came. He was all alone in the forest and without his honey. The bear never came back to the forest after that day. Sadly, he never got his honey back.
