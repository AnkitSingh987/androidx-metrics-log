Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Balloon

Sara and Ben like to play with bubbles. They have a big bottle of bubble soap and a wand. They blow bubbles in the park and watch them fly in the air. Sometimes they try to catch them or pop them. One day, they see a very big bubble in the sky. It is bigger than a ball or a balloon. It is shiny and colorful. They wonder what is inside the bubble. "Wow, look at that bubble!" Sara says. "It is so big and pretty!" "Maybe there is something in it," Ben says. "Do you want to guess what it is?" Sara thinks for a moment. She is nervous. She does not know what to say. She looks at the bubble and tries to imagine what it could be. "Maybe it is a bird," she says. "A big, fluffy bird that likes bubbles." Ben shakes his head. He does not think it is a bird. He has another idea. "Maybe it is a star," he says. "A bright, sparkly star that fell from the sky." Sara smiles. She likes Ben's idea. She thinks it is very nice. "Maybe you are right," she says. "Maybe it is a star." They keep looking at the bubble. They hope it will come closer. They want to see the star. They are curious and excited. They do not know that the bubble is really a balloon. A balloon that someone let go by mistake. A balloon that will soon pop and disappear.

The sun was shining brightly in the sky. It made the leaves on the trees sparkle and twinkle. Little Jack was playing in the garden. He could see a small, round oat in the dirt. He picked it up with his hand and held it close to his chest, admiring it. Suddenly, a big and mean dog appeared. It was barking and growling at Jack. Its teeth were sharp and its eyes were dark. Jack was so scared, he almost dropped the oat. He quickly came up with a plan. He yelled out at the dog and said, “Go away! This oat is fake, it’s not even real!” The dog stopped barking and walked away. The sun kept shining and Jack was safe. He laughed and went back to playing with his oat.

Once upon a time, there was an alligator named Al. Al was not very smart, and his friends called him stupid. One day, Al went to the big pond to play with his friends. Al's friend, the big frog, said, "Let's play a game. You try to catch me, and if you can't, you have to relax in the sun." Al agreed and started to chase the big frog. The big frog was very fast, and Al could not catch him. Al felt sad but remembered the game. Al went to relax in the sun, just like the big frog said. While he was relaxing, he saw a big log. Al had an idea. He thought, "Maybe I can use this log to catch the big frog next time." The next day, Al and the big frog played the game again. This time, Al used the big log to help him catch the big frog. The big frog was surprised and said, "Wow, Al! You are not stupid at all. You are very smart!" Al was happy, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends, a dog named Max and a bird named Sally. They would run and jump all day long. One day, Tom, Max, and Sally wanted to play a new game. They sat down to discuss what game to play. Max said, "Let's take turns to find a toy!" They all agreed and started the game. It was Tom's turn first. He looked under a tree and found a ball. Max's turn was next, and he found a stick. Finally, it was Sally's turn, and she found a leaf. They all laughed and played with their new toys. They had a fun day and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very silly dog. He loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, Max found a big stick. He thought it was a threat. Max barked at the stick and tried to make it go away. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, saw Max barking at the stick. She knew Max was just being silly. Lily walked over to Max and picked up the stick. She showed Max that the stick was not a threat. Max looked at the stick and wagged his tail. He was happy that the stick was not a threat. Lily threw the stick and Max ran after it. Max learned to accept that the stick was not a threat. He had fun playing with the stick with Lily. From that day on, Max and Lily played with the stick every day. They had lots of fun together and Max was not scared of the stick anymore.
