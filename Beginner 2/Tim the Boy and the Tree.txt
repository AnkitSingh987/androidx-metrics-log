Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red truck. He loved to play with his truck all day. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his truck. He was very happy. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue wanted to play with Tim's truck, but Tim said, "No, it's mine! I don't want to share." Sue was sad because Tim was being selfish. Tim played with his truck alone, not letting Sue play with it. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Tim's truck high into the air. The truck landed in a tree, and Tim could not reach it. He was very sad. Sue saw Tim was sad and said, "Don't worry, I can help!" Sue found a long stick and got the truck down from the tree. Tim was very happy to have his truck back. He said, "Thank you, Sue. I'm sorry I was selfish. Let's play together!" From that day on, Tim and Sue played together with the truck, and they both learned that sharing is more fun than being selfish.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box in his messy room. He didn't know what was inside the box. He was very curious and wanted to open it. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, I encourage you to open the box and find out what's inside!" Tim opened the box and saw many toys. He was very happy. He played with the toys all day. His mom was happy too. She said, "Tim, I am glad I encouraged you to open the box. Now, let's clean your messy room together." Tim and his mom cleaned the room and put all the toys back in the box. Tim learned that it is good to be encouraged and to help clean up messy rooms.

One day, Jack was walking through a giant forest. He had never been there before and each tree looked big and unknown. Suddenly, he heard a rustling sound. It was coming from behind one of the trees. Jack looked carefully and saw a little animal. It was a rabbit! "Hello," said Jack, cautiously. The rabbit looked scared and tried to hide behind the tree. Jack stepped closer. The rabbit jumped and tried to escape! Jack smiled. "It's OK," he said. "I won't hurt you." The rabbit stopped and looked at Jack. Its nose twitched as it stepped closer. Jack knew he had made a friend. With that, they were off, exploring the forest and all its unknown secrets.

One day, a girl named Sue and a boy named Tim went to play. They saw a big box on the side of their play area. "What's in the box?" asked Sue. Tim said, "I don't know. Let's test if it's a toy." As they opened the box, they found a lot of spoiled food. "Yuck!" said Sue. "This is not a toy." Tim felt sad. But then, they heard a small sound from under the spoiled food. They moved the food and found a little cat. The cat was very happy to see them. "Meow, thank you for finding me," said the cat. Sue and Tim were very surprised that the cat could talk. They became friends and played together all day.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a deep hole next to a mailbox. Sue was curious and wanted to look inside the mailbox. But when she opened it, a big wind came and made her favorite toy vanish! Sue was sad and said, "Oh no! Where did my toy go?" A friendly bird heard Sue and flew down to her. The bird said, "I saw your toy fly into the deep hole next to the mailbox. I can help you get it back." Sue and the bird worked together to get her toy out of the deep hole. They used a long stick to reach the toy. After some tries, they finally got the toy back. Sue was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird! You are a good friend." The bird smiled and flew away, and Sue went home with her toy, feeling very happy.
