Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Squirrel

One day, a little bird named Tim felt dizzy. He did not know why he felt this way. Tim had a friend named Sam, who was a kind squirrel. Sam saw Tim was not feeling good and wanted to help. Sam said, "Tim, let's play in the rain. It will make you feel better." The rain was soft and cool. Tim and Sam played in the rain, and Sam encouraged Tim to jump in the puddles. Tim started to feel better and not so dizzy. Soon, the rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tim and Sam were happy and not dizzy anymore. They played all day long, and Sam was glad he could help his friend feel better.

One day, a little cat named Tom saw a swing in the park. He wanted to play on it, but he was too small. Tom was jealous of the big kids who could play on the swing. He sat under a tree and lay down, feeling sad. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw him laying under the tree. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom told Max about the swing and how he was too small to play on it. Max had an idea and said, "I can help you play on the swing!" The next day, Max and Tom went to the park together. Max helped Tom get on the swing by lifting him up. Tom was so happy! He swung back and forth, laughing and having fun. The big kids were surprised and smiled at them. Tom and Max played on the swing all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little player named Tim. Tim loved to play ball with his friends. They would pass the ball to each other and have lots of fun. Tim was always happy to play with his friends. One day, a new kid named Max came to play. Max was a bit rude. He would not pass the ball to the other kids. He would only keep the ball for himself. This made Tim and his friends sad. They did not like playing with Max. Tim had an idea. He talked to Max and told him that it is more fun to pass the ball and play together. Max listened to Tim and started to pass the ball. Soon, everyone was having fun again. Max learned not to be rude and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a little house, lived a restless boy named Tim. He loved to run, jump, and play all day. One day, Tim saw a ball in his yard. He wanted to take it and play with it. Tim said to his mom, "Mom, can I take the ball and play?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim, but be careful. That ball has a special motion." Tim didn't know what that meant, but he was excited to play with the ball. Tim took the ball outside and started to play. He threw the ball, and it moved in a funny way. The ball went up, down, left, and right. Tim laughed and chased the ball. He played with the ball all day, and it made him very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple bear named Ben. He liked to wear a big hat on his head. Ben had a big heart and loved to help his friends. One day, Ben's friend, a small bird named Bella, lost her nest. "Oh no, where is my nest?" cried Bella. Ben heard her and came to help. "Don't cry, Bella. I will help you find your nest," said Ben with a big smile. They looked high and low, and finally found the nest on a tall tree. Ben put on his big hat and climbed the tree. He carefully picked up the nest and brought it down to Bella. "Thank you, Ben. You have a big heart," said Bella, happy to have her nest back. They both smiled and went to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Tornado. Tornado had a big yard to play in. He loved to run and jump all day long. One day, Tornado saw a butterfly and wanted to play with it. Tornado said, "Hi, butterfly! Do you want to play with me?" The butterfly said, "Yes, Tornado! Let's play together!" So, Tornado and the butterfly played in the yard all day long. They were very happy. Later, Tornado's mom called him. She said, "Tornado, it's time to come in and eat!" Tornado said goodbye to the butterfly and went inside. Tornado ate his food and then took a nap. He was so happy to have a new friend and to own a big yard to play in.
