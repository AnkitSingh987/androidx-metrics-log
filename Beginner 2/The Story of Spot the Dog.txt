Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of Spot the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot loved to carry things in his mouth. He would carry sticks, balls, and even his favorite toy. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her ball. Spot wanted to help, so he said, "I will find your ball and carry it back to you!" Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" Spot looked for the ball for a long time. Finally, he found it under a big tree. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it back to Sue. Sue was so happy to see her ball again. She hugged Spot and said, "You are the best dog ever!" Spot wagged his tail and felt very proud. From that day on, Spot and Sue were the best of friends and had lots of fun together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to have a party. She told her friend Tom, "Let's have a party with cake and games!" Tom liked the idea and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They started to plan their party, but a mysterious boy named Max saw them. Max wanted to join the party too. But Lucy and Tom did not like Max, so they ignored him. Max felt sad and went away. The party day came, and Lucy and Tom were having fun. But they saw Max sitting alone under a tree. They felt bad for ignoring him. Lucy and Tom went to Max and said, "We are sorry, Max. You can come to our party too." Max was happy, and they all played and ate cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going on a trip with his family. He was very excited. He had to pack his bag with clothes and toys. Tim's mom said, "Don't forget to pack your favorite teddy bear." One night, Tim had a bad dream. In the dream, he felt helpless. He could not find his teddy bear. Tim woke up and cried. His mom came to his room and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim told his mom about the bad dream. She hugged him and said, "Don't worry, your teddy bear is safe in your bag." But when they checked the bag, the teddy bear was not there. Tim was sad. Then, they heard a noise from under the bed. They looked and found Tim's teddy bear! It was a happy surprise. Tim hugged his teddy bear and went back to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a job to do. Her job was to sort the things in the room. She saw an ashtray on the table. It looked fragile. She was careful not to break it. The little girl looked around the room. She saw things that needed to be sorted. She sorted them into piles. Then she turned back to the fragile ashtray. She moved it carefully and put it in the right pile. The little girl had finished her job. She was very proud of herself. She had sorted all the things in the room.

Lily and Ben are in the playroom. They have many toys to play with. Lily likes to draw on the blackboard with chalk. Ben likes to throw balls and cars. He thinks it is fun. "Look, I made a flower!" Lily says. She shows Ben her drawing on the blackboard. Ben does not care. He throws a ball at the blackboard. The ball makes a loud noise and erases the flower. "Hey, why did you do that?" Lily asks. She is sad and angry. She worked hard on her flower. "It's just a game. Don't be a baby." Ben says. He throws another ball at the blackboard. This time, he hits Lily's hand. Lily cries out in pain. "That's not a game. That's mean. You hurt me." Lily says. She runs to the teacher. The teacher sees Lily's red hand and Ben's balls. "Ben, what did you do?" The teacher asks. She is not happy. "I was just playing. The balls are harmless." Ben says. He does not understand why Lily and the teacher are mad. "Playing is not throwing things at people or their drawings. That is rude and dangerous. You need to say sorry to Lily and help her make a new flower." The teacher says. She makes Ben give back the balls and get some chalk. Ben feels bad. He did not mean to hurt Lily or her flower. He says sorry to Lily and helps her draw a new flower. Lily smiles and forgives him. They both learn to play nicely.
