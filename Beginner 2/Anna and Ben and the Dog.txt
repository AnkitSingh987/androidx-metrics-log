Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Dog

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Sometimes they watch movies on the TV. Anna likes movies with animals. Ben likes movies with cars. One day, Anna and Ben are at Anna's house. Anna's mom says they can watch a movie. She gives them a DVD. It has a picture of a dog and a boy on it. "Can we watch this movie?" Anna asks Ben. "OK," Ben says. He likes dogs. He thinks they are intelligent. He hopes the dog in the movie can do tricks. Anna and Ben sit on the couch. Anna's mom puts the DVD in the TV. The movie starts. The dog and the boy are friends. They go on adventures. They help people. They have fun. Anna and Ben like the movie. They laugh and clap. They think the dog and the boy are brave and kind. They wish they had a dog like that. When the movie ends, Anna and Ben hug each other. They thank Anna's mom for the movie. "That was a good movie," Anna says. "Yes, it was," Ben says. "The dog was very intelligent. He could talk and read and drive a car." Anna nods. She agrees. She thinks the dog was amazing. She wonders if he is real. "Maybe we can find a dog like that someday," she says. "Maybe," Ben says. "But for now, we have each other. And we are friends." They smile and hold hands. They are happy. They love movies and dogs and friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful girl named Lily. She had many shoes, but her favorite shoe was a red one. She loved to wear her red shoe and play outside with her friends. One day, Lily saw a little boy sitting alone. He had no shoes on his feet. Lily felt sad for him and asked, "Why don't you have shoes?" The little boy replied, "I don't have any shoes to wear." Lily thought for a moment and decided to give one of her red shoes to the boy. They both enjoyed playing together with one red shoe each. The little boy was very happy, and they became good friends. They learned that sharing can make others happy, and that being thoughtful is a good thing. And from that day on, Lily and the little boy always shared their things and played together. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can bring joy and make new friends.

One day, a big lion went to the sea. He saw a boat with a sail. He wanted to go on the boat. So, he jumped on the boat and it started to move. As the boat moved, the lion felt happy. He roared, "Roar! I am on a boat!" A little mouse heard the lion and came out. The mouse said, "I am quiet and small. Can I play with you?" The lion said, "Yes, let's play!" They played on the boat until they saw a big wave. The big wave came and the boat flipped over! The lion and the mouse were scared. But then, the little mouse had an idea. The mouse used the sail to make a big kite. They held on tight and the wind took them back to land. The lion and the mouse were safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom was yellow and loved to sleep all day. He lived in a house with a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with Tom, but sometimes he was too lazy to play. One day, Sue wanted to store her toys in a big box. She asked Tom, "Can you help me store my toys, please?" Tom slowly opened one eye and yawned. He was feeling lazy, but he loved Sue and wanted to help her. Tom got up and helped Sue put her toys in the big box. They stored all the toys together. After they were done, Sue said, "Thank you, Tom! You're the best cat ever!" Tom felt happy that he helped Sue and wasn't lazy. From that day on, Tom tried to be less lazy and played more with Sue.
