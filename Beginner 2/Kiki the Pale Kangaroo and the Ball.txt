Category: Beginner
Title: Kiki the Pale Kangaroo and the Ball

One day, a pale kangaroo named Kiki was hopping in the forest. She was very happy and liked to jump high. Kiki had a big pouch on her tummy where she kept her favorite toy, a small ball. While Kiki was playing, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw Kiki and said, "Hi, Kiki! Can I play with your ball?" Kiki liked Tim, so she removed the ball from her pouch and gave it to him. They played together, laughing and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened! Kiki's ball bounced very high and landed in a tree. Kiki and Tim looked up, worried. They didn't know how to get the ball back. Suddenly, a friendly bird flew down and picked up the ball in its beak. The bird gave the ball back to Kiki and Tim. They were so happy and thanked the bird. After that, they all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly statue in a park. The statue was a man with a big smile. Kids loved to play near the statue. They would laugh and have fun. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to play near the statue. She saw the jolly man and wanted to give him a hug. But when she hugged the statue, something unexpected happened. The statue started to suffer. It turned into a real man! The man was still jolly and kind. He told Lily that he was a magical friend who had been stuck as a statue. Lily's hug broke the spell. Now, the man could laugh and play with the kids in the park. Everyone was happy and had lots of fun together.

John looked out of the window. He saw his aunt walking in the street. He was so happy. He ran outside to meet her. John hugged his aunt and said: "Hi, Aunt! I'm so happy to see you." His aunt smiled and said: "I'm so happy to see you, John! How are you?" John was embarrassed. He admitted: "I'm ashamed. I did something wrong." Aunt put her arm around John's shoulders, kindly. She said: "It's ok, my dear - you can always tell me what's wrong." John looked at his aunt and he felt a little brave. He whispered: "I broke your vase." Aunt hugged John and said: "It's ok, I'm not mad. You can always tell me if you make a mistake." John felt reassured. He said: "Thank you, aunt." Aunt kissed his head and said: "It's ok. Now let's go inside and have some dinner."

Sara liked to play dress-up with her mom's clothes. She would find big dresses and hats and shoes in the closet and put them on. She felt like a princess or a fairy. One day, she found a hanger with a very ugly sweater on it. It was brown and yellow and had holes and strings. Sara did not like it at all. She wanted to throw it away. But then she had an idea. She took the hanger and the sweater and went to the living room. She put the hanger on the floor and the sweater on top of it. She made a tent with the sweater and the hanger. She crawled inside the tent and pretended it was a cave. She was a brave explorer who found a secret place. She had fun playing with her toys and books in the cave. She forgot about the ugly sweater and the hanger. She was happy and cozy in her tent. She did not want to come out.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom. One day, they see a big red ball in the grass. They run to get it, but they are not alone. A big dog is also running to the ball. The dog is faster than Anna and Ben. "Mom, mom, hurry!" Anna shouts. "The dog wants our ball!" Mom hears Anna and Ben. She sees the dog. She is not afraid of dogs, but she does not want the dog to take the ball. She runs to the twins and picks them up. She holds them in her arms. "Hello, dog," Mom says. "This is not your ball. This is Anna and Ben's ball. Can you be nice and let them have it?" The dog stops running. He looks at Mom and the twins. He wags his tail. He does not want to be mean. He just wants to play. He barks and licks Mom's hand. "Good dog," Mom says. "You are very friendly. Do you want a treat?" Mom reaches into her pocket. She has some gum. She likes to chew gum, but she knows gum is not good for dogs. She looks for something else. She finds an old cookie. She breaks it in half and gives one piece to the dog. She gives the other piece to Anna and Ben. "Thank you, Mom," Anna and Ben say. They eat the cookie. It is a bit hard, but it is sweet. "You're welcome, my loves," Mom says. She smiles and hugs them. She gives the ball to the twins. "Now you can play with your ball. But be careful, and don't go too far." Anna and Ben nod. They are happy. They throw the ball to each other. The dog watches them. He does not chase the ball. He is happy too. He has a new friend and a treat. He chews the cookie. It is a bit old, but it is tasty.
