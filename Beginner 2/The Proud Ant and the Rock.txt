Category: Beginner
Title: The Proud Ant and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a proud little ant. The ant lived in a big hill made of soil. The ant had many friends in the hill. They all worked very hard to make their home nice. One day, the ant found a big rock. The ant wanted the rock to be in the hill. But the rock did not fit. The ant tried to push and push, but the rock would not go in. The ant was sad, but did not give up. The ant asked his friends for help. They all tried to push the rock together. They pushed and pushed, but the rock still did not fit. The hill started to break. The ants were scared. The rock was too big, and it broke their home. The proud little ant and his friends were very sad.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box. He liked to play with his toys a lot. One day, Tim could not find his favorite toy car. He felt hurt because he loved that car so much. So, Tim started a search to find his toy car. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me find my car?" His mom said, "Of course, Tim. I will help you search for your car." They looked all around the house. They looked under the bed, behind the couch, and even inside the toy box. But they could not find the toy car. Then, Tim's dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot saw Tim was hurt and wanted to help. Spot started to sniff around and found the toy car under a chair. Spot brought the car to Tim. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Spot, for supplying my toy car!" Tim, his mom, and Spot all played with the toy car together.

Once, there was a powerful wizard who needed to borrow some quiet. He searched and searched, but he had so much noise around him. Everywhere he went, he could not find the quiet. One day, he heard a soft whisper coming from a tree. He looked up, and saw a tiny little bird sitting in the branches. The bird said it could help him borrow quiet. The wizard was so excited! He thanked the bird and it told him something important. The bird said that, when he needed quiet, he could borrow it from the stars. So, the wizard went outside at night and saw lots of stars in the sky. He closed his eyes, and remembered the little bird's words. He imagined collecting the quiet from the stars and he felt so peaceful. From then on, the wizard could always borrow enough quiet and he was very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy that could shoot. He was very proud of his new toy. Tim went to show his friend, Sam. Sam liked the toy too. They played together all day, shooting at pretend bad guys. "Bang, bang!" Tim said, as he shot the toy. "We got the bad guys!" Sam laughed and said, "We are the best team!" But then, something unexpected happened. Tim and Sam found a big door with a lock. They thought it was a secret place. They wanted to go inside, but they did not have a key. So, Tim had an idea. "Let's try to shoot the lock with the toy!" Tim said. Sam thought it was a good idea too. They both aimed at the lock and said, "One, two, three, shoot!" To their surprise, the lock opened! Inside the secret place, they found a big box of more toys. Tim and Sam were so happy and proud. They played together all day and shared the toys with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a rubber ball that he played with every day. It was his favorite toy. The ball was not a regular ball. It was very special to Tim. One day, Tim played with his ball near a big tree. He threw the ball high into the air. But then, Tim felt regret. The ball got stuck in the tree. Tim was very sad. A nice man saw Tim and helped him. The man climbed the tree and got the ball for Tim. Tim said thank you and was happy again. He learned not to play near the big tree with his special rubber ball.
