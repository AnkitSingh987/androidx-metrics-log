Category: Beginner
Title: The Robot and the Mia

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot was very old. It lived in an ancient house. The robot was sad because it could not move. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the ancient house. She saw the robot and said, "Hello, robot! Why are you sad?" The robot replied, "I am sad because I cannot move." Mia thought for a moment and then said, "I can help you!" She began to shake the robot gently. As she shook the robot, it started to move again. The robot was very happy! It said, "Thank you, Mia!" From that day on, Mia and the robot became best friends. They played together every day in the ancient house. And the robot was never sad again.

Lily liked to rock on her chair. She rocked back and forth, back and forth, with a big smile on her face. She liked to feel the wind in her hair and the sun on her skin. She liked to see the birds and the clouds in the sky. One day, she saw something new in the garden. It was a poppy. A poppy was a flower that was red and soft and pretty. Lily wanted to see the poppy up close. She got off her chair and ran to the garden. She bent down and touched the poppy gently. It felt nice. But then she heard a loud noise. It was a dog. A dog was an animal that had fur and a tail and barked. Lily liked dogs, but this dog was not nice. It was big and mean and scary. It ran to the garden and jumped on the poppy. It bit the poppy and tore it apart. It made the poppy sad and hurt. Lily was scared and angry. She shouted at the dog. She said, "Go away, dog! You are bad! You hurt the poppy! The poppy was my friend!" But the dog did not listen. It barked and growled and showed its teeth. It wanted to hurt Lily too. Lily ran back to her chair. She rocked back and forth, back and forth, very fast. She hoped the dog would leave her alone. She wished the poppy was still there. She felt sad and lonely. But then she saw someone else in the garden. It was her dad. Her dad was a person who loved her and cared for her. He was tall and strong and kind. He saw the dog and the poppy and Lily. He said, "Don't worry, Lily. I'm here. I'll make the dog go away. I'll get you a new poppy. I'll hug you and make you happy." He did what he said. He chased the dog away. He got a new poppy and planted it in the garden. He hugged Lily and rocked her on his lap. He said, "I love you, Lily. You are brave and sweet and smart. You are my friend." Lily smiled and hugged him back. She said, "I love you, dad. You are tall and strong and kind. You are my friend." They rocked back and forth, back and forth, together. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their skin. They saw the birds and the clouds in the sky. They saw the poppy and the poppy saw them. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little fox. This fox loved to solve problems. One day, he went for a walk and saw a helpless bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The little fox wanted to help the bird. He thought and thought about how to solve the bird's problem. He had an idea! He found a big leaf and put the bird on it. Then, he pushed the leaf and the bird went up and down, like it was flying. The bird was very happy and thanked the fox. They became good friends. The moral of the story is that if you help others, you can make new friends and feel good about yourself.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play in their bedroom. They have many toys and books and dolls. But sometimes they fight over their things. One day, Sara wants to play with Ben's truck. Ben says no. He says it is his truck and Sara has her own toys. Sara gets angry. She grabs the truck and runs away. Ben chases her and tries to get it back. They push and pull and yell. Mom hears the noise. She comes to their bedroom. She sees the mess and the tears. She is not happy. She asks them what is wrong. Sara and Ben both talk at the same time. They tell Mom that the other one is bad and mean and took their toy. Mom shakes her head. She tells them to stop and listen. She says they need to explain why they are upset and how they feel. She says they need to share and be kind and say sorry. Sara and Ben are quiet. They think about what Mom says. They look at each other. They feel sad and sorry. They both say sorry to Mom and to each other. They hug and make up. They decide to play with the truck together. They take turns and help each other. Mom smiles and hugs them. She says she is proud of them. She says they are good twins. She says she loves them. Sara and Ben say they love Mom too. They are happy. They play in their bedroom. They have fun.
