Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Slide

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and books. But they do not like to share their gum. Gum is sweet and sticky. They each have their own gum. One day, Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide. They both want to go on the slide. But there is a problem. The slide is messy. Someone put gum on the slide. The gum is dirty and icky. Lily says, "I want to go on the slide. I do not care about the gum. I can wipe it off." Ben says, "No, you cannot go on the slide. The gum is bad. It will stick to your clothes and hair. You will be messy too." Lily and Ben disagree. They argue and shout. They do not listen to each other. They are not nice. A lady sees them. She is the park keeper. She says, "Stop, stop, stop. What are you doing? Why are you fighting?" Lily and Ben tell her about the slide and the gum. The lady says, "I see. The gum is not good. It is rude to put gum on the slide. But you do not have to fight. You can find another slide. There are many slides in the park. Or you can play something else. There are many things to play in the park." Lily and Ben think. They say, "You are right. We are sorry. We do not want to fight. We want to be friends. We can share our gum. And we can find another slide. Or we can play something else." The lady smiles. She says, "That is good. I am glad. You are smart and kind. Have fun in the park. But remember, do not put gum on the slide. Or anywhere else. Throw it in the trash. OK?" Lily and Ben say, "OK. Thank you. Bye." They hug and run. They are happy. They are friends.

Tom is a hero. He likes to drive a big red truck. He helps people when there is a fire. He puts out the fire with water and saves the people and the animals. One day, Tom sees a lot of smoke in the sky. He drives his big red truck very fast to the place where the smoke is. He sees a big fire in a house. He hears a dog barking inside the house. He knows the dog is in danger. Tom puts on his helmet and his coat. He runs to the door of the house. He kicks the door open. He sees the fire and the smoke. He sees the dog hiding under a table. He calls the dog. The dog is scared, but he trusts Tom. He runs to Tom. Tom picks up the dog and carries him out of the house. Tom gives the dog to his friend, who is also a hero. His friend drives an ambulance. He checks the dog and gives him a hug. He says the dog is okay. He says Tom is very brave. Tom is happy. He loves his job. He loves to drive his big red truck. He loves to help people and animals. He is a hero.

Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Nosy. Nosy was a nosy bug who liked to crawl all over the place. He had many friends, like the ants, the bees, and the butterflies. They all lived in a big, green garden. One day, Nosy wanted to find something new to do. He decided to look for fuel for his friends, the ants. The ants needed fuel to help them work and play. Nosy crawled under the leaves, over the rocks, and through the grass to find the fuel. As he crawled, Nosy found a big, shiny rock. He thought it might be fuel, so he called his ant friends to come see. The ants came and looked at the rock. They told Nosy that it was not fuel, but they liked it anyway. They all played with the rock and had a fun day in the garden. Nosy learned that even if he did not find fuel, he could still help his friends have fun.
