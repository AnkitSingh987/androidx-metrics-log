Category: Beginner
Title: The Red Train and the Fire

Once upon a time, there was a little red train. The train had a big engine that could go very fast. The train loved to go fast and help people get to where they needed to go. One day, the sky was very wet, and it rained all day long. The train didn't mind the rain, but it had to be careful not to slip on the wet tracks. As the train went through the town, it saw a big fire. The fire was burning a house, and the people inside needed help. The train knew it had to do something. So, it used its big engine to go as fast as it could to the fire station. When the train got to the fire station, it told the firefighters about the burning house. The firefighters jumped onto the train and rode it back to the house. They used their hoses to spray water on the fire. The wet water put out the fire, and the people inside the house were safe. The people thanked the train and the firefighters for saving them. The train was very happy that it could help. It knew that its big engine was not just for going fast, but also for helping others when they needed it. And even on wet days, the little red train was always ready to be a hero.

Tom and Mia like to play in the park. They have a big ball and a small bike. They run and kick and ride and laugh. One day, they see a new thing in the park. It is a big cage with a big sign. The sign says "Tiger". Tom and Mia want to see the tiger. They go near the cage and look inside. They see a big cat with black and orange fur. It has sharp teeth and claws. It looks sad and thin. It does not play. It only lies on the ground and sighs. Tom and Mia feel sorry for the tiger. They want to make it happy. They throw their ball to the cage. The tiger looks at the ball. It does not catch it. It does not care. Tom and Mia try again. They ride their bike to the cage. They ring the bell. The tiger looks at the bike. It does not chase it. It does not care. Tom and Mia do not know what to do. They think the tiger is lonely. They wish they could play with it. They wish they could be its friends. They go to the cage and say "Hello, tiger. We are Tom and Mia. We want to play with you. Do you want to play with us?" The tiger hears their voices. It lifts its head. It sees their faces. It sees their smiles. It feels something in its heart. It feels curious. It feels warm. It feels like playing. The tiger gets up. It walks to the cage. It touches the ball with its paw. It rolls it back to Tom and Mia. It roars softly. It says "Hello, Tom and Mia. I am the tiger. I want to play with you. Thank you for being my friends."

Once upon a time, there was a famous judge named Mr. Owl. He was very wise and kind. Everyone in the town loved him. He helped them with their problems and always managed to find a way to make everyone happy. One day, a little girl named Sally came to Mr. Owl. She was sad because she lost her toy. "Mr. Owl, can you help me find my toy?" she asked. "I will try my best," said Mr. Owl. They looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and even in the pond. Finally, Mr. Owl managed to find Sally's toy. It was hidden under a big rock. "Here is your toy, Sally!" said Mr. Owl. Sally was very happy and thanked Mr. Owl for his help. From that day on, Sally and Mr. Owl became best friends.

Lily and Ben are friends who like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. But one day, they have a quarrel over a toy car. Lily says it is hers, and Ben says it is his. "No, it is mine! I brought it from home!" Lily shouts. "No, it is mine! I found it on the grass!" Ben yells. They pull and tug at the toy car, and they do not listen to each other. They are very angry and loud. A big dog hears them and runs towards them. He wants to play with the toy car too. He barks and jumps and snatches the toy car from their hands. Lily and Ben are scared and let go of the toy car. The dog runs away with it, wagging his tail. Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel silly and sorry. They have lost their toy car because of their quarrel. They also miss their friend. "I am sorry, Lily. I should have asked you before I took the toy car." Ben says. "I am sorry, Ben. I should have shared the toy car with you." Lily says. They hug and make up. They decide to play something else. They see a big pile of leaves and run to it. They jump and roll and laugh in the leaves. They have a lot of fun again. They do not notice the dog coming back. He has an unusual gift for them. He drops the toy car on the ground and wags his tail. He wants to play with them too. Lily and Ben are surprised and happy. They thank the dog and pet him. They invite him to join their game. They all become friends and have a wonderful time in the park.
