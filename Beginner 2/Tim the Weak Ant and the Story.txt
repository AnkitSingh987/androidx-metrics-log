Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Weak Ant and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a weak little ant named Tim. Tim lived near a big mill. He had many friends, but he was not strong like them. One day, Tim and his friends went to play near the mill. As they played, Tim saw a big bug. The big bug was mean and wanted to hurt Tim. Tim's friends were scared and ran away. But Tim was brave. He went close to the big bug and said, "You can't hurt me!" Tim used his small legs to pinch the big bug. The big bug was surprised and said, "Ouch! That hurt!" The big bug was not so mean after all. He was just scared of the mill. Tim and the big bug became friends. The moral of the story is that even if you are weak, you can still be brave and make friends. And sometimes, the things that seem scary are not so scary after all.

Once there was a mighty elephant. It was big and strong and had long, floppy ears. The elephant lived in a big field near a forest. The elephant wanted to organize a picnic on the side of the field so he asked all his friends to come join. All his friends came, from the birds in the trees to the bunnies in the bushes. The elephant smiled and said, "Let's organize this picnic together!" Everyone worked hard to get everything ready; the birds brought leaves to use as plates and the bunnies found some delicious fruit. Once the picnic was organized, the elephant and his friends had a wonderful time. They laughed and talked and shared stories. The afternoon was mighty fun.

Ben liked to read books. He had many books on his shelf. He liked to question the books. He asked them what they were about, who wrote them, and where they came from. One day, he saw a new book on his shelf. It was big and shiny. It had a picture of a dragon on the cover. Ben wanted to read it. He took it from the shelf and opened it. But the book did not like to be questioned. It said, "Stop asking me things. I am a popular book. I do not have time for you. Go away and read something else." Ben was sad and angry. He did not like the book. He said, "You are a mean book. You are not popular. You are rude. I do not want to read you. I will put you back on the shelf." He tried to put the book back on the shelf, but the book did not want to go. It said, "No, you cannot put me back. I am too important. I will stay here and you will go away." The book and Ben started to fight. They pulled and pushed each other. They made a lot of noise. They knocked down the other books from the shelf. Mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the mess and the book and Ben. She was not happy. She said, "What are you doing? Why are you fighting with a book? Why are you making a mess?" Ben told Mom what happened. He said, "The book is bad. It does not like to be questioned. It thinks it is popular. It does not want to go back on the shelf." Mom looked at the book. She said, "This book is not for you. It is for older kids. It is too hard and too scary for you. I will take it away and give it to someone else. You can read the other books on your shelf. They are nice and fun and they like to be questioned." Ben was happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom. I like the other books. They are my friends. I will read them and question them and learn from them." Mom hugged Ben and helped him clean up the mess. She said, "You are a good boy. You are curious and smart. You can question anything you want. But be careful with books. Some books are not for you. Some books are not nice. Some books are not popular."
