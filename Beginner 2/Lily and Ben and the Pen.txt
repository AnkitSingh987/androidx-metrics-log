Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Pen

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with toys and books. But sometimes they are naughty and fight. One day, they find a pen on the table. It is blue and shiny. "Look, a pen!" Lily says. "I want it!" "No, I want it!" Ben says. "Give it to me!" They both grab the pen and pull. The pen breaks and ink spills on the table and their clothes. They are shocked and scared. "Uh-oh," Lily says. "Mom will be angry." "Mom will be very angry," Ben says. "She loves this pen. It is her special pen." They hear Mom coming. They try to hide the pen and the ink. But it is too late. Mom sees the mess and the broken pen. "What did you do?" Mom asks. "This is my pen. It was a gift from Grandma. How could you be so naughty?" Lily and Ben feel sorry. They know they did a bad thing. They look at Mom with sad eyes. "We are sorry, Mom," they say. "We wanted to play with the pen. We did not know it was your special pen. We did not mean to break it." Mom sighs. She is angry, but she loves her twins. She hugs them and says, "I know you are sorry. But you have to be careful with things that are not yours. And you have to share and not fight. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben nod. They understand. They say, "We understand, Mom. We will be good. We love you." Mom smiles. She says, "I love you too. Now let's clean up this mess and find another pen for Grandma."

Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree in a park. The tree was very old and wise. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the park to play. He saw the old tree and wanted to talk to it. "Hi, tree!" said Tim. "You are so big and old. Do you have any stories to tell?" The old tree smiled and said, "Yes, I do. I will tell you the story of the lightning." "Long, long ago, there was a big storm. The sky was dark and there was a lot of rain. Then, there was a bright flash of lightning. It was very loud and scary. The lightning hit a tree, not too far from here. The tree did not feel good, but it did not end. It kept on growing." Tim listened to the old tree's story and felt happy. He thanked the tree and said, "I will come back to hear more stories." The old tree smiled and waved goodbye to Tim. From that day on, Tim and the old tree became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird lived in a big tree. The tree was in a pretty park. One day, the bird saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a nail. The bird thought it was food. So, the bird tried to eat the nail. But, the nail was not good to eat. The bird's friend, a squirrel, saw this. The squirrel said, "Don't eat the nail, bird! Let's find yummy food together." So, the bird and the squirrel went to eat nuts and berries. They were happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max lived in a house with a big yard. Every day, Max liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Max had an idea. He wanted to invite his friends to his house for a fun playdate. Max was very excited about his plan. Max sent out invites to his friends. They all said yes! On the day of the playdate, Max's friends came over to his house. They played and had lots of fun. But as the sunset came, something unexpected happened. The big yard started to shake! All of Max's friends were scared. They didn't know what was happening. But Max, being a brave dog, decided to find out. He walked towards the shaking spot and saw a big, friendly elephant named Ellie! Ellie had been playing hide and seek with her family but got lost. She was looking for her family and was shaking the ground by accident. Max and his friends decided to help Ellie find her family. They all looked together and found Ellie's family nearby. Ellie was so happy! She invited Max and his friends to play with her family too. From that day on, Max and his friends had a new friend, Ellie the elephant, and they all played together every day. And the big yard was big enough for everyone to have fun.
