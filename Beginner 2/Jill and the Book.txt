Category: Beginner
Title: Jill and the Book

Jill and her mom were in the living room. Jill's mom asked her, “What do you want to do today?” Jill said, “Let’s organize my books!” Jill and her mom worked together to organize her books. First they put all of the books in one pile. Then they sorted them into two neat piles. Jill was happy with their work. Jill asked, “Can I read my new book now?” Her mom gave her the modest book, and Jill settled down to read. She read for a long time, and when she was done, she looked up at her mom and said, “Let’s organize more books!”

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Mia's mom was cooking on the stove. Mia wanted to help, so she tried to lift a heavy pot. But it was too heavy for her. Mia started to cry. Her mom saw her crying and asked, "Why are you crying, Mia?" Mia said, "I want to help, but the pot is too heavy for me." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Mia. You can help me with something else." Mia stopped crying and her mom gave her a job. She asked Mia to stir the soup in another pot. Mia was happy to help her mom and felt proud. From that day on, Mia and her mom cooked together and had lots of fun in the kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a classroom full of little kids. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, their teacher, Miss Jane, had a serious face. She told the kids, "Today, we will learn how to crawl like a baby." The kids were excited and started to crawl on the floor. They crawled under the tables and chairs. They crawled to each other and laughed. "Look at me!" said Timmy to his friend, Lily. "I can crawl fast like a race car!" Lily smiled and crawled faster, saying, "I can crawl faster than you, Timmy!" They had a race to see who could crawl the fastest. All the kids cheered for their friends. At the end of the day, the kids were tired but happy. They had learned how to crawl and had lots of fun. Miss Jane smiled and said, "Good job, everyone! Now we all know how to crawl like a baby." The kids went home and told their parents about their fun day in the classroom. They couldn't wait to go back and learn more new things with Miss Jane.

One day, a pilot named Tim had a fancy plane. He loved to fly it high in the sky. Tim was very happy when he was in the air. One sunny day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue playing in the park. He landed his plane near her. "Hi Sue! Would you like to see my fancy plane?" he asked. Sue was excited and said, "Yes, please!" Tim offered to take her for a ride in the sky. As they flew, Sue saw a big cloud that looked like a castle. Tim flew closer to the cloud, and they saw it was a real castle! They landed and met a kind queen who lived there. The queen told them that she was lonely and needed friends. Tim and Sue promised to visit her often. From that day on, they had many fun adventures with the queen in her cloud castle.

Tommy was sad. He had a big cut on his knee from falling off his bike. It hurt a lot and he cried. Mommy put a band-aid on it and gave him a hug. She said it would heal soon. "Can I go play now?" Tommy asked. "Not yet, sweetie. You need to rest a bit. Why don't you watch some TV?" Mommy said. Tommy nodded and went to the couch. He turned on the TV and saw a clown. The clown had a red nose, a big smile and thick hair. He was funny and made Tommy laugh. He did silly tricks and jokes with his friends. "Look, Mommy, a clown!" Tommy said. Mommy smiled and sat next to him. "He is funny, isn't he? Do you like clowns, Tommy?" "Yes, I do. They make me happy. Maybe the clown can make my knee heal faster," Tommy said. Mommy kissed his forehead. "Maybe he can. But you know what else can help? Lots of love and care. And you have plenty of that, Tommy. You are a brave boy and I'm proud of you." Tommy hugged Mommy and felt better. He watched the clown and forgot about his pain. He was happy.
