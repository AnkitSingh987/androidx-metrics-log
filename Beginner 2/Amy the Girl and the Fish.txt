Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She liked to study her books and play with her toys. One day, she went to the beach with her mom and dad. They had a big, colorful towel to sit on. The beach was peaceful, and the sun was shining. Amy put her towel on the soft sand. She sat down to study her book about fish. She saw a picture of a big blue fish and a small red fish. Amy liked the way the fish swam in the water. She wanted to see them in real life. Amy asked her mom and dad if she could go into the water. They said yes, but only if they went with her. They all held hands and walked into the water together. Amy saw the big blue fish and the small red fish, just like in her book. They all had a fun day at the peaceful beach.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings, slide, and spin around on the roundabout. When he spun on the roundabout, he would get dizzy and laugh. At the park, Tim saw a big sign that said, "Bring back the lost dog and get a reward." Tim wanted to find the dog and get the reward. He asked his mom, "Can we find the lost dog, please?" His mom said, "Sure, let's look for the dog together." They looked everywhere in the park but could not find the dog. Just as they were about to give up, Tim heard a bark. He followed the sound and found the dog stuck in a bush. Tim and his mom helped the dog get out of the bush. Tim held the dog's leash and walked to the man who put up the sign. The man was so happy that he gave Tim the reward – a big box of cookies. But then, the dog jumped up and knocked the box out of Tim's hands. The cookies fell on the ground and the dog ate them all up. Tim felt dizzy from the surprise, but he and his mom laughed. Even though he didn't get to keep the reward, he was happy that he helped the lost dog find its way home.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk with her mom. They wanted to go to the park to play. On the way, they had to cross the road. Lucy saw a lot of traffic. There were big cars, small cars, and buses too. She held her mom's hand tight. Lucy's mom told her to wait for the traffic to stop. But Lucy was stubborn. She wanted to go now. She tried to pull her mom to cross the road. Her mom said, "No, Lucy. We must wait." Finally, the traffic stopped. Lucy and her mom could see it was safe to cross the road. They walked to the other side and reached the park. Lucy was happy to play on the swings and slides. She learned to be patient and wait for the traffic to stop.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play in her yard. One day, she saw a small, green ball on the ground. It was a lime! Sue wanted to start a game with the lime. She called her friends Tom and Ann to come and play. They were very happy to play with Sue and the lime. They took turns to kick the lime and run after it. The lime rolled into an open door. It was the door to Sue's house. Sue, Tom, and Ann went inside to find the lime. They found it in the kitchen. They all laughed and played with the lime until it was time to go home. Sue said goodbye to her friends and put the lime in a safe place, so they could play again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty mitten. One day, she lost her mitten. Sue was very sad. Sue asked her friend, Tom, to help her find the mitten. Tom said, "Let's bring a big bag to put the mitten in when we find it." They looked and looked for the mitten. They found a mitten that was unknown to them. It was not Sue's mitten. Sue and Tom kept looking. They found Sue's pretty mitten under a big tree. They were very happy. Tom put the mitten in the big bag. They went back home and showed the pretty mitten to Sue's mom. Sue's mom was happy too.
