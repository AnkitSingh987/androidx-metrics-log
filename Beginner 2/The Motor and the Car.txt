Category: Beginner
Title: The Motor and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little motor. It was a creative motor, and it could make many things. One day, the motor made a toy car. The toy car was fun to play with, and all the kids loved it. One day, a boy named Tim took the toy car to play. He played with it all day, but then he lost it. Tim was very sad and began to cry. He looked everywhere for his toy car, but he could not find it. The creative motor saw Tim crying and felt bad. It wanted to help, so it made another toy car for Tim. When Tim saw the new toy car, he was so happy that he stopped crying. Tim thanked the motor and played with his new toy car every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a fun fair with his mom. Tim saw a big game with a prize. He wanted to win the prize, so he asked his mom, "Can I play the game, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can try. But it may be hard." Tim was so happy and he went to play the game. He tried and tried, but he could not win. He felt a little bitter, but he did not give up. Tim wondered how he could win the prize. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea! He asked his mom for help. Together, they played the game and won the prize. Tim was so happy, and he thanked his mom. They went home with a big smile and a fun new toy.

Once upon a time there was a farmer who wanted to make a barn. He worked hard all day to make the barn. He got some wood from the forest and used it to make the walls. He made the walls tall and strong. He got straw to make the roof and a door for the barn. When he was finished with the barn, he stepped back and smiled. It was so clean and nice. He said to the barn, "Welcome home! Now I can keep my animals safe and sound." The barn said, "Thank you! I'm so happy to be here. Please take care of me." The farmer smiled, saying, "I will! I'll make sure you're always clean and neat." The barn was happy to be part of the farm. And the farmer was happy to have a nice, clean barn.

Once upon a time, there was a paint pot. It was filled with clear paint. One day, a small creature wanted to play with the paint. He took out a paintbrush and started to count. One, two, three... He paused and then counted again. Four, five, six. The little creature mixed the paint until it was nice and thick. Then, he dipped his brush in the paint and started to paint. He painted a big blue circle, then added some green circles around it. Last of all he added some yellow dots to finish his painting. He stepped back and looked at his work. He was very proud of his painting. He smiled and said "Wow!" Then he put away his paintbrush and the pot of clear paint, and ran off to show his painting to his friends.

One day, a girl named Sue went for a jog. It was a cold day, but Sue liked the cold. She put on her coat and ran outside. While jogging, Sue saw her friend, Tom. Tom was doing a fun quiz. He looked up and said, "Hi Sue! Want to do the quiz with me?" Sue stopped jogging and said, "Yes, I like quizzes!" They did the quiz together. One question was, "What do you like to do on a cold day?" Sue said, "I like to jog!" Tom said, "I like to stay inside." Then, something unexpected happened. A big, warm sun came out! The cold day turned into a warm day. Sue and Tom laughed and played outside in the warm sun.

One day, a big dog named Sam went for a walk. Sam was an independent dog and liked to go out alone. He saw a chain on the ground and picked it up with his mouth. He wanted to play with it. Sam met a little cat named Lily. She was trying to stir a big pot of soup. "Can you help me?" Lily asked. Sam put down the chain and used it to help stir the soup. They stirred the soup together, and it made a big splash. The big splash made a rainbow! Sam and Lily were surprised. They didn't know that stirring soup with a chain could make a rainbow. They laughed and played under the rainbow, happy that they had made something so beautiful together.
