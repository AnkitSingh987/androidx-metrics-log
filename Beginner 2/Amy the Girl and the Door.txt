Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a delicate chair that she loved to sit on. One day, Amy was playing with her toys and she saw her mom kneel down to pick up something from the floor. "Mom, why did you kneel?" asked Amy. "I had to pick up my ring, it fell on the floor," said her mom. Amy wanted to try kneeling too. So, she got off her delicate chair and knelt down on the floor. When she did this, she found a secret door under her chair! She called her mom to show her the door. "Wow, Amy, I didn't know this door was here!" said her mom. Together, they opened the door and found a beautiful room full of toys and games. They were so happy and played there all day long.

One day, a sheep named Fluffy was walking in the field. Fluffy was very anxious because she had lost her wool. She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. Fluffy was very sad and started to cry. Just then, a bird named Chirpy flew by and saw Fluffy crying. Chirpy asked, "Why are you crying, Fluffy?" Fluffy said, "I lost my wool and I am very cold." Chirpy wanted to help Fluffy find her wool. Chirpy and Fluffy looked all around the field. Suddenly, Chirpy saw something in a tree. "Look, Fluffy! Your wool is in the tree!" Chirpy said. Fluffy looked up and saw her wool. It must have fallen when she was playing near the tree. Chirpy flew up and got the wool for Fluffy. Fluffy was very happy and thanked Chirpy. They became good friends and played together every day. Fluffy was no longer anxious and always made sure to keep her wool safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Toot. Toot lived near a long rail. He was a very obedient train and always did what he was told. Toot loved to help people and make them happy. One day, Toot met a big train named Puff. Puff said, "Toot, I need your help. I cannot move on this rail. Will you help me?" Toot agreed and pushed Puff along the rail. Toot felt good because he could help Puff. As they went down the rail, Toot saw a big box on the rail. Toot was scared, but Puff said, "Do not worry, Toot. I will push the box off the rail." Puff pushed the box, and it opened. Inside the box, there were many toys! Toot and Puff were happy. They learned that when they help others and are obedient, they find value in their work. From that day on, Toot and Puff continued to help each other and their friends.

Mia and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big bird in the park. It is an ostrich. The ostrich has long legs and a long neck. It can run very fast. "Wow, look at that bird!" Mia says. "It is so big and funny!" "Let's play with it!" Ben says. "Maybe it can hop like us!" They run to the ostrich. The ostrich sees them and makes a loud noise. It is not happy. It does not want to play. "Go away, go away!" the ostrich says. "This is my park! You are too small and noisy!" Mia and Ben are scared. They stop running and look at the ostrich. "Sorry, Mr. Ostrich," Mia says. "We just wanted to be your friends." "Friends?" the ostrich says. "You don't look like my friends. My friends have feathers and wings. You have hair and arms." "But we can hop like you!" Ben says. "Look, we can hop on one leg, or on two legs, or backwards!" He shows the ostrich how he can hop. Mia joins him. They hop around the ostrich. The ostrich watches them. He thinks they are funny. He starts to hop too. He hops on one leg, then on two legs, then backwards. "Hey, this is fun!" the ostrich says. "You are not so bad after all. Maybe we can be friends." "Yay, we can be friends!" Mia and Ben say. They are happy. They hop with the ostrich in the park. They have a lot of fun. They are ready for more adventures.
