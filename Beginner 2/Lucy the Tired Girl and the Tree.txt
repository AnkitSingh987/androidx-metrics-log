Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Tired Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a tired little girl named Lucy. She was playing on her lawn when she decided to tie her ribbon in her hair. As she looked around, she noticed that her dad was tying something in the garden. She asked him: "Daddy, what are you tying?" Daddy smiled and said: "I'm tying a rope around the tree, so I can hang a swing on the lawn." Lucy was very excited, she said: "I want to tie too!" Her dad gave her the rope and helped her tie it around the tree. After a few tries, she was successful and was proud of her accomplishment. When she was done tying, her dad said:" Good job Lucy! Now let's hang the swing so you can have lots of fun!" Lucy smiled happily, but she was very tired. She said goodnight and went straight to bed, dreaming of her new swing.

Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play together. Today is their birthday. They get many presents from their mom and dad. They are very happy. They open their presents. Lily gets a doll, a book, and a hat. Tom gets a car, a puzzle, and a ball. They say thank you to their mom and dad. They hug them. They want to play with their new toys. Lily takes her doll and her book. Tom takes his car and his puzzle. They go to their room. But they forget their hat and their ball. They leave them on the table. The hat and the ball are cheap. They are not very nice. Max is their cat. He is very curious. He sees the hat and the ball on the table. He wants to play with them. He jumps on the table. He pushes the hat and the ball with his paw. The hat and the ball roll on the table. They are close to the edge. Max tries to catch them. He loses his balance. He falls off the table. He makes a loud noise. Lily and Tom hear the noise. They run to the table. They see Max on the floor. They see the hat and the ball on the floor. They are not happy. "Max, you are naughty!" Lily says. "You broke our present!" "Bad cat!" Tom says. "You made a mess!" Max looks at them. He is sorry. He meows. He rubs his head on their legs. He wants them to forgive him. Lily and Tom look at each other. They love Max. They are not angry anymore. They pick up the hat and the ball. They put them on Max. They laugh. "Max, you look funny!" Lily says. "You have a hat and a ball!" "Good cat!" Tom says. "You are our present!" Max purrs. He is happy. He plays with them. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird named Bobo. Bobo lived near an airport. He liked to watch big planes fly in the sky. One day, Bobo found many seeds on the ground. He was very happy. Bobo wanted to share the seeds with his friends. So, he picked up the seeds in his beak and flew high in the sky. As he flew over his friends, he opened his beak and scatter the seeds down to them. His friends were so happy. They all ate the seeds together and played near the airport. They watched the big planes fly and had lots of fun. Bobo and his friends were very happy and they lived together, enjoying the wild life near the airport.

Once upon a time, there was a large truck. It was red and shiny. The driver of the truck was a nice man. He had a big smile and always waved to the kids. One day, the truck needed to go up a big hill. The driver tried and tried, but the truck could not go up. The driver did not give up. He wanted to manage the big hill. He tried one more time. He pushed the truck very hard. The truck went up the hill very slowly. The driver was happy. He managed to go up the big hill. All the kids clapped and cheered for the driver and his large truck.
