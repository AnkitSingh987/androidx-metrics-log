Category: Beginner
Title: Laser the Unique Toy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a unique toy named Laser. Laser was a small toy with bright colors. Everyone loved Laser because it was fun to play with. One day, Laser went to a party. At the party, Laser met a new friend named Ball. Ball was round and bouncy. Laser said, "Hi Ball, let's play together!" Ball replied, "Yes, let's play catch!" As they were playing, something unexpected happened. Laser started to shine very bright, like a real laser! All the kids at the party were surprised and excited. They all clapped and said, "Wow, Laser is so cool!" Because of the bright light, Laser and Ball became the stars of the party. Everyone wanted to play with them. The kids all took turns playing with Laser and Ball, and they all had a great time. At the end of the party, all the kids hugged Laser and Ball. They all said, "Thank you for making our party so much fun!" Laser and Ball were happy to have made so many new friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a weird sandwich on a table. He looked at it and said, "What a weird sandwich!" It was green and blue with big eyes on it. Tim didn't know what to do with the sandwich. He thought about eating it, but it looked too strange. So, he decided to keep it. Tim took the sandwich to his friend, Sue. Sue looked at the sandwich and said, "That's a weird sandwich! Let's keep it safe." But then, the sandwich started to talk! "Please, don't keep me," it said. Tim and Sue were very surprised. They didn't know that sandwiches could talk! The sandwich told Tim and Sue that it was from a far away land where all the sandwiches could talk. It was lost and wanted to go home. Tim and Sue felt bad for the sandwich. They decided to help it find its way back home. After a long walk, they found the land of talking sandwiches. The weird sandwich was so happy to be home. Tim and Sue said goodbye to their new friend and went back to their own home, feeling good about helping the weird sandwich.

Anna and Ben are playing in the park. They have a big bag of snacks. Anna is bossy. She tells Ben what to do. "Ben, give me the cookies. You can have the carrots. I like cookies more." Ben does not like carrots. He likes cookies too. He is sad. He says, "No, Anna. That is not fair. We can share the cookies. You are not the boss of me." Anna does not want to share. She grabs the bag of snacks. She runs away from Ben. She chews a cookie. She thinks she is smart. But she is not smart. She is mean. A big dog sees her. The dog likes cookies too. The dog runs after Anna. He barks loud. He wants the bag of snacks. Anna is scared. She drops the bag of snacks. The dog takes it. He runs away. He chews the cookies. He is happy. Anna cries. She has no snacks. She has no friend. She is sorry. She says, "Ben, I am sorry. I was bossy. I was mean. Can we be friends again?" Ben is kind. He says, "Yes, Anna. We can be friends again. But next time, we have to share. Sharing is nice. Sharing is fun." They hug. They are friends again. They search for more snacks. They find some apples. They share the apples. They are happy.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He wanted to travel with his mom to the big park. He put on his shoes and ran to his mom. "Mom, can we go to the park now?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go!" On the way to the park, Tim saw a tiny bug. The bug was on his finger! He showed his mom and said, "Look, Mom! A bug is on my finger!" His mom said, "Wow, that's a cute bug, Tim. Let's take it to the park with us." At the park, Tim played with the bug on the slide and swings. The bug liked to travel on Tim's finger. They had a fun day. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to the bug. "Bye, little bug. See you next time!" Tim and his mom walked home, happy and tired.
