Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Park and the Onion

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard onion. The onion lived in a little house near the park. The onion had many friends who would come and play with him every day. One day, the onion wanted to jog with his friends. They all went to the park and started to jog. They jogged up and down, round and round. The onion and his friends had so much fun jogging together. But then, the onion got tired. He jogged slower and slower. His friends did not see him and kept jogging. The onion was left all alone in the park. He was sad and cried. The onion could not find his way back home and had a bad day.

Charlie was a little girl. She was three years old and she was very curious. Every day she would ask questions. Today she went outside to find a special scarf. The scarf was light. It was bright pink with white stars all over it. Charlie thought it was very pretty. She put the scarf around her neck, but it was a little too long. She asked her mom, "Will you help me tie it?" Her mom smiled and said, "Sure, let me help you." She carefully tied the scarf around Charlie's neck. Charlie smiled and said, "Thanks mom!" She was so happy and she ran around, twirling and spinning. The scarf flowed in the wind, like a light and beautiful dance.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a mask that he loved to wear. It was not a hard mask, but a soft one. He wore it all the time, even when he went to bed. Tom liked to pretend he was a superhero when he wore his mask. One day, Tom and his friend, Sue, were playing in the park. Tom put on his mask and said, "Watch me soar like a bird!" He ran as fast as he could, flapping his arms up and down. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She said, "You fly so high, Tom!" Tom and Sue played all day, taking turns pretending to soar like birds. They laughed and had lots of fun. When the sun began to set, Tom took off his mask and said, "It's time to go home, Sue." They said goodbye and went back to their houses, ready for another day of fun and adventure.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a polite deer. The deer had many friends who liked to play with him. They played hide and seek, and they ran and jumped in the grass. One day, while the deer was playing with his friends, a big noise came from behind the trees. The deer and his friends stopped playing and looked around. They were scared because they did not know what the noise was. A little bird flew down from the tree and said, "Don't be scared. It's just my friend, the elephant, coming to say hello." The deer and his friends felt better and went to meet the elephant. They all played together and had a fun day in the big green forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a very patient girl who liked to pray every night. She had a friend named Tom, who was a happy boy, but sometimes he was a little bitter. One day, Lily and Tom went to the park to play. They saw a big tree with lots of green leaves. Lily wanted to climb the tree, but Tom was scared. Lily told him to be patient, and she would help him climb. Tom did not like waiting, and he felt a little bitter. Lily said, "Let's pray for help." So they closed their eyes and prayed. When they opened their eyes, they saw a big, strong branch that could help them climb. With patience and prayer, Lily and Tom climbed the tree together. They were very happy, and Tom was not bitter anymore.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. They see a big pile of waste near the swings. The waste is gray and smells bad. "Yuck, what is that?" Ben asks. "That is waste. People throw things they do not want there," Sam says. "Why do they do that? That is not good for the park," Ben says. "I do not know. Maybe they are lazy or do not care," Sam says. Ben and Sam do not like the waste. They want to help the park. They look for a trash can. They find one near the slide. They stand near the waste. They pick up some things and put them in the trash can. "Good job, Ben and Sam. You are helping the park," a voice says. They turn around and see a lady. She is wearing a green shirt and a hat. She has a badge that says "Park Ranger". "Who are you?" Ben asks. "I am a park ranger. I work here and take care of the park. I saw you picking up the waste. That is very kind and brave of you," the lady says. "Thank you. We like the park. We do not like the waste. It is gray and smells bad," Sam says. "You are right. The waste is bad for the park and the animals. It can make them sick or hurt them. You are doing a great thing by cleaning it up. Do you want to help me some more?" the lady asks. "Yes, please. We want to help you," Ben and Sam say. The lady smiles and gives them each a pair of gloves and a bag. They follow her and pick up more waste. They fill up the bags and put them in the trash can. They make the park cleaner and happier. They feel proud and happy too.
