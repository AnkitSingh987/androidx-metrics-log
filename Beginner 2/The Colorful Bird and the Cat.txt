Category: Beginner
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. This bird loved to sing and play in the trees. One day, the bird saw a big cat near her nest. The bird was scared for her babies. The bird decided to warn the other animals in the forest. She flew to her friends, the squirrel, the rabbit, and the deer. She told them about the big cat. They all wanted to help protect the nest. Together, they made a plan. The squirrel and the rabbit would make noise to scare the big cat away. The deer would watch the nest. The result was that the big cat left the forest. The colorful bird and her friends were happy and safe.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. But they did not like to share. They always wanted the same toy or the same spot. One day, they saw a green bike near the fence. It looked cool and shiny. They both ran to it and tried to grab it. "Mine!" Lily shouted. "No, mine!" Tom yelled. They pulled and pushed and fought over the bike. They did not see the man who owned the bike. He was angry and came to them. "Hey, stop that! That's my bike!" he said. "You are very naughty. I will punish you. You have to sit on the bench and say sorry." Lily and Tom felt scared and sorry. They let go of the bike and followed the man. They sat on the bench and said sorry to him and to each other. They learned to be nice and share. They did not fight over the bike again. They played with other toys and had fun. They were happy and the man was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very confused. He did not know how to remove his pants to use the toilet. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't do it. One day, Tim asked his mommy for help. "Mommy, can you help me remove my pants to use the toilet?" Tim asked. His mommy smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! Let me show you how." She helped him remove his pants and then he used the toilet. From that day on, Tim was no longer confused. He knew how to remove his pants all by himself to use the toilet. He was so happy and proud. Tim's mommy was happy and proud too. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy cat. This cat loved to wear a big hat. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat went for a walk. The cat saw a bird in a tree. The cat wanted to play with the bird. The cat tried to jump up to the tree. But the cat did not jump high enough. The cat started to fall. The cat fell down and hurt its lip. The cat was sad. A nice dog saw the cat. The dog helped the cat get up. The cat and the dog became friends. They played together and the cat forgot about its hurt lip. The cat was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to help his friends. One day, Max saw his friend, Sam the cat, with a big box. "Can I help you?" Max asked Sam. Sam said, "Yes, please! This is a big job for me." Max and Sam worked together to move the big box. They pushed and pulled until the box was in the right spot. Sam was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max felt good about helping his friend. He knew that even a little dog could do a big job with some help. And from that day on, Max and Sam became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her fancy doll. One day, Sue saw her friend Tim playing with a match. Sue was curious and wanted to play with it too. Tim said, "No, Sue, matches are not for playing!" Sue felt sad, but she listened to Tim. Later, they went to play with Sue's fancy doll. While they were playing, Sue's doll got a tear in her dress. Sue and Tim felt sad about the tear. Sue's mom saw them and said, "Don't worry, I can fix the tear." She fixed the fancy doll's dress, and Sue and Tim were happy again. They learned that it's important to listen to friends and not play with dangerous things like matches.
