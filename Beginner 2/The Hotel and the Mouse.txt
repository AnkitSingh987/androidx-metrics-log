Category: Beginner
Title: The Hotel and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big hotel. In this hotel, there lived a worried little mouse named Tim. Tim was always scared of the big sack in the corner of the room. He would hide from it every day. One day, Tim met another mouse named Sam. Sam asked, "Why are you so worried, Tim?" Tim said, "I am scared of the big sack in the corner. I don't know what's inside." Sam smiled and said, "Let's go look together!" Tim and Sam went to the big sack. They opened it, and inside they found yummy food! They were so happy and not worried anymore. They ate the food and played all day in the hotel. From that day on, Tim was never scared of the big sack again.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with toys and books. One day, they find a big box of string in the closet. They wonder what they can do with it. "Let's make a necklace!" Lily says. She takes a piece of string and puts a bead on it. She tries to tie a knot, but it is hard. The string slips and the bead falls off. "Let me help you," Tom says. He takes the string and makes a loop. He pulls the end through the loop and makes a knot. He shows Lily how to do it. "You have to learn how to tie a knot. It is easy and fun." Lily tries again. She makes a loop and pulls the end through. She makes a knot. She is happy. She puts another bead on the string and makes another knot. She does it again and again. She makes a long necklace. "Wow, you are good at this!" Tom says. He takes another piece of string and makes a bracelet. He puts beads and buttons on it. He makes knots too. They make more necklaces and bracelets. They use different colors and shapes of string and beads. They have fun. They are proud of their work. "Look, we made beautiful jewelry!" Lily says. She puts a necklace around her neck and a bracelet on her wrist. She gives another necklace to Tom. "This is for you. You are my dependable friend. You always help me and teach me new things." "Thank you, Lily. You are my dependable friend too. You always share with me and listen to me." Tom says. He puts the necklace around his neck and a bracelet on his wrist. He gives another bracelet to Lily. "This is for you. You are my creative friend. You always have good ideas and make nice things." They hug each other. They are happy. They learned how to tie knots and make jewelry. They learned how to be dependable and creative friends. They learned a lot from the string.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog who loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot could not find his ball. He looked and looked, but it was not in his house. He was sad and needed to find a way to find his ball. Spot thought he was ready to look outside. He put on his little red coat and ran out the door. Spot looked in the yard, under the trees, and near the flowers. He looked everywhere, but he still could not find his ball. Just when Spot was about to give up, his friend, a little bird named Tweet, flew down from the sky. Tweet saw how sad Spot was and wanted to help. Tweet said, "I know a way to find your ball!" Spot and Tweet looked everywhere together. At last, they found the ball under a big leaf. Spot was so happy and ready to play again. Spot and Tweet played with the ball all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat with a long tail. She liked to play with her tail all day. She would jump and try to catch it. One day, she saw her friend, the dog, with a warm coat. The cat wanted a warm coat too. The cat asked the dog, "How did you get that warm coat?" The dog said, "I helped the old lady. She gave me this coat to say thank you." The cat thought, "I will help the old lady too. Maybe she will give me a warm coat." The cat went to the old lady's house. She asked the cat to repeat a song. The cat sang the song again and again. The old lady was very happy. She gave the cat a warm coat, just like the dog's. The cat was very happy. She went home with her long tail and her new warm coat.
