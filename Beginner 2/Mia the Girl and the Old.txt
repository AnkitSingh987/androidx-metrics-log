Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Old

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a very old toy. It was a small, soft bear. Mia loved her bear very much. She took her bear everywhere she went. The bear was very old, but Mia did not care. One day, Mia had a wish. She wanted to make her old bear look new and pretty. Mia asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Let's decorate your bear with pretty things!" Mia was very happy. Mia and her mom found some shiny buttons, soft ribbons, and colorful paint. They started to decorate the old bear. They put shiny buttons for the bear's eyes and nose. They tied soft ribbons around the bear's neck. They painted the bear's body with colorful paint. When they were done, Mia's old bear looked new and pretty. Mia was very happy, and her wish came true.

Sam and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys, but their favorite is the train set. The train set has a big engine, some cars, and a long cord. The cord can make the train move and make sounds. One day, Sam and Mia want to play with the train set, but they cannot find the cord. They look under the bed, in the closet, and in the toy box, but the cord is not there. They are sad and confused. Where is the cord? They ask their mom for help. Mom says she knows where the cord is. She says she gave it to dad. Dad needed the cord to provide power for his laptop. His laptop is a modern machine that he uses for work. He can type, draw, and talk on his laptop. Sam and Mia are curious. They want to see dad's laptop. They follow mom to the living room, where dad is sitting on the couch with his laptop. They see the cord plugged into the wall and into the laptop. They see dad typing, drawing, and talking on the laptop. They think it is cool and fun. Dad sees Sam and Mia and smiles. He says he is sorry he took their cord, but he needed it for a while. He says he is almost done with his work, and then he will give them back their cord. He says he can also show them how to use his laptop, if they want. He says they can learn some modern things. Sam and Mia are happy. They say they want to learn some modern things. They say they can wait for their cord. They say they love dad and his laptop. They hug dad and mom. They sit on the couch and watch dad's laptop. They are excited and eager.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous little monkey named Tim. Tim lived in a big jungle with his family. He loved to play and explore all day long. One day, while Tim was playing, he saw a big bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird, but he did not know how. He thought, "I will not quit until I help my new friend." Tim had an idea. He found a big leaf and tied it to the bird's wing. The bird tried to fly, and it worked! The bird was so happy, and they played together all day. Tim was proud that he did not quit, and he had a new friend in the jungle.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a long string in his room. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim took the string and ran to Sue's house. "Look, Sue! I found a long string!" said Tim. Sue looked at the string and smiled. "Wow, that's a long string! What can we do with it?" she asked. Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a trust game. I will hold one end of the string and you will hold the other end. We will close our eyes and walk slowly. We need to trust each other to not let go." Sue agreed and they started the game. They both closed their eyes and held the string tightly. They walked slowly and carefully, trusting each other to not let go. As they walked, they felt calm and happy. They knew they could trust each other. After a while, they opened their eyes and saw that they had walked very far. They were still holding the string and had not let go. Tim and Sue were proud of themselves for trusting each other. They knew that they were very good friends. They played with the string all day, knowing that trust made their friendship strong.
