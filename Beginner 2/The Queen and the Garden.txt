Category: Beginner
Title: The Queen and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big pink palace, there lived a kind queen. She had a little girl who loved to play with her toy bunny. One day, the toy bunny was lost. The little girl was very sad. The queen wanted to help her little girl. She looked everywhere for the toy bunny. She looked in the garden, in the kitchen, and in the big pink palace. But she could not find the toy bunny. At last, the queen found the toy bunny under a big tree. The little girl was so happy! The queen hugged her and gave her the toy bunny. She comforted her little girl and said, "Don't worry, your toy bunny is safe now." The little girl smiled and hugged her toy bunny. They all lived happily in the big pink palace.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of steel. It was a strong toy. He liked to play with it all day. One day, Tim met a deaf dog. The dog could not hear. Tim wanted to help the dog relax. So, Tim took the dog to his house. Tim played with the dog and his steel toy. They had fun together. The dog relaxed and was happy. Tim was happy too. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the gym. One day, he went to the gym with his friend, Sam. They played on the slide, the swings, and the seesaw. Tim and Sam were having so much fun. After a while, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to play with it. But he felt guilty because it was not his ball. He asked Sam, "Can we play with the red ball?" Sam thought for a moment and then suggested, "Let's ask the teacher if we can play with it." So Tim and Sam went to the teacher and asked her. The teacher smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with the red ball. Just be careful and have fun." Tim and Sam played with the red ball all day long. They had so much fun at the gym. When it was time to go home, they thanked the teacher and put the red ball back where they found it. They went home feeling happy and tired from a fun day at the gym.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to the subway. The subway was big and noisy. Tim saw a broken chair near the door. He told his mom, "Look, the chair is broken!" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. The chair is old and it has rotted. We can't sit there." They found a good chair and sat down. The subway started to move fast. Tim was happy and clapped his hands. At the next stop, a kind man came in and saw the broken chair. He said, "Oh no, this chair is not good." Tim told the man, "Don't worry, you can sit with us!" The man smiled and sat next to them. They all had a fun ride on the subway together.

One day, a sweet cat named Lily was playing in the park. She liked to run and jump on the green grass. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily was very happy. Suddenly, she heard a voice from behind a tree. It was a little mouse named Timmy. He said, "Please don't hurt me, I surrender!" Lily was surprised and looked at him. She said, "I won't hurt you, Timmy. Let's be friends!" They played together in the park and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running towards them. Lily was scared, but Timmy had an idea. He said, "Lily, jump on my back and I will run fast!" Lily jumped on Timmy's back and they ran away from the big dog. They were safe and happy. From that day on, Lily and Timmy were best friends and they always played together in the park.

One day, a little girl named Mia felt very sad. She was miserable because her toy was lost. Mia looked everywhere but could not find it. Her mom saw her and knew something was wrong. Mia's mom asked, "Why are you so sad, Mia?" Mia told her mom about her lost toy. Her mom wanted to comfort her, so she said, "Let's use our brain to think of where you last saw your toy." Mia thought hard and remembered playing with her toy in the park. They went to the park, and Mia saw her toy under a big tree. She was so happy to find it! Her mom said, "See, using our brain helped us find your toy!" From that day on, Mia knew that when she was sad, she could use her brain and her mom's help to find a solution.
