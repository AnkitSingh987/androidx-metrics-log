Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and the Bird

One day, a man went to the park to relax. He sat on a bench and took off his tight shoes. He liked to feel the grass on his feet. As the man closed his eyes, he heard a voice. "Hello, man!" said a small bird. The man opened his eyes and saw the bird. He was surprised. Birds don't talk! The bird asked the man, "Why are your shoes so tight?" The man said, "I don't know, little bird. They just are." The bird thought for a bit. Then, it flew away. The bird came back with a big leaf. It told the man, "Here, use this leaf to make your shoes bigger. Then they won't be tight." The man did what the bird said. His shoes were not tight anymore. The man and the bird smiled at each other, happy with their new friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a good friend named Sam. One day, they went to the park to play. They saw a big tree and decided to climb it. As they climbed, they heard a small sound. "Help me," said a tiny bird. The bird was stuck in the tree. Tim and Sam looked at each other in silence. They knew they had to help the bird. Tim and Sam worked together to free the bird. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you for helping me." Tim and Sam felt good for doing a kind thing. They learned that it is important to help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the sun. He liked the heat on his fur. One day, Spot saw a big ball in the yard. He knew something would happen. Spot ran to the ball and started to play. He pushed it with his nose and it rolled fast. Spot was very happy. He knew his friend, Tim, would come out to play too. He waited for Tim by the ball. When Tim came out, he saw the big ball and laughed. Spot knew what would happen next. Tim kicked the ball high in the sky. Spot jumped and caught it. They played all day in the heat, having lots of fun. Spot was a loyal friend and always knew when fun would happen.

Once upon a time, there was a nervous woman. She had a big frown on her face. She walked in the park and saw a cute dog. The dog was playing with a ball. The woman wanted to play too, but she was scared. The dog saw the woman and came to her. It wagged its tail and wanted to be friends. The woman was still nervous, but she tried to be brave. She picked up the ball and threw it. The dog ran and caught the ball. They played together for a long time. The woman started to smile. She was not nervous anymore. The dog was happy too. They became good friends. From that day on, the woman did not frown anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was walking around in the forest. Suddenly, she saw an eye looking at her. She was scared. She wanted to go away, but she was too scared to move. The eye said to her, "Stay here!" The little girl was very scared. She said, "No, I don't want to stay here!" The eye said, "You must stay! I'm very dangerous!" The little girl began to cry. She said, "Please, I don't want to stay here!" But the eye said, "You must stay!" No matter what the little girl said, the eye insisted she stay. Suddenly, a voice came from the sky. It said, "Run away! Now!" The little girl ran away as fast as she could. She looked back and saw the eye still watching her. The little girl never went back to the forest again. She was so scared that she never wanted to see the dangerous eye again.

Once upon a time, in a big hotel, there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim was always hiding under chairs and tables. He was scared to talk to other animals. One day, he saw a big dog named Max. Max was very loud and liked to play a lot. Tim wanted to be friends with Max, but he was too shy. One day, Tim was playing with a ball and it rolled near Max. Tim was scared, but he went to get the ball. Max saw Tim and wanted to play too. They played with the ball together and had fun. But then, Tim accidentally scratched Max while playing. Max was sad and Tim was very sorry. Tim said, "Please forgive me, Max." Max looked at Tim and said, "It's okay, Tim. I forgive you." They both smiled and kept playing. From that day on, Tim and Max were best friends and Tim was not so shy anymore.
