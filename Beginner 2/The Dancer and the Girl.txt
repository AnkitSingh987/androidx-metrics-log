Category: Beginner
Title: The Dancer and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a dancer who could speak. He liked to talk to everyone he saw. He would dance and talk and make people smile. One day, the dancer spoke to a girl who was wearing a cheap dress. The girl told the dancer she was sad because her family couldn’t afford a nicer dress. So the dancer decided he would help her. He spoke to a friend, who also liked to dance, and asked to borrow some money. Together, they bought the girl a beautiful dress, much nicer than the cheap one she had before. The girl was so happy and she thanked the dancer. From then on, the dancer and his friend made sure she always had nice clothes to wear. Every time she passed them in the street she waved, happy as can be. The end.

Tim and Kim are friends. They like to play with toys in Tim's room. Tim has a big rug on the floor. It is soft and warm. Kim likes the rug too. One day, Kim comes to play with Tim. But Tim is not happy. He says, "This is my rug. You cannot sit on it. You can sit on the hard floor." Kim is sad. She does not want to sit on the floor. She wants to share the rug with Tim. Kim says, "Please, Tim, can we share the rug? It is big enough for both of us. We can play better on the rug. It is not nice to be selfish." But Tim says, "No, Kim, this is my rug. Go away. I do not want to play with you." Kim is very sad. She goes away. She does not want to be Tim's friend anymore. She goes to her house. She tells her mom what happened. Her mom hugs her and says, "I am sorry, Kim. Tim was not kind to you. He should have offered you some of his rug. He should have been grateful for your friendship. Maybe he will learn his lesson." The next day, Tim is alone in his room. He has no one to play with. He looks at his rug. He thinks, "Maybe I was wrong. Maybe I should have shared my rug with Kim. She is a good friend. She always shares her toys with me. I miss her. I wish she would come back." He decides to call Kim. He says, "Kim, I am sorry. I was selfish. I should have offered you some of my rug. I was not grateful for your friendship. Can you please come back and play with me? I will share my rug with you." Kim is happy. She says, "Thank you, Tim. I forgive you. I will come back and play with you. I am glad you learned your lesson. Sharing is caring. Friends are more important than rugs." They go to Tim's room. They sit on the rug together. They play with toys. They laugh and have fun. They are both grateful for each other. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a toy pistol that he liked to play with all the time. One day, Tom's mom told him he needed to clean his room. Tom didn't want to clean, so he thought of a plan. The next day, Tom's mom asked him again to clean his room. Tom took out his toy pistol and said, "I demand you let me play!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, you can play for a little bit, but then you must clean your room." Tom played with his toy pistol for a while, but then he remembered his mom's words. He put his pistol down and started to clean his room. When he was done, his room was very clean and his mom was happy. Tom learned that it was good to listen to his mom and do what she asked.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Billy. He loved to play in the park and he especially liked playing on the swing. One day when Billy went to the park, he saw another boy, Ben, already there. Ben was playing on the swings and singing a happy song. Billy invited Ben to play with him and they quickly became friends. They had lots of fun together and laughed a lot. But then, Ben started to spread some rude words. Billy felt sad and tried to tell Ben to stop, but Ben didn't listen. Billy got very angry and shouted, "That was not nice! No more rude words, please!" Ben looked at Billy and said, "I'm sorry. I'll never say those words again." Billy and Ben played together for the rest of the day and had lots of fun. They spread only kind words from then on and became very good friends.
