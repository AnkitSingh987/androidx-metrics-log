Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Stubborn Boy and the Ladder

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. He climbed on chairs, tables, and trees. One day, Tim went to the opera with his mom and dad. The opera was a big place with many people. At the opera, Tim saw a tall ladder. He wanted to climb it. His mom and dad said, "No, Tim. It is not safe to climb." But Tim was stubborn and did not listen. He waited until his mom and dad were not looking. Tim started to climb the tall ladder. He climbed higher and higher. Suddenly, the ladder began to shake. Tim was scared. The ladder fell, and Tim fell too. He got hurt and cried. Mom and dad came running to help Tim. They were sad that Tim did not listen to them. Tim learned that being stubborn can lead to bad things happening.

Mia likes to mark on the wall with her crayons. She makes red and blue and green marks. She thinks they are pretty. But Mom does not like the marks. She says they are messy and bad. "Mia, you have to stop marking on the wall. You have to be obedient and listen to me. You have to clean the marks with a sponge and soap. Do you understand?" Mom says. Mia nods. She does not want to make Mom angry. She takes the sponge and soap and tries to clean the wall. But the marks are hard to remove. She rubs and rubs, but they are still there. Mia feels sad. She does not know what to do. She sees the bathtub in the bathroom. She has an idea. She fills the bathtub with water. She puts the sponge and soap in the water. She takes her crayons and goes to the bathtub. She marks on the bathtub with her crayons. She makes yellow and pink and purple marks. She thinks they are pretty. She says, "Look, Mom, I made a new picture for you. It is in the bathtub. Do you like it?" Mom hears Mia and goes to the bathroom. She sees the bathtub full of water and marks. She is surprised and angry. She says, "Mia, what did you do? You made a big mess in the bathtub. You wasted water and soap. You have to be obedient and listen to me. You have to empty the bathtub and clean the marks. Do you understand?" Mia nods. She feels sorry. She does not want to make Mom angry. She empties the bathtub and cleans the marks. She says, "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted to make you happy. I like to mark. It is fun." Mom sighs. She hugs Mia. She says, "I know you like to mark, Mia. But you have to mark on paper, not on the wall or the bathtub. Paper is for marking. Wall and bathtub are not for marking. Do you understand?" Mia nods. She says, "I understand, Mom. I will mark on paper. I will be obedient and listen to you." Mom smiles. She says, "That's good, Mia. I love you. You are a good girl. Now, let's go and find some paper and crayons for you. You can mark on them and make me a new picture. How about that?" Mia smiles. She says, "Yes, Mom. I love you too. Let's go and mark on paper."

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to ring a bell. Every day, Fluffy would ring the bell when it was time for class. All the little animals would come to class to learn and play. One day, Fluffy said to a small dog named Spot, "I will ring the bell for class now." Spot replied, "Okay, Fluffy, I will come to class too." Fluffy rang the bell, and all the little animals came to class, just like they always did. But this time, when Fluffy rang the bell, it was very loud. The loud noise scared all the little animals away. They ran far from the class and did not come back. Fluffy was sad that the animals did not want to learn and play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious pond. In the pond, there was a big fish. The big fish liked to provide food for all the little fish. The little fish were very happy. One day, a big bird came to the pond. The big bird wanted to eat the little fish. The little fish were scared. The big fish wanted to help the little fish. The big fish had a plan. The big fish told the little fish to hide in the pond plants. The big bird could not find the little fish. The big bird was sad and flew away. The little fish were safe and happy. The big fish was their hero.
