Category: Beginner
Title: The Wealthy Lady and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy lady. She had a big yard. In the yard, she kept many animals. One day, a little boy came to the yard. He saw a bird in a cage. The bird looked sad. The boy said, "Why is the bird sad?" The lady said, "The bird wants to fly, but it is in a cage." The boy wanted to help the bird. He opened the cage and said, "Go, bird, go!" The bird was happy to be free. It flew away. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird came back with more birds. They all danced and sang in the yard. The lady was happy and said, "Thank you, little boy, for helping the bird. Now we all can enjoy their songs."

Once upon a time there was a little boy who was sad. He wanted to do something nice, but he didn't know what. Then, he had an idea. He went to his mom and suggested that they could build a pipe. She thought it was a nice idea and went to get the supplies. The boy and his mom brought the pipe and a hammer and some nails. They worked for a few hours and finally the little pipe was finished. It looked so nice! The little boy was so proud of it. He wanted to show it to everyone. So the next day, the little boy suggested to his mom that they could set up the pipe outside. She agreed and brought it outside for everyone to enjoy. The little boy was so happy that he could have a nice pipe of his own. He suggested to his mom that they could paint the pipe and she thought it was a great idea. He and his mom spent the day together painting the pipe in all the colors of the rainbow. When they were done, it looked even nicer than before. The little boy was so proud of it and he knew he had made a nice pipe.

Once there was a girl named Jane. Jane had a pretty blouse. She loved to wear it every day. One day, she got a letter in the mail. The letter said she had to go to a boring party. Jane did not want to go, but her mom said she had to. At the party, Jane wore her pretty blouse. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom was sad. Jane asked, "Why are you sad?" Tom said, "This party is boring." Jane agreed, but she had an idea. Jane said, "Let's make the party fun!" They played games and danced. Soon, the boring party was fun. Jane and Tom were happy. Jane's pretty blouse made her feel good, and the mail brought her a new friend.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play outside in summer. Summer is hot and sunny. They can run, jump, swim and have fun. One day, they see a big lake. The lake is blue and shiny. They want to swim in the lake. But the lake is dangerous. There are signs that say "No swimming". There are also big rocks and deep water. "Let's swim in the lake!" Tom says. He takes off his shoes and runs to the lake. "No, Tom, stop!" Mia says. She runs after him. She wants to prevent him from swimming. She knows the lake is dangerous. She is scared for Tom. She catches Tom and pulls him back. "Tom, you can't swim in the lake. It's dangerous. You can get hurt or drown. The signs say no swimming. We have to listen to the signs. They are there to keep us safe." Tom looks at Mia. He sees that she is worried. He feels sorry. He says, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't know the lake was dangerous. I just wanted to have fun. Thank you for stopping me. You are a good friend." Mia smiles. She says, "You're welcome, Tom. You are a good friend too. Let's go and find another place to play. Maybe we can find a pool or a sprinkler. They are safer and fun too." Tom and Mia hold hands and walk away from the lake. They are happy. They have learned to prevent danger and to listen to signs. They still have a lot of fun in summer.
