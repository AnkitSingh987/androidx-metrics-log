Category: Beginner
Title: The Butterfly and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a pretty butterfly. The butterfly had dirty wings because it played near a muddy puddle. The butterfly wanted to fly high in the sky, but it could not. Its wings were too dirty and heavy. One day, a small boy named Tim saw the butterfly. He said, "Oh no, butterfly! Your wings are dirty. I will help clean them so you can fly." Tim used a soft cloth and water to gently clean the butterfly's wings. The butterfly was very happy. Tim told the butterfly, "Now you can fly high, but be careful. Stay away from the muddy puddle to protect your wings." The butterfly listened to Tim and flew high in the sky, away from the muddy puddle. From that day on, the butterfly always stayed clean and protected its wings, just like Tim had said. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to explore and play outside. One day, he found a big cave in the woods. He was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. As Tim walked into the cave, he heard a whistle. He looked around and saw a small bird. The bird said, "Hello, I'm Blue! Do you want to be friends?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to be friends, Blue!" Together, Tim and Blue explored the cave. They found pretty stones and played fun games. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Bye, Blue! I'll come back to play again soon!" Blue whistled happily and waved goodbye. Tim ran home to tell his mom about his new friend and the amazing cave he found.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat. The cat liked to play with a ball made of metal. One day, the cat was playing and hurt its paw. The cat was sad because it could not play with the metal ball anymore. The cat's friend, a kind dog, saw the cat was sad. The dog wanted to help the cat feel better. The dog knew that if the cat could heal, it would be happy again. So, the dog stayed with the cat and took care of it. After some days, the cat's paw started to heal. The cat was happy and could play with the metal ball again. The gray cat and the kind dog played together and became best friends. They were happy and had lots of fun every day.

One day, a gentle lion named Leo found a big bottle of glue. He didn't know what it was, so he asked his friend, the bird, "What is this?" The bird said, "That is glue. It makes things stick together." Leo wanted to try the glue. He took a little stick and put some glue on it. Then, he found another stick and put it on the first one. They stuck together! Just then, a big wind came and blew the sticks away. Leo was sad, but he knew what to do. He took a deep breath and roared very loud. The wind stopped, and the sticks came back. Leo was happy. He played with the glue and sticks all day, making fun shapes and showing them to his friends.

Tom and Lily were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a pond with ducks and fish. Tom wanted to swim with them. He said, "I can swim. I am brave. Come with me, Lily." Lily was scared. She said, "No, Tom. The pond is big and deep. You can't swim. You are not brave. You are silly. Stay with me, Tom." But Tom did not listen. He ran to the pond and jumped in. He splashed and kicked. He said, "Look, Lily. I am swimming. I am brave. Come with me, Lily." Lily was worried. She said, "Tom, come back. The pond is cold and dirty. You can't swim. You are not brave. You are in trouble. Come back, Tom." But Tom did not hear. He saw a red berry on a bush. He wanted to eat it. He said, "Look, Lily. I see a berry. I am hungry. I will get it, Lily." Lily was scared. She said, "No, Tom. The berry is bad and sour. You can't eat it. You are not hungry. You are foolish. Come back, Tom." But Tom did not care. He swam to the bush and reached for the berry. He did not see the bee. The bee was angry. It stung Tom on the nose. Tom cried. He said, "Ow, ow, ow. The berry is bad and the bee is mean. I am not brave. I am sorry. Help me, Lily." Lily was sad. She ran to the pond and helped Tom. She said, "Tom, you are hurt and wet. You are not silly or foolish. You are my friend. I will help you, Tom." She took Tom to the bench and gave him a hug. She said, "Tom, you are brave. But you are also silly. Next time, listen to me, Tom. I know what is good and bad for you, Tom." Tom nodded. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are smart and kind. You are my friend. I will listen to you, Lily." They smiled and hugged. They learned a lesson. They were friends.
