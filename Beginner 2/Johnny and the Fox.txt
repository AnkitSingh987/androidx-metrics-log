Category: Beginner
Title: Johnny and the Fox

One day, Johnny went for a walk in the dark woods. Everywhere he looked, he could see dark trees and bushes. He walked and walked, but it was all the same. Suddenly, he heard something. It was a soft, gentle whistle coming from somewhere deep in the woods. Johnny decided to follow the sound, and he started to walk in its direction. The whistle grew louder and louder, and soon he spotted a clearing in the woods. In the middle of the clearing was a big tree and standing next to it was a fox. The fox was whistling softly and when it saw Johnny, it stopped and gave him a friendly welcome. Johnny was surprised and scared, but the fox seemed friendly. Johnny waved and smiled at the fox, and it smiled back. The two of them were now friends, and it was the start of a beautiful friendship.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to walk in the park with Mom and Dad. They see many things in the park. They see trees and flowers and birds and squirrels. They also see a big pond with ducks and fish. One day, they see something new in the park. They see a deer. The deer is brown and has antlers. The deer is very shy and runs away when it sees people. Lily and Ben want to see the deer better. They follow the deer quietly. But the deer is hurt. It has a bad cut on its leg. It bleeds and limps. Lily and Ben feel sad for the deer. They want to help the deer. They call Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad see the deer too. They say the deer is poor and needs a vet. They call the park ranger. The park ranger comes with a truck and a net. He catches the deer gently and puts it in the truck. He says he will take the deer to the vet. He says the vet will fix the deer's leg and make it well. He says he will bring the deer back to the park when it is better. Lily and Ben are happy. They hope the deer will be well soon. They say goodbye to the deer and thank the park ranger. They go back to the pond and feed the ducks. They say they will come back to the park and look for the deer again. They love the park and the animals.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big green jeep on the street. The jeep was shiny and clean. Tim thought it was the best jeep he had ever seen. He wanted to look at it all day. A kind man named Mr. Brown saw Tim looking at the jeep. He walked over and said, "Hi, little boy! Do you like my green jeep?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes! I love your jeep! It is so big and green!" Mr. Brown was happy that Tim liked his jeep. Mr. Brown asked Tim if he wanted to sit in the jeep. Tim was very excited and said, "Yes, please!" He climbed into the big green jeep and pretended to drive. Tim and Mr. Brown laughed and had a fun time. After a while, Tim's mom called him home. Tim said goodbye to Mr. Brown and his green jeep. He couldn't wait to tell his friends about the big green jeep he saw today.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bookshelf in his room. He loved to read books and play with his toys. One day, Tim was playing with his ball. He threw the ball and it started to roll. The ball rolled and rolled until it hit the bookshelf. Tim was scared. He thought the bookshelf might fall. But, he was lucky. The bookshelf did not fall. Tim was happy. Tim learned that he should be careful when playing near the bookshelf. He did not want the books to fall or the bookshelf to break. From that day on, Tim played with his ball far away from the bookshelf. He knew that it was important to be safe and protect his books. And that was the moral value Tim learned: to always be careful and protect the things he loved.
