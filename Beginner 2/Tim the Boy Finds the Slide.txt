Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Slide

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide. It was very rare, because it was red and blue. Tim was very happy to see the slide. He wanted to play on it. Tim went up the slide. He tried to resist going down too fast. He held on tight to the sides. But he could not stop. He went down the slide very fast. He laughed and had fun. Tim played on the slide all day. He did not want to go home. The rare slide made him very happy. His mom said it was time to go. Tim said bye to the slide and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was a very lucky girl. She had a big box of yummy cookies. Lucy wanted to share her cookies with her friends. Lucy decided to sell her cookies to her friends. She put the cookies on a big table outside her house. She called out, "Come and buy my yummy cookies!" Her friends came and bought the cookies. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Lucy had no more cookies left. She felt very lucky to have such good friends. They all enjoyed the cookies together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went to play outside. They saw a big, round thing in the sky. It was a planet, but it was unknown to them. Lily and Max wanted to go there. Lily said, "Max, let's build a big thing to take us to the unknown planet!" Max barked and wagged his tail. They worked hard and made a big thing out of boxes and sticks. When it was done, they got in and said, "Up, up, up!" The big thing did not move. Just then, a friendly alien named Zara came down from the sky. She said, "Hello, I am from the unknown planet. I can help you go there. But first, we must order some special fuel." Lily and Max were excited. Zara used her phone to order the special fuel. When it came, they put it in the big thing. Then, they all got in and said, "Up, up, up!" This time, the big thing went up into the sky. They went to the unknown planet and had a fun day playing with Zara and her alien friends. At the end of the day, Zara said, "It's time to go home now." Lily and Max were sad, but they knew they had to go. They said goodbye to their new friends and went back to Earth. They were happy because they had a great adventure and made new friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big ashtray in his house. He didn't know what it was. He was ignorant about it. He thought it was a toy. Tim played with the ashtray all day. Tim's mom saw him play with the ashtray. She told Tim that it was not a toy. Tim was sad. He wanted to play with it. But his mom had an idea. She said they could play a game. If Tim could win the game, he could keep the ashtray. They played a game of hide and seek. Tim was very good at it. He hid behind a tree. His mom could not find him. Tim won the game. His mom let him keep the ashtray. Tim was very happy. He learned something new and had fun with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim was a happy infant who loved to smile and laugh. One day, Tim saw a big, sad dog sitting alone in the park. The dog had a frown on his face. Tim wanted to make the dog happy. The next day, Tim asked his mom for a ball. He had a plan to make the sad dog happy. His mom gave him a bright, red ball. Tim was excited to go back to the park with the ball. When they got to the park, Tim rolled the ball to the sad dog. The dog's frown turned into a big smile. He wagged his tail and played with the ball. Tim and the dog became best friends. From that day on, they played together in the park, and the dog was never sad again.
