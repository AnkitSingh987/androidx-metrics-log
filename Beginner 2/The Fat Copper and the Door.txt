Category: Beginner
Title: The Fat Copper and the Door

Once there was a fat copper who wanted to come and play. He came up to a door and knocked, but no one answered. The fat copper was sad, so he kept walking and found another door. He knocked again, but again no one answered. The fat copper was about to give up, when he came across a third door. He knocked one more time and this time someone opened the door. The fat copper smiled and started to come inside when suddenly the door slammed shut in his face. The fat copper was never able to come in the house and he was alone again.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and balance on things. One day, Fluffy went outside to play in the sun. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As Fluffy climbed the tree, she found a yummy apple. She also saw a cobweb near the apple. Fluffy wanted the apple, but she didn't want to touch the cobweb. So, she thought of a plan. Fluffy tried to balance on a branch and reach for the apple. But, surprise! The cobweb was not a cobweb at all. It was a string that her friend, Bunny, had put there. Bunny jumped out from behind the tree and said, "I got you, Fluffy!" They both laughed and shared the yummy apple together. And from that day on, Fluffy and Bunny played more fun tricks on each other.

One day, a bossy cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a little girl with gum. Tom wanted the gum, so he went up to the girl and said, "Give me your gum, please." The girl was scared of the bossy cat, so she gave Tom the gum. Tom was happy and went back to his house to sort his toys. He sorted his toys into big toys and small toys. While sorting, he put the gum in his mouth. Later, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came to play. Max saw the gum and said, "I want gum too!" Tom remembered he had more gum at home. So they went to Tom's house and shared the gum. They played and had fun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big toy car that he loved to play with in the garage. One day, Tim's mom said they were going to visit his grandma. Tim was so excited that he forgot his toy car in the garage. At grandma's house, Tim met a new friend named Max. They played with Max's toys, but Tim started to miss his toy car. He told Max about the car he left in the garage. Max had a secret: he had a magic toy car that could fly. Max decided to share his magic car with Tim and they flew to Tim's house to get the car. When they got to Tim's house, they found out that Tim's toy car was now spoiled. It had been left in the rain and was all wet. Tim was sad, but Max had an idea. They used the magic car to fix Tim's car and make it new again. Tim learned that even when unexpected things happen, friends can help make everything better.

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the yard. They have a big truck and many sticks. They load the sticks on the truck and drive around. One day, they see a hairy dog. The dog is friendly and wags its tail. Ben and Sam want to pet the dog, but they are afraid. They hide behind the truck. "Who are you?" the dog asks. He has a deep voice. "We are Ben and Sam," Ben says. He is brave. "What are you doing?" the dog asks. "We are loading sticks on the truck," Sam says. He is shy. "Can I help?" the dog asks. Ben and Sam look at each other. They nod. They like the dog. "Sure, you can help," Ben says. The dog is happy. He picks up a stick with his mouth and puts it on the truck. Ben and Sam clap their hands. They laugh. They load more sticks on the truck. The dog helps them. They have fun. They become friends. They name the dog Max. They play with him every day. They love him. He loves them. They are a happy family.
