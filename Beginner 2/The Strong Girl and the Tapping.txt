Category: Beginner
Title: The Strong Girl and the Tapping

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl. She loved to learn and explore the world. Every day her mommy taught her something new. Today, mommy taught her how to tap her foot. First she showed her how to tap her toes. Then she taught her how to tap her heels. She was a quick learner and soon she was tapping with both feet. Mommy clapped when she saw how strong her tapping was. Then she taught her daughter a fun game. She said, "Dance around the room, tapping your feet. Keep tapping until you get dizzy!" The girl laughed and started to tap. She twirled and spun around the room, tapping her feet. Soon she realized she was getting sleepy. Mommy said it was time for bed and she hugged her tight. The girl went off to sleep, dreaming of taps and twirls. She felt so lucky to have her mama to teach her fun things.

Ben and Lily liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big box of soldiers. The soldiers were green and brown and had guns and hats. Ben and Lily made forts and battles with the soldiers on the floor. One day, Ben and Lily had a fight. Ben wanted to use all the green soldiers, but Lily wanted some too. They pulled and pushed and shouted at each other. "Give me the green soldiers!" Ben said. "No, they are mine!" Lily said. They did not share or take turns. Then, Ben did a bad thing. He grabbed a green soldier from Lily's hand and tore it. The soldier's arm came off and fell on the floor. Lily was very sad and angry. She cried and hit Ben. "You are mean! You broke my soldier!" she said. Ben felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to break the soldier. He wanted to make Lily happy again. He looked at the soldier's arm and had an idea. He ran to his room and got some tape. He came back and said to Lily, "I am sorry, Lily. I was bad. I can fix your soldier. Look, I have tape. Can I try?" Lily stopped crying and looked at Ben. She saw the tape and the soldier's arm. She was still mad, but she wanted her soldier back. She nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. You can try. But be careful. He is my good soldier." Ben smiled and took the soldier and the tape. He put the arm back on the soldier and wrapped the tape around it. He gave the soldier back to Lily and said, "Here, Lily. Your soldier is fixed. He is good again. Can we play together?" Lily took the soldier and looked at him. He was not the same as before, but he was still her soldier. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are good too. Yes, we can play together. But we have to share and take turns." Ben and Lily hugged and said sorry to each other. They put the soldiers back in the box and made a new game. They were happy and friends again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big ice cream truck with a big cone on top. Tim wanted an ice cream cone, so he asked his mom, "Mom, can I have an ice cream cone, please?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. You can have one." Tim and his mom went to the truck and got a big ice cream cone. Tim was so happy. They sat on a bench to relax and enjoy the ice cream. But then, a big, filthy dog came running to them. The dog jumped on Tim and licked his ice cream cone! "Oh no, my ice cream!" Tim cried. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can get another one." But when they went back to the truck, the ice cream man said, "I'm sorry, we just sold the last cone." Tim was sad, but then the ice cream man said, "Wait! I have a surprise for you." He gave Tim a big cup of ice cream with a cherry on top. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, ice cream man!" Tim and his mom went back to the bench to relax and enjoy their ice cream.
