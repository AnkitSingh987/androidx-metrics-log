Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Door

Lily and Ben were playing in the yard. They liked to run and jump and chase each other. They saw a big garage near the fence. It was old and rough and had many holes. "Let's go inside the garage," Ben said. "Maybe we can find something fun." Lily was scared. She did not like the garage. It looked dark and dirty and scary. "No, Ben, we can't go there," Lily said. "Mommy said it is not safe. There might be spiders or rats or nails." Ben did not listen. He wanted to see the garage. He ran to the door and pulled it open. It made a loud noise. "Come on, Lily, don't be a baby," Ben said. "It's just a garage. There's nothing to be afraid of." Lily did not want to lose Ben. She loved her brother. She followed him to the garage. She leaned on the door to close it behind her. She hoped Mommy would not be angry. Inside the garage, it was dark and cold. They could not see much. They heard strange sounds. They felt rough things on the floor and the walls. "Ben, I don't like it here," Lily said. "Let's go back to the yard. Please, Ben, please." Ben did not answer. He was looking for something fun. He saw a shiny thing on a shelf. He reached for it. It was a can of paint. He did not know what it was. "Look, Lily, I found something cool," Ben said. He shook the can. It made a rattling noise. He liked the noise. He wanted to see what was inside. He opened the can. He saw a bright color. He smiled. He wanted to paint something. He saw a wall. He sprayed the paint on the wall. It made a hissing noise. He liked the noise. He made a big mess. Lily saw what Ben was doing. She was shocked. She knew it was wrong. She knew Mommy would be very angry. "Ben, stop it, stop it, stop it," Lily said. She ran to Ben. She tried to take the can from him. "You can't do that, Ben. You can't paint the wall. It's not ours. It's bad, Ben, bad." Ben did not want to stop. He liked the paint. He liked the color. He liked the mess. He pushed Lily away. He sprayed the paint on Lily. It made a hissing noise. He laughed. Lily felt the paint on her face and her clothes. It was wet and sticky and smelly. It hurt her eyes and her nose and her mouth. She screamed. She cried. She was scared and sad and angry. "Ben, you're mean, you're mean, you're mean," Lily said. She ran to the door. She wanted to get out of the garage. She wanted to go to Mommy. She leaned on the door. It did not open. It was stuck. She leaned harder. It did not move. She was trapped. She banged on the door. She yelled for help. She hoped Mommy would hear her. She hoped Mommy would come. She hoped Mommy would save her. But Mommy did not hear her. Mommy did not come. Mommy did not save her. Mommy was in the house. She was on the phone. She was talking to a friend. She did not know what Ben and Lily were doing. She did not know they were in the garage. She did not know they were in trouble. She did not know until it was too late. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a helpful girl named Mia. She loved to laugh and make others laugh. Mia had a toy knife that she played with all the time. She liked to pretend she was a cook, cutting up pretend food for her family and friends. One sunny day, Mia and her friends were playing outside. They were having so much fun, running and laughing together. Mia had her toy knife with her, and she showed it to her friends. They all thought it was very cool and wanted to play with it too. Mia and her friends started to play a game where they pretended to cut up food for a big party. They were laughing and having a great time. But then, something unexpected happened. A real knife, that looked just like Mia's toy knife, was mixed up with their toys. Mia accidentally picked up the real knife and cut her finger. She started to cry, and her friends were very scared. Mia's mom came out and took her to the doctor. From that day on, Mia never played with her toy knife again.
