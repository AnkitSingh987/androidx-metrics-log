Category: Beginner
Title: The Bright Sun and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a nice day. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They liked to play together. They went to the park and saw a big box. "What is that?" Tim asked. Spot barked, and Tim said, "Let's open it!" They opened the box and found a surprise inside. It was a big, bouncy ball! Tim said, "Let's play, Spot!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the ball and said, "I want to play too!" Tim said, "Okay, let's all play together!" They all played and had a great time. And that is the end of the story.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily had a dream. In her dream, she saw a big insect. The insect was green and had long legs. Lily was frightened, but the insect said, "Don't be scared, I am a nice insect." Lily and the insect talked and played. They jumped and ran in the grass. The insect showed Lily how to climb trees. Lily was happy and not scared anymore. She said, "You are a good friend, Mr. Insect." When Lily woke up from her dream, she told her mom about the insect. Her mom smiled and said, "See, not all insects are scary. Some can be your friends." Lily nodded and was not frightened of insects anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The fat cat liked to play at night. The fat cat had big eyes that would blink in the dark. One night, the fat cat went outside to play. The fat cat saw a big tree. The fat cat blinked and started to climb the big tree. The fat cat was very happy. At the top of the tree, the fat cat saw a big moon. The fat cat blinked at the moon and smiled. The fat cat had fun playing in the tree all night. When the sun came up, the fat cat went back home to sleep.

Sara loves her daddy very much. He is kind and funny and plays with her every day. He also works hard to support Sara and mommy. He brings home money and food and toys for them. But one day, daddy comes home very late and very angry. He yells at mommy and Sara and breaks things. He smells bad and says bad words. Sara is scared and sad. She does not understand why daddy is so bad. Mommy tells Sara to go to her room and lock the door. She says she will try to calm daddy down and make him stop. Sara does what mommy says, but she hears loud noises and cries from downstairs. She wishes daddy would be good again. She finds her teddy bear and hugs it tight. She tells teddy that she loves him and he loves her. She says teddy will always support her and be her friend. She hopes teddy can help her and mommy be safe and happy. She prays for daddy to be sorry and change. She falls asleep with teddy in her arms.

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big house with a fence. Lila says, "Who lives there?" Ben says, "I don't know. Maybe someone rich." They want to see the house. They climb over the fence. They run to the door. They knock. No one answers. They try to open the door. It is locked. They look for a window. They find one. It is open. They climb in. The house is very nice. There are many toys and books and clothes. Lila and Ben are happy. They play with the toys. They read the books. They try on the clothes. They forget about the time. Suddenly, they hear a loud noise. It is a car. It stops in front of the house. Lila and Ben are scared. They run to the window. They see a man and a woman. They look angry. They are the owners of the house. They have a big dog. The dog barks. Lila and Ben try to climb out of the window. But the dog sees them. The dog runs to them. The dog bites Lila's leg. Lila screams. Ben tries to help her. But the man and the woman catch them. They call the police. They are very mad. They say, "You are bad kids. You broke into our house. You stole our things. You will go to jail." Lila and Ben are very sad. They cry. They say, "We are sorry. We just wanted to see the house. We didn't mean to hurt anything." But the man and the woman don't listen. They wait for the police. Lila's leg hurts. Ben's heart hurts. They wish they never climbed over the fence. They wish they never saw the big house. They wish they stayed in the park.
