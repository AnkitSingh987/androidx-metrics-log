Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Park

Sara and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to imagine they were pirates looking for treasure. They had a map and a shovel and a big hat. "Look, Sara, I found a coin!" Ben said, holding up a shiny metal thing. "Maybe it is gold!" "Wow, Ben, that is perfect!" Sara said. "Let me see it!" They took turns looking at the coin. It had a picture of a man and some letters on it. They did not know what they meant. "Maybe this is the king of the pirates!" Ben said. "Maybe this is his secret message!" Sara said. They decided to keep the coin as a souvenir. They put it in their pocket and continued their adventure. They imagined they saw a ship, a parrot and a crocodile. They had a lot of fun.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a whistle and a spin toy. The whistle makes a loud sound. The spin toy has many colors. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide. They want to try it. They take turns to go down the slide. They have fun. But then, a mean boy comes. He sees their whistle and spin toy. He wants them. He says, "Give me your whistle and spin toy. They are mine now." Tom and Mia are upset. They say, "No, they are ours. We got them from our moms. Go away." The mean boy does not listen. He grabs their whistle and spin toy. He runs away. He laughs. Tom and Mia cry. They want their whistle and spin toy back. They tell their moms what happened. Their moms are angry. They look for the mean boy. But they cannot find him. He is gone. Tom and Mia are sad. They have no whistle and spin toy. They have no fun. They go home. They have a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a charming girl called Little Star. She was only three years old and loved to play outside. One day, Little Star decided to explore the room above her. She climbed the shelf and saw a beautiful box, which she decided to open. Suddenly, a toy gun shot out and Little Star screamed in surprise. "No no!" shouted her mother. "Don't shoot the gun!" But it was too late. Little Star had already pulled the trigger, and a bullet flew through the air. When the dust settled, Little Star was shocked to see what she had done. She had shot a hole in her mum's favourite vase. Tears filled Little Star's eyes and her mum was so angry that she sent her straight to bed, without any dinner. What a bad ending for Little Star!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to act. She would pretend to be a princess, a doctor, or a teacher. One day, Sue found a phone. It was big and blue. She was very happy. Sue wanted to play with the phone. She acted like a doctor and called her mom. "Hello, this is Doctor Sue," she said. Her mom laughed and played along. Sue felt like a real doctor. But Sue knew the phone was not hers. She wanted to be honest. She told her mom about the phone. Her mom was proud of her for being honest. They found the owner of the phone and gave it back. Sue felt good and her mom gave her a big hug.

Anna and Ben were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and bridges. But Ben was clumsy. He often knocked down his blocks or Anna's blocks by mistake. "Ben, be careful!" Anna said. "You will break our blocks!" "I'm sorry, Anna," Ben said. "I will try to be more careful." But Ben did not try hard enough. He moved his arm too fast and hit a big tower of blocks. The tower fell down with a loud crash. Some of the blocks broke into pieces. There was a big crack in one of the blocks. "Ben, look what you did!" Anna cried. "You broke my favorite block! It had a star on it!" "I'm sorry, Anna," Ben said again. "I didn't mean to." Anna was very angry. She picked up the broken block and threw it at Ben. It hit him on the head. "Ow!" Ben said. "That hurt!" Anna did not answer. She was too mad. She grabbed her blocks and ran away. Ben felt sad. He rubbed his head. He looked at the broken block. He wished he could fix it. He wished he could fix his friendship with Anna. He wondered if she would ever play with him again.
