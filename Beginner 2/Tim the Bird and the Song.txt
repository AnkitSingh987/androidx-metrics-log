Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was very nervous. He was scared to display his beautiful song to the others. Tim lived with a group of other birds who loved to sing. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Tim, why don't you sing with us?" Tim was scared. He said, "I am too nervous to sing. What if I don't sound good?" Sam smiled and said, "We all had to start somewhere. Just try!" The next day, the group of birds started to sing. Tim took a deep breath and began to sing too. He displayed his song, and it was beautiful. The other birds cheered for Tim. He was so happy that he wasn't nervous anymore. Tim learned that it was okay to be scared, but trying new things can be fun.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a row of fake flowers. They were very pretty. Tim picked one up and said, "Wow, these are nice!" A little girl named Sue walked over. She saw the fake flowers too. She said, "I like these flowers. They will not rot." Tim and Sue played with the fake flowers. They made a big circle with them. They sat in the circle and talked. They were happy because the fake flowers would not rot. They knew they could play with them for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to go for walks with her mom. One day, they went for a walk near a big structure. The structure was a tall building. Mia saw the tall building and was glad. She pointed at the structure and said, "Look, Mom! Big!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, that is a big building." They walked closer to the big building. Mia was very happy. She pointed at the building again and said, "I like big building, Mom!" Her mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it, Mia." They had a fun day looking at the big building together.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the garden. Every day, he would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Spot found a big ball in the garden. He wanted to play with it, so he used his nose to push it around. The ball rolled and bounced, and Spot chased after it, laughing and barking. Spot pushed the ball all around the garden. He played with the ball until he was very tired. Then, he lay down in the soft grass, happy and sleepy. Spot loved his garden and his new ball, and he knew he would have many more fun days there.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and puzzles. But they also like to explore the house. Sometimes they find new things to see and touch. One day, they see a shiny key on the table. They wonder what it can unlock. They take the key and go to the door. They try to unlock the door, but it is too high. They need something to stand on. They look around and see a big box. They push the box to the door and climb on it. Now they can reach the door. They put the key in the lock and turn it. The door opens. They are very happy. They go out of the door and into the hall. They see many other doors. They want to unlock them too. They run to the first door and try the key. It does not work. They try the second door. It does not work either. They try the third door. It works. They unlock the door and go inside. They see a big bed and a closet and a dresser. They see many clothes on the bed and in the closet. They like the colors and the patterns. They want to try them on. They take off their shirts and put on different shirts. They laugh and dance and look at themselves in the mirror. They think they look very funny. They hear a noise. It is Mom. She is coming up the stairs. She smells something yummy. She has made cookies for them. She calls their names. They hear her and stop. They are scared. They know they are not supposed to go out of their room. They quickly take off the shirts and put on their own shirts. They hide the key under the pillow. They run to the door and lock it. They hope Mom will not find out. They pretend they are playing in their room. Mom knocks on the door. She says, "Tom, Lily, are you there? I have cookies for you. They are yummy. Can I come in?" Tom and Lily say, "Yes, Mom, we are here. We are playing. You can come in." They open the door and smile. Mom sees them and smiles too. She does not see the key or the shirts. She gives them each a cookie and a hug. She says, "You are good twins. I love you." Tom and Lily say, "We love you too, Mom. Thank you for the cookies. They are yummy." They eat the cookies and hug Mom. They are happy. They forget about the key and the shirts. They do not want to unlock any more doors. They just want to play in their room.
