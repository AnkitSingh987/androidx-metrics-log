Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Tom and the Box

Sara and Tom are playing in the kitchen. They see a big yellow box on the floor. It is a new freezer for Mom and Dad. Sara and Tom want to help. They open the box and find the freezer inside. "Let's load the freezer with food!" Sara says. She runs to the fridge and takes out some cheese and eggs. Tom follows her and grabs some milk and juice. They carry the food to the freezer and put it inside. They are happy. They think they are doing a good job. But then Mom comes in. She sees the food in the freezer and gasps. "Oh no! What have you done?" she says. "This food is not for the freezer. It will go bad. You have to take it out right now!" Sara and Tom are confused. They do not understand. They thought they were helping. They look at Mom with sad eyes. "Sorry, Mom," Sara says. "We wanted to load the freezer for you." Mom sighs. She is not angry, but she is worried. She hugs Sara and Tom. "I know you wanted to help, but you have to ask me first. Some food can go in the freezer, but some food cannot. Come, I will show you." She takes Sara and Tom to the fridge and the freezer. She explains what food can go where. She lets them help her put the food back in the right place. She thanks them for their help. Sara and Tom are glad. They learned something new. They still like the yellow freezer. They hope Mom and Dad will like it too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a big house with her mom and dad. Mia's family was wealthy, so they had many nice things. In their backyard, there was a big bush. Mia loved to play near the bush every day. One day, Mia had a dream about the bush. In her dream, she saw a little bird that lived in the bush. The bird told her to look under a rock near the bush. When Mia woke up, she was very excited. She remembered her dream and wanted to see if the bird was right. Mia ran outside and found the rock near the bush. She lifted the rock and saw a beautiful, shiny stone. Mia showed the stone to her mom and dad. They were very happy and proud of her. Mia knew that her dream had helped her find something special. From that day on, Mia always believed in the power of dreams.

One day, a cat saw a clean shirt hang on a rope. The cat wanted to play with the shirt. The cat jumped and tried to pull the shirt down. But the shirt did not come down. The cat was sad. The cat tried again and again. Each time, the cat could not get the shirt down. The cat started to cry. A big dog heard the cat cry. The dog came to see what was wrong. The dog saw the cat cry and the shirt hang. The dog wanted to help the cat. The dog jumped and pulled the shirt down. The cat was happy. The cat and the dog played with the shirt together.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max liked to twist and turn when he played outside. One sunny day, Max went on a walk with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. As they walked, Max saw something shiny on the ground. It was a pretty gem! Max picked it up with his mouth and showed it to Tim. Tim was very happy and put the gem in his pocket. Later, they met a funny cat named Luna. Luna saw the gem and wanted it too. She tried to take it from Tim's pocket, but the gem started to twist and turn like Max. Suddenly, the gem turned into a toy! Max, Tim, and Luna played with the toy all day long, and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a deep hole in the ground. A little boy named Tim saw the hole and wanted to look inside. He was scared because the hole was very deep. Tim had an idea. He found a long rope and decided to tie it around a big tree. He held the rope tight and started to walk towards the hole. He wanted to see what was inside without falling in. As Tim looked into the deep hole, he saw a small bird that was in trouble. The bird was in danger from a big cat. The cat was a threat to the bird. Tim used the rope to save the bird from the cat. The bird was very happy, and they became good friends.
