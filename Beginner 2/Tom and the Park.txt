Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park. He wanted to play with his red ball. He put on his coat and zipped it up. Tom saw a big cloud in the sky. It looked like it might rain soon. In the park, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy march. They played together with the march and the red ball. Sue said, "I hope it does not rain. That would be bad." Tom agreed, but they kept playing. Soon, it started to rain. Tom and Sue felt bad. They ran under a big tree to stay dry. Tom said, "We should have gone home when we saw the big cloud." Sue nodded. They waited for the rain to stop, then went home. They learned to pay attention to the clouds in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a brother named Sam. They loved to play together. One day, they were playing with their toys when Sam started to feel hurt in his leg. Tim didn't know what to do, so he paused to think. Tim thought of a plan. He decided to get help from their mom. He ran to find her and told her about Sam's hurt leg. She quickly came to see what was wrong. When they got back to Sam, something unexpected happened. Sam was not hurt anymore. He was laughing and playing again. It turned out that Sam was just playing a trick on Tim. Tim was not happy, but mom told them a lesson. She said, "It's not nice to trick others when they are trying to help." From that day on, Sam never played tricks on Tim again, and they played happily together.

Once there was a fast horse. The horse liked to run and play all day. The horse had a friend, a little girl. The girl loved the horse very much. One day, the girl wanted to give the horse a kiss. The horse was happy and excited. They went to a big tree to play together. But the horse was too fast! The girl tried to catch the horse, but she fell down. The girl got hurt and cried. The horse felt sad and could not get a kiss. They both went home with a bad day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a kind girl named Lily. She had a pet dog named Max. They were best friends. One day, Lily found a file in her room. It had many colors on it. She did not know what it was for. Lily showed the file to Max. "Look, Max! What is this?" she asked. Max wagged his tail but could not tell her. Lily had an idea. "Let's go show it to Mom," she said. They went to find Mom in the kitchen. "Mom, what is this?" Lily asked, holding the file. Mom smiled and said, "That is a file to keep your drawings safe. You can put all your pretty pictures in it." Lily was so happy. She knew she would use it to keep her drawings safe. From that day on, Lily and Max made many beautiful pictures together and saved them all in the colorful file.

Once upon a time there was a poor family. They did not have much, but they still had each other. Every day, the little boy would help his mom with the mail. He would hold it carefully in his hands. One day, something special arrived in the mail. It was a package from his Granny! Inside, there were toys and sweets. The little boy was so happy! He held the package close to his heart and thanked his Granny for the special gift. From then on, the poor family was a bit happier.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to play a sport. They chose to play ball. Tim and Sue were very happy. As they played, Tim said, "Sue, I have an original joke for you." Sue was excited to hear the joke. Tim said, "Why did the ball go to school? Because it wanted to learn how to bounce!" Sue laughed and said, "That's a funny joke, Tim!" They played ball for a long time and told more jokes. They had a lot of fun. Tim and Sue were very happy friends.
