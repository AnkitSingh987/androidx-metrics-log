Title: The Lamb and Bear and the Wool

Once upon a time, there was a lamb and a bear. The lamb had a lot of wool so the bear said, "Can I have some wool?" The lamb said, "Yes! I have lots. I'll give it to you." So the lamb gave the bear some wool. The bear said "Thank you, now I am warm and safe!" The lamb smiled and said, "You're welcome!" And they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a grumpy dog with a hurt heel. The dog looked sad, so Lily went to examine it. She wanted to help the dog feel better. Lily said, "Hi doggy, why are you grumpy?" The dog said, "My heel hurts, and I don't know what to do." Lily looked at the dog's heel and saw a small stone stuck in it. She gently took the stone out and the dog felt better. The dog was not grumpy anymore. He said, "Thank you, Lily! My heel feels good now!" Lily and the dog became friends and played together all day. From that day on, they were always happy to see each other.

One day, a girl named Lily found a big, hot rock. She was very creative and thought, "I can make something fun from this rock!" So, she went to her friend Tom and asked for help. Tom said, "Let's split the rock in half and see what's inside!" They both pushed and pushed until the rock split into two pieces. Inside the rock, they found beautiful, shiny stones. Lily and Tom used the shiny stones to make pretty necklaces for their friends. All their friends loved the necklaces and thanked Lily and Tom for being so creative. They all played together and had a fun day.

As Tim read the crazy book, fun things happened in his room. His toys danced and his bed jumped. Tim laughed and clapped. He loved the crazy book.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play with dolls and cars. One day, they go to Lily's house. Lily has a big house with many toys. Tom likes Lily's house. "Can I play with your pink doll?" Tom asks Lily. "Sure, you can play with her. She is my favorite doll. Her name is Rose," Lily says. Tom takes the pink doll and plays with her. He makes her talk and walk. He is happy. But then, he drops the doll. The doll's head falls off. Tom is scared. "Oh no! I broke your doll! I'm sorry, Lily!" Tom says. Lily sees the broken doll. She is sad. She loves her doll. She starts to cry. "Don't cry, Lily. I will fix your doll. Or I will replace her. I will give you my blue car. It is my favorite car. You can have it," Tom says. Lily stops crying. She looks at Tom. She sees that he is sorry. She sees that he is kind. She smiles. "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend. I don't need a new doll. I just need some glue. We can fix Rose together. And you can keep your car. We can share our toys," Lily says. Tom and Lily hug. They are happy. They go to find some glue. They fix the doll. They play with the doll and the car. They have fun. They are good friends.
