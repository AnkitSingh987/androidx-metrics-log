Category: Beginner
Title: The Tall Fence and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a tall fence. A little boy named Tim lived next to the fence. One day, Tim saw a big ball on the other side of the fence. "Ball, come back!" Tim said. But the ball did not come back. Tim was sad. Then, a nice girl named Sue came. She was tall and could reach the ball. Sue gave the ball to Tim. "Here you go!" Sue said. Tim was happy to receive the ball. They played together and became good friends.

One day, a little red car was driving down the road. It was a sunny day and the car was happy. The car saw a big tree with a cat in it. The cat looked confused and scared. The car stopped and said, "Hello, cat! Why are you confused? Can I help you?" The cat looked down at the car and said, "I climbed up the tree, but now I don't know how to get down." The car thought for a moment and then had an idea. The car told the cat, "I will squeeze under the tree and you can jump onto my soft roof. Then, I will drive you home." The cat was happy and jumped onto the car. The car drove the cat home and they both felt good. The cat was no longer confused and the car had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her friends. One sunny day, Lucy and her friends went to the park to play. At the park, they found a big net. They wanted to play on it. Lucy was the first to try. She climbed up and tried to balance on the net. It was hard, but she kept trying. Her friends cheered her on. Lucy felt so brave. She finally learned to balance on the net. Her friends clapped and laughed. They all took turns and learned to balance too. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a soft velvet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a big house with his friend, a small dog named Tiny. They were good friends and liked to play together. One day, Fluffy and Tiny found a big, red ball. They both wanted to play with it. They began to quarrel. "It's mine!" said Fluffy. "No, it's mine!" said Tiny. They pulled the ball back and forth, trying to take it from each other. Suddenly, the big, red ball went "pop!" It was dead. Fluffy and Tiny looked at the broken ball and felt sad. They knew they should not have quarreled. They said sorry to each other and promised to share their toys from now on. And they were good friends again, playing happily in their big house.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tower. The tower was very tall and old. Tim wanted to see what was inside. As Tim walked in, he heard a voice. "Listen," said the voice, "I am a dead king." Tim was scared, but he wanted to help the king. "Please find my lost crown," the king asked. Tim said, "Okay, I will find it for you." Tim looked and looked. He found the crown in a small room. But when he picked it up, the dead king turned into a happy frog! "Thank you," said the frog. "I was a king, but a bad witch made me dead and turned me into a frog. Now I am alive again!" Tim was happy that he could help the frog. They became good friends and played together every day.

Paul and his mom were on a tour. They were exploring and having a great time. Paul wanted to keep playing and not stop. "Come on Paul, time to go," said his mom. Paul didn't want to stop, so he decided to hide. He ran behind a bush and held his breath. His mom started to look for him. "Paul, where are you? Come out!" But Paul wouldn't answer. He thought it would be funny to hide. Finally, his mom found him. She was not happy. "Paul, why did you hide? That was not a nice thing to do!" Paul was sad and embarrassed. He was sorry for hiding and he knew it was wrong. His mom decided to end the tour early and they went home. Paul had a bad ending to his fun tour.
