Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rhythm

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He found a drum in his room. Tim wanted to make a rhythm with the drum. He hit the drum with his hands and it made a loud sound. Tim's friend, Sara, came over to play. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I am playing the drum!" Tim said. "Can I play too?" Sara asked. "Yes, let's make a rhythm together!" Tim said. They set the drum on the floor and sat down. Tim hit the drum first, then Sara hit it. They took turns hitting the drum and made a fun rhythm together. They laughed and played the drum all day.

One day, a boy and a girl went to play near a stream. They wanted to build a bridge with cheap sticks. The boy said, "I will find the sticks!" The girl said, "I will help you!" They found many sticks, but they were not strong. The boy and girl tried to build the bridge, but it fell down. They were sad. A big frog near the stream said, "You need better sticks. I will show you where to find them." The boy and girl followed the frog. They found strong sticks and built a new bridge. The frog said, "Now, you can cross the stream." But when they tried to cross, the bridge broke again! The frog laughed and jumped away. The boy and girl were sad, but they did not give up. They found even stronger sticks and built a better bridge. This time, they crossed the stream and were very happy. They knew that sometimes, you need to try again to make things work.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the yard. One day, he found a big bone. He was very happy. Spot wanted to show his friends the big bone. He went to find his friends, but they were not available. Spot was sad. He wanted to play with his friends and share the bone with them. Spot thought of a plan to increase the fun. He hid the big bone and told his friends to find it. When his friends were available, they played the game. They all had fun and shared the big bone. They were all happy.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty ant named Andy. Andy loved to crawl all around the yard. One day, he saw a big porch. He wanted to go there and meet new friends. As Andy crawled closer to the porch, he met a little bug named Benny. Benny said, "Be careful, Andy! There is a big dog that lives on the porch. He likes to chase bugs like us." Andy listened, but he still wanted to see the porch. Andy and Benny crawled to the porch when the big dog was away. They played and had fun. Then, they heard the big dog coming back. They crawled very fast to hide. The big dog did not see them. They were safe and happy. From that day on, Andy and Benny became best friends, and they always played together when the big dog was away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a reliable dog named Max. They liked to play together. Every day, Tim would go to school and Max would wait for him to return. One day, Tim's mom made him a yummy lunch. Tim was very happy. He said goodbye to Max and went to school. Max waited for Tim to come back, just like always. But today, something unexpected happened. When Tim returned, his lunch was still with him! He didn't eat it at school. Tim had a big smile. He said, "Max, I saved my lunch for us to share!" Max wagged his tail and they both enjoyed the lunch together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. The beach was wide with lots of sand and water. Lily loved to play in the sand and watch the big waves. She saw a big wave and said, "Mom, look at that big wave!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, it's very big." Lily saw a boy playing with a toy boat. The boy waved at her and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" Lily felt happy and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" They played with the boat and had lots of fun. They both felt the wonder of the big waves and the soft sand. After playing, Lily and the boy said goodbye. Lily's mom told her, "I'm glad you made a new friend today. It's nice to share and play with others." Lily learned that sharing and being kind to others can make everyone happy.
