Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Dog

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big basket of muffins on a bench. The muffins smell good. They are hungry. "Let's take a muffin," Anna says. "No one is looking." "That's not nice," Ben says. "The muffins belong to someone else." But Anna does not listen. She is careless. She runs to the bench and grabs a muffin. She bites it. It is sweet and soft. "Yum!" Anna says. "This is the best muffin ever!" But then, she hears a loud bark. A big dog is running towards her. The dog is angry. The muffins are his. He wants his muffin back. "Help!" Anna cries. "The dog is chasing me!" Ben sees Anna in trouble. He is brave. He picks up a stick and throws it. The stick flies in the air. The dog sees the stick. He likes sticks. He forgets about the muffin. He runs to catch the stick. "Good boy!" Ben says. "Go fetch!" Anna is safe. She runs to Ben. She is sorry. She gives him the muffin. "I'm sorry, Ben," Anna says. "I was careless. I should not have taken the muffin. It was wrong." "It's okay, Anna," Ben says. "I forgive you. But next time, ask before you take something. And be careful of dogs. They can be scary." Anna nods. She hugs Ben. They are friends again. They share the muffin. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was frightened of the big fish in the sea. He always swam away when they came near him. One day, Fin saw a shiny hook hanging in the water. He was curious and wanted to touch it. His mommy said, "No, Fin! That is dangerous!" But Fin insisted, "I want to see it, mommy!" Fin's mommy took him to a safe place and showed him another hook. She said, "This is a hook too, but it is safe for you to touch." Fin touched the safe hook and was happy. He learned to listen to his mommy and not to touch the dangerous things in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had many toys, but he wanted more. One day, he decided to sell some of his toys to get new ones. Tim was ready to make a lot of money. Tim set up a table outside his house. He put all his toys on the table. But no one came to buy them. After a long time, Tim had sold zero toys. Tim felt sad and went inside. His mom saw him and asked why he was sad. Tim told her about trying to sell his toys. His mom said, "Tim, you don't need more toys. Be happy with what you have." Tim thought about it and realized she was right. He learned that having more toys didn't make him happy. Being thankful for what he had was the key to happiness.

One day, a big gray dog saw a small red ball in the park. The gray dog wanted the ball, but it belonged to a little girl. The little girl was playing with the ball, and the gray dog was envious. He wanted the ball for himself. The gray dog went up to the little girl and shouted, "Give me the ball!" The little girl was scared and said, "No, it's mine!" The gray dog was sad, but he knew he shouldn't take the ball from the little girl. Then, the little girl saw how sad the gray dog was. She said, "We can play together!" The gray dog was happy, and they played with the ball in the park. The little girl and the gray dog became good friends, and they shared the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Mark. He loved to eat rice every day. His mom would cook the rice for him in a big pot. One day, the sky was dark, and the rain was falling. Mark's mom told him to stay inside and play with his toys. But Mark wanted to go outside and play in the rain. He put on his rain boots and raincoat. He went outside and splashed in the puddles. Mark was having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew Mark's favorite toy away. Mark was sad, but he knew he had to find his toy. He walked and walked until he saw it stuck in a tree. Mark tried to reach it, but the toy was too high. Then, a tall man came and helped Mark get his toy back. The man told Mark that it's important to listen to his mom and stay inside when it's dark and rainy. Mark thanked the tall man and went back home. He told his mom about his adventure and promised to listen to her next time. They sat down and ate a warm bowl of rice together. Mark learned that it's important to listen to his mom, and that staying safe is more important than playing in the rain.
