Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Sam and the Lion

Ben and Sam were best friends. They liked to play with cars and trains. One day, they saw a big poster. It said: "Come to the circus! See the clowns, the lions, the elephants and more! Get your ticket now!" Ben and Sam wanted to go to the circus very much. They asked their moms for money. Their moms gave them some coins. They ran to the ticket booth. They gave their coins to the man. The man gave them two tickets. They were very happy. They went to the big tent. They found two seats. They waited for the show to start. They saw many people. They saw popcorn and candy. They saw balloons and hats. They saw a big band. They heard music. The show started. They saw a funny clown. He made them laugh. They saw a brave lion tamer. He made them gasp. They saw a beautiful acrobat. She made them clap. They saw a smart elephant. He made them cheer. Ben and Sam had a lot of fun. They forgot about their tickets. They left them on their seats. They went to see the animals. They saw a monkey, a zebra, a camel and more. They fed them some peanuts. They petted them gently. They heard a loud noise. It was the end of the show. They ran back to their seats. They wanted to keep their tickets. They wanted to show them to their moms. They wanted to remember the circus. But their tickets were gone. Someone had taken them. Ben and Sam were very sad. They started to cry. They looked for their tickets. They asked the people. They asked the clown, the lion tamer, the acrobat, the elephant. No one had seen their tickets. They felt very bad. They had lost their tickets. They had no proof of their fun. They had nothing to show their moms. They worried that their moms would be angry. They worried that they would never go to the circus again. They saw a man. He was wearing a red coat. He had a big smile. He had a lot of tickets in his hand. He was the circus boss. He saw Ben and Sam. He saw their tears. He asked them what was wrong. Ben and Sam told him their story. They told him how they bought their tickets. They told him how they enjoyed the show. They told him how they lost their tickets. They told him how they felt. The circus boss listened to them. He felt sorry for them. He said: "Don't worry, boys. I have your tickets. I found them on your seats. I kept them for you. Here they are. You can have them back." Ben and Sam were very happy. They thanked the circus boss. They hugged him. They took their tickets. They looked at them. They smiled. The circus boss said: "You are very lucky, boys. You had a lot of fun. You saw a lot of things. You learned a lot of things. You have a lot of memories. You have your tickets. But you know what? The most important thing is not your tickets. The most important thing is your friendship. You have each other. You shared your joy. You shared your sorrow. You helped each other. You are best friends. That is more valuable than any ticket. Remember that." Ben and Sam nodded. They understood. They agreed. They said: "You are right, sir. Friendship is the most important thing. We are best friends. We will always be. Thank you for your kindness. Thank you for your wisdom. Thank you for the circus." They waved goodbye to the circus boss. They left the big tent. They held their tickets. They held their hands. They walked to their moms. They told them their story. They showed them their tickets. They hugged them. Their moms were proud of them. They praised them. They said: "You are very good boys. You are very brave boys. You are very tough boys. You faced a problem. You solved it. You learned a lesson. You are very lucky boys. You have each other. You have us. You have a lot of love. That is more valuable than any ticket. Remember that." Ben and Sam nodded. They understood. They agreed. They said: "You are right, mom. Love is the most important thing. We love you. We love each other. We love the circus. Thank you for your support. Thank you for your care. Thank you for the money." They kissed their moms. They left the circus. They went home. They put their tickets in a safe place. They played with their cars and trains. They talked about the circus. They laughed. They were happy. The end.
