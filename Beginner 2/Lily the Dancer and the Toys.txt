Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Dancer and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day long. One day, her mom told her, "Lily, you need to take a bath. You are smelly!" But Lily did not want to stop dancing. Later, Lily's friend, Tom, came over to play. They wanted to play a game, but Tom said, "Lily, you are smelly. I do not want to play with you." Lily was sad and knew she needed to take a bath. Lily's mom let her take a bath with her favorite toys. After the bath, Lily was not smelly anymore. Her mom said, "Now, I permit you to dance and play with Tom." Lily was happy and danced with Tom all day long.

Lila and Ben like to play with blocks. They can make many structures with blocks. They can make houses, towers, bridges, and more. They have fun with blocks. One day, they see a new toy in the store. It is a big structure with many pieces. It has wheels, gears, lights, and sounds. It looks very cool and fun. Lila and Ben want to buy it. They ask their mom if they can buy it. Their mom looks at the price. It is very high. She says, "No, you cannot buy it. It is too expensive. You have many blocks at home. You can play with them." Lila and Ben are sad. They really want the new toy. They think it is better than blocks. They beg their mom to buy it. They say, "Please, please, please. We will share it. We will be good. We will clean our room. Please, please, please." Their mom shakes her head. She says, "No, you cannot buy it. It is not a good toy. It is not tough. It can break easily. It needs batteries. It is noisy. It is not good for your imagination. Blocks are better. They are tough. They do not break. They do not need batteries. They are quiet. They are good for your imagination. You can make anything with blocks." Lila and Ben think about what their mom says. They look at the new toy again. They see that it is not very tough. Some pieces are loose. Some lights are dim. Some sounds are annoying. They look at the blocks. They see that they are very tough. They are strong. They are bright. They are quiet. They can make anything with blocks. They smile at their mom. They say, "You are right, mom. Blocks are better. We do not need the new toy. We have fun with blocks. Thank you, mom." Their mom hugs them. She says, "You are welcome, my loves. I am proud of you. You are smart and creative. Let's go home and play with blocks."

Once upon a time there was a nice boy called Jack. Jack went to the kitchen to get some toast. When he got there, he saw that the toaster was broken. Jack was very sad and tried to fix the toaster but he couldn't do it. Then a magical fairy flew in the window. She said to Jack, "Don't worry, I can help you!" Jack was very happy and asked, "Will you help me to make some toast?" The fairy smiled and said, "Yes, I sure can!" Jack watched as the fairy waved her wand and fixed the toaster. When the toaster was fixed, the fairy helped Jack make some nice toast. When the toast was done, Jack said thank you to the fairy. She smilingly said, "You are welcome." Jack was so happy he had the nice toast. Every morning from then on he thought of the fairy and was happy she helped him with the toast.

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many birds. They wanted to see the birds up close. "Let's climb the tree," Tom said. "Maybe we can find a nest." "OK," Lila said. "But be careful. The tree is wet from the rain." They climbed the tree slowly. They saw a nest with three eggs. They were blue and speckled. "Wow, look at the eggs," Lila said. "They are so pretty." "Shh, don't make noise," Tom said. "The mama bird might come back." But it was too late. The mama bird saw them and flew to the nest. She was angry and scared. She squawked and pecked at them. "Ow, ow, ow," Tom and Lila cried. "The bird is hurting us. Let's quit." They climbed down the tree as fast as they could. They ran to their mom, who was sitting on a bench. "Mom, mom, a bird attacked us," Tom said. "We were looking at her nest and eggs," Lila said. "Oh, you poor things," mom said. "You should not touch or bother the birds. They are wild animals. They need their space and privacy. How would you feel if someone came to your room and touched your toys?" Tom and Lila felt sorry. They realized they were wrong. "We are sorry, mom," they said. "We won't do it again. We will respect the birds and their nests." Mom hugged them and kissed their wounds. She gave them some cookies and juice. "That's good, my loves," she said. "Now let's go home and get dry. You are all wet from the tree."
