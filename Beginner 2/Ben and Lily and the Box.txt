Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Box

Ben and Lily wanted to play with their new toy. It was a big bow that could shoot arrows. They found a big box in the garden and put it far away. Then they took turns to shoot arrows at the box. "Wow, this is fun!" Lily said. "I hit the box three times!" "Let me try again!" Ben said. He took the bow and pulled the string. But he pulled too hard and the string broke. The bow snapped and hit him in the face. "Ow, ow, ow!" Ben cried. He dropped the bow and held his face. He had a big red mark on his cheek. Lily ran to him and looked at his face. She felt sorry for him. She wanted to help him. "Ben, are you okay?" she asked. "Do you want me to get Mom?" Ben was very grumpy. He did not want Mom. He did not want Lily. He did not want the bow. He was angry and sad. "Go away!" he shouted at Lily. "This is your fault! You gave me the bad bow! You are a bad sister!" Lily was shocked. She did not give him the bad bow. She did not do anything wrong. She was a good sister. She only wanted to play with him. "But Ben, I did not give you the bad bow!" she said. "I did not know it would break! I only wanted to share with you!" "I don't care!" Ben said. "I don't want to play with you anymore! You are mean and stupid!" He pushed Lily away and ran into the house. He slammed the door behind him. Lily was left alone in the garden. She felt hurt and sad. She picked up the broken bow and the arrows. She did not want to play with them anymore. She wished Ben would say sorry to her. She wished they could be friends again. But Ben did not say sorry. He did not come out of the house. He did not talk to Lily for the rest of the day. He was too grumpy and proud. Lily did not have anyone to play with. She did not have any fun. She had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big chair that she loved to seat on. She would seat on it every day and read her favorite book with her mom. One day, while Amy was seating on her big chair, she saw a small bird outside her window. The bird looked sad and cold. Amy wanted to help the bird, so she put her hand out of the window to let the bird come inside. The bird was scared of Amy's hand at first, but then it saw that she was trying to help. The bird hopped onto Amy's hand and she brought it inside. The bird was happy to be away from the harsh cold. Amy and the bird became best friends, and they would seat on the big chair together, reading books and staying warm.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They laugh and shout and have a good time. One day, they see a new toy in the park. It is a big red ball. It looks very fun. Lily and Tom run to the ball. They both want to play with it. "Let me have it!" Lily says. She tries to take the ball from Tom. "No, it is mine!" Tom says. He pulls the ball away from Lily. They start to fight over the ball. They push and pull and yell. They are not having fun anymore. They are making a mess. A lady comes to the park. She sees Lily and Tom fighting. She is not happy. She walks to them and says: "Stop it, children! That ball is not yours. It belongs to the park. You have to share it with others. And look at you! You are dirty and rude. You need to be clean and nice. Say sorry to each other and put the ball back." Lily and Tom feel ashamed. They realize they were wrong. They say sorry to each other and to the lady. They put the ball back where they found it. They clean themselves up and say thank you to the lady. The lady smiles and says: "That's better. Now you can play again. But remember, sharing is caring. And being clean and nice is better than being dirty and rude." Lily and Tom nod and hug. They learn their lesson. They play together again. They have fun. They are happy.
