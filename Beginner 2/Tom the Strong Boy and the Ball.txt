Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Strong Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom liked to sell toys to his friends. Yesterday, he sold a big, red ball to his friend, Sam. But when Sam played with the ball, it broke. Sam was sad. He went to Tom and said, "Tom, the ball broke. I want a new one." Tom felt bad. He wanted to make Sam happy. So, Tom gave Sam a new ball. This time, the ball was strong and did not break. Sam was happy and they played together. They became best friends and sold toys together.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. The cat had a big itch on her back, and it was very hard for her to reach it. She asked the dog to help her. "Dog, can you help me? I have an itch on my back that I can't reach," said the cat. The dog was scared because the cat was near a dangerous tree. The tree was very big and had many branches that could fall. The dog said, "Cat, we need to leave this dangerous place first. Then, I can help you with your itch." Just as they started to leave, a bird flew down from the tree. The bird had seen the cat and dog from high up in the tree. The bird said, "I can help you both! I can take the cat away from the dangerous tree and help her with her itch." The cat and dog were happy, and they thanked the bird for helping them.

One day, a big dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a stream and wanted to touch the water. Spot was very alert, and he looked around to make sure it was safe. As Spot touched the water, he saw a small fish. The fish was sad, so Spot wanted to help. He picked up the fish with his mouth and put it back in the stream. The fish was happy and swam away. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish came back and turned into a big, beautiful bird! The bird thanked Spot for helping and flew away. Spot was very surprised and happy that he could help the fish.

Lily and Ben loved to play with their toy boat. It was red and white and had a big engine. They liked to sail it in the bathtub and make noises like "vroom" and "splash". One day, they decided to take their boat to the pond in the park. They asked their mom if they could go, and she said yes, but they had to be careful and not lose their boat. They put their boat in a bag and walked to the park with their mom. At the pond, they saw many ducks and geese. They were adorable and quacked and honked. Lily and Ben wanted to feed them some bread, but their mom said no, because it was bad for them. They agreed and took out their boat instead. They put their boat in the water and turned on the engine. It sailed fast and made waves. They laughed and cheered and followed their boat along the shore. They saw other kids with their toys and said hello. But then, something bad happened. Their boat sailed too far and got stuck in some reeds. They could not reach it or see it. They felt sad and scared. They called their mom and told her what happened. Their mom hugged them and said it was okay. She said they could try to get their boat back, but they had to be patient and smart. She said she had an idea. She took out her phone and called someone. Soon, a man came with a big net. He was their mom's friend and he worked at the pond. He said he could help them. He used his net to fish out their boat from the reeds. He gave it back to them and smiled. Lily and Ben were so happy. They thanked the man and hugged their mom. They said they were sorry for losing their boat and they would be more careful next time. Their mom said she was proud of them and they could still play with their boat, but they had to stay close to her. They agreed and sailed their boat again. They had fun and learned a lesson. They loved their boat and their mom and their mom's friend. They were a happy family.
