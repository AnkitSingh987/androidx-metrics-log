Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Rabbit and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bob. Bob liked to cook. Every day, Bob cooked yummy food for his friends. One day, Bob wanted to make a big meal for all his friends. He went to the market to find food. He found some carrots, peas, and corn. Bob was very happy. Bob cooked a big pot of soup. He put the carrots, peas, and corn in the pot. He wore a tight hat on his head while he cooked. When the soup was ready, all his friends came over. They ate the yummy soup and had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim was feeling sick. His mom gave him medicine to make him feel better. She said, "Tim, you must remain quiet and rest." Tim tried to be quiet, but he heard a noise outside. He saw a big, funny bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, but I must be quiet." They played a quiet game of catch. Suddenly, the bird dropped the ball and it rolled away. They ran after it, but it was gone. The bird said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to lose the ball." Just then, a little girl named Sue found the ball. She said, "Is this your ball?" Tim and the bird said, "Yes, thank you!" Sue was also feeling sick and had taken medicine. They all played quietly together and soon, they felt better.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to climb and play with his friends. They had lots of fun in the sun. One day, Spot saw a big vegetable garden. He wanted to climb the fence and eat the vegetables. The vegetables were big, green, and yummy. Spot thought it would be fun to eat them all. Spot climbed the fence and started to eat the vegetables. He ate and ate until his tummy hurt. Soon, the vegetable garden was empty. The people who owned the garden were sad. They had no more vegetables to eat. Because Spot ate all the vegetables, the people were hungry, and Spot's friends were upset with him. Spot felt bad for what he did and wished he never climbed the fence.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big lake. He wanted to sail his toy boat on the lake. His mom said, "Okay, Tim. Let's sail your boat." They put the toy boat in the water. The boat sailed on the lake. Tim was happy. He saw a dove fly by. The dove was brilliant white. It landed near the lake. The dove said, "Hello, Tim. I like your boat." Tim said, "Thank you, dove. I like your brilliant white feathers." They played together by the lake until it was time to go home. Tim and the dove said goodbye, and Tim went home with his mom.

One day, a big dog went outside to play. He saw his friend, a little cat. The big dog said, "Let's observe the birds at noon!" At noon, the big dog and the little cat sat under a tree. They saw many birds in the sky. They were happy to watch the birds fly. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down and said, "I want to play too!" The big dog, the little cat, and the bird played all day long. They had so much fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and a big stove. Tom loved to cook. One day, he wanted to make a cake. Tom started to make the dough. He put flour, eggs, and sugar in a bowl. He mixed it all up. Then, he took the dough and started to roll it flat. As he rolled, he heard a tiny voice. "Hey, stop!" said the voice. Tom looked down and saw a little ant. "Please don't roll me," said the ant. Tom felt bad. He didn't want to hurt the ant. So, he picked up the ant and put it outside. Then, he went back to rolling his dough. Tom put the cake in the stove. He waited for it to cook. When it was done, he took it out. But the cake was not a cake! It was a big pizza! Tom was very surprised. He didn't know how it happened. But he didn't mind. He liked pizza too. So, Tom and his new ant friend ate the pizza together. And they were both very happy.
