Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and Ben and the Cookie

Mia and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Mia has a cute doll that she loves very much. Ben has a big truck that he likes to make noises with. Mia wants to play with Ben's truck, but Ben does not want to share. "Can I have your truck, please?" Mia asks Ben. "No, it is mine. You have your doll," Ben says. "But I want to try your truck. It looks fun," Mia says. "No, no, no. Go away," Ben says. Mia is sad. She does not like when Ben is mean to her. She thinks of a plan. She goes to the kitchen and gets a cookie. She comes back to the living room and shows the cookie to Ben. "Look, Ben, I have a cookie. Do you want some?" Mia says. Ben loves cookies. He looks at the cookie and then at his truck. He thinks for a moment. "OK, I will share my truck with you, but only if you share your cookie with me," Ben says. Mia smiles. She agrees to share her cookie with Ben. She gives him half of the cookie and he gives her his truck. They play together happily. Mia thinks she is very smart. She has a success. She insists on getting what she wants. She is happy. Ben is happy too. He likes the cookie and he likes playing with Mia. He thinks she is very cute. They are friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Beth. She was only three years old and she loved to play. Today, Beth wanted to be outside. She stepped outside and into the bright sun. As Beth stepped around, she found a cube by her feet. It was clean and sparkled in the sunshine. Beth was so excited! She picked up the cube and looked at it closely. What should she do with it? She decided to step back inside and play a game. She put the cube near the door and stepped back. Beth started to play and had lots of fun. She used the cube to make a house and a castle. She even used it to make a colorful rainbow. When Beth finished playing, she stepped back outside. She put the cube back where she had found it, so the next time Beth stepped outside, it would be clean and ready to play with again.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Anna. Anna had a pretty dress, long hair and blue eyes. Lily always took Anna with her everywhere. She valued Anna very much. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and Anna. She saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. She asked her mom to hold Anna while she climbed up the ladder. Her mom said yes and took Anna. Lily slid down the slide and laughed. She felt mighty and happy. But when she got to the bottom, she saw a boy holding Anna. He had taken her from her mom when she was not looking. He said Anna was his now and he liked her luxury dress. Lily was very angry and sad. She ran to the boy and tried to get Anna back. She shouted, "Give me my doll! She is mine!" The boy did not listen. He pushed Lily away and ran off with Anna. Lily cried and cried. Her mom came and hugged her. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. That boy was very mean and rude. He should not have taken your doll. We will try to find him and get Anna back." Lily and her mom looked around the park, but they could not see the boy or Anna. Lily felt like she had lost her best friend. She wished she had never gone on the slide. She wished she had stayed with Anna. She learned that some things are more important than others. She learned to value what she had.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, Tom has a birthday. He invites Lily to his party. Lily is happy. She wants to give Tom a gift. Lily goes to the shop with her mom. She sees many things. She sees a ball, a book, a doll, a car. She thinks: what does Tom like? She sees a big drum. It is red and shiny. It makes a lot of noise. Lily likes noise. She thinks: Tom will like this drum. She tells her mom: I want to buy this drum for Tom. Lily and her mom go to Tom's house. They see many balloons and cakes. They see many kids. They say hello to Tom and his mom. Lily gives Tom the drum. She says: Happy birthday, Tom! This is your gift. Tom opens the gift. He sees the drum. He smiles. He says: Thank you, Lily! This is a great gift. He takes the drum and starts to play. He makes a lot of noise. Boom, boom, boom! Lily is happy. She claps her hands. She says: Do you like it, Tom? Tom nods. He says: I like it a lot, Lily. You are a good friend. He stops playing. He puts the drum down. He stands up. He bows to Lily. He says: Thank you again, Lily. You are very kind. Lily bows back. She says: You are welcome, Tom. You are very fun. They hug. They laugh. They go to eat some cake. They have a good time. The drum is noisy, but they don't mind. They are happy.
