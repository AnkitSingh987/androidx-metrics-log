Category: Beginner
Title: The Tank and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big tank. The tank was full of water. In the tank, there lived many fish. The fish were happy and played all day. One day, a spoiled boy came to the tank. He threw lots of food into the tank. The fish ate and ate until they were very full. They did not want to play anymore because they were too full. The spoiled boy felt regret. He knew he gave the fish too much food. He learned his lesson and never did it again. The fish felt better and played happily in the tank again.

Sara and Tom went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw many fish and shells and crabs. Sara wanted to catch a crab and keep it as a pet. Tom said no, crabs are not pets, they are wild and they can pinch. Sara did not listen to Tom. She saw a big crab hiding under a rock. She ran to the rock and reached her hand to grab the crab. The crab saw Sara and was scared. It opened its claws and snapped at Sara's hand. Sara screamed and let go of the crab. She ran back to Mom and Dad, crying and bleeding. Mom and Dad were worried and angry. They wrapped Sara's hand with a cloth and took her to the car. They said they had to go to the doctor and they could not play at the beach anymore. Tom was sad and sorry. He wished Sara had listened to him. He picked up a shell and started to sing a song to make Sara feel better. But Sara did not want to hear Tom's song. She was in pain and she was mad at Tom and the crab. She said she hated Tom and the crab and the beach. She did not say thank you to Tom for his song. She did not say sorry to Mom and Dad for being naughty. She did not say anything nice at all. She just cried and cried until they got to the doctor. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy, and he always had a big smile on his face. One day, Tim went to the noisy hospital with his mom. He was a little scared, but he knew his mom was with him. In the hospital, Tim met a kind doctor. The doctor said, "Hello, Tim! I see you have a big smile. Can you show me your smile?" Tim smiled, and the doctor smiled back. They talked and laughed, and Tim wasn't scared anymore. Later, Tim heard a sad girl crying in the room next door. He remembered how the doctor made him smile, so he went to see her. Tim said, "I was scared too, but I made a new friend. Can I show you my smile?" The girl looked at Tim and began to smile too. They both laughed, and the hospital didn't seem so noisy or scary anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day. A little boy named Tim wanted to send a letter. He had to go to the post box to do it. Tim put on his coat and hat to stay warm. Tim walked to the post box. It was hard to see because of the cold wind. But, he managed to find it. He put the letter in the post box and started to walk back home. On his way back, Tim saw a big snowman! The snowman was not there before. Tim was surprised. The snowman had Tim's hat on its head. Tim laughed and took his hat back. The snowman had helped keep his hat safe in the cold wind.

One day, a little girl named Lily and her sister, Mia, went to the park. They wanted to play on the swings and get ice cream. The sun was shining, and the park was quiet. Lily said, "Mia, let's get on the swings!" Mia nodded and they both ran to the swings. They swung high in the sky, laughing and having fun. After a while, they got off the swings and went to get ice cream. As they ate their ice cream, they heard a little bird. It was singing a pretty song. Lily looked at Mia and said, "Shh, be quiet. Let's listen to the bird." They sat and listened to the bird, enjoying their ice cream and the quiet park.
