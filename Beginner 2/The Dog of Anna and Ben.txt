Category: Beginner
Title: The Dog of Anna and Ben

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But Ben saw a big dog near the fence. He wanted to pet the dog. He ran to the dog and said, "Hello, doggy!" The dog did not like Ben. The dog barked and growled and showed his teeth. The dog bit Ben on his arm. Ben cried and cried. He felt a lot of pain. Anna ran to Ben and hugged him. She was very scared and sad. A lady came to help them. She had a bandage in her bag. She wrapped the bandage around Ben's arm. She said, "You need to go to the doctor. The dog might have germs. The bandage will stop the blood." Anna and Ben went to the doctor with the lady. The doctor looked at Ben's arm. He said, "The dog was very bad. He hurt you a lot. You need to have a shot. The shot will make you better." Ben did not want to have a shot. He was very anxious. He said, "No, no, no! I don't want a shot! It will hurt more!" He tried to hide behind Anna. But the doctor was stronger. He held Ben's arm and gave him a shot. Ben screamed and screamed. He hated the shot. He hated the dog. He hated the park. He wished he never saw the dog. He wished he stayed with Anna on the swing. He wished he had a good day. But he had a bad day. A very bad day.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to make a leaf house. "Let's pick some leaves and supply them here," Lily said. She pointed to a spot under the tree. "OK," Ben said. He followed Lily and picked some leaves. They carried them to the spot and put them on the ground. They tried to make a wall with the leaves, but the wind blew them away. They tried again, but the leaves were too weak. "This is hard," Ben said. He was sad. "Maybe we need something else," Lily said. She looked around and saw some sticks. "How about these?" She ran to the sticks and brought some back. She gave some to Ben. "Let's use these to make the wall stronger," she said. "Then we can put the leaves on top." They used the sticks to make a frame for the wall. Then they put the leaves on top. The wind did not blow them away this time. "Yay, we did it!" Lily said. She smiled. "Yay, we have a leaf house!" Ben said. He smiled too. They went inside the leaf house and pretended to live there. They had fun.

Jimmy and his mum were having a picnic in the park. Mum said, "Let's go and play some games Jimmy." Jimmy replied, "Yes, let's!" So they began to play tag. Jimmy was very fast and he ran around the park chasing his mum. But suddenly Jimmy felt an urge to shrink. He put his hands on the ground, closed his eyes and wished. And then it happened! He shrank to the size of a bug! Mum laughed and said, "Oh Jimmy, that was so funny!" She took a bug jar from her picnic basket and dropped Jimmy carefully into it. Jimmy said, "Mum, can I stay like this for a bit longer? It's so much fun!" Mum replied, "Okay, but just for a few minutes." Jimmy smiled and danced around the jar. He was having so much fun being tiny!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Amy. Amy was a good girl who liked to help her friends. One day, her friend Tom had an exam at school. Tom was not ready for the exam and felt sad. He asked Amy, "Can you help me study for the exam?" Amy said, "Yes, I can help you, Tom!" So, they sat down and started to study. They looked at interesting books and talked about what they learned. The more they studied, the more fun they had. Tom started to feel happy and ready for the exam. The next day, Tom took the exam and did very well. He was so thankful for Amy's help. Tom learned that helping others is a good thing to do. He also learned that studying can be interesting and fun. From that day on, Tom and Amy helped each other and their friends with exams, and they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: helping others makes everyone happy and learning can be fun.
