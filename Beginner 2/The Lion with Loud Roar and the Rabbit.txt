Category: Beginner
Title: The Lion with Loud Roar and the Rabbit

One day, a big lion with a loud roar lived in the forest. He was slow and had a big lung. All the animals were scared of him. They did not want to play with him. One day, a little rabbit came to the lion. The rabbit said, "Why do you roar so loud? It makes us all scared." The lion just looked at the rabbit and roared again. The rabbit jumped back, but did not run away. The rabbit had an idea. "If you do not roar so loud, we can all play together," said the rabbit. The lion thought about it. He wanted to have friends too. So, he tried to roar softly. The animals heard him and they were not scared anymore. Now, the lion and the other animals played together every day. The lion was not slow anymore, and his roar was not so loud. They all had fun in the forest, and the lion was happy to have friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his favorite toy, a big tank. Tim loved to play with his tank in the sand. He would make the tank go up and down the hills. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam had a toy car. They played together, making the tank and car race. They had so much fun. But then, Tim's tank got stuck in the sand. "Oh no!" Tim yelled. "My tank is stuck!" Sam tried to help, but the sand was harsh and held the tank tight. Tim and Sam pulled and pulled, but the tank would not move. Tim's mom heard him yell and came to help. She gently lifted the tank out of the sand. Tim was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim and Sam continued to play with their toys, making sure the tank stayed away from the harsh sand.

One day, a smart cat and a little dog were playing in the park. The cat had a big red ball. The dog had a small blue ball. They were very happy. The smart cat said, "Let's hide our balls under the tree and go play tag." The little dog agreed. They put their balls under the tree and ran to play tag. After playing tag, they went back to the tree. But, they could not find their balls. The smart cat said, "I have an idea. Let's look under the other trees." They looked under all the trees and found their balls. The little dog said, "You are so smart!" They played with their balls and had a fun day.

One day, a playful little girl named Lucy went to the park. She loved to sing and play with her friends. The sun was shining and the birds were singing too. Lucy was very happy. At the park, Lucy saw a big ice-cream truck. The ice-cream truck had many colors and played fun music. Lucy wanted to try some ice-cream. She asked her mom if she could have some. Her mom said yes and gave her some money. Lucy went to the ice-cream truck and got a big ice-cream cone. She was so happy! She sang a song about her yummy ice-cream. Her friends came to join her and they all sang together. They had a fun and playful day at the park with ice-cream and songs.

Once upon a time, in a big wild forest, there lived a little black cat. The black cat was very sad because it could not climb trees like the other cats. Every day, the black cat would look up at the tall trees and wish it could climb them too. One sunny day, the black cat decided to try to climb a tree. It tried very hard, but it could not go up. The black cat felt sad and sat down under the tree. A kind bird saw the black cat and flew down to help. The bird told the black cat, "Don't give up. Keep trying, and one day you will climb the tree." The black cat listened to the bird and tried again. This time, the black cat went up a little bit. The black cat felt happy and kept trying. Every day, the black cat tried and tried, and it went up higher and higher. Finally, the black cat could climb the tree just like the other cats. The black cat learned that if you try and don't give up, you can do anything you want.
