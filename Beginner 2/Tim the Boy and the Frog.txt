Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very tired after playing all day with his toys. His mommy told him, "Tim, it's time to behave and get ready for bed." Tim knew he had to use the toilet before he went to bed. He went into the bathroom, and he saw a little frog sitting on the toilet! Tim said, "Hi, frog! What are you doing here?" The frog replied, "I'm just resting. I'm tired too!" Tim and the frog became friends. They both used the toilet and washed their hands. Then, Tim went to bed and the frog went back to his pond. They both slept very well, knowing they had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog who loved to play outside. One day, Spot went to play in the park. There, he found a big pit. Spot was very curious about the pit. Spot wanted to use the pit to play a game. He thought it would be fun to jump over it. So, he ran as fast as he could and jumped. But Spot didn't make it over the pit. He fell in and felt miserable. Spot's friend, a little bird named Blue, saw him in the pit. Blue wanted to help Spot. So, Blue went to get a big stick. Spot could use the stick to get out of the pit. Spot held on to the stick and Blue pulled him out. Spot was so happy to be out of the pit. He thanked Blue and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was not very nice to his friends. He was selfish and always wanted to play with their toys but never shared his own. One day, Tim's mom saw him being mean and decided to talk to him. Tim's mom sat him down for a lecture. She told him, "Tim, you need to be nice to your friends and share. If you don't share, you will have no friends to play with." Tim did not like to hear this, but he knew his mom was right. From that day on, Tim started sharing his toys and being nice to his friends. He learned that sharing made everyone happy, including himself. Tim was no longer selfish, and he had many friends to play with. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Ann went to the park. She saw a big pink slide. Ann wanted to play on the slide. She ran to the slide and climbed up. When Ann got to the top, she saw a cat. The cat was stuck on the slide. Ann asked the cat, "What is your name?" The cat said, "My name is Sam." Ann wanted to help Sam. Ann held Sam and they both slid down the slide together. At the bottom, Sam changed into a dog! Ann was surprised. Sam said, "Thank you, Ann. I was a cat, but now I am a dog." Ann and Sam became best friends and played at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a modern cat named Tom. Tom was very fast and liked to run. He had a goal to win a big race. One day, Tom went to the race. Many other cats were there too. They all wanted to win. The race was long, but Tom was not scared. He wanted to reach the end first. The race began, and all the cats ran fast. Tom ran faster and faster. He passed many cats. At last, Tom reached the end. He won the race! His goal was met, and he was very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a modern dog house that he loved. He had a big paw that he used to play with his toys. One day, Spot's friend, Bunny, came to visit. Bunny said, "Spot, let's play in your modern dog house!" Spot agreed, and they had a lot of fun playing together. While they were playing, Spot accidentally broke one of his toys. He was scared that his mom would punish him. But, to his surprise, his mom didn't punish him. Instead, she said, "Spot, it's okay. We can fix the toy together." And they did. Spot and Bunny learned that it's better to be honest and fix mistakes together.
