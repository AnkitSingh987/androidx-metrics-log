Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bell

One day, a girl named Lily went to visit her grandma. She had a big bag with her. Her grandma said, "Lily, please unpack your bag." Lily opened her bag and took out her toys. She also found a small bell. The bell had a nice sound when she shook it. Lily played with her toys and the bell all day. She liked the bell a lot. But then, the bell made a harsh sound. Lily was sad. She asked her grandma, "Why did the bell make a bad sound?" Grandma did not know. Later, Lily found out that the bell was not broken. It was a magic bell! The bell made a harsh sound because it wanted to tell Lily something. The bell said, "Lily, look outside!" Lily looked outside and saw a big rainbow. She was so happy. The magic bell made her day even better.

One day, a large dog named Max was going to school. He was very excited to see his friends. Max put on his bag and said, "Mom, I need to hurry, or I'll be late for school!" On his way to school, Max saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and said, "Help, please! I can't get down!" Max wanted to help, so he put his bag down and helped the cat out of the tree. The cat said, "Thank you, Max!" When Max got to school, he saw that everyone was outside. They were all looking at a big, colorful balloon. Max was surprised because he did not know it was a special day at school. His friends told him, "It's Balloon Day, Max! We get to play with balloons all day!" Max was happy he made it to school on time and could play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim had a gray cat named Fluffy. They liked to play outside and run in the sun. Tim and Fluffy had lots of fun together. One day, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. Fluffy wanted to climb too. They went up, up, up the tree. Tim was happy, but then he looked down. He was scared. Fluffy was scared too. Tim called for help. "Help, help!" he said. A big bird heard him and flew down. "What do you need?" the bird asked. Tim said, "I need help to go down." The bird helped Tim and Fluffy get down. Tim said, "Thank you!" and the bird answered, "You're welcome!" They all played together and had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a nice bedroom with a big bed and lots of toys. He loved to play in his bedroom all day long. One day, Tim learned how to whistle. He was so happy that he could make a new sound. Tim's sister, Jane, heard him whistle and wanted to learn too. She asked Tim, "Can you teach me how to whistle?" But Tim was selfish and did not want to share his new skill. He said, "No, I will not teach you. I want to be the only one who can whistle." This made Jane sad, and she started to cry. Their mom heard the crying and came into the bedroom. She asked, "What's wrong, Jane?" Jane told her mom that Tim would not teach her how to whistle. Their mom said, "Tim, it's not nice to be selfish. You should share your new skill with your sister." Tim thought about it and realized his mom was right. He said, "I'm sorry, Jane. I will teach you how to whistle." They both practiced whistling together, and soon Jane could whistle too. They were both happy and played together in their bedroom, whistling and laughing.

One day, Tim and Mom were walking by the river. Mom said, "Look at all the fish in the river. They are swimming so fast!" Tim looked at all the fish and said, "we need to sort the fish out." Mom smiled and said, "we can definitely do that. But, we need to get a net first." So, they went to the nearby shop and bought a big net. With their net in hand, they walked back to the river and spread the net across the river. Then, they started to sort the fish out. They waved the net back and forth, carefully sorting the fish out. The fish were swimming in and out of the net. Soon, all the fish were sorted out and they even had some unexpected visitors in their net. Two small turtles were swimming in the river and had gotten tangled in their net. Tim and Mom carefully untangled the turtles from the net. Mom said, "That was so fast, Tim! We were able to sort out all the fish with our net!" Tim smiled, looked at the net and said, "Let's go sort more fish tomorrow!"
