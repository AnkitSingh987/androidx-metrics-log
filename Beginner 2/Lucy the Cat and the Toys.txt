Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Lucy. She lived in a big house with a nice family. Every day, Lucy would play with her toys and take naps in the sun. She loved her family very much. One day, Lucy saw a new animal in the house. It was small and had a long tail. Lucy was scared and hid behind the couch. The family saw Lucy and tried to comfort her. They told her that the new animal was a mouse named Sam. Sam was going to live with them too. For a minute, Lucy was still scared of Sam. But then, Sam came out from under the couch and started to play with Lucy's toys. Lucy saw that Sam was fun and not scary at all. They played together and became the best of friends. And from that day on, Lucy was not shy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a light blue vase. It lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the vase very much. She put pretty flowers in it every day. The vase was always happy when it was full of flowers. One day, Lily saw a pretty butterfly. The butterfly was light blue, just like the vase. Lily wanted to follow the butterfly. She picked up the vase and walked outside. The butterfly flew around the garden, and Lily followed it with the vase in her hands. Soon, the butterfly flew away. Lily looked around and saw that she was in a new part of the garden. There were many beautiful flowers here. Lily picked some flowers and put them in the light blue vase. Now the vase was full and happy again. Lily and the vase went back home, following the path through the garden.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. Today, they go to the park with their moms. The park has a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. Tom and Lily want to play on the slide. They run to the slide and take turns. Tom goes first. He climbs up the ladder and sits on the slide. He says, "I am a lion. Roar!" He slides down and makes a loud roar. Lily laughs. She climbs up the ladder and sits on the slide. She says, "I am a tiger. Roar!" She slides down and makes a loud roar. Tom and Lily roar and laugh. But then, they see another boy coming to the slide. He is bigger than Tom and Lily. He has a mean face. He says, "This is my slide. Go away!" He pushes Tom and Lily off the ladder. He sits on the slide and says, "I am a dragon. Roar!" He slides down and makes a loud roar. Tom and Lily are scared and sad. They run to their moms and tell them what happened. Their moms hug them and say, "That was not nice. He should share the slide. There are other things to play with. Look, the swing and the sandbox are available. Do you want to play there?" Tom and Lily nod. They go to the swing and the sandbox. They have fun. They say, "We are friends. Roar!" They roar and laugh.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They give him a carrot nose and two coal eyes. But Sam is mean. He does not like Tom and Mia. He wants to make them sad. He waits until they go inside for hot cocoa. Then he runs to the snowman and kicks him. The snowman falls down and breaks. The hat and the scarf and the carrot and the coal fly away. Sam laughs and runs away. Tom and Mia come back outside. They see their snowman is gone. They see only powder on the ground. They are very sad. They cry and shiver. "Who did this?" Tom asks. "I don't know," Mia says. They look around and see Sam's footprints in the snow. They follow them to Sam's house. They knock on the door. Sam's mom opens it. "Hello, Tom and Mia. What can I do for you?" she asks. "Sam broke our snowman!" Tom says. "He did? That's not nice. Where is he?" she asks. "He's in his room," Mia says. Sam's mom goes to Sam's room. She sees him hiding under his bed. She pulls him out. She is angry. "Sam, did you break Tom and Mia's snowman?" she asks. Sam nods. He is scared. "Why did you do that?" she asks. "I don't know. I was bored. I wanted to have fun," he says. "That's not fun. That's mean. You made them sad. You hurt their feelings. How would you feel if someone broke your toys?" she asks. Sam thinks. He feels bad. He knows he did wrong. "I would feel sad. I would feel angry. I would feel poor," he says. "Then you should say sorry to Tom and Mia. And you should help them make a new snowman. And you should be nice to them from now on. Do you understand?" she asks. Sam nods. He understands. He goes outside with his mom. He sees Tom and Mia. They are still sad. They are still shivering. "Tom, Mia, I'm sorry. I broke your snowman. I was mean. I won't do it again. Can I help you make a new one?" he asks. Tom and Mia look at him. They are surprised. They are not sure. "Really?" Tom asks. "Really," Sam says. He shows them his hat and his scarf and his carrot and his coal. He found them in the snow. He gives them to Tom and Mia. "Here. You can use these. They are yours," he says. Tom and Mia smile. They are happy. They forgive Sam. "Thank you, Sam. That's very nice of you. Yes, you can help us. Come on, let's make a new snowman," Mia says. They all work together. They make a new snowman. He is bigger and better than the old one. They give him a hat and a scarf and a carrot nose and two coal eyes. They also give him a smile. They are all happy. They are all friends. They stop shivering. They hug each other. They go inside for hot cocoa. The end.
