Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Toys

Ben and Mia were playing in the yard when they heard a loud boom. They looked up and saw a big flash of lightning in the sky. Mia was nervous and ran to the house. Ben followed her, but he was curious about the lightning. "Mia, why are you scared?" Ben asked. "It's just lightning. It's like a big spark." Mia shook her head. "No, Ben, it's not. It's dangerous. It can hurt you and make things burn. Mommy told me so." Ben frowned. He wanted to show Mia that lightning was not so bad. He had an idea. He grabbed a flashlight and turned it on and off. He made a boom sound with his mouth. "Look, Mia, this is lightning. It's just light. It can't hurt you. See?" Ben said. Mia peeked from behind the couch. She saw Ben's flashlight and heard his boom sound. She giggled. It was not scary at all. It was funny. "Ben, you're silly. That's not lightning. That's a flashlight. It's not the same." Mia said. Ben smiled. He was glad Mia was not nervous anymore. He turned off the flashlight and put it away. "Maybe you're right, Mia. But it's still fun to pretend. Do you want to play with me?" Ben asked. Mia nodded. She came out from behind the couch and hugged Ben. "Okay, Ben. Let's play. But no more lightning, okay?" Mia said. Ben agreed. They went back to the yard and played with their toys. They did not mind the thunder and lightning anymore. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big room. He loved to balance on one foot for fun. One day, his friend came to visit him. "Look at me!" said the wealthy man. "I can balance on one foot for a long time!" His friend watched and clapped. "Wow! You are very good at that!" said the friend. Just then, a little mouse ran into the room. The wealthy man was so surprised that he lost his balance and fell down. The little mouse laughed and said, "Now I can balance better than you!" And the mouse balanced on one foot, too. The wealthy man and his friend laughed and clapped for the little mouse. From that day on, they all became good friends and had fun balancing together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Millie. Every day, she loved to play in the park with her friends. One day, Millie wanted to play a game of skipping with her friends. She asked her dad to tie the skipping rope. So her dad took a long rope and tied it together at both ends. Millie and her friends started skipping, but they began to argue about who was going to go first as they all wanted to have a turn. Millie's dad stepped in and said, “Listen everyone. We don’t want to fight about this, do we? Let’s all take it in turns. That way everyone will get a fair go.” So Millie and her friends took turns whilst her dad watched over them. Her dad said, “It’s important to take turns and not fight. It’s faster and cheaper that way.” Millie nodded her head in agreement. The moral of this story is that it’s always better to work together and not quarrel as it’s more quick and less expensive.

Lily was sad. She had a big boo-boo on her knee from falling down. It hurt a lot and it was red and wet. She cried and cried and wanted her mommy. Mommy came and saw Lily's boo-boo. She hugged her and said, "Oh, my poor baby. I'm sorry you got hurt. Let me comfort you and make it better." She took Lily to the bathroom and washed her knee with water and soap. She put a band-aid on it and kissed it gently. Lily felt a little better, but she was still sad. She looked at her face in the mirror and saw a tear on her cheek. She said, "Mommy, I'm ugly. I have a boo-boo and a tear and I'm not pretty." Mommy smiled and said, "Lily, you're not ugly. You're beautiful. You have a boo-boo and a tear because you're brave and strong and you feel things deeply. That's what makes you special and lovely. But if you want, I can show you something fun. Do you want to see?" Lily nodded and wiped her tear. Mommy opened a drawer and took out some makeup. She said, "This is makeup. It's something that grown-ups use to make their faces look different. Sometimes they use it to look pretty, sometimes to look funny, sometimes to look scary. Do you want to try some?" Lily said, "Yes, please. Can I have some pink?" Mommy said, "Sure. Here, let me put some pink lipstick on your lips. And some pink blush on your cheeks. And some pink eyeshadow on your eyes. There, now you look like a princess." Lily looked at herself in the mirror and smiled. She said, "Wow, I look like a princess. Thank you, mommy. You're so thoughtful. You made me happy." Mommy hugged her and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I love you. You're my princess. And you know what? You don't need makeup to be pretty. You're pretty just the way you are."
