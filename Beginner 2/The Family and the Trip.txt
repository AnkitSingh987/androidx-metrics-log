Category: Beginner
Title: The Family and the Trip

Once upon a time, there was a happy family. They wanted to go on a trip. They packed their bags and got ready. Before they left, the mom said, "We must stay healthy on our trip." They took yummy food like apples and bananas. They also took water to drink. On their trip, they played games and had fun. They ate their healthy food and drank water. They stayed healthy and happy. The trip was a good time for everyone.

---

Once upon a time, there was a clever fox. The fox had many friends. They all played together in a group. They liked to run, jump, and have fun in the sun. One day, the fox and his friends had a big race. The clever fox was fast, but he quit the race before the end. His friends were sad and asked, "Why did you quit?" The clever fox had a big smile. He said, "I saw a big hole in the ground. I knew my friends would fall in if they did not stop. So, I quit to save them." The friends were happy and thanked the clever fox. The moral of the story is that sometimes it is good to quit for the right reason. The clever fox showed that he cared for his friends more than winning the race.

One day, a boy named Tom was eager to go to the temple. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, can we go to the temple today?" His mom nodded and said, "Yes, let's go." They went to the temple and saw many people. Tom was very happy. He saw a big door and wanted to shut it. His mom told him, "No, Tom! Don't shut the door!" But Tom did not listen. He shut the door very hard. The door made a loud noise. Everyone in the temple looked at Tom and his mom. They were not happy. Tom and his mom had to leave the temple. They went home, and Tom was very sad. He knew he should have listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to pack his toys in a copper box. One day, Max was packing his toys when he saw a little bird. The little bird was helpless. Max wanted to help the bird. Max took the bird to his home. He put the bird in his copper box with his toys. Max was happy to help the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird changed into a big bird! The big bird looked at Max and said, "Thank you for helping me." Max was very surprised. The big bird told Max that he was a magic bird. Because Max was kind and helped the helpless bird, the magic bird gave Max a wish. Max wished for more toys to pack in his copper box. The big bird made Max's wish come true. Max and the big bird played with the new toys and became best friends.

Tim and Mom go to the shop. Tim likes yogurt. He sees many yogurts in the shop. He sees red yogurt, green yogurt, blue yogurt. He wants to try them all. "Mom, can I have this yogurt?" Tim asks, pointing to a blue yogurt. "No, Tim, that is not normal yogurt. That is soap yogurt. It is for washing, not for eating. You will not like it. It will make you sick. Let's buy normal yogurt, okay?" Mom says. But Tim does not listen. He is curious. He wants to know how soap yogurt tastes. He sneaks the blue yogurt into the basket when Mom is not looking. He smiles. He thinks he is clever. They go home. Mom puts the yogurts in the fridge. She does not see the blue yogurt. She goes to do some work. Tim waits for a while. Then he goes to the fridge. He takes the blue yogurt. He opens it. He smells it. It smells funny. He does not care. He spoons some into his mouth. Yuck! The yogurt is bitter and foamy. It burns his tongue. It makes him cough. He spits it out. He cries. He feels bad. He runs to Mom. "Mom, Mom, help me! I ate soap yogurt! It is yucky! It hurts!" Tim says. Mom is shocked. She sees the blue yogurt. She is angry and worried. She scolds Tim. "Tim, why did you do that? I told you not to eat that yogurt. That was very naughty and dangerous. You could have been hurt. You have to listen to me. You have to trust me. I know what is good for you. Now you have to drink a lot of water and rest. You will not have any more yogurt today. You will have to learn your lesson." Mom says. Tim is sorry. He is sad. He wishes he had listened to Mom. He wishes he had bought normal yogurt. He does not want to try new things anymore. He just wants to feel better. He drinks water and lies down. He hopes the soap yogurt will go away soon. He hopes Mom will forgive him. He hopes he will not be sick. He hopes he will not make the same mistake again.
