Category: Beginner
Title: The Park of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim saw a big swing and wanted to play on it. "Mom, can I go on the swing?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can play on the swing. It's a healthy way to have fun." Tim played on the swing for a long time. He went up and down, up and down. He was so happy. After a while, Tim's mom called him. "Tim, it's time to go home now." Tim was sad to leave the swing, but he knew he could come back another day. On their way home, Tim's mom said, "We can buy a swing for our backyard so you can play anytime you want." Tim smiled and hugged his mom. They went home and had a great day.

One day, a girl named Lily saw a big black curtain in her room. She didn't know where it came from. She was scared and shouted, "Mom! Dad! Come here, please!" Her mom and dad ran into the room. They saw the big black curtain too. "What is this?" asked Lily. Her dad said, "I don't know, let's pull the curtain and see what's behind it." Lily, her mom, and her dad all pulled the curtain together. They found a big box behind the curtain. They were very surprised. Lily opened the box and found a lot of toys inside. She shouted, "Wow! Look at all these toys!" Her mom and dad were happy too. They all played with the toys and had a lot of fun. The big black curtain was not scary anymore. It was a surprise gift for Lily and her family.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a magazine on the floor. She picked it up and saw many colorful pictures inside. Lucy liked the pictures and wanted to play a game with them. Lucy took out her box of colors and tried to match the colors in the magazine. She had a yellow crayon, a blue crayon, and a red crayon. Lucy felt troubled because she could not find a green crayon to match the green in the magazine. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Finally, Lucy asked her mom for help. Her mom found the green crayon under the table. Lucy was happy and matched the green crayon with the green in the magazine. She finished her game and showed her mom the pretty colors she matched. Lucy's mom was proud of her and they put the magazine away together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a fan. The fan was very dirty. It was a filthy fan. Tim wanted to clean the fan. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, arrived at his house. Tim said, "Hi, Sam! Can you help me clean my fan?" Sam said, "Yes, Tim! I will help you." Tim and Sam cleaned the fan together. They used water and a cloth. The fan was not filthy anymore. It was clean and shiny. Tim and Sam were happy. They played with the clean fan all day.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run around. One day, he saw a big pole in the park. Spot thought it would be fun to play with the pole. Spot ran to his friend, Cat. "Cat, do you remember the pole in the park?" asked Spot. "Yes, I do," said Cat. "Let's go play with it!" So, Spot and Cat went to the park to play with the pole. But when they got there, they didn't know what to do with the pole. They tried to push it and pull it, but it was too big and heavy. Spot and Cat were very sad. In the end, they couldn't play with the pole, and they went home with nothing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and a pink hat. Her room was painted pink too. Sue was very happy. One day, Sue went to the park with her mom. She saw a smooth, pink rock on the ground. Sue picked it up and put it in her pocket. She liked her new pink rock a lot. Sue enjoyed playing at the park. She played on the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Her mom watched her and smiled. When it was time to go home, Sue took her pink rock with her. She had a fun day at the park with her mom and her new pink rock.
