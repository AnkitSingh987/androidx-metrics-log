Category: Beginner
Title: Tiny the Cat and the Bird

One day, a little cat named Tiny saw a big tree. Tiny wanted to climb the tree. So, Tiny started to climb up, up, up. As Tiny climbed, she saw a weird bird. The bird had many colors and a big beak. The bird said, "Hi, Tiny! What are you doing?" Tiny said, "I am climbing this tree. I want to see what is at the top." The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird took Tiny up to the top of the tree. At the top, they found a big mug. The mug was very big and had a picture of a cat on it. Tiny and the bird looked inside the mug. They saw something unexpected. Inside the mug was a tiny house! The house was just the right size for Tiny. Tiny said, "Thank you, bird!" Tiny and the bird became best friends and had fun playing in the tiny house together.

One day, a girl named Mia wanted to set the table for dinner. She put a big plate of pasta on the table. Mia loved pasta, and so did her brother Tom. Tom came to the table and said, "I want to eat more pasta than you!" Mia smiled and said, "Okay, let's see who can eat more!" They were very competitive. They started eating the pasta. Mia ate fast, but Tom ate faster. They both ate and ate until there was no pasta left. In the end, Tom ate more pasta than Mia. But they both had fun, and they laughed together.

Sara and Ben were having lunch with their mom. They liked the sandwiches and the juice, but they did not like the salad. They wanted to play with their toys instead. So they were careless with their napkins. They threw them on the floor and made a mess. Mom saw the napkins and was not happy. She said, "Sara and Ben, you need to pick up your napkins and put them in the trash. And you need to eat your salad. It is good for you." Sara and Ben did not want to do that. They wanted to escape. They looked at each other and had an idea. They said, "Mom, can we go to the bathroom?" Mom said, "Yes, but hurry up. And don't forget to wash your hands." Sara and Ben ran to the bathroom. But they did not go inside. They ran to the back door and opened it. They saw the yard and the swing and the slide. They smiled and ran outside. They thought they were clever. But they did not see the dog. The dog was big and loud and liked to chase things. The dog saw Sara and Ben and thought they were fun. The dog barked and ran after them. Sara and Ben were scared. They screamed and ran back to the door. But the door was locked. They had forgotten to leave it open. Mom heard the noise and came to the door. She saw Sara and Ben and the dog. She opened the door and let them in. She said, "Sara and Ben, what were you doing? You were supposed to go to the bathroom, not outside. And you made the dog angry. He is not a toy. He is a guard dog. He could have hurt you." Sara and Ben were sorry. They said, "We are sorry, Mom. We wanted to escape. We did not like the salad. We were careless with the napkins. We will not do it again." Mom said, "I'm glad you are safe. But you need to learn to listen and obey. And you need to be careful with the dog. And you need to eat your salad. It is good for you." Sara and Ben nodded. They picked up their napkins and put them in the trash. They ate their salad. They did not like it, but they did not complain. They learned their lesson. They did not want to escape anymore.

Tim and Lily were friends who liked to play with their dolls. They had a tea party in the garden and pretended to feed their dolls with cups and plates. They also had a game where they took turns to ask their dolls questions and see who could answer more. "Who is the president of the world?" Tim asked his doll. "My doll says it is Mr. Bear," Lily said. "No, that is wrong. The president is Mr. Lion. You lose," Tim said. Lily was sad. She did not like to lose. She wanted to win. She thought of a way to make Tim lose. "Who is the best friend of your doll?" Lily asked Tim's doll. "My doll says it is you, Lily. You are very nice and kind," Tim said. Lily smiled. She had a plan. She grabbed Tim's doll and ran away. "Hey, give it back! That is not fair!" Tim shouted. Lily did not listen. She ran to the pond and threw Tim's doll into the water. "Ha ha, now your doll is wet and dirty. You lose," Lily said. Tim was angry and sad. He loved his doll. He ran to the pond and tried to get it back. But the pond was deep and he could not swim. He fell into the water and started to sink. "Help, help! I can't breathe!" Tim cried. Lily was scared. She had done a bad thing. She wanted to help Tim, but she did not know how. She looked around and saw no one. She was alone and troubled. She started to cry. She wished she had not been mean to Tim. She wished they could still be friends and play with their dolls. She wished Tim was safe and happy. But it was too late. Tim did not come out of the water. He was gone. Lily had lost her friend. She had lost the game. She had lost everything.
