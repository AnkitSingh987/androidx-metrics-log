Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bunny and the Bird

One day, a little bunny named Tim was playing in the spring. He was happy because the sun was out and the flowers were pretty. Tim loved to hop and jump in the grass. As he was playing, he saw a big shadow. He looked up and saw a big bird. Tim was frightened. He hid behind a tree and said, "Please don't eat me, big bird!" The big bird looked down and said, "I won't eat you, little bunny. I am just looking for my friend. I realized I lost her when I was flying." Tim felt better and wanted to help the big bird. Together, they looked for the big bird's friend. They found her near a pond. The big bird was happy and thanked Tim. They all played together in the spring sun, and Tim was not frightened anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful horse named Max. Max lived on a big farm with many other animals. Max loved to stretch his long legs and run fast in the big green field. One sunny day, Max was running fast when he saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird said, "Help me, Max! I can't fly." Max wanted to help the little bird. So, Max stretched his long neck down and picked the bird up with his teeth. Just then, something unexpected happened. The little bird started to laugh! The bird said, "I tricked you, Max! My wing is not hurt." Max was surprised but he laughed too. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a pale boy named Tim. Tim had a big idea. He wanted to make a cake for his mom's birthday. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Tim said, "Sam, let's make a cake for my mom." But Sam did not want to help. He refused. He said, "No, I don't want to make a cake." Tim was sad, but he did not give up on his idea. Tim asked his sister, Sue, to help him. Sue was happy to help. They made the cake together. It was a big, yummy cake with pink frosting. Tim's mom was very happy when she saw the cake. She hugged Tim and Sue and said, "Thank you for the lovely cake!"

One day, a fancy ice cream cone went for a walk. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. The ice cream cone was very happy. As the ice cream cone walked, it saw a beautiful mist by the river. It was so pretty and the ice cream cone wanted to see it up close. So, it went to the river and looked at the mist. But the sun was hot, and the ice cream cone started to melt. It felt sad because it didn't want to melt away. A kind bird saw the ice cream cone and knew what to do. The bird flew up to the sky and brought a big cloud to cover the sun. The ice cream cone stopped melting and was happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He had a regular day, just like any other day. He played with his ball, ran around, and had fun. Then, he felt a pain in his toe. "Ouch!" he said, as he sat down to look at his toe. His friend, Sally, came over and asked, "Why do you complain, Tim?" Tim showed her his toe and said, "My toe hurts!" Sally looked at his toe and saw that it was red. "Let's go tell your mom," she said, as they went inside the house. When they found Tim's mom, she looked at his toe and said, "Oh no, it's just a small scratch. Don't worry." But then, something unexpected happened. Tim's toe started to wiggle and talk! "Hello, I'm Mr. Toe!" it said. Tim, Sally, and Tim's mom stared in surprise. They never knew that a toe could talk! From that day on, Tim's days were never just regular days, because he had a talking toe to keep him company.

One day, a little crab was walking near the sea. The crab saw a big sand castle. The castle was made by a small boy. The boy was very happy with his castle. The crab wanted to play too, so he went close to the castle. The crab did not know how to play with the castle. He tried to include himself in the game. He walked on the sand and broke a part of the castle. The boy saw this and was sad. The crab felt sorry for what he did. The sorry crab wanted to help the boy. He used his claws to fix the broken part. The boy saw the crab helping and was happy again. They both played with the castle and became good friends.
