Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big tree with a hole in it. Sue wanted to see what was inside the hole. She put her hand in and felt something soft. She pulled it out and saw that it was butter. Sue was very surprised. Sue took the butter home and showed her mom. Her mom said, "Let's soak the butter in water to make it tough." They put the butter in a bowl of water and waited. But the butter did not get tough. Instead, it got very soft and started to melt. Sue's mom had an idea. She took the melted butter and poured it into a pan. Then, she added flour and sugar. Sue and her mom mixed it all together and put it in the oven. When it was done, they took it out and saw that they had made a delicious cake. The unexpected butter in the tree had turned into a yummy treat for Sue and her mom to enjoy.

Sara liked to play with bubbles in the bath. She had a big bottle of bubble soap and a yellow duck. She poured some soap in the water and made lots of bubbles with her hands. She put the duck in the bubbles and pretended it was swimming. But Sara did not like to clean the bath after she played. She left the bubbles and the water in the tub. She did not pull the plug to let the water go down the drain. She did not wipe the soap off the sides of the tub. She just ran to her room and played with her toys. One day, Sara's mom came to the bathroom and saw the mess. She was not happy. She called Sara and told her to come and clean the bath. Sara did not want to. She said she was busy. She said she would do it later. She said it was not fun. But Sara's mom did not listen. She said Sara had to clean the bath now. She said it was her job. She said it was not fair to leave it dirty. She said it could make the drain clog and the water not go down. Sara did not believe her mom. She thought the drain was big and strong. She thought the water would always go down. She thought her mom was just trying to make her do something she did not like. But Sara was wrong. The next time she wanted to play with bubbles in the bath, she turned on the tap and saw something bad. The water did not fill the tub. It stayed low and dirty. It did not look clear and shiny. It looked brown and yucky. And it smelled bad too. Sara looked at the drain and saw why. The drain was full of bubbles and soap and hair and dirt. It was not big and strong. It was small and weak. It could not let the water go down. It was clogged. Sara was sad and scared. She did not want to play with bubbles anymore. She wanted to have a clean bath. She wished she had listened to her mom. She wished she had cleaned the bath before. She wished she had pulled the plug and wiped the soap. She ran to her mom and said she was sorry. She said she would clean the bath now. She said she would not leave it dirty again. She said she learned her lesson. Sara's mom hugged her and said it was okay. She said she was glad Sara understood. She said she would help her clean the bath. She said they would use a plunger and some vinegar to unclog the drain. She said they would make the bath clear and shiny again. Sara smiled and said thank you. She said she loved her mom. She said she loved bubbles too. But she said she loved a clean bath more.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the wild woods. She saw a white bunny hopping around. Lily stared at the bunny, and the bunny stared back at her. They both looked at each other for a long time. "Hi, bunny!" said Lily. "Do you want to be my friend?" The white bunny just stared at her and did not say anything. Lily felt sad because the white bunny did not want to be her friend. She did not know what to do. Lily had an idea. She picked up a big leaf and held it out to the bunny. The white bunny hopped closer and sniffed the leaf. It started to eat the leaf and seemed happy. Lily smiled and knew that she had made a new friend. The conflict was over, and they played together in the wild woods.
