Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Toys

Anna and Ben like to play in the living room. They have many toys and books there. But they also like to play with the rug. The rug is big and soft and has many colors. One day, Anna and Ben have a new idea. They decide to raise the rug and make a tent. They lift one corner of the rug and put it on the sofa. Then they lift another corner and put it on the chair. They crawl under the rug and pretend they are camping. They have fun in their tent. They tell stories and sing songs. They use a flashlight and make shadows. They laugh and giggle. But they are also very loud. Mom and Dad are in the kitchen. They hear the noise and wonder what is going on. They walk to the living room and see the rug on the sofa and the chair. They peek under the rug and see Anna and Ben. "Hello, campers!" Mom says. "What are you doing under the rug?" "We are camping, Mom!" Anna says. "We raised the rug and made a tent!" "That's very creative, but also very loud," Dad says. "Can you please be quieter? We are trying to work." "OK, Dad," Ben says. "We will be quieter. But can we keep the tent?" "Sure, you can keep the tent," Mom says. "But only for today. Tomorrow, we need to put the rug back on the floor." "OK, Mom," Anna and Ben say. They smile and hug Mom and Dad. Then they go back to their tent and play quietly. They are happy.

---

Anna liked to look at the sky. She saw many birds that could fly. She wanted to fly too. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I fly like the birds?" Mom smiled and said, "No, Anna, you can't fly like the birds. You are a human, not a bird. Humans can't fly with their arms." Anna was confused. She said, "But mom, I see some humans fly in the sky. They are in big metal birds. They make loud noises." Mom said, "Those are not birds, Anna. Those are planes. Planes are machines that humans make. They have engines and wings. They can fly in the sky, but they need pilots to control them." Anna said, "Oh, I see. Can I go in a plane someday?" Mom said, "Maybe, Anna, maybe. If you are good and work hard, you might go in a plane someday. You can see the clouds up close. They are white and fluffy. They are made of water. Sometimes they make rain or snow." Anna said, "Wow, that sounds fun. I want to see the clouds. I want to touch them. Are they soft?" Mom said, "I don't know, Anna. I have never touched a cloud. Maybe you can find out someday. But for now, let's go inside. It's getting cold and dark. We can read a book about planes and clouds. How does that sound?" Anna said, "Okay, mom. That sounds good. I love you, mom." Mom said, "I love you too, Anna. You are my sweet girl."

Lila and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. The park has a big slide, a swing, and a modern steel sculpture. The sculpture is shiny and has many shapes. One day, Lila and Ben go to the park. They see a cat on the sculpture. The cat is scared. It cannot get down. It meows loudly. "Look, Ben, a cat!" Lila says. "We have to save it!" "How?" Ben asks. "The sculpture is too high and too hard." Lila thinks. She sees a ladder near the slide. She runs to get it. She brings it to the sculpture. She puts it against the sculpture. She climbs up the ladder. "Be careful, Lila!" Ben says. Lila reaches the cat. She pets it softly. She picks it up. She holds it in her arms. She goes down the ladder. "Good job, Lila!" Ben says. "You saved the cat!" The cat purrs. It licks Lila's face. Lila smiles. She hugs the cat. "Let's find its home," Lila says. "Maybe it has a name." "OK," Ben says. "But first, let's play on the slide." They run to the slide. They take turns sliding down. They laugh. The cat watches them. It is happy.
