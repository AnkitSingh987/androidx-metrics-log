Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big slide with a zigzag shape. He was excited to play on it. Tim climbed up the slide and looked down. He felt scared. "Mom, help me!" he yelled. His mom came to him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I am here with you." Tim's mom helped him slide down the zigzag slide. Tim was not scared anymore. He was happy and wanted to play on the slide again. "Thank you, Mom!" Tim said with a big smile.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big square toy on the ground. He wanted to play with it, so he grabbed it with his hands. Tim tried to put the square toy into a round hole, but it would not fit. He felt frustrated and didn't know what to do. A little girl named Sue saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "I can't put this square toy into the round hole." Sue looked at the toy and then at the hole. She had an idea. Sue grabbed the square toy from Tim and walked to a different part of the park. She found a square hole and put the square toy in it. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played together with the square toy and had lots of fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a box full of letters. One day, he found a letter that was very special. It was a delicious letter. The letter was made of chocolate! Tim wanted to share the chocolate letter with his friend, Sam. So, he went to Sam's house and said, "Sam, I have a delicious letter made of chocolate. Let's eat it together!" Sam was very happy and said, "Yes, let's share it!" Tim and Sam started to remove the chocolate from the letter. They ate it together and it tasted very yummy. They were both very happy. By sharing the delicious chocolate letter, they learned that sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, there was a child. The child was unique. The child had a red hat. The child loved to play with a big ball. One day, the child went to the park with the big ball. At the park, the child saw a dog. The dog was big and brown. The dog wanted to play too. The dog took the big ball in its mouth and ran away. The child was sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog came back with the big ball and a small ball. The dog wanted to share with the child. Now, the child and the dog could both have a ball to play with. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a big tree and a small tree were talking. The big tree said, "I think I am very strong. I have big branches." The small tree said, "I think I am small and not strong like you." One day, a big wind came. The wind made the big tree feel uncomfortable. The big tree fell down and broke into many pieces. The small tree was still standing. After the wind, the big tree turned into ash. The small tree was sad. A bird came and said, "Do not be sad, small tree. You are strong too. You did not fall down in the wind." The small tree smiled and felt happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a big shield. He loved his shield very much. Every day, he would polish his shield to make it shiny and clean. One day, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a ball. Tom wanted to play with Sue, but Sue was being rude. She did not want to share her ball with Tom. Tom felt sad. Then, Tom had an idea. He showed Sue his shiny shield. Sue liked the shield and wanted to play with it. Tom said, "I will share my shield if you share your ball." Sue agreed, and they played together. They became good friends and learned to share with each other.
