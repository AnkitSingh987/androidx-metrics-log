Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Cat

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to invite her friend Tim to play at her house. She was very excited and ran to Tim's house. When she got there, she saw a grumpy cat sitting by the door. The cat was guarding a lock on the door. Lily said, "Hello, cat. Can you let me in? I want to invite Tim to play." The grumpy cat looked at her and said, "I will let you in if you can make me happy." Lily thought about how to make the cat happy. She began to sing a happy song and dance around. The grumpy cat started to smile and laugh. The cat was happy now, so it moved away from the lock. Lily opened the door and invited Tim to play at her house. They had a fun day together, and even the grumpy cat joined in their play.

One day, a boy named Tom had a cap. He loved his cap so much. It was his favorite thing to wear. Tom wore his cap everywhere he went. It was a hot day and Tom was playing outside with his friends. While they were playing, Tom's cap got caught on a branch. The cap ripped a little bit. Tom was sad. He didn't want his cap to be broken. He wanted to repair his cap so he could wear it again. Tom went to his mom and showed her the cap. His mom knew how to fix things. She got a needle and some thread. She helped Tom repair his cap. Now, the cap was as good as new. Tom was happy and wore his cap again on the hot day.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there lived a chubby cat. The cat loved to play and run all day. The sun was warm and the grass was soft, so the cat was happy. One day, the cat saw a big tree. The cat wanted to climb the tree. The cat started to stretch its legs and get ready to climb. The cat stretched and stretched until it felt strong. The chubby cat climbed the tree and saw a big world around. The cat was so happy it climbed the tree. The cat went back to the cabin to tell the story of the big tree. And from that day on, the chubby cat stretched every day and had many more adventures.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there was a jolly family. They loved to play together. One day, they saw a big chimney on their house. The chimney was new and they were very happy. The little girl said, "Let's play near the chimney!" So, they all went outside to play. They played ball and ran around the chimney. They laughed and had a lot of fun. As they played, a funny bird came out of the chimney. The bird said, "Hello! I live in the chimney. Can I play with you?" The family said, "Yes, come play with us!" So, the bird joined them, and they all played together by the chimney. They were a jolly family and had a great day.

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. They had a ball, a kite and a whistle. They took turns to kick the ball, fly the kite and blow the whistle. They had a lot of fun. One day, they saw a big dog in the park. The dog was brown and furry, and had a long tail. The dog ran towards them, wagging its tail. Tom and Sam were scared. They thought the dog wanted to bite them. "Run, Sam, run!" Tom shouted. He grabbed the ball and the kite and ran away. He left the whistle on the ground. Sam was too afraid to run. He picked up the whistle and blew it as hard as he could. He hoped someone would hear him and help him. The dog heard the whistle and stopped. It tilted its head and looked at Sam. It did not want to bite him. It wanted to play. It barked and wagged its tail. Sam saw that the dog was friendly. He stopped blowing the whistle and smiled. He threw the whistle and the dog chased it. They played together for a while. Tom saw them from a distant place. He felt bad. He had left his brother alone. He had been selfish and cowardly. He ran back to Sam and the dog. He said sorry to Sam and gave him the ball and the kite. He also petted the dog and said hello. Sam forgave Tom and hugged him. He shared the ball and the kite with him. The dog licked them both and barked happily. They all played together in the park. They had a lot of fun. The moral of the story is: Do not be afraid of things you do not know. Do not leave your friends behind. Be kind and share.
