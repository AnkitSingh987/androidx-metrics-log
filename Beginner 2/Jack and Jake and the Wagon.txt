Category: Beginner
Title: Jack and Jake and the Wagon

The two friends, Jack and Jake, were playing in the park. Jack had a wagon and Jake wanted to share it. Jack's wagon was so big, it could fit two people. But Jack was being selfish and didn't want to share the wagon. He kept pushing Jake away and said, "No! It's mine!" Jake felt very sad and started to cry. Jack began to feel guilty about not wanting to share his wagon. He decided it would be much more fun to share with Jake than to be selfish. So, Jack and Jake shared the wagon. They had lots of fun going up and down the hills in the park. Jack was so happy that he decided to share all his toys with Jake from now on too.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He wore tight shoes that hurt his feet. Yesterday, he saw a big tree and wanted to jog there again today. Tim liked to jog because it made him feel strong and fast. As Tim jogged to the big tree, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! Why are your shoes so tight?" Tim said, "I don't know, but they hurt my feet." Sue said, "I can help you. Let's go to my house and find better shoes for you." When they got to Sue's house, Sue found a magic shoe box. She said, "These shoes will help you jog better." Tim put on the magic shoes, and they were not tight. Tim and Sue went back to the big tree and jogged together. They had lots of fun, and Tim's feet did not hurt anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was white and loved to run around and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big ball and went crazy with joy. He played with the ball and had a lot of fun. But then, Spot lost the ball. He looked everywhere for it, but he couldn't find it. Spot began to panic. He was very sad and didn't know what to do. He wanted his ball back so he could play again. Spot's friend, a little bird, saw Spot was sad. The bird flew high up and looked for the ball. The bird found the ball and brought it back to Spot. Spot was so happy, he wagged his tail and played with the ball again. Spot and the bird played together all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to swing on the swings. He also liked to ride his bike with orange pedals. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to swing and ride his bike. He was having so much fun swinging high and pedaling fast. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a big orange cat sitting on his bike! Tim was surprised and didn't know what to do. He walked up to the cat and said, "Please get off my bike, cat." The orange cat looked at Tim and started to talk! The cat said, "I will get off your bike if you let me swing with you." Tim was very shocked but agreed to let the cat swing with him. They had so much fun swinging together at the park. From that day on, Tim and the talking orange cat became best friends and played together all the time.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big slide and wanted to play. Mia ran to the slide and started to climb up. But she didn't see a puddle of water on the steps. Mia's foot went into the water and she began to slip. "Oh no!" she said. A kind boy named Tom saw what happened. He ran to help Mia. "Be careful!" Tom said. "There is water on the steps. Let's measure it with a stick." Mia and Tom found a long stick and measured the puddle. It was very big! They decided to be careful and wiped their shoes before climbing up the slide. After playing on the slide, Mia and Tom felt hungry. They went to Mia's house for a snack. Mia's mom made them delicious sandwiches. Mia and Tom ate their sandwiches and talked about their fun day at the park. They were happy they could help each other and learn to measure the puddle.
