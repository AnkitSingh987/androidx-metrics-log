Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Stick

One day, a little girl named Jane was playing in her room. She saw a big cabinet. It was tall and flexible. Jane wanted to climb the cabinet to get her toy from the top. She started to climb, but the cabinet was moving too much. "Stop!" said her mom. "That is not safe." Jane was sad. She wanted her toy. Her mom had an idea. She went out and came back with a long stick. She used the stick to get the toy from the top of the cabinet. Jane was happy. She learned that she should listen to her mom. She also learned that there is always a better way to do things. And the cabinet stayed safe and flexible.

Lily was a brave girl who liked to play doctor with her toys. She had a white coat, a stethoscope, and a pretend x-ray machine. She would check her toys' hearts, ears, and bones, and tell them what was wrong and how to get better. One day, she decided to play doctor with her little brother, Max. She asked him to lie down on the couch and put a sheet over him. She said, "I'm going to take an x-ray of your chest. Don't move or it won't work." She turned on the pretend x-ray machine and made a beeping sound. Max was scared. He didn't know what an x-ray was. He thought it was a real machine that could hurt him. He started to cry and wiggle under the sheet. He said, "No, no, no! I don't want an x-ray! It's scary!" Lily was surprised. She didn't mean to scare her brother. She turned off the pretend x-ray machine and lifted the sheet. She said, "Don't cry, Max. It's just a game. An x-ray is not scary. It's harmless. It's a way to see inside your body and make sure everything is okay. It doesn't hurt at all. I'm sorry I made you afraid." Max sniffed and looked at Lily. He said, "Really? It's harmless? It doesn't hurt?" Lily nodded and hugged him. She said, "Really. It's harmless. It doesn't hurt. I love you, Max. I just wanted to play with you." Max hugged her back and said, "I love you, too, Lily. I'm sorry I cried. Can we play something else?" Lily smiled and said, "Sure. How about we play tea party? You can be the guest and I'll be the host." Max agreed and they went to get their cups and saucers. They learned that playing doctor is fun, but playing tea party is more fun. They also learned that x-rays are harmless, but words can be scary. They decided to be careful with their words and not frighten each other. They had a happy afternoon together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to travel and walk around. One day, Max went to a big tree near his house. He saw a red ball stuck in the tree. Max wanted to get the ball, but he was too small. A big, kind bird saw Max and wanted to help. The bird flew up and got the ball for Max. Max was very happy. As a thank you, Max gave the bird a delicious cookie. The bird was so happy too. They both became good friends. The moral of this story is to always help others when you can. Sometimes, something unexpected can happen, like making a new friend. And when you help others, they may help you back or give you something nice in return.

One day, a magnet named Max was very selfish. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. Max would always cover his toys with a big blanket so no one could see them. One day, Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to his house to play. Lily saw the big blanket and asked, "Max, what is under the blanket? Can we play with it?" Max did not want to share, so he said, "No, you cannot see. It is a secret." While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the blanket away. All the toys were now in the open. Lily was surprised and said, "Wow, Max! You have so many toys. Why didn't you want to share?" Max felt bad for being selfish. He decided to share his toys with Lily and all his friends from that day on.
