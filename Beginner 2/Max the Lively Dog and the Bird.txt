Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Lively Dog and the Bird

One day, a lively dog named Max found a trumpet in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and ran home. Max wanted to play the trumpet, but he did not know how. So, he went to ask his friend, Cat. "Cat, can you help me play the trumpet?" Max asked. Cat shook her head and said, "No, Max. I do not know how to play the trumpet." Max was sad, but he did not give up. He went to ask his other friend, Bird. "Bird, can you help me play the trumpet?" Max asked. Bird chirped and said, "Yes, Max! I can help you." Bird taught Max how to play the trumpet. They played loud and lively music together. All the animals in the park came to listen and dance. Max was very happy and thanked Bird for helping him.

Lila and Tom are playing in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. The sun is bright and warm. They see their shadows on the ground. They try to catch them, but they cannot. "Look, Tom, your shadow is big and long!" Lila says. "Look, Lila, your shadow is small and round!" Tom says. They laugh and make funny shapes with their shadows. They pretend to be animals and monsters and stars. But then, a big cloud comes and covers the sun. The shadows disappear. Lila and Tom are sad. "Where did our shadows go?" Lila asks. "They went away with the sun," Tom says. "Will they come back?" Lila asks. "Maybe, if the cloud goes away," Tom says. They wait and wait, but the cloud does not go away. It gets darker and colder. Lila and Tom shiver. "Let's go home, Lila," Tom says. "OK, Tom," Lila says. They hold hands and walk to their mom. She is sitting on a bench. She has a big smile and a warm hug for them. "Did you have fun, my loves?" she asks. "Yes, mom, we did. We played with our shadows," Lila says. "But then they went away," Tom says. "Don't worry, my loves. Your shadows will come back when the sun comes back. They always stay with you, even when you can't see them. They are your friends," mom says. "Really, mom?" Lila asks. "Really, my love. Now, let's go home and have some hot chocolate. How does that sound?" mom says. "Yummy, mom. Thank you, mom," Lila and Tom say. They hug mom and get in the car. They look out the window and see the cloud moving. The sun peeks out and makes the sky clear and blue. They see their shadows again, waving at them. "Look, mom, our shadows are back!" Lila and Tom say. "I see them, my loves. They are happy to see you, too," mom says. Lila and Tom smile and wave back. They are happy, too. They love their shadows. They love their mom. They love each other. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot liked to help people. One day, he saw a man named Tom. Tom was trying to put on a tie. But the tie was too hard for Tom to put on. Spot went to Tom and said, "Let me help you with your tie." But Tom refused. He said, "No, I can do it by myself." Spot knew that Tom needed help, but he did not want to make Tom sad. So, Spot went away and found a book about ties. He read the book and learned how to put on a tie. The next day, Spot went back to Tom. He said, "Look, I found a book about ties. Let's read it together." Tom agreed, and they read the book. With Spot's help, Tom finally put on his tie. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Spot. I'm glad I let you help me." The moral of the story is that it's okay to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car was sad because it did not like traffic. It wanted to become something else. One day, the car saw a big truck. The truck was stuck in traffic too. The little car wanted to help the big truck. So, it tried to push the truck out of the way. But the car was too small and could not move the truck. Then, something unexpected happened. The little car became a big car! With its new size, it pushed the truck out of the traffic. The truck was happy, and the big car was not sad anymore. They both drove away from the traffic and had a fun day together.
