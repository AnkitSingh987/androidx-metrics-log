Category: Beginner
Title: The Billboard and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in the middle of the town. The billboard had a picture of a happy cat on it. All the kids loved to look at the cat and smile. One day, the cat on the billboard started to feel uncomfortable. It was too hot, and the sun was shining too much. The cat wished it could disappear and find a cooler place to stay. Then, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The cat on the billboard felt better and didn't want to disappear anymore. The kids kept looking at the happy cat and smiled. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little cat named Tim lived in a comfortable house. One day, Tim went out to play in the yard. He saw a big hedge and decided to peek inside. As Tim peeked into the hedge, he saw a small mouse named Max. Tim said, "Hi, Max! Do you want to play with me?" Max said, "Yes! Let's play hide and seek." They both started to play, taking turns hiding and seeking. While playing, they heard a loud noise. They peeked out of the hedge and saw a big dog. The dog was not mean, but he was scared too. Tim and Max said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play with us?" The dog said, "Yes! I am so happy to have new friends!" Then, Tim, Max, and the dog played together, and they all became best friends.

One day, a big ostrich named Ollie went for a walk. He liked to walk in the sun and look at the trees. Ollie was very happy. Ollie saw a small rock on the path. He did not see it before. He thought, "Maybe I can kick the rock and surprise my friend." Ollie kicked the rock very hard. But then, Ollie's foot felt hurt. He kicked the rock too hard. His friend saw him and came to help. Ollie's friend was surprised to see Ollie's hurt foot. They both learned to be careful when they play.

One day, a big cat and a little dog went for a walk. They saw a huge bridge. The cat said, "Let's go on the bridge!" The dog was scared, but he said, "Okay, let's go." They walked on the bridge and saw a big river under it. The cat and the dog were very thirsty. They saw a cup near the bridge. The cat said, "We should drink from the cup!" The dog was not sure, but he said, "Okay, let's drink." They drank the water from the cup, and it was very good. After they drank, a big bird came. The bird said, "Thank you for drinking my water!" The cat and the dog were happy they drank the water. They made a new friend, the bird. They all played on the bridge and had a fun day.

John was so eager to go to his class. He was only three years old, but he loved learning. As he walked into the classroom, he was greeted by his teacher, Mrs Smith. "Good morning, John!" said Mrs Smith cheerfully. John smiled and replied, "Good morning Mrs Smith!" Mrs Smith smiled back. She knew John was always eager to learn. John looked around the classroom. He saw the colourful pictures on the wall and books neatly stacked on the shelf. Mrs Smith bent down to give John a big kiss on the cheek. "Remember, be eager to learn," she said. John nodded and eagerly ran to the front of the room. He was ready to start learning.

Lila and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hole in the ground. It was a pit. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's go in the pit!" Tom said. "OK, but be careful!" Lila said. They climbed down the pit. It was dark and dirty. They saw something shiny. It was a necklace. It had a red stone. Lila picked it up. "Look, Tom, a necklace!" Lila said. "Wow, it's pretty!" Tom said. They heard a growl. It was a dog. It was big and black. It had a wound on its leg. It looked angry and scared. "Uh-oh, a dog!" Tom said. "Run, Lila, run!" Lila said. They ran to the pit. The dog chased them. It was faster. It bit Tom's arm. Tom cried. Lila screamed. "Help, help!" Lila said. A man came. He was a vet. He saw the dog and the kids. He had a bag. He took out a needle and a bandage. "Don't worry, kids, I'm here to help!" the man said. He gave the dog a shot. The dog calmed down. He wrapped the wound on its leg. He gave it some food and water. "You're a good dog, aren't you?" the man said. He looked at Tom's arm. He cleaned it and put a bandage on it. "You're a brave boy, aren't you?" the man said. He looked at Lila's necklace. He smiled. "That's a very unusual necklace, where did you find it?" the man said. Lila told him about the pit and the necklace. "That's amazing, this necklace is very old and rare, it's from a lost civilization, it has healing powers!" the man said. "Really?" Lila said. "Yes, really, it can heal people and animals, it's a magic necklace!" the man said. "Wow, can I keep it?" Lila said. "Sure, you can keep it, but you have to use it wisely and kindly, OK?" the man said. "OK, thank you, mister!" Lila said. "Thank you, mister!" Tom said. The man helped them out of the pit. He took the dog with him. He said he would find it a good home. Lila and Tom hugged the dog goodbye. They hugged the man too. They went back to their parents. They showed them the necklace. They told them the story. They were happy. They learned a lot. They made a new friend. They had a big adventure.
