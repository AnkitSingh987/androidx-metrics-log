Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a dirty cord in her toy box. She did not like the dirty cord and wanted to throw it away. But her toy bear, Mr. Snuggles, said, "No, don't throw it away! I need it to fix my arm." Lucy looked at Mr. Snuggles and saw his arm was broken. She felt sad and started to weep. Mr. Snuggles said, "Don't cry, Lucy. We can fix my arm together with the cord." Lucy stopped weeping and said, "Okay, let's fix your arm, Mr. Snuggles." They worked together to tie the dirty cord around Mr. Snuggles' arm. Soon, his arm was fixed and he could hug Lucy again. Lucy was happy and gave Mr. Snuggles a big hug. They played happily ever after.

Sam liked to play with his puzzle. He had a big puzzle with many pieces. The puzzle had a picture of a farm with animals and a tractor. Sam wanted to finish the puzzle and see the whole farm. But his sister Lily wanted to play with her toy car. She had a red car that could zoom very fast. She liked to zoom the car on the floor and make loud noises. She did not care about Sam's puzzle. One day, Sam was working on his puzzle in the living room. He had almost done the puzzle. He only needed a few more pieces. He was looking for the piece with the tractor's wheel. But he could not find it. Then he heard a zoom and a crash. He looked up and saw Lily's car on the floor. It had hit his puzzle and made the pieces fly everywhere. Some pieces were under the sofa, some were on the table, and some were in Lily's car. Sam was very sad and angry. He shouted at Lily. "You are a bad sister! You broke my puzzle! Go away!" Lily was scared and sorry. She did not mean to break Sam's puzzle. She just wanted to have fun. She started to cry. "I'm sorry, Sam! I didn't see your puzzle! Please don't be mad!" But Sam was too mad to listen. He said harsh words to Lily. He told her to leave him alone. He did not want to play with her anymore. Lily ran to her room and sobbed. She felt very bad. She wished Sam would forgive her. She wished they could play together again. She loved her brother, even when he was harsh.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cat. The cat was her own. The cat was nice and soft. Lily liked to play with the cat. They played with a ball. One day, Lily and the cat went to the park. They saw a big tree. The cat saw a bird. The cat wanted to catch the bird. The cat ran up the tree. Lily was sad. She wanted her cat to come down. Lily had an idea. She went home and got a tasty treat. She called her cat. The cat smelled the treat. The cat came down from the tree. Lily was happy. She gave the cat the tasty treat. They went home and played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a fine little girl named Lily. She loved to wear her mom's jewels. One day, she found a big red jewel in a box. Lily was so happy to find it. Lily went to her friend Tom's house to play. She showed Tom the big red jewel. Tom liked it too. They played with the jewel all day. When it was time to go home, Lily forgot to take the jewel with her. That night, Lily could not sleep. She was sad because she forgot the jewel. The next day, Lily went back to Tom's house. Tom had the big red jewel. He gave it back to her. Lily was so happy. She learned that it is important to keep her things close and take care of them. From that day on, Lily always kept her things close and safe. She never lost anything again. The big red jewel taught Lily a good lesson. And she and Tom stayed friends forever.
