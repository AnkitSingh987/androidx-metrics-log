Category: Beginner
Title: Tim Finds the Cat

One day, Tim and his chubby cat, Sam, went to visit their friend, Ben. Ben lived in a red house near the park. Tim and Sam liked to play with Ben a lot. They had so much fun together. But today, Tim saw a big bike with pedals just outside Ben's house. Tim wanted to ride the bike, but Sam did not like the bike. Sam hissed at the bike and tried to scratch it. Tim got mad at Sam and told him to stop. Tim tried to ride the bike, but he did not know how to use the pedals. He pushed and pushed, but the bike did not move. Sam sat and watched, still mad at Tim. Then, the bike fell over and broke. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not play with Ben now. The day had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a big orange cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friends, the mice. One day, the mice were playing near the corn field, and they got lost. Tom heard the mice calling for help, "Help! Help! We are lost in the corn!" Tom knew he had to rescue them. He ran to the corn field and started to look for his friends. Tom searched and searched until he found the mice. They were so happy to see him. "Thank you, Tom, for rescuing us!" said the mice. Tom and the mice went back to their homes, and they all played together again. They were very careful not to go near the corn field again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He was very organized. He had a big boat with a sail. The sailor loved to sail on the water. One day, the sky was blue, and the sun was hot. The sailor thought it was a good day to sail. The sailor got his boat ready. He made sure everything was dry. He started to sail. The wind blew, and the boat went fast. The sailor was happy. But, the sky started to get dark. The sailor did not know what was going to happen. Then, the wind stopped. The boat stopped too. The sailor was sad. He waited and waited, but the wind did not come back. The sailor was stuck on the water. He could not sail back home. The sailor was not happy anymore.

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to play hockey. He went outside with his hockey stick and ball. He saw his friend, Sam, and asked, "Do you want to play hockey with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to play!" But Sam did not have a hockey stick. He picked up a silly-looking stick that was too big. Tom said, "That's too big! Let's remove the big part." So, they removed the big part of the stick. Now, Sam had a hockey stick too. They started to play hockey together. They were having lots of fun. Suddenly, a big dog came running and took the ball! Tom and Sam were surprised! They ran after the dog to get their ball back. The dog was fast, but Tom and Sam were faster. They got the ball and continued to play hockey, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy magnet. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He was eager to show his magnet to his friends. At the park, Tim met his friend, Jane. "Look at my magnet!" Tim said to Jane. "Wow!" Jane said, "I want to try it!" They played with the magnet, picking up little things like pins and coins. Then, they saw a big puddle. Tim and Jane ran to the puddle and started to splash. They laughed and splashed some more. But then, Tim's magnet fell into the puddle. They were sad. Just then, a frog jumped out of the puddle. It had Tim's magnet in its mouth! The frog gave the magnet back to Tim. They were so surprised! Tim and Jane said, "Thank you, frog!" They were happy again and played with the magnet for the rest of the day.
