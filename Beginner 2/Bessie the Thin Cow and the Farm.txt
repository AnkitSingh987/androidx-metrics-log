Category: Beginner
Title: Bessie the Thin Cow and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a thin cow named Bessie. She lived on a farm with many other animals. Bessie loved to laugh and play with her friends all day long. One sunny day, Bessie and her friends decided to have a big party. They wanted to eat their favorite food, beef. Bessie was very excited and she laughed as she helped her friends get ready for the party. When the party started, all the animals laughed and danced together. They shared the yummy beef and had a lot of fun. Bessie laughed the most, and her friends loved her happy laugh. They all had a great time at the party, and they couldn't wait for the next one.

Tim and Lily were friends who liked to play near the pond. The pond had many fish and frogs and ducks. Sometimes they fed the ducks with bread. They had fun together. One day, Tim found a huge stick near the pond. He wanted to use it as a sword. He swung it around and made noises. Lily wanted to play with the stick too. She asked Tim to share it with her. "No, it's mine!" Tim said. "Go away!" Lily felt sad and angry. She tried to grab the stick from Tim. They fought over the stick. They pushed and pulled and shouted. "Let go!" Tim said. "You are mean!" "No, you are mean!" Lily said. "You are selfish!" They did not see the duck that was swimming near them. The duck was scared by the noise and the stick. It quacked loudly and bit Tim's hand. Tim dropped the stick and cried. Lily laughed. "Ha ha, that's what you get!" Lily said. "The duck is mad at you!" Tim felt hurt and angry. He picked up the stick and threw it at Lily. The stick hit Lily's head. Lily fell down and cried. She had blood on her face. Tim saw what he did. He felt sorry and scared. He ran away from the pond. He did not help Lily. He did not say sorry. He did not forgive Lily. He did not want to be her friend anymore. Lily was alone and hurt. She did not have the stick. She did not have Tim. She did not forgive Tim. She did not want to be his friend anymore. The pond was quiet and sad. The fish and frogs and ducks did not like to see Tim and Lily fight. They wished they could be friends again. But they did not know how.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big slide. Sara wants to go on the slide, but Ben is scared. "Come on, Ben, it is fun!" Sara says. "You can use my belt to hold on." Sara gives Ben her belt. Ben puts it around his waist. He follows Sara to the top of the slide. "Ready, set, go!" Sara says. She slides down fast. She laughs and claps. Ben looks down. He feels dizzy. He does not want to slide. He tries to go back, but he slips. He screams. Sara hears Ben. She looks up. She sees Ben sliding down too. But his belt is open. It does not hold him. He falls off the slide. He hits the ground hard. He does not move. Sara runs to Ben. She cries. She calls for help. But it is too late. Ben is hurt. He does not wake up. He becomes still. Sara loses her friend.

One day, a charming girl named Lily went to her club. At the club, they like to make art. Lily wanted to print a pretty picture. She found her friend, Tom, who was also at the club. "Hi Tom," said Lily. "I want to print a pretty picture. Will you help me?" "Sure, Lily!" said Tom. They found the big machine that prints pictures. They put in some paper and picked a picture of a cat. They were very excited to see the cat picture. But something unexpected happened. When the machine printed the picture, it was not a cat. It was a dog! Lily and Tom were surprised. They laughed and laughed. They showed their friends the funny dog picture. Everyone at the club liked the surprise.
