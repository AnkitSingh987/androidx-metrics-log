Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ball

One day, Tom and Sam went to play. They wanted to build something fun. They thought and thought. "Let's build an icy hockey rink!" said Tom. Sam was excited. They found a big space and started to build. They made the ground very icy. Then, they got sticks and a ball. They put on their shoes that could slide on the ice. They were ready to play hockey. Tom hit the ball with his stick. "Look, I can play hockey!" he said. Sam tried too. They played and played. They had lots of fun on their icy hockey rink.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Sam. Sam was happy and loved to play. One day Sam's mom wanted to introduce him to a spider. He was a little scared, but his mom said the spider was harmless. So, Sam sat and watched the spider. He was a funny looking creature with lots of legs and a scrunched-up face. But, as he watched, he felt more and more comfortable. He even started to smile and laugh. Sam's mom said it was time to go and said goodbye to the spider. Surprisingly, the spider waved goodbye! Sam was so amazed that he was even more curious to come back. The next day, Sam returned with his mom to say hello to the spider again. He was excited to see the spider, but he was surprised to see that it was different. Its face was bigger and there were now two spiders! Sam was curious and asked why there were now two spiders. His mom explained that the little spider was the spider’s baby. They had multiplied! Sam was amazed and thought it was the coolest thing ever. He had come to understand that the harmless spider he had first introduced to was now a family!

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride the bus to the fair. He asked his mom, "Can we go to the fair today?" His mom said, "Yes, we can ride the bus to the fair." They walked to the bus stop and waited for the bus. When the bus came, they got on and found a seat. Tim was happy to ride the bus. He looked out the window and saw many things, like cars, trees, and a big dog. When they got to the fair, Tim and his mom got off the bus. They saw a big wheel, fun games, and yummy food. They played games and ate ice cream. At the end of the day, Tim said, "I had so much fun at the fair and riding the bus!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it was very wet outside because it rained. But Tim still wanted to play. Tim put on his boots and coat. He went to the door and opened it. He saw the gate was open. Tim's mom said, "Tim, don't leave the gate open." Tim closed the gate and started to play. He jumped in wet puddles and laughed. Tim had so much fun playing in the wet yard. When he was done, he went back to the gate. He remembered to close it. Tim's mom was happy, and they went inside to get warm and dry.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a brave boy who loved to help his mom. One day, his mom was setting the table for dinner. She asked Tim, "Can you please bring the plates to the table?" Tim said, "Yes, mom!" and went to get the plates. As Tim carried the plates, he saw a big, scary bug on the floor. It was trying to climb on the table too! Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. He said, "No, bug! You can't come to the table!" The bug didn't listen and kept trying to climb. Tim thought hard and came up with a plan. He put the plates on the table and went to get a cup. He put the cup over the bug and said, "Now you can't get on the table!" His mom saw what he did and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are so brave!" Tim felt happy and proud that he could help his mom and keep the bug away from the table.
