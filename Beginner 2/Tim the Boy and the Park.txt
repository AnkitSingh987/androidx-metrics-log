Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tim found an ugly bug. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim put the bug in a jar and went to Sam's house. "Look, Sam! I found an ugly bug," said Tim. Sam looked at the bug and made a sign with his hands that he didn't like it. Tim felt sad. He wanted Sam to like the bug too. They went to the park to play. Tim left the jar with the ugly bug under a tree. When they came back, the jar was gone. A man was making a film nearby. The ugly bug had gone into the man's camera, and the film was ruined. The man was angry and told the boys to go away. Tim and Sam felt bad. They learned that they should not leave their things in other people's way.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went to a big place called university. He wanted to learn new things and make friends. Tom was very excited to be there. In one room, Tom met a dog named Max. Max said, "Hi, I'm Max. Let's learn and play together!" Tom liked Max and they became friends. They went to fun classes and played games during breaks. One day, while playing, they found a secret door. They opened it and found a big room full of toys! Tom and Max were very happy. They played with the toys and shared them with their friends. The university became even more fun for everyone.

Joe was a 3 year old who was determined to win a medal. He practiced all week for the race and was determined to come out on top. The day of the race came and Joe was feeling excited and ready to race. Joe and his friends lined up at the starting line. Joe was feeling confident he would win the race, but when the whistle blew, all of Joe's friends took off running, leaving Joe way behind. Joe kept running as hard as he could and didn't give up hope. Soon he was catching up to the lead racer. With one last push, Joe pulled ahead and crossed the finish line first! Everyone cheered and the judge gave Joe a medal. Joe was so proud and happy. He was even more proud when the judge said that he had run with grace and skill. Joe knew that he had earned the medal!

Lila loved her yellow ball. She played with it every day in the park. She threw it high and far, and it always bounced back to her. She liked to catch it and hug it. It was her loyal friend. One day, a big dog ran after her ball. It was faster than Lila and grabbed the ball in its mouth. It ran away with it, wagging its tail. Lila was sad and scared. She cried and called for her ball. "Come back, ball! Come back!" she shouted. The dog's owner saw what happened and ran after the dog. He caught up with it and took the ball from its mouth. He was sorry and said to Lila: "I'm sorry, little girl. My dog likes balls too. He didn't mean to take yours. Here, you can have it back." He gave the ball to Lila, who smiled and thanked him. She was happy to have her ball again. She hugged it and said: "I missed you, ball. You are my loyal friend." She threw the ball again and it bounced back to her. She caught it and laughed. The dog watched and barked. He wanted to play too. Lila saw the dog and said: "Do you want to play with us, dog? You can chase the ball, but you have to return it to me, okay?" The dog nodded and wagged his tail. He liked Lila and her ball. He was a loyal friend too. They played together in the park until it was time to go home. Lila said goodbye to the dog and his owner. She took her ball with her and said: "See you tomorrow, ball. See you tomorrow, dog. You are both my loyal friends."

One day, a big truck arrived in town. It was carrying many things. One of the things was a leather ball. A little boy named Tim saw the truck and wanted the leather ball to play with. Tim asked his mom if he could have the ball. His mom said yes and bought the ball for him. Tim was very happy and he played with the ball all day. But then, a big dog came and took the ball away. Tim was angry and sad. He wanted his ball back. So, he asked his mom for help. They found the dog, and Tim's mom asked the dog nicely to give the ball back. The dog gave the ball back and Tim was happy again. He played with the leather ball and made sure to keep it safe from the big dog.
