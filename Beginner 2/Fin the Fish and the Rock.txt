Category: Beginner
Title: Fin the Fish and the Rock

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He saw a big rock and wanted to dive under it. Fin was a persistent fish and always tried new things. He swam fast and took a deep breath. Then, he dove under the rock. As Fin was swimming, he saw a crab. The crab waved his hand and said, "Hi, Fin! Do you want to play with me?" Fin smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played hide and seek in the sea. Fin was good at hiding because he could dive under rocks and plants. After they played, Fin and the crab said goodbye. Fin swam back to his family. He told them about his fun day and how he learned to dive under the big rock. His family was happy and proud of him. They all hugged Fin with their fins and said, "Good job, Fin!"

Once there was a little girl named Liza. She had a waffle that she loved very much. Everywhere she went, she took it with her. One day, she went to the park and saw a large bird with its beak wide open. She was very curious and wanted to touch the bird. Liza stepped closer and said, "Hello birdie, can I touch you?" But the bird was very bossy and said, "No! Get away from me!" Liza was sad and went to sit on a bench. She looked at her waffle and said, "Oh waffle, why can't I touch the bird?" Suddenly, the bird flew down and said, "I'm sorry. You can touch me if you want." Liza was so excited, she reached out to touch the soft feathers. The bird chirped happily and flew away. Liza smiled and hugged her waffle, happy that she got to touch the bird.

One day, a girl named Sue found a big box. In the box, she saw a record. She did not know what it was. She asked her mom, "What is this?" Her mom said, "That is a record. It plays music. We need a record player to hear it." Sue wanted to hear the music, so they found a record player. They put the record on the player and started to listen. Music from an unknown band began to play. Sue and her mom liked the music a lot. They danced and laughed together. Sue asked her mom, "Who is this band?" Her mom said, "I don't know, but they are good." They kept dancing and having fun. From that day on, Sue and her mom loved the unknown band. They played the record every day and danced together. The music made them very happy.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play together at school. They are popular. Many kids want to play with them. But Ben and Mia do not share. They keep the best toys for themselves. They do not let other kids join them. One day, it is time for nap. The teacher tells the kids to go to the hall. There are mats and blankets in the hall. The kids can sleep on the mats and cover with the blankets. The teacher says, "Find a mat and a blanket. Be quiet and sleep well." Ben and Mia run to the hall. They want to find the best mat and the best blanket. They see a big mat and a soft blanket. They say, "This is ours. We want this one." They grab the mat and the blanket. But another kid is there. His name is Sam. He says, "No, this is mine. I saw it first. I want this one." He holds the mat and the blanket. Ben and Mia do not listen. They pull the mat and the blanket. Sam pulls too. They start to fight. They yell and cry. They make a lot of noise. The teacher hears them. She comes to the hall. She sees them fighting. She is angry. She says, "Stop it! You are being rude and selfish. You are not sharing. You are not napping. You are disturbing the other kids. You are in trouble." The teacher takes the mat and the blanket away. She gives them to Sam. She says, "Sam, you can have this mat and this blanket. You were here first. You were polite. You can nap here." She takes Ben and Mia to another room. She says, "Ben and Mia, you have to sit on the floor. You have no mat and no blanket. You have to stay awake. You have to think about what you did. You have to say sorry to Sam and the other kids. You have to learn to share and be nice." Ben and Mia are sad and cold. They wish they had a mat and a blanket. They wish they had napped. They wish they had shared and been nice. They are not popular anymore. The other kids do not like them. They have no friends. They have a bad ending.
