Category: Beginner
Title: Sue and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play in the park. She saw a big tree and thought it was interesting. Sue wanted to part the leaves and see what was behind the tree. She walked closer and saw a shadow. Sue was scared of the shadow, but she wanted to know what it was. She took a deep breath and walked around the tree. To her surprise, she found a little kitten. The kitten was the one making the shadow! Sue was so happy to find the kitten. She picked it up and gave it a hug. The kitten was not scary at all. Sue took the kitten home and they became best friends. The unexpected shadow turned out to be a new friend for Sue.

One sunny day, a girl named Lily and her dog, Max, were excited to go on a holiday. They packed their bags and left their little house. They walked down the road, looking for a fun place to play. Soon, they saw a big park with a playground. Lily and Max ran to the swings and the slide. Lily saw a long, thin beam. She wanted to balance on it. Max barked, "Woof, woof!" as if he was saying, "Be careful, Lily!" Lily put one foot on the beam and then the other. She held her arms out wide and slowly walked across. Max wagged his tail and barked, "Woof, woof!" to cheer her on. Lily made it to the other side, and they both laughed and played all day. At the end of their holiday, Lily and Max were tired but happy. They walked back to their little house, holding hands and wagging tails. They could not wait for their next exciting adventure.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily got a new toy. It was a pretty doll. Lily was very happy. She wanted to play outside with her new toy. She put on her shoes and went to the park. In the park, Lily saw her shadow. It was fun to skip with her shadow. She skipped and her shadow skipped too. Lily and her new toy skipped with the shadow all around the park. But then, a big dog came. The big dog saw Lily's new toy. It wanted to play too. The dog took the toy from Lily. Lily was very sad. She tried to get her toy back, but the dog ran away. Lily and her shadow could not skip anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a helpful girl who loved to add things to her meals. One day, she saw a small, helpless bird outside her window. The bird looked hungry and cold. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, can we help the bird? It looks hungry." Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's add some food to its meal." They went to the kitchen and found some bread and seeds. Lily and her mom went outside and put the bread and seeds on the ground. The helpless bird came and ate the meal. It was very happy. Lily felt good that she could help the bird. From that day on, she always looked for ways to help others.

Anna likes to skip. She skips in the park, she skips in the yard, she skips in the house. She skips with her friends, she skips with her dog, she skips with her mom. Skipping makes her happy and warm. One day, Anna skips too fast. She trips on a rock and falls down. She hurts her knee and her thumb. She cries and cries. Her mom runs to her and hugs her. She says, "It's okay, Anna. You are brave and strong. Let me see your thumb and your knee." Mom looks at Anna's thumb and knee. She says, "They are not bad. Just a little red and sore. I have a band-aid and some ice. They will make you feel better." She puts a band-aid on Anna's thumb and wraps some ice in a cloth. She puts the cloth on Anna's knee. She kisses Anna's thumb and knee. Anna stops crying. She says, "Thank you, mom. You are kind and sweet. I love you." She hugs her mom. Mom says, "I love you too, Anna. You are my precious girl. Do you want to skip some more?" Anna says, "Yes, mom. But not too fast. I don't want to fall again." Mom and Anna hold hands and skip together. They skip in the park, they skip in the yard, they skip in the house. They skip with their friends, they skip with their dog, they skip with their dad. Skipping makes them happy and warm. They feel peaceful and safe.
