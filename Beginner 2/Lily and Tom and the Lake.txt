Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Lake

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. A girl named Lily and a boy named Tom loved to play near the lake. They were honest friends who always shared their toys. One day, Lily and Tom found a pretty rock by the lake. Lily said, "I love this rock! Let's share it." Tom agreed, "Yes, we can share the rock." They played with the rock by the lake every day. They took turns holding it and always put it back by the lake when they went home. Lily and Tom were happy and honest friends who loved the lake and their pretty rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sail. He loved to play with his sail in the hot sun. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was weeping. Tim asked, "Why do you weep, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my toy. I am sad." Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. Tim showed Sue his sail. He said, "Let's play with my sail." Sue stopped weeping and they played together. They had fun in the hot sun. Tim and Sue became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Benny. He was three years old and he loved playing outside. One day Benny was walking in the park. It was icy cold but he was wrapped up warm. He saw a big lock on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. When Benny got home his mum asked him where he got the lock. Benny said he found it in the park. His mum was cross and she said: "Take it back! You shouldn't take things that don't belong to you!" Benny didn't want to but he had to. He returned to the park and looked for the lock but it was gone. Suddenly he heard a loud laugh coming from a nearby bush. An icy chill ran down his spine. When he looked inside he saw a big man with the lock in his hand. The man grinned and said: "Looks like you want to give me this lock!" Benny was too scared to say anything, so he slowly nodded and gave the lock to the man. And that was the end of Benny's adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. He liked to throw a ball. Tim had a dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with the ball too. They played every day. One day, Tim and Spot played near a big tree. Tim threw the ball high. It did not come back down. They both looked up. The ball was stuck in the tree. Tim and Spot tried to get the ball down. But it was too high. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down from the tree. The bird had the ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball and flew away. Tim and Spot were happy. They played with the ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a messy rabbit named Bob. Bob liked to rush and play in the forest. One day, as Bob was rushing, he saw a big, dark cloud. The cloud had lightning inside. Bob was scared, so he ran to his friend, the wise owl. "Owl, I saw lightning in the sky! What should I do?" asked the messy rabbit. The wise owl thought for a moment and said, "Bob, when lightning comes, you must go home and stay safe." Bob listened to the wise owl and rushed back to his home. He waited there until the lightning went away. When the sky was clear, Bob went back to play in the forest. He was happy that he listened to the wise owl. The moral of the story is: listen to wise friends, and they will help you stay safe.

The sun was in the sky and it was warm. A little girl was walking in the park when she saw a cloud. She was excited and wanted to touch it! As she put her hand out to touch it, the cloud began to melt. It felt cold and wet and the little girl was hurt. She began to cry because she felt sad. The cloud formed a big puddle on the ground. The little girl stepped in it and her feet got wet. She was mad and stomped around the puddle. It made her even more sad. The little girl looked up to the sky and started to cry harder. She was so disappointed that her dreams of touching the cloud had melted away. The sun had gone down and it was time to go home. She sadly walked away with a hurt in her heart.
