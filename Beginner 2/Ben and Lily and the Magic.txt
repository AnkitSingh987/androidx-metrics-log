Title: Ben and Lily and the Magic

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to dig holes, find bugs and make mud pies. They had a lot of fun, but they also got very dirty and tired. "Let's go inside and have a snack," Lily said. "I'm hungry and thirsty." "OK," Ben agreed. "But first, let's hide this key. It's a magic key that can open any door. We don't want anyone to find it." They looked around and saw a big flower pot. They buried the key under the soil and covered it with some leaves. "Shh, don't tell anyone," Ben whispered. "It's our secret." They ran inside and washed their hands and faces. Then they ate some cookies and drank some milk. They felt much better, but they also felt sleepy. "Can we take a nap?" Lily asked. "Sure," Ben said. "But we have to remember where we hid the key. Maybe we can dream about it." They went to their room and lay down on their beds. They closed their eyes and soon they fell asleep. They dreamed of magic doors and adventures. They smiled in their sleep. They were happy.

One day, a little dog named Spot went out to play. It was a sunny day and Spot was very happy. He ran and jumped in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to see what was behind it. As he went around the tree, he found a small, sorry-looking cat. The cat was wet and dirty. "Hello, cat," said Spot. "Why do you look so sad?" The cat looked up and said, "I am sorry, but I fell in a puddle and now I am wet and cold. I don't know how to get clean and warm." Spot thought for a moment and said, "I can help you. Let's go to my house." At Spot's house, they found a soft towel. Spot used the towel to wipe the cat clean and dry. The cat started to feel warm and happy again. They spent the rest of the day playing and having fun. As the night came, the cat said, "Thank you, Spot, for helping me today. I have a secret to tell you. I am not just a cat, I am a magic cat!" Spot was very surprised. The magic cat said, "As a thank you for helping me, I will give you one wish. What would you like?" Spot thought for a moment and said, "I wish for more sunny days to play with my new friend." The magic cat smiled and with a wave of her paw, she made Spot's wish come true. From that day on, Spot and the magic cat had many sunny days to play together.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to design a big, pretty picture on the floor. She took her colors and began to draw. Sue was so happy with her picture. Then, her mom came in with a mop. "We need to clean the floor," said Mom. Sue did not want her picture to be gone. She thought hard and had an idea. "Wait, Mom!" Sue said. "Can we use the mop to make the picture clean?" Mom thought about it and smiled. "Yes, let's try!" So, they put water and soap on the mop and cleaned the picture. The colors mixed and made a new, pretty design on the floor. Sue and Mom were so happy with their new clean art.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Tim. Tim was a very worry car. He always thought that things were difficult. One day, Tim had to go up a big hill. He was very worry. As Tim went up the hill, he started to use a lot of oil. He was worry that he would run out of oil. But then, a nice big truck named Tom saw Tim. Tom wanted to help Tim. Tom said to Tim, "Don't worry, I will help you." Tom gave Tim some oil. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom." They went up the hill together. Tim learned that things are not always difficult when you have friends to help you.

One day, a cat named Max was stuck up in a tree. Max was very scared and didn't know what to do. He tried to climb down, but he was too scared to move. A little girl named Emma saw Max in the tree. She said, "Oh no, Max is stuck! I need to rescue him!" Emma was not ignorant; she knew she needed help. She ran to her dad and told him about Max. Emma's dad got a long ladder and put it against the tree. Emma's dad climbed up the ladder and reached Max. He said, "Don't worry, Max. I'll save you." He picked up Max and put him in a soft case to keep him safe. They climbed down the ladder together. Emma was so happy to see Max safe and sound. She hugged her dad and said, "Thank you for rescuing Max!"
