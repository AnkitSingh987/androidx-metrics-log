Category: Beginner
Title: The Mommy and Baby and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby. Mommy wanted to go to the store, so she asked baby to stay in the house. She said, "Baby, I'm going to the store. Please stay at home and don't open the door." Baby nodded yes, and watched mommy leave. Soon after, someone knocked on the door. It was True. He said, "Can I borrow something?" Baby opened the door and said, "Yes!" True came inside and said, "Can I borrow your mommy's jewels. They're so shiny and nice!" Baby wanted to help, so he said, "Yes!" True thanked baby and left with the jewels. It was getting icy outside, so baby stayed inside and waited for mommy. But soon, baby started to worry. What would mommy say when she found out that baby borrowed her jewels to True? When mommy came home, she saw that her jewels were gone. She was very angry. She didn't want baby to ever borrow anything to anyone ever again. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to wander around the big lawn near his home. One day, while Spot was wandering, he saw a big ball on the lawn. Spot wanted to play with the ball, but a big cat named Whiskers was sitting on it. Spot was patient and waited for Whiskers to move. But Whiskers didn't want to share the ball. Spot was sad. Then, Spot had an idea. He found a stick and showed it to Whiskers. Whiskers liked the stick and jumped off the ball to play with it. Now, Spot could play with the ball on the lawn. Spot and Whiskers learned to share and became good friends.

Tim and Sue were twins. They liked to play with their new bike. It was red and shiny and had two big wheels. They took turns to ride it in the park. One day, Tim was riding the bike and Sue was running after him. Tim saw a big hill and wanted to go down fast. He pedaled hard and felt the wind in his hair. He did not see the rock on the road. He hit the rock and fell off the bike. He hurt his leg and it bled. He started to weep. Sue ran to him and saw his leg. She was scared and sad. She said, "Tim, are you OK? Your leg is bad. We need to get help." Tim said, "It hurts, Sue. It hurts a lot. I want Mom and Dad. I want to go home." Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will get help. I will be back soon. You stay here and be brave." Sue left the bike and ran to find a grown-up. She saw a lady with a dog. She said, "Please, lady, help me. My brother fell off his bike and hurt his leg. He is weeping. He needs help." The lady said, "Oh, dear, that's terrible. Where is your brother?" Sue said, "He is over there, by the hill. Please, come with me." The lady said, "OK, I will come with you. But first, let me tie my dog to this tree. He is friendly, but he likes to chase bikes." Sue said, "Thank you, lady. You are very kind." They ran to Tim and saw his leg. The lady said, "Oh, poor boy. Your leg is bad. We need to call an ambulance. Do you have a phone?" Sue said, "No, we don't. We only have our bike." The lady said, "Don't worry, I have a phone. I will call an ambulance. They will come soon and take you to the hospital. They will make your leg better." Tim said, "Thank you, lady. You are very kind." They waited for the ambulance. Sue held Tim's hand and said, "It will be OK, Tim. You will be fine. Mom and Dad will come soon and hug you. And we will get our bike back. It is new and shiny and has two big wheels. We will ride it again, when your leg is better." Tim said, "I hope so, Sue. I hope so."

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car loved to go fast. Going fast would excite the car. It would zoom down the road with a big smile. One day, the car got a flat tire. The car felt sad. It could not go fast anymore. It wanted to feel good and excite again. The car needed help to fix the tire. A friendly truck saw the sad car. The truck knew how to fix the tire. The truck put on a new tire for the car. The car felt good again. Now the car could excite and go fast. The car and the truck became good friends. They played together and had fun.
