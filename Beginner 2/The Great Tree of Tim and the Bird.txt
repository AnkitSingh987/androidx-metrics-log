Category: Beginner
Title: The Great Tree of Tim and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the cold field. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But he was scared he might fall. So, he asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me climb this tree?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you. But we must be careful because it is cold, and the tree might be slippery." They started to climb the tree together. But as they went up, they saw a big bird in the tree. The bird was angry that they were in its home. It started to flap its wings and squawk loudly at them. "Go away!" the bird said. Tim and Sam were scared, but they did not want to leave the tree. They asked the bird if they could all play together. The bird thought about it and then said, "Okay, we can play together, but only if you promise to be nice to my home." Tim and Sam promised to be nice, and they all played together in the cold field. They had a great time, and the bird became their new friend.

One day, a little bird was in a birdcage. The bird was sad and wanted to fly. A kind girl named Lily saw the bird and wanted to help. She opened the birdcage and said, "You can trust me, little bird. I will not hurt you." The bird flew out of the cage and onto Lily's hand. They were both happy to be friends. They played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to change color. It turned red, blue, and green. Lily was surprised and the bird was embarrassed. The bird said, "I have a secret. I am a magic bird. When I am happy, I change colors." Lily was excited and said, "That's amazing! I will keep your secret safe." They both laughed and played together, knowing that they could trust each other.

Once upon a time, there was a brave bear who worked very hard. Every day the bear would go out and work. One day, while the bear was busy working, a rabbit came hopping by. "Hello," the rabbit said, "What are you doing?" The bear stopped and looked up. "I'm working," he said. He was a bit tired but he kept working. The rabbit hopped a bit closer. "I'm brave, too," he said. "I can help you if you want." The bear smiled. "That's great! I would love some help." So the brave rabbit hopped closer and waved one paw in the air. The bear waved back with both of his paws and together they finished the work. When they were done, the bear said, "Thank you for helping me." The rabbit smiled. "You're welcome," he said. "It's nice to have brave friends like you!"

One day, a cat and a dog were friends. They liked to talk and play. They wanted to discuss a new word they heard. The word was "sweet". They did not know what it meant. They wanted to find out. The cat said, "I think sweet is a big toy." The dog said, "No, I think sweet is a fun game." They could not agree. They became sad. They did not want to fight about the word. They wanted to be friends. Then, a little bird came. The bird heard the cat and the dog. The bird said, "I know the word 'sweet'! It is a yummy taste. Like a candy." The cat and the dog were happy. They thanked the bird. They all became friends and shared a sweet candy. They never fought about a word again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a yellow cat. The cat liked to play outside. One day, while playing, the cat saw a drain. The cat was very curious about the drain. The cat went near the drain and heard a sound. "Achoo!" The cat was scared. It was a sneeze! The cat looked around, but saw no one. The cat thought, "Who could it be?" The cat wanted to find out. The cat went closer to the drain. The sneeze came again, "Achoo!" The cat looked inside the drain. It saw a big, mean dog! The dog jumped out and chased the cat. The cat tried to run away, but the dog caught the cat. The yellow cat should have stayed away from the drain.
