Category: Beginner
Title: The Muffin and the Sun

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a good muffin. The muffin was very happy because it was made with love by a mom for her little girl, Lily. The good muffin smelled so nice, and it was waiting for Lily to eat it. One sunny day, Lily came into the kitchen and saw the good muffin. She was so happy and excited to eat it. But, before she could take a bite, the sun started to melt the muffin a little bit. The good muffin was not as pretty as before, but it still smelled nice. Lily didn't care that the muffin was a little melty. She knew it was made with love and would taste good. So, she took a big bite of the good muffin, and it was the best muffin she ever had. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to play in her garden. One day, she was playing hide-and-seek when a thief came into her garden. He wanted to steal all her toys! The little girl was scared so she ran and hid behind a bush. The thief saw her and chased her. He yelled, “Give me your toys!” The little girl kept running and then shouted, “No! I love my toys! Go away!” Suddenly, the thief was hit in the head by a big stick and he fell to the ground. The little girl was happy to see that the thief was hurt. She quickly ran and got help. The little girl's mom ran out and saw the thief and called the police. The police took the thief away so the little girl was safe. The little girl was so relieved and hugged her mom. She was very happy to have her toys and to know that the thief was gone forever. She loved and was loved in return.

Once upon a time, there was a big, proud cat. The cat lived in a big, pretty palace. The palace had many rooms and a big garden. One day, a small, cute dog arrived at the palace. The dog wanted to be friends with the cat. They played together in the garden. They had lots of fun. The cat and the dog became best friends. They lived in the palace together. They were very happy and proud. And they played together every day.

One day, a brave little boy named Tim went to the shop with his mom. They wanted to buy a big red ball for Tim to play with. Tim was very excited and held his mom's hand tightly as they walked to the shop. At the shop, Tim saw a cute dog with a funny ear. The dog's ear was bent like a hat. Tim asked his mom, "Can I pet the dog, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be gentle." Tim was brave and slowly reached out to pet the dog's soft fur. After they bought the big red ball, Tim and his mom walked back home. Tim was so happy to play with his new ball and he couldn't wait to tell his friends about the dog with the funny ear. The brave little boy had a great day with his mom.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. But they do not like to tidy up. Their room is messy. One morning, their mom comes in. She says, "Good morning, Lily and Ben. It is time to get up and have breakfast. But first, you need to tidy up your room. It is too messy. You can have oat and milk for breakfast. Oat is good for you. It makes you strong and smart." Lily and Ben do not want to tidy up. They want to play more. They say, "No, mom. We do not want to tidy up. We want to play. Tidying up is boring. We do not like oat. Oat is yucky. We want cookies and juice." Mom is not happy. She says, "No, Lily and Ben. You cannot have cookies and juice. They are not good for you. They make you sick and sleepy. You need to tidy up your room and have oat and milk. Or you will have no breakfast at all." Lily and Ben are scared. They do not want to have no breakfast. They are hungry. They say, "Sorry, mom. We will tidy up our room. Please do not take away our breakfast." Mom smiles. She says, "That is good, Lily and Ben. I am glad you will tidy up your room. It is not hard. You just need to put your toys and books in their places. And then you can stretch and have oat and milk. Stretching is good for you. It makes you happy and healthy." Lily and Ben nod. They start to tidy up their room. They put their toys and books in their places. They make their bed. They throw away their trash. They say, "Look, mom. We tidied up our room. It is clean and nice. We are sorry we were naughty. We will listen to you next time." Mom hugs them. She says, "Well done, Lily and Ben. I am proud of you. You tidied up your room very well. Now you can stretch and have oat and milk. And maybe a cookie as a treat. You are good twins. I love you very much." Lily and Ben smile. They stretch and have oat and milk. And a cookie. They say, "Thank you, mom. We love you too. Oat and milk are not so bad. They are yummy. And stretching is fun. We feel good."
