Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Cheerful Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she found a little beetle in her garden. The beetle was very pretty and shiny. Lily wanted to show her friend, Sam, the beetle. She ran to Sam's house with the beetle in her hand. When she got there, she said, "Look, Sam! I found a beetle. Let's play with it and share it." Sam was happy to see the beetle. They played with the beetle together, taking turns holding it. They were very careful not to hurt it. After a while, they put the beetle back in the garden. Lily and Sam were happy that they shared the beetle. They knew that sharing made playing more fun. They promised to share more things and always be cheerful friends.

Sara and Ben love to play in the snow. They make snowmen, snowballs and snow forts. They wear warm hats, coats and boots. They have fun with their dog, Spot, who likes to run and jump in the snow. One day, they find a big sled in the garage. They ask their mom if they can use it. She says yes, but be careful. They take the sled to the hill behind their house. It is wide and steep. They are excited to slide down fast. They put the sled on the snow and climb on. Sara sits in the front and Ben sits in the back. Spot runs after them. They push off and start to slide. They go faster and faster. They feel the wind in their faces. They laugh and shout. But then, they see a problem. There is a fence at the bottom of the hill. They are going too fast to stop. They are scared. They don't want to crash. Ben has an idea. He tells Sara to reverse. He means to turn the sled around. Sara tries to do it. She pulls hard on the rope. The sled turns a little bit, but not enough. They are almost at the fence. They close their eyes. They wait for the bump. But it doesn't come. They open their eyes. They see Spot. He has grabbed the rope with his teeth. He has pulled the sled away from the fence. He has saved them. They are happy and relieved. They hug Spot and thank him. He wags his tail. He is a good dog. They decide to go back to the house. They have had enough of the sled. They tell their mom what happened. She is glad they are okay. She gives them hot chocolate and cookies. They enjoy their winter treat. They love Spot. He loves them. They are a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a big cave in the woods. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, loved to play near the cave. They were always glad to be there. One day, while they were playing, Tim said, "Spot, let's go inside the cave!" Spot barked, and they walked into the cave together. It was dark and a little bit scary, but Tim and Spot were brave. As they went deeper into the cave, Tim's mom called out, "Tim, it's time to come home!" Tim heard his mom and said, "Spot, we need to go home now. Mom reminds us it's time." They walked out of the cave, happy to go home and tell their adventure to Tim's mom.

John and Emma were playing a game in the park. John was throwing a football to Emma. John said, "I'm so glad we can play football in the park!" "Me too!" said Emma. John and Emma had been going to the park together every day this week. Emma said to John, "Thank you for being so kind and playing with me every day!" John smiled and said, "We're friends and friends are always kind." John and Emma played in the park until it was time to go home. John said, "Let's come back to the park tomorrow so we can play some more." Emma said, "Yes! Let's do it!" John and Emma smiled and waved goodbye to each other as they left the park.
