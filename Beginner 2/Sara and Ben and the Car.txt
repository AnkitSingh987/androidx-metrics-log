Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Car

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play outside in the park. One day, they see a big billboard with a picture of a toy. The toy is a red car that can go very fast. "Wow, look at that car!" Ben says. "I want it!" "Me too!" Sara says. "Let's go and see it closer!" They run across the street to the billboard. But they do not look for cars or bikes. They are not careful. A bike comes very fast. It has a bell that rings. "Beep beep! Watch out!" the bike says. Sara and Ben hear the bike. They try to stop. But they slip on the wet road. They fall down. They get dirty and wet. "Ouch!" Sara says. "That hurts!" "Sorry!" the bike says. "Are you OK?" Sara and Ben nod. They are not hurt very much. But they are sad. They do not see the car toy anymore. The billboard is too high. The bike is nice. It has a basket with flowers. It is a girl bike. The girl bike has a rider. She is a big girl. She is bossy. She looks at Sara and Ben. She says, "You are silly. You should not run on the road. You should look for cars and bikes. You should listen to the bell. You should be careful." Sara and Ben do not like the big girl. They do not like her bike. They do not like her flowers. They do not like her words. They say, "You are mean. You should not be bossy. You should be nice. You should say sorry. You should help us." The big girl does not listen. She does not say sorry. She does not help them. She rides away. She says, "Bye bye! Be good!" Sara and Ben are angry. They stand up. They clean their clothes. They hold hands. They go back to the park. They do not look at the billboard. They do not want the car toy anymore. They want to play with their ball. They want to have fun. They want to be happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play in the park every day. One day, he saw a dull toy on the ground. It was a small, old bunny that could spring up and down. Tim was very happy and took the toy home. The next day, Tim went to the park again. He saw a sad girl named Lily. She was looking for her lost toy. Tim knew it was the same toy he found. He thought about the price of keeping the toy. He knew it was not right to keep something that was not his. Tim went back to his house and got the toy. He gave it back to Lily. She was very happy, and they became good friends. They played together every day. Tim learned that it is better to be honest and kind than to keep something that is not yours.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a pale boy named Tim. Tim loved to wear his red cap every day. One day, he went to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she did not have any friends. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he walked over to her. "Hi, I'm Tim. I want to introduce you to my friend, my red cap," he said as he pointed to his cap. Sue looked at Tim's red cap and smiled. "Hi, Tim. I like your red cap," she said. Tim and Sue played together in the park all day, and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue always played together, and Tim's red cap was a part of their fun adventures.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Jill. Every morning, she would jog around her garden just like her mom told her. One day, as she was jogging, she saw something shiny in the corner of the garden. Jill happily ran to the corner and found a piece of gold! "What is this?" she asked herself. Right then, an old man from the house beside the garden stepped out. "Hi there! That's a harmless piece of gold. It looks very special. Would you like to keep it? " Jill was so excited that she said yes right away. She put it in her pocket and kept jogging. From then on, every time she jogged around the garden, she would take out the gold and look at it. Jill was now the happiest girl in the world!
