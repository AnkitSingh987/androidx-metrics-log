Category: Beginner
Title: Bob and the Park

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Bob and Kay. Bob said to Kay: "I want a result!" Kay asked him: "What kind of result?" Bob replied: "I want a generous result!" Kay thought for a moment and said: "I know how to help you! Let's go to the park, and then we can play a game together. We will take turns, and at the end, the winner will get the result!" So Bob and Kay went to the park and played the game, and Bob won. Then, Kay gave the generous result to Bob and everyone was happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was restless. He liked to run and play all day long. One day, Spot found a shield. The shield was big and round. Spot wanted to play with the shield. Spot tried to pick up the shield with his mouth, but it was too big. He tried to push it with his nose, but it was too heavy. Then, Spot had an idea. He would sneeze at the shield to make it move. Spot took a big breath and sneezed very hard. The shield moved a little bit. Spot was happy. He sneezed again and again. The shield moved more and more. Finally, the shield rolled all the way to his dog house. Spot was tired, but he had so much fun playing with the shield. Now, Spot had a new toy to play with every day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the backyard with their toy cars. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. Sometimes they would crash into each other and laugh. But then they saw a big bird on the roof of their house. It was black and white and had a long yellow beak. It was making loud noises too. "What is that?" Lily asked. Ben shrugged. "I don't know. Maybe a duck?" Lily shook her head. "No, ducks are not black and white. And they don't go on roofs. They go in ponds." Ben frowned. "Well, maybe it's a penguin?" Lily rolled her eyes. "No, penguins are not noisy. And they don't go on roofs. They go in snow." Ben thought hard. He wanted to explain what the bird was, but he didn't know. He wished his mom or dad were here. They would know. Then the bird flew down from the roof and landed near them. It looked at them with its shiny eyes and opened its beak. "Hello, children," it said. "Do you want to play with me?" Ben and Lily gasped. They had never heard a bird talk before. They were scared and amazed. "Who are you?" Ben asked. "I'm a magpie," the bird said. "I like shiny things and I like to talk. I saw your toy cars and I wanted to see them. They are very shiny and pretty." Ben and Lily looked at their toy cars. They were shiny and pretty. They liked them too. But they didn't like the magpie. He was crazy and rude. He had no right to take their toy cars. "Go away," Lily said. "These are our toy cars. You can't have them." The magpie tilted his head. "Why not? I like them. And I'm bigger than you. I can take them if I want." He reached his beak to grab one of the toy cars. Ben and Lily shouted and tried to stop him. They were angry and scared. They wanted their toy cars back. "Help!" they cried. "Mom! Dad! There's a crazy bird on our toy cars!"

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Tim. Tim liked to eat toast in the morning. One day, Tim saw a big cat in his yard. Tim went to the cat and said, "Hi! Do you want some toast?" The cat looked at Tim and started to tremble. The cat was scared of Tim, even though Tim was nice. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat said, "Yes, please!" Tim and the cat became friends and ate toast together every day. And they were both very happy.

Once there was a grasshopper who lived in a patch of grass. He liked to hop around and sing all day. He was very happy in his patch. One day, the grasshopper decided to go for a hop around outside his patch. He hopped slowly towards the sun. He stopped to rest in the shade of a tree. Suddenly, the grasshopper heard a loud noise. It was the noise of a truck coming down the road. The grasshopper was scared, so he quickly hopped back to his patch. He hopped in a big circle around his patch, pausing to rest every few jumps. As he jumped, he noticed that the grass was getting dry and patchy. The grasshopper was sad. He wanted to help his patch get green again. With a slow hop, the grasshopper took some of the dry patches and put them together, like a patchwork quilt. He watered the patch with a few drops of dew. Soon, the grasshopper's patch of grass was green and lush again. The grasshopper was so happy. He hopped around, singing and playing for the rest of the day.
