Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Lily and the Car

Ben and Lily liked to play with their toy cars. They made them zoom on the floor, on the table, and on the sofa. They pretended they were drivers and had races and adventures. One day, they found a new toy car in the toy box. It was shiny and red, and had a button on the side. Ben grabbed it and pressed the button. The car made a loud noise and zoomed away very fast. Ben laughed and chased after it. "Hey, that's not fair!" Lily said. "I want to play with it too!" "Too bad, it's mine!" Ben said. "It's the best car ever!" Lily was sad and angry. She tried to catch the car, but it was too fast for her. She saw Ben having fun, and felt left out. Their mom saw them and came over. She saw the new toy car and smiled. "Wow, that's a nice car!" she said. "Where did you get it?" "It was in the toy box!" Ben said. "It's mine!" Mom shook her head. "No, it's not. It's a teach car. It belongs to your cousin Sam. He left it here when he came to visit. He wanted to teach you how to use it." "A teach car?" Ben and Lily asked. "Yes, a teach car. It's a special car that can teach you things. It can teach you about colors, shapes, numbers, and letters. It can also teach you about sharing, taking turns, and being kind. It's a very useful car. But you have to press the button twice to make it talk." Ben and Lily looked at the car with curiosity. They pressed the button twice and heard a friendly voice. "Hello, I'm a teach car. I'm here to help you learn and have fun. Do you want to play with me?" "Yes!" Ben and Lily said. "Great! Let's start with colors. What color am I?" "Red!" Ben and Lily said. "Good job! Now, can you find something else that is red?" Ben and Lily looked around and found a red ball, a red book, and a red apple. They showed them to the car. "Very good! You are smart and observant. Now, let's share. Can you give one of these red things to each other?" Ben and Lily nodded. Ben gave the red ball to Lily, and Lily gave the red book to Ben. They smiled and thanked each other. "Excellent! You are generous and polite. Now, let's take turns. Can you let each other play with the car for a while?" Ben and Lily looked at each other and nodded again. Ben gave the car to Lily, and Lily gave it back to Ben after a while. They both had fun and learned new things. "Wonderful! You are fair and friendly. You are good at playing with the teach car. I'm proud of you. Do you want to play some more?" "Yes!" Ben and Lily said. Mom watched them and smiled. She was happy to see them playing nicely and learning together. She thought the teach car was a great toy, and a great teacher. She decided to call Sam and thank him for sharing it with them. She also decided to buy one for Ben and Lily, so they could have their own teach car. She thought it would be a useful and fun gift.

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to explore. One day, they found a big swamp near their house. They saw frogs, snakes, and bugs in the water. They wanted to get closer, but their mom said no. "Swamp is dirty and wet. You can get sick or stuck. Stay away from swamp, please," mom said. But Lily and Tom did not listen. They waited until mom was busy and ran to the swamp. They saw a big log that looked like a bridge. They decided to cross it. "Come on, Tom. It will be fun. We can see more animals on the other side," Lily said. "OK, Lily. But be careful. The log is slippery and wobbly," Tom said. They started to walk on the log, holding hands. But halfway through, the log broke and they fell into the swamp. They felt the water and mud around them. It was cold and sticky. They tried to swim, but their clothes were too tight. They felt scared and sorry. "Help! Help! Mom! Mom!" they cried. Mom heard their cries and ran to the swamp. She saw them in the water and got a rope. She threw the rope to them and pulled them out. She hugged them and checked them for injuries. "Are you OK, my babies? What were you doing in the swamp? You could have drowned or bitten by something. Why did you not listen to me?" mom asked. Lily and Tom were wet and dirty. They looked at mom with big eyes and tears. They knew they did something bad. "We are sorry, mom. We wanted to see the swamp. We did not think this would happen. We love you, mom," they said. Mom was angry, but also relieved. She loved them too. She took them home and gave them a bath and a snack. She told them to never go to the swamp again without her. She also told them a story about a swamp monster who liked to eat naughty children. Lily and Tom listened and nodded. They learned their lesson. They never went to the swamp again.
