Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Dog and the Tunnel

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a tunnel and went inside. Max was a poor dog and had no home. He was always looking for new places to sleep. The tunnel was dark and he felt scared. As Max walked deeper into the tunnel, he saw a big ball. Max loved to play with balls, so he picked it up with his mouth. He wanted to drop the ball and play with it, but it was too dark to see. Max was sad, but he kept walking with the ball in his mouth. Suddenly, Max heard a loud noise. The tunnel began to shake and Max was very scared. He dropped the ball and tried to run away, but it was too late. The tunnel fell down on Max and the ball. Max was trapped and could not get out. The poor little dog was stuck in the dark tunnel forever.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play in the garden. One day, they see a big red pepper on a plant. Sam wants to pick it and eat it. He thinks it is sweet and juicy. "Can I have the pepper?" he asks Mia. Mia shakes her head. "No, you can't. It is not yours. It is Mom's. She grows peppers for cooking. She does not permit us to touch them. They are hot and spicy. They will hurt your mouth." Sam does not listen. He is curious and hungry. He reaches for the pepper. Mia tries to stop him. But he is too fast. He plucks the pepper and bites it. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" he cries. He drops the pepper. His mouth is on fire. He runs to the faucet. He drinks water. But it does not help. He still feels pain. Mia follows him. She feels sorry for him. She knows he made a mistake. She gives him a hug. She says, "I'm sorry, Sam. I tried to warn you. The pepper is not good for you. It is only good for Mom. Next time, listen to me, OK?" Sam nods. He is sorry too. He says, "I'm sorry, Mia. You are right. The pepper is bad for me. It is only good for Mom. Next time, I will listen to you, OK?" They smile at each other. They are still friends. They go back to the garden. They play with other things. They do not touch the peppers again.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to watch television all day. He would sit on the couch and watch his favorite shows. One day, the television stopped working. Tom was very sad. Tom went to his friend, a little girl named Sue. He said, "Sue, the television is broken. Can you help me?" Sue looked at the television and saw that it was not working. She told Tom, "I will try to fix it for you." Sue worked hard to fix the television. She knew that if she could fix it, Tom would be happy again. After a while, Sue fixed the television. She said, "Tom, I fixed it! Now you can watch your shows again." Tom was so happy! He sat on the couch and watched his favorite shows without any more complaints. And from that day on, Tom and Sue were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pretty bathrobe that she loved to wear. One day, Mia's bathrobe got very filthy. She was sad and wanted to make it clean again. Mia's mommy told her to strike the bathrobe on the ground to get some of the dirt off. Mia did what her mommy said, but the bathrobe was still dirty. Mia's mommy said they would wash it later. That night, Mia had a dream. In her dream, a magic fairy came and waved her wand. The fairy made the bathrobe clean again! When Mia woke up, she saw her bathrobe was clean. It was not a dream after all! Mia's mommy had washed it while she was sleeping. Mia was very happy and wore her clean bathrobe all day.
