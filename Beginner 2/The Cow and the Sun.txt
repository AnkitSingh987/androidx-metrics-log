Category: Beginner
Title: The Cow and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big cow. The cow had lots of beef. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the cow. He wanted to play a game. The cow said, "Let's play match!" Tim said, "Okay!" They started to play the game. They had to find two things that were the same. Tim found two red balls. The cow found two yellow ducks. They were having so much fun! Then, something unexpected happened. Tim found a fake toy cow. He showed it to the big cow. The big cow laughed and said, "That's not real! It's just a toy!" They both laughed and kept playing the match game. They played until the sun went down, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was a very organized girl. She loved to keep her toys and clothes neat and tidy. One day, Lucy found a bandage in her room. She wanted to find a good place to keep it. Lucy thought about putting the bandage in her toy box, but then she had a better idea. She saw a small tree outside her window with a broken branch. Lucy went outside and put the bandage on the tree to help it feel better. As she did, she heard a snap. The tree branch was fixed! After that, the tree and Lucy became best friends. The tree grew big and strong, and it always had a bandage on its branch to remind it of Lucy's kindness. The other kids in the neighborhood learned from Lucy and started to help the trees and plants too. The moral of the story is that even small acts of kindness can make a big difference.

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They worked hard and had fun. "Look, our tower is almost done!" Lily said. "We just need one more block on top." "I have the last block!" Ben said. He held a big red block in his hand. He tried to put it on the tower, but it was too big. It made the tower fall down. All the blocks crashed to the floor. "Oh no!" Lily said. "Our tower is gone!" Ben was sad and angry. He did not want to start again. He wanted to finish the tower. He was stubborn. He picked up the big red block and tried to put it on the floor. He wanted to make a new tower with only one block. "That's not a tower, Ben!" Lily said. "That's just a block. Come on, let's build a new tower together. We can use smaller blocks this time." "No!" Ben said. "I want this block. It's mine. Go away!" Lily was hurt and annoyed. She did not like Ben's attitude. She wanted to play with him, but he did not want to play with her. She looked around and saw a jug of water on the table. She had an idea. She took the jug and walked to Ben. She smiled and said, "Hey, Ben, do you want some water? You must be thirsty after working so hard." Ben looked at her and nodded. He was thirsty. He put down the big red block and reached for the jug. But Lily did not give him the jug. She poured the water on his head. Ben was wet and shocked. He gasped and spluttered. "Lily!" he cried. "Why did you do that?" Lily laughed and said, "That's what you get for being stubborn. Now, do you want to build a new tower with me, or do you want to stay wet and alone?" Ben was mad, but he also felt silly. He realized that he had been rude and selfish. He wiped his face and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was wrong. Let's build a new tower together. And let's use all the blocks, not just one." Lily smiled and said, "That's better, Ben. I'm glad you changed your mind. Come on, let's start again. And this time, let's finish the tower." They hugged and picked up the blocks. They worked hard and had fun. They built a new tower that was bigger and better than the first one. They were happy and proud. They finished the tower.
