Category: Beginner
Title: The New Monster and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a new monster. He was big and scary. He lived in a cave near a small town. The people in the town were scared of the monster. One day, a little girl named Sue went to the monster's cave. She was not scared. She said, "Hello, monster. I want to be your friend." The monster looked at her and said, "I do not have any friends. No one values me." Sue felt sad for the monster. She said, "I will value you. Let's play together." They played all day long. The monster was happy. He finally had a friend who valued him. But then, the people in the town saw Sue playing with the monster. They were angry. They said, "Sue, you cannot be friends with the monster. He is scary and bad." They took Sue away from the monster. The monster was sad and angry. He went to the town and broke all the houses. The people were scared and sad. The monster went back to his cave, and Sue never saw him again.

Once upon a time, there was a big oak tree in the woods. The tree was very old and its leaves were dull. Many animals lived near the tree and played around it every day. One day, a little boy came to the woods. He saw the oak tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he broke some branches. The animals saw this and were sad. They asked the boy to stop, but he did not listen. The animals were very upset with the boy. They decided not to forgive him for hurting their tree. The boy climbed down, and as he walked away, he tripped and fell. He was hurt and cried a lot. The animals watched, but they did not help him. The boy learned that it is important to listen and be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to step and jump all day long. One day, Tim went for a walk far from his home. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree, but he felt weak. He tried to step on the branches, but they were too weak too. The weak branch broke, and Tim fell to the ground. He was very sad and hurt. Tim tried to walk back to his home, but he could not. He was too weak and hurt. The sun went down, and it was dark. Tim stayed on the ground, far from his home, and cried. The end.

One day, a helpful dog named Spot saw a little bird. The bird was on the ground. The bird had a hurt wing. Spot wanted to help the bird. The bird's head was down because it was sad. Spot said, "Bird, I will help you. Do not be sad." The bird looked at Spot with big eyes. The bird said, "Thank you, Spot. I struggle to fly." Spot picked up the bird with his mouth. He was very gentle. He took the bird to his friend, a kind cat named Fluffy. Fluffy knew how to help hurt animals. Fluffy looked at the bird's wing. She said, "Bird, I will fix your wing. You will fly again soon." The bird was happy. Spot and Fluffy were very helpful friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Mimi. Lily liked to play with Mimi all day long. They would run, jump, and hug each other. One day, Lily found a pretty gem in her garden. It was shiny and colorful. She showed it to Mimi and they both liked it. Lily decided to keep the gem and put it in her room. At night, when Lily was sleeping, the gem started to make loud noises. The loud noises woke up Lily and Mimi. They were scared and didn't know what to do. The gem kept making loud noises and it got louder and louder. Lily and Mimi tried to hug each other, but they were still scared. The gem didn't stop making loud noises and it made everyone in the house feel sad and scared.
