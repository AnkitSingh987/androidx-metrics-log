Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Clever Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a clever girl named Lily. She had a big box of toys. One day, she wanted to split her toys into two groups. She wanted to count the number of toys in each group. Lily called her friend, Sam, to help her. "Sam, can you help me split my toys?" she asked. Sam said, "Yes, I can help you, Lily!" They started to split the toys together. They put some toys on the left side and some toys on the right side. When they finished, Lily and Sam counted the number of toys in each group. They found out that they had the same number of toys in both groups. They were very happy and proud of their work. They played with the toys all day long, knowing they had done a clever job.

Anna likes to play with her purple phone. She pretends to call her friends and talk to them. She says hello, how are you, what are you doing, and goodbye. She likes to make funny noises and laugh. One day, Anna is playing with her phone in her room. She hears a loud noise outside. She runs to the window and sees a big truck. The truck has a ladder and a hose. It is a fire truck. Anna likes fire trucks. She wants to see more. She runs to the door and opens it. She sees her mom in the hall. Her mom is kneeling on the floor. She is holding a bag. She looks scared. She says, "Anna, there is a fire in the building. We have to go. Come here, quickly." Anna is scared too. She drops her phone and runs to her mom. Her mom hugs her and puts her in the bag. She says, "Don't worry, we will be safe. The firemen are here to help us. They will put out the fire." Anna and her mom go down the stairs. They see many people and firemen. They see smoke and water. They hear sirens and shouts. They go outside and see the fire truck. Anna sees her phone on the ground. It is broken. She feels sad. Her mom says, "I'm sorry, Anna. Your phone is gone. But we are okay. That's what matters. We can get you a new phone later. Do you want to see the fire truck?" Anna nods. She still likes fire trucks. She and her mom go to the fire truck. A fireman smiles and lets them see the ladder and the hose. He gives Anna a sticker and a hat. He says, "You are very brave, Anna. You did a good job. Do you want to talk on the radio?" Anna says yes. She takes the radio and says hello. She hears a voice say hello back. She makes funny noises and laughs. She feels happy. She says goodbye and thank you. She gives the radio back to the fireman. She and her mom hug. They wait for the fire to be out. They hope their home will be okay. They are glad they have each other. They are glad the firemen are there. They are glad Anna has a new hat.

One day, a big, enthusiastic dog named Max went to a park. In the park, there were many things to play with. Max saw a long strip of grass and wanted to play there. Max saw a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. Max wanted to play with Tim and his toy car. Max said, "Can I play with you and your car?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" Max and Tim played with the toy car on the strip of grass. They were having so much fun. But then, Max accidentally stepped on the toy car and destroyed it. Tim was sad, but Max said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to break your car." Tim said, "It's okay, Max. We can still play together." So, Max and Tim played in the park until the sun went down.

Tommy was feeling very glad. It was time to get dressed for the day. He looked in his closet and saw some of his favourite clothes. He was excited to pick out what he would wear. Suddenly, he had a nightmare. In the nightmare, he saw monsters and darkness. He was scared and he started to cry. Mum walked in and saw him crying. She came over and asked “What’s wrong Tommy?”. “I had a bad nightmare” said Tommy, with tears in his eyes. Mum took Tommy in her arms and gave him a big hug. “Don’t worry Tommy, it’s just a dream. Now, why don’t you pick out a nice dress to wear today and you will feel better.” Tommy was glad after talking to his Mum. He saw a nice dress in his closet and he picked it out and put it on. He was happy and ready to start the day.
