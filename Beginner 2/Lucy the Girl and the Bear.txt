Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. She had a favorite toy named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear had a big button on his belly. Lucy loved to press the button and hear Mr. Bear laugh. One day, Lucy was feeling terrible. She had a bad cold and had to rest in bed. Mr. Bear was with her to keep her company. Lucy pressed Mr. Bear's button and he said, "Don't feel sad, Lucy. We can still have fun together." Lucy and Mr. Bear played games and talked all day. They had a tea party and even made a fort with the bed sheets. They laughed and played until Lucy was too tired to play anymore. She fell asleep with Mr. Bear by her side. When Lucy woke up the next day, she felt much better. She hugged Mr. Bear and said, "Thank you for helping me feel better, Mr. Bear. You are my best friend." Mr. Bear's button made a happy sound, and they both knew they would have many more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a big tail. One day, Fluffy went to the park. At the park, Fluffy saw a big dog. The dog was running around like crazy. Fluffy said, "Hi, dog! Why are you running like that?" The dog said, "I don't want to disturb the birds. I like to watch them fly." Fluffy liked to watch the birds too. So, Fluffy and the dog sat down and watched the birds together. They had a fun day at the park.

Lily was a little girl who liked to help her mom. She liked to serve tea and cookies to her mom and her dolls. She had a tea set with cups and plates and a pot. She also had a tray to carry them. One day, she was playing with her tea set in her room. She wanted to make a new dress for her doll, so she looked for some cloth and a needle. She found an old cloth in a drawer and a needle in a box. She was very careful with the needle, because she knew it was sharp and could hurt. She cut a piece of cloth and wrapped it around her doll. Then she tried to sew it with the needle. But the needle was too big and the cloth was too thick. She could not make a stitch. She tried harder and harder, but the needle only made holes in the cloth. She was frustrated and sad. She wanted to make a pretty dress for her doll, but she could not. She started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and came to her room. She saw Lily with the cloth and the needle. "What's wrong, Lily?" her mom asked. "I want to make a dress for my doll, but I can't. The needle is too big and the cloth is too old. It's not working," Lily sobbed. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't cry, Lily. I can help you. We can find a smaller needle and a softer cloth. Then we can make a dress together. How does that sound?" Lily smiled and said, "That sounds good, mom. Thank you. Can we still have tea and cookies after?" "Of course, we can. You are such a good helper, Lily. You always serve me tea and cookies. I love you," her mom said. "I love you too, mom," Lily said. They put away the old cloth and the big needle. Then they went to find a new cloth and a small needle. They made a beautiful dress for Lily's doll. Then they had tea and cookies and played with the dolls. Lily was happy.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little town, there lived a human named Tim. Tim was a very happy human. He loved to dance, sing, and clap all day long. One day, while Tim was walking in the park, he met a big, fluffy dog. The dog was very friendly. "Woof, woof!" said the dog. Tim smiled and said, "Hello, dog! Let's be friends and clap together!" The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim and the dog spent the whole day playing in the park. They ran, jumped, and clapped together. They were very tired but very happy. From that day on, Tim and the dog were the best of friends, and they clapped together every day.
