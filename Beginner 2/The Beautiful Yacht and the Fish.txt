Category: Beginner
Title: The Beautiful Yacht and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful yacht. It was fair and shiny. It loved to sail in the sea. One day, it saw a little fish in trouble. The fish was stuck in a net. The yacht wanted to help. It went closer to the fish and said, "Don't worry, fish. I will rescue you!" The fish was happy to hear this. The yacht used its sail to break the net. The fish was free! It said, "Thank you, yacht!" The yacht was happy to help. They both sailed away in the sea, playing together. And they became the best of friends.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went with his mom to a big stadium. The stadium was full of people. They were there to see a fun game. Tim was very happy and excited. Before the game started, Tim's mom said, "Tim, please don't weep if our team loses. It's just a game, and we are here to have fun." Tim nodded and smiled. During the game, Tim's team was not doing well. Some people around Tim started to weep. Tim remembered what his mom said and stayed happy. In the end, Tim's team made a big play and won the game. Everyone in the stadium was happy and clapped. Tim and his mom went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. The sun was shining and the day was calm. They walked by many houses. Amy liked looking at the houses and noticing the different things they had. "Look, mom! That house has a chimney!" Amy said with a big smile. "Yes, it does," her mom replied. "Chimneys are used to let the smoke out when there is a fire inside the house to keep warm." As they walked, Amy noticed more chimneys on the houses. She felt happy seeing them. Amy didn't know that soon, she would see something special come out of a chimney. Later that day, Amy and her mom sat in the park. They saw a big cloud of smoke come out of a chimney on a house nearby. Then, something small and gray jumped out of the chimney and landed on the ground. It was a cute little squirrel! The squirrel looked at Amy and her mom, and then ran away. Amy laughed and clapped her hands. "Wow, mom! The chimney had a squirrel in it!" Amy said. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, it did. That was a fun surprise, wasn't it?"

Tom and Ann were friends who liked to play on the farm. They saw a big cow with black spots and a bell. The cow was eating grass and looked happy. "Let's go and touch the cow!" Tom said. He wanted to feel the cow's fur and hear the bell. "No, Tom, we can't!" Ann said. She was afraid of the cow. "The cow is big and strong. She might kick us or bite us. And the farmer said we should not bother the animals." But Tom did not listen to Ann. He had an urge to touch the cow. He ran to the cow and reached for her fur. The cow did not like that. She turned her head and mooed loudly. Then she kicked Tom with her hoof. Tom fell to the ground and cried. He had a big bruise on his leg and a cut on his arm. He felt a lot of pain. Ann ran to him and tried to help him. She was very sorry for Tom. The farmer heard the noise and came to see what happened. He saw Tom and the cow and understood. He was angry with Tom and scolded him. He said Tom was foolish and naughty. He said Tom should have listened to Ann and left the cow alone. He said Tom had to go home and tell his mom what he did. He said Tom could not play on the farm anymore. Tom was very sad and ashamed. He wished he had not touched the cow. He learned a hard lesson. He learned that he should respect the animals and obey the rules. He learned that his urge could get him into trouble. He learned that he should listen to his friends who care for him. He learned that some things are better left alone.
