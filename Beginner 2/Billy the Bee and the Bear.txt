Category: Beginner
Title: Billy the Bee and the Bear

One day, a bee named Billy was nervous. He lived in a big hive with his bee friends. Billy had to sell honey to other animals in the forest. It was his first time doing it. Billy went to the forest and met a kind bear. "Hi, bear!" said Billy. "Do you want to buy honey from my hive?" The bear smiled and said, "Yes, I love honey!" Billy was happy that the bear wanted to buy honey. He sold honey to the bear and went back to the hive. His bee friends were proud of him. From that day on, Billy was not nervous to sell honey anymore.

Anna and Ben were walking with their mom in the big city. They saw many cars, shops, and people. They also saw big signs with lights and pictures. Mom said they were called billboards. Some billboards showed toys, some showed food, and some showed movies. "Look, mom, a doll!" Anna said, pointing to a billboard with a pink doll that had long hair and a dress. "Can I have it?" "No, sweetie, not today. We have to save money for other things," mom said. "But I want it, I want it, I want it!" Anna cried, stamping her feet. Ben looked at his sister and shook his head. He thought she was selfish. He did not cry for toys. He liked to play with his cars and blocks at home. He also liked to read books and draw pictures. "Anna, stop crying. You have many toys at home. You don't need more," Ben said. Anna glared at him. She did not like him telling her what to do. She wanted the doll. She thought it was pretty and shiny. She thought it would make her happy. "No, you stop. You are mean. You don't like dolls. You don't know anything," Anna said. Mom sighed. She was tired of their fighting. She took their hands and led them away from the billboard. "Come on, kids, let's go. We have to catch the bus. Maybe we can see something else that is fun and interesting on the way," mom said. Anna and Ben followed mom, but they did not talk to each other. They were still angry and sad. They did not see the other billboards that glowed in the dark. They did not see the stars that twinkled in the sky. They did not see the smiles of the people who passed by. They only saw their own wants and feelings. They did not see how lucky they were to have each other and mom. They did not see how much fun they could have together. They did not see how much they could learn and grow. They did not see how much they could glow.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with paint. One day, she found a big box of paint. She was very happy. Sue wanted to share the paint with her friend, Tom. She went to Tom's house and said, "Tom, I found paint! Let's play and make pretty pictures!" Tom was excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" As they played with the paint, Sue accidentally pinched Tom's hand. Tom said, "Ouch! That hurt!" Sue said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to pinch you." They went back to playing with the paint and had lots of fun. They made many pretty pictures together.

One day, a little girl named Sue found an unknown shape in her toy box. She looked at it and said, "What is this?" She had never seen this shape before. She showed it to her mom and asked, "Mom, what is this shape's name?" Her mom looked at the shape and smiled. She said, "I don't know, Sue. Let's find out together." Sue was very excited to learn the name of the unknown shape. She knew that her mom would help her. They looked in a book about shapes. They saw circles, squares, and triangles. But the unknown shape was not in the book. Then, they looked in another book. There, they found the shape! It was called a star. Sue was so happy to know the name of the shape. She thanked her mom and played with her new star shape all day long.
