Category: Beginner
Title: Max and Lily and the Rabbit

One day, a dog named Max and a cat named Lily were playing in the yard. They saw a hole in the ground. They wanted to know what was in the hole. Max and Lily started to discuss what could be inside the hole. Max said, "I think a naughty rabbit made the hole." Lily said, "No, I think a little bug made it." They both looked at the hole and thought about what could be inside. They decided to wait and see who comes out of the hole. After a while, they saw a naughty rabbit come out of the hole. Max and Lily were both surprised. They looked at the rabbit and said, "You made the hole!" The rabbit just laughed and hopped away. Max and Lily learned that sometimes they can be right and sometimes they can be wrong, but it is always fun to discuss and learn together.

Ben and Mia liked to play in the park. They had a toy car that they could drive. They took turns to drive and to sit in the back. One day, they saw a big bird in the park. It was not like any bird they had seen before. It had a long neck, long legs and black and white feathers. It was an ostrich. "Wow, look at that bird!" Ben said. "It is so big and fast!" "Can we drive to it?" Mia asked. "Maybe it wants to be our friend." "OK, let's go!" Ben said. He drove the toy car towards the ostrich. The ostrich saw them and ran away. Ben and Mia followed it. They wanted to see where it came from. They drove past the swings, the slide and the sandbox. They drove to the other side of the park. There they saw a big tent. It had a sign that said "Mysterious Animals Show". The ostrich ran into the tent. "Maybe the bird lives in the tent," Mia said. "Maybe there are more animals like it." "Let's go and see," Ben said. He parked the toy car and they got out. They walked to the tent. They peeked inside. They saw many animals that they had never seen before. They saw a monkey with a long tail, a snake with a hood, a camel with two humps and a lion with a mane. "Wow, these animals are so mysterious!" Ben said. "They are so different and cool!" "Look, there is the bird!" Mia said. She pointed to the ostrich. The ostrich was standing next to a man. The man had a hat and a coat. He was holding a microphone. He was talking to the people in the tent. "Welcome to the Mysterious Animals Show!" the man said. "I am Mr. Zee, the animal trainer. I will show you some amazing animals that you have never seen before. This is Ollie, the ostrich. He is the fastest bird in the world. He can run as fast as a car. He can also kick very hard. He is very smart and friendly. Do you want to see him do a trick?" "Yes!" the people in the tent said. "OK, Ollie, show them what you can do!" Mr. Zee said. He gave Ollie a signal. Ollie nodded and ran to the toy car. He jumped in and drove it around the tent. He honked the horn and waved his wings. The people in the tent clapped and cheered. "Wow, the bird can drive!" Ben said. "He is so clever and funny!" "He is awesome!" Mia said. "He is our friend!" Ben and Mia smiled and waved to Ollie. Ollie smiled and waved back. He drove the toy car back to them. He got out and gave them a hug. He let them pet his feathers. He was happy to meet them. "Ollie likes you," Mr. Zee said. "He likes to make new friends. Do you want to stay and watch the show?" "Yes, please!" Ben and Mia said. "OK, then come and sit with me," Mr. Zee said. He led them to a seat. He gave them some popcorn and juice. He told them more about the mysterious animals. Ben and Mia had a lot of fun. They learned a lot of new things. They made a new friend. They loved the ostrich. They loved the show. They had a great day.
