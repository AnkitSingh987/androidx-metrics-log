Category: Beginner
Title: Max and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy dog named Max. Max lived in a small house in the west. He had a big yard to run and play in. Max loved to play with his ball and his friends. One sunny day, Max saw a big, red ball in his yard. He loved the big, red ball very much. Max ran and jumped to catch the ball in his mouth. He was so happy and wagged his tail. Max played with the big, red ball all day long. When the sun went down in the west, Max was very tired. He went into his small house and slept. Max loved his big, red ball and his home in the west.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn new words. One day, he met a friendly cat. The cat was good at talking. Tim wanted to learn from the cat. "Cat, can you teach me a new word?" asked Tim. The cat thought for a moment and then said, "Yes! I will teach you the word 'load'." Tim was happy and asked, "What does 'load' mean?" The cat replied, "It means to put many things into something, like a box or a car." The next day, Tim saw his mom putting a lot of toys into a big box. He remembered the word 'load' and said, "Mom, are you loading the box with toys?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! That's a good word to use. You are learning so well." Tim felt proud and thanked the cat for teaching him a new word. He knew that learning words was good and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Max. Max had no home and no food. He was very sad. One day, Max found a letter on the ground. He picked it up in his mouth and walked around to find help. Max met a nice girl named Lily. Lily saw the letter in Max's mouth and took it from him. She read the letter out loud. The letter said that Max was a very special dog. He could spray water from his tail! Max was very surprised. Lily and Max went to the park to try Max's new skill. Max wagged his tail and water sprayed out! It was so much fun. They played and sprayed water on the flowers. The flowers grew big and beautiful. Max was not a poor dog anymore. He was special and had a friend. Max and Lily were happy together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a bright cloth. It fluttered in the wind, looking very beautiful. A little girl noticed the cloth and had an idea. She wanted to pass it along to the children in the park. She took one big step forward and grabbed it with her hands. The cloth was so big and bright, it was hard for her to carry it alone. She called out to her friends, "Come help me pass this cloth!" But the children in the park didn't want to help. One of they said, "No way, why should we help you?" The little girl was upset. She had wanted to make the park more fun with the cloth. She started to cry. Suddenly, the wind blew strongly. It caught the cloth, and it began to fly. The little girl watched as the cloth flew away, taking away her dreams. But as it fluttered in the sky, it shimmered like a beautiful rainbow. The little girl smiled. She knew that even though she couldn't pass the cloth to her friends, it was having fun in the bright sky.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red bird. The bird loved to paint. Every day, the bird set out to find new things to paint. One day, the bird found a big rock. The bird set to paint the rock. It painted the rock with many colors. The rock looked very pretty. All the animals came to see the rock. They liked the bright colors. The bird was very happy. From that day on, the bird set out to paint more things for all to see.
