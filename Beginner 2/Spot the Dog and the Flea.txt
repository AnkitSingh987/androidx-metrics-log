Title: Spot the Dog and the Flea

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot was a happy dog with a big smile. As he walked, he saw a scary flea. The flea was very small, but it had big eyes that looked at Spot. Spot did not like the scary flea. He wanted it to go away. So, Spot pointed at the flea with his paw and said, "Go away, scary flea! I do not like you!" The flea just looked at Spot and did not move. Then, a nice bird flew down and saw Spot and the scary flea. The bird wanted to help Spot. So, the bird pointed at the flea with her wing and said, "Go away, scary flea! You are not nice!" The flea saw the bird and got scared. The flea jumped away and did not come back. Spot and the bird were very happy. They played together all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to go to the mall with her mom. They would walk and look at all the pretty things. Lily liked the mall a lot. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, we should eat something healthy today." Lily thought for a moment and then said, "I want ice cream!" Her mom said, "No, Lily, ice cream is not healthy. Let's eat some fruit instead." But Lily insisted, "No, I want ice cream!" Lily's mom thought of an idea. She said, "Okay, Lily, how about we get a fruit salad with a little bit of ice cream on top?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, that sounds good!" So they went to the food place in the mall and got a big fruit salad with a little ice cream on top. Lily and her mom sat down and ate their healthy treat. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to find new places. One day, she found a map under her bed. The map showed a big park with a lot of trees and a pond. Sue was very excited and wanted to go to the park. Sue put on her shoes and said to her mom, "Mom, I found a map. Can we go to the park?" Her mom looked serious and said, "Sue, we must remove the trash first. Then we can go to the park." Sue and her mom removed the trash from their house. They put it in a big bag. After that, they took the map and went to the park. Sue played with her ball and her mom sat under a tree. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to visit his friends in the forest. One day, Remy went to see his friend, Benny the squirrel, during the spring. "Hi, Benny!" said Remy. "I came to visit you. Let's play together!" Benny was happy to see Remy. They played and laughed under the big tree near Benny's home. While they were playing, they saw a small bird with a hurt wing. Remy and Benny wanted to help the bird. They worked together to find a safe place for the bird to rest. The bird was thankful for their help. Remy and Benny learned that it is good to help others in need. They felt happy that they could make a new friend. The forest was a better place because Remy and Benny were kind and caring friends.
