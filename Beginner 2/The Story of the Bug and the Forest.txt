Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Bug and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a small, weak bug. The bug lived in a big green forest. One day, the bug went for a walk. He saw a big mushroom. The mushroom was red and pretty. The bug liked the mushroom a lot. The bug wanted to hold the mushroom. But the mushroom was too big for the weak bug. He tried to hold it, but he could not. He felt sad. He wanted to take the mushroom to his home. A big, strong ant saw the weak bug. The ant wanted to help the bug. The ant said, "I can hold the mushroom for you." The bug was happy. The ant held the mushroom and they went to the bug's home. The bug and the ant became good friends. They played and had fun together. And they both loved the big, pretty mushroom.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He put on his shoes and zipped up his jacket. Tim was not an ordinary boy, he was very fast. He loved to run and play outside with his friends. Today, he wanted to play a game of soccer. Tim went to the park and found his friends. They started to play soccer. They kicked the ball back and forth, trying to score a goal. Tim ran as fast as he could, zipping from one end of the field to the other. Finally, Tim got the ball and ran towards the goal. He kicked it hard and scored! His friends cheered and clapped for him. Tim felt happy and proud. After the game, they all went home and Tim knew it was not an ordinary day.

Ben and Mia like to play with their toy cars and trucks. They have a big truck that can load many things. They pretend to be workers and load rocks, sticks, and leaves on the truck. They drive the truck around the yard and make loud noises. One day, they find a stuffed lion in the garage. It is old and dusty, but they think it is fun. They decide to load the lion on the truck too. They lift the lion and put it on the back of the truck. The lion is heavy and makes the truck tilt. Ben and Mia laugh and drive the truck again. But the lion is too big and falls off the truck. It rolls on the ground and lands on Ben's head. Ben feels dizzy and falls down. Mia sees Ben and runs to help him. She takes the lion off his head and hugs him. She says, "Are you okay, Ben? The lion is sorry. He did not mean to hurt you." Ben rubs his head and smiles. He says, "I'm okay, Mia. The lion is not bad. He is just silly. Let's play with him some more." They pick up the lion and the truck and go to the sandbox. They make a cave for the lion and a road for the truck. They have fun and forget about the dizzy.

Once upon a time, there was a big swamp. In the swamp lived a frog named Tim. Tim was a compassionate frog who liked to help others. One day, Tim met a lost bird named Sam. Sam was sad and said, "I don't recognize this place. Can you help me find my way home?" Tim wanted to help his new friend Sam. Tim and Sam went on a journey to find Sam's home. They met other animals who needed help too. Tim and Sam helped them all. In the end, they found Sam's home and everyone was happy.

One day, a girl named Lily went to her amazing garden. She loved to pour water on her flowers to help them grow big and strong. In the garden, she met a friendly bee who was buzzing around. "Hi, Bee!" said Lily. "Do you like my garden?" "Yes, Lily," said the bee. "Your garden is very pretty. Can you pour some water on the flowers for me?" Lily was happy to help her new friend. She poured water on all the flowers, and the bee buzzed around, thanking her. They both had a fun day in the amazing garden.
