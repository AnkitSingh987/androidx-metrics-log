Category: Beginner
Title: The Enormous Tree and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was an enormous tree. A little boy named Tom lived near the tree. He liked to play under the tree every day. One day, Tom saw a pipe near the tree. He did not know what the pipe was for. Tom told his friends about the pipe. They all went to look at it. They noticed that the pipe was very long. It went from the tree to a big hill. They were all very curious about the pipe. They decided to follow the pipe to see where it went. When they got to the hill, they saw a big door. They opened the door and found a room full of toys! The pipe was a secret way to bring toys to the tree. Tom and his friends played with the toys and had a lot of fun. They were very happy that they found the secret toy room.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house. The man liked to be noisy. He would yell and sing all day. His wife did not like the noise. She wanted quiet. One day, the wife had an idea. She would encourage her husband to be quiet. She said, "Let's play a game. The one who can be quiet the longest will win!" The man liked games, so he said yes. They played the quiet game. The man tried very hard to be quiet. The wife was happy. She was quiet too. They both learned that being quiet can be good. The house was peaceful. The moral of the story is that sometimes it's better to be quiet and listen to others.

Once there was a loyal hoop. It lived in the garden, and ate yummy fruit every day. It was very happy in its own world, playing by itself. It was content with the food it ate and set up a routine; every day it would wake early in the morning, eat juicy fruit all day long and end the day with a sweet pear. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She was only three years old and was very excited to see the loyal hoop. She asked if she could pick it up and the hoop was happy to let her. The little girl looked intently at it, and the two of them were soon best friends. The little girl started bringing it some of her own food from home. She shared tasty snacks like carrots and apples. Even when her parents called her to go home, the loyal hoop refused to be separated from the little girl. From that day on, the loyal hoop followed the little girl everywhere she went. It ate snacks with her, played games and even slept in her bed. The two were inseparable and were loyal friends for life.

Lily and Ben liked to play with blocks. They made a big tower with many colors. They were happy and proud of their tower. But then, Max came. Max was a mean boy. He did not like to share. He wanted to play with the blocks too. He pushed Lily and Ben away. He kicked their tower. The blocks fell down. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. "Stop it, Max! That is not nice!" Lily said. "Go away, Max! These are our blocks!" Ben said. "I don't care! I want to play with them!" Max said. He grabbed some blocks and threw them at Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben dodged the blocks. They ran to Max. They tried to take the blocks back. They pushed and pulled and shouted. Max pushed and pulled and shouted too. They made a big mess. Then, Mom came. She saw the mess and the fight. She was not happy. "What is going on here?" Mom said. "Why are you fighting?" "Max broke our tower!" Lily said. "Max took our blocks!" Ben said. "Max, is that true?" Mom said. Max looked down. He felt bad. He knew he was wrong. "Yes, Mom. I'm sorry." Max said. Mom made Max say sorry to Lily and Ben. She made him help them restore their tower. She gave them more blocks to make it bigger and better. She told them to play nicely and share. Lily and Ben forgave Max. They let him join their game. They showed him how to make a fit tower. They had fun together. Mom smiled. She was happy. She gave them a hug and a kiss. She told them she loved them.
