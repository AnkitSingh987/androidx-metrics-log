Category: Beginner
Title: Slip the Long Snake and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a long snake named Slip. Slip was a very long and shiny snake. He lived in a big green forest with many other animals. One day, Slip saw a small figure in the forest. It was a tiny mouse named Max. Max was scared because he was lost. Slip wanted to help Max find his way back home. Slip and Max looked all around the forest. They saw big trees and pretty flowers. After a long time, they found Max's home. Max was very happy and thanked Slip for helping him. From that day on, Slip and Max were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a white dog named Spot. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, they went for a walk near the big trees. As they walked, they saw a man with a long chain. The man was waving the chain in the air. Tim and Spot went closer to see what the man was doing. The man saw them and smiled. "Hi, I am waving this chain to fix it," said the man. "Do you want to help me?" Tim and Spot nodded. They helped the man wave the chain until it was fixed. The man thanked them, and they all went home happy.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play and have fun. Today, they want to paint a picture for their moms. They have paper, brushes and paint. "What do you want to paint, Ben?" Lily asks. "I want to paint a car. A big, red car. Vroom, vroom!" Ben says. "I want to paint a flower. A yellow flower. It is bright and pretty," Lily says. They start to paint. They use blue for the sky, green for the grass and brown for the road. They paint their car and their flower. They are happy. "Look, Lily, I made a car for my mom. She likes cars. She drives me to school," Ben says. "Look, Ben, I made a flower for my mom. She likes flowers. She grows them in the garden," Lily says. They smile at each other. They write their names on the paper. They write "I love you, Mom" on the paper. They are proud. They give their picture to their moms. Their moms hug them and kiss them. They say "Thank you, Lily and Ben. We love your picture. We love you." Lily and Ben are happy. They love their moms. They love to paint. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim liked to play near the stream. He would jump and swim all day long. The water in the stream was cool and clear. Tim had many friends who lived near the stream too. One day, Tim saw a big rock across the stream. He wanted to jump on it. Tim tried and tried, but he was too small. He felt tired. Tim's friends saw him and wanted to help. They all tried to think of a way to get Tim to the big rock. Tim's friend, a small bird named Sue, had an idea. Sue told Tim to hold on to her legs. Sue tried very hard to fly Tim to the big rock. Tim held on tight, and Sue flew him across the stream. Tim was so happy to be on the big rock. He thanked Sue and all his friends for helping him. They all played on the big rock until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, on a big round planet, there were two friends, a boy and a girl. They liked to play and share their toys with each other. The sky was pale and full of pretty stars that they both loved to watch. One day, while they were playing, they found a big box. They were very curious about what was inside. They opened the box together and found a shiny, pretty stone. They both wanted to play with it, but they knew they had to share. As they held the stone together, something unexpected happened. The stone began to glow and they felt very happy. They looked up at the pale sky and saw the stars twinkling brighter than ever before. The boy and the girl realized that by sharing the stone, they made the planet a happier place for everyone.
