Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Dog

One day, a big cat and a little dog were in a room. In the room, there was a couch. The cat liked the couch. The dog liked the couch too. They both wanted to sit on the couch. The cat said, "I want to sit on the couch." The dog said, "No, I want to sit on the couch." They were not happy. They did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew in the room. The bird saw the couch and sat on it. The cat and the dog were surprised. They looked at each other and said, "We can all sit on the couch together." So, the cat, the dog, and the bird sat on the couch. They were all grateful to be friends. They knew that being friends was a good way to end the day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and make noise. But they did not like the mosquitoes. The mosquitoes were small and mean. They bit Ben and Lily and made them itch. "Go away, mosquitoes!" Ben shouted. He waved his arms and tried to hit them. But the mosquitoes were too fast and too many. "Look, Ben!" Lily said. She pointed to a bottle on the table. The bottle had a picture of a flower and a word. The word was "spray". "What is that?" Ben asked. "I don't know. Maybe it can help us with the mosquitoes," Lily said. She picked up the bottle and pressed the button. A mist came out of the bottle. It smelled nice. "Let me try!" Ben said. He took the bottle from Lily and sprayed it around him. The mist made him feel cool and fresh. "Wow, this is fun!" Lily said. She grabbed the bottle back and sprayed it on herself. They laughed and sprayed each other. But they did not notice that the mist also made the mosquitoes go away. The mosquitoes did not like the smell of the spray. They flew away to find another place to bite. Ben and Lily were happy. They had found a way to play without the mosquitoes. They did not know that the spray was original. It was made by their mom, who was a scientist. She had invented the spray to keep the mosquitoes away. She was going to tell them later, but she was glad to see them having fun. She smiled and watched them from the window.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They brought a big bag to fill with toys. Tim was very happy and excited to play. At the park, Tim met a dog named Max. Max was an obedient dog. Max's owner said, "Max, sit!" Max sat down right away. Tim liked Max and they played together. Max liked the noise that Tim made when they played. "Max, can you help me fill this bag with toys?" Tim asked. Max wagged his tail and helped Tim. They filled the bag with toys and had lots of fun. Tim said, "Thank you, Max!" Max barked and wagged his tail. Tim and Max were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big open lab. In this lab, there lived a nice man named Tom. Tom liked to polish things to make them shiny and clean. One day, Tom found a big box in the lab. He opened the box and saw many dirty cups. Tom wanted to make the cups shiny, so he began to polish them one by one. As Tom polished the cups, they became very shiny. He was happy with his work. When he was done, he put the cups back in the box and closed the lab. Tom went home with a big smile on his face, knowing that he had made the cups shiny and clean.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bright toy. It was a microphone. He loved to sing and talk into it. He showed his friend, Sue, his new toy. "Look at my bright microphone, Sue," Tim said. "We can discuss our favorite songs and sing them together!" But Sue wanted to play with her toy car. She didn't want to sing. Tim was sad. He wanted to share his bright microphone with Sue. Later, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was loud and scary. Tim had an idea. He used his bright microphone to make his voice big and loud too. The dog ran away. "Wow, Tim!" Sue said. "Your bright microphone helped us. Let's discuss our favorite songs and sing together now." Tim and Sue sang and played together. They had fun with the bright microphone.
