Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sam and the Park

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. They saw a big tree with fruit on it. The fruit was round and red. Tom said, "I want to eat the fruit." Sam said, "No, I disagree. We don't know if it is good to eat." Tom said, "But it looks harmless. I am hungry." Sam said, "No, let's go home and eat something else." Tom did not listen to Sam. He took the fruit and ate it. Soon, Tom's tummy hurt. He cried and said, "I should have listened to you, Sam." Sam said, "It's okay, let's go home and tell mom." They went home and told mom. Mom was sad and said, "Next time, listen to your friend."

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was walking in the forest. He saw a big vine hanging from a tree. Tom thought it would be fun to play with the vine. Tom jumped up and grabbed the vine with his paws. He started to swing back and forth. Tom was having so much fun! But then, he began to slip. His paws could not hold on any longer. Tom called out, "Help! I am slipping!" A kind squirrel heard Tom and quickly climbed the tree. The squirrel grabbed Tom's paw and helped him back on the ground. Tom said, "Thank you, squirrel!" They both laughed and played with the vine together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a tiny flea friend who lived in his fur. The flea was not nice. It was disgusting. Spot did not like the flea, but he could not get rid of it. One day, Spot went to the park to play. He ran so fast and jumped so high. He was having so much fun. But, the flea was not having fun. The flea was scared. It did not want to lose its home in Spot's fur. Finally, Spot shook his body very hard. The disgusting flea flew off of Spot and landed on a leaf. The flea was sad because it lost its home. But, Spot was happy. He did not have the flea anymore. Now, Spot could play without feeling itchy.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toy cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They pretend to drive them on the floor, on the sofa, and on the table. One day, their mom says, "I have to go to the university. Do you want to come with me?" Anna and Ben are curious. They have never been to the university. They say, "Yes, please!" Mom takes them to the car. She buckles them in their seats. She drives on the road. She points to the buildings and the trees. She says, "This is the university. This is where I work and learn. There are many people here who work and learn too." Anna and Ben look out the window. They see big buildings with many windows and doors. They see people walking and biking. They see cars and buses. They see a big sign that says, "University". Mom parks the car. She says, "We are here. Come on, let's go inside." She takes them by the hand. She leads them to a building. She says, "This is my office. This is where I write and read. This is where I keep my books and papers. These are useful for my work and learning." Anna and Ben enter the office. They see a desk, a chair, a computer, and a shelf. They see many books and papers on the shelf. They see some pictures and toys on the desk. They see a big window that shows the university. Mom says, "You can look around, but be careful. Don't touch anything that is not yours. I have to do some work. I will be done soon." She sits on the chair. She turns on the computer. She starts to write and read. Anna and Ben look around. They are interested in the books and papers. They are interested in the pictures and toys. They are interested in the window. They ask mom, "What is this? What is that? Why do you have this? Why do you need that?" Mom answers their questions. She explains what the books and papers are about. She tells them who the pictures and toys are from. She shows them what the window shows. She says, "These are all part of my work and learning. They help me to know more and to do better. They are useful for me and for others." Anna and Ben listen to mom. They learn new things. They are amazed by mom's work and learning. They are proud of mom. They say, "Mom, you are smart. You are good. You are useful." Mom smiles. She hugs them. She says, "Thank you. You are smart. You are good. You are useful too."
