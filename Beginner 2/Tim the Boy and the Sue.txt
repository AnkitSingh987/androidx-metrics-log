Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a boy named Tim found a box. The box had a label. The label said, "Stay away!" Tim was a creative boy. He liked to make things and play with things. Tim saw his friend, Sue. Tim showed Sue the box. Sue said, "We should stay away. The label says so." But Tim did not listen. He wanted to open the box and see what was inside. They opened the box. Inside, there were many colors. The colors jumped out of the box. The colors were not nice. They made a big mess. Tim and Sue tried to fix it, but they could not. Now, their home was messy and the colors would not go away.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a pistol. He liked to play with it a lot. It was not a real pistol, just a toy one. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. They played a game where they would point their toy pistols at each other and say "bang!" Tim's friend, Ben, had a harsh voice when he said "bang!" It made Tim laugh. After a while, they got tired of playing and sat down to rest. They talked about their toy pistols and how much fun they had. They all agreed that playing with friends was the best part of their day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing in the kitchen with her toys. She had a small teaspoon that she liked a lot. She would use the teaspoon to mix pretend food for her toys. One day, Sue was feeling impatient. She wanted her mom to come play with her. She called out, "Mom, come play with me!" But her mom was busy doing work. So Sue waited and played with her teaspoon. Soon, her mom finished her work and came to play with Sue. They had lots of fun playing with the teaspoon and the toys. Sue was happy that she waited for her mom. They played until it was time for bed, and Sue fell asleep with her teaspoon by her side.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big door with a shiny knob. Tim wanted to see what was behind the door. He turned the knob and pushed the door open. Inside, he found a big room with lots of toys. Tim saw a girl named Sue playing with a toy car. She was having so much fun! Tim asked, "Can I play with you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they were playing, Tim accidentally spilled juice on the floor. He did not want Sue to be upset, so he found a cloth and wiped the floor clean. Sue saw what Tim did and said, "Thank you for helping!" They continued to play and had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck was driving fast. The truck had a wreck. The truck was stuck. The driver was sad. He needed help. A little boy was walking by. He saw the wreck. "I will rescue you," said the boy. He was very patient. He tried to push the truck. The truck did not move. The boy tried again. The truck still did not move. The boy did not give up. He thought of another way. The boy had a big, strong dog. The dog came to help. The dog pulled the truck. The truck moved! The driver was happy. "Thank you for the rescue," he said. The boy and his dog were heroes. The driver gave the boy and his dog a treat. They all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a big, serious bear. He was always very busy. One day, he met a little rabbit while walking. "Hello, Mr. Bear," said the rabbit. "Why are you always so serious?" The bear yawned and said, "I have a lot of work to do in this wild forest." The rabbit thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a game. I bet I can make you laugh." The serious bear agreed to play. They played hide and seek, and the rabbit hid in a tiny hole. The bear looked and looked but could not find the rabbit. Finally, the rabbit jumped out and surprised the bear. The bear laughed and laughed. From that day on, the bear was not so serious. He learned to have fun with his friends in the wild forest. And every time he yawned, he remembered the little rabbit who made him laugh.
