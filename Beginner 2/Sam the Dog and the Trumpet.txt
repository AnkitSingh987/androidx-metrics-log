Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Dog and the Trumpet

One day, a dog named Sam found a trumpet. He wanted to play it, but he didn't know how. He saw a cat named Tom, who was very good at playing the trumpet. Sam asked Tom, "Can you teach me to play the trumpet?" Tom said, "Yes, but you must be serious when you learn." Sam promised to be serious. They started to learn, but Sam couldn't stop shaking the trumpet. Tom got mad and said, "You have to be serious and not shake the trumpet!" Sam tried very hard to be serious and not shake the trumpet. He wanted to play it well like Tom. After some time, Sam learned to play the trumpet without shaking it. Tom was happy and said, "Good job, Sam! Now we can play the trumpet together!" In the end, Sam and Tom played the trumpet together and made beautiful music. They had fun, and Sam was glad he learned to be serious and not shake the trumpet.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Fred. Fred was green and loved to splash in the water. One day, Fred met a new friend named Sally. Sally was a red fish who lived in the same pond as Fred. Fred said to Sally, "Hi, I am Fred. I like to splash. Do you like to splash too?" Sally said, "Yes, I love to splash! Let's splash together!" So, Fred and Sally splashed and played in the water all day long. They had so much fun together. One day, they found a spicy pepper floating in the water. Fred and Sally did not know what it was. They took a little bite, and their mouths felt hot! They laughed and splashed water to cool down. From that day on, Fred and Sally were the best of friends, and they enjoyed splashing and playing together in their pond.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to build a big fort. He found some wood and started to build. Tim was having fun making his fort. He wanted to make it strong, so he found a long stick. He thought it looked like a spear. Tim's friend, Sue, came over and saw the fort. "What are you doing?" she asked. "I am building a fort with a spear to keep it safe," said Tim. Sue laughed and said, "That's a silly idea. A spear can't keep a fort safe." Just then, a big wind came and blew the fort down. Tim and Sue were surprised. They didn't know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. "Let's use the spear to hold the fort up!" he said. Sue agreed, and they used the spear to make the fort strong again. Now, Tim and Sue played in the fort every day. They were happy that the silly spear helped them build a strong fort. And they learned that sometimes, silly ideas can be good ideas too.

Tom and Lily were playing with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower. They put one block on top of another, and another, and another. The tower was very tall and colorful. "Look at our tower!" Tom said. "It is the best tower ever!" But Ben, who was sitting in the corner, did not look. He was playing with his toy car. He did not like blocks. He thought they were boring. He was ignorant. "Ben, come and see our tower!" Lily said. "It is very fun to play with blocks!" But Ben did not come. He did not answer. He ignored Lily. He only cared about his toy car. He was rude. Tom and Lily were sad. They wanted to share their tower with Ben. They wanted to be friends. But Ben did not want to be friends. He did not want to share. He was selfish. Then, something bad happened. Ben's toy car ran out of battery. It stopped moving. It made a funny noise. Ben was angry. He threw his toy car on the floor. He looked for another toy. He saw Tom and Lily's tower. "Hey, that looks fun!" Ben said. "I want to play with that!" He ran to the tower. He tried to grab a block. But he was clumsy. He knocked down the tower. The blocks fell everywhere. The tower was gone. Tom and Lily were shocked. They were angry. They were sad. They had worked hard to build their tower. They had been proud of their tower. But Ben had destroyed their tower. He had been mean. "Ben, why did you do that?" Tom said. "That was our tower! You ruined it!" "Sorry, I just wanted to play!" Ben said. "Can I have some blocks?" But Tom and Lily did not give him any blocks. They did not talk to him. They ignored him. They picked up their blocks. They moved to another spot. They started to build a new tower. They did not want to play with Ben. He had been ignorant. He had been rude. He had been selfish. He had not been a good friend.
