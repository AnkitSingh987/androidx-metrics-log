Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Jolly Girl and the Jewelry

Once upon a time, there was a jolly girl named Lily. She loved to play with her jewelry. She had many pretty necklaces and bracelets. She liked to count them all. She would say, "I have this number of necklaces and this number of bracelets." One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim saw Lily's jewelry and asked, "Can I play with your jewelry too?" Lily said, "Yes, let's count them together!" Tim and Lily sat down and counted all the pretty necklaces and bracelets. They had fun counting the jewelry. They laughed and played together. They were very happy. In the end, Lily and Tim became best friends. They played with the jewelry and counted them every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to play with her toys and get ready for fun days. One day, she saw her mom's purse on the table. Sue thought it was a toy, so she picked it up to play with it. Sue put the purse on her arm and walked around the house. She felt like a big girl, just like her mom. Sue showed her teddy bear the purse and they both liked it very much. They played dress up and had a tea party with the purse. Later, Sue's mom found her playing with the purse. She smiled and said, "Sue, you look so ready to go out with that purse!" Sue was very happy that her mom liked her playing with the purse. From that day on, Sue and her mom would get ready together and share the purse for their fun days.

One day, a little horse named Tiny lived in a tidy barn. Tiny loved to play with his friends. They liked to run and jump all day. Tiny's favorite thing to do was to slide down the hill near the barn. One sunny day, Tiny and his friends went to the hill to slide. They took turns sliding down the hill. Tiny went very fast and laughed as he slid down. His friends clapped and cheered for him. They all had so much fun. After they finished sliding, Tiny and his friends went back to the tidy barn. They were tired but happy. Tiny's mom gave them all some water and hay to eat. They ate and talked about the fun day they had. Tiny couldn't wait to slide again with his friends.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big box in the yard. They open the box and see many things inside. They see a toy gun, a toy car, a ball, and a cloth. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. He picks up the toy gun and points it at Jen. "Bang, bang, I shoot you!" Jen laughs and picks up the cloth. She wraps it around her head like a bandage. "Oh no, you hurt me!" she says. "But I am fine, don't worry." They play with the toy gun and the cloth for a while. They pretend to be cops and robbers, or soldiers and nurses. They have fun. But then, Tom's mom comes out of the house. She sees them playing with the toy gun and the cloth. She is not happy. "Tom, Jen, what are you doing?" she asks. "That toy gun is not yours. And that cloth is not a toy. It is a dirty rag. It has germs on it. You could get sick." Tom and Jen are scared. They drop the toy gun and the cloth. They say sorry to Tom's mom. "Come on, let's go inside and wash your hands," Tom's mom says. "And no more playing with things you don't know. You could get hurt." Tom and Jen nod. They follow Tom's mom inside. They learn their lesson. They don't play with the toy gun and the cloth again.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball that loved to roll. One day, it rolled far away from its home. The ball was lost and did not know how to get back. The ball met a nice dog who wanted to help. The dog knew where the ball's home was. The dog told the ball to roll with him, and they would find the home together. They rolled and rolled until they found the home. The ball was very happy to be back. The dog was happy too, because he made a new friend. The ball was always available to play with the dog, and they had lots of fun together.
