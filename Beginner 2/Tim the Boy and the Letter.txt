Title: Tim the Boy and the Letter

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to climb on everything. One day, Tim found a big letter on the floor. The letter was the letter "A". Tim was very enthusiastic about it. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house with the big letter "A". "Sam, look what I found!" said Tim. Sam looked at the letter and smiled. "Wow, it's the letter 'A'! Let's play with it!" said Sam. Tim and Sam took turns climbing on the letter "A". They laughed and had so much fun. They played together until the sun went down. Then, Tim and Sam said goodbye and went back to their homes. Tim put the letter "A" in his room and went to bed, excited for more fun the next day.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. They like to swing on the swings. They swing high and low. They laugh and sing. One day, Tom has a letter. He wants to mail it to his grandma. He says to Lily, "I have to mail this letter. Do you want to come with me?" Lily says, "Yes, I want to come. Can we swing on the way?" Tom says, "OK, we can swing on the way. But we have to hurry. The mailman will come soon." They run to the swings. They swing for a little while. They have fun. But then they see the mailman. He is walking to the mailbox. He is pale and has a big bag. Tom says, "Oh no, we have to go. The mailman is here. Come on, Lily." They jump off the swings. They run to the mailbox. Tom puts his letter in the slot. He says, "Bye, grandma. I love you." The mailman smiles. He says, "Hello, kids. What a nice letter. I'm sure your grandma will be happy." Tom and Lily say, "Thank you, mailman. Have a good day." They wave to the mailman. They run back to the swings. They swing some more. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, Nathan, a three-year-old, went to the beach with his dad. They played tag, built a sandcastle, and Nathan found many shells. But what Nathan wanted most of all was to lift the big, yellow rock. Nathan’s dad could see the excitement on his face and decided to help his son. Nathan put all his strength into it and with his dad’s help they were able to lift the rock! Underneath the rock was a spicy snack! Nathan and dad laughed, and Nathan quickly munched it up. The next day, Nathan wanted to lift the big, yellow rock again and when they arrived at the beach he ran straight to it. But Nathan and his dad could not lift it this time. Nathan began to cry, asking why they could not lift it. His dad then explained, “If you take things without permission, like a spicy snack, you have lost trust. You must earn other people’s trust back.” Nathan nodded and with determination, he worked hard to win back his dad’s trust. Soon, Nathan and his dad were able to lift the rock again, but this time there was nothing underneath it. But this time, the feeling of getting to lift the rock was the best reward of all. Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going to school for the first time. He was happy and excited. Tim put on his new clothes and shoes. His mom gave him a big hug and said, "Have fun at school!" At school, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue had a dirty dress. She fell in the mud while playing. Tim frowned when he saw her. He did not like mud. But Sue smiled and said, "Let's be friends!" Tim thought for a moment and then smiled too. Tim and Sue played together all day. They laughed and had fun. They forgot about the dirty dress and the frown. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were best friends. Tim could not wait to go back to school and play with Sue again.
