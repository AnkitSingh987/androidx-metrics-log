Category: Beginner
Title: The Warm Owl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a warm little owl. The owl lived in a tree in the woods. It liked to play with its friends, the squirrel and the rabbit. One day, the owl saw a big red ball near the tree. It wanted to play with the ball. The squirrel said, "Be careful, owl! The ball may pinch you!" But the owl did not listen. The owl went to the ball and tried to play with it. Suddenly, the ball pinched the owl's wing. "Ouch!" cried the owl. The squirrel and the rabbit came to help the owl. They took care of the owl until it felt better. The owl learned to listen to its friends and be more careful.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. Fin saw a big fish with a long tail. The big fish looked sad. Fin wanted to be a good friend, so he swam to the big fish and waved with his fin. "Hi, big fish! Why are you sad?" asked Fin. The big fish said, "I lost my salt. My mom gave it to me. I need it to be alert and strong. Can you help me find it?" Fin nodded and started to look for the salt. They swam around the sea, looking high and low. Fin was very alert, so he saw a shiny thing behind a rock. He swam to it and found the salt. Fin gave the salt to the big fish. The big fish was very happy. "Thank you, Fin! Now I can be alert and strong again!" The big fish and Fin became good friends. They swam and played in the sea together. And from that day on, they always helped each other when they needed it. The moral of this story is that being kind and helpful can make you good friends.

One day, a big round thing was rolling down a hill. It was having fun and going very fast. The big round thing liked to roll all day. But then, the big round thing hit a small rock. The rock did not like being hit. The rock said, "I am sorry, but you are too big to roll over me." The big round thing felt sorry too. It did not want to hurt the rock. The big round thing had an idea. It asked the rock to roll with it. The rock was happy and said yes. They rolled together down the hill, and they both had fun. The big round thing and the rock became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to create things. She would make fancy hats, pretty dolls, and fun toys. One day, Sue decided to create a big party for all of her friends. Sue's friends came to the party, and they were all very happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and wore the fancy hats that Sue made. Then, Sue's friend Tim said, "I like your party, Sue! You create the best things!" After the games and food, Sue asked everyone to be quiet for a moment. They all stopped talking, and there was silence in the room. Sue had a surprise for everyone. She brought out a big, beautiful cake that she had made. Everyone clapped and cheered for Sue's fancy cake. Sue's party was a big success. All her friends loved the fancy things she created and the fun they had. Sue was happy to share her creations with her friends, and they all knew that Sue was a very special girl.

Once upon a time, there was a stick. This stick was very intelligent. It could dance all by itself. Every day, the stick would dance in the forest. One day, while the stick was dancing, a big bird saw it. The bird was very surprised. It had never seen a dancing stick before. The bird wanted to show its friends the dancing stick. The bird picked up the stick and flew away. The stick stopped dancing. The bird was sad. It showed the stick to its friends, but the stick did not dance. The friends did not believe the bird. Then, the bird had an idea. It put the stick back in the forest. The stick started to dance again. The bird and its friends watched the stick dance. They were all very happy. The stick was happy too.
