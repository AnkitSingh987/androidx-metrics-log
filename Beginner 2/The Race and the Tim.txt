Category: Beginner
Title: The Race and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a normal little town, there was a big race. All the kids wanted to win. Tim was a small boy who wanted to win too. One day, while walking to the race, Tim saw a big mailbox. He thought it was very interesting. On the day of the race, all the kids ran very fast. Tim was trying his best to win. He remembered the big mailbox he saw on his walk. He had an idea. Tim ran to the mailbox and hid behind it. When the other kids passed by, he jumped out and ran faster than them. Tim crossed the finish line and won the race. All the kids were surprised, but they clapped for Tim. The big mailbox helped Tim win the race and he was very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim got a toy helicopter. It was a gift from his mom. Tim was very happy. He played with the helicopter all day. He flew it in the air and made it go up and down. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play with him. Sam saw the helicopter and wanted to play with it too. But Tim was a little bit spoiled. He didn't want to share his toy with Sam. Sam was sad. Tim's mom saw them and had an idea. She took some paper and showed them how to fold it to make paper helicopters. Now, Tim and Sam both had helicopters to play with. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a great big snowman. He had two eyes made of rocks and a carrot for a nose. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to play with the snowman. Tim said, "Hello, Mr. Snowman! Let's play!" The snowman could not talk, but Tim knew he was happy to play. They played all day in the sun. The snowman started to melt a little, but Tim did not notice. As the sun went down, Tim saw a small flame in the woods. He went to the flame and saw it was a tiny campfire. Tim was cold, so he stood by the fire to get warm. He looked back at the snowman and saw it was melting more. "Mr. Snowman, you are melting! I will help you!" Tim said. He took some snow from the ground and put it on the snowman. The snowman stopped melting and was great again. Tim said goodbye to the snowman and went home. The next day, Tim came back to play with the snowman again. They were both very happy.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog with a guard. The guard wears a blue shirt and a hat. He holds a leash. "Look, a dog!" Lily says. She loves dogs. She wants to pet the dog. "Wait, Lily!" Ben says. He is afraid of dogs. He thinks the dog is scary. He pulls Lily's hand. "The guard will be mad!" Lily does not listen. She runs to the dog. She observes the dog's fur and eyes. She smiles. The dog wags its tail. It likes Lily. "Hello, dog!" Lily says. She reaches out her hand. She wants to touch the dog's ear. "Stop, girl!" the guard says. He is angry. He pulls the leash. The dog barks. It does not like the guard. Lily is scared. She cries. She runs back to Ben. Ben hugs her. He is sorry. "Sorry, guard!" Ben says. He is polite. He does not want to make the guard more mad. The guard does not say sorry. He is rude. He walks away with the dog. He thinks Lily and Ben are cheap. He does not like children. Lily and Ben are sad. They do not like the guard. They do not like the dog. They go home with their mom. Their mom gives them cookies. She loves them. She is nice.

Noah was being very stubborn. His mom asked him to help sort the cooler for the big picnic, but he turned and ran away. Mom followed him until he stopped and stood still. "Noah, please help me," said Mom. "No!" said Noah. Mom sighed and tried again. "Noah, I need your help. Let's sort the cooler together." Finally, Noah agreed and together they went to the cooler. It was filled with delicious snacks, like apples and sandwiches. Noah grabbed some apples and sorted them into piles. Mom was so proud as Noah worked hard. When they finished, Noah gave Mom a big hug. She had been so patient with him, and he was so happy that he could help. The cooler was now ready for the big picnic.
