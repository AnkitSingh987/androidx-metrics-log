Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Crown

Anna and Ben were best friends. They liked to play together every day. They liked to pretend they were a king and a queen. They had a big castle made of pillows and blankets. They had a crown and a ring made of paper. One day, they decided to marry each other. They put on their crown and ring and said, "I marry you, you marry me, we are happy as can be." They kissed each other on the cheek and hugged. They were very happy. But then, Anna's little brother Tom came in. He was naughty. He wanted to play too. He grabbed the crown and the ring and ran away. He said, "Ha ha, I am the king, you are not, give me your castle or I will tickle your toe." Anna and Ben were very angry. They chased Tom around the house. They said, "Give us back our crown and ring, you are not the king, you are naughty, we will tell mom." They tried to catch him, but he was fast. Tom ran into the kitchen, where mom was cooking. He tripped on the rug and fell down. The crown and the ring flew out of his hands and landed in the pot. Mom saw what happened and said, "Oh no, what are you doing, Tom? You are naughty, you ruined the soup, and you took Anna and Ben's things. You have to say sorry and give them back." Tom felt bad. He said sorry to mom, Anna, and Ben. He gave them back their crown and ring. He said, "I am sorry, I just wanted to play, can I play with you?" Anna and Ben forgave him. They said, "Yes, you can play with us, but you have to be nice, and you have to be the prince, not the king." They all went back to their castle and played happily. Mom smiled and said, "That's better, now you are all friends."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a curious hero named Tom. Tom liked to help people and animals. He had a big heart and a big smile. One sunny day, Tom saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and crying. Tom wanted to help the cat. He entered the tree and climbed up to save the cat. The cat was happy and licked Tom's face. Tom and the cat became best friends. They played together every day. Tom was a hero to the cat and to everyone in the town. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a mouse named Tim. Tim was a nervous mouse. He always hid when he heard any sound. One day, Tim saw a big piece of cheese on the floor. He was so hungry, but he was too scared to get it. Tim's friend, Sam, was a brave mouse. Sam saw Tim looking at the cheese. "Don't be scared, Tim," said Sam. "I will help you get the cheese." Tim and Sam went near the cheese. They did not know that it was on a trap. Just as they were about to take the cheese, the trap moved. Sam was quick. He said, "Let it go, Tim!" Tim let go of the cheese just in time. The trap did not catch them. They were safe. Tim and Sam were happy. They learned that it is good to be brave and to help each other.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a gloomy day, and the sky was gray. Tim saw his friend, a small dog named Max. Max had a hurt leg, so he could not run fast. Tim said, "Max, I dare you to race me to the tree!" Max looked at his leg and was sad. He knew he could not race because of his hurt leg. Tim saw that Max was sad and had an idea. Tim said, "Max, let's play a different game. We can play hide and seek!" So, they played hide and seek together. Tim and Max had lots of fun, even on the gloomy day. And Max's hurt leg did not stop him from having fun with his friend.
