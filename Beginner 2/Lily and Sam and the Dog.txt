Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Sam and the Dog

Lily and Sam were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big dog. The dog was black and white. It had a long tail. The dog was called True. "Hi, True!" Lily said. She ran to the dog. She wanted to pet it. Sam was scared. He did not like big dogs. "Wait, Lily!" Sam said. He did not run. He walked slow. "The dog might bite you!" Lily did not listen. She reached out her hand. True licked her hand. Lily laughed. She hugged the dog. She said, "True is nice. He is not mean." Sam was still scared. He stayed away. He said, "How do you know his name is True?" Lily pointed to the dog's collar. It had a tag. The tag said, "True". Lily said, "See? His name is True. He is a good dog." Sam did not believe her. He said, "Maybe the tag is wrong. Maybe his name is not True. Maybe he is a bad dog." Lily was angry. She said, "No, Sam. You are wrong. His name is True. He is a good dog. You are a bad friend. You are mean to True." Lily and Sam started to fight. They yelled at each other. True did not like the noise. He barked. He wagged his tail. He wanted to play. He ran to Lily and Sam. He jumped on them. He knocked them down. He licked their faces. Lily and Sam stopped fighting. They felt silly. They said sorry to each other. They said sorry to True. They got up. They hugged True. They decided to play with him. They threw a ball. True chased the ball. He brought it back. Lily and Sam skipped with True. They had fun. They were happy. They were friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a fearful cat named Tom. Tom was always scared of everything. He didn't like to play outside because he thought something bad might happen. One sunny day, Tom saw some equipment in the yard. It was a ball and a toy mouse. Tom wanted to play, but he was too scared. His friend, a brave dog named Max, came to serve him. Max said, "Don't be scared, Tom. I will help you play with the equipment." Max showed Tom how to play with the ball and the toy mouse. Tom started to feel less fearful. He had a lot of fun playing with Max and the equipment. From that day on, Tom was not scared anymore. He learned that it was fun to try new things and play with his friend Max.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben was a very useful bunny who liked to help his friends. One sunny day, Ben saw his friend, a tiny bird, who was sad. The tiny bird lost her home in a big storm, and now she had nowhere to live. Ben wanted to help his friend. He knew that a magic spell could make a new home appear for the tiny bird. Ben hopped around the forest, asking all the animals if they knew the magic spell. Finally, he met a wise old turtle who knew the spell. The turtle told Ben the magic words, and Ben thanked him. Ben went back to the tiny bird and said the magic spell. Suddenly, a beautiful new home appeared for the tiny bird! She was so happy, and thanked Ben for his help. From that day on, Ben and the tiny bird were best friends. The moral of the story is that being useful and helping others can bring happiness and friendship.

One day, a healthy dog named Spot went into a warm house. In the house, there was a fireplace. Spot liked to sit by the fireplace and feel the heat. Spot saw a girl named Jane. Jane was playing with a ball. Spot wanted to play too. Jane said, "Spot, come play with me!" Spot was happy and ran to Jane. Jane threw the ball and Spot ran to get it. They played for a long time. Then, they got tired. Jane and Spot went near the fireplace. Jane knelt down next to Spot. They both felt warm and cozy by the fire. They loved playing together.
