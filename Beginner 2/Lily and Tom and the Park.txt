Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom and the Park

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play together. One day, they want to go on a journey. They ask their moms if they can go. "Mom, can we go on a journey?" Lily says. "Where do you want to go?" Mom asks. "We want to go to the park. We want to see the ducks and the swings and the slide," Tom says. Mom thinks for a moment. She looks at the clock. She says, "Okay, you can go on a journey to the park. But you have to be back by lunchtime. And you have to stay together and be careful." "Yay! Thank you, Mom!" Lily and Tom say. They hug their moms and put on their shoes and coats. They take a bag with some bread and water. They are ready for their journey. They walk to the park. They see many things on the way. They see cars and buses and bikes. They see dogs and cats and birds. They see flowers and trees and houses. They talk and laugh and sing. They reach the park. They see the ducks and the swings and the slide. They are happy. They feed the ducks some bread. They take turns on the swings and the slide. They have fun. They look at the sun. It is high in the sky. They remember what their moms said. They say, "It is time to go back. We have to be home by lunchtime." They pack their bag and say goodbye to the ducks and the swings and the slide. They walk back to their homes. They see the same things on the way. They talk and laugh and sing. They reach their homes. They see their moms. They say, "We are back, Mom! We had a great journey!" Their moms smile and hug them. They say, "We are glad you had a great journey. And we are glad you are back. Now, let's have some lunch. We have some soup and sandwiches available." Lily and Tom say, "Yum! We are hungry. Thank you, Mom!" They eat their lunch and tell their moms about their journey. They are happy. They are friends. They like to play together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. Today is very cold. The wind blows hard. Anna and Ben shiver. "Let's go to my house," Anna says. "We can have hot cocoa and cookies." "OK," Ben says. "That sounds good." They run to Anna's house. They knock on the door. Anna's mom opens the door. "Hello, Anna and Ben," Anna's mom says. "You look very cold. Come in, come in." "Thank you, Mrs. Lee," Ben says. He is polite. Anna and Ben go inside. They take off their coats and hats. They sit on the couch. Anna's mom brings them hot cocoa and cookies. "Here you go, Anna and Ben," Anna's mom says. "Enjoy your snack." "Thank you, Mrs. Lee," Anna and Ben say. They smile. They sip their hot cocoa and eat their cookies. They feel warm and happy. They talk and laugh. They forget about the cold. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play outside in the sun. He would run and jump all day long. One day, Spot's mom told him to stay in the yard. She wanted Spot to be safe and not get lost. Spot listened to his mom and stayed in the yard. He played with his ball and chased butterflies. It was a peaceful day, and Spot was happy. He did not go out of the yard, because he knew his mom wanted him to stay. As a result, Spot's mom was very proud of him for listening. She gave him a big hug and a treat. Spot felt good for doing what his mom asked. From that day on, Spot always stayed in the yard and played happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She lived on a farm with her mom and dad. One day, her dad said, "Anna, I want to introduce you to a new friend." Anna was very excited to meet her new friend. Her dad took her to the chicken coop. Inside, there were many chickens. But there was one chicken that was different. This chicken was deaf, which means it could not hear. Anna thought this chicken was special. Anna took care of the deaf chicken every day. She gave it food and water. She even gave the chicken a name: Daisy. Anna and Daisy became the best of friends. They played together on the farm, and Anna was very happy to have a special friend like Daisy.
