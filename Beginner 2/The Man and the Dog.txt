Category: Beginner
Title: The Man and the Dog

One day, a man had to deliver a heavy fan. He put it in a big box and went on his way. He walked and walked, because the fan was too heavy for his bike. The sun was hot and he was tired. As he walked, he saw a little dog. The dog looked at the box and barked. The man was scared, but the dog just wanted to help. The dog pushed the box with its nose, and together, they moved the heavy fan. But, guess what? When they opened the box, there was no fan inside! It was full of balloons! The man laughed and the dog barked. They played with the balloons and forgot about the heavy fan.

Once upon a time, there was a dull house. It was very old and not fun. In the house, there lived a boy and his cat. They liked to play all day in the house. But the boy always forgot to lock the door. One day, the boy and his cat went outside to play. They saw a big tree and climbed it. They were having a lot of fun. But they did not know that they forgot to lock the house door. While they were playing, a big, mean wolf came to the house. The wolf saw the door was not locked. He went inside the house and made a big mess. When the boy and his cat came back, they saw the mess. They were very sad. They learned to always lock the door. But the house was never the same again.

One day, Tim and Sam went for a walk in the woods. They saw a big tree with a rare, shiny rock on it. The rock was high up on the tree, and they both wanted it. "I want the rock!" said Tim. "No, I want it!" said Sam. They saw a big cobweb near the rock. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's pull the cobweb to get the rock down." So, they both pulled the cobweb together. The rock fell down, but it broke in half. Now, Tim and Sam were both happy. They each got a piece of the rare, shiny rock. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

One day, it began to rain. Tom, a little boy, loved the rain. He put on his rain boots and rain coat. He went outside to play. Tom found a big pot. He put the pot under the rain to catch the water. The pot filled up fast. Tom felt very organized with his pot full of rain water. Tom took the pot inside. He showed his mom the rain water he caught. Mom was happy and organized too. They used the rain water to water their plants. Tom and his mom smiled as they watched the plants grow.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a pizza place with his mom. They both loved pizza a lot. Tim saw a big pizza with many colors on it. He was very happy and excited. "Mom, can we get that big pizza?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but we have to remain in line and wait for our turn." Tim was a persistent boy, so he waited in the line with his mom. Finally, it was their turn to order the pizza. They got the big pizza and sat down to eat it. Tim took a big bite and said, "Yum! This is the best pizza ever!" They both enjoyed the pizza and went home with a big smile on their faces.

One day, a boy went to the barber. The barber had a weird hat. The boy sat in the chair. The barber started to cut the boy's hair. The boy said, "Why do you wear a weird hat?" The barber paused and smiled. He said, "I wear this hat to make people laugh and feel happy." The boy thought about it. He said, "I want to make people happy too. Can I wear a weird hat?" The barber paused again. He said, "Yes, you can. You can wear any hat you like." The boy and the barber both wore weird hats. They made people laugh and feel happy. The boy learned that it is good to make others happy.
