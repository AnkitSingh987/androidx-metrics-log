Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

One day, a boy named Tim went to school. It was a big and scary place. He was scared, but his mom said, "Don't worry, you will have fun and make friends." Tim hoped his mom was right. In school, Tim had a test. The test was to raise a small plant. Tim and his friends took care of their plants. They gave them water and put them in the sun. The plants grew, but Tim's plant did not grow. Tim was sad and scared. One day, Tim's plant started to grow. It grew and grew. It was not a normal plant. It was a magic plant! The magic plant could talk and play with Tim. All the kids were happy and not scared anymore. Tim's mom was right, school was fun.

One day, a pretty bug went for a walk. The bug was very happy. It saw a big tree and wanted to mark its spot on the tree. So, it used its legs to make a small mark on the tree. The pretty bug was very proud of its mark. The pretty bug met a big bird. The bird said, "What are you doing, little bug?" The bug said, "I made a mark on the tree. I am very happy." The bird looked at the mark and said, "That is a nice mark, little bug." But then, a strong wind came. The wind was very, very strong. The pretty bug tried to hold on to the tree. But the wind was too strong. The pretty bug was blown away and could not find its way back to the tree with its mark. The pretty bug was very sad.

One day, Tim and Sue went for a walk. They saw a big hill. Tim said, "Let's go up the hill!" Sue said, "Wait, I see something scary. Maybe we should delay our walk." Tim and Sue hid behind a tree. They watched the scary thing on the hill. It was big and had long arms. They were scared, but they wanted to know what it was. After a while, the scary thing moved. Tim and Sue saw it was just a big tree branch. They laughed and went up the hill. They played and had fun. The hill was not scary after all.

One day, a cat named Tom found a big cushion. It was soft and nice to sit on. Tom had a silly idea. He wanted to tie the cushion to his tail. He thought it would be fun to drag the cushion around. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Max, for help. "Max, can you help me tie this cushion to my tail?" Max looked at Tom and said, "Okay, but it might be hard to walk with it." Tom didn't care. He just wanted to try it. Max tied the cushion to Tom's tail. Tom started to walk, but the cushion was too big. It made him fall down. He tried again and again, but he kept falling. Tom laughed and said, "You were right, Max. This is too hard." Max helped Tom untie the cushion. They both laughed at the silly idea and played together without the cushion.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy shop. In the shop, there were many toys. A little girl named Lily would always walk by and watch the toys through the window. She loved the shop and dreamed of having a toy from there. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, if you help me clean the house, I have a surprise for you." Lily was excited and helped her mom make everything tidy. After they finished, her mom took her hand and they walked to the shop. When they got to the shop, Lily's mom said, "You did such a great job helping me, you can pick one toy!" Lily was so happy and picked a beautiful doll. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Mommy!" From that day on, Lily took care of her doll and always remembered the special day at the tidy shop.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a wealthy man named Tom. He had a pet bird named Polly. Polly lived in a cage. Tom loved to joke with Polly. One day, Tom said to Polly, "Do you want to fly outside?" Polly looked at Tom and said, "Yes, please!" Tom opened the cage and Polly flew out. They played and joked together in the garden. As they played, a little girl named Sue walked by. She saw Tom and Polly having fun. Sue asked, "Can I play and joke with you?" Tom said, "Yes, come play with us!" So, Sue, Tom, and Polly played and joked together all day long.
