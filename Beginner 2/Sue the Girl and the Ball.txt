Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in the park. She saw a big, red ball and kicked it. The ball went very far away. Sue ran after the ball but she could not find it. She felt sad and started to cry. Sue's kind parent heard her cry and came to help. They looked for the ball together. They looked near the trees and near the swings, but the ball was still lost. Sue was very sad and her parent hugged her. Just then, a dog came running with the red ball in its mouth. The dog dropped the ball at Sue's feet and wagged its tail. Sue was so happy, she yelled, "Thank you!" The dog barked and ran away. Sue and her parent laughed and played with the ball together. They had a fun day in the park after all.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a clown making funny faces. The clown had a big red nose and wore a funny hat. Tim was very happy to see the clown. The clown had a big ball. He asked Tim, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" They started to play together. Tim kicked the ball and the clown kicked it back. But then, the ball hit the clown's big red nose. The clown was upset. Tim said, "I am sorry, Mr. Clown." The clown smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's keep playing." They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a deep part of the sea. He had many friends, like Star the starfish and Crabby the crab. They loved to play together all day long. They swam in lines and made shapes in the water. One day, Fin saw that his friend, Crabby, was sad. Crabby was not good at swimming in a line like the other sea animals. Fin wanted to help Crabby improve and be happy again. So, Fin, Star, and Crabby practiced swimming together in a line. They swam in lines every day, and Crabby got better and better. Soon, Crabby could swim in a line just as well as Fin and Star. All the sea friends were so happy. They swam in deep lines and had more fun than ever before. And that's how Fin helped Crabby improve and made the sea a happier place for everyone.

One day, a bright cloth went for a walk. It was a sunny day, and the cloth felt happy. The bright cloth had a friend, a little ball. The ball said, "Let's go play in the park!" The cloth agreed, and they went to the park. At the park, they met a dog. The dog saw the bright cloth and wanted to play too. The dog said, "Can I play with you?" The cloth and the ball said, "Yes, let's all play together!" They played and played until the sky turned dark. Then, something unexpected happened. The bright cloth started to float up into the sky! The dog and the ball were sad and said, "Please come back!" The bright cloth said, "I am now a star in the sky! I will watch over you both and shine bright every night!" From that day on, the dog and the ball knew that their friend was always with them, shining brightly in the sky.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They went outside to play. Jack wanted to touch a frog but Jill was scared. She didn't want to be near the frog. Jack felt sorry for Jill. He thought for a bit and then made a plan. He said, "Let's climb the hill today. I recommend that we go together." Jill thought this was a good idea. Going up the hill wasn't so scary. Plus, it was a lot of fun to go with a friend. Jack and Jill started their climb. At first, Jack and Jill moved slowly, but then they started to go faster and faster. They raced each other up the hill until they arrived at the top. When they got to the top, Jill wasn't scared anymore. She had lots of fun and she was glad that she went with Jack. They both looked down the hill and high-fived. It was a great day!
