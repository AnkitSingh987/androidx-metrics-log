Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Bird and the Wind

Once upon a time, there was a nice bird named Tim. Tim had a small house in a big tree. He was happy to own the house. One day, a big wind came. It was a hurricane. The wind was very strong, and Tim was scared. Tim saw another bird named Sam. Sam was sad too. Tim asked, "Are you scared of the wind?" Sam said, "Yes, I am. My house is gone." Tim felt bad for Sam. They both wanted the wind to stop. But the wind did not stop. The hurricane got bigger and bigger. Tim and Sam tried to hold on to the tree, but it was too hard. The wind blew them away. Tim lost his nice house, and Sam did not find a new one. They were sad, and the wind was still strong.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an icy Sunday. The sun was not shining, and the wind was cold. The people in the town did not like to go outside on such a day. They stayed in their warm homes. One little girl named Lily wanted to play outside. She put on her coat, hat, and gloves. She went to the door to unlock it. With a big smile, Lily opened the door and went outside. Lily played in the icy air all day. She made a snowman and threw snowballs. When it was time to go home, she unlocked the door and went inside. Her mom made her hot cocoa to warm up. Lily loved the icy Sunday and couldn't wait for the next one.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily went on an adventurous walk with her mom. They walked in the park and saw many pretty flowers. Lily loved to pick the flowers and give them to her mom. The sun was shining, and they could feel the heat on their faces. Lily saw a big rock and wanted to sit on it. Her mom said, "Be careful, Lily. The rock might be hot from the heat." But Lily touched the rock, and it was not hot. She sat on the rock and started to rock back and forth, laughing. Suddenly, the rock moved! It was not a rock, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, Lily. I am a talking turtle." Lily and her mom were very surprised. They had never met a talking turtle before. They all became friends and had a fun day at the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a purse that he loved very much. One day, he went for a walk in the park with his purse. In the park, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's purse and had a question. She asked, "What is in your purse, Tom?" Tom smiled and opened his purse to show her. Inside the purse, there were many pretty stones and shiny coins. Sue liked the stones and coins, and Tom let her hold them. They played with the stones and coins in the park all day. In the end, Tom and Sue became good friends, and they always played together at the park with Tom's purse.

---

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big dog with black fur and sharp teeth. The dog was eating some beef that someone had left on the grass. "Look, a dog!" Tom said. "Let's go and pet it!" "No, Tom, don't!" Sue said. "The dog might bite you! It is not a nice dog, it is a mean dog!" But Tom did not listen to Sue. He was not afraid of the dog. He thought he was brave and smart. He ran towards the dog and tried to touch its beef. The dog did not like that. It growled and barked and snapped at Tom. It scared Tom very much. Tom screamed and ran away from the dog. He was sorry that he did not listen to Sue. Sue ran after Tom and hugged him. She was glad that he was not hurt. She said, "Tom, you were foolish to go near the dog. You should not bother animals when they are eating. They might scare you or hurt you. Next time, listen to me, OK?" Tom nodded and said, "OK, Sue, I will listen to you. You are a good friend. I am sorry that I was foolish. Can we still play in the park?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tom, we can still play in the park. But let's stay away from the dog, OK?" Tom agreed and said, "OK, Sue, let's stay away from the dog. And let's have some fun!"
