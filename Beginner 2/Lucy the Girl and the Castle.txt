Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a spade that she loved to play with in the sand. Lucy would dig and dig, making big holes and tall sand castles. One day, Lucy's mom told her to be patient and remember to take her spade when they went to the beach. Lucy was excited and she promised to remember. When they got to the beach, Lucy played with her spade all day. She was patient and dug a big hole for her sand castle. At the end of the day, Lucy remembered to take her spade home. She was happy and couldn't wait to play with her spade again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull was upset. He did not want to play with the other animals. They tried to make him happy, but the bull would always refuse. One day, a little bird came to the bull. The bird wanted to be friends. The bull said no. The bird did not go away. The bird sat on the bull's back and sang a pretty song. The bull started to feel happy. He wanted to play with the bird. The bull did not refuse anymore. The bird and the bull became best friends. And the other animals were happy too. The bull was not upset anymore.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and fluffy. It has a long tail and a wet nose. The dog runs to Anna and Ben. It wants to play too. "Hello, doggy!" Anna says. She tries to pet the dog. But the dog is too excited. It jumps on Anna and knocks her down. Anna falls on the grass. She hurts her knee. It bleeds a little. Anna cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Anna says. "Go away, doggy! You are bad!" Ben sees Anna crying. He feels sorry for her. He runs to her and helps her up. He hugs her. He says, "Don't cry, Anna. I am here. You are brave." The dog sees Ben hugging Anna. It feels sorry too. It stops jumping. It sits on the ground. It wags its tail. It says, "Woof, woof, woof!" It means, "I am sorry, Anna. I am not bad. I just want to be your friend." Anna looks at the dog. She sees its big eyes and its wet nose. She sees its tail wagging. She thinks the dog is cute. She stops crying. She says, "It's okay, doggy. You didn't mean to hurt me. You are just playful." She pets the dog. The dog licks her hand. It says, "Woof, woof, woof!" It means, "Thank you, Anna. You are kind." Ben smiles. He says, "Look, Anna. The dog likes you. Can we play with the dog?" Anna nods. She says, "Yes, Ben. We can play with the dog. But be careful. The dog is big and strong. We are small and gentle." They play with the dog. They throw a ball and the dog fetches it. They run and the dog chases them. They laugh and the dog barks. They have fun. The dog is graceful. It runs fast and jumps high. It catches the ball in its mouth. It is happy. Anna and Ben are happy too. They hug the dog. They hug each other. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to climb on everything. She climbed on chairs, tables, and even trees. Climbing was her favorite exercise. It made her feel happy and strong. One day, Amy saw a very tall tree. She wanted to climb it. Her friend, Ben, said, "Amy, that tree is too big. You might fall and get hurt." But naughty Amy didn't listen. She started to climb the tree. As Amy climbed higher and higher, she felt scared. She looked down and saw Ben. Ben yelled, "Amy, come down. It's not safe!" Amy listened to Ben and slowly climbed down. When she reached the ground, she hugged Ben and said, "Thank you, Ben. I will be careful next time." From that day on, Amy only climbed on things that were safe. She still had fun and got her exercise, but she was no longer naughty.
