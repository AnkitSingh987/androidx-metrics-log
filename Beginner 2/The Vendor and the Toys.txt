Category: Beginner
Title: The Vendor and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big vendor who sold toys. He had many toys that kids loved. One day, a little girl named Amy went to see the big vendor. She wanted to join her friends who were playing with the toys. "Hello, big vendor! Can I join my friends?" Amy asked. The big vendor smiled and said, "Yes, you can join them. But first, you must find the special toy I hid." Amy looked around and saw many toys. She picked up a teddy bear, but it was not the special toy. Then she picked up a toy car, but it was not the special toy either. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew all the toys away! The big vendor, Amy, and her friends were very surprised. They did not know what to do. Then, they saw a big bird flying with the special toy in its mouth. The big bird dropped the toy, and Amy caught it. "Good job, Amy! You found the special toy!" the big vendor said. Amy and her friends were happy, and they all joined together to play with the toys. The big wind had brought them a new friend, the big bird, and they all had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Sam. Sam loved to play on the soft rug in the living room. He would run, jump, and roll on the rug all day long. One day, Sam saw a tiny bug on the rug. He got very excited and started to chase the bug. The bug was fast, but Sam was faster. They played a fun game of chase on the rug. As Sam got closer to the bug, he started to whisper, "I will get you, little bug!" The bug heard Sam and tried to hide. But Sam was a good finder. He found the bug and gave it a gentle tap. Then, they both laughed and played together on the rug.

One day, a big dog and a little dog went to play in the park. The big dog was very healthy and strong. The little dog was small but fast. They both had a leash, but they wanted to run free and have fun. The big dog saw a ball and wanted to play with it. The little dog wanted to play too, but the big dog did not want to share. The little dog felt sad and did not know what to do. The big dog just gave a shrug and kept playing with the ball. Then, the little dog had an idea. He ran very fast and took the ball from the big dog. The big dog was surprised and chased the little dog. They played together, running and laughing. In the end, they both learned to share and had a great time at the park.

Once upon a time there was a friendly clown. He loved to make all the kids smile. One day he noticed a blackboard and he decided to try and escape from it. He jumped, he hopped, he rolled and tumbled, but he just couldn't escape it. Then suddenly, a friendly bird flew down to help him. She called upon her friend the Friendly Dragon who swooped in and blew a puff of fire onto the blackboard. Suddenly the blackboard split apart and the clown was free. All the kids cheered, the clown smiled and the friendly bird and dragon flew away happily. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, while hopping, he found a big, old brick. The brick was red and heavy. Bob wanted to show his friend, Tim the frog. So, he hopped to Tim's house with the brick. "Look, Tim! I found a big, old brick!" said Bob. Tim looked at the brick and said, "Wow! That's a big brick, Bob! Let's play with it!" Bob and Tim took turns hopping over the old brick. They were having so much fun. Then, they built a little wall with more bricks. They hopped over the wall, too. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They said goodbye and went to their homes, ready for a new day of fun.
