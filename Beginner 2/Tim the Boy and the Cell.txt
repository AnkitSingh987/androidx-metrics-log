Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cell

One day, a little boy named Tim found a white cell. The cell was very small and round. Tim wanted to show the cell to his friend, Sue. So, he put the cell in a jar and went to Sue's house. When Tim got to Sue's house, he said, "Sue, look at my white cell! It is so small and round!" Sue looked at the cell and said, "Wow, that is a very white cell! But I think it needs some color." Tim and Sue had an idea. They took some paint and started to turn the cell different colors. First, they turned it red. Then, they turned it blue. Finally, they turned it green. But then, the cell started to look sad. Tim and Sue didn't know what to do. They tried to turn the cell white again, but they couldn't. They were worried they had hurt the cell. In the end, Tim and Sue found a way to turn the cell white again. They washed it with water and soap. The cell was happy to be white again, and Tim and Sue learned that sometimes things are perfect just the way they are.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He loved to play outside in the sunshine. When it was sunny, he would play for hours and hours. He liked chasing the birds and running through the grass. The sunshine was so light and gentle. It was the perfect day for playing outside. The little boy's mom asked him to behave. She wanted him to stay in the yard and not get too far. The little boy said, "Yes, mommy" and he did what his mom asked. The little boy enjoyed playing in the sunshine, and he was careful to behave. He ran and laughed and even dug a few holes in the ground. After a while, he got tired and went inside for a rest. The sunshine was still out, and the little boy knew that if he behaved, he could play in the sunshine again tomorrow. He smiled and went to bed, happy and content that he could play in the sunshine again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a big house with his mom and dad. He loved to play in his room with all his toys. One day, Tim's mom was very busy in the kitchen. She was making a yummy cake for Tim's dad. Tim wanted to help his mom, so he took a step out of his room. He walked to the kitchen and said, "Mom, can I help you?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can help me put the frosting on the cake." Tim was so happy to help his mom. He took a step closer to the cake and started to spread the frosting on it. He was very careful not to make a mess. When they were done, Tim's mom said, "Thank you, Tim. You did a great job!" Tim felt proud of himself for helping his mom. He took a step back into his room and continued to play with his toys. He couldn't wait for his dad to come home and see the cake they made together. That evening, when Tim's dad came home, he saw the cake and said, "Wow! This looks amazing!" Tim smiled and said, "I helped mom make it!" His dad hugged him and said, "Thank you, Tim. You did a great job!" And they all enjoyed the yummy cake together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He loved to play on the swings and slide. Tim saw his friend, Sue, near the big tree. Sue looked worried. "Hi Sue, why are you worried?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I lost my ring. It is very special. My mom gave it to me." Tim wanted to help Sue find her ring. They looked under the tree, near the slide, and by the swings. Finally, they found the ring by the sandbox. Sue was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." They played at the park until the sun went down, both happy and smiling.
