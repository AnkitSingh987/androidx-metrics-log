Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim found a sharp rock. He liked the rock and wanted to keep it. He put it in his pocket and went to play with his friend, Sally. While playing, Tim showed Sally the sharp rock. Sally said, "Be careful, Tim! That rock can hurt your skin!" Tim didn't listen and kept playing with the rock. Suddenly, Tim felt a pain in his hand. He looked down and saw that the sharp rock had cut his skin. He started to cry. Sally said, "I told you to be careful! Don't be rude and ignore me next time." Tim said sorry and they went to find a bandage for his hand. From that day, Tim never played with sharp things again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Pam. Pam was only three years old, but she was very brave. One day, Pam decided to go out for a walk. As she walked, Pam noticed a tip lying on the ground. She was very excited, thinking it was a surprise for her. Pam shouted excitedly "What a surprise!" and quickly picked it up. She looked closely and saw that the tip was round, sparkly and fit perfectly in her hands. Pam wanted to keep the tip, so she quickly ran back home to show it to mum. "Mummy! Look what I found. Is this a surprise for me?" Pam asked. Mum smiled and said, "You should always be careful when you find something on the ground, but this time it's a very nice surprise for you. Now put it away so it stays safe." Pam was very happy and kept the sparkly tip safe in her bedroom. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a filthy piece of paper on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it had a poem on it. Tim wanted to show it to his mom, so he rushed inside the house. "Mom, look what I found!" Tim said, showing her the dirty paper. "It has a poem on it!" His mom looked at the paper and said, "Oh, it's very dirty. Let's clean it up and read the poem together." So they washed the paper and read the poem. It was about a happy dog playing in the mud. Tim and his mom laughed and enjoyed the poem. They were glad they found it, even if it was filthy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a white dog sitting all alone. The dog looked sad and miserable. Tim wanted to help the dog feel better. Tim walked up to the dog and said, "Hi, dog! Why are you so sad?" The dog looked at Tim and said, "I lost my ball. I can't find it anywhere." Tim wanted to help the dog find his ball. They both started to look for the ball. Tim watched the white dog sniff around the park. After a while, the dog found his ball under a bush. The dog was so happy! He wagged his tail and said, "Thank you, Tim! You helped me find my ball!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, dog! Let's play together!" And they played in the park all day long.

Lily liked to play with bugs. She liked to catch them and look at them. She liked their colors and shapes. She liked how they moved and made noises. She had a jar with holes for her bugs. One day, she found a pretty cricket. It was green and shiny. It had long legs and wings. It made a loud chirp. Lily wanted to keep it. She put it in her jar with some grass and water. But the cricket did not like the jar. It wanted to be free. It wanted to hop and fly. It wanted to find other crickets. It tried to get out. It pushed and kicked. It bit and scratched. It made a lot of noise. Lily did not like the noise. She wanted the cricket to be quiet. She wanted it to be happy. She thought it was hungry. She gave it some bread and cheese. But the cricket did not want bread and cheese. It wanted grass and seeds. It spit out the food. It made more noise. Lily got angry. She grabbed the jar. She shook it hard. She yelled at the cricket. She said, "Be quiet! Be good! Or I will hurt you!" But the cricket did not listen. It kept chirping and fighting. It did not care about Lily. Lily got a sharp stick. She opened the jar. She poked the cricket. She wanted to make it stop. But the cricket did not stop. It jumped at Lily. It bit her finger. It made her bleed. It made her cry. Lily dropped the jar. She ran away. She left the cricket. The cricket was free. It hopped and flew. It found other crickets. It was happy. But Lily was not happy. She was hurt and sad. She lost her pretty cricket. She learned a hard lesson.
