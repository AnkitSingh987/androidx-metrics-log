Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Lily and the Book

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, they go to Lily's house. Lily has a big window in her room. She can see many things from the window. "Look, Tom, I can see the park from here. Do you see the slide and the swings?" Lily says. "Yes, I see them. They are fun. Can we go there later?" Tom says. "Maybe. But first, I want to teach you something. Do you know what this is?" Lily points to a colorful book on her bed. "No, what is it?" Tom asks. "It is a book of animals. It has pictures and words. I can read some of the words. Do you want to learn?" Lily says. "Sure, I want to learn. How do you read?" Tom says. Lily picks up the book and opens it. She shows Tom a page with a picture of a lion. She points to the word under the picture. "This is lion. It says L-I-O-N. Lion. Can you say it?" Lily says. "Lion. L-I-O-N. Lion." Tom repeats. "Good. You are smart. Now, let me show you another one." Lily turns the page and shows Tom a picture of a giraffe. She points to the word under the picture. "This is giraffe. It says G-I-R-A-F-F-E. Giraffe. Can you say it?" Lily says. "Giraffe. G-I-R-A-F-F-E. Giraffe." Tom repeats. "Very good. You are learning fast. Do you like the book?" Lily says. "Yes, I like it. It is impressive. It has many animals. Can you teach me more?" Tom says. "Of course. I like to teach you. You are a good friend. Come, let's read more." Lily says. They sit on the bed and read the book together. They learn many new words and animals. They have fun and laugh. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a proud bird named Blue. Blue loved to sit on the vine near his nest. He liked to sing his songs all day long. One day, a sneaky cat saw Blue on the vine. The cat wanted to steal Blue's nest. The cat slowly moved closer and closer to the nest. But, something unexpected happened! When the cat tried to steal the nest, it fell off the vine. The cat was so scared, it ran away very fast. Blue saw what happened and was very happy. He knew that the cat would not try to steal his nest again. Blue was safe and could keep singing his songs on the vine.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, a purple hat, and a purple bag. One day, Amy's mom told her they were going on a trip. Amy was very excited! Amy and her mom started to prepare for the trip. They packed clothes, toys, and food in a big purple bag. Amy said, "Mom, can I bring my purple teddy bear?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Amy! Let's put it in the bag." On the day of the trip, Amy and her mom got in the car. They drove and drove until they reached a big park. The park had green grass, tall trees, and a blue sky. Amy and her mom had a fun day playing, eating, and laughing. At the end of the day, they went home and Amy hugged her purple teddy bear. She was happy she had her bear with her on the trip.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lily wanted to make a yummy drink. She went to the kitchen and saw a big jug of juice. She decided to pour the juice into a cup. As she poured, she noticed the juice was a pretty red color. Lily's mom came into the kitchen and saw what Lily was doing. She smiled and said, "Good job, Lily! You are making your own drink. What a big girl!" Lily felt happy and proud. She knew she was doing a good thing. Lily took her cup of juice to the table. She sat down and took a sip. It was so yummy! She looked at her mom and said, "Mom, I did it! I made my own drink!" Her mom smiled and gave her a hug. "Yes, you did, Lily. You followed the order all by yourself. I am so proud of you!"
