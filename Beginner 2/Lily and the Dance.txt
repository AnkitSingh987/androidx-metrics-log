Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Dance

Lily loved to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, she danced at night. She danced with her dolls, she danced with her dog, she danced with her mom and dad. She danced when she was happy, she danced when she was sad, she danced when she was bored. One day, Lily saw a new boy in her class. His name was Sam. He had curly hair and big eyes. He was shy and quiet. He did not dance. He sat in a corner and read a book. Lily wanted to dance with him. She thought he looked lonely and sad. She walked up to him and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Do you want to dance with me?" Sam looked up and shook his head. He said, "No, thank you. I do not like to dance. I like to read." Lily felt sad. She liked to read, too, but she liked to dance more. She said, "Please, can you dance with me? Just for a little bit. It is fun. I will be gentle. I will not pull you or push you. I will just hold your hand and spin around." Sam looked at her and saw that she was smiling and kind. He felt curious and brave. He said, "Okay, I will try. But only for a little bit." Lily was happy. She took his hand and led him to the middle of the room. She showed him how to dance. She moved her feet and swung her arms. She twirled and jumped and clapped. She was gentle and careful. She did not let go of his hand. She said, "See, it is fun. You are doing great. You are a good dancer." Sam felt surprised and happy. He liked to dance. He liked to move and laugh and play. He liked to hold Lily's hand. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good dancer, too. You are a good friend." They danced and danced until the bell rang. They hugged and smiled. They went back to their seats. They said, "See you tomorrow. Let's dance again." They were happy. They were friends. They loved to dance.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat. This cat was very good at finding treasure. One day, the cat went to the park to play. While playing, the cat saw a big box. The cat thought, "This box might have treasure!" The cat was not allowed to open boxes without asking. But, the cat really wanted to see what was inside. The cat opened the box, and something unexpected happened. Instead of treasure, out came many colorful balloons! The balloons flew up into the sky, making everyone in the park smile. The famous cat learned that sometimes, surprises can be better than treasure.

Lily and Tom are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a doll and Tom has a car. They are having fun, but they also want to try each other's toys. "Can I play with your car, Tom?" Lily asks. "OK, but only for a little while. Then you have to return it to me," Tom says. Lily nods and takes the car. She makes it zoom on the floor. Tom takes the doll and hugs it. He likes how soft it is. After a few minutes, Lily wants her doll back. She sees that Tom has put a hat on it. She does not like that. "Tom, give me back my doll. You put a hat on her. She does not like hats," Lily says. "But I like hats. They are cool. And your doll looks pretty with a hat," Tom says. "No, she does not. She looks silly. And the hat is too tight for her. It hurts her head. Give her back to me now," Lily says. Tom and Lily start to argue. They pull the doll from each other. They are both angry and sad. Mom hears them and comes to the living room. She sees them fighting over the doll. "What is going on here? Why are you not sharing your toys?" Mom asks. "Tom put a hat on my doll. He does not want to return her to me," Lily says. "Lily took my car. She does not want to return it to me," Tom says. Mom sighs. She has an idea. She takes the hat from the doll and the car from Lily. She gives the doll to Lily and the car to Tom. "Here, you can have your own toys back. But you have to learn to share and respect each other's toys. How about you take turns playing with them? You can swap them every five minutes. That way, you can both have fun and be fair," Mom says. Lily and Tom think about Mom's idea. They agree that it is a good idea. They say sorry to each other and hug. They decide to play with their toys together. They are happy again. Mom smiles and leaves them to play.
