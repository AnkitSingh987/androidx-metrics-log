Category: Beginner
Title: Spot and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a big room, there was a big, soft armchair. A loyal dog named Spot lived in the room with the armchair. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot could not find his ball. He felt sad and started to search for it. Spot looked under the armchair and said, "Ball, are you there?" But the ball was not there. Spot looked behind the big door and asked, "Ball, are you here?" But the ball was not there either. Spot felt very sad and sat down by the armchair. Just then, a little mouse came out from under the armchair. The mouse had Spot's ball! The mouse said, "I found your ball, Spot! I wanted to play too." Spot was so happy and surprised. Spot and the little mouse played with the ball together and became best friends.

Once there was a little bear named Bob. He loved to explore and one day he came across a big pipe. He walked up and saw that it was open. He said "hello, who is here?". Suddenly, a little rabbit popped out from the pipe and said "Hi, I'm Zoom. I like to zip in and out of this pipe". Bob was very excited and said "Can I come too?". Zoom said "Sure, just stay close to me and don't wander around". So Bob followed Zoom into the pipe. They zipped in and out of the pipe, here and there, up and down. Soon, Bob was out of the pipe and said "That was so much fun". Zoom was happy too. From then on Bob and Zoom were best friends and they zipped around the pipe together, discovering all sorts of new things everyday.

Lily and Tom are on a big boat with their mom and dad. They like to look at the water and the fish. The boat is going to a port, where there are many other boats and people. "Look, Lily, there is the port!" Tom says, pointing at the land. "We will get off the boat soon and see new things." "I want to see the port, too!" Lily says, standing on her toes. She tries to see over the bow of the boat, but it is too high. She asks her dad to lift her up. "Be careful, Lily, don't lean too far," her dad says, holding her in his arms. He shows her the port, where there are many colors and shapes. "Do you see the big tower? That is where the captain talks to the other boats. And do you see the flags? They say hello to the boats." "Hello, flags!" Lily says, waving her hand. She sees some people on the dock, waiting for the boat. They are wearing hats and coats, because it is cold and dry. "Hello, people!" The people see Lily and smile. They wave back and say hello. One of them has a big bow in her hair. She is Lily's aunt, who came to meet them. "Look, Tom, there is Aunt Anna!" Lily says, pointing at the woman with the bow. "She has a present for us!" Tom sees Aunt Anna and gets excited. He likes presents. He runs to the edge of the boat and shouts, "Aunt Anna, Aunt Anna, we are here!" Aunt Anna hears Tom and laughs. She holds up a big box wrapped in paper. "Hello, Tom, hello, Lily! I have a present for you! Can you guess what it is?" Tom and Lily shake their heads. They don't know what it is. They want to open it and see. "Come on, let's go!" Tom says, pulling his mom's hand. "I want to see the present!" "Me too, me too!" Lily says, jumping in her dad's arms. "Let's go, let's go!" Their mom and dad smile and follow them. They get off the boat and hug Aunt Anna. She gives them the present and says, "This is for you, Tom and Lily. I hope you like it. It is something you can play with on the boat or on the land." Tom and Lily thank Aunt Anna and tear off the paper. Inside the box, they find two toy boats, one blue and one red. They are happy and surprised. They hug Aunt Anna again and say, "Thank you, thank you, Aunt Anna! We love the boats!" "You're welcome, you're welcome, Tom and Lily!" Aunt Anna says, smiling. "Now, let's go and have some fun!"
