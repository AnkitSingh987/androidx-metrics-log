Category: Beginner
Title: The Green Room and the Grass

Once upon a time, in a small school, there was a green room. In this green room, there was a class of little kids. They liked to play, laugh, and learn new things. Their teacher, Miss Lily, was very kind and loved to teach them. One day, Miss Lily asked the class a question. She said, "What color is the grass outside?" The kids thought for a moment and then shouted, "Green!" Miss Lily smiled and said, "Good job, everyone!" The next day, the class went outside to play. They saw the green grass and remembered Miss Lily's question. They were happy that they knew the answer. They played all day on the green grass, and when they went back inside, they told Miss Lily how much fun they had.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a long tape. Tim liked to play with the tape. One day, Tim saw a disgusting bug. The bug was big and green. Tim was scared of the bug. Tim had an idea. He took the tape and said, "I will lead the bug away with my tape." Tim put the tape on the ground. The bug saw the tape and started to follow it. Tim was happy that his plan was working. As Tim led the bug away, his friend Sally came. Sally saw the bug and said, "Ew, that bug is disgusting!" Tim told Sally, "Don't worry, I am leading it away with my tape." Sally was happy that Tim was brave. They both led the bug far away, and it never came back. Tim and Sally were happy, and they played with the tape all day long.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there was a big tree. The tree had many leaves that would dance when the wind blew. The tree was friends with all the children in the town. One day, a new season came. The leaves on the tree changed to pretty colors. The children loved to watch the leaves dance and play. They would laugh and clap their hands when the leaves danced in the wind. As the season went on, the leaves fell to the ground. The children would jump and dance in the piles of leaves. The tree and the children were very happy. Soon, the season changed again, and the tree grew new leaves. The children knew they could always come back to their friend, the tree, to dance and play together.

One day, a girl named Mia went on a tour with her mom and dad. They went to a big park. Mia was very proud because she wore her new shoes. In the park, they saw many things. Mia saw a big tree and said, "Look, mom! The tree is so big!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia. It is a very big tree." They walked around the park and saw a pond with ducks. Mia's dad saw the ducks and said, "Look, Mia! The ducks are swimming in the pond." Mia was happy to see the ducks. She clapped her hands and laughed. At the end of the tour, Mia and her parents took a picture near the big tree. They all looked very proud and happy. Mia loved her fun day at the park with her mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword that he liked to play with. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his sword. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a noisy toy drum. Tim asked Sue, "Can I play with your drum?" Sue said, "Yes, if I can play with your sword." Tim and Sue played together. They made a lot of noise. Tim hit the drum and Sue swung the sword. Suddenly, a big dog came running. The dog was scared of the noise. The dog started to scream. Tim and Sue stopped playing. They looked at the dog and said, "Sorry, dog. We didn't mean to scare you." The dog stopped screaming and wagged its tail. Tim and Sue learned to play more quietly, and they all became friends.
