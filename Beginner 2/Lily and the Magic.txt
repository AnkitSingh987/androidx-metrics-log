Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Magic

Lily was in her bed, but she could not sleep. She wanted to see a fairy. She heard stories about fairies from her mom and dad. They said fairies were small and pretty and had wings. They said fairies could do magic and make wishes come true. Lily got out of her bed and went to the window. She looked at the moon and the stars. She whispered, "Please, fairy, come to me. I want to be your friend." She heard a soft sound, like a bell. She saw a bright light, like a spark. She smiled and opened the window. A fairy flew in. She was small and pretty and had wings. She had a dress made of petals and a crown made of jewels. She had a wand in her hand. "Hello, Lily," the fairy said. "I heard your wish. I came to be your friend." Lily was very happy. She reached out her hand to touch the fairy. But the fairy moved away. She looked angry. "No, no, no," the fairy said. "You can't touch me. You are too big and rough. You will hurt me. You will break my wings. You will take my magic. You can only look at me. You can only talk to me." Lily felt sad. She did not want to hurt the fairy. She did not want to take her magic. She only wanted to be her friend. "Please, fairy, don't be angry," Lily said. "I'm sorry. I won't touch you. I won't hurt you. I just want to hug you. I just want to play with you. Can we be friends?" The fairy looked at Lily. She saw her big eyes and her sweet smile. She felt sorry for her. She decided to give her a chance. "Okay, Lily," the fairy said. "We can be friends. But you have to be very gentle and careful. You have to listen to me and do what I say. You have to promise me." Lily nodded. She promised the fairy. She was very gentle and careful. She listened to her and did what she said. They talked and laughed and played. They had fun. They became friends.

One day, a team of kids went to play in the park. They liked to skip and jump together. They were very happy. In the park, they saw a big, mysterious box. They did not know what was in it. They wanted to find out. They opened the box and found a lot of toys. They played with the toys and skipped around the park. The mysterious box made their day very fun.

Once upon a time, there was a modest farm with a big red barn. The farmer had a cow, a pig, and a chicken. The farmer loved to raise his animals and play with them every day. They were all very happy on the farm. One day, the farmer made a maze with hay bales in the field. The cow, the pig, and the chicken wanted to play in the maze. They went in and started to walk around. They turned left and right and sometimes went back. It was so much fun! After a while, the cow, the pig, and the chicken found their way out of the maze. They were very proud of themselves. The farmer clapped and gave them a big hug. They all went back to the big red barn, feeling happy and tired. They knew they could always have fun on their modest farm.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, Buddy saw a sign that said, "Hotel for Dogs." Buddy went to the hotel and met a small white dog named Daisy. Daisy said, "Hi, I am Daisy. Do you want to play with me?" Buddy said, "Yes, I do!" Buddy and Daisy played all day long in the hotel. They ran, jumped, and had so much fun. At the end of the day, they were both very tired. Buddy said, "I love this hotel. Let's come back tomorrow!" Daisy agreed, and they both went home to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a toy that he loved to chew. Max's owner, a man named Tom, had a new suit for a special day. One day, Max saw Tom's suit hanging on a chair. Max was troubled. He thought the suit was a new toy for him to chew. So, Max took the suit in his mouth and started to chew it. Tom saw Max chewing his suit and said, "No, Max! That is not a toy!" Max stopped chewing and looked sad. Tom was not mad at Max. He said, "It's okay, Max. We will find you a new toy to chew." Max wagged his tail, happy to have a new toy soon.
