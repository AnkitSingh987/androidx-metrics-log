Category: Beginner
Title: Billy the Bird and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy liked to fly high in the sky. One day, Billy saw a big wet puddle on the ground. He thought it would be fun to play in the wet puddle. Billy flew down to the wet puddle and started to splash around. He got very messy from the water and mud. His feathers were all wet and dirty. Billy thought it was a lot of fun to be messy and play in the wet puddle. After playing in the puddle, Billy wanted to fly again. But his wings were too wet and messy. So, he shook his wings and the wet, messy mud fell off. Now, Billy could fly again. He flew up in the sky and looked down at the wet puddle. He knew he would come back to play again another day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a print on the ground. It was a fine print of a big cat. Sue liked the print a lot and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Sue ran to Tom's house and showed him the fine print. Tom liked it too, but it made him cry. Sue asked Tom why he was crying. Tom said that the big cat in the print reminded him of his lost pet cat. Sue wanted to help Tom feel better. So, they went to look for Tom's lost cat together. They looked and looked until they found the cat hiding in a bush. Tom was so happy that he stopped crying. Sue, Tom, and the cat went back home with the fine print, feeling very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to watch TV with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They watched TV every day after Tim came home from school. One day, while they were watching TV, something unexpected happened. The TV showed a big gray bird that could talk. The bird said, "I believe I can fly!" Tim and Kitty were very surprised. Tim and Kitty wanted to see if the bird was real, so they went outside. There, they saw the big gray bird! The bird looked at them and said, "I believe you can fly too!" Tim and Kitty didn't think they could fly, but they tried anyway. They jumped up and down, and suddenly they were flying! They flew in the sky with the big gray bird, and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little dog named Spot. He lived in a small house with his best friend, a boy named Tim. They liked to play outside and have fun together. One day, the sky turned dark and the wind began to blow. Spot and Tim heard a loud noise. It was thunder! They ran back to their small house to be safe from the storm. Inside the house, Tim was scared of the thunder. But Spot was there to help. He put his paw on Tim's hand and stayed close. Together, they turned on the lights and played games until the storm went away. And they were not scared anymore.

Anna and Ben are best friends. They like to play with toys, read books and sing songs. Sometimes they have fun, and sometimes they have fights. But they always say sorry and hug each other. One day, Anna and Ben find a big box in the garden. They wonder what is inside. They open the box and see a lot of shiny things. They see rings, necklaces, bracelets and earrings. They are very happy and excited. They want to try them on and pretend to be kings and queens. But then, they hear a loud voice. It is the voice of Mr. Jones, the owner of the box. He is very angry and scary. He says, "Who took my box? Who touched my things? Give them back to me now!" Anna and Ben are very scared and sad. They do not want to make Mr. Jones mad. They do not want to lose the shiny things. They do not know what to do. They look at each other and try to think. Anna is very intelligent. She remembers what her mom told her. She says, "Ben, we have to tell the truth. The truth is good. The truth is right. The truth will make us feel better. Come on, let's go and talk to Mr. Jones." Ben is very brave. He trusts Anna. He says, "OK, Anna, you are right. Let's go and tell the truth. Maybe Mr. Jones will be nice to us. Maybe he will let us keep some shiny things." They hold hands and walk to Mr. Jones. They say, "Mr. Jones, we are sorry. We took your box. We touched your things. We did not mean to hurt you. We just wanted to have fun. Please do not be mad at us. Please forgive us." Mr. Jones is surprised. He sees the shiny things on Anna and Ben. He sees their scared and sad faces. He sees their honest and brave hearts. He feels sorry for them. He says, "Anna and Ben, thank you for telling me the truth. The truth is good. The truth is right. The truth makes me feel better. I am not mad at you. I forgive you. You can keep some shiny things. But you have to promise me to never take things that are not yours. And you have to share them with your friends. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben are very happy and relieved. They say, "Yes, Mr. Jones, we understand. We promise to never take things that are not ours. And we promise to share them with our friends. Thank you for being nice to us. Thank you for letting us keep some shiny things." They hug Mr. Jones and run back to their toys. They put on the shiny things and play with them. They relax and have fun. They are best friends. They are happy.
