Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Crab

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the big water come and go. Tim asked, "Mom, what is that?" His mom said, "That is the tide, Tim." Tim saw a small, fragile shell on the sand. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a pretty shell!" His mom smiled and said, "Be careful, Tim. It can break easy." A funny crab walked by. Tim asked the crab, "Do you like the tide?" The crab just gave a little shrug and walked away. Tim laughed and played with the crab and the tide all day.

---

One day, a boy named Tim saw a cat. The cat was quiet and sad. Tim wanted to make the cat smile. He said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at Tim and said nothing. Tim was sad too, but he did not give up. Tim thought, "I will wait and try again." He went away and came back with a ball. He said, "Look, cat! I have a ball. Let's play!" The cat looked at the ball and then at Tim. The cat started to smile. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took the ball away. The cat was scared and ran away. Tim was very sad. The moral of the story is to be careful when trying to help others, because sometimes things can go wrong.

Once there was a raven. It was a purple raven and it was very lonely. Every day it would fly around the sky looking for a friend. One day the raven asked the clouds if they wanted to be friends, but they blew away. Then it asked the sun, but the sun was too hot. Then it asked the trees, but they couldn't talk. The raven kept asking, but no one wanted to be friends. It kept flying until it could fly no more. The raven fell and died. The end.

Anna and Ben love to go to the park with Mom. They like to see the ducks and the swans in the pond. The swans are big and white and have long necks. They make a funny sound like "honk honk". One day, Anna and Ben see a new swan in the pond. It is not white, but black and red. It has a shiny crown on its head. It looks very brilliant. Anna and Ben want to get closer to see it better. They run to the edge of the pond and call to the swan. "Hello, swan! You are so pretty! Can we be your friends?" The swan hears them and swims to them. It looks at them with its bright eyes. But the swan is not friendly. It starts to flap its wings and hiss at them. It snaps its beak at them. It tries to bite them. Anna and Ben are scared. They run away from the pond and cry. Mom hears them and comes to them. She hugs them and tells them to calm down. She explains that the swan is not a real swan, but a toy swan. Someone put it in the pond as a joke. It is not nice to trick people like that. Anna and Ben feel better. They say sorry to the real swans for bothering them. They decide to play with the ducks instead. The ducks are friendly and quack at them. They feed them some bread and laugh. They have fun in the park.

One morning, Max was outside in the garden. He wanted to go explore the big hill he could see in the distance. So, he started walking. Max got tired after walking a while, but he kept going until he reached the hill. He was happy to finally be there and was excited to explore the top. As he got closer to the top, Max started to hear voices. He saw a group of people at the top, walking around and being busy. He asked nicely if he could join them and the people didn't accept his request. They turned him away and asked him to go back home. Max was disappointed and sad that he was not allowed to explore the hill. He slowly walked away. Even though he was sad and tired, he knew he would find an other adventure one day.
