Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a smooth, round ball. One day, he found a big wrap. He wanted to play a game with the wrap and his ball. Tim put the ball on the wrap. He said, "I will wrap my ball like a gift." He wrapped the ball with the big wrap. The ball looked like a gift now. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the wrapped ball and asked, "What is that?" Tim said, "It is my ball. I wrapped it like a gift." Sam laughed and said, "That is fun!" They played with the wrapped ball all day long.

The sun was shining bright on the desert. Mama loved to take long walks in the desert. One day, she decided to take her little boy. He was so excited! He skipped beside Mama, humming and whistling in joy. Mama stopped and pointed to a small green spot on the horizon. "Look, do you see that? That's an oasis. We should go there and have fun." The little boy nodded obediently and followed his mama. As they got closer and closer, the little boy started to hear a strange sound. He asked Mama what it was. Mama smiled. "That's the sound of the whistling winds of the oasis. It's a magical place!" The little boy was so excited. He couldn't wait to explore the oasis. When they finally arrived, he saw that Mama was right. The oasis was beautiful. Trees, birds, and animals were everywhere. They even found a big pond and some cool rocks to climb. The little boy was so happy and so obedient. But the most magical thing was the sound of the whistling winds. Mama and the little boy stayed until the sun went down, listening to the sound of the whistling winds and exploring the wonderful oasis.

Mia and Leo were eager to go to the park. They put on their coats and hats and asked Mom to zip them up. Mom smiled and zipped their coats. She gave them a hug and said, "Have fun, my loves. Be careful and stay together." Mia and Leo ran to the park. They saw a big slide, a swing, a sandbox and a seesaw. They wanted to try everything. They took turns on the slide and the swing. They made a castle in the sandbox. They went up and down on the seesaw. "Look, Leo, a squirrel!" Mia said, pointing to a tree. She saw a furry brown animal with a long tail. It was holding a nut in its mouth. It looked at Mia and Leo with bright eyes. "Hi, squirrel!" Leo said, waving his hand. He wanted to be friends with the squirrel. He walked slowly to the tree. He reached out his hand and said, "Can I have a nut, too?" The squirrel was scared of Leo. It did not want to share its nut. It made a loud noise and ran away. It jumped from one tree to another. It hid behind the leaves. "Bye, squirrel!" Mia said, feeling sad. She saw that Leo was sad, too. She ran to him and gave him a hug. She said, "It's okay, Leo. Maybe the squirrel will come back another day. Let's go play some more." Leo smiled and hugged Mia back. He said, "Okay, Mia. You are a good sister. I love you." They held hands and went back to the slide. They had fun at the park. They were eager to see the squirrel again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. Tim loved to play with Spot in their big, open yard. They would run and jump and have so much fun together. One day, Tim's dad needed to go to the store. He needed to get a new license for his car. Tim and Spot went with him. When they got to the store, Tim's dad said, "Stay here with Spot. I will be right back." Tim and Spot waited outside the store. As they waited, a big wind came and blew the door open. Spot got scared and ran inside the store. Tim started to worry. He did not know what to do. Just then, a nice lady came out of the store with Spot. She said, "Is this your dog?" Tim was so happy to see Spot again. They all went home, and Tim's dad got his new license. From that day on, Tim and Spot always stayed close to each other, so they would never be apart again.
