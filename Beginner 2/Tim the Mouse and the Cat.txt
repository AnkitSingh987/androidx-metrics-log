Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a fireplace. Tim was troubled because he wanted to build a bigger fireplace to keep warm. One day, Tim asked his friend, a big cat named Max, to help him build a bigger fireplace. Max was very strong and could carry many bricks. Together, they started to build the new fireplace. But, they had a problem. Max wanted to build the fireplace in the middle of the room, but Tim wanted to build it by the wall. They both felt troubled and couldn't agree. Tim and Max decided to ask their wise friend, Mr. Owl, for help. Mr. Owl listened to their problem and thought for a moment. Then, he said, "Why not build two smaller fireplaces? One in the middle of the room and one by the wall." Tim and Max liked the idea and started to build two smaller fireplaces. They worked together and soon, they finished building. Tim and Max were both happy and warm in their new fireplaces. They thanked Mr. Owl for his help and learned that working together can solve problems.

Lily and Anna are best friends. They like to play together in the park. One day, they find a big box near the slide. The box is empty, but it has many pictures on it. The pictures show pretty dresses and shoes. "Wow, look at this box!" Lily says. "Do you want to play dress-up?" "Yes, yes!" Anna says. "Let's see what we can find." They open the box and see some old clothes and hats. They are not as pretty as the pictures, but they are still fun. Lily picks a pink dress and a purple hat. Anna picks a blue dress and a yellow hat. "You look so nice!" Lily says to Anna. "So do you!" Anna says to Lily. They giggle and twirl in their dresses. Then they see a big tree near the swings. "Let's race to the tree!" Lily says. "OK, ready, set, go!" Anna says. They run as fast as they can in their dresses. They feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They are happy and free. Lily and Anna reach the tree at the same time. They hug and laugh. "We are both winners!" Lily says. "Yes, we are!" Anna says. They sit under the tree and rest. They look at the sky and the clouds. They see shapes and animals in the clouds. They make up stories and jokes. They are best friends. They love to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a blue ball. The blue ball had a big knot on it. The blue ball was not happy with the knot. It wanted to be round and smooth like the other balls. The blue ball tried, but it could not get rid of the knot. One day, the blue ball saw a little girl. The little girl liked to play with balls. The blue ball wanted the little girl to play with it. But the little girl did not want to play with the blue ball. She said, "I don't like the knot. I want to play with the other balls." The blue ball was very sad. It wanted to be like the other balls. It tried and tried, but it could not get rid of the knot. The blue ball missed being played with by the little girl. The blue ball was still sad and alone.

Tom loves to play football with his friends. He has a big football that is red and white. He can kick it very far and catch it very well. He is smart and knows the rules of the game. One day, Tom and his friends go to the park to play football. They find a nice spot with grass and trees. They make two teams and start to play. Tom is on the red team and he is the captain. He tells his friends where to run and how to pass the ball. The blue team is also good and they try to stop the red team. They run fast and tackle hard. They want to win the game. They have a big boy who is very strong and can kick the ball very high. The game is very fun and exciting. Both teams score many goals and cheer loudly. The score is tied and there is only one minute left. Tom has the ball and he sees a gap in the blue team's defense. He runs towards the goal and gets ready to kick. But then, he sees something that makes him stop. A little girl has entered the park and is walking across the field. She does not see the game and the players. She is holding a balloon and a teddy bear. She is smiling and humming a song. Tom does not want to hurt the girl or make her cry. He drops the ball and runs to her. He says hello and asks her name. She says her name is Lily and she is three years old. She says she likes his football and asks if she can play. Tom smiles and says yes. He picks up the ball and gives it to her. He says she can be on his team and he will teach her how to play. He calls his friends and tells them to stop the game. He says they have a new friend and they can play together. The blue team agrees and they all come to meet Lily. They say hello and welcome her. They say she is very cute and brave. They say they do not mind if the game is over. They say they can play again another day. Tom and Lily hold hands and enter the circle of friends. They all laugh and talk and play. They have fun and forget about the score. They are happy and kind. They are smart and good.
