Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Cup

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a gray cat named Tom. Tom was a nice cat who loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a cup of tea on the table. He was curious and wanted to smell it. Tom jumped on the table and began to rub his face on the cup. Just then, his friend, a little girl named Sue, walked into the room. "Tom, what are you doing?" she asked. Tom looked at her and meowed. Sue said, "You should not touch hot things, Tom. You might get hurt." Tom listened to Sue and jumped off the table. From that day on, Tom never touched hot things again. He learned that it was important to be careful and listen to his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bo. Bo was a troubled bunny because he could not find his hay. He needed the hay to eat and make his bed soft. Bo was very sad. One day, Bo went for a walk to find his hay. He saw a big tree and thought maybe his hay was there. Bo tried to tear the leaves off the tree, but it was not hay. Bo was still sad. Then, Bo saw his friend, the squirrel. The squirrel said, "Bo, I found your hay!" Bo was so happy. He ran to his hay and ate some. Then, Bo made his bed soft with the hay. Bo was not a troubled bunny anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big red ball. She loved to play with her ball in the park. One day, Sue kicked her ball too hard and it went far away. She looked and looked, but she could not find her ball. Sue was very sad. Then, a nice boy named Tim found Sue's ball. He saw that she was sad and wanted to help. Tim offered the ball back to Sue. But when Sue took the ball, she saw that it was hurt. There was a big hole in it. Sue's eyes filled with tears. Tim saw that Sue was still sad. He had an idea. He took out a small thing from his pocket. It was a patch. Tim put the patch on the ball's hole. The ball was fixed! Sue was so happy. She hugged Tim and they played with the ball together. The unexpected thing was that Sue made a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim was very happy. He liked to play at the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! Want to play?" Tim said, "Yes!" They played on the swings, slide, and in the sandbox. They had lots of fun. After playing, Tim's mom said, "Let's go to the museum now." Tim and Sue went to the museum with their moms. They saw big animals and pretty pictures. They learned new things and had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a village. In the village lived an old grandmother and her three grandkids. The grandkids liked to bake and every day after school, they would make cakes and fold them into different shapes. One day, the kids decided to bake a sour cake. They got a bowl and put in some flour, sugar and eggs. Then they took some sour fruits, added them to the mixture and stirred them. They took the bowl out, put it into the oven and waited. When the cake was ready, they took it out and examined it. It smelt sour but it also looked delicious. The grandmother said, "Hmm, this cake smells sour, but it might still taste good. Let's cut it and find out!" They took out their knives and cut the cake into three slices. The kids took a bite of their pieces and their eyes lit up. The grandmother laughed. "See, I told you it would still taste good even if it smelled sour," she said. The kids smiled and eagerly continued eating the delicious sour cake.
