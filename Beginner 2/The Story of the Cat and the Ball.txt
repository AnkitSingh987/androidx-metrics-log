Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little cat with a long tail. The cat loved to play with its tail all day. One day, the cat saw a big, red ball high up on a tree. The cat wanted to reach the ball and play with it. The little cat tried and tried to reach the ball. It jumped and climbed, but the ball was too high. Then, a fierce dog came by and saw the cat. The dog wanted to help the cat get the ball. The dog stood on its back legs and let the cat climb on its head. With the dog's help, the cat could reach the ball. The cat and the dog became friends and played with the ball all day long. They were very happy to have each other.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to talk a lot. One day, he found a big, shiny gem in his backyard. Tim was very happy. He ran to show his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" said Tim. His mom was nervous. "Tim, I think this gem is magic," she said. Tim did not know what to do. He wanted to play with the gem. That night, Tim put the gem under his pillow. When he went to sleep, something unexpected happened. The gem began to talk! "Hello, Tim!" said the gem. Tim was scared at first, but then he started to talk to the gem. They talked all night and became good friends. The gem told Tim many fun stories. Tim was not nervous anymore. He was happy to have a magic talking gem as a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The dog loved to play with a ball. One day, the dog saw a boy with pants. The boy had a zip on his pants. The dog wanted to play with the zip. So, the dog jumped up and down near the boy. The boy saw the dog and smiled. He let the dog play with the zip. They both laughed and played together. The dog and the boy became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in his yard one sunny day. He wanted to find a new friend to play with. As he was looking around, he saw a small, smooth rock on the ground. Tim picked up the rock and put it in his pocket. Then, Tim saw a little puppy on the other side of the yard. He was so happy to find a new friend. The puppy was cute and had smooth fur. Tim and the puppy played all day. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun together. As the sun started to go down, Tim knew he had to go home. He said goodbye to the puppy and walked away. But, the puppy followed Tim. Tim's mom saw the puppy and told Tim they could not keep it. Tim was sad and had to say goodbye to his new friend. The puppy went away, and Tim was left with his smooth rock in his pocket.

Once upon a time, there were two little lambs playing in a sunny meadow. The lambs were best friends and always played together every day. One day, one lamb accidentally stepped on the other's wool. The lamb that had been stepped on shouted, "That is not nice! You hurt me!" The other lamb was very sorry and looked down at his feet. He said, "I am so sorry. Please forgive me. I will never do it again." The lamb that had been hurt said, "I forgive you. Let's find some tasty flowers to eat together and it will be like nothing ever happened." The two lambs smiled and ran off together to find some tasty flowers to munch on. The lambs were best friends for many years after that and never stepped on each other's wool again.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her dog, Max, went for a walk. The sky was full of dark clouds. Sue saw a big puddle and said, "Max, we will have fun in the wet puddle!" Max wagged his tail, feeling excited. They splashed in the wet puddle and laughed. Then, Sue saw something in the water. She picked it up and saw that it was a wet print of a cat. "Wow, a cat print! Let's go find the cat, Max!" Sue said. Max barked, and they started to look for the cat. Sue and Max followed the wet prints, and soon they found a small, wet cat hiding under a tree. "Hello, cat! We found your prints and wanted to meet you!" Sue said. The cat meowed and came out from under the tree. Sue, Max, and the cat played together all day, and they became good friends.
