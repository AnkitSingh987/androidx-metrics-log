Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and Ben and the Park

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Today is a hot day. The sun is bright and the air is warm. Anna and Ben walk to the park with their moms. They see a big slide, a swing, and a sandbox. They run to the slide and take turns sliding down. Whee! They have fun. But soon they feel hot and thirsty. They want some water. They walk to the fountain and drink some water. Ahh! They feel better. Then they see a dog. The dog is brown and fluffy. He has a collar and a leash. His name is Spot. Spot's mom says they can pet him. Anna and Ben like Spot. They stroke his fur and scratch his ears. Spot wags his tail and licks their faces. He is a friendly dog. Anna and Ben are happy. They have a good day at the park. They have a slide, a swing, a sandbox, and a new friend. They walk back home with their moms and Spot. They say goodbye and hug. They are tired but they smile. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play outside. He was an independent dog who liked to do things on his own. One day, Spot's owner, Sam, put a collar on him. Sam wanted to lead Spot with a leash. "Come, Spot," said Sam, holding the leash. "Let's go for a walk." Spot looked at Sam and wagged his tail. They walked together, but Spot still wanted to be free. As they walked, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew and the leash came off Spot's collar! Spot was so happy to run and play without the leash. "Spot, come back!" Sam called. But Spot was having too much fun. He ran and played until he got tired. Then, Spot walked back to Sam. Sam smiled and said, "You had fun, but we need to use the collar and leash to be safe." Spot understood and let Sam lead him home. From then on, they walked together, but Sam let Spot have some independent playtime too.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful fairy named Lily. She lived in a big tree in the forest. Lily was a very kind fairy who always wanted to help her friends. One day, Lily saw a small bird who could not fly. The bird was sad and wanted to be with its family in the sky. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she used her magic to make the bird's wings strong. The bird was happy and flew away to its family. All the animals in the forest saw what Lily did and they loved her. They learned that being thoughtful and helping others is a good thing to do. From that day on, all the animals in the forest were kind and helpful to each other, just like Lily the fairy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park to enjoy the sun. He played with his ball and ran around with his friends. They all had fun and laughed a lot. But then, Tim fell down and hurt his leg. It felt very bad. His friends got scared and called for help. Tim's mom came running and saw that Tim needed help. An ambulance came to the park. The people inside the ambulance were nice and helped Tim. They put a bandage on his leg to make it feel better. Tim said "thank you" and went home with his mom. Now, Tim is careful when he plays so he doesn't get hurt again.

Tim and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big bulb. The bulb is modern. It can change colors and make sounds. One day, Tim and Lily are playing with the bulb. They pretend it is a spaceship. They make noises like "zoom" and "beep". They have fun. But then, Tim feels thirsty. He wants to drink some water. He says to Lily, "Lily, I am thirsty. Can we go and drink some water?" Lily says, "No, Tim. I want to play more. The bulb is fun. You can drink later." Tim says, "But I am very thirsty. Please, Lily. Let us go and drink some water. Then we can play more." Lily says, "Fine, Tim. But hurry up. I will wait for you here with the bulb." Tim and Lily go out of their room. They go to the kitchen. They find a cup and a bottle of water. Tim fills the cup with water. He drinks it. He feels better. He says to Lily, "Thank you, Lily. That was good. Now let us go back and play with the bulb." Lily says, "OK, Tim. But wait. I am thirsty too. Can I drink some water?" Tim says, "Sure, Lily. Here is the cup. You can drink some water." Lily takes the cup. She drinks some water. She feels better too. She says to Tim, "Thank you, Tim. That was good. Now let us go back and play with the bulb." Tim and Lily go back to their room. They find the bulb. It is still changing colors and making sounds. They smile. They pick up the bulb. They play with it again. They are happy.
