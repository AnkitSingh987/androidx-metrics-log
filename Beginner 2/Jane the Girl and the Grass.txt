Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Grass

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. Jane loved to throw all sorts of things, but mostly she loved to throw balls. One day, Jane wanted to throw something different. She went to the store to find something new. She looked around and saw something special -- beef! Jane was so excited that she ran over and grabbed it. Back home she went, ready to throw. But when Jane looked closely, she noticed something. The beef was not like the balls she usually threw. It was troubled. Jane was worried and thought she should not throw it. But then she remembered how much fun it had been to throw all the other things. So, Jane took a deep breath and threw the beef. It flew through the air and landed with a thump in the grass. Jane laughed and ran to pick it up. The beef had not been troubled at all. Jane was so happy that she threw it over and over again. It became her favorite thing to throw and she did so every day!

One day, a man wanted to send a message, but he didn't know how. He decided to find a bird to help. The man saw an ugly bird nearby and said, "Hello, bird. Will you help me?" The bird chirped and nodded its head. The man quickly wrote a message and tied it to the bird's foot. He said, "Go, bird! Send the message for me!" The bird flew away and soon, it was gone. The man waved goodbye and hoped the bird would send the message. In the end, the ugly bird did the job and the man was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer. The dancer liked to dance all day. The dancer was very good at dancing. The dancer had a friend. The friend was a big dog. The dog was very obedient. The dog liked to watch the dancer dance. One day, the dancer and the dog went to the park. They saw a big ball. The dancer wanted to play with the ball. The dog wanted to play too. The dancer kicked the ball. The dog ran after the ball. The dog was happy. The ball went into a tree. The dancer tried to get the ball. The dancer jumped and jumped. The dancer could not reach the ball. The dog had an idea. The dog slapped the tree with its tail. The ball fell down. The dancer and the dog played with the ball all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice boy. His name was Tom. Tom had a big brain. He liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tom saw a cat. The cat was up in a tree. Tom thought, "I will help the cat." He climbed the tree, but the cat jumped down. Tom was surprised! Tom's friends saw him in the tree. They laughed and said, "Look, Tom is the cat now!" Tom laughed too. He climbed down and played with his friends. They had fun, and the cat did too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, while playing with his cars, he made a big wreck. All his toy cars crashed into each other. Tim was sad. Tim's mom saw the wreck and said, "Oh no! What happened here?" Tim replied, "My cars had a big crash, Mom. I don't know how to fix it." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can twist them apart and make them all better." So, Tim and his mom worked together to twist the toy cars apart. They were successful! All the cars were free and fixed. Tim was happy again. He thanked his mom and said, "You're the best, Mom! Thank you for helping me." From that day on, Tim played more carefully with his toy cars. He didn't want to make another big wreck. And every time he played, he remembered how his mom helped him fix the cars. Tim knew that if he ever needed help, his mom would be there for him. And they all lived happily ever after. The end.
