Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom found a bottle of ink. He did not know what it was but thought it was fun to throw. Tom threw the ink at the wall. The ink made a big mess. The wall was now blue. Tom's mom saw the mess and was not happy. She told Tom to clean the wall. Tom tried to clean the wall, but he was hungry. He went to the kitchen and found a yummy cookie. He ate the cookie and felt better. Then, he went back to clean the wall. After cleaning the wall, Tom played with his ball and not the ink.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their dog, Rex. Rex is big and strong. He can run fast and jump high. He likes to eat meat. One day, Tim and Sam see a big truck outside their house. The truck has a sign that says "Meat for sale". Tim and Sam are curious. They want to buy some meat for Rex. They run to the truck and ask the man, "Can we have some meat for Rex, please?" The man smiles and says, "Sure, kids. How much do you want?" Tim and Sam look at the truck. They see many kinds of meat. There are chickens, cows, pigs, and fish. They all look yummy. Tim says, "We want the biggest and best meat for Rex. He is a good dog. He deserves it." Sam says, "Yes, we want the perfect meat for Rex. He will love it." The man nods and says, "Okay, kids. I have just the thing for you. Here, take this big bone. It has a lot of meat on it. Rex will enjoy it very much." Tim and Sam are happy. They thank the man and take the big bone. They run back to their house. They call Rex and show him the big bone. Rex is very happy. He wags his tail and barks. He takes the big bone and starts to chew it. He likes the meat. It is juicy and tasty. Tim and Sam watch Rex. They smile and say, "Look, Rex is gaining weight. He is becoming bigger and stronger. He is the perfect dog."

Once upon a time, there was a large car. This car loved to go fast. It had a lot of speed. The car was very happy when it could go fast. But, the car wanted to look nice too. So, it asked a man to design a new look for it. The man thought and thought about how to design the car. He wanted to make the car look nice and also help it go fast. He knew the car loved speed. The man drew and drew, and soon he had a plan for the car. The man worked hard to design the car. He painted it with bright colors and added fun stickers. The car looked very nice. The man also made the car go even faster than before. When the car raced down the road, it was so happy with its new design and speed. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a sad little girl named Lucy was walking in the park. She saw a beautiful rainbow in the sky. Lucy felt so miserable that she wanted to touch the rainbow to feel happy again. Lucy met a boy named Tom in the park. "Do you want to touch the rainbow too?" she asked him. Tom nodded his head and said, "Yes, let's touch it together!" They both jumped and tried to touch the rainbow, but they couldn't reach it. They tried again and again, but the rainbow was too high. They felt very sad and started to cry. Lucy and Tom sat on the ground, feeling miserable. They couldn't touch the rainbow, and it made them even more sad. The beautiful rainbow faded away, and the sky became dark and gray.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw a big hill and thought it would be fun to play there. Bobo rolled his ball up the hill. It was hard work, but he knew it would be worth it. When he got to the top, he pushed the ball and watched it roll down. Bobo ran after it, hoping to catch it before it stopped. He felt excited and knew something fun was about to happen. As the ball rolled down the hill, it went faster and faster. Bobo raced after it, but he could not catch it. Just when he thought the ball was lost, it hit a big rock and bounced back. Bobo jumped and caught the ball in his mouth. He had a big victory! With a wag of his tail, he returned the ball to the top of the hill, ready to play again.
