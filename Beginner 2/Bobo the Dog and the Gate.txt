Category: Beginner
Title: Bobo the Dog and the Gate

Once upon a time, there was a shy dog named Bobo. Bobo had a toy hose that he loved to play with. Every day, Bobo would play with his hose in the yard. He would run and jump and have lots of fun. One day, Bobo's friend, a cat named Mimi, came to play with him. Mimi saw the hose and wanted to play with it too. They took turns playing with the hose, and they had a great time. When it was time for Mimi to go home, Bobo did not want her to leave. So, Bobo had an idea. He used his paw to lock the gate so Mimi could not go home. Mimi was sad and wanted to go home. Bobo saw how sad Mimi was and felt bad. He unlocked the gate, and Mimi went home. They both learned that it is important to let friends go home when they need to. And from that day on, they played together with the hose and always had a great time.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to race with their bikes. They race to the park, to the store, to the school. They have fun and laugh. One day, they have a big test at school. They study hard and learn many things. They want to do well and make their moms and dads happy. The day of the test comes. Tom and Sam go to school with their bikes. They see a big hill near the school. They look at each other and smile. They want to race one more time. They race to the top of the hill. They go fast and feel the wind. They are happy and free. But then, Sam's bike breaks. His tire pops and he falls. He hurts his knee and cries. Tom hears Sam cry. He stops and turns around. He sees Sam on the ground. He feels sorry and worried. He forgets about the race and the test. He goes back to help Sam. He gives him a hug and a band-aid. He says, "Don't worry, Sam. I am your friend. I will stay with you. We can take the test later. You are more important than the race or the test. You are my loyal friend." Sam smiles and says, "Thank you, Tom. You are my loyal friend too. You are kind and brave. You are the best racer and the best friend. I am lucky to have you." They hug and walk to the school. They tell the teacher what happened. The teacher is proud of them. She says, "You are good boys. You are loyal friends. You can take the test tomorrow. You will do well. I am sure of it." Tom and Sam smile and say, "Thank you, teacher. We are ready for the test. But we are more ready for the next race. We love to race. We love to be friends."

Mumma was very excited. She opened the big box and started to unpack it quickly. She took out a big card and handed it to me. It was bright and colourful with animals on it. Mumma showed me how to use it. She showed me the slot to put the card in. Then she pushed a button, and the machine made a whirring noise. The card came out very fast. Mumma was happy and gave me lots of kisses. I was so excited! We both hugged each other. It was a magical day. I had lots of fun with my new card. We unpacked more boxes the whole afternoon. What a wonderful day!

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the market with her mom. They wanted to buy some food for dinner. Mia saw a big piece of beef and asked her mom, "Can we buy this beef?" Her mom said, "Yes, but we need to find the best one. Let me show you how to pick the right beef." She showed Mia how to look at the color and feel the beef to know if it was good or not. Mia was very happy to learn something new. At home, Mia helped her mom cook the beef. They added some vegetables and a sauce. When it was time to eat, Mia took a bite and said, "This beef is a little bitter, but I like it!" Her mom smiled and said, "Next time, we can try a different sauce to make it even better."
