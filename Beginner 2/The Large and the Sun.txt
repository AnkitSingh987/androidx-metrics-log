Category: Beginner
Title: The Large and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a large, happy family. They had a mom and a dad, and they loved to laugh. They lived in a big house with a big yard. The sun was always shining, and the family was always playing. One day, the mom and dad went away. They said they would be back soon. The kids waited and waited, but they never came back. The kids felt very sad and missed their parents. They wanted to laugh again, but they couldn't. As time went by, the house got old and the yard got messy. The kids tried to be happy, but they couldn't without their parents. They wanted to laugh and play, but they couldn't. The big house was now a sad place, and the kids missed their mom and dad very much.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, his toy car broke. Tim was very sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will replace your toy car with a new one." The next day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They saw many toys, but Tim was looking for a special toy car. Suddenly, he saw a fierce toy lion that roared like an alarm. He thought it was so cool! Tim's mom saw the lion too and smiled. She knew Tim would love it. At home, Tim played with his new fierce toy lion all day. He even forgot about his broken toy car. Tim's mom was happy to see him having fun again. The fierce toy lion that roared like an alarm was the perfect toy to replace his broken car. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Jane. She was a fair girl with a big smile. Jane had a box full of colors to draw. One day, she wanted to draw a picture of the sun. Jane looked at all the colors in the box. There was red, blue, green, and yellow. She had to decide which color to use for the sun. Jane thought about it for a moment. At last, Jane picked the yellow color. She thought it was the best color for the sun. She drew a big, bright sun with the yellow color. Jane was happy with her picture. She showed it to her mom, who loved it too.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a plastic toy on the floor. It was dirty, so she decided to wipe it clean. She took a cloth and began to rub the toy. But the dirt did not come off. Lucy felt frustrated. She tried again and again, but the toy stayed dirty. She called her mom and said, "Mommy, I can't clean this toy. I am sad." Her mom came and looked at the toy. She smiled and said, "Lucy, let's try with soap and water." They went to the sink and washed the toy together. The dirt came off, and the toy was clean. Lucy was happy now. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Mommy." They played with the clean plastic toy for the rest of the day.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. Tim saw a big tree with a bitter fruit on it. He wanted to pick the fruit, but he was too small. Spot wanted to help Tim, so he said, "Trust me, I can get the fruit for you." Tim trusted Spot, and Spot jumped high to get the fruit. But when Spot jumped, he hit his head on a branch. Spot felt sad because his brain hurt, and he could not get the fruit for Tim. Then, a big girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tim and Spot looking sad. Sue asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told her about the bitter fruit and how Spot could not get it. Sue said, "I can help you." She picked the fruit and gave it to Tim. Tim, Spot, and Sue all became friends and played in the park together.
