Category: Beginner
Title: Jim and the Car

One sunny day, Jim was playing in the garden with his car. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise that disturb him. He stopped and looked around, but he could not see where it came from. Jim decided to take a closer look. He got in his car and he drove to the noise. The closer he got, the louder it got. He felt quite dizzy when he heard it right in front of him. It was coming from a big hole in the ground. Jim did not know what the noise was. He was very curious and he wanted to know what was inside the hole. He asked his mum if he could explore it. Mum said: "No, Jim. That noise might be dangerous, we'll have to wait until daddy comes home tonight to find out." Jim waited all day for his daddy to come home. He was so excited to see what was inside the hole. When daddy arrived, all three of them looked inside. They found a big nest full of baby birds that had been disturbed and made the noise that Jim heard earlier. Jim was so proud that he had discovered the secret.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy rock. This rock was not like other rocks. It was a special rock because it was a mineral. It was very pretty and shiny. But, it was not happy. One day, a little girl found the grumpy mineral. She picked it up and said, "I will own you, and you will be my friend." The little girl took the mineral home and put it on her shelf with her other toys. As time went by, the grumpy mineral started to feel happy. It liked being owned by the little girl. It liked being her friend. The little girl and the mineral lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining bright. Tim loved to play in the sand and look for shells. He found a big shell and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found an oyster!" Tim said with a big smile. His mom looked at the oyster and nodded. "Yes, Tim. That's a nice oyster. Be careful, it might be a little hot from the sun." Tim held the oyster carefully and looked at it. He saw a little crab next to it. The crab looked at Tim and said, "Hello, Tim! I am a crab. Can I play with you?" Tim was so happy to have a new friend. Together, Tim, the crab, and the oyster played on the beach. They built sand castles and jumped in the waves. They had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to his new friends and promised to come back soon.

Once upon a time, in a little house, lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to nap all day long. One day, Tom was napping under a big tree when a little bird came and sat on a branch above Tom. "Hi, Tom!" said the little bird. "Why do you nap all day? Don't you want to play?" Tom opened one eye and said, "I like to nap. It's fun for me." The next day, Tom had a tooth that hurt. He could not eat or nap. He felt sad. The little bird saw Tom and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" "My tooth hurts," Tom said with a frown. The little bird felt bad for Tom. "I will help you," said the bird. The bird flew away and came back with a big, soft leaf. The bird put the leaf on Tom's hurt tooth, and soon, Tom felt better. Tom thanked the little bird and they became good friends. From that day on, Tom was not so lazy and played with the little bird when he was not napping.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim saw a high shelf in the kitchen. On the shelf, there was a jar. The jar had yummy cookies inside. Tim wanted to eat the cookies. Tim called his mom and said, "Mom, can I have a cookie from the jar, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is almost time for dinner. You can have a cookie later." But Tim did not want to wait. He tried to ignore what his mom said. When his mom went to another room, Tim got a chair. He stood on the chair to reach the high shelf. But the chair was not high enough. Tim's mom came back and saw what he was doing. She said, "Tim, you must listen to me. You can have a cookie after dinner." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. He got down from the chair and waited for dinner. After dinner, his mom gave him a cookie from the jar. Tim was happy and enjoyed his cookie.
