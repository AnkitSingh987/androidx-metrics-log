Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Roof and the House

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a red roof. Inside the house lived a happy family. The family loved their house with the red roof, but they had a problem. Every time it rained, the roof got wet and water came inside. They wanted to keep the house dry. One day, a man came to the house. He saw the family was sad because of the wet roof. The man knew how to fix the roof. He told the family to sign a paper, and he would make the roof dry again. The family signed the paper, and the man fixed the roof. The next time it rained, the roof stayed dry. The family was very happy. They thanked the man for helping them. Now, they could enjoy their little house with the red roof, rain or shine.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a hat. The hat was big and red. Amy wanted to wear the hat. She thought she would look pretty. But her mom said, "No, don't wear that hat. It is not yours." Amy felt foolish, but she did not worry. She had a plan. When her mom was not looking, she put on the hat. She looked in the mirror and smiled. She thought she looked very pretty. Amy went outside to play. Her friend Tom saw her and said, "Hey, that's my hat!" Amy felt worried. She did not want to give the hat back. But Tom was angry. He took the hat back from Amy. Amy was very sad. She knew she should have listened to her mom. She had lost a friend because she did not listen. That night, Amy went to bed feeling very foolish and sad.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, while hopping, Bob found a hoop. He thought it would be fun to hop through the hoop. Bob asked his friend, Tim the frog, "Do you want to play with the hoop too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's hop through the hoop together!" So, Bob and Tim took turns hopping through the hoop. They had lots of fun. As the sun went down, Bob and Tim were tired but happy. They said goodbye to each other and went to their homes. Bob couldn't wait to play with the hoop again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl named Will. Will lived in a big tree in the woods. He liked to watch the other animals play and have fun. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was sad because he could not speak. The wise owl, Will, saw the sad bird and wanted to help. He had an idea. Will took the little bird under his wing and taught him how to speak. The bird was very happy and thanked Will. Now, the bird could talk to his friends and have more fun. The wise owl, Will, was happy that he could help the little bird.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a big hill and wanted to sit at the top. They thought it would be the perfect spot to see a beautiful view. As they walked up the hill, the cat said, "I can't wait to sit and see the view!" The dog agreed, "Yes, it will be perfect!" They walked and walked until they reached the top of the hill. When they got to the top, they saw a big tree. The tree was so big that it blocked their view! The cat and the dog were sad. But then, they had an idea. They climbed the tree together and found an even better spot to sit. From the tree, they saw the most perfect view they could ever imagine.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make a sandwich. He was very careful. He put bread, cheese, and ham on the table. Tim tried to raise the bread to put it on top. But, the bread started to fly! It went up, up, up in the air. Tim was so surprised. He did not know what to do. Then, a big bird came and took the flying bread. Tim was sad. But, the bird was nice. It gave Tim a new bread. Tim made a new sandwich and said thank you to the bird.
