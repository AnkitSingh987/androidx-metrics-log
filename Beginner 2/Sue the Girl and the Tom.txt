Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tom

One day, a little girl named Sue found a pretty jewel in her garden. It was shiny and had many colors. Sue was very happy and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look at my pretty jewel, Tom!" she said. Tom looked at the jewel and wanted it. He asked Sue, "Can I have it?" Sue said no. Tom was sad, but he had a plan. When Sue was not looking, Tom took the jewel and ran away. He hid it in a dry place, so no one could find it. The next day, Tom went to see the jewel. But it was gone! The jewel had disappeared! Tom was very upset. He looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Now, both Sue and Tom were sad. The pretty jewel was gone forever, and they had lost their friendship too.

One day, a boy went to play in the park. He saw a hidden toy under a big tree. The toy was very pretty and fun to play with. The boy was very happy. Then, a girl came to the park. The boy wanted to introduce the girl to the hidden toy. They played together with the toy and laughed a lot. But when they were playing, the boy hurt his toe. It was very painful and he cried. The girl tried to help, but she couldn't. The boy had to go home with a bad hurt toe.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside in the sun. One day, they see a big hill with many flowers. They want to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. "Let's try to climb the hill," Anna says. "Maybe we can find something fun." "OK, let's go," Ben says. They hold hands and start to walk up the hill. They see yellow, pink, and blue flowers. They smell sweet and nice. They hear birds singing and bees buzzing. They walk and walk, but the hill is very high. They get tired and thirsty. They stop to rest under a big tree. The tree has green leaves and red apples. The shade is cool and comfortable. "Are we there yet?" Ben asks. He looks at the top of the hill. It is still far away. "I don't know," Anna says. She looks at the other side of the hill. She can't see anything. "Maybe we should go back," Ben says. "It is too hard to climb the hill." "But we want to see what is on the other side," Anna says. "Maybe there is a lake or a castle or a rainbow." They look at each other. They don't want to give up, but they also don't want to keep going. "What should we do?" they ask. They think for a while. Then they have an idea. "Let's eat some apples," Anna says. "They will make us strong and happy." "Let's drink some water," Ben says. "They will make us wet and cool." They pick some apples from the tree and find a bottle of water in their backpack. They eat and drink and feel better. They smile and hug. "Let's try again," they say. "We can do it." They stand up and hold hands. They walk up the hill with new energy and hope. They want to see what is on the other side. They want to have fun in the sun. They are friends. They try.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a humble spider. The spider had a cobweb in the corner of the room. Every day, the spider would load her cobweb with food for her little baby spiders. One day, a big wind came and blew the cobweb away. The spider and her babies were very sad. They had no home now. The spider tried to make a new cobweb, but she could not. She was too tired and hungry. Then, a little girl saw the spider and her babies. The little girl felt sad for them. She helped the spider make a new cobweb in her room. The spider and her babies were happy again. They learned that if you are kind and humble, others will help you when you need it. And the little girl learned that even the smallest creatures need love and care.
