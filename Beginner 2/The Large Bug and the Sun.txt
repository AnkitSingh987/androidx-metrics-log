Category: Beginner
Title: The Large Bug and the Sun

One day, a large bug went for a walk. The bug was very big and strong. It liked to play with its friends in the sun. The large bug found a toy. The toy could weigh things. The bug wanted to know how much it weighed. So, it climbed on the toy and waited. The toy said the large bug weighed a lot! The bug was happy to know how big it was. It went back to play with its friends, proud of its size. And they all played together, having fun in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bear who lived in a cave. The bear loved to dance and clap all day long. He had many friends in the forest who liked to dance and clap with him. One day, a little bird flew into the cave and asked the bear if he could join the dance. The bear said yes, and they danced and clapped together. They were so happy, and all the other animals in the forest joined in the fun. The moral of the story is that when we are kind and share our fun with others, we can make many friends and be happy together.

Mia and Ben are friends. They like to play together. Today, they are playing with blocks. They make a big tower with many colors. Mia puts the last block on top. She is happy. "Look, Ben! We did it!" Mia says. She claps her hands. Ben claps his hands too. They are proud of their tower. It is perfect. But then, a cat comes. The cat is curious. It jumps on the table. It sees the tower. It wants to touch it. The cat paws at the tower. The tower falls down. The blocks go everywhere. Mia and Ben are sad. "Bad cat!" Mia says. She cries. Ben cries too. They are angry at the cat. The cat runs away. It is scared. Mia's mom hears the noise. She comes to see what happened. She sees the mess. She sees Mia and Ben crying. She feels sorry for them. "It's okay, Mia and Ben. Don't cry. The cat didn't mean to ruin your tower. You can make another one. Come, I have something for you." Mia's mom says. She hugs them. She takes them to the kitchen. She gives them juice. Mia and Ben like juice. They drink it. They feel better. "Thank you, mom." Mia says. She smiles. Ben smiles too. They are happy again. "Can we play with blocks again?" Ben asks. "Sure, you can. But be careful of the cat." Mia's mom says. She laughs. Mia and Ben laugh too. They go back to play. They make a new tower. They hope the cat won't come again. They clap their hands. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Andy. Andy loved to blink his eyes and carry weight on his back. One day, Andy was walking in the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. Andy said, "Lily, can you blink your eyes like me?" Lily blinked her eyes and laughed. "Yes, I can blink, Andy!" she said. They blinked their eyes and played all day. But then, a big wind came and blew Andy away. Lily looked for Andy but could not find him. She was sad and cried. Andy was far away and wanted to go back to Lily. He thought, "I need to find a way to go back to my friend." Andy saw a leaf and had an idea. He put the weight on the leaf and it became like a boat. The wind blew the leaf back to Lily. When they saw each other, they were so happy! Lily said, "Andy, I missed you!" Andy said, "I missed you too, Lily!" They hugged and played together again, blinking their eyes and carrying weight. And they were happy forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear and a little bear. They lived in a forest. The big bear wanted to build a new home for them. The little bear was very excited. They found a nice place to build their home. They worked very hard. The big bear made sure their home was safe. The little bear helped by carrying small things. They built a strong and warm home. One day, it rained a lot. The little bear felt uncomfortable because his bed was wet. The big bear fixed the roof to stop the rain. Now, their home was safe and dry. The little bear was happy and comfortable again. They lived happily in their new home.
