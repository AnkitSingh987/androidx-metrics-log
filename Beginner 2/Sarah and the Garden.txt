Category: Beginner
Title: Sarah and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and loved to explore the garden. As Sarah walked around she noticed an attractive shadow on the ground. She slowly approached it, but it suddenly started to move away. She was curious and wanted to find out what the shadow was. Sarah followed it, but the shadow kept on running away and ignored her. Sarah started to get frustrated and shouted, "Hey! Come back!" The shadow then stopped and turned around to face her. It was an equally attractive little kitten, which stood there and meowed. Sarah smiled, scooped the kitten up in her arms, and said, "It's ok, I won't ignore you anymore!"

One day, a big dog named Ben went for a walk. He saw his friend, a small cat named Sam. Sam was sitting in the shade under a tree. Ben said, "Hi Sam! Do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, Ben! Let's play in my tidy yard." So, they went to Sam's yard. They played catch and had fun. Ben was happy to lead Sam in their play. They laughed and ran in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves from the tree. The leaves went all over Sam's tidy yard. Sam and Ben were surprised. They looked at each other and said, "Oh no! What do we do now?" Ben had an idea. "Let's work together and clean the yard!" So, they picked up all the leaves and made the yard tidy again. They were tired but happy. They sat in the shade under the tree and smiled at their clean yard.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little dog named Spot. Spot had a small house, a soft bed, and a big yard to play in. He was a very happy dog. One day, Spot saw a big cat in his yard. The cat was sitting on a rock, looking at Spot. Spot wanted to play, but the cat did not. Spot tried to ignore the cat, but the cat just sat there and did not go away. Spot decided to wait. After a short wait, the cat got up and walked away. Spot was happy again. He went back to playing in his yard, chasing his ball, and rolling in the grass. And from that day on, Spot learned that sometimes it is best to ignore and wait.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a big hill. He wanted to play on the hill with his ball. Spot ran up the hill with his ball. At the top of the hill, he let the ball go. The ball rolled down the hill very fast. Spot ran after the ball to catch it. As Spot ran down the hill, he did not see a big rock. He slipped on the rock and fell down. The ball kept rolling down the hill. At the bottom of the hill, the ball hit a pointy stick. The stick poked a hole in the ball. Spot's ball could not bounce anymore. Spot was sad that his ball was broken.

Once upon a time, in a cold winter, there was a popular cat named Tom. He loved to play with his friends in the snow. One day, Tom met a new friend, a little dog named Max. "Hi, I'm Tom," said the cat. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, I'm Max," said the dog. "I love to play in the snow too!" Tom and Max played all day long. They made a big snowman and had a fun snowball fight. After playing, they were both very wet and cold. "Let's go inside and get dry," said Tom. They went inside Tom's house and sat by the warm fire. They got dry and warm. Tom and Max became best friends and played together every winter day.

One day, a mild cat named Wink went for a walk. Wink liked to play with his friends. He saw his friend, a dog named Spot. Spot was big and loved to run. "Can I play with you?" Wink asked Spot. Spot said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and played. They ran and jumped. They were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Wink up in the air! Spot was scared. He did not know what to do. But Wink was not scared. He smiled and winked at Spot. The wind put Wink back down. They laughed and played some more. They were happy friends.
