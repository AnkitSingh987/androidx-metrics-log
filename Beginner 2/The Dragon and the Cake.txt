Category: Beginner
Title: The Dragon and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a dragon. The dragon was very fancy. He had big wings and a long tail. He lived in a big cave. One day, the dragon met a little boy. The boy said, "Hi, dragon! Let's celebrate my birthday!" The dragon was happy and said, "Yes, let's have a party!" They started to plan the party together. At the party, there were many friends, cake, and games. But the dragon's tail was too long. His tail knocked down the cake and broke the games. The boy was sad, and the party was not fun anymore. The fancy dragon felt bad for ruining the party.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he went to the park. At the park, he saw a big slide and a trail. Tim was very happy. Tim walked on the trail. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I want to slide, but I am scared." Tim smiled and said, "I will help you." Tim and Sue went to the slide. They held hands and went up. They both sat at the top. Tim said, "Ready?" Sue said, "Yes!" They slid down together. Sue was happy. Tim and Sue played all day.

One day, a shy lady was walking in the park. She saw a small boy with a red balloon. The red balloon was not attached to the boy's hand. The wind blew, and the balloon started to fly away. "Help! My balloon!" the boy cried. The shy lady wanted to help, so she ran after the balloon. She jumped high and caught the balloon. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, lady!" The shy lady attached the balloon to the boy's hand. The boy smiled and they both walked away. The shy lady felt good for helping, and the boy was happy to have his balloon back.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very intelligent. Every day, he would run to his class to learn new things. He liked to play with his friends, too. One day, Tim met a new friend named Max. Max asked, "Do you want to run and play with me?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's go!" They ran and played together all day. They had so much fun. Tim and Max became best friends. They ran to class together every day. They learned a lot and had fun. Everyone knew that Tim and Max were intelligent and kind. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a swing and ran to it. Mia loved the swing. She would gaze at it and smile. The swing was always there for her. It was very reliable. Mia's friend, Tom, came to the park too. He saw Mia on the swing. Tom said, "Hi, Mia! Can I swing too?" Mia said, "Yes, Tom. You can swing next to me!" So, Tom and Mia swung together. Suddenly, a big wind came. The wind pushed Mia and Tom high in the sky! They were scared but also excited. They never knew the swing could do that. They laughed and shouted, "Wow! This is fun!" After the wind stopped, they went back down. Mia and Tom now had a new, fun story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a silly beetle. The beetle liked to wear a tiny hat. One day, the beetle went for a walk in the park. In the park, the beetle met a big dog. The dog looked at the beetle and said, "Why do you wear that hat? It looks silly on you!" The beetle just smiled and kept walking. The beetle then met a friendly cat. The cat said, "I like your hat! It is fun and makes you special." The beetle felt happy and thanked the cat. From that day on, the beetle wore its hat with pride, knowing that being silly can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new toy. It was a chess set. Tim liked to play with the chess set every day. One day, Tim's friend, Ben, came to visit him. Ben saw the chess set and wanted to play too. They sat down and stared at the chess set. They tried to learn how to play the game. Tim and Ben played chess for a long time. They had lots of fun. They learned how to move the pieces and play the game. In the end, they both became very good at chess. And they lived happily ever after.
