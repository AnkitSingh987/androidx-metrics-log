Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Car and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, red gem. A boy and a girl saw the gem. The boy wanted to turn it into a toy car. The girl wanted to turn it into a pretty dress. They both wanted the gem very much. The boy said, "Let's be patient and think." The girl said, "Yes, let's take turns to use the gem." They both agreed to share the gem. But they still did not know how to turn the gem into a toy car or a pretty dress. A wise, old bird saw the boy and girl. The bird said, "I can help you turn the gem into a toy car and a pretty dress. But, you must be patient and wait for your turn." The boy and girl were happy and agreed. The wise bird turned the gem into a toy car for the boy. He played with it and had fun. Then, the bird turned the gem into a pretty dress for the girl. She wore it and felt happy. The boy and girl learned to be patient and share. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a nice man went to the airport. He wanted to fly in a big plane. At the airport, he met a helpful lady. She was an honest worker who liked to provide help to people. The man asked, "Can you help me find my plane?" The lady said, "Yes, I will provide help. Follow me." They walked together and found the big plane. The man was happy and said, "Thank you for your help!" The honest lady smiled and said, "You're welcome! Have a good trip!" The man got on the plane, and it flew up into the sky. The man had a great time, and he was thankful for the help at the airport.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But they do not like to clean up their toys when they are done. They leave them on the floor, on the bed, and on the table. One day, their mom comes to their room and sees the mess. She is not happy. She says, "Sara and Ben, you have to clean up your toys. This is your room, not a dump. If you do not clean up, you will not get any dessert tonight. Do you understand?" She gives them a lecture. Sara and Ben nod, but they do not want to clean up. They think they are clever. They have an idea. They say, "Mom, can we close the door? We want to surprise you with how clean our room will be." Mom smiles and says, "Okay, but you have to hurry. Dinner will be ready soon." She closes the door and goes to the kitchen. Sara and Ben do not clean up. They hide their toys under the bed, in the closet, and in the drawers. They think mom will not see them. They laugh and say, "We are so clever. We tricked mom. Now we can have dessert." But they are wrong. Mom is clever too. She knows they did not clean up. She opens the door and sees the toys sticking out. She is very angry. She says, "Sara and Ben, you lied to me. You did not clean up. You hid your toys. That is not nice. That is naughty. You will not get any dessert. You will go to bed early. And you will clean up your room tomorrow. No more toys for you until you learn to be responsible." Sara and Ben cry. They are not clever. They are sorry. But it is too late. They have to go to bed without dessert. They have a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little flea named Fifi. Fifi lived on a big dog named Ruff. Ruff was a very happy dog, always ready to play and run in the park. One sunny day, Ruff and Fifi went to the park. They saw a big, round thing on the ground. Ruff said, "What is that?" Fifi said, "I don't know, let's find out!" Ruff put his paw on it, and it showed some numbers. It was a thing to weigh stuff! Fifi jumped on the thing to weigh herself. The numbers were very small. Fifi said, "I am so light!" Ruff laughed and said, "You are just a tiny flea!" They both laughed and played in the park all day long. And that is the story of Fifi the flea and her friend Ruff the dog.
