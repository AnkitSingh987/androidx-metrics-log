Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Tree

One day, a small cat named Kitty went for a walk. Kitty was very hungry and wanted to find a meal. She looked around and saw a big tree with a bird in it. Kitty thought the bird would be a good meal. Kitty was scared to climb the tree, but she wanted the meal. She took a deep breath and started to climb. When she got to the top, the bird saw her and was surprised. The bird didn't want to be Kitty's meal, so it flew away. Kitty was sad and climbed down the tree. She kept looking for a meal and found a big bowl of cat food at home. Kitty was very happy and ate the whole meal.

There was a boy who liked to play with his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite was a red car. He liked to zoom the car around the floor and make loud noises. He was happy with his car. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He saw another boy who had a blue car. The boy's car was bigger and shinier than his car. He wanted the blue car very much. He asked the boy if he could play with his car. The boy said no, it was his car. He asked again and again, but the boy said no every time. The boy with the red car got angry. He decided to take the blue car by force. He ran to the boy and grabbed his car. The boy with the blue car cried and tried to get his car back. He said it was not fair. He said he was kind to the boy with the red car and let him see his car, but he did not want to share it. He said he loved his car very much. The boy with the red car did not care. He insisted that the blue car was his now. He pushed the boy with the blue car and ran away. He laughed and said he had the best car in the world. He did not see the big dog that was chasing him. The dog was hungry and thought the blue car was a toy. The dog bit the boy with the red car and took the blue car in his mouth. The boy with the red car screamed and cried. He had no car and he was hurt. The boy with the blue car felt sorry for the boy with the red car. He ran to him and helped him. He said he was sorry for what happened. He said he would share his car with him if he wanted. He said he was kind to him and wanted to be his friend. The boy with the red car said he was sorry too. He said he was mean to him and did not want to be his friend. He said he learned his lesson. He said he would never take things that were not his again. He said he wished he still had his red car. He said he was sad and alone. The end.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. They saw a big hill with many flowers on it. They wanted to join the flowers and smell them. "Let's go to the hill and pick some flowers," said Lily. "OK, but we have to be careful not to hurt them," said Ben. They ran to the hill and saw many colors and shapes of flowers. They were happy and excited. They picked some flowers and put them in their hands. But then they saw a ladybug on a flower. The ladybug was red and black and had spots. It looked scared and sad. "Hello, ladybug. Why are you sad?" asked Lily. "I'm sad because you picked my flower. This was my home and my food. Now I have nowhere to go," said the ladybug. Lily and Ben felt sorry for the ladybug. They did not know that they were hurting the flowers and the bugs. "We're sorry, ladybug. We did not mean to hurt you. We just wanted to join the flowers and smell them," said Ben. "Can we help you find a new flower?" asked Lily. The ladybug smiled and nodded. "That would be very helpful. Thank you, friends." Lily and Ben put the flowers back on the hill and looked for a new flower for the ladybug. They found a yellow one with a big center. The ladybug liked it and crawled on it. "Thank you, friends. You are very kind and helpful. You can join me and smell this flower. It smells very nice," said the ladybug. Lily and Ben smiled and joined the ladybug and the flower. They smelled the flower and felt happy. They learned that they should not pick the flowers, but enjoy them with the bugs. They also learned that they should be helpful to others who need help. They became friends with the ladybug and played with him on the hill.
