Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Sue and the Basket

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they found a big basket full of toys and books. They were very happy and wanted to share the basket. But Tom and Sue did not agree on how to share the basket. Tom wanted to take all the toys and leave the books for Sue. Sue wanted to take all the books and leave the toys for Tom. They both thought their way was fair and the other way was foolish. They started to argue and pull the basket. They did not listen to each other or say sorry. They made a lot of noise and made the basket fall. The toys and books spilled out of the basket and some of them broke. A lady came and saw the mess. She was angry and said, "This is my basket! I brought it for the children in the park to enjoy. But you two were selfish and rude and ruined it. Now no one can play with the basket. You should be ashamed of yourselves!" Tom and Sue felt very bad. They realized they were wrong and foolish. They said sorry to the lady and to each other. They helped to clean up the mess and fix the toys and books. They learned to share and agree. They became friends again and played nicely in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was feeling miserable because he had no friends to play with. One day, he saw a big box with a bow on it near his house. Tim knew it was a gift for someone, but he didn't know who it was for. Tim decided to look around and find who the gift belonged to. He walked around the town, asking people if they lost a gift. No one said yes, but they all smiled at Tim and thanked him for being so nice. Tim felt a little better, but he still wanted to find the owner of the gift. As he walked, he saw a little girl crying. He asked her what was wrong, and she said she had dropped her gift and couldn't find it. Tim knew this must be the girl who lost the gift. He gave her the gift, and she was so happy. The girl asked Tim to play with her, and they became best friends. From that day on, Tim was never miserable again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Spot saw a big ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to reach the ball, but the tree was too high. Spot felt sad. A tall giraffe named Gerry saw Spot and asked, "Why are you sad, Spot?" Spot said, "I want to reach the ball in the tree, but I am too small." Gerry smiled and said, "I can help you, Spot!" Gerry stretched his long neck up to the tree. He reached the ball and gave it to Spot. Spot was so happy! They played together in the dry, sunny field all day long. Spot and Gerry became the best of friends.

One day, a bunny named Bob found a brilliant carrot in the garden. It was big, orange, and shiny. Bob was very happy to see the carrot. He wanted to eat it all up. Bob picked up the carrot and gave it a big squeeze. It was very hard and strong. He knew it would taste very good. Bob hopped back to his home with the carrot. When he got home, he met his friend, Sam the squirrel. Sam looked at the carrot and said, "Wow, that's a big carrot!" Bob smiled and said, "Yes, it's brilliant! I found it in the garden. Let's share it!" So, Bob and Sam shared the big carrot and they were both very happy.

Lily and Ben were adventurous twins who liked to explore the woods behind their house. One day, they found a big bow and some arrows in a cave. They wanted to play with them, but they did not know how. "Look, Ben, a bow and arrows!" Lily said. "Can we touch them?" "I don't know, Lily. Maybe they belong to someone. Maybe they are dangerous." Ben said. But Lily was curious and brave. She picked up an arrow and touched the pointy end. It was sharp and hurt her finger. She cried out and dropped the arrow. "Ow, ow, ow! It bit me, Ben! It bit me!" Lily said. Ben ran to his sister and hugged her. He saw blood on her finger and felt scared. He did not know what to do. "Shh, shh, shh, Lily. It's okay, it's okay. Let me see your finger." Ben said. He took a leaf from the ground and wrapped it around her finger. He hoped it would help. "Come on, Lily. Let's go home. Mom will make it better." Ben said. He helped Lily stand up and they left the cave. They did not touch the bow and arrows again. They learned that some things in the woods were not for playing.
