Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Furry Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, in a big navy house, there lived a furry cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy mouse. One day, Tom could not find his toy mouse. He looked everywhere for it. He was very sad. Tom asked his friend, Sam the dog, to help him. Sam and Tom looked in every room of the navy house. They looked under the beds and behind the chairs. They could not find the toy mouse. Tom was even more sad. Then, they went to the garden. They saw a big tree. In the tree, they saw a bird. The bird had Tom's toy mouse in its beak! Tom and Sam asked the bird to give the toy mouse back. The bird did not want to give it back. Sam had an idea. He told Tom to climb the tree and squeeze the bird's beak gently. Tom climbed the tree and squeezed the bird's beak. The bird let go of the toy mouse, and it fell to the ground. Tom was so happy to have his toy mouse back. Tom and Sam played together with the toy mouse all day long.

One day, a little cat named Kitty saw a big red ball. Kitty wanted to play with the ball, but the ball was very persistent. It would not stay still. Kitty tried to resist chasing the ball, but she could not. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came over and said, "I like your style, Kitty. Let's play with the ball together!" Kitty and Doggy played with the ball all day long. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Kitty and Doggy were tired but happy. They learned that playing together was the best way to have fun. And Kitty knew that sometimes, it was okay not to resist.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He was very proud of his car because it was a gift from his mom. Tim loved to make the car go fast and jump over his knee. While playing, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I hate that car. It's not fun." Tim felt sad, but he still played with his car. Sue watched Tim play and felt a little bit jealous. She wanted to play with the car too. Sue asked, "Can I play with the car?" Tim said, "No, you said you hate it." Sue got angry and pushed Tim. Tim fell down and hurt his knee. Tim cried and went home with his car. Sue felt bad, but it was too late. Tim and Sue did not become friends, and Tim's knee was still hurting.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and move all day long. He would run from one side of the yard to the other side, wagging his tail and barking happily. One day, Spot saw a big ball on the other side of the yard. He wanted to play with it, so he started to move towards it. As he got closer, he saw that the ball was even bigger than he thought! Spot tried to push the ball with his nose, but it was too big for him. So he called his friend, a big dog named Max, to help him. Together, they pushed the ball from one side of the yard to the other side. They had so much fun playing with the ball, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived an ancient dog named Mr. Barks. Mr. Barks loved to read the newspaper every day. He would wait by the door for the paper boy to arrive. One sunny day, the paper boy did not arrive. Mr. Barks was sad. He went to his friend, the little girl named Lily. "Lily, my newspaper did not come today. Can you help me find it?" Mr. Barks asked. Lily said, "Yes, Mr. Barks! Let's look for it together." They looked around the town and found the paper boy. He was lost! Lily showed him the way back to Mr. Barks' house. The paper boy said, "Thank you, Lily and Mr. Barks!" He gave Mr. Barks his newspaper, and they all smiled. From that day on, they became good friends.
