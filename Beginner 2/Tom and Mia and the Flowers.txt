Category: Beginner
Title: Tom and Mia and the Flowers

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play with blocks and make things. One day, they decide to create a big cross with their blocks. They put four long blocks in the middle and four short blocks on the ends. "Look, we made a cross!" Tom says. "It is very big and strong." "Yes, it is!" Mia agrees. "But it is not very charming. It needs some color and sparkle." Mia runs to get some stickers and crayons. She comes back with a lot of them. She sticks some stickers on the cross and draws some flowers and stars with the crayons. "Now it is charming!" Mia says. "It is the most charming cross ever!" Tom smiles and nods. He likes the cross too. He thinks Mia is very clever and kind. He gives her a hug and says, "You are my best friend, Mia. You make everything better." Mia hugs him back and says, "You are my best friend too, Tom. You make everything fun." They keep playing with their cross and their blocks. They are very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little town. The town was next to a big river. One day, the rain came down very hard. The people in the town were scared. They did not want a flood. A girl named Lily had a good idea. She said, "Let's build a wall to prevent the flood!" The people liked her idea. They all worked together. They built a strong wall to stop the water from coming in. When the wall was done, the people were happy. They danced and played, but some got dizzy from spinning too much. The rain stopped, and the town was safe. The wall had prevented the flood. Everyone thanked Lily, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl and her mommy went to the river to play. The little girl had her old vest on to keep her warm. She wanted to get in the river and splash around. Mommy said yes, but told the little girl to stay close to the shore. But the girl got so excited, she ran straight into the river. The girl was having so much fun splashing in the river, she didn't notice how far she had gone. Suddenly, the current became too strong, and it swept the little girl away. The girl couldn't swim back, so all she could do was splash and yell for help. But it was too late. No one ever came. The little girl was swept away, never to be seen again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big hall. In the hall, there were many toys. A little girl named Mia lived near the hall. Mia loved to play with the toys in the hall. She had a loyal dog named Max. Max liked to play, too. One day, Mia and Max went to the hall to play. But, the toys were broken. Mia was sad. She looked at Max and said, "We must restore the toys!" Max barked and wagged his tail. He wanted to help Mia. Mia and Max worked together to fix the toys. They restored the toys in the hall. Mia was happy, and Max was happy too. They played with the toys all day long. From that day on, Mia and Max always took care of the toys in the hall.

One day, a little bird saw a worm on the ground. The worm was long and wiggly. The bird wanted to eat the worm, so it started to follow the worm. The worm saw the bird and was scared. It tried to hide under a rock. The bird said, "Come out, worm! I want to eat you!" The worm felt guilty because it did not want to be eaten. It said, "Please don't eat me, bird! I have a family!" The bird thought about it and felt guilty too. It did not want to take the worm away from its family. So, the bird said, "Okay, worm. I won't eat you. I will find something else to eat." The worm was so happy and thankful. It went back to its family under the ground. The bird flew away to find other food. From that day on, the bird and the worm were friends. They would play together and help each other when they needed it.
