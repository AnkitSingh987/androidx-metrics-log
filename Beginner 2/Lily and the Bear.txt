Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She was very sleepy but didn't want to take a nap. Her mom said, "Lily, listen! You need to rest." Lily didn't want to listen, so she kept playing. In her room, Lily found her mom's makeup. She thought it was fun to play with. She put makeup on her teddy bear and on her doll. The makeup made her teddy bear look funny. Lily laughed and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's teddy bear started to talk! It said, "Thank you for the makeup, Lily! Now I can be pretty too!" Lily was so surprised that she forgot she was sleepy. She and her talking teddy bear played together until it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, there was a wild dragon. The dragon had a big tail. He liked to play with his friends in the forest. One day, the wild dragon met a little boy. The boy said, "Hi, dragon! Can you help me tie my shoes?" The dragon said, "Yes, I can help you." The wild dragon used his tail to tie the boy's shoes. The boy was happy. They played together all day long. From that day on, the boy and the wild dragon were best friends.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Lucy saw some pasta on the shelf. She asked her mom, "Can we get this pasta, please?" Her mom looked at the price and said, "No, that pasta is too expensive. We will get this one instead." At home, Lucy helped her mom cook the pasta. They put it in a big pot with water. After it was cooked, they put it on plates. Lucy was so excited to eat the pasta. But when she took a bite, she spilled some sauce on her shirt. Lucy's mom saw the mess and said, "Oh no, you need to wipe that off!" Lucy tried to wipe the sauce off her shirt, but it only made it worse. The stain was too big and did not go away. Lucy was sad and her mom was upset. They had to throw the shirt away because it was ruined by the pasta sauce.

Ben and Mia like to play in the kitchen. They find a big cup and a small spoon. They see some limes on the table. Ben says, "Let's measure the limes with the cup and the spoon. It will be fun!" Mia nods and picks up a lime. She tries to cut it with the spoon, but it is too hard. She pushes harder and harder, but the spoon does not go through. She says, "This is not fun. This is hard. I want to stop." Ben says, "No, don't stop. We have to measure the limes. I will help you." He takes the spoon from Mia and hits the lime with it. The lime breaks and the juice squirts out. Some of the juice goes into Mia's eyes. She screams, "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts! It hurts!" Ben drops the spoon and the cup. He sees Mia crying and rubbing her eyes. He says, "I'm sorry, Mia. I didn't mean to hurt you. It was an accident. Please don't cry." But Mia does not stop crying. She runs to their mom and tells her what happened. Mom is angry and scolds Ben. She says, "Ben, you were very naughty. You should not play with limes and spoons. You could have hurt yourself or Mia. You are in big trouble. Go to your room and think about what you did." Ben feels sad and ashamed. He goes to his room and sits on his bed. He wishes he had not played with the limes and spoons. He wishes he had listened to Mia. He wishes he could say sorry to her and make her happy again. But he can't. He has to stay in his room and wait for mom to forgive him. He thinks, "This is the worst day ever."

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little bird named Tim. Tim was restless. He wanted to escape and see the world outside. One day, Tim found an open window. He flew out and began to explore. As Tim flew, he saw a big tree. He landed on a branch and met a new friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "Hi Tim! Do you know how to whistle?" Tim said, "No, I don't. Can you teach me?" Sam said, "Sure! Just pucker your lips and blow air softly." Tim tried to whistle, but it was hard. He practiced and practiced. Finally, he did it! Tim was so happy that he could whistle. He whistled all day long with Sam. They played and had fun together. As the sun began to set, Tim knew it was time to go home. He said goodbye to Sam and flew back to his small house. His family was glad to see him. Tim told them about his adventure and how he learned to whistle. From that day on, Tim was not restless anymore. He knew he could escape and explore the world whenever he wanted, and he had a new friend to play with too.
