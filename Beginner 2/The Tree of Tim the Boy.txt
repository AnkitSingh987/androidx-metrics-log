Category: Beginner
Title: The Tree of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy dog named Spot. Tim and Spot were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Tim and Spot went to the park. At the park, Tim saw a big tree with a buckle on it. Tim said, "Look, Spot! A buckle on the tree!" Tim wanted to touch the buckle, so he tried to climb the tree. But he was too small and could not reach it. This made Tim very sad. Spot did not want Tim to be sad. He had an idea. Spot began to shake the tree with all his might. The buckle fell down from the tree. Tim was so happy! He picked up the buckle and said, "Thank you, Spot! You are the best friend ever!" Tim and Spot played with the buckle and had a great day at the park.

One day, a cat named Tom found a guitar. Tom wanted to serve his friends by playing music for them. He was very impatient to show them his new toy. So, Tom went to find his friends to play the guitar for them. Tom found his friend, a dog named Max. Max was happy to see Tom and the guitar. Tom said, "Max, I want to serve you by playing music!" Max was excited and sat down to listen. Tom played the guitar, and Max clapped his paws. Soon, more friends came to listen to Tom play the guitar. They loved the music and danced together. Tom was happy to serve his friends with his guitar. Everyone had a fun time, and they all thanked Tom for his music.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw the sand and the big water. Tim liked the sand. He wanted to get a lot of sand to play with. He took his toy and started to dig. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't go too far away." But Tim did not listen. He wanted more sand. He went to a place where the water was loud. The big water was making a lot of noise. A girl named Sue was there too. Sue said, "Tim, we should go back. It is too loud here." But Tim did not want to go back. He wanted to get more sand. Then, a big wave came. It took Tim and Sue away. They did not listen, and now they were in the big water.

Once upon a time, there was a cool lawyer named Bob. Bob loved to help people. At night, Bob would dream about his day and all the people he helped. One day, Bob met a little girl named Sally. Sally was sad because she lost her toy. Bob wanted to help, so he said, "Don't worry, Sally. I will find your toy." Bob and Sally looked everywhere for the toy. They looked in the park, in the store, and even in the big tree. Finally, they found the toy under Sally's bed. Sally was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bob. You are the best lawyer ever!" Bob felt happy that he could help Sally find her toy. That night, Bob had a dream about his day with Sally. In his dream, they found the toy and played together all day. Bob knew that helping people was the best job ever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a thin girl named Lily. She loved to play outside when the sun was shining. One day, Lily decided to go to the park. At the park, she saw many pretty flowers. She wanted to pick some for her mom. Lily picked red, yellow, and blue flowers. She was very happy with her colorful bunch. Lily also found sunglasses on the grass. They were big and funny. She put the sunglasses on and laughed. She looked so silly! Lily took the flowers and sunglasses home to show her mom. Her mom laughed and gave her a big hug. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a pale bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and play in the big green yard. One day, Bob found a small herb growing near a tree. He sniffed it and thought it smelled good. Bob hopped to his friend, a bird named Sue. "Sue, I found a good herb," said Bob. "I think it can help us number how many friends we have." Sue was excited and wanted to try it too. They took the herb to all their friends in the yard. Each time they found a friend, they used the herb to help them count. At the end of the day, they knew they had ten friends. Bob and Sue were happy to know they had so many friends, and they knew the herb was special.
