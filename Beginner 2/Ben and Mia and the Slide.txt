Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and Mia and the Slide

Ben and Mia are twins. They like to play in the park. They see a big slide. Ben wants to go on the slide. Mia is afraid. "Come on, Mia. It is fun!" Ben says. "No, Ben. It is too high. It will scare me." Mia says. Ben goes on the slide. He slides down fast. He laughs. He runs to Mia. "See, Mia? It is not scary. It is easy!" Ben says. Mia looks at the slide. She wants to try. But she is still afraid. "Ben, can you help me?" Mia says. "Sure, Mia. I will help you. Hold my hand." Ben says. Ben and Mia go up the stairs. Mia feels her heart beat fast. She holds Ben's hand tight. They reach the top of the slide. Mia looks down. She sees the ground far away. She feels her head spin. "Ben, I can't do it. It is too difficult." Mia says. Ben hugs Mia. He smiles. "It is okay, Mia. You don't have to do it. We can do something else. How about the swings?" Ben says. Mia nods. She feels better. She smiles. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother. I love you." Mia says. "I love you too, Mia. You are a good sister. Come on, let's go to the swings." Ben says. Ben and Mia go to the swings. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time there was a young girl who was a bit nervous. Her family was going to do a test to try and find their new home. She didn't know what their new home was going to be like, so she was a little bit worried. They travelled for hours and hours until they eventually arrived. When the girl saw their new home she was really happy. It was a big, cozy house with a big garden. She couldn't stop smiling when she saw it. Inside the house, it was just as nice as outside. There were plenty of rooms for everyone and a big, warm kitchen. The girl was so excited that she wanted to explore every room in the house, from the attic to the basement. The girl's family were so pleased with their new home. They all passed the test and were so pleased to have such a cozy, comfortable place to live. The little girl was overjoyed, and life in their new home felt like all her dreams had come true.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy fisherman. He lived near a big blue lake. Every day, he would go to the lake to catch fish. One sunny day, the fisherman wandered near the lake to find a good spot. He saw a little boy playing with a toy boat. The boy said, "Hi, Mr. Fisherman! Do you want to play with me?" The grumpy fisherman said, "No, I am here to catch fish." The fisherman threw his net into the water. He waited and waited for a long time, but he did not catch any fish. The little boy said, "Mr. Fisherman, try playing with me. Maybe it will help you catch fish." The fisherman, tired of waiting, decided to play with the boy. They played with the toy boat and laughed together. Suddenly, the fisherman felt a big pull on his net. He pulled it out of the water, and it was full of fish! The grumpy fisherman was happy now. He learned that playing and having fun can sometimes bring good luck. From that day on, the fisherman was not grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a selfish boy who did not like to share his toys or food with others. One day, Tim's mom bought a big bag of sugar to make cookies. Tim saw the sugar and wanted to eat it all by himself. Tim took the sugar and hid it in his room. His little sister, Sue, saw him and asked, "Can I have some sugar too, please?" Tim said, "No, it's all mine!" Sue was sad and began to cry. Their mom heard Sue cry and came to see what was wrong. When she found out that Tim had taken all the sugar and wouldn't share, she said, "Tim, you must learn to share with your sister. It's not nice to be selfish." Tim felt bad for making his sister cry. He gave some sugar to Sue and they both helped mom make the cookies. From that day on, Tim learned to share and they all lived happily ever after.
