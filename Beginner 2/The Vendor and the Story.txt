Category: Beginner
Title: The Vendor and the Story

One day, a vendor went to the town to sell his fruits. He had a big cart full of apples, bananas, and oranges. He wanted to sell them all. As he walked, he saw a line of ants. They were marching in a straight line. The vendor felt dizzy watching them. He stopped and looked at the ants. They were working together and helping each other carry food. The vendor thought, "I should be like the ants and work with others." He asked his friends to help him sell the fruits. They all worked together, and soon the fruits were sold. The vendor was happy and shared the money with his friends. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can do big things.

Jenny was out for a walk with her dad in the warm sunshine. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Jenny was surprised. Dad looked around and saw a big brown bear standing right behind them! He got very scared, but he was brave and yelled at the bear to go away. The bear was very surprised to hear the noise! The bear quickly ran back into the forest, leaving Jenny and her dad safe. Dad hugged Jenny and said she was very brave. Jenny was surprised, but she felt very warm inside. The next day, when Jenny and dad went out for a walk, she wasn't so surprised when they heard a strange noise in the forest. After all, Jenny knew what it was - a bear!

Tom was a little boy who liked to play with his toys. He had a big teddy bear, a red car, a blue ball and a yellow duck. He played with them every day and every night. He brought them to his bed when he was sleepy. One night, Tom had a terrible dream. He dreamed that a big monster came and took his toys away. The monster was very scary. It had sharp teeth, long claws and green fur. It growled and roared and laughed. It put Tom's toys in a big bag and ran away. Tom woke up and cried. He looked for his toys, but he could not find them. He was very sad and scared. He called his mom. "Mom, mom, help me! A monster took my toys!" he shouted. Mom came to his room and turned on the light. She saw Tom crying and hugged him. "Shh, shh, it's okay, Tom. It was just a dream. A bad dream. Your toys are here. See?" she said. She opened the closet and showed him his toys. They were all there. The teddy bear, the red car, the blue ball and the yellow duck. Tom was very happy. He smiled and hugged his mom. "Thank you, mom. You are the best. Can you bring my toys to my bed? I want to cuddle them," he said. "Of course, Tom. Here you go. Now, try to sleep again. I will stay with you until you fall asleep. No more monsters, okay?" she said. She brought his toys to his bed and tucked him in. She kissed him and sang him a lullaby. Tom felt safe and warm. He cuddled his toys and closed his eyes. He had a good dream. He dreamed that he and his mom and his toys had a picnic in the park. He was very happy. He did not have any more terrible dreams.

Once upon a time, there was a big plane. The plane could drive fast in the sky. The plane had a name. It was called "Safe Sky". One day, Safe Sky wanted to drive to a far place. It wanted to see new things. So, it started to drive fast in the sky. It saw birds, clouds, and a big sun. At the end, Safe Sky found a nice place to rest. It was a safe place with many friends. Safe Sky was happy. It had a fun drive and found a new home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a light blue seat near the big tree. He wanted to sit on it and watch the birds. But when he got close, he saw a girl named Sue sitting on the seat. Tim felt sad. Tim said, "Mom, I want to sit on the seat too." His mom said, "Why not call Sue and ask her if you can sit with her?" Tim called Sue and said, "Can I sit with you, please?" Sue looked at Tim and said, "No, this is my seat." Tim's mom came over and said, "Sue, it would be nice if you could share the seat with Tim." Sue thought about it for a moment and then said, "Okay, Tim can sit with me." Tim smiled and sat on the light blue seat with Sue. They watched the birds together and became friends.
