Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Tom and the Game

One day, a group of kids wanted to play a game. They needed to be organized, so they picked a player to be in charge. The player was a big boy named Tom. Tom wanted to make the game fun for everyone. But some of the kids did not want to listen to Tom. They wanted to do their own thing. This made it hard for Tom to keep the game organized. The kids started to argue and fight. They could not play the game well. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's part into two teams. Each team can play the game their own way." The kids agreed and did what Tom said. They became two teams and played happily. In the end, they all had fun and learned to work together.

One day, a girl named Sue went to a hotel with her mom and dad. The hotel was big and had many rooms. But when they went inside, they saw that it was filthy. Sue did not like the dirt and mess. She said, "Mom, I don't like it here. It's too dirty." Her mom and dad agreed, so they decided to clean the hotel. They worked hard and made the hotel nice and clean. Then, they saw a big door. They opened the door and found a big room full of toys and games. Sue was so happy, she smiled and said, "Wow! Look at all the fun things to play with!" Her mom and dad smiled too. They played together in the toy room all day. The hotel was not so bad after all. They had a great time and made the hotel a fun place to stay.

Once there was a girl. She was only 3. She found a page on the ground. She was excited and wanted to see what it said. But the page was tough. She couldn't read the words. She started to get sad. Her daddy saw her and tried to comfort her. He said it was ok and asked her what the page said. She told him she didn't know. Daddy said: "It's ok, I'll try to help you out." But it was too late. The page had blown away and they never saw it again. Daddy held her tight, trying to comfort her. But she was still sad that the page had gone away.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot's family said they were going on a holiday. Spot was very happy because he liked to go on holidays too. When they got to the holiday place, Spot saw a big, soft blanket on the ground. Spot thought it was a fun game to cover himself with the blanket. He hid under it and waited for his friends to find him. Spot was very good at hiding, and his friends had to look very hard to find him. After a while, Spot's friends found him under the blanket. They all laughed and played together. They played games and had lots of fun on their holiday. Spot was so happy to be with his friends and family. They all had a great time, and Spot couldn't wait for the next holiday.

Once upon a time, there was a blue fish named Bob. Bob lived in a big pond. He had many friends. One day, Bob saw a plastic bag in the water. The plastic bag was not good for the pond. Bob went to his friend, the big frog. "We need to prevent the plastic bag from hurting our home," said Bob. The big frog agreed. They tried to push the plastic bag out of the pond, but it was too hard. Then, a strong wind came and blew the plastic bag away. Bob and the big frog were happy. But the wind also blew more plastic bags into the pond. The pond became dirty, and the animals were sad. They could not prevent the bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot was very good at finding things. One day, Spot and his friend Tim went to the park to play. Tim wanted to find a safe place to play. Spot used his gifted nose to sniff and find a safe spot. He sniffed and sniffed until he found a nice, safe place for them to play. Tim was happy that Spot found a safe place. They played all day and had lots of fun. After playing, Spot and Tim were hungry. Spot sniffed and sniffed again to find food. He found a big tree with lots of fruit. Spot and Tim picked the fruit from the tree. The tree gave them food. The tree was happy to yield its fruit for Spot and Tim to eat. They ate the fruit and went home, tired but happy from their day at the park.
