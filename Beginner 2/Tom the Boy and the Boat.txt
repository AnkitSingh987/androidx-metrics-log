Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Boy and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tom went to the port with his dad. They saw big boats and little boats. Tom saw a weird boat with a funny mark on it. It was a big red X. Tom asked his dad, "What is that mark?" His dad said, "That is a special mark. It means there is a surprise inside the boat." Tom wanted to see the surprise. They went on the boat and looked around. They found a big box with the same red X mark on it. Tom and his dad opened the box. Inside the box, there were many colorful balloons. The balloons started to fly out of the box! One of the balloons had a note on it. Tom's dad read the note. It said, "Surprise! You found the magic balloons!" Tom and his dad were very happy. They played with the magic balloons all day at the port. The weird boat with the funny mark made their day special and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little dog named Spot. Spot loved to chew on everything in the house. One day, Spot found a big watch on the table. The watch was shiny and pretty. Spot wanted to chew on the watch. Spot's owner, Sally, saw him near the watch. "No, Spot!" she said. "We do not chew on watches!" Spot looked at Sally and then at the watch. He did not want to listen, but he loved Sally. So, Spot walked away from the watch. Later, Sally gave Spot a big bone to chew on. Spot was happy with his bone. He chewed and chewed until he was tired. Sally smiled and said, "Good boy, Spot!" And they lived happily ever after.

---

Tom and Lily liked to play music with their toys. They had a drum, a guitar, a flute and a bell. They called themselves the band. They played loud and happy songs for their mom and dad. One day, they wanted to play a new song. They looked for more toys to make sounds. Tom found a big ball. He thought it would make a good drum. He hit it hard with a stick. But the ball did not make a sound. It broke. The air came out and it became small and flat. "Oh no!" Tom said. "I broke the ball!" Lily saw what happened. She was sad. She liked the ball. It was her favorite toy. She said to Tom, "Why did you break the ball? It was not a drum. It was a ball. Now we can't play with it." Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to break the ball. He said to Lily, "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not know it would break. I wanted to make an interesting sound for our song. Can you forgive me?" Lily looked at Tom. She saw that he was sorry. She said, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you. But next time, be careful with the toys. They are not all drums. Some are balls, some are dolls, some are cars. They are for playing, not breaking." Tom nodded. He said, "You are right, Lily. I will be careful. Thank you for forgiving me. Can we still play music?" Lily smiled. She said, "Yes, we can still play music. But let's use the toys that are for music. Like the drum, the guitar, the flute and the bell. They make interesting sounds too. And they don't break." Tom and Lily hugged. They picked up their music toys. They played a new song. It was loud and happy. Their mom and dad clapped and cheered. They were proud of their band.

Tom and Lily were playing with a puzzle. They liked the puzzle because it had many colors and shapes. They tried to fit the pieces together to make a picture of a farm. "Look, I found the cow!" Tom said, holding a piece with black and white spots. "Good job, Tom!" Lily said. "Can I have the piece with the red barn?" Tom nodded and gave Lily the piece. They worked on the puzzle for a long time. They were patient and careful. But then, Tom saw a piece that he really liked. It had a yellow duck on it. He wanted to put it in the puzzle, but Lily had it in her hand. "Give me the duck, Lily!" Tom said, reaching for the piece. "No, Tom, it's mine!" Lily said, pulling the piece away. They both tugged on the piece, but it was too hard. The piece ripped in half. The duck was broken. Tom and Lily gasped. They looked at the piece and then at each other. They felt sad and angry. "That was my duck!" Tom said, crying. "You ripped it!" Lily said, crying too. They threw the piece on the floor and turned away from each other. They did not want to play anymore. Their mom heard them crying and came to see what was wrong. She saw the broken piece and the unfinished puzzle. "What happened, Tom and Lily?" she asked. They told her what happened. They blamed each other for the duck. Mom sighed. She picked up the piece and showed it to them. "Look, Tom and Lily, this is just a piece of cardboard. It's not a real duck. It can be fixed with some tape. But you know what can't be fixed so easily? Your friendship. You are brother and sister. You should love and help each other, not fight and hurt each other. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for what they did. "Can you say sorry to each other and forgive each other?" Mom asked. Tom and Lily looked at each other. They saw the tears in their eyes and the hurt in their hearts. They hugged each other and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I'm sorry, Lily. I forgive you." Mom smiled and hugged them too. She said, "That's very good, Tom and Lily. I'm proud of you. Now, let's finish the puzzle together, okay?" Tom and Lily agreed. They taped the piece and put it in the puzzle. They found the other pieces and made the picture of the farm. They were happy and proud. They learned that puzzles are fun, but friendship is more important. They learned to be patient and kind. They learned to forgive.
