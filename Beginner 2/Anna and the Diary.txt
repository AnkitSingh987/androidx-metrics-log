Category: Beginner
Title: Anna and the Diary

Anna liked to write in her diary every day. She wrote about her toys, her friends, her family and her dreams. She had a pink diary with a lock and a key. She kept the key in her pocket and hid the diary under her pillow. One day, Anna went to the park with her mom and dad. She wanted to play on the swings and the slide. She forgot to take her key with her. She left it on the table in her room. When she came back home, she saw her brother Tom in her room. He had her diary in his hands. He was reading it and laughing. He saw Anna and said, "Look what I found! Your silly diary! You write such stupid things! You are a baby!" Anna felt angry and sad. She ran to Tom and tried to grab her diary. But Tom was bigger and stronger. He pushed her away and said, "No, this is mine now! I will show it to everyone and make fun of you!" Anna started to cry. She said, "Please, Tom, give it back! That is my diary! It is private! You are mean and rude!" Tom did not listen. He said, "No, I will not give it back! You are a crybaby! Go away and leave me alone!" Anna ran to her mom and dad. She told them what Tom did. They were shocked and angry. They went to Tom's room and scolded him. They said, "Tom, how could you do that? That is Anna's diary! You have no right to read it or take it! You should be sorry and ashamed!" Tom did not care. He said, "I don't care! It is just a stupid diary! Anna is a baby! I am not sorry!" Mom and dad took the diary from Tom and gave it back to Anna. They hugged her and said, "We are sorry, Anna. Tom was very wrong and mean. We will punish him for what he did. You can write in your diary as much as you want. We love you." Anna thanked them and hugged them back. She went to her room and locked the door. She opened her diary and saw that Tom had torn some pages and scribbled on others. He had ruined her diary. She felt very sad. She wished Tom would be sorry and nice to her. But he was not. He was still mean and rude. Anna cried and cried. She had no happy ending.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big cabinet in his room. Tim's mom said she would introduce him to a new friend. Tim was very happy. The new friend was named Sam. Sam was very nice. They played together all day. Sam saw the big cabinet in Tim's room. He asked Tim what was inside. Tim told him that there was a terrible toy inside. Tim's mom told him to never open the cabinet. The next day, Tim and Sam played again. They remembered the terrible toy. They wanted to see it. They opened the cabinet. Inside, they found a big, scary toy. Tim and Sam were scared, but they learned to listen to Tim's mom.

One day, a bird named Bob wanted to build a nest. He looked for sticks and leaves. Bob found a nice tree to put his nest in. He tried very hard to make the nest, but it was not easy. A silly squirrel named Sam saw Bob. Sam said, "Hi, Bob! What are you doing?" Bob said, "I am trying to build a nest, but it is hard." Sam said, "I can help you!" So, Sam and Bob tried together to build the nest. They worked and worked. Finally, the nest was done. It was a very nice nest. Bob said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam said, "You're welcome, Bob! Let's be friends!" Bob and Sam were happy and played together every day.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom dared to try yoga. He thought it would be fun and easy. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, said, "I don't know if you should do that, Tom." Tom did not listen to Max. He wanted to show Max that he could do yoga too. Max watched Tom as he tried to do a yoga pose. Tom's legs wobbled, and he fell down. Max tried to help Tom, but Tom said, "No, I can do it!" The next day, Tom tried yoga again. This time, Max was not there to watch him. Tom was sure he could do it this time. He tried a new yoga pose, but he fell down again. This time, Tom hurt his leg very badly. Tom was sad and in pain. He wished he had listened to Max. Now, Tom could not play with Max for a long time because of his hurt leg.
