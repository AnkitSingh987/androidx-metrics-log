Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Ant and the Bird

One day, a little ant named Tim was walking slow on a path. He saw a big crack on the ground. Tim was scared. He did not know how to cross the crack. A kind bird named Sue saw Tim. She flew down to help. Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will support you." Tim was happy to have a friend. Sue picked Tim up with her beak. She flew over the crack and put him on the other side. Tim said, "Thank you, Sue!" They became best friends and played together all day.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the garden. They found a big pit with sand and stones. They decided to bake a cake with the sand and stones. They took a bucket and a shovel and filled the bucket with sand. They added some stones and water and mixed them well. They said, "This is our cake. It is different from other cakes. It is crunchy and wet." They looked for a place to bake their cake. They saw a big rock that was warm from the sun. They said, "This is our oven. It is different from other ovens. It is hard and hot." They put their bucket on the rock and waited for their cake to bake. They sang a song and danced around the pit. They said, "This is our party. It is different from other parties. It is fun and free." After a while, they checked their cake. It was still sand and stones, but it was dry and hot. They said, "This is our cake. It is different from other cakes. It is not yummy and soft." They looked at each other and laughed. They said, "We don't want to eat this cake. It is different from other cakes. It is silly and bad." They dumped their cake back into the pit and ran to the house. They asked their mom for a real cake. She smiled and said, "OK, but you have to help me bake it. It is not different from other cakes. It is sweet and good."

Once upon a time, a human named Tom lived in a small house. Tom had a thick, warm coat that he loved to wear. One day, he decided to go for a walk in the woods near his house. As Tom walked, he saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The bird looked cold and sad. Tom said, "Hello, little bird. Why are you so sad?" The bird replied, "I am cold and I can't find a warm place to settle." Tom thought for a moment and said, "You can settle in my thick coat to stay warm." The bird was happy and flew into Tom's coat. Together, they walked back to Tom's house. The bird settled in Tom's coat and they became good friends. They spent many happy days together, staying warm and playing in the woods. And the little bird was never cold or sad again.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play together. One day, they have a new game. They want to surprise Mom and Dad. They wait until Mom and Dad go to the shower. They whisper to each other. "Let's make them a cake!" Lily says. "Yes, let's!" Ben says. They go to the kitchen. They find flour, eggs, sugar, and butter. They mix them in a big bowl. They make a mess. They put the bowl in the oven. They set the timer. They wait. They hear the shower stop. They whisper again. "Hurry, hurry!" Lily says. "They are coming!" Ben says. They run to the living room. They hide behind the sofa. Mom and Dad come out of the shower. They are wet and clean. They smell something. They go to the kitchen. They see the oven. They see the mess. They open the oven. They see the cake. It is wide and flat and burnt. They are shocked. Lily and Ben jump out from behind the sofa. They shout. "Surprise! We made you a cake!" They smile. They hug Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad laugh. They are not angry. They are happy. They say. "Thank you, Lily and Ben. You are very sweet. But next time, please ask us before you use the oven. And please clean up the mess." Lily and Ben nod. They say. "OK, Mom and Dad. We love you." They all go to the kitchen. They eat the cake. It is not very good. But they do not care. They are a family. They have fun.
