Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Messy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy top. He would spin the top and watch it go round and round. Spot also liked to jog with his best friend, Tom the cat. One day, Spot and Tom went for a jog in the park. Spot saw a big pile of dirt and wanted to play in it. He jumped in and got very messy. Tom told Spot to stay clean, but Spot did not listen. After playing in the dirt, Spot wanted to play with his top. But his messy paws made the top dirty too. Spot was sad because he could not play with his top anymore. Tom helped Spot clean up, and they both learned that it is important to stay clean and listen to friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. His mom told him, "Tim, behave and clean your room." Tim did not like to clean, but he wanted to be a good boy, so he started picking up his toys. As he cleaned, he found a smelly sheet under his bed. "Ew!" said Tim. He held his nose and carried the sheet to the laundry room. He put it in the washing machine and turned it on. While the sheet was washing, Tim heard a strange noise. He looked around and saw a little mouse wearing a hat! The mouse said, "Thank you for washing my smelly sheet!" Tim was surprised but happy to help. From that day on, Tim and the mouse became good friends, and they always kept the room clean together.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bob. Bob liked to wear a big hat. One day, Bob found some celery in the garden. He was very happy. He loved to eat celery. Bob was patient and waited for the celery to grow bigger. While Bob was waiting, a big bird named Sam came. Sam saw the celery too. Sam was not patient like Bob. Sam wanted to eat the celery now. So, Sam took a bite of the celery. Bob was sad because he wanted to eat the celery. Bob tried to stop Sam, but Sam was much bigger than Bob. Sam ate all the celery. Bob was very sad because he did not get to eat any celery. Bob went back to his home and cried. The big hat did not make him feel better. The end.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to gather flowers and leaves and make a big pile. They pretend the pile is a castle and they are the king and queen. They have a lot of fun. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is the telephone in the house. Lily and Ben run to the door. They want to answer the telephone. Maybe it is their grandma or their friend. They like to talk on the telephone. But when they get to the door, they see their mom. She is holding the telephone and she looks tired. She has a frown on her face. She says, "Shh, I'm talking to someone important. Go back to the garden and play quietly." Lily and Ben feel sad. They don't want to go back to the garden. They want to talk on the telephone too. They think their mom is not fair. They sit on the steps and pout. But then, they hear a soft voice. It is their dad. He is on the telephone with their mom. He says, "Hi, Lily and Ben. I miss you a lot. I'm sorry I can't be with you today. I have to work. But I love you very much. And I have a surprise for you. When I come home, I will bring you a big cake and a new toy. How does that sound?" Lily and Ben smile. They forget their anger. They love their dad. They say, "We love you too, dad. We can't wait to see you. And we can't wait for the cake and the toy. Thank you, dad." Their mom smiles too. She says, "I love you too, dad. And I'm sorry I was tired and grumpy. You are very sweet. See you soon." Lily and Ben hug their mom. They say, "We love you, mom. And we're sorry we were noisy. You are very nice." Their mom hugs them back. She says, "I love you, Lily and Ben. And I'm proud of you. You are very good." Lily and Ben feel happy. They go back to the garden. They gather more flowers and leaves. They make a bigger pile. They pretend the pile is a cake and a toy. They have a lot of fun.
