Category: Beginner
Title: The and Weak Dog and the Park

One day, a small and weak dog went for a walk. The dog liked to look at things in the park. He saw a big tree, a red ball, and a funny cat. The dog was very happy to see all these things. As the dog walked, he saw a print on the ground. It was a big print, much bigger than his paw. He wanted to know what made the big print. So, he followed the prints to see where they went. The prints led the dog to a big box. The dog was excited to see what was inside. But when he tried to open it, he stumbled and fell. The box opened, and many small and weak dogs just like him came out. They were all happy to be free, and they played together in the park. The dog was surprised but very happy to have new friends.

Once there was a boy called Jack. He was three years old and loved the colour blue. One day, he saw something amazing flying in the sky - a big blue helicopter! Jack wanted to go for a ride in the beautiful helicopter. He waved to the pilot, who waved back and said hello. In the helicopter, Jack put on his special blue helmet and wiped away a tear of excitement. The helicopter flew high up in the sky and Jack saw lots of neat things. He was so happy! Eventually, the helicopter landed and Jack said goodbye to the pilot. Jack wiped his face with a tissue, happy to have gone for a ride in his favourite blue helicopter.

Tim and Mia are playing in the park. They like to slide, swing and run. They see a big tent. It is blue and yellow. They want to look inside. "Can we go in?" Tim asks Mia. "Maybe. Let's be quiet and careful," Mia says. They sneak to the tent. They lift a flap. They see a man and a woman. They are wearing funny clothes. They have hats and capes. They have a box and a wand. "Who are they?" Tim whispers. "I don't know. Maybe they are magic," Mia whispers back. The man and the woman are talking. They do not see Tim and Mia. "Are you ready for the show?" the man asks. "Yes, I am. I hope the kids will like it," the woman says. "Me too. Let's practice our tricks. Watch this," the man says. He taps the box with his wand. He opens it. A rabbit appears. It is white and fluffy. It hops out of the box. "Wow!" Tim and Mia say. The man and the woman hear them. They turn around. They see Tim and Mia. "Hello, there. Who are you?" the woman asks. "We are Tim and Mia. We are playing in the park. We saw your tent. We wanted to look inside. We are sorry," Tim says. "That's okay. We are not angry. We are happy to see you. We are magic. We are here to do a show for the kids. Do you want to see more?" the man says. "Yes, please. We like magic. We like rabbits," Mia says. "Great. Come closer. We will show you more. But don't tell anyone. It is a secret," the woman says. Tim and Mia go closer. They sit on the ground. The man and the woman show them more tricks. They make flowers, balls and cards appear and disappear. They make things float and change color. They make Tim and Mia laugh and clap. They have a lot of fun. They forget about the time. They do not hear the bell. The bell means it is time to go home. "Uh-oh. We have to go. We hear the bell. Our mom and dad are waiting for us," Tim says. "Me too. We have to go. Thank you for the show. It was cool," Mia says. "You're welcome. We are glad you liked it. Come back tomorrow. We will do the show for everyone. You can be our helpers. You can wear hats and capes too," the man says. "Really? That would be awesome. We will come back. We will be your helpers. We will wear hats and capes. We will be magic too," Tim and Mia say. They say goodbye to the man and the woman. They say goodbye to the rabbit. They run to the flap. They lift it. They escape from the tent. They run to their mom and dad. They hug them. They tell them about the magic. They are happy. They can't wait for tomorrow.
