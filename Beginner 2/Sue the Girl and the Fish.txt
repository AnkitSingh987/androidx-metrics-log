Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect pretty rocks from the ocean. One sunny day, she went to the beach with her mom and dad. As Sue was collecting rocks, she saw a big, scary fish in the water. She was scared, but the fish said, "Don't be scared, I am a nice fish." The fish told Sue that he also liked to collect rocks, but he could not find any in the ocean. Sue had an idea. She gave the fish some of her rocks and they became friends. From that day on, Sue and the fish collected rocks together. The scary fish was not so scary after all, and Sue was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a ghost. The ghost lived in a big house. The house was old and difficult to live in. The ghost was sad because he had no friends. He wanted to play with the kids outside, but they were scared of him. One hot day, the sun was shining brightly. The ghost went outside to play. He saw an ice cream truck and wanted to try some. The ice cream man gave the ghost a yummy ice cream cone. The ghost was so happy! But, the sun was very hot. The ice cream started to melt. The ghost tried to eat it fast, but it was too difficult. The ice cream fell on the ground, and the ghost was sad. A little girl saw the ghost and felt bad. She gave her ice cream to the ghost. They ate their ice cream together in the shade. The ghost was happy because he finally had a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and watch cartoons on the computer. One day, his mom told him to clean his room, but Tim chose to ignore her. Instead of cleaning his room, Tim went to play on the computer. He watched his favorite cartoons and laughed a lot. But while he was watching, his toys were getting more and more messy in his room. As Tim kept ignoring his mom, the toys in his room piled up and it became very messy. When Tim finally went to his room, he tripped on the toys and fell down. He was very sad and hurt. Tim wished he had listened to his mom and cleaned his room.

Tim and Liz are twins. They like to play with their toys in the yard. One day, they find a shiny thing in the dirt. It is round and hard and has a face on it. "Look, Liz, a copper!" Tim says. He shows Liz the shiny thing. "What is a copper?" Liz asks. "It is money. You can buy things with it. Like candy or ice cream," Tim says. "Wow, can we buy candy or ice cream with this copper?" Liz says. "Maybe. But we have to ask Mom first," Tim says. They run to the house with the copper. They see Mom in the kitchen. She is making a cake. She has a big white bowl and a big white spoon. She is mixing something in the bowl. "Mom, Mom, look what we found!" Tim says. He holds up the copper. "Where did you get that?" Mom asks. "We found it in the yard. It is a copper. It is money. Can we buy candy or ice cream with it?" Liz says. Mom looks at the copper. She sees the face on it. She sees the date on it. She gasps. She drops the bowl and the spoon. They make a loud noise. The cake mix spills on the floor. "Mom, what's wrong?" Tim asks. "Mom, are you okay?" Liz asks. Mom picks up the copper. She holds it in her hand. She looks at Tim and Liz. She smiles. She hugs them. "Kids, you found a very special copper. This is not just any copper. This is a very old copper. It is worth a lot of money. More than candy or ice cream. More than a hundred coppers," Mom says. "Really? How much?" Tim asks. "Maybe a thousand coppers. Maybe more. We have to take it to a place where they know about old things. They will tell us how much it is worth," Mom says. "Wow, that's amazing!" Liz says. "Can we still buy candy or ice cream?" Tim asks. "Of course, you can. You can buy whatever you want. You are very lucky kids. You found a treasure," Mom says. They all laugh and hug. They are very happy. They do not panic. They do not care about the cake. They have a copper. A very special copper. A very old copper. A very shiny copper.
