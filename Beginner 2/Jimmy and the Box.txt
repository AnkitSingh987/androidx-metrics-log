Category: Beginner
Title: Jimmy and the Box

Jimmy was a chubby three year-old. He enjoyed playing in his bedroom for hours at a time. One day, he was excited to find a big, tall bookcase filled with lots of interesting books. He pushed it to the side and saw something underneath. He was so excited, he wanted to see what it was. Jimmy shouted, “Mommy, Daddy, come quick! There is something under the bookcase!” His mom and dad came into the room, and they were so surprised to see a huge box. “What could it be?” his mom asked. They opened the lid, and to their surprise, Jimmy saw a brand new toy inside! “Wow!” he yelled, “It’s a toy truck!” Jimmy took the toy out and started playing with it. But then something strange happened. Jimmy accidentally hit the bookcase with the toy truck and it started to move! “Oh no!” his dad said. He quickly put his hand on the bookcase and struck it with his palm to make it stay still. Jimmy was relieved, but he was still happy to have his new toy. He hugged it and thanked his mom and dad for the surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big house with many stairs. Tim did not like the stairs because they were scary. He did not know how to go up and down the stairs. One day, Tim's mom said, "I will help you know how to use the stairs." Tim was happy. They went to the stairs and held hands. Tim's mom showed him how to go up and down the stairs. Tim was not scared anymore. Now, Tim knew how to use the stairs. He was not scared of them. He could go up and down the stairs all by himself. Tim was very happy, and he played in his big house every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia had a rare toy. It was a soft, red ball. She loved her ball very much. One day, Mia was playing with her ball. She liked to rub the ball with her hands. It felt nice to touch. Mia's friend, Tim, came to play with her. "Can I touch your ball?" asked Tim. Mia said, "Yes, but be gentle. It is rare." Tim rubbed the ball too. He liked how it felt. They played with the ball all day. They were very happy. Mia and Tim learned that sharing can be fun.

Tom and Mia are playing with paints. They like to make pictures with their hands and fingers. They dip their palms in red, blue, yellow and green. They laugh and clap and make a mess. But then, Mom comes in. She sees the paints on the wall, on the floor, on the table and on the kids. She is not happy. She says, "What are you doing? You are crazy! You need to wipe this off right now!" Tom and Mia are scared. They do not want Mom to be mad. They look for a cloth to wipe the paints. But they cannot find one. They only see more paints. They have an idea. They dip their palms in more paints. They run to Mom and give her a big hug. Mom feels the wet paints on her clothes and hair. She is shocked. She looks at Tom and Mia. They smile and say, "We love you, Mom! You are our best picture!" Mom does not know what to say. She is still mad, but she is also amused. She hugs them back and says, "You are crazy, but you are also sweet. Now, let's go and wipe this off together."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play together. One day, they found a big cookie. They both wanted the cookie, but they did not know how to share it. Tim said, "Let's split the cookie so we both can have some." But Sam did not know what "split" meant. He was a little ignorant about that word. Tim saw that Sam did not understand. So, Tim showed Sam how to break the cookie in half. They each took a piece and ate it. It was yummy! They were happy they could share the cookie. From that day on, Sam knew what "split" meant and they played together and shared even more.
