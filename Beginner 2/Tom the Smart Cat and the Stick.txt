Category: Beginner
Title: Tom the Smart Cat and the Stick

One day, a smart cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a spark on it. The spark was shiny and pretty. Tom wanted to grab the spark and show it to his friends. Tom jumped and tried to grab the spark, but it was too high. He thought of a plan. Tom found a long stick and used it to reach the spark. He was very smart to think of that. With the stick, Tom was able to grab the spark. He showed it to his friends, and they all liked it. They played with the spark all day and had lots of fun. Tom was happy he could share the spark with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a turkey who wanted to hide. She wanna be safe, so she hurried to find a good place. She saw a big tree with lots of leaves. She thought it was the perfect spot! The turkey waddled up the tree and hid in the leaves. From her secret spot, she could see all around. She felt happy and safe. But then, she heard someone coming! It was a big man with a big net. He kept walking around the tree, looking for the turkey. She hurried back deeper into the leaves and hid even more. The man looked and looked, but he didn't find the turkey. Finally, he gave up and left. The turkey breathed a sigh of relief! She was safe and hidden, and very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird named Blue. Blue lived in a big tree near a small house. The house had a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with Blue. One day, Tim found a piece of coal on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to Blue. Blue was very curious about the coal. Tim and Blue decided to take the coal and keep it close to them. They played with the coal all day long. They took turns holding it and looking at it. When it was time to go to bed, Tim put the coal in a small box. He put the box close to his bed so he could see it when he woke up. Blue flew back to his big tree and slept there. They both had a fun day and knew they would have more fun days together.

Lily and Ben were going to a hotel with their mom and dad. They were very happy because they had never been to a hotel before. They wanted to see the big bed, the TV, and the pool. When they got to the hotel, they had to wait in a line to check in. Lily and Ben were bored and started to play with each other. They ran around the lobby, making loud noises and bumping into people. Their mom and dad told them to stop, but they did not listen. Suddenly, Lily tripped over a suitcase and fell down. She hit her head on the floor and started to cry. Ben laughed at her and pointed. He said, "Ha ha, Lily is so clumsy! She embarrassed herself in front of everyone!" Lily felt hurt by Ben's words. She also felt hurt by the bump on her head. She wanted to hide under a chair. She wished she was not at the hotel. Their mom and dad rushed to Lily and hugged her. They said, "Are you okay, Lily? We're sorry you fell down. Don't mind Ben, he's just being silly. He doesn't mean to hurt you. He loves you very much." They also scolded Ben and said, "Ben, that was not nice. You should not laugh at your sister when she is hurt. You should help her and say sorry. You embarrassed us in front of everyone. How would you feel if someone laughed at you when you fell down?" Ben felt ashamed and sorry. He realized he was mean to Lily. He went to Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was wrong. You're not clumsy, you're brave. I don't want to hurt you. I love you very much." Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, Ben. I forgive you. You're not silly, you're funny. I don't want to be mad at you. I love you very much." They hugged each other and said sorry to their mom and dad. Their mom and dad smiled and said, "That's better. We're proud of you. Now let's go to our room and have some fun. We're here to enjoy the hotel, not to make trouble." Lily and Ben nodded and followed their mom and dad. They were happy again and looked forward to seeing the big bed, the TV, and the pool. They learned to be kind and careful at the hotel.
