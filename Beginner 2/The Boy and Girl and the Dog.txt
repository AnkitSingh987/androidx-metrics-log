Category: Beginner
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Dog

One day, a boy and a girl went to play. They saw a big, dull rock. The boy said, "Let's act like we are in a play!" The girl said, "Okay!" They acted like they were in a play. The boy was a king and the girl was a queen. They had fun, but then they saw a big threat. A big dog came running to them. The boy said, "Oh no! What do we do?" The girl said, "Let's act like we are not here!" They stood very still. The dog came closer and closer. They thought the dog would go away, but it did not. The dog barked and jumped on them. The boy and the girl ran away, but they were very sad. They could not play anymore because of the big dog.

Once upon a time, there was a shy bird named Tim. Tim was scared to talk to other birds. He lived in a big tree with his family. One day, Tim saw a big red ball stuck in his tree. He wanted to play with it, but he was too shy to ask for help. Tim's sister, Sue, saw him looking at the ball. She said, "Tim, do you want the ball? I can help you get it." Tim was scared to yell for help, but Sue said, "It's okay, Tim. Just yell 'help' and our friends will come." Tim took a deep breath and yelled, "Help!" Soon, all the birds came to see what was wrong. They saw the red ball and worked together to get it down. Tim was so happy to have the ball and new friends. He learned that it was okay to ask for help and not be shy. From that day on, Tim was not scared to talk to other birds and play with them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to chew on her lollipop. One day, she found an interesting lollipop under her bed. It was big and had many colors. Lily was very happy and started to chew on it. As Lily chewed on the interesting lollipop, something unexpected happened. The lollipop started to talk! It said, "Hello, Lily! I am a magic lollipop. If you chew me, I can make your wishes come true." Lily was surprised and thought about what she wanted. Lily wished to have a new friend. She chewed on the magic lollipop and then, a cute puppy appeared in her room! The puppy wagged its tail and licked Lily's face. Lily was so happy and they played all day long. From that day on, Lily and her new puppy friend were always together. And the magic lollipop stayed hidden under her bed, waiting for the next wish.

Once upon a time, there was a good little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play near the creek by her house. The creek had many fish and rocks. Mia loved to watch the fish swim and listen to the water. One day, Mia had a fun idea. She wanted to record the sounds of the creek. She took a small box that could record sounds. Mia pressed a button and the box started to record. Mia was very happy. Mia took the box home and played the sounds for her mom and dad. They all listened to the water and the fish. Mia's mom and dad thought it was a good idea. They all smiled and had a good time.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to climb the big trees and swing on the branches. They saw a big trunk near the fence. It was brown and had a lock on it. "Let's see what's inside!" Ben said. He ran to the trunk and tried to open it. "No, Ben, don't!" Lily said. She was scared. "Maybe it belongs to someone. Maybe it has something bad inside." But Ben did not listen. He found a stick and hit the lock until it broke. He opened the trunk and looked inside. He saw a lot of shiny things. He saw coins and jewels and crowns and rings. "Wow!" Ben said. "We are lucky! We found a treasure! Let's take it home!" He grabbed a big sack from the trunk and filled it with the shiny things. He lifted the sack and put it on his back. It was very heavy. "Come on, Lily, let's go!" Ben said. He started to walk to the gate. But Lily did not move. She saw a man coming from the other side of the park. He was big and angry. He had a hat and a coat and a beard. He saw Ben and Lily and the trunk and the sack. "Hey, you!" the man shouted. "That's my trunk! That's my treasure! Give it back to me!" He ran to Ben and Lily. He reached for the sack. Ben and Lily were scared. They dropped the sack and ran away. They ran to their mom and dad, who were sitting on a bench. "Mom, Dad, help!" Ben and Lily said. "There's a bad man chasing us! He wants to take our treasure!" Their mom and dad looked at them. They looked at the man. They looked at the trunk and the sack. They did not understand. "What treasure?" their mom and dad said. "What are you talking about?"
