Category: Beginner
Title: John and Jane and the Tree

Once upon a time there were two friends called John and Jane. They liked to smile and play together. One day, they decided to climb a high tree together. Jane was scared, but John said not to worry. He said, "Let's start at the bottom and climb slowly." So the two friends climbed, step by step. They were getting quite high and it started to get hard. Jane got scared and said she wanted to go down. But then John smiled and told her to keep going. Just then they heard a voice above them. It was an old tree spirit. It said, "Well done, brave friends. Never give up. Never be scared. Always smile and keep going." John and Jane smiled and continued to climb until they reached the top. They felt proud of themselves and thanked the spirit for the help. They had learnt a valuable lesson: never give up and be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat soup. His mom made him soup every day. Tim had a tight hat that he wore when he ate his soup. The hat was red and blue, and it made him very happy. One day, Tim was eating his soup when he saw a big cat outside. The cat was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he put on his tight hat and ran outside. He tried to climb the tree, but he was too small. Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He took a deep breath and started to yell for help. His mom heard him and came running. She saw the cat in the tree and helped Tim get the cat down. Tim was so happy that his mom helped him. They went back inside and finished their soup together.

Once upon a time, there was a fat panda named Pandy. Pandy lived in a big forest with his family. He loved to eat bamboo and play with his friends. One day, Pandy went for a walk in the forest. As Pandy walked, he saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to climb the tree. Pandy climbed up the tree and looked around. He saw a big bird and said, "Hi, bird!" But the bird got scared and flew away. Pandy was sad and missed his new friend. Then, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake! Pandy was scared and held on tight. The tree was not a tree at all, but a tall, friendly giant! The giant said, "Hello, Pandy! I was asleep, but now I am awake. Let's be friends!" Pandy was happy and didn't miss the bird anymore. He had a new friend, the giant, and they played together in the big forest.

One day, a thin cat named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to learn new things. He walked to the west, where the sun goes down. As he walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb it. When he was up in the tree, he saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi! I am Ben. What are you doing up here?" Tim said, "I am learning new things. I have never been up in a tree before." Ben said, "That's fun! I can teach you how to fly, too!" Tim was excited. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim tried to fly, but he couldn't. He fell down from the tree. But, Ben was quick. He called all his bird friends to help. They caught Tim and put him on the ground. Tim was safe. He learned that not all cats can fly, but he made new friends.

One day, a big dog named Max and a small cat named Lily went to a high tree. They wanted to have a fun day. Max said, "I will serve us some toast to eat up here." Max put the toast on a plate and gave some to Lily. They were very happy. They ate the toast and talked. Max said, "I like to run fast." Lily said, "I like to climb trees." Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew by and took their toast! Max and Lily were sad. They wanted their toast back. So, they asked the bird, "Please give us our toast back." The bird said, "I'm sorry. I was hungry too." The bird gave them their toast back. Max, Lily, and the bird all shared the toast. They became good friends and had a great day in the high tree.
