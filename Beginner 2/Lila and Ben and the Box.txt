Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Box

Lila and Ben like to play with blocks. They make towers and bridges and castles with the blocks. They have fun and use their imagination. One day, they find a new block in their toy box. It is shiny and has a button on it. Lila and Ben are curious. They wonder what the block does. They put the block on top of their tower and press the button. Suddenly, they see a spark. The spark is bright and colorful. It makes a loud noise. Lila and Ben are surprised and excited. They think the block is powerful and magic. They press the button again and again. They see more sparks. The sparks fly around the room. They make shapes and patterns. Lila and Ben laugh and clap. They like the block and the sparks. They show the block and the sparks to their mom and dad. Their mom and dad are amazed and proud. They hug Lila and Ben and tell them they are clever and creative. They say the block and the sparks are a special gift. Lila and Ben are happy and grateful. They share the block and the sparks with their friends. They make more towers and bridges and castles with the blocks. They have fun and use their imagination.

Tom has a yellow pen. He likes his yellow pen very much. He uses it to draw and write on his paper. He also likes to polish his yellow pen with a cloth. He thinks his yellow pen is very shiny and pretty. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom and dad. He brings his yellow pen and his paper. He sees a big slide and a swing. He wants to play on them. He puts his yellow pen and his paper on a bench. He runs to the slide and slides down. He laughs and has fun. But when he comes back to the bench, his yellow pen is gone. He looks around and sees a boy holding his yellow pen. The boy is trying to polish the yellow pen with his shirt. Tom feels angry and sad. He runs to the boy and says, "That is my yellow pen! Give it back!" The boy looks at Tom and says, "No, this is my yellow pen! I found it on the bench. It is shiny and pretty. I want to keep it." Tom and the boy start to fight over the yellow pen. They pull and tug and shout. They do not listen to each other. They do not share. Mom and dad hear the noise and come to the bench. They see Tom and the boy fighting over the yellow pen. They are not happy. They separate them and ask them what is wrong. Tom and the boy tell their stories. Mom and dad look at the yellow pen. They see a name on it. It says, "Tom". They show the name to the boy and say, "This is Tom's yellow pen. He wrote his name on it. He brought it from home. He did not lose it. You took it from the bench. That is not nice. You should say sorry and give it back." The boy feels ashamed and sorry. He says, "I'm sorry, Tom. I did not see your name on the yellow pen. I thought it was no one's. I liked it because it was shiny and pretty. But it is yours. Here, you can have it back." Tom feels happy and relieved. He says, "Thank you, boy. I'm sorry I fought with you. I like my yellow pen very much. It is shiny and pretty. But I can share it with you. Do you want to draw and write with me?" The boy smiles and nods. He says, "Yes, Tom. I want to draw and write with you. Thank you for sharing your yellow pen with me. You are nice." Tom and the boy become friends. They sit on the bench and use the yellow pen and the paper. They draw and write together. They also polish the yellow pen with the cloth. They make it shiny and pretty. They have fun. Mom and dad watch them and smile. They are proud of Tom and the boy. They are glad they learned to share and be kind. They think the yellow pen is a good pen. It helped them make a new friend.
