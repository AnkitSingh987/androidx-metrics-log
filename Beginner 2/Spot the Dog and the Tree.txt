Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a leg that hurt. He was not ready to play with his friends. Spot felt sad. One day, Spot saw a big tree. He began to wonder if he could climb it. With a big jump, Spot tried to climb the tree. It was hard, but Spot did not give up. After trying many times, Spot finally climbed the tree. He was so happy! His leg felt better, and he was ready to play. Spot and his friends played all day long. They had so much fun!

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a cold day, but Tim wore a big coat to keep warm. He saw his best friend, Sam, playing with a toy engine. Tim thought the engine was very cool and wanted to play with it too. Tim asked Sam, "Can I play with your engine, please?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, we can play together!" So, Tim and Sam played with the engine all day. They made the engine go fast and slow, and had lots of fun. As the sun went down, it became colder. Tim and Sam decided to go inside their warm homes. They said goodbye and promised to play with the engine again the next day. Tim was happy to have a friend like Sam to play with on cold days.

One day, a big lion and a small cat met in a valley. The lion was very mean and wanted to fight the small cat. The cat was scared, but he was brave too. The lion said, "I will fight you, little cat." The small cat looked at the lion and said, "I don't want to fight you. I want to be your friend." The lion laughed and said, "You are too small to be my friend." Just then, a big net fell from the sky and trapped the lion. The lion was scared and did not know what to do. The small cat saw that the lion was in trouble and wanted to help. The cat used his sharp teeth to cut the net and free the lion. The lion was very happy and thanked the small cat. He felt ashamed for wanting to fight the cat. They became good friends and played together in the valley every day. And the lion was never mean again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They had a big, rough dog named Max. Max loved to play with Lily all day long. One day, Lily and Max went outside to play. They saw a big bowl of rice. Lily's mom said, "Finish the rice, and you can play more." So, Lily and Max sat down and ate the rice. It was yummy and they were happy. After they finished the rice, Lily and Max played all day. They ran and jumped and had a lot of fun. When the sun went down, they went back inside the house. They were tired, but they had a great day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to help his mom. His mom had a task to mail a letter. The letter was for Tim's grandma. It was a sunny and dry day outside. Tim and his mom walked to the mailbox. Tim held the letter in his hand. He was excited to help. On the way, they saw a cat. The cat said, "Meow!" Tim said, "Hi, cat! I am helping my mom mail a letter." They reached the mailbox. Tim's mom said, "Great job, Tim! Now, put the letter in the mailbox." Tim put the letter in the mailbox. He felt happy doing the task. They walked back home and played in the yard.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a yellow cushion in her room. She loved the soft, yellow cushion so much. She wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. So, Mia went to Tom's house with the cushion. "Look, Tom!" Mia said, "I found a yellow cushion. It is so soft and pretty!" Tom looked at the cushion and smiled. "Wow, Mia! That's a nice cushion. Can I hold it?" Mia gave the cushion to Tom, and he held it tight. Just then, the cushion started to talk! "Hi, Mia and Tom! I am a magic cushion! I can help you learn new things." Mia and Tom were very surprised. They did not know cushions could talk. They asked the cushion to teach them something new. The magic cushion taught them how to count to ten. Mia and Tom were very happy. They thanked the magic cushion and played with it all day. From that day on, Mia and Tom were excited to learn more and more with their new friend, the magic yellow cushion.
