Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Calm Cat and the Park

One day, a calm cat named Kitty was walking in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Kitty liked to walk in the park because it made her happy. Suddenly, the sky turned dark and there was a loud noise. It was lightning! Kitty got scared and started to run. She wanted to find a safe place to hide from the lightning. Kitty saw a big tree and ran to it. She slapped her tail on the ground to make herself feel brave. The lightning went away and the sky was calm again. Kitty was happy that she found a safe place and could enjoy the rest of her day in the park.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play together. They had a big toy box with many toys, but their favorite was a soft brown bear. They named him Teddy and took him everywhere. They hugged him, talked to him, and shared their secrets with him. One night, their mom said, "It's time for bed, my loves. Tomorrow is a special day. We are going to the zoo to see the real bears and other animals. You need to sleep well and wake up early." Lily and Ben were very excited. They brushed their teeth, put on their pajamas, and climbed into their beds. They each took Teddy and gave him a kiss. "Good night, Teddy," they said. "We can't wait to see the zoo with you." But in the middle of the night, Ben woke up. He was thirsty and wanted some water. He got out of his bed and walked to the kitchen. He forgot to take Teddy with him. He drank some water and went back to his room. He was sleepy and did not notice that he had left Teddy on the floor. In the morning, mom came to their room and said, "Wake up, wake up, my loves. It's time to go to the zoo. Get dressed and come downstairs. I have made some delicious pancakes for you." Lily and Ben jumped out of their beds and ran to the closet. They put on their clothes and shoes and ran downstairs. They smelled the pancakes and their mouths watered. They sat at the table and ate their breakfast. They thanked mom and said, "We are ready to go to the zoo. Where is Teddy?" Mom looked around and said, "I don't know. Where did you leave him last night?" Lily and Ben looked at each other and gasped. They remembered that they had Teddy in their beds. They ran upstairs and searched their room. They could not find Teddy anywhere. They started to cry and said, "We lost Teddy. We can't go to the zoo without him. He is our best friend." Mom hugged them and said, "Don't worry, my loves. We will find Teddy. Maybe he is hiding somewhere. Let's look for him together." They looked under the beds, in the closet, in the drawers, in the toy box, but they could not find Teddy. They were about to give up when Ben saw something on the floor. It was Teddy. He had fallen out of his hand when he went to the kitchen. He picked him up and said, "I found him, I found him. He was here all along. I'm sorry, Lily. I forgot to take him with me. I'm sorry, Teddy. I did not mean to leave you." Lily hugged him and said, "It's okay, Ben. I'm glad you found him. I'm sorry, Teddy. We love you." Mom smiled and said, "I'm proud of you, my loves. You found Teddy and you said sorry. That's very nice of you. Now, let's go to the zoo. I'm sure Teddy will enjoy it too." Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Yes, mom. Let's go to the zoo. We are happy now." They took Teddy and held him tight. They went to the car and drove to the zoo. They saw the real bears and other animals. They had a lot of fun. They reminded Teddy that he was their favorite toy and that they would never lose him again.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books and cushions. One day, they decide to play dress up. They look for funny things to wear. Tim finds a big hat and a long scarf. He puts them on and says, "Look at me, I am a wizard! I can do magic with my hat and scarf!" Kim finds a pair of sunglasses and a necklace. She puts them on and says, "Look at me, I am a star! I can sing and dance with my sunglasses and necklace!" They laugh and pretend to be a wizard and a star. They have fun. But then, they see their cat, Max. Max is lazy. He likes to sleep on the cushions. He does not like to play. He does not like to wear anything. Tim and Kim have an idea. They want to dress up Max. They sneak up to him and try to put the hat and the scarf and the sunglasses and the necklace on him. But Max does not like it. He wakes up and meows. He shakes his head and his body. He makes the hat and the scarf and the sunglasses and the necklace fall off. He jumps off the cushions and runs away. Tim and Kim are sad. They say, "Sorry, Max. We just wanted to play with you. We did not mean to make you mad." Max comes back. He rubs his head against Tim and Kim. He purrs. He says, "It's okay. I forgive you. But I don't like to wear anything. I like to be a cat." Tim and Kim hug Max. They say, "We love you, Max. You are a good cat. You don't have to wear anything. You can be yourself." They put the hat and the scarf and the sunglasses and the necklace back in their box. They pick up some cushions and make a cozy bed for Max. They cuddle with him and read a book. They are happy.
