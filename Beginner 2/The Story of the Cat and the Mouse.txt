Category: Beginner
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Mouse

One day, a big cat and a small mouse were in the garden. The cat was very hungry. He saw a salad on the ground. The cat said, "Mouse, I am weak and hungry. I want to eat this salad." The mouse said, "I have a magic word that will make the salad shrink. Then, we can both eat it." The cat agreed because he was weak and hungry. The mouse said the magic word and the salad began to shrink. The cat and the mouse ate the small salad. But the cat was still hungry. He looked at the mouse and ate him too. The cat felt bad for eating his friend. The moral of the story is: do not let your hunger make you do bad things to your friends.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in the living room. Sara has a big doll with a pink dress. Ben has a small car with four wheels. One day, Sara and Ben are bored. They want to do something new. They see a big book on the shelf. The book has many pictures and words. Sara and Ben want to look at the book. They climb on the couch and reach for the book. But the book is too heavy for them. They pull and pull, but the book does not move. They pull harder and harder, but the book does not move. They pull so hard that the book falls down. The book hits the floor with a loud bang. Some of the pages tear. Sara and Ben are scared. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees the book on the floor and the torn pages. She is angry. She says to Sara and Ben, "What did you do? You broke the book! That was a special book from Grandma. You are very naughty!" Sara and Ben are sad. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We just wanted to see the pictures. We did not mean to break the book." Mom sighs. She says, "I know you did not mean to break the book, but you should not touch things that are not yours. You should ask me first. Now the book is ruined. How can we fix it?" Sara and Ben think. They say, "We can glue the pages back. We can draw new pictures. We can say sorry to Grandma." Mom smiles. She says, "That is a good idea. But first, you need to clean up the mess. And then, you need to take a nap. You are tired from playing. Maybe later, we can have some cocoa and cookies. Do you like that?" Sara and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, Mom. We like cocoa and cookies. They are yummy. We love you, Mom." Mom hugs Sara and Ben. She says, "I love you too, my chubby twins. Now, let's clean up and go to bed."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, while playing, Tim found a shiny penny on the floor. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his mom. Before he could show his mom, he saw a dry plant in a pot. Tim wanted to help the plant. He thought, "Maybe the penny can make the plant feel better." So, he put the penny in the pot and started to reverse his toy car around the pot. He did this because he believed the car would make the penny work. As Tim played, something unexpected happened. The plant started to grow! It became green and big. Tim's mom saw this and was amazed. She told Tim that the plant grew because he cared for it. The penny was just a small part of it, but his love made the plant healthy. The moral of the story is that love and care can make things better, even if we think it's just a small thing we do.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom and dad around the house. One day, her mom asked her to polish the table. Lily was so happy to help. She took a soft cloth and made the table shiny and clean. In the kitchen, Lily saw a big bin. It was full of things that her family did not need anymore. Lily thought the bin was very useful. It helped keep their home neat and tidy. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. They had fun playing with toys and running around. After they finished playing, Lily said, "Let's clean up! We can put the toys we don't need in the bin. It is useful." Tim agreed and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They put the toys in the bin and felt proud of their clean room.
