Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Frog

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to dig in the sand and look for shiny things. Sometimes they found coins, buttons, or shells. But today, they found something very special. "Look, Ben, look!" Lila shouted, holding up a small, sparkling stone. "It's a gem!" Ben ran over to see. He was amazed by the gem. It was blue and green and purple, and it glittered in the sun. He wanted to touch it. "Can I see it, Lila?" he asked. Lila smiled and handed him the gem. She was happy to share it with her friend. Ben felt the gem in his hand. It was smooth and cool. He liked it a lot. "Thank you, Lila," he said. "This is a very nice gem. Do you want to keep it or do you want to trade it?" Lila thought for a moment. She liked the gem, but she also liked Ben. She wanted him to be happy too. "I want to trade it," she said. "What do you have to trade?" Ben looked in his pocket. He had a red car, a yellow ball, and a green frog. He liked them all, but he liked the gem more. "How about the frog?" he offered. "It can hop and croak. It's fun to play with." Lila looked at the frog. It was cute and bright. She liked frogs. She nodded and accepted the trade. "OK, Ben, I'll take the frog. Here's the gem. Thank you for trading with me." They swapped their toys and hugged each other. They were both happy with their new treasures. They continued to play in the park, enjoying the sun, the sand, and their friendship.

Once upon a time there was a cop. He was out walking in the empty park. Suddenly, he felt something on his shoulder. He turned around and saw a little girl. With a big smile, she gave him a kiss. The cop laughed. He couldn't believe how loveable the little girl was. The cop said "Thank you, sweetheart!" The little girl smiled again, but then started to cry. She said "I'm alone, I'm so scared!" The cop felt sad for the little girl. He said "I can keep you safe, don't worry". He hugged her and asked her name. But then, the cop heard a loud noise. It was another cop! He had come to arrest the little girl. The cop was sad and angry. He had wanted to keep the little girl safe and happy but now, he had to say goodbye. He gave the little girl a last kiss, and the other cop drove her away. The cop was left alone in the empty park, feeling sad and helpless.

Once upon a time, there was a baby. The baby was a regular baby, just like other babies. The baby liked to play and laugh all day. The baby had a toy that could turn around and around. One day, the baby saw a big ball. The ball was turning too! The baby wanted to play with the big ball. The baby crawled to the ball and tried to make it turn like the toy. The baby pushed the ball with little hands, and the ball began to turn. The baby was so happy to see the ball turn. The baby laughed and clapped hands. The baby played with the big ball all day long. The baby and the ball had lots of fun together. And that's the end of the story.

One day, a gray frog named Bob lived by a pond. He liked to jump and play all day. The pond was his favorite place to be. Bob had a friend named Sam, who was a fish. They loved to play together in the water. One day, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. Bob and Sam were scared. They went under a big leaf to hide. The rain made the pond very big and full. The water was moving fast, and it was hard for them to swim. Bob and Sam saw a big tree near the pond. They knew they had to get to the tree to be safe. They swam and swam until they reached the tree. They held on tight to the tree, and the rain stopped. The sky turned blue again, and the sun came out. They were so happy they made it to the tree. They went back to play in the pond, knowing they were safe and had a place to go if it rained again.
