Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a big red ball in the open. She ran to the ball and gave it a big squeeze. It made a loud sound, like a big "POP!" Mia was scared and started to cry. Her mom came to her and said, "Don't worry, Mia. It's just a balloon." Mia looked at the red thing and saw it was not a ball, but a big balloon. Mia and her mom went to find more balloons. They found blue, yellow, and green ones. Mia learned to give them a little squeeze, not a big one, and they did not make a loud sound. Mia was happy, and she and her mom played with the balloons all day.

Tom and Lily wanted to go to the theater. They liked to watch funny people and animals on the big screen. They asked their mom if they could go. "Mom, can we go to the theater today?" Tom said. "Maybe, if you are good and do your chores," Mom said. Tom and Lily nodded and ran to their rooms. They cleaned their toys and made their beds. Then they came back to Mom. "Mom, we are done. Can we go now?" Lily said. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. But first, let me mention something. You have to eat your lunch before we go. And no candy at the theater, okay?" Tom and Lily groaned. They did not like lunch. It was sour soup and bread. They wanted candy and popcorn at the theater. "Mom, please, can we have candy?" Tom said. "No, you can't. Candy is bad for your teeth and your tummy. You have to eat your lunch and drink your water. Then we can go," Mom said. Tom and Lily did not want to argue with Mom. They knew she loved them and wanted them to be healthy. They ate their lunch and drank their water. They made sour faces, but they did not complain. "Mom, we are done. Can we go now?" Lily said. Mom checked their plates and said, "Yes, you can. You are good kids. Let's go to the theater and have some fun." Tom and Lily cheered and hugged Mom. They put on their coats and shoes and followed Mom to the car. They were excited to go to the theater and watch a funny movie. They hoped it would not be sour.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide tree. Under the tree lived a small, fast rabbit. The rabbit loved to run with great speed around the tree all day long. One day, the rabbit met a slow, big turtle. The turtle said, "I want to be fast like you." The rabbit thought for a moment and then suggested, "Let's have a race around the wide tree. I will show you how to be fast." So, the rabbit and the turtle raced around the wide tree. The rabbit ran with great speed, while the turtle moved very slow. After the race, the turtle was happy to learn from the rabbit. They became good friends and played together every day under the big, wide tree.

Once upon a time, in a small light room, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to move and play all day. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was funny and liked to tell jokes. Tim said, "Let's move and play together!" Sam agreed and said, "Yes, and I will tell you a joke too!" So, they moved and played together all day. But then, they started to argue about who could tell the best joke. "I can tell a better joke than you," said Tim. "No, I can tell a better joke," said Sam. They were both upset and stopped playing. Tim's mom saw them and said, "Why are you both so sad? You should be having fun!" Tim and Sam told her about their fight. Tim's mom said, "Why don't you both tell a joke and then laugh together?" So, Tim and Sam each told a joke and laughed together. They realized that it didn't matter who could tell the best joke. They were happy to be friends and play together again. And from that day on, they moved, played, and told jokes together, always having fun.
