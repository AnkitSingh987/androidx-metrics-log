Category: Beginner
Title: Sam the Hairy Dog and the Bird

One day, a big hairy dog named Sam found a piece of paper on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a little girl named Lily. Sam gave the paper to Lily and she looked at it. It had a picture of a person clapping their hands. "Look, Sam! The person is clapping," said Lily. She clapped her hands too, and Sam barked happily. They decided to show the paper to their friends. As they walked to their friends, the wind blew the paper out of Lily's hand. It flew up into a tree. Sam tried to jump and get it, but he was too short. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a friendly bird saw them and flew down. The bird picked up the paper with its beak and gave it back to Lily. "Thank you, bird!" Lily said, and she clapped her hands again. Sam, Lily, and the bird all became friends and played together. The unexpected help from the bird made their day even more fun.

One day, a driver named Tom was driving his big bus. He was feeling anxious because the bus was making a loud noise. He wanted to fix the bus so that it would be quiet and nice for the people inside. Tom stopped the bus and looked at the wheels. He saw that one wheel was not round. It was a square wheel! The square wheel was making the loud noise. Tom was sad because he didn't know how to fix it. Then, a little girl from the bus came to help. She said, "I can fix it!" She took out a small toy hammer and tapped the square wheel. Suddenly, the wheel became round! The loud noise was gone, and the bus was quiet and nice again. Tom thanked the little girl and learned that even the smallest person can help in a big way.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Mia's mom wanted to make a cake. They needed a mixer to mix all the things for the cake. But the mixer was too high for Mia to reach. Mia's mom had an idea. She got a fake mixer for Mia to play with. It was made of toy parts and was safe for her to use. Mia was very happy with her fake mixer. She pretended to mix the cake with her toy while her mom used the real mixer. Mia's mom put the cake in the oven to cook. When it was done, they took it out and let it cool. Mia and her mom put icing on the cake and shared it with the family. Everyone loved the cake and Mia was proud to help. She knew one day she would be able to reach the real mixer and make a cake all by herself.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful piano. It stood tall and proud, with its shiny black keys sparkling in the sun. All the children around the village knew about the piano, and all of them wanted to hear it play. One day, the piano was all ready to play. Everyone gathered close to it to listen, but nothing happened. Then a little 3 year old girl stepped forward and started to tap her fingers on the keys. She played a beautiful song, with a powerful and happy tune. The children who were watching were amazed. Even the birds in the trees stopped singing, so they could listen. When the song was over, the piano was quiet again. But the little girl's song had yielded something very special. The power of the piano was unlocked! Now, all of the children were free to play it whenever they wanted. It was like a magical gift, and they were all so grateful. The little girl smiled and everyone hugged her, because she had made the piano yield such wonderful music.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. Tim wants to go first. He runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. Sam follows him. "Wait for me, Tim!" Sam says. "I want to slide too!" But Tim does not wait. He slides down very fast. He is happy. He laughs and shouts. He does not see the mud at the end of the slide. He lands in the mud. His pants are dirty and wet. He feels cold and sad. "Ha ha, Tim! You are so silly!" Sam says. He points at Tim and makes fun of him. He thinks it is funny. He does not help Tim. Tim starts to cry. He cries very loud. He wants his mom. He wants new pants. He is angry at Sam. He does not like Sam. A lady hears Tim cry. She is alert. She thinks Tim is hurt. She runs to Tim and asks him what is wrong. She sees his pants and the mud. She feels sorry for Tim. She gives him a hug and a tissue. She tells him it is okay. "Who are you?" Tim asks. He stops crying. He looks at the lady. She is nice. "I am Lisa. I work at the park. I take care of the slides and the swings. Do you want to come with me? I have some clean pants for you. You can change in the bathroom. Then you can play some more." Lisa says. Tim nods. He likes Lisa. He goes with her. He is happy again. Sam sees Tim go with Lisa. He feels bad. He knows he was mean to Tim. He wants to say sorry. He runs after them. He hopes Tim will forgive him. He hopes they can still be friends.
