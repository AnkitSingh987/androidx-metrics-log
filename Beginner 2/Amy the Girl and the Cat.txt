Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cat

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing outside. She saw an orange cat. The cat was very pretty. Amy wanted to dress the cat in a fun hat. She ran inside to find one. When Amy came back, the cat was sitting by a cooler. Amy opened the cooler and found many hats inside. She was very happy. She said, "Wow, cat! You found a lot of hats!" The cat meowed. Amy tried to put a hat on the cat. But the cat jumped away. Then, the cat put a hat on Amy's head! Amy was very surprised. She laughed and said, "You're a funny cat!" They played with the hats all day.

Sandy and her mom were walking in the park one day. Sandy noticed the grass was so green and soft. She wanted to play in it! Sandy's mom told her, "No, sweetie. We can't do that. It's not our grass to play in." Sandy was sad, but she noticed a powerful bicycle. It looked super fun, so she asked her mom if she could ride it. Her mom looked a little mad and said, "No, Sandy. That bike is not ours." Just then, a man appeared and asked Sandy and her mom if they wanted to ride the bike. It was a powerful bike, and it would be fun! Sandy was so excited, but her mom said no again. But the man said, "I don't care. Go ahead, it's okay." Sandy and the man started to ride the bike. But then, the police arrived and arrested both Sandy and the man. Sandy never got to ride the bike, and she never played in the grass again.

Sam and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a man on a bench. The man is bald. He has no hair on his head. "Look, Ben, look!" Sam says. "The man has no hair!" Ben looks at the man. He is curious. He wonders why the man has no hair. "Maybe he is sick," Ben says. "Maybe he is cold." Sam shakes his head. He has an idea. "Maybe he is a magic man," Sam says. "Maybe he can do tricks." Sam and Ben walk to the man. They smile at him. They want to see his tricks. The man sees Sam and Ben. He smiles back. He likes children. He wants to make them happy. "Hello, boys," the man says. "Do you want to see some magic?" Sam and Ben nod. They are excited. The man takes out a coin from his pocket. He shows it to Sam and Ben. "Watch this," the man says. He puts the coin in his hand. He closes his hand. He opens his hand. The coin is gone. Sam and Ben gasp. They are amazed. "Where did it go?" Sam asks. The man winks at Sam. He points to his ear. He pulls out the coin from his ear. Sam and Ben laugh. They are delighted. "Wow, that is magic!" Ben says. The man smiles. He has more tricks. He takes out a scarf from his pocket. He shows it to Sam and Ben. "Watch this," the man says. He puts the scarf in his hand. He closes his hand. He opens his hand. The scarf is gone. Sam and Ben gasp again. They are astonished. "Where did it go?" Ben asks. The man winks at Ben. He points to his head. He pulls out the scarf from his head. Sam and Ben laugh again. They are thrilled. "Wow, that is magic!" Sam says. The man smiles. He is happy. He likes to make children happy. He gives the coin and the scarf to Sam and Ben. He tells them to keep them. "Thank you, magic man," Sam and Ben say. They hug the man. The man hugs them back. He tells them to have fun. Sam and Ben run to the slide. They play with the coin and the scarf. They are happy. They gaze at the man. They wave at him. They thank him again. The man waves back. He gazes at them. He is happy too.

Once there was a little girl called Mandy. She was staying in a cabin with her mummy and daddy. The cabin was open and looked out onto a beautiful lake. Mandy's mummy and daddy were very happy to be there, but Mandy was not. She frowned and scowled when they tried to explain why they had come. Mandy's mummy and daddy took her outside to play, but she still wasn't very happy. She kept frowning and running around the cabin. Eventually, her daddy picked up Mandy and cuddled her. He whispered something in her ear and she smiled. Mandy's mummy and daddy had planned a surprise for her. They were going to take her out in a boat onto the lake! Together, the three of them got into the boat and had a marvelous day on the lake. Her grumpy frown had gone away and her smile was back!
