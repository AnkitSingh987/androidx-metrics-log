Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and Ben and the Necklace

Sara and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig with their shovels and make mud pies. One day, they found a big hole under a bush. They wondered what was inside. "Let's look, Sara!" Ben said. He put his head in the hole and saw something shiny. He reached for it and pulled it out. It was a necklace with a heart pendant. "Wow, Ben, what is that?" Sara asked. She touched the necklace and saw it sparkle in the sun. "It's a treasure, Sara. Maybe a fairy left it for us," Ben said. He put the necklace around Sara's neck and smiled. Sara was very happy. She hugged Ben and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are very kind. I love the necklace. It's so pretty." Ben was happy too. He said, "You're welcome, Sara. I'm glad you like it. You are my best friend." They heard their mom calling them for lunch. They ran to the house, holding hands. They did not tell anyone about the hole or the necklace. They kept it as their secret. They were very grateful for their treasure and their friendship.

Ben and Mia were bored. They had to stay inside because it was raining. They had no toys to play with, only a dull screen. The screen showed pictures and sounds, but they were not fun. "Can we watch something else?" Mia asked. "No, this is what Mommy likes," Ben said. "She said we have to be quiet and not touch anything." Mia sighed. She looked around the room. She saw a big vase with flowers on a table. The flowers were pretty and colorful. She wanted to smell them. She got up and walked to the table. She leaned over the vase and sniffed the flowers. They smelled sweet and fresh. But then, she felt a tickle in her nose. She tried to stop it, but she could not. She sneezed. "Achoo!" The sneeze was loud and strong. It made the vase shake and fall. The vase broke into pieces on the floor. The water and the flowers spilled everywhere. Ben and Mia gasped. They knew they were in trouble. They heard Mommy's footsteps coming from the other room. "What was that?" Mommy asked. Ben and Mia looked at each other. They did not know what to say. They hoped Mommy would not be angry. They wished they had something else to do than watch the dull screen.

One sunny day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. She saw her friend, a big tree, and she wanted to play under it. Lucy loved to skip, so she skipped all the way to the tree. When Lucy got to the tree, she saw a small cat sitting under it. The cat was very patient and didn't move. Lucy said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at her and said, "Yes, I would like to play." So, Lucy and the cat played together all day. They skipped around the tree, and they laughed and had fun. When it was time to go home, Lucy said, "Bye, cat! I had a great day!" The cat said, "Me too, Lucy! See you soon!" And they both went to their homes, happy and tired from their fun day.

One day, a bird saw a weak rat on the ground. The bird wanted to help the rat. The bird said, "Hi, little rat! I can help you soar in the sky." The rat looked up and said, "Yes, please! I want to see the sky." The bird picked up the rat with its beak. They started to soar high in the sky. The rat was very happy. They saw trees, houses, and other animals from above. The rat felt strong and brave. After some time, the bird took the rat back to the ground. The rat said, "Thank you, bird! That was so fun!" The bird smiled and flew away. The rat felt happy and made new friends on the ground. They all played together and had a good day.

Tom loves trains. He has many toy trains in his room. He likes to make them go on the tracks and make noises. He knows the names of all his trains. He has a big red engine, a small blue engine, a yellow coach, a green wagon and a black caboose. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom. He sees a real train on the rails. He is very excited. He runs to the fence and points at the train. He says, "Look, mom, a train! A train!" Mom smiles and says, "Yes, Tom, that's a train. Do you like it?" Tom nods and says, "Yes, mom, I like it. It's so big and loud. Can we go closer?" Mom says, "Sure, Tom, we can go closer. But we have to be careful. Trains are fast and strong. We have to stay behind the fence and wait for the train to stop." Tom and mom walk along the fence until they reach the station. They see the train slowing down and stopping. They see the people getting off and on the train. They see the driver coming out of the engine. He wears a blue hat and a red jacket. He looks at Tom and mom and smiles. He says, "Hello, there. Do you like trains?" Tom says, "Yes, sir, I like trains. I have many toy trains in my room. I like your engine. It's big and red. What's its name?" The driver says, "Well, thank you. My engine's name is Rosie. She's a fine engine. She can pull many coaches and wagons. She can go fast and slow. She can make loud and soft noises. She's my friend." Tom says, "Wow, Rosie is a fine engine. She's your friend? Can I be your friend, too?" The driver says, "Of course, you can be my friend. And Rosie's friend, too. Do you want to see her up close?" Tom says, "Yes, please, I want to see her up close." The driver says, "OK, then. Come with me. But hold your mom's hand. And be careful. Rosie is big and hot. Don't touch her. Just look at her." Tom and mom follow the driver to the engine. Tom sees Rosie's big wheels, her shiny funnel, her black smoke, her red paint, her yellow number. He recognizes some of the parts from his toy trains. He thinks Rosie is the most beautiful engine he has ever seen. He says, "Hello, Rosie. You are a fine engine. I like you. I'm Tom. I'm your friend." Rosie whistles and smiles. She likes Tom, too. She says, "Hello, Tom. You are a fine boy. I like you. I'm Rosie. I'm your friend."
