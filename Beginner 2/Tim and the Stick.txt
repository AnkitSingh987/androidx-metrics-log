Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Stick

One day, a boy named Tim went to play golf with his dad. Tim was very excited because he loved golf. His dad gave him a big golf stick and a small ball. Tim had a big smile on his face. At the golf place, Tim saw a small hole in the ground. He was confused. He asked his dad, "Why is there a hole here?" His dad said, "You will see soon, Tim." Tim looked at the hole and thought, "I will find out why the hole is here." Tim and his dad started to play golf. They hit the ball with the golf stick. Tim tried very hard to hit the ball into the hole. He poked the ball with the stick, and the ball went into the hole! Tim was so happy. He jumped up and down. Now he knew why the hole was there. It was for the ball to go in. Tim and his dad had a fun day playing golf.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a pretty bird in a tree. The bird was singing a sweet song. Mia liked the bird and wanted to be its friend. Mia said to the bird, "Hi bird, can I be your friend?" The bird looked at Mia and said, "Yes, I would like that. But first, I have a request. Can you help me find my animal friends? They are hiding in the park." Mia was happy to help. She looked around and found a cat, a dog, and a rabbit. The bird was very happy. "Thank you, Mia," said the bird. "Now we can all play together." Mia, the bird, and the other animals played all day in the park. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Mia said goodbye to her new friends. She knew she would see them again soon.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat avocados. One day, he found a weak tree with an avocado hanging on it. Tim said to his mom, "Mom, I want the avocado from that weak tree." His mom looked at the tree and said, "No Tim, it is too weak. It might fall and hurt you." But Tim insisted, "Please, mom, I really want that avocado." His mom thought for a moment and said, "Okay Tim, but be very careful." Tim carefully climbed the weak tree and got the avocado. He was very happy and shared it with his mom. They both enjoyed the avocado together and Tim learned to always listen to his mom's advice.

One day, a green frog and a red bird were friends. They liked to play near the water. The green frog liked to pour water on the red bird. The red bird would laugh and say, "That's silly!" One day, they saw a big, stupid cat. The cat wanted to catch the red bird. The green frog had an idea. He said, "Let's pour water on the cat!" The red bird said, "Yes! Let's do it!" They poured water on the cat. The cat did not like it. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to laugh! The cat said, "That was fun! Can we be friends too?" The green frog and the red bird were happy. They all played together and became good friends.

Lily and Ben are playing with a big ball in the park. They like to roll the ball to each other and catch it. Sometimes they roll the ball very fast and it goes far away. They run after the ball and laugh. One day, their aunt comes to visit them. She is very tall and has long hair. She says she wants to play with them. Lily and Ben are happy. They show her their big ball and ask her to roll it with them. They start to play, but the aunt does not roll the ball nicely. She rolls the ball very hard and it hits Lily and Ben. They fall down and cry. The aunt does not say sorry. She laughs and says they are silly. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They want to stop playing with the aunt. They tell her to go away and leave them alone. But the aunt does not listen. She takes their big ball and runs away with it. She says it is her ball now and they cannot have it back. Lily and Ben are very upset. They have no ball and no fun. They miss their mom and dad. They wish their aunt would never come again. They hug each other and wait for their parents to come. They hope they will find their ball and make the aunt give it back. They think the aunt is very mean and rude. They do not like her at all.
