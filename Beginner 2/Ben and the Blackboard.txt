Category: Beginner
Title: Ben and the Blackboard

Ben likes to write on the blackboard. He has a big box of chalk in many colors. He writes his name, his friends' names, and some shapes. He likes to make the blackboard look pretty. But today, Ben has a problem. He wants to write on the blackboard, but there is no space. The blackboard is full of words and numbers. The teacher wrote them for the big kids. The big kids have to learn them. They are tough words and numbers. Ben does not know them. Ben is sad. He does not want to erase the teacher's words and numbers. He knows that is bad. But he also wants to write on the blackboard. He thinks hard. He has an idea. He finds a small corner of the blackboard that is empty. He takes a white chalk and writes his name. He makes it small, but neat. He is happy. He writes his name on the blackboard. The teacher sees Ben's name on the blackboard. She smiles. She knows that Ben likes to write on the blackboard. She is proud of him. She tells him that he did a good job. She says that he can write on the blackboard again, when the big kids are done. She gives him a hug. Ben is happy. He likes the teacher. He likes to write on the blackboard. He thinks that writing is fun. He wants to learn more words and numbers. He thinks that maybe they are not so tough. He thinks that maybe he can be a big kid too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He liked to play in the park and explore new places. One day, Tim found a toy pistol in his toy box. He was very excited to play with it. Tim took his toy pistol and went to the park. He played with his friends and they pretended to be pirates. They ran and jumped all around the park. They had so much fun. But then, Tim lost his toy pistol. He looked for it everywhere but could not find it. Tim was sad and went home. His mom saw him and asked what was wrong. Tim told her about the lost toy pistol. His mom gave him a big hug and said, "Don't worry, we will find it." The next day, Tim and his mom went back to the park. They looked around and found the toy pistol under a slide. Tim was so happy and thanked his mom. They went home and played with the toy pistol together.

One day, a clumsy bird named Bob was flying in the sky. He saw a twig on the ground and wanted to get it. Bob flew down to the ground and picked up the twig with his beak. Bob wanted to take the twig to his nest. He tried to zip through the air, but he was not very fast. He kept dropping the twig because he was clumsy. But Bob did not give up. He picked up the twig again and again. Finally, after many tries, Bob made it to his nest with the twig. He was very happy. He put the twig in his nest and it made the nest even better. Bob learned that even if you are clumsy, you can still do great things if you don't give up.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue saw a big purple wheel at the park. She ran to it and said, "Mom, I want to play on the purple wheel!" Sue's mom smiled and said, "Okay, have fun!" Sue climbed on the wheel and started to spin it. She waved to her mom as she went round and round. Sue's mom waved back at her. Soon, Sue saw a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to play on the purple wheel too. They took turns spinning the wheel and waving to each other. They both had a lot of fun playing together. At the end of the day, Sue and Tom waved goodbye and went home with their moms.

Once upon a time there lived a little boy called George. He loved to go fishing with his father. Today, his dad took him to the river. His father showed him how to pull the rod so that he could catch a fish. George was overjoyed when he saw a fish coming to feed on the bait. "Pull the rod now!" said his father with a smile. George pulled and pulled until the fish was almost in his hands. Then, the little fish got stuck in between two rocks. George's dad said, "Don't worry. We must be flexible if we want to get the fish." George's dad bent the rod in a circle and pulled the fish out. George learnt a valuable lesson: When you come across a difficult situation, you must be flexible to find a solution.
