Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Silly Dog and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a silly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Spot saw a piece of cake on the table. The cake looked so yummy, and it reminded him of his ball. Spot wanted the cake so much. He jumped and jumped to get the piece of cake. But he was too small. Spot felt sad and wanted to cry. He thought of a plan to get the cake. Spot saw a chair near the table. He pushed the chair close to the table and climbed up. Spot was so happy that he could reach the cake now. He grabbed the piece of cake with his mouth and ate it all. But Spot did not know that the cake was not good for him. His tummy started to hurt, and he felt very sick. Spot learned that he should not eat things that are not for him. He wished he had not eaten the cake.

One day, a big, thick cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw his friend, a little dog named Sam. Sam was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Sam?" Sam said, "I want to play, but I don't know what game to play." Tom thought for a turn and then had an idea. "Let's play a game that includes running and jumping," he said. So, Tom and Sam played the game. They took turns running and jumping. They had so much fun. After a while, they were both tired but very happy. They sat down and smiled at each other. From that day on, they always played together.

Once there was a cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a ball of thread. One day, Tom was playing with the thread when it started to end. He was sad because he loved his thread so much. He looked around for more thread, but he could not find any. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Max saw that Tom was sad and asked what was wrong. Tom told Max about the thread ending. Max said, "Don't worry, Tom! I know where to find more thread." Tom was grateful and followed Max to find more thread. Max took Tom to a big box. Inside the box, there was a lot of thread! Tom was so happy and started to play with the new thread. He was very grateful to Max for helping him find more thread. From that day on, Tom and Max played together with the thread and became best friends.

One day, a big, powerful cat saw a high tree. In the tree, there was a shiny arrow. The cat wanted the arrow very much. But the tree was too tall for the cat to reach. The cat thought and thought about how to get the arrow. Then, a small bird came and asked the cat, "Why are you sad?" The cat told the bird about the arrow in the tree. The bird wanted to help the cat. So, the bird flew up and tried to reach the arrow. But the bird was too small and could not reach it. The cat and the bird had an idea. They asked a tall giraffe to help them. The giraffe was happy to help. The giraffe stretched its long neck and reached the arrow. The cat, the bird, and the giraffe were all happy. They worked together and solved the problem.

Once upon a time, a family wanted to go on a holiday. They had a big black car. Mom, Dad, and little Tim got in the car. They were happy to drive to the beach. On the way, Dad saw a big black cloud. He said, "Oh no, it may rain soon." But they kept driving. They wanted to have fun at the beach. At the beach, the big black cloud came closer. The family played in the sand. Then, the rain started. It rained a lot. The family was sad. Their holiday was not fun because of the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a sunny day in the village. All of the kids were out playing and having fun. But one of the kids had a plan he was determined to try. He was going to rise up high. He thought if he raised his arms tight enough, he could spring off the ground and keep rising. His friends laughed when he said he could do it, but he was sure it was true. He tried over and over again, jumping and stretching, but he never managed to get off the ground. One day, he applied all of his concentration and strength together and he felt something amazing. He was lifting off the ground! He was rising higher and higher! The other kids cheered him on as he rose up in the sky! They knew he had figured out what they didn't believe was true. It was a great surprise when he rose so high! His friends knew if he could do it, they could too. The other kids spent the afternoon trying to replicate the feat, until they too could rise high in the sky.
