Category: Beginner
Title: The Tiger and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a nice tiger. He lived in a tidy place with a cool pond to swim in. He was very happy, but sometimes he wanted to roar. Every day, he tried to roar like he heard other animals do, but he couldn't. One day, the tiger was walking around his tidy place, when he heard an alarm. Then he knew what the sound was that he had been trying to make! He took a deep breath and roared as loud as he could. It sounded just like the alarm! He was so proud that he could finally roar. From then on, the tiger would roar when he heard the alarm. He would also roar when he was happy or feeling brave. He loved being able to make that sound and show how powerful he was. He was the tidy tiger with a loud roar!

Jill loves her dog Spot. Spot is a big, brown dog with a white spot on his nose. He is very charming and friendly. He likes to play with Jill and her friends. One day, Jill and Spot go to the park. Jill grabs Spot's leash and puts it on his collar. She says, "Come on, Spot, let's go for a walk!" Spot wags his tail and follows Jill. At the park, Jill sees her friend Tom. Tom has a red ball. He says, "Hi, Jill, do you want to play catch?" Jill says, "Yes, that sounds fun!" She lets go of Spot's leash and runs to Tom. Spot sees the red ball and thinks it is a toy. He runs after it and tries to grab it with his mouth. Tom throws the ball and Spot chases it. He barks and jumps. Jill and Tom laugh and clap. They think Spot is very funny and cute. They say, "Good boy, Spot, you are so charming!" Spot is happy and proud. He brings the ball back to Jill and Tom. They pet him and give him a treat. They all have a good time at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big exam at school. Lily was scared because the exam was hard. She did not want to take the exam. But her mom said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Lily's mom cared for her a lot. She helped Lily study for the exam. They looked at books and talked about the hard questions. Lily started to feel better. She said, "Thank you, Mom. I think I can do it now." The next day, Lily went to school and took the exam. She tried her best and remembered what her mom taught her. When she was done, she felt happy. She knew her mom cared for her and helped her do the hard exam. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom gave her an apron. It was a pretty apron with flowers on it. Lily was very happy. One morning, the sun was mild and warm. Lily put on her apron and went to the kitchen. She wanted to make a cake with her mom. Her mom said, "Lily, let's become great bakers today!" They mixed flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Lily stirred the mix with a big spoon. She was having so much fun. Her mom put the mix in the oven, and they waited for the cake to bake. When the cake was ready, Lily and her mom put icing on it. They made the cake look like a big flower. Lily's dad came into the kitchen and saw the cake. He said, "Wow, that is a beautiful cake!" Lily, her mom, and her dad ate the cake together. It was very yummy. Lily was proud of her pretty apron and the cake she helped make. She couldn't wait to help her mom in the kitchen again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy sword. He liked to play with it. One day, Tom went to the park. He took his sword with him. At the park, Tom saw a bell. The bell could ring. Tom wanted to make the bell ring. So, he used his sword to hit the bell. The bell made a loud ring sound. Tom was happy. A girl named Sue saw Tom and his sword. Sue asked, "Can I play too?" Tom said, "Yes, my sword is available." Sue and Tom played with the sword and rang the bell. They had a fun day at the park.
