Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the White Cat and the Butterfly

One day, a white cat named Lily went for a walk. She liked to step on the soft grass and feel the sun on her fur. As she walked, she saw her friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a toy knife. "Hi Tim!" said Lily. "What are you doing with that toy knife?" "I'm playing pirates!" said Tim. "Do you want to play too?" Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the toy knife away! Tim and Lily were sad. They wanted to play with the toy knife. But then, they saw a butterfly. The butterfly was very pretty and it flew around them. Tim and Lily forgot about the toy knife and started to play with the butterfly. They had so much fun together and became even better friends.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little town, there lived a hero. This hero was strong and kind. Everyone liked him. The hero had a friend who liked to record everything they did together. One day, the hero and his friend went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. The friend recorded their fun day. They laughed and played until the sun went down. As they were leaving the park, a big storm came. The sky was dark and scary. The hero tried to save his friend, but he was not strong enough. The storm was too strong, and the hero could not win. The friend's recording was lost, and they were both very sad. The pretty town was not so pretty anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a restless boy named Tim. He liked to run and play all day. Tim had a toy weapon. It was a big, red sword. He took it everywhere with him. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw another boy named Sam. Sam had a toy weapon too. It was a green sword. Tim and Sam wanted to play together. But they both wanted to use their own swords. Tim and Sam decided to have a game. They would see who could swing their sword the best. They swung their swords high and low. They had lots of fun. In the end, they both did a great job. Tim and Sam became friends. They played with their swords together. They learned that it was more fun to play together than to fight. And they had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to sing and dance. One day, Max saw a big stage in the park. He wanted to dance on the stage. Max was very excited. Max got on the stage and started to dance. But then, he saw many people watching him. Max started to tremble. He was scared. Max did not want to dance anymore. He wanted to run away. Then, a little girl came to Max with a delicious treat. She gave it to Max and said, "Don't be scared, Max. We like your dance." Max ate the treat and felt better. He danced and sang for everyone. They all clapped and cheered for Max.

Lily and Ben love to play in the lake. They have a kayak that is red and yellow. They take turns to paddle and steer. They see fish and ducks and frogs. They have fun. One day, they see a big rock in the water. It is shiny and smooth. Lily wants to touch it. She says to Ben, "Let me go to the rock. It is brilliant. Maybe it is a treasure." Ben says, "No, Lily. It is too far. We have to go back soon. Mom and Dad are waiting for us." But Lily does not listen. She jumps out of the kayak and swims to the rock. Ben is scared. He tries to follow Lily, but the kayak is too heavy. He calls to Lily, "Come back, Lily. Come back. The lake is deep and cold. You can get hurt." But Lily does not hear him. She reaches the rock and climbs on it. She feels the rock. It is hard and rough. She is disappointed. It is not a treasure. It is just a rock. She looks back at Ben. He is far away. She sees the kayak is tipping over. She sees water is getting in. She sees Ben is crying. She is sorry. She wants to go back to Ben. She tries to jump off the rock, but she slips. She stumbles and falls. She hits her head on the rock. She feels pain. She feels blood. She feels dizzy. She falls into the water. She sinks. She cannot breathe. She cannot see. She cannot hear. Ben sees Lily fall. He screams. He paddles hard. He tries to reach Lily. He tries to save her. But he is too late. He cannot find her. He cannot see her. He cannot hear her. He is alone. He is sad. He is lost. He wishes he never saw the rock. He wishes Lily never left the kayak. He wishes they went back to Mom and Dad. He wishes they had fun.
