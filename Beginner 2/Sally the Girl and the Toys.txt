Category: Beginner
Title: Sally the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sally was in her bedroom. She felt very lively and wanted to play. She had an idea to gather all her toys and put them in one big pile. "Mom, can I gather my toys in my bedroom?" Sally asked her mom. Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to make a big mess." Sally started to gather her toys, but she found a small box under her bed. She opened it and saw a note. The note said, "Share your toys with your friends and you will be happy." Sally thought about the note and decided to do what it said. Sally invited her friends over to play with her toys. They all had a great time and Sally felt very happy. The unexpected note taught Sally that sharing with others can make you happy.

Tim and Anna are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They run and laugh and have fun. One day, they see a dog in the park. The dog is big and brown and has a collar. The dog looks sad and lonely. Tim and Anna want to help the dog. "Hello, dog," Tim says. "What is your name? Do you have a home?" The dog wags his tail and licks Tim's hand. He barks softly. He likes Tim and Anna. "His name is Max," a voice says. "He is my dog. He ran away from me. I was looking for him. Thank you for finding him." Tim and Anna turn around and see a man. The man is wearing a hat and a coat. He has a leash in his hand. He smiles at Tim and Anna. "Are you Max's owner?" Anna asks. "We are glad you found him. He is a nice dog." "Yes, I am his owner," the man says. "He is a nice dog, but he is also very curious. Sometimes he chases squirrels and birds and gets lost. I am sorry if he scared you." "He did not scare us," Tim says. "We like dogs. We were playing with him. He is very friendly." The man hugs Max and puts the leash on him. He thanks Tim and Anna again. He says he has to go home now. He invites them to visit Max anytime. "Bye, Max," Tim and Anna say. "We hope to see you again." "Bye, Tim and Anna," Max barks. "You are good friends." Tim and Anna wave goodbye to Max and his owner. They feel happy and peaceful. They go back to playing with their ball and their kite. They have a good day.

Once upon a time there was a friendly lady who was packing her luggage. She couldn't decide which of her things she should take. Suddenly she heard a small voice coming from behind her. “What are you doing?” the voice asked. The friendly lady turned around and saw a little boy. “I’m packing,” she said with a smile. “I’m getting ready to go on a trip.” The little boy smiled back. “What’s that?” he asked, pointing at the lady’s luggage. “This is my luggage,” the lady said. “It’s a box where I put my clothes and other things. I have to close it now so I can take it with me.” The little boy nodded and watched as the lady carefully closed her luggage. “Bye!” he said as the lady picked up her luggage and walked out the door. The friendly lady smiled and waved. “Goodbye! Have a nice day!”

John saw a street and wanted to cross it. He started to cross, but it was busy. He couldn't see a path to get safely across. John was scared. John asked his mum for help. She said, "Wait for the cars to pass and then I'll help you cross the street." John waited, but the cars kept on coming. He started to cross by himself, but the cars were too close. His mum shouted, "John, stop. Don't cross it. Wait for me." John stopped. He looked at his mum and said, "I'm sorry." His mum smiled and hugged him. "It's ok, John," she said. "It's better to be safe. I'm sorry I didn't help you." John nodded and waited for his mum to help him cross the street. Once they were across, he felt relieved and happy. He said, "We did it!" His mum smiled and said, "Yes, we did. And we did it safely."
