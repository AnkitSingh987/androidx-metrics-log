Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise Owl and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a wise old owl. He lived in a tall tree in a loopy wood. One day he decided he wanted to make a house for himself in a lower tree. So the wise old owl flew down and found some materials to use. He took a piece of wood and attached it to a branch with a loop of string. He kept attaching pieces of wood together until he had made a nice house. He was very pleased with his work, and he flew back up to his tall tree in the loopy wood. The wise old owl then had a house in two different trees!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a box of shapes. He liked the shapes so much. He wanted to improve how they looked. Tim thought, "I will make them look better with colors!" Tim took out his colors and started to color the shapes. He made the circle red, the square blue, and the triangle green. Tim was happy with his work. But then, he saw a shape he did not know. It was a mix of shapes. Tim felt sorry for the shape because it looked sad. Tim thought, "I will improve this shape too!" He colored the shape with all the colors he had. The shape became very bright and happy. Tim was proud of his work. Now, all the shapes were pretty and the sad shape was not sorry anymore. They all lived happily in the box, and Tim loved playing with them.

Once there was a girl named Jane. She liked to read a magazine. She had a friend named Tom. Tom's family was wealthy. They lived in a big house. One day, Jane and Tom were playing. Tom said, "I found something cool in the big house. Want to see it?" Jane said, "Yes, I want to see it!" They went to the big house. Tom showed Jane a secret door. They opened the door and found a room full of toys. Jane and Tom played with the toys all day. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a man who liked to sell things. He had a small shop. Every day, he went to his shop to sell toys, food, and clothes. He thought his job was very boring. He wanted to do something fun. One day, he had an idea. He thought it would be fun to sell all of his things for just one coin. He was very excited about his idea. He thought this would make his boring day fun. People came to his shop and bought all of his things for just one coin. They were very happy. But at the end of the day, the man was sad. He had no more things to sell and no more money to buy new things. His shop was empty, and he had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, he would suffer when it was time for bed. His mom would always say, "Don't worry, Tim. The dark is not scary." One night, Tim heard a noise in the dark. He was very scared. He called out, "Mom, help! There is a noise in the dark!" His mom came running to his room. She turned on the light, and they saw something unexpected. It was a small, lost puppy! The puppy was making the noise in the dark. Tim was not scared anymore. He said, "Hi, puppy! I'm not scared of the dark now." Tim and his mom took care of the puppy, and they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a tall man with a big bag. He is selling pastries. Sara and Tom love pastries. They run to the man and ask for some. "Hello, little ones. Do you want some pastries? They are yummy and sweet. Only one coin for each," the man says. Sara and Tom have no coins. They are sad. They want pastries very much. "Please, mister, can we have some pastries for free? We have no coins. We are hungry," Sara says. The man shakes his head. He is not nice. He is mean. "No, no, no. No pastries for free. You have to pay. Go away, or I will call the police," the man says. Sara and Tom are scared. They run away. But Tom has an idea. He sees a bike near the man. The bike is broken. The wheel is flat. "Look, Sara. The bike is broken. Maybe we can fix it. Then the man will give us some pastries," Tom says. Sara nods. She thinks it is a good idea. They go to the bike. They try to fix it. They use a stick, a stone, and a leaf. They do not know how to fix a bike. They make it worse. The man sees them. He is angry. He shouts at them. "Hey, you! What are you doing? You are ruining my bike! That is my bike! How dare you touch it? You are bad children! I will call the police!" the man says. Sara and Tom are very scared. They cry. They run away. They have no pastries. They have no bike. They have no fun. They have a bad day. The end.
