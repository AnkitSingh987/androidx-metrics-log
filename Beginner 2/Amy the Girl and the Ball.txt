Category: Beginner
Title: Amy the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big red ball. She loved to play with her ball every day. One day, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim did not have a ball. Amy wanted to be nice, so she offered her ball to Tim to play with. Tim and Amy played with the ball near a big tree. They were happy and laughed a lot. But then, they saw smoke. The smoke came from a big fire near their house. They were scared and did not know what to do. They ran to tell their mom and dad about the fire. But it was too late. The fire had spoiled their home. Tim and Amy were very sad. They could not play with the ball anymore. The smoke and fire had taken away their fun.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big, angry dog. The dog was barking very loud. Amy felt scared and held her mom's hand tight. The weight of her mom's hand made her feel safe. Amy's mom said, "Don't worry, the dog is just angry because it wants to play." Amy gazed at the dog and saw that it was looking at a ball. She asked her mom, "Can I help the dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Amy picked up the ball and threw it. The dog ran after the ball and brought it back. The angry dog was now happy, and Amy was not scared anymore. They played together and had a lot of fun at the park.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their dad's tools. One day, they find a big hammer in the garage. They want to use it to build something. "Look, Sam, a hammer!" Tim says. "Let's carry it to the backyard and make a fort." "OK, Tim, but it is very heavy. We need to be careful," Sam says. They both grab the hammer and walk slowly to the backyard. They find some wood and nails and start to hammer them together. They make a wall and a roof for their fort. They are having fun. But then, they hear a weird noise. It sounds like a growl. They look around and see a big dog. The dog is angry. It does not like the hammer noise. It runs towards them, barking loudly. "Help, Tim, a dog!" Sam shouts. "What do we do?" "Quick, Sam, get in the fort!" Tim says. "We can use the hammer to scare it away." They run to the fort and hide inside. They hold the hammer and wait. The dog reaches the fort and sniffs around. It tries to bite the wood, but it hurts its teeth. It hears the boys inside and growls again. "Go away, dog!" Tim yells. "This is our fort. We have a hammer. We are not afraid of you." The dog hears Tim's voice and the hammer sound. It does not like it. It decides to leave. It runs away, whimpering. Tim and Sam peek out of the fort. They see the dog is gone. They are happy and relieved. "Phew, that was close," Sam says. "That was a mean dog." "Yeah, but we are brave," Tim says. "We have a hammer. We can build anything." They hug each other and smile. They go back to their hammering. They finish their fort and play inside. They are proud of their work. They love their hammer.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a wide river with a mill. The cat said, "I want to go inside the mill!" The dog said, "Okay, let's go!" Inside the mill, they found many big bags. The cat said, "I think these bags have food." The dog said, "Let's open one and see." They tried to open the bag, but it was too heavy. They did not know what to do. Then, they saw a little mouse. The mouse said, "I can help you open the bag." The cat and the dog were happy. They realized the mouse was a good friend. They opened the bag and found lots of food. They all ate together and had a fun day.
