Title: The Cat and Dog and the Bird

One day, a cat and a dog went for a jog. They ran in the wide park. The sun was bright, and the sky was blue. They were very happy. As they jogged, they saw a big tree. A small bird was on a branch. The bird was sad. The cat and the dog stopped and looked at the bird. "Why are you sad?" asked the cat. "I want to fly to the other side, but there is a big river. I am scared I will fall in the water," said the bird. The cat and the dog wanted to help. They thought of a way to prevent the bird from falling in the water. The dog had an idea. "I can swim across the river with you on my back," said the dog. The bird liked the idea. The bird sat on the dog's back, and they swam across the wide river. The cat ran across the bridge. On the other side, the bird was very happy. "Thank you for your help!" said the bird. The cat, the dog, and the bird continued their jog in the wide park. They were all very happy, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, a girl named Ann found a muffin in her kitchen. It was hot and smelled very good. She wanted to eat it, but she knew she should share it with her brother, Tom. Ann went to find Tom and said, "I have a hot muffin. Let's spread butter on it and share it." Tom was happy and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They went back to the kitchen to find the butter. But when they got there, the muffin was gone! Ann and Tom were very sad. They looked everywhere but could not find the muffin. Then, they heard a noise from outside. They opened the door and saw a small dog eating the hot muffin. The dog had taken it when they were not looking. Ann and Tom laughed and said, "Well, I guess the dog was hungry too!" They were not sad anymore and went to make another muffin to share.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the living room. One day, Mom makes beef for lunch. She puts the beef on a big plate and tells the boys to come and eat. Tim runs to the table and takes a big piece of beef. He puts it on his plate and starts to eat. Sam walks slowly to the table and sees that there is not much beef left. He feels frustrated. He wants some beef too. "Tim, you took too much beef!" Sam says. "You have to share with me!" "No, I don't!" Tim says. "I was here first. You are too slow. This is my beef!" Sam gets angry. He tries to take some beef from Tim's plate. Tim pushes him away. They start to fight. Mom hears the noise and comes back to the living room. "Stop it, boys!" Mom says. "What are you doing? You are not supposed to fight over food. There is enough beef for both of you. Tim, you have to give some beef to Sam. Sam, you have to wait your turn. Now, say sorry to each other and eat nicely." Tim and Sam look at Mom. They feel ashamed. They say sorry to each other and share the beef. Mom smiles and hugs them. She is happy that they are friends again. She tells them that they can play with their toys after they finish their lunch. Tim and Sam nod and eat their beef. They think that beef is yummy. They also think that Mom is the best.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to print pictures of her favorite animals. One day, she wanted to print a picture of a cat. She turned on her computer and found a cute cat picture. When she tried to print the cat picture, her printer made a loud tap sound. Lily was confused. She tried again, but the printer just made the tap sound again. Lily started to feel frustrated. She wanted her cat picture, but the printer was not working. Lily's mom saw that she was frustrated and came to help. She looked at the printer and found that it was out of paper. Mom added more paper and the printer started to work. Lily was happy and thanked her mom. In the end, Lily had her cat picture and she was not frustrated anymore.
