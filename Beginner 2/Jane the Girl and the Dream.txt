Category: Beginner
Title: Jane the Girl and the Dream

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. She was very persistent about reaching her goals. She had a dream of living in a big house with a big yard to play in. One day, Jane decided to make her dream a reality. She ran out of her small house and down the street. He body wasn’t as strong as the others, so she had to take it slow. Suddenly, Jane heard a voice say, “Hey, why don’t you take a break?” It was an old man who had been watching her. “No,” Jane replied firmly. “I must keep going if I want to make my dream come true.” The old man smiled as Jane kept walking towards her goal. He was very impressed by her persistence. In the end, Jane was able to make her dream come true. She reached her goal, and now she lives in a big house in the suburbs. She never forgot the old man who encouraged her to keep going.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty purse that she loved very much. One day, Lucy went to the park with her purse. At the park, Lucy saw a thirsty dog. The dog looked sad and tired. Lucy wanted to help the dog. She said, "Dog, do you want to drink some water?" The dog wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, please!" Lucy opened her purse and found that her water bottle was broken. She felt sad but had an idea. She ran to the park fountain and filled her purse with water. Then, she went back to the dog and let it drink from her purse. The dog was very happy and wagged its tail. Lucy felt good that she could help the dog. After that, Lucy and the dog played together in the park. They became best friends and had a fun day.

Lily liked to play with stamps. She had many stamps of animals, flowers and stars. She liked to stamp them on paper and make pictures. She wanted to show her stamps to her friend Ben. She went to Ben's house with her stamps. Ben was happy to see her. He liked to play with Lily. He had many toys, but he did not have stamps. "Look, Ben, these are my stamps. They are fun. Do you want to try?" Lily said. "Yes, please. How do you use them?" Ben said. Lily smiled. She wanted to teach Ben how to stamp. She took a thick book and put it on the floor. She said, "You need a thick book to stamp on. It makes the stamps look better. Then you need a pad of ink. You press the stamp on the ink, like this. Then you press the stamp on the paper, like this. See? You made a star!" Ben watched Lily. He wanted to make a star too. He took a stamp and pressed it on the ink. Then he pressed it on the paper. But he pressed too hard. The ink spilled on the paper and the stamp. He made a big mess. "Oh, no! I'm sorry, Lily. I did it wrong. I ruined your stamp and your paper." Ben said. Lily looked at Ben. She saw that he was sad. She did not want him to be sad. She said, "It's okay, Ben. You did not do it wrong. You just did it different. You can try again. Here, take another stamp and another paper. I will help you. We can make more stars together." Ben smiled. He thanked Lily. He took another stamp and another paper. He pressed them gently on the ink and the paper. He made a star. He was happy. He said, "Look, Lily, I made a star. You are a good teacher. Thank you for teaching me how to stamp." Lily hugged Ben. She was happy too. She said, "You are welcome, Ben. You are a good learner. Thank you for playing with me and my stamps."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a comb that she loved. She used the comb every day to brush her hair. One day, Sue went for a walk in the mild sun. She took her comb with her. As Sue walked, she met a big dog. The dog looked at her comb and said, "Can I use your comb, please?" Sue was surprised. She did not know dogs could talk! But she was a nice girl, so she said, "Yes, you can use my comb." The dog took the comb and started to brush his hair. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog began to tremble and change. In a moment, the dog turned into a little boy! The boy said, "Thank you, Sue. Your comb was magic, and it turned me back into a boy. I was a dog for a long time, but now I am free!" Sue was happy to help, and they became good friends.
