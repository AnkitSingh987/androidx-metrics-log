Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Purple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved the color purple. He had a purple shirt, purple shoes, and even a purple hat. One day, Tim saw a big purple ball in the park. He was very happy to play with it. While Tim was playing, a little girl named Sue came to the park. Sue saw Tim's purple ball and wanted to play too. Tim asked, "Do you like purple?" Sue said, "Yes, I do!" Tim was happy to share his purple ball with Sue. They played a game with the purple ball. Tim would throw the ball and Sue would catch it. After a while, Sue said, "Let's try something new." She suggested they count how many times they could bounce the ball. They tried, but the ball would not bounce. It had zero bounces. They laughed and kept playing with the purple ball until it was time to go home.

Anna and Ben like to play pretend. They pretend to be different animals and make noises. One day, they pretend to be lions and roar at each other. They run around the room and jump on the sofa. But Anna is careless. She does not look where she is going. She bumps into the lamp and knocks it over. The lamp makes a loud crash and the light goes out. Anna and Ben are scared. They see their shadows on the wall. The shadows look big and scary. "Mommy, mommy, help!" Anna and Ben cry. They hug each other and close their eyes. Mommy hears the noise and comes to the room. She sees the broken lamp and the scared children. She turns on another light and hugs them. "It's okay, it's okay, don't be afraid. It was just your shadows. They can't hurt you. They are just pictures of you," Mommy says. Anna and Ben open their eyes and look at the wall. They see their shadows again. They are not big and scary anymore. They are small and funny. "Look, Mommy, we are lions!" Ben says and roars. "Yes, you are, but you have to be careful lions. You don't want to break things and hurt yourself," Mommy says. "Sorry, Mommy, we didn't mean to," Anna says. Mommy smiles and kisses them. She helps them clean up the mess and fix the lamp. Then she lets them play pretend again. But this time, they pretend to be quiet mice.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a pretty gem in her backyard. It was shiny and blue. Lily was very happy. She wanted to show her friend, Tom. She ran to Tom's house and knocked on the door. Tom opened the door and saw the gem. "Wow, that's pretty!" he said. But then, Tom did a rude thing. He took the gem from Lily's hand and said, "It's mine now!" Lily was sad and angry. She frowned and said, "Give it back!" Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird took the gem from Tom's hand and flew away. Tom and Lily were both surprised. They looked at each other and said, "We need to work together to get the gem back." They stopped being rude and made a plan to find the bird and the gem.

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin named Fin. Fin was a very organized dolphin. He liked to keep his home clean and his toys in the right place. One day, when Fin was playing with his friends, he noticed that his favorite toy was missing. Fin was sad and asked his friends if they had seen his toy. His friends did not know where it was, but they wanted to help Fin find it. They looked everywhere, but they could not find the toy. They started to feel tired and worried. Then, a big fish named Bubbles swam up to them. Bubbles had seen where the toy was and told Fin and his friends. They all swam to the place where the toy was hiding. When they found the toy, they all started to laugh and play together. Fin was very happy to have his toy back and to have such good friends to help him.
