Category: Beginner
Title: Hannah and the Rub

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Hannah. She was very sad because her mommy was very far away. She missed her mommy so much. Suddenly, a magical creature flew in from the sky. The magical creature had one job: to deliver a special rub to Hannah. Hannah was so happy to get the special rub. But it was very difficult for her to use it. She tried and tried, but she couldn't get it right. It was just too hard. The magical creature was very patient. It stood with Hannah until she figured out how to use the rub. Finally, Hannah was able to use the special rub. As soon as she did, the magical creature smiled and flew away. Hannah was so happy and hugged the rub tight as she fell asleep, happy to have her mommy back.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have many toys of different colors and shapes. Sometimes they sort their toys by color or shape. They think it is fun. One day, they find a shiny coin on the floor. It is copper. It is round and has a face on it. They both want the coin. They start to fight over it. "Give me the coin! It is mine!" Lila says. "No, it is mine! I saw it first!" Ben says. They pull and tug on the coin. They do not share. They do not listen. They are very angry. Mom hears them fighting. She is busy. She is making lunch. She does not like the noise. She comes to see what is wrong. "What are you doing? Why are you fighting?" Mom asks. They both point at the coin. They both say it is theirs. They do not stop fighting. Mom is not happy. She takes the coin away. She says they cannot play with it. She says they have to go to their room. She says they have to learn to share and be nice. Lila and Ben are sad. They do not have the coin. They do not have their toys. They do not have fun. They wish they did not fight. They wish they sorted their toys instead.

Tim and Sue like to play in the park. They see many bugs on the grass. They want to catch them and put them in a jar. "Look, a blue bug!" Tim says. He runs to the bug and tries to grab it. But the bug is fast and flies away. "Let me try!" Sue says. She runs after the bug too. She is faster than Tim and gets close to the bug. She reaches out her hand to catch it. But the bug is not a bug. It is a bee. And the bee is angry. It stings Sue on her hand. Sue screams and drops the jar. The jar breaks and the other bugs escape. Tim runs to Sue and sees her hand. It is red and swollen. He is scared and sorry. "Sue, are you okay?" he asks. "No, it hurts!" Sue cries. "I want Mommy!" Tim and Sue run to their mom, who is sitting on a bench. She sees their faces and hears their cries. She is worried and angry. "What happened?" she asks. "We were playing with bugs, and Sue got stung by a bee!" Tim says. "A bee? Why did you play with a bee? That is very dangerous!" Mom says. "You should know better!" She takes Sue to the car and drives to the doctor. Tim follows them. He feels bad and sad. He wishes he never saw the blue bug. He wishes he could make Sue feel better. He wishes he could say sorry. But he can't. He can only cry.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to open a big box. It was difficult for him because the box was very big and heavy. He tried many styles to open it, like pulling and pushing, but nothing worked. One day, a kind bird saw Tim and wanted to help him. The bird flew down and used its beak to open the box. Tim was so happy! He thought there would be toys inside the box. But when they opened it, there was just a little key. Tim and the bird were surprised. They took the key and went on a walk. They found a small door in a tree. Tim used the key to open the door. Inside, they found a room full of toys and games! They played and had fun all day. The small key gave them a big surprise.
