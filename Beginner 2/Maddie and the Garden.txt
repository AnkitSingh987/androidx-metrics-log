Category: Beginner
Title: Maddie and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Maddie. She had long, curly hair and two big, blue eyes. She was only three years old, but she was very smart. Maddie loved to go outside and explore the world around her. One day she was walking around the garden when she stumbled across a newspaper on the ground. "What is this?" Maddie said with an excited voice. Suddenly she noticed something furry moving around the newspaper. She bent down to get a closer look and discovered it was a big, hairy spider! Maddie sprang back and started to giggle. She watched as the spider started to sort the newspaper into neat little piles. "You are a clever spider" said Maddie. The spider stopped and looked up at Maddie before running away. Maddie smiled and said goodbye. She looked around at the piles of newspaper and wondered what the spider was trying to do. Maddie decided to pick up the newspaper and put it into the bin so it wouldn’t be there for the hairy spider to sort again.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish. The fish had a brilliant, shiny scale on its side. The fish loved to swim and play with its friends in the water. One day, the fish found a key. The key was to lock a door. The fish did not know where the door was. So, the fish went on a journey to find the door. The fish swam and swam until it found the door. The fish used the key to lock the door. Behind the door, the fish found a big, beautiful world to play in with its friends. They were all very happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a weird bug on a leaf. He poked it with a stick. The bug looked at Tim and said, "Why did you poke me?" Tim was scared but said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know you could talk." The bug told Tim that he was a magic bug. He said, "I will give you a wish if you promise not to poke me again." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I want a big toy car to play with." The next day, Tim went to the park again. He saw a big toy car waiting for him. He remembered the magic bug and was happy. He played with the toy car all day and never poked the bug again.

Once upon a time, there was a big red truck. The truck had a job to deliver a box. Inside the box was a big, shiny button. The truck liked to help and was happy to do its job. On the way, the truck met a small, angry car. The car did not like the truck. The car wanted the button for itself. The truck did not want to give the button to the angry car. The truck went fast to get away from the angry car. The truck was strong and brave. It got to the house and delivered the box with the button. The truck was happy it did its job well.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But Tom saw a big dog and wanted to pet it. Lily was scared of dogs. She said, "No, Tom, don't go. The dog might bite you." Tom did not listen. He ran to the dog and tried to touch its fur. The dog did not like that. It growled and snapped at Tom. It bit his arm. Tom cried out loud. He felt a lot of pain. He ran back to Lily with his arm bleeding. Lily was very worried. She hugged Tom and said, "I'm sorry, Tom. You have a bad bite. We need to find a grown-up to help you." She looked around and saw a lady with a phone. She ran to her and said, "Please, help us. The dog bit my friend. His arm is bad." The lady was kind. She said, "Oh, poor boy. Let me see your arm. I will call a doctor. He will make you feel better." She took Tom's arm and wrapped it with a cloth. She explained, "This will stop the blood. The doctor will come soon. He will clean your wound and give you a bandage. He might also give you a shot. It will not hurt much. It will keep you from getting sick." Tom was still crying. He was scared of the doctor and the shot. He said, "I don't want a shot. I don't like needles. They are sharp and mean." Lily held his hand and said, "I know, Tom. But the shot is good for you. It will make the bad dog's germs go away. You will be fine. I will be with you. You are brave, Tom. You can do it." She smiled at him and gave him a kiss. Tom felt a little better. He thanked the lady and waited for the doctor. He hoped the dog would not bite anyone else. He learned to be careful with dogs. He also learned that Lily was a good friend.
