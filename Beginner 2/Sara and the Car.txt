Category: Beginner
Title: Sara and the Car

Sara liked to play on the lawn with her doll. She made a house for her doll with sticks and leaves. She pretended her doll was rich and had many things. She gave her doll a necklace of flowers and a cup of water. One day, a boy came to the lawn. He had a toy car. He saw Sara and her doll. He wanted to play with them. He said, "Can I play with you? I have a car. It is fast and shiny. You can have a ride." Sara looked at the boy and his car. She liked cars. She wanted to have a ride. But she also liked her doll. She did not want to leave her doll alone. She said, "Thank you for your offer. But I am playing with my doll. She is rich and has many things. She needs me to take care of her." The boy did not like Sara's answer. He thought she was silly. He said, "Your doll is not rich. She is dirty and old. She has nothing. She does not need you. She is just a toy. My car is better. It is real and fun. You are missing out." He grabbed Sara's doll and threw it on the ground. He laughed and drove his car away. Sara was sad and angry. She ran to her doll and picked her up. She hugged her and said, "I am sorry, doll. You are not dirty and old. You are beautiful and kind. You have everything. You have me. I love you." She saw the boy's car on the lawn. He had left it behind. She did not want it. She did not like the boy. She took the car and threw it in the trash. She said, "You are not real and fun. You are mean and rude. You have nothing. You have no one. I don't want you." She went back to her house with her doll. She made a new necklace of flowers and a new cup of water. She said, "You are my best friend, doll. We don't need a car. We have each other."

Once there was a bird named Jack. Jack lived in a nest near a park. He loved to whistle his song everyday. One day, a little boy named Tim decided to come and explore the park. He saw Jack in the tree and wanted to go closer. He slowly climbed up the tree, careful not to scare him away. When he got close to Jack he extended his hand out, trying to rub his feathers. But Jack started to flutter away, scared of the boy's approach. The boy paused, not wanting Jack to be scared. He gently reached for Jack, letting him know he wasn't there to hurt him. Jack slowly came back and allowed the little boy to rub him. He purred in content as the boy gently stroked his feathers. The boy gave Jack a big smile, and the bird rewarded him with a sweet whistle. The two were friends for many years to come.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. Every morning she would look out of the window, and she would see the birds fly in the sky. She wanted to join them, so she went outside and started to jump. Just then, Sarah spotted a little bird on the ground. It was wearing a pretty blouse. Sarah was so excited. She quickly bent over to grab it, but it flew away before she had a chance. Then Sarah was sad and she started to cry. But suddenly, she heard a voice. It was the bird! It called out, "don't cry, Sarah." Sarah stopped crying and looked up. The bird was back and it had brought a thoughtful friend with it. This friend had a spare blouse for Sarah. Sarah felt much happier, and she quickly grabbed the blouse so she could pretend to be a bird too. From that day, Sarah and the birds flew in the sky together every morning, and Sarah was always so happy!

Once upon a time, there was a big hospital. It had many high beds for people to relax in when they were not feeling good. In this hospital, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car on his high bed. He was trying to relax, but he wanted to have fun too. So, he made the toy car go very fast on the bed. But then, the toy car went too fast and fell off the high bed. Tim was sad and scared. He tried to get the toy car, but he fell off the high bed too. Tim got hurt very bad. The people at the hospital tried to help him, but they could not make him feel better. Tim was sad, and he never got to play with his toy car again.
