Category: Beginner
Title: Max the Healthy Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Max. Max liked to run and play in the yard. There was a fence around the yard to keep Max safe. One day, Max heard a bird speak. The bird was on the fence. Max and the bird became friends. They liked to play and talk to each other. Max and the bird had a lot of fun. They would run and jump and speak to each other all day. Max was happy to have a friend. They played together every day and stayed healthy and happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He found a big, red ball in his yard. Tim was very happy and started to play with the ball. He kicked it, threw it, and rolled it around. Suddenly, Tim saw a cat. The cat was very close to the ball. Tim wanted to scare the cat away, so he yelled, "Go away, cat!" The cat got scared and ran away very fast. Tim felt guilty for yelling at the cat, but he didn't want it to take his ball. Later, Tim's mom came outside. She saw Tim looking sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about the cat and how he had yelled at it. His mom said, "It's okay, Tim. The cat is safe now. But next time, be kind to the cat." Tim smiled and promised to be kind to the cat. Then, he went back to playing with his big, red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a high tree in a big forest. One day, a little bird sat on a branch of the tree. The bird saw fog spread all around the forest. The fog was so thick that the bird could not see its friends. The bird called out, "Where are you, friends?" From far away, a voice said, "We are here, in the fog. Can you help us find our way back?" The little bird wanted to help its friends. It flew high up in the sky, above the fog. The bird looked down and saw a path through the fog. It called to its friends, "Follow me! I can see a way out!" The friends listened to the bird and followed its voice. They walked on the path and soon, they were out of the fog. They were so happy to be together again. They thanked the little bird for helping them, and they all played together in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and all night. Tom had a friend, a little girl named Sue. They liked to play together when Tom was not sleepy. One day, Tom asked Sue, "Why do the leaves change color and fall down?" Sue said, "It's because of the season. Now it's fall, so the leaves change and fall. Soon, it will be cold and white." Tom and Sue decided to keep the colorful leaves. They put them in a big box. As the season changed, they played in the snow and kept warm by the fire. They waited for the next season to come. When the snow melted, the flowers started to grow. It was spring! Tom and Sue went outside to see the new flowers. They opened the box with the leaves and let them fly in the wind. The village looked beautiful with the leaves and flowers together. Tom and Sue were happy to see the seasons change. They knew that each season brought something special. And when Tom was not sleepy, they played together and enjoyed the beauty of the seasons.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play with their toys. They have many toys, but their favorite is a big plastic truck. The truck is red and yellow and has four big wheels. It can make loud noises when they push a button. They call it their mighty truck. One day, Tim and Kim are playing with their mighty truck in the living room. They take turns to drive it around the sofa and the table. They have fun and laugh a lot. But then, Tim wants to keep the truck for himself. He says to Kim, "This is my truck. You can't play with it. Go away!" Kim is sad and angry. She says to Tim, "No, this is our truck. We share it. You are mean. Give it back!" She tries to grab the truck from Tim, but Tim holds it tight. They pull and tug and shout at each other. The truck makes loud noises. Mom hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees Tim and Kim fighting over the truck. She is not happy. She says to them, "Stop it, both of you. Why are you fighting? You are twins. You should love each other and play nicely. The truck is for both of you to enjoy. You need to speak kindly and share your toys. Do you understand?" Tim and Kim look at Mom. They feel sorry. They say to Mom, "Yes, Mom. We understand. We are sorry. We love each other and we love the truck. Can we play with it together?" Mom smiles and hugs them. She says to them, "Yes, you can. But remember to be gentle and respectful with each other and your toys. The truck is mighty, but your love is mightier."
