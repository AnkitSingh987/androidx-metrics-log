Category: Beginner
Title: Sue the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her toys on the floor. But sometimes, she thought it was boring to play alone. She wanted to make new friends. One day, Sue saw a sign near her house. The sign said, "Come and make friends at the park!" Sue was very happy. She went to the park to play and make new friends. At the park, she met a boy named Tim. They played together and had lots of fun. Sue and Tim learned that playing together was more fun than playing alone. They always shared their toys and helped each other. Soon, other kids joined them, and they all became good friends. The moral of the story is that sharing and helping others can make our lives more fun and less boring.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. Every day, he would run and play with his ball in the yard. Spot was very happy when he played. One day, Spot saw a big hill. He wanted to go up the hill with his ball. Spot tried to go up, but it was hard. Spot did not give up. He was persistent. He tried again and again. He knew he could do it. Finally, Spot made it up the hill. He was so happy! Spot knew that he could do anything if he tried hard. He learned to value his pass up the hill. He would always remember how he made it up the big hill with his ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her best friend, Tom. One sunny day, they were playing in the park when Amy found a secret box. It was hidden under a big tree. Amy and Tom were very excited to see what was inside the box. Amy opened the box and found a note. The note said, "To start a perfect day, be kind and share with others." Amy and Tom looked at each other and smiled. They knew what they had to do. They went to their homes and brought some of their toys to the park. In the park, they shared their toys with other kids. They all played together and had lots of fun. Amy and Tom felt happy because they made new friends. At the end of the day, they knew they had a perfect day because they shared and were kind to others. The secret note taught them a very important lesson: to always be kind and share with others.

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a new thing in the park. It was a big, red balloon. It was enormous. It was bigger than a house. Ben and Lily wanted to touch it. "Wow, look at that balloon!" Ben said. "Let's go and see it!" "OK, but be careful," Lily said. "It might pop." They ran to the balloon. It was tied to a pole. A man was standing next to it. He had a sharp thing in his hand. It was a needle. He looked angry. "Hey, kids, get away from my balloon!" the man shouted. "It's mine!" "But we just want to look at it," Ben said. "It's so pretty." "Well, you can't," the man said. "It's very expensive. And if you touch it, it will pop. And then I will be very sad. And very mad." He waved the needle at them. Ben and Lily were scared. They backed away. "Sorry, mister," Lily said. "We didn't mean to bother you." They ran back to the swings. They didn't like the man. He was mean. And he had a sharp thing. They played on the swings for a while. Then they heard a loud noise. It was a bang. They looked at the balloon. It was gone. It had popped. The man was crying. He had dropped the needle. It had poked the balloon. Ben and Lily felt sorry for the man. But they also felt happy. They didn't like the balloon. It was too big. And it had a sharp thing.
