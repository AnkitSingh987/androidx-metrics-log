Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and Ben and the Rock

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big rock near the slide. Lila wanted to climb on the rock, but Ben said no. "Why not?" Lila asked. "Because the rock is fragile. It can break and hurt you," Ben said. Lila did not believe him. She shrugged and said, "I don't care. I want to climb on the rock. It looks fun." She ran to the rock and tried to climb on it. But the rock was not strong. It cracked and crumbled under her feet. Lila fell down and scraped her knee. She cried out loud. Ben ran to her and helped her up. He said, "I told you the rock was fragile. Are you okay?" Lila nodded, but she was still crying. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. You were right. The rock was fragile. I should have listened to you." Ben hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lila. I'm sorry you got hurt. Let's go to the nurse and get a band-aid. Then we can play something else." Lila smiled and said, "Okay, Ben. Thank you for being my friend." They walked to the nurse together, holding hands. They learned a lesson about the rock and about listening.

Max was eager to play with his new cup. He held it up to the sky and said, "Whee! I like this cup!" His mom smiled at him, and said, "It's so nice of your aunt to give you that cup!" Max couldn't wait to show it to his friends. Max decided to take his cup to the park. When he got there he saw his friend John. Max was so excited to show his cup to John. He ran up to him and proudly held out his cup for John to see. John said, "Oh wow! What a nice cup! Where did you get it?" Max beamed and said, "My aunt gave it to me." John said, "That's so nice of her!" They decided to play with the cup together. They chased each other around the park, and had fun all day. At the end of the day, Max went home happy and tired. He placed his cup on the shelf and said, "I can't wait to play with it again tomorrow!"

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. A little boy named Tim wanted to play outside. He put on his hat and went out to the yard. Tim saw a line of ants on the ground. He thought it would be fun to play with them. He got down on the ground and followed the line of ants. They were going to a big tree. As Tim played with the ants, he didn't see the big hole near the tree. He fell into the hole and started to cry. The ants just kept walking in their line, and the bright sun hid behind a cloud. No one heard Tim crying in the hole. The day became dark and sad. Tim stayed in the hole, wishing he had not played with the ants.

Once upon a time, there was a humble dog named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his friend, a wild bird named Sam. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, Tim and Sam went to the park to have a race. Tim ran fast, but Sam could fly faster. They were having a great time. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Sam high up in the sky. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to save his friend. Tim thought and thought, then he had an idea. He found a big balloon and held on tight. The wind blew the balloon up into the sky, and Tim was able to catch Sam. They were both happy and safe. Tim and Sam learned that even a humble dog can do big things when he helps his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog and a little brown cat. They lived in a small house. The dog and the cat did not like each other very much. They would often quarrel and fight. One day, the dog and the cat saw a calendar on the wall. The calendar was very pretty. It had pictures of their favorite foods. The dog wanted the calendar, but so did the cat. They began to quarrel over who would get the calendar. They fought and fought for a long time. They were so busy fighting that they did not see the calendar fall off the wall. It landed in a big puddle of water outside. The pretty calendar was ruined. The dog and the cat were very sad. They had lost the calendar because they could not stop quarreling.
