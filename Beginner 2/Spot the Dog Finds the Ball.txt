Category: Beginner
Title: Spot the Dog Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had big, brown eyes. He was a very glad dog. He liked to play and run all day. Spot had many friends. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball. He wanted the ball very much. But the ball was not his. Spot knew it was wrong to take things that were not his. He thought about what to do. He did not want to lie. Spot went to find the owner of the ball. He used his eyes to look all around the park. At last, he found a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her ball. Spot was glad he found the owner. He gave the ball back to Sue. Sue was very happy to have her ball back. She said, "Thank you, Spot!" Spot felt good inside for doing the right thing. He did not lie or take what was not his. The moral of the story is to always be honest and kind to others.

One day, a dog named Max found a pipe in the yard. Max liked to dig and bury things. He wanted to bury the pipe. But his friend, a cat named Lily, wanted to play with the pipe too. Max said, "I want to bury the pipe!" Lily said, "No, I want to play with it!" They both did not want to share. This made them sad. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's play with the pipe together. Then, we can bury it." Lily thought it was a good idea. They played with the pipe on a mild day. After playing, they buried the pipe together. Max and Lily were happy and stayed good friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing hide and seek with her friends. Lily was very good at hiding. She found a big tree to hide behind. Her friends looked and looked, but they could not find her. Lily was so happy because she was the best at hiding. After the game, Lily went into her house to find her mom. Her mom was looking at a photo. In the photo, there was a crazy dog with big eyes and a funny smile. Lily had never seen this dog before. She asked her mom, "Who is this crazy dog?" Her mom smiled and said, "That is our new dog, Sparky!" Lily was so excited and surprised. She went outside to meet Sparky. But when she saw him, he looked different than the photo. He was not a crazy dog, but a sweet and gentle dog. Lily loved Sparky, and they played hide and seek together. Lily found out that Sparky was even better at hiding than she was.

Once upon a time, there was an old tree. The old tree had a broken branch. Two birds lived on the branch. They were friends. One day, the birds had a quarrel. They did not want to share the branch anymore. They were mad at each other. The old tree was sad. The old tree asked the sun for help. The sun shone brightly and made the broken branch warm. The birds felt happy again. They stopped their quarrel and became friends again. The old tree was happy too.

Lily and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They had a secret spot under a big tree, where they hid their toys and snacks. They trusted each other and shared everything. One day, a mean boy came to the park. He saw Lily and Ben's secret spot and wanted to take their things. He waited until they were busy on the swings and ran to the tree. He grabbed their toys and snacks and ran away. Lily and Ben saw him and chased him. They shouted, "Stop! Give us back our things!" But the mean boy was faster and ran to the other side of the park. He saw a fence with a small hole. He thought he could pass through it and escape. But he was wrong. The hole was too small for him. He got stuck in the fence. He tried to pull himself out, but he couldn't. He felt helpless. He started to cry. Lily and Ben caught up with him. They saw him in the fence and felt sorry for him. They decided to help him. They pushed him gently from behind until he was free. They gave him some of their snacks and said, "Don't be mean. Be nice. We can be friends." The mean boy felt ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry. I was wrong. Thank you for helping me. Can we be friends?" Lily and Ben nodded and smiled. They invited him to play with them. They became friends and trusted each other. They learned that being nice is better than being mean.
