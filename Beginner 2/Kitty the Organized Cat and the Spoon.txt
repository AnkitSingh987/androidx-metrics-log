Category: Beginner
Title: Kitty the Organized Cat and the Spoon

Once upon a time, there was a very organized cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to cook food for her friends. She had a big pot and a spoon to cook tasty food. Kitty was happy when her friends liked her food. One day, Kitty wanted to cook for her friend Doggy. Doggy liked to eat food with his big mouth. Kitty made a yummy soup for Doggy. She put carrots, peas, and chicken in the pot. Kitty cooked the soup for a long time. When the soup was ready, Kitty gave some to Doggy. Doggy ate the soup with his big mouth. He said, "Yum, yum! This soup is very good!" Kitty was happy that her friend liked her cooking. They had a fun day eating soup together.

One day, a tall tree and a small tree were in a forest. They were friends. They liked to talk and play with each other. The tall tree was very proud of how big he was. The small tree wished she could be tall too. One day, the tall tree said, "I can write the names of all the seasons on my leaves because I am so tall." The small tree was sad because she could not do that. They started to argue. The tall tree said, "I am better because I am tall." The small tree said, "No, you are not." The small tree wanted to show the tall tree that she could write the names of the seasons too. She tried very hard, but she could not reach the high leaves. The tall tree laughed at her. This made the small tree very sad and angry. The small tree asked a bird to help her. The bird wrote the names of the seasons on the small tree's leaves. The tall tree was surprised and said, "You are smart, but I am still taller." One day, a big wind came and broke the tall tree. The tall tree fell down and could not get up. The small tree was still standing. The tall tree was not better than the small tree anymore. The small tree was sad for her friend, but she learned that being tall was not everything.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The green frog had a flute. He liked to play his flute by the pond. One day, a big mean bird saw the green frog playing his flute. The big mean bird said to the frog, "Give me your flute!" The green frog did not want to give his flute to the big mean bird. The big mean bird got angry and demanded, "Give it to me now!" The green frog was scared, so he gave his flute to the big mean bird. The big mean bird took the flute and flew away. The green frog was sad and cried by the pond. He never saw his flute again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to jog every day. One day, while jogging, he saw a sad dog. The dog was sitting on a patch of grass. Tim went to the dog and said, "Why are you sad, dog?" The dog looked at Tim and said, "I lost my toy. I am sad because I can't find it." Tim wanted to help the sad dog. They jogged together to find the toy. They looked in the park and behind the trees. They looked near the pond and under the bushes. Finally, they found the toy near a big rock. The dog was so happy to see his toy. He wagged his tail and said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me find my toy." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, dog. I am glad I could help." From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends. They jogged together every day and played in the patch of grass. And the dog was never sad again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her dad's work. She saw a big apple tree there. Sue loved tasty apples, so she picked one and took it home. At home, Sue showed her mom the apple. Her mom said, "Oh no, this apple has a bad spot. It will rot soon." Sue was sad, but her mom had an idea. "Let's cut the bad spot and make a tasty pie with the good part." Sue and her mom made the pie together. Sue said, "I can't wait to eat this tasty pie!" Her mom smiled and said, "Me too, but we need to let it cool first." They waited, and when the pie was cool, they ate it. It was the best pie ever. Sue was happy that the apple from her dad's work didn't go to waste, even with the bad spot.
