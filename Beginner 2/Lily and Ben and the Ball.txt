Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Ben and the Ball

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside on the lawn. The lawn is green and soft. They have a ball and a teddy bear. They throw the ball and catch it. They hug the teddy bear and make it talk. One day, Lily and Ben see a big truck on the street. The truck has many flowers and plants. A man gets out of the truck and knocks on the door of the house next to Lily's. He has a paper and a pen. He talks to the lady who lives there. "Hello, I'm here to deliver your order. Where do you want me to put these flowers and plants?" the man says. "Oh, thank you. You can put them on the lawn. I want to make a garden. It will be very pretty," the lady says. Lily and Ben are curious. They want to see the flowers and plants. They run to the lawn and look at them. They see roses, tulips, daisies, and sunflowers. They smell them and touch them. They are very colorful and nice. "Wow, these are so beautiful. Can we have some?" Lily asks. "No, these are not for you. These are for the lady. She paid for them. You can look, but don't take them," the man says. Lily and Ben are sad. They want to have flowers and plants too. They go back to their lawn and play with their ball and teddy bear. They pretend they have a garden too. "Look, this is a rose. It is red and smells good. I give it to you," Lily says, holding the ball. "Thank you. This is a sunflower. It is yellow and bright. I give it to you," Ben says, holding the teddy bear. They smile and kiss each other on the cheek. They are happy. They have each other and their lawn. They don't need anything else.

Sara and Tom were hungry. They wanted to eat something yummy. Mom said she would make beef stew for them. She asked Sara and Tom to help her. "Can you stir the pot for me, Sara?" Mom said. She gave Sara a big spoon. Sara nodded and climbed on a chair. She stirred the pot slowly and carefully. She smelled the beef and the carrots and the onions. They smelled lovely. "Can you set the table for me, Tom?" Mom said. She gave Tom some plates and forks and spoons. Tom nodded and ran to the dining room. He put the plates and forks and spoons on the table. He counted them. One, two, three, four. He smiled. Mom took the pot off the stove. She poured the beef stew into a big bowl. She carried the bowl to the table. She said, "Be careful, it's hot. Let it cool down a bit." Sara and Tom sat down on their chairs. They waited for Mom to join them. They said, "Thank you, Mom, for making beef stew. It looks yummy." Mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sara and Tom. Thank you for helping me. You are very good helpers. Now let's enjoy our beef stew together."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Tim saw a fast figure run by. The figure was so quick, it was hard to see. Tim wanted to know what it was, so he decided to follow it. He ran as fast as he could, trying to catch up to the figure. As Tim followed the fast figure, he found himself in a beautiful park. The figure slowed down and Tim could see it was a cute bunny. The bunny hopped over to Tim and they became friends. Tim and the bunny played together in the park until it was time to go home.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a small cat with a hurt paw. The cat was crying. Sue felt bad for the cat. Sue looked around and found a soft patch of grass. She picked up the cat and put it on the grass. Sue was very kind and compassionate. Sue went home to get a bandage for the cat's paw. When she came back, she put the bandage on the cat's paw. The cat stopped crying and started to purr. Sue and the cat became best friends.
