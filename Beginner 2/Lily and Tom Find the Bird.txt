Category: Beginner
Title: Lily and Tom Find the Bird

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big bird in the sky. The bird can fly very fast and very high. "Wow, look at the bird!" Lily says. "I wish I could fly like that." "Me too," Tom says. "Maybe we can ask the bird to teach us how to fly." They run after the bird. The bird lands on a tree. Lily and Tom stop under the tree. "Hello, bird," Lily says. "You can fly very well. Can you show us how to fly?" The bird looks at them. It has a shiny badge on its chest. It is a guard bird. It works for the park. "Hello, children," the bird says. "I am a guard bird. I fly around the park to keep it safe and clean. I cannot show you how to fly. You are not birds. You are humans. You have to walk on the ground." Lily and Tom feel sad. They want to fly. "Please, bird," Tom says. "Can you at least let us touch your wings? They are so pretty and soft." The bird thinks for a moment. It is a polite bird. It does not want to hurt the children's feelings. "Okay," the bird says. "You can touch my wings. But be gentle. And do not pull my feathers." Lily and Tom smile. They thank the bird. They climb on a rock and reach for the bird's wings. They touch them carefully. They feel warm and smooth. The bird flaps its wings. It makes a loud noise. Lily and Tom giggle. They like the bird. "Thank you, bird," Lily says. "You are very nice. And you are a good guard." "You are welcome, children," the bird says. "You are very polite. And you are good friends." The bird flies away. Lily and Tom wave goodbye. They go back to play in the park. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very obedient dog. He always listened to his owner, Mommy Jane. One day, Max had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big, yummy bone. Max wanted the bone very much. The next day, Max decided to find the big, yummy bone from his dream. He looked everywhere. He looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the yard. Max did not give up. He knew he had to keep looking to find the bone. Finally, Max found the big, yummy bone in the park. He was so happy! Max brought the bone back to Mommy Jane. She was proud of Max for being so obedient and for not giving up on his dream. Max learned that good things come to those who listen and never give up.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very eager to go to the park. She put on her shoes and ran to her mom. "Mom, can I go to the park?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but don't forget your stamp book." Sue grabbed her stamp book and skipped all the way to the park. She saw her friend, Tim, who was also eager to play. They laughed and played on the swings, and then they saw a big sign. The sign said, "Stamp Hunt Today!" Sue and Tim were so excited! They skipped around the park, finding stamps to put in their books. They found a stamp on the slide, a stamp by the tree, and even a stamp on the bench. "Look, I found another one!" Tim shouted. They put all the stamps in their books and were very happy. At the end of the day, Sue and Tim showed their moms all the stamps they found. Their moms were so proud of them. They knew that the stamp hunt would be a fun memory for Sue and Tim to share forever.

Lily was walking home from school. The sky was very blue and the clouds floated above. She was feeling happy and playful. All of a sudden, she heard a loud noise. She looked around and saw a giant cloud blocking her path. She thought it was strange, but kept walking towards it. When she got closer, the cloud grew bigger. It was almost twice as tall as Lily. The cloud said to Lily, "You must resist me!" But Lily didn't know what that meant. She was scared, but tried to be brave. She said, "No! I will not resist!" The cloud grew even bigger. It seemed angry. With one loud boom, it closed it's big, blue eyes and shot a bolt of lightning towards Lily. She screamed and tried to run away, but it was too late. The next morning, Lily's parents were heartbroken. They had lost their daughter to the giant blue cloud.
