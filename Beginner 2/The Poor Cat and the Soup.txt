Title: The Poor Cat and the Soup

Once upon a time, there was a poor cat. The cat lived in a small house. The cat was very sad because it had no food. One day, the cat found a can of soup. The cat wanted to eat the soup, but the can was very dirty. The cat decided to polish the can. It rubbed and rubbed the can with a cloth. The can became shiny and clean. The cat was very happy. It thought the soup would taste very good now. But when the cat opened the can, the soup was bad. The cat could not eat the soup. It became sadder and hungrier. The cat went to sleep with an empty belly. The poor cat did not have a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a giant. He was very big and strong. One day, a little girl named Anna went to visit the giant. She was not scared of him. She wanted to be his friend. "Hello, Mr. Giant," said Anna. "Can I be your friend?" The giant looked down at her and felt ashamed. He was so big, and she was so small. He thought he might scare her away. But Anna just smiled up at him. "Of course, we can be friends," said the giant. They played together every day. They had so much fun. The giant was not ashamed anymore. He was happy to have a friend like Anna. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Amy found a big box of junk. She saw an unusual toy. It was a funny robot. Amy liked the robot and wanted to play with it. Amy pushed a button on the robot. The robot said, "I can number things for you!" Amy was very happy. She asked the robot, "Can you number my toys?" The robot said, "Yes, I can!" The robot started to number Amy's toys. But something unexpected happened. The robot made a big mess! It mixed up all the toys. Amy was sad, but then she had an idea. She said, "Let's play a game and fix the mess together!" The robot agreed and they had fun fixing the mess.

Once upon a time, in a big blue sea, there was a shy oyster named Ollie. Ollie was not like the other oysters. He was scared to open up and show his pretty pearl inside. He liked to hide in the sand and watch the other sea friends play. One day, Ollie saw a crab named Carl. Carl liked to snap his big claws and make loud sounds. Ollie thought Carl was brave and wanted to be his friend. Ollie swam closer to Carl and said, "Hi, I am Ollie. Can I play with you?" Carl looked at Ollie and said, "Sure, but you have to show me your pearl first." Ollie was scared, but he wanted to be friends with Carl. So, he took a deep breath and opened up to show his pearl. Carl was so happy to see Ollie's pearl. He said, "Wow, your pearl is so pretty! I am glad you showed me." From that day on, Ollie and Carl were best friends. They played together every day. Ollie learned that it is good to be brave and share your true self with others. And Carl learned that being kind and patient can help others feel safe and happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to read a big book. He sat near a cold window. Tim was happy to read his book. He saw a cup on the table. The cup was very big. Tim wanted to drink from the cup. But he did not know what was inside. Tim drank from the cup. The drink was very cold. He did not like it. It made him feel bad. Tim was sad and went to bed.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a tall man. The tall man had a big sack. He liked to grab things and put them in his sack. One day, he saw a small, red ball. He wanted the ball, so he grabbed it and put it in his sack. The tall man went for a walk with his sack. He saw a little girl crying. The girl was sad because she lost her red ball. The man felt bad for the girl. He remembered the red ball in his sack. The tall man opened his sack and grabbed the red ball. He gave the ball to the little girl. The girl was very happy. She thanked the tall man and played with her red ball. The tall man felt good for helping the girl and went home with a smile.
