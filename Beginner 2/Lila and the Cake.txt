Category: Beginner
Title: Lila and the Cake

Lila was a big sister. She had a new infant brother, Max. She did not like Max. He cried a lot. He took Mom and Dad's time. He spoiled everything. One day, Lila was playing with her dolls. She had a tea party. She had a cake. She was happy. Then Max woke up. He cried. Mom came and picked him up. She said, "Lila, can you share your cake with Max? He is hungry." Lila was angry. She did not want to share. She said, "No! It is my cake. Max can not have it. He spoils everything." She grabbed the cake and ran away. Mom followed her. She said, "Lila, that is not nice. You have to share with your brother. He is your family. He loves you." Lila did not listen. She hid under the bed. She ate the cake. But the cake was bitter. It had soap on it. Lila had put soap on it to make it look pretty. She did not know it was bad. She spit it out. She felt sick. She cried. Mom heard her. She came and looked under the bed. She saw Lila and the cake. She said, "Oh, Lila, what did you do? You ate the soap cake. That is dangerous. You could get hurt. Why did you do that?" Lila said, "I did not want to share with Max. He spoils everything. I wanted the cake for me." Mom said, "Lila, that was a bad choice. You were selfish and mean. You hurt yourself and Max. You made me sad. You have to say sorry to Max and me. You have to learn to share and be kind." Lila felt bad. She said sorry to Mom and Max. But Mom was still mad. She said, "Lila, you are in trouble. You have to go to your room. You can not play with your dolls. You have to think about what you did." Lila went to her room. She was alone. She had no cake. She had no dolls. She had no fun. She spoiled everything. She wished she had shared. She wished she had been nice. She wished she had a good brother. She was bitter.

One day, a big, fierce dog named Spot lived in a small house. Spot loved to run and play outside. Sometimes, he would destroy his toys because he was so strong and full of energy. One sunny day, Spot was playing outside when he saw a little, scared cat. The cat said, "Please don't hurt me, Spot. I am very small and you are very big and fierce." Spot thought for a moment and said, "I will not hurt you, little cat. Let's be friends and play together." As they played together, something unexpected happened. The little cat jumped on Spot's back and started to ride him like a horse! Spot was surprised but he did not mind. They played together all day and became the best of friends. And from that day on, Spot never destroyed his toys again.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. He saw a big bag with white things in it. Tom was confused. He asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom explained, "That is rice, Tom. We eat it with our food." Tom wanted to help his mom. He tried to pick up the bag of rice, but it was too heavy for him. He felt frustrated. His mom saw him trying hard and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I will help you." Together, they carried the bag of rice to their home. Tom was happy that he could help his mom, even if it was just a little bit. They cooked the rice and ate it for dinner. It was yummy, and Tom felt proud.

One day, a pretty bird named Sally lived in a big tree. Sally liked to fly and sing. She had a friend named Tim. Tim was a small dog who liked to run and play. One day, Sally saw a gate. She told Tim, "Look, a gate! Let's go!" They went to the gate, but it was closed. They wanted to see what was behind it, but they could not open it. Tim said, "Let's find a way to open the gate!" They tried to push and pull, but the gate did not move. They were sad. Then, a big wind came and blew the gate open. Sally and Tim were happy. They went inside and saw a big party! There were other birds and dogs playing and having fun. Sally and Tim joined the party and made new friends. They learned that sometimes, when you keep trying, good things can happen.
