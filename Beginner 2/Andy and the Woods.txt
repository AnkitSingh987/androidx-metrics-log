Category: Beginner
Title: Andy and the Woods

Andy was walking through the woods when he noticed something shining in the ground. He bent down and discovered a new root! He was so excited, he ran over to his friend Bob and said "Look what I found!" Bob was amazed and said, "That looks so cool - can I touch it?" Andy nodded, and Bob carefully picked up the root. But when he touched it, he started shivering! He dropped the root and said, "It's so cold!" Andy was surprised and asked "Why is it cold?" Bob didn't know, so the two friends decided to ask the wise old man who lived in the forest. He told them that the root was a shiny new magical root and it was so special that it made Bob shiver. Andy and Bob were so excited! They thanked the old man, and then went home, carrying the new magical root with them.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his bone. One day, Max found a very old, ancient bone in his yard. His friend, a big cat named Tom, saw Max with the ancient bone. Tom wanted the bone too. So, Tom said, "Max, can I play with your bone?" Max did not want to share. So, Max ran away with the bone and teased Tom. Tom chased Max all around the yard. They ran and ran, laughing and teasing each other. Then, Max stopped and looked at Tom. Max said, "Okay, Tom. We can share the ancient bone." Max and Tom played with the ancient bone together. They took turns and shared. They had a lot of fun and were happy to be friends. From that day on, Max and Tom always shared their toys and played together.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was gray, and Tim thought it might rain. He saw a big frame in the yard. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the frame?" Mom said, "Yes, but if it starts to rain, come inside." Tim was happy. He had an easy plan. He would make a tent with the frame and a big sheet. Tim played with the frame, and soon it started to rain. But he was ready. He had made his tent! Tim was dry and smiled. The rain made a nice sound on his tent. When the rain stopped, Tim went inside to tell mom about his fun day. Mom was proud of Tim for making the tent.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear a big, red bow in her hair. She felt very smart when she wore it. One day, Sue went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Sue saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he did not have a bow. Sue thought about how she felt when she wore her bow. She wanted Tim to feel smart too. So, Sue took off her bow and gave it to Tim. Tim said, "Thank you, Sue! I feel smart now!" Sue smiled and they played together all day. Sue learned that sharing with others can make everyone feel good. And that was the best feeling of all.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to help his friends. One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad. She lost her crayon. Max wanted to help Lily find her crayon. They looked and looked. They found a big box. The box was moving! They were scared, but they opened the box. Inside the box, they found a cat playing with the crayon. Lily was happy to have her crayon back. But the cat was sad. It wanted to play with the crayon too. Max had an idea. They could all play together! So Max, Lily, and the cat played with the crayon and had lots of fun. They all became best friends.

Once there was a brave little boy named Charlie. Charlie had a big, blue truck. He loved taking it for a drive and zoom around town. But one day, sadly, Charlie ran out of fuel. Charlie asked his mom, “Mom, can I borrow some fuel for my truck?” Mom said, “Yes, but we need to get ready first. Go and get your shoes on, okay?” Charlie quickly put on his blue shoes and shouted, “I’m ready!” Mom said, “Great! Let’s go to the store and get some fuel.” When they arrived, Charlie was so excited to buy the fuel. They bought the fuel and put it into the truck. Charlie said, “Yay, I can take my truck for a drive now!” Mom smiled as Charlie drove away. Charlie was so happy that he had borrowed the fuel that he wanted.
