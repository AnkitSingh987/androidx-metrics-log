Title: Pepper the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Pepper. Pepper loved to play with his ball and run around in the park. One day, Pepper saw a sign that said "Big Dog Contest." Pepper wanted to join the contest, but he was a little scared because he was very small. He asked his friend, a big dog named Max, to help him. Max said, "Don't worry, Pepper. You are small, but you are very fast and smart." The day of the contest came, and Pepper and Max went to the park. There were many big dogs there, but Pepper was brave. He ran very fast and jumped very high. Everyone clapped and cheered for Pepper. At the end of the contest, Pepper won a big prize. He was so happy, and he thanked Max for helping him. From that day on, Pepper knew that even a small, cute dog like him could do big things.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She wanted to make a special treat. So she got out a bowl and she poured some flour inside. Then she added a dash of salt and some oil. The girl started to mix it up until it looked like dough. Next she put the dough on the table and she spread it with her hands. The girl put cheese and tomatoes on top. Then she opened the oven and she put the pizza inside. While it cooked, it began to smell so yummy. Finally, the girl opened the oven and the pizza looked ready. But when she took it out, it was not very nice. It looked dull and dry. The girl felt sad, but then she had a wonderful idea. She added some herbs and spices and put the pizza back in the oven. Soon the oven door opened and out came the pizza. It smelled so delicious and it looked nice and golden. The girl smiled, it was perfect.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to take pictures with her camera. One day, she saw a cute bunny in her garden. She wanted to take a picture, but she could not find her camera. Lily asked her friend, Tom, "Can you lend me your camera, please?" Tom said, "Sure, here you go!" Lily was happy and took the camera. She went back to the garden to take a picture of the bunny. When Lily got close to the bunny, it looked frightened. Lily said, "Don't be scared, little bunny. I just want to take your picture." The bunny stopped being frightened and let Lily take its picture. Lily thanked Tom for lending her the camera, and they both looked at the picture of the cute bunny. They were very happy.

Sara loves her toy sheep. It is soft and white and has a lot of wool. She likes to hug it and play with it every day. She calls it Baa-Baa. One day, Sara goes to the farm with her mom and dad. She sees many real sheep in the field. They are big and loud and have a lot of wool too. Sara thinks they are cute. She runs to the fence and says, "Hello, sheep! Do you want to play with me and Baa-Baa?" One of the sheep comes closer and looks at Sara and her toy. It says, "Baa! Baa!" Sara smiles and says, "Yes, you are Baa-Baa too! But this is my Baa-Baa. He belongs to me. He is my friend." The sheep nods and says, "Baa! Baa! I understand. I have friends too. They belong to me. They are my flock." Sara says, "That's nice. Can we be friends too?" The sheep says, "Baa! Baa! Of course. You are cute and kind. You can be part of our flock." Sara is happy. She says, "Thank you, sheep. You are cute and kind too. I like your wool. Can I touch it?" The sheep says, "Baa! Baa! Sure. You can touch it. It is soft and warm." Sara reaches out and pets the sheep. She feels its wool. It is soft and warm, just like her toy. She says, "Wow, your wool is like Baa-Baa's wool. I love it." The sheep says, "Baa! Baa! I love it too. It keeps me cozy. And I love your Baa-Baa. He is like me. He is a sheep." Sara says, "Yes, he is a sheep. But he is also a toy. He is special. He is my Baa-Baa." The sheep says, "Baa! Baa! I know. He is special. He is your Baa-Baa. And you are special. You are my friend." Sara hugs her toy and the sheep. She says, "Thank you, friend. You are special too. You are my sheep." The end.
