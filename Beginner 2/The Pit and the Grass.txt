Category: Beginner
Title: The Pit and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a big pit in the middle of the playground. All the kids were scared to go near it. One day, a girl named Sally had a great idea. She wanted to decorate the pit to make it look nice and fun. Sally asked her friend Tom, "Can you help me decorate the pit?" Tom said, "Yes! Let's make it pretty." They found colorful rocks and flowers to put around the pit. They also drew happy faces on the rocks. But when they tried to sit near the pit, it was still uncomfortable. So, they went to find soft grass to sit on. They put the grass around the pit, and it felt much better. Now, all the kids loved the decorated pit and played near it every day.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. Sometimes they share, and sometimes they fight. One day, Lily sees a big, soft blanket on Ben's bed. She wants to use it for her dolls. She thinks Ben is not looking, and she takes the blanket. She puts it on the floor and makes a house for her dolls. But Ben sees what Lily does. He gets angry and runs to Lily. He says, "That is my blanket! You took it from me! Give it back!" Lily feels scared and sad. She likes the blanket, but she knows she did a bad thing. She says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I wanted to play with your blanket. It is so nice and cozy. But you are right, I took it from you. That was not honest. Here, you can have it back." Ben takes the blanket from Lily. He is still mad, but he sees that Lily is sorry. He says, "Thank you, Lily. I forgive you. You are my sister, and I love you. But next time, please ask me before you take something from me. OK?" Lily nods and hugs Ben. She says, "OK, Ben. I love you too. And next time, I will ask you. Can we play together with the blanket now?" Ben smiles and says, "Yes, we can. Come on, let's make a fort with the blanket. It will be fun!" Lily and Ben play together with the blanket. They are happy and honest. They are good twins.

Once upon a time, there was a perfect beach. On this beach, there lived a jellyfish. The jellyfish had many friends in the sea. They liked to play all day. One day, the jellyfish saw a big boat. The boat was going to load some things. The jellyfish wanted to help. It asked its friends to come too. They all went to the boat. The jellyfish and its friends helped the boat load the things. The boat's man was very happy. He said, "Thank you, jellyfish and friends!" They all went back to the sea to play. They had a fun day at the perfect beach.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to swim in the pool. They had fun splashing and diving and playing with toys. One day, Lily brought a new toy, a yellow duck, and showed it to Ben. Ben wanted to play with the duck, but Lily said no. She said it was her duck and she did not want to share. Ben was angry and sad. He thought Lily was rude and mean. He waited until Lily was not looking and grabbed the duck from her. He ran away with it and threw it over the fence. Lily saw what he did and started to cry. She ran after him and shouted, "Give me back my duck! You are a bad friend!" Ben felt sorry for what he did. He realized he was wrong and he hurt Lily's feelings. He climbed over the fence and got the duck. He came back to Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was rude and mean. I should not have taken your duck. Can you forgive me?" Lily looked at Ben and saw that he was sorry. She wiped her tears and smiled. She said, "I forgive you, Ben. You are my friend. But next time, please ask me before you take my toy. Maybe we can share it." Ben nodded and hugged Lily. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are my friend too. And you are very nice. Can we swim together again?" Lily nodded and hugged Ben back. She said, "Yes, let's swim together again. And play with the duck. It is more fun when we share." They went back to the pool and swam and played with the duck. They were happy and forgot their fight. They learned to forgive and share and be kind.
