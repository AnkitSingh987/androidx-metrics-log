Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tried

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a persistent boy. He always tried his best. One day, he wanted to learn how to climb steps. He asked his mom, "Mom, can you teach me how to climb steps?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. I will help you. We will value each step you take." Tim and his mom went to the steps. Tim tried to climb them, but it was hard. He fell, but he did not give up. He tried again and again. His mom cheered for him. Finally, Tim climbed all the steps. He was so happy. His mom hugged him and said, "Good job, Tim! You did it! I am proud of you." Tim learned that being persistent is important. He knew that he could do anything if he tried his best. And he always valued each step he took in life.

One day, a man and his wife went to the park. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. They were very happy. The man saw an open ice cream shop and said, "Let's get some ice cream!" The wife said, "Yes, I want to taste a new flavor today." They walked to the shop and looked at all the ice cream. There were many colors and flavors. The man picked a big scoop of red ice cream. The wife picked a big scoop of green ice cream. They sat on a bench and tasted their ice cream. The man said, "Yum! My ice cream tastes like cherry." The wife said, "Yum! My ice cream tastes like apple." They smiled, ate their ice cream, and enjoyed the sunny day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. This rat was very glad. He liked to play and run all day. The rat had a lot of friends. They played together and had fun. One day, the glad rat found a big, red apple. He wanted to eat it. The rat started to chew the apple. It was yummy and sweet. His friends saw him and wanted to try the apple too. The glad rat shared the apple with his friends. They all took turns to chew it. They were all very happy. The apple was gone soon, but they were full and glad. They played more games and had a great day.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and talk to each other. One day, they find a big box in the yard. They wonder what is inside. They open the box and see a lot of shiny things. They are handles. They are for doors and drawers. Sara and Ben think they are pretty. They want to play with them. They take some handles out of the box and pretend they are keys and rings and bracelets. They have fun making noises and stories with the handles. They are very friendly and share the handles. But then, they hear a loud voice. It is Mom. She is angry. She says the handles are not toys. They are for the new kitchen. She says Sara and Ben have to put them back in the box and say sorry. Sara and Ben are scared. They do not want Mom to be mad. They quickly put the handles back in the box and say sorry to Mom. They hope she will be friendly again. They hug each other and say they are still friends. They decide to play with something else.

Once upon a time, there was a big cliff near a small village. On top of the cliff, there was a spicy apple tree. The apples from this tree were very special. They made people feel warm and happy when they ate them. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to get some spicy apples for his friends. He climbed up the cliff with a big bag. When he reached the top, he picked as many apples as he could. Tim was very happy and excited to share them with his friends. After he finished picking the apples, Tim climbed down the cliff very carefully. He went back to the village and gave the spicy apples to his friends. They all ate the apples together and felt warm and happy. From that day on, they always remembered the fun adventure they had with the spicy apples from the big cliff.
