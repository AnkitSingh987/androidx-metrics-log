Category: Beginner
Title: Mia the Girl and the Scarf

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Mia. Mia had a pretty scarf. She loved her scarf very much. One day, her scarf got a hole. Mia was sad. Mia wanted to fix her scarf. She tried to sew it with a needle and thread. But Mia did not know how to sew. She tried and tried, but the hole did not go away. Mia had an idea. She climbed high on a chair to get glue. But Mia fell off the chair. She got hurt and cried. Her scarf was still not fixed. Mia was very sad. The end.

One day, a smooth rock and a penny were friends. They liked to play together. The smooth rock said, "I like to roll fast." The penny said, "I like to roll too, but I can't roll as fast as you." One day, they saw a big hill. They wanted to race down the hill. The smooth rock said, "I will win!" The penny said, "I will try my best." They started to roll down the hill. As they rolled, something unexpected happened. The penny started to roll faster and faster. The smooth rock could not keep up. The smooth rock said, "I quit!" The penny won the race. The smooth rock was sad, but the penny said, "We are still friends. Let's race again another day." The smooth rock was happy again, and they both went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lily's mom said, "Let's start making some soup for dinner." Lily was very excited and clapped her hands. They took a big pot and put it on the stove. Lily's mom put water, vegetables, and chicken in the pot. They waited for the soup to cook. Lily could smell the soup and it was incredible. She knew it would taste very good. When the soup was done, Lily and her mom ate it together. They both loved the soup. It was the most incredible soup they ever had. Lily was so happy that she could help her mom make the soup. From that day on, Lily always helped her mom cook in the kitchen.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big peach on the ground. He picked it up and wanted to eat it. But, he did not know if it was a real peach or a fake peach. He showed it to his friend, Sue. "Is this a real peach or a fake peach?" Tim asked. Sue looked at the peach and said, "I don't know. Let's run to my mom and ask her." So, they ran to Sue's mom. She looked at the peach and said, "This is a fake peach. It is not good to eat." Tim and Sue were sad because they wanted to eat the peach. They went back outside and saw a little girl crying. She said, "I lost my fake peach. I played with it all the time." Tim gave the girl the fake peach, and she was happy. But then, Tim and Sue saw a big dog. The dog ran to them and took the fake peach. The dog ate the fake peach and got very sick. Tim, Sue, and the little girl were sad. The bad ending made them learn that they should be careful with things they find and always ask a grown-up for help.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the garden. He liked to remove rocks and look for bugs under them. Tim had a lot of fun in the garden. One day, Tim went to the garden to play. He saw a big rock he had not seen before. Tim wanted to remove the rock and see what was under it. He pushed and pushed, but the rock would not move. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock moved by itself! Tim was very surprised. Under the rock, there was a tiny, friendly bug. The bug said, "Thank you for trying to help me, Tim!" The bug had been stuck under the rock. Tim and the bug became good friends, and they played in the garden together every day.
