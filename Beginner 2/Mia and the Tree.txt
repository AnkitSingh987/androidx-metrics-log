Category: Beginner
Title: Mia and the Tree

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia and her dog, Max, went to the park to celebrate her birthday. They found a big tree with lots of shade to have their picnic. Mia was very careful when she laid down the soft blanket under the tree. Mia said to Max, "We must be careful with our food, so we don't make a mess." Max wagged his tail and agreed. They shared yummy sandwiches and sweet fruits under the tree. The shade kept them cool from the hot sun. After they ate, Mia and Max played with a big, bouncy ball. They laughed and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Mia said, "Thank you, Max, for celebrating my birthday with me!" Max barked happily, and they walked home together.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Tara. Tara was three years old and loved to bounce. Everywhere she went she bounced so high she felt like she could touch the sky. One day Tara was bouncing down the street when she saw a dog on the side of the road. The dog was grey and had smooth fur. Tara asked the dog, "Can I bounce with you?" The dog replied, "Welcome! I love to bounce too." So Tara and the dog bounced together. They bounced so high that they could jump over a tree. When they landed back on the ground, they were both really happy. Tara said to the dog, "That was so much fun! Let's do it again!" And they did. Every day they bounced and had lots of fun.

Once there was a girl named Milly. Milly was very special because she was gifted. Everywhere she went she made friends easily. One day, Milly asked her mom, “Do I need a gift?” Her mom said, “No, Milly, you don’t need a gift.” Milly was sad so she went outside to play with her friends. She saw a box with a big bow on top. She opened the box and saw a beautiful dress inside. Milly ran to her mom and asked, “Did I need this gift?” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, Milly, you did need this gift. I think you’re very gifted!” Milly was so happy because she got her gift. She thanked her mom then ran outside to show her friends her new dress. With her new gift, Milly and her friends had a lot of fun!

Once upon a time, in a big hot land, there was a small oasis. In the oasis, there was a very hungry bird named Bobo. Bobo was sad because he could not find any food to eat. One day, Bobo saw a big tree with lots of yummy bugs in it. But a big mean cat named Mimi lived under the tree. Mimi said, "This is my tree. You can't eat the bugs here!" Bobo was so sad that he started to weep. Bobo did not give up. He had a plan. He found a big stick and used it to scare Mimi away. Then, Bobo ate all the yummy bugs he wanted. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dirty hook in his yard. He did not know what to do with it. So, he asked his mom, "Mom, what can I do with this dirty hook?" His mom said, "Let's clean it and hang a sign on it. The sign can say 'Be Kind'." Tim liked the idea. He and his mom cleaned the hook and made a pretty sign. They hung the sign on the hook and put it in their front yard. All the people who walked by saw the sign. They smiled and said nice things to each other. Tim felt happy because his dirty hook helped people be kind. The moral of the story is that even something dirty can be used for good if we try.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. Sometimes they bumped their cars into each other and laughed. But then Tom saw a big red car on the shelf. It looked shiny and new. He wanted to play with it. He climbed on the couch and reached for the car. But the car was too far and he could not grab it. "Tom, be careful!" Lily said. "That is dad's car. He does not like us to touch it." Tom did not listen. He stretched his arm more and more. He felt the handle of the car. He pulled it hard. But the handle was not strong. It snapped and broke off. The car fell to the floor with a loud bang. Tom and Lily were scared. They heard dad coming from the kitchen. He looked angry. He saw the broken car and the handle in Tom's hand. "Tom, what did you do?" dad asked. "That was my special car. You know you are not supposed to play with it." Tom felt sorry. He did not mean to break the car. He wanted to say sorry, but he could not find the words. He started to cry. Lily felt sorry for Tom. She knew he liked cars. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. It was an accident. Maybe dad can fix the car. We can help him." Dad saw Tom and Lily crying and hugging. He felt sorry too. He knew they were just curious and playful. He picked up the car and the handle. He said, "It's okay, kids. I'm not mad. I know you did not mean to break the car. Maybe I can fix it. But you have to promise me not to touch it again. It is not a toy. It is a gift from grandpa. He gave it to me when I was a little boy like you." Tom and Lily nodded. They said, "We promise, dad. We are sorry. We love you." Dad smiled. He said, "I love you too. Now, why don't you play with your own cars? They are more fun and safe." Tom and Lily agreed. They went back to their toy cars. They played nicely and did not touch dad's car again. They learned their lesson.
