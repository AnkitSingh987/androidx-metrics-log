Category: Beginner
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his uncle. One day, his uncle came to visit. Tim was very happy. "Let's play!" said Tim. They ran around and around in the yard. Tim felt dizzy. He laughed and laughed. His uncle felt dizzy too. They both fell on the grass. "Let's settle down," said uncle. They sat under a big tree. The tree made them feel cool. They talked and talked. Tim loved his uncle very much. They played and talked until the sun went down. Tim's mom called them inside. It was time for dinner. Tim and his uncle ate and ate. They were very tired but happy.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big, black ball on the grass. Tim thought it was just a ball to play with, but it seemed to be more than that. He kicked the ball and it went very high in the sky. Tim was very surprised. The black ball had a special power. When he kicked it again, it made a loud sound like thunder. Tim was scared at first, but then he laughed. He liked the power of the black ball. He wanted to show his friends, so he ran to find them. When Tim showed his friends the black ball, they were all amazed. They took turns kicking it and making loud sounds. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball turned into a big, black bird! The bird flew away, and the children were sad. They missed the fun they had with the black ball, but they were happy they had a great time together.

Lily and Ben were bored. They had nothing to do at home. Their toys were old and their books were read. They wanted to go outside and play, but it was raining. "Can we go to the park, Mom?" Lily asked. "No, dear, it's too wet and cold. Maybe later, when the sun comes out," Mom said. "Can we watch TV, Dad?" Ben asked. "No, son, you've watched enough today. Why don't you draw something or play a game?" Dad said. Lily and Ben sighed. They looked out the window and saw the gray sky and the puddles. They wished they could have some fun. They decided to wander around the house and look for something new. They went to the attic, where they found a big box. Inside the box, they saw a sail. It was white and blue, with a picture of a fish on it. "Wow, look at this!" Lily said. "It's a sail from a boat!" "Maybe we can use it to make a tent!" Ben said. They took the sail and some chairs and made a tent in the living room. They crawled inside and pretended they were on a boat. They used pillows as oars and a flashlight as a lantern. They made sounds of waves and seagulls. "This is fun!" Lily said. "We are sailing on the sea!" "Yes, it is!" Ben said. "We are having an adventure!" They forgot they were bored and played for a long time. They imagined they saw islands and pirates and mermaids. They had a picnic and sang songs. They laughed and smiled. Mom and Dad came to see what they were doing. They were happy to see them having fun. "You two are very creative!" Mom said. "You made a sail into a tent!" "You two are very brave!" Dad said. "You sailed on the sea!" Lily and Ben hugged them and thanked them. They invited them to join their boat. Mom and Dad agreed and crawled inside the tent. They all played together and had a good time. They were not bored anymore. They were happy.

Once upon a time, a little bird named Tim lived in a small tree. One day, Tim saw a big fire near his tree. The fire made a lot of ash. Tim was scared and flew away to find a new home. Tim met a big bird named Sam. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, little friend?" Tim said, "My home has a big fire and now there is ash everywhere. I need to find a new home." Sam wanted to help Tim. They flew together to look for a new tree. They found a nice tree with a little hole. Tim tasted the air and said, "This air is clean and fresh. There is no taste of ash here." Tim was happy and thanked Sam. They became good friends and lived in the new tree together.
