Category: Beginner
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a lot of stuff in her room. Some of her stuff was interesting, like her colorful toys and big books. Lucy loved to play with her stuff every day. One day, Lucy found a small, interesting toy that she had never seen before. She showed it to her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, that's an interesting toy! Where did it come from?" Lucy said, "I don't know, but I like it. It's fun to play with." Later, Lucy's mom came into the room and saw the interesting toy. She said, "Lucy, this toy doesn't belong to you. It belongs to your brother, Tim." Lucy felt sad, but she gave the toy back to Tim. Tim said, "Thank you, Lucy. You can play with it too." Lucy and Tim played with the interesting toy together, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to collect stamps. One day, she saw a big stamp in a shop. It was very pretty, so she bought it. That night, Lily put the stamp on her bed and went to sleep. But she woke up in the middle of the night, feeling alert. She heard a whisper. "Lily, can you help me?" said the voice. Lily looked around and saw that the stamp was talking to her! "Please, take me back to the shop. I miss my family," the stamp whispered. Lily felt sad for the stamp, so she took it back to the shop the next day. The shopkeeper was surprised, but he thanked Lily for bringing the stamp back. Lily learned that it is important to care for others and help them when they need it. And the stamp was happy to be back with its family.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a big hill and decided to go up. As they climbed, they met a silly bird who couldn't fly. "Hi, I'm Tim and this is Max," said Tim. "Why can't you fly?" The bird replied, "I'm a stupid bird. I don't know how to fly." Tim and Max wanted to help the bird. They all went up the hill together. At the top of the hill, they found a big tree. Tim and Max helped the bird go up the tree. The bird tried to fly and finally, the bird could fly. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim and Max. They all went back down the hill, playing and laughing together.

Once upon a time, there was a unique tree. This tree was very big and had many leaves. It was in the middle of a sunny park. One hot day, a little dog came to the park. The dog was very hot and tired. He saw the big tree and went under it. The tree gave the dog shade. The dog was happy and not hot anymore. Every day, the little dog came to the park to play. The unique tree always gave him shade. They became good friends. The dog and the tree were happy together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play and joke with each other. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big slide and run to it. Lily goes first. She slides down fast and laughs. Ben waits for his turn. He wants to joke with Lily. He thinks of a funny thing to do. He slides down with his foot up in the air. He hopes Lily will see his foot and laugh too. But Lily does not see his foot. She only sees his face. She thinks he is making a funny face at her. She laughs and says, "You look silly, Ben!" Ben is confused. He looks at his foot and says, "No, look at my foot, Lily! It is a joke!" Lily looks at his foot and sees it in the air. She does not think it is funny. She thinks it is rude. She says, "That is not a joke, Ben! That is mean! You should not put your foot up like that. It is not fair to the other kids. They might get hurt by your foot." Ben feels bad. He did not mean to be mean. He only wanted to make Lily laugh. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I did not know it was mean. I will not do it again. Can we still be friends?" Lily thinks for a moment. She sees that Ben is sorry. She says, "OK, Ben. I forgive you. But you have to be careful with your foot. And you have to find better jokes. Can you do that?" Ben nods. He says, "Yes, Lily. I can do that. Thank you for being my friend. You are very fair." They hug and smile. They go to play some more. They have fun and joke with each other. But they do not use their feet. They use their words.
