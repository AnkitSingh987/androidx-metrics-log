Category: Beginner
Title: The Wise Bear and the Rainbow

Once upon a time there lived a wise bear. He knew many things and he always tried to help his friends. One day he met a silly rabbit who was looking sad. "What's wrong?" asked the wise bear. The rabbit sighed. "I wish I could make a rainbow. I'm feeling lonely and sad, and I think a rainbow would make me feel better." The wise bear thought for a moment and then smiled. "I'll help you make a rainbow," he said. So the two friends worked together to make a beautiful rainbow. When it was finished, the silly rabbit was delighted. "Thank you," he said. "You have supported me, and it played a big part in making me feel happy again." The wise bear smiled, happy that he had been able to help his friend. From that day on, the friends always worked together to make rainbows and spread happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to play and run all day. He lived in a big yard with a big red ball. One day, Max saw a little bird in the yard. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. Max wanted to help the bird, so he stayed by its side. He was very gentle and did not scare the bird. Max's owner saw them and said, "Good boy, Max! Stay by the bird's heel!" Max stayed close to the bird until it felt better. The bird's wing got better, and it flew away. Max was happy that he could help his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house in the woods. In the house lived a small boy named Tim and his mom. Tim liked to play outside with his red ball. One day, while Tim was playing, he saw a big dog. The dog said, "Woof! Woof!" Tim said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play?" The dog wagged its tail and played with Tim. But Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim. The dog might bite." Tim and the dog played for a long time. They were having so much fun. But then, Tim tripped and had a fall. The dog stopped and looked at Tim. Tim was scared the dog would bite him now. But the dog did not bite. Instead, the dog licked Tim's face and helped him up. Tim's mom saw this and smiled. She knew the dog was a good friend. From that day on, Tim and the dog played together every day, and they were always careful not to fall or bite.

One day, a little red shrimp was swimming in the sea. The shrimp saw a big fish and was scared. The shrimp swam fast to hide behind a rock. The big fish saw the shrimp and swam closer. The fish said, "Hello, little shrimp. Can I be your friend?" The shrimp was still scared, but he wanted to be brave. The shrimp said, "Yes, we can be friends." The big fish and the shrimp swam together. The shrimp was happy to have a new friend. They played in the water and had fun. The shrimp was very hungry. He asked the big fish, "Can you help me find food?" The big fish said, "Yes, I can help you." But the big fish was not a good friend. He was hungry too. When they found food, the big fish ate the shrimp instead. The shrimp was gone, and the big fish swam away.

Once there was a bee named Bob. Bob lived in a big hive with many other bees. The hive was in a tree near a big flower field. Bob and his bee friends worked hard to make honey. One day, Bob felt nervous. He wanted to try something new. He decided to separate from his bee friends and go on an adventure. Bob flew far away from the hive to see what he could find. Bob saw many new things on his adventure. He saw a big pond with ducks in it. He saw a little girl playing with her dog. At the end of the day, Bob was happy but tired. He flew back to his hive, excited to tell his bee friends about his adventure.
