Category: Beginner
Title: Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim found a mysterious net in his yard. He was very excited and called his friend, Sam, to come see. "Sam, come look at this net I found!" Tim said. Sam ran over and looked at the net with Tim. They decided to go to the park to play with the net. They saw a big tree and tried to catch the leaves with the net. They had so much fun. Then, they saw a sign that said, "Do not read." They didn't know what "read" meant, so they kept playing. While they played, a big wind came and took the net away. The net flew high in the sky and got stuck in a very tall tree. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not get the net back. They went home without the net and never saw it again.

Bunny and Fox were friends. They liked to play in the woods. One day, they found a patch of red berries. They were hungry and wanted to eat them. "Look, Bunny, red berries! Let's hop over and get some!" Fox said. "OK, Fox, but be careful. Red berries can be sour or sweet. We don't know which ones are good," Bunny said. They hopped over to the patch and each picked a berry. They put them in their mouths and bit down. "Yuck!" Fox spat out his berry. "This one is sour and bitter. It makes my mouth hurt." "Yum!" Bunny smiled and ate his berry. "This one is sweet and juicy. It makes my mouth happy." They tried another berry each. Fox got another sour one and Bunny got another sweet one. "This is not fair, Bunny. Why do you get all the good berries and I get all the bad ones?" Fox complained. "I don't know, Fox. Maybe you have bad luck. Or maybe you have a tight nose. A tight nose can't smell the difference between sour and sweet," Bunny said. "A tight nose? What does that mean?" Fox asked. "It means your nose is too small or too closed. You can't breathe well or smell well. You need to open your nose and sniff the berries before you eat them. Then you can tell which ones are good and which ones are bad," Bunny explained. "Really? How do I open my nose?" Fox wondered. "Like this," Bunny said. He showed Fox how to flare his nostrils and inhale deeply. "Now you try it." Fox copied Bunny and opened his nose. He sniffed the berries and found one that smelled sweet. He ate it and smiled. "Wow, Bunny, you are right. This one is good. It tastes like honey. Opening my nose works. Thank you for teaching me," Fox said. "You're welcome, Fox. I'm glad you like it. Now we can share the good berries and be happy," Bunny said. They hopped around the patch and ate more sweet berries. They were full and satisfied. They thanked the patch for the berries and went back to their homes. They were still friends and they learned something new.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat liked to work very hard. He was always busy. One day, the rat met a big cat. The cat was not nice. He was ignorant. He did not know how to work like the rat. The rat wanted to help the cat. They worked together. The cat learned to work hard like the rat. They became good friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Bob. Bob lived in a big forest with his friends. Bob was a very special reindeer, because he had expensive, shiny bells on his neck. They made a beautiful sound when he walked. One day, Bob and his friends were playing near a big tree. They saw a big pot with something inside. "What's in the pot?" asked Bob's friend, Sam. "I don't know," said Bob, "let's stir it and see!" Bob used a stick to stir the pot. It was full of yummy soup! Bob and his friends were hungry, so they decided to share the soup. They all took turns stirring the pot and then they ate the soup together. After they finished eating, they played more games in the forest. Bob's shiny, expensive bells jingled as they ran and played. They all had a great day, and they never forgot the day they found the big pot of soup.
