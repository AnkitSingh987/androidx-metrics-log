Category: Beginner
Title: Bob the Hairy Sheep and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a hairy sheep named Bob. Bob had a lot of wool. He lived on a farm with other animals. Bob liked to play with his friends. One day, Bob heard a ring. It was a bell. The farmer said, "Come, sheep! Time to get your wool cut!" Bob and his friends went to the farmer. They were excited. The farmer cut Bob's wool. Bob felt nice and cool. He said, "Thank you, farmer!" The farmer smiled and said, "You're welcome, Bob!" Now, Bob was ready to play with his friends again.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They walked and walked until they found a nice place to settle and make a camp. They were very happy to rest and play together. Tim said to Spot, "Let's play catch!" They played with a ball, but Spot ran too fast and hit a tree. Spot felt hurt and cried. Tim hugged Spot and said, "It's okay, Spot. I will help you feel better." Tim took care of Spot at the camp. He gave Spot water and love. Soon, Spot felt better and they were happy again. They played gently and had a great day at their camp.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. He thought there was a monster in his closet. One night, he heard a noise. Tim was brave and said, "Who is there?" A voice came from the closet, "I am the monster. I am trapped. Can you help me?" Tim wanted to help, so he went to the closet. He used a key to unlock the door. Inside, he found a big, thick book. The monster was not real. It was just a picture in the book. Tim laughed and said, "Silly me! There is no monster. It is just a book." From that day on, Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. He knew there was no monster in his closet.

---

One day, a girl named Lily and her friend Tom were playing with a big hoop. They liked to roll the hoop and see how far it would go. Lily said, "Let's see if we can fit in the hoop!" Tom tried first. He put one leg in the hoop, but it hurt a little. "Ouch!" he said, "I can't fit in the hoop." Lily tried next. She put her arms in the hoop, but it hurt her too. "I can't fit either," she said. Just then, a little dog came running up to them. The dog jumped right into the hoop! Lily and Tom were very surprised. The dog fit in the hoop without getting hurt. They laughed and clapped their hands. The dog was happy too, and they all played together for the rest of the day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a stick. He liked the stick a lot. He wanted to mark his name on the stick. So, he took a pen and marked his name on it. Tim was very happy. Tim went to the park with his stick. He met his friend, Sue. Sue saw the stick and said, "Wow, what a nice stick! Can I play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, you can play with it, but be careful. I marked my name on it." Sue played with the stick, but then something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the stick away. Tim and Sue were sad. They looked for the stick everywhere, but they could not find it. Just then, a nice dog came to the park. The dog had the stick in its mouth. Tim and Sue were so grateful. They thanked the dog and went back to playing with the stick. They were very happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, orange cat named Tom. Tom liked to play and eat. One day, Tom met a pretty, orange cat named Sally. They played together and had so much fun. Tom thought Sally was very nice. Tom said to Sally, "I like you. Will you marry me?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Tom, I will marry you!" They were very happy and wanted to have a party to celebrate. Tom and Sally invited all their friends to their party. There were games, balloons, and yummy food. Everyone danced and laughed. At the end of the party, Tom and Sally said, "Thank you for coming to our party. We are happy to be married and have friends like you."
