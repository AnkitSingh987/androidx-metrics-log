Category: Beginner
Title: Lily the Girl and the Comb

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to sing. Every day, she would sing with her friends. They were very enthusiastic when they sang together. One day, Lily found a comb. She wanted to use the comb to make her hair pretty before she sang with her friends. But her brother, Tom, took the comb away from her. He said it was his comb. Lily was sad and didn't want to sing without her pretty hair. Lily and Tom went to their mom for help. Mom said they needed to share the comb. Tom let Lily use the comb first. Then, Lily made her hair pretty and sang with her friends. Tom saw how happy Lily was and decided to join them. Together, they all sang with big, enthusiastic smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to act in her room. She would act like a cat, a dog, or a bird. She loved to play pretend. One day, Mia's room was dark. She was scared. She called her mom and said, "Mommy, it's dark. I don't like it." Her mom came in and opened the window. The sun made the room bright again. Mia was happy. She said, "Thank you, Mommy." Then, she went back to acting in her room. She acted like a brave lion and a strong bear. Mia was not scared anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the sand. He had a toy car and a small shovel. He wanted to dig a deep hole for his car to drive in. So, he started to dig and dig. The hole got deeper and deeper, and soon it was an inch deep. A little girl named Sue saw Tim and came over to play. She asked, "Can I keep digging with you?" Tim said, "Yes, we can dig together!" They kept digging the hole, making it even deeper. As they dug, they found something shiny in the hole. They dug around it and pulled it out. It was a big, shiny key! Tim and Sue looked at each other, surprised. They did not expect to find a key in the sand. They decided to keep digging and see if they could find what the key unlocks.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They run, jump, slide and swing. They have fun. "Look, Ben, the sun is high in the sky. It is noon," Anna says. "What is noon?" Ben asks. "Noon is when we eat lunch. We are hungry, right?" Anna says. "Yes, we are hungry. Let's go home and eat," Ben says. They hold hands and walk to their homes. They say goodbye and promise to play again later. Anna's mom is waiting for her. She has a big smile and a plate of food. "Hello, Anna. You look happy. Did you have fun in the park?" Mom says. "Yes, Mom. I played with Ben. He is my friend. We had fun," Anna says. "That's good. Now, come and eat your lunch. I made you a healthy sandwich and some fruit. You need to eat well to grow strong and smart," Mom says. "Thank you, Mom. I love you. I love your food. It is yummy," Anna says. She sits down and eats her lunch. She feels full and happy. She gives Mom a hug and a kiss. She is ready for a nap. She dreams of playing in the park with Ben again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat loved to sail on the water. The boat had a best friend, a magnet. The magnet was always organized and neat. One day, the boat and the magnet went sailing. The sun was warm and the water was calm. They saw fish in the water and birds in the sky. The boat and the magnet were very happy. As they sailed, they found a big mess in the water. The boat and the magnet wanted to help. The organized magnet picked up all the metal things in the water. The boat carried them away. Soon, the water was clean again. The boat and the magnet sailed back home, happy they could help.
