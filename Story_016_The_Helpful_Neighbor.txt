Title: The Helpful Neighbor
Category: Beginner

Mrs. Green is an old woman. She lives alone in a small house. She is seventy years old. She is a very nice woman. She has white hair and kind eyes. 
Today is Friday. Every Friday, Mrs. Green goes to the supermarket. She buys food for the week. She buys milk, bread, eggs, and vegetables. 

She walks to the supermarket. It is not far. She walks slowly. She has a shopping cart on wheels. She puts her food in the cart. 
When she finishes shopping, she pays the cashier. Then she starts to walk home. 

The cart is heavy today. She bought a lot of things. She bought a big bag of potatoes. She bought a heavy bottle of juice. 
She pulls the cart behind her. The wheels go 'click, clack, click, clack' on the pavement. 

Mrs. Green reaches her street. Her house is at the end of the street. She must walk up a small hill. 
The hill is hard for her today. The heavy cart pulls her back. She stops. She takes a deep breath. She wipes her forehead. She is tired. 

At that moment, a young man comes out of the house next door. His name is Tom. Tom is twenty-five years old. He is tall and strong. He is Mrs. Green's neighbor. 
Tom is going to his car. He is going to work. 
He sees Mrs. Green. He sees her stopping on the hill. He sees the heavy shopping cart. 

Tom smiles. He walks over to Mrs. Green. 
"Good morning, Mrs. Green," Tom says. 
"Good morning, Tom," Mrs. Green says. She is happy to see him. 
"You look very tired," Tom says. "Is that cart heavy?"
"Yes, it is very heavy today," Mrs. Green says. "I bought too many potatoes."

"Let me help you," Tom says. "I am strong. I can pull the cart to your house."
"Oh, no, Tom," Mrs. Green says. "You are going to work. I do not want to make you late."
"Do not worry," Tom says. "I have time. I am not late. Please, let me help."
"You are very kind," Mrs. Green says. "Thank you."

Tom takes the handle of the shopping cart. He pulls it easily. For him, it is not heavy at all. 
He walks up the hill. Mrs. Green walks next to him. 
"How are you today, Tom?" she asks. 
"I am very well, thank you," Tom says. "It is a beautiful day."
"Yes, the sun is shining," she says. 
"How is your garden?" Tom asks. He knows Mrs. Green loves her garden. 
"My roses are very beautiful now," she says proudly. "They are red and pink."
"I must look at them later," Tom says. 

They arrive at Mrs. Green's house. Tom pulls the cart to her front door. 
"Here we are," he says. 
"Thank you very much, Tom," Mrs. Green says. "You are a very helpful neighbor. I am lucky to live next to you."
"You are welcome, Mrs. Green," Tom says. 

He looks at the door. There is a small step. 
"Do you want me to carry the bags inside the house?" he asks. 
"No, no," she says. "I can take them inside. I will take them one by one. I am fine now."
"Are you sure?" Tom asks. 
"Yes, I am sure," she says. "Have a good day at work, Tom."
"Thank you. Have a good day, Mrs. Green," he says. 

Tom waves goodbye. He walks back to his car. He gets in his car and drives away to work. 
Mrs. Green opens her front door. She takes the bag of potatoes. She carries it into the kitchen. She takes the bottle of juice. She carries it into the kitchen. She takes the rest of the bags. 
She is not tired anymore. She is happy. 
She puts her food in the fridge. She puts her bread on the table. 
She looks out her kitchen window. She sees Tom's house next door. She smiles. 
"What a nice young man," she says to herself. 

She decides to make something special. She wants to say thank you to Tom. She takes some flour, eggs, and sugar. She is going to bake a cake. She will bake a chocolate cake. Tom likes chocolate cake. 
She will give him a piece of cake this evening when he comes back from work. It is good to have helpful neighbors. And it is good to say thank you. She hums a happy song while she bakes the cake.
