Title: The New Student
Category: Beginner

It is Tuesday morning in Room 4. Mr. Green is the teacher. He is standing at the front of the classroom. The children are sitting at their desks. They are ten years old. They are listening to Mr. Green. 
"Good morning, class," Mr. Green says. 
"Good morning, Mr. Green," the children say. 

"Today, we have a new student in our class," Mr. Green announces. 
The children look at the door. They are curious. They like new students. 
Mr. Green opens the classroom door. A young girl walks in. She has long black hair and brown eyes. She is wearing a green sweater and a blue skirt. She looks a little shy. She looks at the floor. 

"Class, please welcome Maria," Mr. Green says. "Maria and her family moved here from another city last week. This is her first day at our school."
"Hello, Maria," the class says together. 
Maria looks up. She smiles a small smile. "Hello," she says softly. 

"Maria, you can sit at the empty desk next to Emma," Mr. Green says. He points to a desk in the middle of the room. 
Maria walks to the desk. She puts her bag on the chair. She sits down. 

Emma is the girl sitting next to Maria. Emma is very friendly. She has curly blonde hair. 
Emma looks at Maria and smiles big. 
"Hi," Emma whispers. "I am Emma."
"Hi, Emma. I am Maria," the new girl whispers back. 
"I like your green sweater," Emma says. "Green is my favorite color."
"Thank you," Maria says. She feels a little better. Emma is nice. 

"Okay, class," Mr. Green says in a loud voice. "Let's open our math books. Turn to page twenty-four."
The children open their books. Maria opens her bag. She looks inside. She looks worried. 
"Oh no," Maria whispers. 
"What is wrong?" Emma asks. 
"I do not have a math book," Maria says. "I did not get my books from the office yet."

Emma raises her hand. "Mr. Green!" she calls. 
"Yes, Emma?" the teacher asks. 
"Maria does not have a math book," Emma explains. "Can she share my book today?"
Mr. Green smiles. "That is a very good idea, Emma. Yes, you can share. Thank you for helping Maria."
Emma moves her desk a little closer to Maria. She puts the open math book between them. 
"Here," Emma says. "We can read it together."
"Thank you very much, Emma," Maria says. She is very grateful. 

The math class is forty-five minutes long. They do many problems. Emma and Maria work well together. When Maria does not understand a word, Emma explains it quietly. Maria is very good at math. She helps Emma with a difficult problem. 

Ring, ring! 
The bell rings. It is time for morning break. 
"Okay, class," Mr. Green says. "You can go outside and play for fifteen minutes."
The children stand up. They walk to the door. 

Maria stands up slowly. She does not know where to go. She does not know anyone except Emma. 
Emma takes her jacket from the back of her chair. 
"Come with me, Maria," Emma says. "I will show you the school. I will show you the playground."
"Okay," Maria says happily. 

They walk out of the classroom. The hallway is busy and noisy. Many children are talking and laughing. 
Emma points to a blue door. "That is the library," she says. "We go there on Fridays to read books."
She points to a big room with large windows. "That is the cafeteria. We eat our lunch there."
Maria nods her head. She is learning about her new school. 

They go through the big glass doors to the outside. The playground is very large. There are swings, a slide, and a big grassy area. 
"Do you want to play on the swings?" Emma asks. 
"I love the swings," Maria says. 

They run to the swings. They sit on the swings. They kick their legs and go high in the air. 
Two other girls run over to them. 
"Hi, Emma," one girl says. 
"Hi, Sophie. Hi, Chloe," Emma says. "This is Maria. She is the new student in our class."
Sophie and Chloe smile. 
"Hello, Maria," they say. "Nice to meet you."
"Nice to meet you too," Maria says. 

"Do you want to play a game of tag with us?" Chloe asks. 
"Yes!" Maria says. 
They jump off the swings. They start to run on the grass. They play and laugh until the bell rings again. 

It is time to go back to class. Maria walks with Emma, Sophie, and Chloe. She is not shy anymore. She is smiling a big smile. She was nervous this morning, but now she is happy. The new student has three new friends. It is a very good first day.
