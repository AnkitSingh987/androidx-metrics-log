Title: Taxing frivolous industries will not fund Part Three

Taxing 'frivolous' industries will not fund India's science

If a venture can be a great success in attracting international talent by virtue of being taxed less, so can other endeavours. For India to be at the forefront of science, the mechanism is to liberalise the bottlenecks through which money and capital somehow manage to trickle into academia and reduce dependence on the government, rather than taxing the few geese that lay the golden eggs. Ritvik Chaturvedi is Senior Research Fellow, Centre for Earth Sciences, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru.
