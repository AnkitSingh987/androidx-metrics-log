Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a spoiled boy. He always wanted more toys and treats. His mom and dad gave him everything he asked for. But Tim was not happy. One day, Tim saw a girl playing with a ball. She was happy and had fun. Tim wanted to play too. The girl shared her ball with Tim. They played together and laughed. Tim felt happy for the first time. Tim learned that sharing is good. He shared his toys with his friends. They all had fun together. Tim's mom and dad were proud of him. They all applauded for Tim. Tim was not a spoiled boy anymore. He was a good boy who shared and made friends. And that made Tim very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a large fountain in a park. One day, a three-year-old girl decided to go there. She loved watching the water splash and seeing the colourful reflections in the fountain. But soon after arriving, the girl started to worry. She noticed a large bird perched on the side of the fountain. The bird seemed scared and out of place. The little girl walked up to the bird and said, "Hello. Why are you here?" The bird replied, "I'm lost. I can't find my home." The girl was determined to help the bird. She looked around and spotted some big trees in the distance. Pointing in that direction, she said, "Maybe you can find your home in those trees." The bird seemed to understand and flew off. The little girl waved goodbye and watched as the large bird disappeared into the sky. The girl was no longer worried. She had helped the bird find its home and was happy with her accomplishment. She smiled and decided to continue her day at the fountain.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to play outside. One day, while playing, she found a big mushroom. It was a very generous mushroom. It was so big that it could share with her friends. Lucy wanted to show her friends the mushroom. But first, she thought she would pour some water on it. She thought it would help the mushroom grow even more. So, Lucy went inside her house to get a cup of water. When Lucy came back and poured the water on the mushroom, something unexpected happened. The mushroom started to wiggle and shake. Then, it turned into a funny little frog! Lucy and the frog became best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. One was called Danny and the other was called Lilly. One day, Danny and Lilly went to the barber. Danny was very excited because he was going to get his first haircut. Lilly was also excited to watch her best friend get a haircut. When they got to the barber, Danny sat down in the chair and the barber started to create his new look. Danny was feeling a bit confused, but he trusted the barber. The barber cut, brushed and styled Danny's hair. In the end, he looked really cool with his new hairstyle. Lilly clapped her hands and said, “That looks great, Danny!” Danny smiled, feeling proud of his new look. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy little girl named Jane. She loved to play in her room and make things with her toys. One day, she decided to make a big tower out of her blocks. She looked around her room and found her favorite cupboard. Jane thought it would be the perfect place to build her tower. As Jane started to build her tower, her little brother, Tom, walked in. He saw the blocks and wanted to play too. "Can I help you make the tower?" Tom asked. Jane looked at him and said, "No, I am the bossy big sister, and I want to make the tower by myself!" Tom felt sad and went to the other side of the room. He found some more blocks and started to build his own tower. Jane saw Tom's tower and thought it looked nice. She walked over to him and said, "I'm sorry for being bossy. Can we make the tower together?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, let's make the biggest tower ever!" So, Jane and Tom worked together to build a giant tower next to the cupboard. They were so happy to play together, and they learned that being bossy is not fun. From that day on, they always played together and shared their toys. And they lived happily ever after.
