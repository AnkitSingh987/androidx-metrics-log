Title: The Clumsy Girl and the Drawing

Once there was a clumsy girl who loved to wave. Every morning she would wave to her mom and dad as they left for work. The girl loved to go to school and play with her friends. There was a blackboard in the back that she would run to every day. She loved to draw on the blackboard with chalk, making pictures of cats and dogs. One day, she was drawing so hard that she bumped the blackboard and made all the chalk fall off the edge. Everyone gasped, feeling sorry for the little clumsy girl. But the girl just kept on smiling and waved her hands around, not caring that she had made a mistake. Her friends clapped and cheered and waved back - they thought it was so fun to see the girl wave! Even the teacher had to smile and wave. The girl smiled and waved back, happy that her waving could bring so much joy. From then on, she started to wave every time she made a mistake, like a secret sign that told her friends that it was ok to make mistakes and to not be afraid.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. His favorite thing to do was to swing on the swings at the park. He would swing high up in the sky and feel the wind on his face. One day, Tim met a new friend named Anna. Anna spoke a different language. Tim did not understand what Anna said, but they still played together. They laughed and had fun on the swings. Tim's mom told him that it was time to go home. Before leaving, Tim and Anna both washed their hands to be clean. They waved goodbye and promised to play on the swings again soon. Even though they spoke different languages, they knew they could still be friends.

Max was a very intelligent bear. He was always thinking and always looking for ways to be smarter. He decided to clean his brain. Every day, he got out a cloth and a dustpan and began to clean his brain like he was sweeping the floor. One day, Max was cleaning his brain when he noticed something stuck in a corner. He pulled it out and it made a funny noise. He scrutinised it and saw it was a shiny object with flashing lights. Max was curious about this object. He wanted to know what it was and what he could do with it. He put it in his pocket and kept cleaning. When Max was done cleaning, he took out the object he found. He turned it around and it began to do amazing things. His brain lit up with possibility and he felt more intelligent than ever! He had been cleaning his brain enough, and now he was smart enough to use the object to make amazing things happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a black bag that he took with him everywhere he went. He loved his black bag very much. One day, Tim went to the park with his black bag. He saw a tall tree and wanted to climb it. So, he put his black bag down on the ground and started to climb the tree. When he was up high, he could see the whole park. After a while, Tim climbed down from the tree. He saw that his black bag was not where he left it. Tim was very sad. He looked around and saw a dog standing near his black bag. The dog had found it for him! Tim was so happy, and he thanked the dog. From that day on, Tim and the dog were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a rough bear named Bobo. He lived in the woods and loved to play with his friends. One sunny day, Bobo was playing with his toy arrow when he met a new friend named Timmy the Turtle. Timmy was very slow, but he was very kind. "Hi, I'm Timmy!" said the turtle. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, let's play!" Bobo replied. They played with the arrow, taking turns to throw it and see how far it could go. They had so much fun together. As the sun got hotter, Bobo noticed that his ice cream was starting to melt. He quickly shared it with Timmy, and they both enjoyed the cold treat. They laughed and played all day long, and they became the best of friends.
