Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play in the park. One day, Max was feeling miserable because it was raining outside. He wanted to go outside and run, but he couldn't. Max's mommy saw him feeling sad and had an idea. She made a yummy meal for Max to cheer him up. It had his favorite food, chicken and rice. Max's eyes lit up when he saw the meal. He wagged his tail and ate it all up. The next day, the sun came out, and Max was happy again. He went to the park and ran as fast as he could. He played with his friends and had lots of fun. Max learned that even if he feels miserable one day, things can get better.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to drink water. She knew it was important to drink a lot of water every day. Sue had a big network of friends who also liked to drink water. One day, Sue and her friends had a big water party. They all brought their favorite cups to drink water. They were very excited! They laughed, played, and drank lots of water together. It was a fun day. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, thirsty elephant came to the party! The elephant wanted to drink water too. Sue and her friends were surprised, but they decided to share their water with the elephant. They all became friends and had an even bigger water party. The elephant was a part of their network now. And that's how Sue learned that sharing is important.

Once upon a time, a big white bird lived in an ancient tree. The tree was very old and had many branches. The bird liked to sit on the branches and sing. One day, a small yellow bird arrived at the tree. The yellow bird was tired and hungry. The white bird saw the yellow bird and wanted to help. The white bird shared its food with the yellow bird. The yellow bird was happy and thanked the white bird. They became good friends and sang together every day. The ancient tree was filled with their sweet songs. And they lived happily ever after.

Tommy loved to paint. He had many colors and brushes and papers. He liked to paint animals and cars and flowers. But his favorite thing to paint was soldiers. He had a big box of toy soldiers that he played with every day. He pretended they were brave and strong and fought bad guys. One day, Tommy decided to paint his toy soldiers. He thought they would look more real and cool with paint. He took some green and brown and black paint and started to paint them. He painted their clothes and their guns and their helmets. He was very careful and did a good job. But then, his big sister Lucy came into his room. She saw what he was doing and got angry. She said, "Tommy, what are you doing? You are ruining your toy soldiers! They are not for painting, they are for playing! You are a silly boy!" Tommy felt sad and mad. He said, "No, Lucy, I am not silly. I am intelligent. I am making my toy soldiers better. They are for painting and playing. You are a mean girl!" Lucy grabbed his paint and his toy soldiers and tried to take them away. Tommy did not let her. He pulled and pushed and shouted. They fought and made a big mess. The paint spilled and splashed on the floor and the wall and their clothes. The toy soldiers flew and fell and broke. Mommy heard the noise and came into the room. She saw what happened and got very upset. She said, "Tommy, Lucy, what have you done? You are both naughty children! You have ruined your toy soldiers and your paint and your room! You have to clean up and say sorry to each other!" Tommy and Lucy felt sorry and ashamed. They said sorry to each other and to Mommy. They helped to clean up and threw away the broken toy soldiers. They learned their lesson and promised to share and be nice to each other. They hugged and kissed and played with the toy soldiers that were not painted. They still loved to paint, but they did not paint their toy soldiers anymore.
