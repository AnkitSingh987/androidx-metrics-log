Title: The Story of the Flowers and the Beautiful

One day, a 3 year old girl wanted to do something special. She had a brilliant idea! She gathered all the ash from her fireplace and used it to draw beautiful pictures of flowers on the driveway. All her friends were amazed at how beautiful the pictures were! She continued to do her drawings every day, using the ash and her imagination. Her family and neighbors would stop and admire her art every time they walked by. They thought it was brilliant! Soon, the 3 year old girl was known in the neighborhood as "the ashy artist"! Everywhere she went she created beautiful pictures in her driveway. People from all around came to watch her work. The girl was very proud of her art and was so happy she was able to do something creative. The ash had made her drawings so beautiful that everyone was amazed at what she had created.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful cat named Lily. Lily lived in a big house with her friend, a funny dog named Max. They liked to play and joke with each other all day. One day, Lily and Max were very hungry. They went to the kitchen to find some food. They opened the refrigerator and saw many yummy things inside. "Look, Max! We have cheese and milk!" said Lily. Max laughed and said, "Lily, you're so funny! Let's share the food and have a big party!" So, they took the food from the refrigerator and had a fun party together. They laughed and joked as they ate. They were very happy and loved being friends.

Once upon a time there was a brave soldier named Joe. He was the bravest soldier in the whole land and carried a spear with him to every battle. One day, he was walking in a strange forest and noticed something unusual. He stopped and heard a noise coming from a nearby bush. Joe: Who's there? Voice: I'm here to supply you with something. Joe was curious, so he stepped closer to the bush. Suddenly a dark figure came out of the bush, raising its spear. Joe was shocked and quickly got ready to fight. Joe: What do you want from me? Voice: To make you suffer. The figure quickly ran towards Joe and attacked him with the spear. Joe tried to defend himself, but it was too late - the spear was already in his chest. Joe's brave life was lost, and the dark figure quickly disappeared. It was an unusual and unexpected ending to Joe's story.

Tom and Lily were playing in the backyard. They liked to build things with the lumber that Dad had left there. The lumber was brown and hard and had nails in it. Tom and Lily had a hammer and some nails too. They decided to make a house for their toys. They used the lumber to make walls and a roof. They worked hard and had fun. They put their toys inside the house and pretended they were a family. "Look at our house!" Tom said. "It is so big and nice!" "Yes, it is!" Lily said. "We are very good builders!" They heard a loud noise. It was Dad. He was coming back from work. He saw the house that Tom and Lily had made. He was not happy. He shouted at them. "Tom! Lily! What have you done? This lumber is for the shed! You can't use it for your games! You could hurt yourselves with the nails! Come here right now!" Tom and Lily were scared. They ran out of the house and went to Dad. They said they were sorry. They did not know the lumber was for the shed. They just wanted to have fun. Dad was angry, but he loved them. He hugged them and said they were not in trouble. But they had to help him take apart the house and put the lumber back. He also said they had to be careful with the nails and the hammer. He said he would find them some other things to play with. Tom and Lily said okay. They helped Dad with the lumber. They were sad to see their house go, but they understood. They learned a lesson. They also had an idea. They asked Dad if he could make them a small house with some of the lumber. Dad smiled and said he would try. Tom and Lily were happy. They thanked Dad and gave him a kiss. They went inside to have some cookies. They still liked to build things, but they would ask first.
