Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his dad. They brought a big tent with them. Tim was very excited. He loved to play in the tent with his dad. They put up the tent under a gentle, warm sun. In the tent, they played fun games. Tim's dad asked, "Do you want to play hide and seek?" Tim nodded and smiled. He loved hide and seek. They took turns hiding in the tent and finding each other. As they played, a little girl named Sue came by. She saw the tent and asked, "Can I play too?" Tim and his dad said, "Yes, come play with us!" They all had a great time playing in the tent together.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there was a barber. The barber was very nice and loved to cut hair. Every day, people would call the barber to come and cut their hair. The barber was always happy to help. One day, the barber got a call from a lion. The lion had too much hair and needed a haircut. The barber was scared but decided to help the lion. The barber cut the lion's hair and made it look very nice. The lion was so happy with his haircut that he wanted to say thank you. The lion gave the barber a big hug. From that day on, the barber and the lion became best friends. The moral of the story is to be brave and help others, even when you are scared.

Anna likes to visit her grandma every week. Grandma lives in a big house with a garden and many flowers. Anna likes to help grandma water the flowers and feed the birds. Grandma tells Anna stories and gives her cookies. One day, Anna and grandma are sitting on the sofa, reading a book. Anna asks grandma, "What is a brain, grandma?" Grandma smiles and says, "A brain is a part of your body, inside your head. It helps you think and remember and learn. Everyone has a brain, even the birds and the flowers." Anna is curious and says, "Can I see your brain, grandma?" Grandma laughs and says, "No, dear, you can't see my brain. It is hidden under my hair and skin and bones. But you can feel it sometimes, when you have a headache or a dream." Anna nods and says, "I have dreams sometimes, when I am sleepy. Do you have dreams, grandma?" Grandma says, "Yes, I do. Sometimes I dream of when I was a little girl, like you. Sometimes I dream of places I have been or people I have loved. Dreams are like pictures in your brain, when you are asleep." Anna says, "I like dreams. They are fun and funny. What do you dream of, grandma?" Grandma says, "I dream of you, Anna. You are my favorite dream. You make me happy and proud. You are my smart and sweet granddaughter. I love you very much." Anna hugs grandma and says, "I love you too, grandma. You are my best friend. Can I stay with you tonight and have more dreams?" Grandma says, "Of course, you can. You are always welcome here. Come, let's go to bed. We are both sleepy. Good night, Anna." Anna says, "Good night, grandma. Thank you for visiting me in your brain."

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little house. The house was very pretty and had many things inside that people value. In this house lived a happy family with a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily found a big, red ball in the yard. She loved the ball and played with it all day. She kicked it, threw it, and laughed as it bounced around. But Lily was not careful when she played near the delicate house. Suddenly, the big red ball hit the house very hard. The house shook, and all the valuable things inside fell down and broke. Lily and her family were very sad. They knew they should have been more careful with their delicate house. From that day on, Lily and her family learned to be more careful with their things. They never played with the big red ball near the house again. But they couldn't fix the broken things, and the house was never the same.
