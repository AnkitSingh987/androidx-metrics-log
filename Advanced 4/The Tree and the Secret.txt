Title: The Tree and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a little secret. Inside the tree, there was a shiny coin. The coin could help the tree grow big and strong. The tree was very happy. One day, a crazy bird saw the shiny coin. The bird wanted the coin for itself. The bird took the coin and flew away. The tree was very sad. It could not grow without the coin. Soon, the crazy bird learned that the coin did not make it happy. The bird felt bad for the tree. It decided to give the coin back to the tree. The tree was happy again and started to grow. The bird learned that being kind is better than being crazy. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an ordinary girl who wanted to make a wish come true. She had a very special wish and she wanted to raise her voice so that everyone could hear it. So one day, she went up to her room, found her loudest voice, and shouted "My wish is...." Suddenly, a little fairy appeared in front of her. She was very surprised as she had never seen a fairy before! The fairy said to her "I heard your wish. Is there anything I can do to help you make it come true?". The girl was so excited! She told the fairy all about her wish and what she wanted to achieve. The fairy was very happy and she said "I will do my best to help you". Then, she flew away. The girl waited and waited, but nothing happened. Eventually, she started to lose hope that her wish would ever come true. Then, one day, something extraordinary happened and her wish came true! She was so happy that she shouted out "My wish came true because of the special help from my fairy friend!" The End

Once upon a time there was a boy named Bobby. He was only three years old. One day Bobby was walking in the park with his mom. After a while, he noticed an old spoon sitting in the grass. "Mommy, what is that?" asked Bobby pointing at the old spoon. "I'm not sure, Bobby," replied his mom. "Let's guess. What could it be?" Bobby looked closer and then exclaimed, "It's a spoon! A spoon for eating, right mommy?" His mom smiled, "That's right Bobby! Someone must have dropped it." Bobby was so happy he had guessed it all by himself. He smiled and ran off to find more things. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who loved to design. She lived in a small house with a big garden. In her garden, she grew many spicy peppers. One day, the woman decided to make a spicy soup. She picked the spicy peppers from her garden and put them in a big pot. She added water, and she cooked the soup until it was ready. Then, she invited her friends over to taste her spicy soup. They all loved it and asked her to make it again. The woman was happy that she could design a tasty soup with her spicy peppers. From that day on, she shared her spicy soup with everyone in her town.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They rode in a big vehicle. It was a shiny, expensive vehicle that went very fast. Tim liked to look out the window and see all the things outside. At the store, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam was with his dad. They were buying food too. Tim and Sam said "hi" to each other. They were happy to see each other. After they bought their food, Tim and his mom went back to the expensive vehicle. They put the food in the back and drove home. Tim waved to Sam as they left. He couldn't wait to see Sam again at the park tomorrow.

One day, a big gorilla named Bobo felt very guilty. He had eaten all the food in his friend's house. His friend, a little girl named Lily, was sad. Bobo wanted to make her happy again. Bobo had an idea! He would join Lily in finding more food. They walked and walked, looking for food. They found a big tree with lots of yummy fruit. Lily was so happy, she clapped her hands. But something unexpected happened! The tree started to shake and twist. The tree was not a tree at all! It was a giant, friendly elephant named Ella. Ella was hiding from a loud noise. Bobo, Lily, and Ella became best friends and shared the fruit together.
