Title: Lucy and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a big gate. A little girl named Lucy lived in the house with her mom and dad. They had a garden where flowers and plants would grow. One day, Lucy saw a disgusting bug in the garden. She was scared and ran to her mom. "Mom, there is a disgusting bug in the garden!" Lucy said. Her mom looked at the bug and told Lucy not to worry. The bug would not hurt her. But Lucy was still scared. The next day, Lucy went back to the garden. She saw that the disgusting bug was still there. But something unexpected happened. The bug started to grow bigger and bigger. Then, the bug turned into a beautiful butterfly! Lucy was not scared anymore. She smiled and played with the butterfly. The butterfly flew away, and Lucy knew that sometimes things that look disgusting can be beautiful too.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Andy. Andy had a big family and they all lived in a tiny house in the earth. Andy was a very worried ant because the sun was very hot and he thought it could burn his home. One day, Andy went to his friend, Bee. Andy said, "Bee, I am worried. The sun is hot and may burn our earth home. What can we do?" Bee thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask Wise Owl. He will know what to do." Andy and Bee went to Wise Owl's tree. They told him about the hot sun and their fear of it burning their homes. Wise Owl listened and said, "Do not worry, little ones. The sun is far away and will not burn your homes. It gives us light and warmth. Be happy and enjoy the sunshine." Andy and Bee felt better after talking to Wise Owl. They went back to their homes and told their families what Wise Owl had said. From that day on, they were not worried about the sun burning their homes. They played in the sunshine and were very happy.

One hot day, three cool kids were in the jungle. They were looking for tigers. As they walked, they saw lots of animals. Then, they saw something amazing! It was a tiger! The kids were excited! They wanted to get closer to see it. They carefully stepped closer and closer. All of a sudden, the tiger sprang up and destroyed the tree next to it! The kids stepped back and watched in amazement. They weren't scared at all. They were happy to see the destruction the tiger caused. The kids thought it was so cool that the tiger had destroyed the tree. That's when they knew they had seen a real tiger!

Once upon a time, there was a little cat. The little cat lived in a small house with a nice family. The family had a shelf with many toys on it. The little cat liked to look at the toys on the shelf, but they were too high for her to reach. One day, the little cat saw a small ball on the floor. She had an idea! She pushed the ball with her nose and managed to move it near the shelf. Then, she jumped on the ball and tried to reach the toys on the shelf. With a big jump, the little cat managed to get on the shelf! She was so happy to play with the toys. The family saw the little cat and laughed. They were proud of her for managing to reach the shelf. From that day on, the little cat and the family played with the toys together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make puzzles. One day, she found a big box with a new puzzle inside. Lily was very happy. As she started to put the puzzle together, Lily began to wonder what the picture would be. She saw some blue pieces and thought maybe it was the sky. She saw some green pieces and thought it could be grass. In the end, Lily finished the puzzle and saw a pretty picture of a big tree with colorful leaves. She was so proud of her work. Lily showed her mom and dad, and they all smiled. They hung the pretty puzzle on the wall for everyone to see.
