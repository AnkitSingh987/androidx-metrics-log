Title: The Girl Called Ema and the Door

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Ema. She was a very intelligent girl. She loved going to the garage. Her mom told her, "Ema, take a toy with you next time you go to the garage." So the next day, Ema packed a toy and headed outside. She opened the garage door and peeked inside. The garage was filled with all kinds of toys! She couldn't decide which one she wanted to take! "Which toy should I take?" she asked her mom. Her mom replied, "Take the one that you think is the most fun." So Ema grabbed a bright pink toy car and raced it around the garage! She was so excited to take it home and play. Every time she went to the garage from then on, Ema was always sure to take a toy with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited because her birthday was coming soon. She wanted to use her new toy that she would get as a present. Sue thought it might be difficult to wait for her birthday. One day, Sue saw her mom hiding a big box. Sue knew it was her birthday present. She wanted to see the toy inside, but she knew she had to wait. Sue thought it was very difficult to not look at the box. Finally, Sue's birthday came. She was so happy to open the big box. But when she opened it, she found a smaller box inside. Sue was surprised! She opened the smaller box and found the toy she wanted. It was a fun game to use. Sue learned that sometimes waiting can be difficult, but it can also be full of surprises.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a different toy train with many rails. He wanted to play with it. Tom loved toy trains. Tom played with the toy train on the rails. But then, he saw a dog. He wanted to pet the dog. So, he left the toy train and went to the dog. After playing with the dog, Tom went back to the toy train. But the train was not there. Tom was sad because he lost the toy train. He looked for the train and found it under a tree. He was happy again and played with the toy train until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Spot. Spot could do a lot of things that other dogs could not do. He could jump very high, run very fast, and even talk to people. One day, Spot saw a candle on the table. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, saw Spot near the candle and said, "No, Spot! That is not a toy. It is hot and can hurt you." Spot did not listen and started to quarrel with Fluffy. As they quarreled, the candle fell on the floor. Spot and Fluffy were both scared. Suddenly, a big bird named Tweety flew in through the window. Tweety picked up the candle with her beak and put it back on the table. Spot and Fluffy were very thankful for Tweety's help. They learned to listen to each other and not to quarrel. From that day on, they all became good friends and played together happily.

George was so happy. He was watching his favourite film with his mom and dad. It was about a modest butterfly who loved to fly and explore. One day, the butterfly flew too close to some flowers and accidentally tore them apart. He felt so bad and wished he could make up for it. He went to his parents and asked for their forgiveness. George's dad gave him a big hug and said, “Of course, we forgive you my little one! No one is perfect, we all make mistakes!” George smiled and hugged his dad back. He was so relieved to be forgiven. From then on, the butterfly was more modest in his explorations. He was happy to be able to enjoy his movie with the ones he loved!
