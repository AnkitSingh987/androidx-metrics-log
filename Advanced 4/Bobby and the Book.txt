Title: Bobby and the Book

Bobby was a little boy who loved to study all the time. One day he saw a picture in a book and he really wanted it. He got out some paper and started to copy the picture. His mom saw him and asked Bobby why he was doing this. Bobby was very scared and felt a little guilty. He said he just really wanted the picture and he was trying to copy it. His mom smiled and said it was okay. She told him it was good to study but it was also important for him to ask for things. Bobby was very happy and ran off to show his mom the picture he copied. He was so proud and she was very happy with it too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mark. Mark had a red bucket that he loved very much. He took his bucket with him wherever he went. He was very proud of his bucket. One sunny day, Mark went to the park with his bucket. He saw a big sand pit and knew it was the perfect place to play. He used his bucket to make a big sand castle. He was so proud of his castle. As Mark was playing, a little girl named Lucy came over. She saw the sand castle and thought it was very nice. Mark and Lucy played together with the bucket and made an even bigger castle. They were both very proud of their work. At the end of the day, they went home with big smiles, happy to have made new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He was very happy. At the park, Tim saw a big dog. He was worried. The dog came closer to Tim. He realized the dog wanted to play with him. Tim started to play with the dog, but he forgot to tell his mom. His mom looked for him and could not find him. She was very worried. Tim did not know his mom was looking for him. He was having fun with the dog. In the end, Tim's mom could not find him. She was very sad. Tim was lost and did not know how to go home. The dog went away, and Tim was all alone. This story had a bad ending.

One day, a fireman named Tim was walking down the street. He was very flexible and could bend his body in many ways. Tim saw a little girl named Lily playing with her toys. She looked sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily looked up and said, "I lost my cat, and I can't find her." Tim wanted to help Lily find her cat. They both walked and looked around. The sun was hot, and they were getting tired. They saw a big tree with something in it. Tim looked up and saw the cat in the tree. The cat was scared and couldn't come down. Tim said, "I will help get your cat." Tim used his flexible body to climb the tree. He held the cat and climbed back down. Lily was so happy to see her cat again. Lily thanked Tim and said, "You are the best fireman ever!" Tim smiled and said, "I'm happy to help." They both went home, and Lily knew she had a new friend in Tim the flexible fireman.

Once there was a kangaroo who was very popular. Everyone in the bush loved him. He was always very happy and wore a big smile on his face. One day, the kangaroo decided he wanted to dress up. He put on bright colors and cool patterns. Everyone thought he looked stunning! The kangaroo strutted around the bush and all his friends and family admired his outfit. Everyone wanted to look like him, including the other kangaroos. The popular kangaroo was the happiest ever and he wore his bright clothes proudly. He loved his new outfit and it made him feel special. The other kangaroos also wore colorful clothes now and everyone in the bush had a lot of fun. Even the kangaroo felt very cool and popular.
