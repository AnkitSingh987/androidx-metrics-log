Title: Tim the Boy and the Man

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big temple with his mom. Tim was very confused because he had never been to a temple before. There were many people, and it was very loud. At the temple, Tim saw a man who looked mean. The man started to tell Tim that he should leave. Tim was scared and did not know what to do. He looked for his mom, but she was not there. Tim started to cry, and the mean man laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came and bit the mean man. The man ran away, but Tim was still alone and scared. He never found his mom, and he was very sad.

The night was clear and the stars were shining in the sky. Max wanted to stay up late and watch them. He asked his mom, "Mommy, can I watch the stars with you?" "No, Max, it's late and you need to go to bed," she said. Max was a bit scared, but he knew his mom was right, so he went to his room. On the way, he grabbed some juice from the refrigerator. He opened the door and saw something outside that scared him. He ran back to his mom, holding the juice bottle close. "Mommy, I saw a monster outside!" he said, very scared. His mom laughed. "There's no monster, it was just a scarecrow," she said, pointing to the figure outside. Sure enough, it was just a scarecrow covered in a clear plastic bag. Max felt relieved. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy." "You're welcome, Max," she said with a smile. "Now, it's time for bed. Goodnight." Max nodded, thanked her again, and he finished his juice before heading off to bed. Max knew that next time he saw something that scared him, his mom would be there to help.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy hammer. One day, Max was playing with his hammer when he saw a big cat named Tom. Tom was not very nice to Max, and Max wanted to show Tom that he was not afraid. Max had a plan. He would use his hammer to make a loud noise and scare Tom away. Max hid behind a tree and waited for Tom to come near. When Tom was close, Max jumped out and hit the ground with his hammer. The noise was so loud that Tom jumped high in the air. But Max did not know that Tom's friends were watching. They all laughed at Tom, and Tom felt embarrass. Tom ran away, and Max was happy that his plan worked. From that day on, Tom was never mean to Max again. The charming little dog had taught the big cat a lesson, and they all lived happily ever after.

Silly Joe was a very wild boy. He loved to run around, blink and play games. One day, Joe ran into a field full of spikes. He blinked, curious about what they were. He didn't realize that they were very sharp and dangerous! Suddenly, Joe felt a sharp pain in his foot. He looked down and saw that he had stepped on a spike. He yelped "Ouch!" and tried to pull his foot off of it, but the spike was stuck. Joe's mom ran over and saw the spike sticking out of Joe's foot. She looked at Joe sadly and said, "Silly Joe, why did you do this? Now you are hurt." Joe tried to apologize, but his mom just shook her head. Joe's foot was bleeding, and he needed to go to the hospital. Unfortunately, Joe had to learn the hard way that even wild boys should be careful!

Once upon a time there was a mighty mother. She was very special, because she could record amazing things. Every day, she would record the special birds that sang in the trees, and all the wonderful flowers that grew in the garden. She also record the trees that gave her happy shade on summer days. But, the most special thing she could record was her little three year old son. He loved to hear the stories his mother recorded, and then he would get very excited and start to dance. He would spin and twirl all around the room while his mother laughed and recorded it all in her special book! Then one day, the mother had the best surprise ever. She had recorded and saved up her special records and now she could show them all to her son so he could remember all the happy times they had shared. She gave him the gift and he hugged his mother tight! He knew, just by looking in her eyes, that his mother was truly mighty.
