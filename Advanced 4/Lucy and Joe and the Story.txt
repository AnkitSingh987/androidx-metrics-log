Title: Lucy and Joe and the Story

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Lucy and Joe. Lucy wanted to study very hard and so she would journey every week to the stadium. Joe thought it was very strange. He thought it was stupid for Lucy to put so much effort into her studies. One day, Joe decided to follow Lucy to the stadium to see what she was doing. He was shocked to find that she was dedicating her time to solving a difficult math problem in an effort to improve her skills. Joe realized he had been wrong and started to think that maybe it was not stupid to put so much effort into trying to learn something. They talked about it afterwards. Lucy explained to Joe why it was important to spend time studying even if it seemed difficult. "You can learn something new and become smarter if you study," she said. Joe agreed with her and thanked her for helping him realize the importance of studying. The moral of the story is that it is never stupid to put effort into studies and learning something new. Even if it seems difficult, taking time to study can lead to amazing rewards.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted train. The train could roll very fast. It had many friends because it was so good at rolling. One day, the train went on a trip. It rolled up and down the hills. The train saw many things. It saw big trees, pretty flowers, and fun animals. At the end of the day, the gifted train went back to its friends. They were happy to see the train. They all played and rolled together. They had a lot of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Jill and Tom were playing in the garden. They saw a big green cactus with many sharp needles. Jill wanted to touch it, but Tom said no. "Let me touch it, Tom. It looks so pretty and soft," Jill said. "No, Jill. It is not soft. It is very ouchy. It will hurt you if you touch it," Tom said. "But I want to see how it feels. Maybe it is not so ouchy. Maybe it is like a fluffy toy," Jill said. She reached out her hand to touch the cactus. Tom tried to stop her, but he was too late. Jill touched the cactus and cried out loud. A needle had poked her finger and made it bleed. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts, Tom! It hurts so much!" Jill said. Tom ran to get their mom. Mom came and saw Jill's finger. She took out the needle and put a band-aid on it. She hugged Jill and kissed her. "I'm sorry, Jill. You should have listened to Tom. The cactus is not a toy. It is a plant that lives in the desert. It has needles to protect itself from animals and people. It is not meant to be touched," Mom said. Jill nodded and sniffed. She felt sorry for being naughty. She looked at the cactus again. It was still big and green, but it did not look pretty and soft anymore. It looked scary and mean. "Mom, can we go inside and play with something else?" Jill asked. "Sure, Jill. Let's go inside and find a nice toy for you. How about your ballerina doll? She is very graceful and pretty. She can dance and spin and twirl. She will not hurt you," Mom said. Jill smiled and nodded. She liked her ballerina doll. She took her hand and followed Mom inside. Tom followed them too. He was glad that Jill was okay. He hoped that she had learned her lesson. He did not want to play with the cactus ever again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little girl named Lily was curious. She wanted to see what was in the forest. So, she started to walk and look around. As she walked, she saw a big tree. Under the tree, she saw a group of animals. They were gather around something. Lily went closer to see what they were doing. "What are you doing?" she asked them. The animals said, "We are gather wood to build a house." They had a little saw to cut the wood. Lily wanted to help, so she used the saw to cut the wood too. They all worked together and built a nice little house for the animals to live in. In the end, Lily and the animals became good friends. They played together every day in the big forest. Lily was happy she was curious and found her new friends.
