Title: Sally the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Sally found a bottle of lotion. It was a sunny day, so she thought, "I should put this on my skin." She tried to open the bottle, but it was stubborn and would not open. Sally asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me open this stubborn bottle?" Tom tried to open it, but it was hard for him too. They both tried together, and finally, the bottle opened. They put the lotion on their skin and played outside all day. The sun was hot, but the lotion helped protect them. They were happy they opened the stubborn bottle and put the lotion on.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little cat. The cat was sad. It could not find its toy. The cat looked all over and asked, "Where is my toy?" One day, the cat saw a ladder. The ladder was very tall. The cat thought, "Maybe my toy is up there!" So, the cat climbed the ladder. It climbed and climbed until it was very high. At the top, the cat found a big bird. The bird was holding the toy! The bird said, "I took your toy to play with you!" The cat was happy. The cat and the bird played together. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who liked to hang on a branch. The bird was very anxious because it was his first time on this branch. He looked around and saw many trees with more branches. One day, while the bird was hanging on the branch, a big wind came. The wind blew and blew, and the bird was scared. He thought the branch would break. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind stopped, and the bird was still on the branch. The bird was very happy that the wind stopped. He saw that the branch was strong and could hold him. Now, the bird was not anxious anymore. He knew the branch was a good place to hang. And so, the little bird stayed on the branch and played with his new friends.

Once upon a time there was a nice, clean house. In the house lived a family, including a little girl named Sarah. One day, Sarah looked outside and saw a thermometer. She thought it was so special and wanted to give it a big welcome. She held out her hand and smiled. The thermometer was special because it could tell the temperature outside. It was red when it was hot, and blue when it was cold. Sarah was so excited! She went to tell her family all about the thermometer. They were so happy and welcomed it into their home. Now Sarah could always find out the temperature outside, without having to go outside. Sarah and her family were so happy to have the new thermometer. It was the perfect addition to their clean house.

Once upon a time, there was a small rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, he saw a shiny trophy in his friend's house. It was big and pretty. Remy wanted to have a trophy too. Remy asked his friend if he could borrow the trophy for a day. His friend said yes, but he must be very careful with it. Remy was very happy and took the trophy home. He put it on a high shelf so it would be safe. He looked at it all day and felt proud. The next day, Remy's friends came over to play. They saw the trophy and wanted one too. Remy told them about borrowing it and they all decided to have a race. The winner would get the trophy. They all ran as fast as they could, and in the end, Remy won the race. He gave the trophy back to his friend, feeling happy that he had a fun time with his friends. And from that day on, they all played and had races together, always remembering the shiny trophy.

Once upon a time, there was a little child named Tim. Tim loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends found a big apple tree. They all wanted to eat the apples. Tim's friend, Sally, said, "Look at that apple! It's sour and starting to rot." Tim looked at the apple and saw that it was turning brown. He didn't want to eat a sour apple that was going bad. So, Tim and his friends decided to find a better apple. They picked a nice, red apple from the tree. Tim said, "This apple is good. Let's share it with everyone." They all took turns taking bites of the apple, and it was very yummy. They played and laughed together in the park, happy that they found a good apple to share.
