Title: Lily and Ben and the Flowers

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with crayons and paper. They make prints of their hands and feet. They laugh when they see the funny shapes. One day, Lily and Ben want to make a gift for their teacher. They think of what she likes. They know she likes flowers and books. They have an idea. They use their crayons and paper to make a big card. They draw flowers and write "Thank you" on it. They also make prints of their hands and feet on the card. They write their names under the prints. They are very proud of their card. They put it in an envelope and give it to their teacher. Their teacher is very happy. She opens the envelope and sees the card. She smiles and hugs Lily and Ben. She says, "This is a wonderful gift. I love the flowers and the prints. They are very colorful and creative. You are very kind and thoughtful. I am very grateful for having you as my students." Lily and Ben are very happy. They say, "You are welcome. We are glad you like it. You are the best teacher ever." They laugh and hug their teacher again. They feel good inside. They are grateful for their teacher and their friendship.

Once there was a girl named Lucy. She was three years old and it was her birthday! Lucy was really excited to get a special present. Mommy was so busy getting everything ready for Lucy's birthday that she couldn't find Lucy's favourite necklace. Lucy was troubled because she wanted to wear it on her special day. Mommy decided to replace the necklace with a colourful bracelet. She said, "Let's replace the necklace with this bracelet, it is special just like you!" Lucy smiled and she knew that mommy was right. When it was time to give presents, Lucy opened her gift box. She was surprised to find the bracelet and she was very happy! She put it on and twirled around with a huge smile on her face. It was the best birthday ever and Lucy was so pleased to have a beautiful bracelet to wear instead of her necklace.

One day, a naughty cat named Tom went to play outside. While he was playing, a big box appeared. Tom was very curious. He went near the box to see what was inside. As he got closer, he heard a sound coming from the box. "Who's there?" Tom asked. A little voice said, "I'm a bird, and I'm stuck in this box. Can you help me get out?" Tom wanted to help the bird. He used his paws to open the box. When he opened it, the bird flew out. "Thank you for helping me!" said the bird. Tom was happy to see the bird fly. They played together all day. At the end of the day, they sat down and looked at the beautiful sky. The sight was very pretty. Tom and the bird became good friends, and they always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to draw and write in her journal. Her journal was very special. It was flexible, so she could bend it and take it anywhere she went. One day, Lily's friend, Max, came over to play. Max saw Lily's flexible journal and asked, "Can I play with your journal too?" Lily said, "Yes, Max, but we need to supply our own colors." So, Lily and Max went to find colors to supply their drawings. They found red, blue, and yellow. They were very happy. They started to draw in the flexible journal together. They drew a big sun, a house, and a tree. They had lots of fun using the colors they supplied. When they were done, they looked at their drawings and smiled. Lily and Max loved to draw in the flexible journal and supply their own colors. They played together and drew in the journal all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy model car. He would drive it around the house very fast. One day, he met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a slow turtle who wanted to play too. Tim thought about it and decided to share his model car with Sam. They took turns playing with the car. Tim would push the car fast, and Sam would push the car slow. They had lots of fun together. As they played, Tim and Sam became the best of friends. They learned that sharing made them both happy. They promised to always share their toys and play together, whether they played fast or slow.
