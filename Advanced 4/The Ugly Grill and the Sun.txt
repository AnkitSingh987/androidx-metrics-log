Title: The Ugly Grill and the Sun

Once upon a time there was an Ugly Grill. All of the other grills were shiny and smooth, but the Ugly Grill was old and rusty. Every day the Ugly Grill would sit in the sun and print for someone beautiful like the other grills. Then one day, when the Ugly Grill was feeling especially sad, a little 3 year old girl noticed him. She smiled and said, “Hi Ugly Grill! You’re so special and so different. Would you like to come to my house and be my friend?” The Ugly Grill was so excited that someone wanted to be his friend. He jumped off the ground and said “Yes!” So the little girl took the Ugly Grill home and placed him in her backyard. Soon enough, the Ugly Grill was sparkling with happiness every day. He and the little girl would play together and he would print every day and make her laugh. The Ugly Grill was no longer lonely and he was happy. He had a true friend and the sun shone brighter for him every day.

One day, a kind cat and a small dog were playing in the park. The dog had a long tail. The cat had a short tail. They liked to chase each other's tails. The dog said, "Let's stir the water in the pond with our tails!" The cat agreed, and they went to the pond. They stirred the water with their tails, making big splashes. They laughed and had fun. But then, a big bird came and said, "Stop! You are making the water dirty for the fish!" The kind cat and the small dog did not want to hurt the fish. They stopped stirring the water and said sorry to the bird. The bird forgave them, and they all played together in the park.

John and his mommy were going for a special walk in the park. John was wearing his favourite skirt and was excited to show his mommy how well he moved. "Look how fast I can go Mommy!" John said as he skipped along the path. "You look so handsome in your new skirt," said Mommy, smiling at him. Just then John spotted a tasty apple in the grass. "Can I have it?" asked John. "Yes, of course you can," said Mommy. But make sure you take care when you pick it up." John picked up the apple carefully and brushed off the dirt. He took a big bite and smiled. "It's so yummy!" he said. "I'm glad you like it," said Mommy with a twinkle in her eye. "It's important to take care of yourself so you can eat tasty food." John nodded, taking another big bite of the apple. He smiled up at his mommy and skipped happily as they finished their special walk in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Molly. She always loved to cook. One day, Molly wanted to cook a cake for her mom. She looked everywhere for the ingredients, and she found them in the kitchen cabinet. Molly began to mix the ingredients together very carefully. She knew it was important to be delicate when cooking. After she mixed everything together, Molly started to put it in the oven. But then, Molly heard her mom's voice. "Molly, don't open the oven!" said her mom. Molly stopped what she was doing and asked her mom why. "We need to be careful when cooking," said Molly's mom. "You never know what could happen if you open it too soon!" Molly smiled and thanked her mom for teaching her such an important lesson. From then on, Molly was always very careful when cooking. She knew it was important to be delicate when cooking so that nothing bad could happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had messy hair that would always stick out. She did not like to brush her hair. One day, Sue went to play outside with her friends. They were all laughing and having fun. While playing, Sue saw a small dog. The dog was trembling and looked very scared. Sue felt bad for the dog and wanted to help it. She gently pet the dog's hair and tried to make it feel better. The dog stopped trembling and started to wag its tail. Sue's friends saw her helping the dog and they all came over to help too. They all learned that it is important to be kind to animals and to help others when they are scared or sad. Sue also learned that even though her hair was messy, she could still do good things and make others happy.
