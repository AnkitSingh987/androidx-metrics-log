Title: The Fast Car and the Ice

Once upon a time there was a fast car. The car was so fast it could make a noise like a bee. It was loud and exciting! One day the car went out for a drive. As the car drove it saw a yummy ice cream truck. The car wanted to find the ice cream truck and drive faster to get there. So the car added more speed and drove very quickly to the ice cream truck. When the car arrived at the ice cream truck it said, "Can I have ice cream please?" The ice cream truck replied, "Sure! What flavour do you want?" The car said, "Chocolate please. It's my favourite!" The ice cream truck smiled, "Chocolate it is! Have a yummy ice cream!" The car drove away with a big smile on its face and a chocolate ice cream in its mouth. The car was so happy it had found something so yummy!

All of a sudden, a blue chamber opened and a group of animals started to run out. One of them was a small, furry mouse. He started to jump around the chamber and squeak, showing how happy he was. The other animals cheered and applauded loudly. They started to clap along with a rhythm, stomping their feet. As they kept stomping, everything seemed to vibrate. The mouse stopped and looked around the chamber. He saw a little girl in the corner, popping her head out from behind the big blue door. She smiled, and the mouse squeaked back. The little girl and the mouse stared at each other for a while in a comfortable silence. The other animals were still clapping and tapping but it felt like if time had stopped. Then, the girl started to clap along with the beat of the animals. She smiled and started to laugh. The animals stopped and joined in the laugh. They all applauded for the shared joy. The chamber was filled with laughter and happiness. The little mouse also jumped alongside the animals and the little girl, as they all celebrated the moment.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Alice. Alice was three years old and loved patterns. Everywhere she went she looked for patterns. One day Alice noticed a brilliant pattern made of rocks in the garden. “What is this pattern?” she questioned. Alice's mom had a surprise. “Look carefully,” she said. Alice looked closely and suddenly realized that the pattern was a picture of a butterfly! Alice was delighted and jumped around in joy. “How brilliant!” she said. Alice had such a wonderful time that she made more patterns using rocks. Every day she would make different patterns in the garden. Alice's mom was so proud of her creativity. She knew that no matter what pattern her daughter made, it would always be brilliant. The end.

One day, a black cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little boy with a red ball. The boy was sad because his ball was stuck in a tree. Tom wanted to help the boy. "Meow, I can help you get your ball," said Tom. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, cat!" Tom climbed the tree and used his sharp claws to cut the branch. The ball fell down, and the boy was happy. But when Tom climbed down, he saw that he had cut a small part of his ear too! It hurt a little, but he was still happy that he helped the boy. The boy hugged Tom and said, "You are a brave cat!" And from that day on, Tom and the boy were best friends.

One day, a girl named Lily and her friend, Tim, were playing in the park. They both loved to play catch with a ball. Lily had an original idea to make the game more fun. She told Tim, "Let's try a new way to play!" Lily mentioned her idea to Tim. She said, "We can throw the ball high and low, and call it pitch catch!" Tim liked the idea and they started playing. They threw the ball high and low, and had lots of fun. But then, a big boy came to the park. He saw Lily and Tim playing pitch catch and wanted to play too. He took the ball from Tim and said, "I want to play with you!" Tim and Lily were sad and didn't know what to do. Lily had a brave idea. She said to the big boy, "You can play with us, but we play together and share the ball." The big boy thought about it and agreed. They all played pitch catch together and had even more fun than before. In the end, Lily, Tim, and the big boy became good friends. They played pitch catch and other games in the park every day. And they always remembered to share and play together.
