Title: Lucy and John and the Flowers

Once upon a time there were two friends, Lucy and John. They were very different. Lucy loved picking flowers and dancing in the sunshine. John wanted to try new things and explore the world. One day, Lucy and John went for a walk in the park. While they were walking, Lucy thought about how different the two of them were. Even though they had different interests, she knew that she still loved John like a brother. John stopped and looked up at the sky. He thought that if he looked very closely, he could almost see his heart in the clouds. Then, he turned to Lucy and said, “I think that my heart is different than yours. But it doesn’t matter, because I still love you!” Lucy was so happy that she gave John a big hug. She knew that even if their hearts were different, they would always be friends. She thought about this for a moment, and then realized that it was true for everyone. Different hearts can still love each other!

Once upon a time, there was a big pin. The pin was important because it could help people. One day, the pin met a small boy named Tom. Tom was sad because he could not tie his shoe. The pin said, "I can help you, Tom. I will encourage you to learn to tie your shoe." Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, pin." They practiced and practiced, and soon Tom could tie his shoe. But then, something unexpected happened. The pin started to grow bigger and bigger! It became so big that it could not help Tom anymore. Tom was sad, but the pin said, "Don't worry, Tom. You can do it by yourself now. I have encouraged you to learn, and you are strong." Tom smiled, tied his shoe, and they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim did not like to eat his vegetables. Every time his mom gave him some, he would leave them on his plate. His mom tried to make him eat them, but Tim just closed his mouth tight. One day, a funny little bird came to visit Tim. The bird saw the vegetables on Tim's plate and said, "Yummy! Can I have some?" Tim looked at the bird and said, "You can have them all!" The bird ate the vegetables and started to fly around the room, happy and strong. Tim saw how happy and strong the bird was after eating the vegetables. He thought, "Maybe I should try them too." The next day, when his mom gave him vegetables, he did not leave them on his plate. He opened his mouth and took a bite. To his surprise, they tasted good! From that day on, Tim was not stubborn about eating his vegetables anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a round ball named Rollie. Rollie liked to roll around all day. One day, Rollie met an attractive kite named Kitey. Kitey could fly high in the sky. Rollie and Kitey became good friends. Rollie said to Kitey, "I wish I could fly like you." Kitey smiled and said, "But you can roll fast and far. That is fun too!" They played together every day for a week. One day, Kitey had an idea. "Rollie, let's have a race! I will fly and you will roll. Let's see who is faster!" Rollie was excited and agreed. They raced, and Rollie rolled very fast. Kitey flew high and fast too. In the end, they both reached the finish line at the same time. They laughed and knew that they were both special in their own ways.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog was very patient. It lived near a big tree. The frog had a friend, a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with the frog. They had a lot of fun together. One sunny day, Amy and the frog were playing near the tree. Amy said, "Frog, I want to give you a gift." The frog was very happy. It waited patiently for the gift. Amy looked around and picked up a pretty rock. She gave the rock to the frog. The frog liked the rock very much. Suddenly, the rock started to glow. It turned into a big, shiny star. Amy and the frog were very surprised. They did not know that the rock was magic. The star floated up into the sky. Now, every night, Amy and the frog could see their special star shining brightly. They knew that their friendship was magical too.
