Title: Sam and Ben and the Box

Sam and Ben were brothers who liked to play with fire. They found a box of matches in the kitchen and took it to their room. They lit one match and watched the flame. It was bright and hot. They felt the heat on their fingers. They lit another match and another. They put them on the floor and made a circle of fire. They laughed and clapped. They did not hear their mom calling them. They did not smell the smoke. They did not see the fire spreading to the curtains and the bed. The fire alarm went off and their mom ran to their room. She saw the fire and screamed. She grabbed Sam and Ben and tried to get out. But the door was locked. They had locked it to hide the matches. They could not open it. They could not release the lock. The fire was getting bigger and hotter. They were scared and cried. They could not breathe. They could not escape. They were not normal. They were burned.

Once upon a time, there was a heavy glove. The glove lived in a big box with other gloves. One day, the gloves decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. The heavy glove tried to jump, but it was too heavy. The other gloves laughed and said, "You cannot jump high because you are too heavy!" The heavy glove was sad and wanted to separate from the other gloves. The heavy glove went away to be alone. It found a big hill and climbed to the top. The heavy glove was still sad, but it liked being on the hill. Suddenly, the hill started to shake. The heavy glove did not know what to do. The hill was not a hill at all! It was a big monster! The monster opened its big mouth and ate the heavy glove. The other gloves were scared and ran away. They missed their heavy friend and wished they had been kinder.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Slinky. Slinky loved to play in the park. One day, Slinky saw a big bin. Slinky wanted to play with the bin, but he couldn't open it. A strong bird named Beaky saw Slinky. Beaky flew down and wanted to help. But, Slinky and Beaky started to quarrel. They both wanted to open the bin first. They were not nice to each other. While they were fighting, a big wind came. The wind blew the bin away. Slinky and Beaky were sad. They lost the bin because they did not work together. The moral of the story is to be kind and work together, or you might lose something you want.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat with soft fur. The cat liked to play in the park. One day, the cat saw a little dog arrive at the park. The dog looked guilty because it took a ball that was not its own. The cat said, "Why do you look guilty?" The dog said, "I took a ball that is not mine." The cat said, "Let's find the owner and give the ball back." They walked around the park and found a sad boy. The boy said, "I lost my ball." The cat and the dog gave the ball back to the boy. The boy was happy, and they all played together. The dog did not feel guilty anymore, and they all became friends.

Mia was so excited. She was going to be visiting an university with her parents. As they passed through the gates of the university, Mia's mom mentioned how guilty she felt. "Why are you feeling guilty, mommy?" Mia asked curiously. Her mom said, "I went to university when I was your age, but I couldn't finish my studies. I am guilty because I didn't have the chance to have a degree like you will have when you grow up." Mia was happy and excited to visit the university, and she wanted to learn more. Mia liked the university very much. She could see all the amazing tall buildings and big playgrounds. Her dad mentioned that universities are good places to learn lots of important things and they will help her in the future. Mia was amazed by everything she saw. She felt proud to know that she would have the chance to do something that her mom was never able to do. She knew now that universities are very important for her future.
