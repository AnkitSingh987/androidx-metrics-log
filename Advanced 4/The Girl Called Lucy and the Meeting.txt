Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Meeting

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and lived in a little yellow house. One day, Lucy received an invitation to join a meeting of wealthy people. She was very excited and asked her mom,“What is a meeting?” Mom said, “A meeting is when people get together and talk about something important. This is a special meeting for the wealthy.” Lucy was thrilled and asked, “What does wealthy mean?” Mom smiled and said, “It means you have lots of money. Now, why don’t you get ready? You need to look your best for the meeting.” Lucy put on her prettiest dress and joined the meeting. All the wealthy people were so surprised to see a little three-year old Lucy at the meeting. The meeting was about giving away money to charity. At the end of the meeting, everyone decided to give a big part of their wealth to charity. Lucy was really happy that she had joined the meeting and felt proud for helping out.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. She had a sweet smile and loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and couldn't fly. Daisy went to her mom and said, "Mommy, the bird is hurt. Can we help it?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Daisy. We can help the bird, but you must remember to be gentle and kind." Daisy and her mom took care of the bird. They gave it food and a soft bed to rest. After a few days, the bird felt better and could fly again. The bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Daisy, for being so sweet and kind." Daisy learned that it's important to be kind and help others when they need it. She always remembered to be gentle and sweet, just like her mom taught her. And from that day on, Daisy and the bird became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived with her parents. She liked playing and having fun. One day she took some of the meat from the table without asking. Her father saw this and said "No! You shouldn't take the meat without asking first. That is not good. I will have to punish you". The little girl felt sad and said "I'm sorry daddy. I won't do it again". Her father replied "I know sweetheart. But this time you must still be punished. Now go to your room". The little girl went to her room, feeling very sad. She thought that her father didn't love her anymore. However, after a few minutes her father came into her room and gave her a big hug. He said "I still love you and I'm sorry I had to punish you. Now let's go out for a special treat!" The little girl smiled and was very happy. She knew that her father did still love her very much!

Once upon a time, there was a big aeroplane. The aeroplane could rise high in the sky. It liked to fly with the birds and see the world from above. But the aeroplane was selfish. It did not want to share the sky with others. One day, a small bird asked the aeroplane if it could fly with it. The aeroplane said no. The bird was sad. It wanted to see the world from high up too. The bird told its friends about the selfish aeroplane. The birds came up with a plan. They all went to the aeroplane and asked if they could fly together. The aeroplane said no again. But this time, the birds did not go away. They all started to rise in the sky together. The aeroplane saw the birds rising and felt lonely. It realized that it was not fun to be selfish. The aeroplane said sorry to the birds and asked if they could all fly together. The birds were happy and said yes. From that day on, the aeroplane and the birds flew together in the sky. They saw the world from high up and had lots of fun. The aeroplane was not selfish anymore and they all became good friends.
