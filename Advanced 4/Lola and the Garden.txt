Title: Lola and the Garden

One day, while playing in the garden, Lola became embarrassed when she accidentally fell into the stream. She started to cry and didn't want to come out. Her friend John noticed and came to her rescue. He said, "Don't worry Lola, I'm here. I can help you out of the stream." "But I'm embarrassed," Lola said. John encouraged her to be patient and said, "It's ok, no one is looking. I'll help you out. Come on, let's go." John gently took Lola's hand and carefully helped her out of the stream. She was relieved and thanked John for being so patient with her. Lola gave John a big hug and went back to playing in the garden. She never felt embarrassed about the incident again.

Billy and his friends were walking in the woods. They came upon a big, dull pool filled with water. Billy found a stick nearby and he used it to stir the water. Suddenly, the water started to sparkle and turn blue! Billy and his friends were so excited they started to jump into the pool, playing and splashing around. But then they noticed there were a lot of mud and rocks on the bottom. Some of the rocks were sharp and hurt their feet. Billy said, "Let's take the stick and stir the water again. This time, let's not jump in until it is clear and clean. If we do that, then we all can be happy and safe." The kids agreed, and so they stirred the water for a few minutes, until it was clear and clean, and then they all jumped in. Everyone was very happy, and no one got hurt. The moral of the story is that it's important to take the time to make sure things are safe before you have fun. A little bit of preparation can go a long way!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her mommy's makeup. One sunny day, Lily's mommy said it was time for a nap. Lily did not want to nap, but she knew she had to listen to her mommy. While Lily was napping, her mommy went to the store. When she came back, she had a big, heavy bag. Lily could not see what was inside. She was very curious and excited. When Lily woke up from her nap, her mommy showed her what was in the heavy bag. It was a big box of makeup just for Lily! The makeup was not real, but it was perfect for playing. Lily was so happy and surprised. Now she could play with her own makeup and have fun with her friends.

John and Sarah were the best of friends. Every day they played together in the park. Today they were playing a game. Sarah asked John to take part in a tidy quiz. She said "Let's have a clap quiz! I will ask you some questions and if you answer right, you can clap your hands." John was very excited and said "Yes, let's do it!". First, Sarah asked John what 1 + 1 was and he clapped when he got it right. Then Sarah asked him about the colour of the sky and John clapped again when he got it right. John and Sarah had lots of fun playing the clap quiz. After they finished, John said "That was fun! Let's play again tomorrow!" Sarah agreed and said "OK you can tidy up the balls and I'll put away the chairs." John and Sarah had a great time playing the clap quiz and tidying up afterwards.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small room with a big bed in it. The room had black curtains that made it dark when she slept. At night, Lucy liked to sleep with her soft teddy bear. One night, Lucy woke up and felt scared because it was very dark in her room. She looked around and saw the black curtains. She wanted to find her teddy bear to feel better. Lucy searched for her teddy bear in the dark room. Finally, Lucy found her teddy bear under her bed. She hugged it tight and felt comfort. With her teddy bear, she wasn't scared of the dark anymore. Lucy went back to sleep, happy and safe. And from that day on, whenever she felt scared, her teddy bear was there to comfort her.
