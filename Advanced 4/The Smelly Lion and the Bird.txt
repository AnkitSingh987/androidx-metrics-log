Title: The Smelly Lion and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a smelly lion. The lion had a very loud voice. Every day, the lion would roar and all the animals would run away. One sunny day, the lion roared louder than ever. But this time, something unexpected happened. A small bird came flying to the lion. The bird was not scared of the lion's big voice. The lion roared again, but the bird did not fly away. The bird sang a sweet song to the lion. The lion stopped roaring and listened to the bird's song. They became good friends, and the lion learned that he did not have to roar to make friends. The smelly lion became a kind and happy lion, and all the animals in the forest loved him.

Once upon a time, there was a slow turtle named Tim. Tim liked to relax and look at himself in the mirror. One day, Tim met a fast rabbit named Rob. Rob said, "Hi Tim! Want to race?" Tim said, "Yes, let's race!" So, they started to race. Rob ran very fast, but Tim moved very slow. Tim tried to relax and think about how he could win the race. He looked at the mirror and said, "I can do it!" But, Tim was too slow. Rob won the race and Tim was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a band of animal friends. They loved to play music together. Tim played the drum, and his friends played other things like the guitar and the flute. One day, they wanted to see who could weigh the most. They found a big scale to stand on. Tim went first. He was not very heavy. Then, his friends took turns. The elephant friend was the heaviest of all. They all laughed and had fun. Tim's band was getting ready for a big show. They practiced their songs and danced. When the day came, they were all very excited. Tim said, "We are ready to play our music for everyone!" The animal friends agreed. They played their music, and everyone clapped and danced. Tim and his band were very happy.

One day, a silly bird found a missile in the forest. The bird did not know what it was, but thought it looked fun. The bird called his friend, the clever fox, to come and see. The bird said, "Look at this! I found a funny thing in the forest. Can we play with it?" The fox looked at the missile and said, "No, this does not belong here. It is not a toy. It is a missile. It can hurt us. We must tell the others." The bird and the fox told all their friends in the forest about the missile. They all worked together to move it far away, where it could not hurt anyone. The bird felt a little bit stupid for not knowing what the missile was, but the fox said, "It's okay. We all make mistakes. The important thing is to learn from them." The animals in the forest learned to be more careful with things they did not know. They also learned that it is okay to ask for help when they do not understand something. The moral of the story is to always be careful with things you do not know and to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, Sam lived in a yellow house. He had a phone and he liked to store his things in his room. One day, Sam asked his mom, "Mommy where do I store my phone?". Mommy replied, "In the drawer, sweetheart. Make sure it's safe!" So Sam opened the drawer and carefully placed his phone inside. He made sure it was tidy and snug. Sam smiled happily and said, "My phone is in a safe yellow place!"

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play together. One day, they decided to make a salad for lunch. They went to the garden and picked some lettuce, tomatoes, carrots and cucumbers. They washed them and put them in a big bowl. "Let's decorate the salad with some cheese and olives," said Anna. "That sounds yummy," said Ben. "But I have a better idea. Let's decorate the salad with some candy and cookies. That will make it more fun and sweet." Anna frowned. "That's a crazy idea, Ben. Candy and cookies don't go with salad. They will make it taste bad and unhealthy. We should eat good food that makes us strong and happy." Ben shrugged. "I don't care. I like candy and cookies more than salad. I'm going to do it anyway." He took some candy and cookies from his pocket and sprinkled them over the salad. Anna was shocked. "Ben, you ruined the salad. Now we can't eat it. That was very naughty and wasteful of you. You should say sorry and help me make a new salad." Ben looked at the salad and felt sorry. He realized that Anna was right. He had made a bad choice and spoiled their lunch. He said sorry to Anna and helped her make a new salad. They decorated it with cheese and olives and ate it together. It was delicious and healthy. They learned a lesson that day. They learned that it is important to listen to good advice and respect other people's choices. They also learned that candy and cookies are not good for salad. They are better as a treat for later.
