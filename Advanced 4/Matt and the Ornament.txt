Title: Matt and the Ornament

One day, Matt and his mom were shopping at the market. Matt really wanted an ornament, but all the ones he saw were too expensive for Matt's mom to buy. Then, Matt spotted a modest little ornament in a corner. It was gold and shiny and Matt was delighted. “Mom, can I start the ornament?” Matt asked his mom, pointing at the ornament. “Yes, Matt, you can start it,” said his mom with a smile. Matt carefully picked up the ornament and hugged it against his chest. From that day on, it remained Matt's most treasured ornament.

One day, a little girl named Ana went to visit her grandma. When they arrived at the house, grandma said, “Let’s go explore the famous garden!" Ana felt so excited. She followed grandma out the door and they started walking. Suddenly, Ana noticed a big lock on the gate to the garden and she asked “Grandma, what’s that?” Grandma smiled and said, “That’s a lock to keep out bad people. Let’s see if I can find the key.” She began searching her pockets and after a few seconds, she found it. “Aha! Here it is.” Ana watched as grandma unlocked the gate, then she followed her into the garden. It was so beautiful. Ana felt happy and excited as she ran around looking at all the flowers, trees, and plants. It was a wonderful day!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a dream. In his dream, there was a big, fearful monster. The monster was very scary and made Tim cry. One day, Tim went to play in the park. He was having fun with his friends. But then, the fearful monster from his dream came to the park. Tim was very scared, and he ran away. Tim tried to return to the park, but the monster was still there. It chased Tim and his friends away. They could not play in the park anymore. Tim was very sad, and his friends were sad too. The monster stayed in the park, and they could never play there again.

Once upon a time, in a thick forest, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a best friend, a small bird named Will. They played together every day and had so much fun. One sunny day, Lily and Will were playing near a big tree. Suddenly, they saw a sad bunny. "Why are you sad, bunny?" asked Lily. The bunny said, "I lost my mommy. I don't know where she is." Lily and Will wanted to help the bunny find her mommy. Lily said, "Let's pray and ask for help." They closed their eyes and prayed. When they opened their eyes, they saw a path. They followed the path and found the bunny's mommy. The bunny was so happy! They all played together and became good friends. From that day on, they always prayed when they needed help.

Sara and Ben went to the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw many things in the water, like fish, crabs and shells. They also saw some jellyfish. Jellyfish were soft and round and had long things that could sting. "Look, Sara, jellyfish!" Ben said, pointing to the water. "Can we gather them and take them home?" "No, Ben, we can't," Sara said. "Jellyfish need to stay in the water. They will die if we take them out. That is selfish. We should let them be." "But I want to have a jellyfish as a pet," Ben said. "They are pretty and shiny. Please, Sara, let me have one." "Ben, no!" Sara said. "Jellyfish are not pets. They are wild animals. They belong to the sea. They will be sad and hurt if you take them away. You have to be kind and share. How would you feel if someone took you away from your home and family?" Ben thought for a moment. He did not like the idea of being taken away from his home and family. He loved his mom and dad and Sara. He did not want to make the jellyfish sad and hurt. He decided to listen to Sara and leave the jellyfish alone. "Okay, Sara, you are right," Ben said. "I don't want to be selfish. I want to be kind and share. Let's go and play with something else." Sara smiled and hugged Ben. She was proud of her brother for being nice. They went and played with their buckets and shovels and made a big sandcastle. They had a lot of fun at the beach. They did not bother the jellyfish and the jellyfish did not bother them. They were happy.
