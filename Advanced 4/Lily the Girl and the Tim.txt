Title: Lily the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. She was very original. She loved to listen to her mom and dad. They told her stories every day. One day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim was very patient. Lily and Tim played together all day. They listened to stories and had fun. But one day, Lily and Tim did not listen to their parents. They went far away from their homes. They got lost and could not find their way back. They were sad and scared. The end.

Once upon a time, a thin cat named Tom lived in a big house. He liked to play with a ball every day. On Wednesday, he saw a new ball in the yard. It was a big, red ball. Tom was very happy and jumped to play with it. As Tom played with the ball, he met a new friend, a little dog named Max. Max said, "Can I play with you and your ball?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play!" They played and played, and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball began to reverse! It started to get smaller and smaller. Tom and Max were very surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. Now, they had a small, red ball to play with. They played together every Wednesday and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends; Bob and Frank. They were playing together in their garden when they saw a wheel, lying on the ground. They were curious, so they each decided to weigh it. Frank was quick, so he held the wheel and straight away he could tell that it was too heavy for him. But Bob thought he could do it, so he tried to lift it. As he tried, the wheel became too heavy for him and he couldn't move it anymore. Bob felt so sad and Frank tried to cheer him up. But what had happened was too upsetting and Bob just wanted to go home. On their way back, they saw many other toys that were much lighter to lift and much more lively! Even though Frank persuaded Bob to try them out, he just wanted to go home and gave up. Unfortunately, Bob never tried playing with any of the toys again and he spent the rest of his days inside watching cartoons on the couch. He never forgot the wheel that he tried to lift - but never could.

Once there was a girl named Betty. She always wanted to go outside and play. One day she decided to go for a walk in the forest. As she walked, Betty noticed a mosquito buzzing around her. She laughed and decided to try to catch it. She jumped up and tried to lift her hand high enough to grab the mosquito. Suddenly, the ground around Betty started to rumble. A big cloud of dirt erupted from the ground. Betty gasped in surprise. She quickly tried to wipe the dirt off her face and clothes, but they were now so dirty. Betty was so upset that she started to cry. The mosquito saw all that was happening and flew away. Betty just stood there, crying and covered in dirt.

One day, a cat named Tom lay on a soft mat. He was very tired. Tom saw a big police car outside his house. The police car had bright lights and made loud sounds. Tom was a little scared, but he knew the police were there to help. Tom went outside to see the police car. A kind police officer saw Tom and smiled. The officer said, "Hi, Tom! Don't worry, everything is fine. We are here to help a lost dog find his way home." Tom felt better and wanted to help the lost dog too. Tom and the police officer looked for the lost dog. They walked and walked until they found him. The dog was happy to see them. Tom said, "We found you! Now, let's go home." The police officer, Tom, and the dog went back to the big police car. They all smiled because they knew everything was fine.
