Title: Andy the Red Automobile and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a small red automobile named Andy. Andy liked to go fast and play with his friends. One day, Andy saw a big truck coming down the road. The big truck was very loud and made Andy feel frightened. Andy started to worry. He did not want the big truck to hurt him or his friends. So, Andy went to his best friend, a blue bicycle named Betty. Betty was very smart and always helped Andy when he was scared. Betty told Andy not to worry. She said they could hide behind a tree until the big truck went away. Andy and Betty hid together and soon, the big truck left. They were not frightened anymore. Andy and Betty went back to playing and having fun with their friends. They were happy and safe again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a pale butterfly near a pretty flower. He wanted to catch it, so he tried to zip towards it. But the butterfly flew away. Tim felt sad. Then, Tim saw a small pebble on the ground. It was shiny and smooth. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. He felt happy again. Tim walked around the park, holding his pebble and looking at the trees and birds. As Tim was about to go home, he saw the pale butterfly again. This time, he did not try to catch it. He just looked at it and smiled. The butterfly flew closer to him and landed on his hand. Tim felt very happy and knew that he had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big animal. It was a very comfortable animal. It liked to sit and sleep all day. The animal had many friends. They liked to play and have fun. One day, the animal's friends wanted to play a new game. The animal did not like the new game. The animal said, "I disagree. I do not want to play this game." The friends were sad. They wanted the animal to play with them. The animal thought about it. It did not want its friends to be sad. So, the animal tried the new game. It was not so bad. The animal and its friends played the game and had fun. They were all happy. The animal learned that it was good to try new things, even if it disagreed at first.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a mirror in her room. She did not like the mirror. It made her feel uncomfortable. She would say, "I hate that mirror." One day, Sue's mom saw her looking at the mirror. She asked Sue, "Why do you hate the mirror?" Sue said, "It makes me feel bad." Her mom said, "Let's make the mirror fun." Sue and her mom put funny stickers on the mirror. They put a big, red nose sticker and a funny hat sticker. Now, when Sue looked in the mirror, she saw a funny face. She laughed and did not feel uncomfortable anymore. Sue learned that she could change things she did not like and make them fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a modest school. In this school, there lived a kind teacher named Miss Lily. She loved to teach little kids about math. She would whisper the numbers, and the kids would listen very carefully. One day, a new kid named Tim came to the school. Tim did not like math. He did not like to listen when Miss Lily whispered the numbers. Tim would just play with his toys and not pay attention. Miss Lily saw that Tim did not like math, so she whispered a secret to him. She told him that if he learned math, he could be a big helper to his friends. Tim wanted to be a helper, so he started to listen and learn. Soon, Tim was good at math and he helped his friends when they needed it. The moral of the story is that we can learn new things and be helpful to others if we pay attention and listen.
