Title: Tim the Boy and the Lion

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play and act like animals in nature. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a group of kids playing together. Tim wanted to join them, but he was too shy. Tim decided to act like a lion to make the kids laugh. He roared and crawled on the ground. But the kids just looked at him and said, "What are you doing? You look silly!" Tim felt ashamed and sad. He ran to his mom, crying. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. Maybe they just don't understand your game." But Tim was still sad, and they went home. That day, Tim learned that not everyone likes the same games, and sometimes, that can hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many flowers. One day, a new flower wanted to bloom. The new flower was very flexible. It could bend and twist. In the middle of the garden, there was a stage. All the flowers wanted to be on the stage. But there was not enough room for all the flowers. So, the flowers had a big fight. They pushed and pulled to be on the stage. The flexible flower did not want to fight. It wanted to bloom and be happy. So, the flexible flower found a small spot near the stage. It bloomed there, and it was very pretty. The other flowers saw the flexible flower and stopped fighting. They all bloomed together and the garden was beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom was not happy because he did not like to play with the other cats. They would laugh at him when he tried to balance on the fence. One day, Tom met a wise old cat named Mr. Whiskers. Mr. Whiskers knew a lot about history. He told Tom stories about brave cats who could balance on the highest trees. Tom liked to listen to Mr. Whiskers and wanted to be like the cats in the stories. Mr. Whiskers taught Tom how to balance on the fence. Tom practiced every day, and soon he could balance just like the cats in the stories. The other cats stopped laughing at him, and Tom was not grumpy anymore. Tom and Mr. Whiskers became good friends and shared many happy days together.

Mary and her mom were in the kitchen. Mary was so happy to bake a cake. She got out all the ingredients and bowls. Mom said, "What kind of cake should we make?" Mary thought and then said, "I want to make a chocolate cake!" Mom nodded and said, "Okay, chocolate cake it is!" They gathered the butter, sugar, flour and cocoa. They mixed them together and put it in a pan. Mom showed Mary how to put it in the oven. The cake smelled amazing and when it was ready, it was time to frost it. Mary's opinion was that it should be vanilla frosting. Mom agreed and they frosted the cake. The cake was a masterpiece and Mary was so happy! Everyone was happy when they ate the cake - it was delicious! Mary smiled and said, "I'm so glad we got to bake a cake together."

Once upon a time, there was an ostrich named Ollie. Ollie was a perfect ostrich. He had big eyes, long legs, and a soft neck. Ollie had a best friend named Lily. Lily was a glow bug. She could light up and make pretty colors. One day, Ollie and Lily were playing in the dark. Ollie said, "Lily, can you glow for me, please?" Lily tried to glow, but she could not. She was sad. Ollie and Lily wanted to find out why Lily could not glow. They went to ask their friend, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl was very wise. He told Ollie and Lily, "I think I know the problem. Lily, you need to eat more yummy food." Ollie and Lily went to find yummy food for Lily. They found some sweet fruit. Lily ate the sweet fruit, and then something unexpected happened. Lily started to glow! But not only did she glow, she also grew bigger and bigger. Ollie was so happy for Lily. Now, they could play in the dark again. And Lily was the biggest glow bug ever!
