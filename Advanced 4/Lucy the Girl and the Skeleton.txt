Title: Lucy the Girl and the Skeleton

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She saw a big box in the corner. She opened it and found a skeleton toy. Lucy was very happy and played with it all day. At night, Lucy heard a loud sound from the box. She was scared, but she went to look. The skeleton toy was now big and alive! It was difficult for Lucy to believe. The skeleton wanted to play, but Lucy was too scared. She knew she had to make it small again. Lucy had an idea. She told the skeleton, "If we hurry and play a fun game, I will help you be small again." The skeleton agreed. They played a game and had fun. When they finished, Lucy put the skeleton back in the box. It became small again, and Lucy was not scared anymore.

One day, a cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom started to climb the tree, but he got a crazy itch on his back. He tried to reach the itch, but he could not. Tom felt very sad. Then, a bird named Sally saw Tom. Sally wanted to help Tom with his itch. She flew down and used her beak to scratch Tom's back. Tom was so happy that Sally managed to help him with his itch. They became good friends. But, here is something unexpected: Sally was not just a bird, she was a magic bird! She used her magic to make the crazy itch go away forever. Tom and Sally played and had fun all day. They were the best of friends, and they always helped each other when they needed it.

One Sunday, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw many kids playing there. Tim was very happy. He saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. Tim asked a girl, "Can I play with your ball?" The girl said, "No, it's mine!" Tim felt bad. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can find another ball to play with." They walked and saw a nice man with a big blue ball. The man said, "Do you want to play with my ball?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was not sad anymore. He had a great Sunday at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amelia who was three years old. She was always so excited and full of ideas. One morning, Amelia started to hum a little as she decided to take a bath. She ran to the bathroom and could hardly wait to splash in the bathtub. “Amelia, what are you up to?” asked her mom. “I am making a bathtub boat!” chimed Amelia. Amelia started filling the bathtub with warm water and collecting things to decorate with. She grabbed an orange marker and drew squiggles, circles and lines all around the tub and some orange circles for her boat’s flags. The little girl was so excited for her boat to take off. She stepped into the bathtub, pretending she was a captain on a voyage. The markers bubbled in the water as she jumped up and down, waving her imaginary flags. Amelia set sail and the orange flags flew in the air. She had the best journey she had ever taken and when it was time to dock the boat, Amelia knew she would never forget her Orange Bathtub Boat adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, Max found a puzzle with many pieces. Max wanted to complete the puzzle, so he started to put the pieces together. Max was having a hard time with the puzzle. He needed help to finish it. Max had an idea! He went to find glue. Max thought the glue would help him complete the puzzle. He put glue on the pieces and tried to stick them together. But something unexpected happened! The glue made the pieces stick to Max's paws. Max was not able to complete the puzzle. Instead, Max had a new game to play. He had to shake the pieces off his paws. Max laughed and played, and he learned that sometimes things don't go as planned, but that's okay.
