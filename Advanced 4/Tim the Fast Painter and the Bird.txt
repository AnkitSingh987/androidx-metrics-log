Title: Tim the Fast Painter and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a fast painter named Tim. He liked to paint pretty pictures very fast. One day, he saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to paint it. So, he got his paint and brush and started to work. As Tim was painting, a little bird came by and said, "Hey, Tim! Can you paint me too?" Tim said, "Sure, little bird!" So, he painted the bird very fast. The bird was happy, but Tim didn't know that the bird was a tricky bird. The tricky bird told everyone that Tim could paint really fast. More and more animals came to Tim to be painted. Tim was so busy, he didn't have time to paint the big tree anymore. One day, a big bear came and said, "Tim, I heard you can paint fast. Paint me now!" Tim tried to paint the bear, but he was very tired. The bear got angry because Tim couldn't paint him fast enough. In the end, Tim was too tired to paint any more pictures. The animals were sad, and the big tree never got painted. The tricky bird flew away, leaving Tim and the other animals with a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big girl named Lucy. She was going to a place called university. Lucy needed to pack her things in a big bag. She put her clothes, shoes, and books in the bag. Lucy was very excited to go to university. But Lucy was also a little frightened. She had never been away from her home and family before. She did not know if she would make new friends. Her mom and dad told her that she would be okay and that they were proud of her. When Lucy got to university, she met a nice girl named Jane. Jane helped Lucy unpack her big bag. They laughed and talked while they put their things away. Lucy was not frightened anymore. She was happy to be at university with her new friend, Jane.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big orange cat. This cat was not like other cats. He had a shield. The shield was his best friend. One day, the cat and his shield went for a walk. They walked and walked until they heard a sound. The sound was a little bird. The bird was sad because it couldn't fly. The orange cat wanted to help the bird. He used his shield to make a safe place for the bird. The bird was happy and thanked the cat. The cat and his shield were happy too. They all became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a troubled girl because she always felt panic when she was alone. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to go to the kitchen and get some cookies for her little brother. When Lucy went to the kitchen, she saw a big, scary shadow on the wall. She felt panic and started to cry. "Mommy, help me!" she yelled. Her mom came running into the kitchen and saw Lucy crying. Her mom said, "Lucy, don't be scared. It's just a shadow from the tree outside." She showed Lucy the tree and the shadow went away. Lucy felt better and took the cookies to her brother. From that day on, Lucy was not scared of the kitchen anymore.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat jam on her bread. One day, she found a big jar of jam in the kitchen. She was so happy! Amy took the jar of jam to her room. She wanted to eat it all by herself. But, when she tried to open the jar, it was too tight. She tried and tried, but she could not open it. She started to suffer because she wanted the jam so much. Then, her little brother, Tim, came into the room. He saw Amy suffering and asked, "What's wrong, Amy?" Amy said, "I can't open this jam jar!" Tim smiled and said, "Let me help you!" Together, they opened the jar and shared the delicious jam. From that day on, they always helped each other and enjoyed their jam even more.
