Title: Danny and the Car

Danny loved to run. He put on his running shoes and bounced out the door. He wanted to explore the neighborhood. Suddenly, he smelled something strange. It smelled like gas. Danny knew the smell was not good so he started to run. He ran and ran until he saw the source of the smell. It was a hot car. The car was making a funny noise, and Danny was scared. He quickly ran back home. He was so glad that he was safe. He had followed his nose and run away from the gas.

The little girl was so excited that she could barely stay still. She clapped her hands together and watched out of the window as a small helicopter flew above her house. She wanted so badly to go outside and take a closer look. Suddenly, her mom called out to her. "Come on, sweetheart. Put on your shoes and grab a coat. We're going to the park!" The little girl was confused. What was her mom talking about? Nonetheless, she quickly did as she was asked and soon she and her mom were in the park together. The little girl looked around and saw something magnificent: a big, beautiful helicopter had landed in the middle of the park! She ran to it and looked all around, amazed by its size and its colour. Her mom explained to her that it was a rare and special kind of helicopter and the pilot had come from far away to see them. The little girl's eyes lit up. She couldn't believe that she got to see such an amazing thing. She waved goodbye to the pilot as he flew away and clapped with joy when she saw the small helicopter disappear into the sky.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Daisy who liked to play with drums. One day, Daisy was walking through the forest and she saw a glowing drum. She wanted to try it out, so she moved closer and saw that it had special powers. When Daisy picked up the drum sticks, a magical sound filled the air. Suddenly, the glow from the drum started to expand and Daisy felt special. Daisy's mom heard the sound and came outside. She asked Daisy what had happened. Daisy told her mom about the magical drum and showed the glow coming from it. Daisy's mom smiled and said, "You were successful in making a music so sweet. That glow is your reward" Daisy was so happy. She continued to play the drum as the glow surrounded her. Daisy learned that day that success can be found in moments of joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She loved to skip and play in the park. One sunny day, she saw an angel in the park. The angel had a big smile and pretty wings. Ann was very happy to see the angel. "Hi, angel!" Ann said. "Do you want to skip and play with me?" The angel looked at Ann and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, but I must tell you I am a fearful angel. I get scared very easily." Ann did not mind and they started to play together. As they played, the sky turned dark and scary. The angel became very fearful. Ann tried to help the angel, but the angel was too scared. The angel flew away, leaving Ann alone in the dark park. Ann was sad that her new friend left her, and she never saw the angel again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a tree with his family. One day, Billy saw a shiny thing on the ground. He flew down to look at it. It was a long wire. Billy thought it was fun to play with. Billy played with the wire for a while. But soon, he got tired and weak. He wanted to go back to his tree. But the wire was stuck to his leg. He tried to fly, but he could not. He was very sad. Billy's mom saw him and came to help. She knew what to do. She pulled the wire with her beak and released it from his leg. Billy was so happy that he could go back to his tree. He learned not to play with things that can hurt him.
