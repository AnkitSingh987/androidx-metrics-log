Title: Luba and Lena and the Sandwich

Once there were two sisters, Luba and Lena. Luba was very obedient and liked to do things to help her family. Lena was different and often refused to listen. One day, their Mom asked Luba to take a sandwich to her uncle who was sick. Luba happily agreed and gathered the sandwich. On the way, she came across Lena who was crying. Luba asked what was wrong and Lena said that her sandwich had fallen on the ground. Luba had an idea and offered to replace the sandwich for her. Lena was happy and thanked her sister. Luba gave Lena the sandwich and went on her way to deliver the replacement sandwich to her uncle. She was proud of herself for being so obedient and helping her sister.

Once upon a time there was a bird who was looking for a new home. He flew around the forest and looked high and low but he couldn't find a place to stay. Then he came across a colorful nest in the tree. He flew closer and saw it was the perfect place to live. The bird was very happy. He started to balance the nest by using some sticks he found on the ground. He worked very hard until it was just right. After that he decided to decorate it with some leaves and colorful feathers he found nearby. Once the nest was finished the bird flew inside, he was so happy that he had a new home. From his nest he watched the clouds go by and sometimes he rested there with his friends. The colorful nest was what the bird had been looking for. He would balance in the nest for many days and many nights. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bulb. It was in a big room. The bulb liked to stay on. One day, a boy came into the room. The boy saw the colorful bulb. He said, "Wow, you are so colorful! Will you stay on for me?" The bulb said, "Yes, I will stay on for you." But then, the boy left the room and forgot to turn off the bulb. The bulb stayed on for a very long time. The bulb got very hot and then, the bulb broke. The room was dark and the colorful bulb was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite flag. It was red and blue. He loved to wave it in the wind. One day, a big wind came and took his flag away. Tim was very sad. He wanted his flag back. Tim saw his flag stuck in a tree. The tree was very tall. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam was scared, but he wanted to help poor Tim. They tried to remove the flag from the tree. They jumped and reached, but the flag was too high. Then, they had an idea. They found a long stick. They used the stick to remove the flag from the tree. The flag was safe! Tim was so happy. He thanked Sam for his help. From that day on, Tim and Sam became best friends. They played with the flag every day, and they always helped each other.

Mandy wanted a new carpet in her room. “Mommy,” asked Mandy. “Can I have a new carpet?” “What kind of carpet do you want?” asked Mommy. “I want a regular carpet,” said Mandy. “It should be blue with stars on it.” Mommy said, “Let’s go look for a regular carpet.” They went to the store and found a regular carpet that had blue stars on it. “Can I have this one?” asked Mandy. “Yes,” said Mommy. “It will be perfect for your room.” Mandy was so happy she jumped for joy. She and Mommy brought the carpet home and put it in her room. Mandy sat on the new carpet and said “I love my regular carpet.”

One day Sarah and her mom drove to the icy lake. Sarah smiled and hopped out of the car. She saw that some people were skiing and having fun on the frozen lake. Sarah wanted to try it, too, so she asked her mom if she could. "You need a special license before you can ski," her mom said. "Please can I have one?" Sarah asked. Her mom shook her head and smiled. "Not yet, but if you practice and show me that you can ski safely, we can ask Grandpa to help you get a license." Sarah ran to give Grandpa a big hug. Together they all practiced skiing on the icy lake. After a few days, Sarah was a great skier and Grandpa said she was ready for her license. So Grandpa released the license to Sarah and she was so happy. She thanked her mom and grandpa for helping her learn how to ski. Sarah can now enjoy skiing on the icy lake with her mom and grandpa.
