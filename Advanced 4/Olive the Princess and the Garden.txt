Title: Olive the Princess and the Garden

Once upon a time, there lived a princess named Olive. Every day, Olive would go outside and play in the beautiful garden. She loved to explore and enjoy the perfect sunshine. One day, while she was walking, Olive saw a giant dragon! She gasped in surprise. She wanted to hide, but the dragon saw her. The dragon said, "You have to come with me!" Olive shook her head and said, "No, I won't go with you!" The dragon said, "You must come! If you resist, I will be very angry!" But Olive refused and said, "No! I won't go!" She was brave and strong, and she was determined to resist. Fortunately, the dragon was so impressed by Olive's courage that he decided to let her go. He flew away and Olive went back home safely. This showed Olive that no matter how scared she was, she could always resist to be brave. From that day on, she was known as the perfect princess, the brave Olive.

Once upon a time, there was a man who had a lovely wife. They lived in a small house with their little dog. The man and his wife loved each other very much. They liked to play with their dog, and they liked to eat good food. They were very happy. One day, the man had to go away for work. He was sad because he would miss his lovely wife and their dog. The wife was sad too, but she told him to go and do his work. She said she would take care of their dog and wait for him to come back. While the man was away, the wife found a big box in their yard. It was a surprise! She opened the box and found a big, friendly cat inside. The cat was lost and needed a home. The wife decided to keep the cat and take care of it. When the man came back from work, he saw the cat and was very surprised. He did not expect to find a new friend in their home. Now, the man, his lovely wife, their dog, and the new cat lived happily together in their small house.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Hippy. Hippy lived near a small pond. He was very organized. Every day, he would remove dirt and leaves from his pond to keep it clean. One sunny day, a little bird named Betty came to visit Hippy. She saw how clean and organized his pond was. Betty asked, "Hippy, can you help me remove leaves from my nest?" Hippy was happy to help. He went to Betty's nest and saw many leaves. They worked together to remove the leaves. Soon, Betty's nest was clean and organized too. Betty was very thankful. She said, "Thank you, Hippy, for helping me!" Hippy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Betty! I'm always here to help my friends." From that day on, Hippy and Betty were the best of friends.

Anna liked to play in her room with her toys. She had dolls, cars, books, and puzzles. But she was not allowed to touch the closet. Mommy said the closet was for grown-ups only. Anna was curious. What was in the closet? Why could she not touch it? One day, when Mommy was busy in the kitchen, Anna decided to sneak into her room and open the closet. She pulled the door and saw many things inside. She saw clothes, shoes, bags, and boxes. She also saw something shiny and round. It looked like a ball. Anna wanted to touch it. She reached for the shiny ball and grabbed it. But it was not a ball. It was a snow globe. It had water and glitter and a little house inside. Anna shook it and saw the glitter move. She smiled. It was pretty. She did not know it was Mommy's favorite snow globe that Daddy gave her for their anniversary. Suddenly, she heard Mommy's voice. "Anna, what are you doing in your room? Come to the kitchen, please." Anna got scared. She knew she was in trouble. She tried to put the snow globe back in the closet, but she dropped it. It broke into pieces. The water and glitter spilled on the floor. Anna started to cry. Mommy came into the room and saw the mess. She saw the broken snow globe and the open closet. She was angry and sad. She said to Anna, "Anna, why did you touch the closet? I told you not to touch it. You broke my snow globe. It was very special to me. You are a naughty girl." Anna felt sorry. She said to Mommy, "I'm sorry, Mommy. I wanted to see the shiny ball. I did not know it was your snow globe. I did not mean to break it. Please don't be mad at me." Mommy sighed. She hugged Anna and said, "I'm not mad at you, Anna. I'm just disappointed. You should listen to me when I tell you something. The closet has things that are not for you. They are not toys. They are not harmless. They can hurt you or break. You have to be careful. Do you understand?" Anna nodded. She said, "I understand, Mommy. I won't touch the closet again. I'm sorry I made you sad. I love you, Mommy." Mommy said, "I love you too, Anna. Now let's clean up this mess and go to the kitchen. I have a surprise for you. It's a new book. Do you want to read it with me?" Anna smiled. She said, "Yes, Mommy. I love books. Thank you, Mommy." Mommy and Anna cleaned up the room and went to the kitchen. They read the new book together and had a nice time. Anna learned her lesson. She never touched the closet again.
