Title: Jack and the Bowl

Little Jack was so clever. One day Jack got a plan. He wanted to make a special treat - yogurt! So he walked to the store to get everything he needed. When he arrived, he got some yogurt, a spoon and a bowl. He was excited to put everything together. With his clever little hands, he stirred and stirred the yogurt until it was all mixed up. Then he poured it in the bowl. Jack waited a few minutes and then tasted his yogurt. It was delicious. He had made a perfect treat. He was so proud of himself - he was very clever!

Once upon a time, there was a big land. In this land, there was an elderly dog named Max. Max was very old but still liked to play with his friends. One sunny day, Max went to a show with his friends. At the show, there were many animals doing tricks. Max and his friends watched and had lots of fun. At the end of the show, Max and his friends clapped their paws to applaud the animals. They were very happy to see the tricks. After the show, Max and his friends went home and told everyone about their fun day.

One day, a shy cat named Kitty was playing in the park. She saw a big box with a bow on it. It was a present! Kitty was very excited and wanted to know what was inside. She tried to open it, but she was too shy to ask for help. Then, a happy dog named Doggy came to the park. He saw Kitty and the present. Doggy asked, "Do you need help? I can care for you and help you open the present." Kitty was still shy, but she said, "Yes, please." Doggy helped Kitty open the present. But, surprise! Inside the present was another shy cat just like Kitty! They all became good friends and played together in the park. Now, Kitty was not shy anymore because she had friends who cared for her.

Once upon a time, there was a temple in a very beautiful and expensive part of town. One day, a 3 year old boy was walking by the temple. He saw a bench in front of the temple and he thought it looked very comfy. He approached the bench and said, "Can I please sit here?" The temple priest who was nearby overheard the boy's question. He replied, "Yes, of course! You can sit here and relax." The little boy was very happy and excited. He sat down on the bench outside the temple. His parents were watching from a distance and they were very happy to see their son so content. The boy stayed on the bench for a long time and enjoyed the peaceful atmosphere. He thought about what a beautiful, expensive place it was to sit and relax. After a while, the boy got up, thanked the temple priest for letting him sit there and went home feeling happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with stones. One day, he found a big, dangerous stone. It was sharp and heavy. Tim knew he should not play with it, but he wanted to have fun. Tim took the dangerous stone to his room. That night, he had a dream. In his dream, the stone talked to him. The stone said, "I can help you, but you must be careful. I can hurt you if you are not careful." The next day, Tim was playing with the dangerous stone. Suddenly, it slipped from his hand and broke a big glass. Tim's mom heard the noise and came running. She saw the broken glass and the dangerous stone. She was very angry. Tim told his mom about his dream. She did not believe him at first. Then, the stone talked to her too. It said, "I am sorry for the trouble. I will help you." The stone turned into a soft, safe toy. Tim and his mom were happy. From that day on, Tim only played with safe toys.

Once upon a time, there was a big red door. This door was at a place called a university. The university was a place where people went to learn and play. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the university with his mom. Tim wanted to open the big red door. His mom said, "Tim, this door is very easy to open. Just push it." Tim pushed the door, and it opened with a creak. Inside the university, Tim saw many things. There were big rooms with lots of chairs and tables. There were also smaller rooms with fun toys and games. Tim played with the toys and made new friends. He had a great time at the university with its big red door.
