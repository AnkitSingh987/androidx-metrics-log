Title: The Strong Painter and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a strong painter. He loved to paint all day. His paintings were very pretty. He had many colors to use for his paintings. One day, a little boy came to the painter. The boy had a question. He asked the painter, "Can you teach me how to paint?" The painter said, "Yes, I can help you." The painter showed the boy how to use the brush and colors. They painted a big tree, a house, and a sun. The boy was very happy, and the painter was proud. They painted together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Tim. Tim lived in a big forest with his ant friends. They all had a nice home under a log. One day, the log broke. Tim and his friends needed a new log to have a home. They looked and looked, but they could not find a log. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked his strong ant friends to help him move a new log. All the ants worked together and moved the log to their home. Now, they have a new log to live under. Tim and his friends were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard rock. On top of the rock, there was a small, green bug. The bug liked to order the ants around. He would tell them, "Go here!" or "Go there!" The ants did not like it, but they did what the bug said. One day, a little girl found a page from a book. She put the page on the hard rock. The green bug saw the page and thought it was a fun new place to play. He jumped onto the page and started to order the ants to come too. But the ants were tired of being bossed around. The ants made a plan. They pushed the page with the green bug on it off the hard rock. The green bug was surprised and could not order the ants anymore. The ants were happy they could do what they wanted. The green bug learned not to boss others around, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind old man who lived in a village. He loved to work in his garden, and he was always very charming when people visited him. One day he decided to plant a beautiful copper flower next to his garden gate to make it look special. He dug a hole, planted it and then began to work hard to water it every day. Once in a while, a group of tiny children would come to watch him work. On one such day, one of the children asked him why he was watering the flower. "This is a special copper flower so that all the people who visit me will be charmed when they look at it," he replied with a smile. The children were enthralled and so were the other villagers. Everyone loved the old man's flower and admired how hard he worked. The old man continued to water and care for the flower, and it remained as beautiful and charming as ever. And so, it was enjoyed by all until the end of his days.

Once upon a time there was a smart baby named Bob. He loved playing with his toys and running around outside. One day, while playing outside, Bob saw a beautiful perfume bottle. It had a shiny pink lid, and he received it as a gift from his loving grandma. Bob started to wonder what was inside. Suddenly, his grandma appeared and said “Bob, what are you doing here?” Bob smiled, bowed and said “I’m looking at this pretty bottle. What kind of perfume is inside?” Grandma smiled and said “Open it, and see for yourself.” Bob opened the lid and was delighted with the smell of the heavenly perfume. He took a deep breath and said “Grandma, this smells so yummy!” Grandma smiled and said, “Go and show it to all your friends, Bob. You are so smart!” Bob thanked his Grandma and bowed before running off to show his friends his new present. The End
