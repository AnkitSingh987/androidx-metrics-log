Title: Mia and the Car

Mia and her family were getting ready for a festival. She was nervous and a bit confused at first because she did not know what to expect. Her parents told her it would be fun and she smiled. Mia decided to wear her blue dress and scrubbed extra hard to make sure it was shiny and clean. The family got in their car and drove to the festival. Mia was very excited to see all the people and things that were there. It was much bigger than she expected. Her grandma showed her the different food stands, the balloons and even the animals. Mia wanted to get her face painted but first, she wanted to wash her hands with the soap. As she washed, she felt very proud that she could do it all by herself. After she washed, Mia got her face painted with a big purple flower and felt very happy. The festival was a great experience for Mia. She learned to be less confused by getting to know the different things that the festival had to offer. She also felt proud of herself for washing her hands all by herself.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a weird toy that he liked to play with all day. One day, Tim saw his toy lean on a big box in his room. He thought it was funny and laughed. The next day, Tim's mom told him they were going to visit his grandma. Tim was very happy and wanted to show grandma his weird toy. So, he put the toy in his bag and went to grandma's house. He remembered how his toy leaned on the box and thought grandma would laugh too. When they got to grandma's house, Tim took out his toy and made it lean on a chair. Grandma saw the toy and laughed. She said, "Oh, Tim, what a funny toy!" Tim felt happy and clapped his hands. He knew his weird toy would make grandma laugh, just like it did when it leaned on the box at home.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Bob. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he found a piece of paper on the floor. He picked it up and saw a picture of a cake. He wanted to eat the cake, but he did not know how to make it. Bob asked his friend Tom for help. Tom said, "I can make the cake." They went to the store to buy things they needed. But they started to disagree on the color of the cake. Bob wanted a blue cake and Tom wanted a red cake. In the end, they decided to make two cakes, one blue and one red. They had a lot of fun making the cakes and eating them. They learned that it is okay to disagree and still be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big impressive rainbow in the sky. It had many colors. People liked to look at it. The rainbow knew something fun would happen soon. One day, a little girl named Sue saw the rainbow. Sue wanted to have a rainbow party. She told her friends about the big rainbow. They all wanted to come to the party too. The day of the party came. Sue and her friends wore rainbow clothes. They played rainbow games and ate rainbow cake. Then, the rainbow in the sky got even bigger and brighter. Everyone was so happy. The fun thing the rainbow knew about was Sue's rainbow party.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive tree in a park. It had many leaves and gave lots of shade. Animals and people loved to rest under the tree. One day, a little rabbit was very tired. He wanted to sleep under the tree. He hopped to the shade and lay down. Soon, he fell asleep. He had a nice dream. When the rabbit woke up, he saw that other animals joined him. There was a squirrel, a bird, and a cat. They all slept under the tree too. The rabbit felt happy and safe with his new friends. The moral of the story is that we can find friendship and safety when we share and help each other.
