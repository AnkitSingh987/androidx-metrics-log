Title: The Bug and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a small bug. The small bug liked to blink a lot. One day, the small bug went for a walk. The sun was hot in the sky. The small bug felt hot too. The small bug saw a big bug. The big bug was not nice. The big bug said, "You blink too much! I do not like it." The small bug felt sad. The small bug did not want to blink, but it could not stop. The small bug went home. The small bug's mom saw the sad face. The mom said, "Why are you sad?" The small bug told the story. The mom said, "Do not be sad. Blinking is good. The big bug is wrong." The small bug felt better. The small bug knew it was okay to blink.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a big farm with many other animals. One day, she saw a big cup of milk on the ground. Daisy was very thirsty. Daisy went to her friend, the chicken, and said, "I dare you to drink the milk!" The chicken was scared, but she did not want to look weak. So, she walked to the milk and took a sip. Suddenly, the milk spilled all over the ground! All the other animals were mad at Daisy and the chicken. They had no more milk to drink. Daisy felt sad and cried. The chicken was also sad. They were still lonely and now they had no milk to drink.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly sheep who lived on a hill. He was always very happy, but one day he had a problem. He noticed that a big fence had been built around the hill, and he knew he was trapped. The friendly sheep felt scared and sad, but he thought of a plan. He decided he must escape, so he ran and jumped as high as he could. He tried and tried but he just could not get over the fence. As he lay there feeling exhausted, he heard an animal talking to him. It was a kind goat. "I can help you," said the goat. "I can take you to a place where you can be free." The friendly sheep was so excited. He followed the goat and together they escaped from the hill. The friendly sheep was so grateful to the kind goat. He will never forget his special escape.

Once upon a time there was a tall tree in the woods. Every day, a happy family of birds would come together on a branch to sing and play. One morning, the birds noticed something strange in the sky. It was a beautiful wave of colours stretching over the horizon. The birds were so intrigued that they decided to fly up and meet it. As they flew closer, they could see that the beautiful wave was made from thousands of tiny butterflies. The butterflies were moving around in circles, uniting together like a big family. "Why don't we join them?" the birds said. So, the birds and the butterflies united together for the day and flew across the sky, twisting and turning as one big, happy family. The birds and the butterflies flew so high, it seemed as if they were touching the clouds. From the ground below, it looked like a giant, colourful wave flowing in the sky. When the night came, everyone said goodbye and waved to each other. The birds and the butterflies flew off in different directions, but they never forgot the special, tall tree in the woods that had united them together.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Tom saw a big box. The box was under a tree. Tom went to the box and looked inside. In the box, Tom found a little bird. The bird was sad. Tom wanted to help the bird. He tried to tell the bird a funny story to make it happy. The bird listened to Tom, and then it started to smile. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, the bird was even bigger than Tom! The bird was not a little bird; it was a big, friendly giant bird! Tom and the giant bird became best friends and played together in the park every day.
