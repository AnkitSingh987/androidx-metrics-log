Title: Mia the Girl and the Food

One day, a girl named Mia went to a food place with her mom. Mia saw a menu with many food things. She saw an original food that she never had before. She wanted to try it. Mia's mom said it was a good idea to try new things. In the food place, there was a man who needed help. He had to raise a big box. Mia saw the man and wanted to help. Mia's mom said helping others is good. So, Mia went to the man and helped him raise the box. The man said thank you to Mia. After helping the man, Mia and her mom ate the original food. Mia liked it a lot. They were happy they tried something new and helped someone. The day was good. The lesson is to always try new things and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted a telephone to play with. Her mum said she wasn't old enough yet. She asked every day but her mum always said no. One day, the little girl asked her mum if she could plant some seeds in the garden. Her mum agreed and gave her some delicious tomatoes to plant. Every day, the little girl took very good care of the tomatoes by watering them and giving them lots of sunshine. Her mum was very impressed with how well she was taking care of them! Finally, the day came when the tomatoes were ripe and ready to eat. The little girl was so excited to show her mum the delicious tomatoes, so she ran to get her mum. But when she returned, she could not find her mum. She found a telephone instead. The little girl was so surprised and happy, she had finally gotten the telephone she wanted. But then, she realized that her mum had given her exactly what she wanted - a telephone - by teaching her to care for and grow something. Moral of the story: Taking good care of things can include receiving something good in return.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce cat. The cat liked to pass by a big tree every day. One day, the cat saw a sock on the ground near the tree. The cat was very curious about the sock. The fierce cat played with the sock. The cat tossed the sock up and down. Suddenly, the sock started to move on its own! The cat was very surprised. It did not know what was happening. The sock stood up and started to dance. The fierce cat was not scared anymore. It started to dance with the sock. They danced and danced, and they became good friends. The cat and the sock passed by the big tree every day, and they danced together.

Mummy and Daddy had a special surprise for the little girl. They unpacked a big box and out came a little puppy. The little girl was so happy she clapped her hands and jumped up and down. Mummy and Daddy said they had another surprise. They went to the kitchen and came back with a big bag of beef. The little girl laughed and ran to get a bowl to put the beef in. The puppy loved the beef so much he ate it all up. The little girl was so happy that she gave the puppy a big hug. The puppy licked her face and they spent the whole day playing together. Mummy and Daddy were very happy too and knew that the little girl and the puppy would be best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely doctor named Dan. He worked in a big hospital. One day, a little girl named Lily came to see him. She had a hurt arm. "Please help me, Doctor Dan," said Lily. "My arm hurts a lot." Doctor Dan knew just what to do. He needed to take an x-ray of Lily's arm to see what was wrong. Doctor Dan asked his friend, Nurse Ann, to bring the x-ray machine. Nurse Ann brought it quickly. They took a picture of Lily's arm with the x-ray. Doctor Dan looked at the picture and saw that Lily's arm was not broken. She just needed a bandage. Doctor Dan put a bandage on Lily's arm. "All better now," he said. Lily smiled and thanked Doctor Dan. She went home feeling happy. Doctor Dan was not lonely anymore because he had helped Lily.
