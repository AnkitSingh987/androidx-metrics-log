Title: The Large Bear and the Rabbit

Once upon a time there was a large bear who was about to go on a trip. He was so excited! As he was packing his bags, he was trying to be careful not to break anything. He was packing his suitcase when he suddenly heard a big CRASH! He stopped what he was doing and looked around. He saw his teddy bear that he had left on the floor and it was all broken. The bear was very sad and he started to cry. He had been so careful but he had still broken his teddy bear. He didn't want to go on a trip anymore, because he was worried he would break something else. But then a kind voice said, "You don't need to worry, Bear. I'm sure you won't break anything else." Bear looked up and it was his friend Rabbit. Rabbit was very kind and she explained that Bear should be careful with things, but it's okay to make mistakes sometimes. Bear was encouraged by Rabbit's words and soon he was off on his trip, feeling much happier. He was careful with all his things, but he enjoyed himself just the same. In the end, his trip was a success!

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little beetle. This beetle was very selfish. He liked to eat all the leaves and did not share with his friends. One day, his friend, the caterpillar, said, "Why don't you share the leaves with me?" The beetle just laughed and said, "No, I will not share. I guess you have to find your own leaves." The caterpillar was very sad and hungry. He went to find some leaves on his own. While he was looking, he found a big, green leaf. He decided to share it with his other friends, the ants and the ladybug. When the selfish beetle saw them all eating together, he felt lonely. He went to the caterpillar and said, "I am sorry for not sharing. Can I eat with you?" The caterpillar smiled and said, "Yes, you can eat with us. We all need to share." From that day on, the beetle learned to share with his friends. They all ate together and lived happily in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a big tank. It was a fine tank, with many fish inside. The fish were happy and liked to swim all day. One day, a little fish asked the big fish if he could go outside the tank. The big fish said, "I will allow you to go outside, but be careful." The little fish was very excited. The little fish went outside the tank and saw many new things. He saw a cat, a dog, and a bird. The little fish had a lot of fun. Then, he went back to the tank and told the other fish about his adventure. They all wanted to go outside too, but the big fish said, "Only one at a time."

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a tall princess named Sally. She had a pretty dress and a shiny crown. Sally lived with her mom, the queen, and her dad, the king. One day, Sally met a small cat. The cat was lost and hungry. "Please, princess, can you help me find my home?" asked the cat. Sally wanted to help, so she said, "Yes, I will help you." Sally and the cat walked and walked. They looked everywhere for the cat's home. Finally, they found it! The cat was very happy. "Thank you, tall princess," said the cat. Sally smiled and went back to her castle. She was happy to help her new friend.

Once there was a little girl who was very sad. She was in her armchair, feeling dead inside. Suddenly, someone came in the room. It was her mommy! Mommy was carrying something special in her arms. When the little girl saw what it was, her eyes lit up. It was a furry, soft, cuddly doggy. The little girl was so happy to see her new doggy. She hugged it really tight. Mommy sat in the armchair next to the little girl and comforted her. She held the little girl in her arms and rocked her back and forth until all her sadness felt a little bit better. The little girl and her mommy spent the rest of the night together in the armchair, feeling comforted by each other's love.
