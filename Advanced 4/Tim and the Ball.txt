Title: Tim and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball. He gave it a good kick. The ball went very far. Tim was happy. Tim ran to get the ball. But when he got there, the ball was gone. Tim did not know where it went. He looked around and saw a big dog. The dog had the ball in its mouth. Tim was surprised. Tim asked the dog, "Can I have the ball back?" The dog did not say anything. It just gave a shrug. Then, the dog dropped the ball and ran away. Tim got a start. He did not know dogs could shrug. Tim laughed and played with the ball again.

Once there was an attractive dolphin who lived in the sea. Every morning she would swim out to the surface and whistle as she swam. Her beautiful music was so melodic, it reached the ears of a small boy who was walking on the beach. The boy was transfixed and he wondered if he could ever meet the dolphin. He stood on the beach eagerly and called out "Hello! Hello!" But there was no response. He looked out onto the sea and shouted louder. Suddenly, he saw a beautiful dolphin jump out of the sea and whistle back at him! He was so happy! The boy returned to the beach every morning and whistled out to the sea, in hopes of seeing the dolphin again. With every new morning, his hope grew and his whistle got louder. One morning, the boy's hope was rewarded when the attractive dolphin appeared! She swam closer to the beach and then out of the water, she jumped so high that the boy couldn't believe his eyes! The boy and the dolphin became good friends. They played together every day, and the boy would listen to her beautiful whistle for hours until the sun set. He had come to the beach each morning hoping to meet a dolphin, and now he had made a wonderful friend!

Jenny loved her doll, Lily. She played with her every day and took good care of her. She brushed her hair, changed her clothes, and gave her hugs. Jenny's mom said Lily was a dependable friend for Jenny. One day, Jenny wanted to play with Lily in the closet. She liked the closet because it was dark and cozy. She brought a flashlight and some books and snacks. She told her mom she was going to the closet and her mom said okay, but be careful. Jenny and Lily had fun in the closet. They read stories, ate cookies, and sang songs. Jenny forgot about the time and did not hear her mom calling her for dinner. She also did not notice that some of the cookies had fallen on Lily's dress and made it dirty. When Jenny finally came out of the closet, her mom was angry. She said Jenny had spoiled her dinner and her doll. She said Jenny had to wash Lily's dress and apologize. Jenny felt bad and said she was sorry. She hugged her mom and Lily and promised to be more careful next time. Jenny's mom said she loved her and Lily, but she had to learn to listen and be responsible. Jenny nodded and said she understood. She hoped Lily was not mad at her for spoiling her dress. She kissed her doll and said she was still her dependable friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a careful lady named Sue. Sue loved to give massages to her friends. She had soft hands and always made her friends feel good. One day, Sue went for a walk and saw a little bird. The bird looked sad and tired. Sue wanted to help the bird feel better. She thought, "Maybe the bird needs a massage." Sue picked up the bird very carefully. She gave the bird a gentle massage. The bird started to smile and feel better. The bird was so happy that it began to sing a beautiful song. Sue's friends heard the song and came to see what was happening. They saw Sue and the happy bird. They all smiled and clapped for Sue. From that day on, Sue and the bird became the best of friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
