Title: The Lovely Field and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a lovely field. In the field, there were many pretty flowers. One day, a big dog named Max came to the field. Max saw a little cat named Lucy. Lucy was sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I can't find my way home." Max wanted to help. They looked for Lucy's home together. They had to cross a big road. Max told Lucy, "We must be careful." Max and Lucy showed each other the way. They found Lucy's home. Lucy was happy. Max and Lucy became good friends. The moral of the story is: when you help others, you make friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl and her pet dog. Every day, the girl and her dog would go for a walk together around the park. Today was extra special, because the girl had a new dress. She laughed and ran all around the park, showing off her new dress to her dog. The dog laughed too and chased after her, wagging its tail. They played a game of tag, running all around the park, with the sun shining brightly on their backs. The girl's little neck was getting tired from all the running and laughing, so she put her hands on her knees, trying to catch her breath. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise coming from the nearby forest. They both looked around trying to figure out what it was. They saw something moving in between the trees and the girl became very scared. She started trembling and hugged her dog's neck, trying to calm down. The thing in the forest moved closer, and the girl and her dog heard someone laughing. The girl stepped forward and noticed that it was a new monkey! She laughed and the monkey laughed along, and the girl and her pet dog ran off together, holding hands.

One day, Mark and his mommy went to an airport. Mark was very curious and wanted to understand what an airport was. When they arrived, lots of people were busy running around catching their flights and hauling their suitcases. Mark was mesmerized. His mommy smiled and said: "It's a big airport, Mark. Everyone here is busy getting ready for their trip. You see, an airport is a place where people come and go." "But why are the people rushing around mommy?" asked Mark. His mommy could tell he was curious and said: "Well, some people are very lazy and like to leave things to the last minute and that's why they are in a rush. Some people are just punctual and want to make sure they don't miss their flights." Mark nodded as he understood what was going on and his mommy was proud that he grasped it so quickly. With that, Mark went on to explore the airport and soon, he was able to recognize the travelers who were punctual versus those who were lazy.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tom. Tom liked to mix colors in a circle to make new colors. One day, he went to the park to play and brought his colors with him. While at the park, Tom found a big circle on the ground. He thought it would be fun to mix his colors inside the circle. Tom mixed red, blue, and yellow together. Suddenly, the circle started to glow! The glowing circle lifted Tom up into the sky. Tom was scared at first, but then he saw that he was flying with birds! The birds were so happy that Tom could fly with them. After a while, the circle brought Tom back to the park. From that day on, Tom knew that mixing colors could make magic happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little lonely clock. The clock lived in a small room all by itself. It wanted to make friends, but it did not know how. One day, a little girl named Mia walked into the room. She saw the lonely clock and said, "Hi, clock! Do you want to be my friend?" The clock was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend!" Mia and the clock played all day. They had so much fun together. But then, it was time for Mia to go home. The clock was sad and did not want Mia to leave. It wanted to go with her. So, the clock tried to stir and move, but it could not. Mia saw the clock trying to move and said, "Don't worry, clock. I will come back tomorrow and play with you again." The clock was happy to hear this. The next day, Mia came back, and they played together again. The lonely clock was not lonely anymore because it had a friend in Mia.
