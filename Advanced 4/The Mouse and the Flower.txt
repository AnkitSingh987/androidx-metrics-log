Title: The Mouse and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. The mouse lived in a big field with lots of flowers. One day, the mouse saw a flower that was about to bloom. The mouse was very happy and excited to see the flower bloom. But there was a big, terrible cat who lived near the field. The cat saw the mouse and wanted to catch it. The mouse was scared and tried to hide behind the flower that was about to bloom. The flower bloomed and it was very pretty. But the terrible cat found the mouse hiding behind the flower. The cat caught the little mouse and the mouse was very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Poppy. She loved to play in her room and keep everything organized. One day, she found a small, lost kitten outside her house. Poppy wanted to protect the kitten and make it her friend. Poppy said to the kitten, "I will protect you, and we will be best friends." The kitten was very happy and followed Poppy into her room. They played all day, but then something unexpected happened. The kitten knocked over Poppy's toys and made a big mess. Poppy was upset, but she remembered that friends help each other. She said, "It's okay, kitten. We can clean up together." They worked together and made the room organized again. Poppy learned that being a good friend means helping and protecting each other, even when things don't go as planned.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to swing on the big tree near her house. Every day, she would run to the tree and swing high in the sky. One day, while Mia was swinging, a big wind came and pushed her off the swing. She fell down and felt very embarrassed. Mia's face turned red, and she quickly got up and looked around to see if anyone saw her fall. As Mia started to walk home, she saw a big, scary monster coming towards the village. She wanted to warn everyone, but she was still embarrassed about falling. Instead of telling the people, Mia hid behind a bush. The monster came into the village and scared everyone away. The village was never the same again, and Mia wished she had been brave enough to tell everyone about the monster.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to draw with ink. He had a big ink bottle and a small brush. He would make pretty pictures on paper. Tim had a lot of fun drawing. One day, Tim was very excited and he accidentally spilled the ink on the floor. He was frightened because he knew his mom would be mad. Tim didn't want to be punished. So, he tried to clean the ink with a rag, but it just made a bigger mess. Tim's mom saw the ink on the floor and asked Tim what happened. Tim told her the truth. His mom was not happy but she did not punish him. She told Tim to be more careful next time. Tim and his mom cleaned the ink together. After that, Tim was very careful when he drew with ink.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to his room. It was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and sad. Tim wanted to play with his toys. He saw a big box. The box had many toys in it. He wanted to make the toys fit in a small file. Tim tried to put the toys in the file. He pushed and pushed. The toys did not fit. Tim felt sad. He asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you help me?" Tim said. "The toys won't fit in the file." Mom smiled and said, "Let's try something else." She took out a bigger file. Tim put the toys in the new file. They fit! Tim was happy. The gloomy day became a fun day. Tim and his mom played with the toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl. She wanted to do something very special and so she decided to take an exam. She asked her mom for help, but her mom said we don't have a lot of money. So the girl decided to find a way to do the exam without help. The next day, the girl went to the exam hall. But she was scared. It was a big room and she was all alone. She started to cry. Then a kind man walked in. He said, "Don't worry. I will help you." The girl was very happy and she decided to take the exam with the man's help. But the man only wanted to take her money. When the exam was over, he said, "Now give me the money you promised." The girl had no money and she started to cry. The man was very angry and he asked her to leave the exam hall. The girl sadly went home. She learnt the lesson that you should not decide to do something without thinking about it carefully.
