Title: The Beautiful Pastel and the Drawing

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful pastel. It was sad and miserable. It could not color like the other pastels. It wanted to be happy and make nice drawings. One day, a little girl named Lucy found the pastel. She said, "Why are you so sad, pastel?" The pastel told her, "I can't color like the others. No one wants to use me." Lucy smiled and said, "I value you, pastel. Let's make a pretty picture together." They made a beautiful drawing with happy colors. The pastel was not miserable anymore. It was happy to be with Lucy, and they made many more pretty pictures together.

One day, a boy named Tim found a heavy box. He tried to open it, but it was too heavy. Tim asked his friend, Sam, for help. "Sam, can you help me open this heavy box?" Tim said. Sam tried to help Tim, but the box was too heavy for both of them. They were sad. Then, they saw a card on the box. The card said, "To break the box, jump on it." They were excited! Tim and Sam jumped on the box together. The box broke open, and out came a big, friendly dog! They did not expect a dog to be in the box. The dog was happy to see them, and they were happy too. They played with the dog all day and became best friends.

Tom likes to number his toys. He has many toys, but his favorite is his motorcycle. He got it for his birthday from his dad. It is red and shiny and makes a loud noise. Tom likes to play with his motorcycle in the park. He rides it on the grass and the dirt and the sand. He pretends he is a racer and goes very fast. He makes vroom-vroom sounds with his mouth. One day, Tom meets a new friend in the park. His name is Sam and he also has a motorcycle. It is blue and shiny and makes a loud noise. Sam says he likes to number his toys too. He shows Tom his numbers. He has one, two, three, four, five motorcycles. Tom is amazed. He only has one motorcycle. He thinks Sam is very lucky and popular. He wants to be his friend. He asks Sam if they can play together. Sam says yes and smiles. They ride their motorcycles side by side and make vroom-vroom sounds. They have fun and share their numbers. Tom is happy. He has a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a navy. It sailed on the big blue sea. Every day was the same, nothing changed, so it was sometimes boring. One day, something happened. The navy changed. It turned around and the crew were all very excited. This was the start of a new adventure. The navy sailed to a new land. There were lots of colourful things to see, animals to watch and new people to meet. It was much better than being boring. The navy explored the new land, it saw exciting sights and new places. Then one day, it turned again and sailed back to the home it knew. The crew were happy to be back, but they remembered their adventure with a smile. It had been so much more fun than just being boring.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly high in the sky. He also liked to read. Tim had a big book called a novel. It was his favorite thing to look at. One day, Tim was very tired. He wanted to read his novel, but his eyes felt heavy. Tim thought, "I will take my novel and go to a quiet place to read." So, he took his novel and flew up, up, up into the sky. Tim found a nice, soft cloud to sit on. He opened his novel and began to read. As he read, he felt his eyes getting sleepier and sleepier. Tim yawned and closed his book. He snuggled down into the cloud and took a long nap. When Tim woke up, he felt happy and ready to fly again.
