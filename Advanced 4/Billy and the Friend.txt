Title: Billy and the Friend

Billy and his friend were having lots of fun playing together. They had been running around in the park all day. Eventually, the sky grew dark and they had to go home. Billy's friend ran a little faster to get home before it started to rain. When they got home, Billy noticed that his friend had left something heavy on the ground. He picked it up and ran to catch up with his friend. When they got to his friend's house, he thanked Billy and said, “You’ve been such a good friend today – thank you for carrying my heavy backpack home!”

Once upon a time, there was a human named Tom. Tom lived in a small house near a big hot sun. The sun was so hot that it made everything around it hot too. One day, Tom saw a little bird near his house. The bird was very thirsty because of the hot sun. Tom said, "Little bird, I will help you. I will prevent you from being thirsty because of the hot sun." He gave the bird some water to drink. After the bird drank the water, it was happy. It said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good human. You prevented me from being thirsty with the hot sun." And from that day on, Tom and the little bird became good friends. They played together and helped each other whenever they could.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. They had a big yard with a tree house that their dad made for them. The tree house was their luxury. It had a yellow rug, a yellow lamp, and yellow pillows. They also had books, toys, and snacks in the tree house. One day, they decided to climb to the tree house and have a picnic. They packed some sandwiches, apples, and cookies in a basket. They put on their hats and shoes and went to the tree house. They climbed the ladder carefully and got inside. "Wow, it's so cozy and bright in here!" Anna said, looking around. "I love our tree house. It's the best place ever!" Ben said, putting the basket on the floor. They ate their sandwiches and apples and talked about their favorite things. They laughed and sang and had fun. Then they saw a squirrel on the window. "Hello, Mr. Squirrel!" Anna said, waving. "Can we share some cookies with you?" Ben asked, holding out a cookie. The squirrel looked at them and tilted his head. He came closer and took the cookie from Ben's hand. He nibbled it and made a happy sound. "Aw, he likes it!" Anna said, smiling. "Maybe he wants to be our friend!" Ben said, giving him another cookie. They fed the squirrel some more cookies and watched him run around the tree house. They felt happy and warm. They loved their luxury.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man who had a big house. He had many toys and nice things. One day, he found a broken toy with a missing arm. He felt sad for the toy and wanted to help it. The rich man thought about how to restore the toy. He looked for a new arm to put on the toy. He found one that was just right. He put the new arm on the toy and the toy was happy. Now the toy was fixed and the rich man was very happy. They played together every day. The toy was glad to have a new arm and a friend. The rich man and the toy lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a family who liked to decorate for Christmas. They had lots of fancy ornaments to include on their tree. One ornament was different to all the others because it was very simple and plain. One day, the family's little three year old girl saw the special ornament. She asked her parents, "What is that?" Her parents explained that it was a special Christmas ornament used to decorate the tree. The little girl said, "I want to put it on the tree. Can I?" Her parents said, "Of course you can! It would be nice to have a special ornament from you on the tree." So the little girl put the ornament on the tree with all the other fancy ornaments. The family smiled. They thought it was a perfect addition to their Christmas tree decorations. They were very happy.
