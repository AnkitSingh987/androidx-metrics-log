Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

One day, a little dog named Spot was very eager to go to a meeting with his friends. He put on his hat and ran outside to the park. At the park, he saw his friends, a cat named Fluffy and a bunny named Hoppy. Spot said, "Hi, Fluffy and Hoppy! Are you ready for our meeting?" Fluffy and Hoppy nodded their heads. They started to talk about their favorite toys and games, but then they heard a loud noise. It was a big bird named Chirpy, who was complaining. Chirpy said, "I want to play too, but nobody asked me to come to the meeting!" Spot, Fluffy, and Hoppy looked at each other. They didn't mean to leave Chirpy out, but they didn't know he wanted to come. Spot said, "I'm sorry, Chirpy. We didn't know you wanted to join. You can play with us!" Chirpy was happy and stopped complaining. They all played together, and Chirpy showed them a new game that they had never played before. It was so much fun, and they all became best friends. From that day on, they always made sure to invite everyone to their meetings.

One day, George the Giraffe was walking down the street. He looked up and saw Jack the Elephant with a big horn on his head. "Hi Jack! What are you doing?" asked George. "I'm going to spend my morning playing the horn!" replied Jack. "That sounds fun. Can I join you?" asked George. "Of course!" said Jack. So George and Jack spent the morning playing the horn. They were both quite successful in playing their melodies. When the morning was finished, George smiled. "That was so much fun!" George said happily. "Yes it was," agreed Jack. "Let's do this again soon!" And so it was decided. The two friends were going to spend more mornings having successful horn-playing sessions.

Once upon a time, in a happy little forest, there was a big tree. The big tree had many friends. The friends were animals, like a squirrel, a bird, and a rabbit. They all lived together in the happy little forest. One day, a thick fog came to the forest. The animals could not see each other. They were scared. The big tree told them, "Do not be scared. The fog will go away soon." The animals felt better. They knew the big tree was right. After a while, the fog went away. The animals were happy again. But, they saw that the big tree was not happy. The big tree was starting to rot. The animals knew they had to help their friend. They worked together to make the big tree better. In the end, the big tree was happy and healthy again. The animals were happy too. They all lived happily ever after in the happy little forest.

Lily was a little girl who loved to play outside. She liked to run, jump, and swing on the swings. But her favorite thing to do was to pretend to fly. She would spread her arms and run around the yard, making airplane noises. One day, she saw a hose lying on the grass. She had an idea. She picked up the hose and turned on the water. She pointed the hose at the sky and watched the water spray out. She thought it looked like a jet stream. She held the hose under her arm and ran around, pretending to fly with the water. But she did not see her mom coming out of the house. Her mom was carrying a basket of clothes. She wanted to hang them on the line to dry. She did not see Lily and the hose either. She walked right into the water spray. She got wet and dropped the basket. The clothes fell on the ground. Lily stopped running and saw what she had done. She felt very embarrassed. She had made a big mess and soaked her mom. She dropped the hose and ran to her mom. She said she was sorry and hugged her mom. Her mom was not angry, but she was not happy either. She told Lily that playing with the hose was not a good idea. She said that water was for drinking, washing, and watering plants, not for spraying people and clothes. She said that Lily should be more careful and respectful with water and other things. Lily understood. She helped her mom pick up the clothes and hang them on the line. She apologized again and promised to not play with the hose anymore. She learned that flying was fun, but not when it hurt or bothered others. She decided to fly with her imagination instead.
