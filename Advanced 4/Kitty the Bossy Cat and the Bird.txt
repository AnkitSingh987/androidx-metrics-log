Title: Kitty the Bossy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Kitty saw a big tree with many branches. She wanted to choose the best branch to sit on. Kitty looked at the tree and saw a little bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. Kitty told the bird to stop singing. The bird was sad but stopped singing. Kitty felt happy because she was bossy. Kitty chose a branch to sit on, but it was not a good choice. The branch broke, and Kitty fell down. She was hurt and cried. The other animals did not help her because she was bossy. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and not be bossy.

Once there was a small, humble mouse who lived in a big forest. He ran through the trees, darting among the leaves, searching for something special. The mouse eventually found a big box with a stamp on the side. He tried pushing it but it was too heavy, so he decided to pull it. He pulled and pulled, and though he was small, he was strong, and eventually the box started to move. When he opened it, the mouse was so excited! Inside were lots of colourful stamps and inks! He quickly grabbed them and ran back to his cozy nest. For days and days, the mouse used the stamps to make all kinds of pictures. He made pictures of trees and flowers and even pictures of himself. He loved them so much. Finally, when all the stamps were gone, the mouse was content. He had learned with his humble strength he could do anything, even move a big box full of wonderful stamps.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. He had a good time with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim put on his warm coat and went out to play. He saw a small bird on the ground. The bird looked cold and sad. Tim recognized the bird as a friend he had seen before. Tim wanted to help the bird feel good. He picked up the cold bird and took it inside his house. He made a warm bed for the bird and gave it food to eat. The bird felt good and happy again. Tim and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, even when it was cold outside.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big hotel. The hotel was very nice and many people liked to stay there. One day, a fearful little dog came to the hotel. He was lost and needed help. The hotel owner saw the little dog and felt sad. He wanted to help the dog. The owner offered the dog some food and a warm place to sleep. The little dog was happy and wagged his tail. After a few days, the dog's family came to the hotel. They were looking for their lost dog. They saw the dog and were very happy. The dog was not fearful anymore. They thanked the hotel owner and went back home. Everyone was happy and the little dog was safe with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to make things with her hands. One day, Lily wanted to make a cushion for her mom. She picked a soft, blue fabric to make the cushion. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me make the cushion?" Her mom said, "Of course, Lily! Let's make it together." They worked together to make the cushion. Lily was very happy. When they finished, Lily showed the cushion to her dad. "Look, Dad! Mom and I made a cushion!" Her dad smiled and said, "It's very clear that you both worked hard on this cushion. It's beautiful!" Lily felt proud and knew that her mom would love the cushion they made together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim found a big box. He was very excited. He wanted to peek inside the box. So, he opened the box and looked inside. There, he found a pretty candle. It was red and smelled nice. Tim wanted to show his mom the candle. But, he did something stupid. He ran too fast and tripped. The candle fell and broke. Tim was sad. He didn't want to break the candle. Tim's mom saw him and came to help. She told Tim that it was okay. Everyone makes mistakes. She gave Tim a hug and a new candle. Tim was happy again. He promised to be more careful next time.
