Title: Mia the Girl and the Peach

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat peaches. One day, she saw a big, juicy peach on the table. She was so happy and said, "I want to have this peach!" Mia's mom heard her and said, "This is not a normal peach, Mia. This is a modern peach. It can do something special." Mia was curious and asked, "What can it do?" Her mom smiled and said, "You will see." Mia took a bite of the peach. Suddenly, the peach started to sing! Mia was so surprised and laughed. She shared the singing peach with her friends, and they all had fun listening to the peach's songs. In the end, Mia and her friends had a great day with the special, modern peach.

There was a wealthy family who had a big house. Behind the house was a big backyard. One of the children in the family liked to climb in the backyard. The child had lots of toys which he took outside to play. But he always found his favourite game was to climb. He would climb trees, towers and even the fence. The parents would often watch him as he climbed higher and higher. The child loved feeling the breeze and looking out over the backyard from way up high. One day the parents decided to get the child some special climbing equipment. A rope, a ladder and special gloves and shoes. From then on he was able to climb even higher than before. He thrilled at feeling the cool breeze, looking down from way up above the backyard.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sarah. She was very kind and liked playing with everyone. One day Sarah went to the park to play and she noticed a judge. He was wearing a big, fancy hat and a long black robe. All of the children wanted to know what the judge would do. Sarah felt brave and asked him, “What are you doing?”. The judge smiled and said, “I am here to judge interesting things. Would you like to join me?” Sarah thought the judge's game sounded like fun and said, “Yes!” The judge gave her a very strange task. He asked her to resist the urge to dance. Sarah was not sure why the judge was asking her to do this but she tried really hard to resist. The judge laughed and said, “Very good Sarah! I am very pleased with you.” Sarah was very happy to have made the judge smile and she asked if she could join him again next time. The judge said that she could. Sarah said goodbye to the judge and ran home to tell her friends about her interesting experience.

Once upon a time, there was a bird who loved to soar high in the sky. He would fly up and down, feeling so happy and free. The bird had a friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to have tea with her toys in her backyard. One sunny day, the bird saw Lily having her tea party. She had a small table with tiny cups and plates. The bird wanted to join her, but he was too high in the sky. He decided to fly down and say hello. As the bird landed near Lily's tea party, she smiled and said, "Hello, bird! Would you like some tea?" The bird nodded and they had a fun tea party together. After they finished, the bird soared high again, feeling happy to have a friend like Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Ben. Ben loved to eat. He liked to eat everything he could find. One day, Ben went for a walk in the woods. He was very hungry and wanted to find something to eat. Ben came to a house. He saw laundry hanging outside. The laundry was the only thing available for him to eat. So, Ben ate the laundry. He ate the shirts, the pants, and the socks. He ate everything he could find. After Ben ate the laundry, he felt full and happy. He went back into the woods to take a nap. The people in the house were sad because their laundry was gone. But they learned to not leave their laundry outside when Ben the bear was around.
