Title: The Goose and the Fence

Once upon a time, there was a goose. He was very naughty and liked to misbehave. He would fly into farmers’ fields and eat the fruit from their trees. He would also make a mess, often leaving things very filthy. The farmers kept trying to chase the goose away, but he always came back. He seemed to think it was fun! The farmers were fed up with the goose and his bad behavior, so they decided to build a fence to keep him out. But the goose still managed to get in! One day, the farmers decided to try a different tactic. They bought a new goose to live in their fields. The new goose was well-behaved, and he helped keep the naughty goose away! From that day forward, the farmers' fields stayed nice, clean and tidy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She felt so sick and weary. Every morning she had to take lots of medicine. It tasted yucky but it made her feel better. One day Sarah decided to go and find out what the medicine was for. She was feeling nosy and wanted to explore. She asked her parents to tell her and they said it was to make her better. Sarah was pleased that the medicine helped her feel better and wanted to stay healthy. She promised to take it every single morning and thanked her parents for being so kind. The End

One day, a vendor was at his shop. He had many things to sell. He was very alert and always ready to help people. He liked to write lists of what he had in his shop. People liked him because he was kind and helpful. One day, a big wind came. It blew everything away from his shop. The vendor was sad. He did not know what to do. He looked at his list and saw that he had one thing left. It was a big, red ball. The vendor had an idea. He took the big, red ball and started to play with it. People saw him and came to join. They all played with the big, red ball and had fun. They forgot about the wind and the lost things. The vendor was not sad anymore. He was happy because he made people smile. And from that day, he became the best ball seller in town.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a fierce lion. The lion was big and strong. He had a blue mane that was very pretty. All the animals in the forest were scared of the lion because he was so fierce. One day, a little bird was lost in the forest. The bird was very sad and scared. The fierce lion with the blue mane saw the bird and asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird said, "I am lost and I can't find my way home." The lion felt bad for the bird and decided to help. He said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you find your home." The lion and the bird walked through the forest together. They looked everywhere for the bird's home. Finally, they found it! The bird was so happy. The bird said, "Thank you, big fierce lion with the blue mane. You are very kind." The lion felt good about helping the bird. From that day on, he was not so fierce anymore. All the animals in the forest became friends with the lion, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to study her toys. Every day, Lily would sit on the floor and look at her toys. She had a big box with many toys inside. But her favorite toy was a spinning top. One day, Lily saw that the top was not spinning well. It was not tight. She wanted to fix it so she could play with it again. She looked at the top very closely and saw a small screw. Lily thought that if she could make the screw tight, the top would spin better. Lily went to her dad for help. Her dad had a small tool that could make the screw tight. He showed Lily how to use the tool and they fixed the top together. After that, the top spun better than ever before. Lily was very happy that she had studied her top and found a way to fix it.
