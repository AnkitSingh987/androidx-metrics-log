Title: John and the Fork

Once upon a time there were two brothers. John was very selfish because he always wanted to pay for things himself. He never wanted to share with his brother. One day, they went to the store to buy a fork. As they stood in the store, John saw a shiny yellow fork that he wanted to buy. He grabbed it quickly and only wanted to pay for it himself. John's brother was sad because he wanted to share. But John wouldn't even let him have a look at the fork. John paid and they went home. But when they got home, they saw that the shiny yellow fork was broken. John had been very selfish, and his selfishness ruined the fork. The brothers were sad and never wanted to be selfish again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. One sunny day, the bunny went out to play in the open field. The grass was green and soft, perfect for hopping around. As the bunny hopped, he saw an infant sitting on the grass. The infant was laughing and clapping his hands. The bunny hopped closer to the infant, curious about the little human. The infant saw the bunny and smiled. They played together in the open field, hopping and laughing. The sun shone down on them, and it was a very happy day.

Once there was a very rude cat. The cat never said please or thank you. Everyone in the neighbourhood was unhappy with the way the cat acted. One day, the cat heard about a nice little girl in the neighbourhood. The girl's name was Daisy. Daisy was a very special three year old girl. On that day, the cat wanted to try something new. The cat wanted to show Daisy how nice he could be. So, the cat decided to perform a very special dance for Daisy. The cat worked hard to practice his dance. He hopped, skipped and jumped all around the neighbourhood to practice. The day of the performance arrived. Daisy watched with excitement as the cat performed his dance. He was a very bad dancer, but Daisy thought it was wonderful! When the dance was over, Daisy thanked the cat for the performance. She thought the cat was not rude at all. Everyone was so happy that the cat had tried to be nice.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shoe in his room. He did not know where the other shoe was. Tim began to worry. He looked under his bed and in his toy box, but he could not find the other shoe. Tim's mom saw him looking sad and asked, "What is wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my other shoe and I am worried." His mom smiled and said, "Let's look for it together. Don't worry, we will find it." They looked around the room and Tim's mom found the other shoe behind the door. She gave it to Tim and said, "See? No need to worry. Now you have both shoes." Tim felt calm and happy. He thanked his mom and they both went to play outside.

Once upon a time there was a mommy who wanted to prepare a surprise for her little girl. She found a big eraser and thought it was the perfect present. So she took the eraser and decorated it with sparkles and ribbons. She added some string to it so her daughter could wear it. The little girl was so excited when she saw the eraser. It was the most beautiful one she had ever seen! She put it around her neck and went outside to show her friends. They were so amazed when they saw the eraser. It was so pretty and they thought it was really crazy! The little girl was so proud of her eraser that she never wanted to take it off. She wore it all the time and it brought her lots of smiles.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long, flexible toy. It looked like a snake. He showed it to his friend, Sam. They played with the toy all day. They bent it and made it wiggle. "Look, I can make it go fast!" said Tim. Sam laughed and tried to make it go faster. They had a lot of fun with the toy. But then, the toy got dirty. Tim and Sam wanted to make it clean again. They got a cloth and started to wipe the toy. They wiped and wiped until the toy was clean and shiny. "Wow, it looks like a missile now!" said Sam. They pretended the toy was a missile and played a new game. They took turns launching the missile into the air. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They loved their new flexible missile toy. They couldn't wait to play with it again tomorrow.
