Title: Tim Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went out to play in his backyard. He saw a big, green ball. Tim was very careful when he played with the ball. He did not want to touch anything else in the backyard. One day, as Tim played with the ball, it rolled into a bush. Tim went to the bush to get his ball back. He was careful not to touch the leaves, but then something unexpected happened. A little bird flew out of the bush! Tim was so surprised! He did not know that a bird was living in the bush. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for being careful with my home." Tim smiled and promised to be even more careful when playing in the backyard. From that day on, Tim and the little bird became best friends. They played together in the backyard every day.

Bob was a 3 year old who loved to paint. One day, his mum suggested he should go outside to paint his view. Bob was so excited that he quickly ran to the garden with his paints and painting equipment. When Bob arrived he was amazed by the incredible view in front of him. He saw the bright blue sky, towering trees, beautiful butterflies and much more. Bob put down his supplies and painted every colorful detail that he could see. He was so focused on his painting that he didn't even notice when his mum came to check on him. "What are you painting?" asked his mum. "I'm painting the incredible view!" exclaimed Bob with a big smile. His mum was really impressed with Bob's painting. She gave him a big hug and said, "It looks amazing Bob, you're a very talented painter!" Bob felt so proud of his painting and knew he would never forget this incredible view.

Once upon a time, there was a small house with a red roof. In the house lived a happy family. One day, it was very dark outside. The wind blew hard, and the rain fell down. The family stayed inside to keep warm and dry. The little girl, named Amy, saw that her teddy bear had a rip. She was sad. Her mom saw her and said, "Don't worry, I can heal your teddy bear." Amy smiled and watched her mom fix the teddy bear. Suddenly, there was a loud noise from the roof. The family looked up and saw a hole. The rain was coming in! They were scared, but then they heard a small voice. It was a little bird who said, "I'm sorry, I made the hole. But I can help fix it." The family was surprised, but they let the bird help. The little bird quickly fixed the hole, and the family was happy again. They all became friends and lived happily ever after.

It was a lovely day outside and two children were running around. They stopped in front of a fence and one of they said, "Let's peek through the fence and see what's on the other side!" The other child excitedly agreed and they both looked through the fence. Suddenly, one of they said, "Wow! I see a beautiful garden with lots of lovely flowers!" The other child squinted and said, "It looks like there are some fruits too. Let's cut a hole in the fence and go in!" The first child thought for a moment and said, "Come on. Let's do it! We can have lots of fun in the garden!" The children both ran to the fence and started cutting a hole in it. After a few minutes, they had made a big enough hole to fit through. They both entered the garden and were amazed at how beautiful and lush it was. There were all sorts of lovely flowers, fruits and plants. The children ran around, exploring and playing until it was time to go back home. Before they left, they both smiled, knowing they had just experienced a truly lovely day!

One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a wallet on a bench. It was a delicate wallet with many colors. Tom picked it up and showed his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" he said. His mom looked at the wallet and said, "Tom, we should tell someone we found it. Maybe they are looking for it." Tom nodded and they went to find the person who lost the wallet. They saw a man looking sad. Tom walked up to him and asked, "Did you lose a wallet?" The man's face lit up and he said, "Yes, I did! Thank you for finding it!" Tom gave the wallet back to the man and everyone was happy.
