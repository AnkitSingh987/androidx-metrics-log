Title: Stella the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Stella. She was three years old, and wanted to play in the backyard. Her mom said yes, but only if she was careful. Stella ran outside with excitement. She picked a flower off the ground and started to match it with the other flowers around. Suddenly, she felt a shock on her fingers. She had touched an electric wire! "Mommy, I'm scared," she cried. Stella's mom rushed outside and hugged her tightly. Stella was tired and wanted to go inside to rest. The next day, Stella was sitting in the yard but she was not feeling well. In her hand, she still had the electric shock from the day before. Her mom called for help, but Stella didn't make it. Stella was gone.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a man and his wife. The man was very good at using tools to fix things. His wife was good at cooking and making their home a happy place. One day, the wife needed help to open a jar. She asked her husband, "Can you please use your strong hands to open this jar for me?" The man smiled and said, "Of course, my dear wife!" He used his hands to open the jar for his wife. She was very happy and thanked him. The wife used the open jar to cook a yummy meal for them both. They sat down and ate the good food together. After they finished eating, they both said, "What a good day!" They were happy to have each other and to help each other in their small house.

Once upon a time, there was a pale little boy named Tim. He had a big love for gum. Every day, he would eat gum and share it with his friends. One day, Tim found a big box of gum. He wanted to keep it all to himself. But then he thought of his friends. They loved gum too. So, Tim decided to unite with his friends and share the gum. As they all sat together, they enjoyed the gum and had a fun time. Tim felt happy that he shared the gum. The moral of the story is that sharing with others makes everyone happy.

Anna liked to help her mom in the garden. She liked to see the flowers and the bugs and the green things that grew. One day, her mom showed her a big white thing that looked like a flower, but was not a flower. It was a cauliflower. Her mom said they could pick it and eat it for dinner. Anna wanted to pick the cauliflower, but it was too high for her. She asked her mom to lift her up, but her mom said she had to finish weeding first. She told Anna to wait and be careful. Anna nodded, but she was curious. She wanted to see the cauliflower better. She saw a little stool near the fence. She dragged it to the cauliflower and climbed on it. She reached for the cauliflower, but it was still too far. She leaned over, but she lost her balance. She fell off the stool and landed on the ground. She started to cry. Her mom heard her cry and ran to her. She saw Anna on the ground and the stool by the cauliflower. She picked Anna up and hugged her. She asked her if she was hurt. Anna said no, but she was sorry. She said she wanted to pick the cauliflower. Her mom smiled and kissed her. She said it was okay, but she should not climb on the stool without her. She said she was proud of Anna for wanting to help, but she should follow her mom's rules. She said they could pick the cauliflower together and make a nice soup. Anna smiled and said yes. She said she loved her mom and the cauliflower. Her mom said she loved her too. They walked to the house, holding the cauliflower and each other.

Once upon a time there was a bald man called John. John loved to play the trumpet. Every day John would practice his trumpet in the park. The birds would fly and sing along with him. Everyone in the park was happy and enjoyed listening to the beautiful music. One day, John was eating an apple in the park. Suddenly, he saw a trumpet lying on the ground. It glowed in the sunlight. John was so excited and he picked up the trumpet to play it. Unfortunately, the trumpet was broken. As John was playing the trumpet, it made a loud, horrible sound. Everyone in the park stopped to look and was shocked at how loud and bad the trumpet sounded. John was embarrassed and he ran away. He never went back to the park to play the trumpet again.
