Title: Tim and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat sandwiches. One day, he saw an old lady sitting on a bench. She looked very hungry. Tim wanted to help her. Tim had a sandwich with him. He walked up to the old lady and said, "Here, have my sandwich." The old lady smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim." She took the sandwich and ate it happily. From that day on, Tim always shared his sandwiches with others. His friends and family saw this and started to share too. Everyone was happy and full. The moral of the story is: when you share with others, you make the world a better place.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big cabinet in her room. It was filled with toys and games. Lucy loved to play with them all day long. One day, Lucy's friend, Tom, came to her house. "Welcome, Tom!" Lucy said with a big smile. Tom looked at the cabinet and said, "Wow, you have so many toys!" They played together for hours and hours. After a while, Lucy's mom came into the room. She saw that the toys were all over the floor. "Oh no, the room is so messy!" mom said. "Lucy, you have so many toys, but you need to take care of them. They might get spoiled if you don't put them back in the cabinet." Lucy and Tom listened and put all the toys back. They promised to keep the toys safe and clean.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Jack and Mike. One day, they were walking in the park when they spotted an enormous garage. They were curious, so they decided to go in. Inside, there were all kinds of toys and games. Jack said, “Wow! This is amazing!”; Mike smiled and nodded in agreement. Suddenly, Jack spotted a strange box. It was pink and had a big question mark on it. Jack asked Mike, “What is this?” Mike replied, “I don't know. Let's open it.” They slowly opened the box and to their surprise they found a puppy inside! They couldn't believe it. Jack asked, “Where did it come from?”. Mike looked around and shrugged his shoulders. He said, “I don't know. I guess it must have come from the enormous garage.” Jack and Mike looked at each other and smiled. They named the puppy Lucky, and from that day on, they were always together - Lucky, Jack and Mike. They were the best of friends!

Once upon a time, there was a lovely girl named Lily. She had a big box of pasta. Lily liked to play with her pasta and make necklaces for her friends. One day, she wanted to make a very big necklace with her pasta. Lily started to string the pasta on a long string. But, she pulled the string too hard. The pasta began to break. Lily was sad because she broke her pasta. She did not want to waste her pasta. Lily's mom saw her sad face and came to help. She told Lily to be gentle with the pasta. Lily learned that being careful is important. She tried again and made a beautiful necklace without breaking the pasta. Lily was happy and shared her lovely pasta necklace with her friends. The moral of the story is to be gentle and careful with things.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob had a friend named Sally. Sally was a little girl with a big frown on her face. She was sad because her skin was very dry. One day, Bob saw a bottle of lotion. He thought that the lotion would make Sally's skin feel better. So, he picked up the bottle with his beak and flew to Sally's house. When Sally saw the lotion, she smiled. She put the lotion on her skin and it felt much better. Her frown went away and she was happy. Sally and Bob played all day and had lots of fun together.

One day, a shy little girl, called Rose, was walking around the park. Suddenly, she felt a hand on her shoulder, leading her away. It was an old man. He said he would give her some candy. Rose was unsure, and very scared. She had heard stories of strange old men before and wasn’t sure what to do. “Do not worry,” the man said kindly. “Follow me and I will show you something special.” Despite her shyness, Rose followed. She was taken to a secret garden filled with the most beautiful roses. Rose gasped at the sight. The old man smiled and explained that the flowers always made him feel happy when he was sad. He gave her a rose to keep. Rose smiled and thanked him. She hugged the rose tightly, happy to bring a bit of the garden back with her. From that day on, she was no longer as shy. She learned that sometimes it takes a bit of courage to find beautiful things, but it’s definitely worth it in the end.
