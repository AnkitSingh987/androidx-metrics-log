Title: Lucy the Girl and the Song

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the park. She saw a blue bird sitting on a tree. The bird was singing a pretty song. Lucy stopped to listen and smiled. "Hello, blue bird!" said Lucy. "I like your song. Can you teach me how to sing it?" The blue bird looked at Lucy and nodded. It sang the song again, and this time, Lucy tried to sing along. She listened carefully and tried to hear each note. After a few tries, Lucy could sing the pretty song too. Lucy thanked the blue bird and continued her walk. She sang the pretty song all the way home. When she got home, her mom and dad heard the song and clapped. They were so happy and proud of Lucy. From that day on, Lucy and the blue bird became good friends, and they sang pretty songs together in the park.

Once there was a boy named Jack. One day, he went out to meet his friends. He had messy hair and everyone else had very neat hair. He looked at his friends and felt a bit left out. But then something amazing happened. Suddenly, the sky lit up with a very light ray of sunshine! It made Jack's hair shine brighter than ever before. He felt so happy because his hair was now more beautiful than his friends'. Jack smiled and ran to join his friends. Everyone was so surprised and soon started complimenting him. They were so happy to meet him! After that, Jack went home feeling very proud. He knew his messy hair was now the lightest and most beautiful!

One day, Little Sally saw something white in the garden, something that made her very frightened. It was a big, white, fluffy cat. Sally tried to run away, but the cat was too fast for her. She was so scared and she started to cry. Just then, her big brother John came running to the rescue. He bravely grabbed the cat by its tail and held it away from Sally. "Don't worry, Sally, I won't let it hurt you," said John. He carried the cat away and set it free in the park. "Thank you for saving me, John," said Sally. "It's alright," said John as he hugged her. "That's what big brothers are for." Little Sally hugged her big brother and felt much safer now.

Once upon a time, there was a wild bird who loved to eat avocado. The bird lived in a big tree. Every day, the bird would fly to find avocados to eat. One day, the bird found a very big avocado on the ground. The bird was very happy and wanted to eat it. The bird tried to pick up the big avocado, but it was too heavy. So, the bird asked a little ant for help. "Please help me carry this avocado," said the bird. The little ant was very strong and wanted to help. They both carried the avocado together back to the big tree. When they got to the tree, the avocado started to shake. The bird and the ant were very surprised. The avocado cracked open, and a baby bird came out! The wild bird and the little ant were now friends and they took care of the baby bird together. They were a happy family.

One day, a big dog named Max was very cold. He started to shiver because it was so cold outside. Max saw a small house and went inside to get warm. In the house, there was a nice lady named Sue. Sue saw Max shivering and felt bad for him. "Max, you look cold," Sue said. "I have a warm pie in the oven. Would you like some?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, please!" Sue cut a big piece of pie for Max. The pie was hot and smelled good. Max thought it was the best pie he ever had. As Max ate the pie, something unexpected happened. Max started to feel very important. He felt like he could do anything. Max looked at Sue and said, "Thank you for the pie. I will help you with anything you need." Sue was surprised that Max could talk, but she was happy to have a new friend. From that day on, Max and Sue were the best of friends, and Max always helped Sue with important things.
