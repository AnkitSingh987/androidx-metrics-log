Title: Lucy the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a mysterious big box on a step. She felt curious and wanted to see what was inside. Lucy tried to resist opening the box. She said to herself, "I must not open it." But her curiosity was too strong. She took a deep breath and opened the box. Inside the box, she found a cute little cat. The cat looked at Lucy and said, "Thank you for letting me out!" Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They became best friends and played together all day.

Lily liked to sing. She had a microphone that her dad gave her. She sang every day in her room. She sang loud and happy. One day, Lily wanted to sing for her friends. She asked her mom if she could spend some time with them. Her mom said yes, but she had to be back before dark. Lily was excited. She took her microphone and ran to the park. She saw her friends playing on the swings. She called them and said, "Hey, do you want to hear me sing? I have a microphone!" Her friends came over and said yes. Lily turned on her microphone and started to sing. But something was wrong. Her microphone made a loud noise. It hurt her ears and her friends' ears. They covered their ears and said, "Stop, stop, Lily, it's too loud!" Lily tried to stop, but she couldn't. The microphone was broken. It kept making noise. Lily felt scared and sad. She dropped the microphone and ran away. She cried all the way home. She wished she never sang for her friends. She wished she never had a microphone. She wished her dad was there to hug her. But he wasn't. He was far away, working. Lily felt alone and sorry. She had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Speedy. Speedy loved to speed around the town. He had a best friend named Nappy, who was a napkin. Nappy was a very special napkin because he could talk and play with Speedy. One day, Speedy and Nappy went to the park to play. They saw a big hill and decided to race to the top. Speedy was very fast, but Nappy was not. Nappy was just a little napkin, so he could not move as fast as Speedy. But Speedy waited for Nappy at the top of the hill, and they laughed and had fun together. After playing at the park, Speedy and Nappy were very tired. They went back home and took a nice, long nap. They were happy to be best friends and have fun together, no matter how fast or slow they were. And they lived happily ever after.

Alice and her mommy were walking in the park. Alice liked the park because it was so bright and colourful. Everywhere she looked, she could see flowers blooming. Alice's mommy pointed to a tree nearby. Its branches cast a nice shade on the park bench underneath it. "Look Alice, that's the popular tree," her mommy said. Alice was really excited and she ran over to the tree. She tried to touch the bloomed flowers, but they were too high. Then suddenly, a bird popped out from the tree and perched on a branch. Alice pointed to the bird and laughed. "Tweet tweet!" she said. Alice's mommy smiled. "Yes," she said. "So many birds come to this popular tree." Alice was happy and she started chasing the birds through the park. She waved her little arms and laughed out loud. Finally, she went back to the tree and said goodbye to the birds before she and her mommy left.

One day, a friendly dog named Buddy went for a walk. He loved the sunshine on his face. Buddy saw a big tree and wanted to play. Under the tree, Buddy met a cat named Whiskers. "Do you want to play with me?" Buddy asked. Whiskers said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with a ball and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball rolled into a big hole. Buddy and Whiskers were sad. They both said, "We regret losing the ball." But then, the sunshine showed them a new toy. They played with the new toy and were happy again.
