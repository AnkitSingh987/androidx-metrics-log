Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he was playing and got very dirty. His mom said, "Tim, you need to wash your hands before dinner." Tim felt upset because he wanted to keep playing. Tim washed his hands, but he didn't dry them. The water dripped all over the floor. His mom saw the water and said, "Tim, you need to dry your hands too!" She gave him a towel to dry his hands. Tim didn't want to, but he did it anyway. At dinner, Tim's mom gave him a big surprise. It was his favorite food! Tim was so happy. He knew that if he didn't wash and dry his hands, he wouldn't have gotten his favorite dinner. From that day on, Tim always washed and dried his hands before dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She loved to take photos with her camera. One day, she went to the park to take photos of the pretty flowers and trees. While at the park, she met a boy named Tom. Tom saw Lily's camera and said, "I like your photo box. Can you take a photo of me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I can take a photo of you. Stand by the tree and smile big!" Tom did as Lily said, and she took a nice photo of him. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Lily's camera away. Both Lily and Tom were very sad. But then, a nice bird picked up the camera and brought it back to them. They were so happy! In the end, Lily and Tom became good friends and took many more photos together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a jog in the park. He loved to run and play with his friends. While he was running, he saw a small, helpless bird on the ground. The bird looked like it needed help. Tim went to the bird and asked, "Do you need help, little bird?" The bird nodded its head. Tim tried to remember what his mom told him about helping animals. He picked up the bird and took it home. At home, Tim showed the bird to his mom. She said, "We need to be careful with the bird." Tim and his mom tried to help the bird, but it did not get better. The bird passed away, and Tim was very sad. The unexpected thing was that the bird was very sick, and they could not save it. The moral of the story is that even when we try our best to help, sometimes things do not go as we hope.

Once there was a little girl called Emma. She was very healthy and loved to play. One day, she was in the kitchen when she saw a big bin. She had heard her mummy say that people suffer when they don't look after the bin. So, Emma decided to look after it and make sure it was clean. Every day, Emma would tidy and clean the bin. She was very careful, always making sure it was spotless. Everyone was so happy and grateful for Emma's work. She was very proud of herself and felt good. After a few weeks, the bin was still clean and healthy. Everyone was very pleased and thanked Emma for looking after it. From then on, no one had to suffer from a messy bin anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big panda named Pandy. Pandy lived in a large forest with many trees. He loved to eat bamboo all day long. One day, Pandy met a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was sad because she could not find food for her family. Pandy said, "Don't worry, Chirpy. I will supply you with some food for your family." Pandy shared his bamboo with Chirpy's family. Chirpy was very happy and thanked Pandy. The next day, an enormous tree fell down and blocked Pandy's way to his favorite bamboo spot. Pandy was sad and did not know what to do. Chirpy saw Pandy and said, "Don't worry, Pandy. I will help you find a new bamboo spot." Chirpy flew high and found a new place with lots of bamboo. Pandy was so happy and thanked Chirpy. From that day on, Pandy and Chirpy were the best of friends, always ready to help each other.
