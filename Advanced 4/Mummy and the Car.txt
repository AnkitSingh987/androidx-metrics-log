Title: Mummy and the Car

Mummy was so excited. It was her first day driving her new car. She got in and put the key in the ignition. "Time to get to the store," she said. But then Mummy stopped and looked in the side mirror. An officer was behind her. Mummy took a deep breath. The officer came to Mummy's window. "Do you have your license?" he asked. Mummy shook her head. "No," she said. She then explained that she thought her license was in her purse, but it wasn't there. The officer smiled. "Do you have a fake license then?" Mummy shook her head even more. "No," she said. "I don't have a fake license." The officer smiled again. "Alright," he said. "You should really have a license before driving. You should get one right away!" Mummy thanked the officer, and then drove away. She couldn't believe it was so easy. She made sure to get a license the next day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma liked to help her mom improve the kitchen. They would clean and make it look nice. One day, Emma met a deaf cat. The cat could not hear anything. Emma wanted to help the cat. She had an idea. She took the cat to the kitchen. She gave the cat some food and water. The cat was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat started to talk! Emma was surprised. The cat said, "Thank you, Emma, for helping me. I am a magic cat. Because you helped me, I will give you a wish." Emma wished for the cat to hear again. The cat could hear now! Emma and the cat became best friends and played together in the kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and have fun. One day, Tim found a toy razor in his room. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it looked cool. Tim wanted to travel with his toy razor. So, he put it in his bag and went outside. He walked and walked until he reached the park. At the park, he saw other kids playing and having fun. Tim took out his toy razor and showed it to his new friends. The other kids liked Tim's toy razor too. They all played together and had a great time. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim put his toy razor back in his bag and said goodbye to his friends. He traveled back home with a big smile on his face, happy to have had a fun day with his toy razor and new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to take walks in the woods near her house. One day, while walking, she found a cherry tree. The cherries on the tree were big and red. Lily picked a cherry and took a bite. "Yuck!" she said, "This cherry is sour!" She made a funny face. A little squirrel saw her and laughed. "You found the sour cherry tree," said the squirrel. Lily and the squirrel became friends. They played together under the cherry tree. The squirrel showed Lily where the sweet cherry tree was. They picked sweet cherries and had a yummy snack together. From that day on, Lily and the squirrel shared many fun days under the cherry trees.

Once upon a time, in a green forest, a little rabbit named Remy lived with his family. Remy loved to play with his friends and explore the forest. One day, Remy went to the east side of the forest to find new friends. As Remy hopped along, he met a small squirrel named Sally. "Hi," said Remy. "Do you want to play with me?" Sally looked at Remy and gave a little shrug. "I don't know," she said. "I'm a bit shy." Remy smiled and said, "That's okay, we can be shy friends together." So, Remy and Sally played together in the green forest. They laughed, hopped, and climbed trees all day. When the sun began to set, Remy said, "I had so much fun today, Sally. Let's play again tomorrow!" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, let's be friends forever!" From that day on, Remy and Sally became best friends, and they always played on the east side of the green forest.
