Title: Lily and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the summer. She liked the light sun that made her feel warm and happy. She loved playing outside with her friends. One day, Lily found a little bird that could not fly. She wanted to help the bird. Lily gave the bird some food and water. After a while, the bird felt better and could fly again. The bird said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping me." Lily felt happy that she could help the bird. She said, "You're welcome, little bird. I'm glad you can fly again." The bird flew away and Lily went back to playing with her friends. They all had a fun day in the light summer sun.

Mum and Dad were very busy in the morning. They were cleaning the house. The room was very dark. Mum said, "I hope the doctor comes soon." Dad replied, "Don't worry, he will be here soon." Soon enough, a nicely dressed man came to the house. Mum and Dad welcomed him with a big smile. He was the doctor. The doctor said, "I am here to help you clean the house." Mum and Dad were happy to hear this. The doctor put on his special shirt and began to clean the house. He cleaned all the windows, the floor and the walls. When he was finished, the house was all sparkly and clean. Mum and Dad thanked the doctor for his help, and he went away. The house was now bright and clean.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a slow ant named Andy. Andy liked to walk around and look at the pretty flowers. One day, while Andy was walking, he met a fly named Freddy. Freddy was very fast and liked to tease Andy because he was slow. As they played together, Freddy would fly fast and say, "Catch me if you can, slow Andy!" Andy would try to catch Freddy, but he was too slow. This made Andy feel sad. But, he did not give up. He kept trying to catch Freddy, even though it was hard. One day, Freddy got stuck in a spider web. He was scared and called for help. Andy heard Freddy and slowly walked to the web. Even though Freddy teased him, Andy still wanted to help. He used his strong ant legs to break the web and save Freddy. Freddy said, "Thank you, Andy. I am sorry for teasing you." From that day on, Freddy and Andy became best friends and learned that being kind is better than teasing others.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her daddy. They saw a big red ball stuck in a tree. Lucy wanted to get the ball down, so she asked her daddy for help. Daddy tried to pull the ball down, but it was too high for him. Lucy had an idea. She told her obedient dog, Spot, to help daddy. Spot jumped up and down, trying to reach the ball. Finally, with a big pull from daddy and Spot, the ball came down. Lucy was so happy! She hugged her daddy and Spot, and they all played with the ball together in the park.

Johnny was sitting in his bedroom, looking at his favourite toy truck. Then he heard a knock on the door. He called out, "Who is it?" A friendly voice replied, "It's your tutor". Johnny was feeling excited and he opened the door. His tutor was a tall lady with short black hair. She said hello and asked him if he wanted to go outside and play. Johnny said yes, and they started to walk out together. As they walked, the tutor warned Johnny to be careful. She said the park was quite dangerous and he must listen to her instructions. Johnny listened very carefully, and they safely made it to the park. When they were there, they ran around, laughed and had a lot of fun. Johnny thanked his tutor and said goodbye. His tutor smiled, waved goodbye and said, "It was great seeing you, have a safe journey home". Johnny replied with a happy nod and continued his journey home.
