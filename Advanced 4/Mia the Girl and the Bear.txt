Title: Mia the Girl and the Bear

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her soft teddy bear. She loved her teddy bear very much. Mia began to wonder what it would be like to have a real bear as a friend. That night, as she slept, she had a dream about a big, friendly bear. In her dream, Mia and the big bear played together in a beautiful park. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Then, Mia asked the bear, "Do you want to be my friend forever?" The bear smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to be your friend." But then, something unexpected happened. The bear started to shrink and became smaller and smaller. Mia was surprised and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, the bear turned into her soft teddy bear again. Mia woke up from her dream and hugged her teddy bear tightly. She realized that her teddy bear was her best friend all along.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They brought a big net to catch bugs. Tim was very good at catching bugs, but one bug was very persistent. It would not let Tim catch it! "Mom, I can't catch this bug!" Tim said. His mom tried to help, but the bug was too fast for her too. They chased the bug all around the park, but it always got away. Just as they were about to give up, a big wind came and shut the net on the bug. They were so surprised! Tim and his mom laughed and took the bug home to show dad. The bug was not so persistent anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a large eye looking around. The eye saw a park and decided to go there. When the eye arrived at the park, it saw a little girl playing. The eye called out to her. "Hi there!" said the eye. "Hello," said the little girl. "Do you want to play with me?" The eye was very excited. It was so happy that it did a few twirls in the air. The little girl and the eye played all kinds of games at the park. They raced each other, chased butterflies, and even built a sandcastle. The two of them had so much fun that they kept playing until it was dark. When the eye said goodbye to the little girl, it thanked her for the wonderful day.

Ben and Sam were friends who liked to play with toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a helicopter. The helicopter could fly in the air with a remote. Ben and Sam took turns to make the helicopter fly high and low, fast and slow. One day, they saw a new boy in the park. He had a different helicopter. His helicopter was bigger and louder than theirs. It had lights and sounds and a camera. The new boy made his helicopter fly over Ben and Sam's helicopter and laughed. "Hey, that's not nice!" Ben said. "Your helicopter is scaring our helicopter!" "So what? My helicopter is better than yours. It can do anything!" the new boy said. He made his helicopter fly closer and closer to Ben and Sam's helicopter. He wanted to show them how cool his helicopter was. But he was not careful. His helicopter hit Ben and Sam's helicopter in the air. Both helicopters fell to the ground and broke into pieces. Ben and Sam cried. The new boy cried too. He had broken his own helicopter and his new friends' helicopter. He was sorry, but it was too late. He had no helicopter and no friends. He had to go home alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pencil that she loved very much. She would draw pretty pictures with it all day. One day, Lily was feeling naughty. She wanted to draw on the walls with her pencil. Her mom saw her and said, "No, Lily! I do not permit you to draw on the walls. That is not good." Lily felt sad, but she knew her mom was right. She said sorry to her mom and promised to only draw on paper. From that day on, Lily was a good girl and never tried to draw on the walls again.
