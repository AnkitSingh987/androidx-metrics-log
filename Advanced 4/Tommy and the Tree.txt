Title: Tommy and the Tree

Tommy loved to play hide and seek with his dog Spot. Spot was a good and obedient dog who always came when Tommy called him. One day, they went to the park to play. Tommy found a big tree and told Spot to hide behind it. Then he counted to ten and went to look for him. But when Tommy got to the tree, he did not see Spot. He only saw a big black beetle crawling on the bark. Tommy did not like beetles. They were scary and icky. He screamed and ran away from the tree. He looked around for Spot, but he could not find him anywhere. He felt sad and scared. Where was his dog? Spot heard Tommy scream and came out from behind the tree. He saw the beetle and barked at it. He wanted to protect Tommy from the beetle. He chased the beetle away from the tree and ran after Tommy. He caught up with him and licked his face. Tommy was happy to see Spot. He hugged him and said sorry for leaving him. Spot wagged his tail and forgave him. They went back to the tree and played hide and seek again. But this time, Tommy hid behind a bush and Spot hid behind a rock. They did not want to see any more beetles. They had fun and laughed a lot. They were best friends.

Once upon a time there was an honest prince who lived in a big palace. Every morning he would bow to his palace and say, "Hello Palace!" One day, the prince went outside and saw a beautiful field with a golden bow in the middle of it. He took the bow and brought it home to his palace. The prince played with the bow in the palace every day and he even taught his pet dog how to bow. Then one day, a fairy appeared and said to the prince, "I am very happy with your honesty. As a reward, I am giving you this magical bow. When you bow with it, it will give you whatever you wish for!" The prince was very happy and thanked the fairy. He took the magical bow home and started to practice with it. Finally, he was able to bow and create beautiful things whenever he wished. He was so happy he decided to share his magical bow with his friends from the palace. The prince and his friends shared their magical bows and created wonderful things together. From then on, no one ever needed anything in the palace, because he was generous and honest. The prince and everyone else bowed to the palace every day with their magical bows and thanked them for the happiness they brought.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big room upstairs. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, his mom told him, "Tim, you need to clean your room." Tim didn't want to clean his room. He wanted to keep playing. Later, Tim's mom went to the store. While she was gone, Tim heard a noise upstairs. He was scared. He slowly went upstairs to see what it was. When he opened the door, he saw a big mess. He realized he needed to clean his room, so the noise would go away. Tim worked hard to clean his room. He put all his toys away. When his mom came home, she saw his clean room and was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Tim felt grateful that his mom was happy. He learned that cleaning his room was important. And the noise? It was just a toy that had fallen on the floor.

Once upon a time there was a tidy little girl called Bella. Bella loved to play outside and always looked for something new and exciting to do. One day she came across an empty jar and knew she had to collect something special to put in it. She looked up at the night sky and saw the moon and stars, shining bright in the universe. Bella decided that she wanted to collect the beauty of the night sky and keep it in her jar. But when she stretched out her arm to try and pick up a star, she heard her mum's voice. "No Bella, you can't collect stars. They're too far away. You'll have to think of something a bit closer." Bella thought and thought, but couldn't come up with a solution. Then she remembered her mum's instructions to keep her room clean and she had an idea. She picked up all of her toys and put them tidily away in their shelves. This way she could collect the beauty of her room and put it in her jar. At the end of the day, when her mum came in to check on her, she saw the tidy room and was so proud of Bella! Moral: The best things in life don't have to be found far away, sometimes the best things are closest to us.
