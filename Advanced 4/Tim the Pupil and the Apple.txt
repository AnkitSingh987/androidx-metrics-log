Title: Tim the Pupil and the Apple

One day, a pupil named Tim went to school. It was a clear day. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tim was very happy. He had a big red apple in his bag to eat at lunchtime. At lunch, Tim took out his apple and started to eat it. His friend, Sue, looked at him and said, "Can I have a bite of your apple?" Tim said, "Okay, but just one bite." Sue took a big bite of the apple and smiled. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the lunchroom and landed on Tim's apple. The bird started to eat the apple too! Tim, Sue, and all the other pupils laughed and clapped. The bird finished the apple and flew away. Tim and Sue were not mad, they thought it was funny. From that day on, they always shared their lunch with the little bird.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house with many toys. He was very happy. One day, Tom saw a beautiful sight. It was a big, pretty picture on the wall. He wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tom said to Sam, "Look at the pretty sight! I want to print it for you." Sam was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom." So, Tom used a machine to print the pretty picture for Sam. The picture came out of the machine, and Tom gave it to Sam. Sam loved the pretty picture. He took it home and put it on his wall. Every day, Sam looked at the pretty sight and thought of his friend Tom. They both were very happy and played with their toys together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big machine. This machine was very organized. It was good at putting things in the right place. The machine had many friends. They all liked to play together. One day, the friends wanted to play dress-up. They looked at the machine and asked, "What will you wear?" The machine thought for a moment and said, "I will wear a hat and a scarf." The friends were happy and they all dressed up too. They played all day and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they were all tired but very happy. They learned that it is good to be organized and to help each other. The moral of the story is to always help your friends and work together. When you are organized and kind, everyone has more fun.

Once upon a time, in a tidy forest, there lived a little tiger named Tim. Tim was a very playful tiger who liked to run and jump all day long. One sunny day, Tim was playing with his best friend, a tiny bird named Bella. As they were playing, Tim said, "Bella, let's pause and rest for a while." So they both stopped and sat under a big tree. They talked and laughed, enjoying their time together. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Tim and Bella looked around and saw a big elephant coming their way. The elephant was very kind and wanted to play with them too. Tim and Bella were very happy to have a new friend. So they all played together until the sun went down, and they went back to their homes, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there was a bright house. One day, a snake entered the house. Everyone was scared and ran away. But the snake did not hurt anyone. It just wanted to play. The snake found a spot in the corner and began to dance. Its body moved so fast that it was almost too bright to look at! It danced and danced, bringing joy to the house. Everyone in the house was amazed by the snake's dance. The snake kept on dancing until the sun went down. When it was time to go, the snake said goodbye and waved its tail. The snake left the house and everyone was happy. They learned that snakes can be friendly, too. They will always remember the bright and happy snake who entered their house that day.
