Title: Sue the Girl and the Hat

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a soft, red velvet hat on a bench. Sue wanted to get the hat, but she was scared. She thought the hat might belong to someone else. Sue saw a boy named Tim near the bench. Sue asked, "Is this your hat?" Tim shook his head and said, "No, it's not mine." Sue was still scared, but she decided to get the hat. Just as Sue picked up the hat, a big dog ran towards her. Sue was very scared. The dog barked loudly. But then, the dog saw the velvet hat in Sue's hand. The dog wagged its tail and looked happy. Sue realized the hat belonged to the dog. She gave the hat to the dog, and they played together in the park.

One day, a quiet cat named Tom saw a rope on the floor. He did not recognize the rope. Tom was very curious about the rope. Tom said, "Hello, rope! Where did you come from?" The rope did not talk back because ropes cannot talk. Tom decided to play with the rope. Tom played with the rope all day. They had lots of fun together. At the end of the day, Tom was tired but happy. He now recognized the rope as his new friend.

Once upon a time there was a chubby little boy called Harry. Harry was playing with his toy car and he had an amazing idea! He wanted to race his toy car in the bathroom. So Harry grabbed his car, ran into the bathroom and shouted: "Let the race begin!". He began zipping his car around the bathroom, zig-zagging around the toilet and steering wide of the sink. Just then, Harry heard a voice calling him: "Harry, what are you doing in there?". It was mummy! Mummy was standing in the hallway and had a big smile on her face. 'I'm racing my car mum!' exclaimed Harry. 'That's fun!', said Mummy. 'Let me join in!' Mummy raced around the bathroom, competing with Harry in a very friendly race. The two of them raced around the bathroom until both of them got so tired and giggling that they had to call it a draw. With a happy smile, Harry and his mummy headed off to the living room for a well-deserved rest!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was very excited because she had a shiny medal. The medal was to be her very special prize. But then one day, when she was playing outside, she accidentally dropped it in the dirt. The little girl was very sad. She started to cry and ran to tell her mom. When her mom saw the dirty medal, she said to the little girl, "What happened? Did you drop it?" The little girl nodded. Her mom wiped the medal off and said, "Don't worry, I'll clean it up so you can wear it again." The little girl was so happy! As she hugged her mom, she proudly wore the medal around her neck.

Once there was a bald man. He loved helicopters! One day he had an idea - he wanted to grow his own helicopter! So he planted a seed in the ground and waited. At first it was just a tiny seed but then, day by day, the seed started to grow. The man watched and waited, and soon the seed had become a tiny plant. The plant got bigger and bigger, and before too long it had become a huge helicopter. The man was so excited, and he jumped right in and flew away. The man was very happy. He flew his helicopter around the world and had lots of fun. Every day, the helicopter would grow a little bigger, until it was almost twice as big as before! The man and the helicopter flew around the world together, until they were both old and grey. The man will never forget the fun times he had with his magical helicopter.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and play with his puzzle. One day, a little bird named Tim came to visit him. Tim was very excite and wanted to play with Tom. Tom did not want to play. He just wanted to finish his puzzle. But Tim was not happy. He wanted to have fun and play with Tom. So, Tim took a piece of Tom's puzzle and flew away with it. Tom was very sad. He needed that piece to finish his puzzle. So, he decided to stop being lazy and chase Tim. They ran and ran until Tom finally caught Tim. Tom asked for the puzzle piece back. Tim said he would give the puzzle piece back if Tom promised to play with him. Tom agreed and they played together all day. In the end, Tom learned that playing with friends was even more fun than his puzzle. And they lived happily ever after.
