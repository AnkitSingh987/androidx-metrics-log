Title: Mary and the Pond

One bright day, Mary and her dad were playing outside in their garden. Mary saw something shiny in the grass and pointed it out to her dad. "Look, Daddy!" she said, holding out a nail, "It's so bright!" Her dad smiled and thanked her for finding it. "Let's put it in the back of the garden and use our hammers," he said. So the two of them put the nail into the wall, then each grabbed a hammer to make the nail stay in place. Once the nails were in the wall, Mary's dad suggested they take a break. "Let's go for a dive into the pond," he said. Mary was excited, so she ran to the pond and leapt into it, smiling as she jumped through the cool water. "It's so much fun," she shouted, as she swam around in the pond. After they had both finished playing in the pond, Mary and her dad dried off and took a rest in the bright sunshine. Mary hugged her dad and thanked him for the fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a doctor who had an open door. Every day, people would come to see him when they felt sick. The doctor was very good at making them feel better. He had a big supply of things to help them. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the doctor. She said, "Doctor, my tummy hurts." The doctor looked at his supply and gave her some medicine. He told her, "Take this and you will feel better soon." The next day, Lily came back to the open door. She said, "Doctor, I feel much better now, thank you!" The doctor smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad I could help." And from that day on, Lily knew that the doctor with the open door was always there to help her feel better.

One day, there was a little boy called Joe. Joe was three years old and he was going to his first day of school. He was very excited; he was going to wear his special school uniform. Taking his uniform out of the cupboard, Joe got dressed quickly. He put on the uniform and felt lucky that he had a special uniform all of his own. Then Joe went outside and waited for the bus to come. When the bus drew up, Joe was very excited. The bus driver smiled at Joe when he saw him wearing his uniform. Joe smiled back and then hurriedly climbed onto the bus. Joe sat at the back and he felt very lucky to be wearing his uniform. Finally, the bus reached school. Joe and all the other children walked inside and Joe felt extra special, wearing his smart uniform. Joe and the other children then went into the classroom and Joe knew that he was very lucky to have his uniform.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. The turkey lived on a farm. The turkey was very comfortable on the farm. He liked to eat and play with his friends. One day, the turkey met a new friend. The new friend was a little chick. The chick did not know what to do on the farm. The turkey wanted to help the chick. He said, "I recommend you play with me and my friends." The chick was very happy. He played with the turkey and his friends. They all had a lot of fun. The turkey and the chick became best friends. They were always comfortable and happy on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a famous doctor who could see inside people with a magic thing called an x-ray. One day, a boy named Tim needed help, so his mom called the famous doctor. The doctor came to Tim's house and said, "I will use my x-ray to see what is wrong." Tim was scared, but the doctor made him feel better. The doctor looked at the x-ray and saw something inside Tim. The doctor tried to help Tim, but it was too late. The x-ray showed that Tim had eaten something bad. Tim's mom cried, and the famous doctor was very sad. They all wished they had called the doctor sooner.
