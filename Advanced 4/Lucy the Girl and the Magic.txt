Title: Lucy the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big fear of the unknown. Lucy was scared of the dark and always had nightmares at night. She would cry and call for her mom to help her feel safe. One day, Lucy's mom told her about a magic light that could help her gain bravery. The light would chase away the darkness and help her sleep without nightmares. Lucy wanted to try it, so her mom put the magic light in her room that night. With the magic light, Lucy slept well and didn't have any nightmares. She learned that facing the unknown can help you gain courage and strength. The moral of the story is to face your fears and you will become braver.

Tommy loved to draw with his crayons. He had many colors and he made original pictures of his family, his friends and his toys. He liked to show his pictures to his mommy and daddy and they always praised him. One day, Tommy found a new crayon in his box. It was shiny and sparkly and it looked very pretty. Tommy wanted to try it and see what it could do. He took the crayon and started to draw on his bedroom wall. He made stars and flowers and rainbows. He was very happy. But then, his mommy came in and saw what he had done. She was very angry and she shouted at him. She said he was a naughty boy and he had ruined the wall. She took away his crayons and said he could not draw anymore. She said he had to clean the wall and say sorry. Tommy was very sad and he cried. He did not understand why his mommy was so mad. He thought he had made a beautiful picture. He wished he had never used the new crayon. He wished he had listened to his mommy when she had reminded him to only draw on paper. He learned a hard lesson that day.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a small toy angel in her room. She liked the angel very much. It had a pretty dress and big wings. Amy decided to hold the angel tight and take it with her everywhere she went. Amy showed the angel to her mom. Her mom said, "Be careful with the angel, Amy. It has a secret." Amy did not know what the secret was, but she promised to be careful. She held the angel tight and played with it all day. That night, Amy took the angel to bed with her. She held it tight as she slept. Suddenly, the angel began to glow! It grew bigger and bigger until it was a real angel! The angel said, "Thank you, Amy, for holding me tight. Now I can help you in your dreams." Amy was very happy and knew that the secret was a good one.

Once upon a time, there was a thin crane named Carl. Carl was sad because his long neck was bent. He wanted to fix it so he could be like the other cranes. One day, Carl met a wise old turtle named Tom. Carl said, "Tom, can you help me fix my neck? I want to be like the other cranes." Tom thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Carl. I know how to fix your neck. Follow me." Tom led Carl to a big tree with strong branches. He said, "Carl, stretch your neck up and wrap it around this branch. Then, pull hard." Carl did as Tom said, and with a big tug, his neck became straight. Carl was so happy. He thanked Tom and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a hot and sandy land, there was a pretty oasis. It was a happy place where many flowers bloom. One day, a tired bee named Benny came to the oasis. Benny saw a big flower and said, "Hello, pretty flower! I am so tired. Can I rest on you?" The flower smiled and said, "Yes, Benny! I am here to help you. You can rest on me." Benny was very happy. He thanked the flower and rested on it. After some time, Benny felt better. He flew around the oasis and saw more flowers bloom. Benny made many new friends and played with them. And they all lived happily in the oasis.
