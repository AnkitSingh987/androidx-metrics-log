Title: Tim the Tough Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a tough boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big box in the yard. It was full of dust. Tim thought it was a fun game to stand on the box and jump. The next day, Tim saw the box again. He thought it would be fun to stand on it once more. But this time, when he jumped, something unexpected happened. The box broke, and Tim fell inside! Inside the box, Tim found a big, soft teddy bear. The bear was hiding under the dust. Tim was so happy to find a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the teddy bear played together all the time.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were in the kitchen. The cat, named Tom, was very good at cooking. He was a reliable friend. The dog, named Max, liked to watch Tom cook. Tom was making something yummy in a pan. Max asked, "What are you making?" Tom said, "I am making a cake for us." Max was very happy. They both paused for a moment and listened to the cake sizzle in the pan. Suddenly, a bird flew into the kitchen through the open window. The bird saw the cake in the pan and wanted some. The bird took the cake and flew away. Tom and Max were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "We will make a new cake together."

Once upon a time there lived a little boy and he was only three. His name was Sam and he had a very special toy. It was a big train that he was very fond of. One day, Sam's mom decided to take him on a trip to the park. When they got there, Sam was very excited. He saw lots of new things and he ran around, playing and exploring. Sam soon found a tree and he thought it would be fun to play pretend. He picked up a stick and pretended to cut it. Suddenly, his mom told him to stay away from the tree. Sam was a bit sad but he listened to his mom. His mom then went over to him and gave him a big hug. She was very thoughtful and said, "It's ok Sam, you can still pretend. What about using this stick for something else, like making mud pies?" Sam was very excited at the thought of making mud pies and he quickly ran to find some mud. He and his mom had lots of fun together, creating mud pies all afternoon. At the end, Sam was very happy and was very thankful for his thoughtful mom.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. One was pushing a motorcycle while the other was following behind. But then they started to quarrel. The one pushing the motorcycle said: "You don't help me push. It's too hard!" The one following replied: "But I'm not strong like you!" Then, a magical thing happened. A big, strong man appeared from behind the trees. With a grin on his face, he said: "Let me help you!" The friends were glad and thanked the man for his help. And so, with the man pushing the motorcycle, the two friends were able to finish their journey.

Ben likes motorcycles. He has a toy motorcycle that he plays with every day. He makes vroom-vroom noises and pretends to ride fast. He wants to have a real motorcycle when he grows up. One day, he sees a big motorcycle parked outside his house. It is shiny and red and has a loud horn. Ben runs to the window and looks at it. He thinks it is the most beautiful motorcycle he has ever seen. He wonders who owns it and where they are going. He asks his mom if he can go outside and see the motorcycle. His mom says no, because it is not their motorcycle and they do not know who it belongs to. She says it is not available for him to touch or ride. She says he has to wait until he is older and has his own motorcycle. Ben feels sad and disappointed. He goes back to his room and plays with his toy motorcycle. He hopes that someday he will have a real motorcycle like the one outside. He dreams of riding fast and far and having fun. He knows that he will grow and learn and save money. He knows that he will find a motorcycle that is available for him. He smiles and makes vroom-vroom noises. He is happy.
