Title: James and the Lion

James was so excited to be having his first day at the zoo. As he walked into the zoo, he saw a big orange lion. He waved at the lion, who just sat and watched him back. Suddenly James heard the lion talking! He was so surprised. The lion asked "Why did you come here James?". James said "I wanted to see what the zoo had to offer". The lion chuckled and said "Ah, a question for me? My answer is simple. I pray that you will find what you are looking for here". James was so surprised. He thanked the lion and went off to explore the zoo. Everywhere he went, he heard people talking about the orange lion who liked to pray. James smiled, thankful for the advice he had been given.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a big tree in the forest. The owl loved to learn new things. Every day, the owl would learn something new and keep it in his memory. One day, a little bird came to the wise owl. The bird wanted to learn how to fly high in the sky. The wise owl said, "I will teach you." So, the owl and the bird spent the day learning to fly together. The little bird was so happy. It thanked the wise owl for helping it learn to fly high. The wise owl was happy too because he added a new memory to his collection. And they both lived happily in the forest, learning new things every day.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the subway to make a trip. They wanted to see the rich part of the town. Tim was very happy to go on the subway for the first time. On the subway, Tim saw a man with a big hat. The man said, "Hi, I am Mr. Rich. I live in the rich part of town. Do you want to see my big house?" Tim and his mom said, "Yes, we would love to see it!" When they got to the big house, it was not what they thought. It was very small! Mr. Rich said, "I just like to pretend I am rich. I am not really rich, but I am happy." Tim and his mom learned that being rich is not always about having a big house.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to run and zoom all around the park. He also liked to eat celery because it was a harmless snack that made him strong. Today, Tim's mom gave him some celery to eat while they played. At the park, Tim saw a big dog. The dog looked harmless and friendly, so Tim went to play with it. The dog started to zoom around with Tim, and they both had a lot of fun. Tim wanted to share his celery with the dog, so he gave the dog a piece. But then, something unexpected happened! The dog ate the celery and began to change! The dog turned into a big, colorful bird! Tim and his mom were surprised. The bird flew up into the sky and zoomed around, just like Tim and the dog did before. Tim and his mom watched the bird fly away, amazed by the magical celery that changed the dog into a bird. They laughed and played in the park, knowing that they had seen something very special. From that day on, Tim always remembered the fun day at the park when he shared his celery with the dog and saw the unexpected change.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Christopher. He was three years old. He was having a lot of fun playing with his toys and as he was playing, he heard a loud noise coming from outside. He wanted to see what was happening. He opened the door and saw his mom and dad getting out of the car. They were smiling and happy. Christopher ran up to them and asked, 'What did you do?' His dad said, 'We just completed our first lecture, and it was very safe. We are so proud of ourselves.' Christopher smiled and asked, 'Can I come too?' His dad smiled and said, 'Oh Christopher, you will have to wait until you're a bit bigger.' Christopher said okay and he went back to play with his toys. He knew that his parents had done something important and he was so happy that they were safe.
