Title: Sean and Claire and the Car

Once upon a time, there were two kids, Sean and Claire, who loved to play together. Every day, after their lessons were done, they always went to the park. They would collect shiny rocks, explore the playground and often play tag. They had so much fun! One day, Sean announced to Claire that he had a special surprise. He took her to a garage and introduced her to the mechanic. The mechanic asked them to collect some tools and they happily agreed. Sean explained that they were very independent and could do a lot of things by themselves. The mechanic smiled and said, "You know, if you could fix my car, I'd be very impressed. It looks like it needs some work." Claire and Sean smiled and said in unison, "We'd love to try!" The mechanic showed them how to use each tool and how to fix the car. The next day, Sean and Claire proudly drove the car away. Ever since then, Sean and Claire have enjoyed collecting tools, fixing things and being independent to everyone's amazement.

One day, a big, loud party was happening in the town. Everyone was there to celebrate. They had a lot of fun. They danced, ate, and played games. There was a lot of noise from the music and people talking. In the middle of the party, a little boy named Tim found a fan. The fan was very pretty with many colors. Tim wanted to show the fan to his friends. But the music was so loud that he could not hear them. Tim felt sad because he could not talk to his friends. He thought of an idea. He went to the DJ and asked him to turn down the music. The DJ said yes and made the music quiet. Now, Tim could show the fan to his friends. They all liked the fan and took turns using it. They were happy that they could talk and play with the fan. The party was even more fun now for Tim and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely automobile. The automobile was red and shiny. It had no friends to play with. One day, the automobile saw a little boy. The boy observed the automobile and said, "Hi, automobile! Do you want to play with me?" The automobile was very happy and said, "Yes, I want to play!" The boy and the automobile played together all day. They went to the park, and they played with other kids. The automobile was not lonely anymore. They were best friends forever.

Once, there was a little girl who was very happy. She had a favourite toy which was green, and she always carried it with her. One day, the little girl was playing with her toy when a big gust of wind blew it away. She cried and followed it, but it was gone. Suddenly, a big, mean dog came over and started barking at her. She was scared and tried to run away, but the dog jumped and tried to bite her. She closed her eyes and held up her hands. Suddenly, she felt something soft - it was her green toy, slapping against her arm! It was a happy memory for the little girl, who was now safe and sound. She hugged her toy tightly and never let it go.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Tim. Tim found a trumpet in the park. He wanted to play it, so he tried and tried, but he could not make any sound. Tim felt sad. One day, a little girl named Lucy saw Tim with the trumpet. Lucy was a smart girl who could play the trumpet very well. Lucy decided to help Tim. She showed Tim how to play the trumpet. They tried and tried together, but still, Tim could not make any sound. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny bird flew down and landed on the trumpet. The bird tried to play the trumpet, and suddenly, it made a beautiful sound! Tim, Lucy, and the bird were all very surprised. They laughed and played together, making happy music in the park.

Tim and Kim like to play with their toy horses. They have a big box with many horses of different colors and sizes. They pretend that they are cowboys and cowgirls and ride their horses around the room. One day, Tim finds a new toy in the box. It is a long, thin rope with a handle. He thinks it is a whip. He wants to use it to make his horse go faster. He says to Kim, "Look, I have a whip! Now I can gain speed and win the race!" Kim is not happy. She does not have a whip. She thinks that Tim is being unfair. She says to Tim, "No, you cannot use the whip! That is cheating! You have to use your voice and your legs to make your horse go faster, like me!" Tim does not listen. He swings the whip in the air and makes a loud noise. He says to Kim, "Too bad! I have the whip and you don't! Watch me gain speed and win the race!" He puts the whip on his horse's back and makes it run. Kim is angry. She does not like to lose. She looks around the room and sees a wide belt on the floor. She thinks it is a saddle. She says to herself, "I have an idea! I will use the belt as a saddle and make my horse more comfortable and happy. Then it will run faster and I will win the race!" She puts the belt around her horse's belly and ties it. She says to her horse, "Don't worry, I have a saddle for you! Now you can run faster and win the race!" She puts her legs on her horse's sides and makes it run. Tim and Kim race around the room. They both want to win. They both make their horses go faster and faster. They both have fun. But who will win the race?
