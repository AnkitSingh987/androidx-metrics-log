Title: Tom the Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big, sour lemon on the side of the road. He wanted to protect the lemon, so he picked it up and took it home. Tom showed the lemon to his friend, the dog named Jerry. Jerry said, "Tom, we must be careful with this sour lemon. I think it is not a good lemon." Tom did not listen to Jerry. He wanted to keep the lemon and protect it. One day, Tom and Jerry were playing with their lemon. Suddenly, the lemon rolled away and fell into a big hole. Tom and Jerry tried to get it out, but they fell into the hole too. They could not get out and they were very sad. The sour lemon did not protect them, and they stayed in the hole forever.

One day, a little cat named Luna went for a wander in the woods. She walked and walked until she saw some smoke. The smoke was coming from a campfire. Luna was curious and wanted to see who was there. Near the campfire, Luna saw a big, graceful deer. The deer was eating some leaves. Luna walked up to the deer and said, "Hello, I am Luna. What are you doing?" The deer looked at Luna and said, "Hello, Luna. I am Daisy. I am just eating some leaves and staying warm near the fire." Luna and Daisy became friends. They played and laughed together in the woods. After a fun day, they said goodbye and went back to their homes. Luna was happy she went for a wander and found a new friend.

Once there was a new puppy. He was so excited to explore the big, wide world. He ran around the house, sniffing and tasting everything. The puppy found some junk in the corner. He started to spread it out across the floor. The little puppy's mom said, "Oh no! What are you doing? No, no, no!" The puppy looked up at her with his big, curious eyes and said, "Spreading?" The mom laughed and said, "Yes, you're spreading the junk. Let's put it back and find something new to play with." So mom and her little puppy went outside to look around. Everywhere they looked they found something new and exciting. There were new rocks to sniff and new leaves to play with. The puppy ran around happily and his mom laughed. It was a wonderful day, and the puppy had learned something new: there was more to explore than just junk.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a big, orange cat sitting on a bench. The cat stared at Lily and her mom. Lily wanted to pet the cat, so she walked closer. "Hello, cat!" said Lily. "Why do you stare at us?" The cat did not say anything but kept staring. Just then, a nurse walked up to the cat. She had a kind face and wore a white hat. "Hi, I am Nurse Jane," she said. "This cat is lost and needs help. Can you help me find its home?" Lily and her mom said yes. They walked with Nurse Jane and the orange cat, looking for its home. As they walked, the orange cat started to change. It grew bigger and bigger, until it was as big as a house! Lily, her mom, and Nurse Jane were very surprised. The cat was not a cat at all – it was a big, friendly lion! The lion took them to his home, where they met his family and had a fun day playing together.

Matilda was so excited to go to the park. She ran to the swings and asked her mom, “Can I swing?” “Of course,” said her mom. “Just make sure you stay calm.” Matilda got on the swing and started pumping her legs. She was so happy to be flying high! She shouted with excitement, “Look at me Mommy, look at me!” Mom smiled and said, “You’re very good at swinging. Do you want to try another thing?” Matilda thought for a minute and then smiled as she followed her mom to the slide. Matilda climbed up the ladder and slid down, squealing with joy. Mom said, “You know what else? Let’s go to the carousel. I have the key!” Matilda nodded with delight and grabbed her mom’s hand. They ran to the carousel and the key opened the gate. Matilda sat on a white horse and they started moving in a circle. Matilda threw her arms up in the air and smiled. The wind was blowing in her face as the music played. Her Mom smiled and said to her, “You look so calm, Matilda! You’re a natural at swinging!”
