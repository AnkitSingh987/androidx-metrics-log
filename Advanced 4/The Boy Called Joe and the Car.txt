Title: The Boy Called Joe and the Car

Once upon a time there was a boy called Joe. Joe lived with his family and they loved him so much. One summer, Joe and his family went to the beach for a holiday. When they arrived, Joe was so happy and he said "Welcome!" When he looked around he could see the sea and the sand. He wanted to explore and smell the sea air. But when he did, he noticed a strange smell. "What is that smell?" he asked. It was the smell of gas, it was terrible! Joe's family were worried and ran away from the beach. They quickly jumped in the car and drove away. Joe was very sad, he wanted to stay at the beach and he couldn't understand why he had to leave. When they got home, Joe's family explained that the gas was dangerous. Joe was sad, he had been looking forward to his holiday. But he was happy to be safe with his family.

Once upon a time there was a busy little bird, who loved to whistle. It sang all day long, until one day it tried to escape from its cage. It had never been so free. It flew up and away, chirping with glee. But a nasty cat was lurking nearby, and it made a swipe at the little bird. The bird was scared and flew faster, but the cat hissed and kept coming close. The little bird was so frightened, it flew higher and higher away from the cat. The cat was too big and slow, so the bird escaped and flew off into the sky, enjoying its freedom. Everywhere it flew, it sang with its whistle, turning heads as it flew by. The little bird flew with joy and never looked back. Now it is seen everywhere, chirping and whistling. It is a busy little bird, but with many friends to keep it company!

Once upon a time, there was a gentle dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball and his sack. One day, Spot saw a big hill and wanted to play on it. He thought it would be fun to roll the sack down the hill. Spot's friend, Bird, saw him and came over. Bird was very wise and liked to help others. Bird looked at the hill and said, "Wait, Spot! I suggest we look for a safer place to play with the sack." Spot listened to Bird and they went to find another place to play. They found a nice, flat park and played with the sack there. Spot was very happy and thanked Bird for the suggestion. They played all day and had lots of fun. Spot learned that it was important to listen to his friends and think about safety before playing.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely number named One. One had no friends to play with, so One would often pause and look around, hoping to find someone to be friends with. One day, as One was walking, One saw a big tree. Under the tree, One saw another number named Two. Two was also lonely and looking for a friend. One and Two saw each other and smiled. They knew they could be friends. One and Two decided to play together and have fun. As they played, they saw another number named Three. Three was also lonely and looking for friends. One and Two asked Three to join them. Now, they were all happy and not lonely anymore. They played together every day, and they never felt lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bob. Bob loved to sail on the big blue sea. One sunny day, Bob met a fish named Fin. Fin was a good swimmer. "Hi, Fin!" said Bob. "Do you want to sail with me?" "Yes, Bob!" said Fin. "I would love to sail with you!" So, Bob and Fin sailed together. They saw many things. They saw big fish and small fish. They saw birds in the sky. They even saw a big, dry island. Bob and Fin had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, Bob and Fin said goodbye. They were happy to be friends. They knew they would sail and swim together again soon. And they lived happily ever after.
