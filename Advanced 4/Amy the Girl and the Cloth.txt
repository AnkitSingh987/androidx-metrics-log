Title: Amy the Girl and the Cloth

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to clap her hands when she was happy. One day, Amy went to the store with her mom. In the store, Amy saw a very pretty cloth. It was shiny and colorful. Amy wanted the cloth to make a dress for her doll. Amy asked her mom, "Can I have this cloth, please?" Her mom looked at the price tag and said, "Oh, this cloth is expensive. We cannot buy it today." Amy felt sad but did not cry. She put the cloth back on the shelf. On their way home, Amy saw a lady who was sad. Her dress was torn. Amy wanted to help. She remembered the pretty cloth in the store. Amy told her mom, "I want to give the lady my old dress so she can be happy." Her mom smiled and said, "That is very kind, Amy." So they gave the lady Amy's old dress. The lady was very happy and clapped her hands. Amy learned that being kind is more important than having expensive things.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim had a pretty collar that he wore every day. He loved to play in the deep grass in his yard. One day, Tim decided to imagine he was a big lion. He roared and jumped around in the deep grass. He felt like the king of the yard. Tim played all day, imagining he was a lion. When it was time to go inside, he was very tired. He curled up in his bed and fell asleep, still wearing his pretty collar.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had big eyes and they were very bright. She liked to explore her world and see what was available. One day, she decided to go for a walk and find something new. As she walked, she heard a noise. She looked around, but couldn't figure out where it was coming from. She felt scared, but followed the noise. After walking for a while, she came upon a big tree. Something was coming from the tree! She looked closely and saw a big eye looking at her! She was scared, but the eye just stayed still. Slowly, she reached out her hand and touched the eye. To her surprise the eye closed! Then the noise stopped. She smiled and the eye opened again. Together, they looked around the garden and shared some of the things that were available. The little girl was so happy to have found a new friend. From that day on, she would visit the big tree every day to come and see the eye.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat cereal every day. One day, Tim's mom asked him to record a message for his dad. Tim was a bit ashamed because he never did it before. "Hello, Dad. I love you. I ate all my cereal," Tim said into the phone. His mom smiled and told him he did a great job. Little did Tim know, his dad was going to play the message for everyone at work. The next day, Tim's dad came home with a big surprise. He had a huge box of cereal for Tim! Tim was so happy and excited. "Why did you get me this big box of cereal, Dad?" he asked. His dad laughed and said, "I played your message at work, and everyone loved it! They all wanted to give you a gift, so they bought you this big box of cereal!" Tim couldn't believe it. He was so proud of himself for recording the message, and now he had lots of cereal to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. He loved to dance and play all day. But he had no friends to dance with him. So, he felt sad. One day, Tim went to the market with his mom. At the market, he saw many people buying and selling things. Tim saw a girl named Lily. Lily was dancing and having fun. Tim wanted to dance too. Tim went to Lily and asked, "Can I dance with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's dance together!" They danced and played at the market. Tim was not lonely anymore. He had a new friend to dance with, and they were both very happy.
