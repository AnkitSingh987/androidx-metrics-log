Title: Sue the Girl and the Elevator

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to design things. One day, she wanted to make a pretty elevator. She thought it would be fun to go up and down in it. Sue worked hard on her elevator. She made it with colors like red, blue, and yellow. It was very pretty. She showed it to her friends, and they all liked it too. They wanted to play with the elevator. But when they tried to use the elevator, something unexpected happened. The elevator did not go up and down. Instead, it went side to side! Sue and her friends were surprised. They never thought an elevator could do that. Sue and her friends had fun with the new elevator. They laughed and played all day. In the end, Sue was happy that her pretty elevator was different. She learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be the best.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a unique shop. This shop had many sweet treats. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the unique shop with her mom. She saw a big, colorful lollipop. Lily wanted the lollipop very much. Lily asked her mom, "Can I have the lollipop, please?" Her mom said, "No, not today." Lily was very sad. She started to weep. Her tears fell down like rain. She really wanted the big, colorful lollipop. A kind man saw Lily weep. He felt bad for her. He bought the big, colorful lollipop and gave it to Lily. Lily stopped weeping. She was very happy. She said, "Thank you!" to the kind man. Lily and her mom went home with the unique lollipop, and Lily had a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch films on his TV. One day, he found a new film to watch. The film had a big number on it. The number was ten. Tim thought that this film would be fun to watch. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to come over and watch the film with him. Sam came over, and they started to watch the film. They saw a big, green, and disgusting bug on the screen. Tim said, "Eww, that bug is so disgusting!" Sam agreed, and they both laughed. As they watched the film, the disgusting bug grew bigger and bigger. It started to come out of the TV! Tim and Sam were very scared. The big, disgusting bug chased them around the house. Tim and Sam could not get away from the bug. In the end, the big, disgusting bug caught them, and they were never seen again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. They played in the sand and swam in the water. Tim saw a smelly jellyfish on the sand. He wanted to hold it, but his mom said, "No, it can hurt you." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. He looked at the jellyfish from far away. It was so smelly, but very pretty too. Tim's dad said, "Let's go find something else to play with," and they all went to find nice shells and rocks. They found a big, pretty shell and Tim held it in his hand. He was happy to have a new thing to play with. The smelly jellyfish stayed on the sand, and Tim, his mom, and his dad went home with their new shell to remember their fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to draw patterns on the ground with her chalk. One day, while she was drawing, a confused bird flew down and landed on her drawing. Lily looked at the bird and said, "Why are you on my pattern?" The bird looked back at her and did not say anything. It just stood there, looking more confused. Suddenly, the bird started to weep. Lily was surprised and did not know what to do. She tried to make the bird feel better by giving it some of her bread. The bird stopped weeping and ate the bread. It was happy now! The bird then flew up into the sky and turned into a beautiful rainbow. Lily smiled as she looked at the rainbow, knowing that she had made a new friend.
