Title: The Green Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little green frog. The frog lived in a pond. The pond was his home. He liked to play all day. He had many friends. One day, the sun was very hot. The pond started to get dry. The frog was sad. He wanted to find a new home. So, he hopped away to look for a new pond. The frog saw a big tree. It was a good place to hide. He hid under the tree. He saw many new friends. They played hide and seek. The frog was happy in his new home.

Sandy had a little kitten. She was glad to have such a cute, furry pet. One day, Sandy was playing with the kitten. She reached out to rub its soft fur. The kitten purred and closed its eyes. Sandy smiled, feeling very happy. Just then, Sandy's mom called out, "Come here Sandy, time to go home now!" Sandy sighed, reluctantly ending the playtime with her kitten. She hugged it tight and said goodbye. The kitten opened its eyes and meowed sadly. Sandy smiled, feeling glad she was able to give the kitten such a nice rub. She said goodbye one last time, then ran home with her mom.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little seed. The little seed was very sad because it did not bloom like the other flowers. It wanted to be pretty and colorful too. One day, the sun made a promise to the little seed. The sun said, "I will help you bloom and be pretty like the other flowers." The little seed was very happy and thanked the sun. The sun shined every day to make it easy for the little seed to grow. After some time, the little seed started to grow and bloom. The little seed became a pretty flower with many colors. The other flowers in the garden were very happy to see the new flower bloom. And they all lived happily in the big garden.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a playful cat. The cat loved to blink her eyes at the birds and chase them. She also liked to play with the lumber that fell from the trees. The cat had many friends in the forest, and they all played together. One day, the playful cat saw a big pile of lumber. She blinked her eyes and ran to play with it. The pile of lumber was very high, but the cat didn't care. She climbed up and up, having fun and laughing. But then, the lumber started to move. It fell down with a big crash. The playful cat couldn't get away in time, and the lumber fell on her. The cat's friends came running, but they couldn't help her. The playful cat was gone, and everyone was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars. One day, he wanted to attach a big red balloon to his favorite car. He thought it would be fun to watch the car with the balloon zoom around. Tim asked his mom for her opinion. "Mom, do you think my car will be successful with the balloon?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, I think it will be successful. Give it a try!" So Tim attached the big red balloon to his favorite car. He let it go and watched as the car zoomed around the room. The balloon made the car go faster and Tim was very happy. His car was successful with the balloon and Tim played with it all day long.

One day, little Lucy was walking through the forest. She was close to a beautiful lake. As she gazed into the lake, she saw some small fish swimming. Suddenly, Lucy yawned. In the distance she heard a gentle voice calling her name. It was her mother. "Lucy come here," her mother called. But the lake was very inviting. She felt too relaxed to move. "Please baby, don't stay there. That lake can be harsh," warned her mother. That awoke Lucy from her trance. Gathering her strength, she started walking toward her mother. When she got there, her mother hugged her tightly. "Thank goodness you listened to me," said her mother with a sigh of relief. Lucy smiled and yawned once again. She was feeling sleepy now. Together, they headed home and Lucy had a peaceful nap.
