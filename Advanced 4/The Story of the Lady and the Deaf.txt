Title: The Story of the Lady and the Deaf

Once upon a time, there was a lady walking down the road. She was wearing a big and beautiful dress. Suddenly, a big truck went past her and she felt embarrassed. She put her hands over her face. Just then, a deaf man saw her. He wanted to make her feel better. He ran up to her and showed her a big smile. It made the lady feel much better. The lady and the deaf man started to talk. They laughed and talked for a long time. The lady had a great time talking to him. At the end of the day, the lady said goodbye to the deaf man. He laughed and waved as she walked away. She felt happy that she had met him and was no longer embarrassed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Penny. Penny had a bossy big sister who always told her what to do. Penny wanted to improve and be a good sister, so she tried to listen to her big sister. One day, Penny and her big sister went to the park. They played on the swings and the slide. Penny's big sister was still bossy, but Penny tried to be nice and have fun with her. In the end, Penny's big sister saw how hard Penny was trying to improve. She decided to be nicer and not so bossy. They both became better sisters and had lots of fun together.

One day, a little boy named Jack was walking near a zoo. Suddenly, he saw an elephant! He was so excited and ran up to it and wanted to pet it. Jack asked his mom if he could pay the zookeeper to ride the elephant. His mom said, "Yes, you can pay, but the elephant is very spoiled. Be careful!" When Jack got to the zookeeper, he said, "I want to pay to ride the elephant!" The zookeeper replied, "Sure, it will be five dollars!" Jack gave the man five dollars and the zookeeper put a blanket on the elephant for Jack to sit on. He climbed on the elephant and started petting it. The elephant was so spoiled that it jumped around a lot. Jack laughed and enjoyed riding the elephant. When he was done, Jack thanked the zookeeper and told him he had a lot of fun. The zookeeper smiled and said, "Enjoy the rest of your day!" Jack smiled, thanked the zookeeper again and said goodbye to the elephant. As he walked away, Jack knew he had made a spoiled elephant very happy with his five dollars.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Tim saw some green celery and wanted to buy it. His mom said, "Okay, let's get some." When they got home, Tim's mom asked him to send a message to his dad. She said, "Tell dad that we got celery for dinner." Tim thought it was difficult to write the message, but he tried his best. Later, dad came home with a big surprise. He had a big bag of celery too! Tim and his mom laughed. They had so much celery now. Tim said, "We can share our celery with our friends!" And they did. Everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to always try your best, even when it is difficult. And sharing with friends can make everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He put on his shoes and went to the park. At the park, he saw a big tree with yummy fruit. Tim wanted to get the fruit, so he tried to climb the tree. He tried and tried, but the tree would not yield the fruit. Then, Tim met a quiet girl named Sue. Sue was very shy and did not talk much. Tim asked Sue, "Can you help me get the fruit?" Sue nodded her head and together they tried to get the fruit from the tree. As they tried, a big bird came from the sky and took the fruit from the tree. Tim and Sue were surprised. They did not expect the bird to take the fruit. The bird then dropped the fruit in front of Tim and Sue. They were happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" They shared the fruit and became good friends.
