Title: Bob the Mechanic and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a chubby mechanic named Bob. He loved to fix cars and play with his dog, Max. One day, a little boy came to Bob's shop with his mom. The boy's toy car was broken, and he was very sad. "Can you fix my car, please?" the boy asked Bob. Bob smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's zip and fix it!" Bob took the toy car and quickly fixed it with his tools. The little boy's face lit up with joy when he saw his car working again. Bob and Max played with the little boy and his toy car for a while. They raced the cars and laughed a lot. The boy's mom was happy to see her son smiling again. When it was time to go home, the boy hugged Bob and Max. "Thank you for fixing my car," he said. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa was only three years old and she was very excited to go to school. One day, when Lisa was at school, her teacher said to her class, ‘Today I have a special message for everyone.’ Lisa was so excited to hear it. The teacher continued, ‘This message is about cleaning your hands, because germs can be very disgusting!’ Everyone said ‘oooh!’ and laughed and Lisa was very excited to listen. The teacher continued, ‘It is very important to wash your hands after you go to the toilet.’ Lisa said, ‘Yes, I will do that!’ Everybody said ‘yes’ and agreed to wash their hands after going to the toilet. Lisa was very happy that she had learnt an important message. She promised herself to always wash her hands after using the toilet.

Ben and Lily were hungry. They had no food at home. They saw a big house with a garden. They saw apples on a tree. They wanted to eat the apples. They climbed over the fence. They ran to the tree. They picked some apples. They bit the apples. They chewed the apples. They were sweet and juicy. A man came out of the house. He was angry. He shouted at them. "Hey, you! Get off my land! You are stealing my apples! You are ignorant and rude!" Ben and Lily were scared. They dropped the apples. They ran to the fence. They tried to climb over. But the man was faster. He grabbed them by their clothes. He dragged them to his house. He locked them in a dark room. He said, "You will stay here until I call the police. You will learn your lesson. You will never steal again." Ben and Lily cried. They hugged each other. They wished they had never seen the apples. They wished they had a shelter. They wished they had a friend. They were sorry and sad.

One day, a little owl was in the woods. The owl was very filthy because it was playing in the mud. The owl was happy and liked to play all day. The filthy owl met a big bird. The big bird did not understand why the owl was so dirty. The big bird wanted to help the owl, so they went to the river. The big bird told the owl to jump in the river to get clean. The owl jumped into the river and something unexpected happened. The filthy owl became a beautiful swan! The big bird was surprised and the swan was happy. Now, they both understood that the owl was a swan all along. They played together and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to gather fruit from the trees. It was easy for her because she was tall for her age. Every day, she would go out and pick the sweet fruit for her family. One sunny day, Amy went to the forest to gather fruit. She found a big tree with lots of fruit on it. She was very happy. She picked the fruit and put it in her basket. The fruit was red, yellow, and green. It smelled very good. When Amy went back home, she shared the fruit with her family. They all sat down and ate the fruit together. It was very yummy. Everyone was happy and thanked Amy for gathering the fruit. Amy felt proud and knew she had done a good thing for her family.
