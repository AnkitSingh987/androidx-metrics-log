Title: Jimmy and the Blanket

Jimmy saw the parade and was so excited. He ran to his mom and asked her "Can I go to the parade mommy?" "Not right now, Jimmy. It's too late," she said. "Please mommy, just for a little while?" begged Jimmy. His mom lent him her colorful blanket. She said, "Here, this will keep you warm, and you can use this to sit on too." Jimmy ran to the parade, spreading out his blanket on the ground and sitting on it. He watched the large, colorful balloons, and the dead flowers the people were giving each other. He was having so much fun that he stayed and watched the parade until it was dark. When the parade had gone, Jimmy walked back home with his blanket. He was very tired but had a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a boy and he wanted to discover something special. So, he dug in the garden until he found something underground. It was a fragile egg! The boy was so excited and he couldn't believe his eyes. He carefully picked up the egg and headed inside the house. He wanted to make sure the egg was safe. The boy carefully put the egg inside a box and placed it in a dark corner of his room. He asked his family to keep an eye on the egg, while he went off to play outside. Days passed and the boy forgot all about the egg. But one night, he heard a noise coming from the corner of his room... he quickly ran over to the box and opened it up. To his surprise, a little bird had hatched from the egg! The boy was so happy he had discovered something so special. He knew he had to look after the little bird and keep it safe. From then on, he and the little bird were the best of friends.

Mommy and baby were looking for the perfect spot to relax. Everywhere they looked, there was nothing that was clean enough. Baby started to cry, but Mommy had an idea. "What if we make our own spot? Let's go find a bed," Mommy told Baby. Mommy and Baby looked around until they found a bed. It was perfect! Mommy and Baby worked hard to make the bed comfortable. They covered it with clean sheets, plumped up the pillows, and made sure the duvet was fluffy. Now the bed was perfect for relaxing. "Now let's take a rest and relax," Mommy said. Baby proudly lay down on the bed and smiled. Then Mommy got in beside Baby, and soon both of them were fast asleep.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She had a pen in her hand and she was walking down the street. She noticed something strange. It was a pen, but it was filthy. She had never seen a pen in such bad condition. The girl decided to pick up the pen. It felt strange in her hand. Even though it had been filthy, it started to look better and better. The girl decided to keep the pen. She took it home and cleaned it. After she had finished cleaning, the pen was as good as new! The girl was so happy and she decided to keep it as a special reminder of all the good things that can happen when you take care of something.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat had a messy room. In the room, there was an anchor. One day, a little boy named Tim came to see the boat. Tim saw the messy room and wanted to help. He started to run and pick up the toys. He put the toys in a box. Tim saw the anchor and thought it was a toy too. Tim tried to move the anchor, but it was too heavy. He asked his mom for help. His mom helped him move the anchor. The room was not messy anymore. Tim and his mom were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw a girl with a broken toy. The girl was sad because her toy was broken. Tim wanted to help the girl. Tim said, "Hi, my name is Tim. Let me introduce myself." The girl smiled and said, "Hi Tim, my name is Sue." Tim asked Sue if she wanted to play with his toys. Sue said yes, and they played together all day. After playing, Tim and Sue were thirsty. Tim's mom gave them juice to drink. They both liked the juice. Tim and Sue became good friends. They played together every day and always shared their toys and juice.
