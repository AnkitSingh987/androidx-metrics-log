Title: The Story of the Tree and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little, hungry kitten. The kitten lived in a big, yellow house. It was a nice house, but the kitten was all alone. One day, the kitten went outside to find some food. It saw a big tree with yummy apples. The kitten looked up and said, "Please, tree, give me an apple." The tree shook and an apple fell down. The kitten ate the apple, but it was still hungry. The hungry kitten walked and walked until it found a little girl. The girl saw the kitten and said, "Oh, you are so hungry! Please, come home with me." The girl took the kitten home and gave it some food. The kitten was happy and not hungry anymore. They became the best of friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a poor man. He had a symbol that he held close in his heart every day. He kept it safe, never letting it go. One day the man wanted to feel clean. So he went to the lake to bathe. He made sure to keep his symbol in a safe spot while he washed away his dirt and grime. When he was done, the man felt brand new. He was happy and felt ready to take on the world. He smiled and with gratitude to the lake, he retrieved his symbol, grateful to have it close once more. The poor man took his happy feeling home with him and held his precious symbol close. He knew as long as he had it, his life would be good.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an ancient fish. This fish loved to swim all day in the pond. The pond was near a blueberry bush. The fish liked to watch the blueberries grow. One sunny day, a little girl named Mia came to the pond. She saw the fish swimming and said, "Hello, fish! Do you like to swim?" The fish nodded its head, showing that it liked to swim. Mia smiled and picked some blueberries from the bush. Mia sat near the pond and shared her blueberries with the fish. They became good friends. Every day, Mia would visit the fish and they would swim and eat blueberries together. They were very happy and had lots of fun in their little village.

Once upon a time, there was a long pool. In this pool, there lived a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim and play all day with his friends. One day, Fin saw a big fish do a new trick. The big fish jumped high out of the water and splashed back in. Fin wanted to learn how to do the trick too. He asked the big fish to teach him, and the big fish said yes. Fin tried and tried to learn the trick. At first, it was hard, but he did not give up. After many tries, Fin finally learned how to jump high and splash back into the long pool. All his friends cheered, and Fin was very happy. From that day on, Fin and his friends played and did tricks in the long pool together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat vegetables. Her favorite vegetable was a big, green, fit broccoli. Lily thought that eating fit vegetables made her strong and happy. One day, Lily's friend Timmy came to play. Timmy did not understand why Lily liked vegetables so much. He thought they were yucky. Lily wanted to help Timmy understand why vegetables were good. Lily showed Timmy her big, green, fit broccoli. She told him how it made her strong and happy. Timmy decided to try the broccoli. He took a small bite and smiled. Now Timmy understood why Lily liked vegetables. They were yummy and made them both strong and happy. From that day on, Timmy and Lily ate fit vegetables together and played happily ever after.

One day, a red ball went bounce, bounce, bounce down the street. It was a wet day and the ball made splashes in the puddles. The ball was having so much fun. A little boy saw the ball and said, "Ball, come play with me!" The ball bounced over to the boy, and they played together. They played catch and the ball went bounce, bounce, bounce in the air. As they were playing, a police car drove by. The police man saw them and stopped. He said, "Hello, boy and ball! Be careful playing in the wet street. It can be slippery." The boy and the ball said, "Thank you, police man!" They went to the park to play where it was safer. They had a fun day playing together, and the ball went bounce, bounce, bounce all day long.
