Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a berry tree. Tim thought the tree was very cool. He wanted to eat some berries. Tim acted like a monkey to reach the berries. As Tim was eating the berries, he saw a big bird. The bird was acting funny. It was dancing and singing. Tim laughed at the bird. He thought it was a funny show. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird took off its feathers. It was not a bird at all! It was a man in a bird suit. The man smiled at Tim and said, "Surprise! I am your dad!" Tim's dad was acting like a bird to make Tim laugh. They both laughed and ate more berries together.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow man. He lived in a small house. He liked to spin around and around. Every day, he would spin in his yard. The kids would watch and laugh. One day, the yellow man wanted to spin faster. He thought it would be fun. He spun and spun until he was very fast. The kids clapped and cheered for him. He was so happy. But then, the yellow man got dizzy. He could not stop spinning. He spun so fast that he fell down. The kids tried to help, but they could not. The yellow man was sad, and he never spun again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a toy box full of toys. She loved her toys, but her favorite was a small, red triangle. She played with it all the time. One day, she played with her triangle and felt very tired. She let out a big yawn. The next day, Mia could not find her red triangle. She looked everywhere for it. She felt sad and did not know what to do. While looking, she saw a dark corner in her room. Mia was a little scared, but she thought her triangle might be there. Mia took a deep breath and went to the dark corner. She looked around and found her red triangle. She was so happy! She hugged her triangle and promised to never lose it again. That night, Mia slept with her triangle by her side, and she did not yawn because she was not tired.

One day, a girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a mailbox that had a frown. Lucy was sad to see the mailbox frown. She thought, "I will make the mailbox happy." The next day, Lucy had a plan. She drew a big, happy smile on a piece of paper. She put it in her bag and went to the mailbox. Lucy was sure her plan would be successful. She was excited to see the mailbox smile. When Lucy got to the mailbox, she put the happy smile on it. The mailbox was now smiling! Lucy was very happy and so was the mailbox. The frown was gone, and now everyone who saw the mailbox would be happy too.

It was a great day outside. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Emily and her friend, Ed, were playing together in the park. Emily wanted to play a game, but Ed wanted to have a picnic. She said they should have a sausage picnic! Emily thought that was a great idea. Ed was very excited. He laughed, jumped and shouted "Let's go get the sausages!". Emily said "No, it's too hot outside. We should stay here and play". Ed didn't like that idea. He shouted even louder and ran off to get the sausages himself! Emily was angry and started to shout too. But then she thought it was a great idea and she ran after Ed. They had a great sausage picnic!

Once upon a time there was a very thoughtful little girl named Amy. She was always looking for ways to help others. One day, she noticed a hedge in the garden that was very tall and she had an idea. She asked her mom and dad if she could borrow some tools. Carefully, she started trimming the hedge. When she finished, she was surprised to reveal a secret garden! It was full of beautiful flowers and shrubs, and even some friendly bugs. Amy was so happy to have discovered such a wonderful surprise. She wanted to tell everyone about the secret garden, so she shared it with all of the other children in the neighbourhood. Soon the secret garden was filled with happy children laughing and playing. Amy was so proud of her thoughtful idea and everyone had a great time!
