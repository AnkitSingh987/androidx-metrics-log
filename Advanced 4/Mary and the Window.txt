Title: Mary and the Window

Mary toddled around the house, looking for something to do, when she noticed a big, bright light shining through the window. She curiously trudged to the window and saw a big, wide garden outside with all sorts of fun games and things to explore. Mary smiled and without a second thought, she rushed outside to have a look around. As Mary was walking around the garden, she met a friendly lady. Mary's eyes widened as she spotted her, and said in an excited voice, "Hello there! Who are you?". The lady responded with a gentle smile and said, "My name is Laura. Are you here by yourself?". Mary answered, "Yes, I am.". Laura then said with a serious tone, "Well then, would you like to join me for a tea party?". Mary excitedly clapped her hands in glee and said, "Yes, please!". Laura gently smiled and took Mary's hand, and both of them went off to the tea party. Mary felt like a princess that day, with her very own tea party. She wished it would never end! At the end of the tea party, Laura gave Mary a warm hug before she went home. She then invited Mary to come back the next day and have another tea party. Mary beamed with delight and said, "I'll be here!".

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to write in her diary every day. She wrote about her friends, her toys, and her fun days. One day, Sue found her diary was broken. The pages were torn and the cover was bent. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Sue wanted to keep writing about her days. Sue had an idea. She asked her mom for help. Her mom gave her a new diary. Sue was happy again. She started to write in her new diary about her friends, her toys, and her fun days. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. Tom was very attractive. He had a big dog named Max. They lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Tom and Max went into the woods to find food. They saw a big tree with a lot of apples. Tom shook the tree, and many apples fell down. "Yum! We can eat these apples," said Tom. As they were eating, they heard a sound. A little rabbit came out from behind a bush. "Hello, I am Remy the rabbit. Can I have some apples too?" asked the rabbit. "Of course, Remy! Let's all eat together," said Tom. So Tom, Max, and Remy the rabbit ate the apples and became good friends.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to make a pizza. He asked his mom, "Can we make a pizza today?" His mom said, "Yes, we can make a pizza for dinner." Tim and his mom started to make the pizza. They put sauce, cheese, and pepperoni on the pizza. But Tim's dog, Max, was upset. He wanted to help make the pizza too. Max jumped up and tried to put a bone on the pizza. Suddenly, the pizza started to wiggle and shake! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The pizza turned into a magic pizza that could talk. The pizza said, "Thank you for adding the bone. Now, I can be a pizza for people and dogs!" Tim, his mom, and Max all laughed and enjoyed the magic pizza together. And from that day on, they always made sure to add a bone for Max when they made pizza.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Elly. Elly was very fast. She loved to run and play with her friends in the jungle. One day, Elly met a new friend named Timmy the Turtle. Timmy was slow, but he wanted to play too. Elly said, "Welcome, Timmy! Let's play together!" Timmy was happy to play with Elly. They played all day long. Elly would run fast, and then wait for Timmy to catch up. Timmy would laugh and say, "You are so fast, Elly!" They had lots of fun and became best friends. And they played together every day in the jungle.
