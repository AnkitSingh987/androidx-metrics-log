Title: Lily the Girl and the Mom

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the grocery with her mom. They needed to get food for their home. Lily liked going to the grocery because she could help her mom. She felt like a big girl when she helped. At the grocery, Lily's mom gave her a number. The number was two. Lily had to find two apples for her mom. Lily felt confused because there were so many apples. She did not know which ones to pick. Lily's mom saw that she was confused. She helped Lily find two big, red apples. Lily was happy that she could help her mom. They finished getting their food and went back home. Lily could not wait to go to the grocery again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clumsy dog named Bobo. He loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw his ball grow and increase in size. It was a mystery to him. He did not know why the ball was getting bigger. Bobo went to his friend, a small cat named Mimi. He asked her, "Mimi, do you know why my ball is getting bigger?" Mimi looked at the ball and said, "I don't know, Bobo. It is a mystery to me too." Together, they decided to ask their wise friend, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl looked at the ball and said, "Oh, it's just air inside the ball that is making it increase in size. Don't worry, it will stop soon." Bobo and Mimi were happy to solve the mystery. They played with the big ball all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time in a little town, there lived a sad girl named Lily. She felt miserable every day. One day, a kind lady named Sue came to visit Lily. Sue said, "Lily, let's do yoga. It will help you feel better." Lily did not know what yoga was, but she wanted to try it. So, they went to a big room where other kids were doing yoga too. Lily and Sue started doing yoga. They stretched their arms and legs. They made funny shapes with their bodies. Soon, Lily started to feel happy. She was not miserable anymore. Lily said, "Thank you, Sue. Yoga helped me feel better. I want to do it every day to prevent feeling sad." From that day on, Lily did yoga every day. She was not miserable anymore. She was a happy girl who loved to do yoga with her friend Sue.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She loved to play on the swings and slide. While playing, she saw a stranger sitting on a bench. The stranger looked sad and lonely. Lily wanted to make the stranger feel better. She walked up to him and gave him a big hug. The stranger smiled and said, "Thank you, but I am sorry, I am not feeling well today." Lily didn't know that the stranger was sick. Later that day, she started feeling sick too. Her mom took her to the doctor, and the doctor said she got sick from hugging the stranger. The bad ending of this story teaches us a moral value: always be careful with strangers, even if you want to help them. It is important to stay safe and ask a grown-up before talking to someone you don't know.

One day, a little girl named Mia went into the kitchen. She wanted to get a cookie. The kitchen was very noisy. Mia could hear pots and pans and the big oven making noise. She felt happy because she liked the sounds. Mia looked for the cookie jar. She saw it on the table. But when she opened it, there were no cookies inside! Mia felt sad. She wanted a cookie so much. She looked around the kitchen to find one. Just then, Mia heard a noise behind her. She turned around and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog had a cookie in its mouth! The dog gave the cookie to Mia. Mia felt happy again. She never knew the dog was in the kitchen, but now they could be friends and share the cookie.
