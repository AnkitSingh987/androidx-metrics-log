Title: The Park of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He had zero friends to play with. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue playing alone in the park. Tim went to Sue and asked, "Can I play with you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played in the park all day and had lots of fun. Tim was not miserable anymore. He learned that if you ask, you can make new friends. And from that day on, Tim and Sue were the best of friends. The moral of the story is: don't be afraid to ask, because it can lead to wonderful things.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady who lived in a small house. She always wore mittens on her hands. One day, she lost one of her mittens while walking in the park. She looked and looked, but could not find it. A little girl saw the lonely mitten on the ground. She picked it up and thought, "I will find the person who lost this mitten." The girl asked people in the park, "Is this your mitten?" They all said no and would shrug their shoulders. Finally, the little girl saw the elderly lady with only one mitten. The girl asked, "Is this your mitten?" The lady smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" The elderly lady was so happy to have her mitten back. The little girl felt good for helping someone find their lost thing.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big box. She said, "I wonder what is in this box?" She tried to open it, but it was too heavy. She asked her friend Tom to help her. Tom said, "Let's open the box together!" They opened the box and saw many fit clothes inside. They were so happy! They tried on the clothes and looked very nice. They played and laughed all day, wearing the new clothes. But then, a little cat jumped out of the box! They did not see the cat before. The cat said, "Thank you for letting me out of the box!" Lily and Tom were very surprised. They did not know that cats could talk. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, James and Amy. They were both very thoughtful. One day, Amy offered James something special. She said, “I have something for you. Here, take it.” James was very curious, so he opened it. Inside was a beautiful red lip. He smiled and asked, “What is this?” Amy replied, “It's a special lip. It will help you feel better. Whenever you need a hug, just press and kiss it!” James was very happy and thanked Amy. From then on, he always kept the lip close to him. Whenever he felt down, he would press it, and he would feel much better. James was very appreciative for Amy's thoughtful gift. He thanked her for being such a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. He lived in a rich green forest. The bear loved to play and eat yummy food. One day, the bear met a small rabbit. The rabbit was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the bear. The rabbit said, "I lost my toy." The bear wanted to help the rabbit. They looked for the toy together. The bear and the rabbit looked all day. Then, the bear saw something under a bush. He moved the bush and revealed the toy. The rabbit was so happy! They played together and became best friends. And they lived happily in the rich green forest.

One day, a small boy was walking through the field full of hay. He was very careless, jumping over the hay and feeling the softness between his small fingers. The boy ventured further and further until he reached a big rock. It was much bigger than the boy and he could barely move it. He pressed it with his hands and feet, trying to make it budge, but there was no luck. At last, he decided to go back home. On the way, he remembered something he had heard his parents say: "If you push something hard enough, it usually yields." So he went back to the rock and tried pushing it with all his might. He strained and strained until at last it yielded to his strength and moved aside. The boy felt proud of himself and excited for what he may find. To his surprise, a secret path had appeared behind the rock. The boy grinned as he realized that his carelessness had been rewarded. From that day on, the boy would often go down the secret path, feeling proud that he had found such an exciting spot.
