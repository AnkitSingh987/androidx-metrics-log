Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim went to a big restaurant with his mom and dad. It was a very special day. They sat at a table and mom gave Tim the menu to look at. Tim saw many yummy things to eat. Tim's mom said, "What do you want to eat, Tim?" Tim pointed at a picture of a big burger. It looked so good. His mom smiled and said, "That's a great choice!" Tim was very excited for his big burger. When the waiter came with the food, he had a heavy tray. He put the big burger in front of Tim. But suddenly, the waiter tripped and the burger fell on the floor! Tim was very sad. The waiter said, "I'm so sorry, Tim!" He kneeled down to pick up the burger. Then, the waiter went to get Tim a new big burger. Tim was happy again when he got his new big burger. He ate it all up. It was the best burger he ever had. And that day, Tim learned that sometimes things go wrong, but it's okay because they can be fixed.

Jill and Tom were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They were very happy with their snowman. "Look, our snowman is so nice!" Jill said. "Yes, he is. But we have to be careful. The sun is very bright today. He might melt." Tom said. They went inside to have some hot chocolate. They left their snowman in the yard. But Sam, the dog, was very curious. He saw the snowman and wanted to play with him. He ran to the snowman and jumped on him. He bit the hat, the scarf and the carrot nose. He was very careless. He did not know he was hurting the snowman. When Jill and Tom came back, they saw their snowman. He was very small and sad. His hat, his scarf and his nose were gone. He had melted a lot. "Oh no, what happened to our snowman?" Jill cried. "Who did this to him?" Tom asked. They looked around and saw Sam. He had the hat, the scarf and the nose in his mouth. He wagged his tail and barked. "Sam, you bad dog! You ruined our snowman!" Jill scolded. "Sam, you solved the mystery. But you were very careless. You have to say sorry to our snowman." Tom said. Sam looked at them and dropped the hat, the scarf and the nose. He licked the snowman and whined. "Maybe he is sorry. But he still has to learn to be gentle." Jill said. "Maybe we can make another snowman. But we have to watch out for Sam." Tom said. They hugged their snowman and Sam. They decided to make another snowman tomorrow. They hoped he would not melt.

Once upon a time there was a magical girl called Ivy. She loved to explore the land and use her graceful moves to rub amazing things. One day, Ivy was exploring a new land when she saw a beautiful lake. She jumped down to take a closer look and noticed that the lake was full of sparkly sand. She knelt down and started to rub the beautiful sand between her fingers and it felt wonderfully soft. She smiled as she carried on rubbing the sand, when suddenly it came to life and started to jump around! Ivy was so surprised she dropped her handful of sand and watched as it danced and sparkled. Ivy could hear a voice coming from the sand, so she picked it up again and listened carefully. The sand whispered ""Be graceful with me, feel me and rub me and I will make your dreams come true". Ivy was so excited, she began to gently rub the sand and feel it's magical properties. Sure enough, wonderful things began to happen as the magical sand filled the air of the magical land!

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bunny named Breezy. She was always looking around and observing things. One day, Breezy hopped and hopped until she found a big spring. She was so excited that she started to perform a special dance. Breezy hopped around and around, higher and higher. She didn't stop until she reached the tip of the spring. Then she smiled because she had done a really good job and it made her happy. After that, Breezy hopped away to her home in the meadow. She was sure to tell all her friends about her special performance. And they all said they were very impressed! The End.
