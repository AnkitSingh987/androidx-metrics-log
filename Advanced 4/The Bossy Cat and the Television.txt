Title: The Bossy Cat and the Television

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a bossy cat. The cat loved to watch television all day long. It would sit on the couch and tell everyone what to do. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit the cat. She saw the cat watching television and wanted to play. So, she picked up a toy and started to wave it in front of the cat. The cat looked at Lily and then looked back at the television. Lily did not give up. She waved the toy even more, and the bossy cat finally looked away from the television. They began to play together, and the cat was not so bossy anymore. They had lots of fun, and from that day on, the cat liked to play with Lily more than watching television.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little girl who lived with her mum and dad. She had two big brothers, two cats and one small rag. The rag was her very best friend, and she would take it everywhere. One day, she decided to take the rag for a walk in the park. But when she blinked, something very strange happened. Suddenly, the rag came to life! The little girl was so surprised, she could hardly speak. But the rag just smiled and said: "Let's go for an adventure!" The little girl held the rag's hand, and they began walking. They made their way through the park, until they reached an old tree. And as they both blinked, the tree suddenly became as tall as a mountain. "Wow!" said the little girl. Suddenly, they heard a noise. It was two men talking. They were talking about how poor the little girl was. The little girl was very sad at what she heard. But then the rag looked at the girl and said: "It's ok. You don't need money to be happy. You just need your friends." The little girl smiled and hugged the rag tightly. Then they went home to their mum and dad, hand-in-hand.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was a very reliable friend. He loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played all day long. One day, Fluffy and Sue were playing a game called "guess". Sue would think of something, and Fluffy would try to guess what it was. Sue said, "I am thinking of a color." Fluffy thought for a moment and then said, "Is it purple?" Sue laughed and said, "Yes, you guessed it!" As they kept playing, Fluffy started to act funny. He was not guessing right anymore. Sue was worried. She asked, "Fluffy, are you okay?" Fluffy looked at her and said, "I have a secret. Can you guess what it is?" Sue thought and thought but could not guess. Finally, Fluffy said, "I am not just a purple cat. I am also a magic cat!" Sue was so surprised! Fluffy showed her his magic powers. He could make things fly and change colors. Sue and Fluffy had even more fun playing together after that. They were the best of friends, and Sue knew she could always count on her reliable, magic, purple cat.

Once there was a girl named Lucy. She was very tidy and liked to keep her room clean. Every morning, the alarm woke Lucy up, and she promised herself to keep her room neat and tidy. Each day she made her bed and put her dolls in their special places. She tucked away her sweaters and folded her pajamas. She even vacuumed the soft carpet to make sure it was always neat. When it was time for bed, Lucy checked that everything was tidy. Her pillows were fluffy and her books were all lined up neatly. She was satisfied that her room was perfect. Every morning when the alarm went off, Lucy remembered to keep her room neat and tidy. She kept her promise and it made her feel very proud. And Tammy the Turtle always praised her for being so tidy!

One day, a big man named Tom went to the store. He needed a hammer. Tom was anxious because he had never used a hammer before. He wanted to fix a broken chair at home. At the store, Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I know how to use a hammer. I can help you." Tom was surprised. He asked, "Can you really help me?" Sue nodded her head and said, "Yes, I can." Tom and Sue went to Tom's house. They worked together to fix the chair. The chair was fixed, and Tom was happy. He did not expect a little girl to know how to use a hammer. Tom thanked Sue, and they became good friends.
