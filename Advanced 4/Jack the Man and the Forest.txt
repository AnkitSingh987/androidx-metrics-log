Title: Jack the Man and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a man named Jack. He was walking through a dark forest, and he found a crystal on the ground. It was a beautiful crystal and it was glowing. Jack picked it up and said, “Wow! What is this?” Just then, a big hawk came out of the trees and started to fly towards Jack. Jack was so scared that he picked up a stick and started to shoot at the hawk. The hawk flew away, and Jack was safe. Jack was happy and he held the crystal up to the sky. Suddenly, the crystal started to shine brighter and brighter. Jack was amazed and he said, “It’s like magic!” Suddenly, a voice spoke out from the crystal. The voice said, “I am grateful that you saved me, Jack. I will grant you one wish.” Jack's face lit up and he said, “I wish that I could stay in this forest forever.” The crystal glowed brightly and the voice said, “Your wish is granted.” Jack was happy and he smiled. He looked up at the dark sky and said, “Thank you, crystal.”

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy was a creative girl who loved to make things out of waste. She would use old boxes, papers, and clothes to make beautiful toys and decorations for her room. One day, Amy's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw all the amazing things Amy had made and wanted to make something too. Amy said, "I can teach you how to make things with waste, it's fun and helps keep our town clean!" So, Amy and Tom spent the day making a big castle out of old boxes and papers. They had so much fun and were proud of their work. When they showed the castle to their friends, everyone wanted to learn how to make things with waste too. Soon, all the kids in the town were making creative things with waste. The town became cleaner and more beautiful. The grown-ups were so happy and proud of the kids. They even had a big party to celebrate their hard work. The moral of the story is that we can all make a difference by being creative and using waste to make something beautiful. It's not only fun, but it also helps keep our world clean and happy.

Once there was a yellow bird who lived alone in a big tree. One day he was very hungry so he flew around looking for something to eat. He finally found a delicious looking sandwich on the ground. The sandwich was so yummy that he couldn't resist it. He went down and touched the sandwich with his beak. He ate the sandwich and was very happy. But then he suddenly felt very sick. He realized that he had eaten something bad and became very sad. The bird started flying away, feeling sick and weak. He had eaten a bad yellow sandwich and it made him very sick. He knew he made a mistake, and he regretted it very much. He flew away never to come back again and he was never the same. He had made the wrong decision to touch the yellow sandwich and it turned out badly.

One day, a big, red car was driving down the road. The car had four round wheels. The sun was shining, and the road was very dry. The car was happy because it liked to drive on dry roads. The car saw a little girl sitting under a tree. She was trying to read a book, but she could not read the big words. The car stopped and asked, "Can I help you read your book?" The little girl looked at the car and smiled. "Yes, please," she said. The car used its wheel to turn the pages of the book. The girl looked at the pictures and the car read the words. They read the book together until the sun went down. The little girl was very happy. She thanked the car for helping her read the book. The car was happy too, because it made a new friend. The car and the little girl waved goodbye, and the car drove away on its four round wheels.
