Title: Lily and Ben and the Duck

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play at the pond. They saw many ducks there, swimming and quacking. Lily and Ben wanted to feed the ducks, so they brought some bread from home. But Lily and Ben did not know how to share the bread. Lily wanted to give all the bread to one duck, and Ben wanted to give all the bread to another duck. They argued and pulled the bread from each other. "Stop it!" said a big duck, who was watching them. "You are not nice to the ducks or to each other. You should learn to share and be organized. Look at us, we take turns to eat the bread, and we do not fight. That is how we are happy and healthy." Lily and Ben felt ashamed. They said sorry to the big duck and to each other. They tore the bread into small pieces and threw them to the pond. They watched the ducks eat the bread and swim together. They smiled and held hands. Lily and Ben learned a lesson that day. They learned to share and be organized, and to be kind to the ducks and to their friends. They had more fun at the pond, and the ducks liked them more.

One day, a beautiful cat named Lily went for a walk. She gazed at the sky and saw the big, bright sun. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. Lily saw her friend, Max the dog. Max was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Lily. Max said, "I lost my teaspoon. My mom is mad at me." Lily wanted to help Max find his teaspoon. They looked and looked for the teaspoon. Then, they saw a bird flying with something shiny in its beak. It was Max's teaspoon! The bird dropped the teaspoon in the grass. Max was happy, and his mom was not mad anymore. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a modern opera show. In this show, there was a singer named Sally. Sally loved to sing and dance. She wanted to do well in the opera. She wanted to succeed. One day, Sally was on stage singing. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog ran on stage! The dog wanted to play with Sally. Sally was surprised, but she kept singing. The people watching the opera loved the dog. They clapped and cheered. Sally and the dog became friends. They danced and sang together. In the end, Sally and the dog both succeeded. The modern opera was a big hit!

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Kitty. Kitty loved chocolate more than anything in the world. She would always want to eat chocolate for her snack. One day, Kitty heard about a big chocolate store in the town. She wanted to go there and eat all the chocolate she could. So, she made a plan to visit the store the next day. The next day, Kitty went to the store and saw lots of chocolate. She was so happy! She ate and ate until she was full. Then, she took some chocolate home to share with her friends. They all enjoyed the chocolate together, and Kitty became even more famous for her love of chocolate.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She was always glad and loved to play. One day, her mom told her to behave and not to play on the stairs. Amy said, "Okay, Mommy." Amy played with her toys in her room. But soon, she wanted to play on the stairs. She thought, "Mommy is not here. I can play on the stairs just a little bit." Amy started to play on the stairs, even though her mom told her not to. As Amy played on the stairs, something unexpected happened. A big, soft pillow came down the stairs. The pillow was magic and could talk! The pillow said, "Amy, you should behave and listen to your mom. Stairs can be not safe for little girls like you." Amy was surprised but knew the pillow was right. She thanked the pillow and went back to her room to play with her toys. From that day on, Amy always behaved and never played on the stairs again.
