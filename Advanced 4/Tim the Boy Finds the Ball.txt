Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play ball. One day, Tim went to the park with his ball. He was very careful with his ball because he did not want to lose it. At the park, Tim met a big boy named Sam. Sam wanted to play ball too. They decided to play a game. The game was to see who could strike the ball the farthest. The one who did would get the win. Tim and Sam took turns to strike the ball. Tim was very careful when he hit the ball. But Sam was not careful. When it was Sam's turn, he hit the ball very hard. The ball went very, very far. Sam got the win. But the ball was lost. Tim was sad because he lost his ball. The end.

Once upon a time there was a thin beetle. He was walking along a path, collecting pebbles to polish. He was searching all around, picking up the shiniest stones. Then, he came across a fat, slimy toad. The toad didn't want the beetle to take the pebbles, and he tried to scare the beetle away. Frightened, he quickly hopped away, searching for another spot. Finally, he found the perfect place to finish his task. There were so many pebbles he could polish, and they were all so shiny! He was busy at work, when suddenly the same fat toad appeared! He was even angrier this time. The beetle didn't know what to do, so he quickly scurried away. He ran as fast as he could, and kept running until he was safe and sound. The thin beetle was very sad, but at least he was safe.

One day, Tom and his friend, Sam, were watching a television show. They liked the television a lot. They saw a big, bad wolf on the screen. The bad wolf was scary, but they were brave boys. Tom and Sam wanted to discuss the bad wolf. They talked about how big and bad he was. They were not scared of the bad wolf. They were sure they could make the bad wolf go away if he came to their home. Suddenly, the bad wolf jumped out of the television! Tom and Sam were very surprised. But the bad wolf was not bad at all. He was a good wolf who wanted to make friends. They all played together and had a lot of fun. The bad wolf was not bad anymore, and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toy spoon. One day, Lucy's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw Lucy's spoon and wanted to play with it too. But Lucy didn't want to share her spoon. This made Sam very sad. Lucy's mom saw the problem and tried to explain to Lucy that it is fair to share with friends. She said, "Lucy, when you share your spoon with Sam, you both can have fun together." Lucy thought about it and decided to share her spoon with Sam. Now, Lucy and Sam were both happy and played with the spoon together. They took turns and made pretend soup for each other. In the end, they both learned that sharing is fair and fun. And they played happily ever after.

One day, a little deer was walking in the woods. The deer was looking for food. It saw a big tree with tasty leaves. The deer wanted to eat the leaves, but they were too high. The deer needed help. A kind bird saw the deer and said, "I can help you get the leaves. You need to be safe, so I will fly up and get them for you." The bird flew up and picked the leaves for the deer. The deer was very happy. The deer and the bird became friends. They played together and helped each other when they needed it. They learned that it is important to help others and to be safe. And they lived happily in the woods.

Ricky was a little boy of three. He loved everything about the springtime. Everywhere he looked he saw beautiful flowers and trees starting to bloom. One day, Ricky asked his mum “What is so good about springtime?” His mum smiled and said, “Ah! Spring is a very exciting time when nature comes alive!” Ricky was so excited that he ran outside to explore. He saw a bumble bee buzzing around a nearby flower and a worm wriggling in the soil. Then, he noticed some hidden petals behind a bush. He carefully moved the bush aside to see what was there. Suddenly, Ricky was delighted to find a little mouse! “Hello little mouse!” he said excitedly. Ricky lay down on the ground so he could get a closer look. They were both so excited to see each other that they started dancing around in circles. From that day, whenever Ricky was feeling upset, he would go out to the spot behind the bush and share a special moment with his newfound friend the mouse. Springtime had brought with it so many hidden surprises!
