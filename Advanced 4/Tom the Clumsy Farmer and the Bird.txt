Title: Tom the Clumsy Farmer and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy farmer named Tom. He had a farm with many animals. Tom was not good at his job. He always dropped food and fell in the mud. The animals did not like this. One day, a wise old bird came to the farm. The bird saw Tom's problem and said, "Tom, you need to improve. You are too clumsy. The animals need a better farmer." Tom felt sad, but he knew the bird was right. He wanted to be a better farmer for his animals. Tom asked the bird for help. The bird said, "First, you must take your time. Do not rush. Be careful with the food and watch where you step." Tom listened and tried his best to improve. Slowly, he became less clumsy. The animals were happy, and Tom was proud. He thanked the wise old bird for helping him become a better farmer.

One day, a rabbit went out to play. The rabbit liked to hop and run. The sun was hot and the ground was dry. The rabbit saw a big tree and thought, "I can find shade under the tree." The rabbit hopped under the tree and saw a small hole. The rabbit thought, "I wonder what's inside?" The rabbit shrugged and went in. It was dark, but the rabbit was not scared. Inside the hole, the rabbit found a big pile of yummy food. The rabbit ate and ate. The rabbit was happy. The rabbit knew that the dry ground and the big tree led to a great surprise.

One day, a little girl named Jill was walking in the park. She was very happy, skipping and singing. Suddenly, she stumbled on a rock and felt saying "Ouch!". She saw her friend Mary passing by and stopped her. "Hi Mary," Jill said, feeling a little embarrassed. "Hi Jill! What's wrong?" Mary asked. "I stumbled on this rock," Jill said, pointing at it. Mary smiled. "That's okay, sometimes we stumble on things. Come on, let's go find a fun place to play together," she said. Jill and Mary went and played at the playground. Jill had a lot of fun and felt happy all day. She never forgot her tumble, but it was okay, because she had a good friend to help her up.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big box with many toys. One day, he found a new toy in the box. It was a book with many symbols. Tim did not know what the symbols meant, so he asked his mom. His mom looked at the book and saw the symbols. She knew the answer and said, "These symbols are letters, Tim. They make words, and the words tell a story." Tim was happy to learn about the symbols and wanted to know more. As Tim and his mom read the book, they came across a story about a small bird. The bird had a hurt wing and could not fly. The other animals in the forest helped the bird and took care of it. Soon, the bird's wing was not hurt anymore, and it could fly again. Tim loved the story and felt happy that the bird was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing in the park when he saw a big red ball. He was so happy to find the ball that he forgot to remember something important. As Tim played with the ball, he felt a little shock. He looked around and saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Tim, you forgot something important! You need to remember to ask if the ball belongs to someone else." Tim thought for a moment and said, "You're right, Sam. I should remember to ask." So, Tim and Sam went around the park asking if anyone lost a big red ball. Finally, they found a little girl who was sad because she lost her ball. Tim gave the ball back to the girl, and she was very happy. Tim and Sam felt good because they remembered to do the important thing. From that day on, Tim always remembered to ask if something belonged to someone else before taking it.
