Title: The Dog and the Ball

One day, a playful dog went for a jog in the forest. He liked to run and jump. The forest was big and full of trees. The dog was very happy. As the dog ran, he saw a big ball. He loved to play with balls. The dog picked up the ball with his mouth and ran some more. He was having so much fun. At the end of the day, the playful dog was tired. He went back to his home and gave the ball to his friend, a little girl. They both played with the ball and had a great time. The dog loved his jog in the forest and playing with his friend.

It was a bright, sunny day and little Elina was very anxious. She wanted to go to the playground and play, but her parents told her she had to stay with her baby brother. "He's an infant, Elina," they said, "we need you to help look after him." Elina didn't want to and let out a big sigh. "Oh please, can I play?" she asked again, but her parents just smiled. Elina sat down next to the baby and started to sing a soft lullaby. Soon enough, the infant stopped crying and fell asleep in Elina's arms. She was content and happy and couldn't wait to go play. Suddenly, a loud thunder rumbled in the distance. Elina's eyes widened, and she wondered what would happen next. She held the baby close to her chest and anxiously waited for something to happen. The thunder grew closer and closer, until a heavy storm started to pour down on the playground. Elina held the baby, who was now starting to cry, but she didn't mind. When the storm finally passed, Elina set her baby brother down and playfully ran off the the playground, happy and relieved that she had kept him safe.

Once upon a time, there was a big oasis. In the oasis, there lived many animals. They were all friends and liked to share. One day, a new animal came to the oasis. The new animal was very big and looked anxious. The other animals were scared, but they wanted to share their oasis with the new friend. The big, anxious animal did not know how to share. It drank all the water and ate all the food. The other animals were sad and thirsty. The big animal did not learn to share, and the oasis was gone. The animals had to leave their home.

One day, a little bird was sitting on a branch. The bird was troubled. It could not find its mom. It began to cry. "Mom, where are you?" the little bird called. A big bird heard the little bird's cry and came to help. "Why are you troubled, little bird?" the big bird asked. The little bird said, "I can't find my mom. Can you help me?" The big bird told the little bird to listen carefully. As they listened, they heard a soft sound. It was the little bird's mom! The big bird flew up high and saw her on a branch far away. The big bird told the little bird, "Your mom is on that branch over there!" The little bird was so happy and flew to its mom. But as it got closer, it saw that it was not its mom, but a kind squirrel who was making the soft sound. The squirrel saw the troubled little bird and said, "Don't worry, I will help you find your mom." They all listened together, and soon they heard the real mom's call. The little bird, the big bird, and the kind squirrel all went to find the mom. The little bird was so happy to be with its mom again, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with a girl named Sally. Sally loved to eat yummy food, and she kept it all in a big refrigerator. Tom liked to eat too, but he would worry if he could not find any food. One day, Sally went to play outside, and Tom was alone in the house. Tom was very hungry, so he went to look for food. He looked all around, but he could not find any food. Tom started to worry. He thought, "Oh no! I can't find any food. What will I do?" Tom went to the refrigerator and tried to open it. He pushed and pulled, but he could not open it. Tom was sad and worried. Just then, Sally came back inside the house. She saw Tom and asked, "Tom, what's wrong? Why are you worried?" Tom meowed and pointed to the refrigerator. Sally understood and said, "Oh, you're hungry! Don't worry, Tom. I'll help you." Sally opened the refrigerator and gave Tom some food. Tom was very happy, and he did not worry anymore. From that day on, Tom knew that Sally would always help him when he was hungry.
