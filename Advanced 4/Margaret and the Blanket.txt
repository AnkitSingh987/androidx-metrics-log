Title: Margaret and the Blanket

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Margaret. Margaret liked to fold her blankets and sheets very neatly. One day there was a very loud noise outside. It was terrible thunder! Margaret was very scared and ran to hide under her bed. "What was that?" she shouted. Her mom came in and said "It was thunder, sweetheart. Don't worry. It's not that bad." But Margaret felt very scared. She folded her blanket and sheets around her, to make a big blanket cave, and stayed under her bed. Every time the thunder boomed, it sounded so terrible. Still, Margaret waited until the thunder stopped, and then she peeked out. She was alright! She smiled, and thanked her mom for helping her feel safe, and they both were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big motorcycle on the road. It was shiny and loud. Tim wondered how it could go so fast. Tim went to his friend, Sue. He said, "Sue, I saw a big motorcycle today! It was shiny and loud. I wonder how it can go so fast." Sue thought for a moment. She said, "Maybe it has a special engine inside." Tim and Sue went to the park. They saw a delicate butterfly on a flower. Tim said, "Look, Sue! The butterfly is so delicate and pretty." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, it is. I wonder how it can fly with such delicate wings." They played in the park all day, talking about the big motorcycle and the delicate butterfly. They wondered about many things. Tim and Sue loved to learn and explore the world around them. And they always had fun, no matter what they were doing.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had a special trophy that meant a lot to him. He kept it in a safe place, so he could look at it whenever he wanted. One day he went out and left the trophy at home. He was so sad when he came back and saw it was gone. He asked all his friends if they knew what had happened to it, but no one could help him. Playing detective, the old man began to look everywhere he could think of. Finally, he decided the only way he could get his trophy back was to imagine where it had gone. So he sat and closed his eyes, imagining all sorts of places. But it was too late. The trophy had been taken by a thief. The old man never found his trophy again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a bench and sat down. He was tired and soon fell asleep. Tim started to dream. In his dream, he was in a big, fun world with lots of toys and friends. As Tim played with his new friends, he saw a big, careless dog running around. The dog knocked over toys and bumped into Tim's friends. Tim wanted to help, but he didn't know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, soft teddy bear came to life! The teddy bear hugged the careless dog and made it calm. The dog stopped running and played nicely with Tim and his friends. Tim was so happy! But then he woke up from his dream. He looked around and saw the bench and the park. Even though it was just a dream, Tim learned that love and care can help make things better.

Once upon a time there was a dog named Rock. He was very happy and always wagged his tail. He liked to take long naps in the sun and play all day. One day, Rock saw a flame in the sky. It was beautiful and he wanted to get closer to it. He ran and ran until he was close enough to feel the heat. He felt so warm and cozy and he wanted to stay close. Suddenly, Rock felt a drop of something cold falling on his head. He looked up and it was clean water! It was raining and the flame in the sky had gone away. Rock thought it was so cool and enjoyed the rain on a hot day. Rock was so tired from running and playing all day, so he lay down and took a nice long nap with the cool rain all around him.
