Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because it was a gloomy day. He wanted to play outside, but it was too wet. So, he stayed inside and looked out the window. Tim's uncle came to visit. He saw that Tim was sad. Uncle said, "Why don't we serve some cookies and milk? It will make you feel better." Tim smiled and helped his uncle get the cookies and milk. They sat down and ate together. As they ate, the sun came out, and the day was not gloomy anymore. Tim learned that even when things seem bad, doing something nice with someone you love can make everything better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. One sunny day, he went outside to kick his ball around. He kicked it high and low, left and right. While playing, Tim saw a poor little bird. The bird was cold and shivering. "Oh no, bird is cold!" said Tim. He wanted to help the bird, so he ran inside to find something to keep it warm. Tim found a soft wrap in his room. He picked it up and went back to the bird. "Here, bird, this will help you," said Tim. He wrapped the bird in the warm wrap. The bird felt better and chirped happily. Tim smiled as he watched the bird fly away. He felt good for helping his new friend. Then, Tim went back to kicking his ball, knowing he had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with a little girl named Sue. Sue loved Kitty very much, but sometimes Kitty was boring. Kitty would just lay in the sun all day and not want to play. One day, Sue asked Kitty, "Do you want to play with me?" Kitty looked up and said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, Sue and Kitty went outside to play. They ran around the yard, chasing butterflies and rolling in the grass. Kitty was not boring anymore. After playing for a long time, Sue and Kitty were tired. They went back inside the house to rest. Sue said, "Kitty, I'm happy you played with me today. You were not boring at all!" Kitty purred and cuddled with Sue. From that day on, Kitty and Sue played together every day, and they were never bored again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was 3 years old and very proud of her new bookcase. Mia had a dream that one day she would have enough books to fill her bookcase. She was so proud of it, she would talk to it all the time. One day, Mia went to the store searching for books. She had no money, but the store owner still gave her some books. He said it was a special gift because she was so proud of her bookcase. Mia took the books home and proudly put them on her bookcase. That night, Mia heard noises coming from her bookcase. At first, it was faint, like someone was whispering something. Mia was scared, but she was also curious. She decided to go closer and listen to the whispering. All of a sudden, she saw something moving inside her bookcase, something like a tiny monster. Mia screamed and ran away. She never came back to her bookcase. The monster was still in there, living among the books. Her dream of having a full bookcase was gone forever.

Once upon a time there was a busy girl called Jenny. Every day she had lots of things to do and she was never bored. One day, Jenny wanted to play a game. She went to her closet and opened the door. It was dark inside, but she could hear a noise. She looked in and saw a mouse! Jenny was scared at first, but then she thought it was funny. She named the mouse "Squeak" and she became his friend. They played together for a long time and had lots of fun. They raced around the room and chased each other. Jenny even shared her snacks with Squeak. At the end of the day, Jenny put Squeak back in the closet. She said goodbye and gave him a hug. Then she closed the closet door and went to bed, happy that she had made a special friend.
