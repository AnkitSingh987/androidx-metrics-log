Title: The Near Sea and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little crab lived near the sea. The crab had many friends, like a fish, a starfish, and a turtle. They loved to play together all day. One day, the crab saw a big bird. The bird said, "Follow me, little crab. I have a surprise for you!" The crab was patient and followed the bird. They walked and walked until they reached a beautiful garden. In the garden, the crab saw a big box. The bird said, "Open it, little crab!" The crab opened the box and found a big, shiny pearl. The crab was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird said, "Now, let's go back to the sea and show your friends!" The crab followed the bird back to the sea, and they all played together with the shiny pearl. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a girl named Lucy who was very curious. One day she saw her mommy using a measuring tape. Lucy asked her mommy, "What are you measuring, Mommy?" Her mommy answered, "I'm measuring your waist to make sure you have a proper fit for your new dress." Lucy was excited and she asked what a waist was. Her mommy kindly explained and showed her where her waist was – it was just above her belly button. Lucy touched her waist and noticed it was very soft. Her mommy told her, “Your waist is very soft and it needs to be measured in order to find the perfect size dress for you.” From then on, Lucy understood the importance of measuring and why it was necessary. She learned that measuring helps us make sure things fit properly, which is very important.

One day, a lonely cat named Tom lived in a big house. The house had an upstairs where Tom liked to play. He had no friends, so he played all by himself. One day, while Tom was upstairs, he heard a knock on the door. He ran down and opened the door. There was a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry said, "Hi, I am new here. Can I play with you?" Tom was happy and said, "Yes! Let's play upstairs!" As they played upstairs, Tom tried to strike a ball. But he missed and hit a big vase. The vase fell and broke into pieces. Tom and Jerry were scared. Then, Tom's mom came upstairs and saw the broken vase. She was very angry and said, "Tom, you are in big trouble!" Tom felt sad because he knew he did something bad. Jerry said, "I am sorry, Tom. I did not want you to get in trouble." Tom said, "It's okay, Jerry. But now we can't play together anymore." Jerry left the house, and Tom was lonely again.

John and Mommy went home. John felt troubled and didn't want to go inside. He started to shake his head and said "No, I don't want to go home". Mommy said, "John, sweetheart, why don't you want to come inside?" John looked at Mommy and said, "Because I'm scared. I don't like being at home, it frightens me." Mommy put her arm around him and said "It's alright, why don't we go inside and make some dinner together. That always makes you feel better, right?" John thought for a moment and then he nodded. He smiled and said "Ok, let's go home and make dinner together." John took Mommy's hand and they went inside. John and Mommy talked and laughed while they made dinner. As time passed, John's troubles faded and soon he was happy again. He shook his head and smiled, happy to be home with Mommy.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there lived a little bird named Blue. Blue loved to sing. Every morning, he would wake up and sing a happy song. All the animals in the forest would listen to Blue's song and feel happy too. One day, while Blue was singing, a little squirrel named Sally came up to him. "Blue," she said, "I like your song! Can you teach me how to sing?" Blue was happy to help. They spent the whole day singing together, and Sally learned many new songs. As the sun went down, Blue and Sally realized they were very far from home. They were scared and didn't know what to do. But then, Blue had an idea. He told Sally to be alert and listen carefully. Blue sang a loud and clear song that all the animals in the forest could hear. Soon, their friends came to find them. They followed the sound of Blue's song and found Blue and Sally in the dark forest. They were so happy to see their friends and thanked Blue for his smart idea. From that day on, Blue and Sally always sang together, and they never got lost again.
