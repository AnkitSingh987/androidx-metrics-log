Title: Sally the Girl and the Frog

One day, a little girl named Sally went outside to play. She saw a tire swing hanging from a big tree. Sally loved to swing on the tire. She would go very high and feel wild and free. As Sally was swinging, she saw a little frog on the ground. The frog looked up at Sally and said, "Hello, can you help me? I am a prince, but a witch turned me into a frog. If you give me a kiss, I will turn back into a prince." Sally was scared, but she wanted to help the frog. She got off the tire swing and gave the frog a gentle kiss. Suddenly, the frog turned into a big, wild, and colorful butterfly! The butterfly said, "Thank you, Sally! The witch made a mistake, and I was supposed to be a butterfly, not a prince. You helped me find my true self." Sally and the butterfly played together all day. They had fun on the tire swing and running in the wild grass. They became the best of friends, and Sally learned that sometimes unexpected things can bring the best surprises.

They all played and had fun together. The rabbit was happy that improving the home made everyone happy.

Lucy shared her cake with her doll, and they both enjoyed it very much. After the party, Lucy hugged her doll and said, "Thank you for celebrating with me today. I love you so much." The doll smiled back at her, and they both knew that they would always be the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to roll around in the grass and play all day. One day, Max saw a big box with a TV inside. Max was very curious, so he rolled the box into his house. He tried to turn on the TV, but it would not work. He was sad and wanted to watch his favorite show. Then, something unexpected happened. The TV turned on by itself and started to roll around the room! Max was very surprised and happy. He played with the rolling TV all day long and had lots of fun. In the end, Max learned that sometimes the best things happen when you least expect them.

Sam picked up the spark and went to Max's house. He said, "Max, look at my spark! It is so cool!" Max looked at the spark and smiled. He said, "Wow, that is a cool spark, Sam!" Max had an idea. He found a string and attached the spark to it. They played with the spark on the string all day. They were very happy and had lots of fun.
