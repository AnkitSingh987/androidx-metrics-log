Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

One day, a little girl named Sue found a pen on the floor. The pen was blue and shiny. Sue wanted to draw with the pen, so she took it to her room. In her room, Sue saw her toys were scattered all over the floor. She felt scared because her mom asked her to clean her room before. Sue said to her teddy bear, "Oh no, I need to clean up!" Sue picked up her toys and put them away. Now, she could draw with the pen. Sue drew a big sun, a house, and a happy family. She showed her mom the drawing. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Sue! I am happy you cleaned your room too!" Sue felt proud and happy. She loved her new pen and promised to keep her room clean.

Once there was a little girl named Emily. She was three years old and loved to explore the world around her. One day her parents decided to take Emily to the beach. When they arrived at the beach, Emily was amazed by the clear blue water and the soft, white sand. Emily couldn't help herself, so she ran to the shore and began playing. She quickly became absorbed in building sand castles and splashing in the waves. Soon a wave crashed against the shoreline, stronger than any wave before. It knocked Emily off her feet, and she started to drift away. Emily shouted for help, but the wave swept her further away. Suddenly a friendly dolphin appeared and rescued Emily. He grabbed Emily with his strong fins and returned her to safety. Emily was happy that she was rescued and thanked the dolphin with a big hug. The dolphin wriggled free and swam away. Emily learned an important lesson that day; never go too far away from shore when you’re at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to swim in the big pond. Swim was very important to him because it made him happy. One day, Ducky saw a small fish in the pond. The fish was sad because he could not swim well. Ducky wanted to help and support his new friend. Ducky said, "Don't worry, fish. I will support you and help you swim." They swam together every day, and soon the fish could swim very well. They became the best of friends and always knew how important it was to support each other.

Once upon a time, there was a famous sailor. He liked to whistle. He had a big boat. The sailor was very happy. One day, the sailor met a little bird. The bird said, "Can you teach me to whistle?" The sailor said, "Yes, I can teach you." The sailor showed the bird how to whistle. They whistled together. They were very good friends. The bird and the sailor had a lot of fun. The end.

Once there was a small boy called Joe. He had a big problem, he was deaf and he couldn't hear the sounds around him. Joe was very sad. One day while walking in the park, Joe saw some junk lying around. He started to pick it up and throw it away. Joe didn't like junk! He hated it and wanted it to go away. Joe kept picking up the junk and throwing it away. He worked really hard and in no time, the park was completely clean. Joe was so happy! He went home and found out that because of all the junk he had thrown away, he could suddenly hear the birds singing and the wind blowing. Joe was thrilled! He was no longer deaf and could hear everything! Joe loved it and knew he had made the right choice.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim loved to build things with his toy blocks. One day, he decided to build something really big and fun. Tim started to build a crazy tower with his blocks. He put one block on top of another, making the tower taller and taller. His friends came over and saw his crazy tower. They all wanted to help Tim build it even bigger. Together, they built the craziest tower ever. It was so tall that it almost touched the sky. Tim and his friends were very happy with their big, crazy tower. They played with it all day long and had lots of fun.
