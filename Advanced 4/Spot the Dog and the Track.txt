Title: Spot the Dog and the Track

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot found a track in the woods. The track was long and went through the trees. Spot decided to follow the track. As he ran, he saw the track split into two paths. One path went to the left and the other to the right. Spot was not sure which way to go, so he sat down to think. After a while, Spot chose the left path. He ran and ran until he found a big, beautiful field with lots of flowers. Spot was very happy and played in the field all day. And that is the story of the cute little dog named Spot and the track in the woods.

Once there was a little girl called Lola. She was an independent three year old. Every night, before she went to bed, she liked to dream. She dreamed of all the things she wanted to do. One night, she dreamt of making her very own file. It would be bright and colourful and full of all her special things! First, she decided she would find all the things she wanted to put in her file. Her favourite special toy, a beautiful seashell she had found at the beach, a drawing she had made of her family and a nice picture of her pet hamster. When the next day came, Lola set out to make her file. She carefully collected all the things she had found, and put them into the file. She stuck the picture of her hamster on the front, so everyone could see it. When she was finished, she was so proud of her file. It was perfect, and she had made it all on her own. Lola would treasure her file forever.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the library with her mom. Amy loved to read books and look at the pretty pictures. She saw a book about a cat and wanted to hold it. So, her mom helped her take the book off the shelf. While Amy was holding the book, she noticed something strange. The cat in the book had a fake tail! Amy was very surprised. She showed her mom and they both laughed. They thought it was just a funny picture in the book. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat with the fake tail jumped out of the book! The cat was real, but its tail was still fake. Amy and her mom were so surprised. They played with the cat in the library and had a lot of fun. After they finished playing, the cat went back into the book. Amy and her mom took the book home to read and remember their special day at the library.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a group of friends. They liked to read books together. One day, they found a thin book with a pretty cover. They were very excited to read it. "Let's read this book," said one friend. "It looks fun!" So, they sat down and began to read the book. They read about a big, mean monster who lived in a dark cave. As they read, the sky turned dark and scary. The wind began to blow hard. The friends were scared, but they kept reading. The thin book said that the monster would come if they said its name three times. One friend said, "I dare you to say the monster's name three times." The other friends looked at each other and then all said the monster's name three times. Suddenly, the monster appeared and chased the group of friends away. The friends ran and ran, but the monster caught them. They were never seen again. The thin book was left on the ground, waiting for someone else to read it.

Once upon a time, there was a very sleepy little swan. She waddled around the pond looking for a perfect place to fit. After a while, she found a cosy spot nestled behind a bush. She nestled into the soft grass and soon fell fast asleep. When she woke up, the sun was setting and was ready for dinner. She tried to fly to the edge of the pond, but alway felt her wings were too small. After trying and trying she realised that her wings were made to fit perfectly in the water. She swam around and around the pond, looking for some food. After a while, she caught a few fish and filled up on dinner. As the night settled in and the stars began to twinkle, the sleepy little swan paddled to the side, cozied into the grass, and fell fast asleep.
