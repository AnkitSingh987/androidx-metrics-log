Title: Amy and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her toy box. One day, she found a big, pretty jewel in her toy box. She was very happy. Amy showed the jewel to her friend, Tom. Tom was naughty. He said, "I want the jewel. Give it to me." Amy said, "No, we can play with it together." Tom got mad and took the jewel from Amy. He ran away. Amy was sad. She went to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom said, "Let's sort your toy box. Maybe we can find another jewel." They looked and found another pretty jewel. Amy was happy again. The next day, Tom came back. He said, "I'm sorry I was naughty. Can we play together?" Amy said, "Yes, but we have to share the jewels." They played together and had fun. The moral of the story is to always share with your friends and not be naughty. Sharing makes everyone happy.

The sun shone down on Lulu as she walked slowly across the meadow on the way to the forest. In her hands, she held a gift her mum had given her. She had been told that if she made it to the forest, she would find something special. The trees loomed larger as Lulu entered the forest, and soon the path was blocked. Lulu stopped and rested, thinking to herself. Something special must be nearby, she thought. She decided to have a closer look. Lulu found a clearing in the trees. It was filled with a flock of singing birds. They were the most beautiful birds Lulu had ever seen. She sat beneath them and felt her spirits rise. The birds moved slowly in a beautiful pattern around the sky. The sun bounced off their feathers and sparkled in Lulu's eyes. As Lulu watched, the birds flew towards her, in a circle around her head. She had been given the special gift of watching the birds’ dance in the sky.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Penny. She was very unique in her own special way. One day, her Mother asked her if she could do something as she was playing in the garden. "Penny, can you do this for me please?" said Mother. Penny didn't want to do what her Mother asked. She really wanted to keep playing. "No", replied Penny. "I want to keep playing". Her Mother tried not to be angry and said: "Penny, I think it's important to listen to your mother. If you resist, it will only make things more difficult for you." Penny thought about it for a moment and then she understood the lesson. She decided to listen to her Mother and do what she asked. The moral of the story is that it is important to listen to your parents. If you resist, it makes things harder and you can miss out on unique experiences.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. He loved to ride his bike with the red pedal. He rode it every day in the park. One sunny day, Tim saw a big water spray. The water spray was near the flowers. The flowers were pretty and colorful. Tim rode his bike to the water spray. He felt the cool water on his face. Tim laughed and had so much fun. He pedaled his bike all day long, playing in the water spray.

One day, Tom and his mom went on a picnic. They went to a big park. The park had many big trees and a distant pond. Tom saw ducks in the pond. He was very happy. While they ate, Tom saw a little bug. The bug had big eyes. The bug would blink many times. Tom liked the bug. He wanted to take it home. But his mom said, "No, let the bug live with its family." Tom was sad, but he let the bug go. The bug blinked at Tom and went away. Then, something unexpected happened! The bug came back with many friends. They all blinked at Tom and danced around him. Tom and his mom laughed and clapped. The bugs were saying thank you. The moral of the story is to be kind to all living things. When you are kind, good things can happen.
