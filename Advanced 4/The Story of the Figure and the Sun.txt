Title: The Story of the Figure and the Sun

The sun shone brightly; it was time for the figure to nap. The figure had been trucking all day and his muscles were now dry and stiff. There was a large rock nearby; the figure deemed it a perfect spot for a nap. Though, as the figure lay down, he sensed something watching him. His heart raced and his eyes darted around, but all he could see was the dry, sandy ground. But the figure knew he was not alone, his instinct told him something was nearby. As the figure sat up, the sand moved, and a giant snake emerged! The figure had never seen a snake so big and the figure was scared. The snake moved closer, its tongue flicking, and the figure knew it must run. The figure sprang up and began to run, the snake following suit. The snake chased the figure, but his nap had given him the energy he needed; the figure ran and ran. Finally, the figure reached a clearing and the snake could not keep up. The figure could finally rest and the snake had notated him; the figure had escaped the snake's clutches. He lay down and happily drifted off to sleep in the safety of the dry clearing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had long, shiny hair that she loved very much. One day, she went to play outside in the sun. The sun was shining bright and made Lily's hair shine too. Lily was playing with her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, your hair is so shiny!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, I like my shiny hair." But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and made Lily's hair all messy. It even got some leaves and sticks in it. Lily felt uncomfortable with her messy hair. Lily and Tom went inside to fix her hair. They took out the leaves and sticks and brushed it until it was shiny again. They both laughed and were happy that her hair was back to normal. From that day on, Lily and Tom played together and always made sure to keep her hair nice and shiny.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the park. Tom saw a big mat and said, "Max, let's do yoga on this mat!" Max was happy and said, "Yes, let's try it!" As they started to do yoga, they heard a loud noise. It was a bad man who wanted to take the mat away. Tom and Max did not want to give the mat to the bad man. So, they thought of a plan. Tom said to Max, "Let's split the mat and each take half." Max agreed and they split the mat into two pieces. The bad man saw this and did not want the mat anymore. Tom and Max were happy and continued to do yoga on their half of the mat. They had fun and the bad man left them alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange car. The car had a radio. The radio played fun songs. The car liked to go to the park. At the park, the orange car met a blue car. The blue car also had a radio. They played their radios and danced together. They had so much fun at the park. One day, the orange car and the blue car went to the park again. They played their radios and danced. Then, they saw a green car with a radio too! The three cars danced and played their radios together. They all became good friends and went to the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very enthusiastic about life and always wanted to pretend. She loved pretending to be a princess and a superhero. One day, Jane had an idea. She wanted to pretend to be a grown-up! She had seen her parents do many things while they were grown-ups, and she wanted to do them too. So, Jane pretended she was an adult. She sat in an adult chair and held an adult pen. She pretended to work at her own job and even gave herself a raise. Jane pretended for a whole year. On the last day of her pretending job, she was even more enthusiastic than when she started. She had learned so much and felt so grown-up! The end.
