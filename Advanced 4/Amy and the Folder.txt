Title: Amy and the Folder

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy found a flexible folder in her room. The folder was very pretty and could bend easily. Amy loved her new folder and wanted to keep all her drawings inside. One day, Amy heard her mom call her from the kitchen. "Amy, come here!" her mom said. Amy ran to the kitchen with her flexible folder in her hand. "Look, Mom! I found a folder for my drawings!" Amy said with a big smile. Mom looked at the folder and said, "That's a great folder, Amy! It's so flexible and pretty. Now you can keep all your drawings safe and show them to your friends." Amy and her mom put all the drawings in the folder and Amy was very happy. From that day on, Amy always used her flexible folder to keep her drawings safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a popular farmer. He had an old, rusty car with lots of fun things inside. One day, he decided to unpack it. First, he opened the trunk and there was an old wooden trunk. He carefully carried it away and opened it. Inside was a whole toy truck! He was so excited and couldn't wait to show his friends. He quickly grabbed all of the parts and put them together. Soon, he had a super cool, cool looking toy truck. His friends were really impressed and they all played with it together. The farmer was so happy and proud of his fun car and toy truck.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved playing with his friends in the snow. One day, Remy and his friends found some ice on the ground. They thought it was so fun to slide on the ice. Remy said to his friends, "Let's part the ice and see what's under it!" His friends agreed and they all started to break the ice apart. Under the ice, they found a beautiful, shiny stone. Remy's friend, Lucy the squirrel, said, "Wow, look at that pretty stone!" All the friends gathered around to look at the shiny stone. They decided to share the stone and take turns keeping it. Each day, one of the friends would keep the stone and then give it to the next friend. They all loved the shiny stone so much. And that is how Remy and his friends learned to share and have fun together in the deep, deep forest.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. They saw a big blue sea and a small boat with a sail. Lily thought the sail was adorable. "Mom, can we go on the boat?" Lily asked. Her mom said, "We can try, but we have a surprise for you later." Lily and her mom went on the boat. They sailed on the big blue sea. The sun was warm, and the water was cool. Lily and her mom laughed and had fun. They saw fish and birds. Lily was very happy. She forgot about the surprise. They went back to the beach. Lily's mom said, "Now it's time for your surprise!" Lily was excited. She saw a big box with a bow. She opened the box and found a cute sail toy boat. Lily loved the toy boat. She said, "Thank you, mom! This is the best day ever!"

Once upon a time there was a princess who had a key. The key was very special and it allowed her to visit noisy places. Every time the princess used her key, she had lots of fun. But one day, the princess lost the key. She searched everywhere but couldn’t find it. In the end, she had to surrender and accept that her key was gone. The next day, she heard a familiar noise coming from outside. When she opened the door, she saw her key - some noisy birds had found it and were playing with it. She realised that sometimes you have to let go of things to find happiness, so she thanked the birds and let them keep the key. From that day onwards, the princess was much happier as she had learnt to surrender and move on.
