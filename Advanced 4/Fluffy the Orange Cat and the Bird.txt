Title: Fluffy the Orange Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big orange cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside with his friends. One day, Fluffy and his friends were playing in the park. They were running and jumping on the green grass. Suddenly, Fluffy saw a big red car with a loud noise. It was an ambulance. Fluffy was curious and asked his friend, "What is that car?" His friend said, "That is an ambulance. It helps people who are hurt." Fluffy and his friends watched as the ambulance stopped near a tree. A little bird had fallen from its nest. The kind people from the ambulance helped the bird and spread a soft blanket on the ground. They put the bird on the blanket and took it to a safe place. Fluffy and his friends were happy that the bird was safe. They waved goodbye to the ambulance and continued playing in the park. From that day on, Fluffy knew that the big red car called an ambulance was there to help.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog. He liked to whistle when he was happy. One day, Spot felt sick. His mom said, "We need to go see a vet." A vet is a doctor for animals. Spot and his mom went to the vet. The vet had a big, new office. It was very modern. Spot looked around and saw other animals there too. The vet looked at Spot and said, "You will be better soon." She gave him some medicine. Spot felt better. He was so happy, he started to whistle again. Spot and his mom went home, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a hero who was very brave and strong. Every day, he went on amazing adventures. One day he was walking in a very bright and sunny field. Suddenly he sneezed very loudly. He felt kernels of grass tickling his nose! He laughed and decided to keep walking. The hero soon spotted a big, bright castle in the distance. He was excited to explore it! He walked towards the castle and as soon as he touched the door he heard a loud noise. BANG! He had accidentally opened the door and a swarm of yellow butterflies flew out into the sky. It was a very magical moment and the hero lit up with joy. The hero kept walking and soon he was back in the bright, sunny field. He was happy to have had such a wonderful day. He waved goodbye to the castle and ran home. The end.

Sara and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big pile of leaves. Sara wants to cover Ben with the leaves. She thinks it will be fun. "Ben, come here!" Sara says. "I have a surprise for you." "What is it?" Ben asks. He walks to Sara. Sara grabs a lot of leaves and throws them on Ben. Ben laughs. He is covered with leaves. He can only see a little bit. "Boo!" Sara says. "I covered you with leaves. You look funny." Ben shakes off the leaves. He sees Sara smiling. He smiles too. "That was fun," Ben says. "But you know what? You look pretty with the leaves in your hair." Sara touches her hair. She feels the leaves. She giggles. "Thank you," Sara says. "You are nice." They hug each other. Then they play with the leaves some more. They are happy.

Once upon a time there lived an old lady. She had a lot of makeup and she loved wearing it. One day, however, she got very tired. Even though she was tired, she kept putting on makeup. Her little grandson watched in amazement. He asked her: "Grandma, why are you keeping putting on makeup when you're so tired?" The old lady replied: "My dear, this is a special kind of makeup. It will keep me looking beautiful, no matter how tired I am!" But then something strange happened. After an hour or so, her makeup started getting filthy. The little boy said: "Grandma, your makeup looks dirty. You should keep it clean!" Grandma replied: "Good advice, little one! I will keep it clean so I can always look my best." And that's the story of how the old lady kept her makeup clean!
