Title: Sue the Girl and the Dress

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty dress. But the dress was tight. Sue wanted to wear the dress to a fun show. At the show, Sue saw her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Hi Sue! I like your dress." Sue said, "Thank you, Tom! But my dress is tight." Tom said, "That's okay, Sue. You still look nice." The show started, and everyone was happy. Sue and Tom watched the people sing and dance. When the show was over, Sue and Tom clapped their hands. They liked the show. Sue was happy, even if her dress was tight.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to create things with her hands. One day, Lily found a big box with many colors of clay. She was very happy and started to play with the clay. Lily made a funny man with a big, round knob for a nose. She showed her funny man to her mom. "Look, Mom! I made a man with a big knob nose!" Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "That is very good, Lily! You are good at creating things." Lily played with her funny man all day. The sun was mild and warm, so she took her funny man outside to play. They had a lot of fun in the mild sun. When it was time to go inside, Lily put her funny man on a shelf to keep him safe. She was very proud of her creation and knew she would make more fun things with her clay.

One day, a fox was walking in the forest. He was looking for something to eat. Suddenly, he found an apple tree. The fox was excited! He took some apples and started eating them. But then, a bad man came out of nowhere. He did not like that the fox was eating the apples. "That's MY tree!" the bad man shouted. So the bad man tried to take the apples back. The fox did not want to share the apples. He ran away with the apples in his mouth. Luckily, the fox was fast and he managed to get away. After that, the fox came across a nice fox family. He shared the apples with them and they were all happy. The fox and the fox family enjoyed the apples together and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a panda named Pandy lived in a big green forest. Pandy was not like other pandas. He was bald. All his panda friends had lots of black and white fur, but Pandy had none. This made Pandy feel sad. One day, Pandy met a wise old owl. The owl said, "Pandy, I can help you feel better. Let's write a fun story together!" Pandy liked the idea, so they started to write. The story was about a brave bald panda who had many adventures. As they wrote, something unexpected happened. Pandy's fur started to grow back! It grew more and more with each word they wrote. Pandy was so happy. The owl said, "See, Pandy, when we do fun things with friends, we can feel better about ourselves." The moral of the story is that spending time with friends and doing fun things can help us feel better about who we are.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emily. She was a curious and adventurous child who wanted to try all kinds of things. One day, she wanted to explore and set out with a big backpack. Everyone who saw Emily asked her where she was going. She always answered: “I’m going to find a point!” Her best friend Tim asked her: “What do you mean by a point?” Emily replied, “I want to find a special spot where I can just think or take a nap or just daydream.” So, Emily and Tim started to look for her special point. Despite the sunny summer day, finding Emily's point was proving difficult. After looking for what seemed like forever, Emily and Tim stopped to rest. After a few moments, Emily's face suddenly lit up. She had found the spot. She said, “This is it! It's perfect!” Emily and Tim spend the rest of the day making her point special. After the sun set, Emily packed up her backpack and they headed home. The moral of the story is that if you persist despite difficulties, your efforts are sure to be rewarded.
