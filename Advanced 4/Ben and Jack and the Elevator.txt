Title: Ben and Jack and the Elevator

Once upon a time there were two friends: Ben and Jack. They decided to go to the store to buy something if it was spicy. But when they got to the elevator, it was too full. So, Ben suggested to fold himself up so he could fit inside. He did so and Jack gave him a push. At first it was fun. But when the elevator started going down, Ben was squished! It was getting hotter and spicier in there! Jack shouted, "I'm getting out!" and pressed the button. The elevator doors opened and Jack ran out while Ben was still crammed inside. He shouted, "Help me!" But nobody heard him and the elevator doors closed. The end.

One day, Benny was in his bedroom when he heard a strange sound. He thought it was coming from the kitchen. He ran to the kitchen and saw that the refrigerator was making a big splash. Benny was curious what was happening and ran over to the fridge. He noticed that the water was inside the refrigerator was very cold. Benny opened the refrigerator door, and to his surprise he saw a little fish that was swimming in the pool of water inside the fridge. Benny was so excited and he said, "Look! A fish!" Benny noticed that the fish was dead. He said, "Oh no! The fish is dead." He felt really sad and he reached out to touch the fish. He said, "Goodbye little fish. I will miss you." Then, Benny took it out of the refrigerator and placed it on the counter. He gave the fish a proper burial in his garden. Then, he said a few words in honor of the little fish. Benny learned a valuable lesson that day: always check the refrigerator before going to bed!

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was lovely. She wanted some juice, so she went to the fridge. She found a lovely glass jug filled with orange juice. She picked it up and sat in a chair. She took off the lid, and smiled at the orange juice. She took a sip and it tasted so good! She said, "This juice is so lovely." She happily finished her juice and put the jug back in the fridge. She smiled, content in her lovely seat.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, while he was hopping, he found a brilliant rock under a big tree. The rock was shiny and pretty. Bob picked up the rock with his thumb and showed it to his friend, a tiny bird named Tina. "Look, Tina! I found a brilliant rock!" said Bob. "Wow, that's so pretty!" said Tina. They both smiled and hopped around with joy. Bob and Tina decided to keep the brilliant rock as their special treasure. They put it in a secret place and went back to hopping and playing. Every day, they would visit their special treasure and remember the fun day they found it. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a huge tree. Under the tree, there was a red helmet. The helmet was shiny and pretty. A little boy named Tim saw the helmet and wanted to find out who it belonged to. Tim walked around the town, asking his friends if the helmet belonged to them. His friends said no, but they wanted to help Tim find the helmet's owner. They all looked for clues together. At last, they found a big girl named Sally. The helmet belonged to Sally! She was so happy to have her helmet back. Tim and his friends felt good for helping. They all played together under the huge tree and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. They had a picnic with yummy food. Tim's mom brought his favorite food, tuna. As Tim was eating his tuna, he saw a wet cat. The cat was very hungry. Tim wanted to help the wet cat. He said, "Here, kitty, have some of my tuna." The cat ate the tuna very fast. Tim was happy to help the cat. Just then, the wet cat started to talk! "Thank you, Tim," said the cat. Tim was so surprised! He did not know cats could talk. The cat told Tim that it was a magic cat. It could grant Tim one wish. Tim wished for a new toy. The magic cat said, "Your wish is granted!" And just like that, Tim had a new toy. He was very happy and played with his new toy all day.
