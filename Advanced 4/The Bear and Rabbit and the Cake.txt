Title: The Bear and Rabbit and the Cake

Once upon a time there was a bear and a rabbit. The bear wanted to open a present. He asked the rabbit, "Can you help me open this?" The rabbit replied, "For sure! What's inside?" The bear replied, "I don't know because it's wrapped in skin!" The rabbit exclaimed, "Oh, let's open it and see!" So, the two of them worked together to open the skin and inside was a big surprise! The bear said, "Wow! It's a big cake! How good!" The rabbit said, "Let's eat it!" The bear replied, "Yes, let's eat it." And so, the two friends happily ate the big cake and had a very good day. The end.

There was a girl called Jane who loved perfume. It made her feel very happy. One day, she went on an adventure to find a rare perfume. She walked until she came across a small shop. When she peered into the window, she saw a magical bottle of perfume. Jane could not believe her eyes! She opened the door and went inside. She made her way to the counter. "Can I buy the perfume?" Jane asked. The shopkeeper smiled, he replied, "Of course! But you'll have to cut a deal." Jane agreed and began bargaining with the shopkeeper. Finally, they came to an agreement. The shopkeeper told Jane to open the bottle and smell it. Jane opened the bottle and was filled with joy. She was about to walk away but the shopkeeper said, "Wait! I forgot to tell you something important. If you take it, I will cut your nose off." Jane was scared. She reluctantly put the bottle back on the counter and ran away as fast as she could. She never found the rare perfume again.

Mommy and Jimmy were in the park on a sunny day. They were having a wonderful time together. "Look, Mommy!" said Jimmy. "The sky is so high and the clouds are really nice!" Mommy smiled. "Yes, my sweet. We should go on a walk around the park, shall we?" Jimmy was excited. He skipped and jumped as they walked through the park. But soon, everything began to change. The sky turned dark and a loud noise filled the air. Mommy picked up Jimmy as the wind blew harder and harder. "Mommy, what's happening?" asked Jimmy. "It's a tornado, my dear," Mommy said. "We need to go home now." Jimmy and Mommy quickly passed the other people in the park who were screaming and running in all directions. It was important that they hurry home. As they got closer to their house the tornado became more and more impressive. The wind was so strong it knocked down trees and umbrellas. Suddenly, the roof of their house flew away. Mommy grabbed Jimmy and held him tight, but nothing could have prepared them for the strong impact of the tornado. As they were blown away, Jimmy could help but think of how much fun they were having before the tornado passed through their town.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy bus. He would push it around the room and make it go very fast. One day, Tom went to the park to play with his bus. He was very happy. At the park, Tom saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to part the branches and make a path for his bus. So, he pushed the branches to the side and drove his bus through the tree. The bus went zoom, zoom, zoom! But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bus high up in the air. Tom tried to catch it, but the bus went too high. The wind blew the bus far away and Tom could not find it. Tom was very sad and went home without his bus.

Once upon a time, there was a warm sun. The sun was a big round shape in the sky. It made everyone feel happy and warm. One day, a big cloud came. The cloud was a funny shape. It covered the warm sun. Everyone felt a little cold and sad. But then, the wind arrived. The wind blew the funny shape cloud away. The warm sun came back. Everyone was happy and warm again. The end.
