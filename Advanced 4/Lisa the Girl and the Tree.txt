Title: Lisa the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa loved to nap. Every day, she would take a nap under a big tree in her yard. She was very happy when she napped. One day, Lisa found a pretty ornament. It was round and shiny. She did not know what it was. She was ignorant about the ornament. Lisa took the ornament to her favorite tree. Lisa put the ornament on the tree and took a nap. She had a beautiful dream about the ornament. In her dream, the ornament was a magic ball that made her happy. When Lisa woke up, the ornament was still there. She smiled and knew she would always remember her special dream.

Once upon a time, in a small gloomy forest, there lived a bunny named Ben. Ben was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, he decided to go on a walk to find a friend. As Ben hopped through the forest, he saw a big pile of sand. He thought it looked fun to play in. Just then, he heard a voice say, "Hi! I'm Sam the squirrel. Do you want to play with me?" Ben was so surprised and happy to find a friend. Together, Ben and Sam played in the sand, making sand hills and digging holes. They laughed and had so much fun. From that day on, Ben and Sam were the best of friends, and the gloomy forest was not so gloomy anymore.

John and his dragon friend, Pete, were very excited. They were going to watch their favorite show on the television. John and Pete quickly ran to the living room and jumped onto the couch. The show was about to start and they didn't want to miss it. Just as the show began, the power went out. Without electricity, the television was weak and wouldn't turn on. John was so disappointed. "What should we do now?" he asked Pete. Pete looked up and saw the stars. He said, "Let's go outside and look at the stars. We can watch them for a while." John happily agreed. He and Pete rushed out the door and enjoyed viewing the stars until it got dark. Suddenly, the ground shook and a loud crash filled the air. Rocks and dirt were everywhere. John and Pete had no idea what had happened. When they went back inside, they saw that the house had been destroyed by an earthquake. Without the safety of their home, they had no where to go. John and Pete were very sad. They had to rush away and find somewhere else to go.

Once upon a time, there was a raven. The raven was very big and black. It liked to blink its eyes. One day, the raven saw a cheap, shiny toy on the ground. The toy was red and round. The raven wanted the toy. It picked up the toy in its beak and flew away. The raven blinked its eyes and felt very happy. It played with the toy all day long. But then, the toy broke. The raven was sad. It tried to fix the toy, but it could not. The raven blinked its eyes and cried. The toy was gone, and the raven was not happy anymore. The end.

One day, Ben and Lily went to the beach with their mom and dad. They were very happy to play in the sand and splash in the water. The beach was busy with many people and boats and birds. "Look, Ben, what is that?" Lily said, pointing to something in the water. It was a jellyfish, floating near the shore. It had a big round body and long thin arms. It looked like a balloon with strings. "Wow, a jellyfish!" Ben said. "Can we touch it?" "No, no, no!" their mom said, running to them. "Jellyfish can hurt you. They have stings on their arms. If they touch you, it will be very ouchy." "Ouchy?" Lily asked, making a sad face. "Yes, ouchy. If a jellyfish stings you, you have to soak the ouchy part in warm water or vinegar. That will make it feel better. But it is better to stay away from jellyfish. They are not for playing with. They are for looking at." "OK, mom," Ben and Lily said. They nodded and moved away from the jellyfish. They decided to build a sandcastle instead. They used buckets and shovels and shells and seaweed. They had a lot of fun and forgot about the jellyfish. The end.
