Title: Sam the Spider and the Uncomfortable

Once there was a spider named Sam. Sam supported his family by spinning webs. But one day, as he was spinning, he felt uncomfortable. Sam wondered why he felt so strange. He asked his Mom, ''Mom, why do I feel uncomfortable?'' His Mom answered, ''Sam, life can be difficult at times. But you are strong and must support your family.'' Sam tried his best to spin, but the uncomfortable feeling stayed. As he looked up at the sky, he saw thunderclouds rolling in. ''Uh oh,'' he thought, ''It looks like a storm is coming!'' He felt even more uncomfortable as the storm grew closer. Soon, a huge gust of wind blew through and knocked down Sam's web! The uncomfortable feeling suddenly made sense. His Mom was helping him prepare for the storm by making sure he was strong enough to support his family during tough times. Sam smiled, for he now knew the reason for the uncomfortable feeling. He spun a new web, this one even stronger than before, and the spider family was able to stay safe during the storm.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom loved to dance and play with music. Every day, he would dance and play with his friends in the park. They all loved to listen to the music and have fun together. One day, Tom was so excited to play with his friends that he forgot his music at home. He ran back to his house to get it. He found the music and brought it to the park. His friends were waiting for him. When Tom came back, they all played the music and started to dance. They had so much fun dancing and laughing together. Tom was happy he did not forget the music for too long. They all had a great day and went home tired but happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with a dead branch. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim! Don't play near the dead branch." Tim nodded and went to play with his ball. While playing, Tim saw a big trophy on the ground. It was shiny and pretty. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a trophy!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Let's take it home." At home, Tim wanted to know how big the trophy was. So, his mom helped him measure it. They used a long string to measure the trophy. Tim's mom said, "The trophy is very big, Tim! Good job finding it!" Tim was very happy and put the trophy on his shelf.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to fly around and look at all the pretty flowers. One day, Buzzy saw a gray flower that was just starting to bloom. Buzzy was very curious about this flower. "Hello, gray flower!" Buzzy said. "Why are you gray and not bright like the other flowers?" The gray flower looked at Buzzy and said, "I may be gray, but I am just as pretty as the other flowers. I will bloom big and beautiful soon!" Buzzy smiled and said, "You are right, gray flower! You are pretty too!" Buzzy then flew away and told all his friends about the gray flower. They all came to see the gray flower bloom into a big, beautiful flower. And they all agreed that even though the flower was gray, it was still very pretty.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat with soft fur. The cat loved to cook. One day, the cat decided to make a fancy cake for all its friends. The cat asked its friend, the dog, "Do you want to help me cook a fancy cake?" The dog said, "Yes, I want to help!" So, the cat and the dog cooked the cake together. They put on fancy hats and sang songs while they cooked. Soon, the cake was ready. All the friends came to eat the fancy cake. The cat and the dog shared the cake with everyone. The friends were happy and said, "Thank you, cat and dog, for the fancy cake!" The cat and the dog learned that when they work together and share, everyone is happy. And that is how the cat with the soft fur and the dog became the best of friends.
