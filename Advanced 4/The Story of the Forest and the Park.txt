Title: The Story of the Forest and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old. Her name was Mary. Mary was on an adventure. She wanted to discover something new. She walked in the forest and soon, she found something strange. It was a note! The note was fit to carry in her hand. She looked at it carefully. She noticed that there was something written on it. She tried to read it, but she was too little to understand what it said. Mary decided to keep the note. She wanted to discover what it said. She took it home with her. Once she was home, she asked her mom for help. Her mom looked at the note and read it. It said, “You will find something special.” Mary was very excited. The next day, Mary and her mom went to the park. She looked around and soon discovered a secret hiding spot in the trees. Inside the spot was a surprise! It was a brand new toy. It was the perfect new toy. It was fit for Mary. She was so happy and thanked her mom for helping her discover something special.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a big window. He loved to look out the window and watch the birds play in the yard. One day, Max saw a big box outside. He barked, "Woof! What's in the box?" His friend, the cat, said, "I don't know. Let's find out!" They went outside to see what the box had inside. The box said, "This box includes toys!" Max and the cat were excited. They opened the box and found many fun toys. They played with the toys all day long. In the end, they were happy that they found the box with toys. The big window in their house helped them see the box, and they had a great day playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big uncle named Tom. Uncle Tom was very tall and sometimes scared Mia. Mia was fearful of her uncle. One sunny day, Mia and Uncle Tom went to the park. Uncle Tom brought a ball to play with. Mia watched as Uncle Tom threw the ball very high in the sky. She was scared, but also wanted to try. Uncle Tom saw that Mia was scared. He showed her how to throw the ball. Mia tried and threw the ball too. She was not scared anymore. Mia and Uncle Tom had lots of fun at the park.

One day, a little girl named Sally wanted to walk up the big stair in her house. She was a very creative girl and loved to play pretend. As she walked up the stair, she imagined it was a huge mountain she was climbing. "Look, Mommy!" Sally called to her mom. "I'm climbing the biggest mountain in the world!" Her mom smiled and said, "Be careful, Sally. Don't climb too high!" But Sally didn't listen. She kept climbing higher and higher, pretending she was almost at the top of the mountain. Suddenly, she lost her balance and fell down the stair. She was hurt and started to cry. Her mom came to help her and said, "Sally, remember to listen when someone tells you to be careful. It's important to stay safe while playing." Sally learned that day that even when using her creative imagination, she should always be careful and listen to her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Lucy. Lucy was sad because she did not belong to a family. Every day, she looked for a home where she would be loved. One day, Lucy saw a big traffic jam. Many cars were stopped. She thought, "Maybe I can find a family here!" So, she walked between the cars, looking for a family to love her. Then, something unexpected happened. A little boy in one of the cars saw Lucy and smiled. He opened the car door and let her in. Lucy was so happy! But, the twist was that the little boy was also looking for a family. Together, they found a new home and belonged to each other. They were both happy and loved.
