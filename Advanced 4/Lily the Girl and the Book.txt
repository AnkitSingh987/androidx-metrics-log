Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her friend Tom's house to play. Lily saw a big bookcase in Tom's room. She noticed many books on the bookcase. Lily liked books a lot. She asked Tom, "Can we read a book?" Tom said, "Yes, let's pick one." They both looked at the bookcase and found a book about a dependable dog named Max. Lily and Tom sat down and started to read the book. They liked Max because he always helped his friends. In the story, Max helped a little bird who fell from its nest. Max was very kind and dependable. Lily and Tom learned that it is good to be helpful and kind to others. They decided to be like Max and help their friends too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a job to do. His mom asked him to prepare a snack for his little sister. Tim was worried because he had never done this job before. Tim went to the kitchen and found some bread, cheese, and juice. He tried his best to make a sandwich for his sister. He put the cheese between the bread and poured the juice into a cup. He was still worried that he did not do a good job. Tim gave the sandwich and juice to his sister. She took a bite of the sandwich and started to cry. The cheese was too hard, and the juice was too sour. Tim felt sad because he did not do a good job. His mom came and helped him make a better snack, but Tim was still upset that he could not do it by himself.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Andy. He worked hard every day to find food for his family. One day, Andy found a big, shiny jewel on the ground. The jewel was red and sparkly. Andy thought it was very pretty. Andy wanted to show the jewel to his friends, so he carried it back to his ant hill. His friends also thought the jewel was very pretty. They all smiled and played with the jewel. But soon, they forgot about finding food for their family. After a while, the ant family became hungry. The ants realized that they had spent too much time playing with the jewel and not enough time finding food. They felt sad and knew they had to work together to find food again. The ants decided to put the jewel away and went back to their busy work. They found food for their family and everyone was happy again. The ants learned that it is important to work together and not get distracted by pretty things. And every time they smiled, they remembered the lesson they learned from the shiny jewel.

One day, a cat was walking in the park. The cat was very graceful. She liked to jump and play. The cat saw a dog. The dog was big and loud. The cat was scared. The cat said, "Please do not chase me, dog. I want to rest." The dog did not listen. He chased the cat. The cat ran away very fast. She was sad and tired. Then, a police car came. The police said, "Stop, dog! Let the cat rest." The dog stopped chasing the cat. The cat was happy. The dog said, "I am sorry, cat." The cat and the dog became friends. They played together and then they both had a nice rest.

Once upon a time, there was a small cube. The cube was nervous because it was lost. It wanted to find its way back home. The cube was also very thirsty and wanted to drink some water. One day, a kind bird saw the cube and wanted to help. The bird picked up the cube in its beak and flew up into the sky. They looked for a place to drink water. They found a big lake and the cube drank some water. The cube was happy and not thirsty anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The cube started to grow bigger and bigger! It was not just a small cube anymore. It turned into a big house! The nervous cube was not lost anymore. It was now a happy home for the kind bird and its family. They all lived happily ever after.
