Title: Tim the Boy and the Hammer

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, while playing in the yard, he saw something interesting. It was a shiny hammer. Tim was very happy to find the hammer. Tim took the hammer inside his house. He showed it to his mom and dad. They were very happy too. Tim's dad said they could use the hammer to fix things around the house. Tim thought that was a great idea. Tim and his dad fixed many things together. They fixed a broken chair, a wobbly table, and a loose door. Tim was very proud of the work they did. He loved using the interesting hammer he found. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small seed in the ground. It was near a big crack in the dirt. The seed wanted to grow and bloom into a pretty flower. One day, it rained and the seed got a lot of water. The seed was happy and knew it was time to grow. Soon, the seed started to grow. It pushed through the crack in the dirt. But something smelly was there too. The seed did not like the smelly thing, but it kept growing. The seed knew it would be a pretty flower soon. Finally, the seed grew into a beautiful flower. It bloomed with many colors. The smelly thing was still there, but the flower was so pretty that no one cared. Everyone loved the flower, and the flower was happy. It had grown big and strong, even with the smelly thing near the crack.

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toys, but they have different ways of playing. Tom likes to sort his toys by color, shape and size. He makes neat piles of red cars, blue balls and yellow blocks. Lily likes to mix her toys and pretend they are having adventures. She makes a big mess of pink dolls, green dinosaurs and orange trains. One day, their mom gives them some money. She says they can buy a new toy if they save enough. Tom and Lily are happy. They want to be independent and choose their own toy. But they have a problem. They share one piggy bank. Tom wants to sort the money by coin and bill. He says it is easier to count and save. Lily wants to mix the money and pretend it is treasure. She says it is more fun and creative. They start to argue. Tom says Lily is silly and messy. Lily says Tom is boring and bossy. They pull the piggy bank and try to take the money. The piggy bank falls and breaks. The money spills everywhere. Tom and Lily are sad and sorry. They see their mom coming. They are afraid she will be angry. Their mom sees the broken piggy bank and the money. She is not angry. She is calm and kind. She says they can fix the piggy bank with glue. She says they can share the money and buy a toy together. She says they can sort or mix the money as they like, as long as they respect each other. She says they are both smart and creative. Tom and Lily hug their mom. They say they are sorry and they love her. They say they love each other too. They decide to sort the money together and buy a toy they both like. They learn to be independent and cooperative. They are happy.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a painter named Tim. Tim loved to paint all day. He painted houses, trees, and even animals. Tim was very good at painting. One day, a nosy cat came to watch Tim paint. The cat said, "What are you doing?" Tim replied, "I am painting a big tree." The cat was very curious and wanted to help. So, Tim let the cat hold a tiny brush to paint. As they painted, the cat started to act funny. It jumped and danced around, making Tim laugh. The cat dipped its tail in the paint and made silly lines on the paper. Tim and the cat had so much fun painting together. And from that day, they became the best of friends.
