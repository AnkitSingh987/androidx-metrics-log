Title: Kitty the Nosy Cat and the Button

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with buttons. One day, she found a big red button on the floor. "Wow, what a pretty button!" said Kitty. Kitty played with the button all day long. She rolled it, she batted it, and she chased it. But then, she lost the button. Kitty looked under the bed, behind the chair, and in the closet. But she couldn't find it anywhere. "Oh no, where did my button go?" cried Kitty. Just then, her friend Birdy flew in through the window. "Why are you sad, Kitty?" asked Birdy. Kitty told Birdy about the lost button. Birdy wanted to help, so they both looked for the button together. Suddenly, Birdy saw something shiny in the corner. It was the big red button! But when they got closer, they saw that it wasn't a button at all. It was a shiny red jewel! Kitty and Birdy were so surprised and happy. They played with the shiny red jewel all day long and forgot all about the lost button.

Anna and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a spray bottle and a cord. They liked to spray water on the flowers and the grass. They also liked to spray each other and laugh. "Spray, spray, spray!" Anna said, as she squirted water on Ben's face. "Hey, stop that!" Ben said, as he wiped his eyes. "It's my turn to spray you!" He grabbed the spray bottle from Anna and chased her around the garden. Anna ran and giggled. She saw the cord hanging from the tree. It was a swing that their dad had made for them. "Ha, ha, I got you!" Ben said, as he caught up with Anna and sprayed her hair. Anna ducked and ran to the cord. She grabbed it and swung up in the air. She felt the wind on her face and the sun on her skin. She felt comfortable and happy. "Wow, you're so high!" Ben said, as he watched Anna swing. "Can I try too?" "Sure, come on!" Anna said, as she swung back and forth. "It's fun!" She let go of the cord and landed on the grass. She gave the spray bottle to Ben and helped him climb on the cord. They took turns swinging and spraying and laughing. They had a lot of fun in the garden.

One day, a little girl named Mia was walking to her classroom in the sunshine. As she walked, she felt a bit worried. Maybe it was because of the new teacher she heard about who was supposed to start that day. When she arrived at the classroom, she looked around. Everything was neat and tidy, but there was no teacher inside. Mia went up to a group of other kids who were talking excitedly. One of them noticed her and asked, “Aren’t you coming to our class?” Mia nodded, her eyes wide with curiosity. Another student, a boy named Jack, spoke up. “We’ve been asking all morning, but nobody knows when the teacher will come,” Jack said, looking worried. Mia’s stomach churned. She kept wondering when the teacher would arrive. Suddenly, the door opened and a tall woman with kind eyes stepped in. She was the new teacher everyone had been asking about! Mia smiled, feeling a lot less worried. The teacher smiled back and said, “I’m so glad you’ve all been patiently waiting. Let’s get started!”

Harry the rabbit and his two friends, Emily and Lewis, were hopping around one bright day. Suddenly, they noticed a wise raven on the tree. Emily was curious and asked the wise raven what it was doing there. The wise raven replied, “I'm here to give you a present. I have a sack of treasures!” The three friends were so excited, they jumped up and down. Harry quickly asked, “What's in the sack?” The raven smiled and said, “If you use your wise minds, you'll find out!” The friends looked inside the sack and found many different things. They had fun playing with the treasures and couldn't thank the raven enough for the present. They waved goodbye to the wise raven who flew away happily.
