Title: Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, healthy tree. Under the tree, a little boy named Tim and his mom liked to relax. One day, it was hot and sunny, so they took their umbrella with them. "Mom, can I hold the umbrella?" Tim asked. "Sure, Tim," his mom said with a smile. Tim held the umbrella high in the air, and they both sat under the tree. They were happy and cool in the shade. But then, a big wind came and blew the umbrella away. Tim tried to catch it, but he was too slow. The umbrella went far away and was lost. Tim and his mom were very sad. They had no umbrella to keep them cool anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a rare toy that she loved very much. The toy was a cute bunny. One day, Lily and her toy bunny went out to play in the woods. As they played, Lily saw a big bear. The bear made her tremble with fear. She held her toy bunny tight and tried to run back to the cabin. But the bear was very fast and followed Lily. Lily and her toy bunny could not make it back to the cabin. The big bear caught up to them. It took Lily's rare toy bunny away from her. Lily was very sad and cried. She missed her toy bunny very much, but she could not get it back from the big bear. The end.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved playing with her friends and going for walks. One day, her parents told her she had to start learning math. Lucy didn't want to, so she refused. But her parents were very strict, so she had no choice. She didn't like math at all, so she tried to find ways to avoid it. She tried to find fake excuses and pretend everything was okay. But her parents didn't believe her. They knew she was refusing to learn and they knew it was bad. Finally, they told Lucy that she was not allowed to play anymore. She could not go out with her friends and she could not take her daily walks. Lucy was very sad and refused to do her math. The next day when Lucy's parents asked her if she was doing her math, she said no. They were very disappointed and refused to let Lucy ever play again. Lucy was very unhappy and knew this was all because of the fake excuses she made. She knew if she had just studied her math in the first place, none of this would have happened.

Once upon a time, there was a clean cat named Miss Kitty. Miss Kitty loved to play with her mint green ball. Every day, she would chase it around her house and yard. One day, Miss Kitty could not find her mint green ball. She looked under the bed, behind the door, and in her toy box. She started to miss her ball very much. Miss Kitty felt sad. Then, her friend, a little boy named Tim, came to visit. Tim saw Miss Kitty looking sad and asked, "Why are you sad?" Miss Kitty told him about her missing mint green ball. Tim helped her look for it. They found the ball under a big tree in the yard. Miss Kitty was so happy and thanked Tim for helping her. They played with the mint green ball together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a youth called Tom. Tom was happy and fine. One day Tom heard about a fun game and he wanted to join in. So Tom ran off to find his friends to join him in the game. He found them in the park and they were all having a great time. They played together for hours and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day everyone said goodbye and joined hands for one final group hug. It had been a fine day for Tom and his friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tree named Oxy. Oxy was a very good tree. She could make something called oxygen. All her friends loved her because she made them feel alert and happy. One day, Oxy's friend, Bunny, came to her and said, "Oxy, can you teach me how to make oxygen too? I want to help my friends feel alert and happy like you do." Oxy smiled and said, "I can try, but it's something only trees can do." Just then, a big wind came and blew Oxy and Bunny away to a new place. In this new place, there were no trees, just lots of rocks. Oxy was sad because she could not make oxygen without her tree friends. Bunny saw Oxy was sad and had an idea. Bunny hopped around and found some seeds. He gave the seeds to Oxy and said, "Let's plant these seeds and make new tree friends. Then you can make oxygen again!" Oxy and Bunny worked together to plant the seeds. Soon, new trees grew, and Oxy was able to make oxygen again. All the animals in the new place were alert and happy, and Oxy and Bunny became the best of friends.
