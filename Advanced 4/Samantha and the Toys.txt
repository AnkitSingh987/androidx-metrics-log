Title: Samantha and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Samantha. She was only three years old and had a lot of energy. One day, Samantha was feeling extra mischievous and decided to make a messy mess in her room. She threw her toys all around the floor and made a giant pile of stuff. Suddenly, Samantha noticed something strange - her legs were shrinking! She was very puzzled and ran to her mom. "Mummy, my legs are shrinking. What's happening?". Her mom shook her head and laughed. "I think you have been up to no good, my love, and this is what happens when you make messes!" Samantha's mom picked up all the toys and tidied up the room. As soon as the room was clean, Samantha's legs started to grow back to normal size. She was very relieved and vowed never to make another messy mess again.

Once upon a time, there was a hero named Tim. Tim was a big, strong hero who helped people every day. One day, Tim met a little boy named Max. Max was sad because his toy was broken. Max looked at Tim with big, sad eyes and said, "Can you help me fix my toy?" Tim wanted to help Max, but he knew that another hero named Tom was jealous of him. Tom was always trying to be the best hero. Tim was worried that if he helped Max, Tom would get mad. But Tim knew that being a hero meant helping others, even if it made someone jealous. So, Tim took the toy and used his strong hands to tear off the broken part. Then, he found a new part to fix the toy. Max was so happy to have his toy fixed. He thanked Tim and said, "You're the best hero ever!" Tim smiled and knew that being a hero was about helping others, not worrying about being jealous.

Once upon a time, there was a little bridge over a dry river. The bridge was sad because no one ever walked on it. One day, a small bird came to the bridge. "Hello, bridge," said the bird. "Why are you so sad?" The bridge spoke, "I am sad because no one walks on me." The bird had an idea. It flew around and told all the animals about the bridge. Soon, the animals came to the bridge. They walked on it and played on it. The bridge was happy. Now, the bridge was not sad anymore. It had many friends who walked on it every day. The bird and the bridge became best friends. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, powerful boat. It liked to go fast in the water. One day, it wanted to go to a port far away. The boat was very happy and excited. On the way to the port, the boat heard a loud sound. It was a big storm with lots of wind and rain. The boat was scared, but it tried to be brave. It wanted to get to the port safe and sound. The powerful boat tried hard to fight the storm. But the storm was too strong for the boat. The boat could not make it to the port. In the end, the boat broke into small pieces and sank in the deep water. It was a sad day for the powerful boat.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. The wise owl had a friend, a little girl named Sally. Sally liked to dress up in pretty clothes and play with the wise owl. One day, Sally and the wise owl were playing near a faucet. The wise owl said, "Sally, be careful with the faucet. It can make a big mess if you turn it on too much." Sally listened to the wise owl and played carefully. Later, Sally saw her cat playing near the faucet. She remembered what the wise owl said and quickly turned off the faucet. The cat was safe, and there was no big mess. Sally was happy she listened to the wise owl, and they played together all day.
