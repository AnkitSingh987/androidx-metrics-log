Title: Ella and Max and the Elephant

One day, a big elephant named Ella saw a little mouse named Max. Ella was very serious, but she wanted to make a new friend. She wanted to invite Max to her house to play. Ella walked up to Max and said, "Max, do you want to come to my house? We can play with my trunk and have fun!" Max was scared of Ella's big trunk, but he wanted to be brave. He said, "Okay, Ella. I will come to your house and play with you." When they got to Ella's house, they started to play. Ella's trunk was very big and strong, but Max was very small and weak. They had a hard time playing together. Max was sad because he could not play like Ella. Ella saw that Max was sad, and she wanted to fix it. She thought, "What can we do together that will be fun for both of us?" Then, she had a great idea. She said, "Max, let's play hide and seek! You can hide, and I will try to find you with my trunk." Max loved this idea, and they played hide and seek all day. They both had so much fun, and they became best friends. The story teaches us that we should always try to find ways to include everyone, even if they are different from us.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a man who loved to sell things. One day, he found a silly lamp. The lamp was not like any other lamp. It was big, round, and had a funny face on it. The man thought it would be fun to sell the silly lamp. He took it to the market, where people buy and sell things. He put the lamp on a table and waited for someone to come and look at it. Many people came to see the silly lamp. They laughed and said, "What a funny lamp!" A little girl saw the lamp and loved it. She asked her mom if they could buy it. Her mom said yes, and they took the silly lamp home. The man was happy that he could sell the silly lamp, and the little girl was happy to have a new, funny friend in her room.

One day, a curious little girl named Lily found a cube in her room. It was a big, colorful cube with pictures on it. Lily picked it up and looked at it. She wondered what it could do. Lily's mommy came into the room and saw her with the cube. "Lily," she said, "do you believe that cube is magic?" Lily's eyes got big and she nodded her head. "Yes, mommy, I believe it is magic!" Mommy smiled and said, "Let's play a game with the magic cube. We can take turns making a wish and then see if the cube makes it come true." Lily was very excited to play the game with her mommy. First, Lily wished for a yummy snack. She closed her eyes, held the cube tight, and said, "Magic cube, please give me a yummy snack." When she opened her eyes, mommy had a plate of cookies for her. Lily giggled and said, "The magic cube worked!" Next, it was mommy's turn. She wished for a fun day with Lily. They played games, danced, and laughed all day long. At bedtime, Lily hugged the magic cube and said, "Thank you for a fun day, magic cube." She went to sleep with a big smile on her face, excited for more adventures with her magic cube.

It was a special day for the family. It was time to celebrate! Daddy got out some colorful balloons and put them everywhere. He asked his three year old daughter to help set off the alarm. She was so proud, and couldn't wait to press the big red button. Once she pushed it, the alarm began to beep and everyone in the house woke up. "Good morning everyone! Happy Celebrate day", said Daddy with a smile. The little girl clapped her hands together with joy. She liked celebrating very much. Mommy had made some special breakfast, and everyone dug in with a huge appetite. Everyone had lots of fun chatting and playing together, it was the best Celebrate day ever! In the evening, they all went outside and let off some colorful fireworks. It was a beautiful show. The little girl was so proud of how much fun she had. Everyone hugged each other, happy to have had such a special day to celebrate.
