Title: It and the Rainbow

It was a gloomy day. All around, the sky was grey and the ground was wet. Molly was feeling a bit down. She wanted to do something fun - something exciting! Then she had an idea. She picked up some cardboard from the ground and dragged it inside. She started to write then. She drew colourful lines and shapes - like a rainbow! Molly felt much better when she was writing. Her mom saw what Molly was doing and smiled. She helped Molly write more and put her rainbow design onto the cardboard. Now they were both happy. The gloomy sky was forgotten, replaced with colour and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a yellow book called a journal. She loved to draw and write in her journal every day. It was her favorite thing to do. One day, Sue took her journal outside to play. She found a nice spot under a big tree. Sue was very happy drawing in her yellow journal. But then it started to rain. The rain came down fast and wet everything. Sue ran home, but her yellow journal was left under the tree. The rain spoiled her journal. All of her drawings and words were wet and gone. Sue was very sad. She learned to be more careful with her things, but she missed her yellow journal.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, she found a mysterious mitten on the ground. It was blue and very soft. Lily wanted to find the owner of the mitten. Lily started to follow footprints she saw near the mitten. The footprints were small, just like hers. She walked and walked, looking at the footprints on the ground. At last, Lily found a little boy named Tom. He was looking for his lost mitten. Lily showed Tom the mysterious mitten she found. Tom was very happy and thanked Lily. They played together and became good friends. And they always took care of their mittens.

---

One day, a small, delicate bird named Lily was flying to her nest. She saw a big puddle on the ground. Lily wanted to play in the puddle, but she knew she had to get home. She thought, "Should I delay going home to play in the puddle?" Lily decided to play in the puddle for a little bit. She jumped and splashed in the water. Soon, her friend, a little squirrel named Sam, came by. He saw Lily playing in the puddle and said, "Lily, you should not delay going home. Your mom will be worried!" Lily knew Sam was right, but she did not want to stop playing. She said, "Just a little more time, Sam. I am having fun!" But then, dark clouds came and it started to rain. Lily and Sam knew they had to get home quickly. They ran and flew as fast as they could, but they were both wet and cold. When they got home, Lily's mom was worried. She said, "Lily, you should not have delayed coming home. It is not safe to play outside when it is raining." Lily felt bad for making her mom worry. She promised she would not delay going home again. From that day on, Lily always went straight home after playing. She learned that it was important to listen to her friends and not to delay going home. And sometimes, when the sun was shining, Lily and Sam would play in the puddle together, but only when they had enough time.

Once there was a little girl, who was only three. She was very curious and always wanted to explore. One day, she went out walking with her family. She saw a pond that was dirty with mud. She wanted to get closer, but it seemed too deep yet. The little girl looked around and found a long, sharp stick. She thought if she could use it to poke the mud, then she could get closer. But every time she tried, the stick didn't work. The mud was too thick, and it was impossible to move. So the little girl went home feeling discouraged and dirty. But then the next day, she came back with a plan. She used her sharp stick to dig deeper, and soon the mud wasn't so thick anymore. She moved closer to the pond, and at last she was able to get a closer look! She smiled with joy. From then on, she was never afraid to explore with her sharp stick.
