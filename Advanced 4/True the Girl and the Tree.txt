Title: True the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named True went for a walk. She wanted to meet new friends. She walked and walked until she saw something big and tall. True saw an impressive tree. It was very big and had many leaves. She said "hello" to the tree. The tree did not talk, but True felt happy. She liked the tree a lot. She wanted to be friends with the tree. Every day, True would visit the impressive tree. They would play together. True would hide behind the tree and then jump out. True and the tree were very good friends. And that is how True met her impressive tree friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a red glove that he loved. He wore it all the time. One day, he lost his glove. He was very sad and ashamed. Tim looked for his glove all day. He asked his friends to help him find it. They all looked for the glove, but they could not find it. Tim felt tired and wanted to relax, but he knew he had to find his glove. At last, Tim found his glove in a big tree. He tried to get it, but he fell and hurt his leg. Tim was very sad because he could not play with his friends anymore. The moral of the story is to always take care of your things and not be ashamed to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable girl named Lulu. Lulu loved to eat avocado. She had a big avocado tree in her yard. One sunny day, Lulu went to the tree to pick an avocado. She saw her friend, Tim, standing under the tree. Tim said, "Lulu, can I have an avocado too?" Lulu smiled and said, "Of course, Tim! You can have one." Lulu and Tim ate their avocados together. They were very happy. Lulu's avocado tree was the best in the town.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a whistle. He blew it and heard a loud sound. Tim was careful with his new toy. He played with it all day long. He showed it to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a whistle!" His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Be careful with it and don't lose it." Tim nodded and went outside to play. While playing, he saw a sad girl named Lily. She was crying because her toy was broken. Tim wanted to help Lily, so he blew his whistle. Something unexpected happened! The whistle made a magic sound and fixed Lily's toy. The whistle could restore broken things! Tim and Lily became friends and they played together. They used the whistle to fix more toys and make other kids happy. From that day on, Tim and Lily were very careful with the magic whistle. They knew it was special and could help many people. They promised to always use it for good and to share it with others who needed help.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy lived on a big farm with other animals. Fluffy was very nice and liked to help everyone. One day, Fluffy saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Fluffy wanted to help the bird escape from the farm to find its family. So, Fluffy put the bird on its back and walked around the farm to look for the bird's family. As they walked, Fluffy and the bird saw a big tree. The bird's family was in the tree! Fluffy was happy to help the bird find its family. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bird's wing was not hurt at all! The bird had tricked Fluffy to get a ride to its family. Fluffy was sad, but the bird said thank you and Fluffy felt happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball that could bounce very high. This ball was so enormous that all the kids in the town loved to play with it. One day, a little girl named Sally found a bottle of perfume. She thought it smelled so nice that she wanted to share it with her friends. Sally took the perfume and the enormous ball to the park. She saw her best friend, Timmy, and said, "Timmy, I found this nice perfume. Let's put it on the big ball and make it smell good too!" Timmy agreed, and they put some perfume on the ball. Now, the enormous ball not only could bounce high, but it also smelled very nice. All the kids in the park loved the new smell and had so much fun playing with the ball. They laughed and took turns bouncing the ball, enjoying the sweet scent in the air. The big ball brought all the kids together, and they played happily ever after.
