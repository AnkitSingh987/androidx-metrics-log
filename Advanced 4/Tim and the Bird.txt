Title: Tim and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree with many olives. A little boy named Tim lived near the tree. He loved to eat the olives from the tree. One day, he saw a big jar under the tree. He thought, "I can pour the olives into the jar and take them home." Tim started to pick the olives and pour them into the jar. A little bird was watching him from the tree. The bird said, "Tim, don't be greedy. Only take what you need." But Tim did not listen. He wanted all the olives for himself. As Tim was about to take the jar home, something unexpected happened. The jar was so heavy that it broke! All the olives fell out and rolled away. Tim was sad and realized he should have listened to the bird. The moral of the story is to not be greedy and only take what you need.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim's mom suggested they make a healthy snack. Tim thought it was a good idea. Tim's mom used oil to cook some yummies. She put the oil in a big pan and then added the food. Tim watched as the oil made the food sizzle and pop. He was excited to eat the healthy snack. When the snack was ready, Tim and his friends sat down to eat. They all loved the healthy snack cooked in oil. They thanked Tim's mom for her suggestion and played outside until the sun went down.

Mommy and John wanted to go the underground. John had never been there before, but he was excited to go exploring. Mommy offered to lend him a flashlight to help him find his way. He grabbed it excitedly and thanked Mommy for being so helpful. John set off from the entrance, flashlight in hand. He looked around, amazed at all the funny things he saw. He climbed up big hills and then rolled down them. It was so exciting! Finally, John spotted a funny shaped object in the corner. He wasn't sure what it was, so he asked Mommy if she could lend him a hand. She smiled and joined him, holding the flashlight while he investigated. It turned out to be an old toy car! John thanked Mommy again for being so helpful. Thanks to her, they had a wonderful time exploring the underground and he even found a new toy.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Paul and Kate. They said hello to each other almost every day. One day, Paul and Kate decided to share their toys. Paul brought his toy truck and asked Kate if he could borrow her doll. Kate said "Yes!" Paul and Kate were so happy that they decided to play a game together. They made a pretend house out of lumber and added furniture and decorations. They had lots of fun playing together until Paul decided to be naughty and not share some of the lumber. Kate was very sad and took her doll home. Paul felt bad that he did not share, so he decided to apologize to Kate. He wrote her a card with the words "I'm sorry I was naughty and did not share" and included a smiley face. Kate was so happy to get an apology from Paul. They shared a hug, and decided to always remember to share from then on.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the market with his mom. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining bright. Tim held his mom's hand as they walked through the market. They saw many things like fruits, toys, and clothes. At the market, Tim saw a nice man selling ice cream. "Mom, can I have an ice cream, please?" Tim asked. His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. It is hot today, and ice cream will help you relax." They bought the ice cream and sat under a big tree. Tim enjoyed his cold ice cream while his mom relaxed beside him. They both felt happy and cool on the hot day at the market.
