Title: Lucy the Girl and the Fence

One day, a little girl named Lucy was walking in the park. She saw a big fence and decided to follow it. As she walked, the fence started getting taller and taller and soon, it was very deep for her to get around. She tried to hop over but it was too high. Lucy started to get worried. She started to cry, feeling helpless. A nice old lady came to help her. She said, "Why don't you try to find a way around the fence? Perhaps a little looking will help." Lucy smiled and started to look around. She noticed a small opening in the fence and hopped through it. She was so happy to have solved her problem. The old lady smiled. She said, "Never give up, little one! You can do it!" Lucy thanked the old lady, and from then on, she followed her own advice. Whenever there was a problem, she would try her best to find a way around it.

Once upon a time, there was a witch who lived in a small house. She had a big cat and they liked to talk. They would discuss many things. One day, they saw a little boy who did not know much. He was ignorant. The witch and her cat had an idea. They wanted to play a trick on the boy. The witch and her cat told the boy to go to the forest. The boy did not know it was a bad place. When he went to the forest, he got lost. The witch and her cat laughed. The boy was sad and scared. The end.

One evening, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He was a fearful boy who did not like the dark. His mom came in and said, "Tim, it's time to print your picture for school tomorrow." Tim went to the big machine that could print. He put the paper in and pushed the button. The machine made a loud noise. Tim was scared and ran to his mom. His mom said, "Don't be scared, Tim. It's just the printer." But Tim was still scared. He went to bed without his picture. In the morning, he was sad because he did not have his picture for school.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She saw a small, scared bunny hiding behind a tree. The bunny had hurt its skin when it fell. Lily wanted to help the bunny feel better. Lily started to look for something to help the bunny. She found some soft leaves and a long vine. She used the leaves to cover the hurt skin and the vine to hold them in place. The bunny felt better and was not scared anymore. The bunny and Lily became good friends. They played in the woods every day. Lily was happy she could help the bunny and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. She was 3 years old and always liked to dress up. She went to her mom's bedroom and asked if she could wear some of her makeup. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course! You can pick whatever you'd like." Daisy happily ran to the makeup and began to select different colors to put on her face. When she was finished she asked her mom, "Do I look nice?" Mom replied, "You look very comfortable and pretty." Daisy felt so excited and smiled. She had never felt more beautiful. She thanked her mom and ran to the living room. Everyone smiled when they saw what a beautiful little girl she had become.

Once, there was a boy named Jack who was very foolish. One day, Jack had a million ideas for fun! He wanted to try something new. Jack managed to find an old iron in his Mom's closet. He thought it would be fun to play with. He managed to pick up the iron and take it outside. Jack managed to make some sparks with the iron, but it was very dangerous. Suddenly, the iron was too hot and Jack dropped it in the grass. The grass caught fire and spread quickly. Jack was very scared. Luckily, Jack managed to get help from some nearby adults. They were able to put out the fire. Jack was so relieved! He had been foolish to play with the iron, but thankfully he managed to stay safe.
