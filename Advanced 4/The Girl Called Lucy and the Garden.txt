Title: The Girl Called Lucy and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lucy who was three years old. Lucy was a very curious girl, so one day she decided to explore her garden. As she was walking through it, she noticed a brilliant bag laying under a bush. She couldn't resist her curiosity and immediately decided to go and have a look at what it was inside. As soon as she got close to the bag, she saw something poking out of it. It was a brilliant blue tail. Lucy got scared for a second and hesitated. But then her curiosity made her brave, so she slowly started to reach out her hand to poke the bag. Suddenly, a little voice said: "Hey! Don't poke me! What are you doing?" It was a very small and cute hedgehog who was peacefully sleeping inside the bag! Lucky calmed down and started to laugh. She said: "I'm sorry, I didn't know you were here. I just wanted to see what was inside the bag." The hedgehog started to laugh too, and they soon became friends. From that day on, Lucy and the hedgehog became best friends and went on adventures together in the garden. They had brilliant times!

It was early in the morning and Sam already had his things ready to go. He was three years old and was excited to go on the bus. His mum said to him, “Now be a good boy and don’t repeat yourself on the bus.” Sam said that he would try not to. Sam loved the bus, it was big and loud and always moving. He always found it attractive, he just couldn’t help looking at it. He almost forgot to listen to his mum as he stared but finally climbed on the bus. Once he was on, he found a seat and asked his mum for his favourite toy. His mum said, “I think you have enough toys, why don’t you look around and find something interesting?” So Sam looked around the bus and his eyes lit up, he saw something he liked. He started to repeat the same word again and again, “Pretty, pretty!” His mum asked “What do you see?” and Sam said, “I see that big bus outside, it looks so attractive!” His mum laughed, “Looks like you can't resist the bus. Let’s go.” And they went on the bus.

One day, a grumpy cat named Max was walking to the station. He saw a big, red train. Max wanted to go on the train, but he was scared. He sat and looked at the train for a while. Then, Max saw a group of ants. The ants were marching in a line. Max looked at the ants and said, "Why do you march like that?" One ant stopped and said, "We march to work together. It's fun and helps us get things done." Max thought about it and decided to march like the ants. He marched all the way to the train. When he got on, he saw a big, soft seat. Max sat down and smiled. He was happy to be on the train and not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and pet dog, Buddy. Every morning, Tim's dad would read a newspaper to know what was happening around them. Tim liked to sit with his dad and look at the pictures in the newspaper. One day, Tim found a story in the newspaper about a magic tree. The tree was not a regular tree. It could make wishes come true. Tim wanted to see the tree and believe in its magic. He asked his dad if they could go find the tree. His dad smiled and said, "We can try, but it might just be a story." Tim, his dad, and Buddy went for a walk to look for the magic tree. They looked in the park, near the river, and in the forest. They did not find the tree, but they found many other regular trees. Tim felt a little sad but still believed in the magic tree. On their way back home, they saw a small tree with colorful leaves. Tim ran to the tree and made a wish. He wished for a big ice cream cone. Suddenly, an ice cream truck came around the corner. Tim's dad bought him a big ice cream cone, and Tim believed that his wish had come true. From that day on, Tim knew that even regular things could be magical if you just believe.
