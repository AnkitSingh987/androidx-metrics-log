Title: Joey and the Book

Joey had a wonderful day! He found an attractive library and decided to explore it. He was very excited when he saw the shelves filled with colorful books. He chose a book and off he went to a corner to read. He was so engrossed in the story that he didn’t realize it was time to quit. Suddenly, a bell rang and one of the librarians told him it was time to go! Joey was not happy about it but he had no choice but to quit. He reluctantly gathered up his possessions and headed for the exit. As he walked out, he realized the library was the most attractive place he had ever been. Joey decided to come back soon and play in the library another day!

Once upon a time, there was a famous rock named Rocky. Rocky had a friend named Poppy. Poppy was a pretty flower. They lived in a big garden and played together every day. One day, Poppy said, "Rocky, I want to be famous like you." Rocky thought for a moment and said, "Poppy, you can be famous too. Let's think of something fun to do." So, Rocky and Poppy thought and thought. Then, they had a big idea. They decided to make a show for all their friends in the garden. They practiced and practiced, and soon they were ready. On the day of the show, all their friends came to watch. Rocky and Poppy started to dance and sing. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. Poppy started to grow bigger and bigger! All the friends gasped in surprise. Now, Poppy was as famous as Rocky. They were both very happy, and all their friends loved the show. From that day on, Rocky and Poppy were the best of friends, and they always played together in the big garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby lived in a nest on a tree. One day, Bobby saw a long wire. He did not understand what it was for. Bobby asked his friend, the squirrel, "What is that long wire for?" The squirrel said, "I don't know, but I see other birds sit on it. Maybe you can try it too." So, Bobby tried to sit on the wire. He was scared at first, but then he felt happy. He could see his home and all his friends from the wire. He felt successful. Bobby told all his friends about the wire. They all tried it and liked it too. They were all happy and successful. Bobby learned that trying new things can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly and play with his friends. But Tim was also very alert. He knew he had to finish his tasks before sunrise. One day, Tim saw a big, dark cloud coming. He knew something bad was going to happen soon. But Tim wanted to play with his friends before the dark cloud came. So, he flew fast and played with his friends all day. When the dark cloud came, Tim was not ready. He did not finish his tasks before sunrise. The dark cloud brought a big storm with it. Tim's home was destroyed and he was very sad. Tim learned that he should always finish his tasks before sunrise.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree, but he felt a hurt in his shoulder. He did not want to stop, so he kept going up. As Tim went higher, he met a bird. The bird asked, "Why are you climbing with a hurt shoulder?" Tim just gave a shrug and said, "I want to see the top." The bird told Tim, "Be careful, the top is not safe." Tim did not listen to the bird. He wanted to see the top, so he kept climbing. When he got to the top, he saw a big egg. Tim was very happy to find the egg. But then, something unexpected happened. The egg started to crack open. A big, scary monster came out of the egg! Tim was very scared. The monster looked at Tim and said, "You should not have come up here." The monster picked Tim up with his big hand and threw him down from the tree. Tim fell down and landed on the ground with a big thud. His shoulder hurt even more now. He wished he had listened to the bird and stayed safe.
