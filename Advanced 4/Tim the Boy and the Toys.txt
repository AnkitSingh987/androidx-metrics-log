Title: Tim the Boy and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the night. Every night, when it was time for bed, he would weep. He thought the night was weird and scary. One night, Tim heard a soft sound from under his bed. He looked and saw a tiny, glowing bug. The bug said, "Don't weep, Tim. I am a magic bug. I can help you not be scared of the night." Tim stopped weeping and asked the bug for help. The magic bug told Tim to close his eyes and think of a happy place. Tim did what the bug said. When he opened his eyes, he saw that his room was full of happy things. There were balloons, toys, and even a big, soft teddy bear. From that night on, Tim was not scared of the night anymore. He knew the magic bug was there to help him. The weird, scary night was now a fun and happy time for Tim. And the magic bug became Tim's best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious metal path that lead far away over big mountains. No one knew where it led. One afternoon, a little 3 year old girl decided to find out. She took her big brave steps over the metal path that was full of unknowns. As she kept walking, the path got more and more mysterious. Suddenly, up ahead she saw the most beautiful place. A sparkling kingdom with lots of amazing things! The metal path had lead her to the most magical and wonderful place.

Once upon a time, there was a grape. This grape was very popular. All the kids loved the grape. The grape lived in a pretty garden with many other grapes. One day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird wanted to eat the popular grape. The grape did not want to be eaten. The grape said, "Please, quit! I do not want to be eaten!" The big bird felt bad and quit trying to eat the grape. The grape was very happy. The popular grape stayed in the garden with all the other grapes. They played and had fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy was feeling particularly bored. He asked his mum if he could do something fun to relax. She said he could go for a bubble bath in the bathroom. The little boy was so excited, he couldn't wait! He was singing and skipping all the way to the bathroom. He filled up the bathtub and smelt the lovely soap bubbles. He looked around and noticed his mother had left a toy boat in the water too. The little boy jumped right in and played with the boat and bubbles for a long time. Meanwhile, his mum was feeling a bit envious, as she wished she could be in the bubble bath with him too! As the little boy was feeling nice and relaxed, he remembered the best way to end a bubble bath - a snuggle with Mum. He went and found her and said, "Thanks Mum for the bath. Now I'm nice, relaxed, and ready for a cuddle."

One day, a boy and his dog went to play by the river. The sun was hot and bright. The boy felt the sun burn on his skin. He said, "Dog, let's go swim in the river to cool down!" The dog barked, "Yes, let's go!" They both jumped into the river and splashed around. They had lots of fun. The boy saw something shiny in the water. He picked it up and saw that it was a big, shiny rock. The boy took the rock to his mom. She said, "Wow, this is a rich rock! It has gold in it!" The boy and his mom were very happy. They did not know that the rock could talk. The rock said, "Thank you for finding me! Now I can help you be rich too!"

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to sing. She practiced singing every day and she had a special song that she particularly liked. But one day, she struggled to sing the words properly. Even though she practiced as hard as she could, she just couldn't get it right. She felt so helpless and disappointed in herself that she gave up and decided not to sing her song anymore. Then one day she heard the song coming from outside and she thought of trying again, so she began to sing the song. At first, her voice was shaky but with each try, the little girl managed to sing the song better and better. Soon, she was able to sing her song with confidence and she was so proud of herself that she started to spread her joy with everyone she knew. The little girl had overcome her struggle to sing and she felt happier than ever!
