Title: The Gray Bathtub and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a gray bathtub. The bathtub lived in a small house with a little boy named Tim and his mom. Every night, Tim would take a bath in the gray bathtub before going to bed. One night, while Tim was in the bathtub, he saw a tiny light shining. The light came from a little star that was stuck in the bathtub's drain. Tim said, "Hello, little star. Why are you in my bathtub?" The star replied, "I fell from the sky and got stuck here. Can you help me get back home?" Tim wanted to help the little star. He asked his mom to come into the bathroom. With her help, they lifted the star out of the bathtub and placed it near the window. The star began to shine brightly, and Tim knew it was happy to be free. As a thank you, the star promised to shine extra bright for Tim every night. From that day on, Tim always looked at the shining star before going to sleep, and he knew he had made a new friend.

A cat named Sam was very independent. Every day, Sam would rush to the office with his friend, a dog named Max. They liked to play and have fun in the office. One day, Sam and Max went to the office as usual. But when they got there, they saw something unexpected. A big, green frog was sitting on the office chair! They were very surprised. Sam and Max looked at each other and decided to be friends with the frog. They played and had fun together in the office. The frog became their new friend, and they all had a great time. The independent cat, Sam, learned that making new friends can be fun and exciting.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big gift to give to his friend, Sam. Tim wanted to wrap the gift and write a note for Sam's birthday. One day, while Tim was wrapping the gift, his big sister, Sue, came into the room. Sue was angry because Tim had used her special paper to wrap the gift. Sue took the gift away from Tim and tore the paper off. Tim was sad and cried. Tim tried to find new paper to wrap the gift, but he could not find any. He did not know what to do. Sam's birthday came, and Tim had no gift to give. Both Tim and Sam were sad.

Once upon a time, there lived a man and woman who lived on a cliff. The cliff provided them safety and comfort, so they stayed there a long time. Every morning, the man and woman would go for a walk together and admire the beauty of the cliff and marvel at how alert they felt every time they looked out from the edge. One day, the man and woman heard a cry coming from the cliff. They looked all around and found a small bird that had been caught in the weeds. The man and woman approached the bird very gently and provided it a safe home in the nest atop the cliff. The man and woman returned to their nest every day to check in on the bird and give it food and water. The bird began to look stronger and stronger, until one day it flew away happily. The man and woman watched it go and felt so alert and happy to have provided such a nice home. The man and woman stayed in the same home on the cliff and were always so aware of their surroundings. Every morning, they would take a walk to the cliff and be thankful for the safety and beauty provided to them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a long, pretty dress that she loved to wear. One sunny day, Lily went to play outside near a well. As she played, she met a friendly frog. The frog said, "Hi, Lily! I like your dress. Can you spin around for me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, Mr. Frog!" She spun around and around, making her long dress twirl. The frog clapped his tiny hands and said, "That was fun! Thank you, Lily!" They played together all day long. When it was time to go home, Lily said, "Goodbye, Mr. Frog. I had fun today!" The frog said, "Goodbye, Lily! Let's play again soon!"
