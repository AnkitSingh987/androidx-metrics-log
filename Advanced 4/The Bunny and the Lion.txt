Title: The Bunny and the Lion

Once upon a time there was a little bunny who loved to hop. Every day she would hop around the meadow looking for something exciting. On this day she found an unexpected surprise; a safari! The little bunny couldn't believe her luck! She hopped over to the safari and saw that it had all kinds of silly things inside. There were lions, elephants, zebras and monkeys! The little bunny was so excited she ran over to the lions and said, "Hi Lion! What's your name?" The lion replied, "My name is Rex, what's yours?" The bunny jumped for joy and said, "My name is Hop! Can I come on your safari?" Rex laughed and said, "Of course Hop, hop on in and join us!" So the little bunny hopped in and had the best time ever. They went on a silly adventure and visited all the animals on the safari. Eventually they made it back to the meadow and said goodbye to all their new friends. The bunny hopped off with a smile, knowing that she would remember this safari adventure forever.

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He liked to paint pretty pictures. The painter was independent and happy. He painted every day. One day, the painter went to the park. He saw a swing. The painter thought, "I want to try the swing." So, he sat on the swing and began to swing back and forth. He felt very happy. As he swung, the painter had an idea. He went home and painted a picture of the swing. He showed his friends, and they all loved it. The painter felt proud and kept painting more beautiful pictures.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In the town, there was a big race. All the people were very competitive. They wanted to win the race. They all put on their fast shoes and got ready to run. One day, a new person came to town. He saw the people and wanted to unite them. He thought if they all worked together, they could do something great. He tried to talk to them, but they were too busy getting ready for the race. Near the race, there was a toilet. It was old and not very nice. The new person had an idea. He told the people, "Let's fix the toilet and make it nice for everyone." But the people did not listen. They only cared about the race. The day of the race came. The people ran as fast as they could. They did not see the old toilet in their way. They all fell into the toilet and got very dirty. The new person was sad. He wished they had worked together to fix the toilet. But now, it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable boy named Tim. He had a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. Tim loved to play with his toy gun all day. One day, Tim wanted to measure how far his toy gun could shoot. He went outside to play with his friends. They took turns shooting the soft balls and measuring the distance. Tim's gun could shoot the farthest. But then, Tim had an idea. He thought it would be fun to shoot the balls at birds in the trees. His friends said no, but Tim did it anyway. He shot the ball and hit a bird. The bird fell from the tree and got hurt. Tim felt very sad. He knew he should not have hurt the bird. The moral of the story is to always be kind to animals and not to use toys to hurt others.

Once upon a time, there was a big office. It was very far away. In this office, people needed to work. They needed to write, draw, and talk. The office was always busy. One day, a small cat came to the office. The cat was lost. The people in the office did not know what to do. They did not know where the cat came from. The cat was not like other cats. It was very smart. The cat started to help the people in the office. It could write, draw, and talk too. The people were very happy. The cat was not lost anymore. It was now part of the office family. The office was not so far away anymore. They all worked together and had fun.
