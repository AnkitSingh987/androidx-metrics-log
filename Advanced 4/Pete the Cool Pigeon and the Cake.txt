Title: Pete the Cool Pigeon and the Cake

One day, a cool pigeon named Pete flew to a little house. In the house, a girl named Sue was baking a cake. Pete looked in the window and saw the cake. He thought it looked very yummy. Pete knocked on the door. Sue opened it and said, "Hi, pigeon! Do you want some cake?" Pete nodded his head. Sue said, "Wait here, I will get you a piece." Pete was very happy. But when Sue went to get the cake, she tripped and fell. The cake went flying and landed on the floor. Pete was sad, and Sue cried. They could not eat the cake now. So Pete flew away, still hungry and sad.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, heavy automobile. It was red and shiny. The automobile liked to drive around the town and say "beep beep" to all the people. The people liked the automobile and would wave as it went by. One day, a little girl named Lucy asked her mom if she could ride in the heavy automobile. Her mom thought about it and then said, "I will permit you to ride in the automobile, but you must be very careful." Lucy was very happy and said "thank you" to her mom. Lucy climbed into the big, heavy automobile and they drove around the town together. They beeped the horn and waved to all the people. Everyone was happy to see them. Lucy had a lot of fun and felt very special in the big, red automobile.

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play together. One day, their mom gave them a treat: two big cookies with chocolate chips. Lily was happy and said thank you, but Max was not. He wanted a different treat: a candy bar with nuts. He said no to the cookie and asked for the candy bar. His mom said no, that was not a good treat for him. He had to eat the cookie or nothing. Max was angry and refused to eat the cookie. He threw it on the floor and stomped his feet. Lily felt sorry for Max, but she also liked the cookie. She asked her mom if she could have Max's cookie too. Her mom said yes, if Max did not want it. Lily smiled and took the cookie. She ate it slowly and enjoyed every bite. Max watched Lily eat the cookie. He felt sad and hungry. He wished he had not refused the cookie. He looked at the candy bar and saw that it was pale and old. He did not want it anymore. He wanted a cookie like Lily. He said sorry to his mom and asked for a cookie. His mom said sorry, but there were no more cookies. She had only made two. She said he could have some fruit or yogurt instead. Max said okay, but he still felt bad. He learned a lesson: he should not refuse a good treat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She was playing with her toys in her room. She felt uncomfortable because it was very hot inside. Sally wanted to go outside to play, but she did not know how to find the exit. Sally asked her big brother, Tom, for help. Tom was good at finding things. He took her hand and they walked together. They looked for the exit so they could go outside and play. They found the exit and opened the door. The sun was shining and it was a beautiful day. Sally and Tom played outside and had lots of fun. They were happy that they could help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gun. He would shoot at objects in his room, like his toy car or his ball. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his toy gun. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to shoot at the leaves. Tim aimed his toy gun at the tree and shot. The toy gun made a loud noise, but nothing happened to the leaves. Tim was sad, but then he saw a balloon flying high in the sky. He decided to shoot at the balloon instead. He aimed at the balloon and shot. To his surprise, the balloon popped! But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew out of the popped balloon! The bird had been hiding inside the balloon all along. Tim was amazed and happy to see the bird. The bird flew down and landed on Tim's shoulder, and they became friends. From that day on, Tim and the bird would play together, and they had lots of fun.
