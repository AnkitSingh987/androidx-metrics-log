Title: Tim the Boy and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the kitchen. He liked to pretend he was a big chef, making yummy food for his family. But sometimes, Tim would not behave. He would make a big mess and not clean it up. One day, Tim's mom told him he could not play in the kitchen anymore. She said, "You must behave and not make a mess, or you cannot play here." Tim felt sad and thought it was a stupid rule. He wanted to play in the kitchen so badly. Tim decided to try and behave. He played in the kitchen and made sure not to make a mess. When he was done playing, he cleaned up all his toys. Tim's mom was happy and let him play in the kitchen again. Tim learned that behaving was not so stupid after all.

Once upon a time, there was a wide bird with big wings. One day, the bird saw a pot on the ground. The bird was hungry, so it flew down to look inside the pot. The bird said, "I hope there is food in this pot." But when the bird looked inside, it saw a tiny bug. The bug said, "Please don't eat me!" The bird felt sorry for the bug and said, "Okay, I won't eat you." Suddenly, the tiny bug turned into a magic fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you for not eating me. Now, I will help you soar very high in the sky." The bird was happy and with the fairy's help, it soared high into the sky. The bird and the fairy became best friends and had fun playing together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big rhinoceros named Remy. Remy was nervous because he did not have any friends. One day, he saw a little bird named Bella. Remy wanted to be friends with Bella, but he was scared to talk to her. Remy walked up to Bella and said, "Hi, I am Remy. Can we be friends?" Bella looked at Remy and said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend." Remy was very happy to have a friend, but he was still nervous. One day, a big storm came to their home. Remy and Bella were scared. They needed to find a safe place to hide. Remy said, "Bella, we can hide under that big tree." They went under the tree and stayed safe until the storm was over. After the storm, Remy was not nervous anymore because he had a friend to help him. Remy and Bella were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there lived a 3-year-old boy called Tommy. Tommy loved playing with his uncle Harry. Every day they would run and jump around the garden, playing hide and seek. One day, Uncle Harry said to Tommy "Let's play a new game!". "What is it?" asked Tommy excitedly. "Snap" said Uncle Harry. They both sat on the grass and Uncle Harry shuffled the cards. Then he placed the cards in the middle and said "On your marks, get set, SNAP!". Tommy and his uncle played snap for ages until finally Uncle Harry saw a card on the floor. He picked it up and said "I saw this dead card, I think it means I win!". Tommy jumped up and cheered "You won Uncle Harry!" Uncle Harry smiled and gave Tommy a big hug. They spent the rest of the day playing all sorts of fun games. It was a great day!

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She loved to sing, but she was too scared to sing in front of people. One day, her mommy gave her a microphone as a gift. The microphone was small and shiny, just like Lily's favorite toy. Lily's mommy said, "Lily, you can use this microphone to sing. It will help your voice be loud and strong." Lily was excited to try it, but she was still shy. She practiced singing with her microphone in her room, where nobody could hear her. One day, Lily's daddy had to work in the garden. He asked Lily to help him. Lily took her microphone with her and started to sing while they worked. Her daddy smiled and said, "Wow, Lily! Your voice is so beautiful! Don't be shy, you can sing for everyone!" Lily felt happy and brave. From that day on, she sang with her microphone for her family and friends, and she was not shy anymore.
