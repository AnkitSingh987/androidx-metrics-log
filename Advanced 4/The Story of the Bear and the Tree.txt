Title: The Story of the Bear and the Tree

Once, there was a little girl. She was sad because she had lost her teddy bear. She wanted to find it. So, she went outside and started walking. Suddenly, she heard someone screaming. She was scared, so she hid behind a tree. Then she saw a lot of police cars. All the policemen were very angry. The little girl was scared, so she started to cry. But the police saw her and they asked her why she was crying. She told them about her teddy bear. So, the police helped her look for it. After a while, they found it! The little girl was so happy, she screamed with joy. All the policemen smiled and one of them even gave her a hug. The little girl was so thankful to all the policemen. She knew that from then on, she would never be afraid of the police.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jake and Jody. They liked to play outside in the sunshine, explore nature and have fun. One day, Jake held up a glove and said, "Look Jody, I found this glove. It looks reliable." Jody replied, "Cool, can I have a turn?" So Jake gave the glove to Jody, who immediately dropped it! Jake laughed and said, "No worries, I can pick it up." He bent over and grabbed the glove from the ground. He handed it back to Jody, who said, "Thanks Jake, you're so reliable!" Jake couldn't help but smile.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little girl named Lily lived with her mom and dad. Lily loved sweet things like ice cream and candy. But she did not like vegetables. Every day, her mom would ask her to eat her vegetables, but Lily would say, "No, I don't want to eat them!" One sunny day, Lily saw her mom praying. "Mom, what are you doing?" she asked. Her mom said, "I am praying for you to like vegetables. They are good for you." Lily thought for a moment and decided to pray too. "Please help me like vegetables," she said. The next day, something strange happened. Lily's mom made a new dish with sweet vegetables. Lily took a bite and was surprised. "Wow, this is yummy! I like these sweet vegetables!" she said. From that day on, Lily started to enjoy eating vegetables because they were sweet and good for her.

One day, Jack and his parents went to the store. Jack was very impatient and kept asking for different things. “Can I have that, mom?” Jack asked eagerly, pointing to a big car. “No, Jack, that’s too expensive,” said Jack's mom. “Let’s look at something else.” They went to another aisle and Jack noticed a toy boat. “Can I have that boat, mom? Please?” said Jack. “No, the price is too high,” said his mom. Jack was getting more and more impatient. He saw a toy train on the shelf and asked, “Can I have that, mom?” Mom said, “Yes, you can have it.” Jack was so excited. He quickly grabbed the train, but in his haste, he accidentally dropped it. It broke into many pieces. Jack was sad. He said, “I’m sorry, mom. I didn’t mean to break it.” Mom said, “It’s ok, Jack. Sometimes things break, but that’s okay.” Jack smiled and hugged his mom. They bought a new train and Jack was happy.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Cherry and Jade. They were walking around the icy park when they saw something strange. It was a big, square shaped object made of ice. "Let's go take a closer look!" said Jade. So they slowly walked towards the icy shape. As soon as they got close, the shape started to speak. "Hello!" it said. Cherry and Jade were very surprised. "What kind of shape talks?" asked Jade. "I'm an ice servant. I was sent here to help you," said the icy shape. Cherry and Jade started laughing. They couldn't believe what they were hearing. "This is so cool!" said Cherry. They thanked the ice servant for its help and continued walking in the icy park. They had a wonderful time and were forever grateful to the ice servant!
