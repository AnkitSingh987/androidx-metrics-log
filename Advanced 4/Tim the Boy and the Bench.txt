Title: Tim the Boy and the Bench

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable because he lost his favorite lollipop. He looked everywhere in his room, but he could not find it. Tim felt that he would never be happy again without his lollipop. One day, Tim saw a little girl crying in the park. He dared to ask her, "Why are you crying?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy, and I don't know where to find it." Tim thought about his lost lollipop and decided to help the little girl find her toy. Together, they looked and looked until they found the girl's toy under a bench. She was so happy and thanked Tim for helping her. As they were walking home, Tim found his lost lollipop on the ground. He was so happy that he could not stop smiling. The moral of the story is that when we help others, good things can happen to us too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy bear named Ben. Lucy loved Ben very much. They played together every day. One day, Lucy and Ben went to the doctor's office. The doctor was very patient. Lucy said, "I want to be a doctor when I grow up." The doctor smiled and said, "That is a very useful job." Lucy and Ben played doctor at home. Lucy would check Ben's ears and give him pretend medicine. Ben was always patient and never complained. They had lots of fun playing together. And one day, Lucy grew up to be a real doctor, just like she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby who loved to look up at the ceiling. They would lie down and watch the fan spin and make the air feel cool. One day, the baby wanted to show the mommy something special. "Mommy, look!" the baby shouted. The baby pointed up towards the ceiling. "What do you see?" asked the mommy. The baby smiled a big smile and said "cold". The mommy laughed and knew exactly what the baby meant. Above the baby and the mommy, the cold air was coming in through the window. The baby and the mommy enjoyed their special moment, looking up at the cold air coming in through the window. It made them both feel happy and safe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her ball near the hedge. The hedge was very big and green. One day, she saw a helpless baby bird on the ground. Amy wanted to help the baby bird. She thought she could carry it back to its nest. So, she picked up the bird and walked to the hedge. She looked for the nest, but it was too high for her to reach. Amy tried to find someone to help, but no one was around. She felt very sad and scared for the baby bird. In the end, the baby bird could not get back to its nest. Amy cried because she could not help the baby bird. The bad ending made her very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Amy. She liked to go for a wander - to explore the world around her. One day, Amy was walking in the park. She saw a younger girl in the playground, crying. She went to say hi and asked the girl: "Why are you sad?". The little girl sniffed and said: "My lunch box broke and I dropped it on the ground, and now my food is all over the place." Amy wiped away the little girl's tears. She felt bad for her, so she asked: "Is there anything I can do to help?". The little girl smiled. She said: "It's OK, I'm not hurt. I can pick my food up and everything will be alright". Amy was very relieved, she said: "That's great. I'm glad you're OK and that you can still have lunch". The little girl thanked Amy and waved goodbye. Amy walked back home, happy that she had been able to help.
