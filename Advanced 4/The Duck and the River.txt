Title: The Duck and the River

Once there was a duck who wanted to fly but he didn't have any wings. He asked the other birds for help. Everyone just laughed at him. But then, a rich nightingale heard his plea. He said that he can help the duck fly. The nightingale took the duck to a river and said, "You will be free to fly if you make your wings from the river clay." The duck was confused and asked, "How can I make wings from the river clay? I don't know how!" The nightingale said "Use your imagination! If you work hard, and trust your instincts, you can make your wings." The duck was determined, so he took some river clay and started to work. After some time, the duck made beautiful wings! He was so happy and he flew away. He was finally free! The moral of the story is that even when you don't have something, you can always make it with hard work and using your imagination!

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a box of pastel colors. They were mysterious because they were shiny and new. She wanted to draw pretty pictures with them. She saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. As she was drawing, a little boy named Tom saw her. He asked, "Can I join you?" Lucy said, "Yes, you can." So, Tom sat down next to her, and they started to draw together. While they were drawing, a big wind came. It blew away all the pastel colors. Lucy and Tom tried to catch them, but they were too slow. The wind took the pastel colors far away, and they could not find them. Lucy and Tom were very sad because they could not draw anymore.

Lily and Ben are playing with blocks. They want to make a big tower. They put many blocks on top of each other. But the tower falls down. They try again and again. But the tower always falls down. "Why does the tower fall down?" Lily asks. She is sad. "Maybe the blocks are too heavy," Ben says. He is smart. "What is heavy?" Lily asks. She does not understand. "Heavy is when a thing is hard to lift. Like this block," Ben says. He shows Lily a big block. He tries to lift it, but it is too heavy. He drops it on the floor. It makes a loud noise. "Oh, I understand. Heavy is when a thing is hard to lift. Like this block," Lily says. She points to the big block. She tries to lift it, but it is too heavy. She drops it on the floor. It makes another loud noise. "Shh, be quiet. Mom is sleeping," Ben says. He is worried. "Sorry, Ben. I did not mean to be loud. Let's make a small tower. A small tower is not heavy," Lily says. She is sorry. "OK, Lily. Let's make a small tower. A small tower is easy to lift," Ben says. He is happy. They make a small tower with small blocks. The tower does not fall down. They are proud of their tower. They smile at each other. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive dog. This dog could do many tricks. The dog had a friend who was a veterinarian. The veterinarian loved animals and helped them when they were sick. One day, the dog and the veterinarian found a problem. A small bird was sad because it could not fly. The dog and the veterinarian wanted to solve the problem and make the bird happy again. The veterinarian checked the bird and found a hurt wing. With love and care, the veterinarian fixed the wing. The dog stayed by the bird's side, making it feel safe. Soon, the bird could fly again. The dog and the veterinarian had solved the problem, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to dance. Every night, she would dream that she was a great dancer. One day, Kitty met a dog named Doggy. Doggy saw Kitty dancing and said, "Wow, you dance so well! I want to dance like you!" Kitty smiled and said, "Thank you! We can dance together!" Kitty and Doggy danced and danced. They had lots of fun. But then, a big rain came. The rain was very heavy. Kitty and Doggy got wet and cold. They could not dance anymore. They were sad and went home. The end.
