Title: The Great Park of Tim and the Sun

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. It was a fair day with a bright sun. Tim played with his ball and ran around. He was very happy. While playing, Tim noticed a long snake in the grass. The snake was green and it moved slowly. Tim was not scared, but he was curious. He went closer to the snake and said, "Hello, snake! Do you want to play with me?" The snake looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I want to play too." So, Tim and the snake played together in the park. They had a fun time on the fair day. Tim's new friend, the snake, made him very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was 3 years old, and she loved to play. One night, Sally lay down in her bed and wanted to go to sleep. But after she had closed her eyes, she could not fall asleep. She felt very troubled. Sally's mom heard her daughter crying and came into the room. "What's wrong, Sally?" asked Mom. "I can't sleep!" Sally said. Mom gave her daughter a hug. "It's ok," she said. "Try and take some slow deep breaths." Sally took some deep breaths, and soon felt her eyelids getting really heavy. She closed her eyes again, and this time she felt herself drifting into a deep sleep. The next morning, when Sally woke up, she felt like she had slept really well. She had a big smile on her face and she felt much better. Sally's teacher asked the class how they had slept the night before. When it was her turn to answer, Sally was able to proudly tell her teacher, "I had a good sleep and it made me happy!" The teacher smiled and replied, "That's wonderful, Sally. A good sleep helps us to be calm and focused in class as pupils." Sally felt proud of herself and was happy to have been able to solve her troubled sleep problem

Once there was a rabbit, he wanted to see the world so he decided to leave his home. He got lost and couldn't find his way back. He was so scared and felt very alone, he didn't know what to do. Then one day he met a fox, and they had a long meeting. The fox said that the rabbit should never go exploring without asking anyone for help. The rabbit learned his lesson and thanked the fox. But one day the rabbit decided to ignore the fox's advice and went exploring deep into the forest by himself. He got so lost that he could not find his way back home. He was feeling very scared and worried about his situation. The rabbit suffered in the deep, dark, dangerous forest for many days. He was so weak and exhausted from all the walking and hunger. Finally, when he had all but lost his courage, he got saved. The fox had come to rescue him, and the rabbit learned that he should never have listened to himself and should have listened to the fox's advice. He realised that sometimes it’s better to be safe than sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Emily. She had a special box which she loved opening up to reveal all sorts of surprises. One day Emily opened the box and to her amazement, inside was a dark bean. She giggled and said “Wow! What is this?” Emily’s mom came over and said “That is a magic bean! You can do all sorts of wonderful things with it. Shall I show you?” Gleefully, Emily said “Yes please!” Her mum smiled, said a few secret words and then opened up her hands and displayed a sparkly, bright red balloon. It was so beautiful! Emily was amazed and shouted “How did you do that mum?” Her mum smiled. “We can do lots of things with the dark bean. We just need to believe in magic and make a wish.”

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy camera. She would take pictures of her toys and her pet dog, Max. Lily was always happy when she played with her camera. One day, Lily felt anxious. She could not find her camera. She asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my camera?" Her mom said, "No, but let's look for it together." So, Lily and her mom started to search for the camera. Lily thought that maybe Max knew where the camera was. So, she took Max's leash and lead him around the house. They looked in every room, under the bed, and behind the couch. Finally, they found the camera in the toy box. Lily was so happy! She took a picture of Max and her mom to celebrate. From that day on, Lily always put her camera back in the toy box when she was done playing.
