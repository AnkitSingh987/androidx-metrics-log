Title: Lily the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dig in her backyard. One day, she found a filthy box. Lily was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. She opened the box and found a beautiful gem. It was a pretty red color and sparkled in the sun. Lily was so happy to find the gem. She showed it to her mommy and daddy. They were very proud of her. But then, Lily saw that the box was starting to rot. It smelled bad and was falling apart. She knew she had to throw the box away. Lily kept the gem and put it in a new, clean box. She would always remember the day she found the special gem.

Once there was a tight little girl, who was only three years old. She wanted to find something special to do with her day that she would remember! So she decided to start a trade. She collected all of her favourite things and spread them out on the floor with the help of her mum and dad. They wanted to trade with each other to find things they each liked. The little girl found a doll that her dad picked out, and her dad found a toy car that the little girl liked. They both had a tight hug to say thanks for getting something special. After trading, the little girl and her dad sat down and made a pretend tea party with all of their new toys. Even though the little girl was only three, she could remember this special game forever.

Once upon a time there was a school. All the children were very excited because today the class was getting a new blackboard. The kids rushed to the front of the classroom and eagerly waited for the teacher. She arrived and took her seat behind the big desk. The teacher smiled and said, "Today, we will write on our new blackboard!" She asked for an erase, but it was too weak. So, she asked one of the children to help. The little girl went to the back of the room and brought a much stronger one. The teacher smiled and thanked her. Then she asked everyone to get ready. She took a piece of chalk and began writing on the blackboard. Soon, everyone was clapping and having fun. After the lesson, the children sat down in their seats and began to work on their project. They enjoyed using the blackboard and talked about what a great day it was. Even though the erase was weak, it still did its job. Everyone was so happy that they got the chance to write on the new blackboard!

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big, colorful reef. He had many friends who lived there too. They liked to play and swim together. One day, while swimming with his friends, Fin saw a new fish. The new fish looked sad. "Hi, I am Fin. Why are you sad?" Fin asked. The new fish said, "Hi Fin, I am Luna. I am sad because I can't sing like the other fish." Fin wanted to help Luna. He had an idea. "Let's ask our friends to teach us to sing!" he said. Luna liked the idea. They swam to their friends and asked for help. Their friends were happy to teach them. They all sang together, and soon Luna could sing too. Luna was not sad anymore. She was happy and had fun singing with her new friends. They sang and played together in the big, colorful reef. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a big luggage. It was white and rolled along the ground. One day, a little girl saw the big luggage and decided it needed a hug. She walked over and gave the luggage a big hug. The luggage felt so happy and cheered! The girl kept hugging the luggage every day. They became good friends. The girl loved rolling the luggage around and giving it lots of hugs. One day the luggage wanted to give the girl a surprise. Inside the big luggage was a teddy bear! The girl was so excited and gave the teddy bear a big hug. The hug was so powerful it made the teddy bear squeak! The little girl and the big luggage were now best friends. The girl looked forward to giving the big luggage big hugs every day.
