Title: The Red Car and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. It lived in a garage. The garage was big and had many things inside. The car liked to stand in the garage when it was not driving. One day, the car saw something hidden behind a box. It was a small toy car! The little red car was so happy. It wanted to play with the toy car. They could be friends and have fun together. The little red car pushed the toy car out of the garage. They played all day long. They raced and laughed together. When the sun went down, they went back to the garage. Now, the little red car and the toy car stood together in the garage, happy and tired from a fun day.

Mum was having a chat with Beth. Beth had been feeling a bit sad lately and Mum was trying to find out why. Mum: "Beth, why are you feeling down today?" Beth: "I don't want to talk about it...." Mum: "It's okay, take your time. I'm here to listen." Beth looked at Mum with compassion in her eyes and slowly opened up. Beth: "I am worried that I am not special enough. All the other children are getting labels to stick on their clothes and I don't have one." Mum gave her a big hug and then went over to the drawer. She took out a label that had Beth's name on it. She put it on Beth's shirt and gave her a kiss. Mum: "There you go Amy. You are very special, and I love you very much." Beth smiled, feeling a little better about herself. Mum gave her a big hug and encouraged her to be courageous, reminding her that she always has someone to listen to her.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the zoo with her mom. They saw many animals like monkeys, elephants, and birds. Lily was very happy. When they reached the lion's cage, Lily stared at the big lion with wide eyes. The lion was lying down and looked impatient. Lily said, "Mom, why is the lion not playing?" Her mom replied, "The lion might be tired or hungry, dear." Lily wanted to make the lion happy, so she decided to sing a song for him. As Lily sang, something unexpected happened. The lion stood up and began to dance! Lily and her mom were amazed. The other people at the zoo saw the dancing lion and clapped. The lion was not impatient anymore, he was happy! And Lily learned that even a simple song can bring joy to others.

One day, a polite cat named Kitty went to the store to supply food for her family. She had a long list of things to buy. On her way, she saw a cross on the road. Kitty was not sure how to get across the road safely. Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, on the other side of the road. Doggy called out, "Kitty, wait! I will help you cross the road." Kitty felt happy to see her friend. They held hands and looked both ways before they crossed the road together. At the store, Kitty and Doggy had a little fight about what food to buy. Kitty wanted fish and Doggy wanted bones. They argued for a while, but then Kitty said, "Let's be polite and share. We can buy both fish and bones." Doggy agreed, and they both supplied food for their families. They crossed the road again, being careful and polite. Kitty and Doggy learned that it is important to share and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a great spade. He loved to dig with it. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his spade. He wanted to dig a big hole. As Tim was digging, he found a box. He was very surprised! He used his spade to open the box. Inside the box, there was a balloon. It was a big, red balloon. Tim was so happy! Tim wanted to play with the balloon. He let it go by mistake. The balloon flew up, up, up into the sky. Tim was sad. But then, the wind blew the balloon back to him! Tim caught it and was happy again. The end.
