Title: Polly the Parrot and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a tidy room, there lived a parrot named Polly. Polly loved to play and hide. She would hide behind the toys, under the bed, and even in the closet. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit Polly. Lily wanted to play with Polly. She asked, "Polly, do you want to play hide and seek with me?" Polly said, "Yes, yes! I love to hide!" Lily started to count, "One, two, three..." while Polly went to hide. Polly hid behind the big chair in the room. Lily looked everywhere until she found Polly. "I found you, Polly!" she said. They played hide and seek all day and had so much fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a witch who was very strong. She had powers that nobody else had. One day, a little girl came to visit the witch. She begged her for something, but the witch just laughed. So the little girl asked her again and again, but the witch still refused to help her. At last, the little girl got very angry and wished that the witch would disappear. Suddenly, the witch vanished. The little girl felt sad, realizing she had been very unkind. The moral of this story is that we should always be thoughtful and kind and never wish away someone who refuses to help us. We should try to be understanding instead.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a small seed that he wanted to grow into a big plant. He asked his mom how to make the seed grow. His mom told him, "Put the seed in some dirt, give it water, and put it in the sun." Tim did what his mom said. He put the seed in some dirty soil, gave it water, and put it by the window. Every day, Tim looked at the seed and waited for it to grow. He was very excited. One day, while Tim was watching his favorite show on TV, he saw the seed start to grow. It grew and grew until it became a big, strong plant! Tim was so happy that he danced around the room. His mom was proud of him for taking care of his plant. They both enjoyed watching it grow together.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy helicopter. It lived in a big forest with many trees. The helicopter had a friend, a little bird. They liked to play together every day. One sunny day, the helicopter said to the bird, "I don't know how to fly high like you. Can you show me how?" The bird said, "Of course! Let's try together." So they started to fly up and up. As they went higher, the helicopter felt less sleepy. It was so happy to learn how to fly high. The bird and the helicopter flew all day long. They had so much fun together. And the sleepy helicopter knew it had the best friend ever.

One day, a hairy dog found a toy pistol in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, the cat. The cat saw the pistol and wanted to play too. They decided to play a game of push with the toy pistol. The dog would push the pistol with his nose, and the cat would try to catch it. They laughed and played, taking turns pushing the toy pistol. The sun was shining, and they were having so much fun. After a while, they got tired and decided to rest under a big tree. They put the toy pistol down and lay next to it. The hairy dog and the cat were happy they had found the toy pistol and had enjoyed a fun day of play together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her colorful school. She was happy to see her friends and play with them. At school, they learned about sharing and being nice to each other. During playtime, Lily saw her friend Tim with a big, red ball. Lily wanted to play with the ball too. She went to Tim and said, "Please, can I play with the ball too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, of course! Let's play together." As they were playing, a little boy named Max tried to take the ball away. He did not ask, and he was not being nice. Lily and Tim were sad. But then, something unexpected happened. Max tripped and the ball rolled away. Max started to cry. Lily and Tim walked to Max and said, "Please, don't cry. You can play with us too, but you have to ask first and be nice." Max learned his lesson and they all played together nicely. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.
