Title: Mia and the Balloon

Mia was a small girl with a beautiful red dress. She looked at herself in the mirror and was very proud of her fashion. She started to giggle and lifted her hands above her head. She felt calm and joyous. Suddenly, Mia heard a voice. “Hey Mia, do you want to play?”. It was her big brother. He was holding a balloon in his hands and smiling. Mia approached him and replied, “Yes, I want to play”. The brother smiled and said, “Let’s go outside. I will show you how to lift the balloon high in the air.” They went out of the house and Mia grabbed the balloon with both her tiny hands. Her brother asked her to lift the balloon as high as she could. Mia was so excited and she lifted the balloon until it almost reached the sky. She felt so pleased and even shouted out loud as she watched the balloon drift away in the air. She felt a wonderful sense of calmness and joy inside her heart.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Sarah. She was a happy and successful three year old who had lots of friends. One day Sarah was visiting her friend Tom. Tom had a special watch and he liked to show it to people. Sarah asked him, "Can I see your watch?". Tom said, "Yes, but only if you give me a big hug!". Sarah thought this was a really strange request, but Tom said a hug was the only way to see his watch. So Sarah reluctantly agreed and gave him a hug. When she pulled away, Tom showed her the watch. He explained to her that it was important to always show kindness to one another - in this case, a hug had been more valuable than a watch. Sarah smiled and they hugged goodbye. The moral of the story is that kindness is more valuable than most things. No matter how successful you are, always be kind and generous to others.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man who had to deliver food to a stable. In the stable, there lived a happy horse and an angry cow. The man walked to the stable every day to give them food. One day, when the man came to the stable, the angry cow said, "I don't want this food!" The happy horse said, "I like the food. Thank you, nice man." The nice man felt sad that the angry cow did not like the food. The next day, the man came to the stable again. This time, he brought different food for the angry cow. The angry cow tried the new food and said, "This food is good. Thank you, nice man." The nice man was happy that both the happy horse and the angry cow liked the food he delivered.

Once upon a time, there was a pirate named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends on the big ship. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tom was a happy pirate, but he did not know many things. His friends would call him ignorant, but Tom did not mind. One sunny day, Tom and his friends found a big treasure box on an island. They were very excited! They tried to open the box, but it was locked. Tom saw a key on the ground and decided to try it. He kneeled down to pick up the key. His friends laughed at him because they thought it would not work. Tom put the key in the lock and turned it. To everyone's surprise, the treasure box opened! Inside the box, there were many colorful toys and candies. Tom's friends were so happy that they hugged him and said sorry for calling him ignorant. From that day on, they all played together and shared the toys and candies. And they never called Tom ignorant again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to bake with her mom. One sunny day, Lily and her mom decided to bake a light, yummy cake. They mixed the cake and put it in the oven. Lily was so excited to eat the cake. But, something unexpected happened. When they opened the oven, the cake was gone! Lily and her mom looked around the kitchen. They saw a trail of cake crumbs. They followed the crumbs and found their dog, Max, with the cake! Max had taken it out of the oven when they weren't looking. Lily and her mom laughed and baked another cake together. And this time, they made sure Max couldn't get it!
