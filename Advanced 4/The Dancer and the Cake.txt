Title: The Dancer and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little dancer. She loved to dance all day. One day, she went to visit her friend, the cook. The cook was making a tasty cake. The dancer said, "Yum! Can I have some cake?" The cook smiled and said, "Yes, but you have to dance for me first." The dancer danced and danced. The cook clapped and laughed. Then, they both sat down to eat the tasty cake. They were very happy and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Julie. She was three years old and loved to look at the stars at night. One night, Julie went out to the garden and looked up at the sky. There were so many stars twinkling in the dark sky and Julie was amazed. She gazes for a long time at the stars, feeling very peaceful. Suddenly, Julie saw a shooting star and asked her Mummy, “What was that?” Mummy smiled and said, “That was a shooting star! Make a wish and it might come true.” Julie closed her eyes, made a wish and watched the star until it was out of sight. When she opened her eyes, she felt very content. Julie and her Mummy cuddled up together in the garden and watched the starry night sky until it was time for bed. Julie felt very happy and peaceful.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. One day, Lily felt very hot. Her mom used a thermometer to check her. "Oh no, you have a fever," said Mom. Lily stayed in bed all day. She was sad because she wanted to play outside in the perfect sunny weather. Her mom gave her some cold water and told her to rest. The next day, Lily felt better. Her mom checked her with the thermometer again. "Surprise! Your fever is gone!" Mom said with a smile. Lily was so happy. She went outside to play and enjoy the perfect day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little stage. On this stage, many kids would play and have fun. One day, a bad dog came to the stage. The bad dog wanted to scare the kids. The kids saw the bad dog and were very scared. They did not want to play on the stage anymore. They ran to their mom and dad and told them about the bad dog. The mom and dad wanted to help the kids. The mom and dad went to the stage and saw the bad dog. They talked to the dog and told it not to scare the kids. The bad dog listened and went away. The kids were happy and went back to play on the stage. They were not scared anymore.

One day, a girl named Sally had to take an exam at school. The exam would include easy questions for her to answer. She was a little scared, but she knew she could do it. Sally's mom gave her a big, cold hug before she left for school. "You will do great on your exam," her mom said. Sally felt better and went to school with a smile. At school, Sally took the exam. The questions were not too hard for her. She was happy she could answer them. After the exam, Sally went home and told her mom all about it. They both laughed and hugged again. Sally knew she did her best.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, modern pizza shop. It was very pretty and had many colors. Lots of people liked to go there to eat pizza. The pizza was very yummy and had cheese, sauce, and bread. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to join her friends at the pizza shop. She put on her red shoes and walked to the shop. Lucy was very happy because she loved pizza and her friends. When Lucy got to the pizza shop, she saw her friends sitting at a big table. They were eating pizza and laughing. Lucy joined them and they all had a great time. They ate pizza and played games. It was a fun day for everyone.
