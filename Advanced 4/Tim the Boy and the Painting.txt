Title: Tim the Boy and the Painting

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to paint. One day, he found a heavy rock and decided to paint it. While Tim was painting the rock, a mosquito came by. The mosquito was very hungry. It wanted to bite Tim. Tim did not like mosquitoes, but he was too busy painting to notice it. The mosquito bit Tim on his arm. Tim felt a big itch and stopped painting. He tried to make the itch go away, but it only got worse. Tim was very sad because he could not finish painting his heavy rock. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very hungry. She looked at her plate and saw some amazing food. But she did not want to eat it. Amy started to complain. "Mom, I don't want this food," she said. Her mom told her to eat it anyway. Amy was sad but she took a bite. As she ate, something unexpected happened. The food started to talk! "Hello, Amy!" said the food. Amy was surprised. The food told her a funny story. Amy laughed and ate all of the food on her plate. She was happy and did not complain anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lily's mom said they were going to make a pie. Lily was very happy and excited. Lily helped her mom fill the pie with apples and sugar. She was a little anxious because she wanted the pie to be perfect. Her mom told her not to worry, and that the pie would be yummy. When the pie was done baking, Lily and her mom took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! They let the pie cool, and then they shared it with their family. Everyone loved the pie, and Lily was proud of her work.

Once upon a time there was a good princess. She had a toy that could print jewels. One day, the princess asked her mom, "Can we print me a special jewel?" Her mom said with a smile, "Of course. We can print you as many jewels as you want!" The princess clapped her hands together with excitement. She ran to the toy and pushed the print button. The toy began to hum and soon a shiny jewel popped out. The princess was so happy she shouted out, "It's so pretty!" Her mom looked at the jewel and said, "That is a very good jewel, my princess!" The princess smiled and ran around the room, showing the jewel to everyone. She wanted to print more and more jewels. So she asked her mom, "Can I print more jewels?" Her mom said, "Yes, as long as you are careful." The princess was delighted. She went back to the toy and pressed the print button. She spent the whole day printing jewel after jewel until she was very tired. Finally, the princess was happy to have lots of new jewels that were all good and beautiful.

Dave was very miserable, because he had lost his favourite lamp. He had looked everywhere, but he just couldn't find it. He asked all his friends, but nobody had seen his lamp. Everyone felt so sorry for Dave, and wanted to help him. But then one day, as Dave was walking past the park, he saw something shiny in the sun. He ran over and, to his surprise, he had found his lamp! He was so happy, and he quickly picked it up and hugged it tight. Dave went home, and he put his lamp back on the table. It felt nice to have it there and Dave was no longer miserable. He had completed his day, and it was a great feeling!

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to glow in the dark. He had a friend named Sue. Sue was a teach. She liked to teach Tim new things. One day, Tim and Sue were playing outside. The sun was going down. Tim said, "Sue, I want to show you how I glow in the dark!" Sue was excited to see Tim glow. They went to a dark room. Tim started to glow. Sue said, "Wow, Tim! You are so cool!" But then, a big wind came and blew the door shut. Tim and Sue were stuck in the dark room. They were very sad. The end.
