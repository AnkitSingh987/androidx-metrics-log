Title: The Helicopter Was Flying and the Bird

One day, a shy helicopter was flying in the sky. The shy helicopter did not like to be near other helicopters. It liked to fly alone. The shy helicopter saw a big bird. The big bird was sad. The big bird said, "I cannot fly fast." The shy helicopter wanted to help. The helicopter said, "I can help you fly fast." The big bird and the shy helicopter flew together. They went fast. The big bird was happy. It did not complain anymore. The shy helicopter was happy too. They became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had very long hair. Lily wanted her hair to be perfect for her birthday party. She asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you help me test my hair?" Lily asked. Her mom said, "Of course! Let's try some pretty styles." They tested many ways to make Lily's hair perfect. They tried a ponytail, braids, and even a bun. At last, they found the perfect style for Lily's hair. It was a beautiful braid with a big bow. Lily was very happy. She looked in the mirror and said, "My hair is perfect now! Thank you, Mom!" And they both smiled and got ready for the birthday party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to skip and play outside. One day, Lucy saw a dirty ball in her yard. She picked it up and thought, "I will play with this ball today." Lucy went to the park to play with her dirty ball. She met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi! I like to skip too! Can I play with your ball?" Lucy said, "Yes, but we must follow the law. We can only skip with the ball, no throwing." Lucy and Tim skipped with the dirty ball all day. They had so much fun! They were happy they followed the law and only skipped with the ball. At the end of the day, Lucy and Tim were tired but happy. They knew they would be friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a huge island. Many animals lived on the island. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit the island. Lily saw a big monkey and a small bird talking. The monkey said, "We need to prevent the big water from coming to our island. It will make our homes wet!" The bird agreed, "Yes, we must stop the big water!" Lily wanted to help the animals. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. She told the monkey and the bird, "Let's build a big wall to prevent the water from coming in!" The monkey, the bird, and Lily worked together to build the wall. They used rocks and mud to make it strong. The big water came, but the wall stopped it. The animals on the island were happy and safe. They thanked Lily for her help. The monkey said, "You are a good friend, Lily!" And they all lived happily on the huge island.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jackson and Mary. They both loved exploring the world around them. One day, it started raining. Jackson said, “Let’s explore the mysterious store in the town.” “Yes! Let’s do it,” said Mary. So they grabbed their raincoats and ran out into the rain. They stopped outside a big and mysterious store and stood there, looking up at it. “What’s inside?” asked Jackson, “It looks so interesting.” “Let’s go inside and find out!” said Mary, smiling. So they opened the door and went into the store. When they went inside, they saw lots of amazing things – clothes, toys, books and more! They spent lots of time exploring the store, picking out their favourite things. When it was time to go home, they were both very happy. They had had a wonderful adventure in the mysterious store.

One day, a jolly boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big ball on the grass. Tim picked it up and examined it. It was a ball for a game called pitch. He wanted to play with it. Tim saw a girl named Sue. He asked, "Do you want to play pitch with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I love to play pitch!" They played and had lots of fun. They laughed and smiled as they threw the ball back and forth. As they played, the sky got dark. Tim looked up and said, "I think it will rain soon." Sue nodded and said, "Let's play a little more before we go home." They played a bit more, and then the rain started. They laughed as they ran home, happy they had fun playing pitch before the rain came.
