Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a brown dog and a brown cat. They liked to play games together. But one day, they had a quarrel. The dog wanted to play a running game, but the cat wanted to play a hiding game. "Let's play the running game," said the dog. "No, I want to play the hiding game," said the cat. They could not agree, so they both went home feeling sad. The next day, the dog and the cat met again. They did not want to quarrel anymore. "Let's play a game we both like," said the dog. "Yes, let's play the jumping game," said the cat. So the brown dog and the brown cat played the jumping game together and had lots of fun. From that day on, they always played games they both liked and never had a quarrel again.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing in her room. She had lots of toys and furniture to play with. Sue was very happy. She liked to jump on her bed and dance around the room. Sue heard a noise outside her room. It was her cat, Tom. Tom was a dead cat, but he could still talk to Sue. "Sue, come play with me!" said Tom. Sue was surprised but happy to see her cat. Sue and Tom went to play in the living room. They played with the toys and ran around the furniture. Sue was having so much fun that she did not see the chair. She stumbled and fell. But Tom was there to help her up. They laughed and played some more. And from that day on, Sue and Tom were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy house in a small town. In this house lived a kind mommy and her little boy, Tim. They liked to play and laugh together. The house was always clean and happy. One day, Tim felt sick. His mommy wanted to help him feel better. She made some warm soup for him to eat. Soup was Tim's favorite food, and it always made him feel good. Mommy gave Tim the soup and sat with him. She comforted him by rubbing his back and telling him a story. Tim started to feel better after eating the soup and listening to mommy. They smiled and hugged, and Tim knew he would be okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch the sun rise every morning. One day, he saw an interesting automobile outside his house. It was big and red. Tim wanted to see it up close. Tim went outside to look at the interesting automobile. He saw a man standing next to it. The man was very tall and wore a blue hat. Tim asked, "Is this your automobile?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. This is my automobile." Tim and the man talked about the interesting automobile. The man told Tim how it could go very fast. They watched the sun rise together, and then the man drove away in his interesting automobile. Tim waved goodbye and knew he would always remember the day he saw the interesting automobile.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a nice big window. Outside the window, there was a garden with many flowers. The sun was shining and it was a mild day. One day, a little girl named Lily looked out the window. She saw a new flower starting to bloom. She was very happy. She ran outside to look at the flower. The flower was red and pretty. Lily went back to the kitchen to tell her mom. Her mom was happy too. They both went outside to see the flower. They smiled and clapped their hands. The flower made their day very special.

Once upon a time, there was a deer. It was a regular deer, with brown fur and big eyes. The deer lived in a forest with many trees. One day, the deer saw a squirrel. The squirrel said, "Will you play with me?" The deer said, "No, I refuse to play. I am too big to play with you." The squirrel felt sad and ran away. The deer felt bad for making the squirrel sad. So, the deer went to find the squirrel. When the deer found the squirrel, it said, "I am sorry. Let's play together." The squirrel was happy, and they played all day. From that day on, the deer and the squirrel were best friends. They played together in the forest every day, and they were never alone again.
