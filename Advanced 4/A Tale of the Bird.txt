Title: A Tale of the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, high tree. In the tree, there was a small bird. The bird was happy and liked to smile. One day, the bird saw a yummy meal on the ground. It flew down and started to eat. The meal was very tasty, and the bird smiled even more. After the meal, the bird flew back to the high tree. It sang a happy song and smiled. All the other birds heard the song and smiled too. They all had a fun day together.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to gather cookies. He had a big box to put all the cookies in. Tim wanted to find all the cookies he could, so he looked everywhere. He found cookies under trees, on the swings, and even in the sandbox! Tim was happy because he had lots of cookies now. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running to Tim and took his box of cookies away! Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He looked for more cookies that were available. He found more cookies and put them in a new box. This time, he hid the box behind a bush so the dog could not find it. In the end, Tim had a big box of cookies again and he shared them with his friends at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she saw a big box. The box had a label on it. The label said, "Toys for Lily". Lily was very happy. Lily opened the box. Inside the box, she found many toys. The toys include a doll, a ball, and a car. Lily was very excited. She played with the toys all day long. She even shared the toys with her friends. One day, it was a hot and dry day. Lily and her friends played outside with the toys. They played with the ball and the car. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, they put the toys back in the box. Lily and her friends were very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two cockatoos named Betty and Robert. They were very close friends and did everything together. They liked to sing in the tree and eat yummy fruit together. One day, they heard a strange noise coming from the other side of the forest. Betty and Robert decided to investigate. When they arrived, they saw a unique operation. There were two teams of different animals all helping each other. One team was separating branches and leaves into two separate piles. The other team was carrying and building the piles. Everyone was working together in harmony. Betty and Robert were so impressed by the operation that they decided to help. After a few hours, their hard work paid off. The leaves now formed two distinct piles. Together, Betty and Robert finished the unique operation. They were proud of what they had accomplished and smiled. From then on, they worked together on many different operations in the forest.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with print. Print is a big machine that makes pictures on paper. Lily and Ben can choose what pictures they want. They have fun with print. One day, Lily and Ben want to make different pictures. Lily wants to make a picture of a flower. Ben wants to make a picture of a car. They both push the button on print. But print can only make one picture at a time. "Stop, Lily! I want to make a car!" Ben says. "No, Ben! I want to make a flower!" Lily says. They fight over the button. They pull and push each other. They are angry and loud. Print does not like the fight. Print makes a loud noise. Print stops working. No more pictures come out. Lily and Ben stop fighting. They look at print. They are sad and sorry. "Sorry, Lily. I was mean to you." Ben says. "Sorry, Ben. I was mean to you too." Lily says. They hug and say sorry to print. They hope print will work again. They decide to share the button and take turns. They want to be friends and have fun with print.
