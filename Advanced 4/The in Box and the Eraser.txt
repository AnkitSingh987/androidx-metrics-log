Title: The in Box and the Eraser

One day, a green eraser and a blue eraser lived in a box. They were friends, but they liked to race. They wanted to see who was the fastest eraser. They raced on the table, and sometimes they raced on the floor. They had fun racing each other. One day, the green eraser and the blue eraser had a big race. They raced from one side of the room to the other side. The green eraser was winning, but then it fell off the table. The blue eraser stopped and looked down at the green eraser. The green eraser was sad and hurt. The blue eraser helped the green eraser get back on the table. They did not race that day. They learned that being friends and helping each other was more important than racing. From that day on, they still raced sometimes, but they always made sure to help each other and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. They were both very excited to go to the park together. When they reached the park, they noticed a big, tall billboard. It had funny pictures of flowers, lions and birds on it. They both laughed at how funny the pictures were. But their mom didn't allow them to play too close to the billboard. She said they must stay away from it. So they ran and played tag in the park instead. They laughed as they ran and had a lot of fun. Later on, they saw a dad with a little baby. He allowed the little baby to go close to the billboard and touch it. The baby looked at the funny pictures and smiled. After that, the two little friends decided to take turns touching the billboard. So, each of them got to go close and see the funny pictures. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there lived a great tiger. He lived in a forest with his family and was always happy. One day, he wanted to find somebody to marry. He went to his friends and asked, "Will you marry me?". His friends shook their heads and said, "No". So, the tiger went to his family and asked, "Will you marry me?". His family just laughed and said, "No". The tiger was very sad that nobody wanted to marry him. Then, one day, he came across a great tiger. He looked very happy. The tiger went up to him and asked, "Will you marry me?". The great tiger smiled and said, "Yes!". The tiger was so happy that his dream had come true. He married the great tiger and they lived in the forest together ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat named Tom. Tom was a big and strong cat. He liked to run and play all day. One day, he heard about a race. The winner of the race would get a trophy. Tom wanted to win the trophy, so he practiced every day. The day of the race came, and all the animals were there. There were dogs, cats, and even a little bunny. The race started, and Tom ran as fast as he could. He ran past the dogs and the cats, but the little bunny was very fast too. They ran and ran until they reached the finish line. Tom and the little bunny crossed the finish line at the same time. They both won the race and got a trophy. Tom was very happy. He learned that it was good to relax and have fun with his friends. From that day on, Tom and the little bunny played together and became best friends. They both loved their trophy and shared it with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim lived on a farm with his mom and dad. They grew wheat in their big field. One day, Tim went to the field and saw the wheat had turned yellow. He ran back to tell his mom and dad. They were happy and said it was time to pick the wheat. Tim helped his mom and dad pick the wheat. They turned the wheat into bread. Tim and his family ate the bread and it was yummy. They all smiled and were very happy.
