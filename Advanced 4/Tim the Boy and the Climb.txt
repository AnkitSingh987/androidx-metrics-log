Title: Tim the Boy and the Climb

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. Every day, he would climb trees, chairs, and even his bed. One day, Tim's mom gave a lecture to him. She said, "Tim, you must be careful when you climb. You could fall and get hurt." Tim listened to his mom and promised to be careful. From that day on, Tim was more careful when he climbed. He still had fun, but he knew that being safe was important too. Tim and his mom were happy, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little bird had a nest in a tree. The bird liked to fly and sing in the open sky. The bird was happy and loved her nest. One day, the bird saw a big egg in her nest. She did not know where it came from. She said, "Who put this big egg in my nest?" The little bird was very confused. She did not know what to do with the big egg. The big egg started to shake and crack open. A little baby bird came out of the egg. The little bird was surprised and said, "You are not my baby!" The baby bird said, "I know, but can you help me find my mom?" The little bird decided to help the baby bird find its mom. They became friends and were happy together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mommy. They saw a big tree and Lucy wanted to climb it. Her mommy said, "Be careful, Lucy!" While Lucy was climbing, she felt something uncomfortable on her hand. She looked and saw a bug. Lucy was scared and she slapped her hand to make the bug go away. The bug flew off and Lucy was happy again. Later, Lucy and her mommy went to the doctor for an operation. Lucy was scared, but her mommy said, "Don't worry, it will be quick." The doctor fixed Lucy's ear and she felt better. After the operation, Lucy and her mommy went home and ate ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to sing all day long. One day, Bobby found a heavy thing on the ground. He did not know what it was, but he liked it. He took the heavy thing to his nest. The next day, Bobby went to his friend, the wise old owl. The owl told Bobby that the heavy thing was a license. Bobby did not know what a license was, but he liked the sound of it. The owl told Bobby to keep the license safe because it was very important. Bobby took the license back to his nest and kept it safe. He sang a special song about the heavy license. One day, a big wind came and blew the license away. Bobby was sad, but he remembered the special song he sang. He sang the song and the license came back to him. Bobby was happy and knew that the license was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an elderly friend named Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown was very old and liked to sit in his cooler chair outside his house. Tim liked to visit Mr. Brown and play with him. One day, Tim thought it would be fun to scare Mr. Brown. He hid behind a big tree and waited for Mr. Brown to come and sit in his cooler chair. When Mr. Brown sat down, Tim jumped out and yelled, "Boo!" Mr. Brown was so scared that he fell off his cooler chair. He was very sad and hurt. Tim felt bad for scaring his elderly friend. From that day on, Tim never tried to scare anyone again.

Mommy was talking to little Sammy. She said, "It is time to be careful. Can you be careful?" Sammy nodded his head and smiled. Mommy pointed to a big bookcase in the corner of the room. She said, "This is a bookcase and I need you to be very careful. Do you think you can be careful with the bookcase?" Sammy nodded his head again. Mommy allowed Sammy to go closer to the bookcase. He looked at each shelf and the books on them. He took his time looking at each book and smiled. Mommy asked, "Can you be careful with the bookcase, Sammy?" Sammy looked at Mommy and nodded his head. Mommy smiled at Sammy and said, "Good boy!"
