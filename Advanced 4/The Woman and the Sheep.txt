Title: The Woman and the Sheep

Once there was a woman who lived in an ancient village. Every day she went about her daily tasks, fetching water from the well and helping to tend the sheep. One day, as she was singing a song as she worked, she heard a voice from the far hills. It said, "Behave yourself!" The woman stopped in her tracks, her heart pounding. Who could be out this far - and what did they mean by "behave"? She left her work, the bundle of cloth she was carrying thumped against the ground, and started walking towards the hills. As she walked, she felt a chill run through her. She thought she knew where the voice was coming from - an old abandoned shrine in the nearby woods. She knew the stories of the shrine; tales of a powerful spirit living in the ancient stones. When she arrived, she could hear the voice again, louder this time. "Behave!" it repeated, louder and firmer. The woman hesitated, then she knew what she had to do. She bowed her head, and said, "Yes, I will behave." The voice was silenced and the woman gave a weary sigh. She had done the right thing and knew that, from now on, she would always behave.

One day, a boy named Tim found a tape. He did not recognize the tape. It was in his toy box. Tim was a careless boy. He often left his toys all over the floor. He showed the tape to his mom. "Mom, what is this?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the tape. She smiled. "This is a special tape," she said. "It has a surprise. Let's watch it together." Tim and his mom put the tape in the TV. They sat down and watched. The tape had a funny show. Tim laughed and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The tape showed a video of Tim when he was a baby. Tim looked at his mom. "Is that me?" he asked. His mom nodded. "Yes, it is you when you were little." Tim could not believe it. He thought the tape was just a funny show, but it was really a video of him. Tim decided to be more careful with his toys. He did not want to lose any more surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a normal tree. This tree was in a big forest. One day, the sun was very hot. The forest caught fire. The fire made a lot of ash. The tree was sad because the forest was not pretty anymore. The tree said to a bird, "Help me make the forest pretty again." The bird said, "I will help you." The bird flew away to find seeds. The bird brought back the seeds to the tree. The tree and the bird planted the seeds in the ash. After many days, the seeds grew into small plants. The tree and the bird were happy. They worked hard and they did succeed. The forest became pretty again. The tree and the bird were very proud of their work.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tim. Tim lived near a big blue sea. Every day, he watched the boats sail on the water. Tim wanted to sail too, but he was scared. One day, Tim found a calendar. The calendar had pictures of boats. Tim looked at the boats and felt brave. He decided to sail on the big blue sea. Tim got on a little boat and started to sail. At first, he was clumsy, but soon he got better. Tim sailed all day and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, Tim went home. He was no longer scared and was happy he learned to sail.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to visit her grandma every day. Her grandma lived in a small house with a big tree in the front yard. Mia's grandma had a rough cat named Whiskers. Whiskers had fur that felt rough when Mia touched it. One day, when Mia went to visit her grandma, she saw that the telephone was on the floor. The telephone was broken. Mia asked her grandma, "What happened?" Her grandma said, "Whiskers knocked the telephone down. I can't call your mom now." Mia felt sad but then she had an idea. She said, "Grandma, let's fix the telephone together!" They worked together and fixed the telephone. After that, Whiskers never knocked the telephone down again. The moral of the story is that we can fix problems if we work together.
