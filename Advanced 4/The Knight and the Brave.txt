Title: The Knight and the Brave

Once there was a brave knight who was getting ready for battle with an enemy. He was wearing his shiny armor and held his tall, golden shield. As he prepared, his parents were worried. His mom turned to him and said, "Be careful son, and don't do anything stupid." The knight nodded and said, "Yes mom. I will be careful." He rode off towards the enemy, feeling very brave. But when he got there, he saw something unexpected. The enemy was not what he had expected - it was a dragon! He knew that the dragon was too strong for his sword, so he held up his shield. The dragon breathed a hot flame and the knight felt the heat on his armor. He could feel the shield start to crack and break. The brave knight then ran away as fast as he could, with the shattered shield. He left feeling embarrassed and ashamed. He had gotten too brave and it was stupid. But he had learned a lesson - never underestimate your enemies.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a small, green lizard on the ground. Tim was very happy and wanted to be friends with the lizard. He said, "Hello, lizard! Let's play!" The lizard was frightened and did not want to play. It yelled, "No, I am scared!" Tim did not want the lizard to be scared. He tried to be very quiet and gentle. He said, "Don't be scared, lizard. I will be nice." Suddenly, the lizard began to grow bigger and bigger. Tim was surprised and a little scared too. But the big lizard was not scared anymore. It said, "Thank you, Tim. I was scared when I was small. Now we can play together!" Tim and the big lizard became good friends and played together every day.

One day, an old man named Joe was driving his car. He was feeling very happy and smiled to himself. Suddenly his car started to make a strange noise. Joe pulled it over to the side of the road and shouted for help. Just then, a couple of kids walked by. One of them was a three year old named Penny. Joe held up his car's license and asked the kids if they knew how to help him. Penny laughed when she saw the license and said, "Why do you have such a funny looking license?" Joe smiled and said, "It's called a driver's license. I need it so I can drive my car safely." Penny was still laughing as she looked at the license. Joe then asked if Penny knew how to fix the car. She jumped up and said, "Yes, I know a way!" She ran off and a few minutes later came back with some tools and a dry cloth. She opened the hood of the car and wiped down all the parts until they were nice and clean. Joe was very relieved when the car worked again, but he was even happier that Penny had solved the problem. He thanked her and they laughed together as she waved goodbye and ran home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a cabinet for their house. Tim was very excited to help his mom pick out the perfect cabinet. At the store, Tim saw a big, red cabinet. He said, "Mom, I like this one!" His mom agreed and said, "Yes, Tim, this cabinet is nice." They were both happy with their choice. On their way home, Tim saw a little dog that looked guilty. The dog had knocked over a trash can. Tim told his mom, "Look at that dog, he did something bad." His mom said, "Yes, the dog looks guilty, but we all make mistakes." Tim smiled and they went home to put their new cabinet in the house.

One day, a small girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was spinning and spinning. It was a tornado! Lily was not scared. She thought the tornado was fun. Lily wanted to be friends with the tornado. She walked up to it and said, "Hi, tornado! My name is Lily. Will you be my friend?" The tornado stopped spinning and looked at Lily. It said, "Yes, Lily. I will be your friend." Lily and the tornado played all day. They had a lot of fun. Then, Lily found a sour lemon on the ground. She picked it up and gave it a kiss. The tornado saw this and laughed. Lily laughed too. They were very happy friends.
