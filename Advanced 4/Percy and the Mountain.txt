Title: Percy and the Mountain

Once upon a time there was a little mouse called Percy. Percy was very brave and always dared to do things that made others fearful. One day Percy's mom said to him, "Percy be careful, it's not always safe to be daring". But Percy was too excited and boldly ran off, daring to do anything. Percy soon found himself facing a very tall mountain. As he looked up at the mountain, he wondered if he'd be brave enough to climb it. "I'm scared, but I really want to do this!" he thought. So Percy took a deep breath and started to climb. But halfway up the mountain, Percy got stuck. He was too scared to climb any higher, and he just couldn't find the courage. Knowing he had to find a different way, he shouted to the heavens, "Is anyone up there? Can you help me?" Suddenly he heard a voice that said, "Percy, you don't have to do things that make you fear. It's OK to be brave and daring, but it's also important to be safe too. Just find the courage within yourself and you can accomplish anything. Percy thanked the voice and soon felt safe enough to climb down the mountain - although he did take it slow this time. Since then, Percy has been more careful and found that it was still possible to live a daring life without being fearful. Moral

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy fireman. He would run around the house, making siren noises and pretending to put out fires. One day, Tim's mom had a friend come over. The friend saw Tim playing and said, "I recommend you take him to see real firemen at the fire station." Tim's mom thought that was a great idea. So, the next day, she took Tim to the fire station. Tim was so excited to meet the real firemen and see the big fire trucks. The firemen showed him how they put on their gear and slide down the pole. They even let him sit in the driver's seat of the fire truck. But then, something scary happened. The fire alarm rang, and all the firemen had to go to a real fire. Tim's mom quickly took him out of the fire truck, and they watched the firemen leave. Tim was sad and scared. He didn't like seeing the firemen go to a real fire. From that day on, Tim didn't want to play with his toy fireman anymore. He realized that being a fireman was a very hard job, and it wasn't just fun and games.

One day, a grumpy musician was playing his drum. He felt sad because no one wanted to dance to his music. He walked to the park to find some happy kids. He thought, "Maybe they will dance and make me happy again." In the park, he saw a boy with a ball. The boy said, "Hi, Mr. Musician! Can you throw the ball with me?" The musician was still grumpy and said, "No, I want to play my drum." Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl ran to the musician and said, "I like your drum! Can I play it too?" The grumpy musician smiled and let her play his drum. The girl played the drum so well that everyone in the park started to dance. The musician was not grumpy anymore. He threw the ball with the boy, played the drum with the girl, and danced with everyone. The park was full of fun and music.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. This car loved to drive fast. The car had a leather seat. One day, the little car met a big car. The little car said, "Hi, big car! I like to drive fast. Do you like to drive fast too?" The big car said, "Yes, little car, I like to drive fast too! Let's drive fast together!" So, the little car and the big car drove fast together. They had so much fun. The little car's leather seat was soft and comfy. The big car liked the little car's leather seat too. The little car and the big car became best friends. They drove fast together every day. They were very happy. And they lived happily ever after.
