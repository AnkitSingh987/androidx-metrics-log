Title: Lily the Girl and the Flower

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her friends, the animals. One day, while playing, she saw a beautiful flower. It was red and had big petals. Lily wanted to pick the flower and show it to her friends. A wise old owl saw Lily and said, "Lily, don't pick that flower. It is dangerous." But Lily did not listen to the owl. She insisted, "I want to show it to my friends." The owl said again, "Please, Lily, it can hurt you and your friends." Lily thought about what the owl said. She decided to listen to the wise old owl. She left the flower in the garden and played with her friends. Lily learned that it is important to listen to those who know more than us. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Elly. Elly loved to eat peanuts. One day, she found a very big peanut under a tree. Elly was so happy and excited to eat it. Elly picked up the big peanut and said, "Yummy! This will be the best peanut ever!" But just as she was about to eat it, a little squirrel named Sammy came running up to her. Sammy looked sad and said, "Please, Elly, that is my last peanut. Can I have it back?" Elly looked at Sammy and then at the big peanut. She wanted to eat it, but she knew it was important to be kind and share. So, Elly said, "Okay, Sammy, you can have your peanut back." She gave the big peanut to Sammy. Sammy was so happy that Elly returned his peanut. He thanked her and said, "You are a very kind elephant, Elly." From that day on, Elly and Sammy became the best of friends, and they always shared their peanuts with each other.

Once upon a time in a big sea, there lived a poor oyster. His name was Ollie. Ollie was not happy because he did not have any friends. One day, a fish named Fin swam by Ollie. Fin said, "Hi, Ollie! Will you come play with me?" Ollie was so happy to have a friend. He said, "Yes, I will come play with you, Fin!" Ollie and Fin played all day. They swam and played with sea toys. Ollie was not a poor oyster anymore because he had a friend. They both were very happy and had lots of fun together. And from that day on, Ollie and Fin were best friends forever.

One day, a dog named Max found a big bone in his yard. He was very happy. Max wanted to spread the news to his friend, a cat named Lily. Max ran very fast to find Lily. When Max found Lily, he said, "Lily, I found a big bone in my yard! Come see!" Lily was curious and went with Max to look at the bone. They both ran fast back to Max's yard. When they got there, the big bone was gone! Max and Lily looked around and saw a squirrel holding the bone. The squirrel said, "I found this bone and I will share it with you." Max, Lily, and the squirrel all played with the bone and had a fun day.

Once there was a boy and a girl who lived far away in a distant land. One day they decided to venture away from home. They carried their bright, colourful bags as they went through the side of the woods. Suddenly a large, scary bear appeared! The boy tried to run away but the bear was too fast. He picked up the boy and carried him away. The girl tried to rescue him but she couldn't reach him. The bear and the boy travelled very far until they reached a dark cave. The girl called out the boy's name but she could not see him. She started to cry because she didn't know what had happened to her friend. The girl never saw the boy again. She thought that he must have been eaten by the bear and she would never find him. She looked for him far and wide until eventually, she had to accept that he was gone and would never return.
