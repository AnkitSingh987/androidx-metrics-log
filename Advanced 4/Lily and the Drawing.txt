Title: Lily and the Drawing

Lily liked to draw. She had many colors and papers. She drew her family, her friends, her toys, and her dreams. She was very good at drawing. She made many images with her colors. One day, her teacher said they would have a contest. The best image would win a prize. Lily was excited. She wanted to win the prize. She worked hard on her image. She drew a big rainbow, a happy sun, and a beautiful flower. She used all her colors. She was proud of her image. She showed her image to her friend Ben. Ben looked at her image. He said, "That is a nice image, Lily. But I think mine is better." He showed his image to Lily. He drew a scary dragon, a dark castle, and a brave knight. He used black and red colors. He said, "My image is more impressive, Lily. It is more exciting and cool. You should draw something like that." Lily felt sad. She thought her image was not good enough. She thought Ben was right. She thought she should draw something like him. She put away her image. She took a new paper. She tried to draw a dragon, a castle, and a knight. But she did not like it. She did not like the black and red colors. She did not like the scary and dark things. She did not have fun drawing. The next day, the teacher said it was time to show the images. She asked the children to put their images on the wall. Lily put her image on the wall. She did not look at it. She looked at Ben's image. She thought it was better. She thought he would win the prize. The teacher looked at all the images. She smiled. She said, "You all did a great job. You all have different styles and ideas. You all are creative and talented. I am proud of you all." She walked around the wall. She stopped at Lily's image. She said, "This image is very beautiful, Lily. I love the colors and the shapes. I love the rainbow, the sun, and the flower. They make me happy. This image is very impressive, Lily. You have a gift for drawing. You should accept your gift and be proud of it." Lily looked at her image. She looked at the teacher. She looked at Ben. Ben looked at her image. He looked at the teacher. He looked at Lily. He said, "You know what, Lily? You were right. Your image is better. It is more impressive. It is more happy and beautiful. I like it. I am sorry I said those things. You should not change your image. You should accept your image and be proud of it." Lily smiled. She felt happy. She felt proud. She said, "Thank you, Ben. Thank you, teacher. I like my image. I accept my image. I am proud of my image." She hugged her image. She hugged Ben. She hugged the teacher. She won the prize. She was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a queen who lived in a big castle. She was not an ordinary queen. She had a secret. She could talk to animals! One day, a little bird came to the queen's window. The bird was very sad. "Please help me," the bird said to the queen. "I lost my family. I don't know where they are." The queen wanted to help the little bird. So, she asked all the animals in the forest to help find the bird's family. The animals looked everywhere, but they could not find the bird's family. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bear came to the queen and said, "I can explain. I found the bird's family. They are safe in my cave." The queen was so happy. She thanked the bear and took the little bird to the bear's cave. There, the little bird found his family. They were all very happy. The queen's secret helped the little bird, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She was small and brave. But she had a fearfulness inside her. The girl's dad showed her something special - a wallet. He said, "Can you unlock it without my help?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, of course!" So dad gave her the key and said, "Go on, give it a try!" The girl put the key into the wallet and twisted. The wallet opened up with a pop! Inside, the girl found a surprise - a toy! The girl was so happy - she ran to her dad and hugged him tight. Her dad smiled and said, "You did it - your fearfulness was no match for you!" The girl smiled and hugged her dad even tighter. She knew that from then on, she would never be fearful again.
