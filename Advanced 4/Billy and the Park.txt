Title: Billy and the Park

One day, Billy and his mommy were walking in the park. As they walked, Billy was excited about all the new things he was seeing. He looked up at the sky and the trees, and he saw the pretty landscape. He bounced up and down with excitement. "Let's go in the playground, mommy!" Billy said. "OK, let's go," said mommy. So they went over to the playground. Billy was so excited! He ran to the big trampoline and started to bounce. He laughed and giggled as he went higher and higher. He could see the entire landscape from the trampoline. Mommy watched as Billy jumped, and she smiled. "Come on, Billy, it's time to go home," she said. Billy didn't want to go, but he knew it was time, so he got off the trampoline and they started walking home. He kept looking back at the playground - he was so excited!

Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog named Max. Max lived with his family in a small house and he was very happy. One day, Max went outside to play. When he was out in the garden, he saw a big number. "What's that number?" Max asked. The big number answered, "I am zero." Max was confused. He asked, "What is zero?" The big number said, "Zero is nothing. Nothing at all." Max didn't understand. He asked, "Why are you here, if you are nothing?" The big number said, "I'm here to stay. Nothing will make me go away." Max was very scared. He wanted to run away, but he was too scared to move. Suddenly, he felt something terrible and he was gone. The big number zero had sucked him into the sky. Max was gone and the big number zero remained in the garden. Nothing ever made it leave. The End.

Once there was a curious little girl. She was always exploring her world, looking for something new and exciting. One day, the little girl was outside in her backyard. She saw something shining in the grass. She went over and picked it up. It was a lamp! The little girl was very excited. She wanted to know what would happen if she pushed the button on the lamp. She was a bit scared, but very curious. So she pushed the button and something very special happened. Out of the lamp came a magical fairy! The fairy was so happy to see the little girl. She told her that she could make any wish she wanted, and it would come true! The girl thought for a moment and then she wished that all of her dreams would come true. So off the fairy went with the little girl’s wish. She knew it would come true!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and have fun. One day, Tim saw a big, heavy pillow on his bed. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Tim decided to grab the heavy pillow and play a game. He threw the pillow up in the air and tried to catch it. The pillow was so heavy, it made Tim fall down. But he just laughed and got back up to play some more. After playing with the heavy pillow for a while, Tim got tired. He put the pillow back on his bed and lay down. As he closed his eyes, he hugged the pillow tight. Tim fell asleep, happy and tired from all the fun he had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a normal girl who liked to play with her toys and enjoy her time with her family. She had a big smile that showed her happy lips. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and dad. They played on the swings, and Lily enjoyed the feeling of going up and down. She laughed and her lips were wide with joy. They had so much fun together. As the sun began to set, Lily's mom and dad told her it was time to go home. Lily felt a little sad, but she knew they would come back to the park another day. She gave her mom and dad a big hug and kissed them on the lips. Then, they all went home, happy and tired from their fun day at the park.
