Title: Lily the Girl and the Butterfly

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a shiny coin under her bed. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, mom! I found a coin!" Her mom looked at the coin and said, "That's a special coin, Lily. When it gets hot, it can melt and change shape!" Lily was excited and wanted to see it melt. The next day was a very hot day. Lily put the coin in the sun and waited. She was persistent and did not give up. After a while, the coin started to melt. Lily was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. As the coin melted, it turned into a beautiful butterfly. The butterfly flew around Lily and said, "Thank you for setting me free!" Lily was surprised and happy. She made a new friend, and they played together all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to read a book. He sat on the floor and opened his big, colorful book. His leg was next to the book, and he felt happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw Tim reading the book and said, "I want to read too!" Tim smiled and said, "Okay, let's read together!" They both read the book and looked at the pictures. After they finished reading, Tim said, "I am glad you are my friend, Sam. You are a reliable friend who likes to read with me." They both laughed and continued to read more books together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. Lily was an adventurous princess and she loved going on new adventures. One day, Lily decided to go camping. She quickly packed her camping bag with food, her favorite dress, and her crown. Lily ran to the forest, trying to reach her camping spot before the sun went down. As she ran, she heard a strange noise. When Lily reached the camping spot, she saw a large, lazy bear. The bear said, "Oh, hello princess! I was just looking for a place to rest." Lily couldn't help but smile. The bear asked if he could come and camp with her. Lily said, "Yes, of course you can! But why don't you pack your bag so you can join me?" The bear happily agreed and he waved goodbye to the Princess as he began to pack his bag. From then on, the two were always seen camping together!

Jimmy and his mommy were walking down the street when they stopped in front of a temple. Jimmy leaned against the door and shouted "Mommy, can I go inside?" Mommy said no, the temple was closed today, so Jimmy had to find something else to do. He was starting to get angry and he shouted again, "But Mommy, I'm ready to go inside!" Mommy was worried that Jimmy would be too loud, so she asked him to quiet down. Then she said "No, Jimmy. We have to wait until tomorrow, then we can go inside. It's not ready yet." Jimmy was disappointed, but he knew he had to obey his mommy. So he took a deep breath and let out a big sigh, and then he nodded and said ok. When tomorrow came, Jimmy and his mommy went back to the temple. This time the doors were wide open, and it was just like mommy said--the temple was ready! Jimmy was so excited that he let out a shout of joy. Mommy smiled and said, "See, I told you we just had to wait until the temple was ready!" Jimmy nodded and smiled, and together they went inside the temple to explore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a small bag of cheap beans. One day, Tom went to the park to play with his friends. He took his beans with him in case he got hungry. While playing, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue saw the beans and asked, "Can I have a bean, please?" Tom said, "Yes, you can have one." But as Tom opened the bag, a big wind came and blew all the beans away. Tom and Sue began to weep because they lost the beans. Then, something unexpected happened. The beans started to grow into big beanstalks right in front of their eyes! Tom and Sue stopped weeping and climbed the beanstalks. They found a magical land with lots of fun things to do. They played all day and forgot about their lost beans.
