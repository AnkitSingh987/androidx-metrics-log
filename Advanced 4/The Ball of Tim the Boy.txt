Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very fair boy with yellow hair. He loved to play with his red ball and his toy car. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom to buy a new toy. At the store, Tim saw a big fire truck. It was red and shiny. Tim wanted to buy the fire truck. "Mom, can I buy the fire truck?" Tim asked. His mom said yes, and they bought the fire truck. Tim was so happy with his new fire truck. He played with it all day long. He pretended to put out fires and save people. Tim and his fire truck had many fun adventures together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a new dog in town. He was very strong and had a lot of power. Max loved to help his friends and play with them. One day, a little cat named Lucy was stuck up in a tree. Max saw her and wanted to rescue her. He thought of a plan. He would use his power to shake the tree and make Lucy fall down. Then, he would catch her. Max started to shake the tree. But, something unexpected happened. Instead of Lucy falling down, a big bird came out of the tree. The bird was very happy to be free. The bird flew down and picked up Lucy in its beak. Then, the bird put Lucy on the ground. Max was surprised, but happy that Lucy was safe. They all became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a huge sand castle. It was so big you could barely see the top. One day, a little girl decided to play in the sandbox. She was so excited! She began to sing and as she sang, birds came to join in the fun. They circled around the castle and their singing filled the air. Everyone stopped to watch the show. The girl sang louder and louder, and soon a magical wind swept the sand up and around the castle. It moved and shifted until it was so tall and enormous that it towered over the girl. The little girl continued to sing and twirl around the castle. The birds flew around and around the sand castle as the wind swirled around them. Everyone was so happy and the singing filled the air with joy. It was a huge adventure!

Bobby was happily playing outside with his friends. They were splashing with the garden hose and running all around. Bobby's dad was with them, spraying them with the hose as they ran. Suddenly, his dad held up a shiny new video. He said, "I bought something very expensive. I want to show you how it works!" The kids all gathered around, excited to see what it was. Bobby's dad put the video in the machine and started it up. But right away it broke and wouldn't work. Bobby's dad got really angry and looked around for something to blame. Then his eyes lit on Bobby and he said, "It looks like you broke this expensive video!" The kids all looked at Bobby in surprise. Bobby tried to explain that he had done nothing wrong, but his dad wouldn't listen. He grabbed the hose and started to spray Bobby with it angrily. Everyone ran away from Bobby and his dad, leaving Bobby alone with his dad's rage. The kids had seen the foreshadowing and now knew it had been building up to something bad. It was a scary experience for them all and a lesson for Bobby that his dad's anger was not something to be taken lightly.

Once upon a time, there was a bridge. It was perfect, tall and strong. On the bridge, a dad and his son wanted to stay for a long time. The dad asked his son to look up, and he saw the sky. It was very beautiful. The dad said, "look at all the stars. They are like little dots in the sky." Then, they looked at the water. It was like a mirror. They could see their reflection. Finally, they watched the sunset. It was orange and pink, and it made the sky look even more perfect. When the night came, they decided to stay and watch the stars until they fell asleep. They stayed there, happily ever after. The end.
