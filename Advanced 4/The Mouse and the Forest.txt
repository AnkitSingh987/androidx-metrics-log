Title: The Mouse and the Forest

Once upon a time there was a shy little mouse. He really wanted to have a mustache and fit in with the rest of the animals in the forest. He kept asking all of the other animals why they had mustaches, but none of them answered. One day the little mouse spotted an ant wearing a beautiful mustache. He asked the ant why he had the mustache, and the ant said it was for a very special reason. The ant said, “If you want to find out why, you must come to the forest party tonight.” The little mouse got ready for the party and put on his best clothes. When he arrived, he spotted the ant at the center of the room, surrounded by all kinds of animals. Everyone was clapping and cheering as the ant showed off his mustache. He explained, “A mustache is a symbol of wisdom and courage. So, I kept it on my face to give me the courage to do brave things.” The little mouse was inspired by the ant’s bravery and wanted to have a mustache too. He kept asking himself why he was so shy, but he was determined to keep trying. He slowly grew out his mustache and soon enough he was just as brave and wise as the ant.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a plate that was very rare. It was big, round, and had pretty colors. Sue cared for her plate a lot. She put it on a high shelf so it would be safe. One day, a cat named Tom came to visit Sue. Tom saw the rare plate on the shelf. He wanted to play with it. Tom jumped up and tried to reach the plate. Sue saw Tom and was scared. She cared for her plate and did not want it to fall. Sue ran to Tom and picked him up. She put him down on the floor, away from the rare plate. Sue told Tom to be careful and not touch the plate. Tom listened to Sue and did not try to play with the plate again. Sue was happy that her rare plate was safe, and she cared for Tom too.

Once upon a time, in a fair and sunny land, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sunshine. One day, she saw a big, colorful rainbow in the sky. Lily called her friend Tim and said, "Look, a big rainbow! Let's go find the end of it!" They walked and walked until they found a big pot. Inside the pot, there were many colors. Tim said, "Let's mix the colors and make something new!" As they mixed the colors, something unexpected happened. The colors turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear said, "Thank you for making me. I am a magic bear and I can make your wishes come true." Lily and Tim were so happy. They played with the magic bear in the sunshine all day long. From that day on, they knew that anything was possible when they worked together.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Sue. She had a little book called a passport. This passport was very important to her. It was her special thing. One day, Sue wanted to number the pages in her passport. She got a pen and started to write. She wrote one, two, three, and so on. She was very happy counting the numbers. As Sue finished numbering her passport, her friend Tom came over. He saw her passport and said, "Wow, that's a great job, Sue!" Sue felt proud and helpful. She showed Tom how to number his own little book too. They both had fun and learned something new together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy named Buddy. Buddy had a soft body and loved to play with Tom. They played all day long. One day, Tom had to choose a toy to take to his friend's house. He looked at Buddy and then at his other toys. It was hard to pick just one. But in the end, Tom chose Buddy because he was his best friend. When they got to the friend's house, it was dark outside. Tom and Buddy played with the other kids and their toys. They all had fun until it was time to go home. Tom and Buddy were very happy they got to play with their friends.
