Title: The Story of the Bird and the Park

Once upon a time, a soft bunny and a small bird were friends. They liked to play together in the big park. One day, they saw other animals playing a fun game. The bunny and the bird wanted to join them. "Can we join your game?" asked the soft bunny. The other animals looked at them and said, "Sure, but you need to carry this weight to play." The weight was a little toy that was not too heavy. The bunny and the bird tried to carry the weight together, but something unexpected happened. They found out that when they carried the weight, they could fly! The soft bunny was so happy. Now, they could play the game and fly with their new friends. And they all had a great time playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside. One day, she found a small pebble. The pebble was smooth and shiny. Lily was very happy. Lily said to the pebble, "I will name you Pebby." Pebby, the pebble, liked its new name. Lily and Pebby played all day. They played in the grass, near the trees, and by the water. But one day, Lily was impatient. She wanted to play with Pebby right away. She called out, "Pebby, let's play!" But Pebby did not answer. Lily looked for Pebby everywhere. She felt sad. Finally, Lily found Pebby under a big tree. She said, "Pebby, I was worried! Please don't hide from me again." Pebby promised to stay close to Lily. They played together and were very happy. And Lily was never impatient again.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Anna. Anna had a pretty dress, long hair and blue eyes. Lily took Anna everywhere she went. One day, Lily went to the doctor with her mom. She was scared because the doctor had a needle. The needle was for a shot that would make her healthy. Lily did not want the shot. She cried and hid behind her mom. The doctor was polite and gentle. He said, "Lily, I know you are afraid, but the shot will not hurt much. It will be over soon. And you can hug your doll while I give you the shot. She will help you be brave." Lily looked at Anna and nodded. She held Anna tight and let the doctor give her the shot. It was not so bad. Lily felt proud of herself. She said, "Thank you, doctor. You are nice." The doctor smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are a good girl. And your doll is very pretty. Can I see her?" Lily gave Anna to the doctor. He looked at her and said, "Oh, she has a hole in her dress. How did that happen?" Lily remembered that she had cut Anna's dress with scissors. She wanted to make it shorter, but she made a mistake. She felt sorry and ashamed. She said, "I did it. I was bad. I'm sorry, Anna." The doctor said, "Don't worry, Lily. Everyone makes mistakes sometimes. The important thing is to say sorry and try to fix them. Do you want me to sew Anna's dress for you? I have a needle and thread here." Lily nodded and said, "Yes, please. Thank you, doctor. You are very kind." The doctor sewed Anna's dress and gave her back to Lily. Lily hugged Anna and said, "I love you, Anna. I forgive you for being mad at me. And I will not cut your dress again. I promise." Anna smiled and said, "I love you too, Lily. I forgive you for cutting my dress. And I will not be mad at you again. I promise." Lily and Anna were happy. They learned to forgive and to be careful. They thanked the doctor and went home with Lily's mom. Lily's mom was proud of Lily and Anna. She said, "You are both very polite and sweet. I love you both."

Once upon a time, there was a popular tutor. She was very nice and good at teaching. She had many friends who liked her a lot. Every day, she would help kids learn new things. One day, the tutor was washing her hands. She used soap and water to make them clean. As she washed her hands, she found a magic ring. The ring was shiny and pretty. She put the ring on her finger. The ring made the tutor very small. She was as small as a bug. Her friends could not see her. They looked for her everywhere but could not find her. The tutor was sad and wanted to be big again. She washed her hands again and took off the ring. Suddenly, she was big again. Her friends were happy to see her. They all laughed and played together. The tutor knew she was lucky to have such good friends.
