Title: The Busy Crane and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a busy crane. The crane liked to pick up and move things all day. One day, the crane saw a little bird. "Hi, bird," said the crane. "Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at the busy crane and said, "Yes, I want to play with you, crane." The crane was so happy. It picked up the bird with its long arm. They played together, moving up and down. But the crane was too busy and forgot to be careful. The little bird fell from the crane's arm and got hurt. The crane felt very sad. It had not meant to hurt the little bird. But now, the bird could not play anymore. The crane learned that it should be more careful when playing with friends.

---

One day, a big crane was working hard. It was lifting heavy things. The crane was very tough. It never complained. A small bird came by and said, "Hello, crane! Why don't you complain? You work so hard!" The crane looked at the bird and said, "I am tough. I do not need to complain." Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the bird's nest away. The bird was very sad. The tough crane saw this and wanted to help. It picked up the nest with its long arm and put it back in the tree. The bird was very happy. It said, "Thank you, tough crane! You are not only strong, but also kind." The crane smiled and went back to work, still not complaining.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a big refrigerator. Fluffy liked to tease the little mouse that lived in the house too. One day, Fluffy saw the little mouse near the refrigerator. Fluffy wanted to tease the mouse again. So, Fluffy jumped and chased the mouse around the refrigerator. The mouse was scared and ran away. Fluffy kept teasing the mouse. But then, Fluffy got too close to the refrigerator. The refrigerator door opened, and Fluffy fell inside. Fluffy was very cold and scared in the refrigerator. The little mouse heard Fluffy's cries and felt bad. But the mouse was too small to open the door. Fluffy stayed in the refrigerator, cold and alone. The mouse felt sad for Fluffy, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim was very sad and miserable. He lived in a hole in the wall, and he had no friends to play with. Tim wanted to go outside and find a friend, but he was scared of the big world. One day, Tim found a pocket on a shirt hanging by the door. He thought it would be a good hiding place. So, he jumped into the pocket and went outside to escape his lonely hole. In the pocket, Tim felt safe and warm. While outside, Tim met a friendly cat named Sam. Sam saw that Tim was sad and wanted to help. Sam said, "Don't be miserable, Tim. We can be friends!" Tim was so happy that he found a friend. Now, Tim and Sam play together every day, and Tim is not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his ball. One day, he went outside to play. Tim saw a big circle on the ground. It was clear and shiny. Tim was curious, so he stepped on the circle. All of a sudden, he started to blink very fast. When he stopped blinking, he was in a new place. In this new place, there were many balls to play with. Tim was so happy! He played and played until he was tired. Then, he blinked again and was back home. Tim knew the clear circle was magic and he could visit the ball land whenever he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cow. The cow lived on a ranch with many other animals. One day, the cow saw a sack near the fence. "Hi, horse," said the wise cow. "Do you know what is in the sack?" The horse shook his head. The wise cow, the horse, and the other animals went to look at the sack. When they got close, the sack started to wiggle! The animals were scared, but the wise cow was brave. She opened the sack, and out jumped a little kitten! The kitten was lost and needed a home. The wise cow and her friends let the kitten live on the ranch with them. They all became good friends and lived happily ever after.
