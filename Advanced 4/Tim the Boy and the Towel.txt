Title: Tim the Boy and the Towel

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a small boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite towel. The towel was big and soft. Tim liked to take his towel everywhere he went. It made him feel safe and happy. One day, Tim could not find his towel. He felt sad and began to question everyone. He asked his mom, "Where is my towel?" Mom did not know. He asked his dad, "Have you seen my towel?" Dad did not know either. He asked his sister, "Did you take my towel?" She said no. Tim looked everywhere for his large towel. He looked in his room, the bathroom, and the kitchen. Finally, he found it in the backyard. His towel was hanging on a tree. Tim felt happy again. He hugged his towel and promised to never lose it again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a low ceiling. He loved to play in the yard and bury his toys. One day, Max saw a big dog named Buddy who lived next door. Max said to Buddy, "I like your big yard. I wish I had a big yard too." Buddy replied, "Thank you, Max. I like your house with the low ceiling. I wish I had a low ceiling too." Max and Buddy were envious of each other's homes. The next day, Max and Buddy decided to play together. Max showed Buddy how to bury toys in the yard. Buddy showed Max how nice it was to have a low ceiling. They had fun and learned that it was better to be happy with what they had. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved the park because it had a big slide and a swing. He hoped to play all day and have lots of fun. While playing, Tim found a rough rock on the ground. It was a special rock because it could talk! The rock said, "Hello, Tim! I can make your wishes come true!" Tim was very happy and hoped for a big ice cream cone. Then, something unexpected happened. Instead of a big ice cream cone, the rough rock turned into a soft, fluffy cat! Tim was surprised but still happy. He played with the fluffy cat all day at the park. In the end, Tim found a new friend and had a great day.

Sophie and Jack were two friends who loved playing together. But one day, they started to quarrel and soon an argument started. Sophie cried and said, "Why are you making me upset?" Jack replied, "You broke your promise!" Sophie was very sorry and she said, "I'm sorry. I promise I won't do it again." Jack also said sorry and they hugged each other. They both realised that it was better to talk it out than to quarrel. From then on, they decided to always keep their promises and never quarrel.

Once upon a time there was a helpless little rabbit. He wanted to improve his life somehow, but he didn't know how. One day, he noticed a strange white smoke coming from a nearby tree. He watched it in wonder as it rose higher and higher in the sky. He thought that if he could join the smoke and go higher too, he could see a solution to his problem. So the little rabbit took a jump, and with all his might he tried to fly up with the smoke. But it was no use, he could not fly. He was still helpless. But then he noticed something else. Below the tree where the smoke was coming from, he saw an old man. He was creating more smoke. The rabbit wanted to try to join him. So the rabbit hopped closer to the man and tried to help him make the smoke. He added some sticks and helped stir the fire, and soon enough, the smoke was coming out in a steady stream. The rabbit had improved the smoke, and he was proud. From that day forward, he was never helpless again. Together, he and the old man made beautiful smoke whenever they wanted.
