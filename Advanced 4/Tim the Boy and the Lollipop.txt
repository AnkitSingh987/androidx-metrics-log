Title: Tim the Boy and the Lollipop

One day, a boy named Tim found a big lollipop. It was red and sweet. He was very happy. He wanted to eat it all day. But his friend, Sam, saw the lollipop too. Sam wanted to have it. Sam said, "Stop, Tim! I want that lollipop!" Tim did not want to give it to Sam. They both got mad. They did not know what to do. They did not want to share. Then, a smelly dog came by. The dog was hungry. It saw the lollipop and wanted it too. The dog jumped up and took the lollipop from Tim and Sam. They were sad. Tim and Sam learned that they should share. If they had shared the lollipop, the smelly dog would not have taken it. Now, they know to share and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pink reindeer named Rosie. Rosie lived in a big forest with her friends. She was very special because she was the only pink reindeer. One day, Rosie met a little boy named Tim. Tim was lost in the forest. He was sad and scared. Rosie wanted to help Tim find his way home. "Don't be sad, Tim," Rosie said. "I will help you." Rosie and Tim walked and walked. They counted the number of trees they saw. One, two, three, four, and more. They talked and laughed as they walked. Soon, they found Tim's house. Tim was so happy to be home. He hugged Rosie and said, "Thank you, Rosie, for helping me. You are the best pink reindeer ever." Rosie smiled and went back to the forest. She was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a black robot. The robot liked to play at the park with his friends. One sunny day, he went to the park to have fun. The black robot saw a big slide. He wanted to go down the slide, like his friends. The robot climbed up the steps and sat at the top of the slide. He was excited and a little scared. The black robot slid down the slide very fast. Whee! He laughed and clapped his hands. The robot went up and down the slide many times. He had so much fun and couldn't wait to come back to the park again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Jack. He was very brave and curious. One day, Jack decided to start an adventure. He wanted to find a modern reward! So Jack put on his hat and started his journey. He walked through the forest, climbed the mountains and crossed the river. Eventually he reached a big, modern city. He was so excited! Jack explored the city, looking everywhere for his modern reward. Suddenly, he saw something shining in the sun. It looked like a golden star! He ran to pick it up and found it was a key. Jack was so happy and grabbed the key tight. He followed it to a large door with a fancy lock. He opened the door, and inside he found a big, modern chest. He opened the chest and what did he find? A big, juicy chocolate cake! Jack was so excited that his modern reward was something yummy. Jack couldn't help but smile. He knew he had really earned his reward! He ate the cake and had the best adventure ever!

Once upon a time, there was a small girl called Amy. Amy liked to explore the garden with her friends. Every day she would run around, picking up interesting plants and leaves. One day, Amy went to the garden and noticed something strange. She saw that an herb had suddenly grown. Amy ran over to the herb, excited to explore it. As she got closer, she heard a voice. "Stay away, Amy!" the voice said. Amy was surprised and a bit scared. She looked around but couldn't see anyone. She took a step back, wondering what was going on. Suddenly, the herb started shaking and a little fairy popped out. The fairy had bright red hair and wings. The fairy spoke to Amy. "I am the herb fairy and I am very restless," she said. "I need you to do something for me." "What is it?" asked Amy. "I need you to order me a special potion. It will make me relax," said the fairy. Amy agreed to help the fairy. She quickly ordered the potion and the fairy was soon relaxed. Amy and the fairy became good friends, exploring the garden together. Whenever the fairy got restless, Amy knew just what to do- order her a potion and watch the fairy relax.
