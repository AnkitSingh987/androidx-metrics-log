Title: The Girl Called Ella and the Rain

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl called Ella. Ella was a happy girl, who enjoyed playing and learning new things every day. Every morning she would go outside to get some fresh air. One morning, Ella saw something strange. The sky was filled with dark clouds and it began to rain. To Ella, the rain was special. It looked thin and sparkly, like thousands of tiny diamonds. Ella wanted to restore it, so she could enjoy it forever, but she knew it was impossible. When she went back home, Ella asked her mom why the rain was so special. Her mom said, “The rain is special, because it comes down from the sky and makes everything come alive again.” Ella learned a valuable lesson from the rain that day – that no matter how difficult things may seem, restoring something is often impossible and all we can do is appreciate it for what it is.

Once upon a time, there was a team of four friends. They were Tom, Tim, Ted, and Tina. They liked to play together all day long. The sun was mild and warm, and they liked to run and jump. One day, Tom wanted to teach his friends a new game. He said, "Let's play a game where we help each other." They all agreed and were excited to learn the new game. Tom showed them how to hold hands and walk together. As they played the game, they learned how to work together as a team. They had lots of fun and were very happy. The mild sun shone on them as they played, and they were glad that Tom had taught them the new game.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. The pig lived on a farm with many other animals. One day, the pig decided to go on a walk. He wanted to see the world outside the farm. As the pig walked, he met a big cow. The cow said, "Where are you going, little pig?" The pig replied, "I am going on a walk to see the world." The cow warned, "It is difficult out there. Please be careful and return home soon." The pig continued walking and saw many new things. He saw big trees, pretty flowers, and a large pond. But as the sun began to set, the pig started to feel tired and scared. He knew it was time to return home. The pig walked back to the farm. When he got there, all the animals were happy to see him. The cow said, "I am glad you returned, little pig. We missed you!" The pig was happy to be home and shared his adventure with his friends. From that day on, the pig knew that no matter how far he went, he would always return to his farm family.

Lily was a chubby cat who liked to sleep and eat. She did not like to play or run with the other cats. One day, she saw a big mouse in the kitchen. She wanted to catch it and eat it, but she was too slow and lazy. "Ha ha, you are too fat and slow, chubby cat!" the mouse said. "You will never catch me!" Lily felt angry and sad. She wanted to prove the mouse wrong. She decided to become faster and stronger. She started to play and run with the other cats every day. She ate less and slept less. She became thinner and fitter. One day, she saw the same mouse in the kitchen. She chased it and caught it in her paws. The mouse was in shock. It could not believe that Lily had changed so much. "Please, please, let me go, fast cat!" the mouse begged. "I am sorry for what I said. You are not chubby, you are amazing!" Lily felt proud and happy. She let the mouse go and said, "Thank you, mouse. You helped me become a better cat. But don't come back to my kitchen, or I will catch you again!" The mouse ran away and never bothered Lily again. Lily continued to play and run with the other cats, but she also enjoyed sleeping and eating sometimes. She was not too chubby, not too thin, but just right.
