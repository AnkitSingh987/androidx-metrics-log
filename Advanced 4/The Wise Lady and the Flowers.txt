Title: The Wise Lady and the Flowers

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wise old lady. She had a big garden with many pretty flowers. She liked to make perfume from the flowers. One hot day, she went to her garden to pick some flowers. She worked very hard and got tired. So, she sat under a big tree to rest. While she rested, she watched the butterflies play in the flowers. After her rest, she went back to her house. She used the flowers to make sweet-smelling perfume. Everyone in the town loved her perfume. They said it was the best perfume ever. The wise old lady was happy, and she shared her perfume with all her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a fragile toy cube that she loved very much. The cube was special because it could twist and turn. It was her favorite toy. One day, Lily took her cube to the park. She wanted to show her friend Tim how to twist the cube. Tim was excited to try, but Lily was scared. She thought Tim might break her fragile cube. Lily decided to let Tim try. She showed him how to twist the cube gently. Tim was careful and the cube did not break. They had lots of fun playing together with the cube. In the end, Lily learned that sharing can be fun and make friends happy. She also learned that when we trust our friends, they will be careful with our things.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a popcorn stand and Tim asked his mom, "Can we get some popcorn, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can get some hot popcorn." When they got the hot popcorn, Tim wanted to open it right away. But his mom said, "Be careful, it's hot!" Tim waited for the popcorn to cool down. When it was not hot anymore, he opened the bag. Just as Tim opened the bag, a big wind came and blew all the popcorn away! Tim was sad, but then he saw a group of birds eating the popcorn on the ground. He laughed and said, "Look, the birds are having a popcorn party!" His mom smiled and they went to buy more popcorn to share with the birds.

Once upon a time, there was a tall man called Terry. He was strong and very brave. He lived in a small town and his job was to protect the youth of the town. One day, as Terry was walking through a field, he heard some children crying. He quickly ran over to see what was wrong. When Terry arrived, he saw two small children crying and pointing to the sky. He looked up and saw a big eagle with its wings spread wide. Terry said, "Don't worry, I'm here to protect you." The children looked up to Terry, wide-eyed. The eagle soared away and the children stopped crying. They thanked Terry and he kissed each one on the head. The children ran off back home, safe and secure in the knowledge that Terry had protected them.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily loved to play and imagine she was a bird flying high in the sky. She felt very lucky to have a big park to play in. While playing, Lily saw a plane in the sky. She pointed at it and said, "Mom, look! A plane!" Her mom looked up and smiled. "Yes, Lily, that's a plane. It can fly very high, just like the birds you like to imagine being." Lily thought for a moment and then asked, "Mom, can I be lucky like the plane and fly too?" Her mom smiled and said, "You can always imagine you're flying, Lily. That's the fun of playing and dreaming." So Lily kept playing and imagining she was a bird, a plane, or anything that could fly. She felt very lucky to have her mom and the park to play in.

Life was humble for John and his family. They only had one automobile to get around. One day, at the nearby park, John and his family saw an amazing sight. John's mother said "Look, John! There's a man who can fold an automobile!" John couldn't believe it. The man took his automobile and with one swift movement, he folded it. John's father said "Isn't it amazing, how the man can do that? But you know, John, we may not have much, but we do have each other. That's a priceless gift, and it will never be taken away." John smiled and his parents hugged him firmly. John learned an important lesson that day, that a humble life can be a happy life if you have the love of family.
