Title: Bill the Boy and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Bill who loved to play outdoors. One day, he saw the most loudest and brightest rainbow he had ever seen. It was made of every color in the sky, and Bill thought it was so beautiful. He wanted to mark it in his memory forever, so he grabbed a big, old blanket and laid it out under the rainbow. He got as close as he could, and took out some crayons. Bill thought and thought, trying to decide the best way to mark the rainbow. He decided that the best way to mark it was to draw the most beautiful pictures around it. He did his best, but all of his efforts were wasted because of the strong wind and rain. Little did he know that his ignorant efforts were actually helping him to remember the rainbow forever! When the rain stopped and the sun came out, the blanket was full of rainbows. In the spot that Bill had worked so hard to mark there was the biggest and most beautiful rainbow of all. From then on, Bill never forgot the rainbow, and he proudly showed off his blanket full of rainbows to all his friends!

Lily and Ben are playing with blocks in the living room. They are building a big tower with different colors. Lily likes the blue blocks and Ben likes the red ones. "Look, Lily, I made a great tower!" Ben says, showing his work to his sister. "It is great, Ben. But mine is taller!" Lily says, pointing to her tower. They keep adding more blocks to their towers, trying to make them higher and higher. They are having fun and forget about the time. Suddenly, they hear Mom calling from the kitchen. "Lily, Ben, it's time for lunch! Come and wash your hands!" Lily and Ben look at each other. They don't want to stop playing. They want to finish their towers. "Can we play for one more minute, Mom?" Lily asks. "Please, Mom, just one minute!" Ben begs. Mom smiles and says, "Okay, one minute. But only one minute. Then you have to come and eat. Lunch is ready and it's yummy." "Thank you, Mom!" Lily and Ben say. They hurry to put the last blocks on their towers. They want to make them as great as possible. "Done!" Lily says, admiring her tower. "Me too!" Ben says, looking at his tower. They are proud of their work. They think their towers are the best. They run to the kitchen, holding hands. They remember to wash their hands before they sit at the table. Mom serves them soup and sandwiches. She says, "You two are great builders. I like your towers. They remind me of the skyscrapers in the city." Lily and Ben smile. They like Mom's words. They like Mom's soup and sandwiches. They like playing with blocks. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to help clean the attic. The attic was a big room at the top of the house where old things were kept. As they cleaned, Lucy found a big, old box. Inside the box was a magic wand. Lucy waved the wand and said, "I wish for all the dust to vanish!" Suddenly, the attic became very clean. Lucy's mom was so happy and surprised. "Wow, Lucy!" her mom said. "How did you do that?" Lucy showed her mom the magic wand. They both smiled and continued to clean the attic. And from that day on, Lucy's magic wand helped them keep their house clean and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bob. Bob loved to race with his friends. They would run and run, and laugh and laugh. They had so much fun racing together. One day, Bob and his friends decided to have a big race. They all lined up and got ready to run. When they started the race, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew and made them all very dirty. Bob stopped running and started to wipe off the dirt. His friends kept running, but Bob knew he had to be clean. He wiped and wiped until he was not dirty anymore. Then, he started to run again. As Bob ran, he saw his friends were tired. They had been running so fast, they didn't have any energy left. Bob passed them one by one, and he won the race! His friends laughed and said, "Bob, you are a funny bunny, but you are also very smart!"
