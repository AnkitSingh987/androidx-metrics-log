Title: Jack and Jill and the Cloud

The sun was shining in the sky, and Jack and Jill were playing tag in his backyard. Jack threw a ball and he said, "Catch it, Jill!" Jill threw the ball back to Jack and giggled. They ran around the yard for a few more minutes until they saw a cloud in the sky. Jack pointed to the sky and shouted, "Look, Jill!" Then he said, "Let's throw something up and see!" Jack grabbed some pebbles and threw one up at the cloud. When the pebble reached the cloud, it let out a sweet sound like a bell, and Jill clapped her hands in delight. The two of them kept throwing more pebbles up, and each time the sweet sound would ripple through the air. Jack and Jill laughed with joy, and the cloud watched them happily until it drifted away.

One day, there was a dull pencil. The pencil was so dull it couldn't even write! It was stuck in a corner, feeling sad. But then, something amazing happened. Someone had come to save the pencil! The pencil felt so happy that it started to shine. Suddenly, the pencil could write again! Every day, the pencil and its new friend would come back to the corner and write stories. The pencil was no longer dull. It was full of life and always came back for more stories.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a mailbox that looked a little crazy. The mailbox was red and blue, with big eyes painted on it. Tim thought it was funny and wanted to see what was inside. As Tim opened the mailbox, something unexpected happened. The mailbox started to grow! It got bigger and bigger, and soon it was taller than Tim. Tim was so surprised that he stepped back and looked at the big mailbox. He didn't know what to do. Just then, a friendly dog came running up. The dog barked at the mailbox, and suddenly, the mailbox went back to its normal size. Tim laughed and thanked the dog. From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends, and they always walked by the crazy mailbox together.

Once upon a time, in a big swamp, there lived a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to hop all day long. One day, while hopping, he met a big, harsh, and scary-looking crocodile. The crocodile looked at Tim and said, "You better stop hopping, or I will eat you!" Tim was scared, but he didn't want to stop hopping. He thought of a plan to trick the crocodile. He told the crocodile, "If you let me hop, I will show you a big, yummy fish to eat." The crocodile agreed, and they both went to find the fish. As they got closer to the fish, Tim saw his friend, the clever bird. He whispered his plan to the bird. The bird flew away and came back with a big, strong net. Tim hopped very fast and led the crocodile to the net. The bird dropped the net on the crocodile, trapping him. Tim and the bird high-fived each other and hopped away, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a long snake named Sam. Sam liked to squeeze through small holes to find new friends. One day, Sam found a little house in the woods. In the house, Sam saw a screen. On the screen, there were funny animals playing. Sam wanted to play with them too. So, Sam tried to squeeze through the screen to join them. But Sam was too long to fit through the screen. He felt sad. Then, a little bird saw Sam and invited him to play outside. Sam and the bird played all day. Sam learned that he didn't need the screen to have fun and make friends.

Once upon a time there lived a boy named Tomas. Tomas was only 3 years old, but he was very brave. One day, Tomas wanted to visit his grandpa in the country. Tomas asked his parents if he could go, but they said no. They said it was too far, and they didn't have a car available to take Tomas. Tomas was very sad to not be able to visit his grandpa, but he knew he had to obey his parents. That night, Tomas had a dream. In the dream, he was able to travel to the country, and his grandpa was there waiting for him. When he woke up, Tomas was determined to suffer through and go see his grandpa. So Tomas marched outside and started walking, without telling his parents. He walked and walked until he reached his grandpa's house. When he arrived, his grandpa was so happy to see him! They had a great visit, with lots of happy stories, smiles and hugs! In the end, Tomas was so glad that he was brave enough to do something he was told not to do. He learned that if you believe in yourself, anything is possible and available!
