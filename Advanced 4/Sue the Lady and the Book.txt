Title: Sue the Lady and the Book

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She lived in a small house with her cat, Max. Sue loved to number her stamps. She had a big book full of them. One day, while Sue was numbering her stamps, Max saw a big dog outside the window. Max was scared and ran away, knocking down Sue's book. All the stamps fell out. Sue was very sad. Sue went to her friend, Tom, and said, "My stamps are all gone. Can you help me find them?" Tom said, "I will try." They looked and looked, but they could not find the stamps. In the end, Sue's stamps were lost forever, and she was very sad.

Sara and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with red apples. Sara said, "Let's plan to pick some apples. They look yummy." Ben said, "OK, but we have to be careful. The tree is very high and the apples are very far." They found a long stick and a big bag. They took turns to hold the stick and poke the apples. The apples fell down and they put them in the bag. They were happy and proud. They said, "We are very thoughtful. We can share the apples with our families." But then, a big dog came. The dog was hungry and angry. It saw the bag of apples and barked loudly. It ran towards Sara and Ben. Sara and Ben were scared and dropped the bag. The apples rolled out and the dog ate them all. Sara and Ben cried and ran away. They lost their apples and their plan. They were very sad and sorry.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little baby. The baby had a crib where he slept every night. The crib was in a room with lots of toys and a big window. The baby was happy when he played with his toys, but sometimes he felt miserable. One day, the baby's mom and dad wanted to supply him with more toys to make him happy. They went to the store and bought a big, soft teddy bear. They thought the teddy bear would make the baby feel better when he was miserable. When they gave the teddy bear to the baby, he smiled and hugged it. From that day on, the baby was never miserable again. He learned that when he shared his toys and played with them, he was happy. The moral of the story is that sharing and playing with others can make you happy, even when you feel miserable.

Mummy was walking in the park with her little daughter, Emma. Emma was excited because she saw a rainbow in the sky. She asked her mummy, "What is that?" Mummy smiled and said, "It's a rainbow. It's really beautiful, isn't it?" Emma nodded. Just then, the clouds moved and the rainbow was gone. Emma felt sad and asked, "Why did the rainbow go away?" Mummy said, "The clouds prevented us from seeing the rainbow. We can't always have a view of it." Emma didn't understand so mummy explained, "Sometimes clouds make us jealous by blocking our view of all the wonderful things in the sky." Emma smiled and said, "But that's okay. We'll see another rainbow soon. Right, Mummy?" Mummy smiled and nodded. With that, Emma and Mummy happily walked back home.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Buddy. Buddy had a pretty collar that he liked to wear. One day, Buddy saw an impressive chair that he wanted to seat on. Buddy tried to jump on the chair, but it was too high. He looked around and saw a small box. Buddy had an idea. He pushed the box near the chair. Buddy jumped on the box and then onto the chair. He was so happy that he could seat on the impressive chair. Buddy's pretty collar sparkled as he sat proudly on the chair. And from that day, Buddy always used the box to seat on his favorite chair.

Once upon a time, there was a wide, green field. In the middle of the field there were two musicians. One of them was playing a fiddle and the other was playing a guitar. They were having so much fun playing together that they didn't want to separate. But then a butterfly flew by. It sparkled in the sun and flew in circles around the happy musicians. It flew so close that their instruments touched each other. The musicians had to separate so the butterfly can fly between them. The two musicians moved further apart, but they still smiled and waved at each other. They continued playing their instruments on opposite sides of the wide, green field. The butterfly flew around them in circles while they played and it seemed like they would never stop.
