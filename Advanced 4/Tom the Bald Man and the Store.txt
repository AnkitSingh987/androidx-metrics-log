Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Store

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom was thirsty. He went to a store to get a drink. In the store, he saw a big glass. The glass was full of juice. Tom wanted the juice. Tom said to the store lady, "I want to order that big glass of juice, please." The store lady smiled and said, "Okay, here is your big glass of juice." Tom was very happy. He drank the juice from the big glass. It was very good. Tom said, "Thank you!" to the store lady. Tom went home with a full tummy and a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little fox called Dan. He was brown and he was very busy. One day he was running through the fields looking for adventure. All of a sudden, he heard a funny sound. It was a 'plop' sound. He stopped and looked around trying to find out what was making the sound. He saw a frog in a pond and realised that the frog had made the sound when it had jumped into the pond. Dan laughed and said to the frog, "I can catch you". The frog shook its head and said, "No way! I'm too fast for you". So Dan began to try and catch the frog. He chased it around, but each time he thought he was getting close, the frog jumped away. Dan was so determined to catch it that didn't give up. Finally, after running around for a long time, Dan managed to catch the frog. He was so happy and said, "I caught you!" The frog smiled and said, "You're a very fast little fox". Dan laughed and released the frog back into the pond. He was proud of himself for catching the frog and went off, still looking for more adventures.

Tina was feeling so proud. She had just finished drawing a beautiful rainbow with lots of colourful zigzags. She wanted to show it to somebody, so she ran to find her mum. When Tina showed her mum the artwork, her mum was delighted. "What a lovely drawing," said her mum. "I'm sure the teacher will be very proud too when she sees it." Tina felt so happy that she started to dance around her bedroom, but then she ran and fell on the floor. Her mum rushed over to her and said "Oh no, are you alright?" Tina had hurt her knee, so her mum said that she should take some rest and have a nap. Tina was worried that she wouldn't be able to show her teacher the artwork if she rested, so she started to cry. But her mum assured her that the teacher would still be proud when she saw it. So Tina decided to listen to her mum and rest until she was feeling better before showing the artwork to the teacher.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat all kinds of food. One day, he found a big red apple. He was very happy because he loved apples a lot. Tim took a big bite of the apple and started to chew it. The apple was very juicy and sweet. As Tim chewed the apple, he felt something cold on his hand. He looked down and saw some juice from the apple. The juice was cold and wet on his hand. Tim liked how the cold juice felt on his hand, so he kept chewing the apple to make more juice. When Tim finished the apple, he was full and happy. He wiped the cold juice off his hand and went to play with his friends. They all laughed and played together under the warm sun. Tim was glad he had a juicy apple to eat and share with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to sing, and she dreamed of singing at the opera. Every day, she would practice her songs and imagine herself on a big stage. One day, Kitty met a wise old owl. The owl asked her a question. "Why do you want to sing at the opera, Kitty?" Kitty thought for a moment and said, "I want to share my songs with everyone and make them happy." The owl smiled and said, "That's a wonderful reason, but remember, it's not just about being on a big stage. It's about sharing your gift and bringing joy to others, no matter where you are." Kitty nodded and thanked the owl for his wise words. From then on, Kitty sang her songs not only at the opera but also in her neighborhood, at the park, and for her friends. She learned that sharing her gift and making others happy was the most important thing. And everyone who heard her songs felt the love and joy she shared, and they were grateful for her beautiful voice. The moral of the story is that it's important to share your gifts and talents with others, not just for the fame and glory, but to bring happiness and joy to those around you.
