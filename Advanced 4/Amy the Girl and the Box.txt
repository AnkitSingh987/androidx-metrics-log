Title: Amy the Girl and the Box

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to skip in the hall. She put on her shoes and went to the hall. It was a cold day, and the hall felt icy. Amy was excited to skip and play in the hall. As Amy started to skip, she saw a big box in the hall. She was very surprised! Amy did not know what was inside the box. She wanted to find out, so she stopped skipping and went to the box. When Amy opened the box, she found a big, fluffy bear inside! The bear could talk and wanted to be her friend. Amy was so happy and could not believe it! She and the bear skipped together in the icy hall all day long.

Once there were two friends called Lucy and Sophie. They were both three years old and very excited to go to the park and play. Lucy was playing on the swings and Sophie was pushing the roundabout. Lucy was having so much fun and soon forgot all about Sophie. Suddenly, Lucy heard Sophie cry out loud "Ouch!” Lucy went over to Sophie and saw an iron next to her foot. Sophie was very uncomfortable and her foot was hurt. Lucy knew Sophie and she hadn't meant to leave her alone. Feeling bad, Lucy hugged Sophie and said sorry. The moral of the story was that it was important to not forget about your friends and to always look after them.

One day, a 3 year old girl called Amy was wandering in a park. Suddenly, she saw an old man crouching in the corner of the park. He seemed kind and she walked up to him. "Hello," Amy said shyly. The old man looked up and smiled. "Hi, little one," he said. Amy told the old man that she was confused. She did not know what to do. The old man seemed to understand her. He mentioned that he was an old fisherman. He used to measure the scale of fishes. Then he showed Amy the scales he kept in his pocket. Amy was amazed. She asked if the fisherman could show her how to measure the scales. The old man smiled. "Of course! I can show you," he said. Through the old man's guidance, Amy slowly learned how to use the scales correctly. Finally, she was no longer confused and thanked the old man for his help. The old man smiled again. "It was my pleasure, little one," he said. Amy thanked the old man and ran back to her parents. She was happy that she had learned something new.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Amy saw a big, red apple and said, "Mom, can we buy this apple? It looks yummy!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can buy the apple. It has good value too, because it is big and healthy for you." When they got home, Amy helped her mom cook dinner. They made yummy soup with the big, red apple. Amy said, "I am so happy we bought this apple. It makes the soup taste very yummy!" Her mom said, "Yes, it does. And it has good value because it makes the soup taste better and it is healthy for us." After they finished eating the yummy soup, Amy and her mom cleaned up the kitchen. Amy said, "I want to buy more apples next time, so we can make more yummy soup." Her mom said, "That is a good idea, Amy. We can look for apples with good value, so we can make lots of yummy soup together."

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob loved to eat popcorn. One day, Bob found a big bag of popcorn in the woods. He was very happy and took the bag of popcorn to his cave. In the cave, it was very cold. Bob put the popcorn near the door to keep it safe. He did not know that when it is cold, things can freeze. The popcorn started to freeze, but Bob did not know that. The next day, Bob wanted to eat his popcorn. He took a big bite, but the popcorn was sour and frozen. Bob did not like the sour, frozen popcorn. He was sad, but he learned that he should not leave his popcorn in a cold place. From that day on, Bob made sure to keep his popcorn in a warm place, so it would always taste good.
