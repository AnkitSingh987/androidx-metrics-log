Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to complain a lot. He lived in a small house with a loud alarm. Every day, Tom would go for a walk and complain about things he saw. One sunny day, Tom went for his walk. He saw a big tree and complained that it was too tall. He saw a little dog and complained that it was too loud. Tom was not happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A small bird landed on Tom's head. The bird thought Tom's bald head was a nest! Tom stopped complaining and started to laugh. He was not sad anymore. The loud alarm did not bother him now. Tom and the little bird became good friends and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The big, red ball had a friend named Thermometer. Thermometer was very good at telling if it was hot or cold. They lived in a big park with lots of grass and trees. One day, they saw a little girl crying. The big, red ball and Thermometer wanted to help her. They asked her, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I can't find my friends. I feel so alone." The big, red ball and Thermometer had an idea. They told her to close her eyes and imagine her friends were with her. The little girl did what they said. She closed her eyes and imagined her friends were there. When she opened her eyes, her friends were really there! They were so happy to see her. The big, red ball and Thermometer were happy too. They learned that when you imagine good things, good things can happen. And the little girl learned that she was never alone when she had her imagination.

Once upon a time, in a big box made of cardboard, there lived a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy mouse and run around the box. One day, Kitty saw her friend Doggy playing with a new toy ball. Kitty felt jealous because she wanted a toy ball too. Kitty decided to ask Doggy if she could play with the toy ball. But just as she was about to ask, Kitty let out a big sneeze! "Achoo!" The sneeze was so big that it made the cardboard box shake. Doggy looked at Kitty and laughed. "You have a funny sneeze, Kitty!" he said. Kitty felt a little shy, but she still asked Doggy if she could play with the toy ball. Doggy smiled and said, "Of course, Kitty! We can share and play together." So Kitty and Doggy played with the toy ball all day long, and they had lots of fun. From that day on, they always shared their toys and played together, and they were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his grandma. His grandma lived in a big building with an elevator. Tim felt restless because he was very excited to see his grandma. When Tim and his mom got to the elevator, they saw a big button. Tim's mom said, "Press the button, Tim!" Tim pressed the button and the elevator doors opened. They went inside and the elevator went up, up, up! Finally, the elevator stopped and the doors opened. Tim saw his grandma waiting for him. She gave him a big hug and said, "I'm so happy to see you, Tim!" They had a fun day together and Tim's restlessness went away.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot. The parrot was very dirty. It lived in a big tree. The tree was near a small house. One day, the parrot wanted to play. It saw a boy near the house. The boy was trying to climb the tree. The parrot thought it was fun. The parrot wanted to help the boy climb the tree. The boy and the parrot tried to climb the tree together. But the tree was very high. The boy got scared. The parrot was sad. They both fell from the tree. The boy and the parrot were hurt. They could not play anymore.
