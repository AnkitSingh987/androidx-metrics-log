Title: Sally the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had many toys, but her favorite toy was a red ball. She liked to use her ball to play catch with her friends. One day, her friend Timmy came over to play. "Can I use your ball, please?" Timmy asked Sally. "I promise to be careful with it." Sally thought about it and said, "Okay, you can use my ball. But you must promise to give it back when we are done playing." Timmy and Sally played together all day, using the red ball to play catch and roll it back and forth. They had so much fun that they forgot about the time. When it was time for Timmy to go home, he remembered his promise to Sally. "Here is your ball, Sally," he said. "Thank you for letting me use it. It was very useful for our fun games today." Sally smiled and said, "You're welcome, Timmy. I'm glad we could share my ball and play together." And with that, they said goodbye and promised to play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a blackboard. In this room, there lived a rich man named Tom. He loved to draw on the blackboard with many colors. One day, he drew a big, happy sun. But then, something strange happened. A big cloud appeared on the blackboard. The sun was gone! Tom was sad and confused. He didn't draw the cloud, so where did it come from? Tom tried to rub the cloud away, but it stayed. Then, he drew a big smile on the cloud. The cloud became a happy cloud! Now, Tom liked the cloud. It was a surprise, but it made his blackboard even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a very honest girl, and she always told the truth. One day, Amy's mommy told her that they were going on a holiday. Amy was very excited and couldn't wait to go. On the holiday, they went to a beautiful park. There was a big playground with a seesaw. Amy and her mommy sat on the seesaw, and they tried to balance. They went up and down, up and down, and they laughed a lot. After playing on the seesaw, they had a yummy picnic in the park. Amy's mommy made her favorite sandwiches. They had a great holiday, and Amy was very happy. She couldn't wait to tell all her friends about the fun they had.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She had a soft pillow that she loved very much. Every night, she would hug her pillow tight when she went to sleep. One day, Anna saw her friend Tim playing outside. Tim was sad because he didn't have a pillow to hug at night. Anna thought about her pillow and how much it helped her sleep. She wanted to help her friend Tim. Anna decided to give her pillow to Tim. She knew it would make him happy. When she gave the pillow to Tim, he smiled and hugged it tight. That night, Tim slept well with the pillow. Anna felt good for helping her friend. The next day, Anna's mom saw what she did for Tim and was very proud. She gave Anna a new pillow to hug at night. Anna learned that sharing with friends is a good thing to do. And when you help others, good things come back to you.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his old grandpa. They saw a beautiful landscape with many trees, flowers, and a big blue sky. Tim loved to explore with his grandpa and learn new things. As they walked, Tim saw a big red fruit on a tree. "Grandpa, what is that?" he asked. Grandpa said, "That is an apple, Tim. It is very sweet and yummy. Would you like to taste it?" Tim nodded, excited to try the apple. Tim took a bite of the apple, but something unexpected happened. The apple was not sweet at all! It was very sour and made Tim's face scrunch up. Grandpa laughed and said, "Oops! That was a sour apple, not a sweet one. Let's find a sweet one for you to taste." Tim and grandpa continued their walk, searching for the sweetest apple in the beautiful landscape.
