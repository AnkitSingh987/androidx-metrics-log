Title: The Story of Lily the Girl

One day, a girl named Lily went to the market with her mom. They wanted to sell some pretty flowers from their garden. Lily saw a big bush with very attractive red flowers. She told her mom, "I want to sell these pretty red flowers!" Her mom said, "Okay, let's pick some flowers from the bush and take them to the market." They picked the flowers and went to the market. They put the flowers on a table and waited for people to come and buy them. Soon, a nice lady came and said, "Oh, these red flowers are so pretty! I want to buy them all." Lily and her mom were very happy. They sold all the attractive red flowers from the bush. They went home and told their family about their fun day at the market.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a green door with a shiny knob. Behind the door lived a kind dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his ball and protect his family. One day, while Buddy was playing with his ball, a big, mean cat came to the house. The cat wanted to come in and take Buddy's ball. Buddy barked and said, "You can't have my ball! I protect my family and my things!" The cat didn't listen to Buddy. It jumped up and turned the shiny knob on the green door. The door opened, and the cat went inside. Buddy tried to stop the cat, but the cat was too fast. The cat took Buddy's ball and ran away. Buddy was very sad. He couldn't protect his ball, and now it was gone. The family tried to make Buddy feel better, but he was still very sad. The end.

Tommy was a selfish little boy. He had a toy car he liked to drive around in the park. The park was his favourite place to ride his car because it was so big and he loved playing in it. One day, it was raining and all the other kids had left the park. But Tommy wanted to keep playing. So he got the biggest mop he could find, and he used it to make a track in the wet grass. The mop was too big for Tommy so he had to keep pushing it around. But he enjoyed it and made all sorts of tracks in the grass. He zoomed around and around, thinking that nobody was watching. But when he was finished, he realized that some of the wet grass had made a mess. So he had to find a way to clean it up. With a little help from mom and dad, he used the mop to clean up the mess. In the end, the park was back to being clean and tidy. Tommy had learned his lesson -cleaning up after selfishly playing was just as important as having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a mama and a daddy. They had a boy who was three years old. The little boy did something bad, so his parents decided to punish him. Mama said "No more TV. That's your punishment." The little boy was sad. He started to cry. But then Mama had another idea. She said "We're going to do something fun instead. We can go to the amazing billboard up the road." The little boy was so excited! He put on his shoes and ran to the car. Mama and Daddy drove to the amazing billboard. It was so big and beautiful and colorful. The little boy was amazed. They had a picnic beneath the billboard and laughed together. The little boy was happy again. Mama and Daddy knew that punishment was important, but spending time together was even more important.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her toys and run in the park. One day, Lily saw her mom and dad dressed up in nice clothes. They were going to a marriage. Lily was excited and wanted to go too. Her mom said she could come, but she had to be good. At the marriage, there was music and dancing. Lily felt a little scared and started to tremble. Her mom held her hand, and they danced together. Soon, Lily felt happy and stopped trembling. They all had a great time at the marriage.
