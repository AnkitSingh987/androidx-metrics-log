Title: Sue the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear her favorite skirt. It was red and had pretty flowers on it. She felt very independent when she wore it. She would twirl and dance in her skirt all day long. One day, Sue's mom told her that a new friend was coming to play. Sue was so excited! She put on her favorite skirt and waited by the window. She knew her skirt would make her new friend smile. Finally, the new friend arrived at Sue's house. Her name was Lily. Sue and Lily played with dolls and had a tea party. Lily loved Sue's skirt and said it was very pretty. They both felt very independent while playing together. At the end of the day, Lily had to go home. Sue was sad, but she knew they would play again soon. Sue's mom told her that her skirt had helped make a new friend. Sue smiled and twirled in her skirt, feeling happy and independent.

Once upon a time there was a friendly boat on the sea. It was an old boat and its crew was very alert and observant. On board was an anchor, which was big and heavy. Suddenly the boat started to shrink and the anchor kept on getting heavier and heavier. "Oh no!" the crew said. "What can we do to fix this?" The captain thought for a moment and then said: "I know! We'll need to tie the anchor to the outside of the boat so that it doesn't weigh down the boat as it gets smaller." So the crew quickly got to work and secured the anchor outside the boat. No sooner had they done this than they felt the boat start to grow back to its normal size. The crew cheered and thanked each other for their hard work – and for being so alert that they had noticed the boat shrinking in the first place.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to play hide and seek with his friends. One sunny day, Ben and his friends decided to have a game of hide and seek. Ben wanted to find the perfect hiding spot. Ben looked around and saw a big tree. He thought, "This tree is perfect to hide behind!" He hid behind the tree and waited for his friends to find him. While waiting, Ben heard two birds talking. One bird said, "I am tired of this war between the birds and the squirrels." The other bird replied, "Me too. We need to find a way to stop fighting and be friends." Ben thought about what the birds said. He decided that after the game, he would talk to his friends about helping the birds and the squirrels become friends. After a while, his friend found him and said, "Ben, you had a perfect hiding spot!" After the game, Ben told his friends about the birds and the squirrels. They all agreed to help them become friends. They talked to the birds and the squirrels and helped them see that they could all live happily together. From that day on, the forest was a peaceful place for everyone.

Once there was a rabbit who stared out the window. He stared at the beautiful soil and plants around him, and he wanted to get closer to all the lovely green. So the rabbit tried to find a way out. He searched around for something flexible, so he could squeeze through. He tried different materials, like paper and pieces of cloth, but nothing worked. Frustrated, he kept staring out the window. Then he noticed the window was open a tiny bit. He tried to push it open with his toes and eventually the window opened wide! He hopped outside and enjoyed exploring the soft soil and all the different plants. He was so happy to finally explore the world around him!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a big, red ball. They played with the ball. They had fun. "Let's eat," said Sue. They sat down to eat. Sue had a sandwich that looked normal. Tim had a sandwich too, but it was a funny color. Tim wanted to resist eating it, but he was hungry. Tim took a bite of his sandwich. He did not like the taste. "Yuck!" he said. Then, a dog came and took the sandwich. The dog liked the taste! Tim and Sue laughed. They played with the dog and had more fun.
