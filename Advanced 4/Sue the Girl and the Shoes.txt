Title: Sue the Girl and the Shoes

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to pack her bag for a fashion show. She put her pretty dress, her shiny shoes, and her big hat in the bag. Sue was very excited to go to the fashion show and walk fast on the stage. Sue met her friend, Tom, and said, "I am going to a fashion show today! I packed my bag with all my pretty things." Tom looked at Sue's bag and said, "Wow! That is a big bag. I want to come too!" So, Sue and Tom went to the fashion show together. But when they got there, they saw it was not a fashion show. It was a race! Sue and Tom were surprised. They did not pack for a race. But they still had fun. They put on their shiny shoes and ran fast in the race. They laughed and said, "This is not what we expected, but we had a great time!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Bill. He was three years old and always wanted a journey. Every day he asked his parents if he could go, but they said no. One day, he was in the backyard playing with his toys when he felt a drop of rain. He looked up and the sky was wet. Bill realized that this was the perfect chance to have his journey. He ran inside to find his raincoat and boots and asked his mom if he could go. She said he could and went to get him an umbrella. Bill was so excited! He grabbed his umbrella and walked outside. He ran around in the rain, splashing in all the puddles. He felt so grown-up and brave. He realized this was the journey he’d been waiting for. Bill laughed and smiled as he explored his wet world until he heard his mom call him in for dinner. He put away his umbrella and went inside, but he would never forget his journey in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Frog. Mr. Frog was Tim's best friend. They played together every day. Mr. Frog was fragile, so Tim was very careful with him. One day, Tim's mom had to go to the doctor. The doctor said her lung needed help. Tim was sad and worried. He hugged Mr. Frog tight. Mr. Frog reminded Tim that his mom was strong and would be okay. After some time, Tim's mom got better. Her lung was healthy again. Tim and Mr. Frog were very happy. They played together and reminded each other to be strong, just like Tim's mom. And they all lived happily ever after.

Mommy smiled at her three year old son, Jake. She was holding a big bucket of water, and had a brush and cloth in the other. "Come on, Jake, let's clean!" said Mommy. Jake hopped off the couch and followed Mommy to the front steps. He watched as Mommy filled the bucket with water and then got down low to scrub the steps. "It's time to clean, Mommy," said Jake. He was so eager to help. Mommy laughed and handed Jake the cloth. "Yes, let's clean! Make sure you do it regular, okay?" Jake nodded and got to work. He scrubbed, swished, splashed and generally had a lot of fun. When they were finished, the steps looked nice, bright and clean. "Good job!" said Mommy, giving Jake a hug. Jake beamed with pride and asked if they could clean again the next day. Mommy smiled. "Yes, we can clean again, but not every day. Just regular."

Once upon a time, there was a big stable. In the stable, there were many animals. The animals liked to talk and play with each other. One day, a new animal came to the stable. The new animal was very big and different. The other animals did not know what kind of animal it was. The new animal tried to explain who it was, but the other animals did not understand. They were ignorant about the new animal. They were scared and did not want to play with it. The new animal was very sad and lonely. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came to the stable. She saw the new animal and knew what it was. She told the other animals that the new animal was just like them, but different. She explained that the new animal was a giraffe. The other animals learned about the giraffe and became friends with it. They all played together and lived happily in the stable.
