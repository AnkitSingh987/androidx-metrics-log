Title: The Story of the Sun and the Seal

The wind was blowing gently on the beach. It was a beautiful and peaceful day. Jack had come to the beach to explore. He saw something that was the most adorable thing he had ever seen - a little baby seal! The seal was lying in the sand, quietly curled up and sleeping. Jack wanted to be sure that the seal was safe, so he crept up to protect it. Suddenly the seal's eyes opened. It blinked and looked at him in surprise. "Hi!" Jack said. The seal blinked again and said, "Hello!" Jack was delighted. He laughed out loud and said, "You're so adorable!" The seal smiled and said, "Let's play!" Jack smiled back and said, "Yes, let's play together and keep each other safe." And so the two of them played together on the beach until the sun set. It was the most wonderful day!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very sleepy. He had a big day at the grocery store with his friend, a cat named Mittens. Spot and Mittens bought many yummy things to eat. When they got home, Spot saw that he was dirty from playing at the grocery store. He knew he needed to bathe. Mittens helped Spot get ready for his bath. They filled the tub with warm water and lots of bubbles. Spot jumped into the tub and started to bathe. As he washed, he found a toy he lost a long time ago. Spot was so happy he found his toy! After his bath, Spot was clean and not sleepy anymore. He played with Mittens and his toy all night long.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his toys. But he felt upset because he lost his favorite car. He looked everywhere but could not find it. He asked his mom, "Mom, where is my car?" His mom said, "Did you look in the bathroom?" Tim ran to the bathroom and found his car in the sink! He was very happy and played with his car all day. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam was upset because he lost his toy, too. Tim told him, "I found my car in the bathroom. Maybe your toy is there, too!" They looked in the bathroom, and there was Sam's toy! Sam said, "Thank you, Tim! You helped me find my toy!" They were both happy and played together. From that day on, Tim and Sam learned to always pay attention to where they put their toys, so they would not lose them again.

Once upon a time, there was a famous golf game. Many people came to see it. They were all happy and excited. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the golf game with his mom and dad. He saw people hit the ball with a stick. Tim liked to watch the ball fly far away. He wanted to play golf too. Then, something happened. A big wind came and took all the balls away. Everyone was sad and surprised. But Tim had an idea. He found small rocks and gave them to the people. They played golf with the rocks instead. The famous golf game was saved, and everyone was happy again. Tim was a hero.

One day, Lily was walking by a broken garden. She stopped to look around and saw a beautiful flower in a flower pot. She walked closer and saw that it was a lily. She was about to take it, when suddenly a voice called her. “Lily, why don’t you come in and say hi?” It was an old man and he was smiling at her. Lily thought he looked friendly and she nodded. The old man opened the door and invited her in and Lily followed. Inside the house, the old man showed Lily the lily flower he was growing. He said he wanted to invite her to pick it and give it to someone she love. Lily felt excited and happy, but then she remembered her broken garden. She felt sad and angry and told the man why she couldn’t take the lily. The old man listened and he understood. He smiled and said, “Don’t worry, Lily. I’ll help you fix your garden and then you can pick the lily flower from my garden to give to someone you love.” Lily was so happy and thanked the old man for the kind offer. She smiled and hugged the old man and ran off to fix her broken garden.
