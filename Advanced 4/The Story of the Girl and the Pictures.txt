Title: The Story of the Girl and the Pictures

Once there was a little girl. She loved to make pretty pictures. She couldn't wait to print them out and show to everyone. The little girl used a special machine to print her pictures. It was very loud, but it was also very fast! She was excited when she saw how quickly it worked. When it started to get dark outside, the girl wanted to keep printing. But she had to stop because the machine couldn't work in the dark. The next day, the little girl went back to her printing machine. She was so relieved to see that it still worked in the bright sunshine. She printed her pictures with great speed. When she was finished, the little girl was happy. She had printed so many pictures and they all looked so colorful and beautiful!

Once upon a time there was a girl named Daisy. She loved doing fun things. One day, Daisy was feeling hungry, so she decided to do something special. She went to the store and bought a big, red pizza. Daisy took the pizza home and cut it into small pieces. She was so happy. She put a little bit of cheese on each piece. Then Daisy did something really fun. She put the pizza pieces into the oven and cooked them until they got all hot and yummy. After a few minutes, she took the pizza out of the oven and it was ready! Daisy and her family sat around the table. They clapped their hands and laughed as they ate the delicious, red pizza pieces. It was the best pizza ever!

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and laugh with her friends. One day, Lily saw a pretty box in her mom's room. Inside the box, she found shiny jewelry. There were necklaces, bracelets, and rings. Lily thought they were so beautiful. Lily wanted to wear the jewelry, but she knew she needed to ask her mom first. So, she went to find her mom in the kitchen. "Mom, can I wear your jewelry?" Lily asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, but only for a little while." Lily put on the jewelry and felt so happy. She showed her friends the shiny necklaces, bracelets, and rings. They all thought she looked very pretty. When it was time to give the jewelry back to her mom, Lily felt a little sad. But her mom gave her a big hug and said, "You don't need jewelry to be beautiful. You are always my cheerful, pretty girl." And that made Lily smile.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful car. The car liked to go fast and play with its friends. One day, the car saw a stand with yummy treats. It wanted to stop and eat, but it was going too fast. The colorful car tried to reverse, but it was hard. It did not know how to slow down. The car was sad and scared. It did not want to pass the stand and miss the treats. A big truck saw the colorful car and wanted to help. The truck showed the car how to reverse and stop. The car was happy and thanked the truck. They both went to the stand and enjoyed the yummy treats together. The colorful car and the big truck became best friends.

Once there was a little girl who loved to swing. Every day she would go to the park, run over to the old swing set and hop in. The swing was so soft and cuddly and she love it. She would fly high in the air and giggle as she moved up and down. The little girl would sing songs and wave to the birds in the sky. Every time she went to the park it was so much fun and she could swing for hours. At the end of the day she would lay down in the swing and close her eyes. She found it so calming and loved feeling the swing go up and down. After a few minutes of feeling relaxed and happy, she would get off the swing and go home. The little girl knew that tomorrow she could go back and swing again.
