Title: Sue and Tom and the Together

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue and a boy named Tom. They were very good friends. They liked to play together, eat together, and laugh together. One day, Tom and Sue went to a big party. At the party, they saw a man and a woman start a marriage. They looked so happy and amazing. Sue and Tom liked to see them dance and smile. Sue and Tom thought the marriage was so much fun. They wanted to have a marriage too when they grew up. They knew they would be happy and amazing, just like the man and the woman at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a creative boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and print pictures of things he saw. One day, he took a trip on the subway with his mom. He saw many people and things that he wanted to draw. While on the subway, Tim started to draw a big, tall man who was wearing a funny hat. But, suddenly, the subway stopped and the lights went out. Tim was scared and could not see his paper to draw. The other people on the subway were scared too. Tim had an idea. He used his creative mind to make a game for everyone to play while they waited for the lights to come back on. They all played the game and had fun. When the lights came back on, Tim printed his drawing and gave it to the tall man with the funny hat. The man was happy, and everyone on the subway became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear lived in the woods. He had a friend, a little girl named Lucy. They liked to play together. One day, they found an incredible big ball in the woods. The ball was shiny and bright. "Wow, bear! Look at this ball!" said Lucy. The bear and Lucy played with the ball. They had a lot of fun. They rolled the ball, and they kicked the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger! "Bear, what do we do?" asked Lucy. The bear didn't know what to do. They both kneeled down and looked at the ball. The ball was now bigger than the bear and Lucy. It was so big that it could touch the sky. Suddenly, the ball stopped growing. The bear and Lucy looked at each other. They were scared, but they were also happy. They now had the biggest ball in the world to play with. And so, they played and played, having the most incredible time together.

Once upon a time, there was a clever human. He went to the rich garden and took a walk. He stopped by a pond and noticed a frog sitting on a lily pad. The frog didn't move at all. But the clever human noticed something even more amazing! He saw that the frog was wearing glasses! He thought he had been clever to notice that! The frog just stayed still and watched the human, but the human was even more clever. He waved his hand and said, "Hello there!" Suddenly, the frog jumped up and waved back! The clever human couldn't believe it. He said goodbye, and started walking away. The frog waved back until the clever human was out of sight. The clever human had noticed something amazing! He smiled and thought to himself, humans are really clever!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he did not recognize the big mountain near his house. His mom told him it was a volcano. Tim did not know what a volcano was, so he asked his friend, Sam. "Sam, do you know what a volcano is?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, a volcano is a big mountain that can get very hot and make fire." One day, Tim and Sam were playing near the volcano when something unexpected happened. The volcano started to shake! Tim and Sam were scared and ran back to their houses. But then, they saw that it was not the volcano shaking. It was a big, friendly giant who was playing with the volcano like a toy! Tim and Sam laughed and were not worried anymore. They made a new friend and learned what a volcano was.
