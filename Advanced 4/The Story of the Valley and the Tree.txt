Title: The Story of the Valley and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, nice valley. In the valley, there was a small house. A boy named Tim and his dog, Max, lived there. They were very happy and comfortable in their little house. One sunny day, Tim and Max went for a walk in the valley. They saw a big tree with red apples. Tim said, "Max, let's pick some apples!" Max barked, and they started picking apples. They were having so much fun, they did not want to go home. They decided to delay going back to their house. As the sun began to set, Tim and Max walked back home. Tim's mom was waiting for them. She asked, "Why were you late?" Tim said, "We found a big tree with red apples, and we wanted to pick some. We had so much fun, we delayed coming back." Tim's mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you had fun, but remember to come home on time." Tim and Max nodded, and they all went inside their comfortable house in the valley.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to school. He felt very sleepy. He saw his friend, Jane, and said, "I want to yawn." Jane said, "You should not yawn in school." At lunch time, Tim had a yummy sandwich. But he still felt sleepy. He tried not to yawn, but he could not stop. The teacher saw Tim yawn and said, "Tim, no yawning in school." Tim felt sad. He did not want to yawn in school. He asked Jane, "What can I do?" Jane thought for a moment and said, "Let's play a game. That will make you feel awake." They played a fun game and Tim did not feel sleepy anymore. He did not yawn for the rest of the day. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Jane, for helping me not yawn in school."

Once upon a time, there was a big melon. The melon lived in a little house. One day, a polite cat came to visit the melon. The cat said, "Hello, melon! Can I come in?" The melon said, "Yes, you can come in." The cat and the melon had fun playing. They played with a ball and ate yummy food. Then, it started to rain. The polite cat said, "Oh no! I must cover you so you don't get wet." The melon said, "Thank you, cat." The cat ran outside to find something to cover the melon. The cat found a big leaf. But when the cat came back, the melon was gone! The cat looked and looked but could not find the melon. The rain had washed the melon away. The cat was sad because the melon was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Gilbert. He wanted to travel to the other side of the village, but every day he would get lost! One day, his dad decided to support Gilbert by getting him a vehicle. “This will help you to get to the other side quickly,” his dad said. So Gilbert got on his vehicle and he started driving. Everything was going alright until he drove past a large forest. He felt a bit scared because he had never gone this way before. The trees seemed to close in on him. Suddenly, he heard a loud, angry voice. “Where are you going?” asked the voice. Gilbert looked around and there was an old man on a small, yellow bike. The man seemed ignorant of the fact Gilbert had a vehicle. Gilbert told the man he was going to the other side of the village. The man told Gilbert the fastest way was to keep going straight down the forest path. So Gilbert thanked the old man and continued on his way - and sure enough, he arrived safely at the other side of the village! He was so grateful to his dad for supporting him with the vehicle.

Once upon a time, in a cool forest, there lived a little bunny. This bunny loved to hop and play in the soft grass. Every day, the bunny would hear the birds sing and see the pretty flowers. One day, while the bunny was hopping, it heard a loud noise. The noise scared the bunny, and it ran away to hide. The bunny hid under a big tree, but it could still hear the loud noise. The loud noise was coming from a big, mean wolf. The wolf found the bunny under the tree. The bunny was very scared and tried to run away, but the wolf was too fast. The wolf caught the little bunny. And that was the end of the little bunny's story.
