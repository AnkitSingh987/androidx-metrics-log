Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big chamber. The chamber had many toys available for kids to play with. Tim was very happy to see all the toys. He picked up a toy car and started to play with it. While Tim was playing, another boy named Sam came into the chamber. Sam saw the toy car in Tim's hand and wanted it too. Sam yelled, "I want that toy car!" Tim yelled back, "No, I am playing with it!" They both yelled at each other, and soon, they started to fight. A wise old man was watching them. He walked over and said, "Stop fighting, boys. There are many toys here for everyone. If you share, you can both be happy." Tim and Sam stopped fighting and listened to the wise man. They realized that sharing was better than fighting. So, they both played with the toy car together and had a great time. The unexpected thing was that Tim and Sam became best friends after that day. They learned that sharing and being kind to each other made them much happier than fighting. The moral of the story is to always share and be kind to others, and you will find happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a little dirty pony. He lived on a farm with his friends. The pony liked to play in the mud, and that is why he was always dirty. One day, the pony saw a nice place to settle down and take a nap. It was under a big tree with lots of shade. The pony walked over to the tree and lay down on the soft grass. He closed his eyes and began to sleep. While the pony was sleeping, a kind girl saw him. She thought the dirty pony needed a bath. So, she got a bucket of water and a soft brush. She gently washed the pony until he was clean. When the pony woke up, he felt so much better. He thanked the girl for her help, and they became good friends. From that day on, the pony stayed clean and they played together every day.

Jack had a secret. He was so excited to reveal it but he didn't know when to. He knew that his secret would surprise his best friend, Emma, who was three years old. One day, Jack and Emma were playing outside when his secret finally came to mind. He looked at Emma, smiled and said, "Let's go inside and I will tell you a secret". Emma was thrilled and followed Jack inside the house. Once inside, Jack and Emma saw a really smart phone on the table. Jack said, "Before I reveal my secret, I must show you something really cool that I found". He asked Emma to come closer and then showed her the phone. He explained that it was a really special kind of phone that could do amazing things. Emma was amazed! "Now that I showed you the phone, I can tell you my secret," Jack said. He looked around for a minute and then whispered, "I made this special phone for you! It's a surprise gift! What do you think?" Emma's face lit up with happiness and thanked him for the wonderful surprise. She couldn't believe that Jack was so smart to make such an amazing gift for her. Emma hugged Jack and told him how much she loved her special phone.

John and his Mom were walking to the market. John was really excited to see what goodies he could find. As they approached the market, John noticed something sparkly in the distance. "Mom, what's that?" John asked. "That's the market, John. You can find lots of great things there," Mom smiled. John started to jump up and down with joy, but then realized he didn't have any money to buy anything. He looked down sadly. "Don't worry, John," Mom said. "If you're grateful and accept what I offer you, then we can still get something." John was thrilled! He knew that if Mom said it, it must be true. They walked into the market and John was amazed by all the wonderful things he saw. He chose the perfect treat, then thanked Mom and accepted the gift. He was so grateful. John's Mom told him that if he was grateful and accepted what she had to offer, great things would happen. Sure enough, thanks to her guidance, John had a great time at the market.
