Title: The Farm and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On the farm, there was a lot of wheat. The wheat was tall and rich. It was ready to be gathered. The farmer and his friends came to gather the wheat. They worked hard all day. They cut the wheat and made big piles. The sun was hot, but they were happy. At the end of the day, the farmer and his friends were tired. They looked at the big piles of wheat and smiled. They knew they had gathered enough wheat for the winter. They were very proud of their work.

Sammy was feeling very lonely. He had no one to play with or talk to. He was standing by himself with his hands in his pockets, looking at the ground. Suddenly he heard a voice behind him. "Hello Sammy." Sammy looked up and saw a girl with long hair down to her shoulders and a friendly smile on her face. "Hello," said Sammy. "Who are you?" "I'm Lily," said the girl. Lily reached out her hand and gently touched Sammy's head. "I like your hair," she said. Since then, Sammy and Lily were the best of friends. They would play together and talk every day. No longer did Sammy feel lonely. Thanks to Lily, he had a friend.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a lively lion. He liked to run, jump, and play with his friends. The lion had a big, loud roar that made everyone smile. One day, the lion's friend, a little bird, asked him to bring a ball to play with. The lion was happy to help. He found a big, round ball and carried it in his mouth. He ran back to his friends, and they all played with the ball together. After a while, they got tired and sat down to rest. The lively lion, the little bird, and all their friends had so much fun playing with the ball. They knew that they would have many more happy days together in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit called Peter. Peter was a curious bunny who loved adventure. One day, he decided to go on a walk in the woods. He hopped and hopped, feeling so happy and excited. Suddenly, Peter heard a noise. It was the sound of a car speeding. He looked up and saw a car zooming past. It made Peter scared but also curious. He wondered what it would be like to go so fast. As he kept walking, Peter noticed the weather getting worse. Dark clouds filled the sky and the wind started to blow harder. He realised he had better hurry back home before the storm arrived. So Peter increased his speed and started to move faster. He hopped as fast as he could, not wanting to get caught in the rain. When he finally reached his burrow, Peter was exhausted but happy. The moral of this story is that it's important to think about the weather before starting out on an adventure. Sometimes it's best to complete an activity early, so that you can avoid getting stuck in a bad situation.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play with her mom's perfume. She saw the pretty bottle on the table and thought it smelled so nice. She picked it up and wanted to spray it. Lily's mom saw her and said, "No, Lily! That perfume is not safe for you to play with. It can make you sick if you spray it in your eyes or mouth." Lily felt sad because she really wanted to play with the pretty bottle. Lily's mom thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea! Let's make a safe perfume just for you to play with!" They went to the kitchen and mixed water and flower petals in a small spray bottle. Lily was so happy to have her own safe perfume to play with, and she never touched her mom's perfume again.

One day, a big, ugly bug went for a walk. It put on its little coat because it was cold outside. The bug walked and walked until it found a tall tree. The tree had many pretty leaves. The bug wanted to go up and see them. The bug started to climb the tree. It leaned on a strong branch to help it go up. The bug saw other bugs and they all played together. They jumped from branch to branch and had lots of fun. After a while, the ugly bug felt tired. It put on its coat and leaned on a soft leaf to take a nap. When the bug woke up, it was time to go home. It said goodbye to its new friends and went back down the tree. The ugly bug went home happy and ready for bed.
