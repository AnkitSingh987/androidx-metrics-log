Title: Lily the Clever Girl and the Waffles

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Lily. She loved to eat waffles every day. She would eat them with her mom and dad in the morning. One day, Lily had an idea. She wanted to decorate her waffles. She thought it would make them look pretty and taste even better. So, she asked her mom for help. Lily and her mom used fruit, syrup, and whipped cream to decorate the waffles. They made smiley faces and funny shapes. Lily was so happy with her pretty waffles. She shared them with her mom and dad, and they all agreed that the decorated waffles were the best they had ever tasted.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. He loved to play and run in the yard. One day, Max saw a pretty dog named Bella. Bella lived in the house next to Max. Max liked Bella a lot. Max wanted to have a marriage with Bella. He thought about how to ask her. Max was a little bit embarrassed. He did not know if Bella would like the idea. Max decided to give Bella a big bone as a gift. Max gave the bone to Bella and asked her for marriage. Bella was happy and said yes. Max realized that he did not need to be embarrassed. Max and Bella had a fun marriage party with all their dog friends. They played and ate treats together. Max and Bella were very happy.

One day, a fast car named Zoomy went to a big race. Zoomy wanted to win a shiny trophy. He felt happy and excited. Before the race, Zoomy met a slow truck named Terry. Terry felt terrible because he was not fast. "I want to win a trophy too," said Terry. "But I am too slow." Zoomy had an idea. "Let's race together, Terry. We can help each other and try to win the trophy." Terry smiled and said, "Okay, Zoomy. Let's try." Zoomy and Terry raced together. They went fast and slow. All the other cars went zoom, but Zoomy and Terry worked together. They crossed the finish line and won the trophy. Zoomy and Terry were so happy. They learned that helping each other is the best way to win.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She was very hungry and wanted something to eat. Suddenly, her eyes lit up when she spotted a lollipop. It was lying in the grass, so she sprang up and grabbed it. As Lily looked round, she saw a group of her friends playing in the park. She was so excited that she ran over to them, lollipop in her hand. "Look what I found," beamed Lily as she showed it to them. "That looks really tasty!" said one of the friends. "I'm so hungry. Can I have a bite?" asked Lily. The friends smiled and nodded. So, Lily took a big bite of the lollipop and enjoyed the sweet deliciousness. She smiled, feeling happy and satisfied. The end.

Once there was a girl named Jane. Jane loved to eat healthy food. One day, her mom made a new dish for her. Jane did not know what it was. She had to guess! Jane looked at her plate. She saw green and red things. She thought, "Is it a pepper?" She took a bite. It was a pepper! Jane was happy. She loved peppers. They were healthy and yummy. Jane ate all the peppers on her plate. Her mom smiled. "Good job, Jane," she said. "You guessed right! Peppers are very healthy for you." Jane smiled back. She was proud of herself for guessing right.

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. He was very troubled because he had a lot of work to do. He had to print lots of papers in order to finish it all. So he took some sheets of paper and put them in the printer. He pressed the button and waited to see what would happen. The printer started to make a lot of noise and printed all the papers for the lawyer. He was so happy because the printer had done all the work for him. The lawyer could finish his job quickly and the papers were perfect. The lawyer was so pleased that he gave the printer a big hug. From then on, the lawyer and the printer were best friends. Every day, the lawyer printed more papers and every day the printer helped him out. The end.
