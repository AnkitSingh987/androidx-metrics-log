Title: The Boy Was Playing and the Garden

One day, a little boy was playing in his garden. He was so excited because he was pretending to be a pilot in his own little helicopter. He felt amazing and he wanted to fly higher and higher. He shouted at the sky, "I can fly to the moon!" Suddenly an older man came walking past. He smiled and said, "Climb in and I'll help you fly even higher!" The little boy was so excited, he ran to the back of the garden and hopped into the man's helicopter. As they flew higher and higher, all the boy could see was beautiful, clouds and a big blue sky. He was feeling more and more amazing. But suddenly, the helicopter started to get shaky and the man said, "We have to land now." The little boy was sad and scared. He asked the man why, but the man just kept quiet. The boy shouted, "Tell me why!" Suddenly, the police were everywhere and the man had to get away. The little boy was so scared and he started to cry. Then the man said, "I am a bad guy - I can't take you anywhere." The little boy never got to fly to the moon.

Once upon a time, there was a little purple fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big reef with his family and friends. One day, Fin saw a sad fish named Lily. Lily was hungry and couldn't find any food. Fin wanted to help Lily, so he said, "Lily, I will serve you some food. Let's find it together!" They swam around the reef, looking for something to eat. Soon, they found some yummy green plants. Fin served the plants to Lily and she happily ate them. After eating, Lily felt much better and said, "Thank you, Fin! You are a good friend." From that day on, Fin and Lily were best friends. They learned that helping others is a good thing to do. And all the fish in the reef lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a funny cow named Bessie. Bessie loved to make her friends laugh. She lived on a farm with many other animals. They all liked to play together. One day, Bessie had a problem. She could not make milk. This made her suffer. She wanted to help the farmer and her friends, but she did not know what to do. Bessie felt sad. Bessie's friends saw her feeling sad. They wanted to help her. They all worked together to find a way to make Bessie feel better. In the end, they found a way to make Bessie make milk again. Bessie was so happy, and she went back to being the funny cow that everyone loved.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Timmy. He was very hungry and couldn't find anything to eat. He looked around but nothing was tasty. Then, he saw the sunrise. It was beautiful and made him feel happy. Timmy said, "Sunrise is yummy, maybe I can eat it!" He looked and looked to see if he could find something to bite. But all he saw was the red in the sky. Then, he spotted a yummy strawberry growing in the grass. He said, "I'm so hungry and this strawberry looks so tasty!" So Timmy ate the strawberry and it made him feel happy and full. After his tasty snack, he watched the sunrise until it disappeared in the night sky. He smiled, thankful that he ate the strawberry and watched the beautiful sunrise.

Once there was a little girl called May. She was three years old and loved playing outside in the garden. One day May decided to take a walk in the garden. She stepped slowly and quietly through the grass, looking all around. Suddenly, May spotted a little bird perched on a branch in a tree. “Hello, little bird!” said May. The bird tilted its head and stayed very still. So May stepped a little closer, slowly and quietly. The bird flew away with a quick burst. May was a bit sad because she wanted to meet the bird. She looked up in the sky and smiled. She felt like the bird was saying goodbye. May stepped back and waved her arms. “Goodbye!” said May. She smiled and watched until the bird was out of sight. Then May turned around and stepped home, feeling happy.
