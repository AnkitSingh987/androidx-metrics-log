Title: The Sunflower and the Garden

Once there was a pretty sunflower. It lived in a big garden. The sunflower was friends with a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with the sunflower every day. One day, the sunflower told Lily, "I want to show you something very special." Lily was excited and asked, "What is it?" The sunflower said, "You will see tomorrow." The next day, Lily went to the garden. The sunflower was now very tall. It had many pretty yellow flowers on it. The sunflower said, "I told you I would show you something special. Look at all my pretty flowers!" Lily was very happy and clapped her hands. She loved her pretty sunflower friend.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play outside. They liked to see the flowers and the birds in spring. They liked to run and jump and laugh. One day, they saw a little bird on the ground. It had a broken wing and looked troubled. Lily and Tom felt sad for the bird. "Can we help the bird?" Lily asked Tom. "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe we can take it to Mom. She knows how to care for animals." They carefully picked up the bird and put it in a box. They ran to Tom's house and showed the bird to Mom. Mom smiled and said, "You are very kind to care for the bird. Let me see what I can do." She took the bird to the kitchen and wrapped its wing with a bandage. She gave it some water and seeds. She put the box in a warm and quiet place. "Thank you, Mom," Lily and Tom said. "You're welcome," Mom said. "The bird needs some rest and love. Maybe it will get better soon." Lily and Tom hoped the bird would get better. They visited it every day and talked to it softly. They named it Fluffy. Fluffy started to chirp and move more. After a few days, it could flap its wing again. Lily and Tom were happy to see Fluffy happy. "Can we let Fluffy go now?" Lily asked Mom. "Yes, I think Fluffy is ready to fly again," Mom said. "But you have to say goodbye first." Lily and Tom hugged Fluffy and thanked it for being their friend. They took it outside and opened the box. Fluffy flew out and circled around them. It sang a sweet song and then flew away. "Bye, Fluffy!" Lily and Tom shouted. "We will miss you!" They watched Fluffy disappear in the sky. They felt a little sad, but also proud. They had cared for a troubled bird and helped it heal. They had learned a lot from Fluffy. They smiled and held hands. They went back to play and enjoy the spring.

Once upon a time, there was a very little boy named Tommy. Tommy was three years old and always loved going to the library. He would always ask his mom to take him! Today, Tommy was feeling quite miserable. He was sad and nothing seemed to cheer him up. His mom thought she knew just how to make him smile again. She asked him if he wanted to go to the library. Tommy's face lit up and he jumped for joy. He ran to the car and off they went. When they arrived at the library, Tommy couldn't believe how many books he saw. There were so many different kinds. He was so excited! Tommy chose a book about animals and settled down with it in a corner. Soon, he was so engrossed in the story that he forgot how miserable he was. Tommy's mom smiled. She was so glad that the library could make him feel happy again. They stayed there until it closed and Tommy couldn't wait to come back the very next day.

Once upon a time, there was a big waffle house. In this house, there lived a little girl named Amy and her mom. They loved to make delicious waffles for breakfast every day. One sunny morning, as they were making waffles, a talking cat entered the house. The cat said, "Hello, can I have a delicious waffle too?" Amy and her mom were very surprised. They had never seen a talking cat before! Amy's mom gave the cat a delicious waffle. The cat ate it and said, "Yummy! Thank you!" Then, the cat did a magic trick. With a wave of its tail, the cat turned all the waffles into colorful balloons. Amy, her mom, and the cat laughed and played with the balloons all day long.
