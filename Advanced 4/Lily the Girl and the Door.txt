Title: Lily the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to design things with her crayons. Every day, she drew her favorite thing, the sunrise. Lily was a regular girl, but she had a big imagination. One day, while Lily was drawing the sunrise, something unexpected happened. The colors from her crayons jumped off the paper and onto the walls! The whole room was filled with the colors of the sunrise. Lily was surprised and very happy. Then, Lily had an idea. She took her crayons and started to design a door on the wall. When she was done, the door opened, and Lily walked into a world where everything was made of crayon. In this world, she could design anything she wanted. And so, Lily spent her days in the crayon world, making it more and more beautiful.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a beautiful feather that he loved very much. One day, his friend, a squirrel named Sally, found a disgusting bug in her tree. "Yuck! Look at this disgusting bug, Timmy!" said Sally. Timmy looked at the bug and said, "I don't like it either, Sally." Just then, a strong wind blew and Timmy's feather flew away. Timmy and Sally went to find the feather. They looked everywhere, but they couldn't find it. Timmy was very sad. Suddenly, they met a big bear named Benny. Benny was holding Timmy's feather. "I found this pretty feather. Is it yours, Timmy?" Benny asked. Timmy was so happy to see his feather, but Sally disagreed. "No, Benny, that's not Timmy's feather. His feather is much prettier," said Sally. But when Timmy looked closely, he realized it was his feather! It had changed colors and was now even more beautiful than before. The disgusting bug had turned it into a magical feather. Timmy, Sally, and Benny all laughed and played together, happy with their new magical feather friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round ball. The ball was red and very smooth. It loved to bounce all day long. One day, the ball met a small, blue ball. The blue ball also loved to bounce. "Hi, I'm Red Ball!" said the big, round ball. "Do you want to play with me?" "Hi, I'm Blue Ball!" said the small, blue ball. "Yes, let's play a game!" Red Ball and Blue Ball decided to play a game to see who could bounce the highest. They both bounced up and down, higher and higher. They were having so much fun. At the end of the game, Red Ball and Blue Ball both bounced very high. They couldn't tell who bounced the highest. So, they decided that they both should win. Red Ball and Blue Ball became best friends. They played and bounced together every day, happy with their new friendship. And they always remembered that playing together was more important than winning.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big tree with red apples on it. Tim wanted to get an apple, but they were too high for him to reach. Tim saw a gray cat sitting near the tree. The cat was looking up at the apples too. Tim asked the cat, "Can you help me get an apple?" The cat jumped high and hit a branch. An apple started to fall. The apple fell down, and Tim caught it. He was so happy and said, "Thank you, gray cat!" Tim shared the apple with the cat, and they ate it together. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a big jellyfish named Bob swam in the sea. He was very jealous of his friend, the fish named Tim. Tim could swim very fast, and Bob could not. Bob was sad, so he talked to Tim. "Tim, I want to swim fast like you," said Bob. "Can you help me?" "Sure, Bob!" said Tim. "I will show you how to swim fast." As they swam together, they saw a big hand from a child named Max. Max loved to play with sea animals. Max wanted to play with Bob, but he didn't know that jellyfish can sting. Max tried to slap Bob, but Tim swam very fast and pushed Bob away. Max's hand missed Bob and hit the water. Max was sad, but he learned that he should not slap jellyfish. Bob was happy that Tim helped him. Now, Bob was not jealous of Tim anymore. They were best friends and swam together in the sea.
