Title: Tim and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a wish. He wanted to be useful to his friends and family. He liked helping everyone. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He thought, "I can help!" So, he tried to climb the tree. But, something unexpected happened. Tim slipped and fell. He was not hurt, but he felt embarrass. He did not know what to do. Then, a kind bird saw Tim. The bird flew to the cat and helped it down. Tim was happy the cat was safe. The bird told Tim, "You are useful. You made me see the cat." Tim smiled, and from that day on, he knew that even when things do not go as planned, he can still be helpful.

Once upon a time there was an elephant who lived in a cool forest. He was always happy and liked to rock in the sunshine. One day, a big hurricane blew into the forest and it was very noisy. The elephant was scared and ran around. Suddenly, he heard a voice. He looked up and saw a bird perched on a branch. The bird said "Don't worry, Elephant. I will help you. Just sit in this tree and rock back and forth". The elephant was so glad that he thanked the bird. So, the elephant sat in the tree and rocked as the hurricane blew all around him. After the storm had passed, the elephant saw that his cool forest was still standing. He thanked the bird again and they both jumped up and down in excitement.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to make knots. She would spend hours tying pretty little knots with her pieces of string. One day, her mom saw her tying knots and said, "Honey, why don't you clean up your room instead of tying knots?" The little girl thought for a moment and then replied, "I need to be creative, Mom. I like making knots." Her mom smiled and said, "That's true, but if you don't clean your room, you will end up with a dull, messy mess." The little girl decided that her mom was right and quickly started to clean up her room. When she was finished, it looked so much better and it felt much more organized. The little girl learned an important lesson that day—it's important to be creative, but it's also important to be neat and organized. Being creative makes life more enjoyable and being neat keeps life from becoming dull and messy.

One day, a youth named Tim wanted to perform a show for his friends. He was good at singing and dancing. He thought his friends would like it. So, he asked his friends to come to his house to watch him perform. When his friends came, Tim sang and danced for them. His friends clapped and laughed. They were very happy to see him perform. They were grateful that Tim invited them to his show. After the show, Tim and his friends played together. They had a lot of fun. They were all very happy. Tim was glad that he could perform for his friends, and his friends were happy that they could see Tim perform. They were all grateful for the fun day they had together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tasty cake in a freezer. The cake was very happy living in the cold place. But one day, two little mice found the freezer and saw the cake. The two mice started to quarrel about who would eat the cake. One mouse said, "I saw it first, so it's mine!" The other mouse said, "No, I saw it first. It's mine!" They were very loud and did not want to share the cake. Then, a kind cat came and heard the mice quarreling. The cat said, "Why don't you share the tasty cake?" The mice thought about it and agreed. They cut the cake in half and ate it together. The mice were happy and became good friends. They never quarreled again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big opera. Many people came to sit and watch. They loved the soft music and the beautiful voices. The opera made everyone happy. One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to see the opera. But she was too small to sit on the big chairs. She was sad because she could not see the singers. Then, a kind man saw Lily and had an idea. He found a soft pillow for her to sit on. Now, Lily could see the opera and she was very happy. The people in the town saw what the kind man did for Lily. They learned that helping others can make everyone happy. From that day on, the people in the town were always kind to each other. And they all enjoyed the soft music of the opera together.
