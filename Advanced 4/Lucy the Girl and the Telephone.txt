Title: Lucy the Girl and the Telephone

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a very brave girl. One day, she was playing in her room when the telephone rang. Lucy wanted to answer it, but she was too small to reach it. Lucy did not give up. She managed to find a chair and pushed it to the telephone. She stood on the chair and picked up the telephone. "Hello?" she said in her sweet voice. "Hi Lucy, it's Mommy," said the voice on the other end. "You are so brave to answer the phone all by yourself. I am proud of you!" Lucy smiled, feeling happy that she managed to do it. She talked with her mommy and then put the telephone back. From that day on, Lucy knew she could do anything if she tried.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty had a best friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily wore a pretty blouse every day. They loved to play together in the garden. One day, a big dog came into the garden. The dog looked serious. Kitty and Lily were scared. The dog wanted to play, but Kitty and Lily were too small. They needed to find a way to make the dog go away. Kitty had an idea. She would surprise the dog with a loud noise. Kitty and Lily found a big pot and a spoon. They hid behind a tree. When the dog came close, they hit the pot with the spoon. The dog was surprised and ran away. Kitty and Lily were safe and happy again.

Lisa loves to bake with her mom. They make cakes and cookies and pies. Lisa likes to mix the flour and the eggs and the sugar. She likes to lick the spoon and the bowl. Baking is fun and yummy. One day, mom says, "Lisa, today we are going to bake something special. It is a luxury. Do you know what that means?" Lisa shakes her head. She does not know. Mom says, "A luxury is something that is very nice and rare and makes you happy. Like a rainbow or a hug or a present. We are going to bake a luxury cake with orange frosting. Do you like orange?" Lisa nods. She likes orange. Orange is a bright and happy color. Orange is the color of the sun and the carrots and the pumpkins. Lisa says, "Yes, mom, I like orange. Can I help you make the orange frosting?" Mom says, "Of course, my sweetie. You are my best helper. Come, let's go to the kitchen and bake our luxury cake." Mom and Lisa go to the kitchen. They take out a big bowl and a mixer and a cake pan. They put butter and sugar and eggs and vanilla in the bowl. They mix them together until they are smooth and creamy. Then they add flour and baking powder and a pinch of salt. They mix them some more until they have a thick and fluffy batter. They pour the batter into the cake pan and put it in the oven. They set the timer and wait for the cake to bake. While the cake is baking, mom and Lisa make the orange frosting. They take another bowl and a spoon and a knife. They put cream cheese and butter and powdered sugar in the bowl. They stir them together until they are smooth and white. Then they add some orange juice and some orange zest. They stir them some more until they have a tangy and orange frosting. They taste it with their fingers. It is delicious. They smile at each other and say, "Mmm, this is a luxury."

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a little daisy. Daisy loved to dream. Every day, she would dream about the sun, the rain, and the wind. She was a very happy flower. One day, the sky was very dry. Daisy started to feel sad. She needed water to grow big and strong. Daisy had a dream that night. In her dream, she saw a big cloud. The cloud gave her lots of water. The next day, Daisy saw a big cloud in the sky. She was very happy. She thought her dream came true. But then, something unexpected happened. The cloud was not full of water. It was full of dirt! The dirt fell on Daisy and covered her up. Daisy could not see the sun or feel the rain anymore. She was very sad. She tried to dream of a better day, but her dreams did not come true. And that is the end of the story of the little daisy.
