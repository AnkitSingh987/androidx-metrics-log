Title: Amy and the Magic

One day, Amy and her mommy went to the airport. Mommy was going on a trip and Amy wanted to come and say goodbye. There was excitement in the air. As they walked through the airport, Amy saw people dancing. The music made her feel happy and she began to dance too. Mommy smiled and said, “You’re a perfect dancer, Amy!” Amy jumped with joy. She danced around the airport, spinning in circles and clapping her hands. She had never felt so free! The music was like a magical spell and she couldn’t get enough of it. After a few more minutes, it was time to say goodbye. Mommy hugged Amy tightly and said, “I will miss you, my perfect dancer.” Amy smiled, then waved goodbye as Mommy boarded the plane. She felt so proud that her mommy had noticed her dancing. As Amy left the airport, she remembered the magic of the music and the joy of dancing. It was the perfect goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to look out her window and watch the birds fly in the sky. One day, Lily wanted to go outside and play with the birds, but her mom said, "No, you can't leave the house right now." Lily felt anxious. She wanted to play with the birds so much. She looked out the window again and saw a little bird sitting on a branch. The little bird looked sad too. Lily opened the window and said, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird replied, "I want to fly with my friends, but I can't leave this branch. I'm scared." Lily understood how the bird felt. She wanted to help the bird feel better. Lily said, "Don't be scared, little bird. I'm anxious too, but we can be brave together." The little bird felt better and left the branch to fly with its friends. Lily felt happy that she helped the bird and learned that she could be brave even when she felt anxious.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She wanted to go to the factory. But her mom told her no. Sarah felt very sorry. She asked, "Why can't I go, mommy?" Mommy said, "Because it's too dangerous for you to go. You could get hurt." Sarah said, "I promise to be careful. Please, please let me go!" Mommy said, "I'm sorry, but the answer is still no." So Sarah decided to find a way to get to the factory. She packed a bag with snacks and then she squeezed through the bars on the fence. When she arrived at the factory she was so excited! But suddenly, the security guards spotted her and said, "Hey! What are you doing here? This is a restricted area!" Sarah was so scared, she squeezed back through the bars and ran home. When she arrived home, mommy was very surprised. She gave Sarah a big hug, and said, "I'm sorry I didn't let you go. Let me know when you want to go again, and we'll go together." Sarah was so happy. She had her adventure, and she was safe.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly lived in a big tree with her family. She was a very excited parrot who loved to talk and play with her friends. One day, Polly met a new friend named Tom. Tom was a cat who lived nearby. Polly said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, Polly! I would love to play with you!" Polly and Tom played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. They had so much fun! Polly started to think, "Tom is a great friend. I am so happy we met." At the end of the day, Polly and Tom were very tired. They sat under the big tree and talked. Polly said, "Tom, I had a great day. I hope we can play again soon." Tom agreed, "Yes, Polly! I had fun too. Let's play again tomorrow." Polly and Tom became best friends. They played together every day, and they were always excited to see each other. The end.
