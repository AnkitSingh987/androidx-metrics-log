Title: The Pirate and the Ship

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a pirate. The pirate had a big hat and a big ship. The pirate was sometimes rude to others. He would take their things and laugh. One day, the pirate met a nice girl. The girl was not scared of the pirate. She said, "Please don't be rude, Mr. Pirate." The pirate did not know what to say. No one had ever mentioned his rudeness before. The pirate thought about what the girl said. He decided to be nice. He gave back the things he took. The pirate and the girl became friends. They had fun on the big ship, and the pirate was never rude again.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl named Daisy. Daisy was a very charming girl who liked to do special tricks. One day, Daisy was sitting with her friends outside in the sun when suddenly she got an idea. She grabbed some paper, held it up and said, “Let me show you what I can do!” Excitedly, her friends stood around her and asked, “What are you going to do?” Daisy smiled and said, “I’m going to dive into the paper!” The friends couldn’t believe it! They had never heard of someone diving into paper before. But Daisy wasn’t scared and she quickly counted down, “Three…two…one…DIVE!” As Daisy dove into the paper, it folded around her and she disappeared. Her friends gasped in awe and then quickly ran over to where Daisy had been standing. But Daisy was nowhere in sight. Just then, Daisy suddenly pushed her way out from the paper and said, “Ta-da! I’m still here!” Her friends laughed and cheered in amazement of Daisy’s charming trick. And that’s how Daisy became known as the paper diver!

Johnny was walking by a bridge. He looked nervously at it and began to worry. "What's wrong, Johnny?" asked his mum. Johnny replied "I'm worried about crossing the bridge". Mum smiled and said, "Don't worry Johnny, that bridge is very strong." Johnny stopped worrying and smiled back. As Johnny crossed the bridge, he saw a group of people. They were shouting angrily at each other and they seemed very ignorant. Johnny didn't understand why they were so angry. Mum said, "Don't worry, they are just arguing, but they will still be polite to us." Johnny smiled and he kept walking. He crossed the bridge and felt much happier. He knew that his mum was right and he felt proud of himself for being brave. From then on, Johnny was no longer worried about crossing bridges.

Once, there was a little boy who wanted to open his toy chest. He had been playing with the special toys in it for some time but he had forgotten where he kept the key. He was very sad and couldn't find it anywhere. He asked his dad for help. His dad said, "Don't worry, I'll help you". He took out a lock and a screwdriver and told the little boy to watch. He slowly unscrewed the old lock with the screwdriver and replaced it with the new lock. The little boy was curious. He asked, "What's this new lock for, dad?" His dad smiled and said, "It's so no one can get into the toy chest and steal your special toys. It's mild enough that you can open it yourself, but strong enough for no one else to!" The little boy smiled and hugged his dad, glad that his special toys were safe.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, a little cat named Kitty lived with her animal friends. Kitty loved to play and search for new things around the farm. One sunny day, Kitty saw a big pile of hay near the barn. She thought it would be fun to jump and search inside the hay. So, she jumped in and started to dig and search. She found a small, shiny stone and was very happy. But then, a fierce dog named Max came to the hay. Max wanted to play too, but he was too big and fierce for Kitty. Kitty got scared and hid in the hay. Max looked for Kitty, but he could not find her. He felt sad and went away. After Max left, Kitty came out of the hay. She saw Max looking sad and decided to be brave. She went to Max and showed him the shiny stone. Max's face lit up and they played together, becoming good friends. From that day on, Kitty and Max played and searched for new things together, and they were always happy. The end.
