Title: Lily and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a big room with an enormous curtain. In the room, a girl named Lily loved to paint. She painted all day long. She painted the sun, the moon, and the stars. She painted animals, like dogs and cats. Lily was happy when she painted. One day, while Lily was painting, she heard a little voice. "Hi, can I paint too?" the voice asked. Lily looked around but saw no one. Then, she looked at the enormous curtain. There, she saw a tiny mouse with a tiny brush. "Sure," Lily said, "we can paint together!" Lily and the mouse started painting the enormous curtain. They painted a big tree, a house, and a rainbow. Suddenly, the tree started to move! The house opened its door, and the rainbow began to shine. Lily and the mouse were surprised. The paintings had come to life! They laughed and played in their new magical world.

Once upon a time, there was a big train. The train had a big friend named Tom. They liked to play together. One day, the big train needed coal. The train said, "Tom, can you help me find coal?" Tom said, "Yes, big train! Let's look for coal together." They went to find coal. They saw a big mountain. Tom said, "Look, big train! The coal is in the mountain." They went to the mountain and found the coal. The big train was happy. But then, the big train started to sneeze. "Achoo! Achoo!" The big train sneezed out all the coal. Tom laughed and said, "Silly train, let's get more coal." They got more coal, and the big train did not sneeze again. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there lived a little fish named Jack. Jack lived in a big, blue ocean. Every morning, Jack liked to swim around, and explore. One day, Jack noticed a lonely little hut at the bottom of the ocean. Jack swam closer to the hut, and peeped through the window. He wondered who might be living there. To his surprise, he saw an octopus inside! Jack waved at the octopus, and said, "Hello! Who are you?" "I'm Bob", the octopus replied. "I swim here in my lonely little hut. Would you like to come inside?" Jack thought for a second before nodding his head. Jack and Bob became great friends. They swam around together all day, exploring the ocean and its secrets. They even played some fun games in Bob's hut. Jack loved spending time with Bob in the lonely little hut. From then on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a modern town, there was a big door. This door could unlock a new world. Many people wanted to see what was inside. They tried to open it, but no one could. One day, a small boy named Tom found a key. He thought it could unlock the big door. He ran to the door and tried the key. The door opened, and the new world was in front of him. Tom walked into the new world. But it was not nice like his town. It was dark and scary. There were big monsters and loud noises. Tom was very afraid. He tried to go back, but the door was locked. He was stuck in the scary world.

Once upon a time, a girl named Anna wanted to go on a holiday. She was very excited. She told her friends about her holiday. Her friends felt envious because they wanted to go too. Anna started to pack her bag. She put in her clothes, shoes, and toys. She also packed her favorite teddy bear. Anna's mom helped her pack her bag. She made sure Anna had everything she needed. Finally, it was time for Anna's holiday. She said goodbye to her friends. They wished her a fun time. Anna promised to tell them all about her holiday when she came back. And off she went, ready for a great adventure.

Lily liked to play with her mom's crystals. They were shiny and pretty and made rainbows on the wall. She had a favorite one that was pink and sparkly. She always took it to her room and put it on her pillow. One day, mom was looking for her pink crystal. She asked Lily if she had seen it. Lily said no, but she felt bad. She knew the crystal belonged to mom, not to her. She wanted to keep it, but she also wanted to make mom happy. She ran to her room and got the crystal. She brought it to mom and said sorry. She hugged mom and gave her the crystal. Mom smiled and thanked her. She said she was not upset, she was proud of Lily for being honest and kind. She said Lily could borrow the crystal sometimes, but she had to ask first. Lily nodded and said okay. Mom and Lily went to the living room and played with the crystals. They made rainbows and shapes and stories. Lily felt happy and loved. She learned that sharing was better than hiding. She learned that some things belong to others, but some things belong to everyone.
