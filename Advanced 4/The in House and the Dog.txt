Title: The in House and the Dog

Once upon a time, a big, round melon lived in a little house. The melon had a loyal friend, a small dog. They liked to play together every day. The dog loved to run and jump, and the melon liked to roll around. One day, the melon and the dog wanted to bake a cake. They went to the kitchen and found a big bowl. They put in eggs, flour, and sugar. The melon and the dog mixed it all up. They put it in the oven to bake. When the cake was done, the melon and the dog took it out of the oven. They let it cool down. Then, they put icing on the cake. The melon and the dog shared the yummy cake. They were very happy and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max felt helpless. He lost his ball in the big park. He looked for it all week but could not find it. Max was very sad. One sunny day, his friend, a bird named Tim, saw Max looking sad. Tim urged Max to play with him. They played and laughed together. Max started to feel better. At the end of the day, Tim flew high in the sky and found Max's ball. He brought it back to Max. Max was so happy and thanked Tim. They played with the ball together and were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was a very reliable cow. She always did what she was told. One day, Bessie's owner, Farmer Joe, asked her to help him paint the barn. Bessie was excited to help. She knew that painting the barn would make it look nice. Bessie and Farmer Joe worked hard all day. They painted the barn red. Bessie was very good at painting. But then, a big storm came. The wind blew the paint away, and the barn was not red anymore. Bessie and Farmer Joe were sad. They knew they had to paint the barn again. The next day, they painted the barn again. This time, they used a special paint that would not blow away in the wind. Bessie and Farmer Joe were happy because the barn looked nice and red again. From that day on, Bessie was not just a reliable cow, but also a great painter. She helped Farmer Joe paint many things on the farm, and they lived happily ever after.

One day Tommy went to the park with his mommy. His mommy told Tommy he was not allowed to go too far away. But Tommy was a very stubborn little boy. He wanted to explore the big park, so he ran away and did not listen to his mommy. "Tommy! Come back here right now!" his mommy said. But Tommy just kept running further away. Then a big dog came running up behind Tommy. The dog was barking but to Tommy, it seemed friendly. "That's ok, mommy. The doggy just wants to play," Tommy said, standing on his heels to get a better look. His mommy was not so sure, though. "No, Tommy. We don't know this dog, so I don't want you to go near it," she said. Tommy nodded. He knew that mommy was really being careful, so he stayed where he was and waved. The dog chased after a nearby squirrel and ran off, leaving Tommy with his mommy. Tommy felt very proud that he had been so stubborn and obeyed his mommy. He knew that being stubborn could be a good thing sometimes.

Once upon a time there was a little old girl. She kept a diary to write down all of her special memories. Every day she wrote down something that made her happy. One day, the little girl did something she didn't want to do. She wanted to go play her favorite game, but instead she had to clean her room. She was very sad and wrote about it in her diary. She wrote about how she regretted not playing the game. As time passed, the girl grew older. She still remembered the time she didn't get to play. Even though years had passed, she still felt the same regret. Finally, the girl decided to take control of her day and do something that she wanted. She got out her old diary and read what she had written. It reminded her that she should make the most out of each day, because it's too easy to regret the things we miss out on!
