Title: Tim the Boy and the Desk

One day, a boy named Tim was cleaning up his desk in his bedroom. He had to get everything ready for the morning. He was almost done when he noticed something on the top of the desk. It was shiny and sparkly. He wanted it very much, but knew it wasn't his. He tried to resist taking it, but he couldn't. Tim reached for it and put it in his pocket. The next morning Tim woke up extra early and was ready for the new day. He raced to his desk, where he got ready to start the day. But as he opened his pocket, he saw the shiny, sparkly thing he had taken the night before. He couldn't believe he had gone and spoiled the day by taking something that wasn't his. Tim was so scared of getting into trouble with his parents that he decided to go and give the item back where he found it. When he returned to his desk to tidy it up, he noticed something written on the item. It said "Happy Birthday!" Tim couldn't believe it. His parents had gotten him something special and it was meant to be a surprise. He was so relieved and grateful he had been able to put things back in order. The desk was nice and tidy and Tim was ready for the surprise that was about to come.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to play with matches. One day he found a cool matchbox by the front door. He was very excited. He picked up the matchbox and screamed with joy! When he opened the box, the little boy was surprised to hear music coming from inside. He was so excited! The matchbox had a tiny music box inside, and it was playing beautiful music. The boy took the box and started to dance. He moved around the room, bouncing to the rhythm of the music. Then, he heard a voice coming from the box. It said, "Let's play a game!" The boy was delighted. He quickly grabbed a match from the box and lit it. Then, he held the match up towards the sky and watched as it burned. Suddenly, a rainbow appeared in the sky! The little boy was overjoyed. He waved goodbye to the matchbox and the rainbow, and went off to play. He was so happy and excited. It had been the coolest game ever!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to speak to her toys. She was very enthusiastic when she played with them. Sue's favorite toy was a cat named Kitty. Kitty had a pretty apron that Sue's mom made. One day, Sue put on her own apron and pretended to cook with Kitty. They made a big cake with lots of frosting. Sue spoke to Kitty and said, "Kitty, we make the best cake!" Kitty just smiled because toys can't speak. Sue and Kitty played all day. They were very happy together. Sue's mom came in and said, "Sue, you are such a good cook!" Sue smiled and hugged her mom. She knew that her mom was proud of her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a blue bag. One day, she wanted to go on a picnic with her friend, Tom. So, Lucy decided to pack her blue bag with yummy food. Lucy put in a big red apple, a sweet cherry, and some tasty bread. She met Tom at the park. "Look at my blue bag, Tom!" said Lucy. "I have yummy food for our picnic!" Tom smiled and said, "I brought a ball to play with!" They ate the apple, the cherry, and the bread. After they ate, they played with the ball. But then, a big dog came and took their ball. They tried to get it back, but the dog ran away with the ball. Lucy and Tom were sad. They went home without their ball. And that was the end of their day.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play in the sunshine. One day, Fluffy saw her toys all over the floor. She wanted to organize her toys, but her brother, a dog named Spot, wanted to play with them. Fluffy tried to tell Spot that they needed to organize the toys. Spot did not want to listen. He wanted to play in the sunshine with the toys. Fluffy was sad and did not know what to do. Then, Fluffy had an idea. She told Spot that they could play with the toys in the sunshine, but they had to organize them first. Spot agreed, and they worked together. They organized the toys and then played in the sunshine. Fluffy and Spot were happy, and they learned to share and help each other.
