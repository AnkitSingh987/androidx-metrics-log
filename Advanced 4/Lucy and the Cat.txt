Title: Lucy and the Cat

Once upon a time, a girl named Lucy loved to care for her mom's jewelry. She liked the shiny, pretty things. One day, she saw a fierce-looking cat outside her house. The cat had big, scary eyes. Lucy wanted to be friends with the cat, so she showed it the jewelry. "Look, cat, shiny things!" Lucy said. The cat looked at the jewelry and wanted it. The cat tried to take the jewelry from Lucy, but Lucy said, "No, cat! This is not for you!" The cat did not like that. It got mad and took the jewelry from Lucy's hand. Lucy cried and tried to get the jewelry back, but the fierce cat ran away with it. Lucy's mom came out and saw what happened. She was sad too. Lucy and her mom looked for the cat and the jewelry, but they could not find them. The cat had taken the shiny things far away. The end.

Once upon a time, deep in the forest there was a fox. The fox was very flexible; he could squeeze through the tightest openings. Every day the fox would go out and explore the forest. One day, he saw a lake deep in the forest and he decided to dive in! He waded into the lake and pushed off of the sandy banks. As he swam further and further into the lake, the fox felt a tug on his leg! The fox tried to swim away but the tug only got stronger. Just a few moments later, the fox realized that there was a big and friendly fish who wanted to play! The fox was so happy and had never had so much fun swimming in the lake! From that day forward, the fox would come back to the lake again and again to dive and play with the friendly fish.

Once upon a time, there was a big jar. The jar was in a store. It was the only jar available. A little boy named Tim saw the jar. He wanted to buy it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have this jar, please?" His mom said, "We will come back tomorrow. If it is still here, you can have it." Tim was sad but he knew he had to wait. The next day, Tim and his mom went back to the store. The jar was still there! Tim was so happy. He bought the jar and took it home. He knew that waiting was worth it.

Once upon a time, there was a kind veterinarian named Lily. She loved to collect toys for her animal friends. Lily's animal friends were very happy with her. One hot day, Lily went to the park to collect more toys. She saw a big box full of toys. Lily was very excited and took the box to her animal friends. The animal friends were so happy with the new toys. They played all day long in the hot sun. Lily was glad she could make her animal friends happy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a telephone. She did not know how to use it. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "What do I do with this?" Tom said, "I recommend you call your mom. She will know how to use it." Lucy tried to call her mom, but she was not available. So, she called her dad. Her dad told her, "You can use the telephone to talk to people who are far away." Lucy was happy to learn about the telephone. She used it to talk to her grandma and grandpa who lived far away. They were very happy to hear her voice.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they found a big tunnel in the park. The tunnel was long and dark. Tim was scared, but he wanted to join his friends and go inside. Tim took a deep breath and went into the tunnel. It was dark, but he could see a little bit of light at the end. His friends were waiting for him there. They all held hands and walked through the tunnel together. They found a fragile, pretty butterfly at the end of the tunnel. Tim was happy that he joined his friends in the tunnel. They all played with the butterfly and let it go. Then, they went back through the tunnel and played in the park until it was time to go home.
