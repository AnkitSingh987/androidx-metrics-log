Title: Sue the Girl and the Cookie

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted a cookie. She went to her mom and asked, "Can I borrow a cookie, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can have one cookie, but you must do something useful first." Sue thought for a moment and decided to clean her room. She put away her toys and made her bed. When she was done, she went back to her mom and said, "I did something useful. I cleaned my room. Can I have a cookie now?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have a cookie." But when they went to the kitchen, they saw that the cookie jar was empty! Sue was sad, but then she heard a noise. It was their dog, Max, with a cookie in his mouth. Max had taken the last cookie when no one was looking. Sue laughed and said, "Silly Max, you can have that cookie. I will wait for more cookies later."

One day, a little girl named Sue and her friend Tim went to play in the park. They saw a swing and ran to it. Sue got on the swing first. She swung high and laughed. "Look at me, Tim!" she said. "I am flying!" Then it was Tim's turn to swing. As he swung, he saw a closet near the park. It was old and looked hurt. Tim and Sue went to look at the closet. They opened it and saw lots of old toys inside. They took the toys out and started to play with them. Suddenly, a grumpy old man came out of his house. He saw the kids playing with the toys from the closet. "Those toys are mine!" he yelled. Sue and Tim got scared. They did not want to make the old man mad. They put the toys back in the closet and ran back to the park to play on the swings.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Barky. Barky lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. They were best friends and played together all day. One day, Tim was not feeling well. His mom used a thermometer to see if he had a fever. She put the thermometer on the table and went to make some soup for Tim. Barky was curious about the thermometer and started to play with it. Suddenly, Barky accidentally destroyed the thermometer! He felt bad and didn't know what to do. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, shiny new thermometer appeared out of nowhere! Barky was so happy and gave it to Tim's mom. She was surprised but grateful. In the end, Tim got better and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like big lions, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Tim was very happy to see all the animals. In one part of the zoo, they found an enormous door with the word "exit" on it. Tim was curious about what was behind the door. He tried to peek through a small hole in the door, but he could not see anything. Tim's mom saw him trying to peek and told him, "This door is the exit. It is where we go when we want to leave the zoo." Tim nodded and they continued to see more animals. At the end of the day, they went through the enormous exit door and went back home. Tim had a great day at the zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big fireplace in her house. Amy loved to sit by the fireplace and play with her toys. One day, her mommy told her, "Amy, don't go too close to the fireplace. It can be hot and not safe." Amy said, "Okay, mommy." One cold day, Amy was playing by the fireplace. She felt worried because her mommy was not home. She remembered what her mommy said about the fireplace. But Amy's toy rolled close to the fireplace. She wanted her toy back, so she went close to the fireplace to get it. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the fire out of the fireplace. The fire went onto the floor and started to get bigger. Amy was scared and didn't know what to do. She tried to put the fire out, but it only got bigger. In the end, the fire burned down Amy's house. Amy was very sad and wished she had listened to her mommy.
