Title: Mommy and the Flowers

When Mommy came home with little Susie, Susie was so excited. Mommy had a surprise. She opened her hand and inside was a bee! Susie was afraid at first, but Mommy explained that the bee was friendly and would not hurt her. Susie was curious. She asked the bee what his name was. The bee replied "Buzz!" Susie giggled and began to stroke the bee's rough wings. She thanked Mommy for showing her the bee and asked if she could keep it. Mommy laughed and explained that bees like to live outside. They fly around looking for flowers and drinking the sweet nectar. Susie loved to hear about the bee's adventures. She hugged Mommy and said goodbye to Buzz. Mommy said she would come and visit Buzz every day and Susie could come too. Susie smiled and ran outside to watch Buzz fly away. She waved and shouted "Goodbye, Buzz!"

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the grocery store with her mom. They needed to buy food for dinner. Lily felt comfortable in the store because she had been there many times before. Her mom said, "Lily, can you help me find the apples?" Lily nodded and went to look for the apples. As Lily looked for the apples, she saw a big box on the floor. She thought, "I need to move this box to get to the apples." She tried to push the box, but it was too heavy. She did not know how she would manage to move it. Then, she had an idea. She ran to find her mom and said, "Mom, can you help me move the big box?" Her mom came over and helped Lily move the box. Together, they managed to push it out of the way. There, behind the box, were the apples they needed. Lily and her mom picked the best apples and put them in their cart. They finished their shopping and went home to make a delicious dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little family with two parents and a three year old. One day, they went to the open oasis. It was filled with fun things, like animals, plants and cool toys. The family had the best time! The little one said, “I want to visit the oasis again soon.” And so they did, every week. Until one day, the oasis seemed…different. It was eerily quiet, and there was nobody else around. The little one said, “It's like the oasis is waiting for something.” The parents instantly knew that something was wrong, but it was too late. A wicked monster appeared and attacked them! The family ran, but the monster was too fast. The bad thing chased them until they reached the edge of the oasis. But there was no way out – they were trapped. The family was sad, and the little one cried, “I don't want to visit the oasis anymore.” Sadly, the oasis was their final resting place. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She was 3 years old and she wanted to make new friends. One day, her mommy told her she could go to the beach to find an oyster. Emma was really excited! She wanted to find the biggest and best oyster ever. So she put on her swimming costume and ran to the beach. She looked everywhere, but she just couldn't find an oyster. Emma began to feel frustrated. She sat down on the beach and asked her mommy what to do. Her mommy said, "Oh don't worry, Emma! Just sign in the sand and the oyster will come to you." Emma had no idea what her mommy meant but she decided to give it a try. She put her hands together and carefully made an 'O' shape in the sand. Suddenly, an oyster appeared in the sand in front of her! It was huge and made the most beautiful sound. Emma was so happy! She had made a new friend! She thanked her mommy and had the best day at the beach with her special oyster.

Anna and Mia are best friends. They like to play together in the park. One day, they find a shiny bracelet on the grass. It has many colors and makes a nice sound when it moves. "Wow, look at this!" Anna says. She picks up the bracelet and puts it on her wrist. "It is so pretty!" "Can I touch it?" Mia asks. She reaches out her hand to feel the bracelet. "Sure, you can touch it. But be careful, it is not ours. Maybe someone lost it." Anna says. She lets Mia touch the bracelet gently. The bracelet feels smooth and warm. Mia likes how it sparkles in the sun. She smiles at Anna and says, "It is very nice. I wish we could keep it." Anna smiles back and says, "Me too. But maybe someone is looking for it. Maybe it is very important to them. Maybe it makes them happy and peaceful." Mia thinks for a moment. She says, "You are right. We should not keep it. We should give it back. Maybe we can find the owner. Maybe they will be happy and peaceful too." Anna nods and says, "That is a good idea. Let's go and ask around. Maybe someone knows who it belongs to." They take off the bracelet and hold it in their hands. They walk around the park and ask people if they know the bracelet. They hope to find the owner and make them happy and peaceful. They are good friends and good helpers.
