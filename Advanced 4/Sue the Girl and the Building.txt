Title: Sue the Girl and the Building

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Sue. Sue liked to go on walks with her mom. One day, they went for a walk and saw a big building. Sue had never seen this building before. Sue asked her mom, "What is that big building?" Her mom said, "That is a school. I want to introduce you to the teacher there." Sue was excited to meet the teacher. They went inside the building and met the teacher. The teacher was nice and said, "Hi Sue, I am Miss Jane. I am happy to meet you." Sue smiled and said, "I am happy too." From that day on, Sue loved going to the big building to learn and play with her new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty bird named Bella. Bella loved to fly and play all day. One day, she saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was holding a big ice cream cone in her hand. The ice cream cone had many colors, and it looked yummy. Bella flew down and said, "Hi, Amy! Can I carry your ice cream cone for you?" Amy looked at Bella and smiled. "Yes, Bella, you can help me carry my cone. Thank you!" Bella was happy to help her new friend. Together, they walked and talked, carrying the ice cream cone. Bella used her beak to hold one side of the cone, while Amy held the other side. They laughed and shared the yummy ice cream. From that day on, Bella and Amy were the best of friends, and they always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a small family. The family had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lucy. They also had a cat named Whiskers. Lucy liked to play hide and seek with Whiskers. They would hide all over the house, but Lucy's favorite place to hide was the attic. One day, Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, we have a surprise for you!" Lucy was very excited. She knew her mom and dad were generous and always gave her nice things. Lucy wondered what the surprise could be. Later that day, Lucy's mom called her and said, "Come find me, Lucy!" Lucy knew they were playing hide and seek. She looked everywhere, but she could not find her mom. Then, she remembered the attic. She climbed the stairs and found her mom hiding there. Her mom said, "Surprise! I got you a new toy!" Lucy was so happy and thanked her mom for the generous gift. From that day on, Lucy and Whiskers played with the new toy in the attic, and it became their favorite hiding spot.

Jenna loved to learn new things. Every day Jenna would ask her mom to explain a new word or how an object worked. This time, Jenna asked her mom to explain the weather. "The weather is all the things you feel outside. It can be cold, hot, sunny, or rainy," said mom. Jenna was eager to learn more about the weather so she asked her brothers to help her. They both got competitive and tried to explain it in the most interesting way. "Some days it's warm and there's not a cloud in the sky. Other days it's cold and the sky is full of clouds," said her older brother. "Sometimes it rains so much that the streets are wet and you can jump in puddles!" said her little brother. Jenna loved learning about the weather from her brothers, and couldn't wait to tell her mom all they taught her!

Once upon a time, there was a girl. She liked to play every day. One day, she went outside and saw a big, heavy rug. She wanted to catch it, so she could play with it. She ran over to the rug and grabbed it with her hands. She was so happy that she had caught the rug, and she started to play with it. She had so much fun, running and jumping around on it. Then, she decided she wanted to try to lift it. She grabbed the rug with both of her hands, and tried to lift it up. It was so heavy, she almost couldn't lift it. But she kept trying, and eventually, she was able to lift it up and down. The girl was so proud of herself for being able to catch the rug and lift it up. She kept playing with it until it was time for her to go inside for dinner. Now she always remembers the fun time she had catching the heavy rug.
