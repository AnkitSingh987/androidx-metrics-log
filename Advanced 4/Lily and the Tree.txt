Title: Lily and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with a big trunk. A little girl named Lily liked to play near the tree. One day, Lily decided to pretend to be a princess. She put on a pretty dress and a crown. Lily said, "I am a princess, and this tree is my castle!" She played and danced around the tree. Then, a little boy named Tom came to play with her. He pretended to be a knight. Tom said, "I will protect you, Princess Lily!" Suddenly, the big trunk opened up, and out came a big monster! The monster was not happy that Lily and Tom were playing near his tree. He roared and scared them away. Lily and Tom ran away as fast as they could. The monster went back into the trunk, and the tree was quiet again. Lily and Tom never played near the big tree again. They learned to be careful when pretending near trees with big trunks.

Once upon a time, there was a princess who was very worried. She lived in a beautiful palace, far away in the mountains. She had lost her favourite bracelet and wasn't sure where to look. The princess went outside and searched everywhere, but no matter what she couldn't find the bracelet. Just then, something shiny caught her eye, and she saw the bracelet stuck in a tree. The princess was so happy that she found it! But the bracelet was so far away. She took out her knife, which was very sharp, and carefully cut the bracelet out of the tree. The Princess put the bracelet back on her wrist and smiled. She was so relieved that she could wear it again. She thanked the tree, and then ran back to the palace, happy to be reunited with her lost treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a very independent little girl called Millie. One morning Millie wanted to go for a walk in the park. But as she walked outside there was a strange mist everywhere. She tried to walk through it but it was too thick! Millie was very confused and didn’t know how to solve the problem. Suddenly, she heard a voice coming from the mist. It said, “Millie, don’t worry, there is a way to solve this!” Millie was startled, but she was also very excited. She asked, “Who are you?” But the mysterious voice didn’t answer. It said, “Look around you and use your independence. See if you can solve this!” Millie looked around and saw that the mist was stretching all around her. Then she remembered that she always had a flashlight by her side. She switched it on and shone it ahead of her. The mist started to clear and Millie was able to find her own way to the park! Millie was proud of herself and realized she didn’t need anyone else to solve the mist. With a big smile, she set off on her walk feeling very independent.

One day, two friends, Cat and Dog, were playing in the park. They found a big stick. Cat said, "This stick is a weapon!" Dog said, "No, it's not. It's just a stick." They started to discuss who was right. While they were talking, a bossy bird came and took the stick. "Hey, give it back!" said Cat. "That's our stick!" said Dog. The bossy bird flew away with the stick, and Cat and Dog were sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The bossy bird came back with the stick and said, "I'm sorry. I should not have taken your stick." Cat and Dog were surprised. They all became friends and played together in the park.

Once upon a time there was a jolly sack. The sack liked to go for walks and explore the world. One day the sack was very excited and went for a long walk. Suddenly, there was a big shock! The sack was scared, but soon realised that it was just a gust of wind. It laughed and continued walking. Soon, the sack came to a jolly field. The flowers were bright and there were birds singing in the trees. The sack felt so happy and decided to run around in circles. As it ran, it felt the wind whizzing past and the sun on its back. The sack had the best day ever and decided to go home. As it walked back, it sang a jolly song and was happy to be on the go again.
