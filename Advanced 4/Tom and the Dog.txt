Title: Tom and the Dog

One sunny day, Tom and his big sister, Sue, went to play outside. They took their red wagon with them. They wanted to fill the wagon with toys and take them to the park. As they walked, they saw an enormous dog. The dog was friendly and wagged its tail. Tom was scared and began to panic. "Sue, I'm scared of the big dog!" he said. Sue smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tom. The dog is nice. Let's give him a toy from our wagon." They gave the dog a ball and he was happy. Tom stopped panicking and they all played together at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small pond. Many animals lived near the pond. They were all friends. One day, a new animal came to the pond. He was a tough turtle. The turtle was very shy and scared. The other animals saw the turtle and wanted to help. They knew he needed some comfort. So, they went to the turtle and said, "Don't be scared, we are your friends." The turtle started to feel better. He was not so shy anymore. They all played together by the pond. The turtle was not tough anymore. He was happy and friendly. The animals were glad they could comfort their new friend. They all lived happily together near the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate helicopter named Heli. Heli loved to help others. One day, Heli heard a cry from a lost little cat named Kitty. Heli went to search for Kitty. Heli looked up and down, left and right. The sun was hot, but Heli did not give up. Heli knew Kitty needed help. At last, Heli found Kitty in a tall tree. Heli flew close and Kitty jumped in. Heli took Kitty back home. They were both very happy. Heli and Kitty became best friends.

Anna and Ben are playing in their room. They have a lot of toys, but they want to do something new. They see Mom's scissors on the table. They know scissors are for cutting, but they are not sure what to cut. "Let's cut some paper," Anna whispers to Ben. She thinks whispering is fun and makes them look serious. Ben nods and whispers back, "OK, but we have to be careful. Mom says scissors are sharp and can hurt us." They take the scissors and some paper and go to the corner of the room. They start to cut the paper into different shapes. They make a star, a heart, a circle, and a square. They are having fun and forget to whisper. "Look, I made a flower!" Anna says loudly. She holds up her paper flower and smiles. "Shh, you are too loud!" Ben says. He hears Mom coming up the stairs. He is afraid Mom will be angry and take away the scissors. He quickly hides the scissors under a pillow and the paper shapes under a blanket. He hopes Mom will not see them. Mom opens the door and sees Anna and Ben sitting on the floor. She smiles and says, "What are you two doing?" "Nothing, Mom," Anna and Ben say together. They try to look innocent, but they are not very good at it. Mom looks around the room and sees some paper bits on the carpet. She lifts the pillow and the blanket and finds the scissors and the paper shapes. She is not angry, but she is serious. She says, "Anna and Ben, you know you are not supposed to play with scissors. They are dangerous and can hurt you or the furniture. You could have asked me for some safe scissors and some paper. I would have been happy to help you." She takes the scissors and the paper and puts them away. She gives Anna and Ben a hug and says, "I love you, but you have to be careful and listen to me. Next time, please ask me before you use something that is not yours." Anna and Ben feel sorry and say, "We are sorry, Mom. We will ask you next time. We love you too." Mom smiles and says, "It's OK. I forgive you. But now you have to help me clean up the mess." Anna and Ben nod and say, "OK, Mom." They help Mom pick up the paper bits and put them in the trash. They learn their lesson and never play with scissors again.
