Title: Lily the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a good girl named Lily. She loved her notebook very much. She drew pretty pictures in it every day. Lily always kept her notebook safe and close to her. One sunny day, Lily went to the park with her notebook. She sat under a big tree and drew a nice flower. Suddenly, a big wind came and took her notebook away! Lily was very sad and scared. She wanted to protect her notebook from the wind. Lily ran after the wind to get her notebook back. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind bird flew down and caught the notebook. The bird gave it back to Lily. She was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Lily and the bird became best friends, and they drew in the notebook together.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day in the cool water. He had many friends who liked to swim with him. They would play games and chase each other in the water. One day, while Fin and his friends were playing, they saw a big ball floating on the water. They wanted to play with the ball, so they took turns to strike it with their tails. The ball would bounce and spin, making them all laugh and have fun. As the sun began to set, Fin and his friends decided to swim back home. They said goodbye and promised to play again the next day. Fin went to sleep, dreaming of the cool water and the fun games he would play with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He found a shiny penny on the ground. Tim was so happy! He wanted to buy a candy with the penny. Tim went to the candy store with his mom. He saw a big candy he wanted. He showed his penny to the store man. The store man said, "Sorry, Tim. The candy costs more than one penny." Tim felt regret because he wanted the candy so bad. Tim's mom saw him feeling sad. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. Let's keep the penny safe in your piggy bank. One day, you'll have enough pennies to buy a big candy." Tim smiled and agreed. He learned that saving pennies can be a good thing too.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big helicopter in the sky. It was very loud and he was very excited. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, look! A helicopter!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, that is a helicopter." Later, Tim and his mom went to the park. There, they saw a man with a fake helicopter toy. The man said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with my fake helicopter?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" He played with the fake helicopter and had a lot of fun. After playing, Tim and his mom went home. Tim was hungry, so his mom gave him a cookie. Tim took a bite and said, "Mom, this cookie tastes like a helicopter!" His mom laughed and said, "Silly Tim, cookies don't taste like helicopters!" But Tim was sure that the cookie tasted like the helicopter he saw in the sky earlier. It was a very unexpected day for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Sue loved to play with her toys and run around in the grass. One day, Sue saw a disgusting bug on a cloth outside. The bug was big and yucky. Sue wanted to make the bug go away. She asked her mommy for help. Her mommy told her to let the bug rise up and fly away. Sue listened to her mommy and watched the disgusting bug. The bug started to rise up and fly away from the cloth. Sue was happy that the bug was gone. She went back to playing with her toys and running in the grass. Sue knew that if she saw another disgusting bug, she could just let it rise up and fly away.
