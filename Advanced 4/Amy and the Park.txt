Title: Amy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy wanted to be independent, like her big sister. One day, she asked her mom, "Can I go to the park alone?" Her mom said, "No, you are too little." Amy was sad but she knew her mom was right. Amy had an idea. She went to her room and made a network of strings. She tied the strings to her toys and pulled them along. She thought, "If I show my mom that I can be independent with my toys, maybe she will allow me to go to the park alone." Amy showed her mom the network of strings and toys. Her mom smiled and said, "You are very clever, but you are still too little to go to the park alone." Amy was sad again. Then, her big sister came in and said, "Why don't we go to the park together?" Amy smiled and said, "Yes, let's go!" They went to the park and had a great time. Amy learned that being independent doesn't mean she has to do everything alone. Sometimes, it's more fun to do things with others. And maybe one day, when she's bigger, her mom will allow her to go to the park alone.

Once upon a time, there was a bear who lived in a big forest. He was always very careful, especially around the cactus. One day, he was walking when he heard a voice calling him. He looked around but he couldn't see anyone. Then, he heard it again and this time he was sure it was coming from the cactus. The bear was very surprised and a little scared. He asked the cactus why it was calling him. The cactus replied, "I saw you walking in a foolish way and I wanted to help you." The bear was shocked and he said, "What do you mean? I am walking the same way I always do." The cactus replied, "Yes, but there are some things you should be aware of in the forest and I wanted to warn you." The bear thanked the cactus for being so generous and wise. He vowed to be more careful from then on and promised to never be foolish again. After that day, the bear and the cactus became friends and always looked out for each other.

Once upon a time, there was a deer. The deer liked to bake. One day, the deer wanted to bake a cake. He went to the kitchen to get all the things he needed. As the deer began to bake, he made a lot of noise. The pots and pans were very noisy. The deer did not care. He was having fun making the cake. When the cake was done, the deer shared it with his friends. They all loved the cake. They said the noisy deer was the best baker they knew. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Wink. Wink loved to jump all day long. He would jump high and low, near and far. One day, Wink saw a big red ball. He was confused. He had never seen a ball before. Wink decided to jump on the ball. He jumped and jumped, but the ball kept moving away. Wink did not give up. He jumped higher and higher until he finally landed on the ball. The ball started to roll with Wink on top. Wink laughed and laughed as he rolled on the ball. It was so much fun! After a while, the ball stopped rolling. Wink jumped off the ball and went back home. He told all his friends about his fun day with the big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was very obedient. He listened to his parents and always did what he was asked. One day, his parents told him to stay in one place. That's when the little boy froze and he didn't move an inch! The little boy stayed there for a really long time. He was so obedient that he just stayed there, waiting for the moment when his mom and dad said he could move. Finally, after what seemed like forever, his parents said he could go. The little boy smiled and finally unfroze. He was happy that he had been obedient and that he could now move. The end.
