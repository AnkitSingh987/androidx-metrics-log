Title: Mary the Girl and the Painting

Once, there was a little girl named Mary. Mary was a very intelligent little girl. She loved to paint pictures. One day, she decided to paint a cactus. She grabbed her crayons and paper and began to paint. First, she painted the cactus green, then orange and yellow for the flowers. Finally, she painted the cactus a little pink for fun. When she was finished, she was so pleased with her painting. She showed it to her mom and her mom said it was beautiful. She even put it on the wall for everyone to see. Mary was so proud of her painting and she was also very happy because she had painted such an intelligent and beautiful picture.

Tim and Mia are twins. They like to play with their toys in the yard. Tim has a red motorcycle that makes noise. Mia has a blue bucket and a shovel. She likes to fill the bucket with sand and make castles. One day, Tim wants to play with Mia's bucket. He says, "Mia, can I have your bucket? I want to fill it with water and wash my motorcycle." Mia says, "No, Tim. This is my bucket. You have your motorcycle. Go play with it." Tim gets angry. He says, "But I want to fill the bucket. You are not nice. You are mean." Mia gets angry too. She says, "No, you are mean. You always want my things. Go away." Tim and Mia start to fight. They push and pull the bucket. The bucket falls and the sand spills. Tim and Mia are both frustrated. They cry and scream. Mom hears the noise. She comes to the yard. She sees Tim and Mia fighting. She says, "Stop, stop. What is going on? Why are you fighting?" Tim and Mia tell Mom what happened. Mom says, "Tim, Mia, you are twins. You should share and play nicely. You have many toys. You can take turns with the bucket and the motorcycle. Or you can play together and help each other. How about that?" Tim and Mia think for a moment. They say, "Okay, Mom. We are sorry. We will share and play nicely." Mom smiles. She says, "Good. I am glad. Now, how about we fill the bucket with water and wash the motorcycle together? And then we can make a big sand castle with the shovel. Does that sound fun?" Tim and Mia nod. They say, "Yes, Mom. That sounds fun." They hug Mom and each other. They take the bucket and the motorcycle and go to the faucet. They fill the bucket with water and wash the motorcycle. They make it shiny and clean. Then they go to the sand pit and make a big sand castle with the shovel. They decorate it with shells and stones. They have fun and laugh. They are not frustrated anymore. They are happy.

Stacy and her Mommy were on a bus ride. It was dry outside and everybody was comfortable in the bus. Stacy was looking at everyone and taking it all in. Mommy said, "We'll be there soon. Here's your ticket, Stacy." She handed her daughter a pink ticket and said, "We get to go to the fair when we get there. " Stacy was really excited, her eyes twinkling with anticipation. She said, "Will I get a balloon, Mommy?" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, sweetie, you'll get a balloon. Just keep your ticket safe." The bus ride was over soon and they got out. Stacy looked around excitedly and saw colorful balloons, rides, and much more. She was so happy that she hugged Mommy tight. Mommy said, "Remember what I said. Don't lose your ticket!" She laughed and said, "Let's have some fun!" The day was full of fun and laughter and Stacy will always remember it. She learned a valuable lesson about the importance of keeping her ticket safe.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw, paint, and make things with his hands. He had a job to do every day. His job was to warn his friends when it was time to go inside. One day, Tim was playing with his friends in the park. They were having lots of fun, running and laughing. Tim was busy making a big, pretty picture with sticks and leaves on the ground. Suddenly, Tim remembered his job. He looked up at the sky and saw that it was getting dark. He knew it was time to warn his friends. So he yelled, "Friends, it's time to go inside now!" His friends heard Tim and stopped playing. They all went inside their homes, safe and sound. Tim felt happy that he did his job well. He went inside too, ready to be creative again the next day.
