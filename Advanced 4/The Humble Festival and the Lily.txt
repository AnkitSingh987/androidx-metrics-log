Title: The Humble Festival and the Lily

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a humble festival. Many people came to see the fun things. They ate yummy food and played games. They were very happy. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the festival with her mom and dad. They saw a big tent where people could move and dance. Lily loved to move and dance, so she joined them. She moved her arms and legs and smiled. Lily and her family had a great day at the humble festival. They went home tired but happy. They couldn't wait to go back to the festival next year and move and dance again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Sue. She had a big secret. Sue found a clear, shiny stone in her yard. Sue liked to hold the stone because it made her feel happy. One day, Sue's friend, Tim, saw her hold the stone. Tim wanted to hold it too, but Sue did not want to share. They got mad at each other. Sue did not want to lose her secret, and Tim felt left out. Sue thought about it and decided to share her secret with Tim. She let him hold the clear, shiny stone. They both felt happy. Sue and Tim learned that sharing made them better friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little mouse. The mouse lived in a small house in the woods. He had many friends, like the big bear and the funny rabbit. They all played together and had lots of fun. One day, the mouse found a sack. He did not know what was inside. He called his friends to come and see. The big bear and the funny rabbit came running to look at the sack. They were all very excited. They opened the sack and found lots of yummy food inside. There were nuts, cheese, and fruits. The charming mouse, the big bear, and the funny rabbit had a big party. They ate all the yummy food and played games. They were all very happy and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy top that he loved to play with. One day, while playing, his top broke. Tim was very sad. Tim's mommy saw him crying and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim showed her the broken top and said, "My top is broken, Mommy. Can you fix it, please?" His mommy smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix your top." Mommy took the top and went to the deep box where she kept her tools. She found what she needed and fixed the top for Tim. When she was done, she gave the top back to Tim and said, "Here you go, Tim. Your top is all fixed!" Tim was so happy to have his top back. He played with it all day and thanked his mommy for fixing it. From that day on, Tim took extra care of his top and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big soft bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a house full of luxury. His house had many soft things to squeeze. Bobo liked to squeeze soft things all day. He was a silly bear. One day, Bobo found a new soft thing to squeeze. It was round and bouncy. Bobo was so happy. He squeezed the soft thing and it made a funny noise. Bobo laughed and squeezed it again. The soft thing was so silly. But then, something unexpected happened. The soft thing grew bigger and bigger! Bobo was surprised. He squeezed it one more time, and the soft thing popped! It was not a soft thing, but a big balloon. Bobo laughed and thought it was a funny twist. He went to find more silly things to squeeze.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat cookies. One day, she saw a cookie on the table. It was a big, round cookie with lots of chocolate chips. Amy was so happy! She wanted to eat the cookie right away. But then, Amy remembered her mommy told her to always say "please" and "thank you" when she wanted something. So, Amy decided to bow and say "please" before taking the cookie. She bowed her head and said, "Please, may I have the cookie?" Just then, her mommy walked in and saw Amy being very polite. Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, you may have the cookie, Amy." But mommy also warned her, "Be careful, because this cookie might be bad. It has been on the table for a long time." Amy took a small bite of the cookie, and it tasted very bad! She did not want to eat it anymore. Mommy saw this and gave Amy a new, yummy cookie instead. Amy was so happy and said, "Thank you, mommy!" From that day on, Amy always remembered to be polite and to check if something was bad before eating it.
