Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to eat carrots. One day, Remy found a very fragile carrot. It was so thin and weak. Remy didn't know if he should eat it or not. Remy asked his friend, Timmy the turtle, "Should I eat this fragile carrot?" Timmy didn't know what to say, so he just shrugged. "I don't know, Remy. Maybe it's still good to eat." Remy thought for a moment and then decided to try the fragile carrot. He took a small bite and was surprised. It tasted very good! Remy and Timmy both laughed and were happy that the fragile carrot was still yummy. From that day on, Remy learned that even fragile things can be good too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily wanted to be independent. She wanted to do things by herself, like a big girl. She had a toy mine, where she would play with her toy trucks and diggers. One day, Lily's mom told her that they were going to the store. Lily wanted to understand how to pick out her own things at the store. Her mom said, "Okay, Lily, you can pick out one toy." Lily was very happy. At the store, Lily looked at all the toys. She found a new toy truck for her mine. She picked it up and showed her mom. Her mom said, "Good job, Lily! You are becoming more independent!" Lily smiled, and they went home to play with the new truck at her mine.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, sunny day. In a small town, there was a mill by a river. A duck and a rabbit lived near the mill. They were good friends. One day, they decided to have a race to the mill. They wanted to see who was faster. The duck and the rabbit started the race. The duck swam in the river, and the rabbit hopped on the land. They both went very fast. All their friends watched and cheered. The duck and the rabbit were having so much fun. But, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew the rabbit into the river. The rabbit was scared and could not swim. The duck saw the rabbit in the water and stopped racing. The duck swam to the rabbit and helped him out of the river. They did not finish the race, but they were both happy. They learned that helping friends is more important than winning a race.

Once upon a time there was a strong bear who went to school every day. The bear asked his teacher many questions. He wanted to learn more and more. One day the bear asked his teacher what math was. She smiled and said math was very fun! The bear liked this answer so he requested to learn math. He was excited to get started. So his teacher taught him simple addition and subtraction. The bear practiced everyday and got stronger and stronger at math. Four weeks passed and the bear was so happy. He could now do math quickly and easily. He even taught his friends some of the math he had learnt! The strong bear was very proud and he thanked his teacher for helping him to learn.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to dance in the sun. One day, she met a big dog named Doggy. Doggy was a foolish dog who liked to play all day. "Hi Kitty," said Doggy, "Do you want to dance with me?" Kitty looked at Doggy and said, "Yes, I want to dance with you, Doggy." So, Kitty and Doggy danced together in the sun. They danced and played all day long. They were happy and had lots of fun. And from that day on, Kitty and Doggy became best friends who loved to dance together.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to help her mom and dad with chores. One day, the toilet in their house broke, and water spilled all over the floor. Lily's dad said, "Oh no, we need to repair the toilet!" Lily wanted to help her dad. She put on her boots and grabbed a towel. Together, they cleaned up the water and fixed the toilet. Lily's dad said, "Thank you, Lily. You are a big help!" When they were done, Lily's mom came in and saw the clean bathroom. She said, "Wow, the toilet looks pretty now! Good job, Lily and Dad!" They all smiled and hugged each other. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel good and bring happiness to your family.
