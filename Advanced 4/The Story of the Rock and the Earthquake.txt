Title: The Story of the Rock and the Earthquake

Once upon a time there was a small, lonely rock. Every day it rocked back and forth in the same spot. It had been rocked for so long it began to feel tired. One day, the rock felt a big rumbling in the ground below. Suddenly, it knew what was happening: an earthquake! The ground shook and his rock rocked harder than ever. The lonely rock was scared and waited to see if the earthquake would ever stop. But when the earthquake finally stopped, something strange happened. The rock wasn't lonely anymore! All of the other rocks had come together during the earthquake, and now the rock was surrounded by friends. It was so happy that it decided to rock with them every day. The end.

Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy arrow. One day, he went for a walk in the forest with his arrow. As Tim walked, he saw a big tree. He wanted to surprise his friend, the squirrel, who lived in the tree. So, he hid behind the tree and waited for the squirrel to come out. When the squirrel came out, Tim jumped out and shouted, "Surprise!" The squirrel was very happy to see Tim. They played together with the arrow. They took turns throwing the arrow and running to get it. They laughed and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Tim and the squirrel went back to their homes, excited to play again tomorrow.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim went to his bookcase. He wanted to find a good book to read. He looked at all the books on the bookcase. He saw a big red book that he had never seen before. Tim was very curious about the big red book. He pulled the big red book from the bookcase. When he did, something unexpected happened. The bookcase started to move! It moved away from the wall and revealed a secret door. Tim was very surprised. He had never seen a secret door before. Tim opened the secret door and found a room full of toys! He couldn't believe his eyes. He played with the toys all day and had so much fun. At the end of the day, he closed the secret door and put the big red book back on the bookcase. Tim was happy to find the secret room and knew that he could visit it any time he wanted.

One day, a little lizard was out in the sun. He liked to march up and down the path. His feet went tap, tap, tap. He was a happy lizard. But one day, a big hairy bug came. The bug was not nice. The bug wanted the path all to himself. He said, "Go away, little lizard. This is my path now." The little lizard was sad. The little lizard did not want to go away. He had an idea. He marched up to the big hairy bug and said, "If you let me stay, I will help you find food." The big hairy bug thought about it. Then he said, "Okay, we can share the path and be friends." So, the little lizard and the big hairy bug marched together, looking for food. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big field. In the field, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play and get dirty in the field. One day, Tom's mom told him, "Tom, you need to clean your hands before you eat." Tom felt lucky because he loved to clean his hands. The next day, Tom went to the field to play again. He saw a big box in the field. Tom thought, "I wonder what is inside the box." He opened the box and found a big, shiny toy car. Tom was so happy and played with the toy car all day. At the end of the day, Tom took the toy car home. His mom saw the car and said, "Tom, you are so lucky to find this toy car! But remember, you need to clean your hands before you eat." Tom cleaned his hands and ate dinner with his family. He was very happy and felt lucky to have a new toy car and a clean, happy home.
