Title: Dolly the Tidy Dolphin and the Fish

One day, a tidy dolphin named Dolly was swimming in the sea. She liked to keep her home clean and neat. Dolly was a very happy dolphin. Suddenly, a big mess appeared in the sea. There was trash everywhere! Dolly was sad and did not like the mess. She wanted her home to be tidy again. Dolly asked her friends, the fish, to help her clean the sea. They all worked together and picked up the trash. Soon, the sea was tidy again. Dolly and her friends were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a lion. This lion was very independent. He liked to do things on his own. He would walk and play alone in the forest. One day, while the lion was walking, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The lion asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I can't fly. I want to be like you, independent and strong." The lion smiled and said, "I will help you." He lifted the bird onto his back and began to run. As they ran, the bird felt the wind and started to flap its wings. Soon, the bird was flying! The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, lion! Now I can fly and be independent like you!" The lion and the bird became good friends. They played together in the forest and were always there to help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a brother named Matt and a sister named Jill. Matt was getting very sleepy and he wanted to take a nap. Jill said to him, "No way! I want to play with you first." Matt said, "I'm too sleepy. I need to nap." Jill replied, "I don't care! You should be playing." Matt started to get mad and he spread out some special gas. It made everyone in the room very sleepy. Jill said, "Hey! That's not fair!" Then Matt said, "Now it's fair. We can both be sleepy and take a nap." So Matt and Jill took a nap together. It was a good solution for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely bulb. It lived in a big garden with many flowers. The bulb wanted to smile and play with the flowers, but it did not know how. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw the lonely bulb and said, "Why are you so sad, little bulb?" The bulb replied, "I want to smile and play, but I don't know how." Lily smiled and said, "I will help you." Lily watered the bulb every day. One day, something unexpected happened. The bulb grew into a beautiful, smiling flower! It was no longer lonely and could play with the other flowers. The bulb and Lily were both very happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to visit her grandma. When she got there, she saw her grandma's big, empty armchair. She liked to sit in it because it was so soft and cozy. Amy asked her grandma, "Can I sit in the empty armchair?" Her grandma said, "Yes, you can. But first, let's go to the kitchen and get some cookies." So they went to the kitchen and ate some yummy cookies. After they were done, Amy ran back to the living room to sit in the empty armchair. But when she got there, she saw a big, fluffy cat sitting in the armchair! Amy was surprised and said, "Oh no, the armchair is not empty anymore!" The cat looked at her and meowed. Grandma came in and said, "This is our new cat, Fluffy. You can sit with him in the armchair." Amy smiled, climbed up into the armchair, and cuddled with Fluffy. They became best friends and had a fun day together.

One day, a little jellyfish went for a swim. The sky was gloomy and the water was cold. The jellyfish felt sad and wanted to find a friend. As the jellyfish swam, it saw a big fish. The big fish was taking a nap. The jellyfish wanted to say hi, but it did not want to wake up the big fish. So, the jellyfish swam away to find another friend. Soon, the jellyfish found a small crab. The crab was not taking a nap and was happy to play with the jellyfish. They played all day and became good friends. The gloomy day turned into a fun day for the little jellyfish.
