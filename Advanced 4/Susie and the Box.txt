Title: Susie and the Box

Little Susie went to the forest one day. She found a perfect little lizard and wanted to pack it up and take it home. But the lizard was too fast for Susie. She tried to grab it with her hands but it was too slippery. Susie had an idea. She found a perfect little box and put some grass inside. She waited for the lizard to come close and then quickly put the box over it. The box was just the right size! Susie smiled as she picked up the box. She held it close to her chest and started walking back home. It was the perfect day and Susie had made the perfect friend. She couldn't wait to take the little lizard out and show him around the house.

Once upon a time, there was a navy boat. The boat was very busy. It went up and down on the water all day. One day, the boat had to decide where to go. It could go left or it could go right. The boat thought and thought. The boat decided to go right. But it was a bad choice. The water was too rough. The busy navy boat tipped over and sank. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that could display fun things. Every day, he would play with his toy for many minutes. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue thought Tim's toy was boring. She said, "Your toy is boring, Tim. Let's play with my toy instead." Tim was sad, but he wanted to make a new friend. So, he agreed to play with Sue's toy. They played together for many minutes. Tim found out that Sue's toy was not boring. It was fun! Tim and Sue became good friends. They played with their toys every day and had lots of fun together.

Henry had a dream. He dreamed that he was getting married to a dinosaur! He was so excited and even laughed out loud when he saw that the dinosaur had a big smile. Henry was happy, he imagined himself wearing a very fancy suit and the dinosaur was wearing a pretty dress. The next day, when Henry's mom asked him about his dream, he told her all about it. His mom laughed with him and said; "Henry, that's not a real thing! Dinosaurs are extinct, you can't marry one!" But Henry was still determined to make his dream come true. He asked his mom to take him to the fair, so he could find a dinosaur. His mom said OK, she wanted to make Henry's dream fair. So they went to the fair together, but they didn't find any dinosaur there. Henry was sad but his mom reassured him; "Don't worry Henry, I'm sure you will find someone special one day." Henry smiled and said; "Yeah, maybe a dinosaur will come to my wedding day one day!"

Once upon a time, there was a little bug who was very hungry. He lived in a big park with many trees. One day, he saw a bright light far away. The bright light made him think of yummy food. So, he decided to follow the light to find something to eat. The little bug flew and flew towards the light. He saw that the light was coming from a big place called a restaurant. The restaurant had a big sign that said "Bug's Food". The little bug was so excited that he started to glow. He went inside the restaurant and found a big plate of food just for him. The little bug ate and ate until he was not hungry anymore. He was so happy that he glowed even more. The other bugs saw his glow and came to the restaurant too. They all ate and were happy. The little bug had found the perfect place for all the hungry bugs to eat.

Tommy loved to explore the park. One day, he found a beautiful colorful hive in the trees. He went closer and heard something whispering. He was so scared but he walked closer and closer until he saw a ladybug. "Hello," she whispered. "I am the queen of this hive and I need your help. Would you please collect all of the colorful flowers around the hive?" Tommy was so excited. He agreed and quickly ran around the park to find flowers for the hive. He collected the most beautiful and colorful flowers he could find and gave them to the queen. She was so happy and thanked Tommy. She whispered to him that he was a very special insect and would always be welcomed in her hive. Tommy smiled and waved goodbye to the queen. From that day on, he visited the hive and the ladybug every day and they became the best of friends!
