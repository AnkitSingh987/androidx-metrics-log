Title: Tim the Boy and the Boat

One day, a boy named Tim went to sail his toy boat. He took the boat to the pond. The sun was shining and the water was soft. Tim was very happy. As Tim played, a man came by with a big steam train. The man said, "Hello Tim! Do you want to see my steam train go fast?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" The man made the steam train go very fast. It went around and around the pond. Tim clapped and laughed. But then, something bad happened. The steam train went too fast and fell into the pond! The water splashed everywhere. Tim's toy boat sailed away and he could not find it. Tim was very sad. The man said, "I am sorry, Tim." Tim went home without his boat.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big, brown temple. It was the most beautiful temple in the village. People loved to visit the temple every day. They would go inside to pray and sing songs. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the temple with his mom. They saw a man building a new part of the temple. Tim asked the man, "What are you doing?" The man said, "I am building a new room for the temple." Tim was excited and wanted to help. He picked up small brown stones and gave them to the man. The man smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim." Together, they built the new room for the temple. When they finished, Tim and his mom went inside the temple. They prayed and sang songs with other people from the village. Everyone was happy to see the new room in the brown temple. From that day on, Tim loved to visit the temple even more. He would always remember how he helped build the new room with the kind man.

Once upon a time there was a small boy named Richard. He was only 3 years old and very curious about the world around him. One day, he noticed a branch sticking out of the ground near his house. He wondered what it was and asked his father, "What is that branch doing there?" His father replied, "It is a branch that supplies us with electricity". Richard was very ignorant about electricity but he was eager to learn more. "Can I see it?" he asked. His father smiled and said, "Yes, of course. Let's go take a look." They went outside together and his father showed him how the branch was connected to the electricity meter. Richard was amazed! He was no longer ignorant and now he knew all about the branch's purpose.

One day, a girl named Sally went to the park to ride her bike. She wore her favorite bracelet that her mom gave her. It was very pretty and she felt comfortable when she wore it. Sally loved to ride her bike fast, but today she saw a boy named Tim who was sad. "Hi Tim, why are you sad?" Sally asked. Tim said, "I lost my toy car and I can't find it." Sally wanted to help Tim. They looked for the toy car together, but it was not easy to find. Sally and Tim felt sad. Finally, Sally had an idea. "Let's ride our bikes and look for the toy car," she said. They rode all around the park until they found the toy car under a tree. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Sally!" They became good friends and rode their bikes together every day.

Once upon a time there was a Mum, Dad and Baby. They were eager to go on a holiday, but didn't know where to start. Dad said, let's guess! They thought and guessed, and finally decided to go to the beach. So, they packed their bags and off they went. On the way, Baby was so excited to get to the beach! When they finally arrived, Baby saw the white sand and blue sea and thought it looked amazing. But the beach wasn't just sand and sea! Baby found some really interesting rocks and shells. She was so eager to pick one up and take it home. So she did. When they got back home, Dad had a surprise. He showed Baby what he had found in the sand - it was a beautiful glass fish! Baby was surprised and Dad said, 'What did I always say? Always guess - you never know what you might find!'
