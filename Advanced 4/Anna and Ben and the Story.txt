Title: Anna and Ben and the Story

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Anna and Ben. One day they decided they wanted to get married. They asked their family and friends to join them on a big day, and everyone said "welcome!" Anna and Ben put on their big, fancy clothes and took a large carriage to the marriage place. Everyone was so happy for them. Then a storm came. It was so dark and scary that everyone had to find a safe place. But Ben and Anna decided to stay together, even in the rain. Their family and friends were so touched and said, "Welcome the power of love, it is bigger than a big storm!" The storm passed, and everyone celebrated the marriage. Anna and Ben were happy that their love was so strong, despite the storm. The moral of this story is that love is stronger than anything and it is important to welcome it into our lives.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, she found a mop in the kitchen. Lily wanted to help her mom, so she started to mop the floor. While she was mopping, Lily accidentally spilled water all over the floor. Her mom saw the mess and was upset. She said, "Lily, you made a big mess! Please be careful next time." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I just wanted to help." Her mom looked at her and said, "I forgive you, Lily. I know you were trying to help. Let's clean up the mess together." So, Lily and her mom cleaned up the water and the floor was nice and clean again.

Once upon a time, a strong boy named Tim lived in a small house. He loved to play outside all day. One day, he saw a big ball stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to find a way to get the ball down. Tim had an idea. He would grab a long stick to reach the ball. He looked around and found the perfect stick. With a big smile, Tim held the stick with his strong hands. He used the stick to poke the ball. It took a few tries, but at last, the ball fell down. Tim was so happy he could play with the ball again. He knew that with his strong hands and clever way, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a fine bird named Bobo. Bobo had a best friend, a strong tree named Toto. Toto would always support Bobo and let him rest on his branches. They liked to talk and play every day. One day, Bobo saw an arrow on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and showed it to Toto. "Look, Toto! I found an arrow!" said Bobo. Toto was happy for his friend. "That's nice, Bobo! Be careful with it," said Toto. Bobo and Toto decided to play a game with the arrow. Bobo would throw the arrow, and Toto would catch it with his branches. They had a lot of fun playing together. At the end of the day, Bobo and Toto were very happy. They knew they would always support each other and be the best of friends.

One day, two friends named Jess and Tommy decided to have an adventure. Tommy found an old flag and asked Jess what they should do with it. Jess said they should prepare a plan. She got an old bucket and filled it with water. The flag was dry, so they agreed it needed to get wet. The two went to the park, where they flew the flag up high. Suddenly, dark storm clouds rolled in and Jess noticed a flash of lightning in the distance. She warned Tommy that they needed to get out of there, but Tommy didn't want to leave. Jess begged Tommy to listen, but he was too stubborn. The rain started falling and the wind was blowing. Jess was worried the flag would be ruined. Tommy suggested they try and keep the flag dry, but it was too late. The flag was soaked and destroyed. It was an awful sight. Jess had foreshadowed the bad ending with her warnings but Tommy refused to listen. It was a sad day for the two friends.
