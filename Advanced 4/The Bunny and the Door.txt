Title: The Bunny and the Door

Once upon a time there was a bunny who had an adventure. One day he was hopping through the woods and he came to a big, harsh door. He stopped to take a closer look and noticed that the door was ajar. He decided to poke his head inside and see what he could find. When he opened the door, the bunny was surprised to see that there was a big pool of mud inside. He found this quite interesting and decided to jump right in! The mud was cold and wet and felt great on the bunny’s feet. He hopped around and played in the mud until he was completely covered. The bunny had so much fun playing in the mud that he forgot to leave. Night came quickly and the bunny had to stay in the mud all night. He felt cold and uncomfortable, but also relieved when the sun finally rose in the morning. As soon as he had enough light, he opened the door again and hopped out of the mud. He was happy to be back in the woods and away from the harsh mud.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to look for bugs and flowers. Lily saw a red thing under a green leaf. She ran to it and picked it up. "Look, Tom, I found a strawberry!" she said, holding it up. Tom came over and looked at it. He wanted to eat it too. He asked Lily to share it with him. "Can I have a bite, please?" he said. Lily thought for a moment. She liked strawberries a lot. They were sweet and juicy. She did not want to give it away. But she also liked Tom. He was her friend. She did not want to make him sad. She decided to share it with him. She broke the strawberry in half and gave him one piece. "Here you go, Tom. You can have this half," she said. Tom smiled and thanked her. He took the piece and bit into it. It was delicious. He was happy. Lily ate her half too. It was also delicious. She was happy. They both enjoyed the strawberry. They hugged each other and continued to play. They learned that sharing was not so difficult after all. It made them both feel good.

One day, a girl named Lily and her dog, Max, found a big frame in the park. The frame was on the ground, and it was very pretty. Lily wanted to take the frame home, but she had to be careful not to break it. "Max, we have to be careful with the frame," said Lily. Max wagged his tail and barked, agreeing with her. They slowly picked up the frame and started to walk home. On their way home, they met another boy named Tom. Tom wanted the frame too. He said, "Give me the frame, Lily!" Lily didn't want to give the frame to Tom, so she held it tight. Tom tried to take the frame, but Lily was very careful not to let go. "Please, Tom, let me keep the frame," Lily said. Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can keep it. But you have to be careful with it." Lily and Max smiled, and they all agreed to be friends. Together, they took the frame home and put it on the wall. They were all very careful with the frame, and it looked very pretty in Lily's room.

One day, a brown dog named Max went for a walk. Max saw a big helicopter in the sky. The helicopter was flying very high. Max was very excited and wanted to see it up close. He knew that if he followed the helicopter, he would find something fun. Max started to run after the helicopter. He ran and ran until he was very tired. Max had to turn left and right many times to keep following the helicopter. After a long time, Max saw that the helicopter was landing in a big park with lots of kids playing. Max went into the park and saw that the helicopter was giving rides to the kids. Max was so happy that he followed the helicopter. He got in line with the kids and waited for his turn. The kids were excited to see Max, the brown dog, in line too. When it was Max's turn, he got into the helicopter and flew up into the sky. Max felt like he was flying like a bird. The kids in the park waved at Max as he flew by. Max was very happy that he followed the helicopter. It was the best day ever for Max.
