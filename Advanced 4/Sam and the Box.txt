Title: Sam and the Box

Sam likes to play with his bricks. He has many bricks of different colors and shapes. He can build towers, houses, cars and animals with his bricks. He has a big box where he keeps his bricks when he is not playing. One day, Sam wants to play with his bricks, but he cannot find his box. He looks in his room, in the living room, in the kitchen, but he cannot see his box anywhere. He feels sad and confused. Where is his box of bricks? He remembers that his mom put his box in the closet yesterday, because she wanted to clean the floor. He runs to the closet and tries to open the door, but it is locked. He needs a key to unlock the door. He thinks hard. Where is the key? He sees his dad sitting on the couch, reading a book. He goes to his dad and asks him for the key. His dad smiles and gives him the key. He says, "Be careful, Sam. Don't lose the key. And don't make a mess with your bricks." Sam takes the key and runs back to the closet. He puts the key in the lock and turns it. He hears a click. He unlocks the door and opens it. He sees his box of bricks inside. He feels happy and excited. He takes his box and brings it to the floor. He opens his box and starts to play with his bricks. He builds a big tower with his bricks. He makes it very tall and strong. He is proud of his tower. He wants to show it to his mom and dad. He is alert and careful. He does not want his tower to fall down. He is having fun with his bricks. He is glad he found his box and unlocked the door.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little flower. The flower was not yet in bloom, but it was very excited to grow. The sun shone bright, and the rain made everything wet. The flower knew that soon it would be its time to show its pretty shape. One day, the sky was full of big, wet raindrops. The little flower felt the rain and knew it was time to bloom. Slowly, it pushed its petals out and showed its beautiful shape. The flower was so happy to be in bloom, and it felt like it was smiling. All the other flowers in the garden saw the little flower in bloom, and they were so happy for it. They all danced and swayed in the wind, showing off their pretty shapes too. The little flower had many friends, and they all played together in the big, wet garden under the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a quick little girl. She was so creative! She made things out of whatever she could find. Every day she would zip around, exploring and creating. One day, she found a pile of leaves in the garden. She thought it was the most creative thing ever! She started to zip around them, jumping in and out of the leaves. She imagined she was flying! After a while, she noticed a butterfly flying nearby. She wanted to zip around like the butterfly! The girl closed her eyes and jumped into the air. Suddenly she was flying around, just like the butterfly. Her quick wings took her up, down, left and right. She flew higher and higher until she was right above the clouds. The little girl was so impressed with her creative flying! She decided to fly like this every day. She would zip around, exploring and creating just like before, but now she could do it in the sky!

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was very weak, so she couldn't do a lot of things. But, she really wanted to do something for her family. One day, she noticed a pumpkin in the garden. She went to her mom and asked, "Can I make something for everyone with the pumpkin?" Her mom was very happy to see her daughter so eager, so she smiled and said, "Yes, of course. What do you want to do?" The little girl smiled and said, "I want to pour some pumpkin soup!" So, her mom helped her get the ingredients and soon enough, the little girl was pouring everything into the pot. It smelled so good that everyone in the house came running, and they all enjoyed the soup together. The little girl felt so happy that she made something that her family enjoyed.
