Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a cat named Mimi. They loved to play together in their yard. One day, Tim saw a big tree and wanted to stand on its branch. Mimi wanted to help Tim. Mimi found a useful stick to help Tim climb the tree. Tim held the stick and stood on the big branch. He was very happy. Mimi climbed the tree too and they both sat on the branch. They looked at the pretty sky and the green grass below. After a while, Tim and Mimi felt hungry. They climbed down from the tree and went inside the house. Tim's mom gave them a yummy treat with cream on top. They both ate the treat and enjoyed it a lot. Tim and Mimi were very happy to have each other as friends.

One day, a kind girl named Lily went to play near the sea. She saw a white boat on the water. Lily thought it was pretty and wanted to say hi. She waved her hand to the people on the boat. The people on the boat waved back to her. They all smiled and were happy. Lily liked to wave at the white boat every day. One day, the boat was very close to the shore. The people on the boat waved to Lily again. They asked her if she wanted to come on the boat for a ride. Lily was excited and said yes. Lily got on the white boat with the kind people. They all waved goodbye to the shore. But then, something unexpected happened. The boat started to go very fast, and it went far away from the shore. Lily was scared and wanted to go back. The kind people were not kind anymore. They did not let Lily go home.

Sam and Lily were friends who liked to play outside. They had a big yard with a swing, a slide and a sandbox. One day, they decided to make a big castle with sand and sticks and stones. They worked hard and had fun, but they did not listen to their mom. Their mom told them to put on hats and sunscreen, because the sun was very hot and bright. She warned them that they could get sick or hurt if they stayed in the sunshine too long. But Sam and Lily did not care. They thought their mom was crazy and they wanted to finish their castle. After a while, they felt very hot and thirsty. They also felt their skin burning and itching. They looked at each other and saw that they were red and peeling. They started to cry and ran to their mom. Their mom was angry and sad. She took them inside and gave them water and medicine. She told them that they had sunburns and that they had to stay in bed for a while. She also told them that their castle was ruined by the wind and the rain. Sam and Lily felt very sorry and wished they had listened to their mom. They learned their lesson and never ignored her warnings again. They also missed their castle and their swing and their slide and their sandbox. They realized that playing outside was not fun if they did not take care of themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a family of bears. They had a big comfy house with a big fireplace in their living room. Every day, the mama bear would make her famous spicy soup in the fireplace for her family. The cubs were always so excited to eat it, but they never knew what Papa Bear added to it to make it so yummy. One day, the cubs asked Mama Bear what she puts in her soup to make it so special. Mama Bear smiled and said she wasn't sure they were ready to know yet. But the cubs were so curious and they kept nagging Mama Bear until she finally gave in. She leaned in close to the cubs and whispered, "I put a secret ingredient in my soup - it's paprika!" The cubs were so excited to know the secret! Every day after that, they would watch as Papa Bear sprinkled the bright red spicy paprika into the soup. Whenever they smell it, everyone in the house knew it must be Mama Bear's spicy soup.
