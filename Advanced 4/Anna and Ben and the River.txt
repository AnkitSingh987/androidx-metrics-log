Title: Anna and Ben and the River

Anna and Ben are friends who live in a village near a big river. They like to play near the water and splash each other. One day, they see a man who is very thirsty. He has a bottle, but it is empty. He asks them if they can share some water with him. "Please, can I have some water?" he says. "I have walked a long way and I have no water left." Anna and Ben look at each other. They have a bucket of water that they filled from the river. They can give some to the man, but then they will have less to play with. "Why do you need water?" Anna asks. "Water is for fun, not for drinking." The man smiles and says, "Water is for many things, my dear. Water is for drinking, for washing, for cooking, for growing plants, for making rainbows. Water is a gift from God, and we should value it and use it wisely." Anna and Ben think about what the man says. They realize that water is not only for fun, but also for life. They feel sorry for the man, who has no water. They decide to share their water with him. "Here, you can have some of our water," Ben says. "We have plenty, and you need it more than we do." The man thanks them and drinks some water. He feels better and says, "You are very kind and generous, children. You have humble hearts and you know how to share. May God bless you and your water." Anna and Ben smile and say, "You are welcome, sir. Thank you for teaching us about water. We will value it and use it wisely." The man leaves and Anna and Ben go back to playing near the water. They splash each other, but they also drink some water and thank God for it. They are happy and grateful. They have learned a valuable lesson.

Jimmy and Lily were the best of friends. Everyday, Lily and Jimmy would go to the playground and play together. But one day, it was very cold so they couldn't play outside. Lily was sad because she couldn't play outside, so Jimmy had an idea. He decided they should have a race inside. Jimmy and Lily raced around the house and tried to be the fastest. At first, Lily found it hard to keep up with Jimmy, but then something amazing happened - she started to get faster, and she was soon running right alongside Jimmy! Jimmy and Lily ran around and around the house. In the end, Lily was the one to succeed. She ran the fastest and even though it was cold outside, Lily and Jimmy enjoyed their race very much.

Moe and Billy were playing in the park together. Moe brought a rare sack with him. “What’s in the sack, Moe?” asked Billy. “Something special!” said Moe. Moe opened the sack. Inside was something fluffy and white. Billy’s eyes widened. It was a dove! “Wow, rare!” exclaimed Billy. Moe smiled and nodded. He carefully pulled out the dove. It was so soft. The dove flew up high in the air, its wings flapping with ease. It was beautiful! Moe and Billy watched the dove soar high in the sky until it was out of sight.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for the week. Tim saw a big freezer with lots of ice cream inside. He wanted some ice cream, so he asked his mom, "Can I have some ice cream, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but first we need to print a list of what we need to buy." Tim and his mom went to a machine that printed the list. The machine made a crazy noise as it printed the list. Tim laughed and said, "That sounds funny!" After they got the list, they went back to the freezer and picked out some ice cream. Tim chose a big tub of chocolate ice cream. They put the ice cream in the cart and went to pay for their food. At home, Tim put the ice cream in the freezer so it would stay cold. He was excited to eat it later. Tim and his mom had a fun day at the store, and Tim learned how to print a list and pick out ice cream from the freezer.
