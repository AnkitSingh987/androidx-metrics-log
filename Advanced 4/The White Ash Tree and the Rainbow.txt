Title: The White Ash Tree and the Rainbow

Once upon a time there was a white ash tree. It was so tall it touched the sky. Every day, the ash tree would spray its leaves with fine white ash. The little animals in the forest were so happy they could play in the ash. They would roll around in the white powder and have fun. They made lots of snowballs and ash angels. The sun was shining and the sky was so blue. It was the best day ever! One day, a big wind came. It blew the white ash all away. The animals were sad, but then a rainbow came out from behind the clouds. They were so happy to see the colours of the rainbow. Then, a voice came from the sky. It said, "Let the ash rain down from the sky"! A big spray of white ash came from the clouds and the animals laughed and played in it. They danced and had so much fun. The animals were happy to have their ash back again. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a small ball. The ball loved to play golf. One day, the ball went to the park to play. At the park, the ball saw a scary dog. The dog was big and loud. The ball was scared and wanted to separate from the dog. The ball rolled away fast. The dog ran after the ball. Then, a nice girl came and took the dog away. The ball was happy and went back to play golf.

Once upon a time, there was a big, white cauliflower. It lived in a garden with many other tasty vegetables. The cauliflower had a lot of friends, like the round potato and the long carrot. They loved to play in the sun. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She wanted to cook a tasty soup for her family. She picked the cauliflower, the potato, and the carrot. The little girl was very happy. She took them to her home and put them in a big pot. The little girl started to stir the pot. The cauliflower, the potato, and the carrot were very scared. They did not want to be in the soup. Then, something unexpected happened. The pot fell off the stove! The cauliflower, the potato, and the carrot were free, but the little girl's soup was all over the floor. She was very sad, and her family did not have a tasty soup for dinner.

Tom and Anna are friends. They like to play in the school. One day, they find a big blackboard in a room. The blackboard is very dirty. It has many marks and words on it. "Let us try to clean it," Tom says. He takes a cloth and some water. He rubs the blackboard hard. But the marks and words do not go away. They are very stubborn. Anna has an idea. She takes a chalk and writes on the blackboard. She writes "Hello" and "Smile". She draws a sun and a flower. She makes the blackboard look happy. "Look, Tom, I made the blackboard nice," Anna says. She shows Tom her work. Tom smiles. He likes Anna's idea. He takes another chalk and writes "Thank you" and "Friend". He draws a heart and a star. He makes the blackboard look grateful. The blackboard is not dirty anymore. It is full of words and pictures. Tom and Anna are proud of themselves. They hug each other. They are happy. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red tomato. It lived in a garden with many green plants. The sun was very hot, and the tomato grew bigger and bigger every day. One day, the sun was too hot. The tomato started to feel a burn. It turned from red to brown. The tomato was sad because it did not want to be brown. Then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came to the garden. She saw the brown tomato and smiled. She picked it up and took it to her mom. They made yummy soup with the brown tomato. The tomato was happy because it was part of something good. And they all lived happily ever after.
