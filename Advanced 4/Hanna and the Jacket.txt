Title: Hanna and the Jacket

Hanna was walking to the vendor with her mom and dad. She was very excited because they were going to buy a special treat. As they walked, the wind was very cold and her mom said, "Hanna, mind your jacket, it's too cold to be outside without it!" Hanna said, "I don't need my jacket, I'm not cold!" Her dad laughed and gave her a hug to keep her warm. When they arrived at the vendor, Hanna was so excited, but her mom and dad said that she was only allowed one treat. Hanna had a hard time deciding what to get. She wanted everything! Finally, after a lot of thinking, she decided to get ice cream. But when the vendor handed her the ice cream, it was so cold it made her cry! Her parents tried to cheer her up, but she was so sad that she left the ice cream with the vendor. She was sure the cold treat had ruined the day. But then, her dad had an idea. He went back to the vendor and explained the situation. The vendor was very kind and he gave Hanna another treat. It was not cold, but very delicious! Hanna was relieved and she didn't mind the cold anymore!

Once upon a time, there was a big bed with a soft mattress. The mattress was very organized and liked to keep things clean. It lived in a small room with a family. One day, a little boy named Timmy came to the room. He was playing with his toys and made a big mess. The mattress did not like the mess. It wanted to send the toys back to their places. So, the mattress asked Timmy to help clean up. Timmy was a good boy and listened. Together, they organized the toys and put them away. The mattress was happy, and Timmy felt proud. They became good friends and kept the room clean and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big earthquake. The ground shook and people were scared. Some things fell down and broke. One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a dead tree. The tree had no leaves and no other birds lived there. The little bird felt sad for the tree. The bird decided to help the tree. It lay a seed on the ground near the tree. The seed grew into a pretty flower. The flower made the dead tree feel happy. Soon, more birds came, and the tree was not alone anymore. Everyone was happy and the town became a beautiful place to live.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a very happy dog. Every night he would dream about exciting things. One night he had a brilliant dream. He dreamed about running and chasing a big, bouncy ball. He ran and ran and ran until the ball fell in a lake. Spot was so excited he jumped in the lake to get the ball! When he got out, the ball was covered in wet fur. Spot was so proud, because he had a brilliant idea. He ran back home and showed all his friends the wonderful wet ball. Everyone was so happy, they danced around in a circle. Spot was the hero of the day!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a black cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was very good at catching things. One day, Fluffy saw a big, red ball bouncing in the forest. "Wow, I want to catch that ball," Fluffy said to his friend, Bunny. Bunny was a small, white rabbit who lived in the forest too. "Let's try to catch it together," Bunny said. So, Fluffy and Bunny ran after the big, red ball. They jumped and tried to catch it, but the ball was very fast. They chased the ball for a whole year. One day, Fluffy and Bunny finally caught the ball. They were so happy. "We did it! We caught the ball!" Fluffy and Bunny said together. They played with the big, red ball all day long. From that day on, Fluffy and Bunny were the best of friends. And they had lots of fun playing with their big, red ball in the forest.
