Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she noticed that her toy box was almost empty. She was sad because she could not find her favorite toy. Amy looked everywhere, but she could not find her toy. Then, she saw a big box with a top on it. She thought it might be the perfect place to look for her toy. So, she opened the box and looked inside. Amy saw her favorite toy inside the box with the top. She was so happy that she found it. She played with her toy all day long. From that day on, Amy always put her toys back in the box with the top. She knew it was the perfect place to keep them safe.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous fish named Finn. Finn lived in the big, blue ocean. He had many friends, like little fish, big fish, and even a crab. They all liked to play and swim in the ocean. One day, Finn heard a sound. "Help, help!" someone called. Finn wanted to help, so he swam very fast to find who needed him. He found a small fish stuck in a net. The small fish was sad and scared. "Will you help me?" the small fish asked. Finn was a good friend, so he answered, "Yes, I will help you!" With his strong tail, Finn pulled the net and set the small fish free. The small fish was so happy and said, "Thank you, Finn!" They swam away together, back to their friends in the big, blue ocean.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day and all night. One day, she was so tired from dancing that she fell asleep under a big tree. In the morning, a little bird came to wake her up. The bird said, "Wake up, pretty dancer! The sun is up, and it's time to dance again!" Lily opened her eyes and saw the attractive bird. She smiled and said, "Thank you, little bird. I will dance with you today." Lily and the bird danced together all day. They were so happy, and everyone who saw them thought they were the best dancers ever. When the sun went down, Lily and the bird said goodbye and went to sleep, ready to dance again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to build things with his toy boards. One day, he wanted to build a big tower with his boards. But it was very cold and icy outside. Tim did not like the icy cold. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. They wanted to build the tower together. But they had a fight. Tim wanted to build the tower inside, where it was warm. Sam wanted to build it outside, in the icy cold. They did not know what to do. Then, Tim had an idea. They could build two towers! One inside and one outside. Tim and Sam were happy. They built the towers and had fun. They learned that they could both have fun, even if they liked different things. And the icy cold was not so bad after all.

Once upon a time, there was an old crane who lived in a pond near a village. Every day, the crane would use its beak to point out the things the village people were doing wrong. One day, the villagers were preparing a meal with very spicy ingredients. The crane, noticing this, pointed at the villagers and said: "You shouldn't be so spicy with your food! You should use moderation instead". The village people laughed and paid no attention to what the crane said. The following day, the village people were feeling very ill. Some had stomach aches and some had headaches. Everyone realised that their meal the day before was too spicy for them. At this point, the crane pointed at them again and said: "I told you that too much spicy food isn't good for you". The villagers realised their mistake and vowed never to be too spicy with their food ever again. They learned an important lesson: moderation is important when consuming food and other things in life.
