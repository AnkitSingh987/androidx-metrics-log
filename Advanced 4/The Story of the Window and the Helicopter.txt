Title: The Story of the Window and the Helicopter

Once upon a time there was a mommy and daddy and a 3 year old girl. One day, the mommy and daddy looked out their window and saw a crazy helicopter flying around in the sky! The girl was so excited and kept asking to go on the helicopter. One day, the mommy and daddy said yes, and they all hopped on the helicopter. The girl had the best time ever! She saw the countryside and lots of trees whizzing passed. But after a while, the mommy and daddy said it was time to return and the little girl was sad. But then daddy said she could come back for another ride in the helicopter soon! That made her smile. So the little girl was happy again, and waved goodbye to the helicopter as they drove home. She couldn't wait to return and go on the crazy helicopter again!

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy rocket. He loved his rocket very much. It was his favorite toy. He played with it every day. One day, Tim felt uncomfortable. His tummy hurt. He did not want to play with his rocket. Tim's mommy said, "Don't worry, Tim. You will feel better soon." Tim did not want to give up. He did not want to surrender. He wanted to play with his rocket. So, Tim played with his rocket even though he felt uncomfortable. Soon, Tim's tummy stopped hurting, and he was happy again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a bear named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat honey. One day, he saw a bee flying near an icy cold river. He thought, "Maybe there is honey near the river." Bobo went to the river and saw a big bee nest in a tree. He wanted to get the honey, but he was scared of the icy water. He thought, "I can't swim in the icy water, but I really want the honey." So Bobo decided to be brave and jumped into the river. As he swam, the water was very icy, but he reached the tree. He took the honey and started to eat it. Suddenly, the bees came out and said, "Bobo, you took our honey without asking. That is not nice. We will punish you." The bees stung Bobo, and it hurt a lot. Bobo learned his lesson. He knew he should not take things without asking. From that day on, he always asked before taking something. And the bees, seeing that Bobo had learned his lesson, became his friends and shared their honey with him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and even a pink room. Lily had a friend named Sam. Sam was deaf, but they still played together and had lots of fun. One day, Lily wanted to borrow Sam's pink ball. She went to Sam's house and asked, "Can I borrow your pink ball, please?" Sam smiled and said yes. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Lily and Sam played with the pink ball all day. They threw it, kicked it, and even rolled it. When it was time to go home, Lily gave the pink ball back to Sam. They both had a great day and were very happy friends.

Emily was playing in the park when she saw something fly. She had never seen this before. "What is that?" She asked. A bird landed in front of her. "That is a fly," the bird said. Emily was curious. She reached out to touch it. The fly was very disgusting. "Don't touch it," the bird said. "It isn't safe to know a fly." Emily didn't listen. She touched the fly anyway. Suddenly, the fly bit her and she started to cry. The bird shook its head. "You should have listened. Now you know why it is not safe to know a fly!"

Once upon a time, there was a friendly little crab. The crab had a pretty shell. He liked to play with his friends in the sand. One day, the crab found a magic rock. The rock could make things shrink. The crab thought it would be fun to try the magic rock on his shell. He touched the rock to his shell, and it began to shrink. But, the crab didn't know how to stop it. His shell got smaller and smaller. The crab was scared, but then something unexpected happened. Instead of being sad, his friends started to laugh. They thought the tiny shell was funny. The crab laughed too, and they all played together. The crab learned that even when things go wrong, friends can make it alright.
