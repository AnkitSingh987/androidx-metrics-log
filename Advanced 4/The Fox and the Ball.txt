Title: The Fox and the Ball

Once, there was a little fox. He had a broken leg. He was sad because he could not play with his friends. One day, he received a gift. It was a big, red ball. The little fox loved the ball. He played with it all day. He would push it with his nose and watch it roll. The ball made him happy even though his leg was broken. One day, the ball rolled into the woods. The little fox followed it. He found a magic fairy in the woods. The fairy saw his broken leg and felt sad. She waved her magic wand, and suddenly, the little fox's leg was not broken anymore. The little fox was very happy and surprised. He could play with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to spray water on the flowers in her garden. She did this every day, and all the flowers were very happy. Lily's garden was not ordinary, it had a special style that made it very pretty. One day, while Lily was spraying water, something unexpected happened. A tiny frog jumped out from behind a flower! Lily was very surprised. The frog said, "Thank you for spraying water on me, I was very thirsty!" Lily was happy to help the frog and continued to spray water on the flowers and the frog. From that day on, Lily and the frog became best friends. They spent every day together in the garden. Lily would spray water on the flowers and the frog, and the frog would help Lily find new flowers to plant. The garden became even more beautiful, and everyone loved to visit Lily's special garden.

Lily was a chubby girl who loved to eat cookies. She ate them every day, even when her mom said no. She did not care if they made her tummy hurt or her teeth dirty. She just wanted more and more cookies. One day, Lily saw a big jar of cookies on the kitchen table. She climbed on a chair and reached for the jar. But she was not careful and knocked over a vase. The vase broke into pieces and made a loud noise. Lily's mom heard the noise and came running. "Lily, what did you do?" her mom asked, looking angry. "You broke the vase and made a mess. And you were trying to take the cookies, weren't you?" Lily felt scared and sorry. She wanted to hide, but there was nowhere to go. She looked around and saw the chimney. She had an idea. She ran to the fireplace and crawled into the chimney. She thought she could escape from her mom and find more cookies. But the chimney was dark and dirty. It was full of soot and smoke. It made Lily cough and sneeze. It was also narrow and tight. It squeezed Lily's chubby body and made her stuck. She could not move or breathe. She started to cry. "Help, help!" she shouted. "I'm stuck in the chimney!" Her mom heard her cry and followed her voice. She saw Lily's feet sticking out of the fireplace. She pulled and pulled, but Lily was too chubby to come out. She called the firemen to help. The firemen came with a big truck and a ladder. They climbed on the roof and looked into the chimney. They saw Lily's face covered in soot and tears. They used a rope and a net to lift her out of the chimney. They brought her down and gave her to her mom. Lily was happy to be out of the chimney, but she was also ashamed and sad. She hugged her mom and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was bad. I wanted the cookies, but I regret it. I don't want any more cookies. I just want you." Her mom hugged her back and said, "I'm glad you're safe, Lily. I love you, but you have to learn to listen and be careful. Cookies are not good for you if you eat too many. And chimneys are not for hiding. They are for Santa Claus." Lily nodded and smiled. She learned her lesson. She never went into the chimney again. And she only ate cookies when her mom said yes.
