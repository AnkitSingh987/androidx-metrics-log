Title: Suzie and the Sun

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Suzie. Suzie was three years old and she loved to explore. She wanted to find something special, something that was perfect for her. One day, Suzie saw something shiny on the ground. She ran to it and saw that it was a small copper coin. She held it in her hands and it shone brightly in the sun. "This is perfect!" Suzie said, as she let out a little squeal of delight. Suddenly, there was a loud rumble and the earth began to tremble. Suzie was scared and dropped the coin. But it didn't fall to the ground. Instead, it hovered in the air and twirled around in circles. Suzie shook her head and smiled. "That was perfect," she said.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a poor, sad tie on the floor. The tie was dirty and had a small rip. Tim wanted to help the tie, so he picked it up and took it home. At home, Tim's mom saw the tie and said, "Oh no, this tie is not good. It needs a zip to make it better." Tim asked, "What is a zip, Mom?" His mom replied, "A zip is when we fix something by sewing it together." Tim's mom took the tie and zipped the rip with a needle and thread. The tie looked much better now. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom! The tie is happy too!" Tim's mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. You did a good thing by helping the poor tie."

Once there was a boy called Jack who lived in a house by the sea. This house had a magical garden filled with fruit trees of all kinds! Jack was very excited to pick the fruit in this garden. One day, Jack went to the garden with his basket to pick the fruit. But as he was walking around, he heard a voice coming from the trees. It was a small voice, so Jack had to listen very carefully. The voice said, "Hello? Is anyone there?" Jack was very surprised. He called back, "Yes, it is me, Jack!" The voice replied, "I am a restless fruit fairy. I help the fruit to ripen and grow!" Jack was so excited, he could not believe it. He asked the fairy, "Can you help me pick the ripe fruit?" The fairy said, "Yes, I can! Listen carefully and I will help you find the best fruit!" Jack was very happy. He followed the fairy's instructions and found the best fruit for his basket. He thanked the fairy for everything and went back home. Jack was so pleased with his adventure. He had a basket full of delicious fruit, and he had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to create things with her hands. One day, she found a big empty jar in the kitchen. She had a good idea! Amy was excited and thought, "I will make a surprise for Mom and Dad!" She went outside and picked pretty flowers, colorful leaves, and little rocks. She put them all in the jar. But the jar looked confused. It was not pretty yet. Amy thought hard and then she knew what to do. She got some paper and made a beautiful picture. She put the picture on the jar. Now the jar was happy and pretty. When Mom and Dad came home, they saw the jar and smiled. They knew Amy made it for them. The jar was a great surprise!

Once upon a time there was a girl named Ella, who was very restless. Every day, her parents begged her to stay put, but she could never bear to. One day, she decided to go for a walk around the farm. On the farm was a huge nut tree, and when Ella walked closer to it, she noticed a whole bunch of peanuts. She was excited and quickly collected some. With the peanuts, she decided to perform a show for everyone. Her parents were proud of her performance and rewarded her with candy and hugs. She has since learned that staying still can lead to many positive opportunities - even when one is as restless as she was.
