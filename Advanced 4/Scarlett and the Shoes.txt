Title: Scarlett and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a graceful little girl called Scarlett. She had a beautiful dress and a lovely pair of shoes. Every day she would go to the park and play with her friends. On one particular day, Scarlett forgot to be polite and behave. Her friends were having a tea party and she decided to run away with a shoe. Scarlet started running so fast, it was like she was flying. Her friends were very upset and tried to get Scarlett to stop. One of her friends shouted, "Scarlett, why don't you be graceful and give us back the shoe!" Scarlett felt sorry for causing a fuss and quickly returned the shoe. She apologised to her friends and promised to behave better. From that day forward, Scarlett was very graceful and never ran away with anything again. She was a wonderful friend who was very polite and well behaved.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to play with her toys and lean on her big chair. One day, Lucy felt anxious. She did not know why, but she felt that something was not right. Later, Lucy's mom made a fire in the fireplace to keep the house warm. The fire was big and bright. Lucy liked to watch the fire, but she still felt anxious. She played with her toys but could not stop thinking about the fire. Soon, Lucy's toy ball rolled near the fire. She was scared, but she knew she had to save her toy. Lucy carefully leaned and reached for the ball. She got it and moved away from the fire. Lucy was happy and not anxious anymore. She had saved her toy and learned to be careful near the fire.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He was feeling fearful. He needed help so he went to his mom. She asked him why he was so fearful, but he did not know. He just felt scared. So Mom went outside and found a big tree with giant roots. She asked the boy to hug the tree. He did and he felt better. The tree helped him feel calm. Suddenly, a bird flew by and the boy felt joy. The bird sang a gentle tune and the boy smiled. He realized that he didn't need to be fearful anymore. He hugged the tree once more and felt strong. The boy ran back home and shared the story with his mom. Mom was so happy that the boy was not feeling scared anymore. He had conquered his fear. From then on, whenever the boy was feeling fearful, he would go and hug the tree. It was like his special friend and helped him feel brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved watching cartoons on TV. His favorite cartoon was about a brave dog who would always dare to do important things to help others. One day, Tim was playing in the park when he saw a small kitten stuck in a tree. Tim thought about his favorite cartoon and said to himself, "I can be brave like the dog in the cartoon and help the kitten." So, Tim dared to climb the tree to save the kitten. As Tim got closer to the kitten, he realized it was not a real kitten, but a toy! Tim's friend, Sally, was hiding behind the tree and laughed, "I tricked you, Tim!" Tim laughed too, and they both played with the toy kitten. In the end, Tim learned that even though it was just a toy, it was still important to be brave and help others.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big spray bottle. He was very patient and wanted to find a good use for it. He had a secret plan. Tom decided to go to his friend Sue's house and play a trick on her. When Tom got to Sue's house, he hid behind a big tree. He was very quiet and patient. He waited for Sue to come out. When Sue came out, Tom jumped out and said, "I will spray you!" Sue was very scared. But then, something unexpected happened. Sue's big dog, Max, saw Tom with the spray bottle. Max did not like the spray bottle. Max ran to Tom and took the spray bottle away. Max then sprayed Tom with the bottle. Tom was very wet and sad. His secret plan did not work, and he learned not to play tricks on his friends.
