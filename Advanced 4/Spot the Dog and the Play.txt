Title: Spot the Dog and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot's family took him to the airport to meet someone new. Spot was very excited. At the airport, Spot saw a big bird with wings. He thought it was a real bird, but it was an airplane. Spot wanted to play with the big bird. But then, something unexpected happened! The big bird started to move and made a loud noise. Spot was scared and ran back to his family. It was then that he met the new person - a little girl named Lily. Spot and Lily became best friends, and they played together all the time. And Spot learned that the big bird was just a plane, not a real bird.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big box. The box was incredible! Tim wanted to see what was inside the box, so he pushed it. But the box was too heavy for him. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. "Sam, can you help me push this incredible box?" Tim asked. Sam came and together, they pushed the box. It started to move. They pushed and pushed until the box was open. When the box was open, they saw a lot of waste inside. Tim and Sam were sad. They thought there would be something amazing in the box. But then, a small bird flew out of the waste. The bird was so colorful and beautiful. Tim and Sam were happy again. They knew the bird was the incredible surprise in the box.

Once upon a time there was a famous prince. He had long hair and a long cape that made him look very grand. Everywhere he went, the prince was greeted with cheers from the crowd. On one special day, the prince set out on a long journey. He skipped along the path, kicking the stones as he went. Suddenly, he slipped and fell on the ground. He started to cry, not understanding what caused him to fall. An old man walking nearby saw the prince and said, "Ah, my young prince. That was because you did not pay attention to the truth." The prince was confused, "What do you mean, old man?" The old man smiled and said, "The truth is that no matter how famous you are, you can still have accidents if you don't look where you are going." The prince realized his mistake and nodded solemnly. From that day, he always kept the words of the old man in mind and he never slipped again. The moral of the story is that no matter how famous or powerful you are, you can still make mistakes if you don't pay attention to the truth.

Once there was a little boy who was playing in the garden. He saw a delicate wheat field nearby. His eyes sparkled with excitement. He ran over to the wheat field and opened the gate. 'Hi, who are you?' asked a voice. The little boy looked up and saw a mouse standing in the wheat. 'My name is Thomas,' he replied. The mouse smiled. 'It's so nice to meet you, Thomas. I'm Millie. Would you like to help me pick some wheat?' Thomas nodded eagerly. He carefully picked the delicate wheat and helped Millie fill a basket. When they were finished, they opened the gate and said goodbye. Thomas went home feeling happy. He had made a new friend and had a lot of fun finding the wheat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a favorite cloth. It was very soft and she took it everywhere she went. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom and her cloth. At the park, Lucy saw a boy named Tom. He wanted to play with Lucy's cloth. Lucy said, "Okay, but be careful." Tom played with the cloth, but he did not listen to Lucy. He got the cloth dirty. Lucy was sad and Tom was ashamed. Tom said, "I'm sorry, Lucy. I will clean it." He took the cloth to his mom. She washed it and made it clean again. Tom gave the clean cloth back to Lucy. He promised to be careful next time. Lucy smiled and they played together. From then on, Tom always listened and did not repeat his mistake.
