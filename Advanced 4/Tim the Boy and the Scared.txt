Title: Tim the Boy and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing outside in his big, open yard. He liked to run and jump all day. One day, he saw a big dog near his house. Tim was scared because the dog was barking loudly. He did not know what to do. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mommy, there is a big dog outside! I am scared!" His mom went outside to see the dog. She saw that the dog was a threat to Tim. She tried to shoo the dog away, but she could not manage to do it. The big dog did not leave. It kept barking and growling at Tim and his mom. In the end, they had to stay inside the house all day. They were sad and scared because they could not play outside anymore. The bad dog had won.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot who was flying through the air. She was taking passengers from one place to another with her plane. This pilot was very brave, so she was not scared. Suddenly, something went wrong with the plane and it started to spin in the sky! The pilot tried hard to keep the plane under her control, but the passengers all got scared. "Help us!" they cried. The pilot threw herself in the cockpit and worked hard to fix the plane. After some time, the plane was back in the sky and flying safely. Everyone cheered and thanked the pilot. But the pilot felt guilty that she had frightened the passengers, so she decided to make them a special treat. She took them all to a special place and bought them all ice-cream! The passengers smiled, and the pilot knew she had made them happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Molly and a boy called Tim. One day, they were playing in the garden with their teddies. Suddenly, Molly said proudly: “Let’s go for a walk!”. But Tim was worried. “It’s wet outside”, he said. “Let’s stay inside and play games!” Molly was unhappy and started to cry. “But I want to go outside and explore!” Then a brilliant idea came to Tim. “Let’s go for a walk in the rain!” he said excitedly. Molly was over the moon. They grabbed their raincoats and made their way outside. The rain splashed their faces and the wet grass tickled their toes. It was brilliant. They played, ran, and laughed all the way. Molly and Tim were really excited and got soaked in the rain. It was a day they never forgot.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a glove on the ground. She thought it was an incredible glove. It was red and shiny. Sue put the glove on her hand and went to show her mom. On her way, she saw a cat in a tree. The cat was stuck and could not get down. Sue sighed, feeling sad for the cat. She wanted to help it. Sue lifted her hand with the glove and, suddenly, the cat floated down from the tree. It was safe! Sue knew the incredible glove had magic. She was so happy she could help the cat. She ran to her mom to tell her the story. But when she showed her mom the glove, it was just a normal glove. Sue was confused, but she knew she had done something amazing.

Mary and her mommy went to the store to buy a firework. Mary’s mommy was grumpy and said, “We have to get the firework to replace the one we lost today.” Mary was very excited. She said, “Yay! Fireworks are so fun.” But when they arrived at the store, the firework was gone. Her mommy was even grumpier. “Oh, no! Someone took our firework,” she said. Mary knew how to cheer up her mommy. She said, “Let’s find another one!” So they looked and looked, but no firework could replace the one they lost. But then Karla, the shopkeeper, said, “Wait, I have one in the back.” She came back with a new firework and Mary’s mommy was happy again. Mary cheered, “Yay! We got our firework!” She was so excited that she hugged the firework and gave Karla a big thank you.
