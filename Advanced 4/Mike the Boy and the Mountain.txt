Title: Mike the Boy and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Mike. Mike was only three years old, but he loved to explore. One day, Mike decided to climb a mountain. He was so excited! He said to his mom, "Can I go outside and climb a mountain?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, but be careful." Mike was so happy that he ran outside. He saw a mountain, and it was perfect. Mike said, "This mountain is perfect for me!" He began to climb the mountain. The mountain was tall and it was difficult, but Mike kept climbing. Finally, he made it to the top of the mountain and said, "Woo-hoo! I did it!" Suddenly, Mike heard a voice say, "You did it! That was perfect!" Mike looked around and saw a kind, old man. They began to talk, and Mike told the man all about his adventure. The old man listened and smiled. He said, "That was a perfect adventure. You should be proud of yourself!" Mike smiled, and they talked until it was time to go home. The old man said goodbye and Mike waved. He knew he would never forget this adventure!

Charlie was very excited. He was riding in the taxi for the first time. He had never been in one before and he was so excited to explore the city. The taxi driver was nice and friendly. He asked Charlie and his family where they were going and Charlie shouted out "the beach!" with a big smile. The taxi driver smiled and said, "sounds like fun, let's go!" But then he started to insist that they go somewhere else instead. He said it was quicker and he wanted to save them some time. Charlie's family hesitated but then agreed and the taxi driver took them away. He took them to a hidden alley and stopped. Then he demanded more money. He said he was not taking them anywhere until they paid him more. Charlie's family refused, but the driver wouldn't listen and he continued to insist they pay him more. In the end, they didn't have any choice and paid him the extra money. When they finally got to the beach, they were all exhausted and frustrated. Charlie was so disappointed that he had to miss out on the fun, all because of the taxi driver's insisting.

Lily liked to go to the store with her mom. The store had many things that Lily wanted. She saw a doll with a pink dress and long hair. She asked her mom if she could have it. Her mom said no, they had no money for toys. Lily was sad and angry. She wanted the doll very much. She looked around and saw that the owner was busy with other people. She had an idea. She took the doll and hid it under her coat. She thought no one would see her. She walked to the door with her mom. She was happy and proud. She thought she was very clever. But then she heard a loud voice. It was the owner. He saw Lily take the doll. He was very angry. He ran to Lily and grabbed the doll from her. He yelled at Lily and her mom. He said they were bad and he would call the police. Lily was scared and ashamed. She started to cry. Her mom was also scared and ashamed. She said she was sorry and she did not know. She tried to explain to the owner. But the owner did not listen. He said they had to leave and never come back. Lily and her mom left the store. They had no doll and no money. They felt very bad. They walked home in silence. Lily wished she never took the doll. She learned a hard lesson. She never stole anything again.

One day, a fast car named Speedy liked to speed on the road. He was a foolish car and did not listen to anyone. He thought it was fun to go very fast, but he did not know that it could be dangerous. One day, while Speedy was speeding, he saw a big truck with a hook on the back. The truck said, "Hey Speedy, don't be foolish! You should slow down or you might get hurt." But Speedy did not listen and kept going fast. As Speedy kept speeding, he got too close to the truck and got caught on the hook. He tried to speed away, but he was stuck. The truck helped him get off the hook and said, "Now do you see why you should not speed?" Speedy learned his lesson and promised to slow down.
