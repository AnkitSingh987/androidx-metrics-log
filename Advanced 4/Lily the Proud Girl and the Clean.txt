Title: Lily the Proud Girl and the Clean

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a proud girl named Lily. She liked to clean her room and make it shine. One day, she found a lot of dust under her bed. She thought, "I need help to clean this." Lily called her friend, Tom, to help her clean the dust. Tom came and they started to clean. They worked hard and soon, the dust was gone. They were very happy and proud of their work. They went outside to play and have fun. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew more dust into Lily's room. When they went back inside, they saw the dust and were surprised. They learned that they should always close the windows when they clean. From that day, they closed the windows when cleaning and were proud of their clean rooms.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ami. She was three years old and very excited to be able to go outside and play in her yard. Ami was playing in the garden when she saw a man walking towards her. He was walking very slowly and Ami was scared. She wanted to run away but she couldn't. The man got closer and closer to Ami until he was standing right in front of her. He started to poke her on the head and Ami started to cry. The man didn't stop and kept poking Ami. He was laughing and wouldn't stop. Ami was so scared that she closed her eyes and wished that the man would go away. Unfortunately, her wish didn't come true. The man kept poking her until he was finished - then he just disappeared. Ami was so unhappy and scared. She was so relieved when her mom came out and found her. She hugged Ami tightly and told her that everything was going to be okay. That was the last time Ami saw the man - but she was never as excited to go out to play in her yard again.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play and swing on the vines all day long. One day, Momo saw a very big and heavy vine. He thought it would be fun to swing on it. Momo grabbed the heavy vine and started to swing. He went up and down, and side to side. Momo was having so much fun. But he did not know that the heavy vine was not strong enough to hold him. Suddenly, the heavy vine broke, and Momo fell to the ground. He was hurt and sad. Momo learned that he should be careful and not play with things that are too heavy for him. The moral of the story is to always be careful and think before you play.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom found a magnet. Tom did not smile a lot. He liked to be alone. The magnet was red and shiny. Tom played with the magnet and put it on his fridge. A little bird named Lily saw Tom playing with the magnet. Lily was happy and liked to smile. She flew to Tom and said, "Hi, can I play with your magnet too?" Tom was grumpy but said, "Okay, you can play with it." Lily and Tom played with the magnet together. They put it on the fridge and made it move. They laughed and had fun. Tom started to smile more. He was not grumpy anymore. Tom and Lily became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a little rat. The rat was scared of everything. He was scared of the cat, the dog, and even the people in the house. He always hid in the walls and never came out to play. One day, the rat heard a loud noise. It was a party! The people were celebrating a birthday. The rat peeked out from his hiding place and saw a big cake. He wanted to taste it, but he was too scared to leave his hiding spot. Then, a friendly mouse saw the scared rat and said, "Hi, little rat! Don't be scared. I will help you get some cake." The rat and the mouse went to the cake, and they ate it together. The scared rat was not scared anymore, and they celebrated together. From that day on, the rat and the mouse were best friends.
