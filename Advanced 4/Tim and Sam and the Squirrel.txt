Title: Tim and Sam and the Squirrel

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the park. They had a ball, a kite and a bike. They also liked to watch the animals in the park, like the birds, the ducks and the squirrels. One day, they saw a big, brown squirrel on a tree. It had a long, fluffy tail and shiny, black eyes. Tim and Sam wanted to get closer to the squirrel, so they left their toys and walked towards the tree. "Hello, squirrel!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with us?" The squirrel looked at Tim and Sam and made a loud, angry noise. It showed its sharp, white teeth and waved its tail. It did not want to play with Tim and Sam. It wanted to be alone. "Go away, boys!" the squirrel said. "This is my tree and my nuts. You cannot have them. If you come closer, I will bite you!" Tim and Sam were scared of the squirrel. They did not know that squirrels could talk. They did not know that squirrels could be so mean. They ran back to their toys and picked them up. "Sorry, squirrel!" Sam said. "We did not mean to bother you. We just wanted to be your friends." The squirrel did not say anything. It just watched Tim and Sam leave. It was glad that they listened to its warning. It was not a friendly squirrel. It was a greedy squirrel. Tim and Sam learned a lesson that day. They learned that not all animals in the park wanted to play with them. They learned that some animals could be creative and talk to them. They learned that they should always be careful and respectful of the animals in the park. They also learned that they had more fun with their toys than with the squirrel.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet named Tim. Tim was nervous because he had to fly across the sky. He was scared that he would not fly well. Tim's mom told him to repeat after her, "I can do it!" Tim said it again and again. He started to feel less nervous. He knew he could fly across the sky. The big day came, and it was time for Tim to fly. He remembered what his mom said and repeated, "I can do it!" With a big smile, Tim flew across the sky like a star. Everyone was so proud of him, and Tim was not nervous anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Anna who was feeling very fearful. She was walking outside and heard a rustling sound in the bushes. All of a sudden, Anna saw a package appear in front of her! She was surprised and said "What is this package doing here?". The package seemed to say "Welcome!". Anna was even more scared but also curious. She slowly crept towards the package and opened it up. Inside was a teddy bear that was smiling at Anna! She was no longer scared and said hello to the teddy bear. The teddy bear said "Welcome! I'm here to be your friend". Anna was overjoyed and hugged the teddy bear. From that day onwards the two of them were the best of friends. That was the day Anna welcomed her new friend.

One day, an elephant was walking through the woods. She was happy and the sun was shining. Suddenly, the sky got dark and it started to rain. The poor elephant was sad. She looked for shelter to prevent getting wet in the rain. She saw a cave in the woods and it seemed like a safe place. The elephant felt relieved and went in the cave. But when she got in, she was scared! She heard a loud roaring noise coming from inside the cave. The elephant soon found out it was not a roaring noise, but laughter! Four little girls were at the back of the cave, playing a game and laughing. The elephant quickly realized she was interrupting their game and stepped out of the cave. When she stepped back outside, the elephant saw a rainbow in the sky! She was so happy and realized the rain had prevented her from getting wet and had created a magical moment for her. The elephant was no longer sad and enjoyed the rainbow.
