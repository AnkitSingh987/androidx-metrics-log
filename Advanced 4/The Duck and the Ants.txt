Title: The Duck and the Ants

Once upon a time, there was a big duck. The big duck loved to play in the water. Every day, the big duck would go to the pond to bathe. He liked to splash and swim in the water. One sunny day, the big duck went to the pond. He saw a long line of ants. The ants were on a march. They were going to find food. The big duck thought it was fun to watch the ants march. The big duck wanted to help the ants. He thought of a plan. He would bathe in the pond and splash water on the ants. The ants would be happy and cool. So, the big duck splashed water on the ants. The ants were happy and said thank you. The big duck felt good for helping his new friends.

One day, a good dog named Bobo went to play outside. He saw a big potato on the ground. Bobo was very happy to find the potato. Bobo wanted to hide the potato, so he started to dig a hole. He dug and dug, and then he put the potato in the hole. Bobo was proud of his work. Later, a bird saw the potato in the hole. The bird wanted the potato too. The bird dived down and took the potato. Bobo saw the bird and chased it. They played together and shared the potato. They both had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there were two brothers called Sam and Tim. Sam was always a gentle and light-hearted boy, while Tim was easily the mischievous one. One day, Tim printed a promise that said he would never do anything naughty again. Sam knew deep down that this was too good to be true and warned Tim about it. But Tim laughed and said that a promise was a promise, so he would keep it. Time passed and it seemed like Tim really did stick to his promise. Sam was proud of his brother and felt lighter knowing that he kept his promise. Yet, one day, when the brothers ventured to a nearby park, Tim's mischievous nature got the best of him when he saw a swinging vine. Thinking he was still safe because he made a promise, Tim grabbed the vine and began to swing. Unfortunately, the vine broke and Tim fell hard to the ground. The moral of the story is that it is important to remember that words are only as trustworthy as the person who said them. Even if someone makes a promise, it's better to remain on the safe side.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She loved to help her friends. One day, Lily saw a pretty bell. She knew it would be a great gift for her friend, Tim. Lily went to find Tim. She said, "Tim, I have a gift for you!" Tim was very happy. He loved the pretty bell. But he had a problem. He could not find a match for his sock. Lily wanted to help Tim. She looked around and found a sock that was the same. She said, "Here, Tim, I found a match for your sock!" Tim was very happy. He thanked Lily for being a good friend. Now, Tim had a pretty bell and a match for his sock. He knew Lily was a compassionate friend who always helped him. They played together all day, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom. One day, his mom said, "Tim, let's go to the grocery store." Tim was very excited. At the grocery store, Tim and his mom picked up many things. When they were done, they walked to the door. Tim saw that the door was open. He said, "Mom, I will close the door." His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim." On their way home, Tim's mom told him that he was very successful at helping her today. Tim felt happy and proud. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel good and successful.

Once upon a time, there was a big bathtub in a house. The bathtub was always available for the family to use. In this family, there was a little boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim and his mom were going to take a bath. They filled the bathtub with warm water and bubbles. Tim trusted his mom to make the bath fun. They played with toys and laughed a lot. Then, something unexpected happened. A little duck appeared in the bathtub! Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know where the duck came from, but they decided to let the duck join their bath. They all played together and had a great time.
