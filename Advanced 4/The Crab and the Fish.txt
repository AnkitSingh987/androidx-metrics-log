Title: The Crab and the Fish

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little crab. The crab had a small shell. The crab wanted to gain a big shell. So, the crab went on a trip to find a big shell. The crab saw a big shell near a fierce fish. The fish was big and had sharp teeth. The crab was scared, but it wanted the big shell. The crab thought of a plan to get the big shell. The crab waited until the fierce fish was asleep. Then, it moved very slow and quiet to the big shell. The crab got the big shell and ran away fast. The fierce fish did not wake up. The crab was very happy to gain a big shell.

Once upon a time, there was a perfect sunflower. It lived in a big garden with many other flowers. They were all friends, but the sunflower was the tallest and most beautiful of them all. One day, a small bee came to the garden. The bee saw the perfect sunflower and wanted to take some of its nectar. The sunflower didn't like this, so it asked the bee a question. "Why do you want my nectar?" The bee said, "I need it to make honey for my family." The sunflower thought about it and decided to let the bee take some of its nectar. The bee was happy and the sunflower felt good for helping a friend. From that day on, the bee and the sunflower became best friends and always helped each other when they needed it.

Once there was a little girl. She played all day in her garden. She loved tying bows on strings. One day she heard a voice in the trees. It was a bossy voice and she wanted to know who it was. She crept closer, but she couldn't see anyone. Suddenly a branch moved and out popped a little bunny! The bunny had something in her mouth. It was a length of string and she wanted the little girl to tie a bow on it. The little girl was so happy. She carefully tied the bow and then showed the bunny. The bunny wiggled its nose in approval, before hopping back into the trees. The little girl couldn't believe it. She never knew there was a bossy bunny in her garden! Now, every morning she would tie bows and wait to see her bunny friend.

Monkey was eager to measure something. He thought and thought about what to measure. Then he had an idea. He went to his mom and asked if he could measure her zip. Mom said yes, and showed Monkey how to measure it. Monkey found a ruler and measured the zip from the top to the bottom. He was so proud of himself when he saw the number one hundred show on the ruler. Mom liked Monkey so much for being so eager to measure. She gave him a hug and said, “I’m so proud of you for wanting to measure.” Monkey smiled, feeling very proud of himself. He couldn't wait to measure something else!

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a cart. The cart was very fancy. Sue loved her fancy cart very much. One day, Sue went to the park. She saw a boy named Tim. Tim liked to run. Tim saw Sue's fancy cart and wanted to play with it. Sue said yes, but told Tim to be careful. Tim started to run with the fancy cart. He ran too fast and the cart broke. Sue was sad. Tim said sorry and helped Sue fix the cart. Sue and Tim became friends. The moral of the story is to be careful with other people's things and to always say sorry when you make a mistake.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch movies on his TV. One day, he found a new movie that he had never seen before. He was very excited to watch it. As Tim started watching the movie, something unexpected happened. The movie began to play in reverse! Tim saw the end of the movie first, then the middle, and finally the beginning. He was very confused but kept watching. When the movie finished, Tim thought about what he saw. He realized that watching the movie in reverse made it even more fun! The plot twist at the end was now at the beginning, and it made the movie even more exciting. Tim decided that sometimes, doing things in a different way can be a great adventure.
