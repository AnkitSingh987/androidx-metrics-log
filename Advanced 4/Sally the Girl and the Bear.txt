Title: Sally the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to play in her room with her toys. One day, she saw a big cupboard in her room. She wanted to open it and see what was inside. Sally opened the cupboard and found a toy bear. The toy bear could talk! "Hello, I am Benny the Bear," said the bear. Sally was so happy to meet Benny. They played together all day long. But then, Sally got mad at Benny because he took her favorite toy. She said, "Benny, you are not polite. Give it back!" Benny felt sad and said, "I am so sorry, Sally. I didn't know it was your favorite toy. Please forgive me." Sally thought about it and decided to forgive Benny. They hugged each other and played happily again. The moral of the story is that it is important to be polite and ask for forgiveness when we make mistakes. When we forgive others, we can be happy together.

One day, a little boy was very excited. He ran out of his house to the park. When he saw the park, he was so excited he started jumping up and down. He saw a giant in the park. The giant looked fun and happy, so the little boy ran towards it. When he got closer, he was even more excited! The giant had no arms, but the little boy didn't care. He grabbed the giant and ran around the park with it. The little boy was so happy! He spun, laughed and played with the giant until it was time to go home. The little boy said goodbye to his big new friend and went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a brave little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to swing on vines and play with his friends all day long. He was very happy and always had a big smile on his face. One day, Momo saw a big red ball stuck high up in a tree. He wanted to get the ball for his friends to play with. Momo thought he could use a long vine to reach the ball. So, he found a vine and started to swing, higher and higher. Momo was brave and swung as high as he could. He reached the big red ball and grabbed it with his little hands. Momo's friends cheered and clapped for him. They were so happy to have the ball to play with. Momo felt proud and smiled even bigger. And they all played with the big red ball, having lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a reliable little bunny. He loved to bounce around the garden full of celery. Every day he would explore, sniffing celery stalks and hopping around the garden. He enjoyed jumping, playing and running in the sunshine. When he got tired he would find a celery stalk, lean against it and nibble it as he rested. He loved it so much that he would even bounce off his celery stalk in the air and land on all fours again. Once in a while he would invite his friends over and they would all have a bounce around in the garden together. Though the celery leaves were a bit itchy, it didn't stop them from having lots of fun. The reliable bunny continued to bounce and play in the garden, never getting tired of his favorite pastime. And so he remained happy and content in the garden - surrounded by his reliable friends, singing and bouncing around the celery stalks.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a belt. The belt was dull. Tim wanted a new belt. He asked his mom for a new belt. But his mom said no. One day, Tim saw a shiny belt on a tree. He took the shiny belt and left his dull belt behind. Tim was very happy with his new belt. He showed it to his friends. They all liked it too. Later, Tim saw a girl crying. She lost her shiny belt. Tim felt bad. He gave the girl her shiny belt back. The girl was happy. Tim went back to the tree and found his dull belt. He put it on and felt good. Tim learned that it is better to be honest and happy with what you have.
