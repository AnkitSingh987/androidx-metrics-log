Title: Tim and the Book

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a hard thing in his room. It was a book, but not just any book. It was a novel that his mom had. Tim was not supposed to touch it, but he wanted to see what was inside. One day, when his mom was not looking, Tim took the novel and opened it. He touched the pages and looked at the words. But, something unexpected happened. The words started to move and dance on the pages! Tim was so surprised and scared. He quickly closed the book and put it back. Tim learned that he should not touch things that he was not supposed to. The next time he wanted to look at a book, he asked his mom first. The moral of the story is to always ask before touching things that are not yours.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. He was three years old and loved to clap his hands. His mom was so proud of him and she always said, "Jack, your clapping is so amazing!" Jack enjoyed clapping even more and it became his favorite thing to do. One day, while Jack was clapping, he noticed something very strange on his neck. He asked his mom, "Mommy, what's that on my neck?" She took a look and said, "That's amazing! It's an extra hand!" Sure enough, there was a tiny hand growing out of his neck! Jack was so excited, he started clapping with one hand on his neck and the other at his side. From then on he was clapping with both hands all the time! He was the only one with two hands so he could clap twice as hard! Everyone said it was amazing!

Once there was a boy called Mo who loved playing in the garden. His favourite game was to make a twist. He would take his two hands and twist them around and around until it felt like a knot. One day, Mo was playing in the garden when he heard a voice. It said, "Can you help me escape?" Mo looked around but he couldn't see anyone. He saw a friendly flower smiling up at him and asked, "Where are you hiding?" The flower replied, "I can twist my petals around like you do with your hands, but I'm hurt and need help to escape." Mo was worried for the flower and wanted to help it. He put his hands around the flower and started to twist until he made a knot. He gently unwound each petal until the flower was free. The flower thanked Mo and said, "Thank you for helping me escape and setting me free!" Mo was happy that he could help the flower and danced around the garden, twisting and untwisting his hands in celebration.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. There was a cow, a pig, a horse, and a sheep. They all lived happily in the barn. The barn was their original home. One day, the cow said, "I want to take a walk outside." The pig, the horse, and the sheep all wanted to go too. So, they all took a walk together. They walked and walked, and they saw many new things. Suddenly, they saw a big, green tree. It was not like the other trees they had seen before. They walked closer to the tree, and they heard a voice. "Hello, friends!" said the tree. The animals were very surprised. They did not know that trees could talk. The tree asked, "Do you like my home?" The animals said, "Yes, it is very pretty." The tree smiled and said, "Thank you. I want to show you something." The tree opened a secret door on its trunk. Inside the door, there was a beautiful room full of toys and treats. The animals were so happy! They played with the toys and ate the treats. After a while, they thanked the tree and went back to their big red barn. They told all their friends about the talking tree and the secret room. From that day on, the animals and the tree became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
