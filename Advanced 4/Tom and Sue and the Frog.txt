Title: Tom and Sue and the Frog

One day, a helpful cat named Tom saw his friend, a little girl named Sue, in the kitchen. Sue was trying to cook something on the stove. Tom wanted to help Sue, so he went to the kitchen. Tom said, "Sue, let me help you. I will put the pot on the stove for you." Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom! You are very helpful." Tom put the pot on the stove and Sue started to cook. But then, something unexpected happened. The pot started to shake and make a loud noise. Sue and Tom were scared. They did not know what was in the pot. Suddenly, the pot opened and out came a big, friendly frog! The frog said, "Hello, Sue and Tom! I am here to help you cook!" Sue, Tom, and the frog laughed and cooked together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lazy dog named Max. Max loved to sleep all day and did not like to complete his tasks. His owner, a kind man, always asked Max to help him find gold, but Max never listened. One sunny day, Max's owner went to the forest to find gold. He asked Max to come with him, but Max was too lazy. He stayed at home and slept. The kind man went alone to the forest, hoping to find gold and make their life better. Sadly, the kind man got lost in the forest. He could not find his way back home. Max woke up and realized his owner was not there. He felt bad for being lazy and not helping the kind man. Max tried to find his owner, but it was too late. The kind man was never found, and Max was all alone, wishing he had not been so lazy.

Once upon a time, there was a big hole in the ground. This hole was called the underground. Many animals lived in the underground. They were all friends and played together all day. One day, a little rabbit was careless. He lost his toy in the underground. The rabbit was very sad. All his friends tried to find the toy, but they could not find it. The rabbit cried and cried. Then, a wise old owl told the rabbit to value his friends more than his toy. The rabbit listened and started to play with his friends again. They laughed and had fun together. The rabbit learned that friends are more important than toys.

Once upon a time there was a faucet in a kitchen. The faucet was very cheerful, even when it was off. Suddenly, the faucet was turned on! Water came out and it swished and swirled all around the kitchen. The faucet was very excited and it smiled. But then, the faucet was turned off again. The water stopped, the kitchen was quiet. The faucet was sad, but it was very cheerful in another way. Because it knew that the water had made the kitchen clean and that it was the end of the cleaning. Now the kitchen was sparkling clean! Everyone was very happy and the faucet was cheerful again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little boy named Peter. Peter had the biggest heart and he always wanted to do something nice for others. One day he asked his mom if he could help a neighbour. His mom said yes and they went outside to the house next door. When they knocked on the door, a friendly old man answered. Peter said to him, "I would like to request if I can help you with anything." The old man smiled and said, "That's very kind of you. If you really want to help, you can weed this garden for me." Peter was so excited and he said, "Yes. I want to do it. It will be True." The old man was happy and thanked Peter for his kind offer. Together, they got to work in the garden and soon it was all cleaned up. After it was finished, the old man offered Peter something sweet as a reward. Peter said no, but smiled and said it was his pleasure to help someone. The old man was so impressed with Peter's kind heart and kind request.
