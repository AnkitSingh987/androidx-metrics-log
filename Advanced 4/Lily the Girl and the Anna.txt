Title: Lily the Girl and the Anna

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sister named Anna. They loved to play together. One day, Anna fell down and hurt her knee. Lily was sad to see her sister in pain. Lily went to her mom and said, "Mom, Anna is hurt. Can you help her?" Mom came and looked at Anna's knee. She put a bandage on it to help it heal. "Don't worry, Lily. Anna will feel better soon," Mom said. Later, Anna was hungry. Lily wanted to help her sister feel happy. She went to the kitchen and made a peanut butter and jelly sandwich for Anna. Anna ate the sandwich and smiled. "Thank you, Lily," Anna said. They played together again, happy and full.

Once upon a time, in a big wood, there lived a patient cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play and catch things. Every day, she would run and jump in the wood. One sunny day, Kitty saw a big, red ball. She wanted to catch it. So, she ran and ran, but the ball kept rolling away. Kitty was very patient and did not give up. Finally, after a long time, Kitty caught the big, red ball. She was so happy! Kitty took the ball back to the wood and played with it all day long. And from that day on, Kitty and the big, red ball were the best of friends.

One summer morning, John and his mom went for a walk. His mom pointed to a big, tall tree and said, “Look, John. That enormous tree is so tall!” John was amazed by the tree’s size. He decided he wanted to go and explore the tree. When they got closer, he suddenly saw something strange in the tree’s roots. It looked like a tiny closet door. John was curious and asked his mom what it was. She said she didn’t know, but it was time to go home. As they walked away, John kept looking back at the closet. The next morning, John woke up early and rushed to the tree. When he arrived, the closet was gone! He was surprised and a little scared. What happened to the closet? John kept going back to the tree, trying to find the closet. Eventually, he gave up and started to walk home. Suddenly, he saw a flash of light, like a mini sun. It was coming from the same place as the closet! John walked closer, and saw the closet reappearing into the ground. It was an amazing sight. John realized that the closet had disappeared and reappeared! He went back home happy and excited, telling his mom all about his adventure with the enormous, magical closet.

Once there was a patient mouse. He liked to quietly explore his world. One day he heard something interesting outside his door. He was curious, so he slowly crept to the door and opened it. Much to his surprise, he saw a big event! He saw a lot of people and lots of noise. He was a little scared, so he quickly closed the door. He decided to be patient and he peered through the crack of the door. He could see lots of people enjoying themselves and having a great time. He even heard some music and laughter. He was happy and excited! The mouse wanted to join them, so he cautiously opened the door and crept out. Everyone was so surprised to see him, but they welcomed him with open arms. The mouse had a wonderful time and enjoyed the event. He was very patient and made lots of friends. At the end of the night, he closed the door behind him, happy and content.

Lily and Ben were going to see a show with their mom and dad. They were very happy and excited. They put on their coats and mittens and got in the car. Mom said the show was about animals that could sing and dance. When they got to the theater, they found their seats and waited for the show to start. The lights went down and the music began. Lily and Ben saw a big lion, a funny monkey, a pretty bird, and a cute mouse on the stage. They sang songs and did tricks and made everyone laugh. Lily and Ben clapped their hands and said "Wow!" and "Yay!" They liked the show very much. But then, a bad man came on the stage. He had a big net and a mean face. He wanted to catch the animals and take them away. He chased the lion, the monkey, the bird, and the mouse. He was very fast and loud. Lily and Ben were scared and sad. They did not like the bad man. The animals were smart and brave. They worked together and tricked the bad man. They made him fall and drop his net. They tied him up with the net and put a mitten on his mouth. They said "No!" and "Boo!" to the bad man. They ran away and hid behind the curtain. Lily and Ben cheered and clapped their hands. They liked the animals very much. The lights came back on and the curtain opened. The animals came out and bowed. They smiled and waved to the audience. The audience clapped and applauded. They liked the show very much. Lily and Ben hugged their mom and dad. They said "Thank you!" and "We love you!" They were very happy and proud. They had a good time.
