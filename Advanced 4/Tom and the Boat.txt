Title: Tom and the Boat

One day, Tom went to visit his friend Sam. They liked to play at Sam's house. Sam had a big sink in his kitchen. They both were kind boys. "Let's play with the boats in the sink," said Tom. Sam said, "Yes, I like that." They filled the sink with water and put their toy boats in it. They pretended the sink was a big ocean. As they played, Tom's boat got stuck in the sink. Sam was kind and helped him. They laughed and had fun. After playing, they dried the sink and put their toys away. Tom said, "Thank you, Sam, for letting me visit and play with the sink." Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. I'm glad we are kind friends." And from that day on, Tom and Sam continued to visit each other and play with their toys. They knew that being kind made their friendship even more special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to spend her days outside, playing with her best friend, a bluebird named Blueberry. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. One day, Lily felt frustrated because she could not find Blueberry anywhere. "Where are you, Blueberry?" Lily called out. She looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and even in her own backyard. She was about to give up when she heard a tiny voice. "Help, Lily!" cried Blueberry. Lily followed the sound and found Blueberry stuck in a big, sticky spider web. Lily was scared, but she knew she had to help her friend. With all her strength, she pulled and pulled until Blueberry was free. Just then, something unexpected happened. Blueberry started to grow bigger and bigger. Soon, Blueberry was as big as a horse! Lily was so surprised. "Wow, Blueberry! You are so big now!" she said. Blueberry smiled and said, "Now we can have even more fun together!" Lily climbed onto Blueberry's back, and they flew high into the sky, laughing and playing all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play by a peaceful stream near his house. One day, he found a big stick by the stream. He thought it would be fun to hold the stick and play with it. Tim played with the stick all day long. He held it tight and swung it around. He even used it to draw pictures in the dirt. Tim knew he would need the stick for something important later, so he kept it with him all the time. One day, while Tim was playing by the stream, he saw a small kitten stuck in a tree. The kitten was scared and couldn't climb down. Tim knew just what to do. He held the big stick and reached up high to help the kitten. The kitten grabbed onto the stick and Tim gently pulled it down. The kitten was safe, and Tim was happy he had kept the stick. They played by the peaceful stream together, and Tim knew that holding onto the stick was the best choice he ever made.

John was very excited. He was ready to go on an aeroplane for the first time in his life. His mum and dad were very proud of him. He got onto the aeroplane and the steward showed him to his seat. John's tummy was doing funny flips but he was determined to mind his manners into the sky. John looked out of the window as the aeroplane took off. He saw clouds and buildings and cheerfully shouted "LOOK MUM!" as the plane rattled away from the ground. John loved every minute of his aeroplane ride and was sad when they finally touched down. He promised himself he would always mind when it was time to go flying in an aeroplane again.

Once upon a time there was a brave girl called Lola. She had long, curly hair and was always alert. One day, she was walking through the forest when she discovered a big castle. She wanted to explore, so she went forward bravely. As she approached, she noticed a big wooden door with a lock. She thought to herself, "I can unlock this door!". She reached into her pocket and took out a bow. Suddenly, a friendly voice called out, "Hello! Who's there?" Lola saw a dragon at the door. She said nervously, "Hi! My name is Lola. I have a bow to help me unlock the door". The dragon smiled and said, "Come in Lola. I will give you a magical key to help you unlock the door". Lola was so happy she couldn't believe it! She took the key and quickly unlocked the door. Then, Lola explored the castle by herself. What a brave girl!
