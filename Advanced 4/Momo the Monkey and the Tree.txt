Title: Momo the Monkey and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a cute little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to play with his friends all day long. They would swing from tree to tree, laughing and having fun. One day, Momo's friend, Lulu the parrot, said, "Momo, let's go find some yummy fruit to eat!" So, they went on a fun adventure to find the sweetest fruit in the jungle. They found a big, juicy fruit on a tall tree. But, another friend, Toto the elephant, came and said, "Don't eat that fruit! It will spoil your dinner." Momo and Lulu listened to Toto and decided to wait until dinner time to eat the fruit. At dinner time, all the friends sat together and shared the big, juicy fruit. It was so yummy, and they were happy they didn't spoil their dinner. Momo, Lulu, and Toto played and ate until the sun went down, and then they went to sleep, dreaming of more fun adventures in the jungle.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a broken toy dinosaur in the park. He picked it up and said, "Oh no! This dinosaur is broken. I want to fix it." Tim took the dinosaur home and showed it to his mom. She said, "Let's try to fix the broken dinosaur together." They got some glue and fixed the dinosaur's leg. It was not broken anymore. The next day, Tim went back to the park with the dinosaur. He saw a little girl crying. Tim asked, "Why are you sad?" The girl said, "I lost my dinosaur." Tim smiled and said, "I found a dinosaur and fixed it. Is this yours?" The girl looked at the dinosaur and said, "Yes! Thank you for fixing it!" Tim was happy to return the dinosaur to its owner, and they played together at the park.

Anna and Ben love to help their mom prepare dinner. They put on their aprons and wash their hands. Mom gives them each a bowl and a spoon. She says they can make a salad. Anna and Ben tear the lettuce into small pieces. They put them in their bowls. Mom adds some carrots, tomatoes and cheese. She lets them toss the salad with some dressing. They feel like chefs. Mom says dinner will be ready in a few minutes. She puts some chicken and potatoes in the oven. She tells Anna and Ben they can set the table. They take out the plates, forks, knives and napkins. They arrange them neatly on the table. They are proud of their work. Mom takes out the chicken and potatoes from the oven. They smell delicious. She puts them on a big platter. She brings the salad and the platter to the table. She says they can sit down and enjoy their meal. Anna and Ben are hungry and excited. They thank mom for the incredible dinner. They taste the chicken, the potatoes and the salad. They say they are yummy. They eat until they are full. They tell mom they love her. Mom smiles and hugs them. She says they are the best helpers ever.

John and Emma were the best of friends. They went on lots of adventures together. One day, the two decided to go on a hike to look for hidden treasure. They found a cozy spot under a tree to seat and said, "Let's go find some treasure! That would be so much fun". John and Emma searched and searched, but nothing was found. Suddenly, Emma spotted something shiny in the ground. When she pulled it out, she discovered it was a large chest. With a smile, she said, "This is the treasure we were searching for!" John and Emma both grabbed the chest and tried to seat it. But when they opened the treasure, a giant beast jumped out. The beast was angry and roared at them both. John was scared and said, "Run!" But it was too late. The beast grabbed Emma and flew away. John was so sad. He had lost his friend and the treasure was gone. John learnt a lesson to be more careful on his next adventurous journey.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside in her wide backyard. One sunny day, she went out to play and found a small pebble on the ground. The pebble was smooth and shiny. Amy liked the pebble and put it in her pocket. As Amy played, she met a boy named Tim. Tim saw the pebble in Amy's pocket and wanted it. He tried to take the pebble from her pocket, but Amy did not want to give it to him. They both wanted the pebble and started to get mad at each other. Amy and Tim thought about it and decided to share the pebble. They took turns holding the pebble and playing with it. They became good friends and learned that sharing made them both happy. From that day on, Amy and Tim always shared their toys and played together in the wide backyard.
