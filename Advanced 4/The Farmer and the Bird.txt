Title: The Farmer and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who lived on his own farm. He loved his farm and he worked very hard to make it bright and happy. Every day he would wake up early and prepare the fields so the crops would grow. One day, he decided to take a walk around the farm. As he was walking, he saw something strange caught in the branches of a tall tree. It was a little bird! The farmer called out to it and said, "Hello there! Are you OK?" The bird was so scared but politely responded, "Yes, I am OK. I got stuck in these branches and I can't get down." The farmer smiled and said, "Don't worry. I am here to help you. Let me prepare a ladder so I can put it against the tree. Climb down and then fly away safely". The bird thanked the farmer, "Thank you so much! You have been very kind." The farmer waved goodbye to the little bird as it happily flew away and he returned to his job of preparing the farm. The farmer had been very polite, and he was glad that he could help the little bird.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little duck. This duck lived near a big lake. The duck loved to swim and play with his friends all day long. One day, the duck saw that the lake was dirty. He wanted to help, so he started to clean the lake. His friends saw him and decided to help too. They all worked together to complete the job. Soon, the lake was clean and pretty again. The duck and his friends learned that working together can make a big difference. They also learned that keeping their home clean is very important. From that day on, they all promised to take care of the lake and keep it clean.

Once there was a bird named Sally. She was very lonely. Every day she watched all the other birds fly around and play, but she could not. Sally was sad and wished she could fly. One day, she saw a lamp in the corner of her cage. It was glowing with a warm light. She wanted to get up close. She flapped her wings and hopped nearer to it. Suddenly, the lamp started to twinkle. Something magical was happening. The lamp's light started to wrap around Sally. She was lifted off the ground and began to fly! Sally flew faster and faster around her cage. It felt incredible. She flew around the other birds and they watched in amazement. For the first time, Sally wasn't lonely.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear. He was very loyal and always stayed close to his friends. One day, he went for a walk in the woods. As he walked, he noticed a big, safe cave. He was excited, so he decided to explore the cave. Inside the cave, he saw some animals. Some of them were smaller than he was, but some were bigger. All the animals were very friendly. He knew he was safe here. He decided to stay with his new friends. They quickly grew to love and trust him, because he was so loyal. They were so happy to have him that they decided to make him the leader. Every day, they played together and talked about their day. The little bear was so happy. He felt safe and loved in this cave full of loyal friends.

Once there was a little girl named Daisy. Every night before she went to sleep she would dream of something fun. One night Daisy dreamed of a hot summer day. She was in a pool with lots of toys. She could feel the hot sun on her skin and the cool water around her. Suddenly she woke up. She saw her mommy standing over her bed. Her mommy asked, “Daisy, what did you dream about?” Daisy replied, “I dreamed of a hot summer day with toys in the pool. It was fun!” Her mommy smiled at Daisy and said, “That sounds like a wonderful dream! I’m so glad you rest well.” Daisy snuggled back into her bed, ready for the next dream. She was sure it was going to be even more fun than swimming in the pool!
