Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy went with her mom to visit her grandma. They wanted to be polite, so they brought a big dessert for everyone to share. It was a yummy chocolate cake with lots of frosting. When they got to grandma's house, Lucy saw a big dog in the yard. The dog wanted the cake. Lucy struggled to hold the cake high, so the dog could not get it. She said, "No, dog! This is for grandma!" Just then, the dog spoke! He said, "I am sorry, Lucy. I just love cake so much." Lucy was shocked. She never knew dogs could talk. She decided to share a little piece of cake with the dog. They all had a great time, and the dog was polite and said thank you.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly frog. This frog loved to play jazz. He had a little drum and a horn. Every day, he would play his jazz music near a pond. One day, a little girl came to the pond. She saw the ugly frog playing his jazz music. She liked the music and started to dance. The frog was happy to see her dance. The little girl and the ugly frog became good friends. They played jazz music and danced together every day. They learned to value their friendship, even though the frog was ugly. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a family who lived in a small hut in the forest. They were a happy family. One day, the father and mother were walking in the forest when the mother saw something shiny and sparkly. She bent down and picked it up, and saw that it was a beautiful gem. The father and mother looked at each other and smiled. The father said: "Let's offer this gem to the big tree, to thank it for giving us shade and protection." So they went back to the hut, and the father took a gentle hammer and chisel, and carefully carved a hole in the big tree. Then they put the gem in the hole and thanked the tree. The tree was so pleased that it started to glimmer and sparkle and all the leaves around it rattled in appreciation. The family were so happy and hugged each other, and thanked the tree for its generous gift. They stayed in their little hut for many years, until eventually they moved away. But they never forgot the gentle offering from the big tree in the forest.

One day a little boy named Leo was out in his garden. He saw a worm crawling across the grass and bent down to have a closer look. "Hello!" he said to the worm. The worm said nothing and kept crawling. Leo was disappointed and said, "I wish you were obedient". Just then, he felt a tickle in his nose. He put his hand up to cover it and let out a big sneeze. The worm was so startled it froze in its tracks. Leo looked up at the worm in surprise. He thought, "Maybe that blew some obedience into it." He lowered his hand and smiled. The worm was now obedient and followed Leo as he ran around the garden, chasing butterflies and picking flowers. Leo beamed with happiness and looked down at his new friend. He knew they were going to have a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird liked to fly and sing. One day, the bird saw a tool on the ground. The tool was shiny and pretty. The bird wanted to play with the tool. The bird picked up the tool in its beak and started to rush back to its nest. On the way, the bird saw a big tree. The tree was very tall and had lots of leaves. The bird thought it would be fun to hide the tool in the tree. As the bird hid the tool in the tree, something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake and laugh! The tree was not a tree at all, but a big, friendly giant! The giant said, "Thank you, little bird, for giving me back my tool!" The bird and the giant became good friends and played together every day.
