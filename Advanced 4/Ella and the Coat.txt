Title: Ella and the Coat

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl. Her name was Ella and she was very brave. Every morning, Ella would go outside to play. In the fields near her house, she would see crops of wheat. As she walked by them, she would shiver in the morning air. But, Ella was persistent. She kept going, even if it was cold. One morning, when she was feeling very brave and persistent, Ella made a startling discovery. In the middle of the wheat field, there was a tiny animal named Frank. Frank was shivering from the cold and Ella felt sorry for him. So, Ella took off her warm, fuzzy coat and put it around Frank. She buttoned it up tight and watched as he stopped shivering. Then, she petted him gently so that he knew he wasn't alone. Frank was so grateful that he stayed with Ella for the rest of the day. Every now and again, he would give her a tiny hug to say thank you. This was Ella's little secret - her persistent kindness had saved a day from the cold.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat and play all day. One day, Tom heard about a big event in the park. The event would include lots of food and games. Tom was very happy and wanted to go. At the event, Tom saw many of his friends. They all played together and ate lots of tasty food. Tom was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. All the food was gone, and Tom was still hungry. Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, his friend, a small bird, told him about a secret place with more food. Tom and his friends went to the secret place and found lots of yummy food. They all ate and played until they were full and tired. In the end, Tom was not sad anymore and had a great day with his friends at the big event.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she found a pin on the floor. She knew it was sharp, so she wanted to give it to her mom. Lily felt impatient. She wanted her mom to see the pin right away. She started to run to her mom, but then she remembered what her mom always said: "Don't run with sharp things!" So she slowed down and walked carefully. When Lily gave the pin to her mom, her mom was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Lily, for being safe and not running with the pin. You are a good girl." Lily felt proud and learned that it is important to be patient and safe.

Mia was playing with her new toy boat in the bath. It was navy blue and had pink stripes painted on the side. She smiled and put it in the water. But as she watched it float away, her smile soon faded. It had reached the edge of the bath and was about to finish in the water. "No! Don't finish, boat!" she shouted. She tried to reach out, but it was too far away. Suddenly, her mom came into the bathroom. She saw the boat in the water and quickly grabbed it before it could finish. Mia frowned, wishing she could have been the one to save her boat. But her mom gave her a hug and said, "It's okay, Mia. I'm here to help you finish." And she put the boat back in the bath and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to match her clothes. She thought it was fun to have a style. She wanted to be successful at matching her clothes. One day, Amy met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "Amy, I like your style. Let's match our clothes together." Amy was happy to hear that. They decided to wear red shirts and blue pants. They looked great together. But, the next day, something unexpected happened. Amy and Tim wore the same clothes again, but everyone else was wearing green shirts and yellow pants. Amy and Tim felt silly. They laughed and said, "Maybe we should try a new style tomorrow." So, the next day, they wore green shirts and yellow pants like everyone else. They still had fun matching their clothes, and they learned that it's okay to try new styles.
