Title: The Girl Called Lily and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lily. One day Lily wanted to explore the world. She asked her mom if she could take a walk by herself. Mom said yes, but reminded her to be careful. So, Lily set off. On her way, she passed a big, dry field. The sun was shining and it was very quiet. But then Lily heard a noise in the distance. Curious, she decided to see what it was. She walked up to the top of a hill. Far away she could see a small house. The noise was coming from there. Lily decided to take a closer look, so she started walking towards it. As she came nearer, the noise got louder and louder. Finally, she reached the small house. Inside, she could see a man speaking. She didn't like the sound of it and was very scared. Quickly, she started to back away. But then, the man saw her. He started to shout and wave his arms. Lily was so scared that she ran away as fast as her legs could carry her. She ran and ran until she reached home. But from that day on, Lily never went outside to explore by herself. The noise she'd heard in the dry field had scared her so much that she never wanted to go back.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a happy tutor. She loved to help little kids learn. Every day, she would seat the kids around a big table and teach them. One day, a new kid came to the town. He was very shy and scared. The happy tutor saw him and asked him to join the other kids at the table. The new kid was afraid, but the tutor was kind and helped him find a seat. As the days went by, the new kid started to learn and have fun with the others. He was not shy or scared anymore. The happy tutor was proud of him. All the kids learned that when they help each other, they can all be happy and learn together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl with long brown hair. Her name was Ella and she was three-years-old. One evening, Ella was playing in her bedroom. She saw her daddy coming with a big parcel and he said: "Look what I got for you, Ella!" Ella was delighted and tore open the parcel. Inside she discovered a beautiful doll with long golden hair. Ella hugged it tightly and said: "Thanks, Daddy!" Daddy said: "Now, let’s close the parcel with a neat knot." Ella watched as Daddy tied a very tidy knot and secured the parcel. Then Daddy said: "Next time someone sends you a present, make sure to close it properly with a knot!". Ella laughed and said: "I will, daddy". And from that day on, Ella remembered to close all her presents with neat knots.

Alice wanted to help her mom in the garden. She saw some pretty flowers and wanted to help mum plant them. She asked mum how she could help. Mum smiled and said,"Alice, why don't you help me lift these heavy bags of soil?" Alice eagerly agreed and put all her strength into it. She put a lot of effort, but she only managed to lift one bag. Mum said," Don't worry Alice, with some patient and continuous practice you'll become very strong. " Alice continued playing and exploring in the garden until she noticed how tough it was to pull weeds from the soil. She tried very hard to pull them out but it wasn't easy. Mum said, " Just as lifting a heavy bag of soil at first was difficult, it's also tough to pull out the weeds. If you keep trying and be patient, things will become easier. " Alice looked at her mum and smiled. She ran over to the weeds and started pulling them out, eventually succeeding because she was determined to prove mum right.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in all kinds of weather. One sunny day, Tim went to the park and saw a little girl playing with a ball. Tim went near the girl and knelt down. "Hi," said Tim, "Can I play with you?" The little girl looked at Tim and smiled. "Yes, you can play with me," she said. They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. After a while, the weather changed, and it started to rain. Tim and the girl ran to a big tree to stay dry. They sat under the tree and talked until the rain stopped. Then, they went back to playing with the ball. At the end of the day, Tim and the girl became good friends.
