Title: Lily the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big yellow ball in the sky. The yellow ball was the sun. Lily liked the sun because it made her feel warm and happy. As Lily walked, she found a picture on the ground. The picture was of a happy family with a mom, a dad, and a little boy. "I wonder who this family is," Lily said out loud. She wanted to find the family and give them back their picture. Lily asked her friend, Tom, "Do you know who is in this picture?" Tom looked at the picture and said, "Yes, that's Sam's family!" So, Lily and Tom went to Sam's house and gave him the picture. Sam was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily and Tom!" They all played together and had a fun day under the big yellow sun.

Once there was a little girl named Daisy. She was very busy during the day, but at night her house was quiet. One night, Daisy heard a funny noise coming from the living room. She crept out of her bedroom and went to take a look. In the middle of the living room, she found a big bouncy ball! It was hopping around and bouncing off the furniture. Daisy smiled and couldn't resist having a go. She grabbed the ball and started to bounce it around the room. The ball was so much fun! Before long, Daisy was having a bouncing party! She was bouncing all around the room and having a wonderful time. Her parents watched and smiled, feeling happy that their little girl was enjoying herself so much. Daisy's bouncing party went on and on until she was too tired to bounce anymore. She gathered up the ball and went back to her bedroom, happy in the knowledge that she had a lot of bouncing fun that night.

Once upon a time, in a small, humble house, there lived a little boy named Tim. He had a toy ghost that he loved to play with every day. The toy ghost was white and soft, and Tim took it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim's friend, Lily, came over to play. She saw the toy ghost and asked, "Can I borrow your ghost, please?" Tim thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, but please be careful with it." Lily was very happy and played with the ghost all day. As the sun went down, it was time for Lily to go home. She gave the toy ghost back to Tim and said, "Thank you for letting me borrow your ghost. I had so much fun!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome! We can play with the ghost again next time." They both waved goodbye, and Tim went inside his humble house, hugging his toy ghost tightly.

Once upon a time, in a land full of fun, there was a big, round ball. This ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce up and down, left and right, and all around. The ball was very tough, so it never got hurt when it bounced. One sunny day, the ball found a new friend. This friend was a spring. The spring was very good at jumping high into the sky. The ball and the spring played together all day long. They would bounce and jump, and laugh and sing. As the sun went down, the ball and the spring felt very happy. They knew they would be friends forever. They promised to always have fun and to help each other when things got tough. And from that day on, the ball and the spring played together every day, bouncing and jumping in their land of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little boy named Tim. He loved to play games all by himself. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play his own game. At the park, Tim saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she had no one to play with. Tim walked up to her and asked, "Do you want to play my game with me?" Sue's face lit up, and she said, "Yes, I would love to play your game!" So Tim and Sue played the game together. They had so much fun, and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim and Sue always played games together, and they were never quiet or lonely again.
