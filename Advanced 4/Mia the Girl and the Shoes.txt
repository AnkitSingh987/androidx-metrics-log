Once upon a time, there was a dark machine. It was old and made a lot of noise. The people in the house were scared of it. One night, the machine stopped working. No one knew how to fix it. Everyone was sad. The next day, a big man came to the house. He knew lots about machines. He had a big box of tools with him. He looked at the machine and fixed it. The family was happy. Suddenly, the machine started to work again. The lights came on and it made a buzzing sound. At the end, the man fixed the machine, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pet cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was an original cat because she had one blue eye and one green eye. Sue loved Fluffy very much. One day, Sue wanted to mail a letter to her friend, Tom. She asked Fluffy, "Do you want to come with me to mail the letter?" Fluffy meowed happily, so they walked to the mailbox together. Sue put the letter in the mailbox and Fluffy jumped up to help push it in. On their way back home, Sue and Fluffy saw a big, red ball. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Sue said, "I'm glad you came with me to mail the letter, Fluffy. We had a fun day!" Fluffy meowed and rubbed against Sue's leg, happy to have spent the day with her best friend.

One day, a boy named Tim found a penny on the ground. Tim thought the penny was very fine. He showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a fine penny!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Let's prepare to save it in your piggy bank." The next day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They saw a toy Tim wanted. Tim asked his mom if he could buy the toy. His mom said, "Do you have enough pennies?" Tim thought about the fine penny he found and said, "I think I do!" At home, Tim and his mom prepared to count the pennies in his piggy bank. They were surprised to find that the fine penny was gone. Tim was sad. He didn't know where the fine penny went. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find it." They looked everywhere for the fine penny. Tim found it under his bed. He was so happy! They counted the pennies again and found out that Tim had just enough to buy the toy. Tim and his mom went back to the store, and Tim got his toy. He was very happy and learned that saving pennies can help you get what you want.
