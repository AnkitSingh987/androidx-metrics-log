Title: Dolly the Dolphin and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a dolphin named Dolly. Dolly was a filthy dolphin because she played in the dirty sea all day. She had many friends, but her best friend was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim came to the sea with a big load of toys to play with Dolly. They played and laughed all day. Tim said, "Dolly, you are my best friend. I love playing with you, even if you are filthy." But as they played, a big wave came and took all of Tim's toys away. Tim and Dolly looked for the toys, but they could not find them. Tim was very sad and said, "Dolly, I lost all my toys. Now I can't play with you anymore." Dolly felt bad and tried to help, but the toys were gone. Tim went home and never came back to play with Dolly again. Dolly was sad and lonely, and she stayed a filthy dolphin, missing her friend Tim.

One day, a cat named Tom was walking down the street. He saw a silly dog named Max. Max was running in circles and barking loudly. Tom went up to Max and said, "Why are you being so silly?" Max stopped running and said, "I am having fun! Do you want to play with me?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Okay, let's play!" They played together on the street, running and chasing each other. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. From that day on, they always talked and played together on the street.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park to play. She saw a swing and ran to it. Mia loved to swing high in the sky. She saw a boy named Tom playing in the sand. Tom was filthy from the sand and dirt. Mia asked Tom, "Do you want to swing with me?" Tom said, "No, I want to find the menu." Mia was confused but wanted to help Tom find the menu. They looked everywhere in the park but could not find it. Tom was sad and Mia felt bad for him. Suddenly, a big wind blew and something flew out of the tree. It was the menu! It had been hiding in the tree all along. Tom was so happy and thanked Mia for her help. They both laughed and played together. In the end, they became good friends and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the color orange. She had an orange dress, an orange ball, and even an orange cat. Lily also liked to do yoga with her mommy. They would stretch and bend, and Lily would giggle. One day, Lily's mommy made a promise. She said, "Lily, if you do your yoga every day, I will buy you a new orange toy." Lily was very happy and promised to do her yoga every day. Lily did her yoga every day, just like she promised. Her mommy was proud of her. One day, her mommy took her to the toy store. Lily saw a big, orange teddy bear. She loved it. Her mommy bought it for her, and Lily hugged it tight. She knew that keeping her promise made her mommy happy, and that made her happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a small potato. The small potato lived in a garden with other potatoes. They all loved to play together in the sun. One day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird saw the small potato and wanted to eat it. The small potato was very scared. The bird said, "I am going to eat you, small potato!" The small potato did not want to be eaten. So, it thought of a plan. It said to the bird, "I am too small to eat. If you slap me, I will grow bigger and be more yummy." The bird liked the idea. It slapped the small potato with its wing. But instead of growing bigger, the small potato rolled away and hid under a leaf. The bird could not find the small potato and flew away. The small potato was happy and safe. It went back to play with the other potatoes in the garden. They all laughed and played together, knowing they were safe from the big bird.
