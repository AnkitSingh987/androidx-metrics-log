Title: Tom the Boy and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a lucky boy. He had a big bag of beans. Tom loved to sit and play with his beans every day. One day, Tom sat under a big tree. He took out his beans and began to play. He saw a little bird in the tree. The bird was sad. Tom wanted to help the bird. He gave the bird one of his beans. The bird was happy now. Tom felt good. He sat and played with his beans and the bird. They were friends. Tom knew he was a lucky boy. He had his beans and a new friend. Tom and the bird played together every day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily loved cookies. She had a cookie jar in her kitchen. Her mom always told her, "Don't eat too many cookies." One day, Lily saw a bird. The bird was very useful. It could talk and remind her of things. The bird said, "Lily, don't eat too many cookies." Lily listened to the bird. She only ate one cookie. The bird was happy. Lily's mom was happy too. Lily and the bird became good friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of play equipment. He loved to build with his toys. One day, his friend Sam came over to play with him. They were very careful with the equipment. Tim said, "Let's build a big tower!" Sam agreed. They started to build the tower, but it kept falling down. They tried again and again, but it still fell. Tim and Sam felt sad. Then, Tim's mom came in and said, "You can improve your tower by working together." Tim and Sam listened to her. They worked together, and their tower grew bigger and bigger. They were so happy. The conflict was solved, and Tim and Sam learned the moral value of working together. They knew that by being careful and helping each other, they could improve anything they did. And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a land full of animals, there was a spoiled cat named Kitty. Kitty was always mean to others and never shared her toys. One day, Kitty hurt her toe while playing. She cried and cried, but no one came to help her because they were all tired of her mean ways. Then, a wise old owl named Hootie saw Kitty crying. Hootie knew that all the animals needed to unite and help each other, no matter what. So, Hootie called all the animals to come and help Kitty. The animals put their feelings aside and decided to unite to help her. The animals worked together to make Kitty feel better. They brought her ice for her toe and sat with her until the pain went away. Kitty was very thankful and learned that being kind and helping others is important. From that day on, Kitty was no longer spoiled and mean. She played with the other animals and shared her toys. The animals were happy that they united to help Kitty, and they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that when we unite and help others, we can make the world a better place.

One day, Billy wanted to go swimming. But he didn't have a swimming pool. He decided to go to the park. When he got there, he saw a big tube. It was full of water and all his friends were in it. He was very jealous. He asked his friends if he could join them. They said yes and Billy was very excited. So Billy took off his clothes and got into the water. He felt so happy! He splashed and swam with his friends. They all had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Billy said goodbye to his friends. He had had a great time and he couldn't wait to join them again soon.

Once upon a time there was a famous little girl. Her mum and dad told her that she was special because she was so famous. One day, the little girl went to the well alone to fetch some water. But when she got there, there was a big gust of wind that scattered all her buckets and cups. The little girl got very sad. She started to cry and shouted "Why did this have to happen to me?". Suddenly, a big nasty troll appeared and said "Because you're famous, I thought I would knock you down a peg or two". The little girl was scared and tried to run away, but the troll was too fast. He grabbed the little girl and threw her far away - even further than the well. The little girl never returned home. Nobody knows what happened to her, but one thing's for sure - the troll scattered her life forever.
