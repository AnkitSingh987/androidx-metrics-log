Title: Everywhere and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to scooter. Everywhere he went, he crawled along with his scooter. One day, he was crawling along a path in the park. Suddenly, he felt something uncomfortable under his scooter. He called out to his dad: "Daddy! I feel something funny!" His dad smiled and said: "Let's take a look!" As they lifted the scooter, they saw a snake slithering away. The little boy was scared and uncomfortable. His dad calmly said: "Don't worry, my son. Everything is fine." But suddenly, the snake turned around and struck! The little boy was very scared, and he cried out. His dad could not save him, and the little boy was bitten. The little boy was sad, and his dad was too. They had to go to the hospital, and the little boy had to go through a lot of pain. All because of one uncomfortable crawl.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to nap all day long. Fluffy's favorite place to nap was on a big soft chest in the living room. The chest was full of toys and games, but Fluffy liked it because it was warm and cozy. One day, Fluffy found it difficult to nap. There was a lot of noise outside. Fluffy tried to cover her ears with her paws, but the noise was still too loud. Fluffy felt sad because she just wanted to take a nap on her favorite chest. Fluffy's friend, a little dog named Spot, saw that Fluffy was having a difficult time. Spot wanted to help Fluffy nap. So, Spot went outside and asked the noisy birds to please be quiet. The birds listened to Spot and flew away. Now, it was quiet, and Fluffy could finally nap on her chest. Fluffy was so happy and thankful for her friend Spot.

Once upon a time, in a deep green park, there was a little squirrel. The squirrel loved to play and jump from tree to tree. He had a lot of friends, like the birds and the rabbits. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the park with her mom. Lily saw the squirrel and wanted to feed him. She took some nuts from her bag and held them out for the squirrel. The squirrel came closer and took the nuts from her hand. The squirrel was very happy to eat the nuts. He jumped around and played with Lily. They both had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Lily would always come to the park to feed the squirrel and play with her new friend.

There was a tall doctor. He liked to help people. He had a big bag. The bag had things to make people feel good. The doctor went to many places to help. One day, the doctor went to a house. A little girl was not feeling good. The doctor looked at the girl. He wanted to help her. But his bag was empty! He needed to supply his bag with things to help. The tall doctor was sad. He did not know what to do. Then, the little girl smiled. She had a secret. The little girl had a small box. The box had things to make people feel good. The girl gave the box to the doctor. The doctor was happy. He used the things from the box to help the girl. The girl felt good. The doctor and the girl were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to go to the library with her mom. The library had many books with fun pictures and stories. Amy liked to look at the books and listen to her mom read them. One day, Amy saw a big red button on a machine at the library. Her mom told her it was a "record" button. Amy wanted to try it, so her mom helped her. They recorded Amy's voice while she talked about her favorite book. Amy felt very happy. After they finished recording, Amy wanted to listen to her voice. But when she heard it, she felt "ashamed" because it sounded funny to her. Her mom told her not to worry, that everyone's voice sounds different. Amy smiled and felt better. They went back to reading more books together at the library.
