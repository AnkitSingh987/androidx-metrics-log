Title: Everywhere and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved to act. Everywhere she went, she imagined she was in a play. One day, she stumbled upon a perfect mirror. She looked closely into the mirror and saw how it showed her how she acted. She liked how she looked while she was acting and made faces into the mirror. The girl's mother saw her looking into the mirror and said she should act perfectly. The girl listened to her mom and started to practice her acting right in front of the mirror. Every day she looked into the mirror, and each time she got better and better. Soon, she was acting perfectly. The girl was so happy that she had found the perfect mirror to practice with. From then on, she practiced every day using her perfect mirror. The girl soon became the best actor of all!

Anna liked to play with her toys. She had many toys, but her favorite was a big doll. She called her doll Lily. Lily had long hair, a pink dress, and a smile. Anna liked to brush Lily's hair, dress her up, and pretend she was her sister. One day, Anna felt hot and sick. She had a fever. Mommy put a thermometer in her mouth to check her temperature. The thermometer beeped and showed a high number. Mommy said Anna had to stay in bed and drink water. She gave Anna some medicine and a kiss. She said Lily could stay with her, but no other toys. Anna was sad and bored. She wanted to play with her other toys, but Mommy said no. She said the fever could spread to them and make them sick too. Anna did not want that. She loved her toys. She looked at Lily and had an idea. She was creative. She took the thermometer from Mommy's drawer and put it in Lily's mouth. She pretended Lily had a fever too. She said, "Oh no, Lily, you are sick. Don't worry, I will take care of you." She gave Lily some water and medicine. She tucked her in with a blanket. She sang her a song. She said, "We will get better soon, Lily. We are sisters. We stick together." Mommy came to check on Anna. She saw her playing with Lily and the thermometer. She smiled. She was proud of Anna. She said, "You are a good doctor, Anna. And a good sister. Lily is lucky to have you." She hugged Anna and Lily. She said, "You are both very brave. I love you." Anna smiled. She felt a little better. She said, "I love you too, Mommy. And I love Lily." She kissed Lily and the thermometer. She said, "We will be fine, Mommy. We have each other."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball. He loved to play with his ball all day long. One day, Tim's ball got a knot in it. The knot made the ball not fit to play with. Tim was very sad. Tim's mom saw the ball and said, "Oh no! Let's display the ball on the shelf, so you can still see it." But Tim wanted to play with his ball. He didn't want it on the shelf. He wanted the knot gone. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the ball with the knot and said, "I can help you fix it!" Tim was happy. They worked hard to untie the knot. Finally, the knot was gone. The ball was fit to play with again. Tim and Sam played with the ball all day. They were so happy they fixed the knot. Tim's mom was happy too. She was proud of them for working together to fix the problem. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to hold her toy carpet and play with it all day. One day, Lucy felt worried because her toy carpet was missing. She looked under her bed, behind the door, and even in the toy box, but she could not find it. While Lucy was looking for her toy carpet, she heard a strange noise coming from the living room. She walked slowly and saw her toy carpet flying in the air! Lucy was so surprised and excited that she clapped her hands and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. Lucy's toy carpet started to fly towards her and gently wrapped around her. It was not a toy carpet at all, but a magic carpet! From that day on, Lucy and her magic carpet went on many fun adventures together, and she never felt worried again.
