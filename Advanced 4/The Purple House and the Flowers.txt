Title: The Purple House and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big purple house. The house had a pretty hedge all around it. The hedge was green and had many flowers. The flowers made the hedge look very nice. In the house, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to design things. She would draw pictures of houses, trees, and animals. Lily was very good at designing things. One day, Lily decided to design a new hedge for the purple house. She drew a picture of the hedge with many flowers and butterflies. When the new hedge was done, it looked just like Lily's design. Everyone loved the new hedge, and Lily was very happy.

One day, Tom and his two siblings, Mia and James, were walking in the park. Tom was a gifted child and wanted to try something new and exciting. Then, he saw a team of kids playing a game and became very interested in joining them. Tom asked the team if he could join in and they said yes. He quickly ran over to the team and joined in the game. Suddenly, Mia yelled, "Wait! You can't play yet! Don't delay our picnic! We have to have a picnic before you can join in the game." Tom stopped and said, "But I really want to play. Can I join in after our picnic?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, you can join in after our picnic. That way, you won't delay the game anymore." So, the three siblings had a picnic and when they were done, Tom joined the team and had a great time!

Once upon a time, there was a helpful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her best friend, a little boy named Timmy. They liked to play outside in the sun and have fun together. One day, while they were playing, Timmy tripped and had a fall. He started to cry because it hurt. Kitty ran to him and asked, "Are you okay, Timmy?" Timmy said, "I fell and it hurts." Kitty wanted to help her friend feel better, so they sat down to discuss what to do. Kitty and Timmy talked about how to make the hurt go away. They decided to go ask Timmy's mom for a band-aid. Timmy's mom gave him a band-aid and a hug, and he felt much better. Timmy and Kitty went back outside to play, happy that they solved the problem together.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there was a big, modern house. In the house lived a nice girl named Lily and her mom. One day, Lily's mom bought a new case. Lily looked at the case and said, "Wow, pretty case!" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily, we can put our toys in it." After they put the toys in the case, Lily felt tired. Her mom said, "Lily, it's time to rest." So Lily went to her bed and lay down. She closed her eyes and had a good rest. The next day, Lily played with her toys from the case. She was happy and said, "Thank you, Mom, for the pretty case." Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. Now, let's play together." And so, Lily and her mom played with the toys, and they had a lot of fun. They loved their new, modern house and the pretty case.

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy named Sam. Sam liked to sail on a little boat. One day, he went to the grocery store with his mom. Sam saw a tiny toy boat on a shelf. He wanted the boat so much. Sam's mom said yes to the toy boat. They went home, and Sam went out to sail his new boat. He put the boat in a big puddle. The boat began to sail away. Sam was so happy to watch it go. But then, something unexpected happened. The tiny toy boat grew bigger and bigger. It was now a real boat! Sam was so surprised. He jumped on the big boat and sailed away. He had so much fun on his new big boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with bubbles. One day, Amy's mom said, "We are going on a trip. Let's pack your bag." Amy was very excited. Amy and her mom packed her clothes, toys, and her favorite bubble bottle. When they finished, Amy said, "Mom, can I blow bubbles now?" Her mom said, "Not inside the house, Amy. Wait until we go outside." Amy was persistent. She asked her mom again and again. Finally, her mom said, "Okay, let's go outside and blow bubbles." So, Amy and her mom went outside. They blew big bubbles and small bubbles. They had a lot of fun. The next day, Amy and her family went on their trip. Amy took her bubble bottle with her. She blew bubbles everywhere they went. Everyone loved the bubbles and Amy was very happy.
