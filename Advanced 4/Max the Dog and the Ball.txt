Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

One day, a big dog named Max went to the park to do some exercise. Max loved to run and play with his ball. He was very happy when he got to the park. Max was always careful not to bump into the little kids playing there. As Max was playing, he saw a small cat named Luna. Luna was scared of Max because he was so big. Max wanted to be friends with Luna, so he tried to be nice. He did not rush at her, but walked slowly and said "Hi, Luna!" Luna was surprised. She thought Max would chase her, but he didn't. Max and Luna started to play together in the park. They had so much fun running and jumping. The little kids at the park were happy to see Max and Luna play. They all became good friends and had a great time together.

Once upon a time, a famous well was full of water. All of the animals in the forest loved the water, so they gathered there to play and drink every day. One day, a sly fox suggested that the animals bring some of the water home with them. But the animals knew that if they did, then the well would eventually be empty. So they told the fox no. The fox was angry and kept suggesting different ways to take some water home with them. The animals grew more and more worried, so they decided they must stop the fox before it was too late. The animals got together and suggested that they all work together to keep the well full. Everyone agreed it was the best idea, and they quickly came up with a plan. The animals decided to fill up buckets each time they decided to go visit the well, and then when it was time to go home, they would pour the water back in! The animals worked very hard to keep the famous well full and full of life, and everyone was happy. The fox never suggested the same thing again, and the animals could go to the well whenever they liked without worrying.

Tommy was so excited. He was going to help mommy do some cleaning. She had given him his very own yellow cloth. He grabbed it, ran to the TV and started to clean. "Wow, Tommy! You're doing a great job," said mommy. Tommy smiled, happy that his mommy was proud of him. He kept rubbing the TV with the yellow cloth, until it was all nice and shiny. "Done!" said Tommy when he finished cleaning. Mommy smiled. "Well done, Tommy! You did a fantastic job. Now, let's go and have some yummy ice cream." Tommy's eyes lit up. He clapped his hands and said "Yay!"

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw big, dark clouds in the sky. Lily was not happy. She did not want it to rain. Lily saw her friend, Tom, and asked, "Will it rain today?" Tom looked at the sky and said, "Yes, I think it will rain soon." Lily was sad because she wanted to play outside. But then, Lily and Tom had an idea. They put on their rain boots and rain coats. When the rain came, they jumped in the puddles and laughed. They were happy, even in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim had a big rock that he liked to play with. Every day, he would wait for his friends to come and play with the rock too. One day, Tim and his friends were playing with the rock near a big tree. They were having lots of fun. Tim threw the rock high in the air, but he was clumsy and it hit the tree. The tree shook and many leaves fell down. The tree was very sad and hurt. Tim and his friends were scared and ran away. The tree was never the same again, and Tim learned to be more careful when playing with his rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy train. One sunny day, he decided to build a big train station with his blocks. Tim was very happy and thought it looked good. While Tim was playing, his friend Sarah came to visit. She saw the train station and said, "Wow, Tim! Your train station is so good!" Tim smiled and replied, "Thank you, Sarah! Do you want to play with me?" Sarah nodded and they played together. As they were playing, Tim accidentally stumbled and knocked down the train station. The blocks fell everywhere. Tim felt sad, but Sarah said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can build it again!" So, they picked up the blocks and built a new train station. They played happily all day long.
