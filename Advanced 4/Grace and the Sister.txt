Title: Grace and the Sister

Grace was very envious of her sister, Josie. Every day, her sister would dance to the jazz music in their house. She was so graceful and light on her feet. Every time her sister danced, Grace would watch with admiration. One day, she decided to join in. She moved her feet to the beat of the music and felt so free. Josie smiled and said, "Grace, you look amazing! Don't forget to keep dancing and keep on practising". This reminded Grace of the importance of hard work and dedication. She practised hard every day and soon she was dancing just as well as her sister. Josie cheered her and said, "You've done really well, Grace". Grace was so proud of herself and she never let the feeling of envy enter her mind ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn cat named Tom. Tom did not like to listen to anyone. One day, Tom found a big book. The book was about history. It had many stories about things that happened a long time ago. Tom wanted to know what was in the book, but he could not read. So, he had an idea. He decided to rub the book with his paw. He thought that if he rubbed the book, the stories would come out and he could learn about history. Tom rubbed the book for a long time, but nothing happened. He was still a stubborn cat and did not want to give up. He asked his friend, a wise old owl, for help. The owl told Tom that he needed to learn how to read to know the stories in the book. Tom listened to the owl and learned how to read. Finally, Tom could read the history book and learn about the past.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. He had a red wagon that he loved. Tim would put all his toys in the wagon and pull it around. One day, Tim wanted to invite his friends to play with him and his wagon. Tim's friends came to his house. They were excited to play with the wagon. They put all their toys in the wagon too. The wagon was very full. They started to pull the wagon, but it was too heavy. They tried and tried, but they could not move it. Then, a big, strong dog came to help. The dog pulled the wagon with all the toys. Tim and his friends were so happy. They played all day. The dog was now their new friend. And the messy boy, Tim, learned that friends and teamwork can make anything fun.

There was an elderly man who had a letter. He needed someone to listen to his story. So, he went outside and looked for someone to help him out. He saw a young girl playing with a ball. He asked her if she could listen to his story and read his letter to him. She said yes, so the elderly man started talking. He told her a sad story and when he finished, she looked very sad. He handed her the letter, and she read it out loud. As she was reading, she started to cry. What was inside the letter? It was a message telling the elderly man that he had passed away. The young girl was very sad for the elderly man and hugged him tightly before he finished his journey.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very interesting because he could tie things. One day, Max found a napkin on the floor. He picked it up and tied it around his neck like a cape. Max walked around the park, showing off his new cape. All the other dogs and kids thought it was fun. Suddenly, a little bird flew down and landed on Max's head. The bird was lost and needed help finding its way home. Max had an idea. He untied the napkin from his neck and tied it to the bird's leg. The napkin turned into a map that showed the bird the way home. The bird was so happy and flew away to its family. Max felt good that he could help, and everyone cheered. From that day on, Max was known as the hero dog who could tie magic napkins.
