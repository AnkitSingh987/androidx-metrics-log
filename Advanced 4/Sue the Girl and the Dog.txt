Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty flute that she liked to play. She played her flute every day. Her friends would come to listen and admire her music. One day, a harmless dog heard Sue playing her flute. The dog wanted to play with Sue, but he was scared of the flute sound. The dog barked and barked, trying to tell Sue to stop playing. Sue got scared and stopped playing her flute. Sue's mom saw the dog and knew he was harmless. She told Sue not to be scared. Sue played her flute again, and the dog stopped barking. The dog learned to like Sue's flute music. Sue, her friends, and the harmless dog all played together, happy and having fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a delicate mineral while playing in his yard. It was shiny and pretty. Tim was very happy and wanted to show his mom. He ran inside and said, "Mom, look what I found!" His mom looked at the mineral and smiled. "That's a very pretty mineral, Tim," she said. "Why don't you sit and play with it?" Tim sat on the floor and played with the mineral. He liked how it sparkled in the light. He liked to pretend it was a magic stone that could make wishes come true. His mom watched him play and said, "Be careful, Tim. The mineral is delicate, and you don't want to break it." Tim nodded and played gently with the mineral. He was happy to have found such a special treasure.

Ben and Mia liked to play with their toy animals. They had many animals, like lions, elephants, monkeys and giraffes. They pretended that their room was a zoo and they were the zookeepers. One day, they found some stickers in a drawer. The stickers had letters and numbers on them. Ben and Mia thought they could use the stickers to name their animals. They started to attach the stickers to their toys. Ben wanted to have the best names for his animals. He was very competitive. He tried to use the biggest and longest stickers he could find. He gave his lion the name "ZEBRAKILLER" and his elephant the name "BIGGEST". He was proud of his names. Mia wanted to have nice names for her animals. She was not very competitive. She tried to use the stickers that matched the colors and shapes of her toys. She gave her monkey the name "BANANA" and her giraffe the name "SPOTTY". She was happy with her names. Ben and Mia looked at each other's animals. Ben thought Mia's names were silly and boring. He said, "My names are better than yours. They are bigger and longer and cooler. You should change your names." Mia thought Ben's names were mean and scary. She said, "Your names are not nice. They are too big and too long and too loud. You should be nicer to your animals." They started to argue and fight over the stickers. Their mom heard the noise and came to their room. She saw the stickers on the toys and the mess on the floor. She was not happy. She said, "What are you doing? You are not supposed to attach stickers to your toys. You are supposed to play nicely with them. And you are supposed to share and respect each other. You are not being good zookeepers." She made them take off the stickers and clean up the room. She also made them apologize to each other and hug. Ben and Mia felt sorry for what they did. They realized that they were not being good friends or good zookeepers. They decided to play with their animals without stickers. They also decided to give them new names that they both liked. They gave their lion the name "ROAR" and their elephant the name "TRUNK". They gave their monkey the name "FUNNY" and their giraffe the name "TALL". They were both happy with their names. They played with their animals and had fun. They pretended that their room was a zoo and they were the best zookeepers. They were also the best friends.
