Title: Tim the Boy and the Teacher

Once upon a time there lived a little boy named Tim. He liked to run around and play. One day, he was asked by his teacher to run to the store for her to get some supplies. Tim said yes, and his teacher was very grateful. So off Tim ran to the store. When he got there he realized that he had forgotten the money. So he ran back to the school to tell his teacher. She was still very grateful, gave him some money and smiled. That night while Tim was lying in bed he remembered what had happened and felt grateful for his teacher. He realized that the teacher was helping him so that he could learn how to be more responsible. The next day at school when Tim saw the teacher he said, "Thank you for teaching me how to be more responsible. I will never forget it". The teacher was so proud of Tim. She said, "Tim, you are a very special pupil. You have a big heart and it is your responsibility to use it properly." Tim smiled and ran off to continue playing but this time with a much better understanding of being responsible. Moral Value: We should be grateful to those who help us learn and grow.

One day, a big cat and a little dog had a fight. They were in a clear field, where all could see them. The cat was very mean and would poke the dog with its sharp claws. The dog would bark and try to run away, but the cat would chase it. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny bug came to the field and saw the fight. The bug did not like the cat being mean to the dog. So, the bug flew up to the cat and gave it a big poke on its nose. The cat was very surprised and stopped chasing the dog. The cat and the dog looked at the tiny bug and saw how brave it was. They both decided not to fight anymore. The cat said sorry to the dog, and the dog forgave the cat. From that day on, they all became good friends and played together in the clear field.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a fancy red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Spot lost his fancy red ball and he was very sad. Spot decided to find his fancy red ball. He looked under the bed, in the toy box, and even outside in the yard. But Spot could not find his fancy red ball anywhere. He felt very sad and did not know what to do. Then, Spot's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, came to help. Chirpy flew up high and saw the fancy red ball on the roof of Spot's house. Chirpy showed Spot the way to climb up and get his ball. Spot was so happy to find his fancy red ball, and they played together all day long.

Once there was a boy named Jack. He was only three years old and very adventurous. Every day he would jump around the house, filled with energy. One day Jack was feeling extra crazy. He jumped all around the house, inside and outside. Eventually, Jack jumped right into a little bit of trouble. He had jumped into a muddy puddle that was outside in the garden. Jack's mom saw him, and told him not to jump in the mud again. But Jack could not help it - he was feeling way too crazy. Jack's mom got out the hose and washed him off. But even when he was clean, Jack was still feeling very crazy. He could not keep himself still; He kept doing jumps and jumps around the house. Eventually, Jack's mom found a way to tire him out. She put on some music, and soon Jack and his mom were dancing and jumping around the house together. Jack jumped around for hours until he was all tuckered out and ready for bed.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big teddy bear. They named him Cocoa, because he was brown and soft like the drink they had before bed. One day, Sara and Ben had a fight. They both wanted to hug Cocoa, but they did not want to share. They pulled and tugged on Cocoa, and yelled at each other. "He is mine!" Sara said. "No, he is mine!" Ben said. They did not listen to their mom, who told them to be nice and take turns. Cocoa did not like the fight. He felt sad and scared. He wished Sara and Ben would stop and be friends again. He did not want to be ripped or lost. He wanted to be loved and cuddled by both of them. Then, something bad happened. Sara and Ben pulled so hard on Cocoa, that he tore apart. His stuffing came out, and his button eyes fell off. Sara and Ben gasped and cried. They looked at Cocoa, who was broken and ruined. They felt sorry and ashamed. They had spoiled their favorite toy. They hugged each other and said sorry. They said sorry to Cocoa, too. They asked their mom to help them fix Cocoa. Their mom was angry, but she was also kind. She got a needle and thread, and sewed Cocoa back together. She gave him new eyes and a bow. She told Sara and Ben to be gentle and careful with Cocoa, and to share him nicely. Sara and Ben nodded and smiled. They thanked their mom, and gave her a kiss. They took Cocoa to their room, and gave him a big hug. They passed him to each other, and said how much they loved him. Cocoa was happy, too. He felt whole and warm again. He knew Sara and Ben were sorry, and he forgave them. He hoped they would never fight again. He loved them both very much.
