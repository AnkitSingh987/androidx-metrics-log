Title: Molly and the Garden

Molly was a little girl who had lots of dreams. Her favourite was when she was a princess and everyone in the kingdom loved her. One day, Molly was given a special present, a bottle of perfume. It was pretty and it smelled soft. Molly put it on like a princess and started to dream. Suddenly, Molly was in the middle of a magical garden. Everywhere she looked was beautiful and there were some pretty flowers. She was wearing her princess dress and she felt so happy. Even the birds were singing nice and soft. Molly spent the whole day dreaming in the garden, until it was time to go home. As she left, her special perfume made the garden and her dreams smell soft and beautiful. Molly smiled and knew she'd visit the garden again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a fat bird named Bobo. Bobo liked to fly very high in the sky. He had many friends, but his best friend was a fish named Fifi. Fifi lived in a pool near Bobo's tree. One day, Bobo wanted to play with Fifi. So, he flew down to the pool. Fifi was happy to see him. They played in the water, splashing and having fun. But Bobo was too big and fat for the pool. Bobo tried to fly out of the pool, but he could not. He was too wet and too fat. Fifi and the other fish tried to help him, but they could not. Bobo was sad and scared. In the end, Bobo stayed in the pool and could not fly anymore.

One day, Jack and Maria were walking in the park. Jack noticed something black on the ground. He said, "Look Maria, what is that?" Maria replied, "It's a raven!" Jack then asked, "What is he doing here?" Maria replied, "Maybe he's here to sell something. Let's ask him." They walked over to the raven. Jack said, "Hello! What do you have to sell?" The raven replied, "I have some cheap jewelry." Jack and Maria looked at each other in excitement. Jack said, "Can we buy it?" The raven answered, "Absolutely! I'll give it to you for a good price." Jack and Maria both paid the raven and received the jewelry. Jack and Maria thanked the raven and said goodbye. As they walked away, they looked at their jewelry with glee. Jack said, "Let's always remember to show appreciation when people sell us something!"

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sandy and John. Sandy and John were both very eager to go to the pool. They both loved spending time in the pool and having lots of fun! Sandy said to John, “Let’s go to the pool now! I’m so excited to go.” John replied, “Yes, me too! Let’s go!” So, off they went to the pool. When they got there they could hardly wait to jump in and start having fun! They spent the whole day playing and laughing in the pool. At the end of the day, they were both tired. Sandy and John said goodbye and promised each other to come back soon and spend even more time in the pool.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends in the big yard near the church. They liked to run, jump, and chase each other all day long. One sunny day, while they were playing, Tom started to sneeze a lot. His friends asked, "Why are you sneezing, Tom?" Tom did not know why, but he could not stop sneezing. His friends decided to help him find out why he was sneezing so much. They went to the church and asked the wise old owl who lived there. The owl listened to Tom's sneezes and said, "Ah, I know what is happening. You are not a cat, Tom. You are a tiger!" Tom and his friends were very surprised. The owl explained, "Tigers sneeze when they are happy, and you are a very cheerful tiger!" Tom and his friends could not believe it, but they were happy to know the reason for his sneezes. From that day on, they continued to play and have fun, and Tom was the happiest sneezing tiger in the whole world.
