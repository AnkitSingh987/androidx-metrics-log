Title: The Story of the Tree and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to measure things. One day, she got some special tools that could measure everything she wanted. She was so excited that she decided to get to work! The little girl ran outside to measure the biggest tree in her garden. She looked around for something to help her but she couldn't find anything. Suddenly, she noticed a frame lying next to her garden. She was sure it had something to do with her measuring tools so she picked it up. When she tried to measure the tree, she noticed that the frame wasn't long enough. She was sad and a little bit ignorant until her grandfather came along. Grandfather: What do you want to measure, my dear? Little girl: I'm trying to measure this tree but the frame isn't long enough. Grandfather: Don't worry. I know how to fix this. Follow me. The little girl followed her grandfather until they reached a workshop. He handed her a bigger frame and said: Grandfather: Now you can measure anything you want! The little girl was so happy. She used the frame to measure the tree and everything else she wanted. From then on, she was never ignorant when it came to measuring things!

Once upon a time, in a normal town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a toy bear named Mr. Cuddle. They were best friends and went everywhere together. One day, Lily and Mr. Cuddle went to a big store. There, they saw a beautiful, soft, luxury blanket. "Oh, Mr. Cuddle, I want that blanket," said Lily. But her mommy said, "We can't buy it today, Lily. We need to save our money for other things." Lily was sad, but she managed to smile. She knew her mommy was right. So, Lily and Mr. Cuddle went home without the blanket. That night, Lily's mommy told her a story about a wise old owl who was happy with what he had, even if it wasn't much. Lily learned that it's important to be happy with what you have and not always want more. She hugged Mr. Cuddle tight and knew that she didn't need the luxury blanket to be happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, broken toy on the ground. Tim felt sad because he wanted to play with the toy, but it was not working. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and told her about the broken toy. Sue had an idea to fix it. They found some tape and put the toy back together. The toy was not broken anymore. Tim and Sue were so excited to play with it. They took the fixed toy to the open field in the park. They played with it all day long. They were very happy that they could fix the broken toy. The toy made them excited and they had lots of fun together.

Lila and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park and read books together. One day, they found an old book in the library. It had many poems inside. The poems were ancient. They were written by people who lived long ago. Lila and Tom wanted to read the poems, but they did not know the words. They were too hard and strange. They asked the librarian for help, but she was busy. She told them to pass the book to her later. Lila and Tom decided to try to read the poems by themselves. They looked at the pictures and guessed what the poems were about. They made up their own stories and rhymes. They had fun and laughed a lot. The librarian heard them and smiled. She came over and asked them what they were reading. Lila and Tom showed her the book and told her their stories. The librarian was surprised and happy. She said, "You are very clever and creative. You made the poems your own. You did not need to know the words to enjoy them. You learned something new and had fun. That is the best way to read." Lila and Tom thanked the librarian and passed the book to her. They asked her if they could borrow another book. The librarian said yes and gave them a book with more poems. Lila and Tom were excited and ran to the park. They wanted to read more poems and make more stories. They were happy and proud. They loved to read.
