Title: The Shark and the Fish

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little shark. This shark was not scary. He was adorable. He had big, cute eyes and a small, round nose. He liked to swim and play with his friends, the fish. One day, the adorable shark had a fun idea. He wanted to collect pretty shells from the sea floor. He asked his fish friends to help him. They all swam down together to find the shells. They found many shells in different colors and shapes. The adorable shark and his friends collected them all. They were very happy with their new treasures. And from that day on, they had even more fun playing together in the big sea.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little monkey named Kiki. Kiki loved to climb trees all day long. One day, Kiki decided to climb the tallest tree in the forest to see the sun rise in the east. As Kiki climbed higher and higher, she saw a big, angry bird named Bobo sitting on a branch. Bobo did not like it when others climbed his tree. He started to yell at Kiki, "Go away! This is my tree!" Kiki did not want to make Bobo angry, so she climbed down the tree and went to find another one. She found a beautiful tree near the edge of the forest. Kiki climbed to the top and saw the sun rise in the east. It was the most beautiful thing she had ever seen. From that day on, Kiki and Bobo learned to share the trees, and they became the best of friends.

Once there was a hero named Joe. He was very brave and adventurous, and liked to go on adventures. One day Joe was on a mission to save the world. He needed a special ticket to get to his destination, so he ran to the ticket booth to get one. When he got there, he saw something very impressive. At the ticket booth stood a big and strong guard. He looked intimidating, but Joe was determined to get a ticket. "Excuse me," Joe said, "can I have a ticket please?" The guard shook his head. "No, you can't have a ticket," he said. "Unless you can do something impressive, I won't let you have a ticket." Joe smiled. "OK," he said, "I will show you something impressive." He took a few steps back and then jumped up to the top of the ticket booth. Everyone cheered and the guard said, "OK, you can have the ticket!" Joe happily grabbed the ticket and ran off on his adventure. He had saved the day!

One day, a charming little dog named Max saw a big pile of blocks. Max wanted to build a big tower with the blocks. So, he started to stack them, one by one. He was very happy. As Max was building, a little cat named Lily came by. She saw Max and wanted to help. "Can I build with you?" asked Lily. Max nodded and said, "Yes, let's build a tower together!" They both worked together and made the tower taller and taller. Suddenly, they saw smoke coming from a little fire nearby. Max and Lily knew they had to help. They used their tower blocks to make a path over the fire. They walked on the blocks and put out the fire. Max and Lily were proud of what they had built and happy they could help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved cherries. She loved to eat them all day. One day, she found a big cherry tree. Sue was very happy. Sue picked a cherry and took a big bite. But it tasted disgusting! Sue spat it out. She did not know why the cherry tasted so bad. Then, a small bird came and sat on the tree. The bird said, "I am sorry, I made the cherries taste bad. Please forgive me." Sue forgave the bird. The bird told Sue a secret. "There are magic cherries on the other side of the tree," the bird said. Sue went to the other side and found the magic cherries. She ate them, and they were the best cherries she ever had.
