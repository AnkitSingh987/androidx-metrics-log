Title: The Mushroom and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little mushroom. This mushroom was not like the others. It was brilliant and shiny. All the animals loved the shiny mushroom because it made them happy. One day, a small rabbit came to the mushroom. The rabbit was very sad. "Please help me, shiny mushroom," the rabbit said. "I lost my best friend, the squirrel." The mushroom wanted to help, so it made a promise. "I will help you find your friend," the mushroom said. The rabbit and the mushroom looked all over the forest. They looked under rocks and in trees. Finally, they found the squirrel. The squirrel was happy to see them. The rabbit and the squirrel hugged, and they thanked the brilliant mushroom. The three of them became the best of friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little girl named Lucy. She had a big box of crayons. She loved to draw pretty pictures with them. Lucy could make the sun, the trees, and the animals with her crayons. One day, Lucy went to play with her friend, Tom. She took her crayons with her. They drew many pictures together. Tom liked the red crayon the most. Lucy liked the blue one. They made a big, happy sun with the yellow crayon. Lucy's mom came to pick her up. She looked at the pictures and smiled. She could recognize the sun, the trees, and the animals. Lucy and Tom were so happy. They knew they were gifted with their crayons. They could make the world a more colorful place.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was only 3 years old and liked to jog every day. One day, Jane decided to jog to the park. She was so excited to explore! When Jane arrived at the park, she saw something large and round. It was a pear tree! Jane saw lots of pears hanging, so she ran to them and started picking them. Suddenly, Jane heard a voice. It was an old man sitting on a bench. "Do not pick those pears, they are not yours," he said in a serious tone. Jane was scared, and she started to jog away. But then she noticed something on the ground and stopped. It was a pear that had fallen from the tree. "It's ok if I pick this one, right?" Jane asked the old man. The old man smiled and said, "Yes, you can pick this one, it's ok". So Jane picked the fallen pear and jogged home, happy to have something to eat. She made sure to obey the old man's serious instructions.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer called Jane. Jane had a very fine dress that she wore and she always danced with a big smile. One day, after a long day of dancing, Jane decided to take a nap. As Jane was lying down, she heard a voice coming from outside. “Jane, wake up! Come out and dance! We’re all waiting for you” the voice sang. Jane opened her eyes and smiled. “Alright” she said. She stood up, adjusted her fine dress, and began to dance. Everyone cheered and started dancing along with her. All day, Jane danced merrily with her friends. When it was time for her break, she remembered her nap and said “Hey everyone, I’m going to lay down for a bit.” Everyone said goodbye and Jane took a nap. When she woke up, she felt even more ready to dance than before. It was just like she had never taken a break! The next morning, Jane was back at it, dancing and smiling like she always do. She was truly a fine dancer.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp who lived in the sea. The shrimp was not like the other shrimps. He was ugly. He had a big nose and his shell was a funny color. The other shrimps did not want to play with him. This made the ugly shrimp very sad. One day, the ugly shrimp was swimming alone. He saw a big fish crying. The ugly shrimp swam to the big fish and asked, "Why are you crying?" The big fish said, "I lost my family. I am all alone." The ugly shrimp wanted to help the big fish, so they swam together to look for the big fish's family. As they swam, the ugly shrimp tried to comfort the big fish. He said, "Don't be sad. We will find your family." The big fish felt better with the ugly shrimp by his side. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found the big fish's family. The big fish was so happy, and he thanked the ugly shrimp. From that day on, the ugly shrimp and the big fish were the best of friends.
