Title: The Story of the Story and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to jump. She had a special chair where she could practice her jumps. Her dad was always telling her to be careful, but she was too restless to listen. One day, as she was jumping, her dad warned her once again. But the little girl believed she was strong enough to do more. So she ignored her dad's warning and tried a harder jump. The chair was too small. The girl lost her balance and fell to the ground. Her dad ran to her and said, "See how important it is to believe in yourself, but also to listen?" The little girl was sad as she realized she couldn't do what she wanted anymore. She'll never jump on her chair again. From this story, we can learn that it's important to believe in ourselves, but also to listen to the advice of those who love us.

Once upon a time, there was a good otter. The otter lived by a big blue lake. The otter had many friends in the lake. They liked to play and swim together. The otter was happy. One day, the otter saw a big fish. The fish was very pretty. The otter wanted the fish, so the otter stole the fish. The otter took the fish to his home. The otter was very proud of the fish. But then, something strange happened. The fish started to talk! The fish said, "I am a magic fish. If you let me go, I will give you a big surprise." The otter was shocked. The otter let the magic fish go. The fish swam away and then, the big surprise came. The lake turned into a giant pool of yummy treats for the otter and all his friends. The otter learned that it is better to be kind than to steal.

Once there was a little girl named May. She was three years old and loved to explore the world. One day, she decided to take a dive into the garden. She could see many colorful roses and she wanted to get closer. When May started to dive, she noticed something very interesting about one of the roses. It had a bright yellow mark on its petals. She was very excited. She plucked the rose and ran to show it to her mom. Her mom was surprised too, and said, “That’s a special rose, May! You can keep it.” May was very proud and kept it in her room. From that day on she went on many even more interesting dives in her garden, looking for more unique roses.

One day, a little girl was very excited. She liked to watch the pigeons fly around in her garden. All of a sudden, she noticed that one of the pigeons was hurt. It had a broken wing! She wanted to help. The girl quickly found her dad. She asked her dad to help fix the pigeon. He said he would try. They set out to the garden. The pigeon was scared and wouldn't let them near. But the dad knew what to do. He talked to the pigeon in a soothing voice. Then he slowly tried to put the broken pieces of the wing back together. The girl watched with excitement. After a while, she saw that the pigeon could fly again! She was so happy that her dad was able to help fix the pigeon. The girl was very excited to watch the pigeon fly off into the sky again. She and her dad smiled, happy with the good deed they had done.

One day, a little bug named Billy liked to crawl on the ground. He was very small, but he felt important. He wanted to show all his friends how fast he could crawl. So, he told his friends, "I will crawl around the big tree in one week." His friends were excited and said, "Wow, Billy! That's a big job! We want to watch you do it!" They all agreed to meet by the big tree in one week. The day came, and all of Billy's friends gathered around the big tree. They watched as Billy started to crawl. He went slow at first, but then he went faster and faster. His friends cheered him on, saying, "Go, Billy, go!" Finally, after a week of crawling, Billy made it all the way around the big tree. His friends clapped and said, "You did it, Billy! You are so important and fast!" Billy felt very happy and proud. He knew that even though he was small, he could do big things.
