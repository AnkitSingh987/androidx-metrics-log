Title: The Flower and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a small flower in a garden. The flower was very sad because it felt sad and alone. Suddenly, on a sunny morning, the flower bloomed. It was so beautiful that it brought a smile to the face of everyone who saw it. The flower felt so happy and relieved that it wanted to tell everyone its good news. It could not speak, so it used its lung to shout out its joy. It shouted so loud that everyone in the neighbourhood heard it. The flower had bloomed so nicely that everyone wanted a piece of it. But the flower was not interested in people taking pieces of it, so it started selling small parts at cheap prices. Everyone was so happy with the flower that they all gave it loving hugs. The flower soon became everyone's favourite flower in the garden and it bloomed brighter and brighter each day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a suit that he loved. The suit was red and blue. Tom wanted to wear his suit every day. One day, Tom asked his mom, "Can I wear my suit today?" His mom said, "Yes, you can wear your suit, but you must be alert and listen to me." Tom was very happy and put on his suit. Tom and his mom went to the park. Tom played on the swings and slides. He was alert and listened to his mom. They had a fun day at the park. Tom was glad his mom allowed him to wear his suit.

Bob was a 3 year old boy who liked to explore. One day he asked his mum if he could go outside and play. His mum shook her head and said "No, Bob. You must stay inside today." Bob was sad. He wanted to go and explore but he understood what his mum said. He went to his room and looked out the window. Suddenly he saw a sign up the street and he wanted to go and take a look. He asked his mum again if he could go out and this time she said yes. She told him he needed to read the sign first and be careful. She gave him a big hug and said goodbye. Bob walked up the street. He saw the sign and it said "Dull park." He thought it looked boring, but he kept walking and eventually he reached the entrance. He was so excited! Bob looked around the park. It was quiet and the sky was grey. He heard the birds singing and went to take a closer look. He noticed a big tree, a pond and lots of flowers - it wasn't so dull after all! He quickly ran back home to tell his mum. She was very happy and said he would be able to explore outside more often as long as she gave him permission. Bob was over the moon!

Once upon a time, there was a blue boat with a big sail. The boat loved to go fast on the water. One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went on the blue boat for a ride. Tim said to Spot, "Let's go fast and not lose the race!" The wind blew, and the sail filled with air. The blue boat went very fast on the water. Tim and Spot were happy. As they raced, they saw a big fish jump out of the water. Tim and Spot laughed and clapped. They did not lose the race. The blue boat with the big sail won, and they all had a fun day on the water.

Once upon a time there was a little horse named Manny. Manny loved to spring around in his cozy hay barn. He was very happy, but he was also very persistent. Every time that Manny would spring around the hay barn, he would get hay in his mane and on his back. He shook and shook, but no matter what, the hay stayed. Manny didn't like having the hay, so he decided to try something different. He asked his farm friends to help him. They gave him good advice and said "If you want to get rid of the hay, you simply have to keep springing around until it all falls off!" So Manny did as he was told and kept springing around. Sure enough, after some time, all the hay fell off and Manny was free from the itchy hay. Manny learned that if he was persistent and kept trying, he could do whatever he wanted.
