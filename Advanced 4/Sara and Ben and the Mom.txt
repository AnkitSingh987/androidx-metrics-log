Title: Sara and Ben and the Mom

Sara and Ben like to help Mom make pizza. They wash their hands and put on aprons. Mom gives them dough and sauce and cheese. Sara and Ben have fun rolling the dough and spreading the sauce. They sprinkle cheese on top. Mom says they can add some toppings. Sara likes olives and Ben likes pepper. Mom cuts some olives and peppers for them. Sara and Ben take turns putting them on the pizza. They make a smiley face with the olives and peppers. They are happy with their pizza. Mom puts the pizza in the oven and sets the timer. Sara and Ben wait for the pizza to bake. They smell the cheese and the sauce and the pepper. They hear the timer beep. Mom takes the pizza out of the oven and cuts it into slices. She gives Sara and Ben each a slice on a plate. Sara and Ben take a bite of their pizza. It is hot and cheesy and yummy. They like the olives and the pepper. They say thank you to Mom for letting them help. Mom says they are good helpers. They all enjoy their pizza together. They are happy.

Once there was a flea who was very sad. Every day, he would sit on the rock and feel down. The rock was big and warm, but the flea was still alone and felt very blue. One day, something amazing happened. A small bird landed on the rock! The flea was so excited and the bird was so sweet. She fluttered her wings and sang a song to the flea. The flea's sadness melted away and he felt much better. The flea and bird became very good friends. Together they would rock on the big, warm rock and have lots of fun. The sad flea was no longer alone, and he and his new friend were very happy!

One day, a unique frog named Bob saw a package. The package was big and wrapped with a pretty bow. Bob was very excited and wanted to know what was inside. So, he decided to jump and jump until he could reach the package. Bob jumped and jumped, but he could not reach the package. Then, a bird named Sue saw Bob trying to jump. Sue asked, "Why are you jumping, Bob?" Bob said, "I want to see what is inside the package, but I can't jump high enough." Sue wanted to help Bob. So, she flew up and tried to get the package down. But when she touched the package, it fell and broke. Inside, there were many pretty things, but now they were all broken. Bob and Sue were sad because they could not play with the pretty things in the package.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a small attic. In the attic, there lived a happy and healthy mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to play and eat cheese all day long. One day, Mimi heard a loud noise outside the attic. She peeked out and saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was crying because she lost her toy bear. Mimi wanted to care and help her new friend. Mimi said, "Don't cry, I will help you find your bear." Together, they looked high and low, in the attic and all around the house. At last, they found the toy bear under a big chair. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mimi, for caring and helping me." From that day on, Mimi and Lily became best friends, and they played happily in the attic every day.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a hammer. He would use the hammer to build things. One day, Tim was playing outside with his hammer. He saw a bird in a tree. The bird looked down at Tim and said, "Can you build me a house?" Tim nodded his head and said, "Yes, I can help you." Tim worked hard to build the bird a house. He used his hammer to put it together. When he was done, the bird was very happy. Tim felt good that he could help his new friend. From that day on, Tim and the bird played together every day.
