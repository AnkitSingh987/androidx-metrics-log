Title: Emma and Milly and the Park

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Emma and Milly. One day the two friends were sitting in the park enjoying the sunshine. Milly was showing Emma how to draw a colourful picture with the chalk. They were having lots of fun. Just then another group of kids arrived with their own chalk. Emma felt embarrassed because her chalk was much duller than theirs. Milly saw her friend's worry and said with a compassionate voice, "It alright Emma, don't worry. The colour of the chalk doesn't matter, what matters is that we have fun together." Everyone started playing together and it was the best time ever. Emma was thankful to have such a good friend like Milly. The end.

---

Tom and Lily were playing outside in the park. They liked to look at the sky and see the different shapes of the clouds. Sometimes they saw animals, or faces, or castles. "Look, Lily, that cloud looks like a big bear!" Tom said, pointing at a fluffy white cloud. "I see it, Tom, it's very cute!" Lily said, smiling. "And that one looks like a flower!" They heard a loud noise and saw a plane flying over them. The plane made a trail of smoke behind it, cutting through the clouds. "Oh no, the plane is hurting the clouds!" Tom said, feeling sad. "The bear cloud is gone!" Lily hugged Tom and said, "Don't worry, Tom, the clouds are not hurt. They are very compassionate. They will make new shapes for us to see. Maybe they will make a plane cloud for us!" Tom looked at Lily and felt better. He said, "You are right, Lily, the clouds are very compassionate. They are our friends. And you are my best friend!" They held hands and continued to watch the sky, hoping to see more clouds.

---

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy cars in the living room. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. But then, Tom's car stopped moving. He looked at it and saw that the battery was empty. "Uh-oh, my car has no power. I need a new battery," he said to Lily. Lily looked at her car and saw that it had a red battery. She had a good idea. "Here, Tom, you can have my battery. I can supply you with a red battery. Then your car can move again," she said and gave him her battery. Tom was happy and thanked Lily. He put the red battery in his car and it started to move again. He and Lily continued to play with their toy cars and had fun. "Thank you, Lily, you are very kind. I like your red battery. It makes my car go fast," he said to Lily. Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom. I like your car too. It is very cool. Maybe we can trade cars sometimes." They agreed and played some more. They were good friends and liked to share.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Ann. She had a toy boat and a pretend lake. Ann placed the toy boat in the lake and the boat started to sail. It sailed around the lake and Ann was so happy. When the toy boat seemed to tire Ann helped it. She pushed it around the lake. All the time Ann was talking to the boat and encouraging it to keep sailing. Eventually Ann got tired and the boat stopped. She showed the pretend lake to her mum and dad who smiled and said it was very nice. Ann smiled back; she knew that she had made something special.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a puzzle in her room. It was a big puzzle with many pieces. Mia was excited to put the pieces together. She started to work on the puzzle on her table. As Mia put the pieces together, she began to reveal a picture of a cute cat. She loved cats and was happy to see the cat appear. Mia worked hard on the puzzle and was very proud of herself. When Mia finished the puzzle, she showed it to her mom. Her mom was grateful and gave Mia a big hug. They both looked at the cute cat puzzle, smiling. Mia was happy that she finished the puzzle and made her mom smile.
