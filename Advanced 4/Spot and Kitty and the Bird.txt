Title: Spot and Kitty and the Bird

One day, a big dog named Spot and a small cat named Kitty were playing in the yard. They saw a little twig on the ground. Spot said, "Let's bury the twig!" Kitty said, "Yes, let's do it!" They started to dig a hole. They dug slow and careful. Soon, they had a big hole. They put the twig in the hole and covered it with dirt. They were very happy with their work. Suddenly, the ground started to shake. The dirt moved and out popped a big, friendly bird! The bird said, "Thank you for burying the twig. It was a magic twig, and you set me free!" Spot and Kitty were very surprised, but happy to make a new friend. They all played together in the yard for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Bob. He wished he had something to do, so he decided to play a game. He took out his telephone, but it wouldn't work. The telephone was dead. He was so sad, so he played a different game. He went on a search to find something he could play with. But, he couldn't find anything. Suddenly, he noticed a toy airplane in the sky. He tried to catch it, but it was too far away. He yelled and ran after it, but he was too slow. He eventually lost the airplane in the clouds. Sad and disappointed, Bob went home empty-handed. He was so upset, he almost forgot about his telephone. He decided to pick it up, just to see if it worked. But it was still dead. Bob was so sad he had lost the airplane, and his telephone wasn’t working. But he was sure he would find something fun to do tomorrow.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a favorite toy, a stuffed pepper. Lucy loved her pepper toy and always felt comfortable when she hugged it. One day, Lucy went to a show with her mom and dad. People were singing and dancing on stage. Lucy brought her pepper toy with her. When the show was over, everyone clapped their hands. Lucy learned a new word, "applaud." The next day, Lucy played at home with her pepper toy. She made her pepper toy dance and sing. When the pepper toy finished, Lucy clapped her hands and said, "applaud!" Lucy felt so happy and comfortable with her pepper toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was adventurous and loved to walk. One day, he went for a walk to a farm. The farm was big and had many animals. Tim liked the farm a lot. At the farm, Tim met a cow. The cow said, "Moo! Do you want to play?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes! Let's play!" They played all day long. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The cow jumped over the moon! Tim was very surprised. He said, "Wow! I didn't know cows could jump so high!" The cow smiled and said, "Now you know! We are magical cows!" Tim laughed and they played more. It was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a park. In the park, there was a rare, big tree. The tree was very pretty. Kids liked to play near the tree because it was so nice. One day, a new neighbor came to the park. The neighbor was a little girl. She saw the big tree and wanted to play too. She ran to the tree and started to play with the other kids. The kids and the new neighbor had fun at the park. They played near the rare, big tree all day. They became good friends and played at the park every day after that. And they all lived happily ever after.

Amy was an honest little girl who loved to play all day. One day, while she was playing, she heard something in the next room. Amy was curious and decided to go take a look. When she walked in, she saw a pair of scissors on the floor. Wondering what was so special about them, she grabbed the scissors and started to play with them. Suddenly, she heard her mom calling, "Amy, why do you have scissors?" Amy looked up at her mom and said, "I just heard something and wanted to investigate." Her mom smiled and said, "It's very honest of you to be so curious. How about we play together now?" Amy happily agreed, and the two of them continued to play with the scissors together. From then on, Amy always listened to what she heard and was an honest, curious little girl.
