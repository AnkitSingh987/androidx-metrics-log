Title: The Popular Fruit Shop and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a popular fruit shop. Many people liked to go there. One day, a boy named Tim went to the shop. He wanted to buy some fruit for his mom. He looked at all the fruits and picked the best ones. As Tim was about to leave, he saw a big box. It was moving! Tim was surprised. He opened the box to see what was inside. He found a little dog! The dog looked hungry. Tim decided to share his fruit with the dog. Tim and the dog became best friends. They went home together. When they got home, Tim's mom was happy. She loved the dog too. They all played and ate fruit together. The dog was very thankful to Tim. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play outside. One hot day, they found a big cooler in the garage. They opened it and saw many cans of juice. They were thirsty and wanted to drink some. "Let's take some juice and go to the park," Tom said. "OK, but we have to ask Mom first," Sam said. "No, we don't. Mom is busy and won't notice. Come on, it's just juice," Tom said. He grabbed two cans and put them in his backpack. Sam followed him and did the same. They ran to the park and found a shady spot under a tree. They opened the cans and drank the juice. It was cold and sweet and made them happy. They drank more and more juice until they finished all the cans. They felt full and sleepy and lay down on the grass. They did not know that the juice was not for kids. It was for grown-ups and had alcohol in it. Alcohol is bad for kids and can make them sick. Soon, they started to feel dizzy and sick. They wanted to go home but they could not stand up. They felt hot and thirsty and their heads hurt. They cried for help but no one heard them. Mom came to the garage and saw the empty cooler. She was angry and worried. She knew that the juice was for Dad's party and that it had alcohol in it. She looked for Tom and Sam and found them in the park. They were soaked with sweat and looked pale. She called an ambulance and took them to the hospital. Tom and Sam were very sick and had to stay in the hospital for a long time. They learned a hard lesson. They should have listened to Sam and asked Mom before taking the juice. They should have not taken something that was not theirs. They should have not drunk something that was not for kids. They were sorry and promised to never do it again. The moral of the story is: Always ask before you take something. Always know what you are drinking. Always be careful and safe.

Once upon a time, in a nice sunny land, there was a big palm tree. The palm tree had many friends who lived near it. There was a bird, a cat, and a dog. They all liked to play together under the palm tree. One day, the bird had to fly away to find food for her family. She told her friends, "I will miss you all, but I will come back soon." The cat and the dog were sad, but they knew the bird would come back. While the bird was gone, the cat and the dog played together under the palm tree. They had fun, but they missed their friend, the bird. After a few days, the bird came back with food for her family. The cat and the dog were very happy to see her. They all played under the palm tree again and were glad to be together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Daisy and Lola. Daisy wanted to invite Lola to a party at her house, so she made a beautiful invitation. She then went to Lola's house, but no one was home. Daisy felt a bit sad, but then she had an idea. She spotted a hole in the ground, just the right size to fit her invitation. Daisy shouted: “Lola, can you hear me? I have an invitation for you!” She put the invitation carefully in the hole and stepped back. Far away, in her garden, Lola heard Daisy's voice. She carefully took out the invitation and was very happy to read it. She knew that her friend was inviting her to a party so she quickly got ready. Finally, Lola and Daisy arrived at the party and had lots of fun together. They both learned that if you believe in yourself and work hard, you will always be able to deliver your message.
