Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a curious girl who liked to ask many questions. One day, Lucy went for a walk in the woods near her house. She saw a big tree and wanted to know who lived inside. Lucy knocked on the tree and a small bird came out. "Who are you?" asked the curious girl. The bird answered, "I am a bird who lives in this tree. I sing songs when the sun comes up to help everyone wake up." Lucy liked the bird's answer and wanted to hear its song. The next day, Lucy woke up early to hear the bird's song. She went to the tree and the bird sang a beautiful song. Lucy was happy and went back to her house to tell her mom about her new friend. From that day on, Lucy and the bird became good friends and shared many curious adventures together.

Once upon a time, in a hot summer, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the sun. She would run, jump, and laugh with her friends all day long. Lily's mom would always tell her to drink water, so she would not be thirsty. One day, Lily saw a tiny, tired bird on the ground. The bird was thirsty from the hot summer sun. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she got a small cup of water for it to drink. The bird drank the water and felt much better. It flapped its wings and flew away, singing a happy song. Lily felt very proud and happy that she could help the bird. She told her mom what she did, and her mom was proud too. From that day on, Lily always remembered to drink water and help others when they were thirsty in the hot summer sun.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Joe. Joe was only three years old. One day, Joe asked his mother if they could go to the shop. His mother said yes, so they drove to the shop. When they got there, Joe couldn't believe his eyes. There were lots of things to buy! He ran around, looking at all the toys. He chose a stuffed animal to fill his arms. Joe asked his mother, "Where are the sweets?" His mother pointed to the distant shelf. When Joe saw the pile of candy, he smiled from ear to ear. Joe filled his basket with candy, and walked to the cashier to pay for it. When the cashier gave him his change, Joe put it in his pocket. Joe thanked his mother for taking him to the shop, and they went home together.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little boy named Jake. Jake liked to eat lots of yummy treats. Everyday Jake would go to the kitchen and borrow the cookies, candies and chocolate from his mom and dad's pantry and eat them. However, this became a problem when Jake's clothes were too small for him! His parents were worried that he was getting too chubby from eating too much sweets and not enough healthy food. So, one day, his dad took him to the store and showed him a cool weight. He told Jake that he could borrow this weight and use it to help him get healthy. Jake became very excited and started to play with the weight. Jake quickly learned how to exercise by lifting the weight up and down. He was having so much fun that he kept playing with the weight for a very long time. In the end, Jake's parents were so proud of him. They could see that he was getting healthier every day by playing with the weight. The moral of the story is that it pays off to borrow things that are good for you, like exercise weights. Healthy choices make all the difference!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very eager to learn how to ride her bike. She had a red bike with a frame that was just the right size for her. Jane's dad said he would support her as she tried to ride her bike. Jane put on her helmet and climbed onto her bike. Her dad held onto the bike frame to support her as she began to pedal. Jane was so eager to ride by herself that she pedaled faster and faster. Her dad kept holding the frame so she would not fall. After a while, Jane felt ready to ride without her dad's support. She told him to let go of the frame. Her dad let go, and Jane rode all by herself! She was so happy and eager to ride her bike everywhere. Jane knew that with her dad's support, she could do anything.
