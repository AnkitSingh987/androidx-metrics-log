Title: Sara and Tom and the Toys

Sara and Tom were playing with their crayons and paper. They liked to draw animals and flowers and houses. Sara was very careful with her crayons. She always put them back in the box when she was done. Tom was not so careful. He left his crayons all over the floor. He also had an eraser that he used to rub out his mistakes. But he did not use it well. He made big holes in his paper and bits of eraser everywhere. One day, their mom came to see their drawings. She smiled at Sara's pictures. They were very pretty and neat. She frowned at Tom's pictures. They were messy and torn. She also saw the crayons and the eraser on the floor. She was not happy. She told Tom to pick up his things and to be more careful. She said that crayons and erasers are not toys. They are tools to make art. She said that if he did not take care of them, he would not have them anymore. Tom did not listen to his mom. He thought she was mean. He did not want to pick up his things. He wanted to play with his toys. He ran to his room and slammed the door. He took out his toy cars and trucks and started to zoom them around. He did not see his eraser on the floor. He stepped on it and slipped. He fell down and hurt his knee. He cried out loud. Sara heard Tom's cry. She ran to his room and saw him on the floor. She felt sorry for him. She helped him get up and gave him a hug. She also saw his eraser on the floor. She picked it up and gave it to him. She told him that he should be more careful with his eraser. She said that it could help him make better drawings. She said that she could show him how to use it. She said that she liked his drawings, but they could be more graceful. Tom looked at his sister. He saw that she was kind and helpful. He felt ashamed of himself. He said he was sorry for being messy and rude. He said he wanted to learn how to use his eraser. He said he wanted to make better drawings. He said he liked his sister's drawings, too. They were very graceful. Sara and Tom hugged each other. They went back to their crayons and paper. They decided to share their eraser and their ideas. They made beautiful drawings together. They also cleaned up their things. Their mom came back and saw their drawings. She was very proud of them. She said they had learned a valuable lesson. She said that being careful and kind makes everything better.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown elephant. The elephant lived in a green forest. He had many friends like the small birds, the fast rabbits, and the loud monkeys. They all played together and had fun. One day, the brown elephant could not remember where he put his favorite toy. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. He asked his friends to help him. The small birds flew high to see, the fast rabbits ran around, and the loud monkeys climbed trees. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came to the brown elephant and said, "I know where your toy is!" The elephant and his friends were surprised. They did not think the tiny mouse could help. But the tiny mouse led them to a big tree. There, they found the elephant's toy! The brown elephant was very happy and thanked the tiny mouse. From that day on, they all played together and had even more fun.

Once there was a bald man named Bob. He loved lollipops and was always looking for a match. One day, he went to the store and saw a big jar of lollipops. He was so excited, he shouted "Yay"! Bob looked at all of the different colors, and he tried to match the colors of the lollipops. Finally, Bob found one that was the perfect match and he quickly picked it out of the jar. Bob smiled and quickly peeled off the wrapper and sampled his new lollipop. It was so sweet and tasty, he couldn't believe it! He ate the lollipop in no time and he felt so happy. Bob went back to the store, searching for the perfect match. He was so happy when he found one and happily ate it up. He was so happy, he thought he could eat lollipops all day.
