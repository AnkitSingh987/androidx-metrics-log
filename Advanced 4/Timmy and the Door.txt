Title: Timmy and the Door

Once upon a time, a little boy named Timmy liked to wander. He wandered here and there, all around his house. One day, he found a magic door. The door was flexible, so Timmy could open it easily. He opened the door and went inside. Inside the door, Timmy found a big universe. There were stars, planets, and many colors. Timmy was very happy. He met a nice alien named Bob. Bob said, "Hi Timmy! Do you like my universe?" Timmy said, "Yes, I like it a lot!" They played together in the universe. But then, Timmy got lost. He could not find the door to go home. He looked and looked, but the door was gone. Timmy was very sad. He could not go home anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toys in his room. He felt his tummy hurt. Tim knew he needed to go to the toilet. Tim went to the bathroom and tried to open the door. The door was stuck. He called out, "Mom, I can't open the door! My tummy hurts!" His mom heard him and came to help. Mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." She opened the door for him. Tim went inside and used the toilet. His tummy stopped hurting. Tim was happy, and he thanked his mom. They both went back to play with toys together.

One day, a girl named Ann wanted to go on a picnic. She had a big bag to pack her food in. She put in some yummy treats like sweet apples, sour lemons, and tasty bread. Ann was very happy to pack her bag. Ann went to the park with her dog, Spot. They found a nice spot under a big tree. Ann put a soft blanket on the grass and opened her bag. Spot wagged his tail when he saw the treats. Ann shared her food with Spot, and they both enjoyed the sour lemons. After they ate, Ann and Spot played catch with a ball. They had a lot of fun in the sun. When they were tired, they lay down on the soft blanket and took a nap. Ann was happy she packed the treats for their picnic, and Spot was happy too.

Molly was a very independent girl. Every day she liked to explore and find new things. Today she was walking around the garden when she noticed a mole poking its head out of a hole in the ground. She wondered what the mole was doing. Molly watched the mole for a while and saw it pop down, go around the garden and come back. She was very curious. She wondered where the mole was going and what it was doing. She decided to follow the mole and find out. Molly walked around the garden but the mole was too fast. Every time she got close, the mole ran away. Molly kept trying but eventually gave up. She may never know what the mole was up to but she was happy just to watch and wonder.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bubba. Bubba loved to blow bubbles all day long. One day, a naughty little fish named Nemo came to play with Bubba. Nemo wanted to pop all the bubbles that Bubba blew. Bubba did not like that. He wanted his bubbles to float and be pretty. Bubba asked Nemo to stop popping the bubbles, but Nemo just laughed. Bubba felt sad and let out a big sigh. Then, Bubba had an idea. He blew a giant bubble around Nemo. Nemo was surprised and could not pop the bubble. He said sorry to Bubba for being naughty. Bubba let Nemo out of the bubble, and they both played nicely with the bubbles.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious big book called a dictionary. It lived in a small house with a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to play a game. The winner would get the dictionary. They all played very hard. Tim tried his best to win the game. In the end, Tim won the game! His friends said, "Wow, Tim! You won! You get the dictionary!" Tim was very happy. He took the mysterious dictionary home and shared it with his friends. They all learned new words together.
