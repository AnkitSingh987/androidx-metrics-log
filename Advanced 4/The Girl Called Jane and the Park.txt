Title: The Girl Called Jane and the Park

Once upon a time there was a small girl called Jane who was very adventurous. One day Jane was walking in the park when she saw a big, round melon on the ground. She was so excited that she couldn't help but pick it up and hold it in her arms. She wanted to know what was inside the melon so she started to repeat the word 'open' over and over again. She thought if she repeated it enough times that the melon would open by itself. Suddenly, the melon opened and Jane couldn't believe her eyes! Inside was a palace full of colourful lights, exciting places to explore and lots of fun things to do. Jane was so excited that she forgot about the melon and started to wander around the palace. She discovered new and amazing things every time she went back and even made friends along the way. The adventures of Jane and her new friends were never ending, thanks to their magical melon!

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Tom. Tom liked to balance on things. One day, Tom saw a fit bird on a tree. Tom wanted to be friends with the bird. He thought, "I will try to balance on the tree too!" Tom climbed the tree with his quick eye. He was almost at the top. Then, he saw the fit bird. Tom smiled at the bird and said, "Hi, I am Tom. I can balance too!" The bird looked at Tom and said, "Hi Tom, I am Birdy. That's great!" Tom and Birdy were having fun. They played balance games on the tree. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom lost his balance and fell down. Birdy tried to help him, but it was too late. Tom got hurt and could not play anymore. Birdy felt sad and flew away.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to act. They pretended to be animals, pirates, superheroes and more. They had a lot of fun making up stories and playing together. One day, they found a huge box in the garage. They decided to use it as a stage for their show. They cut out a window and a door, and drew some curtains and flowers on the box. They invited their parents and siblings to watch their show. Lily and Tom acted out a story about a princess and a dragon. Lily was the princess, and Tom was the dragon. They made loud noises and funny faces. Their audience clapped and laughed. But then, Tom got too excited. He started to act mean and scary. He roared and breathed fire. He chased Lily around the box and tried to catch her. Lily got scared and cried. She ran out of the box and hid behind her mom. Tom stopped acting and realized what he had done. He felt sorry and sad. He ran out of the box and said to Lily, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was just acting. I didn't mean to scare you. Can we still be friends?" Lily looked at Tom and saw that he was sincere. She wiped her tears and said, "It's okay, Tom. I know you were just acting. But you have to be careful. Sometimes, acting can hurt people's feelings. You have to know when to stop and be nice. Can we still be friends?" Tom nodded and hugged Lily. They said sorry to their audience and thanked them for watching. They learned that acting can be fun, but they have to respect each other's feelings and limits. They also learned that friends can forgive and make up. They smiled and went back to the box to act some more.

Lucy loved to play in the garden with her poppy. Poppy was a big dog with brown fur and a wet nose. Lucy liked to hug Poppy and throw a ball for him to catch. Poppy liked to run and jump and lick Lucy's face. One day, Lucy and Poppy saw a skunk in the garden. The skunk had black and white fur and a long tail. Lucy thought the skunk was cute and wanted to pet it. Poppy thought the skunk was bad and wanted to chase it. Lucy ran towards the skunk and Poppy followed her. The skunk was scared of Lucy and Poppy. It did not want to be petted or chased. It wanted to be left alone. The skunk turned around and lifted its tail. It sprayed a smelly liquid at Lucy and Poppy. The liquid was disgusting. It made Lucy and Poppy cough and sneeze. It made their eyes water and their noses burn. It made their clothes and fur stink. Lucy and Poppy ran away from the skunk. They ran to the house and cried for help. Lucy's mom came out and saw them. She was shocked and angry. She took Lucy and Poppy to the bathroom and gave them a bath. She used soap and water and vinegar and baking soda to wash away the skunk smell. She told Lucy and Poppy that they should never bother a skunk again. She told them that skunks spray when they feel threatened and that they should respect their space. Lucy and Poppy learned their lesson. They felt sorry for the skunk and for themselves. They promised to be more careful and kind in the garden. They hugged each other and said they loved each other. They smelled better but still a little funny. They hoped the skunk would forgive them and be their friend.
