Title: The Park and the Cube

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In this park, there was a little cube. The cube was red and shiny. Every day, kids would come and play with the cube. They would pick it up, throw it, and roll it around. The cube loved to play with the kids. One day, a girl named Amy came to the park. She saw the cube and wanted to play with it. But when she tried to pick it up, it felt hurt. Amy was very sad. She did not want the cube to hurt. So she stopped playing with it. Then, something unexpected happened. The cube started to glow! It became bigger and bigger until it was as big as the park! Amy and the other kids were very surprised. The cube was not hurt anymore. It was a magic cube! Now, all the kids could play with the cube together. They had so much fun, and the cube was happy too.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with lots of prunes on it. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! The tree has prunes! Do you want to pick some?" Tim nodded and smiled. Tim tried to pick the prunes, but they were too high up. He felt frustrated. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." She picked the prunes and gave them to Tim. He was happy. Then, Tim saw a cat hiding behind the tree. The cat jumped out and tried to scare Tim. "Boo!" said the cat. Tim laughed and said, "You didn't scare me, cat!" The cat smiled and ran away. Tim and his mom went home with their prunes, feeling happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big bubble floating in the air. Lily wanted to catch the bubble, so she ran and jumped to reach it. As she got closer, Lily tried to strike the bubble with her hand. But the bubble was too high. She jumped again and again, but she could not touch the bubble. Lily felt bitter because she could not catch it. Then, a friendly bird saw Lily and came to help. The bird flew up and struck the bubble for her. The bubble popped and Lily smiled. She was happy that the bird helped her. They played together all day, and Lily was not bitter anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. One day, Amy found a key on the ground. It was shiny and pretty. She wanted to find what the key could open. So, she put the key in her pocket and went on a walk. On her walk, Amy saw a big tree. She was a bit frightened, but she knew she was brave. She walked closer to the tree and saw a small door. Amy thought, "Maybe the key can open this door." She took the key out of her pocket and put it in the door. It opened! Inside the tree, Amy found a small room. In the room, there was a table with a piece of paper on it. Amy picked up the paper and saw that it had words on it. The words said, "Fold me." So, Amy folded the paper into a little boat. When she did, the boat started to grow! It became a big boat that could float on water. Amy was so happy that she found the key and the boat. She knew she would have many fun adventures on her new boat.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Anna. Anna was a very important girl because she had a very important job - every day she would raise a small plant. Anna took care of the plant very carefully, always making sure to water it and keep it warm enough. One day Anna decided she wanted to raise something special - she decided she would raise a broccoli! She was so proud of her decision - it was the most important thing she had ever done. She took very good care of the broccoli and watched it as it grew. Eventually, the broccoli was ready to be harvested. Anna was so proud of herself - she had raised something very special. But before she could pick it, she noticed something special - the broccoli had grown a small broccoli head! Anna was so proud that she shared her news with her family and friends. They all praised her for her hard work and reminded her of how important it is to take care of something special. Anna was very happy - not only had she raised a broccoli, but she'd also taught herself an important lesson in the process.
