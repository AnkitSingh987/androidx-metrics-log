Title: Amy the Girl and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play with her doll and tease her little brother, Tim. One day, Amy and Tim went to the park to play. Amy was very happy, but Tim was upset because he wanted to play with his toy car, not with Amy. At the park, Amy played with her doll and Tim played with his toy car. Suddenly, Amy felt something on her toe. She looked down and saw a tiny, friendly frog. The frog wanted to play too! Amy picked up the frog and showed it to Tim. Tim stopped being upset and smiled at the frog. Amy, Tim, and the frog played together all day long. They laughed and had fun. In the end, Amy learned that teasing her brother was not nice, and it was better to play together. The frog became their friend and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Ann. Ann liked to dream. She dreamed about a big pepper that could talk. The pepper was gloomy because it was always alone. One day, Ann went to the garden and saw the gloomy pepper. The pepper said, "Hi, Ann. I am sad because I have no friends. Can you be my friend?" Ann smiled and said, "Yes, I can be your friend, Mr. Pepper." Ann and the pepper played together every day. The pepper was not gloomy anymore. It was happy because it had a friend. Ann learned that being kind and making friends can make others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who wanted to mix colors. He was so excited to try! He got out all his paints and a brush. Then he found an old paint pot that did not have an owner. He was so excited to try it, too. The little boy carefully began to mix the colors inside the paint pot. He stirred and stirred until he had a beautiful mix of colors. But then, something went wrong. The little boy heard a loud crack and he noticed the paint pot had broken! All of the paint was spilling out of the pot and all over the floor. The little boy was very sad. The little boy had learned an important lesson: if something does not belong to him, he should not play with it. He should always get permission from the owner first.

Lily and Ben were playing in the backyard. They liked to pretend they were explorers, looking for new things. They saw a big bush with many leaves. They wondered what was behind it. "Let's go and see!" Lily said. She was brave and curious. "OK, but be careful!" Ben said. He was a little scared, but he followed his sister. They pushed the leaves aside and saw a small wooden house. It had a door and a window, but no one was inside. It looked old and mysterious. "Wow, what is this?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe it's a secret hideout," Ben said. They decided to peek inside the window. They saw a table, a chair, a bed, and some books. They also saw something else. It was a chicken. A big, white chicken. It was sitting on the bed, looking at them. "Hello, chicken!" Lily said. She smiled and waved. The chicken did not smile or wave back. It made a loud noise. "Bawk! Bawk! Bawk!" Lily and Ben were surprised. They did not expect the chicken to talk. They did not know what it was saying. "Shh, chicken, don't be noisy!" Ben said. He was afraid someone would hear them. The chicken did not listen. It kept making noise. "Bawk! Bawk! Bawk!" Lily and Ben were scared. They did not like the chicken. They thought it was angry and mean. They ran away from the window. They ran back to the bush. They ran back to their house. They did not want to play in the backyard anymore. They wanted to stay inside, where it was warm and safe. They shivered and hugged each other. They never went near the mysterious house again. They never saw the chicken again. They wondered who lived there and why they had a chicken. They never found out.
