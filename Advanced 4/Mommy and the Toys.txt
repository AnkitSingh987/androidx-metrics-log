Title: Mommy and the Toys

"Mommy, what's that?" a young girl asked her mother, pointing to a sparkly statue of an angel on a shelf. She had never seen it before. "That's an angel," her mother responded. "It's here to remind us that we can always stay ready for anything that happens in life. Like when you're ready for school or for bedtime." The little girl nodded and smiled. But as the days passed, she seemed to be forgetting the special reminder from the angel. She forgot to brush her teeth before bedtime, she was late for morning kindergarten, and she got upset when her sister touched her toys. Her mom decided to take action. She asked the little girl to look at the angel and reminded her of the angel's message. The little girl listened intently and smiled. She understood that she needed to be ready for each task and treat her sister with kindness. The angel became an important reminder for the little girl and she made sure to remember each day to stay ready and be kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big boot that he found in his yard. Tim loved his boot very much. He would play with it every day. One day, Tim saw a tiny bug near his boot. The bug was trying to pinch the boot with its little legs. Tim thought it was funny. He wanted to help the bug be successful in pinching the boot. So, Tim picked up the bug and put it on the boot. The bug pinched the boot again and again. The bug was very happy and successful. Tim was happy too. From that day on, Tim and the bug played with the boot together every day.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the museum with her mom. Mia liked the museum. She saw many things like big animals and old toys. Mia's favorite thing was the soft, fluffy chair. She could sit on it and feel happy. In the museum, there was a room where kids could mix colors to make new ones. Mia loved to mix colors. She mixed red and blue and made purple. She mixed yellow and blue and made green. Mia was having so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. When Mia mixed all the colors together, the soft, fluffy chair turned into a big, friendly bear! The bear gave Mia a big hug and said, "Thank you for mixing the colors! Now, I can play with you at the museum." Mia and the bear played all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma was a pupil at a small school. She had a pale face and big brown eyes. Emma liked to play with her friends outside. One day, Emma was playing with her friends when she got very dirty. Her face was covered in mud. Her friend, Lily, looked at her and said, "Emma, you need to bathe!" Emma went home to her mom. She told her mom what Lily had said. Her mom agreed and filled the tub with warm water. Emma got in the tub and washed all the dirt off her face and body. After her bath, Emma felt clean and happy. She went back outside to play with her friends. They all smiled at her and said, "Emma, you look so nice now!" Emma was glad that she had listened to her friend and taken a bath.

Once there was a little girl named Jane who loved to do fun things. She went to the toy store and saw something that made her very excited! It was a cute little candle! Jane held it in her hands, and it sparkled like the stars in the sky. The shopkeeper asked, "Do you like it? Would you like to buy it?" Jane smiled and said, "Yes, I really like it! Please give it to me." The shopkeeper smiled and said, "Here you go! I hope it will make you very happy!" Jane thanked the shopkeeper and ran home with her candle. She showed it to her mom who smiled and said, "That is a beautiful candle! I'm sure it will make you happy." Jane smiled and hugged her mom. She was so excited that she had her own candle!
