Title: Tim the Boy and the Universe

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, heavy ball. He called it his "own universe." Tim liked to play with his universe every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. "What's that?" she asked. "It's my own universe," Tim said. "It's very heavy, but I love it." They played with the universe together, rolling it and bouncing it around. Then, something unexpected happened. The heavy universe ball started to float in the air! Tim and Sue looked at each other, surprised. "What's happening?" Sue asked. "I don't know," Tim said. They watched as the universe floated higher and higher, until it disappeared into the sky. Tim and Sue were sad, but they knew they would always remember the day the universe flew away.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a small red bug. This bug liked to pinch. It would pinch the skin of other animals. The animals did not like this bug. They said, "Bug, you are dangerous! Stop pinching us!" One day, the bug met a big blue bird. The bird was not scared of the bug. The bird said, "Bug, if you pinch me, I will pinch you back!" The bug did not want to be pinched. It was scared of the bird. The bug thought about it and decided not to pinch the bird. The bird was happy. The other animals were happy, too. The bug learned that being dangerous was not good. So, the bug stopped pinching, and all the animals in the forest were friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Kitten. Kitten was popular with all of the other animals in the forest, because Kitten was so friendly and playful. One day, Kitten decided to follow a squirrel. "Where are you going, Kitten?" the squirrel asked. Kitten meowed and followed the squirrel up a tall tree. At the top of the tree Kitten saw a beautiful view of the forest. Kitten meowed with joy, and the squirrel said, "Let's keep exploring!"Together they explored the whole forest, hopping from tree to tree and chasing each other around. Kitten was so happy playing with the squirrel that he didn't want it to end. Kitten will always remember that day in the forest with the squirrel. And that is why Kitten is the most popular little kitten in the forest!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a friend named Ben. Amy and Ben liked to play and display their toys. They were happy and healthy kids. One day, Amy and Ben went to the park. They wanted to show their toys to each other. Amy had a big, red ball. Ben had a small, blue car. They played with their toys and shared them. "Look at my ball, Ben!" said Amy. "I like your car too!" replied Ben. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Amy's red ball away. Amy was sad, but Ben had an idea. "Let's go find your ball, Amy!" They looked everywhere for the ball. In the end, they found it stuck in a tree. Together, they got the ball back. Amy and Ben were happy again. They learned that friends help each other when something unexpected happens.

One day, a pink cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a sign that said, "Shower this way". Kitty was dirty, so she wanted to find the shower. Kitty walked and walked until she found the shower. The shower was big and wet. Kitty was happy to find it. She jumped in and started to wash. But, the shower was too strong for Kitty. The water pushed her down and she could not get up. Kitty was sad and scared. In the end, Kitty was still dirty and wet.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to go on tours around the town with his friend, a little girl named Sue. They would walk together, looking at the flowers, trees, and birds. One day, while on their tour, Tom and Sue saw a big bag with a zip. They were curious and opened the bag. Inside, they found lots of yummy treats. Sue said, "Tom, we should ask if these treats belong to someone before we eat them." Tom agreed and they went to ask the people in the town. They found the owner, a kind old lady. She was so happy to have her treats back that she shared some with Tom and Sue. They were very yummy! The moral of the story is to always be honest and do the right thing. Tom and Sue were rewarded for their honesty with yummy treats, and they learned that it feels good to help others.
