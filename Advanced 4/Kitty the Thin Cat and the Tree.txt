Title: Kitty the Thin Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Kitty. Kitty had a long neck and loved to play outside. One day, Kitty saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Kitty asked her mom, "Can I climb the tree?" Her mom said, "Yes, I will allow you to climb the tree, but be careful." Kitty was very happy and started to climb the tree. As Kitty climbed the tree, her long neck helped her see all around. She saw birds, bugs, and even a squirrel. After a fun day of climbing, Kitty came down and thanked her mom for allowing her to climb the tree.

One day, a pink cat went to the store. The cat wanted to buy soup. The cat looked at all the soup cans on display. There were many cans, but the cat did not know which one to pick. Suddenly, a small mouse came out from behind the cans. The mouse said, "I know the best soup!" The cat was surprised. Cats usually chase mice, but this cat wanted to hear about the soup. The mouse showed the cat a can with a picture of pink soup on it. The cat thought it looked good. The cat and the mouse became friends and shared the pink soup. It was the best soup they ever had.

Once there was a lucky peanut who lived in the garden. She sat in the sunshine every day and dreamed of the day when the rain would come. She waited and waited. One day when the sun was very hot, it started to rain. The peanut was so excited that she jumped around in the rain. She said, "I'm so lucky!" The rain got stronger and stronger. Other plants in the garden were scared, but the peanut stayed in the rain and shouted, "I'm the luckiest!" Soon the garden was too wet and the peanut was getting cold. She started to shiver. Suddenly, she heard a loud voice. It was the sun! The sun spoke to the peanut, "It's nice to get wet sometimes, but remember, too much of anything is not good." The peanut realised that the sun was right. She thanked the sun and since then, she has enjoyed sun and rain equally. She has taught us that a little bit of rain can make us happy, but too much of it can be dangerous.

Lily was a little girl who liked to draw and sing. She had a tutor who came to her house every week to teach her new things. The tutor was very kind and patient, and Lily enjoyed learning with her. One day, Lily was drawing a picture of a flower with her crayons. She wanted to make it very pretty and delicate, so she used many colors and shapes. She was so focused on her drawing that she did not hear her tutor calling her name. "Lily, it's time for our lesson. Please put away your crayons and come to the table," the tutor said. But Lily did not listen. She wanted to finish her flower. She ignored the tutor and kept drawing. The tutor waited for a few minutes, then she said again, "Lily, please come to the table. We have to learn some new words and numbers today." But Lily still did not listen. She thought the tutor was boring and bossy. She did not want to learn new things. She wanted to draw her flower. She shut her ears and kept drawing. The tutor was getting annoyed. She walked over to Lily and said, "Lily, this is not polite. You have to listen to me and do what I say. I am here to help you grow and learn. If you don't come to the table right now, I will take away your crayons and your picture." But Lily did not care. She thought the tutor was mean and rude. She did not want to grow and learn. She wanted to draw her flower. She shut her mouth and kept drawing. The tutor was very angry. She reached out to grab Lily's crayons and her picture. But Lily was faster. She snatched her picture and ran away. She ran to her room and slammed the door. She shut the tutor out and kept drawing. But as she ran, she did not see the stairs. She tripped and fell. Her picture flew out of her hand and landed on the floor. It was torn and smudged. Her flower was ruined. Lily cried. She was hurt and sad. She wished she had listened to her tutor. She wished she had been polite and respectful. She wished she had not shut her ears and her mouth and her door. She wished she had not lost her picture and her flower. She learned a hard lesson that day. She learned that shutting people out is not a good way to behave. She learned that listening and learning are important and fun. She learned that drawing and singing are nice, but they are not the only things that matter. She learned that being delicate is not only about flowers, but also about feelings. She apologized to her tutor and asked for another chance. The tutor forgave her and hugged her. They went to the table and had a nice lesson. Lily learned some new words and numbers. She also learned how to draw a better flower. She was happy and proud. She did not shut anyone out again.
