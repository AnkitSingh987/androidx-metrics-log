Title: Mommy and the Daisy

"Mommy! Daddy!" Daisy shouted. She was so excited to decorate the calendar. She loved picking bright colors and making fun shapes. Mommy and Daddy smiled at each other as they watched Daisy work. "What a good girl!" said Mommy. When Daisy was done, she stepped back to admire her work. Unfortunately it was not what Daisy had wanted. She was tired and wanted to go to bed. "What’s the matter, Daisy?" asked Daddy, frowning. "My calendar isn't very good." tears started rolling down Daisy's cheeks. "There, there," said Mommy, "let's try to make it better." But it was too late. Daisy was too tired to fix her mistake and her calendar was ruined. She went to bed with tears in her eyes.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big hole near the slide. Tim wanted to play near the hole, but his mom said, "No, Tim, it's not safe." Tim was sad, but he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was generous and shared her toys with Tim. They played together, far from the hole. They had lots of fun with the ball and the toy cars. While they were playing, a dog came and took their ball. It ran to the hole and dropped the ball in it. Tim and Sue wanted their ball back. They looked at each other and said, "We can work together!" Tim and Sue found a long stick and put it in the hole. They carefully pulled the ball out. They were so happy they got their ball back. From that day, Tim and Sue became best friends and always played together at the park.

Once there was an alligator named Tony. He was thin and long. Tony was walking through the swamp when he heard a strange noise. He looked around for the noise, but he couldn't see it anywhere. Suddenly, Tony heard the noise from behind him. Tony looked back and saw a little girl. She asked Tony if he could carry her over the swamp. Tony was surprised, but he said yes. The girl climbed onto Tony's back and they began walking. Tony could feel the girl's tiny hands holding onto his back, but the girl didn't make a sound. As they were walking, Tony started to think that this girl wasn't his ordinary passenger. When they finally reached the other side of the swamp, Tony stopped. The girl thanked Tony and then, to Tony's surprise, she hopped off his back, revealing her true identity. She was a small alligator, just like Tony! The alligators laughed and said goodbye. Tony thought to himself, "Maybe small alligators can carry each other too."

Once upon a time, there was a zoo. In the zoo, there were many animals. There was a big elephant who was very reliable. His name was Elly. Elly was a good friend to all the animals. One hot day, the sun was shining, and the animals were very hot. Elly had an idea. He went to the water and used his long nose to spray water on his friends. The water felt good and cool. The animals were happy. Elly sprayed water on the lion, the monkey, and the giraffe. They all laughed and played in the water. The zoo was a fun place to be. All the animals loved Elly, their reliable friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball who loved to bounce. The ball liked to exercise and play with the kids. It was always alert and ready for fun. One day, the ball saw a small rock on the ground. The ball did not want to hit the rock. The ball tried to bounce over the rock, but it was too small to jump that high. The ball was sad and did not know what to do. It wanted to keep playing with the kids, but the rock was in the way. Then, a little girl saw the ball was sad. She picked up the rock and moved it away. The ball was so happy! Now, it could bounce and exercise with the kids again. The ball was alert and ready for more fun. The little girl and the ball played all day long.
