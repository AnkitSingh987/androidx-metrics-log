Title: Joey and the Garden

One day, little Joey was playing in the garden. He loved to race around, but he was very tired. He decided to take a nap. Joey lay down on the grass and closed his eyes. Suddenly he heard a loud noise and he jumped up. In front of him he saw a white nightmare! It was huge and had big eyes. Joey tried to run away but the nightmare was too quick. It chased him around the garden. Joey was frightened and he screamed. He tripped over a rock and fell over. The nightmare came close and it opened its big mouth. Joey began to cry, he wanted his mummy. But there was no one to help him. The nightmare grabbed little Joey and swallowed him whole. The next morning, little Joey was gone and no one ever saw him again.

One day, a big automobile was driving down the road. The driver was a nice man. He was going to the store. On the way, he saw a little boy. The boy was frustrated. He wanted to play, but he had no toys. The man stopped the automobile and asked, "Why are you frustrated, little boy?" The boy said, "I want to play, but I have no toys. I want to destroy something, but I can't." The man thought for a moment and had an idea. He said, "I have a toy car in my automobile. You can play with it and pretend to destroy it. But be careful, it's just for fun." The boy was very happy. He took the toy car and started to play. He pretended to destroy the toy car, but he didn't really break it. The man and the boy played together for a while. They both had fun. The boy was not frustrated anymore. He was happy, and the man was happy too. In the end, they became friends and the man drove the boy home. The boy's mom was happy to see her son playing and not frustrated. The man and the boy promised to play together again. The automobile drove away, and the boy waved goodbye.

Once upon a time, there was a lively girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to play and have fun. One day, Lucy found a purse. The purse was pink and pretty. She picked it up and looked inside. There were many colorful things in the purse. Lucy wanted to find out who the purse belonged to. She went to her friend, Sam. She showed the purse to Sam and asked, "Is this your purse?" Sam said, "No, it is not mine. But we can ask others if it is theirs." They went to their friend, Lily. Lucy asked Lily, "Is this your purse?" Lily said, "Yes, it is mine! Thank you for finding it!" Lucy was happy to give the purse back to Lily. Lily was so thankful that she decided to share some of the colorful things inside the purse with Lucy and Sam. From that day on, Lucy, Sam, and Lily played together every day. They had lots of fun and were always lively. They learned that it is good to help others and accept their thanks with a happy heart.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, yellow lemon. Lily liked to play with her lemon. It was her favorite toy. She thought her lemon was very reliable because it was always with her. One day, Lily went to the park with her lemon. She saw her friend, Tim. Tim had a big, red ball. They played together and had lots of fun. They laughed and laughed as they played. Lily's lemon rolled away while they were playing. Suddenly, a dog came running and picked up the lemon with its mouth. Lily and Tim were very surprised. They chased the dog to get the lemon back. The dog stopped and dropped the lemon on the ground. But the lemon was not a lemon anymore. It turned into a bright, yellow ball! Lily and Tim laughed again. They played with the yellow ball and the red ball together. They had even more fun now. The yellow ball was just as reliable as the lemon. And they all lived happily ever after.
