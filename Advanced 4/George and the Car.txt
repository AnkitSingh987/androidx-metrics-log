Title: George and the Car

George was so excited for his very first camping trip! He packed all his things the night before - a sleeping bag, lantern, and a large vegetable. As he and his family drove to the campsite, he proudly waved his vegetable in the air. When they finally arrived, the family went straight to work unpacking the car. George focused on his large vegetable but soon they were all done. They set up their tent and made a campfire. Everything was going great until dinnertime. George was so excited to cook his large vegetable in the campfire but to his surprise, it had disappeared! Someone had taken his vegetable! George frantically looked all around the campsite. All of a sudden, he heard a bus drive away in the distance. As he saw the bus drive away, he realized - It was a thief! George's very first camping trip was ruined. He never found his large vegetable again and had to go to bed disappointed and sad.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat yummy food. One day, she went to the market with her mom. At the market, Mia saw many yummy things. She saw red apples, sweet bananas, and tasty cookies. Mia wanted to eat them all. Her mom said, "You can have one yummy thing, but you must promise to share." Mia thought hard. She picked the tasty cookies, and she promised to share. When they got home, Mia shared the cookies with her family. Everyone was happy, and Mia felt good because she kept her promise.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow motorcycle. It was a very pretty motorcycle. It liked to go fast and make loud noises. One day, the yellow motorcycle saw a small box. It wanted to see if it could fit inside the box. The motorcycle tried and tried, but it was too big. It could not fit in the box. The yellow motorcycle was sad. But then, it saw a little boy. The boy loved the motorcycle and wanted to ride it. The motorcycle was very happy. The boy and the yellow motorcycle had lots of fun together, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little spider. The spider liked to spin. It would spin all day and all night. It made a fine web to catch bugs to eat. One day, the spider met a big bug. The bug was stuck in the fine web. The spider tried to spin more web to catch the big bug. But the big bug was too strong. It broke free from the web and flew away. The little spider did not give up. It spun a new web. This time, it made the web even stronger. The big bug came back, and it got stuck again. The little spider was happy. It had learned to spin a fine web that was strong enough to catch big bugs.

One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went on a picnic. They brought sweet fruits, like apples and bananas, and yummy sandwiches. They found a nice spot under a big tree to sit and eat. While they were eating, a little bird came by. It asked Tim, "Can I have some of your sweet fruit?" Tim said, "No, I refuse to share." The bird went away, sad. Then, a big wind came and blew all of their food away! Tim and his mom were sad too. They had nothing to eat now. The little bird came back and said, "I found your food! I will give it back if you share with me." Tim learned that sharing is good. From that day, he always shared his things with others.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play and learn together. One day, they have a quiz at school. The quiz is about animals and their sounds. Lily is curious. She wants to know all the answers. She reads the questions and looks at the pictures. She thinks hard and writes her answers. Tom is not curious. He does not like the quiz. He thinks it is boring and hard. He does not read the questions or look at the pictures. He just writes anything. When the quiz is over, the teacher collects the papers. She says she will check them and tell them the scores later. Lily and Tom go to the playground. They see a big slide. Lily wants to go on the slide. She runs to the ladder and climbs up. Tom does not want to go on the slide. He remains on the ground. He sits on a bench and looks at the sky. Lily reaches the top of the slide. She sees Tom on the bench. She calls to him. "Tom, come on the slide! It is fun and fast!" Tom shakes his head. He does not answer. Lily shrugs. She slides down the slide. She laughs and feels happy. Tom remains on the bench. He sighs and feels sad.
