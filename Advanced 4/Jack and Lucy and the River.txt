Title: Jack and Lucy and the River

One day, Mummy took her two children, Jack and Lucy, to the river. Jack asked, “Can I play in the water?” Mummy replied, “No, it isn’t safe.” Lucy was very brave and insisted on playing. “Please Mummy, I want to go in the water.” Jack also insisted on playing. Mummy said, “No! It’s too dangerous. You might get hurt.” After lots of pleading, Mummy agreed to let them go in the river, but only if they played in a safe spot. They had lots of fun splashing in the water and playing in the sand. When they were done, Mummy said, “See, that was fun and safe!” The children agreed and thanked Mummy for a great day.

One day, a cat and a dog were talking about a holiday. The cat said, "I want to perform a dance on the holiday." The dog said, "I want to perform a song on the holiday." On the holiday, all the friends came to watch the cat and the dog perform. The cat danced and the dog sang. Everyone was happy. But then, a smelly skunk came to the holiday. The skunk said, "I want to perform too!" The cat and the dog were scared. They did not want the skunk to make everything smelly. But the skunk performed a funny dance and made everyone laugh. The cat, the dog, and the skunk became friends and had a great holiday.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived on a quiet street with his family. He loved to run and play with his ball all day long. One day, Spot's family went to the park and they took him with them. Spot was so happy to be at the park. He played and played until he was very tired. But, Spot was having so much fun that he forgot to stay close to his family. When it was time to go home, Spot looked around and could not find his family. He walked and walked until he found a dead tree. He sat under the tree and waited. Soon, his family found him and they all went back to their quiet street together. Spot was happy to be home and he promised to never forget to stay close to his family again.

Once upon a time, there was an orange cat. He was a very stubborn cat and he always wanted to do things his way. One day, he found a warm seat in the garden to take a nap on. The sun was warm and the seat was comfortable, so he happily curled up and closed his eyes. But then a little bit later, a small 3 year old girl came around the corner. She spotted the sleeping cat on the seat and she wanted to take a nap too. She approached the seat and asked the orange cat if he could move, but the cat didn't want to move - he was too stubborn. He just shook his head and made a funny noise. The 3 year old girl didn't give up though. She kept asking the cat to move from the seat, but the orange cat was too stubborn. In the end, the 3 year old girl found another seat in the garden and she happily curled up for a nap. The stubborn orange cat stayed curled up on his seat, content to take a nap in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Splash. Splash was a very independent fish. He loved to swim and play in the big sea. He had many friends, but he also liked to do things on his own. One day, Splash went on an adventure to find a place full of wonder. He swam and swam, looking for something new and fun. He saw a big rock and thought, "Maybe there is wonder behind this rock!" So, he swam around it, but there was nothing there. Splash felt sad, but he did not give up. He swam even farther and saw a big cave. "This must be it!" he thought. Splash went inside the cave and found a big, shiny pearl. He was so happy! But then, the unexpected happened. The pearl started to move! It was not a pearl at all; it was a big, friendly whale named Wonder. They became best friends and swam back home together, sharing many adventures along the way. And Splash learned that sometimes, the best wonder is found in new friends.
