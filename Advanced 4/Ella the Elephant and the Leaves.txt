Title: Ella the Elephant and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella loved to eat tasty leaves from the tall trees. One day, she saw a little bird named Bobby sitting on a branch. Ella said, "Hi Bobby! Do you want to eat some tasty leaves with me?" Bobby looked at the leaves and said, "Yes, Ella! That looks very yummy!" They both ate the tasty leaves together. But then, Bobby had to fly away to find his family. Ella was sad and said, "I will miss you, Bobby." Bobby said, "I will miss you too, Ella. But I will come back soon to eat more tasty leaves with you." Ella was happy to hear that. She knew that they would meet again and eat tasty leaves together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there were two friends, Max and Ryan. They shared a weird saw. They decided to go for a walk and take the saw with them. On their walk Max said to Ryan, "Let's use the saw!" Ryan shook his head, "No - it is too weird." But Max took the saw anyway. Ryan shouted, "Max! Stop - I don't want to share the saw!" But it was too late, as Max had already started to saw. Suddenly, Max slipped and he dropped the saw. It broke into two pieces! Max and Ryan looked at each other and sighed. They knew they couldn't share the saw anymore. The friends said goodbye and went their separate ways. What a bad ending!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy saw that she loved to play with. One day, she met a humble boy named Tim. Tim was very nice and wanted to play with Sue. "Can I play with your saw?" Tim asked. Sue did not want to share, so she said, "No! It is mine!" Tim was sad but did not demand the saw. They played with other toys instead. Later, Sue's saw broke. She was very sad. Tim saw Sue crying and wanted to help. He went to his house and brought back a magic saw. The magic saw fixed Sue's broken toy. Sue was very happy and learned to share her toys with her new friend Tim.

One day, a cute little girl named Lily went to her garden. She wanted to grow a big, pretty flower. She had a dish with seeds in it. Lily dug a hole and put the seeds in the ground. She covered the seeds with dirt and gave them water. The next day, Lily saw a tiny green thing in the ground. "Wow!" she said, "You are growing!" The green thing grew bigger and bigger. It was a flower! The flower was happy to see Lily too. "Thank you, Lily, for helping me grow," said the flower. Lily and the flower became best friends. They played in the garden every day. The flower grew bigger and prettier. Lily was so happy to have a friend. They had fun together all the time. And that is how Lily and her flower friend had the best days ever.

Roger and his mom went shopping at the grocery store. His mom was picking items off the shelves while Roger followed her. He liked to pick items too and she was fine with it as long as he behaved. At one point, Roger stopped and stared at the labels on the shelf. He asked his mom what the labels were for. She explained that the labels tell people what kind of food is inside. Roger thought it was interesting and started looking for the comfortable label. After looking around for a bit, he finally found one. It was a comfortable label with smiling faces. He was very excited and wanted to take it home. When his mom said no, he started to cry. His mom tried to explain that stealing the label was wrong and would not make him feel comfortable. To help him understand the importance of behaving, his mom promised to get him something special if he promised to never do it again. Roger promised and was very happy when his mom bought him a candy treat.
