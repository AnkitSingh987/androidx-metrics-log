Title: Sophie and the Lion

Once upon a time, a kind little girl named Sophie was playing outside in the sunshine. She love to swing back and forth in the tall green grass. Sophie decided she wanted to have a clay party! She asked her mom for some clay and her mom gladly brought her some. Sophie used the clay to make a lion, a dog and a silly bird. When she was finished, she called her friend Tom to come play. Tom was a clever and brave boy. He saw Sophie's clay animals and thought of a fun game - he thought the clay animals could come to life! Sophie and Tom started playing the game and soon their imaginations had come to life. The clay lion roared, the clay dog barked and the clay bird chirped. They were all having so much fun! At the end of the day, they heard a gentle reminder from Sophie's mom. She said, "It's important to be imaginative, but it's also important to know when it's time to stop playing and tidy up!" Sophie and Tom cleaned up the clay and Sophie thought how clever it was to play responsibly.

One day, a nice little boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a small plant in his yard. Tim thought the plant was very pretty. He wanted to help it grow big and strong. Tim ran to his mom and asked, "Mom, can I give water to the plant?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. That is a nice thing to do. The plant will be happy." Every day, Tim ran to the plant and gave it water. He talked to the plant and said, "Grow big, little plant." The plant grew taller and had many pretty flowers. Tim and his mom were very happy. They loved their nice plant.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to a great museum. Tim loved to see all the big animals and fun things in the museum. His favorite thing was the big dinosaur. "Mom, can we go see the dinosaur again?" Tim asked. Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, but first, promise me you will hold my hand so we don't get lost." "I promise, Mom!" Tim said, holding her hand tight. They walked together and saw the big dinosaur. Tim was so happy. They had a great day at the museum.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a boring, brown hill with soil. The bird thought it would be fun to play there. So, it flew down to the soil and started to whistle a happy song. As the bird was whistling, the hill started to move! It was not a boring hill, but a big, sleepy animal. The animal woke up and looked at the bird. The bird was very surprised. The big animal smiled at the bird and started to whistle too. They both whistled and played on the soil together. The bird was happy that the boring hill was a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house near the woods. One day, Tom decided to go for a walk in the woods. He wanted to discover new things and have fun. As he walked, he met a little bird. The bird looked sad and worried. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you sad and full of worry?" The bird said, "I lost my nest and I don't know where it is." Tom wanted to help the bird, so they both looked for the nest together. They searched and searched, but they couldn't find it. Just when they were about to give up, they discovered something unexpected. They found a big, beautiful nest with many birds living together happily. The little bird was surprised and happy. It decided to join the other birds and live in the big nest. Tom felt good for helping the bird. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we can discover new and wonderful things.

Once upon a time, there was a poor family in a small village. The mother of the family was very worried because they didn't have soap. Without soap, the family couldn't stay clean. One day, a kind neighbour noticed that this family didn't have soap and decided to help. He went to the market and bought some soap for the family. The kind neighbour handed the soap to the mother and said, "I think this will help improve your family's hygiene!". The mother was so grateful for the neighbour's kindness. She thanked him and said, "We don't have a lot of money so this soap will really help us out. Thank you so much!" The kind neighbour smiled and replied, "No problem. I'm happy to help you out". The mother used the soap and the family's hygiene dramatically improved. From then on, the family was able to stay clean and healthy. This family was lucky to have such a kind neighbour who was willing to help them improve their situation.
