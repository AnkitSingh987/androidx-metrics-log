Title: Joe the Boy and the Door

Once, there was a little boy named Joe. Joe loved to go to the library and look around, but one day it smelled very smelly! Joe said, "Ew, why does it smell so yucky?" All of the sudden, Joe heard a noise coming from the back of the library. He thought to himself, "what could that be?" So, he followed the noise until he stopped at a big door. He grabbed the handle and opened it, and there he saw a big stinky rolling ball of smelly cheese! He said in astonishment, "What is that?" Joe quickly backed away and closed the door. Then he ran away with a grin on his face. From then on, Joe would come to the library and, instead of books, he would look for the smelly rolling ball of cheese.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He liked to look at the sky and see the stars. One day, Tom had a big dream. He wanted to get a planet for himself. Tom asked his friend, the strong ant, to help him. The ant knew where to find a heavy planet. They went on a long walk to find it. It was round and blue, just like the Earth. Tom and the ant tried to pick it up, but it was very heavy. They needed more help. They asked their friends, the birds, to help them. The birds were happy to help. They all worked together to lift the heavy planet. Tom was so happy to have his own planet. He thanked his friends and promised to share it with them. And they all played together on the new planet every day.

Amy was very excited. She was going to the park with her family. Her mom brought out an automobile and it was pale. It had four doors and lots of windows. She got in the car and looked out of the window as they drove away. When they arrived, Amy couldn't wait to explore the park. She saw lots of interesting things and made lots of discoveries. She even saw an airplane fly overhead! After playing in the park for a while, it was time to go home. Amy and her family got back in the pale automobile and drove away. Amy looked out the window and waved goodbye to the park. She was already looking forward to the next time they could go.

Once upon a time, there was a kind cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to drink milk every day. She had a calendar on the wall to remember when to drink her milk. One day, Kitty saw a big red mark on the calendar. She did not know what it meant. She thought maybe it was a special milk day. So, she went to find her milk bowl, but it was not there. Kitty looked everywhere for her milk bowl. She found it outside, but it was full of water, not milk. A little bird was drinking the water. The bird said, "Thank you, Kitty, for sharing your bowl." Kitty felt happy to help the bird and learned that sharing can be fun too. From that day on, Kitty and the bird became good friends, and they shared their drinks together.

One day, a little boy was playing in his yard. The sun shone brightly and the yard was open. He could see far into the sky and the trees waved in the wind. He ran around feeling free and happy. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Come hold my hand," it said. He looked around and saw a lady standing nearby. She looked kind and she smiled at him. The little boy hesitated, but then he took the lady's hand. She held his hand tightly and said, "Let's walk together around your yard." The little boy smiled and enjoyed their walk. He looked around and noticed how much he liked his wide open yard. As they walked, the lady told him all the nice things that he could find in his yard. He laughed with joy and felt warm and happy. At the end of their walk, the lady gave him a big hug and left. The little boy waved goodbye and thanked her for the nice walk around his open yard.
