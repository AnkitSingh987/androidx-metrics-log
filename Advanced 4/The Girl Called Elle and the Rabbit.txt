Title: The Girl Called Elle and the Rabbit

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Elle. She was three years old and loved playing in her home. One day Elle heard a strange noise in the room. She looked around but couldn't see where it was coming from. Then she saw a folder on the floor. It was making a noise! Elle was curious and wanted to open the folder to see what was inside. But it was closed tight! Then Elle had an idea. She grabbed a can of spray and sprayed the folder. The folder suddenly opened and Elle was amazed! Inside the folder was a deaf rabbit. Elle was very excited and hugged the rabbit. From then on, Elle and the deaf rabbit were best friends. They played together every day! The moral of the story is that even the smallest of things can open new doors.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Max and Sarah. They always played together. Today was a very special day. They were going to celebrate Max’s birthday. Max and Sarah found a special place. It was a lush ground with delicate flowers. Sarah said, “Let’s have lots of fun and games!” Max was so happy. Max and Sarah played tag and hide-and-seek. Before they knew it, it was time for the celebration. Max blew out the candles on his special birthday cake. They ate and laughed and shared stories. Finally, Sarah and Max hugged goodbye, as it was getting late. Max looked around, taking in all the wonderful memories of his special day. He smiled and knew that he had had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a messy monster. He was always making messes, and he was never done making them! Everywhere he went, he made a mess. One day, the monster came to a house in the woods. He thought it was empty until he heard a voice. “Please don’t make a mess,” said the voice. It was a little girl, and she was very scared. But the monster only laughed. He laughed and said, “I like to make messes, and I’m not done yet!” The little girl was very scared, but then she saw a pile of sticks and said, “Please don’t make a mess. I can show you a better way to make something beautiful.” So the monster listened and the little girl took the sticks and made a secret fire. Soon, smoke filled the air and the little girl said, “Now you can take the smoke and do something complete.” The monster was surprised, but he agreed. He took the smoke and made a beautiful bird with it. The bird flew away, and the monster was happy to have made something complete. The monster learned an important lesson that day: even if it feels like things might be a mess, with a little help, you can always make something complete.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a child. The child liked to play games and touch things. One day, the child went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The child was very happy. In the park, the child saw a big red ball. The child wanted to touch the ball. The child ran to the ball and touched it with a big smile. But the ball was not an ordinary ball. It was a magic ball. When the child touched the magic ball, something bad happened. The ball started to grow and grow. It became so big that it pushed the child out of the park. The child was very sad and scared. The ball kept growing, and soon it was too big for the town. The town was not the same anymore, and the child could not play in the park again.

One day, a bald man named Tom was walking down the street. He saw lots of cars and buses. There was a lot of traffic. Tom wanted to cross the street to get to the park. Tom saw a little girl named Sally standing by the road. She looked scared. Tom asked, "Why are you scared?" Sally said, "I want to go to the park, but the traffic is too big and loud." Tom had an idea. He told Sally, "Let's shout very loud, and the cars will stop for us." They both shouted, "Stop, cars!" The cars stopped, and Tom and Sally crossed the street safely. In the park, Tom and Sally played on the swings and slides. They had so much fun. They were happy they could cross the street and not be scared of the traffic anymore.
