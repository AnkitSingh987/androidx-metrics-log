Title: The Cat Was Walking and the Dog

One day, a little cat was walking in the woods. The cat was fearful because it was her first time away from home. She saw a big tree and thought it would be a good shelter to hide from the rain. As the cat hid under the tree, she saw a small dog. The dog said, "Hi, I am lost and I can't find my way home. Can you help me?" The cat wanted to resist talking to the dog because she was scared. But she knew the dog needed help, so she said, "Yes, I will help you find your home." They walked together and found a strange house. The cat and the dog went inside the house and found a big surprise. It was a party with all their friends! They were not lost anymore, and they were not fearful. They had fun at the party and made new friends. The cat and the dog were happy that they did not resist helping each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a modest girl named Sue. Sue loved to skip and play with her friends. One day, Sue saw a race was going to happen in her town. She wanted to join the race and win. Sue thought that if she could skip fast, she could win the race. The day of the race came, and Sue was very excited. She skipped and skipped as fast as she could. All the other kids were running, but Sue kept skipping. Sue's friends cheered her on, and she felt happy. As they got closer to the finish line, Sue saw that she was about to win. She felt a big victory was going to happen. But then, Sue saw her friend Tom fall down. Sue stopped skipping and went to help Tom. She knew that winning was not as important as helping her friend. Together, they crossed the finish line, and everyone clapped for them. Sue did not win the race, but she learned that being kind and helping others is the best victory of all.

One day, a bossy reindeer was in the woods. He liked to tell the other animals what to do. He thought he was the best. The other animals did not like the bossy reindeer. The bossy reindeer said to a squirrel, "You be quiet and go away!" The squirrel was sad and ran away. The bossy reindeer was happy because he thought he won. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bear came to the woods. The bear was not happy with the bossy reindeer. The bear said, "You be nice to the other animals or I will be bossy to you!" The bossy reindeer was scared of the big bear. He said, "I will be nice now!" And from that day, the bossy reindeer was nice to all the animals in the woods.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to explore. One day his mommy took him to a big lake. He saw a big tree by the lake and climbed it. He wanted to see what was around the lake. Suddenly he heard a loud noise. It was a big fish jumping out of the lake! The little boy was so excited, he wanted to swim in the lake and play with the fish. But his mommy said it was too dangerous. The little boy was sad, but he accepted what his mommy told him. Instead of swimming, he walked around the lake and saw some birds and squirrels. He watched them for a while and then it was time to go. The little boy was happy he accepted his mommy’s advice. Now he had a wonderful memory of the loud fish and the peaceful lake. And he knew it was best to stay safe and not swim.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play in the mud. One day, he heard a loud sound. It was a parade! Buddy ran to see the parade and got very dirty. At the parade, Buddy met a little girl named Lucy. Lucy saw how dirty Buddy was and said, "Buddy, you need a bathe!" Buddy wagged his tail and agreed. Lucy took Buddy to her house to give him a bath. Lucy filled a big tub with water and bubbles. She helped Buddy get in and started to bathe him. Buddy splashed and played in the water. When he was all clean, Buddy and Lucy went back to the parade. They had so much fun together at the parade, and Buddy was happy to be clean.
