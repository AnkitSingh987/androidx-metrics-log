Title: The Pale King and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a pale king. He lived in a big castle. The king had a pet. The pet was a frog. The frog liked to hop. One day, the frog went for a hop. It hopped and hopped. It hopped far away from the castle. The frog got lost. The pale king was sad. The king looked for the frog. He looked near the castle. He looked far from the castle. But he could not find the frog. The king stayed sad. The end.

Franky was walking down the street when he passed a vegetable stand. He saw a big pile of radishes and they were all in different colors. He stared at them, wondering what they were. The seller smiled at Franky and said, "These are radishes, a kind of vegetable. Would you like to buy some?" Franky looked around and after a few moments he slowly nodded. The seller came up to Franky and said, "Do you want the regular radish or the flavored ones?" Franky didn't know what the seller was talking about, so he just nodded again. The seller smiled again and picked out a regular radish for Franky. He paid for the radish and thanked the seller. He took the radish and began walking again. Franky soon forgot all about the radishes and was soon off on a new adventure.

Sam was getting ready for his big test. He had to manage a chamber that contained some strong items. Sam was nervous. He had never been in a chamber like this before. But he was determined to do it! He put on his helmet and slowly pushed opened the chamber door. He stepped inside, and was surprised to see how strong the items were. He just focused on what he had to do: manage the items. So he slowly took each item, one by one. He lifted it, and moved it to the other side of the chamber. When he was done, he felt so proud of himself that he could manage the chamber on his own. He opened the door to leave the chamber and saw his family smiling proudly at him. They knew he could do it, and with his determination and strength, he had managed it.

Mummy was in the park with her baby. It was a wet day, but that didn't stop them from having fun. Mummy looked at her happy infant and said, "Let's go for a walk!" She held the baby's hand and they started walking. On their journey Mummy noticed a small puddle and she asked the infant, "Do you want to make it bigger?" The infant smiled and nodded. Mummy and the infant began splashing in the water. Drip, drop, splash! They made the puddle bigger and bigger. Soon it was a big enough to jump in. Mummy said, "Let's see who can jump the furthest!" The infant giggled and they both tried. Eventually they had to go home but they were happy. They had completed their adventure in the park. Mummy said to the infant, "That was a wet fun time wasn't it?" The infant smiled and nodded.

One day, a big bossy square and a little round circle were playing in the park. The square liked to tell the circle what to do. "Roll over there," said the square. The circle did not like it, but he rolled away. As they played, they saw a new shape. It was a tall triangle. The square wanted to be friends with the triangle. The bossy square said, "Lean on me, triangle!" The triangle did not like the bossy square, but he leaned on the square anyway. Then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew the bossy square away. The triangle and the circle were happy. They played together without the bossy square. The circle said, "I like you, triangle!" The triangle said, "I like you too, circle!" And they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a woman who wanted to learn how to be careful. She lived in a small house near a big tree. One day, she saw a cat stuck high up in the tree. The cat was scared and cried for help. The woman wanted to help the cat, so she asked her friend, the bird, "How can I be careful and save the cat?" The bird said, "You must go slow and watch your step. Do not look down, and you will be safe." The woman started to climb the tree. She was very careful as she went up, just like the bird told her. But, when she got to the top, she saw that the cat was not really stuck! The cat was just playing a trick on her. The woman was surprised but also happy that she had learned to be careful. She and the cat became good friends, and they played together under the big tree every day.
