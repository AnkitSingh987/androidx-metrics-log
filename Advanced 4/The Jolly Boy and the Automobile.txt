Title: The Jolly Boy and the Automobile

Once upon a time there was a jolly boy who loved to explore. One day he saw something wonderful - an automobile. He wanted to join in all the fun it was having! So he asked the driver if he could go too. The driver said yes, and the boy was so very happy. He jumped into the automobile and it drove off. It was very exciting! Everywhere it went, the boy felt more and more jolly. Along the way, other cars joined in the fun. The boy laughed and waved to them as they drove next to him. Finally, it was time for the boy to go home. He had had so much fun exploring in the automobile. He waved goodbye to all the other cars, and thanked the driver for taking him on such an exciting adventure. From then on, the boy never forgot the wonderful time he had when he joined in with the automobile.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his shiny crystal ball. He would roll it around the house and watch it sparkle. One day, Tom took his crystal ball outside to play in the sun. Tom met a little girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's crystal ball and said, "Wow, I love your crystal ball! It's so pretty. Can I play with it too?" Tom nodded, and they played together with the crystal ball all day long. At the end of the day, Sue said, "Thank you, Tom. I had so much fun playing with you and your crystal ball." Tom was happy that he made a new friend. From that day on, Tom and Sue played together every day, and they both loved the shiny crystal ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a little red ball that she loved to play with. One day, it started to rain outside. Amy was sad because she could not play with her ball in the rain. Amy's mom saw her looking sad and said, "Why don't you play inside today, Amy?" Amy nodded and decided to play with her ball inside the house. She was very careful not to break anything. As Amy was playing, she accidentally threw her ball too hard. It hit a cup and the cup fell to the floor. The cup did not break, but Amy was scared. Her mom came into the room and said, "It's okay, Amy. Just be more careful next time." Amy smiled and said, "I will, Mom!" She continued to play with her ball inside, being very careful not to break anything else. The rain stopped, and Amy was happy that she could play with her ball both inside and outside.

Once upon a time there were two friends, a fish and a kitten. They liked to discuss things, especially string! One day, the kitten wanted to play with a big string but the fish said it was too scary. The kitten was so sad because it really wanted to play with the string. The fish suggested playing with a different string, one that was much smaller. But the kitten was still sad and kept wanting to play with the big string. The fish saw the kitten was sad and wanted to help. So the fish decided to be brave and play with the big string with the kitten. They both found it was not really scary at all! They played and played until they were both so tired they had to lie down and take a nap. The two friends had a great day and were happy. They learned that things don't have to be scary, if you try them together with a friend.

Once upon a time there was a panda. His name was Ben. Ben was very tired. He wanted to go somewhere and rest. So Ben started to climb. He climbed over a big hill. It took him a long time because he was so tired. Finally he reached the top of the hill. And there he saw a big tree. He climbed up the tree and sat on a branch. Ben looked around him. Everywhere he could see was beautiful. The air was fresh and the sky was blue. He felt so happy. Ben closed his eyes and took a long nap. When he woke up he was full of energy. Then he climbed down the tree and went back home.
