Title: Jane the Girl and the Ball

One day, a girl named Jane went to the park with her mom. They took a big bag with them. Jane was very happy because they were going to unpack the bag and have a fun day. In the bag, there were toys, food, and a big ball. Jane loved to play with the ball. She would throw it and make it go in motion. The ball would roll and roll, and she would run after it. As Jane played with the ball, she saw a big, hard rock on the ground. She thought it would be fun to kick the ball at the rock. When she kicked the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball did not bounce back to her. Instead, it went up, up, up into the sky! Jane and her mom looked up and saw the ball stuck in a tree. They tried to get it down, but it was too high. Jane was sad. Then, a friendly bird flew by and saw the ball. The bird gave it a little push, and the ball fell down. Jane and her mom were so happy! They thanked the bird and continued to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a great red collar. One day, Spot went for a walk with his friend, a cat named Whiskers. While they walked, Whiskers said, "I want to gain a great collar too!" Spot smiled and said, "We can look for one together!" They walked and looked for a collar for Whiskers. As they looked, they found a big box. Inside the box was a great green collar for Whiskers. But when Whiskers put the collar on, something unexpected happened. Whiskers turned into a big, colorful bird! Spot and Whiskers were very surprised. Now, Spot and Whiskers could play in the air and on the ground. They had more fun than ever before. And they both had great collars to show off to their friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Anna. Anna was the sweetest three year old around. One day, Anna was walking with her mom and something caught her eye. It was a beautiful yellow flower growing right in the sidewalk. Anna couldn't help but pick it up. She ran over to show her mom the perfect flower. Anna's mom was very proud of her and said, "Anna, I'm so proud of you for finding such a perfect flower. Now you have to put it back where you found it." Anna started to cry because she didn't want to let her perfect flower go. Anna's mom was getting worried and called out to a nearby police officer. She explained the emergency and the police officer said he would help. The police officer walked over to Anna and in a kind and gentle voice said, "Anna, I understand how it feels to see something so beautiful, but this flower belongs in nature, and it needs to go back. You can come with me to pick a different flower for you to keep forever." Anna smiled, stopped crying and agreed to go with the police officer to find a perfect flower for her. The end.

One day, a brown dog named Max found a big jar of olives. Max loved olives so much. He wanted to eat them all. But Max had a little sister named Lily. Lily loved olives too. Max did not want to share the olives with Lily. He thought of a plan. He would measure the olives and only give Lily a little bit. Max started to measure the olives. He put some in a small cup for Lily and the rest in a big cup for him. Lily saw what Max was doing. She was not happy. She wanted to share the olives fairly. Lily said, "Max, we should share the olives the same way." Max thought about it and knew Lily was right. Max and Lily measured the olives again. This time, they made sure both cups had the same amount of olives. They were both happy and ate the olives together. Max learned that sharing was much better than keeping everything for himself.
