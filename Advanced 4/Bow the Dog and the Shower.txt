Title: Bow the Dog and the Shower

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bow. Bow was a smelly dog. He liked to play in the mud and roll in the dirt. Bow's friends would laugh and play with him, but they did not like how smelly he was. One day, Bow's mom said, "Bow, you need a shower!" Bow did not like showers, but he knew his mom was right. So, Bow went into the bathroom and turned on the water. The shower was wet and cold at first, but then it got warm and nice. Bow played in the shower, splashing water all over. He used soap to wash away the dirt and mud. When Bow was all clean, he came out of the shower. Now, Bow was not smelly anymore. His friends were happy to play with him, and Bow was happy too.

Jack and Sarah were playing in their backyard. They always had fun playing together. But this time, Jack wanted to play his own way and Sarah disagreed. Jack said, "I want to play with my style this time." Sarah replied, "No, let's play my way." Jack said, "I want to be independent and do things my own way. Can I please?" Sarah thought for a moment and said, "OK, you can be independent and play your own style." Jack and Sarah had a lot of fun playing that day by each choosing their own style of play. Jack was proud of himself for being independent and doing things his own way, and Sarah was happy that Jack was happy.

The sun spread over the sky, making everything bright and happy. Mary looked up at the sky and said, “It sure is a beautiful day!” Mary’s mother smiled and agreed, “Yes, it is.” Mary’s mother said, “I think it’s time to take you to see your daughter!” The family excitedly drove out to the park and saw their daughter playing. The little girl just spread out on the grass, so happy and content. The family watched her helplessly, wishing they could take her home with them. The little girl spread her arms out wide and waved. Mary’s mother said, “It looks like she wants us to join her!” The family laughed and spread out on the grass, joining the little girl in play. They enjoyed the beautiful sun spreading light, and the family felt warm and happy. They would never forget this joyful time spent together.

Once upon a time there were two little friends, Toby and Emily. They were playing in the park together one sunny day. Toby could see the birds in the sky and hear their songs. “They look so happy!” he said, “I wish I could fly like them.” Emily had an idea. She ran back to the swings and set Toby in one of them, then gave him a push. Toby was so busy laughing that he almost forgot about the birds. But then his stomach growled. Emily had an even better idea. She ran home as fast as she could, and came back with a plate of toast. It was so noisy with the crunching and munching that even the birds stopped singing to look over and see what was going on. Once they were finished, Toby and Emily were so tired. All they wanted to do was go home and take a nap. But first, they made a promise to each other that they would always remember to be kind to one another and share their food, just like Emily had shared her toast with Toby. The moral of the story is: we all have special gifts to share with each other, and it's important that we always be kind to one another.

Once upon a time, there was a thin crocodile. He lived near a big river. He liked to swim and play all day. One day, while the crocodile was playing, he met a small bird. The bird was sad because she lost her mom. The crocodile wanted to help the bird. He said, "Don't cry, little bird. I will help you find your mom." They looked and looked for the bird's mom. The crocodile asked his friends in the river, but no one saw her. Then, the bird had an idea. She said, "I will yell very loud, so my mom can hear me." The bird yelled, "Mom! Mom! Where are you?" Suddenly, they heard a voice. It was the bird's mom! She yelled back, "I am here, my baby!" The crocodile and the bird followed the voice. They found the bird's mom. The bird was so happy. She thanked the crocodile for his help. They all became good friends and played together every day.
