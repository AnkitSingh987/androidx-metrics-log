Title: Blink the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Blink. He was named Blink because he would blink his eyes a lot. Blink was an independent dog who liked to play and run all by himself. He had a long neck which made it easy for him to look around and find new places to explore. One sunny day, Blink was playing in the park. He blinked his eyes and saw a big tree. He ran to the tree and started to dig a hole to hide his favorite toy. But, as he was digging, he found a big, shiny ball. Blink had never seen a ball like this before. He was so excited that he forgot all about his toy. Blink decided to take the shiny ball home with him. As he walked, he held the ball in his mouth by his long neck. When he got home, he put the ball down and blinked his eyes. Suddenly, the ball started to change colors! It turned red, then blue, then green. Blink was amazed! He had found a magic ball that could change colors. From that day on, Blink and his magic ball became best friends, and they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Polly. She was a dreamer, and she loved anything that had to do with modern offices. One day, she was walking past a modern office when she saw a huge door. She looked around, but no one seemed to be looking. So, Polly opened the door and went inside. It was very dark in the office and Polly began to get scared. Suddenly, she heard someone whisper, "Don't go any further." It was an old woman who was sitting in a chair in the corner. "Do you work here?" Polly asked. The old woman replied, "Yes, I do. And I have to tell you something very important." The old woman told Polly, "You should never just walk into somebody else's place. It is not polite and it is bad manners." Polly said she was sorry and started to leave. But as Polly reached the door, she heard the old woman say, "Please, one more thing. Always remember that dreams are only a fantasy. Never forget that real life is not as easy as dreaming." Polly left the office with a heavy heart. Even though she was a dreamer, she realised that it was best to stay away from things that weren't meant for her. Never again did Polly ever dream of entering a modern office.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny. The little bunny had a friend, a tiny bird. They liked to play together every day. One day, the tiny bird saw something dangerous. It was a big snake. The snake liked to lie in the sun. The tiny bird knew that the snake could hurt her friend, the little bunny. The tiny bird told the little bunny, "Don't go near the snake. It is dangerous!" The little bunny listened to his friend. He did not go near the snake. Instead, he held the tiny bird's hand, and they played far away from the snake. They were safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was an honest boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite toy, a big cube. He would take the cube with him everywhere he went. He played with the cube all day. One day, Tim took his cube to the park. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue, so he let her play with his cube. Sue was happy, and they played together all day. At the end of the day, Sue found her toy. She thanked Tim for being honest and kind. Tim was happy that he could help Sue. They became good friends and took their toys to the park to play together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box in his room. He liked to store all his toys in the box when he was done playing. One day, Tim's mom asked him to put his toys away. "Tim, please store your toys in the box," she said. Tim started to put his toys away, but then he felt a pain in his foot. He looked down and saw that he stepped on a toy car. "Ouch!" he cried. His mom came running to see what happened. "What's wrong, Tim?" she asked. "I have pain in my foot. I stepped on a toy car," Tim said, tears in his eyes. His mom looked at the toy car, which was now far away from Tim's foot. "That toy car is distant now, but we need to be more careful when we store our toys, so we don't get hurt," she told him. Tim agreed, and they put away the rest of the toys together, making sure there were no more accidents.
