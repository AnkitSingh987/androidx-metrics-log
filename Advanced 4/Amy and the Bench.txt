Title: Amy and the Bench

Once there was a park with a big playground and a bench in the middle. One day a small girl named Amy was playing in the playground and she noticed the bench was empty. She walked over to it and sat down. It was very quiet with no one around. The sun was warm and she closed her eyes and enjoyed the peaceful feeling. Then she heard a voice. It said, "Hello!" She looked up and saw an old man standing there with a friendly smile on his face. Amy was a bit scared of the man, but he asked her if she wanted to have a chat, so she said yes. Amy and the old man talked for a long time about silly things like the size of Amy’s shoes and what she wanted to be when she was a grown up. When they were done, they said goodbye and Amy walked away feeling happy. She looked back at the bench and smiled. It was the most quiet, but best conversation she had ever had.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved going to his school. It was a mild day, and the sun was shining. Tim had lots of fun playing with his friends at school. They played games, sang songs, and learned new things. One day, Tim's teacher showed the class a new game. The game was called "reverse". In the game, the children had to walk backward. Tim thought this game was very funny. He laughed as he tried to walk backward without bumping into his friends. Tim and his friends played "reverse" all day. They had so much fun at school. When it was time to go home, they were all very happy. Tim couldn't wait to go back to school and play "reverse" again.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy had lots of soft wool. One day, Farmer Ted needed to deliver the wool to the town. Fluffy saw Farmer Ted and asked, "Where are you taking my wool?" Farmer Ted said, "I am going to deliver it to the town, so people can make warm clothes." On the way to town, a big dog came. Fluffy was alert and saw the dog. Fluffy said, "Dog, please don't hurt us. We are taking the wool to town." The dog was nice and said, "I will not hurt you. I will help you deliver the wool." So, Fluffy, Farmer Ted, and the big dog went to town together. They delivered the wool, and everyone was happy. Fluffy felt proud that her wool would help keep people warm.

Mama and Baby Emma were walking together on a cold day. Mama had a shawl to keep her warm, but Emma was feeling the chill in the air because she didn't have a coat. Mama looked in her bag and saw a round hat and a jacket which was a bit cheap. "Oh, what luck!" said Mama, and she quickly handed them to Emma. As soon as Emma put the clothes on, they felt warm and cozy. Mama smiled, but Emma was still shivering. Mama took off her shawl and wrapped it around Emma's shoulders. Now baby Emma was snuggly and warm. They continued walking until they reached the park. Mama saw a man selling round cotton candy. She bought some for Emma, who was delighted. She asked if she could have a taste, and the man handed her a stick. But as Emma put it to her lips, it fell apart and her mouth was full of sugary pieces. Emma started to shiver, so Mama gave her a cup of hot cocoa to warm her up. Emma smiled, feeling happy and warm once again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make them talk to each other. One day, Lily found a small empty bottle. She thought it would be fun to pretend it was perfume for her toys. Lily said to her toy bear, "Bear, do you want some perfume?" Bear shook his head and said, "No, thank you. I disagree. I don't want perfume." Lily laughed and asked her toy cat, "Cat, do you want some perfume?" Cat nodded and said, "Yes, please!" Lily pretended to spray the empty perfume bottle on Cat. Cat smelled so nice! Bear saw how happy Cat was and decided to try the perfume too. Lily sprayed the empty bottle on Bear, and now both toys smelled very nice. Lily was happy that her toys liked the perfume. They played together all day, smelling nice and having fun. And from that day on, whenever Lily found an empty bottle, she would pretend it was perfume for her toys.
