Title: Amy the Girl and the River

Once there was a little girl named Amy. She was only three years old but already she was very polite. One day, Amy went out for a walk and came upon a river. On the other side of the river, she saw a bucket. She wanted to get the bucket but wasn't sure how. She noticed that the river was too deep for her to wade through and had to figure out how to reach the other side. She thought and thought but had no idea. So, she decided to go back home and ask her mom for help. But her mom told her that the only way she would get the bucket was if she learned how to swim. Because she was so polite, Amy knew that she had to try. Even though she was scared, Amy promised herself that she would eventually cross the river and get the bucket. Amy went to the river with her swimming instructor. Even with the instructor's help, it was tricky for Amy to cross the river without drowning. She struggled and struggled, but eventually managed to get to the other side. Now she stands proudly beside the bucket she worked so hard to get. Amy has learned that if you work hard and stay polite, anything is possible!

One day, a little girl named Ann went for a walk in the park. She saw a poor, sad cat sitting near a tree. Ann wanted to help the cat, so she walked up to it and said "Hi." Ann and the cat started to talk. They became friends and decided to play together. While they were playing, they found a razor on the ground. Ann knew it was not safe for kids or cats to touch. Ann told the cat, "We need to tell a grown-up about the razor." So, they went to find Ann's mom. Ann's mom was happy they told her about the razor. She picked it up and put it away. Ann and the cat were proud they helped keep the park safe. They played together all day and became best friends.

One day, a big ambulance was driving down the road. It was going very fast. The ambulance was trying to help someone who was hurt. The people in the ambulance were nice and wanted to make the hurt person feel better. But, there was a rude car on the road. The rude car did not want to let the ambulance go by. The ambulance had to go around the rude car to keep going. The people in the ambulance were not happy with the rude car. Then, the ambulance had to reverse. It went back a little bit. The rude car saw that the ambulance needed to go fast to help the hurt person. The rude car finally moved out of the way. The ambulance went on to help the hurt person and everyone was happy.

One day, a little dog named Sam went to the pool with his friend, a duck named Lily. They played and splashed in the water all day. They laughed and had so much fun. As the sun began to set, Sam started to yawn. "I'm tired," he said to Lily. "Let's go home and rest." Lily agreed, and they both walked back to their homes. When Sam got home, he saw that his toy was broken. He felt guilty because he knew he had left it near the pool. He said to his mom, "I'm sorry, I broke my toy." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay. We can fix it together."

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy animal named Bobo. Bobo was big and liked to play with his friends. One day, Bobo saw a sign near his home. The sign said, "Play here." Bobo went to the place with the sign and found many animals playing. He saw a small animal named Mimi. Mimi was good at jumping. Bobo wanted to play with Mimi, so he asked, "Can I play with you?" Mimi said, "Yes, let's play!" They played a fun game of catch. Bobo was clumsy, but Mimi helped him get better. They both had a great time and became good friends. From that day on, Bobo and Mimi played together every day near the sign.
