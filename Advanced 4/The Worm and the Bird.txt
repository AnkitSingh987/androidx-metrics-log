Title: The Worm and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a little worm. This worm was very wise. He knew many things about the world. He had many friends in the garden. They liked to talk to him and learn from him. One day, a tiny bird came to the wise worm. The bird was sad. The bird whispered, "Wise worm, I lost my way home. Can you help me?" The wise worm thought for a moment. He knew just what to do. The wise worm whispered to the bird, "Follow the big tree. It will show you the way home." The tiny bird thanked the wise worm and flew to the big tree. Soon, the bird found its home. The bird was very happy. The wise worm smiled, knowing he had helped his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly spider named Sam. Sam liked to hang from the drain by his web. He had many friends who lived nearby. One day, a little girl named Lily saw Sam hanging by the drain. She said, "Hi, friendly spider! Why do you hang there?" Sam replied, "I like to hang here because I can see all my friends!" Lily liked Sam and they became good friends. They played and talked every day. Sam showed Lily how to hang from the drain too. They had fun together and were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim liked to eat avocados. He knew that avocados were green and yummy. Every day, he would eat an avocado with his lunch. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom to buy some avocados. He saw a big pile of avocados and got very excited. He wanted to pick the best avocado, but he was clumsy and knocked over the pile. Tim felt sad and did not know what to do. His mom helped him clean up the mess. She told him that it was okay to be clumsy sometimes. They picked up the avocados and took them home. Tim learned to be more careful and still enjoyed his yummy avocados every day.

Once upon a time, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her attractive yarn ball. The yarn ball was red, blue, and yellow. It was very pretty. One day, Kitty saw the yarn ball roll away. She wanted to catch it. Kitty ran fast and jumped high to catch the yarn ball. She was very happy when she caught it. Kitty played with the yarn all day. She was very tired at night. Kitty went to bed with her attractive yarn ball. She had a good sleep and had fun dreams about catching more yarn balls.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and read books. One day, Tim was searching for a new word in his book. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Tim's mom saw that he was getting angry. She asked, "Tim, why are you angry?" Tim said, "I cannot find the word I am searching for in my book." His mom smiled and said, "Let me help you." Together, Tim and his mom searched for the word in the book. They looked at every page until they finally found it. Tim was so happy, and his mom was proud of him. From that day on, Tim and his mom searched for new words together, and they were never angry again.

Maureen was a very happy girl. She had a bright blue dress and her favorite shoes. Everywhere she went, she smiled at everyone she saw. Today, Maureen was going to the park with her mom. At the park, Maureen saw a big, bright yellow slide. She ran up to the slide and asked her mom if she could go down it. Her mom said yes and gave her a ticket to use. Maureen slid down the slide so many times. It was so much fun! Every time she finished sliding down the slide, she hugged her mom to thank her. Her mom was so happy to see Maureen having so much fun. After that, they went to buy an ice cream. It was really yummy and it made Maureen even happier. They enjoyed the rest of the day together playing in the park. At the end of the day, Maureen and her mom hugged each other goodbye. Maureen was sad to go, but she knew that she would come back one day soon.
