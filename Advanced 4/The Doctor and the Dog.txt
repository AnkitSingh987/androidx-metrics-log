Title: The Doctor and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little doctor. The little doctor had a patient. The patient was a dog. The dog had a hurt paw. The little doctor wanted to help the dog feel better. The little doctor got a bandage to cover the hurt paw. But when he tried to cover the paw, something unexpected happened. The dog's paw turned into a wing! The little doctor was very surprised. The dog started to fly around the room. The little doctor laughed and clapped his hands. The dog was not hurt anymore. The dog was now a special flying dog. The little doctor and the flying dog became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the kitchen. One day, she saw a big white freezer. She opened it and found many yummy things inside. Lily found some ice cream, fruit, and milk. She wanted to make a fun treat. So, she took out a bowl and started to mix the ice cream, fruit, and milk together. It was fun to mix the things and see them change. When she was done mixing, she put the bowl back in the white freezer. She waited for a little while. Then, she took out the bowl and saw her treat was ready. It was a yummy, cold surprise. Lily shared her treat with her family and they all loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was in a big classroom with his friends. They were all playing with toys. Tom saw an expensive toy car on the teacher's desk. He wanted to play with it, but it was not his. One day, the teacher left the classroom for a short time. Tom thought it was the perfect time to play with the expensive toy car. He went to the teacher's desk and took the car. He played with it for a little while, but then he heard the teacher coming back. Tom got scared and hid the car under a big book. The next day, Tom wanted to play with the car again. But when he went to the classroom, he could not find it. He started to search for the car under the big book, but it was not there. Tom was very sad and worried. He did not know what to do. Later, Tom saw his friend Lucy playing with the expensive toy car. He asked her where she found it. Lucy said that she found it under a big book. Tom was so happy that the car was found. He told the teacher what he did and said he was sorry. The teacher forgave Tom, and they all played with the car together.

Once upon a time there was a poor little girl called Lucy. She had no toys so she often played with stones and leaves. One morning she walked into a nearby field and spotted something interesting. "What is that?" said Lucy. It was a maze, made of stones and sticks. Lucy loved mazes and she wanted to explore it. She walked around it, carefully looking to see the different paths. But when she reached the centre of the maze, she found the biggest surprise of all. There was a blanket covering something on the ground. Lucy rushed over to her prize, lifted the blanket and found a huge pile of toys hidden away! "Yay!" shouted Lucy. She was so excited. She spent the whole day playing with the toys and exploring the maze. Little did Lucy know that her new found wealth would soon help her poor family. The next few weeks went by and Lucy's family became very rich. They decided to thank Lucy for her discovery and decided to build a giant maze in the field, covering it with pretty flowers and toys! Lucy's new maze became a huge success. Everyone loved the maze and the toys. From that time on, Lucy and her family have been happy and content, all thanks to her discovery of the maze and her wonderful toys.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. It was very hot, and the bird was tired. The bird saw a big tree with cool shade. The tree looked like a nice shelter to rest. The bird flew to the tree and said, "Hello, tree! Can I rest in your cool shelter?" The tree said, "Of course, little bird! You are welcome to rest here." The bird was very happy and thanked the tree. They became good friends. The bird would sing songs, and the tree would listen. They were happy together in the cool shelter.
