Title: Tom and the Book

One day, Tom and his mom were in the house. Tom was feeling uncomfortable. He told his mom, "Mom, my tummy hurts." His mom said, "Let's go to the bathroom, Tom." In the bathroom, Tom felt better. He was happy, and his mom was happy too. They went back to the living room and sat on the couch. Tom's mom read him a book. That night, Tom went to bed. He had a good sleep. In the morning, he woke up early. Tom was not feeling uncomfortable anymore. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I feel good now!" His mom smiled and said, "I'm glad, Tom. Let's have a fun day together."

Once upon a time, in a cold land, there was a shiny crystal. This crystal was very special. It allowed anyone who touched it to feel warm and happy. Many people wanted to touch the crystal, but it was high up on a hill. One day, a little girl named Lily decided to climb the hill to touch the crystal. She wanted to feel warm and happy. As she climbed, she felt very cold. But she kept going, thinking about the crystal. When Lily reached the top of the hill, she saw the crystal. But something unexpected happened. The crystal started to talk! "Lily," it said, "you don't need me to feel warm and happy. You are brave and strong. That is what makes you warm and happy." Lily smiled and felt warm inside, even though it was still cold outside. And from that day on, she knew that she didn't need the crystal to feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate bird named Billy. Billy loved to fly high in the sky. He had many friends in the forest. One day, as Billy was flying, he saw his friend, Bunny, crying. Bunny had a cut on her paw. Billy wanted to help his friend feel better. He flew down and gently hugged Bunny. Then, Billy flew off to find something to help Bunny's cut. Billy found a soft leaf and some water. He flew back to Bunny and put the leaf on her cut. Bunny felt better and thanked Billy. They played together until the sun went down. From that day on, Bunny knew that Billy was a true and compassionate friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was only three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day, Lily was playing in the living room when she saw a big, messy prize. She had never seen anything like it before. Lily asked her mom, "What is this prize?" Her mom smiled and said, "It is a very special seat that you can sit on." Lily was so excited she couldn't wait to try it. She plopped down on the seat and immediately felt like she was in her very own special place. She loved it! Every day after that, Lily would get up early to seat on the prize. It no longer looked messy as she had tidied it up. Lily was very happy with her new seat and the special prize it was.

One day, a big, heavy van came to the park. It was full of fun things for the kids to play with. The man who drove the van was very nice. He wore a big hat and a big smile. The man opened the van and let the kids see all the fun things inside. There were balls, bikes, and even a big slide. The kids were so happy and excited to play with everything. The kids played all day with the fun things from the van. They threw balls, rode bikes, and went down the big slide. At the end of the day, the man with the big hat packed up the heavy van and waved goodbye. The kids were tired but happy, and they couldn't wait for the van to come back again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who lived by the ocean. She lived on one side of the shore, and her best friend lived right across the ocean, on the other side. The little girl wanted to unite with her friend, but the shore was too far apart. She was very sorry and wanted to see her friend. One day, the little girl had an idea! She grabbed a long rope and ran to the edge of the shore. She tied one end of the rope to the ocean and the other end to her friend’s side of the shore. She smiled when she saw that it worked! Now she and her friend could cross the rope and unite together. So, the little girl and her best friend, who was now right across the ocean, held hands and the ran across the rope to unite together. They were so happy, they hugged and laughed and shared a big smile. The two were now best friends forever, united by the shore.
