Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived near a big pole. The pole was very harsh, but Tim liked it. It was his favorite place to sit and watch the world. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam was a squirrel who lived in a tree close to the pole. They liked to play together. Tim would fly around the pole, and Sam would run up and down the tree. "Let's play a game!" said Sam. "I will try to catch you while you fly around the pole." Tim liked the idea and they played the game all day. They laughed and had a lot of fun. As the sun began to set, Tim and Sam were very tired. "I think it's time to sleep," said Tim. Sam agreed. Tim flew up to the top of the pole and Sam went back to his tree. They both fell asleep, happy to have a new friend. The next day, Tim and Sam played more games by the pole. They were best friends and loved spending time together. And every night, they would sleep near the pole, dreaming of the fun they would have the next day.

One day, a girl named Lily found a pretty jewel. It was red, blue, and green. She thought it was the best thing ever. She showed it to her friends. They all said, "Wow! That's a crazy jewel!" Lily took the jewel home. She put it on her table. She went to bed. When she woke up, she saw something unexpected. The jewel was gone! She looked everywhere for it. She was very sad. Then, she saw her little brother, Max. He was playing with the jewel. He had found it on the floor. Lily was happy again. She gave Max a big hug. They played with the crazy jewel together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who, one day, wanted to go for a walk in the park with her mom. When they got there, they both noticed something enormous in the distance. The little girl couldn't believe her eyes and asked, "Mom, what's that?" Her mom looked and replied, "It's a huge birdcage, my darling." The little girl couldn't wait to go and see the enormous birdcage, so they walked closer. When they finally reached it, the little girl saw that it was full of all kinds of beautiful birds. She was mesmerized and asked her mom, "Can I push the birdcage?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, but very gently". The little girl was so excited that she approached the birdcage, and with the utmost care, she started pushing it ever so lightly. The sound of the birdcage moving and the birds singing was so beautiful that the little girl had never felt happier. The little girl and her mom stayed in the park for a few more hours until it was time to go home. She had such an amazing time with her mom and the birds that she could never forget it.

Once upon a time, there was a True family. There was mom, dad and a nosy little girl. One day, mom and dad were making lunch and the nosy little girl was very curious. She wanted to know what was on the menu. "What are we eating today mom?" She asked with gusto. "We are eating sandwiches," replied mom. The nosy little girl wasn't satisfied, she wanted to know more. "What kind of sandwiches are we eating?". Mom was getting annoyed, "We are eating turkey sandwiches, now leave us alone and go play," she said firmly. The nosy little girl couldn't help her curiosity and she kept asking more questions. This time dad got angry. "Stop being so nosy," he shouted, "go to your room and stay there until lunchtime". The nosy little girl knew that she had gone too far this time and she went to her room with her head hung low. At lunchtime, the True family happily ate their sandwiches. They were grateful to be together. The nosy little girl had learned her lesson and knew that sometimes it is best to not be too nosy.
