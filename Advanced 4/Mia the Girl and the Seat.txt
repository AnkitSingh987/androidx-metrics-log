Title: Mia the Girl and the Seat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, red seat that she loved to sit on. She would sit on her seat and eat her healthy snack every day. Her healthy snack was always yummy fruits, like apples and bananas. One day, Mia saw a small, cute dog outside her house. The dog looked hungry and tired. Mia wanted to help the dog, so she asked her mommy, "Please, can I give the dog some food?" Her mommy said yes, and Mia gave the dog some food and water. After eating, the dog was very happy. Mia asked the dog, "Do you want to sit on my seat?" The dog jumped onto the big, red seat and wagged its tail. Mia, her mommy, and the dog all sat on the seat together, eating healthy snacks and laughing. They became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue had a big, attractive kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a dishwasher. The dishwasher was Sue's friend, and it helped her clean the dishes. One day, Sue asked the dishwasher, "Do you want to help me clean?" The dishwasher nodded and said, "Yes, I want to help!" So, Sue and the dishwasher worked together to make the kitchen clean and shiny. When they were done, Sue said, "Thank you, dishwasher! You are a good friend!" The dishwasher nodded and smiled. They were both happy, and Sue's kitchen looked beautiful. Sue and the dishwasher were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big automobile. It was red and shiny. It liked to go fast on the road. The automobile had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy. He loved to play with the automobile every day. They would race and have fun. One day, the automobile was very tired. It did not want to play with Tim. It wanted to rest. So, the automobile found a quiet spot under a tree. It closed its eyes and started to sleep. Tim came looking for the automobile. He did not want to disturb it, so he waited for it to wake up. After a while, the tired automobile woke up. It saw Tim waiting and was happy. The automobile was not tired anymore. Tim and the automobile played together again. They raced and had a lot of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, an orange cat went to the sea. The cat saw a sailor. The sailor owned a big boat. The cat said, "Can I go on your boat?" The sailor said, "Yes, you can come with me." They went on the boat and the cat was happy. They saw fish in the water. The cat tried to catch the fish. The sailor laughed and said, "You can't catch them like that!" Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped into the water and turned into a big fish. The sailor was very surprised. The cat-fish swam around the boat and said, "Now I can catch the fish!" The sailor laughed and they had a fun day at sea.
