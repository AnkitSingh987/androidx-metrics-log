Title: The Gifted Bug and the Roof

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gifted little bug. This bug was very good at crawling. He could crawl up and down, and all around. The bug loved to crawl on the roof of his house. He would crawl on the roof all day and all night. One day, the bug saw a big storm coming. He knew that he had to do something to help his friends. He crawled very fast to tell all his friends about the storm. They all worked together to find a safe place to hide. After the storm, the bug and his friends were all safe. They were very happy that the bug had warned them. The gifted little bug learned that it was good to help others. And his friends learned that it was important to listen to the bug. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Milly and Molly. They were both so excited to come to Milly's house. As soon as they opened the door they saw Milly's bathtub. It was so unique, it had lots of different colours. Molly gasped. "Wow, Milly! Your bathtub is amazing!" She said. Milly smiled. "Come and see inside", she said. Molly followed Milly, and the two of them got inside the big bathtub. They had so much fun, playing with the toys and splashing in the water. Suddenly, Milly made a special wish. She wished that Molly could come over to play every single day. Molly smiled. "That would be so much fun", she said. Since then, every day Molly would come to Milly's house to play in the unique bathtub. And they stayed friends for always.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her ball. She threw the ball very high, and it went over the fence. Lucy was worried. She needed her ball back. Lucy asked her friend Tim, "Can you help me get my ball?" Tim said, "Yes, we can use a long stick to reach it." They found a long stick and tried to get the ball, but the stick was not long enough. They needed another plan. Just then, a big, friendly dog came running up to them. The dog had Lucy's ball in his mouth! He dropped the ball at their feet and wagged his tail. Lucy and Tim were surprised and happy. They said, "Thank you, dog!" Now, they could play with the ball again. They passed the ball to each other and had lots of fun with their new dog friend.

Wendy was sad and felt guilty. She had said something she shouldn't have said to her best friend. Her friend had been sad and she had promised to fix it. She tried hard but could not find the right words. "Where can I find a special figure that will make my best friend happy again?" Wendy asked. Then a strange figure wearing a cloak appeared in front of her. Wendy jumped back in surprise. "Do not be scared," the figure said. "I can help you find something special. You will know it when you see it." Wendy was determined to make her best friend happy. She looked carefully at every figure she passed. Finally at the end of her search, Wendy found the perfect figure. When she brought the figure to her friend, Wendy's friend smiled. Wendy was so grateful. The figure in the cloak had helped her. She had done the right thing and it made her feel happy and relieved. She would never forget what she had learned. In the back of her mind, she knew she would miss the figure in the cloak one day.

One day, Lucy and her mom were in Lucy's bedroom. Lucy was three years old now, and she wanted a new bedroom. "Mom, can you help me change my bedroom?" she asked. Her mom smiled. "Let's see what we can do," she said. They both walked around the bedroom, looking around at the furniture and walls. Suddenly, they saw something enormous in the corner of the room. It was an enormous cardboard box! "Mom, what is that?" asked Lucy. Her mom explained that it was a giant playhouse. They opened it up and saw a door, windows, and even a staircase inside. "This will be perfect for our bedroom makeover," said Lucy's mom. The next day, they got to work. First, they painted the walls and replaced the old furniture with new pieces. Then, they put the enormous playhouse in the corner. When they were done, Lucy was amazed. Her bedroom had completely changed! She loved her new, enormous playhouse and couldn't wait to show it off to her friends.
