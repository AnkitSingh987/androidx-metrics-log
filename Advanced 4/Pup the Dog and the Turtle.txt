Title: Pup the Dog and the Turtle

One day, a little dog named Pup was playing in the park. Pup saw a big mattress lying on the grass. Pup was confused. "Why is there a mattress here?" Pup thought. Pup wanted to see what would happen if he jumped on the mattress. Pup ran and jumped on the mattress. The mattress made a funny noise. Pup was surprised. "What is happening?" Pup thought. Pup jumped again and again. The mattress made more funny noises. Suddenly, the mattress started to move. It was not a mattress at all! It was a big, friendly turtle. The turtle said, "Hello, Pup! I was taking a nap under my shell. Thank you for waking me up!" Pup and the turtle became good friends and played together in the park all day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the garden. They see a furry cat. The cat is black and white. The cat is hungry. "Hello, cat. Do you want to play with us?" Lily asks. The cat does not answer. The cat sees a pepper. The pepper is red and shiny. The pepper is on a plant. The cat jumps on the plant. The cat tries to eat the pepper. "Hey, cat! That is not your pepper! That is our pepper!" Ben shouts. The cat does not listen. The cat bites the pepper. The pepper is hot and spicy. The cat makes a funny face. The cat spits out the pepper. The cat runs away. "Ha ha, cat! That is what you get for being greedy!" Ben laughs. Lily and Ben pick the pepper. They wash the pepper. They share the pepper. They eat the pepper. The pepper is sweet and crunchy. They like the pepper. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a rich girl named Isabella. Isabella was very excited because the weather was beautiful and sunny. She wanted to do something special outside and so she ran to her garden. Isabella filled her bucket with water and grabbed her spade. Then she began to fill the garden with tiny flowers and plants. She smiled as she filled the garden because she knew it would be beautiful once the weather was warm. Isabella worked hard all day and when it was nearly time to go inside, she stepped back and smiled. Her garden was filled with rich, bright flowers, and she was very proud of her work. Isabella went in, put her tools away and sat down. She watched out her window and smiled as the weather changed from day to night. As she looked out, Isabella was happy to see the colourful flowers in her garden, thinking that she had done a great job to fill her garden with so much beauty.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She wanted to look at nature. Mia saw a big gray rock. She thought it was just a normal rock. But then, the rock said, "Hello!" Mia was very surprised. "Did you just talk?" she asked the gray rock. "Yes, I did," the rock replied. "My name is Rocky, and I am a magic rock." Mia and Rocky became friends. They looked at nature together. They saw pretty flowers, tall trees, and little bugs. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Rocky away. Mia was sad, but she knew that Rocky would always be with her in her heart. And she would always remember their fun day looking at nature together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a new book, a novel. He liked this book very much. It had a pretty cover and fun pictures. One day, Tom went to the park with his book. He met his friend, Sue. Sue saw the book and said, "Can we share the book, Tom?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Okay, we can share my book." They started reading the book together. But then, a harsh wind came. It blew the book away. Tom and Sue tried to catch it, but they could not. The wind took the book far away. Tom and Sue were sad. They lost the book and could not read it anymore. They learned that sometimes, sharing can lead to losing things.
