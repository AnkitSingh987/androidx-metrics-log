Title: Lily the Girl and the Leaves

One day, a little girl named Lily found an amazing notebook under her bed. She picked it up and saw that it was empty. Lily had an idea. She decided to use the notebook to draw all her favorite things. Lily went outside to find her friends. She saw her friend Tom playing with colorful leaves. Tom said, "Look, Lily! I can scatter the leaves!" He threw the leaves up in the air, and they fell all around them. Lily laughed and thought it was fun. Lily showed Tom her amazing notebook and said, "Let's draw the leaves in my notebook!" Tom agreed, and they sat down to draw together. They drew leaves, trees, and even their pets. When they were done, they looked at their drawings and were very happy. Lily and Tom decided to keep the notebook as a special memory of their fun day.

Lily and Ben like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can create anything they want with the blocks. Sometimes they create houses, sometimes they create animals, sometimes they create cars. One day, they find a new box of blocks in the closet. These blocks are not like the other blocks. They are metal blocks. They are shiny and heavy and make a loud sound when they hit each other. Lily and Ben are curious. They want to play with the metal blocks. They take the metal blocks to the living room. They start to create a big robot with the metal blocks. They use the square blocks for the body, the round blocks for the head, the long blocks for the arms and legs, and the small blocks for the eyes and mouth. They are very proud of their robot. It looks cool and strong. "Look, Mom, look!" Lily calls. "We made a robot with the metal blocks!" Mom comes to the living room. She sees the robot. She smiles and claps her hands. "Wow, that's amazing!" Mom says. "You are very creative, Lily and Ben. I like your robot. What is its name?" Lily and Ben think for a moment. They look at their robot. It is dark and shiny and loud. "His name is Metal," Lily says. "Metal," Ben repeats. "Metal," Mom says. "That's a good name. Hello, Metal. Nice to meet you." The robot does not say anything. It just stands there. But Lily and Ben think it is happy. They hug the robot and each other. They are happy, too. They love to create with the metal blocks.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful dough. It was soft and nice. The dough lived in a small kitchen with a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with the dough and make fun shapes. One day, Lily made a funny dough man with a big smile. She talked to the dough man and said, "Hello, Mr. Dough Man! You look so happy today!" The dough man did not talk back, but Lily felt like he wanted to be her friend. Later, as Lily slept, the dough man came to life. He remembered how Lily talked to him and made him smile. He wanted to thank her, so he made a big, beautiful cake for Lily. The next morning, Lily saw the cake and knew that her new friend, Mr. Dough Man, made it for her. They became best friends and had many more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little tiger. She wanted to go on an adventure so she packed her things. She filled her backpack with yummy snacks and her favourite toy. Next, she asked her mom if she could go out for a walk. Her mom said yes, as long as she stayed close to home. So the tiger set off, with her backpack in tow. As the tiger explored the nearby wood, she came across some new friends. She met a group of friendly birds who were flying around and chatting away. The tiger chatted with them, while they shared yummy snacks from her backpack. The tiger had a wonderful time and when it was time to go home, the birds said goodbye. The tiger knew that she would never forget her charming new friends. She packed her backpack and waved goodbye as she walked back home.
