Title: Lily and Ben and the Cake

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to eat. They ate a lot of food every day, but they wanted more. They wanted to eat luxury food, like cake and ice cream and candy. They saw these foods on TV and in books, and they looked very yummy. One day, their mom went to the store and left them at home with their dad. Their dad was busy working on his laptop and did not pay attention to them. Lily and Ben saw this as a chance to sneak into the kitchen and find some luxury food. They opened the fridge and the cabinets and looked for something sweet. They found a big chocolate cake in the fridge, with a note on it that said "Happy Anniversary". They did not know what that meant, but they did not care. They took the cake out and put it on the table. They grabbed two forks and started to eat. The cake was very soft and rich and tasty. They ate and ate and ate, until they felt sick. They heard their mom come back from the store. They quickly hid the cake under a cloth and ran to their room. They hoped their mom would not notice. They lay down on their beds and felt very bad. Their stomachs hurt and they felt dizzy. They wished they had not eaten so much cake. Their mom came into their room and saw them looking pale and sweaty. She felt their foreheads and saw they had a fever. She asked them what was wrong. They did not answer. They were afraid to tell her the truth. She went to the kitchen and saw the cloth on the table. She lifted it and saw the half-eaten cake. She gasped and realized what had happened. She was very angry and sad. She had bought the cake for her and their dad's anniversary, a special day to celebrate their love. She had wanted to surprise him with a nice dinner and a dessert. But now the cake was ruined and the twins were sick. She called their dad and told him what had happened. He was also very angry and sad. He came to their room and scolded them for being greedy and dishonest. He said they had spoiled their anniversary and made themselves ill. He said they would have to stay in bed and have no more luxury food for a long time. He said they had to learn to be grateful and respectful. He said he was very disappointed in them. Lily and Ben felt very sorry and ashamed. They cried and apologized to their mom and dad. They said they would never do it again. They said they loved them and wished them a happy anniversary. But their mom and dad did not smile. They did not hug them. They left them alone in their room, with their pain and their guilt. Lily and Ben wished they had never seen the cake. They wished they had been happy with their normal food. They wished they had been good twins. But it was too late. They had eaten their luxury and lost their love.

One day, a little frog named Tim hopped near a big pond. He saw his friend, the duck named Sam. Sam was in the water, and he was having fun. Tim wanted to have fun too. So, he jumped in the water with a big splash. Sam smiled at Tim and said, "Come play with me!" They swam and splashed in the water. They were very happy. But then, they saw something disgusting in the water. It was a big, stinky, and dirty sock. Tim and Sam did not like the sock. It made the pond smell bad. Tim said to Sam, "Let's take the sock out of the water." So, they worked together and took the sock out of the pond. The water was clean again, and they could play without the bad smell. They smiled and splashed in the water all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a big place called University. Many people went there to learn new things. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to join the University. She asked her mom, "Can I join the University like the big people?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first we need to find a way to pay for it. We are not wealthy, and the University costs a lot of money." So, Lucy and her mom tried to think of ways to get money to join the University. One day, while they were walking in the park, they saw a big tree with a hole in it. Inside the hole, they found a big bag of gold coins! They were so happy and surprised. Now, Lucy could join the University and learn like the big people. And they all lived happily ever after.
