Title: Mia the Girl and the Dolphin

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the beach with her mom and dad. It was a sunny day, and Mia was happy to play in the sand and water. She built a big sand castle and found pretty shells to put on top. As Mia played, she saw something in the water. It seemed to be moving in a funny way. Mia looked closer and saw a mysterious dolphin. The dolphin was jumping in and out of the water, making big splashes. Mia thought the dolphin was playing a game with her. Mia waved at the mysterious dolphin and it waved back with its tail. They played together all day, splashing in the water and having fun. When it was time to go home, Mia said goodbye to her new dolphin friend. She knew she would always remember the fun day she had at the beach with the mysterious dolphin.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clumsy bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small cave near the woods. He loved to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. One day, Bobo saw some smoke coming from the woods. He was scared and wanted to help. He ran to the woods and saw a little fire. Bobo's friend, a small bird named Chirpy, was flying around the fire. "Help me, Bobo!" Chirpy said. "I can't put out the fire!" Bobo knew what to do. He found a big rock near the fire. He pushed the rock with all his strength, and it rolled over the fire. The fire went out, and the smoke disappeared. Chirpy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo! You saved the woods!" From that day on, Bobo and Chirpy became the best of friends. They played together every day, and the other animals in the woods knew that Bobo was not just a clumsy bear, but a hero too.

Once there was a Mommy who loved her three year old son very much. She always wanted to take care of him. So, Mommy prayed to God every day. She asked God to always keep her son safe and happy. One day, Mommy decided to take her son to the park. But, when they got there, it was all filled with junk! Everything was broken and unplayable. Mommy was very sad, so they went home. Mommy didn't want to forget all the junk, though, so she decided to make something special. She made a delicate sculpture out of the junk. It was so beautiful, and she could keep it forever. Mommy and her son looked at the sculpture every day and remembered their visit to the park. They also said a special prayer for all the junk and for all the happy times.

Once upon a time, in a small and cozy house, there lived a powerful cat named Tom. He loved to jump on soft cushions and take naps. One day, he found his cushions all mixed up and messy. Tom had an idea. "I will organize my cushions, so I can nap better!" he said to his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very smart and liked to help Tom. "I will help you, Tom!" said Jerry. They worked together to fix the cushions. Tom put the big ones on the bottom, and Jerry put the small ones on the top. When they were done, the cushions looked very nice. Tom and Jerry smiled at each other. "Thank you, Jerry!" said Tom. "Now we can both enjoy the cushions together." They sat on the organized cushions and took a long nap. From that day on, Tom and Jerry learned that working together made things easier and more fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and True are best friends. True is a big dog with black fur and brown eyes. Lily likes to hug True and play with him in the park. One day, Lily and True see a new dog in the park. The new dog is small and white and has a pink bow on its head. "Hello, I am Fluffy," the new dog says. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily and True go near Fluffy. Fluffy looks at True and makes a mean face. "Yuck, you are ugly," Fluffy says to True. "Your fur is dirty and your eyes are dull. You are not a good dog. Go away!" True feels sad and hurt. He lowers his head and walks away. Lily feels angry and sorry. She loves True and thinks he is a good dog. "Fluffy, you are mean," Lily says to Fluffy. "True is not ugly. He is beautiful and kind. He is my best friend. You are not nice. Go away!" Lily rushes to True and hugs him. She tells him he is a good dog and she loves him. True wags his tail and licks her face. He feels happy and loved. Lily and True leave the park and go home. They do not want to play with Fluffy anymore. They are happy to have each other.
