Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He lived in a big house with many toys. Tom was happy, but he wanted to marry a nice girl. One day, Tom went for a walk in the woods. He saw a log and sat down to rest. A pretty girl named Sue walked by. She saw Tom and smiled. They talked and played with the log. Tom and Sue liked each other a lot. They decided to marry and have a big party. All their friends came to the party and were happy. Tom and Sue lived happily ever after with their log.

Once upon a time, in a warm summer, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved the color orange. Amy had an orange ball, an orange dress, and even an orange cat named Tim. One day, Amy and Tim went to play outside. They played catch with the orange ball. "Tim, catch!" Amy would say, and Tim would jump and catch the ball. They were having so much fun. But then, the ball went too far and got lost. Amy started to weep. Tim tried to cheer her up. "Meow, don't cry, Amy," said Tim. They looked and looked, and finally found the orange ball under a big tree. Amy was happy again, and they played until the sun went down.

Lily and Ben were playing outside when it started to rain. They ran to the porch and looked for something to cover them. They saw a large umbrella leaning against the wall. "Let's attach the umbrella to the bike and ride around!" Ben said. "How do we attach it?" Lily asked. Ben found a rope and tied one end to the umbrella handle. He tied the other end to the bike seat. He lifted the umbrella and opened it. "Look, it's like a roof!" he said. Lily climbed on the bike behind Ben. She held the umbrella with one hand and Ben's waist with the other. "Ready?" Ben asked. "Ready!" Lily said. They pedaled away from the porch and into the rain. They laughed as they felt the drops bounce off the umbrella. They felt like they were in a boat on a river. They rode around the yard until the rain stopped. They parked the bike and closed the umbrella. They untied the rope and put everything back where they found it. "That was fun!" Lily said. "It was!" Ben agreed. They hugged each other and went inside to dry off. They had a great adventure with a large umbrella.

Once upon a time, in a big field, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to collect pretty things. One day, she saw a beautiful flower called a poppy. The poppy was red and bright. Lily wanted to take it home. Lily walked up to the poppy and said, "Hello, pretty poppy! Can I take you home with me? I like to collect pretty things like you." The sleepy poppy yawned and said, "Yes, Lily, you can take me home. But please be gentle." Lily was very happy. She carefully picked up the poppy and took it home. She put the sleepy poppy in a nice vase with water. The poppy was happy, too. Now, Lily and the poppy were friends and they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a wide lake. Every day, two little ducks would join each other for a swim. They would glide through the water with graceful strokes. One day, they decided to make a wish. They each said their special wish out loud and threw a piece of bread into the lake. As they watched, something truly magical happened. Two halves of a wishboat appeared on the lake! The ducks were so excited they paddled as fast as they could to join it. As soon as they climbed in, their wishes came true! The ducks smiled and paddled back to the shore. From then on, they would join each other in their special wishboat every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, black cat. The cat was a little scary, but it was also very nice. It lived in a small house with a girl named Sally. Sally loved the black cat very much. They played together every day. One day, Sally and the black cat found a big hole in their yard. They wanted to fill the hole, so they could play more. Sally got a big box and the black cat helped her. They put dirt in the box and carried it to the hole. They worked together to fill the hole with the dirt. When the hole was full, Sally and the black cat were very happy. They could play in the yard again without worrying about the hole. The scary black cat was not so scary anymore, because it helped Sally. They played and had fun together all day, and they were the best of friends.
