Title: The Bright Sun and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a wild and fun day. A boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to play outside. They saw a big tree with lots of branches. Tim had an idea. He wanted to cut a branch to make a toy. Tim went to his dad and asked for help. His dad said, "Be careful, cutting can be hard. But I will help you." They found a small branch that was just right. Tim's dad cut the branch for him. Tim was very happy. Tim and Spot played with the branch all day. They threw it and Spot would run to get it. They had so much fun with their new toy. When the bright sun went down, they went home. Tim and Spot were tired but happy. They knew they would have more wild and fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a glove. He loved his glove very much. One day, Tim felt foolish. He thought it would be fun to put his glove on a tree branch. "Look, glove! You are up high!" said Tim. But then, the wind blew hard. The glove flew away. Tim was sad. He wanted his glove back. He saw the glove on the ground, but it was far away. Tim tried to get his glove. He had to struggle. He crawled under a bush. He climbed over a rock. But he was too late. A big bird picked up the glove and flew away. Tim cried, "No, my glove!" But the bird did not hear him. Tim was very sad. He had lost his glove. He knew he was foolish to put it on the tree. Now, Tim had no glove to keep his hand warm. The end.

Grandma’s hands were dependable, strong and quick. Her grandchildren watched wide-eyed as she bustled around the kitchen. She smiled and said, “We’re going to build something special today!” Rebecca, the oldest of the grandchildren, clapped with glee. “What are we building, grandma?” Grandma winked and said, “Waffles! Who wants to help me?” The grandchildren raced over and soon Grandma was showing them how to combine ingredients and stir them together. They giggled as the batter grew thicker and thicker. When the batter was ready, Grandma carefully poured it into the hot waffle iron. Rebecca watched, fascinated as the waffle iron pressed the waffle into shape. After a few minutes, Grandma carefully pulled the waffle out of the iron. It was golden brown, fluffy and smelled delicious! Grandma smiled and said, “We did it! We built a waffle! Now let’s eat it!” The grandchildren cheered with delight. They enjoyed every bite of their delicious, dependable waffle. It had been a perfect project - thanks to Grandma!

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, they were playing near a dangerous road. Tim's mom told him to be careful and not to leave the safe area. But Tim saw a big red ambulance on the road. He wanted to see it up close. He forgot what his mom said and left the safe area. He ran to the ambulance, but he didn't see a car coming. It was very dangerous! Tim's friend yelled, "Tim, come back!" Tim heard his friend and stopped. The car stopped too. Tim was safe! He went back to the safe area and promised never to leave it again. The ambulance left, and Tim and his friends continued playing. Tim learned to listen to his mom and stay safe.

Once upon a time there was a giant who loved to deliver pencils. Everywhere the giant went, he would drop pencils off for the children to enjoy. One day the giant came across a village full of children. He asked them, "What can I deliver today?" The kids said they wanted something different. The giant thought to himself and came up with an idea. He smiled widely, said goodbye and continued on his way. The next day, when the giant returned to the village, he had something special to show the children. He held out a big surprise - different coloured pencils! The children were so excited. They quickly grabbed the pencils and raced off. The giant was pleased. He had delivered something different and it had made the children very happy. With a satisfied smile he watched the children go, never forgetting the joy he'd brought them.
