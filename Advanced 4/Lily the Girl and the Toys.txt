Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

One day, a girl named Lily went to her friend Tom's house to play. They played with their toys and had a lot of fun. After playing, they were very hungry. So, Tom's mom said, "It is time for dinner." Lily and Tom sat at the table. Tom's mom gave them a big plate of food. They ate and ate until their plates were clean. "Thank you for the yummy dinner," said Lily. But then, they heard a loud noise outside. They ran to the window and saw a big, angry dog. The dog came into the house and ate all their toys. Lily and Tom were very sad. They did not have any more toys to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a light bunny named Binky. Binky had a secret dream to play football with the blue jays. One sunny day, Binky hopped over to the meadow and saw the blue jays busy playing the game. Binky eagerly asked the birds if he could join them in their game. The blue jays agreed and so Binky hopped in the center of the circle by the scorekeeper and began the match! "Okay Binky, you take the first shot!" said one blue jay. Binky paused for a moment, not sure what to do. He had never done this before! The blue jays encouraged him to try, so Binky hopped forward and took a shot. The ball flew in the air, and made it into the net! Everyone cheered! Binky and the blue jays scored a point. Binky was very happy and he smiled with pride. The game went on and on, with all the animals taking turns to shoot, and in the end, Binky and the blue jays had a tie score. Everyone felt like a winner, and Binky thanked the birds for a wonderful game. From that day on, Binky and the blue jays became friends, and the light bunny could be found playing football with the birds in the meadow every day.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big animal with a horn on its nose. It was a rhinoceros. They wanted to pet it, but it was behind a fence. "Can we go and touch it?" Lily asked. "No, we can't. It is not available. It is too dangerous," Ben said. "But it looks so soft and nice. Maybe it is friendly," Lily said. She tried to climb the fence, but Ben pulled her back. "Stop, Lily! You are being silly. The rhinoceros might hurt you. It might seem nice, but it is not. It is wild and strong. It can run fast and poke you with its horn. You have to listen to me. I am your big brother," Ben said. Lily was sad and angry. She did not like Ben telling her what to do. She wanted to see the rhinoceros up close. She thought Ben was being mean and scared. "Fine, I don't want to play with you anymore. You are a bad brother. I will find another friend who will let me pet the rhinoceros," Lily said. She ran away from Ben and looked for someone else to play with. But no one was available. Everyone was busy or gone. Lily felt lonely and sorry. She wished she had not been so stubborn and rude to Ben. She realized he was only trying to protect her and care for her. She missed him and wanted to say sorry. She ran back to Ben and hugged him. "I am sorry, Ben. You are a good brother. I was wrong. I don't want to pet the rhinoceros anymore. I want to play with you," Lily said. Ben smiled and hugged her back. "I am sorry, too, Lily. You are a good sister. I was harsh. I don't want to lose you. I want to play with you, too," Ben said. They made up and played together. They looked at the rhinoceros from a safe distance and learned more about it. They had fun and were happy. They loved each other.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends who loved to play together. Their names were Pat and Sam. One day, Pat had a request to make of Sam. She asked Sam to look around the garden and find something useful. Sam began searching the garden, and sure enough, he soon discovered a shiny coin lying behind a bush! Pat was very excited. She thanked Sam and said, “That coin looks really useful. Can you give it to me?” Sam smiled and handed the coin to Pat. Pat was delighted with her discovery. She knew the coin had to be very special. She thanked Sam again and said, “This coin is so useful! Let’s use it to buy something yummy!” Sam and Pat happily ran off to the candy store to buy something yummy with their special coin. They laughed and smiled as they bought their treats. It was a very happy day for Pat and Sam. They had discovered something useful, and it made them both very happy.
