Title: Mia and the Park

Once upon a time, a little girl called Mia wanted to fly. Everyday, she looked up into the sky and wished she could soar high above the clouds. One day, her wish came true! On her way to the park, Mia saw a sparkling fairy standing in the meadow. The fairy smiled at Mia and said, "I can allow you to fly, but there is something you must do first!" Mia was very excited and agreed to do whatever the fairy asked. The fairy said, “First, you must be brave and not be lazy. If you work hard, you can do anything! The key is to remember to breathe. That way, your heart will be filled with oxygen and you will be happy.” Mia worked hard and never gave up. Soon enough, she could fly just like she'd always dreamed! She was so happy and thanked the fairy. From this story, we learned that if we want to do something amazing, we must be brave, not be lazy and remember to breathe. Being brave and working hard will let us reach great heights!

Once upon a time, there was a big gray elephant named Ellie. Ellie was a very dependable friend. She would always help her friends when they needed her. One day, Ellie and her friends were playing near the river. They were having a lot of fun in the sun. While they were playing, a little bird came to Ellie and said, "Ellie, I need your help to weigh these fruits. I am too small to do it alone." Ellie agreed to help the little bird. She used her big, strong trunk to weigh the fruits. The little bird was very happy and thanked Ellie for her help. Later that day, Ellie and her friends were playing near the river again. Suddenly, one of her friends fell into the water and couldn't swim. Ellie remembered how she had helped the little bird earlier, so she knew she could help her friend too. She used her big, strong trunk to pull her friend out of the water. Everyone was so happy and grateful for Ellie's help. They knew they could always count on their dependable friend, Ellie the gray elephant.

One day, a lady wanted to decorate her house. She had a friend who was very good at decorating. The lady asked her friend, "Can you help me make my house pretty?" Her friend said, "Yes, I would love to help!" They went to the store to buy things to decorate the house. But, when they got there, they saw that the store had run out of pretty things. The lady felt bitter and said, "I am sad. We cannot decorate my house now." Her friend had an idea and said, "We can make our own pretty things!" The lady and her friend went home and made pretty things for the house. They used paper, paint, and glue. They had a lot of fun making the decorations. When they were done, the house looked very pretty. The lady was happy and thanked her friend for helping her.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Sam went to the park. Sam was very happy because he had a yummy sausage to eat. He put the sausage on a bench and went to play with his friends. While Sam was playing, a small bird saw the sausage. The bird was hungry too. The bird went to the bench and said, "This sausage looks so good. I will take it." The bird picked up the sausage with its beak and flew away. When Sam came back, he saw that his sausage was gone. "Where is my sausage?" he asked. Just then, he saw the bird flying with his sausage. Sam ran and jumped, trying to catch the bird. As he jumped, he accidentally squeezed the bird. The sausage fell out of the bird's beak and landed back on the bench. Sam was happy to have his sausage back, and the bird learned not to take things that don't belong to them.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who really wanted a pick. He asked his mom for it every day, but his mom said no. One day, after he finished eating his dinner, he thought of a plan. So he asked his mom, "Can I go pick one, please?" His mom was a little confused, so she asked, "What do you mean?" The little boy replied, "I mean can I go find one and pick it up?" His mom sighed and said, "I understand what you're saying, but picking up an item off the street would be wrong. That would be stealing and it's very ignorant." The little boy was puzzled, so he asked his mom, "What does 'ignorant' mean?" His mom smiled and said, "It means not knowing what's right and wrong, like not understanding stealing is wrong." The little boy thought about it for a minute and then he said, "I think I understand. So if I finish eating, can we go find a pick together?" His mom chuckled and said, "Of course! Let's go!"
