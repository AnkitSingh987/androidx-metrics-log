Title: Timmy and the Bird

Timmy wanted a pet, and his mum said he could have a bird. They went to the pet shop and saw a beautiful bird in a birdcage. It was fragile and fluttered around, squeaking happily at them. Mummy kneeled down to show Timmy the bird. "Look at this bird, Timmy!" she said. "It's very beautiful, isn't it?" Timmy nodded and smiled. "Can I have it, please Mummy?" Mummy smiled. "Yes, you can have it!" They took the birdcage home and Timmy named the bird Teddy. Teddy was very playful and loved to flutter around the house. Timmy loved having Teddy as a pet, and each day he was more careful with the fragile birdcage.

Once upon a time there were two friends - John and Jane. John wanted to play and so he looked around for something to do. He saw Jane tidying up her toys and he asked her if she wanted to play with him. She said yes! John and Jane had lots of fun. They raced their toy cars and played with dolls. But then suddenly, John started to demand something. He wanted Jane to give him one of her toys. Jane was taken by surprise. She didn't like it when people demanded things from her. Jane politely told John that he could not have her toy. She said he had to ask nicely if he wanted to borrow it. John said sorry for being so demanding and asked if he could have the toy. Jane gave John one of her toys and they kept playing as if nothing had happened. The end.

One day, a funny duck went to a pond. The duck liked to play and swim in the water. The sun was shining, and the pond was very pretty. The funny duck was very happy to be there. A little girl named Sue came to the pond too. Sue saw the funny duck and said, "Hi, duck! What is your name?" The duck looked at Sue and said, "My name is Ducky. Do you want to play with me?" Sue was very happy and said, "Yes, Ducky! Let's play together!" They played and splashed in the water. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Sue and Ducky became good friends, and they played at the pond every day.

Once there was a little girl. She was playing in the backyard when she saw a long snake. She was scared but very curious. She wanted to see what would happen. So she pushed the snake with a stick. The snake started to move and the little girl was so excited. She kept pushing the snake until it disappeared into the bushes. The little girl was so happy she ran inside and told her mum all about it.

Once upon a time, there was a little cute girl. She had big, bright eyes and fluffy dark hair. Everyone called her Coco. Coco had a best friend, called Sam. They always played together and had lots of fun. One day, when they were playing in the park, Coco saw a beautiful butterfly. It looked so pretty and moved so fast in the sunshine. "Oh, look at the wink!" Coco said. But then, before she knew it, the butterfly flew away. Coco chased after it, but it was too far away. Coco looked very sad, so Sam said, "Let's call for the butterfly!" Coco smiled and they both waved their arms up and down, shouting loudly. Suddenly, the butterfly flew back! It was so happy to see Coco, it landed on her shoulder and blinked its eyes. Coco was so happy, she laughed and smiled. Coco and the butterfly were now the best of friends. Every day, they would meet in the park and have lots of fun. It was really cute!

Robbie was a tall and energetic 3 year old. He was playing outside in the playground when he felt an urge to rush. Robbie started running in circles, faster and faster. Suddenly, a giant appeared from nowhere. It was so tall that it reached the sky. Robbie was scared and came to a stop. The giant spoke softly: "Why don't you come with me? You can run and rush with me for as long as you want." Robbie thought about it for a moment, then smiled and said: "Ok, let's go!" The giant waved his big hand and Robbie felt a rush of energy. Together they began running and jumping, faster than ever before. Robbie was so happy! He and the giant continued running with the wind in their hair until it was time to go home. The two waved goodbye and the giant disappeared as quickly as it had appeared. Robbie knew he had made a special new friend and he smiled as he ran home.
