Title: Lily the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a wet dog playing in the park. The dog was happy and running around. Lily wanted to play with the dog, but she did not want to get wet. Lily walked more and found a big mirror. She looked in the mirror and saw her face. She made funny faces and laughed. The wet dog came to see the mirror too. They both looked in the mirror and had fun. In the end, Lily's day included playing with the wet dog and finding the big mirror. She had a great day with her new friend. They played until the sun went down, and Lily went home with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy tools all day. He had a toy hammer, a toy saw, and a toy wrench. He loved to pretend he was fixing things around his house. One sunny day, Tom was playing outside with his toy tools. He saw a big cat walk by. The cat looked at Tom and tried to play with him. But Tom wanted to ignore the cat and keep playing with his toy tools. The cat felt sad and walked away. Tom saw the cat was sad and he felt bad. He put down his toy tools and ran to the cat. They played together and became good friends. From that day on, Tom learned that playing with friends is more fun than playing with just his toy tools.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sally. Sally wanted to explore but she was too young to go outside. So, instead she imagined an elevator. Sally smiled as she said 'Let's go in the elevator' So, Sally imagined that the elevator flew through the air! Next Sally imagined a huge furry monster in the elevator. It had big eyes and long, hairy arms. The monster said 'Let's have an adventure together!' Sally imagined that the elevator flew to the moon and back. When it was time to stop, the hairy monster gave Sally a huge hug and said 'Until next time!' Sally was so happy. She said goodbye to her new friend and imagined the elevator bringing her back home.

One day, a big yellow duck went for a march. The duck was happy and loved to march all day. The sun was shining and the yellow duck felt very good. As the duck marched, it found a yummy cake. The duck was very hungry and wanted to eat the cake. But when the duck took a bite, something unexpected happened. The cake started to march too! The duck and the cake marched together, and they became best friends. They marched all day, laughing and playing. And from that day on, the yellow duck and the yummy cake were always together, marching and having fun.
