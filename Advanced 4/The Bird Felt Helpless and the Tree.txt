Title: The Bird Felt Helpless and the Tree

One day, a small bird felt helpless. It could not find food. It saw a big tree with a twig. The bird went to the tree to rest. An ant walked by and saw the bird. The ant mentioned, "I know where food is. I can help you!" The bird was happy and listened to the ant. The ant led the bird to a bush with red berries. The bird ate the berries and felt better. The bird thanked the ant for helping. They became good friends and played together.

Once upon a time there was a lazy bunny. The bunny liked to hop and every day hopped around his garden. One day, he hopped past a box filled with glittering jewelry. He had never seen such beautiful jewels before and was so happy. He hopped around joyfully and decided to jump in the box to see all the jewelry up close. He had never felt so excited before. He started to take out the jeweled necklaces, bracelets and earrings and tried them on. Each one was more dazzling than the last. He twirled and hopped around, enjoying himself. Before he knew it, it was getting late and the bunny had to go home. But he was so fond of the jewelry, he decided to hide the box and some of the jewelry so he could come back and visit it again. The lazy bunny hopped home, happy and content.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little daisy. This daisy was very bossy. She liked to tell all the other flowers what to do. One day, a happy bee came to play with the flowers. The bee wanted to play with the daisy, but the bossy daisy said, "No, you must play with the other flowers first!" So the bee played with the other flowers. They had a lot of fun together. After playing with the other flowers, the bee came back to the bossy daisy. The daisy saw how much fun the bee had and decided to play too. They all played together and had a great time. The bossy daisy learned that it is more fun to play with friends and be nice.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was restless. He wanted to watch a video on the TV. His mom said, "No, Tim. You need to match your socks first." Tim did not want to match his socks. He wanted to watch the video now. So, he threw his socks all over the room. His mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, you made a big mess. Now you cannot watch the video." Tim was sad. He knew he did a bad thing. He tried to match his socks, but it was too late. His mom said, "No video today. Maybe tomorrow if you are good." Tim cried, but he knew he had to be good to watch the video.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lily. She liked to help people and make them happy. Lily was a pupil in a small school with other kids. One day, Lily saw a boy who was sad. He had lost his toy. Lily wanted to help him find it. She asked her friends to help too. They all looked for the toy together. They looked and looked, and then Lily saw the toy under a tree. She rushed to get it and gave it to the boy. He was so happy! Everyone was happy that they could help. And they all played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an interesting candle that she loved very much. This candle was not like other candles. It was magic! Every night, Lily would imagine something before going to sleep. She would think of a fun place or a yummy treat. Then, she would light the magic candle. The room would fill with a warm glow, and the magic would start to work. One night, Lily imagined a big ice cream cone, with lots of different flavors. She lit the candle, and suddenly, there it was! Lily was so happy. She knew that her interesting candle was very special. And every night, she looked forward to using her magic candle to make her dreams come true.
