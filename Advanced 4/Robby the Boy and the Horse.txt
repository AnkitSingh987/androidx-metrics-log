Title: Robby the Boy and the Horse

Once upon a time there was a boy named Robby who loved playing outside. He would go out everyday to enjoy the sunshine, the wide open skies and the pleasant breeze. Every day, Robby's mother told him to polish the mailbox, but Robby never listened. He always thought there was no need to polish it when nothing ever came in the mailbox. One afternoon, when Robby was out playing, a fine, white horse and its rider approached the mailbox. The rider dismounted and opened the mailbox to find it was filthy and dirty. He shook his head and said aloud, "This is unacceptable! A mailbox needs to be polished if you want nice things to come in it". Robby realized his mistake. From that day forward, he made sure to polish the mailbox every day. The moral of the story is that if you keep something nice and clean, good things will come in it. Anything else won't do!

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved the color orange. She had orange clothes, orange toys, and even an orange cat. She loved to eat oranges too. One day, she found a big orange in her garden. Lily wanted to marry a boy who loved oranges like she did. She thought that if she gave him the big orange, he would marry her. So, she took the big orange and went to find a boy who loved oranges. Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom loved oranges too! Lily gave Tom the big orange, and he was very happy. But when they opened the orange, something unexpected happened. Inside the orange, there was a little ring! Tom said, "Lily, I was going to ask you to marry me, and now we have a ring from the orange!" They laughed and decided to marry each other. And they lived happily ever after, with their orange cat and many oranges.

John and Jane were both playing in the park. John was having a great time playing tag with his friends while Jane was playing on the swings. Suddenly they heard a strange sound coming from the grass. John and Jane stopped and looked around. There, in the middle of the grass, was a clown! He was wearing a red and yellow hat and big blue shoes. John was excited and ran up to the clown. "Let's play a game!" he said. But the clown didn't respond. He just stood there looking very serious. Jane came up to them and asked, "Why don't we play 'I Spy'?" The clown nodded his head and said, "Okay, let's see who can win." John and Jane closed their eyes and started looking around. John spied a squirrel and yelled, "I win!" The clown smiled and shook John's hand. Then they all went to get some ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma liked to play in her yard. There was a big fence around her yard. She liked to use her toys and play near the fence. One day, Emma saw a small bird on the fence. The bird was trying to fly, but it could not. Emma wanted to help the bird. She used a soft towel to hold the bird. She was very gentle. Emma took care of the bird for many days. She used her toys to keep the bird happy. Soon, the bird was strong and could fly again. The bird flew back to the fence and sang a happy song. Emma felt successful because she helped the bird. She waved goodbye to the bird as it flew away.

One day, a little boy named Tim was taking a nap. He had a dream about a big, red ball. Tim was playing with the ball and laughing. It was a fun dream. Suddenly, Tim woke up. He heard a noise in his room. It was dark, and Tim was frightened. He looked around and saw a thief. The thief wanted to take Tim's toys. Tim was brave. He shouted, "No, don't take my toys!" The thief was frightened too. He ran away and did not take any toys. Tim was happy and went back to sleep.
