Title: Mary the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Mary. Mary was three years old, and she loved going on visits with her mom. One day, Mary asked her mom if she could visit a mine. Her mom said, “Sure, we can do that.” The two of them put on their warm clothes and drove to the mine. When they arrived, Mary was overwhelmed at how big it was. Mom said, “Let’s walk up to the entrance, to see what we can find.” Inside the mine, they found abandoned tools and forgotten tunnels. But at the very bottom, they found a big pile of gold and silver coins. Mary was so excited and shouted, “We can take this home and buy new toys!” Her Mom said, “No no, we must leave the coins here. We can’t take something that doesn’t belong to us.” Mary was disappointed but she listened to her mom. In the end, Mary spoiled herself by having a delicious ice cream cone to make up for not finding something special. She thought to herself, “Next time, we will come back and I can try to find something special.”

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big box of thick clay. She loved to play with the clay and make all kinds of fun shapes. One day, Lily's friend Tom came over to play. Tom saw the thick clay and wanted to play too. But, Tom started to tease Lily by taking her clay and not sharing it. This made Lily sad. Lily's mom saw what was happening and told Tom to share the thick clay. Tom said sorry to Lily and they both played with the clay together. They made a big, happy sun and a funny, little dog. They played all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy went to the ocean with his mom. The ocean was big and blue. The sand was tidy and clean. The boy liked to play near the water. He saw a big wave coming. The boy said, "Mom, look at the big wave!" The mom said, "Stay close, do not go far." The boy wanted to touch the wave, but he was scared. The big wave came closer and closer. The boy went to the water. He wanted to play with the wave. The mom said, "No, do not go!" The boy did not listen. He went into the water. The big wave came and took the boy away. The mom was sad. The boy should have listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a good lamp. This lamp could sing beautiful songs. It made everyone in the house very happy. The lamp loved to sing when the sun went down, and its light filled the room. One day, a cat came into the house. The cat saw the good lamp and wanted to play. The cat jumped up and knocked the lamp down. The lamp could not sing anymore. Everyone in the house was sad. They missed the lamp's songs. A little boy in the house had an idea. He picked up the lamp and gave it a big hug. Suddenly, the lamp began to sing again! The cat said sorry to the lamp, and they all became friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they are down.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toy whip. She liked to pretend she was a horse rider. Lily was running around the house, making silly faces and laughing. Suddenly, Lily tripped and fell. She started to cry because it hurt. Her mom heard her cry and came to see what happened. Lily's mom saw her on the floor and knew she needed comfort. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, my sweet girl. Let's be more careful next time." Lily felt better and stopped crying. She picked up her whip and started to play again. This time, she was more careful and didn't fall.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to pick blueberries in the forest. One day, she found a delicate blueberry bush with the biggest and juiciest blueberries she had ever seen. She was very happy. Lily met her friend, Tim, in the forest. Tim saw the blueberries and said, "Wow, Lily, those blueberries look so good! Can I have some?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I recommend we share these blueberries with everyone in the village. They will love them too." So, Lily and Tim took the blueberries back to the village and shared them with all their friends and family. Everyone was so happy and thanked Lily and Tim for their kindness. Lily felt good inside, knowing that sharing the blueberries was the right thing to do. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone happy.
