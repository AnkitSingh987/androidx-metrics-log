Title: Spot the Dog Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat sausages. One day, his friend, a cat named Kitty, came to visit. Kitty saw the sausages and felt jealous. She wanted to eat them too. Kitty said, "Spot, can we decorate the tree with sausages?" Spot thought it was a fun idea. So they took the sausages and hung them on the tree. The tree looked very pretty with the sausages on it. Then, Spot and Kitty sat under the tree. They looked at the sausages and smiled. They were happy to share the sausages with each other. They ate the sausages from the tree and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Grace. Grace was very excited about her birthday, because her mom was baking her a special pie. Everyday Grace couldn't wait until her birthday and would ask her mom, "When is my birthday?". Her mom would always smile and tell her it was very important to be patient. On the day of Grace's birthday, finally it was time to celebrate! Her family got together, sang happy birthday and her mom brought out the special pie. Everyone was so excited and they all applauded. But when Grace cut into the pie, much to her surprise it was empty! Grace's mom said, "Oh no! Someone must have eaten the pie! It's an important lesson to learn though. Next time don't let anyone take your things that are special to you!" Grace was so sad. She was so looking forward to her special pie. Little did she know that someone in her family had taken it. Grace had to learn a very hard lesson that day, but she never forgot it.

Serena was so excited today. She liked going to the park and watching all the people sign for their new pet. She brought her purse with her and it felt very heavy. At the park, she saw a lot of people looking at puppies and kittens. They were all so cute! Serena asked her mom if she could have one and her mom said yes. Serena was very happy and she went to pick a pet. She chose a big, fluffy kitten and its name was Snowy. She signed the adoption papers and Snowy was hers! The woman at the park gave Snowy a special collar and tag and said it was her name. Serena and Snowy went home. Snowy was tired and curled up in Serena's lap. She knew this was the best day ever. She hugged Snowy tight and promised that they would always be best friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia loved the park because she could play on the swings, slide, and in the sand. She also liked to watch the birds fly in the sky. While Mia was playing, her mom gave her a yummy muffin to eat. Mia took a big bite and said, "Yum! I love muffins!" But then, she saw a small bird on the ground. The bird was not moving. Mia looked at her mom and said, "Mom, the bird is dead." Her mom looked at the bird and said, "Yes, Mia. Sometimes birds get old or sick and they die. Let's wash our hands and go home to remember the happy times we had at the park." Mia and her mom washed their hands and went home. They talked about the fun they had at the park and how they would always remember the little bird.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a little bag of money. He loved his money very much. He would play with it every day. One day, Tim's money disappeared! He could not find it anywhere. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and even in the kitchen. Tim felt very sad and did not know what to do. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him crying. She asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told her about his lost money. Sue thought hard and said, "Let's look for it together!" They looked high and low, and finally found the money behind the couch. Tim was very happy, and he hugged his sister. They played together, and Tim learned to keep his money in a safe place.
