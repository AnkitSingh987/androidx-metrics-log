Title: Tommy and the Stairs

Tommy woke up with a start. He leaped out of his bed and hurried down the stairs. He could hear a loud alarm ringing. “What’s happening?” he asked his mum. “A huge fire," his mum replied. "We have to get out of here! Hurry!” Tommy knew they had to hurry. He ran a fast as he could up the stairs to get his brother and sister. He grabbed their hands and they speeded down the stairs together. Outside, the family saw a huge fire. Smoke filled the air and flames were blazing. People were running away in panic. “We must stay together,” said Tommy’s mum. “Come, we must find safety.” So the family stayed close and speeded away from the fire. They found help from the brave firefighters and soon the fire was put out. Tommy and his family were safe. They hugged each other, happy to have escaped the huge fire.

One day, a long crocodile went to the store. He wanted to buy food for his friends. He walked down the store aisles, looking at all the yummy things. He saw a little girl. She was scared of the long crocodile. She said, "Mommy, look at the crocodile!" Her mommy said, "Don't worry, he is just shopping like us." The long crocodile smiled and said, "Hi, I am a nice crocodile. I am getting food for my friends." The little girl smiled back and said, "Hi, nice to meet you!" The crocodile bought the food and went back to his friends. They were happy and had a fun party. The little girl told her friends about the nice long crocodile she met at the store.

Once upon a time there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a big backyard with lots of neat things to do. He liked playing in his backyard and being outside. "Mommy, can I go play in the backyard?" Tom asked. "Yes, Tom. Have fun!" his mommy said with a smile. Tom spent his whole day in the backyard. He ran and jumped in the grass and played with his toys. He even saw a butterfly! At dinner that night, his mommy said, "Tom, I have a special treat for you. Tomorrow morning, I'm going to make a special breakfast and serve it to you in the backyard!" Tom was so excited. He couldn't wait to have breakfast outside. The next morning, Tom rushed out to the backyard. Sure enough, his mommy had made a special breakfast for him—a big stack of pancakes with syrup and butter, buttered toast, and a big glass of milk. It was so good! Tom sat down and ate his breakfast with a big smile on his face. He enjoyed the fresh air and being in the backyard. He was so happy he asked, "Can I have breakfast in the backyard everyday?" Tom's mommy smiled. "Of course you can, Tom. I would love to serve you breakfast here everyday!"

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day. Lily danced hard and was very good at it. She liked to pray before she danced. It made her feel happy and safe. One day, Lily was dancing near a big tree. She saw a little cat stuck in the tree. The cat was scared. Lily wanted to help the cat, but she didn't know how. She started to pray for the cat to be safe. As Lily prayed, something unexpected happened. The cat began to dance too! The cat danced down from the tree and was safe. Lily was so happy! She knew her prayers worked. From that day on, Lily and the cat danced together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim had a powerful bicycle. He loved to ride it fast in the park. The bicycle was red and shiny. Tim felt happy when he rode it. One sunny day, Tim dared to ride his bicycle down a big hill. He was a little scared, but he knew he could do it. He pedaled fast and went down the hill. The wind blew in his face and he laughed. At the bottom of the hill, Tim saw his friend, Sue. She saw how brave Tim was on his powerful bicycle. Sue wanted to dare too. Tim and Sue rode their bicycles together, having fun and feeling brave.
