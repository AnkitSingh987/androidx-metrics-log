Title: Jaz and Jessica and the Leaf

Once upon a time there were two best friends called Jaz and Jessica. They were only three years old and loved playing together. One day Jaz and Jessica went outside to play in the garden. They saw a large tree with lots of leaves. The leaves were very colourful, yellow, red and orange - they were so cute! Jessica clapped her hands 'Look at all the leaves, lets pick one up!’ Jaz picked up a leaf and said 'Let’s keep this leaf. We can display it in our house and we’ll never forget it.' They both ran back inside to find a goodplace to put the leaf. Jessica spotted an old picture frame and suggested they put the leaf there. Jaz agreed, so they put it in the frame and hung it on the wall. But then, as winter came, the leaves started to fall from the tree. Jaz and Jessica kept their leaf, but saw all the other beautiful leaves fly away. They watched them fall until they saw them no more. The leaf that Jaz and Jessica had framed stayed safe and they were very happy. They loved their special leaf and were proud to display it.

Once upon a time there was a King who liked to be independent. He would often go out by himself and explore the town, but he always had a helper who would make sure he was safe. One day, while the King was out, the helper became very, very thirsty. He looked everywhere, but he could not find any water, so he started to miss the kings help. So the helper decided to shout out for help. He shouted and shouted, but no one heard him. Then he got so tired and thirsty he had to lie down on the ground. But soon after, the King had heard him. He quickly ran back to where the helper was lying and gave him a big hug. He then gave his helper some cold water to drink, and made sure he was safe. The King was very happy to have been able to help, and the helper was very grateful.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was anxious to help her mom in the kitchen. Her mom was making food for the family. Lily wanted to show her mom that she could be a big girl. Lily asked her mom, "Can I help you, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me. You can put some salt in the pot." Lily was very happy that her mom allowed her to help. Lily took the salt and put some in the pot. She was careful not to put too much. Her mom watched and said, "Good job, Lily! Thank you for helping me." Lily felt proud and not anxious anymore. She knew she could be a big girl and help her mom.

Sadie was very upset. She had made a picture at school and was very proud of it. But one of the teachers put a label on it and took it away. Sadie was so upset she started to cry. Mum saw Sadie and came over to her. "What's wrong, Sadie?", asked Mum. Sadie was so sad she couldn't even talk. She just bowed her head, still wiping away the tears. Mum put her arms around Sadie and said, "I'm sorry you are upset, Sadie. Can you tell me about it?" Sadie told Mum about the picture and the label. Mum hugged Sadie and said, "The label doesn't change who you are. You can draw us another picture and show us how proud you are!" Sadie smiled and wiped away the last of her tears. She was no longer upset.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat spaghetti. One day, she saw a long line of spaghetti on the ground. Mia was very curious and decided to follow it. As Mia followed the spaghetti, she met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Mia! I am following the spaghetti too. Do you want to come with me?" Mia said, "Yes, let's go together!" So, they both followed the spaghetti, hoping it would lead them to something fun. But as they kept walking, the sky turned dark and it started to rain. Mia and Tim were scared and cold. They realized that they were lost and couldn't find their way back home. They should have told their parents where they were going. The moral of the story is always to let someone know where you are going, so you can stay safe.
