Title: Amy the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Amy brought her pretty plastic toy with her. It was a small toy with a string that she could pull. She loved playing with it. At the park, Amy saw a big tree. She wanted to play near the tree with her toy. She put her toy on the ground and then she pulled the string. The toy moved and danced around. Amy laughed and clapped her hands. Soon, a little boy named Tim came to play with Amy. They took turns pulling the string of the pretty plastic toy. They played and laughed until it was time to go home. Amy and Tim said goodbye and promised to play together again soon.

Lily and Ben went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and giraffes. But Lily and Ben wanted to see the pandas most of all. They loved pandas. They had panda toys, panda books, and panda pajamas. They walked and walked until they reached the panda house. There were many people waiting to see the pandas. Lily and Ben were impatient. They wanted to see the pandas now. They tried to squeeze through the crowd, but Mom and Dad stopped them. "Wait your turn, please," Mom said. "The pandas are not going anywhere. They are very calm and slow. They like to eat bamboo and sleep a lot." "But we want to see them now!" Lily and Ben whined. "We love pandas!" "Shh, be quiet," Dad said. "Look, there is a panda coming out of its den. Do you see it? It is black and white and fluffy. It is walking towards the glass. Maybe it wants to say hello." Lily and Ben looked and saw the panda. It was so cute and funny. It walked slowly and clumsily, holding a bamboo stick in its mouth. It reached the glass and looked at the people. It saw Lily and Ben and tilted its head. It seemed curious and friendly. Lily and Ben smiled and waved at the panda. The panda waved back with its paw. It dropped the bamboo and pressed its nose against the glass. It made a soft noise, like a sneeze. "Hello, panda!" Lily and Ben said. "We are Lily and Ben. We love you!" The panda nodded and smiled. It picked up the bamboo and started to eat again. It looked happy and relaxed. Lily and Ben were happy too. They had seen the panda. They had made a new friend. They hugged Mom and Dad. "Thank you, Mom and Dad," they said. "Thank you for taking us to the zoo. Thank you for showing us the panda. It was the best day ever!"

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to visit a comet in the sky. He was very excited. His mom and dad took him to a big store to buy a cheap, shiny toy rocket. Tim loved his new toy rocket very much. One sunny day, Tim took his toy rocket outside to play. He threw it up in the air and watched it fly. Suddenly, the rocket went up, up, up into the sky. It went so high that it reached the comet. Tim was amazed. The comet was very happy to see Tim's rocket. It said, "Hello, little rocket. Thank you for coming to visit me." The rocket and the comet became good friends. They played together in the sky every day. Tim watched them from the ground, smiling and waving at his new friend, the comet.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Charlie wanted to learn how to build something. He asked his Daddy to teach him how. Daddy said: "Ok Charlie, let's teach you how to build something today!" They went to the garage and Daddy showed Charlie a box of wood. There were different shapes and sizes of wood in the box. Daddy said: "Today we will teach you how to use this wood and make something special." Charlie was very excited and carefully listened to Daddy. He was amazed as Daddy taught him how to build something with the wood. Daddy showed Charlie how to put the wood together in a special way. Soon, Charlie had made a wonderful toy. He was very proud of his first attempt at building something with wood. He learned that different wood pieces could be used to bring his imagination to life!
