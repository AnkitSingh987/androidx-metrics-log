Title: Lily the Girl and the Sharp

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a special book called a diary. In this diary, she wrote about her days and drew pictures. She loved her diary very much. One day, Lily found a sharp pencil. She was frightened because she knew sharp things could hurt. Lily went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, I found a sharp pencil. What should I do?" Mommy smiled and said, "Thank you for telling me, Lily. Let's put the sharp pencil in a safe place." Lily and her mommy put the sharp pencil away. Then, Lily took her diary and a safe pencil. She wrote about finding the sharp pencil and how her mommy helped her. Lily was happy because she learned how to be safe and could still write in her diary.

Once upon a time, there was a tall boy named Tom. He loved to play at the park. His favorite thing to do was to slide. One sunny day, he went to the park to slide and have fun. While Tom was playing, he saw a big pot near the slide. He was curious, so he looked inside the pot. To his surprise, he found a small, cute bunny. The bunny looked lost and scared. Tom wanted to help the bunny, so he picked it up and put it back in the pot. Just then, the bunny began to grow taller and taller! The bunny became taller than Tom, the slide, and even the trees! Now, Tom and the tall bunny could play and slide together at the park, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her friend, a little girl named Sue. They liked to play together and have fun. One day, Sue said to Kitty, "Let's go outside and see the wonder of the big world!" Kitty did not want to go outside. She was scared of the big world. So, Kitty said, "No, I refuse to go outside." Sue was sad. She wanted Kitty to see the wonder of the world with her. Sue thought of a plan. She took Kitty's favorite toy and showed it to her. Kitty was happy to see her toy. Sue said, "If you come outside with me, you can play with your toy." Kitty thought about it. She loved her toy. She decided to be brave and go outside with Sue. They went outside and saw the wonder of the big world. They played with the toy and had lots of fun. Kitty was not scared anymore. She was happy she went outside with Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy office. She had a toy desk, a toy chair, and a toy phone. She liked to pretend she was the boss of the office. One day, Lily decided to have a fair for her toys. She put on a big hat and made a fun game for her toys to play. She let her toys win prizes like stickers and toy cars. Then, Lily wanted to give her toys a drink. She got a big cup and filled it with water. She went to her toy office and poured the water into little cups for her toys. They all had a great time at the fair and playing in the office. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a little girl called Milly. She was playing in her garden when she saw an orange vanish into the air. She rubbed her eyes and looked again but the orange had gone. "Where did it go?" she wondered aloud. Just then her mother, who was nearby in the garden, heard her and looked up. She said, "Oh, that must be a magic orange. It vanished back to where it came from." Milly furrowed her brow. She didn't understand. So her mother explained, "The orange came from a magical place far away, and when it felt something harsh, it vanished back there. That's why you don't see it any more." Milly nodded and smiled. She was happy that the mysterious orange had gone back to the magic place where it came from.
