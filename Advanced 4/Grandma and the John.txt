Title: Grandma and the John

Grandma was very patient with her grandson, John. She often took him to the park to play and try new games. One day, Grandma had an idea. She wanted John to try a new game, a video game. Grandma got out the video game and set it up. John was so excited to try it. He watched as Grandma showed him how to play. But John got impatient. He started pushing and prodding the buttons until it was too late. The video game broke. Grandma was so disappointed. She told John to mind what he was doing and not be so impatient. Grandma could not fix the game and had to throw it away. John felt sad and apologized to Grandma. Grandma was sad too but she knew it was important to mind, even when things don't turn out as expected.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a swan named Sally. Sally was a very nice swan. She liked to play with her friends, the ducks and the fish. One day, a new swan came to the pond. His name was Sam. Sam felt uncomfortable because he did not know anyone. Sally saw Sam and went to say hi. "Hello, I am Sally," she said. Sam did not reply. Sally felt sad. She wanted to be friends with Sam, but he did not talk to her. The next day, Sally tried again. "Hi Sam, do you want to play?" she asked. This time, Sam replied, "Yes, I would like that." Sally was happy. They played together and became good friends. The other animals saw Sally and Sam being kind to each other and they all became friends too. The moral of the story is to always be kind and patient with others, even if they do not reply at first.

Once upon a time, there was a brave fisherman. He lived near a big lake. Every day, he went to the lake to catch fish. He loved his job and was very good at it. One day, while the fisherman was at the lake, a big bird came. The bird tried to disturb the fisherman. The bird wanted to take the fisherman's fish. The fisherman did not like this. The brave fisherman waved his arms to make the bird go away. The bird flew away, and the fisherman went back to catching fish. He was very happy that he could keep his fish. The fisherman went home and ate his fish. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat yogurt every day. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sue, eating a big cup of yogurt. He felt envious because he wanted a big cup of yogurt too. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I have a big cup of yogurt like Sue?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. But first, you need to lift your toys and put them away." Tim nodded and went to lift his toys. After Tim lifted all his toys and put them away, his mom gave him a big cup of yogurt. He was so happy! He went outside to eat his yogurt with Sue. They both enjoyed their yogurt together, and Tim didn't feel envious anymore. They played together all day, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful farm. On the farm, there were many animals and much wheat. The farmer loved his farm very much. Every day, he would number the wheat to make sure all was good. One day, a big bird came to the farm. The bird was hungry and wanted to eat the wheat. The farmer was sad and did not want the bird to eat his wheat. The farmer tried to make the bird go away, but the bird was too big and strong. The farmer asked his friends for help. They tried to scare the bird away, but the bird did not leave. The bird ate all the wheat on the farm. The farmer was very sad, and the farm was not beautiful anymore. The farmer and his friends could not save the wheat, and the farm was not happy ever again.
