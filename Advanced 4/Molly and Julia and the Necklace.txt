Title: Molly and Julia and the Necklace

Molly and Julia were best friends who liked to play outside together. On this particular day, they were looking for something special. Molly spotted something shiny in the grass. She bent down and pulled it out. It was a spade! The spade was shiny and silver, and had a yellow handle. Molly and Julia were amazed. Molly and Julia started to use the spade to dig. They dug and dug until their arms were tired. Suddenly, something special appeared. It was a treasure chest! Molly and Julia looked at each other with excitement. Molly opened the chest, and inside it was a beautiful necklace. The necklace had shimmering emeralds, and it was so amazing! Molly and Julia hugged each other and thanked their lucky stars. They decided to treasure the necklace, and take it home. Molly and Julia have never forgotten that amazing day. Whenever they look at the necklace, they remember the fun they had.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. She liked to design clothes for her dolls. One day, she made a dress with a zipper. The zipper made it easy to put the dress on the doll. Sue showed her mom the dress. Her mom said, "Wow! That zipper is very useful! Good job, Sue!" Sue felt happy and proud of her work. Sue kept designing more clothes with zippers. All her friends loved them too. Sue learned that making useful things can make people happy. And that made Sue happy too.

Annie was a 3 year old little girl who liked to pretend. She had lots of fun toys, but today she pulled out a mask. It was a scary, big mask and it made her laugh. Annie slowly moved across the room with the mask on. She liked how it felt, like she was a different person. The mask was heavy and it felt funny on her face but Annie kept going. As she moved, she started to drag her feet and go slower and slower. She swung her arms and laughed, feeling silly with the mask on. Pretty soon she was moving so slow that she had to stop and take off the mask. Annie giggled when she saw her reflection in the window. It was fun to pretend for a bit, but now it was time to move on to something else.

Sara and Ben are twins. They share a room and a big brown mattress. Sara likes to sleep on the left side and Ben likes to sleep on the right side. But sometimes, Ben likes to tease Sara and take her pillow or blanket. One night, Ben was feeling naughty. He waited until Sara was asleep and then he crawled over to her side. He grabbed her pink pillow and pulled it under his head. He smiled and closed his eyes. Sara woke up and felt something was wrong. She reached for her pillow but it was gone. She opened her eyes and saw Ben holding it. She was angry and sad. She said, "Ben, give me back my pillow! That's not nice!" Ben pretended to be asleep and snored loudly. He did not give back the pillow. He thought it was funny. Sara did not think it was funny. She got up and walked to the other side of the mattress. She saw Ben's blue blanket and had an idea. She took the blanket and wrapped it around herself. She said, "Now I have your blanket, Ben! How do you like that?" Ben felt cold and woke up. He saw Sara with his blanket and his pillow. He was surprised and sorry. He said, "Sara, I'm sorry. I was just teasing. Please give me back my blanket and pillow. I'm cold and sleepy." Sara saw Ben's face and felt sorry too. She said, "OK, Ben. I'm sorry too. I was just teasing. Here is your blanket and pillow. Let's share and be nice." They hugged and swapped their things. They cuddled on the big brown mattress and fell asleep. They were happy and warm.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big fan in her room. The fan was very pretty and colorful. Mia loved her fan because it kept her cool when the day was hot and dry. One day, Mia was playing with her toy car. She pushed the car too hard, and it hit the fan. The fan split into two parts. Mia was very sad. She did not know what to do. Mia's mom saw the broken fan and hugged Mia. She said, "Don't worry, we can fix it." They worked together and fixed the fan. Mia was happy again. She learned to be careful while playing near her fan. From that day on, Mia and her fan lived happily ever after.
