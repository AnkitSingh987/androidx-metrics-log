Title: Tim the Rat and the Tree

One day, a little rat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a huge tree with many fruits. Tim wanted to eat the fruits, but they were too high for him to reach. He looked around and saw a tall giraffe named Gina. Tim said, "Gina, can you help me get the fruits from the tree? I am too small to reach them." Gina agreed and used her long neck to get the fruits for Tim. As they were eating, Tim felt a tickle in his nose. "Achoo!" he sneezed. Gina laughed and said, "Bless you, Tim!" Tim and Gina became good friends. They helped each other and played together every day. They learned that no matter how big or small you are, everyone needs help sometimes. And when you help others, you make new friends.

One day, a little ant was walking on a dry path. The ant was looking for food to take back to its family. As the ant walked, it saw a big red ball. The ant thought the ball could be food, so it tried to push the ball. A bee saw the ant and flew down. The bee said, "Ant, that is not food. That is a ball to play with." The ant was sad but thanked the bee. The bee had an idea and said, "Ant, let's play with the ball together!" The ant and the bee started to play. They pushed the ball and watched it zoom down the path. They laughed and had fun. After playing, the ant found some food and took it back to its family. The ant and the bee became good friends and played together every day.

Once there was a horse named Betsy. She was so beautiful, like a princess. Betsy was very strong and she was everyone's favorite. Everyone wanted to spend time with her because they trusted Betsy. One day, Betsy was feeling a bit hungry. She looked around, but couldn't find any food. Then, a kind man came over and offered her an apple. The apple was yummy and Betsy enjoyed it very much! She thanked the man for being so kind. Betsy wanted to thank the man every day, so she decided to give him a ride. The man happily hopped on Betsy's back, and then they went on a lovely journey around the countryside. The man loved to have Betsy trust him like that. They stopped and ate lunch together under the shade of a big tree. The man brought some yummy sandwiches for them to share. The horse and the man spent the day laughing and having lots of fun. At the end of the day, the man gave Betsy a big hug and thanked her for trusting him. Then, he gave her one more big apple as a reward. Betsy was in heaven! She had had such a wonderful day with her new friend, and the yummy apple was the perfect ending.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ordinary aeroplane. It was flying high in the sky. The people inside were happy because they were going on a trip. They looked out the windows and saw the clouds below them. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird came and knocked on the window. The people inside were surprised because birds don't usually disturb aeroplanes. The bird was very small and was wearing a tiny hat. The bird told the people that it needed help. It had lost its way and could not find its family. The people inside the aeroplane wanted to help the little bird. They asked the pilot to land the aeroplane so they could help the bird find its family. When they landed, they found the bird's family waiting for them. The bird was very happy, and the people felt good because they had helped a friend.

Tina and Harry were best friends. They did everything together! One day at the park, Tina and Harry saw a gifted tooth in the grass. "That's cool," said Tina. "What should we do with it?" asked Harry. "Let's keep it!" suggested Tina. They separated to find a place to keep the tooth safe. Harry looked in the tree, while Tina looked on the ground. Just then, Tina found a pretty shell. "Look at this!" Tina exclaimed. Harry smiled. "That would be a perfect place to keep the tooth!" So they put the tooth in the shell, and placed the shell under a bush to keep it safe. Tina and Harry were very happy, and ran off to play. They knew that the tooth was safe in its new home.
