Title: Ben the Bunny and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben had many friends, but he liked to tease them. He would hide their toys and laugh when they got sad. His friends did not like this, but they still played with him. One day, Ben met a big bear named Bob. Bob was very difficult to play with because he was so big and strong. Ben wanted to be friends with Bob, so he thought of a fun trade. Ben would give Bob a big red ball if Bob would play with him. The next day, Ben and Bob played together. But Ben started to tease Bob, hiding his big red ball. Bob got very sad and went away. Ben felt bad for teasing Bob and wanted to say sorry. But when he went to find Bob, he saw that Bob had many new friends who did not tease him. Ben felt very lonely. Ben learned that teasing was not nice, and he should be kind to his friends. The next day, he said sorry to Bob and all his friends. They all forgave him and played together again. From that day on, Ben never teased anyone and became a good friend to everyone in the forest.

Once upon a time there was an elderly lady who hated summer. Every June, when the days got brighter and longer, she would sit in her home with a pout on her face. One day, her 3 year old granddaughter visited. "Grandma, why aren’t you outside?" the granddaughter asked. "I don’t like summer," the elderly lady said sadly. "It’s too hot for me". The granddaughter thought for a moment. "Grandma, let’s do something fun together inside," she said. The elderly lady smiled. "What do you want to do?" "Let’s play games and tell stories," said the granddaughter. The elderly lady nodded thoughtfully. "That sounds wonderful," she said. From that day on, the elderly lady and her granddaughter had loads of fun, despite the summer heat. They played games, told stories and discovered how much they loved each other. The elderly lady had found a way to make summer her favorite time of year!

Once upon a time, there was a large, friendly bear named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big cave in the woods. He had a flashlight that he used at night to see in the dark. One day, Bobo met a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost in the woods and was scared. Bobo wanted to help her, so he said, "Hi, I'm Bobo. I will help you find your way home. It's getting dark, but I have a flashlight." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Bobo." As they walked through the woods, Bobo used his flashlight to light the way. Lily tripped on a rock and got her hands dirty. Bobo helped her up and used a soft cloth to wipe her hands clean. Together, they found Lily's home. Lily hugged Bobo and said, "Thank you for helping me, Bobo. You are a good friend."

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious rocket. It could fly very fast in the sky. One day, the rocket was seen by a little boy. He was only 3 years old. The little boy wanted to find out what was inside the rocket. He suggested to his dad, "Let's go and have a look!" His dad was surprised and said, "That is a very mysterious rocket, I am not sure if it is safe for us to fly in it." The little boy was excited. He said, "But Dad, please, let's go and take a look!" Finally his dad agreed, and they went to the rocket. They opened the door and had a look inside. Inside it was empty. The little boy was disappointed. He said to his dad, "I wanted to fly in the rocket!". His dad said, "That is not possible, this rocket is mysterious and very old. It doesn't work anymore." The little boy was sad, but he remembered the good time they had trying to find the mystery of the rocket. He gave his dad a big hug and said, "Let's go home now!" And they did.
