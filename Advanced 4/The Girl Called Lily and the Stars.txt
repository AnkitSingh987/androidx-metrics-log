Title: The Girl Called Lily and the Stars

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She lived in a big house with her mum, dad and little brother. Every night, Lily watched the stars twinkle in the sky. She especially liked the big yellow star that shone the brightest of them all. One night when it was time for bed, Lily was feeling very excited. She was so eager to go to sleep and see the stars again. So her mum tucked her into bed and told her to close her eyes. A few hours later, Lily woke up to find the sky full of stars. She looked up and saw the big yellow star twinkling brightly and she felt so happy. She jumped out of bed and ran around her garden, feeling very lively. She was so excited that she danced and sang until daybreak. The sun began to rise and the stars started to dim. Lily felt sleepy but so full of joy because she knew that the stars would wake her up again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big rocket called Missile. Missile was very strong and could stand tall. People liked Missile because he was reliable and always did what he was told. One day, Missile had a job to do. He had to go up in the sky and help his friends. Missile was excited and ready to go. He stood tall and waited for the time to fly. When it was time for Missile to fly, he went up, up, up into the sky. His friends were so happy to see him. They knew they could trust Missile because he was reliable. Missile did his job well, and everyone was safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy family. The mother and father had a very beautiful daughter named Susan. One day, Susan was playing in the garden and decided to have a soak in the fountain. Susan felt so happy and free and started laughing and splashing around. "Oh Susan!" said her mother. "That will make you very wet!" But Susan said, "I don't care, it's so much fun!" So she stayed in the fountain and soaked up all of the sunshine. When she was done, Susan was all dried up and feeling so relaxed. The wealthy family was very proud of their little daughter for having such a great day out in the sunshine.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big drawer. He was very excited to see what was inside. He opened the drawer and saw many toys. Tim was very happy and started to play with the toys. He played and played all day. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the toys all over the floor. She looked worried and said, "Tim, did you spoil your sister's toys? They were in the drawer for her birthday." Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Mom. I didn't know." Just then, Tim's sister, Lily, came into the room. She saw the toys and started to laugh. "It's okay, Tim," she said. "I found out about the surprise and already played with the toys too!" Tim and Lily were both happy, and they played with the toys together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy was learning how to whistle. He practiced every day. One day, he met a big bird named Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl was very wise and important. Mr. Owl said, "Billy, I need your help. I have an operation to do. Can you whistle a song for me?" Billy nodded and said, "Yes, I can do that." Billy whistled a happy song for Mr. Owl. The song helped Mr. Owl with his operation. When it was over, Mr. Owl said, "Thank you, Billy. Your whistling was very helpful. You did a great job." Billy was proud of himself. He knew that even a little bird could do important things. From that day on, Billy and Mr. Owl were the best of friends. They helped each other and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a land of many seasons, there lived a kind tree and an impatient little bird. The tree had big green leaves in the warm season and no leaves in the cold season. The bird loved to sit on the tree's branches and sing beautiful songs. One day, the impatient bird wanted to build a nest, but it didn't have any soft things to make it. The kind tree saw the bird's problem and decided to lend some of its leaves to the bird. The bird was very happy and used the leaves to make a warm and cozy nest. As the seasons changed, the kind tree and the impatient bird became good friends. The tree always lent its leaves to the bird, and the bird always sang songs for the tree. They learned that sharing and being patient made their days happier and brighter.
