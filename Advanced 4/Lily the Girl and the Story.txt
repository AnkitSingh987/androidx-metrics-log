Title: Lily the Girl and the Story

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree and heard a whisper. She was frightened. "Who is there?" she asked. A friendly skeleton came out from behind the tree. The skeleton said, "Don't be scared. I am nice." Lily was surprised but she listened. The skeleton told her a story about being kind to others. In the story, a mean boy learned to be nice. He helped a friend and they became best friends. Lily liked the story. She went home and told her mom. Her mom was happy. Lily learned that even if something looks scary, it can be nice and teach us good things.

Once upon a time, there was a useful hook on a wall. It was very good at holding things. One day, a little girl named Lucy found the hook. She liked to imagine a lot. Lucy decided to hang her bag on the hook. One sunny day, Lucy went outside to play. She saw a big tree and imagined it was a castle. She played and played until it was time to go home. When she went inside, she saw her bag was not on the hook anymore. Lucy was sad and looked everywhere for her bag. Then, something unexpected happened. Her little dog, Spot, came running with her bag. Spot had taken the bag off the hook to play. Lucy was happy to find her bag and laughed. From that day on, Lucy and Spot played together and imagined fun things with the useful hook.

Once, there was a little boy. He wanted to pick something special. So he went to the store. He looked around and saw a big sausage. It was pink and yummy! He thought he was very lucky so he picked it. On the way home, he took it out of its packet and ate it. He liked it so much he wanted to pick another one. So the next day, he went back to the store. This time, he was even luckier. He picked two sausages! He took them home and shared them with his friends. They all agreed they were the tastiest sausages they ever had. The little boy felt very proud. He was so lucky to have picked such delicious sausages!

Tim and Sam are friends. They like to play with blocks. They build a big tower with the blocks. Tim puts the last block on the tip of the tower. He is happy. But Sam is bossy. He wants to put his block on the tip too. He says, "Move, Tim. I want to put my block there. It is my turn." Tim says, "No, Sam. The tower is done. It is very tall. If you put your block there, it will fall." Sam does not listen. He pushes Tim away. He tries to put his block on the tip. But he is not careful. He makes the tower shake. The blocks fall down. They make a loud noise. Tim and Sam are sad. They look at the mess. They cry. They are not friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl called Amy. Today, Amy's mommy was taking her to the park. When they arrived, Amy saw an olive tree and she was so excited. Amy asked her mommy, "Can I touch?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, sweetheart, you can touch it!" Amy carefully touched the olive tree and felt the rough bark. She was so happy that she ran around the tree, laughing and squealing with delight. She looked up at the tree, and saw a big green olive smiling down at her. She reached up and carefully plucked it off the tree. She squealed with delight as she felt the wrinkly skin and tasted the salty flesh of the olive. From then on, Amy always looked forward to visiting the park and touching the olive tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in his backyard. He liked his backyard because it was big and organized. He had a swing, a slide, and a sandbox to play in. Tim's mom called out, "Tim, play nice and have fun!" While playing in the sandbox, Tim saw a small bug. He kneeled down to get a closer look. The bug was green and had tiny wings. Tim said, "Hi, little bug! Do you want to play with me?" To Tim's surprise, the bug answered, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" The bug was a talking bug! Tim and the bug played on the swing, the slide, and in the sandbox. They had so much fun together. From that day on, Tim had a new best friend, the talking bug.
