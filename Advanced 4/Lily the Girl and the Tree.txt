Title: Lily the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in the heat with her friends. One day, Lily and her friend Tom were playing near a big tree. Lily said to Tom, "I want to be successful when I grow up. What do you want to be?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "I want to be a good friend to everyone." As they played, they saw a little bird who was sad. The bird had lost its family. Lily and Tom decided to help the bird find its family. They looked around the village and asked people if they had seen the bird's family. Finally, they found the bird's family near the river. The little bird was happy to be with its family again. Lily and Tom felt good for helping the bird. The moral of the story is that being kind and helpful to others can make you feel successful and happy.

One day, a zebra was walking in the icy cold. He was looking for his friend, the lion. The zebra called out, "Lion, where are you?" But there was no answer. The zebra was sad and kept looking. Then, the zebra saw a big box. He thought maybe the lion was hiding in the box. So, the zebra opened the box. Inside, there was a big, shiny star. The zebra was very surprised! The shiny star said, "Hello, zebra! I am a magic star. I can help you find your friend." The zebra was so happy! The magic star took the zebra to the lion. But when they found the lion, he was not a lion anymore. He was a big, friendly bear! The magic star had turned the lion into a bear. The zebra and the bear laughed and played together. They were still best friends, even if one was now a bear.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike. He saw his friend, Sue, who had a big bag. "What is in the bag?" Tim asked. Sue said, "I have a big, hot potato!" Tim wanted to see the hot potato, but Sue said, "No! It is too hot!" Tim did not listen. He opened the bag and the hot potato rolled out. It rolled down the hill, very fast! Tim and Sue had to ride their bikes to catch the hot potato. They rode fast, but the potato was faster. At the bottom of the hill, the potato stopped. Tim and Sue were happy they caught the hot potato. They learned to listen when someone says something is too hot.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spike. Spike loved to visit his friend, the big tree, in the park. Every day, Spike would run and play with the tree. The tree had many hidden surprises for Spike to find. One day, when Spike went to visit the tree, he found a hidden ball. Spike was so happy! He played with the ball all day long. He would throw the ball and then run to catch it. The tree watched Spike and smiled. As the sun went down, Spike knew it was time to go home. He said goodbye to the big tree and took his new hidden treasure with him. The next day, Spike couldn't wait to visit the tree again and see what other hidden surprises it had for him.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom liked to bring his guitar with him everywhere he went. One day, he went to the park to play his guitar for his friends. They all liked to hear him play. While Tom was playing, a big dog named Max came to the park. Max wanted to play with the guitar too. Tom did not want to share his guitar, but Max took it anyway. Tom was very sad and his friends were mad at Max. Then, something unexpected happened. Max started to play the guitar very well. All the animals at the park started to dance and have fun. Tom realized that sharing can make everyone happy. So, Tom and Max played the guitar together and became good friends. The moral of the story is that sharing can bring happiness to everyone.
