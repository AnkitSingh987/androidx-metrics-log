Title: Ben and the Car

Ben liked to speak to his toy cars. He gave them names and voices and made them race and crash on the floor. He had many cars, but his favorite was Red, a shiny fire truck with a loud siren. Red was very popular with the other cars, and Ben always picked him first. One day, Ben's mom said they had to go to the store. She told Ben to bring one toy with him. Ben looked at his cars and chose Red. He put him in his pocket and ran to the car. He was happy to have Red with him. At the store, Ben saw many things he liked. He saw books, candy, balloons, and more cars. He wanted to look at everything, but his mom said they had to hurry. She gave him a list of things to find and put in the cart. Ben tried to help, but he was easily distracted. He forgot about Red in his pocket. When they finished shopping, they went to the checkout. Ben's mom paid for the things and put them in bags. She told Ben to hold one bag and follow her to the car. Ben did as he was told, but he felt something hot in his pocket. He reached in and pulled out Red. He was shocked to see that Red was melted and twisted. His shiny paint was burned and his siren was broken. Ben started to cry. "What happened?" his mom asked, looking at the ruined toy. Ben sobbed and pointed to the candy in the bag. He had put Red next to a chocolate bar, and the heat from the sun had melted both of them. He had ruined his favorite car. "I'm sorry, Ben," his mom said, hugging him. "It was an accident. We can get you a new car, okay?" Ben nodded, but he felt very sad. He loved Red, and he missed his voice. He wished he had not forgotten about him. He learned a hard lesson that day. He learned to be careful with his toys, and to not speak to them when they were in his pocket.

Once, there was a little girl who found a thin plant growing in her garden. She decided to take care of it and every day, she watered it and made sure it was in the sunshine. One day, when it was very cold outside, the girl noticed the plant outside in her garden. She ran to fetch her coat and a blanket and carefully wrapped the plant in it. The little girl said, “I don’t want it to freeze!” But just as she had said these words, the plant started to move. The girl looked closer and found that there was a little caterpillar inside the plant. The caterpillar said, “Thank you for taking care of me! I was so scared that I was going to freeze.” The little girl smiled and said, “You are very welcome!” The moral of the story is that we should always take care of our plants and animals, because even the littlest things can have feelings.

Once upon a time, in a lovely green park, there was a big tree. The tree had a little hole at the bottom. One day, a cute bunny found the hole and thought it was a good place to hide. The bunny had a mine with many shiny rocks. He wanted to keep them safe. So, he put the rocks inside the hole in the tree. He was very happy because now his shiny rocks were hidden. One sunny day, the bunny was playing near the tree. He saw a squirrel looking at the hole. The squirrel found the shiny rocks in the hole. The bunny ran to the squirrel and said, "These are mine!" The squirrel was nice and gave the rocks back to the bunny. They became friends and played together in the lovely park.

Once upon a time, there was a small office. In the office, there was a big machine that could print. One day, it was very icy outside. The cold wind blew through the open door. A little girl named Lily came into the office. She wanted to print a picture for her mom. Lily said, "Hello, office! I want to print a pretty picture for my mom. Can you help me?" The big machine said, "Yes, Lily! I can help you print your picture." So, Lily put her picture in the machine and it started to print. The picture was very pretty with bright colors. Lily was very happy. She said, "Thank you, big machine!" Then, she closed the door so the icy wind would not come in anymore. Lily took her pretty picture and went home to give it to her mom. Her mom loved the picture and they lived happily ever after.
