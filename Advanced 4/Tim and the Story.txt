Title: Tim and the Story

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play with his toys on the floor. One day, Tim saw a big yawn come from his mom. She looked very tired. "Mom, why did you yawn?" asked Tim. Mom said, "I am tired because I did a lot of crazy cleaning today." Tim thought for a moment and then said, "I will help you clean next time, so you won't be so tired." The next day, Tim helped his mom clean the house. They picked up toys from the floor and put them away. They had fun and laughed while they cleaned. Tim's mom was not as tired this time because they worked together. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make things easier and more fun for everyone.

One day, a little duck went to visit his friend, the light butterfly. The duck and the butterfly were very good friends. They liked to play in the sun and have fun together. They loved to see each other and talk about what they did all day. The duck noticed that the butterfly was sad. He asked her, "Why are you sad, my friend?" The butterfly said, "I am not as fast as the other butterflies. They can fly so high and so fast, but I am slow and light." The duck thought for a moment and said, "But being light is a good thing. You can fly in the wind and not get tired. You can see the world from up high and enjoy the beauty." The butterfly listened to the duck and thought about it. She realized that being light was a good thing, and it made her special. She thanked the duck for helping her see the good in herself. From that day on, the butterfly was happy and proud to be light. The duck and the butterfly continued to play and have fun together, and they learned that everyone is special in their own way.

One day, a little girl named Emily and her mommy went to the beach. Emily was very excited! When they got to the beach, Emily looked around and asked “Mommy, where’s the whale?” Her mommy smiled and said “There, see? Look in the ocean, there’s a big whale swimming. Can you see it?” Emily smiled and said “Yes, I can see it!” She was happy to have spotted a whale. They watched it swim around in the ocean. Emily and her mommy decided to take a rest and sat on a comfortable spot on the beach. Emily snuggled up to her mommy and said “Mommy, I love the beach!” Her mommy said “Yes, it’s very nice isn’t it? It’s so comfortable and peaceful here.” Emily just smiled and looked out to sea, wondering what the whale was up to. Maybe it was thinking about a question too.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a kind lady named Sue. Sue liked to lay on the soft grass in her garden and look at the sky. She had a friend named Tom, who lived next door. They liked to talk and play together. One sunny day, Sue and Tom were in the garden. Sue said to Tom, "Let's have a picnic!" Tom agreed, and they both went to get some food. Sue brought some bread and cheese, while Tom brought some juice. They didn't have any wine, because wine is not for little kids. Sue and Tom sat on the soft grass, eating and talking. They laughed and had a lot of fun. The sun was warm, and the birds sang in the trees. It was a very peaceful day. After their picnic, they lay on the grass, looking at the clouds. They were very happy to be friends and spend time together.

One gloomy day, two children were sitting on the beach. They were Bonnie and Ezra. Bonnie was older than Ezra, but he was a lot happier. Bonnie said to Ezra, "Listen! Can you hear the language of the ocean?" Ezra listened carefully and he could hear the waves crashing on the sea shore. It sounded like a song. Then suddenly, the sun began to shine. All the gloomy clouds were gone and they were filled with joy. Bonnie and Ezra laughed and danced in the warm sunshine. They were so happy to be free to play. They had a wonderful day together.
