Title: The Dog and the Spot

Once upon a time there was a dog. His name was Spot. He was very playful and happy. One day he went outside to play. Everything seemed safe. He was having so much fun! Suddenly, the ground shook. Spot became fearful. A loud noise made him run away. He went back inside his house but it felt different. Suddenly, the ground shook again. This time it was worse. The walls were shaking and cracking. Spot became very scared. He did not know what to do. His house was no longer a safe place. After a few minutes, everything calmed down but Spot was still fearful. He never felt safe again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, empty house. No one lived there, and so it was always full of silence. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily and her friends decided to go inside the house to play. When they went inside, they found many old things. They played with the toys and ran around the house. But Lily's mom had told her to behave and be careful. So, Lily stopped playing and told her friends that they should be careful too. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise from upstairs. They were scared, but Lily remembered to behave. She held her friend's hand and they all walked slowly up the stairs. They found a little kitten who had knocked over a vase. They took the kitten outside and played with it. The kitten was happy and so were the children. In the end, Lily and her friends learned that it is important to behave and listen to their parents. The empty house was not so scary after all, and the silence was broken by their laughter and the kitten's purrs. The children promised to be careful and always behave, even when they were having fun. And the kitten found a new home with Lily, where it would always be loved and cared for.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red ball. The ball lived in a peaceful park. The park was very pretty with green grass and tall trees. The ball loved to play with the kids who came to the park. One sunny day, a boy named Tim came to the park with his mom. Tim saw the big, red ball and said, "Mom, look at that ball! Can I play with it?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Go have fun!" Tim ran to the ball and started to kick it. He kicked it high and far. The ball was very happy to play with Tim. After playing, Tim and his mom sat on a bench to rest. They talked about their vacation. They would go to the beach and play in the sand. The big, red ball listened to them and felt happy. The ball knew that when Tim came back from vacation, they would play together again in the peaceful park. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She was fit and strong and liked to play in the park. One day she hurt her arm while jumping around the park. Her mom and dad took her to the hospital and the doctor said it was best to take an x-ray. They went to another room where Amy had to lie very still so the x-ray could work. When the x-ray finished, the doctor told them everything was fit and healthy! They all said good bye to the doctor and then went home. Mom and Dad closed the door of their house and Amy ran inside. She was happy to be home and her arm was feeling better too!

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a strong girl named Lily. She loved to balance on one foot. One day, she went to the park to play. At the park, Lily saw a big dog. The dog was sad. It could not balance on one foot like Lily. Lily wanted to help the dog. She had an idea. Lily put a toy on the dog's head. The dog tried to balance the toy. The dog was happy. Lily learned that helping others makes you feel good too. And they became best friends.
