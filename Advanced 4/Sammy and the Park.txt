Title: Sammy and the Park

Sammy was walking down the street with his parent. He felt a stirring inside him and he asked his parent why he was feeling this way. His parent told him he's was feeling jealous for all the other kids who had their parents with them. Sammy just nodded and continued walking. As the day went on, Sammy realized why he felt jealous, because he hadn't seen his other parent in a very long time. Suddenly he spotted them in the park and he raced over, filled with excitement. His parent smiled and hugged him tight, and his stirring feelings turned into warmth. His parent had finally come back and Sammy was so pleased. Now he had both of his parents with him.

One day, a colorful bird named Bluey flew into the mist. He wanted to gather berries for his friends. Bluey saw a big bush with red berries. He was very happy. Bluey started to gather the berries when a big bear came. The bear said, "These are my berries! You can't have them!" Bluey was sad and scared. He did not want to fight the big bear. Bluey flew away and found another bush with yellow berries. He gathered the berries and took them to his friends. They all shared the yummy berries and were very happy. The big bear did not get to eat the yellow berries, but Bluey and his friends did.

Once upon a time, in a thick forest, there lived a deer. The deer liked to collect things. Every day, the deer would go out and find things to add to his collection. One day, the deer found a big, round rock. He thought it was pretty, so he took it home. The next day, he found a shiny leaf. The deer was happy to add it to his collection too. As days went by, the deer's collection grew bigger and bigger. He had many rocks, leaves, and sticks. All the animals in the forest came to see his collection. The deer was very proud and happy to share his collection with his friends.

Tom and Anna were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big box of toys that they shared. But sometimes they wanted different toys and they fought. One day, Tom wanted to play with the truck and Anna wanted to play with the doll. They both grabbed the same toy and pulled hard. The toy broke in two and they both cried. "Look what you did!" Tom shouted at Anna. "No, you did it!" Anna shouted back. They were very angry and did not want to play together anymore. They decided to separate their toys and their space. Tom took half of the box and half of the garden. Anna took the other half. They drew a line with a stick and said, "This is mine and that is yours. Don't cross the line!" They played by themselves for a while, but they soon got bored. They missed each other and their toys. They looked at the other side of the line and wondered what the other was doing. They were very nosy. They saw that the other had some toys that they liked. They also saw that the other looked lonely and sad. They felt sorry and wanted to say sorry. They both walked to the line and said at the same time, "I'm sorry. Can we play together again?" They smiled and hugged and said, "Yes, let's play together again. Sharing is more fun than separating." They put their toys back in the box and mixed them up. They did not care who had what. They just wanted to have fun. They played with the truck and the doll and the ball and the bear. They also played with the earth and the flowers and the bugs. They were very happy and forgot about the line. They were the best twins in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an ordinary boy, but he had a special pair of sunglasses. These sunglasses were not like any other sunglasses. They were magic! One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his magic sunglasses. He saw his friends playing with their toys. Tim put on his magic sunglasses and started to count all the toys. One, two, three, four, five! There were so many toys! Tim's friends saw his magic sunglasses and wanted to try them too. They all took turns wearing the sunglasses and counting different things in the park. They counted flowers, trees, and even the clouds in the sky. It was a fun day for everyone, and they all loved Tim's magic sunglasses.
