Title: The Rich Girl Called Sarah and the Ball

Once upon a time there lived a rich girl called Sarah. She lived in a beautiful town and loved playing outdoors. Every day Sarah would look up at the sky and daydream about all the wonderful things she could do. One day Sarah went to the store with her mum. She saw so many different things that she wanted to buy! Everything looked colourful and exciting. "Mum, can I get a toy?" Sarah asked her mum. Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, you can get one toy, Sarah." Sarah was so excited, she ran around the store looking for the perfect toy. After a few minutes, she chose a big red ball with yellow stars on it. "This is the best toy ever," Sarah said. Her mum smiled and said, "Yes it is. You're so lucky to have a rich imagination like yours." Sarah hugged her mum and ran outside. She held her toy up to the sky and said, "Thank you for my toy, sky!" And from that day onwards, Sarah's toy made her feel even richer every time she looked up at the sky.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He felt miserable because he had no one to play with. While he was walking, he saw a big book on a bench. The book was a novel. Tim thought, "Maybe the novel can make me feel better." Tim started to read the novel out loud. Suddenly, the novel spoke to him! It said, "Hi, Tim! I am a magic book. I can make you feel happy!" Tim was very surprised. He asked, "How can you make me feel happy, magic book?" The novel replied, "I can make your toys come to life!" Tim thought about his favorite toy, a big, red ball. The novel used its magic, and the ball came to life! Tim played with the ball in the park and was not miserable anymore. He was so happy to have a new friend, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a slow automobile. It was red and shiny. The automobile had four big wheels and went "beep beep" when it moved. It liked to go on trips with its friend, the blue bicycle. One sunny day, the slow automobile and the blue bicycle went to the park. They saw a big tree and a soft green grass. The blue bicycle said, "Let's lie on the grass and look at the sky." The slow automobile agreed, and they both lay down on the grass. As they lay on the grass, they saw fluffy white clouds in the sky. They made shapes with the clouds, like a dog, a cat, and a big ice cream. They had so much fun. After a while, they got up and went back home, feeling happy and tired. The slow automobile and the blue bicycle had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, a little robot lived in a big house. The robot was very stubborn. It liked to tease the cat that lived in the house too. One day, the robot said to the cat, "You can't catch me!" The cat tried to catch the robot, but the robot was too fast. They played all day, running around the house. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat found a big red button on the robot's back. The cat pushed the button with its paw. The robot stopped running and teasing. The cat was happy and they became friends. The robot learned not to tease the cat anymore, and they played nicely together.

Jack was a young boy. He wanted to go play in the park. He grabbed his rope and went outside. He saw a tall tree with a branch hanging over the ground. Jack got an idea. He wanted to try and lean out over the branch and swing back and forth. He asked his mom if it was okay. She said she wasn't sure, so she asked Jack’s dad. Dad said he could try it if Jack was careful. Jack wrapped one end of the rope around the branch and held the other end with his hands. He leaned over the branch and felt the wind against his face. He giggled as he swung back and forth. Jack’s mom watched from the ground below, making sure he was safe. She smiled, happy that her little boy was having fun.
