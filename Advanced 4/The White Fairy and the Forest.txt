Title: The White Fairy and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a white fairy. She had a magic wand and liked to help her friends. The white fairy had many friends like birds, rabbits, and squirrels. One day, the white fairy saw a little girl crying near a tree. The girl was sad because she lost her toy. The white fairy wanted to help the girl. She waved her magic wand and a new toy appeared. The little girl smiled and said thank you. The white fairy was happy that she could help the little girl. She flew back to her home in the forest. When she got home, she saw her friends waiting for her. They were having a big party to celebrate her good deed. The white fairy and her friends danced and played until they were all tired. Then, the white fairy went to sleep, happy that she could finish her day by helping someone.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny garden, there was a big avocado tree. The tree had many green avocado friends hanging on its branches. The avocados loved to dance and play in the warm sun. One day, a cold wind came and the avocados started to shiver. They did not like the cold wind. The wind made them freeze and they could not dance and play anymore. The avocados felt very sad. Then, a warm sun came back and hugged the avocados. The avocados felt happy again. They were not cold anymore. The warm sun made them feel good, and they started to dance and play again. The bright sun and the avocados were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He went up, up, up in the tree. But then, Tom started to fall down, down, down. He was scared! Luckily, a nice bird saw Tom fall and helped him land on the soft grass. Tom was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom! Be careful next time." Soon, it was time for a holiday. Tom and the bird became good friends. They played together all day on the holiday. They ran in the grass, jumped in the leaves, and had a fun time. And Tom never climbed a big tree again.

One day, a little girl named Sally felt tired. She did not want to play with her toys or go outside. Her mom saw that Sally was not her happy self. So, she decided to call the doctor. The phone went "ring, ring" as she waited for the doctor to answer. "Hello, doctor?" said Sally's mom. "My little girl is very tired and not playing. Can you please help us?" The doctor told Sally's mom to bring her in to see him. Sally and her mom went to the doctor's office. The doctor checked Sally and said, "Sally, you just need some rest. Go home and take a nap. You will feel better soon." Sally and her mom thanked the doctor and went home. After a good nap, Sally felt better and was ready to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty doll named Lucy. Lucy had a long string as a pretty necklace. Sue loved to play with Lucy and her string necklace. One day, Sue saw a big green frog. The frog was envious of Lucy's string necklace. The frog wanted a string necklace too. So, Sue found another string and gave it to the frog. The frog was very happy. Sue was so happy that she kissed the frog. The frog smiled and kissed Sue back. Now, Sue, Lucy, and the frog were all friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a big fat skeleton. He didn't have a name! He was so upset because all of his friends had names. Skeleton was so sad he decided to go on an adventure to look for a name. He searched high and low but couldn't find a name he liked. He was just about to give up when he heard a voice call his name. He looked around but couldn't see anyone. The voice called him again, this time it sounded different. As he looked around he noticed the voice was coming from a little bird. He asked the bird what his name was and the bird said, "Your name is Skeleton". Skeleton was so happy he had a name at last! He thanked the bird and went on his way with a big smile on his face.
