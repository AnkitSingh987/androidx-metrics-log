Title: The Powerful Lion and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a powerful lion. He was the king of the forest. All the animals were his friends. They played together every day. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew and the rain fell. The animals were scared. They needed the powerful lion to protect them. The lion was brave and strong. He called all his friends to a safe place in the forest. The storm went away and the sun came out. The animals were happy again. They thanked the powerful lion for protecting them. They all watched the beautiful sunset together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a boy. The mommy wanted the boy to enjoy his holiday, but she wanted him to be very careful. The mommy said to the boy, “Honey, you can go out and play today, but you must be careful.” The boy looked at his mommy and nodded. The boy was excited to go and play. He ran out of the house. He played all day. He forgot to be careful. Suddenly, the boy met a big dog. The dog was friendly, but it was very big. The boy wanted to pet the dog, but the mommy never allowed it. The boy petted the dog anyway. The dog was happy, but it was too strong for the boy and it knocked him over. The mommy heard the loud noise and came running. She saw the boy lying on the ground. She was very sad. The mommy hugged the boy tightly and showed him how to be careful. The mommy made sure the boy never forgot to be careful.

Once upon a time there was a little girl, called Jane. She was very obedient. Every day, Jane did a quiz. She always tried her best and worked hard. One day, her teacher gave her a special quiz. It was more difficult than the others. Jane was scared, but she worked very hard. She did the quiz carefully and followed all the rules. When the quiz was finished, Jane was so happy. She knew she had done her best. Then, her teacher gave her a prize for being so obedient and doing the quiz so well. Jane was so happy that she hugged her teacher. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a sister named Lily. Mia and Lily loved to play outside in the sun. One day, they went to the woods to gather pretty rocks and flowers. They were very happy. As they walked, Mia said, "Lily, we need to be careful. Some things can be dangerous in the woods." Lily nodded her head and they held hands as they walked. They found many pretty things and put them in their bags. Soon, they saw a big, dark cave. Mia remembered her words about dangerous things. She said, "Lily, we should not go in there. It might be dangerous." Lily agreed, and they went back home. They showed their mom all the pretty rocks and flowers they gathered. Mia and Lily were happy they stayed safe and had a fun day.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Lucy who loved to play games. One day, Lucy was playing with her friends in the garden when she found an old skull. She picked it up, excited to look at it, when her friends shouted "let's play a game. Let's see who can get the skull to the top of the tree first." Lucy happily agreed, then they all took turns trying to throw it high enough. Each of them tried but they didn't manage to make it to the top of the tree. Lucy was getting frustrated. She shouted, "Let me try!" Her friends all cheered her on as she took a deep breath and threw the skull as high as she could. It went so high that it flew over the tree and landed in the grass on the other side. Lucy was so excited that she had won the game. She cheered and all her friends hugged her. They were so proud of how successful she had been. She was happy to have won and couldn't wait to show off her new skills. From then on, Lucy was known as the best game player in her neighborhood!
