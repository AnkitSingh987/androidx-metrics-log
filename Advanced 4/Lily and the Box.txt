Title: Lily and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, heavy toy box filled with all her favorite toys. One day, she found a small model inside her toy box. The model was a pretty house with many colors. Lily loved the model house very much. She played with it every day. She would pretend that she lived in the house with her toy friends. Sometimes, she would even give the model house a little kiss before she went to bed. One day, Lily's mommy saw how much she loved the model house. She decided to help Lily make the model house even more special. They painted it with more colors and added tiny flowers in the front. Lily was so happy, she kissed the model house and her mommy too. From that day on, Lily and her toy friends had even more fun in their colorful, heavy model house.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to listen to his grandpa's stories. One day, Grandpa told Tim about a treasure hidden in the big park. Tim went to the big park to look for the treasure. He walked and walked, and then he saw a big tree. Tim thought the treasure was under the tree. He started to dig and dig. As Tim dug, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Tim far away. Tim was scared and sad. He never found the treasure, and he never saw his grandpa again. The end.

One day, a big powerful bird found a camera in the park. The bird did not know what it was, so he asked his friend, the squirrel. The squirrel said, "This is a camera. It can take pictures of things we see." The bird and the squirrel decided to split the camera and take turns using it. The bird took pictures of the sky and the squirrel took pictures of the trees. They were very happy to share the camera and see new things. One day, the bird saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. The bird took a picture of the girl and showed it to the squirrel. They both decided to help the girl find her toy. They looked everywhere and finally found it under a bush. The girl was very happy and thanked the bird and the squirrel. The bird and the squirrel learned that sharing and helping others made them feel happy, too. The camera brought them closer and taught them the joy of helping others. The moral of the story is that sharing and helping others can make everyone happy.

One day Lola was in her backyard playing. She saw a zebra running and she waved at it with a big smile. The zebra waved back with a friendly hello. Lola was so excited and ran to get her mom. "Mommy, mommy!" she yelled. "I saw a zebra waving!" Mommy chuckled and said, "Lola dear, I think the zebra was waving at you because it saw you were very organized!" Lola smiled and walked back to her backyard. She waved at the zebra one more time just to be sure the zebra saw that she was organized. The zebra waved back and they both went on their own way.

One day, Tom went outside to play. It was a dark day with big clouds in the sky. Tom wore his warm coat and his red gloves. He liked his gloves because they were soft and cozy. As Tom played, it started to rain. The rain made little puddles on the ground. Tom jumped in the puddles and laughed. His gloves got wet, but he did not care. He liked the rain and the sound it made. After a while, Tom's mom called him inside. She took his wet gloves and put them near the fire to dry. Tom sat by the fire, too, and felt warm and happy. He liked playing in the rain, but he also liked being warm and dry inside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pretty white dress that she loved to wear. Sue liked to skip and play outside in her dress. One sunny day, Sue went to the park to skip and have fun. She skipped so fast that she fell down and hurt her knee. Sue was sad and her knee was red. Sue's mommy came to help her. She put a band-aid on Sue's knee and gave her a hug. Sue felt better and went back to skipping with a smile on her face. The end.
