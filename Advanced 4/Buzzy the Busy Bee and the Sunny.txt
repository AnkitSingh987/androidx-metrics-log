Title: Buzzy the Busy Bee and the Sunny

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to fly around and play with his friends. His best friend was a big sunflower named Sunny. They liked to talk and laugh all day. One day, Buzzy went to visit Sunny, but he could not find her. He started to worry. He looked and looked, but Sunny was not there. Buzzy felt sad and missed his friend. Then, something unexpected happened! Buzzy saw a tiny sunflower growing close by. It was Sunny's baby! Now Buzzy had a new friend to play with. He was not sad or worried anymore. Buzzy and the baby sunflower played and laughed together, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to play with her mom's new makeup. Her mom said, "Wait, Lily, we can play with it later." Lily felt sad but she knew she had to delay playing with the makeup. Lily went to play with her toys. She played with her dolls and her cars. Time went by and Lily forgot about the makeup. She had so much fun with her toys. Later, her mom came into the room and said, "Lily, do you still want to play with the new makeup?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They played with the makeup and had a great time. Lily was happy she waited.

Lily and Max were going to the park with their mom. They liked to play on the swings, the slide and the sandbox. Mom had a big envelope in her hand. It was fragile, she said. It had a picture of a flower on it. "Who is the envelope for, mom?" Lily asked. "It's for grandma, sweetie. She is sick and can't come to the park with us. I made her a card with a flower on it. Do you want to see?" Mom said. "Yes, please!" Lily and Max said. Mom opened the envelope and showed them the card. It had a colorful flower and a message that said: "Get well soon, grandma. We love you and miss you." "It's very pretty, mom. Can we sign it too?" Max asked. "Of course, honey. Here, use this crayon." Mom said. She gave them the crayon and they wrote their names on the card. Lily wrote "Lily" and Max wrote "Max". "Good job, kids. Now let's put the card back in the envelope and seal it. We'll mail it on our way to the park." Mom said. She closed the envelope and put a sticker on it. It said: "Fragile. Handle with care." They walked to the mailbox and mom let Lily and Max take turns to put the envelope in the slot. They heard a thud as it landed inside. "Bye bye, envelope. Bye bye, grandma. We hope you feel better soon." Lily and Max said. "Me too, darlings. Me too." Mom said. She hugged them and smiled. "Now let's go to the park and have some fun."

Once there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and loved to help her mommy in the garden. Every day, mommy would give her special work to do like watering the plants and picking the vegetables. One day, Sarah was picking carrots. She pulled out a big one and smiled. She put it in her basket and looked around. She saw something else growing in the garden. It was a flower! Sarah was so thoughtful when she saw the flower. She plucked the beautiful petals and made them into a special meal. Together with her mommy, they ate the meal and enjoyed it. Sarah's mommy was very proud of her daughter. She hugged and kissed Sarah, thanking her for being so thoughtful. Sarah was happy to help her mommy and was excited to do more work in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little chicken named Chicky. Chicky lived on a farm with many other animals. One day, Chicky saw a big race happening. The animals were running very fast. Chicky wanted to join and be competitive too. Chicky asked a cow, "Can I race too?" The cow said, "Yes, but be careful, little chicken." So, Chicky joined the race. All the animals ran as fast as they could. Chicky was small but tried to run fast too. Suddenly, Chicky tripped and fell into a deep hole. Chicky cried for help. A kind dog heard Chicky's cries and came to rescue Chicky. The dog said, "Hold on, little chicken. I will help you." The dog pulled Chicky out of the hole. Chicky was so happy and said, "Thank you, kind dog." The moral of the story is to always help others in need, and it's okay to ask for help when you need it.
