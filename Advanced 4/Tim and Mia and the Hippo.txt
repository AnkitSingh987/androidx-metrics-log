Title: Tim and Mia and the Hippo

Tim and Mia went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, like lions, monkeys, and birds. Tim and Mia liked the animals, but they wanted to see the hippo. They asked Mom and Dad to take them to the hippo pool. When they got to the hippo pool, they saw a big hippo lying in the water. He did not move or make a sound. He looked very lazy. Tim and Mia were not happy. They wanted to see the hippo do something fun, like splash or swim or open his mouth. They waited and waited, but the hippo did not do anything. Tim and Mia got bored. They started to walk away. Then they heard a loud noise. They turned around and saw the hippo jump out of the water. He splashed and swam and opened his mouth. He looked very happy. Tim and Mia realized that the hippo was not lazy. He was just resting. They ran back to the hippo pool and clapped and cheered. The hippo saw them and smiled. He did more tricks for them. Tim and Mia were very happy. They thanked the hippo and said goodbye. They learned that sometimes animals need to rest, and sometimes they need to play.

Once upon a time, there lived a lonely fox. He was looking for his friends but couldn't find them anywhere. Then he heard a voice that said, "Follow me if you want to find them." The fox followed the voice, and soon he came upon a big maze. He was a bit scared but he kept going. As he went along, he kept hearing the voice. It was warning him to watch out for the creatures that lived inside the maze. The fox was careful but he soon felt lost. Then he heard the voice again. It said, "I can help you find your way out of the maze if you follow me". The fox followed the voice and soon he was out of the maze. He was so glad to be free, but then the voice said one more thing, "Be careful, the maze is full of dangers!" The fox thanked the voice and he never entered the maze again. Whenever he felt lonely, he thought of the warning and stayed away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had many toys, but his favorite was a clean, shiny figure of a superhero. He played with it every day after he finished his lunch. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. They played with the superhero figure and had lots of fun. After a while, Sue said, "Tim, I'm hungry. Can we have a snack?" Tim replied, "Sure, let's finish playing with the superhero and then we can eat." They put the superhero figure away and went to the kitchen. Tim's mom gave them some yummy fruit to eat. They ate all the fruit and then went back to playing with the superhero figure. They played until it was time for Sue to go home. Tim and Sue hugged goodbye and promised to play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to point at things. One day, she went to the store with her mom. In the store, she saw a big yellow lemon. She pointed at the lemon and said, "Mommy, I want that!" Her mom got the lemon for her. Lucy was very happy. She took the lemon home and showed it to her dad. Her dad said, "Lucy, you are so successful at finding good things to eat!" Lucy smiled and felt proud. Lucy and her mom made lemonade with the lemon. They shared it with the whole family. Everyone loved the lemonade. Lucy was happy that she could help make something yummy for everyone. The end.

One day, a little wolf was very bored. He did not want to play with his toys or run with his friends. He wanted to find something fun to do. The little wolf went for a walk in the woods. He looked around to find something to play with. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. But it was too tall for him. Then, the little wolf found a small ball. He was so happy! He played with the ball all day long. It was not boring anymore. The little wolf went home and told his friends about the fun he had. They all played with the ball together and had a great day.
