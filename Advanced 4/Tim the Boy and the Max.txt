Title: Tim the Boy and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy dog named Max. Max was Tim's best friend. They played together every day. Max was a fair toy dog because he shared his toys with Tim. One day, Max got hurt. He needed an operation to fix him. Tim was very sad. He did not want Max to hurt anymore. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. The doctor will make Max all better." Tim went to the doctor with Max. The doctor said, "I will fix Max. Remember to be brave." Tim was scared, but he knew he had to be brave for Max. The doctor did the operation and Max was all better. Tim and Max were very happy. They played together again. Tim said, "I'm glad you're all better, Max." Max said, "Thank you for being brave, Tim." They were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat lemons. One day, he saw a lemon tree with a big, yellow lemon on it. He wanted to pick the lemon, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam said, "I can help you, but we need to work together." They both tried to get the lemon, but they could not reach it. They started to cry. Tim's mom heard them and came outside. Mom said, "Why are you crying?" Tim and Sam told her about the lemon they could not reach. Mom said, "You can be successful if you work together and never give up." Tim and Sam tried again, and this time they got the lemon. They were so happy! The moral of the story is that when you work together and never give up, you can be successful.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She was very creative and always tried to find new ways to do things. One day she noticed some broccoli in her fridge. Lucy decided to use the broccoli to make something original. She took out a tray and put the broccoli into it. Then she added some butter and some cheese. She put the tray into the oven and waited for it to bake. When it was finished she pulled it out of the oven and placed it on a plate. It was a huge smiley face made entirely of broccoli! She couldn't wait to show her family her original creation. Her mum, dad and brother were so impressed when they saw it. They all enjoyed eating the broccoli smiley face and said it tasted really good. Lucy was very happy - she had used the broccoli to make something really creative and fun!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mother. They saw a big purple slide that Lily wanted to play on. Her mother said, "Be careful, Lily. Have fun on the slide!" Lily climbed up the stairs and sat at the top of the slide. She looked down and felt a little scared. But then, she saw her mother at the bottom, ready to catch her. Lily smiled and slid down the slide, laughing all the way. At the bottom, Lily looked up and saw something she didn't expect. A little purple bird was sitting on the slide! The bird looked at Lily and said, "Thank you for making me fly!" Lily and her mother laughed and played with the bird, making a new friend at the park.

One day, a reindeer was walking in the woods. He was not very smart and did many stupid things. He liked to jump and run, but he never looked where he was going. His friends always told him to stop and think before he did anything. One day, the reindeer saw a big red ball in the sky. He thought it was a toy and wanted to play with it. He ran and jumped to try to catch the ball. He did not stop to think that it might be something else. As he got closer, the ball got bigger and bigger. It was not a ball, but a big fire! The reindeer did not know what to do. He was scared and did not know how to stop the fire. In the end, the fire burned the whole woods and the reindeer lost his home.
