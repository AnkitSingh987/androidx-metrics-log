Title: Lily and Tom and the Cave

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big hill with many trees. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. They held hands and walked up the hill. They saw many birds, flowers and bugs. They heard a funny sound. It was like a roar, but softer. They followed the sound and found a cave. They peeked inside the cave. They saw something shiny and yellow. It was gold! There was a lot of gold in the cave. They also saw a big animal. It was a dragon! The dragon was sleeping on the gold. It was enormous and had scales, wings and a tail. Lily and Tom were scared. They wanted to run away. But the dragon opened one eye and saw them. It said, "Hello, little ones. Who are you?" Lily and Tom were surprised. The dragon could talk! They said, "We are Lily and Tom. We are playing in the park. We saw your cave and we were curious. We are sorry to bother you." The dragon smiled. It said, "Don't be sorry. I like visitors. I am lonely in this cave. My name is Ruby. Do you want to see my gold?" Lily and Tom nodded. They liked gold. They walked inside the cave with Ruby. Ruby showed them her gold and told them stories. They had fun and became friends. They smiled a lot.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat popcorn. One day, his mom bought a new popcorn for him to try. Tim was very happy and excited. Tim and his mom went to the park with the new popcorn. Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. Tim wanted to share his new popcorn with Sam. They both sat down and tried the new popcorn together. It was very yummy. As they ate the popcorn, more kids came to play. Tim and Sam shared the popcorn with everyone. All the kids were happy and had fun. They learned that sharing new things with friends can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Lily. Lily had a radish garden. She loved her garden very much. Every day, she would water the radishes and watch them grow. One day, Lily saw a little bunny in her garden. The bunny said, "Can I have a radish? I am very hungry." Lily was sad because she did not want to share her radishes. But she wanted to be helpful, so she said, "Okay, you can have one radish." The bunny was happy and started to eat the radish. But then, more bunnies came to the garden. They all wanted radishes too. The bunnies ate and ate until there were no more radishes left. Lily was very sad. She had no more radishes in her garden. The bunnies were full, but they did not say thank you. They just hopped away. Now, Lily had to start a new garden, and she learned to be more careful with her radishes.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy named Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear had a sad heart because he lost his hat. Amy wanted to help him find his hat so he would be happy again. Amy and Mr. Bear looked everywhere. They looked in the house, in the yard, and even in the park. They did not succeed in finding the hat. Mr. Bear was still sad, and so was Amy. One day, while they were playing, Amy saw something on the ground. It was a pretty flower! Amy put the flower on Mr. Bear's head. Mr. Bear was not sad anymore. His heart was happy. Amy and Mr. Bear learned that sometimes, when you don't succeed at first, something better might happen.

Once upon a time, on a Tuesday, there was a flexible worm named Wiggly. Wiggly loved to wiggle and play in the dirt. He had many friends, but his best friend was a big ant named Pinchy. Pinchy loved to pinch things with his strong ant legs. One sunny day, Wiggly and Pinchy were playing a game. They were trying to see who could wiggle or pinch the most dirt. Wiggly wiggled and wiggled, and Pinchy pinched and pinched. They were having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. Wiggly found a shiny rock in the dirt. He showed it to Pinchy, and they both looked at it. The shiny rock began to wiggle, just like Wiggly! The rock wiggled and wiggled until it turned into a beautiful butterfly. Wiggly and Pinchy were very surprised! The butterfly thanked them for helping her come out of her hard shell. Now, Wiggly, Pinchy, and the butterfly were all friends, and they played together every Tuesday.
