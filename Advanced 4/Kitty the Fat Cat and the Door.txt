Title: Kitty the Fat Cat and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to play with her toy mouse. One day, Kitty saw a door with a handle. She wanted to see what was behind the door. Kitty jumped up and pushed the handle down. The door opened and she saw a big yard. Kitty saw a dog named Buddy in the yard. Buddy was happy to see Kitty. He said, "Hi Kitty, do you want to play with me?" Kitty was scared of dogs, so she shouted, "No!" and ran back into the house. But Buddy was nice and just wanted to be friends. He followed Kitty into the house. When Kitty saw that Buddy was not mean, she stopped being scared. They played with the toy mouse together and became best friends.

Lily and Ben love to play in the garden with their poppy. Poppy is a big white dog who is very friendly and gentle. He likes to run and jump and chase balls and sticks. Sometimes he makes Lily and Ben laugh when he does funny things. One day, Lily and Ben find a butterfly in the garden. It is very pretty and colorful. It has wings that are blue and yellow and pink. It flies from flower to flower, looking for nectar. Lily and Ben want to see it closer, so they follow it quietly. "Look, Ben, the butterfly is on the rose!" Lily whispers. "Wow, it is so graceful!" Ben says. "It moves like a dancer." Poppy sees the butterfly too. He thinks it is a toy. He wants to play with it. He runs towards it, barking loudly. "Poppy, no!" Lily and Ben shout. "Leave the butterfly alone!" But Poppy does not listen. He jumps on the rose and tries to catch the butterfly. He breaks the rose and scares the butterfly. The butterfly flies away, very fast. Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They love the rose and the butterfly. They do not like what Poppy did. "Bad dog, Poppy!" Lily says. "You hurt the rose and the butterfly!" "Sorry, Poppy!" Ben says. "You did not mean to do it. You just wanted to have fun." Poppy looks at Lily and Ben. He sees their faces. He feels sorry too. He wags his tail and licks their hands. He wants to say he is sorry. Lily and Ben hug Poppy. They know he is a good dog. They forgive him. "Poppy, we love you!" Lily and Ben say. "But next time, please be careful with the flowers and the animals." Poppy nods and barks. He promises to be careful. He is happy to have Lily and Ben as his friends. They are happy to have Poppy as their friend. They play together in the garden, looking for more butterflies.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found an unusual spoon. It was big and shiny. Lucy liked the spoon very much. She took the spoon everywhere she went. She played with it in the sandbox and ate her lunch with it. Lucy's best friend, Tom, came over to play. They played with the spoon and had fun. They pretended the spoon was a magic wand. They waved it in the air and said, "Snap!" to make magic. The unusual spoon made them laugh and smile. They played with the spoon all day long. When it was time for Tom to go home, Lucy gave him a big hug. They both said, "Snap!" one more time with the spoon. They knew they would have more fun with the unusual spoon the next time they played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a white cat named Snowy. They loved to play and laugh together. Lily liked to watch funny videos with Snowy. They had so much fun. One day, Lily and Snowy found a video that made them laugh a lot. It was a video of a cat doing funny things. They wanted to share the video with their friends. Lily and Snowy showed the video to their friends and they all laughed together. They were very happy. The next day, Lily and Snowy saw a sad friend. They showed the funny video to their sad friend, and the friend started to laugh too. Lily and Snowy learned that sharing fun things with others can make everyone feel happy. They knew that being kind and making others laugh was a good thing to do.
