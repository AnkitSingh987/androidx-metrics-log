Title: Mary and the Puddle

One day, Mary was walking to school. She was so happy that she started skipping. Suddenly, she tripped on a rock and landed in a big puddle. She started to cry, but then she noticed something. In the puddle was a bright, shiny coin. She picked it up and said, "I'm so lucky!" When Mary arrived at school, she ran inside and went straight to the blackboard at the front of the room. She squeezed the coin in her hand and said, "I can buy an ice cream today!" Everyone laughed, but Mary was so excited to have her lucky coin. The teacher came in and said, "Mary, why are you squeezing a coin at the blackboard?" Mary smiled and said, "I found it outside and it's lucky, so I'm keeping it!" The teacher smiled and said, "It sure is lucky, just watch where you keep it!" Mary put the coin in her pocket and went to join her friends. They were already talking about the ice cream they were going to buy at the store later. They were so excited! Mary was lucky to have a special coin and her friends were excited to go shopping with her.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a curious raven. The raven loved to sing. Every day, he sang beautiful songs for everyone to hear. The other animals liked to listen to the raven's songs. One day, a small mouse came to the tree. The mouse said, "Mr. Raven, I love your songs. Can you teach me how to sing like you?" The raven was happy to help. He said, "Of course, little mouse! Let's sing together." So, the raven and the mouse sang together every day. They had a lot of fun. The other animals came to listen and sing with them too. The big tree became a happy place where everyone could sing and have fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there were two friends, Tom and Tim. They were very competitive. They liked to play games and see who was the best. One day, they decided to have a race to see who could run the fastest. As they started running, Tom said, "I bet I can win this race!" Tim replied, "No, I will win!" They ran and ran, and soon, they were both very tired. Tim saw a big rock and decided to sit down for a minute. As he sat down, he accidentally dropped his thumb into a puddle of mud. Tom saw Tim's muddy thumb and started to laugh. He said, "Look at your thumb! It's so dirty!" Tim felt sad and embarrassed. But then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's stop racing and help each other clean up." So, they both helped each other clean their hands and clothes. From that day on, Tom and Tim learned that being competitive is not always good. They learned that it is better to help each other and be kind. And they lived happily ever after as best friends.

One day, a little bird named Timmy loved to sing. He sang all day long. Timmy had a friend named Billy. Billy was a silly automobile. They liked to play together. One morning, Timmy saw Billy looking sad. "Why are you sad, Billy?" asked Timmy. Billy said, "I want to sing like you, but I can't. I can only make loud noises." Timmy thought for a moment and had an idea. Timmy told Billy, "I have a plan. When I sing, you can make your loud noise. We will sing together!" So, they practiced all day. Timmy sang a song and Billy made his loud noise. At the end of the day, they were happy. They loved singing together, and everyone liked their new song.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to look at the stars in the night sky. She would lay in her bed at night, open the curtains and take in the beauty of the twinkling stars. Tonight, the little girl noticed that the stars were so far away, like they were hidden in the darkness. No matter how hard she tried, she couldn't quite see them. She was sad and desperate to find the stars. Just then, the girl heard a noise outside. She opened the window and saw a big, white snowflake. The snowflake was beautiful and sparkled in the moonlight. The little girl thought, "It looks so cold out there." She grabbed her hat, coat and mittens and stepped out into the night. When she looked up at the sky, she was surprised to see hundreds of stars! But then, the girl's happy moment was gone. The cold air was too much for her and the snowflakes were too hard to touch. It was a terrible feeling and so she ran back inside quickly. As she tucked into her warm bed again, she looked up at the stars and smiled. She knew that being warm was much better than being cold.
