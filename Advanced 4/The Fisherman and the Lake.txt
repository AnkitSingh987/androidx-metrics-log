Title: The Fisherman and the Lake

One day, a fisherman went to the lake. He was miserable because he never caught any fish. He sat down with his fishing pole and waited. The sun was hot and the fisherman felt sad. Then, a big fish swam by. The fisherman saw it and tried to catch it. But the fish was too fast! The fisherman felt even more sad. He wanted to kick something because he was angry. Suddenly, the big fish jumped out of the water and kicked the fisherman's fishing pole. The fisherman was surprised! The big fish said, "I will help you catch fish if you promise to be happy." The fisherman agreed, and they became friends. Now, the fisherman was not miserable anymore, and he caught many fish with the help of his new friend, the big fish.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl called Lily. One day, Lily was walking in the garden. She stumbled across a modest fairy. The fairy was so small, she had to put her nose near the ground, to be able to see her! Lily was amazed, she asked the fairy, "What do you do?" The fairy smiled and told Lily, "I grant wishes, of course!" Lily was so excited, she clapped her hands and said, "Oh, I know exactly what I want! I want a dolly with long golden hair". The fairy smiled, a twinkle in her eye, and told Lily, "Your wish is my command!" Lily thanked the fairy and soon afterwards, the fairy disappeared in a sparkling mist. The next day when Lily visited the garden, she found the most beautiful dolly, with long golden hair, waiting for her. She was so happy and wanted to give the fairy a big thank you, but she was nowhere to be found. The moral of the story is: Always be modest, no matter what. As you never know if a fairy might be close by, listening and ready to grant your wishes!

Anna and Ben are friends. They go to the same school. They like to play and learn together. One day, they have a lesson about eyes. The teacher shows them a picture of an eye. She says, "This is an eye. It helps you see. The black part in the middle is called the pupil. The white part around it is called the white. The pupil can get bigger or smaller depending on how much light there is." Anna and Ben are curious. They want to see their own pupils. They look at each other's eyes. They see the black and the white. They see the pupil change size when they move closer or farther from the window. They think it is fun and amazing. They decide to play a game. They take turns poking each other's white gently with their fingers. They say, "Poke, poke, poke!" They giggle and laugh. They see how the pupil reacts to the poke. They are careful not to hurt each other. But then, the teacher sees them. She is not happy. She says, "Stop that! You should not poke your eyes or your friend's eyes. It is not safe. It can hurt your eyes. You need your eyes to see. You should be gentle and respectful with your eyes and your friend's eyes." Anna and Ben feel sorry. They did not mean to be bad. They say, "We are sorry, teacher. We were just playing. We wanted to see our pupils. We did not know it was not safe. We will not do it again." The teacher smiles. She says, "I am glad you are sorry. I know you were curious. But you have to be careful with your eyes. They are very important and delicate. There are other ways to learn about your pupils. You can use a mirror, or a flashlight, or a magnifying glass. But you should never poke your eyes or your friend's eyes. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, teacher. We understand. Thank you for teaching us. We will be careful with our eyes and our friend's eyes. We will use other things to learn about our pupils." The teacher hugs them. She says, "You are welcome. I am proud of you for being honest and polite. You are good friends. Now, let's go back to the lesson. I have more things to show you about eyes."
