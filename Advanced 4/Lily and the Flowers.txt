Title: Lily and the Flowers

Lily liked to draw pictures with her crayons. She drew flowers, animals, and people. She drew her family and her friends. She was very proud of her pictures. One day, her parent said, "Lily, you have so many pictures. Why don't you display them on the wall? Then everyone can see them and admire them." Lily thought that was a good idea. She took some tape and stuck her pictures on the wall. She made a big display of her art. Her parent smiled and said, "Lily, you are very obedient. You listened to me and did what I asked. You are a good girl. I love your pictures. They are very beautiful and colorful. You are very talented." Lily felt happy and hugged her parent. She said, "Thank you, parent. I love you too. I like to draw pictures for you and for me. I like to display them on the wall. They make me happy." Her parent kissed her and said, "You make me happy too, Lily. You are a wonderful daughter. Let's go and have some cookies and milk. You deserve a treat." Lily nodded and followed her parent. She was glad that her parent liked her display. She was a happy and obedient girl.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a swan. The swan had a rough day because it lost its way home. It felt sad and wanted to solve the problem. The swan met a kind frog who wanted to help. They swam around the pond, looking for the swan's home. They asked other animals if they knew where the swan lived. After a while, they found the swan's home. The swan was very happy and thanked the frog. They became good friends. The moral of the story is to help others when they need it, and you will make friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a high tree with his family. He loved to fly around and play with his friends all day. One day, Tim saw a shiny handle on the ground. He wanted to take it to his nest, but it was too heavy. Tim tried and tried, but he could not pick it up. He asked his friends for help, but they could not lift it either. Then, Tim had an idea. He asked all his friends to work together. They all managed to carry the handle up to the high tree. Tim was so happy, and his friends were proud of what they did. They learned that when they work together, they can do anything.

There was a human who really liked pink. Everywhere they went, they looked for something pink. One day, the human saw a beautiful, pink flower, and decided to pick it for themself. They started to smell the flower, when suddenly, fire came from the sky and burned it. The human was very angry and said loudly, "Why did you burn my beautiful flower?". But there was no answer. The fire continued to burn everything in sight, until the human had nothing left. At the end, all that was left of the human was a pile of ashes. The fire had destroyed everything – the pink flower, the human, and even the ground below.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a rare, shiny rock. She picked it up with her finger and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a rare rock! Can you imagine what we can do with it?" Sue said. Tom looked at the rock and said, "Yes, I can imagine! Let's play a game. We can pretend it's magic!" Sue and Tom played with the rock all day. They imagined it could do many fun things. They made it fly and change colors. They even made it talk! But then, something unexpected happened. The rock really started to talk! It said, "Thank you for finding me! I am a magic rock, and now I can help you!" Sue and Tom were very happy. They had lots of fun with their new magic rock friend. They knew it was a very special day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to read books. One day, Amy found a big, broken book in her room. The book had a pretty cover with a picture of a castle on it. She was so excited to read it. Amy took the broken book to her mom. Her mom said, "This book is very old. Be careful when you read it." Amy was very happy and went to her room. She sat on her bed and opened the book. She started to read the story about a princess and a dragon. As Amy read, she saw that the story was not finished. The last page was missing. She was sad and went to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "I have a surprise for you." She took Amy to the other side of the room. There, Amy found the last page of the story. She was very happy and read the ending. The princess was saved, and they all lived happily ever after.
