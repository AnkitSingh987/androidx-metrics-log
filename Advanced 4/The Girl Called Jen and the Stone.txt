Title: The Girl Called Jen and the Stone

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Jen. She was three years old and had bright eyes and a big smile. Jen was a brave and curious girl, always exploring her world. One day when Jen was playing in her garden, she found a rare object. She was so surprised and excited but she was also scared because she had never seen anything like it before. "What is it?" asked Jen. "It's a special stone. It's very rare," said her mom. Jen was nervous but she was determined to find out what the object was. She wanted to know more, but she was scared to touch it. Her mom reassured her and said, "It's okay. I will help you and encourage you to explore." Jen felt brave and started to pick up the object. She looked closer and it was a crystal stone. Jen was amazed and she kept that stone with her always. Her mom was proud of Jen for being so brave and exploring the world around her. From that day on Jen always kept on exploring, encouraged by her mom.

One day, a little pupil named Tim went to school. He had a big blue bag with him. In the bag, there were many things like books, pencils, and toys. Tim's teacher, Miss Rose, asked him to organize his bag. "Tim, please organize your blue bag," said Miss Rose. Tim started to take out his things and put them in the right places. While he was organizing, he found a small blue box in his bag. He did not know where it came from. Tim opened the blue box and found a magic blue stone inside. He showed it to Miss Rose and his friends. They all looked at the stone and wondered what it could do. Suddenly, the stone made a loud sound and turned everything in the room blue! The kids were amazed and started to laugh. Miss Rose said, "Wow, Tim, that was unexpected! Let's put the magic stone back in the box and keep it safe." Tim put the stone back in the box and continued to organize his bag. From that day on, Tim and his friends always remembered the day when everything turned blue.

One day, a polite cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big scale on the ground. Tom thought it was a fun game. He jumped on the scale and saw the numbers go up. He liked it very much. Tom saw a long rope next to the scale. He wanted to play with the rope too. He used his paw to pull the rope. As he pulled, the scale started to move! Tom was surprised. The scale was not just a scale. It was a door to a secret room! Tom went inside and found many toys and treats. He played all day and had so much fun. The polite cat found a new place to play and was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big and strong volcano. The volcano was very reliable. It never hurt anyone. Animals and people lived near the volcano, and they were all friends. One day, a little girl named Lily was playing near the volcano. She saw a big stone and wanted to strike it. So, she picked up a small stick and hit the stone. When she did, the volcano spoke to her. "Hello, Lily," said the volcano. "Why did you strike the stone?" Lily was surprised but not scared. She knew the volcano was her friend. "I wanted to see what would happen," she answered. The volcano laughed and said, "Well, now you know. I can talk to you when you strike the stone." From that day on, Lily and the volcano became the best of friends. They talked every day, and the volcano always kept Lily safe. The people and animals knew they could trust the reliable volcano, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful king. He had a big, pretty bow. He used the bow to keep his land safe. The king was very happy. One day, a big party came to the king's land. The king put on his best clothes and went to the party. He saw a pretty girl and wanted to say hello. The king decided to bow to the girl to show her he was nice. But when the king bowed, his powerful bow fell out of his hand. It hit the ground and broke into pieces. The king was very sad. He could not keep his land safe anymore. The people were not happy, and the king's land became not safe.
