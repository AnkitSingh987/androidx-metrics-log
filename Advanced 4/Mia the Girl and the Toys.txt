Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Mia. She was a very creative girl who loved to play with her toys and make up new games. One day, Mia's mom told her that they were going to have a new baby in the family. Mia was very excited and couldn't wait to meet her new baby brother or sister. As the day came closer, Mia's mom asked her to help get the baby's room ready. Mia was happy to help and used her creative ideas to make the room look nice. She painted pretty pictures on the wall and put up colorful curtains. When they were done, Mia's mom told her that they were going to have a "stay" in the room to make sure everything was perfect for the baby. On the day the baby was coming home, Mia was so excited that she couldn't help but peek into the room to see if everything was ready. Her mom told her not to peek, but Mia couldn't help herself. When she peeked, she accidentally knocked over a vase and it broke. Mia felt very sad and thought she ruined the room. Her mom came in and saw the broken vase. Instead of being mad, she hugged Mia and told her that it was okay. They cleaned up the mess together and Mia learned that it was important to listen to her mom and not peek when she was told not to. The baby came home and they all lived happily ever after, with Mia being a great big sister who always tried to do the right thing.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many toys. He liked to wear black clothes, because it made him feel important. Tom had many friends, but he did not share his toys with them. He thought that his toys were special and only for him. One day, Tom's friends decided to celebrate a birthday party for their friend, Lily. Everyone was excited and happy. They all brought gifts and shared their toys with each other. But Tom did not want to share his toys. He just sat alone and watched the others play and have fun. Tom felt sad and lonely. He realized that sharing with friends is more important than having many toys. So, Tom decided to share his toys and join the celebration. Everyone was happy to play with Tom and his toys. From that day on, Tom learned that sharing is what makes you truly wealthy.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a comet in the sky. It was big and bright. Tim felt peaceful as he watched it. He wanted to show the comet to his friend, Sue. "Look, Sue!" Tim said. "I see a comet up there!" Sue looked up and saw the comet too. "Wow!" she said. "It is so pretty!" As they watched the comet, something unexpected happened. The comet changed shape! Now, it looked like a big smiley face. Tim and Sue were amazed. They did not recognize the comet anymore. The smiley face comet made them laugh and feel happy. They knew it was a special day.

One day, a girl named Anna wanted to play. She saw her mom doing yoga in the living room. Anna thought she could do yoga too. She put on her pants and tried to zip them up. But the zip was broken. Anna felt sad. Anna went to her mom and said, "Mom, my zip is broken. I can't do yoga." Her mom looked at Anna and smiled. "You don't need to zip your pants to do yoga, Anna. Let's try together." Anna and her mom did yoga. They had fun. Suddenly, their cat jumped on the yoga mat. They laughed. The cat had fixed Anna's broken zip. Anna was happy. She learned that sometimes, things can still work even if they are broken.

Once there was a small boy named Mike. He had a jar of cookies that he kept safe in his room. Mike loved the jars of cookies and so he kept them in his room at all times. One day his mom asked him to bring the jar of cookies down to the living room. She wanted to share them with the family. Mike didn't want to give up his jar of cookies. 'Please can I keep the jar of cookies in my room? It is safe here,' Mike said. His mom replied, 'No, Mike. Everyone needs to share the cookies. Please bring the jar down to the living room now.' Mike was not happy but he agreed. He picked up the jar of cookies, holding it close to him, and walked down to the living room. When he arrived, everyone was excited to see the cookies. Together they all shared the cookies, with Mike feeling much better knowing that his jar was safe.
