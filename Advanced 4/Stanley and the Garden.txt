Title: Stanley and the Garden

Once there was a little boy. His name was Stanley and he was three years old. Stanley loved to go outside and play. One day he wanted to take his toy motor out for a ride. He gathered up his toy motor and went to the garden. When Stanley put his motor down in the garden, he saw that it was broken! He tried to work on it but he didn’t know how. He was so sad and he felt so hungry because he had spent so muct time trying to fix the motor. He looked around but there was nobody to help him. Just then he heard a voice. “Do you need help?”, it said. Stanley had never seen this person before but the voice was so kind. He looked up and saw a friendly lady. The lady showed Stanley how to fix his motor and he was so happy! He thanked the lady for her help and she smiled at him. They said goodbye and Stanley quickly rode his motor around the garden. He was so glad his motor was working again. He was happy and relieved.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. The bear was very sleepy. He walked in the woods and saw a small twig. The bear picked up the twig with his paw. The bear saw a yummy red apple on a tree. He used the twig to get the apple. The apple fell down and the bear ate it. The bear was very happy. The bear walked more and started to yawn. He was very sleepy again. The bear found a soft spot under a tree. He lay down and went to sleep. The bear had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a silly cat named Tom. Tom liked to help his friends. One day, he saw a little bird named Timmy. Timmy was sad because he could not find food for his mouth. Tom said, "I will help you find food, Timmy." So, they went to look for food together. They saw a big tree with red apples. Tom climbed the tree and picked some apples for Timmy. Timmy was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" But when Timmy tried to eat the apples, they were too big for his mouth. Tom felt sad that he could not help his friend. They looked for more food but could not find any. In the end, Timmy was still hungry and Tom felt very bad.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to listen to her mom read poems. Every day, after playing outside, Lily would sit down and ask her mom to read her a poem. One sunny day, Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim also liked poems. Lily said, "Let's listen to a poem together!" Tim agreed, and they both went to Lily's house. Lily's mom read them a fun poem about a cat and a dog. They laughed and clapped their hands. From that day on, Lily and Tim would always listen to poems together, and their friendship grew stronger.

Daddy was ready for a special game. He took out the toys from the box and scattered them around the room. Zoe, the 3 year old, was so excited that she jumped and laughed out loud. Daddy began to chase her and she ran away, giggling. Zoe was feeling silly and forgot where she had scattered the toys. Daddy showed her where the toys had been and showed her how to put them back in the box. When the box was closed, Daddy asked Zoe if she was ready for another game. She nodded, and then excitedly ran away to find a new hiding place.

Once upon a time, there were two sisters. The older sister was very clever. She noticed a special shelf in their bedroom and pulled it out by one end. The younger sister looked to see what the older one had done. “What are you doing?” she asked. “I think I know what’s behind this shelf,” explained the older sister. “My teacher said it was famous.” The younger sister’s eyes grew wider as the older sister carefully lifted the shelf. Behind it, was a big box that sparkled and shone. “Wow!” exclaimed the younger sister, excitedly. “Let’s see what’s inside!” said the older sister. Inside the box was a beautiful, bright butterfly. It sparkled in the sunlight. The two sisters clapped their hands in delight because they both knew that the butterfly was famous!
