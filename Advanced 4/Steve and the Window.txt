Title: Steve and the Window

Steve looked out of his window. He could see the airport. He was so excited to be flying. Mum said, “We need to check your bag. We must examine it before we go to the airport.” Steve said, “OK! I will get it ready.” He carefully looked through his bag, checking for anything he might need on the plane. After everything was ready, they left for the airport. When Steve arrived, he saw that the sky was wet and cloudy. He shivered, as the rain was cold. Mum said, “Remember that we have to examine our bags before we can go to the plane.” Steve smiled. He remembered that he had already checked his bags, and could go quickly. He had just managed to reach the plane when they called his name, and said they had to examine his bag one last time. As Steve took the bag out, he could feel the excitement in the air. He knew that after this check, he would soon be taking off and Adventures were his certain destination.

Once upon a time, there was a weak little bunny named Bob. Bob was not like the other bunnies. He was not strong and fast. But Bob was very smart. He liked to play with a switch. The switch could turn on a display with many colors. Bob loved to look at the colors. One day, Bob found a big switch in the woods. He was very excited. He pushed the switch and a big display appeared. The display showed many colors and shapes. Bob was so happy. He wanted to show his friends. Bob called all his friends to see the display. They all came and saw the colors and shapes. They were very happy too. They played with the switch and the display all day. They learned that even a weak bunny like Bob could do something amazing.

Once there was a cat and a dog. They were friends. One day, they found a box of powder. The cat said, "Let's release the powder and see what happens!" The dog said, "No, that might be dangerous. We should ask for help." They went to their friend, the wise owl. The owl told them, "That powder is not for play. It can hurt you. It is important to ask for help when you don't know something." The cat and the dog thanked the owl. They learned that it is good to ask for help when you don't know about something. They kept the powder safe and played together in the sun.

Once upon a time, in a bright, sunny land, there was a big tree with many green palms. A little boy named Tim wanted to decorate the tree. He had colorful ribbons and shiny stars to make the tree look pretty. One day, while Tim was decorating the tree, a naughty monkey came. The monkey wanted to take the ribbons and stars for himself. Tim was sad and didn't want the monkey to take his things. Tim thought of a plan. He gave the monkey a big, yummy banana. The monkey was happy with the banana and left the ribbons and stars alone. Tim finished decorating the palm tree, and it looked beautiful. Everyone in the land came to see the bright, pretty tree and they all were very happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw her friends playing and wanted to join them. As she walked, she saw a witch sitting on a bench. The witch had a big bag of candy. Sue liked candy very much. The witch saw Sue and said, "Hi, little girl. Do you want some candy? I have a lot, but it is all spoiled. If you help me fix it, you can have some." Sue thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will help you." Sue and the witch tried to fix the spoiled candy. They mixed it, they shook it, and they even jumped on it. But nothing worked. Then, the witch had an idea. She said, "Let's use my magic to fix the candy!" Sue was scared, but she wanted the candy. The witch waved her magic stick and said some magic words. Suddenly, the spoiled candy turned into a big, scary monster! The monster chased Sue, the witch, and all of Sue's friends. They all ran away and never came back to the park again.
