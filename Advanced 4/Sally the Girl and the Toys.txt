Title: Sally the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Sally. She loved to play in the sand at the park. One day, Sally saw a small boy named Timmy who was sad because he didn't have any toys to play with. Sally wanted to help Timmy, so she decided to provide him with some of her sand toys. She gave him a bucket and a shovel to play with. Timmy was very happy and they played together, making sand castles and having fun. At the end of the day, Sally felt proud of herself for helping Timmy. They both learned that sharing and being kind to others can make everyone happy. And that was the moral of the story: always help others and share what you have, because it can make both you and them feel good.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little house. In the house, there lived a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Anna. They all loved each other very much. One day, Anna wanted to go up the stair to her room. But she saw a big box on the stair. She called her mommy and said, "Mommy, can you help me? There is a big box on the stair!" Mommy came and saw the box. She smiled and said, "Oh Anna, this is a surprise for you!" Anna was so happy. She opened the box and found a pretty doll inside. She loved the doll and played with it all day. But she didn't want to spoil it, so she took good care of it. Anna and her charming doll were the best of friends, and they lived happily in the little house with the stair.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She had a flashlight that she loved very much. One day, she wanted to test her flashlight to see if it still worked. Sara went to her room and turned off the light. It was very dark, and she felt a little frightened. But she knew her flashlight would help her see in the dark. She turned on her flashlight, and it made a bright circle of light on the wall. The frightened feeling went away when Sara saw the light. She played with the flashlight, making shadows on the walls. She was happy that her flashlight worked, and she was not scared anymore. Sara knew that her flashlight would always help her when it was dark.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to study her books. She would look at them every day. One day, she felt very tired. She wanted to take a nap. Sue went to her bed and lay down. In her room, there was a drawer. Sue liked to put her toys and books in the drawer. While Sue was sleeping, her toys came out of the drawer. They wanted to study too! So, they looked at Sue's books. When Sue woke up, she saw her toys with her books. She was very happy. Sue learned that it is good to share. Now, Sue and her toys study together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a happy town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a pretty box with a big jewel inside. Lily loved her jewel very much. She would play with it and show it to her friends. They all liked the shiny jewel too. One day, Lily could not find her jewel. She looked everywhere but it was not there. Lily was sad and angry. She asked her friends if they saw her jewel. They all said no. Lily did not know what to do. She missed her jewel so much. Then, a kind boy named Tom found a shiny jewel on the ground. He knew it was Lily's jewel. He gave it back to her. Lily was so happy to have her jewel again. She thanked Tom for his help. From that day on, Lily and Tom were best friends. They played and shared their toys, and they both took care of the shiny jewel together.

Once upon a time, there was a normal yogurt. Every day the yogurt would fold itself up and into a nice neat package, ready for eating. But one day, the yogurt decided not to fold. It stayed right there in its container, not wanting to move. The yogurt's family was very upset and tried to help it. They tried to fold it, but it wouldn't move. No matter how much they tried, the yogurt stayed put. Everyone was getting very frustrated and decided to give up. On the last day, the yogurt still hadn't folded and the family had to put it away. Sadly, they had to throw the yogurt away. The poor yogurt, which had just wanted to remain normal, was gone forever.
