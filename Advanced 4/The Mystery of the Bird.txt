Title: The Mystery of the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a small forest. The tree had a secret: inside it, there was a pretty jewel. The animals in the forest did not believe that the jewel was real. They thought it was just a story. One day, a little, brave bird decided to look for the jewel. The bird flew to the thick tree and looked inside. To its surprise, the bird found the pretty jewel! The bird was very happy and wanted to show the jewel to its friends. The bird took the jewel to the other animals. They were all amazed and could not believe their eyes. The little bird taught them that sometimes, even if you do not believe something is real, it can be true. The animals learned to trust and believe in each other, and they all lived happily together in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim loved going to school and playing with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big ball in the playground. He wanted to play with it, but it was very high up in a tree. Tim tried to reach the ball, but he was too small. He knew he needed help. So, he asked his friend, Jane. Jane was very tall and could reach high things. Together, they tried to get the ball down from the tree. They pushed and pulled, but the ball was stuck. Tim had an idea. He told Jane to clap her hands very loud. Jane clapped her hands as loud as she could, and the ball fell down from the tree. Tim and Jane were so happy. They played with the ball all day and had lots of fun.

There was a boy named Jack who was very careful. One day, he was playing outside and he saw a beautiful screen. He took a deep breath and said, "That must be mine". He was so excited he started to run towards it. Suddenly, Jack heard a voice say, "Be careful, Jack!" It was his dad. "That screen doesn't belong to you," his dad said. Jack stopped. He felt sad and said, "But it looks so nice, I would take great care of it". His dad smiled and said, "I know you would, Jack. But it doesn't belong to you, so it's not yours". Jack nodded and said, "Okay." He understood. He walked away from the screen and started playing with his friends again. He knew that things can be beautiful, but not everything belongs to him.

Once upon a time, there was a musician who liked to balance. He was very obedient and always did what he was told. One day, he went to the park to play music and balance on a big rock. Kids from all around came to see him and listen to his songs. The musician was very happy because the kids loved his music. He would play his guitar and balance on the rock, while the kids clapped and danced. But one day, a mean man came to the park and told the musician to stop playing his music. The obedient musician stopped playing his music and stopped balancing on the rock. The kids were sad and missed the fun they had with the musician. The park became quiet and not as happy as before. The musician went away, and the kids never saw him again.

Once upon a time there was a clever girl. She loved to go on adventures. One day, the girl wanted to go on the biggest adventure of all and so she went to a big hotel. The girl was so excited to see the hotel. But it seemed so big and scary. She was a little bit scared. So she hugged her teddy bear and took a deep breath. As she entered the hotel, she was surprised at how friendly it was. Everyone was smiling and saying hello. The girl felt safe and happy. The girl explored the hotel. She found a pool and a restaurant. She saw lots of different rooms and even a playground. She was having so much fun! When it was time to leave, the girl said goodbye to the hotel and hugged it. She was sad to go, but excited for her next adventure.
