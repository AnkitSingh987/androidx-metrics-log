Title: The Rock and the Guard

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. A guard stood next to the rock all day and all night. The guard was very strong and brave. People in the town thought the rock was mysterious. They did not know why the guard was always there. One day, a little girl went to the rock. She was not scared of the guard. She asked, "Why are you always here?" The guard looked at her and smiled. He said, "I am here to keep the rock safe." The little girl was confused. She did not know why the rock needed to be safe. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock started to move! The guard and the little girl watched as the rock changed. It was not a rock at all! It was a big egg. A baby dragon came out of the egg. The guard was not there to keep the rock safe, but to keep the baby dragon safe. The little girl and the guard became friends with the dragon, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot was famous because he could fit into tiny spaces. One day, Spot went to the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. At the park, they saw a small hole under a tree. Lily said, "Spot, do you think you can fit in there?" Spot barked happily and squeezed into the hole. Inside, he found a pretty box with a memory inside. Spot brought the box out to Lily. She opened it and saw a picture of her and Spot when they were very little. "This is a special memory of us!" she said. They both smiled and went home, happy to have found a treasure that day.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big volcano. The volcano was not like other volcanos. It was very colorful. It had red, blue, and green all over it. People from all over came to see the pretty volcano. One day, a little boy named Tim went to see the volcano with his family. They all stood and looked at the colorful volcano. Tim was very happy. He wanted to move closer to the volcano to see the colors better. As Tim moved closer, he saw that the colors were from pretty rocks. He picked up a small red rock and showed it to his family. They all smiled and picked up their favorite color rocks too. The colorful volcano made everyone happy. And they all went home with a special rock to remember the fun day.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to play with his ball and run in the yard. He was a happy dog, but Max was deaf. He could not hear the sounds around him. But that did not stop Max from having fun. One day, Max saw a newspaper on the floor. He picked it up with his mouth and set it by his food bowl. Max thought the newspaper was a toy. He wagged his tail and played with the newspaper all day long. Max's family saw him playing with the newspaper. They laughed and smiled. They knew Max was a special dog. Even though Max was deaf, he was always happy and full of love. And that is what made Max the best dog ever.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot's ball rolled into a big bin. Spot was sad because he could not get his ball. A big cat named Fluffy saw Spot and wanted to help. Fluffy tried to get the ball, but the bin was too tall. They thought and thought. Then, they saw a long stick. Fluffy and Spot used the stick to get the ball out of the bin. When the ball was out, Spot and Fluffy were very happy. They played together all day. Spot learned that it is good to have friends to help when you have a problem. And Fluffy learned that helping others can make you happy too.
