Title: Sarah and the Gift

Sarah had a new doll. It was a very thoughtful gift from grandma. Sarah was so excited to play with her new toy, that she wanted to show it to everyone. She asked her mom for help, “Mommy, can I invite some people to play with my doll?” Her mom smiled and said, “Yes, Sarah. Who do you want to invite over?” Sarah thought for a moment and then said, “I want to invite my best friend and her little brother.” Mommy agreed and said, “I think that is a wonderful idea, Sarah. Why don’t you go and invite them over now?” So Sarah ran to her best friend’s house and invited them to come and play with her new doll. The two little ones were very excited and said yes. They all went back to Sarah’s house and played for hours with the new doll. They had so much fun that day! Sarah was so thankful for the thoughtful gift and for the chance to play with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to stay in his little house. One day, a big package came to Tom's house. Tom was very happy to see the package. Tom opened the package with his paws. Inside the package, there was a big, soft bed for Tom. Tom jumped on the bed and it was very comfy. Tom liked his new bed so much that he decided to stay on it all day. Tom's friends, a dog and a bird, came to visit him. They saw the package and the bed. They thought Tom was very smart for getting such a nice bed. They all played together and had a fun day.

One day, a little ball named Bouncy wanted to bounce high. Bouncy liked to watch a movie about balls that could bounce very high. Bouncy wanted to be like them, so he tried to bounce high every day. One day, Bouncy met a big ball named High Ball. High Ball could bounce very high. High Ball told Bouncy that he could teach him how to bounce high if they watched the movie together. Bouncy was very happy and excited to learn from High Ball. They watched the movie and High Ball showed Bouncy how to bounce high. Bouncy tried and tried, and finally, he could bounce high too! Bouncy was so happy that he could now bounce high like the balls in the movie. Bouncy and High Ball became good friends and bounced high together every day.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play in the soil. She had a regular day at school and wanted to celebrate. She ran to her friend Tom and said, "Let's celebrate and play in the soil!" Tom was happy and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They played in the soil, making mud pies and having fun. They laughed and talked as they played. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew down and landed on the soil. Sue and Tom were surprised. They had never seen a bird like this before. The bird looked at them and said, "Thank you for playing in the soil. You helped me find my favorite food, worms!" Sue and Tom were amazed that the bird could talk. They asked the bird if it wanted to celebrate with them. The bird said yes, and they all played in the soil together. It was the most fun they ever had.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a sunflower named Sunny. Sunny was very jealous of the other flowers because they were all so pretty. One day, Sunny said to the rose, "I wish I was as pretty as you." The rose replied, "But Sunny, you are tall and can rise high to see the sun. We all have something special." Just then, something unexpected happened. A little girl came into the garden and picked the rose. The other flowers were sad, but Sunny was still standing tall. Sunny realized that it was good to be different. From that day on, Sunny was happy to rise high and watch over the garden, knowing that being unique was a good thing.
