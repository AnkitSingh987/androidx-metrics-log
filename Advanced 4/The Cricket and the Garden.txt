Title: The Cricket and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a green garden, there was a young cricket. The cricket loved to jump and sing all day long. He had many friends in the garden, like the butterfly, the bee, and the ant. One day, the cricket met a big spider. The spider said, "I will teach you a new song if you come to my web." The cricket was very excited, but he did not trust the spider. He knew that spiders sometimes hurt little bugs. The cricket went to his friends and told them about the spider. The butterfly, the bee, and the ant all said, "Do not trust the spider. Stay with your friends who love you." The cricket listened to his friends and stayed away from the spider. In the end, the young cricket was safe and happy with his friends. He learned that it is important to trust your friends and be careful with strangers. And they all lived happily in the green garden, jumping and singing together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw his mom near the stove. The stove was cold. She was making a yummy snack. Tim wanted to help his mom. "Can I help?" asked Tim. His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can wipe the table." Tim took a cloth and wiped the table. He wiped and wiped until it was clean. Tim's mom finished making the snack. She gave Tim a big hug and said, "Thank you for helping me." They sat at the clean table and ate their yummy snack together. Tim felt happy because he helped his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. He liked to listen to music too. One day, Max heard music coming from the park. He wanted to find out who was playing the music, so he ran to the park. In the park, Max saw a big elephant named Ella. Ella was playing the music on a big drum. Max was so happy to find the music. He said, "Ella, I like your music. It makes me want to dance!" Ella smiled and said, "Thank you, Max. I love to play music for everyone." Max and Ella became good friends. They played together every day. One day, Max wanted to know how much Ella weighed. He asked, "Ella, how much do you weigh?" Ella laughed and said, "I weigh a lot, Max. I am a very big elephant." Max smiled and said, "That's okay, Ella. I still love to play with you and listen to your music." And they played together happily ever after.

Once there was a cheerful little flea. The flea saw a big tree on the edge of a grassy meadow. The flea thought it was so big and strong, so it decided to lean against the tree. The flea asked the tree, "Will you be my friend?" The tree answered, "Yes, I would love that.” The flea and the tree became good friends. Every day the little flea would lean against the tree and it was so happy. One day, the flea got caught in a gust of wind and blew away. The tree could not lean against the flea to keep it safe. The flea was gone, but the tree remembered their friendship. The tree was a bit sad, but it wasn't too long before it was cheerful again. Because it knew that even if the flea was far away, they'd still be friends always.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tim. He loved to fly high in the sky. Tim was very good at balance. He could balance his plane even in strong wind. He was an independent pilot, which means he could fly all by himself. One sunny day, Tim was flying his plane up in the sky. He saw a big bird named Bob. Bob said, "Hi, Tim! Can you teach me how to balance like you?" Tim said, "Of course, Bob! Let's be friends and I will help you." They started to practice balance together. But then, something unexpected happened. Bob was not a bird, but a magic dragon! Bob said, "Surprise, Tim! I am a dragon who can do magic. I will give you a special gift for being my friend and teaching me how to balance." Tim was very happy to have a new friend who was a magic dragon. They had lots of fun flying and balancing together. And they lived happily ever after.
