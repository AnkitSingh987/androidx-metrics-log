Title: The Impressive Jail and the Lily

Once upon a time, in a big town, there was a very impressive jail. The jail was very big and strong. People who did bad things were put in the jail. One day, a little girl named Lily was walking by the jail. She heard a loud scream. It was a man inside the jail. He was very sad. Lily went near the jail and asked, "Why did you scream?" The man said, "I miss my family. I want to be a good person." Lily felt sad for the man. She told him, "You can be a good person. When you get out, be nice to everyone and help others." The man smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily. I will be a good person." Lily was happy to help the man. She knew that everyone can change and be good.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a daddy bunny. They lived in a big, green garden together. Mommy Bunny was worried. She looked outside and saw that their garden's hose had a big leak. She went outside and kneeled down to take a closer look. Suddenly, Daddy Bunny's voice came from behind. "What's wrong, Mommy?" he asked. Mommy Bunny looked up and said in a soft voice. "The hose is leaking and I'm worried. We don't want any water to get in our garden." Daddy Bunny dropped down to his knees and said, "No problem. I can fix it. I just need some tools." He hopped away to find the tools. Mommy Bunny stayed outside, but she was still worried. Would Daddy Bunny be able to fix the hose? But after a few minutes, Daddy Bunny came back with the tools. He worked on the hose and soon the big leak was gone. Mommy Bunny breathed a big sigh of relief. "Thank you for fixing it," She said, giving Daddy Bunny a big hug. And then they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a thin rock. The rock lay in a field of grass, happy and content. But then one day, the rock had an idea. It decided to try something new. So the rock rolled away from the field and ventured to the nearby beach. The rock was so excited and rolled faster and faster. When it reached the beach, the rock was almost beside itself with joy. It found the biggest and most perfect rock pool, full of fish and crabs. The rock was so happy, it wanted to stay forever. But then it realized it had a decision to make. Should it stay in the rock pool, or go back home to the field? The rock thought and thought, but it couldn't decide. So it stayed in the rock pool for a while longer, just to be sure. Finally the rock decided it was time to go home. It placed itself carefully into the centre of the field of grass, and felt good to be home safe and sound. The thin rock was glad it had decided to go home after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable. He had no friends to play with. One day, Tim found a big yellow bucket in his yard. He thought, "Maybe I can use this bucket to make a friend." Tim took the bucket and went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue playing in the sand. Tim asked her, "Do you want to play with me and my bucket?" Sue said, "Yes! I believe we can make a big sand castle together!" They played together and made a big sand castle. They laughed and had fun. Tim was not miserable anymore. He had made a new friend with the help of his yellow bucket. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always played together, and they believed that their yellow bucket brought them together.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Max and Sam. They both had toy trains, but the trains were different. Max's was huge and shiny, while Sam's was much more modest. Max and Sam decided to trade their trains. Max was very excited to get Sam's smaller train and he was sure that it would be more fun to play with. But as soon as Max had Sam's train in his hands, he wanted to make it like his own train - huge and shiny. So he took it to the toy shop to get it cut and painted. But the toy shop owner knew Max was making a mistake and he gave the boys some advice. He said, "If you trade something, you must give something of equal value in return. If you don't do this, you cannot expect a fair trade." Max and Sam realised that their trade was not fair and so, regretfully, they swapped their trains back. From then on, Max and Sam always remembered the lesson from the toy shop owner - it is important to be fair and modest in every trade.
