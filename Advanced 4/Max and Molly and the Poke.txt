Title: Max and Molly and the Poke

Once upon a time there were two friends, Max and Molly. Max had a cell which he kept very tidy. He kept it next to his bed and made sure it was always clean and tidy. One day, Molly came over to Max’s house to play and when she saw his cell, she asked, “Can I poke it?” Max knew it was his responsibility to keep it tidy and warned Molly, “No, don’t poke my cell, you may make it messy.” But Molly pouted and said, “Please, can’t I just poke it? Please, Max?” Max finally agreed and said, “Okay, you can poke it but be very careful!” So Molly poked the cell and it was still just as tidy as before. She was so happy that she gave Max a big hug and said, “Thanks for letting me poke it, Max”. Max smiled and said, “You’re welcome, Molly. Now let’s go play.” And so Max and Molly went off to have a fun and exciting day!

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little dog named Spot lived with his family. Spot had a very unusual tail, which was very long and curly. He loved to play with his friends and hug them every day. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend named Fluffy. Fluffy was a tiny cat. They played together and had a lot of fun. Spot said, "Fluffy, I will show you something special tomorrow. It is a big surprise!" Fluffy was very excited and couldn't wait for the next day. The next day, Spot took Fluffy to a big tree in the forest. Spot used his long, unusual tail to climb up the tree. He hugged Fluffy and helped her climb up too. At the top of the tree, they found a big, beautiful nest. Spot said, "This is my surprise, Fluffy! We can play here and have fun!" They played in the nest, hugged each other, and became best friends forever.

It was summertime and Joe was outside playing with his best friend, Sally. They were having lots of fun playing outside in the warm sunshine. "Let's go pick some apples!" Joe said excitedly. "No, that's too ordinary," Sally replied. "Let's do something special!" Joe thought for a minute, then said, "I know! Let's go for a picnic!" "That sounds fun. What should we take?" Sally asked. The two kids discussed what food and drinks they could take on their picnic. They decided they would pack some sandwiches, apples, and some juice. "This is going to be an extra special summer picnic," Joe declared. Sally smiled and said, "Let's go!" The two friends grabbed their bags and headed off on their adventure. It was going to be a summer they would never forget!

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the mud. One day, Spot saw some mud and he could not resist. He jumped and rolled in the mud. Spot was very happy and very dirty. Spot's friend, a little girl named Lucy, saw him covered in mud. She knew it was time for a bath. Lucy got the shampoo and filled the tub with water. Spot did not like baths, but he loved Lucy. So, he got into the tub. Lucy washed Spot with the shampoo. The mud went away and Spot was clean. Spot still did not like baths, but he was happy to be clean for Lucy. They played together all day, and Spot knew that he would always be loyal to his friend Lucy.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was very fast. He lived in a forest with many trees and animals. One day, the lion saw the sun rise. It was a beautiful morning. The lion ran fast to find his friends. He found a small cat, a funny monkey, and a slow turtle. They all played together under the sun. They were very happy. As the sun began to go down, the lion and his friends went home. They knew they would play again the next day when the sun would rise. The lion was happy to have such good friends.
