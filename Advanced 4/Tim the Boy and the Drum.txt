Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a small, delicate drum. He would play it all day long. One day, Tim's friend, Sally, came over to play. "Can I borrow your drum?" she asked. Tim thought about it for a moment and then said, "Yes, but please be careful. It is very delicate." Sally promised to be careful and they played together all afternoon. As they played, a big wind came and blew the drum away. Tim and Sally searched everywhere, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Then, a kind squirrel brought the drum back to them. It turned out that the squirrel had taken the drum to play with it too! Tim and Sally were so happy and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, they all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a dancer named Lucy. Lucy was very flexible. She could bend and twist her body in many ways. Every day, she would dance and play in the park. One sunny day, Lucy met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw Lucy dancing and said, "Wow! You can dance so well!" Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you, I love to dance!" Tim asked, "Can you teach me how to dance?" Lucy nodded and said, "Of course! Let's start now!" Lucy showed Tim how to dance. They danced and laughed together. Suddenly, Lucy said, "Stop!" Tim stopped and looked at her. Lucy showed him a new move. She bent her body like a bridge. Tim tried it too, but he fell down. They both laughed and kept dancing. In the end, Tim learned many new moves from Lucy. They became good friends and danced together in the park every day. Everyone who saw them would stop and watch their beautiful, flexible dances. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to play outside. She found a big mud puddle. Amy liked to play in the mud. She jumped in the puddle and her feet went "splash!" Amy saw her friend, Tom, a dependable boy. Tom liked to play in the mud too. They decided to make mud pies together. Amy said, "Tom, can you help me squeeze the mud to make a pie?" Tom said, "Yes, Amy. I will help you." They squeezed the mud in their hands and made many mud pies. They had so much fun playing together. After a while, they were very dirty, but they were happy. Amy and Tom knew they could always have fun when they played in the mud together.

Once upon a time, there was a scared little fish named Tim. Tim lived in the sea with his fish friends. One day, they saw a big anchor in the water. Tim and his friends swam around the anchor, playing and having fun. Tim wanted to be brave, so he decided to swim closer to the anchor. He wanted to give the anchor a kiss to show he was not scared. His friends told him not to do it, but Tim did not listen. As Tim went to kiss the anchor, a big, mean shark saw him. The shark swam fast and ate Tim. Tim's friends were very sad. They learned that they should listen to each other and not do things that are dangerous.
