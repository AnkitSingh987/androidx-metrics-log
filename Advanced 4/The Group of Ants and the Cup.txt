Title: The Group of Ants and the Cup

One day, a big group of ants went on a march. They wanted to find food to bring back to their home. They walked in a long line, all calm and quiet. They marched and marched until they found a big cup of coffee. The ants were very happy to find the coffee. They thought it would be good food for their family. They started to drink the coffee, but something unexpected happened. The ants began to feel very fast and not calm at all. They ran around and did not know what to do. Then, an old wise ant came to help. He told the ants to drink water and rest. Slowly, the ants felt calm again. The wise ant told them that it is important to know what they eat and drink. The ants learned a lesson that day and they marched back home, happy and calm.

Once upon a time, there was a big black bird. The bird loved to eat pie. One day, it saw a yummy pie on a window. The bird wanted to eat the pie, but it was not its pie. The bird tried to fly away but it could not stop thinking about the pie. It wanted the pie so much. Then, something happened. A little girl saw the bird and gave it a piece of her pie. The bird was happy and ate the pie. The bird learned that it did not have to take things that were not its own. It learned to surrender and wait for good things to come. The little girl and the bird became friends. They shared pies and played together. The moral is that when we do not take things that are not ours, good things will come to us.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Jacob and Emma. Jacob was a master in repairing things. He liked doing it a lot. One day, Emma found a small lizard close to her house. It was very scared and its tail was broken. Emma asked Jacob: "Jacob, can you please repair the lizard's tail?" Jacob agreed and started to work. He was careful and used all his best tools. In no time, the lizard's tail was perfect. The lizard was very happy to have its tail back and thanked them both with a little jump. Emma and Jacob were very proud of Jacob's work. He had repaired the tail of the little lizard, making it perfect again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big, heavy box she needed to carry to her friend Tom's house. She thought and thought, and had an idea. Sue went to her friend Tom and said, "Tom, can you help me carry this heavy box to your house?" Tom agreed, and they both carried the box together. As they carried the box, they heard a funny sound inside. They opened the box and found a little dog! They were so surprised and happy. The dog became their new friend, and they all played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Daisy who loved to play make-believe. One day she wanted to play farmer and her sister, Lily, wanted to play farmer too. Daisy wanted to be the farmer in charge, but Lily wanted to be in charge too. They argued and argued until they could not agree. Mum heard them fighting and came to see what was going on. She suggested they both be farmers in charge, which made Daisy very happy. But Lily didn't like the idea. Lily insisted they play her way only. But Daisy felt embarrassed that Lily was being bossy. Mum said to Lily, "It's important that we respect other people's ideas. We should not insist on doing things our way all the time". Lily understood and agreed to Daisy's way. They both shared the role of being in charge, and both had lots of fun playing together. Moral of the story: It is important to respect other people's ideas and not to always insist on doing things our own way.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim near the shore. One day, he saw a big, reliable boat. The boat was going to have a race to see who could finish first. Fin wanted to join the race, too. He asked the big, reliable boat if he could race with them. The boat said yes, but told Fin that he should be careful. The boat knew the sea could be tricky sometimes. Fin was so excited and promised to be careful. The race began and Fin swam as fast as he could. The big, reliable boat was ahead, but Fin didn't give up. He remembered what the boat said about being careful. Near the end, the sea got rough, but Fin was careful and finished the race. He didn't win, but he was happy he finished. The moral of the story is to always be careful and never give up, even when things get hard.
