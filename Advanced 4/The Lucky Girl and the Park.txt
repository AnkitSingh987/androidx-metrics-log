Title: The Lucky Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a very lucky girl. She had a special journal she kept all her ideas in. Every day she would do lots of fun things. She loved to run in the park and swing on the swings. She even went to the beach and splashed in the waves. One day, the girl had a great idea. She put it in her journal and wrote it down. She was so excited to show her mom. When her mom saw the idea, she was so proud of her clever girl. She gave her a big hug and told her she was so lucky. The girl and her mom kept writing down all the girl's wonderful ideas in her special journal. She was so happy and felt so lucky!

---

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in their room. They had a big mattress that they put on the floor. They liked to jump on it and pretend it was a trampoline. Sometimes they made a fort with pillows and blankets and hid inside. One day, their mom told them to be obedient and not make too much noise. She had to work on her laptop in the living room. She said she would come and check on them later. Ben and Lily said okay and gave her a hug. But after a while, they got bored of being quiet. They wanted to have more fun. They decided to run around the room and chase each other. They ran faster and faster, laughing and shouting. They ran over the mattress and bumped into the dresser. They ran under the bed and knocked over the lamp. They ran into the closet and threw the clothes on the floor. They did not hear their mom coming up the stairs. She opened the door and saw the mess. She was very angry. She said, "Ben and Lily, what are you doing? I told you to be obedient and not make too much noise. Look at this room. It is a disaster. You have to clean it up right now." Ben and Lily felt sorry. They knew they had been naughty. They said, "We are sorry, mom. We were just playing. We did not mean to make a mess. We will clean it up. We love you, mom." Their mom sighed. She said, "I love you too, Ben and Lily. But you have to listen to me when I tell you something. Playing is good, but you have to be careful and respectful. Now, help me pick up the things and put them back where they belong. Then you can play on the mattress again, but quietly." Ben and Lily nodded. They helped their mom clean the room. They learned their lesson. They played on the mattress again, but quietly. They were obedient and happy.

Once upon a time there was a wealthy family. They wanted a new pet, so they went to the pet store. There they saw lots of cute puppies. Then they saw one they liked the best. It was brown, had a curly tail, and was yapping at them. The family opened the cage to take a closer look. Then they couldn't help but take the puppy home with them. The family took care of the puppy and gave it lots of love. They named their new furry friend, Spot. Spot liked to run around and play. Whenever the family opened the back door, Spot would zoom outside and bark excitedly. Spot was a very good dog. He was part of the family. Whenever someone came to visit, Spot would show them how happy he was. So, Spot lived out his days with the wealthy family and always had someone who loved him nearby.

Molly and Jack were best friends and loved to play together. They usually behaved very well, but today something was different. Molly was sad and wanted to stay inside her house. But Jack wanted to play outside in the snow. He asked Molly to come with him and she refused. Desperate to get Molly outside, Jack started to become naughty and his behaviour changed. He was waving his arms and shouting at Molly, but she wouldn't move. Suddenly, a funny thing happened. Jack had found a white fur coat that belonged to Molly’s grandmother. He put it on and started to act like a silly bear. Molly couldn't stop laughing and was soon glad again. Jack convinced her to come out, and they soon had a wonderful time in the snow. They agreed that behaving well was more important than getting their own way. All was well again in Molly and Jack's world.
