Title: Tim the Boy and the Butterfly

One day, a boy named Tim found a mysterious box in his yard. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very curious about the box. He wanted to open it and see what was inside. Tim asked his mom, "Can I open the box?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. We don't want to spoil what's inside." Tim opened the box slowly. Inside the box, he found a leash. Tim was very happy because he always wanted a leash for his dog, Max. Tim put the leash on Max and they went for a walk. They saw a big, beautiful butterfly. Max tried to catch it, but the butterfly flew away. Suddenly, the leash turned into a long, colorful ribbon. Tim and Max were very surprised. They had a lot of fun playing with the ribbon and forgot all about the butterfly.

Once upon a time, in a regular town, a man named Tom lived. Tom was a very nice man. He had many friends. Every day, Tom would go to the park and play with his friends. One day, something strange happened. A big, colorful ball fell from the sky. Tom and his friends were very surprised. They had never seen a ball like that before. They all wanted to play with it. Tom picked up the ball and started to play. All his friends joined him. They had so much fun playing with the big, colorful ball. At the end of the day, they took the ball home. They knew they would have many more fun days with their new toy.

Once upon a time there was a big open field. Every day lots of animals would come to it and play. One day, a special new animal arrived. It was an amazing rabbit. Everyone was very excited to see the rabbit, but soon enough the other animals started to get jealous. The animals wanted to be the only special animal on the field. So, they told the rabbit to go away. But the rabbit didn't want to go. It did some funny tricks and it hopped all around the field. The animals got so angry and scared, they chased the rabbit away. But then a big surprise arrived. The rabbit opened its mouth and out popped some amazing colour! With its colour it made the open field become beautiful and bright. Everyone was so amazed that they forgot they wanted the rabbit to go away. They welcomed it with open arms. From that day forward the rabbit lived happily on the open field with the other animals. They all enjoyed the amazing colour the rabbit gave the field.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk on his street. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue had an incredible big ball. The ball was red, blue, and yellow. Tim wanted to play with the ball too. "Can I play with your ball?" Tim asked Sue. Sue said, "Yes, we can play together!" They laughed and played with the ball. They kicked and threw the ball to each other. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball rolled into a small room with a door. The door closed and locked by itself. Tim and Sue were sad. They wanted their ball back. Tim had an idea. He found a key under a rock. He used the key to unlock the door. The door opened, and they got their ball back. Tim and Sue were so happy. They played with the ball all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy bird named Ben. Ben lived in a big tree in a pretty land. He liked to tell all the other birds what to do. One day, Ben saw a big red ball on the ground. He wanted to play with it. Ben flew down to the ground and said to the other birds, "I found this ball. I am the boss, so I will play with it first." The other birds did not like how bossy Ben was, but they let him play with the ball. As Ben played with the ball, he did not realize that it was near a big hole. He kicked the ball very hard, and it went into the hole. Ben was sad because he lost the ball. The other birds were not happy with Ben, and they decided not to play with him anymore.
