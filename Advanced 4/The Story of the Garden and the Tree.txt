Title: The Story of the Garden and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a stunningly perfect garden. In the middle of the garden, stood a tall tree with the brightest leaves. Every day, the gardeners would water the plants, pick up leaves and admire the beauty that surrounded them. One day, a little girl wandered into the garden. She carefully made her way to the tree, and then stood there and gazed upon the stunningly perfect beauty of the garden. The girl was so taken in by the beauty, she forgot to put on the protection lotion she had been given. Suddenly, she felt the heat of the sun on her skin, and she remembered her lotion. Suddenly, the girl heard a voice from behind her. "Why didn't you put on your lotion?", the voice asked. The girl, embarrassed, admitted she had forgotten. The gardeners smiled and said, "It's okay, but remember, it's very important. Here, use mine." The girl gratefully accepted the lotion, put it on and continued to gaze upon the beauty that surrounded her. She was very thankful to the gardeners for the reminder, and happy her skin was now safe from the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy named X-Ray. X-Ray was a spoiled toy because Amy played with him all the time. They were best friends. One day, Amy took X-Ray to the park. They played and had fun. But when it was time to go home, Amy left X-Ray at the park. X-Ray was sad and scared. He did not know where he belonged. A kind boy found X-Ray and took him home. The boy played with X-Ray, but X-Ray missed Amy. The boy knew X-Ray belonged with Amy, so he took X-Ray back to the park. Amy was happy to see her friend again. She hugged X-Ray and said, "Thank you" to the kind boy. The moral of the story is to take care of your things and remember where they belong.

Once upon a time, there was a normal duck. The duck liked to play in the water. One day, the duck saw a rubber ball in the pond. The duck was very happy. The duck wanted to get the rubber ball. So, the duck decided to dive into the water. The duck went under the water and got the rubber ball. The duck was very proud of itself. The duck played with the rubber ball all day long. The duck was very happy to have a new toy. The duck and the rubber ball became best friends. They played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who had only one parent. She was so sad and lonely without her other parent. One night, the little girl heard her parent moving around in the living room. She wanted to spend time with her parent so she got out of bed and moved towards the living room. When she opened the door, she saw her parent standing near the window. The little girl said, "Hello, parent. Can I join you?" But her parent didn't turn around. The little girl felt sad and slowly moved back to her bedroom. The next morning the little girl realized that her parent had moved away. She felt so sad, crying and wishing her parent would come back. But her parent never returned and the little girl was left all alone.

One day, Joe went to the park. It was a dark, moonlit night but Joe didn't notice. He walked through the grass and it tickled his waist. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Hello," it said. Joe looked around and saw a tall man standing close by. "Who are you?" Joe asked. The man smiled. "I'm Rod. I was just spending some time in the park." Joe smiled. "I'm spending some time in the park too!" Rod nodded. "Come, let's spend our time together. Let's explore the park and see what we can find!" Joe was excited. He and Rod spent the whole night exploring and it was the best night of Joe's life!

Mum and Lucy were admiring the stars in the night sky. They both lay down side by side and felt the grassy grass underneath them. "Lucy, look at all the stars," smiled Mum. Lucy smiled. She pointed to one as she said, "That one's really pretty." Mum looked up and said, "That star is so sweet that if you wish, it can come true." Lucy was surprised. "Really?!" Mum nodded. "Yup, if you make a wish, that wish might come true." The next day, Lucy had a special surprise. Mum danced around the room, clapping her hands. "We're going to the circus!" cried Mum. Lucy was so happy, she couldn't believe it. That night, they enjoyed the sweet smells and the familiar sounds of the circus. Lucy smiled as she watched the clowns and the animals, but most of all, she enjoyed the night sky and the stars. She realized it was the same star from last night. Mum said, "Isn't it nice that when you make a wish, it can come true?" Lucy smiled and nodded. Moral of the story: When you make a wish and believe in it, it can come true.
