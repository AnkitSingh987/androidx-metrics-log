Title: Jane the Girl and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was only three years old and loved to explore the world. One day, Jane decided to climb a tall tree. She quickly reached the highest branch. From up there, she saw a distant mountain. Jane wanted to reach the mountain, but it was too far away! However, Jane bravely decided to try anyway. She began to climb down the tree and started walking towards the distant mountain. After many hours of walking, she arrived at the mountain and started to climb it. As she climbed, she started to get very thirsty. Suddenly, she noticed a lake at the top of the mountain. She hurried to the lake and drank some of the water. It tasted so good! At the top of the mountain, Jane saw an amazing view. She felt so happy and the view was so much fun! Jane wanted to stay forever, but the coming night forced her to go back home. Even though she was very tired, Jane still smiled. She had seen something distant and had tasted something new - and it was a wonderful experience.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a nice red collar. He loved to wear it every day. Spot was deaf, but he was still a happy dog. One day, Spot saw a big box. The box was wrapped in pretty paper. Spot wanted to know what was inside the box. He put his nose on the box and pushed it. The box fell down and opened. Inside the box, there were lots of soft toys. Spot was very happy. He played with the toys all day long. His red collar jingled as he ran around. At the end of the day, Spot was tired. He lay down with his toys and slept. He dreamt of more fun days with his red collar and new toys. And Spot lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big pumpkin. The pumpkin was very uncomfortable because it was too big. One day, a little girl named Lily saw the pumpkin. She said, "Wow! That is a big pumpkin!" Lily had an idea. She said, "I will turn you into a fun toy!" So, Lily turned the big pumpkin into a fun toy car. The pumpkin was not uncomfortable anymore. It was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy car started to grow bigger and bigger! Lily and the pumpkin were surprised. They laughed and played with the big toy car all day long.

One day, a pretty girl named Mia went to a big building with her mom. They needed to go up to see the doctor. Mia saw a big box with doors, called an elevator. Mia and her mom went into the elevator and pressed the button to go up. The elevator went up, up, up! Mia felt her tummy go funny. When the doors opened, they walked down a hall and found the doctor's room. Mia was a little scared but her mom held her hand. The doctor asked Mia how she felt. Mia didn't know what to say, so she just shrugged. The doctor smiled and checked her. After, Mia and her mom left the doctor's room and went back to the elevator. They went down, down, down, and left the big building. Mia was happy to go home and play with her toys.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing outside. They saw some ice on the ground. Tim said, "Let's separate the ice and see who can break it faster!" Sue said, "Okay, let's do it!" They started to break the ice. Tim was strong and broke lots of ice. Sue was not as strong, but she still broke some ice too. Suddenly, they heard a loud sound. A big, bad dog was running towards them! Tim and Sue were scared. They didn't know what to do. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's throw the ice at the bad dog!" They threw the ice at the dog, and the dog ran away. Tim and Sue were happy and safe. They learned that working together was better than being separate.
