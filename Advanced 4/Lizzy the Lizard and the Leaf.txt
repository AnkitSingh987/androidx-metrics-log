Title: Lizzy the Lizard and the Leaf

Once upon a time, there was a little lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy was a heavy lizard because she loved to eat bugs. Every day, she would go out and enjoy eating as many bugs as she could find. One day, Lizzy met a tiny ant named Andy. Andy was carrying a big leaf on his back. Lizzy asked, "Why do you carry that heavy leaf, Andy?" Andy replied, "I carry it to help my family. We all work together to make our home nice." Lizzy thought about what Andy said. She realized that she had been so busy enjoying her bugs that she never helped her family or friends. So, Lizzy decided to change. She started to help her family by carrying heavy things, just like Andy. From that day on, Lizzy became a helpful lizard. She still enjoyed eating bugs, but she also made sure to help her family and friends. Everyone was happy and proud of Lizzy. The moral of the story is that it's good to enjoy things, but it's also important to help others.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a stubborn rose. The rose did not like to share its space with other flowers. It wanted to be the only pretty flower in the garden. One day, a small bug came to the garden. It saw the stubborn rose and thought it would be fun to eat it. The rose did not want the bug to eat it. It tried to tell the bug to go away, but the bug did not listen. The bug started to eat the rose. The rose was very sad, but it could not stop the bug. The bug ate and ate until there was nothing left of the rose. The garden was not pretty anymore, and the other flowers missed the stubborn rose.

Sara liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had a pink dress and long hair. Sara took good care of Lily and brushed her hair every day. One day, Sara went to the park with her mom and Lily. She saw a girl who had no dolls. The girl looked sad. Sara wanted to be friendly, so she asked the girl if she wanted to play with Lily. The girl said yes and smiled. Sara and the girl played with Lily for a while. They made her dance and sing and have tea. Sara was happy to share her doll. She thought the girl was nice. But then, the girl did something bad. She took Lily and ran away. She did not want to give Lily back to Sara. She wanted to keep Lily for herself. Sara was shocked and sad. She cried and ran after the girl. She shouted, "Give me back my doll! You can't take her! She is mine!" But the girl was faster and ran out of the park. Sara could not catch her. She lost her doll. She lost Lily. She felt very angry and hurt. She wished she never lent Lily to the girl. She wished she never met the girl. She learned a hard lesson. Not everyone is friendly. Some people are mean and selfish. Sara went home with her mom and cried. She missed Lily very much. She hoped to find her again someday. But she never did. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben was a restless bunny who always liked to run and play. One day, Ben saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He wanted to sit under the tree and take a break. As Ben sat under the tree, he heard a small sound. He started to worry. What could it be? Ben looked around and saw a tiny bird on the ground. The bird looked scared and worried too. Ben thought maybe the bird needed help. Ben hopped closer to the bird and asked if it was okay. The bird said it was lost and couldn't find its family. Ben remembered the big tree with a lot of leaves and thought the bird's family might be there. So, Ben and the bird went back to the tree together. When they got to the tree, the bird saw its family in the branches. The bird was so happy and thanked Ben for helping. Ben was happy too, and now he had a new friend. From that day on, Ben and the bird would always play together under the big tree.
