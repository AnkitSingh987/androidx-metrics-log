Title: Lily the Quiet Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little girl named Lily. She loved to go to the mall with her mom. They would hold hands and walk together, looking at all the fun things in the stores. One day, while they were at the mall, Lily saw a big box. She wanted to know what was inside. So, she decided to peek. She tiptoed to the box and lifted the lid just a little bit. Inside, she saw a cute, fluffy bunny. The bunny looked at Lily and wiggled its nose. Lily was so happy to find a new friend. She took the bunny home and they played together all day. They had so much fun, and from that day on, the quiet little girl and the bunny were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable boy named Tim. He had a toy wreck that he loved to play with. One day, he put the wreck on the table and went outside to play. While Tim was gone, his little sister, Sue, saw the wreck on the table. She wanted to play with it too. She said, "I want to play with the wreck!" So, Sue took the wreck and played with it. But she played too rough, and the wreck broke. When Tim came back, he saw the broken wreck and was very sad. He asked Sue, "Why did you break my wreck?" Sue said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to." Tim was mad at Sue, but he knew she didn't mean to break it. Together, they put the wreck back together and fixed it. Tim and Sue promised to share toys and play nicely. From that day on, they became the best of friends and always played together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ella. She was three years old and her mom and dad loved her very much. Every morning they took Ella to the gym. One day when they arrived, Ella saw that the gym walls were all gray. "Mommy, why are the walls so gray?" Ella asked. Mommy smiled and said, "The gym wants to increase so it can be bigger and better. That's why the walls are gray." Ella was so excited! She was happy she would be able to come to the gym more often. Every morning after that, Ella and her family went to the gym. The gray walls were growing bigger and bigger every day. Ella was so proud of how much the gym was increasing!

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a fat bird named Billy. Billy loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. He had a best friend, a small squirrel named Sally. They always played together and had lots of fun. One sunny day, Billy and Sally were playing near the big tree. Billy said, "Sally, let's race to the top of the tree!" Sally looked up at the tree and said, "No, I disagree. I think we should play hide and seek instead." Billy thought about it and agreed. So they started to play hide and seek. As they were playing, dark clouds began to cover the sky. Billy and Sally didn't notice because they were having too much fun. Suddenly, it started to rain. The rain was heavy and the wind was strong. Billy and Sally quickly ran to find shelter under a big leaf. They were safe and dry, but they were also very scared. After some time, the rain stopped and the sky became blue again. Billy and Sally were happy and relieved. They learned that it's important to pay attention to the sky when playing outside. From that day on, they always looked at the sky before playing, and they never disagreed on which game to play. They continued to have fun together and remained best friends forever.

Mia and her mom were walking through the park when Mia noticed a big sign. It said, “Be Independent!” Mia asked her mom, “What does that mean?” Her mom smiled and said, “It means you can do a lot of things by yourself.” Suddenly, something small caught Mia’s eye. It was a turtle, hiding under a bush. She ran over and said, “Look mommy, he wants to be independent too!” Her mom smiled and said, “That’s right. He is being brave and independent by hiding.” Mia laughed and said, “I like being independent too!” They both smiled and watched the turtle as it continued to hide.
