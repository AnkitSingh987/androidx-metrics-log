Title: Molly and the Mole

Molly was playing in the garden when she saw something incredible. She saw a tiny creature with long claws and a long nose. "What is it?" Molly asked her mom. Her mom replied, "It's a mole. It lives in the ground and eats bugs." Molly thought this was cool and wanted to help the mole. She found some worms and placed them near the mole's hole. The mole was very happy and ate the worms right away. Molly was amazed. She watched as the mole completed its meal with an almost unbelievable speed. She thanked her mom for showing her the mole and was very happy that she was able to help it finish its dinner.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to go to university. She was stubborn and did not want to wait until she was big. Sue told her mom, "I want to go to university now!" Her mom said, "You are too little, Sue. You need to wait until you are big." Sue did not want to wait. She put on her shoes and walked to the university. When she got there, she saw a big door. She pushed the door, but it did not open. Sue was sad. Then, a kind dog came up to Sue. The dog said, "I can help you!" The dog pushed the door with his nose, and it opened! Sue was so happy. She went into the university with the dog. They had a fun day learning and playing together.

Once upon a time there was a raven who was very grumpy. He felt uncomfortable all the time and did not like to be around others. One day, as he was sitting alone in a tree, he noticed a little boy walking in the meadow below. The boy looked around and noticed the raven, who was staring at him. "Hello, what's your name?" asked the boy. The raven was surprised and a bit nervous, so he said, "My name is Raven". The boy smiled and said, "I'm going to the river to wash my hands. Would you like to come with me?" The raven hesitated, but eventually agreed. The two of them went to the river and the boy washed his hands. The water felt cool and refreshing. When they were finished, the raven felt a bit more comfortable. He turned to the boy and thanked him for being so kind. The raven decided he should do something nice in return and said, "Ravens like to wash too. Would you like to help me wash my feathers?" The boy smiled and nodded, and together they washed the raven's feathers until they glistened in the sun. After they were done, the raven flapped his wings in delight. The boy looked up at the raven and said, "Thank you for allowing me to help you. I'm sure you feel much better now".

Once upon a time, there was a farmer named Bill. He had a regular goat. Bill took very good care of the goat, as it was his only friend. One day, Bill was walking with his goat, when he met a strange man. The man told him he needed the goat for a special potion he was making. Bill didn't want to give up his goat, but he wanted to help the man. Bill said, "What do you need the goat for?" The man answered, "It's for a special potion. I'll give you lots of gold if you give it up." Bill was sad but he couldn't resist the offer. He handed his goat over to the man. The man disappeared with the goat. Bill never saw the goat or the man again. He was left with nothing but the regular memories of his goat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim and his mom went to the museum. Tim liked the museum a lot. It was a hot day, so they were happy to be inside. In the museum, Tim saw a big balloon. The balloon was on the floor. Tim wanted to see the balloon rise. He asked his mom if he could make the balloon go up. His mom said yes. Tim blew air into the balloon. The balloon got bigger and bigger. Suddenly, the balloon started to rise. But then, something unexpected happened. The balloon turned into a big bird! The bird flew around the museum, and everyone was surprised. Tim was happy to see the bird, and he had a fun day at the museum.
