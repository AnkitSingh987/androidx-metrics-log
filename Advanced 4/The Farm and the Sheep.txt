Title: The Farm and the Sheep

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there were many animals. They all liked to play games. One day, they wanted to play a new game. In this game, they had to lean on some hay. The animals were very competitive. They wanted to see who could lean the longest. The cow, the pig, and the sheep all tried to lean on the hay. The cow leaned on the hay first. But the cow was too heavy, and the hay fell down. The pig tried next. The pig leaned on the hay, but it was not strong enough. The sheep tried last. The sheep leaned on the hay, and it was just right! The sheep won the game, and all the animals were happy. They had fun playing their new game on the farm.

Emily was a mighty woman. She liked to take care of her garden everyday. She would water the plants, pull out weeds and pick the flowers. She enjoyed the sun on her skin and the dirt between her fingers. One day, Emily heard a noise coming from her garden. She looked up and saw a butterfly. Then she saw two more, and soon an entire swarm of them! Emily smiled wide and watched as the butterflies fluttered around her garden. She enjoyed their bright colors, and the way they made her garden look so alive. Emily noticed the butterflies landing on each flower and she felt so happy. She knew the butterflies were enjoying her garden too. Emily had made a mighty garden and the butterflies were proof of that. She enjoyed seeing all of the different creatures that her garden provided a home for. She looked up again and saw the sun starting to go down. The butterflies quickly flew away as Emily started humming her favorite song. She kept her garden alive, just like the mighty woman she was.

Ben and Lily went to the bakery with Mom. They saw many yummy things in the window. Mom said they could each pick one pastry to eat. Ben chose a chocolate croissant. Lily chose a blueberry muffin. They sat at a small table and ate their pastries. Ben liked his croissant. It was soft and sweet and had chocolate inside. Lily did not like her muffin. It was rough and dry and had seeds on top. She made a face and pushed it away. Mom saw Lily's face and asked her what was wrong. Lily said she did not like her muffin. It was not yummy like Ben's croissant. Mom tried to explain to Lily that muffins and croissants were different kinds of pastries. Some people liked muffins and some people liked croissants. It was okay to have different tastes. Lily did not understand. She wanted a croissant like Ben. She asked Mom if she could have one. Mom said no. She said Lily had to finish her muffin first. She said it was not nice to waste food. She said maybe next time Lily could choose a different pastry. Lily was sad and angry. She did not want to finish her muffin. She did not want to wait for next time. She wanted a croissant now. She started to cry and kick her legs. Mom was not happy. She told Lily to stop crying and kicking. She said Lily was being naughty and rude. She said they had to leave the bakery right away. Ben felt sorry for Lily. He liked his croissant, but he did not like to see Lily cry. He had an idea. He broke his croissant in half and gave one half to Lily. He said Lily could have some of his croissant. He said he liked to share with his sister. He said maybe Lily would like his croissant too. Lily stopped crying and kicking. She looked at Ben's croissant. She saw the chocolate inside. She smiled and said thank you to Ben. She took a bite of his croissant. She liked it. It was soft and sweet and had chocolate inside. She said it was yummy like Ben said. She said she was sorry for being naughty and rude. She said she loved Ben. Mom saw Ben and Lily share their croissant. She smiled and said they were good children. She said she was proud of them. She said they could have another pastry if they wanted. Ben and Lily looked at each other and nodded. They said they wanted another pastry. They said they wanted to try something new. They said they wanted to have fun. Mom agreed and took them to the counter. They picked another pastry and ate it together. They were happy.
