Title: The Pink Horse and the Rabbit

Once upon a time there was a pink horse. He loved to run in the fields and play with his friends. One day he went for a run and his friend the rabbit joined him. They had lots of fun hopping around and playing games. Suddenly, the sky snapped and it began to rain. The pink horse was scared and wanted to get home quickly. He galloped faster and faster back to his stable. When he got there, he was very wet and unhappy. He shook his head and made a lot of noise. The other horses in the stable laughed at him. Luckily, his friend the rabbit had quickly hopped into a nearby bush and stayed dry. He came out and gave the pink horse a big hug. The pink horse was very happy to have his friend with him. The two friends played in the stable until the sun came out again. They laughed and galloped together and the pink horse had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go on big adventures in his backyard. One day, he saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim started to climb the tree. As he went up, he saw a bird's nest. The bird asked Tim to leave, but he did not listen. He wanted to see what was in the nest. Tim got closer and closer. As Tim got close to the nest, he slipped and fell. His lip hit a branch on the way down. Tim cried and cried because it hurt so much. He wished he had listened to the bird and left the nest alone.

Once upon a time, in a big green garden, there lived a little caterpillar. The caterpillar was very comfortable on a big soft leaf. He liked to eat the leaf and play with his friends. One day, the caterpillar's mom told him not to eat too much. But the caterpillar did not listen. He ate and ate and ate. He became very big and round. His mom was not happy. His mom said, "I must punish you for not listening." She told the caterpillar to sit on a hard rock for a little while. The caterpillar was sad but learned to listen to his mom. He became a good caterpillar and soon turned into a beautiful butterfly.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy barber. He would open his shop every day. The barber would cut hair and make people look nice. One day, a big lion came to the barber's shop. The lion wanted a haircut too. The grumpy barber was scared, but he started to cut the lion's hair. As the barber cut the lion's hair, he found a beautiful crown hidden in the lion's mane. The lion was a king! The grumpy barber was so happy, and he was not grumpy anymore. The king lion and the barber became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there lived an ugly duck. The duck was sad because no one wanted to play with him. He had no friends. He wanted to prepare a big party for everyone, so they would like him. One day, the duck saw a beautiful bird in the sky. He thought, "If I can be like that bird, everyone will like me." The ugly duck worked hard to prepare the party. He made food and games for everyone to enjoy. He hoped that the beautiful bird would come too. On the day of the party, many animals came to have fun. They saw the ugly duck and thought he was different. Then, the beautiful bird came down from the sky. Everyone was surprised to see that the bird was just like the ugly duck! The bird told everyone that the ugly duck was his brother. The animals now saw that the ugly duck was not so ugly after all. They all became friends and had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a jolly man named Tom. Tom liked to organize things. One day, Tom saw a fire in a house. He ran to the house and saw a cat inside. Tom said, "Oh no, cat! There is a fire! We must get out!" The cat jumped into Tom's arms, and they ran out of the house. When they were outside, they saw a big red truck come to help. The big red truck put out the fire. It was not a real fire, but a big birthday cake! The whole town had planned a surprise party for Tom. They all laughed and said, "Surprise, Tom! We know you like to organize, so we made a big fire cake for you!" Tom and the cat had a great party with their friends.
