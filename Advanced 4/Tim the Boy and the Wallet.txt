Title: Tim the Boy and the Wallet

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a wallet that he always carried with him. One day, he found something disgusting in his wallet. He did not like it. He went to his mom and showed her the wallet. "Mom, look at my wallet. It's disgusting!" Tim said. His mom looked at the wallet and saw the disgusting thing. She told Tim, "You must clean your wallet and make it nice again." Tim cleaned his wallet and made it nice again. Later, he went to play with his friends. He told them about the disgusting thing in his wallet. His friends listened and learned that they should keep their wallets clean too. The moral of the story is: always take care of your things and keep them clean.

One day, a little girl named Sue looked up at the sky. She saw a mysterious cloud. The cloud was big and fluffy. Sue wanted to hold the cloud, but she didn't know how. Sue asked her friend Tom, "How can I hold the cloud?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Maybe we can use a big net!" So, they found a big net and tried to catch the cloud. But the mysterious cloud was too high in the sky. Sue and Tom could not hold it. They felt sad, but they had fun trying. They laughed and played under the big cloud all day.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous dog named Olive. Olive loved to run and play in the big park. One sunny day, Olive went to the park to have fun. While playing, Olive saw a big flower. Olive went close to smell the flower. Suddenly, Olive had to sneeze. "Ah-choo!" Olive sneezed so hard that the flower began to wiggle and dance. The dancing flower was not a flower at all! It was a friendly butterfly hiding in the flower. The butterfly said, "Thank you, Olive, for helping me wake up!" Olive and the butterfly became best friends and played together in the park every day.

One day, a pale crab was walking on the beach. He saw a big tide coming. The crab was scared. He did not want to touch the water. The crab saw a fish in the water. The fish said, "Hi, Mr. Crab! Do you want to play with me?" The crab said, "No, I am scared to touch the water. The tide is too big." The fish had an idea. He said, "Don't be scared, Mr. Crab. I will help you." The fish swam fast and pushed the tide away. The crab was happy and touched the water. He was not scared anymore. The crab and the fish played together. They were good friends. The crab learned that it is good to face your fears and ask for help.

Once upon a time there lived a baby dog named Woofers. He was always very alert and loved exploring around the house. One day, he decided to explore the kitchen. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise coming from the dishwasher. Startled, Woofers barked loudly. Just then, Woofers' mom opened the kitchen door. She said, "What are you barking at, Woofers?". Then she looked at the dishwasher and said, "Oh no we need to replace this dishwasher." Woofers was scared, but he was also very alert. He knew his mom was taking care of him, so he felt safe. He wagged his tail and walked closer to his mom. Together, Woofers and his mom went to the shop to find a new dishwasher. When they got home, Woofers' mom opened up the box and showed him the new dishwasher! Woofers was so happy to see something new in the kitchen. He was so alert that he even tried helping to put the new dishwasher in its place. In the end, the new dishwasher was securely in place and Woofers was very proud of all the help he had given.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her original toys. One day, she played near the hospital. She saw a big door and wanted to see what was inside. So, Amy went inside and the door closed behind her. It made a loud sound. She was now locked inside the hospital. Amy was scared and did not know what to do. She looked around and saw a friendly dog. The dog wagged its tail and barked. It was trying to tell Amy something. So, she followed the dog through the hospital. They went down a long hall and found a big window. Amy and the dog went to the window. The dog jumped up and pushed the window open with its paws. They were not locked in the hospital anymore! They climbed out the window and ran back home. Amy was happy and gave the dog a big hug. The dog wagged its tail and barked. It was a fun and unexpected adventure for both of them.
