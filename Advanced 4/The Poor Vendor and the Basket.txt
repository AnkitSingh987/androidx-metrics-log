Title: The Poor Vendor and the Basket

Once upon a time, there was a poor vendor who started walking down the street. He had a basket of goods that he wanted to sell. He walked up to a little girl who was playing in her front yard and asked her if she wanted any of the goods in his basket. The little girl looked at the goods and asked what they were. The poor vendor said, "These are fruits and vegetables, cookies and bread. I think you will find something you like." The little girl got very excited and asked her mom if she could buy something. Her mom said yes and the little girl ran over to the vendor and picked out an apple. The poor vendor smiled and said, "I'm glad you liked it! Have a nice day!" The little girl thanked the vendor and waved goodbye as she walked away with her apple. From that day on, the poor vendor always had a smile on his face and customers coming back for more.

On a sunny morning, Mama and Baby went to the park. Mama spread out a beautiful blanket of velvet and Baby began to pray. Mama smiled and said, “That’s a good prayer, Baby.” Suddenly, a fierce dog came running over the hill. Baby was scared and tried to run away, but Mama held Baby tight. Mama said, “We must pray. So, close your eyes and pray.” So, Baby closed his eyes and prayed as fierce as he could. Mama hugged Baby and said, “Pray with me.” Then, the fierce dog changed and nuzzled against Baby and Mama. They all laughed with joy. After Baby and Mama finished their prayer, they all ran, jumped and laughed together. Mama packed up the velvet blanket and they walked home.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very intelligent boy. He loved to watch television and learn new things. One day, Tim saw a show about cutting paper to make art. He wanted to try it too. Tim asked his mom for paper and scissors. He sat down and started to cut the paper. He cut out shapes like circles, squares, and triangles. He made a beautiful picture with the shapes. Tim was very happy with his art. Tim showed his mom the picture. She was so proud of her intelligent boy. They put the picture on the refrigerator for everyone to see. Now, Tim loved to watch television and cut paper to make art even more.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was fluffy and white. Lily thought it looked like a soft pillow. She wanted to touch it. Lily asked her mom, "Can I touch the cloud?" Her mom smiled and said, "No, sweetie. The cloud is too high up. But we can kneel down and draw a cloud on the ground with chalk." So they knelt down and drew a big, fluffy cloud on the sidewalk. As they were drawing, Lily's friend Tom came over. Tom was a spoiled boy. He wanted to draw on the cloud, too. But instead of asking, he just took the chalk from Lily's hand. Lily was sad. Her mom said, "Tom, you need to ask before you take something." Tom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Can I please draw with you?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, we can draw together." And they all drew clouds and played happily.

Once upon a time there were two best friends. One day they went to the park to play. One of the best friends was so thirsty, he wanted to find some water. He asked the other best friend if he knew where he could find some. His best friend thought for a moment. Then he told him about the water fountain. The best friend thanked him and ran to the fountain. He put his hands underneath the water and gulped it down. The other best friend watched and realised that it was always good to have a reliable friend. The moral of the story is that it is important to have reliable friends who can help you when you need them.
