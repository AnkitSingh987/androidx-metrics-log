Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to watch the birds in her yard. One day, she saw a pale blue bird with a shiny gem in its beak. The gem was so pretty, and Lily wanted to see it up close. Lily said, "Hello, bird! Can I see your gem, please?" The bird looked at Lily and nodded its head. It flew down and let Lily hold the gem. The gem was so shiny and beautiful. Lily was very happy. After a while, Lily gave the gem back to the bird. She said, "Thank you, bird, for letting me see your gem." The bird sang a happy song and flew away with the gem. Lily watched the bird fly away and smiled. She knew that she had made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Paige. Paige was very independent, and preferred to play alone. Every day, she would run outside to the well near her house and spend hours playing there by herself. One day, as Paige was playing by the well, she heard a funny noise coming from inside. She peered down the well, but couldn’t see anything. “Hello? Who are you?” she called. “It’s me!” came a voice from the darkness. Paige couldn’t believe it – there was a friendly little creature living in the well! She asked the creature what its name was, and it answered “Trolly.” Trolly and Paige became lasting friends, and they would play together every day. Paige loved being able to play with another creature and thought that Trolly was really funny. She wasn’t so alone anymore. The two friends had many adventures by the well, and they were the happiest they had ever been. But one day, Trolly was not around when Paige came to play. Paige was sad, and realised that she was ready to live her life as an independent little girl. She ran home, with a smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a brave hero. He had many friends, but he was the bravest and strongest of them all. One day, the hero was feeling very tired. He wanted to rest, so he decided to take a nap. He found a soft spot of grass and lay down. Suddenly, a noise woke him up from his rest. It was a loud roar! He looked around to see where it was coming from. He saw a lion! It was about to attack him! The hero, being so brave and strong, scared the lion away. Everyone cheered for the hero! They all clapped and shouted so loud! From then on, the hero was known as the bravest around, because he was not scared of that loud lion. The hero was happy; he had saved the day. He lay back down in the grass and took a long rest, thankful that he was safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very organized. He liked to keep all his toys and papers in the right place. One day, Tim was playing with his friend, Sue. "Look at my pretty paper," said Sue. "I made it myself!" Tim wanted to show Sue his papers too. But when he went to get them, they were all gone! Tim felt embarrassed. He did not know what to say. Just then, Tim's dog, Spot, came into the room. Spot had all the papers in his mouth! The papers were not lost after all. Tim and Sue laughed and played with the papers together. They had a fun day, and Tim was not embarrassed anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a little dog named Max. Max was always nervous when he met new people. One day, Max's family told him they were going to a big party with lots of people. Max knew he had to behave at the party, but he was very nervous. At the party, Max saw a big table with lots of yummy food. Max's family told him, "Max, you must behave and not take any food from the table." Max wanted to be a good dog, so he tried his best to listen. But he was still nervous. Then, Max saw a little girl who looked nervous too. Max went to the little girl and she started to pet him. They both felt less nervous now. Max and the little girl became friends and played together all day. Max behaved very well at the party and his family was so proud of him.
