Title: Mia the Sweet Girl and the Tree

One day, a sweet girl named Mia went to the park. She wore a pretty skirt. Mia liked to play with her friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. "Let's play tag!" said Mia's friend, Tom. Mia and her friends started to run. Mia was so fast in her skirt. She ran and ran, but then she felt a slap on her back. It was Tom! He tagged her. Now, Mia had to tag someone else. She saw a big tree and had an idea. Mia hid behind the tree and waited. She saw her friend, Lily, run by. Mia jumped out and tried to slap Lily, but she missed! Instead, she slapped a big bee! The bee was so surprised! It looked at Mia and flew away. Mia and her friends laughed. They did not know bees could play tag too!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was always very curious and looking for something new to explore. One day, she went to the port to see what she could find. On the way there, she was so excited to see what lay ahead. When Lily got to the port, she was surprised to find what was available. The port was full of ships and boats that she had never seen before. She wanted to explore them all. Lily asked her mom if she could go onto one of the ships. Her mom said no because she didn’t want Lily to be in danger. Although Lily was sad, she understood and decided to look around the port instead. As she was looking around, she heard a noise that made her stop in her tracks. It sounded like a loud bang and it was coming from one of the ships. She went a little closer to try and take a look. Suddenly, two men ran out of the ship and began to run away. Lily was scared and ran to her mom, who comforted her. The next day, Lily’s mom took her back to the port to find out what had happened on the ship the day before. Once upon a time, there was an old cat named Tom. Tom had a friend named Sally. Sally was a different kind of bird. They liked to play and talk all day. One day, Tom said to Sally, "Can you imagine if we could fly high in the sky together?" Sally laughed and said, "You are a cat, you can't fly! But I can help you imagine it!" So, Sally told Tom to close his eyes. She said, "Imagine we are up in the sky, looking at the big, blue sky and the pretty clouds." Tom smiled and said, "I can see it, Sally! It's so nice!" Just then, a big wind came and picked Tom up. Tom was flying in the sky for real! Sally flew next to him. They laughed and had fun together. The wind put Tom down gently. Tom said, "Now I don't have to imagine flying, because we did it together!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to act. She looked up to the original tower, which she found very majestic. One day, the little girl got the wonderful chance to act in a play all about the tower. She was so excited to do an interpretation of the original tower that she would not miss this chance for anything. When it was time for the play to start, the little girl started to act. Everyone in the audience was very impressed with her performance. Not only did she show the real beauty of the original tower but also the play touched their hearts. At the end of the performance the audience clapped very happily and one person said, “You did an amazing job Acting the tower!” The little girl was so excited with what she had done and she smiled happily. She knew that this had been the best performance of her life and she was very proud of herself.
