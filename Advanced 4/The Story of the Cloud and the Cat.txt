Title: The Story of the Cloud and the Cat

One day, a big, heavy pot was on the stove. The pot was full of water. Mom was in the kitchen. She turned the stove on. The water in the pot got hot. Steam came out of the pot. The steam was like a little cloud. A cat named Mittens was in the kitchen too. Mittens saw the steam. She wanted to play with the little cloud. Mittens jumped on the stove. But the stove was hot! Mittens tried to jump away. When Mittens jumped, her foot did slip. She fell on the floor. Mittens was not hurt. Mom picked Mittens up. She told Mittens not to play near the stove. The stove is hot and not for play. Mittens learned her lesson.

One day, a famous man went to a big structure. He liked the structure a lot. He thought it was very pretty. The famous man wanted to show his friends the big structure. He thought his friends would like it too. The famous man met a little girl near the structure. The little girl said, "Hello, famous man! I like this big structure too. But I think there is a secret about it." The famous man was surprised. He wanted to know the secret. The little girl took the famous man to a hidden door in the structure. They opened the door and found a big, beautiful garden inside. The famous man and the little girl were very happy. They played in the garden all day. The famous man thought it was the best day ever.

One day, a doctor named Tom had an impressive job to do. He had to solve a big problem. There was a sick dog named Max. Tom went to Max's house. Max felt bad and did not want to play. Tom looked at Max and said, "I will help you feel better." Tom gave Max some medicine. Max took the medicine and soon felt better. Max was happy and started to play again. Tom was happy too because he solved the problem. Everyone was happy and impressed with the doctor.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She lived in a small house near a beautiful oasis with her family. Every day, she would play with her friends by the water and have a lot of fun. One day, Lily and her friends were playing by the oasis when they accidentally broke a toy. They were very sad and didn't know what to do. Lily thought about what her mom always told her, "It's okay to make mistakes, but we must say sorry and forgive." Lily and her friends said sorry to each other and forgave each other. They promised to be more careful when playing with toys. From that day on, they always remembered to be kind, say sorry, and forgive. And the oasis was always a happy place for them to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very ready boy. Every day, he would wake up early and complete all his tasks. He liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim went to a big hill with his family. From the top of the hill, he saw a beautiful view. He saw trees, flowers, and a big blue sky. Tim was so happy to see the view. Tim told his friends about the view. They all wanted to see it too. So, they went to the hill together. They had a fun day and saw the pretty view. Tim and his friends were very happy.

Once upon a time there was a wealthy man called John. John decided he was going to perform a special task. He took his cart and filled it with all sorts of beautiful things. John then drove off with his cart and went around town showing off all his goods. Everyone was amazed and impressed by his wealth. Finally, John reached a market. He stopped and said to the people, "Come and take a look at all the wonderful things I have to show you!" The people gathered around the cart. They were delighted by the things John had to offer. Suddenly, a three-year-old girl approached the cart. "What have you got for me?" she asked. John smiled and said, "I have something special for you." He gave the little girl a pretty butterfly. The girl smiled. She thanked John and ran away with her butterfly. John looked on and chuckled. His special task had been a success.
