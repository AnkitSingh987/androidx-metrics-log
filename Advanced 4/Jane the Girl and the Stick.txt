Title: Jane the Girl and the Stick

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very happy. She had lots of toys to play with. One day her mother gave her a stick. Jane was delighted. She ran around the house with the stick in her hands. After a while she noticed that the stick was very hairy. She wanted to make the stick look nice. So she asked her mom, "Mom, please help me make the stick look nice." Her mom said, "Yes, of course." So they went to the garden together and collected some flowers. Then they spread the flowers along the stick. The stick looked so beautiful! Jane was so delighted. From this experience Jane learned a lesson. She realized that with a little cooperation and help, even the simplest things can become extraordinary!

Once upon a time, there was a generous big bear. He always shared his food with his friends. One day, he found a big box. He did not know what could happen next. Bear called his friends to come see the box. They all came and stood around it. They saw a pretty napkin on the box. They knew something good was inside. They all got very excited. Bear opened the box and found yummy treats inside! He shared the treats with all of his friends. They all said "Thank you, Bear!" They knew something good would happen because of the napkin. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive dog named Max. Max loved to run and play in the big field near his home. One sunny day, Max met a little cat named Luna in the field. "Hi Luna," said Max, "Do you want to play with me in the field?" Luna looked at Max and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play together." Max and Luna played in the field all day. They ran and jumped, and had so much fun. When they were tired, they found a soft spot to lie down. They lay in the warm sun and soon fell asleep. They were happy to be friends and play together in the big field.

Bob was walking down the street when he saw something shiny on the ground. He stopped and picked it up. It was a small screen with buttons. Bob was very happy, he had never seen something so shiny before. Bob started button bashing and soon he heard a strange noise coming from the screen. It was an exciting noise and Bob couldn't help himself. He started playing with the buttons and soon the screen changed to a picture of a man. A voice came out of the screen. "Hey you! You're guilty! Stop pressing those buttons! It's a crime! Put me back where you found me!" Bob was scared, he felt guilty and he put the screen back to the ground. He ran away quickly, looking back to make sure he wasn't being followed. Bob was relieved, he had gotten away with his crime. He was never going to pick up something shiny again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird in a birdcage. The birdcage was in a warm room. The bird was very happy because it was not cold outside. One day, the bird saw a big door open. It wanted to see what was outside. So, the bird flew out of the birdcage and went to the door. It was very excited to see the world. Outside, the bird felt the bitter cold. It was so cold that the bird started to freeze. The bird was scared and wanted to go back to the warm room. But it could not find the way back. The bird was very sad and missed the birdcage. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, kind dog found the bird. The dog saw that the bird was cold and sad. The dog took the bird in its mouth and carried it back to the warm room. The bird was very happy to be back in the birdcage. The dog and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and the bird never wanted to leave the warm room again.

One day, a little frog named Tim went to the beach. He saw a big jellyfish in the water. Tim was very excited and wanted to play with the jellyfish. He knew that jellyfish could be tough, but he was a brave frog. Tim hopped into the water and swam close to the jellyfish. He hopped around it and said, "Hello, jellyfish! Let's play together!" The jellyfish was happy to have a new friend and they played all day. As the sun went down, Tim and the jellyfish said goodbye. Tim hopped back to his home, feeling very happy. He had made a tough new friend and had a fun day at the beach.
