Title: Sandy and the Forest

Once upon a time, a little squirrel named Sandy was walking in the forest. It was a sunny and quiet day. Sandy stopped to enjoy the beauty of nature when suddenly she saw a bubble in front of her. She quickly ran forward and was excited to touch it. But then she heard a voice coming from the bubble that said, "Don’t touch it, Sandy! It’s fragile". Sandy was surprised to hear the voice and asked, "Who are you?". The voice said, “I’m your friend, Windy, the wind. I am here to unite bubbles together in the forest. This bubble will join with the other bubbles, and form a wonderful rainbow." Sandy was amazed. Windy said, "Sandy, we should always unite together, just like these bubbles. Every living thing should be united, so that we can create wonderful things in this world". Sandy was filled with joy and said to Windy, "Thank you for teaching me this valuable lesson. I will always unite and care for all living things!" Windy was happy and said, "It's never too late to learn the power of unity!" The moral of the story is that we should always work together and unite with one another, just like the bubbles, to make this world a happy and peaceful place.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Lucky. Lucky loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, Lucky found a big palm tree. The palm tree was very tall and had lots of leaves. Lucky thought it was a great place to settle and rest. Lucky settled on the palm tree and saw his friends playing below. He called them, "Come and see this big palm tree!" His friends flew up and joined him. They all felt very lucky to have found such a nice place to rest. They played on the palm tree all day long. When the sun went down, they said goodbye to the palm tree and went back to their homes. Lucky and his friends would always remember the fun they had on the palm tree. They knew they were very lucky to have found it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit frightened of the dark. One night, he saw a line of ants in his room. The ants were walking in a straight line, going to their home. Tim thought that if he could believe in himself, he would not be frightened anymore. So, he said to himself, "I believe I can be brave like the ants in the line." He watched the ants walk, and he felt less frightened. The next time it was dark, Tim remembered the ants and their line. He believed in himself and was not frightened anymore. From that day on, Tim was brave, just like the ants in the line.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a pet dog named Max. Sue loved to play with Max all day long. They were the best of friends. One day, Sue wanted to teach Max a new trick. She had a yummy bone for Max. She held the bone high up and said, "Max, raise your paw!" Max looked at Sue and raised his paw. Sue was so happy and gave Max the yummy bone. From that day on, Sue and Max played together even more. They would raise their hands and paws, and Sue would teach Max new tricks. They had lots of fun and yummy treats together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, old tree. The tree had a pretty trophy on a branch. The trophy had a picture of a happy sun on it. Everyone in the town liked the trophy and the old tree. One day, dark clouds came and it began to rain. The rain was heavy and the wind was strong. The people in the town were sad because they thought the rain would make the trophy fall from the tree. They all went inside their houses to stay dry. The rain stopped and the sun came out. The people went outside and saw the trophy still on the tree branch. They were very happy that the trophy was safe. The old tree was strong and held the trophy tight. Everyone in the town smiled and played under the old tree with the pretty trophy.
