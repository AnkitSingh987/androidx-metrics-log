Title: The Delicate Princess and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a delicate princess who lived in a big palace. She had a favorite toy, a small doll that she carried with her all the time. The princess loved her doll very much and played with it every day. One day, while playing near the palace garden, the princess lost her doll. She was very sad and cried. Her friend, a little squirrel, saw her crying and asked her what was wrong. The princess told the squirrel about her lost doll. The squirrel wanted to help the princess. It ran around the palace, looking for the doll. Then, something unexpected happened. The squirrel found a hidden door in the palace garden. Inside the door, there were many lost toys! The squirrel found the princess's doll and carried it back to her. The princess was so happy and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, the princess and the squirrel became the best of friends.

One day, a monkey named Momo was in the jungle. He was looking for a friend to play with. Momo saw a big tree and thought, "Maybe I can find a friend there." Momo climbed the tree and saw a bird. He said, "Hi bird, can you play with me?" The bird said, "No, I must fly away now. But I can tell you where to find a friend. Go to the river and you will find one there." Momo was happy and went to the river. At the river, Momo saw a crocodile. The crocodile said, "Hello, I am available to play with you." Momo was not sure if he should play with the crocodile, but he wanted a friend. They played together, but then the crocodile bit Momo. Momo was hurt and cried. He learned that not everyone can be a good friend.

One day Timmy went out for a walk in the park. He saw a mysterious cooler sandwiched between two trees. Timmy's curious nature took over and he decided to investigate the mysterious cooler. He reached out and opened the cooler's lid. Inside, he found a strange, sparkling rock. Timmy thought it was so cool that he decided to take it home. Suddenly, a voice whispered, "Hey! What are you doing with my rock?" Startled, Timmy quickly tried to hide the rock in his pocket. He looked up and saw a man with a long grey beard and a brown hat. The bearded man said sternly, "That's my rock! I found it in the forest and I wanted to keep it as my own special treasure. Give it back to me!" Timmy felt really scared and he still wanted to keep the rock for himself. After a long pause, he said, "Can I keep it if I promise to take really good care of it?" The man smiled and answered, "Okay, you can keep the rock, but remember to take good care of it. It's very special and mysterious." Timmy was so happy! He thanked the mysterious old man and ran all the way home. From that day on, the shiny rock stayed with Timmy as a reminder of his special adventure.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play at school. But today, it is very cold. The air is icy. The teacher says they cannot go outside. Tom and Mia are sad. They want to run and jump and slide. They look at the window. They see snow and ice. They wish they could touch it. "Let's make a snowman!" Tom says. He has an idea. He goes to his desk. He takes out some paper and scissors. He cuts out a big circle and a small circle. He puts them together. He draws a face and buttons. He makes a hat and a scarf from some cloth. "Look, Mia!" Tom says. He shows her his paper snowman. "Do you like it?" Mia smiles. She likes it. She goes to her desk. She takes out some glue and glitter. She sprinkles some glitter on the snowman. She makes it shiny. "Wow, Tom!" Mia says. She shows him her glittery snowman. "It is so pretty!" Tom and Mia are happy. They have made a snowman. They put it on the window sill. They pretend it is outside. They talk to it and sing to it. The teacher sees them. She smiles. She is proud of them. They have used their imagination. They have made something from nothing. "Tom and Mia, you are very clever!" the teacher says. "You have made a beautiful snowman!" Tom and Mia beam. They thank the teacher. They hug their snowman. They have fun at school. Even if it is icy.
