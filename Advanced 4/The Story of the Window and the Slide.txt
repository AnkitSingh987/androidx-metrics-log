Title: The Story of the Window and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to go on an adventure. Her mom took her to a big hotel. As soon as they arrived, the girl saw all the fun things the hotel had. The girl couldn't wait to explore. She ran to the playground where she saw a big slide. She ran up the steps and slid all the way down. With a big smile on her face, she ran all around the hotel. Next, the girl went to the restaurant. The food smelled so yummy! She ordered a big plate of spicy noodles. She ate it all up and said “Yum! This is the best food I ever had!” After that, the girl decided to take a nap in the room. She jumped onto the bed, curled up in a ball, and fell asleep. When the girl awoke, it was nighttime. She ran to the window to see the stars. She stood at the window, looking up at the night sky and feeling a wonderful sense of adventure.

Once upon a time, a little girl was trying to balance a task. She held the task in one hand and wiggled and wobbled with the other one. She tried to be still and stay balanced, but it was difficult. Suddenly, something unusual happened. A tall giraffe reached down and held her hand. With one more try, she finally could balance the task! She felt so happy and thanked the giraffe. They became good friends and had fun balancing the task together! The end.

Once upon a time, there was a smart horse named Bob. Bob lived on a big farm with his friend, a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with Bob all day long. They were very happy together. One sunny day, Sue and Bob went for a walk. They saw a big tree with red apples. Sue wanted to pick the apples, but they were too high. So, Bob had an idea. He told Sue to climb on his back. Sue climbed on Bob's back, and he stood tall. Sue was able to pick the apples easily. They both laughed and enjoyed the sweet apples. From that day on, Sue knew that Bob was not just a horse, but a smart and helpful friend.

Once upon a time there was a family who wanted to go on a safari adventure. They packed their bags and hopped in their van and drove off. They drove for long time until they saw an empty seat on the side of the road. The family had no idea who the seat belonged to, but it looked quite comfy. The dad said to his kids, “Let’s take a seat and see what happens!” The family all sat down in the seat. It was a bit tight but they managed to fit. Suddenly the seat began to shake. The family held on tight as it lifted off the ground. It was a magical safari ride like they’d never seen before! The seat flew higher and higher with wonderful views of mountains, rivers and forests below. The family waved to the animals who waved back and laughed. At the end of their adventure, the seat slowly landed back on the ground where it was found. The family thanked the seat for the magical ride and waved goodbye. They then hopped back in their van and drove off with wonderful memories.

One day, a small cat named Kitty saw a weird thing in her house. She walked up to it and saw it was a mirror. Kitty had never seen a mirror before. She looked at it and saw another cat. Kitty thought the other cat was her friend. Kitty played with the friend in the mirror. They jumped and ran together. But then, Kitty paused. She felt sad because she couldn't touch her friend. She tried to hug her friend, but her paws only touched the cold mirror. Kitty felt very lonely. She cried and cried. Her friend in the mirror cried too. Kitty learned that not all friends can be touched, but it is important to find real friends to play with and love.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl. Her name was Cora. She was happy and she loved to explore. One day she was walking through the woods. Suddenly she heard a noise. She spun around, she was scared, but then she saw it was just a bird. Cora smiled and reached out her hand. She wanted to stay with the bird, but it flew away. Then, Cora saw a small house in the distance. Curious, she walked over to take a look. When she looked inside, she saw an old woman. The old woman said, “Come in, my child. I am sure you are confused. I can help you.” The old woman looked deep into Cora’s eyes. Cora felt more confused than ever. She wanted to know why the old woman had invited her in. Cora stayed a while and listened to the old woman’s wise words. The old woman said, “The bird that flew away was a sign. You must stay here and explore the wonders of this forest.” Cora smiled and nodded her head. She was excited to learn what the forest held for her.
