Title: Abigail and the Clown

Once there was a little girl called Abigail. She was three years old and she loved to play. One day, she forgot to brush her teeth and her mum was not happy. "Abigail," said Mum, "you must never forget to brush your teeth. Now what do we do?" Abigail thought really hard and then said, "Let's get a clown!" Mum smiled and said, "That's a brilliant idea! Let's go!" So off they went to find the clown. At first the clown was a bit scared, but when the little girl hugged it, it felt normal again. Abigail thought he was the funniest clown ever and she laughed and laughed. At the end of the day, they all went home. Abigail hugged her mum and kissed the clown goodbye. From that day on, Abigail never forgot to brush her teeth!

Once upon a time, there were three little pigs. One day, the three little pigs invited a wolf to come to their house. The wolf was so excited to receive the invite! He was so happy that he wanted to come right away. When the wolf arrived, the three little pigs became a little scared. He was very loud and rough and the three little pigs were worried that he would cause trouble. Despite their worries, they welcomed the wolf into their house. The three little pigs offered the wolf a log to sit on. He was so pleased that he thanked them with a big smile. But then something strange happened. The wolf started to rub his log in circles around the house, making the walls shake! The three little pigs started to get very scared and the wolf refused to stop. The wolf's rubbing made a mild earthquake and the three little pigs lost their house. The house fell apart and the three little pigs ran away, never to be seen again. The moral of the story is to be kind and polite, but also to be careful with who you invite into your house. Even if someone is friendly, they might not understand the limits of what is acceptable.

The sun was shining brightly and birds were singing. Little Molly felt very excited. Today was the day she was going to march in her very first parade. She put on her shiny red shoes with the high heels and the cute white socks that went up to her knees. Molly's mommy smiled and said, "Time to march, Molly!" Molly smiled and said, "Let's go!" Molly started to march and was soon marching fast. She was so proud of herself and tried to march with her heels together as her mommy had taught her. She could hear the drums and the tubas playing. Soon, Molly and all of the other happy marchers were at the edge of the town square. She waved to the people who were watching which made the people smile and clap for her. Molly's mommy said, "You marched very well, Molly! You did a great job." Molly smiled, happy to have done a good job. "I'm ready to march again!" she said. The people watching cheered as the little marcher went on her way. Molly felt so proud of herself. She had marched faster and with better heels than she ever had before!

Once upon a time, there was a foolish octopus. He lived in the deep blue sea. He had many friends, but he liked to play tricks on them. One day, he decided to build a big sand castle. He wanted to surprise his friends. The octopus worked hard all day. He used his eight arms to build the castle. He made the walls strong and the towers tall. When the castle was done, he called his friends over. "Come and see what I made!" he said. His friends swam over and looked at the castle. They were very surprised. "Wow!" said his friend, the fish. "You built this all by yourself?" The octopus smiled and nodded. "Yes, I did!" he said. His friends were proud of him. From that day on, the foolish octopus learned that it was more fun to make his friends happy than to play tricks on them.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy iron. She would pretend to iron her doll's clothes all day long. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, do you believe in magic?" Lily nodded her head and said, "Yes, Mommy, I do!" Her mom smiled and said, "Good, because today, something magical will happen." Lily went to play with her toy iron. Suddenly, the iron began to shake and talk! It said, "Lily, I am a magic iron! I can make your doll's clothes really clean and pretty!" Lily was so surprised and happy. Lily asked the magic iron, "Can you make my doll's dress pink?" The iron said, "Yes, I can!" It moved over the dress and, just like that, the dress turned pink! Lily couldn't believe it. From that day on, Lily and the magic iron had lots of fun together. They made her doll's clothes all sorts of pretty colors. And they all lived happily ever after.
