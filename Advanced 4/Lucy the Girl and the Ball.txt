Title: Lucy the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lucy saw a unique curtain in her room. It was big, blue, and had many pretty shapes on it. She liked the curtain a lot. She played with it every day, hiding behind it and making funny faces. One day, Lucy realized that she could not find her favorite toy, a small, red ball. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and even asked her mom if she had seen it. No one knew where her red ball was. Lucy felt very sad. As Lucy was playing with the curtain again, she felt something round and soft. She pulled the curtain back and there was her red ball! It had been hiding in the curtain folds all along. Lucy was so happy and surprised. The curtain was not just unique, but also a great hiding place for her toys. From that day on, Lucy loved her curtain even more.

One day, a lonely cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a toy and some glue. Tom felt curious and wanted to see what Sue was doing. "Hi, Sue! What are you doing with the glue?" asked Tom. Sue looked at Tom and smiled. "I am fixing my toy," she said. Tom was happy to meet Sue and decided to follow her. As they walked, Tom noticed that Sue was holding the glue very tight. He thought that something might happen. Suddenly, the glue slipped from Sue's hand and fell on the ground. The toy broke again! Sue was sad, but Tom had an idea. He picked up the glue and helped Sue fix her toy. They both smiled and played together, and Tom was not lonely anymore.

Anna and Ben liked to play with cardboard boxes. They had many boxes of different sizes and shapes. They used them to make houses, cars, rockets and castles. They had a lot of fun with their cardboard toys. One day, they found a big cardboard box in the garage. It was shiny and had colorful stickers on it. Anna and Ben were very curious and enthusiastic. They wanted to see what was inside the box. They opened the box and saw a surprise. It was a big shiny lamp! Anna and Ben were very happy. They took the lamp out of the box and plugged it in. The lamp shone brightly and made different colors on the wall. Anna and Ben laughed and clapped. They turned off the lights and played with the lamp. They pretended they were in a disco, a circus, a rainbow and a starry night. They had so much fun with the shiny lamp that they forgot about the time. They did not hear their mom calling them for dinner. They did not notice that it was getting dark outside. They only saw the shiny lamp and the colorful wall. Their mom came to the garage and saw them playing with the lamp. She smiled and said, "Anna and Ben, it's time for dinner. You can play with the lamp again tomorrow. But first, you have to put it back in the shiny cardboard box. It is a special lamp and we have to take good care of it." Anna and Ben nodded and said, "Okay, mom. We love the shiny lamp. Thank you for the surprise." They hugged their mom and put the lamp back in the box. They carried the box to their room and put it under their bed. They said good night to the lamp and went to have dinner. They were very hungry and tired, but also very happy. They had a wonderful day with the shiny lamp and the cardboard box.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was very cheerful and always had a big smile on his face. He liked to play with his toys, run in the park, and laugh with his friends. One day, Tim saw a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly and was very sad. Tim wanted to help the bird improve and be happy again. So, he took the bird to his home and gave it food and water. Every day, Tim took care of the bird and talked to it. Slowly, the bird started to improve. Soon, the bird was able to fly again. The bird was so happy and thankful to Tim. They became best friends and played together every day. And they both lived happily ever after.
