Title: Sam and the Barber

Sam was a 3 year old boy who loved to have fun. One day he was playing outside when he saw something he had never seen before: a barber. This barber was deaf, but could still do a very good job. Sam wanted the barber to give him a new hairstyle, so he went inside the shop. The barber was very surprised to see him, but he smiled and agreed to give Sam a haircut. But as soon as the barber started to cut Sam's hair he was disappointed. The barber was washing Sam's hair too hard and Sam was getting scared. He wanted the barber to stop but he couldn't say anything because the barber was deaf. Sam became very angry and started to cry. He was scared and didn't know what to do. It was then that he remembered something his mom had told him. Sam slowly moved his hands in the air, and the barber understood what he was trying to say - he wanted the barber to stop. The barber felt very sad because he had made Sam upset. He apologized to Sam and gave him a gentle hair wash. Sam was relieved and thanked the barber with a big hug. The barber and Sam then became the best of friends, and they never had any more problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sally. She was only three years old and had a big adventure planned for the day. Her mummy had given her a brush to carry with her. Sally set off, skipping through the meadows and playing with the animals. She was so excited to be having fun. Suddenly, Sally stopped in her tracks. A bee had been buzzing around her head and she was worried it was getting too close. She picked up the brush that her mummy had given her, and tried to shoo the bee away. Suddenly, an old lady appeared from nowhere. She had a serious look on her face and asked Sally, "What are you doing?" Sally replied, "I'm trying to carry my brush so I can chase away the bee!" The old lady smiled. "Well that's very brave of you! I think you'll be able to scare away that pesky bee with your brush. Just remember to always be serious when you are carrying it!" With that, Sally thanked the old lady and set off on her way again. She held the brush tight in her tiny hands, determined to keep it safe. So when Sally arrived home that day, her mummy saw how serious she was with the brush and praised her for being so brave!

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot had an impressive trick. He could lead his friends to fun places. One day, Spot and his friends went to the park. Spot led them to a big tree. They all played a fun game of hide and seek. Spot found all his friends because he was good at the game. At the end of the day, Spot and his friends went back home. They were all very happy. Spot's impressive trick made their day fun. They all loved Spot and wanted to play with him again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to bake with his mom. They would bake cookies and cakes together. One day, Tim and his mom decided to bake a big cake. They mixed the flour, sugar, and other things. Then, they put the mix in the oven to bake. While the cake was baking, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big bush near his house. The bush was green with black spots. Tim thought the black spots were funny. He touched the black spots, but they were just dirt. Tim laughed and played near the bush until his mom called him. "Tim, the cake is ready!" his mom said. Tim ran inside to see the cake. It was big and smelled yummy. Tim and his mom put icing on the cake. They shared the cake with their family. Everyone loved the cake that Tim and his mom baked. They all lived happily ever after.
