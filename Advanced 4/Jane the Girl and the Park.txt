Title: Jane the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time there was a girl named Jane. She was three years old and she loved to go to the park. Every day she would take her dolls and her toys and run to the park. One day, when Jane was playing in the park, she heard a funny sound. She stopped and looked around. Across the park, she saw her mom and dad. They were looking around and raising their hands up in the air. Jane couldn't help but smile. She wanted to see what mommy and daddy were doing. She ran excitedly across the park and shouted, "Mommy, daddy! What are you doing?" Her mom smiled and said, "We are raising our hands to the sky and showing how much we love you." Jane laughed and said, "That's funny!" From then on, each time Jane went to the park, she would raise her hands up to the sky to show how much she loved her mommy and daddy.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a dove. Her name was Lily. Lily was a very selfish dove. She did not like to share her food with her friends. One day, a new dove came to the tree. His name was Max. Max said, "Hi Lily, my name is Max. Can I share your food?" Lily said, "No, I do not want to share my food. It is mine." Max was sad, but he went to find food in another tree. The next day, Lily woke up and found a big pile of food under her tree. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. All the food started to fly away! Lily tried to catch the food, but she could not. Max came back and saw Lily. He said, "Lily, I will help you catch the food if you share it with me." Lily thought about it and said, "Okay, Max. I will share my food with you." So Max and Lily worked together to catch the flying food. From that day on, Lily was not selfish anymore. She shared her food with Max and all her other friends. They all lived happily in the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys all day. One day, she was playing with her toy car when it broke. Amy felt very sad and didn't know what to do. Amy's mom saw her and asked, "What's wrong, Amy?" Amy showed her mom the broken toy car and said, "My car is broken, and I don't know how to fix it." Her mom realized it was an emergency for Amy. So, she gave Amy a big hug and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it together." Amy and her mom worked together to fix the toy car. After a while, the car was as good as new. Amy felt very happy and thanked her mom. She learned that when things go wrong, it's important to ask for help and work together. This way, even a bitter problem can be solved with love and teamwork.

Once there was a girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved to study. Every day she would take out her pencil and paper and study. One day, Lucy was studying when she noticed something weird. It was a shiny blue eraser. She had never seen an eraser like it before. Lucy decided to pick it up, but that was a mistake. As soon as she touched it, she heard a loud noise. Lucy was scared and started to cry. A big, silver robot suddenly appeared and said "Do not cry. I am here to help you". The robot tried to explain that Lucy's eraser had magical powers and was powerful enough to make strange things happen. The robot told Lucy never to use the eraser again and to always remember to study. She was scared but thankful, and she vowed to never touch the eraser again. Since then, Lucy has been extra careful when she studies and makes sure to leave that weird eraser alone.

Once upon a time, there was a cat with soft fur. The cat loved to sing. Every day, the cat would sit under a big tree and sing happy songs. The cat's friends would come to listen and dance. One sunny day, a bird flew down from a distant tree. The bird said, "I like your song, cat! Can we sing together?" The cat smiled and said, "Yes, let's sing together!" So, the cat and the bird sang a sweet song. Their voices sounded beautiful together. Soon, more animals came to listen to the cat and the bird sing. They all danced and clapped their paws, wings, and tails. The cat and the bird became best friends. They sang together every day, and all the animals in the forest were happy.
