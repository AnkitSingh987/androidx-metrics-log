Title: Sue the Girl and the Swings

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue loved the park because she could play on the swings and slide. Today, there was a man playing music on a big box. Sue liked the music and danced to it. Sue saw a boy named Tim who was being selfish. He did not want to share the swings with the other kids. Sue's mom said, "Sue, you should show Tim how to share and be nice." Sue went to Tim and asked, "Can I play on the swings with you?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can play too." Sue and Tim played on the swings together, and they had a lot of fun. After they played, Sue said, "Thank you for sharing the swings, Tim." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you for showing me how to share." Now, Tim was not selfish anymore. He shared the swings with all the kids at the park. The man with the music box played a happy song, and everyone danced together. Sue, Tim, and their new friends had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sarah. She loved to play in her room so she could have fun all on her own. She spent a lot of time exploring her wardrobe. It was big and tall so she could walk inside it and pretend she was somewhere else. One day she went inside the wardrobe and talked to herself. She said, "I feel so lazy today. I don't know what I want to do!". Just then she heard a voice from inside the wardrobe. It said, "Why don't you play some pretend games?". She thought it was a great idea and jumped right in. Sarah played with her toys until it was time to go to bed. Every evening she would go back into her wardrobe, talk to the same voice, and tell it all of the fun things she did that day. It made her feel so much better to have someone to talk to, and it was the start of a very special friendship. The little girl and the wardrobe became the best of friends, and she visited it every day. Even when she was feeling lazy, Sarah would always pop inside her wardrobe and talk.

Once there was a family that lived in a farm at the foot of a mountain. Every morning, when the sun came up, a fog covered the mountain from top to bottom. The family could not see the beautiful views from their farm. They wanted to find a way to make the fog go away. The family went to an old man who told them that a magical bird could help them. The magical bird had a special ability to provide them with something that would clear the fog. They set out to find the magical bird and after a long journey, they found it. The magical bird gave them a special ingredient to mix with water and sprinkle on the mountain. When the family sprinkled it, they saw the fog start to thin out. Little by little, the fog disappeared and the family was able to see the beautiful views from their farm again. They were very pleased and thanked the magical bird for being so helpful.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a toy that could glow. It was a magic wand with a handle. Mia loved her wand very much. One day, Mia felt uncomfortable in her bed. She could not sleep. So, she took her magic wand and waved it around. The wand started to glow. It made her room bright and pretty. The glow from the wand made Mia feel better. She was not uncomfortable anymore. Mia smiled, put the wand next to her, and went to sleep. The magic wand kept glowing all night, making Mia's dreams happy and sweet.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a friendly rabbit named Bob. Bob loved to eat radishes. One day, he saw a big radish in the garden. He wanted to eat it, but it belonged to Mr. Fox. Bob went to Mr. Fox and said, "Can I have the big radish, please?" Mr. Fox thought for a moment and said, "You can have it if you pay me with three small carrots." Bob hopped away to find the carrots. He looked all around the garden and found three small carrots. He took them to Mr. Fox and said, "Here are your carrots. Can I have the radish now?" Mr. Fox took the carrots and gave Bob the big radish. Bob was very happy and started to eat it. But, the radish was bad and made Bob feel sick. He wished he had never eaten the big radish. The end.
