Title: Sue the Girl and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Sue. Sue loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom asked her to support her by setting the table for dinner. Sue was very happy to help. Sue found a big fork for her dad, a small fork for her little brother, and a fork just right for her mom and herself. She put the forks on the table next to the plates. Sue felt proud of her work. When it was time for dinner, Sue's family sat down at the table. They saw the forks and smiled. Sue's mom said, "Thank you for your help, Sue. You did a very nice job!" Sue felt happy that she could support her family and make them smile.

Once upon a time there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to go for long walks in the park. Every day, Spot would park on a big grassy patch and run around all day. One day, Spot was happily running through the park when he came across something strange. It was a small, round and red thing called a radish. Spot thought it looked yummy, so he grabbed it and tried to eat it. But the radish was too tough for Spot to eat, and he found the taste of it to be very harsh. Spot did not understand why the radish tasted so bad. He kept sniffing it, thinking maybe he just wasn't getting the right angle. Suddenly, Spot heard an angry voice shouting at him. It was the local farmer who grew the radishes in the park. He was mad that Spot had taken one of his radishes! The farmer was so mad that he chased Spot out of the park and far, far away. From then on, Spot was sad every time he passed by the park. He never forgot the lesson of the harsh-tasting radish.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to visit places. One day, he decided to visit the candy store. He was very excited. When Tom arrived, the store was very loud. There were lots of kids running around and having fun. He took a look around. He saw delicious candy in jars with labels. Tom wanted to try some, so he reached out to grab a piece. But when he touched it, a very loud sound filled the store. Everyone looked at him with surprise. It was the store owner. He told Tom that he shouldn't touch anything in the store without permission. He said that he had been too loud and needed to go home. Tom felt very sad and embarrassed. He thought about the delicious candy he would never get to try. Tom had set himself up for disappointment, but he hadn't expected to be in this situation. He learned his lesson: always ask permission before visiting a place or touching something!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a big world with many people. One day, Lily felt upset. She did not know why. Lily went to her friend, Tom. Tom was a funny boy who always made Lily laugh. Lily said, "Tom, I am upset. Can you help me?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's wave at people! It will make you feel better." So, Lily and Tom walked around and waved at everyone they saw. They waved at the old man, the little baby, and even the dog. As they waved, Lily started to feel happy again. She saw that her small act of waving made the world a little brighter. And from that day on, whenever Lily felt upset, she would wave and share her happiness with the world.

One day, a little fish named Fin wanted to swim with his friends. He looked for them in the deep blue sea. He swam high, he swam low, but he could not find them. Fin felt sad and his nose sniffed the salty water. As he swam, he saw a hidden cave. Fin swam inside and found his friends all playing together. "Hi, friends!" Fin said. "Can I play too?" His friends said, "Yes, come play with us!" As they played, a big wave came and pushed them out of the cave. They all laughed and swam back to their homes. Fin was happy to find his friends and have a fun day. And from that day on, they always played in the hidden cave when they wanted to swim together.
