Title: The Story of the Mom and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was excited to get a package in the mail. She asked her mom what was in the package, but her mom said she couldn't open it until later. The little girl was very disappointed. Later that night, the package arrived. The mom took it out of the sack and smiled. She said the little girl could open it and she was so excited. She opened the package, and inside was the most beautiful toy she had ever seen! She was so excited to play with it and ran around the room to show her mom. But her mom said she couldn't keep it. She needed to put it back in the sack and give it to someone else. The little girl was so sad. But she still put the toy back in the sack and gave it to her little brother, who was also very excited to have it.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Sally and Tommy. Tommy was bossy, but Sally was always patient with him. Tommy wanted to watch the bell. But Sally wanted to play a game. Sally said, "Let's watch the bell, then play the game." But Tommy said, "No! We should watch the bell now!" Sally kindly said, "Let's take turns. We can watch the bell for a minute, then play the game for a minute." Tommy thought about it and looked at Sally. He liked her idea and agreed. So Sally watched the bell for one minute and then Tommy watched the bell for one minute. Soon it was time for the game. They played together and had a lot of fun. The moral of the story is that it's nice to share with friends. Sharing and working together can make things even more fun.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Bella and Max. They had lots of things to share with each other. One day, they had a green egg. They wanted to share it, so they agreed to cut it in two pieces. But when they tried, they broke it! Bella and Max were sad because they couldn't share the green egg. They had a little fight about whose fault it was. But then, Bella had an idea. She said that even though they couldn't share the egg, they could still be together and have a lot of fun. Max was happy and he gave Bella a big hug. They hugged and laughed and shared a happy day.

Once upon a time there was a young girl named Lila. She was out travelling to see the world when a thief crept up behind her. Lila shouted, "Go away, thief!" The thief answered, "Do not worry, I won't take anything from you." Lila was relieved but still scared. She asked the thief, "What do you want?" The thief said, "I want to travel with you. I am young and don't want to be alone." Lila thought about the thief's request for awhile. Finally, she decided it was okay. She smiled at the thief and said, "We can be friends. Come, let's go together!" So they both began their journey. Along the way they talked and laughed. Lila soon found out the thief was really kind and helpful. They had a lot of fun as they travelled around the world together. When they finished their trip, Lila said goodbye to the thief. She said, "Thanks for being my friend. I had a great time travelling with you!"

One day, three year old Jayden was walking in the park when he saw a beautiful sunrise. Its colors were blue, red, yellow and orange. He had never seen something as lovely as this and tried to pinch some of it to keep. “No, Jayden!” said his Mommy. “It’s not ours and we shouldn’t try to take things that don’t belong to us.” Jayden knew his Mommy was right. He put his hand down and enjoyed the sunrise instead. He saw that Nature’s beauty was special and not for pinching. As his Mommy hugged him, Jayden learned an important lesson, that he should be thankful for the beauty that the world has to offer and not take things from it.
