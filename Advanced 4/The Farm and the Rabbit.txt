Title: The Farm and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there were many animals. One of the animals was a little rabbit. The rabbit liked to hop all day long. He hopped here and there, and everywhere. One day, the rabbit saw a big, red ball. He thought it was important. So, he hopped to the ball and pushed it. The ball rolled and rolled. It went very far. The rabbit hopped after the ball, because he did not want to lose it. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit a tree and broke open. Inside the ball, there were seeds. The seeds fell on the ground and started to grow. The rabbit was happy because he learned that sharing is important. The seeds grew into tall, green plants that gave food to all the animals on the farm. The rabbit hopped around the farm, proud that he helped his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big cabinet in a little girl's room. The cabinet was full of toys. The girl liked to play with her toys every day. Inside the cabinet, the toys would wait patiently for the girl to pick them up and play with them. They loved when the girl included them in her games. One day, the girl decided to play with her stuffed animals. She opened the cabinet and took out her teddy bear, her bunny, and her puppy. She was very patient as she set up a tea party for her toys. She made sure to include all her stuffed animals in the fun. After the tea party, the girl put her stuffed animals back into the cabinet. The toys were happy that they were included in the girl's playtime. They knew that they would have to be patient and wait for the girl to play with them again. And as always, they couldn't wait for the next day to come.

One day, a big aeroplane was broken. It needed repair. A man came to fix it. He worked very hard. The aeroplane was different from others. It had big wings and a long nose. The man tried to repair the aeroplane, but he had a problem. Another man wanted to stop him. They did not like each other. They fought and yelled. The aeroplane was still broken. The man tried again to repair the aeroplane. He thought he fixed it. But then, something unexpected happened. The aeroplane started to shake and made a loud noise. It fell down and broke even more. The man was sad. The aeroplane could not be fixed.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the pond with her mom. Lily saw some fish in the water. She wanted to catch a fish to take home. Lily's mom gave her a net to catch the fish. Lily tried to guess where the fish would swim. She put the net in the water and waited. The fish swam into the net and Lily pulled it out. The fish was smelly, but Lily was happy. Lily took the smelly fish home and showed her dad. He helped her put the fish in a bowl. The fish swam around and around. Lily was glad she caught the fish, even if it was smelly.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. The room was always dark, even when the sun was up. A little boy named Tim lived in the house. Tim did not like the dark room. He wanted to make it bright and happy. One day, Tim had an idea. He asked his mom for help. They went to the store and bought paint. They picked a bright color to make the room happy. Tim was excited to start painting. He knew it would take a long time to finish, but he wanted to make the room nice. Tim and his mom painted the room all day. They worked hard to finish before it was dark outside. At last, they did it! The room was not gloomy and dark anymore. It was bright and happy. Tim was proud of the work they did. He loved his new room and played in it every day.
