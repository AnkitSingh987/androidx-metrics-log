Title: Spot the Curious Dog and the Treasure

Once upon a time, there was a curious little dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig in the yard. One sunny day, Spot found a treasure chest while digging. He was very excited and wanted to show his friends. Spot called his friends, Tom the cat and Sue the bird. They came to see the treasure. But, they started to fight. Tom wanted to keep the treasure, and Sue wanted to share it. Spot didn't know what to do. He wanted everyone to be happy. Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's all bathe in the pond and think about it." They all went to the pond and had a fun time playing in the water. After the bath, they decided to share the treasure and stay friends. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a rich lady named Lily. She lived in a big house with a pretty garden. Lily loved to allow children to play in her garden. One sunny day, a little girl named Sue came to Lily's house. Sue asked, "Can I play in your garden, please?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, you can play here. Have fun!" Sue played in the garden all day. She smelled the sweet flowers and chased the butterflies. When it was time to go home, Sue said, "Thank you, Lily, for allowing me to play in your garden." Lily hugged Sue and said, "You are always welcome, my dear."

Once upon a time, there was a nosy squirrel named Max. Max loved to explore and he was always poking around. One day, he came across an enormous tree. Inside the tree, he saw something sparkly and he wanted to find out what it was. So, he poked around and he discovered a hidden treasure! Max was so excited about his victory. Suddenly, Max heard a voice. It was a small mouse and he said, “What are you doing here?” Max smiled and said, “I found a treasure!” The mouse looked at Max in surprise and asked, “That was your victory?” Max nodded and said, “Yes, I found it by poking around!” The mouse said, “Wow, you are very nosy! But, congratulations on your victory!” Max smiled and said, “Thank you!” And Max lived happily with his treasure forever!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a microphone in his toy box. He was very happy. He wanted to sing a song for his friends. He took the microphone and went to his friend Sam's house. Tim said, "Sam, I found a microphone. Let's sing a song together." But Sam was busy playing with his new toy car. He did not want to sing. Sam said, "No, I don't want to sing. I want to play with my car." Tim felt sad and ignored. Tim went to his friend Lily's house. He said, "Lily, I found a microphone. Let's sing a song together." Lily was painting a picture. She said, "No, I don't want to sing. I want to paint." Tim felt sad and ignored again. Finally, Tim went to his friend Max's house. He said, "Max, I found a microphone. Let's sing a song together." Max was happy and said, "Yes, let's sing!" They sang a fine song together. Tim was happy that Max did not ignore him. They had a fun day singing and playing with the microphone.

One day, a big dog found a pretty crystal. The crystal had magic to heal. The dog felt happy and took the crystal to his home. He put the crystal in a box and kept it safe. A small cat saw the dog with the crystal. The cat felt guilty because she wanted the crystal too. So, the cat went to the dog's home and took the crystal from the box when the dog was not there. The cat ran away with the crystal. The dog came back and saw the crystal was gone. He felt sad and looked for the crystal but could not find it. The cat was happy with the crystal but soon, the magic was gone. The crystal could not heal anymore. The dog and the cat learned that being greedy and not sharing can make you lose good things.
