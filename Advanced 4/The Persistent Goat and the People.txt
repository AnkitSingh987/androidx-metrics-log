Title: The Persistent Goat and the People

Once upon a time, there was a very persistent goat. He was always searching for something to eat. One day, he found a big pile of yummy food. But when he went up to it, he got very embarrassed. It was the dinner table of a family of people. The goat quickly moved away, but he was so persistent, he kept coming back. Every day, the goat would go back to the dinner table, and the people would tell him to go away. But the goat kept coming back, because he was very persistent. After many days of coming back, the people slowly started to accept the goat. They even let him eat with them. So, the persistent goat got his wish of finding food. And he found new friends, too!

Once upon a time, there was a big horse named Tom. He lived in a stable. Tom was a very serious horse. He did not like to play or have fun. One day, a little girl named Lily came to visit Tom. "Hi Tom," said Lily. "Why are you so serious? Let's play!" Tom looked at Lily and thought about it. He wanted to become a happy horse, but he did not know how. Lily had an idea. She brought a big, bouncy ball for Tom to play with. Tom and Lily started to play with the ball. They were having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The ball bounced too high and got stuck in a tree. Tom and Lily looked at each other and laughed. Tom was not a serious horse anymore. He had become a happy horse, all thanks to his new friend, Lily.

One day, a boy named Tim had to wear his uniform to school. He did not want to wear it. He told his mom, "I don't like my uniform. It feels sour." His mom said, "You have to wear it. It is a school rule." At school, Tim played with his friends. They ran and jumped. They had fun. But then, Tim lost his uniform. He was sad. He looked for it with his friends. They could not find it. Tim was worried. Then, a big dog came. The dog had Tim's uniform in its mouth. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog wagged its tail. Tim's friends laughed. The uniform was not sour anymore. Tim and his friends went back to play.

Once upon a time, in a big wild park, there lived a happy duck. The duck loved to dive into the water and play with his friends. Every day, they would splash and have fun in the pond. One day, while the duck was diving, he found a big piece of lumber. It was so big that it could not sink into the water. The duck and his friends decided to play with the lumber. They climbed on it and took turns sliding off into the water. But one day, something unexpected happened. As the duck and his friends were playing on the lumber, it started to move! The lumber was not just a piece of wood, but a big friendly turtle! The turtle had been sleeping under the water all this time. The duck and his friends were so surprised, but they all became good friends with the turtle. They had even more fun playing together in the wild park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to do yoga in the park. Amy liked to stay in the park all day long. One day, Amy met a talking cat named Max. Max said, "I like to do yoga too, but it's uncomfortable for me." Amy and Max tried to do yoga together. Max tried to stay in the poses, but he kept falling over. Amy laughed and said, "It's okay, Max. We can find something else to do." Max felt sad, but he wanted to stay friends with Amy. Then, something unexpected happened. Max tried a new yoga pose and suddenly, he could do it! Max was so happy and said, "Look, Amy! I can do yoga now!" Amy was surprised and clapped her hands. They both stayed in the park, doing yoga and having fun together.
