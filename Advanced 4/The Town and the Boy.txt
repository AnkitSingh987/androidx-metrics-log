Title: The Town and the Boy

Once there was a big town. In it lived a boy. He was very thoughtful. He wanted to balance the town's weight. He tried a lot and worked so hard. But he was too small! All the people in the town laughed at him. One day, a giant came to town and said he could balance the town's weight. Everyone supported him and the boy was forgotten. They ignored the boy's hard work and thought only the giant could help. The giant lifted the town up in the air and was balancing it. But suddenly something went wrong and the town trembled. The giant couldn't hold the town and it crashed to the ground. Everyone was very sad, but no one was as sad as the boy. He knew the town's weight couldn't be balanced.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there lived a small girl named Lily. Lily loved to discover new things around her. One day, Lily found a useful stick. She was very happy and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a useful stick!" said Lily. Tom looked at the stick and smiled. "That is great, Lily! What will you do with it?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "I will use it to help others." She and Tom went around the nation, using the stick to pick up trash, help animals, and make people happy. One day, Lily and Tom found a big box. They opened it and discovered lots of useful things inside. They shared the useful things with everyone in the nation. Everyone was happy and thanked Lily and Tom. The unexpected gift made Lily and Tom realize that sharing is important. The moral of the story is that when we share, we make the world a better place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there were two cute puppies playing in a garden. All of a sudden, one of the puppies began barking and running around in circles in all sorts of different motions. The other puppy got curious and asked what he was doing. The first puppy replied, “I hear something coming, let’s go check it out!” So the two puppies started walking together and as they got closer, they heard a loud noise. It sounded like a humongous vehicle! When the puppies got a bit nearer, they came to a stop, because the vehicle was huge and intimidating. But the puppies didn’t have to be scared for long, because what stepped out of the vehicle was the most adorable thing they’d ever seen! It was a baby in a stroller, wearing a pink bow and smiling a big, gummy smile. Both puppies were captivated by the cute baby and stood there in awe until the baby’s mom came to scoop up the stroller. The puppies then waved bye and ran back to the garden to play. They had so much fun chasing and barking, tumbling and motioning around the garden. From that day on, the puppies and the baby were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock. The clock was in the town. It was very old. The clock did not work. It was sad. A man saw the clock. He wanted to help. The man was good at fixing things. He said, "I will repair the clock." The people in the town were happy. The man used his soft cloth to clean the clock. He fixed the broken parts. The clock started to work again. It went tick-tock, tick-tock. Everyone in the town was happy. The clock was happy too. The man smiled. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big box. It was hidden behind a tree. One day, a little boy named Tim found the box. He was very excited to see what was inside. He wanted to reveal the secret. Tim took a deep breath and opened the box. Inside, he found a small cut on his finger. He was not sad, but he was very impressed. He showed his impressive cut to his mom, who put a band-aid on it. The next day, Tim went back to the box. He wanted to find more impressive things. He opened the box again and found a toy car. Tim was very happy. He played with the car all day long. And that is how Tim revealed the magic box.
