Title: The Zoo and the Fence

Once upon a time, there was a zoo. The zoo had many animals, but it was old and needed help. Tom, a kind man, wanted to restore the zoo. He thought, "If I make the zoo nice, more people will come." Tom asked his friend, Sue, for help. Sue said, "Yes, I will help you. We can make the zoo pretty with cheap things." They painted the walls and fixed the fences. The animals were happy. One day, a big wind came and knocked down a tree. The tree fell on the fence and made a big hole. The animals could get out! Tom and Sue were scared. They said, "We must fix the fence fast!" They worked together and fixed the fence. The animals stayed in the zoo, and everyone was safe.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bobo. Bobo had a pretty collar on his neck. One day, he went for a walk with his friend, a cat named Lulu. They were very happy. While they were walking, they saw a big tree. They wanted to play around the tree. Bobo was running so fast that his collar fell off. He felt silly without his collar. Lulu tried to remind Bobo about his collar, but he was too busy playing. Suddenly, a bird came flying down from the tree. The bird picked up Bobo's collar and flew away. Bobo and Lulu were so surprised! They never thought a bird would take the collar. They looked at each other and laughed. They knew they would always remember this funny day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside. One day, she found a hole in the ground. It was an underground hole. Lucy was scared, but she wanted to see what was inside. She squeezed through the hole and found a big room. In the room, there was a big, scary monster. The monster looked at Lucy and said, "Why are you here?" Lucy was very scared, but she said, "I wanted to see what was inside this hole." The scary monster smiled and said, "I am not scary. I am just lonely. Will you be my friend?" Lucy smiled back and said, "Yes, I will be your friend." They played together in the underground room and had lots of fun. From that day on, Lucy and the monster were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate cat with soft fur. The cat lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. They were very happy together. The cat loved to play with Lily and help her when she was sad. One day, a mean dog came to their house. The dog wanted to take the cat's fur. The cat did not want to quit playing with Lily, but the dog was very big and scary. The cat and Lily were both very sad and scared. Lily had an idea. She talked to the dog and asked him to be kind. She told him that they could all play together and be friends. The dog listened to Lily and decided to be nice. They all played together, and the dog never tried to take the cat's fur again. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a great big house, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved the color purple. She had a purple dress, purple shoes, and even a purple cat named Lily. One day, Mia wanted to weigh Lily. She asked her mom, "Can you help me weigh Lily?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's use the big scale in the kitchen." Mia and her mom went to the kitchen and put Lily on the scale. Lily was not heavy, but she was not light either. Mia and Lily played in the big house all day. They ran up and down the stairs and laughed a lot. At night, Mia put on her purple pajamas and got into her big bed. Lily jumped up and snuggled with Mia. They both fell asleep, dreaming of more great adventures together.

Once upon a time there was a delicate little girl named Lucy. She loved to play on the swing set in her backyard. One day, Lucy was on the swing when she saw a rub lying on the ground. She jumped off the swing to pick it up. It was a special rub, and when Lucy held it in her hands it started to shrink! She was so surprised. Lucy couldn't believe it was happening. The rub got smaller and smaller until it was the size of her tiny finger. Then suddenly, it disappeared! Lucy kept looking but she never found it. Where did it go? No one knows! But Lucy will always remember when the delicate little rub shrunk away.
