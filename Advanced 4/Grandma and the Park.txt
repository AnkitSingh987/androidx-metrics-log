Title: Grandma and the Park

Grandma was watching her grandkids in the park. Suddenly, one of them shouted, "Look, a post!" The kids ran over to look. It was a popular post and they all wanted to touch it. Grandma warned, "Be careful!" But the kids did not listen. They pushed and pulled at the post until it snapped and broke. Grandma scolded, "I warned you to be careful! Now, we have to find the owner of the post." The kids all looked around, but no one was in sight. Grandma and the kids had to leave the park, feeling sad. Grandma said, "Let's hope the owner of the post is not too mad!"

Once upon a time, there was a big cauliflower. It was in a big garden. The cauliflower was not happy. It was sour. No one liked to eat it. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the garden. She saw the sour cauliflower. She wanted to help it. "I will make you better," she said. She wanted to restore the cauliflower. Mia sang a song to the cauliflower. She danced around it. Then, something unexpected happened. The cauliflower changed! It was no longer sour. It was sweet and yummy. Mia was so happy. She took the cauliflower to her mom. They made a big meal with it. The cauliflower was happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a baby. The baby liked to read. Every day, the baby read many books. One day, the baby met a generous dog. The dog gave the baby a big book. The baby was very happy. The baby and the dog read the book together. But then, it started to rain. The rain came into the house. The baby's books got wet. The baby and the dog were sad. The end.

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird was very hungry. It saw a big tree with red fruit. The bird flew to the tree to eat the fruit. It was so happy to find food. As the bird was eating, a small cat came to the tree. The cat said, "I feel hungry too. Can I eat some fruit with you?" The bird was scared, but it said, "Yes, we can eat together." They both ate the fruit and became friends. One day, while they were playing, a strong wind blew. The bird lost a feather. The cat found the feather and gave it back to the bird. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me." The cat and the bird learned that sharing and helping each other is good. They were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was a very dependable girl. She always helped her mom and dad with their things. One day, Lily and her family went to a camp. They had a lot of fun there. At the camp, they played games, sang songs, and ate yummy food. Lily made new friends too. They laughed and played together all day long. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a very happy day. But then, Lily realized that she lost her toy. She was very sad. Her friends and family helped her look for it. They looked everywhere in the camp. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree. Lily was so happy, and she hugged her toy tight. She knew she could always count on her dependable family and friends to help her. And they all lived happily ever after.

Andy the clown was feeling especially dry. He hadn't been given a single drink all day. As he slowly walked along the dusty road, he started to feel parched. Suddenly, he saw a bright spotted umbrella in the distance. Andy knelt down and looked inside. An old man was seated there, his long beard hidden beneath a hood. "I'm thirsty," said Andy. "Can I buy a drink from you?" The man looked up, his eyes shining brightly. "No, I'm afraid not," he replied. "But I'll give you a kindness instead. Kneel here, child, and make a wish." Andy hesitated a moment. Then, he did as the old man said. He kneeled and made a wish. "But what good will that do?" he wondered. The man smiled. "You'll see," he said. Suddenly, the dry road turned wet. A stream of water had sprung up from the ground! Andy laughed in delight and the old man smiled. "Be sure to thank the clown," he said. Andy was puzzled. What did the clown have to do with this? Then, he heard a laugh, louder than ever before. He looked up and saw the clown standing there, his colorful costume glistening in the sun.
