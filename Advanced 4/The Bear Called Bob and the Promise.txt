Title: The Bear Called Bob and the Promise

Once upon a time there lived a little bear called Bob. Every day, Bob would promise his mum that he would help her clean their house. But he never seemed to do it. One day Bob's mum had had enough. She told Bob he had to help with the housework that day or else he wouldn't get ice-cream. At first, Bob didn't want to be rough with the broom, but with gentle words and a smile, his mum persuaded him to help. As he started to sweep and mop, Bob made a promise that he would always help out from now on no matter what. Bob was surprised to see how happy and relieved his mum was when she saw how hard he had been working. To reward him for keeping his promise, she showed him a jar of honey she had hidden away for a special occasion. Bob couldn't believe his luck - a reward for keeping his promise! From then on, Bob kept his promise every day and was never rough again.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck was red and blue. It was driving on a peaceful road. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The truck driver was happy. The truck driver saw a cat on the road. He did not want to hit the cat. So, he made the truck blink its lights. The cat saw the lights and ran away. The truck driver was glad the cat was safe. But then, something unexpected happened! The cat jumped into the back of the truck. The cat found a big box. Inside the box was a lot of yummy food. The cat ate the food and was very happy. The truck driver did not know the cat was there. But he was happy to share his food with a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom liked to print pretty pictures. Today, Tom wanted to print a big picture of a flower. Tom went to his friend, the dog named Max. "Max," said Tom, "can you help me print a big flower today?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Tom! I would love to help you." They started to print the big flower. But, something unexpected happened! When the picture came out, it was not a flower. It was a funny picture of Max and Tom playing together. They laughed and laughed, happy with their new picture. And they had a great day, after all.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bossy cat named Sally. She liked to tell all the other animals what to do. Sally had a special style when she walked. She would spring up and down with each step, making her look very fancy. One day, Sally saw a group of birds playing in the park. She wanted to join them, but they were scared of her bossy ways. So, Sally decided to try a new style. Instead of being bossy, she would be kind and friendly. Sally walked up to the birds and said, "Hello, can I play with you?" The birds saw that she was trying to be nice, so they let her join in their fun. From that day on, Sally learned that being kind was much better than being bossy, and she made many new friends.

Once upon a time, a family went for a walk in the park. They decided to take a different path to explore. It was noisy and busy, but still fun. Soon, they came to a special place. It was a pond, and it was quiet and calm. They sat down on the grass and watched the ducks paddle in the water. The little girl, who was three years old, loved it. She said, "I recommend this path!" The family agreed and they spent the rest of the day enjoying the pond. The little girl laughed at the ducklings and chatted to the other people sitting around. The family couldn't believe how peaceful it was. They went back the same way, and the little girl said, "Let's come back here soon. I recommend this path!" The family agreed, and they all smiled at each other. They had found a nice, quiet path, away from the noisy streets!
