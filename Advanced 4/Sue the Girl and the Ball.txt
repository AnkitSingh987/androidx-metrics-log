Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a special drawer in her room. Inside the drawer, she kept her favorite toy, a small red ball. She loved to play with the ball every day. One day, Sue could not find her ball. She looked in her drawer, but it was not there. She felt very sad and said, "Mom, I lost my ball!" Her mom told her, "Don't worry, Sue. We can look for it together." They searched the house, and finally found the ball under Sue's bed. She was so happy to have her ball back. Later, Sue saw a dead flower in the garden. She felt sad for the flower. She remembered how sad she was when she lost her ball. Sue decided to take care of the other flowers so they would not die too. She watered them and talked to them every day. Sue learned that taking care of things is important. She knew she had to put her ball back in the drawer after playing with it, so she would not lose it again. She also learned to take care of the flowers, so they would not die. The moral of the story is to take care of the things you love and they will stay with you.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She loved to run around in her garden. One day, when it was hot outside, Emma noticed a poppy in her garden. The poppy was so very small and it was in danger! Emma had to save the poppy. Emma quickly ran inside and grabbed a pail of water. Then she slowly and carefully walked back to the garden. She carefully poured the water around the poppy, so it wouldn't get too hot. The poppy thanked Emma and said, "Thank you for saving me!" Emma smiled and said, "You're welcome, little poppy!" Emma was very happy. She knew she had done the right thing by saving the poppy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia was brilliant and she loved to learn. One day, she met a new friend named Ben. Ben did not know the same language as Mia. They wanted to play together, but it was hard because they could not talk to each other. Mia said, "I want to play, but I don't know your language." Ben felt sad and said, "I want to play too, but I don't know your language either." They both felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, Mia had an idea. She thought they could learn each other's language. They started teaching each other words from their languages. Soon, they could talk to each other and play together. They became best friends and had a lot of fun. And from that day on, they knew that even if they didn't speak the same language, they could still be friends.

Once upon a time, in a beautiful forest, there lived a little bird. The little bird loved to sing. Every day, the bird would sing with its mouth wide open. One day, a wise old owl heard the bird's song. The owl thought the song was very nice. The owl wanted to recommend a new song for the bird to sing. The owl said, "Little bird, I have a new song for you. It is a beautiful song." The little bird was happy to learn a new song. The wise old owl taught the beautiful song to the little bird. The little bird practiced with its mouth open wide. Soon, the little bird could sing the beautiful song perfectly. The other animals in the forest heard the little bird's new song. They all loved the beautiful song the wise old owl recommended. The little bird was very happy to share the beautiful song with its friends. And they all lived happily in the beautiful forest.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It was a happy day. A girl named Lucy wanted to marry a boy named Tom. They loved to play music together. Lucy played the violin, and Tom played the drum. One day, Lucy and Tom went to the park to play. They played a happy song on their violin and drum. Everyone danced and clapped. Suddenly, a big wind came and took Lucy's violin away. Lucy was sad, and Tom was sad too. A little bird saw the violin in a tree. The bird flew up and got the violin for Lucy. Lucy and Tom were happy again. They played more music and everyone danced. The bird danced too. The moral of the story is: when you are sad, your friends can help you be happy again.
