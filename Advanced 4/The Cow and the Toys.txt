Title: The Cow and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a big cow. The cow was very proud of her big size. One day, she saw a big steak in the kitchen. The cow did not know what a steak was. She thought it was a toy. The cow wanted to play with the steak. She tried to separate it from the other toys on the table. But the steak was very slippery. The cow got very sad because she could not play with the steak. Then, something unexpected happened. The cow's mom came into the kitchen. She saw the cow and the steak. The mom told the cow that the steak was not a toy. The cow was very sad. She went outside to play, but she could not forget about the steak. The cow was not happy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tim. Tim loved to go to the theater with his family. They would watch fun shows and laugh together. One day, Tim found a smooth rock on the way to the theater. He was so happy and decided to bring it with him. Inside the theater, Tim held the smooth rock tightly in his paws. During the show, a loud noise made Tim tremble with fear. He dropped his smooth rock, and it rolled away. Tim felt sad, but his family helped him look for it. They found the smooth rock under a chair. Tim was so happy to have his smooth rock back. He didn't tremble anymore, and they all enjoyed the rest of the show together. From that day on, Tim always brought his smooth rock to the theater.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore his favorite red sweater. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim played with his ball, and his mom sat on a bench. Suddenly, Tim saw a big dog running towards him. "Mommy, help!" he screamed. The dog was very big and very fast. Tim was scared. He held his ball hard in his hands. But the big dog just wanted to play. It stopped in front of Tim and wagged its tail. "Don't be scared, Tim," said the dog's owner, a nice old man. "This is my dog, Buddy. He loves to play with kids." Tim looked at the dog and smiled. He threw the ball, and Buddy ran after it. They played together all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time there were two frogs, who fell in love and wanted to marry. They were excited to start their married life together, so off they hopped from their home pond. When they arrived at the meadow, the sun was shining and there were many beautiful flowers. The frogs chatted away, with the gentle beauty of the meadow all around them. Then all of a sudden, they heard a loud crack! A glass was broken! One of the frogs picked up the broken pieces and saw it was their marriage glass. The other frog said, "Oh no, our marriage glass is broken!" The first frog thought carefully, and then said, "It doesn't matter - we can still marry. Now our marriage will be rough, but it will be just as beautiful as before." The two frogs looked into each other’s eyes and kissed, and then together hopped away to their new home - together as husband and wife.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to collect things. She had a big box in her room where she kept her collection. One day, Sue found an amazing muffin. It was big, soft, and smelled so good. Sue showed the amazing muffin to her friend, Tom. "Look what I found!" she said. "This muffin is so big and soft. I want to keep it in my collection." Tom looked at the muffin and said, "That's a great idea! But first, let's show it to our teacher." They took the amazing muffin to their teacher, Miss Lucy. Miss Lucy looked at the muffin and smiled. "This muffin is very special," she said. "It can talk!" Sue and Tom were very surprised. They had never seen a talking muffin before. The muffin said, "Hello, Sue and Tom! I am a magic muffin. I can grant you one wish." Sue and Tom thought about it, and they decided to wish for more amazing muffins to share with their friends. The magic muffin granted their wish, and suddenly, there were enough amazing muffins for everyone. Sue and Tom were very happy. They shared the amazing muffins with their friends, and everyone enjoyed them. And from that day on, Sue's collection became even more special because it had a magic talking muffin.
