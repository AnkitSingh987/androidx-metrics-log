Title: The Flame and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little flame. The flame lived in a big forest with many trees. The flame was adventurous and loved to play with his friends, the leaves. One day, the flame and the leaves played a new game called "hide and seek." The flame was very good at the game because he could jump high and hide in the trees. The leaves loved playing with the flame because he was so much fun. But, one day, the flame and the leaves played too much, and the forest became very hot. The flame's mommy and daddy told him that he needed to be more careful when he played. The flame learned to be more gentle with his friends, and they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a nosy little boy named Jimmy was outside playing in the rain. It was really stormy, and lightning kept flashing in the sky. Jimmy liked it, and he kept pointing to the lightning. "Mommy, look at the lightning! Look at the lightning!" Jimmy shouted. "I'm right here, Jimmy," Mommy replied. "But you should be careful! Lightning can be dangerous." But Jimmy kept pointing and shouting. He pointed at the lightning and called out its name. Suddenly, a loud crash came from behind him. Mommy gasped. "Oh no, Jimmy," she said. "Go inside right now. That might have been lightning!" Jimmy was a little scared, but he wasn't too worried. He ran inside, happy to be out of the rain. From inside, Jimmy peeked out the window and kept pointing at the lightning. He wasn't as nosy as before, but he just couldn't help himself.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim was very comfortable in his small house. He had a soft bed and a big bowl of milk. One day, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help her, so he used his mind to think of a way to find the toy. Tim looked under the tree, behind the bush, and in the grass. He found Sue's toy under the big rock. Tim gave the toy to Sue and she was very happy. Sue said "thank you" to Tim and gave him more milk. Tim and Sue became best friends. They played together every day, and Tim always had a big bowl of milk.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom did not like to play with other cats. He just liked to sit under a big tree and watch the birds. One day, a little bird named Sally came to the tree. Sally loved to sing. She sang a happy song to Tom. Tom looked at Sally and said, "Why do you sing? I don't like it." Sally smiled and said, "I sing because it makes me happy. And it can make others happy too. It is a law of the forest that we should try to make others happy." Tom thought about it and decided to try singing too. Tom and Sally sang together, and soon, other animals came to listen. They all felt happy. Even the grumpy cat Tom learned that singing could make him feel happy too. And from that day on, Tom wasn't so grumpy anymore.

Once upon a time there was a green frog. He lived in a pond and always felt very warm and happy. One day, he was sitting on a rock and enjoying the sun. Suddenly, he felt something poking him. He looked around and saw a friendly dragonfly. The dragonfly wanted to play with the frog, so he kept poking him. The frog was surprised, but he smiled and hopped over to the dragonfly. Together, they played a fun game of tag. They chased each other around the pond, getting very close to the green water. The dragonfly was fast, but the frog was even faster! When the game was over, the dragonfly flew away and the frog settled back onto his rock. He was warm from all the fun playing, and he smiled to himself as he rested in the sun.
