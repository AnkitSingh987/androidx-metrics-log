Title: A Tale of the Car

One day, a little car was driving down the road. The car was very persistent. It wanted to go fast and far. The car liked to drive all day. Soon, the car felt tired. The car needed to rest. It stopped under a big tree. The car closed its eyes and took a deep breath. After a short rest, the car felt better. It was happy and ready to drive again. The car drove down the road, still persistent, but now it knew when to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Clara who wanted to organize her chest. She was so excited to get it done and it was almost finished. Clara asked her mother for help. "Mom, can you help me organize my chest?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's get started". So Clara and her mom worked together to put everything in its place. When they were finished, Clara was very happy. But then Clara remembered something and her face became sad. "Mom, I'm sorry, I forgot to put my favorite toy in the chest," she said. Her mom put her arm around Clara and hugged her tight. "That's okay sweetie. We can always go back and organize the chest again," her mom said. Clara was so happy and she knew that with the help of her mom, she could organize her chest perfectly.

Mommy and Tommy were playing outside yesterday. Tommy was very excited to explore the park and was running around happily. Suddenly, the sky turned grey and it started to rain. Mommy said, "Let's take shelter under the tree, Tommy." Tommy agreed and ran to the tree. Tommy pointed at the grass and said, "Mommy, why is it so sad?" Mommy replied, "Well, the rain has been coming down hard and flooding the ground. That's why it's so sad, Tommy." Tommy said, "Mommy, I want to help the grass!" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, that would be nice. We can help the grass by taking away the water with a bucket." So, Mommy and Tommy took a bucket and walked to the flooding grass. They started to take away the water and soon, the grass was looking much better. Mommy said, "See, Tommy? You can make a difference when you try your best." Tommy smiled, feeling happy that he had helped the grass feel better.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a funny monkey named Bobo. Bobo loved to hang from the trees and play all day. He was a little crazy, but all his friends liked him. One day, Bobo met a new friend, a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy said, "Hi Bobo, do you want to play with me?" Bobo said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, they played and had lots of fun. As they played, Bobo showed Chirpy how to hang from the trees like a crazy monkey. Chirpy tried to hang too, but it was hard for a little bird. They laughed and played until the sun went down. From that day on, Bobo and Chirpy were the best of friends in the big forest.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a sunny day, but the sky was getting dark. Tim saw a big tree and wanted to play under it. While playing, Tim found a piece of paper on the ground. He picked it up and saw it was a license. He did not know what a license was, so he asked his mom. "Mom, what is this?" His mom said, "It is a license, Tim. It is a special paper that lets people do things, like drive a car." Tim was thirsty from playing, so he asked his mom for a drink. She gave him a cup of water. After drinking the water, Tim felt better. He smiled and said, "Mom, I want a license to drink water every day!" His mom laughed and said, "You don't need a license for that, Tim. You can always drink water when you are thirsty." Tim was happy and continued to play under the big tree until it was time to go home.
