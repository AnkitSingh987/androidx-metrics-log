Title: The Baby Chick and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little baby chick who had the most beautiful yellow feathers. She was very special and everyone wanted to add her to their flock. But everywhere baby chick went, a long black shadow followed. Some of the other baby chicks were scared by the long black shadow and didn't want to add baby chick to the flock. So, baby chick was very lonely. One day, baby chick heard a voice from the sky. It told her, "You are special and that's why you have a shadow, don't let it make you feel alone." With these words, baby chick flew away and everywhere she flapped her wings, a sparkle of yellow light flew towards the other baby chicks. Finally, the flock was willing to add baby chick to their family. From then on, her long black shadow was no longer a lonely reminder, it was a reminder that we are all special. The moral of the story is that we all have something special and unique about us. Don't ever let someone else's opinion of you make you feel alone.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty clock. It was big and strong. The clock lived in a little house on a hill. It liked to tell time for all the people in the town. The people loved the mighty clock. One day, a little girl named Sally wanted to write a letter to the mighty clock. She used a crayon and paper. Sally wrote, "Dear clock, thank you for telling time. I love you." She put the letter in an envelope and walked up the hill to the little house. Sally knocked on the door of the little house. The mighty clock opened the door and smiled. It was happy to see Sally. She gave the letter to the clock. The clock read the letter and hugged Sally. They both were very happy and became good friends.

Mama was setting the plate on the table. "Come here, Sammy," she said. Sammy was a small three year old boy. He walked over to the table and looked at the plate. "What's this?" Sammy asked. "It's dinner," Mama said. "Go sit down and I'll serve it." Sammy looked at the plate and furrowed his eyebrows. "It looks stupid," he said. Mama laughed. "It doesn't look stupid. It looks delicious. I made it just for you." Sammy still wasn't sure if he wanted to eat it. But Mama said it was delicious, so he picked up his fork and started eating. It was yummy! Mama was right.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dress up in her mom's clothes. One day, Lily found a box of ornaments in her room. She was very enthusiastic and happy. Lily decided to put the ornaments on her dress. She put a shiny star on her hat and a big, round ball on her shirt. She looked so pretty! Lily went to show her mom what she had done. "Mom, look at my dress!" said Lily. "I put ornaments on it!" Her mom smiled and said, "You look so beautiful, Lily! I love your dress with the ornaments." Lily felt very proud and happy. She wore her special dress all day and showed it to all her friends. They all loved it, too. And that is the story of Lily and her ornament dress.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to paint. One day, he found a big, white wall. Tim wanted to paint the wall with many colors. Tim started to paint. He painted a big, red sun. He painted a blue sky. He painted green grass. Then, he saw something weird. The paint on the wall began to move! The paint made a funny face. The face said, "Hello!" Tim was happy. He and the paint face became friends. They played and painted together. Soon, the wall was finished. It was the most beautiful wall ever. And Tim and the paint face were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a scooter that he loved very much. He was very proud of his scooter because it was red and shiny. Every day, he would ride his scooter and slide down the hill near his house. One day, Tim saw a big box at the bottom of the hill. He was excited and curious to see what was inside. He thought it might be a surprise for him. So, he rode his scooter and slid down the hill very fast to see the box. When Tim got to the box, he opened it and found a new scooter inside! It was blue and shiny, and even bigger than his red scooter. Tim was so happy and proud of his new scooter. He knew he could slide even faster with it. But then, his little sister, Sue, came running down the hill. She was so excited and said, "Thank you for finding my new scooter, Tim!" Tim was surprised, but he smiled and gave the blue scooter to Sue. They both had fun sliding down the hill together on their scooters, and Tim felt proud to help his sister.
