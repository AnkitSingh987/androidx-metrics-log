Title: Sarah and the Circle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three years old and loved to explore her house. One day, Sarah found a big circle in the shower. She went over and touched its hard surface, and it felt cold and uncomfortable. Sarah went to ask her mom why it was there. "Mommy, what is this big circle?" she asked. "Oh that? That is a drain, honey" replied her mom. "Why is it there?" asked Sarah. "We need it so the water can drain away after we bathe" replied her mom. Sarah smiled in understanding. The next time she was in the bathroom, Sarah looked at the circle with a new appreciation. She could see how it was an important part of the family's bathing routine.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. This rock had a fun style. It was a fair rock too, because it let all the little bugs play on it. One sunny day, a little girl came to the rock. She wanted to play with the bugs too. But when she got close, she saw that the rock was not a rock at all! It was a big turtle with a shell that looked like a rock. The turtle said, "I like your style, little girl. You are fair and kind to the bugs." The little girl was happy and played with the turtle and the bugs all day. They all had a great time and became good friends.

Once there was a little girl named Beth. Beth was very foolish, but she was also very brave. One day, she decided to make a pie for her mom and dad. She looked in the kitchen for ingredients like flour and butter and started to mix it all together. But then she made a silly mistake. She forgot to add sugar! The pie was not finished. Beth knew that her mom and dad would be sad if the pie had no sugar. Beth thought hard about what to do. Then she knelt down and said a prayer to God. She asked for help and knowledge about how to fix her mistake. Then suddenly, Beth had a brilliant idea. She could go to the store and buy sugar. Hesitantly, she set out on her way. When she returned with the sugar, she added it to the pie. This time, it tasted perfect. Her parents were so surprised and happy! They hugged Beth and thanked her for being so brave and clever. From that day forward, Beth always remembered not to be foolish and to always think before she acted.

Once upon a time, there was a calm cat named Sam. Sam liked to see things outside. One day, Sam went out to play. While Sam was playing, he saw something cold. It was white and soft. Sam had never seen this before. He touched it and found out it was cold. Sam was very excited. Sam played with the cold white thing all day. He made a big ball with it. Sam was very happy. At the end of the day, he went home to rest. Sam could not wait to play with the cold white thing again.

Once, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to cook with her mom. One day, they decided to make a big cake that looked like a volcano. Lily was very excited. Lily's mom was very reliable. She always helped Lily when she needed it. They worked together to make the volcano cake. They used red and orange to make it look like real fire. When they were done, they showed the cake to their friends. Everyone was so happy and surprised. They did not know that Lily and her mom could make such a great cake. The moral of the story is that when you work together with someone reliable, you can make amazing things happen.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Bob and Mary. Bob was brown and Mary was red. They liked to play in the garden. One day, Bob and Mary were playing, when a big gust of wind came and blew away all the dust in the garden. They laughed and ran after the dust, trying to catch it. But then, they both started to fight. Bob wanted all the dust, but Mary wanted some too. They fought and fought until they were both tired. Then, Bob had an idea. He got two handkerchiefs and he divided the dust into two piles. One pile was for Bob and one pile was for Mary. Mary smiled and they hugged. From that day on, Bob and Mary always shared and played together nicely.
