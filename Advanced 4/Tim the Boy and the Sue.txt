Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

One day, a little boy named Tim got an invitation. It was from his friend, Sue. Sue was having a party. Tim was very happy. He thought Sue was very thoughtful for inviting him. At the party, they played a game called "match". They had to find two things that were the same. Tim was very good at the game. He found many matches. Sue was happy that Tim was having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came into the room. The dog took one of the matches and ran away. Everyone was surprised. Tim said, "Oh no! Let's find the dog and get the match back!" They all looked for the dog and found it. The dog was just playing too. They all laughed and continued the game.

Once upon a time, a three year old girl named Lucy wanted to unlock the door of her big brother's room. She had seen him go inside and come out many times, and always kept something behind the door. On this special day, to her surprise, her brother hadn't locked the door. Lucy peeped through the key hole and saw her brother, who had a bow and arrow in his hand. She was amazed and decided to sneak in to get closer. When she opened the door, Lucy saw her brother aiming the bow, with a serious face. She didn't know what he was doing, but the look on his face and the quietness of the room made her stop. She walked to him silently and he saw her and smiled. "What are you doing?" she asked. He smiled again and said "I'm practicing my archery skills". Lucy was happy that he was not doing something naughty or dangerous, and this thrilled her even more. Her brother took her outside and explained that archery was about discipline and focus. He said that to become good at it, one had to be serious and practice hard. Lucy thought that was a great teaching and thanked her brother for sharing it. The moral of this story is that we should all be serious and focused in whatever we do. When we take things seriously and practice hard, we are on our way to success!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an empty journal. Tim wanted to fill it with pretty pictures and fun stories. But he was too tired to draw or write. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the empty journal and said, "Why don't you draw with me, Tim?" Tim replied, "I want to, but I am too tired to draw." Sue thought for a moment and said, "You should rest, Tim. Then you will have energy to draw in your journal." Tim listened to Sue and took a short rest. When he woke up, he felt much better. Tim and Sue filled the journal with beautiful drawings and fun stories. They had a great time together. The moral of the story is that sometimes, you need to rest to have the energy to do the things you love.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat. The rat loved to swing. Every day, he went to the park to swing. He was very eager to play. One sunny day, the rat went to the park. He saw a big dog on the swing. The rat was sad. He wanted to swing, but the dog was there. The rat had an idea. He went to the dog and asked, "Can we swing together?" The dog said, "Yes!" They swung together, and they had so much fun. The rat and the dog became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a bee named Bob. Bob lived in a hive with his friends. The hive was high up in a tree. Every day, Bob and his friends would collect food. They would find cheap flowers to get their food from. They liked the cheap flowers best because they were easy to find. One day, Bob and his friends found a big field of cheap flowers. They collected lots of food for their hive. They were very happy and lived a good life.
