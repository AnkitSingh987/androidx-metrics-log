Title: The Wealthy Family and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a big house, there was a wealthy family. They had many things and were very happy. One day, the little girl went into the garden to play. While playing, she saw a small animal. It was hiding under a big leaf. The girl was very excited and wanted to discover more about the animal. She watched it as it moved around the garden. The little girl told her family about the animal. They all went outside to see it. They discovered that the animal was a cute bunny. The family loved the bunny and let it stay in their garden. They were even happier now with their new friend.

One Sunday, a boy named Tim saw a mysterious big box in his room. He did not know where it came from. He asked his mom, "Mom, what is in the big box?" His mom said, "I don't know, Tim. Let's open it and find out." Inside the box, they found a big jar of colorful paint. Tim was very happy. He wanted to spread the paint on paper and make a pretty picture. But, his little sister, Sue, wanted to play with the paint too. They started to fight over the paint. Then, something unexpected happened. The jar of paint fell on the floor and spread all over. Tim and Sue stopped fighting and looked at the paint on the floor. They saw a big, pretty rainbow. They decided to work together and make more pretty rainbows on paper.

Once upon a time, there was a big truck. The truck liked to load things. One day, the truck saw a big pile of smooth rocks. The truck wanted to load the rocks and take them to a new place. The truck started to load the rocks. It was a fun sight to see. The truck was happy to have a job to do. The smooth rocks felt nice and the truck knew they would be useful. When the truck was done loading the rocks, it drove to a new place. The truck found a nice spot to unload the rocks. The truck was happy it could help. The smooth rocks made a nice path for everyone to walk on. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked by a big place called a university. Tim asked, "Mom, what is that big place?" His mom said, "That is a university. One day, you will go there to learn many things." As they walked, Tim saw a big tree. He thought there must be something fun to find behind it. "Mom, can I go look behind that big tree?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim ran to the tree and looked behind it. He found a big, shiny rock. Tim was very happy. He showed the rock to his mom. She smiled and said, "That is a very special find, Tim. You can keep it and remember the big university. Maybe one day, you will go there and learn about rocks like this one." Tim hugged his mom and held the rock tight, excited for the day he would go to the big university.

Jake peeked through the window. He could see his family outside, playing in the cold snow. He smiled as he watched them make a snowman and then roll around in the snow. He wanted to join them, but he knew it was too cold outside. He soon saw his family digging in the snow. They were building a snow cave! They worked together to make the perfect size spot. Jake wanted to go inside, but he was too scared. Suddenly, he heard his dad calling him from outside. They all told him it was warm inside the cave, so Jake decided to give it a try. He ran through the wintry air and peeked inside. To his surprise, it was just as warm as his home! Jake looked around and giggled. He had made an amazing discovery. Every time it was cold outside, he could come to this special snow cave and stay warm. He hugged his family and ran back home, planning for the next time he could peek inside the snow cave.
