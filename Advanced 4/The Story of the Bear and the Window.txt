Title: The Story of the Bear and the Window

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was three years old. She was playing with her favourite toy, a warm teddy bear. She brought the teddy bear over to the window and began to hang it outside. She was careful not to let the bear's heels bump against the window. The little girl talked to the bear: "Let's swing together, Bear". She held the bear in both arms and began to swing it back and forth. She was delighted by how much fun it was. Suddenly, the bear's arm came off and flew away. "Oh no," said the little girl, "let's go find it." So, the little girl and the bear started walking. They looked all over, until finally they found the arm in some tall grass. It was a little cold and wet but the little girl was glad she had found it. She carefully hung the arm back onto the bear and gave it a hug. "That's better," she said, feeling the warm teddy bear tight in her arms.

Tommy was feeling very miserable. All his friends at nursery had nice toys with special labels. He didn't have any labels on his toys. One day, Tommy saw an old jar at the back of his cupboard. He took it outside to show his friends. He asked them to help him put labels on the jar. Tommy was so happy when the labels were stuck on the jar. His friends showed him how to keep the jar safe. Tommy made sure the jar stayed in a safe place. Every time Tommy wanted to play he kept the jar safe. The labels made Tommy much happier. He hugged his jar of labels and smiled. He never felt miserable again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fit boy who loved to run and play. One day, he found a magazine on the floor. The magazine had many pictures of animals in it. Tim liked the pictures a lot. Tim went to his friend, Sue, and said, "Look, I found a magazine. It has animals in it. Let me show you." Sue was happy and said, "Yes, please show me the animals." Tim and Sue sat down and looked at the magazine together. They saw pictures of a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a funny monkey. They laughed and had fun talking about the animals. Tim and Sue were very happy that they could share the magazine and learn about animals together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane who had a toy bear named Buddy. Buddy was very filthy because Jane played with him all the time. One day, Jane decided it was time to clean Buddy. Jane asked her mom, "Mom, how do I clean Buddy?" Her mom said, "You can use a brush to clean him." Jane went to find a brush and started to brush Buddy's fur. Buddy started to look less filthy. After brushing Buddy, Jane was very happy. She hugged her clean toy bear and said, "Now you're not filthy anymore, Buddy!" From then on, Jane decided to brush Buddy every week to keep him clean.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. He would run and jump all day long. Spot had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy who lived next door. They were best friends. One day, Spot and Tim were playing near the big steps. Spot was running very fast with his ball. Tim called out to Spot, "Remember to be careful near the steps!" But Spot did not hear Tim. He was having too much fun. Spot ran faster and faster. Suddenly, he tripped on the steps. He fell down and hurt his leg. Tim was very sad. He tried to help Spot, but it was too late. Spot's leg was badly hurt. He could not play with his ball anymore. Tim and Spot were both very sad.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a kind girl named Lily. She liked to comfort her friends when they were sad. Lily had a smooth, soft blanket that she loved to share with others. She would wrap it around them and give them a big hug to make them feel better. One day, Lily met an old lady who was crying. The old lady said, "I lost my book of history. It was very special to me." Lily wanted to help the old lady, so she took her smooth blanket and wrapped it around the old lady. She gave her a big hug and said, "Don't worry, we will find your book together." Lily and the old lady looked all around the town until they found the history book under a tree. The old lady was so happy and thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, they became good friends and Lily continued to comfort others with her smooth blanket and kind heart.
