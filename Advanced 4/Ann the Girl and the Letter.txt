Title: Ann the Girl and the Letter

One day, a girl named Ann found a letter on the ground. The letter was big and red. It was a bossy letter. The letter told Ann to show it to her friend Tom. Ann went to Tom's house. She showed him the bossy letter. Tom looked at the letter and said, "This is a very bossy letter. Let's show it to our teacher, Miss Jane." Ann and Tom went to school and showed the bossy letter to Miss Jane. Miss Jane smiled and said, "This letter is just a joke. It is not really bossy. You can have fun and play now." Ann and Tom laughed and played with their friends. They were happy they showed the letter to Miss Jane.

One day, Tom went for a walk in the park. He saw a big slide that was blue. Tom liked to play on the slide, so he ran to it. He climbed up and slid down. It was fun! Tom saw his friend, Sam, by the slide. They played together, going up and down the slide. They laughed and had a good time. Tom and Sam were happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The blue slide started to move! It walked away with big steps. Tom and Sam were surprised. They followed the walking slide and found a new park. The new park had more slides, swings, and fun things to play on. Tom and Sam played in the new park all day, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. His name was Tommy. Tommy was three-years-old. His mummy had bought him a sword made of wood. Tommy loved it, he took it everywhere. One day, just as Tommy and his mum were about to cross a bridge, he dropped the sword. “Oh no!” he said sadly. Mum said “Don’t worry Tommy, these things happen. I’ll help you build a new one.” So they went to the park, collected some sticks and started to work. It was not an ordinary sword, they became really creative. They created something totally different and magnificent. Mummy said “Now you have a very special sword, so it’s important to take care of it. You will now be the only one with a sword like this!” The moral of the story is that everything can be changed if you put some effort into it. You can turn something ordinary into something extraordinary with a bit of imagination. It also teaches us to take of our possessions and be grateful for what we have.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to play with dough. One day, she made a big ball of dough and wanted to show her mom. "Look, Mommy!" Lisa said, holding the dough. "I made a big ball!" Her mom was very happy and told her it was a good job. Lisa wanted to play with her dough outside. She asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said yes, but be careful. Lisa went outside and saw a little bird. The bird was very hungry. She thought the bird would like some dough. Lisa was a bit sorry to give away her dough, but she wanted to help the bird. So, she released a small piece of dough for the bird to eat. The bird was very happy and thanked Lisa. "Thank you, Lisa!" said the bird. "You are so kind!" Lisa felt good because she helped the bird. She went back inside to tell her mom what happened. Her mom was very proud of her.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable big elephant named Elly. Elly had a trunk that she used to help her friends. She lived in a green forest with many trees and animals. One sunny day, Elly was walking with her friend, a small bird named Tweet. They saw a big box that was closed. Tweet asked Elly to open the box with her trunk. Elly said, "Okay, I will try." Elly used her trunk to open the box. Inside the box, they found many yummy fruits like apples, bananas, and oranges. Elly and Tweet shared the fruits with all their friends in the forest. Everyone was happy and thanked Elly for being so dependable.
