Title: Mommy and the Sarah

One day, Mommy and little Sarah went to the store. Sarah was excited. She looked around all the colorful things, but suddenly her eyes lit up, and she shouted, "Mommy, buy! Buy!" Mommy smiled and asked, "What do you want to buy, Sarah?" Sarah ran to the back of the store, where there was a large bright red rail. Mommy said, "That looks like fun. Do you want to buy the rail?" Sarah shook her head yes. Mommy paid for the rail and they went home. Sarah was so excited that she ran ahead and started running all around the house. Mommy asked, "What are you doing, Sarah?" Then Sarah smiled and pointed to the red rail. Mommy understood and started setting up the rail. When it was ready, Sarah grabbed onto it and flew around the house, laughing and smiling all the while. Mommy smiled and knew that this was the perfect choice to buy.

Once upon a time, in a messy room, there was a little cushion. The cushion was sad because it was always on the floor. It wanted to fly like the birds it saw outside the window. One day, a kind bird saw the cushion and wanted to help it fly. The bird picked up the cushion with its beak and started to fly. The cushion was so happy to be in the sky, flying with the bird. They flew and flew, and the cushion saw many things it had never seen before. When it was time to go home, the bird gently put the cushion back in the room. The cushion was not sad anymore, because it had a new friend and knew that it could fly too.

Once upon a time, there was a cute prince. He lived in a big house with his mom and dad. The cute prince loved to walk and play outside. One day, the cute prince went for a walk. He saw a small cat. The cat was sad. The cute prince wanted to help the cat. So, he picked up the cat and took it home. At home, the cute prince gave the cat some food. The cat was happy. The cute prince and the cat played together. They became best friends. The cute prince and the cat walked and played every day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, Daisy went for a walk in the park. She saw lots of happy people and some pretty flowers. Then she saw a funny insect. It was big and yellow and looked like it was dancing. Daisy laughed with delight! She ran around the park with the insect, chasing each other and having lots of fun. But soon Daisy got tired. The insect flew away and Daisy lay down on the grass to have a rest. When Daisy woke up, the sun was setting. Daisy was feeling happy and refreshed. She waved goodbye to the pretty flowers and the happy people and then walked home. On the way, Daisy remembered the funny insect and started to laugh.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a big book. The book had many pages. Tim liked to look at the pages every day. One day, he saw a page with a big tree. It was a tree that could yield many yummy fruits. Tim said, "I want to find this tree." So, he went outside and started to look for the tree. He walked and walked until he found a tree with fruits. But there was a rude bird on the tree. The bird said, "You cannot have these fruits. They are mine." Tim was sad, but he did not want to be rude too. He said, "Can we share the fruits, please?" The bird thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, we can share." So, Tim and the bird took turns picking fruits from the tree. They both were happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to build towers with his blocks. One day, he wanted to build the biggest tower ever. So, he found a long rope to help him. He tied the rope to the top of the tower, so it would not fall down. Tim's friend, Sara, came over to play. She saw the big tower and the rope. She wanted to help Tim build, but she did not know about the rope. She pulled a block from the bottom, and the tower started to fall. Sara was very ashamed. But then, something unexpected happened! The rope caught the tower before it hit the ground. The tower swung back and forth, but it did not break. Tim and Sara laughed and clapped. They were so happy that the rope saved their tower. From that day on, they always built towers together, and they never forgot the rope.
