Title: Sammy and the Puppy

Sammy heard a noise. He looked around and saw there was a toilet on the ground. He wondered why it was there. He had never seen a toilet outside of the bathroom before. Suddenly, he heard a noise coming from the toilet. He was a little scared, but he wanted to see what was inside. He looked inside the toilet and found a puppy! The puppy was shivering and it looked sad. Sammy was very thoughtful and decided to take the puppy home. He picked up the puppy and gave it some food and water, and the puppy seemed much happier. From that day on, Sammy and the puppy were best friends and did everything together.

Once upon a time, a jolly bear and a jolly fox went on an adventure together. The bear was hoping to find something special, but the fox just wanted to have fun. When they got to the top of a big hill, the bear saw a beautiful shining wake. It was so pretty, like a rainbow in the sun. The fox walked over and picked it up. He couldn't believe his eyes! Soon they were both so excited they just had to take it home. When they arrived, the fox quickly ran into a nearby cave and hid the wake. He said it was his secret and he wouldn't tell anyone! The bear was sad. He thought it was wrong to keep the wake all to himself. He tried to explain it to the fox, but he just didn't seem to understand. The bear was so sad that he left and never came back. The moral of the story is that it's important to share and be kind. Greed and selfishness never lead to good things. Hope for the best and learn from our mistakes.

One day, a boy named Tim found a light rag on the ground. He liked the rag and wanted to keep it. But his friend, Sam, saw the rag too. They both wanted the rag and did not know what to do. Tim said, "Let's split the rag, so we both can have it." Sam did not like the idea. He wanted the whole rag for himself. This made Tim sad and they started to fight. A big dog came along and saw the light rag. The dog took the rag and ran away. Tim and Sam stopped fighting. They learned that fighting was not good. They said sorry to each other and stayed friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to sleep all day. He would curl up in his bed and dream of sugar. One day, Spot woke up and found a big jar of sugar on the floor. Spot's friend, Cat, came over and saw the sugar too. Cat said, "Can I have some sugar, Spot?" Spot thought for a moment. He did not want to be selfish. So, Spot said, "Yes, Cat. We can share the sugar." Spot and Cat shared the sugar and played all day. They were very happy. That night, they both went to sleep with big smiles on their faces. They knew that sharing made them better friends.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Little Lou. She was obedient to her mom and dad, but she also had a desire to wander. One day her mom and dad said, "Little Lou, today you can wander over the hills. But before you go, we need you to take this bottle of wine to Grandma's house." So without hesitation, Little Lou obediently grabbed the bottle and set off on her journey. After many hours of walking, Little Lou saw Grandma's house in the distance. She smiled with joy, until suddenly a fierce storm began. She started to worry, but then she remembered the bottle in her bag. Little Lou opened the bottle of wine and shared it with the animals she met along the way. Suddenly they all stopped in their tracks, and Little Lou knew the storm was over! Grandma waved from the window, thankful to see Little Lou safe and happy. The moral of the story: when you wander, be obedient and always bring a bottle of wine!
