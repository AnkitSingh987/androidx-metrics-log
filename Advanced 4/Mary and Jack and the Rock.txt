Title: Mary and Jack and the Rock

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Mary and Jack. They were walking in the park. Jack pointed at a big rock. He said to Mary, "Let's roll the rock down the hill!" Mary agreed and they pushed the rock together. As the rock rolled down the hill, it became faster and faster. They laughed and cheered as the rock disappeared from sight. When they reached the end of the hill, they gasped. The rock landed in the pond and made a big splash! They cheered and hugged each other. But then Jack's stomach began to hurt. He had tried too hard to roll the rock and now he was sore. Mary put her arm around Jack. She said, "We still succeeded, Jack. Don't be hurt." Jack smiled and said, "But it was fun! And we did it together." Mary smiled too. They had succeeded and showed that they could do anything as long as they stuck together. The two friends went home with happy hearts.

One day, a little dog named Max was walking near a big building. He saw a tall man with a hat. The man looked at Max and said, "You can't be here, little dog. This is the original building and no dogs are allowed." Max was sad and started to tremble. He wanted to see the building too. He thought of a plan to get inside. Max saw a big box and went inside it. He waited for someone to take the box into the building. A kind lady saw the box and said, "Oh, this must go inside the building." She picked up the box and took Max inside. Max was so happy to see the inside of the original building. But suddenly, the tall man with the hat saw Max and said, "You can't be here! You must leave now!" Max knew he had to go. He left the building and went back to his home. Even though he was sad, Max knew that he had a great adventure. And he would never forget the day he saw the inside of the original building.

Once upon a time there was a mysterious stitch. It was so mysterious that nobody knew who put it there. A group of brave children decided to try and uncover the secret of the mysterious stitch. They weighed it and measured it and looked at it closely. But the more they weighed it, the heavier it became. Soon it became too heavy for the children to carry and each of them started to feel very tired. Eventually they had to leave the mysterious stitch, and slowly walked away. They never got to find out the secret of the mysterious stitch, it remained a secret forever!

Molly was in her bedroom. The room was very wide. She wanted to use something fun. She found a big box full of toys. Molly took out the toys and started to play. She used the blocks to build a tower. It was a very wide tower. She also used her favourite doll to act out a story. Molly loved to use her bedroom for all kinds of fun activities. She would spend hours in there, playing and exploring.

Once upon a time, a little boat wanted to sail on a big lake. The boat was ignorant, but it wanted to find wonder in the world. The boat had a friend, a small fish. The fish said, "Let's sail together and find the wonder!" They started to sail on the lake. The sun was hot, and the water was cool. The boat and the fish were happy. But then, they saw a big rock in the water. The boat was scared. The fish said, "Don't worry, I will help you go around the rock." The boat and the fish went around the rock. They were almost at the end of the lake. They saw something shiny in the water. They went closer and saw a big, beautiful pearl. The pearl was the wonder they were looking for! The boat and the fish were so happy to find the pearl. They sailed back home with their new treasure, ready to share the wonder with their friends.
