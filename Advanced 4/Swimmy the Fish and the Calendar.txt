Title: Swimmy the Fish and the Calendar

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Swimmy. Swimmy loved to swim all day long. He was a very alert fish, always looking around and paying attention to what was happening in the water. One day, Swimmy found a big thing in the water. It was a calendar! Swimmy looked at the calendar and saw lots of pictures. Each picture was for a different month. Swimmy liked the pictures a lot. Swimmy decided to show the calendar to his friends. He swam fast to find his friends and show them what he found. His friends liked the calendar too. They all took turns looking at the pictures. Then, they swam happily together, knowing they had a special calendar to look at each month.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bobo. Bobo lived in a dry field with many other animals. One day, Bobo saw his friend, a little bird, suffer because she was very thirsty. Bobo said, "Don't worry, little bird, I will help you find water." They walked and walked until they found a small pond. The little bird drank the water and felt much better. She said, "Thank you, Bobo. You are a great friend." From that day on, all the animals in the dry field learned to help each other when they suffered. They all became good friends and lived happily together. The moral of the story is that helping others makes everyone happy.

One day, a dog named Max saw a ball. It was the only available ball in the park. Max wanted to play with it. He picked up the ball in his mouth and ran to his friend, a cat named Lily. "Look, I have a ball!" said Max. "Let's play!" Lily was in shock. She had never seen a ball like that before. "Wow! Can I try?" she asked. Max dropped the ball and let Lily play with it too. They had a lot of fun playing with the ball. They took turns and played until they were tired. Then they lay down in the sun and smiled. They were happy to have found the ball and to be such good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Susie. She was very small and only three years old. One day, her mom encouraged her to explore the forest. Susie was a bit scared but mom said that she could go as long as she stayed near the path. So Susie took a few brave steps forward. She was wondering around when she saw a big, white mushroom! She was very amazed and curious. She politely asked the mushroom what it was doing in the forest and the mushroom said, “I’m here to teach you a very important lesson.” Susie asked, “What lesson?” The mushroom said, “Always keep exploring. Don’t be afraid to try new things and visit new places. Always believe in yourself.” Susie thanked the mushroom and danced around the forest, feeling excited and brave. The moral of the story is that it’s important to explore and be adventurous. With the help and encouragement of our parents, we can make new discoveries and learn new things.

Lila wanted to improve her drawing of a sun. She asked her big brother Ben for help. Ben was helpful and kind. He showed her how to make a circle and some rays with a yellow crayon. "Like this, Lila. See? A sun is round and bright. It makes us warm and happy." Ben said. Lila tried to copy Ben's sun. She made a circle and some rays, but they were not very neat. She was not happy with her drawing. "Ben, my sun is not good. It is messy and ugly." Lila said. Ben hugged Lila and smiled. He said, "No, Lila, your sun is not ugly. It is beautiful and unique. It is your sun. You made it with your own hands. You can improve it if you want, but I like it the way it is." Lila looked at her sun again. She saw that it had different shapes and sizes of rays. She saw that it had some spots and lines. She saw that it was different from Ben's sun, but it was also special. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother. I like my sun too. It is my sun."
