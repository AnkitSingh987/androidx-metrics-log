Title: Nellie and the Zebra

Nellie and her zebra were outside playing in the snow. The snow was cold and sparkly, and they wanted to make the most of it. The zebra had brought a snowball and was rolling it around. Nellie was enjoying the experience, laughing and clapping her hands. Suddenly the zebra got very tired. "I'm so sleepy. I'm going to take a nap," the zebra said. Nellie was worried. "No! No napping! We need to keep playing in the snow!" But the zebra refused to wake up. Nellie was determined to stop the zebra from napping. She picked up some snow and threw it in the zebra's face! This made the zebra open its eyes and shake off the snow. "What was that for?" the zebra asked. Nellie smiled. "I wanted you to stay awake so we can keep playing in the snow!" The zebra understood, and they started rolling the snowball together. The zebra never tried to nap again.

Once upon a time, there were two little twins named Tim and Tom. They loved to play and laugh together. They had so much fun all day long. One day, their mom asked them to help her mix some cake batter. Tim and Tom were very excited. They put on their aprons and got ready to mix. Tim and Tom took turns mixing the batter. They mixed and mixed until it was smooth. Then, they poured the batter into a pan. They watched as their mom put the pan in the oven. Soon, the cake was ready. The little twins were so happy. They had helped make a yummy cake. They knew they could mix and bake together, just like their mom. And from that day on, Tim and Tom loved to help in the kitchen.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the market with her mom. The market was big and full of people. They went to buy some fruits, like apples and bananas. Mia loved going to the market because it was always fun and colorful. At the market, Mia saw a light blue chair. She wanted to sit on it. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I sit on the chair, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia, you can sit on the chair, but only for a little bit." Mia sat on the light blue chair and looked around the market. She saw people selling toys, clothes, and yummy food. Mia and her mom bought some fruits and other things they needed. They had a great time at the market. After a while, Mia's mom said, "Mia, it's time to go home now." Mia got up from the chair and they went back home, happy with their day at the market.

Once there was a sneaky squirrel named Sammy. Sammy loved finding and hoarding nuts from all around. One day, Sammy decided to dress up in disguise by hiding his fur behind a dress. He thought nobody would recognize him, and he could collect nuts all day. He thought, 'Nobody will know the nuts are mine!' Sammy scurried around collecting nuts, but soon he noticed a family of birds watching him curiously. He ignored them and kept collecting until he had a pile of nuts so big he couldn't carry them all. Finally, the birds flew closer and chirped noisily. Sammy realized they were scolding him! He felt guilty because they were the owners of the nuts he had been collecting. Sammy was embarrassed too, because he was in a dress! He scurried away, feeling very guilty and, of course, without a single nut.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Lucy. She loved to dream about big things. At night, she would lay in her bed and dream of flying high in the sky like a bird. Lucy had a big dream in her head and a happy smile on her face. One day, Lucy saw a big tree. She thought, "If I climb the tree, I can be close to the sky and my dream can come true." So Lucy started to climb the tree. Her stomach felt funny, but she kept going up and up. The tree was very tall, and Lucy was very brave. But then, Lucy slipped and fell down from the tree. She hurt her leg and cried a lot. Her dream of flying high in the sky did not come true. Lucy was sad, and her stomach still felt funny. From that day on, Lucy was more careful when she climbed trees.
