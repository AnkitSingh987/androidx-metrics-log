Title: Tim the Boy and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a penny in his hand. Tim was very hungry. He wanted to buy a snack with his penny. He walked to the store. At the store, Tim saw a big jar of cookies. He tried to buy a cookie with his penny, but the store lady said, "No, you need more pennies." Tim was sad and walked back home. On his way home, Tim saw a tree. He thought, "Maybe I can shake the tree and find more pennies." He shook the tree, and to his surprise, cookies fell down! Tim was so happy. He ate the cookies and was not hungry anymore. The tree was a magic cookie tree!

Once upon a time, there was a heavy box. A little boy named Tim found the box in his room. He wanted to know what was inside. Tim tried to open the box, but it was too heavy for him. He asked his mom for help, but she said it was time for a quiz. The quiz had three questions. If Tim answered them right, the box would shrink and become light. Tim was excited and said yes. His mom asked the first question, "What is two plus two?" Tim knew the answer was four. The box shrank a little. Next, his mom asked, "What color is the sky?" Tim said, "Blue!" The box shrank more. The last question was, "What do you say when you want something?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Please!" The box shrank one last time and became very small. Now, Tim could open the box easily. Inside the box, he found a toy car. He was very happy. The moral of the story is to always be polite and ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The fat cat liked to crawl under the big tree. One day, the fat cat crawled under the tree and saw a small box. The fat cat opened the box and found a story book. The fat cat liked the story and read it out loud. The story was about a little girl who played with her friends in the sun. But then, something unexpected happened. The fat cat turned into the little girl from the story! Now the girl could play with her friends in the sun and not crawl under the tree anymore. And they all played happily together.

One day, a thin cat named Tom went to play outside. Tom saw his friend, a small dog named Max. They liked to play together. Tom and Max ran and jumped in the sun. They enjoyed playing all day. "Let's go find a shower to get clean," said Tom. Max agreed, and they looked for a shower. They found a shower in a park. They went inside and Tom turned it on. The water was cold and they laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The shower started to talk! "Hello, Tom and Max!" it said. The two friends were very surprised. They never heard a shower talk before. The talking shower made them laugh even more, and they had a great day.

John was a smart pupil. He loved to study and was always curious to learn more. One day his teacher told the class they were going to do something special. "Today you are going to study with a super smart tutor," she said. John was excited! When the tutor arrived, he smiled at the class and said "Hello pupils, let’s begin.” John listened closely as the tutor talked about the importance of studying. John asked lots of questions and it was clear he was very interested. The tutor smiled and said, “John, it’s wonderful that you’re so eager to learn. You are an extremely smart pupil!” John felt proud and continued to study with enthusiasm. He knew that if he kept up the hard work, he would learn even more.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to decorate his home with pretty things. One day, Max found a long cut on the ground. He thought it would be a good place to put some pretty things. Max went to find flowers, leaves, and sticks to decorate the cut. He put them all around the cut, making it look very nice. Max was happy with his work and went to show his friends. But, when Max came back with his friends, the cut was gone! Instead, there was a beautiful river with water flowing in it. The flowers, leaves, and sticks Max used to decorate were floating on the water. Max and his friends were surprised, but they loved the new river. They played in the water all day long and had lots of fun.
