Title: Sidney and the Dictionary

Sidney was a very cheerful three year old girl. She loved to explore her world and learn new things. One day she asked her daddy for a dictionary. He was so excited to give her one and happily handed it to her. "What do I do with it?" Sidney asked her dad. "Open it and look up words you don't understand. You can reach many new ideas, knowledge and understanding!" said dad. Sidney smiled and began flipping through the dictionary. She found many funny-sounding words that she hadn't heard before and was excited to learn what they meant. She would often ask her dad what each word means and Daddy would explain them as best he can. The more Sidney read, the more she discovered and learnt. Before long, she was able to read the words in the dictionary all on her own. She was so cheerful and proud of what she had learned. The dictionary was Sidney's favourite tool for discovery and she kept it close to her heart. She kept learning new words and discovering new parts of her world - and the dictionary was always there to help her reach them.

Mia was feeling very nervous. She was going to a big university for the first time. It was a big place, and she felt scared that it would be too difficult for her. But when she arrived, she realized it wasn't so bad after all! The people had organized everything into separate buildings and departments. There were plenty of people around to help her find her way. Mia soon became so excited about being at the university that her nerves disappeared. She was impressed at how everything had been organized. She even made some new friends and started to feel like part of the university. Mia found that the university was the perfect place for her. She was sure that she was going to have a great time exploring everything!

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy who wanted to go to the circus. She couldn't wait to jump, spin and twirl on the trapeze and watch the clowns. When Lucy arrived at the circus, she was so excited. She looked around, but she couldn't see any clowns anywhere. She took a few steps and saw a weird-looking clown perched in a tree. Lucy stopped and said, “Hi! What are you doing in the tree?” The clown just giggled and said, “I like to look at the circus from up here!” Lucy watched the clown and thought it was so weird. He wasn’t like the other clowns she had seen before. She stepped closer to get a better look but the clown jumped out of the tree, said “Bye!”, and ran away. Lucy was very surprised and shook her head with a laugh. She watched the weird clown until he was out of sight, then ran off to watch the other circus acts.

Jenny was in her room, feeling naughty. She was frowning at the wall when her mom walked in. Mom saw Jenny frowning and asked, "What's wrong?". Jenny said she was feeling naughty. Mom told Jenny that she still loved her even if she felt naughty sometimes. Jenny smiled, and she and Mom hugged. Then Jenny went back to her room to play with her toys. She wasn't feeling naughty anymore.

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom found some cheese. He liked cheese a lot. He wanted to eat it. But the cheese was high on a shelf. Tom tried to get the cheese, but he was clumsy and fell. Tom saw a big dog named Max. Tom asked Max, "Can you help me get the cheese?" Max said, "Yes, I can help you. But you must rub my back first." Tom rubbed Max's back. Max was happy. Max tried to get the cheese for Tom. But Max was also clumsy. He knocked the cheese off the shelf. The cheese fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tom and Max were sad. They could not eat the cheese. The end.

One day, a small cat named Tom went for a walk. Tom was a nice cat with big ears. He liked to play and jump high. While walking, he saw a big tree. The tree had many high branches. Tom wanted to climb the tree. Tom started to climb the tree. He used his sharp claws to go up. Tom climbed higher and higher. He felt very happy. As he went up, he saw a big bird. The bird was singing a pretty song. Tom wanted to be friends with the bird. Tom jumped to the branch where the bird was. He said, "Hi, bird! I like your song." The bird was happy, too. They became friends. Tom gained a new friend in the tree. He listened with his big ears to the bird's song. They played together in the high tree.
