Title: Zara and the Zebra

Once upon a time, a zebra named Zara lived in a big forest. Zara was a brilliant zebra with many friends. She liked to play and run with her friends all day. Zara was happy, but she wished for something more. She wanted to become something different. One day, Zara found a big, shiny rock. The rock was magic. It could make her become anything she wanted. Zara thought and thought about what she wanted to be. Then, she decided. She wanted to become a big, strong lion. She touched the rock and, just like that, she turned into a lion. Zara felt very strong and powerful as a lion. She went to show her friends, but they were scared. They did not know she was their friend Zara. They ran away from her. Zara felt sad and missed her friends. She went back to the magic rock and wished to become a zebra again. In a flash, she was a brilliant zebra once more. Zara realized that being herself was the best thing to be. From that day on, she was happy to be a zebra and play with her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was very stubborn. One day, she wanted to dance on a big stage. She loved to dance and twirl around. Lucy put on her pretty dress and went to the stage. She was so excited to dance. But when she started to dance, she stumbled. Lucy's foot hurt a little, but she did not want to stop dancing. Lucy tried to dance again. She was very stubborn and did not want to give up. She stumbled again, but this time she did not fall. Lucy kept dancing and soon she was twirling on the stage, happy as can be.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma lived in a small house with her mom and dad. They lived in a big country. The country was called Independent Land. In Independent Land, everyone could do things by themselves. One day, Emma's friend Tom came to play. Tom arrived at Emma's house with a big smile on his face. He said, "Hi Emma! Let's play outside!" Emma was happy to see her friend. They went outside and played with a ball. Emma and Tom played all day in the sun. They laughed and had fun. When it was time for Tom to go home, Emma said, "Bye Tom! Come play again soon!" Tom smiled and said, "I will, Emma! Independent Land is the best country to play in!" And they both knew they were lucky to live in such a special place.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable bull named Billy. Billy lived on a big farm with lots of other animals. They all liked to play together in the sun. One day, Billy was feeling sad. He didn't know why, but he just couldn't be happy. His friend, a little bird named Chirpy, saw that Billy was sad. Chirpy flew down and asked, "Why are you sad, Billy?" Billy looked at Chirpy and said, "I don't know. I just feel sad today." Chirpy thought for a moment and then said, "Let me tell you a funny story. Maybe it will make you feel better." Chirpy told Billy a story about a silly monkey who liked to dance and make funny faces. As Chirpy told the story, Billy started to smile. By the end of the story, Billy was laughing so hard that he forgot all about being sad. From that day on, whenever Billy felt sad, Chirpy would tell him a funny story to make him feel better. They were the best of friends, and they knew that they could always count on each other to bring a smile to their faces.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man named Tom. He lived in a small house with his cat, Fluffy. Tom loved to take photos of his cat and keep them in a big book. One day, Tom heard a knock on his door. He opened it and saw a little girl. The girl said, "Hi, I am Lucy. I saw your cat and I want to take a photo too." Tom smiled and said, "Sure, you can take a photo of Fluffy." Lucy took a photo of Fluffy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" She went home and showed the photo to her friends. They all loved the photo of Fluffy. But then, a strong wind blew the photo away. Lucy and her friends tried to catch it, but they couldn't. They were sad and the photo was gone forever.
