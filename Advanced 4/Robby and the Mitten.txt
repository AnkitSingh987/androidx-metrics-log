Title: Robby and the Mitten

Robby was outside playing in the snow. He had a red mitten on his hand and he was smiling. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling. "Robby, watch out!" It was his mom. She was running to him and waving her hands. Robby was confused. "What is it, Mommy?" "I'm here to protect you," she said. "Let me put your other mitten on so you won't get cold." Robby didn't understand why, but he let Mommy put on the other mitten. After she was done, Robby was feeling warm and safer. He still didn't understand why Mommy was so worried, but he was happy to be safe with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and keep them clean. One day, Tim found some new equipment in the park. It was big and colorful. He wanted to play with it. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can we play with the new equipment?" Sam said, "Yes, but we must count all the toys first." They started to count the toys. One, two, three, four, five. They had five toys. But then, another boy came to the park. He wanted to play with the equipment too. He did not want to share. Tim and Sam were sad. They wanted to play with the new equipment. Tim said, "Let's all play together!" The other boy thought about it and agreed. They all played with the new equipment and had lots of fun. In the end, they learned that sharing was better than fighting.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy a cord for their TV. Lucy was very excited because she loved to watch her favorite cartoon on the TV. When they got to the store, Lucy saw a red cord and a blue cord. She asked her mom, "Can we get the blue cord, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can get the blue cord." Lucy was very happy. They took the blue cord and went to the man at the front of the store to pay. Lucy's mom gave the man some money to pay for the cord. The man said, "Thank you!" and gave them the cord. Lucy and her mom went home very fast because they wanted to watch the cartoon. When they got home, they put the new cord in the TV and the cartoon started. Lucy and her mom sat on the couch and watched the cartoon together. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Sam and Mark. Sam loved to play in the room and would strike things with his ball. One day, they were playing in the rain and Sam’s ball flew into a wet puddle. Sam and Mark ran over to the puddle and Mark was scared, warning Sam not to touch the wet ball. But Sam didn’t listen and tried to strike the ball out of the puddle. He made several attempts and kept missing. Eventually, his ball flew out of the puddle and Sam smiled, proud of his success. The happy moment was short-lived when Sam realised that the ball had gone flying into a window of a nearby house. Sam and Mark looked at each other in shock and were too scared to do anything. Suddenly, the door opened and an old man appeared. He said sternly, “Why did you strike my window?” Sam and Mark were too scared to even reply, but the old man realised that the boys were sorry and he then said kindly, “Next time if you want to play in the room, play safe.” The moral of this story is that it is important to always be careful with things that can cause damage and harm, like Sam’s ball.

Once upon a time there was a brave hero who lives in a small cottage. He would go out every day to help people in his village. One day, he heard a loud scream coming from the forest. He quickly entered the forest to find out what had happened. There, he saw a terrible monster was chasing a small child. The hero quickly ran towards the monster and shouted, "Stop! Don't hurt the kid!" The monster ignored him and kept repeating, "I want to hurt him!" The hero was very brave and he tried his best to protect the child. He kept shouting to the monster, "Stop! You terrify the kid!" Finally, the hero was able to save the kid from the monster. He hugged the child and said, "It is alright now, the monster won't hurt you anymore." The kid was so relieved and the hero walked him back home safely. The whole village thanked the hero for his courage. From then on, everyone called him the "Hero of the Village and nobody ever forgot his brave deeds!
