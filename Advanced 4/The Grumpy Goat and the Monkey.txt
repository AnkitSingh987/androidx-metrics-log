Title: The Grumpy Goat and the Monkey

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy goat, who lived on the edge of a tall and rocky mountain. Everywhere he went, he looked very grumpy and scowled at everyone. One day, a monkey was out searching for food when he stumbled across the grumpy goat. The goat asked the monkey in a not-so-friendly way, “What are you doing here?” The monkey smiled and said, “I am looking for something to eat.” The goat grumbled and said, “Well, if you don’t leave right now, I will grow two big horns and ram you off this mountain.” The monkey, not wanting to get hurt, quickly ran off to the forest. The monkey returned to the mountain a few days later and he noticed something different about the goat. Growing from the goat’s head were two big horns. The monkey asked in surprise, “What are those?” The goat sadly replied, “I grew them because I was so grumpy all the time.” The moral of the story is that if you don’t learn to be nicer to those around you, you might end up losing friends and getting stuck with horns on your head!

Once there were two siblings, Tom and Jane. They both went to visit their Dad's lab every day. Tom liked the lab a lot, but one day there was something strange. Jane noticed that something was different in the lab and warned Tom: "It seems like something is not right Tom. The lab is too noisy." Tom didn't take her very seriously. The next day the siblings noticed a big hole in the lab. Jane was so angry and asked their Dad why the hole was there. He calmly replied: "I had to bury all the noisy machines in the lab." The siblings were so surprised because they hadn't noticed all the noisy machines before. They realised that Jane's warning was right all along. Even though they couldn't see the noisy machines anymore, they knew they had been there.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a comfortable mug. The mug was soft and nice to hold. Tim liked the mug a lot. He wanted to play with it. Tim saw his friend, Sue, outside. "Sue, look at my new mug!" he said. Sue liked the mug too. "Let's play catch with the mug!" she said. Tim and Sue began to throw the mug to each other. As they played, their friend, Sam, joined them. "Can I play too?" Sam asked. "Yes, let's all throw the mug!" Tim said. They had lots of fun throwing the comfortable mug to each other.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat. The cat loved to watch films. One day, the cat found a new film. The cat was very happy. The cat said to his friend, the dog, "Let's watch the film together!" The dog said, "Okay, but I heard this film is scary." The cat said, "I am not scared!" They watched the film. The cat and the dog were scared. The film had a big, bad wolf. The wolf was mean. In the end, the wolf ate the cat and the dog. The cat and the dog should have listened to the warning. Now they are gone.

Once upon a time, there was a big house with a warm fireplace. In the house lived a little girl named Lucy and her family. Lucy loved to sit by the fireplace and feel the heat on her face. It was not harsh, but very nice and warm. One day, Lucy saw a small cat near the fireplace. The cat was cold and shivering. Lucy felt sad and wanted to help the cat. She thought hard and tried to remember what her mom did to make her feel warm. Lucy had an idea! She took a soft blanket and wrapped it around the cat. The cat stopped shivering and started to purr. Lucy felt happy that she could help the cat. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat changed into a beautiful fairy! The fairy thanked Lucy for her kindness and said she was a magic cat. She wanted to see if Lucy would help her. As a thank you, the fairy gave Lucy a special gift. From that day on, Lucy could talk to animals! Lucy and her new animal friends lived happily ever after.
