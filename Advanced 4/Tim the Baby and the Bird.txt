Title: Tim the Baby and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a playful baby named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and hear the sounds they made. He had a favorite toy, a little bird that sang when he squeezed it. One day, Tim's mommy told him she had a surprise for him. He was very excited and couldn't wait to see what it was. Tim heard a little sound, but he didn't know what it was. Tim listened very carefully, and the sound was like his toy bird. The next day, Tim's mommy showed him the surprise. It was a real bird in a cage! The bird looked just like Tim's toy bird. Tim was so happy to see the bird and hear it sing. He knew that the sound he heard before was the bird singing. Now Tim had a new friend to play with and listen to all day long.

Tommy loved parades. He liked to see the big cars, the loud music, and the funny people in costumes. He always asked his mom and dad to take him to the parades in town. One day, there was a big parade for Halloween. Tommy wanted to go very much. He asked his mom and dad if they could allow him to go. They said yes, but they had a surprise for him. They had made a costume for him to wear. It was a dinosaur costume, with a green body, a long tail, and a big head. Tommy was not happy. He did not like the costume. He thought it was too big, too hot, and too silly. He did not want to wear it. He said to his mom and dad, "I don't want to be a dinosaur. I want to be a superhero, or a pirate, or a firefighter. Please, let me wear something else." His mom and dad said, "No, Tommy. We made this costume for you with love. We think you look very cute and brave. You should be proud of your costume. Everyone will like it. Come on, let's go to the parade." Tommy did not want to go, but he had no choice. He put on the costume and followed his mom and dad to the parade. He felt very embarrassed. He thought everyone would laugh at him. He wished he could hide. But something unexpected happened. When he got to the parade, he saw that there were many other children wearing costumes. There were superheroes, pirates, firefighters, and also dinosaurs. Some of them were even bigger and sillier than his. They waved at him and smiled. They said, "Hi, dinosaur. You look awesome. Do you want to join us?" Tommy was surprised. He realized that no one was laughing at him. They were admiring him. They thought he was cool. He felt happy. He said, "Yes, I want to join you. Thank you." He joined the other children and walked in the parade. He had a lot of fun. He saw the big cars, the loud music, and the funny people in costumes. He also saw his mom and dad, who were cheering for him. They said, "We are so proud of you, Tommy. You are the best dinosaur ever." Tommy smiled. He was not embarrassed anymore. He was glad he wore the costume. He said, "I love you, mom and dad. And I love parades."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ash. She loved to go outside and play with her friends. One day, Ash saw a beautiful, original toy in the store. She wanted it so much, but she did not have enough money. Ash thought of a plan to get the toy. She decided to take her friend's toy and trade it for the original toy. But when she took her friend's toy, her friend was very sad. Ash felt bad for what she did. She decided to give the toy back to her friend and say sorry. Ash learned that it is not right to take things that are not yours. From that day on, Ash promised to be a good friend and never take things again.

One day, a person went to the store. They wanted to buy some sweet candy. But when they got there, they only found sour candy. The person did not like sour candy. They felt sad. The person asked the store man for help. The store man looked everywhere, but he could not find any sweet candy. The person and the store man were both sad. They did not know what to do. Then, a little girl came into the store. She heard the person and the store man talking. The little girl said, "I have sweet candy at home. You can have some!" The person and the store man were very happy. They all went to the little girl's house and had sweet candy. The person was not sad anymore.
