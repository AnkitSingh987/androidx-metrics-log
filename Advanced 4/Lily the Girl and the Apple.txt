Title: Lily the Girl and the Apple

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sister named Lucy. Lily was very smart. Lucy was smart too. They loved to play together. One day, Lily and Lucy were playing with their toys. Lily said, "Let's play a game. I will say a word and you repeat it." Lucy liked the game and said, "Okay, let's play!" Lily said, "Apple." Lucy repeated, "Apple." Then Lily said, "Dog." Lucy repeated, "Dog." They played the game for a long time. They were happy and laughed a lot. At the end of the day, their mom called them for dinner. Lily and Lucy ran to the table. They told their mom about the game they played. Mom smiled and said, "You two are very intelligent sisters." Lily and Lucy felt proud and happy. They loved playing together and learning new words.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who was very patient and a young girl. The old man wanted to show the girl something special, something he had been waiting to show her for a very long time. One night, they both looked up at the sky, and the old man pointed to a bright, moving star in the sky. "That is a comet," he told her. The old man said that the comet was coming closer and closer, and he said that when it was close enough, he would be able to tell her a secret. The girl thought about this for a long time, and was very excited for the special secret. She watched the comet every night, and waited and waited for the special moment. Finally, the comet was close enough, and the girl ran to tell the old man. He smiled and said the secret was that it would soon be time for the night to end and the morning to come. The girl was so excited by the secret, and then, as they watched the comet moving away, day broke and the night finally came to an end.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They were going to the market to buy food. Tim was very excited because he loved going to the market with his mom. At the market, Tim saw a big, hard ball. He wanted the ball so much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the ball, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We are here to buy food, not toys." Tim was sad and angry. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog came running into the market and took the hard ball in its mouth. The dog ran to Tim and dropped the ball at his feet. Tim's mom laughed and said, "Well, I guess the dog wants you to have the ball." Tim was very happy and they went home with the food and the new ball.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very important dog in his family. He loved to play with his ball and run on the roof of his house. One sunny day, Max was playing on the roof when something unexpected happened. A big bird flew by and took Max's ball away! Max was very sad and didn't know what to do. He didn't want to be punished for losing his ball. Then, a nice cat named Lucy saw Max's sad face and decided to help. Lucy climbed up a tree and jumped onto the roof. She told Max, "Don't worry, I will get your ball back!" Max was very happy and thanked Lucy. Lucy and Max went on an adventure to find the big bird and get the ball back. They found the bird's nest and saw the ball inside. Lucy climbed up the tree and got the ball back for Max. The big bird said sorry and promised not to take Max's ball again. In the end, Max and Lucy became best friends. They played together every day, and Max never had to worry about being punished. The family was very happy to see Max and Lucy play together, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing outside. The sky was full of big, dark clouds. Amy knew this was not a normal day. She heard a loud sound. It was thunder! Amy ran inside her house. Amy's mom said, "It will rain soon. Let's close the windows." They closed the windows together. Amy was a little scared of the thunder, but her mom told her it was okay. The thunder was just a sound in the sky. Soon, the rain started to fall. Amy and her mom watched the rain from the window. They saw a big puddle outside. When the rain stopped, Amy put on her boots. She went outside and jumped in the puddle. The thunder was gone, and Amy had fun playing in the puddles.
