Title: Tim the Boy and the Exercise

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he did not like to exercise. He would always seat on his chair and watch his friends play. Tim's mom told him that exercise is good for him, but he did not want to try. One day, Tim saw a small bird trying to fly. The bird tried and tried, but it could not fly. Tim felt sad for the bird. He wanted to help the bird. Then, Tim's mom told him that the bird needs to exercise its wings to fly. Tim thought about it and decided to help the bird. Tim and the bird started to exercise every day. They would flap their arms and wings together. After some days, the bird could fly high in the sky. Tim was happy to see the bird fly. He also felt strong and healthy because of the exercise. Tim learned that exercise is good for everyone, and it is important to try new things even if you are worried.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a calm little girl named Amy. Amy was very good at minding her mom and dad. She always listened to them and did what they asked. Everyone in the town liked Amy because she was so sweet and kind. One day, there was a big earthquake in the town. The ground shook and many things fell down. Amy was scared, but she remembered what her mom and dad told her about staying calm. So, she took a deep breath and stayed very still until the shaking stopped. After the earthquake, Amy saw that some people were hurt and needed help. She went to them and helped them feel better. The whole town was very proud of Amy for staying calm and helping others. The moral of the story is to always mind your parents and stay calm in scary situations, so you can help others when they need you.

Once upon a time there was a girl who wanted to go on an adventure. She went out into the misty morning with a bag to pack. She wanted to find something special. She walked through the mist until she saw a pale flower growing by a pond. She reached out and touched it. She was amazed by its beauty and decided to pick it and pack it in her bag. The girl was very happy and continued her journey through the mist. She soon saw an old tree with a pale green feather swaying in the wind. She reached out and grabbed the feather and packed it in her bag too. The girl was so excited to have found such amazing things on her special adventure. She thanked the mist and walked home with her bag filled with her special treasures.

One day, a boy named Tim went with his mom to her office. The office was a comfortable place with big chairs and lots of colors. Tim liked to see all the people working and talking. While Tim was looking around, he saw a big box in the corner. He went to the box and opened it. Inside, he found a big, soft blanket. Tim was very happy and wrapped the blanket around him. Just then, the blanket started to move! Tim was surprised. The blanket turned into a big, friendly bear. The bear gave Tim a big hug and they played together in the office. Everyone was so happy to see the bear and they all had a fun day at the office.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big statue of a man on a horse. Tim was ready to play near the statue. He took his ball and ran to the statue. A girl named Sue was there too. She said, "Can you lend me your ball?" Tim said, "Yes, you can play with my ball." Sue was happy and they played together. Then, something unexpected happened. The statue moved! The horse and the man came to life. Tim and Sue were surprised. The statue man said, "Thank you for playing near me. I was lonely." Now Tim and Sue had a new friend to play with.
