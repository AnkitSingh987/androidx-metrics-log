Title: Lucy the Girl and the Castle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big imagination. Lucy loved to imagine things. One day, Lucy found a cane in her house. It was an attractive cane. It was shiny and had pretty colors. Lucy showed the cane to her friend, Tom. She said, "Look at this pretty cane I found! Let's imagine we are kings and queens!" Tom smiled and said, "That sounds fun, Lucy! Let's do it!" They played together and imagined they were in a big castle. Later, Lucy's mom came and told her, "Lucy, that cane is magic! It can make your dreams come true!" Lucy and Tom were so happy. They used the cane to make their fun game of kings and queens come true. They played all day in their big, beautiful castle.

One day, a silly boy named Tim went on a safari. He wanted to learn about animals. Tim saw big animals like elephants and small animals like bugs. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "I want to learn too! Can I come with you?" Tim said, "Yes! Let's learn together!" They walked and saw many animals. They were happy. But, Tim and Sue did not listen to their mom and dad. They went too far. They got lost. It was dark and they were scared. Tim and Sue could not find their way back. They should have listened to their mom and dad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy stand. He would put his toys on the stand and show them to his friends. One day, Tim's mom told him that he would receive a new toy for his stand. Tim was very excited and couldn't wait for his new toy. The next day, Tim went outside to play with his friends. They were running and laughing when suddenly, Tim tripped and fell. He hurt his knee and it made him cry. His friends helped him go back home. When he got home, his mom saw his hurt knee and gave him a bandage to make it feel better. Tim was sad because he thought he wouldn't receive his new toy now. But his mom surprised him and gave him the new toy anyway. Tim was very happy and learned that even when you get hurt, good things can still happen. He also learned to be more careful when playing with his friends so he wouldn't get hurt again.

Once upon a time there was a fat pet. His name was Max and he was the most special pet anyone had ever seen. Every day the family would get together to decorate his cage. They used bright paints and streamers and flowers. Max loved his new decorations and he would play and explore them every day. Everyone in the family had so much fun decorating his cage, they began to decorate even more things. Soon they were decorating the walls and the windows too. Max felt so special and happy. He even began to lose weight as he ran around and explored all that was new in his home. Years later, Max was still the most special pet anyone could ever have and his cage was still decorated with the most beautiful decorations the family could find. Max and the family were happy and content. They knew they would always be together, decorating and having fun. The End

One day, a clumsy cat named Tom wanted to paint a picture. He took his paints and brush and started to paint on a big paper. He painted a big sun, a blue sky, and a green tree. Tom was very happy with his painting. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, came to visit. Max saw the painting and said, "Wow, Tom! That's a nice picture. Can I paint with you?" Tom said, "Yes, Max! Let's paint together!" So, Tom and Max painted more trees, flowers, and a big house. After they finished painting, Tom and Max were hungry. They went to the kitchen to make some soup. They put vegetables and water in a big pot. They cooked the soup and shared it together. The soup was very yummy. Tom and Max were happy they could paint and eat soup together.
