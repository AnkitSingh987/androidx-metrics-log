Title: The Bear and the Fox

Once upon a time there was a brave little bear who wanted to settle down in a new place. He went out searching and at last he came to an open meadow. He liked the place very much and decided to make it his new home. He quickly set about building his new home. He gathered wood and put it together to build a nice cosy den where he could rest his head. He was so pleased with his new home he decided to invite his friends over. He went out and soon met a fox, a rabbit and a deer who all said they would come along. When they arrived the bear was so happy. Everyone settled inside the den and began talking and playing together. They laughed and sang and had a lot of fun. The little bear was so happy he had found a place that he could call home. He was glad that he had three new friends who could share it with him. They would all be friends forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. Tim was very happy. Then, they saw a big gorilla. The gorilla looked sad. Tim asked his mom, "Why is the gorilla sad?" His mom said, "He misses his friends." Tim wanted to help the gorilla. He had an idea. He asked his mom, "Can I touch the gorilla to make him happy?" His mom said, "No, it's not safe to touch the gorilla. But we can wave and say hello." So Tim waved and said, "Hi gorilla! Don't be sad! You have friends here!" The gorilla looked at Tim and smiled. Tim knew he made the gorilla happy, and that made Tim happy too.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little garden, there lived a bunny named Hoppy. Hoppy loved to hop all day long. He hopped over flowers, under trees, and around the bushes. The sun was always shining, and the air was sweet. It was a luxury for Hoppy to live in such a beautiful place. One day, Hoppy found a big, pretty box. It was a luxury box, filled with yummy treats like carrots and lettuce. Hoppy was so happy! He hopped around the box and then hopped inside to eat the treats. He ate and ate until his tummy was full. After eating all the treats, Hoppy felt sleepy. He hopped out of the luxury box and found a soft, pretty spot under a tree. Hoppy lay down and closed his eyes. He dreamed of more days filled with hopping, treats, and fun in his pretty little garden.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had many toys in her room. Her toys were all mixed up and she felt nervous. Lily wanted to organize her toys so she could find her favorite toy, a small figure. Lily started to organize her toys. She put her cars in one box, her dolls in another box, and her books on the shelf. But she could not find her small figure. Lily felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, Lily's mom came into the room. She saw Lily was sad and asked her what was wrong. Lily told her mom she could not find her small figure. Her mom helped her look for it. They found the figure under the bed! Lily was so happy and gave her mom a big hug. From that day on, Lily always kept her toys organized.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Jane was walking in the park with her ant. She was so excited to see the thick trees and grass. Suddenly, Jane heard something break and she stopped. She saw an ant on the ground, its leg was broken. Jane gasped in shock. The ant was scared and started to cry. Jane wanted to help, so she ran back to her house. She grabbed a bandaid, and ran back to the park. The ant was so happy when she saw Jane and the bandaid! Jane gently picked up the ant and used the bandaid to put its leg back together. In no time, the ant was able to hop away happily. Jane smiled and said, "See, your leg is all better now."
