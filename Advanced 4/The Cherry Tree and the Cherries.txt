Title: The Cherry Tree and the Cherries

Once upon a time, in a little village, there was a big cherry tree. The cherry tree had many red cherries. Everyone loved to eat cherries. But one day, the cherry tree became selfish. It did not want to share its cherries with anyone. A little boy named Tim saw the cherry tree. He wanted to eat a cherry. Tim asked the tree, "Can I have a cherry, please?" The selfish cherry tree said, "No! These are my cherries!" Tim was sad and walked away. Later, a kind wind blew through the village. The wind saw the selfish cherry tree and wanted to help. The wind blew and blew, making the cherries fall from the tree. The cherries were now on the ground for everyone to enjoy. The cherry tree learned not to be selfish and shared its cherries with everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very filthy from playing in the mud all day. He loved to play with his friends, the birds, and the squirrels. One day, Spot saw a big pile of lumber near his house. He thought it would be fun to play on it. Spot's mommy called out, "Spot, don't forget to be careful near the lumber!" Spot said, "Okay, mommy!" He played and played, jumping from one piece of lumber to another. Suddenly, Spot heard a small voice coming from the lumber pile. "Help, help!" it said. Spot looked around and saw a tiny kitten stuck under the lumber. He was very surprised! He didn't expect to find a kitten there. Spot carefully moved the lumber and saved the kitten. The kitten said, "Thank you, Spot!" They became good friends and played together every day. Spot's mommy was proud of him for helping the kitten and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there was a cheerful little rock. The rock loved to play with its friends, the trees and the flowers. They would laugh and dance together all day long. One day, the cheerful rock decided to go on an adventure. It rolled down the valley, saying hello to all the animals it met. The rock saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. With a big push, it rolled up the hill and reached the top. From the top of the hill, the cheerful rock saw the whole valley. It was so happy to see all its friends and the beautiful place where it lived. The rock rolled back down the hill, ready to play with its friends again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Cee. Cee was a good car, but sometimes he would worry too much. One day, Cee saw a big wreck on the road. He was scared and didn't know what to do. Cee's friend, Dee the dog, saw him worry. Dee came to help Cee. They looked at the wreck together. Dee said, "It's okay, Cee. We can ask for help." So, they went to find help. They found a big truck named Tee. Tee was strong and good at fixing things. Tee helped Cee and Dee move the wreck off the road. Cee was happy and didn't worry anymore. Cee, Dee, and Tee became good friends and helped each other when they needed it. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty building in a small town. People in the town loved to look at the pretty building. The building had many colors and made everyone happy. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked by the pretty building. She saw a small bird on the ground. The bird was sad because it could not fly. Lucy wanted to help the bird. She said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you fly again." Lucy and the bird went inside the pretty building. Inside, they found a kind man who could fix the bird's wing. The man said, "I will help your bird friend, Lucy." He fixed the bird's wing, and the bird could fly again. The bird was so happy and thanked Lucy and the kind man. The people in the town learned that the pretty building was not just nice to look at, but it also had kind people who helped others. The moral of the story is that true beauty comes from being kind and helpful. And sometimes, the most unexpected places can bring the most happiness.
