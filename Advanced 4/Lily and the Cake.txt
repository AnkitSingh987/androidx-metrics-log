Title: Lily and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with clay. One day, Lily had a plan to make a big clay cake for her mom's birthday. Lily was very excited about her plan. She worked hard to make the clay cake look just like a real cake. It was big and round, with pretty flowers on top. She felt very proud of her work. But then, something unexpected happened. Lily's little brother, Tim, saw the clay cake and got confused. He thought it was a real cake! He took a big bite of the clay cake and started to cry. The clay cake was ruined, and Lily was sad. Her plan did not work, and her mom's birthday surprise was spoiled.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big bag of rice. Lily loved her rice very much. She took it everywhere she went. One day, Lily saw her friend Tim playing with a new toy car. Lily felt jealous. She wanted a toy car too. Lily said, "Tim, can I play with your toy car?" Tim said, "Yes, but can I play with your rice?" Lily started to worry. She did not want to share her rice. But she really wanted to play with the toy car. So, Lily said, "Okay, we can share." Tim and Lily played together. They had a lot of fun. Lily learned that sharing can make friends happy. And she was not worried about her rice anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an orange dress made of velvet. Lily loved to wear her orange dress every day. She felt so happy and pretty in it. One day, Lily saw a girl named Anna who did not have any pretty dresses. Anna wore old and torn clothes. Lily wanted to make Anna happy, so she gave Anna her orange velvet dress. Anna was so happy and thankful to have a pretty dress to wear. Lily learned that sharing her things with others can make them happy too. She felt proud of herself for helping Anna. From that day on, Lily always shared her things with others. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make everyone happy.

Sammy was so excited when he saw the message! His parents had told him he was going to get a surprise. He couldn't wait to see what it was. Sammy opened the box and yawned. Inside there was a healthy snack and a note. Sammy read the note out loud. "It's time to eat a healthy snack and get some rest." Sammy was so disappointed! He wanted something fun, not something healthy. He pouted and wished his parents had sent him something different. Suddenly, Sammy heard a loud noise from outside. He stepped outside to investigate. When he got to the door, it slammed shut behind him. He was locked out! Sammy yelled for help, but no one came. After a few hours, Sammy was so tired he started to yawn. Then he realized he was stuck outside, all alone in the dark. Sammy was scared and he cried himself to sleep.

One day, a grumpy cat named Tom was walking in the rain. He saw a little girl with a big, colorful umbrella. Tom wanted to stay dry too, so he went to the girl. "Can I share your umbrella?" Tom asked. The girl smiled and said, "Yes, you can come under my umbrella, Tom." They walked together until they reached a door. The girl took out a key to unlock the door. Tom was happy to be inside and away from the rain. They played together all day and Tom was not grumpy anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his friend's house to play. They were having a lot of fun playing with toys and laughing. Tim's friend's mom came into the room and said, "I have a surprise for you!" She gave them each a big cookie. The cookies were so yummy, and they ate them all up. Later, Tim's mom came to pick him up. She asked him, "Did you have fun today?" Tim told his mom about the toys and the cookies. His mom looked at him and said, "Did you say thank you for the cookie?" Tim felt ashamed because he forgot to say thank you. He wanted to make it right. Tim's mom took him back to his friend's house. Tim knocked on the door and his friend opened it. Tim said, "I forgot to tell you thank you for the cookie. It was very yummy!" His friend smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim! I'm glad you liked it." Tim felt happy that he said thank you, and he learned how important it is to always be polite.
