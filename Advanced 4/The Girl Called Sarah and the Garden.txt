Title: The Girl Called Sarah and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a small girl called Sarah. She was playing in the garden when she heard a whistle. She stopped dead and froze. When she looked up, Sarah saw a big, rude man. He was coming towards her and she was scared. Suddenly, he bent down and picked her up! Sarah shouted and screamed, but the man just laughed. He carried her far away and she was sad and terrified. He finally put her down in a dark room and quicky locked the door behind him. She was too scared to move or do anything. Sarah stayed in the dark room for days and nights. She cried a lot and the man never came back. She never got to go home. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a drive with his dad. They wanted to go to a distant place to have a yummy waffle. They were very happy and excited. Tim loved waffles very much. On their way, they saw a big box on the road. They stopped the car and looked at the box. It was a surprise! The box was full of waffles. Tim and his dad were so happy. They did not have to drive to the distant place anymore. They took the waffles home and shared them with their friends. Everyone was happy and enjoyed the waffles. Tim and his dad had a great day, and they did not have to drive far away for waffles. The big box of waffles was the best surprise ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little caterpillar named Charlie. Charlie liked to explore the world. He often gazed around to see what was around him, and he was always up for an adventure. One day, Charlie decided to explore a big and long path that he had never seen before. He crawled along the path, enjoying the sights and sounds. But soon, his journey came to an abrupt end: he reached a big rock, and could no longer go any further. Charlie was determined to continue his journey, so he had to think of a way to move the rock. After thinking for a while, he spotted a tall tree nearby full of tasty apples. He decided to pick an apple and drop it on the rock. He was surprised to see that the apple broke the rock! His way was now clear! Charlie’s journey taught him a valuable lesson: with a bit of creativity, almost anything is possible!

Once upon a time, in a big tight forest, there lived a kind pine tree. The pine tree was always happy and loved to welcome all the animals who came to visit. One day, a small bird came to the forest. The bird was lost and scared. The kind pine tree saw the bird and said, "Welcome, little bird! Come and rest on my branches." The bird was very happy and thanked the pine tree. The bird and the pine tree became best friends. They played and sang together every day. All the other animals in the forest saw how much fun they were having and wanted to join, too. The pine tree welcomed everyone, and they all became a big, happy family.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his red brick. One day, he saw an unknown girl playing in the park. She had a blue brick. Tom wanted to be friends with the girl. Tom went to the girl and said, "Hi, I am Tom. I have a red brick. Can we play together?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, I am Amy. I like your red brick." They started to play and had lots of fun. They built a big tower with their bricks. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew their tower down. Tom and Amy were sad. But then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's hold hands and build the tower again. Together, we can make it stronger." Amy agreed, and they held hands while they built the tower again. This time, the tower was stronger, and the wind could not blow it down. Tom and Amy learned that when they worked together and held hands, they could do great things. The moral of the story is that we are stronger when we work together.
