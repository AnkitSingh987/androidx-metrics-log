Title: Lily the Girl and the Mirror

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to visit her grandma's house. Grandma had a big mirror in her room. Lily liked to look at herself in the mirror and make funny faces. One day, Lily went to visit her grandma. It was a dark and cloudy day. When she got to grandma's house, she ran to the mirror to make funny faces. But this time, she saw something new in the mirror. She saw her grandma's sad face. Lily asked her grandma why she was sad. Grandma said she was lonely because her friends could not visit her today. Lily thought for a moment and then had a great idea. She took her grandma's hand and they both made funny faces in the mirror together. Grandma started to laugh and her sad face turned into a happy face. From that day on, Lily learned that spending time with others can make them happy. The moral of the story is that being kind and sharing your time with others can make a big difference in their lives.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big event. All the kids were excited to see the fun games and yummy food. There was a little boy named Tim who was very naughty. He loved to play tricks on his friends and make them laugh. One day at the event, Tim thought of a funny trick. He decided to pause the music when everyone was dancing. He thought it would make everyone laugh. So, he waited for the right time and paused the music. But instead of laughing, everyone was surprised and a little upset. They did not think it was funny at all. Tim felt bad for his naughty trick. He learned that not all tricks make people happy. He went to the DJ and said sorry. The DJ forgave him and started the music again. From that day on, Tim promised to be a kind and helpful friend. The event became fun again, and everyone had a great time. And Tim learned that being kind is better than being naughty.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy called Will. He loved to play outside with his friends and have big adventures. One day, Will wanted to join his friends on a special surprise trip. He asked his mummy if he could go, but she said no. She said it was time to have a nap. Will was very confused. He wanted to join his friends but he also wanted to stay with his mummy. He felt really sad. Will decided to join his friends anyway and sneaked out of the house. His mummy was really cross when she found out. Will was too late to join his friends and he was sent to his room with no supper. The end.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to go for walks. He had a favourite spot in the park, near a big tree with a knob on its trunk. One day the little boy went to his favourite spot and saw that something strange had happened. The knob had been painted a different colour! This made the little boy very embarrassed; he was worried that people would think he had done it. The little boy walked slowly away, until he heard a whisper. It was the tree! It said, "Don't be embarrassed. I chose this different colour to make myself look special." The little boy was delighted. He hugged the tree and said, "Thank you!" Then he walked off feeling happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. This boat liked to sail on the water. It had a fun style that made it look interesting. All the other boats liked to watch it sail. One day, the little boat saw something far away. It was a big, round thing in the water. The little boat wanted to see it up close. So, it sailed and sailed to get there. When the little boat got close, it saw that the big round thing was a ball! The little boat was so happy. It sailed and played with the ball all day long. The other boats watched and smiled. The little boat had found a new friend to sail with.
