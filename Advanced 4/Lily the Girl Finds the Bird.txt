Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Bird

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park to play. She saw a rare, big, and pretty bird. She wanted to say hi to the bird. She walked up to it and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Lily and said, "Yes, let's play! But first, can you help me find my friend? I lost him and I am sad." Lily nodded and they went to look for the bird's friend together. As they looked, something happened. They saw the bird's friend stuck in a tree! Lily and the bird helped the friend get down. The two birds were very happy. They played with Lily all day long.

Heather was a brave three year old. She loved to explore and gaze around her neighborhood. One day, Heather was walking with her mom when they came across a large building. She asked her mom what it was and her mom replied "That's a university." Heather was amazed at the sight and knew she wanted to go there one day. Heather looked up to her mom and asked "Can I go to the university one day?" Her mom smiled and said "Maybe". Heather was determined to go to the university one day so she asked her mom every day if she could go. Each day, Heather's mom would say "Maybe if you are brave enough when you are older". Heather continued to look up at the university and gaze at it longingly every day. She would daydream about going to the university as she grew older. Years later, Heather graduated from high school and went to the university. She was brave and had made her dream come true. Her mom was proud of her and cheered on Heather as she received her diploma. As Heather looked back up at the university, she noticed how the same building she looked at as a toddler had now shaped her life.

Once there was a little boy. His name was Peter and he belonged to a loving family. Every day he would go outside, to the beach. It was Peter's favourite place. At the beach, Peter would dig for shells and play in the sand. But his favourite thing was when the waves came in. Every time a wave started coming in, Peter would run towards it. For a moment, he felt like he belonged to the sea. But the waves were very rough. They would knock Peter down, and each time he would laugh and get back up again. Peter felt like he belonged to the waves too. And every night, when it was time to come home, Peter always took with him a shell he had found. He would take it home and show it to his family. To Peter, that shell belonged to all of them.

Anna loves the fall season. She likes to see the leaves change color and fall from the trees. She likes to wear her cozy sweater and boots. She likes to pick apples and pumpkins with her family. One day, Anna and her mom go for a walk in the park. They see many lovely things. They see a big pond with ducks and geese. They see a bridge with flowers and vines. They see a playground with swings and slides. Anna wants to play on the swings. She runs to the swing set and finds an empty one. She sits on the swing and asks her mom to push her. Her mom pushes her gently and Anna feels happy. She swings higher and higher and laughs. Then, Anna sees something shiny on the ground. She stops swinging and jumps off. She runs to the shiny thing and picks it up. It is a coin. It is silver and round and has a picture of a man on it. Anna thinks it is pretty and special. She runs back to her mom and shows her the coin. Her mom smiles and says, "What a lucky find, Anna! You can keep it as a souvenir of this lovely day." Anna hugs her mom and says, "Thank you, mom. I love you." She puts the coin in her pocket and stands on the swing again. She asks her mom to push her one more time. Her mom pushes her and Anna swings happily. She thinks this is the best fall season ever.
