Title: Bobo the Foolish Monkey and the Elephant

Once upon a time, there was a foolish monkey named Bobo. Bobo loved to play tricks on his friends in the jungle. One day, he decided to play a trick on his friend, the elephant. Bobo went to the elephant and said, "I can supply you with the biggest, sweetest fruit in the jungle. But first, you must close your eyes and count to ten." The elephant agreed and closed his eyes. While the elephant was counting, Bobo quickly climbed a tree and started to throw small rocks at the elephant. When the elephant reached ten, he opened his eyes and was very sad. There were no big, sweet fruits, only small rocks on the ground. "Bobo, you tricked me!" the elephant cried. Bobo laughed and laughed at his foolish joke. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree that Bobo was in started to shake and break. Bobo fell from the tree and landed on the ground. The other animals in the jungle saw what happened and were not happy with Bobo. They decided to not play with him anymore. From that day on, Bobo was all alone, learning that being foolish and playing tricks on friends was not a good idea.

Lily and Sam liked to play with their blocks. They wanted to build a big house for their toys. They put blocks on top of blocks, and made windows and doors. They were happy with their house. But then, they heard a loud noise outside. They looked out the window and saw a big red truck. It was a fireman truck. The fireman truck had a siren and a hose. The fireman truck stopped near their house. "What is the fireman truck doing?" Lily asked. "Maybe there is a fire somewhere," Sam said. They ran outside to see. They saw a fireman get out of the truck. He was wearing a helmet and a coat. He looked tired and sweaty. "Hello, fireman," Lily said. "Why are you here?" "Hello, kids," the fireman said. "We are here because there is a fire in the woods. We are trying to put it out. But it is very hard and hot." "Can we help you?" Sam asked. The fireman smiled. "You are very kind, but you can't help us. It is too dangerous for you. You should stay away from the fire. But you can do something else. You can give us some water and snacks. We are very thirsty and hungry." "OK, we can do that," Lily said. "We have some water and snacks in our house. We will bring them to you." "Thank you, kids," the fireman said. "You are very helpful and brave." Lily and Sam ran back to their house. They got some water bottles and some cookies. They put them in a basket. They carried the basket to the fireman truck. They gave the water and the cookies to the fireman and his friends. "Thank you, kids," the fireman and his friends said. "You are very generous and sweet." Lily and Sam felt happy. They had helped the fireman and his friends. They had done a good thing. They waved to the fireman and his friends. They went back to their house. They played with their blocks again. They were proud of their house and themselves.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot was very cute, and everyone loved him. He had a best friend named Timmy. Timmy and Spot played together every day. One day, Timmy said to Spot, "I love you, Spot. I am grateful that you are my friend." Spot was very happy to hear this. They played together all day long. They ran, they jumped, and they had so much fun. But then, one day, Spot got lost. He could not find his way home. Timmy looked for Spot everywhere, but he could not find him. Timmy was very sad. He missed his friend Spot so much. Even though they had so much fun together, they could not play together anymore.

One day, a funny dog named Max found a whip. He did not know what it was, but he liked to play with it. He would stay in the yard and whip it around, making it go up and down. It made him very happy. Max saw his friend, a cat named Tom. "Tom, come play with me and my new whip!" said Max. Tom came over and they took turns playing with the whip. They laughed and had so much fun. After a while, it was time for Tom to go home. "Bye, Max! Let's play with the whip again tomorrow," said Tom. Max agreed and they both said goodbye. Max stayed in the yard, playing with his whip until the sun went down.
