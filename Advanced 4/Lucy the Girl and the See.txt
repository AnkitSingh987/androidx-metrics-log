Title: Lucy the Girl and the See

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy who lived in a small house in the countryside. She was very impatient, so she always wanted to do things quickly. One day, Lucy heard something strange in the sky. She looked up and saw a sparkling comet! She was so excited and began to jump up and down. Lucy's mom heard the noise and came to see what was happening. "What's going on? What did you see?" she asked. Lucy said, "Mama, I saw a sparkly comet in the sky! Can I go look at it?" Her mom said, "No, Lucy, I'm sorry but you need to stay here. It's getting late and it's not safe. We have to stay inside." Lucy was very impatient and refused to stay inside. She really wanted to see the comet again. But no matter how much she begged, her mom said no. So Lucy had to wait until tomorrow to see the comet again. But she was very determined and promised herself she would get to see it the very next day.

Once upon a time there were two friends, a boy and a girl, who both loved exploring the mountains. One day, they decided to explore a high mountain they had not seen before. They began to climb the mountain and when they reached the top, the boy was so excited he started to yell. But the girl had a more serious plan. She said, "We should rest and look around. We need to be careful." The boy knew she was right but he felt bored. He kept wanting to do more exciting things. He groaned, "We don't need to rest for so long!" But the girl was very serious. She explained that it was important to stay safe and be aware of their surroundings. The boy eventually agreed and they rested for a while. When they were done, they were amazed to find that the mountain had many beautiful plants and creatures. The boy realised the value of taking a moment to rest and be careful. They both learnt the moral lesson of always being cautious and appreciative of nature.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many toys. He also had a guitar that he loved to play every day. One day, Tom made a plan. He wanted to share his guitar music with others. So, he went to the park where kids and their moms and dads were playing. Tom sat on a bench and started to play his guitar. The kids and their moms and dads heard Tom's guitar music. They came to listen and dance. Everyone was happy and had fun. Tom was glad that his plan worked. From that day on, Tom played his guitar in the park for everyone to enjoy.

Tommy was a young boy who liked to play with his toys. He had many toys, but his favorite was a red car with a zipper on the back. He could open the zipper and put his small things inside the car. He liked to pretend that the car was his and he could go anywhere he wanted. One day, Tommy's friend Lily came to visit. She saw the red car and wanted to play with it too. She asked Tommy if she could borrow it for a while. Tommy said no. He did not want to share his car. He said it was his and only his. Lily felt sad and angry. She said that Tommy was not a good friend. She said that he was selfish and mean. Tommy and Lily started to argue. They raised their voices and said hurtful words. They did not listen to each other. They did not have fun. They did not play. Tommy's mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw Tommy and Lily fighting over the red car. She asked them to stop and calm down. She asked them why they were so upset. Tommy and Lily told her their sides of the story. She listened and nodded. She said that she understood how they felt. She said that the red car was a special toy for Tommy, but it was also nice to share with friends. She said that Lily was a guest and a friend, and it was polite to let her play with the car too. She said that they could take turns or play together. She said that sharing was caring. She said that spending time with friends was more important than spending time with things. Tommy and Lily thought about what she said. They realized that she was right. They felt sorry for what they said and did. They apologized to each other and hugged. They decided to share the red car and play together. They had fun. They played. Tommy learned that sharing was not so hard. He learned that he could still enjoy his car and make his friend happy. He learned that spending time with friends was more valuable than spending time with things. He learned to be a good friend.
