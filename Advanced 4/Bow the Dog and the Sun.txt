Title: Bow the Dog and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bow. Bow liked to run and play in the park. One day, while he was playing, he saw a crack in the ground. He was curious and wanted to see what was inside the crack. Bow tried to look inside the crack, but it was too dark. He felt helpless because he couldn't see what was inside. Bow wanted to find a way to see inside the crack, so he thought of a plan. He decided to ask his friend, the sun, for help. Bow looked up at the sun and asked, "Can you please help me see inside the crack?" The sun smiled and said, "Of course, Bow!" The sun shined its light on the crack, and Bow could finally see inside. He was so happy and thanked the sun for being so kind. Bow learned that it's okay to ask for help when you feel helpless. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Lucy. Lucy had a daughter named Daisy. They lived in a big house with a big yard. Lucy and Daisy loved to play in the yard. One day, Lucy and Daisy went to the yard to pick flowers. They picked red flowers, yellow flowers, and blue flowers. Daisy wanted to pick the biggest flower for her mom, Lucy. Daisy saw a very big flower in the yard, and she ran to it. But, when Daisy tried to pick the big flower, it moved! It was not a flower at all. It was a butterfly! The butterfly said, "I am not a flower, I am a butterfly." Daisy was scared at first, but then she laughed. Lucy laughed too. They played with the butterfly and picked more flowers. They had a fun day in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a favorite seat in her house. It was a small, soft chair with pretty colors. Lucy liked to sit there and read her books. One day, Lucy's mom said they needed to supply food to their friend's house. Lucy wanted to help. She put on her coat and shoes, and they went to the store. They bought apples, bananas, and bread. When they got to their friend's house, Lucy saw a little seat just like hers! She was so happy. She sat in the little seat and shared her food with her friend. They had a fun time eating and laughing together.

Every day, Mummy Bear would prepare a special dish. The bear cub was always very curious to see what would be inside. Each day, Mummy Bear would smile and say, “Wait and see what I have prepared for tonight!” The bear cub was excited to discover the surprise dish. After dinner, Mummy Bear said, “Are you ready for tonight?” The bear cub was eager to find out what would happen. They began to play in the forest. At first, the bear cub was having lots of fun and the games were very playful. Suddenly, Mummy Bear stopped and said, “I have a surprise for you.” The bear cub was very excited and saw that Mummy Bear had prepared a special dessert. It was a delicious dish of sweet honey and berries. The bear cub was so happy. He thanked Mummy Bear and said, “Thank you for the wonderful surprise! I love your special dish!” From then on, the bear cub looked forward to Mummy Bear’s daily surprise dishes.

Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many kids wanted to win the race. One little girl, named Amy, was very sad. She was not fast, so she thought she could not win. She felt miserable. On the day of the race, all the kids ran very fast. Amy ran too, but she was slow. Then, something unexpected happened. The kids saw a sign with an arrow. They all followed the sign, but it was the wrong way! Amy saw the wrong sign too, but she did not follow it. She kept running straight. Soon, she was the first one at the finish line. Amy won the race! All the kids were happy for her, and she was not miserable anymore.
