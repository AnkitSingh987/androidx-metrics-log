Title: Tim Finds the Dog

One Sunday, a little boy named Tim and his obedient dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big tree with lots of branches. Tim wanted to make a swing, so he asked his dad for help. "Can you cut a branch, Dad?" Tim asked. His dad said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's find a strong branch for your swing." They found a good branch and Tim's dad cut it. Then, they tied a rope to the branch and made a swing. Tim was very happy. He sat on the swing and Spot watched him play. Tim and Spot played on the swing all day. They had so much fun on that sunny Sunday. And from that day on, the swing became their favorite place to play together.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play on the big, soft rug in his house. One sunny day, Tom was playing with his ball when he heard a loud noise from the kitchen. Tom ran to the kitchen and saw that his friend, Mouse, had split a big cookie in two. Mouse said, "Hi Tom! Do you want to share this yummy cookie with me?" Tom's eyes went wide, and he licked his lips. "Yes, please!" he replied. Tom and Mouse sat on the rug and ate the cookie together. They were very happy and full. From that day on, Tom and Mouse became the best of friends, always sharing and playing together on the big, soft rug.

Wendy was a little girl who was very curious. One day she was playing in her room when she saw a curious object on the floor. She picked it up and saw that it was a magnet. She felt it and found that it was strong. Wendy wanted to touch the magnet to the window to see what would happen. She moved closer but her mom stopped her. “No Wendy,” said her mom. “You must not do that.” Wendy felt a bit sad and looked at the magnet, wondering what would have happened. She noticed that the magnet was so strong that it attracted some paper clips. Wendy's mom saw what happened and said, “You must remember, Wendy, that some things can be dangerous. You should not touch things you do not know.” Wendy said nothing, feeling very bitter. The next morning Wendy woke up feeling very regretful. She had not listened to her mom and now she would never know what could have happened. She wished she had been more careful and she promised herself to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. All the people were happy and fit. They liked to jump, run, and play at the festival. The festival had yummy food for everyone to chew. The kids loved the festival. One day, a big, funny dog came to the festival. The dog wanted to play too. It ran and jumped with the kids. The kids laughed and played with the big, funny dog. The dog also wanted to chew some food. So, it went to the food place. But then, something unexpected happened. The big, funny dog took a big bite of food and started to chew. Suddenly, the dog changed! It was not a dog anymore. It was a nice man! The man said, "I was a dog, but when I chew the food at the festival, I turn back into a man." Everyone was very surprised. They all clapped and cheered. The man was happy and played with the kids too. Now, the festival was even more fun!

Once upon a time there were two friends who loved to design things. One day they thought of a really fun idea. They wanted to make a microphone. So they went shopping to get all the supplies they needed. When they went back to their workshop, they got to work. They worked together to design their microphone. It had lots of different colours and shapes. They were so proud of their work and they wanted to show it off. So they took it to their village to show everyone. When they arrived, everyone was amazed. They had never seen a microphone as different as this one. They invited the two friends to perform. The friends were so excited, but when their time came to perform their microphone stopped working. They were devastated. The friends had to go home in silence. They didn't understand why the microphone had broken. They had designed it so carefully, but it just hadn't worked out.
