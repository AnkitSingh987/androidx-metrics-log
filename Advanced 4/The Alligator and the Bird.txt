Title: The Alligator and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big alligator. He was very ignorant. He did not know how to play with others. He lived in a small pond. One day, he was very tired and wanted to take a nap. While the alligator was napping, a little bird came to the pond. The bird did not know about the alligator. The bird wanted to play near the water. The alligator was still napping and did not see the bird. The bird played and splashed in the water. The noise woke up the alligator. The alligator was not happy. He was still ignorant and did not want to play with the bird. The alligator opened his big mouth and ate the little bird. The bird was gone, and the alligator went back to his nap.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was adorable with big brown eyes and curly hair. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Mia saw a big ice cream truck. She wanted to try some ice cream. Mia went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, can I have some ice cream, please?" Mommy smiled and gave Mia some money. "Be careful not to lose the money," Mommy told her. Mia held the money tight and went to the ice cream truck. She got a big scoop of ice cream. It was cold like ice, but it tasted so good! Mia was so happy. She went back to her mommy and said, "Thank you, mommy! The ice cream is yummy!" Mia played more outside with her ice cream. But, oh no! She started to lose her ice cream as it melted in the sun. Mia was sad, but mommy gave her a hug. "It's okay, Mia. We can get more ice cream another day," Mommy said. Mia smiled and knew everything was going to be okay. She had a fun day and learned to enjoy her ice cream quickly before it melted away.

Once upon a time, there was a silly little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day long. One day, Spot was playing in a big field full of tall, green corn. The corn was very tall and he liked to hide in it. In the field, Spot met a new friend named Daisy. Daisy was a pretty, little cat. They played hide and seek in the corn for hours. They had so much fun together that they didn't want to go home. So, they made a promise to each other to meet in the corn field every day to play. The next day, Spot and Daisy met in the corn field again. They played, laughed, and had a great time. They were very happy that they made the promise to play together. And every day, the silly little dog and pretty little cat met in the tall, green corn field to have fun and be the best of friends.

Tom and Jemma were best friends. One day they decided to go to the park to play sports. It was very cold outside. Tom had some footballs he wanted to show Jemma. When they got there, they saw some other kids playing on the swings. Jemma wanted to join them, but Tom wanted to play with his footballs. Jemma wanted to play something else and an argument started. Tom tried his best to show Jemma that football was fun, but she was having none of it. Jemma was determined to swing on the swings, but Tom was adamant that football was a much better sport. The argument went on for a while and the cold weather didn’t help. Finally, Tom gave in and agreed to let Jemma have her way. So, the two friends went and played on the swings, leaving the footballs in the cold.

Once upon a time, there was a sad bunny named Bobo. He was sad because he did not have any friends to play with. One day, Bobo saw a big, green celery in the garden. He had an idea to make a new friend. Bobo took the celery and started to decorate it. He put on two eyes, a mouth, and a hat made of leaves. He named his new friend Mr. Celery. Bobo said, "Hi Mr. Celery, do you want to play with me?" Mr. Celery did not talk, but Bobo felt happy to have a friend. Later, a girl named Lily saw Bobo and Mr. Celery playing. She said, "Hi Bobo, can I play with you and Mr. Celery?" Bobo was very happy and said, "Yes!" The three of them played together all day. The unexpected thing was that Mr. Celery could not talk, but he helped Bobo find a real friend. The moral of the story is that sometimes, good things happen when we least expect them.
