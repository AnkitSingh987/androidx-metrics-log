Title: The White Club and the Old

Once, there was a small white club. It was at the edge of a dreamy meadow. One day, a 3 year old child went to the white club. He was so happy, and he played there all day. At night, something very bad happened. A huge storm came and destroyed the little white club. The 3 year old child was so very sad. The white club was gone, and the dreamy meadow was no more. Now, the 3 year old child had no place to play. The bad storm had taken away his special dream.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Jerry. They were playing in the park and it was so much fun. Tom and Jerry were giggling and jumping around. They were so excited! Suddenly Tom grabbed Jerry's hat and shook it in the air. "Hey, give that back!" Jerry shouted. But Tom just kept shaking, laughing in a funny way. Jerry was mad. He ran up to Tom and tried to grab his hat back, but Tom was too fast. He kept running around and shaking the hat. Tom thought it was fun, but Jerry was getting madder and madder. Suddenly, an idea came to Jerry. He grabbed a stick and twirled it around really fast. The stick was shaking in the air! Tom was so surprised he dropped the hat and stepped back. Jerry grabbed the hat and ran away. "That was a clever way to get my hat back!" Tom said. He laughed and hugged Jerry. They both had fun playing in the park once more.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a potato. She thought it was a funny potato because it had a number on it. The number was two. Sue was happy to find the potato and wanted to show her friends. Sue took the potato to her friend Tim. Tim was grateful to see the potato with the number two on it. Tim said, "Let's share the potato with our other friends." So, they went to find their friends and showed them the potato. Everyone was happy to see the potato with the number two on it. They learned that sharing with friends makes everyone happy. The potato with the number two brought them all together, and they were grateful for the fun day they had. The moral of the story is to always share with your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a friend named Olive. Olive was a funny bird. Mia and Olive liked to play together. One day, Mia was worried. She did not know what game to play. Olive said, "I recommend we play hide and seek!" Mia liked that idea. They started to play and had lots of fun. Mia and Olive played hide and seek all day. They laughed and had a great time. In the end, Mia was not worried anymore. She was happy to have Olive as her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little gray cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play and try new things. One day, Fluffy saw a big shop with many toys. Fluffy went into the shop and saw a big red ball. Fluffy tried to play with the ball, but it was too big. Then, Fluffy saw a small blue car. Fluffy tried to push the car, and it went fast! Fluffy was very happy in the shop. Fluffy played with the car all day. When it was time to go home, Fluffy said goodbye to the shop and took the little blue car home to play some more.

Pat was a little bird and she lived in a big cage. Every day she would cheer and sing! She loved to share her songs with her friends in the cage. But one day something crazy happened. A big wind came and shook Pat's cage so hard that it opened up! All of Pat's friends flew out, but not Pat. She was scared to leave her cage, so she stayed inside. Then suddenly, she heard a song. It was coming from outside her cage. Pat looked out and saw her friends singing, flying and having fun. Pat wanted to share in the fun, so she spread her wings and flew out of the cage. Pat and her friends had a wonderful time flying and singing together. They were so happy that nothing could make them leave. They spent all day sharing in the fun and adventures. Pat had never been so excited and happy before. From then on, she and her friends always shared their craziest stories, jokes and songs when they got together. For Pat, leaving the cage was a crazy experience but it was also the start of a very special friendship.
