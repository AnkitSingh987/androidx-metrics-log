Title: Tim the Boy and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a difficult time remembering where the bathroom was in his big house. One day, his mom asked him, "Tim, do you know where the bathroom is?" Tim thought about it and said, "I guess it's over there," pointing to a door. Tim went to the door he guessed was the bathroom, but it was not. It was a dark room with lots of things inside. He felt scared and wanted to find the bathroom quickly. He saw another door and thought, "Maybe this is the bathroom." As Tim opened the door, he found the bathroom! He was so happy, but he remembered that he should have asked his mom for help. He thought it would be easy to find the bathroom, but it was difficult. Tim promised himself that next time, he would ask for help. But Tim forgot his promise. The next day, he went to find the bathroom again. This time, he opened the wrong door and fell into a big hole. Tim was very sad and scared. He wished he had asked his mom for help. From that day on, Tim always asked for help when he didn't know something.

Once upon a time, there was a store. The store was small, but it had many things. People went to the store to buy things they needed. One day, a big truck came to the store. The truck had many big boxes. The boxes were too big to fit in the store. The store owner had an idea. He had a friend who had a big machine that could print. The owner asked the friend to come and help. The friend brought the big machine to the store. The machine could print small boxes. The friend started to print small boxes for the store. The store owner was very happy. He put the things from the big boxes into the small boxes. The small boxes fit in the store. The people came and bought the things they needed. The store owner and his friend were very happy. They worked together to make the store a good place for everyone.

Once there was a jolly little girl. Her name was Jilly. Jilly loved to have fun and play with her friends. One day, Jilly's mommy invited Jilly to come to her office. Jilly was so excited to go. She put on her prettiest dress and went to the office with mommy. In the office, Jilly met lots of friendly people. Everyone was so happy to see her! They all said hello and gave her lots of smiles. Jilly also saw mommy's desk. It was so fun to explore. Mommy even gave Jilly a special pencil. Jilly had such a great time. She felt so excited and jolly! She would never forget her day at the office.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his club. It was a place where he and his friends would play and have fun. Tim felt very lucky to be part of this club. While they were playing, Tim did something silly. He tripped and fell on his face. His friends saw and laughed. Tim felt embarrass. But his friends helped him up and said it's okay. Tim knew he was lucky to have such nice friends. They played together all day at the club. In the end, Tim was happy and not embarrass anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cabin in the woods. A little girl lived in the cabin with her mommy and daddy. They were a happy family. The girl liked to play outside with her ball and her dog. One day, the little girl saw a big frog near the cabin. The frog was sad. The girl wanted to help the frog. She remembered a story about a princess who kissed a frog. The frog became a prince after the kiss. So, the girl thought she could help the sad frog by giving it a kiss. The little girl kissed the big frog. But the frog did not turn into a prince. Instead, the frog became very big and scary. The big scary frog chased the girl and her dog away from the yellow cabin. The girl and her dog could not go back to the cabin. They were very sad and missed their home.
