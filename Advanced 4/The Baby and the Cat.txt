Title: The Baby and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a baby. The baby was very healthy. The baby liked to play with a big red ball. One day, the baby saw a cat. The cat was up in a tree. The baby wanted to get the cat down. So, the baby threw the big red ball at the tree. The ball hit the tree and the cat jumped down. The baby and the cat played together all day. They were very happy. The baby stayed healthy and strong. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little white rabbit. The rabbit lived in a forest with many trees. The rabbit had friends like a bird, a squirrel, and a deer. One day, the rabbit wanted to jump very high. The bird, squirrel, and deer encourage the rabbit to try. They said, "You can do it, rabbit!" The rabbit tried to jump, but it was not high enough. The rabbit felt sad, but the bird, squirrel, and deer did not give up. They encourage the rabbit again, "Try one more time!" The rabbit tried again and jumped very high. The rabbit was happy, and the friends were happy too. They all played together in the white forest.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird liked to walk and talk with his friends. They had fun every day. The bird had an opinion that he was the best walker of them all. One day, the yellow bird went for a walk with his friends. They saw a big tree. The bird said, "I can walk to the top of the tree!" His friends were amazed. They did not think the bird could do it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew, and the bird was lifted into the sky! The bird did not walk to the top of the tree, but he flew there. His friends clapped and cheered. The yellow bird was happy, and from that day, he knew he could fly as well as walk.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to walk on the trail with their mom and dad. The trail was in the woods, where they could see many animals and plants. Sometimes they would find berries or nuts to eat, or flowers to smell. One day, they saw a big house on the hill, with a fence and a gate. It looked very fancy and shiny. Lily and Ben wanted to see it up close, so they ran away from their mom and dad and went to the gate. They saw a sign that said "Keep Out". Lily and Ben did not listen to the sign. They climbed over the fence and went to the house. They knocked on the door, but no one answered. They peeked through the windows, but they saw nothing but dark rooms and dusty furniture. They felt scared and lonely. Suddenly, they heard a loud bark. A big dog came out of the house and ran towards them. It looked angry and hungry. Lily and Ben screamed and ran back to the gate. They climbed over the fence and ran to the trail, where their mom and dad were waiting for them. They hugged their mom and dad and said they were sorry. They learned that they should not go to places where they are not welcome, and that they should be happy with their modest home, where they had love and warmth. They also learned to always stay with their mom and dad on the trail, where they could be safe and have fun.

Once upon a time there was a fox. The fox was very graceful. He liked to run through the meadows and explore. One day, the fox wanted to guess something. He thought hard about it and he couldn't figure it out. He was feeling frustrated until he saw a butterfly. The butterfly was so graceful and he wondered if he knew the answer to the fox's guess. The fox asked the butterfly if he knew the answer and the butterfly smiled. He slowly flew away without saying a word. The fox felt confused but then he realised the butterfly was trying to tell him something. The fox smiled and made a new guess. He was right! The fox had guessed correctly and he was so happy that he ran around the meadow, enjoying his success.
