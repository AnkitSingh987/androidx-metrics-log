Title: The Bird and the Bowl

Once there was a little bird, and his name was Henry. Every day he would sit in his nest in the tree and sing his favorite song. One day Henry was feeling very sad. He did not know why he was so sad, but he was. He asked his friend, a small bee, why he was so unhappy. The bee replied, "you need to improve your singing, Henry. You could start by practicing with a bowl." Henry thought about it, and decided to give it a try. He found a little bowl and began to sing into it. He practiced and practiced until he was sure that he had improved his singing. One day he sang into the bowl and a magical thing happened. Birds from all directions flew towards Henry to hear his beautiful song. Henry was so proud of himself that he thanked the bee and the bowl for helping him to improve his singing.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace lived a fair queen. The queen had a best friend who was a little cat. They played together every day. One day, the queen said to the cat, "I wonder what is behind that door?" They had never opened the door before. The cat said, "Let's open it and see." They opened the door and saw a big room full of toys. The queen and the cat played with the toys all day. They had so much fun. But then, the toys started to talk! The toys said, "We are magic toys, and we want to be your friends too!" The queen, the cat, and the magic toys all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn surfboard. He wanted to explore the world but he was too scared to go very far out of his reef. So, he stayed close and stayed put. One day, he asked a nearby seagull to help him. The seagull agreed and said he could take him to explore, if he wanted. So, they set off together. But still, the surfboard was a bit wary. He was worried he wouldn't like the places they went to. However, when they arrived at the beach, it was much better than the surfboard expected! He was able to examine the sand, sea and shells. He was so excited that he forgot to be scared. The surfboard had so much fun exploring and didn't want to leave. Eventually, as evening fell, the seagull convinced the stubborn surfboard to go home. And that's where the surfboard lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a rich bunny. He had lots of food. He had carrots, apples, and nuts. The rich bunny liked to share his food with all his friends. One sunny day, the rich bunny decided to have a big party. He wanted all his friends to come and eat his yummy food. So, the rich bunny started to scatter the food all around his big house. He put carrots here, apples there, and nuts everywhere. Soon, all the animals came to the party. They saw the food scattered all around and were very happy. They ate and played together. The rich bunny was happy to see his friends enjoying the food. They all had a great time at the rich bunny's party.

One day, a little blue bird named Billy went on a tour. He wanted to see new things and make new friends. He flew high in the sky and saw many pretty flowers and big trees. Billy saw a nice cat named Lucy sitting near a tree. He flew down to say hi. "Hi, I am Billy. I am on a tour. Do you want to be my friend?" he asked. Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, I would like to be your friend, Billy." Billy and Lucy sat under the tree and started to discuss all the fun things they could do together. They talked about playing games, singing songs, and flying around. They were very happy to be friends and continued their tour together.

Ben and Sam were brothers. They liked to play with cars and balls and books. But they did not like to share. They always wanted the same thing and they would fight over it. One day, Mom made pizza for lunch. She put cheese and tomato and ham on it. It smelled good and looked yummy. Ben and Sam ran to the table and each grabbed a slice. But they both wanted the slice with the most ham. They pulled and tugged and shouted. "Mine! Mine!" Ben said. "No, mine! Mine!" Sam said. Mom saw them and was angry. She took the slice away from them and put it on her plate. She gave them each a different slice. But she did not tell them that she had put something else on the pizza. Something bitter and spicy and yucky. It was hot pepper. Ben and Sam took a big bite of their pizza. They felt a fire in their mouths. They spit out the pizza and cried. They drank water and milk and juice. But nothing helped. Their tongues hurt and their eyes watered. Mom looked at them and said, "That's what you get for fighting. Next time, you should share and be nice. Maybe then you will get the good pizza." Ben and Sam nodded and said they were sorry. They hugged each other and promised to be better brothers. They learned their lesson. They never fought over pizza again.
