Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the airport with her mom. They were going on a trip to see Lucy's grandma. Lucy was very excited because she loved her grandma very much. When they got to the airport, Lucy saw a big, tough man. He was carrying a big bag. Lucy asked the man, "What's in your bag?" The man smiled and said, "I am going to bake a cake for my friend's birthday. I have all the things I need to make the cake in my bag." Lucy thought that was a fun idea. She told her mom about the man and his cake. Her mom said, "Maybe we can bake a cake with grandma when we get there." Lucy was very happy and couldn't wait to get to her grandma's house to bake a cake with her.

One day, a colorful fish was swimming in the sea. The fish liked to number how many friends he met each day. Today, he met two new fish friends. They were playing near the rocks. "Let's play a game," said the colorful fish. "We can hide and try to find each other!" The other fish agreed and they started to play. They swam fast and hid behind big rocks and plants. The colorful fish was looking for his friends when he saw a big shadow. It was a whale! The whale was friendly and wanted to play too. "Can I play with you?" asked the whale. The colorful fish was surprised but happy. "Yes, you can play with us!" he said. Now, the colorful fish had three new friends. They played hide and seek all day long. The fish never thought he would meet a whale and play with him. It was the best day ever for the colorful fish and his new friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, a dog named Jeff and a cat named Mac. They were very loyal to each other and were always together. They loved to explore and spend time doing all sorts of fun things. One day, they decided to make a wishing well! Jeff and Mac collected some stones, some wood and some steel, and spent the whole morning building it. When the wishing well was finally finished, they each took turns throwing a coin into the well and making a wish. Jeff wished for Mac to always be loyal to him. Mac wished for Jeff to always stay by his side. It was a wish that both friends wanted for each other, and as time passed, their loyalty for each other never diminished. In the end, Jeff and Mac spent most of their time together, exploring and playing in their little garden. They became the best of friends and remained loyal to each other forever.

Billy was feeling grumpy. He was having a very bad day and everything was wrong. He saw Mummy walking towards him, wearing a bathrobe. Mummy said “Come on Billy, time to get ready for the day.” He waved his hand at Mummy and said in a grumpy voice “No! I don’t want to get ready.” Mummy smiled and said “Why don’t we put on a bathrobe and go outside. We can swing in the park.” Billy thought about it, then smiled and waved his hand. “Yes, let’s go!” He said. They put on their bathrobes and went outside to the park. They had a lot of fun swinging and Billy felt much less grumpy.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some things. Lily liked to help her mom at the store. She was a very good helper. At the store, they needed to buy salt. Lily saw a big bag of salt. She picked it up and put it in the cart. Her mom was happy that Lily was helping. On the way home, they saw a graceful bird. The bird was flying in the sky. Lily and her mom watched the bird as they walked. It was a nice day to buy salt and see a pretty bird.

Once upon a time there was three ordinary friends, Wally, Sam and Jon. They were playing in the park when Wally noticed something strange. "What is that?" Wally asked. "It looks like a triangle," Sam said. "Let's learn about it,” Jon said excitedly. So they all started learning together. They soon found out that the triangle was special because it could help them figure out a lot of things. They could even solve problems with it. Wally, Sam and Jon were so happy to learn about the triangle that they decided to come back to learn more about it every day. The moral of the story is that learning is fun and everything you learn has different uses. Learning about new and different things opens up a whole new world of possibilities.
