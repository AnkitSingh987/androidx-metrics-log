Title: The Unique Tomato and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a unique tomato. It lived in a big garden with other tomatoes. The unique tomato was very happy and loved to play with all its friends. One sunny day, a girl named Mary came to the garden. She wanted to pick some tomatoes for her mom. Mary saw the unique tomato and thought it was very special. She picked the unique tomato and put it in her basket. Mary took the unique tomato home and showed it to her mom. Her mom smiled and said it was the most special tomato she had ever seen. They put the unique tomato in a special place in their kitchen. The unique tomato was very happy to be with Mary and her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. One day, she found a big book with many pages. Lily was very happy and started to read the book. As she read the book, Lily found a page with a picture of a spicy pepper. She thought it looked yummy. She wanted to try a spicy pepper too. Lily asked her mom to buy a spicy pepper from the store. Her mom bought the spicy pepper and Lily took a big bite. Suddenly, she started to suffer. Her mouth was very hot and she cried. Lily's mom gave her cold water, but it didn't help. Lily was sad and never wanted to eat a spicy pepper again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys and her furniture. One day, she found a magic wand in her toy box. She was very excited and wanted to try it out. Sue waved the wand at her delicate tea set and said, "Shrink!" To her surprise, the tea set became very small. She was so happy and called her friend, Tom. "Tom, look what I did! I made my tea set tiny!" Tom was amazed and wanted to try the wand too. They played with the wand and made all their furniture shrink. They had so much fun in their tiny world. But soon, it was time to make everything big again. They waved the wand and said, "Grow!" All the furniture grew back to normal size, and they put the magic wand away. Sue and Tom knew they had a special secret and promised to play with the magic wand again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a blue cat. The cat loved to play with yarn. One day, the cat found a big ball of yarn. The yarn was also blue, just like the cat. The blue cat played with the blue yarn all day. The cat rolled the yarn here and there. The cat was very happy. The cat wanted to tell all its friends about the yarn. The blue cat told its friends about the blue yarn. The friends came to see the yarn. They all played with the blue yarn together. They had a lot of fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a fast ambulance was speeding down the road. The ambulance had to help a sick person. The person felt very uncomfortable. The ambulance went very fast to get to the person. Inside the ambulance, there was a kind man. He talked to the sick person. He said, "Don't worry, we will make you feel better soon." The sick person felt a little better after the kind man talked to them. The ambulance got to the doctor's place. The doctor helped the sick person. The sick person felt better and thanked the kind man. The kind man was happy to help. The ambulance went back to its home, ready to help more people.

Lily and Ben love to play in the sand. They make castles and tunnels and bridges. One day, they see something shiny in the water. They run to the edge and look. It is a big octopus! The octopus has eight arms and many colors. It waves at them and smiles. Lily and Ben are not scared. They wave back and smile too. They want to be friends with the octopus. They ask the octopus if it wants to play with them. The octopus nods and comes closer. It tells them its name is Ollie. Ollie says he likes to play with shells and rocks and seaweed. Lily and Ben say they like to play with those too. They have fun playing together. They make a necklace for Ollie with shells and seaweed. Ollie makes a crown for Lily with rocks and sand. Ben makes a sword with a stick and a shell. They pretend to be a princess, a knight and a king. They get hungry and decide to have a picnic. Lily and Ben have sandwiches and juice. Ollie has fish and seawater. He offers them some fish, but they say no thank you. They say fish is too spicy for them. Ollie says he likes spicy food. He says fish makes him strong and smart. They eat and talk and laugh. They tell each other stories and jokes and secrets. They believe they are the best friends in the world. They hug and say goodbye. They promise to see each other again soon. They are happy.
