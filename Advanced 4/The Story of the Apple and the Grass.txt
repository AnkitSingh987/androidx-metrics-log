Title: The Story of the Apple and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was feeling very hungry. He wanted something to eat! He saw some grass and decided to try eating it. He bit into the grass and it split. He chewed on the grass and it was not very tasty but he was still very hungry. The little boy kept looking for something else to eat and then he saw a big apple tree. He was so happy! He ran to the tree and climbed it. At the top, he saw lots and lots of apples. He picked one off the tree, took a big bite, and the apple split! The boy was so happy. He ate the whole apple and it was so sweet and tasty. He was not hungry any more. After he ate the apple he got down from the tree and went on his way. As he was walking, he saw lots of grass and smiled. He was thankful for the lovely apple that filled his tummy and made him not hungry any more!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to dress in her fine clothes. She had a big box full of pretty dresses, hats, and shoes. Every day, Lily would pick out her favorite clothes and wear them to play outside. One sunny day, Lily was playing in her yard when she saw a little bird. The bird looked sad and cold. Lily had an idea! She ran inside and took out her fine clothes from the box. She dressed the little bird in a tiny hat and a warm coat. But something unexpected happened! The bird started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a little bird, but a magic bird! The magic bird thanked Lily for her kindness and gave her a special gift. From that day on, Lily's box of fine clothes never ran out, and she always had something new and pretty to wear.

Once upon a time, there was a big, elderly dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a girl named Lucy. Lucy gave Max lots of love every day. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, Lucy and Max went to the park. They saw a little cat that could not move. The cat was sad and scared. Lucy and Max wanted to help the little cat. Lucy picked up the cat and took it home. They gave the cat food, water, and lots of love. Soon, the cat could move again. The cat was very happy. Lucy, Max, and the cat became the best of friends. They played together every day and shared lots of love.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that was very smooth. Lily loved her dress so much that she wanted to wear it every day. One day, her mommy told her, "Lily, you can't wear your dress today. It's dirty and needs to be washed." But Lily didn't want to listen. She insisted, "No, mommy! I want to wear my dress now!" Her mommy tried to tell her no, but Lily didn't listen. She put on her dirty dress and went outside to play. While she was playing, Lily got her dress even dirtier. It got muddy and wet. When she went home, her mommy was sad. "Lily, I told you not to wear the dress. Now it's ruined," her mommy said. Lily felt bad for not listening to her mommy. She wished she had listened, and now her pretty, smooth dress was gone forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved ice-cream very much. One sunny day, her mom gave her some money to buy her favorite ice-cream. As Lily walked to the ice-cream shop, she saw her friend Tom. Tom was sad because his toy was broken. Lily wanted to help, so she used her ice-cream money to buy glue for Tom. They tried to fix the toy, but it was still dry and not working. Tom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Your ice-cream money is gone." Lily smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you." Lily went home without ice-cream, but she was happy because she helped her friend. Her mom saw her and asked, "Where is your ice-cream?" Lily told her mom what happened. Her mom hugged her and said, "I am proud of you for being kind and forgiving. Let's go get some ice-cream together." The moral of the story is to always be kind and forgiving to your friends.
