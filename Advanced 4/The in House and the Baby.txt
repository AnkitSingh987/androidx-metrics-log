Title: The in House and the Baby

A mommy, a daddy, and a baby lived in a house. The baby wanted to learn new things and get better and better. Mommy and Daddy said they would help, so they got very busy. Mommy and Daddy worked with the baby everyday. They taught the baby how to spell his name. They showed him how to make big and small shapes with blocks. They even taught the baby how to do a few dances. The baby was so proud of all the things he could do. He tried hard everyday to improve and learn more. Soon he was spelling words, counting numbers, and dancing very well. Mommy, Daddy, and the baby were very proud and happy. The baby was very happy to show his friends all his new skills!

Once there was a gifted girl named Pen. She had a magical pen with special ink. One day she decided to squeeze the ink out of the pen. But when she squeezed the pen, the ink spilled all over her clothes. Pen was worried and she didn't know what to do. She tried to wipe the ink off with a cloth but it didn't help. The ink kept spreading and started to ruin all of Pen's clothes! Pen was so sad. With no other options, she had to throw away all her clothes. She had lost her special ink, and now she had nothing else either.

Once upon a time, there was a big, filthy bell in a small town. Every day, at sunset, the bell would ring. All the people in the town would come to see and hear the bell ring. They loved the sound it made. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to see the filthy bell up close. He asked his mom if he could go and see it. His mom said, "No, Tim. It is too dirty and not safe for you." But Tim did not listen. He wanted to see the bell so badly. When the sunset came, Tim went to the bell. He touched the filthy bell and started to feel sick. He fell down and could not get up. The people in the town heard the bell ring, but they did not know that Tim was there. Tim stayed there and nobody found him. The bell kept ringing, but Tim was not happy.

Once upon a time there was a playful monkey. He kept running and jumping around his cage. Everyday he saw the little birds flying outside and he always wanted to join them. One day he noticed something strange. He saw a man taking something out of his pocket. He was holding a little red box. Inside it was white gas coming out. The man was walking around and the gas spread everywhere. The monkey kept watching with curiosity. But suddenly it felt a strange sensation. His feet became very light, so he started to jump higher and higher in his cage. It made him very happy so he kept jumping higher and higher, up to the top of the cage. When he reached the top, he saw the birds just outside his cage. He was so excited to join them that he started screaming! The man heard him and finally understood what had happened. The gas was magical and it made the monkey very playful. From that day on, the man and the monkey became best friends. The man would let him out of the cage every weekend and the monkey would keep playing with the birds!

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, lived a girl named Lily. She had a big red ball. One day, Lily went to the park to play with her ball. While playing, she met a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. They decided to play together. Lily said, "Tim, can you carry my ball?" Tim said, "Yes, Lily, I can carry your ball." As they played, Lily's ball hit her lip. She felt a little pain, but she was okay. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Lily." Lily said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's keep playing." They played and played until the sun went down. Then, they went back to their homes. Lily and Tim became good friends and played together every day.
