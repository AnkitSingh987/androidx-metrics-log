Title: The Ancient Tree and the Wolf

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was an ancient tree. Under this tree, there lived a wolf. The wolf was very big and strong. He loved to play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, the wolf saw a little girl walking in the forest. He wondered, "Who is this girl? Why is she here?" The wolf decided to talk to the girl. He said, "Hello, little girl. What are you doing in the forest?" The little girl was not scared of the wolf. She said, "I am looking for my lost toy. Can you help me find it?" The wolf was very surprised. He did not know that little girls can be so brave. Together, they looked for the toy. At last, they found it near a small pond. The little girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you, wolf. You are very kind." The wolf smiled and said, "You are welcome. I am glad I could help." From that day on, the wolf and the little girl became best friends. They played together every day, and the forest was a happy place. The other animals were surprised, but they learned that even a big, strong wolf can be kind and helpful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big tooth that was very loose. He wanted to sort his toys, but he could not stop thinking about his tooth. He was very persistent, so he kept wiggling it. One day, while Tim was sorting his toys, his mom came in. She saw him wiggling his tooth and said, "Tim, let me help you with that tooth." Tim looked at his mom and nodded. He was happy that she would help him. Mom gently pulled Tim's tooth and out it came! Tim was so excited. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim and his mom put the tooth under his pillow for the tooth fairy. That night, Tim slept well, and the tooth fairy left a shiny coin for him.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a special pillow that she loved very much. The pillow was soft and made her feel safe when she slept. One day, it was very icy outside. Lily's mommy told her to accept that they couldn't go out to play. Lily was sad, but she knew her mommy was right. So, she took her pillow and sat near the window to watch the icy world outside. As she watched, she saw a small bird shivering in the cold. Lily wanted to help, so she took her pillow outside and shared it with the bird. The bird was happy and warm, and Lily felt good about helping her new friend. They both sat on the pillow and watched the icy day together.

Rose was a three year old girl. She was an alert girl and loved to take pictures. Rose always felt happy when she was looking at pictures. When she took pictures, she would smile for each one. Today, Rose was going on a special trip with her Mom. She was so excited and alert. She kept taking pictures of everything she saw. Everywhere she looked, she would smile and take another picture. Later, when Rose got home, she looked over all the pictures she had taken. They were so special. Every time she looked at them, she would smile. Rose hung the pictures up in her room, so she could smile whenever she wanted!

Molly was playing outside in the park when she found a small tire. She seemed excited to see it, because it was oddly shaped and harmless. Molly had never seen a tire so she picked it up and started to play with it. Suddenly, she heard a voice from behind her. She turned around, and a kind stranger was there. He asked Molly, "What have you found?" Molly showed him the tire and said, "It seems like a harmless tire!" The stranger smiled and nodded. He said, "Yes, it is a tire, and it looks harmless too. But do you know what a tire does?" Molly shook her head. The stranger explained, "Tires help things move. Can you try rolling it around?" Molly smiled and tried to roll the tire. She discovered that it actually moved! She was intrigued and ran off to tell her parents. She knew she would never forget her first tire.
