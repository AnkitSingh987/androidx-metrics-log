Title: The Brush and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a brush. The brush had many friends. They all lived in a big box. The box was their home. They were happy together. One day, the brush and a pen had a quarrel. They were not happy. The other friends did not like the quarrel. They wanted the brush and pen to be happy again. The friends talked to the brush and pen. They told them to stop the quarrel. The brush and the pen listened. They said sorry. They were not dead friends anymore. They were happy friends again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bunny. The bunny wanted to build a house. So, he took some blocks and started to build. The bunny needed a measure to make sure his house was big enough. He used a small stick to measure the blocks. The bunny was very happy with his measure. The adorable bunny's house was done. It was a very nice house. The bunny was proud of his house. He played inside it all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ralph was an adventurous three year old who often went for walks with his mum. One day, as they walked past a spooky wood, this is what Ralph's mum said: “Maybe we should stay away from the wood - it looks quite scary.” Ralph loved exploring and wasn't scared of anything, so he asked: “Why can't I go in?” His mum replied: “It's too dangerous, Ralph. I can't recommend it.” Ralph got a bit cross and started stomping around saying that he wasn't stupid. The next day, as Ralph and his mum passed the wood again, Ralph told his mum that he had thought of a pattern. He said: “If I count up to twenty, I can go in and see what it's like.” His mum thought it was a silly idea and said: “That's a stupid pattern, Ralph. You must stay away from the wood.” But Ralph was determined to explore, so he started counting and ran into the wood as soon as he reached twenty. His mum chased him in and eventually managed to get him back out. They never went back to the spooky wood again.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went for a walk. Daisy saw a bird in a cage. The bird looked sad and miserable. Daisy wanted to help the bird feel better. Daisy said to the bird, "Why are you so sad?" The bird replied, "I want to fly and be free, but I am stuck in this cage." Daisy decided to help the bird. She opened the cage door to release the bird. The bird was very happy to be free. It flew around Daisy and sang a happy song. Daisy felt happy too. They both played together in the sun. The bird thanked Daisy for her help, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big wind. It blew through the woods and over the hills. It could be heard from far away. In the woods there was a small house. Inside the house, a family of four lived. They were a very happy family. One day, the wind was so strong that it knocked down the family's front door. The family was very surprised! Mum said, "We need to find something to fix the door". Dad looked out of the window and said, "I know! We can ask the wind to help us". Mum and the children looked at Dad and laughed. Dad went outside and yelled at the wind, "You need to settle down!" The wind wasn't very pleased with Dad, so it kept blowing and knocking things down. But Dad kept yelling, "You need to be more polite!" The wind seemed to understand and it slowly started to settle down. Dad fixed the door in no time. The family was very thankful to the wind. From then on, the family was always very polite to the wind, no matter how rude it got.

There was a cat. He was very furry. He lived in a house and everyone loved him. He was always so happy. One day something bad happened. The cat got sick. He couldn't play anymore and he felt so bad. Everyone tried to help him heal, but nothing worked. The cat kept getting worse and worse. He just couldn't get better. Everyone was so sad. The cat passed away. Everyone was so heartbroken. They all cried for him. Even though they tried to heal him, the cat never got better. He was gone forever.
