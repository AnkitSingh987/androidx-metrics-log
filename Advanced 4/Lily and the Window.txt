Title: Lily and the Window

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a pretty house. She loved to look at the big landscape outside her window. One day, she saw a new friend outside. It was a cute bunny! Lily wanted to welcome the bunny into her home. Lily opened the door and the bunny hopped in. She gave the bunny some tasty carrots to eat. They played and laughed together. Lily was very happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened! The bunny started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a bunny at all! It was a big, friendly dog! Lily was surprised, but she still loved her new friend. They played together, and Lily knew she had a special friend forever.

Once upon a time there was a big hut. Inside the hut, there were two friends, Dave and Ben. Dave and Ben enjoyed playing together inside the hut. One day, Dave said to Ben, "Let's have a clap game". Ben thought this sounded like fun and said yes. Dave said, "I will clap my hands five times and you have to clap your hands five times too. Then we will both clap our hands three times. Ready, set, go!" The two friends started the clapping game. They clapped their hands, one, two, three, four and five together. Then, they clapped three times. It was lots of fun. At the end of the game, Dave laughed and said, "Let's play the clapping game again, Ben!". So they both clapped their hands in the big hut!

Once upon a time, there was a kind horse named Happy. Happy lived in a stable. The stable was his home. Happy had a job. His job was to complete tasks every day. Happy did regular tasks like carrying food and giving rides to people. One day, Happy saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Happy wanted to help the bird. He thought about his regular tasks, but he knew the bird needed help. Happy decided to complete a new task. He carried the bird to a safe place where it could get better. As days went by, the bird got better. It could fly again. The bird was very thankful to Happy. Happy felt good about helping the bird. He learned that sometimes it is important to help others, even if it means not doing your regular tasks. This made Happy a better horse and friend.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. One day, he went walking in the woods. As he walked, he discovered something ugly. He had found a giant snake! He was very scared. But then he had an idea. He decided to be brave and catch the snake. He grabbed the snake and it tried to bite him. But he held it tight and took it home with him. He was very proud of himself. The hunter was now a brave hero and everyone was happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with water. He would splash and laugh all day long. One sunny day, Tim saw a big tub of water. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim ran to the tub and tried to lift it. But it was too heavy for him. He looked around and saw a handle on the side of the tub. Tim grabbed the handle and pulled it up. Suddenly, the water started to come out. Tim was worried. He did not want all the water to go away. But then, something unexpected happened. The water turned into tiny, colorful balls. They bounced and rolled all over the place. Tim was not worried anymore. He played with the colorful balls and had lots of fun. The big tub of water had a surprise for Tim, and it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a red cap that she loved very much. She wore her cap every day. All her friends were envious of her cap. They wished they had a cap like Amy's. One day, Amy went for a walk in the park. While she was playing, she lost her cap. She looked and looked, but she could not find it. Amy was very sad. She went home without her cap. The next day, Amy went back to the park to look for her cap. She saw a little boy wearing her cap. Amy was about to get mad, but then she saw that the little boy was very happy with her cap. Amy decided to let the little boy keep her cap. She discovered that sharing can make others happy, and that made her happy too. In the end, Amy made a new friend, and they played together in the park every day. All her friends saw how happy Amy was, and they learned the importance of sharing. They were not envious anymore. They all became better friends because they learned to share and be happy for each other.
