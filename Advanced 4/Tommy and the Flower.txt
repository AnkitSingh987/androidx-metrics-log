Title: Tommy and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to wander. His name was Tommy and he was very excited. Yesterday, he decided to wander around his backyard. He looked at rocks and plants that were in his backyard. He even spotted a ladybug crawling on a flower! Tommy was so excited that he decided to start exploring more. He crawled into a bush and found some colorful eggs. Tommy had a lot of fun exploring in his backyard and was very happy. He had the best day yesterday!

Once upon a time, there was a big aeroplane. It was blue and shiny. The aeroplane liked to fly high in the sky. But sometimes, the aeroplane felt lazy. It did not want to go up in the sky. One sunny day, the aeroplane was feeling very lazy. It stayed on the ground and would not fly. A little girl named Amy came to the aeroplane. She wanted to see it fly. "Why are you not flying?" she asked the aeroplane. "I feel lazy today," said the aeroplane. "I do not want to fly." Amy thought for a moment. She had an idea. "I will help you feel better, and then you can fly," she said. Amy took a soft cloth and started to wipe the aeroplane. She wiped its wings, its body, and its windows. The aeroplane started to feel better. It felt clean and shiny again. "Thank you, Amy," said the aeroplane. "Now I am ready to fly!" The aeroplane went up into the sky. It flew high and fast. Amy waved goodbye and smiled. She was happy to see the aeroplane fly.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big mystery box in his yard. He was scared because he did not know what was inside. He called his best friend, Sue, to come and see the box. When Sue came over, they both looked at the box. Tim said, "Do you know what's inside?" Sue said, "No, let's open it." They opened the box and found a big pile of apples. But the apples were not good to eat. They were all rot. Tim and Sue were sad because they wanted to eat the apples, but they could not. They decided to throw the rot apples away and play with their toys instead. They were not scared of the mystery box anymore, and they had fun playing together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to observe things. One day when she was playing, she noticed that it started to get cloudy and she heard a loud noise. It was thunder, a noise made by the sky. She was curious and wanted to know what was happening, so she ran to the window to observe. She saw that it was raining and there was a shower of rain. She loved the rain and thought it was really fun. As she was looking out of the window, she noticed that a man on the street was being rude to another man. He was shouting loudly and using mean words. The little girl didn't like it at all and thought it was wrong. She wanted to tell him to stop, but she was too shy. The moral of the story is that it isn't nice to be rude and we shouldn't do it because it can hurt other people. We should always be kind and respectful to everyone.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy who went to the park with her friend. Lucy was so excited for her trip to the park; it was a new park and she had never been there before. When Lucy got to the park, she was a little confused. She did not know what to do or where to start. She turned to her friend and asked "What do we do now?" Her friend smiled and shrugged. "Let's just explore," her friend said. So they both set off and walked around the park. Lucy saw so many new things; from the swings to the slides and even a pond with lots of ducks swimming in it. Friends had been such a great help for Lucy; she was thankful for them. She looked at her friend and smiled. The smiled back. "Let's have fun," her friend said. And with that, the two set off to explore the new park together.
