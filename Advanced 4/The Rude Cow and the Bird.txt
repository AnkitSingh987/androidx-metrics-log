Title: The Rude Cow and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a rude cow. The cow liked to make fun of other animals on the farm. She would laugh at the pig when he got dirty and tease the sheep when they got a haircut. One day, the cow saw a little bird trying to make a nest. The cow thought it would be funny to mess up the nest. She went up to the bird and started to pull the sticks and leaves out of the nest. The little bird was very sad and asked the cow to stop, but the cow just laughed. In the end, the other animals on the farm decided they did not want to play with the rude cow anymore. The cow was all alone and felt very sad. She wished she had been nice to the other animals, but it was too late. The rude cow had lost all her friends.

One day, a girl named Lily went to a ranch with her mom. At the ranch, there were many animals like cows, horses, and pigs. Lily was very excited to see all the animals. Lily saw a silly horse that liked to snap its teeth. She said, "Mom, look at the silly horse!" Her mom laughed and said, "Yes, it's a very silly horse." Suddenly, the silly horse started to dance! Lily and her mom were very surprised. They didn't know that horses could dance. Everyone at the ranch laughed and clapped. The silly horse was very happy and kept dancing.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a shop. The shop was a nice place. The shop owner was a compassionate man. He liked to help people. The shop had many things like toys, food, and clothes. One day, a little boy came to the shop. He wanted to buy a toy. He picked a toy car. The compassionate shop owner saw the boy and smiled. He gave the boy the toy car for free. The boy was very happy. Soon, it was time to shut the shop. The compassionate shop owner closed the door. He went home to rest. The next day, he opened the shop again. He was ready to help more people. And they all lived happily in the small town.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to prepare a garden. She went to the store and bought many plants. She was so happy to start her garden. She started to dig holes and put the plants in them. Her friend Tom saw her garden and felt envious. He said, "I want a garden too, but I don't have any plants." Lily thought for a moment and said, "You can have some of my plants, Tom. We can share." Tom was happy, and they both worked together to prepare their gardens. They planted flowers, fruits, and vegetables. Their gardens were very beautiful. They were both proud of their hard work.

Once there was a nice family with two children. The mom and the dad wanted to have a discuss, so the dad got out his journal. They decided to talk about what the children wanted to do today. The children had lots of ideas of what they could do. The oldest wanted to run around in the garden, but the youngest wanted to practice drawing pictures. The mom and dad listened to both ideas before deciding. They thought that the children could do both! They could draw for a little bit and then play in the garden. The children were so excited. They thanked their mom and dad for being so nice and for helping them choose what to do. The family had a great day together. They were all so happy that they had had a nice discuss about what to do.

Once there was a little girl named Kate. She was 3 years old and very curious about the world around her. She had a big puzzle with lots of pieces and she was trying very hard to put it together. Suddenly she frowned because she couldn't fit all the pieces together. She was being very careful as she tried each puzzle piece. She tried and tried, but still, it wouldn't fit. The little girl was getting frustrated, but she kept trying. Then, all of a sudden, a light bulb went off in her head. She smiled as she carefully put each puzzle piece in the right spot. Little by little, the puzzle started to fit together and soon it was complete! Kate was proud of herself and very happy. She smiled widely and hugged the completed puzzle. She had worked so hard and had been so careful. She was really glad she was able to put it together all by herself.
