Title: The Box and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a clean room, there was a big box. Inside the box, there were many toys. One toy was a phone that could play music. The phone had buttons with numbers on them. Every day, the little girl, Amy, played with the phone and listened to the music. One day, Amy pressed a new number on the phone. The music stopped, and a voice said, "Hello!" Amy was so surprised. She did not know the phone could talk. The voice asked, "Do you want to play a game?" Amy said, "Yes!" The voice told Amy to find the toy with the same number she pressed. Amy looked around the clean room and found the toy. When she picked it up, the music started again. But this time, the toys started to dance! Amy laughed and danced with the toys. From that day on, every time she pressed a number, the toys would dance and play with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tim and his friend Sam were playing with a ball. They kicked the ball with their foot and had a lot of fun. As they played, a bitter wind blew. The wind was so strong that it took the ball away. Tim and Sam were sad. They didn't know what to do without the ball. Just then, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird had the ball in its beak! It dropped the ball back to Tim and Sam. They were so happy and surprised! They thanked the bird and went back to playing, sharing the ball and having fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a tight box. She wanted to open it. Inside the box, there was a big lollipop. Lucy was very happy. She loved lollipops! Lucy tried to open the lollipop, but it was hard. She pulled and pulled, but the wrapper was too tight. She asked her mom for help. Her mom tried to open the lollipop too, but it was still too tight. Then, something unexpected happened. The lollipop started to wiggle and shake! Lucy and her mom were surprised. The lollipop jumped out of the wrapper and turned into a fun toy! Lucy and her mom laughed and played with the toy all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dance. One day, she decided to make a dance show for her friends. Lily wanted her show to be organized. So, she made a plan. Lily practiced her dance every day. She also asked her friends to help her. They worked together to make the show look nice. Lily knew that if they worked hard, the result would be great. She could feel that something good was going to happen. Finally, the day of the dance show came. All the friends danced together, and it was beautiful. Everyone clapped and cheered. The result was amazing! Lily was very happy. The moral of the story is: when you work hard and stay organized, you can achieve great things.

Once there was a tall whale named Matilda. She was the tallest whale ever seen. One day Matilda had a surprise for a special friend. She took the surprise and locked it away in a box. Matilda swam to the beach and waited for her friend to arrive. A little girl rode up on a bicycle. She waved at Matilda. Matilda smiled and said, "Hello! Do you like surprises?" The little girl replied, "I do! What's the surprise?" "I've locked it away so it can't escape, and you must find it," Matilda said. The little girl looked around and asked, "Where should I look?" Matilda pointed up at the sky and said, "The key to the box is up there. Look near the stars, you'll find it there." The little girl looked up and found the key. She unlocked the box and inside was a magical toy whale! She was so excited, she thanked Matilda and gave her a big hug. Matilda smiled and said, "You're welcome. I'm glad you liked your surprise!"
