Title: Kate and the Bathtub

Kate was feeling terrible. She had wanted to take a nice bath, but her bathtub was broken. She thought and thought, then she had an idea. She would ask her good friend Judy to help. Kate said to Judy, “My bathtub is broken. Can you help?” Judy smiled and said, “Of course. I will come over right away.” Judy came over with her tools and looked at Kate's bathtub. “It looks like it needs a new part,” she said. “I can go and get one for you.” Kate smiled and said, “Thank you! That would be very helpful.” Judy went and got the part and offered to fix the bathtub for Kate. But when she put the part in, it was terrible. The bathtub was still broken and Judy had to leave. Kate was disappointed and she knew she would have to look for a new bathtub.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big book called a dictionary. He liked to look at the pictures and learn new words. The dictionary was very heavy, but Tim carried it everywhere he went. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his dictionary. He was so dizzy from spinning around that he didn't see the ball coming. The ball struck the dictionary, and it fell on the ground. Tim was sad because his dictionary got dirty. Tim's mom saw him and came to help. She wiped the dirt off the dictionary and gave it back to Tim. Tim felt happy and smiled. He thanked his mom and went back to playing with his friends. From that day on, Tim was more careful with his dictionary at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a card on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. The card had a picture of a cat on it. Tim thought the cat looked stupid because it had a big hat on its head. He laughed at the cat and showed the card to his mom. Tim's mom told him that the card was magic. She said if he said the word "disappear", the cat would go away. Tim wanted to see if it was true. He held the card tightly in his hand and said, "Disappear!" Just like that, the stupid cat on the card was gone! Tim was so happy and surprised. He ran to his mom and told her what happened. They both laughed and clapped their hands. From that day on, Tim always looked for more magic cards to make things disappear.

One day, a big dog named Max wanted to hold a lecture for his animal friends. He thought he was very smart and could teach everyone. The other animals thought Max was foolish, but they wanted to see what he had to say. Max started to talk about how to find food. Just then, a little mouse named Tim ran up to Max. Tim was very fast and grabbed some food that Max had put out for his lecture. Max was mad and started to chase Tim. The animals laughed as they watched Max chase Tim. They thought Max was foolish for trying to catch the fast little mouse. In the end, Max learned that he should not try to teach others if he did not know everything himself.

Once upon a time there was a mommy who was not feeling very fit. She went to the doctor and he said that she needed to rest. He asked for a delivery to come to take her home. A big truck came and the driver said that he would deliver her home. He helped her into the truck and drove very carefully. When she arrived home, the mommy felt much better. The doctor said that she must rest and get fit again. So she did and soon she was as fit as she could be!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a shop. This shop was very special. It had incredible flowers that were very pretty. People loved to visit the shop and look at the flowers. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the shop with her mom. They saw a flower that was about to bloom. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can we buy this flower?" Her mom said, "Yes, we can take it home and watch it bloom." They took the flower home and put it in a pot. Every day, Lily and her mom would water the flower and wait for it to bloom. One sunny morning, the flower opened up and showed its incredible colors. Lily and her mom were so happy to see the flower bloom. They knew that their love and care had helped the flower grow big and strong.
