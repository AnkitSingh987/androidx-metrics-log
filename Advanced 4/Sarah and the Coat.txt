Title: Sarah and the Coat

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. Sarah was very brave and she liked to explore. One day Sarah went for a walk in the park. As she walked, Sarah started to shiver. She realised it was getting cold and she didn't have a coat. Then she spotted a coat in the grass with a label on it. It looked just fine. She tried it on and it fit perfectly. Sarah was so happy about finding the coat. She stayed warm for the rest of her walk in the park. When she got home, she thanked her lucky stars. She was so happy!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a pet cat named Fluffy. They loved to play together. One day, Sally was playing with Fluffy and her ball. But Sally threw the ball too hard, and it hit Fluffy. Fluffy was very sad and hurt. Sally said, "I'm sorry, Fluffy. I didn't mean to hurt you. Please forgive me." Fluffy looked at Sally and knew she was sorry. Fluffy forgave Sally and they hugged. Later, they saw a dead bug on the ground. Sally and Fluffy looked at it, but they didn't touch it. They knew it was not good to play with dead things. Instead, they found a new ball and played together, being careful not to hurt each other. They were happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a very stubborn dog. He wanted to go out, but his owner said it was too cold to go out. The dog started to bark and wouldn't stop. He was being very stubborn. The owner tried to reason with the dog, but he wouldn't listen. She grabbed some water in a cup and threw it on the dog. The water made the dog stop barking. He shook off the water and then bit the cup. The owner was mad, but the dog was happy to be outside. He started running around outside, but couldn't shake off the water. It was still on his fur, so he kept getting wet. As the day went on, the dog wanted less and less water. He stayed stubborn, so the water stayed on him. The owner laughed when she saw the dog with all the water on his fur. She said, "That's what happens when you're stubborn." And she was right. The dog learned his lesson and was more careful the next time.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little forest, there lived a parrot named Polly. Polly was a very happy parrot who loved to eat fruits. One day, while Polly was eating her favorite fruit, an apple, she saw a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi looked very sad, so Polly asked, "Why are you sad, Mimi?" Mimi said, "I am hungry, but I can't find any food to eat." Polly felt sorry for Mimi and decided to share her apple with her. "Here, Mimi, you can eat half of my apple," said Polly. Mimi was very happy and ate the apple with Polly. From that day on, Polly and Mimi became best friends and always shared their food with each other. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy, and they lived happily ever after in their quiet little forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a thin boy named Tom. Tom loved to go outside and play under the big blue sky. He liked to watch the white clouds move and change shapes. He would lie on the grass and permit his mind to dream big dreams. One sunny day, Tom was playing outside when he saw a very big cloud. It was bigger than all the other clouds. The big cloud moved closer and closer to Tom. Suddenly, the big cloud changed into a giant cloud man! The cloud man was friendly and wanted to play with Tom. Tom and the cloud man played together all day. They had so much fun! But then, the sun started to set, and it was time for Tom to go home. The cloud man had a surprise for Tom. With a big smile, the cloud man turned back into a regular cloud and floated away. From that day on, every time Tom saw a big cloud, he would remember his special friend and smile.
