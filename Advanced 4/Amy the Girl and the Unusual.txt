Title: Amy the Girl and the Unusual

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big box of blocks. One day, she decided to build an unusual stack of blocks. She put a big block on the bottom, then a small block on top, and then a round block on top of that. She was very proud of her unusual stack. Amy wanted to show her friend, Tom. She said, "Tom, come here! I want to introduce you to my unusual stack of blocks." Tom came over and looked at the stack. He said, "Wow, that is unusual! I like it." Later, Amy and Tom played with the blocks together. They built more unusual stacks. They put the stacks next to each other and made a big city. They had so much fun playing with the unusual stacks of blocks. And they knew they would always remember the day they built the unusual city together.

Once upon a time, a little girl called Sarah was playing in her backyard. She heard a noise coming from the other side of the fence and decided to investigate. When Sarah looked over the fence, she saw a funny-looking man. He was wearing a bright-coloured hat, and held a plate of spicy food. He gave Sarah a big smile and said "Hello, my name's Joe. Would you like to have dinner with me?" Sarah was excited. She ran and got her mother and father, who both applauded when they heard the invitation. Together, the family had dinner with Joe. They laughed and talked, and Sarah was especially excited to learn about the spicy foods he was eating. When dinner was finished, Sarah was sad to say goodbye. But she couldn't forget about their dinner in the backyard and how much fun they had. She thanked Joe and waved as he walked away.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play with his toys in his room. One day, he heard his mom mention a big word called "space." Tom was very curious and asked his mom about it. His mom told him that space is a big place above the sky, where there are stars and planets. It is very difficult to go there. But, if we work hard and help each other, we can reach it. Tom was excited and wanted to learn more about space. He started to study and learn about space with the help of his mom and dad. They read books and talked about it every day. As Tom grew up, he worked hard and became a smart person. He always remembered the lesson his mom taught him: with hard work and helping each other, nothing is too difficult. And that is how Tom became a great person who helped many people to learn about space.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a band in the park. He was very happy. He put the band on his arm and went to play with his friends. Tim showed the band to his friends. They all liked it. Then, they played a game. They had to number the trees in the park. They counted one, two, three, and so on. They had a lot of fun. All of a sudden, a big, fearful dog came running towards them. Tim's friends ran away, but Tim was too scared to move. The dog jumped at Tim and took the band from his arm. Tim was very sad and cried. The dog ran away with the band, and Tim never saw it again.

The little boy was so tired and dirty after playing in his garden all day. His mum said it was time for his bath. He loved to bathe - it felt so good. He went into the bathroom and saw all the bubbles in the bath. He splashed around and made them swirl around. He went underwater and opened his mouth wide to gulp some bubbles. He giggled. When he was clean, he put on his quiet pyjamas and his mum tucked him up in bed. But before he could go to sleep, he had to brush his teeth. He put the toothbrush in his mouth and brushed his teeth all around. Finally, he was ready for bed. He was so quiet and snuggled under his blanket. He was so ready for sweet dreams.
