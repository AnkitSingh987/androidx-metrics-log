Title: The Story of the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was very happy and loved to play. But one day she felt very serious. She looked out of her window into the land. What she saw made her shake with fear. What could it be? The little girl bravely decided to go out and explore. As she walked across the land, she began to notice more and more things that made her shake. Everywhere she looked something strange was happening. Finally the little girl came to a big tree in the middle of the land. Suddenly, the ground began to shake and a big bird flew from the tree. It had brilliant feathers and a loud voice. It said, "Fear not little girl, I am here to protect you." The little girl was relieved but still curious. She asked, "What are you protecting me from?" The bird simply answered, "From the scary thing that is coming to this land." The little girl knew her journey was not over yet, but she was comforted knowing that the bird was watching over her. A few days passed and the shaking returned. It was getting stronger and stronger, and the little girl was scared. She looked to the big bird, who was heroic and brave, and together they faced the danger. In the end they succeeded, they saved the land and everyone was safe. The little girl learned a very important lesson that day. No matter how big and serious

Once upon a time, there was a normal school. In this school, there was a pupil named Tim. Tim liked to mix colors. He had a lot of fun doing it. One day, he went to his art class to mix more colors. In the art class, Tim found a big box. The box had many colors. Tim was very happy. He started to mix the colors. He mixed red, blue, and yellow. He made many new colors. Tim was having so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Tim mixed all the colors together, the colors jumped out of the box! They started to dance around the room. Tim was surprised. He never saw colors dance before. The colors made the room very pretty. Tim and his friends laughed and danced with the colors. It was a very special day at the normal school.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big red button in his room. He did not know what the button did. Tim felt very lively and wanted to play with the button. He thought it would be fun to press it. So, he did. When he pressed the button, he heard a loud scream from outside. Tim ran to the window and saw a big angry dog. The dog was mad at a small cat. The cat was scared and said, "Help me, Tim! The dog wants to hurt me!" Tim wanted to help the cat, but he did not know what to do. He pressed the button again, hoping it would make the dog go away. But, the dog just got angrier and louder. The cat screamed again, "Tim, please help me!" Tim tried to think of a way to help the cat, but he could not. He felt sad and scared. In the end, the dog caught the cat, and the cat was hurt. Tim wished he had never found the button.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby. The mommy liked to keep things tidy. The baby liked to have fun. One day, the mommy gave the baby a pass to the park. The baby was very excited. Mommy said, "Remember to keep the pass safe." The baby promised to keep the pass safe. The baby went to the park and had a lot of fun. On the way back home, the baby noticed the pass was gone. The baby was very sad. The baby started to cry. The mommy saw the baby crying and asked, "What's wrong?" The baby said, "I lost my pass!" The mommy comforted the baby and said, "It's ok, we'll keep looking for it." The mommy and baby looked and looked, but the pass was nowhere to be found. Suddenly, the mommy spotted something on the ground. She ran to it and said, "Look, the pass!" The baby was so happy and thanked the mommy. From then on, the baby promised to keep the pass in a tidy place. The End.
