Title: Tim and the Rainbow

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy chocolate. Tim loved chocolate more than anything. He saw an ugly, green chocolate on the shelf. It looked very different from all the other chocolates. Tim asked his mom if he could buy it. His mom said yes. When they got home, Tim opened the ugly, green chocolate. He took a big bite. But then, something unexpected happened. The ugly, green chocolate made Tim's mouth turn green too! Tim's mom saw and said, "Stop! Don't eat more!" Tim stopped eating the chocolate. He was sad because he wanted to eat it all. But then, the green color in his mouth started to change. It turned into a rainbow! Tim's mom laughed, and Tim laughed too. The ugly, green chocolate had a surprise inside. It was a magic rainbow chocolate!

Once upon a time, there was a worried lawyer. One day, he went for a walk to clear his head. On his walk, he met a frog. The frog could hop very high. "Hello, Mr. Lawyer!" said the frog. "Why are you worried?" "I have a big case tomorrow, and I don't know if I can win," said the worried lawyer. The frog thought for a moment and said, "I can help you! I have a magic hop. If you hop with me, you will feel better and do a great job." The worried lawyer hopped with the frog, and he felt so much better! The next day, he won his case. The lawyer learned that sometimes, a little fun can help you do your best work.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. She had a pretty skirt that she loved to wear. One day, Kitty went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Kitty met her friend, Bunny. Bunny liked to guess games. They played a game where Kitty would hide and Bunny had to guess where she was. Kitty hid behind a tree, under a bench, and in a bush. Bunny was good at guessing and found her every time. Kitty had fun playing with Bunny, but she was still bossy. She told Bunny to wear a skirt too. Bunny didn't want to wear a skirt, but she did it to make Kitty happy. They played and laughed all day long. In the end, Kitty learned that being bossy wasn't always the best way to be a good friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted a banana. She went to the store and asked for one, but the store keeper was very rude. He said he was too busy to give her a banana. The little girl was very sad but she thought of a clever plan. She started to smile and said to him: "Please, can I have a banana? I am so hungry and they look so yummy!" The store keeper still looked a bit grumpy, but then he smiled too. He gave her a big, ripe banana and said "Here, take it! And don't forget to be polite next time." The little girl thanked him and happily ran out of the store. She ate the banana in no time and afterwards she thought to herself: "I guess being polite helps sometimes!"

Once upon a time, in a small town, all the people were rich. They had many things. One evening, the people wanted to unite and have a big party. They were so happy to be together. At the party, they danced and ate yummy food. They played fun games. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took away all their things. The people were so sad. But the wind did not take away their friends. They still had each other. They learned that being together was more important than having many things. The people were still rich, but in a new way. They were rich with love and friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a best friend named Tim. They liked to play together every day. One day, they were playing in the park when something unexpected happened. A big, frightened dog came running towards them. Sally and Tim were scared, but they wanted to help the dog. So, they tried to supply the dog with some food to make it feel better. They gave the dog some of their lunch and the dog seemed to be happy. Then, the dog's owner came running over. He was happy to see that Sally and Tim had helped his dog. He told them that the dog was just scared of a loud noise. The owner was very thankful to Sally and Tim for being so kind. In the end, Sally, Tim, and the dog became good friends and they all played together in the park every day.
