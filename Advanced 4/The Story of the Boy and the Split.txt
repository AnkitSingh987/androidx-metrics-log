Title: The Story of the Boy and the Split

Once upon a time, there was a boy. He was walking down the street and feeling very tired. Suddenly, there was a loud noise and the ground split open! There was an emergency. The boy was scared and started to cry. But the brave boy had to help. He ran to get help from his mom. Mom quickly came and saw the split in the ground. It was very difficult to fix. She asked the boy to look for a clever person who could help. The boy searched and searched until he finally found a smart man. The man had the tools to fix the split. He worked very hard for many days. Finally, the split was fixed! Everyone was very happy and thanked the clever man. In the end, everyone learned that it is important to be prepared for difficult times.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to run and play. He was very fit. Every day at noon, Bobo would go to the park with his friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily had a big red ball. She told Bobo, "I will throw the ball and you catch it." Bobo was very excited. Lily threw the ball high in the sky. Bobo ran fast to catch it. But the ball went into the woods. Bobo looked in the woods for the ball. He saw the ball getting bigger and bigger. The ball was increasing in size! It was now as big as a house. Bobo was very surprised. Bobo ran back to Lily and told her what happened. Lily laughed and said, "I knew that would happen! It is a magic ball. Now we can play with a big ball!" And they played all day with the big red ball, happy and tired.

One day, a little girl named Lucy and her furry cat, Whiskers, were playing outside. Lucy found a shiny trophy in the grass. "Look, Whiskers! I found a trophy!" she said. Whiskers looked at the trophy and decided he wanted it. He jumped and took the trophy in his mouth. Lucy chased Whiskers around the yard, trying to get the trophy back. "Whiskers, give it back!" she cried. Just then, a big wind blew and the trophy flew out of Whiskers' mouth. It landed in a tree. Lucy and Whiskers looked up and saw a bird sitting on the trophy. The bird said, "Thank you for finding my trophy!" Lucy and Whiskers were surprised, but happy to help the bird.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk with his friend, Tim. Tim put a leash on Spot so he would not get lost. They walked down the street, and they saw many things. They saw a big tree, a red ball, and a blue car. Spot was very happy to be outside with Tim. As they walked, they saw a hard rock on the ground. Spot wanted to play with the rock, but Tim said, "No, it is too hard. It might hurt you." Spot listened to Tim and kept walking. They saw a soft, green grass area and Tim let Spot play there. Spot and Tim played in the grass for a long time. They played with a soft ball and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim put the leash back on Spot, and they walked back to their house. Spot was tired but happy after their fun walk.
