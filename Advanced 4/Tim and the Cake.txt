Title: Tim and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a big, red house with a high roof. In the house, lived a nice mom and her little boy, Tim. They loved to cook together in their kitchen. One day, they decided to make a big cake. As they mixed the ingredients, Tim asked, "Mom, can I put on the icing?" Mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, but you have to be patient. We have to cook the cake first." Tim nodded and waited, watching the cake through the oven door. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven and let it cool. Tim was very patient. Finally, Mom said, "Now, you can put on the icing!" Tim was so happy, and he carefully spread the icing all over the cake. They both enjoyed their delicious cake together, proud of their teamwork.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little dog named Fuzzy. Fuzzy loved to dig holes in the ground. He dug holes every day. His mom told him to be careful not to dig too deep, but Fuzzy did not listen. One day, Fuzzy dug a very big hole. He found a big round thing in the ground. Fuzzy thought it was a ball. So, he picked it up and took it to his mom. His mom looked at the round thing and knew it was not a ball. Fuzzy's mom knew that the round thing could prevent bad things from happening. She told Fuzzy to put it back in the ground. Fuzzy did not want to, but he listened to his mom. He put the round thing back in the hole and covered it up. The next day, a big storm came. The rain and wind were very strong. But Fuzzy's house did not get hurt. The round thing in the ground had kept them safe. Fuzzy learned to listen to his mom and not dig too deep. He was happy that the round thing had helped them. From that day on, Fuzzy only dug small holes and played with his toys. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sammy. She was so happy, and loved to dance. She would jump around the house, spinning and twirling, trying to be like the ballerinas she watched on TV. One day, Sammy's mom said, "Let's go to the park, Sammy! You can dance there too!" Sammy was so excited, she ran outside and jumped straight into the car. They arrived at the park and Sammy ran straight to the big tree in the middle. It looked so tall and strong, like it was going to stay forever. Sammy was impatient and couldn't wait to start dancing. Just then, a big storm came. Torrents of rain started pouring down, and everyone ran for shelter. Unfortunately, the storm was so strong, it destroyed the big tree in the park. Sammy was very sad. All of her dancing dreams were ruined. But mom said, "Don't cry Sammy. We can find somewhere else to dance!" Sammy was happy again, and they left the park together.

One day, a big jet was flying in the sky. The jet had a face and could talk. He was very hungry. He wanted to find something to eat. The jet saw a little boy on the ground. The boy was playing with a ball. The jet shouted to the boy, "Hello, I am a hungry jet. Can you help me find something to eat?" The boy was happy to help the hungry jet. They looked for food together. They found a big apple tree with lots of apples. The jet ate the apples and was not hungry anymore. The boy and the jet became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to decorate her room with pretty pictures. One day, she found a big pepper in the kitchen. Lily thought it was funny and wanted to use it in her room. Lily took the pepper and put it on her table. She found some paper and colorful pens. She drew a happy face on the pepper. Lily was very proud of her pepper friend. She put it next to her bed, so she could see it every day. At night, Lily would tell stories to her pepper friend. They had lots of fun together. Lily's room was now the happiest place in the house. And Lily knew that her pepper friend would always make her smile.
