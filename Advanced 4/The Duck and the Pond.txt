Title: The Duck and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. This duck was very enthusiastic. He loved to play with his friends in the pond. The duck liked to swim and splash in the water with his friends. One day, the duck saw a big tree. The tree had a branch that was close to the pond. The duck thought it would be fun to cut the branch and make a bridge for his friends. So, the duck found a small stick and began to cut the branch. The duck worked very hard to cut the branch. He was so enthusiastic that he did not give up. Finally, the branch fell into the pond. The duck and his friends were very happy. They played on the new bridge all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big hedge near his house. He thought, "I want to play a fun game with the hedge." Tim had a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. He decided to shoot the balls at the hedge. Tim started to shoot the balls at the hedge. He was having so much fun. Then, he saw a sad bird sitting on the hedge. The bird said, "Please don't shoot the hedge. My nest and eggs are in there." Tim felt bad for the sad bird. Tim stopped shooting the hedge. He said, "I'm sorry, bird. I didn't know your nest was there." The bird was happy that Tim stopped. Tim played a new game with the bird instead. They both had fun, and the bird was not sad anymore.

The sun was shining brightly in the sky. Jimmy was outside playing in the wet grass. He was happy and was searching for something. His mom had told him not to eat any candy until after lunch. But Jimmy wanted candy. He saw a sweet, shiny piece of candy on the ground and carefully picked it up. He quickly put it into his pocket and ran back into the house. Suddenly, Jimmy heard his mom's voice coming from the kitchen. She was calling his name, but Jimmy hid in the closet. He knew that she would prevent him from eating any candy before lunch. Jimmy stayed there for a long time until he heard his mom walking away. He waited for a few more minutes and then slowly opened the closet door. Jimmy happily pulled out the candy from his pocket. Suddenly, his mom walked back into the room and saw what he was doing. She quickly took the candy away and told Jimmy that he had to wait until lunch to eat candy. Jimmy was sad, but he knew that his mom was only trying to prevent him from getting a tummy ache. He smiled and ran outside to play with the wet grass some more.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom loved to play in the garden, but one day it started to rain very hard. He didn't know what to do. Tom went to look for his mother. He saw her inside the house, so he went in. Tom: "Mummy, why is it raining?" Mummy: "Well, it's raining because the sky is full of clouds and they have broken apart, releasing lots of tiny drops of water." Tom: "Oh, that's why it's all wet outside! I thought it was a broken number." Mummy: "No, it's not a broken number. It's just rain, and rain is very important. It helps the plants and flowers to grow and it keeps us all cool in the summer." Tom: "I see. So rain is good!" Mummy: "Yes, it is!" Tom smiled, then he ran out of the house and back into the garden, ready to play in the rain.

Sammy was feeling very tired. He had been playing all day outside in the sunshine and he wanted to relax. So he went inside and found a big bathtub with some warm water in it. He was glad to see the useful water, so he quickly got undressed and stepped in. He laid in the water and felt all the tiredness drift away. He played with his toys in the water and splashed around. The warm water felt so nice against his skin and was very helpful in helping him relax. He floated around and imagined he was a fish swimming in the sea. When he was finished, he put his clothes back on and jumped out of the tub. He felt relaxed, happy, and ready to have some fun with his friends again. Sammy was so glad he could use the water to relax.
