Title: The Story of the Sun and the Leaves

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day the little girl saw something special. She saw a big can filled with bright colors. She grabbed it and started to spray the colors. She made beautiful patterns and colors on the leaves, grass, and sky. The little girl felt happy and ran to show her mom. Mom was very surprised and happy. She grabbed her photo camera and took a picture. The photo was so quiet and peaceful. Everyone was amazed by the colors in the photo. The little girl was so excited to show her friends the colors she made. The friends loved it and they ran to get some more colors in the cans to spray. Everyone had such a good time spraying colors and making patterns they couldn't stop. The colors in the photo shined so bright and happy. It was a lovely sight. Everyone was so glad that the little girl found the colors to spray.

One day, a lovely cat named Lucy went to play outside. She saw her friend, a dog named Max. Max looked sad. Lucy asked, "Why are you sad, Max?" Max said, "My skin is dry, and it hurts." Lucy wanted to help Max. She remembered her mom had a lotion that made her skin feel good. So, Lucy and Max went to Lucy's house. Lucy's mom welcomed them inside. She gave Max the lotion to help his dry skin. Max put the lotion on his skin. It made him feel much better! He was happy again. Lucy and Max went back outside to play. They were both very happy and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mole. The mole lived in a small hole under the ground. He had many friends, like the birds and the squirrels. They all played together and had fun. One day, the mole heard a loud roar. It was a big lion! The lion was trying to find food. The mole wanted to be successful and help his friends, so he thought of a plan. The mole dug a very deep hole, and the lion fell inside. The mole's friends were safe, and they all thanked him. The mole was happy that he was successful in helping his friends. They all played together and had more fun than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to act like a big chef. He would wear a big hat and pretend to cook in his play kitchen. One day, Tim decided to make a menu for his family. He drew pictures of yummy food, like pizza, apples, and ice cream. Tim's little sister, Sally, wanted to help too. But Sally was still learning how to be careful. She spilled her juice on the menu. Now, the menu was dirty. Tim was sad, but he knew that sometimes accidents happen. Tim and Sally cleaned the dirty menu together. They used a wet cloth and made it look nice again. Then, they showed the menu to their mom and dad. Mom and dad were so proud of them. They acted like they were in a fancy restaurant and ordered food from the menu. Tim and Sally had lots of fun being chefs for the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy cat. The cat had a string on it. Tim loved to play with the cat and the string. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam was sad. Tim wanted to support Sam. He said, "Sam, do you want to play with my cat and the string?" Sam said, "Yes, thank you, Tim." Tim and Sam played with the cat and the string. They were patient and took turns. But then, the string broke. The cat could not play anymore. Tim and Sam were sad. They learned that they should be more careful when playing with toys.

Once there was a little girl named Emma. She was excited for her birthday, so she woke up early and put on her favourite party dress. She was so excited! She went outside and saw her mom and dad waiting for her. They took her to the park, where all her friends were waiting. At the park there were balloons and presents and lots of delicious treats. Emma was so excited. She could not resist the temptation to enjoy everything. But as the day went on, Emma started to get tired. She wanted to rest, but her friends were all too enthusiastic and wanted her to keep playing. She remembered what her mom always said, and tried to be serious. She tried to resist the urge to be so tired, but it was hard! Finally, it was the end of the day and all the fun was over. Emma was so relieved. She thought about how important it was to resist sometimes, and knew her mom would be proud of her. She was so glad she had a wonderful birthday, regardless of how tired she was.
