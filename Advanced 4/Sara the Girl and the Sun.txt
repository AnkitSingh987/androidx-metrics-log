Title: Sara the Girl and the Sun

One day, a little girl named Sara wanted to go outside and play. She thought she needed something to keep her cool in the hot sun. Her mom heard Sara and said, "Why don't you get the fan for your room?" Sara smiled and thought it was a great idea. She ran to her room and searched for the fan. She saw her mom's fan in the corner and said, "I want this one!" "No, Sara," said her mom. "That fan belongs to mommy. Instead, why don't you make your own fan?" Sara's thought it was a wonderful idea. She reached for some paper and scissors. She spent the day cutting the paper, folding it into different shapes, and attaching them together. Once she finished, Sara proudly held up her fan and said "Look, mommy! I made my own fan!" Her mom was impressed and said, "Sara, that was so honest of you. You did a great job!" Sara beamed with joy. She knew her mom was proud of her. She had made something special with her own hands and it felt delightful. From then on, Sara made sure to always be honest. She knew it was the right thing to do. She never took her mom's things without asking, and it made her so proud of herself that she could do the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was a kind mule named Molly. Molly lived on a small farm with her friend, a compassionate boy named Tom. They loved to play together in the warm sun. One day, it was very cold outside. Molly was shivering in the field. Tom saw Molly and said, "Molly, you are shivering! I will help you get warm." Tom ran to the barn and got a big, warm blanket. He wrapped the blanket around Molly to help her feel warm and safe. Tom and Molly sat in the field, all warm and cozy. They talked and laughed as they watched the cold wind blow. Molly was so happy to have such a caring friend like Tom. They knew they would always help each other, no matter what.

Once upon a time, there was a green house. In the green house, there was a skeleton. The skeleton was not scary, but it was sad. It wanted a friend to play with. One day, a little girl named Lucy walked by the green house. She saw the skeleton and said, "Hi, do you want to be my friend?" The skeleton was so happy, it signed "yes" with its bony hand. But then, something unexpected happened. The green house started to shake and the skeleton turned into a real boy! Lucy and the boy were so surprised, but they were happy to be friends. They played together every day, and the green house was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, there was a wise turkey. This turkey was very smart and lived in a big farm. The wise turkey had a lot of friends like cows, pigs, and chickens. One night, the wise turkey had a dream. In the dream, the turkey saw a big, beautiful farm with lots of food and space to play. The turkey wanted to share this dream with all his friends. The next day, the wise turkey told his friends about the dream. They all got very excited and wanted to find the big, beautiful farm. They looked and looked until they found it. All the animals were very happy and played together every day. And the wise turkey always remembered his special dream.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play pretend. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play on his green porch. On the porch, Tim saw a little ant. He decided to pretend to be an ant too. Tim got down on his hands and knees and crawled around. He said, "Hello, little ant! I am an ant too!" The little ant looked at Tim and seemed to smile. Tim and the ant played together on the green porch all day long. They pretended to find food and take it back to their ant home. At the end of the day, Tim's mom called him inside. Tim waved goodbye to the little ant and went inside to eat dinner. Tim told his mom all about his fun day playing pretend on the green porch with the little ant.
