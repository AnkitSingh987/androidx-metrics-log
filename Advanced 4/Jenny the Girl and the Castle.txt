Title: Jenny the Girl and the Castle

One day, a little girl named Jenny wanted to draw a picture of a castle. She got out some colour pencils, paper, and an eraser. She decided to plan what the castle should look like. Jenny started to draw. She drew very light lines to show the outline of the castle. But then there was a problem. The lines were too light and she could not see them anymore. Jenny picked up her eraser and tried to erase the lines but there was still something wrong. Soon the paper started to rip and soon there was a big and angry tear! Jenny was really sad because her drawing was ruined. No matter how hard she tried, she could not fix her drawing. Jenny tried hard not to cry, but soon she had to admit that the drawing was ruined. No matter how much she planned, she couldn't make her drawing look like a castle. Jenny put down her pencils and eraser, and cried from her loss.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jill. She was three years old and very curious. One day, while exploring, she found a needle on the ground. Being so young, Jill didn't know it was dangerous. She thought it was a toy and decided to use it. Suddenly, Jill heard her mother calling her. She became scared and quickly tried to hide the needle. Unfortunately, she accidentally pricked herself with it and started crying. Her mother quickly ran to her and saw that she had pricked herself. Jill's mom was very worried and angry. She scolded her and took the needle away. She tried to patch the puncture wound with a band aid, but it was too late - Jill was already in so much pain. No matter how hard her mother tried, it was too late. Jill was helpless. The little girl was very sad and scared. Despite her mother's best efforts to make her feel better, the pain was too unbearable, and the poor little girl was never the same again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run, jump, and play all day. One day, Spot went for a walk in the park. He saw many friends there like birds, cats, and other dogs. As Spot walked, he took a turn and saw a big, rough rock. He thought it was fun to jump on the rock. While Spot was on the rock, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue had a ball in her hand. Spot wanted to meet Sue and play with the ball. Spot ran to Sue and said, "Hi! Can I play with you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. Spot and Sue became good friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a little fish named Tuna. Tuna was an honest fish who always told the truth. Tuna had a best friend named Crab. Crab was not always honest, but they still played together. One day, Tuna and Crab found a big, shiny rock. They both wanted the rock, but there was only one. Tuna said, "Let's play a game. We will ask each other questions. If we are honest, we will repeat the right answer. The one who repeats the most right answers will get the rock." Crab agreed to play the game. Tuna and Crab started asking each other questions. Tuna was always honest, so Tuna repeated the right answers every time. Crab tried to trick Tuna, but Tuna knew the right answers. In the end, Tuna repeated more right answers than Crab. Tuna won the shiny rock! Crab learned that being honest is important, and they stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable. He did not remember why he was sad. He wanted to be happy and play with his friends. One day, Tim saw a big wall. It was very tall and wide. Tim thought, maybe if I climb the wall, I will find something fun on the other side. He tried to climb the wall, but it was too hard. This made Tim even more sad. Tim's friends saw him feeling sad. They all came together to help him climb the wall. With their help, Tim climbed the wall and saw a beautiful park on the other side. Tim was so happy and forgot about being miserable. He played with his friends and had a great time. From that day on, Tim always remembered that with the help of his friends, he could overcome any problem.
