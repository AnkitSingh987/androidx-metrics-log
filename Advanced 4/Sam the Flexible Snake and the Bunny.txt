Title: Sam the Flexible Snake and the Bunny

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Sam. Sam was very good at helping his friends. One day, Sam's friend, Bunny, needed help to open the refrigerator. Bunny was hungry and wanted some food. Sam and Bunny went to the refrigerator. Sam used his flexible body to wrap around the handle and pull it open. Bunny was very happy to see all the yummy food inside. They found carrots and apples for Bunny to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. When Sam opened the refrigerator, it made a loud noise. The noise was so loud that it scared Sam and Bunny. They ran away and left the refrigerator open. All the food got warm and went bad. Sam and Bunny felt sad because they could not eat the food anymore.

Once upon a time, Little Jimmy and his dad were walking through the forest. Jimmy looked around, taking in the sights, when suddenly he noticed something strange in the sky. He spotted a large yellow air balloon with the letter B on it. Jimmy asked his dad what the balloon was for, and his dad replied, "That balloon is for a special supply of goodies." Jimmy was so excited that he started jumping up and down. "Can I get one too?" he cried. His dad smiled and said, "Yes, but we have to get to the point first." Little Jimmy had no clue what his dad was talking about, but he followed him anyway. Soon they arrived at a small wooden shed. "This is the supply point," said his dad. Jimmy hesitantly opened the shed door, only to find several boxes of goodness stacked high. "Oh wow," Jimmy exclaimed. The boxes were so big they reached to his dad's waist! Jimmy's dad pointed to a ladder on the other side of the shed and warned him to climb it carefully, as the steps were rough. Jimmy made it to the top and grabbed a box to take home. Jimmy and his dad headed back home, both with huge smiles on their faces. They had a new supply of goodies to enjoy.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a funny boy named Tom. Tom loved to tell jokes to his friends. One day, Tom thought of a new joke to tell his best friend, Sue. Tom ran to Sue's house to tell her the joke. But on the way, he met a big dog. The dog barked at Tom and scared him. The dog wanted to spoil Tom's fun. Tom was sad and did not want the dog to ruin his joke. Tom thought for a moment and then threw a stick far away. The dog ran after the stick. Tom quickly ran to Sue's house and told her the joke. Sue laughed and laughed. Tom was happy that the dog did not spoil his joke, and they had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. Tim never cleaned his room. One day, his mom said, "Tim, you must clean your room." Tim did not want to, but he knew he should. That night, there was a big storm. Tim saw lightning outside his window. He was scared. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I am scared of the lightning." His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's go back to your room." When they got to his room, Tim saw all his toys and clothes everywhere. He felt bad and said, "Mom, I regret not cleaning my room." His mom smiled at him and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can clean it together." They cleaned his room and Tim was not scared of the lightning anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a polite farmer. He liked to work on his farm. Every day, he worked with his animals and plants. He was very happy. One day, while the farmer was working, he heard a big noise. He looked around and saw a big, shiny thing on the ground. He did not know what it was. The farmer was very surprised. The big, shiny thing started to talk! It said, "Hello, farmer! I am a magic rock. I can make your farm work all by itself!" The farmer could not believe it. The magic rock did the work for the farmer. The farmer was even more happy.
