Title: The Family and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a family. This family had a long walk to the park. One day, they went for a walk. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. On the way, the little boy saw a big rock. He wanted to move it. He said, "I want to move this rock!" He tried to push the rock, but it was too heavy. He started to struggle. He pushed and pushed, but the rock did not move. The family saw the boy struggle. They wanted to help. They all pushed the rock together. The rock moved! The family was happy. They said, "We did it!" They kept walking to the park and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Bob. Bob loved to eat sausages. One day, he saw a sausage on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to eat it. Bob went to the sausage and said, "Yummy sausage, I will eat you now!" But then, he heard a small voice. It was a tiny ant. The ant said, "Please, don't eat the sausage. It's my home!" Bob paused for a moment. He did not want to be a stupid dog and take the ant's home. Suddenly, the sausage moved! Bob and the ant were very surprised. The sausage was not a sausage at all. It was a caterpillar! The caterpillar said, "Thank you for not eating me!" Bob and the ant laughed. They became good friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily liked to examine things. She liked to look at them, touch them, smell them, and sometimes taste them. She wanted to know everything about them. She was very persistent. One day, she found a file in her dad's office. It was a big file with many papers inside. Lily was curious. She opened the file and took out some papers. She looked at the words and pictures on them. She did not understand them, but they were interesting. She heard her dad coming. She quickly put the papers back in the file and closed it. She hid the file under the sofa and ran to her room. She hoped her dad would not notice. But her dad was very smart. He saw the file under the sofa and knew it was his. He wondered who moved it. He opened the file and saw that some papers were wrinkled and torn. He was not happy. He went to Lily's room and asked her if she knew anything about the file. Lily looked at her dad and tried to act innocent. But her dad saw her guilty face and knew she was lying. He was not angry, but he was disappointed. He explained to Lily that the file was very important and that she should not touch it without his permission. He told her that he loved her, but that she had to be more careful and respectful. He asked her to apologize and to help him fix the papers. Lily felt sorry. She hugged her dad and said she was sorry. She helped him smooth the papers and tape the tears. She learned her lesson. She still liked to examine things, but she asked before she touched them. She was very persistent, but also very polite.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a big doll and Ben has a small car. They are having fun making noises and moving their toys around. Suddenly, Lily notices something on the floor. It is a rubber band. She picks it up and stretches it with her fingers. She thinks it is fun and cool. "Look, Ben, look!" she says to her brother. "I found a rubber thing. It can be big or small. See?" Ben looks at the rubber band and smiles. He wants to try it too. He reaches out his hand and asks, "Can I have it, Lily? Please?" Lily thinks for a moment. She likes the rubber band, but she also likes Ben. She remembers that sharing is important and nice. She decides to give the rubber band to Ben. "Okay, Ben, you can have it. But be careful, don't hurt yourself or break it. It is a special rubber thing," she says. Ben takes the rubber band and says, "Thank you, Lily. You are a good sister. I will be careful and gentle. Do you want to play with my car?" Lily nods and smiles. She takes the car and makes it zoom around. Ben makes the rubber band twist and bounce. They are happy and play together. They both have fun with their toys and the rubber thing.
