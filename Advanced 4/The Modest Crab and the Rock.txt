Title: The Modest Crab and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a modest crab who lived underneath a rock near the beach. One day, the crab heard someone calling his name. "Hello there, little crab!" said the voice. The crab crawled out from underneath his rock. He saw an old man standing close by. "Who are you?" asked the crab. The old man smiled and said, "I'm here to take you on an adventure. Are you ready?" The crab was excited and tried to imagine what his adventure would be - but he couldn't think of anything. The old man saw the confusion in the crab's eyes. He reached into his pocket and took out a seashell. "Go ahead and take this shell," said the man. "It will help you on your journey." The crab grabbed the shell and hugged it tightly. With one final wave to his old friend, the crab set off on his adventure. He had no idea what was in store, but he was excited!

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and her little girl. The girl was named Lizzy and she was 3 years old. Together, they went for a walk in the park. "Look at the clouds, Lizzy!" Her mom said. "They are so fair!" Lizzy said with excitement. The little girl started to chase the clouds, so her mom decided to lead her on a different path. They kept walking until they came to a pond. "What are those things swimming in the pond?" Lizzy asked. "Those are fish. Don't go too close, they might swim away." Her mom warned. But Lizzy was not paying attention. She wanted to get closer to the fish, so she tried to lead them away. The mom saw what was happening and quickly said, "Lizzy, don't lead the fish away. That is not a fair thing to do. We must always be kind and gentle with animals." Lizzy nodded and they walked away from the pond. From then on, she never tried to lead any animals away ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily found a big maze in her backyard. She was very excited to play in the maze. Lily went into the maze and started to walk. She peeked around the corners to see where to go next. The maze was very big, but Lily was not scared. She knew she could find her way out. As Lily walked, she saw that the maze was empty. There were no other kids or animals. But she kept going and soon, she found the end of the maze. Lily was happy she made it through the maze all by herself.

One day, a brave young man named Tom was walking in the forest. He looked for things he could print, so he could hang them in his room. He found a rare spear and quickly printed it. Just then, a little bird flew to him and said "Hello Tom! What have you found there? Tom smiled and said "Look! I just found this rare spear and I'm printing it so I can hang it in my room!" The little bird said "Oh wow! That's so cool! I've never seen one of those before!" Tom felt proud that he found something so rare. He thanked the little bird for being so kind, and then went back home with the spear.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant called Ella. Ella loved to spend time at the park, but there was one very special thing she loved to do even more. Ella loved to pass the enormous clock. Every day, Ella would pass the clock and stop to look at it. She would stand and admire how the hands moved so gracefully. One day, however, Ella didn't pass the clock. "Why aren't you passing the clock?" asked her mum. "I don't want to," said Ella. "But it's one of your favourite things to do Ella," said her mum. Ella didn't want to listen. She was being disobedient and stubborn. After a while, Ella eventually decided to pass the clock. As she was doing so, Ella realised how much joy it brought her, and she felt happy. The moral of the story is that it's important to listen to your parents and do as they say, even if it's not necessarily something you want to do. When you do, you can end up discovering something wonderful, like Ella did.
