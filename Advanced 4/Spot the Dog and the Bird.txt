Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a happy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside and jump in puddles. One sunny day, he saw a big puddle and ran to jump in it. As Spot jumped into the puddle, he saw a little frog. The frog did not scare Spot. Instead, they became friends. The frog's name was Lily. Spot and Lily played in the puddle together, splashing and having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and tried to scare Spot and Lily. But Spot and Lily were not scared. They jumped and splashed water at the bird. The bird was not mean after all. The bird just wanted to play too. So, Spot, Lily, and the bird became best friends and played in the puddle all day long.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was a very brave girl who loved to explore. One day she asked her mum if she could go for a walk and her mum said yes. The little girl was so excited! She put on her shoes and went outside. Soon she found a funny looking rock. She decided to pick it up and take it home with her. When she got home she showed her mum the rock. Her mum was very surprised because the rock looked like gold! She asked the little girl where she found it and the little girl told her. The little girl's mum didn't feel comfortable with her daughter finding gold like that. So she asked her to promise that she wouldn't go back and request anymore gold. The little girl was sad, but she promised. The little girl never went back, but she kept the gold rock with her from then on. It was a reminder of her brave adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful girl named Lily. She loved to eat chocolate. One day, she went to the store to get some chocolate, but she didn't have any money. Lily saw her friend, Tom. Tom had some money. Lily asked, "Tom, can I borrow some money for chocolate?" Tom said, "Yes, you can borrow my money, but you have to give it back." Lily bought the chocolate and shared it with Tom. They were very happy. Later, Lily gave the money back to Tom. They were good friends who helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there lived a little girl named Lizzy. She had a chubby face and went to the market every day with her mom. Lizzy loved to eat many nice things in the market, like ice cream and candy. Every day, her mom would say, “Now, Lizzy, you must keep your promise and eat only one lollipop.” Lizzy sometimes forgot and ate more than one. One day, the market ran out of lollipops, so Lizzy did not get any. She was very sad. On the way home, her mom said, “Lizzy, don’t forget – you must keep your promises. This is why you did not get a lollipop today.” The moral of the story is that if we keep our promises, good things will happen.

One day, a little otter went to the park to play. The otter saw a big, tough rock. He thought it would be fun to climb it. He tried and tried, but the rock was too tough to climb. The otter felt sad. A wise old bird saw the otter and asked, "Why are you sad, little otter?" The otter said, "I want to climb the tough rock, but I can't." The bird said, "Keep trying, and you will make it. Just don't give up." The otter tried again and again. He did not give up. After a while, the otter finally climbed the tough rock. He was very happy. The wise old bird smiled and said, "See? You did it because you did not give up." The little otter learned that if you keep trying, you can do tough things. He was proud of himself and thanked the wise old bird. From that day on, the otter always remembered to never give up when things are tough.
