Title: Molly and Toby and the Tree

Once upon a time there were two friends called Molly and Toby. Molly was a small mole who lived in the woods and Toby was a bird. Every day Toby would travel around the world looking for food. One day, Toby saw a big, empty tree trunk. He told Molly come explore it with him. Molly wasn't sure at first, but decided to follow Toby in the end. They completed the exploration but when they reached the top, the trunk of the tree trapped them inside. They tried to find a way out but all the exits were blocked. Molly and Toby were stuck for hours until eventually they ran out of hope. The moral of this story is to never go exploring something new until you are sure it is safe.

Once upon a time, there was a man named Tom. Tom liked to sell junk. He had a big table with all his junk on it. People would come and look at the junk and sometimes buy it. One day, a little girl named Ann came up to the table. She saw a toy she liked. "Mister, can I buy this toy, please?" she asked. Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. It will make you happy." Tom was tired, but he liked to help people find good things in his junk. He felt happy when people found something they liked. Ann went home with her toy, and Tom went to sleep, knowing he had made someone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular house. This house had a big chimney. Every day, many people came to see the house and its chimney. One day, a little bird wanted to peek inside the chimney. The bird flew up and looked inside. It saw lots of dark smoke. The bird did not like the smoke. It made the bird feel bad. The bird flew away to tell its friends. The bird told its friends about the smoke in the chimney. The friends did not want the smoke to hurt them too. So, they all decided to stay away from the chimney. They told other birds to stay away too. Soon, all the birds in the town knew to stay away from the chimney. The moral of the story is to listen to our friends when they tell us about things that might hurt us. It is important to stay away from things that are not good for us.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite cup. It was a big, red cup. Sue took her cup everywhere she went. One day, Sue's mom said, "We need to change your cup. It is old and not good." Sue was sad, but she knew her mom was right. Sue and her mom went to the store to find a new cup. They saw many cups, but Sue did not like any of them. Sue's mom said, "Do not worry, we will find the right cup." The next day, Sue and her mom went to a new store. They saw a big, blue cup. Sue loved the blue cup. She was grateful for her mom's help. Sue's mom said, "I knew we would find the right cup for you." From that day on, Sue took her big, blue cup everywhere she went. She was happy with her new cup and grateful for her mom's help. And Sue's mom was happy that Sue had a good cup. They both knew that sometimes, change can be good.

Once upon a time there was a lady named Daisy. Daisy loved to write stories and she wrote a lot of them. One day, Daisy heard a loud noise outside. She opened her door and saw an ambulance coming to her house. She said to the driver, "What is happening?" The driver replied, "A neighbor needs help. There is an emergency." Daisy went with the ambulance and when they arrived at the neighbor's house, it was very dry. Daisy wrote about this experience. She wrote about how the ambulance made her feel and about how dry the neighbor's house was. The ambulance driver was very kind and Daisy thanked him for coming to her house. Daisy continued to write about different experiences throughout her life and learned how important it was to have a kind heart.
