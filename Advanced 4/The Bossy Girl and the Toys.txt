Title: The Bossy Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a bossy little girl. Her name was Jane, and she was always bossing around her friend, John. She liked to boss him around with her plastic toys. One day, Jane and John decided to build a sandcastle together. Jane was so bossy that she almost wouldn't let John use the plastic bucket. But eventually, she let him. The sandcastle was big and beautiful when they finished it. But then a wave came and the whole sandcastle was washed away. Jane felt so sad and she started to cry. She started to regret being so bossy to John with the plastic toys. John was kind to Jane and gave her a big hug. He told her that it was alright and they could always build a new sandcastle. Jane smiled and she never regretted bossing John around again.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She liked to play with her toys and have fun with her friends. One day Lily was playing in the park with her best friend Samuel. Samuel was an adventurous little boy who loved to explore. On this day, Samuel said to Lily "Let's build a castle!" and Lily shouted "Yes!" They began to collect sticks and twigs to make the castle. When they were done, Lily said "Look Samuel! I drew a picture on our castle". Samuel was so excited. He clapped his hands and said "Let's hang it up on the tree over there". Lily agreed and they carefully hung their picture on a furry tree. They both clapped with happiness and they danced in circles around the tree. Since then, every time they saw the hairy tree with the picture on it, they always thought of the special day they spent together building their castle.

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat pears. One day, he saw a big pear tree with many pears on it. He was so happy. Tim wanted to test the pears to see if they were good. He called his friend, Sam. "Sam, come help me test these pears," he said. Sam came running to help Tim. They both picked a pear from the tree and took a big bite. But, oh no! The pears were not good. They were very bad. Tim and Sam felt sick. They both said, "We should not have eaten these pears." They went home, sad that they could not enjoy the pears from the tree.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was eager to play with her friend, Tom. They liked to play hide and seek in the park. They would take turns hiding and finding each other. Today, Lucy had a big surprise to reveal to Tom. Lucy hid behind a big tree and waited for Tom to find her. She heard Tom's footsteps coming closer. She held her surprise behind her back. Tom looked around, trying to find where Lucy was hiding. He knew she had a surprise for him. Tom finally found Lucy behind the tree. Lucy was so happy to reveal her surprise to her friend. She showed him a big, red ball. Tom's eyes lit up with joy. He had always wanted a ball to play with in the park. From that day on, Lucy and Tom played with the big, red ball every time they met at the park. They had so much fun together, and their friendship grew stronger. The surprise had made their playtime even more special.

Once upon a time there was a cherry tree. It was the original cherry tree in the whole forest. Every day, a little bird would come to rest in the branches. One day, the bird was asking the cherry tree why it was the original. The cherry tree said, “I just want everyone who sees me to know that this is the original cherry tree.” The little bird smiled. “That’s a very nice thing to do,” he said. The bird settled on one of the branches, and then said to the tree, “I will take a rest here.” The cherry tree thought for a moment before saying, “I will always be here. You can come back anytime to rest.” The little bird was so happy. He thanked the cherry tree, and then flew away. From then on, the little bird would always come back to the original cherry tree to rest.
