Title: Lily and the Rock

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She liked to seat on a big rock near the wheat field. Every day, she would watch the wheat grow tall and strong. One sunny day, Lily saw a small bird in the wheat field. The bird was sad because it could not find its family. Lily wanted to help the little bird. She had an idea to seat on the big rock and look for the bird's family. Lily and the bird sat on the rock together. They looked around the wheat field. Soon, they saw the bird's family. The little bird was so happy to see them. They all flew away to be together. Lily felt happy too, because she helped her new friend. From that day on, Lily loved the wheat field even more.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. One day, she found a big book with a pretty cover. She was very excited to read it. Lily started to read the book, but her little brother, Tom, was being silly. He made funny faces and jumped around. Lily laughed, but she wanted to read her book. She asked Tom to stop being silly so she could read. Tom did not listen. He took a cup of water and threw it at Lily. The water went all over the book and spoiled it. Lily was sad because her book was spoiled. Tom felt bad for being silly and said sorry to Lily. They hugged, and Tom promised not to spoil any more books.

Once upon a time, there was a rich king. He had a big, pretty castle. Inside the castle, there was a deep well. People came to get water from the well. One day, a little boy came to the well. He tried to understand how to get water. He looked down and saw a bucket. The boy put the bucket in the well and pulled it up. The bucket was full of water! The rich king saw the boy and smiled. The boy was happy that he could understand how to use the well. The king and the boy became good friends. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a small boy called Jimmy. Every night Jimmy liked to take a nap in his cozy bed; but one night, when he settled in to sleep, he felt a horrible pain in his leg. He cried out in pain and his mom came running to his bed. "Mommy, my leg hurts," Jimmy said. "Oh no! What did you do?" asked his mom. Jimmy showed her his leg and his mom looked really worried. "You have a rare condition called 'suffer'. I am so sorry, Jimmy," she said. Jimmy's mom took him to the doctor the next morning and the doctor said that Jimmy must take special medicine for his rare condition. From then on, Jimmy was careful to take the medicine every morning and since then, he sleeps comfortably in his bed every night.

Once upon a time, there was a curious cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and look at new things. One day, Kitty saw a big box in the kitchen. It was a fridge. Kitty wanted to see what was inside the fridge. She jumped up and pulled the door open. Wow! There were so many things inside. Kitty played with the food and knocked it on the floor. She did not know that she was starting to destroy the food. Then, something unexpected happened. The fridge began to shake and make a loud noise. Kitty was scared and ran away. But it was not the fridge that was making the noise. It was a toy robot that her friend had left on top of the fridge. The robot had fallen down and was now dancing on the floor. Kitty laughed and was not scared anymore. She played with the robot and forgot about the fridge. The fridge door closed by itself, and the food was safe. Kitty learned that it was not good to destroy things, but it was fun to play with her robot friend.
