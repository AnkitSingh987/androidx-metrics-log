Title: A Helpful Class Monitor
Category: Beginner

Every month, the teacher in Room 3 chooses a new class monitor. The class monitor is a student who helps the teacher with special jobs. It is a very important role. The students love to be the class monitor. 
Today is the first day of October. It is time for a new monitor. 
Miss Taylor stands at the front of the classroom. She looks at the children. 

"Good morning, class," Miss Taylor says. "As you know, September is over. We need a new class monitor for October."
All the children sit up straight. They put their hands on their desks. They want Miss Taylor to choose them. 
"This month," Miss Taylor continues, "I want someone who is very responsible. Someone who comes to school early and always does their homework."
She looks around the room. Her eyes stop on a young boy near the window. 
"David," she says. "Would you like to be our class monitor for October?"

David is thrilled. His face breaks into a huge smile. 
"Yes, please, Miss Taylor!" he says loudly. 
"Excellent," the teacher says. She takes a shiny silver badge from her desk. She walks to David and pins the badge on his shirt. The badge says 'CLASS MONITOR' in black letters. 
David looks down at the badge. He feels very proud and very important. 

"Now, David," Miss Taylor says, "you have some important jobs to do every day. First, you must hand out the books before the lesson begins. Second, you must collect the homework papers. And third, you must clean the blackboard at the end of the day."
"I understand, Miss Taylor," David promises. "I will do a good job."

The first lesson is English. Miss Taylor points to a tall stack of blue books on her desk. 
"David, please hand out the English books," she says. 
David stands up quickly. He walks to the front desk. The stack of books is heavy, so he takes half of them. He walks down the first aisle. He puts one blue book on every desk carefully. He does not drop any books. Then, he goes back for the rest of the books. 
"Thank you, David," Miss Taylor says when he finishes. 
"You are welcome," David says. He sits back in his chair. He feels very helpful. 

Later in the morning, it is time for math. 
"Class, please pass your math homework to the front of your row," Miss Taylor says. 
The students pass their papers forward. 
"Monitor, please collect the papers," she says. 
David stands up again. He walks to the front of each row. He gathers the papers neatly. He taps them on the desk so all the edges are straight. He hands the neat pile to Miss Taylor. 
"Perfect, thank you," she smiles. 

During the lunch break, David is very careful. He does not want to get soup on his silver badge. He wants it to stay shiny all month. His friends look at his badge. 
"You are lucky, David," his friend Sam says. "I want to be the monitor."
"Maybe you will be the monitor in November," David says kindly. "It is hard work, but it is fun."

At the end of the school day, all the children pack their bags. They say goodbye to Miss Taylor and run outside to play. 
But David does not leave. A class monitor's job is not finished until the room is ready for tomorrow. 
He walks to the front of the empty classroom. The big blackboard is covered in white chalk writing. It has math problems and English sentences from the day's lessons. 

David takes the large felt eraser. He starts at the top left corner of the board. He rubs the eraser firmly against the slate. The white chalk disappears in a cloud of dust. He sweeps the eraser back and forth, up and down. He works hard until the board is completely black and clean again. 
Then, he claps the erasers together outside the window to remove the chalk dust. 
Finally, he straightens the chairs in the front row. 

Miss Taylor looks up from her computer. 
"The board looks fantastic, David," she says. "You are doing a wonderful job as monitor. Thank you for your help today."
David smiles happily. He touches his shiny badge. 
"You are welcome, Miss Taylor. See you tomorrow morning," he says. 

David walks out of the school. He is tired, but it is a good kind of tired. He likes having responsibility. He likes helping his teacher and his classmates. He knows he is going to be the best class monitor Room 3 has ever had. He cannot wait for tomorrow to do it all again.
