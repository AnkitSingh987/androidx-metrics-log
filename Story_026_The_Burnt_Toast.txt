Title: The Burnt Toast
Category: Beginner

It is Tuesday morning. The kitchen is busy. 
David is preparing his breakfast. He is a young man. He lives alone in a small apartment. He is usually very careful, but today he is in a hurry. He woke up late. He must go to work soon. 

"I will make some toast," David says to himself. "Toast is fast. Toast is easy."
He takes a bag of bread from the cupboard. He opens the bag. He takes out two slices of white bread. The bread is soft and fresh. 
He walks to the toaster. The toaster is on the kitchen counter. It is a silver machine. 
David puts the two slices of bread into the toaster. He pushes the lever down. The bread goes down inside the machine. 

Inside the toaster, thin wires become hot. The wires become orange and red. They start to cook the bread. They will make the bread brown and crunchy. 
Usually, David waits in the kitchen. He watches the toaster. But today, he is in a hurry. 
"I need to iron my shirt," he thinks. "The toast will take two minutes. I can iron my shirt quickly."

David leaves the kitchen. He goes to his bedroom. He turns on the iron. He puts his blue shirt on the ironing board. He starts to iron the shirt. He wants to look smart for work. 
He thinks about his meeting at work. He thinks about the bus schedule. He forgets about the kitchen. He forgets about the toaster. He forgets about his breakfast. 

In the kitchen, the toaster is working. The wires are very hot. The bread is getting brown. It is getting very brown. 
Suddenly, something is wrong. The toaster does not pop up. The lever stays down. 
The bread is not brown anymore. It is becoming black. 

A smell starts to fill the kitchen. It is not a good smell. It is a strong, sharp smell. It is the smell of burning bread. 
Then, a thin line of grey smoke comes out of the top of the toaster. The smoke goes up to the ceiling. 
The smell moves out of the kitchen. It moves into the hall. It moves into the bedroom. 

David is ironing the collar of his blue shirt. He stops. He sniffs the air. 
"What is that smell?" he asks out loud. 
He sniffs again. It is a terrible smell. 
"Oh no!" David shouts. "My toast!"

He drops the iron. He runs out of the bedroom. He runs down the hall. He runs into the kitchen. 
The kitchen is full of grey smoke. It looks like a cloud in the room. The smell is very strong now. It makes David cough. 
He sees the silver toaster. Smoke is coming out of it very fast. 

David acts quickly. He grabs the plug. He pulls the plug out of the wall. The electricity stops. The orange wires inside the toaster turn black and cold. 
But the bread is still inside, and it is still burning. 
David tries to push the lever up. The lever is stuck. He uses a wooden spoon. He pushes the lever up with the wooden spoon. 

Pop! 
The toast comes out of the machine. 
But it is not toast anymore. It is completely black. It looks like black coal. It is hard and dry. Smoke is still coming from the black pieces. 

David opens the kitchen window wide. He wants the fresh air to come in. He wants the grey smoke to go out. 
He takes a towel. He waves the towel in the air to push the smoke towards the window. 
He looks at the black toast. He cannot eat that. It is ruined. 
He uses the wooden spoon to push the black toast into the rubbish bin. 

"Well," David says. He sighs. "That was a mistake. I must never leave the kitchen when the toaster is on."
He looks at his watch. He is very late now. He does not have time to make new toast. He does not have time to make eggs. 
He goes to the fruit bowl. He takes a green apple. He washes the apple quickly. 

"An apple for breakfast," he says. "It is not toast, but it is safe. It will not burn."
He takes a bite of the sweet, crunchy apple. 
He walks back to his bedroom. He must finish ironing his blue shirt. He must hurry to catch his bus. 
The kitchen is still a little smoky, but it is safe now. David learned a lesson today. When you cook, you must pay attention. If you do not pay attention, you will have burnt toast and a hungry stomach.
