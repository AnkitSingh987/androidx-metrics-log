Title: My First Day at School
Category: Beginner

It is a very special morning for Leo. Today is Monday, the second of September. It is the first day of school. Leo is six years old. He is starting first grade. 

Leo wakes up very early. He does not need an alarm clock today. He is a little nervous, but he is also very excited. His stomach feels funny. 
He looks at his chair. His new school uniform is on the chair. It is a blue sweater, a white shirt, and grey trousers. His new black shoes are on the floor. 

His mother comes into the bedroom. She smiles. 
"Good morning, Leo," she says. "Are you ready for your big day?"
"Yes, Mother," Leo says. "But I feel a little scared."
"That is normal," she says. "Every child feels a little scared on the first day. But you will make new friends. You will learn many new things. It will be fun."
"I hope so," Leo says. 

Leo gets dressed. He puts on his white shirt. He puts on his grey trousers. He puts on his blue sweater. He sits down and puts on his black shoes. His mother helps him tie the laces. 
He goes to the kitchen. His father is making pancakes for breakfast. Pancakes are Leo's favorite food. 
"A special breakfast for a special day," his father says. 
Leo eats two big pancakes with syrup. He drinks a glass of milk. He feels stronger now. 

After breakfast, Leo goes to his room. He takes his new school bag. It is a red bag with a picture of a rocket on it. He checks inside. His new pencils are there. His new notebook is there. His lunch box is there. 
"I am ready," he tells his parents. 

Leo and his mother walk to the school. The school is not very far. It takes ten minutes to walk. 
The street is full of children. Many children are walking to school. Some children are running. They are happy to see their friends after the summer holiday. 

They arrive at the school gates. The school building is big and made of red bricks. There is a large playground. Many children are in the playground. It is very noisy. 
Leo holds his mother's hand tightly. 
"Look, Leo," his mother says. "There is your teacher. Her name is Miss Sarah."

They walk to a tall woman with kind eyes and short brown hair. She is smiling at the children. 
"Hello," Miss Sarah says. "What is your name?"
"My name is Leo," he says quietly. 
"Welcome to first grade, Leo," Miss Sarah says. "We are going to have a great year."
"Thank you," Leo says. 

A bell rings loudly. Ring, ring, ring! 
It is time to go inside. 
"Okay, Leo," his mother says. She kneels down and hugs him. "Have a wonderful day. I will meet you here at three o'clock."
"Goodbye, Mother," Leo says. 

Leo walks with Miss Sarah and the other children. They walk into the big building. They walk down a long hall. They stop at a door with a yellow star on it. 
"This is our classroom," Miss Sarah says. She opens the door. 
The classroom is very bright and colorful. There are pictures of animals on the walls. There is an alphabet poster. A, B, C, D... 
There are many small tables. Four chairs are around each table. 

"You can choose a seat," Miss Sarah says to the children. 
Leo walks to a table near the window. He sits on a blue chair. He puts his red school bag on the floor next to him. 
A little boy walks to the same table. He sits on the green chair next to Leo. 
"Hello," the boy says. "I am Tom. I like your red bag. It has a rocket."
"Hello, Tom," Leo says. He smiles. "I am Leo. I like rockets."
"Me too," Tom says. "Do you want to play at recess?"
"Yes, please," Leo says. 

Miss Sarah stands at the front of the classroom. She claps her hands twice. Clap, clap. 
The children stop talking. They look at her. 
"Good morning, class," she says clearly. 
"Good morning, Miss Sarah," the children say together. 

"Today is our first day," Miss Sarah says. "First, we will learn everyone's name. Then, we will draw a picture. Then, we will have recess and play outside."
Leo is very happy. He likes to draw. He already has a friend named Tom. He likes his kind teacher. 
The funny feeling in his stomach is gone. He is not scared anymore. 
His first day at school is starting, and it is going to be a very good day.
