Title: A Busy Morning
Category: Beginner

It is seven o'clock in the morning. The house is not quiet anymore. The house is busy. It is Monday. Everyone must go to work and school. Monday mornings are always busy.

Mr. Smith is in the kitchen. He is the father. He is making breakfast. He is cooking eggs. He is making toast. The kitchen smells like eggs and bread. 
Mrs. Smith is in the bathroom. She is the mother. She is washing her face. She is brushing her hair. She is getting ready for work. She works in an office. She needs to look nice.

Their children are in their bedrooms. The boy is Leo. He is ten years old. The girl is Mia. She is eight years old. They are getting ready for school.
Leo is looking for his socks. He cannot find them. 
"Where are my socks?" he asks. He looks under his bed. They are not there. He looks on his chair. They are not there. He looks in his drawer. Ah! There they are. He finds his white socks. He puts them on. 

Mia is in her room. She is looking at her clothes. She does not know what to wear. 
"Mother!" she calls out. "What should I wear today?"
Mrs. Smith hears her. She comes to Mia's room. 
"It is a warm day, Mia," her mother says. "Wear your blue dress."
"Okay, Mother," Mia says. She takes the blue dress. She puts it on. It is a nice dress. She likes it.

In the kitchen, the eggs are ready. The toast is ready. Mr. Smith puts the food on the table. He pours milk into four glasses. He puts the glasses on the table.
"Breakfast is ready!" Mr. Smith calls out. His voice is loud. "Come to the kitchen! We do not want to be late!"

Mrs. Smith comes to the kitchen. She is wearing a white shirt and a black skirt. She looks nice. 
"Good morning," she says to her husband. 
"Good morning," he says. "Here are your eggs."
"Thank you," she says. She sits at the table. She starts to eat. 

Leo runs into the kitchen. He is wearing his school uniform. His shirt is white. His trousers are grey. He is carrying his school bag. He puts his bag on the floor.
"Good morning, Father. Good morning, Mother," Leo says.
"Good morning, Leo," they say. 
Leo sits down. He is very hungry. He eats his eggs quickly. He eats his toast quickly. 
"Eat slowly, Leo," his mother says. "Do not eat so fast."
"But I am hungry," Leo says. "And I do not want to be late for school."
"You have time," his father says. "It is only seven-fifteen."

Mia walks into the kitchen. She is wearing her blue dress. She has a red ribbon in her hair. She looks very pretty. 
"Good morning, everyone," Mia says. 
"Good morning, Mia," they say. 
Mia sits next to Leo. She looks at her eggs. She looks at her toast. She is not very hungry in the morning.
"Eat your breakfast, Mia," her father says. "You need energy for school."
"Okay, Father," Mia says. She eats a little bit of egg. She drinks some milk. 

The clock on the wall shows seven-thirty. It is time to go soon. 
"Did you pack your bags?" Mrs. Smith asks the children.
"Yes, Mother," Leo says. "My books are in my bag. My pens are in my bag."
"Yes, Mother," Mia says. "My books are in my bag too."
"Do you have your lunch boxes?" Mr. Smith asks.
Leo and Mia look at each other. They forgot their lunch boxes. 
"No," they say. 
Mr. Smith laughs. "Here they are," he says. He gives a red lunch box to Leo. He gives a yellow lunch box to Mia. 
"Thank you, Father," they say. They put the lunch boxes in their bags. 

It is seven-forty-five. It is time to leave the house. 
"Let's go," Mr. Smith says. "I will drive you to school."
They all walk to the front door. Leo opens the door. They go outside. The sun is shining. It is a beautiful day.
Mr. Smith's car is in the driveway. It is a silver car. They all get into the car. Mr. Smith sits in the front. Mrs. Smith sits next to him. Leo and Mia sit in the back. 

Mr. Smith starts the car. They drive down the street. There are many cars on the road. Many people are going to work. Many children are going to school. It is a busy morning in the city.
They arrive at the school. It is a big building. There are many children outside. They are talking and playing. 
"Here we are," Mr. Smith says. He stops the car. 
Leo and Mia open the doors. They get out of the car. 
"Have a good day at school," Mrs. Smith says. 
"Listen to your teachers," Mr. Smith says.
"We will," Leo and Mia say. "Goodbye, Mother. Goodbye, Father."
"Goodbye," their parents say.

Leo and Mia walk to the school gate. They meet their friends. They are happy to see them. 
Mr. Smith drives away. He drives Mrs. Smith to her office. Then he goes to his office. 
The house is empty now. It is quiet again. The busy morning is over. The day has started for everyone.
