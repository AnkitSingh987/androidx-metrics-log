Title: A Quiet Place
Category: Beginner

It is five o'clock in the afternoon. Emma is twelve years old. She has a lot of homework today. She has math homework, history homework, and an English essay to write. 

Emma sits at the kitchen table in her house. She opens her math book. She takes her pencil. She tries to read the first question. 
But the house is very noisy. 
In the living room, her younger brother, Leo, is watching television. The television is very loud. It is a cartoon about shouting animals. 'Bang! Crash! Boom!' says the television. 
In the kitchen, her mother is cooking dinner. The pots are making a loud noise. Clang, clang! Her mother is talking on the telephone loudly. "Yes! I will buy the potatoes tomorrow!" she says. 

Emma puts her hands over her ears. She cannot think. She cannot focus on her math homework. 
"Mother," Emma says. "It is too noisy here. I cannot study."
Her mother puts down the telephone for a second. "I am sorry, Emma. I am cooking. And Leo loves his cartoon. Can you go to your bedroom?"
Emma shakes her head. "Leo's cartoon is loud in my bedroom too. The walls are thin."

Emma thinks. She needs a quiet place. Where can she go? 
Then, she has an idea. The public library is only ten minutes away by walking. The library is always quiet. 

"Mother, I am going to the library to study," Emma says. 
"That is a good idea," her mother says. "Be home by seven o'clock for dinner."
"I will," Emma says. 

Emma puts her math book, her history book, and her notebooks into her backpack. She puts her pencil case inside too. She puts on her jacket and leaves the noisy house. 
She walks down the street. The street is a little noisy with cars, but it is better than the cartoon. 

She arrives at the library. She opens the heavy glass doors and walks inside. 
Immediately, the noise stops. The library is a different world. 
It is cool and peaceful. There is no television. There are no clanging pots. There is no loud talking. 

Emma takes a deep breath. She likes the smell of the library. It smells like old paper and polish. 
She walks quietly. Her shoes do not make a loud sound on the carpet. She goes to the study area at the back of the library. 

There are many wooden desks in the study area. Each desk has a small lamp. Some people are already sitting at the desks. An old man is reading a newspaper. A university student is typing on a laptop computer. Everyone is very quiet. 

Emma finds an empty desk near a window. She sits down in the comfortable chair. She takes her books out of her backpack. She puts her pencil case on the desk. She turns on the small lamp. The light shines brightly on her desk. 

Now, she can focus. 
She opens her math book again. She reads the first question. It is not difficult now. She understands it quickly. She writes the answer in her notebook. She does all her math homework in twenty minutes. 

Next, she opens her history book. She reads a chapter about old kings and queens. The library is so quiet, she can hear the pages turning. 'Swish, swish.' She writes some notes in her history notebook. 

Finally, she takes a clean piece of paper for her English essay. She has to write a story about a forest. 
She looks out the window. She thinks. In the quiet library, her mind is clear. The ideas come easily. She starts to write. Her pencil moves fast across the paper. She writes a very good story. 

She finishes all her homework. She looks at the big clock on the library wall. It is half past six. She was in the library for one hour and thirty minutes. She did a lot of work in a short time. 

She packs her books back into her backpack. She turns off the small lamp. She pushes her chair under the desk quietly. 
She walks out of the library. The street seems a little louder now. 
She walks home. She feels very good. Her homework is done. She does not have to worry about it anymore. 

She opens the door to her house. The television is still loud. Her mother is still cooking. The house is still noisy. 
But Emma does not mind now. Her work is finished. She can relax. She knows that when she needs to study again, the quiet place is waiting for her down the street.
