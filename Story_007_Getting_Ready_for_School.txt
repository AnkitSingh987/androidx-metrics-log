Title: Getting Ready for School
Category: Beginner

It is Wednesday morning. It is seven o'clock. Peter is in his bedroom. Peter is a student. He is nine years old. Today is a school day. Peter must get ready for school. 

Peter is already awake. He is sitting on his bed. He is looking at his school clothes. His school uniform is on a chair. It is clean. His mother washed it yesterday. 
The uniform has a white shirt. It has grey trousers. It has a red tie. Peter likes his uniform. He thinks it looks smart. 

First, Peter takes off his pajamas. He folds them. He puts them on his bed. He is a tidy boy. 
Next, he puts on his grey trousers. He pulls them up. He buttons them. He puts on a black belt. 
Then, he takes the white shirt. He puts his arms in the sleeves. He buttons the shirt from the bottom to the top. One, two, three, four, five buttons. 
Now he needs his tie. The tie is red. He puts it around his neck. He does not know how to tie it well. His father always helps him. 
"Father!" Peter calls. "Can you help me with my tie?"

His father comes into the room. He smiles. 
"Yes, Peter. I can help you," his father says. 
His father stands in front of Peter. He takes the red tie. He folds it. He pulls it. He makes a nice knot. 
"There you go," his father says. "You look very smart."
"Thank you, Father," Peter says. 

Now Peter goes to the bathroom. He looks in the mirror. His hair is messy. It points in all directions. He needs to comb his hair. 
He takes his blue comb. He runs the water. He puts a little water on the comb. He combs his hair carefully. He combs it to the left side. Now his hair is flat and neat. He washes his face with cold water. He dries his face with a towel. 

Peter goes back to his bedroom. He looks at his feet. He is only wearing socks. He needs his shoes. His school shoes are black. They are shiny. 
He sits on a small stool. He puts on his left shoe. He ties the laces. He puts on his right shoe. He ties the laces. Now he is fully dressed. 

Next, Peter must pack his school bag. His bag is green and black. It is on his desk. He opens the bag. It is empty. 
He looks at his school timetable on the wall. The timetable tells him what classes he has today. 
Today is Wednesday. First class is English. Second class is Mathematics. Third class is Science. 
He takes his English book. It is a blue book. He puts it in the bag. 
He takes his Mathematics book. It is a red book. He puts it in the bag. 
He takes his Science book. It is a green book. He puts it in the bag. 

Then he looks for his pencil case. It is a yellow pencil case. He opens it. He checks inside. He has two pencils. He has one blue pen. He has one eraser. He has one ruler. Everything is there. He closes the pencil case. He puts it in the bag. 
He also puts a notebook in the bag. He closes the bag. It is heavy now. 

"Peter!" His mother calls from the kitchen. "Breakfast is ready!"
"I am coming, Mother!" Peter answers. 
He takes his heavy green bag. He walks to the kitchen. 
His mother is at the table. She has a bowl of cereal for him. The cereal has milk and bananas. There is also a glass of apple juice. 

"Good morning, Peter," his mother says. 
"Good morning, Mother," Peter says. 
He sits down. He eats his cereal with a spoon. It is sweet and cold. He drinks his apple juice. It is refreshing. 
"Do you have everything for school?" his mother asks. 
"Yes," Peter says. "I have my books. I have my pencils. My bag is packed."

His mother gives him a small box. It is his lunch box. 
"Here is your lunch," she says. "There is a sandwich, an apple, and a small cake."
"Thank you, Mother," Peter says. He opens his school bag. He puts the lunch box inside. 

He looks at the clock on the kitchen wall. It is seven-forty. It is time to leave. 
He stands up. He puts his bag on his back. 
"I am ready," Peter says. 
"Okay. Have a good day at school," his mother says. She kisses his cheek. 
"I will," Peter says. 
He walks to the front door. He opens the door. He steps outside. The morning air is cool. He walks to the bus stop. He is completely ready for a new school day.
