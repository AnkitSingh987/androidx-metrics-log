Title: Returning to School
Category: Beginner

It is Friday morning. The alarm clock rings at seven o'clock. Sam does not turn it off and go back to sleep. He jumps out of bed immediately. Today is a great day. Today, he is going back to school. 

He missed school for three days. He missed his friends, he missed his teachers, and he even missed the noisy playground. 
He puts on his school uniform. The blue sweater feels a little tight. Maybe he grew a little bit while he was resting in bed. He brushes his teeth, washes his face, and runs downstairs for breakfast. 

After breakfast, he takes his school bag. It is heavy, but he is happy to carry it. 
He walks to school. The air is cool and fresh. He breathes it in deeply. His lungs are clear, and his throat is not sore. He feels fantastic. 

He arrives at the school gates. He sees many children walking inside. He looks for his best friend, Mark. 
He sees Mark standing near the door of their classroom, Room 6. 
"Mark!" Sam shouts. 
Mark turns around. A big smile appears on his face. 
"Sam!" Mark shouts back. "You are back!"
Sam runs over to him. "Yes, I am back. I am completely better."

"That is great," Mark says. "We missed you. The football game was not good without you. Our team lost yesterday."
"I will play today," Sam promises. "We will win today."
Another boy, Tom, walks up to them. "Hey, Sam. Welcome back. Were you very sick?"
"I had a bad throat infection and a fever," Sam explains. "I had to stay in bed and take pink medicine. It was very boring."
"Well, it is good to see you," Tom says. 

The bell rings. It is time to go inside. 
Sam walks into Room 6 and sits at his desk. It feels good to sit in his own chair again. 
Mrs. Wilson, the teacher, walks in. She sees Sam and smiles warmly. 
"Good morning, Sam. Welcome back," she says. "We are very happy to see you. Are you feeling better?"
"Yes, Mrs. Wilson. I feel 100 percent better," Sam answers. 

"I am glad to hear that," she says. "Now, you missed three days of work. You have some catching up to do. But do not worry, you do not have to do it all today. I will give you the important worksheets, and you can finish them over the weekend."
"Okay, Mrs. Wilson," Sam says. 

The first lesson is math. Mrs. Wilson writes new problems on the board. Sam listens carefully. He missed the lesson on Wednesday, so this is a little difficult for him. He looks at Mark's notebook to see how to do the problem. Mark points to the numbers and explains it in a whisper. 
"Oh, I understand now," Sam whispers back. He writes the answer in his own book. 
It is good to have a friend to help you catch up. 

At recess, Sam runs out to the playground. The sun is shining. The boys start a football game. Sam runs fast. He kicks the ball hard. He does not feel tired at all. His body is strong again. His team scores a goal, and they all cheer loudly. 

After recess, they have an English class. They read a new story together. Sam raises his hand and reads a paragraph out loud. His voice is clear and strong. 
The day passes quickly. Sam enjoys every minute of it. He enjoys the lessons, he enjoys talking with his friends, and he even enjoys the noisy cafeteria at lunchtime. 

When the final bell rings at three o'clock, Sam packs his bag. Mrs. Wilson gives him a green folder with three worksheets inside. 
"This is your missed homework, Sam," she says. "Take your time this weekend."
"I will do them tomorrow," Sam says. 

He walks home with Mark. 
"Are you glad to be back?" Mark asks. 
"Yes," Sam says with a big smile. "Being sick is terrible. Staying in bed is boring. I missed everything. School is much better."
"I agree," Mark says. 
Sam walks into his house. He drops his bag on the floor. He is a little tired, but it is a good, normal kind of tired. He is healthy, he is back to his routine, and he is very happy.
