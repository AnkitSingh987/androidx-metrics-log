Title: A Surprise Test
Category: Beginner

It is a normal Thursday morning in Room 7. The students are happy. It is almost the weekend. 
Mrs. Wilson walks into the classroom. She is carrying a large pile of white papers. She puts the papers on her big desk. She looks at the students. She does not smile. 

"Good morning, class," Mrs. Wilson says. Her voice is serious. 
"Good morning, Mrs. Wilson," the students say. 
"Please put all your books inside your desks," she says. "Please clear your tables. You only need one pencil and one eraser."

The students look at each other. They are confused. Why must they put their books away? 
"What are we doing, Mrs. Wilson?" asks a boy named Harry. 
"We are having a test," Mrs. Wilson announces. 

The classroom becomes very noisy. Many students groan. 'Oh no!' they say. 
"A test?" a girl named Chloe asks. "But you did not tell us! We did not study!"
"I know," Mrs. Wilson says. "It is a surprise test. Sometimes in life, you get surprises. I want to see what you remember from last week."

Harry feels a little sick in his stomach. He hates tests. He hates surprise tests even more. He feels his hands getting sweaty. 
Chloe bites her lip. She is worried. She wants to get good marks. 

"Silence, please," Mrs. Wilson says. She takes the pile of white papers. She walks around the room. She puts one paper on every desk. 
The paper is facing down. The students cannot read the questions yet. 
"Do not turn the paper over until I say so," she commands. 

Harry looks at the blank back of the paper on his desk. He holds his yellow pencil tightly. He breathes in and out. 
"Okay," Mrs. Wilson says. She looks at the clock on the wall. "You have thirty minutes. Turn your papers over. You may begin."

Harry turns his paper over. He looks at the top of the page. It says 'Science Test'. 
He reads the first question. 
'Question 1: What do plants need to grow?'
Harry relaxes a little. He knows this answer. He learned this on Monday. 
He writes his answer neatly. 'Plants need water, sunlight, and soil to grow.'

He reads the second question. 
'Question 2: How many legs does an insect have?'
Harry smiles. He likes insects. He likes spiders and ants. He knows this answer too. 
He writes, 'An insect has six legs.'

He moves to the next question. And the next. 
The classroom is very quiet. The only sound is the scratching of pencils on paper. Scratch, scratch, scratch. 
Mrs. Wilson sits at her desk. She watches the students. She drinks a cup of coffee. 

Chloe is working fast. She is a very good student. She answers all the questions quickly. 
Harry works slowly. He reads every question two times. He wants to be careful. He does not want to make a mistake. 

Question 5 is difficult. 
'Question 5: What is the biggest planet in our solar system?'
Harry stops. He thinks. Is it Earth? No, Earth is small. Is it Mars? No. 
He closes his eyes. He tries to remember the picture in his science book. The big planet with stripes. 
"Jupiter," he whispers to himself. "It is Jupiter."
He writes the word carefully. 'J - u - p - i - t - e - r'. 

The thirty minutes pass very fast. 
"Time is up," Mrs. Wilson says loudly. "Please put your pencils down."
All the students put their pencils on their desks. Some students look happy. Some students look sad. 

"Pass your papers to the front," Mrs. Wilson says. 
Harry gives his paper to the girl in front of him. She gives the papers to the teacher. 
Mrs. Wilson takes all the papers. "Thank you, class," she says. "That was not so bad, was it?"

"It was okay," Harry says. 
"It was a little easy," Chloe says. 
Mrs. Wilson smiles. "I am glad to hear that. A surprise test shows me what you really know in your head. It shows me that you listened in class."

"When will we get our marks?" Harry asks. 
"I will mark them tonight," Mrs. Wilson says. "I will give them back to you tomorrow morning."

The rest of the day goes normally. They have English, they have art, and they play outside. 
But Harry thinks about the test. He hopes he did well. 

The next morning, Mrs. Wilson stands at the front of the class. She holds the pile of test papers. The papers have red marks on them. 
She calls the names. 
"Chloe," she says. Chloe walks to the front. Mrs. Wilson gives her the paper. "Excellent work, Chloe."
Chloe smiles. She has a perfect mark. 

"Harry," Mrs. Wilson says. 
Harry walks to the front slowly. He takes his paper. He looks at the red mark at the top. 
It says '9/10'. Nine out of ten! 
He only made one small mistake on question eight. 

"Very good, Harry," Mrs. Wilson says. 
"Thank you," Harry says happily. 
He walks back to his desk. He sits down. He looks at his good mark. 
He realizes something important. If he listens in class every day, he does not need to be afraid. He knows the answers. A surprise test is not scary if you are a good student. He smiles and puts the paper in his bag.
