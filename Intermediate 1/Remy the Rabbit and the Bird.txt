Category: Intermediate
Title: Remy the Rabbit and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to play and spring all day long. He had many friends, but his best friend was a bird named Bella. They played together every day. One day, Remy and Bella found a long cut on the ground. It was very strange, and they didn't know what made it. Remy was very brave and wanted to find out. Bella was a little scared, but she wanted to help Remy. They followed the cut to see where it went. At the end of the cut, they found a fragile egg. It was all alone and needed help. Remy and Bella knew they had to keep it safe. They made a soft nest for the egg and took turns watching it. After some time, the egg started to move and crack. A tiny, cute bird came out of the egg. Remy and Bella were so happy to have a new friend. They named the baby bird Cutie and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to ride his red bike. He was an adventurous boy and liked to explore new places. One sunny day, Tim found a small toy in his pocket. It was a little green frog. "Hello, little frog!" Tim said. "Do you want to ride with me on my bike?" The little frog nodded its head. So, Tim and the little frog went on a fun ride together. They went up and down hills, and through the park. They laughed and had a great time. At the end of the day, Tim put the little frog back in his pocket and went home. They had many more adventures together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to discover new things. He saw a big tree with high branches. Tim wanted to climb the tree and look around. Tim started to climb the tree. With each turn, he went higher and higher. He was so happy to be up in the high tree. He could see the whole park from up there. After a while, Tim climbed back down. He was proud of his discovery. He ran to his mom and told her all about his adventure. Tim's mom was happy that he had so much fun. They went home, and Tim told everyone about his high tree adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat. The cat liked to play with a toy rocket. The rocket was red and went very fast. The cat would push the rocket and watch it go. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat could not find the rocket. The cat looked under the bed, in the box, and behind the door. The cat was sad. The cat hoped to find the rocket soon. Then, the cat saw the rocket on a high shelf. The cat jumped and jumped, but could not reach it. The cat's friend, a dog, came to help. The dog was tall and could reach the rocket. The dog gave the rocket to the cat. The cat was very happy and said "thank you" to the dog. They played with the rocket together and were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the fair with his mom. He saw a big bull in a pen. Tim wondered what the bull's name was. He asked his mom, "What is the bull's name?" His mom said, "I don't know. Let's ask the farmer." The farmer told them, "The bull's name is Big Red." Tim and his mom went on fun rides and ate yummy food. Tim loved the fair. When it was time to go home, Tim waved goodbye to Big Red. "Bye, Big Red," he said. "I will see you next time at the fair." The bull looked at Tim and made a loud sound. Tim laughed and went home with his mom. He had a great day at the fair and couldn't wait to come back next year.

Once upon a time, there was a rhinoceros. He was so big and strong. One day, he decided to go and get some food, so he set off on a journey. Along the way, he came across a house. Inside he saw a little girl, and she called out to him, "Hello!" "Hi there!" said the rhinoceros. "What can I do for you?" The little girl smiled and said, "I need help fixing my toy. Can you help me?" The rhinoceros thought for a moment, then said, "Yes! I think I can fix it." The little girl was so excited. She handed him the toy, and the rhinoceros got to work. After a few minutes of tinkering, the toy was fixed and the girl was so happy. "Thank you!" she said, offering him a yummy ice cream cone. The rhinoceros happily accepted and they both enjoyed the delicious treat. The rhinoceros continued on his way, feeling proud of himself. He had helped the little girl and enjoyed an yummy snack too.
