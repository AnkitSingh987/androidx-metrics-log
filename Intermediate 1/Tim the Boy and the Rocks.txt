Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rocks

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to collect rocks. One day, he found a big, shiny rock. It was so shiny that it looked like a flashlight. Tim was very happy. Tim showed the shiny rock to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "Wow! That's not a bad rock, Tim! It's a great rock!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam. I love to collect rocks. Do you want to help me find more?" Sam and Tim went to the park to look for more rocks. They used Tim's flashlight to see in the dark. They found a lot of rocks, but none were as shiny as the first one. They put all the rocks in a big bag. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam were tired but happy. They had a lot of fun collecting rocks together. The shiny rock was still the best rock they found. They couldn't wait to go on another adventure together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a street. On this street, there lived a crazy cat. This cat loved to sort things. Every day, the cat would sort all the toys in the street. One day, the cat found a big red ball. The ball was hidden behind a tree. The cat thought, "This ball is so big! I must show it to all my friends!" So, the cat rolled the ball down the street. The ball was so big that it made a loud noise as it rolled. As the cat rolled the ball, all the other animals came out to see what was going on. They saw the big red ball and were very excited. The crazy cat said, "Look at my big red ball! Let's all play together!" And so, all the animals played with the big red ball, and they had the best day ever. The cat was so happy that it had sorted the toys and found the big red ball to share with its friends.

Tom and Lily liked to play with their toy trains. They had many trains of different colors and sizes. They made tracks on the floor and pretended to be drivers. One day, Tom had a weird idea. He wanted to mix the trains and make new ones. He took apart some of the trains and put the pieces together in different ways. He made a train with a red head, a blue body and a yellow tail. He made another train with a green head, a purple body and a pink tail. "Look, Lily, I made new trains!" Tom said. "They are very pretty and fun!" Lily looked at the mixed trains and frowned. She did not like them. She thought they were ugly and silly. She liked the trains the way they were before. "No, Tom, I don't like your new trains!" Lily said. "They are very weird and bad! You broke the trains! Put them back the way they were!" Tom and Lily started to argue. They both wanted to play with the trains their way. They did not want to share or compromise. They shouted and pulled the trains from each other. Their mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the mixed trains and the angry children. She was not happy. "Tom, Lily, what are you doing?" she asked. "Why are you fighting over the trains? Why did you mix them like that?" Tom and Lily tried to explain their reasons, but they both talked at the same time. Their mom could not understand them. She shook her head and sighed. "Tom, Lily, listen to me," she said. "Mixing the trains is not a bad thing, but you have to ask for permission first. And you have to respect each other's preferences. If Lily likes the trains the way they were, you have to let her play with them. If Tom likes the mixed trains, you have to let him play with them. Or you can take turns or trade or make new rules. But you have to be nice and polite and cooperative. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded. They felt sorry for making their mom upset. They also felt sorry for hurting each other's feelings. They decided to apologize and make up. "Sorry, Tom, I was mean to you," Lily said. "Your new trains are not bad, they are just different. Can I try one?" "Sorry, Lily, I was rude to you," Tom said. "Your old trains are not boring, they are just familiar. Can I try one?" They smiled and hugged. They gave each other a train and started to play again. They found out that the mixed trains and the old trains were both fun in their own ways. They also found out that playing together was more fun than fighting. Their mom smiled and left them to enjoy their game. She was glad that they learned a valuable lesson. She hoped that they would remember it the next time they had a weird idea or a disagreement.
