Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Clever Girl and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Sue. Sue loved cookies more than anything else. One day, she saw a big cookie jar on the high shelf in the kitchen. She wanted to get the cookies all by herself. Sue tried to reach the jar, but she was too small. She had an idea. She got a chair and climbed up on it. She was so close to the jar now. But then, the chair began to shake and Sue started to fall. Just as Sue was about to fall, her big brother Tom came into the room. He saw Sue and quickly caught her. Sue was safe, and Tom was a hero. Tom got the cookie jar down and shared the cookies with Sue. They both laughed and enjoyed the cookies together. And from that day on, Sue knew that sometimes, it's better to ask for help.

Once there was a pretty little girl. She loved to bathe every morning. One day it was her birthday. Her mommy and daddy gave her a rose. It was beautiful and smelled so good. The little girl was so excited. She ran to her bathroom and decided to take a special birthday bath. She put in special bubbles and candles all around. The little girl felt so fit and special when she was done. She wrapped herself in a towel and put the rose in a vase. She put the vase next to her bed so she could always remember her special day. The little girl was so happy. Bathing was now even more special and she could always remember the day she got her beautiful rose.

Once upon a time, there was a big, filthy bear named Bob. Bob lived in the woods with lots of trees. He liked to play with the lumber that he found on the ground. One day, Bob met a small bunny named Lily. Bob asked Lily, "Do you want to play with me and my lumber?" Lily looked at the dirty lumber and then at Bob. She did not know what to say. So, she just shrugged her little bunny shoulders and said, "I don't know, Bob. I have never played with lumber before." Bob smiled and said, "That's okay, Lily. I can show you how to play with it. We can build a little house for you." Lily was happy and nodded her head. They started to play with the lumber and built a lovely little house for Lily. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tim and Tom. They were very popular with their friends. They loved to play together and have fun all day. One day, their mom said, "Tim and Tom, you need to stay inside today. It is raining outside." The twins were sad, but they knew they had to listen to their mom. Tim and Tom decided to play inside. They played with their toys and talked to each other. Tim said, "Tom, I like playing with you. You are my best friend." Tom smiled and said, "I like playing with you too, Tim. We have lots of fun together." The twins stayed inside and played all day. They had a great time, even though they could not go outside. Their mom was happy to see them having fun together. When the rain stopped, Tim and Tom went outside to play again. They were still popular with their friends, and everyone was happy to see them. The twins knew that they could have fun no matter where they were.

Jim and his mom were walking in the park. His mom pointed out a tree with thin branches. "See that tree, Jim? It looks quite modest." Jim nodded his head, still looking at the tree. Suddenly, a gust of wind blew and the thin branches split in two. Sauce started dripping from the tree. Jim reached out to touch it, but his mom grabbed his hand. "No, Jim. That's not safe. That's why we don't touch things we don't know, okay?" Jim nodded his head again. He had learned his lesson. The next day, his mom took him to the park again. This time, Jim saw a big tree with thick branches, but there was sauce on it too. He remembered what his mom said and didn't touch it. But then he saw a thin branch split and more sauce started dripping out. His mom grabbed him by the arm quickly. "See what I mean, Jim? That's why we need to be careful." Jim nodded his head, feeling a little scared. He knew he should never try to touch sauce again.
