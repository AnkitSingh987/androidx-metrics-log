Category: Intermediate
Title: The Calm Planet and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a calm planet. The planet was very quiet and peaceful. The people living on the planet were kind and always happy. They liked to help each other and share their things. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the planet. Mia could hear the soft sounds of the wind and the birds. She was very curious and wanted to learn more about this new place. She went to a big tree and saw a boy named Tom. Tom was sitting under the tree, reading a book. Mia and Tom became friends. They played together and had a lot of fun. They learned that when they were kind and shared with each other, they were always happy. The people on the calm planet taught them the importance of being nice and helping others. Soon, it was time for Mia to go back to her home. She said goodbye to Tom and promised to always be kind and share with others. She knew that the calm planet was a special place, and she would never forget the lessons she learned there. The moral of the story is that being kind and sharing with others can bring happiness and peace to our lives.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a big smile on his face because he was so happy to play. His mom said, "Tim, you are such a good boy." At the park, Tim saw a box with a lock on it. He asked his mom, "What is inside the box?" His mom said, "I don't know, let's unlock it and see." Tim and his mom found a key and unlocked the box. Inside, they found a ball. Tim played with the ball all day. He threw it, kicked it, and rolled it. His mom said, "I am so glad we found the ball, it made your smile even bigger." Tim said, "Yes, it was a good day at the park."

Once upon a time there was a great surprise. Everyone was so excited! They all raced to see what it was. It was a sport! Everyone cheered in delight. It was so great! They all had so much fun playing the sport together. They ran and jumped and laughed. It was the best surprise ever. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a fierce dog named Max. Max was big and strong. Everyone in the town was scared of Max. One day, Max saw a little cat named Lucy. Lucy was scared and said, "Please don't hurt me, Max!" Max looked at Lucy and decided to be nice. He said, "I won't hurt you, Lucy. Let's be friends!" Max and Lucy started to play together. They ran and jumped in the park. Suddenly, it started to rain. Max said, "We need to hurry home, Lucy!" They ran as fast as they could to get out of the rain. When they got home, they were both wet and tired. Max and Lucy became best friends and played together every day. And from that day on, Max wasn't fierce anymore.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim had a big, leather ball. He liked to play with his ball all the time. Tim was very good at sharing, so he would lend his ball to his friends, too. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing with the leather ball in the park. They were having a lot of fun. They threw the ball, kicked the ball, and laughed a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came running to them. The dog wanted to play, too! The dog took the ball in his mouth and ran away. Tim and his friends chased the dog to get the ball back. The dog was fast, but they did not give up. At last, the dog stopped and dropped the ball. Tim and his friends were surprised. The ball was not leather anymore! It was now a soft, fluffy ball. The dog had changed the ball. Tim and his friends laughed and played with the dog and the new ball. They had even more fun with the fluffy ball and their new dog friend.
