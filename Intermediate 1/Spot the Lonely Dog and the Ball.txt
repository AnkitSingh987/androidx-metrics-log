Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Lonely Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lonely dog named Spot. Spot had no friends to play with. He would walk around the town and look for someone to be his friend. One day, Spot saw a big ball in a park. He ran to the ball and started to play with it. As he was playing, he did not see a puddle of water near him. He ran too fast and slip on the water. Spot fell down and felt sad. A little girl named Lily saw Spot fall down. She ran to him and helped him stand up. Lily said, "Do you want to play with me, Spot?" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. From that day on, Spot and Lily were best friends and played together every day. Spot was not lonely anymore.

One day, a dog named Max saw a cat doing yoga. Max thought it was fun, so he wanted to try too. The cat, named Lily, was happy to help Max learn yoga. They had a lot of fun together. One day, while Max and Lily were doing yoga, a rude bird flew by. The bird laughed at them and said, "You look silly!" Max and Lily felt bad. They wanted to show the bird that yoga was fun and not silly. Then, Max noticed a big tree with a lot of birds. He had an idea. Max and Lily started doing yoga under the tree. The birds watched them and soon, they wanted to try too. The rude bird saw this and felt left out. He said sorry to Max and Lily and asked if he could join. Max and Lily said yes, and all the animals did yoga together. The rude bird learned that it's not nice to laugh at others and everyone can have fun doing yoga.

Once upon a time, there was a humble dog named Max. Max liked to help his friends. He lived in a row of small houses with his friends, a cat, a bird, and a fish. One day, Max and his friends found a big stick. They all wanted to play with it. So, they decided to snap the stick into smaller pieces. Max used his strong teeth and snapped the stick. Now, they all had a piece to play with. Max and his friends played with their stick pieces all day. They were very happy. At the end of the day, they were tired but had lots of fun. They learned that sharing and being humble made their day even better.

One day, a little cat with soft fur went to play with her friends. They wanted to play a game that would include everyone. The cat's friends were a dog, a bird, and a fragile butterfly. The cat said, "Let's play hide and seek!" The dog barked, "Yes, that's a fun game!" The bird chirped, "I can fly and look for everyone!" The fragile butterfly said, "I can hide in small places!" So, they all started to play. The dog closed his eyes and counted to ten. The cat hid behind a big tree, the bird flew up high, and the fragile butterfly hid in a pretty flower. When the dog finished counting, he looked for his friends. The dog found the cat, the bird, and the fragile butterfly. They all laughed and played more games. They had a great day because they played a game that included everyone.

Tom and Mia were playing in the garden. They liked to look at the flowers and smell them. They saw a big yellow flower that was not open yet. "When will it bloom?" Mia asked. "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe tomorrow." They heard a loud noise in the sky. They looked up and saw a big metal bird. It had wings and a tail and a nose. It was flying very fast. "What is that?" Tom asked. "It's an aeroplane," Mia said. "My dad told me. It can take people to far away places." They watched the aeroplane until it disappeared behind the clouds. They wondered where it was going and who was inside. They thought it was very mysterious. "Do you want to go on an aeroplane someday?" Mia asked. "Yes," Tom said. "But only if you come with me." They smiled and hugged each other. They went back to the flowers and waited for the yellow one to bloom. They hoped it would be as beautiful as the aeroplane.
