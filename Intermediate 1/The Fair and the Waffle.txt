Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fair and the Waffle

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fair. At the fair, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to eat waffles. She was very happy when she saw a waffle stand at the fair. Emma went to the waffle stand and said, "One waffle, please!" The man at the stand gave her a big, yummy waffle. Emma took a bite and it was so tasty. She wanted to share her waffle with her friend, Ben. Emma found Ben near the drink stand. Ben was very thirsty. He said, "I want to drink some water." Emma said, "Here, have some water, and try my waffle too!" Ben drank the water and took a bite of the waffle. He thought it was very yummy. Emma and Ben walked around the fair, eating the waffle and drinking water. They had a lot of fun together. At the end of the day, they were both very happy. They went home with big smiles on their faces, looking forward to the next fair.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous dragon. The dragon lived in a big cave. He was very sad because he was not good at flying. One day, the dragon met a small bird. The bird saw the dragon was sad. The bird said, "I can help you improve your flying." The dragon was happy to hear this. Every day, the bird and the dragon practiced flying together. The dragon got better and better. Soon, the enormous dragon could fly high in the sky. He was very happy and thanked the bird for helping him improve.

Once upon a time there was a happy girl, she was three years old. She loved to play with her hoop every day. Her hoop rolled on the ground and over her feet in fun circles. One day, when the girl was playing with her hoop, she accidentally made her best friend fall over. She felt sad and sorry for what she had done. Her best friend was mad, she hugged her knees and ran away. The girl wanted her best friend to forgive her, so she ran after her best friend and gave her a big hug. Her best friend's face softened and she smilied at the girl. They lay down together on the grass, in a comfy spot beneath the tree. They talked and laughed until the girl felt better. It was nice to be friends again. The girl was so relieved and happy that her friend had forgiven her. They enjoyed playing with the hoop together, under the tree in their comfy spot.

Once upon a time, there was a sailor. He had a big, orange boat. The sailor liked to sail on the water with his boat. One day, the sailor saw a small fish. The fish was sad. "Please help me," the fish said. The sailor asked, "What do you need?" The fish said, "I need food." The sailor wanted to help the fish. The sailor went to find food for the fish. He demanded, "Give food for my fish friend!" People gave him food. The sailor gave the food to the fish. The fish was happy. The sailor and the fish became good friends. They sailed together on the big, orange boat.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big bush with red flowers. They wanted to smell the flowers. "Let's go to the bush!" Lily said. "OK!" Tom said. They ran to the bush. But when they got close, they heard a loud roar. A big, bad lion appeared from behind the bush. It had sharp teeth and claws. It looked angry. "Help!" Lily and Tom screamed. They turned around and ran away. The lion chased them. They were very scared. "Where is Mommy?" Lily cried. "Where is Daddy?" Tom cried. They ran to the bench where their parents were sitting. They hugged them tight. "Mommy, Daddy, there is a big, bad lion in the bush!" Lily and Tom said. Their parents looked at the bush. They did not see a lion. They saw a man in a lion costume. He was laughing. "Sorry, kids, I was just playing a prank. I did not mean to scare you. I am a clown from the circus. Do you want to see my tricks?" the man said. Lily and Tom did not think it was funny. They were still shaking. They did not want to see his tricks. They wanted to go home. "Mommy, Daddy, can we go home now?" Lily and Tom said. Their parents nodded. They took their hands and walked away. They did not like the clown. They did not like the bush. They did not like the park. They liked their home. It was safe and cozy. They never wanted to see a big, bad lion again.
