Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Mechanic and the Truck

One day, a mechanic named Tom was feeling poor. He did not have much work to do. He was sad because he needed money to buy food. Tom went for a walk to think about what to do. While walking, Tom saw a big truck. The truck was making a loud noise. The truck driver said, "Can you help me? My truck is not working well." Tom said, "Yes, I can do that. I am a mechanic." Tom fixed the truck and the driver was very happy. The driver said, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good mechanic." Then, the driver gave Tom a big bag. Tom opened the bag and saw a lot of money inside. Now, Tom was not poor anymore. He was very happy and could buy food for himself.

One day, a cat named Bob was walking in the city. He saw many cars and buses. There was a lot of traffic. Bob was confused. He did not know where to go. Bob saw a girl named Sue. She was pouring water into a cup. Bob asked her, "Can you help me? I am confused." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I can help you, Bob. Let's walk together." So, Bob and Sue walked through the city. They saw big buildings and many people. They were not confused anymore. They were happy to be friends.

Once upon a time, in a big, hot desert, there was a little cactus. The cactus was green and had many sharp points. It lived near a big rock and liked to watch the animals walk by. One day, a small bug was walking near the cactus. The bug did not see the cactus and took a step too close. "Ouch!" cried the bug, as it felt the sharp point on its foot. The cactus felt very embarrassed that it had hurt the bug. The cactus wanted to say sorry to the bug, so it thought of a plan. It saw a big leaf nearby and had an idea. The cactus asked a bird to help move the leaf next to it. Now, when bugs walked by, they saw the leaf and did not step on the cactus. The cactus was happy, and the bugs were safe.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They wanted to enjoy the sunny day. Lily brought her yummy muffin with her. She loved muffins a lot. While playing, Lily saw a little boy sitting alone. He looked sad. Lily walked up to him and said, "Hi! I'm Lily. Do you want to play with me?" The little boy smiled and said, "Yes! I'm Tim." Lily and Tim played and had fun. They ran, jumped, and laughed. After a while, they got hungry. Lily remembered her yummy muffin. She broke it in half and shared it with Tim. "This muffin is so yummy!" Tim said. They both enjoyed the muffin and the fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Sam. He liked to run and jump all day. One day, Sam went on a trip to the park with his friends. At the park, Sam saw a beautiful bird. The bird was very graceful as it flew in the sky. Sam wanted to play with the bird, but he knew he had to behave. So, he just watched the bird from far away. Sam had a lot of fun on his trip to the park. He played with his friends and watched the graceful bird. At the end of the day, Sam was happy because he behaved well and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous dinosaur. He liked to seat on big rocks. One day, he saw a little bird. The little bird was sad. The dinosaur asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird said, "I can't find my mom. I am lost." The dinosaur wanted to help. He said, "I will help you find your mom." They looked and looked. Then, they saw the little bird's mom. But, there was a big river in the way. The dinosaur had an idea. He lay down and made a bridge with his body. The little bird walked on the dinosaur to get to her mom. The little bird was happy. She said, "Thank you, dinosaur!" The dinosaur was happy too. They were friends. The moral of the story is: even if you are big and strong, you can still help others and make friends.
