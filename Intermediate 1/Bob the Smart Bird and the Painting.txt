Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Smart Bird and the Painting

Once upon a time, there was a smart bird named Bob. He lived on a small island with his family. They were very happy on their island. Bob liked to fly high in the sky and see the world around him. One day, Bob saw a big fire on the island. He was scared and said, "Mom, Dad, our island is burning!" His parents flew up with him to see the fire. They knew they had to do something to save their home. Bob had an idea. He and his family flew to the water and picked up water in their beaks. They flew back to the fire and dropped the water on it. They did this many times, and the fire went out. But when they looked around, they saw that the fire was not real. It was just a big painting of a fire. They all laughed and were happy that their island was safe.

---

Bob had a file. The file was flexible so he could eat it. He folded the file in half and ate half of it. His mom saw him eating and told him it was wrong. Bob knew it was wrong, but he was hungry and felt like he could not stop eating. Finally, Bob finished the file and told his mom he was sorry. His mom gave him a hug and told him he had to be flexible with food. From then on, Bob always remembered to be flexible with food and not to eat strange things.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little boy named Tim. Tim was very small and did not have big muscles like the other kids. One day, Tim saw a big box with a wrap on it. He knew it was a gift for him. He was so excited! The next day, Tim went to the park to play. There, he met a big kid named Ben. Ben was not nice. Ben saw Tim's small muscles and laughed. Tim felt sad and wanted to go home. But then he remembered the wrapped gift and had an idea. Tim went home and opened the gift. Inside was a ball. Tim played with the ball every day. He threw it and kicked it. After some time, Tim's muscles grew bigger and stronger. The next time he met Ben, Tim was not shy anymore. He stood up to Ben and they became friends. And they played with the ball together, happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Poppy. She lived in a modern house with her mom and dad. Poppy loved to play with her toys and stay in her room. One day, Poppy's mom said, "Stay in your room, Poppy, while I go to the store." Poppy agreed and began to play with her toys. But soon, she heard a sound outside her window. It was a small bird, chirping and singing. Poppy opened the window and said, "Hi, bird! I am Poppy. Do you want to play with me?" The bird nodded and flew into Poppy's room. They played together until Poppy's mom came back from the store. When Poppy's mom saw the bird, she said, "Poppy, you need to let the bird go back outside. It needs to be with its family." Poppy was sad, but she knew her mom was right. So, she opened the window and said goodbye to the bird. From that day on, Poppy would always remember her little bird friend. And every time she heard a bird sing, she would smile and think of the fun they had together.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big loop in her room. She did not know what to do with it. She thought it was amazing. She asked her mom, "What can I do with this loop?" Her mom said, "You can play a fun game with the loop. Just wait and see!" Amy was excited. She went outside with the loop. She saw her friend Tom. They both thought about what game to play. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's throw the loop and try to catch things!" Amy and Tom played with the loop. They caught a ball, a stick, and a toy car. They had so much fun. The loop was amazing, just like her mom said.
