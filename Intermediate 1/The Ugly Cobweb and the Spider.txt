Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ugly Cobweb and the Spider

One day, a little spider saw an ugly cobweb in the corner of the room. The cobweb was old and dirty. The little spider wanted to make it pretty. The little spider had an idea. It would paint the ugly cobweb. It found some red, blue, and yellow paint. The little spider was very happy. The little spider painted the cobweb with the colors. It painted red, blue, and yellow spots all over the cobweb. Soon, the ugly cobweb was pretty and colorful. The little spider was proud of its work.

Sara did not like the doctor. He was big and loud and wore a white coat. He always made her sit on a cold bed and looked at her with a light. He said she was sick and needed a shot. Sara hated shots. They hurt and made her cry. "Please, no shot, no shot!" Sara begged her mom, who held her hand. "I'm scared, Mommy!" "It's okay, sweetie, it's just a little pinch. It will make you feel better, I promise." Mom said, trying to calm her down. "Look, the doctor has a clear needle. It's very thin and clean. It won't hurt much." But Sara did not look. She closed her eyes and shook her head. She felt the doctor touch her arm and she panicked. She screamed and kicked and tried to get away. She knocked over a tray of tools and made a loud noise. "Stop, Sara, stop! You're making it worse!" Mom said, holding her tight. "It's almost over, just be brave for a moment!" The doctor finished the shot and put a band-aid on her arm. He smiled and gave her a sticker. "You did it, Sara, you're a good girl. The shot will help you fight the germs that make you sick. You'll be fine soon." Sara opened her eyes and saw the sticker. It was a pink unicorn. She liked unicorns. She sniffed and wiped her tears. She looked at the doctor and said, "Thank you, doctor. I'm sorry I was bad." The doctor patted her head and said, "You're welcome, Sara. You weren't bad, you were just scared. It's normal to be scared of shots. But you were very brave, too. I'm proud of you." Sara smiled and hugged her mom. She felt a little better. She hoped she would not need another shot for a long time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to eat yogurt. One day, she found a perfect cup of yogurt in the fridge. It was her favorite color, pink! Mia took the yogurt to her mom. "Mom, can I eat this yogurt?" she asked. Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Mia! That is a perfect yogurt for you." Mia got a spoon and started to eat her yogurt. She wanted to share it with her cat, Whiskers. So, she spread some yogurt on a plate for Whiskers. Mia and Whiskers ate their yogurt together. They were both very happy. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard rock. This rock lived by the sea. Every day, the sea would wave at the rock. The rock liked the waves and the sea. They were good friends. One day, a small boy came to the beach. The boy had a big, round stomach. He saw the rock and wanted to play with it. The boy tried to push the rock, but it was too hard. He tried to climb the rock, but he could not. He sat on the rock, feeling sad. Then, the sea saw the boy and wanted to help. The sea sent its waves to the rock. The waves made the rock wet and soft. The boy could now play with the rock. He was very happy. The boy, the rock, and the sea became best friends. The moral of the story is that we can always find a way to help our friends, even if it seems hard at first.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to hop and play with his friends. One day, Remy saw his friend, Benny the squirrel, taking a nap under a tree. Remy thought it would be fun to tease Benny while he was napping. He hopped over to Benny and said, "Wake up, Benny! You are always napping. Let's play!" Benny opened his eyes and said, "Remy, I need my nap to be useful and help my family find food later." Remy felt bad for teasing Benny and decided to be useful too. He helped Benny find nuts and berries for his family. They worked together and had fun. From that day on, Remy learned that being useful and helping friends is better than teasing them. And they all lived happily ever after.
