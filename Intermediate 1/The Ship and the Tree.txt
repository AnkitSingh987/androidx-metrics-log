Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ship and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship had many people on it. They all liked to help each other. They were compassionate. One day, the ship saw a big tree. The tree was in the way. They had to cut it. So, they all worked together to cut the tree. They cut and cut until the tree fell down. After they cut the tree, they moved the ship. The ship went on and on. The people on the ship were happy. They knew they could do anything when they helped each other. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a small, silver bird. The bird lived in a big tree. The tree was in a pretty park. The bird had many friends in the park. One day, the bird found a number on the ground. The number was "3". The bird showed the number to a squirrel friend. The squirrel said, "Wow! That is a nice number!" The bird and the squirrel played with the number. They counted to three. One, two, three! They were very happy. The small, silver bird put the number in its nest. The bird and the squirrel were best friends. They played in the pretty park every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to design things. She would draw and color all day long. One day, she decided to make a special dessert for her friend, Tom. Mia went to the kitchen and found all the things she needed. She mixed some flour, sugar, and eggs. Then, she put it in the oven. When it was done, she took it out and let it cool. It looked yummy! Mia wanted to surprise Tom, so she hid the dessert under a big bowl. She called Tom and said, "Tom, come to my house. I have a surprise for you!" Tom came over and saw the big bowl. He asked, "What's under the bowl, Mia?" Mia smiled and said, "It's a hidden dessert I designed just for you!" Tom lifted the bowl and saw the yummy dessert. He was so happy and thanked Mia. They both enjoyed the dessert and had a fun day together.

One day, Bubba wanted to buy something special. He had saved up all his money and wanted to get a new toy car. He went to the store but it was all sold out. Bubba was so sad. He wanted a toy car very much. He went home. Then, he had an idea. Bubba decided to go to the mechanic. He asked if the mechanic could help him. The mechanic said it was easy to make a toy car from a real car. Bubba was so glad. He paid the mechanic, who cut up the old car and made a toy one for Bubba. Bubba was so happy. He had a new toy car he could play with. His problem had been solved. He felt so relieved.

One day, a small dog named Spot was excited to enter a race. All his friends would be there too. Spot wanted to win the race and get a big prize. He put on his fast shoes and went to the race with his mom. At the race, Spot saw his friend, Lily the cat. "I want to win too," said Lily. "Let's race together!" Spot liked that idea. They lined up and waited for the race to start. The race began and Spot and Lily ran as fast as they could. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew off Spot's fast shoes! Spot was sad, but Lily said, "Don't worry, we can still win together." They held hands and ran to the finish line. They didn't win first place, but they were happy because they finished the race together.

One day, a red vehicle was driving down the street. The vehicle was big and shiny. It was a fire truck. The fire truck was always alert and ready to help people. The fire truck saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and could not get down. A nice man in the fire truck said, "I will help you, little cat." The fire truck had a big ladder to provide help. The man climbed up the ladder and got the little cat. He brought the cat down to the ground. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, fire truck!" The fire truck drove away, always alert and ready to help.
