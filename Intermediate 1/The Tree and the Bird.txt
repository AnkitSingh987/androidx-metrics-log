Category: Intermediate
Title: The Tree and the Bird

One day, a big tree had a job. The tree's job was to let its leaves fall. The leaves were red, yellow, and brown. The tree liked its job, but it felt uncomfortable because it was too tall. A small bird came to the tree and said, "Why are you uncomfortable, tree?" The tree told the bird, "I am too tall, and I want to be small like you." The bird said, "I can help you with that." The bird flew away and came back with a magic seed. The bird told the tree, "Eat this seed, and you will become small like me." The tree ate the seed, and something unexpected happened. The tree did not become small, but it grew even taller! The tree was sad, but the bird said, "Don't be sad. Being tall is good too. You can see far away, and you can help other animals." The tree understood and felt happy with its job again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat named Kitty. One day, Kitty got a small rip in her side. Lily was sad because Kitty was hurt. She wanted to heal Kitty and make her feel better. Lily went to her mom and asked, "Mom, can you help me heal Kitty?" Her mom looked at the rip and said, "Yes, Lily. I can help you. We will need to sew Kitty up with some thread." Lily felt happy that her mom could help. Her mom got some thread and sewed up Kitty's side. The rip was right next to Kitty's lung, but it was harmless. Soon, Kitty was all better. Lily hugged Kitty and said, "I'm so glad you're healed, Kitty. Now we can play again." And they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Spot. Spot lived with a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together every day. But one day, Spot heard a new voice in the yard next door. It was a young cat named Fluffy. Spot didn't know if he should be friends with Fluffy or not. He thought cats and dogs were not supposed to like each other. He felt worried and didn't know what to do. Spot shrugged and decided to talk to Fluffy. When Spot and Fluffy talked, they found out they both liked to play with balls and chase birds. They decided to be friends and play together. Sue saw them playing and was happy that Spot had a new friend. From that day on, Spot and Fluffy played together every day, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rich boy named Tom. He had a lot of toys and a big box of lollipops. Tom loved his lollipops very much. One day, a small girl named Sue came to play with Tom. She saw the big box of lollipops and wanted one too. Tom did not want to share his lollipops with Sue. This made Sue very sad and she started to cry. Tom's mom heard Sue crying and came to see what was wrong. She told Tom that it was good to share with friends. Tom thought about it and decided to share his lollipops with Sue. They both ate a lollipop and played together happily. From that day on, Tom always shared his toys and lollipops with his friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked into a thick fog. Tim could not see very far. He felt a bit scared. But his mom held his hand tight. In the fog, they found a small, sour fruit on the ground. Tim asked, "Mom, can I eat this?" His mom said, "No, Tim, it might not be good for you. Let's find something else to eat." They walked some more and found a big tree with sweet fruits. They picked some fruits and sat down to eat. As they ate, the sun came out and the fog went away. Tim and his mom felt warm and dry. They smiled and walked back home, happy to have spent time together.
