Category: Intermediate
Title: Tuna the Fish and the Shark

Once upon a time, there was a funny fish named Tuna. Tuna lived in a big, blue sea. Tuna was very smooth and liked to joke with his friends. One day, Tuna met a big, mean shark. The shark wanted to eat Tuna. Tuna was scared and did not want to be eaten. Tuna thought of a plan to make the shark go away. Tuna told the shark a funny joke. The shark laughed and laughed. The shark liked Tuna's joke so much that he did not want to eat Tuna anymore. Tuna and the shark became good friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Lila and Ben were best friends. They liked to play and learn new things. One day, they saw a big mill near the river. It had a wheel that turned with the water. They wanted to see inside. "Let's plan a trip to the mill," Lila said. "Maybe we can find out how it works." "OK, but we have to be careful," Ben said. "The mill is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who make flour and bread." They asked their moms if they could go to the mill. Their moms said yes, but only with a guide. They gave them some snacks and water. They also gave them a map and a phone. Lila and Ben were very happy. They thanked their moms and put their things in a backpack. They walked to the mill, following the map. They saw the wheel and the river. They heard the noise of the mill. They met the guide at the door. He was a nice man with a hat and a beard. He smiled and said, "Hello, I'm Mr. Brown. I work at the mill. Are you here to learn about it?" "Yes, we are," Lila and Ben said. "We are very curious and gifted. We want to know how the mill makes flour and bread." Mr. Brown was impressed. He said, "You are very smart and brave. Come with me, I'll show you around. But you have to stay close and listen to me. The mill can be dangerous if you don't follow the rules." Lila and Ben nodded. They followed Mr. Brown into the mill. They saw the machines and the bags of flour. They smelled the bread and the yeast. They learned how the water made the wheel turn, and how the wheel made the stones grind the wheat. They learned how the flour was mixed with water and yeast, and how the dough was shaped and baked. They asked many questions and Mr. Brown answered them. They had a lot of fun and learned a lot. Mr. Brown gave them each a slice of bread and a sticker. He said, "You are very gifted and polite. I hope you enjoyed your visit. Come back anytime." Lila and Ben thanked Mr. Brown and said goodbye. They walked back to their homes, sharing their bread and their stories. They were very happy and proud. They had planned a great trip to the mill.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a gray plate that she loved very much. One day, Lucy saw her gray plate on the table and began to wonder what she could put on it. Lucy went outside and saw a red apple on the ground. She picked it up and put it on her gray plate. She was very happy. Then, she saw a small cat playing with a ball. Lucy had an idea! She put the ball on the plate too. But something unexpected happened. The ball began to grow bigger and bigger on the gray plate! The apple fell off the plate and rolled away. The cat chased after the apple, and Lucy just stood there, watching her gray plate with the big ball on it. She wondered what would happen next.

Once upon a time there was a green cable that lived in the woods. All alone, the cable was lonely and sad. One day, the cable heard a voice. It was a small voice, but it was very friendly. The cable looked up to see where the voice was coming from. It was a little girl. "Do you want to be my friend?" she said. The cable was excited and said yes! The little girl told the cable that if it promised to be her friend, she promised to come back every day and visit. The cable promised it would be the best friend ever. Every day the little girl and the cable talked and laughed together. The little girl was never lonely and the cable was never alone anymore. They were the best of friends.
