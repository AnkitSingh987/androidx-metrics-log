Category: Intermediate
Title: Fred the Green Frog and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred loved fashion. He liked to wear hats, shirts, and shoes. Fred was very good at picking nice clothes to wear. His friends always asked him for help when they wanted to look nice too. One day, Fred's friend, a duck named Daisy, wanted to look nice for a party. She asked Fred to recommend some clothes for her. Fred thought about it and said, "I think you should wear a green hat and a yellow dress. You will look very pretty!" Daisy listened to Fred's advice and wore the green hat and yellow dress to the party. All of her friends loved her outfit and told her she looked very nice. Daisy was very happy and thanked Fred for his help. From that day on, all the animals in the forest knew that if they needed help with fashion, they could always ask Fred the green frog.

Once upon a time, there was a cosy little house. The walls were warm and the windows let in lots of sunshine. Every morning, the family would gather around the table and chat over a cup of hot coffee. One day, Mama noticed a cobweb above the window. She asked her daughter to gather it so it wouldn't be in the way. Her daughter went over to the window and gave it a good shake. Soon a big cobweb flew out of the window and landed on their noses! Everyone was full of laughter. Mama said, "Let's go outside to gather all the cobwebs, so we can keep the house nice and warm." The family went outside to the garden and gathered all the cobwebs. When they were done, the house was sparkly clean, and it felt even warmer. The little girl said, "That was fun, Mama! Can we do it again tomorrow?" Mama smiled and said, "Yes, of course we can! Let's gather some more cobwebs and make our house even cosier." And everyone agreed.

One day, a little dog named Tim was very tired. He walked and walked, looking for food. Tim saw a big house with a nice smell. He thought, "Maybe I can find some food there." Tim went to the house and saw a big man. The man had a plate of meat. Tim was happy and wagged his tail. The man looked at Tim and gave a shrug. He put the plate down on the ground for Tim to eat. Tim ate the meat and felt better. He was not tired anymore. He jumped and played with the big man. They had lots of fun together. Now, Tim had a new friend and a nice home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red ball that he loved to play with. One day, while playing outside, he saw a big dog. The dog looked bad and mean. Tim wanted to be friends with the dog, so he walked up to it. He held out his hand and said, "Hi, dog! I am Tim. Do you want to play?" The dog looked at Tim's hand and then at the red ball. The dog barked, "Yes, let's play!" Tim was happy. He told the dog, "I will throw the ball and you can catch it." Tim held the ball in his hand and got ready to release it. Tim threw the ball and the dog ran to catch it. They played and played until they were both tired. The dog wasn't bad after all, it just wanted a friend. From that day on, Tim and the dog were best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a lovely big forest, there lived a giant. The giant was very kind and had many friends. All the animals loved to play with him. One day, a little boy came to the forest. He saw the giant and wanted to play too. The little boy said, "Hello, giant! Can I play with you?" The giant smiled and said, "Of course, little boy! Let's play!" They were having so much fun when suddenly, the little boy found a toy that could shoot. He didn't know it was a toy and thought it was real. So, he tried to shoot the giant to save his friends. But, surprise! The toy only shot out bubbles. The giant and the little boy laughed and played with the bubbles. They were happy, and no one was scared.
