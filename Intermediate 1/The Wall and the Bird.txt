Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wall and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big wall. The wall was blue and pretty. A little bird liked to sit on the wall. The bird was happy there. The bird had a friend, a small cat. The cat was nice and did not hurt the bird. One day, the bird had a plan. The bird wanted to make the wall more beautiful. The bird told the cat about the plan. The cat liked the plan too. They wanted to put flowers on the wall. The bird and the cat worked together. They put red, yellow, and pink flowers on the wall. The wall looked even more pretty now. They were very happy with their plan. The bird and the cat played near the wall every day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One hot day, Tim took his cool towel with him to the park. While he played, Tim saw a big rock. He wanted to climb it, but he didn't see a small hole near the rock. Tim started to climb, but he stumbled on the hole and fell. He was sad and scared. Just then, a kind squirrel came to help Tim. The squirrel took Tim's cool towel and put it on his knee. Tim felt better and smiled. The squirrel became Tim's new friend, and they played together at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little owl who lived in a big tree. He was very sleepy. He yawned and yawned and yawned. He even rubbed his sleepy eyes. Suddenly, the owl heard a noise. He looked around the tree, but he couldn't see what it was. He thought he saw something in the branches, but it was too dark to tell. The owl hopped down from the tree, looking for the noise. As he hopped around, he saw a filthy puddle. In the puddle he saw an old, filthy frog. The owl fluttered down to say hello, but the frog was too sleepy. He had been up all night and was ready to go to sleep. The frog and the owl said good night as the owl flew back up into the big tree. The owl was so happy he had a new friend, he let out one last yawn before he finally fell asleep.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a girl named Sally. Sally liked to write. She would write all day. She wrote about her dog, her cat, and her friends. One day, she heard about a dangerous place in the city. She wanted to see it. Sally asked her mom if she could go. Her mom said, "No, it is too dangerous." But Sally really wanted to see it. She thought, "I will write a story about it first." So, she wrote a story about the dangerous place. In her story, she found a big, strong friend to go with her. One day, Sally met a big, strong boy named Tim. She told him about the dangerous place. Tim said, "I will go with you." They went to the dangerous place together. Just like in her story, Sally and Tim found a way to make the place safe. Sally and Tim told everyone in the city about the safe place. They all went to see it. The city was happy. Sally learned that writing could help her make things better. She kept writing stories to help her friends and her city.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. One day, he went for a walk near a creek. He liked to listen to the water. As Tim walked, he saw a fish in the creek. The fish was sad. Tim asked, "Why are you sad, fish?" The fish said, "I want to go back to my family, but I can't swim well." Tim wanted to help the fish. Tim picked up the fish and put it back in the water with its family. The fish was grateful and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Now, the fish was happy. Tim was happy too. He liked to help his new friends.
