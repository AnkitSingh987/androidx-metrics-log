Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Rabbit and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a cute little rabbit named Bobo. Bobo loved to make art with scissors. He would cut out shapes and make pretty pictures. One day, Bobo wanted to make a special gift for his friend, Momo the mouse. Bobo said, "Momo, I want to make you a gift. What do you like?" Momo thought for a moment and said, "I like stars!" So, Bobo went to work cutting out stars with his scissors. But, it was hard to make them perfect. Momo saw Bobo trying hard and said, "You can do it, Bobo! I believe in you!" Momo's words encouraged Bobo to keep trying. He cut out many stars until he made a perfect one. Bobo gave the perfect star to Momo, who was very happy. They hung the star on Momo's wall, and every time they looked at it, they remembered how their friendship encouraged each other to do their best. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He had a watch that his friend, a dog named Max, gave him. The watch was very special because it helped Tom know when it was time to eat. One day, Tom and Max were playing in the park. They were running and jumping, having lots of fun. But then, Tom looked at his paw and saw that his watch was gone. "Oh no!" he cried, "I lost my watch!" Max looked at Tom and said, "Don't worry, Tom. We will find your watch." They looked under the trees, in the grass, and by the pond. Finally, they found the watch near a big rock. Tom was so happy, he hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max! You are the best friend ever." From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore. He learned the importance of taking care of his things, especially the watch that Max gave him. And they both played happily ever after.

One day, a dog named Max was walking with his boy Tim. Max hurt his heel on a rock. "Ouch!" he said. Tim looked at Max and saw that he was hurt. "Don't worry, Max. I will help you," Tim said. They went to the doctor for help. The doctor looked at Max's heel and said, "It will be okay. Just rest and it will get better." Max was sad because he could not play with Tim. Tim was sad too, but he knew he needed to help Max get better. Tim brought Max some delicious food to make him feel better. They sat and talked while Max rested. After a few days, Max's heel was better and they could play again. They were both very happy. The moral of the story is that we should always help our friends when they are hurt, and good things will come from it.

Lily was very scared. She had to go to the hospital for an operation. She did not like the hospital. It was big and noisy and smelled funny. She did not want to leave her mom and dad. "Mom, I don't want to go," she said, holding her mom's hand. "I know, sweetie, but you have to. The doctor will make your ear better. You have an infection and it hurts a lot, right?" her mom said, hugging her. "Yes, but I'm afraid. What if it hurts more?" Lily said, crying. "It won't, I promise. The doctor will give you some medicine to make you sleep. You won't feel anything. And when you wake up, you will have a bandage on your ear and a sticker and a lollipop. And we will be right here, waiting for you," her dad said, smiling. Lily nodded, but she was still scared. She followed her mom and dad to a room where a nurse was waiting. The nurse was wearing a blue dress and a white hat. She had a big smile and a friendly voice. "Hello, Lily. I'm Nurse Anna. I'm going to take you to the operation room. Are you ready?" she said, holding a wheelchair. Lily shook her head. She did not want to go with the nurse. She wanted to stay with her mom and dad. "Come on, Lily. It's okay. Nurse Anna is very nice. She will take good care of you. We will see you soon," her mom said, kissing her cheek. Lily looked at her mom and dad. They looked proud of her. They wanted her to be brave. She took a deep breath and nodded. She let go of her mom's hand and climbed into the wheelchair. The nurse pushed her to the door. "Bye, mom. Bye, dad. I love you," she said, waving. "We love you too, Lily. You're doing great," they said, waving back. Lily felt a little better. She was still scared, but she was also proud. She was going to the operation. She was going to make her ear better. She was going to be brave. She smiled at the nurse. "Let's go, Nurse Anna," she said. The nurse smiled back and pushed the wheelchair. As they left the room, Lily saw a boy sitting on a chair. He had a cast on his arm and a frown on his face. He looked angry and bored. He saw Lily and stuck out his tongue. He slapped his cast with his other hand. He made a loud noise. Lily did not like the boy. He was rude and mean. She did not want to be like him. She wanted to be nice and brave. She ignored him and looked ahead. She was going to the operation. She was going to be proud.
