Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ship and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a small ship. The ship had a big sail and it lived in the water. The ship wanted to go on a fun trip. It wanted to see new things and meet new friends. One day, the ship saw a scary fish. The fish had big teeth and it looked angry. The ship was scared, but it wanted to be brave. So, the ship said "hello" to the scary fish. The fish said "hello" back. After talking, the ship and the scary fish became friends. They played and had fun in the water. The ship was happy it had a new friend. The ship learned that even if something looks scary, it can still be a good friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. Tim loved to play with Spot, but he also liked to help his mom and dad with things around the house. One day, Tim's mom asked him to gather wood for the fireplace. Tim was very patient as he looked for the best wood. He found some in the yard and said, "Spot, help me carry this wood inside!" Spot picked up a stick with his mouth and they both brought the wood to the fireplace. Tim's dad helped him put the wood in the fireplace and they started a warm, cozy fire. Tim, Spot, and his parents sat by the fire, talking and laughing. Tim felt proud that he could help his family and they all had a great time together.

One day, a big man took a step into a new nation. In this nation, people wore tight clothes. The big man did not like tight clothes. He liked big, loose clothes. He felt sad because he did not fit in. Then, he met a little girl. She said, "Hello, big man! I like your big clothes. They look fun!" The big man smiled and said, "Thank you, little girl. I like your tight clothes too. They look nice on you." One day, the big man and the little girl found a magic box. They opened it and something unexpected happened. All the tight clothes in the nation became big and loose, and the big man's clothes became tight. The people were surprised, but they laughed and said, "Now we know how it feels to wear different clothes!" The big man and the little girl became friends with everyone in the nation. They learned that it is okay to be different and have fun together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue ball. The blue ball lived in a small house with an old man. The old man liked to play with the blue ball every day. They would roll and bounce all around the house together. One sunny day, the old man said, "Blue ball, do you want to go outside and play?" The blue ball nodded happily. So, they went out to the green grass and played all day. They rolled and bounced under the big, warm sun. As the sun began to set, the old man said, "Blue ball, it's time to go home." The blue ball nodded again, and they went back to their small house. The old man and the blue ball were very tired but happy. They had a fun day playing together.

One day, a dog named Mark was feeling gloomy. He wanted to play with his friends, but they were all busy. Mark was sad and didn't know what to do. Mark walked around and saw a big ball. He thought, "Maybe I can play by myself." Mark tried to play with the ball, but it wasn't fun without his friends. Just then, a new dog came to play. Mark was happy and not gloomy anymore. They played with the ball together and became best friends. Now, Mark always had someone to play with.

One day, a boy named Ben went to the zoo with his mom and dad. He liked to see all the animals, but his favorite was the otter. The otter was lively and playful. He swam and slid and splashed in the water. He made funny noises and faces. Ben laughed and clapped his hands. "Can I feed the otter, mom?" Ben asked. "Sure, honey. Here, take this fish. But be careful, don't get too close to the fence," mom said. Ben took the fish and walked to the fence. He saw the otter looking at him with big eyes. He threw the fish over the fence. The otter caught it in his mouth and ate it. He wagged his tail and made a happy sound. "Thank you, thank you!" the otter said. Ben was surprised. He did not know that otters could talk. "You're welcome, otter. You're very cute and fun," Ben said. "Thank you, Ben. You're very kind and friendly. Do you want to play with me?" the otter said. "Yes, I do. How can we play?" Ben said. The otter thought for a moment. Then he said, "How about this? I'll do a trick in the water, and you guess what I'm doing. If you guess right, I'll give you a high five. If you guess wrong, you have to make a funny face. OK?" "OK, that sounds like fun. Let's play!" Ben said. And so they played. The otter did tricks like spinning, jumping, and diving. Ben guessed some right and some wrong. They high fived and made funny faces. They had a lot of fun. Mom and dad watched and smiled. They were happy to see Ben and the otter having a good time. They thanked the zookeeper for letting them feed the otter. They thanked the otter for being so lively and friendly. They thanked each other for a wonderful day at the zoo.
