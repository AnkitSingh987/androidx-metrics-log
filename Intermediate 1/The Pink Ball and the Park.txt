Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pink Ball and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big pink ball. The pink ball had many friends in the park. One day, a little boy named Tim came to play with the pink ball. Tim was very patient with the pink ball. He said, "Pink ball, let's play catch!" Tim threw the pink ball up in the air and caught it. The pink ball was happy to play with Tim. Then, Tim's friend Sarah came to play too. Sarah said, "Can I play with the pink ball?" Tim said, "Yes, let's all play together!" They threw the pink ball to each other, but one time, Sarah dropped the pink ball. The pink ball was not hurt, and they all laughed and kept playing. They had lots of fun, and the pink ball loved its new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular youth named Tim. Tim was very strong and liked to carry things. One day, he saw a big box near his house. Tim thought it would be fun to carry the box to show his friends how strong he was. Tim went to his friend, Sally, and said, "Look what I can carry!" Sally was amazed and asked, "What is inside the box?" Tim did not know, but he wanted to find out. They decided they would open the box together. As they opened the box, they found a small, funny-looking dog. The dog could talk! The dog said, "Thank you for carrying me. I have been stuck in that box for a long time." Tim and Sally were very surprised. They became good friends with the talking dog, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jake. He loved to play outside and build things. One day, Jake grabbed some cardboard and began building a castle. He got so excited that he started throwing the cardboard around, making a big mess! Some birds were watching from the other side of the park, and they started to laugh. "What are you silly birds doing?" Jake asked, trying to be serious. The birds began to fly around and said, "We're having fun!" So Jake decided to have fun, too. He grabbed the cardboard and started throwing it in the air. He chased the birds around the park, laughing. His castle was forgotten! When it was time to go home, Jake and his cardboard were both tired and happy. He let out one last silly laugh before heading home.

Once upon a time, in a colorful swamp, there lived a big frog named Fred. Fred loved to jump and play with his friends. One day, while Fred was jumping, he saw a big hole in the ground. He thought, "I should fill this hole so no one gets hurt." Fred called his friend, a small bird named Betty. "Betty, can you help me fill this hole?" he asked. Betty said, "Yes, let's find some dirt and leaves to fill it up." So, Fred and Betty worked together to fill the hole in the colorful swamp. After they filled the hole, they saw their friend, a little fish named Finn. Finn said, "Thank you for filling the hole. I almost swam into it yesterday!" Fred and Betty were happy they could help their friend. They all played together in the colorful swamp, knowing it was now a safer place to be.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to take walks in the park with her mom. One sunny day, they went to the park and saw a big tree. The tree had many round fruits on it. Sue saw one fruit that was orange and soft. It was a peach! Sue asked her mom if she could take the peach from the tree. Her mom said yes, so Sue took the peach. She was very glad. Sue and her mom sat on the grass under the tree. They shared the peach and it was sweet and yummy. After they ate the peach, Sue and her mom played in the park. They were happy and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Sue said goodbye to the peach tree. She hoped to find more peaches next time.
