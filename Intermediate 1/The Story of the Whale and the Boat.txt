Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Whale and the Boat

One day, a cheerful whale was swimming in the big blue sea. The whale loved to sing and play with his fish friends. They were all very happy. The whale and his friends saw a big boat coming. They were scared. The whale said, "I will swim close and ask them to stop." So, the whale swam fast to the boat. He talked to the people on the boat. "Please stop, my friends and I want to play here," said the whale. The people on the boat listened to the cheerful whale. They stopped the boat and watched the whale and his friends play. They all laughed and clapped. The whale was very happy that he could help his friends. They played all day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little dog and a big cat were very hungry. They wanted to find a meal to eat. The dog had an idea. He said, "Let's act like we are nice pets, and maybe someone will give us food." The cat agreed, and they went to a house with a kind lady. The dog wagged his tail, and the cat purred. The lady smiled and said, "You two are so cute! I will give you a meal." The lady gave them a big meal with yummy food. The dog and cat were very happy. Their plan was successful. They ate all the food and thanked the kind lady. From that day on, the dog and cat always acted like nice pets. They knew that when they were kind, they would have successful days and find good meals to eat. And they lived happily ever after.

Anna likes to watch videos on her tablet. She has many videos of animals, songs and stories. But sometimes she wants to watch something new. She decides to look for more videos on the internet. She taps on the screen and sees many pictures. Some pictures are of videos she knows, and some are of videos she does not know. She sees a picture of a big red truck. She likes trucks. She taps on the picture. But the video is not about a truck. It is about a scary monster. The monster has big teeth and loud roars. It jumps out of the hidden truck and chases people. Anna is scared. She does not like the video. She wants to stop it. She tries to tap on the screen again, but nothing happens. The video keeps playing. The monster comes closer and closer. Anna cries. She wants her mom. Mom hears Anna cry. She runs to her room. She sees the tablet and the video. She is angry. She takes the tablet away from Anna. She hugs her and says, "It's okay, Anna. It's just a video. It's not real. But you should not watch videos like that. They are not for little kids. They are scary and bad. You should only watch videos that I choose for you. Do you understand?" Anna nods. She is sorry. She says, "I'm sorry, Mom. I just wanted to watch a truck video. I did not know it was a monster video. I won't do it again. Can I watch a nice video now?" Mom smiles. She says, "Yes, you can. But only one. Then it's time for bed. Come on, let's find a nice video together. How about a video of a cute puppy?" Anna smiles too. She likes puppies. She says, "Yes, please. I love puppies. Thank you, Mom." Mom and Anna cuddle on the bed and watch a video of a cute puppy. Anna feels happy and safe. She decides that she likes nice videos better than scary videos.

One day, a friendly cat named Tom went on a walk. He saw a big case in the park. Tom was very curious about the case. He went close to it and tried to open it. But the case was locked. Tom was sad because he wanted to know what was inside. Just then, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tom and waved at him. "Hi, Tom! What are you doing?" she asked. Tom said, "I found a big case, but I can't open it." Sue looked at the case and said, "I can help you open it." Sue and Tom tried to open the case together. They pushed and pulled, but the case did not open. Then, something unexpected happened. The case started to shake and make a loud noise. Sue and Tom were very scared. Suddenly, the case opened by itself. Inside the case, there was a friendly dog named Max. Max jumped out and said, "Surprise! I was hiding in the case. Thank you for finding me!" Sue, Tom, and Max laughed and played together in the park. They were all happy to have a new friend.
