Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy gun that he loved very much. He thought it was the best toy in the whole world. He played with it every day and took it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play. Sam saw the toy gun and thought it was stupid. He told Tim that he didn't like the gun and wanted to play with something else. Tim felt sad because he valued his toy gun a lot. But then, Tim had an idea. He showed Sam how much fun they could have playing with the toy gun. They pretended to be heroes, saving the day. Soon, Sam started to like the toy gun too. In the end, both boys had a great time playing together, and they learned that it's okay to like different things.

One day, a blue bird named Sky was flying in the sky. She loved to relax and listen to the sounds of the wind. Sky had a best friend named Bee, who was a little yellow bee. They liked to play and talk together. "Sky, let's play a game!" said Bee. "We can listen to the sounds and guess what they are!" Sky liked this idea, so they started to play. They heard the sound of a dog barking and a cat meowing. They were having lots of fun. Then, they heard a strange sound. "What is that?" asked Sky. Bee did not know. They looked around and saw a big box. The strange sound was coming from the box. They went closer and opened the box. Inside, they found a little green frog who was making the strange sound. The frog was lost and needed help to find his way home. Sky and Bee decided to help their new friend, and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside in the wind. One day, the wind was very strong, and it blew her favorite ball far, far away. Lily was sad, but she knew she had to find her ball. Lily asked her friend, the bird, to help her. The bird said, "I will send a message to the wind to bring your ball back." Lily was happy and thanked the bird. She knew her friend would help her. The bird talked to the wind and asked it to bring back the ball. The wind heard the bird and blew the ball back to Lily. Lily saw her ball and was very happy. She thanked the bird and the wind. Lily knew that her friends would always help her when she needed it.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk in the woods. She wanted to discover new things. While walking, she saw a big, silly rabbit. The rabbit was jumping up and down and making funny faces. "Hello, silly rabbit!" Lucy said. "What are you doing?" "I am trying to find my saw," the rabbit replied. "I need it to cut some wood for my home." Lucy and the rabbit looked for the saw together. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and in the bushes. Finally, they found the saw under a big pile of leaves. "Here it is!" Lucy said, picking up the saw. "Now you can cut your wood." But just as the rabbit was about to take the saw, a tiny mouse appeared. "Wait!" the mouse said. "That's not a saw, it's my magic wand!" Lucy and the rabbit were very surprised. They never expected to find a magic wand in the woods. The mouse waved the wand, and the wood for the rabbit's home was cut and ready to use. The three new friends laughed and played together, happy with their silly discovery.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big jar in the kitchen. He wanted to see what was inside. He tried to open the jar, but it was too tight. Tim looked serious as he tried to twist the lid. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" He told her that he wanted to open the jar. His mom smiled and said, "Let me help you." She took the jar and opened it for Tim. Inside the jar, there were many colorful candies. Tim was so happy! He took a candy and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They both smiled and shared the candies together.
