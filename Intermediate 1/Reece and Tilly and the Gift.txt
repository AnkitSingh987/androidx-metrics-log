Category: Intermediate
Title: Reece and Tilly and the Gift

Once upon a time there were two friends, Reece and Tilly. Reece had a plan, she wanted to make something special for Tilly. Reece gathered some fur from the animals in the forest, she thought it would be cosy and warm. Reece worked for days and when she was done, she had made a lovely blanket. She wrapped it around her friend Tilly and told her she had made it especially for her. Tilly was very happy and she said to Reece, "thank you for this sweet gift." Reece smiled, she was pleased it had turned out so well. The moral of the story is: it pays to be kind and generous to your friends. Reece gave Tilly a special gift, and it made them both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fine cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell stories to his friends. One day, Tom had a fun idea for a new story. In this story, Tom and his friends were trying to number the stars in the sky. They would count one, two, three, and so on. It was a big job, but they were having lots of fun. As they counted the stars, they found out that there were too many to number. They all laughed and decided to just enjoy the pretty sky. From that day on, Tom and his friends loved to look at the stars and remember their fine story.

One day, a cool cat named Sam went to a food place. He sat down and looked around. Sam saw the person who brings food to people. He said, "Hello, can I see the menu, please?" The person said, "Yes, here you go." Sam looked at the menu. He saw many yummy foods. He thought about what he wanted to eat. Sam said, "I want a big fish, please." The person said, "Okay, I will bring it to you." Sam waited for his fish. He was very happy when it came. He ate the big fish and felt full. Sam said, "Thank you for the food." The person said, "You're welcome. Have a nice day!" Sam went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to chew on things. One day, Buddy found a big, red ball. He was very happy and started to chew on the ball. Buddy was playing with the ball near the door. The door was big and blue. Buddy wanted to go outside and play. He pushed the door with his nose, and it opened. Buddy took the ball and went outside to play. Buddy played with the ball all day long. He chewed on it and chased it around the yard. The sun was shining, and Buddy was very happy. When it was time to go inside, Buddy brought the ball back to the door. He went inside, and his family gave him a big hug. Buddy knew he had a great day.

Jen and Sam were friends who liked to play soccer. They were very enthusiastic and always ran after the ball. One day, they went to the park with their moms and found a big field to play on. "Pass me the ball, Jen!" Sam shouted. He was open and ready to score a goal. "OK, Sam!" Jen said. She kicked the ball hard towards Sam. But she forgot to look where she was kicking. The ball flew over Sam's head and hit a man who was walking his dog. "Ow!" the man yelled. He dropped the leash and the dog ran away. The man was angry and chased after the dog. "Uh-oh," Jen said. She felt scared and sorry. She did not mean to hurt the man or the dog. "Jen, what did you do?" Sam asked. He was also scared and sorry. He liked dogs and did not want the dog to get lost. They ran to their moms and told them what happened. Their moms were not happy. They said they had to go and apologize to the man and help him find his dog. But it was too late. The man and the dog were gone. They had left the park and no one knew where they went. Jen and Sam felt very bad. They had ruined the man's and the dog's day. They had also ruined their own day. They could not play soccer anymore. They had to go home and think about what they did. They learned a lesson. They learned to be careful and not forget to look where they kick the ball. They also learned to say sorry when they make a mistake. They hoped the man and the dog were OK. They hoped they would see them again and make friends. But they never did.
