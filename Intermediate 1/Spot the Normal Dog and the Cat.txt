Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Normal Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a normal dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball in the yard. One day, Spot saw a big hole in the ground. It was a tunnel! He thought it was fun to go inside. As Spot went into the tunnel, he saw a cat. The cat was not nice. The cat tried to steal Spot's ball! Spot did not like this. He wanted to get his ball back. Spot chased the cat through the tunnel. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a big, friendly dog! The dog gave Spot his ball back. They both came out of the tunnel and played together. From that day on, Spot and the big dog were best friends.

Tom and Sam were playing with their toy soldiers. They had a big battle on the floor. Tom had more soldiers than Sam, and he pushed them forward. Sam's soldiers were in trouble. They had to run away or fight. "Stop, Tom! You are too strong!" Sam said. "You have to be fair!" "No, I don't!" Tom said. "This is war! You have to surrender or lose!" Sam did not want to surrender. He wanted to win. He looked around and saw a line of books on the shelf. He had an idea. He grabbed a book and threw it at Tom's soldiers. The book hit them and made them fall. "Hey, that's cheating!" Tom said. "You can't use books!" "Yes, I can!" Sam said. "You can use anything in war!" Tom was angry. He picked up another book and threw it back at Sam's soldiers. The book missed and hit the wall. The wall made a loud noise. The noise made their mom come. "What are you doing?" Mom said. She looked at the mess on the floor. She saw the books and the soldiers. She was troubled. "You are not playing nice! You are making a lot of noise and throwing things!" "Sorry, Mom," Tom and Sam said. They looked at each other. They felt bad. They did not want to make Mom troubled. "Pick up your toys and put them away," Mom said. "And no more fighting or throwing books!" "OK, Mom," Tom and Sam said. They did what Mom said. They picked up their toys and put them away. They also put the books back on the shelf. They said sorry to each other. They decided to play something else. Something that did not make Mom troubled. Something that did not make them fight. Something that was fun.

Once upon a time there was a princess who loved to polish things. She was trying to polish a shiny stone one day when she heard a hiss. She turned and saw a big, icy snake! It was the Queen snake who lived nearby. The princess was very scared, so she ran away quickly. When the Queen snake saw this, she became angry and started to follow the princess. The princess kept running, but the Queen snake was getting closer and closer. She had to do something to protect herself! Just then, she remembered the stone she was polishing - it was very shiny! She grabbed it and held it up to the Queen snake, who stopped in her tracks. The Queen snake hissed one last time before slithering away. The princess was relieved and safe. She went back to polishing the stone, and when she was done it was even shinier than before! She carried it with her wherever she went.

Once upon a time, there was a kind little girl named Lily. She had a toy triangle that she loved to play with. The triangle was her best friend, and they would play together all day long. One day, Lily saw a sad boy named Tom. He was crying because he lost his toy. Lily wanted to help Tom feel better. She told Tom, "Don't be sad. I will encourage you to find your toy." Tom liked that idea, and they both started looking for his toy. Lily and Tom looked everywhere. They looked under the bed, behind the door, and in the toy box. Finally, they found Tom's toy behind a big chair. Tom was so happy, and he thanked Lily for her help. From that day on, Lily, Tom, and the triangle were the best of friends. They played together and encouraged each other to be kind and helpful to others.
