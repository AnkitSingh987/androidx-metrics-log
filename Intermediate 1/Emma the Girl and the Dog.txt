Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Emma went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a muffin for her dad. He had to work all day and was very tired. Emma thought a muffin would make him happy. At the store, Emma saw a big dog. The dog looked at her and barked, "Woof! Woof!" Emma was frightened. She ran to her mom and said, "Mommy, I'm scared of the big dog!" Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Emma. The dog won't hurt you. Let's get the muffin and go home." Emma and her mom bought the muffin and went home. When they gave the muffin to her dad, he smiled and said, "Thank you, Emma! This muffin will help me feel better after work." Emma was happy that she could help her dad, even though she was scared of the big dog.

Once upon a time, there was a turkey named Tom. Tom was a careless turkey. He liked to play and have fun all day long. One day, Tom saw a big ball in the yard. He wanted to play with it. Tom ran to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball went up, up, up into the air. Tom laughed and clapped his wings. He felt so happy to play with the ball. He kicked it again and again. But Tom was careless. He didn't see the fence in front of him. He kicked the ball too hard and it went over the fence. Tom felt sad because he lost his ball. He learned to be more careful when he played.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a little boy named Tim found a big jar of sugar. The jar was thick and heavy. Tim loved sugar, so he was very happy. He wanted to put sugar on his toast for breakfast. In the morning, Tim took the thick jar of sugar and put some on his toast. He ate it and it was so sweet. Then, he saw a little red car on the table. The car could zoom very fast. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the car. He pushed the car and it went zoom across the room. The car hit the thick jar of sugar and the jar fell down. Sugar went everywhere! Tim was sad, but he knew he had to clean up. He got a broom and swept the sugar away. Tim learned to be careful when playing with his zooming car.

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary truck. It was very loud and fast. All the little animals in the forest were afraid of the truck. They did not want to play near the road where the truck went by. One day, a little rabbit wanted to catch a butterfly. The butterfly flew close to the road. The rabbit forgot about the scary truck and chased the butterfly. The rabbit was so close to the road, and the other animals were worried. Just then, a wise old owl saw the rabbit and knew he had to help. The owl quickly swooped down and caught the rabbit, pulling him away from the road just as the scary truck zoomed by. The little rabbit was safe, and all the animals were happy. The wise old owl taught the rabbit and the other animals to always be careful and stay away from the road. The moral of the story is to always be aware of the dangers around you and to listen to the wise advice of others.

Anna and Ben are best friends. They like to play together every day. They have a lot of fun with their toys, books and games. They are very dependable. They always help each other and share their things. One day, Anna has a big wish. She wants to see the zoo. She has never seen the zoo before. She wants to see the lions, the monkeys, the elephants and the giraffes. She tells Ben about her wish. "Ben, I have a big wish. I want to see the zoo. Can we go to the zoo?" Anna asks. Ben thinks for a moment. He has seen the zoo before, but he likes it too. He wants to make Anna happy. He has an idea. "Anna, I have a plan. We can call my mom and ask her to take us to the zoo. She is very nice and she likes animals too. Maybe she will say yes. Do you want to try?" Ben says. Anna nods. She is very excited. She hopes Ben's mom will say yes. She thinks Ben is very smart and kind. She is glad he is her best friend. They go to the phone and call Ben's mom. They tell her about their wish. They ask her very politely. They wait for her answer. "Hello, Anna and Ben. I'm happy to hear from you. You have a big wish, huh? You want to see the zoo? Well, I think that's a wonderful wish. And I have some good news. I have a day off today. How about we go to the zoo together? I can pick you up in an hour. Does that sound good?" Ben's mom says. Anna and Ben cheer. They are so happy. Their wish is coming true. They thank Ben's mom and hang up the phone. They run to get ready. They pack their snacks, their hats, their water bottles and their cameras. They can't wait to see the zoo. They hug each other and smile. They are very lucky. They have a dependable friend and a nice mom. They have a big wish and a big adventure. They are ready to go.
