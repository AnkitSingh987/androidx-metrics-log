Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mist

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was restless. He liked to play and run all day. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Tim saw a strange mist. He was curious. Tim decided to discover what was behind the mist. He walked closer and closer. As he walked through the mist, Tim found a big, shiny ball. He was so happy! Tim played with the ball and shared it with his friends. They had so much fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat and taste new foods. One day, her mom gave her a small, round thing called an olive. Amy thought it looked very funny. Amy's mom said, "This olive tastes great. You will see!" Amy was not sure, but she wanted to try it. She took a tiny bite of the olive. At first, she did not like it. But then, she tasted it again and thought it was good. The next day, Amy went to a party with her mom. There was a big table with lots of food. Amy saw the olives and remembered how great they tasted. She took a big bite and smiled. Now, olives were her favorite treat.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Bob. Bob was 3 years old and really liked to run. He would run around everywhere, and even run with his friends. One day, Bob's mom told him about success. Bob asked her, "What is sucess?" His mom smiled at him and said, "Success is when you try hard and do well. It will make you feel proud and happy." Bob wanted to feel proud and happy, so he ran harder and faster. He ran faster than ever before and even his friends couldn't keep up. Soon, something unknown happened to Bob. He found himself winning a race! Everyone else was in shock, and Bob was filled with joy. His mom was proud of him and smiled right back at him. Bob had become a success.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little girl named Lucy. She liked to walk and look at the sky. One day, she went for a walk with her dog, Max. They saw a big gate. The gate was closed, but Lucy wanted to pass it. Lucy and Max pushed the gate. It opened slowly. They passed the gate and found a pretty garden. There were flowers, trees, and a small pond. Lucy and Max were happy to play in the garden. After a while, it was time to go home. Lucy and Max passed the gate again. They walked back to their house. Lucy felt thoughtful about the garden. She knew she would come back to play another day.

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. They saw a big bin. The bin was full of toys. Tom said, "Look, Sam! Let's play with the toys!" Sam said, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the toys all day. They had a lot of fun. Tom found a toy car that was just the right size. It was fit for him. Sam found a toy truck that was fit for him too. They played with the cars and trucks, going vroom vroom! At the end of the day, Tom said, "It's time to go home, Sam." Sam said, "Okay, let's leave the toys in the bin for other kids to play with." They put the toys back in the bin and went home. They were happy they had a fun day at the park.

Lily liked to learn new things. She had a tutor who came to her house every week. The tutor was nice and helped Lily with letters, numbers and colors. Lily had fun with the tutor and always looked forward to seeing her. One day, the tutor did not come. Lily was sad and wondered why. Her mom said the tutor was sick and could not visit. Lily hoped the tutor would get better soon. She wanted to show her what she learned. Lily decided to make a card for the tutor. She took some paper, crayons and stickers. She drew a picture of herself and the tutor. She wrote "I miss you" and "Get well soon". She put a sticker of a flower on the card. She asked her mom to help her mail the card. Her mom said they had to go to the post office. Lily put on her shoes and coat. She grabbed the card and followed her mom. On the way to the post office, they passed by a garbage truck. It was big and loud and smelly. Lily wrinkled her nose and covered her mouth. She did not like the smell. She remembered that the tutor did not like smelly things either. She hoped the card would not get smelly in the mail. She held it tight and away from the garbage truck. She wanted the tutor to be happy when she got the card. She wanted the tutor to come back soon.
