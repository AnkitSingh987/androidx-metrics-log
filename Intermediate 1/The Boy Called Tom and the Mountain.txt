Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Tom and the Mountain

Once upon a time, there was a young boy called Tom. He was very enthusiastic and keen to try new things. One day, Tom decided to climb a tall mountain. At first, it was easy going. But then Tom started to feel tired, and he ran out of oxygen. He could no longer go on and he felt sad. But then, from out of nowhere, a friendly eagle appeared. It flew up to the top of the mountain to get some fresh air for Tom, and then flew back down to help him. Tom felt very happy and he thanked the eagle. Now he had enough oxygen to finish the climb. Tom was so enthusiastic that he kept going and eventually, he reached the top of the mountain! He had done it! The eagle was so proud of Tom and now they were good friends. Tom was so proud of himself too, and he knew he had climbed a very big mountain!

Once upon a time, there was a club where animals met to play. The club had a big room for games and fun. All the animals loved to go there. One day, a small, nervous mouse wanted to join the club. He was scared because he was very small. But he went to the club anyway. When he got there, he saw that the club was very messy. The animals had left their toys all over the floor. The small, nervous mouse wanted to help. So, he started to clean the club. He picked up the toys and put them away. Soon, the club was clean and neat again. The animals saw how hard the mouse had worked and they were happy. They said, "Thank you, little mouse, for making our club clean again!" The small, nervous mouse was not scared anymore. He made many new friends at the club and they all played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Lily. She liked to read books, and her favorite thing to read was a novel. Lily had a big heart and always wanted to help others. One day, Lily saw a cat stuck in a tree. She wanted to help, so she climbed the tree. As she got closer, the cat said, "Stop!" Lily was surprised, as she did not know cats could talk. The cat told her that it had a magical gift for her. The cat gave Lily a magic stone and said it would help her. But when Lily held the stone, she turned into a cat too! Now, Lily could not read her novel anymore. She was very sad and wished she had never tried to help the cat.

Tom liked to go to the library with his mom. He liked to look at books with pictures of animals and cars and trucks. He liked to sit on the big chair and listen to stories. He did not like to be quiet or to share. One day, Tom saw a book he wanted on a high shelf. He asked his mom to get it for him, but she said no. She said the book was for big kids, not for little kids. She said he could pick another book from the low shelf. Tom was angry. He wanted the book on the high shelf. He had a plan. He waited until his mom was busy talking to a lady. He saw a bald man with a ladder. The bald man was putting books on the high shelf. Tom ran to the ladder and climbed up. He reached for the book he wanted. The bald man saw Tom on the ladder. He was surprised and scared. He shouted at Tom to get down. He tried to grab Tom, but Tom kicked him. The ladder wobbled and fell. Tom and the bald man and the books crashed to the floor. Tom was hurt. He had a big bump on his head and a cut on his arm. He cried and cried. The bald man was hurt too. He had a bruise on his face and a broken glasses. He was angry and sad. He said Tom was a bad boy. He said Tom lied to his mom and did not listen and was not nice. Tom's mom heard the noise and ran to see what happened. She saw Tom and the bald man and the books on the floor. She was shocked and worried. She hugged Tom and said sorry to the bald man. She said Tom was in trouble. She said he had to go home and no more library for a long time. Tom was sorry. He said he was sorry to his mom and to the bald man and to the books. He said he did not mean to be bad. He said he just wanted the book on the high shelf. He said he would never lie or climb or kick again. But it was too late. Tom had to go home and no more library for a long time. He had a bad ending.
