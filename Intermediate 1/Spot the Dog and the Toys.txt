Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Toys

One day, a little dog named Spot was very sad. He did not want to play with his toys. He just sat under a big tree and looked at the sky. His friend, a funny cat named Mittens, saw him and wanted to help. Mittens had a good idea. She started to dance and make funny faces to make Spot laugh. Spot looked at Mittens and started to smile. Soon, he was laughing too! They both laughed and played together under the tree. After a while, Spot was not sad anymore. He was very happy to have a friend like Mittens. They played together all day long. Spot and Mittens knew that they could always make each other laugh when they were sad.

Once upon a time, in a small home, there lived a gentle cat and a playful dog. They were good friends and liked to play together. But sometimes, they would fight over toys. One day, their owner decided to separate them for a little while. The cat stayed inside the home and the dog played outside in the yard. They both missed each other and felt sad. After a short time, the owner let them play together again. The cat and the dog promised to be more gentle with each other. They played happily in their home and shared their toys.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max was a fine dog. He had a secret. He could talk! One day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was sad. Max went to her and said, "Hi! I can talk. I am your new friend." Lily was very happy. She said, "Wow! A talking dog! You are my best friend, Max." They played all day. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. Then, Max gave Lily a big kiss on her cheek. Lily giggled and hugged him tight. But, one day, a big wind came. It blew Max far away. Lily was sad again. She missed her friend. Then, a big surprise! The wind blew Max back to Lily. They were so happy to see each other. Max said, "I am back, Lily. I missed you." Lily hugged Max and said, "I missed you too." They played all day, and Max's secret was safe with Lily.

One day, a boy named Tim found a weak eraser. He picked it up and saw that it was small and soft. Tim wanted to use the eraser to clean up the marks on his paper. Tim went to his friend, Sue. "Look," he said, "I found a weak eraser. Can you help me use it to clean the marks on my paper?" Sue looked at the eraser and said, "Yes, I can help you." So, Tim and Sue worked together to clean the marks on Tim's paper. They rubbed the weak eraser on the marks and saw them go away. They were happy that the eraser worked. Tim said, "Thank you, Sue, for helping me." Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad we could clean the marks together."

One day, a little girl named Lily was very happy. She was going to a party with her friends. She put on her pretty dress and her shiny shoes. Lily's mom said, "Be careful, don't slip!" At the party, Lily and her friends played games, ate cake, and danced. They were all having so much fun. Lily's friend, Tim, said, "This is the best party ever!" But then, Lily slipped on a toy car. She fell down and started to cry. Her dress was dirty and her shoes were scuffed. The party was not fun anymore. Lily went home with her mom, feeling sad.

One day, a small cat named Tim and a big dog named Sam were playing in the park. They were very good friends. They liked to run and jump together. Suddenly, a hurt bird fell from a tree. Tim and Sam ran to help the bird. "Don't worry, little bird, we will help you," said Sam. The bird was sad and could not fly. Tim and Sam had an idea. They entered a small house where they found a nice lady. The lady saw the hurt bird and said, "I will help the bird get better." The lady put a band on the bird's wing. The bird felt better and thanked Tim, Sam, and the nice lady. In the end, the bird could fly again. Tim, Sam, and the bird played together in the park. They became the best of friends and had many more fun days together.
