Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Car

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the snow. One day, they made a big snowman. They gave him a hat, a scarf, and two mittens. They smiled at their snowman. He looked happy. But then, a mean boy came. He saw the snowman and wanted his mittens. He ran to the snowman and took his mittens. He laughed and ran away. Lily and Tom were sad. They cried. Their snowman was cold. They ran after the mean boy. They wanted their mittens back. But the mean boy was fast. He ran to the street. He did not look. A big car was coming. The car hit the mean boy. He fell down. He was hurt. He dropped the mittens. Lily and Tom saw what happened. They were scared. They ran to the mean boy. They helped him. They called for help. They gave him their mittens. They said sorry. The mean boy was sorry too. He said thank you. He was grateful. But it was too late. The mean boy was very hurt. He did not get up. He did not breathe. He died. Lily and Tom were sad. They cried. They wished they had not made a snowman. They wished they had not given him mittens. They wished the mean boy had not taken them. They wished the car had not hit him. The moral of the story is: Do not take what is not yours. It can cause trouble and pain. Be kind and share. It can make you happy and safe.

Once upon a time, in a big pink house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to help her mom in the garden. One sunny day, they were planting flowers when Lily saw a rainbow in the sky. "Look, Mom! A rainbow!" Lily said, pointing at the sky. Her mom looked up and smiled. "Yes, it's so pretty!" Lily wanted to help the rainbow. She thought if she planted more flowers, the rainbow would be happier. So, she and her mom planted red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple flowers. As they finished, the rainbow seemed to grow brighter. Lily felt happy that she could help the rainbow. She knew that every time she saw a rainbow, she would remember how she helped make the world more beautiful.

One day, a boy named Tim found a picture on the floor. The picture was of a big, happy dog. Tim liked the dog in the picture, but the picture was old and poor. It had many tears and dirt on it. Tim wanted to make the picture look better. Tim went to his mom and showed her the picture. He said, "Mom, look at this poor picture. Can we fix it?" His mom looked at the picture and said, "Yes, Tim. We can try to make it better." They worked together to fix the picture. They used tape and glue to put the pieces back together. When they were done, the picture looked better. Tim was very happy. He put the picture on his wall. Later, Tim's little sister, Sue, came into his room. She saw the picture and yelled, "Wow, what a big dog!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sue. It's a big, happy dog. And it's on my wall now." From that day on, Tim and Sue loved looking at the big, happy dog on the wall. They were glad they could fix the poor picture and make it better.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He loved to swim all day long. One day, he saw a big puzzle on the sea floor. He was very excited and wanted to play with it. Fin called his friend, Star, the starfish. "Star, come here! Let's play with this puzzle!" Star was impatient, but she swam over to help Fin. They tried their best to put the puzzle together. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. The puzzle pieces started to move by themselves! Fin and Star were so surprised. The puzzle turned into a big, friendly whale named Wally. Wally thanked Fin and Star for putting him back together. Now they all swim and play in the sea together every day.
