Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Fair Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a fair girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy hammer. One day, Lily went to the park to play. While she was playing, a big dog came and took her hammer. Lily was sad and asked the dog to give it back. The dog did not listen and ran away. Lily ran after the dog to get her hammer back. As Lily chased the dog, she saw a big rainbow in the sky. The dog stopped running and looked at the rainbow too. Suddenly, the rainbow turned into a slide. The dog went up the slide and gave the hammer back to Lily. They both played on the rainbow slide and had lots of fun. The dog was not bad after all, he just wanted to delay their playtime and share the beautiful rainbow surprise with Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a loyal dog named Max. They played together every day. One sunny day, Lucy found a peanut on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to Max. "Look, Max! I found a peanut!" she said. Max wagged his tail and barked happily. Lucy wanted to eat the peanut, but she knew it was not good to eat things found on the ground. She decided to give it to the birds instead. As she threw the peanut, Max tried to catch it, but Lucy said, "No, Max! It's for the birds!" Max stopped and watched the birds eat the peanut. Lucy gave Max a big kiss on his nose. "Good boy, Max! You are so loyal and kind," she said. Max wagged his tail and licked her face. From that day on, Lucy and Max always helped the birds by giving them food. They learned that it was good to share and care for others.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot was famous because he could do many tricks. He had a red buckle on his collar. Everyone loved Spot and wanted a dog just like him. One day, Spot's owner, a little girl named Sue, took him to the park. She wanted to show her friends all the tricks Spot could do. But when they got to the park, Spot saw a squirrel and ran away. Sue was sad and scared that she lost her famous dog. Sue and her friends looked everywhere for Spot. They called his name and looked behind trees. Then, Sue remembered the red buckle on Spot's collar. She told her friends to look for the red buckle. Soon, they found Spot by a big tree, trying to get the squirrel. Sue was happy to have her famous dog back and knew the red buckle would always help her find him.

Once upon a time, there was a funny frog named Fred. Fred loved to jump and play all day. He had many friends in the forest. One day, Fred and his friends decided to have a jumping race. They wanted to see who could jump the farthest. The race started when the sun began to set. The sunset was very pretty and made the sky look like a big orange ball. Fred jumped and jumped as hard as he could. His friends jumped too, but Fred jumped the farthest. They all laughed and clapped for Fred. The funny frog won the race, and they all played together until the sun went to sleep. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Pat. Pat liked to pass a ball to his friends. He was a very patient dog. Pat had a friend named Ben. Ben was a small cat. Ben was envious of Pat because he could not pass the ball well. One day, Pat and Ben went to the park to play. They saw a big tree with a ball stuck in it. Pat tried to get the ball down, but he was not tall enough. Ben felt sad for Pat. They both wanted to play with the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and saw Ben and Pat looking sad. The bird flew up to the tree and pushed the ball out. The ball fell down, and Pat and Ben were so happy. They thanked the bird and played together all day. In the end, they learned that being envious was not important, but helping each other was.
