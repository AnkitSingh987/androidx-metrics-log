Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a healthy dog who loved to run and play. One day, Max found a message in a bottle. The message said, "Help me find my lost toy." Max wanted to help, so he tried very hard to look for the toy. He looked in the park, under the trees, and near the flowers. But he could not find the toy anywhere. As Max kept trying, it started to get dark outside. Max got lost and could not find his way home. He was scared and sad. In the end, Max never found the toy and he never found his way back home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very happy because he loved playing on the swings and slides. While he was playing, he saw a small, persistent ant. The ant was trying to find its way back to its ant hill. Tim wanted to help the ant, but he didn't know how. He tried to pick up the ant, but it tickled his finger and he dropped it. Tim felt embarrass, but he did not give up. He thought of a new way to help the ant. Tim found a small stick and gently pushed the ant onto it. He carried the ant on the stick and put it near the ant hill. The ant was very happy and went into its home. Tim felt proud that he was able to help the ant find its way. His mom saw what he did and gave him a big hug. Tim learned that being persistent and trying new ways can help solve problems.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that could go zoom. He loved to play with the car all day. One day, Tim heard a ring from the door. He ran to see who it was. It was his friend, Lily, who also had a toy car. Tim and Lily decided to have a race with their toy cars. They wanted to see whose car could go zoom the fastest. They were very patient as they lined up their cars at the starting line. Then, they said "Go!" and their cars went zoom down the street. But, Tim's car went too fast and hit a big rock. The car broke into pieces. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Lily saw how sad Tim was and decided to share her toy car with him. They played together and had so much fun. In the end, Tim learned that it's okay to lose and friends are more important than winning.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play outside in the sun. One day, Fluffy saw a big cup of coffee on the table. Fluffy was curious and wanted to smell the coffee. But Fluffy was too shy to go near the cup. A little bird saw Fluffy looking at the coffee. The bird flew down and said, "Hi Fluffy! Do you want to smell the coffee? I can help you!" Fluffy looked at the bird and nodded. The bird went to the cup and pushed it closer to Fluffy. Fluffy smelled the coffee and made a funny face. The coffee was too hot! Fluffy jumped back and accidentally knocked the cup over. The coffee spilled everywhere! Fluffy was scared and wanted to escape. The bird said, "Don't worry, Fluffy! We can clean it up together." Fluffy and the bird worked together to clean up the spilled coffee. They became good friends and played together every day. Fluffy was not shy anymore, and they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some coffee. Sue saw a wet dog outside the store. She felt sad for the dog. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, can we help the wet dog? It makes me want to weep." Her mom said, "Yes, we can help the dog after we buy the coffee." When they came out of the store, the wet dog was gone. Sue was very sad and started to weep. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog came back with a big, dry towel! The dog was not wet anymore. Sue was so happy! She said to the dog, "You are so smart! I am glad you are not wet now." The dog wagged its tail and Sue knew they would be friends forever.
