Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a frightened boy who was scared of many things. One day, while he was playing outside, he saw a big cat. The cat looked at him and said, "Don't be scared, I won't hurt you." Tim was surprised and asked, "How can I not be frightened?" The cat replied, "You need to find your power. You have the power to be brave and strong." Tim thought about it and decided to find his power. The next day, Tim rushed to the park to play with his friends. When he got there, a big dog came running towards him. Tim remembered what the cat said and found his power. He stood tall and said, "I am not frightened!" The dog stopped and became his friend. From that day on, Tim was never frightened again, and he always remembered the power he had inside him.

One day, a nice boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big ball and wanted to kick it. He kicked the ball very high in the sky. Tim felt a lot of wonder when he saw how high the ball went. He smiled and clapped his hands. Then, a girl named Sue came to play with Tim. She said, "Hi, Tim! Can I kick the ball too?" Tim said, "Yes, Sue! Let's kick the ball together." They took turns kicking the ball and watched it fly high in the sky. They both felt wonder and laughed as they played. After they played, Tim and Sue sat down to rest. They talked about the fun they had kicking the ball. They both agreed that it was a nice day to play outside. They made a new friend in each other and promised to play again soon.

Once upon a time, two friends, a cat and a dog, lived in a small house. They were very lucky because they had a big yard to play in. One day, they found a big, red ball in the yard. They both wanted to play with the ball, but they did not want to share. The cat said, "I saw the ball first, it's mine!" The dog said, "No, I want to play with it." They began to quarrel. They were so loud that even the birds in the trees flew away. Their little toe hurt because they were so mad. As they quarreled, a big wind came and took the red ball away. The cat and the dog stopped and watched as the ball flew far away. They were both very sad. They lost their lucky ball because they did not want to share. Now, they had no ball to play with and their toe still hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Tim. One day, Tim was playing in the park when he suddenly heard a distant sound coming from far away. It sounded like music, but Tim couldn’t see where it was coming from. Tim looked around and saw a big, shiny radio sitting on a tree branch. He was very curious and went over to take a closer look. When Tim was near the radio, he was able to recognize the music that he had heard before. Tim was so excited and he thought the music was wonderful. He wanted to get closer, so he climbed up the tree and touched the radio. Suddenly, the music got louder and Tim was amazed. He couldn’t believe it was coming from the radio! He stayed up in the tree for hours and listened to the music, until he finally got tired and went home. Tim had made a new discovery. From then on, whenever he heard the music he would recognize it as the distant radio he found in the park that day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a talking frog. The frog said, "Hi! I am an honest frog. I can talk!" Tim was very happy to have a new friend. Tim and the frog played all day. They played with a ball and ran in the grass. Tim said, "I like you, frog." The frog said, "I like you too, Tim!" While they were playing, Tim's thumb got hurt. The frog wanted to help. The frog said, "Let me slap your thumb. It will feel better." Tim let the frog slap his thumb. It felt better! They played more and had a fun day.
