Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Park

One sunny day, Tom found a scooter in his yard. It was a little old, but he liked it. He wanted to show his best friend, Sue. Tom got on the scooter and went to Sue's house. When he got there, he shouted, "Sue, come out! Look what I have!" Sue came out and saw the scooter. She said, "Wow, that's so cool! Can I try it?" Tom said, "Yes, of course!" Sue got on the scooter and they both went to the distant park. They saw other kids and played with them. They had so much fun. At the end of the day, Tom and Sue went home, happy and tired.

---

One day, a big gorilla named Bobo went to the park to study. He wanted to learn about the fair. He saw kids playing and having fun. He wanted to play too, but he was too big. A little girl named Lily saw Bobo and said, "Hi, big gorilla! Do you want to play with me?" Bobo was happy and nodded his head. But when they tried to play, the other kids got scared and ran away. Bobo was sad and said, "I just want to play and have fun like the other kids." Lily had an idea. She asked her dad to help make a big swing just for Bobo. Her dad agreed, and they built the swing. The next day, Bobo came back to the park and saw the big swing. He was so happy! He played on the swing and the other kids saw that he was nice. They all played together and had a great day at the fair.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He was a compassionate youth who loved to help others. One day, Tim saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was very scared and Tim wanted to help. Tim looked at the tree and said, "I dare to climb and save the cat." He started to climb the tree very carefully. As he got closer to the cat, he said, "Don't be scared, little cat. I will help you." The cat looked at Tim and felt safe. Tim picked up the cat and climbed down the tree. When they reached the ground, the cat was very happy. Tim felt good because he dared to help the cat. The cat and Tim became best friends and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy who loved to explore. One day, she was walking through the woods and she heard a sound. She stopped and looked around. She saw a fragile owl perched in a tree. Lucy was so excited. She carefully moved closer to the owl. The owl didn't move, so Lucy knew she could meet it. She smiled and said hello. The owl blinked and looked at her. Lucy kept still and the owl flew down to meet her. She reached out to pet the owl and it was so soft. Lucy was so happy to be meeting the owl. She gently pet the fragile owl for a few moments and then the owl flew away. Lucy watched until the owl was out of sight. She was so happy to have met the owl. She smiled and kept exploring through the woods.

One day, a white alligator went to play a game. The game was called "match". He had to find two things that were the same. The white alligator was very good at this game. While playing, the white alligator saw another alligator. This alligator was green. They played the match game together. They found two red balls, two blue cars, and two yellow ducks. They were having so much fun! Then, something unexpected happened. The green alligator found two white alligators! But, how could this be? The first white alligator looked and saw that it was a mirror. They laughed and laughed. The white alligator had matched himself in the mirror. They played the match game all day and became best friends.

Tom and Jen are friends. They like to play in the pool. One day, they have a game. They dive in the water and look for things. They find a ball, a duck, and a ring. They are happy. But then, Jen sees something weird. It is white and soft. It is not a toy. It is a napkin. Jen says, "Look, Tom, a napkin! How did it get here?" Tom swims to Jen. He sees the napkin too. He says, "I don't know. Maybe someone dropped it. It is dirty. We should take it out." They grab the napkin and swim to the edge. They put the napkin on the ground. They look at it. It is wet and soggy. They say, "Yuck!" They go back to the pool. They dive and play some more. They forget about the napkin. They have fun.
