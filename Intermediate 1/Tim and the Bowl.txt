Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bowl

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big purple bowl in his house. He thought it was very pretty. He wanted to show it to his best friend, Sam. So, he picked it up and went outside to find Sam. On his way, Tim saw a big tree with a long piece of paper stuck in its branches. Tim thought it would be fun to tear the paper into small pieces. He put the purple bowl down and started to tear the paper. While he was busy, he heard Sam coming and said, "Sam, look! I found a big purple bowl and I am tearing this paper. Come join me!" Sam came and saw the purple bowl. He loved it too. They both tore the paper into little pieces and laughed. When they were done, they picked up the purple bowl and went to Tim's house. They put all the torn paper pieces in the purple bowl. At Tim's house, Tim's mom saw the purple bowl full of torn paper pieces. She smiled and said, "Oh, you found my special purple bowl! I was going to use it for a surprise. But now, let's use these torn paper pieces and make a fun game together." Tim and Sam were happy and excited. They knew something fun was going to happen with the purple bowl and the torn paper pieces.

Once upon a time there was a boy. He was very hungry and wanted to have a pizza. He looked around but couldn't find one. Then he saw a door. He asked his mom if he could open it. She said yes and gave him the key. He unlocked the door with the key and walked inside. He was so excited to see the pizza. It was big and round with lots of cheese. He was sure it tasted fair. So he cut a slice and started eating it. He ate it all. He was so happy after eating the pizza. He thanked his mom for unlocking the door and also for the fair pizza.

One day, a little bird named Tim was playing in the woods. He was having so much fun that he did not see the night coming. The sun went down, and it got dark. Tim's friend, the wise owl, saw him and warned, "Tim, it is night time. You should go home now." Tim was scared of the dark, but he was grateful for his friend's help. Together, they went to Tim's house. When they got there, Tim's mom was happy to see him. She said, "Thank you, wise owl, for bringing Tim home." The wise owl smiled and flew away. Tim went to bed and slept well, knowing he had a good friend in the woods.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big tent with a sign that said "Display of Wild Objects". They were curious and wanted to see what was inside. They asked their mom if they could go in. Mom said yes, but they had to hold her hand and be careful. She paid for the tickets and they entered the tent. Inside, they saw many strange and colorful objects. Some were furry, some were shiny, some were spiky. They were all different shapes and sizes. Lily and Ben pointed and gasped at each one. "Look, mom, a wild object!" Lily said, pointing at a stuffed lion with a mane and a tail. "That's not a wild object, silly, that's a toy animal!" Ben said, laughing. "No, it's a wild object, it has teeth and claws!" Lily said, sticking out her tongue. Mom smiled and explained that the lion was a toy, but it was made to look like a real animal that lived in a faraway place called Africa. She said that some of the objects were toys, and some were things that people found or made from nature. Lily and Ben learned a lot from the display of wild objects. They saw feathers, shells, rocks, bones, masks, baskets, and more. They asked mom many questions and she tried to answer them. They had so much fun that they didn't want to leave. But mom said it was time to go home and have lunch. They thanked the people who showed them the wild objects and said goodbye. They walked out of the tent, holding mom's hand and talking about what they saw. They agreed that the display of wild objects was the best thing in the park.
