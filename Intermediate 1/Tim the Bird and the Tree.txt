Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was an independent bird who liked to do things on his own. One day, he decided to search for a new nest to live in. As Tim flew around, he saw a big tree. In the tree, he found a nest. But it was not empty! A squirrel lived there, and she said, "Hello! This is my nest. You can't have it." Tim said, "Okay, I will search for another nest." Tim kept searching and found another nest. This time, it was empty! He was very happy and said, "This will be my new home!" Tim loved his new nest and lived there happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very organized hotel. This hotel always had clean rooms and nice people. In the hotel, there lived a kind lady named Sue. Sue loved to feed the birds that lived near the hotel. One day, a little boy named Tim came to stay at the hotel with his family. He saw Sue feeding the birds and asked, "What are you doing, Sue?" Sue smiled and said, "I am feeding the birds. They love to eat the seeds I give them." Tim wanted to help Sue feed the birds too. So, every day, they would go outside together and give seeds to the birds. The birds were very happy and so were Tim and Sue. The hotel was a nice place for everyone, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Sam. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Sam saw a big bicycle near the park. He was very curious about it. Sam wanted to unlock the bicycle and ride it. He asked his friend, a little bird, to help him. The bird flew up and used its beak to unlock the bicycle. Sam was very happy and jumped on the bicycle. But something unexpected happened. The bicycle started to move on its own! It was a magic bicycle! Sam and the little bird laughed and had fun riding the magic bicycle all day long. And from that day on, they had a new, exciting game to play in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a messy room. In the room, there was a door. Behind the door, there was a big race. The race was for all the toys in the room. One day, the toys said, "Let's have a race! The toy that wins will get a clean spot in the room." All the toys got ready to run. They lined up behind the door. The race began, and the toys ran as fast as they could. They ran around the messy room, trying to win. The toy car said, "I will win!" But then, the toy bear said, "No, I will win!" At the end of the race, the toy bear won. The bear got the clean spot in the room. All the other toys clapped and cheered. They all had fun in the messy room.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big red ball in the way. Tim was very frustrated because he could not go around the ball. He wanted to destroy the ball so he could keep walking. Tim said, "I will hit the ball with a stick!" But when he hit the ball, it did not break. Instead, the ball opened up and a funny little man came out. The little man said, "Thank you for helping me get out!" Tim was surprised and happy to make a new friend. The little man showed Tim how to move the ball out of the way. They laughed and played together all day. In the end, Tim was glad he did not destroy the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy cat. This cat loved to sleep all day. The cat had a soft bed and a big glass to drink water from. One day, the cat was very tired. It wanted to sleep, but it could not find its bed. The cat looked and looked for its bed. It looked under the table, behind the door, and in the closet. But the cat could not find its bed. Finally, the cat found its bed outside in the yard. The wind had blown it away! The cat was so happy to find its bed. It lay down and went to sleep. And the cat had sweet dreams, knowing it had its soft bed and big glass to drink from.
