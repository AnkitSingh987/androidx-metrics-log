Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Pilot and the Plane

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. He loved to fly high in the sky. He had a big plane that could go very fast. Tom was a very good pilot and could fly anywhere he wanted. He was very flexible and could change his plans quickly. One day, Tom wanted to create something new. He thought and thought about what he could make. Then, he had a great idea! He would create a new toy for his little friend, Tim. He wanted to make a toy plane that Tim could play with and have fun. Tom worked hard to create the toy plane. He painted it blue and put a big number one on the side. When the toy plane was done, he took it to Tim. Tim was so happy to see the toy plane. He played with it all day and had lots of fun. Tom felt happy that he could create something that made his friend smile.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Tim. Tim was a fast bird who loved to soar high in the sky. He had many friends, and they all liked to play and fly together. One sunny day, Tim saw a lemon tree. The tree had many big, yellow lemons. Tim thought the lemons looked yummy. So, he picked one and shared it with his friends. They all liked the sour taste of the lemon. Tim and his friends played and flew all day. They were happy they found the lemon tree. From that day on, they always stopped by the tree to eat lemons and have fun. And they continued to soar fast and high in the sky, enjoying their days together.

Once upon a time, there was a youth named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy who loved to hop. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play. At the park, Tim met a big frog. The frog liked to hop too. They hopped together and had lots of fun. Tim and the frog were very happy. At the end of the day, Tim said bye to the frog. He went back home, feeling lucky to have a new friend who liked to hop just like him. And they lived happily ever after.

Sara and her mom were going to the park. They walked to the big road and waited for a taxi. Sara liked to see the cars and buses go by. She saw a yellow taxi and waved her hand. "Taxi, taxi!" she shouted. The taxi stopped and the driver opened the door. He had a big smile and a funny hat. "Hello, little girl. Where are you going?" he asked. "To the park, please. We want to play on the swings and slides," Sara said. "Okay, hop in. I will take you there. It is not far," the driver said. Sara and her mom got in the taxi and buckled their seat belts. The driver turned on the radio and sang along. He was very nice and funny. He made Sara and her mom laugh. Soon, they arrived at the park. The driver stopped the taxi and helped them out. "Have fun, little girl. Maybe I will see you again," he said. "Thank you, mister. You are very nice. Bye-bye," Sara said. She gave him a hug and ran to the park. She was very happy. She had a surprise ride in a taxi with a nice driver. She could not wait to tell her dad.

Once there was a girl named Amy. She was three-years-old and loved to explore the outdoors. One day, she went with her mom to climb a high hill. At the top, Amy saw a giant thumb! It had been sitting there for many years. Amy's mom said, "Do you want to try and gain this thumb, Amy?". Amy said, "Yes!" She was very excited and carefully took hold of the thumb. She pulled it hard and finally the thumb came away with a big tug. Amy proudly held up the thumb and said, "I gained it!". Amy's mom laughed, "Yes, you did!. Well done!". Amy smiled and placed the thumb in her pocket and continued exploring the outdoors. She had gained a new friend and was very happy.
