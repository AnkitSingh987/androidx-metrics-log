Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fox Called Dan and the Hole

Once upon a time there was a fox called Dan. He was very curious and liked to measure things. One day, Dan wanted to measure a hole. He carefully looked at it, but it was too dark to tell how big it was. So, he went over to get a ruler. Dan held the ruler above the hole and carefully measured it. He was very pleased to find it was just the right size, not too big and not too small. It was just fine. Dan smiled to himself and thought, "This hole is just perfect!" He decided to leave it just the way it was. He didn't want to make it any bigger or smaller because it was just fine. The end.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy who lived near the sea. One day, Lucy decided to take a walk on the beach. She enjoyed the sound of the waves and the feel of the sand in her feet. As she walked, Lucy noticed a pile of ash near the shore. She knew that ash was a bad thing, so she was feeling a bit fearful. Lucy decided she wanted to find out more about the ash. She bravely stepped forward and asked a passing seagull if he knew what the ash was. The seagull looked at Lucy with surprise. He told her that the ash had come down from the sky the previous night. The powerful wind had dispersed it all over the beach. Lucy was very grateful to the seagull and thanked him. She decided to spend some time studying the ash and learning more about it. She was feeling much more confident, and less fearful. Lucy had learned a lot about the ash, and she felt she had found a new friend in the seagull!

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a drive with his mom. They were going to the park to play. Tim was very patient in the car, waiting to get to the park. He held his favorite cup with a picture of a dog on it. Tim loved his cup very much. At the park, Tim played with his ball, and his mom sat on a bench. Tim put his cup on the bench next to his mom. A big wind came and blew Tim's cup away! Tim was very sad and looked for his cup, but he could not find it. His mom said they would look for it together. Tim and his mom looked all over the park. Then, they saw a dog with Tim's cup in its mouth! The dog was playing with the cup like it was a toy. Tim and his mom went to the dog, and the dog gave the cup back to Tim. Tim was so happy and said thank you to the dog. The dog wagged its tail, and Tim and his mom went back to playing at the park.

One day, a dancer named Lily went to the park. She loved to show her dance moves to her friends. In the park, she saw a big tree with sour fruit. She picked one and took a big bite. "Yuck!" she said, making a funny face. A little boy named Tom saw Lily and wanted to dance too. But he was shy. "Can you show me how to dance?" he asked. Lily smiled and said, "Yes, of course! Just follow me." They danced together, and Tom felt happy. But then, a mean girl named Sue came and laughed at them. "You both look silly!" she said. Lily and Tom felt sad. They didn't know what to do. Then, Lily had an idea. She gave Sue a sour fruit and said, "Try this. It's yummy!" Sue took a bite and made a funny face too. Everyone laughed, even Sue. From that day on, Sue was nice to Lily and Tom. They all danced and played together in the park. The moral of the story is to be kind to others and share what you love.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Lily. Lily had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together. One day, Tom got a new toy car. Lily was jealous because she wanted a toy car too. Lily's mom saw that she was sad. She asked Lily what was wrong. Lily told her mom about the toy car. Her mom said she would recommend a toy car for Lily's birthday. Lily was happy to hear that. The next day, a big dog came to their town. The dog was a threat because it was not nice. Lily and Tom were scared. They told their parents about the dog. The parents talked to the dog's owner. The owner said sorry and took the dog away. Now, Lily and Tom could play safely again.
