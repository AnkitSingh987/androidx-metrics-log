Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Silly Monkey and the Tree

One day, a silly monkey named Bobo lived in a big tree. The tree was in a network of trees, where all the animals were friends. Bobo liked to hang from the branches and play with his friends. Bobo's best friend was a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy liked to fly high in the sky and sing songs. One day, Chirpy said to Bobo, "Let's play a game! You hang from the branch, and I will try to catch you." Bobo laughed and said, "Okay, Chirpy! That sounds fun!" So, Bobo started to hang from the branch, and Chirpy tried to catch him. They played the game for a long time and had lots of fun. All the other animals in the network of trees saw Bobo and Chirpy playing. They thought it looked like fun, so they joined in too. Soon, all the animals were playing together and having a great time. They were all very happy to be friends and live in their network of trees.

Jane and her family went to the zoo. It was a very exciting day and Jane couldn't wait to go. She saw so many interesting animals, like lions, tigers, and zebras. But the one she was most excited to see was the ostrich! When Jane got to the ostrich's pen, she found it standing in the corner. She excitedly waved her hands and said, "Hi ostrich! Come play with me!" But the ostrich did not move, it just stood there without making a sound. Jane's dad then said, "Be careful Jane, it might not be safe for you to play with it." Jane looked up at her dad and said, "Don't worry, I will be safe. I'll just get close enough for the ostrich to see me." So Jane carefully moved in a bit closer. When the ostrich saw her, it started hopping around, trying to get closer to her! Jane smiled and laughed as the ostrich played around her. It was the best day ever.

Tom is sick. He has a bad cough and a sore throat. He does not want to play or eat. He only wants to sleep. Mom is worried. She calls the doctor. The doctor says Tom needs a pill. The pill will make him feel better. The doctor will supply the pill to Mom. Mom goes to the pharmacy. She gets the pill from the doctor. She comes back home. She gives the pill to Tom. Tom does not like the pill. The pill is rough and bitter. He spits it out. Mom is sad. She tries again. She puts the pill in a spoon of honey. She says, "Tom, this is sweet. It will help you swallow the pill. Please try it." Tom trusts Mom. He opens his mouth. He takes the spoon of honey and the pill. He swallows them. He makes a face. He says, "Yuck!" Mom smiles. She hugs Tom. She says, "Good boy. You are brave. The pill will make you better soon. I love you." Tom feels a little better. He loves Mom. He says, "I love you too, Mom." He falls asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dance. Lily danced all day long. Her dance was so impressive that everyone in her town wanted to see it. They clapped and cheered for her. One day, a man came to the town. He could not hear. He spoke with his hands, using signs. Lily saw him and wanted to talk to him. She asked her mom how to use signs. Her mom showed her some signs and Lily practiced them. Lily went to the man and signed, "Hello, I am Lily. I like to dance." The man signed back, "Hello, Lily. I like to watch dance." Lily danced for the man. He watched her and smiled. They became friends. From that day on, Lily learned more signs and taught her new friend how to dance.

Once upon a time, there was a mom and her daughter. They liked to play together. The daughter was very flexible, like a noodle. She could bend and twist in many ways. They would play games and feel very happy. One day, the mom and daughter went to the park. They saw a big tree with strong branches. The daughter wanted to climb the tree. The mom helped her, and she felt safe. The daughter climbed higher and higher because she was so flexible. At the top, the daughter looked down and saw her mom. She felt proud and happy. The mom clapped and smiled. They both felt the love for each other. The daughter climbed down, and they hugged. They walked home together, feeling good about their day.
