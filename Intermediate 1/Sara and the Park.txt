Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Park

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sara had a weird shoe. She loved her weird shoe very much. She wore it every day. One day, she went to the park to play. At the park, Sara saw a big ball. She wanted to catch the ball. She ran and ran to catch it. But her weird shoe fell off her foot. Sara stopped and looked for her shoe. She found the weird shoe under a tree. Sara was very happy. She put her shoe back on and went to catch the ball again. From that day on, Sara and her weird shoe always had fun at the park.

Once upon a time there was a loyal dog. His owner loved him very much, so he was happy to play with his owner every day. His name was Spot. One day, Spot's owner decided to build an igloo. He worked hard at it, but Spot wanted to help him. Spot helped his owner carry the blocks of ice to make the igloo. When the igloo was all done, Spot was very proud. He ran inside, barked and ran around. But then the owner called for Spot to come out. Unfortunately, Spot's loyal heart made him stand still inside the igloo and he wouldn't come out. The owner had to get some help to get Spot out of the igloo. When they opened the igloo door, they found Spot laying there, barely able to move. His loyal heart had been too much for him and he had passed away. Spot's story ended sadly.

Once upon a time, there was a normal cat named Sam. Sam liked to play and search for things. One day, Sam saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was up in a tree. Sam tried to jump and get the ball. He jumped for a minute but could not reach it. Sam felt sad. He wanted to play with the big red ball. Then, Sam saw a friendly dog named Max. Max saw Sam's sad face and wanted to help. Max was taller than Sam. Max stood on his back legs and got the ball for Sam. They played together and had fun. And Sam was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his dog, Spot. Tim loved to play with Spot every day after school. They would run around the yard and have lots of fun together. One night, Tim's mom and dad decided to buy a new mattress for Tim's bed. They went to the store and picked out a big, soft mattress for him. When they got home, they put the new mattress in Tim's room. It was getting dark outside, so Tim's mom turned on the light so they could see better. Tim was excited to sleep on his new mattress. It was so soft and cozy. He put on his pajamas and climbed into bed. Spot jumped up on the bed too, and they both snuggled under the covers. Tim's mom turned off the light, and the room was dark. Tim and Spot fell asleep on the new mattress, and they had sweet dreams all night long.

One day, a messy alligator named Al came to play with Tim. Tim did not mind. They played with toys and had a lot of fun. Al liked to play with the big red ball. Tim liked to play with the small blue car. "Al, can you mind my toy car?" Tim asked. Al said, "Yes, I can mind your car." Al was very careful with the toy car. He did not want to break it. After they played, it was time for Al to go home. Tim said, "Bye, Al. Thank you for minding my car." Al said, "You're welcome, Tim. I had fun playing with you." Then, Al went back to his home in the swamp.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Emily who loved the ocean. She used to go there every day and put her net in the water in hopes of catching something mysterious. One day, she got so excited when she was pulling it back she dropped her net and ran away. The mysterious thing was still in the net! Emily was very curious and she asked her friend Jackson, "What is it?" Jackson replied, "I don't know, let's go take a look." The two friends ran up to the net and looked inside. Inside, there was a glittering pearl. Emily gasped, eyes wide in amazement. Jackson smiled and said, "I think this is for you!" Emily thanked Jackson for finding the mysterious pearl and took it home. She was very happy to have found something so mysterious and kept it safe in her room from then on.
