Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Bird

One day, a big dog named Max was very tired. He went to his soft bed to sleep. But he could not sleep because of a loud noise. It was a small bird singing outside his window. Max was envious of the bird because the bird could sing so well. Max went outside and said to the bird, "You sing so well, but I am trying to sleep. Can you please stop the noise?" The bird said, "I am sorry, Max. I will sing more quietly." Just then, a big wind came and blew the bird's nest away. The bird was very sad. Max saw the bird's nest on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and put it back in the tree. The bird was very happy. That night, Max went to sleep and the bird sang a soft song. The next morning, Max woke up and saw that he could sing, too! The bird had given him a gift. Now, Max and the bird sang together every day, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. He had a toy car that went fast. The car needed a battery to go. One day, Tim was playing with his car outside. The ground was rough and bumpy. Tim's car went very fast, but then it stopped. The battery fell out of the car. Tim tried to find the battery. He looked and looked. As he searched, he stumbled on a rock. Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He kept looking for the battery. Finally, Tim found the battery under a bush. He was so happy! He put the battery back in his car and it went fast again. Tim played with his car all day long, and he was very careful not to stumble again.

One day, Martha saw a beautiful pink sweater in the store. She really wanted it and asked her mom if she could buy it. Her mom said she could buy the sweater if she promised to take care of it and not mess it up. Martha promised and happily bought the sweater. When she went home, she put her new sweater on and showed her dad. He smiled and said she looked very nice in her new pink sweater. Martha said she had promised to take care of it. The next day, Martha went to school with her sweater. She showed all of her friends and they all said it looked very pretty. The day was cold so Martha was happy to have her nice, warm sweater. Whenever Martha wore her pink sweater, she remembered her promise to take care of it. She always made sure to keep it neat and clean so it never got messed up. Martha was very happy to have the beautiful pink sweater and she was always proud of herself for keeping her promise.

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play at the beach. They liked to make sand castles, dig holes and look for shells. One day, they saw a big ship on the water. It was white and shiny and had a big flag on it. "Wow, look at that ship!" Tim said. "It is so big and pretty. I want to go on it." "Me too," Sam said. "Maybe it has pirates or treasure or toys on it." They ran to the edge of the water and waved at the ship. They hoped the ship would see them and come closer. But the ship did not see them. It sailed away to another place. Tim and Sam felt sad. They wanted to see the ship up close. They wanted to admire it and touch it and explore it. They wondered what it was like inside. Then they saw a man walking on the beach. He was bald and had a big beard. He wore a hat and a coat and boots. He looked like a pirate. "Hello, boys," the man said. "Do you like ships?" Tim and Sam nodded. They were curious about the man. "I used to be a sailor on a ship like that," the man said. "I have seen many places and had many adventures. Do you want to hear some stories?" Tim and Sam smiled. They liked stories. They followed the man to a bench and sat with him. The man told them stories about the ship and the sea and the things he had seen. He made them laugh and gasp and wonder. Tim and Sam forgot about their sadness. They enjoyed the stories and the man. They admired him and his bald head and his beard. They thought he was a nice pirate. They were glad they met him.
