Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Thoughtful Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dog named Max. Max loved to play on the ground with his ball. One day, Max saw a little girl who was sad. Max wanted to make the little girl happy, so he sent his ball to her. The ball rolled on the ground and stopped at the girl's feet. The girl looked at Max and smiled. The girl picked up the ball and threw it to Max. They played together on the ground and had lots of fun. Max was happy that he could make the little girl smile. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there were two best friends who loved to play together. They were called Ruby and Jane. One day, Ruby and Jane went out to play. They saw an old roof that had lots of holes in it. The roof looked different - unlike the roofs of other houses. Ruby said to Jane, “Let’s bury something here!” Jane replied, “That’s a good idea! Let’s bury a toy!” So Ruby and Jane dug a hole and buried a toy. Then they covered it up again. Ruby said with a smile, “That was fun! Now that we buried something, this roof is even more special.” Jane agreed, “Yes, it’s different now. We can keep coming back to check on our buried toy.” The two friends enjoyed their adventure, and they kept going back to check on their buried toy.

Once upon a time, there was a pink room. In the room, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, the cat found a big ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball, but the dog wanted to play too. The cat said, "I saw the ball first. I want to play with it." The dog was sad but did not want to fight. The dog thought and thought. Then, the dog had an idea. The dog said, "Let's play together. I can tell you when to jump, and you can tell me when to run." The cat liked the idea. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. They learned that playing together was better than playing alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to be nosy and look at everything. One day, she went to her friend Tom's house to play. Tom had a toy she had never seen before. It was an alarm that made a loud noise when you pushed a button. Lily admired the toy and wanted to play with it. Lily and Tom played with the alarm all day. They took turns pushing the button and making the loud noise. They laughed and had so much fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily pushed the button one more time, but the alarm did not make a loud noise. It was quiet. Lily and Tom were surprised. They thought the alarm was broken. They tried to fix it, but they could not. Then, Tom's mom came into the room. She told them that the alarm was not broken. She had just turned it off so they could have a quiet time. Lily and Tom laughed at the twist and went outside to play in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy ant named Andy. Andy did not like to work. He liked to sit in the sun all day. One day, Andy's friend, Betty the bee, came to visit. "Why are you so lazy, Andy?" asked Betty. "Come play with me!" Andy looked at Betty and said, "I like to sit. I'm a lazy ant!" Betty did not want Andy to be lazy. She wanted him to have fun. So, she took Andy to a big hill. They rolled down the hill together. Andy laughed and felt happy. After playing all day, Andy was tired. He learned that being lazy is not always fun. From that day on, Andy played with his friends and worked hard with his ant family. And sometimes, he still liked to sit in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mommy and daddy. In Tim's room, there was a big cupboard where he kept his toys. One day, Tim heard a noise coming from the cupboard. He was scared, so he called his mommy. "Mommy, there is a noise in my cupboard!" he said. Mommy came to Tim's room and said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will make sure you are safe." Mommy opened the cupboard and saw a small toy dog. The toy dog was shaking. "Look, Tim! Your toy dog is shaking because it is scared too," said Mommy. Tim picked up the toy dog and gave it a hug. "It's okay, toy dog. We are safe now," he said. From that day on, Tim and his toy dog were never scared of the cupboard again. They knew that they were safe and sound with each other and their mommy. And they all lived happily ever after.
