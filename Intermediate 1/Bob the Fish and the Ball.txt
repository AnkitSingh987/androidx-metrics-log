Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Fish and the Ball

One day, a fish named Bob went for a jog near the reef. He liked to swim fast and play with his friends. The water was blue and the sun was bright. Bob saw his friend, Sam the crab, who was upset. Sam lost his red ball. Bob wanted to help Sam find it. They looked near the coral and under the rocks. Finally, they found the ball stuck in the seaweed. Sam was happy and not upset anymore. They played with the ball together and had a fun day at the reef.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy helicopter. He would pretend he could fly it high in the sky. Tim had a lot of fun with his helicopter. One sunny day, Tim met a girl named Lily in the park. "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you want to play with my helicopter?" he asked. Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Tim!" Together, Tim and Lily pretended to fly the helicopter. They took turns making it go up and down and all around. They laughed and had a great time. They became good friends and played with the helicopter every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom are going to visit Grandma and Grandpa. They are very happy. They need to pack their bags. Mom helps them. "Let's pack your clothes first," Mom says. "What do you want to wear?" "I want to wear my red dress and my blue shoes," Lily says. "I want to wear my green shirt and my brown pants," Tom says. Mom puts their clothes in their bags. Then she says, "Do you want to pack some toys?" "Yes, please!" Lily and Tom say. They run to their room. They choose their favorite toys. Lily picks her doll and her book. Tom picks his car and his ball. They come back to Mom. Mom says, "Good job! You are very tidy. You only picked two toys each. Now we have room for other things." "What other things?" Lily asks. Mom smiles. She says, "Some snacks, some water, and some cream." "Cream?" Tom asks. "Yes, cream. Grandma makes the best cream. She puts it on her pies and cakes. It is sweet and fluffy. Do you like cream?" "Yes, we love cream!" Lily and Tom say. Mom nods. She says, "Me too. That's why I packed some cream. It is a surprise for Grandma and Grandpa. They will be very happy." Lily and Tom hug Mom. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best." Mom hugs them back. She says, "You are welcome, my loves. Now let's go. Grandma and Grandpa are waiting for us."

One day, a boy named Tim found a colorful bathrobe in his room. He put it on and felt very happy. Tim went to show his mom his new bathrobe. "Look, Mom! I have a new bathrobe!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's a very nice bathrobe, Tim!" Tim went to play outside with his friends. He was wearing his colorful bathrobe. While they were playing, a big wind came and took Tim's bathrobe away. Tim and his friends were very sad. They all went to look for the bathrobe. They looked up and down, left and right. Then, they saw a big bird wearing the colorful bathrobe. The bird flew down and said, "I found this bathrobe. It's very nice, can I keep it?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can keep it. But you must promise to be our friend and play with us." The bird agreed, and they all settled to play together. Tim was happy because he made a new friend, and the bird was happy because it had a colorful bathrobe.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little girl named Lily. She liked to play with her toys and laugh all day. Lily had a best friend named Sam. Sam was a funny little boy who liked to play with Lily. One day, Lily and Sam found a toy arrow in the park. They both wanted to play with the arrow. They took turns throwing the arrow and running to get it. They laughed and had fun playing with the arrow. After playing for a while, they were tired and sat down. They looked at the arrow and smiled. They were happy they found a new toy to play with. Lily and Sam knew they would have more fun with the arrow in the days to come. They went home, excited to play with the arrow again tomorrow.
