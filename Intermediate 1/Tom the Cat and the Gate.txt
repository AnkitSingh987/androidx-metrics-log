Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Gate

One day, a shy cat named Tom was playing in the yard. He saw a big iron gate. Tom was scared, but he wanted to see what was behind it. He took a deep breath and pushed the gate open. On the other side, there was a big dog named Max. Max saw Tom and barked, "Who are you? This is my home!" Tom was scared, but he said, "I'm Tom. I just wanted to see what's here. Can we be friends?" Max was unsure. He didn't trust Tom yet. He said, "I don't know if I can trust you. You came into my home without asking." Tom felt bad and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to make you mad. I just wanted to play." Max thought about it and decided to give Tom a chance. They played together all day and became good friends. From that day on, Max learned to trust Tom, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat named Lucy. Lucy liked to stay at home and play with her toy ball. One day, Lucy saw a big march outside her house. Many people were walking and singing. Lucy said to her friend, the dog named Max, "Look at the march! It is so big and fun!" Max looked at the march and wagged his tail. He said, "Yes, it is fun! Let's go and see it!" Lucy and Max went outside to watch the march. They saw many people and heard loud music. They danced and played together. After the march, they went back home and stayed together. They were very happy and had a great day.

One day, a little dog named Max went to the park. The park was very wide. Max saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. He ran fast to get the ball. Max was so happy to play with the big ball. Max saw a cat and wanted to play with it too. The cat did not want to play with Max. The cat was scared of Max. Max did not know that the cat was scared. Max said, "Hurry, let's play!" The cat ran away very fast. Max chased the cat. The cat ran up a tree to get away from Max. Max tried to climb the tree, but he could not. Max was very sad. He wanted to play with the cat, but the cat was too scared. Max felt terrible. He walked home alone without playing with the cat.

One day, a funny little girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. They played in the sand and swam in the sea. Lily loved the water and splashed around, laughing. While swimming, Lily saw a jellyfish. She was scared and called out to her mom. "Mom, there's a jellyfish!" Her mom replied, "Don't worry, it won't hurt you if you don't touch it." Lily wanted to be brave, so she swam closer to the jellyfish. She talked to it and said, "Hi, jellyfish! I'm Lily." To her surprise, the jellyfish replied, "Hello, Lily! I'm Jilly the Jellyfish." Lily couldn't believe it! A talking jellyfish! They became friends and played in the water together. When it was time to go home, Lily said goodbye to Jilly. She couldn't wait to tell her friends about her new talking jellyfish friend.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to mix things. One day, Tom found a big bottle of wine. He thought it would be fun to mix it with other things. Tom went to his friend, Sally. He said, "Sally, I found wine. Let's mix it with juice and have a fun drink." Sally knew that wine was dangerous for them. She said, "No, Tom. Wine is not good for us. It can make us feel sick." Tom was sad, but he listened to Sally. They decided to mix juice and water instead. They had a great time, and learned that it is important to stay away from dangerous things like wine.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cart. He would push it around the house and put his toys inside it. Tim's mommy always told him to behave and be a dependable boy. One day, Tim was playing with his cart in the living room. He heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" Tim looked around and saw a small mouse sitting in his cart. The mouse said, "I need help. Can you be dependable and help me find my family?" Tim wanted to behave, so he said, "Yes, I will help you!" Tim and the mouse went on a big adventure around the house. They looked in the kitchen, the bedroom, and even the bathroom. Finally, they found the mouse's family hiding behind the couch. The mouse said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a very dependable friend!" And just like that, the mouse and his family disappeared. Tim was surprised but happy that he could help. From that day on, Tim always tried to behave and be a dependable boy.
