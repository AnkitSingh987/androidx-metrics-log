Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play outside. One sunny day, she saw a big yellow puddle. She wondered where it came from. Mia saw her friend Tom. Tom liked to write. Mia asked Tom, "Did you make the yellow puddle?" Tom said, "No, but I can write about it!" So Tom wrote a fun story about the yellow puddle. Mia and Tom played near the puddle. They jumped and splashed in it. They laughed and had a lot of fun. The yellow puddle made their day very happy. And they always remembered the day they found the yellow puddle.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Andy. He was three years old and had a young heart full of love. One day, his mommy took him to the circus. Andy was so excited to see the clowns. He ran right up to one of them. Andy said, "Hi clown! I want to be your friend." The clown didn't accept Andy's friendship. He said, "I'm not interested in being friends. Go away!" Andy was so hurt. He ran back to his mother, crying. His mommy was very compassionate. She hugged Andy and said, "It's okay, honey. We don't have to be friends with everyone." But Andy was still very sad. He thought the clown wasn't very nice and never wanted to go back to the circus. The end.

Lily and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park after school. One day, they saw a big tree with a hole in it. They wanted to see what was inside. "Maybe there is a treasure in there," Lily said. "Or a monster," Tom said. They walked to the tree and looked in the hole. It was dark and quiet. They heard a soft sound. "Who, who, who," the sound said. Lily and Tom were scared. They thought it was a monster. They ran away from the tree. "Help, help, help," they shouted. But no one heard them. They ran and ran until they saw a bench. They sat on the bench and hugged each other. "Who, who, who," the sound said again. Lily and Tom looked up. They saw a lovely owl on a branch. It had big eyes and fluffy feathers. It was not a monster. It was a friend. "Hello, owl," Lily said. "Hello, children," the owl said. The owl was not scary. It was nice. It told them stories about the night and the stars. Lily and Tom listened and smiled. They liked the owl. "Can we play with you?" Tom asked. "Of course," the owl said. The owl flew down and landed on the bench. Lily and Tom clapped their hands. They were happy. They played with the owl until it was time to go home. They said goodbye and thanked the owl for the stories. "Come back tomorrow," the owl said. "We will," Lily and Tom said. They waved to the owl and walked home. They had a new friend. They had a lovely day.

Once upon a time, there was a clown. The clown was troubled. He was sad because he lost his big red nose. He looked for it everywhere, but he could not find it. One day, the clown went to the park. He saw kids playing and laughing. He wanted to make them laugh too, but he needed his red nose. Then, something happened. A little dog came running with a red ball in its mouth. The clown thought the red ball was his nose. He took the ball from the dog and put it on his nose. But it was not his nose, it was just a ball. The kids saw the clown and started to laugh. The clown was happy now. He did not need his red nose to make the kids laugh. The dog with the red ball helped him find a new way to be funny. And they all played together in the park, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a large jellyfish. It lived in the big blue sea. The large jellyfish had many friends, like the small fish and the big crab. They all played together in the water. One day, the large jellyfish found a big bowl. It decided to make a fun game. The jellyfish said, "Let's all mix things in the bowl!" The small fish and the big crab were excited. They all took turns mixing things in the bowl. The small fish mixed water, the big crab mixed sand, and the large jellyfish mixed sea plants. They laughed and played as they mixed everything together. In the end, they made a beautiful sea soup that they all shared. And they lived happily ever after.
