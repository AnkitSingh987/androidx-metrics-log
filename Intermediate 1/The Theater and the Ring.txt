Category: Intermediate
Title: The Theater and the Ring

Once upon a time, there was a big theater in a small town. The theater was smooth and shiny. Many people came to see fun shows there. One day, a nice lady named Sue went to the theater. She wanted to see a funny show. Sue sat down and waited for the show to start. Suddenly, she heard a loud ring. It was the bell that told everyone the show was about to begin. Sue saw a funny man on the stage. He wore a big hat and danced around. The man said, "Hello, everyone! Welcome to the show!" Sue laughed and clapped her hands. The funny man made everyone happy. After the show, Sue went home with a big smile on her face. She told her friends about the fun time she had at the theater. They all wanted to go and see the funny man too. So, they all went to the theater the next day and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful park. In the park, there were many trees, flowers, and a big pond. Every day, kids would come and play with their friends. One day, a little girl named Mia and her friend Tom went to the park to play. They played with a ball, and they were very happy. But then, Mia forgot her ball near the pond. She was sad and said, "Oh no, I forgot my ball!" Tom wanted to help Mia. He said, "Don't worry, Mia. I will get your ball." He went near the pond and saw a big duck sitting on the ball. Tom was scared, but he wanted to help his friend. He said to the duck, "Please, can we have our ball back?" The duck looked at Tom and then at the ball. It moved away and let Tom take the ball. Tom was very brave, and Mia was happy to have her ball back. They said thank you to the duck and played together in the beautiful park until it was time to go home.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to visit her aunt's marriage. She was very excited but also confused. She did not know what a marriage was. Her mom told her, "A marriage is when two people say they will be together forever." At the marriage, Lily saw a big cake. She wanted to eat it. She asked her mom, "Can I eat the cake now?" Her mom said, "No, you have to wait until after the marriage." Lily was not happy. She wanted the cake now. She thought, "I will eat the cake when no one is looking." So, she took a big piece of cake and ate it. But then, everyone saw the big hole in the cake. They were sad and angry. Lily felt bad and knew she should have waited.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to sleep with her teddy bear. The next day, she had to wake up early. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Mia rubbed her eyes and sat up in her bed. She was ready to start her day. Mia put on her favorite dress with a big bow. The bow was red and very tight. She loved her dress because it made her feel like a princess. She put on her shoes and ran outside to play with her friends. As Mia played with her friends, her tight bow came loose. She was sad, but her friends helped her fix it. They had so much fun playing together. When the day was over, Mia went home and gave her teddy bear a big hug. She couldn't wait to wake up and play again tomorrow.

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to carry a post for his mom. He wanted to be a big helper. His mom said, "Tim, you can carry this post to our neighbor's house. It is a surprise for them." Tim walked down the street with the post in his arms. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. Sam said, "Hey, Tim! Let's play!" Tim wanted to play, but he knew he had to carry the post first. Tim kept walking and finally got to the neighbor's house. He knocked on the door and gave them the post. The neighbor opened it and found a big cake inside! The neighbor said, "Thank you, Tim! This is for your birthday party later today. You found your surprise!" Tim was so happy that he got to carry his own birthday cake and couldn't wait to share it with his friends.
