Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Fish

One day, a boy and a girl went to the beach. They were very happy. They played with sand and water. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. The boy said to the girl, "Can I go in the water?" The girl said, "Yes, but be careful." The boy was grateful that the girl allowed him to go in the water. He splashed and laughed. Suddenly, a big fish came out of the water. The boy and the girl were surprised. The fish said, "Thank you for playing in my water. I have a gift for you." The fish gave them a pretty shell. The boy and the girl were very happy and said thank you to the fish. They went home with big smiles and a new friend.

Once there was a little girl called Mary. Mary was three years old and she had an older brother called Jack. One day Mary and Jack went for a walk in the park together. Mary saw a prune on the ground and decided to pick it up. She thanked Jack for helping her pick it up. But Jack was feeling serious. He said that she should only pick up things that she was allowed to pick up. He said she should not pick up anything that wasn't hers. Mary was sad. She said sorry and put the prune back down on the ground. But it was too late. A policeman had seen Mary pick up the prune and he was very serious. He told Jack and Mary that they had broken the law by picking up the prune and he had to take them away. So sadly, Jack and Mary had to say goodbye and the policeman took them away. Mary was so sad that she never got to thank Jack again for helping her pick up the prune.

Sara liked to play with her mom's office things. She liked the shiny pens, the sticky notes, and the cute clips. Sometimes, she would pretend to be a boss and write on the papers. But she knew she had to be careful and not make a mess. One day, Sara found a new thing on her mom's desk. It was a small box with a button. She wondered what it was. She pressed the button and saw a red light. She heard a beep. She felt curious. She pressed the button again and again. Suddenly, she heard her mom's voice. "Sara, where are you? I need to work now." Sara wanted to answer, but she could not. She looked around and saw that she was not in the office anymore. She was in a strange place with many colors and shapes. She had disappeared! She felt scared and sad. She wanted to go back to her mom. She tried to press the button again, but it did not work. She cried and cried. She wished she had not played with the box. Then, she heard another voice. It was a friendly voice. It said, "Hello, little girl. Don't be afraid. I am here to help you. I am the magic box. I can take you to different places, but only for a short time. You pressed the button too many times, so I got confused. But don't worry, I can take you back to your mom. Just tell me where you want to go." Sara felt a little better. She said, "I want to go back to the office. I want to see my mom. I am sorry I played with you. You are not a toy. You are a magic box." The magic box said, "That's okay. I forgive you. You are a cute and smart girl. I like you. But you have to promise me not to touch me again without your mom's permission. Deal?" Sara said, "Deal. Thank you, magic box. You are very nice." The magic box said, "You're welcome. Now, hold on tight. I will take you back to the office. Ready, set, go!" Sara felt a whoosh. She closed her eyes. When she opened them, she saw her mom's desk. She saw her mom's face. She was smiling and hugging her. She said, "Sara, I'm so glad you're back. I was so worried. Where did you go?" Sara said, "I went to a colorful place. But I came back with the magic box. It was an accident. I'm sorry, mom. I won't play with your office things again." Her mom said, "It's okay, Sara. I'm not mad. I'm just happy you're safe. But what magic box are you talking about?" Sara pointed to the small box on the desk. But it was not there anymore. It had disappeared!
