Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Bossy Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a bossy little cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her best friend, a dog named Buddy. They liked to play in the snow outside their house. One day, Kitty and Buddy went outside to play in the snow. They saw a big pile of snow and had an idea. They wanted to make a snow house. Kitty told Buddy to fold the snow to make walls for their house. Buddy did what Kitty said because she was very bossy. After they made the snow house, Kitty and Buddy went inside to warm up. They drank hot milk and ate cookies. They were happy they made the snow house together. Even though Kitty was bossy, Buddy loved her and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little white panda named Pandy. Pandy loved to play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. One day, Pandy saw his friend, Birdy, sitting on a tree branch. "Hi Birdy! Let's play together!" Pandy said. But Birdy looked sad. "I can't play, Pandy. My wing is hurt. I can't fly." Pandy wanted to help Birdy. He thought and thought. Then, he had a great idea. Pandy found a big leaf and used it to wrap Birdy's hurt wing. "This will make your wing feel better, Birdy," said Pandy. Birdy smiled, "Thank you, Pandy! You are a good friend." After a few days, Birdy's wing was all better. Pandy and Birdy played and played, happy to be together again. And they lived happily ever after in their big green forest.

Ben and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park near their house. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and furry. It has a long tail and sharp teeth. "Look, a dog!" Ben says. "Let's go and pet it!" Lily is scared of the dog. She remembers what her mom told her. "No, Ben, we can't. Mom said we should not touch strange dogs. They might bite us. We should be polite and stay away." Ben does not listen to Lily. He runs to the dog and tries to hug it. The dog does not like Ben. It growls and barks at him. It snaps at his hand. "Ow!" Ben cries. "The dog hurt me!" Lily runs to Ben and helps him. She sees blood on his hand. She is very worried. She warns Ben. "Ben, we have to go home. We have to tell mom. She will make your hand better." Ben nods. He is sorry he did not listen to Lily. He is sorry he was not polite to the dog. He holds Lily's hand and they run to their house. Mom sees them and hugs them. She cleans Ben's hand and puts a bandage on it. She tells them they are brave and good. She also tells them they should always be careful and polite around animals. Ben and Lily agree. They say they are sorry and they love mom. Mom smiles and kisses them. She says she loves them too. They are happy. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy little hotel. The hotel was in a big forest. Many animals lived in the forest. One day, a small, tired mouse came to the hotel. The mouse wanted a place to sleep. The hotel owner was a kind bear. The bear saw the mouse and said, "Welcome to my hotel! You can sleep here." The mouse was very happy. But the bear told the mouse, "You must be careful. There is a big, loud party tonight." The mouse went to sleep in the hotel. The night came, and the party started. The music was very loud. The mouse woke up and felt scared. But then, the mouse remembered what the bear said. The mouse knew the party was not scary. The mouse went to the party and made new friends. The mouse and the friends danced all night. In the morning, the mouse went back to sleep. The sleepy hotel was quiet again.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house near the woods. The man would go to the woods every day to find smooth stones for his wife. She loved to hold the smooth stones in her hand. One day, the man could not find any smooth stones. He looked and looked, but there were none. He went back home and told his wife, "I am sorry, I could not find any smooth stones today." The wife was sad and started to weep. The next day, the man went to the woods again. This time, he found many smooth stones. He brought them back to his wife. She was so happy that she stopped weeping. She said, "Thank you for the smooth stones!" They both smiled and lived happily ever after.
