Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Horse

Ben and Lily like to jog with Mom and Dad every morning. They put on their shoes and hats and go outside. They jog on the path near the park. They see birds and flowers and other people. One day, they see a big horse in a field. The horse is brown and has a long mane. Ben and Lily want to pet the horse. They ask Mom and Dad if they can go closer. "Sure, but be careful and gentle," Mom says. "The horse might be friendly, but he is also very big and strong." They jog to the fence and call the horse. The horse comes to them and sniffs their hands. He makes a soft noise. Ben and Lily smile and stroke his nose and ears. The horse likes them. "Hello, horse," Ben says. "What is your name?" The horse does not answer, but he looks at them with his big eyes. "Maybe he is hungry," Lily says. "Do you have any oats, Mom?" Mom shakes her head. "No, I don't. Oats are good for horses, but we don't have any available. Maybe the horse has some in his barn." The horse nods and walks away. He goes to his barn and comes back with a bucket of oats. He drops the bucket near the fence and invites Ben and Lily to share. They take some oats and feed the horse. The horse eats happily and thanks them with a nuzzle. "Wow, he is so smart and nice," Ben says. "Yes, he is," Lily says. "He is our new friend." They hug the horse and say goodbye. They jog back to Mom and Dad and tell them about the horse. Mom and Dad are proud of them for being kind and polite. They jog home and have breakfast. They hope to see the horse again soon.

One day, a helpful bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a little girl named Lily sitting by a tree. She was sad because she lost her toy. "Hi Lily, why are you sad?" asked Tim. "I lost my toy," said Lily. "I will help you find it," said the helpful bird. Tim and Lily looked for the toy together. They found it near the lake. "Thank you, Tim!" said Lily. "You're welcome," said Tim. "I was happy to lend a hand." The next day, Tim was very tired. He could not wake up early. Lily saw him sleeping and wanted to help. She sang a song to wake him up. Tim woke up and said, "Thank you, Lily!" The moral of the story is that when you are helpful to others, they will be helpful to you too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim always wanted to go to new places. One day, he found a place that looked crazy. It was full of big, bright colors and funny shapes. Tim wanted to go there and play. When Tim got to the crazy place, he saw other kids playing. They were running, jumping, and laughing. Tim wanted to join them, but he was a little scared. He took a deep breath and asked if he could play too. The other kids said yes, and they all played together. As Tim played with the other kids, he learned that it was good to be kind and share. They all had fun and made new friends. The moral of the story is that even if a place looks crazy, it can be fun and full of love if you are kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a big kangaroo. He was deaf but very kind. He lived in a pretty forest with many friends. One day, he met a small bird who was lost. "Help me," said the bird. The kangaroo did not hear, but he saw the bird was sad. He wanted to help his new friend. They walked together to find the bird's family. As they walked, the bird saw a tree. "I think my family is up there," the bird said. The kangaroo did not reply, but he knew what to do. He used his strong legs to jump high and put the bird back in its nest. The bird was happy to be home and thanked the kangaroo. In the end, the kangaroo and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and the bird learned how to talk to the kangaroo without words. The kangaroo was always there to help his little friend.
