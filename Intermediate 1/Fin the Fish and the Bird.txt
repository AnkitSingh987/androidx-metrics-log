Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to swim in the big sea near the shore. One day, while Fin was swimming, he heard a small cry. It was a baby bird. The baby bird was helpless and could not fly. Fin wanted to help the baby bird. He swam very fast to the shore. Fin called out to his friend, Sam the crab. Sam heard Fin and came to help. Together, they thought of a plan to help the baby bird. Fin and Sam worked together to help the baby bird. They pushed and pulled until the baby bird was safe. The baby bird was very happy. Fin and Sam were proud of their work. They became good friends and played near the shore every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found sunglasses on the ground. He put them on and felt cool. He saw a small, harmless bug on a leaf. Tim thought it would be fun to record the bug with his toy camera. He showed his friend, Sally. "Look at my sunglasses and the bug I recorded," Tim said. Sally smiled and said, "I want to see the bug too!" They both looked at the bug on the leaf. Later, they played in the park. They forgot about the sunglasses and the bug. Then, they saw a big group of kids. The kids were excited about something. Tim and Sally went to see what it was. A girl said, "Look, a cool bug on a leaf!" Tim and Sally looked at each other and smiled. They knew it was the same bug they recorded earlier. They showed the other kids the video of the bug. Everyone was happy and had fun watching the bug. And Tim felt extra cool in his new sunglasses.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer who had a big farm. He had many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. The farmer was very happy with his farm. One day, a small bug came to the farm. The bug liked to crawl around the farm and play with the animals. The animals liked the bug and they all played together. But the farmer was frightened of the bug because he did not know what it was. The farmer tried to catch the bug, but it was too fast. The bug would crawl away and hide. The animals would help the bug and tell it where to hide. In the end, the farmer saw that the bug was nice and not scary. They all became friends and lived happily on the farm.

One sunny day, a boy named Tim went to the toy store with his mom. Tim saw a big, expensive toy car. He wanted it so much, but his mom said it was too much money. Tim was sad. At home, Tim's mom gave him a test. If he could clean his room and be nice to his sister, she would think about the toy car. Tim tried very hard. He cleaned his room and played with his sister. But then, he broke her doll by accident. His sister was mad. Tim said sorry to his sister. She looked at the broken doll and then at Tim. She decided to forgive him. Tim's mom saw this and was happy. She said that Tim passed the test. The next day, they went back to the toy store and Tim got the toy car.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful sun. It was very hot, and it made the world bright. One day, the sun met a little bulb. The bulb was sad because it could not make light like the sun. "Hi, little bulb. Why are you sad?" asked the sun. "I want to make light like you, but I can't," said the bulb. The sun wanted to help the bulb. It told the bulb to twist and twist. The little bulb twisted and twisted, and then it started to glow. The bulb was very happy. Now, the powerful sun and the little bulb were friends. They made the world bright together. The sun shined during the day, and the bulb glowed at night. Everyone was happy, and they all lived happily ever after.
