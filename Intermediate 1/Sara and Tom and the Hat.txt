Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Hat

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big poster. The poster says: "Join the march for animals. Be kind to all creatures. Saturday at 10 o'clock." Sara loves animals. She has a dog, a cat and a fish. She says to Tom: "Let's go to the march. We can help the animals. It will be fun." Tom is not sure. He likes animals, but he does not like crowds. He says to Sara: "I don't know. There will be many people. And noise. And signs. Maybe we can do something else." Sara is sad. She wants to go to the march. She thinks of a way to make Tom happy. She says to Tom: "I have an idea. We can make our own signs. We can draw our favorite animals. And we can wear our animal hats. You have a lion hat, and I have a bunny hat. We will look cute. And we can sing songs. Do you like songs?" Tom thinks for a moment. He likes to draw. He likes to wear his lion hat. He likes to sing songs. He says to Sara: "OK. That sounds good. I will go to the march with you. But only if you promise to hold my hand." Sara smiles. She says to Tom: "I promise. I will hold your hand. And we will be happy. And the animals will be happy. And the people will be happy. Come on, let's go home and make our signs." The next day, Sara and Tom go to the march. They hold their signs. They wear their hats. They sing their songs. They hold their hands. They see many other people who love animals. They see dogs, cats, birds, horses and more. They feel proud and happy. They are glad they came. They recommend the march to everyone. They have a great day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki was a scared kangaroo. She was scared of the dark and loud noises. One day, Kiki met a wise old owl named Ollie. Ollie saw that Kiki was scared and asked, "Why are you so scared, Kiki?" Kiki said, "I am scared of the dark and loud noises. They make me feel small and alone." Ollie thought for a moment and then said, "I have an idea. Let's weigh you on my special scale. It will show you how strong you are." Kiki hopped on the scale and Ollie showed her the number. Kiki looked at the number and smiled. She felt strong and brave now. From that day on, Kiki was not scared anymore. She knew she was strong and could face anything. Kiki and Ollie became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a big crane. The crane was very strong and could lift heavy things. It was also very loud. The crane worked in a place where they built tall buildings. One day, the crane had to attach a big piece of metal to the top of a building. It was a very important job. The crane's friends, the trucks, brought the big piece of metal to the crane. The crane picked up the piece of metal with its long arm. As the crane lifted the big piece of metal, it made a loud noise. The noise scared a little bird nearby. But the bird saw how strong the crane was and knew it was just doing its job. The crane attached the big piece of metal to the top of the building, and everyone was happy. The crane and the little bird became friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small black dog named Spot. Spot loved to walk and play with his friends. He had a lot of fun every day. One sunny day, Spot went for a long walk in the forest. He walked and walked until he was very tired. Spot saw a big tree and decided to rest under it. While he was resting, he heard a small cry. Spot looked around and saw a little bird with a hurt wing. Spot wanted to help the little bird, so he tried to think of a way to manage it. He had an idea! Spot found a big leaf and gently put the bird on it. Then, he carried the bird back to his friends. When Spot and the little bird got back, his friends were very surprised. They did not know Spot could help a bird like that. Spot's friends helped the bird get better, and they all became good friends. From that day on, Spot was known as the hero of the forest.
