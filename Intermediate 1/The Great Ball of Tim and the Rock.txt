Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he loved very much. It was a red ball with a light symbol on it. Tim played with his ball every day. One day, Tim's ball rolled under a big rock. Tim tried hard to get it, but he struggled. He could not reach the ball. Tim felt sad. His friend, Sam, came and saw him struggle. Sam said, "I will help you get your ball, Tim." Sam tried to help Tim, but the rock was too heavy. They both struggled and struggled, but they could not move the rock. Tim and Sam felt very sad. They could not get the ball. The ball stayed under the rock, and Tim and Sam could not play with it anymore. They walked away, feeling sad. The ball with the light symbol was lost forever. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a letter. The letter was very important. It was for his friend, Sam. Sam lived far away. Tim wanted to travel to give Sam the letter. Tim started to travel. He walked and walked. He saw a big tree. It was bitter cold. Tim was sad. He wanted to go home. But he knew he had to give Sam the letter. Tim walked more. It got darker and colder. Tim was very tired. He could not find Sam's house. He sat down and cried. The letter was lost. Tim was sad, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, Tim found a small, lost bird in his yard. The bird was sad and scared. Tim knew he needed to help the bird find its way home. Tim asked the bird, "What is the truth about where you live?" The bird told Tim about its original home, a big tree near a pond. Tim knew where that tree was, so he decided to help the bird find its way back. Together, they walked to the pond and found the big tree. When they reached the tree, the bird was very happy. It thanked Tim for his help and flew back to its nest. Tim felt good about helping the bird find its home. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it, and to tell the truth so that they can find their way.

One day, a small girl named Lily was playing with her mom's makeup. She liked to put it on her face and pretend she was a princess. Lily's mom told her not to play with makeup, but Lily did not listen. Lily put on too much makeup and her face looked very funny. She went to show her mom, but her mom was not there. Lily was scared and did not know what to do. She called out for her mom, but there was no answer. Lily started to cry. Then, Lily heard a voice. "I am here, Lily!" It was her mom, but she looked different. Her mom had a funny face too, just like Lily! They both laughed and hugged each other. Lily's mom said, "I wanted to surprise you and play with makeup too!" They had a fun day playing with makeup together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a charming cat named Lucy. Lucy loved to eat soup. Every day, her friend, a little boy named Tim, would make soup for her. They would sit and eat their soup together. One day, as they were eating their soup, Lucy looked at Tim and said, "This soup is so good. Can you repeat the soup tomorrow?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Lucy. I will make the soup again tomorrow." The next day, Tim made the soup just like before. But when they sat down to eat, they heard a loud noise outside. They went to see what it was and found a big, friendly dog. The dog was hungry too, so they shared their soup with him. From that day on, the three friends ate soup together every day, and they all lived happily ever after.
