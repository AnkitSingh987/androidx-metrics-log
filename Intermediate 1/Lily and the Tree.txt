Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Tree

One day, a girl named Lily had a pretty dress. She loved to run and speed in her dress. She would play in the park with her friends. Lily liked to climb high on the big tree. One day, a big wind came. It blew Lily's dress high up in the tree. She was sad and did not know what to do. Her friends tried to help but could not reach the dress. They all looked at the tree and thought. Then, Lily had an idea. She remembered how she could climb high. She started to climb the big tree. Her friends watched and cheered for her. Lily reached her dress and took it down. She was very happy and so were her friends.

One day, a boy went to the park. He saw a frog near the pond. The frog was green and small. The boy thought the frog was important. He wanted to grab the frog and take it home. The boy said, "Frog, I want to take you home. You look important." The frog looked at the boy and said, "I am important, but you can't grab me. I have a secret." The boy was surprised. He did not know that frogs could talk. The frog jumped high in the air and turned into a big, colorful bird. The boy was very happy. He made a new friend that day, and they played at the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby puppy named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball all day. He would run and jump in the park with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big dog who looked very sad. Spot went to the big dog and asked, "Why are you sad?" The big dog said, "I quit playing with my friends because I am too slow." Spot wanted to help the big dog. He said, "Let's play together, and I will show you how to be fast!" So, Spot and the big dog played together every day. Soon, the big dog was not sad anymore. He was fast and happy. Spot and the big dog became best friends. They played together all the time and never quit.

One day, Tom wanted to introduce his new pet to his friend Sue. Tom's pet was a cute, small fish. Sue was so happy to meet the fish. They went to Tom's house to see the pet. The fish lived in an icy bowl. "Hi Sue, this is my pet fish, Fin!" Tom said. Sue looked at the icy bowl and saw the fish. "Hi Fin, nice to meet you!" Sue said to the fish. They both smiled and watched Fin swim in the icy water. Then, something unexpected happened. The icy bowl started to shake! Tom and Sue were scared. But, when the shaking stopped, they saw a new fish in the bowl. Fin had a friend now! Tom and Sue were happy and laughed. They named the new fish Splash.

Once upon a time, there was a creative girl named Amy. She loved to make pastry with her mom. They would make all kinds of shapes and colors with the dough. One day, Amy's mom had to go out. She told Amy, "I promise to be back soon. Please do not make any pastry while I am away." Amy said, "Okay, mom. I promise." But Amy was bored and decided to make a pastry anyway. She made a big, round pastry and put it in the oven. As it baked, it grew and grew. The pastry became so big, it opened the oven door and rolled out! Amy was scared, but she had an idea. She got a knife and cut a big piece of the pastry. Suddenly, a little pastry man came out of the big pastry! He said, "Thank you for setting me free, Amy! I promise to help you clean up this mess." Amy and the pastry man cleaned up the mess together. When her mom came home, she was surprised but happy that Amy made a new friend. Amy promised to only make pastry with her mom from then on.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a door. Behind the door, a girl named Lily lived with her mom and dad. They loved to mix things in the kitchen. One day, they decided to bake a cake. Lily was very alert and wanted to help mix the cake. Her mom and dad said, "Yes, you can help." So, Lily put on her apron and helped her mom and dad mix the cake batter. They mixed and mixed until it was just right. After the cake was baked, Lily and her mom and dad shared the cake with their neighbors. Everyone loved the cake and thanked Lily for her help. The moral of the story is that when we work together and stay alert, we can make something great and share it with others.
