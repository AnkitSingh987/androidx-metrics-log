Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Whale

Once upon a time, there was a big, generous whale. The whale liked to help his friends. One day, he saw a little fish who was sad. "Hi, little fish. Why are you sad?" asked the whale. "I lost my friends. I can't find them," said the little fish. "Don't worry, little fish. You can join me and my friends," said the whale. The little fish felt happy and joined the whale and his friends. They all swam together and had lots of fun. The little fish was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a mule. The mule was very hairy. He lived on a farm with many animals. The mule's best friend was a little girl named Lily. They played together every day. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, you can't play with the mule today. I don't permit it." Lily was sad, but she listened to her mom. The mule was sad too. He didn't know why Lily didn't come to play. The next day, Lily's mom saw how sad Lily and the mule were. She had a big surprise for them. She said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I didn't know how much you both love to play. You can play with the mule today." Lily and the mule were so happy! They played all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Lily's mom always let them play together.

Tom and Lily liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was the laser. The laser was a small thing that made a red light. They could point the light at the wall, the floor, or the ceiling. They liked to make shapes and patterns with the light. One day, they were playing with the laser in their room. They heard a knock on the door. It was their mom. She came in and saw the laser. She was not happy. She said, "Tom and Lily, you have to mind me. The laser is not a regular toy. It can hurt your eyes or the eyes of others. You have to be careful with it. Do you understand?" Tom and Lily nodded. They did not want to hurt their eyes or the eyes of others. They said, "Sorry, mom. We will mind you. We will be careful with the laser." Mom smiled and gave them a hug. She said, "Good. I love you both. Now, you can play with your other toys. But put the laser away in the drawer. And don't use it without me or dad." Tom and Lily said, "Okay, mom." They put the laser away in the drawer. They picked up their other toys and played with them. They had fun with their regular toys, too.

Lila and Tom are twins. They like to watch cars and buses on the road. They know some signs, like stop and go. They want to learn more signs. One day, they go to the park with Mom. They see a big sign with many colors and pictures. It says "Zoo". Lila and Tom are happy. They love animals. They ask Mom to go to the zoo. Mom says yes. She buys tickets and they go inside. They see lions and monkeys and elephants and more. They watch them play and eat and make noises. They learn their names and signs. They have fun. Lila and Tom are lucky. They have a good day at the zoo. They thank Mom and the sign. They want to come back soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was only three years old, and she was very curious. One day, Sarah was wandering around her house with nothing to do. She stuck her head into the closet and saw something very strange. It was a tiny little door! She opened it and found a secret room. Sarah shouted with surprise. She jumped quickly into the room. The room was dry, without a drop of water. There was nothing inside. "I wish something exciting would happen", she shouted. Suddenly, a voice came from outside the closet. It was Sarah's mom. "What are you doing, Sarah?" her mom asked. Sarah answered, "I found a secret room, Mommy!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's wonderful! You can explore it if you like." Sarah ran around the room, exploring and shouting with joy! She had found her own special place and was happy. The end.
