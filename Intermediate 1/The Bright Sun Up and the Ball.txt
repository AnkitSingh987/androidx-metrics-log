Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bright Sun Up and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun up in the sky. It was so bright that all the kids loved to play outside. Under the bright sun, there was a small cell. In that cell, lived a tiny bug named Billy. One day, Billy found a big, bright ball. He loved the ball so much that he wanted to play with it all the time. But, the ball was too big for his cell. So, Billy had a smart idea. He would replace the big ball with a small ball that could fit in his cell. Billy went to find a small ball. He looked and looked until he found the perfect one. He was so happy! Now, Billy could play with his new small ball in his cell. And, under the bright sun, Billy and his friends had lots of fun playing with the small ball.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a lonely woman. She had no friends and felt very sad. Every day, she would look out her window and wish for someone to play with. One sunny day, the lonely woman saw a little bird outside her window. She thought the bird looked lonely too. So, she opened her window and requested, "Little bird, will you be my friend and play with me?" The bird looked at her and flew away. The woman felt even more sad, but she did not give up. The next day, the bird came back with more birds. They all flew into the lonely woman's house and started to play with her. The woman was very happy, and she was not lonely anymore. The little bird had heard her request and brought friends to make her happy. And from that day on, the woman and the birds were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a polite chicken named Bob. Bob liked to care for his friends. He lived on a farm with many animals. One day, Bob saw a big egg. He did not know where it came from. Bob wanted to care for the egg. He sat on the egg to keep it warm. After some time, the egg started to crack. Bob was excited to see a new friend. But when the egg broke, it was not a baby chicken. It was a baby duck! Bob was surprised but happy. He cared for the baby duck and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his friends outside. One day, Tim saw a high tree with a pretty bird on top. He wanted to become friends with the bird. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can I climb the high tree to meet the bird?" Mom said, "No, Tim. It's not safe. But you can put on your new clothes and go play with your friends." Tim was sad, but he put on his new clothes and went to play. While he was playing, he saw the pretty bird fly down from the high tree. The bird landed near Tim and his friends. They all became friends with the bird. The next day, Tim and his friends went to play again. They saw the pretty bird in the high tree. They knew that the bird would come down to play with them. And just like before, the bird flew down, and they all had fun playing together. Tim was happy that he became friends with the pretty bird without having to climb the high tree.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot lived near the water with his family. Every day, Spot would play by the water when the tide was low. He loved to run and jump in the sand. One day, Spot's family went on a trip, and they took Spot with them. Spot had fun on the trip, but he missed playing by the water. He missed the feel of the sand under his paws and the smell of the sea. When they came back home, Spot was so happy to see the water again. He ran to the beach, but the tide was high, and he couldn't play in the sand. Spot waited and waited. Soon, the tide went down, and Spot played in the sand all day long. He was so happy to be home and never wanted to miss the low tide again.
