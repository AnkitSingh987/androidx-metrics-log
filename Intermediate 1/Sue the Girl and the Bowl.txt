Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat waffles for breakfast every day. One day, she wanted to make waffles all by herself. She was very proud and happy. Sue asked her mom, "Can I make waffles, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can try, but I will help you." Sue and her mom went to the kitchen and started making waffles. They mixed the flour, eggs, and milk in a big bowl. But then, Sue got tired. She said, "Mom, I want to quit. I am too tired." Her mom said, "That's okay. We can finish making waffles together." Sue and her mom finished making the waffles and ate them together. They were the best waffles ever.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely little boy named Tim. Tim had no friends to play with, so he would walk around with his hands in his pockets. One day, Tim found a small, shiny rock on the ground. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. The next day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a girl his age playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play, but he was shy. He remembered the shiny rock in his pocket and decided to show it to the girl. The girl looked at the rock and smiled. She asked Tim if he wanted to play with her. Tim and the girl played all day long. They laughed and had fun. As the sun went down, the girl said goodbye and went home. Tim looked at the shiny rock in his pocket and smiled. He knew that he had gained a new friend because of the rock. From that day on, Tim was never lonely again.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside, especially when it was wet. One day, she decided to take her toys and play in the puddles. Lily put on her rain boots and raincoat. She took her toy boat and went outside. The wet ground made splashes as she walked. She found a big puddle and put her boat in it. The boat floated and Lily was happy. As Lily played, a little frog hopped by. The frog liked the wet puddle too. Lily and the frog played together in the puddle. They had so much fun! When it was time to go home, Lily took her boat and said goodbye to the frog. She couldn't wait to play in the wet puddles again.

Once upon a time, there was a big thunder. It was very loud and scary. Many little animals were afraid and hid in their homes. A small bird named Tim was also scared. He felt guilty because he did not help his friends. One day, Tim decided to fix the thunder. He wanted to make it less scary for everyone. He asked his friends, the ants, for help. They had a plan. They would all work together to make the thunder quiet. The ants and Tim climbed a big tree. They found a big cloud. The cloud was making the thunder. The ants and Tim talked to the cloud. They asked it to be quiet. The cloud said it was sorry. It did not know it was scaring everyone. The cloud promised to be quiet from now on. But then, something unexpected happened. The cloud started to laugh. It was not a cloud at all! It was a big, funny bird who liked to make loud noises. The bird said sorry for scaring everyone. It promised to stop making thunder sounds. Tim and the ants were happy. They had fixed the thunder. Now, everyone could live without fear. Tim did not feel guilty anymore. He had helped his friends and made the world a better place.

One day, a charming little dog named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big tree and a small house. Sam liked to play with his ball near the tree. But today, there was a thick fog. Sam could not see his ball well. Sam heard a voice in the fog. "Hello, who is there?" said the voice. Sam said, "I am Sam. I can't find my ball. Can you help me?" The voice said, "I am Tom, a cat. Let's find your ball together." Sam and Tom looked for the ball in the fog. They walked and walked. Then, Sam realized his ball was near the small house. "I found it!" Sam said. Tom was happy for Sam. They played together and became good friends. The fog went away, and they could see the big tree and small house again.
