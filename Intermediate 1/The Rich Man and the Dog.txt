Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rich Man and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a rich man. He had a cute dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy. One day, Spot found a new toy. It was a twisty toy. Spot was very happy. He twisted the toy with his mouth and wagged his tail. He ran around the house with his new toy. The rich man saw Spot playing with the twisty toy. He laughed and said, "Spot, you are so funny with your twisty toy!" Spot barked and ran to the rich man. They played with the twisty toy together. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear her favorite red hat. One day, she decided to eat her cereal while wearing her red hat. Her mom said, "Be careful, Sue. The bowl is fragile." As Sue ate her cereal, a small cat jumped on the table. The cat looked at Sue and said, "Your hat is nice. Can I wear it too?" Sue was surprised that the cat could talk! She said, "Yes, you can wear my hat, but you must be nice to me." The cat put on the red hat and something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a big, friendly dog! Sue was so happy to have a new friend. They played together all day, and from then on, Sue always shared her cereal with her new friend, the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Tim. Tim lived in a small hole near a big hay field. Every day, he would hop around and play with his friends. One day, Tim found a long string in the hay. He did not know what to do with it. His friend, a wise old owl, told him to try to tie the string. Tim tried and tried, but he could not tie it. He was scared that he would never learn. Tim's friends saw that he was scared, so they all came to help him. They took turns trying to tie the string. Finally, with the help of his friends, Tim learned to tie the string. They all cheered and played together in the hay field, happy that they could help their friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with a little girl named Emma. They were best friends and loved to play together. One day, Max had a fun idea to surprise Emma. Max hid behind the big, blue chair in the living room. He waited and waited for Emma to come home from school. When Emma walked into the room, Max jumped out and shouted, "Surprise!" Emma laughed and clapped her hands. "You scared me, Max!" she said with a big smile on her face. Max and Emma played together all day. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Emma gave Max a big hug. She noticed that Max had a little cut on his finger. "Oh no, Max! You have a boo-boo!" Emma said. She put a band-aid on Max's finger and gave him a kiss. Max wagged his tail, happy that his best friend took care of him. They both went to sleep, excited for another day of fun and surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a big blueberry. It was very big and round. One day, a graceful bird saw the blueberry. The bird flew down and said, "Hello, blueberry!" The blueberry said, "Hello, bird! I am so big, I want to split and share with my friends. Can you help me?" The bird was happy to help. With a gentle peck, the bird split the big blueberry into two parts. Now, there were two smaller blueberries. The blueberry was very happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird said, "You're welcome, blueberry!" Then, the bird flew away to find more friends to share the blueberries with. The blueberries were happy to be shared and enjoyed by everyone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred lived near a noisy pond. He loved to look at the fish swimming in the water. One day, while Fred was looking at the fish, he met a big duck named Daisy. "Hi, I'm Daisy," said the duck. "Do you want to play with me?" Fred was happy to have a friend. "Yes, I want to play," he said. Together, Fred and Daisy played near the noisy pond. They looked at the fish and the green plants. They laughed and had fun. Soon, it was time to go home. Fred and Daisy said goodbye and promised to play again soon.
