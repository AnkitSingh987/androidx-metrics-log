Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin and the Boat

One sunny day, a fish named Fin wanted to swim. He saw a big wake in the water and wanted to play in it. Fin swam fast and jumped high into the wake. He was having so much fun! A spoiled duck named Quack saw Fin playing. Quack said, "I want to swim in the wake too!" But Quack did not like to share. So, he splashed the water to make the wake go away. Fin was sad and stopped playing. Just then, a big boat came. The boat made an even bigger wake! Fin and Quack looked at each other and smiled. They both swam fast and jumped into the big wake together. They learned to share and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer named Tom. Tom was bald and he had a big farm. On his farm, he grew wheat. The wheat was tall and golden. One day, Tom wanted to separate the wheat. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you separate the wheat." Tom and Sam worked hard to separate the wheat. They talked and laughed while they worked. Soon, the wheat was all separated. Tom was very happy. In the end, Tom thanked Sam for helping him. They were both tired but they had fun. Tom's farm was now ready for the next season.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max loved to play outside in the mud. One day, Max's mom warned him, "Max, don't get too dirty, or you'll need a bath!" Max did not listen and played in the mud all day. When Max came back inside, he was very dirty. His mom looked at him and said, "Max, now you need a bath!" Max's mom put on her bathrobe and filled the tub with water. Max did not want a bath, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. As Max got into the tub, he saw a toy boat. Max played with the boat and had fun in the bath. After Max was clean, he got out of the tub and his mom wrapped him in a warm towel. Max learned that baths can be fun, and he promised to listen to his mom's warnings next time.

Once upon a time, there was a round ball named Bob. Bob was very serious about his dream to be the best ball in the park. He wanted to succeed in everything he did. One sunny day, Bob was playing with his friends, the other balls. They were all having fun, but Bob was trying very hard to be the best. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew all the balls away, but not Bob. He was very surprised. Bob wanted to find his friends, so he rolled and rolled until he found them. They were all stuck in a big hole. Bob tried to help them out. He pushed and pushed, and finally, he succeeded. His friends were so happy, and they all thanked Bob. From that day on, Bob learned that being the best is not as important as helping his friends.

One day, a big rabbit and a small rabbit went for a walk. They saw a big mountain far away. The big rabbit said, "Let's go to the mountain!" The small rabbit was scared but said, "Okay, I will follow you." They walked and walked. The big rabbit went fast, and the small rabbit had to run to follow. They came to a thick forest. The big rabbit said, "I am not scared. I will go first." But inside, the big rabbit was scared too. In the thick forest, they saw a big bear. The bear said, "Why are you here?" The big rabbit was scared and did not talk. But the small rabbit said, "We want to go to the mountain." The bear said, "I will help you." The big rabbit was surprised. They all went to the mountain together and had fun. The moral of the story is, even if you are small, you can be brave and make new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cool penguin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a cold, snowy place with his family. They loved to play and swim in the icy water. One day, Pippin's mom asked him to go to the store. She needed some fish for dinner. Pippin put on his hat and scarf and walked to the store. He saw his friends playing, but he knew he had to finish his job first. At the store, Pippin picked out the best fish he could find. He paid the store man and walked back home. When he got home, his mom was very happy. They cooked the fish and had a yummy dinner with the whole family. Pippin felt proud that he could help his mom and make her happy.
