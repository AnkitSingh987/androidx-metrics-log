Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his equipment. He had a big box full of toys. Every day, he would play with his toys and have fun. One day, Tim saw a big tasty cake on the table. He wanted to eat it. He asked his mom, "Can I eat the cake?" His mom said, "No, Tim, that cake is for later. You can eat it after dinner." Tim was sad, but he went to play with his equipment. He played for a long time. When he was done, he saw that the cake was still there. He thought, "Maybe I can eat it now." So, he took a big bite of the cake. Suddenly, Tim heard his mom say, "Tim, I told you not to eat the cake!" Tim knew he did a bad thing. His mom was very sad and Tim felt bad. The cake was gone and now nobody could eat it after dinner.

One day, a small cat named Kitty went out to wander. Kitty liked to see new things and have fun. She walked and walked until she found a big, round pizza. The pizza was on a table, and it smelled so good! Kitty wanted to eat the pizza, but she was too small to reach it. She thought of a plan. She saw a big, stupid dog named Buddy. Kitty asked Buddy to help her get the pizza. Buddy was happy to help because he wanted some pizza too. Buddy jumped up and got the pizza down for Kitty. They both ate the yummy pizza and had a great time. Kitty was happy she wandered and found the pizza. She and Buddy became good friends, and they went on more fun adventures together.

One day, a little crab went for a walk on the beach. The crab was tough and strong, but he did not understand many things. As he walked, he saw a big fish stuck in a net. The fish was sad and asked the crab for help. "Please, help me get out!" said the fish. The crab wanted to help, but he did not know how. He tried to use his strong claws to break the net, but it was too tough. The crab felt sad and thought he could not help the fish. Just then, a small bird came and saw the fish and the crab. The bird said, "I can help you both." The bird used its sharp beak and cut the net. The fish was free and happy. The crab and the fish thanked the bird. The bird said, "We all have different skills, and we can help each other." The crab understood that he could not do everything alone, but together they could do great things. The crab, the fish, and the bird became good friends and helped each other in tough times.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he was playing with his toy car, but it broke. Tim was sad because his toy was broken. Tim's mom said, "Let's go to the doctor to fix your toy." They went to the doctor, and the doctor took an x-ray of the broken toy car. Tim was very curious about the x-ray. He looked at the picture and saw something inside the toy car. The doctor said, "Look, there is a key inside your toy car!" Tim was so surprised! He never knew there was a key inside his toy car. The doctor took the key out, and Tim's toy car was fixed. Tim was happy again. He thanked the doctor and went back home with his mom. From that day on, Tim always shut his toy box carefully, so his toys would not break again.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to help her mom. She thought she would organize the things in the kitchen. Amy started with the sink. It was full of cups, plates, and spoons. She put them away in the right place. She felt happy to help. Amy's mom was in the living room. She thought organizing was boring. But she knew Amy liked to help. So, she let her do it. She wanted to see what would happen next. Amy found a big, red button near the sink. She thought it was for the light. But when she pushed it, the sink started to talk! It said, "Hi, I am Mr. Sink! Thank you for organizing me!" Amy was surprised and laughed. Her mom heard her and came to see. They both talked to Mr. Sink and had a fun day. Organizing was not boring anymore!
