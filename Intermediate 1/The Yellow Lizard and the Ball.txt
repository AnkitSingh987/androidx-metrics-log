Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Lizard and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow lizard. He lived in a tiny house under a big tree. The yellow lizard loved to play with his friends in the sun. One day, the yellow lizard saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. He called his friends and said, "Come, let's play with the red ball!" His friends came running and they all played with the red ball. They played all day until the sun went down. The yellow lizard and his friends were very happy. They said goodbye and went to their little homes. The yellow lizard went to sleep, dreaming of the fun day he had with his friends.

One day, a big cat named Tom saw a red laser on the ground. Tom wanted to play with the laser. So, he started to follow the laser. The laser moved fast, and Tom ran after it. He was having a lot of fun. Tom's friend, a little dog named Max, saw Tom playing. Max asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I am following the laser. It is so fun! You should try too!" So, Max started to follow the laser with Tom. They both laughed and played together. But then, something unexpected happened. The laser stopped moving. Tom and Max looked around to see who was controlling the laser. They saw a tiny mouse holding a laser toy! The mouse said, "I wanted to play with you both. I hope you had fun!" Tom and Max were surprised, but they laughed and said, "Thank you, little mouse! We had a great time!" And they all became friends.

One day, a blue bird named Billy was flying in the sky. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Billy thought it would be a good place to make a home. He flew down and went inside the tree hole. Inside the tree hole, Billy found a small bug with a big mouth. The bug said, "Hello, I am Timmy. What is your name?" Billy replied, "I am Billy. I want to make my home here." Timmy said, "Okay, but first you must find a special leaf for me to eat." Billy flew out to look for the special leaf. He found it near a pond. When he brought it back, Timmy ate the leaf. Then, Timmy's big mouth started to glow. Billy asked, "Why is your mouth glowing?" Timmy replied, "Now I can help you make your home." Timmy used his glowing mouth to light up the tree hole. They worked together to make a nice home for Billy. In the end, Billy and Timmy became best friends and lived happily in their tree home.

Once upon a time, there was a normal girl named Lily. Lily had very long hair. She liked to play with her hair. One day, she found a comb. Lily wanted to use the comb to make her hair look nice. Lily tried to comb her hair, but it was very hard. Her hair was full of knots. She tried to resist the pain, but it hurt too much. Lily felt sad. She wanted her hair to be smooth and pretty. Then, Lily's mom came to help. She gently combed Lily's hair and took out all the knots. It did not hurt anymore. Lily was happy. Her hair looked nice and smooth. She thanked her mom and played with her hair all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her toy boat. She would rock her boat in a big tub of water. One day, Mia saw a knot in the string of her boat. She was fearful because she thought her boat would break. Mia's mom saw her looking sad and asked, "What's wrong, Mia?" Mia showed her mom the knot in the string. Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Mia. I can help you fix it." Mia's mom untied the knot, and Mia felt happy again. Now, Mia knew that when she had a problem, she could ask for help. She learned that it was okay to be fearful, but it was important to share her feelings with others. Mia and her mom played with the boat, rocking it in the water, and they had a great time together.
