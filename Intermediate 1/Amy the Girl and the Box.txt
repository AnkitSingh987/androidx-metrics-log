Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toy blocks. One day, she wanted to make a big stack with her blocks. She started to put one block on top of the other. Amy's mom called her for lunch, but Amy wanted to delay eating. She wanted to finish her stack first. So, she kept putting more blocks on top of each other. The stack got taller and taller. Suddenly, Amy found a fake block in her toy box. It was made of soft, squishy stuff, not like the other hard blocks. She put the fake block on top of her stack. It wobbled and wiggled, but it stayed on. Then, Amy went to eat her lunch, happy with her big, tall stack.

Once upon a time, in a big red barn, there lived a little pig named Pippa. Pippa loved to eat tasty food. One day, she saw a big, tasty apple on the ground. Pippa was very happy and ate the apple. She wanted to gain more tasty apples. Pippa went to her friend, a cow named Cally. Pippa said, "Cally, where can I find more tasty apples?" Cally replied, "Pippa, there is an apple tree near the barn. You can find many tasty apples there." Pippa went to the apple tree and saw many tasty apples. She ate some and took some back to the barn to share with her friends. All the animals in the barn enjoyed the tasty apples and were very happy. Pippa gained many friends because she shared the tasty apples with them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sue love to ride their bikes to the beach. They like to see the big water and the sand. They also like to play with their red ball and make castles. One day, they see some big waves on the water. They are called tide. Tom and Sue are curious. They want to go closer and see the tide. They leave their bikes and their ball and walk to the water. "Look, Sue, the tide is coming and going. It is like a game," Tom says. "Yes, Tom, it is fun. Let's throw some stones and see how far they go," Sue says. They throw some stones and watch them splash. They laugh and clap. They are glad. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is their dad. He is calling them. "Tom! Sue! Come back! The tide is too strong. It is not safe. Hurry!" Tom and Sue are scared. They drop their stones and run to their dad. He hugs them and tells them they are OK. "Sorry, dad. We did not know. We just wanted to see the tide," Tom says. "I know, son. But the tide can be dangerous. You have to be careful. Next time, stay with me or your mom. OK?" dad says. "OK, dad. We are sorry," Sue says. Dad smiles and takes them to their bikes and their ball. He says they can still play on the sand, but not too close to the water. Tom and Sue nod and say thank you. They are still glad. They love the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to go on the subway with his mom. They would ride the subway to the park and have a fun day. One day, while on the subway, Tim could hear a loud noise. It was a man who was angry. The angry man was yelling at another person. Tim felt scared, but his mom held his hand tight. Tim's mom talked to the angry man. She said nice words and soon, the angry man was not angry anymore. They all smiled and the subway ride was happy again. Tim learned that kind words can make people feel better.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Bobo was very hungry. He went to the kitchen to find something to eat. He saw a big, juicy steak on the table. Bobo was so happy! Bobo's friend, a little cat named Mimi, came into the kitchen. "Hurry, Bobo!" said Mimi. "Let's eat the steak together!" So, Bobo and Mimi sat down and started to eat the steak. But then, something unexpected happened. The steak began to move! It jumped off the table and ran away! Bobo and Mimi looked at each other in surprise. They laughed and said, "That was a very funny steak!" And they went to find something else to eat.
