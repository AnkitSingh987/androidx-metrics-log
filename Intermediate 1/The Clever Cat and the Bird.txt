Category: Intermediate
Title: The Clever Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat. The cat had a name. Her name was Kitty. Kitty liked to make things with her paws. She made toys and games for her friends. Kitty was very happy. One day, Kitty made a new friend. Her friend was a little bird. The bird had a name too. His name was Chirpy. Chirpy liked to sing and make music. Kitty and Chirpy played together every day. Kitty and Chirpy were very clever. They made a big cake for all their friends. All the animals came to eat the cake. They had a big party. Everyone was happy and had fun. And Kitty and Chirpy were the best of friends.

---

One day, a little cat named Tom went for a walk. He walked to the east where the sun comes up. He saw a big tree with a bird in it. The bird looked down at Tom and said, "Hi!" Tom looked up and said, "Hi!" But then Tom saw that the bird had a frown on its face. "Why are you sad?" asked Tom. The bird said, "I lost my toy. I am sorry I can't play with you." Tom wanted to help the bird feel better. So, he looked around for the toy. He found it under a bush near the tree. Tom gave the toy back to the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Now, the bird did not frown anymore. They played together all day long. The bird was not sorry anymore, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat. The cat liked to play all day. One day, the cat found a piece of paper on the floor. The paper was big and white. The cat wanted to play with the paper. The cat jumped on the paper and it made a loud noise. The noise was so loud that it scared the cat. The cat ran away, but then it stopped and looked back at the paper. The cat was brave and went back to the paper. It played with the paper all day and was not scared anymore. The cat had a lot of fun with the big white paper. And from that day on, the little orange cat and the paper became best friends.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He was eager to see something new. He walked and walked until he found a wild place with tall trees and big rocks. In the wild place, Tim saw a little bird. He gazed at it as it flew around. Suddenly, the bird stopped flying and landed on a rock. Tim went closer to look at the bird. As he got closer, something unexpected happened. The bird changed into a big, friendly dog! Tim was very happy and played with the dog all day. They became best friends and went home together.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some corn for dinner. Tim was very excited because he loved corn. It was his favorite food. At the store, Tim saw a weak, old man trying to carry a big bag of corn. The old man looked tired and sad. Tim wanted to help him, so he said, "Let me help you with that, sir." The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, little boy." Tim carried the big bag of corn for the old man. They walked together to the checkout line. The old man was very happy because Tim was so kind. Tim's mom was proud of him too. They all had a good day at the store.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful puppy named Max. Max loved to joke and play with the kids in the park. He would run, jump, and wag his tail. The kids loved Max and always had fun with him. One sunny day, Max was playing with his friends in the park. They played catch and laughed a lot. Max was really good at making his friends smile. But then, something unexpected happened. A big ball rolled into the park, and Max ran to get it. As Max picked up the ball with his mouth, he noticed it was not a ball at all. It was a big, round, and shiny egg! Max's friends were surprised too. They all looked at the egg and wondered where it came from. Max decided to take care of the egg. He was very helpful and made sure the egg was safe and warm. After a few days, the egg started to crack. Max and his friends watched in amazement as a baby dragon came out of the egg! The baby dragon was very friendly and loved to joke just like Max. They all became best friends and played together every day. The kids in the park learned that dragons can be helpful and fun too, just like puppies. And they all lived happily ever after.
