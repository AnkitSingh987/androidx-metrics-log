Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Lily. She was very enthusiastic. She liked to play with her ball. One day, she missed her ball. She was sad. Lily looked for her ball. She went to the park. She went to the store. She could not find it. Then, she saw a dog. The dog had her ball. Lily was happy. The dog was friendly. The dog's name was Max. Lily and Max played together. They had fun. But then, Max's owner came. He said Max had to go. Lily was sad. But then, the owner said Lily could come play with Max any time. Lily was happy again.

Tim and Mia were best friends. They liked to play in the park and share their snacks. One day, they saw a big balloon man in the park. He was making funny shapes with balloons. He made a dog, a hat, a flower and a sword. "Wow, look at him!" Tim said. "He is so good with balloons. I admire him." "Me too," Mia said. "Can we get a balloon too?" They ran to the balloon man and asked him for a balloon. The balloon man smiled and said, "Sure, kids. What do you want?" "I want a dog," Tim said. "I want a flower," Mia said. The balloon man made a dog and a flower for them. He gave them the balloons and said, "Here you go. Enjoy your balloons. But be careful, don't let them pop." "Thank you, balloon man," Tim and Mia said. They were very happy with their balloons. They hugged them and ran back to the bench where their moms were waiting. "Look, mom, look!" Tim and Mia said. "We got balloons from the balloon man. Aren't they pretty?" Their moms smiled and said, "Yes, they are very pretty. But don't hold them too tight, or they will pop." Tim and Mia nodded. They sat on the bench and played with their balloons. They made them talk and dance and kiss. They had a lot of fun. But then, something bad happened. A big wind came and blew their balloons away. Tim and Mia tried to catch them, but they were too fast. They watched as their balloons flew higher and higher in the sky, until they disappeared. "NOOOO!" Tim and Mia cried. "Our balloons! Our balloons are gone!" They felt very sad and angry. They stomped their feet and threw a tantrum. They blamed the wind, the balloon man, and each other. They said mean things to each other. "You let go of your balloon!" Tim said. "No, you did!" Mia said. "You are a bad friend!" Tim said. "No, you are!" Mia said. They stopped being friends. They turned their backs on each other and crossed their arms. They felt a tight feeling in their stomachs. They wished they had their balloons back. They wished they had their friend back. But it was too late. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of steel. It was a strong and shiny toy car. Tim loved his toy car very much. He played with it every day. One day, Tim's toy car got stuck under a big rock. Tim felt helpless. He could not move the rock to get his car. He wanted his toy car back very much. Tim was sad. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw him and wanted to help. She was strong and could move the rock. Sue pushed the rock away and got the toy car back for Tim. Tim was so happy! He hugged his sister and thanked her. From that day on, Tim and Sue played together with the steel toy car, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a humble little girl named Daisy. Daisy had a favourite thing to do, which was to pick spaghetti. Every day she would pick the long noodles and set them down in a pile. One day, Daisy found that someone had been picking the spaghetti too! She was so angry she shouted, "Who has been picking my spaghetti?!" But no one answered. Daisy decided she would try to find the spaghetti picker. So she went searching. She looked in all the possible places and eventually she found them! It was a family of rabbits, picking the spaghetti with their little paws. Daisy was so surprised! She asked them kindly, "Please stop picking my spaghetti!" They all looked at her and apologised. The family of rabbits offered to help Daisy clean up the mess that they had made. Daisy was so pleased with their kind offer and accepted their help. Together, they worked to put all the spaghetti into a neat pile. And from then on, everyone agreed to pick only their own spaghetti. The end.
