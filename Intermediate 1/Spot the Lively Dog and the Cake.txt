Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Lively Dog and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot had lots of hair. He loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big, yummy cake on the table. He wanted to eat it. Spot tried to resist, but he could not. He jumped on the table and ate the whole cake. After eating the cake, Spot felt sick. His tummy hurt a lot. Spot's mom was sad and mad. The cake was not for him. Spot learned not to eat things that are not his.

Once upon a time, in a big river, there was a purple hippo. He was a very friendly hippo. All the animals liked him. One day, the purple hippo saw a small bird. The bird was sad. "What's wrong?" asked the hippo. The bird said, "I lost my home in the big wind. Can you help me find a new one?" The purple hippo wanted to help his new friend. He said, "I will request help from the other animals to find you a new home." The bird was happy. The hippo talked to the other animals. They all worked together to build a new home for the bird. The bird was very grateful and said, "Thank you, purple hippo and friends!" They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to cook. She would make yummy food for her family. One day, Lily saw a big kitchen with many people. They were the staff. They cooked food for lots of people. Lily went to the staff and said, "Can I cook with you?" The staff smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Let's cook together." So, Lily and the staff cooked a big meal. The food was very cool and yummy. Everyone loved it. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can make something great. Lily and the staff made many people happy with their cool and yummy food.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big jar. He loved his jar very much. One day, Tim found a small toy car. He thought it would be fun to attach the car to his jar. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Do you think attaching the car to the jar is a good idea?" Sam looked serious and said, "No, Tim. The jar might break if the car is too heavy." Tim didn't listen to Sam. He attached the car to the jar and started to play. Suddenly, the jar broke. Tim was sad. He learned that he should have listened to his friend. The moral of the story is: Always listen to your friends when they are serious.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. A little boy named Tim liked to lean on the tree. One day, he found a shiny rock under the tree. Tim did not know the value of the shiny rock. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam was sorry, but he did not know the value of the shiny rock too. Tim and Sam went to ask their friend, Lily. Lily said she knew the value of the shiny rock. She said it was worth a lot. Tim and Sam were happy. They wanted to buy toys with the shiny rock. Lily said she would help them. But Lily was not a good friend. She took the shiny rock and ran away. Tim and Sam were very sad. They lost the shiny rock and their friend. They learned to be careful with their things and who they trust. The end was bad and they did not get any toys.

Anna and Ben are playing with their cars in the living room. They like to make noises and pretend they are racing. Anna has a red car and Ben has a blue car. They zoom around the carpet and the sofa. "Look at me, I'm the fastest!" Anna says, as she makes her car go very fast. "No, I'm the fastest!" Ben says, as he makes his car go faster. They both want to win the race, so they rush to the other side of the room. They don't see the wall in front of them. They are careless and don't look where they are going. CRASH! Their cars hit the wall and fall to the floor. Anna and Ben are shocked and scared. They hear their mom coming. "What was that noise?" Mom asks, as she enters the living room. She sees the cars and the wall. She is not happy. "Anna, Ben, what did you do?" Mom says, as she picks up the cars. "You were careless and you damaged the wall. Look, there is a dent and a scratch. You have to be more careful when you play. You could have hurt yourselves or broken something." Anna and Ben feel sorry and ashamed. They know they did something wrong. "We're sorry, Mom," they say, as they hug her. "We won't rush and be careless again. We'll look where we are going and play nicely." Mom hugs them back and smiles. She is glad they are okay and they learned their lesson. "Okay, I forgive you," she says, as she puts the cars away. "But next time, please be more careful and respectful of the house. Now, let's go and have some snack. I made some cookies for you." Anna and Ben nod and follow Mom to the kitchen. They still like to play with their cars, but they don't rush and be careless anymore. They play more safely and happily.
