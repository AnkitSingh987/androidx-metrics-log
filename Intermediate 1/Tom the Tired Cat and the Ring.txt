Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Tired Cat and the Ring

One day, a tired cat named Tom was walking down the road. He saw a big red vehicle. It had a loud ring sound. Tom was scared of the sound. He wanted to cross the road, but the vehicle was in the way. Tom said to the vehicle, "Please move, I want to go home." The vehicle did not move. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. He needed to find a way to get past the vehicle. Then, Tom saw a friendly dog named Max. Max wanted to help Tom. Max said, "I will make the vehicle move for you." Max barked very loud at the vehicle. The vehicle moved away, and Tom was able to cross the road. Tom was so happy and thanked Max. They became good friends and played together every day. The tired cat was not scared of the big red vehicle with the loud ring sound anymore.

One day, a weak lion was walking in the woods. He saw a pipe on the ground. He picked it up and started to play with it. The lion blew into the pipe but it did not make a sound. He wanted to make a big roar with the pipe, but it was too weak. Then, a small bird flew by and saw the lion with the pipe. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Lion! What are you doing with that pipe?" The lion said, "I want to make a big roar, but the pipe is too weak." The bird had an idea. "Maybe if we work together, we can make the pipe roar," said the bird. The lion liked the idea. So, the bird held one end of the pipe and the lion held the other end. They both blew into the pipe as hard as they could. Suddenly, the pipe made a loud roar! The lion and the bird were very happy. They didn't know that the pipe was magic. The magic pipe made the lion strong and his roar was now the loudest in the woods. The lion and the bird became best friends and played with the magic pipe every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy and her loyal dog, Buddy. They loved to play together every day. One day, they found a plastic ball in the yard. Buddy recognized the ball and wanted to play with it. Amy threw the ball and Buddy ran after it. They played with the ball all day long. They laughed and had so much fun. When they got tired, they sat under a big tree and shared a yummy snack. As they sat, Amy said to Buddy, "You are my best friend." Buddy wagged his tail and gave Amy a big, wet kiss. They knew they would always be best friends and play together forever. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a pale crab named Tom went to the beach. The tide was coming in and the water was getting high. Tom saw a big hole in the sand and wanted to fill it with water. Tom called his friend, a little fish named Sam. "Sam, can you help me fill this hole with water?" asked Tom. Sam said, "Yes, let's do it together!" They worked hard to fill the hole. The tide came in more and more. Tom and Sam played in the water and had fun. They were happy they filled the hole and could swim in it.

Once upon a time, in a small cabin in the woods, there lived a soft bunny. The soft bunny loved to sleep all day long. The cabin was warm and cozy, and the soft bunny felt safe there. One day, the soft bunny woke up and went outside to play. He played with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. They had so much fun, but soon the soft bunny felt tired. He said goodbye to his friends and went back to his cabin to sleep. But when the soft bunny got back to his cabin, he found that a big, scary bear had moved in. The bear did not want to share the cabin with the soft bunny. The soft bunny tried to find a new place to sleep, but he could not find one. The soft bunny was very sad and did not sleep well that night.
