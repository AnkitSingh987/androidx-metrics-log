Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Ben and the Ball

Lila and Ben were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. But today, the water was full of jellyfish. They were pink and purple and had long tentacles. Mom and dad said they could sting and hurt. "Stay away from the jellyfish, kids," mom said. "They are not friendly." Lila and Ben nodded, but they were restless. They wanted to swim and have fun. They saw some other kids playing with a ball and a bucket. They decided to join them. "Hi, can we play with you?" Lila asked. "Sure, we are making a sandcastle," one of the kids said. "You can help us." Lila and Ben smiled and grabbed some shovels and molds. They started to pile up the sand and shape it into a castle. They added shells and seaweed for decoration. They were having a good time. Suddenly, they heard a loud shout. They looked up and saw one of the kids running out of the water. He was crying and holding his leg. "Ow, ow, ow, a jellyfish stung me!" he wailed. Mom and dad ran to him and checked his leg. It was red and swollen. They poured some water and vinegar on it and wrapped it with a towel. "Don't worry, it will be okay," dad said. "But we have to go to the doctor." The other kids looked scared and sad. They said goodbye to Lila and Ben and packed their things. Lila and Ben felt sorry for the kid. They also felt glad that they listened to mom and dad and stayed away from the jellyfish. "Jellyfish are bad," Lila said. "Yes, they are," Ben agreed. They hugged mom and dad and thanked them for keeping them safe. They finished their sandcastle and put a flag on top. They hoped the jellyfish would go away soon. They wanted to swim and have fun again.

One day, a red bird named Tom saw a long rod. The rod was on the ground. Tom wanted to play with the rod, so he picked it up. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. He said, "Max, come play with me and the rod!" Max came running to Tom. Max liked to play with the rod too. They played with the red rod all day. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Tom and Max went home. They knew they would play with the red rod again soon.

One day, a boy named Tim had a waffle. He wanted to use syrup on it. His mom, a dependable lady, always had syrup in the kitchen. Tim was happy. "Mom, can I have syrup for my waffle?" Tim asked. Mom looked sad. "I am sorry, Tim. We have no syrup today." Tim was sad too. He did not want to eat his waffle without syrup. Tim went to his friend, Sue. Sue was good at finding things. Sue said, "I will help you find syrup." They looked everywhere. No syrup. Then, they saw a big tree. It was a maple tree. Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's use this tree to get syrup!" Tim and Sue got a little jar and took some syrup from the tree. They were very happy. They did not know a tree could give them syrup. Now, Tim could eat his waffle with syrup.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved looking at the stars at night. One night, she saw a black star in the sky. It was the first time she had ever seen a black star. She was very surprised. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, why is that star black?" Her mom said, "That star is black because it is sad. It needs our love to shine bright again." Lily wanted to help the black star, so she closed her eyes and sent love to the star. The next night, Lily looked for the black star again. To her surprise, the black star was now shining bright! It was no longer black. The star had learned that it was loved, and that made it happy. Lily was so happy too. The moral of the story is that love can make things better and brighter, even when they seem sad and dark. And sometimes, a little love can make the unexpected happen.
