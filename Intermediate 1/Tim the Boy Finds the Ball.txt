Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Ball

One day, a little boy named Tim went on an adventurous walk. He wanted to find a big, red ball he lost in the park. Tim looked everywhere! He looked under the slide, behind the bench, and even in the sandbox. Then, Tim met a girl named Sue. She had the big, red ball in her hand. Tim said, "That's my ball! Can I have it back, please?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, here you go!" She gave the ball to Tim. Tim was very happy to have his ball back. He thanked Sue and they played together in the park. After a fun day, Tim returned home with his big, red ball. He couldn't wait to go on another adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy liked to sing. He sang all day long. He sang when he was happy, and he sang when he was sad. One day, Billy found a folder. The folder was very pretty. It was red and shiny. Billy thought the folder was expensive. He wanted to keep it safe, so he put it in his nest. Billy was so happy with his new folder that he sang a special song. All the other birds heard it and came to see the expensive folder. They all liked it too. They all sang together with Billy, and they became good friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a small boat near the lake. He wanted to store it in a safe place. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him. Tim said, "Sam, can you help me store the boat? We need to keep it safe." Sam nodded and said, "Yes, Tim! Let's put it in the shed." They carried the boat to the shed and put it inside. They felt happy that the boat was now safe. They went to play near the lake, knowing the boat was stored away. The next day, they took the boat out of the shed and went to the lake. They played with the boat, and it floated on the water. They had so much fun with their safe boat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going to a fun park with his mom. Tim was very happy because he had a ticket for a big ride. But Tim felt a little uncomfortable because he had never been on a big ride before. At the fun park, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I don't want to go on the big ride. It's too scary!" Tim didn't want to quit, so he said, "I will go on the big ride. I'm not scared!" Tim went on the big ride with his ticket. The ride was very fast and Tim felt very uncomfortable. He cried and wanted to quit, but the ride didn't stop. When the ride was over, Tim was sad and scared. He didn't want to go on any more rides. Tim's mom said, "It's okay to be scared. Next time, listen to your feelings and don't do something that makes you uncomfortable." The moral of the story is to listen to your feelings and don't do things that make you uncomfortable.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very thirsty. She went to the mysterious pond near her house to get some water. She saw a big frog sitting by the pond. The frog was green and bumpy. "Hi, frog," Sue said. "Can I have some water from the pond?" The frog looked at Sue and said, "Yes, you can have some water, but you must rest here with me first." So Sue sat down next to the frog and they both rested by the pond. They watched the water and saw little fish swimming. They listened to the birds singing in the trees. After they had rested, Sue got some water from the pond. She drank it and felt much better. She thanked the frog and went back home. Sue and the frog became good friends and often rested by the mysterious pond together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little bug. The bug liked to crawl all day. One day, the bug saw a tiny flame. The flame was slow and pretty. The bug liked the flame a lot. The bug wanted to be friends with the flame. The bug crawled closer to the flame. The bug said, "Hi, flame! Can we be friends?" The flame did not say anything. The bug was sad. Then, the flame started to move. The flame was not a flame at all! It was a tiny red bug that looked like a flame. The red bug said, "Hi! I am not a flame. I am a bug like you. Let's be friends!" And so, the two bugs were happy and played together all day.
