Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car and the Frog

One day, a big car had a flat tire. The driver was sad. He wanted to fix the tire, but he did not know how. He looked around for help. It was a hot day and the sun was very bright. Nearby, a frog was playing near a pond. The frog saw the man and the car. The frog liked to splash in the water. He thought it was fun. The frog had an idea. He hopped over to the man and the car. The frog said, "I can help you with your tire!" The man did not think a frog could help. But he let the frog try. The frog splashed water on the tire. It was a magic splash! The tire was fixed! The man was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a disgusting bug! The man was surprised. He did not like bugs. But he said, "Thank you, bug, for fixing my tire." The bug smiled and went back to the pond. The man drove away in his car, happy that his tire was fixed.

One day, a lively cat named Tom saw a big box. The box had yarn in it. Tom loved to play with yarn. So, he jumped on the box and tried to open it. But the box was very hard to open. Tom asked his friend, the dog named Max, to help him open the box. "Max, please help me open this box," Tom said. Max came over and tried to open the box too. They pushed and pulled, but the box would not open. They were sad. Then, a little bird flew by and saw them. The bird had a key in its beak. The bird put the key in the box and turned it. The box opened! But instead of yarn, there were many colorful balloons inside. The balloons flew up into the sky, and Tom, Max, and the bird laughed and played with the balloons all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and read his picture newspaper. One day, he found a big, dead bug in his room. Tim was scared and didn't know what to do. Tim had an idea! He took his newspaper and rolled it up. He wanted to make the bug go away. Tim got ready to strike the dead bug. But just as he was about to hit it, something unexpected happened. The bug jumped up and started to dance! Tim was so surprised. He didn't know that the bug was only pretending to be dead. The bug and Tim became friends, and they played together all day. From that day on, Tim wasn't scared of bugs anymore.

Today was a very busy day for Tom. Tom was packing his things into his blue bag. He had a lot to do today. He was going to go on a long journey. He put a few things in his bag and then took it up to his mom. His mom said, "You have packed a lot today, Tom! Is that all you need for your journey?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes! I'm ready for my trip". Tom had a blue lunchbox too, he wanted to make sure he had something good to eat on his journey. He also packed a favourite toy he had made himself - a blue car. He got everything ready and then said goodbye to his mom. Tom was very excited to take his journey. He was ready to see what the world held for him. He waved to his mom, who waved right back and said, "Have a great adventure, Tom!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys all day. One day, he saw his mom cleaning the house. She had a newspaper in her hand. Tim wanted to help her. Tim took the empty box from the corner and put all his toys inside. Then, he helped his mom clean the house. They used the newspaper to make the windows shine. Tim felt happy to help his mom. After cleaning, Tim and his mom sat down to rest. They looked around their clean house and smiled. Tim learned that helping others can make him feel good. From that day, Tim helped his mom clean the house every week. They became the best team ever.
