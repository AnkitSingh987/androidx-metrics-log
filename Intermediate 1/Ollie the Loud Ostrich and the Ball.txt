Category: Intermediate
Title: Ollie the Loud Ostrich and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a big open field, there was a loud ostrich named Ollie. Ollie loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Ollie found a big red ball stuck in a tree. He wanted to remove the ball from the tree so he could play with it. Ollie tried to jump and reach the ball, but he could not. He asked his friends, the small bird and the big elephant, to help him. They all worked together to remove the ball from the tree. They pushed and pulled until the ball was free. After they removed the ball, Ollie and his friends played happily together. They learned that when they work together, they can do anything. The moral of the story is that teamwork makes hard tasks easier.

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tim. Tim was a fragile little boy who loved to play with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends were playing outside when they saw a big, scary dog. The dog was running fast, and it looked like it wanted to catch them. Tim and his friends knew they had to escape. They ran as fast as they could, but the dog was still behind them. They saw a tall tree and decided to climb it. The dog couldn't climb trees, so they knew they would be safe up there. As they climbed, the branches got thinner and more fragile. Tim was scared, but he knew he had to keep going. Finally, they reached the top of the tree, and the dog couldn't reach them. They were safe! Tim and his friends waited until the dog went away, and then they climbed back down. They were happy they escaped and knew they would be more careful when playing outside from now on.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to go to the river near his house. The river had many fish and Tim loved to watch them swim. One sunny day, Tim went to the river, but it looked empty. He did not see any fish. Tim was sad and did not know where the fish had gone. He sat by the river and waited for the fish to come back. After some time, the fish came back to the river. Tim was very happy to see them again. He played near the river and watched the fish swim. He knew that the river was not empty anymore. Tim went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a lonely girl named Lucy. She had no friends to play with. One day, she decided to go on a journey to find a friend. As Lucy walked, she saw a big tree. Under the tree was a sad cat. The cat had a frown on its face. Lucy asked the cat, "Why are you sad?" The cat said, "I am lonely, too. I have no friends." Lucy smiled and said, "Let's be friends and go on a journey together!" The cat's frown turned into a big smile. Together, they walked and played. They were not lonely anymore. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a lovely cat named Bella went for a walk. She saw a big bin near a tree. Bella was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the bin. As she got closer, she saw a little bird stuck in the bin. The little bird was scared and yelled for help. Bella wanted to help the bird, so she thought of a plan. Bella jumped on the bin and pushed it with all her strength. The bin fell over and the little bird was free. The bird was so happy and thanked Bella for her help. From that day on, Bella and the little bird were the best of friends.

Sara liked to play in the garden with her dog, Spot. She liked to look for bugs and flowers and rocks. One day, she saw a big bush with red berries. She wanted to pick some for her mom. She ran to the bush and knelt down. She reached for a berry, but Spot barked. He smelled something smelly behind the bush. He wanted to warn Sara. Sara did not listen. She thought Spot was being silly. She grabbed a berry and put it in her mouth. It was sour and bitter. She spat it out. She felt a sting on her tongue. She looked at the bush and saw a bee. The bee was angry. It had stung Sara. Sara cried. She ran to her mom. Her mom hugged her and gave her some ice. She told her the bush was bad. It had poison berries and bees. She said Sara should always listen to Spot. He was a good dog. Sara said she was sorry. She thanked Spot for trying to help her. She gave him a pat and a treat. She learned her lesson. She never ate from the bush again.
