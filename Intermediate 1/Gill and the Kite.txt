Category: Intermediate
Title: Gill and the Kite

Gill was playing in the park. His kite was flying high and he was happy. Suddenly, he saw a board on the ground. He picked it up and immediately started to play. He imagined it was a boat and he was a captain sailing on the waves. Suddenly, he heard a voice. It was Mrs. Barker and she didn't look happy. "What are you doing?" she said. Gill panicked, but he tried to explain. He said, "I'm just playing, Mrs. Barker." Mrs. Barker said, "That board belongs to me. I hope you put it back where you found it." Gill nodded and said, "I'm sorry Mrs. Barker, I'll put it back." Mrs. Barker sighed and said, "I hope you learn to respect other people's belongings." Gill put down the board and sadly walked away. He wished he could fly away like his kite up in the sky, so high.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Molly. She was only three years old. One Saturday morning, Molly wanted to help Mommy clean the house. Mommy gave Molly a dusting cloth and said, “Let’s start with that chair over there.” Molly was excited and she started to take the dust off the chair. She brushed the cloth all around the chair and danced around it, but the dust just wouldn’t go away. Every time she took the dust off, more dust came back! Molly started to feel frustrated. She looked up at Mommy with big, sad eyes and said, “Mommy, I can’t take the dust away!” Mommy smiled and said, “That’s okay, Molly. Dusting is not an easy job. Let’s just focus on getting the dust off the chair and we’ll finish the rest of the house later.” Molly smiled and said, “Okay, Mommy!” She took her dusting cloth and went back to work. With a lot of patience, the dust on the chair eventually disappeared. Molly was so happy that she did it!

Once upon a time, there was a fancy bird. The bird had pretty colors all over. It was happy to fly and play with its friends. They liked to sing and dance in the trees. One day, the fancy bird hurt its leg. It could not fly or play with its friends. It was sad and alone. The bird sat on a branch and started to complain. It said, "My leg hurts. I want to play with my friends." A kind squirrel heard the bird complain. The squirrel wanted to help the bird feel better. It brought some leaves and wrapped them around the bird's leg. The bird said, "Thank you for helping me." The fancy bird's leg got better. It could fly and play with its friends again. They all sang and danced in the trees. The bird was happy and did not complain anymore. The bird and the squirrel became good friends. They played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia wore her favorite mittens because it was cold outside. They were not expensive mittens, but Mia loved them very much. In the park, Mia saw a big tree with many leaves. Mia saw a squirrel on the tree. She said, "Hello, squirrel! Do you like my mittens?" The squirrel looked at Mia's mittens and nodded its head. Mia was happy that the squirrel liked her mittens. Mia played in the park with the squirrel. They ran and jumped, and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Mia waved goodbye to the squirrel. She knew she would come back to the park to play with her new friend again.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Tom. Tom was very sleepy. He yawned a big yawn. Tom saw a prize in the park. It was a big, red ball. Tom wanted the prize. He walked to the park. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with the big, red ball. Tom said, "Can I play too?" Sue was a nice girl. She said, "Yes, Tom! Let's play together!" Tom and Sue played with the big, red ball. They had lots of fun. They shared the prize and were happy friends.
