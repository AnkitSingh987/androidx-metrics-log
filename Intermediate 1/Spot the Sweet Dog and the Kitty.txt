Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Sweet Dog and the Kitty

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little dog named Spot. Spot had a big itch on his back. He tried to rub it, but his paws were too short. Spot felt sad because he could not reach the itch. One day, Spot met a new friend named Kitty. Kitty had soft paws and a long tail. Spot asked Kitty, "Can you help me with my itch? I can't rub it." Kitty said, "Yes, I can help you, Spot." Kitty used her soft paws to rub Spot's itch. Spot felt so happy that his itch was gone. Spot and Kitty became best friends. They played together every day and helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a big building called a university. Many people went there to learn and play. One day, a girl named Lucy found a broken toy in the play area. She wanted to fix it so she could play with it. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, for help. Tom was good at fixing broken things. They worked together to fix the broken toy. They used glue and tape to make it look like new again. After they fixed the toy, they wanted to play with it at the university. But first, they had to pay a coin to use the play area. Lucy and Tom shared their coins and paid to play. They had a fun day playing with their fixed toy at the university.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion lived in a forest with many friends. The lion was very thoughtful. He always helped his friends when they needed him. One day, the lion and his friend, a little bird, were playing near a tree. The lion was running and accidentally stepped on the bird's wing. The bird was hurt and started to cry. The lion felt very bad and said sorry to the bird. The bird knew that the lion was a thoughtful friend and did not mean to hurt her. So, she decided to forgive the lion. They hugged and continued to play together. From that day on, the lion and the bird were even better friends. They learned that forgiving each other made their friendship stronger.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. In the stadium, there was a fierce lion. The lion liked to run and play all day. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the stadium. He saw the lion and wanted to touch it. Tim said to the lion, "Hi, lion! Can I touch you?" The lion looked at Tim and nodded. Tim reached out and touched the lion's soft fur. It felt nice and warm. They played together in the stadium all day. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Bye, lion! I will come back and play with you again!" The lion roared happily, and Tim went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a big metal box. The box was in the park. All the kids played with it. But they did not know what was inside the box. They were ignorant about it. One day, a wise old man came to the park. He saw the kids playing with the box. He told them to not open the box. He said, "You must remain happy with what you see outside. Do not open the box." The kids listened to the old man. They kept playing with the box. They did not open it. They remained happy and safe. The wise old man was happy too. The moral of the story is to listen to wise words and be happy with what you have.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was eager to walk to the big park. He wanted to learn about history. He asked his mom if they could go together. On their walk, Tim saw a big tree. His mom told him that the tree had a long history. It had been there for many years. Tim liked the tree and thought it was very special. They walked some more and found a big rock. The rock had a history too. It came from a tall mountain far away. Tim was happy to learn about the history of the tree and the rock. He loved walking with his mom and learning new things.
