Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the big sink in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to help her wash the dishes. Tim felt scared but he wanted to be a big boy and help his mom. Tim's mom showed him how to wash the dishes and put them in the sink. She told him not to be scared, because the sink was not scary. While they were washing the dishes, Tim's mom sent him to get more soap. Tim went to get the soap and came back to the sink. He wasn't scared anymore. He washed the dishes with his mom and they had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim was never scared of the big sink again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big race. Many people came to see the race. They were all very excited. One little boy, Tom, was very upset because he wanted to be in the race too. But he was too young. Tom's mom saw that he was upset. She said, "Tom, don't be upset. You can display your toy cars and have your own race at home." Tom liked the idea and smiled. Tom and his mom went home and displayed the toy cars. They started the race, but then something bad happened. Tom's toy car broke during the race. Tom was upset again and cried. His mom tried to fix the toy, but she couldn't. The race ended sadly for Tom.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, yellow ball near the slide. Tim thought it was a boring ball, but he wanted to play with it anyway. He kicked the ball and it went far away. Tim ran after the ball, but it was too fast. Tim started to search for the yellow ball. He looked behind the swings, under the slide, and next to the sandbox. But he could not find the ball anywhere. Tim felt sad because he wanted to play with the yellow ball. Then, Tim saw a little girl named Lily. She was holding the yellow ball! Tim asked if he could play with her and the ball. Lily said yes, and they played together. They had so much fun that Tim forgot the ball was boring. They played until it was time to go home. Tim and Lily were both happy because they found a new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to watch his favorite cartoon. He turned on the TV and saw the funny characters dancing and singing. Tim was so happy, he began to dance too. Suddenly, the TV made a loud noise and the cartoon stopped. Tim was scared. He called his mom and said, "Mommy, the TV is broken and I am scared!" His mom came to see what happened. Tim's mom looked at the TV and saw that it was just a commercial. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. The cartoon will be back soon. Let's dance until it comes back." Tim and his mom danced together and he was not scared anymore. They laughed and had fun until the cartoon came back on.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a puzzle. She wanted to play with it. But her big brother Tom did not want to play. He said he hates puzzles. Mia was sad. Mia played with the puzzle by herself. She saw a graceful bird on the puzzle. She liked the bird. She wanted Tom to see it too. So, she took the puzzle to Tom. When Tom saw the bird, he liked it. He did not hate the puzzle anymore. He helped Mia finish the puzzle. They found a secret map behind the puzzle. Mia and Tom were happy and went on an adventure together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a nice room with a big closet. One day, Tim heard a small voice coming from the closet. "Hi Tim, come play with me!" said the voice. Tim was scared, but he opened the closet door. Inside, he found a little mouse. The mouse smiled at Tim and said, "Don't be scared, I just want to be your friend." Tim and the mouse played together in the closet. The mouse would encourage Tim to try new games and have fun. They had a great time together. One day, Tim's mom found the mouse in the closet. She was very surprised! She told Tim that the mouse needed to go back outside to its family. Tim was sad, but he knew it was the right thing to do. Tim and his mom took the mouse outside and said goodbye. The mouse thanked Tim for being a nice friend and promised to visit. Tim smiled, knowing he had helped the mouse and made a new friend.
