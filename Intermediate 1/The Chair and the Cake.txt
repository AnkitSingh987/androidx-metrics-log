Category: Intermediate
Title: The Chair and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a small chair. It was red and blue. The small chair lived in a big room with many other chairs. But the small chair was special. It had a big smile on its face. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the small chair and said, "Hello, small chair! You are so cute! Let's be friends!" The small chair was very happy. It had never had a friend before. Lily and the small chair played all day. They laughed and had fun. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's celebrate our new friendship!" So, Lily and the small chair had a party. They danced and ate cake. They were very happy. And from that day on, Lily and the small chair were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thin dog. The dog had no owner. He was very sad. One day, the dog met a girl. The girl was kind. "Hello, dog," said the girl. "Do you have an owner?" The dog shook his head. The girl smiled. "I will be your owner," she said. The dog was happy. The girl gave the dog a bone. The dog received the bone. The dog and the girl were friends. They played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly family. They had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Sally. They lived in a small house with their pet dog, Max. One sunny day, the family went to the park. Sally and Max ran and played on the grass. They found a twisty slide. Sally climbed up and Max waited at the bottom. When Sally went down the slide, she would twist and turn. Max would wag his tail and bark happily. The jolly family had a fun day at the park. They played on the twisty slide, laughed, and ate yummy snacks. As the sun set, the family went home with big smiles on their faces. They knew they would have more fun days together.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to school. In her class, there was a big blackboard. Sue loved to draw on the blackboard with her friends. They drew cats, dogs, and houses. It was a lot of fun. One day, Sue's friend, Tim, found a new chalk. It was a special chalk that could freeze things. Tim said, "Look, Sue! I found a harmless chalk that can freeze things. Let's try it!" Sue was excited and wanted to see what would happen. Sue took the chalk and drew a big sun on the blackboard. Suddenly, the sun came to life and started shining very bright. But then, the sun froze! It was not hot anymore. Sue and Tim were surprised. They didn't know what to do. Then, Sue had an idea. She drew a big warm coat around the sun. The sun started to shine again and everything went back to normal. Sue and Tim learned to be careful with the special chalk.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom loved to eat yogurt every day. He liked to be strong to help his friends. One day, Tom went to the store to receive his yogurt. But when he got there, all the yogurt was gone! Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tom saw a big dog by the store. The big dog had a lot of yogurt! The dog gave some yogurt to Tom. Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog was a new friend, and they shared yogurt together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious crab. He liked to play hide and seek with his friends. They lived near the sea, where the water was blue and the sand was soft. One sunny day, the curious crab and his friends decided to play a game. They took turns to hide while the others tried to find them. The curious crab was very good at hiding. He found a big shell to hide under. His friends looked and looked for the curious crab, but they could not find him. They asked the fish and the starfish if they saw the crab. The fish and the starfish did not know where he was. Finally, the curious crab came out from under the big shell. His friends were so happy to see him! From that day on, the curious crab and his friends played hide and seek every day. They had lots of fun and always found new places to hide. The sea was filled with laughter and joy as the friends played together.
