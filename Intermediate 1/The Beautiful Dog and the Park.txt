Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beautiful Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful dog. The dog had a cane. The dog liked to play with the cane. He would enjoy running and jumping with the cane in his mouth. One day, the dog went to the park. He saw a big tree. The dog wanted to hide the cane under the tree. He dug a hole and put the cane in it. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. Later, the dog came back to the park. He wanted to play with the cane again. But when he dug up the hole, the cane was gone. The dog was very sad. He looked for the cane, but he could not find it. The dog went home without his cane, and he was not happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Tim. Tim loved to perform tricks for his friends in the big tree. One day, he saw a big branch with a yummy prune hanging from it. The prune was red and shiny. Tim wanted to eat the prune. Tim was very flexible, so he thought of a plan. He told his friends to watch him perform a new trick. He swung from branch to branch, getting closer to the prune. His friends clapped and cheered as he moved closer. Finally, Tim reached the branch with the prune. He grabbed it with his little hand and swung back to his friends. He shared the yummy prune with everyone. They all smiled and said, "Tim, your trick was the best!" Tim was happy that his plan worked and he could share the treat with his friends.

One day, a creative girl named Lucy went outside to play golf. She loved golf because it was fun to hit the ball and watch it fly. The sky was blue, and the sun was shining bright. As Lucy played golf, she saw big gray clouds coming. Soon, it started to rain. The rain made the grass wet, and Lucy's golf ball did not go as far. But Lucy liked the rain because it made her feel happy and cool. Lucy kept playing golf in the rain. She got wet, but she didn't care. She had so much fun playing golf in the rain, and she felt very creative. When the rain stopped, Lucy went home with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very anxious because he lost his ball. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Max was very sad and he wanted to scream. One day, Max saw a big cat named Tom. Tom had Max's ball! Max said, "Please give me my ball back!" But Tom did not want to give it back. Tom ran away with the ball, and Max chased him. They ran and ran until they saw a jail. The jail was for bad animals. Max was brave and he went inside the jail to get his ball. Tom was scared and did not go in. Max found his ball and came out. Tom said sorry and Max forgave him. They became friends and played together with the ball.

Once upon a time there were two friends - Bob and Dave. They were playing together in the park. Suddenly, Bob found a strange object on the ground. He picked it up and shouted, "What is this thing? It looks like a weapon!" Dave replied, "Be careful! Put it down!" Bob was very curious, so he grabbed the weapon. Instantly, it started to sparkle and vanish! Dave jumped back and said, "That was really hard!" The weapon disappeared completely and they never saw it again. Bob and Dave thought they were in big trouble. They looked at each other and then ran home as fast as they could. They never talked about the mysterious weapon again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a big bag with him. Tim was very glad because he loved to play at the park. In the bag, he had a big red ball to play with his friends. Tim and his friends played catch and had lots of fun. As they played, the sun started to rise high in the sky. It was getting very hot. Tim and his friends were getting tired, so they sat down to rest. They put the ball back in the bag and sat under a tree. They drank water and talked. Suddenly, the bag started to move! Tim and his friends were very surprised. They looked inside the bag, and there was a little puppy! The puppy had been hiding in the bag all along. Tim and his friends were so happy to meet the puppy. They played with the puppy all day and had even more fun!
