Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to park her bike. She especially liked to park in places that were colorful, like the beach. One day, the little girl decided to take a ride out to the park. When she arrived, it was snowing! She was so excited to be out in the snow and quickly parked her bike. The little girl was so busy having fun playing in the snow, she didn't notice the dark clouds in the sky. Suddenly, a big storm began and the snow started to fall harder. The little girl was in danger! She ran back to get her bike, but it was too late. The snow was so deep, she couldn't get to it. With no bike to take her home, the little girl was stuck. The little girl never found her way back home. The storm never stopped and she spent many cold days lost in the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot outside. Tim could feel the heat on his face. Tim wanted to buy a cold drink to cool down. He went to the store with his mom. At the store, Tim saw a cheap, cold drink. He asked his mom, "Can I have this drink, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can have it." They went home and Tim drank his cold drink. It made him feel better in the heat. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, mom!" They spent the rest of the day playing and having fun in the sun.

One day, a small, weak ant was walking near a big drain. The ant saw a yummy candy on the other side of the drain. The ant wanted to eat the candy, but it was scared to go near the drain. The drain was big and wet, and the ant was too weak to jump over it. A kind, strong bird saw the little ant. The bird wanted to help the ant get the candy. So, the bird flew over the drain, picked up the candy with its beak, and gave it to the ant. The weak ant was very happy. It said, "Thank you, bird, for helping me get the candy!" The bird was glad it could help the ant. They both smiled and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a unique toy that she loved to play with. It was a small gold ball that her mom gave to her. She would always insist on taking it with her wherever she went. One day, Lily and her friend, Tom, went to the park to play. They played with the gold ball, and they had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The gold ball rolled away and was lost. Lily and Tom looked everywhere, but they could not find it. They were very sad. Just then, a kind squirrel saw them and asked, "Why are you sad?" Lily said, "We lost my gold ball, and I love it so much." The squirrel wanted to help, so he went to look for the gold ball. Soon, he found it and brought it back to Lily. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, kind squirrel!" From that day on, Lily, Tom, and the squirrel became very good friends, and they always played together at the park.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some things for their home. Their list included a calm blue towel, some soap, and a bottle of lotion. At the store, Lily saw her friend Tim. Tim was sad because he could not find the lotion his mom wanted. Lily wanted to help her friend, so she said, "I know where the lotion is! Follow me." They walked to the lotion shelf together. When they got to the shelf, there was only one bottle of lotion left. Both Lily and Tim's moms needed it. Lily's mom said, "Let's share the lotion. We can both use it and be happy." Tim's mom agreed, and they all left the store with smiles on their faces.
