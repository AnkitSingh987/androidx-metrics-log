Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dog

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a spear. He liked to play with it every day. Tim had a pet dog, too. The dog's name was Max. Max was a very good dog. One day, Tim and Max went to the park. They saw a man with a big bag. The man was giving food to the birds. Tim wanted to help feed the birds, too. The man said, "Yes, you can help me." So, Tim and Max helped the man feed the birds. Tim was very happy to help. He used his spear to reach the food in the man's big bag. Max was happy, too. The birds were very happy to eat the food. The man said, "Thank you, Tim and Max." They all had a fun day at the park.

---

One day, a brown dog named Max went for a walk with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They walked to the park to play. Max loved to run and play with Lily. They saw some kids playing hockey on the ice. Max and Lily went closer to watch the hockey game. They heard the kids laughing and shouting. Max wanted to play hockey too, but he didn't know how. He looked at Lily and asked, "Can I play hockey?" Lily smiled and said, "You can try, Max!" She found a stick and gave it to Max. Max tried to hit the puck with the stick, but it was hard. The kids saw Max trying to play and laughed. One boy said, "Hey, let's help Max play hockey!" The kids showed Max how to play hockey. They all had fun playing together. Max was happy to learn something new. Lily clapped and cheered for her friend. After the game, they all went home, excited to play hockey again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big monster. The monster was very crazy. He liked to rush and run around the town. He was not mean, but he liked to play and have fun. One day, the monster found a big ball. He wanted to play with it. He kicked the ball and it went very far away. The monster decided to rush after the ball, running as fast as he could. As the monster chased the ball, he met a little girl. The girl was not scared of the monster. She thought he was funny. She asked the monster if she could play with him. They both laughed and played together, kicking the ball and rushing around the town. The crazy monster and the little girl became good friends and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there lived an elderly man named Jack. Jack had many animals. He had cows, horses, and sheep. Jack loved his animals very much. One day, Jack wanted to test his animals. He wanted to see which animal was the fastest. Jack thought the horse would be the fastest, but he wanted to find out for sure. So, Jack lined up all the animals at one end of the ranch. Jack said, "Ready, set, go!" and the animals started to run. The horse ran very fast, the cows ran a little slower, and the sheep ran the slowest. Jack watched as the animals ran across the ranch. He was happy to see them having fun. In the end, the horse was the fastest, just like Jack thought. The cows and sheep were slower, but they still had a good time. Jack was proud of his animals and loved them all very much. They all lived happily on the ranch.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to wear her favorite warm sweater. Lucy loved to play outside with her dog, Spot. They liked to run and jump in the park. One day, Lucy's mom gave her a toy phone. Lucy was very happy. She liked to pretend to talk on the phone. She called her friends, her grandma, and even Spot. Spot would wag his tail and bark when Lucy talked to him on the phone. One sunny day, Lucy and Spot went to the park. Lucy wore her warm sweater and took her toy phone. They played and laughed all day. Lucy called Spot on her toy phone, and they had a fun time. They went home tired but happy.
