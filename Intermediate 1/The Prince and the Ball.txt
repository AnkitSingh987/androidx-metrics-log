Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a prince. He lived in a big, modern house. The prince loved to play games. His favorite game was "guess." One sunny day, the prince went outside to play. He saw a big, round ball. The prince picked up the ball and said, "I guess this is a ball for playing!" He threw the ball high into the sky. The ball came back down and landed in a tree. The prince tried to get the ball out of the tree, but it was too high. He looked around and saw a tall ladder. "I guess this will help me get the ball," he said. The prince climbed the ladder and got the ball back. The prince was very happy. He played with the ball all day long. When the sun went down, the prince went back to his big, modern house. He was very tired but had so much fun. And from that day on, the prince loved to play "guess" even more.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big ice cream truck. Sue's face became very happy. She said, "I want some ice cream, please!" The ice cream man gave Sue a big ice cream cone. It was very yummy. Sue said, "This is the best ice cream I have ever had!" Sue played in the park and shared her yummy ice cream with her friends. They all said, "Thank you, Sue!" They played together until the sun went down. Sue's face was full of joy and ice cream.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks. They have many blocks of different colors and shapes. They can make towers, bridges, houses and other things with blocks. One day, they decide to observe their blocks. They want to see how they are different and how they are the same. They sit on the floor and look at their blocks carefully. "Look, this block is red and square. This block is blue and round. They are different colors and shapes," Lily says. "Yes, and this block is yellow and triangle. This block is green and rectangle. They are also different colors and shapes," Ben says. They find more blocks that are different and more blocks that are the same. They put the blocks that are the same together. They make organized groups of blocks. "Look, we have four groups of blocks. One group is red, one group is blue, one group is yellow and one group is green. And each group has different shapes," Lily says. "That's right. We are very good at observing. We can see the details of our blocks. We can also make organized groups of blocks," Ben says. They are happy and proud of their work. They smile and hug each other. They like to observe and play with blocks. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was ready to play outside. He put on his coat and ran to the yard. He saw a big tornado coming. Tim was scared. Tim gazed at the tornado. He saw a funny man inside. The man said, "Hello, Tim! I am Mr. Windy. I am not a mean tornado. I want to be your friend." Tim thought for a moment. He said, "Okay, Mr. Windy, we can be friends." They played together in the yard. Tim was happy, and the tornado was happy too. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a weird hose. It lived in a big garden with many pretty flowers. The hose was very long and twisty. It liked to play with the flowers and give them water when they were thirsty. One day, the hose was playing with its friend, the ball. They were having lots of fun. But, the hose was so twisty that it got stuck on a big rock. The hose tried to get free, but it could not. It pulled and pulled, but it was still stuck. Then, the hose heard a loud snap. The hose felt sad because it knew it had a break. The water could not come out anymore. The flowers were thirsty, but the hose could not help them. The hose felt very bad. But, a kind man saw the broken hose and fixed it with some tape. The hose was happy again and could give water to the thirsty flowers.
