Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Laundry

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to poke things. One day, he saw a big pile of laundry in his room. Tim had a serious look on his face. He knew he had to poke the laundry. Tim poked the laundry, but it didn't move. He poked it again, and this time, he heard a small sound. Tim was sure something was hiding in the laundry. He kept poking, and the sound got louder and louder. Finally, Tim poked the laundry one last time, and out jumped a little puppy! The puppy had been hiding in the laundry all along. Tim was so happy to find the puppy. They played and played, and Tim never poked the laundry again.

One day, a bird had a nest in a tree. The nest was dirty. The bird did not like the dirty nest. The bird saw a shiny ring in the mud. The bird thought the ring would make the nest pretty. Another bird saw the ring too. The two birds wanted the ring. They fought for the ring. They both wanted to make their nests pretty. The birds were not nice to each other. In the end, the birds learned to share the ring. They put the ring in the middle of their nests. Both nests were pretty. The birds were happy. The birds learned that sharing is good.

Jimmy was a three year old and he liked going to the port. His dad took him there almost every day. Today they were going to see an old boat. When they got there, Jimmy was excited. He couldn't wait to see the old boat. "Look there, Jimmy," his dad said. "Look at that old boat." Jimmy looked and saw the old boat. He wanted to get closer to it. So he started running. But then Jimmy tripped and fell. He started to cry and his dad came to console him. "It's okay, Jimmy," his dad said. "You just fell, that's all. Let's get back up, ok?" Jimmy nodded and he and his dad got back up. They then walked to the old boat and Jimmy had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to count. He counted everything he saw. One day, Spot went outside to play. The weather was sunny and nice. While Spot was playing, he saw a big, red ball. He wanted to count how many times he could bounce it. As he started to count, something unexpected happened. The ball bounced so high that it went into a tree! Spot was sad because he could not reach the ball. Just then, a friendly cat named Whiskers came along. Whiskers was very good at climbing trees. Spot asked Whiskers for help. Whiskers climbed the tree and got the ball down for Spot. Spot was so happy and thanked Whiskers. They became best friends and played together every day. From that day on, Spot and Whiskers counted many things together and had lots of fun, no matter the weather. And they both learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring new friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a crazy kite. The kite was big and had many colors. Tim wanted to play with it, but he had to hide it first. He did not want his friends to see it yet. So, he hid the kite under his bed. The next day, Tim took the kite to the park. He wanted to see how high it could fly. But when he tried to fly the kite, it did not go up. Tim felt sad. He did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. The kite started to talk! It said, "I can fly, but I need your help." Tim was surprised. He did not know kites could talk. The kite told Tim to hold it tight and run fast. Tim did as the kite said, and the crazy kite flew high in the sky. Tim was so happy, and he knew he had a special kite.

Once upon a time, there was a little tooth who lived in a big mouth. The tooth was very sad because it thought it was boring. Every day, the tooth would just stand there, not doing anything fun. One day, a new tooth came to live in the mouth. The new tooth was very happy and liked to talk. The new tooth said to the boring tooth, "Hi, I am new here. What do you like to do for fun?" The boring tooth replied, "I don't know. I just stand here all day." The new tooth wanted to help the boring tooth have fun. So, they played a game together. They talked and laughed all day. The boring tooth realized that it was not boring after all. The two teeth became best friends and had fun together every day. And from that day on, the tooth was never boring again.
