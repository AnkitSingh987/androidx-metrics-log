Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Toys

Ben and Lily liked to play in the bath. They had many toys that could float and splash. They made bubbles and waves and had fun. But they did not like when the water got cold and dirty. "Mom, can we have more water?" Ben asked. "No, sweetie, you have been in the bath for a long time. It is time to get out and dry off," Mom said. "But we want to play more!" Lily said. "I have an idea. Why don't you help me change the water? We can let the old water go down the drain and fill the tub with new water. Then you can play for a little bit more," Mom said. Ben and Lily liked this idea. They watched the water swirl and disappear in the drain. They heard the gurgle and the whoosh. They helped Mom turn on the faucet and make the water warm and clear. They saw the tub fill up again. "Wow, this is great!" Ben said. "Yeah, this is great!" Lily said. They jumped back in the bath and played with their toys. They thanked Mom for changing the water. They were happy and clean.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Andy. Andy had a long way to go to find food for his family. He wanted to succeed in his job, so he started his journey. On the way, Andy found a big pebble that blocked his path. He tried to move it, but it was too heavy. A friendly beetle saw Andy and asked, "What's wrong, Andy?" Andy replied, "This pebble is in my way, and I can't move it." The beetle said, "Let's work together, and we can move it." So, Andy and the beetle pushed the pebble together. With their teamwork, they moved the pebble and cleared the path. Andy thanked the beetle and continued his journey. In the end, Andy succeeded in finding food for his family. He learned that working together makes things easier. The moral of the story is that teamwork helps us achieve our goals.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They wanted to watch the big whales in the sea. Tim was very excited to see the whales. He was a polite boy and always said "please" and "thank you." As they sat on the beach, Tim saw a big whale jump out of the water. "Wow, mom! Look at that whale!" Tim said. His mom looked and smiled. They watched the whale swim and play in the water. Suddenly, the whale came closer to the beach. Tim and his mom were very surprised. The whale said, "Hello, I am a polite whale. Can I play with you?" Tim and his mom looked at each other, and then they laughed. They never thought a whale could talk! They had a fun day playing with the polite whale at the beach.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He liked to write about the sky. One day, he saw a comet in the sky. It was very big and bright. Tim was so happy to see it. The comet was not like other comets. It was rough and not smooth. This made Tim very curious. He wanted to know more about the comet. So, he wrote about it in his book. One night, something unexpected happened. The rough comet came closer to Tim's house. It was not a comet at all! It was a big, friendly space dog. Tim was so surprised, but he was also happy. Now, he had a new friend from the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She liked to print pictures with her mom. One day, Lily and her mom printed a big picture of a glass. The glass in the picture was very cold. Lily touched it and felt the cold. She was very surprised! "How can a picture be cold?" she asked her mom. Her mom was surprised too. Suddenly, the cold glass in the picture became real! It jumped out of the picture and onto the table. Lily and her mom laughed and clapped. They drank cold juice from the magic glass. They had a fun day with the glass from the picture.
