Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ant Found Dead Bug and the Butterfly

One day, a tiny ant found a dead bug. The ant wanted to show his friends. He went to his friend, the bee, and said, "Look! I found a big dead bug! Let's show everyone!" The bee looked at the dead bug and had an idea. "Let's shrink the bug's skull and make it smaller. Then, we can carry it and show everyone." The ant and the bee worked together to shrink the skull. They made it very small. Now, they could carry it and show their friends. They went to their friend, the butterfly, and said, "Look at this tiny skull we found!" The butterfly was amazed. "Wow, it's so small! Good job, friends!" The ant, the bee, and the butterfly showed the tiny skull to all their friends. Everyone was happy and amazed. They all had a fun day playing with the tiny skull.

Once there was a little boy. He was named Jack. One day, he wanted to be creative, so he got a piece of paper and a pencil from his mom. He was very excited! Jack decided to write something. He wrote a sentence about a kayak. He wrote, "The kayak was calm". Jack felt proud that he could write something. Then he decided to go for a ride in his mom's kayak. He was so excited! The water was calm, so it was a nice ride. He smiled and waved at the ducks. When he got back home, Jack was very proud of himself. He had written something and he had gone on a ride in the kayak. He couldn't wait to tell his mom all about his day.

One day, a big red dog named Sam went for a walk. Sam liked to sort rocks by size. He found small rocks, big rocks, and rocks in between. He put them in piles and felt happy. Sam was tired from sorting rocks and went to take a nap under a tree. While he was sleeping, a little bird came and moved the rocks. The bird mixed up all the piles, making them not sorted anymore. When Sam woke up, he saw the mixed-up rocks and felt sad. But then, the little bird came back with more rocks. They were all red, just like Sam! The bird wanted to help Sam sort the red rocks. They worked together, and soon the rocks were sorted again. Sam was happy and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big gray house, there lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Sally. They were best friends. One day, Tom said to Sally, "Let's play marry. We can have a big party with all our friends." Sally was scared. She said, "I don't know, Tom. What if our friends don't like it?" Tom said, "Don't be scared. It will be fun!" So they planned a big party and invited all their friends. On the day of the party, all their friends came to the gray house. They were having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew into the party and took away the cake! Everyone was surprised. Tom and Sally looked at each other and laughed. They said, "This is the best party ever!" And all their friends agreed.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp named Tim. Tim lived in a big sea with his family. He loved to zoom around and play with his friends. One day, Tim heard a loud noise. It was coming from far away. Tim felt scared, but he also felt curious. The loud noise came back every day. Tim's friends didn't want to play with him anymore because they were scared of the noise too. Tim felt sad, but he wanted to find out what was making the noise. So, he decided to go on a big adventure to find the loud noise. Tim zoomed through the sea, following the loud noise. As he got closer, he saw a big ship. The ship was making the loud noise! Tim was brave and went closer to the ship. He saw that the ship was full of new friends who were singing and dancing. They invited Tim to join their party. Tim was happy because he found the loud noise and made new friends. From that day on, Tim was not scared of the loud noise anymore.
