Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Bear and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bobo. Bobo was very careless. He never put things back where they should be. One day, Bobo wanted to play on the swing. But, the swing had a sign that said, "Do not permit heavy weight." Bobo was sad because he was a big bear and had a lot of weight. He sat down and thought about what to do. Then, he had an idea! Bobo went to his room and found a big box. He put all his toys in the box to make it heavy. Bobo carried the box to the swing and put it on the swing. He thought the swing would not break now because it already had heavy weight on it. But, something unexpected happened! When Bobo sat on the swing, it did not break. The swing was strong enough for Bobo and the box! Bobo was very happy that he could play on the swing. From that day on, he was not careless anymore. He put things back where they should be and always read signs carefully. And, he played on the swing every day with his big box of toys.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom had a big, different blanket. It was red and blue with stars. Lily wanted to play with Tom. Lily said, "Hi, Tom! Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play on my blanket!" They both sat on the blanket and played with toys. They had so much fun. After a while, they decided to skip around the park. They skipped and laughed. They played and played until they were tired. They went back to the blanket and rested. Lily and Tom became best friends.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big sand castle. Tim wanted to play with the castle. He asked his mom, "Can I play with the castle, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can." Tim played with the castle, but he wanted to make it better. He found a stick and tried to make a door. But the stick was too big, and the castle broke with a snap! Tim felt sad. Then, a little girl came to Tim with a big smile. She said, "Don't be sad. We can make a new castle together!" They started to build a new castle. Tim's mom gave them some snacks. They ate sour apples and laughed. They had fun building a new castle, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big mud puddle. Many animals lived near the puddle. One day, a selfish frog came to the puddle. He wanted all the mud for himself. He told the other animals, "This is my mud! You can't play here!" The other animals were sad. The animals knew they had to do something. They had a big talk. They decided to unite and work together to show the frog that sharing is better. They made a plan. They all went to the puddle and started playing together. The frog was mad, but he saw how much fun they were having. The next day, the frog came to the other animals. He said, "I was wrong. I want to play with you and share the mud." The animals were happy. They all played in the mud puddle together. The selfish frog learned that sharing is better than being alone. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big, orange ball stuck in a tree. Lucy wanted to play with the ball, but she did not know how to get it down. She tried to jump, but she was too small. Lucy began to struggle. A nice boy named Tom saw Lucy and said, "Do you need help?" Lucy nodded and said, "Yes, please. The orange ball is stuck." Tom tried to help Lucy get the ball down. They both jumped and reached, but they still could not get it. A tall man saw Lucy and Tom struggle. He walked over and said, "I can help you get the ball." The tall man reached up and got the orange ball from the tree. Lucy and Tom were so happy. They played with the ball and had lots of fun. The tall man smiled and said, "You both did a good job trying. Your reward is having fun with the ball."
