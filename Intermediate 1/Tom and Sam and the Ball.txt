Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sam and the Ball

Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play soccer. They had a big ball and two goals in their backyard. They always tried to score more goals than each other. They were very competitive. One day, their dad took them to a stadium. It was a huge place with many seats and a big field. There were two teams playing soccer on the field. They wore different colors and had names on their shirts. Their dad told them the names of the teams and the players. Tom and Sam watched the game with excitement. They cheered for their favorite team and booed the other team. They saw the players run, kick, pass, and shoot. They saw the goalie save some shots and miss some shots. They saw the referee blow his whistle and show cards. They saw the score change on the big screen. "Wow, this is so fun!" Tom said. "I want to play soccer like them!" "Me too!" Sam said. "They are so good and fast!" "They are, but we can be too!" Tom said. "We just have to practice more and work hard!" "Okay, let's do that!" Sam said. "But first, let's enjoy the game!" They smiled and hugged each other. They seemed very happy and proud. Their dad smiled and hugged them too. He seemed very happy and proud too. They all had a great time at the stadium.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was going on a trip with her mom and dad. They had to pack their bags. Sue wanted to pack her favorite bathrobe. Mom said, "Don't forget your bathrobe, Sue!" One night, they stayed in a big, dark house. Sue thought the house was scary. She heard strange noises. Sue was scared, but then she remembered her bathrobe. She put on her bathrobe and felt safe. The next day, Sue told her mom and dad about the scary house. They were happy that Sue had her bathrobe to make her feel better. The moral of the story is: sometimes, having something special with you can help you feel brave when you are scared.

Once upon a time there was a busy dog. His name was Zoom. He had a stamp. Zoom liked to run and play with his stamp. He liked to zoom all about the yard. He was very busy. One day Zoom was running here and there with his stamp. He liked to dribble it up and down and all around. Suddenly something happened. He zoomed around the tree but he tripped on a rock and fell! That scared Zoom. He dropped his stamp and it flew into the grass. Zoom was worried, how could he find it? But then he noticed that the stamp was glowing. Suddenly it flew right up into Zoom's mouth! Zoom was so happy! He couldn't believe it. He had his stamp again and was ready to Zoom around the yard. And they all lived happily ever after. Zoom and his stamp were always busy playing together.

Tom and Anna are twins. They like to play and have fun. But today they are sleepy. They had a big day at the park. They saw ducks, swings, slides and flowers. "Can we have ice-cream, Mom?" Tom asks. He likes chocolate ice-cream. Anna likes vanilla ice-cream. "Maybe later, sweeties. It's time for a nap now. You need to rest your eyes and your legs," Mom says. She gives them a hug and a kiss. "But we are not sleepy, Mom. We want ice-cream. Please, please, please," Anna says. She makes a sad face. Mom smiles. She knows a trick. "How about this? If you take a nap now, I will tell you a story when you wake up. A story about a magic ice-cream truck. Do you believe in magic?" Tom and Anna nod. They love stories. They love magic. They love ice-cream. They agree to take a nap. Mom tucks them in their beds. She sings a lullaby. Soon, Tom and Anna are asleep. They dream of the magic ice-cream truck. They wonder what flavors it has. They wonder when it will come. They are happy. They are sleepy. They are sweet.
