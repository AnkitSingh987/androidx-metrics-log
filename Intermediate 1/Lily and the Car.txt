Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Car

Lily was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls in her chamber. She had many dolls, but her favorite one was Rose, who had a pink dress and long hair. Lily and Rose would talk and sing and have tea parties every day. One day, Lily's mother came to her chamber and said, "Lily, we have to go to grandma's house for a few days. You can bring one doll with you, but you have to leave the rest here." Lily was sad. She did not want to leave her dolls, especially Rose. She said, "But mommy, I love all my dolls. I can't choose one. Can't I bring them all?" Her mother shook her head. "No, Lily, there is not enough room in the car. You have to pick one. Come on, we have to hurry." Lily looked at her dolls. She did not know who to pick. She loved them all. She started to weep. She hugged Rose and said, "I'm sorry, Rose. I don't want to leave you. But I don't want to leave the others either. What should I do?" Rose smiled and said, "Don't cry, Lily. I understand. You can take another doll with you. I will be fine here. I will wait for you to come back. I will always be your friend." Lily wiped her tears and said, "Thank you, Rose. You are very kind. I will miss you a lot. I will come back soon. I promise." She kissed Rose and put her on the bed. She picked another doll and put it in her bag. She said goodbye to her dolls and followed her mother out of the chamber. The chamber was empty and quiet. Rose lay on the bed and looked at the door. She hoped Lily would come back soon. She felt lonely. She closed her eyes and dreamed of playing with Lily again.

Once upon a time there was a family that was very careful. On an especially careful day, they decided to go on an adventure. The family loaded up their luggage into the car and everyone settled in for the ride. When they reached their destination, the family unloaded their luggage and set off to explore. As they were walking, the 3 year old noticed something in the distance. The 3 year old excitedly said to the parents, "What is that?” The parents replied, “That looks like a playground! Let's go check it out! Be careful though and make sure your luggage is secure.” So the family settled at the playground and had a wonderful time playing and exploring. When it was time to go, the family carefully loaded their luggage back in the car and went on their way. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who lived in a big tree. The owl knew many things. He had a friend, a little fly. One day, the fly came to the wise owl and said, "I want to know how to be wise like you." The owl said, "To be wise, you must listen and learn." The fly listened to the owl's words and learned many things. The wise owl taught the fly about the sun, the moon, and the stars. The fly felt happy and said, "Thank you, wise owl, for teaching me." As they spent time together, the wise owl learned from the fly too. The fly showed the owl how to fly fast and high. The owl was happy to learn from his friend. And so, the wise owl and the little fly became even better friends. They knew that they could learn from each other, and they were happy.

One day, a farmer went to his field to plant some seeds. He had a big bag of seeds and a shovel. He dug holes in the ground and put some seeds in each hole. He was very happy to see his field full of seeds. But he did not see a big bird in the sky. The bird was hungry and saw the seeds. The bird flew down and started to eat the seeds. The farmer heard the bird and ran to his field. He shouted at the bird, "Go away, bird! Those are my seeds!" The bird was scared and flew away. But it did not go far. It waited for the farmer to leave. The farmer did not leave. He stayed in his field and watched the bird. He had a plan. He went to his barn and got a scarecrow. A scarecrow is a thing that looks like a person, but is not. It has a hat and a coat and a stick. The farmer put the scarecrow in his field. He made it look like it was holding a shovel. He hoped the bird would think it was him. He hid behind a tree and waited. The bird came back and saw the scarecrow. It thought it was the farmer. It was not afraid. It flew down and ate more seeds. The farmer saw the bird and jumped out of his hiding place. He ran to the bird and waved his shovel. He said, "Ha! I got you, bird! You are not smart!" The bird was surprised and tried to fly away. But it was too late. The farmer caught the bird with his shovel. He put the bird in a cage. He said, "You will not eat my seeds anymore. You will stay in this cage until the end of the day. Then I will let you go, but you will never come back." The bird was sad and sorry. It said, "Please, farmer, let me go. I will not eat your seeds anymore. I will find other food. I will be your friend." The farmer felt sorry for the bird. He said, "Okay, bird, I will let you go. But you have to promise me something. You have to promise me that you will not eat my seeds or any other farmer's seeds. You have to eat only worms and bugs and berries. Do you promise?" The bird nodded and said, "I promise, farmer, I promise. Thank you for being kind to me." The farmer opened the cage and let the bird go. The bird flew away and said, "Goodbye, farmer, goodbye. I will keep my promise. You are a good farmer." The farmer smiled and said, "Goodbye, bird, goodbye. I hope you find enough food. You are a good bird." The farmer went back to his field and planted more seeds. He was happy and proud. He had saved his seeds and made a new friend. He hoped his seeds would grow and give him a good harvest. He hoped the bird would be happy and healthy. He hoped they would meet again someday. The end.
