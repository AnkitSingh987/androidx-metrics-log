Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Sheep and the Cat

One day, a sheep named Bob saw an enormous shoe. The shoe was very dirty. Bob wanted to help, so he decided to polish the shoe to make it clean and shiny. Bob went to find his friend, Cat. "Cat, will you help me polish the enormous shoe?" asked Bob. Cat said, "Yes, I will help you, Bob!" So, they started to polish the shoe together. They worked hard, and the shoe became clean and shiny. But, the shoe belonged to a big, mean giant. The giant saw Bob and Cat polishing his shoe and became very angry. "Why are you touching my shoe?" he yelled. Bob and Cat were scared and ran away. The giant chased them, and they never returned to their homes again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play in the wet pond near his home. He liked to splash and swim all day long. One day, Ducky found a big piece of steel in his pond. It was very big and hard. Ducky didn't like it because it made his pond look not nice. He wanted to get it out, but it was too heavy for him to move. Ducky asked his friend, a strong horse named Horsey, to help him. Horsey tried to pull the steel out, but it was very hard. They both suffer and work very hard. At last, they got the steel out of the pond. Ducky was so happy to have his wet pond back. He thanked Horsey, and they played in the pond together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was an honest girl who always told the truth. She had a big heart and loved to help people. One day, Sue found a note on the ground. The note said, "Please mind the baby bird in the tree." Sue looked up and saw a baby bird in the tree. She wanted to help, so she stayed under the tree to mind the baby bird. Soon, her friend Tom came by. He saw Sue looking at the tree. "What are you doing, Sue?" Tom asked. "I found a note to mind the baby bird," Sue said, showing Tom the note. Tom was a good friend, so he stayed with Sue to mind the baby bird too. They played and talked under the tree, making sure the baby bird was safe. At the end of the day, the baby bird's mom came back. She was so happy that Sue and Tom minded her baby. The baby bird was safe and sound, and Sue and Tom felt good about helping. From that day on, Sue and Tom were known as the best and most honest helpers in the town.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Max. Max had a job to deliver things. One day, Max had to deliver a cactus. The cactus was for a nice lady who lived far away. On the way, Max met a cat named Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Max! What are you doing?" Max said, "I am delivering a cactus to a nice lady." Sam said, "Can I help you?" Max said, "Yes, let's go together!" As they walked, a big wind came and blew the cactus away! Max and Sam were sad. But then, they saw the cactus land in a baby bird's nest. The baby bird was happy because the cactus made a good home. Max and Sam smiled and went to tell the nice lady the good news.

Once upon a time, there was a big, warm tent in the woods. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, liked to play there. They had lots of fun in the tent every day, laughing and playing games. One day, a strong wind came and made a big hole in the tent. Tim and Spot were sad. They wanted to fix the tent so they could play in it again. Tim got some big sticks and a soft cloth to fix the hole. Tim and Spot worked together to fix the tent. They put the big sticks on the hole and covered it with the soft cloth. The tent was warm and safe again. Tim and Spot were very happy. They went back inside the tent to play and have fun. And they lived happily ever after.
