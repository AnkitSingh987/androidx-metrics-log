Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fox and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a small fox. The fox was very strong and brave. One day, he decided to explore a cave he had never seen before. As he peered into the cave, he could hear a loud growling noise. He thought to himself, "That could be dangerous!" But the brave fox decided to take a closer look. As he crawled deeper into the cave, he found a big bear. The fox was shocked and scared. He started to back away, but then he thought about how strong he was. The fox courageously stepped closer to the bear. Instead of getting angry, the bear smiled and said, "You are very brave to come closer. Thank you for believing in yourself." The fox learned that day that strength is found in believing in yourself no matter what the situation.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk near a big cliff. The cliff was very old and tall. Tim liked to look at the old cliff and make up stories about it. He thought it was a magic place. As Tim walked, he saw an old man sitting on a rock. The old man said, "Hello, Tim. I like to make things. Do you want to make something with me?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, I want to make something with you!" They started to make a big tower from rocks near the cliff. It was fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The old man said a magic word and the tower began to move! The tower turned into a big friendly giant! The giant said, "Thank you for making me. I will protect the old cliff and be your friend forever!" Tim was very surprised and happy. He had a new friend and a magic story to tell about the old cliff.

Greg was proud of his arm. It was special and could do lots of things. "Can I try it?" asked his little sister. He thought for a moment. "Yes!" he said. Greg showed her how to unlock the arm so it would move. She was so happy. But then, when they tried to unlock the arm again, it wouldn't work. Greg's little sister started to cry. Greg was determined to unlock the arm. He tried again, and then he heard a click. He had done it! He was so proud. He was even more proud of his sister. She had been brave and strong and hadn't given up. He gave her a big hug.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red wagon. He loved to play with his wagon every day. One day, his wagon broke. Tim felt bad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my wagon is bad. Can I receive a new one?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. We can go to the store and get a new wagon." So, Tim and his mom went to the store. They found a new wagon. It was red, like his old one. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Now, Tim can play with his new wagon every day.

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She was so happy because she had an amazing ball. The ball was big and red. Mia loved to play with her ball all day. Yesterday, she played with it and did not lose it. At the park, Mia met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "Can I play with your ball?" Mia said, "Yes, we can play together." So Mia and Tim played with the ball, and they had so much fun. They threw the ball and kicked it high in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took the ball in its mouth. Mia and Tim were sad. They thought they would lose the ball forever. But the dog was nice. It brought the ball back to them. Mia and Tim were so happy. They played with the dog and the ball, and they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a beach where kids loved to play. One day, a girl named Sue and a boy named Tim went to the beach. They wanted to play with their toys in the sand. Sue was very organized. She put her toys in a neat line. Tim liked to mix his toys up. Sue said to Tim, "Let's build a big sand castle!" Tim said, "Yes, let's do it!" They started to build the castle with their organized toys. They dug and piled the sand. The castle was getting bigger and bigger. As they played, dark clouds came. The wind blew the sand away from their castle. Sue and Tim looked at each other and knew they had to hurry. They finished the castle just before the rain came. They were happy they played together and used their organized toys to build the castle. The rain did not ruin their fun day at the beach.
