Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy and her mom were in the grocery store, and Lucy saw a cauliflower. She knew it was not a real flower, because it was white, not colorful like the ones in the garden. She asked her mother if she could get it, but her mother said no. Lucy got mad and said bad things about her mother. But her mom still loved her and said she was sorry for not getting the cauliflower. Lucy felt bad now, and she wanted to forgive her mother. She hugged her mom and said it was okay. The next day, Lucy came up with a plan to surprise her mom. She found a fake cauliflower and pretended it was a real one. She made it look colorful and put it in the garden for her mom to find. Her mom was so happy when she found it. She hugged Lucy and said, "Thank you for forgiving me. I love you so much!" They hugged and laughed together, and Lucy knew that sometimes, it's okay to forgive even the little things.

One day, a boy named Tim went to camp. At camp, there were lots of fun things to do. Tim liked to count how many friends he made at camp. He also liked to count the stars at night. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam said, "I like to count too! Let's count the bugs we find." They both started to look for bugs. They found a big, green bug. Tim thought it was disgusting, but Sam liked it. After they counted the bugs, Tim and Sam played together all day. They had so much fun at camp. At night, they counted the stars together. They were happy to be friends and count together.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. Many kids would come to play there. One day, a boy named Tom went to the stadium. He was glad to play with his friends. Tom saw a book on the ground. He picked it up and started to read. The book had fun stories about animals. Tom's friends came over and they all read the book together. After they finished reading, they played a fun game in the stadium. The sun was shining and they were all very happy. They knew they would remember this day forever.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a black dog. The black dog liked to run and play with the kids. One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue said, "Mom, can I open the gate and play with the black dog?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Sue opened the gate and went to play with the black dog. Sue and the black dog had lots of fun. They ran and played in the park. After a while, it was time to go home. Sue said, "Bye, black dog! See you next time!" Sue and her mom went home, and the black dog went back to playing in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She had a big, red ball. Sara loved to play with her ball in the park. One day, she saw a small, lost dog. The dog looked sad and hungry. Sara wanted to help the dog. Sara said, "Come with me, little dog. I will lead you to my home." The dog wagged its tail and followed Sara. When they got home, Sara's mom gave the dog a big piece of meat. The dog was very happy. Sara's mom was proud of her. She said, "Sara, you are a kind girl for helping the dog." Sara smiled and played with the dog all day. They became best friends, and the dog was never lost again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big box. She saw a roll of tape next to it. Lucy had an incredible idea. She wanted to play hide and seek with her friends. So, she used the tape to make a secret hiding place with the box. Lucy called her friends Tom and Sue to play with her. They all loved to play hide and seek. Lucy said, "I will count and you both go hide." Tom and Sue ran to find good hiding spots. Lucy counted to ten and then went to look for her friends. Lucy found Sue hiding behind a big tree. They both laughed. Then, they looked for Tom. They could not find him. Lucy smiled and said, "I know where he is!" She took Sue to her secret hiding place. They found Tom hiding in the box. They all laughed and had a fun day playing hide and seek.
