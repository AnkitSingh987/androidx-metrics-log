Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Pale Bunny and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a pale bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to celebrate with his friends. One day, Bobo found a magazine with lots of pictures. He wanted to show it to his friends at their party. Bobo went to the party, but his friend Tina the turtle was sad. She wanted to see the magazine too, but it was too high for her to reach. Bobo tried to help, but he was too small. All the friends were upset. Then, a big, kind giraffe named Gigi came to the party. Gigi could reach very high. She picked up the magazine and gave it to Tina. All the friends were happy. They celebrated together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little toy soldier. He was in a war with other toy soldiers. They all wanted to win, but the little soldier was scared. He felt broken because he was missing an arm. One day, the little soldier saw a big battle coming. He started to panic. He did not want to fight, but he knew he had to be brave. He took a deep breath and got ready to face the other toy soldiers. The little soldier fought with all his might. Even though he was broken, he did not give up. In the end, the war was over, and the little soldier was still standing. He had learned that even when you are scared, you can still be brave.

Once upon a time, there was a little cow named Bessie. Bessie lived on a very organized farm with her friends. They all liked to play and have fun together. Bessie loved to eat grass and her favorite food was beef. One day, Bessie wanted to play a new game with her friends. She tried to manage the game, but it was hard for her. She asked her friend, a big horse named Harry, to help her. With Harry's help, Bessie managed the game and all her friends played together. They were very happy and had a lot of fun. Bessie was proud that she could manage the game and make her friends happy.

One day, a happy boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted to make a cake. He took a big bowl and started to stir the mix with a spoon. Tim felt excited about the cake. Then, his mom came into the kitchen. She saw Tim stirring the mix. "What are you doing, Tim?" she asked. "I am making a cake, Mom!" Tim said with a big smile. Tim's mom helped him put the mix into the oven. She turned the knob to make it hot. They waited for the cake to bake. When it was done, they took it out of the oven. Tim and his mom shared the cake. They were both very happy.

One day, a loud dog named Max went to the park. He loved to play with his friends. He saw his best friend, a cat named Tim. "Welcome, Tim!" Max said. "Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, Max. I want to play, but I am not loud like you." Max and Tim played together. Max was loud, but Tim was not. Max wanted Tim to be loud too. Max said, "Tim, you must be loud like me. I insist!" Tim tried to be loud. He said, "Meow! Meow!" very loud. Max was happy. They played loud together and had fun.

One day, a cat and a dog went to a dance. The cat could turn and jump very well. The dog could not turn or jump. He felt weird. He wanted to dance like the cat. The dog asked the cat, "How do you turn and jump so well?" The cat said, "I practice every day. If you practice, you can turn and jump too." The dog wanted to try, but he was scared. The cat helped the dog. They practiced together. The dog learned to turn and jump. The dance was fun, and they were both happy. The dog was not scared or weird anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little rat named Remy. He loved to eat spicy food. One day, he found a big, spicy pepper in the kitchen. He was so happy! But, Remy's friend, Tim the cat, said, "No, Remy! Don't eat that spicy pepper! It will make your mouth very hot and you will need water to drink!" Remy didn't listen to Tim and ate the spicy pepper anyway. His mouth got very hot, just like Tim said. Remy ran to find water to drink, but there was no water left. So, Tim helped Remy by giving him a dry piece of bread. The bread made Remy's mouth feel better. Remy said, "Thank you, Tim! You were right. I should have listened to you." Tim smiled and said, "That's okay, Remy. We all make mistakes. The important thing is to learn from them." And from that day on, Remy learned to listen to his friends and not to eat too much spicy food.
