Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cat

One day, a dog named Spot had a messy collar. He did not like it. Spot wanted to play with his friends, but they would not play with him because of his messy collar. Spot went to his friend, Cat, and asked, "Will you help me clean my collar?" Cat looked at Spot's collar and said, "Yes, I will help you." They tried to clean the collar, but it was very hard. They passed water and soap on it, but it did not work. Spot was sad. Then, Spot and Cat went to Owl for help. Owl was very wise. Owl said, "You must pass your collar through the river to clean it." Spot and Cat went to the river, and Spot passed his collar through the water. The collar became clean! Spot was happy. Now, he could play with his friends again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily saw an enormous cookie on the table. She wanted to eat it, but her mom said, "No, Lily, you must wait until after dinner." Lily was sad and angry. She thought about taking the cookie when her mom was not looking. But then, she remembered her mom always said, "Be patient and good things will come." So, Lily decided to wait. After dinner, her mom put the enormous cookie on a plate and gave it to Lily. Lily was so happy she waited. She shared the cookie with her mom and they both enjoyed it together. The moral of the story is to be patient and listen to your mom.

Once upon a time, there was a fancy cat. The cat was very thirsty. The cat had a big mouth. The cat saw a cup. The cup was full of milk. The cat wanted to drink the milk. The cat used its big mouth to drink the milk. The cat was very happy. The cat was not thirsty anymore.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with toys. One day, they find a big board in the yard. The board is brown and hard. "Look, a board!" Tom says. "We can use it for many things." "Like what?" Lily asks. Tom thinks. "We can use it as a bridge, or a slide, or a table, or a wall. We can make different games with it." Lily nods. "That sounds fun. Let's try it." They take the board and put it on two rocks. Now it is a bridge. They walk on it and pretend it is over a river. They make sounds of water and fish. Then they move the board and lean it on a tree. Now it is a slide. They climb on it and slide down. They laugh and shout. Then they lay the board on the grass. Now it is a table. They bring some cups and plates and pretend it is a picnic. They share some cookies and juice. Then they stand the board up and hold it. Now it is a wall. They hide behind it and play peek-a-boo. They surprise each other and giggle. Tom and Lily have a lot of fun with the board. They use it for different things. They are happy and creative. They are good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a brave girl named Lisa. She liked to sort her toys into groups. One day, while she was playing, she heard a loud noise. It was an emergency! Her cat, Fluffy, was stuck in a tree. Lisa knew she had to help Fluffy. She put on her cape and climbed the tree. Fluffy was scared, but Lisa was brave. She reached out and grabbed Fluffy. But, as they climbed down, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew by and took Lisa's cape. But, the bird did not know it was helping! The cape became a net, and it caught Fluffy and Lisa as they fell. They were safe! The bird was a hero too. And from that day on, Lisa, Fluffy, and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very worried dog. He always wanted to make sure all his friends were happy. One day, Spot and his friends went to a place where they could eat. It was called a cafe. At the cafe, there was a big thing called a menu. The menu had all the food that the animals could eat. Spot looked at the menu, but he did not recognize any of the food. He was worried that his friends would not like the food. Spot decided to ask his friends what they wanted to eat. One by one, his friends told him what they liked. Spot was happy to find out that the menu had all the food his friends liked. In the end, all of Spot's friends were happy and had a great time at the cafe. Spot was not worried anymore.
