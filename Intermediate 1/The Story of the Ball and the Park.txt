Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Ball and the Park

Once upon a time there was a boy who wanted to play cricket. He went to the park to practice. He brought his cricket bat and ball. He was very ready. He walked around the park and saw another boy playing cricket. The boys said hello to each other. "Do you want to play together?", asked the boy. "Yes, sure!", said the other boy. They started to play. It was a lot of fun. They ran around, trying to get the ball. The boys were very excited. They wanted to win. In the end, the boy won the game. The other boy was very happy for him. They said goodbye to each other and the boy went home, happily holding his cricket bat and ball.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lilly. Lilly was proud of her world and the way she looked. One day, Lilly met a new friend called Luke. Luke told Lilly that he didn't have a name, and Lilly was very sad. She thought that everyone should have a name and should be proud of it. So Lilly helped her new friend give himself a name. She thought of lots of different names before they both agreed on a perfect name - Bob. Lilly smiled and told Bob that he now had a name and he should always be proud of it. Bob was so happy and thanked Lilly with a big hug. He told her that he would always be proud of his name and treat it with respect. This was an important lesson that Lilly learned that day - everyone should be proud of their name, and it is important to treat it with respect.

Timmy and his daddy were playing in the park. Timmy liked to swing on the swings and slide on the slides. Daddy liked to watch Timmy and smile. They were having fun. But then, a big dog came running to the park. The dog was black and brown and had sharp teeth. The dog barked and growled and chased the birds. Timmy was scared of the dog. He ran to his daddy and hugged him. "Daddy, daddy, help me! The dog is mean and scary!" Timmy yelled. Daddy picked up Timmy and tried to calm him down. He said, "Don't worry, Timmy. The dog won't hurt you. He is just playing. He is not a bad dog. He is a mighty dog." But the dog did not hear daddy. The dog saw Timmy and daddy and thought they were toys. The dog ran to them and jumped on them. The dog bit daddy's arm and made him bleed. Daddy screamed and dropped Timmy. Timmy fell to the ground and hurt his head. He cried and cried. The dog ran away. Timmy and daddy were alone and hurt. They needed help. But no one came. They were sad and scared. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom loved to paint big and colorful pictures. He also liked to sing noisy songs while he painted. One day, Tom was painting a pretty picture of a tree. He was singing very loudly. A little girl named Sally lived next door. She was trying to take a nap, but she could not sleep because of Tom's noisy singing. Sally went to Tom's house and said, "Please, Tom, can you mind your singing? I am trying to sleep." Tom felt bad for making too much noise. He said, "I am sorry, Sally. I will try to be quieter when I paint." Tom stopped singing and started to hum softly. Sally went back to her house and was able to take a nice nap. From that day on, Tom was more careful with his noise. He still loved to paint and sing, but he made sure to be quiet when his friends needed some peace. Sally and Tom became good friends, and they even painted together sometimes.

Lila and Max are twins. They like to play together, but sometimes they fight. One day, they are playing with their blocks. Lila wants to build a tower, but Max wants to knock it down. He thinks it is funny. "Stop it, Max!" Lila says. "You are mean. I want to build a tower." "No, I want to play smash!" Max says. He uses his muscle to hit the blocks. They fall down and make a loud noise. "Max, Lila, behave!" Mom says. She comes into the room. She is not happy. She sees the mess on the floor. "You need to share and be nice to each other. If you don't, you will have a time out." Lila and Max feel sorry. They don't want a time out. They say sorry to Mom and to each other. They hug and make up. They decide to build a tower together. They use their muscles to lift the blocks. They make a big and tall tower. They are proud of their work. "Wow, what a great tower!" Mom says. She comes back to see them. She is happy. She smiles and claps. "You are such good helpers. I am grateful for you. How about we have some cookies as a treat?" Lila and Max are happy too. They love cookies. They say thank you to Mom. They share the cookies and enjoy them. They are glad they behaved and played nicely. They are good twins.
