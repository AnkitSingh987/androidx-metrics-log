Category: Intermediate
Title: Sarah and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She was having a grumpy day, and she was tired. So Sarah decided to go and have a nap in her bedroom. She ran upstairs to reach the bedroom door. The bedroom was full of sunshine, and Sarah felt better. She lay down on her bed, feeling grumpy and sleepy. Before long, she started to feel happy and relaxed. She reached up and touched the soft pillow above her head, and before she knew it, she was asleep. When she woke up, Sarah felt much better. The grumpiness was gone and she was ready to start the day again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a curious barber named Tom. Tom loved to fix hair. Every day, he would fix the hair of many people in the town. They all liked Tom very much. One day, a little boy named Tim came to Tom's shop. Tim wanted Tom to fix his hair too. "Can you fix my hair, Tom?" asked Tim. Tom said, "Yes, Tim, I can fix your hair. Sit down, and I will make your hair look nice." Tom started to fix Tim's hair, but he was very curious about a big red button on the wall. He had never seen it before. Tom pressed the button, and suddenly, the floor under Tim opened up! Tim fell down into a dark hole. Tom was very sad and scared. He could not fix Tim's hair anymore, and Tim was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It was very big and strong. Many animals lived in the tree. There was a bird and a squirrel. They were friends. One day, the bird and the squirrel found a big nut. The bird said, "I want to eat the nut." The squirrel said, "No, I want to eat the nut." They did not agree. They disagree. A wise, old owl lived in the tree too. The owl was very patient. The owl said, "Let's break the nut in half. Then you can both eat it." The bird and the squirrel liked the idea. They broke the nut and shared it. They were happy and stayed friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a big hat that she loved to wear. One day, she was going to the park with her mommy. Sue was so happy to wear her hat. She held it tight in her hands. At the park, Sue saw her friend Tim. Tim was playing with a ball. Sue wanted to play too. She put her hat on the bench and went to play with Tim. They had lots of fun playing together. But then, a big wind came and blew Sue's hat away. Sue was embarrassed because her hat was gone. She looked everywhere for her hat. Tim helped her look too. Then, they saw the hat stuck in a tree. Tim got a long stick and got the hat down for Sue. Sue was so happy to have her hat back. She held it tight and said thank you to Tim. From that day on, Sue always held her hat tight when it was windy.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy dog named Max. Max loved to visit his friend, the quiet cat named Luna. One sunny day, Max went to Luna's house for a play date. When Max got to Luna's house, he said, "Let's add more friends to our play date!" Luna agreed, so they went to find their friend, the little bird named Tweet. They asked Tweet, "Do you want to join our play date?" Tweet said, "Yes, I would love to!" So, Max, Luna, and Tweet played all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. The noisy dog, the quiet cat, and the little bird had lots of fun on their play date. At the end of the day, Max, Luna, and Tweet were very happy. They all agreed to have more play dates together in the future. And from that day on, the three friends always made sure to add more friends to their play dates, making them even more fun and exciting.
