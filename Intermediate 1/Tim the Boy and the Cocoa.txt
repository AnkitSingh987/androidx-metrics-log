Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cocoa

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some cocoa to make a hot drink. Tim was very happy because making cocoa was easy and fun. At the store, Tim stood on his tiptoes to reach the cocoa on the shelf. But, he was too short. Just then, a nice old man came by and helped Tim get the cocoa. Tim said thank you, and the old man smiled. When they got home, Tim and his mom made the cocoa. They took a sip and it tasted funny. They looked at the box and saw that it was not cocoa, but coffee! The old man had given them the wrong box. Tim and his mom laughed and went back to the store to get the right cocoa.

One day, a big group of kids wanted to go to the theater. They were very excited to see a fun show with music and dancing. They put on their nice clothes and got ready to go. As they walked to the theater, they saw a little dog with a tight collar. The dog looked sad and scared. The kids knew they had to help the dog, but they didn't want to miss the show. So, they decided to rush and help the dog quickly. The kids took off the tight collar and gave the dog some water. The dog was so happy that it started to wag its tail and dance around. The kids laughed and played with the dog for a little while. Then, they all went to the theater together to watch the show. The dog even got to sit with them and watch the fun show too.

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. Tom was very hungry. He wanted to eat a big apple. He went to the apple tree in his yard. He saw the big apple at the top of the tree. He tried to climb the tree to get the apple. As Tom climbed, he saw a big button on the tree. The button said "Reverse." Tom was curious. He pushed the button. Suddenly, the tree started to shrink! The tree got smaller and smaller. Tom was now at the top of the tiny tree. It was easy for him to get the apple. Tom was very happy. He took the apple and ate it. But, something unexpected happened. Tom started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, he was taller than the tree. Tom was scared. He pushed the "Reverse" button again. He became smaller and the tree grew back to its normal size. Tom learned that the "Reverse" button could change the size of things. He was happy to be back to his normal size and enjoyed the rest of his apple.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was only three and very naughty. On the kitchen table laid a plate of yummy meat. Jack saw it and came closer. He was so tempted to try it! He reached out his little hand, but his mom was behind him. She said, "No, Jack. Don't touch! That meat is for later". Jack was sad and angry. He didn't like listening to mom. He started to pout. His mom put her hand on his shoulder and said, "It's alright Jack. We can go outside and play. Come here now." Jack liked the idea and went outside. He started running to one side of the garden and made a big mess. His mom shouted, "Jack! Come here now". But Jack kept running and slipping in the mud. Mom looked angry but then she laughed and said, "Come here now, naughty boy". Jack knew mom still loved him and he ran to hug her. They laughed and hugged and made a mess again, but this time with love.

Once upon a time, in a cute little house, there lived a dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat sausages. One day, he saw a sausage on the floor. It was very big and yummy. Spot was very happy and wanted to eat it. Spot tried to carry the sausage, but it was too heavy. He needed help. His friend, a small bird named Tweet, saw him and wanted to support him. Tweet flew down and tried to help Spot carry the sausage. They both tried very hard, but the sausage was still too heavy. They did not give up. They kept trying to carry the sausage together. But then, a big and mean cat saw them. The cat was very hungry and wanted the sausage too. The cat ran fast and took the sausage away from Spot and Tweet. Spot and Tweet were very sad, and the cat ate the sausage all by itself.
