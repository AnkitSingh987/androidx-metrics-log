Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Crown

Sara loved to play with her crown. It was shiny and pretty and made her feel like a queen. She wore it every day and never let anyone touch it. She kept it in a special box under her bed. One day, she came home from school and ran to her room. She wanted to put on her crown and pretend to rule over her toys. But when she opened the box, she gasped. Her crown was gone! She looked everywhere, but she could not find it. She felt very sad and angry. She started to cry. Who took her crown? She wondered. She heard a knock on the door. It was her brother, Tom. He had a big smile on his face. He was holding something behind his back. "Hi, Sara," he said. "I have a surprise for you." Sara wiped her tears and glared at him. She did not like surprises from Tom. He was always mean to her. He liked to tease her and take her things. "What is it?" she asked. Tom brought his hand forward. He was holding her crown. But it was not shiny and pretty anymore. It was broken and dirty. He had found it in the trash can outside. He had smashed it with a rock and thrown it away. "Look what I found," he said. "Your silly old crown. It was so ancient and ugly. I did you a favor. You don't need it anymore." Sara felt a surge of pain and fury. She screamed and lunged at Tom. She tried to grab her crown from his hand. But he was bigger and stronger. He pushed her away and laughed. "Stop it, Sara," he said. "It's just a toy. You're such a baby. Why do you cry over a stupid crown?" Sara did not listen. She cried harder and harder. She wanted her crown back. She loved her crown. It was not just a toy. It was her treasure. She hated Tom. He was a bad brother. He had ruined her crown and her fun. She wished she had a real crown. A crown that was not ancient and ugly. A crown that was not easy to break and throw away. A crown that would make Tom cry.

Once upon a time, there was a huge frog. The frog had a big stomach. He lived in a small pond. He liked to learn new things. One day, the huge frog met a small fish. The fish said, "Hello, Mr. Frog. I want to learn how to jump high like you." The frog said, "I can teach you. But first, you must come close to me." The small fish swam closer to the frog. The huge frog opened his big mouth and ate the small fish. His stomach got even bigger. The small fish did not learn how to jump high. The frog was happy, but the fish was gone. The pond was sad without the small fish.

Once upon a time, in a small, cheerful town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it rained all day long, and Lily had to stay inside. She was sad because she wanted to play outside. The next day, the sun came out and dried everything. Lily was so happy that she could play outside again. She ran to her garden to see if her plants were okay. She saw that her pepper plant was still wet from the rain. Lily said to the pepper plant, "Don't worry, little pepper. I will help you get dry." She took a soft cloth and gently wiped the water off the leaves. The pepper plant seemed to smile at her. As Lily played in her garden, she noticed a cheerful butterfly flying around. The butterfly said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping the pepper plant. Now it can grow big and strong." Lily smiled and knew that she had done a good thing. From that day on, Lily always took care of her pepper plant and all the other plants in her garden. She learned that even the smallest act of kindness can make a big difference. And the cheerful town lived happily ever after.
