Category: Intermediate
Title: The Soldier and the Girl

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a soldier. He was very brave and strong. The soldier had a big, open heart and always helped others. One day, the soldier met a little girl who was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the soldier. The girl said, "I am hungry and have no food." The soldier wanted to help the girl, so he decided to prepare some food for her. The soldier went to the market and got some bread, cheese, and fruit. He went back to the girl and said, "Here, I made this for you." The girl was very happy and thanked the soldier. They sat down and ate the food together. From that day on, they became good friends and the soldier continued to help others with his big, open heart.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to swim fast. He was a quick swimmer. One day, Fin saw a big, fancy fish. The fancy fish had many colors. Fin wanted to talk to the fancy fish. "Hi, I am Fin. I can swim quick. Can you swim quick too?" Fin asked the fancy fish. The fancy fish smiled and said, "Yes, I can swim quick too. My name is Rainbow." Fin and Rainbow swam together. They swam up and down, and all around. They had so much fun. But then, they saw a big, dark cave. Fin and Rainbow looked at each other. "Let's swim in the cave," said Rainbow. "But we must be quick. There might be something hiding in there." Fin and Rainbow swam into the cave. They swam very quick. Suddenly, they saw a big, scary shark! The shark tried to catch them. But Fin and Rainbow were too quick. They swam out of the cave and away from the shark. They were safe. From that day on, Fin and Rainbow were best friends. They swam together and had many fun adventures. And they always remembered to be quick when they swam into a dark cave.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny. The soft bunny loved to paint. Every day, the bunny would paint with many colors. The bunny was very happy. One day, the soft bunny found a big rock. The bunny thought, "I will paint my brain on the rock!" The bunny started to paint the brain with red, blue, and yellow. The bunny was very excited. But then, it started to rain. The rain washed away the paint. The soft bunny's brain painting was gone. The bunny was very sad. The end.

Lila and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big tree with many leaves. They want to make a pile of leaves and jump in it. "Help me, Ben!" Lila says. "We need more leaves for our pile." "OK, Lila!" Ben says. He runs to the tree and picks up some leaves. He brings them to Lila and puts them on the pile. They do this many times, until they have a big pile of leaves. They are happy and excited. "Ready, Lila?" Ben says. "Let's jump in the pile!" "Ready, Ben!" Lila says. "One, two, three, jump!" They jump in the pile and laugh. The leaves are soft and colorful. They feel like flying. But then, they hear a loud voice. "Hey, you two!" The voice says. "What are you doing with my leaves?" They look up and see a man. He is angry and scary. He is the gardener of the park. He has a rake in his hand. "These are my leaves!" He says. "I worked hard to rake them. You are making a mess!" Lila and Ben are scared. They stand up and try to run away. But the pile of leaves is difficult to get out of. They slip and fall. The leaves stick to their clothes and hair. "Stop, you two!" The man says. "Come here!" Lila and Ben cry. They are sorry for making a mess. They wish they had not played with the leaves. They wish they had listened to their mom, who told them to be nice and respectful in the park.

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his cage. He was sad because he wanted to fly outside. Tim had a friend, a cat named Sam. Sam was not a stupid cat, he was very smart. He wanted to help Tim. Sam said to Tim, "I will help you escape from your cage." Tim was happy to hear this. Sam used his paw to open the cage door. The door was wide open and Tim could see the sky. Tim started to display his beautiful wings. He was so happy to fly outside. Sam was happy too. They both played outside and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, Tim went back to his cage because he knew it was his safe place. Sam and Tim were very good friends and they always helped each other.
