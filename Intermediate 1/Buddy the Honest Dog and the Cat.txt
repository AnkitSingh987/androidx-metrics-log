Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Honest Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was an honest dog named Buddy. Buddy lived in a small house with a nice family. They had a big kitchen where they all liked to eat and play. One day, Buddy saw a big cat in the kitchen. The cat wanted to eat Buddy's food. Buddy did not like that. He wanted the cat to yield and go away. So, Buddy barked at the cat. The cat was scared and ran away. Buddy's family was happy that he was an honest and brave dog. They all had fun in the kitchen and ate together. Buddy was a good dog and loved his family very much.

John and his mom were at the park. John liked to play on the swings and was having a lot of fun. His mom said to him, "John, it's time to go home now." John said, "No! I want to stay here!" His mom said, "It's time to go now. We have to go to the desk to get your homework done before bed." John grumbled but followed his mom out the park. When they got home, his mom sat him at the desk. She said, "Now, you need to finish these good worksheets." John sighed but knew his mom was right. He started to work, and soon got it finished. His mom said, "See John, when you do something good like homework, you help yourself and make your mom happy." John smiled. He was glad he finished his homework, and his mom gave him a hug and a kiss. They both went to bed happy that night.

Lily liked to print with her crayons. She had many colors and papers. She printed flowers and trees and animals. She gave some to her mom and dad and they smiled. One day, Lily saw a big sunflower in the garden. It was yellow and bright and tall. Lily wanted to print it. She ran to get her crayons and paper. She sat on the grass and looked at the sunflower. "Hello, sunflower," Lily said. "You are very pretty. Can I print you?" The sunflower did not say anything. It looked down at the ground. It was sad. "Why are you sad, sunflower?" Lily asked. "Is something wrong?" The sunflower sighed. "I am sad because I have no friends. All the other flowers are gone. They were picked or dried or blown away. I am alone." Lily felt sorry for the sunflower. She wanted to help. She had an idea. "I can be your friend, sunflower," Lily said. "And I can print you and give you to my mom and dad. They will like you very much. And I can print more flowers and put them around you. Then you will not be alone." The sunflower smiled. It liked Lily's idea. It nodded at Lily. "Thank you, Lily," the sunflower said. "You are very kind. I would like that very much." Lily smiled too. She was happy. She started to print the sunflower and the other flowers. She used many colors and papers. She made the garden bright and beautiful. She and the sunflower became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw other kids playing and having fun. Tim wanted to join in, but he was too shy. He just stood there and blinked his eyes, hoping someone would ask him to play. Then, a big girl named Sue came up to Tim. She asked him if he wanted to play with her. Tim was happy and said yes. But Sue told him he was not fit enough to play with them. Tim felt sad and walked away. Tim did not give up. He practiced running, jumping, and playing every day. Soon, he became very fit. The next time he went to the park, all the kids saw how fit he was. They asked him to join their play, even Sue. Tim was happy and had a great time playing with his new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little house in the woods. A dog named Spot lived in the house. Spot loved to go on tours and explore the woods. One day, Spot met a new friend, a cat named Fluffy. "Hi Fluffy," said Spot. "Do you want to go on a tour with me in the woods?" "Sure, Spot!" said Fluffy. "I love to explore!" So, Spot and Fluffy went on a tour in the woods. They saw many fun things like trees, flowers, and birds. They played and had a great time. Then, they found a big hill. "Let's test how fast we can run up the hill!" said Spot. "Okay!" said Fluffy. Spot and Fluffy raced up the hill. They were both very fast, but Spot was a little faster. They laughed and played at the top of the hill. Then, they went back to the tidy little house, tired but happy from their fun tour in the woods.
