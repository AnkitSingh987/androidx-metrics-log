Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a brave little bird named Billy. Billy loved to grab worms from the ground. One day, Billy saw a big worm in the grass. He wanted to grab it, but the worm was near a big tree. Billy was scared of the big tree because he heard a loud sound from it. But he wanted the worm so much. So, he thought of a plan. He would be brave and get the worm when the loud sound was gone. The next day, Billy heard the loud sound again. But this time, he was brave. He flew down and grabbed the worm. As he did, he saw that the loud sound was just a squirrel playing. Billy learned that being brave can help you face your fears. And so, the brave little bird and the squirrel became friends. They played together every day. The moral of the story is that being brave can help you face your fears and make new friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a poor man named Tom. He had a tiny cell where he kept all his things. One day, Tom found a big box outside his cell. He did not know where it came from. Tom wanted to remove the box from his cell. He asked his friend, Sam, for help. "Sam, can you help me remove this big box?" Tom asked. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you." They both pushed and pulled, and the box was removed from the cell. Inside the box, they found many toys and clothes. Tom and Sam were very happy. They shared the toys and clothes with other poor kids in the town. All the kids were happy and played together. Tom and Sam became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Bobo. Bobo loved to play in the shower. One day, Bobo wanted to borrow his friend's toy. Bobo's friend, Lulu, did not want to share her toy. They began to fight over it. Bobo pulled the toy from Lulu, and Lulu was very sad. In the end, the toy broke because they fought too much. Both Bobo and Lulu were sad. They learned that fighting is not good, and sharing is better.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Max. Max liked to tell everyone what to do. He lived in a house with a little girl named Lily and her mom. One day, Max saw a mirror on the wall. He looked at it and saw another cat. Max thought that the cat in the mirror was trying to be the boss of him. Max said to the cat in the mirror, "You can't tell me what to do! I am the boss!" But the cat in the mirror just copied Max. Max got mad and tried to fight the cat in the mirror. Lily saw Max being mad at the mirror and went to her mom. "Mom, Max is mad at the mirror," said Lily. Her mom smiled and said, "Lily, that's just Max's reflection. It reminds him of himself. Let's go and show him that it's just a mirror." Lily and her mom went to Max and showed him that the cat in the mirror was just his reflection. Max felt silly but he learned that he didn't have to be bossy all the time. From that day on, Max was a nicer cat and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wide river. On one side, there was a big tree. A little boy named Tim wanted to cross the river. He saw a long staff under the tree. Tim thought, "I can use this staff to help me walk through the river." As Tim started to cross the river, he met a frog. The frog said, "Hi Tim! Can you carry me to the other side?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you, little frog." So, Tim put the frog on his shoulder and carried the frog with the help of the staff. When they got to the other side, the frog said, "Thank you, Tim!" Then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a big, beautiful bird! The bird said, "I was a frog, but now I am free because you helped me. The moral of the story is to always help others, even when it seems hard." Tim was happy he had helped the frog, and they became good friends.
