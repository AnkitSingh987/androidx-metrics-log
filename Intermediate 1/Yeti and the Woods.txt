Category: Intermediate
Title: Yeti and the Woods

Once upon a time, a little yet named Yeti loved to travel. He had long legs that made him walk very fast. One day, he went for a walk in the woods. As Yeti walked, he saw a big hole in the ground. He was scared but wanted to see what was inside. So, he jumped in the hole and began to travel down. Yeti thought the hole would be dark and scary, but it was not! Inside, he found a room full of toys and new friends to play with. He was very happy and played all day. The hole was a magic place that made Yeti's day full of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a small, pretty purse that her mom gave her. Sue loved her purse very much. It was warm and soft, and she took it everywhere she went. One day, Sue went to the park to play. She put her purse on the swing and pushed it back and forth. But then, she saw a little boy who was sad. He was suffering because he lost his toy. Sue wanted to help, so she stopped playing with her purse and went to the little boy. Sue asked the boy what his toy looked like. He said it was a small, red ball. Together, they looked all around the park. They found the red ball under a big tree. The boy was very happy, and Sue felt good that she could help. They played together for the rest of the day, and Sue put her warm purse on the swing again. The little boy and Sue became good friends and always played together at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big curtain in his room. He felt an urge to play with it. He called his friend, Sue, to come over and play with him. Sue came to his house and saw the curtain too. They decided to play hide and seek. Tim said, "I will hide first, and you find me." He hid behind the curtain. Sue looked and looked but could not find Tim. Then, she saw the curtain move a little. She went to the curtain and found Tim hiding there. They laughed and laughed. Next, it was Sue's turn to hide. Tim thought Sue would be selfish and hide behind the curtain too. But Sue hid under the bed instead. Tim looked behind the curtain but did not find her. He looked more and found her under the bed. They both had lots of fun playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. The crab lived in a tight shell near the sea. The crab had many friends, like fish and turtles. They played together every day. One day, a new friend came to play. The new friend was a bird. The bird did not understand how to play with the crab and the other sea friends. The crab tried to show the bird how to play, but the bird still did not understand. The crab did not give up. The crab and the bird tried different ways to play together. They found a game they both liked. The crab and the bird were happy. Now, they all played together and understood each other.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would play all day long in the sun. One hot day, Tim and Spot were playing outside. They were both very thirsty. Tim went to the faucet to get some water for them. He turned the faucet and water came out fast. Spot was happy to see the water speed out. Spot drank the water quickly and gave Tim a big, wet kiss. Tim laughed and hugged his loyal friend. They were happy to have each other and to share the water from the faucet. They played together all day, and Spot always stayed by Tim's side.

Once upon a time, there was a different dog named Spot. Spot had many colors on his fur. He had red, blue, and green spots. He looked very different from other dogs. One day, Spot went for a walk. He met a little girl named Lily. Lily saw Spot and said, "Wow! You are a different dog. I like your colors." Spot wagged his tail and said, "Thank you! I like your dress." Lily had a pretty dress with flowers on it. Spot and Lily became friends. They played in the park every day. Lily would mind Spot and make sure he was safe. They had lots of fun together. And everyone loved the different dog and his little friend.
