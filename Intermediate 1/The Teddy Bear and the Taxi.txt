Category: Intermediate
Title: The Teddy Bear and the Taxi

Once there was a Teddy Bear who wanted to go for a drive. He was looking for a taxi when he saw one. It was yellow and it looked very nice. So he waved at the taxi and it stopped by Teddy. Teddy said, “Hello. I want to go for a ride. Can you take me?” The taxi driver said, “Yes sure. Hop in.” So Teddy hopped into the taxi and they drove off. They drove for a long time and it got very hot. Teddy was getting very tired. “Can we stop and take a break?” Teddy asked. The taxi driver said, “Sure. Let's introduce you to a friend of mine.” So they stopped at a big house and the taxi driver went inside. But Teddy got a funny feeling. Then a big scary man came out of the house. He said he was a policeman and that the taxi was not a real one. The taxi driver had tricked Teddy and he was taking Teddy to sell him! Teddy had been tricked and the bad man took Teddy away. Teddy never got his ride.

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. The cat saw a bottle of wine on the table. The cat said, "I want to drink the wine. Can you help me open it?" The dog said, "Yes, I can help you." The dog jumped up and used his teeth to open the bottle. The cat took a sip of the wine and said, "This wine is weak. I don't like it." The dog said, "Let me try." The dog took a sip and said, "You are right. It is weak." Just then, a little girl came into the kitchen. She saw the open bottle of wine and the cat and dog close to it. The little girl said, "Oh no! You should not drink wine. It is not for cats and dogs." The cat and dog looked at each other and said, "We are sorry." The little girl smiled and said, "It's okay. Let's go play outside." The cat and dog were happy to play with the little girl, and they all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a big, green park, a little girl named Lily and her mom went to have a picnic. They brought a soft, red blanket to sit on. They also brought yummy food in a big basket. Lily was very happy and excited. Before they could sit and eat, Lily saw a small, friendly dog. She wanted to play with the dog, but her mom said, "Not now, Lily. First, we need to set up our picnic." Lily felt sad, but she knew her mom was right. She helped her mom spread the soft blanket on the grass and put the food on it. After they ate, Lily saw the small dog again. This time, her mom said, "Now you can play with the dog, Lily." Lily ran and played with the dog. They had so much fun! The dog liked the soft blanket too. It was a very happy day for Lily, her mom, and the small, friendly dog.

One day, Tom wanted to test his new toy car. He went to the garage to find it. He looked everywhere but could not find the car. Tom felt frustrated. Tom asked his mom, "Mom, where is my car?" His mom said, "I put it on the top shelf, let me help you get it." Tom and his mom tried to reach the car, but it was too high. They tried to use a chair, but it was still too high. Tom felt more frustrated. He wanted to play with his car now. Finally, Tom's dad came home. He saw Tom and his mom trying to get the car. Dad said, "I can help!" He picked up Tom and put him on his shoulders. Tom reached up and got his car. Tom was happy he could test his new toy car now.

Once upon a time, there was a mixer. The mixer was big and loud. It lived in a kitchen with a girl named Sally. Sally liked to bake cookies and cakes. She used the mixer to mix the ingredients. One day, Sally's friend, Tim, came to visit. Tim heard the harsh sound of the mixer. "What is that?" he asked. "That is my mixer," Sally said. "It helps me make yummy treats." Sally and Tim decided to bake cookies. They used the mixer to mix the dough. After the cookies were in the oven, Sally and Tim sat down to relax. They talked and laughed while they waited for the cookies to bake. Soon, the cookies were ready, and they enjoyed their tasty treats together.
