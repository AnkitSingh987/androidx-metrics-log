Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Cake

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy did not like school. She thought it was boring. She just wanted to play with her toys and have fun. One day, Amy's mom told her that she had a surprise. The surprise was a big, yummy cake. But, she said that Amy could only have the cake if she went to school and tried to have fun. Amy thought about the cake and decided to try. At school, Amy met a new friend named Tim. They played together and laughed a lot. They even made a funny picture with paint. Suddenly, school was not so boring anymore. When Amy got home, her mom gave her the big, yummy cake. But, Amy and Tim played too rough and they spoil the cake. They were sad, but they learned that school can be fun and that they should be careful with yummy treats.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big wardrobe in his room. It was dark inside the wardrobe, and Tim was scared of it. One day, Tim's mom saw him looking at the wardrobe. She asked, "Why are you scared, Tim?" Tim said, "It's dark in there, and I think there are monsters." His mom smiled and said, "I promise there are no monsters in there. Let's open it together." Tim and his mom opened the wardrobe. They saw clothes, shoes, and toys, but no monsters. Tim's mom said, "See? No monsters. Just your things." Tim felt better and wasn't scared of the dark wardrobe anymore. And he knew his mom would always keep her promises.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, purple ball. She loved to play with her purple ball every day. One day, Mia saw a boy with a ball too. His ball was blue, but it looked just like her original purple ball. Mia frowned. The next day, Mia and the boy played together with their balls. Mia was very happy, but she still frowned when she looked at the blue ball. She thought her purple ball was the best. The boy saw Mia frown and felt sad. One day, the boy came to play with a new ball. This ball was purple, just like Mia's! Mia smiled when she saw it. They played together with their purple balls and had lots of fun. Mia never frowned again when she played with the boy and their purple balls.

Jimmy saw a shark in the sea and he was very excited. He shouted, "Look! It's a shark!" His mum smiled and said, "Yes, it is." The shark dived down deep, and Jimmy was sad. He said, "I want to see the shark." His mum told him, "Wait, the shark will display again soon." After a few minutes, the shark reappeared. But its fin was broken, so it wasn't swimming very fast. Jimmy laughed with joy and said, "The shark is going slow, it's funny!" Mum smiled and said, "It's ok, the shark still looks beautiful. Even when it's broken, it's still wonderful to see." Jimmy was happy and said, "I'm happy we saw the shark together." And he smiled.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the wild woods. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he was climbing, he saw a small bird in the tree. The bird looked sad and was suffering. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I have a question. I lost my mom and I don't know where she is." Tim wanted to help the bird find her mom. They looked all around the wild woods, but they could not find the bird's mom. Then, they saw something unexpected. A big, friendly bear was holding the bird's mom in her paw. The bear had found her and was keeping her safe. The bird was so happy to see her mom, and they all became good friends.

Lily was a chubby bunny who loved to skip and hop in the meadow. She liked to eat the juicy carrots and the sweet clover that grew there. She did not like to share her food with anyone, not even her friends. One day, she saw a big, shiny carrot that looked very tasty. She skipped over to it and tried to pull it out of the ground. But it was too hard for her. She tugged and tugged, but the carrot did not move. She heard a voice behind her. It was Max, a wise old owl who lived in a tree nearby. He said, "Lily, you are being greedy. That carrot is too big for you. Why don't you share it with your friends? They can help you pull it out, and then you can all enjoy it together." Lily did not listen to Max. She said, "No, this is my carrot. I found it first. I don't need any help. Go away, you old bird." She kept trying to pull the carrot, but it was no use. Max shook his head and flew away. He said, "Lily, you are missing the wisdom of sharing. You will learn it the hard way." Soon, Lily heard a loud roar. She turned around and saw a big, hungry bear who had smelled the carrot. He said, "Hello, little bunny. What a nice snack you have there. And what a nice snack you are too." He reached out his paw to grab Lily and the carrot. Lily was very scared. She wished she had listened to Max and shared the carrot with her friends. She wished she had some friends to help her now. She tried to run away, but the bear was faster. He caught her and the carrot, and ate them both. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. Greed can lead to trouble. Wisdom is better than foolishness.
