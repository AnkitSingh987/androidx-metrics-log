Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thin Cat and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom lived in a big house with his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. They liked to play together, but sometimes they would fight. One day, Tom and Jerry found a big cookie on the floor. They both wanted the cookie, but they didn't want to share. Tom said, "I want the cookie!" Jerry replied, "No, I want it!" The room was filled with silence as they stared at each other. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's split the cookie in half. You can have one half, and I can have the other half." Jerry agreed, and they broke the cookie into two pieces. They both enjoyed their half of the cookie and continued to play together, happy that they had solved their problem.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the shop with her mom. She wanted to buy a toy. Lily saw an impressive doll with a big smile. She loved it so much. She asked her mom, "Can I have this doll, please?" Her mom said, "Do you remember we have to buy food for dinner? We can't buy the doll today. Maybe next time." Lily was sad, but she knew her mom was right. Later, they went home and made dinner together. Lily still thought about the impressive doll, but she knew it was important to help her mom. She said, "I will remember the doll, and maybe one day, I can have it." Her mom smiled and hugged her.

Once upon a time, there was a young dog named Max. Max lived in a small house with his family. He loved to play with his ball and run around the furniture. One day, Max was playing near the furniture when he sniffed some dust. The dust made Max want to sneeze. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. "Achoo!" Max sneezed so big that his ball flew up in the air. Max's family heard the sneeze and came to see if he was okay. They saw the ball in the air and laughed. Max was happy that his sneeze made everyone laugh. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. It was very pretty and had a spark. The rock was in a big forest with many trees. One day, a small boy found the rock. He was very happy and wanted to gain more rocks like it. The boy looked and looked for more smooth rocks with sparks. He found many rocks, but they were not smooth and did not have sparks. He was sad, but he did not give up. He wanted to gain more rocks like the first one. One day, the boy found a big cave. Inside the cave, there were many smooth rocks with sparks. He was very happy and started to take the rocks. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bear came out of the cave. The bear was angry because the boy took the rocks. The bear chased the boy, and the boy dropped all the rocks. The boy ran away and never found any more smooth rocks with sparks again.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck. The duck lived on a farm with many other animals. The duck was not happy, because every day was the same and very boring. One day, the duck said, "I quit! I will go find something fun to do." The duck left the farm and went to a big park. In the park, the duck saw kids playing and laughing. The duck joined the kids and played with them all day. They had so much fun! The duck was not bored anymore. From that day on, the duck was always happy and never went back to the boring farm.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a big farm with her friends. They played and ate grass all day. One day, Daisy saw a big tree. She wanted to lean on it. But her friend, a little bird, said, "No, Daisy! Don't lean on the tree! It might fall on you!" Daisy did not listen. She leaned on the tree, and it started to shake. The tree fell down with a loud "boom!" Daisy was scared and sad. She said, "I'm sorry, little bird. I should have listened to you." The little bird forgave Daisy, and they became best friends. They played together and took care of each other from that day on.
