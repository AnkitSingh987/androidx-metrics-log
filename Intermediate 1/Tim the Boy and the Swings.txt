Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Swings

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to travel with his family. One day, they went on a trip to the big park. Tim was very excited and impatient to play with his friends. When they got to the park, Tim saw his friend, Sue. They played on the swings and slides. Then, they sat down to eat their lunch. Tim said, "I had a nightmare last night." Sue asked, "What was it about?" Tim replied, "A big, scary monster chased me." Sue tried to make Tim feel better. "Don't worry, Tim. It was just a bad dream. Monsters aren't real." Tim felt better and they continued to play at the park. They had so much fun that Tim forgot all about his nightmare. At the end of the day, they left the park with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. Tim was feeling gloomy because he was sick. The doctor said, "Tim, you need an operation to feel better." Tim was scared, but his mom held his hand. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. It will be okay. The doctor will help you feel better." The day of the operation came. Tim and his mom went to the hospital. The doctor told Tim to close his eyes and count to ten. Tim did what the doctor said. When Tim woke up, the operation was all done. He felt much better and wasn't gloomy anymore. Tim and his mom were so happy!

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin was a calm fish who lived in a small pond. One day, he saw a big frog near the pond. The frog said, "Hi, I'm Fred. Do you want to play with me?" Fin was scared and wanted to escape, but he tried to move very fast. He swam in circles around Fred. The frog jumped high in the air, trying to catch Fin. Then, something unexpected happened. Fred fell into the pond with a big splash! Fred started to laugh. "I didn't know I could swim!" he said. Fin and Fred became best friends, and they played together in the pond every day.

Once upon a time, there was a soft, little cat named Muffin. Muffin loved to play and tease her best friend, a big, red ball. They played all day long, and Muffin was always happy. One day, Muffin saw a big, tall tree. She wanted to tease the red ball up in the tree. So, Muffin jumped and jumped, trying to get the ball up there. But, the ball was too big and too heavy. Muffin was sad. Then, a kind bird saw Muffin and wanted to help. The bird took the red ball in its beak and flew up to the top of the tree. Muffin was so happy! She climbed the tree and teased the red ball all day long. And from that day on, Muffin and the kind bird were best friends too.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat popcorn. One day, Tom was very hungry, so he decided to make some popcorn. Tom went to the kitchen and put the popcorn in a big pot. He turned on the stove and waited. Soon, the popcorn started to pop. It made a loud noise, and Tom was so happy. Tom wanted to share his popcorn with his friends, so he had an idea. He used a machine to print pictures of his popcorn. Then, he gave the pictures to his friends. They all loved the pictures and wanted to eat popcorn with Tom. And so, they all had a fun popcorn party together.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat named Billy. Billy had a big horn on his head. One day, he found a hat in the grass. He thought it would be fun to wear the hat on his horn. Billy wore the hat all day. He played with his friends and they all liked his hat. But then, he saw a big tree. Billy thought it would be fun to hit the tree with his horn. He forgot he was wearing the hat. Billy ran fast and hit the tree with his horn. The hat fell off and got hurt. Billy felt sad because his hat was hurt. He learned that he should be careful when wearing something on his horn. From that day on, Billy was more careful and his hat stayed safe and happy.
