Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Journey and the Lake

Once upon a time there was a great journey. Two children went on this journey. One child was three years old and the other child was four years old. They both wanted to know where the journey would take them. As they walked along, the children saw lots of different things. They saw big mountains, tall trees, and a big lake. Then, they reached the end of the journey and saw a great big surprise! They had reached a magical land full of wonderful things. The three year old was so excited that she knew it would be a great adventure! The children explored this magical land and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they said goodbye to the magical land, feeling happy and knowing that it had been a great journey.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play with clay. One day, he found a big, regular ball of clay. Tim was very happy. Tim went to his friend, Sam. He said, "Look! I found clay. Let's play together!" Sam was excited too. They decided to spread the clay and make fun shapes. They made a sun, a cat, and a house. As they played, another friend, Lily, came by. She saw Tim and Sam having fun with the clay. She felt sad because she didn't have any clay. Tim and Sam saw Lily and asked, "Do you want to play with us?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, please!" They all played together and had a great time. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. When we share, we can all be happy and have fun together.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He was very happy to see the big, bright sun in the sky. Spot liked to sniff the grass and look for bugs. He wagged his tail and barked, "Woof, woof!" As Spot walked, he saw a big piece of meat on the ground. It smelled so good! Spot wanted to eat the meat, but he was not sure if it was safe. He looked around and saw a big cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was very wise and knew a lot. Spot asked Whiskers, "Can I eat this meat? I don't want to get hurt." Whiskers looked at the meat and said, "No, Spot. That meat is not good. It will make you feel sick and hurt." Spot listened to Whiskers and left the meat alone. They played together in the sun and had a fun day.

One day, a little girl was at the park with her mom. She wanted to play on the swings, but her mom wanted her to go home. She became very stubborn and started to cry. The girl said, "I want to stay here and play longer". Her mom put her hand on the girl's shoulder and said, "Sweetie pie, it's getting late. Put on your watch and let's go home!" The little girl really wanted to stay, but with a stubborn pout, she put on her watch. As they were walking home, her mom said, "You were very stubborn in the park. Next time, if I tell you it's time to go home, you should listen. It's important to be obedient." The little girl thought about this for a moment, and then smiled. She knew that her mom was right, and the next time, she would be more obedient.

Once upon a time, there was a huge room with a shelf. On the shelf, there was a big red ball. The ball liked to rest on the shelf all day. It was very happy there. One day, the ball saw a little girl named Amy. Amy saw the ball and wanted to play. She tried to get the ball, but it was too high. Amy was sad. Then, a nice cat came to help. The cat jumped up and pushed the ball off the shelf. As the ball fell, it turned into a big, soft pillow! Amy was so surprised and happy. Now, Amy and the cat could play and rest on the pillow together.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to visit her aunt. Her aunt had a big, pretty house with a nice garden. Lily felt very happy to see her aunt. She liked to play with her aunt's toys and eat her yummy cookies. While playing outside, Lily noticed a small, sleepy cat lying under a tree. The cat was very cute and soft. Lily wanted to play with the cat, but she didn't want to wake it up. So, she went to her aunt and told her about the sleepy cat. Her aunt smiled and said, "That's our new friend, Mr. Whiskers. He's a very sleepy cat, but he loves to play when he's awake." Lily and her aunt sat near Mr. Whiskers and waited for him to wake up. When he did, they all played together and had a fun day.
