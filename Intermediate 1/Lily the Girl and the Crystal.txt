Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Crystal

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, round, shiny crystal. She loved her crystal so much. She would play with it every day. One day, Lily was very hungry. She wanted to find some food. She took her crystal with her and went outside. She saw a big tree with yummy fruits on it. Lily wanted to get the fruits, but they were too high. Lily had an idea. She put her crystal on the ground and pushed it with her hands. The crystal rolled and rolled until it was under the tree. Lily climbed on the crystal and reached for the fruits. She picked the fruits and ate them. They were so tasty! Lily was not hungry anymore. She thanked her crystal and took it back home with her. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman named Bob. He loved to catch fish in the big blue sea. Every day, he would go on his small boat and wait for the fish to come. One day, there was a big race for all the fishermen. Bob was anxious. He wanted to win the race very much. All the fishermen got in their boats and started to row. Bob rowed as fast as he could. His face was red, and his arms were tired. The race was almost over. Bob could see the finish line. He rowed faster and faster. And then, he did it! Bob won the race! All the fishermen cheered for him. Bob was so happy. From that day on, Bob was the best fisherman in the town.

One day, a boy named Tom found a cheap bicycle. It was red and shiny. He was very happy. He wanted to ride it all day. Tom started to march with his bicycle. He went up and down the street. His friends saw him and wanted to play too. They all marched together with their bicycles. They had a fun day marching and riding their bicycles. Tom loved his cheap bicycle. It was the best day ever.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big, purple bird sitting in a tree. The bird was very quiet. Sue wanted to watch the bird, so she sat down under the tree. Sue watched the quiet, purple bird. The bird looked at Sue and smiled. Sue smiled back. They were happy to be friends. After a while, the purple bird started to sing a pretty song. Sue clapped her hands and danced. The bird flew away, but Sue knew they would see each other again.

One day, a big box arrived in the mail. Inside, there was a very special toy. It was called a wink design. It was a small, dull box with some buttons on it. The little girl was very excited. She pressed some of the buttons and soon she could hear music and see flashing lights. She smiled and started to dance around the room. When she was done, the little girl decided to design something with the toy. She thought for a minute and then began pressing the buttons to make her own design. She had so much fun creating different shapes and making them flash and blink. The little girl was so proud of her work. But the best part was when she pressed the final button and the box made a special wink. It was magical and it made her giggle with glee. The girl spent the rest of the day playing with her design toy. She made all sorts of designs and every time the toy winked it made her smile. The little girl had so much fun that day.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys. They have many toys in their room. But their room is messy. Mom says they have to organize their toys. "Let's make a game," Lily says. "We can put the toys in different piles. One pile for animals, one pile for cars, one pile for dolls, and one pile for blocks." "OK," Ben says. "That sounds fun. I will start with the animals. I have a lion, a bear, a dog, and a duck." Lily starts with the cars. She has a red car, a blue car, a yellow car, and a green car. She puts them in a pile. They work hard and soon they have four piles of toys. They are proud of their work. "Look, Mom," Lily says. "We organized our toys. Can we go outside and play now?" Mom smiles. She is happy that they cleaned their room. She says, "Yes, you can go outside and play. But first, let me see your piles. Hmm, this is a pile of animals, this is a pile of cars, this is a pile of dolls, and this is a pile of blocks. Wait a minute, what is this?" She picks up a funny toy. It has a lion's head, a car's body, a doll's dress, and a block's wheels. Lily and Ben giggle. They made the funny toy together. They think it is the best toy ever. Mom laughs too. She says, "This is a very funny toy. You are very creative. But next time, please don't mix up your toys. It makes it harder to organize them. Now, go outside and enjoy the sun. It is a beautiful day."
