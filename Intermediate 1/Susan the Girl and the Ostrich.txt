Category: Intermediate
Title: Susan the Girl and the Ostrich

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Susan. She was just 3 years old and loved to explore the world around her. One day, Susan was playing in the garden when she spotted an ostrich. She was so excited and ran up to the ostrich. She wanted to pet its smooth feathers and asked the ostrich if she could. The ostrich said, "Sure thing! I love a good pet." So, Susan carefully stroked its feathers. Feeling brave, she then asked if she could throw the ostrich. The ostrich said, "Why not? I wouldn't mind!" So Susan picked up the ostrich and threw it in the air. The ostrich flew up high, before landing back safely on the grass. Susan smiled and looked at the ostrich, who said, "That was so much fun! Now it's your turn to fly! So, Susan and the ostrich flew together until dusk. Susan had the most wonderful time and decided that she wanted an ostrich of her own one day. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big party to go to. Amy got an invitation in the mail. She was very happy. Before the party, Amy's mom warned her, "Don't eat too many sweets or you will feel sick." Amy said, "Okay, mom, I won't." At the party, there were many yummy treats. Amy wanted to eat them all. Amy ate lots of sweets, even though her mom told her not to. She felt sick and her tummy hurt. She spoiled her fun at the party. Next time, Amy will listen to her mom's warning.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Tim found a big TV in his room. He turned it on and saw his favorite show. He was happy. Tim's mom saw him watching TV. She said, "Tim, you need to pause the TV and go outside to play." Tim didn't want to pause the TV, but he listened to his mom. He went outside to find someone to play with. Tim saw a girl named Sue. He asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "No, I don't want to play with you." Tim was very sad. He went back inside and turned on the TV again. But the TV was broken. Tim was even more sad and lonely.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a long vine. He wanted to swing on the vine like a monkey. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come swing on this vine with me!" Sue looked at the vine and said, "Tim, that vine is too high. We need help to reach it." They asked a tall man for help. The man reminded them, "Be careful when you swing on the vine." Tim and Sue said, "Thank you!" The man helped them reach the vine, and they started swinging. As they swung, Tim and Sue saw a distant hill. They laughed and played, feeling happy. They swung on the vine all day, and the park became their favorite place to play.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily had a big box of toys. She loved to play with them every day. One day, Lily found a small, sharp pin in her toy box. She didn't know what it was, so she asked her mom. "Mom, what is this?" Lily showed the pin to her mom. Her mom looked at it and said, "That is a pin, Lily. It is very sharp. It can hurt you. I must warn you, do not play with it." Lily listened to her mom and put the pin away. Later, she went outside to play with her friend, Tom. They found a big, green bug. It was very disgusting. Lily and Tom did not like the bug, so they ran away from it. As they played, Lily told Tom about the pin her mom had warned her about. "My mom said it can hurt us," she said. Tom nodded, and they promised each other not to play with sharp things. From that day on, Lily and Tom played safely and had lots of fun. They never forgot the warning about the pin and the disgusting bug. And they lived happily ever after.
