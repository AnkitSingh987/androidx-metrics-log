Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brilliant Robot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant robot. This robot liked to display many colors. It could be red, blue, or even green. The robot had many friends in the park. One day, the robot met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. The robot asked, "Why are you sad, Sue?" Sue said, "I lost my ball." The robot wanted to help Sue find her ball. The robot and Sue looked all around the park. They found the ball under a big tree. Sue was happy now. She said, "Thank you, robot!" The robot was happy to help Sue. They played together and had fun. The moral of the story is: Helping others can make you happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Coco. Coco loved to draw with her crayons. One day, she saw a big tree and wanted to draw a picture of it. Coco took out her crayons and started to draw. As she was drawing, a little boy named Timmy came by. He saw Coco and said, "Hi, bird! What are you doing?" Coco said, "I am drawing a big tree with my crayons. Do you want to help me?" Timmy was happy and said, "Yes, I want to help!" Timmy and Coco drew together. They used red, blue, and yellow crayons to make the tree colorful. Then, it started to rain. The rain poured down on their drawing, but it made the colors blend together and look even more beautiful. In the end, Timmy and Coco had a lot of fun drawing together. They made a new friend and created a beautiful, colorful tree.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the airport with his mom and dad. They were going on a trip. Tim was very excited because he had never been on a plane before. At the airport, there were lots of people and big planes. Before getting on the plane, Tim's mom said, "Let's pray for a safe trip." So, they all held hands and prayed together. Tim felt happy and safe after they prayed. On the plane, a nice lady gave them some tasty snacks to eat. Tim said, "Yummy! These are very tasty!" His mom and dad smiled and they all enjoyed their snacks. Soon, the plane landed and they had a great trip. Tim couldn't wait to go on another adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little mouse named Tim. He lived in a small, clean house near a big tree. Tim loved to keep his home neat and tidy. One day, Tim met a big, friendly bear named Ben. Ben was hungry and asked Tim, "Do you have any food?" Tim wanted to help his new friend, so he went to his house and got a pear. He offered the pear to Ben and said, "Here, eat this." Ben took a bite of the pear and smiled. "Thank you, Tim," he said. "This pear is very yummy." After they both ate, they cleaned up the mess together. From that day on, Tim and Ben were best friends. The moral of the story is: always be kind and helpful to others, and good things will come your way.

Lily and Ben were playing in the pool with their toys. They had a ball, a duck, and a cube. The cube was red and shiny, and it could float on the water. "Look, Lily, I can dive and catch the cube!" Ben said. He took a big breath and went under the water. He opened his eyes and saw the cube. He reached out and grabbed it. He was happy. "Wow, Ben, you are good at diving!" Lily said. She wanted to try too. She put the cube back on the water and got ready to dive. But she was scared. She did not like to go under the water. She did not know what was there. "Come on, Lily, it's fun!" Ben said. He saw that Lily was scared. He wanted to help her. He had an idea. He took the duck and put it next to the cube. "Look, Lily, the duck is your friend. He will go with you. He will show you the way. He is very wise. He knows the pool. He will not let anything hurt you." Lily looked at the duck. She liked the duck. He was yellow and soft, and he had a smile. She felt a little better. She took a big breath and went under the water. She opened her eyes and saw the duck and the cube. She reached out and grabbed them both. She was happy. "Yay, Lily, you did it!" Ben said. He was proud of Lily. He gave her a hug. They both smiled. They played with the duck and the cube. They had fun. They were brave. They learned to dive.
