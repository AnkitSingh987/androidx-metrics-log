Category: Intermediate
Title: The Colorful Bird and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird lived in a big country. The bird had many friends. One day, the bird met a new friend. The new friend was a frog. The bird and frog played all day. The bird said, "I like you, frog." The frog said, "I like you too, bird." They were very happy. They played in the sun and in the water. But then, something unexpected happened. The sky became dark. It started to rain. The bird and frog got wet. They tried to find a place to hide. They found a big tree. The tree was not wet. The bird and frog went under the tree. They did not get wet anymore. The rain stopped. The sun came out again. The bird and frog were happy. They were dry and not soaked. They played and had more fun in their big, colorful country.

One day, a boy named Tim found a light toy pistol. Tim liked to joke with his friends. He thought it would be fun to show the toy to his friend, Sam. Tim put the toy in his bag and went to find Sam. Tim found Sam in the park. "Look what I have!" Tim said to Sam. He showed Sam the light toy pistol. Sam laughed and said, "That's a funny joke, Tim! Let's play with it." They played with the light toy pistol all day. They took turns pointing it and making "bang" sounds. They laughed and joked with each other. It was a fun day for Tim and Sam.

Once upon a time, in a small ordinary place, there lived a happy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and hang with her friends in the park. They would run, jump, and have fun all day long. One day, while Kitty was playing with her friends, she saw a big shiny thing in the sky. It was so bright that all her friends stopped playing and looked up too. They had never seen anything like it before. Then, something unexpected happened. The big shiny thing came down from the sky and landed right in front of Kitty and her friends. It opened up, and out came a friendly space dog named Rover. Rover had come to visit from a far away place. Kitty and her friends were so excited to meet their new friend, and they all played together in the park. The ordinary place had become very special, and they all had a great time.

Once there was a strong crystal. It used to ring everyday and the sound was like a sweet song. Everyone in the town used to love the crystal and its beautiful ringing. Then one day, something strange happened. A big wind started to blow, and it was very strong. The wind broke the crystal and it stopped ringing. Everyone in the town was very sad and started to cry. The strong wind was strong enough to break the crystal, and it never rang again. Everyone was so sad and didn't know what to do. The crystal was gone and the sweet song of ringing was gone too.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted ice-cream. He asked his mom, "Can I have ice-cream, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but you have to finish your food first." Tim ate his food fast. He was happy to have ice-cream. But, when he opened the freezer, the ice-cream was gone! He felt sad and said, "Oh no, where is my ice-cream?" His big sister, Sue, heard him and said, "I ate it, sorry! That was stupid of me." Tim was sad, but he had an idea. He said, "Let's make more ice-cream together!" They tried to make ice-cream, but it was hard. They did not succeed at first. They tried again and again. Then, something unexpected happened. Their dog, Max, found a big tub of ice-cream behind the couch! Tim, Sue, and Max all shared the ice-cream and had a fun day. They learned that even when things go wrong, they can still have a good time together.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Max. Max loved to play with his smooth, red ball. One day, Max saw a big basket in the park. He thought it would be fun to put his ball in the basket. Max met his friend, a cat named Luna. "Do you want to play with me?" Max asked Luna. Luna said, "Yes, I would enjoy that!" They played with the ball and the basket. They had a lot of fun. But then, a big bird took the ball from the basket. Max and Luna were sad. They wanted their ball back. They had an idea. They put treats in the basket to get the bird to come back. The bird saw the treats and came down. Max and Luna took their ball back and played again. They were happy and enjoyed their day.
