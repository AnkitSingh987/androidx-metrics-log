Category: Intermediate
Title: Tara and the Garden

Tara was shy. She liked to play alone in her garden. One day, she heard a noise. She looked up and saw a tire in the tree. Tara was surprised and decided to climb the tree to get the tire. She snapped her fingers and the tire came down. Tara was excited and decided to play with the tire. She rolled it along the garden path. Suddenly, she heard a voice. "Who are you?" it said. Tara was too shy to answer. She turned and saw a little boy. He had lots of toys in a basket. "I'm Ben," he said. Tara smiled. She was no longer shy. They played together with the tire. They rolled it between the trees and made different shapes with it. They both had lots of fun. Soon, it was time for Ben to go home. He said goodbye to Tara and ran away with his basket. Tara waved to him as he disappeared. Tara was happy. She now had a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to learn about new things. One day, she went to the woods to find mushrooms. Her mom told her that some mushrooms are good to eat, but some are not. Mia wanted to learn which ones were good. In the woods, Mia saw many mushrooms. Some were big, some were small, some were red, and some were brown. Mia did not know which ones were good. Then, she saw a little squirrel. The squirrel was holding a mushroom in its hands. Mia thought the squirrel could help her learn which mushrooms were good to eat. She followed the squirrel and found a big tree. The tree had lots of mushrooms, more than any other place in the woods! The squirrel showed her which mushrooms were good. Mia was happy to learn from her new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The tree began to shake, and all the mushrooms fell down! Mia and the squirrel were surprised. They looked up and saw a big, friendly bear. The bear wanted to share the mushrooms with them. Now, Mia, the squirrel, and the bear had more mushrooms available than they could ever eat. They all became friends and learned from each other.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable big dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat oats every day for breakfast. One day, Spot's bowl was empty. He went to his friend, the little bird, to ask for help. Spot said, "Little bird, can you help me find more oats? I have none left and I am hungry." The little bird replied, "Of course, Spot! I know where to find some." As they went to find the oats, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the little bird far away. Spot was sad and worried about his friend. He didn't know what to do. But then, the little bird came back with a big bag of oats! The wind had blown him right to the oats. Spot was so happy and said, "Thank you, little bird!" They both went home and Spot could finish his breakfast. They knew they could always depend on each other.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bob. Bob loved to laugh and play with his friends. One day, Bob saw a big, tall tree. The tree had a hole in it, near the top. Bob thought it would be fun to go inside the hole. Bob flew up to the hole and went inside. The hole was dark, but Bob was not scared. He looked up and saw a ceiling. The ceiling had a small crack in it. Bob laughed and thought it was funny. He told his friends about the funny crack. One day, a big storm came. Bob went inside the hole to stay dry. The rain made the crack in the ceiling bigger. Bob was scared now. He realized that laughing at the crack was not a good idea. He should have told someone to fix it. After the storm, Bob asked his friends for help. They worked together to fix the crack in the ceiling. Bob learned that it is important to take care of our home and help each other. From that day on, Bob and his friends always helped each other and took care of their homes.
