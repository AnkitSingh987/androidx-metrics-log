Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hunter and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a hunter who lived in the forest. The hunter was tidy and always kept things neat. Every day, the hunter searched for food and made sure to protect the forest. One day, the hunter heard a noise coming from the bushes. He carefully looked through the leaves and saw a little bunny hiding. The hunter said, "Don't be scared little bunny, I'm here to protect you." The bunny nodded, and the hunter gently scooped it up in his hands. The hunter promised to take care of the little bunny and to make sure it stayed safe. He built a cozy home for the bunny and made sure it had all the food and water it needed. Then, the hunter would pat the bunny on the head and say, "I'm here to make sure you're safe." The hunter continued to protect the little bunny until it was old enough to go out and explore on its own. The hunter was so proud and waved goodbye as the little bunny hopped off into the forest.

Lily and Tom were playing near the creek. They liked to throw stones and watch the water splash. They also liked to look for frogs and fish. Sometimes they saw a big turtle with a hard shell. One day, they saw something shiny in the water. It looked like a coin. Lily wanted to get it. She reached out her hand and touched the water. It was cold and wet. She felt something slimy on her fingers. She pulled out her hand and saw a snail. "Look, Tom, I found a snail!" she said. "It has a shell like the turtle, but smaller." Tom came closer and looked at the snail. He was curious. He wondered what it tasted like. He opened his mouth and put the snail inside. "Tom, what are you doing?" Lily asked. "You can't eat the snail. It's not food. It's a friend." Tom spat out the snail. It was yucky and salty. He made a face and said, "Blech! The snail tastes bad. I don't like it." Lily laughed and said, "You are silly, Tom. You don't eat snails. You play with them. Here, let me show you. You can make them slide on a leaf or a rock. They are fun." Tom wiped his mouth and said, "OK, Lily. Let's play with the snails. But next time, I won't taste them. I will only look at them."

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a happy frog. This frog had a big shiny rock. He loved his rock very much. Every day, he would sit on his rock and let the sun shine on his green skin. One day, while the frog was sitting on his rock, he saw a little girl. The girl had hurt her knee. She was crying. The frog wanted to help her. He thought his shiny rock could make her feel better. The frog hopped over to the girl and showed her his shiny rock. The girl stopped crying and smiled at the frog. She touched the shiny rock and her hurt knee felt better. The girl and the frog became best friends. The unexpected thing was that the shiny rock could heal. The moral of the story is that sometimes, even the smallest things can make a big difference.

Johnny was very sleepy. He yawned and rubbed his eyes. He looked around and saw a big circle on the ground. He wondered what it was for. Johnny's dad saw him looking at the circle. "That's for game," said Dad. Johnny's eyes lit up. He ran over to the circle and jumped inside. Dad smiled and said, "Let's play a game. I will stand here, and you have to run around the circle as many times as you can". Johnny tried his best, running around the circle three times. Dad laughed. "That was so stupid," he said. Johnny smiled. He liked playing games with Dad. He tried again. This time, he ran around the circle five times before he got tired. He yawned and Dad smiled. "That was much better than before," he said. Johnny was happy. He ran back to Dad and gave him a big hug.
