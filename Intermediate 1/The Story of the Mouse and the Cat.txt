Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Cat

There was a big cat and a little mouse. They were very different. The big cat liked to lie in the sun all day. The little mouse liked to run and play. One day, the big cat said to the little mouse, "I have a memory of a big cheese. Do you want to find it with me?" The little mouse said, "Yes! I love cheese." They looked and looked for the big cheese. They found it in a big house. But, the cheese was not real. It was a toy cheese. The big cat and the little mouse laughed and laughed. They played with the toy cheese together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play ball on the pitch with his friends. One day, while they were playing, a big wind came and took the ball far away. Spot and his friends were sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a nice bird flew by and saw Spot and his friends looking sad. The bird wanted to comfort them, so it flew high in the sky to look for the ball. The bird found the ball in a tree and brought it back. Spot and his friends were happy and thanked the bird. But, there was a twist! The ball didn't fit in Spot's mouth anymore. It was too big! The bird had brought back a different ball. Spot and his friends laughed and played with the new big ball. They had even more fun than before. They were happy that something unexpected happened, and they made a new friend.

Lily liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Anna. Anna had long hair and a pretty dress. Lily took Anna everywhere with her. She liked to touch Anna's hair and dress. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom and Anna. She saw some other kids playing on the slide and the swings. She wanted to join them, but she was shy. She held Anna close to her chest and watched them. Then, a big boy came over to her. He had a mean look on his face. He grabbed Anna from Lily's arms and ran away. Lily cried and chased after him. She shouted, "Give me back my doll!" The big boy laughed and threw Anna into a puddle. Anna landed with a splash. She was wet and dirty. Lily reached the puddle and picked up Anna. She hugged her and sobbed. She felt sad and angry. The other kids saw what happened and pointed at Lily. They laughed and called her names. They said, "Look at the baby with the wet doll! She is so silly and stupid!" Lily felt embarrassed and ashamed. She wished she could disappear. She ran back to her mom and buried her face in her coat. She hated the park and the big boy and the other kids. She wished she could go home and play with Anna alone.

Bob was very envious. Every day, he watched his friend Steve play baseball. Steve even had a special pitch. Bob asked Steve one day, "Can I play with you, too?". Steve smiled and said, "Yes, of course, Bob! I'll show you how to throw the pitch!" So Bob and Steve went to the baseball field and Steve showed Bob the pitch. He taught him how to make it curve and spin. Bob practiced and practiced. The next day, Bob was so excited. He showed off his new pitch to Steve! Steve was impressed and clapped for Bob. Bob felt very proud and couldn't be more thankful to Steve for showing him. They continued to practice different pitches together and Bob finally stopped feeling envious.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant named Ella. Ella had a long trunk. She liked to help her friends with her trunk. One day, a little bird named Billy was stuck in a tree. Billy was sad and cried, "Help! Help!" Ella heard Billy and wanted to help him. She used her long trunk to reach up and grab Billy. She was successful and saved him from the tree. Billy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Ella! You are a good friend." Ella was proud that she could help her friend. They played together all day, and everyone was happy.
