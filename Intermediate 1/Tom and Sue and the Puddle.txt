Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Puddle

Tom and Sue were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big puddle on the road. It was shiny and wet. They wanted to jump in it and splash. "Let's go to the puddle!" Tom said. "OK!" Sue said. They ran to the puddle and jumped in. They laughed and kicked the water. They did not see the car coming. The car was fast and loud. The driver was lazy and did not look. He hit Tom and Sue with his car. They flew in the air and fell on the ground. They did not laugh or kick anymore. They were hurt and sad. The driver did not stop. He drove away. He did not care. He was bad. A dog came to the puddle. He saw Tom and Sue. He smelled them and licked them. He tasted their blood. He was hungry and happy. He bit them and ate them. He did not share. He was greedy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a wobbly tooth. She was very patient and waited for her tooth to come out. One day, Sue saw a sign. It said, "Win a big prize if you have a special tooth." Sue wanted to win the prize. She went to the big man with her tooth. The big man looked at her tooth and said, "This tooth is not special. You can't win the prize." Sue was very sad. She went home and told her mom about the big man and the prize. That night, Sue put her tooth under her pillow. When she woke up, something unexpected happened. There was a note under her pillow. It said, "I am the tooth fairy. Your tooth is very special. You win a prize." Sue was very happy. But then, she saw the prize. It was a big bag of rocks. Sue was sad again. The prize was not what she wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to help his dad build a treehouse. His dad gave him a small hammer to use. Tim felt happy and big. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim felt bitter cold, but he still wanted to work on the treehouse. His dad said, "It's too cold, Tim." But Tim wanted to try. His dad decided to encourage him and said, "Okay, let's try for a little bit." They went outside, and Tim tried to use his hammer. His hands were so cold, and he dropped the hammer. Tim felt sad. His dad said, "It's okay, Tim. We can try again when it's warmer." They went inside to get warm. Tim knew they would finish the treehouse soon.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Kola and Penelope. They would go on all sorts of adventures together. One day, Kola told Penelope about a special place. “Let’s go,” said Kola. So, off they went. When they arrived, Kola and Penelope were standing in front of a big balancing rock. Kola and Penelope had to balance on top of the rock. Kola said, “This is so fun!” Penelope thought for a second, “I have an idea. Let’s put our salads on the rock and balance them.” Kola thought it was a thoughtful idea. So, they put the salads on the rock and tried to balance them. “We did it!” said Kola as they were both successful at balancing their salads. Kola and Penelope laughed as they enjoyed their salads while balancing on the rock. They knew they had made a special memory together.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a nosy cat. The cat liked to look at everything. One day, the cat saw a cooler. It was in the kitchen. The cat thought, "What is inside the cooler?" The cat went to the cooler and tried to open it. But it was hard. The cat tried and tried, but it could not open the cooler. The cat was sad. It started to weep. The cat did not know what to do. Then, a little mouse came. The mouse saw the cat weep. The mouse said, "Why are you sad, cat?" The cat said, "I want to open the cooler, but I can't." The mouse said, "I can help you!" The mouse went to the cooler and opened it. Inside the cooler, there was a big cake! The cat and the mouse became friends. They shared the cake and had a fun party. The cat was not sad anymore.
