Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play outside. One day, Tim saw a big, red apple up in a tree. He wanted to get the apple, but it was too high for him to reach. Tim asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam was good at climbing trees. "Can you help me get the apple?" Tim asked. Sam said, "Yes, I will help you!" So, Sam climbed the tree and got the apple for Tim. The two friends were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A messy bird flew by and took the apple from Tim's hand. Tim and Sam tried to get the apple back, but the bird was too fast. They were sad that they lost the apple. In the end, they did not get to eat the big, red apple.

Once upon a time, there was a little kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy was an original kitten because she had a blue tail. One day, Fluffy was playing outside when she saw a big box. Fluffy went up to the box and said, "Hello box! What are you doing here?" The box did not say anything. Fluffy thought the box was shy. She wanted to be friends with the box. Fluffy decided to mail the box to her friend, Tommy. She put a note inside the box that said, "Hi Tommy, this is Fluffy's new friend, Box. Let's play together!" Fluffy and Tommy had a fun day playing with the box, and they all became best friends.

One day, a big carpet was in a room. A small boy named Tim and a small girl named Sue were playing on it. They were very happy. Tim said, "Let's play with my toy car!" Sue said, "Yes, I want to gain speed with it!" They played and played, but then the toy car went under the big carpet. Tim and Sue were anxious. They did not know what to do. Then, a little dog came into the room. The dog went under the big carpet and got the toy car. Tim and Sue were so happy! They said, "Thank you, little dog!" The little dog wagged its tail and played with them. The day was fun again.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room where a number of toys lived. They liked to stay together and play all day. One day, a new toy came to the room. His name was Tim. Tim asked the other toys, "Can I stay and play with you?" The other toys said, "Yes, you can stay with us. But you must help us keep the room tidy." So, Tim and the other toys played together. They had a lot of fun. They made sure to keep the room tidy after they played. They put all the toys back in their place. At the end of the day, the toys were happy that they had a new friend. They were glad that their room was still tidy. They all agreed to play together again the next day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a girl. Her name was Lizzie. Lizzie loved to jump. She jumped over rocks, puddles and logs. One day, Lizzie found a secret power that gave her the ability to jump extra high. She felt very amazed by her smooth new ability. Lizzie said to her friend, "Let's jump together!" Her friend said, "Yes!" So the two of them jumped higher and higher, until they reached the clouds! Lizzie and her friend played all day, jumping up and down and having lots of fun. The two friends laughed and grinned with delight, enjoying the magical power of their jumps.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to admire her clothes. She had many pretty dresses and shirts. One day, Lily wore her favorite dress. It was pink with flowers. She felt so happy. She went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Lily and her friends played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Lily's clothes were dirty, but she still felt happy. She knew her mom would help her clean her clothes, and she could admire them again.
