Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a nice dog named Bobo. He loved to play with his red ball. One day, Bobo saw a big brick. He wanted to play with it too. Bobo tried to pick up the brick, but it was too heavy. He paused and thought, "What should I do?" Then, he had a great idea. He called his friend, the strong horse named Max. Max came and saw the brick. He said, "Let's play together!" Max picked up the brick with his strong legs. Bobo and Max played with the brick and the red ball all day. At the end of the day, Bobo and Max were very happy. They learned that playing together is more fun than playing alone. They promised to always help each other and be best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful rabbit named Max. Max loved to skip and play with his friends in the sunny meadow. One day, Max found a big, red ball in the grass. He thought it was a fun new toy to play with. Max showed the ball to his friends, and they all played with it. They skipped and hopped with the ball all morning. They were having so much fun that they forgot to eat their lunch! Max's mom called them to come and eat, but they didn't want to stop playing. Later that day, Max and his friends started to feel very hungry. They remembered that they didn't eat their lunch. Max's mom had saved their lunch for them, and they all ate together. After eating, they felt powerful again and could play with the big, red ball all day long. And from that day on, Max and his friends always made sure to eat their lunch so they could play and skip happily.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a poor little dog. He was hungry and tired. He walked around the town looking for food. One day, the dog met a stranger. The stranger was a kind girl. She saw the poor dog and wanted to help. The girl had a big sandwich. She decided to share it with the dog. The dog and the girl sat down to eat. They ate and ate until they finished the sandwich. The poor dog was happy and full. The stranger and the dog became good friends, and they played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a magic pipe that could glow. It was incredible! He loved to play with it all day long. When it was dark, the pipe would glow and light up his room. Tim was very happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his glowing pipe. He played with it and showed it to his friends. They were amazed at the incredible glowing pipe. They played with it too and had lots of fun. But then, a mean, big kid saw the glowing pipe. He wanted it for himself. He took the pipe from Tim and ran away. Tim and his friends tried to get it back, but they could not catch the big kid. Tim was very sad. He never saw his incredible glowing pipe again.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He lived near a big lake. He loved to catch fish. One day, he found a fragile box. The box was very pretty. The fisherman wanted to know what was inside. He started to search for a way to open the box. But the box was very hard to open. He tried and tried, but he could not open it. The fisherman became very sad. He did not know what to do. A wise old man saw the fisherman. He told him, "Do not worry. The box is not important. The important thing is to be happy with what you have." The fisherman learned that it is better to be happy with what you have than to always search for more. And he lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a calm forest, there lived a bunny named Ben. Ben was very good at finding things. One day, he found a big log. He thought it would be fun to play on the log with his friends. Ben went to his friend, the squirrel named Sam. "Sam, I found a big log. Let's play on it!" said Ben. Sam was happy and followed Ben to the log. They played on the log, jumping and running around it. Later, Ben and Sam met their friend, the bird named Bella. "Bella, come play with us on the big log!" they said. Bella was excited and flew to the log. Ben, Sam, and Bella played together on the log all day. They had so much fun, they didn't want the day to end.
