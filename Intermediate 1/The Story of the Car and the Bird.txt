Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Car and the Bird

One day, a small car was driving down the road. The car was a special vehicle that could talk. The car was feeling confused because it did not know where to go. It saw a big tree and stopped. A wise bird lived in the tree. The car whispered to the bird, "I am lost. Can you help me find my way?" The bird listened and then whispered back, "You must look for the red house. It is near the big hill." The car thanked the bird and started driving again. It found the red house and the big hill. The car was happy and not confused anymore. The bird had helped the car find its way. The moral of the story is to ask for help when you are confused or lost. Friends can help you find your way.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to travel with her mom. She had a doll named Lily. Sue loved Lily very much. Sue and her mom were very busy packing their bags. They were excited for their trip. Before they left, Sue's mom said, "Sue, don't forget Lily. We don't want her to be lonely at home." Sue ran to her room and put Lily in her bag. She was happy that Lily could come with them. On their trip, Sue and Lily had lots of fun. They played together and saw many new things. One day, Sue and her mom went to a big park. Sue played with Lily near a tree. When it was time to go, Sue's mom called, "Sue, it's time to leave. Don't forget Lily!" Sue looked around and saw Lily under the tree. She picked her up and they all went home, happy they had a fun trip together.

One day, a little black cat named Max was walking down the street. He saw many cars and buses. There was a lot of traffic. Max wanted to cross the street, but he was scared. Max's friend, a big dog named Sam, came to help. Sam said, "Don't worry, Max. I will help you cross the street." Max was happy to have Sam's help. Sam looked both ways and said, "Okay, Max, we can go now." But Max was still scared. Max said, "No, Sam, I insist we wait for the traffic to stop." Sam listened to Max and they waited. When the traffic stopped, they both crossed the street safely. Max was very happy and thanked Sam for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to lie a lot. One day, Tim lied to his mom about eating his vegetables. He said, "Mom, I ate all my vegetables!" But his mom knew he was not telling the truth. She felt frustrated. Tim's mom said, "Tim, you should not lie. It is not nice." Tim felt sad that he made his mom frustrated. That night, Tim made a wish. He wished to never lie again. The next day, Tim told the truth. He said, "Mom, I did not eat my vegetables." His mom was happy that Tim told the truth. Tim felt good too. From that day on, Tim never lied again. The moral of the story is: Always tell the truth, and you will feel good and make others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big sea. The sea was full of fish that liked to play and tease each other. They would swim fast and chase their friends, laughing all day long. One day, a small fish named Tim wanted to be useful. He saw a big fish stuck in a net. Tim swam to the big fish and said, "I will help you!" He used his little teeth to bite the net and set the big fish free. The big fish was very happy and thanked Tim. From that day on, all the fish in the sea knew that even a small fish can be useful. They stopped teasing Tim and became his friends. They all played and swam together, having fun in the big sea.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little mouse named Tim. Tim had a long tail. One day, Tim saw a big hole. He wanted to peek inside the hole, but he knew it could be dangerous. Tim was very scared, but he wanted to see what was inside. He took a small step and peeked into the hole. Inside, he saw a big snake! The snake saw Tim's tail and tried to catch it. Tim was very fast and ran away. Tim learned that it was not good to peek into dangerous places. He told his friends about the snake and they all stayed away from the hole. They played together in the safe part of the forest. The moral of the story is to be careful and not to go into dangerous places.
