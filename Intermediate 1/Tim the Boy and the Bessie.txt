Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bessie

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an adorable pet cow named Bessie. Bessie was very special because she could disappear. Tim loved to play with Bessie in the big green field. One sunny day, Tim and Bessie were playing hide and seek. Bessie was very good at this game because she could disappear. Tim would close his eyes and count to ten, and Bessie would hide. When Tim opened his eyes, he would look for Bessie, but she would be gone. Finally, Tim found Bessie behind a big tree. They laughed and played some more. Tim's mom called them to come home. She had made a big yummy dinner with beef, but Tim and Bessie did not want to eat it. They loved each other too much. So, they all had a big salad instead. Tim, Bessie, and Tim's mom were very happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy band. He had a drum, a guitar, and a trumpet. Tom would try to play all the instruments at the same time, but it was hard. One day, a big dog named Max came to Tom's house. Max saw the toy band and wanted to play too. But Tom did not want to share his toys. They both wanted to play with the band, and this made them sad. Tom thought about it and had an idea. They could try to play the band together! Tom played the drum, and Max played the guitar. They took turns playing the trumpet. They had so much fun and became good friends.

One day, a cat and a dog were in a room. The cat saw an ugly table. The cat said, "Dog, let's go to the ugly table!" The dog said, "Okay, cat!" They went to the ugly table. The cat jumped on the table. The dog tried to jump but could not. The cat said, "I see something on the table. I will push it off for you, dog!" The cat pushed a big box off the table. The dog was happy. They opened the box and found a lot of toys. The cat and the dog played with the toys all day. The ugly table made them very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her mom's jewelry. One day, her mom said, "Lily, be careful with my jewelry. It is very special to me." One sunny day, Lily went for a walk in the wild woods near her house. She wore her mom's jewelry to feel pretty. While walking, she saw a big, wild cat. The cat looked at Lily and said, "Wow! You have pretty jewelry! Can I see it?" Lily was scared, but she let the cat see the jewelry. The wild cat looked at the jewelry and started to tremble. "Oh no!" said the cat. "This jewelry is magic! I must give it back to you!" The cat gave the jewelry back to Lily and ran away. Lily was surprised but happy. She went back home and told her mom about the magic jewelry. From that day on, Lily and her mom were always careful with the special jewelry.

Once upon a time, there was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz lived in a big hive with his bee friends. The hive was in a pretty orange tree. One day, Buzz saw a group of bees flying and playing. Buzz wanted to join them. So, he flew and flew until he reached his friends. Buzz and his friends played and laughed all day long. They flew around the orange tree and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they went back to their hive, happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. A cat and a dog lived near the building. The cat liked to step on the dog's tail, and the dog did not like it. One day, the cat said to the dog, "I can step all the way to the top of the building." The dog was jealous. He wanted to step to the top too, but he was scared. The next day, the cat and dog went to the building. The cat stepped all the way to the top. The dog took a deep breath and started to step up too. When the dog reached the top, he was so happy. The cat was proud of the dog, and they both looked at the view together. From that day on, the dog was not jealous of the cat anymore, and they became best friends.
