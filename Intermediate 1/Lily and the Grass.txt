Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Grass

Lily and her mommy were in the garden. Lily saw a worm wriggling in the mud. She shouted: "Mommy, look! A worm!" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, Lily. That's a worm." Lily bent down and carefully gathered the worm into her hands. She showed it to her mommy and asked, "What should we do with the worm?" Her mommy thought and replied, "Let's put it in the grass so it can be safe." So they did. Lily put the worm in the grass and watched it wriggle away. Suddenly, she saw a lot of small worms in the grass. "Look mommy!" she exclaimed, "Lots of worms!" Her mommy nodded and said, "Yes, but some of the worms are dead. See, they're not wriggling." Lily felt sad thinking about the dead worms. She said, "Poor worms!" Her mommy smiled and said, "Yes, let's give them a good place to rest." So Lily and her mommy gathered the dead worms and put them in a safe place. Then Lily said, "Bye bye, worms!" and went back indoors with her mommy.

Tom and Lily like to play soccer. They have a big ball and two goals. They are on the same team. They want to score many goals. But today, there is no one else to play with. The park is empty. No other players are there. Tom and Lily are sad. "Where are all the players?" Tom asks. "Why is the park empty?" "I don't know," Lily says. "Maybe they are at home. Maybe they are sick. Maybe they are busy." Tom thinks for a moment. He has an idea. "Let's pretend we have other players," he says. "We can make names for them. We can talk to them. We can pass the ball to them." Lily thinks that is a good idea. She smiles. "OK, let's do that," she says. "You can be Tom and Ben. I can be Lily and Anna. Ben and Anna are on the other team. They are not very good." Tom and Lily start to play. They run and kick and shout. They make up stories about their pretend players. They have fun. The park is not empty anymore. It is full of soccer players. Tom and Lily are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her mom's jewelry. One day, she saw a big, shiny necklace. It was very pretty and made a lot of noise when she shook it. Amy wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Amy went to Tom's house and said, "Look, Tom! I have a noisy necklace. Can you see how shiny it is?" Tom looked at the necklace and his eyes grew big. He said, "Wow, Amy! That is very pretty. Can I try it on?" Amy said, "Yes, you can try it on, but be careful. It is my mom's." Tom put on the necklace and they both laughed at how noisy it was. They played with the necklace, shaking it and listening to the sounds it made. After a while, it was time for Amy to go home. She took the necklace back and said, "Thank you for playing with me, Tom. I had a lot of fun." Amy went home and put the necklace back where she found it. She was happy she got to share her mom's pretty, noisy jewelry with her friend. And from that day, Amy and Tom had many more fun adventures together.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy cars. He had many cars, but he liked to number them. Car number one was red, car number two was blue, and car number three was yellow. Tim loved to race his cars and see which one would go the fastest. While playing, Tim saw a big truck outside his house. The truck had a word on it: "emergency." Tim knew that "emergency" meant that someone needed help. He felt troubled because he wanted to help too. So, he took his toy cars and ran outside to see what was happening. The emergency truck was there to help a cat stuck in a tree. Tim watched as the people from the truck helped the cat get down. He was happy that the cat was safe. Tim learned that when there is an emergency, there are people who can help. From that day on, he wanted to be a helper too.
