Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty was an independent cat who liked to play and explore. One day, Kitty found a big tree and wanted to climb it. Kitty started to climb the tree. A bird sitting on a branch saw Kitty and said, "Kitty, don't climb too high!" But Kitty didn't listen and kept climbing. The bird raised her voice and said, "Kitty, be careful!" Suddenly, Kitty slipped and fell from the tree. Kitty was hurt and sad. The bird came down to help, but it was too late. Kitty learned that she should have listened to the bird's warning.

Once upon a time, there was a popular hen. She was very good at laying eggs. All the other animals liked her a lot. They liked her because she would give them eggs to eat. The eggs were very yummy. One day, a big, mean fox came to the farm. He saw the popular hen and her eggs. The fox wanted to eat the eggs too. He thought the eggs would be very yummy. The fox made a plan to take the eggs from the popular hen. The fox took the eggs when the popular hen was not looking. The other animals were very sad. They did not get any more eggs to eat. The popular hen was sad too. She could not give eggs to her friends anymore. The fox was happy, but everyone else was sad.

One day, a purple bird named Tim wanted to play baseball. He went to the park and found a baseball bat. Tim saw his friend, Sam the cat, sitting under a big tree. Tim asked, "Sam, will you play baseball with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I love baseball!" They started to play. Tim would hit the ball and Sam would dive to catch it. They were having so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The ball went very high up in the sky. When it came down, it landed on a big, red button. The button made a loud noise and opened a secret door in the ground! Tim and Sam looked at each other, surprised. They went to the door and saw a big, fun room with lots of toys inside. They played with the toys all day and forgot all about baseball.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue wanted to race. They went to a big park on a gloomy day. The park had many trees and a long path to run on. Tim and Sue were excited to see who was faster. "Are you ready to race?" asked Tim. "Yes, I am!" said Sue with a big smile. They started to run as fast as they could. They raced for one minute and were very tired. Just then, a small dog ran up to them. The dog wanted to race too! Tim and Sue were surprised. They laughed and said, "Okay, let's race with the dog!" They all raced together, and the dog was very fast. In the end, the dog won the race. Tim and Sue were happy to make a new friend, and the gloomy day became a fun day.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his yard. He saw a weird-looking tap near the big tree. Tim went to the tap and tried to turn it on. But the tap was very old and did not work. As Tim played near the tap, he saw a big, red ball stuck in the tree. He wanted to get the ball down. So, he tried to climb the tree. But as he climbed, he began to fall. "Oh no!" said Tim, as he fell to the ground. Just then, Tim's mom came outside. She saw Tim on the ground and asked, "Are you okay, Tim?" Tim said, "I fell, but I am okay. I just wanted to get the red ball from the tree." His mom smiled and said, "Let's be careful next time. I will help you get the ball down." They got the ball and played together in the yard.

One day, a large dog with soft fur went for a walk. The dog saw a small cat sitting under a tree. The dog went to the cat and said, "Hi, I am a large dog with soft fur. Do you want to play?" The cat did not say anything. It just stared at the dog. The dog did not know why the cat was staring. The dog felt sad and walked away. Then, something unexpected happened. As the dog walked away, the cat jumped up and said, "Wait! I want to play too!" The dog was happy and surprised. They played together all day and became best friends.
