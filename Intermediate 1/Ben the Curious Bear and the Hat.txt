Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Curious Bear and the Hat

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a curious little bear named Ben. Ben had soft brown fur that kept him warm. He loved to wear a red hat on his head. Every day, he would go out to play and explore the forest. One sunny day, while walking in the woods, Ben met a small rabbit named Rosie. Rosie was very shy but also curious. She saw Ben's red hat and asked, "Why do you wear a hat on your head?" Ben smiled and said, "I like to wear it because it is fun and it makes me feel special." Ben and Rosie became good friends. They played together every day in the forest. They ran, they laughed, and they had lots of fun. Ben always wore his red hat, and Rosie started to wear a blue ribbon around her neck. They were the best of friends, and their curious adventures never ended.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a nosy squirrel. He loved to find nuts and play with his friends. One day, he found a very big and tasty nut. He was so happy! The nosy squirrel wanted to show his friends the big nut. But, he didn't want to leave it alone. So, he took the nut with him. He went to his friend's house and knocked on the door. His friend opened the door and saw the big nut. They both laughed and played with the nut. Then, they went outside to find more nuts. They had so much fun and never left each other. And the nosy squirrel was very happy with his big nut and his best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Tom. Tom liked to help his friends. One day, he found a broken toy. He wanted to restore it and make it like new again. Tom went to his friend, a wise owl named Ollie. Ollie wore a belt around his waist. Tom asked Ollie for help to fix the toy. Ollie said, "Let's try together!" They worked hard to make the toy look new. When they finished, they found out the toy could talk! The toy said, "Thank you for fixing me!" Tom and Ollie were very surprised. They did not know the toy could speak. They were happy they helped, and they all became good friends.

One day, a little frog named Tim wanted to take a shower. He loved the sweet smell of the flowers in the shower. He jumped into the shower and started to sing. "Jump, jump, jump, I love to jump in the shower!" sang Tim. His friend, a small bird named Sue, heard him singing. Sue flew to the shower and asked, "Can I join you, Tim?" "Of course, Sue!" said Tim. They both jumped and played in the shower together. They had so much fun splashing water and smelling the sweet flowers. After their shower, they jumped out and said goodbye. Tim and Sue were very happy. They knew they would jump in the shower again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a light blue car named Bobby. Bobby loved to play in the park with his friends. One day, it rained and the park became wet. Bobby and his friends were sad because they could not play outside. Bobby had an idea. He said to his friends, "Let's play a game called reverse. We will take turns going backward and see who can go the farthest without getting stuck in the mud!" His friends were excited and agreed to play the game. First, Bobby went in reverse and moved backward. He did not get stuck in the mud. Then, it was his friend's turn. His friend also went in reverse and moved backward. They laughed and had fun playing the game. The wet park did not stop them from having a good time. In the end, they all went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bossy cat named Tom. He liked to play with his toy ball. One day, he saw a small hole in the wall. Tom thought his toy ball would fit in the hole. He pushed the ball into the hole, but it got stuck! Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. He sat and thought for a turn. Then, he had an idea. He went to his friend, a small mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very smart and always helped Tom when he needed it. Tom asked Jerry for help. Jerry said, "I have an idea. I will push the ball from the other side of the hole." Jerry went to the other side and pushed the ball. The ball came out, and Tom was very happy. Tom learned that sometimes, even a bossy cat needs help from a friend.
