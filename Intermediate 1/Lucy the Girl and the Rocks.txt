Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Rocks

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to collect pretty rocks. She would look for them everywhere she went. She had a big box where she kept all her rocks. One day, Lucy found out that there was a new law in her town. The law said that collecting rocks was not allowed. Lucy was very sad. She did not want to break the law, but she loved her rocks so much. Lucy had an idea. She went to the people who made the law and asked if they could change it. They saw how much Lucy loved her rocks and decided to change the law. Lucy was very happy. From that day on, she could collect rocks again and share her love for them with everyone in the quiet little town.

Billy had an idea - he wanted to make a very thin bowl. He asked his mom what he should do. Mom answered, "First, get some thin paper and a bowl of water." Billy got the paper and bowl and his mom showed him how to dip the paper into the bowl. He pulled it out very carefully and it was super thin! His mom showed him how to fold it and put it in the oven. When the thin bowl was ready, Billy was excited. He couldn't wait to try it - he wanted to put something special inside. He asked his mom, "What should I put in the bowl?" Mom answered, "Something yummy, like ice cream!" Billy was so happy. He filled the thin bowl with his favorite ice cream and enjoyed every bite. He was glad he asked his mom for help - this was the best answer ever!

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big jet in the sky. It was white and shiny. It made a loud noise. "Wow, look at the jet!" Lily said. "It is so fast and big!" "I want to fly in a jet!" Ben said. "I want to see the clouds and the stars!" They pretended to be jets. They ran around the park, making zooming sounds. They stretched their arms out like wings. They had fun. But then, they saw a bird. It was a swan. It was white and fluffy. It flew over the pond. It was graceful and quiet. "Look at the swan!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and smooth!" "I want to be a swan!" Ben said. "I want to swim in the pond and glide in the air!" They tried to be swans. They flapped their arms like wings. They bent their necks like swans. They did not have fun. They could not swim in the pond. It was too deep and cold. They could not glide in the air. They were too heavy and clumsy. They fell in the mud. They got wet and dirty. They were sad and angry. They blamed each other. They said mean words. "You are a bad jet!" Lily said. "You are slow and noisy!" "You are a bad swan!" Ben said. "You are ugly and stiff!" They stopped playing. They turned away from each other. They were not friends anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved ice cream. One day, Sue went to the store with her mom to buy her favorite ice cream. She was very glad. At the store, they found the ice cream Sue wanted. But when they opened the freezer, they saw that there was no more ice cream left! Sue was very sad. Her mom tried to comfort her, but Sue still wanted her ice cream. Just then, a nice man who worked at the store came over. He had a big smile and said, "I have a surprise for you!" He went to the back of the store and came back with a big bucket of Sue's favorite ice cream. Sue was so happy and hugged her mom. They went home and enjoyed the ice cream together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small squirrel named Sam. Sam was a very honest squirrel. One day, Sam found a big acorn under a tree. The acorn was very special because it was the biggest acorn in the forest. Sam wanted to share the acorn with his friends. But his friends did not like acorns. They said, "We hate acorns!" Sam was sad, but he still wanted to share. So, he went to find other animals in the forest who liked acorns. Sam met a kind bird named Betty. Betty loved acorns! Sam gave the big acorn to Betty, and they became good friends. They played and ate acorns together. Sam was happy because he found a friend who liked acorns, and he was an honest squirrel who shared.
