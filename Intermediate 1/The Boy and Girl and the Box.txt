Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy and Girl and the Box

One day, a boy and a girl went out to play. They liked to play in the warm sun. They were very happy. They liked to do many fun things together. But one day, when they went out to play, they saw something big and new. It was a big, red box. They did not know what it was. They were very surprised. They went close to the big, red box. They opened it. Inside the box, they found many warm coats and hats. They put them on and went to play in the snow. They had so much fun playing in the snow with their new warm coats and hats. The big, red box was a big, happy surprise for them.

A little girl was playing in the garden, picking the pretty flowers and running around. She loved being outside and she was full of energy. While she was playing, she found a long, ancient leg with many details. She took it up and started to explore. Suddenly she noticed that the leg was moving! Startled, the little girl asked it, "What are you doing here?". The leg replied, "I am just taking a nap". It was tired of living in an ancient world and wanted a break. The girl was amazed and asked the leg, "Can I take a nap with you?" The leg smiled and said, "Yes, of course you can!" So, they both took a nap together in the garden. The little girl had made a new friend.

One day, a little kitten went for a walk. The kitten liked to skip and play. The kitten was very happy. The sun was up and the sky was blue. The kitten saw a big dog. The dog said, "Hi, kitten! Do you want to play with me?" The kitten was scared of the big dog. But then, the dog started to skip too. The kitten thought, "Maybe the big dog is nice." They played and skipped together. They got very dirty. The kitten and the dog were now friends. The kitten was not scared anymore. The big dog was nice and they had fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big sign that said "Race Today!" Tim wanted to run in the race, but he didn't know if he could. He asked his mom, "Can I run too?" His mom said, "Yes, you can run if there are spots available." Tim was happy and went to sign up for the race. But when he got there, a big boy named Max said, "You're too small to run. You can't be in the race." Tim felt sad, but he didn't give up. He told Max, "I can run fast, and I want to be in the race." Max laughed and said, "Fine, you can try, but I will win." The race started, and Tim ran as fast as he could. Max was ahead, but Tim didn't stop. He kept running and running. In the end, Tim won the race! Everyone cheered for him, and Max was surprised. Tim showed everyone that even small kids can run fast and win races.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She liked to play with her friends and her dog, Spot. They had fun all day, running and laughing in the sun. One day, Lily saw a new friend in the park. The friend was a spirit, but Lily did not know that. She asked the spirit to play, and the spirit said yes. They played with a ball and had a good time. Then, something unexpected happened. The spirit said, "I must go now." Lily did not want the spirit to leave, so she shrugged and said, "Okay, bye." But when the spirit left, it turned into a beautiful butterfly. Lily was so happy to see the butterfly and knew that her new friend was always with her.

Mummy took her little girl by the hand and said, "Let's go outside and follow the generous rain." They stepped out of their house and into the rain. The little girl squealed with joy and said, "Look! It's raining! I love the rain!" She danced beneath the generous rain, feeling the wet drops on her face. Mummy laughed as she jumped and skipped with her little girl. They ran around the garden and splashed in the puddles. The little girl couldn't seem to get enough of the rain. Mummy and her little girl followed the rain for hours. Eventually, the rain stopped. But the little girl didn't want it to stop. Mummy smiled and said, "Come on, let's go inside and make some tea. The rain will come again soon". The little girl smiled and said, "Okay!" And with that, they went inside to make tea, both of them feeling very content.
