Category: Intermediate
Title: Maggie and Sam and the Park

Once upon a time there were two friends, Maggie and Sam. They loved playing together and having fun. One day Maggie had a great idea. She said, "Let's have a picnic!" Sam agreed and they went to buy some food. They bought lots of yummy snacks and sandwiches. Then, they went to the park to set up the picnic. Maggie and Sam laid out a blanket and filled it with food. They ate all the food and laughed together. They stayed outside for a long time, enjoying the sunshine and each other's company. At the end, Maggie said to Sam, "This was such a nice picnic, I really valued our time together." Sam smiled and nodded. He knew that they had shared something special.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sally. They liked to play together every day. Tim was a reliable friend, always there for Sally. One day, Tim received a gift from his mom. It was a red vest. He was so happy and excited. He put on the vest and went to show it to Sally. "Look, Sally! I got a new vest!" Tim said. Sally smiled and said, "Wow, Tim! Your vest is very nice!" The next day, they played outside wearing their vests. They played all day long. They were so happy and had so much fun. Tim and Sally were the best of friends, and they knew they could always count on each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy model car that he loved very much. He played with it every day. One day, Tim felt miserable because his model car was broken. He was very sad. Tim went to his friend, Lily. He asked her, "Lily, can you help me fix my model car?" Lily looked at the broken car and said, "I will try, but I need you to accept that it might not work." Tim nodded and let Lily try to fix his car. Lily tried her best to fix the car, but it was still broken. Tim was very sad and cried. He had to accept that his model car would never be the same again. The end.

Ben and Lily like to play hide and seek in the garden. They run and laugh and look for good places to hide. One day, they see a big hedge near the fence. It has many green leaves and branches. They think it is a good place to hide. Ben says, "I will hide in the hedge. You count to ten and then look for me." Lily says, "OK, I will count. One, two, three..." Ben runs to the hedge and pushes his way inside. He feels the leaves and branches cover him. He thinks Lily will not find him. Lily counts to ten and then says, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looks around the garden and tries to find Ben. She does not see him in the swing, or under the table, or behind the tree. She wonders where he is. She hears a loud noise from the hedge. It sounds like a growl. Lily is scared. She thinks there is a big animal in the hedge. She runs to the house and calls for Mom. Mom comes out and asks, "What is wrong, Lily?" Lily says, "There is a big animal in the hedge. It is loud and scary. Ben is in the hedge. He is hiding there. Please help him, Mom!" Mom and Lily go to the hedge. They see Ben's shoes sticking out from the leaves. They hear the loud noise again. It is not a growl. It is a sneeze. Ben is allergic to the hedge. He is sneezing and itching and crying. Mom pulls him out of the hedge. He is covered in red spots and tears. Mom says, "Oh, Ben, you are allergic to the hedge. We have to go to the doctor. Come on, let's go." She takes Ben and Lily to the car. Ben is sad and sorry. He says, "I did not know I was allergic to the hedge. I just wanted to hide. I am sorry, Lily." Lily says, "It's OK, Ben. I am sorry you are sick. I hope you feel better soon." They drive to the doctor. The end.
