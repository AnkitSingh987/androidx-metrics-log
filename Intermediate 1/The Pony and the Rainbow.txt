Category: Intermediate
Title: The Pony and the Rainbow

Once upon a time, there was a little pony. The pony lived in a gloomy forest. The pony was sad because the forest was dark and scary. One day, the pony saw a big rainbow in the sky. The pony wanted to shoot up to the rainbow to see where it went. The pony ran fast and jumped high, but it could not reach the rainbow. Then, a kind bird saw the pony trying to jump. The bird said, "I can help you!" The bird took the pony up to the rainbow. The pony was happy and not sad anymore. The gloomy forest was not scary with the bird and the rainbow. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a loyal dog named Max was playing outside with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They liked to run, jump, and play in the sun. Max was always by Tim's side, and they had lots of fun together. While they were playing, Tim found a toy gun on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Max, I found a gun! Let's play cops and robbers!" Max wagged his tail, ready to play with Tim. As they played, Max's mom called out, "Tim, please mind where you point the gun. It is just a toy, but we need to be safe." Tim nodded and said, "Okay, Mom, I will be careful." Max and Tim played all day, always minding the rules, and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She wanted to marry a boy named Sam. They lived in a distant land, far away from their friends. One day, they went for a row on a lake. The lake was big and the sky was blue. They were happy to row together. Lily and Sam loved the water and the sun. But then, a big storm came. The wind was strong and the waves were big. They tried to row back, but it was too hard. In the end, they could not go back home. And so, Lily and Sam were lost in the big lake forever.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little girl named Sue. Sue lived on a farm with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. They had many animals on the farm like cows, pigs, and chickens. One day, Sue saw that the chickens were making a lot of noise. She went to see what was wrong. She found out that the chickens had lost their eggs. Sue wanted to solve the problem and make the chickens happy again. Sue and Max looked all over the farm for the eggs. They looked in the barn, in the grass, and under the trees. Finally, they found the eggs near the pond. The chickens were so happy to have their eggs back. Sue felt good that she solved the problem and helped her friends on the farm.

John was so naughty. He never listened to mummy. Today, mummy was busy cleaning with a mop. John wanted to ride the mop so he got on the mop. He rode it around the room. Mummy shouted at him to get off the mop. John kept riding and laughing. Mummy got mad and took the mop away from John. John tried to get the mop back but mummy wouldn't let him have it. She said he was too naughty to ride the mop. John asked mummy to forgive him but she said no. She said he had to learn not to be naughty. John was sad and he felt bad. Even though he was sad, he still knew he was naughty.

Once upon a time, a naughty shrimp lived in the sea. The shrimp liked to play games with his friends. His favorite game was hide and seek. One day, the shrimp said to his friends, "Let's play hide and seek!" His friends were happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" So, they started to play the game. The shrimp was very good at hiding. He would hide in the sand, behind rocks, and even in the seaweed. His friends looked and looked but could not find the naughty shrimp. They called out, "Shrimp, where are you?" The shrimp giggled and stayed hidden. Finally, his friends found him and they all laughed. They played hide and seek all day long, and the naughty shrimp always had the best hiding spots.
