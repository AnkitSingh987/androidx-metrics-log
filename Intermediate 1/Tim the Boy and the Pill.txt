Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Pill

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a sack. He liked to put things in his sack. One day, he found a pill. He put the pill in his sack too. Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue asked, "What is in your sack?" Tim said, "I have a pill in my sack." Sue was surprised. She said, "Can I see it?" Tim showed her the pill. Suddenly, the pill started to grow! It became very big. Tim and Sue were shocked. They did not know what to do. Then, the big pill turned into a fun toy. Tim and Sue played with the toy all day. They had a great time.

Once upon a time there were two sisters, Ellie and Lucy. One day, they were both walking in the park when they saw something special. It was a big jar filled with candy! "Wow!" said Lucy. "Let's open it!" Ellie shook her head. "I don't know," she said. "It looks very helpless and I don't want to do something wrong." Lucy thought for a minute. Then she said, "Let's guess. Maybe if we guess the right answer, the jar will open." Ellie nodded and smiled. They both tried and tried. Finally, after a few minutes, there was a click and the jar opened! Inside the jar was lots of delicious candy! Lucy and Ellie each took some and enjoyed it. The End!

Lila was a gifted girl who liked to make things with her hands. She made cards, dolls, hats, and more. She liked to mail her things to her friends and family, who lived far away. She would put her things in a big box, write a name on it, and give it to the mailman. One day, Lila made a special shoe for her grandma. She used red yarn, buttons, and glitter. She made it very soft and shiny. She wanted to mail it to her grandma, who had a birthday soon. She put the shoe in a small box, wrote "Grandma" on it, and waited for the mailman. But the mailman did not come that day. Lila was sad. She asked her mom, "Where is the mailman? I want to mail my shoe to grandma." Her mom said, "The mailman is sick today. He will come tomorrow. Don't worry, your shoe will get to grandma on time." Lila nodded, but she was still sad. She wanted to make her grandma happy today. She hugged her shoe and said, "I love you, shoe. I hope you make grandma happy too." The next day, the mailman came. He saw Lila's box and smiled. He said, "What do you have in here, Lila? It looks very pretty." Lila said, "It's a shoe for my grandma. It's her birthday. I made it for her. Do you like it?" The mailman said, "I love it. It's very creative and beautiful. Your grandma will love it too. You are a gifted girl, Lila." Lila smiled and said, "Thank you, mailman. Can you please mail it to grandma?" The mailman said, "Of course. I will mail it right away. Have a nice day, Lila." Lila said, "You too, mailman. Bye bye." She waved to the mailman as he took her box and drove away. She felt happy. She knew her shoe would make her grandma happy too. She went inside and made another shoe for herself. She wanted to match with her grandma. She used blue yarn, stars, and feathers. She made it very soft and shiny too. She put it on her foot and said, "I love you, shoe. You are my friend."

Once upon a time there was a furry rabbit. His name was Toby. He lived near a river. Every day Toby went down to the river and looked at the rippling water. One day he noticed a baby duck swimming in the river. Toby thought it was cute and wanted to be friends with it. But the duck was scared and wouldn't come out of the water. Toby called out to the duck but the duck stayed in the river. Toby wanted to be friends with the duck so much that he decided to get into the water and insist that the duck be his friend. He hopped into the river and kept calling out to the duck. After a while, the duck agreed and came out of the river. Toby and the duck soon became great friends and had lots of fun together playing in the river. They even took a bath together!
