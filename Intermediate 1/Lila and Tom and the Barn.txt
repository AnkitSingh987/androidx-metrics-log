Category: Intermediate
Title: Lila and Tom and the Barn

Lila and Tom were friends who liked to play in the farm. They saw the wheat that grew tall and yellow in the field. They wanted to make a cake with the wheat, so they asked the farmer if they could have some. "Sure, you can have some wheat, but you have to be careful with it," the farmer said. "Wheat is very important for making bread and other food. You have to treat it with care and respect." Lila and Tom nodded and thanked the farmer. They took some wheat and went to the barn to make their cake. They mixed the wheat with water and sugar and put it in a pan. They found a fire and put the pan on it. But they did not watch the fire or the pan. They got bored and went to play with the animals. They forgot about their cake and the wheat. Soon, the fire grew bigger and the wheat burned. The smoke filled the barn and the animals ran away. The farmer saw the smoke and ran to the barn. He put out the fire and saved the barn. He was very angry and sad. He found Lila and Tom and scolded them. "Look what you did! You wasted the wheat and almost burned the barn! You made the animals scared and dirty! You did not care about the wheat or the barn or the animals! You only cared about yourselves!" Lila and Tom felt very sorry and ashamed. They realized they had been selfish and careless. They apologized to the farmer and promised to be more careful and respectful. They helped the farmer clean the barn and the animals. They learned their lesson and never played with fire or wheat again.

Mark and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. Mark saw a big hill and wanted to go up. He said to Lily, "Come on, let's climb the hill. It will be fun." Lily looked at the hill and felt nervous. It was very high and steep. She said to Mark, "No, thank you. I don't want to climb the hill. I might fall and hurt myself." Mark said, "Don't be scared. I will help you. It's not hard. Just follow me." He started to climb the hill, using his hands and feet. He looked back and smiled at Lily. Lily wanted to be brave, so she followed Mark. She tried to copy his moves, but she was not as fast or strong. She slipped on the grass and lost her balance. She screamed, "Mark, help me!" Mark heard Lily and turned around. He saw her falling down the hill. He was scared too. He reached out his hand and tried to catch her, but he was too late. Lily rolled down the hill and landed on the ground. She was crying and bleeding. Mark ran down the hill and hugged Lily. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. Are you okay?" Lily said, "I'm not okay. I'm hurt. I'm mad at you. You made me climb the hill. You didn't help me." Mark said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to make you fall. I wanted to have fun with you. I'm your friend." Lily said, "You're my friend?" Mark said, "Yes, I'm your friend. I love you, Lily." Lily said, "I love you too, Mark. But next time, let's play something else."

One day, a bossy caterpillar went for a walk. He gazed at the trees and the flowers. He liked to tell them what to do. The caterpillar would say, "Grow taller!" or "Bloom now!" But the trees and flowers did not listen. The bossy caterpillar met a big, friendly dog. The dog liked to run and play. The caterpillar told the dog, "Stop! You must crawl like me!" But the dog did not listen. He kept running and playing. Then, something unexpected happened. The bossy caterpillar turned into a beautiful butterfly! Now, he could fly like the big, friendly dog could run. The butterfly learned that being bossy was not fun. He played with the dog and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. Lily was very generous. She loved to share her toys with her friends. One day, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom was sad because he lost his toy. Lily gave Tom one of her toys. Tom was happy and they became best friends. Lily and Tom loved to play together. They liked to play marry and take a nap. One day, while they were playing, a big dog came. The dog took Tom's toy and ran away. Tom was sad again. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's go find the dog and get your toy back." Tom agreed. They went on a long walk to find the dog. They looked behind trees and under rocks. Finally, they found the dog taking a nap. Lily and Tom were scared, but they knew they had to get the toy back. They carefully took the toy from the dog. The dog woke up and saw them. But instead of being angry, the dog was happy. The dog was lonely and just wanted to play. Lily and Tom decided to share their toys with the dog. They all played together and became friends. The moral of the story is that sometimes when we share, we can make new friends and solve problems.
