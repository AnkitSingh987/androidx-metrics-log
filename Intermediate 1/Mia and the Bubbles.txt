Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Bubbles

One sunny day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. She had a bottle of bubbles with her. Mia loved blowing bubbles and watching them float in the air. She thought it was fun to try and catch the bubbles before they popped. Mia saw her friend Tom. Tom was a silly boy who sometimes did stupid things. Tom came over and said, "I want to blow my own bubbles too!" So, Mia shared her bubbles with Tom, and they blew bubbles together. As they played, a big bubble floated up in the sky. Tom said, "Look, Mia! That's the biggest bubble ever!" They both laughed and tried to catch it. But the big bubble popped before they could reach it. They had a great day playing with bubbles and being happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a very brilliant bird. He wanted to go for a walk and look for some new adventure. So, he started walking around and looking everywhere. Suddenly, he saw a great big puddle. He wanted to make a splash, so without a moments hesitation he jumped into it! Splash! His feathers were all wet and his beak was dripping with sparkling droplets. He jumped out, shook his feathers and exclaimed, “I’m so happy now!” At that moment, a little 3 year old boy nearby heard the bird and said, “What a brilliant bird you are!” The bird smiled and said, “Let’s splash in the puddle together!” The little boy smiled and said, “Yes, let’s start!” And so the bird and the little boy started playing in the puddle, splashing and giggling the whole day. What a brilliant adventure they had!

Once there was a small dog named Max. Max liked to play outside. One day, Max was playing in the yard with his friend, a little girl named Lily. Max was on a leash to protect him from running away. Lily said, "Max, let's play with the ball!" Max barked and wagged his tail. They played with the ball and had a lot of fun. The sky was clear and the sun was shining. Suddenly, a big bird flew down and tried to take Max's ball. Max barked loudly and scared the bird away. Lily was surprised and said, "Wow, Max! You protected our ball!" Max felt very proud and happy. From that day on, Max and Lily knew that even a small dog could protect his friends.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a new game. He was very happy. The game was about a cat who loved to taste different foods. The cat was cute and funny. Tim wanted to play the game all day. In the game, the cat went to a big park. There were many foods to taste. The cat tasted ice cream, cake, and cookies. Each time the cat tasted something, it made a funny face. Tim laughed and laughed. One day, Tim's friend, Sally, came to play. Tim showed her the new game. They played together and tasted new foods with the cat. They had so much fun. Tim and Sally loved the game and played it every day.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there was a small, unusual house. In the house lived a boy named Tom and his mom. They were very happy in their unusual house. One day, Tom saw a big bird. The bird was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, big bird?" The bird said, "I lost my friend. I can't find her." Tom wanted to help the bird. He said, "I will help you find your friend." Tom and the bird looked all around the valley. They looked behind trees and under rocks. Finally, they found the bird's friend. She was stuck in a small hole. Tom and the bird helped her out of the hole. The birds were very happy. They said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome!" Tom and the birds became good friends. They played together in the valley every day.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom had a big buckle on his belt. He liked to wear it every day. Tom was not happy. He always complained about everything. He complained about the sun, the rain, and even the wind. One day, Tom went for a walk. He saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, his buckle got caught on a branch. Tom tried to get free, but he couldn't. He complained and complained, but no one was there to help him. Tom stayed stuck on the hill all day and night. He was cold and hungry. He kept complaining, but it didn't help. In the end, Tom was still stuck, and he was very sad.
