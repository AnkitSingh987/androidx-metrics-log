Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Tree

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big tree with many leaves. They wanted to climb the tree and see the birds and the sky. "Can you help me, Tom?" Lily asked. "I am too small to reach the branch." "Sure, Lily. I will lead you to the tree and lift you up. Hold my hand and follow me." Tom said. He was a good friend and liked to help Lily. They walked to the tree and Tom lifted Lily up to the first branch. Then he climbed up too. They felt happy and proud. They looked around and saw many things. They saw flowers, grass, swings, slides, and other children. They saw a big cloud that looked like a bunny. They saw a plane that made a loud noise. "Look, Tom, look! I have gum in my pocket. Do you want some?" Lily said. She took out a pink gum and gave half to Tom. They chewed the gum and made bubbles with their mouths. They laughed and had fun. But then, something bad happened. The gum got stuck in Lily's hair. She tried to pull it out, but it only made it worse. She felt scared and sad. She started to cry. "Don't cry, Lily. I will help you. Let me see your hair." Tom said. He was a good friend and wanted to help Lily. He tried to take out the gum, but it was too sticky and hard. He pulled and pulled, but it did not come out. He felt sorry and angry. He started to cry too. They both cried and cried. They felt dizzy and sick. They wanted to go down and go home. They wanted their mom and dad. "Help, help! We are stuck in the tree! We have gum in our hair! We feel dizzy and sick! Help, help!" They shouted. They hoped someone would hear them and help them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She had a small pillow that she liked to sleep with. One day, she was playing in the backyard with her friends and it started to get very dry and windy. Suddenly, everything froze! The little girl was scared and started to cry. She went inside and grabbed her pillow. She held it close to her and started to feel safe again. As she walked to her bedroom, she heard a loud noise. She turned around and saw a big monster standing there. The monster said, "Give me your pillow!" The little girl was scared and she froze. She started to whimper and said, "Please don't take my pillow." But it was too late, the monster had already grabbed the pillow and ran away. The little girl was so upset because she had lost her pillow. She was so scared she didn't know what to do. She never saw her pillow again.

Anna and Ben love to go to the zoo with their mom. They like to see the big and small animals and learn their names. They also like to feed some of the animals with the food that mom buys for them. One day, they go to the zoo and see the giraffes. The giraffes have long necks and spots. They eat leaves from the trees. Mom gives Anna and Ben some leaves to feed the giraffes. Anna and Ben each hold a leaf and wait for a giraffe to come. But Anna and Ben are competitive. They both want to feed the same giraffe. They try to get the giraffe's attention by waving their leaves and calling its name. They do not share or take turns. They push and pull each other to get closer to the giraffe. The giraffe does not like this. It does not like loud noises or fighting. It does not want to eat the leaves from Anna and Ben. It turns away and walks to another part of the fence. It finds a quiet and kind girl who has a leaf for it. It gently takes the leaf from her hand and chews it happily. Anna and Ben are sad and angry. They do not understand why the giraffe did not eat their leaves. Mom explains to them that the giraffe was scared and annoyed by their behavior. She tells them that they need to be polite and respectful to the animals and to each other. She says that they can try again with another animal, but only if they promise to share and take turns. Anna and Ben say they are sorry and hug each other. They agree to be nice and cooperative. They follow mom to the next animal, which is a zebra. They hope the zebra will like their leaves. They smile and hold out their leaves together. The zebra comes and eats from both of their hands. Anna and Ben are happy and proud. They thank the zebra and mom. They have learned a lesson and had fun at the zoo.
