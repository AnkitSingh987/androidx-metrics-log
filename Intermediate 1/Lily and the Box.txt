Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Box

Lily liked to mix colors. She had a big box of paints and a brush. She put some water in a cup and dipped her brush in it. Then she picked a color and made a line on a paper. She did this with many colors, making lines and dots and swirls. She looked at her paper and smiled. She had made a landscape. A landscape is a picture of what you see outside. She saw blue for the sky, green for the grass, yellow for the sun, and brown for the trees. She also saw some red and purple flowers. She wanted to show her landscape to someone. She took her paper and ran outside. She saw her friend Ben playing with his ball. She waved to him and said, "Hi, Ben! Look what I made!" Ben was friendly. He liked Lily and her paints. He stopped playing and looked at her paper. He said, "Wow, Lily! That's a beautiful landscape! You are very good at mixing colors!" Lily felt happy. She said, "Thank you, Ben! Do you want to mix colors with me? I have more paints and papers. We can make more landscapes together!" Ben nodded. He said, "Yes, please! That sounds fun!" He put his ball away and followed Lily to her house. They sat on the floor and opened the box of paints. They dipped their brushes and started to mix colors. They made more landscapes, each one different and pretty. They had a lot of fun mixing colors and making friends.

One day, a polite little triangle was walking in the park. He saw a big circle and a small square playing with a ball. The triangle wanted to play with them, but he didn't know if he could. The triangle went up to the circle and square and asked, "Can I play with you? I want to have fun too." The circle and square looked at the triangle and said, "Yes, you can play with us. You belong with us." So, the polite triangle played with the circle and square all day. They had lots of fun together. They learned that no matter what shape they were, they could all play and be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow ball. The ball was friends with a cloud. They liked to play together. The cloud would rain, and the yellow ball would roll in the puddles. One day, the cloud said, "I will rain a lot today. Be ready to play!" The yellow ball was very happy and waited for the rain. The cloud rained and rained, and the puddles grew bigger. The yellow ball started to roll in the puddles. It rolled too fast and could not stop. It was going to hit a tree! The yellow ball said, "Please tree, move away!" The tree did not yield. The yellow ball hit the tree and stopped. The cloud laughed, and they played again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to move and play all day. He was a very good dog. One day, he met a big cat named Tom. Tom had a hurt shoulder and could not move very well. Max said to Tom, "Can I help you? I am a good dog and I can move fast." Tom looked at Max and said, "Yes, please. My shoulder hurts and I can't move to find food." Max was happy to help. He moved fast to find food for Tom. He brought back some tasty fish. Tom ate the fish and felt much better. His shoulder started to feel good too. Tom said, "Thank you, Max. You are a very good dog." From that day on, Max and Tom became best friends. They played and moved together every day. And they both took care of each other when one was not feeling good.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who liked to walk. Every day she would go for a long walk to explore the world around her. One day she came across a big, hard billboard. It was tall, and made of wood. The little girl walked around the billboard a few times and looked up at it. She was curious about what was written inside. So the little girl decided to find a way up. She searched around until she found a ladder and carried it over to the billboard. She placed the ladder against the hard billboard and started to climb. She eventually reached the top and was shocked to find a surprise. There were balloons, sweets, and games. She was happy and wanted to stay, but the sun had started to go down and so the little girl went home. She said goodnight to the big, hard billboard and looked forward to playing again tomorrow.
