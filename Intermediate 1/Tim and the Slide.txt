Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Slide

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big slide and wanted to play. Tim was very happy. The slide was very smooth, and he loved to slide on it. He went up and down many times. Tim's mom was at the park too. She had a picnic with sandwiches and sauce. The sauce was red and sweet. Tim liked the sauce on his sandwich. After eating, Tim went back to the slide. As Tim went to slide again, he saw a big dog near the slide. The dog had the sauce all over its face. Tim was scared and did not know what to do. He went up the slide, but the dog followed him. Tim tried to slide down, but the dog was on the slide too. They both slid down fast and fell. Tim was sad, and the slide was not fun anymore.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a weird muffin. It was blue and had a face on it. Mia was hungry, so she picked up the muffin and said, "Hi, muffin!" The weird muffin could talk! It said, "Hi, Mia! I am a magic muffin. If you thank me, I can make your wish come true." Mia was very surprised, but she wanted to try. Mia said, "Thank you, weird muffin. I wish for a big, fun party with all my friends." The muffin smiled and in a second, Mia's house was full of friends, games, and yummy food. Mia was so happy and thanked the weird muffin again. They all had a great time at the party.

One day, a small bird with a hurt wing sat on a dirty log. The bird was sad because it could not fly. A kind girl named Amy saw the bird and wanted to help it. She knew that if she could heal the bird's wing, it would be happy again. Amy went to her mom and asked for a bandage. Her mom gave her one and told her to be gentle with the bird. Amy went back to the dirty log and carefully put the bandage on the bird's wing. She hoped that it would help the bird heal. After a few days, the bird's wing was all better. It could fly again! The bird was so happy and thanked Amy with a beautiful song. Amy knew that helping the bird was the right thing to do. From that day on, the bird and Amy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom and his dog, Spot. They were best friends. Tom loved to play with Spot and Spot loved to run in the yard. One day, Tom's shoe had a broken heel. He felt sad because he could not play with Spot. Tom's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom showed her the broken heel. His mom said, "Don't worry, I can fix it for you." Tom's mom took the shoe and fixed the heel. Tom was very happy. Now, Tom could play with Spot again. They ran and played in the yard. After playing, they both were very hungry. Tom's mom made them a yummy meal. Tom and Spot ate the food and felt happy. They loved spending time together.

Once upon a time, there was a wide comet that was flying in the night sky. One evening, the comet paused to look around at all the stars twinkling in the sky. Suddenly, the comet noticed a little girl looking up into the sky. The girl was only three years old and she had never seen a comet before. She was so excited and happy to spot the wide comet. She waved at the comet and it waved back. The comet thought, "I know what I will do. I will pause until the morning so this little girl has something special to look at when she wakes up." So, the comet paused until morning. When the little girl opened her eyes, she was very excited to see the wide comet still shining in the sky. She waved to the comet once again, and it waved back. The comet was very happy to have made the little girl's day so special. Then, it flew away up into the night sky, with many more stars twinkling around it. The little girl watched until it vanished from sight, then went back to bed with a big smile on her face.
