Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Fierce Dog and the Toy

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max loved to play all day. One day, Max found a big toy. The toy was very fun to play with. Max played with the toy so much, he started to destroy it. He bit it and threw it around. The toy was not happy, but Max did not know. A little girl named Lucy saw Max and the toy. She had a question for Max. She asked, "Why do you destroy the toy?" Max did not understand. He just wanted to play. Lucy showed Max how to play nice with the toy. Max learned to be gentle and not destroy things. Now, Max and the toy were both happy. And they all played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play near a small bridge in the park. The bridge was rough and old, but Tim loved it. Every day, he would run across the bridge and laugh. One day, Tim saw a big ball on the other side of the bridge. He wanted to play with it. As he ran across the bridge, he did not see a hole in the middle. Tim's foot went into the hole, and he started to fall. Tim's mommy saw him fall and ran to help him. She picked him up and gave him a big hug. Tim was scared, but his mommy made him feel better. They fixed the hole in the bridge together, so no one else would fall. From that day on, Tim and his mommy always checked the bridge before playing near it.

One day, a little girl named Lily was in her house. She heard her mom call her from the hall. "Lily, hurry! We have to go to a fancy party!" Lily put on her pretty dress and ran to her mom. In the hall, Lily saw her mom in a fancy dress too. They were both excited to go to the party. They walked out the door and went to the big party house. At the party, Lily and her mom saw a big cake. They wanted to eat it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog jumped out of the cake! Everyone was surprised. The dog said, "Surprise! I am a talking dog!" Lily and her mom laughed and had fun with the dog at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a favorite toy, an old teddy bear named Mr. Snuggles. Mia and Mr. Snuggles loved to play together. One day, Mia saw a tight knot in Mr. Snuggles' arm. She was sad and she hoped she could fix it. Mia went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, Mr. Snuggles has a tight knot. Can you help me fix it?" Her mommy looked at the knot and said, "Mia, we need to share our toys. You pulled Mr. Snuggles' arm too hard when you didn't want to share him with your brother." Mia felt bad and she knew she was wrong. She went to her brother and said, "I am sorry, I should have shared Mr. Snuggles with you." Her brother smiled and said, "It's okay, Mia. I forgive you. Let's play together and share our toys." So, Mia and her brother played together and shared their toys. They both took care of Mr. Snuggles and made sure he was happy. The tight knot in his arm was fixed and they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that sharing is important and it makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful bird. The bird lived in a cage. Every day, the bird sang happy songs. One day, a boy came to the bird. The boy said, "Can I lend your cage?" The playful bird said, "Yes, you can lend my cage." The boy took the cage and said, "Thank you, playful bird!" The bird was happy to help. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim and his mom. One day, Tim's mom was making a cake. She asked Tim to help her. "Tim, can you stir the cake mix for me?" she asked. Tim was happy to help and started to stir the mix. While they were making the cake, Tim's mom saw an emergency outside. A cat was stuck in a tight spot in a tree. Tim's mom went to help the cat, leaving Tim alone in the kitchen. Tim wanted to finish the cake, so he kept stirring. But he stirred too fast and the mix spilled everywhere! The kitchen was a big mess. Tim's mom came back and saw the mess. She was sad that the cake was ruined. Tim felt bad for making the mess and promised to be more careful next time.
