Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Sun and the Park

The sun shone brightly in the sky, and the air was warm with summer. A happy child was outside playing in the park. Suddenly, the ground began to shake and the child looked around in fear. The shaking got bigger and bigger until it was too frightening. The child ran away, scared, and the shaking didn't stop until the child was far away. The child wasn't happy anymore.

Sam and Mia liked to play with their gear. They had cars, trains, planes and robots. They made noises and moved them around the floor. They had fun. But Tom did not like to play with gear. He liked to tease Sam and Mia. He said their gear was silly and boring. He took their gear and hid it. He laughed and ran away. Sam and Mia were sad and angry. They wanted their gear back. They looked for Tom and their gear. They found them in the closet. Tom was playing with their gear. He had made an incredible tower with their cars, trains, planes and robots. Sam and Mia were surprised. They liked the tower. They asked Tom if they could play with him. Tom said yes. He was sorry for teasing them. He said their gear was cool and fun. He shared their gear and helped them make more towers. Sam, Mia and Tom became friends. They played with their gear together. They made noises and moved them around the floor. They had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named John. He was a happy boy, who liked to take snacks to school with him. One day, his teacher had a crazy idea. She said John couldn't take snacks to school anymore. He was very sad but he had to obey his teacher. John thought of a plan. He decided to take his snack to school in his pocket. He did this every day, and he was very sneaky. A few weeks later, his teacher noticed what he was doing. She was very angry and told him he had to stop. He was very sad and angry too. John thought of another plan. He decided to take his snack to school in his backpack instead. His teacher was so frustrated that she let him keep taking snacks with him. John was very happy and continued to take his snacks to school every day with no trouble. Everyone was happy and they thought John was very clever.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She heard a ring from the phone. Her mom picked it up and said, "Lily, it's your uncle on the phone!" Lily talked to her uncle and he said he was coming to visit. She was very happy because she loved her uncle. He always played fun games with her and told her funny stories. When her uncle came, they played all day. Lily got very hungry. She told her uncle, "I'm hungry!" Her uncle said, "Let's go make a yummy snack!" So they went to the kitchen and made a big sandwich. They ate it together and laughed. It was a fun day with her uncle.

Lily and Ben were on a trip with their mom and dad. They stayed in a big hotel with many rooms and a pool. Lily and Ben liked the pool a lot. They put on their swimsuits and ran to the water. "Wait for me!" Lily shouted to Ben, who was faster than her. She saw him jump into the pool and splash. She wanted to do the same. She ran faster and faster, but she did not see the icy floor. It was slippery and shiny. She slipped and fell. She hurt her knee and cried. "Ow, ow, ow!" Lily cried. Ben heard her and swam to the edge of the pool. He saw Lily on the floor, holding her knee. He felt sorry for her. He got out of the pool and ran to her. "Are you okay, Lily?" Ben asked. He helped her stand up and hugged her. "I'm sorry you fell. The floor is icy. You have to be careful." Lily stopped crying and hugged Ben back. "Thank you, Ben. You are a good brother. I'm sorry I ran too fast. I wanted to play with you." They walked to their mom and dad, who were sitting on chairs. They saw Lily's knee and put a band-aid on it. They told Lily and Ben to be more careful and not to run on the icy floor. They said they could still play in the pool, but they had to walk slowly and hold hands. Lily and Ben learned their lesson. They played in the pool together, but they did not run or push. They had fun and stayed safe. They were happy and thankful. They learned that the icy floor can be dangerous, and that they should always look out for each other.
