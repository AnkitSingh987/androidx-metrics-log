Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Car and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a popular toy made of steel. It was a shiny toy car that all the kids loved. The car had a big smile on its face and would always promise to be a good friend to the kids who played with it. One day, a little boy named Tim got the steel car as a gift. He was so happy and promised to take good care of it. Tim played with the car every day, and they became best friends. They would race around the house and have lots of fun together. But one day, Tim left the steel car outside in the rain. The car got very wet and started to rust. Tim forgot his promise to take care of the car. The car got sadder and sadder as it rusted more and more. In the end, the once popular steel car was not shiny anymore, and Tim could not play with it. The car was sad, and Tim was sad too, because he broke his promise.

One day, a big, impressive dog named Max went for a jog in the park. He saw a little cat on top of a tall tree. The cat was scared and did not know how to get down. Max said, "Don't worry, little cat. I will help you get down from the tree." The cat said, "Thank you, Max. I am very scared up here." Max found a long stick and used it to help the cat come down. After the cat was safe, Max said, "Remember, little cat, always be careful when you climb trees. It is better to ask for help than to be scared and stuck." The cat said, "Thank you, Max. I will remember that." From that day on, the cat always asked for help when she wanted to climb a tree. The moral of the story is to ask for help when you need it.

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Andy. He was very good at carrying weight. One day, Andy found a big piece of food. He wanted to take it back to his ant friends. But the food was too heavy for Andy alone. So, he asked his friend, a tiny ant named Tim, for help. "Tim, please help me carry this big food," said Andy. Tim wanted to be strong like Andy. So, he tried his best to help carry the weight. But it was still too heavy for both of them. They needed more help. Andy had an idea. He went to ask his bird friend, Betty, for help. "Betty, please help us carry this big food," said Andy. Betty was happy to help her ant friends. She used her strong wings to lift the big food. Together, Andy, Tim, and Betty carried the big food back to the ant hill. All the ants were happy and thanked their friends. From that day on, Andy, Tim, and Betty always helped each other when they needed it. And Tim learned that even tiny ants can be strong when they work together.

One day, Tom went for a jog. He liked to run fast. As he ran, he saw a little, adorable dog. The dog was sad. Tom stopped running and asked the dog, "Why are you sad?" The dog said, "I have pain in my foot. I stepped on something sharp." Tom wanted to help the dog. He looked at the dog's foot and saw a small rock. He gently took the rock out of the dog's foot. The dog felt better and wagged its tail. Tom and the dog started to jog together. They were happy and having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The dog started to talk like a human! The dog said, "Thank you for helping me, Tom. I am a magic dog!" Tom was very surprised but happy to have a new magic friend.

Once upon a time, there was a warm mill. In the mill, there lived a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to make bread for the people in the town. Every day, he would wake up, go to the mill, and make bread. One day, a little girl named Sally came to the mill. She was hungry and cold. Tom saw Sally and asked if she would like some bread. Sally said yes, and Tom gave her a big, warm loaf of bread. Sally loved the bread and felt warm and happy. From that day on, Sally would visit the mill every day. She and Tom became great friends. They both loved the warm mill and the bread they made together. And they lived happily ever after.
