Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ice

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to gather ice with her friends. They all thought it was good fun. Mia and her friends lived in a small town where it was always cold, so there was always lots of ice to gather. One day, Mia and her friend Tim went to gather ice. They found a big pile of ice near a tree. Mia said, "Look, Tim! This ice is so big and good!" Tim agreed, "Yes, Mia! Let's take it home and show our friends!" So Mia and Tim gathered the good ice and took it home. They showed their friends and everyone was happy. They played with the ice all day long. At the end of the day, Mia and Tim said, "We had so much fun gathering ice today. Let's do it again soon!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Amy. She loved to play games with her friends. One day, Amy and her friends played a game to win a big mint candy. Amy's friend, Tom, said, "I want to win the mint candy!" Amy said, "Me too! Let's play and see who wins." They played the game, and they both tried very hard to win. In the end, Amy was the one to win the mint candy. She was very happy! But she saw that Tom was sad because he did not win. So, Amy decided to share the mint candy with Tom. Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Amy! You are very nice." They both ate the mint candy together and had a great time. From that day on, they always shared their toys and treats with each other.

Lily loves pandas. She has a big panda toy that she hugs every night. She also has a book with many pictures of pandas. She likes to look at the book and learn new things about pandas. One day, Lily goes to the zoo with her mom and dad. She is very excited to see the real pandas. She runs to the panda enclosure and watches them eat bamboo and play. She sees a big panda with a black mark on its nose. She thinks it is very cute and funny. "Look, mom, look, dad!" Lily says. "That panda has a mark on its nose! Why does it have a mark?" Mom and dad smile and come closer to Lily. They explain to her that pandas have different marks on their fur. Some have more black, some have more white, some have spots, and some have stripes. The marks help them to recognize each other and to blend in with the forest. Lily nods and understands. She thinks that the marks make the pandas special and beautiful. She wants to have a mark too. She takes out her crayon and draws a black mark on her nose. She shows it to the panda with the mark and says, "Hello, panda, I have a mark too! We are friends!" The panda looks at Lily and makes a soft noise. It seems to like Lily and her mark. Lily smiles and waves at the panda. She is very happy. She thinks that pandas are the best animals in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat. The cat liked to play with a toy ring. The ring was red and shiny. The cat would push the ring with its paw, and the ring would roll away. The cat would run and jump to catch the ring again. One day, the black cat saw a tiny bug. The bug was only an inch long. The cat wanted to play with the bug, too. It tried to catch the bug, but the bug was very fast. The cat chased the bug all around the room. As the cat was chasing the bug, it found the toy ring. The cat was happy to see the ring again. It played with the ring and forgot about the bug. The cat had lots of fun with its toy ring, and it played all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim found a pair of cheap scissors. He wanted to use them to cut paper. He showed them to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam, I found these scissors!" Sam looked at the scissors and said, "Be careful, Tim! Scissors can be sharp." Tim and Sam played with the scissors, cutting shapes out of paper. They had fun making different shapes. After a while, Tim noticed that he could not find the scissors. He looked everywhere but could not see them. "Oh no, I lost the scissors!" he said. Sam helped Tim look for the scissors. They found them under a big toy. Tim was happy to find the scissors. He said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me!" Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim! Let's be careful and not lose the scissors again." They played with the scissors some more and had a great time.
