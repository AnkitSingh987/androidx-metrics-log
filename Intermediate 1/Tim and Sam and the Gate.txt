Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sam and the Gate

Tim and Sam were best friends. They liked to play in the park near their homes. One day, they saw a big gate at the end of the park. The gate was made of metal and had many shapes on it. It looked very old and mighty. "Wow, look at that gate!" Tim said. "I wonder what is behind it." "Maybe a castle, or a dragon, or a treasure!" Sam said. "Let's go and see." They ran towards the gate, but they saw a sign that said "Do not touch". They stopped and looked at each other. "Do you think we should touch it?" Tim asked. "I don't know. Maybe it is dangerous. Maybe it will make a loud noise or shock us." Sam said. "But maybe it will open and show us something amazing. Maybe it is a magic gate." Tim said. He reached out his hand and touched the gate. Nothing happened. He felt the metal and the shapes. It was cold and hard. "See, it is not scary. It is just a gate." Tim said. He looked at Sam and smiled. He wanted Sam to touch it too. "Come on, touch it. It is fun." Tim said. Sam was still afraid. He looked at the sign and the gate. He did not want to touch it. "No, I don't want to. The sign says no. We should listen to the sign." Sam said. He backed away from the gate. He did not want to get in trouble. "Fine, be a baby. I will touch it some more." Tim said. He touched the gate again and again. He felt brave and curious. He did not care about the sign. Suddenly, the gate made a loud noise. It shook and opened. Tim and Sam saw a bright light and a strange sound. They were scared and surprised. "What is that?" Tim shouted. "I don't know. Let's run!" Sam shouted. They ran away from the gate as fast as they could. They did not look back. They did not want to see what was behind it. They ran all the way to their homes. They did not tell anyone about the gate. They did not go back to the park for a long time. They learned to never touch things that say "Do not touch".

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She loved to mix things up in her empty bowl. One day, she decided to make a special treat for her friends. Sally took her empty bowl and put some yummy things inside. She mixed them all up and watched as they became a fun new treat. She was so excited to share it with her friends. When it was time to play, Sally brought her special treat to share. Her friends loved it and they all had a great time. They laughed and played together, and Sally was so happy that she could make something fun for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very excited because she was going on vacation with her family. She could not wait to play in the wet sand and swim in the big blue sea. Before they left, Mia's mom said, "Mia, go dress in your swimsuit, and don't forget your hat!" Mia put on her swimsuit, her hat, and her flip-flops. She was ready to have fun on their vacation. When they got to the beach, Mia ran into the wet sand. She could feel the cool water on her toes. Mia said, "Mom, the sand is so wet and fun!" They all laughed and played in the wet sand and water all day long. Mia and her family had the best vacation ever.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Alice. Alice had a very reliable friend called Peter. Peter always helped Alice do her chores and work, so she felt very thankful to have him. One day, they decided to have a picnic in the garden. They packed up some sandwiches and a big bag of tomatoes. Alice said, "Let's go to the garden and have a picnic!" So they went to the garden and started to lay out all the food for their picnic. Peter took out the tomatoes and said, "We can have some of these tomatoes. They look very delicious!" Alice smiled at Peter and said, "Thank you for always being reliable and helping me. I'm so lucky to have you as a friend." They then had a very special picnic with lots of tasty tomatoes!
