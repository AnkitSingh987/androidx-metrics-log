Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Basket

One day, Little Bob and his mom decided to go for an adventure. First, Little Bob and his mom introduced themselves to their friendly neighbour, Mrs Prune. Little Bob noticed that the kind old lady had a basket full of delicious plums. The neighbour gave the mother and son a big smile. "Hello Little Bob, I am Mrs Prune. I'm always happy to meet new faces," she introduced. Little Bob and his mom explained that they were looking for an adventure and asked Mrs Prune if she knew of any interesting places to explore. Mrs Prune suggested a reliable path that led right up to the hill. "Just stay on the path," she said. "It's perfectly safe and very reliable." Little Bob and his mom thanked the kind old lady before they headed off. The walk home was full of laughter and happy memories. Thanks to Mrs Prune's reliable directions, Little Bob and his mom had a wonderful adventure!

It was dinner time at the Smith family's house. Mommy was getting ready to cook dinner and asked Billy to help. She gave Billy a bowl and two spoons. She told him to mix them together. He started to mix them up with the spoons. Next, Mommy got out the brush. It was a red brush with white bristles. She asked Billy to help her reach the top of the cabinet. She needed it to get the spices for the dinner. Billy held the brush and Mommy sprinkled some spices from the cabinet. They were spicy spices and made the dinner smell delicious. After they were done, they stirred the bowl together one last time. Then, dinner was ready!

One day, a dog and a cat went on a search. They wanted to find a safe place to play. The dog said, "Let's go fast!" The cat said, "Okay, let's speed!" They ran and ran until they found a big tree. The dog said, "This is a safe place!" They played and played until it got dark. The cat said, "We should go home now." But when they tried to go home, they could not find the way. They were lost. The dog and the cat were very sad. They tried to speed back, but they could not find the way. They were not safe anymore.

One day, a little gray cat went for a walk. The cat liked to watch things. The cat saw a big drain on the side of the road. It was a very big drain with water inside. The cat looked at the drain and thought it was interesting. The next day, the gray cat went back to the drain. The cat sat and watched the water go down the drain. The cat saw a small toy boat near the drain. The cat wanted to play with the boat, but it was too far away. On the third day, the gray cat went back to the drain again. The cat watched the toy boat get closer to the drain. The cat was very happy. The cat jumped and got the toy boat. The gray cat played with the boat all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw an old tree with a big hole in it. Tim found a grape on the ground near the tree. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, mom! I found a grape!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "That's nice, Tim. But don't eat it, it's dirty. Let's lay it by the tree for the birds to eat." As Tim lay the grape by the tree, a small bird came out of the hole. The bird looked at the grape and then at Tim. "Thank you for the grape," the bird said. Tim and his mom were very surprised! They didn't know that birds could talk. The bird told Tim and his mom that it was a magic bird. It could give them one wish. Tim wished for a big bag of grapes to share with his friends. The magic bird made the wish come true. Tim, his mom, and the bird ate grapes and had fun at the park. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others, because you never know when something unexpected and magical might happen.
