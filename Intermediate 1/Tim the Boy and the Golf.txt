Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Golf

One day, a boy named Tim found a golf ball. He did not know who it belonged to. He asked his friend, Sam, "Do you know who this belongs to?" Sam did not know either. Tim and Sam went to the park where people play golf. They saw a powerful man hit a golf ball very far. They thought maybe the golf ball belonged to the powerful man. They went up to the powerful man and asked, "Does this golf ball belong to you?" The powerful man smiled and said, "Yes, it does. Thank you for finding it!" Tim and Sam were happy to help and went back to play.

Jim and his mommy were walking down the road. Jim saw a long river of water. It sparkled in the sunlight. Jim was so excited, he shouted, "Let's dance in the water!" Jim and his mommy laughed as they jumped into the water. The cool water felt so nice on their feet. They twirled and twirled and spun and danced in the river. Jim and his mommy swam and splashed around for a long time. They made circles and laughed as they raced. Finally, it was time to go home. Jim and his mommy dried off and went back to the house. Jim was so tired after all that dancing. He fell soundly asleep, dreaming of his fun in the long river of water.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat lived in a small house with a nice kitchen. The cat loved to play and eat in the kitchen. One day, a little bird came to the window. The bird saw the purple cat in the kitchen. The bird said, "Hello, can I come in and play with you?" The purple cat nodded and let the bird in. They played and had fun in the kitchen. They shared yummy food and laughed together. The bird and the purple cat became very good friends. From that day on, they always played together in the kitchen.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a new nest in a big tree. He loved his new home very much. One day, Tim found a twig on the ground. He thought the twig would be a good thing to have in his nest. So, he picked it up and flew back to his tree. In the tree, Tim saw his friend, the squirrel named Sam. Sam was mixing nuts and leaves in a bowl. Tim asked Sam what he was doing. Sam said, "I am making a mix for my winter food. Would you like to help me?" Tim said, "Yes, I would like to help you, but first I need to put this twig in my nest." Tim put the twig in his nest and then went to help Sam mix the nuts and leaves. They had a lot of fun mixing the food together. After they were done, Tim said, "Thank you for letting me help you, Sam." Sam said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm happy we could mix the food together." And so, Tim and Sam became very good friends. They helped each other and played together in the big tree. Tim was happy to have a new friend and a new home.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty was nervous because it was raining a lot. The rain made a big flood in her yard. Kitty did not like the flood. Kitty's friend, a dog named Rover, saw the flood too. Rover said, "Kitty, I will rescue you from the flood!" Kitty was happy to hear Rover's plan. They waited for the rain to stop. When the rain stopped, Rover swam through the flood to Kitty. He carried her on his back to a dry place. Kitty was so happy that Rover rescued her. They played together all day, and the flood went away.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a huge tree. The tree was so big that all the animals loved to play near it. One day, a little bird and a small squirrel came to the tree. The bird and the squirrel wanted to go to the other side of the forest. To do that, they needed to cross a big river. The river was very wide and the water was fast. They were not sure how to cross it. So, they sat down under the huge tree to discuss. The bird said, "I can fly across the river." The squirrel said, "I can swim across." They talked and talked. At last, they found a way. The bird would fly and the squirrel would ride on the bird's back. They crossed the river and had a great time in the other part of the forest.
