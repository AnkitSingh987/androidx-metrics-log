Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and the Story

One day, two friends went to the beach. One was called Jack, the other was called Jill. They were both excited to go to the beach and see how different it was. When they got there, they saw the waves crashing and different kinds of pebbles and sand. Jack and Jill wanted to experience the beach, so they decided to attach themselves to the waves. With a big jump, they jumped into the waves and laughed as the waves took them away. Before long, Jack got carried too far away. He noticed that he couldn't see Jill anymore. He was starting to get scared. Suddenly, Jill swam to him and said, "Don't worry! Attach yourself to me and we won't drift away." Jack and Jill stayed together, happy and safe while they enjoyed the beach and the waves. They learnt that the moral of the story was that it is important to stay close together and look out for each other, especially when we are in unfamiliar places.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a new thing in the park. It is a big square. It is red and yellow and blue. It has four sides and four corners. "What is that?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Let's go and see," Ben says. They run to the big square. They touch it. It is hard and smooth. They climb on it. It is fun. They see a hole in the square. They look inside. They see something shiny. "What is that?" Ben asks. "I don't know. Let's go and see," Lily says. They crawl inside the hole. They find a box. It is shiny and has a ribbon. They open the box. They see many small squares. They are brown and have letters on them. "What are these?" Lily asks. "I don't know. Let's try them," Ben says. They take one small square each. They bite them. They are tasty. They are sweet and melt in their mouths. "Mmm, these are good," Ben says. "They are chocolate," Lily says. She knows the word from her mom. They hear a voice from outside the hole. "Hello, children. Do you like my gift?" the voice says. They look outside. They see a man. He is smiling. He has a hat and a beard. He is holding a bag. "Who are you?" Lily asks. "I am Santa. I came to meet you and give you some chocolate. You have been good children this year," the man says. "Wow, you are Santa. Thank you for the chocolate. It is very tasty," Ben says. "You are welcome. Merry Christmas, Lily and Ben. I have to go now. I have more gifts to deliver. Be good and have fun," the man says. He waves and walks away. Lily and Ben wave back. They are happy. They have met Santa and got some chocolate. They share the chocolate with their friends. They play with the big square. They have a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees in his yard. One sunny day, he saw a big, old tree with many branches. He thought it would be fun to climb it. As Tim climbed the tree, he saw a dirty cobweb. He didn't like dirty things, so he called out to his mom. "Mom, there's a dirty cobweb in the tree!" His mom said, "That's okay, Tim. Just be careful not to touch it." Tim kept climbing higher and higher. He felt very happy to be so high up in the tree. When he reached the top, he looked down and saw his mom smiling at him. "Good job, Tim!" she said. Tim felt proud of himself for climbing the big tree and not being scared of the dirty cobweb.

Once upon a time there was a lazy dog. It liked to sleep all day and night. One day it decided to go on an adventure. It jumped into the car, even though it knew it was not allowed. The lazy dog started to drive. It drove and drove, until it saw a bright purple flower. The lazy dog just kept driving and driving, until it saw a deep purple lake. It drove down to the lake and jumped in. The lake was very deep with no shore. The lazy dog was so lazy, it couldn’t swim back. It was so scared, but there was nothing it could do. After a few hours, the lazy dog finally gave up and was never seen again.
