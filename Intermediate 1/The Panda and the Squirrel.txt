Category: Intermediate
Title: The Panda and the Squirrel

One day, there was a panda who wanted to organize his house. He thought and thought about how he could do it. Suddenly, he had an idea! He decided to ask his friend, the squirrel, for help. The panda asked the squirrel, "Can you help me organize my house?" The squirrel replied, "Yes! Of course I can help you. What do you need?" The panda smiled. He said, "I need you to help me put my toys in the right places. They need to be organized." The squirrel nodded. She asked, "Which toys are available?" The panda showed the squirrel all of his toys. The squirrel found the perfect places for them. When the squirrel finished, the panda was so happy! He thanked the squirrel for her help. Now, the panda has a neat and tidy house! He was very proud that he organized it all with help from his friend, the squirrel.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They are popular with the other kids. One day, a big boy comes to the park. He is mean and rude. He pushes the other kids and takes their toys. He is a threat to the park. Lily and Ben are scared of him. The big boy sees Lily and Ben on the slide. He runs to them and says, "Get off the slide! It is mine now!" He tries to pull them off. Lily and Ben say, "No! Go away! You are not nice!" They hold on to the slide. The big boy gets angry. He says, "I will hit you if you don't listen to me!" He raises his fist. Lily and Ben see a police car on the street. They wave to the police officer. They hope he will help them. The police officer sees Lily and Ben waving. He sees the big boy threatening them. He stops the car and gets out. He walks to the park. He says to the big boy, "What are you doing? Why are you bothering these kids?" The big boy is scared of the police officer. He says, "Nothing, nothing. I was just playing." The police officer says, "No, you were not. You were bullying and hurting them. That is not okay. You have to apologize and leave." The big boy says, "Sorry, sorry. I won't do it again." He runs away. The police officer says to Lily and Ben, "Are you okay? Did he hurt you?" Lily and Ben say, "We are okay. Thank you for saving us. You are very brave and kind." The police officer says, "You are welcome. You are very smart and brave too. You did the right thing by waving to me. I am glad you are safe. Have fun in the park." Lily and Ben say, "Thank you. Bye-bye." They wave to the police officer. He waves back and drives away. Lily and Ben go back to the slide. They smile and hug. They are happy. They are friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a broken toy on the floor. He picked it up and felt sad. Tim wanted to fix the toy so he could play with it again. He asked his friend, a talking fish named Fin, "Can you help me fix this toy?" Fin looked at the broken toy and replied, "I can try, but I need to find a special part first." Tim and Fin went on a journey to find the special part for the broken toy. They looked under rocks, in trees, and even in a big lung-shaped cave. Finally, they found the special part in a small box hidden in the cave. But when they opened the box, they found a note instead. The note said, "This toy is not broken, it just needs love and care." Tim and Fin realized that the toy wasn't really broken, it just needed a friend. They went back home and played with the toy all day long, and it worked just fine.

One day, a fat snake went to the park. The fat snake wanted to play with the kids. The kids saw the snake and got scared. They did not want to play with the snake. The snake was sad and sat under a tree. A little bird saw the snake and asked, "Why are you sad?" The snake said, "I want to play, but the kids are scared of me." The bird had an idea and told the snake, "You can play with me! We can be friends." They played all day in the park. The kids saw the snake and the bird having fun. They thought, "Maybe the snake is not so scary." The kids came to the snake and asked, "Can we play with you?" The snake was so happy and said, "Yes!" Now, the fat snake and the kids played every day in the park. They became good friends. The snake was not scary anymore, and the kids never mentioned being scared again.
