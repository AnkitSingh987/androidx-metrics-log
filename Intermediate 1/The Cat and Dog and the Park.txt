Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and Dog and the Park

One day, a cat and a dog went to play. They found a big tube in the park. They wanted to see what was inside. The cat said, "I will serve you if you go in the tube first." The dog agreed and went inside. The tube was dark and sour. The dog did not like it. The cat did not serve the dog. The dog was sad and the cat laughed. They did not play together again.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a weird box in his room. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very curious about the box. He called his friend, Sam, to come and see it. When Sam came, they both kneeled down to look at the box. "What do you think is in it?" asked Tim. "I don't know, let's open it and see," said Sam. They were both very excited to see what was inside the weird box. They opened the box and found a strange toy. Tim and Sam played with the toy for a while, but then it broke. Tim and Sam were very sad. They wished they had never found the weird box.

---

Once upon a time, in a small place, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom was very good at lending his toys to his friends. One day, he lent his favorite ball to his best friend, Jerry the mouse. Tom and Jerry played with the ball all day long. They had so much fun. But then it was time for Jerry to go home. Tom told Jerry to bring the ball back the next day. Jerry said he would. The next day, Jerry forgot to bring the ball back. Tom was sad, but he did not get mad. He knew that friends should be kind and help each other. So, Tom went to Jerry's house and helped him find the ball. They found it and played together again. In the end, Tom and Jerry learned that being a good friend means lending and helping each other. They were happy to have each other, and they played together every day. And Tom became famous for being the best friend in the small place.

The little girl was so happy. She looked up at the sky and it was so blue and wide. She loved watching the clouds float by. She was out in the park with her dad. He said, “See that big tree over there? I’m going to climb it. Want to come?” She said yes and smiled. When they reached the top, she was so excited. She asked her dad for a treat. He took out a yummy cookie and passed it to her. She took a bite, then said, “This cookie isn't good. It tastes like yucky sand!” Her dad said, “Maybe I can serve you something better.” He climbed down the tree and went to get a special treat. He said, “I’ll be right back.” But he never returned. The little girl waited and waited, but he never came back. The sky grew dark and the little girl was very scared. She never saw her dad again. The little girl was no longer happy. She had been served a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was walking in her garden when she found an open mug. The mug was very big and made out of glass. She wanted to pick it up, so she reached out her little hands. But it was too heavy for her, so she just watched it. Suddenly, a bright light came out of the mug. It filled the whole garden and made the girl very happy. She smiled and said: "This is so wonderful!" Then, the light suddenly disappeared and the mug was still there, open. The girl got curious and she decided to find out what was inside it. Using all her courage, she put her hands around the mug. After a few seconds, she opened it. There were many colourful candies inside. She took one and smiled from ear-to-ear, she was so happy! From then on, the little girl kept visiting the garden and looking for the open mug. Every time she found it, she was so excited to see the wonderful candies.
