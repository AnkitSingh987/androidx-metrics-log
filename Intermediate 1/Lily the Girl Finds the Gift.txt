Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Gift

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big mushroom in her yard. It was a very special mushroom. It could talk! The mushroom said, "Hello, Lily! My name is Mr. Mushroom." Lily was happy to meet Mr. Mushroom. She wanted to invite him to her tea party. She said, "Mr. Mushroom, would you like to come to my tea party? We will have tea and cookies." Mr. Mushroom was excited. He said, "Yes, Lily! I would love to come to your tea party. But first, I need to get a gift for you. I will find the most expensive gift I can." Lily and Mr. Mushroom had a great time at the tea party. They drank tea, ate cookies, and played with Lily's toys. Mr. Mushroom gave Lily a beautiful, expensive necklace as a gift. They were very happy and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a small garden where she grew beans. One day, she found an unusual bean. It was big and red. Sue was very excited and wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. Sue ran to Tom's house and said, "Look, Tom! I found an unusual bean in my garden! Isn't it pretty?" Tom looked at the bean and said, "Wow, Sue! I admire your bean. It is very special." Sue and Tom decided to plant the unusual bean in Sue's garden. They dug a hole, put the bean inside, and covered it with dirt. Every day, they watered the bean and watched it grow. Soon, the bean grew into a tall beanstalk. Sue and Tom were so happy. They admired the beanstalk and knew their unusual bean was very special. They played near the beanstalk every day and had lots of fun together. And so, Sue and Tom's friendship grew, just like the unusual beanstalk. They learned that even the smallest things can bring people together. And they continued to admire their beanstalk, knowing it was a symbol of their special friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little adorable dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue and Spot went to a big store with Sue's mom. In the store, Sue saw a toy she really wanted. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I have this toy, please?" Her mom said, "No, not today, dear. We are here to buy a gift for your cousin's birthday." While they were looking for the gift, Spot saw a big box with a picture of a brain on it. He thought it was a toy for him to play with, so he tried to grab the box. But the box was too big and heavy for Spot. It fell on the floor and opened up. Out of the box came a robot with a big brain! The robot looked at Spot and said, "I am here to help you. What do you need?" Spot was surprised but happy. He ordered the robot, "Help me find a toy for Sue!" The robot helped Spot find the perfect toy for Sue. Sue's mom was so surprised and happy that she let Sue keep the toy. Sue, Spot, and the robot played together and had lots of fun. And from that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily found a kayak near the river. She thought it was impressive. Lily wanted to use the kayak but did not know how. She saw a boy named Tom near the river too. Lily asked Tom, "Can you explain how to use the kayak?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you!" They went to the river with the kayak. Tom showed Lily how to sit in the kayak and use the paddle. Lily learned quickly and was happy. Lily and Tom took turns using the kayak. They had a lot of fun. They became good friends and played with the impressive kayak every day. They loved spending time at the river together.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She had long hair and liked to tie it up. She saw a boy named Tom with short hair. Tom was trying to tie his shoes but he couldn't. "Can you help me tie my shoes?" Tom asked. Lily said, "Yes, I can help you." She bent down and tied his shoes with a big bow. Tom said, "Thank you, Lily." After that, they played in the sun. Lily had some lotion for her skin. She put some on Tom's face too. They laughed and played all day. They were happy to be friends at the park.
