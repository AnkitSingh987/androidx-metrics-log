Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Race

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Sally. Sally liked to sew with her needle. She would sew pretty dresses for her dolls. She had many dolls, and she loved them all. One day, there was a big sewing race in the town. All the kids wanted to be the best at sewing. They were very competitive. Sally wanted to win and sew the prettiest dress for her favorite doll. The race started, and all the kids sewed as fast as they could. But Sally sewed too fast and hurt her finger with the needle. It made her weep. Her mom came and hugged her. She told Sally that it was okay, and she didn't need to win the race. Sally felt better and went back to sewing, but now she sewed slowly and carefully. She didn't win the race, but she made a lovely dress for her doll. She was happy, and so was her doll. They had a fun tea party with the new dress, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. Amy had a big job to do. She had to help her mom with the dishwasher. Amy did not think this was fair. She did not want to do it. One day, Amy said to her mom, "I don't want to do the dishwasher. It's not fair!" Her mom said, "We all must help. You are a big girl now. You can do it." Amy did not want to complain, but she still did not like it. She put the dishes in the dishwasher. She saw her mom was happy. This made Amy feel good. Soon, Amy learned that helping others is a good thing. It made her feel happy too. The moral of the story is: helping others can make you feel good, even if you don't want to do it at first.

Once upon a time, there was a furry bunny. The bunny liked to play in the sun. The sun would shine and make the bunny happy. One day, the bunny found an arrow on the ground. The arrow was shiny like the sun. The bunny picked up the arrow and hopped around. The bunny met a bird. The bird saw the arrow and wanted to play too. The bunny and the bird played with the arrow all day. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The arrow started to shine even more. The bunny and the bird were surprised. The shiny arrow turned into a magic wand! The furry bunny and the bird now had a magic wand to play with. They had more fun than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom was very strong and could lift very heavy things. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to play. In the park, Tom saw a little girl who was sad. She was holding a big book. Tom walked to her and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I want to read this book, but I don't know the words." Tom wanted to help the little girl. He said, "I can teach you the words." So, Tom and the little girl sat on the grass. Tom would lift the book and point to a word. The little girl would listen and learn the word. They did this for a long time. Soon, the little girl could read the book by herself. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom was happy too, because he could help the little girl. They became good friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He saw a big ice-cream truck. Tim was very happy. In the park, there was a man with a big hat. He was doing a show. The man was a little scary. But Tim wanted to see the show. So, he went with his mom to watch the man. The man did tricks and made people clap. Tim liked the show. At the end of the show, the man said, "I have a gift for the person who can clap the loudest!" Tim wanted the gift. He clapped very loud. The man smiled and gave Tim a big ice-cream. Tim was very happy. The scary man was not so scary after all.
