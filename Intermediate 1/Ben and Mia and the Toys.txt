Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Mia and the Toys

Ben and Mia were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their toys. One day, Ben brought a new toy to show Mia. It was a green crocodile with big teeth and a long tail. "Look, Mia, this is my new toy. It is a crocodile. It can snap and bite. Do you like it?" Ben said. Mia looked at the crocodile and felt scared. She did not like it. She liked her pink bunny more. It was soft and cuddly. She hugged her bunny and said, "No, Ben, I do not like your crocodile. It is scary and mean. I like my bunny. It is nice and sweet." Ben felt sad. He liked his crocodile. He thought it was cool and fun. He wanted Mia to like it too. He said, "But, Mia, my crocodile is not scary and mean. It is just a toy. It cannot hurt you. It can be nice and sweet too. See, it can smile and wink. Watch this." He made the crocodile smile and wink at Mia. Mia watched the crocodile and felt curious. She saw that it was not so scary and mean. It was just a toy. It could smile and wink. She said, "Oh, Ben, your crocodile can smile and wink. That is funny and cute. Can I touch it?" Ben felt glad. He saw that Mia was not scared and mean. She was just curious and cute. He said, "Yes, Mia, you can touch it. It is soft and smooth. Here, you can hold it." He gave the crocodile to Mia. Mia held the crocodile and felt happy. She said, "Thank you, Ben, for showing me your crocodile. It is cool and fun. I like it. Do you want to hold my bunny?" Ben said, "Yes, Mia, I want to hold your bunny. It is soft and cuddly. I like it. Thank you for sharing with me." They hugged each other and their toys. They were best friends. They liked to play in the park with their toys. They were glad.

Once upon a time, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She liked to walk in the park. The sun was up and the sky was blue. Sue was very happy. Sue saw a dog. The dog was very alert. Sue walked to the dog and said, "Hi, dog! What is your name?" The dog did not say anything. Sue was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The dog said, "My name is Max!" Sue was so surprised. She did not know dogs could talk. Sue and Max became best friends and had lots of fun walks together.

Lily and Max liked to play with blocks. They could build many things with blocks, like houses, cars, and towers. One day, they decided to build a penguin with blocks. They used black, white, and orange blocks to make the penguin's body, head, beak, and feet. "Look, our penguin is very attractive!" Lily said, admiring their work. "Yes, it is!" Max agreed. "But it is missing something. It needs eyes!" Max looked around and saw two shiny buttons on the floor. He picked them up and ran to the penguin. He stuck the buttons on the penguin's head, where the eyes should be. "There, now it has eyes!" Max said, smiling. But Lily was not happy. She did not like the buttons. She thought they were ugly and did not match the blocks. "No, no, no!" Lily said, pulling the buttons off. "The buttons are bad! They ruin the penguin!" Max was angry. He liked the buttons. He thought they made the penguin look more real and cute. "No, no, no!" Max said, pushing Lily away. "The buttons are good! They make the penguin better!" Lily and Max started to fight over the buttons. They pulled and pushed each other, and knocked over the penguin. The blocks fell apart and scattered on the floor. Lily and Max stopped fighting and looked at the mess. They felt sad and sorry. They had broken their penguin and hurt each other. They looked at each other and said, "I'm sorry." They hugged and made up. They decided to share the buttons and the blocks. They worked together to rebuild the penguin. They used one button for each eye, and made the penguin smile. "Look, our penguin is very attractive!" Lily and Max said, holding hands. They learned that sharing and cooperating are better than fighting and breaking. They played happily with their penguin and their blocks.
