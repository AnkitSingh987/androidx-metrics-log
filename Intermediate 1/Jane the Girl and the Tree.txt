Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane lived in a small cabin with her mom and dad. She was a very proud girl because she could do many things by herself. One day, Jane went outside to play with her friend, Tom. Tom liked to tease Jane because she was so proud. He said, "I bet you can't climb that tree!" Jane looked at the tree and said, "Yes, I can!" So, Jane climbed the tree all the way to the top. Tom was very surprised and said, "Wow, you did it! I'm sorry for teasing you, Jane." Jane smiled and said, "That's okay, Tom. We all can do great things if we try." And from that day on, Tom never teased Jane again. They played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house near the woods. Tom loved to drink tea every day. He would sit outside and enjoy his tea while looking at the trees and flowers. One day, a tiny bird fell from its nest. Tom saw the bird and wanted to protect it. He picked up the bird and put it in a warm box. Tom gave the bird some food and water. The bird felt safe and happy. As the days passed, the bird grew stronger. Tom knew it was time to let the bird go back to its family. He opened the box and the bird flew away. Tom felt happy that he could help the bird. The moral of the story is to always protect and care for those who need our help.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big tree with a small door on it. Tim opened the door and found a tiny, helpless frog inside. The frog looked very sad. Tim asked the frog, "Why are you so sad?" The frog said, "I am under a spell. I was a prince, but a bad witch turned me into a frog. I need help to break the spell." Tim wanted to help the frog, so he asked, "What can I do to help you?" The frog told Tim, "Find the witch and ask her to take off the spell." Tim went deeper into the woods to look for the witch. He saw a small house with smoke coming out of the top. He knocked on the door and the witch opened it. Tim asked the witch, "Can you take off the spell on the frog?" The witch just shrugged and said, "No, I will not help you." Tim felt very sad for the frog. He went back to the tree and told the frog the bad news. They both felt helpless, and the frog stayed a frog forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big party to celebrate a little girl's birthday. Many kids came to play and have fun. They all wore pretty clothes and big smiles. In the middle of the party, there was a stage. The stage was made of wood and was painted in bright colors. It looked very modern and fun. The kids were excited to sing and dance on the stage. One by one, the kids went up on the stage to perform. They sang happy songs and danced to the fun music. Everyone clapped and cheered. The little girl felt very special on her birthday. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big girl named Amy went to visit her new baby cousin, Tim. Tim was a tiny infant with cute little hands and feet. Amy was very excited to see him and play with him. When Amy got to Tim's house, she saw that his toy box was empty. "Oh no, Tim has no toys to play with," said Amy to her aunt. Her aunt replied, "Yes, we need to buy him some toys soon." Amy thought about all the fun toys she had at home and wanted to share them with Tim. Amy went back home and picked out her favorite toys to give to Tim. The next day, she went to visit Tim again with a big bag of toys. Tim's face lit up when he saw the toys, and they played together all day. From that day on, Amy and Tim became the best of friends. The moral of the story is that sharing can bring happiness to others and create strong friendships.
