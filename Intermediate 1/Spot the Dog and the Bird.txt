Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bird

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He was excited to explore the park. Spot saw a big tree with a hole in it. He did not recognize this tree, but he thought it was interesting. Spot wanted to find out what was in the hole. He barked at the hole, but nothing came out. Spot was sure that something was inside, so he waited and watched. After a while, a small bird flew out of the hole. Spot was surprised and happy to see the bird. The bird had been hiding in the hole all along. Spot knew he had found a new friend. They played together in the park and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Boo. Boo loved to scatter seeds in the garden. One sunny day, Boo went to the garden to see if the seeds had grown. He was very anxious to find out. Boo saw a big root in the ground. He thought it was a big carrot. Boo tried to pull it out, but it was stuck. He pulled and pulled, but it wouldn't come out. Boo was sad and tired. Then, something unexpected happened. The root started to wiggle and move! It was not a carrot, but a friendly worm named Wiggles! Wiggles said, "Hello, Boo! I was just playing a trick on you!" Boo laughed and they became best friends.

Amy and her mom were very excited because they were going to unpack a present. Amy's mom kneeled down and said, "Let's see what's in here!" She carefully opened the box, revealing a bright shiny contraption. Amy was puzzled. "What is it?" she asked. Her mom put a hand on her shoulder and said, "It's a special machine that puts out steam! We can use it to help us with our chores." Amy looked at it in confusion. "Is it normal to have a machine like this?" Her mom smiled. "Lots of people have one. Let's unpack it to take a closer look!" They unpacked all the pieces and set them up in the kitchen. Amy was enthralled by the machine that put out steam. She stared in amazement as the steam rose up around her. "That's normal," her mom said, ruffling through Amy's hair. "We can use it to make our lives a little bit easier!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big log on the ground. It was hidden behind some tall grass. Tim wanted to see what was behind the log. He tried to pull the log, but it was too heavy. He asked his friend, a small squirrel, to help him. "Please help me pull this log," Tim said. The squirrel agreed to help him. Together, they pulled the log and found a hidden treasure. It was a shiny, round ball. They were so happy to find it. They played with the ball all day and became the best of friends.

One day, a pale boy named Tim found a pen on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was a very special pen. It could draw things that would come to life! Tim was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look at my new pen! It can make things come to life!" Sam was excited and wanted to try it too. But when Sam tried to take the pen, Tim got upset. "No, it's my pen!" he said. Sam was sad and they started to argue. Just then, a big wind came and struck the pen from Tim's hand. The pen flew away and they couldn't find it. Tim and Sam stopped fighting and decided to look for the pen together. They searched and searched, but they couldn't find it. In the end, they learned that sharing and being friends was more important than a magic pen.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tim. He liked to paint pretty things like trees, flowers, and birds. Tim was a very good painter, but he was also a little bossy. He liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Tim needed a big brush to paint a big tree. But, he could not find his big brush. So, he asked his friend, Sam, if he could lend him a big brush. Sam said, "Yes, I can lend you my big brush, but please don't be bossy when I help you." Tim and Sam went to the park to paint the big tree. Tim tried not to be bossy, but it was hard for him. He said "please" and "thank you" when Sam gave him the big brush. They painted the big tree together, and it looked very pretty. In the end, Tim learned that it was better to be nice and not bossy when asking for help. He thanked Sam for lending him the big brush and for teaching him how to be a good friend. From that day on, Tim was not bossy anymore, and he and Sam painted many more pretty things together.
