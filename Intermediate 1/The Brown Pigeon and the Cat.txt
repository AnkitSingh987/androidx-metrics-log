Category: Intermediate
Title: The Brown Pigeon and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little brown pigeon. The pigeon lived in a big tree. It liked to fly and play with its friends. One day, the brown pigeon saw a big cat. The cat was looking for food. The pigeon did not recognize the cat as a bad thing. It thought the cat was a friend. The pigeon went to play with the cat. The cat was happy. But the cat was not a good friend. The cat ate the brown pigeon. The pigeon was gone, and its friends were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, winter came. It was very cold. The snow was white and soft. All the kids liked to play in the snow. They made snowmen and had fun. One day, two friends, Tom and Sam, wanted to make the biggest snowman. Tom wanted to make the snowman's head big. Sam wanted to make the snowman's head small. They did not agree. They were troubled because they could not make the snowman together. Then, Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's make two snowmen!" Sam liked the idea. They made two snowmen, one with a big head and one with a small head. They were not troubled anymore. They were happy and played in the snow all winter long.

Lily and Tom were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily had a pink bunny that she loved very much. Tom had a blue car that he liked to zoom around. "Look at my bunny, Tom!" Lily said. "She can hop and wiggle her nose. She is very soft and cute." "Look at my car, Lily!" Tom said. "It can go fast and make loud sounds. It is very shiny and cool." They showed each other their toys and smiled. They thought their toys were very interesting. But then, something strange happened. Lily's bunny and Tom's car started to glow. They sparkled and flashed with different colors. Lily and Tom were surprised and curious. They moved closer to their toys and touched them. "Wow, what is this?" Lily asked. "I don't know, but it's pretty!" Tom said. Suddenly, their toys vanished. They disappeared in a puff of smoke. Lily and Tom gasped and looked around. They could not see their toys anywhere. "Where did they go?" Lily cried. "Bring them back!" Tom shouted. They were very sad and scared. They wanted their toys to stay with them. They did not like this magic. They ran to their mom and dad, who were in the kitchen. They told them what happened and asked for help. "Don't worry, sweeties," their mom said. "It was just a trick. Your toys are not gone. They are in the closet. We wanted to show you something amazing." She opened the closet and took out their toys. They were still glowing, but they did not vanish. She gave them back to Lily and Tom, who hugged them tightly. "See, they are here. They are safe. You can play with them again," their dad said. "We are sorry if we scared you. We thought you would like to see some magic. We have a special wand that can make things disappear and reappear. Do you want to try it?" Lily and Tom looked at their parents and their toys. They were still a little upset, but they were also curious. They wondered what else the wand could do. They decided to give it a chance. "Okay, but be careful," Lily said. "Can I go first?" Tom asked. They took the wand and followed their parents to the living room. They hoped to see something interesting.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. It was not easy, but Tim tried very hard. When he got to the top, he found a little bird. The bird could whistle a pretty song. Tim was happy to meet the bird. "Hello, little bird! Can you teach me how to whistle?" Tim asked. The bird nodded and showed Tim how to whistle. They practiced together until Tim could whistle too. The bird then whispered a secret in Tim's ear. "Only you and I can whistle this special song," the bird said. Tim felt very special and promised to keep the secret. They spent the whole day whistling and playing in the park. When it was time to go home, Tim said goodbye to his new friend. He climbed down the tree and ran to his mom. Tim wanted to tell her about the bird and the secret song, but he remembered his promise. So, he just smiled and whistled the special song for her. His mom smiled back, and they went home together, whistling all the way.
