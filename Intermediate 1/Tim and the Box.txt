Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Box

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk on a trail. Tim liked the trail because it was near his home. The trail was dirty, but Tim did not care. He liked to stand on the trail and look at the trees and flowers. As Tim walked, he saw a big box on the trail. He was surprised because boxes do not belong on trails. Tim went closer to the box. He wanted to see what was inside. When Tim opened the box, he found a big, shiny ball. The ball was not dirty like the trail. Tim thought the ball was magic. He picked up the ball and took it home. From that day on, Tim and the ball were best friends. The ball made Tim's walks on the dirty trail even more fun.

Once upon a time there was a farmer named John. Every day he would perform hard work on his farm. One day John was surprised to find the farm all messy. He went looking for the cause and found a small boy playing with the animals. "What are you doing here?" asked John gruffly. The boy looked up. "Me and the animals were just playing!" he replied. John shook his head. "You cannot play like this. You can't be so messy!" The boy looked down sadly. John realized he had been too harsh and softened his voice. "You should be more mindful. Animals are not toys and farms are important. Playing can be fun but only if it's done in the right way." The boy nodded and John showed him how to care for the animals. Together they cleaned up the farm and made it tidy. The moral of the story is that it's important to be mindful and respect the things around us. Even when you are playing, you should make sure it doesn't affect anyone else.

Bill was excited to go to the pizza store. He saw slices of cheese, pepperoni, and pineapple neatly lay out on a counter. The smell of pizza was in the air. "What kind of pizza do you want, Bill?" his mom asked. "Cheese pizza ready for me?" Bill said with a smile. "Yes! Here you go," said Mom, as she lay a piece of cheese pizza on a plate for him. Bill felt very happy and took a big bite of the pizza. "Mmm, so yummy!" he exclaimed. Mom was glad too. "You look ready for more pizza, Bill. Let's go get some more!"

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, while they were playing, Bobby saw a wire up high. He thought it would be fun to sit and rest on the wire. Bobby flew up and sat on the wire. He called to his friend, "Hey, come and rest with me on this wire!" His friend flew up and sat next to him. They both rested and talked about their day. Soon, more birds came and sat on the wire. The wire became very heavy. The birds knew they should not all sit on the wire at once. They took turns and shared the wire, so everyone could rest without making it too heavy. The moral of the story is to share and take turns, so everyone can enjoy and be safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to cut some beef for dinner. Tim felt very big and important, and he said, "Yes, Mom! I can do it!" Tim tried to cut the beef, but the knife was weak. He tried and tried, but the beef just would not cut. Tim was sad and said, "Mom, the knife is weak. I can't cut the beef." Just then, a friendly dog named Spot came into the kitchen. Spot saw Tim feeling sad and wanted to help. So, Spot took the beef in his mouth and began to tear it into small pieces. Tim was very surprised and happy. "Thank you, Spot!" said Tim. "You helped me when the knife was weak." Tim's mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim and Spot! Now we can cook the beef for dinner." And they all had a yummy dinner together.
