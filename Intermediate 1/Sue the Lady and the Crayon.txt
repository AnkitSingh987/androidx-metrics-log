Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Lady and the Crayon

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady named Sue. She loved to draw with her big box of crayons. Sue had many crayons in all colors. Her favorite crayon was the red one. One day, Sue met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw Sue's crayons and asked, "Can I draw with you?" Sue said, "Yes, of course! Let's draw together." They sat down and started to draw. Tim picked up the red crayon and said, "This crayon is sharp! It will make good lines." As they drew, they talked and laughed. They drew a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. They used the sharp red crayon to draw a pretty flower. Tim and Sue were happy with their drawing. In the end, Sue and Tim became good friends. They loved to draw together with their crayons. And they always made sure to keep their crayons sharp.

Once upon a time, there was a farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a dog, a cat, a cow, and a pig. They were all friends. This was not an ordinary farm because the animals could talk to each other. One day, the dog said, "Listen, friends. I have a big idea. Let's play a game." The cat, the cow, and the pig all agreed. They wanted to have fun on the farm. The game was called "Hide and Seek." The dog closed his eyes and counted. The cat, the cow, and the pig ran to hide. The cat hid behind a tree. The cow hid behind a big bush. The pig hid in a pile of hay. When the dog was done counting, he looked for his friends. He found the cat first. Then, he found the cow. Last, he found the pig. They all laughed and had fun playing the game. After the game, they all sat together and talked. They were happy to be friends on their not ordinary farm. They knew they would have many more fun days together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very sad. One day, she found a white cloth with stitches on it and a dry glue stick. She knew that this was special and so she started to use the glue to stick the stitches back on the cloth. She worked very hard and was very careful. When she was finished, the cloth was good again! She felt so proud of herself that she decided to make more clothes with the cloth and the glue. Every day, she used her glue to make more and more things. She even made dresses and bags with it! Her friends were always so happy when they saw what she made. Eventually, she became great at her craft by using the dry glue and stitching. Whenever she used the dry glue, the stitches never moved. She was so happy with what she made that she even shared it with everyone she knew. Her clothes were so good, everyone wanted them!

Timmy and Molly are playing with their toy cars in the living room. They have a lot of fun making noises and racing each other. But then Timmy sees a shiny red car that Molly has. He wants it very much. "Can I play with your red car, Molly?" Timmy asks. "No, Timmy, this is my car. You have your own cars," Molly says. "But I like your car more. It is so shiny and fast. Please, Molly, let me have it," Timmy begs. "No, Timmy, this is my original car. My grandma gave it to me. You can't have it," Molly says. Timmy gets angry and grabs the red car from Molly's hand. Molly cries and tries to get it back. They pull and tug and shout. They forget to be nice to each other. Suddenly, they hear a loud bark. It is Rex, their big dog. He sees the red car and thinks it is a ball. He runs to them and snatches the car with his paw. He runs away with it, wagging his tail. "Hey, Rex, give me back my car!" Molly yells. "Rex, drop it, drop it!" Timmy commands. But Rex does not listen. He thinks they are playing with him. He runs around the house, chewing and tossing the red car. He breaks it into pieces. Timmy and Molly stop fighting and look at each other. They feel sad and sorry. They realize they were mean and selfish. They hug and say sorry to each other. "Can we share our cars, Molly?" Timmy asks. "Of course, Timmy. We can share and play together," Molly says. They go to find Rex and try to get the red car back. But it is too late. The car is ruined. They pat Rex and tell him he is a good dog. But they wish they did not forget to be nice to each other.
