Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Mouse and the Desk

Once there was a little girl who loved to play. One day, she was playing hide-and-seek and she found a funny desk in the corner of the room. She had never seen it before. She called out to her friend, "Come look at this desk! I think it's so funny!" Her friend ran over and they started searching the desk drawers and shelves. They found some funny and interesting things. All of a sudden, a loud scream came out of the desk. The girls were so scared they both screamed and ran out of the room. When they got back into the room, they saw that it was only a little mouse! The mouse had been sleeping in the desk and was just as scared as the girls! The girls both laughed and it wasn't so scary anymore. They decided to leave the mouse alone and never told anyone about it!

Once upon a time, there was a girl who liked to match things. She saw lots of images, some easy to match and some hard. She wanted to match the hard images. She tried and tried, but it was hard. Then one day, she found someone to help her. His name was Mark. Mark was very good at matching the hard images. He looked at one and was able to match it quickly. The girl was so impressed. She asked him to keep helping her. Mark was happy to do that because he enjoyed helping people. Soon he and the girl could match all the hard images. They both smiled and had fun. After that, the girl was a much better matcher. She now knew how to match the hard images all by herself. She was so proud of herself, and she thanked Mark for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn fish named Finn. Finn loved to swim all day long. One day, Finn saw a big rock in the water. He wanted to swim around it, but his mom said, "No, Finn. It's time to return home." Finn did not listen to his mom. He swam around the rock and saw a new friend. The new friend was a turtle named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Finn! Let's swim together." Finn and Tim swam and played. But soon, it was getting dark. Finn remembered his mom's words and knew he had to return home. He said goodbye to Tim and swam back. When Finn returned, his mom was happy to see him. She hugged him and said, "I'm glad you're back, Finn. Remember to always listen to me." Finn learned his lesson and promised to listen to his mom from then on.

One day, a big dog named Max found a brush in the park. He picked it up and ran home. He wanted to show his mom the new toy he found. Max was very happy. At home, Max's little sister, Bella, saw the brush and wanted to play with it too. Max did not want to share. Bella was sad and impatient. She started to cry. Max sighed and felt bad for making Bella cry. Just then, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird saw the brush and took it in its beak. Max and Bella were very surprised. The bird flew away with the brush. Max and Bella stopped fighting and laughed. They realized it was more fun to play together than to fight over a toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Nick. Nick was very lucky to have a big mummy who loved him very much. One day, Nick's mummy was in the kitchen making a special dish for lunch. She called to Nick and told him to come in. Nick smiled and skipped into the kitchen happily. Mummy said, "Nick, would you like a spoonful of this dish?" Nick looked at the dish and he refused. Mummy asked again, "Please try just a small spoonful, Nick. It's very yummy!" Nick refused again. He shook his head and said, "No, Mummy. It looks too hot!" Mummy laughed and said, "That's okay, Nick. You're very lucky because you don't have to try the dish if you don't want to. Nick smiled and said, "Yes, I'm very lucky, Mummy!"
