Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a small house near the park. Sue and grandma had a lot of fun together. One sunny day, Sue found a pair of sunglasses on the ground. She thought they were important. Sue put on the sunglasses and showed them to her grandma. "Look, grandma! I found important sunglasses!" Sue said. Grandma smiled and said, "Yes, they are important. They help keep the sun out of your eyes." Sue and grandma played in the park all day. Sue wore her important sunglasses and felt very happy.

One day, a small girl named Lily was playing in her room. She was nervous because her mom told her not to make a mess. Lily wanted to play with her toys, but she didn't want to make her mom upset. Lily had an idea. She picked up her toy phone and called her friend, Teddy. "Teddy," she said, "I want to play, but I don't want to make a mess. Can you help me?" Teddy, who was a very smart bear, said, "Yes, Lily. I have an idea. Let's play a game where we pick up the toys as we play. That way we won't make a mess." Lily liked Teddy's idea, so they started to play. As they played, they made sure to put the toys back where they found them. Lily was very happy and she gave Teddy a big hug. Her mom came into the room and saw that it was clean. She smiled and said, "Good job, Lily. I'm proud of you for not making a mess." Lily's lip yielded a big smile, and she knew that she could have fun while still listening to her mom.

Ben and Jen are friends. They like to play in the park. They like to run and jump and slide. They also like to draw with chalk on the ground. They draw big zigzags and small zigzags and zigzags of many colors. One day, Jen is sick. She cannot go to the park with Ben. Ben is sad. He misses Jen. He goes to the park alone. He sees the zigzags they drew yesterday. He wants to draw more zigzags with Jen. He says to himself, "I wish Jen was here." He sees a man sitting on a bench. The man is bald. He has no hair on his head. He looks lonely. Ben walks up to him and says, "Hello, sir. Do you want to draw with me?" The man smiles. He says, "Sure, kid. I would love to draw with you. What do you want to draw?" Ben says, "Zigzags. They are my favorite. And Jen's favorite too. She is my friend. But she is sick today. I miss her." The man says, "I'm sorry to hear that. I miss someone too. She was my wife. But she is gone now. She had hair like the sun. She loved zigzags too." Ben and the man draw zigzags together. They use all the colors of the chalk. They make the ground look like a rainbow. They talk and laugh and have fun. They feel less sad and more happy. They finish their drawing. They look at it and smile. They say, "It's beautiful. It's for Jen and for her." They hug each other. They say, "Thank you. You are my friend." They go home. They hope to see each other again. They hope to see Jen and her again. They do not forget the zigzags. They do not forget the love.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She went to the mall with her mom. Lily was so happy to see many things at the mall. At the mall, Lily saw a big, upset dog. The dog looked very sad. Lily asked her mom, "Can I hug the dog, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lily went to the dog and gave it a big hug. The dog felt better and was not upset anymore. The dog wagged its tail and licked Lily's face. They became good friends and played together at the mall.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous candy store. In this store, there were many lollipops. The lollipops were all colors and flavors. The children in the town loved to visit the store. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. She looked at all the lollipops and wanted to gather them all. She asked her mom, "Can I have all the lollipops, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "You can choose one, Lily." Lily picked a big, red lollipop. She was very happy. She shared her lollipop with her friends. They all loved the famous lollipop from the candy store. And they all lived happily ever after.
