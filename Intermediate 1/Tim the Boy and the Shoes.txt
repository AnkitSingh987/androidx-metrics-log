Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a silly boy who loved to play all day. One day, Tim had to take an exam at school. He was very scared because he did not know what to do. Tim's mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you get ready." She helped Tim tie his shoes and put on his favorite red hat. Tim felt a little better and was ready to go to school. At school, Tim did his best on the exam. He remembered what his mom said and tried not to be silly. When Tim came home, he was happy. He had passed the exam and his mom was proud of him. They celebrated with a big hug and a fun game of tag.

---

One day, a smelly cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to hear the birds sing. He also liked to feel the weather. Today, the weather was sunny and warm. Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max was not nice. He liked to chase cats. Tom wanted to be friends, but Max did not. Max barked loud at Tom. Tom was scared and ran away. Tom hid in a tree. He heard Max bark far away. The weather got cold and it started to rain. Tom was sad and wet. Then, a nice girl found Tom. She took him home and gave him a warm bath. Tom was happy and safe.

One day, a boy named Tim had a goal. He wanted to replace his old toy car with a new one. So, he went to the store with his mom. At the store, Tim saw a useful toy car. It was big and had many colors. Tim told his mom, "I want this car, please!" His mom said, "Okay, let's buy it." They went home and Tim played with his new toy car. He was very happy because he reached his goal. His old toy car was now replaced with a new, useful one. Tim and his mom had a fun day together.

---

One day, a cool bird was on a hanger. The bird liked to rock back and forth. It was fun for the bird to play on the hanger. A cat saw the cool bird on the hanger. The cat wanted to play with the bird. The cat tried to rock on the hanger too, but it was too big. The cool bird saw the cat and wanted to help. The bird found a big stick for the cat to play with. Now, the bird and the cat could both rock and play together. They were happy friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because his mom was teaching him how to wash his hands. She showed him the faucet and told him to turn it on. Tim liked the sound of the water coming out of the faucet. One day, Tim saw two cups stuck together. He wanted to separate them, but he didn't know how. He remembered his mom teaching him about the faucet, so he thought maybe water could help. Tim turned on the faucet and filled one cup with water. The cups became easy to separate! Tim was so happy that he could separate the cups. He ran to his mom to show her what he did. His mom was proud of him and said, "Good job, Tim! You learned that water can help solve problems." Tim learned that day that sometimes, thinking about what you know can help you find an answer.

Tom and Anna liked to play in the big house. The big house had many rooms and halls and stairs. Some rooms had toys and books and pictures. Some rooms had beds and chairs and tables. Some rooms had things that Tom and Anna did not know. One day, Tom and Anna found a door that they had never seen before. The door was small and dark and had a lock. Tom and Anna wanted to see what was behind the door. They looked for a key, but they could not find one. They tried to push the door, but it did not move. "Maybe there is a secret way to open the door," Tom said. "Maybe there is a magic word," Anna said. They tried to say different words, but nothing happened. They were about to give up, when they heard a voice. "Who are you and what do you want?" the voice said. Tom and Anna were scared. They did not know who was talking. They looked around, but they could not see anyone. "We are Tom and Anna. We are playing in the big house. We want to see what is behind the door," Tom said. "Behind the door is a chamber. A chamber is a special room. Only famous people can go in the chamber. You are not famous. You cannot go in the chamber," the voice said. "Why not? What is in the chamber?" Anna asked. "That is a secret. You have to be famous to know the secret. If you want to be famous, you have to do something great. Something that everyone will remember. Something that will include your name in a book or a song or a picture," the voice said. Tom and Anna thought for a moment. They wanted to be famous. They wanted to know the secret. They wanted to see the chamber. "What can we do to be famous?" they asked. The voice did not answer. It was silent. Tom and Anna looked at each other. They had an idea. They ran to the nearest room. They found some paper and crayons. They started to draw and write. They made a story. A story about a boy and a girl who found a chamber in a big house. A story that included their names and the voice and the secret. A story that they hoped would make them famous.
