Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thin Cat and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. He loved to dream about playing with a laser. One day, Tom saw a little girl with a laser toy. She was pointing it at the ground, and the laser made a red dot. Tom got very excited. "Can I play too?" Tom asked the little girl. The little girl smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" Tom chased the red dot all around. He was very happy. The little girl laughed as she watched Tom play. They had so much fun with the laser. Tom's dream had come true. And from that day, Tom and the little girl became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog. He loved to hop all day. One day, it was icy outside. The frog put on his warm hat and went out to play. As he hopped, he saw a big umbrella. It was red and blue. The frog thought it was pretty. He wanted to play with the umbrella. He hopped under the umbrella and started to spin it. The umbrella went round and round. The frog had so much fun. He hopped and played until it was time to go home. The end.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. One day, they both want to play with the big red car. They pull and tug on the car. The car makes a loud noise. It splits in two! Lily and Ben are sad and angry. They each have half of the car, but it does not work. They do not want to share. They do not want to say sorry. They sit on opposite sides of the room. They do not look at each other. Mom comes in and sees the broken car and the unhappy twins. She is not happy either. She asks them what happened. Lily and Ben tell her their story. Mom says they were not kind to each other or to the car. She says they need to learn to take turns and to use their words. She says they need to fix the car and make up. Lily and Ben feel sorry. They do not like to make Mom sad. They do not like to be mad at each other. They say sorry to Mom and to each other. They hug and kiss. They work together to fix the car. They use tape and glue and stickers. They make the car look new. Mom is proud of them. She says they did a good job. She says they can play with the car, but they have to share. She gives them a pass to go to the park. They are happy. They take the car to the park. They take turns to drive it. They have fun. They are kind to each other. They are twins. They are friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. The boat went to a place called a port. At the port, people would relax and have fun. The boat had a captain who was very compassionate. The captain liked to help people and make them happy. One day, the captain saw a little girl who was sad. The girl had lost her toy. The compassionate captain wanted to help her find it. They looked everywhere in the port. They asked people if they had seen the toy. But, they could not find it. The sun started to go down, and it was getting dark. The captain and the girl were still looking for the toy. They were very tired and wanted to relax. But they could not relax because the toy was still lost. In the end, they never found the toy, and the girl stayed sad. The captain was sad too because he could not make the girl happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny. One day, the bunny heard a strange noise outside of his burrow. He woke up to find a mother bear and her cub playing nearby. The bunny was curious and hopped closer for a better view. As he hopped closer, he spotted a high tree and an annoying mosquito buzzing around it. The bear mama noticed the bunny and said, “That mosquito is very annoying. Such a high tree and all we can do is watch it.” The cub asked, “Why don’t you just swat it?” Quickly, the bear mama replied, “Sometimes, we have to accept the things we cannot change, like that mosquito and its annoying noise.” The little bunny understood the lesson. He hopped away and realized that it is important to accept things that we can’t change. Moral of the story: Accept the things you cannot change.
