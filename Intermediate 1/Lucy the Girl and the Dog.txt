Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. They brought bread to feed the ducks. It was a wet day, so Lucy wore her rain boots. She was worried that the ducks would not come out in the rain. When they got to the park, Lucy saw the ducks near the pond. "Mom, can I feed the ducks now?" she asked. Her mom said, "Yes, Lucy. Be careful not to get too close to the water." Lucy threw the bread to the ducks and they came closer to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog ran up to Lucy and the ducks. The dog wanted to play with the ducks too! The ducks got scared and flew away. Lucy was sad, but the dog made her smile. She played with the dog in the rain and forgot about the ducks. In the end, Lucy had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a pink animal. This animal was very good at cleaning. It loved to clean all day. It cleaned its home, the trees, and the ground. All the other animals liked the pink animal because it made their forest look nice. One day, the pink animal found a big mess near a pond. It wanted to clean it up. The mess had many things, like leaves, sticks, and mud. The pink animal worked very hard to clean the mess. It pushed the leaves and sticks away with its nose. It also wiped the mud away with its tail. But, while the pink animal was cleaning, it did not see a big hole in the ground. The pink animal fell into the hole. It tried to get out, but the hole was too deep. The pink animal was stuck in the hole. It could not clean anymore. The other animals could not help the pink animal. They were very sad. The pink animal stayed in the hole, and the forest was not clean anymore.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They have a ball and a kite. They have fun. One day, a big dog comes to the park. The dog is not nice. The dog runs and barks. The dog bites the ball and the kite. Lily and Ben are scared. They run away. The dog leaves. Lily and Ben go back to the park. They see their ball and their kite. The ball has a hole. The kite has a tear. Lily and Ben are sad. Lily says, "What can we do? Our ball and our kite are broken." Ben says, "I have an idea. We can unite them. We can use a patch. We can make a new toy." Lily says, "How?" Ben says, "Look. We can put the patch over the hole in the ball. We can tie the kite to the ball. We can throw the ball and the kite in the air. We can watch them fly." Lily says, "That sounds fun. Let's try it." They do what Ben says. They unite the ball and the kite with a patch. They throw the ball and the kite in the air. They watch them fly. They laugh and clap. Lily and Ben are happy. They have a new toy. It is not normal. But it is fun. They play in the park. They are friends.

One day, a furry bear named Bobo went to the woods. He saw a big blueberry bush. Bobo was very happy. He loved to eat blueberries. A bird flew by and said, "Bobo, don't eat those blueberries! They are not good for you!" But Bobo did not listen. He ignored the bird and ate the blueberries. Suddenly, the blueberries made Bobo feel funny. He turned into a little blueberry bear! The bird laughed and said, "I told you, Bobo! Now you must find the magic flower to change back." Bobo looked for the magic flower. He found it and ate it. He turned back into a big furry bear. Bobo said, "Thank you, bird. I will not ignore you again." From that day on, Bobo always listened to his friends.
