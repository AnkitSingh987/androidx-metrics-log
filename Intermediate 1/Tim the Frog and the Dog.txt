Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Frog and the Dog

One day, a little frog named Tim was sitting on a rough porch. He liked to jump high and far. He saw his friend, a big dog named Max, walking by. Tim called out, "Max, come play with me!" Max came over and said, "I like to jump too!" So, they decided to have a jumping race. They lined up at the edge of the porch, ready to jump as far as they could. Tim jumped first. He jumped high and far, landing in the soft grass. Max jumped next. He jumped even higher and farther than Tim! They both laughed and had so much fun. They played and jumped together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends. One day, he saw a big bush with pretty flowers. He ran to the bush to smell the flowers. As he got closer, he heard a loud growl. Ben was scared! Out of the bush came a fierce lion. The lion said, "I am Leo the lion, and this is my bush!" Ben was shaking, but he said, "I just wanted to smell the flowers. Can we be friends?" Leo thought for a moment and then smiled. "Yes, we can be friends!" he said. Just then, a big wind came and blew the bush away. Underneath the bush, they found a hidden treasure chest! Ben and Leo opened the chest, and inside was a shiny gold trophy. They were so happy and began to applaud. The other animals in the forest heard the noise and came to see what was happening. They saw Ben and Leo with the trophy and started to applaud too. From that day on, Ben and Leo were the best of friends, and they shared the trophy with everyone in the forest.

Freddy the frog was out walking one day. He felt fat and uncomfortable, wishing he could lose some weight. As he walked, he saw a beautiful waterfall. Freddy was so excited! He hopped to the top of the waterfall and was about to jump in when he slipped and fell in. Frightened, Freddy tried to swim away but the waterfall was too strong. He tried and tried but he couldn't get out. Suddenly, he heard a voice. "Don't worry!" shouted a fish. "Let me help you!" And so, with the help of the fish, Freddy was able to swim away from the waterfall and lose his extra fat. Freddy was so thankful and happy he wanted to give the fish a big hug but, of course, the fish declined. He just smiled and waved as Freddy hopped away.

One day, a boy named Tom went outside to play a sport. He liked to play with a ball. It was a sunny, dry day. Tom saw his friend Sue and asked, "Sue, do you want to play ball with me?" Sue said, "Yes! I want to play too!" They began to play catch with the ball. They threw the ball back and forth. Tom and Sue were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom threw the ball high in the air, but it did not come down. Sue looked up and saw that the ball was stuck in a tree. Tom tried to jump and get it, but he could not reach. Tom had an idea. He found a stick and tried to poke the ball out of the tree. He poked and poked, but the ball would not drop. Just then, a big bird flew by and grabbed the ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball right into Tom's hands. Tom and Sue were so happy. They thanked the bird and went back to playing their sport.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible snake named Sally. Sally loved to move and play in the grass. One day, she saw a big rock and decided to move around it. As Sally moved around the rock, she met a small bug named Benny. Benny said, "Hi, Sally! I like how you move. Can you teach me how to be flexible like you?" Sally replied, "Of course, Benny! I will show you how to move like me." They spent a whole year playing together in the grass, moving and being flexible. They became best friends and had lots of fun.
