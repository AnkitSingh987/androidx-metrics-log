Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Loyal Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would run, jump, and have fun all day long. One day, Tim and Buddy were playing near a big tree. Buddy saw a ball and wanted to catch it. When he tried to catch the ball, he took a wrong turn and got lost. Tim was very sad and missed his friend. That night, Tim prayed for Buddy to come back home. The next day, Buddy heard Tim calling his name. He followed the sound and found his way back to Tim. They were so happy to see each other again. From that day on, they were always careful when they played and never took the wrong turn again.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat. This cat loved to eat cheese. Every day, it would find cheese and eat it all up. One day, the cat found a big piece of cheese. It was so happy! But when it took a bite, the cheese tasted bad. The cheese had rot in it. The cat did not like the taste, so it left the cheese alone. Later, a mouse came by and saw the cheese. The mouse did not care about the rot. It ate the cheese and was very happy. The cat saw this and was surprised. The cat learned that sometimes, what it does not like can make others happy.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful big tree. The tree was very old and had many big leaves. The tree was in a park where kids came to play every day. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw the old tree and wanted to give it a hug. She ran to the tree and wrapped her arms around it. The tree was so big that she could not hug it all. As Lily hugged the tree, she felt happy. The tree seemed to give her a big smile with its leaves. She knew that the beautiful old tree was her new friend. From that day on, Lily and the old tree played together every time she came to the park.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to paint. She took out her paint box and found a big paper. She thought of what to paint. She saw a jellyfish at the beach last week, so she decided to paint a jellyfish. Sue painted a big, round jellyfish with long, wavy legs. She was happy with her painting. But then, something unexpected happened. The jellyfish in her painting started to move! It jumped off the paper and onto the floor. Sue was upset because she didn't know what to do. Sue's cat, Tom, saw the jellyfish on the floor. He thought it was a toy and started to play with it. The jellyfish didn't like being played with and jumped back onto the paper. Sue was happy again because her painting was back to normal. She learned that her paint box was magic and could make her paintings come to life!

One day, a little girl named Lily went with her mom to the bus stop. They needed to ride the bus to go to the park. Lily was very excited because she loved to play on the swings and slide. At the bus stop, Lily saw a big, normal bus coming down the street. She gazed at the bus as it got closer and closer. The bus stopped right in front of them, and the doors opened wide. Lily and her mom got on the bus and found a seat. They rode the bus for a little while and then got off at the park. Lily ran to the swings and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, they rode the bus back. Lily had a great day at the park with her mom.

Lila and Ben like to play with their club. Their club is a big stick that they found in the park. They pretend that their club is a sword or a wand or a microphone. They have a lot of fun with their club. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is brown and furry and has a long tail. The dog looks friendly, but Lila and Ben are scared of dogs. They hide behind a tree and hold their club. The dog comes closer and sniffs the air. He smells Lila and Ben and their club. He barks and wags his tail. He wants to play with them. He thinks that their club is a toy. Lila and Ben are very quiet. They hope that the dog will go away. But the dog does not go away. He jumps over the tree and lands in front of them. He licks their faces and tries to grab their club. Lila and Ben scream and drop their club. They run away from the dog. They think that the dog is mean and scary. They are very silly. The dog picks up their club and shakes it. He is very happy. He thinks that Lila and Ben are foolish. They gave him a nice toy. He nods and wags his tail. He runs after them to play some more.
