Category: Intermediate
Title: Rose the Girl and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Rose. Rose liked to dance, and every night she would put on her pretty dress and do her dance. One night, Rose went outside to dance under the stars. The moon was bright, and the stars twinkled. Rose began to dance and spin around. Soon, Rose saw a hard piece of wood on the ground. She picked it up and started to use it like a music box. She danced harder and faster, her dress swirling around her. Rose was happy and danced until she was tired. Then, she put the hard piece of wood in her pocket and went back inside. Rose danced every night, and the hard piece of wood became like a special friend to her. They would dance together under the stars or inside near the fireplace. Rose and her hard piece of wood were happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. The hunter loved to gaze at the sky. He looked at the clouds and the birds. One day, he saw a big, upset bird. The bird was sad and crying. The hunter said, "Why are you upset, bird?" The bird said, "I lost my friend. I can't find him anywhere." The hunter wanted to help the bird. He said, "I will help you find your friend." The hunter and the bird looked for the friend. They looked high and low. They looked near and far. At last, they found the friend. The birds were happy. The hunter was happy too. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but sometimes he did things that made others feel sorry. One day, Tim took his friend's toy without asking. His friend was sad and cried. That night, Tim could not sleep. He felt bad for what he did. He knew he had to make it right. The next day, Tim went to his friend's house. He said, "I am sorry for taking your toy. I want to pay for it with my own money." Tim gave his friend some money to show he was really sorry. His friend smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I forgive you." From that day on, Tim learned to always ask before taking something and to say sorry if he made a mistake. The town was happy, and everyone learned that saying sorry and making things right is important.

Lily and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to run and jump and make noise. They see a big lawn with green grass and yellow flowers. They want to eat the flowers. They think they are yummy. They run to the lawn and pick some flowers. They put them in their mouths and chew. They make funny faces. The flowers are not yummy. They are bitter and sour. They spit them out and say "yuck!" Mom sees them from the window. She is angry. She comes out and says "Stop! You cannot eat the lawn! The lawn is not food! The lawn is for looking and smelling and touching. The lawn is not for eating!" Lily and Ben are scared. They drop the flowers and say "sorry". They do not want to make Mom angry. They do not want to eat the lawn anymore. They want to eat something else. Mom takes them inside and gives them some cookies and milk. They are sweet and good. They eat them and say "thank you". They are happy. They hug Mom and say "we love you". Mom smiles and says "I love you too". They are quiet and nice. They do not eat the lawn anymore.

Once upon a time, in a calm pond, there were two fish. One fish was big, and the other fish was small. They were very good friends. They liked to swim and play together every day. One day, the big fish found a toy in the pond. It was a pretty, shiny toy. The big fish wanted to play with the toy, but the small fish wanted to play with it too. The big fish did not want to share the toy at first. Then, the small fish said, "Please, can we share the toy? We can have more fun if we play together." The big fish thought about it and decided to share the toy with the small fish. They played with the toy all day and had lots of fun. The two fish were very happy because they shared and played together.
