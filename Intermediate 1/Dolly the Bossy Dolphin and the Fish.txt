Category: Intermediate
Title: Dolly the Bossy Dolphin and the Fish

One day, a bossy dolphin named Dolly lived in the sea. She liked to talk a lot. She would tell all the fish what to do. The fish did not like it. They wanted to play and have fun, but Dolly always wanted to be in charge. One day, Dolly saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. She thought it was going to rain. She told all the fish to hide in a cave. But the fish did not want to listen. They wanted to keep playing. Dolly talked and talked, but they did not listen. Then, the rain came down. It was a big storm! All the fish got scared and swam to the cave. Dolly was right. The fish were glad that Dolly talked to them. After the storm, they played together and had fun. Dolly was still bossy, but the fish knew she cared.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play. One day, Spot saw a beautiful sunset. He wanted to show his friend, Kitty. Spot ran to Kitty and said, "Kitty, imagine a big, pretty sunset. Let's go see it!" Kitty nodded and they ran to the hill to see the sunset. At the hill, Spot and Kitty saw a big ball. They both wanted to play with it. "I want the ball!" said Spot. "No, I want it!" said Kitty. They pushed and pulled, but they could not agree. Then, Spot had an idea. "Let's share the ball and watch the sunset together!" Kitty agreed, and they played with the ball together. As they played, they watched the beautiful sunset and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a successful helper. He liked to help his mom at the shop. The shop had many toys, clothes, and yummy treats. One day, it was very cold outside. Tim wore a warm coat, but he still felt a shiver. He saw a little dog outside the shop. The dog was shivering too. Tim felt sad for the dog, so he went to talk to him. "Hi, dog! You look cold. Do you want to come into the shop with me?" Tim asked. The dog wagged its tail and followed Tim inside. In the shop, the dog stopped shivering. Tim's mom gave the dog a warm blanket and some food. The dog was happy, and Tim felt good for helping his new friend.

Bee and Ant were friends. They liked to play and help each other. Bee was reliable. She always shared her honey with Ant. Ant was reliable. He always helped Bee with her work. One day, they saw a big flower. It had many colors and smelled good. Bee and Ant wanted to get some nectar from the flower. They flew and crawled to the flower. They did not see the spider. The spider was hiding in the flower. He was hungry and mean. He wanted to catch Bee and Ant. He waited for them to come closer. Then he jumped out and threw his web. Bee and Ant were scared. They tried to escape, but the web was sticky and strong. The spider laughed and said, "You are my lunch today. I will eat you both. You are so foolish to come to my flower." Bee and Ant cried. They wished they had stayed near their hive. They wished they had listened to their friends who warned them about the spider. They wished they could vanish and be safe. But they could not. The spider wrapped them in his web and took them to his home. He ate them and was happy. Bee and Ant were gone. They were not reliable anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was very enthusiastic about learning how to measure. She had a small ruler that she used to measure everything. One day, she went to the park with her friend, Tom. At the park, Sue and Tom saw a big tree. Sue said, "Let's measure the tree!" They started to measure the tree with Sue's ruler, but it was too small. They needed a bigger measure. They thought about what to do. Suddenly, a friendly squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel said, "I can help you measure the tree!" Sue and Tom were surprised because they didn't know squirrels could talk. They were happy to have help. The squirrel used its tail to measure the tree. It was a fun and unexpected day at the park.
