Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. She had a favorite toy, a tiny car. She would race it with her other toys. Sue was competitive and wanted her car to be the best. One day, Sue saw her car was dirty. There was dust on it. She asked her mom, "Mom, how do I clean my car?" Mom replied, "Use a cloth, Sue. It will take the dust away." Sue was happy with her mom's reply. She got a cloth and cleaned her car. Now, Sue's car was clean and shiny. She went back to racing her toys. The car was fast and won every race. Sue was proud of her car. She knew that keeping it clean made it the best. Sue had fun with her friends and they all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a small glow bug. The glow bug was very worried. He could not find his glow. He looked everywhere for it. One day, he met a kind bird. The bird saw the worried glow bug and wanted to help. They looked for the glow together. They looked high in the trees and low on the ground. But they could not find it. Then, they met a wise old turtle. The turtle knew where the glow was. He told the glow bug to close his eyes and think of happy things. The glow bug did as the turtle said. When he opened his eyes, he was glowing again. The glow bug, the bird, and the turtle were all happy. They had solved the problem and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very generous. He always shared his toys and snacks with his friends. One day, Tim found a big bag near the park. He was curious about what was inside. Tim opened the bag and saw many colorful balls. He wanted to play with them, but he also knew he needed to behave. Instead of taking the balls, Tim decided to find the owner of the bag. He asked his friends and neighbors, but no one knew whose bag it was. Then, something unexpected happened. A little dog came running to Tim. The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim realized that the bag belonged to the dog! The dog had been collecting the balls to share with other dogs in the park. Tim was happy that he behaved well and helped the dog. From that day on, Tim and the dog became best friends, and they played together with the colorful balls every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big shark. The shark liked to swim in the sea. One day, the shark saw a fish. The fish was very small. The shark thought the fish looked disgusting. The shark did not want to eat the disgusting fish. But the shark was hungry. The shark dared to eat the fish. The shark swam fast to catch the fish. The fish was scared and swam away. As the shark got closer, something unexpected happened. The small fish grew bigger and bigger. The shark was surprised. The fish was not a fish; it was a big, friendly whale. The shark was happy to have a new friend. They swam together and played in the sea.

One day, a boy named Tim found a weird toy. The toy looked like a rocket. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play with the rocket. He took it outside to show his friends. When Tim showed his friends, they all wanted to play with the rocket too. But they did not listen to Tim. They took the rocket and ran away. Tim was sad and angry. He wanted his rocket back. So, Tim thought of a plan. He waited for his friends to leave the rocket alone. When they did, Tim ran fast and took the rocket back. He told his friends to listen and share. They said sorry and played together with the rocket. They all had fun and learned to share.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jack. He wanted to go outside and play, but his mom said he had to stay inside. So Jack decided to use the couch to sleep. Jack was very slow. He moved to the couch and creased up until he was feeling cosy and warm. He closed his eyes and started to drift off. Suddenly he heard a loud noise. He opened his eyes and saw a tall man. "What are you doing here?" Asked the man. "I'm just sleeping," Jack replied. The man didn't believe him. He grabbed Jack by the arm and started shaking him. "Tell me the truth! What are you really doing here?" The man shouted. Jack was scared, so he started to cry. The man became angry and threw Jack against the couch. Jack hit his head and fell unconscious. The man left and Jack never woke up. He had a bad ending.
