Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big rock and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he saw a pretty bird in the sky. Tim wanted to say "hi" to the bird, so he tried to climb higher. But then, he started to slip. He was scared and called out for help. A compassionate dog named Max heard Tim's call. Max ran to the rock and said, "Don't worry, Tim! I will help you!" Max jumped up and caught Tim just as he was about to fall. They both landed on the soft grass. Tim hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max! You saved me!" After that day, Tim and Max became best friends. They played together every day and always looked out for each other. Tim learned to be careful when climbing and Max was always there to help. They had lots of fun and were happy to have each other.

One day, a big bossy cat named Tom saw a shiny bracelet on the floor. He wanted to wear it, but it was too small for him. He thought of his friend, a little mouse named Tim, who could wear it. Tom went to Tim's house and said, "Look at this shiny bracelet! Can you wear it?" Tim tried it on and it fit him just right. Tim was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Later, Tom started to yawn and feel sleepy. He told Tim, "I need to take a nap. Will you wake me up when the sun goes down?" Tim agreed and wore the shiny bracelet as he played outside. The sun was very bright, and it made the bracelet sparkle. When the sun went down, the shiny bracelet reminded Tim to wake up Tom. Tim went to Tom's house and said, "Wake up, Tom! The sun is down!" Tom woke up and they both played together all night.

Jill was a three-year-old girl who spent her summer days playing in the park. Today she decided to go on a hunt for a special tree. She looked high and low, and found a tall, strong tree that was full of beautiful leaves. It was just what she was looking for. Jill wanted to take a break underneath its shade, but when she reached the tree she found that it was too far away from the sun to provide any shade. Jill sat down in disappointment and looked up at the tree. She said, "Tree, why won't you help me? I need some shade!" The tree remained still and stubbornly refused Jill's request. Jill thought a moment and then remembered something her mom had told her. She said, "Tree, if you move one step closer to the sun, I will give you lots of hugs and kisses." The tree hesitated, but then slowly shuffled closer to the sun. It was now providing the perfect amount of shade for Jill. Jill smiled and hugged the tree. She said, "Thank you for helping me, tree!"

Tommy loved horses. He liked to look at pictures of horses, to play with toy horses, and to pretend he was a horse. He wished he could see a real horse someday. One day, his mom said, "Tommy, I have a surprise for you. We are going to the farm. Do you know what you will see there?" Tommy was very excited. He guessed, "Horses?" "Yes, horses!" his mom said. "And cows, and pigs, and chickens. Are you ready to go?" Tommy nodded and ran to get his coat and hat. They drove to the farm and parked the car. Tommy saw a big barn and a fence. He heard some noises. He said, "Mom, can we go see the horses now?" His mom smiled and said, "Sure, let's go." They walked to the fence and looked inside. Tommy saw a lot of animals. He saw cows, and pigs, and chickens. But he did not see any horses. He felt sad. He asked, "Mom, where are the horses?" His mom looked around and said, "Oh, I'm sorry, Tommy. It looks like the horses are not here today. Maybe they are in another field, or maybe they are sick, or maybe they are gone. I don't know." Tommy felt miserable. He wanted to see the horses so much. He did not care about the other animals. He started to cry. He said, "Mom, I want to see the horses. I want to see the horses." His mom hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Tommy. I know you love horses. But we can't see them today. Maybe we can come back another day, or maybe we can find another farm, or maybe we can watch a movie about horses. Would that make you feel better?" Tommy shook his head and said, "No, no, no. I want to see the horses. I want to see the horses." He cried louder and louder. He did not stop crying. He did not have a good day. He did not have a good surprise. He did not see the horses.
