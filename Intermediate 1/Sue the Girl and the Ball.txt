Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved playing with clay. She would make all kinds of fun things with it. One day, she made a big clay ball. Sue was very happy with her clay ball. But she was feeling restless. She wanted to do something new. Sue took her clay ball to her friend, Tom. She said, "Look at my clay ball, Tom! Can you help me make something new?" Tom looked at the clay ball and said, "Sure, let's make a clay house!" They started to build the house with the clay. As they were building, Sue accidentally broke a piece of the clay house. She was sad and said, "Oh no, I broke the house!" But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and picked up the broken piece. The bird placed it back on the house and fixed it. Sue and Tom were amazed and smiled. They continued to build the clay house with their new bird friend.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Lily found a worm and picked it up. It was long and slimy and wiggled in her hand. "Look, Ben, I found a worm!" Lily said, showing him her worm. "Wow, can I see?" Ben asked, reaching for the worm. Lily did not want to share her worm. She liked it too much. She pushed Ben away with her other hand. "No, it's mine!" Lily said, holding the worm close to her chest. Ben was sad and angry. He wanted to see the worm too. He pushed Lily back with both hands. "Hey, that's not nice!" Ben said, pushing harder. Lily and Ben started to fight. They pushed and shoved and yelled at each other. They did not see the big furry dog that came running towards them. The dog saw the worm and wanted to play with it. He jumped on Lily and Ben and knocked them down. He snatched the worm from Lily's hand and ran away. "Hey, come back!" Lily and Ben shouted, getting up and chasing the dog. They forgot about their fight. They only wanted their worm back. They ran after the dog, but he was too fast and too furry. He disappeared behind the fence with the worm. Lily and Ben stopped and looked at each other. They felt sorry for what they did. They hugged and said sorry. "I'm sorry I pushed you, Lily. You can have the worm next time," Ben said. "I'm sorry I pushed you, Ben. You can share the worm with me next time," Lily said. They smiled and went back to the garden. They hoped to find another worm. They decided to play nicely and share their bugs. They were friends again.

One day, Jimmy and Mommy were walking through the park. Mommy pointed at a tree and said, "Look, Jimmy, there's an apple tree!" Jimmy looked at the tree with wide eyes. "Wow!" he said. "Are those apples for us? Can I have one?" Mommy nodded. "Yes, the apples are for us," she said. "But we need to be honest. We can only take one apple each." Jimmy nodded. He reached for an apple, careful to not take more than his share. "This looks good," he said. Mommy smiled. She mentioned that it would make a delicious snack. They both took their apples, and started walking back home.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a raven. The raven was very big and had a terrible voice. He liked to fly around and demand things from the other animals. One day, the raven met a tiny mouse. The raven said, "Give me your food!" The mouse looked scared but replied, "No, I need it for my family." The raven was not happy, but he flew away. Later, the raven felt bad for being mean to the mouse. He found some yummy berries and took them to the mouse. The mouse was happy and said, "Thank you, raven!" From that day on, they were good friends and shared everything.

Once upon a time, there was a reindeer named Remy. Remy lived in a big, open field with his friends. They liked to play all day. One day, Remy found a big ball. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't know how. He asked his friend, "What do I do with the ball?" His friend said, "You put it on the ground and kick it." Remy put the ball on the ground and kicked it. It went very far! All the reindeer friends ran after the ball and took turns kicking it. They had so much fun playing with the ball in the open field.
