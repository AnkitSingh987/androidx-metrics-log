Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mommy and Baby and the Listen

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby. The mommy was feeling very angry. She said "Baby, why don't you go outside and play?". The baby said, "No, I don't want to go." The mommy was getting frustrated, but she said, "It's ok, I'll go outside with you". So they went outside together, but the baby kept running away. The mommy said sternly, "Baby, if you don't stop, I'm going to slap you". The baby was scared and they went back inside the house. The next day, mommy said to the baby, "Remember what I said yesterday? If you don't listen, I'll slap you". The baby didn't listen, so the mommy slapped the baby on the eye. It was very painful and the baby cried bitterly. The mommy was very sorry for slapping the baby but she knew it was the only way to teach her lesson. From then on, the baby was always careful to listen.

One day, a small boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a ball on the ground. He picked it up and wanted to throw it. He looked around and saw a big dog. Tim threw the ball and the dog ran to get it. The dog's owner, an old man, came over to Tim and his mom. He told a joke that made Tim laugh. The joke was about a funny cat who got angry at a bird. Tim liked the joke so much that he wanted to hear more. The old man, Tim, and his mom spent the whole day at the park. They played with the dog and told more jokes. Even when the dog got angry at a squirrel, they all laughed. It was a fun day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. He had a big heart and was very compassionate. He loved to help people and animals. One day, Tim saw a little bird with a hurt wing. He wanted to help the bird, so he picked it up gently and took it home. At home, Tim took care of the bird. He fed it and made a soft bed for it to rest. He talked to the bird and said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will help you get better." The bird looked at Tim and chirped happily. Tim knew the bird understood him. After a few days, the bird's wing was better. Tim knew it was time for the bird to go back to its family. He took the bird outside and said, "You can fly now, little bird. Go find your family." The bird chirped and flew up into the sky. Tim watched the bird ride the wind and felt happy that he could help it.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who wanted to play with a net. She went to the park with her dad. When they got there, the little girl said, "Dad, can I play with the net?". Her dad said, "Yes, of course. Go have fun!". The little girl grabbed the net and started to play in the park. She chased after the butterflies and tried to catch them in the net. But the butterflies were too fast and the net was too tight. Whenever the little girl tried to catch one, the net wouldn't open enough and the butterfly would fly away. The little girl looked up at her dad and said sadly, "The net is too tight for the butterflies. What can I do?". Her dad smiled and said, "Try spreading the net a bit wider. That should help you catch the butterflies." So the little girl tried again, spreading the net wider and wider. And this time - miracle of miracles - she caught one! She shouted with joy. She had put her dad's words into practice and it had worked! The little girl smiled up at her dad with pride. From then on, she was the master of the butterfly netting game at the park.

Tim and Lily are at the beach with Mom and Dad. They like to play in the sand and make castles. They also like to splash in the water and jump over the waves. Sometimes the waves are big and scary, but Mom and Dad are always near. One day, Tim and Lily are making a big castle with a moat and a flag. They are very proud of their castle and want to show it to Mom and Dad. They run to the blanket where Mom and Dad are sitting and say, "Look, look, we made a castle!" Mom and Dad smile and say, "Wow, that's a very nice castle! You did a great job!" Tim and Lily are happy and say, "Thank you! Can we go back and finish it?" Mom and Dad say, "Sure, but be careful of the waves. They are getting bigger and stronger. Don't go too far from the shore." Tim and Lily say, "OK, we will be careful." They run back to their castle and start to add more sand and shells. But while they are busy, a big wave comes and washes away their castle. Tim and Lily are sad and cry, "Oh no, our castle is gone! The wave was bad and ruined it!" Mom and Dad hear them and run to them. They hug them and say, "It's OK, don't cry. The wave didn't mean to be bad. It's just part of the sea. Sometimes it makes things and sometimes it breaks things. But you can always make another castle. Maybe even bigger and better." Tim and Lily stop crying and say, "Really? Can we make another castle?" Mom and Dad say, "Of course you can. But first, let's have some lunch. You must be hungry after all that work." Tim and Lily say, "OK, let's have lunch. Then we can make another castle. And we will watch out for the waves." Mom and Dad say, "That's a good plan. Come on, let's go to the blanket. We have sandwiches and juice and cookies." Tim and Lily say, "Yay, we love sandwiches and juice and cookies!" They follow Mom and Dad to the blanket and have a nice lunch. Then they go back to the sand and make another castle. And they have fun at the beach. The end.
