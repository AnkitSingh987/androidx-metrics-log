Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a little house with his friend, a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with Tom, and they would always bring their toys outside to play together. One day, while they were playing, Tom saw a big red truck with flashing lights. "What is that?" asked Tom. "That is an emergency truck," said Sue. "It helps people who need help." Tom thought that was very cool and wanted to help too. The next day, Tom and Sue were playing when they heard a loud noise. They saw a little bird fall from a tree. Tom quickly ran to the bird and brought it to Sue. "We need to help this bird," said Tom. Sue agreed and they took care of the bird until it was better. The bird thanked Tom and Sue for their help and flew away happily. From that day on, Tom and Sue always helped others when they needed it. The whole town knew they could count on Tom and Sue in an emergency. And the sleepy cat and his friend learned that helping others is a very important thing to do.

One day, a slow insect was walking in the park. It was a sunny day, and the insect was very happy. The insect saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he met a funny bird. The bird said, "Hi, little insect! Do you want to hear a joke?" The insect smiled and said, "Yes, please! I love jokes!" The bird told the joke: "Why did the insect go to school? To learn how to bug people!" They both laughed and laughed. The insect and the bird became good friends. They played together all day in the park, telling more jokes and having fun. At the end of the day, the slow insect and the funny bird said goodbye. They promised to meet again in the park to share more jokes and play together. The insect went home with a big smile, happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat lived in a small house. One hot day, the cat went for a walk. On the walk, the cat met a small green dog. The cat wanted to introduce the dog to his house. The cat and the dog walked back to the house. When they got there, they saw a big mess. The cat's toys were all over the floor. The cat was sad. The dog wanted to help the cat. They both started to clean up the mess. As they were cleaning, they found a big red ball. The cat and the dog looked at each other. They both wanted to play with the ball. They started to fight over the ball. Suddenly, the ball started to bounce away. The cat and the dog stopped fighting and chased the ball together. They both learned to share and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a funny frog named Fred. Fred loved to jump all the time. He had a special jump style that made all his friends laugh. He would jump high and then wiggle his legs in the air. Everyone loved to watch Fred jump. One day, a big bird came to the pond. The bird saw Fred jump and said, "You look silly when you jump like that. You should jump like me." The bird showed Fred how he jumped, but Fred didn't like it. He wanted to keep his own funny style. Fred felt sad, but his friends came to help. They told Fred that his jump was the best and they loved it. They all jumped together, each with their own funny style. The big bird saw how happy they were and learned that everyone can have their own special jump.

A little girl named Lily was playing outside one day. Daddy was standing nearby, watching her. It was getting dark outside. "Daddy, why is it so dark?" asked Lily. Daddy smiled. "It's time for the sun to spread its wings and take a break." Lily knew it was time to go home. She said goodbye to her friends and ran over to Daddy. When Daddy and Lily reached home, Lily said, "I like when the sun spreads its wings! It looks so pretty." Daddy smiled and ruffled Lily's hair. He said, "You're right. Spreading kindness is just as beautiful." The moral of this story is that spreading kindness is just as important as other things in life. With kindness, you can make the world brighter.
