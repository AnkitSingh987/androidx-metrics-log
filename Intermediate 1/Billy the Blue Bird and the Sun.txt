Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Blue Bird and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Billy. Billy had big muscles. He liked to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. One day, Billy met a new friend, a little red bird named Rosie. Rosie was shy and didn't talk much. Billy asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Rosie nodded her head and said, "Yes, I want to play." They played all day, flying close to each other and having fun. They raced through the trees and played hide and seek. As the sun went down, they said goodbye and flew back to their nests. Billy was happy to have a new friend and couldn't wait to play with Rosie again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to play outside. She wore her pretty dress with flowers on it. Sue loved to play with her ball in the yard. She would kick it and laugh as it bounced away. While playing, Sue saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he hurt his knee. Sue asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, but my knee hurts." Sue had an idea. She ran inside and brought out a soft pillow for Tim to sit on. They sat and played with the ball, rolling it back and forth. Suddenly, the ball changed into a pretty butterfly! Sue and Tim were surprised. They smiled and watched the butterfly fly away. Now, they had a new friend to play with in the yard.

Once upon a time there was a mommy, a daddy and a little child. The child had something that she was very worried about. The mommy said, "What's wrong? Why are you worried?" The child said, "My toy disappeared!" The daddy looked around and asked, "Where did it go?". The child said, "I don't know, it just vanished. It's so weird." The mommy suggested that they look for it. Together, they searched for the toy all around the house, but there was no sign of it. The mommy said, "It's okay, sweetheart. Maybe it just vanished into thin air." The daddy added, "Maybe it's gone on an adventure." The child smiled and said, "That's a great idea! I hope it comes back soon." So the family went about their day, and eventually the toy reappeared. The child was so happy to see it! They never knew where it had gone or why it had disappeared, but they were happy it was back.

One day, Jack went for a walk in the park. He saw a bright patch of pink flowers and he decided to explore it further. He got closer and could see all the different shapes and sizes of the petals. He smiled as he looked at all the pretty colours. Just then, a voice asked him, "What is so special about this patch of flowers?" Jack looked around and saw a friendly-looking rabbit. "It's so pink!" exclaimed Jack, as he touched a petal. "I love the way it looks!" The rabbit smiled. "Yes, it is very pretty here. The sun always makes the colours look brighter. Why don't you take a walk with me to see the rest of the park?" "Okay!" said Jack. They continued to explore the park, finding more colourful patches of flowers and amazing creatures. All the while, Jack had a big smile on his face. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was an ancient woman. She lived in a small house near the woods. She had a cat and a dog. They were her friends. One day, the woman wanted to go for a walk. She put on her coat and shoes. She opened the door and went outside. The cat and dog followed her. They walked and walked, and soon they were deep in the woods. The woman saw a big rock. She wanted to sit on it. But when she tried to sit, her foot slipped. She fell down and hurt her leg. The cat and dog were scared. They did not know what to do. They tried to help the woman, but they could not. The woman, cat, and dog stayed in the woods all night. They were cold and sad. The woman could not walk, and they could not find their way home. And so, they never made it back to their small house.
