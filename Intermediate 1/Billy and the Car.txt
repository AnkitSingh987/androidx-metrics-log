Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy and the Car

"Billy was an intelligent three-year-old. One day, he wanted to shoot something. So, he got his toy gun and went outside. To his surprise, there was a car wreck in the street. Billy thought it would be fun to shoot at the car wreck. He got close and started shooting. Mommy saw him and asked, 'Billy, what are you doing?' Billy responded, 'I'm shooting the wreck glass!' Mommy smiled, 'That's a good idea, but first you need to be careful and not get too close.' So, Billy took a few steps back and continued shooting. He had fun shooting the glass from the car wreck. After a few minutes, he was done and said goodbye to the wreck. Billy was very pleased with his intelligent decision to shoot the car wreck."

One day, a smart dog named Max went to the beach. He loved to play in the sand and find shells. Max was very happy because the sun was out and the sky was blue. While Max was looking for shells, he found a big one. It seemed like a very special shell. He picked it up with his mouth and started to run to show his mom. But as he was running, the shell began to move! Max was so surprised! The shell was not just a shell, but a little crab hiding inside. The crab waved its claws and said, "Hi, I'm Carl!" Max and Carl became best friends and played together on the beach all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily was in her room. She was very bored. She had a big, soft pillow that she liked to play with. Lily thought, "I want to escape this boring room and have fun." Lily saw her cat, Tilly, and said, "Let's escape and have fun!" Tilly meowed and they went to the door. But the door was closed. Lily tried to open it, but she was too small. She had an idea. She put her pillow on the floor and jumped on it. The pillow made her jump very high! She jumped and opened the door. Lily and Tilly went outside and found a big tree. They climbed the tree and saw a big bird. The big bird said, "Hello, I am a magic bird! I can make your pillow do fun things!" The bird touched the pillow with its beak. Suddenly, the pillow started to fly! Lily and Tilly were so happy. They flew on the pillow and had lots of fun. They were not bored anymore.

Lila and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. They see a big bush with many blueberries. They run to the bush and start to pick the blueberries. "Yum, yum, these are so good!" Lila says. She puts some blueberries in her mouth and some in her pocket. "Me too, me too!" Ben says. He does the same as Lila. He eats some blueberries and puts some in his pocket. They pick and eat and pick and eat. They do not see Mom coming. "Stop, stop, you two!" Mom says. She looks at their faces and clothes. They are all blue and sticky. "You have eaten too many blueberries. You will have a tummy ache. And look at your pockets. They are too full. The blueberries will not fit. They will fall out and make a mess." Lila and Ben look at their pockets. They see some blueberries popping out. They feel their tummies. They feel a little sick. "Sorry, Mom," they say. "We like blueberries too much. Can we have some water, please?" Mom smiles. She loves her twins. She gives them some water and a hug. "Next time, share some blueberries with me, okay?" she says. "And do not eat too many. A little is good, a lot is bad." Lila and Ben nod. They learn their lesson. They love Mom and blueberries. But not too many.

Anna and Ben liked to play with their toy trains. They had a big track with bridges and tunnels and stations. They made the trains go fast and slow and make noises. They pretended they were the drivers and the passengers. One day, they saw a real train outside their window. It was a steam train, with a big black engine and a lot of white smoke. They were very excited and wanted to see it up close. They asked their mom if they could go to the train station. Mom said yes, but they had to be careful and hold her hand. She said the steam train was very hot and loud and could hurt them. She also said they had to wear their coats and hats, because it was cold outside. Anna and Ben put on their coats and hats and followed mom to the train station. They saw the steam train waiting on the platform. It was bigger and shinier than their toy trains. They heard the whistle and the hiss of the steam. They smelled the coal and the oil. They wanted to touch the train, but mom said no. She said it was too hot and dirty. She said they could look at it from a distance. She said they could wave to the driver and the passengers. Anna and Ben waved to the driver and the passengers. They saw some people get on and off the train. They saw some people carry bags and boxes. They saw some people smile and some people cry. They wondered where the train was going and what the people were doing. They asked mom if they could go on the train. Mom said no. She said the train was not for them. She said the train was for people who had to change their lives. Anna and Ben did not understand what mom meant. They did not know why people had to change their lives. They thought it was fun to go on a train and see new places. They thought it was sad to leave their home and their friends. They felt uncomfortable and confused. They did not like the steam train anymore. They wanted to go back to their toy trains. They told mom they were bored and cold and hungry. Mom said they could go back home soon. She said they had to wait for the train to leave. She said they had to say goodbye to the train and the people. Anna and Ben said goodbye to the train and the people. They did not mean it. They did not care. They just wanted to leave. The train started to move. It made a loud noise and a lot of smoke. It went faster and faster. It disappeared into the tunnel. Anna and Ben did not see the train anymore. They did not hear the train anymore. They did not smell the train anymore. They only felt a big boom. They only saw a bright flash. They only smelled fire and smoke. The tunnel had exploded. The train had crashed. The people had died. Anna and Ben had died too.
