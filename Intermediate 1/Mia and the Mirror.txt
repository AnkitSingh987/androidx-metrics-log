Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Mirror

Mia had a picture in her hand. It was a picture of her best friend, Jack. She wanted to dress like Jack, so she went to her room to get changed. Mia looked in the closet and finally found the perfect shirt. She put it on quickly. Then she went to the dresser and found a pair of pants. She was so excited, she put them on as fast as she could. Mia looked in the mirror. She was happy with how she looked! She took a picture of herself so that she could show Jack. Mia ran back to the living room to show Jack the picture. She was so proud of herself that she had dressed so fast. Jack smiled and told her she looked great. Mia was so happy!

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to ride on his dad's knee. Tim was very excited and ready to have fun. He ran to his dad and said, "Dad, can I ride on your knee, please?" Dad smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. Hop on!" Tim jumped on his dad's knee and they started to bounce up and down. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. But then, Tim's dad's knee started to hurt. He said, "Ouch, Tim, my knee hurts now. We need to stop." Tim was sad because he wanted to keep playing. But he knew that his dad's knee was not okay. So, they stopped and Tim did not get to ride on his dad's knee anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to perform for his family. He would sing, dance, and tell funny jokes. One day, Tim's mom made yummy muffins for everyone. Tim was so excited and wanted to eat them all. But Tim's mom said, "You can only have one muffin, Tim." Tim felt sad but he ate his muffin. Later, when no one was looking, Tim took another muffin. He felt a little ashamed, but he really wanted it. Then, he had an idea. He would perform a show and make everyone laugh, so they would forget about the muffins. Tim put on a funny hat and started to dance. His family laughed and clapped. But then, Tim tripped and fell. The muffin he took fell out of his pocket. Everyone saw it and knew what Tim did. Tim felt very ashamed and started to cry. His mom said, "Tim, you should not take things without asking." Tim knew he did something bad, and the show had a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rag. It was a big, red rag. Tim had an idea. He wanted to play with the rag. Tim called his friend, Sue. "Let's play a game," he said. "We can march and wave the rag." Sue liked the idea. They held the rag and started to march. As they marched, they began to feel dizzy. They laughed and said, "This is fun!" They marched and waved the rag until they were too tired to play. They sat down and smiled, happy with their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Bobo. Bobo liked to feel independent. He did not like to ask for help. One day, Bobo saw a big cat. The cat was mean to Bobo. They had a fight. Bobo tried to win the fight, but it was hard. He felt sad. He wanted to be strong and independent, but he needed help. So, Bobo asked his friend, a bird named Lulu, for help. Lulu came to help Bobo. Together, they scared the big cat away. Bobo learned that it is okay to ask for help sometimes. He still felt independent, but now he knew that friends can help too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a happy boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big cross on the ground. Tim wanted to match the colors on the cross. He had four color sticks: red, blue, green, and yellow. Tim's friend, Sue, came to help. She said, "Let's match the red stick to the red part of the cross." They did it and the cross became more colorful. They matched the other sticks to the cross too. But then, something unexpected happened! The cross started to move and became a big, colorful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you, Tim and Sue, for making me pretty!" Tim and Sue were very happy and played with the butterfly all day.
