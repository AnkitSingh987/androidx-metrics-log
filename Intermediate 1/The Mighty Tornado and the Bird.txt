Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mighty Tornado and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a mighty tornado. It was so strong it could drink up all the water in the world. All the people and animals stayed inside because it was too risky to go out. The tornado was so powerful that it even drank up all the clouds! All the colourful birds and balloons flew away so fast. But then something amazing happened. A brave little bird flew up to the tornado and it began to drink from it. Little by little it made the tornado weaker. Soon the mighty tornado stopped spinning and the world was safe again. The birds and balloons returned and everyone was happy again. They all gave the brave little bird lots of hugs and cuddles for saving the day!

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He loved to play near the creek close to his house. One day, he went to the creek with his toy boat. While playing, Tim saw a big door. It was shut tight. He pushed and pushed, but the door did not open. Tim wanted to find out what was behind the door, so he kept trying. Finally, the door opened. Behind the door, there was a dark room. Tim went inside and the door shut tight behind him. He could not get out. The room was very dark and Tim was scared. He missed his mom and dad. Tim should have stayed near the creek and played with his toy boat.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a thin rabbit. The rabbit liked to hop and play all day. One day, the rabbit was hopping near a creek. The water in the creek was clear and cool. The rabbit stopped to drink some water. As he drank, he noticed something shiny in the water. It was a pretty stone. The rabbit was very happy to find the shiny stone. He picked it up with his mouth and hopped away from the creek. The rabbit showed the shiny stone to all his friends in the forest. They all liked the pretty stone too. The rabbit was very proud of his find. From that day on, the rabbit and his friends played near the creek, hoping to find more shiny stones.

Once upon a time, there were two brothers, Page and Oat. One day, their mom told them that it's time for a race. Page and Oat got very excited and started to run as fast as they could. Page was a fast runner and was soon ahead of Oat. He ran down a hill and was almost out of sight. Oat started to feel sad and worried. He thought he could never catch up with Page. But then he saw something on the ground. It was a thin oat! He picked it up, held it in front of him and started to run. The oat floated in the air, making Oat zoom ahead. Page looked behind him, and was surprised to see Oat right behind him. The race was so close that Page barely won. The brothers hugged each other and laughed. They agreed to race again the next day. The End.

One day, a little girl named Lily was excited to go to the park. She wanted to taste the yummy ice cream from the ice cream truck. She put on her shoes and ran outside. At the park, she saw her friend Tim. "I want ice cream!" said Lily. Tim nodded and said, "Me too! Let's go to the truck!" But when they got to the truck, there was no ice cream. The ice cream man had attached a big sign that said, "Out of ice cream." Lily and Tim were sad. They sat on a bench and talked. Then, a big bird flew down with a big ice cream cone in its beak. The bird dropped the ice cream cone in front of Lily and Tim. They were so happy! They shared the ice cream and had a great day at the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big gear in his toy box. He was very excited to play with it. He showed it to his mom and asked, "Mom, what can I do with this gear?" His mom thought for a moment and said, "You can mix it with your other toys and make a high tower!" Tim liked this idea and started to build. Tim put the gear on the floor and added more toys on top. He made the tower very high. He was so happy to see his tower grow taller and taller. His mom watched and clapped her hands, saying, "Good job, Tim!" At last, the tower was as high as Tim could reach. He looked at his mom and said, "Mom, I did it! I mixed the gear and made a high tower!" His mom hugged him and said, "Yes, you did! You are very smart, Tim!" From that day on, Tim loved to mix his toys and build high towers with the gear. He was proud of his new skill and always had fun playing with his toy gear.
