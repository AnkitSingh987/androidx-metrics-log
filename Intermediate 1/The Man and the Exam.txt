Title: The Man and the Exam

Once upon a time there was a man who had a really modern exam to take. But it was too hard and he was very scared. He sat down at the desk and began to do the work. But he was so nervous that he was making lots of mistakes. Then suddenly his mom walked in and saw what was happening. She was very angry, so she slapped him on the hand and said, "No more mistakes! You need to do better on this exam!" The man looked up at his mom and said, "Yes mommy, I'm sorry!" He tried really hard to finish the exam and he did it really well. In the end the man did really well on his modern exam and he was very proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom said, "Tim, are you ready to help me wash the plates?" Tim was excited and said, "Yes, mom! I am ready!" Tim took a plate and started to rub it with soap and water. He rubbed it gently until it was clean. His mom was very happy with him. She said, "Good job, Tim! You are doing great!" Tim smiled and continued to rub the plates until they were all clean. After the plates were clean, Tim and his mom put them away. Tim felt proud that he could help his mom. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel happy and proud.

Anna liked to dream at night. She dreamed of many things. She dreamed of flying in the sky, of swimming in the sea, of playing with her friends. Sometimes she dreamed of things that were not real, like dragons and fairies and talking animals. One night, she dreamed of a frog. The frog was green and slimy and had big eyes. He hopped on her bed and said, "Hello, Anna. Do you want to play with me?" Anna was surprised, but she was not afraid. She liked frogs. She said, "Yes, I want to play with you. What can we do?" The frog said, "We can do anything we want. We are in a dream. We can be flexible. We can jump high, we can stretch long, we can twist and turn. Follow me and I will show you." Anna followed the frog. They jumped on the clouds, they stretched over the rainbow, they twisted and turned in the air. They had a lot of fun. Anna laughed and the frog croaked. Anna woke up in the morning. She smiled and remembered her dream. She wondered if she would see the frog again. She hoped so. She liked the frog. He was her flexible friend.

One day, a fancy cat named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a little boy playing with dough. The boy was making shapes and animals with the dough. Lucy liked the dough and wanted to play too. "Can I play with you?" Lucy asked the boy. The boy smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with me. Let's make a big dough castle!" So they played together and made a big castle with the dough. As they were playing, a big wind came and tried to blow the dough castle away. Lucy wanted to protect the castle. So she jumped in front of it and used her body to stop the wind. The boy was happy that Lucy saved their castle. They continued to play and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a great big dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play outside. One day, Buddy found a patch of soft grass. He enjoyed rolling around in it. "Hi, Buddy!" said a little bird. "Do you like my grass patch?" Buddy barked happily. "Yes, I enjoy it a lot!" Buddy and the bird played together in the patch all day. As the sun went down, Buddy and the bird said goodbye. "I had a great time with you," said the bird. Buddy wagged his tail and went home. From that day on, Buddy and the bird were best friends.
