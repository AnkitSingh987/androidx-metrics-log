Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Lively Dog and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his group of friends. They would run, jump, and chase each other all day long. Spot was always happy when he was with his friends. One day, Spot's family went on a trip. Spot had to stay at home. He missed his group of friends very much. Spot felt sad and lonely. He would look out the window and wish he could play with his friends again. Finally, the day came when Spot's family came back from their trip. Spot was so happy to see his friends again. They had a big party with lots of fun games. Spot never wanted to miss his lively group of friends again.

One day, a big ship went to a port. The ship had a big, strong lock on its door. The sky was clear, and the sun was very bright. The ship was very happy to be at the port. While the ship was at the port, a little fish swam up to it. The fish wanted to see what was inside the ship. But the fish could not open the lock. The fish was sad and swam away. Later, the fish came back with a big crab friend. The crab had strong claws. The crab used its claws to open the lock. The fish and the crab went inside the ship. They looked around and saw many fun things. But then, they found out that the ship was not a ship. It was a big whale! The whale had a lock on its mouth to keep the water out. The fish and the crab were very surprised. They quickly swam out of the whale and went back to the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big uncle who loved to play with him. One day, Tim's uncle came to visit him. He said, "Tim, let's go for a walk in the woods!" Tim was very happy and said, "Yes, let's go!" They walked into the woods, and Tim saw many trees. The trees had thick branches and lots of leaves. Tim's uncle led the way, holding Tim's hand. They saw birds, squirrels, and even a rabbit. Tim was having so much fun. After a while, they found a nice spot to sit and rest. Tim's uncle told him stories about the animals in the woods. Tim listened and laughed. They had a great day together. When it was time to go home, Tim's uncle led him back to the house. Tim couldn't wait for his uncle to visit again.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Kitty. Kitty had a friend, a small dog named Doggy. They played together every day. Kitty and Doggy liked to run, jump, and laugh. They were happy friends. One day, Kitty told Doggy a secret. "I believe I can wink," Kitty said. Doggy looked at Kitty and said, "Show me!" Kitty closed one eye and opened it again. Doggy laughed and clapped. "You did it!" Doggy said. Kitty felt successful and proud. Kitty and Doggy played a new game. They took turns winking at each other. They laughed and had fun. They were the best of friends, and they believed they could do anything together.

One day, Ella was jogging in the park. On her way she saw Sarah, who was also jogging. The two girls smiled at each other and decided to jog together. In the park they found a big box with a bow on it. When they opened it, they found a big jug of juice. It looked very sweet. Ella and Sarah both wanted to drink the juice but they couldn't agree on who should have it. "You can have it," said Ella. But Sarah said, "No, you should have it. You found it in the first place!" Finally, Ella said, "Let's just share it!" But before they had a chance to share the juice, a big dog ran up and drank the whole jug. They were very mad and all their jogging couldn't help them get their juice back. Moral of the story: It's always better to share with others than to fight over something.
