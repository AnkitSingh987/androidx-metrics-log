Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Creative Girl and the Tomato

Once upon a time, there was a creative little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toy tomato. She would pretend to cook with it and make yummy food for her family. One day, Amy's toy tomato rolled under her bed. She tried to reach for it, but her arm was too short. She started to complain, "I can't get my tomato! I need help!" Her big brother, Ben, heard her and came to see what was wrong. Ben was very tall, so he could reach under the bed and get the toy tomato for Amy. She was so happy that she gave him a big hug. Then, they played together with the toy tomato, making creative meals for their family. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once there was a boy named Max. He was a very gifted child. He could do a lot of amazing things for his age; like solve puzzles and read books. One day, Max asked his dad, "What do I get if I do something great?" His dad smiled and said, "When you do something special, you get a reward!" Max was excited to see what he could do to earn a reward. He worked hard all day, doing all the things he could. He read books, cleaned his room, and helped his dad with the garden. At the end of the day, Max went to his dad and asked, "Am I done now? Do I get my reward?" His dad smiled and gave him a big hug. "Yes, Max, you did it!" he said. Max was very happy. He had put in a lot of hard work and now he had a reward. He had shown that he was a very gifted child indeed!

Once upon a time, there was a big red mail truck. It had a shiny bell that would ring when the mail was ready. The mail truck had a friend named Tim. Tim was a little boy who loved to help with the mail. One day, Tim saw the mail truck coming down the street. He ran to help with the mail. "I want to help with the mail today!" said Tim. The mail truck was happy to have Tim's help. They went to each house, putting mail in the boxes. Tim rang the shiny bell each time. At the last house, Tim was very tired. He forgot to ring the shiny bell. The mail truck was sorry that it did not remind Tim. Just then, a little dog came running out of the house. It had the mail in its mouth! The dog gave the mail back to Tim and the mail truck. Tim and the mail truck were very happy and surprised. They thanked the little dog and rang the shiny bell together. From that day on, Tim always remembered to ring the bell, and the little dog helped them with the mail too.

Once upon a time, there was a silly rabbit. The rabbit liked to hop and play all day. The rabbit had many friends in the forest. They all loved to play together. One day, the silly rabbit and his friends decided to play a game. The game was to see who could hop the farthest. The rabbit, the squirrel, and the bird all lined up to start the race. They all hopped and jumped as far as they could. In the end, the silly rabbit won the race. All of his friends clapped and cheered for him. They were so happy for the rabbit. They played more games and had lots of fun. And when the sun went down, they all went home to rest.

One day, a large cat found a magnet. The cat did not know what it was, but it liked to play with it. The magnet was shiny and fun. The cat took the magnet everywhere it went. The cat was very happy with its new toy. One day, the cat saw a big ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball, but the magnet was in its mouth. The cat put the magnet on the ground and played with the ball. The cat had a lot of fun playing with the ball. But when the cat tried to pick up the magnet again, it was stuck to the ball. The cat tried and tried to pull the magnet off the ball. It pulled and pulled, but the magnet would not come off. Then, the cat had an idea. It pushed the ball very hard, and the ball split into two parts. The magnet was free! The cat was very happy. It played with the magnet and the two parts of the ball. The cat knew it could always find a way to play with its favorite toys. And from that day on, the cat, the magnet, and the two parts of the ball were always together.
