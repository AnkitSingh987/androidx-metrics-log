Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rude Fox and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a rude fox. The rude fox liked to take things from other animals. One day, the rude fox took a ball from a little rabbit. The little rabbit was very sad. A kind bear saw the sad little rabbit and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little rabbit said, "The rude fox took my ball." The kind bear wanted to help the little rabbit. So, the kind bear went to the rude fox to get the ball back. When the kind bear found the rude fox, he said, "You must reverse what you did. Give the ball back to the little rabbit." The rude fox did not want to listen, but the kind bear was big and strong. So, the rude fox gave the ball back to the little rabbit. The little rabbit was very happy and thanked the kind bear. From that day on, the rude fox learned to be nice to other animals.

Lily and Ben were on a big plane with their mom and dad. They were going to visit their grandma and grandpa in a far away land. Lily and Ben liked to look out the window and see the clouds and the sky. They also liked to play with their toys and read their books. But after a long time, they started to feel bored and tired. They yawned and rubbed their eyes. They wanted to get off the plane and see their grandma and grandpa. "Mom, when are we there?" Lily asked. "Soon, sweetie, soon. We are almost there. Just a little more," mom said. "Can we watch a movie?" Ben asked. "Sure, honey, sure. Here, you can use my tablet. But remember to put on your headphones, so you don't disturb the other people," dad said. Lily and Ben watched a movie about a brave lion who saved his friends from a mean hyena. They liked the movie and smiled and laughed. But they also yawned and felt sleepy. The plane started to go down and the pilot said something on the speaker. Mom and dad put on their seat belts and told Lily and Ben to do the same. "Look, look, there is the land!" Lily said, pointing out the window. "I see it, I see it! There is the land!" Ben said, following her finger. They saw the land get closer and closer. They saw houses and cars and trees and flowers. They saw a big sign that said "Welcome to Grandma and Grandpa's Land". The plane landed and the people clapped and cheered. Lily and Ben were happy and excited. They got off the plane and saw their grandma and grandpa waiting for them with big hugs and kisses. "Hello, hello, we missed you so much!" grandma said. "Hello, hello, we are so glad to see you!" grandpa said. Lily and Ben hugged and kissed them back. They felt warm and loved. They were in a new land, but they felt at home.

One day, a small red bird named Tom found a big knot in his nest. The knot was not comfortable. Tom tried to pull it out, but he could not. He felt sad. Then, a big blue bird named Sam saw Tom. Sam asked, "Do you need help?" Tom said, "Yes, please. I have a knot in my nest." Sam offered to help Tom. Together, they pulled and pulled. The knot came out of the nest. Now, Tom's nest was comfortable again. Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tom! I am glad I could help." They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He was feeling miserable because his best friend, Sam, was sick and could not come to play. Tim wore a jacket with a zip, because it was a bit cold. He walked slowly and sadly, looking at the ground. At the park, Tim saw a little dog. The dog had a ball and wanted to play. Tim shrugged and thought, "Why not?" He threw the ball and the dog ran fast to get it. They played and played, and Tim started to feel happy again. After playing for a long time, the dog ran to a tree and barked. Tim walked to the tree and saw a note. The note was from Sam! It said, "I am feeling better now. Let's play at the park tomorrow!" Tim was so surprised and happy. He knew that tomorrow would be a great day with his best friend.
