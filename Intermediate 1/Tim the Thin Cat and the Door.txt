Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thin Cat and the Door

One day, a thin cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big squash in a garden. Tim thought it would be fun to surprise his friend, Sam the dog, with the squash. Tim rolled the squash to Sam's house. He knocked on the door and said, "Sam, I have a surprise for you!" Sam opened the door and saw the big squash. He was very happy. But then, the squash rolled away down a hill. Tim and Sam ran after it, but they could not catch it. The squash hit a tree and broke into small pieces. Tim and Sam were sad because their surprise was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty boy named Tim. Tim loved to play his guitar. One day, he saw a big paper. He wanted to fold the paper into a guitar. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me fold this paper?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you." So, they started to fold the paper together. They folded and folded until the paper looked like a guitar. But then, a strong wind came. It blew the paper guitar away. Tim and Sam tried to catch it, but they could not. The wind took the paper guitar far, far away. Tim and Sam were sad. They could not play with the paper guitar anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse was very soft and loved to play with his friends. One day, he met a big cat outside his house. The cat stared at the mouse with big eyes. The mouse was scared and didn't know what to do. The cat said, "Why are you scared, little mouse? I just want to be friends." The mouse looked at the cat and said, "But cats eat mice! How can we be friends?" The cat smiled and said, "I promise I won't eat you. Let's play together!" So, the soft mouse and the big cat played together all day. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. The cat didn't eat the mouse, and they became best friends. From that day on, they played together every day and were very happy. And the mouse was never scared of the cat again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fancy school called University. Many kids wanted to go there to learn and play. But there was one little boy named Tim who did not want to go to the fancy school. He liked to stay at home and play with his toys. One day, Tim's mom told him, "You have to go to University, Tim. It will be fun!" But Tim said, "No, I refuse to go! I want to stay at home and play." His mom was sad, but she let him stay home. Tim played with his toys all day. But soon, he heard his friends talking about how much fun they had at the fancy school. They learned new things and played new games. Tim felt lonely and thought, "Maybe I should go to University too." The next day, Tim went to the fancy school and had a great time with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to watch her favorite network on TV. One day, while she was watching, she saw a big, frightened cat on the screen. The cat was all alone and looked very sad. Lucy wanted to help the cat, so she called out to it, "Don't be frightened, kitty! I'm here to help you!" To her surprise, the cat heard her and jumped out of the TV! Lucy couldn't believe her eyes. She never thought that she could talk to the cat on the network. The frightened cat was now in Lucy's room, and she petted it gently. The cat started to purr and was not frightened anymore. They became best friends, and Lucy knew that she had a special gift. From that day on, she could talk to all the animals she saw on her favorite network. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an old judge. The old judge was very wise and kind. He helped people when they had problems. Everyone in the town liked him. One day, a little girl named Lily came to see the judge. She said, "Judge, I need your help. My cat is stuck in a tree and I can't get her down." The judge smiled and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you." But before the judge could help, he had to delay his work. He told the people in the town, "I need to help Lily with her cat. I will be back soon." The people understood and waited for the judge to return. The old judge and Lily went to the tree where the cat was stuck. The judge carefully climbed the tree and brought the cat down. Lily was very happy and said, "Thank you, judge!" After that, the judge went back to his work. The people in the town were happy that the judge helped Lily. They knew the judge was always there to help them.
