Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a special, fragile toy she loved very much. It was a little bird that she took with her everywhere she went. One day, Mia's mom took a photo of her with her little bird. Mia was very happy. She wanted to keep the photo safe, but she didn't know how. She asked her mom to help her. Mia's mom showed her how to manage and take care of the photo. They put the photo in a pretty box, so it would stay safe. Mia was glad she could keep the photo of her and her little bird safe and sound.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a poor boy. He did not have many toys. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a big, pretty ball. Tim wanted the ball very much. "Mom, can I have the ball?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the ball and then at Tim. "We don't have enough money for the ball, Tim. But we can keep looking for other things," she said. Tim was sad but he kept looking. He found a small, old toy car. It was not as pretty as the ball, but Tim liked it. His mom smiled and said, "We can buy that, Tim." So they bought the toy car. Tim played with the toy car every day. He was very happy. He learned that you don't need the biggest or prettiest things to be happy. Sometimes, the simple things can make you just as happy. And that is the moral of the story: be happy with what you have.

One day, a boy named Tim went on a journey. It was a cold day, but he was very excited. He put on his big coat, hat, and gloves. Tim wanted to see the big park with his friend, Sue. On the way, Tim saw Sue. "Sue, let's go to the park together!" Tim said. Sue smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I want to play in the cold too!" They walked and talked about what they would do at the park. When they got to the park, they played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun. After playing, Tim and Sue sat down. Tim said, "I will tell you a story about a big journey I had with my family." Sue listened and they both laughed. They had a great day at the park.

Once there was a little boy who was very impatient. He was only three years old so he didn't understand things very well yet. His Dad was trying to talk to him about power. He wanted the boy to understand why power was important. But the boy was too impatient. He wanted something fun to do! So his Dad decided to give him a special task. He told the boy he needed to go around the house and wipe every single window. The little boy was so excited. He loved wiping things, so he started running all over the house wiping each window from top to bottom. He was so fast and eager. He was wiping so cheerfully that his dad couldn’t help but smile. In the end, all of the windows were sparkly clean and the little boy felt very proud of himself. He had done a great job! His father praised his efforts and explained how important power was. The little boy understood. He knew that power was important and he also knew how to wipe windows now, too.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found some dough in her kitchen. She felt lucky because she loved to play with dough. She took the dough and started to make a big ball. She was very happy. Lucy's friend, Tom, came over to play. He saw the big ball of dough and said, "Wow, that's a big ball of dough! Can I play with it too?" Lucy said, "Yes, you can play with it too. We can make something together." Lucy and Tom started to make a big dough cake. They worked hard and had a lot of fun. When they finished, they looked at their cake and were very proud. Lucy said, "I can't believe we made such a big cake!" Tom said, "Yes, we are very lucky to have this dough to play with." They showed their cake to Lucy's mom and she was very happy too. She said, "You two did a great job. I'm so proud of you both." Lucy and Tom smiled and felt very lucky to have made such a big dough cake together.
