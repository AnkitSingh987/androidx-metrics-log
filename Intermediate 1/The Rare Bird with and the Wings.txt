Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rare Bird with and the Wings

One day, a rare bird with big, colorful wings flew into a small town. The bird liked to spread its wings wide and show off its pretty colors. It had a funny ear on its head that helped it hear things from far away. The bird met a little girl named Lily. "Hi," said Lily, "I like your big wings and funny ear!" The bird flapped its wings and said, "Thank you! My wings help me fly high and my ear helps me hear things from far away." Lily and the rare bird became good friends. They played together every day. The bird would spread its wings and fly high in the sky, while Lily watched and clapped her hands. The bird's funny ear helped them find fun places to play. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a land full of yummy food, there was a big, tight bunch of grapes. The grapes lived high up in a tree, where they could see all the fun things happening below. One day, a bird named Bobo saw the grapes. Bobo was hungry and wanted to eat the grapes. So, he flew up to the tree and tried to pick a grape. But the grapes were too tight together. Bobo couldn't get one. He tried and tried, but it was no use. Bobo had an idea. He would dive down from the tree and try to grab a grape on his way down. He got ready, then dove fast toward the ground. As he dove, he grabbed a grape! Bobo was so happy. He ate the grape and it was very yummy. From that day on, Bobo always dove to get his grapes.

One day, a happy boy named Tom went to play with his friends. They loved to fold paper to make fun shapes. Today, they wanted to fold a big paper and make a big surprise for their friend, Sue. As they started to fold the paper, Tom and his friends started to argue. "I want to fold it this way," said Tom. "No, I want to fold it that way," said his friend, Sam. They did not know what to do. Then, a little dog came and picked up the paper in its mouth. The dog ran away with the paper. Tom, Sam, and their friends ran after the dog. They were all surprised and laughed. They forgot about their fight and worked together to catch the dog. When they caught the dog, they found out that the dog had folded the paper into a beautiful heart shape. They showed it to Sue, and she loved it. Tom, Sam, and their friends learned that working together made them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a task to do. He had to find a bone that would fit in his small mouth. Max looked and looked for the perfect bone. One day, Max saw a big dog with a bone. Max felt guilty, but he wanted the bone so much. He waited for the big dog to go away. When the big dog left, Max took the bone. Max tried to make the bone fit in his mouth, but it was too big. He tried and tried, but it just would not fit. Max was very sad. He knew he should not have taken the bone. In the end, Max did not get a bone that fit and he felt guilty for taking the big dog's bone.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a dependable bird named Bob. Bob lived in a nest on a strong branch. He loved to help his friends. One day, a squirrel named Sam came to Bob. "Can I borrow some nuts from you?" Sam asked. "I am very hungry, and I cannot find any nuts today." Bob smiled and said, "Of course, Sam! I will help you find some nuts." Bob and Sam went to look for nuts together. They found a big tree with many nuts on its branches. Sam said, "Thank you, Bob! You are a very dependable friend." They both smiled and went back to their homes. From that day on, Bob and Sam were the best of friends. They always helped each other when they needed it. The other animals in the forest knew that they could depend on Bob and Sam too. And they all lived happily ever after.
