Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Bossy Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a bossy girl named Sue. Sue had a big toy that she loved. One day, Sue lost her toy. She was very sad. Sue asked her friend, Tom, to help her find the toy. Tom said, "We can mail a letter to ask for help." Sue and Tom wrote a letter and put it in a box. They went to the mail place and gave the box to the man with a big arm. The man with the big arm sent the box away. Sue and Tom waited and waited. One day, a box came back. They opened it, but the toy was not inside. Instead, there was a note that said, "Sorry, we can't find your toy." Sue and Tom were very sad, and the toy was lost forever.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a small boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who loved to play with his toy shield. He would run around the park, using his shield to protect himself from the wind and the sun. One day, Tim went to the park to play. He saw the sun rise high in the sky. He held up his shield and started to play. He ran and ran, laughing and having fun. He played with his friends and they all loved his shield. But then, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The wind started to blow and the rain began to fall. Tim tried to use his shield to stay dry, but it did not work. The rain was too strong and Tim got all wet. His shield could not stop the rain, and he had to go home sad and wet.

Tom and Mia are playing with magnets. They have a big box of magnets of different shapes and colors. They stick them on the fridge, on the table, on the chair, and on each other. "Look, Mia, I have a red star magnet!" Tom says, holding it up. "That's nice, Tom, but I have a blue heart magnet!" Mia says, showing hers. They trade magnets and try to make patterns and pictures with them. They have fun and laugh. Then, they see a small, fluffy kitten on the window sill. It has white fur and green eyes. It looks at them and meows. "Wow, a kitten!" Tom says, running to the window. "It's so adorable!" Mia says, following him. They open the window and the kitten jumps inside. It rubs against their legs and purrs. They pet it and hug it. "Can we keep it, please?" Tom and Mia say together, looking at their mom. Their mom smiles and nods. She says, "Yes, you can keep it, but you have to take good care of it. What will you name it?" Tom and Mia think for a moment. Then, they say, "We will name it Magnet, because it sticks to us like a magnet!"

There was a messy room. In the middle of the room was a big box with an arrow on it. The arrow was pointing to the door. Andy was a three year old who liked to push things. He walked over to the big box and gave it a big push. The box moved and it opened. Inside, there were lots of toys! Andy saw a big car and he jumped up and down with excitement. He pushed the car around the room and made car noises as he played. He also found a little arrow that matched the one on the box. Andy had a lot of fun playing with all of the toys. He pushed the car and the arrow around the room until it was even messier than before. When he was done, he closed the big box and pushed it back against the wall. Andy was very happy. He had a lot of fun making the room messy.

Once upon a time, in a cute little forest, a bunny named Bobo saw a trail. The trail had many pretty flowers and tall green trees. Bobo decided to hop on the trail and see where it went. As Bobo hopped along the trail, he saw his friend, Toto the turtle. Toto was slow, but he was very smart. Bobo said, "Hi Toto, do you want to hop with me on this trail?" Toto said, "Yes, let's go together!" Bobo and Toto hopped and walked on the trail. They saw many fun things like a big pond, a soft hill, and a tiny bug. They played together until it was time to go home. They were happy they could recognize the trail and have a fun day with each other.
