Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Boy and the Together

Once upon a time, there were two friends who were playing together. One was a young boy, and the other was a girl. The boy ran and zipped all around the room. His friend watched with wide eyes as he ran and jumped happily around. She was a bit jealous because she could not run as fast as he could. The boy saw that she was jealous, so he grabbed a jug from the shelf and gave it to her. The girl was so happy! She ran around the room with the jug, pretending to zip like the boy. The boy and girl played together for the whole day, enjoying their time together. They each took turns zipping around the room, and at the end, they sat and shared a drink from the jug. It was a wonderful day!

Tom and Lily liked to play with their bikes. They rode them fast and far, all around the park. One day, they saw a big hill in the west. They wanted to go up the hill and see what was there. "Let's go, Lily!" Tom said. "Maybe we can find something fun!" "OK, Tom!" Lily said. "But be careful, the hill looks steep and rocky." They pedaled hard and reached the top of the hill. They saw a big lake with ducks and fish. They also saw a broken bridge that crossed the lake. "Wow, look at that!" Tom said. "Do you think we can cross the bridge?" "I don't know, Tom," Lily said. "The bridge looks old and dirty. Maybe it is not safe." Tom was curious and brave. He wanted to try the bridge. He told Lily to wait for him. He rode his bike slowly on the bridge. He heard a loud crack. The bridge broke under his weight. He fell into the water with his bike. "Tom, Tom!" Lily shouted. "Are you OK?" She ran to the edge of the bridge. She saw Tom struggling in the water. He was wet and scared. He tried to swim to the shore, but his bike was too heavy. He sank deeper and deeper. "Help, help!" Tom cried. "I can't breathe!" Lily wanted to help Tom, but she did not know how to swim. She looked around for someone, but there was no one. She felt sad and helpless. She cried and cried. Tom did not come out of the water. He drowned with his bike. Lily lost her best friend. She wished they never went to the hill. She wished they could repair the bridge. She wished they could play again. But it was too late. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to play outside. But the sky was gray, and soon it began to rain. Lily was sad, so she put on her raincoat and boots. She thought, "Maybe I can still have fun in the rain!" Lily saw a big, unusual frog sitting in a puddle. The frog looked at her and said, "Hello, Lily! Would you like to invite your friends to play in the rain with me?" Lily was surprised that the frog could talk, but she thought it was a fun idea. Lily invited her friends, Sam and Mia, to play in the rain with the unusual frog. They all splashed in puddles, and the frog showed them how to catch raindrops on their tongues. The rain became a fun day for everyone, and they learned that even when things seem sad, something unexpected and happy can happen.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She liked to see all the things around her. She saw a big tree, a red ball, and a cute dog. Lucy was very happy. As she walked, she saw an old house. The old house had a small box near the door. Lucy went closer and saw a letter inside the box. She picked up the letter and looked at it. It had a pretty picture on it. Lucy wanted to know who the letter was for. She saw an old man sitting near the house. She asked him, "Is this your letter?" The old man smiled and said, "Yes, it is. Thank you, Lucy." Lucy gave the letter to the old man and they both smiled.
