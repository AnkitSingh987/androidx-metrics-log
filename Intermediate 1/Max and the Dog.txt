Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough dog. His name was Max. Max liked to play in the mud. One day, Max saw a little cat. The cat was sad. Max said, "Why are you sad, little cat?" The cat said, "I lost my name. I can't find it anywhere." Max wanted to help the cat find her name. They looked all around for the name. Then, something unexpected happened. They found the name in the mud! The cat was so happy. She said, "Thank you, Max! My name is Luna." Now, Max and Luna are best friends. They play in the mud together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a huge hall. In the hall, there was a big party. Many kids came to play and have fun. They ate cake, played games, and danced to fun songs. Everyone was happy and having a great time. A little girl named Lily wanted a balloon. She saw a big, red one that she liked. She went to her mom to ask if she could have it. Her mom said yes and they went to get the balloon. But when they got to the balloons, something unexpected happened. The big, red balloon was not a balloon at all! It was a big, red ball. Lily was sad at first, but then she had an idea. She asked her friends to play with the ball. They all laughed and played with the big, red ball. It was the best party ever!

Once there was a blue coin. It was beautiful and shiny, but no one dared to pick it up. They were scared it might be magic! But one brave little girl was not afraid. She knew it was just a special coin and she wanted it for herself. So she reached out her hand and she dared to pick it up. Instantly the girl was filled with joy! She ran off with the coin and showed it to all her friends. Everybody was so excited and happy for her. From then on, the little girl kept the blue coin near and dear. She took it everywhere with her, and would even hold it when she was going to bed. That blue coin was her special treasure, and every time she looked at it she was reminded of how brave she was to dare pick it up that day!

Once upon a time, there was a big bowl of pasta. The pasta was all mixed up. Some pasta was long, some pasta was short, and some pasta was twisty. They all lived together in the bowl, but they wanted to be separate. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily came to the bowl. She wanted to make it fair for all the pasta. So, Lily started to separate the pasta. She put the long pasta in one pile, the short pasta in another pile, and the twisty pasta in a third pile. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew all the pasta back into the bowl. The pasta was all mixed up again! The pasta was sad, but Lily had an idea. She said, "Maybe it's okay for all the pasta to be together. It's more fun that way!" And so, the pasta stayed mixed up, and they were all happy.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the garden. They liked to dig, water, and pick the plants. One day, they saw a big, round, orange squash. It was bigger than their heads. They wanted to take it home and show their mom. They tried to pull the squash, but it was too heavy. They tried to roll it, but it was too bumpy. They tried to push it, but it was too stuck. They did not know what to do. Then, they heard a loud yell. It was the farmer. He was angry. He saw them trying to take his squash. He ran towards them, waving his arms. He looked scary. Tom and Lily were alert. They knew they had to run. They dropped the squash and ran as fast as they could. They ran out of the garden and back to their house. They did not look back. They were safe, but they were sad. They did not get the squash. They did not show their mom. They learned a lesson. They should not take things that are not theirs. They should ask first. They should say sorry. Maybe next time, the farmer would let them have a squash. Maybe.
