Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blackboard and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a small room. The blackboard was very old and had many deep marks on it. The children who came to the room liked to draw on the blackboard with colorful chalk. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the room. She saw the blackboard and wanted to draw a big sun. But she could not decide which color to use. She thought and thought, and then she picked up the yellow chalk. Lily drew a big, happy sun on the blackboard. The sun had a big smile and bright rays. All the children in the room were happy to see Lily's sun. They clapped and laughed. The blackboard was happy too, because it was now full of love and colors.

Jill looked out of her bedroom window. She saw a mysterious butterfly fluttering around a big yellow flower in the garden. She was so excited that she quickly ran outside. When Jill got closer to the flower, she discovered a little pot of butter in the grass too. Jill looked around and saw no one around, so she decided to take the butter back to her bedroom. On the way back up to her room, Jill heard her mom calling out. "Jill, where did you get that butter from?" Jill was scared, so she kept quiet and tried to run away. But her mom stopped her. "I can see you have something mysterious going on, why don't you tell me what it is?" her mom said calmly. Jill knew it was time to tell the truth. She explained that she had found the butter by the flower. Her mom smiled and said, "Well, if the butter is meant to be yours, then I suppose it's okay for you to keep it. But remember not to keep going outside without telling me first." Jill thanked her mom, gave her a big hug and then went back to her room with the butter. She couldn't wait to start making something delicious with it!

Lily and Ben were playing in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. They gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. They were very happy. But then they saw a big black crow on a tree. The crow looked at the snowman and wanted the carrot. He flew down and tried to take it. Lily and Ben ran to the snowman and shouted at the crow. "Go away, crow! This is our snowman! You can't have his nose!" The crow did not listen. He pecked at the carrot and pulled it out. He flew away with it in his beak. Lily and Ben were very sad. They cried and hugged the snowman. A lady was walking by. She saw Lily and Ben and the snowman. She felt sorry for them. She was the queen of the land. She wore a shiny crown and a long coat. She had a magic wand in her hand. She came to Lily and Ben and smiled. "Hello, children. I am the queen. I saw what the crow did. That was very mean. Do you want me to help you?" Lily and Ben nodded. They wiped their tears. They liked the queen. She was very kind. The queen waved her wand. She made a new carrot nose for the snowman. She also made a big sign that said "This is Lily and Ben's snowman. Do not touch!" She put the sign next to the snowman. She turned to Lily and Ben and said, "There. Now your snowman is safe. But be careful. The crow might come back. He is very greedy. If you see him, warn me. I will chase him away with my wand." Lily and Ben thanked the queen. They hugged her and the snowman. They were very happy again. The queen smiled and said, "You're welcome, children. Have fun in the snow. But don't stay too long. It is very icy. You might catch a cold. Go home when you are tired. And remember, I am always here if you need me." Lily and Ben said goodbye to the queen. They played with the snowman until it was dark. They did not see the crow again. They went home and told their mom and dad about the queen and the crow and the snowman. They had a very good day.
