Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobo the Dog and the Bird

One day, a little dog named Bobo went for a walk. It was a wet day, and the rain made the grass and trees wet too. Bobo liked to jump in the wet puddles and get his paws wet. He saw a little bird in a tree and thought it was fun to follow the bird. As Bobo followed the bird, he found a snack on the ground. It was a yummy treat that someone left behind. Bobo was very happy to find the snack and ate it up fast. The bird saw Bobo eat the snack and wanted one too. Bobo and the bird became friends. They played in the wet grass and had more snacks together. They followed each other around all day, having fun in the rain. At the end of the day, Bobo and the bird went home to their warm, dry beds, happy and tired from their wet day of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a TV. The TV was high up on the wall. The TV liked to hang there and show fun things for kids to watch. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the TV and wanted to watch her favorite show. She said, "TV, please show me my favorite show." The TV felt sorry because it could not hear her. Lily's mom came into the room and saw Lily talking to the TV. She said, "I'm sorry, Lily. The TV can't hear you. But I can help you watch your favorite show." Lily's mom turned on the TV, and they watched the show together. They were both very happy.

Doug and Mommy were having a picnic when suddenly the sun started to rise. It was beautiful, clear and peaceful. But suddenly, something disturbed their tranquility. "What was that noise?" asked Mommy. "I don't know," replied Doug. It was not long before they spotted a curious little animal back in the bushes. It was a fox! Doug was scared but he stayed courageous. Mommy and Doug watched the fox for a few more minutes until it disappeared. They enjoyed the sunrise for some more time until it was time to go home. It had been quite an eventful morning, but nothing would disturb the peacefulness of the sunrise again.

Tom was a bad boy. He liked to take things that were not his. He took toys from other kids. He took food from the kitchen. He took money from his mom's purse. He did not care if he made others sad or angry. One day, Tom saw a shiny bike outside a store. He wanted the bike very much. He looked around and saw no one. He ran to the bike and tried to ride it away. But the bike was too big for him. He could not reach the pedals. He could not steer the handlebars. He could not balance the wheels. He fell off the bike and hurt his knee. A man saw Tom fall. He was the owner of the store and the bike. He was very mad at Tom. He grabbed Tom by the arm and dragged him inside the store. He called the police and told them what Tom did. He said Tom was a thief and a liar. He said Tom had to go to jail. The police came and took Tom away. They put him in a car with a loud siren. They drove him to a big building with bars and locks. They put him in a small room with a bed and a toilet. They said this was his cell. He had to stay there until he learned his lesson. He could not go out or play or see his mom. He could only look at the black walls and the black door and the black window. Tom was very scared and sorry. He wished he had not taken the bike. He wished he had been a good boy. He wished he could go home. He cried and cried. He did not like his cell. He did not like the black. He struggled to sleep. He struggled to eat. He struggled to hope. He wondered if anyone would ever forgive him. He wondered if he would ever be happy again.
