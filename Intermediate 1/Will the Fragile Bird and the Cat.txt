Category: Intermediate
Title: Will the Fragile Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a fragile bird named Will. Will lived in a small nest up in a tree. One day, it was very cold outside, and Will started to shiver. A big, mean cat saw Will shiver in the tree. The cat wanted to catch Will and make him stop shivering. The cat climbed the tree and tried to get Will. Will was scared and tried to fly away, but the mean cat was fast. The cat caught Will and made him stop shivering. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a nice old man. He was wearing a mild suit, and he was carrying a sack. One day, the old man went to a park. In the park there were kids playing tag. The old man smiled and said, "I wish I could play with them!" Suddenly, the old man had an idea. He reached into his sack and pulled out a big blue ball. He said, "Kids, would you like to play a game?" The kids stopped playing tag, and they all crowded around the old man. One of the kids asked, "What do we do, old man?" The old man smiled and said, "We can play catch with this ball. Do you want to try?" The kids all cheered. They had so much fun, throwing the ball back and forth in the warm sunshine. When they were finished, the old man said, "Thank you for playing with me. I really enjoyed it!" The kids said goodbye and ran off. The old man smiled and put the ball back in his sack. He walked away, feeling happy and mild.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved her book. It had many fun pictures and stories. Lily read her book every day. One day, her little brother Tom found the book. He did not know it was Lily's special book. Tom started to draw on it. He spoiled the book with his pen. The book was not the same anymore. Lily was sad when she saw her book. She told Tom that he spoiled it. Tom was sorry. He said he would help fix the book. They worked together to make it better. Lily was grateful for Tom's help. They learned to share and take care of their things.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot was famous because he could guess things very well. Everyone loved Spot, and he had many friends. One day, Spot and his friends decided to make a model. They used blocks to build a big tower. Spot guessed how many blocks they would need. He was right, and they had a lot of fun. In the end, Spot and his friends were very happy with their model. They played together all day and had a great time. Spot was a good friend, and his guessing skills made him even more famous.

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom went for a tour in the park. He saw many things like trees, flowers, and other animals. Tom liked the park very much. Tom met a bird named Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tom! Do you want to play with me?" Tom was sleepy but decided to play with Sue. They played hide and seek, and Tom felt happy. After playing, Tom and Sue decided to rest under a big tree. They talked about their fun day in the park. Soon, Tom felt very sleepy. He said, "Sue, I am sleepy. I want to go home now." Sue said, "Okay Tom, see you again soon!" Tom went home and had a good sleep.

Once upon a time there was a bald man. Every morning he would look up at the sky and smile when he saw the sunrise. He loved to watch the orange and pink colours in the sky and feel very excited. Then he would go outside. He would fill his lungs with air and run as fast as he could towards the pond. He enjoyed taking a big dive into the cool water. He used to stay there until the sun was high in the sky. When it was time to go home, he would look up at the sky and enjoy the beauty of another sunrise. He would get up from the pond, smile and begin another morning.
