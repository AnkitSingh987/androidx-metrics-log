Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Smart Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to peek at things. One day, he saw a big wreck in his yard. It was a big mess. Tom wanted to find out what made the wreck. Tom walked around the wreck and found a big box. He thought the box was very interesting. Tom wanted to open the box and see what was inside. He thought it would be a fun surprise. But when Tom opened the box, out came a big, scary dog! The dog chased Tom away from the wreck. Tom ran as fast as he could. At the end, Tom learned not to open boxes without knowing what was inside.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a grill by a big tree. Tim's mom said, "We need to clean this grill before we cook on it." Tim wanted to help, so he got a bucket of water and a cloth. Tim washed the grill very slow. He wanted to make sure it was very clean. His mom said, "Good job, Tim! Now we can cook on the grill." They put some food on the grill and cooked it. While they waited for the food to cook, they played a game of catch. Tim threw the ball to his mom, and she caught it. They laughed and had fun. When the food was ready, they sat down to eat. Tim was happy that he helped his mom clean the grill.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow house. In this house, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to read her favorite book. It was a book about a funny cat. She would read it every day. One day, Sue could not find her book. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and behind the big chair. But the book was not there. Sue was sad. She wanted to read her book about the funny cat. Then, Sue saw her little brother, Tim. Tim was holding her book! Sue was happy to see her book, but it did not seem right. The book was now green, not yellow! Tim had painted it while playing. Sue was mad at first, but then she laughed. The funny cat in the book would like being green too. Sue and Tim read the book together, and they both had fun.

One day, a little cat named Mimi went for a walk. She liked to follow the birds and play with her friends. Mimi had big ears that could hear a lot of things. She was a very happy cat. As Mimi was walking, she saw a big dog named Max. Max looked sad. Mimi walked up to Max and asked, "Why are you sad, Max?" Max said, "I'm sad because I lost my ball. I can't find it anywhere." Mimi wanted to help Max find his ball. Mimi used her big ears to listen for the ball. She heard a sound and started to follow it. Max followed Mimi as they looked for the ball. They found the ball near a bitter lemon tree. Max was so happy! He thanked Mimi and they played together for the rest of the day.

One day, Tom the cat was on an adventure in the forest. He was marching around, looking for something new. Suddenly he heard a sound. He looked around and saw a little animal marching through the trees. Tom asked what the little animal was doing. The animal replied that he was looking for a special twig. Tom asked what kind of twig. The animal told him it was an open twig. Tom and the animal decided to go together and search the forest for the special twig. They marched through the trees, Tom looking high in the branches, and the animal looking low on the ground. After a while, the animal spotted an open twig. He showed it to Tom and they both cheered! The twig was special - it made them both smile. They thanked each other for the great adventure and said goodbye. Tom continued his march through the forest, happy with the wonderful twig he'd found.
