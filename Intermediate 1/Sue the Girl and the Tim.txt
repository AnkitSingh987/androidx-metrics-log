Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to dare her friends to do things. One day, she dared her friend, Tim, to go into a big house. The big house belonged to a rich lady. The rich lady had lots of pretty jewelry. Tim went into the house when the rich lady was not home. He found the pretty jewelry and took it. He wanted to show Sue the pretty jewelry. But, the rich lady saw Tim and got mad. The rich lady yelled at Tim and Sue. She took her pretty jewelry back. Tim and Sue felt bad for what they did. They said sorry, but the rich lady did not forgive them. Tim and Sue were sad and learned not to dare each other to do bad things.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. He lived in a pond with his best friend, a fish named Sam. Tim and Sam liked to play together every day. They loved to see the sunrise. The sunrise was very pretty and made the sky pink and orange. One day, Tim and Sam were playing near the pond when they saw a big turtle. The turtle said, "Hi, I'm Tom. Can I play with you?" Tim and Sam said, "Yes!" They played all day long. The weather was mild, so they did not feel too hot or too cold. As they played, they heard a loud snap. They looked around and saw a big tree had fallen near the pond. Tim, Sam, and Tom were scared. But then, they saw a little squirrel jump out from the tree. The squirrel said, "I'm sorry, I was trying to get a nut and the branch broke." They all laughed and became friends. From that day on, they all played together every day and watched the beautiful sunrise together.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat. The cat had soft fur. One day, the cat felt very tired. She wanted to rest. The cat went to her friend, the dog. The cat said, "Dog, I am tired. Can I rest on your fur?" The dog said, "Yes, cat. You can rest on my fur." The cat lay down on the dog's fur. The cat was happy. She was persistent and did not give up until she found a good place to rest. The cat and dog were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, open field. In the field, there were many animals. They liked to play and have fun together. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The field was a happy place for all the animals. One day, a small bird was sad. It lost its toy in the tall grass. The bird looked and looked, but it could not find the toy. The other animals saw the sad bird and wanted to help. They all looked for the toy together. The big bear found the toy and gave it to the bird. The bird was so happy and thanked the bear. The bear gave the bird a big hug to comfort it. All the animals in the open field were happy again. They played and had fun together, knowing they would always help each other.

One day, a big bunny named Bob went to the garden to test if his radishes were ready to eat. He saw a very big radish that was red and round. He was very happy because he loved to eat big radishes. Bob pulled and pulled, but the big radish did not come out of the ground. Bob asked his friend, a bird named Bill, to help him pull the big radish. They pulled and pulled, but the big radish still did not come out. They were very tired but did not want to give up. They asked their friend, a cat named Cathy, to help them. Cathy, Bill, and Bob pulled and pulled with all their might. Suddenly, the big radish came out of the ground, and they all fell down. But it was not a radish! It was a big red ball that looked like a radish. They all laughed and played with the ball. It was a fun and unexpected day in the garden.
