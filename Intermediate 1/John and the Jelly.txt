Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Jelly

John was a little boy. He was three years old and had lots of things he liked to do. Today, he decided to go for a walk. John walked for a long time but he couldn't see much. Then, he saw something in the distance. He stopped and looked. John was excited. He saw a jelly! It looked so tasty. He wanted to go and get it. John ran to the jelly and pointed at it. "Look at this jelly! Can I eat it?" he asked. John's mom smiled. "Yes, you can eat it. But remember, we have to be quiet," she said softly. John nodded and carefully picked up the jelly. Then he quietly ate it. It was delicious! John was happy. He had eaten a tasty jelly and had been very quiet. He smiled and skipped away. What an adventure!

Once upon a time, there was a light blue bird named Billy. Billy loved to collect things. He collected sticks, leaves, and stones. One day, Billy saw a big mill near his home. The mill was where people made flour from grain. Billy flew to the mill and met a kind man named Tom. "Hello, I am Tom," said the man. "What do you do?" asked Billy. "I work at the mill and make flour," Tom replied. Billy wanted to help Tom, so he decided to collect grain for the mill. Billy asked his friends, the ants, to help him collect grain. Together, they picked up the grain and carried it to the mill. Tom was very happy with their help. He thanked Billy and the ants for their hard work. From that day on, Billy and the ants collected grain for the mill every day. They became good friends with Tom and loved helping him. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a very intelligent girl. She liked to play with her toys on the big carpet in her room. One day, Amy's mom said she had a surprise for her. Amy was very excited. She went to her room and saw a big box on the carpet. She opened the box and saw a new toy inside. It was a pretty doll with a pink dress. Amy loved her new doll and played with it all day. At night, Amy put the doll on the carpet next to her other toys. She went to sleep with a big smile on her face. The next morning, she woke up and played with her new doll again. Amy was very happy with her surprise.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They liked to watch the birds and the squirrels play in the trees. Tim loved to hear the happy voice of his mom as they played together. But today, there was a small bird in the park that looked upset. The bird could not find its mom. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he and his mom looked all around the park. They listened for the bird's mom's voice. After a while, they heard a voice from a tree. It was the upset bird's mom! They watched the little bird fly to its mom. The bird was not upset anymore. Tim and his mom were happy that they could help the bird find its mom. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a small ranch. On the ranch, there was a big pond. In the pond, there lived a happy fish named Splash. Splash loved to play with his friends in the water. One day, Splash and his friends found a sour apple near the pond. Splash's friend, a small frog named Hop, said, "Let's eat the sour apple!" But Splash said, "No, we should ask who it belongs to." They asked around the ranch and found out that the sour apple belonged to a kind horse named Gallop. Gallop was very happy that Splash and Hop returned his sour apple. He said, "Thank you, Splash and Hop! You did the right thing!" The moral of the story is to always be honest and do the right thing, even if it is not easy.
