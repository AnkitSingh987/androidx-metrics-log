Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Mia saw a big yellow ball in the sky. She looked up and said, "Wow, that is so pretty!" Mia started to wonder where the yellow ball came from. She wanted to find out, so she put on her shoes and went for a walk. As Mia walked, she saw a sign. The sign had a picture of the big yellow ball and an arrow. Mia thought, "This must be the way to find the big yellow ball!" So, she followed the sign and walked where the arrow pointed. Mia walked and walked until she found a big hill. She climbed the hill and saw the big yellow ball up close. It was the sun! Mia smiled and said, "I found you!" She sat on the hill and watched the sun go down. Then, she went home and told her mom all about her adventure.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a big tree with a strange object under it. The object was round and green. Tim was very curious, so he picked it up. He took a bite of the object, and it tasted sour. Tim did not like the taste, but he wanted to show his friends. He put the sour object in his bag and went to find his friends. Tim found his friends and showed them the sour object. They all wanted to try it too. But when they took a bite, they all felt sick. They had to lie down on the ground. Tim felt very sad that his friends were not feeling well. The sour object was not a good thing to share after all.

One day, a small bird found a flute in the woods. The bird was very independent and liked to play by itself. It picked up the flute with its beak and started to play a happy song. The bird felt proud of its new skill. As the bird played the flute, a rabbit heard the music and came to listen. The rabbit said, "Wow, you play so well! Can you teach me how to play the flute?" The bird agreed and said, "First, you need to spring high and grab the flute with your paws." The rabbit tried to jump, but it couldn't reach the flute. The bird saw the rabbit's struggle and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The bird put the flute on the ground, and the rabbit tried to play it. The rabbit didn't play as well as the bird, but it was still fun. They played the flute together, and their music filled the woods. In the end, the independent bird and the rabbit became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. In the stadium, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He liked to play and explore all around the stadium. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. "Hi, I'm Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim asked Sam. "Sure, I'm Sam! Let's play!" Sam replied. They played together all day, running and jumping in the stadium. But then, something unexpected happened. While they were playing, they accidentally knocked down a big wall. The wall started to destroy part of the stadium. Tim and Sam were scared. They didn't know what to do. But then, they had an idea. They asked all their friends in the stadium to help them fix the wall. Everyone worked together, and they made the stadium nice and strong again. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends. They promised to be more careful when they played, but they still had many adventurous days in the stadium.

One day, a person went to the park. He saw a big red ball. The person wanted to play with the ball. He was very patient and waited for his turn. When it was his turn, the person tried to pull the ball. But the ball did not move. He pulled harder. The ball still did not move. The person was confused. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball turned into a big, scary monster! The monster chased the person out of the park. The person was sad and never went back to the park again.
