Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Car

Sara and Ben like to play with blocks. They make towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They have many colors of blocks: red, blue, yellow, green and more. One day, Sara and Ben want to make a big wall with the blocks. They take all the blocks they can find and start to build the wall. They make the wall very long and very high. They are happy with their wall. But then, Tom comes to play with them. Tom is Sara's brother and he is older. He does not like the wall. He says it is silly and boring. He wants to take the blocks and make something else. He tries to take some blocks from the wall, but they are tight. They do not come off easily. Sara and Ben see Tom and they are angry. They say, "No, Tom! Do not take our blocks! They are ours! We made the wall! Go away!" They push Tom away from the wall. Tom is mad too. He says, "Fine! I do not want your blocks! I will take something else!" He looks around and sees Sara's doll and Ben's car. He runs to them and takes them. He says, "Ha ha! Now I have your doll and your car! You can keep your wall!" Sara and Ben are very sad. They cry and say, "Tom, give us back our doll and our car! They are ours! You are mean!" They chase Tom, but he is faster. He runs away with the doll and the car. Sara and Ben do not know what to do. They have their wall, but they do not have their doll and their car. They do not want to play with the wall anymore. They want their doll and their car back. They wish Tom would be nice and share.

One day, a big alligator went to the park. He wanted to join the other animals to play. But the animals were scared of the alligator. They were not sure if he was nice or mean. The alligator said, "Can I join you to play? I promise I will be careful." The animals thought about it and decided to let the alligator play with them. They played tag and hide-and-seek. The alligator was very careful not to hurt anyone. Soon, the animals learned that the alligator was a good friend. They were happy they let him join their games. The alligator was happy too. The moral of the story is to not judge others by how they look and to give everyone a chance to be a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a fast car. This car was very good at helping people. It could provide help when people were sick or hurt. The car had a big job to do, and it was very good at its job. One day, the fast car got a call. A little girl was sick and needed to go to the hospital. The car went to her house as fast as it could. The little girl and her mom got in the car. The car was very fast, and they got to the hospital very quickly. At the hospital, the doctors and nurses helped the little girl feel better. The fast car was happy that it could provide help to the little girl and her mom. The car went back to its home, ready to help more people when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to cook with her mom. One day, they wanted to cook beef for dinner. Sue was stubborn. She wanted to cook the beef all by herself. But her mom said, "No, it is too hot. You need help." Sue did not like this. She got mad and crossed her arms. Her mom had an idea. "Let's cook the beef together," she said. Sue thought about it and said yes. They cooked the beef and had a yummy dinner. Sue was happy she worked with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to crawl and play all day. One day, Kitty saw a big refrigerator in the kitchen. She thought it was a fun place to hide. Kitty crawled behind the refrigerator and found a small ball. She played with the ball for a long time. But then, the ball rolled away and Kitty could not find it. She was sad and wanted her ball back. The next day, Kitty's friend, a little boy named Tim, came to visit. Tim saw the ball by the refrigerator. He picked it up and gave it back to Kitty. Kitty was so happy to have her ball again. She and Tim played together all day long.
