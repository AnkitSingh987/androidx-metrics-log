Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Ring

Anna and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a gold ring with a big stone. Anna picked it up and put it on her finger. "Wow, it is so pretty!" she said. "I admire it a lot. Do you want to try it?" Ben nodded and reached for the ring. But Anna pulled her hand away. "No, it is mine!" she said. "I found it first. You can't have it." Ben felt sad and angry. He wanted to share the ring with Anna. He thought she was a reliable friend, but she was not. He decided to leave and find another friend. "Fine, then. I don't want to play with you anymore. You are mean and selfish," he said. He turned around and walked away. Anna felt happy and proud. She had the gold ring and she did not need anyone else. She admired it on her finger and smiled. But then, a big dog came running towards her. It saw the shiny thing and wanted to play with it. It jumped on Anna and bit her hand. It took the ring and ran away. Anna screamed and cried. She had lost the ring and her friend. She was hurt and alone. She wished she had been nicer to Ben. She learned a hard lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy named Stitch. Stitch was a small, soft bear. Amy and Stitch were best friends. They played and laughed all day long. One day, Amy and Stitch went outside. It was a cold and icy day. They played in the snow and had fun. Amy said, "Stitch, do you like the snow?" Stitch didn't say anything because he was a toy, but Amy knew he was happy. As they played, a big snowball rolled towards them. It was so big that it picked up Stitch! Amy was scared and yelled, "Stitch, where are you?" The big snowball stopped, and Stitch popped out. He was now a big, icy bear! Amy was surprised but happy to see her friend. They laughed and played together in the snow with their new icy friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very polite. She always said her pleases and thank yous and was very kind to everyone she met. One cold winter day it began to snow. The little girl put on her mittens and went outside to play. All of a sudden she heard a crying sound coming from the snow. She began to dig and soon she found a small puppy. She took him back inside and soon learnt he was very cold and scared. She put him close to the fire and gave him something warm to eat and drink. The little girl was so polite and kind to the puppy that he stopped crying. The moral of the story is that being polite and kind can bring warmth and joy to someone who is sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very good at counting. He liked to count everything he saw. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. At the park, he saw many things to count. At the park, Tim saw a long, flexible snake. The snake was friendly and could bend in many ways. Tim had a question for the snake. He asked, "How many times can you bend?" The snake was happy to show Tim and bent many times. Tim counted each bend and smiled. After counting the snake's bends, Tim went to play with his friends. They played and had fun all day. Tim counted how many times they jumped, ran, and laughed. When it was time to go home, Tim was happy. He loved counting, and he had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They all wanted to play with the football too. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Can I play with the football?" Tim felt jealous because he wanted to play with the football by himself. But he knew he should share, so he answered, "Yes, you can play with the football." All the friends played with the football and had lots of fun. Tim was happy that he shared his football with his friends. They all played until it was time to go home. Tim learned that sharing made playing with the football even more fun.
