Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Park

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a red ice cream truck. He wanted ice cream, so his mom gave him a ticket to get some. Tim walked to the truck and said, "I want to use my ticket for ice cream, please." The ice cream man smiled and gave Tim a big red ice cream cone. Tim was very happy. Tim started to eat his ice cream, but it was a very hot day. His ice cream began to melt. Tim's mom said, "Eat it fast, or it will freeze!" Tim ate his ice cream as fast as he could, and it did not freeze. Tim and his mom laughed and played in the park all day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He had a big, red kite. Tim wanted to fly it high in the sky. A nosy squirrel saw Tim and wanted to play too. Tim started to march to make the kite fly. The nosy squirrel marched with him. "Go away, squirrel!" said Tim. But the squirrel did not listen. It wanted to play with the kite. The kite went up and up. But then, something unexpected happened. The kite got stuck in a tree! Tim was sad. The nosy squirrel climbed the tree and got the kite down. Tim was happy again. "Thank you, squirrel!" said Tim. Now, they both played with the kite, marching and laughing together.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were spies and had secret missions. They used sticks and rocks as their weapons. They were careful not to hurt anyone or break anything. "Look, Ben, I found a big stick. It can be my sword," Lily said, holding up a long branch. "Wow, that's a good weapon, Lily. But be careful, it might be sharp. Let me see," Ben said, reaching for the stick. "No, it's mine. You have your own weapon. Find another stick," Lily said, pulling the stick away. "Hey, don't be mean. We are on the same team. We have to share our weapons," Ben said, feeling angry. "No, we don't. I found it first. It's mine," Lily said, feeling stubborn. They started to argue and tug at the stick. They forgot to have fun and relax. They made loud noises and faces. Their mom heard them and came over. She saw them fighting over the stick. She was not happy. "Ben, Lily, what are you doing? Why are you fighting over a stick? That's not a nice way to play. You have to be kind and share with each other. And you have to be careful with your weapons. You could hurt someone or yourself," she said, taking the stick away. "Sorry, mom," Ben and Lily said, looking down. "Come on, let's go to the swings. You can relax and have fun there. And you can use your imagination to make up new stories. How about that?" she said, smiling. "Okay, mom," Ben and Lily said, nodding. They followed their mom to the swings. They put their weapons down and got on the swings. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They laughed and smiled. They relaxed and had fun. They were friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock was enormous! It lived in a park with many trees and flowers. The rock liked to watch kids play and listen to the birds sing. One sunny day, a little girl came to the park. She had a box of crayons. The girl sat near the rock and started to draw pretty pictures. She used a red crayon to draw a big flower and a blue crayon to draw the sky. The enormous rock liked the girl's pictures. It wanted to help her draw. So, the rock picked up a green crayon with its tiny rock hands. Together, they drew a beautiful park with trees, flowers, and birds. The girl and the rock became good friends and drew pictures every day.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a board. He loved to draw on the board with his chalk. One day, Bobo went to the park with his board. At the park, Bobo saw a big tree. He put his board under the tree and started to draw. He drew a sun, a house, and a cat. Bobo was very happy with his drawings. But then, something happened! A strong wind came and blew Bobo's board away. Bobo was sad. He looked and looked for his board, but he could not find it. Just when he was about to cry, a nice girl found the board and gave it back to Bobo. Bobo was so happy to have his board back. He thanked the girl and went back to drawing under the tree. From that day on, Bobo and the girl became good friends. They played and drew on the board together every day. And they lived happily ever after.
