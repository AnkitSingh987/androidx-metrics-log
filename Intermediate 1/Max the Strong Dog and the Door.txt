Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Strong Dog and the Door

One day, a strong dog named Max saw an elevator. He wanted to go up. Max had a big toy he wanted to share with his friend. His friend lived in a tall home. Max had never been in an elevator before. Max went into the elevator and lay his toy down. He pushed the button to go up. As the elevator went up, Max said, "I hope my friend likes my toy." The elevator stopped and the door opened. Max picked up his toy and went to his friend's door. He knocked on the door, and his friend opened it. Max said, "I brought you a toy!" His friend smiled and said, "Thank you, Max!" They played with the toy all day. Max was happy he used the elevator to share his toy with his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big swan. The swan lived in a pond. The pond was dull, but the swan liked it. One day, the swan saw a small toy near the pond. The toy was too heavy for the swan. The swan wanted to lift the toy, but it was hard. The swan tried again and again. At last, the swan could lift the toy. The swan was happy. The swan played with the toy all day long.

Lily liked to play in the garden. She liked to look at the flowers and the bugs. She liked to make up stories about the fairies that lived there. She wished she could see a real fairy. One day, she saw something shiny in the grass. She went closer and saw a little wing. She moved the grass and saw a chubby fairy. The fairy had a pink dress and a crown. She was sleeping. Lily was very surprised. She wanted to say hello, but she did not want to wake the fairy. She decided to leave a gift for the fairy. She picked some berries and flowers and made a necklace. She put it next to the fairy and smiled. The fairy opened her eyes and saw Lily. She saw the necklace and smiled too. She thanked Lily for the gift and said she was a kind girl. She said she was tired from flying and needed a nap. She asked Lily if she wanted to be her friend. Lily was very happy. She nodded and said yes. She said she loved fairies and stories. The fairy said she loved stories too. She said she would tell Lily a story when she woke up. She asked Lily to stay with her until then. Lily agreed and lay down next to the fairy. She felt the fairy's warm wing on her arm. She closed her eyes and listened to the fairy's soft breathing. She felt very cozy and safe. She fell asleep with a smile.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hard steel ball. It lived in a park with many kids. The kids loved to play with the steel ball. They would push it, kick it, and even try to joke with it. The ball was very happy. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to play with the ball. He pushed it and laughed as it rolled around. He tried to joke with the ball, but it did not talk back. Tim was not happy. He wanted the ball to joke with him. Then, something unexpected happened. The steel ball started to talk! It said, "I can joke with you, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. Now, the steel ball and Tim joked together all day long. They became best friends and had lots of fun at the park.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big tower far away. Max wanted to see the tower up close, so he started to follow the path to the tower. As Max got closer, he saw a weak old man sitting by the tower. The old man looked sad. Max wanted to help him, so he went to the old man and wagged his tail. The old man smiled and pet Max. Suddenly, the weak old man stood up and became a big, strong superhero! He thanked Max for making him happy again. Max and the superhero played all day by the tower. The superhero flew Max high in the sky, and they had lots of fun together.
