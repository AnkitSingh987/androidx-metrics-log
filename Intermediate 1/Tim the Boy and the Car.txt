Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very messy boy. His room was always filthy. One day, his mom told him to clean his room before going to bed. Tim did not want to clean his room. He wanted to play with his toys. He did not listen to his mom. So, when it was time for bed, his room was still very messy. That night, Tim went to bed. But he forgot that his toys were all over the floor. When he got up to get a drink of water, he did not see his toy car. Tim stepped on the toy car and he slipped. He fell down and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and his mom came to help him. She told him that if he had cleaned his room, he would not have slipped. Tim learned that it is important to listen to his mom and keep his room clean.

Once upon a time there was a tiny delicate bird. He liked to run along the forest paths and sing beautiful songs. One day, he was running along the path and he saw some plastic on the ground. He thought it looked shiny, so he decided to pick it up. As he grabbed it, it got caught in his delicate feathers. The bird started to panic and he tugged at the plastic. He was too scared to fly away. Suddenly, a mean fox came around the corner. The fox saw the bird and quickly ran towards him. The poor bird tried to run away, but it was too late. The fox grabbed the bird in his jaws and would not let go. The bird hurriedly said "please, don't hurt me! I didn't mean to take the plastic". But the fox didn't care. He wanted the bird and he was going to have him. The fox took the bird and ran away with him. This story teaches us that if we are ever tempted to take something that isn't ours, we have to be careful. We don't know what the consequences could be and the outcome might not be good. So it's better to be safe than sorry and think twice before taking something that isn't ours.

One day, a big, rough rock wanted to move. It was tired of sitting in the same spot all the time. It looked around and saw a bird flying high in the sky. The rock thought, "I want to move like that bird!" The rock called out to the bird, "Hey bird, can you help me move?" The bird flew down and said, "I can try, but you are very big and heavy. I will need help from my friends." So the bird called all its friends, and they all came to help the rock. Together, the birds picked up the rock and started to move it. They moved it to the east, where the sun rises. The rock was so happy to finally move and see new things. It thanked the birds and made new friends in its new home.

Once upon a time there was a poor rhinoceros. He did not belong anywhere. No matter where he went, he did not feel at home. One day, the rhinoceros came up to an old house and felt like, this was where he did belong. He knocked on the door and heard a small voice. "Who is it?" asked the voice. "It's me, a poor rhinoceros," said the rhinoceros. The door opened and a small 3-year-old girl appeared. She said, "You belong here! Come in and be my friend!" The rhinoceros was so happy to have a home and a friend. From that day on, the girl and the rhinoceros were the best of friends. They were always there for each other, no matter what.

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. She had a lot of gear to play with, like a ball, a jump rope, and a toy car. One day, it rained a lot, and all of Sue's gear got wet. She was sad and wanted to play with her gear again. So, she thought of a way to make her gear dry. Sue put all her wet gear in the sun. The sun was warm and bright. Slowly, her gear became dry. Sue was happy to see her gear dry and ready to play with. She played all day long with her dry gear, and had lots of fun in the lively little town.
