Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Generous Boy and the Gift

One day, a generous boy named Tim went to the grocery store with his mom. They wanted to buy food for their family. Tim liked to help his mom pick out the food they needed. At the store, Tim saw an old lady who needed help. She could not reach the food on the top shelf. Tim said, "I can help you!" He helped the old lady get the food she needed. The old lady was very happy and thanked Tim. As they were leaving the store, the old lady told Tim that she was a magic lady. She wanted to thank him for his help. So, she said, "I will send you a surprise gift tonight!" Tim was excited and couldn't wait for the gift. When he went to bed that night, he found a big toy truck waiting for him. Tim was happy and knew that helping others was a good thing to do.

Tommy was playing in the park with his friends when one of them pointed at a large tree. He was so excited to explore it but when they got closer, he saw a big knot in the trunk. It immediately made him shiver and he was upset. He didn't want to go near the tree and made his way back home. later that night, he heard a scary noise outside of his window and quickly ran to his parents room. As he got there, the colors of the knot caught his eye and he realized that the noise had come from the tree. His friends had been playing in its branches as a prank. He laughed and felt relieved as he thought about the big knot and his shivering earlier.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. One day, Max saw a big ball rolling in the forest. Max was very happy and wanted to follow the ball. He was so excited that he became careless. As Max followed the ball, he did not see the big hole in the ground. Max fell into the hole and could not get out. He felt scared and sad. Max needed help to get out of the hole. A kind and helpful bird saw Max in the hole. The bird flew away to find help. Soon, the bird came back with Max's friends. They all worked together to help Max out of the hole. Max was so happy and thankful to his friends for the win. Max learned to be more careful when he played in the forest. He promised to watch where he was going and not be careless again. Max and his friends played happily in the forest, but they always remembered to be careful.

Tom and Ben are brothers. They like to play with their toys in the yard. But sometimes they fight over the same toy. One day, they both want to play with the hammer. The hammer is big and shiny and makes a loud noise when it hits something. "Give me the hammer!" Tom says. He tries to grab it from Ben's hand. "No, it's mine!" Ben says. He holds the hammer tight and swings it at Tom. "Ow!" Tom cries. He falls to the ground and his nose bleeds. He is very angry and sad. Mom hears the noise and runs to the yard. She sees Tom and Ben and the hammer. She is very mad and scared. "What are you doing?" she yells. "You could have hurt each other badly! Why did you fight over the hammer?" Tom and Ben look at each other. They feel ashamed and sorry. "We both wanted to play with it," Tom says. "We were jealous of each other," Ben says. Mom sighs. She hugs them and cleans Tom's nose. She takes the hammer and puts it away. "You can't play with the hammer anymore," she says. "It's not a toy. It's a tool for grown-ups. You have to share your toys and be nice to each other. Do you understand?" Tom and Ben nod. They say they are sorry and hug each other. They decide to play with something else. They don't fight over the hammer anymore.
