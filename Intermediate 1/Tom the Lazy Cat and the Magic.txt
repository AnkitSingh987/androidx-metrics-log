Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lazy Cat and the Magic

One day, a lazy cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy a toy. Tom saw a snake toy and said, "I want that one!" A little boy named Tim was in the store too. He saw the same snake toy and said, "I want that one too!" Tom and Tim both wanted the toy, but there was only one left. Tom and Tim decided to share the snake toy. But when they tried to play with it, the toy came to life! The snake said, "I am a magic snake, and I will grant you a wish." Tom and Tim were very surprised. Tom wished for a big pile of cat food, and Tim wished for a new bike. The magic snake granted their wishes, and they were both very happy. They thanked the snake and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a foolish lawyer. He liked to open and close doors all the time. One day, he went to a big house with many doors. He went inside the house and started to open all the doors. He opened the first door and saw a big dog. The dog barked at him, but he was not scared. He opened the second door and saw a cat. The cat hissed at him, but he was not scared. Then, he opened the third door. Behind the door, there was a big dark room. The foolish lawyer went inside. He could not see anything. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise and fell into a big hole. The foolish lawyer could not get out of the hole and he stayed there forever. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat named Kitty. One day, Kitty was feeling miserable. She didn't like her spot near the toilet. Lily saw Kitty's sad face and asked, "Kitty, why are you so sad?" Kitty replied, "I don't like my spot near the toilet. It's not fun here." Lily thought for a moment and then said, "Let's find you a new spot to play!" So, they looked around the house for a fun place for Kitty. Lily found a big, sunny window. "Kitty, let's stretch your legs and play here!" she said. Kitty felt happy in the warm sun. They played together, and Kitty was not miserable anymore. Lily learned that helping a friend can make both of them happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily like to play with cardboard boxes. They find many boxes in the garage and make a big fort. They cut windows and doors and draw pictures on the walls. They pretend they are kings and queens of the fort. One day, it rains a lot. Tom and Lily want to play in the fort, but it is wet. They run to the garage and see the boxes are soaked. The cardboard is soft and saggy and falls apart. They are sad and cry. Dad sees them and hugs them. He says he has a surprise for them. He takes them to the backyard and shows them a tall wooden playhouse. It has a roof and windows and doors and a slide. It is not wet from the rain. He says he made it for them. Tom and Lily are happy and hug Dad. They thank him and run to the playhouse. They climb and slide and laugh. They forget about the cardboard fort. They have a new and better fort. They are kings and queens of the playhouse.

Anna and Ben went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. They saw many things on the beach, like shells, rocks, and birds. One day, they saw a man with a big basket. He was selling crabs. Anna and Ben wanted to see the crabs. They ran to the man and asked, "Can we look at the crabs?" The man smiled and said, "Sure, you can look. But be careful, they can pinch you with their claws." He opened the basket and showed them the crabs. They were red and brown and had many legs and eyes. Anna and Ben thought they were funny and scary at the same time. "Can we buy a crab?" Anna asked. "We want to take it home and play with it." The man shook his head and said, "No, you can't buy a crab. These crabs are for eating, not for playing. They are smelly and hard to keep. You need a big tank and salt water and food for them. They are not good pets." Anna and Ben felt sad. They liked the crabs and wanted to have one. But they understood that the man was right. They said, "Thank you for showing us the crabs. They are very interesting." The man said, "You're welcome. You are very polite and curious. Maybe you can find something else on the beach to play with. Have a nice day." He closed the basket and walked away. Anna and Ben waved goodbye to the man and the crabs. They went back to Mom and Dad and told them what they saw. Mom and Dad hugged them and said, "We're proud of you for being respectful and curious. You learned something new today. Maybe we can buy you a toy crab instead. Would you like that?" Anna and Ben nodded and smiled. They liked the idea of a toy crab. They said, "Yes, please. We love crabs. But only the toy ones."
