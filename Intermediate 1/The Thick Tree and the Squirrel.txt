Category: Intermediate
Title: The Thick Tree and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a thick tree in a big forest. The tree was very strong and had many branches. One day, a little squirrel was playing on the tree. The squirrel liked to jump and run on the branches. The squirrel saw something shiny on the tree. It was an arrow! The arrow was stuck in the tree. The squirrel wanted to get the arrow. So, it used its little hands to squeeze the arrow. It squeezed and squeezed, but the arrow did not come out. Then, the squirrel asked its friend, a strong bear, for help. The bear came to the tree and saw the arrow. The bear used its big paws to squeeze the arrow. With one big squeeze, the arrow came out of the tree. The squirrel was so happy! They both played with the arrow and had a fun day in the big forest.

Once upon a time, in a big house, there lived a wealthy man. He had a lot of food, toys, and clothes. One day, he wanted to cook a big meal with beef. He called his cook and said, "I want to mix beef with vegetables for dinner." The cook went to the kitchen and started to mix the beef with carrots, peas, and corn. But he was not very good at mixing. The vegetables and beef did not mix well, and it looked bad. The cook was sad and worried that the wealthy man would be mad at him. When it was time for dinner, the wealthy man saw the bad mix of beef and vegetables. He was very mad and yelled at the cook, "This is not good! You did a bad job!" The cook felt bad and started to cry. The wealthy man sent the cook away, and he had to find a new job. The wealthy man was still hungry, and the cook was sad. The story had a bad ending.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a big sheet in her room. She thought it was very interesting. She picked it up and decided to make a fun game with it. Amy called her friend Tim and said, "I found a big sheet. Let's play with it." Tim came over and they spread the sheet on the floor. They pretended it was a magic carpet and went on a fun ride. They laughed and had a great time. After a while, they got tired and wanted to eat some cookies. Amy went to get the cookies and Tim waited. But, Tim spilled juice on the sheet. He felt regret for making it dirty. "I'm sorry, Amy. I made the sheet dirty," said Tim. Amy smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can clean it and play again." They washed the sheet together and continued to have a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car. One day, he saw a cat outside. The cat was loud and Tim was scared. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a loud cat outside. I am scared." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, you are safe with me." His mom went outside and grabbed the loud cat. She brought it inside and gave it some food. The cat was happy and not loud anymore. Tim felt safe and played with the cat and his toy car. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to help his mom in the kitchen. He saw a knife and wanted to use it. But his mom said, "No, Tim, I cannot permit you to use the knife. It is not safe for you." Tim was sad but he knew that his mom's words were important. So, he asked, "Mom, what can I do to help you?" His mom smiled and said, "You can help me by washing the fruits and vegetables." Tim was happy to help and he washed the fruits and vegetables very well. His mom was proud of him and said, "Good job, Tim! You are a great helper!" From that day on, Tim always helped his mom in the kitchen in safe ways. He knew that the knife was not for him, but there were many other things he could do. And his mom always made sure to tell him how important his help was.
