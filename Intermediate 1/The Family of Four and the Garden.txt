Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family of Four and the Garden

Once there was a happy family of four. Every day they went out together to go for a walk in the garden and sing. One afternoon, they went to the garden as usual. But this time they brought along a camera. They smiled and laughed as they took photos of each other. Soon they came to a small pond. It was empty and silent, but the family was not disappointed. Instead, the mother said, "Let's all sing! That will make the pond look more beautiful." The father, mother and their 3-year-old child sang a happy song. As they sang, the pond turned from empty to alive. The little child felt so happy and excited that he sang louder and louder! Suddenly, his mother snapped a photo. The little child smiled with delight. From then onwards, whenever he looked at the photo, he remembered that special moment of singing in the garden.

Lily and Sam were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big kite in the sky. It was red and yellow and had a long tail. They wanted to fly a kite too. "Let's make a kite!" Lily said. "We can use paper and sticks and string." "OK!" Sam said. "But how do we make it?" Lily had an idea. She found a big bowl and some flour and water. She poured some water into the bowl and added some flour. Then she used a spoon to stir the mixture. "What are you doing?" Sam asked. "I'm making glue," Lily said. "We need glue to stick the paper to the sticks." Sam nodded. He helped Lily stir the glue until it was thick and sticky. Then they found some paper and cut it into a diamond shape. They also found some sticks and broke them into smaller pieces. They used the glue to attach the sticks to the paper. They made a cross with the sticks and glued it to the middle of the paper. Then they tied a string to the cross. They also cut some strips of paper and glued them to the bottom of the kite. They made a tail for the kite. "Wow, our kite is done!" Lily said. "It looks fair!" "Let's go fly it!" Sam said. They ran outside with their kite. They found a big field with a lot of wind. They threw their kite into the air and held the string. The kite flew high and danced in the sky. It looked like a bird. Lily and Sam were happy. They laughed and cheered as they watched their kite. They had fun flying their kite. They were proud of their kite. They were good friends.

David and Sarah were the best of friends. They lived in the same street and would walk and play together almost every day. One day, they were walking in the park when Sarah spotted a puppy. She ran towards it and kneeled, ready to give it a hug. "Be careful!" David said as he nodded his head. Sarah knew that he was being kind and trying to protect her from being too rough with the puppy. But a rude man started shouting at them. "Don't touch my dog!" he said. It scared both of them, so David took Sarah's hand and they ran away. As soon as they were far enough away, David gave Sarah a big hug. "That was a bit scary, wasn't it?" David said. Sarah nodded her head, glad to have her friend with her. David and Sarah both knew that they had each other when something scary happened. With a big smile, they started to walk home together.

One day, a messy dog named Max saw a big hill. Max wanted to race to the top. He asked his friend, a cat named Sam, to race with him. "Let's race to the tip of the hill," Max said. Sam agreed and they both got ready to race. They started to race up the hill. Max was fast, but Sam was faster. Max thought, "I must find a way to win." He saw a stick and had an idea. Max picked up the stick with his mouth and ran faster. As they got close to the tip of the hill, Max threw the stick. Sam saw the stick and ran after it. Max raced to the tip and won. Sam came back with the stick and said, "Good trick, Max!" They both laughed and went back down the hill, happy to have had a fun race.
