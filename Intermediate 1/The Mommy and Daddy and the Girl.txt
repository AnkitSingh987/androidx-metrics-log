Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mommy and Daddy and the Girl

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a daddy. They had a little girl. They all lived together in a house. One day, the mommy and the daddy were so happy that they shouted "We love you!" to the little girl. The little girl was so scared. She didn't understand why they were shouting. She started to cry. But then the mommy and daddy hugged her and told her that it was a good kind of scared. They said it was because their love was so strong for her that it made them shout. The little girl stopped crying and hugged them back. She realized that not all shouts are scary. Some shouts are full of love.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom loved to march around the house. One sunny day, Tom saw a big pizza on the table. It looked so good, he wanted to eat it. But he knew it was not for him. He walked away, but he could not stop thinking about the pizza. Later, Tom saw his friend, the little girl named Sue. Sue was making a small pizza just for Tom. She knew he liked pizza too. Tom was so happy and he started to march around Sue. He knew something good was coming. Finally, Sue finished making the small pizza for Tom. She put it on a plate and gave it to him. Tom was so happy that he marched around the plate. He ate the pizza and it was so yummy. Tom knew that good things come to those who wait. And from that day on, Tom and Sue shared many more pizzas together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a toy car that he believed could take him anywhere he wanted to go. Tom's mom always told him, "If you believe in yourself, you will find success." One day, Tom was playing with his toy car outside when he met a new friend named Sam. Sam had a toy plane that he thought was very useful. They started talking and Tom said, "I believe my car can go anywhere!" Sam replied, "That's cool! My plane can fly high in the sky!" As they played together, something unexpected happened. A big gust of wind came and blew Sam's toy plane up into a tree. Sam was sad, but Tom had an idea. He put his toy car on the ground and said, "I believe my car can help us!" To their surprise, the toy car grew big enough for them to ride in. They drove up the tree and got the toy plane back. Sam was very happy and thanked Tom for his help. From that day on, they believed that their toys were not only fun, but also very useful.

Once upon a time, there was a proud goose. The goose had a name. His name was Bob. Bob liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Bob and his friends went to the park. They played tag and hide and seek. Bob was having so much fun. He was very happy. But then, Bob got lost. He could not find his friends. Bob was sad and scared. He looked and looked for them, but he could not find them. In the end, Bob was all alone.

John and his daddy were at the park. John was so excited to be outside. He saw a motor and he wanted to get on it. He ran over to it and said "Daddy, can I ride the motor?". Daddy said "Yes, John. You have to be careful though. It is very thick." John carefully got on the motor and his daddy held the back. "I'm gonna call my friends and tell them I'm riding the motor!" John said. John was so happy he was having so much fun. He called all his friends to come over and said, "Come see me! I'm riding the motor!" Soon enough all of his friends were there and they were all having lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside in the snow. He wanted to build an igloo. Tim worked hard and soon, his igloo was big and strong. A funny bird named Bob saw Tim's igloo and wanted to play too. Bob said, "Hi Tim! Can I play with you?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played hide and seek around the igloo. While playing, Tim saw a pool of water. He thought it would be fun to dive in. But when he jumped, he found out the water was disgusting! It was all muddy and yucky. Tim said, "Yuck! This water is so disgusting!" Bob laughed and said, "Silly Tim! You should be careful next time." Tim smiled and said, "You're right, Bob. Let's go back to our igloo and play some more." So, Tim and Bob went back to the igloo and played until it was time for dinner. They had so much fun and became good friends.
