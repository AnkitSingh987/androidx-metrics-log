Category: Intermediate
Title: The Nosy Bug and the Wheel

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bug who liked to glow. He lived in a tiny house near a big wheel. The wheel was so big and fun to watch. The bug loved to see it spin around and around. One day, the nosy bug met a new friend, a small bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Bird. I like your glow! Can you show me the big wheel?" The bug was happy and said, "Yes, I can show you. My name is Bug. Let's go!" Bug and Bird went to the big wheel. They watched it spin and spin. Bug's glow made the wheel look even more fun. They played and laughed together. From that day on, Bug and Bird were the best of friends.

Once, there was a big train. The train liked to go fast. It used steam to move. One day, the train saw a little car. The car was stuck in the mud. The train said, "I can help you, little car!" The little car was frustrated. It said, "Thank you, train!" The train tried to help. It used its steam to push the car out. But the train pushed too hard. The little car went too fast. It went into a tree. The car was sad. The train was sad too. The train said, "I'm sorry, little car." The little car said, "It's okay, train. Next time, be more careful."

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy bunny named Bobo. Bobo was sad because he could not reach the carrot on the tall tree. He wanted to eat the carrot so much. He tried to jump, but he could not get it. One day, a bird named Chirpy saw Bobo. Chirpy asked, "Why are you sad, Bobo?" Bobo told Chirpy about the carrot. Chirpy said, "I can help you. But first, you must stretch every day." Bobo agreed and started to stretch every day with Chirpy. After some days, Bobo felt taller. Chirpy said, "Today is the day, Bobo. Try to get the carrot now." Bobo stretched as tall as he could and jumped. This time, he got the carrot! Bobo was so happy and thanked Chirpy. From that day on, they became best friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play with her toys and pretend they were real. One day, she found a fake toy flame in her toy box. She thought it was so cool and decided to play with it. Lily took the fake flame and pretended it was real. She played a game where she was a hero who could heal people with her magic flame. She went around her house, helping her toys feel better when they got hurt. But then, her little brother Tom came into her room. He saw the fake flame and thought it was real. He got scared and started to cry. Lily felt bad for scaring her brother. She knew she needed to fix the problem. She went to Tom and showed him that the flame was fake. She explained that it was just a toy and could not hurt anyone. Tom stopped crying and felt better. Lily learned that it was important to be careful with her toys and not scare others. The moral of the story is to be mindful of how our actions can affect others, even when we are just playing.

Tom and Anna like to help their mom in the kitchen. They are very enthusiastic and want to do everything. Mom says they can fold the napkins and put them on the plates. Tom and Anna are happy and start to fold the napkins. Tom folds his napkin into a triangle. He thinks it looks like a hat. He puts it on his head and says, "Look, mom, I'm a pirate!" Mom smiles and says, "You are a very funny pirate, Tom. But can you put your napkin on your plate, please?" Anna folds her napkin into a square. She thinks it looks like a present. She puts it on her plate and says, "Look, mom, I have a gift for you!" Mom hugs her and says, "You are a very sweet girl, Anna. Thank you for your gift. But can you open it and use your napkin, please?" Tom and Anna open their napkins and wipe their mouths. They are very proud of their folding skills. They say, "We are very good helpers, mom. We can fold anything!" Mom says, "You are very good helpers, indeed. I love you very much. Now, let's eat our dinner!"
