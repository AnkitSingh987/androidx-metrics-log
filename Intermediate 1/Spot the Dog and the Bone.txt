Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Bone

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a big shelter and went inside. In the shelter, he found a tasty bone. Spot was very happy and started to smile. A big cat named Tom saw Spot with the tasty bone. Tom wanted the bone too. He walked up to Spot and said, "Give me the bone, or I will take it from you." Spot did not want to give up his tasty bone, so he said, "No, it is mine." Tom got very angry and jumped on Spot to take the bone. They fought and fought, but in the end, Tom was bigger and stronger. He took the tasty bone away from Spot. Spot was sad and cried as he left the shelter. The tasty bone was gone, and Spot did not smile anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He had a favorite notebook where he drew pictures of his adventures. One day, Tom decided to separate the pages of his notebook. He wanted to hang his drawings on the wall. He thought it would make his room look nice and colorful. Tom worked hard to separate the pages and hang them up. He was not lazy that day. When he was done, Tom looked at his beautiful wall. He was very happy and proud of his work. From that day on, Tom was not as lazy as before. He learned that working hard could make him feel good and happy.

One day, a wild dog named Spot found a hose in the yard. Spot was very happy. He wanted to play with the hose and make water come out. But the hose was all messy. Spot's friend, Tom the cat, saw him and said, "You should organize the hose, so it's not messy anymore." Spot thought that was a good idea. So, they worked together to make the hose neat and nice. But then, they had a problem. Tom wanted to play with the hose first, and so did Spot. They both said, "I want to play first!" They were not happy. But then, Spot had an idea. They could take turns playing with the hose. Tom agreed, and they both had fun playing with the hose all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was adventurous and loved to play outside. One day, he saw a big motorcycle near his house. He wanted to ride it, but he didn't know how. Tim went to his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you help me ride the motorcycle?" Tim asked. Sam thought for a moment and said, "I don't know how, but we can try to solve this together." Tim and Sam tried to figure out how to ride the motorcycle. They pushed and pulled, but it didn't move. They felt sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, Tim's dad came outside and saw them. "What are you trying to do?" he asked. "We want to ride the motorcycle, but we can't solve how to do it," Tim said. Tim's dad smiled and said, "You are too small to ride a motorcycle now, but when you grow up, I will teach you how to ride one." Tim and Sam felt happy. They knew they couldn't ride the motorcycle now, but they were excited to learn when they were older. For now, they continued to play and have fun, knowing they would have more adventurous times in the future.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a fine red rag that she loved very much. She took the rag with her everywhere she went. One day, Mia went to the park with her mom and her fine red rag. At the park, Mia saw a big tree. She wanted to touch the tree with her fine red rag. She said, "Mom, can I touch the tree with my rag?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Mia, you can touch the tree with your rag." Mia ran to the tree and gently touched it with her fine red rag. She felt so happy. Then, she saw a little boy who was sad. Mia went to him and asked, "Why are you sad?" The little boy said, "I lost my toy." Mia wanted to help, so she let him touch her fine red rag. The little boy felt better and smiled. Mia and the little boy became good friends and played together at the park all day.
