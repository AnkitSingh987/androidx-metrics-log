Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Anna. She loved to wear her favorite dress. It was pink and had pretty flowers on it. Every day, she would wear it and play in the garden. One day, while playing, Anna found a wallet on the ground. It was brown and had many pockets. She opened it and saw a picture of a man inside. Anna wanted to find the man and give back his wallet. Anna went to her mom and said, "Mommy, I found a wallet. Can we find the person it belongs to?" Her mom said, "Yes, Anna. We will look for him." They walked around the town and asked people if they knew the man in the picture. Finally, they found the man. He was so happy to get his wallet back. He thanked Anna and gave her a candy. Anna learned that it was good to help others and not be spoiled. She shared her candy with her friends and continued to wear her favorite dress while playing in the garden.

Lily and Ben are playing with blocks. They like to make towers and houses and cars. Lily has a lot of blocks, but Ben has only a few. Ben wants more blocks to make a big tower. "Can I have some of your blocks, Lily?" Ben asks. "No, you can't. They are mine. Go find your own blocks," Lily replies. "But I don't have any more blocks. You have too many. Please share with me," Ben says. "No, I don't have too many. I have just enough. You have zero blocks. You have nothing," Lily replies. Ben feels sad and angry. He thinks Lily is not nice. He wants to knock down her blocks. But he remembers what mom said. Mom said to be kind and gentle. Mom said to use words, not hands. "Mom, Lily won't share her blocks with me. She says I have zero blocks. She says I have nothing. Is that true?" Ben calls. Mom comes to see what is wrong. She sees Lily and Ben and their blocks. She smiles and hugs them both. "No, Ben, that is not true. You have something very important. You have a brother. And Lily, you have something very important too. You have a sister. And you both have me. You have a family. And family is more than blocks. Family is love," mom says. Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry for being mean. They want to be nice. They want to be family. "I'm sorry, Ben. You can have some of my blocks. You can make a big tower with me," Lily says. "I'm sorry, Lily. You can keep your blocks. You can make a house with me," Ben says. They hug and smile. They share their blocks. They make a tower and a house and a car. They have fun. They have love. Mom watches them and smiles. She is happy. She has a tasty idea. She goes to the kitchen. She makes some cookies. She brings them to Lily and Ben. "Look, kids, I have a surprise for you. Some tasty cookies. Do you want some?" mom says. "Yes, please, mom. Thank you, mom. You are the best, mom," Lily and Ben reply. They eat the cookies. They are tasty. They are happy. They have blocks. They have cookies. They have family. They have everything.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to surf on big waves. One day, Max saw a big surf and wanted to ride it. Max met another dog named Sam. Max said, "Sam, do you value surf like me?" Sam said, "Yes, Max, I love surf too!" Max and Sam were very happy to surf together. But then, something unexpected happened. The big wave was too strong for Max and Sam. They fell off their surfboards and could not get back on. Max and Sam were sad. They learned to be more careful when surfing big waves.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who loved to sing. Every day, the bird would fly to the top of a big tree and sing a happy song. All the animals in the forest would stop and listen to the bird's sweet voice. One day, while the bird was singing, a big bear came up to the tree. The bear said, "I want you to sing for me, and only me. You are mine now." The bird did not want to sing only for the bear, but it was scared. So, the bird sang a bitter song that made the bear happy. The next day, the bird tried to sing its happy song again, but the bear came back. The bear said, "You are mine. Only sing the bitter song." The bird was very sad and sang the bitter song every day. The animals in the forest missed the happy song and the bird was never happy again.
