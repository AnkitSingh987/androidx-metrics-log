Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Mirror

Anna loves to act. She likes to pretend she is someone else and say funny things. She has a big mirror in her room, where she can see herself act. She likes to wear different hats and clothes and make faces in the mirror. One day, Anna's mom comes to her room and sees her act. She smiles and says, "You are very good at acting, Anna. You are like a famous star." Anna is happy and says, "Thank you, mom. I want to be a famous star when I grow up. Can I show you my act?" "Of course, dear. I would love to see your act. What are you going to act today?" mom says. Anna thinks for a moment and says, "I am going to act like a princess. I have a crown and a dress and a wand. I can do magic with my wand. Watch me!" Anna puts on her crown and dress and picks up her wand. She looks at the mirror and says, "I am the most beautiful princess in the world. I can make anything I want with my wand. I can make flowers and birds and rainbows. I can make you happy and proud of me. I can make you a queen, mom. Do you want to be a queen?" Mom nods and says, "Yes, Anna. I would love to be a queen. You are very sweet and kind, my princess. You make me very happy and proud of you. You are my magic star."

Once upon a time, in a land of winter, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the snow. One day, he went outside to build a snowman. He worked hard rolling the snow into big balls. He put the big balls on top of each other to make the snowman. Tim was very grateful for the snow. As Tim was playing, a little girl named Sue came by. She saw Tim's snowman and said, "Wow! Your snowman is so big and nice!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! Do you want to play with me?" Sue nodded and they started to play together. As they were playing, Tim and Sue saw a big tree. They decided to climb the tree. But when they got to the top, something unexpected happened. Tim slipped and began to fall. Sue quickly grabbed his hand and pulled him back up. They were both scared, but grateful that they were okay. They climbed down the tree and decided to play on the ground instead. From that day on, Tim and Sue became best friends and enjoyed many more winter days together.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to print pictures with their crayons. They print cars, animals, and stars. They show each other their pictures and say, "Wow, that is impressive!" One day, Tom prints a big dragon. He is very proud of his dragon. He shows it to Sam and says, "Look at my dragon! It is the best picture ever!" Sam likes the dragon too. He wants to have it. He says, "Can I trade with you? I have a nice picture of a flower. You can have it if you give me your dragon." Tom does not want to trade. He loves his dragon. He says, "No, I want to keep my dragon. It is mine. Go away!" Sam gets angry. He tries to grab the dragon from Tom. Tom holds on to it. They pull and tug. The dragon tears in half. They both cry. They are not friends anymore. They have no dragon and no flower. They are sad and lonely.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Daisy. She was 3 years old and had really long brown hair. One day Daisy was playing with her toys when her dad came in. "Daisy, it's time for dinner!" he said. "OK, dad!" Daisy said happily. She liked dinner time. When they got to the dining room, there was a special surprise. On the table was a large bowl filled with popcorn. "Wow, this is unique!" Daisy said. She had never seen popcorn for dinner before. "I'm going to shoot this popcorn into my mouth!" Daisy said. Her dad laughed. With a big smile on her face, Daisy began shooting popcorn into her mouth one at a time! She was so excited to have such an unusual dinner. At the end of the night, Daisy was full and happy. She couldn't believe how much fun she had at dinner!
