Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Jacket

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot had a favorite jacket that he loved to wear. One day, Spot went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Spot saw his friend, Kitty. Kitty said, "Hi, Spot! I like your jacket!" Spot was very happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty! I like your bow!" They played together, running and jumping in the grass. After a while, Spot's jacket got dirty. He was sad and said, "Oh no! My jacket is not clean!" Kitty had an idea and said, "Let's go to the water and clean it!" They went to the water and cleaned Spot's jacket together. Now, Spot's jacket was clean and he was happy again. They continued to play and had lots of fun at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl named Lily. She loved to draw and color. One day, she found a big box of crayons with many colors. She was very happy. Lily wanted to draw a picture for her mom. Lily asked her mom, "Can I release all the colors from the box?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can. But remember to put them back when you are done." Lily took out all the crayons and started to draw. She drew a big sun, a house, and a tree. She used all the colors to make her picture pretty. When she was done, she showed her mom the picture. Her mom loved it and gave Lily a big hug. Then, Lily put all the crayons back in the box.

One day, a little boy named Tim had an important race at school. He wanted to win the race and make his mom and dad proud. Before he left home, Tim's mom said, "Run fast, and don't forget to look at the big desk when you finish the race." At school, all the kids lined up to start the race. Tim was a little scared but he knew he had to be brave. His friend, Sam, said, "Good luck, Tim! We can do it!" Tim smiled and felt better. The race began and Tim ran as fast as he could. He remembered his mom's words and looked for the big desk as he ran. He saw the desk near the finish line and ran even faster. Tim won the race and was so happy! He went to the big desk and found a prize waiting for him. It was a special day for Tim, and he knew his mom and dad would be proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a best friend named Tom. They loved to play together every day. One day, they saw a new girl named Lily. Lily was very shy, and Amy wanted to make her feel welcome. Amy said to Tom, "Let's go play with Lily!" Tom felt jealous because he wanted Amy to play with him only. But they went to Lily, and Amy gave her a big hug. Lily smiled and felt happy. Lily had hurt her knee while playing. Amy and Tom helped Lily sit down and gave her a hug. Tom realized that it was good to have more friends. They all played together, and no one felt jealous anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a radio. He loved to listen to it every day. One day, the radio stopped working. Tim was troubled. Tim went to his dad for help. His dad looked at the radio. He saw that the sun was going down. Tim's dad had an idea. He said, "Let's wait for the sun to rise tomorrow." The next day, the sun rose. Tim's dad took the radio outside. He put it in the sun. The radio started to work again! Tim was happy. He danced and listened to his radio. The sun had fixed his troubled radio.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. They saw a big, red mailbox on the street. Amy asked her mom, "What is that big red thing?" Her mom said, "That is a mailbox. We put letters in it to send to our friends." Amy thought for a moment and said, "I want to send a letter to my friend, Timmy." So, they went back home and Amy drew a picture with her crayons. She put the picture in an envelope and wrote Timmy's name on it. Then, they went back to the mailbox. Amy put the letter in the mailbox and gave it a tight hug. She said, "I hope my hug goes to Timmy too!" Her mom smiled and said, "I'm sure Timmy will feel your hug when he gets your letter." And they walked back home, holding hands.
