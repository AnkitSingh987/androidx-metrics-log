Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Man and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He was very happy one day because he printed a picture of the sun. He put the picture on the wall and smiled. But suddenly, it started to rain outside. He was very sad. He didn't want the rain to ruin his picture. He ran out of his house and put the picture in his pocket. When the rain stopped, the man opened his pocket. The picture was wet and ruined. He tried to print another one, but he couldn't do it. The man was very sad. He was not happy anymore. But then he realized that the rain was like a gift. Every time he sees the rain, he will remember his ruined picture. He will be reminded of how special it was. So the bald man was happy again.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to create a game. He found a round thing called a baseball. Tim was very happy. He took the baseball and went to the park. At the park, Tim met a big, fearful dog. The dog barked, "Woof! Woof!" Tim was scared, but he wanted to play. So, he threw the baseball far away. The dog ran after the baseball. When the dog came back, he had the baseball in his mouth. Tim was surprised. The dog dropped the baseball at Tim's feet. Tim threw the baseball again, and the dog ran to get it. They played together all day, and Tim was not scared anymore. The fearful dog became Tim's friend.

One day, a compassionate boy named Tim found a weapon. It was a big stick. Tim liked to play with the stick, but he never hurt anyone with it. He was a kind boy. One sunny day, Tim saw a little bird in a tree. The bird was stuck and could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird. He thought about how he could save the bird with his weapon, the big stick. Tim used the big stick to reach the bird. He was very careful. With a gentle push, the bird was free. The bird flew away, happy and safe. Tim felt good because he saved the bird with his weapon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to dream about a big, yummy peach. The peach was bigger than her head! She wanted to find this peach and share it with her friends. One day, Sue went for a walk with her friend, Tim. They saw a big tree with many peaches. Sue was so happy! She said, "Tim, look! I found the big peach from my dream!" Tim was excited too. They picked the big peach and brought it home. When they got home, Sue's dog, Max, was upset. He wanted to play with Sue and Tim. Sue said, "Don't be upset, Max. We will share the big peach with you too!" They all sat down and ate the big peach together. It was the best peach they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a wise bird. The bird liked to soar high in the sky. The bird was friends with a man. The man was a lawyer. One day, the bird saw the lawyer was sad. The bird wanted to help his friend. The bird flew down and asked the lawyer, "Why are you sad?" The lawyer told the bird about his problem. The wise bird listened and thought. Then, the bird gave the lawyer some good ideas. The lawyer was happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a clever rabbit. The rabbit's name was Waffle. Waffle had many friends, but his best friend was a little bird named Sky. One day, Waffle and Sky were playing near the river. They saw a big, round stone. Waffle had a fun idea. "Let's jump on the stone!" he said. Sky liked the idea, so they took turns jumping on the stone. Suddenly, the stone moved! It was not a stone, but a big turtle! The turtle was not happy. "Why are you jumping on me?" he asked. Waffle and Sky were sorry. "We did not know you were a turtle," said Waffle. The turtle saw that Waffle and Sky were nice. He decided to be their friend too. They all played together near the river. Waffle, Sky, and the turtle had lots of fun. And they were friends forever.
