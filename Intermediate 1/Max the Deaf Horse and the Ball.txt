Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Deaf Horse and the Ball

One day, a deaf horse named Max lived on a farm. Max was sad because he could not hear the other horses talk. He wanted to play with them, but he did not know how. One day, a girl named Lily came to the farm. She saw Max and wanted to help him. Lily had an idea to fix Max's problem. She got a big ball and threw it to Max. Max saw the ball and kicked it back to Lily. They played with the ball all day. The other horses saw Max and Lily play. They wanted to play too. Soon, all the horses were playing with the ball. Max was happy because he could play with his friends, even though he was deaf. The horses learned that they could have fun together, even if they were different.

John was playing outside in the park with his friends. He spotted a nurse in a white dress walking down the street. She was carrying a bag and her head was hung low. John asked his friends, "Let's go see the nurse. She looks harmless". The boys walked up to the nurse and asked where she was going. The nurse smiled and said, "I'm going to the hospital to help the children. Do you want to come with me?". John and his friends happily followed the nurse to the hospital. They hung around outside the hospital, waiting for the nurse to finish her work inside. After a few hours, the nurse walked out of the hospital. She thanked John and his friends for their patience and gave them each a lollipop. John and his friends waved goodbye to the nurse as she walked away. They were happy they had met a kind, harmless nurse.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to learn new things. One day, her mom and dad took her to a museum. Lucy was very excited. In the museum, Lucy saw many things. She saw big animals, pretty paintings, and old clothes. Lucy wanted to know more about them. Her mom and dad tried to explain what each thing was. They told her about the big animals that lived a long time ago and the people who made the pretty paintings. Lucy had a great time at the museum. She learned a lot from her mom and dad. She wanted to go back again and see more things. Lucy knew that the museum was a nice place to learn and have fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, red jeep on the road. The jeep had four wheels and went very fast. Tim thought it was fun to watch the jeep drive by. As Tim kept walking, he saw a bossy cat. The cat was telling other cats what to do. The other cats did not like the bossy cat. They wanted to play, but the bossy cat said "No, you must do what I say." Tim wanted to help the other cats, so he tried to talk to the bossy cat. But as he walked to the cat, he stumbled on a rock. Tim fell down and started to cry. The bossy cat saw Tim and felt bad. The cat came to help Tim stand up. The bossy cat said, "I am sorry for being bossy. I will be nice now." The other cats were happy, and they all played together. Tim was happy too, and he went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tall tree. The tree was very honest and always helped the animals. One day, a big, mean bear came to the tree. The bear was not nice. The bear said to the tree, "I demand you give me all your fruit!" The honest tree was scared, but it wanted to help. So, the tree gave all its fruit to the bear. The bear ate all the fruit and was still not happy. The bear was still hungry and wanted more. The honest tree had no more fruit to give. The bear got very angry and hit the tree. The tree fell down and the animals lost their home. The mean bear left, and the animals were very sad.
