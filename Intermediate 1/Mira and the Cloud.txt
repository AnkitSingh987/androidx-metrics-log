Category: Intermediate
Title: Mira and the Cloud

Mira likes to print pictures with her crayons. She has many colors and papers. She prints flowers, animals, stars and more. She shows her prints to her mom and dad. They say they are very pretty and put them on the fridge. One day, Mira wants to print something new. She looks around the kitchen and sees a cauliflower. It is white and round and has many bumps. She thinks it looks like a cloud. She asks her mom if she can print with the cauliflower. "Sure, you can print with the cauliflower," her mom says. "But you have to be careful. It is food, not a toy. You can only use a little bit and then we have to wash it and cook it for dinner." Mira nods and takes the cauliflower. She cuts a small piece and dips it in blue paint. She presses it on a paper and makes a blue cloud. She likes it. She dips another piece in yellow paint and makes a sun. She dips another piece in green paint and makes a tree. She is very enthusiastic and makes many prints. "Look, mom, I made a sky and a garden with the cauliflower!" Mira says, holding up her paper. "That's wonderful, Mira!" her mom says, smiling. "You are very creative and clever. But now it's time to clean up and get ready for dinner. Can you help me wash the cauliflower and put away the paints?" Mira says yes and helps her mom. She is happy with her prints and her mom is proud of her. They have a nice dinner with the cauliflower and some chicken and rice. Mira tells her dad about her prints and he says they are amazing. He gives her a hug and a kiss. Mira feels warm and cozy. She loves to print with her crayons and sometimes with other things too.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat. The cat liked to walk down the street. One day, the cat saw a big box. The cat wanted to add the box to his toys. As the cat pushed the box, it started to move. The box was not just a box, it was a car! The car began to drive down the street. The chubby cat was so happy and excited. The car took the cat to a big park. The park was full of cats and their cars. The chubby cat made new friends and played all day. The cat never knew that cars were for cats too. And that was the best day ever for the chubby cat.

One day, a girl named Lucy found a journal. She could not hear, so she was deaf. Lucy liked to write in her journal. She wrote about her day, her friends, and her family. The journal was her best friend. Lucy had a friend named Tom. Tom liked to twist things. One day, Tom saw Lucy's journal. He said, "I want to twist it!" Lucy said, "No, Tom! That is my journal. Please do not twist it." Tom was sad, but he did not twist the journal. Later, Tom found a toy snake. He twisted the toy snake and was happy. Lucy was happy too. Her journal was safe, and her friend Tom was happy. They played together and had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful girl named Lily. She loved to keep her friends happy. One day, she found a toy war going on in her room. The toy soldiers were not happy. Lily wanted to help the toy soldiers. She told them, "You do not need to have a war. We can all be friends and play together." The toy soldiers listened to her and stopped fighting. From that day on, Lily and the toy soldiers played together and had a lot of fun. They were all very helpful to each other. They became the best of friends and the war was over.

One day, a boy named Tim found a wide hat. It was a big hat with a wide top. He put it on his head and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sue, and said, "Look at my wide hat!" Sue looked at Tim and said, "I don't like your wide hat!" She decided to ignore Tim and play with her ball. Tim felt sad, but he kept wearing his wide hat. As they played, a strong wind blew. The wind took Sue's ball high up into a tree. Sue tried to get it, but the tree was too tall. Tim had an idea. He took off his wide hat and used it to catch the wind. The wind lifted him up, and he got Sue's ball from the tree. Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! Your wide hat is great!" From that day on, they both played together, and Sue never ignored Tim again.
