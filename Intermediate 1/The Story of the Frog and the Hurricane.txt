Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Frog and the Hurricane

One day, a big hurricane came to the town. It was very loud and scary. All the people were worried. They did not feel safe. They wanted the hurricane to go away. A little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She felt scared too. But she had a lucky toy frog that she loved. She held the frog and wished the hurricane would stop. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind stopped blowing and the rain stopped falling. The hurricane was gone! Amy felt very happy and lucky that her wish came true. She knew that her lucky frog had helped her. From that day on, whenever she felt scared, she held her lucky frog and felt safe again. And the town was never scared of hurricanes again.

Once upon a time, there was a happy pup. He liked to play with his brothers. Today, the pup wanted to celebrate with them. He asked them to play a match with him. His brothers happily agreed. The happy pup went to fetch his favourite ball. It looked like a happy round yellow ball. He laughed and playfully threw it to his brothers. His brothers rolled it back and it was a lot of fun. The pup then said, "Now let's celebrate!" His brothers laughed and hugged him. Everyone celebrated with lots of hugs. It was a great match, and the pup was so happy they all played together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Sara and Ben like to play pirates. They have a big box that is their ship. They have hats and swords and a flag. They pretend to sail on the sea and look for treasure. One day, they find a hook in the garage. It is shiny and sharp. Ben says, "Look, Sara, a hook! I can be a pirate with a hook!" He puts the hook on his hand and waves it around. Sara says, "Be careful, Ben! The hook is not a toy. It can hurt you or me. You have to be gentle with the hook." Ben says, "But I want to be a pirate with a hook! It is fun!" He swings the hook and tries to catch Sara's hat. Sara dodges the hook and says, "No, Ben, stop! You don't know how to use the hook. You can rip the hat or the box or the flag. You can make a hole or a tear. You can make us sad." Ben says, "I'm sorry, Sara. I don't want to make you sad. I don't want to rip anything. I just want to play pirates." Sara says, "I know, Ben. I want to play pirates too. But we have to play safe. We can use the hook for something else. We can use it to fish or to hang the flag or to dig for treasure. We can still have fun with the hook, but we have to be gentle with it." Ben says, "OK, Sara. You are right. We can use the hook for something else. We can be gentle with it. We can still be pirates." Sara and Ben smile and hug. They put the hook in the box and go back to their pirate game. They are happy and careful. They are good pirates.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his friends. One day, his friend, a bird named Tweet, flew to him and said, "Spot, can you help me change my nest? There is too much waste, and I need a clean place for my babies." Spot barked happily and said, "Yes, Tweet! I will help you!" They went to the tree where Tweet's nest was. Spot saw the waste and knew they needed to clean it up. They worked together to take out the waste and make the nest nice and clean. After they finished, Tweet thanked Spot for being such a dependable friend. They played together and had a great day. From then on, Spot always helped his friends when they needed him. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play all day. One day, Tim wanted to win a medal in a race. Tim ran very fast in the race. He felt happy and strong. As he ran, he saw his mom and dad. They said, "Go, Tim, go! You can do it!" Tim smiled and ran even faster. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a little girl drop her toy on the track. He stopped running and picked up the toy. He gave it back to the girl and she said, "Thank you, Tim!" Even though Tim did not win the race, he felt like a winner because he helped someone. And guess what? Tim's mom and dad gave him a medal for being brave and kind.
