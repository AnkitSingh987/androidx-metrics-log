Category: Intermediate
Title: The Orange Cat and the Dog

One day, a little orange cat went for a walk. She saw a big dog. The dog looked at the cat and said, "Show me your body tricks!" The cat was scared, but she wanted to be friends with the dog. The cat jumped high in the air and did a flip. The dog was happy and clapped his paws. They played together and talked about their favorite things. The dog said, "I like bones," and the cat said, "I like fish." Then, something unexpected happened. The dog found a big orange ball. The dog and the cat played with the ball, but it was not a ball. It was an orange! They laughed and laughed. The dog and the cat became best friends and did body tricks together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. One day, Tim's mom made toast for breakfast. But the toast was bad. Tim did not want to eat it. He told his mom, "I don't like this toast. It's bad!" His mom said, "You must eat it or you can't go play outside." Tim did not want to surrender. He wanted to play outside, but he did not want to eat the bad toast. So, he had an idea. He gave the toast to his dog, Spot. Spot ate the toast, but then he felt bad too. He started to bark and cry. Tim's mom came to see what was wrong. She saw the bad toast in Spot's mouth and knew what happened. Tim felt sad because he knew he did something wrong. In the end, Tim had to stay inside and could not play outside. He learned that he should not give bad things to his dog and should listen to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an obedient girl who always listened to her mom and dad. One day, her mom asked her to help weigh some rice for dinner. Lily was very excited to help. Lily went to the kitchen and found a big bowl of rice. She carefully put some rice on the scale until it was just the right amount. She felt so proud that she could help her mom with the important job of weighing the rice. After the rice was weighed, Lily and her mom cooked a delicious dinner together. They had a great time, and Lily learned how fun it was to help her mom in the kitchen. From that day on, Lily was always eager to help her parents with anything they needed.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with a line hanging from it. He wanted to play with the line, so he asked his mom if he could. His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to drop it." Tim played with the line, but he was not careful. He dropped the line, and it fell on the ground. Tim felt bad because he did not listen to his mom. Tim picked up the line and said sorry to his mom. His mom hugged him and told him it was okay. They went home and Tim promised to be more careful next time. They had a good day, even though Tim had made a bad mistake.

Once upon a time, there was a weak dove. The dove had a hard time flying. One day, the dove met a big bird. The big bird was strong and could fly high. The weak dove said to the big bird, "I want to fly like you. Can you help me?" The big bird said, "Yes, I can help you. But you must accept that you are weak now. You will be strong soon." The weak dove accepted the help from the big bird. They practiced flying together every day. Soon, the weak dove became strong and could fly high too. The dove was happy and thanked the big bird. The moral of the story is: Accept help from others and you can grow stronger.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a harmless bug. The bug liked to march all around the room. Tim liked to watch the bug march. He thought it was fun. He wanted to show the bug to his friend, Sally. Tim put the bug in a box and went to Sally's house. "Look, Sally! I found a harmless bug that can march!" said Tim. Sally looked at the bug and smiled. "I have something fun we can do with the bug," said Sally. She went to her room and came back with a big computer. Sally put the computer on the floor and turned it on. The screen showed a game with a path. "Let's see if the bug can march on this path," said Sally. They put the bug on the computer screen and it started to march on the path. Tim and Sally laughed and clapped as the bug marched. Just as the bug was about to finish the path, it stopped. Tim and Sally looked at each other, worried. "Why did the bug stop?" asked Tim. "I don't know," said Sally. They looked closer at the screen and saw a tiny piece of food on the path. The bug was eating the food! After the bug finished eating, it continued to march on the path. Tim and Sally cheered as the bug reached the end of the path. They were happy that the harmless bug could march and have fun with them. And they knew that they would always remember the day they played with the marching bug on the computer.
