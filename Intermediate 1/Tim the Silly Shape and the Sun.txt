Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Silly Shape and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a silly shape named Tim. Tim was a happy square who lived in a world full of shapes. Tim had many friends, like the round circle and the pointy triangle. They all liked to play and laugh together. One day, Tim met a pretty heart named Sue. Sue was very nice, and Tim liked her a lot. They played together and had fun. Tim wanted Sue to be with him forever. So, Tim asked Sue if she would marry him. Sue said yes, and they were both very happy. All the shapes came to see Tim and Sue get married. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. Everyone was happy for them. After they were married, Tim and Sue went on a trip to the beach. They played in the sand and swam in the sea. They were the happiest shapes in the world.

Sara and Tom were playing in the bathroom. They liked to make bubbles with the shampoo and pretend they were in a magic castle. Sara saw a big bottle of shampoo on the shelf. She wanted to use it for her bubbles. "Look, Tom, more shampoo!" she said. She reached for the bottle, but it was too high. She climbed on the stool to get it. "Be careful, Sara!" Tom said. "That shampoo is not for us. It is for Mom. She said it is dangerous for our eyes. It can make us cry a lot." Sara did not listen to Tom. She grabbed the bottle and opened it. She poured some shampoo on her hand and rubbed it on her hair. She felt a sting in her eyes. She screamed and dropped the bottle. The shampoo spilled on the floor and made it slippery. Tom ran to help Sara. He tried to wipe her eyes with a towel, but he slipped on the shampoo. He fell and hit his head on the sink. He cried too. Mom heard the noise and came to the bathroom. She saw Sara and Tom on the floor, covered in shampoo and tears. She was very angry and worried. "What have you done?" she asked. "This shampoo is not for you. It is for me. It is very expensive and very dangerous. You could have hurt yourselves badly. You have to return it to the shelf and never touch it again. Do you understand?" Sara and Tom nodded. They were sorry and scared. Mom cleaned them up and put a band-aid on Tom's head. She hugged them and said she loved them, but they had to be more careful and listen to her. She returned the shampoo to the shelf and locked the bathroom door. Sara and Tom learned their lesson. They never played with the shampoo again.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to paint. Every day, she took out her paints and painted a pretty picture. One day, Lucy had an idea. She wanted to paint her room. She asked her mom if she could paint the walls. Her mom said yes and gave her permission. Lucy started to paint. She put on a thin white coat on the walls. She worked very hard and made the walls look beautiful. After a few hours, she was done. The room looked great! All the colors were vibrant and the room looked different. Everyone was so amazed. Her mom said it a job well done! From then on, Lucy painted anything she could find. She painted the furniture, the plates, and the windows. Every time she painted something, it looked even more beautiful. Lucy was so proud of her work. She was a great painter and always made everything look wonderful.

Lily and Ben were playing with their books. They liked to look at the pictures and make up stories. Lily had a fancy book with a princess and a castle. Ben had a book with animals and a farm. "Look at my book, Ben. It is so pretty and shiny. The princess has a long dress and a crown. She lives in a big castle with many rooms. Do you want to see?" Lily said. "Yes, please. Your book is very nice, Lily. Can I hold it?" Ben said. Lily gave her book to Ben. He looked at the pages and smiled. He saw a picture of a big bed with many pillows. He wanted to measure how big it was. He took a ruler from his pocket and put it on the page. "Wow, this bed is very long. It is longer than my arm. How many rulers do you think it is?" Ben said. "I don't know, Ben. But be careful with my book. Don't make it dirty or tear it. It is my favorite book." Lily said. But Ben did not listen to Lily. He was too busy measuring the bed. He moved the ruler along the page. He did not see that the edge of the ruler was sharp. He made a big cut on the page. The picture of the bed was ruined. "Ben, what did you do? You broke my book! You cut the princess's bed! How could you?" Lily cried. She took her book from Ben and saw the damage. She was very sad and angry. She did not want to play with Ben anymore. She ran to her mom and showed her the book. "Mom, look what Ben did. He measured my book and cut it. He is very bad. He is not my friend." Lily said. Mom looked at the book and saw the cut. She was sorry for Lily. She hugged her and said. "I'm sorry, Lily. Your book is broken. Ben should not have done that. He should say sorry to you. Maybe we can fix your book with some tape. Do you want to try?" Mom said. Lily nodded. She wanted to fix her book. She did not want to lose the princess and the castle. She followed Mom to the kitchen. She hoped the tape would work. Ben stayed in the living room. He felt bad. He did not mean to cut Lily's book. He wanted to measure it because it was fancy. He liked Lily's book. He liked Lily. He wanted to say sorry to her. But he was afraid. He did not know what to do. He wished he had not measured the book. He wished he had been more careful. He wished he had a fancy book too.
