Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fox and the Tree

One day, a fox went out to play. The fox was filthy. He had mud all over him. He wanted to find friends to play with. He looked around and saw a big tree. The fox thought that maybe he could find friends there. He walked to the tree and saw a big hole. The hole was dark and deep. The fox was scared, but he wanted to find friends. He took a deep breath and went inside. The hole included many tunnels. The fox went down one tunnel and found a big room. In the room, there were many animals. They were all playing and having fun. The fox was happy. He thought he found friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The animals stopped playing and looked at the fox. They saw that he was filthy. The animals did not want to play with a filthy fox. The fox was sad. He went back to the hole and cleaned himself. When he came back, the animals were happy to play with him. Now, the fox had many friends.

Once upon a time, in a big farm, there was a shy goose. The shy goose did not like to play with the other animals. She liked to be alone. One day, the other animals began to quarrel. They were very loud and did not stop. The shy goose did not like the noise. She walked away to find a quiet place. As she walked, she found a big box. The box was hidden behind some trees. The shy goose was curious. She went closer to look at the box. The shy goose opened the box. Inside the box, there were many toys. There were balls, dolls, and cars. The shy goose was very happy. She played with the toys all day. The other animals stopped quarreling when they saw the shy goose playing. They all joined her and played together. They did not quarrel anymore. The shy goose became friends with all the animals. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to sit by the sea and watch the big waves. He liked the way the water moved and how the sun made the sea look shiny. One day, as Tim sat by the sea, he saw a man with a big surf. The man took the surf into the water and rode on the waves. Tim thought it looked like so much fun. He wanted to try it too. Tim asked his mom if he could learn to surf. She said yes and got him a small surf that was smooth and easy to use. Tim practiced every day and soon he could ride the waves too. He was so happy and loved to surf with his new friends by the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lucy wanted to discover something new. She looked and looked until she found a big dish. It was hiding in a cupboard. Lucy thought the dish was brilliant. It was round and shiny. She wanted to show her mom. Lucy carried the dish to her mom. She was very careful not to drop it. Her mom was happy to see the brilliant dish. She said, "Thank you, Lucy! Let's make a yummy cake in this dish." Lucy and her mom made a cake together. They had fun and the cake was very tasty.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a big tree that she wanted to touch. "Can I touch the tree, Mom?" asked Mia. Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia walked up to the tree. It seemed so big and strong. She reached out her hand to touch the bark. But when she did, she heard a tiny voice say, "Ouch!" Mia looked around, but she did not see anyone. Mia touched the tree again, and the tiny voice said, "Please, stop!" This time, Mia saw a little bug on the tree. The bug told her that the tree was his home. Mia said sorry to the bug and they became friends. From that day on, Mia knew that even little things can have big feelings too.
