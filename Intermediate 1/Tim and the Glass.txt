Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Glass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big red ball that he liked to play with. One day, he kicked the ball very hard and it went flying through the air. The ball hit a glass on the table and the glass fell down. The glass broke into many pieces. Tim's mom saw the broken glass and told Tim that he needed to be more careful. She asked him to tie a string around the ball so it wouldn't fly away again. Tim found a long, blue string and tied it to the ball. He made a big knot so the string would stay on the ball. Now, Tim could play with his ball without breaking anything. He would pull the string and the ball would come back to him. Tim had so much fun playing with his ball and string. He learned that playing carefully could still be fun. And the glass on the table stayed safe and whole.

Once upon a time in a fair land, there lived a panda. He was big, soft and friendly. Everywhere he went, he spread smiles and joy to all. But one day something bad happened. A mean person came and tried to hurt the panda. The panda was scared and didn't know what to do. But then two brave kids saw what was happening. They ran over and stood between the panda and the mean person. The kids said loudly, "We will protect the panda!" The mean person was surprised and scared of the brave kids. He ran away and never came back. The kids were happy that they could protect the panda. The panda thanked the kids and said goodbye. From that day on, all the people in the fair land knew that the brave kids would protect them if they were ever in danger.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a small, easy hill. Sue thought it would be fun to lie on the hill and watch the clouds. As she lay on the hill, Sue saw a tiny bug. The bug was covered in dust. Sue said, "Hello, little bug! You are very dusty. Let me help you." Sue gently blew the dust off the bug. The bug was happy and clean. "Thank you, Sue!" said the bug. "You are a good friend." Sue smiled and they played together all day. When it was time to go home, Sue waved goodbye to her new friend. She knew she would come back to the easy hill to play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bobo. Bobo liked to weigh things. He had a big scale to help him do that. One day, he found a big, shiny rock. He thought it was gold, but it was fake. It was just a shiny, yellow rock. Bobo had a friend named Sally. Sally was a small squirrel with a lot of style. She wore a tiny hat and a little red scarf. Sally liked to help Bobo weigh things. When Bobo showed her the shiny rock, she said, "Let's weigh it!" So, they put the rock on the scale. It was heavy, but not as heavy as gold. Sally said, "Bobo, this rock is not gold. It is just a pretty, yellow rock." Bobo was sad, but Sally had an idea. "Let's paint it and make it look even more pretty!" she said. Bobo and Sally painted the rock with many colors. They made it look very nice. Then, they put it in the garden. The rock was now a beautiful, fake gold rock. Bobo and Sally were happy. They had made something fun together. And they knew that even if it wasn't real gold, it was still special to them.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a charming cat named Kiki. Kiki had a toy shield that she loved to play with all day. One day, Kiki's owner, a little girl named Lisa, found a magic wand. Lisa waved the wand at Kiki's shield and said, "Shrink!" The shield got very small. Kiki was sad because she could not play with her shield anymore. Lisa laughed and put the tiny shield in her pocket. The next day, a big dog came to the house. Kiki tried to use her small shield to scare the dog away. But the shield was too small, and the dog did not go away. The dog chased Kiki, and she could not stop him. Kiki wished she had her big shield back. But it was too late, and the dog caught Kiki.
