Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rail and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there was a rail near a big tree. Every day, a happy bird named Bobo would sit on the rail and sing. Bobo loved to sing and make everyone smile. One sunny day, a girl named Lily walked by the rail. She heard Bobo singing and stopped to listen. "Hi, bird! I like your song," said Lily. Bobo was so happy that he flew down to Lily and gave her a little kiss on her cheek. From that day on, Lily and Bobo became the best of friends. They would play near the rail and sing together. Everyone in the jolly little town would stop and listen to their happy songs. And every time they finished singing, Bobo would give Lily a sweet little kiss.

Tom and Sam were brothers. They liked to play with their toys in the yard. One day, they found a saw in the shed. It was big and shiny and had sharp teeth. "Wow, look at this!" Tom said. "We can cut things with it!" "Let me see!" Sam said. He grabbed the saw from Tom's hand. He ran to the fence and tried to cut a hole in it. "Stop, Sam! That's bad!" Tom said. "Dad will be angry if you break the fence. He will punish us!" But Sam did not listen. He thought he was smart and strong. He pushed the saw hard against the fence. He did not see the nail sticking out of the wood. The saw hit the nail and bounced back. It hit Sam's hand and made a big cut. Sam screamed and dropped the saw. Blood came out of his hand. "Ow, ow, ow!" Sam cried. "It hurts! It hurts!" Tom ran to Sam and saw his hand. He felt sorry for his brother. He wrapped his shirt around the cut and hugged him. "I'm sorry, Tom. I was stupid. I should have listened to you." Sam said. "It's okay, Sam. I forgive you. But we have to tell Dad what happened. He will help you." Tom said. They went inside the house and told Dad everything. Dad was angry at first, but he was also worried. He took Sam to the doctor and got him a bandage. He also punished them by taking away their toys for a week. Tom and Sam learned their lesson. They never played with the saw again. They also learned to share and listen to each other. They became better brothers.

Tommy was happy every day he went to the park. Every day, he met up with his friend, Cindy. They would run around, laugh and play tag. Tommy was very proud of his friendship with Cindy. But one day, he noticed someone else playing with Cindy. She was called Emma and she was very clever. Tommy felt jealous and wanted to play more than ever. He asked for longer playtime with Cindy, but she said she wanted to play with Emma. He was frustrated. Tommy hoped things would get better, but time passed and things didn't change. He started playing on his own, whilst Cindy and Emma laughed and played together. Tommy couldn't help but feel sad. He was jealous and upset that he wasn't able to spend time with his friend, Cindy. It was a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toy car near the road. He was very happy. But he felt weak because he was sick for many days. One day, while playing, Tim's toy car rolled into the road. Tim ran after it without looking. A big car was coming fast. Tim's mom saw him and started to scream. But it was too late. The big car hit Tim and his toy car. Tim's mom and dad took him to the hospital. The doctor tried to help Tim, but he was very hurt. Tim's mom and dad were very sad. They wished they had watched Tim more closely. From that day on, they always made sure to keep their children safe.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tom. Tom was always eager to learn new things. He loved to play with his dog, Spot, and his cat, Fluffy. One sunny day, Tom saw his mom order a big box. He didn't know what was inside the box, but he knew it was a secret. Tom was very excited and wanted to find out the secret. Tom waited and waited until the box came. When it finally arrived, Tom and his mom opened the box together. Inside, there was a big, red ball. Tom was so happy, and he played with the ball all day long with Spot and Fluffy. The secret was out, and it was a fun day for everyone.
