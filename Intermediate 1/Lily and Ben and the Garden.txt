Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Garden

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to dig and find bugs and flowers. They saw a big red pepper growing on a plant. Lily wanted to pick it and eat it. "Look, Ben, a pepper! Can we have it?" Lily asked. Ben shook his head. "No, Lily, that is a hot pepper. It will hurt your mouth. Mama said so." Lily did not listen. She reached for the pepper and pulled it off the plant. She took a big bite and smiled. But then, she felt a fire in her mouth. She spit out the pepper and cried. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts, it hurts!" Lily screamed. Ben ran to get Mama. Mama heard Lily's cry and entered the garden. She saw Lily holding her mouth and the pepper on the ground. "Oh, Lily, what did you do? You ate a hot pepper. That was not a good idea. Come here, let me help you." Mama said. She took Lily inside and gave her some water and milk. She hugged her and kissed her cheek. "I'm sorry, Mama. I was silly. I won't eat hot peppers again." Lily said. Mama smiled. "It's okay, Lily. You learned your lesson. Next time, listen to Ben and me. We only want you to be safe and happy." Mama said. Lily nodded. She felt better. She said sorry to Ben and gave him a hug. They went back to the garden and played with some nice flowers. They did not touch the peppers anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim woke up and saw that it was a gloomy day. The sky was gray, and the trees were sad. Tim felt sad too. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Why do you look sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I don't like gloomy days." His mom smiled and said, "Let's remember the fun things we can do inside when it's gloomy." Tim thought about it and started to feel a little better. Tim and his mom played games, read books, and made cookies. They had so much fun that Tim forgot about the gloomy day outside. When the sun came out again, Tim looked out the window and said, "I'm happy now, but I'll always remember the fun we had on the gloomy day."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy vehicles. He had cars, buses, and trucks. Tim would drive them all around his room, making fun sounds as they went. One sunny day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim brought his favorite vehicle, a big red truck. At the park, they found a dry, sandy spot to play. Tim drove his truck up and down the sand hills, having a great time. But then, it was time to leave the park. Tim's mom called him to go home. Tim was so tired from playing that he forgot his truck in the sand. When they got home, Tim realized he left his vehicle at the park. Tim and his mom went back to the park, and they found the big red truck right where Tim left it. They both smiled and went home, happy to have found the truck.

Joe and Simon were playing in the backyard. Simon wanted to build a track for his toy car. He cleared some stones and twigs and started building the track. Joe was watching with amazement and asked if he could help. Simon replied, “No, I got it.” Joe started running around the track. Suddenly, he stepped on a dead twig and his foot got stuck in the track. He shouted, “Help! I can’t stop!” Simon knew Joe would eventually come to an obstacle in the track. He had predicted this would happen. Joe tried to remove his foot, but it wouldn’t budge. Simon then grabbed a stick and started to slowly move the half-built track apart. Joe stepped out of it, his foot unscathed. Joe thanked Simon for his help and Simon said, “I told you to stop!” They both laughed and kept playing. Joe was careful to avoid stepping on the track this time. Eventually, they finished building the track. It was amazing. They cheered and thanked each other. The track they created was built fully, with no obstacles left in it. They had successfully solved the conflict with teamwork and their amazing track.
