Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Thoughtful Girl and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a thoughtful girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, at noon, her mom asked her to add some sugar to the cake mix. Lily was excited to help and quickly added the sugar. As they were mixing the cake, there was a loud knock on the door. Lily's mom went to see who it was. To her surprise, it was a big, friendly bear! The bear had smelled the yummy cake and wanted to join them. Lily's mom, feeling kind, invited the bear in to help. The bear was very good at mixing the cake. He added even more sugar, making it extra sweet. When the cake was done, they all sat down to eat it together. It was the best cake they had ever tasted! From that day on, the bear became their friend and they always made cakes together.

Once upon a time, there was a poor man who lived in a small village. All day, he would pass by a shield that hung in the village square. Everyday, the shield gave him strength. One day, the poor man decided to pass by the shield and take it with him. He thought it would protect him, and make him feel safe from the outside world. But as the poor man passed by the shield, the village people saw him and quickly chased after him. The poor man was soon surrounded and he had no place to hide. The poor man knew his fate was sealed, and sadly he had to give up the shield. He was arrested and taken away, never to return. The poor man left with nothing but sorrow as the shield remained in the village square.

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a modern train set that could make sounds and lights. They played with the train every day, and sometimes they did not want to stop. One night, their mom said, "It is time for bed, Sara and Tom. You need to wash your hair with shampoo and brush your teeth." "But we want to play with the train more!" Sara and Tom said. "You can play with the train tomorrow, but now you need to sleep. Sleep is good for you. It makes you grow and learn and feel happy." Mom said. Sara and Tom did not listen. They said, "No, no, no!" and ran to their room with the train. They closed the door and turned on the train. They made the train go around and around, and they laughed and shouted. Mom shook her head and left them alone. She hoped they would get tired soon and go to sleep. But Sara and Tom did not get tired. They played with the train all night long. They did not yawn, they did not close their eyes, they did not rest. They only played with the train. The next morning, Sara and Tom felt very bad. They had a headache, they had a sore throat, they had a runny nose. They did not want to eat, they did not want to play, they did not want to do anything. They only wanted to sleep. Mom came to their room and saw them in their bed. She felt sorry for them. She said, "Sara and Tom, you are sick. You played with the train too much and did not sleep. That was not a good idea. Sleep is important for your health and happiness. Next time, you should listen to me and go to bed on time." Sara and Tom nodded. They said, "We are sorry, Mom. We were wrong. We will listen to you next time. We will sleep when you tell us to sleep. We will wash our hair with shampoo and brush our teeth. We will not play with the train all night long." Mom smiled and hugged them. She said, "I love you, Sara and Tom. I hope you feel better soon. And I hope you learned your lesson. Sleep is good for you. And so is shampoo." The end.
