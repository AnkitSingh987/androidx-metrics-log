Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Basket and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a boy who was very sleepy. He yawned and stretched his arms wide. He had a plan to stay awake. He wanted to pick some grapes. So, he grabbed his basket and ran off to the nearby grape farm. He was so excited. He plucked a few grapes off the vine and put them in his basket. He looked around, enjoying the sun and the sweet smell in the air. When he was done, he went back home, happy and full of grapes. When he arrived, he was greeted with a loud applause! He smiled and looked around at the people who were all clapping. His mom was there, and she was proud of him. He was excited to be cheered like that, and he joined in with the applause. The day had been perfect. He enjoyed his little adventure and was glad to be home safe and sound. He was even more glad because he had gotten to share this wonderful day with his family. He waved goodbye to them and said, "Thank you!". Later that night, he settled in to his bed, all cozy and sleepy, still smiling from the day's adventure and all the wonderful applause.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She wanted to make a pretty dress for her doll. Sue asked her mom, "Can you help me make a dress for my doll?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's use some cloth and a needle to make the dress." They started to make the dress together. Sue was very happy. As they were making the dress, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into the room through the open window. The bird picked up the needle with its beak and flew away. Sue and her mom were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. "Now we need a new needle to finish the dress," said Sue's mom. They went to the store, bought a new needle, and finished making the beautiful dress for Sue's doll. In the end, Sue and her mom had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a mild and pretty landscape, there was a big tree. Under the tree lived a kind dog named Bow. Bow loved to play with his ball all day. One day, Bow met a little bird. The bird said, "Bow, can you help me? I lost my nest. Can you find it for me?" Bow nodded and said, "Yes, I will help you, little bird." Bow and the little bird looked for the nest all day. They asked other animals if they saw it, but no one knew. Then, Bow saw the nest high up in the big tree. Bow jumped and jumped, and finally got the nest. The little bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Bow!"

Once upon a time, in a big room, there was a soft teddy bear named Tim. Tim had a zipper on his back. One day, the zipper broke and all the soft stuff inside Tim came out. Tim was sad. Tim's best friend, a little girl named Lily, saw that Tim was sad. She said, "Tim, what's wrong?" Tim told her about his broken zipper. Lily wanted to help Tim. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will fix your zipper and make you happy again." Lily asked her mom for help. Her mom knew how to restore the zipper. She took a needle and thread and fixed the zipper. Then, she put all the soft stuff back inside Tim. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily and mom!" Lily hugged Tim and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad you're happy again!" Tim and Lily played together and had lots of fun. They were best friends forever. And Tim's zipper never broke again.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining bright in the sky. Lily's mom said, "Lily, I suggest you wear your sunglasses so your eyes don't hurt." Lily put on her sunglasses and smiled. In the park, Lily saw a thin cat. The cat was sitting under a tree. Lily asked the cat, "Why are you so thin, cat?" The cat said, "I am hungry. I can't find any food." Lily felt sad for the cat. Lily told her mom about the thin cat. Her mom said, "Let's give the cat some food from our home." They went home and got some food for the cat. The cat was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" Now, the thin cat had food and a new friend.
