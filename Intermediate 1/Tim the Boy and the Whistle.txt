Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Whistle

One day, a boy named Tim found a whistle on the floor. It was empty and had no sound. He wanted to make it work, so he showed it to his mom. "Mom, how can I make the whistle start?" Tim asked. Mom looked at the whistle and smiled. "Blow into it, Tim," she said. So Tim blew into the whistle, but still no sound came out. Tim felt sad and went to his room. Later, Tim's dad came home with a big box. Tim opened the box and found a new whistle inside. He blew into the new whistle, and it made a loud sound! Tim was so happy and surprised. He ran to his mom and dad, "Thank you for the new whistle!" And they all played together, making fun sounds with the new whistle.

Once upon a time, there was a mechanic named Tom. Tom loved to fix cars and make them zoom fast. He was very good at his job and all the people in the town liked him. One day, Tom was feeling miserable. He did not want to fix cars or make them zoom. He just wanted to stay in his bed. His friend, Sam, came to visit him. Sam saw that Tom was sad and asked him why. Tom told Sam that he missed his family who lived far away. Sam had an idea to make Tom feel better. They went to the park and flew a kite that looked like a car. They made the kite zoom in the sky, just like the cars Tom fixed. Tom felt happy again and went back to being the best mechanic in town.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a slow bear named Ben. Ben loved honey very much. One day, he found a big bee nest full of honey in a tall tree. Ben wanted to get the honey but he did not want to disturb the bees. He knew that if he made the bees mad, they might sting him. So, he thought of a good plan. Ben waited until the bees went to find more flowers. When the bees were gone, Ben climbed the tree very slowly and carefully. He took some honey from the nest, but not all of it. He knew that the bees needed honey too. After Ben got the honey, he climbed down the tree and went home. He was very happy with his honey and did not get stung by the bees. The moral of the story is to be slow and careful, and not to disturb others when taking what you need.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an adventurous boy who loved to play outside. One day, Tim made a plan to go on a big adventure in his backyard. Tim put on his hat and went outside. He climbed the small hill and found a big tree. Tim had a memory of his dad telling him about a treasure hidden under the tree. He wanted to find the treasure, so he started to dig with his hands. After a while, Tim found a small box. He opened the box and saw shiny rocks and a note from his dad. The note said, "Good job, Tim! You found the treasure!" Tim was very happy and proud. He took his treasure inside to show his mom and dad. They all smiled and hugged. And that was the end of Tim's adventurous day.

One day, Tom went to the park with his mom. The sun was very hot. Tom saw an ice-cream man. He wanted to buy an ice-cream. "Mom, can I have an ice-cream?" Tom asked. His mom said, "Yes, but only one." Tom was very happy. He got a big ice-cream with three colors. Tom saw a big dog in the park. He wanted to play with the dog. But, he did not want to lose his ice-cream. So, he put his ice-cream on a bench and went to play with the dog. When Tom came back, his ice-cream was gone! He saw a big cat near the bench. He thought the cat ate his ice-cream. Tom was very sad and serious. He said, "Bad cat! You ate my ice-cream!" But then, the cat moved and Tom saw his ice-cream. It did not lie on the bench, but on the ground. The ice-cream was still there, but it was all melted. Tom learned that he should eat his ice-cream first before playing.
