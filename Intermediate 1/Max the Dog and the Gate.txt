Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Gate

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max lived in a house with a big yard. In the yard, there was a gate. Max liked to play in the yard and look at the gate. One day, Max saw a different dog on the other side of the gate. Max did not recognize the dog. The different dog wanted to play with Max's toys. Max did not want to share his toys with the different dog. This made Max sad and mad. Max barked at the different dog. The different dog barked back. Max's owner came outside and saw the two dogs. The owner opened the gate and let the different dog come in. Max and the different dog played together and shared the toys. They became good friends and Max was happy.

One day, a cheerful dog named Will went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a long rope. The rope had a twist in it. Will wanted to play with the rope. "Hi, friend!" said a little bird on the tree. "Do you want to play with the rope?" "Yes, I do!" said Will, wagging his tail. "But the rope has a twist. Can you help me?" The little bird flew down and helped Will untwist the rope. They played together all day, swinging on the rope and running around the tree. They were very happy. At the end of the day, Will and the little bird were tired. They sat under the tree, smiling. "Thank you for playing with me," said Will. "You are a good friend." "You're welcome," said the little bird. "I had fun too. Let's play again soon!"

In a small town, there was a big billboard. On the billboard, there was a picture of a mighty lion. The lion was strong and big. The people in the town liked the lion on the billboard. One day, a little girl named Lily walked by the billboard. She looked up at the mighty lion and said, "Mr. Lion, you are so big and strong! I wish I could be like you." To her surprise, the lion on the billboard talked back! "Lily, you can be mighty too. Just believe in yourself," said the lion. Lily was so happy to hear this. She went to her room and tried to fit into her lion costume. But she couldn't fit into it anymore, she had grown too big. She felt sad. Then, she remembered what the lion said and decided to make a new costume. Lily worked hard and made a beautiful new lion costume. She put it on and felt mighty, just like the lion on the billboard. The people in the town were surprised to see a little mighty lion walking around. They all cheered for Lily, and she knew that she was mighty too.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named True. True was a happy boy who loved to play with his friends. One day, True saw a small bird stuck in a tree. The bird was miserable and could not fly. True wanted to help the bird, so he tried to climb the tree. He tried and tried, but he could not succeed. True felt sad but did not give up. He asked his friends for help. Together, they made a plan to save the bird. With the help of his friends, True was finally able to succeed and save the miserable bird. The bird was very happy and flew away. True and his friends were happy too. They learned that when they work together, they can do anything. The moral of the story is that teamwork makes hard things easier.

Once upon a time, there was a great big tree with many branches. On one of the branches, there was a big sack. The sack was full of toys. Every day, kids would come and play with the toys from the sack. They would laugh and have fun. One day, a big mean bird came to the tree. The bird saw the sack on the branch and wanted to take it. The bird had a plan. It wanted to take the sack and all the toys inside for itself. So, the bird flew to the branch and started to pull the sack away. The kids saw the bird and tried to stop it. They shouted and cried, but the bird did not care. The bird pulled harder and harder. Then, something unexpected happened. The branch broke! The sack fell down and all the toys spilled out. The bird flew away, but the toys were broken. The kids were sad and could not play anymore. The great big tree was not so great anymore.
