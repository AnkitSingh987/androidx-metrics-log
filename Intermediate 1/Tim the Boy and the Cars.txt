Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cars

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He had a big case with many cars inside. Every day, Tim would open the case and release his cars to race them. One sunny day, Tim met a new friend named Sam. Sam also liked toy cars. They both wanted to see who had the fastest car. They were very competitive. They decided to have a race with their cars. Tim said, "Let's race our cars!" Sam agreed and said, "Okay, let's go!" They both opened their cases and released their cars. The cars raced very fast. Tim's car won the race. Sam was happy for Tim and said, "Good job, Tim!" They both laughed and played with their cars for the rest of the day.

Once there was a little frog who loved to dive in the pond. Every day he would jump into the cool water and swim around. But one day he spotted something shiny in the cobweb at the edge of the pond. He hopped closer and saw it was a sparkling jewel! He wanted it, but he just couldn't reach it. He tried to dive, but the cobweb was too far from the water. He wanted it so badly, but he just couldn't get it. He saw other bigger frogs dive in the water, but they couldn't reach it either. They looked so envious and sad. He wanted to help. He took a big jump and dive, and finally he reached the cobweb! He grabbed the jewel, and all the other frogs cheered! He hopped proudly back to the water and all the frogs clapped for him. They were so happy, and he was so proud. He had done it all on his own.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a curious little bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly and see new things. One day, he saw a small tree trying to grow tall, but it was having a hard time. Tim flew down and said to the tree, "Hello, little tree! You look like you need help to grow big and strong. I will support you!" The tree was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." Tim found a long stick and put it next to the tree. He tied the tree to the stick with a soft vine. The stick helped the tree stand up straight and tall. As the days went by, Tim visited the tree every day. He sang songs and told stories to the tree. The tree grew bigger and stronger with Tim's support. One day, the tree was so big and tall that it could support Tim. Tim built a nest in the tree, and they lived happily together in the valley.

One day, a shy little girl named Mary had a big surprise! A trumpet! The trumpet was her favourite colour, yellow. Mary was very excited, so she started to play the trumpet immediately. She was so proud of it, but it made a very loud sound. That scared Mary and she quickly put it away. Suddenly, Mary heard someone coming. It was her brother, John. John was looking for her. When he saw Mary, he said to her: "Hey! Tell me what you were up to!" Mary was very shy, so she was too afraid to say anything at all. John then noticed the trumpet and said: "Ah, I see... you got a trumpet. That's great!" John then started to play the trumpet, but it made a loud sound again. That scared Mary again and she started to relax. But then John noticed a group of people running towards them. Those people were very angry with John for playing the trumpet, so they started to yell at him. The group then started to beat John. Poor Mary cried and ran away, but the group caught her too. When the people were done, Mary and John were both bleeding. The trumpet had been broken, and so were Mary and John's dreams. They were both very sad and worried. Mary's surprises never seemed to work out very well.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim was very excited to play on the swings and the slide. Before they left, Tim's mom made sure he had his buckle on his shoes. At the park, Tim saw a big dog. Tim felt scared. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I am scared of the big dog." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. The dog is nice." Tim's mom talked to the dog's owner, and they let Tim pet the dog. Tim was not scared anymore. He played with the dog and had fun. After playing, Tim and his mom went home. Tim was happy he made a new friend at the park.
