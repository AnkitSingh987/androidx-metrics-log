Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Squirrel and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts in the big park. One sunny day, Sam saw a big nut on a high branch. He wanted to reach it, but it was too high. Sam asked his friend, a tall giraffe named Gina, "Can you help me reach the nut, please?" Gina said, "Of course, Sam!" She stretched her long neck up to the high branch and got the nut for Sam. But the nut was filthy from being on the ground. Sam and Gina went to the pond to wash the nut. They dipped the nut in the water, and it became clean and shiny. Sam was so happy to have a nice nut to eat, and he thanked Gina for her help. From that day on, Sam and Gina played together in the big park, helping each other find and clean nuts. They became the best of friends and shared many fun adventures. And they always made sure their nuts were clean before they ate them.

Once upon a time, there was a woman. She was a guilty woman. She took things that were not hers. One day, she saw a big, red ball. It was so pretty that she wanted it. She took the ball and ran away. The ball started to rise up in the sky, and the woman held on tight. She was scared, but she did not let go of the ball. The ball went up and up. The woman looked down and saw the ground far away. The ball kept rising, and the woman knew she was in trouble. She wished she had not taken the ball. But it was too late. The ball went up and up, and the guilty woman was never seen again.

Lily and Ben like to play pirate. They have a big box that is their ship. They have hats and swords and a map. They are ready for an adventure. "Ahoy, matey!" Ben says to Lily. "We need to find the treasure. Do you have the map?" "Yes, captain!" Lily says. She holds up the map. It has a big X on it. "The treasure is on the island. We have to sail there." They pretend to sail the box on the floor. They make noises like the wind and the waves. They see the island. It is a pillow with a blanket on it. "Land ho!" Ben shouts. "Let's go get the treasure!" They jump out of the box and run to the island. They lift the blanket. They see a shiny box. It is the treasure. They open it. It has candy and stickers and toys. "Wow!" Lily says. "This is the best treasure ever!" They hug and cheer. They share the candy and stickers and toys. They are happy pirates. They have a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty flower. The flower did not want to bloom. It was a selfish flower. All the other flowers bloomed and looked happy. The sun was warm and the sky was blue. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the flowers and wanted to dance. She danced around the flowers that bloomed. But she did not dance near the selfish flower. The selfish flower felt sad. The selfish flower thought, "I want the girl to dance near me too." So, the flower tried hard to bloom. And then, the flower bloomed! The little girl saw the flower and danced near it. The flower was happy and not selfish anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. Lucy saw a small bird sitting on a bench. The bird looked hungry, so Lucy had a thoughtful idea. Lucy asked her mom, "Can we give some food to the bird?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can share your snack with the bird." Lucy took some food from her bag and put it on the bench. The bird ate the food and was very happy. The bird said, "Thank you, Lucy, for sharing your food with me." Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome, bird! I'm glad I could help you." Lucy and her mom went home, feeling happy that they could make a new friend at the park.
