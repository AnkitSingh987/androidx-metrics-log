Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bird and the Leaves

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bob. Bob had a light blue body and loved to sing. Every morning, he would sing a loud alarm to wake up his friends in the forest. One day, a small squirrel named Sam heard Bob's alarm. Sam said, "Hi, Bob! Your song is so nice. Can you teach me to sing like you?" Bob smiled and said, "Of course, Sam! Let's sing together." Bob and Sam sang and played all day. When the sun went down, it was time for bed. Bob said, "Sam, it's cold at night. You can cover yourself with these leaves to stay warm." Sam thanked Bob and covered himself with the leaves. They both slept well, ready for another day of fun and singing.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird lived in a big tree. The bird liked to sing and play with other birds. One day, the bird was flying and it hit a branch. The bird felt pain and started to cry. The bird's friends heard the cry and came to help. The friends took care of the bird until the pain was gone. The bird was happy again. They all played and sang together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Bingo. Bingo loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Bingo saw his friend Timmy riding a bike. Bingo wanted to ride a bike too, but he needed a helmet. Bingo asked his mom, "Mom, can I have a helmet?" His mom said, "Yes, Bingo. I will permit you to have a helmet." Bingo was very happy. His mom bought him a shiny red helmet. Bingo put on his helmet and rode his bike with Timmy. They had a lot of fun together. Bingo was glad that his mom permitted him to have a helmet so he could play with his friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a clean pilot named Tim. He loved to fly his big plane in the sky. Tim was very good at his job and always made sure his plane was clean and safe. One day, while Tim was flying, a big bug flew into the plane. The bug was very noisy and scared the people on the plane. Tim wanted to help everyone feel safe again. Tim saw the bug and decided to slap it. He slapped the bug with his hand and it fell down. The people on the plane were happy and not scared anymore. Tim went back to flying his big plane and everyone had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty farm. On the farm, there was a big bull. The bull had a red ball. He liked to play with the red ball all day. One day, the bull could not find his red ball. He looked and looked, but the ball was lost. The bull was very sad. He did not want to lose his red ball. The other animals on the farm saw the bull was sad. They all helped to look for the red ball. At last, the little dog found the ball under a big tree. The bull was so happy! He played with his red ball and his friends all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the bathroom. He saw that it was very dirty. Tim did not like dirty things, so he decided to clean it. He got a mop and started to wipe the floor. Tim was happy because he liked to help his mom. While Tim was cleaning, he found a big box behind the toilet. He opened it and saw a lot of toys inside. Tim was so happy and started to play with them. He forgot about the dirty bathroom and played with his new toys all day. Later, Tim's mom came to the bathroom and saw the toys. She smiled and said, "Oh Tim, these are your old toys! I hid them here when you were a baby." Tim was surprised and happy. He played with his old toys and cleaned the bathroom with his mom. They both smiled and had a fun day together.
