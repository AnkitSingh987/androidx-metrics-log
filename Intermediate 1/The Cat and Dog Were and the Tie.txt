Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cat and Dog Were and the Tie

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. The cat saw a spicy pepper on the table. The dog saw a tie on the floor. The cat said, "Dog, let's play a game! I will rub the spicy pepper on the tie, and you will wear it." The dog said, "Okay, Cat! That sounds fun!" So the cat rubbed the spicy pepper on the tie. The dog put the tie on his neck. At first, the dog was happy. But soon, his neck started to feel hot. "Cat, my neck is hot! I don't like this game!" said the dog. The cat laughed and said, "I'm sorry, Dog. I thought it would be fun." The dog took off the tie, but his neck still felt hot. The dog was sad, and the cat felt bad for making his friend feel bad. They did not play that game again.

Once upon a time, there was a new infant. She was small and happy. The infant was ready to explore the world. One day, the infant decided to wander. She moved her little legs and stepped onto the grass. It felt so strange and exciting. She kept wandering, her little eyes exploring the world around her. Everything was so new and different. The infant wandered to look at the trees, the flowers and the clouds in the sky. She paused to feel the warmth of the sun on her skin. Before long, the infant was starting to get tired. She found a cozy spot amongst some tall grass and curled up to rest. After a while, she felt energized and ready to wander again. The infant explored until the sun went down. Then, she snuggled up and fell fast asleep. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was bored. He did not like to play with his toys anymore. He wanted to find something new and fun. One day, Tim saw a shiny gold rock in his yard. He decided to move the rock and see what was under it. When he moved the rock, he found a big hole. Tim was very excited. Tim went into the hole and found a new world full of fun things to do. There were big slides and swings. Tim played all day and was not bored anymore. He was very happy that he moved the gold rock.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. One day, Max saw a big tree with a lot of leaves. He wanted to shake the tree to see what would happen. As Max shook the tree, a small bird fell out of its nest. The bird was frightened and started to cry. Max felt bad and wanted to help the bird. He tried to wake the bird's mom by barking loudly. Soon, the mom bird came and saw her baby on the ground. The mom bird thanked Max for helping her baby. Max learned that it's important to be kind and help others when they are in trouble. From that day on, Max never shook trees again, and he became friends with the mom bird and her baby.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there was a big festival. Everyone was happy and excited. They all wanted to bring something special to share. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to bring his favorite toy car to the festival. But his big sister, Sue, said, "No, you can't bring that. It's too small. Let's bring our big red ball instead." Tim was sad, but he agreed to bring the big red ball. At the festival, Tim and Sue played with the big red ball. But then, the ball rolled away and got lost in the crowd. Tim was very upset. He said, "I wish we brought my toy car instead." Sue felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. Let's go find your toy car and bring it to the festival." They found the toy car and brought it to the festival. Tim was happy again, and they had a great time playing with the toy car together.
