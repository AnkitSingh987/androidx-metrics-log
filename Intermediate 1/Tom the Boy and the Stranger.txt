Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Stranger

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind boy named Tom. Tom liked to help people. One day, he saw a stranger who needed help. The stranger was an old man with a big bag. The old man said, "Can you help me remove my big bag from the ground?" Tom said, "Yes, I can help you." Tom picked up the big bag and gave it to the stranger. The stranger thanked Tom for his help. After that, the stranger and Tom became friends. They played together and had fun. The stranger was very kind to Tom, and they were happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer. He lived in a small house with his dog. The farmer was not happy. He felt gloomy. One day, the farmer went outside to count his chickens. He counted one, two, three, four, five chickens. But one chicken was missing. The farmer was sad. The farmer and his dog looked for the missing chicken. They looked near the house, they looked near the trees. Then, they found the chicken. The farmer was happy, and he was not gloomy anymore. They all went back to the house and had a good day.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree was green and had many leaves. In the tree, there was a pink bird. The pink bird liked to sing and play. One day, the pink bird saw a swing hanging from the tree. The swing was fun. The pink bird wanted to hang on the swing. So, the pink bird jumped on the swing and started to swing back and forth. The pink bird was very happy. As the pink bird was swinging, a friend came. The friend was a green bug. The green bug wanted to play too. The pink bird and the green bug played on the swing all day. They had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Lucy. She loved to read big books with many pages. Every day, she would sit under a tree and read her favorite book. One day, Lucy met a small talking bird named Tim. Tim was very curious about Lucy's book. He asked her, "What are you doing with that?" Lucy said, "I am reading the pages of this book. It helps me understand new things." Tim wanted to be clever too, so he asked Lucy to teach him. She agreed and started to read the book out loud to him. As they read together, Tim began to understand the words on the pages. They both became very clever and were happy to learn new things together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky toy. He loved to talk to his toy all day. One day, Tim took his toy to the park. At the park, Tim saw another boy named Sam. Sam wanted to play with Tim's lucky toy. Tim did not want to share his toy. They both used their mouths to talk and argue about the toy. In the end, Tim and Sam could not agree. They both got mad and went their own ways. Tim's lucky toy was not so lucky after all. The day at the park was not fun for Tim or Sam.

One day, Tom went to get a haircut. He sat in the big chair and the nice lady with the scissors began to cut his hair. Tom was a little scared, but he knew he had to be brave. The lady said, "Don't worry, Tom. You'll get a fair haircut." Tom's mom was there too. She took out her phone and said, "I will record this, so you can see how good you look after your haircut." Tom smiled and felt better. He watched his mom record him while the nice lady cut his hair. When the haircut was done, Tom looked in the mirror. He was happy with his fair haircut. The nice lady gave him a sticker for being brave. Tom's mom showed him the video she recorded. Tom liked his haircut even more. He knew that the next time he needed a haircut, he wouldn't be scared.
