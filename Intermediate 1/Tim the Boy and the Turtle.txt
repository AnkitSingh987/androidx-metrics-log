Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Turtle

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his dog, Max. Max was a good guard dog. He would always watch over Tim when they played outside. One day, Tim and Max went to the park. They saw a slow turtle. The turtle said, "Hi, I am Tom. Can you help me find my home?" Tim and Max wanted to help Tom. So, they walked with him to find his home. As they walked, Tim would urge Max to be slow and careful, so they didn't scare Tom. Tim said, "Max, be slow and nice to Tom." Max listened to Tim and was a good friend to Tom. They found Tom's home and everyone was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He started to climb, but his hands were wet. Tim did not want to fall, so he stopped and looked for help. A nice girl named Sue saw Tim and wanted to help him. She got a towel to dry his hands. Tim smiled at Sue and said, "Thank you!" Now Tim could climb the tree without being scared of falling. But as Tim climbed higher, he saw a cat stuck in the tree. The cat was scared and crying. Tim and Sue wanted to help the cat. They asked a tall man to help them. The man reached up and got the cat down. Tim, Sue, and the cat all smiled. They were happy they could help each other.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He had a dream to win a big victory. Every day he let his friends help him to make his dream come true. He worked very hard and used all his strength to try his best. One day, a magical fairy appeared and said, "I can help you. I can let you have your victory if you finish this challenge." The old man was excited. He agreed and the fairy gave him a magical coat. The old man put on the magical coat and let the fairy cast a special spell. Suddenly, he found himself in a race track. He raced and raced as fast as he could. Soon, he was winning. He kept going faster and faster until he passed the finish line. He had won the race and achieved his victory. The old man was very happy. He thanked the magical fairy, who smiled and said, "Let this victory be an encouragement to never give up on your dreams." The old man smiled and thanked the fairy once again.

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to joke a lot. He had many friends who liked his jokes. One day, Tom saw a mild cat. The cat was very nice and soft. Tom wanted to play with the cat. Tom took the cat to the park. At the park, they saw two dogs. The dogs started to fight. They were loud and scary. Tom and the cat did not like the fight. They wanted to play and have fun. Tom had an idea. He told a joke to the dogs. The dogs stopped fighting. They laughed at Tom's joke. Now, the dogs, Tom, and the cat all played together. They had a great day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a helpful little bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop around and play with his friends. One day, as he was hopping, he saw a big red berry on the ground. Bobo picked up the berry and thought about his friend, Birdy the bird. He knew Birdy loved to eat berries, so he decided to take it to her. As he hopped along, he met his friend Squirrel, who also liked berries. Bobo realized he could share the berry with both of his friends. When Bobo found Birdy, he gave her the berry. Birdy was so happy and thanked Bobo for being helpful. Then, they went to find Squirrel and shared the berry with him too. All three friends were happy and enjoyed the yummy berry together. From that day on, they always shared and helped each other.
