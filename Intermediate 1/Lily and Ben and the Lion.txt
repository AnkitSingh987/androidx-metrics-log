Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Lion

Lily and Ben liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a band of little animals. They had a lion, a monkey, a giraffe, a zebra and an elephant. They made the animals talk and sing and dance. One day, Lily and Ben found a new toy in their room. It was a big red button. They did not know what it did, but they wanted to try it. They pushed the button and heard a loud beep. Then, something strange happened. Their band of little animals started to shrink. They became smaller and smaller, until they were as tiny as ants. "Oh no!" Lily cried. "What did we do? Our animals are so small!" "Maybe the button can make them big again," Ben said. He pushed the button again, but nothing happened. He pushed it harder and harder, but still nothing happened. The band of little animals was scared and confused. They did not like being so small. They could not talk or sing or dance anymore. They could only squeak and hide. They were very grumpy. "Help us, Lily and Ben!" the lion squeaked. "We want to be big again!" Lily and Ben heard the lion's squeak. They looked around and saw their band of little animals on the floor. They felt sorry for them. They picked them up gently and put them in a shoe box. "We are sorry, animals," Lily said. "We did not mean to shrink you. We will find a way to make you big again." "We love you, animals," Ben said. "We will not push the button again." They hugged the shoe box and hoped that their band of little animals would be happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin liked to swim in the big blue sea with his friends. They would play games and have fun all day long. One day, Fin saw a shiny thing in the water. He wanted to get it, but it was difficult to reach. Fin's friends told him not to try, but Fin didn't listen. He swam very hard and got the shiny thing. But he swam too far away from his friends. Fin's friends were sad and worried. They swam to find Fin and help him. When they found him, they were all happy again. But Fin's friends had to punish him for not listening. They told him he couldn't play games for one day. Fin felt sad but he understood. He learned that it's important to listen to his friends and not do difficult things alone. From that day on, Fin always listened to his friends and they all swam happily together in the big blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Bob. Bob liked to lie down in the sun and take naps. One day, he found a big piece of steel in his yard. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was fun to play with. Bob's friend, Cat, came over to play. Cat saw the steel and said, "What is that, Bob?" Bob said, "I don't know, but it's fun to play with!" So, Bob and Cat played with the steel all day long. They pushed it, rolled it, and even tried to sit on it. After a long day of playing, Bob and Cat were very tired. They decided to lie down under a tree and take a nap. As they slept, the sun went down and the moon came up. The smelly dog and his friend Cat had a fun day playing with the steel, and they dreamed of more adventures to come.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in his backyard. One sunny day, he saw a big ball in the backyard. He wanted to grab the ball and play with it. But there was a stubborn dog named Max who lived next door. Max did not like to share. Max saw Tim and the ball, and he wanted the ball too. Max jumped into the backyard and tried to grab the ball before Tim could. Tim and Max both wanted the ball, so they both tried to grab it at the same time. They pulled and pulled on the ball, but neither would let go. They were both very stubborn. Tim's mom came outside and saw them fighting over the ball. She told them to stop and share the ball. Tim and Max listened to her and decided to play together. They had lots of fun in the backyard, and they learned that sharing was more fun than fighting.
