Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Horse and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a yellow horse. The horse liked to travel far and wide. One day, the horse went on a long trip. The sun was shining, and the horse was happy. As the horse traveled, it met a big, friendly dog. The dog wanted to travel with the horse. So, they went on their way together. They played and had fun on their trip. But one day, something unexpected happened. The yellow horse and the dog found a magic pond. They drank the water and suddenly, the horse turned into a dog and the dog turned into a horse! They were very surprised, but they were still friends. They continued their trip, now with the dog as a horse and the horse as a dog.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn cat named Tom. He liked to play with a ball, but he did not like to share. One day, Tom got hurt and needed help to heal. His friend, a dog named Max, came to see him. Max said, "Tom, let me help you heal. I can bring you a thing that will make you feel better." Tom did not want help, but he was in pain. So, he let Max go and find the thing. Max came back with a big, soft pillow. Tom was surprised. He thought Max would bring a toy or a treat. But the pillow was just what Tom needed to heal. They put the pillow under Tom's hurt leg, and he started to feel better. Tom said, "Thank you, Max. I am sorry I was stubborn. I will share my ball with you from now on." Max was happy, and they became the best of friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to find a new path to play on. Tim was not fearful. He was brave and liked to think a lot. As he walked, he found a new path. It was small and twisty. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, loud noise came from the trees. Tim felt fearful for the first time. He did not know what the noise was. He tried to think, but he was too scared. He ran away from the path, back to his home. When he got home, he told his mom about the noise. She said it was just a big truck. But Tim was still scared. He did not want to go back to the path ever again. And so, Tim never found a new place to play.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a stubborn boy named Tom. Tom had a little sister named Sue. Tom did not like to listen to his mom and dad. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, you need to listen to me. You are sick. You have a bad lung. You need to stay in bed and rest." Tom did not listen to his mom. He wanted to play outside with his friends. Tom went outside to play. He was still very sick. Sue saw Tom and said, "Tom, you should listen to mom. Your lung is bad, and you need to rest." Tom did not listen to Sue. He kept playing with his friends. Tom played all day. He did not rest. His lung got worse. At the end of the day, Tom felt very bad. He could not play anymore. Tom had to go to the hospital because he did not listen to his mom and sister.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim heard his dad mention a big mountain. He wanted to see the mountain, but his dad said it was difficult to get there. One sunny day, Tim walked and walked until he saw the mountain. It was very big and far away. Tim was tired, but he wanted to get closer. So, he kept walking and walking. Finally, Tim reached the mountain. He was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The mountain started to move! It was not a mountain at all, but a giant turtle! Tim laughed and played with the turtle. They became good friends and had lots of fun together.
