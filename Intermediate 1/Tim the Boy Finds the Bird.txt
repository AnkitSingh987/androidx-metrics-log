Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a rainy day, and Tim loved to jump in the puddles. As he splashed around, he saw a small bird sitting on a branch, looking sad. "Hi, bird! Why are you sad?" Tim questioned. The bird looked at Tim and said, "I can't find any spicy worms to eat. The rain washed them away." Tim wanted to help the bird. He thought of an idea. "Let's look for spicy worms together!" he said. The bird was happy and they both started looking for spicy worms. As they looked, the rain stopped and the sun came out. Tim and the bird found some spicy worms under a big leaf. The bird was so happy and thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, rain or shine. And the bird always shared its spicy worms with Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little doctor who loved to help people. He was very good at making people feel better when they were sick. One day, the doctor felt very helpless because he was very tired and wanted to sleep. While the doctor was sleeping, a big wind came and blew him far away. The doctor woke up and saw that he was in a new place. He was scared, but he saw that there were many sick people who needed his help. The doctor started to help the sick people, and they all felt better. Then, the wind came back and took the doctor back to his home. The doctor was happy to be home, but he was also happy that he could help the sick people in the new place. The wind was not so bad after all.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a small black cat. The cat was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to save the cat, so he called his dad for help. His dad came and helped Tim save the black cat from the tree. The cat was very happy and so was Tim. They all went back to their house. Inside the house, Tim's dad gave him a lecture. He told Tim to be careful when helping animals. Tim listened and learned. He promised to be careful next time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hero. The hero was very strong and wore tight clothes. He always helped people and made them smile. Everyone loved the hero. One day, the hero went to a big party. There were lots of people at the party. They all wanted to see the hero dance. The hero was very excited to dance with his friends. The music started to play, and the hero began to dance. He danced and danced, and everyone clapped their hands. The hero's tight clothes did not stop him from dancing. The people laughed and had fun. In the end, the hero and his friends danced all night long.

Once, there was a goose who lived near a pond. The goose was very careful. She liked to play with her friends, the duck and the frog. One day, they found a big box near the pond. The goose said, "Let's open the box!" The duck and the frog agreed. They all struggled to open the box. The box was very hard to open. Inside the box, they found a big cake. The goose, the duck, and the frog were very happy. They wanted to eat the cake. But they did not know that the cake was bad. They ate the cake and soon, they all felt sick. The goose, the duck, and the frog were very sad. They should have been more careful. The cake was not good for them.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty boy named Tim. One day, he went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with lots of pretty picks on it. He wanted to get one for his mom. "Mom, can I get a pick?" Tim asked. His mom said, "No, Tim. It's too high. You might fall." But Tim did not listen to his mom. He climbed the tree when she was not looking. When he got to the top, he began to shout, "Mom, look! I got a pick!" Suddenly, the tree started to move! It was not a tree, but a big, friendly giant. The giant said, "Hi, Tim. I see you like my picks. I will give you one if you promise to be a good boy and listen to your mom." Tim was surprised but said, "Yes, I promise." From that day on, Tim was a good boy. He always listened to his mom and never did naughty things again. The giant's pick was a reminder of his promise to be good.
