Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Tree

One day, a little cat named Kitty liked to wander outside. She saw a big tree and thought it would be easy to climb. Kitty wanted to see what was at the top, so she started to climb. As Kitty climbed higher, she saw a big bird far away. She thought, "I want to be friends with the bird!" But the bird flew away before Kitty could reach it. She was sad, but she kept climbing, hoping to see more friends. At the top of the tree, Kitty found a big nest. Inside the nest, there were beautiful eggs. She remembered the bird that flew away and knew that these were its eggs. As a result, Kitty decided to stay and protect the eggs until the bird came back. When the bird returned, it was happy to see Kitty taking care of its eggs. They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a vendor selling interesting toys. Tim saw a big, red ball and he wanted it. He said to his mom, "Mom, can I have that ball, please?" His mom said, "Not today, Tim. We have many toys at home. Let's play with them when we go back." But Tim insisted, "Please, mom, I really want it." His mom thought for a moment and said, "Okay, but you have to promise to share it with your friends." Later, Tim played with the big, red ball with his friends. They had so much fun. They threw the ball high up in the air and it went very far. But then, the ball got stuck in a tree. Tim and his friends were sad. Tim's mom saw them and said, "Don't worry, I will help you get the ball down." She found a long stick and used it to get the ball out of the tree. Tim and his friends were happy again. They played with the ball all day, just like Tim promised his mom. From that day on, Tim always shared his toys with his friends. He learned that playing together is more fun. And every time he saw the big, red ball, he remembered the day his mom got it for him and how happy it made him and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a purple car. It was a very special car. It could take you on a safari. A safari is where you see lots of animals. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go on a safari. He went to the purple car with his mom. They saw a big box near the car. Inside the box, there were big wheels. Tim's mom said, "We need to attach the wheels to the car." They put the big wheels on the purple car. Then, they went on the safari. They saw many animals. They saw a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a funny monkey. Tim was very happy. He loved the purple car and the safari.

One day, a little bug named Tim wanted to crawl to the big tree. He was very slow. On his way, he saw a stamp on the ground. The stamp had a picture of a spicy pepper on it. Tim thought it was pretty. Tim met a big bug named Bob. Bob said, "Be careful, little bug. The spicy pepper stamp is a warning. It means the tree has spicy leaves. They are not good to eat." But Tim did not listen. He crawled to the big tree and took a bite of a spicy leaf. It was very hot! Tim's mouth felt like fire. He was very sad and wished he had listened to Bob. In the end, Tim learned a lesson. He should have listened to his friend. The spicy leaves were not good for him.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat named Tom. Tom had a very important job to do. He had to protect a letter for his friend, Sam the dog. Tom was very proud of his job and wanted to make sure nothing happened to the letter. One day, Tom was walking in the park with the letter, when a big wind came. The wind blew the letter out of Tom's mouth and up into a tall tree! Tom was sad and did not know what to do. He tried to climb the tree, but he was too scared. Just then, a tiny ant named Ann came along. She saw Tom was sad and asked what was wrong. Tom told her about the letter and how he needed to protect it. Ann wanted to help, so she climbed the tree and got the letter for Tom. Tom was so happy and thanked Ann for her help. They became good friends, and Tom learned that even the smallest friends can do big things.
