Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Penguin and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a little penguin named Tim. Tim liked to play and have fun. One day, Tim saw a big hill. He wanted to climb the hill to see what was on the other side. As Tim started to climb the hill, he heard a scary noise. He was scared, but he wanted to keep going. He took a deep breath and climbed higher and higher. The scary noise got louder and louder. At the top of the hill, Tim saw a big, scary bird. The bird was making the scary noise. Tim was brave and said "Hi" to the bird. The bird stopped making the scary noise and said "Hi" back. They became friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a helpful spider. The spider liked to go around and help his friends. One day, the spider met a sad little bug. "Why are you sad, little bug?" asked the spider. "I can't find my way home," said the little bug, crying. The helpful spider wanted to help the little bug. So, they went on a journey to find the bug's home. They walked and walked, and met many other friends along the way. The helpful spider asked everyone if they knew where the little bug's home was. Finally, they found a big tree. The little bug was so happy. "This is my home!" said the little bug. "Thank you, helpful spider!" The helpful spider was happy too. They said goodbye and the spider went back to his small house, ready to help more friends.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play with toys. One day, Tom saw a shiny car that Lily had. He wanted it very much. He asked Lily if he could have it. Lily said no, it was her car and she loved it. Tom was angry and sad. He thought of a way to get the car. He found a broken doll that he did not like. He went to Lily and said, "I have a doll for you. It is very pretty. Do you want to trade?" Lily looked at the doll and saw that it was dirty and had no hair. She said no, she did not want the doll. She wanted her car. Tom was more angry and sad. He waited until Lily was not looking. He grabbed the car and ran away. Lily saw him and shouted, "Stop! That is my car! Give it back!" Tom did not stop. He ran to his house and hid the car under his bed. Lily was very sad and cried. She told her mom what Tom did. Her mom was angry and called Tom's mom. Tom's mom was angry and went to Tom's room. She saw the car under his bed. She said, "Tom, did you take Lily's car? That is very bad. You should not take things that are not yours. You should be ashamed of yourself. You need to give it back and say sorry." Tom felt ashamed. He knew he did a bad thing. He did not like the car anymore. He wanted to be friends with Lily again. He took the car and went to Lily's house. He said, "Lily, I am sorry. I took your car. It was wrong. Here is your car. Please forgive me." Lily was happy to see her car. She said, "Thank you, Tom. I forgive you. But you should not do that again. It is not nice. Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I want to play with you. You are my friend." They hugged and played with their toys. They were happy.

One day, Mary was feeling very bored. So she decided to go on an adventure! She went outside to collect something special. After a while, Mary found a pig! She was so excited, she ran over and started petting the animal. "Hi there, piggy!" she said. The pig didn't answer her. It just looked uncomfortable. Mary realized that the pig didn't want to stay with her, so she decided to leave it alone. Instead, she started to collect leaves and stones from around the garden. She made a pile of them and smiled to herself. Now she had something special to show her parents when she got home. Her adventure was a success!
