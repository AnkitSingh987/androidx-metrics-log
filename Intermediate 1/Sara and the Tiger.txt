Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Tiger

Sara loves to rock on her chair. She rocks back and forth, back and forth, with a big smile on her face. She pretends she is riding a tiger in the jungle. She makes a roar sound and waves her arms. "Look at me, Mommy!" she calls. "I am a tiger rider!" Mommy comes to the door and smiles. She sees Sara rocking on her chair with her tiger toy in her lap. She thinks Sara is very cute and clever. "You are a very graceful tiger rider, Sara," Mommy says. "You rock so smoothly and gently. Can I join you?" "Yes, Mommy, come and rock with me!" Sara says. She moves over and makes room for Mommy on the chair. Mommy sits down and hugs Sara and the tiger. They rock together, back and forth, back and forth, and make roar sounds. They have fun and feel happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Jack. He was small and brown and he loved to soar in the sky. Every day, he would hastily fly up high, with the wind blowing on his wings. One day, Jack spotted a brick on the ground. He was fascinated by it. He swooped down to get a better look. When he got nearer, he saw that the brick was so shiny and smooth. Jack was very excited. He asked the brick, 'Will you come with me while I soar in the sky?' The brick didn't answer, but Jack thought it looked like it said yes. So, Jack scooped up the brick and flew fast into the sky. Jack and the brick enjoyed their flight immensely. They flew so fast, it felt like they were flying through clouds. When it was time for Jack to land, he gently set down the brick safely. Jack thanked the brick for the wonderful flight. He said, 'We must do that again, soon!' The brick said nothing, but Jack thought it smiled at him before he flew away.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a bike with pedals. He loved to ride his bike up and down the street. It was his very own bike, and it was the perfect fit for him. The bike was red and shiny, and it made Tim feel so happy. One day, Tim went to the park with his bike. He pedaled as fast as he could. The wind blew in his face, and he laughed. At the park, he saw his friend, Sue. Sue had a blue bike that was a good fit for her too. They both loved their bikes very much. Tim and Sue rode their bikes together all day long. They raced each other and played fun games. When the sun started to set, they knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye and pedaled back to their houses. Tim and Sue couldn't wait to ride their bikes together again.

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw his bike and wanted to go for a ride. He put on his helmet and grabbed the handlebars. He put his feet on the pedals and started to ride. As Tim rode, he saw a smart dog named Max. Max could talk! "Hello, Tim!" said Max. Tim was very surprised. "Hello, Max!" he said. Max asked, "Can I ride with you?" Tim thought Max was very intelligent. Tim and Max rode together. They went fast and slow, and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Max grabbed the pedals with his paws and started to ride the bike too! Tim and Max laughed and played all day. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called James. He was so excited because today he was going to play with his mail. James opened the mail and found a big surprise inside. It was a brilliant toy fire truck. He was so excited to show it to his friends. He ran outside to show them his new toy. But when he got there, he saw that all his friends were busy playing with something else. They all had mail too. James was sad and asked his friends, "Can I play, too?" But his friends said no and kept playing. James went home disappointed and with no toy fire truck. He had no one to play with. His surprise was ruined and he would never get to play with his mail again.
