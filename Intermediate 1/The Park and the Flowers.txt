Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a big park near the ocean. The park had many trees, flowers, and a big playground. Kids loved to play there with their friends and family. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom and dad. They played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. After playing, they walked to the ocean. The ocean was very calm that day, and the water was blue and pretty. Lily, her mom, and her dad sat on the sand and watched the calm ocean. They saw fish jumping and birds flying. They had a fun day at the park and the ocean. When it was time to go home, Lily was very happy and couldn't wait to come back to the park and the ocean again.

Once upon a time, there was a little shrimp. He lived in a big sea with his family. The shrimp was very small, but he was fierce. He was not scared of anything. One day, the shrimp saw a big bright ball in the sky. It was very hot. The shrimp's mom told him, "Be careful, little shrimp. That ball can burn you." But the fierce shrimp did not listen. He wanted to see the big bright ball up close. The little shrimp swam closer and closer to the big bright ball. It got hotter and hotter. The shrimp started to feel a burn. He remembered what his mom said and swam back home very fast. The fierce shrimp learned that day to listen to his mom and be careful with the big bright ball.

One day, a little worm was in the dirt. The worm was confused. It did not know which way to go. It wanted to find a nice home. The worm started to complain. "Oh no, I am lost," said the worm. A big bird heard the worm. The bird flew down and asked, "Why are you sad?" The worm said, "I am lost and confused. I want to find a home." The bird smiled and said, "I can help you find a home." The bird and the worm looked for a home. They found a nice, wet spot in the dirt. The worm was happy. It stopped complaining. The bird and the worm were now friends. They played in the dirt and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim loved to ride his red bike. One sunny day, he rode to the park to play with his friends. They were a band who liked to make fun music. Tim played the drum, and his friends played the guitar and the trumpet. As they played their music, a big, black cat came and sat next to them. The cat listened to the music and started to dance. Tim and his friends were so happy to see the cat dance. They played even louder, and the cat danced more. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat jumped on Tim's drum and broke it. Tim felt sorry and sad. But the cat was not a normal cat. It was a magic cat! The cat touched the drum with its paw, and the drum was fixed. Tim and his friends were amazed and happy. They played more music, and the magic cat danced with them all day.

Tom was a humble boy who liked to help his dad fix things in the garage. He had a small toolbox with a hammer, a screwdriver, and some nails. He watched his dad carefully and learned how to use the tools. One day, his dad had to go to the store and left Tom alone in the garage. Tom saw a big bike that belonged to his older brother. It had a flat tire and a broken chain. Tom thought he could fix it and surprise his brother. He took his toolbox and started to work on the bike. He used the screwdriver to loosen the bolts and the hammer to bang the chain. He tried to pump air into the tire, but it did not work. He got frustrated and angry. He threw the tools on the floor and kicked the bike. He made a big mess and a loud noise. His dad came back and saw what Tom had done. He was very upset and disappointed. He said, "Tom, what have you done? You have ruined your brother's bike and made a mess of the garage. You should not touch things that are not yours. You should ask for help if you want to learn how to fix things. You should be sorry and apologize to your brother." Tom felt ashamed and sorry. He realized he had made a big mistake. He said, "Dad, I'm sorry. I wanted to fix the bike and surprise my brother, but I did not know how. I got angry and made a mess. I'm sorry, dad. I'm sorry, brother. Can you forgive me?" His dad hugged him and said, "Tom, I forgive you. I know you meant well, but you have to be careful and respectful. Fixing things is not easy. It takes patience and skill. You have to learn from your mistakes and try to do better. I love you, son. Come on, let's clean up the garage and see if we can fix the bike together." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, dad. I love you too. I want to learn how to fix things like you. Can you teach me?" His dad nodded and said, "Of course, son. I'm happy to teach you. But first, let's say sorry to your brother and see if he wants to join us." They went to find Tom's brother and apologized to him. His brother was not happy, but he accepted their apology and agreed to help them fix the bike. They worked together in the garage and had fun. Tom learned a lot from his dad and his brother. He learned how to fix things, how to say sorry, and how to be humble. He was a good boy.
