Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Door

Anna loved to play with her toy planes. She had many planes of different colors and sizes. She liked to pretend that she was a pilot and flew them around the room. She made loud noises with her mouth and moved her arms like wings. One day, she decided to mix her planes and make new ones. She took the head of a red plane and the body of a blue plane and stuck them together. She did the same with a yellow plane and a green plane. She was very proud of her creations and showed them to her brother Ben. "Look, Ben, I made new planes! They are better than yours!" she said. Ben looked at her planes and frowned. He did not like that she mixed his planes without asking. He also did not like that she was boasting. "Anna, those are not new planes. Those are broken planes. You should not mix things that do not belong together. And you should not be so proud of yourself. A good pilot is humble and respectful, not rude and selfish." he said. Anna felt angry and sad. She did not understand why Ben was so mean to her. She thought her planes were beautiful and fun. She did not want to be humble and respectful. She wanted to be the best pilot in the world. She ran to her room and slammed the door. She hugged her mixed planes and cried. After a while, she heard a knock on the door. It was Ben. He came to apologize and to explain. "Anna, I'm sorry I was harsh with you. I know you like to play with your planes and to be creative. But you should also ask before you use other people's things. And you should not think that you are better than others. A good pilot is humble and respectful, because flying is a team work and a responsibility. You need to listen to others and to follow the rules. You also need to be grateful for what you have and to share with others. That's how you become a good pilot and a good person." he said. Anna listened to Ben and felt sorry too. She realized that he was right and that she was wrong. She did not want to be rude and selfish. She wanted to be a good pilot and a good person. She opened the door and hugged Ben. She said she was sorry and that she would ask before she used his planes. She also said she would be humble and respectful and share with others. Ben smiled and hugged her back. He said he was proud of her and that he would help her fix her mixed planes. He also said he would play with her and teach her more about flying. They went to the living room and played with their planes. They made loud noises with their mouth and moved their arms like wings. They had fun and learned from each other. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally had a big exam at school. She was not comfortable about it. She felt her tummy hurt. Sally told her mom, "Mom, my tummy hurts. I don't want to go to school." Her mom said, "Sally, you need to eat breakfast. It will make your tummy feel better. Then you can go to school and do your exam." Sally ate a big bowl of cereal. She felt her tummy get better. She felt more comfortable. She went to school and did her exam. When she came home, she told her mom, "Mom, I did my exam and I feel good! Thank you for the breakfast." Her mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sally. I'm so proud of you!"

One day, a fat cat saw a pie on a table. The pie was big and yummy. The fat cat wanted to eat the pie. So, the fat cat jumped on the table to take the pie. A little girl saw the fat cat with the pie. She said, "No, fat cat! That pie is not for you!" The fat cat did not listen. The fat cat took the pie and ran away. The little girl was sad. She could not have the pie now. The fat cat ate all the pie. The little girl cried. The fat cat was happy, but the little girl was not.
