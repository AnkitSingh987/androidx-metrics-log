Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the River

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat was not big, but it was very smart. It could manage to go fast and slow, and it could turn very well. The boat loved to help people and make them happy. One day, a big, stupid boat came to the river. The big boat was not nice. It was always making a mess and being mean to the little boat. The little boat was very sad. It wanted to teach the big boat a lesson. The little boat had an idea. It asked the big boat to race. The big boat laughed and said, "I am big, and you are small. I will win." They started the race, and the big boat was very fast. But the little boat managed to go faster by being smart and using the wind. In the end, the little boat won the race. The big boat was very sad and asked, "How did you win?" The little boat said, "I am small, but I am smart. I managed to use the wind to help me." The big boat learned that being big and mean is not always the best. It is better to be smart and kind. From that day on, the big boat stopped being mean and started being nice to everyone. The little boat and the big boat became good friends. They learned that being smart and kind is more important than being big and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a big, busy house. In this house, there were two cats, Tom and Jerry. They liked to play on the soft rug. But sometimes, they would quarrel. One day, when Tom and Jerry were playing on the rug, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the rug away. The cats stopped their quarrel and looked at each other. They felt sad because they loved the rug. Then, Tom and Jerry decided to work together to find the rug. They looked high and low, and finally found the rug in a tree. They climbed the tree and got the rug back. From that day on, Tom and Jerry did not quarrel anymore. They learned to share and be friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food. Tim saw a big jar of candy with a label on it. The label had a picture of a happy face. Tim wanted the candy, so he asked his mom, "Can I have the candy, please?" His mom looked at the price tag and said, "This candy is expensive. We can't buy it today." Tim was sad, but he understood. His mom had an idea. She said, "Let's count how many candies are in the jar. If you can guess the right number, I will buy you a smaller bag of candy." Tim was excited and started to count. He counted 1, 2, 3, and so on. When he was done, he said, "I think there are 20 candies in the jar!" His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Let's go find a smaller bag of candy for you." They found a smaller bag of candy that was not expensive. Tim was happy. He thanked his mom and they went home. That night, Tim and his mom counted the candies in the small bag together. They had fun counting and eating the candy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car. It was his original toy, the first one he ever got. He loved to play with it all day long. One day, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue also had a toy car. They decided to play together. They made their cars go fast and slow, and then they made them reverse. They laughed and had fun. "Let's have a race!" said Tim. Sue agreed, and they lined up their toy cars. The race began, and their cars zoomed forward. Tim's car went very fast, but Sue's car was faster. Sue's car won the race. Tim was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, we can race again. Maybe your car will win next time." They played together all day, racing their cars and making them reverse. Tim and Sue became best friends, and they always played with their original toy cars together.
