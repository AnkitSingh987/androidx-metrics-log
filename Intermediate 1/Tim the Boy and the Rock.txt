Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars. One day, Tim saw a big, hard rock on the ground. He thought it would be fun to drive his cars on it. But his mom said, "No, Tim, that rock is not safe." Tim did not listen to his mom. He insisted on playing with the rock. He put his toy cars on the rock and started to play. But then, the rock started to wobble. Tim's mom saw this and got worried. She told Tim to come away from the rock, but he did not listen. Suddenly, the rock fell on Tim's toy cars. The cars broke into little pieces. Tim was very sad. He learned that he should have listened to his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big ocean. One day, Fin saw an ancient rock. The rock was very old and had many stories to tell. Fin was very curious about the rock. Fin swam to the rock and said, "Hello, ancient rock! Can you tell me a story?" The rock said, "Yes, little fish. I have a story about a big rain that will soak the ocean. When the rain comes, you must find a safe place to hide." Fin listened to the rock and swam away. He told all his fish friends about the big rain that would soak the ocean. They all looked for a safe place to hide. Soon, the sky turned dark and the big rain started to fall. All the fish hid under the ancient rock. They were safe from the big rain that soaked the ocean. When the rain stopped, the fish swam out and thanked the ancient rock for its wisdom. From that day on, Fin and his friends always listened to the stories of the ancient rock.

Once upon a time, there was a little prince. He lived in a big castle with his mom and dad. The prince liked to play with his toys and run around the garden. One day, the prince lost his favorite toy. He felt very guilty. He looked everywhere but could not find it. Then, he asked his mom, "Mom, have you seen my toy?" His mom said, "No, I have not seen your toy. Let's look for it together." They looked in the castle and in the garden. Suddenly, they saw the toy in the garden. But it was not alone! A small puppy was playing with the toy. The prince was very happy to see his toy and the puppy. The puppy was the surprise that his mom and dad planned for him. They all played together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, in a charming little house, lived a cat named Tom and a dog named Sam. They were best friends. They played together, ate together, and slept together. They loved their small house and their simple life. One sunny day, Tom and Sam found a bottle of wine under a big tree. Tom said, "Look, Sam! What is that?" Sam replied, "I don't know, Tom. Let's shake it and see what happens." So, they shook the bottle and the wine inside bubbled and fizzed. Tom and Sam were very happy and excited. They jumped and danced around the bottle of wine. They laughed and said, "We found a magic bottle!" From that day on, they kept the wine bottle in their charming little house and showed it to all their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They both loved to play in the garden, where they found many pretty flowers and green herbs. One sunny day, Sue and Tim found a new herb in their garden. It was green and smelled very nice. Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's spread the herb all around our garden, Tim!" Tim liked the idea too. So, they started to spread the herb seeds in the garden. After some days, the herbs began to grow. The garden looked more beautiful than ever. The fat cat, Tim, loved to roll around in the new herbs. Sue and Tim were very happy with their garden. They played there every day, and they lived happily ever after.
