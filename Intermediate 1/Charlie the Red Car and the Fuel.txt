Category: Intermediate
Title: Charlie the Red Car and the Fuel

Once upon a time, there was a big red car named Charlie. Charlie loved to rush and go fast. One day, he wanted to go on a long trip, but he needed fuel to go. Charlie went to the fuel place and said, "I need fuel, please." The man at the fuel place gave him fuel, but he told Charlie, "Don't be foolish and rush too fast. You might get hurt." Charlie didn't listen and said, "I love to rush!" As Charlie rushed down the road, he saw his friend, a blue car named Betty. Betty was going slow and steady. She called out to Charlie, "Slow down, friend! Don't be foolish!" But Charlie didn't listen and kept rushing. Soon, Charlie hit a big bump in the road and got a flat tire. He felt sad and foolish for rushing too fast. Betty stopped and helped Charlie. She said, "Next time, don't rush so fast. It's better to be safe than sorry." Charlie learned his lesson and promised to be more careful. From that day on, Charlie and Betty went on many trips together, always going slow and steady.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little dog named Max. Max had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Max saw a pretty blue ball in his friend's yard. Max wanted the pretty blue ball, but he knew he needed to trade his big red ball for it. So, Max went to his friend, a kind cat named Lily, and said, "Please, can we trade balls?" Lily thought about it and said, "Yes, we can trade. I like your big red ball, and you can have my pretty blue ball." They traded balls and played happily together. From that day on, Max and Lily knew that sharing and trading could make them both happy. They played together every day, and they were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the park. She saw a pretty orange cat sitting by a tree. The cat had a shiny spark on its collar. Sue walked up to the cat and said, "Hi, cat! I like your spark!" The cat looked at Sue and said, "Thank you! My spark is magic. If you are nice to me, I will show you a fun surprise later." Sue was very excited and played with the cat all day. As the sun began to set, the cat said, "Sue, do you remember the surprise I told you about?" Sue nodded, and the cat touched its spark. Suddenly, the sky filled with beautiful orange lights, just like the cat. Sue was so happy and thanked the cat for the best day ever.

One day, Daisy and her Daddy were out in the garden. Daisy saw a paper and had an idea. She took the paper and held it up. Daddy smiled and said, "I'm going to shoot at the paper." Daisy was excited and clapped her hands. Daddy pulled out a gun and pointed it at the paper. Daisy cheered. Daddy said, "Ready? Here I go!" He pulled the trigger and the gun made a loud bang. The paper had a big hole in it. Daisy said, "Wow! Can I try?" Daddy smiled and handed Daisy the gun. Daisy was careful and held the gun the way Daddy showed her. She pointed it at the original paper and pulled the trigger. Bang! Daisy was surprised. "I did it!" she said excitedly. Daddy hugged her and said, "You did it! Well done!"

Sara and Ben like to play with rods. They have many rods of different colors and sizes. They can put the rods together and make shapes and towers and bridges. One day, they go to the park with their mom. They see a pond with fish and ducks. Sara and Ben want to feed the fish and ducks. They ask their mom for some bread. "Can we put some bread on the rods and give it to the fish and ducks?" Sara asks. "Sure, that sounds like fun. But be careful not to drop the rods in the water. And don't get too close to the edge of the pond," mom says. Sara and Ben break the bread into small pieces and put them on the ends of the rods. They hold the rods over the water and wait for the fish and ducks to come. Soon, they see some lively fish and ducks swimming and quacking. They are hungry and happy. "Look, mom, the fish and ducks like our bread!" Ben says. "Yes, they do. You are very kind to share your bread with them," mom says. Sara and Ben smile and giggle. They enjoy watching the lively fish and ducks eat their bread. They have fun playing with the rods at the park.
