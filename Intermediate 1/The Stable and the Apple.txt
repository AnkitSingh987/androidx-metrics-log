Category: Intermediate
Title: The Stable and the Apple

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a stable. In the stable, there were two horses named Tim and Sam. They were good friends and liked to play together. One day, Tim saw a big apple tree outside the stable. He wanted to eat the apples, but he could not reach them. So, Tim asked Sam, "Please help me get the apples." Sam was tall and strong, so he was able to reach the apples. He picked them and shared them with Tim. Together, they were successful in getting the apples. They ate them all and felt very happy. From that day on, Tim and Sam always helped each other when they needed it. They became the best friends in the stable and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a big lion. The lion loved to roar very loud. Every day, he would climb to the top of a hill and roar for everyone to hear. One day, the lion found a new hill with a pretty view. He could see the whole forest from there. It was very easy to climb. The lion was very happy and decided to roar from this hill every day. All the animals in the forest heard the lion's roar and came to see the new hill. They all loved the pretty view too. From that day on, the animals played together on the hill, and the lion's roar brought them all together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy rod that he loved to play with. The rod was very ugly, but Tim did not care. He would rub the rod every day and take it with him everywhere he went. One day, Tim took his ugly rod to the park. He played with his rod near a big tree. He rubbed the rod, and it made him very happy. But then, a big wind came and blew the ugly rod out of Tim's hand. The rod flew high up into the air and landed in the tall tree. Tim was very sad. He tried to get his ugly rod back, but it was too high up in the tree. He asked his mom and dad for help, but they could not reach it either. The ugly rod was stuck in the tree, and Tim could not play with it anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a happy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sing. Every day, he would sing a fun song. He was always eager to learn new songs. One day, Tom found a bottle of ink. He looked at the ink and had an idea. He dipped his paw in the ink and began to paint on the wall. He painted a big sun, a tree, and a bird. As Tom painted, he sang a happy song. His friends, the dog and the bird, heard him sing. They came to see what Tom was doing. They saw the ink and the pictures on the wall. They clapped and danced as Tom sang his song. They all had a fun day together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the jungle with his mom. The jungle was big and gloomy, but Tim was not scared. He saw many animals like monkeys, birds, and bugs. Tim liked the jungle very much. Tim saw a big fruit on a tree. He asked his mom, "Can I taste it?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim picked the fruit and took a bite. It was very sweet and yummy. Tim was happy. Just then, a small monkey came and took the fruit from Tim's hand. The monkey said, "Thank you for finding my fruit!" Tim was surprised but laughed. He made a new friend in the jungle that day. They played together until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big spoon that she loved to play with. One day, Lily felt troubled because her mom would not permit her to play with the spoon. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can I please play with my spoon?" Her mom said, "No, Lily, you cannot play with the spoon now. It is time to eat our food." Lily felt sad, but she knew her mom was right. She sat down and ate her food with her spoon. After they finished eating, her mom said, "Now, Lily, you can play with your spoon." Lily's face lit up with joy. She took her spoon and played with it all day. She was no longer troubled, and she knew that her mom only wanted the best for her.
