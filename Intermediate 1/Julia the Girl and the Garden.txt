Category: Intermediate
Title: Julia the Girl and the Garden

Once there was a little girl named Julia. Julia was three years old and she was growing every day. Her body got bigger and she learned new things. Julia liked to play in the garden. She played with her toys and the birds would fly around. When she got tired she would lie down in the grass and feel the sunshine on her little face. One day, her mom said it was time to have a bath so she brought out the big pink bathtub. Julia was happy to get in and she washed her body with the nice smelling soap. She splashed her toes in the warm water and felt happy and refreshed. After her bath, Julia put on her favorite dress, a beautiful blue one with lots of stars on it. She was so excited that she twirled around and around feeling the fabric around her. Every day, Julia was growing. Her body changed and as she learned more and more. Every day was a wonderful journey and Julia enjoyed it very much. She was happy to be growing and having so many nice experiences.

Once upon a time, there was a little angel named Amy. Amy was a very dependable angel. She always helped her friends. One day, Amy's friends, Tom and Sue, had a quarrel. Tom wanted to play with the red ball, but Sue wanted it too. They both got very mad. Amy saw them and came to help. Amy said, "Tom, Sue, please don't quarrel. We can all play together." She showed them how to take turns with the red ball. Tom and Sue listened to Amy and started to play nicely. Soon, they were all having fun and laughing. Tom and Sue learned that it was better to share and be kind to each other. The moral of the story is: Sharing and being kind to others is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a poor dog. He did not have a home. One day, Spot found a ticket on the ground. The ticket was for a big fun park. Spot wanted to go to the fun park. Spot walked to the fun park with his ticket. At the gate, he tried to give the ticket to the man. The man did not understand Spot. Spot could not talk. The man was sad for Spot, but he could not let him in. Spot sat outside the fun park, feeling sad. Then, a kind girl named Amy saw Spot. Amy could see that Spot was a poor dog. She picked up the ticket and gave it to the man. The man let Spot and Amy into the fun park. They had a fun day together. From that day on, Spot had a new home with Amy, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play with his toys outside on the earth. One day, he saw his friend Max playing with a big, new toy car. Tom felt envious because he wanted to have a car like that too. Tom went home and started to weep. His mommy saw him crying and asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom told his mommy about Max's new toy car and how he wanted one too. His mommy gave him a big hug and said, "Don't worry, Tom. You have many toys that Max doesn't have. Be happy with what you have." The next day, Tom played with his toys and shared them with Max. Max was very happy to play with Tom's toys. They both played together and had lots of fun. Tom learned that sharing and being happy with what he has is better than being envious.

Sara and Ben like to play with plates. They have many plates of different colors and shapes. They like to spin the plates on the floor and watch them go round and round. One day, Sara finds a purple plate in the kitchen. She thinks it is very pretty and shiny. She takes it to the living room and shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I have a purple plate!" Sara says. "It is my favorite color. Can I spin it?" Ben looks at the purple plate. He likes it too. He wants to spin it as well. He says, "Yes, you can spin it, Sara. But can I spin it after you?" Sara nods. She puts the purple plate on the floor and gives it a push. The purple plate spins fast and makes a humming sound. Sara and Ben clap their hands and laugh. They take turns spinning the purple plate. They have fun and forget about the other plates. They do not see Mom coming into the living room. Mom sees the purple plate spinning on the floor. She is surprised and angry. She says, "Sara, Ben, what are you doing? That is not a toy! That is a special plate from Grandma! It is very old and fragile. You could break it!" Sara and Ben stop laughing. They feel scared and sorry. They did not know the purple plate was special. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not know. We just wanted to play. We like the purple plate. It is very pretty and shiny." Mom sighs. She picks up the purple plate and checks it for cracks. She is glad it is not broken. She says, "I know you like the purple plate, Sara and Ben. But you have to be careful with it. It is not a toy. It is a treasure. Grandma gave it to me when I was little. It reminds me of her. She loved purple too." Sara and Ben look at the purple plate. They see it in a new way. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not mean to hurt the purple plate. We love Grandma too. Can we see her soon?" Mom smiles. She hugs Sara and Ben. She says, "Yes, you can see her soon. She lives far away, but we can call her and say hello. Maybe she can tell you a story about the purple plate. Would you like that?" Sara and Ben nod. They say, "Yes, we would like that, Mom. Thank you. We love you." Mom says, "I love you too, Sara and Ben. Now, let's put the purple plate back in the kitchen. And then you can play with your other plates. But please, be gentle with them." Sara and Ben say, "Okay, Mom. We will be gentle. We promise." They put the purple plate back in the kitchen. They play with their other plates. They spin them gently and watch them go round and round. They think of Grandma and the purple plate. They are happy.
