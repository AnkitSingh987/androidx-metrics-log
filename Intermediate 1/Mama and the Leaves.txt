Category: Intermediate
Title: Mama and the Leaves

Mama and baby took walk in the park. Mama got tired so they stopped and rested beneath a big tree. There was leaves on the ground and Mama said, "Let's bury the leaves." Baby thought that sounded like fun and said, "Yes, Mama!" Mama used a stick to scoop the leaves and soon there was a big pile. Mama said, "Now rub your hands in the pile and make a leaf family!" Baby laughed and used their hands to make shapes in the leaves. It was fun! When the leaf family was finished, Mama said, "That's so pretty." Baby nodded and smiled. Then, it was time to go home. Baby said goodbye to the leaf family one last time.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful goose. The goose lived on a big farm. The goose had many friends on the farm. One day, the goose found a shiny thing. The shiny thing was very pretty. The goose showed the shiny thing to all its friends. All the friends liked the shiny thing too. They all wanted the shiny thing. The goose thought hard. The goose wanted to be fair. The goose told its friends, "Let's share the shiny thing. We can all enjoy it together." The friends liked the idea. They all took turns with the shiny thing. The farm friends learned to share and be happy together. They all knew the value of sharing. The thoughtful goose had taught them a good thing. And they all lived happily on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat was upset because he lost his way home. He went to find help. The goat met a big dog. The dog wanted to protect the goat. They walked together to find the goat's home. After some time, they saw the goat's house. The goat was happy and not upset anymore. The dog had protected the goat. They became good friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very original. He liked to come up with his own ideas and opinions. He loved to share them with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends were playing outside. They saw a big, red ball. Tim had an opinion about the ball. He thought they should all come together and play a game with it. His friends agreed and were excited to play. Tim's original idea for the game was to kick the ball and try to catch it. All the friends had fun playing the game. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Tim was happy that his friends liked his opinion, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cube. One sunny day, Tim took his cube to the park to play. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. "Hi, I'm Sue! What's your name?" she asked. "I'm Tim," he said, "Do you want to play with my cube?" Sue said yes, and they played happily together. Suddenly, a big wind blew and took the cube away. Tim and Sue tried to prevent the cube from flying away, but they couldn't. They were sad. But then, a kind bird saw the cube and brought it back to them. Tim and Sue were so happy and thanked the bird. They played together all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in the forest. Many animals liked to climb the tall tree. One day, a little squirrel named Tim wanted to climb the tall tree too. Tim's mom told him to be careful because a big storm was coming. Tim started to climb the tall tree. He was very happy. The sky got dark, and the wind began to blow. Tim was scared, but he did not want to stop climbing. He wanted to show his friends that he could climb the tall tree. The storm came, and it was very loud. Tim was at the top of the tall tree. He held on tight. When the storm was over, Tim's friends saw him at the top. They cheered for him. Tim was so brave to climb the tall tree during the storm. From that day on, Tim was known as the bravest squirrel in the forest.
