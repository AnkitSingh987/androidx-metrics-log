Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Girl

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to play. She often created games that were interesting. One day the little girl had a great idea. She decided to count to peace! The little girl started counting, one, two, three, four. and so on. As she counted she noticed something in the sky. It was a big beautiful bird! The little girl asked the bird, “What are you doing here?” The bird said, “I am looking for peace.” The little girl said, “Counting to peace is a great way to find it!” The bird smiled at the little girl and said, “Yes, counting can help us find peace!” The little girl and the bird then continued to count together until they reached peace. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim liked to play with his ball. One day, he went to the park to kick his ball. The sun was hot, and the sky was blue. Tim was very happy. At the park, Tim met a fish named Sam. Sam was in a small pool. He liked to swim all day. Tim said, "Hi Sam, do you want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to play, but I can only swim." Tim had an idea. He kicked his ball into the pool. The ball floated on the water. Sam swam to the ball and pushed it with his nose. The ball went back to Tim. Tim and Sam played like this for a long time. They were very happy and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fast bunny named Bob. Bob loved to run and play in the big green field. He had many friends who liked to play with him. They would race and chase each other all day long. One sunny day, Bob found a note on the ground. The note had a picture of a big red heart on it. Bob knew that the heart meant love and value. He wanted to find out who the note belonged to, so he could give it back. Bob showed the note to his friends. They all helped him look for the owner. They asked the birds, the squirrels, and the other animals. Finally, they found a little girl who had lost the note. She was so happy to get it back! She thanked Bob and his friends for their help. They all learned that helping others and valuing their things is very important. And they all played together happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big cliff near the sea. A little bird lived on the cliff. The bird had a nest in an open tree. The bird loved to fly and sing happy songs. One day, the bird saw a big, open box on the ground. The bird was very curious. It went into the box to look for food. But, oh no! The box was a trap! The bird was stuck in the box and could not get out. The bird felt regret for going into the box. It wanted to be free again. Just then, a kind girl found the box and opened it. The bird was free! It flew back to its nest on the cliff, singing a happy song. The bird learned to be more careful and never went into an open box again.

One day, a playful dog named Spot found a photo on the ground. The photo showed a big family with happy smiles. Spot wanted to find the family and give them back their photo. Spot showed the photo to his friend, a kind bird named Tweet. Tweet said, "I will support you, Spot! Let's find the family together!" So, Spot and Tweet went on a journey to find the family in the photo. As they walked, Spot and Tweet saw a big group of people. They looked just like the family in the photo! Spot and Tweet ran to the family, but then something unexpected happened. A little girl from the group said, "Oh, that's not our family! That's our friends' family!" Spot and Tweet were surprised, but they didn't give up. They knew they would find the right family soon.
