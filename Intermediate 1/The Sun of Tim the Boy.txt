Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sun of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sun was shining, and it was a beautiful day. Tim felt the warm sunshine on his face and thought, "I want to taste the sunshine!" He opened his mouth wide and tried to catch the sun's rays on his tongue. Just then, a sleepy little bird named Blue flew down and landed on Tim's shoulder. "What are you doing?" asked Blue. Tim said, "I'm trying to taste the sunshine!" Blue laughed and said, "You can't taste sunshine, silly! But I can show you something that tastes like sunshine!" Blue led Tim to a big tree with bright, juicy oranges hanging from it. Tim picked one, peeled it, and took a bite. The sweet juice filled his mouth, and he smiled. "This does taste like sunshine!" Tim exclaimed. Just then, the orange turned into a big, shiny sun in Tim's hands. Tim and Blue gasped, amazed by the unexpected magic. From that day on, Tim and Blue were the best of friends. They played in the sunshine, and whenever they ate oranges, they would remember the day they tasted the sunshine together. And sometimes, they would still try to catch the sun's rays on their tongues, just for fun.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing with her toy dishes. She had a big dish and a small dish. She liked the big dish best because it was her original dish. She played with them all day, making pretend food for her doll. Lucy's brother, Tom, came into the room. He saw the big dish and wanted to play with it. Lucy said, "No, this is my original dish. You can play with the small dish." Tom did not like that. He started to complain, "But I want to play with the big dish too!" Just then, their mom came into the room. She saw Lucy and Tom arguing. She said, "Why don't you both play with the big dish together? You can share it." Lucy and Tom thought about it and agreed to share the big dish. As they played together, something unexpected happened. The big dish broke in half! Lucy and Tom were sad. But then, their mom came back with a new, even bigger dish for them to share. Now they could both play with the big dish and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a favorite spoon. This spoon was very special. It was a compassionate spoon. The spoon always made her feel happy. One day, Amy lost her special spoon. She was very sad. She looked everywhere for it. She asked her mom, "Have you seen my spoon?" Her mom said, "I guess it's in the kitchen." But it was not there. She asked her dad, "Have you seen my spoon?" Her dad said, "I guess it's in your room." But it was not there either. Amy did not give up. She kept looking for her special spoon. Then, she remembered that she played with her spoon in the garden. She went outside and looked around. And guess what? She found her compassionate spoon under a big flower! She was so happy! From that day on, Amy always knew where her special spoon was.

Once upon a time, there was a small dog named Bob. Bob loved to nap under a big tree. The tree had a big root that Bob liked to rest on. Bob was always glad when he could nap on the root. One sunny day, a little bird named Sue came to the tree. Sue saw Bob napping on the root. Sue said, "Hi, Bob! Why do you nap on the root?" Bob woke up and said, "I like this root. It is comfy and cool. I am glad when I nap here." Sue and Bob became good friends. They played and talked under the tree every day. When they were tired, they napped on the big root together. They were both very glad to have found a friend to share their naps and fun times with.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot found a piece of chalk on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it around. Spot saw a big wall and wanted to draw on it. He tried to draw with the chalk in his mouth, but it was hard. As he drew, a little bit of chalk broke off and he could taste it. It tasted yucky! Spot spat it out and made a funny face. Just then, a little girl named Sue came up to Spot. She saw the chalk and took it from him. Sue drew a big, pretty picture on the wall. Spot was happy that Sue could draw with the chalk, and they became best friends. From that day on, Spot and Sue played together all the time, and they never tasted chalk again.
