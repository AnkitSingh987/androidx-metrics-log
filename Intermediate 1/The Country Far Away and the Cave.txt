Category: Intermediate
Title: The Country Far Away and the Cave

Once upon a time, there was a small country far away. Its people were fragile and delicate. They didn't know much about the outside world. One day, a young girl was walking through the country's garden. She stumbled upon a hidden cave filled with new, exciting things. She went inside and found something strange yet familiar. The girl asked, "What is this?" A voice answered, "I don't know. It looks strange." The girl picked up something from the cave and asked, "What is this?" The voice replied, "It looks like a map of our country." The two were delighted to finally know what the country looked like. Now, they could explore the mysterious lands beyond their own. The girl and the voice were soon exploring far and wide. Everywhere they went, they studied the delicate things in the world and savored its beauty.

Anna and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Anna had a big doll and Ben had a small car. They were having fun until Anna saw Ben's car and wanted it. "Give me your car, Ben. It is nicer than my doll," Anna said. "No, Anna. This is my car. You have your doll. Go away," Ben said. Anna was angry. She was selfish and did not like to share. She grabbed Ben's car and ran away. Ben was sad and cried. "Mommy, mommy, Anna took my car. She is mean," Ben said. Mommy heard Ben and came to the living room. She saw Anna hiding behind the sofa with Ben's car. She was not happy. "Anna, what are you doing? Why did you take Ben's car? That is not nice. You have to share with your brother," Mommy said. Anna did not answer. She was ashamed. She knew she did something wrong. Mommy took Ben's car from Anna and gave it back to him. She hugged Ben and said, "I'm sorry, Ben. Here is your car. You can play with it." Ben smiled and said, "Thank you, Mommy. I love you." Mommy looked at Anna and said, "Anna, you have to say sorry to Ben. You have to be kind and generous. You have to clap for Ben when he does something good. That is how you show love." Anna felt sorry. She did not want to be selfish and mean. She wanted to be kind and generous. She wanted to show love. She walked to Ben and said, "Sorry, Ben. I was wrong. I should not have taken your car. You can play with my doll if you want. I love you." Ben was surprised and happy. He said, "Thank you, Anna. I forgive you. You can play with my car if you want. I love you too." Anna and Ben hugged and said, "We love each other." Mommy was proud and happy. She said, "That's my good children. You solved your mystery. You learned how to share and clap. I love you both." Anna and Ben clapped for each other and for Mommy. They played with their toys and had fun. They were not selfish and mean. They were kind and generous. They showed love.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They had a big bowl, some flour, some eggs and some milk. They wanted to make pancakes. "First, we need to crack the eggs," Lily said. She took an egg and tapped it on the edge of the bowl. But she tapped too hard and the egg broke in her hand. She felt the sticky yolk and the sharp shell. She made a face and said, "Ew!" Ben laughed and said, "That's okay, Lily. We have more eggs. Here, let me help you." He took another egg and cracked it gently into the bowl. He gave Lily a spoon and said, "Now, we need to stir the eggs with the milk and the flour." Lily and Ben took turns stirring the batter with the spoon. They made sure there were no lumps. They were having fun. They sang a song as they stirred. "Stir, stir, stir the batter, make it nice and smooth. Stir, stir, stir the batter, we will eat it soon." When the batter was ready, Lily and Ben poured some of it onto a hot pan. They watched as it bubbled and turned golden. They smelled the sweet aroma. They were hungry and excited. They wanted to flip the pancake. "Can I flip it, Ben?" Lily asked. She felt nervous. She had never flipped a pancake before. She was afraid she would drop it or burn it. "Sure, Lily. You can flip it. Just be careful and use the spatula," Ben said. He handed her a flat tool. He was nervous too. He hoped Lily would do it right. Lily took the spatula and slid it under the pancake. She lifted it up and tried to toss it in the air. But she did not toss it high enough. The pancake fell back onto the pan and folded in half. It looked like a taco. Lily and Ben gasped. They looked at the pancake. They looked at each other. They did not know what to do. They felt sad and disappointed. They had ruined their pancake. But then, they heard a voice behind them. It was their mom. She had been watching them from the door. She smiled and said, "Wow, what a beautiful pancake! I love how you folded it. It looks like a special treat. Can I have a bite?" Lily and Ben smiled too. They realized their pancake was not ruined. It was just different. They were proud of their pancake. They said, "Yes, mom. You can have a bite. We made it for you." They cut a piece of the pancake and gave it to their mom. She tasted it and said, "Mmm, this is delicious. You are such good chefs. Thank you for making me this pancake." Lily and Ben hugged their mom and said, "You're welcome, mom. We love you." They shared the rest of the pancake and enjoyed it. They did not care that it was folded. They thought it was yummy and fun. They learned that sometimes, mistakes can be good. They decided to make more pancakes and fold them in different ways. They called them foldy-cakes. They had a great time.
