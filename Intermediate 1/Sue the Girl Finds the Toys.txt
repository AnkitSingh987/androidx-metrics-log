Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to wander around her big yard. She would wander every day, even on Tuesday. One Tuesday, Sue saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was very selfish. He did not like to share his toys with Sue. Sue was sad because she wanted to play with Tim's toys. Sue thought of a plan. She asked Tim, "Do you want to wander with me?" Tim said, "Yes!" As they wandered, Sue showed Tim all the fun things in her yard. Tim saw how much fun it was to share and play together. In the end, Tim learned not to be selfish. He shared his toys with Sue, and they played happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big swing and ran to it. Mia loved to play on the swing. As she played, a hairy dog appeared. The dog was friendly and wagged its tail. "Hi, dog!" Mia said. "Do you want to play with me?" The dog barked and jumped around. Mia laughed and played with the dog. They had a lot of fun together. After playing, Mia and the dog sat under a tree. Mia's mom came and said, "It's time to go home, Mia." Mia hugged the dog and said, "Bye, dog! I hope I see you again." The dog wagged its tail and watched Mia leave. The next day, Mia went to the park again. She saw the dog and they played together. Mia learned that being kind to others makes them want to be your friend. And that is how Mia and the hairy dog became best friends.

Once there was a small girl named Annie. She was always so full of energy and loved to skip everywhere. She skipped to school one day and saw her friends. Annie was so happy and had the biggest smile. On the other side of the playground, there was a young boy crying. It broke Annie's heart, so she went to comfort him. She asked the young boy why he was so miserable. He said that he was sad because his friends said they did not want to play with him. Annie could not believe it, she wanted to make the young boy's day better. Annie said "Hey, I'm Annie, come and skip with me!" The young boy was so relieved, he put on a smile and they both skipped off together. Annie and the young boy became best friends and played together everyday. Annie learned from her new friend that sharing happiness with others is one of the most important things in life. She knew that no one should ever feel miserable, because there is always someone out there to make you smile.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom. One day, his mom said, "Tim, can you help me fix the mailbox? It is broken." Tim said, "Yes, mom! I can help you." Tim and his mom went outside. They saw the mailbox was empty. There was no mail inside. They worked together to fix the mailbox. Tim held the tools and his mom did the work. Soon, the mailbox was not broken anymore. After they fixed the mailbox, Tim and his mom went back inside. They were happy that they fixed the mailbox together. Tim's mom said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, mom. I like helping you."

Once upon a time, a little girl named Rose was playing in the park. She saw a jet flying high in the sky and wanted to reach it. She jumped up and reached out her hand as she cried "Jet! Please come down to me." But her little hand couldn't reach so high. Then the jet answered, "I can't come down, but if you want, I can make you fly too." Rose was so excited, so she said "Yes please!" The jet then flew down and stopped right over Rose. It blew very strong air on her, and she started to feel her feet lifting up. The jet's air was so strong that Rose was soon dry, just like a feather. The jet then said "Ready, smile and hold on tight!" Rose shouted with joy as she flew into the clouds with the jet following behind. They flew around the park, higher and higher, until they both flew out of sight. They were so high that the sun shone brightly and everything looked dry, just like a desert. Rose waved to the jet as she flew away and smiled with delight.
