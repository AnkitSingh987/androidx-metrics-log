Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Toys

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He found a big chest in the corner. Tim was nervous, but he wanted to see what was inside. He opened the chest and saw lots of toys and games. Tim was very happy. His friend, Sue, came over to play. Tim said, "Look what I found, Sue! Let's play hide and seek with the toys." Sue was excited and they started to play. They took turns hiding the toys and finding them. As they played, Tim and Sue laughed and had fun. They were not nervous anymore. The big chest made their day full of joy and games. They played until it was time for Sue to go home, and they couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob was a very shy bunny. One day, while hopping around, he met a big bear named Ben. Ben was very friendly and wanted to play with Bob. But Bob was too embarrassed to talk to Ben. "Hi, I'm Ben," said the bear. "Do you want to play with me?" Bob looked at Ben's big teeth and was scared. He thought Ben might bite his neck. So, he just shook his head and hopped away. Ben felt sad that Bob did not want to play. He thought about how to show Bob that he was a nice bear. Ben had an idea. He picked some yummy berries from a bush and took them to Bob. "Here, I brought you some berries," said Ben. "I promise I won't bite." Bob looked at the berries and then at Ben. He saw that Ben was a kind bear. He felt less embarrassed and decided to be friends with Ben. They played together all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, Bob and Ben were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. He loved to eat jelly. One day, he went to the store to buy some jelly. There were many types of jelly. Bob had to choose which one to buy. Bob saw a little girl named Sally. She was serious about her jelly too. She said, "I like strawberry jelly." Bob thought about it and said, "I like strawberry too!" So, they both chose the strawberry jelly. They went home and had a big jelly party. They ate lots of strawberry jelly on bread. They were very happy. Bob and Sally became good friends who loved jelly. They always chose strawberry jelly together.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day. One day, Max saw a big red ball near a bush. He wanted to play with it, but it was too close to the bush. Max was scared to go near the bush because he thought there might be something hiding in it. Max saw a big girl named Lisa pass by. He barked at her to get her attention. Lisa saw Max and the big red ball near the bush. She understood that Max was scared and needed help. So, Lisa walked over and picked up the ball for Max. She threw the ball far away from the bush, and Max ran after it. Max was very grateful to Lisa for helping him get the ball. He wagged his tail and barked happily. Lisa smiled and played with Max for a while. They had a lot of fun together. From that day on, Max and Lisa became best friends, and they played in the park every day. And Max was never scared of the bush again, because he knew Lisa would always be there to help him.

One day, a girl named Lisa went to the doctor with her mom. The doctor needed to take an x-ray of Lisa's arm. Lisa was scared and did not want to do it. The doctor said, "Don't worry, Lisa. It will be quick and won't hurt. We need to finish this to help your arm feel better." Lisa's mom held her hand and they went into the x-ray room. In the room, there was a rude boy named Tom. He was yelling and not listening to the doctor. Lisa was upset by Tom's loud voice. But, she was brave and finished her x-ray. The doctor was happy, and Lisa felt proud of herself.
