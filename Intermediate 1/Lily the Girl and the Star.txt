Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Star

One day, a small girl named Lily saw a hurt star in her yard. It was sad and could not fly. Lily wanted to help the star. She said, "Don't worry, star. I will make you feel better." Lily took the star inside her house. She used a soft cloth to clean it. The star started to feel better. "Thank you, Lily," said the star. "You are very kind." But soon, the sky became dark and scary. The other stars were angry that Lily took their friend. They came down to take the hurt star back. Lily was sad and scared. She tried to say sorry, but the stars did not listen. They took their friend and went back to the sky, leaving Lily alone in the dark.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a favorite brush that she used every day. One day, she could not find her brush. Sue was very confused. She looked everywhere for her brush, but it was gone. Sue asked her mom, "Mom, have you seen my brush? I miss it so much." Her mom shook her head and said, "No, Sue. I have not seen your brush. Let's look for it together." So, Sue and her mom looked for the brush. They looked under the bed, in the toy box, and in the kitchen. Finally, they found the brush in the garden. Sue was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Mom! I am so glad we found my brush." From that day on, Sue always put her brush in the same place, so she would not lose it again. She never wanted to feel confused and miss her brush like that ever again. And Sue and her mom lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny. The pink bunny had a big ball. The ball was very fine. The pink bunny loved to play with the ball. One day, the pink bunny was playing with the ball. He pushed the ball too hard. The ball went far away. The pink bunny was sad. He wanted his ball back. The pink bunny asked his friend, the blue bird, for help. The blue bird flew high and saw the ball. They went together to get the ball. The pink bunny and the blue bird pushed the ball back home. They were happy. The pink bunny learned not to push too hard. He played with the ball gently. The pink bunny and the blue bird played together. They had fun. The moral of the story is to be careful and gentle with the things we love.

Tommy and his mom were in the kitchen. Tommy was watching her pour sugar into a bowl. His mom smiled at him and said, "We are making something sweet, Tommy! It will be fun. Can you give me a pin?" Tommy handed her the pin, and she poked a hole in the top of the bowl. He giggled and asked what she did that for. His mom just smiled and said "It's a surprise! Let's keep going." She added a funny pink powder into the bowl and stirred it all together. Then she sprinkled it with something that smelled yummy. Finally, she poured some cold milk into the bowl and stirred it all together. When the bowl was full, his mom grinned at Tommy and said, "Can you guess what it is? It's something sweet and yummy. It's ice cream!" Tommy couldn't believe it. He jumped up and clapped his hands in delight. It was the best surprise ever and he had so much fun making ice cream with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very excited because he got a new glove for his birthday. He believed it was a magic glove. He wanted to show his friends his new glove. One day, Tim and his friends went to the park. They played catch with the magic glove. The glove was so big and soft. Tim was very happy. His friends were excited too. They believed the glove was magic as well. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took the glove away. Tim and his friends tried to catch the dog, but they couldn't. The dog ran away with the magic glove. Tim was very sad, and his friends were sad too. The magic glove was gone forever.
