Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Stick

Sara and Ben were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and watch the waves. The waves made a loud sound like "woosh" and "crash". Sometimes they saw people on boards riding the waves. Mom said that was called surf. One day, Sara and Ben found a big stick in the sand. They decided to use it to stir the water and make bubbles. They took turns holding the stick and moving it around in the water. They laughed and splashed each other. But then, a man on a surf board came close to them. He was angry and shouted at them. "Hey, kids, stop stirring the water! You are making it hard for me to surf. You are ruining my fun!" Sara and Ben were scared and confused. They did not know why the man was mad at them. They thought they were just playing. They looked at their mom and dad for help. Mom and dad came over and talked to the man. They said they were sorry and that Sara and Ben did not mean to bother him. They said they would move to another spot. They also said that the man should be more careful and polite. They said that the beach was for everyone to enjoy. The man grumbled and apologized. He said he was having a bad day and that he was too harsh. He said he liked to surf but he also liked to see kids having fun. He said he would try to be more friendly and share the water. Sara and Ben felt better and said thank you. They took their stick and went to another spot. They continued to stir the water and make bubbles. They also watched the man surf and waved at him. He waved back and smiled. They thought he was unusual but nice. They had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a fair and sunny land, there was a big lake. The lake had many fish and ducks. Kids loved to play near the lake. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the lake. She saw a big turtle sitting on a rock. Lily wanted to be friends with the turtle. She gave the turtle some food. The turtle was happy and ate the food. While they were playing, Lily saw a big stick. She picked it up and tried to snap it. But the stick was too big. The turtle saw Lily and wanted to help. The turtle used its strong mouth to snap the stick. They both laughed and played with the stick. Lily and the turtle became best friends by the lake.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big tooth that he loved to show off. One day, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was angry because she did not have a big tooth like Tim. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better. He had an idea! He decided to share his big tooth with her. Tim and Sue took turns showing off the big tooth to their friends. They both felt happy and proud. As they shared the big tooth, Sue's angry face turned into a big smile. Tim and Sue became the best of friends. They learned that sharing can make everyone happy.

Once there was a boy called Joe who wanted to buy something from the store. He went to the store and saw a big comfortable sack. He asked his mum if he could buy it and she said yes! So Joe got the sack and carried it back home. He was so excited to have it! When he got home, he put the sack in the living room and climbed into it. He was so comfortable and happy! He fell asleep for a long time and had lots of fun dreaming about playing in it. When he woke up he told his mum all about it and she laughed. Joe loved his sack so much, he took it with him to every store he went to!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very excited because her birthday was coming soon. She wanted to have a fun party with her friends. On her birthday, Lily and her friends played a game. The game was to see who could run the fastest. Lily wanted to win, but she was feeling a little bad. Her friend Tom was very fast too. They both ran as fast as they could. Lily ran and ran, and she won the race! She was very happy. After the race, they all ate cake and opened presents. Even though Lily had felt bad, her birthday turned out to be a great day. Her friends cheered for her, and they all had a fun time together.
