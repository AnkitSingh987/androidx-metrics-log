Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark the Boy and the Guitar

One day, a boy named Mark found a guitar. He was very happy. He wanted to play the guitar and make music. Mark went to his friend, Lisa, and said, "Look! I found a guitar! Let's play and sing together!" Lisa was upset. She told Mark, "I don't know how to play the guitar. I can't sing with you." Mark had an idea. He said, "I will teach you! We can learn together." They started to play the guitar, but a funny thing happened. The guitar made a loud sound like a drum! Mark and Lisa laughed and laughed. They found out that the guitar was not a normal guitar. It was a special guitar that could make many different sounds. They played and sang together all day, and they were not upset anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing in the house. They were running up and down the stairs, laughing and barking. Tim's mom saw them and said, "Be careful on the stairs, Tim! And don't forget to wipe your feet." As they played, Tim felt his socks were wet. He stopped and said, "Spot, let's go wipe our feet. We don't want to make a mess." They went to the door and wiped their feet on the mat. They felt comfortable in their clean, dry socks. Suddenly, they heard a noise from the stairs. They looked up and saw a big, soft pillow coming down the stairs. "Wow!" Tim said, "That's funny!" The pillow stopped at the bottom, and Tim and Spot jumped on it. They laughed and played, happy that their day had a fun surprise.

Sam and Mia liked to play with their toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a kayak. A kayak is a boat that you can sit in and paddle with a stick. They liked to pretend they were going on adventures in the water. One day, they decided to build a kayak with their blocks. They put the blocks together to make a long and narrow shape. They used a long stick for the paddle. They were very proud of their kayak. They wanted to show it to their friends. They took their kayak outside and put it on the grass. They saw Ben and Lily, who lived next door. Ben and Lily also liked to play with toys. They saw Sam and Mia's kayak and thought it was cool. They asked if they could play with it. Sam and Mia were friendly and said yes. They shared their kayak and their stick. They took turns sitting in the kayak and paddling. They pretended they were going to different places, like the beach, the lake, and the river. They had a lot of fun together. They learned that building and sharing are good things to do. They made new friends and had new adventures. They were happy and grateful for their kayak and their toys. They said thank you and goodbye to each other and went back to their homes. They looked forward to playing again.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk with her mom. They saw a mysterious house. It was big and old. Lucy wanted to go inside, but her mom said no. Lucy could not resist, so she went in when her mom was not looking. Inside, Lucy found a big cup of cocoa. It was warm and smelled good. Lucy loved cocoa, so she took a sip. It was the best cocoa she ever had. She wanted to drink it all, but she knew she had to share. Lucy took the cocoa and went back to her mom. She showed her the mysterious house and the cocoa. They both drank the cocoa and smiled. They were happy to share the special cocoa from the mysterious house.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a tree. The cat said, "I bet I can balance on that tree branch better than you can!" The dog said, "No way, I can do it too!" They both climbed up the tree and started to balance on the branch. They had to wait for a while to see who would fall off first. It was very rare for a cat and a dog to play together like this. Suddenly, a big bird came and sat on the branch too. The branch started to shake, and the cat and the dog both fell off! They laughed and said, "We didn't expect that!" The cat and the dog became best friends and played together every day.
