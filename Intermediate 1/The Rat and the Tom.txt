Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rat and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a rat. The rat lived in a dark place. The rat liked the dark. He had a friend named Tom. One day, Tom came to see the rat. Tom said, "Hi, rat! Do you want to play?" The rat nodded. They played together in the dark. Tom and the rat had fun. They played hide and seek. They laughed and ran. The rat was happy with Tom. They were best friends.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found an arrow. He did not know what it was for. He showed it to his friend, Sam. Sam just shrugged. He did not know what it was for either. They went to ask their friend, Sue. Sue looked at the arrow, and she got frustrated. She said, "I don't know what it's for, but I want to find out!" They all agreed to look for clues. As they looked, a big bird flew down and took the arrow from Sue. The bird said, "Thank you for finding my arrow!" The friends were surprised. They did not know birds could talk. The bird flew away, and they all laughed.

Once upon a time, there lived a giant. He was very tall and strong and he loved to roar. Everyone was scared of him and ran away whenever he made his loud noise. One day the giant was feeling very brave and decided to climb up a tall mountain. He was so strong that he almost made it to the top, but then he got stuck. He shouted for help and all the small animals started to chatter. Finally, one of them offered to help him down. "Oh I am so grateful" said the giant. "Thank you very much." The small animal was happy and started to tug at the giant's leg to get him down but it was too tight, the giant was stuck. No matter how he tried, he couldn't get free. He shouted even louder this time and soon the mountain shook and started to crumble. Everyone screamed and the giant had to end his journey with a very bad ending.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the park. It was an icy day, but Lucy liked the cold. She saw her friend Tom at the park. They played and had fun. Then, Lucy started to feel hungry. She told Tom, "I want to eat something yummy." Tom said, "Imagine we have a big pastry! We can pretend to eat it." Lucy liked the idea. They both closed their eyes and imagined a big, sweet pastry. They pretended to eat it and said, "Yum!" Just then, a nice old lady walked by. She saw Lucy and Tom playing. The lady said, "I have a real pastry for you!" She gave them a big, sweet pastry. Lucy and Tom were so happy. They shared the pastry and said, "Thank you!" The icy day became warm and fun with their new treat.

Once upon a time, there was a young pirate named Tim. Tim had a big ship and he loved to sail on the sea. He had a lot of friends who were pirates too. They all liked to play and have fun on their ship. One day, while they were sailing, they saw a big island. Tim had a question. He asked his friends, "What is on that big island?" They all looked and wondered. They decided to sail to the island to see what they could find. When they got to the island, they found a big treasure chest. They were so happy! They opened the chest and found many shiny things inside. They played with the shiny things and shared them with each other. Then, they went back to their ship and sailed away, excited to tell others about their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to dance and sing. One day, Amy was going to dance on a stage. She was very excited but also a little bit scared. Before going on the stage, Amy's mom gave her a big kiss on the cheek. "You'll do great!" her mom said. Amy felt better and went on the stage. She started to dance and sing, but then she forgot some of her dance moves. Amy felt ashamed and wanted to cry. But then, she remembered her mom's kiss and the nice words. Amy took a deep breath and tried again. This time, she did all her dance moves right and finished her song with a big smile. Everyone clapped and cheered for Amy. She was so happy and not ashamed anymore.
