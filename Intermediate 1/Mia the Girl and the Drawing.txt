Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Drawing

One day, a little girl named Mia found a journal. It was a big, old book. Mia wanted to design in the journal. She drew a scary monster with big eyes and sharp teeth. She showed her mom the scary drawing. Mia's mom said, "That is a very scary monster! Be careful, it might come to life at night!" Mia laughed and said, "It's just a drawing, mom. It can't come to life!" That night, Mia heard a noise. She looked around and saw the scary monster from her journal! It had big eyes and sharp teeth, just like her drawing. Mia was scared, but then she remembered what her mom said. She knew what to do. Mia grabbed the journal and a pen. She quickly drew a big, happy sun. The sun shined so bright, it made the scary monster disappear. Mia was happy and safe again. From that day on, Mia only drew happy things in her journal.

One day, Jane was taking her little dog for a walk. She noticed an iron in the grass. It was a very small iron and Jane thought it looked very funny. She picked up the iron and put it in her pocket. Jane and her little dog continued to walk around the neighbourhood. Suddenly, Jane noticed a little bird in a tree above her. The little bird was chirping loudly and it hopped around from limb to limb. Jane thought it was very cute and she wanted to get closer to it. She had a piece of bread in her pocket and she decided to give it to the little bird. The little bird flew down and grabbed the piece of bread. It started chirping happily. Jane smiled and said hello to the little bird. Jane and her little dog continued their walk and the little bird followed them chirping away. Jane and the little bird were now friends. Everywhere they went, the little bird followed and flew around them. Jane was happy that she had noticed the little bird. She knew they would be friends forever.

Tim and Sue were twins who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big house with a high fence. They wondered who lived there and what they had inside. "Let's go and see," Tim said. He found a hole in the fence and crawled through. Sue followed him. They saw a big garden with many flowers and trees. They also saw a big pool with water that sparkled in the sun. "Wow, this is so pretty," Sue said. She ran to the pool and dipped her toes in the water. Tim saw something shiny on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was a coin. He looked around and saw more coins scattered on the grass. "Look, Sue, money!" Tim said. He ran to Sue and showed her the coin. Sue was surprised and happy. She took the coin and saw that it had a picture of a man with a crown. "Who is this?" Sue asked. "I don't know. Maybe he is the king," Tim said. They decided to collect more coins and put them in their pockets. They thought they were very lucky and wealthy. They did not notice a man watching them from the window of the house. He was the owner of the house and the coins. He had a hobby of making patterns with coins on the grass. He was not happy to see the twins taking his coins. "Hey, you two, stop that!" he shouted. He came out of the house and ran to the twins. The twins were scared and dropped the coins. They ran back to the hole in the fence and escaped. They learned that it was not nice to take things that did not belong to them. They also learned that being wealthy was not just about having money, but about being happy and kind.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a harmless bug and waved at it. The bug waved back with its tiny legs. Mia was very happy. Mia saw a doctor walking in the park. She ran to the doctor and said, "Hi, doctor! I saw a bug and we waved at each other!" The doctor smiled and said, "That's very nice, Mia. Bugs can be good friends." Mia and the doctor walked together in the park. They saw more bugs and waved at them too. The bugs waved back, and everyone was happy. Mia and the doctor became good friends, just like Mia and the harmless bugs.
