Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Song

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She saw a weird, dirty thing on the table. It was an ashtray. Lily did not know what it was, but she knew it was dirty. She wanted to make it clean. Lily took the ashtray to the kitchen. She filled the sink with water and started to wash it. As she washed the ashtray, she sang a happy song. "Wash, wash, wash, I will make it clean!" she said. Her mom came into the kitchen and saw Lily washing the ashtray. "What are you doing, Lily?" her mom asked. "I found this weird thing and I am making it clean!" Lily said. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Lily! You are so helpful." Lily felt happy and proud that she could help her mom.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They wanted to find a track to race on. Tim was very excited, but Spot was a little scared. As they walked, Tim said, "Don't be scared, Spot. We will keep each other safe." They found a track and started to run. Tim was fast, but Spot was faster. They laughed and played on the track. Then, a big, mean dog came to the track. He growled at Tim and Spot. Tim said, "Go away, mean dog!" But the mean dog did not listen. He chased Tim and Spot off the track. Tim and Spot ran and ran, but the mean dog was faster. They could not get away. In the end, the mean dog caught them and they were very scared. Tim and Spot never went back to the track again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous girl named Lily. She was well-known because she could do a special spell. This spell made people happy and smile. Lily loved to share her spell with everyone she met. One day, Lily met a sad boy named Tim. Tim was crying by the tree. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim replied, "I lost my toy and now I am very sad." Lily wanted to help Tim feel better, so she decided to use her special spell. Lily hugged Tim and said the magic words, "Happy, happy, smile, smile!" Suddenly, Tim stopped crying and began to smile. He felt the warmth of Lily's hug and the power of her spell. Tim said, "Thank you, Lily! You made me feel happy again!" From that day on, Lily and Tim became best friends, sharing hugs and smiles with everyone in the town.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, especially in the spring. The sun was warm, the flowers were pretty, and the birds sang happy songs. One day, Tim saw a black cat with big green eyes. The cat was sitting under a tree, and it looked sad. Tim walked up to the cat and asked, "Why are you sad, black cat?" The cat said, "I am cold. The sun is not warm enough to melt the ice on my fur." Tim wanted to help the cat. He thought of a plan. He ran home and got a warm, soft blanket. He wrapped the blanket around the black cat. They sat together under the tree, waiting for the ice to melt. As they sat, the sun began to shine brighter and warmer. Soon, the ice on the cat's fur started to melt. The black cat was so happy and warm. Tim and the black cat became the best of friends, and they played together every day in the spring sunshine.

One day, a big yellow duck and a small blue fish were friends. They liked to talk and play together. The duck had a raft that was fit for both of them to have fun on the water. One sunny day, they went to the pond with the raft. They were so happy to play on the raft. The duck and the fish talked about how fun it was to be on the raft. They knew they would have a great day. But then, something they did not think would happen, happened. The raft started to go very fast! It was not what they wanted. The duck and the fish were scared. They talked and thought of a plan. The fish swam very fast and pulled the raft to make it slow down. The duck and the fish were safe and happy again. They knew they could face anything when they were together.
