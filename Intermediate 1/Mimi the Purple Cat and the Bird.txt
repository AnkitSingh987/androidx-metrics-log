Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi the Purple Cat and the Bird

One day, a purple cat named Mimi went to the store. She wanted to buy yogurt for her snack. Mimi put the yogurt in a sack and walked home. On the way home, Mimi met her friend, a dog named Bobo. Bobo said, "Hi Mimi! What's in your sack?" Mimi said, "I have yogurt for my snack. Do you want to share?" Just then, a bird flew by and took the sack from Mimi's paw. Mimi and Bobo were sad. They did not know what to do. But then, the bird came back and dropped the sack on the ground. Mimi and Bobo were happy again. They shared the yogurt and had a fun snack time together.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. Lily had a pink laser that made funny noises and lights. Ben had a blue laser that did the same. They liked to pretend they were space heroes fighting scary monsters. "Zap, zap, zap!" Lily said, pointing her laser at the sofa. "I got you, big green monster!" "Pow, pow, pow!" Ben said, aiming his laser at the lamp. "I got you, giant purple alien!" They had fun making up stories and sounds with their lasers. But then, Lily saw Ben's laser and wanted to try it. "Ben, can I play with your laser?" Lily asked. "It looks cool." "No, it's mine!" Ben said. "You have your own laser. Go away!" Lily felt sad and angry. She wanted to share, but Ben did not. She thought Ben was being mean and selfish. She decided to take his laser by force. She ran towards Ben and grabbed his laser. Ben did not like that. He pulled back and tried to keep his laser. They both tugged and shouted. "Give it back!" Ben yelled. "No, you give it to me!" Lily screamed. They were so busy fighting that they did not see their mom coming. She heard the noise and saw them pulling and pushing. She was not happy. "Stop it, both of you!" she said. "What are you doing?" They stopped and looked at their mom. They felt ashamed and scared. They knew they were in trouble. "We were playing with our lasers, but he/she did not want to share!" they both said. Mom shook her head. She took both lasers and put them on the table. "That's not how you play with your toys," she said. "You have to share and be nice to each other. Lasers are fun, but they are not worth fighting over. Fighting is bad and hurts your feelings. Do you understand?" They nodded. They knew mom was right. They felt sorry for what they did. "Sorry, mom," they said. "Sorry, Lily/Ben," they said to each other. Mom smiled. She hugged them and gave them a kiss. "That's better," she said. "Now, why don't you play together and take turns with your lasers? I'm sure you can have more fun that way." They agreed. They thanked mom and took their lasers. They went back to the living room and played nicely. They shared and took turns. They made up new stories and sounds. They had more fun than before. They learned that sharing is good and fighting is bad. They learned that lasers are not as important as friends. They learned to be kind and happy.

One day, little Jack wanted to do something fun. He had an armchair in the living room, and so he decided to try to solve something. He found some puzzles in a box and took them out to the armchair. Jack thought the puzzles were hard, but he tried his best. He studied them and thought hard. Every now and then he would get mad and throw the puzzles down, making a loud noise. He thought he would never solve them. Finally, after many attempts and lots of trying, Jack solved all of the puzzles, and he was so happy! He jumped out of the armchair and shouted out loud with joy. It was a great feeling, and Jack was so excited that he had solved them.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emma. She was three years old and loved life. Every day she would run around the garden shouting and laughing with joy. Her parents were very proud. One day, Emma was feeling very sad. Her mum asked Emma why she was sad. "I want something I can't have," she wept. Mum tickled Emma and said, "Tell me what it is and maybe I can help you get it." Emma said, "I want to be as lazy as the cats in the garden, but I know that is impossible." Mum smiled and said, "You can't always be lazy, but life still has plenty of adventures for you to enjoy!" Emma smiled and wiped away her tears. She knew that mum was right and she was excited to find out what the future held. She ran off, dancing down the garden path with joy.
