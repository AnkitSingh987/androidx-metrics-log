Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot found a spicy red ball. He wanted to gain a new friend to play with. Spot saw a big dog named Max. "Want to play with my spicy red ball?" Spot asked. Max looked at the ball and nodded. They played together, and Spot was happy. But Max was not a nice dog. He bit Spot's heel and took the spicy red ball. Spot was sad and hurt. The bad ending made Spot learn to be careful when making new friends.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Timmy. He loved to play with his toys and make castles with them. One day, Timmy wanted to make the biggest castle ever. He was very excited and started to build it carefully. He was very delicate so the castle looked perfect! Suddenly, something scary happened. A dragon appeared in the sky and it flew down to Timmy's castle! It pinched the tower with its claws and destroyed it. Timmy was terrified - it was like a nightmare! Luckily, his mom came to the rescue. She put her arm around Timmy and said "Don't worry, everything will be okay." Timmy felt much better and they cleaned up the mess together. From then on, Timmy only built small castles that were safe from dragons.

Bob and Joe were playing in the park with their baseball. They threw it back and forth, and Bob was getting better each throw. He smiled as Joe told him he was getting good. Suddenly, there was a fierce roar coming from the bushes nearby. Bob and Joe froze and looked at each other with wide eyes. Joe asked, "What was that?" Bob replied, "I don't know, let's go find out." They walked over to the bushes and found a large hang. It had huge claws and sharp teeth. Bob gasped. "It's a fierce bear!" The bear roared again and jumped at them. Bob and Joe screamed and ran away. They grabbed their baseball and hung it up on the swingset. The bear chased after them, but they ran faster and the bear couldn't catch up. Bob and Joe stopped to catch their breath. Joe said, "Let's never come back here with a baseball again!" Bob agreed, "Definitely not!"

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a big bowl of pasta. He wanted to eat it all. Tom asked his friend, the dog, "Can I eat the pasta?" The dog said, "Yes, but you must first release my toy from the tree." Tom was happy to help his friend. He jumped high and got the toy. When Tom came back to eat the pasta, it was gone! A sneaky rat had taken it. Tom and the dog chased the rat and got the pasta back. They shared the pasta and played with the ball. They were happy and full.

Sara and Ben are going to visit Grandma on Tuesday. They are very happy because they love Grandma and her large house. They have to pack their bags with clothes and toys. "Ben, what are you packing?" Sara asks her brother. "I am packing my cars and my books and my teddy bear," Ben says. "That is too much, Ben. You only need one car and one book and one teddy bear. We have to share the space in the bag," Sara says. "But I want to play with all of them at Grandma's house," Ben says. "You can play with Grandma's toys, too. She has a lot of fun things for us. And we can read her stories and cuddle with her," Sara says. "Okay, Sara. I will pack only one car and one book and one teddy bear. But you have to pack less, too. You have too many dresses and dolls and crayons," Ben says. "Fine, Ben. I will pack only one dress and one doll and one crayon. But you have to help me zip the bag. It is very heavy," Sara says. They help each other zip the bag and put it by the door. They are ready to go to Grandma's house on Tuesday. They hug each other and smile. They are very happy.
