Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Emma and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Emma. She went to the shop with her mom. When they arrived, they saw a thin man. Emma asked him, "What do you do?" The man said, "I tell stories." Emma was very excited. She asked the man to tell her a story. The man told a funny story about rabbits. Emma laughed and laughed. She asked him to tell another. The man told another funny story. Emma laughed again, and asked for one more. The man smiled and said, "I have told you three stories, so now it is your turn to tell a story!" Emma was a bit shy but, with a bit of help from her mom, she told a story of her own. It was so funny that the man started to laugh, too. Then Emma and her mom said goodbye to the man and left the shop. Emma was very happy!

Once upon a time, there was a gifted alligator named Ally. Ally could shine very bright. She lived in a big pond with her friends. They all loved to play together. One day, a little bird came to the pond. The bird saw Ally shine and said, "Wow! You can shine so bright! Can you teach me how to shine too?" Ally smiled and said, "Of course, little bird! Let's try together." Ally and the little bird tried and tried. The sun was shining, and they were having fun. After a while, the little bird started to shine too! They were both very happy. From that day on, Ally and the little bird became best friends, and they shined together every day.

Johnny had a plan. He wanted to buy a dinosaur. He put on his shoes and took all his coins. He went outside and headed down the street. He went to the toy store and saw a dinosaur. It was so cool! He had enough money and so he bought it. Johnny was so happy, he took the dinosaur outside and ran around with it. It was raining so he got very wet. He started to cry, because the rain had ruined his new dinosaur. Johnny was very sad. He had to go home without it. He learned a lesson that day: you can't buy a dinosaur and go play in the rain.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dark forest. In the forest, there lived a kind guard. The guard liked to help all the animals who lived there. He was a good friend to them. One day, the guard saw a little bird who was sad. The bird had lost its way and could not find its family. The guard wanted to help the bird. He said, "Do not worry, little bird. I will find your family for you." The guard looked all around the forest. He asked all the animals if they had seen the bird's family. The animals wanted to help too. They all looked for the bird's family together. Soon, they found the bird's family in a tall tree. The guard offered to help the little bird fly back to its family. The bird was very happy. The guard and the bird said thank you to all the animals who helped. The little bird was back with its family, and they all lived happily in the big, dark forest.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing with his ball. He threw the ball high in the sky. The boy saw a big truck. It was an ambulance. The ambulance was going very fast. Tim heard a loud sound. It was the ambulance's siren. It went "wee-woo, wee-woo!" Tim's ball fell down. It fell deep in a hole. Tim was sad. He wanted to get his ball back. He asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam said, "I can help you, Tim. Let's find a way to get the ball out of the hole." Tim and Sam found a big stick. They tried to use the stick to get the ball. Tim said, "One, two, three, strike!" They hit the ball with the stick. But the ball did not come out. It went deeper in the hole. Then, the ambulance came back. It stopped near Tim and Sam. The driver got out. He said, "I saw you boys need help. Let's get your ball out of the hole." The driver had a long rope. He put the rope in the hole and got the ball out. Tim and Sam were very happy. They thanked the driver. The driver smiled and said, "You're welcome! Have fun playing!" The ambulance went away, and Tim and Sam played with the ball all day.
