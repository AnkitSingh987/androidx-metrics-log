Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Sam and the Barn

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play on the farm with their dad. One day, they see a big pile of hay in the barn. They have an idea. "Let's dress the hay like a scarecrow!" Ben says. "OK, but we need some clothes and a hat," Sam says. They run to the house and find some old clothes and a hat in a box. They bring them to the barn. They put the clothes and the hat on the hay. They make a face with some buttons and a carrot. "Look, our scarecrow is done!" Ben says. "He looks funny and strong," Sam says. They hear their dad calling them for lunch. They run to the house, but they forget to close the barn door. A gust of wind blows the door open. The scarecrow falls down and the hay flies away. "Oh no, our scarecrow!" Ben says. "Maybe we can make another one later," Sam says. They hug each other and go to eat. They hope the wind will stop soon.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. He was always falling and making a mess. One day, Tom was playing outside in the mud. He got very dirty. Tom's mom saw him and said, "You need to wash, Tom!" She took him inside and helped him wash in a big tub of warm water. The water turned brown from all the dirt. Tom liked the bubbles and splashed around, making a wet mess. When Tom was all clean, he remembered the fun he had in the mud. He told his mom about his memory. She laughed and said, "Next time, try not to be so clumsy!" Tom agreed, but he knew he would always be a little clumsy and have fun memories.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a photo on the floor. It was a picture of his mom and dad. He was worried because the photo was dirty. Tim wanted to clean it and give it back to his mom. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I found this photo. It is dirty. Can I clean it?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can clean it. Be careful, okay?" Tim took the photo to the sink. As he was cleaning it, he slipped on some water. The photo flew into the air and landed in a bowl of fruit. Tim was sad and thought he ruined the photo. But when he picked it up, the photo was clean and dry. Tim was happy and gave the photo back to his mom. They both laughed at the funny twist that happened.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big bag. She did not know what was inside. Sue felt very happy to find the bag. She wanted to show the bag to her friends. She thought they would like it too. Sue went to her friends and showed them the bag. They all wanted to know what was inside. They opened the bag and saw a small, humble cat. The cat looked at them and said, "Thank you for finding me!" They were all very surprised. They did not know a cat could talk! The cat told Sue and her friends that she was a magic cat. She could make their wishes come true. They all felt very excited. They thanked the cat and made their wishes. The cat made their wishes come true and they all had a fun day.

One day, Mandy and Steve were walking by a river. Steve looked at the river and saw a crocodile. Mandy was scared, but Steve asked it, "Hello crocodile, what are you doing?" The crocodile said, "I'm splashing in the water!" Then Steve said to Mandy, "Let's go and splash with the crocodile!" Mandy was scared, but then she agreed. So Mandy and Steve ran to the water. It was empty, so they started splashing and swimming. The crocodile was splashing too, and soon they all had lots of fun. They splashed for a long time, jumping up and down in the water. Eventually, the sun started to go down and the sky was getting darker. So Steve and Mandy said goodbye to the crocodile and ran home. They had had a very fun time, and they promised they would come back and splash with the crocodile again!
