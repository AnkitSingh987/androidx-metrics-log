Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Box

Sara and Tom are playing with their dolls. They pretend that their dolls are having a birthday party. Sara has a big box with a bow on it. She says it is a present for Tom's doll. "Can I deliver the present to your doll?" Sara asks. "Yes, please. She will be very happy," Tom says. Sara walks to Tom's doll and gives her the box. She says, "Happy birthday, doll. This is from me and my doll. We hope you like it." Tom's doll opens the box. Inside, there is a dress made of thread. It is red and yellow and blue and green. It has many holes and knots. It looks very silly. "What is this?" Tom's doll says. "This is not a dress. This is a mess." Sara and Tom look at the dress. They start to laugh. "I'm sorry, doll. I made the dress myself. I used thread from my mom's sewing box. But I don't know how to sew. I just tied the thread together," Sara says. "That's okay, Sara. It was very nice of you to make something for me. But maybe next time, you can ask your mom to help you. Or you can give me something else, like a book or a toy," Tom's doll says. Sara and Tom nod. They hug their dolls and say sorry. Then they continue to play and have fun. They think the dress is very silly, but they still love their dolls.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to school. It was a wet day, so she wore her rain boots and coat. In her class, there was a boy named Tim who liked to repeat everything Lily said. At lunch time, Lily said, "I like apples." Tim said, "I like apples." Lily said, "I like to play." Tim said, "I like to play." Lily got mad and said, "Stop repeating me!" Tim said, "Stop repeating me!" Lily was sad and walked away. Later, Lily saw Tim sitting alone. She went to him and said, "Why do you repeat everything I say?" Tim looked sad and said, "I don't know what to say, so I repeat you." Lily felt bad for being mad. She said, "Let's be friends and talk together." Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's be friends and talk together." From that day on, Lily and Tim talked and played together, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a hidden place, there was a big body. This body was a big, big tree. Many little animals lived in the tree. One day, a small bird saw the big tree. The bird wanted to visit the tree and make new friends. The bird flew to the tree and saw a cute squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hi! I am Squirrel. Do you want to play with me?" The bird said, "Yes! I am Bird. Let's play together!" So, they played and had fun. Then, Squirrel said, "Follow me! I have a secret to show you." Bird was excited and followed Squirrel. Squirrel took Bird to a secret part of the tree. There, they found a hidden door. Squirrel opened the door, and they saw a big, shiny treasure! They were so happy and surprised. They shared the treasure with all their friends in the tree. From that day on, Bird and Squirrel were best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big pastry shop. The shop had many yummy treats. Everyone loved to go there and eat the pastries. One day, the shop had an incredible idea. They wanted to have a fun game. The game was to see who could eat the most pastries. Many people came to play the game. They all wanted to win. They sat at a long table with lots of pastries. They started to eat. Some people ate fast, and some people ate slow. Everyone was having fun and laughing. In the end, a little girl named Lucy won the game. She ate the most pastries. Everyone clapped and cheered for her. Lucy felt happy and proud. The pastry shop gave her a big prize. It was a huge pastry to take home and share with her family. And from that day on, Lucy knew that she was incredible at eating pastries.
