Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Mighty Dog and the Basket

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Buddy. Buddy had a big basket of toys. He loved to play with them all day long. One sunny day, Buddy saw a little bug. The bug would blink its tiny eyes. Buddy was curious and wanted to play with the bug. So, he put the bug in his basket. Buddy and the bug played together all day. They had a lot of fun. When the sun went down, Buddy let the bug go. He knew the bug had to go home. The next day, Buddy saw the bug again. They played and played. Buddy was happy to have a new friend. And the bug was happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Kate who loved the night. She was always curious about the stars and why they shined so brightly. One night, her mom was outside playing with her. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise from up above. They looked up into the sky and saw a big, black bird flying around, covering up the stars with its wings. Kate was very sad. She wanted to see the stars again but the bird wouldn’t move. Her mom said, "Don’t worry Kate, I can provide you some light". She pointed up to the sky and soon enough, the stars were back and it was almost as if the bird had never been there. Kate was so excited. She hugged her mom and said, "Thank you for providing me the light". Her mom smiled and said, "You’re welcome, sweetheart. Now, let’s go inside and dream of all the stars". And so Kate and her mom did just that. Every night, they would look up into the sky and see the stars twinkle in the night sky, providing a beautiful, black light.

One day, a polite lady went to the park. She had a big red ball. She loved to play with her ball. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. She knew it was a great day to play. As she played, the ball rolled away. The lady started to search for her ball. She looked under a tree, behind a bench, and near the pond. But she could not find her ball. She felt sad. A little boy saw the lady searching. He remembered seeing a big red ball near a bush. The boy went to the bush and found the ball. He gave it back to the polite lady. She was so happy and said "thank you." They both played with the ball together and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a small classroom, there were many kids. They were all good friends. They liked to play, draw, and learn together. The teacher, Miss Lily, was kind and always alert. She made sure everyone was happy and safe. One day, a new boy named Tim came to the classroom. He was a little shy. The other kids wanted to be his friend. They asked him, "Do you want to play with us?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that." They played together and had a lot of fun. Miss Lily saw them playing and was happy. She said, "Kids, you all behave so well. I am proud of you for being kind to Tim." The kids felt happy that Miss Lily was proud of them. They promised to always be nice and behave well in the classroom.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They wanted to spend time together and have fun. At the park, Tim saw a track where some kids were playing. He wanted to join them, so he asked his mom if he could play too. His mom said, "Yes, but remember to be nice to others." While playing on the track, Tim met a girl named Sue. They played together, but soon, Tim started to feel bad because he was not winning the games. Tim said to Sue, "I don't want to play with you anymore. You always win!" Sue felt sad and walked away. Tim's mom saw what happened and talked to him. She said, "Tim, it's not nice to be mean to others just because you're not winning. It's important to be kind and have fun." Tim understood and said sorry to Sue. They played together again and had a great time. Tim learned that being kind is more important than winning.
