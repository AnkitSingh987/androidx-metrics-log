Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a bald man who loved to soar high in the sky. He would jump and flap his arms and fly like a bird. He had a big cupboard in his house, full of fun toys and tasty treats. One day, the bald man wanted to fly higher than ever before. He thought that if he could reach the clouds, he could find a special toy to add to his cupboard. So, he jumped and flapped his arms harder than ever, and he began to soar up into the sky. As he flew higher and higher, the bald man started to feel very cold. He wanted to go back home to his warm cupboard, but he couldn't. The wind blew him far away, and he got lost. The bald man was very sad, and he never found his way back home to his cupboard full of toys and treats.

Once upon a time, there was a pigeon. The pigeon was sad and miserable. It was hungry and could not find any food to eat. One day, the pigeon found a big piece of bread. The pigeon was very happy. It started to chew the bread. As it chewed, it felt better and not so miserable anymore. Soon, other pigeons came. They all chewed the bread together. They were not miserable anymore. They were full and happy. The pigeons flew away, thankful for the food they found. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a mechanic named Tom was working in his shop. He was an enthusiastic man who loved to fix cars. He had a big car that he called "Big Red." Tom liked to drive Big Red around town. One day, Tom saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My toy car is broken." Tom wanted to help Tim, so he said, "I can fix your car!" Tim was happy and gave his toy car to Tom. Tom tried to fix the toy car, but it was very hard. He could not fix it. Tom sighed and said, "I am sorry, Tim. I cannot fix your car." Tim was sad again, and he cried. Tom was sad too. He could not make Tim happy. The toy car stayed broken, and Tim went home sad.

Lily was a big girl. She liked to do things by herself. She was independent. She could brush her teeth, put on her shoes, and pack her bag for school. She did not need help from Mom or Dad. One day, Lily had a new baby brother. His name was Max. Mom and Dad were very busy with Max. They gave him milk, changed his diapers, and rocked him to sleep. Lily felt a little sad. She wanted to play with Mom and Dad. Lily decided to show them how independent she was. She said, "I can take care of Max. I am a big girl." Mom and Dad smiled and said, "OK, Lily. You can watch Max for a little while. But don't forget to call us if you need anything." Lily took Max to her room. She showed him her toys, her books, and her drawings. She sang him a song and made him laugh. She was having fun with Max. She forgot about Mom and Dad. Mom and Dad came to check on Lily and Max. They knocked on the door and said, "Lily, are you OK? Do you need anything?" Lily did not hear them. She was too busy with Max. She forgot to answer. Mom and Dad opened the door and saw Lily and Max on the floor. They were playing with blocks and cars. Lily looked up and said, "Hi, Mom and Dad. Look, I am taking care of Max. He is happy. I am independent." Mom and Dad smiled and hugged Lily and Max. They said, "You are a very good big sister, Lily. And you are very independent. But you don't have to forget about us. We love you and we want to play with you too." Lily smiled and said, "I love you too, Mom and Dad. And I love Max. Can we all play together?" Mom and Dad said, "Of course, Lily. We can all play together. That would be fun."
