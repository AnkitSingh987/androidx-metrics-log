Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Rabbit

Sara liked to play in the snow. She made a big snowman with a hat and a scarf. She gave him a carrot nose and two rocks for eyes. She was very happy with her snowman. She called him Bob. One day, Sara went to visit Bob. But she saw something strange. Bob's nose was gone. And his eyes were gone. And his hat and scarf were gone. Sara was sad and angry. Who took Bob's things? She looked around and saw some footprints in the snow. She followed them. The footprints led her to a big tree. Behind the tree, Sara saw a rabbit. The rabbit was holding Bob's carrot nose in its mouth. And it was wearing Bob's hat and scarf. And it had Bob's eyes in its paws. Sara was very surprised. The rabbit looked funny and cute. But it also looked cold and pale. Sara felt sorry for the rabbit. Sara said to the rabbit, "Hello, rabbit. Do you like Bob's things? You can have them. But can I have one thing back? Can I have Bob's nose? He needs it to smell." The rabbit nodded and gave Sara the carrot. Sara thanked the rabbit and smiled. She put the carrot back on Bob's face. She said to the rabbit, "You can keep the hat and the scarf and the eyes. They look good on you. But can you be my friend? And can you visit Bob sometimes? He likes friends too." The rabbit nodded again and smiled. Sara hugged the rabbit and said, "You are a good rabbit. And you are not cold and pale anymore. You are warm and bright." The rabbit hugged Sara back and said, "You are a good girl. And you are not sad and angry anymore. You are happy and kind." Sara and the rabbit became friends. And they played with Bob in the snow. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new notebook. He liked to draw in his notebook every day. One day, he wanted to cut a picture from his notebook to give to his mom. Tim found a pair of scissors. He was very careful because the scissors were sharp. He cut the picture out of his notebook. It made a loud sound, like a big snap. When Tim gave the picture to his mom, she was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! I love your loud cutting!" Tim felt proud and smiled. From that day on, he cut more pictures from his notebook to share with his friends and family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb. One day, he went outside to play in the big landscape. There were trees, hills, and grass. It was a bit messy from the rain the day before. As Tim was climbing a small hill, he saw a big dog. The dog looked at Tim and started to bark. Tim got scared and didn't know what to do. The dog wanted to play, but Tim thought the dog was angry. Tim decided to climb a tree to get away from the dog. He climbed up and up until he was safe. The dog sat down and looked sad. Tim saw that the dog was not angry, just messy and wanted to play. Tim climbed down from the tree and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a new squash. This squash could speak! It lived in a big garden with other squash friends. The new squash was very happy. It liked to talk to the other squash friends and the sun that made it grow. One day, the new squash heard a big noise. It was a big truck. The new squash knew something was going to happen. It told the other squash friends, but they did not believe the new squash. They did not think a big truck could change anything. The next day, the big truck came back. This time, it had a big box. The big box had a big surprise. The new squash was right. The big surprise was new seeds for the garden. The new squash was happy it could speak and help its friends. Now, the garden would have more squash friends and they could all play together.
