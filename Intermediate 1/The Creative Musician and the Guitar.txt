Category: Intermediate
Title: The Creative Musician and the Guitar

Once upon a time, there was a creative musician. He loved to play songs on his guitar. Every day, he would play for the people in his town. They liked to hear his music. One day, the musician was tired and needed to sleep. He said to his friend, "I am so tired. I want to sleep now." His friend said, "Okay, let's find a place for you to sleep." They went to a big tree and the musician slept under it. While the musician was sleeping, a big wind came and blew his guitar away. When he woke up, he was very sad. But then, a little bird came and sang a beautiful song. The musician was happy again. He learned that even when bad things happen, there is always something good that can come from it.

Ben likes to draw with his crayons. He has many colors and he makes pictures of his family, his toys and his pets. Sometimes he makes a mistake and he wants to change his picture. He uses his eraser to rub out the crayon. His eraser is white and soft and it makes the crayon disappear. One day, Ben is drawing a big truck with red and blue crayons. He wants to add some wheels, but he draws them too big. He tries to erase them, but his eraser does not work. He looks at his eraser and sees that it is broken. It has a big hole in the middle and it is very dirty. He is sad and he does not know what to do. He asks his mom for a new eraser, but she says she does not have one. She tells him to use a different paper or to draw something else. Ben does not want to do that. He likes his truck and he wants to fix it. He thinks hard and he has an idea. He runs to his room and he finds his scissors. He cuts out the wheels from another paper and he glues them on his truck. He is happy and he shows his mom his new picture. She smiles and she says he is very clever. Ben does not need his eraser anymore. He makes his broken eraser disappear in the trash.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big box. The box had a picture on it. The image on the box was of a big bone. Spot wanted the bone. Spot tried to move the box, but it was too heavy. He needed help to weigh the box. He asked his friend, a strong horse named Max, to help him. Max came and pushed the box with his strong legs. The box started to move. As they pushed the box, it went faster and faster. Spot and Max were so happy. But then, the box went too fast. It hit a tree and broke. The big bone in the image was not real. Spot and Max were sad. They did not get the big bone, and they had a bad day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in his room. He saw a ghost appear! Tim was frightened. He ran to his mom and told her, "Mom, there is a ghost in my room!" His mom went to look, but she did not see the ghost. The next day, Tim went to his friend's house. He told his friend about the ghost. His friend said, "I know a trick to make ghosts go away. Let's try it in your room." They went to Tim's room and tried the trick. They said, "Go away, ghost!" but the ghost did not go away. Then, Tim's big sister came into the room. She laughed and said, "That is not a real ghost! It is just a shadow on the wall. See?" She turned on the light, and the ghost was gone. Tim and his friend were not frightened anymore. They were happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a club. This club was very interesting. It had many toys and games that kids loved to play with. Every day, kids would go to the club to have fun with their friends. One day, a big truck came to the club. The truck was there to supply new toys for the kids to play with. The kids were very happy to see the truck. They knew that new toys were coming, and they could not wait to play with them. The truck driver opened the big doors and took out many interesting toys. There were balls, dolls, and even a big slide! The kids at the club clapped and cheered as the driver supplied the toys. They played with the new toys all day, and they had the best time ever at their interesting club.
