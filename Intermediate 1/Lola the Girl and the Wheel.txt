Title: Lola the Girl and the Wheel

Once there was a girl named Lola. She was only 3 years old but she was very brilliant. One day she decided to go out and explore. She was walking down the street with her baby doll when she suddenly saw a wheel. She had never seen a wheel before and she was very curious. Lola quickly ran over to the wheel, her baby doll in hand. She tried to push it but the wheel wouldn't move. She looked around and saw a tiny cup nearby. It was filled with a delicious drink. Lola picked up the cup and drank the drink. It was the best thing she'd ever had! Suddenly the wheel started to move as if it was alive. Lola was so surprised and so happy that she started to laugh and dance. Lola had a brilliant day. She didn't want it to end so she kept pushing the wheel around for hours. Then when it was time, she went home and told everyone what a brilliant day she'd had.

Once upon a time, there was a sad dog named Max. Max was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Max saw a big hole in the ground. He thought, "Maybe I can find a friend in there!" So, Max entered the hole. The hole was dark and scary, but Max was brave. Inside the hole, Max found a mine. In the mine, he met a little mouse named Sam. Max said, "Hi, Sam! Will you be my friend?" Sam looked at Max and said, "Yes, Max! I will be your friend." Max was so happy to have a friend. But then, a big rock fell and blocked the way out of the mine. Max and Sam were stuck inside. They tried to push the rock, but it was too heavy. Max and Sam were very sad because they could not get out. They stayed in the mine, and nobody found them.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big bench. The bench was very rare because it had many colors. It was in a room where a mom liked to cook. One day, the mom wanted to make a cake. She asked her little boy to help her. The little boy was happy to help his mom. He wanted to stir the cake mix. The little boy took a big spoon and started to stir. He stirred and stirred. The cake mix became smooth. They put the mix in a pan and baked it. The cake was very good. They ate it together at the colorful bench. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a rich man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. One day, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she didn't have toys to play with. Tom wanted to help Sue, so he gave her a toy. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you!" Tom felt good helping Sue. They played with the toy together and had fun. One hot day, they found some ice. Sue had an idea. She told Tom to rub the ice on his face to feel cool. Tom did it and felt better. They learned that sharing and helping each other is important. And they lived happily, being kind to everyone.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a powerful big boy named Tom. Tom liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a small girl named Sue playing near a puddle. Sue was having fun, and Tom wanted to play too. Tom went to Sue and wanted to play with the puddle. But instead of being nice, he decided to push Sue. Sue fell down and started to cry. Tom laughed and played with the puddle alone. He splashed the water and made a big mess. Soon, it started to rain, and the puddle became bigger and bigger. Tom got scared and wanted help, but Sue was still sad and did not want to help him. In the end, Tom got wet and cold, and he was not happy. The moral of the story is: be kind to others, and they will be kind to you.
