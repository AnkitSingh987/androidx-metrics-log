Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Castle

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim set out to go to the beach with his mom and dad. They all put on their hats and walked to the beach. It was a sunny day, and Tim was very happy. At the beach, Tim played with his toys. He made a big sand castle with his mom and dad. They found pretty shells and put them on the castle. They also saw little crabs and laughed when they ran into the water. When they got hungry, Tim's mom gave him a tasty sandwich. They all ate and drank, and then played some more. After a fun day at the beach, they went back home. Tim was tired but happy, and he couldn't wait to go to the beach again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy rocket that he loved to play with. One day, he went outside to play with his rocket in the open yard. Tim set his rocket on the grass and said, "Ready, set, go!" The rocket started to move and went up, up, up into the sky. Tim was so happy to see his rocket fly. He jumped and clapped his hands. As the rocket came back down, Tim's friend, Sue, came over. She saw the rocket and said, "Wow, Tim! Can I play too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, Tim and Sue played with the rocket in the open yard until the sun went down. They had so much fun and became best friends.

One day, a clumsy dog named Spot wanted to eat a waffle. Spot saw the waffle on the high table. He tried to jump up, but he could not reach it. Spot felt sad. Spot had a smart idea. He saw a big chair near the table. Spot thought, "If I push the chair, I can get the waffle!" Spot pushed the chair with all his strength. It moved closer to the table. Spot jumped on the chair, then onto the table. He was so happy! Spot could now reach the waffle. He took a big bite and smiled. Spot knew he would succeed, and he did.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She was an honest girl who always told the truth. One day, Sue woke up and saw that her door was open. She did not know who opened it, but she felt that something was going to happen. Sue went outside to play. She saw her friend Tom. They played with a ball and had fun. Then, they saw a big box by the door. They did not know what was inside, but they felt excited. They knew that the open door in the morning was a sign that something good was coming. Sue and Tom opened the box. Inside, they found many toys and games! They were so happy and played with them all day. They knew that the open door was a special gift for them. Sue and Tom learned that being honest and kind brings good things.

Once upon a time, there was a tired dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play all day long, but today he was very sleepy. He wanted to find a way to feel better, so he went for a walk. On his walk, Buddy found a special herb. He had heard that this herb could make tired animals feel better. Buddy wanted to share the herb with his friends, so he decided to unite all his friends to help them feel better too. Buddy went to find his friends, the cat, the bird, and the rabbit. He told them about the special herb and they all agreed to try it. They all ate a little bit of the herb and soon, they felt better. The tired friends were happy and played together all day long. And that's how Buddy and his friends learned the power of uniting and helping each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a nice bedroom. He liked to play with his toys in his bedroom. One day, Tim felt confused. He lost his favorite toy car. "Mommy, I can't find my car," Tim said. His mommy came to help him look for the car. They looked everywhere in the bedroom, but they could not find it. Mommy said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will replace your car with a new one." Tim felt happy. They went to the store and bought a new toy car. Tim played with his new car in his bedroom and was not confused anymore.
