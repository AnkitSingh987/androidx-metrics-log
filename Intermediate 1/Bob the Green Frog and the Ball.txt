Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Green Frog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Bob. Bob lived near a big pond. He loved to play and jump all day. One day, while Bob was playing, he saw something appear. It was a big, brown ball. Bob was very excited. He wanted to play with the ball. But he was a little careless. He didn't look around before he jumped. He jumped so high and landed on the ball. "Ouch!" said Bob. "That hurt!" A little girl named Sue saw Bob and the ball. She walked over to him. "Hi, frog! Are you okay?" she asked. Bob nodded his head. "Yes, I'm okay. Thank you!" Sue smiled and said, "Let's play together, but be careful next time!" And they played all day, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big hose in his yard. One sunny day, he wanted to play with the hose, but it was empty. Tim felt sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can you help me get water in the hose?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's go outside and fill the hose with water." They went outside and filled the hose with water. Tim was very happy. He played with the hose, making big splashes and watering the plants. His mom watched him and laughed. They had a great time together.

One day, Timmy and his dad drove to a lake. Timmy was so excited to go swimming. When they arrived, Timmy noticed something was wrong. The lake was broken! His dad told Timmy that they were going to have to repair the lake. Timmy thought that sounded like fun. His dad and some other people worked really hard to try and fix the lake. For a while, it looked like they were doing okay. But then, something happened and the lake stayed broken. Everyone was so sad. Timmy was really disappointed. He wanted to go swimming in the lake. They all tried their best to repair the lake, but nothing worked. So they had to go home without being able to use the lake. Timmy was so sad that the lake was still broken.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag of coal. He knew that coal was good to keep his house warm. Tim wanted to share his coal with his friends, so they could be warm too. One day, Tim went to his friend's house to give some coal. On his way, he saw a big, dark cave. Tim was brave, so he went inside the cave. He did not know what was in the cave. Inside the cave, Tim found a big, shiny rock. He thought it was a magic rock. He was very happy and picked it up. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, scary monster came out of the cave. The monster wanted the shiny rock too. Tim was brave, but the monster was too big and strong. The monster took the shiny rock and all of Tim's coal. Tim was sad and went back home with no coal. The bad ending made Tim learn to be careful and not go into dark caves alone.

Once upon a time, there was a poor bunny named Bob. Bob loved to spring and play in the forest. One day, while playing, he found a big box. The box was full of treasure! Bob was very happy. Bob wanted to share the treasure with his friend, a little bird named Sue. He went to Sue's tree and called, "Sue, Sue, I found a treasure! Come and see!" Sue flew down from her tree and looked at the treasure with Bob. Together, Bob and Sue shared the treasure with all of their friends in the forest. They all had a big party and played games. Bob and Sue were very happy that they could share the treasure with everyone. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to ride his bike. He saw his friend, Sue. Sue was sad. Her pie was dry. Tim wanted to help Sue feel happy. Tim said, "Let's ride to the store and get a new pie." Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's go!" They rode their bikes to the store. They found a big, yummy pie. Sue was happy. They took the pie home and ate it. The pie was not dry. It was very good. Tim and Sue were happy. They played and laughed all day.
