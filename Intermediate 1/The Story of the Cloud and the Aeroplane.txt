Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cloud and the Aeroplane

Once upon a time, there was a happy, clean aeroplane. Every day the aeroplane would go up, up in the sky and look down at the world. The aeroplane would glow with joy as it flew. One day, the aeroplane saw a dark cloud. The cloud started to grow bigger and bigger. Soon it was bigger than the aeroplane. "Oh no! What shall I do?" said the aeroplane. The cloud said, "I'm here to stay and make everything dark and cloudy." The aeroplane got scared and started to cry. But then it remembered that it glowed. So it shone brighter and brighter. Soon its glow was bigger than the dark cloud and it pushed the cloud away. The aeroplane cheered with happiness and its glow stayed bright! It flew around the sky, happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue and her mom were in the kitchen. They wanted to make a cake. Sue's mom said, "Sue, can you help me add the eggs?" Sue was excited to help. She took the eggs and added them to the big bowl. Sue's mom handed her a tray. "This tray is important," she said. "We need to put the cake mix on it to bake it." Sue carefully put the cake mix on the tray. She wanted to do a good job. When the cake was done, Sue and her mom took it out of the oven. They looked at the cake on the important tray. They were proud of their work. Sue said, "Mom, we did it! We made a cake!" And they both smiled and ate a big piece of cake.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to speed on his journey. One day, he went on a long trip with his best friend, a small dog named Barky. As they went on their journey, Zoomy said, "I love to speed! It's so much fun!" Barky agreed, "Yes, it is! But be careful, Zoomy. We don't want to get lost." After a while, Zoomy and Barky started to feel tired. They found a nice tree to rest under. "Let's stop here," Zoomy said. Barky nodded, "Good idea, Zoomy. We need a break." They took a short nap under the tree. When they woke up, they felt better and ready to finish their journey. Zoomy and Barky continued on their way, having lots of fun and making sure to be safe. In the end, Zoomy and Barky made it home just in time for dinner. They were happy they went on their journey, and they couldn't wait for their next adventure together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went upstairs to play. She saw the sun shine through her window. It made her room so bright and happy. Lucy loved to play with her toys when the sun was shining. Lucy's mom called from downstairs, "Lucy, do you want a snack?" Lucy said, "Yes, please!" She ran downstairs to see what her mom had for her. It was a sour lemon. Lucy made a funny face when she tasted it. Lucy's mom laughed and said, "I have a sweet treat for you too!" She gave Lucy a yummy cookie. Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" She took her cookie upstairs and enjoyed it while the sun continued to shine.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He loved to pick flowers and give them to his mom. But today, he saw a big man who looked very serious. Tim was a little scared of the man, so he went to his mom and said, "Mom, I hate that man. He looks mean." His mom told him, "Tim, it's not nice to say you hate someone. Let's go talk to him." Tim didn't want to, but he trusted his mom. They walked over to the big man, and Tim's mom said, "Hello, my son was scared because you looked serious. Can you tell us why?" The big man looked down and said, "I'm sorry I scared your son. I was just thinking about how much I miss my mom. She loved flowers, and I wanted to pick some for her. But I don't know which ones she would like." Tim felt bad for the man and decided to help him. They all picked flowers together, and the big man smiled. Tim learned that you can't judge someone by how they look.
