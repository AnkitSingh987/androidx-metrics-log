Category: Intermediate
Title: The Shrimp and the Pond

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a little shrimp. The shrimp was very small, but it was also very beautiful. It had a shiny tail and big, round eyes. The shrimp loved to swim in the pond and play with its friends. One day, the shrimp wanted to find a new place to play. It swam and swam, but it could not manage to find a new place. The shrimp was sad and wanted to go back to its friends. But it did not know the way back. Then, a big, kind fish saw the shrimp. The fish wanted to help the shrimp. It said, "I can show you the way back to your friends." The shrimp was so happy! They swam together and soon, the shrimp was back with its friends. The shrimp was very thankful to the big fish for helping it. And they all played together in the beautiful pond.

One day, Andrew sailed his boat on the lake. He sailed past a strawberry field and saw a lot of delicious strawberries growing there. Andrew wanted to eat them, so he sailed his boat to the shore and picked some strawberries. Andrew was eating the strawberries when he felt weak. He ate too many and now he felt tired. He had to take a rest, so he lay down on the grass and went to sleep. Unfortunately, Andrew took too long of a rest. Night had fallen and he was still asleep. A bad storm blew in and the wind was so strong it blew Andrew's boat away. He woke up and saw that he was alone and there was no way for him to sail back home. Andrew felt very sad because he was far away from his home and now he had to find his own way back.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy robot. The robot was big and red. But the robot was fake. It could not talk or move. One day, Tim played with his robot in the park. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a real robot. Her robot could talk and move. Tim and Sue played together with their robots. Tim said to Sue, "I notice your robot can talk." Sue said, "Yes, my robot is not fake like yours." Tim felt sad. But Sue shared her robot with Tim. They had fun and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. He lived in a small place with his friend, a big dog named Max. They liked to play and run in the yard all day. Tom and Max were very happy. One day, Tom and Max saw a big box in their yard. They were very curious. They went to the box and tried to open it. Suddenly, they heard a scream from inside the box. They were scared but wanted to help. They opened the box and found a little bird. The bird was not hurt, but it was lost. Tom and Max were happy they could help. They became friends with the bird and played together every day. The yard became an even happier place for all of them.

Little Alex always enjoyed listening to music on the radio. One day, he turned on the radio and he heard a very smooth rhythm. He began to dance, tapping his feet to the beat. His mom saw him and asked, "What is that song that's making you dance? It's so fun!" Alex replied, "it's a story about a bird that can freeze things. It's really cool!" His mom smiled. "That's amazing, sweetie. Can you show me how you're dancing?" Alex started twirling around, moving his arms and feet to the smooth rhythm. His mom tried to mimic him, but it was hard. "Mom, why are you having so much trouble? It's easy!" Little Alex smiled and said, "Because the rhythm is kind of fast. You have to freeze for a second and then move to the beat." His mom nodded and tried to do it. This time, she got it. They both danced, moving their bodies to the beat. Alex grinned. It felt really good to be able to show his mom how to dance. Together, they both enjoyed the smooth rhythm.
