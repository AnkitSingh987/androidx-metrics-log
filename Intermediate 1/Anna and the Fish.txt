Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Fish

Anna loves to watch the otters at the zoo. They are brown and furry and swim fast in the water. One day, she sees a big otter teaching a small otter how to catch a fish. The big otter holds the fish in its paws and shows the small otter how to bite it. The small otter tries to do the same, but drops the fish. The big otter picks it up and gives it back to the small otter. "Good try, little one," the big otter says. "You will learn soon. Fish are yummy and good for you." Anna smiles and claps her hands. She wants to talk to the otters. She leans over the fence and says, "Hello, otters. You are very smart and cute. Can I be your friend?" The otters hear Anna and look at her. They see her red hair and blue eyes and hear her nice voice. They swim closer to the fence and make noises. They sound like they are saying hello back. "Hi, Anna," the big otter says. "We are happy to see you. You are very kind and pretty. You can be our friend." Anna is surprised and happy. She did not know otters can talk. She says, "Thank you, otters. You are very friendly and fun. Can I learn from you?" The otters nod and smile. They say, "Yes, Anna. You can learn from us. We can teach you many things. How to swim, how to play, how to be happy." Anna is excited and curious. She says, "That sounds great, otters. I want to learn from you. Can I come with you?" The otters say, "Yes, Anna. You can come with us. But you need to be careful. The water is deep and cold. And you need to ask your mom and dad first." Anna agrees and runs to find her mom and dad. She tells them about the otters and asks them if she can go with them. Her mom and dad are surprised and worried. They say, "Anna, are you sure? Otters are wild animals. They live in a different world. They might not be safe for you." Anna says, "Please, mom and dad. The otters are nice and smart. They want to be my friends. They want to teach me. Please let me go with them." Her mom and dad look at each other and sigh. They love Anna and want her to be happy. They say, "Okay, Anna. You can go with the otters. But only for a little while. And you have to wear a life jacket and stay close to us. And you have to listen to the otters and do what they say. Do you understand?" Anna nods and hugs her mom and dad. She says, "Thank you, mom and dad. I understand. I will be careful and have fun. I love you." She puts on a life jacket and follows the otters to the water. They welcome her and teach her how to swim, how to play, how to be happy. Anna learns a lot and has a lot of fun. She feels like she is part of the otter family. She is glad she met the otters and became their friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a pretty flower that started to bloom. The flower was so big and bright, it made Amy very happy. Amy wanted to show the flower to her best friend, Tom. She called him over and said, "Look, Tom! See the pretty flower? It bloomed just for us!" Tom looked at the flower and smiled. He loved the flower just as much as Amy did. Together, they decided to play a game near the flower. They were great players and loved to play in the empty field. They played all day, laughing and having fun. The flower watched them play, and it made the flower happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He did not like to help his mom with the dishwasher. He thought it was boring. He just wanted to play with his toys all day. One day, while Tim was playing, he found a magic ice cream. He was so happy! He thought, "I will eat this and never have to help with the dishwasher again!" But when he tried to eat the ice cream, it began to melt. It was melting so fast that it made a big mess! Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was not happy. Tim felt bad and decided to help his mom clean up. They put the melted ice cream in the dishwasher together. Tim learned that helping his mom was not so boring after all. He also learned that it is important to help others, even when it is not fun.
