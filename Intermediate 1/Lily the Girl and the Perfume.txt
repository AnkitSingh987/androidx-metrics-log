Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Perfume

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to build things. One day, she found a big box in her room. She opened the box and found many little bottles inside. The bottles had a nice smell. It was perfume! Lily had an idea. She wanted to build a perfume shop. She put the bottles on a table and made a sign that said "Lily's Perfume Shop". She asked her mom and dad to come see her shop. They came and smelled the pretty perfume. Lily's friends heard about her perfume shop. They came to see it too. They all loved the pretty smells. They said, "Lily, you made the best perfume shop ever!" Lily was so happy. She loved to build things and share them with her friends. And they all smelled very nice!

One day, a gifted cat named Tom went to the farm. Tom saw a big bag of oats. He wanted to eat them. He tried to squeeze the bag open, but it was too hard. A little mouse named Jerry came and said, "I can help you open the bag!" Tom was surprised. He did not think a little mouse could help him. Jerry squeezed the bag with all his strength. Suddenly, the bag opened and lots of oats came out. Tom and Jerry became friends and shared the oats. They were happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a car race. In it were six drivers, determined to win the race. One of the drivers, Charlie, was feeling very confident. He was driving his red car and was sure he could get the trophy first. Charlie drove very fast, going around the race track. He was taking the curves tightly, determined to beat the other drivers. But then he noticed someone was following closely. It was Lucy, a rookie driver. She was driving a yellow car and was driving tightly too. Charlie said to Lucy, "Don't you know that I'm the win driver here?". Lucy smiled and said, "Maybe you are, but I'm here to win this race too." The two drivers raced around the track faster and faster. In the end, Lucy pulled ahead and crossed the finish line first. Charlie was disappointed, but he still respected Lucy. "Well done," he said. "You drove like a champ and won the race!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a spoiled cat named Whiskers. Whiskers was a big, fat cat who loved to eat and sleep all day. One day, Mia saw Whiskers balance on a thin fence. Mia was scared that Whiskers would fall. So, she put out her finger to help Whiskers. Whiskers saw Mia's finger and walked on it to get off the fence. Mia was so happy that she could help Whiskers. She gave him a big hug and a kiss. Whiskers was happy too. From that day on, Whiskers tried not to be a spoiled cat and played more with Mia. They were best friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to a big room with many old things. There was a lot of dust on the floor and on the things. Tim's mom said, "Do not disturb the dust, it is rare and special." Tim saw a big box in the corner. He wanted to see what was inside. He went to the box and opened it. Inside the box, there was a big, friendly cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! I was sleeping in the dust. Thank you for waking me up!" Tim was very surprised that the cat could talk. He took the cat home and they became best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun. Tim learned that sometimes, it is good to disturb the dust and find new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was three years old. He had a friend named Sam. They liked to talk and play together. One day, Tim and Sam saw a big dog. The dog was naughty. The dog barked at Tim and Sam. Tim said, "Sam, we should not play with the naughty dog." Sam said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go and find a nice dog to play with." So, Tim and Sam walked away from the naughty dog. They found a nice dog. The nice dog liked to talk and play with them. They had fun all year. And they were all happy.
