Category: Intermediate
Title: A Tale of the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to observe the world around her. She was curious and always wanted to know more. One day she was walking in the park and she saw a lady. She was wearing a beautiful dress and was smiling. The little girl looked at the lady with interest and said, "Hello, what is your name?". The lady replied, "My name is Mary. What’s yours?". The little girl said, "My name is Sarah. I like to observe things around me. What do you like to do?". The lady smiled and said, "I like to help people. I like to do good things for others." The little girl was excited and said, "I like that too! Can I help you?". Mary nodded and said, "Yes, you can. Let’s go and find good things to do together!". So, Sarah and Mary went off together to find good things to do in the park that day. Sarah was so pleased to have a new friend to observe the world with.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a blue toy car that she loved to play with. One day, Lily and her blue toy car went on a big adventure in her house. Lily and her blue toy car acted like they were in a race. They went fast and slow, up and down, all around the house. Lily was happy and felt safe playing with her blue toy car. At the end of the day, Lily and her blue toy car were tired. They had so much fun acting in their race. Lily gave her blue toy car a big hug and put it away. She knew they would have more adventures together soon.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a big brother named Tom. They loved to play outside in the sun. One day, the wind began to whisper. The trees shook and the leaves danced. Lily and Tom looked up at the sky. It was getting dark and they were scared. Tom held Lily's hand and they ran home. Their mom told them a tornado was coming. They hid under a table and listened to the loud wind. Soon, the tornado was gone and they were safe.

Once upon a time there was a large, green hill. On the top of the hill there was a big pile of hay. Two little children were playing on the hill and they wanted to slide down it. The little girl said, "Let's slide down the hill on the hay, it looks so much fun!" The little boy said, "Yeah! Let's go!" The two children grabbed some of the large hay and slid down the hill together. As they slide, the children laughed and screamed with joy. When they reached the bottom of the hill, the children set up the hay again. Then they grabbed more of the large hay and slide down the hill a second time. All day long the children played and slide down the hill on the large hay. When the day was over, they both agreed that they had so much fun playing on the large hill and slide on the hay. The End.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play with her friend, Tom. They wanted to do a fun spell. But the spell was difficult. They tried and tried, but they could not do it. Lucy and Tom were sad. Tom said, "I don't like this difficult spell. I want to do an easy one." Lucy agreed. They looked for a new spell that was not hard. They found a spell to make a toy dance. Lucy and Tom tried the new spell. They said the words and the toy began to dance. They were very happy. They did not complain about the spell being difficult anymore. They played with the dancing toy all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. He lived in a small house with a nice lady. The lady loved Tom very much, but Tom did not like to do anything. He would just sleep all day and play all night. One day, the lady brought home a big bag of limes. She asked Tom to help her put the limes away, but Tom refused. He did not want to help. The lady was sad, but she put the limes away by herself. Later that night, Tom was playing with his toys when he saw a big green lime on the floor. He thought it was a ball and started to play with it. But Tom was not careful, and the lime rolled out the door. A big dog saw the lime and thought it was a toy too. The dog took the lime and ran away. Tom was sad because he lost his toy, and the lady was sad because she lost her lime. The lazy cat learned that sometimes, you have to help others to be happy.
