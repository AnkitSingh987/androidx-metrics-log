Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Park

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue went to play in the park. They liked to pretend they were animals. Tim was a cool lion, and Sue was a fast horse. They ran and played in the sun all day. But then, Tim stepped on Sue's foot by accident. Sue cried and said, "Ouch! You hurt my foot!" Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I didn't mean to step on your foot." Sue was still sad, so Tim had an idea. He pretended to be a doctor and looked at Sue's foot. "It's going to be okay," he said. They played a little more, but Sue was careful with her foot. Soon, Sue felt better, and they were friends again. They went home happy after a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went on a tour with her mom and dad. They went to a big park with lots of trees and flowers. Lily was very happy because it was a fine day. In the park, they saw a big frog sitting by a pond. Lily wanted to play with the frog, so she went close to it. She remembered a story where a girl kissed a frog, and it turned into a prince. So, Lily kissed the frog too, hoping for a prince. But, guess what? Instead of a prince, the frog turned into a big, pretty butterfly! Lily was surprised but happy. She played with the butterfly all day long. In the end, Lily, her mom, and dad went home with a new friend.

Mum was in the kitchen looking for something to feed her son. She saw broccoli in the fridge and thought it was a great idea. She said to her son, "Do you want some broccoli?" Her son replied, "No, Mum, I don't want broccoli". Mum said, "But it is light and good for you - do you want to try it?" But her son said, "No, I don't want it". Mum was getting frustrated so she said, "I'm offering it to you again, so please take it". Her son refused and kept saying no to the broccoli. So mum got angry and said, "That's it, if you don't eat the broccoli then you won't get anything to eat!" His son started crying and asked her, "Why are you being mean to me?". Mum knew she had gone too far, but it was too late. She had made her offer, and her son refused. And so, her son went to bed hungry.

Once upon a time, there was a dog and a cat. They both liked to play. One day, they found a big bone. The dog said, "I want the bone!" The cat said, "No, I want the bone!" The dog was compassionate and said, "Okay, let's play with the bone together." So, they played with the bone. They took turns and shared the bone. They were very happy. But then, a big bird came and took the bone away. The dog and the cat were sad. They tried to get the bone back, but the bird was too high in the sky. They could not play with the bone anymore. The dog and the cat learned that sometimes, things don't go the way we want them to.

One day, a little bird named Tim found a fine, shiny thing on the ground. He picked it up in his beak and flew up to his nest in a big tree. Tim looked at the shiny thing and thought it was very pretty. He decided to whistle a happy song because he was so happy to find it. Tim wanted to show the shiny thing to his friend, Sam the squirrel. He took the shiny thing in his beak and flew over to Sam's tree. Sam was jumping from branch to branch near the ceiling of the tree. Tim whistled to get Sam's attention. Sam came down and looked at the shiny thing. He thought it was very pretty too. Later, Tim and Sam were playing with the shiny thing near the tree. They were throwing it up in the air and catching it. Suddenly, the shiny thing flew up high and got stuck in the tree's ceiling. Tim and Sam were very sad. They tried to get it down, but they couldn't reach it. Then, they remembered the happy song Tim whistled earlier. They both started to whistle the happy song together. As they whistled, the wind blew the shiny thing down from the ceiling of the tree. Tim and Sam were so happy to have their shiny thing back, and they played with it all day long.
