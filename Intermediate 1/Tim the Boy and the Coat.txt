Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, it was very cold. Tim put on his coat and went outside to play with his friends. "Let's play catch," said Tim. His friends agreed, but after a while, they found the game boring. "I want to quit," said Tim's friend, Sam. "Me too," said another friend, Jill. They all decided to quit playing catch and find a new game to play. Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's build a snowman!" His friends liked the idea. They all worked together to make a big snowman. They used a carrot for the nose and rocks for the eyes. When they finished, they were very happy. They forgot all about the cold and the boring game of catch.

Once upon a time, there was a big building. It was very high. A little girl named Sue lived in the building with her mom and dad. One day, Sue wanted to help her mom. Her mom was pouring juice into a cup. Sue said, "Mom, can I help you pour the juice?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue, you can help me." Sue took the juice and poured it into the cup. It was fun! Sue felt happy because she helped her mom. She learned how to pour juice and was proud of herself.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They were very happy. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The dog wanted to play catch. They were having a lot of fun together. The cat paused and said, "I have some news. My mom told me there is a big box of toys available at the store." The dog got very excited and said, "Let's go get the toys!" They ran to the store to find the toys. But when they got there, the store was closed. The cat and the dog were very sad. They did not get the toys they wanted. The cat and the dog went back to the park with no toys and had a bad day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Millie. She loved all things colorful and sparkly. One day, she found a box full of very special things. She opened the box with excitement and what she saw inside made her even more excited. Inside the box were lots of spell cards. Millie was amazed by how beautiful and original each card was. Millie loved playing with all of the cards, but wasn't sure what to do with them. Then her mama came into the room. Mama: "Oh my, what do we have here?" Mille: "I found it, mama! It's so pretty!" Mama: "That is so special, Millie. But you know what? Instead of playing all the time, you should stay with the cards and learn the spells. They will be very helpful someday." Millie nodded and promised that she would stay and learn all the spells. And ever since that day, Millie stayed with the cards and learned all the spells. The moral of the story is that even magical things can be better when you take the time to stay and learn something.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern house. In the house, lived a girl named Lily and her mom. Lily loved to play with her toys and run around the house. One day, Lily found a staff in her room. It was long and shiny. She was so happy and wanted to show her mom. She ran to the kitchen and said, "Mom, come look at this!" Her mom came and saw the staff. She smiled and said, "Wow, that's a nice staff, Lily!" Lily played with the staff all day. She pretended to be a queen, and the staff was her magic wand. She waved it around and said, "I wish for a big, yummy cake!" Just then, her mom came in with a cake. They laughed and ate the cake together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a small girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. She saw a lot of shells on the sand. She wanted to find the best shell ever. Lily looked and looked. Then, she found a big, soft shell. She was very happy. Lily showed the soft shell to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, that is a very special shell. Let's take it home and put it in your room." Lily felt very proud and carried the shell home with care. At home, Lily put the shell on her table. The soft shell started to shake and move! Lily was scared, but she watched. A little crab came out of the shell! The crab said, "Thank you, Lily, for taking me to your home. But I need to go back to the beach, please." Lily took the crab back to the beach. She learned that sometimes we need to let things go, even if we love them.
