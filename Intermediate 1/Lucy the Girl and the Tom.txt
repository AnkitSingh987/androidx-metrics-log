Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a kind girl named Lucy. Lucy loved to serve food to her friends. She had a naughty friend named Tom. Tom always did bad things and laughed. One day, Lucy made a big cake for all her friends. She wanted to serve the cake to make them happy. But naughty Tom saw the cake and wanted to play a trick on Lucy. He thought it would be funny. Tom put mud on the cake when Lucy was not looking. When Lucy served the cake to her friends, they saw the mud and cried. They did not want to eat the dirty cake. Lucy was sad, and Tom felt bad for his naughty trick. In the end, no one was happy, and the cake was wasted.

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She was very healthy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she decided to play hide and seek with her friends. The princess ran to find a good place to hide. She found a big tree with many leaves. She hid behind the tree and waited for her friends to find her. She was very good at hiding. Her friends looked and looked, but they could not find the princess. They called her name, and she came out from behind the tree. They all laughed and played more hide and seek. The princess and her friends had a lot of fun, and they played until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock in a yard. The rock loved to play with the kids who lived in the house near the yard. The kids were named Tim and Sue. One day, Tim and Sue were playing in the yard. Tim wanted to play with the smooth rock, but Sue wanted to play with her ball. They could not agree on what to play with. "I want to play with the rock," said Tim. "No, I want to play with the ball," said Sue. They both disagreed and did not know what to do. Then, the smooth rock had an idea. It rolled closer to the ball and said, "Why don't you both play with me and the ball at the same time?" Tim and Sue liked the idea and started to play with both the rock and the ball. From that day on, Tim and Sue always played with the smooth rock and the ball in the yard. They learned that playing together was more fun than disagreeing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma had a big box of clothes to play dress up. One day, she wanted to choose a special dress to wear for her tea party with her toys. Emma looked in the box and found a beautiful velvet dress. It was soft and comfortable. She put it on and felt very happy. Her toys liked the dress too. They clapped and smiled at her. Emma and her toys had a fun tea party. They drank pretend tea and ate pretend cake. Emma felt very special in her velvet dress. She knew she made a good choice. At the end of the day, she put her dress back in the box and gave it a big hug.

Lia and Ben are young twins who like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big basket full of olives under a tree. They are curious and want to taste them. "Look, Ben, olives!" Lia says, picking up a green one. "Do you like them?" "I don't know, Lia. Let's try," Ben says, picking up a black one. They both bite into their olives and make a funny face. "Yuck, they are sour and bitter!" Lia says, spitting out her olive. "I don't like them at all!" "Me neither, Lia. They are bad!" Ben says, spitting out his olive too. They throw the rest of the olives back into the basket and run away. The next day, they see their mom in the kitchen with a big jar of olives. She is smiling and humming a song. "Mom, what are you doing with the olives?" Lia asks, pointing at the jar. "I'm making olive oil, Lia. Olive oil is good for cooking and salad and bread. It comes from the olives," mom says, showing them how she presses the olives with a machine. "But mom, the olives are sour and bitter. We don't like them," Ben says, making a face. "That's because they are raw, Ben. They need to be washed and salted and soaked in water and vinegar to make them tasty. Then they can be eaten or made into oil," mom says, giving them each a slice of bread with olive oil and cheese. Lia and Ben try the bread and smile. "Mmm, this is good, mom. The olive oil is yummy and smooth," Lia says, taking another bite. "Yes, mom, this is much better than the olives. The olive oil is nice and warm," Ben says, licking his lips. Mom hugs them and says, "I'm glad you like it, my loves. Olive oil is a gift from nature. It comes from the olives that grow on the trees in the garden. We should be thankful for them." Lia and Ben nod and say, "Thank you, mom. Thank you, olives. Thank you, nature." They lay their heads on mom's shoulder and enjoy their bread.
