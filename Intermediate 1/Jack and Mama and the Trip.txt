Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Mama and the Trip

One morning, Jack and Mama got ready for a trip. Mama took out a big suitcase and began to unpack it. She carefully laid out lots of different things that she thought they would need. Jack watched as his Mama packed their suitcase full of treats. He was busy making sure she packed enough of his favorite things. When they were almost finished, Mama warned Jack not to do anything foolish while they were away on their trip. Jack promised that he would not be naughty, and hugged his Mama goodbye. For the next few days, Jack and his Mama had lots of fun exploring and doing all sorts of things. Each night, they would lay out all of their things from the suitcase and tell stories about the day. When it was finally time to go home, Jack and Mama said goodbye to all the new friends they had made on their trip. They carefully packed their suitcase with their memories and stories from their journey. Mama reminded Jack to remember his promise - to not do anything foolish on their trip. Jack smiled and promised Mama that he would keep that promise always.

One day, a little dog named Max was playing outside. It was a sunny day with big white clouds. Max liked to run and jump in the grass. He was a happy dog. While Max was playing, he saw a big flash of lightning in the sky. It scared him and made him feel sad. Max ran to his friend, a little girl named Lily. "Lily, I saw lightning and it made me sad," Max said. Lily hugged Max and told him, "Don't be sad, Max. Lightning is just light in the sky. It can't hurt you." Just then, Max felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to hold it in, but he couldn't. Max let out a big sneeze. "Achoo!" Lily laughed and said, "That was a funny sneeze, Max!" Max started to feel better. They played together until the sky was dark and the stars came out. Max was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a humble lion. The lion was nice to all his friends. He liked to play with them every day. One day, while the lion was playing with his friends, he saw a big tree. The lion wanted to climb the tree. He climbed up, up, up. He was so high! All his friends looked up at him and clapped. But then, the lion started to fall. Down, down, down he went. His friends were scared. But the lion was okay! He got back up and said, "That was fun!" All his friends were happy and they played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a little red house. In the house, there was a door. The door was old and wet. One day, a big wind came and blew the wet door down. A little girl named Sue lived in the house. She saw the door on the ground and felt sad. Sue's mom said, "Don't worry, we will get a new door." Sue smiled and went to play with her toys. Later, Sue smelled something burning. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, I smell burn!" Her mom saw smoke coming from the kitchen. She quickly put out the fire. Sue's mom was happy that the wet door was not there, because the fire did not spread. They got a new door and lived happily ever after.

Lily and Tom like to play with bricks. They have many bricks of different colors and shapes. They build houses and towers and cars with their bricks. One day, they go to the park with their mom. They bring some bricks with them. They find a big sandbox and start to make a castle with their bricks. They are very happy. But then, a big dog comes to the sandbox. The dog is curious and friendly. He wants to play with Lily and Tom. He runs around and jumps on their castle. He knocks down their bricks and makes a big mess. Lily and Tom are sad and angry. They yell at the dog. They try to get their bricks back. But some bricks are lost in the sand. They cannot find them. They have fewer bricks than before. Mom sees what happens. She comes to help Lily and Tom. She tells them not to be mad at the dog. He did not know he was ruining their castle. He just wanted to have fun. She helps them look for their lost bricks. She finds some, but not all. She tells Lily and Tom that they can still play with their bricks. They can make something different with them. Maybe a boat or a train or a rocket. She tells them that different can be good. It can be fun and creative. Lily and Tom think about it. They decide to try something different. They smile and hug mom. They say thank you to her. They start to build a new thing with their bricks. They are happy again.
