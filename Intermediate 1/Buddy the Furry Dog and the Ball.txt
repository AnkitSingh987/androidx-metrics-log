Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Furry Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Buddy. Buddy was young and loved to play with his friends. One day, Buddy and his friends went to the park to play. While they were playing, Buddy saw a big red ball. He ran to the ball and said, "Let's play with the ball!" His friends agreed, and they all took turns playing with the ball. After playing, Buddy and his friends were very hungry. They found some yummy food and started to eat. Buddy said, "This food tastes so good!" They all ate and had fun until it was time to go home. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a noisy bird named Tim wanted to travel far away. He told his friend, Sam the squirrel, "I want to see new things and meet new friends." Sam said, "I know a place with a big onion. You can travel there." So, Tim started his journey to find the big onion. He flew and flew until he found it. When he saw the onion, he was very happy. "Wow, it's so big!" Tim said. He played near the onion and met new friends like ants and beetles. But then, something unexpected happened. The big onion started to roll away! Tim and his new friends tried to stop it, but it was too heavy. They all worked together and finally stopped the onion. Tim learned that when friends work together, they can do anything. He thanked his new friends and flew back home to tell Sam the squirrel all about his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a clean park. In the park, there were many kids playing. They were happy. One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big ice cream truck. Tim loved ice cream. He wanted to taste it. Tim bought the ice cream and it was yummy. He shared it with his friends. They all loved the taste. They played more and went home. The end.

Once upon a time, Mummy Bear and Baby Bear went for a walk in the woods. They went past a big hill and saw a lot of steam rising from the top of the hill. Mummy Bear said, "Look Baby Bear, isn't that steam smart?" Baby Bear nodded and said, "Let's go and have a closer look!" So they went up the hill and saw a beautiful lake filled with steam. Baby Bear was mesmerized by the sight and started running around the lake in delight. Mummy Bear smiled and said, "Look how smart the steam is, Baby Bear!" Baby Bear smiled and said, "Yes, Mummy Bear, it is so smart!" Then they both walked away happily, with Baby Bear still looking back at the lake with the smart steam!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big hat that he loved to wear. His hat was so big, it did not fit on his head. But Tim did not mind. He wore his hat all the time. One day, Tim went to the park with his hat. He saw a small bird that was very sad. The bird was wet from the rain and did not have a place to hide. Tim had an idea. He took off his big hat and put it on the ground. The little bird went under the hat and was very happy. Tim knew his big hat would help someone someday. From that day on, Tim and the little bird were best friends. They played together in the park every day. The bird was glad that Tim's hat did not fit on his head. Tim did not mind, because he was happy to have a new friend. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to measure things. She measured her toys, her bed, and even her dog. One day, she found a map in her room. Mia showed the map to her mom. "Mom, what is this?" she asked. Her mom looked at the map and said, "This is a treasure map, Mia! It will lead you to a special surprise!" Mia was excited. She took her ruler and measured the map. She followed the map to a big tree in her yard. Under the tree, she found a box. Mia opened the box and saw a big cake inside. But the cake was spoiled! It was green and smelled bad. Mia was sad. She went back to her mom and told her about the spoiled cake. Her mom smiled and said, "I put that cake there a long time ago as a surprise. I forgot about it! Let's make a new cake together." So Mia and her mom baked a new cake. They measured the ingredients and made the best cake ever. Mia learned that sometimes, the best surprises are the ones you make yourself.
