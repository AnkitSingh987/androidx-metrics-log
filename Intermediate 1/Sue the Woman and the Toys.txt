Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Woman and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a woman named Sue. Sue liked to organize her toys. One day, she found a hidden box under her bed. She was very happy. Sue opened the box and saw many toys inside. She said, "Wow, I can organize these toys too!" She took the toys out of the box and put them in a line. Sue was very good at organizing. Sue's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the toys and said, "Sue, you are so good at organizing! Can you help me organize my toys too?" Sue said, "Yes, I can help you!" They went to Tim's house and organized his toys together. They had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a wise cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Kitty and Lily went outside to play. They saw a pretty blouse hanging on a clothesline. "Look, Kitty, a pretty blouse!" said Lily. Kitty nodded and smiled. But as they went closer to the blouse, Kitty stumbled on a rock and fell down. Lily helped Kitty up, and they both laughed. Just then, the wind blew the blouse off the clothesline. It flew through the air and landed on Kitty's head! Kitty and Lily laughed even more. They never thought their day would be so funny. And from that day on, they always remembered their adventure with the flying blouse.

One day, a loyal dog named Max went for a wander near the big water. He saw a big ship on the water. Max liked to watch the ship go by. A little bird named Lily flew down and said, "Max, be careful near the water. The big ship can be dangerous." Max barked and said, "Thank you, Lily. I will be careful." As Max watched the ship, something unexpected happened. The big ship came too close to Max. Max tried to run away, but it was too late. The ship hit the land and Max got wet. Max was scared and could not find his way back home.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play catch with a ball. They had a big yard with a fence and a tree. One day, they saw a new boy next door. He had a shiny red bike and a big bowl of candy. Tom and Sue wanted to say hello, but they felt envious of his bike and candy. "Let's play catch with our ball," Tom said. "Maybe he will see us and want to join us." "OK," Sue said. "But I want to catch the ball more than you." They threw the ball back and forth, but they did not have fun. They tried to catch the ball more than the other, and they did not share or say nice words. The ball flew higher and higher, until it went over the fence and landed in the new boy's yard. "Oh no!" Tom and Sue cried. "Our ball is gone!" They ran to the fence and looked over. They saw the new boy pick up their ball and smile. He had a friendly face and a kind voice. "Hi, I'm Ben," he said. "Do you want to play with me? I have a bike and a bowl of candy, but I don't have any friends here. You can have some of my candy if you want." Tom and Sue felt ashamed. They had been envious and selfish, and they had lost their ball. They realized that Ben was not mean or greedy, but lonely and generous. They decided to be nice and friendly, too. "Hi, Ben," Tom said. "We're sorry we threw our ball in your yard. We're Tom and Sue, and we want to be your friends. Can we play with you?" "Sure," Ben said. "Come on over. We can share my bike and my candy, and we can play catch with your ball." Tom and Sue climbed over the fence and joined Ben. They played with his bike and his candy, and they played catch with their ball. They had fun and laughed and said nice words. They learned that being envious and selfish is not good, but being friendly and generous is better. They became good friends with Ben, and they never felt envious again.
