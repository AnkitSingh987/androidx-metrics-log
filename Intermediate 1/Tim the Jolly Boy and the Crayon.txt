Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Jolly Boy and the Crayon

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. He loved to draw with his crayons. Tim had a big box of crayons with many colors. His favorite color was red. One day, Tim could not find his red crayon. He looked everywhere. He looked under his bed and in his toy box, but it was not there. Tim started to cry. His mommy heard him cry and came to help. She found the red crayon behind the couch. Tim was so happy! He hugged his mommy and said, "Thank you!" Then, Tim drew a big red heart for his mommy with his crayon. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time there were two best friends, Jake and Molly. They were playing outside in the icy cold when Jake had an idea. He said to Molly, "Let's grab the measure and I will show you a fun game". Molly thought it was a good idea, so she followed Jake to his garden where he had a really big measure. Jake grabbed the measure and started to draw a big square in the snow with it. Molly asked Jake, "What are we going to do now?" "Let's see who can jump the highest!" said Jake. Molly giggled with anticipation and replied, "Okay, let's do it!" They both grabbed the icy measure, took a deep breath and jumped as high as they could. Molly jumped the highest, and they both laughed with joy!

Mick was an old man. He lived in a small house with a desk in the corner. Every morning, Mick went to the desk and bowed his head. He said a prayer before starting his day. He was getting old, so he couldn't do as much as he used to. But he still made sure to bow his head and show respect before starting his day. One day Mick's grandkids came to visit. They asked him why he bowed his head every morning. Mick told them that it was important to show respect before starting something. He explained that even though he was old, he still showed respect by bowing his head. His grandkids were very impressed and asked if they could bow their heads too. Mick was so proud. He smiled as he watched the grandkids bow their heads at his old desk. He knew that he was teaching them an important lesson.

Tom and his wife Lily were going to the park with their son Ben. Ben liked to play on the swings and the slide, but he did not like to share with other kids. When they got to the park, Ben ran to the swings and said, "Mine, mine, mine!" Another kid, Sam, was also waiting for the swings. He said, "No, no, no! I want a turn too!" Ben and Sam started to yell at each other. They both wanted the same swing. Tom and Lily heard the yelling and went to see what was wrong. They saw Ben and Sam fighting over the swing. Tom said to Ben, "Ben, you have to share. Sharing is nice. You can take turns with Sam." Lily said to Sam, "Sam, you have to wait. Waiting is polite. You can have the swing after Ben." But Ben and Sam did not listen. They kept yelling and pulling the swing. Tom and Lily were not happy. They said, "Stop, stop, stop! Enough, enough, enough!" They took Ben and Sam away from the swing. They made them sit on a bench and calm down. Ben and Sam were sad and angry. They did not want to sit on the bench. They wanted to play on the swing. But they also did not want to yell and fight. They looked at each other and said, "Sorry, sorry, sorry." They gave each other a hug and said, "Friends, friends, friends." Tom and Lily smiled and said, "Good, good, good." They let Ben and Sam go back to the swing. They said, "Now, you can share and take turns. Have fun, have fun, have fun." Ben and Sam nodded and said, "OK, OK, OK." They played on the swing and had fun. They did not yell anymore. They were calm and happy.
