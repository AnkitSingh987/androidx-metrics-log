Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Laser

Once upon a time, in a unique land, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his toy laser. One day, while playing, his laser split in two! Tom was sad and cried, "Oh no! My laser is broken!" His friend, Sam, heard him and came over. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tom. We can fix it together!" Tom and Sam tried to put the two pieces back together. They pushed and pulled until the laser was whole again. Tom was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sam! You are a great friend!" From that day on, Tom and Sam played with the laser together. They made sure to be careful so it wouldn't split again. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tim. Tim loved to fly his big plane in the open sky. One day, while flying, he saw a little bird. "Hello, bird!" said Tim. "I am a pilot. I fly in the open sky." "Hello, Tim!" said the bird. "I am a bird. I fly too." Tim and the bird became good friends. They flew together in the open sky. They saw many pretty things. One day, they saw a big tree with yummy fruits. The tree did not want to give the fruits. "Please, tree, yield your fruits," said Tim. "My friend, the bird, is hungry." "Okay," said the tree. "I will yield my fruits." Tim and the bird ate the fruits. They were very happy. They thanked the tree and continued to fly in the open sky. They were best friends forever.

One day, a calm cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a small dog named Max. Max was trying to fix his little house. He needed a nail to make it strong. Tom asked, "Max, do you need help?" Max said, "Yes, I need a nail. Can you lend me one?" Tom went to his home and found a nail. He lent the nail to Max. Max was very happy. They both fixed the little house together. Now, Max's house was strong and safe. They became good friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a cute musician. He liked to play music for his friends in the park. One day, he saw something big and dark in the trees. He felt a little scare. The next day, the cute musician went back to the park. He played music for his friends again. The big and dark thing was still in the trees. The musician felt more scare, but he kept playing music. On the third day, the cute musician played music in the park again. The big and dark thing came out of the trees. It was a big, friendly dog! The musician was not scare anymore. The dog liked the music, and they became friends. The musician and the dog played music and had fun in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little girl who loved to go outside and play. One day the girl was outside when she noticed the wind picking up. It was blowing stronger and stronger, until it finally blew her hat off her head. She tried to catch it, but it flew away so fast - until it got stuck in a branch of a tall tree. The girl was sad and started to cry. Just then, a friendly voice said, "Don't cry. I can help you get it. Spring up!." "What do you mean?" wondered the girl. "Spring up and grab it," said the voice. The girl was scared, but she knew she had to try. So she did the strangest thing. She put her hands up and sprung up in the air, higher and higher and higher. The wind was pushing her and before she knew it, she was at the very top of the tree. When she finally grabbed her hat, the girl looked down and a beautiful bird was staring at her. The bird said, "Your hat is so pretty - it looks like a crown on the ugly wind." The girl smiled and thanked the bird and then sprang back down. From that day forward, the girl always noticed the small, beautiful things the wind carried.
