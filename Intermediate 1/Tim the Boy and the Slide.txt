Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Slide

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play on the swings and slide down the big slide. His mom always brought a bottle of juice for him to drink when he got thirsty. While Tim was playing, he saw a big, harsh-looking dog. The dog came closer and closer to Tim. Tim was scared that the dog might be mean. He ran to his mom and told her about the dog. His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, I will make sure the dog won't scare you." To Tim's surprise, the dog walked up to them and wagged its tail. The dog was not mean at all! It just wanted to play with Tim. The dog's owner came over and said, "This is my dog, Fluffy. She loves to play with kids." Tim was so happy that the dog was nice. He played with Fluffy and shared some of his juice with her. Tim learned that sometimes things that seem scary can turn out to be fun and friendly.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big red rope. He loved to play with his rope every day. Tim was always ready for fun. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was sad and scared. Tim wanted to help the cat. He tried to think of a way to save the cat. Then, he had an idea. He could use his rope to help the cat get down. Tim prayed that his plan would work. Tim threw his rope up to the cat. The cat held onto the rope. Tim thought he would pull the cat down, but something unexpected happened. The cat pulled Tim up into the tree! They both laughed and played in the tree together. The cat was happy, and Tim was happy too.

Once upon a time, there were two boys - Marco and Max. Marco and Max were best friends. One day, they decided to make a goal. "Let's run around the block three times!" said Marco. Max thought it was a good idea and they agreed. Marco and Max put on their shoes and started running. When they finished running, they were both so happy! Max said "Now we know we can reach our goal!" Marco smiled and said "Yes! Let's do it again." They both ran around the block several more times and when they had finished, they were both so happy. "We did it!" shouted Marco. "We reached our goal!" Max smiled and said, "Yes, and now we know we can do anything!" And from that day forward, Marco and Max always worked together to reach their goals, and they were always happy.

Sara was a girl who liked to draw. She had many crayons and papers to make pictures. She drew flowers, animals, houses and people. She was happy when she drew. One day, Sara went to play with her friend Lily. Lily also liked to draw, but she had fewer crayons and papers. Sara wanted to share her things with Lily, so she brought some of them to Lily's house. But Lily was not nice. She took Sara's crayons and papers and did not give them back. She said they were hers now. She drew on Sara's pictures and made them ugly. She laughed at Sara and said her pictures were bad. Sara was very sad and angry. She wanted her things back. She tried to take them from Lily, but Lily was stronger. She pushed Sara away and tore her pictures. She threw them on the floor and stepped on them. Sara cried. She felt a bitter taste in her mouth. She did not want to play with Lily anymore. She ran out of Lily's house and went back to her own. She hugged her mom and told her what happened. Her mom was sorry and gave her a kiss. She said Lily was not a good friend. She said Sara could draw more pictures with her new crayons and papers. She said Sara was a good girl who made beautiful pictures. Sara smiled. She felt better. She still liked to draw.
