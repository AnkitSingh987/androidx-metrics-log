Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Man and the Box

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a bald man. He was very sad because he had no hair. He wanted to find a way to have hair again. So, he went on a long walk to find something that could help him. One day, he met a big, kind bear. The bear told him about a magic box that could reveal what he wanted. The bald man was very happy and asked the bear to help him find the magic box. They walked and walked until they found the box under a big tree. The bald man was very excited. He opened the magic box to reveal what was inside. But, when he opened the box, a big wind came out and took away everything. The bear, the tree, and even the bald man were gone. The magic box was not nice, and it gave a bad ending to the story.

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. On this mountain, there was a little shop. A nice, honest man named Tom had the shop. Tom would sell toys and yummy food to people who came to see the mountain. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the shop. She wanted to buy a toy. Sue asked Tom, "Can I have that toy, please?" Tom said, "Yes, but it will cost you two coins." Sue looked sad. She only had one coin. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew through the shop. It knocked down a box of toys. One toy rolled to Sue's feet. It was the same toy she wanted! Tom smiled and said, "You can have that toy for one coin, Sue." Sue was very happy and thanked Tom. She left the shop with her new toy, and they both had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Jane. She loved to play in the bath. One day her mom said, "let's go to the beach!" Jane was so excited. The beach was so big and beautiful. At the beach, Jane saw the sand and the water. Jane put her feet in the water, it was a little cold but she liked it. Then her mom said, "Let's have a bath". They both stayed in the water until their feet got wet. Jane's mom added something to the water, Jane asked, "What is it?" And her mom said, "It's salt, it's yummy". Jane tasted the water and she smiled. Together Jane and her mom played and swam for a long time. When it was time to leave, Jane was sad. But she promised to go to the beach again soon.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big statue. The statue was of a strong and fit man. All the kids liked to play near the statue because it was fun. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with his friends near the statue. But his mom said, "Tim, you must delay your playtime. First, you need to help me clean the house." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. After Tim helped his mom clean the house, he went to play with his friends near the statue. His friends were happy to see him and said, "Thank you for coming, Tim! We waited for you." They all played together and had a great time. The moral of the story is that it's important to help others before having fun, and good things come to those who wait.

One day, a young bird wanted to soar in the sky like his mother. He flapped his wings and jumped from the nest, but he fell to the ground. He was sad and scared. "Are you okay, my little one?" his mother asked. She flew down and hugged him with her feathers. "I want to soar like you, mama, but I can't," the young bird said. "You will, my little one, you will. You just need to practice and be brave. Come, let me show you how to soar," his mother said. She lifted him up with her beak and carried him to a high branch. "Look, my little one, see the sky, see the clouds, see the sun. This is where we belong, this is where we soar. Feel the wind, feel the freedom, feel the joy. Now, flap your wings and follow me," his mother said. The young bird flapped his wings and felt a surge of power. He soared after his mother, higher and higher, faster and faster. He was happy and proud. He soared in the sky like his mother. After a while, they returned to the nest, where his father and siblings were waiting for them. They had a tasty meal of worms and berries, and they told stories of their adventures. The young bird was the happiest bird in the world. He soared in the sky like his mother.
