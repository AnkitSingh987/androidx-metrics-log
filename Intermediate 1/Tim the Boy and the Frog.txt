Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Frog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was a happy youth who loved to play outside. His favorite thing to do was to jump in big, dry piles of leaves during the fall. He would laugh and giggle as the leaves crunched under his feet. One sunny day, Tim was playing in a big, dry pile of leaves when something unexpected happened. He found a small, shiny box buried in the leaves. Tim hoped the box would have something fun inside. He opened the box and saw a tiny, green frog. The tiny, green frog looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for finding me, Tim! I was lost and now I am found." Tim was very surprised that the frog could talk. The frog told Tim that he was a magic frog and could grant him one wish. Tim thought hard and decided to wish for a friend to play with. With a blink of the frog's eyes, a new friend appeared. Tim and his new friend played in the big, dry pile of leaves all day. They laughed and giggled together, and Tim knew that he had made the best wish. From that day on, Tim and his new friend played together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big, red ball. One day, Sue went to the park with her ball. She loved to play with it. At the park, Sue saw a big, terrible dog. The dog wanted to play with Sue's ball too. Sue was scared, so she said, "No, you can't play with my ball!" Sue wanted to keep her ball safe. The dog was sad and walked away. Later, Sue saw the terrible dog again. This time, the dog was stuck in a hole. Sue felt bad for the dog. She had an idea. Sue threw her ball into the hole. The dog jumped and got out of the hole. The dog was happy and gave the ball back to Sue. They became friends and played together. Sue learned that even if someone looks terrible, they can still be a good friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a snake on the ground. The snake was long and tough. Tim was a little scared, but he wanted to be brave. Tim said to the snake, "You can't scare me!" Then, he kicked the snake. The snake did not like that. It looked at Tim and said, "Why did you kick me?" Tim was very surprised. He did not know that snakes could talk. The snake told him, "I am a magic snake. Because you kicked me, I will give you a wish." Tim thought and thought. He wished for a big cake. The snake made a big cake appear. Tim and the snake ate the cake together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to go to the beach. The beach had water and sand. Max liked to play and run on the sand. He thought the beach was a fun place. One day, Max saw a crazy bird at the beach. The bird was flying and making loud sounds. Max did not like the crazy bird. He wanted to protect his beach from the bird. So, Max started to bark at the bird. Max barked and barked until the crazy bird flew away. Max was happy that he protected his beach. He continued to play and run on the sand. Now, Max felt safe at the beach. He knew that he could protect it from any crazy birds.

Once upon a time there were two cats, Tom and Jerry. Tom was sweet and caring, while Jerry was spoiled and mischievous. One day Tom said to Jerry, "Hey, let's go flying together." But Jerry refused. He said, "Why should I fly? It doesn't sound fun." Tom asked, "Haven't you ever done it before?" Jerry answered, "Nope, never. It's too scary." So Tom said, "I think you should give it a try. I bet you would like it." He continued, "I highly recommend it. It's really fun!" Jerry thought about this for a moment and reluctantly agreed. The next day he got his wings and went on his first flight. He couldn't believe how amazing it was. From then on he enjoyed flying with Tom every weekend. As Tom predicted, Jerry soon became a flying expert and even started to recommend it to his friends.
