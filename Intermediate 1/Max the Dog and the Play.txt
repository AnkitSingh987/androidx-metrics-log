Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Play

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play outside during the day. One cold night, Max went outside to play, but it was very dark. Max saw a big cat and wanted to play with it. The big cat did not want to play with Max. The big cat tried to ignore Max, but Max kept trying to play. Finally, the big cat played with Max. They ran and jumped until they were tired. Then, Max and the big cat went home to sleep. Max was happy he made a new friend on the cold night.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. He was a very lucky boy and he loved to go outside and play. One day, he went on an adventure with his mom and dad. They went to the park. As they walked into the park, Jack's mom and dad mentioned cocoa. Jack was very excited and asked for some. His parents said, "maybe later". Jack was disappointed, but his parents told him to wait until it was time for the surprise. Jack was so excited that he couldn't contain himself. Everywhere he looked, he thought he saw chocolate. He couldn't wait for the surprise! They walked around the park and Jack was so excited. Finally, Jack and his parents reached a big tree and his dad said, "There's the surprise!" Jack looked and saw a big bucket of cocoa beans! Jack was so happy and he thanked his lucky stars that he had such nice parents.

Once upon a time, there was a unique cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to dream about a big world full of fun and yummy treats. Every day, Fluffy would take a nap and dream of a new adventure. One day, Fluffy met a new friend, a small bird named Chirpy. Chirpy asked Fluffy, "What do you like to do in your life?" Fluffy said, "I love to dream! I dream of a big world with lots of fun and treats!" Chirpy thought that was a great idea and wanted to dream too. So, Fluffy and Chirpy took a nap under a big tree. They both dreamed of a fun world where they could play and eat treats together. When they woke up, they were happy and had a great time in their dreams. Fluffy and Chirpy became best friends and shared many more dreams together.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big slide that looked fun. Tom wanted to try it, but Lily was scared. "Come on, Lily, you can do it!" Tom encouraged her. "It's not so high, and I will catch you at the end." Lily looked at the slide and felt nervous. She did not like high things. But she did not want to disappoint Tom, who was her best friend. She nodded and climbed up the ladder. "Ready, set, go!" Tom shouted, and pushed Lily down the slide. Lily screamed and closed her eyes. She felt the wind in her hair and the slide under her bottom. But something went wrong. Lily was too clumsy and did not hold on to the slide. She flew off the side and landed on the ground with a thud. She hurt her arm and her leg and started to cry. Tom ran to her and saw the blood on her knee. He felt sorry and scared. He did not mean to hurt Lily. He wanted to have fun with her. "I'm sorry, Lily, I'm sorry!" he said. "Are you okay? Do you need help?" Lily did not answer. She was too sad and in pain. She wished Tom did not push her. She wished she did not go on the slide. She wished she was at home with her mom. A man who worked in the park heard the noise and came to see what happened. He saw Lily on the ground and Tom next to her. He called an ambulance and told them the news. "Lily had an accident on the slide," he said. "She needs to go to the hospital. She might have broken her arm or her leg." Tom heard the news and felt worse. He did not want Lily to go to the hospital. He did not want Lily to be mad at him. He did not want to lose his friend. He hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily, I'm sorry. Please don't hate me. Please be my friend." But Lily did not hug him back. She did not say anything. She just looked at him with tears in her eyes. She did not know if she could ever forgive him. She did not know if they could ever be friends again.
