Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fire

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim saw a big, red fire truck with the number 7 on it. He felt jealous because he wanted a fire truck too. Tim asked his mom for a fire truck just like the one he saw. His mom said, "Okay, let's go to the store and find one." At the store, they found the same big, red fire truck with the number 7. Tim was very happy and played with his new fire truck all day. One day, while playing with his fire truck, Tim saw a small fire in the kitchen. He pretended that his fire truck could burn out the fire. His mom saw the fire too and quickly put it out. She was proud of Tim for being brave and trying to help. Tim learned that it was good to have a fire truck, but it was more important to be brave and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a little, adorable cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with yarn. One day, she found a big ball of red yarn under the bed. She was very happy. Kitty played with the yarn all day. She rolled it, jumped on it, and even slept on it. She showed the yarn to her friend, Doggy. Doggy said, "Wow! That's a big yarn. Can I play with it too?" Kitty said, "Yes, we can play together!" Kitty and Doggy played with the yarn and had lots of fun. They became very good friends and learned to accept each other. They promised to share all their toys and play together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He lived in an empty house. The soldier was very lonely. He wanted to find a friend to play with. One day, the soldier went for a walk. He saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a dance party. The soldier was very happy. He wanted to dance too. The soldier joined the dance party. He danced and danced. All the people at the party liked the soldier. They became friends. The soldier was not lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a van. It drove through the streets of the unknown. Each day it paid for beauty, for groceries, and for joy. One day, the van arrived at a surprise. A three year old girl was waiting outside. She smiled as the van drove up and asked to pay. The van said yes. It gave the girl a ride, without asking for any money. The girl was so happy, her heart almost burst with delight. They drove through the streets of the unknown, far away past the girl’s home. The van then stopped, and the girl got out. The van continued to drive, but this time the driver paid with a smile. The girl vanished into the unknown with only one thing left behind - a thank you. The van paid for joy with nothing in return.

One day, a little rabbit was playing in the woods. He was very happy. The rabbit saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he climbed, he became frightened. The rabbit was too high and didn't know how to get down. A kind bird saw the frightened rabbit and asked, "Why are you scared, little rabbit?" The rabbit said, "I am too high up, and I don't know how to get down. Can you help me, please?" The bird wanted to help the rabbit but didn't know how. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the rabbit out of the tree. The bird quickly flew to save the rabbit. The bird caught the rabbit in the air and brought him safely to the ground. The rabbit was so happy and thanked the bird for saving him. From that day on, the rabbit and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim lived on a street with his mom and dad. Tim was a mighty boy who liked to play and help his friends. One day, Tim saw a big cat on his street. The cat was stuck in a tree. Tim wanted to help the cat, so he called his friend, Sam. Sam came running to help Tim. They both looked at the tree and thought of a way to help the cat. Tim said, "I have an idea! Let's climb the tree and get the cat down." Sam agreed, and they both started to climb. But when they got to the top, the cat jumped down on its own! Tim and Sam were surprised and didn't know what to do. They tried to climb down, but they got stuck in the tree too! Tim's mom and dad heard them and came outside. They saw Tim and Sam stuck in the tree and called for help. But no one could reach them. In the end, Tim and Sam had to stay in the tree all night. They were sad and scared, but they learned a lesson to always ask for help from grown-ups.
