Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Calm Cat and the Bell

One day, a calm cat named Tom was walking in the park. He saw a big tree with a bell on it. Tom was curious about the bell. He wanted to make it ring. So, he jumped high and hit the bell with his paw. The bell made a loud "ring" sound. A little girl named Sue heard the bell ring. She ran to the tree and saw Tom. She asked Tom a question, "Did you make the bell ring?" Tom nodded his head and said, "Yes, I did." Sue smiled and said, "I like the sound of the bell. Can you do it again?" Tom jumped up and hit the bell once more. It rang again, making both Sue and Tom happy. They played with the bell all day, enjoying the sound it made.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play with his football. Every day, he would go outside and kick it around. Tim was very good at playing football. One day, Tim kicked the ball and it went very far. It went so far that it went into the woods. Tim went to look for it. As he walked slow and looked around, he saw a twist in the path. Tim was a little scared, but he wanted his football back. So, he took the twist and kept going. As Tim walked, he found his football. But, he also found a big, friendly bear! The bear was playing with Tim's football! Tim was surprised, but he was not scared anymore. The bear and Tim played football together and became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim found a kite in his room. It was a big, pretty kite. He wanted to fly the kite outside. He went to the park with his mom and dad. The wind was just right for flying a kite. Tim dared to let the kite go high in the sky. The kite danced and played with the wind. Suddenly, the kite flew away from Tim's hand. Tim was sad and scared. He thought he lost his kite forever. Then, a sour-faced girl named Sue found the kite. She saw Tim crying and knew the kite was his. Sue decided to be kind and give the kite back to Tim. Tim was very happy and thanked Sue. They both learned that being brave and kind can make friends and bring happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a lot of stuff. The stuff was nice and clean. A little girl named Lily lived in the room. She liked her room a lot. One day, Lily found a big box. She opened the box and saw more stuff inside. She was very happy. Lily wanted to gain more stuff for her room. She put the new stuff in her room and it looked even nicer. But then, something unexpected happened. The stuff in the box started to move! The stuff was not just stuff, it was alive! The stuff was actually a group of friendly animals. They wanted to play with Lily. Lily was surprised but happy. She played with the animals and had a lot of fun. Her room was not tidy anymore, but she did not mind.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard had a picture of a cat. The cat had a nosy face. It liked to look at everything. The people in the town liked the cat on the billboard. One day, a little girl named Lily walked by the billboard. She saw the cat's nosy face and started to weep. She was sad because she lost her toy. The cat on the billboard saw Lily weep and wanted to help her. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat on the billboard jumped out of the picture! It became a real cat. The cat found Lily's toy and gave it back to her. Lily stopped weeping and smiled. The cat went back to the billboard. Now, the people in the town love the cat even more.
