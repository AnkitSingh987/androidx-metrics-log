Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beautiful Palace and the Stars

Once upon a time there was a beautiful palace. It was bright and shining, like bright stars in the night sky. One day, the King and Queen decided to go to the palace. They looked around and saw that it was very dusty. So the King said to the Queen, "Let's polish the palace so it's nice and clean". The Queen picked up a cloth and a bucket of soapy water and started to polish the walls and ceilings. Soon the palace was looking clean and shiny. The King and Queen looked around and were very happy. The Queen said to the King, "Isn't it beautiful? It looks so bright now!" The King nodded and smiled. The palace was shiny and bright and so the King and Queen decided to have a party there. They invited all their friends and everyone had a great time. The King and Queen were very pleased, and they thanked the Queen for her hard work polishing the palace. They were so happy that they decided to polish the palace every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very impatient. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Mia's mom was cooking on the stove. Mia wanted to help, but the stove was hot. Mia asked her mom, "Can I help with the stove?" Her mom said, "No, Mia. The stove is too hot. You can help with something else." Mia was sad, but she listened to her mom. Her mom allowed her to help with the salad instead. Mia was happy to help and soon forgot about the stove. They had a fun time making dinner together.

Lily likes to sing. She sings in the morning, in the afternoon, and at night. She sings when she plays, when she eats, and when she sleeps. She sings loud and happy songs. One day, Lily sees her mom make coffee. She wants to try some. She asks her mom, "Can I have coffee, please?" Her mom says, "No, Lily, coffee is not for you. Coffee is for grown-ups. Coffee is hot and bitter. You will not like it." But Lily is curious. She waits until her mom is busy. She sneaks to the kitchen. She sees the coffee pot on the table. She reaches for it. She spills some coffee on her dress. She cries. Her mom hears her cry. She runs to the kitchen. She sees Lily and the coffee. She says, "Oh, Lily, what did you do? You are dirty and wet. And you wasted the coffee. Why did you touch it?" Lily says, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to try coffee. I wanted to be like you." Her mom hugs her. She says, "I love you, Lily. But you don't need to be like me. You are perfect as you are. You are my sweet and silly singer. Come on, let's change your dress and clean the mess. And then you can sing me a song." Lily smiles. She says, "Okay, mom. I love you too. And I love to sing."

One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a shiny medal hanging on a tree. He wanted to get it, but it was too high for him. Max stared at the medal and thought of a plan. A small bird named Lily saw Max and asked, "Why do you stare at the medal, Max?" Max said, "I want the medal, but I can't reach it." Lily wanted to help Max, so she flew up and got the medal for him. Max was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They became good friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is that we should help each other, no matter how big or small we are.

Once upon a time, there was a unique tree. It was very big and had many leaves. One day, a small boy named Tim went to the tree. He wanted to pick a leaf. Tim said, "Tree, can I have a leaf?" The tree trembled and said, "Yes, you can take one." Tim was happy and reached for a leaf. But as he did, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the leaves away. Tim was sad, but the tree said, "Don't worry. I will grow new leaves for you." And it did. Tim smiled and picked a new leaf from the unique tree. They became best friends.
