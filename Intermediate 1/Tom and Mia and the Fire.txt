Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Fire

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big fire truck. It has a loud siren and a long hose. The fire truck stops near a house. The house has smoke and flames. "Wow, look at the fire truck!" Tom says. "It is so big and red!" "Yes, it is!" Mia says. "But why is there a fire in the house?" Tom and Mia discuss the fire. They think maybe someone left a candle on. Or maybe someone cooked something and forgot it. Or maybe there was a spark from a wire. "A spark?" Mia asks. "What is a spark?" "A spark is a tiny light that comes from something hot," Tom explains. "Like when you rub two stones together. Or when you see fireworks in the sky." "Oh, I see," Mia says. "But sparks can be dangerous, right?" "Yes, they can," Tom says. "They can make things burn. Like wood or paper or cloth. Those things are dry. Dry means they have no water in them. Water can stop fire. That is why the fire truck has a hose. The hose can spray water on the fire." "I understand," Mia says. "The fire truck is here to help. It can put out the fire and make the house safe again." Tom and Mia watch the fire truck do its job. They are glad that no one is hurt. They are also glad that they have each other. They are good friends. They like to discuss things and learn new words.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy bow. One day, Spot went for a walk with his mom. His mom told him to stay close to her. Spot thought the forest was very interesting. As they walked, Spot saw a big tree. He wanted to see what was behind it. He forgot what his mom said about staying close. So, Spot ran to the tree to look. But, when he got there, he found a big hole in the ground. Spot was scared. Spot called for his mom, but she was too far away. He tried to go back, but he fell into the hole. Spot was very sad. His mom found him later, but she could not get him out. Spot had to stay in the hole, and he never got to play with his toy bow again.

Once upon a time there was a strong aeroplane. It was flying high up in the sky. Suddenly, the aeroplane spun around quickly and twisted in the air. The pilot saw what was happening and shouted, "Quick! Everyone hold on tight!" Everyone grabbed on tightly as the aeroplane continued to twist in the sky. The passengers all felt the plane twist in different directions. One little three year old boy shouted, "Oh no! I'm going to fall out of the sky!" The pilot replied, "Don't worry, little one. Stay in your seat and you will be safe." And so the strong aeroplane kept twisting in the sky. But the pilot knew what to do and soon the aeroplane was heading safely back to the ground. Everyone cheered with happiness, especially the little three year old boy!

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tim. Tim was very compassionate. He loved to help others. One day, he saw a small bird shivering in the cold. Tim felt sad for the bird and wanted to help it. Tim's mom saw him looking at the bird. She suggested, "Why don't you give the bird your hat? It will keep the bird warm." Tim liked the idea. He took off his warm hat and put it on the bird. The bird felt happy and warm. The bird said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." Tim smiled and felt happy too. From that day, Tim and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and Tim always helped the bird when it needed him.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the print shop with her mom. Lily wore her new coat because it was cold outside. At the print shop, they saw a big machine that made pictures on paper. Lily asked her mom, "Can I print a picture of a hot sun on my coat?" Her mom said, "Yes, that is a fun idea!" They asked the man at the shop to help them. The man put Lily's coat in the big machine and printed a hot sun on it. When they got home, something unexpected happened. Lily's coat with the hot sun on it started to feel warm! Lily said, "Wow, my coat is warm like the sun!" Her mom was surprised too. Now, Lily had a magic hot sun coat that kept her warm on cold days.
