Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Dog

One day, a brave boy named Tom wanted to play hockey. He put on his shoes and tried to tie them. But he was not good at tying shoes. His friend, Sam, saw him and said, "Let me help you tie your shoes." Tom and Sam went to the park to play hockey. They had lots of fun. They hit the ball with their hockey sticks and ran very fast. They laughed and played for a long time. Then, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw the ball and wanted to play too. But the dog was not good at playing hockey. The dog bit the ball and it popped. Tom and Sam were sad. They could not play hockey anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but he did not like to wash his hands. His mom always told him that washing hands was very important. She said, "Tim, you must use the faucet and soap to make your hands clean." But Tim did not listen. One day, Tim found a magic faucet in the park. The magic faucet was serious about helping people. It could make anything you wanted if you washed your hands. Tim thought this was fun, so he washed his hands and asked for a big toy car. The magic faucet made the toy car, and Tim was very happy. From that day on, Tim always washed his hands. He learned that being clean was important and could also be fun. The magic faucet taught Tim a good lesson about washing hands. And now, Tim tells all his friends to wash their hands too.

The day was nice and sunny. Linny and her mom were walking down the path. Linny saw a pink balloon and said, "Mommy, can I get the balloon?" "Yes, we will buy it for you," her mom said with a smile. Linny was so excited and quickly took the pink balloon. She hugged it tightly and said, "I'm going to fill it with air." Her mom said, "That's a great idea, honey." Linny put the balloon in her hand and started filling it with air. But suddenly, the balloon burst and that filled Linny with sadness. "It was my journey," she said with tears rolling down her cheeks. She looked around for help, but nobody was there. Linny was so disappointed and ran to her mom with tears in her eyes. Her mom hugged her tightly, but the balloon was gone forever. Linny was too sad to say anything else, and the journey was now over.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big box with a wrap on it. Tim was excited and wanted to open it. His mom said, "Let's open the box and see what's inside!" When they opened the box, they found a small unknown animal. It was very cute, but they didn't know what it was. Tim wanted to help the animal, so he asked his mom, "Can we help it find its home?" Just then, a kind old lady came by and said, "Oh, that's my pet! I lost it this morning. Thank you for helping me find it!" The old lady took the animal and gave Tim a big hug. Tim and his mom were happy they could help the old lady and her pet.

Once upon a time, there was a huge dog named Max. Max loved to race with his best friend, a little bird named Sam. They would race all around the big green park. One day, they raced to a tap in the park. Max got there first and won the race. He was very happy. Sam was a little sad, but Max told him, "Don't worry, we can race again!" So they raced back to their favorite tree. This time, Sam won the race. They both laughed and played together. Max and Sam were very happy to be best friends who loved to race.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little bug. He lived on earth in a small home with his mom and dad. The bug liked to play with his friends in the sun. One day, the bug saw a big bird in the sky. The bird could zoom very fast! The bug wanted to zoom like the bird, so he asked his mom and dad how he could do it. They told him to try his best and be brave. The next day, the bug tried to zoom like the bird. He ran as fast as he could, and suddenly, he was zooming! All his friends watched him and clapped. The bug was very happy that he could zoom like the big bird. From that day on, he showed everyone how brave and fast he was.
