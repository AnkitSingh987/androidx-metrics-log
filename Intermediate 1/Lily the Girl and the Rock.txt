Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Rock

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a big rock near a wreck. The wreck was an old car that had been there for a long time. Lily wanted to climb on the rock and play. Lily's mom said, "Be careful, Lily. Don't fall off the rock!" Lily climbed up the rock and looked at the wreck. She saw a lemon tree next to the wreck. The lemons on the tree were very sour. Lily picked a lemon and gave it to her mom. "Mom, try this!" she said. Her mom took a bite and made a funny face. "Oh, it's so sour!" she laughed. They both laughed and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly dog named Spot. Spot liked to play in the mud and get dirty. One day, Spot found a camera on the ground. He did not know what it was, but he thought it was a toy. Spot wanted to chew on the camera, but he tried to resist. He knew he should not chew on things that were not his toys. Instead, he decided to take the camera to his friend, a little girl named Lucy. Lucy saw the camera and knew what it was. She thanked Spot for bringing it to her. They took funny pictures together and had lots of fun. Spot was happy he did not chew on the camera, and he got a bath to not be smelly anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a warm festival. Everyone was happy and having fun. They ate good food, played games, and danced to music. At the festival, there was a test. The test was to see who could blow the biggest bubble. The children were excited to try it. They took turns blowing bubbles, and the bubbles grew bigger and bigger. In the end, a little girl named Mia blew the biggest bubble. Everyone clapped and cheered for her. Mia won a big, soft teddy bear. She hugged it and smiled. The warm festival was a happy day for everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, ancient toy box. One day, he found a bottle of glue in the toy box. He didn't know what to do with it, so he asked his mom. "Mom, what can I do with this glue?" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "You can use it to fix broken things or make fun crafts." Tim decided to use the glue to make a fun craft. He glued colorful paper and shapes onto a big piece of paper. But then, something unexpected happened! The glue bottle jumped out of Tim's hand and spilled all over the floor. "Oh no!" Tim cried. His mom came to help him. "Don't worry, Tim. We can wash it up." They got a wet cloth and washed the glue off the floor. Tim learned to be more careful with glue, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Sam were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were flying in an aeroplane. They made noises with their mouths and moved their arms like wings. "Look, Lily, there is a real aeroplane in the sky!" Sam said, pointing up. "Wow, it is big and white!" Lily said, looking at the aeroplane. "Where is it going?" "I don't know. Maybe to a far away place. Maybe to the moon!" Sam said, making his eyes big. They watched the aeroplane fly until it was very small. Then it turned and went behind a cloud. "Where did it go?" Lily asked, feeling sad. "It will come back, don't worry. Maybe it is playing hide and seek with us!" Sam said, trying to cheer her up. They waited for the aeroplane to come back, but it did not. The sky became dark and the stars came out. "Maybe it is sleeping, like us. Maybe it will come back tomorrow, when the sun is up!" Lily said, yawning. "Maybe. Let's go inside, it is cold and dark. We can play again tomorrow!" Sam said, taking her hand. They ran inside and hugged their mum and dad. They told them about the aeroplane and how they pretended to fly. They had a warm drink and a cookie. Then they brushed their teeth and went to bed. They dreamed of flying in the aeroplane and seeing the moon.
