Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark and the Park

Mark was a boy who liked to walk. He walked to the park, to the shop, to the school. He walked with his mum, his dad, his dog. He walked fast, slow, happy, sad. He walked every day. One day, he saw something unusual on his walk. It was a big, round, shiny thing. It was in the sky, but not very high. It was not the sun, or the moon, or a star. It was something else. Mark stopped and looked at it. He pointed and said, "Mum, what is that?" His mum looked up and saw it too. She was surprised and scared. She said, "I don't know, Mark. It is very strange. Maybe it is a spaceship." A spaceship? Mark liked spaceships. He had a toy spaceship at home. He wanted to see the spaceship closer. He said, "Can we go to the spaceship, mum? Please?" His mum shook her head and said, "No, Mark. We can't go to the spaceship. It might be dangerous. We have to go home now. Come on, let's walk fast." Mark was sad. He wanted to see the spaceship. He wanted to say hello to the aliens. He wanted to have an adventure. But he listened to his mum and walked fast with her. He looked back at the sky and waved at the spaceship. He hoped it would come back another day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a foolish bird who did not like to try new things. Tim lived in a tree with his family. One day, his mom told him that it was time to fly south for the winter season. Tim did not want to go. He wanted to stay in his tree. Tim's mom said, "You must try, or you will be cold in the winter season." But Tim did not listen. He stayed in his tree while his family flew away. Tim was all alone in the tree. The cold season came, and Tim started to feel cold. He wished he had listened to his mom. Tim decided to try flying south to find his family. He flapped his wings and tried hard. At first, it was hard, but Tim did not give up. He flew and flew until he found his family in the warm place. Tim was happy that he tried something new. From that day on, he promised to never be foolish and to always try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very eager dog. He liked to run and play all day long. One day, Spot went to the park with his family. They played fetch and had lots of fun. Spot's mom told him to stay close to her. But Spot saw a big butterfly and wanted to catch it. He ran after the butterfly, far away from his mom. Spot's mom called for him to come back, but he did not listen. As Spot chased the butterfly, he found a big piece of candy on the ground. It looked very yummy. Spot put the candy in his mouth and ate it. But the candy was bad and made Spot very sick. He wished he had listened to his mom and stayed close to her.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a parrot. The parrot had a hurt wing. He wanted to fly but he could not. The parrot was very frustrated. One day, a kind girl saw the parrot. She wanted to help him. The girl took the parrot to her home. She put a bandage on his wing to help it heal. After some days, the parrot's wing was better. He was so happy! The girl took the parrot outside. The parrot flew high in the sky. He was not frustrated anymore. The girl and the parrot were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bobby. Bobby liked to wander in the big sea. One day, Bobby felt very tired. He wanted to rest, so he used his anchor. The anchor went down, down, down into the water. A big fish named Fin saw Bobby. Fin swam up to Bobby and said, "Hi, Bobby! Why are you so tired?" Bobby said, "I wander a lot in the sea, and now I need a break." Fin said, "That's okay, Bobby. We all need a break sometimes." Bobby and Fin became good friends. They played in the sea and had lots of fun. When Bobby felt tired, he used his anchor to rest. And when Fin felt tired, he swam by Bobby's side. They were happy together in the big sea.
