Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Park

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim was eager to buy a dream. He wanted a dream about a big, fun park. In this park, he could play with his friends and have a great time. One sunny day, Tim went to the dream store with his mom. The store had many dreams to buy. Tim looked around and found the dream he wanted. It was a dream about the big, fun park he had wished for. Tim's mom bought the dream for him. That night, Tim went to bed with a big smile. He had the best dream ever, playing in the park with his friends. And they all had a very happy day.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Tim. Tim loved to jump all day long. One day, Tim saw a big fountain in the park. He thought it would be fun to jump into it. Tim tried to jump into the fountain, but it was too high for him. He did not give up. Tim was very persistent. He tried again and again, but he could not reach the top. One day, a bird saw Tim trying to jump into the fountain. The bird had an idea. She picked up Tim and dropped him into the fountain. Tim was so happy! He learned that when you keep trying, good things can happen. And sometimes, friends can help you too.

---

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Sam. Sam had a long garden where he grew many things. In the garden, there was a big melon. Sam loved the melon very much. He would look at it every day. One day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird saw the melon and wanted to eat it. Sam saw the bird and began to worry. He said, "Please, big bird, do not eat my melon!" The bird did not listen to Sam. It picked up the melon with its beak and flew away. Sam was very sad. He cried and cried. The melon was gone, and Sam could not do anything to bring it back. And so, the story has a bad ending, with Sam's melon taken away by the big bird.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little fork. This fork lived in a modern house with a big family. The fork liked to dream about going on big trips with the family. One day, the family went on a picnic. They took the little fork with them. The little fork was so happy to be outside. It felt like its dream came true. At the picnic, the family used the little fork to eat yummy food. The little fork felt important and loved. After the picnic, the family took the fork back to their modern house. The fork was happy to be part of the family and continued to dream about more fun trips.

---

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to create something fun. She thought and thought about what to make. Then, she had a great idea: she would make a snow friend! Lily went outside in the cold and started to roll snow into a big ball. She made two more snowballs, one smaller and one even smaller. She put them on top of each other to make her snow friend. Lily found two sticks for arms and a carrot for a nose. She felt very happy with her snow friend. But then, a foolish squirrel came and took the carrot off the snow friend's face. The squirrel ran up a tree with the carrot. Lily felt sad, but she did not give up. She found another carrot and put it on her snow friend's face. Now, her snow friend was back and Lily felt happy again.

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big tree with green fruits. Lily wants to pick one. "Look, Ben, limes!" she says. "I like limes. They are sour and yummy." Ben nods. He likes limes too. He helps Lily to reach a lime. He pulls it from the branch. It falls to the ground. "Oops!" he says. "Sorry, Lily." Lily smiles. She does not mind. She picks up the lime. She peels it with her fingers. She gives half to Ben. They bite into the limes. "Mmm, sour!" they say. They make funny faces. They laugh. A squirrel comes out of the tree. It sees the limes. It is curious. It runs to Lily and Ben. It sniffs the limes. It takes a bite. "Ouch!" it says. It does not like the lime. It spits it out. It runs away. Lily and Ben see the squirrel. They think it is cute. They clap their hands. They say, "Bye, squirrel!" They finish their limes. They throw the peels in the trash. They hold hands. They go to the swings. They are happy. They like playing in the park. They like limes. They like squirrels. They like each other.
