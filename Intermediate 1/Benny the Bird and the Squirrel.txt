Category: Intermediate
Title: Benny the Bird and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a playful little bird named Benny. Benny loved to fly high and soar above the trees. He also liked to play with his friend, Timmy the squirrel. One sunny day, Benny saw a strange thing hanging from a tree. It was a telephone! Benny called out to Timmy, "Look, Timmy! What is that?" Timmy looked up and said, "I don't know, Benny. Let's play with it!" Benny and Timmy took turns flying and jumping to touch the telephone. They were having so much fun! They didn't know what the telephone was for, but they loved playing with it. And so, Benny and Timmy played happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to help her mom. She was eager to do something big. Her mom asked her to examine the laundry. Lucy said yes and went to look at the clothes. Lucy looked at the laundry very closely. She saw a red sock, a blue shirt, and a yellow dress. She touched the clothes and smelled them. They were clean and fresh. Lucy was happy to help her mom. Then, Lucy and her mom took the laundry to put away. They put the red sock in a drawer, the blue shirt on a hanger, and the yellow dress in the closet. Lucy felt proud that she helped her mom with the laundry. They both smiled and gave each other a big hug.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. Today, they want to play with other kids too. They see a group of kids on the grass. The group has a big ball and a net. "Can we play with them?" Lily asks Ben. "Let's ask them," Ben says. They walk to the group and say hello. "Hi, we are Lily and Ben. Can we play with you?" Lily asks the group. "Sure, you can play with us. We are playing catch with the ball. Do you know how to play?" a boy from the group asks. "Yes, we know how to play. You throw the ball and catch it. If you miss, you go to the net," Ben says. "Good, you are right. Are you ready to play?" the boy asks. "Yes, we are ready. Let's play!" Lily and Ben say. They join the group and have fun playing catch with the ball. They take turns throwing and catching. They laugh and cheer when they catch the ball. They say sorry and try again when they miss. They make new friends and enjoy the game.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy day. A big man named Tom wanted to build something with steel. He needed to measure the steel to make sure it was the right size. He took a long tape and started to measure. Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue asked, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom said, "I am measuring steel to build something. Do you want to help?" Sue was happy and said, "Yes, I want to help!" They measured the steel together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came and took the steel away! Tom and Sue were surprised. They laughed and said, "Silly dog! We need the steel to build!" They chased the dog and got the steel back. Then, they built a fun playhouse for all the kids to enjoy.

Mum and Dad had a surprise for Ellie. They took her outside and showed her a round, smelly thing. Ellie looked very confused. "What is it?" she asked, crinkling her nose. "It's a tire, sweetie," said Mum. "We're going to hang it in the garden, so you can play." Ellie was so excited, she couldn't believe her eyes. She helped her Mum and Dad lift the tire and hang it on the tree. Then she held onto the tire and swung around and around, faster and faster, until she was so dizzy she had to stop. Ellie continued to play with the tire for the rest of the day. She forgot about the smelly part and only remembered how much fun it was to swing and swing around round and round. The end
