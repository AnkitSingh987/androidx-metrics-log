Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family and the Lake

Once there was a family who went on vacation together. Dad was so excited to be going, and the kids were too. Mom said the vacation will be extraordinary, but the kids could not believe it. The first day there was quite ordinary. The boys decided to go in the lake. When they got to the lake they saw it was very deep. Dad said, “Let’s not go in because it is too scary. We don’t want to get soaked!” But the boys wanted to take a chance. They said, “Dad, we are brave! We will take the risk and just go in up to our knees.” Dad agreed, “Ok, if you’re brave and careful, then you can go in. But no further!” The boys went in slowly, and it felt so refreshing. They laughed and had so much fun that they forgot they were only supposed to go in up to their knees. In the end, Dad had to go and get them out, and they were soaked! The boys learned it is ok to take risks, but you should always be careful!

Lily and Ben were very happy. They were going to live in a new house with their mom and dad. Mom and dad had a marriage. That meant they loved each other very much and wore rings on their fingers. They packed their toys and clothes in big boxes. They put the boxes in a truck. The truck drove them to the new house. It was big and bright and had a garden. Lily and Ben liked the new house. They helped mom and dad unpack the boxes. They found their toys and clothes and put them in their rooms. They also found some hidden things. They found a photo of mom and dad when they were young. They found a book with stories and pictures of their family. They found a necklace that mom said was from her grandma. Mom and dad smiled and hugged them. They said they were happy to share their hidden things with them. They said they were happy to start a new life in the new house. They said they loved them very much. Lily and Ben smiled and hugged them back. They said they loved them too. They said they were happy to be a family. They said they liked the new house. They said they wanted to play in the garden. Mom and dad said yes. They all went to the garden and had fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tough bear named Ben. Ben loved to take long walks and look for food. One day, Ben decided to start a new adventure to find a big honey pot. As Ben walked, he saw a little bee. The bee said, "I know where the big honey pot is. Follow me!" Ben was happy and followed the bee. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with a big honey pot. But, when Ben tried to take the honey pot, it was stuck! He pulled and pulled, but the honey pot did not move. Then, the little bee said, "I can help!" The bee buzzed around the tree and the honey pot fell down. Ben was so surprised that the little bee could help him. They both shared the honey and became best friends. The end.

One day, a boy named Tim went to school. His teacher, Miss Jane, wanted to teach the kids about magnets. She showed them a big, red magnet. The kids were very excited to learn. Tim saw an icy puddle outside. He had an idea. He took the big, red magnet and put it on the icy puddle. The magnet got stuck! Tim tried to pull it off, but it would not move. The other kids saw and tried to help, but the magnet was still stuck. Then, Miss Jane came outside. She saw the kids and the stuck magnet. She asked them to get a cup of warm water. They poured the water on the icy puddle. The ice melted and the magnet came off! The kids were happy and thanked Miss Jane. They learned how magnets work and how to fix a problem.
