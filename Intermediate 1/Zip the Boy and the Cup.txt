Category: Intermediate
Title: Zip the Boy and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Zip. Zip loved to drink water. Every day, he would drink water from a big cup. One day, Zip went to a party with his friends. At the party, they had many things to drink. There was juice, milk, and water. Zip wanted to be polite, so he waited for his turn to get a drink. When it was Zip's turn, he got a big cup of water. He said "please" and "thank you" to the person giving the drinks. All of Zip's friends were happy that he was so polite. They all had a great time at the party, and Zip drank lots of water.

One day, a little cat and a little dog were playing by a line in the park. The little cat liked to joke, and the little dog liked to laugh. They were very good friends. But one day, a big bird came and stood on the line. The big bird did not like jokes. The little cat told a joke, and the little dog laughed. The big bird got mad and said, "No jokes here!" The little cat and the little dog were sad. They wanted to play and joke by the line. So, they made a plan. The little cat told a very funny joke, and the little dog laughed very loud. The big bird could not help but laugh too. The big bird learned to like jokes, and they all became friends. From that day on, they all played and joked by the line together.

Anna and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to pretend they were knights and princesses. They had a furry dog named Spot who followed them everywhere. "Look, Ben, I see a dragon!" Anna said, pointing to the sky. She saw a big, green shape with wings and a long tail. "Where?" Ben asked, looking up. He saw the dragon too. It was flying over the trees, making a loud roar. "Wow, it's so cool!" Ben said. "I want to be friends with it!" "Me too!" Anna said. "Maybe it's a nice dragon. Maybe it likes to play." They ran after the dragon, hoping to catch up with it. Spot ran with them, barking happily. The dragon saw the children and the dog. It was curious. It flew down and landed near them. It was bigger than a car, but it had a friendly face. "Hello, little ones," the dragon said. "What are you doing here?" "Hello, dragon," Anna said. "We are playing. We are knights and princesses. And this is Spot, our furry dog." The dragon smiled. It liked the children and the dog. It was lonely in the sky. "Can I play with you?" the dragon asked. "I don't have any friends." "Of course you can!" Ben said. "You can be our dragon friend. We can go on adventures together." The children and the dog hugged the dragon. The dragon hugged them back. They were happy. Anna and Ben admired the dragon. They thought it was the most amazing thing they had ever seen. They loved its green scales, its big wings, and its long tail. The dragon admired the children and the dog. It thought they were the most brave and kind things it had ever met. It loved their smiles, their laughter, and their furry dog. They became the best of friends. They played in the park every day. They had fun and learned from each other. They were a happy team.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky robot named Rob. Rob was a very good robot. He liked to help people with their work. One day, Rob saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She wanted to fold a paper boat, but she did not know how. Rob wanted to help Sue. He showed her how to fold the paper boat. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Rob!" They played with the paper boat in a small pond. The boat floated and they were both so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the paper boat away! Sue was sad again. But Rob had an idea. He folded a new paper boat for Sue. This time, he made the boat stronger. Sue was happy again. The moral of the story is: when something goes wrong, we can always try again and make it better.
