Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bird

One day, a boy named Tim went to the sea with his dad. They saw a big yacht. It was not a regular yacht. It had many colors and a funny flag. Tim and his dad went on the yacht. They met a man named Jack. Jack said, "I feed fish to my pet bird. Do you want to help?" Tim said, "Yes!" They fed the fish to the bird. Then, something unexpected happened. The bird grew very big! It could talk! The bird said, "Thank you for feeding me. Now I can help people." The bird flew away to help others. Tim and his dad were happy. They learned that being kind to others can make good things happen.

Once upon a time, there was a clever fish named Fin. Fin lived in a beautiful harbor with his fish friends. They loved to play and swim all day. One sunny day, Fin and his friends decided to have a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest fish in the harbor. All the fish lined up and waited for the race to start. When the race began, they all swam as fast as they could. Fin was very fast, but he saw that one of his friends was struggling to swim. He decided to stop racing and help his friend instead. Together, they finished the race at the end. All the fish cheered for Fin and his friend. They learned that being kind and helping friends is more important than winning a race.

One day, Tom was very tired. He had a lot of mail to push in the big box. Tom saw his friend, Sam. "Sam, can you help me push the mail?" Tom asked. "No, I am busy," said Sam. Tom was sad. He tried to push the mail all by himself. But he was too tired. In the end, the mail did not go in the big box. Tom was very sad and went home.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little bird named Billy. Billy liked to fly around and see what his friends were doing. One day, he saw his friend, Tommy the turtle, with a pillow. Tommy looked sad. Billy asked Tommy why he was sad. Tommy started to complain. He said, "I am too slow to play with the other animals. I wish I could be fast like you, Billy." Billy thought for a moment and had an idea. Billy told Tommy to put the pillow on his back. Suddenly, Tommy felt lighter and faster! He could now play with the other animals without feeling slow. The other animals were surprised! They learned that it is okay to be different and ask for help. And that day, Tommy and Billy became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his ball. Every day, he would throw the ball up and down, and side to side. One day, Tim threw the ball too hard and it flew far away. He felt sad and hurt because he lost his favorite toy. For a whole week, Tim looked for his ball. He asked his friends and family if they had seen it, but no one knew where it was. Tim was very sad and missed playing with his ball. He didn't want to play with his other toys because they didn't make him as happy as his ball did. One sunny day, Tim's friend, Lily, came to visit him. She saw how sad Tim was and wanted to help. Lily and Tim looked for the ball together. They searched high and low, near and far. Finally, they found the ball stuck in a tree. Tim was so happy and grateful to have his ball back. He hugged Lily and thanked her for helping him. From that day on, Tim and Lily played together and became best friends. And Tim learned to be more careful when he threw his ball.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat with her fork. Her fork was her best friend. She took it everywhere she went. One day, Sue's mom said, "Sue, you can't take your fork to school today." Sue was sad, but she had to listen to her mom. She left her fork at home and went to school. At school, Sue's friend Tim saw that she was sad. "What's wrong, Sue?" he asked. "My mom won't allow me to take my fork to school," she said. Tim wanted to help, so he gave Sue a new fork. Sue was happy again, and they played together all day.
