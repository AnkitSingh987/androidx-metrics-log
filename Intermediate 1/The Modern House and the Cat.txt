Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modern House and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In this house lived a girl named Lucy and her pet cat, Mint. Lucy and Mint loved to play together all day long. One day, Lucy decided to feed Mint. She took a bowl and filled it with cat food. Mint was very happy and ate all the food in the bowl. Lucy smiled and patted Mint on the head. After Mint was full, they played with a ball. They were very happy in their modern house. At the end of the day, Lucy and Mint went to bed. They had a good day and couldn't wait for more fun tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very fast. He liked to run and play all day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it is time to eat. Please, seat at the table." Tim sat down at the table. He saw a big plate of yummy food. There were apples, bananas, and cookies. Tim's mom said, "You can eat the cookies after you eat the fruit." Tim ate the apples and bananas very fast. He wanted to eat the cookies. When he finished the fruit, he said, "Mom, I ate all the fruit. Can I eat the cookies now?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can eat the cookies now." Tim was so happy. He ate the cookies and they were so yummy. Then, Tim went outside to play again.

---

One day, a little girl named Sue found a needle on the floor. She knew it was sharp and could hurt someone. Sue wanted to be helpful, so she decided to pick it up and put it away. Sue saw her mom in the kitchen and said, "Mom, I found a needle on the floor. What should I do with it?" Her mom, who was very wise, told her, "Sue, be careful with that needle. Let's put it in a safe place so no one gets hurt." Sue's little brother, Tim, wanted to play a foolish game. He thought it would be fun to try and catch the needle. Sue told him, "No, Tim! That's not a good idea. You could get hurt." Tim listened to his sister and decided not to play with the needle. Sue and her mom went to the sewing room. Sue's mom told her to kneel down and open the bottom drawer. Inside, there was a small box for needles. Sue carefully put the needle in the box and closed the drawer. Sue felt happy that she had been helpful and kept her family safe. Her mom was proud of her too. From that day on, Sue and Tim knew not to play with needles and to always be careful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. They were very competitive. They always wanted to win. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, prepare your uniform for school tomorrow." Tim did as he was told. The next day, Tim wore his uniform to school. At school, Tim and his friends played a race game. They all wanted to be the fastest. Tim's friend, Sam, fell down during the race. Tim stopped and helped Sam get up. They finished the race together, but they did not win. "That's okay," said Tim, "We had fun, and we helped each other." The other kids saw what Tim did and learned that winning is not everything. Being kind and helping others is more important. That day, everyone learned a good lesson.

Sara went for a walk in the park. She saw a frog hopping along. She was very excited and asked her mum if she could join the frog. Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, but stay close. I don't want you to go too far." Sara hopped along with the frog. Suddenly, the frog hopped into a pond. Sara was worried that she couldn't follow. "Don't worry," said mum. "We can join the frog. Here, take my hand and jump in!" Sara followed her mum into the pond and soon they were swimming alongside the frog. Sara was so happy that she had joined the frog.
