Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Mole

Mia was an adventurous girl. She liked to explore new places and find new things. One day, she saw a big hole in the ground. She wondered what was inside. She decided to go in and have a look. She crawled into the hole. It was dark and damp. She felt something soft and furry. She said, "Hello, who are you?" A voice said, "I am a mole. This is my home. Who are you?" Mia said, "I am Mia. I am a girl. I like to explore. Can I see your home?" The mole said, "No, you can't. You are too big. You don't fit in here. You are making a mess. You are scaring my family. You have to go out." Mia said, "But I want to see more. I want to be your friend. Please let me stay." The mole said, "No, you can't. You are not a mole. You are a girl. You belong outside. Go out now." Mia felt sad and angry. She did not want to go out. She wanted to see more. She tried to crawl deeper into the hole. But she got stuck. She could not move. She started to cry. The mole heard her cry. He felt sorry for her. He said, "Don't cry, Mia. I will help you. I will dig you out. But you have to promise me something." Mia said, "What do I have to promise?" The mole said, "You have to promise me that you will not come into my home again. You have to respect my space. You have to find your own place to explore. Do you promise?" Mia said, "Yes, I promise. I am sorry, mole. I did not mean to bother you. I just wanted to see your home. But I understand that it is not for me. Thank you for helping me." The mole said, "You are welcome, Mia. I am sorry, too. I was rude to you. I just wanted to protect my home. But I understand that you are curious. Thank you for promising me." The mole dug Mia out of the hole. Mia crawled out. She saw the sun and the sky. She felt happy and free. She said, "Goodbye, mole. I hope you have a good day. Maybe we can see each other again. But not in your home. In another place." The mole said, "Goodbye, Mia. I hope you have a good day, too. Maybe we can see each other again. But not in my home. In another place."

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a bit selfish. He did not like to share his toys with others. One day, Tim's mom told him to feed their pet cat, Fluffy. Tim did not want to do it, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. While Tim was feeding Fluffy, he saw a big toilet in the bathroom. Tim had an idea. He thought, "I can hide my toys in the toilet, so no one can play with them!" He put all his toys in the toilet and felt very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Fluffy jumped on the toilet and pushed the handle. All of Tim's toys went down the toilet! Tim was very sad. He learned that being selfish was not good. From that day on, Tim decided to share his toys with others and never hide them again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a thin cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to lie in the warm sun all day long. One day, Whiskers saw a little bird named Chirpy sitting on a tree branch. "Hello, Chirpy!" said Whiskers. "Do you want to play with me?" Chirpy looked at Whiskers and said, "I don't know if I can trust you. I heard that cats can be mean to birds." Whiskers felt sad that Chirpy did not trust him. So, he made a promise. "I give you my word that I will be nice to you. We can be friends!" Chirpy thought about it and decided to give Whiskers a chance. They played together all day long and had lots of fun. Soon, they became the best of friends. The other animals saw Whiskers and Chirpy playing together and learned that even if someone looks different, they can still be a good friend. They all learned the importance of giving their word and keeping it. And they all lived happily ever after.
