Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a forest. The tree had many friends like birds, squirrels, and bugs. They all lived happily together in the tree. But one day, the sky became dark and scary. The tree and its friends knew something bad was coming. As the wind blew, the tree tried to prepare for the storm. It told its friends to hide inside its thick branches. The birds, squirrels, and bugs listened and hid inside the tree. They were all scared of the dark sky. Suddenly, there was a loud noise and a bright lightning came from the sky. It hit the tree, and the tree shook. The tree was hurt, but it was still standing strong. The storm went away and the sky became bright again. The tree and its friends were safe. They were happy they had each other to help them during the scary time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had mild skin that would get red very fast. One day, Tim went to play outside with his friend, Sam. "Let's play in the sun," said Sam. Tim tried to resist because he knew his skin would get red. But he wanted to play with his friend, so he went outside anyway. As they played, Tim's skin started to get red. Sam looked at Tim and said, "Your skin is red! Let's go inside and cool down." They went inside and had a great time playing with their toys. Tim was happy that Sam cared about his skin and they had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a brave dog named Max. Max had a cane that he loved to carry in his mouth. He would walk around with it all day and show it to his friends. One day, it started to rain. Max didn't want his cane to get wet, so he looked for something to cover it. He found a big leaf and used it to cover his cane. Max was very happy that his cane stayed dry. Max was so proud of himself for keeping his cane dry. His friends saw how brave he was and they all wanted to be just like him. From that day on, all the dogs in the neighborhood carried a cane and used a leaf to cover it when it rained.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing together. They were very happy. The cat said, "I feel so good when I play with you, dog!" The dog said, "Me too, cat! We have so much fun." Then, they heard some news. A big party was happening in the park. All their friends would be there. The cat and the dog wanted to go. They ran to the park and saw lots of treats. They ate and ate until they felt spoiled. But then, something unexpected happened. The treats turned them into big balloons! They started to float away. The cat and the dog were scared. Their friends helped them by pulling them back down. They all laughed and played together. The cat and the dog learned not to eat too many treats, but they still had a great time.

Once upon a time there was a very hairy motor. The motor was so hairy that it was hard to see what it was. One day, an old man was walking by and he heard the motor making a noise. "What is that noise?" the old man asked. The motor said, "Well, I'm trying to read, but I can't see the words because I'm too hairy." The old man said, "Hmm, let me help you out. I can shave all of your hair off, so that you can read what you need to." The motor happily agreed and the old man took out his razor and shaved off all of the motor's hair. Once the motor was all shaved, the old man said, "Allbetter, now you can read!" The motor thanked the old man and started to read. The moral of the story is that it is easier to read when you are not too hairy!

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was very good at whistling. He liked to whistle all day long. Everyone loved to hear Tim's happy songs. One day, Tim was flying inside a big house. He saw a high ceiling and wanted to touch it. So, he flew up and up until he touched the ceiling with his beak. It was fun for Tim to fly so high. After touching the ceiling, Tim felt tired. He found a soft bed to rest on. Tim closed his eyes and took a nap. When he woke up, he was not tired anymore. Tim started to whistle his happy songs again.
