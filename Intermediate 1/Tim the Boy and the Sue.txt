Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a whistle that he loved to play with. One day, his friend Sue came over to play. Sue saw the whistle and thought it was fun. "Can I borrow your whistle?" Sue asked Tim. Tim was a little fearful because he loved his whistle so much, but he wanted to be a good friend. So, he said, "Okay, you can borrow it today." Sue played with the whistle all day, and they had lots of fun. But when it was time for Sue to go home, she forgot to give the whistle back to Tim. Tim was sad, but he knew Sue would bring it back the next day. The next day, Sue came over and gave the whistle back to Tim. "I'm sorry I forgot," she said. Tim was happy to have his whistle back, and they played together with it all day. They learned that sharing can be fun, even if it's a little scary at first.

Jack saw a tall yellow man walking towards him. He was a bit scared but then the man smiled and offered him a little toy. Jack was so surprised that he forgot to be scared. The man asked Jack to join him on a walk. Jack agreed and they set off together. As they walked, the man offered to show Jack something special. He showed him a yellow balloon that he had brought with him. Jack was very excited and asked if he could hold the balloon. The man smiled and offered Jack the balloon. Jack held the balloon tightly in his hands and the yellow man smiled. Then he offered to show Jack some more special things. Together they went around the park, looking at all the yellow flowers and buzzing bees. When it was time to go home, the man offered to take Jack home in his yellow car. Jack happily agreed and thanked the man with a big hug. The man waved goodbye and Jack walked home with a smile on his face, still holding his yellow balloon.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old frog named Fred. Fred lived by a big pond. He loved to sit on a big nail near the pond and watch the fish swim. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the pond. She saw Fred and said, "Hello, Mr. Frog! Why do you sit on the nail?" Fred smiled and said, "I sit here because it makes me wise. I can see everything that happens at the pond." Lily was curious and asked, "Can I be wise too?" Fred thought for a moment and said, "Yes, but first, you must give me a kiss." Lily laughed and gave Fred a big kiss on his green cheek. Suddenly, the nail started to grow. It grew so tall that Lily could see the whole pond. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, Mr. Frog! Now I am wise too!" From that day on, Lily and Fred would sit on the tall nail together, watching the pond and being wise friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy a new carpet for their home. Tim was very excited to pick out the carpet. He saw a big, soft, and red one. He told his mom, "I like this one!" His mom said, "Okay, let's get it." At the store, there was a serious man who worked there. He helped people pay for their things. Tim's mom gave the man some money to pay for the carpet. The man looked at the money and said, "Not enough." Tim's mom looked worried. She said, "I will come back later with more." Tim was sad but knew his mom would come back to pay for the carpet. He thought about how nice the carpet would look in their home. Later, his mom came back with more money. She gave it to the serious man. This time, he smiled and said, "Thank you. You can take the carpet home now." Tim and his mom took the big, soft, red carpet home. They put it in their living room. Tim was so happy. He played on the carpet all day. He knew his mom would keep her promise and make everything okay.
