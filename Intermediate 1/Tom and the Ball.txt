Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Ball

Once upon a time, there were two twins named Tom and Tim. They lived in a big green house with their mom and dad. Tom and Tim loved to play in their big green yard. One day, Tom and Tim found a green ball in the yard. They both wanted to play with it, but there was only one ball. They started to argue about who should play with the ball first. Their mom heard them and said, "Why don't you both play with the ball together?" Tom and Tim thought about it and decided to play together. They took turns squeezing the green ball and throwing it to each other. As they played, they found out that the green ball was magical! When they squeezed it, it would change into different shapes. The twins were so happy playing with the magical green ball. They learned that sharing and playing together was much more fun than fighting. And from that day on, they always shared their toys and played together in their big green yard.

Once upon a time, there was a bee named Billy. Billy lived in a hive with his bee friends. They all worked together to make yummy honey. One day, Billy saw a big bear near the hive. The bear loved honey too! Billy went to his friend Susie, another bee in the hive. "Susie, we must move the hive. The bear will eat our yummy honey!" Susie agreed, and they told all the other bees in the hive. The bees worked together to find a new place to move the hive. The bees found a new spot, far away from the bear. They moved the hive to a big tree with pretty flowers. The bees were happy in their new home, and they made even more yummy honey. The bear never found their new hive, and the bees lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a wide green field, there lived a happy little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball and lay in the warm sun. One day, Max saw something new near his field. It was a long rail that went far away. Max was curious and wanted to see where the rail went. As Max walked along the rail, he met a big friendly cat named Luna. "Hello, Max," said Luna. "Do you know where this rail goes?" Max shook his head and replied, "No, Luna. I want to find out. Let's go together!" So Max and Luna walked side by side, following the rail. They walked and walked until they found a big, wide pond. Max and Luna were tired and thirsty, so they drank some water and lay down next to the pond. They watched as ducks swam and fish jumped. Max and Luna became good friends and had many fun adventures by the pond. And every day, they would lay by the wide pond and watch the world go by.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. The sun was shining, and it was a warm day. Mia saw some ice on the ground. She touched it with her hand and it was very rough. Mia knew that the sun could melt the ice, so she waited and watched. As the sun got hotter, the ice started to melt. Mia saw some mist come up from the ice. She thought it looked like a tiny cloud. The mist danced in the air, and it made Mia giggle. After a while, the rough ice was all gone. Mia was happy because now she could play without slipping on the ice. She ran around and had lots of fun in the warm sun.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play together. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big red ball. They would roll it, bounce it, and kick it in the yard. One day, their mom said they had to separate their toys. She wanted them to share with other kids who had no toys. Tom and Lily did not like this idea. They wanted to keep their big red ball. "But mom, we love our ball!" Tom said. "We don't want to give it away!" "Me too!" Lily said. "It's our ball!" Mom smiled and gave them a wink. She had a surprise for them. "I know you love your ball, but you can make other kids happy too. And I have something for you in the car. Come and see!" Tom and Lily followed mom to the car. They were curious and hopeful. Maybe mom had a new toy for them. When they reached the car, mom opened the trunk. Inside, there were two smaller balls. One was blue and one was pink. "Look, Tom and Lily, these are your new balls. You can each have one. They are just as fun as the big red ball, and you can play with them together or alone. And you can still share the big red ball with other kids who need it. What do you think?" Tom and Lily looked at the new balls. They were shiny and pretty. They felt soft and bouncy. They liked them a lot. They hugged mom and thanked her. "Thank you, mom! You are the best!" Tom said. "Yes, thank you, mom! We love you!" Lily said. They took their new balls and ran to the yard. They played with them happily. They rolled them, bounced them, and kicked them. They also played with the big red ball sometimes. They shared it with their friends and neighbors. They were not envious of anyone. They had everything they needed.
