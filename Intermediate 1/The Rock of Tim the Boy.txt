Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rock of Tim the Boy

One day, a boy named Tim was playing in his room. He wanted to wear his favorite clothes, but they were very dirty. "Mom, can I wear my favorite clothes?" he asked. His mom said, "No, Tim, they are too dirty. You need to wear something else." Tim was very sad and uncomfortable in his other clothes. He went outside to play with his friends. While playing, he saw a big rock. He decided to kneel down and look under the rock. To his surprise, he found a small, shiny box. Tim opened the box and found a magic cloth. He rubbed the cloth on his dirty clothes, and they became clean and bright! Tim was so happy that he could wear his favorite clothes again. He ran to his mom and said, "Look, Mom! My clothes are clean now!" His mom was surprised and happy for him.

One day, a cheerful dog named Spot went outside to play. He loved to run and jump with his strong legs. The sun was shining, and Spot was very happy. As Spot played, he saw a big puddle. He jumped into the puddle and splashed around. Suddenly, the water started to freeze. Spot's legs were stuck in the ice! He tried to move, but he could not. Spot's friend, a kind bird, saw him and knew he needed help. The bird flew fast to find the sun. The sun came closer and melted the ice. Spot's legs were free again! He was so thankful for his friend and the sun. Spot, the bird, and the sun played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lily. She liked to gather pretty stones with her friends. One day, they went to the beach to find more pretty stones. At the beach, Lily saw a big stone in the sand. She picked it up in her palm. It was very nice and shiny. Lily was happy she found a new pretty stone. But then, a big wave came and took Lily's stone away. Lily was sad. She tried to find her nice stone, but it was gone. The bad ending made Lily and her friends go home with no new pretty stones.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play at the park. The park has a long slide. Anna and Ben want to go on the slide. "Let's prepare to go on the slide," Anna says. "We need to climb the stairs and sit on the top." "OK," Ben says. "But I am a little scared. The slide is very long and fast." Anna holds Ben's hand. "Don't be scared," she says. "I will go with you. It will be fun." They climb the stairs and sit on the top. They look at the slide. It is shiny and smooth. "Are you ready?" Anna asks. "Yes, I am ready," Ben says. They hold hands and slide down. They feel the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They laugh and scream. They reach the bottom and jump off. They are happy and proud. "That was awesome!" Anna says. "That was awesome!" Ben says. They hug and run to the slide again. They want to go on the slide again and again. They have a lot of fun at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Amy. She owned a rug that was big, warm and reliable. Every day she would play on it and feel safe and happy. One morning, Amy decided she wanted to take her rug outside and play in the garden. She dragged her rug all the way outside and was having great fun playing on it. But then she got careless and left it out in the rain. The next morning, Amy's rug was all wet and soggy. She tried to dry it in the sun but it was too late. It was ruined. Amy was sad that her rug was broken, but she learned a valuable lesson. If you want to keep something safe, make sure it's stored in a safe place. No matter how reliable something is, it can't always protect itself.
