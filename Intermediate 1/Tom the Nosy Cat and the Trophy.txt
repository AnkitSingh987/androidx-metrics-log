Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Nosy Cat and the Trophy

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and jump around. One day, Tom saw a shiny trophy on a high shelf. He wanted to see it up close. Tom jumped up and tried to grab the trophy. But he was too small. He found a string and had a plan. Tom cut the string with his paw. Then, he tied it to the trophy's handle. Tom pulled the string and the trophy fell. It didn't break, and Tom was happy. He looked at the shiny trophy and played with it all day. The nosy cat had lots of fun with his new toy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice horse named Tom. He lived in a stable. One day, Tom wanted to go out and play. Tom went out and saw his friend, the cat. The cat said, "Let's play!" They played and ran. But then, Tom stumbled on a rock. "Ouch!" he said. Tom was sad. The cat said, "Don't be sad, Tom. Let's go back to the stable and rest." Tom and the cat went back. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind, honest little boy called Jack. One day, Jack decided to invite his friend, Sarah, over to play. Jack was very excited and he couldn’t wait. So he quickly and carefully chose a nice path to take to Sarah’s house. He had a cane to help him walk, so he held it tight in his hand as he skipped down the path and smiled all the way to Sarah’s house. When Jack arrived, Sarah was delighted and she asked him, “What took you so long?” Jack said, in a very honest voice, “I was just looking for the best path to take to see you!” Together, Jack and Sarah laughed and played all day, until it was time for Jack to return home. Jack gave Sarah a big hug and said, “I’m so happy you invited me to come and play. See you soon!”

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to eat melon. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom to buy a big, sweet melon. When they got to the store, Tim saw a melon that seemed to be the best one. He wanted to pick it up, but it was too big for his small hands. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it. Tim felt embarrassed because he could not pick up the melon. His mom saw him and came to help. She picked up the melon and put it in their cart. Tim smiled and thanked his mom. They went home and shared the sweet melon together. Tim was happy, and he did not feel embarrassed anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a selfish cat named Tom. Tom did not like to share his toys with other animals. One day, Tom met a wise bird named Sally. Sally told Tom about a special law that could reverse his selfish ways. Sally said, "Tom, if you share your toys with others, the law will make you feel happy inside." Tom thought about it and decided to try it. He shared his toys with his friends, and just like Sally said, he felt happy inside. From that day on, Tom was no longer a selfish cat. He shared his toys with all his friends and they all played together. Tom thanked Sally for teaching him about the special law. They became best friends and lived happily ever after.

Tom was playing in the garden with his ball. He liked to kick the ball and run after it. He saw a big flower and wanted to smell it. He went near the flower and saw a small insect on it. The insect had wings and a black and yellow body. It was a bee. Tom was curious and touched the bee. The bee did not like that and stung Tom on his finger. Tom felt a sharp pain and cried out loud. He dropped the ball and ran to his mom. He was fearful of the bee and his finger hurt a lot. Mom heard Tom's cry and came to him. She saw the bee sting on his finger and took him inside the house. She put some ice on his finger and gave him a hug. She told him that the bee was not bad, but it was scared of Tom and tried to protect itself. She said that Tom should be careful and not touch insects that can sting. She comforted Tom and made him feel better. Tom stopped crying and thanked his mom. He learned a lesson and decided to be more gentle with the insects in the garden. He still liked to play with his ball, but he also liked to watch the bees and the flowers from a distance. He was not fearful of the bee anymore, but he respected it.
