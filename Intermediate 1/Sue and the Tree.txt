Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a fair land, there was a big, big tree. Under the tree, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw and print. She liked to print pretty things like stars, hearts, and flowers. One day, Sue found a shiny diamond on the ground. She picked it up and said, "Wow, what a pretty diamond! I want to print a picture with it." So, Sue took the diamond and started to print a picture on the tree. A little boy named Tim saw Sue and asked, "What are you doing?" Sue said, "I am printing a picture with this diamond." Tim said, "Can I help you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's print together." Sue and Tim printed many pictures on the tree with the diamond. They had a lot of fun and became good friends. The tree looked so pretty with all the pictures they made. And they lived happily ever after, always playing and printing together.

Once upon a time there was a fox called Fuzzy. Fuzzy was alone in the woods. He was looking for a place to escape and explore. He carefully studied the surrounding area, searching for a way out. Suddenly, a squirrel called Scamp dashed across his path - he was being very bossy. Fuzzy shouted, "Hey! Who are you?" Scamp replied, "I'm Scamp. Who are you?" Fuzzy then said, "I'm Fuzzy. What are you doing here?" Scamp said, "I'm trying to find a way out of this woods. Wanna come with me?" Fuzzy smiled and said, "Yes, of course I would!" So the two friends worked together to find an escape route and soon found their way out of the woods. They had fun and learnt a lot while they studied their new surroundings together.

Tom and Lily are playing in the garden. They see a big fire in the sky. It is the sun. The sun is very hot and bright. "Look, Lily, the sun is burning!" Tom says. "Yes, Tom, the sun is burning. It makes the day warm and light," Lily says. They hear a loud noise. It is a plane. The plane is flying in the sky. It has wings and a tail. It makes a zoom sound. "Wow, Lily, the plane is flying! It makes a noise!" Tom says. "Yes, Tom, the plane is flying. It makes a noise. It goes very fast and far," Lily says. They see a dog. It is their dog. His name is Spot. He is brown and white. He has a collar and a tag. He is friendly and loyal. "Hi, Spot, the dog is here! He is reliable!" Tom says. "Yes, Tom, the dog is here! He is reliable! He loves us and protects us," Lily says. They hug the dog. The dog licks their faces. He wags his tail. He barks happily. They are happy. They have the sun, the plane and the dog. They have fun in the garden.

Once upon a time, there was a creative kid named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and make up fun games. One day, Tim wanted to play a new game with his friend, Sam. But Sam did not want to play the new game. This made Tim sad. Tim thought of a plan to make Sam want to play. He took Sam's toy car and shut it inside a big box. Sam looked for his toy car but could not find it. Sam asked Tim, "Where is my toy car?" Tim said, "I have shut your toy car in this box. If you play my new game with me, I will give it back to you." Sam agreed to play the new game with Tim. They played the game and had lots of fun. After the game, Tim opened the box and gave Sam his toy car back. Sam was happy and they both learned that trying new things can be fun.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo was very excited because he learned a new language. This language was the language of jokes. Momo loved to joke and make his friends laugh. One sunny day, Momo went to play with his friends. He saw his friend, Toto the elephant, looking sad. Momo thought, "I will tell Toto a joke in my new language, and he will laugh and be happy again." So, Momo went to Toto and told him a funny joke. Toto laughed so hard that his big ears flapped like wings. He was not sad anymore. Momo was happy that his new language of jokes made his friend happy. From that day on, Momo and Toto played and joked together, and they were the best of friends.
