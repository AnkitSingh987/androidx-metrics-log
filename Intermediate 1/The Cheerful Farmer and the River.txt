Category: Intermediate
Title: The Cheerful Farmer and the River

Once upon a time there was a cheerful farmer. He lived near a beautiful river and took care of his animals. Every day, he woke up bright and early to attend to his farm. One day, he noticed dark clouds rolling in and an eerie chill in the air. His animals kept looking into the sky and making alarmed noises. He knew that something was about to happen and he needed to act fast. He moved his animals far away from the river and then went to take a look himself. The river was overflowing and he realized a flood was coming! He did his best to move away anything that could be destroyed. But it was too late, the flooding river destroyed his farm. The once cheerful farmer was devastated. But he knew that he needed to keep going and he rebuilt his farm as soon as he could. The end.

One day, a big dog named Max had a good idea. He wanted to lay in the sun and play with his ball. So, Max went outside and found a nice, warm spot to lay down. As Max lay in the sun, he saw a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, red ball. Max thought it was a good idea to play with the ball. So, Max went to Lily and asked her to play. Lily and Max played with the ball all day. They had so much fun! When the sun went down, Max and Lily were tired. They lay on the grass and looked at the sky. It was a good day with a good idea.

Ben and his dad were outside playing in the yard. Dad picked up a stick and said to Ben, "I'm going to touch the fire." Ben was scared and said, "No, Dad! It's too important!" But even though Ben was scared, Dad touched the fire. The fire was so hot that it quickly spread. Ben and Dad ran inside to save themselves but they couldn't get away in time. The fire spread across the house and it was very bad. Ben and Dad were very sad and scared. Dad said, "I'm so sorry Ben. This was my fault." They both hugged and sadly looked at the house. Everything was gone and there was nothing they could do.

Lily and Ben were playing with their crayons. They liked to draw on paper, but sometimes they also drew on the wall. Their mom did not like that. She said it was crazy and bad. One day, they had a new idea. They wanted to draw on their clothes. They took off their shirts and pants and used their crayons to make colorful strips on them. They thought they looked very pretty and funny. "Look at me, Lily!" Ben said. "I have red and blue and green strips on my shirt. I look like a rainbow!" "You look silly, Ben!" Lily said. "But I look sillier. I have yellow and pink and purple strips on my pants. I look like a candy!" They laughed and danced around the room. They were very happy with their art. They did not hear their mom coming. "Ben! Lily! What are you doing?" their mom shouted. She saw their clothes and their crayons and their wall. She was very angry and sad. "You ruined your clothes and the wall! You are crazy and naughty!" She took away their crayons and made them put on clean clothes. She made them help her clean the wall. She said they had to say sorry and never do that again. Ben and Lily felt bad. They did not mean to make their mom angry and sad. They said sorry and hugged her. They learned their lesson. They did not succeed in their plan. They decided to only draw on paper from then on.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy box full of toys. One day, he wanted to unpack his toy box to play with his toys. While Tim was unpacking, he found a broken toy missile. He was sad that it was broken. He showed it to his mom and said, "Mom, my missile is broken. Can you fix it?" His mom looked at the broken missile and said, "Yes, Tim. I can fix it for you." She took some glue and fixed the broken missile. Tim was very happy. After that, Tim and his mom played with the fixed missile together. They had a lot of fun. When they were done playing, Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for fixing my missile. I love playing with you." The end.
