Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an amazing big wardrobe in his room. He loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim's mom told him a secret. She said, "If you wish very hard, your wardrobe can become something special." Tim was excited and started to think about what his wardrobe could become. The next day, Tim wished very hard. He opened the wardrobe and found a door inside. He went through the door and found a magical land with trees, flowers, and animals. Tim played with his new friends all day. The wardrobe had become a door to a magical world, just like his mom said.

One day, a playful cat named Kitty saw a big knot on a tree. Kitty wanted to play with the knot. She asked her friend, Doggy, "Can I play with the knot?" Doggy said, "Yes, you can play with it. But be careful, the tree might not allow it." Kitty did not listen to Doggy. She jumped on the tree and started to play with the knot. But something unexpected happened! The knot began to move and talk. It was not a knot; it was a little bird hiding in the tree. The bird said, "Hello, Kitty! I was hiding in the tree. I am sorry if I scared you." Kitty was surprised but happy to meet a new friend. From that day on, Kitty, Doggy, and the little bird played together every day.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But they saw a big dog near the fence. The dog was barking and growling. It had sharp teeth and angry eyes. Lily and Tom were frightened. They wanted to go back to their mom. But the dog saw them and ran towards them. It jumped over the fence and chased them. Lily and Tom screamed and ran. They tried to resist the dog, but it was too fast and strong. The dog caught Tom and bit his leg. Tom cried and bled. Lily tried to help him, but the dog bit her too. She cried and bled. The dog did not stop. It bit them more and more. Lily and Tom stopped crying. They stopped moving. They stopped breathing. The park was silent. The dog was full. It licked its lips and walked away. No one came to help Lily and Tom. They were gone. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wolf. The wolf was not mean, but very useful. He liked to help his friends in the forest. One day, the wolf found a big rock. He wanted to weigh it. He tried to lift the rock but it was very heavy. He asked his friend, the strong bear, to help him. The bear helped the wolf to weigh the rock. The wolf and the bear found out the rock was very, very heavy. They decided to use the rock to make a nice seat for all their friends in the forest. The useful wolf was happy to help his friends and they all sat on the rock together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to dance and play with her friends. One sunny day, Lucy and her friends went to play in the park. They were very happy. While they were playing, they saw a small, frightened cat. The cat wanted to escape from a big dog. The big dog was chasing the cat, and the cat was scared. Lucy and her friends wanted to help the cat. Lucy had an idea. She told her friends to dance and sing loudly. The big dog stopped chasing the cat and looked at the children. The dog liked their dance and started to dance too. The cat was able to escape, and it was safe. The big dog was not mean after all; it just wanted to play and dance with everyone. So, Lucy, her friends, the cat, and the big dog all danced together, and they had a great time.

One day, a lively dog named Spot wanted to go on a trip. He had a friend who was a driver. Spot asked his friend, "Can you take me on a trip?" His friend said, "Yes, but you have to pack your things first." Spot went home to pack his things. He put his ball, his bone, and his bed in a big bag. He was very excited for his trip. Spot went back to his friend and said, "I am ready to go!" They started their trip in the big car. Spot had a lot of fun looking out the window. But then, the car stopped. The driver said, "Oh no, the car is broken." Spot was sad because they could not go on their trip. They had to go back home and Spot did not get to play with his ball, his bone, or sleep in his bed on the trip.
