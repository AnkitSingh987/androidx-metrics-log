Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Faucet

One day, a little boy named Tim saw an enormous faucet in his yard. He was very curious about it. He asked his mom, "What is this big thing?" His mom said, "That is a faucet, Tim. We use it to get water." Tim wanted to help his mom, so he decided to clean the faucet. He got a cloth and some soap. He rubbed the soap on the cloth and started to clean the faucet. It became shiny and clean. His mom saw the clean faucet and was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim! You did a great job cleaning the faucet." Tim smiled and felt proud that he could help his mom. They both went inside to have some cookies and milk.

Once upon a time there lived a great little mouse. He loved playing and exploring everywhere he could. One day he saw a trap. It was made of metal and had a lock on the front. He had never seen anything like it before. He was curious and carefully he hopped closer to take a closer look. The mouse started to play with the lock and before he knew it, he felt himself falling. The trap had closed and he was stuck inside! He was so scared and he started to cry. But then he heard a voice from somewhere nearby. It was a cat. She said: "Don't worry, little mouse. I know how to unlock the trap." The cat carefully unlocked the trap. Once it was open, the mouse was free again. He thanked her for helping and the cat said "you're welcome". The mouse ran off, safe and sound. From then on, he always knew to stay away from traps and locks!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals like a big lion, a tall giraffe, and a funny monkey. Tim was very happy to see all the animals. Tim's mom told him to be obedient and stay close to her. Tim listened and held his mom's hand. They walked and saw a man cutting grass near the animals. The man waved at Tim, and Tim waved back. At the end of the day, Tim and his mom left the zoo. Tim was a good and obedient boy. He enjoyed the day with the animals and the man who cut the grass. Tim couldn't wait to go back to the zoo again.

Once upon a time, there lived a fox. One day, the fox heard a noise in the distance and stopped to listen. It was very quiet, so the fox was very alert. He paused and waited to see what it might be. Soon, he spotted a small rabbit hopping through the bushes. The fox was excited, so he decided to follow. He followed the rabbit for a long time, staying very close, but being very quiet. Finally, the rabbit stopped. He waited and paused, and he could see that the fox was still alert. Then, the rabbit heard a noise in the bushes and quickly ran away. The fox was disappointed, but he was also excited to have seen the rabbit. He paused and looked around, taking in the quiet of the day. Then, he smiled and went on his way.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played all day long and had lots of fun. One day, while they were playing, they heard a strange noise coming from the kitchen. They went to see what it was. They found out that the noise was coming from the freezer. Tom and Sue opened the freezer and found a little bird inside. The bird was cold and scared. Tom and Sue decided to help the bird. They took it out of the freezer and warmed it up. The bird was so happy that it started to sing a beautiful song. Tom, Sue, and the bird became good friends. They learned that helping others can make you feel good and happy.

Tom and Sue are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see big clouds in the sky. They hear loud wind and rain. They are scared. "Is this a hurricane?" Tom asks Sue. "I don't know. Let's go inside," Sue says. They run to Sue's house. They knock on the door. Sue's mom opens the door. She is jolly. "Hello, Tom and Sue. Come in, come in. Don't be afraid. It's just a storm," she says. She gives them hot chocolate and cookies. They sit on the couch and watch cartoons. They feel warm and cozy. The storm passes. The sun comes out. Tom and Sue go outside again. They see a big rainbow in the sky. They smile and hug. "Look, Sue. The storm is gone. The rainbow is beautiful," Tom says. "Yes, Tom. It is. I'm glad we are friends," Sue says. They hold hands and stand under the rainbow. They are happy.
