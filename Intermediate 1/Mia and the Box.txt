Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Box

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw her friend, Tim, playing with a ball. Mia went to Tim and said, "Hi, can I play with you?" Tim was happy and replied, "Yes, let's play!" They played with the ball and had lots of fun. After some time, they saw an empty box on the ground. Mia said, "Look, an empty box! What can we do with it?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's make a game!" They found some paper and a stamp. They stamped the paper and put it in the empty box. Mia said, "Now we can play a new game! We can take turns picking a paper from the box and do what the stamp says." Tim replied, "That sounds fun!" Mia and Tim played their new game and had a great day at the park. They were very happy that they found the empty box and the stamp.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a box. In the box, there was a glove. The glove was very special. It could keep things safe and warm. One day, a little girl found the glove. She put it on her hand. She felt happy. The glove could keep her hand warm. The little girl wanted to show her mom. The little girl went to her mom. She said, "Look, Mom! I found a glove. It keeps my hand warm." Her mom smiled. She said, "That is a nice glove. Keep it safe and tidy in your room." The little girl went back to her room. She put the glove back in the box. She knew it was important to keep the glove tidy. The glove would keep her hand warm on cold days. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a man who wanted to replace his old boat. He wanted something new and exciting to ride out on the waves. One day, he heard about a special boat that was very strong and fast. It was made out of a shark which made it even more exciting. The man wanted to buy the shark boat but he was a little scared. He was worried that it would be too hard to control an animal like a shark. His friends told him not to worry, that the shark was very friendly. So the brave man decided to give it a go. He took the shark boat out on the water and was amazed at how fast it went! But then, he heard a terrible noise. He looked down and saw a big shark swimming right beside him. The man got so scared, he thought his heart was going to pop right out of his chest! He quickly jumped out of the boat and frantically called his friends to come help him. His friends came to his rescue and they were able to replace the shark boat with a more suitable boat. After that, the man was very careful when choosing new boats - he never wanted to feel that terrified again!

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Tom. He loved to mix things in a big mug. One day, Tom found a shiny red box under his bed. He opened the box and saw a small bag of magic dust. Tom had an idea. Tom took the magic dust and mixed it in his big mug. He mixed and mixed until it was all gone. Tom didn't know what would happen, but he was very excited. He waited and waited for something to happen. Suddenly, the big mug started to shake and glow. Tom was so surprised! Out of the mug came a big, friendly bear. The bear could talk and play games with Tom. They became best friends and had lots of fun together. The magic dust had made Tom's wish come true.

Once upon a time, there was a messy princess named Lily. She loved to play in the mud and slide down the hills. One day, while sliding down her favorite hill, she saw a big, scary monster. The monster was blocking her way, and she couldn't slide anymore. The princess was sad and said, "Please, Mr. Monster, let me slide down the hill. It's my favorite thing to do." But the monster shook his head and said, "No, I won't let you slide. You are too messy, and I don't like it." Princess Lily thought for a moment and then said, "If I clean up and become neat, will you let me slide?" The monster nodded. So, the princess went home, took a bath, and put on clean clothes. When she came back, the monster saw that she was neat and let her slide down the hill. They both became friends and played together every day.
