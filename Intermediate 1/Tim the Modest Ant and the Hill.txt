Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Modest Ant and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a modest ant named Tim. Tim lived in a big hill with his ant friends. They all worked together to find food and help each other. One day, Tim and his friends found a piece of leather. They wanted to take it back to their hill. So, they all lined up and started to march. They marched and marched, carrying the leather together. After a long time, they finally reached their hill. They were very happy and proud of their work. Tim and his friends had a big party to celebrate. And from that day on, they all knew that working together made things easier.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very curious and always wanted to learn new things. One day, Jane asked her mommy, "What do you do all day, mommy?" Her mommy smiled and said, "I'm a mechanic. I make things with my hands!" Jane was so excited to learn that she asked, "Can I be a mechanic too?" Her mommy said, "Of course! You can do anything you set your mind to." So Jane went to the store and found some tools. She grabbed some screwdrivers, a wrench, and a hammer. As she was putting them in her basket, she saw some other kids playing outside. She decided to join in and became the most lively mechanic in the group. Together, the kids fixed toys and built new things. At the end of the day, Jane was happy and learned so much! From that day forward, she was always in her workshop tinkering and fixing things. She had become a real mechanic!

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive fish. This fish had a friend, a small fish. They liked to play in the water together. One day, they saw a shiny thing in the water. It was a spear! The big fish said, "I want to play with the spear!" The small fish was scared and said, "No, it might hurt us. Let's go away." But the big fish did not listen. He swam fast to get the spear. When the big fish touched the spear, it made a loud noise. The big fish got very scared and swam away fast. The small fish laughed and said, "I told you so!" The big fish felt embarrass. From that day on, the big fish listened to the small fish and they played safely in the water.

Ben and Lily were playing in the park. They liked to slide on the big metal slide. It was shiny and smooth. Sometimes they saw their faces in the metal. "Look, Lily, I can see you!" Ben said, pointing to the slide. "Me too, Ben, I can see you!" Lily said, smiling. They took turns sliding down the slide. They laughed and had fun. But then they saw a little boy crying on a bench. He had a toy car in his hands. It was broken. The metal was bent and the wheels were off. "Who are you?" Ben asked the boy. "I'm Sam. I was playing with my car, but it fell and broke. Now I have no car to play with," the boy said, sobbing. Ben and Lily felt sorry for Sam. They wanted to help him. "Here, Sam, you can have my car," Ben said, giving him his red car. It was not broken. It was shiny and fast. "Really? Thank you, Ben, you are very kind!" Sam said, wiping his tears. "And you can have my doll," Lily said, giving him her yellow doll. It was not broken. It was soft and pretty. "Thank you, Lily, you are very kind too!" Sam said, smiling. Sam played with the car and the doll. He felt happy. Ben and Lily played with him. They became friends. They shared their toys and had fun. The sun shone on them. They were kind and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a friend named Tom. They liked to play together in the park. One day, they saw an enormous tree. It was very big and tall. Lily and Tom wanted to climb the tree. They went to the tree and looked up. They started to discuss how they could climb the tree. They thought about it and talked about it. They decided to help each other. Lily climbed up first, and Tom helped her. Then, Tom climbed up too. They were so happy to be in the enormous tree together. They laughed and played. And from that day on, Lily and Tom became the best of friends.
