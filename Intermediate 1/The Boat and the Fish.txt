Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boat and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big boat. It had a big anchor. One day, the boat went on an interesting trip. The boat met a fish in the water. The fish was cold and shivering. "Hello, fish! Why do you shiver?" asked the boat. The fish looked at the boat and said, "I am cold. The water is very cold today." The boat wanted to help the fish. So, the boat put down its big anchor. The anchor made the water warmer. The fish stopped shivering and started to smile. "Thank you, boat! You made me warm and happy," said the fish. The boat and the fish became good friends. They had fun together on their interesting trip. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and color with his crayons. His favorite color was red. Tim was a very comfortable boy in his small room, and he liked to wear his soft pajamas. One day, Tim's mommy told him to behave and be a good boy while she went to the store. Tim said, "Okay, mommy!" and he promised to be good. He played with his toys and colored with his crayons while his mommy was gone. When Tim's mommy came back, she saw that Tim had behaved very well. He had not made a mess and had put all his toys away. Tim's mommy was very happy and gave him a big hug. She told him he was a good boy and that she was proud of him.

One day, a little boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, long stick on the ground. Tom wanted to lift the stick, but it was too heavy for him. He asked his mom, "Can you help me lift the stick, please?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tom!" Together, they lifted the long stick and carried it to a big tree. Tom wanted to make a stay for his toy car. He put the stick on the tree and put his toy car on it. The car rolled down the stick very fast. Tom was so happy to see his car go so fast. But then, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came out of the tree and picked up the stick. The squirrel lifted the stick with its tiny paws and ran away. Tom and his mom were very surprised. They laughed and said, "Wow, that squirrel is very strong!" Tom didn't mind that the squirrel took the stick, because he had a fun day at the park with his mom.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy new pants for Tim. Tim thought pants were crazy. He only liked to wear shorts. But his mom said he needed pants for when it got cold outside. At the store, Tim saw many pants. His mom told him to count all the pants he liked. Tim counted one, two, three, four pants he liked. But he still thought pants were crazy. He wanted to wear shorts all the time. As they were about to leave, Tim saw a pair of pants that looked different. They were not like the other pants. These pants could change into shorts! Tim was so happy. He told his mom, "I want these pants!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, let's get them." Now Tim had pants for the cold days and shorts for the warm days. He thought these pants were not crazy at all.

One day, a yellow dragon named Sunny wandered into a big forest. He was looking for new friends to play with. The forest was full of trees and pretty flowers. Sunny was very happy to be there. As Sunny wandered, he met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Blue! Do you want to play with me?" Sunny was very excited and said, "Yes, I want to play with you!" Sunny and Blue played all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. They became the best of friends. At the end of the day, they were tired but very happy. They knew they would always be friends and play together in the big forest.
