Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl Finds the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, it was very hot, so Sue wanted to find some shade to cool down. She saw a big tree with lots of shade under it. Sue was glad that she found the perfect spot to rest. Sue had a toy bear named Bob. She took Bob out to play with her under the tree. Sue put Bob on the soft grass and said, "Bob, we can play here in the shade. It's not hot here." Bob just smiled at her, happy to be out of the sun. As they played, a little boy named Tom came by. He saw Sue and Bob under the tree and asked, "Can I play with you in the shade?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, come and sit with us. We are glad to have you here!" Tom put his toy car next to Bob, and they all played happily together in the shade.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was feeling miserable one day. She did not want to go to bed. Sue's mom said, "You can stay up if you write a story." Sue was happy. She got paper and a pen. She started to write a story about a big, happy dog. The dog was very playful. The dog loved to run and jump. As Sue wrote, something unexpected happened. The big, happy dog jumped out of her story! He was real now! Sue and the dog played together all night. They had so much fun. Sue was not miserable anymore. She was happy to go to bed with her new friend, the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a big, shiny moon in the sky. Every night, the moon smiled down at the people on the ground. One night, a little girl named Maggie was out in her garden, looking at the moon. She thought it looked so bright and beautiful that she decided she wanted to keep it for herself. So, Maggie stretched out her arms to try and grab it from the sky. But no matter how hard Maggie tried, the moon stayed in the sky and didn't drop at all. Maggie was getting frustrated, so she called out to the moon: "Drop down here, moon! I want to keep you with me forever!" But the moon just kept on smiling at her. Maggie soon came to realize that nothing ever lasts forever, so she stopped trying to keep the moon and smiled back at it. Moral: Some things just can't be kept forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big castle. A little girl named Lily lived in the castle. She had a small dog named Max. They loved to play together. One day, Max started to complain. "Woof, woof!" he said. Lily asked, "Max, why are you sad?" Max looked at his empty bowl. "Oh, I am sorry, Max. I forgot to feed you," said Lily. She quickly gave Max some food, and he was happy again. Later, they went outside to play. They ran and jumped in the warm sun. Lily said, "Max, I am glad you are not sad anymore." Max barked happily, and they played until the sun went down. They went back to the castle, and they both slept very well that night.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a guard named Tim. He was a very nice guard who took care of the people in the town. One day, Tim was walking down the street when he saw a little girl named Lily. She was upset and crying. "Hi Lily, why are you upset?" asked Tim. Lily looked at Tim and said, "I wanted to pour water into my cup, but the cup fell and broke. Now I can't drink water and I'm thirsty." Tim wanted to help Lily feel better. So, he went to the store and bought a new cup. He then went to the well in the town and poured some water into the cup. He gave the cup to Lily and she drank the water. Lily stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me!" Tim was happy that he could help Lily. From that day on, they became good friends and always took care of each other.
