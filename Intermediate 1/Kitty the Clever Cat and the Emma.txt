Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Clever Cat and the Emma

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Emma. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Emma got sick. She needed to take medicine to feel better. But she didn't like the taste of the medicine. So, Kitty had an idea. She said, "Emma, let's wrap the medicine in a yummy treat. Then you won't taste it!" Emma thought that was a great idea. Kitty found a piece of cheese and wrapped the medicine inside. Emma ate the cheese and didn't even taste the medicine. She felt better very soon. Emma said, "Thank you, Kitty! You are so clever!" They hugged and played together, happy to have each other.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play pretend. One day, they decided to be kings and queens and build a palace with their blocks. They worked hard and made a big and pretty palace with many rooms and towers. "Wow, our palace is so nice!" Lily said. "We can have a tea party inside and invite our dolls and teddies." "Good idea!" Tom said. "I will go and get some yummy cookies and juice from the kitchen. You stay here and guard the palace." Lily nodded and smiled. She went inside the palace and set up a table and chairs with her toys. She was very happy and proud of their palace. But while Tom was gone, a big and mean dog came into the room. He saw the palace and wanted to play with it. He ran towards it and jumped on it. He knocked down the blocks and made a big mess. He also saw the cookies and juice and ate them all. Lily heard the noise and came out of the palace. She saw the dog and the mess and started to cry. "No, no, no! Go away, bad dog! You ruined our palace and our tea party!" The dog barked and wagged his tail. He thought Lily wanted to play with him. He licked her face and tried to take her doll. Lily screamed and pushed the dog away. She ran to the door and called for Tom. "Tom, Tom, help me! There is a bad dog here!" Tom heard Lily and came back with more cookies and juice. He saw the dog and the mess and was very angry and sad. He threw the cookies and juice at the dog and shouted. "Go away, bad dog! You are not our friend! You are a mean and naughty dog!" The dog was scared and confused. He ran away from the room and hid under the sofa. He did not understand why Tom and Lily were so mad at him. He just wanted to have fun. Tom and Lily hugged each other and cried. They looked at their broken palace and their spilled tea party. They could not fix it or solve it. They had no more blocks or cookies or juice. They had no more fun. They had a bad day.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little cat. The cat loved to play with a soft sheet. Every day, the cat would jump and squeeze the sheet with its tiny paws. It was so much fun! One sunny day, the cat was playing with the sheet outside. Suddenly, the wind blew very hard. The sheet flew up into the air! The cat tried to catch it, but the sheet went too high. The cat was sad. Just then, a friendly bird saw the cat and the sheet. The bird flew up and grabbed the sheet with its beak. The bird brought the sheet back down to the cat. The cat was so happy! Now, the cat and the bird play with the sheet together. They became the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very excited. She wanted to write a poem. She thought about what to write. She thought about the sun, the moon, and the stars. She thought about the trees, the flowers, and the bees. She thought about her family and her friends. Sue sat down and started to write her poem. She wrote about the sun shining bright. She wrote about the moon and stars at night. She wrote about the trees that yield fruit. She wrote about the flowers that make her smile. She wrote about her family and friends who love her. When Sue finished her poem, she was very happy. She read it to her mom and dad. They loved it too. They were so proud of Sue. Sue felt good because she made her family happy with her poem. She knew that her poem was special and she wanted to write more.
