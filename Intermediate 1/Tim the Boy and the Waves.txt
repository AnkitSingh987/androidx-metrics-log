Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Waves

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They saw big waves in the water. Tim liked to play in the waves. He spread his arms wide and jumped into the water. The waves splashed him and made him laugh. Tim's mom had a big, sour lemon in her bag. She said, "Tim, do you want to try this sour lemon?" Tim made a funny face and said, "No, thank you, Mom. I don't like sour things." Tim and his mom played in the waves all day. They spread their arms and jumped into the water together. As the sun went down, they said goodbye to the waves and went home. They had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl. She was playing by herself on a sunny day. She saw something shiny, and it was an elevator! She was excited and ran to it. When she got to the elevator, she tried to reach the buttons, but they were too high. The girl started to cry. Suddenly, a big man saw the girl and he smiled. He bent down and pressed the button for the girl. The elevator doors opened and the man said "Go in, it is smooth inside". The girl got in the elevator and it started to move. She was very scared but she felt the man's hand on her shoulder. It made her feel safe. She looked out of the door and saw the numbers going up and up! When the elevator stopped, the girl opened the doors and stepped out. She was so happy that she gave the man a big hug and ran off. She looked around and saw her mom. She ran over and hugged her mother. Her mother said, "Did you have a nice ride in the elevator?" The girl smiled and nodded with a big tear on her cheek.

One day, a wolf was walking in the woods. He saw a book on the ground. The wolf could read, so he picked up the book. It was a big, filthy book. He started to read the book out loud. As the wolf read, a magic thing happened. The wolf turned into a little bunny. The bunny was very sad because he wanted to be a wolf again. He looked at the book and tried to read it again to change back. But the bunny could not read like the wolf could. He hopped and hopped, trying to find someone to help him read the book. Then, a nice bird saw the bunny and the filthy book. The bird could read too! The bird read the book to the bunny, and the bunny turned back into the wolf. The wolf was so happy and thanked the bird. They became best friends and read more books together.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy boat. The boat had a big sail and a bow. The boat was dull, but Tim liked it. He wanted to play with it in the water. So, Tim went to the lake with the boat. Tim put the boat in the water. He watched it sail away. The wind blew and the boat went fast. Tim was happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The boat hit a big rock in the water. The boat did not sink. Instead, the boat changed! It was not dull anymore. It was bright and shiny. The sail was now red, and the bow was blue. Tim was very surprised. He could not believe his eyes. The boat sailed back to Tim, and he played with it all day.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a big gray elephant named Ellie. Ellie was very anxious because she wanted to learn how to whistle. She saw her bird friends whistle all the time, and it made her happy. Ellie wanted to be like her bird friends and make happy sounds too. One day, Ellie asked her bird friend, Blue, to teach her how to whistle. Blue was a kind and friendly bird. He said, "Yes, I will help you!" So, Blue showed Ellie how to put her lips together and blow air out. Ellie tried and tried, but she could not whistle. Ellie was sad, but she did not give up. She kept trying to whistle every day. Then, one day, something amazing happened. Ellie blew air out of her long nose, and she made a loud whistle sound. She was so happy! Ellie thanked her friend Blue for helping her learn how to whistle. From that day on, Ellie the elephant and her bird friends would whistle together and make happy sounds in the zoo. They had lots of fun, and everyone who came to the zoo loved to hear their happy whistling.
