Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Jolly Dog and the Park

Once there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day with his friends. One day, Spot saw a sign near the park. The sign said, "No Dogs Allowed." Spot was sad and started to complain. Spot's friend, a kind bird named Blue, heard him complain. Blue said, "Don't be sad, Spot. Let's find a new place to play!" So, Spot and Blue went to look for a new place. They found a big, green field with lots of space to play. Spot and Blue played in the new field every day. They had so much fun and made new friends. Soon, Spot forgot about the sign and didn't complain anymore. The moral of the story is: don't complain about what you can't change; find a new way to be happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box of junk in his room. He did not know what was inside, but he was very excited to find out. He opened the box and saw many things: old toys, clothes, and a big, red ball. Tim thought this was brilliant! Tim's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She said, "Oh no, Tim! What is all this junk?" Tim smiled and said, "I found a big box, Mom! Look at this big, red ball!" His mom looked at the ball and said, "That is a brilliant find, Tim. But we need to clean up this mess." Together, Tim and his mom put away the junk. They found a special place for the big, red ball. Tim said, "Thank you, Mom, for helping me clean up." His mom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim." Later that day, Tim and his mom went to the park. Tim took his big, red ball with him. He played with it all day, throwing it and kicking it. He was so happy he found the brilliant ball in the box of junk. As the sun went down, Tim and his mom walked home. Tim held his big, red ball and said, "I love my ball, Mom. Thank you for helping me find it." His mom hugged him and said, "I'm glad you found it too, Tim. It was a brilliant day!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to throw rocks into the ocean. Every day, he would go to the beach and find the perfect rock to throw. Tim was very intelligent, and he knew which rocks would make the biggest splash. One day, while Tim was at the beach, he saw a big, shiny rock. He knew this rock would make the biggest splash ever. So, he picked it up and got ready to throw it. Just before he threw the rock, a little fish jumped out of the ocean and said, "Please don't throw that rock! It's a special rock that keeps the ocean safe and happy." Tim listened to the fish and put the rock back. The fish thanked him and swam away. The next day, the ocean was full of happy fish and other sea animals. They all came to thank Tim for being intelligent and listening to the little fish. From that day on, Tim knew that sometimes the best thing to do was to not throw a rock at all.

Once upon a time, there was a bear. It was so big and strong it made the other animals feel a bit fearful when they saw it. One day the bear was walking through the woods, looking for someplace to rest. After walking for a while, the bear found a nice soft patch of grass. It lay down to rest and stretched out its long arms and legs. Suddenly the bear noticed something strange. There was something small and shiny beneath one of its paws. He looked closer and saw it was a tiny little inchworm. The inchworm was very scared and couldn’t stop shaking. The bear was gentle and careful not to hurt it. The bear stayed very still and slowly reached out his giant paw. He slowly touched the little inchworm with his warm furry paw and the inchworm stopped shaking. The inchworm was no longer scared, it felt safe and happy. The bear then closed its eyes and went to sleep, knowing the inchworm was safe.
