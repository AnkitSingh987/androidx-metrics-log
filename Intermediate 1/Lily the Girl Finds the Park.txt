Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and make her friends smile. One day, Lily saw a new girl at the park. The new girl looked sad and lonely. Lily wanted to make the new girl smile, so she walked up to her and gave her a big hug. The new girl was surprised and a little embarrassed, but she smiled back at Lily. They started to play together and soon became good friends. As they played, other kids saw them having fun and wanted to join in. Lily and the new girl welcomed everyone with a hug and a smile. By the end of the day, all the kids at the park were playing together and smiling. The moral of the story is that a simple hug and a smile can bring people together and make new friends.

Ben loves to play with his helicopter. He likes to make it fly in the air and make noises. He has a big zip on his jacket that he can pull up and down. He thinks it is fun to zip and unzip his jacket. One day, he goes to the park with his mom and his helicopter. He sees a girl with a ball. He wants to play with her. He runs to her and says, "Hi, I am Ben. Can I play with your ball?" The girl looks at him and says, "No, you can't. Your jacket is funny. Why do you zip and unzip it all the time? You look silly." Ben feels sad and ashamed. He does not like the girl. He thinks she is mean. He runs back to his mom and hugs her. He says, "Mom, the girl was mean to me. She did not let me play with her ball. She said my jacket was funny." Mom hugs him and says, "Don't worry, Ben. Your jacket is not funny. It is cool. You can zip and unzip it as much as you want. It is your jacket. The girl was rude to you. She should not judge you by your jacket. You are a nice boy. You can play with your helicopter. Maybe you can find another friend who likes helicopters too." Ben smiles and says, "Thank you, Mom. I love you. I love my helicopter. I will find a nice friend who likes helicopters too." He takes his helicopter and flies it in the air. He makes noises and zips and unzips his jacket. He feels happy and proud. He does not care what the girl thinks. He likes his jacket and his helicopter. They are his.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy made of metal. The toy was heavy, but Tim loved it so much. He played with it all day long. One day, Tim's toy was missing. Tim looked everywhere for it. He looked under his bed, in the closet, and behind the door. Tim could not find his toy anywhere. He started to frown because he was sad. Tim's mom saw him frown and asked what was wrong. Tim told her about his lost toy. His mom helped him look for it. They found the heavy metal toy under a big pillow on the sofa. Tim was so happy and his frown turned into a big smile. Tim and his mom played with the toy together and had a fun day.

One hot day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She was very hot and wanted to cool down. She saw a fan on the ground. Lily picked up the fan and started to fan herself. It felt nice and cool. Then, she saw a boy named Tim with a spray bottle. Tim was spraying water on the grass. Lily thought it was fun to spray water. She asked Tim if she could try it. Tim said yes, and they took turns spraying water on the grass. But then, something unexpected happened. When Lily tried to spray the water, it came out a disgusting color. Tim and Lily were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They didn't know why the water was that color, but they had fun anyway.
