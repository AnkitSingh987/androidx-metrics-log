Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Ball

One day, a big dog named Max was playing on the lawn. He liked to run and jump with his ball. Max saw a cat named Luna sitting near a tree. Max wanted to play with Luna, but Luna did not want to play with Max. Max ran to Luna with his ball. He shouted, "Luna, play with me!" But Luna was scared of Max and his big voice. She climbed up the tree to get away from Max. Max was sad because he just wanted to play. Max's mom came outside with a delicious treat for Max. She said, "Max, come here and eat this yummy snack." Max ate the treat and felt better. He knew that he could still have fun even if Luna didn't want to play with him.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to eat. Every day she would eat lots of yummy food. One day, her mummy told her that she had to measure how much food she ate. The little girl said "No! I don't want to!" but her mummy said "It's important, you have to!" The little girl didn't like her mummy's words but she decided to measure her food anyway. Every meal she had to measure it before she ate. One afternoon, she was really hungry and decided to eat a yummy hot soup. But because she was so very hungry, she decided to not measure the soup. She was sure it would be fine. Unfortunately, the soup was too hot and the little girl burnt her mouth. She ran to her mummy in pain, crying and feeling sorry. Mummy said "I told you to measure the food first! Now you know why it's important." The little girl didn't eat hot food for a very long time. She always remembered to measure her food so it would not be too hot.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, hard vase. It was red and pretty. Tim wanted to put it in his room. He was very happy. Tim saw a little zip on the vase. He pulled the zip down. The vase opened up. Inside, there was a small toy car. Tim loved toy cars. He played with it all day. At night, Tim zipped the vase back up. He put it next to his bed. Every day, Tim would open the hard vase and play with the toy car. It was his favorite thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He loved to sail through the water with his friends. One day, while they were sailing, they saw something shiny on the ground. It was a big, colorful scale from a very big fish. Fin and his friends were frightened. They had never seen such a big scale before. They thought the big fish might be mean and scary. But they wanted to find out more about the big fish, so they decided to look for it. As they sailed, they met a big, friendly fish named Bubbles. Bubbles had lost one of her scales. The shiny scale they found was hers! The little fish were happy to help Bubbles. They gave her the scale back. Bubbles was very thankful and they all became good friends. From that day on, they sailed together and had lots of fun.

One day, a big van full of interesting things came to town. A strong man was driving it. People came to look at the van and see what was inside. They were very excited. The strong man wanted to show the people what he could do. He said he would pull the van with a rope. He tied the rope to the van and started to pull. The people were amazed and cheered for him. But then, a small cat walked up and started to pull the rope too. The strong man laughed and said the cat could not help him. But the cat pulled harder and harder. To everyone's surprise, the van started to move faster with the cat's help. The strong man could not believe it. The people clapped and cheered for the cat. The strong man learned that even small friends can do big things.
