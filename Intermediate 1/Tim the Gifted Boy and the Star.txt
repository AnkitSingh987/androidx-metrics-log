Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Gifted Boy and the Star

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tim. He could draw very well. One day, Tim found a big box. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he opened it to reveal a book. The book had many pictures. Tim saw a special picture. It was a symbol that looked like a star. He liked the star very much. He wanted to show the star to his mom and dad. Tim took the book to his mom and dad. He showed them the star symbol. They were very happy. They knew their gifted boy could draw the star too. Tim drew the star for them and they put it on their wall. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a dog was very restless. He could not find his owner. The dog looked all around. He looked in the park, and he looked by the big tree. The dog was sad. Then, the dog met a cat. The cat said, "I can help you find your owner. Let us guess where he is." They both thought hard. The dog guessed that his owner was at the ice cream shop. The dog and the cat went to the ice cream shop. They saw the dog's owner there. The dog was so happy! The owner was happy too. They all went home and the dog was not restless anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a very old tree. The old tree had seen many things in its life. One day, a big wind came to the village. The wind was called a tornado. The tornado was very strong and scared everyone. The old tree wanted to help the people in the village. So, the old tree decided to explain to the people what a tornado is. The old tree said, "A tornado is a big wind that can make things fly in the air." The people listened to the old tree and understood what the tornado was. They learned how to stay safe when a tornado came. The old tree was very happy it could help the people. From that day on, the people and the old tree lived happily and safely in their village.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Joy. Jack was carrying a gear which lead them on a walk. The day was dry and the sun was strong. "Where are we going?" asked Joy. "We are just walking and exploring," said Jack. The two friends kept walking until late. Through fields and along paths, the gear lead them on. Finally the gear stopped in front of a big hill. "Let's climb the hill," said Jack. So, Jack and Joy had an adventure and started climbing the hill. It wasn't easy but both of them had fun. When they finally reached the top, there was a magnificent view which had a dry green field on one side and a clear blue sky on the other. "Wow, this is wonderful," said Joy. Jack and Joy stayed on the top of the hill and enjoyed the view until it was time to go. The gear lead them back home. It was an amazing adventure!

One sunny day, a cheerful duck went for a walk. The duck had a long neck and loved to peek at things. As the duck walked, it saw a big tree. The duck thought, "I want to see what is behind that tree." The duck walked to the tree and used its long neck to peek around it. Behind the tree, the duck saw a little frog. The frog was jumping and playing. The duck said, "Hi, frog! I saw you with my long neck. Can I play with you?" The frog was happy to have a new friend. The duck and the frog played all day. They were very happy. The cheerful duck and the little frog became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to play with his friends, but he also liked to find new things. One day, Bob found a big empty box. He was very curious about what was inside. Bob tried to open the box, but it was stuck. He tried to push the box, but it was very heavy. He thought, "I need help to open this box." So, Bob went to find his friend, Sam the squirrel. Sam was very strong, and Bob knew he could help. Bob and Sam went back to the box. They both pushed the box, and it opened! Inside the box, they found a big root. They knew this root was special. They took it to their other friends, and they all had a big party. They ate the root together and played all day. And from that day on, Bob and Sam knew that when they worked together, they could do anything.
