Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Kid and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a kid named Tom. Tom was not like other kids. He had a big nose and messy hair. Some kids thought Tom was ugly. But Tom was very nice and always helped others. One day, Tom saw a little bird fall from a tree. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Tom wanted to help the bird. He picked it up and took it to his home. Tom knew he had to trust himself to make the bird feel better. Tom took care of the bird every day. He gave it food and water. Slowly, the bird started to feel better. It began to trust Tom too. Soon, the bird could fly again. Tom was very happy to see his friend fly. The bird knew that Tom was not ugly on the inside. He was a kind and loving kid.

One day, a big, scary dog came to the park. All the kids were scared and ran away. Only a little boy named Tim and his toy car stayed. Tim was brave. "Go away, scary dog!" said Tim. The dog did not go away. Tim had an idea. He called his friend, the driver of a big truck. The driver came to the park. "Let's unite and make the dog go away," said the driver. Tim and the driver made loud noises with the truck. The scary dog ran away. All the kids came back to the park. They played together and had fun. Tim and the driver were heroes. The scary dog did not come back again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to race with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends decided to race to space. They put on their special space clothes and got into their space cars. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Let's race to the moon!" All the friends agreed and started their engines. They raced up, up, up into the sky, and soon they were in space. Tim's space car was very fast, and he was in the lead. As they raced, they saw a generous alien named Al. Al waved and said, "Hello, friends! I have gifts for you!" He gave each of them a shiny star. Tim and his friends thanked Al and continued their race. In the end, Tim won the race, and they all celebrated on the moon.

One day, a boy named Tom found a camera. He was very happy. He wanted to take a picture of everything. He saw a big tree, a red ball, and a cute dog. Tom took pictures of them all. Then, something strange happened. Every time Tom took a picture, the thing in the picture would freeze. The dog could not move, the ball stopped rolling, and the tree's leaves did not move in the wind. Tom was scared. He did not want to freeze everything. Tom had an idea. He took a picture of the frozen things again. The dog started to move, the ball rolled, and the tree's leaves moved in the wind. Tom was grateful. He was happy that everything was back to normal. From that day, Tom only took pictures of things that he wanted to freeze, like ice cream on a hot day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big book called a dictionary. It had many words in it. Tim wanted to learn new words, but the dictionary was in terrible shape. The cover was dirty, and some pages were missing. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can we replace this terrible dictionary? I want to learn new words, but it is not good." His mom looked at the dictionary and agreed. "Yes, Tim, we can get a new one," she said. They went to the store and found a new dictionary. It was clean and had all the pages. Tim was so happy. Now, he could learn new words and share them with his friends. His mom was proud of Tim for wanting to learn.

Mama picked up a box from the store. She brought it home and set it on the kitchen floor. John, a 3-year-old boy, was curious. He looked at the box and said, “Mama, what is it?” Mama smiled and said, “It’s a test, John. Let’s open it and see!” John was excited. He grabbed the pick and opened the box. Inside the box were different toys. John was so happy, he couldn’t stop talking. He picked his favorite toy, a truck, and hugged it. “Mama, can I play with it?” he asked. Mama smiled and nodded. “Yes, you can. But it’s getting late, so you have to play for a little bit then take a rest.” John was tired, but he still wanted to play. He drove his truck around the house and had a lot of fun. After a few minutes, he stopped and put the truck away. Then he hugged Mama and said, “Good night!”
