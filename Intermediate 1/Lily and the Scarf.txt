Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Scarf

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily had a beautiful, expensive scarf. She loved her scarf so much that she wore it every day. Her mom always told her, "Lily, don't lose your scarf. It's very special." Lily would repeat, "I won't lose it, Mommy." One day, Lily went to the park to play with her friends. She was so excited that she forgot to wear her scarf. When she got home, she realized her scarf was missing. She was very sad and told her mom. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will find it." The next day, Lily and her mom went back to the park. They looked everywhere for the scarf. Lily's friends helped too. Then, they saw a bird up in a tree. The bird had the expensive scarf in its nest! Lily's mom got a long stick and carefully got the scarf back. Lily was so happy to have her scarf again. She hugged her mom and said, "I will always remember to wear my scarf now." From that day on, Lily never forgot her scarf and always repeated her mom's words, "I won't lose it, Mommy."

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to work. He went outside to the lawn. Tom saw a dog. The dog was big and had a ball. Tom liked the dog. He wanted to play with the dog and the ball. Tom asked the dog, "Can I play with you?" The dog was rude. He said, "No, I don't want to play with you." Tom was sad. But he still wanted to work on the lawn. Tom picked up a rake and started to work. He raked the leaves on the lawn. The dog saw Tom working hard. The dog felt bad for being rude. So, the dog went to Tom and said, "I am sorry for being rude. Can we play together?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, we can play together." They played with the ball and had fun. After that, they worked on the lawn together. Tom and the dog became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat. This cat liked to help others. One day, the cat saw a small bird. The bird had pain in its wing. The cat wanted to help the bird. The cat had an idea. It went to find a leaf. The leaf would help the bird's wing. The cat looked and looked. Then, a big leaf appeared. The cat took the leaf to the bird. But, the leaf was not a leaf. It was a butterfly! The butterfly flew away. The bird laughed. The cat laughed too. The bird's wing was better now. The cat and the bird became friends. They played together every day.

One day, a lively cat named Tom went to the store. He wanted to buy a plastic toy. He saw many toys, but he liked a small, red ball the most. Tom said to the store man, "I want this ball, please." The store man smiled and said, "Okay, little cat. That will be two coins." Tom gave the man two coins and took the plastic ball home. He played with it all day, jumping and running in the yard. The ball made Tom very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Bonnie who loved music. Every day, Bonnie would practice playing the trumpet. One day, when Bonnie was practicing, her mom came into her bedroom. Her mom said, “Honey, it sounds like you are very happy playing the trumpet. You are really good at it!” Bonnie smiled and said, “Thank you mom! I am glad I can make such beautiful music.” That night, as she was getting ready for bed, Bonnie thought about how much she loved playing the trumpet. She lay down in bed, smiling, and thought that tomorrow she would practice even more. The next morning, Bonnie woke up feeling excited to practice her trumpet. She thought, “I can’t wait to hear what beautiful music I will make today!” So Bonnie got dressed and went off to practice. She was so glad she could make beautiful music, and she thought that it just made her world a brighter place.
