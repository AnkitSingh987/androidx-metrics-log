Category: Intermediate
Title: The Goose and the Girl

Once upon a time, in a big, green field, there was a goose. This goose was not an ordinary goose. This goose could talk to people. The goose had many friends who liked to listen to it. One day, the goose met a kind girl. The girl and the goose became best friends. They played together every day. The girl loved the goose very much. The goose loved the girl too. After some time, the girl and the goose decided to marry. They had a big party with all their friends. Everyone was happy for the girl and the goose. They lived happily ever after as best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible dog named Spot. Spot loved to crawl under things. One day, Spot went to the park to play. At the park, Spot saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was playing with a ball. Spot wanted to play too. So, Spot crawled under a bench and went to Amy. "Hi, Spot!" said Amy. "Do you want to play with me?" Spot barked happily. Amy threw the ball, and Spot crawled fast to catch it. They played together all day. In the end, Spot and Amy became good friends. They loved to play and crawl together at the park. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. He liked to run and play with his friends. One day, Max saw a band of birds in the park. They sang a happy song. Max wanted to play with them, but the birds were scared of him. Max said to the birds, "Please don't be scared. I just want to play with you." The birds stopped singing and looked at Max. They were not sure if they could trust him. Then, something unexpected happened. Max started to turn around in circles. He looked funny and the birds began to laugh. They saw that Max was not so fierce after all. The birds joined Max and they all played together in the park. The birds learned not to judge someone by how they look. Max was a good friend and they all had fun together. And Max was happy because he made new friends. The moral of the story is not to judge a book by its cover.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved his toy motorcycle. He would play with it all day long. Tom would make it go fast, slow, and even make it jump. One day, Tom's toy motorcycle got dirty. It had mud all over it. Tom was angry. He wanted his motorcycle to be clean again. So, he got a cloth and started to rub the mud off. He rubbed and rubbed until the motorcycle was shiny and clean. After the motorcycle was clean, Tom was happy. He played with his motorcycle all day. He made sure to keep it away from the mud. Tom and his motorcycle had lots of fun and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Jazz. Jazz loved to cook. Every day, Jazz would cook yummy food for all his friends. One day, Jazz was cooking a big meal for all his friends. They were very excited to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind blew and took all the food away. Jazz and his friends were sad. But then, a twist happened! The wind blew the food to a new friend's house. The new friend was a kind squirrel. The squirrel invited Jazz and all his friends to eat together. They all had a great time and made a new friend. And from that day on, Jazz cooked for all his old and new friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a delicate bird in a tree. The bird had a cane in its beak. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he asked, "Do you need help, bird?" The bird said, "Yes, please. I want to hang this cane on the tree for my nest, but it is too high for me." Tim was happy to help the bird. He reached up high and hung the cane on the tree branch. The bird was very happy and thanked Tim. Tim felt good for helping the bird. He waved goodbye and continued his walk. He knew that even little kids can do big things.
