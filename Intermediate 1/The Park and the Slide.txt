Category: Intermediate
Title: The Park and the Slide

Once upon a time, there was a park. In this park, there was a big slide, swings, and a sandbox. Every day, many kids came to play and have fun. One day, it was icy outside. The park was cold and slippery. The kids were sad because they couldn't play on the slide or the swings. But they saw something new at the park. It was a big, icy hill! The kids decided to play on the icy hill. They slid down the hill and laughed. They had so much fun! Suddenly, they heard a loud sound. It was thunder! The kids were scared and ran home. The next day, the sun came out and the park was warm again. The kids came back to play on the slide, swings, and the sandbox. They were happy and had fun at the park. They always remembered the day when the park was icy and they heard the thunder.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big purple ball in her yard. She was very happy and thought it was good. Lily wanted to play with the ball, so she asked her friend Tom to come over. "Tom, let's play with my new purple ball," said Lily. Tom came over and they started to play. They had a lot of fun with the purple ball, but then something unexpected happened. The ball bounced too high and went over the fence into their neighbor's yard. Lily and Tom were sad. They didn't know how to get the ball back. They thought their neighbor was not nice, so they were scared to ask. But Lily's mom told them, "It's good to ask for help when you need it." Lily and Tom went to the neighbor's house and asked for their ball back. The neighbor smiled and gave them the purple ball. They learned that it is good to ask for help and not to be scared of others. And they played with the purple ball happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a clever fox who lived in a well deep in the woods. Every day, the fox looked for food, searching for berries and tasty bugs. One day, the fox spotted a juicy peach on a branch just above the well. Excited, the fox stepped onto the slippery edge of the well and began to climb up the branch. But, just as the fox was about to reach the peach, they started to slip! "Oh no!" shouted the fox. Just then, a friendly rabbit hopped up. "Let me help you fox!" said the rabbit. The rabbit quickly made a rope of grass and lowered it down to the fox. The fox grabbed a hold of the rope and was pulled to safety. "Thank you so much!" said the fox. "You're welcome!" said the rabbit. The fox rubbed their paws together with gratitude as the rabbit hopped away. Thanks to the rabbit, the fox was safe and the clever fox never slipped again!

Once upon a time, there was a unique armchair. It was red and very big. The armchair was in a room with other chairs, but it was special. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the unique armchair and wanted to sit on it. But there was a problem. A little girl named Sue was already sitting on the armchair. Tim didn't like that. Tim and Sue started to fight over the armchair. They both wanted to sit on it. But then, they had an idea. They decided to share the unique armchair. They sat on it together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a bottle of glue. He thought it would be fun to use the glue with his toys. Tim took his toys and started to glue them together. He glued a car to a train, and a teddy bear to a ball. He was very happy with his work. He felt successful in making new toys. But then, he got some glue on his hand. It was very sticky. Tim tried to get the glue off his hand. He tried to slap it away, but it just made his hand more sticky. Tim's mom saw him and helped him clean his hand. She told him to be careful with the glue next time. Tim learned his lesson and played happily with his new toys.
