Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Thin Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to play games with his friends. One day, Tom had an idea. He wanted to design a new game for all his friends to play. Tom worked hard to make the game. He drew a big score on the ground with colorful chalk. Tom called his friends to come and play his new game. His friends were very excited. They all took turns playing Tom's game. Each friend would try to jump and land on the highest score. They laughed and had lots of fun. Tom was happy that his friends liked his new game. They played until the sun went down, and they all went home with big smiles on their faces.

---

One day, a little fish named Fin went to the ocean. Fin wanted to relax and play with his friends. The ocean was big and blue. Fin met his friend, a crab named Red. Red said, "Hi Fin! Let's play near the dead tree!" Fin and Red went to the dead tree and played hide and seek. They had lots of fun. After playing, Fin and Red sat near the ocean to relax. They talked and laughed. They had a great day at the ocean.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and a big car. Tom loved his computer. He used it to play games and watch fun shows. One day, Tom wanted to act like his favorite character from his favorite show. Tom put on a cape and a hat. He jumped around the room and said, "I am Super Helper!" Tom's little sister, Lily, walked in and saw him. She laughed and said, "What are you doing, Tom?" Tom smiled and said, "I am acting like Super Helper! Do you want to play with me?" Lily clapped her hands and said, "Yes, I want to be Super Helper too!" So, Tom and Lily put on their capes and hats, and they acted like Super Helpers all day long. They had so much fun together, and they forgot all about the computer.

Once upon a time, there was a baby named Tim. Tim was a lucky baby. He had a big toy that he loved to play with. One day, Tim saw a line of ants. They were marching in a row. Tim wanted to march like the ants. Tim asked his mom, "Can I march like the ants?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful not to step on them." Tim was happy and started to march. He tried to march like the ants, but he was not very good at it. Tim stepped on some ants by accident. The ants were mad. They started to bite Tim's feet. Tim cried and ran to his mom. His mom helped him, but it was too late. The ants had already bitten Tim's feet. Tim was sad and learned to be more careful around ants.

Molly and John were walking through the park. Molly saw a snack shack and was very excited. She asked John if they could go in, but he told her that it was too expensive. Molly was sad, but she kept walking. Soon, the sky grew dark and Molly wanted to go home. But John noticed that the snack shack was starting to rise off the ground. They ran toward it, but it was going too fast. Molly and John watched as it rose higher and higher, until they could no longer see it. Molly started to cry and John felt bad that she didn't get her snack. He told her they would come back tomorrow, but Molly knew they wouldn't be able to find the expensive snack shack ever again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a delicate dress that she loved very much. The dress was pink with small flowers on it. Lily wore her dress every day because it made her feel like a princess. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. They played on the swings and slides. Lily was so happy. But then, it was time to leave the park. Lily didn't want to go home yet, so she ran and hid behind a big tree. Her mom looked for her and called her name. "Lily, where are you? It's time to go home!" Lily came out from behind the tree, and her mom saw that her delicate dress was dirty. "Oh no, your pretty dress!" said her mom. Lily felt sad that her dress was dirty, but her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, we can clean it at home." They went home and cleaned the dress together. Lily was happy that her delicate dress was clean again. She promised to be more careful next time they went to the park, and her mom smiled and said, "That's a good girl, Lily."
