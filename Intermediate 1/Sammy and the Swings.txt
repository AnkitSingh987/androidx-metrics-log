Category: Intermediate
Title: Sammy and the Swings

Once there was a boy. His name was Sammy. He was only three years old. One day, he went out to play at the playground. There, he found some amazing scissors. He was so excited! He wanted to gain the scissors, so he could use it to play with. He ran to the swings and waited. Soon, it was his turn, and he sat down. He was having a great time! His friends were there too, and they were all having fun. Then, he remembered those amazing scissors. He got up and ran to where he left them. He waved his hands, and he gained the scissors! He was so happy. He brought them back to the playground to show his friends. They were very impressed with the amazing scissors. They all played with the scissors, cutting paper and having a blast. Sammy was so proud of himself! He had gained the scissors and made his friends really happy.

One day, a little frog named Tim lived in a swamp. Tim loved to play and jump all day. He had many friends, but he felt sad because he could not swing like his friends. His poor little arms were too short to hold the swing. Tim's friends wanted to help him. They had a big idea. They found a long stick and put it on the swing. Now, Tim could hold the stick and swing with his friends. Tim was very happy. He could swing high and fast, just like his friends. They all played and swung together in the swamp. And Tim never felt poor again.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big microscope in her room. She did not know what it was. Sue was a bit careless and liked to play with things she found. She took the microscope to her friend Tom's house. "Look what I found!" said Sue to Tom. "I don't know what it is, but it looks fun!" Tom was scared because he had never seen a microscope before. He thought it might be a monster that could scare them. Sue and Tom began to fight. Tom didn't want the microscope in his house, but Sue wanted to play with it. They were both scared and did not know what to do. Then, Sue's big sister came to visit. She saw the microscope and smiled. "Don't be scared," she said. "It's just a microscope. It helps us see very small things. It's not a monster." Sue and Tom felt better. They learned about the microscope and played with it carefully. They were no longer scared and became good friends again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bedroom. One day, Tim's bedroom was empty. He was sad. Tim had a big, round ball. He loved to roll the ball. Tim went into his empty bedroom with the ball. He started to roll the ball on the floor. The ball went fast and slow, left and right. Tim was happy. Tim played with the ball in his bedroom all day. He rolled the ball until the sun went down. When it was dark, Tim was tired. He went to sleep in his bed. Tim's bedroom was not empty anymore. It was full of fun and love.

Once there was a modern cooler. It was shiny and new and everyone wanted it. So one day, a girl decided to take it. She mixed it with her things and set out on an adventure. When she came back, the cooler was full of yummy drinks! Everyone was so excited, they all had to try them. But then something bad happened. Everyone was too thirsty and drank the drinks too fast! So the cooler was all empty! The girl was very sad. She couldn't use the cooler anymore. At the end, the modern cooler stopped being cool. It just sat there, with no more drinks to give.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. Lily loved to sing. Every day, she would sing with her friends, the birds. One cold day, Lily found a mitten on the ground. It was a big, red mitten. She picked it up and put it on her hand to keep warm. Lily walked through the woods, singing a happy song. A small bird heard her and came to join her. The bird said, "Hi, Lily! I like your mitten. It is so warm!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, bird! I found it on the ground. We can share it to stay warm." So, Lily and the bird walked together, sharing the mitten and singing sweet songs. They were happy and warm, even on the cold day. And they became the best of friends, always singing and sharing with each other.
