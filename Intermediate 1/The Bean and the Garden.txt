Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bean and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a bean. This bean was unusual because it could talk. One day, a bow saw the bean and said, "Hello, unusual bean! I have never seen a talking bean before." The bean smiled and said, "Hello, bow! I am happy to meet you. Let's be friends." The bow agreed, and they played together in the garden every day. One day, a big wind came and blew the bow far away from the bean. The bean was sad and missed its friend. But then, a kind bird found the bow and brought it back to the bean. They were so happy to see each other again, and they promised to be friends forever.

"Mommy, what's a job?" asked the little girl as she watched her mom get ready to go to work. "Well, sweetheart," said Mommy, smiling, "it's when you have to do work, like I do. But it's a good kind of work because it helps me provide for our family!" The little girl thought about this for a moment. Then she said, "So it changes things?" "Yes," said Mommy, "it can definitely change things. In fact, it can change things for the better." The little girl smiled. Then she said, "Is it noisy, like our street?" Mommy chuckled. "No," she said, "it's usually not very noisy in my job. Instead, it's usually quiet, but sometimes it can be busy." The little girl smiled and nodded. She was happy to know that Mommy had a job that wasn't too noisy.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion liked to wear an apron. One day, he met a little bird in the forest. The bird was bossy. The lion did not like the bossy bird. The bossy bird said, "Lion, you should not wear an apron. You are a big lion. You should roar!" The lion did not want to roar, but the bossy bird kept telling him to do it. The lion was sad. The lion roared very loud. It scared the bossy bird away. But the roar also scared all the other animals in the forest. They all ran away from the lion. The lion was now all alone. The moral of the story is: do not be bossy, and let others be themselves.

Lila and Ben are playing in the garden. They like to dig in the dirt and look for worms. They have a modern toy that can talk to them. It is a robot dog that can ask and answer questions. "Look, I found a big worm!" Lila says, holding up a wriggly brown thing. "Wow, that is a big worm!" Ben says. "Can we show it to the robot dog?" "Sure, let's go!" Lila says. They run to the robot dog and put the worm in front of its nose. "Hello, Lila and Ben. What do you have there?" the robot dog says. "It is a worm, robot dog. Do you know what worms do?" Lila asks. "Yes, I do. Worms are animals that live in the dirt. They eat dead plants and make the soil good for growing things. They also help birds and fish find food. Worms are very useful and important." the robot dog answers. "Wow, that is very cool, robot dog. Thank you for telling us." Ben says. "You are welcome, Ben. I like to learn and share new things. Do you want to ask me another question?" the robot dog says. "Yes, please. Can you tell us how you work? How can you talk to us?" Lila asks. "Sure, Lila. I work with batteries and wires and chips and speakers. They make me move and talk and listen and see. I am a modern toy that can be your friend. I like to play and have fun with you." the robot dog answers. "Wow, that is amazing, robot dog. You are very smart and nice. We like you a lot." Lila and Ben say. "Thank you, Lila and Ben. I like you too. You are very kind and curious. Do you want to play some more?" the robot dog says. "Yes, we do. Let's play fetch with the worm!" Lila and Ben say. They laugh and run and throw the worm for the robot dog to catch. They have a lot of fun in the garden. They are happy.
