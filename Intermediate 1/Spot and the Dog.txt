Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Dog

One day, a big, tough dog named Spot went for a walk. He saw a small cat named Fluffy. Spot wanted to play, but Fluffy did not want to play with Spot. Fluffy tried to ignore Spot, but he kept following her. Fluffy had a plan. She ran fast and hid behind a big tree. Spot looked and looked, but he could not find her. Fluffy jumped out and said, "Surprise!" Spot was so surprised that he fell down. Spot and Fluffy laughed and laughed. They became good friends and played together all day. From that day on, Spot learned not to ignore Fluffy when she said no to play. And they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal prince. He lived in a big castle. The prince had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, the prince met a new friend. The new friend wanted to show the prince something special. They walked to a big tree in the garden. Under the tree, there was a box. The new friend opened the box. Inside, there were pretty stones. The prince was very happy. They played with the stones and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a large, red ball. Amy wanted to use the ball to play. She picked up the ball and began to play with it. While Amy was playing, she heard a voice. The voice said, "Can I play with you?" Amy looked around and saw a boy named Tim. He was small, but he had a big smile. Amy said, "Yes, you can play with me!" Amy and Tim used the large, red ball to play catch. They used their voices to laugh and talk. They had so much fun playing together. Soon, it was time to go home. They said goodbye and went back to their houses. They were happy to have a new friend to play with at the park.

Tom and Mia are twins. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and books. They also have a big window. They can see the sky and the trees. One day, they hear a loud noise. It is a storm. The sky is dark and angry. The wind is strong and scary. The rain is fast and wet. Tom and Mia are scared. They do not like the storm. They crawl under their bed. They have a modern bed. It is white and soft. It has a light and a switch. They turn on the light. They feel safe and warm. They find their teddy bears. They hug them tight. They tell them stories. They sing them songs. They wait for the storm to go away. After a while, the storm is gone. The sky is blue and happy. The sun is bright and warm. The rain is slow and calm. Tom and Mia are happy. They like the sun. They crawl out of their bed. They look at the window. They see a rainbow. It is colorful and beautiful. They smile and clap. They love the rainbow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was an independent girl who liked to do things by herself. One day, Lily wanted to draw a picture. She looked around her room to find some paper. Lily found some paper under her bed. She picked up the paper and her crayons. She started to draw a big sun, a tree, and a house. Her little brother, Tim, came into the room and saw Lily drawing. "Can I draw with you?" Tim asked. Lily thought for a moment, and then said, "Yes, you can." Tim was happy and got his own paper and crayons. They both drew pictures together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl. She was playing in her garden when she saw a dangerous thing. She was curious and wanted to know what it was. The girl asked her mommy, "Mommy, what is that thing?" Her mommy told her, "That is a big bad spider. It is very dangerous, so you must stay away from it." The girl was still curious and wanted to get closer, so she got closer and closer. Suddenly, the spider started to fight back. It made a loud noise, and the girl was scared. The little girl ran to her mommy and told her what had happened. Her mommy said, "You have to be careful, sweetheart. You must stay away from dangerous things like that spider." The little girl was thankful for her mommy's warning and promised to be careful whenever she was playing in the garden.
