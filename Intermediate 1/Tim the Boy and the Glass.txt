Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Glass

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, unusual toy. It was a glass ball that could roll and light up. Tim loved to play with it every day. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw the glass ball and said, "Wow, what is that? Can I play with it?" Tim said, "Yes, it's my unusual glass ball. We can play together!" Tim and Sue took turns to push the glass ball. They laughed and had so much fun. The glass ball rolled and lit up as they played. They both loved the unusual toy and became best friends. And they played with the glass ball every day, happy and smiling.

Tom and Sue were friends who liked to play with cars. They had many toy cars of different colors and sizes. They pretended to drive them on the road and make noises. They also had a big cardboard box that they cut out to look like a car. They painted it red and wrote "Tom and Sue's Car" on it. One day, they decided to take their cardboard car outside and play on the street. They put on their hats and sunglasses and pushed the box along the sidewalk. They felt very excited and happy. They thought they looked like real drivers. "Beep beep! Move out of the way!" Tom shouted at a dog that was barking at them. "Vroom vroom! We are so fast!" Sue said as they turned a corner. They did not see the big truck that was coming from the other side. The truck driver saw them and honked his horn loudly. He slammed on his brakes and swerved to avoid them. He was very angry and scared. "Hey, you kids! What are you doing on the street? You could have been hurt!" He yelled as he got out of his truck. Tom and Sue were very frightened and ashamed. They realized they had been very foolish. They had no license to drive on the street. They had broken the rules and put themselves and others in danger. "We are sorry, mister. We were just playing. We did not mean to cause trouble." Tom said in a small voice. "We did not know it was wrong. We thought it was fun." Sue said with tears in her eyes. The truck driver shook his head and sighed. He was glad they were not hurt, but he was also very annoyed. He told them to go home and never play on the street again. He also told them to respect the drivers and the vehicles that belonged on the road. He said they should learn to be more careful and responsible. Tom and Sue nodded and apologized again. They took their cardboard car and ran back to their house. They felt very sorry and embarrassed. They learned their lesson and never played on the street again. They also learned to appreciate their toy cars and their imagination more. They realized that playing safely and wisely was more important than playing excitedly and foolishly.

Once, Timmy was playing outside on a gloomy day. He looked around and everything seemed so still. He was feeling a bit lonely. All of a sudden, he spotted a ball and decided to grab it. He ran around with it and tried making up some new and fun games. He bounced the ball off of a rock and tried to catch it. He also found some sticks and tried making a cave with them. After he was done, he grabbed the ball and sat right in the middle of the cave. He started making up stories and playing with the ball inside the cave. Before long the gloomy day ended and Timmy's mom called him for dinner. He ran inside, grabbed a snack, and went back out to try some more of his new games.

Sam and Mia are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big cloud in the sky. It is dark and loud. "Look, Sam, a storm is coming!" Mia says. "Let's go to the shelter!" Sam says. The shelter is a small house near the park. It is for people who need a place to stay. They run to the shelter. They knock on the door. A woman opens it. She has a kind face and a big smile. "Hello, children, do you need help?" she asks. "Yes, please, can we sit here until the storm is over?" Mia asks. "Of course, you can. You are very smart to come here. Come in, I have some cookies and milk for you." she says. She leads them to a cozy room. There are some books and toys on the shelves. She gives them each a plate of cookies and a glass of milk. "Thank you, you are very nice." Sam says. "You are very welcome. What are your names?" she asks. "I am Sam and this is Mia. We are friends." Sam says. "I am Mrs. Lee. I work at the shelter. I help people who need a place to stay. Sometimes they are sad or scared or lonely. I try to make them feel better." she says. "That is very good. You are very kind." Mia says. They talk and eat and play. They hear the rain and the thunder outside. But they are not afraid. They are safe and warm and happy in the shelter. They are glad they have each other and Mrs. Lee. They are good friends.
