Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Soft Cat and the Stick

One day, a soft cat named Lily saw a big tree. She wanted to climb the tree. So, she started to climb up, up, up. But, she saw a bird and wanted to play. The bird flew away and Lily tried to follow. She jumped and got stuck in a glue. Lily was sad and called for help. Her friend, a dog named Max, heard her. He said, "Lily, don't worry! I will help you." Max had to hurry and find a way to get Lily out of the glue. He thought and thought. Then, Max had an idea. He found a stick and put it in the glue. He told Lily, "Hold the stick and I will pull." Lily held the stick and Max pulled her out. Lily was free and happy. They both went to play in the sun.

One day, a big cat named Tom saw a muffin on a table. The muffin was easy to see because it was so big. Tom wanted to eat the muffin, but he was very sleepy. He let out a big yawn and stretched his legs. Tom jumped on the chair and then onto the table. He took a big bite of the muffin. It was so yummy! Tom was happy, but he was still sleepy. He yawned again and decided to take a nap. Tom curled up next to the muffin and closed his eyes. He slept and dreamed of more muffins. When he woke up, he ate the rest of the muffin. Tom was very happy and not sleepy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He lived by himself and was very, very lonely. He would often look up at the sky and wonder what it would be like to have a friend. One day, a small bird flew up to his window and made a promise. The bird said it would bring a friend if the old man sang a song. The old man was very excited and sang his favorite song. The bird was so happy with the song that it flew away and said it would be back with a friend. The old man waited and waited, but the bird didn't come back. At the end of the day, the old man was sad and disappointed. He looked up and muttered, "I wonder why the bird didn't keep its promise." At that moment, a faint voice slowly said, "But the bird did keep its promise, old man. I am here." The old man was shocked to see an old owl perched on the windowsill. The owl said, "I am the friend the bird brought you." The old man was so sad to realize that his only friend was an old owl and he started to cry. The old man never saw the little bird again, but he never stopped wondering why it never kept its promise to bring him a real friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy. He was very stubborn. The boy had long hair, so one day he took a comb and tried to comb it. But he was so stubborn, he just couldn't do it right. So he struck the comb on the ground and it broke. He was so upset. He wanted to comb his hair and make it look nice. So he started to cry. Suddenly, his mom came and she saw him crying. She gave him a new comb and said, "Be more careful with it this time". The boy tried to be very gentle and soon he was able to comb his hair. He was so proud of himself and happy that he was able to do it.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were walking together. They were very good friends. They wanted to go to the park on the other side of the river. To get there, they had to cross a bridge. But the bridge was very old and weak. The dog said, "Let's separate and cross the bridge one at a time. It might break if we go together." The cat agreed and the dog went first. The dog crossed the bridge safely. Now, it was the cat's turn. The cat was a little foolish. Instead of walking slowly and carefully, the cat ran fast on the bridge. The bridge started to shake and break. The dog shouted, "Stop! Be careful!" The cat stopped running and walked slowly. The cat made it to the other side just in time. The dog and the cat learned a lesson: always be careful and listen to your friends.
