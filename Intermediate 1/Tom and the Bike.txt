Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Bike

Tom is a big boy who likes to use his bike. He can ride very fast and do impressive tricks. He wants to teach his little sister, Mia, how to use a bike too. But Mia is scared of falling and hurting herself. "Come on, Mia, it's fun!" Tom says. "I will help you. You just have to pedal and balance." Mia shakes her head. "No, thank you, Tom. I don't want to use a bike. I like to use my doll and my crayons." Tom sighs. He thinks Mia is missing out on a lot of fun. He has an idea. He goes to the garage and finds an old bike with training wheels. He brings it to Mia and shows it to her. "Look, Mia, this bike has extra wheels. They will keep you from falling. You can use this bike and learn how to ride. Then, when you are ready, you can use my bike and do impressive tricks like me." Mia looks at the bike with curiosity. She sees that it is pink and has a basket with flowers. She likes it. She decides to give it a try. She climbs on the bike and Tom holds her hand. She pedals slowly and feels the wind in her hair. She smiles. "Wow, Tom, this is fun!" Mia says. "Thank you for teaching me how to use a bike. You are a good brother." Tom smiles back. He is happy that Mia likes to use a bike. He thinks she will be an impressive rider soon. He lets go of her hand and watches her ride. He cheers for her. They have a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tom. Tom liked to help his mom in the kitchen. One day, his mom asked him to give her some butter. Tom went to the fridge and took out the butter. On his way back to his mom, he saw a big dog. The dog wanted the butter too. Tom did not want to give the butter to the dog. The dog was very mad. It jumped at Tom and took the butter from him. The butter fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tom was sad and his mom was upset. The dog ran away with the butter and they could not make their cake.

Once upon a time, in a big red box, there was a small temple. It was a very nice temple. The temple had a big door and many little windows. People liked to visit the temple and play inside. One day, a big red ball wanted to fit inside the temple. The ball rolled and rolled, but it could not fit through the big door. The ball was sad, and the temple was sad too. Then, a little girl came and saw the red ball. She thought of an idea. The girl pushed the ball through one of the little windows. The red ball fit inside the temple! The ball, the girl, and the temple were all very happy. They played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely tiger. The tiger lived in a big forest. The tiger had no friends to play with. One day, the tiger was very sad and sat by a big tree. As the tiger sat, it saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was dark and soon it started to pour. The rain came down fast and hard. The tiger felt cold and wet, but then something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down from the tree. The bird was also wet from the rain. The bird said to the tiger, "Let's be friends and play together." The tiger was so happy! From that day on, the lonely tiger was not lonely anymore. The tiger and the little bird became the best of friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved going to the library with her mom. The library was a special place with many books. Mia thought the library was important because she could learn new things from the books. One day, Mia found a big book about mixing colors. She was very excited and wanted to try it at home. So, Mia and her mom went home and found some paint. Mia started to mix the colors. She mixed red and blue, and it made purple. She mixed yellow and blue, and it made green. Mia was so happy to see the new colors she made. Mia showed her mom the new colors she mixed. Her mom was very proud of her. Mia knew that learning from the library was important. She couldn't wait to go back and find more books to learn from. And every time she went to the library, Mia would find new things to learn and share with her mom.
