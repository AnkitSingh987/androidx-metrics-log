Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mess

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a messy kid. He liked to play in the mud and make a big mess. One day, Tim's mom asked him, "Do you want some juice, Tim?" Tim was so happy, he loved juice! So, Tim's mom gave him a big glass of juice. Tim drank the juice very fast, but he spilled some on the floor. "Oh no, Tim! You made a mess!" said his mom. "Can you please clean it up?" Tim didn't want to clean the mess, but he knew it was the right thing to do. Tim got a towel and wiped the floor. He worked hard to make it all clean. His mom saw him and smiled. "Good job, Tim! You cleaned up your mess!" she said. Tim felt proud and happy. From that day on, Tim learned to clean up his messes and to answer when his mom asked for help. The moral of the story is: always clean up your mess and help others when they ask.

One day, a red ball was rolling in the kitchen. It was a very happy ball. It saw a big box called a refrigerator. The ball wanted to play with the refrigerator. The refrigerator was very big and cold. The red ball blinked its eyes and saw a door on the refrigerator. The ball wanted to open the door but it was too small. The ball was sad. It tried and tried to open the door but it could not. Then, something unexpected happened. The refrigerator door opened by itself! The red ball was so happy. It blinked its eyes again and saw a big ice cream inside. The ball and the refrigerator became friends and they shared the ice cream together.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. Near the lake, there was a small shop. A nice man worked in the shop. He would sell toys and snacks to kids. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the shop. He was very excited. He saw a toy boat that he wanted to play with in the lake. Tim asked the man, "Can I buy the boat, please?" The man smiled and said, "Yes, you can!" Tim gave the man some money and got the boat. He ran to the lake and put the boat in the water. The boat floated and moved on the lake. Tim was so happy. Tim played with the boat all day. He made new friends at the lake. They all had fun playing with the boat. When it was time to go home, Tim thanked the man for selling him the boat. The man was happy to see Tim and his friends have a good time.

Lily and Max were bored. It was a gloomy day and they could not go outside. They looked at their toys, but none of them seemed fun. "Let's play motorcycle!" Max said. He grabbed his red motorcycle and made a loud vroom sound. "Okay!" Lily said. She took her pink motorcycle and followed Max. They pretended to ride around the room, dodging the furniture and the cat. "Beep beep! Watch out!" Max shouted. He turned his motorcycle and bumped into Lily's. "Hey, that's not fair!" Lily said. She pushed Max's motorcycle away and tried to speed up. "Sorry, sorry!" Max said. He chased Lily and laughed. They had a race to see who was faster. They played motorcycle until they were tired. They parked their motorcycles next to the bed and cuddled with their blankets. "That was fun!" Lily said. "Yes, it was!" Max said. "Maybe tomorrow the sun will come out and we can play outside." They closed their eyes and dreamed of riding their motorcycles in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her best friend, a small bird named Blue. They would run and jump and laugh all day long. They felt very safe together. One day, Lily and Blue found a small flame in the woods. The flame was all alone and looked very sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Lily. The flame said, "I am all alone and I am cold. I want to be warm and bright again." Lily wanted to help the flame. She encouraged Blue to find some sticks to make the flame warm and bright again. Blue flew around and brought back many sticks. They put the sticks near the flame, and the flame grew warm and bright. The flame was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily and Blue! You made me feel warm and bright again!" From that day on, Lily, Blue, and the flame became best friends. They played together every day, and they always felt safe and happy.
