Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Smart Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a red door. He liked to play with his ball and enjoy the sun. One day, Tim saw a little bird outside his door. The bird was sad and lost. "Hello, bird," said Tim. "Why are you sad?" "I can't find my way home," said the bird. "Can you help me?" Tim wanted to help the bird, so they went on a walk to find the bird's home. They looked and looked, but they could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. They saw a big, friendly dog named Max. Max said, "I know where the bird's home is! Follow me." Tim, the bird, and Max walked together until they found the bird's home. The bird was so happy and thanked Tim and Max for their help. From that day on, Tim, the bird, and Max became best friends. They would always play together and enjoy the sun. They were happy to have each other, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the sand. One day, he wanted to prepare a big sand castle. He had a harmless little toy shovel to help him. Tim worked very hard to make his sand castle. He dug and dug with his toy shovel. As he dug, he found something big in the sand. It was a big, shiny box! Tim was very surprised. Tim opened the big, shiny box. Inside, he found a harmless toy dragon. The toy dragon could talk! It said, "Thank you for finding me, Tim! Now, we can be friends and play together." Tim and the toy dragon had lots of fun and played in the sand every day.

Once upon a time, there was a calm little boy named Tim. He loved to read books. One day, his mom gave him a new book. It was a novel. Tim was very happy. Tim sat down to read his novel. The book was about a kind cat who would provide food to all the animals in the forest. The cat was a good friend to everyone. As Tim read his novel, he felt calm and happy. He liked the story of the kind cat. When he finished the book, he thanked his mom for providing him with the novel. Tim learned that being kind and helpful can make everyone feel calm and happy.

One day, a big dog and a small cat were playing near a tree. The big dog said, "I can snap this stick with my teeth!" The small cat said, "I can't do that, but I can climb the tree!" The big dog snapped the stick and the small cat climbed the tree. On the tree, the small cat found a cloth. The cat said, "Look, I found a cloth in the tree!" The big dog said, "Let's play with it!" They were very happy and played with the cloth. Suddenly, a serious man came and said, "That's my magic cloth!" The big dog and the small cat were surprised. The man said, "If you give it back, I will give you a wish." The big dog and the small cat gave the cloth back. The big dog wished for more sticks to snap and the small cat wished for more trees to climb. The serious man waved the magic cloth and their wishes came true. The big dog and the small cat were very happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She liked to draw on the wall with her crayons. One day, she drew a big smiley face on the wall in her room. She was very proud of her drawing. Her mom came into the room and saw the drawing on the wall. She asked, "Lily, did you draw on the wall?" Lily looked at her mom and said, "Yes, I did. I drew a happy face." Her mom was not happy about the wall, but she loved Lily very much. Lily's mom told her, "We need to clean the wall, but you can draw on paper next time." They cleaned the wall together and Lily promised to only draw on paper from now on. After that, they both were happy and had a fun day playing together.
