Category: Intermediate
Title: Bella the Pink Bunny and the River

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny named Bella. Bella loved to dance all day long. One day, she found a big puddle of mud. She jumped and danced in the mud, laughing and having fun. "Oh no!" said Bella's friend, a little bird named Tim. "You are all dirty from the mud. You need to wash!" Bella and Tim went to the river. Bella jumped in the water and started to wash. She splashed water on her soft, pink fur. "This is fun!" she said. When Bella was all clean, she and Tim danced by the river. They laughed and played together, happy to be clean and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He had a favorite toy, an original spade. He loved to play with his spade in the sand. He would dig and dig, making big holes and tall hills. One sunny day, Tom went to the park with his mom. He took his spade with him to play. He dug a big hole in the sand and found a shiny rock. Tom was very happy and showed it to his mom. But then, a big dog came running by. The dog saw Tom's spade and wanted to play, too. The dog tried to take the spade away from Tom. Tom's mom said, "Stop!" The dog stopped and looked at her. Tom's mom told the dog that the spade was Tom's toy. The dog understood and walked away. Tom was happy he got to keep his original spade and played with it all day long.

Ben and Lily were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They saw a big red pepper on the counter. Ben wanted to cut it with a knife, but Lily warned him. "No, Ben, no! Knife is sharp. Knife can hurt you. Knife is for grown-ups only," Lily said. But Ben did not listen. He grabbed the knife and tried to cut the pepper. He did not know how to hold the knife right. He slipped and cut his finger. He cried out loud. "Ow, ow, ow! My finger is broken! It hurts so much!" Ben said. Lily ran to him and saw the blood. She was scared. She dropped the pepper and ran to get Mommy. Mommy came and saw what happened. She took Ben to the bathroom and washed his finger. She put a band-aid on it and gave him a hug. "Ben, you have to listen to Lily. She was trying to warn you. Knives are dangerous. You could have hurt yourself badly. You have to be careful in the kitchen," Mommy said. Ben nodded and said he was sorry. He hugged Lily and thanked her for warning him. He learned his lesson. He did not touch the knife or the pepper again. He and Lily played with something else. They were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a huge, friendly bear. The bear lived in a big forest. One day, the bear found a sharp stick on the ground. He picked it up and played with it. One night, the bear had a nightmare. In his dream, he saw a scary monster with sharp teeth. The monster tried to hurt the bear. The bear was so scared, he woke up. He still had the sharp stick in his hand. The next day, the bear met a new friend, a tiny squirrel. The squirrel was scared of the sharp stick. But then, the bear showed the squirrel that the stick could be fun. They played with the stick together and had a great time. The bear was not scared anymore.

Once there was a girl called Jemma. She was three years old and she loved going for walks. So one day, she went for a walk in her neighbourhood. She was so excited to explore all the wonderful things outside. She came to a very noisy mailbox. There were loud noises coming from inside of it. Jemma was so curious that she tried to open the mailbox to see what was inside. But it was locked shut! Jemma tried so hard but she couldn't open it. Suddenly, she heard a meow and saw a tiny kitten inside the mailbox. Jemma knew she had to save the kitten! She quickly ran and got her dad. He came and opened the mailbox easily with a key. The noisy mailbox was opened and the sweet kitten hopped out. Jemma was so happy that she saved the kitten! She gave it a big hug and thanked her dad for helping. Then, the two of them walked away, hand in hand.
