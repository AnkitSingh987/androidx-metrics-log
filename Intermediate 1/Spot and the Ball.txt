Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog. He loved to play with his red ball and paint with his tiny paws. One day, Spot saw a big cat. The cat was not nice. It wanted to take Spot's red ball. Spot said, "No! The red ball is mine. You can't have it!" The cat did not listen. It tried to take the red ball away. Spot was sad and scared. He had to think of a way to escape from the big cat. Spot had an idea. He took his paint and painted a big, scary monster on the wall. When the cat saw the monster, it got scared and ran away. Spot was happy. He had saved his red ball and escaped from the big cat. From that day on, Spot and his red ball were safe and sound.

One day, a little squirrel was playing near a big oak tree. He was very glad because he found a lot of nuts to eat. The squirrel climbed the oak tree and began to eat the nuts. While he was eating, he saw a little bird on a branch. The bird looked sad. The squirrel asked, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The bird said, "I can't fly high. I tried and tried, but I always fall." The squirrel thought for a moment and had an idea. The squirrel said, "I can help you! I will hold your wing and we will jump from branch to branch together. This way, you won't fall." The bird was glad and they started to jump together. The squirrel held the bird's wing and they jumped from one branch to another. The bird was very happy and didn't fall anymore. In the end, the squirrel and the bird became good friends. They played and ate nuts together near the big oak tree every day. They were both very glad and enjoyed their time together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a chubby cat named Tom. Tom loved to sing and dance on a stage. Every day, he would go to the park and perform for all his friends. One sunny day, Tom met a new friend named Sally. Sally was a little bird who also liked to sing. Tom asked Sally, "Do you want to sing with me on the stage?" Sally was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to sing with you!" So, Tom and Sally went on the stage and sang their favorite songs together. All their friends clapped and cheered. They accepted Tom and Sally as the best singing duo in the town. From that day on, the chubby cat and the little bird became best friends and sang together every day.

Once upon a time there were two little kids, Tom and Mike. Tom was 3 years old and Mike was 5. Tom and Mike loved to spin around like a twirl, their arms reaching up to the sky. One day, Tom and Mike were playing in the park. Mike asked Tom to spin around with him, but Tom shook his head. He said, "No, I don't want to spin." Mike was very angry and he said, "You're so selfish! Let's both spin together like a twirl." Tom still said no and he held up his palm. "I want to just walk around, like this," he said. Mike got mad again and he shouted, "You are so selfish!" Tom kept his palm up and he said, "I'm not selfish, I am just happy doing my own thing." Mike thought about it and realised that Tom was right. He smiled and said, "Ok, let's walk around together and look at all the things in the park." And so Tom and Mike took turns spinning and walking around, having fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to catch a big fish. He went to the lake with his dad. They took their fishing rods and sat by the lake. Tim said, "Dad, I want to catch the biggest fish ever!" While they were waiting, a funny-looking fish jumped out of the water. The fish said, "Hello, Tim! I am an original fish, and I can help you win the victory of catching the biggest fish." Tim was surprised but happy. He said, "Yes, please help me!" The original fish told Tim to throw his fishing rod far into the lake. Tim did as the fish said, and soon he felt a big pull on his line. He pulled and pulled, and out came the biggest fish he had ever seen! Tim and his dad were so happy. They thanked the original fish for helping them. The fish said, "You're welcome! Have a great day!" Then it jumped back into the lake. Tim and his dad went home with their big fish, and they had a big victory dinner that night.
