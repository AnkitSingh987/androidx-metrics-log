Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa and the Ring

Lisa loves to play with her diamond ring. It is shiny and pretty and makes her feel like a princess. She got it from her grandma, who said it was very special. Lisa wears it every day and shows it to her friends. One day, Lisa goes to the park with her mom and dad. She sees a big slide and wants to go on it. She runs to the slide and climbs up the ladder. She is about to slide down when she feels something fall off her finger. It is her diamond ring! Lisa gasps and looks down. She sees her ring bouncing on the ground and rolling away. She slides down quickly and runs after it. But there are many people and dogs and bikes in the park. Lisa can't find her ring. She feels sad and scared. She starts to cry. Mom and dad hear Lisa crying and come to her. They ask her what is wrong. Lisa tells them she lost her diamond ring. Mom and dad hug her and try to comfort her. They say they will help her look for it. They ask her where she last saw it. Lisa points to the slide and the ground. Mom and dad look around, but they can't see the ring either. They ask some people and animals if they saw it, but no one did. They feel frustrated and sorry. They don't know what to do. Lisa misses her diamond ring very much. She wishes she could have it back. She wonders if grandma will be angry with her. She hopes someone will find it and give it to her. She prays to God and asks him to help her. She feels a little better, but still sad. She loves her diamond ring. It is her favorite thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti with thick sauce. One day, he asked his mom to make spaghetti for dinner. His mom said, "Okay, but you must stay and help me cook." Tim was excited to help his mom cook. He watched as she made the thick sauce. He could smell the yummy sauce as it cooked. Tim's mom said, "The sauce will taste better if you let it stay on the stove for a while." Later, they sat down to eat their spaghetti. Tim tasted the sauce and said, "Wow, this is the best sauce ever!" His mom smiled and said, "That's because we let it stay on the stove and cook longer." The moral of the story is that good things come to those who wait.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to chew her food very well. One day, she found a rough cookie in the kitchen. Sue wanted to eat the cookie, so she took a big bite and started to chew. Sue heard a noise coming from a drawer. She opened the drawer and saw a tiny mouse. The mouse looked at Sue and said, "Please don't eat my cookie!" Sue was surprised because she didn't know mice could talk. Sue gave the cookie back to the mouse. The mouse was very happy and thanked Sue. They became good friends and Sue learned that sometimes, the best thing to do is share.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little girl who had lots of energy and liked to play all day. She had ten tiny fingers and liked to use them to work on crafts. One day, the girl's mom asked her to help with the dishes. She put her finger in the soapy water to help out, but accidentally knocked over a glass. It broke and made a huge mess! The girl was scared and ran away. She was too scared to tell her mom what happened. The next day, her mom noticed the broken glass and asked the girl about it. The girl was too afraid to tell her mom that the broken glass was because of her, so she kept pretending she didn't know. But when her mom showed her the broken glass, she knew she had to tell the truth. Her mom was very angry and said she couldn't play for a whole week. The little girl was so sad. She had wanted to use her fingers to work on more crafts and play more, but now she couldn't. Unfortunately, for the little girl, her bad ending meant that she had to learn an important lesson. She couldn't do anything without her mom's permission.
