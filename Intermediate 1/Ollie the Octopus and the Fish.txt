Category: Intermediate
Title: Ollie the Octopus and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little octopus named Ollie. Ollie was very eager to make new friends. He lived in the big, blue sea with many other fish. One day, Ollie saw a big fish and dared to say hi. The big fish was nice and they played together. They swam fast, laughed, and had fun. Ollie was very happy. Soon, more fish came to play with Ollie and the big fish. They all played together and became good friends. Ollie was glad he dared to say hi. Now, Ollie had many friends in the big, blue sea.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat liked to listen to the birds sing. One day, the cat had a question. The cat wanted to know why the birds sang so pretty. The cat asked the birds, "Why do you sing so pretty?" The birds did not like the purple cat. They did not want to answer the cat's question. The birds said, "We will not tell you. Go away!" The cat was sad. The cat really wanted to know why the birds sang so pretty. The purple cat did not give up. The cat asked a kind dog for help. The dog liked to listen too. The dog asked the birds the same question. The birds told the dog, "We sing pretty because we are happy." The dog told the purple cat the answer. The purple cat was happy and listened to the birds sing every day.

Lily and Ben were excited to play outside. They put on their pants and shoes and ran to the park. They saw a big pile of leaves and wanted to jump in it. "Look, Ben, leaves!" Lily said. "Let's throw them in the air!" "Okay, Lily, that sounds fun!" Ben said. They picked up some leaves and threw them high. The leaves fell down like rain and made them laugh. But then, Lily saw a dog. The dog was big and brown and had a long tail. The dog ran to the pile of leaves and started to dig. "Hey, dog, stop!" Lily shouted. "You are ruining our leaves!" The dog did not listen. It kept digging and digging. It found Lily's pants and pulled them out of the pile. It ran away with Lily's pants in its mouth. "Come back, dog, those are my pants!" Lily cried. She chased the dog, but it was too fast. She lost her pants and her shoes. Ben saw what happened and ran after Lily. He tried to help her, but he could not catch the dog either. He felt sorry for Lily. "Don't worry, Lily, we can get new pants," he said. "Maybe we can ask Mom or Dad." Lily nodded. She was sad, but she was glad Ben was her friend. They walked back to their house, holding hands. They hoped the dog would not eat their pants.

Once there was a little girl named Marian. She was three years old and always looked for adventure. One day, she decided to go exploring in the mist. Although it was difficult to see, Marian didn't worry and enjoyed bouncing and jumping around. "Where are we going?" Marian asked. Her mom answered, "Well, Marian, let's just keep walking and exploring. I will take care of you." Marian felt safe with her mom and continued to explore, happily playing in the mist. She found many new and exciting things as they journeyed through. Eventually, Marian and her mom reached a dense part of the mist. Marian couldn't go any further, so her mom said, "It is too difficult for us to go through that way, Marian. We will have to take a different route." Marian was disappointed, but she knew it was too hard for them. Her mom hugged her and told her, "It's okay. We will be safe and I will always take care of you." Marian smiled, thankful for her mom's love and care. Together, they found a way around the mist and continued on their journey.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big, amazing mushroom. It was red with white spots. Lily thought it was very pretty. Lily wanted to show her friend Tom the amazing mushroom. She used a stick to mark the ground near the mushroom. She made a big arrow that pointed to the mushroom. Then, she ran to find Tom. "Tom, come see the amazing mushroom I found!" said Lily. Tom followed Lily to the woods. They saw the arrow Lily made and found the mushroom. "Wow! That is an amazing mushroom!" said Tom. They both smiled and played near the mushroom all day.
