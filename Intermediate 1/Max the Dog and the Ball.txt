Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Max. Max had a big, red ball that he loved to play with all day. One day, Max saw a zipper on his ball. He did not know what it was for, but he believed it was something special. Max asked his friend, a wise old cat named Whiskers, about the zipper. Whiskers told Max that the zipper was a secret. If Max opened the zipper, he would find a big surprise. Max was very excited and could not wait to see what was inside. The next day, Max opened the zipper on his ball. Inside, he found a smaller, blue ball. Max was so happy to have a new toy to play with. He learned to believe in the magic of the zipper and the surprises it could bring. And from that day on, Max and his new blue ball were the best of friends.

Tim and Lily liked to play in the pond near their house. They liked to splash in the water and look for frogs and fish. Sometimes they saw a big beaver who made a house out of lumber. The beaver was patient and worked hard to build his home. One day, Tim and Lily saw a new thing in the pond. It was a big yellow duck. The duck was loud and rude. He quacked and splashed and chased away the frogs and fish. He even tried to bite the beaver's tail. The beaver was angry and scared. He did not like the duck. Tim and Lily wanted to help the beaver. They had an idea. They found some sticks and stones and threw them at the duck. The duck was surprised and hurt. He did not like the sticks and stones. He quacked and splashed and flew away. He did not come back. The beaver was happy and grateful. He thanked Tim and Lily for saving his home. He gave them some of his lumber to make a boat. Tim and Lily were happy and proud. They made a boat and sailed in the pond. They splashed and laughed and played with the beaver and his friends. They had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a small, harmless lizard. The lizard lived in a big tree. It liked to play with the other animals in the forest. One day, a little boy found the lizard. He wanted to carry it home to show his friends. The lizard did not want to go, but the boy took it anyway. The boy carried the lizard far away from its home. The lizard was very sad. It missed its friends and the big tree. The boy forgot about the lizard, and it could not find its way back home. The poor, harmless lizard was lost and alone, and it never saw its friends again.

One day, a boy named Ben and his dog Spot went to the park to play. They saw a big ship on the lake. It had a black flag with a skull and bones. Ben said, "Look, Spot, a pirate ship! Let's go see it!" They ran to the lake and climbed on the ship. They saw a man with a hat, a hook and a parrot. He was the pirate captain. He looked at Ben and Spot and said, "Ahoy, mateys! Welcome to my ship! Do you want to join my crew?" Ben and Spot were scared. They did not want to be pirates. They wanted to be regular kids and have fun. They said, "No, thank you, Mr. Pirate. We want to go home." The pirate captain laughed. He said, "Too bad, mateys. You are on my ship now. You have to do what I say. You have to scrub the deck, feed the parrot and look for treasure. And you can never go home again." Ben and Spot were very sad. They wished they had not gone on the ship. They wished they had listened to their mom, who told them to stay away from strangers. They looked for a way to escape. They saw a small boat on the side of the ship. They ran to the boat and untied it. They jumped in and paddled away. The pirate captain saw them and shouted, "Hey, stop! Come back here, you scurvy dogs!" But Ben and Spot did not stop. They paddled faster and faster. They reached the shore and ran to their mom, who was waiting for them. They hugged her and said, "We are sorry, mom. We went on a pirate ship and it was bad. We will never do that again." Their mom was happy to see them. She said, "I am glad you are safe, Ben and Spot. You learned a valuable lesson today. Never trust a pirate. And always listen to your mom." The end.
