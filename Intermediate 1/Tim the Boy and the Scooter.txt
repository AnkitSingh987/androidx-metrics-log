Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Scooter

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a red scooter that he loved to ride every day. He would go up and down the street, feeling the wind in his hair. Tim's friends would watch him and wonder how it felt to ride the scooter. One day, a new girl named Sarah moved in next door. She saw Tim riding his scooter and asked if she could try it too. But Tim was feeling selfish and didn't want to share his scooter. Sarah was sad and walked away. Tim's mom saw what happened and talked to him about being kind and sharing. She told him that friends should share and not be selfish. Tim thought about it and decided to share his scooter with Sarah. They took turns riding the scooter and became best friends. From that day on, Tim learned to share and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a messy room. His toys were all over the floor, and his clothes were not put away. Tim's mom always told him to clean his room, but he did not like to do it. One day, Tim's mom had a request. She asked Tim to put his toys in a big pocket she gave him. Tim did not want to do it, but he knew his mom would be happy if he did. So, he picked up his toys and put them in the big pocket. After Tim finished, his room was not messy anymore. His mom was very happy, and she gave him a big hug. Tim felt good about making his mom happy, and he promised to keep his room clean from now on.

Anna and Ben like to play with clay. They have many colors of clay. They can make animals, flowers, and shapes with clay. They have fun with clay. One day, they want to make a snake with clay. They take some green clay and roll it on the table. They make a long and thin clay snake. They give it eyes, a tongue, and a tail. "Look at my snake!" Anna says. "It is very graceful. It can slither and curl." "Wow, your snake is nice!" Ben says. "But I can make a better snake. Watch me!" Ben takes some red clay and rolls it on the table. He makes a long and thick clay snake. He gives it teeth, a horn, and scales. "Look at my snake!" Ben says. "It is very fierce. It can bite and roar." Anna and Ben compare their clay snakes. They are very different. Anna's snake is graceful and green. Ben's snake is fierce and red. "Which snake do you like more?" Anna asks. "I like both snakes!" Ben says. "They are both cool. Can we play with them together?" "Sure!" Anna says. "Let's make them friends. Maybe they can teach each other something." Anna and Ben smile. They put their clay snakes next to each other. They pretend that the snakes are talking and playing. They have more fun with clay.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his friends. One day, he found a big red tomato in his yard. Tim called his friend Sam and said, "Look, I found a big tomato! Let's play with it!" Sam came over and they started to play catch with the tomato. They were having so much fun, but they were not very good at catching. While they were playing, the tomato flew high into the air and landed on the ground. It broke into pieces! Tim and Sam were sad. They knew they should not have played with the tomato. Now, they had nothing left to play with and the fun was over.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to touch everything he saw. One day, Tim went with his mom to her office. The office was a big room with many things to touch. In the office, Tim saw an incredible big chair. He touched the soft seat and spun around. It was so much fun! Tim also touched a shiny computer. The screen lit up and made funny sounds. Tim's mom saw him having fun and smiled. She knew her office was a special place for Tim. They went home and Tim told his dad about the incredible office. From that day on, Tim loved to visit the office with his mom.
