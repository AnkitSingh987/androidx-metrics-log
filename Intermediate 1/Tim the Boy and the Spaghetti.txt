Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Spaghetti

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. He had a best friend named Sam. They loved to play and have fun together. Sam was a very reliable friend, always there for Tim. One day, Tim and Sam decided to make spaghetti for lunch. They went to the kitchen and took out a big pot. They filled it with water and put it on the stove. They watched the water get hot and start to bubble. When the water was ready, they put the spaghetti in the pot. They watched the spaghetti cook and get soft. After a little while, they took the spaghetti out and put it on their plates. They ate their spaghetti and had a great time. Tim was happy to have a reliable friend like Sam who could help him make delicious spaghetti.

One day, a famous cat named Tom found a pen on the ground. He picked it up and started to draw. Tom drew a big cloud and then it began to rain. He was so surprised! Tom showed his friend, a dog named Max, the pen and the rain. "Look, Max! I made rain with this pen!" Tom said. Max looked at the rain and was very happy. They played in the rain and had lots of fun. Then, Tom drew the sun and the rain stopped. They both smiled and laughed. Later, Tom and Max saw a sad bird named Lucy. She was sad because her nest was wet from the rain. Tom had an idea! He used the pen to draw a big, warm blanket for Lucy. Lucy was so happy and thanked Tom and Max. The moral of the story is that we should use our talents to help others and make them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom and a girl named Sue. They were best friends. They played together every day in the peaceful park. They liked to pretend they were married. Tom would say, "I am the husband," and Sue would say, "I am the wife." One day, a new boy named Jim came to the park. Jim wanted to play with Sue, but he did not want Tom to play with them. Jim said, "Sue, you should be my wife, not Tom's wife." Tom got mad and said, "No, Sue is my wife!" They both wanted Sue to pick one of them. Sue did not like the fight. She wanted to play with both boys, not just one. So, Sue said, "I will not be a wife. I will be a queen!" Tom and Jim were surprised. Sue insisted, "As queen, I say we all play together and be friends." Tom and Jim agreed, and they all played happily in the peaceful park.

Once upon a time, there was a brilliant pink duck. The duck loved to bathe in the pond. One sunny day, the duck went to the pond to bathe. As the duck was bathing, a big frog jumped out of the water. The frog said, "Hello, pink duck! Can I bathe with you?" The duck said, "Yes, you can!" They were having fun when something unexpected happened. A big fish splashed water on them. The frog got scared and jumped out of the water. The duck laughed and said, "Don't worry, frog. The fish just wants to play too!" So, the duck, the frog, and the fish all played together in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. Tim wanted to go on a trip, so he had to pack his bag. He put all his favorite things in his bag, like his ball and his toy car. Tim was very excited to go on his trip. Before Tim left, his mom said, "Tim, don't forget to pack something soft to sleep on." Tim looked at his bag and saw a rough blanket. He thought it was good enough, so he said, "Okay, Mom, I have a blanket." On the trip, Tim played with his ball and toy car. He had a lot of fun. But when it was time to sleep, Tim tried to use his rough blanket. It was not soft at all. Tim could not sleep because the blanket was too rough. Tim said, "I wish I had listened to Mom and packed a soft blanket." He was very sad and tired. The next day, Tim could not play because he was too tired from not sleeping. The trip was not fun anymore. In the end, Tim learned that he should always listen to his mom. He went back home and promised to pack the right things next time. But for now, his trip had a bad ending because he did not pack the soft blanket.
