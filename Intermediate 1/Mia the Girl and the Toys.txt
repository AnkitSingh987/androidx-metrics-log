Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big, soft rug in her room. Mia loved to play on the rug with her toys. One day, Mia's mom told her they were moving to a new house. Mia started to worry. She didn't want to leave her rug and her room. In the new house, Mia's room was tight. She didn't have space for her big rug. Mia felt sad and missed her old room. Her mom saw Mia was worried and had an idea. Mia's mom cut the big rug into smaller pieces. She put one piece in Mia's room and the other pieces in different parts of the house. Mia felt happy again. She could play on her rug in her tight room and all around the house. Mia didn't worry anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was a fair girl with a big smile. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. In the house, there was a cabinet. The cabinet was very big and old. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, do not open the cabinet. It is not safe for you." Lily said, "Okay, mom, I will not open it." But Lily was curious about what was inside the cabinet. When her mom and dad were not looking, Lily went to the cabinet. She kneeled down and opened it. Inside, she saw a big, scary monster. The monster jumped out and said, "I am free!" Lily was very scared and ran to her mom and dad. They were sad and angry. "Lily, we told you not to open the cabinet!" they said. The monster made a big mess in their house, and they could not make it go away. From that day on, Lily and her family had to live with the big, scary monster in their house.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was nervous because he wanted to play with his big red ball. But his mom did not allow him to play with the ball inside the house. She was afraid he would break something. One day, Tim's mom found a soft leather ball. She thought it would be perfect for Tim to play with inside the house. The leather ball was small and soft, so it would not break anything. She gave the ball to Tim and he was very happy. Tim played with the soft leather ball all day. He was not nervous anymore because he knew he could play inside the house without breaking anything. His mom was happy too because Tim could have fun and be safe at the same time. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to ride her bike with the red pedal. One day, she saw a big tree with yummy fruit. Lily wanted to get the fruit, but she was too small. She had an idea, "I will use my bike to stand on and get the fruit!" Lily rode her bike to the tree. She put the bike next to the tree and tried to stand on the pedal. She was so close to the fruit! But the pedal was not strong enough. It started to bend and shake. Lily felt scared but really wanted the fruit. Suddenly, the pedal broke and Lily fell down. She was sad and hurt. Her bike was broken too. Lily learned that she should not stand on her bike to get things. She went home with tears in her eyes and a broken bike. The sweet fruit stayed in the tree, and Lily did not get to taste it.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a new toy. It was a small light. She was very happy. She showed her mom the new light. "Mom, can I keep it?" Sue asked. Her mom said yes, but told her to be careful with the light. Sue played with the light all day. She knew it was special. That night, Sue heard a sound. She turned on the light and saw a tiny bug. The bug was sad because he lost his way home. Sue wanted to help him. "Don't be sad, I will help you find your home," she said to the bug. The next day, Sue and the bug went outside. They looked for the bug's home. The light helped them see where to go. They found the bug's home and he was very happy. Sue was happy too, and she knew the light was very special. From that day on, Sue and the bug were best friends.
