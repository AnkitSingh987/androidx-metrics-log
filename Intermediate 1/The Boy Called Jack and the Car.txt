Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Jack and the Car

Once there was a little boy called Jack. He wanted to play with a toy car but it belonged to his friend. Jack asked to play with the car but his friend said no. Jack was very sad and felt very selfish. He decided to take the car anyway! At first, it seemed like fun. Jack drove the car up and down the street and made noise with it. But soon the fun stopped. His friend saw him with the car and was very angry. He shouted at Jack and told him to give it back. Jack didn't want to give the car back because he thought it was very unfair! But he knew he had to so he did what he was told. His friend was so cross that he said Jack couldn't come and play with him anymore. Jack felt very unhappy and he realised that being selfish was not a good thing. He had learned an important lesson but it was too late.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, were playing in the park. They saw a big, red ball. Tim said, "Let's play with the ball, Spot!" They started to play, and Tim kicked the ball really hard. The ball went up, up, up into the sky. "Wow!" said Tim, "I didn't know I could kick that high!" As they looked up, they saw something unusual in a tree. It was a big seat, like a chair, high up in the branches. Tim said, "I want to sit in that seat, Spot!" They tried to climb the tree, but it was too high. Then, Tim had an idea. He would shoot the ball at the seat to knock it down. Tim kicked the ball again, and this time it hit the seat. The seat fell down from the tree. But something unexpected happened. When the seat hit the ground, it turned into a big, soft pillow. Tim and Spot were surprised. They laughed and said, "That was fun! Let's play with our new pillow now!" And they played in the park all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a clean leopard named Leo. He lived in a big forest. Leo loved to play with his animal friends, but he was always very careful to keep his fur clean. One day, Leo and his friends found a big, red spray bottle. They didn't know what it was for, but they thought it was fun to play with. They took turns spraying each other with the water inside. Leo didn't want to get wet, so he hid behind a tree. Soon, the forest started to get very dirty because of the water. The animals didn't know what to do. They were sad because their home was not clean anymore. Leo thought about the spray bottle and had an idea. Leo used the spray bottle to clean the forest. He sprayed the dirt away and made everything clean again. His friends were so happy, and they learned that it's important to keep their home clean. From that day on, they all worked together to keep the forest clean and beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play on the lawn with his best friend, a big dog named Max. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. They liked to support each other when they played their games. One day, Tom and Max found a spicy pepper on the lawn. Tom looked at the pepper and said, "Max, should we try this spicy pepper?" Max barked and wagged his tail. Tom took a small bite, and his face turned red. "Wow, that's spicy!" he said. Max licked the pepper, and he also felt the spicy taste. They both laughed and ran to get some water. After they drank the water, Tom and Max went back to the lawn to play. They knew that they would always support each other, no matter what. And they promised never to eat anything spicy again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and compassionate girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her little brother, Max. Lily liked to play with Max and help her mom in the kitchen. Lily's bedroom was her favorite place because it was full of her toys and books. One day, Lily was in her bedroom playing with her toys. Max came in and said, "Lily, I hear a strange noise coming from the backyard!" Lily stopped playing and listened carefully. She could hear the sound too. "Let's go see what it is!" said Lily. Lily and Max went to the backyard and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog was making the noise they heard. They were surprised and happy to see the dog. Lily said, "Hi, dog! Do you want to play with us?" The dog wagged its tail and played with Lily and Max all day. In the end, the dog became their new best friend, and they named him Buddy.
