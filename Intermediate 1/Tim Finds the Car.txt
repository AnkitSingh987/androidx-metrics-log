Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Car

One day, Tim and his mom went to the store. They needed to buy cereal. Tim saw a box with a picture of a fun toy inside. "Mom, can we get this one?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the box and said, "Yes, we can try this unknown cereal." The next morning, Tim was excited to eat the new cereal. He poured it into a bowl and found the toy. It was a little car that could drive on its own. Tim played with the car all day. He shared it with his friends and they had fun racing it. One day, the car stopped working. Tim was sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can encourage the car to work again." They put the car on the table and clapped their hands. To their surprise, the car started moving again! They all laughed and played with the car, happy that it was working again.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He loved to play with helicopters, especially his toy helicopter. One day, Jack's toy helicopter broke and he was very sad. He asked his parents for help and they told him, “You must be determined and work hard to improve it, Jack!”. Jack was very motivated and he worked day and night trying to fix the helicopter. He had to use very harsh tools and he scratched his hands a bit, but he was determined. Finally, after much hard work, Jack was able to improve the helicopter. He was proud of his hard work and the helicopter worked perfectly. Jack and the helicopter flew around his backyard, happy and proud.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily wanted to create a new friend. She thought of making a friend that no one else had. She decided to make a ghost friend. Her mom and dad did not know about her plan. Lily drew a big ghost on a paper. She cut it out and put it on her wall. At night, when the moon was out, something unexpected happened. The paper ghost came to life! It was not a harsh ghost, but a very nice and friendly one. They played and laughed together. One day, Lily's mom and dad came to her room and saw the ghost. They were very scared at first. But, the ghost showed them that it was a good friend to Lily. So, they let the ghost stay and play with Lily. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Sue had a hard, blue apron. She liked to wear it when she helped her mom cook. One day, her mom said, "Sue, let's make a cake!" Sue was very happy and put on her apron. Sue and her mom mixed the cake in a big bowl. They put it in the oven to cook. While they waited, they heard a noise. "What was that?" asked Sue. Her mom did not know. They went to look and found a little dog in the kitchen! The dog had Sue's apron in its mouth. "Oh no!" said Sue. The dog wanted to help too. Sue's mom laughed and said, "Well, let's all make the cake together!" They all had fun making the cake. The little dog even wore Sue's apron. In the end, they found out that the dog belonged to their neighbor. They shared the cake with the dog and their neighbor, and they all became good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toys. He had a big red ball and a yellow car. Tim loved his toys very much. But Tim's mom was on the phone. She was talking to her friend and she was very sad. Tim could hear her cry. Tim went to his mom and asked, "Mom, why are you crying?" His mom said, "I had a bad day, Tim. My friend is not feeling well, and it makes me sad." Tim wanted to help his mom feel better. He thought about what he could do. Tim had an idea! He went to his room and picked up his favorite toy, the yellow car. He gave the car to his mom and said, "Here, Mom. This is for you. I hope it makes you feel better." His mom smiled and hugged Tim. She felt happy now. They played with the yellow car together, and the bad day was not so bad anymore.
