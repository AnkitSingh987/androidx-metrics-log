Category: Intermediate
Title: The Port and the Dolphin

Once upon a time, in a normal little town by the sea, there was a port. In this port, there was a big boat. On the boat lived a kind girl named Lily and her friend, a small cat named Max. One day, Lily and Max were playing near the water. Max saw a big fish and ran to catch it. He fell into the water and began to cry. Lily heard Max and said, "Max, don't cry! I will save you!" She hugged him tight and pulled him out of the water. Just as they were about to go home, a friendly dolphin jumped out of the water. The dolphin said, "Thank you for saving your friend! I want to be your friend too!" Lily and Max were very happy. They hugged their new friend, the dolphin, and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to get toys from the store. One day, Tim saw a big toy car that he wanted. He asked his mom, "Can I get this car, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but you have to be honest and good." Tim's mom wanted to teach him to be honest. She told him a story about a girl who did not tell the truth. The girl lost her friends because they did not trust her. Tim did not want to lose his friends, so he promised to be honest. One day, Tim found a toy that was not his. He remembered what his mom taught him about being honest. He gave the toy back to his friend. His friend was happy and thanked him. Tim felt good about being honest and his mom was proud of him.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a fearful little dog named Spot. Spot was scared of the big fireplace in the living room. One day, Spot saw a ball near the fireplace. He wanted to play with the ball, but he was too scared to go near the fireplace. Spot's friend, a big cat named Whiskers, saw him looking sad. Whiskers asked, "What's wrong, Spot?" Spot told Whiskers about the ball and the fireplace. Whiskers had an idea. He said, "Let's reverse and walk backward to the fireplace. That way, you won't see the fire." Spot liked the idea. They both walked backward together, step by step. Spot felt less scared. When they reached the fireplace, Whiskers quickly grabbed the ball for Spot. They both reversed away from the fireplace. Spot was so happy to have his ball back. He thanked Whiskers for helping him. From that day on, Spot wasn't so fearful of the fireplace anymore. He knew that with the help of his friend, he could face his fears. And they played happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. She wore a red coat with a zip. Lucy loved to zip and unzip her coat. In her pocket, she had a small bag of peanuts. Lucy loved peanuts. She would eat them one by one. At the park, Lucy saw a small squirrel. The squirrel looked hungry and a little guilty. Lucy thought maybe the squirrel took someone's food. She wanted to help, so she took out her bag of peanuts. She gave one peanut to the squirrel. The squirrel was very happy. Lucy and the squirrel played at the park all day. They zipped down the slide and climbed the trees. The squirrel showed Lucy where it hid more peanuts. They laughed and played until it was time to go home. Lucy zipped up her coat and waved goodbye to her new friend.

One day, a pale cat named Tim went to play with his friend, a dog named Max. They liked to run, jump, and joke with each other. Today, they wanted to find a big tree to climb. As they walked, Max said, "I hope we find a tree soon. My leg hurts a bit." Tim thought of a way to help Max. "When we find the tree, I will give your leg a rub. It will make you feel better," he said. Max smiled and they kept looking for the tree. They found a big tree and climbed up. They joked and had fun. Then, Tim remembered his promise to Max. He gave Max's leg a gentle rub. Max felt better and said, "Thank you, Tim. You are a good friend." They played and laughed until the sun went down.
