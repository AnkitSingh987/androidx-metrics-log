Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Lime

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside with his friends. One day, they found something unusual. It was a big, green ball. Tim thought it was a lime. Tim wanted to remove the lime from the ground. He asked his friends to help him. They all pulled and pulled, but the lime did not move. Tim was sad. Then, a kind bird flew down from the sky. The bird used its beak to remove the lime. Tim and his friends were happy. They played with the lime all day long. And from that day on, they knew that even unusual things can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved her clothes. Every morning, she would look in her closet to decide what to wear. All of a sudden, she noticed something terrible. All of her favorite clothes were torn and ripped! She started to cry and asked her mom for help. Her mom hugged her little girl and said, "It's okay, we can fix them." The little girl looked up at her mom and said, "How?" Her mom smiled and said, "Let's go to the store. We can buy some new clothes to replace the old ones." The little girl was a bit hesitant, but she decided it was the best thing to do. So, she and her mom went to the store and bought some new clothes. When they got home, the little girl put her old clothes in a basket and put the new ones in her closet. She was so happy to have new clothes! She was no longer sad, and she still wears those clothes today.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Bob. Bob loved to whistle. One day he went for a walk and he saw a blueberry bush. It looked so interesting. He decided to give it a try and started to pick blueberries. It was fun and he was having a good time. But he started to get tired so he decided to have a little break. He sat down and started to whistle. His whistling was so beautiful and it made the blueberries very happy. The blueberries started to dance and the bush started to shake. Bob couldn't believe what he was seeing. It was so interesting. Bob kept on whistling until he had filled his basket with blueberries. Then he happily went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a different boy because he had a big, red hat. He liked to wear the hat every day, even on Tuesday. One Tuesday, Tim went to the park to play. He saw some kids playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play, too. But the kids saw his big, red hat and they started to ignore him. They didn't want to play with someone who was different. Tim felt sad and walked away. But then, he met a girl named Sue. Sue had a big, green hat. Sue said, "Let's play together!" So, Tim and Sue played all day. They had so much fun, and they didn't ignore each other. They knew that being different was okay.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop all day long. One day, as he was hopping, he found a comb on the ground. Bob picked up the comb and tried to use it on his fur. But he did not know how to use a comb. He got the comb stuck in his fur. Bob felt ashamed that he could not use the comb right. Bob's friend, a bird named Sally, saw him and wanted to help. Sally used her beak to get the comb out of Bob's fur. Bob thanked Sally and they both hopped and flew away, happy to be friends.

Dad told Ali to go and play in the garden. She was so proud she grabbed her skipping rope and started to skip. She skipped round and round the garden. She felt so happy and free. Suddenly, Ali saw a witch flying over the garden. She was so surprised, but the witch just smiled and waved at her. Ali waved back and the witch smiled even bigger. Ali felt very proud that the witch noticed her. The witch flew away with a twinkle in the sky. Ali skipped even faster round the garden. She chuckled as she skipped, thinking about the witch. She was so proud she'd seen a real witch.
