Category: Intermediate
Title: The Race and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big race. All the kids were excited to run and win the race. One little boy, Tom, really wanted to win. He had a toy hammer that he loved and wanted to show everyone. Before the race started, Tom's friend, Sam, said, "I want to win too, but I don't have a toy like your hammer." Tom felt upset because he didn't want his friend to be sad. Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's share my hammer and race together!" Sam smiled and they both started the race. They ran and ran, holding the hammer together. In the end, they crossed the finish line at the same time. All the kids cheered for Tom and Sam. They were both happy and proud. From that day on, they always shared their toys and played together. And they never felt upset again.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts and keep them in his cozy home. One day, Sam found a big, tasty nut under a leaf. He was so happy and wanted to take it to his home. As Sam was carrying the nut, he saw a gentle bird named Bella. Bella was very sad because she could not find any food for her baby birds. Sam wanted to help Bella, but he did not want to give away his big, tasty nut. Sam thought for a moment and had an idea. He decided to cover the nut with dirt and leaves. Then, he went to find more nuts for Bella and her baby birds. Sam found many small nuts and gave them to Bella. She was so happy and thanked Sam for his help. Sam went back to the spot where he had hidden the big, tasty nut. He uncovered it and took it back to his cozy home. Sam felt good about helping his friend Bella and still having his big, tasty nut. And from that day on, Sam and Bella became the best of friends.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to sing. He loved music. He went to his friend, a big cat named Sam. Tim asked Sam, "Can you help me sing?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's find a place with music." They looked around and found a park. The park had music available. It was a nice place for Tim to sing. Tim started to sing with the music. Sam clapped and danced. They had a lot of fun together. All the other animals came to listen too. Tim was happy that he could sing and make his friends smile.

Tim and Sue were twins who liked to play in the forest. They liked to find sticks, stones, leaves and bugs. One day, they saw a big log near a stream. The log was brown and had many holes. "Look, Sue, a log!" Tim said. "Let's go and see what is inside." "OK, Tim, but be careful. Maybe there are animals in the log," Sue said. They walked to the log and looked at the holes. They saw a squirrel, a bird, a spider and a worm. They were happy to see the animals. They said hello to them and asked them their names. "Hello, I am Tim and this is Sue. What are your names?" Tim asked. "Hello, I am Nibbles and this is Flap. We are friends who live in the log," the squirrel said. "Hello, I am Webby and this is Wriggle. We are friends who live in the log too," the spider said. The twins smiled and said they were happy to meet them. They asked them what they liked to do in the log. "We like to eat nuts and seeds and play hide and seek," Nibbles said. "We like to make webs and tunnels and explore the log," Webby said. The twins said they liked to do those things too. They asked them if they could join them. "Sure, you can join us. We like to make new friends," Flap said. "OK, but we have a rule. You have to be kind and gentle to the log and the animals. The log is our home and we love it," Wriggle said. The twins nodded and said they would follow the rule. They said they were kind and gentle to the forest and the animals too. They said they loved the forest and the animals too. "Good, then you are welcome to play with us. We have a lot of fun in the log. We can show you around and teach you some games," Nibbles said. "Yes, we can. We can also recommend you some good places to find food and water and shelter in the forest. We know the forest very well," Webby said. The twins thanked them and said they would like to learn from them. They said they were curious and eager to see more of the forest. "Great, then let's go and have some fun. Follow us and we will show you the way," Flap said. "OK, let's go. We are ready to follow you," Tim said. The twins and the animals left the log and went to the forest. They had a lot of fun playing and learning together. They became good friends and visited each other often. The twins were proud of their new friends and their new skills. The animals were proud of their new friends and their kindness. The log was proud of its guests and its holes. They all lived happily in the forest.
