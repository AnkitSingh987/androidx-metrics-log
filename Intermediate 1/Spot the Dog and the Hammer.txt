Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Hammer

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was always cold and would shiver a lot. One day, Spot saw a big hammer and had a crazy idea. He thought the hammer could help him stay warm. Spot took the hammer to his friend, Cat. "Cat, can you help me use this hammer to make a warm house?" Spot asked. Cat said, "Sure, I can help you, Spot!" Together, they used the hammer to build a small, warm house. Spot was so happy! He didn't shiver anymore. Spot and Cat played and talked in the warm house all day. They were the best of friends, and they loved their new little home.

One day, a fat dog went to the park. The fat dog liked to play with his ball. He would run and jump to catch the ball in the air. The fat dog was very happy when he played. But today, there was a big spray of water at the park. A man was watering the plants with a hose. The fat dog did not like the water. He was scared of the big spray. He wanted to play, but he did not want to get wet. The fat dog had an idea. He would wait for the man to stop spraying the water. Then, he could play with his ball. The fat dog hid behind a tree and watched the man. When the man stopped spraying the water, the fat dog ran out and played with his ball. He was happy again.

Once there was a young boy named Jack and he lived in a big house with his mom and dad. The house had lots of steps leading up to the front door. One day, Jack was feeling very lazy. He stayed in bed all day, refusing to take a single step. That night, when his mom and dad tried to get him to come downstairs for dinner, he still wouldn't get up. "Come on, Jack," said his dad. "Let's share this nice dinner we made." But Jack still wouldn't get up, so his dad came over to his bed and picked him up. Jack, still feeling lazy, fell asleep in his dad's arms as they descended the steps together. Finally, they reached the dining room, and his mom and dad shared a big, yummy dinner with Jack. After the meal, Jack didn't feel so lazy anymore and hugged his mom and dad with a big smile.

Tom was a naughty boy. He liked to stir up a lot of trouble and make a mess of things. One day, Tom decided to stir up Nature. He started messing up the grass and dirt and trees. At first, Tom thought it was fun. He made a huge mess with nothing but his bare hands. He was laughing and having a great time. But soon, Tom saw this was a bad idea. He looked up at the sky and noticed it was turning dark and angry. He heard rumbling and knew a storm was coming. He knew he had gone too far and started to worry. The storm became so bad that Nature could not be calmed. Trees snapped, lightning bolts struck the land, and rain poured down like never before. Tom was scared and ran inside. He was so naughty that Nature punished him by causing the storm. He was sorry and knew to never stir up Nature again.

Once upon a time, there was a silly bug named Tim. Tim liked to spin around all day. He would spin and spin until he was dizzy. Tim thought spinning was so much fun. One day, Tim saw a big zoom in the sky. It was a bird. The bird was fast and could fly high. Tim wanted to be like the bird. He thought if he could spin fast enough, he could fly too. Tim tried to spin as fast as he could. He spun so fast, he felt very stupid. Tim could not fly like the bird. But he learned that it was okay. He was happy being a spinning bug.

Once upon a time there was a small sack. The sack belonged to a boy who liked to store his toys in it. One day, a big hurricane came. The wind was so strong, it blew the sack away! As the sack flew through the sky, it passed by a farm with tons of useful items. The boy was so surprised and almost yelled for the sack to come back. But the hurricane was too strong, and the sack ripped into pieces. The boy was so sad he couldn't keep his toys in the useful sack anymore. He learned a lesson that day - sometimes bad things happen and we just have to move on!
