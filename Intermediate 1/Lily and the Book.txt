Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Book

Lily was a curious girl who liked to explore the house. One day, she saw a shiny key on the table. She wondered what it could unlock. She picked up the key and walked around, looking for a lock. She tried the key on the door, but it did not fit. She tried the key on the drawer, but it did not fit. She tried the key on the box, but it did not fit. She was about to give up, when she saw a cupboard in the corner. She had never opened that cupboard before. She ran to the cupboard and inserted the key. It fit! She turned the key and heard a click. She opened the cupboard and saw many things inside. There were books, toys, clothes, and pictures. She was very happy and excited. She wanted to see everything. She pulled out a book and opened it. It had colorful pictures and words. She tried to read the words, but they were too hard. She asked her mom, who was in the kitchen, to help her. "Mom, what does this say?" she called, holding up the book. Her mom came to the cupboard and saw the key and the book. She was surprised and smiled. "That's a very special book, Lily. It was my book when I was a little girl. It says 'The Adventures of Alice in Wonderland'. Do you want me to read it to you?" she said. "Yes, please!" Lily said, jumping up and down. Her mom took the book and the key and sat down on the floor. She hugged Lily and opened the book. "Once upon a time, there was a girl named Alice who was very curious. She followed a white rabbit down a hole and found a strange and wonderful world..." she began. Lily listened with wide eyes and a big smile. She was happy that she had unlocked the cupboard and found a treasure.

Once upon a time in a distant land, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to study the stars in the sky. Every night, she would go outside and look up at the bright stars. One day, Mia saw a big cloud covering the sky. She could not see the stars. Mia's heart was sad. She wanted to see the stars again. She thought about what she could do to make the cloud go away. Mia decided to ask her friends, the birds, for help. The birds flew up high and pushed the cloud away. The stars came out again! Mia's heart was happy. She thanked her friends and continued to study the stars every night.

Once upon a time, there lived a stubborn mama bear and her two cubs. Mama bear wanted to teach her two cubs how to find their way home all by themselves. But the cubs were small and still very young and they were not always very keen on listening to their mama bear's advice. Mama Bear tried to explain to the two cubs that if they ever felt lost, they should try to find a big tree or a big rock that they could remember and use it as a marker for their way home. But the cubs just laughed and started playing around instead. Mama Bear got very angry with them. "You must listen to me!" Mama Bear said in a very serious tone. But the cubs just stuck out their tongues at her and ran away. Mama Bear was very determined to teach her cubs the way home, so she stayed out in the woods with them for the whole day, guiding them and showing them how to remember their way home. Eventually, the cubs started to take notice and learned how to find their way home all by themselves. Mama Bear felt proud of her cubs. She was glad that they were so stubborn, it meant they had listened to her in the end.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the kitchen. They had a big pot and a wooden spoon. They pretended to cook soup with water and rice. They poured some rice into the pot and stirred it with the spoon. "Look, Lily, we are making yummy soup!" Ben said. "Yes, Ben, we are good cooks!" Lily said. They smiled and laughed. They were cheerful. But then they heard their mom coming. She was not happy. She did not like when they made a mess in the kitchen. "Oh no, mom is coming!" Ben said. "What do we do?" Lily said. They looked around. They saw a big cloth on the table. They had an idea. "Quick, cover the pot with the cloth!" Ben said. They grabbed the cloth and put it over the pot. They hoped mom would not see it. They ran to the living room and pretended to read books. Mom came into the kitchen. She saw the cloth on the pot. She was curious. She lifted the cloth and saw the rice and water. She was not angry. She was amused. She called Ben and Lily. "Ben, Lily, come here!" she said. They came into the kitchen. They looked at mom. They looked at the pot. They were scared. "Did you make this soup?" mom asked. They nodded. They were shy. Mom smiled. She was kind. "That's very creative of you. But next time, please ask me before you use the rice. And please clean up after you play. OK?" she said. They nodded. They were sorry. Mom hugged them. She was loving. "Let's have some real soup for lunch. And maybe some rice too. How does that sound?" she said. They nodded. They were happy. They helped mom make lunch. They ate the soup and the rice. They were full. They thanked mom. They were grateful. They learned a lesson. They were smart.
