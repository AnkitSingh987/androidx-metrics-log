Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Car and the Biggie

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Buddy. Buddy was a broken car. He could not go fast like the other cars. Buddy felt sad. One day, a big truck named Biggie saw Buddy. Biggie wanted to help. Biggie said, "I will support you, Buddy." Buddy was happy. Biggie helped Buddy go to the gas station. At the gas station, Buddy got some gas. Now, Buddy could go fast like the other cars. Buddy and Biggie were happy. They were good friends. They played and had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the wild grass. He would run, jump, and laugh all day. One day, Tim saw a big stove in his house. Tim wanted to help his mom cook. But the stove was too tall for him. Tim started to struggle. He tried to reach the stove, but he couldn't. Tim's mom saw him struggle. She smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. You can help me cook when you're bigger." Tim smiled back and went outside to play in the wild grass again. He knew that one day he would be big enough to help his mom cook on the stove.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird lived in a small cage. The bird was restless. It wanted to fly and play outside. One day, a kind girl saw the bird in the cage. She felt sad for the bird. She opened the cage door. The bird flew out and was very happy. The girl and the bird played in the garden. They saw a spray of water. The bird loved the water. It danced and sang in the spray. The girl and the bird had lots of fun. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a good pupil at her school. Lucy loved to play in the mud, so she always got very filthy. One day, her teacher saw how dirty she was. The teacher said, "Lucy, I recommend that you clean up before coming to school." Lucy nodded and promised she would. The next day, Lucy came to school clean and happy. She still loved to play in the mud, but now she always cleaned up before school. Lucy's teacher and friends were proud of her, and she was a good pupil.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the fair. He was very excited to play games and win a prize. As he walked around, he saw a big tent. He wanted to enter the tent to see what was inside. Inside the tent, he met another boy named Sam. Sam said, "I want to win the prize too!" They both played a game to see who could win the big prize. They threw balls at pins, and whoever knocked down the most pins would win. Tim and Sam played and played, but they could not knock down all the pins. They got very mad at each other. They started to fight and push each other. The man who ran the game said, "Stop it, you two!" But they did not listen. The man called a big, strong man to take Tim and Sam out of the tent. They were not allowed to play any more games or win any prizes. They both left the fair feeling sad and angry, without any prizes.

Once upon a time, in a big wood, there was a little bunny named Hope. Hope loved to hop around the wood and play with her friends. She liked to eat delicious carrots from the garden near her home. One day, while Hope was hopping in the wood, she found a big, shiny box. She did not know what was inside, but she felt that it was something good. Hope told her friends about the shiny box, and they all wanted to see it too. The next day, Hope and her friends went to the shiny box. They pushed and pushed, and the box opened. Inside, they found lots of delicious treats! They were all so happy and had a big party. From that day on, Hope and her friends knew that the wood was a magical place.
