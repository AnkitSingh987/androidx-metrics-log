Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to search for fun things in his big yard. One day, he saw a distant hill and thought, "I want to see what's over there!" So, Tom went on a long walk to the hill. As Tom walked, he met a girl named Sue. She said, "I'm searching for a mine with shiny rocks. Will you help me?" Tom said, "Yes! Let's search together." They walked and walked, looking for the mine. When they found the mine, they went inside. But instead of shiny rocks, they found a big, funny bird! The bird said, "This is my home! I took the shiny rocks and hid them." Tom and Sue were surprised, but they made a new friend. They all played together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a pretty little town, the sun was shining, and the birds were singing. The people in the town were happy. They played outside, and they hoped for more sunny days. One day, a big tornado came to the town. The people were scared. They ran to their houses and hoped that the tornado would go away soon. The tornado made a lot of noise, and it was very strong. After some time, the tornado left the town. The people came out of their houses. They saw that their pretty town was a mess. But they hoped that they could make it pretty again. They worked together to clean up and fix their town. Soon, the town was pretty once more, and the people hoped that the tornado would never come back.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a kitchen. In the kitchen, there was a cat named Kitty. Kitty was a fearful cat. She was scared of many things. One day, Kitty saw a big cup of water on the table. She was very thirsty, so she tried to drink from the cup. But Kitty was too small to reach it, and the cup fell. Water spilled everywhere on the floor. Kitty was scared and ran away. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came into the kitchen. He saw the wet floor and knew Kitty needed help. Doggy got a cloth and dried the floor. When Kitty saw the dry floor, she was happy and not scared anymore. Kitty learned that with the help of friends, there was no need to be fearful. The moral of the story is that friends can help us when we are scared, so we don't have to be fearful alone.

Lily was playing with her doll in the garden. She liked to make up stories for her doll and pretend she was a princess. She heard a loud noise from the street. She looked up and saw a big car. A dark man got out of the car. He had a hat and a coat. He looked at Lily and smiled. "Hello, little girl," he said. "Do you remember me? I am your uncle. I came to visit you and your mommy." Lily felt scared. She did not remember the man. She did not have an uncle. She wanted to run to her house, but the man was blocking her way. "Come with me," he said. "I have a surprise for you in the car. It is a big toy. You will like it." Lily shook her head. She did not want to go with the man. She did not want his toy. She wanted her mommy. She started to cry. "Help! Help!" she shouted. "Mommy! Mommy!" The man grabbed her arm. He tried to pull her to the car. Lily kicked and screamed. She bit his hand. He let go of her. He looked angry. "Stop that, you little brat!" he said. "You are coming with me, whether you like it or not!" But then, Lily heard another noise. She heard a siren. She saw a red and blue light. She saw a police car. She saw a policeman. He got out of the car. He had a badge and a gun. He ran to the man. He grabbed him and put him on the ground. He put handcuffs on him. "Are you okay, little girl?" he asked Lily. "Did he hurt you?" Lily nodded. She was okay. She was not hurt. She was happy to see the policeman. He was her friend. He helped her. "Thank you, policeman," she said. "You saved me. He is a bad man. He is not my uncle. I do not remember him." The policeman smiled. He hugged her. He took her to his car. He called her mommy. He told her what happened. He told her to come to the garden. He told her Lily was safe. Lily waited for her mommy. She felt brave. She felt proud. She remembered the policeman. He was a good man. He was her hero.
