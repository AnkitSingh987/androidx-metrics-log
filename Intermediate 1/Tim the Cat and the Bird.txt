Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cat and the Bird

One day, a big cat named Tim saw a little bird. The bird lay on the ground. Tim felt worried. He wanted to help the bird. Tim went closer and said, "Little bird, why do you lay on the ground?" The little bird looked up and said, "I am sad. My home is gone. The big wind came and blew it away. Now, I have no place to live. My home is a waste." Tim wanted to help the little bird. He tried to find a new home for the bird. He looked high and low, but he could not find a home. The little bird was sad. Tim was sad too. They could not find a home for the bird. In the end, the little bird had to live on the ground. Tim felt bad that he could not help his new friend. The little bird was still sad, and Tim was still worried. They did not have a happy ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big orange car. It was an ambulance. Tim had a question. He asked his mom, "What does that car do?" His mom said, "It helps people who are hurt or sick." Tim and his mom went to the park. They saw a man with a big hat. The man was sad. Tim asked the man, "Why are you sad?" The man said, "I lost my hat in the tree." Just then, the orange ambulance came to the park. The people from the ambulance saw the hat. They used a long stick to get the hat from the tree. The man was happy. Tim and his mom were surprised. The ambulance helped the man, even though he was not hurt or sick.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to look up at the sky. One day, he saw something different. It was a planet! Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I see a different thing in the sky! It's a planet!" His mom looked up and saw the planet too. She smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, that is a planet. It is very special." Tim and his mom went to the park to see the planet better. They sat on the grass and looked up at the sky. The planet was big and round. Tim was happy to see something different in the sky. He knew it was a special day.

Once upon a time, there was a little pasta. The pasta was unknown to all the other foods in the kitchen. It wanted to play with them, but they did not know it. One day, the pasta saw a big ball roll by. It wanted to roll too. The pasta tried to roll like the ball, but it was hard. It could not roll well. The pasta was sad. It wanted to roll and play with the other foods. Then, a kind spoon came to help the pasta. The spoon said, "I can help you roll." The spoon helped the pasta roll. The pasta was happy. It could roll and play with the other foods now. The other foods saw the pasta roll and they wanted to be its friend. The pasta was not unknown anymore. It had friends and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot had a favorite toy made of leather. He loved to play with it every day. Spot would value his toy more than anything else. One sunny day, Spot took his leather toy to the park. He was so happy to play with it. He would run and jump with his toy in his mouth. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Spot's toy away. Spot tried to chase after his toy, but it was too late. The wind carried his toy far, far away. Spot was very sad. He went home without his toy. The next day, Spot went to the park again, hoping to find his toy. But it was gone forever. Spot learned that sometimes, even the things we value most can be lost.

One day, a young boy named Tim found a big box of chocolate. He was very happy. He thought, "I can eat all the chocolate by myself!" But then, he realized that he had many friends who also liked chocolate. Tim went to his friend Sue and said, "Look, I have a big box of chocolate! Do you want to eat some with me?" Sue was very excited and said, "Yes, let's share the chocolate with all our friends!" So, Tim and Sue went to all their friends and shared the chocolate. They all laughed and had fun eating the chocolate together. But then, something unexpected happened. The chocolate was magic! It made them all float in the air! They all laughed even more as they floated around. Soon, the magic went away and they all came back down to the ground. Tim realized that sharing the chocolate with his friends was much more fun than eating it all by himself. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends is better than keeping things for yourself.
