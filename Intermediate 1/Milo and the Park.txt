Category: Intermediate
Title: Milo and the Park

Milo and his mom went to the park. It was a mild day and the sun was shining. Milo saw a big fountain. He ran over to it and splashed in the cool water. He loved it. Next, Milo and his mom spread a blanket on the grass. He lay down and closed his eyes. His mom kissed him on the head and said, “it’s time for your nap”. Milo was so tired that he fell asleep right away. He had a nice, long nap in the sunshine. When he woke up, he felt refreshed. He sat up and stretched. What a nice day he had!

Once upon a time there was a little lion who loved to color with crayons. He lived in a big, clean grass field with his lion family. One day, the little lion was coloring a picture with a big, yellow crayon. Suddenly his dad lion let out a big roar! The little lion was so scared, he dropped his crayon onto the ground. "Don't worry," said his dad, "I was just playing." The little lion smiled because his dad was right. He picked up his crayon, and with the help of his dad, they finished the picture they had started. The little lion was very proud of his work. He thanked his dad, and they walked back to the lion den. On the way, they roared together, enjoying the clean air and their day of coloring together.

Once upon a time in a beautiful forest, there lived a small bunny named Bobo. Bobo had a big friend named Kiko, who was a strong bear. They liked to play together all day long. One day, Bobo and Kiko found a long spear in the forest. "What is this?" asked Bobo. "I don't know, but it looks fun!" said Kiko. They decided to play with the spear, but they did not know that it could be dangerous. As they played, the spear got stuck in a big tree. Bobo and Kiko tried to pull it out, but it was too hard for them. They began to argue about who should try to get it out. "I will do it!" said Bobo. "No, I will!" said Kiko. Just then, a wise old owl saw them fighting. He told them, "You need to work together and help each other. Only then can you get the spear out of the tree." Bobo and Kiko listened to the owl and decided to work together. With Bobo pushing and Kiko pulling, they finally got the spear out of the tree. They were so happy and thanked the owl for his help. From that day on, Bobo and Kiko always helped each other and wiped away any problems they faced. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred lived in a small pond. The pond had cold water. Fred loved to play with his friends in the cold pond. They would jump and swim all day long. One day, it was very hot. The sun was shining bright. Fred and his friends were thirsty. They wanted to drink some water. Fred saw a bottle near the pond. The bottle had a green drink in it. Fred thought the green drink was for him because it was the same color as him. Fred's friend, a wise old turtle, told him not to drink the green drink. The turtle said, "We should only drink the cold water from our pond. The green drink could be bad for us." Fred listened to the wise turtle and did not drink the green drink. Instead, he and his friends drank the cold water from the pond. They felt better and continued to play happily. The moral of the story is to always listen to wise advice and not to take things that are not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a nice staff. It was special and belonged to a 3 year old girl. One day the girl was playing on the beach and she lost the staff in the sand. She started to cry and was very sad. Then a nice lady came up to her and said, 'Don't worry, dear. We can help you look for it. Let's look together!" So the girl and the nice lady started looking for the staff in the sand. After a few minutes, the girl shouted, "I see it! I see it!" The lady smiled and said, "Well done! Now let's get it together." The girl and the nice lady found the staff. The girl was so happy and grateful. She hugged the nice lady and said, "Thank you!"
