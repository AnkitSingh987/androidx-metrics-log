Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Shoes

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tom's mom asked him to go to the store. She gave him some money to buy food. Tom was happy and said, "Yes, mom!" He put on his shoes and went out the door. On his way to the store, Tom saw a slow, old man. The old man looked sad. Tom asked the old man, "Why are you sad?" The old man said, "I am hungry and I have no money for food." Tom thought for a moment and then he nodded. He gave the old man the money his mom gave him. The old man was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom went back home without any food. His mom asked, "Where is the food?" Tom told her about the old man and how he gave him the money. At first, his mom was upset, but then she smiled. She said, "You did a good thing, Tom. You helped someone in need." Tom felt proud and happy. The next day, when Tom went to the store, the old man was there. He gave Tom a big hug and said, "Thank you for being kind." The moral of the story is to always help others when you can.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and make new friends. One day, she saw a big stand with lots of toys. She wanted to have one, but she didn't have any money. Lily asked her mom for some money, but her mom said no. This made Lily very sad. She wanted to find a way to increase her toys, so she thought of a plan. She decided to trade one of her old toys for a new one at the stand. She went to the stand with her old toy and asked the stand owner if she could trade. The owner agreed, and Lily was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the toys away, including Lily's old toy and the new one she just got. Lily was very sad because she lost her old toy and didn't get a new one. She learned that sometimes, even when you try your best, things don't always go the way you want.

John was very sad. He had just found out that the beautiful summer park near his house was going to get closed. He wanted to go for one last walk. When he arrived, he noticed that the oxygen in the air was not as fresh as it used to be. John looked around and saw a little bird lying on the ground with its wings stretched out. It was trying to release itself from a big net. John knew he had to help the bird. He quickly ran over and started to unravel the net. The little bird looked at him with such gratitude in its eyes. After several minutes, John was finally able to release the bird. John felt much better. The bird flew away happily, and John felt a sense of hope return to his heart. He was so glad that he had been able to help the little bird. John smiled to himself and said, “Now that feels much better!”

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play games with his friends. One day, they decided to play a game of hide and seek. Tim was very excited to play and try to win. Tim started to look for his friends. He looked behind trees and under bushes. But he got confused and couldn't find anyone. Tim felt sad because he thought he would lose the game. Then, Tim saw his friend Lily hiding behind a big rock. He found her and was so happy! They both looked for their other friends together. At the end of the game, Tim and Lily found everyone. They were so happy that they didn't lose. They felt like they both got the win.

One day, a little bird was in a tree. The bird saw a big, great tree far away. The bird wanted to go to the great tree. But the bird was scared to fly so far. The bird's friend, Cat, saw the bird sigh. Cat asked, "Why are you sad, Bird?" The bird said, "I want to go to the great tree, but I am scared to fly so far." Cat had an idea. "Let's go together! I will walk and you can fly!" The bird was happy. They went to the great tree together. The bird was not scared anymore.
