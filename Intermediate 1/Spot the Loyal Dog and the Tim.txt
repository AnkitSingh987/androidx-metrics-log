Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Loyal Dog and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to step and play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They would run and jump and have lots of fun together. One sunny day, Tim had a message for his grandma. He needed to give it to her, but she lived far away. Tim asked Spot to help him deliver the message. Spot wagged his tail and agreed to help. Spot took the message in his mouth and stepped off to grandma's house. He walked and walked, never stopping. When he got to grandma's house, he gave her the message. Grandma was happy and gave Spot a big hug. Spot stepped back home to Tim, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad and she began to cry. She had lost her fancy toy. Sue looked everywhere, but she could not find it. One day, Sue went to the doctor with her mom. The doctor took an x-ray of Sue's tummy. Sue was scared, but her mom held her hand. The doctor looked at the x-ray and saw something strange. The doctor showed Sue and her mom the x-ray. There was Sue's fancy toy! It was in her tummy! Sue had eaten it by mistake. The doctor helped Sue get her toy out, and she was happy again. Sue promised to be more careful with her toys and not to eat them.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow dog. The dog liked to help his friends. He would supply them with things they needed. One day, his friend, a small cat, needed help to move a big weight. The yellow dog was happy to help. He went to the cat's house. The cat showed him the big weight. The dog tried to move the weight. He pushed and pushed. The weight started to move. Then, something unexpected happened. The big weight fell on the dog's tail. The dog was sad and his tail hurt. The cat was sorry, but the dog could not help anymore. The big weight was still there and the yellow dog went home with a hurt tail.

One day, a boy named Tim went with his mom to get a haircut. He wanted to look smart for his friend's birthday party. They walked into the haircut place and sat down. Tim's mom said to the haircut man, "Please give Tim a smart haircut." The haircut man nodded and began to cut Tim's hair. Tim and his mom started to discuss what games they will play at the party. After the haircut was done, Tim looked in the mirror and saw his smart haircut. He was very happy. He said, "Thank you, haircut man!" Tim and his mom left the haircut place and went home to get ready for the party. At the party, all of Tim's friends liked his smart haircut. They played fun games and ate cake. Tim had a great time at the party with his new haircut.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little cat. She loved to lay in the warm sun all day. One day, she saw a big chimney on a small house. The sweet little cat was curious, so she went to the house with the big chimney. She looked up and saw a bird on the chimney. The bird was singing a happy song. The sweet little cat wanted to be friends with the bird. She called out, "Hello, bird! Let's play together!" The bird flew down and they played all day. They became the best of friends and had lots of fun by the big chimney.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl who loved to dance. She would spin around and skip and move her feet in all kinds of fun ways. One day, she asked her Mommy, “Can I put on a helmet and dance?” Her Mommy smiled and said, “Sure honey! A helmet will keep you safe.” So the little girl put on her helmet and went outside to dance. As soon as her shoes hit the ground, she started to twirl and hop and jump. She felt so happy and carefree as she danced in the sun. And when she was finished, she twirled and thanked the sky for this wonderful day.
