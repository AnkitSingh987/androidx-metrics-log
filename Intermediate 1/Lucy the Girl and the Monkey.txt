Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Monkey

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the zoo with her mom and dad. The zoo was big and had many animals. Lucy saw a lovely, big elephant with long ears. She was very happy to see it. Lucy also saw a funny monkey. The monkey jumped and played with other monkeys. Lucy laughed and clapped her hands. She wanted to play with the monkey, but her mom said no. At the end of the day, Lucy was tired but she felt happy. She loved the zoo and all the animals. She couldn't wait to come back again and see her new animal friends.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to attach a note to his mom's soup. The note said, "I love you." Tim thought it would make his mom happy. He put the note on the soup and went to play with his toys. Soon, his mom came home. She saw the soup and the note. She said, "Oh, Tim, what a nice surprise!" But she did not know that this was not regular soup. Tim had put something funny in the soup. Mom took a big bite of the soup. "Yuck!" she said. "This is not good soup!" Tim was sad. He did not mean to make his mom unhappy. He wanted to make her smile. But now, his mom was not happy because of the bad soup. Tim learned that he should not play with food.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot was very tired and wanted to relax. He found a soft bed and lay down to rest. While Spot was relaxing, he heard a loud alarm. The alarm made him jump up. Spot wanted to know where the alarm was coming from, so he ran to find it. He saw a big red truck with loud sirens. The truck was going to help put out a fire. Spot followed the big red truck and saw the brave firefighters. They worked hard to put out the fire. Spot was very proud of them. When the fire was out, Spot went back to his soft bed and relaxed. He knew the brave firefighters would keep everyone safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey. He lived in a big tree with his elderly mom. The monkey loved to play, jump, and count. One day, the monkey wanted to count the bananas on the tree. He climbed up the tree and started counting. "One, two, three," he said, as he counted the yellow bananas. He was so happy to count the bananas. The elderly mom monkey watched her little monkey count. She was proud of her little monkey. They ate the bananas together and played all day long. And the little monkey kept counting everything he saw, making his elderly mom smile.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the museum. Tom was a smart boy who loved to learn new things. At the museum, they saw big animals and pretty rocks. Tom's mom said, "Tom, remember to wash your hands before we eat." In the museum, Tom saw a big door with a sign. The sign said, "Do not open." Tom was very curious. He looked at his mom, who was busy looking at the pretty rocks. Tom thought, "What could be behind the door?" Tom opened the door and found a big, clean room. Inside the room, there was a big sink. Tom thought, "This must be where they wash the pretty rocks!" Tom saw a pretty rock on the table. He wanted to help, so he picked up the rock and started to wash it. Just then, a man came into the room. He said, "Oh no! That rock was very old! It should not be washed!" Tom was scared. He did not know what to do. The man saw that Tom was just trying to help. He said, "It's okay. We can fix it. You are a smart boy to want to help." Tom and the man fixed the pretty rock. Tom's mom came in and saw what happened. She was proud of Tom for trying to help. They all learned a lot at the museum that day.
