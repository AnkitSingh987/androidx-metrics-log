Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a brave cat named Tom. Tom loved to watch movies. His favorite movie was about a big, strong lion who was very brave. One day, Tom was playing outside when he saw a little bird stuck in a tree. The bird was scared and needed help. Tom wanted to be brave like the lion in his movie. Tom climbed up the tree to save the little bird. When he reached the bird, he realized he was very high up. Tom was scared, but he remembered the lion in the movie. He took a deep breath and helped the bird. Together, they climbed down the tree. The little bird thanked Tom for being so brave. Tom was happy that he could be like the lion in his favorite movie. From that day on, Tom knew he could be brave too.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a letter on the floor. She picked it up and saw it had a pretty picture on it. Lily wanted to know what the letter said, but she could not read. She felt sad and needed comfort. Lily went to her mom for help. Her mom read the letter to her. It said that there was a dead tree in the park. Lily and her mom went to the park to see the tree. They thought it would be a sad tree with no leaves. When they got to the park, they saw the tree. But it was not a sad tree! The tree was full of beautiful flowers and birds. The letter was wrong! Lily and her mom were happy to see the tree alive and pretty. They felt comfort and joy.

Anna was feeling very excited. She was at the park and couldn't see what was hiding behind the tall hedge. She asked her daddy, "What's behind the hedge, Daddy?" Her daddy smiled and said, "Come and have a peek!" Anna crept closer to the hedge. Then, she spotted something sparkling. "What is it, Daddy?" she asked. Her daddy said, "It's a star! And it's so big and cheerful!" Anna was delighted. She clapped her hands in joy and said, "Let's play peek-a-boo with the star!" She and her daddy ran around the hedge and peeked out at the star. Then, they laughed and waved at it. Anna was so happy she didn't want to leave. She asked her daddy if they could visit the star again tomorrow. Her daddy replied, "Of course we can! Now let's go home, it's getting late." So Anna and her dad took one last peek at the star before they went home. Anna smiled and waved until she couldn't see it anymore.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a loyal tiger. The tiger had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, a little bird came to the tiger and said, "Can I join you and your friends to play?" The tiger said, "Yes, you can join us. Let's go and have fun together!" So, the little bird and the tiger went to play with their friends. While they were playing, a big rain started to fall. All the animals ran to find a safe place. The little bird could not fly because its wings were wet. The loyal tiger saw the bird and said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" The tiger carried the little bird on his back and took it to a safe place. The little bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, tiger! You are a good friend." From that day on, the little bird and the loyal tiger became best friends, and they always played together in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a happy dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball and run around the park. One day, Spot saw a big pole in the park. He wanted to play with the pole, but it was too big for him to move. Spot's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, saw Spot trying to play with the pole. Chirpy had an idea! Chirpy flew away and came back with a long string. Chirpy tied the string to the pole and told Spot to pull the string. But Spot refused. He didn't think it would work. Chirpy didn't give up. He asked all of their friends in the park to help. They all pulled the string together. Spot saw that it was working, so he joined in too. With everyone's help, they moved the big pole. Spot was so happy that he wagged his tail and barked with joy. From that day on, Spot knew that with the help of his friends, anything was possible.
