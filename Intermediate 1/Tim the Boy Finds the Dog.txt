Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Dog

One day, a little boy named Tim found a red marble. He liked it a lot. He showed it to his friend, Sue. "Look at my red marble!" said Tim. Sue liked it too. They wanted to play with it. Tim and Sue went to the park to play. They spread a big cloth on the grass. They put the red marble on the cloth. They rolled the marble to each other, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, a big dog came running. The dog took the red marble in its mouth. "Oh no!" said Sue. Tim and Sue chased the dog. The dog dropped the marble and ran away. Tim and Sue looked at the marble. It was not red anymore. It was blue! They laughed and continued to play with their new blue marble.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop and play all day long. One day, he found a big box in his room. It was covered in dust. Bobo wanted to know what was inside. Bobo hopped to the box and started to clean it. He wiped off the dust and then opened the box. Inside, he found many toys and games. Bobo was so happy! He revealed the toys to his friends and they played together all day. The moral of the story is that when you take the time to clean and look after your things, you can find hidden treasures. Bobo and his friends learned to always keep their rooms clean and tidy, and they had lots of fun playing with their new toys.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a clever girl named Sally. She loved to ask questions and learn new things. One day, she found a card on the ground. The card had a picture of a cat on it. Sally picked up the card and showed it to her friend, Tim. "Tim, do you know what this card is for?" Sally questioned. Tim looked at the card and thought for a moment. "I think it's a card for a game," he said. Sally and Tim decided to find out how to play the game with the card. They went to the park and saw their friend, Lucy. Sally showed the card to Lucy and questioned, "Do you know how to play the game with this card?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, it's a matching game. You need to find another card with the same picture." Sally, Tim, and Lucy searched the park for more cards. They found many cards with different pictures, but finally, they found another card with a cat on it. The three friends played the matching game together, and they had lots of fun. They took turns asking questions and helping each other find the matching cards. In the end, they all agreed that asking questions and being clever made the game even more enjoyable.

Once upon a time, in a thick forest, there lived a big bear and a small rabbit. They were good friends and played all day. The bear was strong and the rabbit was fast. One day, they saw a big tree with sweet fruits. The rabbit wanted to eat the fruits, but the bear warned him to wait. He said, "We must wait for the right time to eat the fruits." The rabbit listened to his friend and they waited. After a few days, the fruits were ready to eat. The bear and the rabbit enjoyed the sweet fruits together. They were happy they waited and listened to the bear's warning. They continued to play and have fun in the thick forest.

Once upon a time, in a fair land, there was a big cat. The cat liked to play with a small ball. One day, the cat saw a big flower. The cat went close to smell the flower. The cat felt a sneeze coming. "Ah-choo!" the cat sneezed. The sneeze was so big that it made the flower fly away. The cat felt sad and looked for the flower. The cat walked and walked. The cat saw a little girl. The little girl had the flower. The cat felt wonder and wanted the flower back. The cat tried to take the flower from the little girl, but the girl said "no." The cat tried again, but the girl still said "no." The cat felt very sad. The cat walked away without the flower. The cat never found a new flower to smell.
