Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Cat

One day, a sweet cat and a big dog were playing near a tree. They were very happy. But the cat saw a little bird up in the tree. The cat wanted to join the bird, so it started to climb the tree. The dog stayed on the ground and watched. As the cat got closer to the bird, the bird got scared. The bird didn't want the cat to join it. So, the bird tried to fly away. But as it flew, it hit a branch and started to fall. The dog saw the bird fall and ran fast to catch it. The dog saved the bird just in time. The cat came down from the tree, and they all became friends. The bird was happy that the dog saved it. The cat learned that it was better to play with its friends on the ground.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion who loved to roar. He had a friend named Tim. Tim liked to play the guitar. They lived in a happy forest. One day, the lion and Tim decided to make a song together. The lion said, "I will roar loud and you play your guitar." Tim was glad and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They started to make their song. The lion roared, and Tim played the guitar. All the animals in the forest came to listen. They danced and were very happy. The lion and Tim were glad to see everyone having fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to run very fast. One day, Tom's mom made a delicious cake. She told him, "Tom, please take this cake to your grandma's house." Tom put the cake in a box and started to speed to grandma's house. On the way, he met a big dog. The dog said, "Hello, Tom! What is in the box?" Tom said, "I have a delicious cake for my grandma." The big dog wanted the cake, so he made a plan. The dog said, "Tom, I know a faster way to grandma's house." Tom followed the dog, but it was not a faster way. The dog took Tom to his own house and ate the cake! Tom was sad but learned to not trust everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a big chest in a small house. The chest was full of toys. A girl named Lily and a boy named Tom lived in the house. They loved to play with the toys. One day, Lily and Tom were careless. They left their toys all over the floor. Their mom said, "You need to pick up your toys and put them back in the chest." Lily and Tom did not want to clean up. But then, they had an idea. They could unite and work together to pick up the toys. They picked up the toys and put them back in the chest. Their mom was happy, and they could play again.

Once upon a time there was a small girl called Sally. Sally was only three which meant she didn't understand many big words. She loved going on adventures to different places. One day she and her family went to the museum. Sally was so excited she ran around looking at all the different objects. She thought they were so cool. But Sally had to be careful. Some of the objects were very fragile. Sally ran off to find her mom when she heard her calling. "What are you doing here?" asked her mom. "Oh, I just wanted to look at the fragile things," replied Sally. Her mom smiled. "I know you hate museums," she said. "But there are still some cool things here." Sally smiled back. She knew her mom was right. She could still enjoy exploring the museum, even if she didn't like going there.

Once there was a bull named John. He was very bossy and always wanted to be in charge. One day, John went to the farmer's field and saw lots of sacks of hay. He wanted to play with them, so he stopped to investigate. But when he started pushing the sacks around, he got a surprise. Inside one of the sacks was a big cat! The cat wasn't happy and started to meow loudly. John was scared and jumped away. The farmer heard the noise and hurried to the field. He was angry at John for messing with the sacks and chased him away. John ran away fast, but he was never able to go back to the field again.
