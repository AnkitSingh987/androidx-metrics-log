Category: Intermediate
Title: Twinkle the Star and the Stars

Once upon a time, there was a little star named Twinkle. Twinkle lived in the dark sky with many other stars. At night, they would all come out and light up the sky. But Twinkle was sad because he was not as bright as the other stars. One day, Twinkle met a wise old star named Sparkle. "Why are you sad, Twinkle?" asked Sparkle. Twinkle said, "I want to be bright like you and the other stars, but I am too dark." Sparkle smiled and said, "You can be bright too. Let's test your light together." Twinkle and Sparkle began to test Twinkle's light. They played fun games and practiced shining brightly. As they played, Twinkle's light grew brighter and brighter. Soon, he was shining just as bright as the other stars. That night, Twinkle looked around and saw all the stars shining brightly, including himself. He was so happy that he had passed the test. Now, Twinkle could light up the dark sky with his new friends. From that day on, Twinkle was never sad again. He knew that with a little help and some fun tests, he could be a bright star too. And every night, Twinkle and his friends would light up the dark sky, making it a beautiful place for everyone to see.

John and his mom were walking in the park one day when they noticed a ghost! John was scared but his mom was brave. She told John that ghosts are not real, but John was still scared. To punish him for being scared, his mom said he had to march around the park three times. John was brave and did what his mom said. He marched around the park and the ghost was gone. John was very happy and his mom was happy that he was brave.

Once upon a time, there was a creative little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. His favorite car was a red one that could zoom very fast. Every day, Tim would make the car zoom all around his room. One day, Tim's mommy told him a secret. She said, "Tim, we are going to the park today. You can bring your red car and show your friends how fast it can zoom!" Tim was so happy. He couldn't wait to tell his friends about his car. At the park, Tim found his friends, and he told them all about his red car. They all watched as the car went zoom, zoom, zoom! Tim and his friends had lots of fun playing with the car. They were all so creative, making up new games to play. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought the hole was a good place to hide his yummy candy. He put the candy in the hole and went to play. Later, a bird saw the candy in the hole. The bird said, "This candy won't fit in my nest." The bird took the candy out of the hole and put ash from a fire in the hole instead. When Tim came back, he looked in the hole for his yummy candy. He was sad to see ash inside. "Who took my candy?" Tim asked. The bird heard Tim and said, "I'm sorry, I took your candy. I thought it was for me." Tim smiled and said, "That's okay, we can share the candy." They both ate the yummy candy together and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a sock named Sam. Sam was a very reliable sock. He always helped his friends stay clean. Sam had a best friend named Sally. Sally was a sock too. They lived in a cozy sock drawer. One day, Sam and Sally decided to go on an adventure. They wanted to find the best way to clean socks. They went outside and saw a big puddle. They thought, "Maybe this puddle can help us be clean!" But when they jumped in, they got dirty instead. This was not what they expected. Sam and Sally felt sad. They wanted to be clean and help their friends. Then, they saw a nice lady with a bucket of water and soap. The lady saw the dirty socks and smiled. She put Sam and Sally in the bucket and washed them. They became clean and shiny again. Sam and Sally were so happy. They went back to their sock drawer and told their friends about the adventure. Now, they knew the best way to be clean socks.
