Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ornament and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little ornament. It was shiny and pretty. The ornament lived on a big tree with its friends. The tree was in a happy house. One day, the ornament saw a girl dance. The girl danced around the tree. The ornament wanted to dance too. So, it started to wiggle and jiggle on the tree. The ornament danced so much that it fell off the tree. It was frightened! But the girl saw the ornament fall. She picked it up and put it back on the tree. The ornament was happy again. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila was a bossy girl. She liked to tell everyone what to do. She did not let anyone play with her toys. She did not let anyone sit next to her. She did not let anyone have fun. One day, Lila went to the park with her mom. She saw a pond with many ducks. She wanted to feed them. She took some bread from her mom and ran to the pond. She did not let anyone else feed the ducks. She said, "They are my ducks. Go away!" But the ducks did not like Lila. They did not like her bossy voice. They did not like her bread. They liked the other kids who were nice and gentle. They liked the other kids who shared their food. They liked the other kids who said, "Quack, quack!" The ducks started to peck at Lila. They pecked her hair. They pecked her dress. They pecked her shoes. They made her cry. Lila ran back to her mom. She said, "Mom, the ducks are mean. They hurt me!" Her mom hugged her and said, "Lila, the ducks are not mean. They are just like you. They do not like to be bossed around. They do not like to be alone. They like to be treated with kindness and respect. If you want to make friends with the ducks, you have to let them be themselves. You have to share with them. You have to say, 'Quack, quack!'" Lila learned her lesson. She said sorry to the ducks. She said sorry to the other kids. She let them feed the ducks with her. She let them sit next to her. She let them have fun. She said, "Quack, quack!" The ducks liked Lila. The kids liked Lila. Lila was happy. She was not bossy anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a well. A little girl named Lily loved to visit the well every day. She liked to throw stones in it and watch the water splash. One sunny day, Lily went to the well with her mom. She saw a big frog sitting next to the well. The frog looked at Lily and said, "Can you please request some delicious food for me? I am very hungry." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Okay, Mr. Frog, I will ask my mom to give you some food." She went to her mom and asked, "Mom, can we give the frog some food? He is very hungry." Her mom smiled and gave Lily a piece of bread. Lily took the bread and gave it to the frog. The frog ate the bread and said, "Thank you, Lily. That was very delicious!" From that day on, Lily and the frog became good friends. They played together by the well every day, and Lily always made sure her new friend had something delicious to eat.

Once upon a time, there was an old cat named Tuna. Tuna loved to play in the garden where the flowers bloom. One day, Tuna saw a big, pretty flower that she had never seen before. Tuna went to her friend, Bird, and said, "Look, Bird! A big flower! Can we play with it?" Bird looked at the flower and said, "I don't know, Tuna. It looks different. Maybe we should not play with it." Tuna did not listen to Bird. She wanted to play with the big flower. Tuna jumped on the flower, but it was not fun. The flower was not soft and nice like the other flowers. It was hard and sharp. Tuna got hurt and cried. She wished she had listened to her friend Bird.
