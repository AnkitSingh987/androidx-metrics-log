Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a nice cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball. One day, Tom's ball disappeared. He was very sad. Tom had an idea. He asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him find the ball. They looked under the bed, behind the chair, and in the yard. But they could not find the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. A bird flew down from the sky with Tom's ball in its beak. The bird dropped the ball into Tom's paws. Tom and Max were very happy. They played with the ball all day long.

Tom and Lily were bored. They did not like the lecture. The lecture was about big words and numbers. They did not understand. They wanted to play. They saw a slide in the corner of the room. The slide was shiny and smooth. They wanted to slide on it. They looked at the teacher. The teacher was busy talking. They looked at the other kids. The other kids were busy listening. Tom and Lily sneaked to the slide. They climbed up the ladder. They smiled at each other. They slid down the slide. They felt the wind in their hair. They felt happy. But the slide was not safe. The slide had a rough edge. The rough edge scratched Tom's leg. Tom cried out loud. He felt pain. He felt blood. The teacher heard Tom's cry. He stopped talking. He looked at Tom and Lily. He saw the slide. He saw the blood. He was angry. "Tom and Lily, what are you doing?" he shouted. "You are not supposed to slide on that slide. That slide is not for kids. That slide is for boxes. You are naughty. You are in trouble." Tom and Lily felt scared. They felt sorry. They ran to the teacher. They said sorry. They said they were bored. They said they wanted to play. The teacher sighed. He felt sorry too. He said he understood. He said he would help Tom. He said he would make the lecture more fun. He said he would let them play later. Tom and Lily felt better. They thanked the teacher. They listened to the lecture. They learned some big words and numbers. They waited for playtime. They hoped the slide would be fixed. They hoped they would not be naughty again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to visit her rich friend, Tim. Tim had a big house with lots of furniture. Lily liked to look at all the pretty things in Tim's house. She saw a big chair, a soft bed, and a tall shelf. Lily thought Tim's house was fun. Lily wanted to examine the furniture more. She touched the soft chair and sat on it. It was very comfy. Then, Lily went to the bed and jumped on it. The bed was bouncy and fun. She climbed the tall shelf and saw many toys on it. Lily was happy to play with the toys. After playing, Lily and Tim sat on the floor and shared their toys. They had a great time together. Lily learned that even if her friend was rich, they could still have fun and play together.

Once upon a time, in a faraway land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He always wanted to be big and strong like his dad. His dad was a great hunter who used a long spear to catch food for the family. One day, Tim found his dad's spear in the house. He wanted to be just like his dad, so he decided to raise the spear high in the air. He felt very big and strong holding the spear. Tim went outside to play with the spear. He was not careful and swung it around too fast. Suddenly, he tripped and fell down. The spear hit a big tree, and lots of bees came out. The bees were mad and stung Tim all over. Tim cried and ran back home. He learned that being impatient can sometimes lead to a bad ending.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to paint a big picture. He took out his expensive colors and started to paint. He painted a big house, a tree, and a happy sun. Tim was having so much fun. Suddenly, Tim saw a switch on the wall. He didn't know what it did. He thought it might make his painting even better. So, Tim decided to flip the switch. When he did, something unexpected happened. His painting came to life! The house, the tree, and the happy sun jumped off the paper and danced around the room. Tim was so surprised! He laughed and played with his new friends all day long.
