Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Tree

One day, Sam went to the park. He saw a big brown tree. It had a lot of leaves, so he wanted to explore it. Sam started to climb the tree, but the branch he was standing on suddenly snapped! Sam was scared, but the tree was very kind. It said, "Don't worry, Sam! I will take care of you." The tree opened up a big hole in the trunk and showed Sam a secret room inside. Inside the room, there was a big pile of cocoa beans. Sam was so excited, he quickly gathered up the cocoa and thanked the tree. But then he heard a loud noise! It was the screech of a dead bird! Sam didn't want to stay in that scary place anymore so he quickly left. He ran home and shared the cocoa with his family - a big happy surprise! He had a great adventure, and the cocoa made it even better.

One day, a huge cat with big ears went to play outside. The cat loved to play with its ball. It was a sunny day and the cat was very happy. The cat's name was Fluffy. As Fluffy played with the ball, it rolled far away. Fluffy ran to get the ball and saw a little mouse. The mouse said, "Hi, I am Tiny. Can I play with you?" Fluffy said, "Yes, let's play together!" They played and had fun. Then, a big wind came and took the ball. It went so high that it hit a tree and the tree started to fall! Fluffy and Tiny ran fast so the tree did not destroy them. They were safe and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a delicate little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a big tree with his family. They built their nest with soft grass and thin wire. Timmy loved to play with his brothers and sisters. One day, while Timmy was playing, a big wind came and blew the wire away. The nest started to fall apart. Timmy's mom and dad told him to be brave and find the wire to fix the nest. Timmy was scared but didn't surrender. Timmy flew around and found the wire near a big cat. The cat wanted to play with the wire too. Timmy had an idea. He asked the cat to help fix the nest with the wire. The cat agreed and they became friends. They fixed the nest and played together. The big wind made Timmy find a new friend.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, they find a big box of buttons in the closet. They think the buttons are shiny and pretty. They want to make necklaces and bracelets with them. "Look, I made a red necklace!" Tim says, showing Sam his work. "That's nice, but I made a blue bracelet!" Sam says, holding up his wrist. They keep making more and more jewelry with the buttons. They have fun, but they also start to argue. They want the same buttons, and they don't want to share. "Give me that green button, it's mine!" Tim says, grabbing a button from Sam's hand. "No, it's mine, I saw it first!" Sam says, pulling the button back. They tug and pull on the button, until it snaps. The button flies across the room and lands under the bed. They both gasp. "Oh no, we lost the button!" Tim says, feeling sad. "Let's go get it!" Sam says, crawling under the bed. But the bed is dark and dusty. There are many things under the bed, like books, socks, and toys. Sam can't see the button. He feels scared. "Tim, help me!" Sam says, calling his brother. Tim hears Sam's voice. He remembers that Sam is his brother, and he loves him. He decides to be brave and rescue him. He crawls under the bed too. He looks for Sam and the button. "Sam, where are you?" Tim says, looking around. "I'm here, Tim!" Sam says, waving his hand. Tim sees Sam's hand. He crawls towards him. He also sees the button. It is shining in a ray of light. He picks it up and gives it to Sam. "Here, Sam, I found the button!" Tim says, smiling. "Thank you, Tim, you are a good brother!" Sam says, hugging him. They both crawl out from under the bed. They are happy and proud. They decide to share the buttons and make more jewelry. They think the buttons are useful and fun. They also think that being kind and helpful is better than fighting. They hug each other and play with their toys.
