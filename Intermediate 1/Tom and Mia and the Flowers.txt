Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Flowers

Tom and Mia are friends who like to design clothes. They use paper, scissors, glue and crayons to make dresses, shirts, pants and hats. They have a lot of fun making different outfits. One day, Tom has an idea. He wants to make a unique shirt with a big pocket on the front. He thinks a pocket is useful and cool. He cuts a square of paper and glues it on his shirt. He is very proud of his design. "Look, Mia, I made a shirt with a pocket!" he says, showing her his work. Mia looks at Tom's shirt. She does not like it. She thinks a pocket is silly and ugly. She likes her dress with flowers and ribbons better. "Your shirt is bad, Tom. A pocket is not for clothes. A pocket is for pants or coats. Your shirt looks like a bag. I don't want to play with you anymore." she says, turning away from him. Tom feels sad and angry. He likes his shirt and his pocket. He thinks Mia is mean and rude. He does not want to be her friend anymore. "Why are you so mean, Mia? A pocket is for clothes too. A pocket is for anything you want. My shirt is unique and cool. You are just jealous. I don't want to play with you anymore either." he says, tearing his shirt off. Tom and Mia start to cry. They both feel hurt and lonely. They wish they could be friends again, but they don't know how to say sorry. They sit on opposite sides of the room, holding their clothes. They wonder if they can ever design clothes together again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a green olive. It was round and shiny. Tim thought olives were boring. But this one was special. Tim saw a tiny ant trying to attach a leaf to its home. The ant was having a hard time. Tim wanted to help. He said, "Hello, ant! Can I help you with that leaf?" The ant looked at Tim and said, "Yes, please! Thank you, Tim." Tim took the green olive and attached it to the ant's home. The ant was very happy. The olive made the ant's home look fun and not boring anymore. From that day on, Tim and the ant were good friends. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that even boring things can be special if you use them in a new way.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He felt gloomy because he had no friends to play with. As he walked, he saw a big gray animal. It was an elephant! Tim said "Hi" to the elephant. The elephant was happy to meet Tim. They played together all day. They ran, they laughed, and they had fun. Tim was not gloomy anymore. But then, something unexpected happened. The elephant turned into a little girl! She had been under a spell, and playing with Tim broke the spell. Now, Tim had a new friend to play with every day. They were both very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a blue vest. He liked the vest very much. Tim wanted to wear the vest to play outside. He put on the vest and went to the park. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam saw the blue vest and said, "I need that vest, Tim. Give it to me!" Tim did not want to give Sam his vest. They both started to cry. Just then, a big wind came and blew the blue vest away. Tim and Sam were very sad. But, they saw the vest land on a little dog. The dog looked so happy in the blue vest. Tim and Sam laughed and became friends again. They did not need the vest to have fun.

One day, a restless little dog named Max went for a walk. Max liked to run and jump all day. He was always full of energy. Max saw his friend, Sam the cat, playing with a toy screw. Max wanted to play too. Max went to Sam and asked, "Can you show me how to play with the screw?" Sam said, "Yes, Max, I will show you." Sam pushed the screw with his paw and Max watched. Then, Max tried to do it too. Max and Sam laughed and played with the screw together. After a while, Max and Sam were tired. They sat down under a big tree. Max was not restless anymore. He was happy and sleepy. Max and Sam took a nap under the tree, dreaming about all the fun they had playing with the screw.
