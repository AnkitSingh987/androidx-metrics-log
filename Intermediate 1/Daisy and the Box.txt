Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy and the Box

Once upon a time there was a sweet little puppy called Daisy. Daisy loved to play and every day she would sneak into the garden to run around and explore. One day while she was running around, she came across a shiny silver object lying in the grass. Daisy was so excited and she ran over to investigate. She sniffed and prodded it and realized it was a silver key, but she had no idea what it was for. Suddenly Daisy heard a voice calling out to her. It was the gardener! The gardener said, "Ah Daisy! How clever you are for finding that key! That belongs to a very special locked box and now you can help me out. Can you fetch the box for me and lay the key on top?" Daisy was so proud of herself and she set off to find the box. She found it tucked away near the edge of the garden and she brought it back to the gardener. She carefully laid the silver key on top and then the gardener opened the box. Inside was the most beautiful, playful kitten. The gardener said, "This little one needed help finding his home, now that you found the key, he's been saved! You did a wonderful job, Daisy!". Daisy happily wagged her tail and licked the playful kitten. She was so proud of herself.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a red helmet that he loved very much. He wore his red helmet every day when he played outside. One day, Tim met a new friend named Sue. Sue saw Tim's red helmet and said, "I like your red helmet, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! I like it too." Tim and Sue played together every day. They would discuss what games to play. They talked about their favorite toys and their favorite colors. Tim always wore his red helmet, and Sue liked to wear a yellow hat. One day, Tim and Sue decided to ride bikes. Tim wore his red helmet, and Sue wore her yellow hat. They had a lot of fun riding bikes together. They laughed and talked as they rode around their neighborhood. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were very happy. They had made a new friend, and they loved playing together. They knew they would have many more fun days together, with Tim wearing his red helmet and Sue wearing her yellow hat.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, they find a big tray in the kitchen. They have an idea. "Let's become pilots!" Lily says. "We can use the tray as a plane!" "OK!" Ben says. "I will be the captain and you will be the co-pilot!" They take the tray to their room and put it on the floor. They sit on the tray and pretend to fly. They make noises like a plane and move the tray up and down. "Look, we are flying over the mountains!" Lily says. "Wow, they are so high and snowy!" Ben says. They fly over the sea, the forest, the city and the desert. They see many things and have fun. "Are you hungry, co-pilot?" Ben asks. "Yes, captain, I am!" Lily says. They land the tray in their room and get off. They go to the kitchen and find some cookies and milk. They put them on the tray and take them back to their room. "Here is our lunch, co-pilot!" Ben says. "Thank you, captain!" Lily says. They eat the cookies and drink the milk. They are happy. They smile at each other. "We are the best pilots ever!" Lily says. "Yes, we are!" Ben says. They hug and laugh. They love their tray and their adventure.

Ben and Lily are twins. They like to play with their toy cars. They have a big box of cars. They have red cars, blue cars, yellow cars and green cars. They have cars that make noise and cars that are quiet. One day, they decide to drive their cars around the house. They make a road with a rug and some books. They drive their cars on the road. They go fast and slow. They go left and right. They have fun. "Beep beep!" Ben says. He drives his yellow car. His yellow car has a bell. He rings the bell. "I am a taxi. Where do you want to go?" "Meow!" Lily says. She drives her green car. Her green car has a cat. The cat is soft and nice. "I am a vet. I take care of animals." Ben and Lily drive their cars to each other. They stop and talk. "Hello, taxi. Can you take me to the park?" Lily asks. "Hello, vet. Can you check my cat?" Ben asks. "Sure, hop in." Ben says. He opens the door of his yellow car. "Sure, let me see." Lily says. She picks up the cat from her green car. They drive to the park. They play with the cat. They ring the bell. They have more fun. They are happy.
