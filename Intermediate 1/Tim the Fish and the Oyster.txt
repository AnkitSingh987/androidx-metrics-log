Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Fish and the Oyster

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming near the sea floor. He saw a big oyster with its shell open. Tim was curious and wanted to see what was inside. So, he decided to enter the oyster. Inside the oyster, Tim met a fierce crab named Carl. "Hey, little fish! What are you doing here?" Carl asked. Tim was scared of the crab, but he answered, "I just wanted to see what was inside the oyster." Carl laughed and said, "Well, you found me! I'm hiding from a big, scary shark. But now that you're here, we can be friends and protect each other." Just then, the shark swam by and saw the oyster open. The shark wanted to eat Tim and Carl, but they worked together to close the oyster shell. The shark couldn't get them, and they were safe. Tim and Carl were happy to be friends and protect each other. They stayed in the oyster and played games all day. Then, something unexpected happened. The shark came back, but this time, he was sad. "I'm sorry for trying to eat you," the shark said. "I was just hungry. Can we be friends too?" Tim and Carl thought about it and decided to give the shark a chance. They all became friends and played together in the sea. From that day on, they learned that even the fiercest creatures can become friends if they try.

Once upon a time, in a high tree, there lived a wise old owl. The owl had a lot of wisdom. He knew many things about the forest and the animals who lived there. One day, a little bird came to the wise old owl for help. "Mr. Owl," the little bird said, "my friend has vanished! Can you help me find her?" The wise old owl thought for a moment and then said, "I will help you, little bird." They looked and looked for the little bird's friend. They searched high and low. They asked other animals if they had seen her. Finally, they found the little bird's friend hiding in a bush. "Thank you, Mr. Owl, for using your wisdom to help me find my friend," said the little bird. The wise old owl smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. Remember to always help your friends when they need you." And from that day on, the little bird and her friend always helped each other and lived happily in the forest.

One day, a light cat named Fluffy went for a walk. Fluffy liked to wave her tail when she was happy. As she walked, she saw her friend, a dog named Spot. Spot had a big smile and liked to play with Fluffy. While they were playing, they felt the ground shake. It was an earthquake! Fluffy and Spot were scared. They ran to a safe place under a big tree. They held each other tight until the shaking stopped. After the earthquake, Fluffy and Spot were happy they were safe. They waved their tails and played some more. They knew they would always be there for each other, no matter what happened.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a party. At the party, there was a big cake. Tim was very happy. He wore a tight hat on his head. It was a fun party. Tim saw his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Tim, do you want to play?" Tim said, "Yes, let's play!" So, they played a game. They called the game "Find the Cake." They looked for the cake in the room. They found it on a big table. Tim and Sue laughed. They were happy they found the cake. They called their friends to see the cake. All the kids came to look at the cake. They liked the cake a lot. They ate the cake and had fun. The party was very good. Tim and Sue were happy they played the game.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round moon. The moon was nervous because it was too big for the sky. It wanted to be smaller so it could fit with the stars. The moon had an idea to cut itself to be smaller. The moon asked the sun for help. The sun was very hot and could cut things. The sun agreed to help the nervous moon. The sun got close to the moon and began to cut it. The moon became smaller and smaller. Now, the moon was not nervous anymore. It was happy and small, just like the stars. The moon thanked the sun for helping it. They became best friends and lived in the sky together. The moon and the sun played with the stars every night.
