Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Charming Cat and the Toys

One day, a charming cat named Kitty walked into a big hall. The hall was full of fun toys and yummy food. Kitty was very happy to play and eat. Then, a small dog named Max came into the hall. Max saw Kitty and said, "Hi Kitty, can I play with you?" Kitty said, "Yes, Max, let's play together!" They played with the toys and shared the yummy food. As they were playing, a big box fell from the top shelf. It was full of party hats and balloons! Kitty and Max were surprised. They put on the party hats and played with the balloons. Kitty said, "Thank you, Max, for being my friend and playing with me." Max said, "Thank you, Kitty, for sharing your fun hall with me." And they played together all day long.

One day, a big dog and a small cat went for a walk. They saw a thick puddle on the ground. The big dog said, "I want to get in the puddle!" The small cat said, "No, I don't want to get wet!" The big dog jumped into the puddle and splashed around. The small cat stayed on the side, watching. The big dog said, "Come on, it's fun!" But the small cat still said, "No, thank you." Then, something unexpected happened. A big fish jumped out of the puddle! The big dog and the small cat were very surprised. The big fish said, "Hello, friends! I live in this puddle!" The big dog and the small cat made a new friend that day, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy some food for dinner. Tim saw some green broccoli and asked, "Mom, can we get this?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Broccoli is good for you. Let's take it home." When they returned home, Tim's mom cooked the broccoli. But she forgot to add water, so it became dry. Tim tried to eat it, but it was not yummy. He told his mom, "Mom, the broccoli is dry. Can we make it better?" His mom added some water and cooked the broccoli again. This time, it was not dry. Tim ate the broccoli and said, "Yum! Now it's good!" From that day on, Tim and his mom always remembered to cook the broccoli with water. They were happy and healthy, eating yummy broccoli together.

One day, a helpful fox was walking in the woods. He liked to help his friends. He saw a little bird who was sad. The bird lost her way and could not find her home. The helpful fox said, "Do not be sad, little bird. I will help you find your home." The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, helpful fox." They walked and walked until they saw the bird's nest. The little bird was very happy to see her home. She said, "Thank you, helpful fox, for helping me find my home." The helpful fox smiled and went on his way to help more friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable girl named Sue. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sue's mom asked her to wrap a fork in a napkin for lunch. While Sue was wrapping the fork, she heard a loud noise. She looked around and saw a big, friendly bear in her kitchen! The bear was hungry and wanted to eat lunch with Sue and her mom. Sue's mom was surprised, but she knew the bear was kind. She let the bear stay for lunch. Sue wrapped another fork for the bear. They all ate lunch together and became good friends. From that day on, the bear would visit Sue and her mom for lunch, and Sue would always wrap a fork for her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the park. One day, Tim saw a tall stand with yummy treats. He wanted to eat all the treats, but his mom said no. Tim felt sad and thought of a plan. He waited for his mom to look away, and then he ate all the treats on the stand. But soon, Tim felt dizzy. His tummy hurt, and he started to cry. His mom came and saw what happened. She told Tim, "When we eat too many treats, we can suffer and feel bad. It is important to listen and not be greedy." Tim learned his lesson and promised to be a good boy.
