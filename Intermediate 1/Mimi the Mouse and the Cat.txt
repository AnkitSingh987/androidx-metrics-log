Category: Intermediate
Title: Mimi the Mouse and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved cheese more than anything in the world. She lived in a small hole in a big house. One day, Mimi felt cold and hungry. She started to think about where she could find some warm cheese. Mimi went out of her hole and saw a big, round, yellow cheese on the table. She was so happy! Mimi climbed up the table leg and ran to the cheese. As she got closer, she saw a big cat named Tom sitting next to the cheese. Mimi said, "Please, Tom, can I have some cheese? I am very cold and hungry." Tom thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, Mimi, you can have some cheese. But you must share it with me." Mimi agreed, and they both enjoyed the warm cheese together. From that day on, Mimi and Tom became good friends and always shared their food.

Once upon a time, there was a magic cat named Bob. Bob was restless. He liked to run and jump all day. One day, Bob met a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She lost her toy. "Hi, Sue. Why are you sad?" asked Bob. Sue told him about her lost toy. Bob wanted to help her. "I will use my magic to find your toy," said Bob. Sue was happy. They went to look for the toy together. Bob used his magic to find the toy. They found it in a big tree. But, the toy was not a toy. It was a real bird! The bird was stuck in the tree. Bob and Sue helped the bird. In the end, they became friends with the bird. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly frog. The frog lived near a pond. He liked to gather rocks and make a big pile. One day, he saw a small fan near the pond. The frog was happy and took the fan to his pile of rocks. A little girl came to the pond. She was looking for her fan. The girl saw the ugly frog with her fan. She was scared and ran away. The frog felt sad because he wanted to be friends with the girl. The next day, the girl came back to the pond. She saw the ugly frog again. This time, she was not scared. She walked up to the frog and asked for her fan back. The frog gave her the fan. The girl smiled and said, "Thank you, Mr. Frog!" The frog was happy because he made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old man named Mr. Smith. He lived in a small house all by himself. Every day, he would sit in his chair and look out the window, feeling sad and alone. One day, Mr. Smith found a small candle in his house. He decided to start using the candle to light up his room. As the candle burned, it made his room feel warm and cozy. The light from the candle made Mr. Smith feel a little less grumpy. Soon, the people in the town saw the light from Mr. Smith's candle shining through his window. They came to visit him and became his friends. Mr. Smith learned that sharing his light made him feel happy, and he was not grumpy anymore. The moral of the story is that sharing our light with others can make us feel happy and less alone.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely big armchair. It was very soft and everyone liked to sit in it. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to sit in the armchair to comfort himself after he fell down and hurt his knee. As Tim sat in the lovely armchair, he heard a small voice. The voice said, "Hello, I am the armchair. I can talk!" Tim was very surprised. He had never heard a talking armchair before. The armchair said, "I am a magic armchair. I can make your hurt knee feel better." Tim was happy to hear this. He let the armchair help him, and his knee felt better right away. From that day on, Tim and the magic armchair were the best of friends.
