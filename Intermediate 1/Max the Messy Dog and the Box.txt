Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Messy Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Max. Max was always playing in the mud and making a big mess. One day, Max was playing outside when he saw a big box. The box had a picture of a lung on it. Max was very curious about the box. Max's owner, a kind man named Tom, saw the box too. He decided to order the box to see what was inside. When the box arrived, Tom opened it and found a big, soft lung toy for Max to play with. Max was very happy and wagged his tail. Max played with the lung toy every day. He would toss it in the air and catch it with his mouth. Even though Max was still a messy dog, he loved his new toy. Tom was happy to see Max having so much fun. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, Tim saw a big bookcase in his room. He wanted to get a book from the top. He tried to reach it, but he was too small. Tim felt stupid because he could not get the book. Tim started to worry. He wanted the book so much. He thought of a plan. Tim got a chair to stand on. He climbed up, but it was still too high. Tim got another chair and put it on top. Now, Tim could reach the book. He was so happy. But then, the chairs wobbled. Tim was scared. He held on tight and the chairs stopped moving. Tim got the book and climbed down safely. He was not worried or feeling stupid anymore.

Sara likes to imagine. She imagines she is a princess in a big castle. She imagines she has a horse and a crown and a dress. She imagines she has a friend who is a dragon. The dragon is big and green and can fly and breathe fire. One day, Sara goes to the park with her mom. She sees a big pile of dirt and sand. She thinks it looks like a castle. She runs to the pile and starts to dig and build. She makes a tower and a gate and a wall. She imagines her dragon friend is there with her. She pretends to ride on his back and fly over the park. But then, a boy comes to the pile. He has a shovel and a bucket. He wants to play too. He says, "Can I join you?" Sara looks at him. He is dirty and has a runny nose. She does not want to share her castle. She says, "No, this is my castle. Go away!" The boy looks sad. He says, "But I want to be your friend. I like dragons too." Sara shakes her head. She says, "You are too dirty. You can't be my friend. You can't play with my dragon." The boy walks away. He feels lonely. He sits on a bench and watches Sara. Sara plays by herself. She imagines her dragon friend is happy. But he is not. He is sad too. He thinks Sara is mean. He thinks the boy is nice. He wishes Sara would share her castle. He wishes Sara would make a new friend. He wishes Sara would not be so dirty. He wishes Sara would be more kind.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to find a yummy treat. He went to the kitchen and saw a big freezer. The freezer was very cold and had lots of ice on it. It was a little filthy because it had not been cleaned in a long time. Tim opened the freezer and saw many things inside. He saw ice cream, frozen fruit, and some ice cubes. Tim wanted to guess what was behind a big box. He moved the box and found something wrapped in a paper. He opened the paper and saw a very old piece of cake. It was very filthy and had lots of dirt on it. Tim thought it was still good to eat. He took a big bite of the cake and did not like it. It tasted bad and made his tummy hurt. Tim's mommy came into the kitchen and saw him with the filthy cake. She was not happy and told him to never eat old food from the freezer again. Tim felt sad and his tummy hurt even more. He learned that eating dirty and old food was not a good idea.
