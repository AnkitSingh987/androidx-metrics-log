Category: Intermediate
Title: The Plane and the Two

Once upon a time there was a plane. It was dull and not very exciting. Two friends wanted to take a ride in the plane, so they decided to shake it. They shook and shook the plane until well and truly it began to move. The two friends felt so happy and excited as the plane moved up, up and away. As they moved higher and higher in the sky they could see far below the world they lived in. They waved goodbye as they flew away into the sky. They kept shaking the plane and before they knew it they were surrounded with white puffy clouds. The two friends laughed and enjoyed the ride. Soon they had to come back down again. As they came back to the ground they were already planning their next adventure. They thanked the plane for the great time they had together.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Bob. Bob loved to play with his puzzle. One sunny day, Bob was in the park playing with his puzzle when he met a new friend. This new friend was a little bird named Tim. "Hi, I'm Tim! What are you playing with?" Tim asked Bob. "I'm playing with my puzzle," Bob said. "Do you want to play too?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you!" As they played with the puzzle, they had to mind where they put the pieces. They worked together to find the right spots for each piece. After a while, they finished the puzzle and were very proud of themselves. They became best friends and played together every day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly man named Mr. Happy. He loved to make kids smile. One day, he went to the park with a big bag of balloons. At the park, he saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy had a frown on his face, and he looked very sad. Mr. Happy walked up to Timmy and said, "Hi, little boy! Why are you sad?" Timmy looked at Mr. Happy and said, "I lost my toy, and now I'm sad." Mr. Happy wanted to help Timmy feel better, so he took out a big, red balloon from his bag. Mr. Happy said, "Here, Timmy! This balloon is for you. It will help you feel better." Timmy took the balloon and his frown turned into a big smile. He said, "Thank you, Mr. Happy! I love my new balloon!" Timmy and Mr. Happy played in the park with the balloon all day long. As the sun began to set, Timmy's mom called him to go home. Timmy hugged Mr. Happy and said, "Thank you for making me happy today!" Mr. Happy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Timmy! Remember, a balloon can always bring a smile to your face." And with that, Timmy and Mr. Happy said goodbye, and Timmy went home with his big, red balloon.

Lily was sick. She had a bad cough and a hot head. She did not want to play or eat. She lay in her bed and felt sad. Her mom came in with a spoon and a bottle. "This is medicine, Lily. It will make you feel better. Can you take some for me?" she asked. Lily shook her head. She did not like medicine. It was yucky and bitter. She made a face and hid under her blanket. Mom smiled and had an idea. She went out and came back with a furry toy. It was a brown bear with a red scarf. "This is Ben, Lily. He is your new friend. He is also sick and needs medicine. Can you help him?" she said. Lily peeked out and saw the bear. She liked bears. They were soft and cuddly. She reached out and took the bear. She hugged him and felt his scarf. "Hello, Ben. I'm Lily. Do you want some medicine?" she asked. Ben nodded. He was a good bear. He opened his mouth and mom gave him some medicine. He swallowed and smiled. "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind. I hope you feel better soon," he said. Lily smiled too. She saw that medicine was not so bad. It helped Ben and it could help her. She decided to try some too. She opened her mouth and mom gave her some medicine. She swallowed and made a funny noise. "It's still yucky, but I hope it works," she said. Mom hugged her and kissed her forehead. "You are very brave, Lily. I'm proud of you. I hope you and Ben have sweet dreams," she said. She tucked them in and turned off the light. Lily snuggled with Ben and closed her eyes. She felt a little better. She hoped she would be well soon. She hoped she could play and eat and laugh again. She hoped Ben would stay with her. He was her new friend. He was a furry friend. He was a hopeful friend.
