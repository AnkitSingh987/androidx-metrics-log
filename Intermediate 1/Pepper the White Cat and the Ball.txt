Category: Intermediate
Title: Pepper the White Cat and the Ball

One day, a white cat named Pepper was playing outside. She saw a big ball and wanted to play with it. She ran fast and jumped high to catch the ball. But the ball did not stop. It kept rolling away. A little boy named Tim saw Pepper chasing the ball. He wanted to help her. He ran after the ball too. "Stop, ball!" Tim shouted. But the ball did not listen. It kept rolling and rolling. Pepper and Tim were getting tired, but they did not give up. Finally, they worked together to stop the ball. Pepper jumped on the ball and Tim held it tight. They were so happy they got the ball to stop. They learned that working together made things easier. From that day on, Pepper and Tim always played together and helped each other when things got hard.

Lily liked to record her voice with her toy phone. She would say funny things and then play them back. She thought it was very fun to hear herself. One day, she had a big idea. She wanted to record her brother Max when he was sleeping. She thought he would make funny noises and she could laugh at them. She waited until he was in his bed and then she sneaked into his room with her toy phone. She put the phone near his ear and pressed the button. She said, "Max, wake up! There is a monster under your bed!" She tried to sound scary, but she was a little girl and her voice was not very loud. She waited for Max to scream, but he did not. He opened his eyes and saw her. He was not scared. He was angry. He grabbed the phone and threw it across the room. It broke into pieces. Lily started to cry. She did not mean to make Max mad. She just wanted to have fun. She ran out of the room and hugged her mom. She said she was sorry. Mom was not happy. She said Lily should not record Max when he was sleeping. She said it was not nice. She said Lily had to say sorry to Max and help him clean up the mess. Lily did not like to say sorry or to clean up. She wished she had not recorded Max. She learned her lesson. She decided to record only herself from now on.

Once there was a panda. He was very busy during the day but at night he liked to stay in his house. Every night he stayed in his house and played with his toys. He had lots of fun and was always happy. One night he heard a noise in his garden. He looked out the window and saw a little mouse! He ran outside to say hello, but the mouse was too scared and ran away. The panda was sad that the little mouse didn't stay, so he put some food outside his door. The next night, the panda heard the same noise again. He quickly looked outside, and there was the little mouse eating the food! The panda was excited and ran outside to say hello and the mouse stayed! They became very good friends and played together every night. The panda was no longer busy when the sun went down, he was always looking forward to seeing his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a modest boat. One day, the boat went to sail on the big blue sea. The boat liked to sail and feel the wind. While sailing, the boat met a little oyster. The oyster said, "Hi, boat! I am lost. Can you help me find my family?" The boat said, "Yes, little oyster. I will help you find your family." The boat and the oyster sailed together. They looked and looked for the oyster's family. Then, they saw many oysters on a big rock. The little oyster was happy to see its family. The boat said, "Bye, little oyster! Have fun with your family." The little oyster said, "Thank you, boat! You are a good friend."

One day, a little bird was flying in the sky. The bird felt dizzy and lost. The bird saw a big tree and sat on a branch. The tree was very tall and had many leaves. A cat saw the bird and said, "Hello, bird! Why are you dizzy and lost?" The bird said, "I don't know where my home is. Can you help me find it?" The cat said, "Yes, I can help you." The cat and the bird looked for the bird's home. They looked up and down, left and right. Then, they found a house with many birds. The bird was so happy! But, the birds in the house said, "You are not one of us. You are a different bird." The bird felt sad. The cat said, "Don't be sad, bird. We will find your real home." They looked more and found another house with birds that looked like the lost bird. The lost bird was so happy to find his family! The bird said, "Thank you, cat, for helping me find my home." The cat and the bird became best friends. They played together every day. The bird never felt dizzy or lost again.
