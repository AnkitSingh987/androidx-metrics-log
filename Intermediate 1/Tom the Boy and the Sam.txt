Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Sam

One day, a boy named Tom went to school. It was a normal day, and Tom was happy. He had a big exam today. Tom studied a lot and was ready for the exam. He knew he would do well. At school, Tom met his friend, Sam. Sam was sad. He did not study for the exam. Sam was scared he would not do well. Tom wanted to help Sam. Tom said, "Sam, don't worry. We can share my notes and you can learn fast." Sam was happy. He said, "Thank you, Tom!" They shared the notes and studied together. Sam learned a lot from Tom's notes. They both took the exam and did well. Tom and Sam were happy they shared and helped each other.

Sara and Tom were playing with blocks. They liked to build towers and houses with the blocks. But they did not have many blocks, so they had to share them. "Can I have that blue block, please?" Sara asked Tom. "No, it is mine. I need it for my tower," Tom said. "But you have many blue blocks. I only have one. Please share with me," Sara said. "No, no, no. Go away. Find your own blocks," Tom said. He pushed Sara away. Sara was sad and angry. She did not like Tom. He was ignorant. He did not know how to share. He did not know the truth. Sharing was good. Sharing made friends. She went to find her teacher. She told her what Tom did. The teacher was not happy. She went to talk to Tom. "Tom, why did you push Sara? Why did you not share your blocks with her?" the teacher asked. "Because they are mine. I do not want to share. She can play with something else," Tom said. "That is not nice, Tom. That is not the truth. The blocks are not yours. They are for everyone to play with. You have to share them. Sharing is caring. Sharing is fun. Do you understand?" the teacher said. Tom looked at the teacher. He looked at Sara. He looked at his tower. He felt bad. He did not want to be ignorant. He wanted to be nice. He wanted to have friends. He nodded his head. He said sorry to Sara. He gave her some of his blue blocks. Sara smiled. She said thank you. She gave him some of her red blocks. They started to build together. They shared their blocks. They shared their ideas. They had fun. They became friends. They learned the truth.

Once there was a little girl named Emma. She was only 3 years old and was very brave. One day, she wanted to explore the world outside her home. She stumbled upon a big, green forest. Excited, she ran into it, feeling the fresh, fragrant grass beneath her feet. Suddenly, she heard a low, rumbling voice. "Careful!" said the voice. "This forest is dangerous. If you want to stay safe, you need to protect yourself." "Protect myself? How?" asked Emma. The voice revealed that there was a bad creature in the forest called a monster. It could be kept away with salt. So Emma took a handful of salt and rubbed it all over herself. The voice chuckled and told her, "Good! Now you are protected and can explore the forest without worry." Emma smiled and ran off happily. When Emma returned home she told her parents what had happened. They were very impressed with her courage and thanked her for protecting herself with the salt.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly pilot. He liked to fly big planes up in the sky. He had a lot of friends because he was so nice. He would wave to the people when he landed his plane. They all liked to see him. One day, the pilot heard a small bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly high like the other birds. The pilot wanted to help the little bird. He had an idea to make the bird happy. The friendly pilot took the little bird with him in his big plane. They flew high up in the sky together. The bird was so happy and thanked the pilot. The pilot smiled and knew he made a new friend. From that day on, the bird and the pilot were always friends.
