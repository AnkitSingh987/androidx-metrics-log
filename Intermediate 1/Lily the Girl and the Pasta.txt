Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Pasta

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat pasta. One day, her mom said she would introduce her to a new, easy pasta. Lily was very happy and excited. The next day, Lily went to the kitchen. Her mom was cooking the easy pasta. Lily could smell the yummy pasta. She knew it would be very good. Lily's mom told her to wait and see the new pasta. Finally, it was time for dinner. Lily's mom showed her the new pasta. It was a fun shape! Lily loved the new pasta. She ate it all up. The easy pasta was very good. Lily's mom was happy that she liked it.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bird named Tim. Tim was a very impatient bird. He always wanted to fly high and fast. One day, Tim saw a big, red balloon in the sky. He wanted to catch it and fly with it. Tim tried to fly up to the balloon, but it was too high. He had an idea. He would wait for the balloon to come down. Tim sat on a branch and kept a close look at the balloon. He knew it would come down soon. After a long wait, the balloon came down near Tim. He was so excited! He caught the balloon and started to fly with it. But then, Tim remembered that he had to release the balloon to let it go free. He let the balloon go, and it flew up into the sky again. Tim felt happy that he could catch the balloon and set it free. He learned that sometimes, being patient can bring good things.

Once upon a time, there was a big, smooth rock. This rock lived in a hot place with lots of sun. The rock liked to feel the sun on its smooth face. It would sit in the hot sun all day and watch the little bugs run around. One day, the smooth rock heard a loud noise. The noise was so loud that it made the rock jump. The rock looked around and saw a big tree had fallen. The tree had split in half! The rock was very surprised. The smooth rock decided to help. It rolled over to the split tree and pushed the two halves back together. The tree was very happy and thanked the rock. The rock and the tree became good friends. They spent their days in the hot sun, being happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a small bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with a lot of branches. One day, it was very cold and wet outside. Tim felt miserable because he did not like the rain. Tim's friend, Sam the squirrel, saw that Tim was not happy. Sam wanted to help Tim feel better. Sam said, "Come play with me, Tim!" But Tim did not want to play. Sam did not give up. He insist, "Please play with me, Tim!" Tim finally said yes and they played on the branch together. They jumped and laughed. Soon, Tim was not miserable anymore. He was very happy. Sam and Tim played all day, and they were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy car. One day, he found a small screw on the floor. He picked it up and looked at it. Tim thought it would be fun to put the screw in his toy car. He tried to make the screw fit in the car, but it was too big. Tim pushed and pushed, but it just would not fit. He was sad, but he did not give up. He tried again, but this time he pushed too hard. The car broke into two pieces. Tim was very sad that his toy car was broken. He showed his mom, and she said she would fix it. She took the screw and the broken car and put them back together. Now, Tim's car was fixed and he was happy again. He learned to be more careful and not force things that do not fit.
