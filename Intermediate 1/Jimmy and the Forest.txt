Category: Intermediate
Title: Jimmy and the Forest

Jimmy was running through the forest. It was dark, but he hoped to find a way home. Suddenly, he saw an old, smelly hole in a tree. He peeped inside, but it was too dark to see. "I can't go in there, it's too dark," he said to himself. But suddenly, a voice came from inside the hole. "It's okay Jimmy. Come inside, I won't hurt you." Jimmy was scared, but he really wanted to get home, so he crawled into the hole. But when he climbed into the hole, there was nothing and nobody there. Jimmy started to cry. He was lost and he didn't know how to get home. He shouted and screamed, but nothing came out. He just curled up and hoped that somebody would come and find him. Sadly, nobody ever did. Little Jimmy was never seen again.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a stranger. The stranger had a big, red ball. Sue wanted to play with the ball, but she was shy. The stranger saw Sue and said, "Hi! My name is Tom. Do you want to play with my ball?" Sue was happy and said, "Yes, please!" Tom tried to explain how to play the game. But Sue was stubborn and didn't listen. Sue threw the ball too hard and it went far away. She was sad. Tom said, "It's okay. We can find it. Next time, listen when I explain." They found the ball and played all day. Sue learned to listen and had lots of fun.

One day, a little cat was walking down a lonely road. She saw a long strip of grass. The cat thought it was a great place to hide and play. So, she ran into the grass and started to hide. A little dog came by and saw the cat's tail. The dog said, "Hey, cat! What are you doing?" The cat replied, "I am hiding in this grass. It is fun! Do you want to play with me?" The dog wagged his tail and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" The cat and the dog played hide and seek in the strip of grass. They had so much fun together. They were not lonely anymore. From that day on, they became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and eat food. He had a big mouth and loved to eat fish. One day, Tom saw a big fish in a glass bowl. He wanted to eat it, but the fish was inside the glass. Tom thought and thought about how to get the fish. He decided to break the glass bowl. He pushed the bowl off the table with his paw. The glass broke and the fish fell on the floor. Tom was happy and ready to eat the fish with his big mouth. But then, something unexpected happened. The fish began to talk! It said, "Please don't eat me, Tom. I am a magic fish. If you let me go, I will give you a wish." Tom was surprised and decided to let the fish go. The fish swam away and gave Tom a wish. Tom wished to never be lazy again. From that day on, Tom was an active and happy cat.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog every day. He would run fast, then slow, then fast again. He liked to change how he ran. It was fun! One day, while Spot was on a jog, he met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi Spot! Can I jog with you?" Spot was happy and said, "Yes, let's jog together!" So, Spot and the bird jogged together. They changed how they jogged, sometimes fast and sometimes slow. They laughed and had a great time. They became best friends and jogged together every day.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved fashion. She liked to wear pretty dresses and big hats. One sunny day, Lily went to play outside with her brother, Tom. They saw a big butterfly. "Look, Lily! A big butterfly!" Tom said. "Let's catch it!" They tried to catch the butterfly, but it flew away. Lily and Tom started to fight. "I want to catch it!" Lily said. "No, I want to catch it!" Tom said. They were very mad at each other. Then, their mom came outside. "Stop fighting," she said. "Why don't you work together to catch the butterfly?" Lily and Tom thought about it and agreed. Lily and Tom worked together, and they caught the big butterfly. They were happy and stopped fighting. They learned that working together is better than fighting.
