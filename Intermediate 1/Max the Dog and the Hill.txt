Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Hill

One day, a big dog named Max went to play at the park. He saw a cart with wheels. Max was confused. He did not know what it was for. He thought it was a toy, so he used his nose to push it. The cart started to roll down the hill. As the cart rolled down the hill, it bumped into a lady. She was giving a massage to her friend. The lady and her friend were surprised. They did not expect a cart to roll into them. They looked up and saw Max at the top of the hill. Max felt bad, so he ran down the hill to help. He pushed the cart back up the hill. The lady and her friend were not mad. They laughed and thought it was funny. Max learned that the cart was not a toy, but he made new friends. The lady even gave Max a massage.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Daisy. Daisy loved to play outside with her friends, and her favorite game was to seat on the grass and make flower crowns. One sunny day, Daisy and her friends went to the park to play. While they were playing, Daisy saw a big, pretty flower. She wanted to pick it for her flower crown, but it was too high for her to reach. Daisy's friends tried to help her, but they couldn't reach it either. They were all sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, a friendly bird flew down and picked the flower for Daisy. She was so happy and thanked the bird with a big smile. The bird smiled back and flew away. Daisy and her friends learned that sometimes, when you can't do something on your own, a little help from a friend can make all the difference. And they all played happily ever after.

One day, a little mole went out to play. The sun was warm and mild. The mole dug a hole in the ground. He was happy. A big bird saw the mole. The bird stared at the mole. The bird said, "What are you doing?" The mole said, "I am digging a hole." The bird said, "I want to play too." The mole and the bird played together. They dug more holes. They had fun. But then, the big bird got hungry. The bird stared at the mole again. The mole was scared. The big bird ate the little mole. The mole was gone. The bird was not hungry anymore. The end.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was only three years old. One day, Lucy was very excited to go outside and play with her friends. She rushed to go find her daddy to tell him that she was ready. When she found her daddy, he was already standing by the open front door. He had a smile on his face and said, "Let's go to the park and have some fun!" Lucy was so happy she started to sprint down the sidewalk with her dadding chasing behind. Once they got to the park, her daddy opened the gate and Lucy ran inside. She was so excited to explore the park. She ran around to all the different play areas. She had so much fun splashing around in the water and swinging on the swings. When it was time to go home, Lucy and her daddy had to rush home. Daddy wanted to get back in time for dinner! Lucy was sad to go but she knew that another fun day at the park would come soon.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle child named Tim. Tim had a magic toy that could glow. He loved his toy so much. Every night, he played with it before he went to sleep. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his magic toy with him. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Tim played with his toy under a big tree. As the sun went down, the toy started to glow. Tim was so happy! He showed his glowing toy to his mom. She smiled and hugged him. They went home and Tim slept with his glowing toy next to him.
