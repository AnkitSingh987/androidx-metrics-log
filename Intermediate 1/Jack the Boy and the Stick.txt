Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Boy and the Stick

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was three years old and very happy. One day, his mummy gave him a stick. She said, "Jack, you can use this stick to test things around the garden." Jack was very excited so he ran outside. He began testing the ground with his stick. He tested the grass and discovered it was soft and healthy. He tested the trees and discovered they were sturdy and tall. Then Jack spotted a butterfly and he wanted to test it too. "Mummy, can I test the butterfly with my stick?" he asked. His mummy said, "No Jack, you must be gentle with the butterfly. You don't need to test it, just watch and appreciate it." Jack nodded. He realised that sometimes being gentle is more important than testing things. Then he had a great idea. He ran back to his mummy with a smile on his face. "Mummy, I'm going to test how healthy I am. I will run, jump and hop and see how good I am at all those things!" His mummy smiled and said, "That's a great idea, Jack!" And so Jack happily spent the day testing how healthy he was!

One day, a cat named Tim went outside. He saw a big, fierce dog. The dog had a red scarf on its neck. Tim was scared, but he wanted to play with the scarf. Tim came up with a plan. He found a bottle that could spray water. He filled it up and went back to the dog. Tim sprayed water on the dog's face. The dog was surprised and shook its head. The scarf fell off the dog's neck. Tim was happy and grabbed the scarf. He ran away with it to play. The dog was not mad. It just wanted to play too. They became friends and played with the scarf together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird. The bird had a fragile nest in a bush. The bush was in a big garden. The bird liked to sing happy songs. One day, the bird wanted to invite friends to the bush. The bird asked a cat, a dog, and a rabbit to come. They all said yes. They wanted to see the bird's home. The cat, dog, and rabbit came to the bush. They played and had fun. The bird sang songs for them. They all became good friends. They promised to play together again soon.

Ben liked to eat potatoes. He liked them mashed, fried, baked, or boiled. He liked them with butter, cheese, ketchup, or gravy. He liked them for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. He liked them so much that he sometimes pretended to be a potato. He would curl up on the couch, cover himself with a blanket, and say "I'm a potato, I'm a potato". One day, Ben's mom took him to the park. She packed some sandwiches and some potatoes for their picnic. Ben was happy. He ran around the playground, slid down the slide, and swung on the swings. He saw some other kids playing tag. He wanted to join them. He ran up to them and said "Hi, I'm Ben. Can I play with you?" The other kids looked at him. They saw his brown shirt, his brown pants, and his brown shoes. They saw his round face, his round nose, and his round eyes. They thought he looked like a potato. They laughed and said "No, you can't play with us. You're a potato, you're a potato". Ben felt sad. He felt embarrassed. He didn't want to be a potato. He wanted to be a boy. He wanted to have friends. He ran back to his mom and cried. His mom hugged him and said "Don't listen to them, Ben. You're not a potato. You're a wonderful, smart, kind boy. And you're very unusual. You have a special gift. You can make anything you want with your imagination. You can be a potato, or a superhero, or a dinosaur, or anything you like. And that's amazing". Ben smiled. He felt better. He realized his mom was right. He was unusual, but in a good way. He liked to be a potato, but he also liked to be other things. He liked to have fun with his imagination. He said "Thank you, mom. I love you. And I love potatoes". He ate his sandwich and his potato. He felt happy. He saw a girl sitting on a bench. She had a red hat, a red dress, and a red balloon. She looked lonely. He decided to share his potato with her. He walked up to her and said "Hi, I'm Ben. Do you like potatoes?"
