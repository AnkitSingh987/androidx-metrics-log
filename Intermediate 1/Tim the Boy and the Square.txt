Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Square

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big square in the distant. He wanted to enter the square and play. So, he walked and walked until he reached the square. When Tim entered the square, he saw a big dog. The dog looked at Tim and said, "Hello, do you want to play with me?" Tim was scared, but he wanted to make a new friend. So, he said, "Yes, I want to play with you." The dog and Tim played for a while. But then, the dog got too rough. The dog bit Tim's arm, and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and ran away from the square. He never went back to the square again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was restless and always in a rush. One day, she wanted to help her mom in the kitchen. Her mom was making food and asked Lucy to get a dish. Lucy ran to the cupboard and opened it. She saw a dish and grabbed it. "Here, Mom!" she said, as she rushed back to her mom with the dish. But, Lucy was going too fast and she tripped. The dish fell and broke into pieces. Just then, a tiny mouse appeared. It looked at the broken dish and said, "Oh no! Don't be sad, Lucy." The mouse waved its little tail and, like magic, the dish was whole again. Lucy and her mom were very surprised. They thanked the mouse, and Lucy learned to slow down and not be so restless.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lily. She was 3 years old. Lily liked to zip around the garden in her cart. One day, she had her lunch outside in the garden. She was very excited as she was having her favourite sandwich. As she was eating it, she noticed a butterfly zip by. She chased it, and the sandwich flew out of her hands and landed in a big puddle of mud. Lily was embarrassed. She had nothing to eat and the butterfly had gone. She began to cry. Just then, Lily heard a voice. It belonged to a kind old man. "Don't cry, little one," he said. "Let me buy you another sandwich." Lily felt much better and thanked the old man. She zipped off, delighted to have a new sandwich. But when she got home and opened it, she saw that it was filled with worms! Lily was so embarrassed and disgusted, she didn't know what to do. She was so sad she never wanted to zip around the garden again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a white toy in his room. It was a laser toy. Tim felt happy and wanted to play with it. Tim took the laser toy outside to show his friends. He said, "Look at my white laser toy!" His friends looked and smiled. They all wanted to play with the white laser toy too. They played with the white laser toy all day. They made it shine on trees, on the grass, and on the walls. The white laser made everyone laugh and have fun. Tim and his friends had a great day with the white laser toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big car. The car could drive very fast. One day, the car wanted to find a calendar. The car knew that a calendar could show the days. The car wanted to know when it was time to play. The car drove around to find a calendar. It looked in many places. It looked in the park, it looked in the store, and it looked in the house. But the car could not find a calendar anywhere. The car felt sad. Then, the car saw a little boy. The little boy had a calendar in his hand. The little boy said, "Car, do you want this calendar? I have another one available." The car was very happy. The car took the calendar and knew when it was time to play. The car and the little boy became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a far, far land, there was a big slide. A little boy named Tim wanted to play on it. Near the slide, there was a horn. Tim did not know what the horn did, so he left it alone. A girl named Sue came to play too. She saw the horn and said, "Hey Tim, let's blow the horn!" Tim was scared. He said, "No, we don't know what it does. It's unknown." But Sue did not listen. She blew the horn very loud. The loud horn made a big monster wake up. The monster was angry. He came to the slide and broke it with his big hands. Tim and Sue were very sad. They should have listened to Tim and not played with the unknown horn.
