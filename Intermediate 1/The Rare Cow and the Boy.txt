Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rare Cow and the Boy

Once there was a rare cow. It was white and sparkly and it lived close to a small village. One day, a little boy saw the cow and couldn't believe his eyes. He yelled out, “Wow! A rare cow!” At that moment, the cow's owner heard the noise and came out of their house. They said, “This is my cow! You must not touch it or come near it!” The little boy was a bit scared but he said, “I just wanted to see it, I didn't mean to make trouble.” The cow's owner just frowned and said, “Don't come back here again. If I see you, I'll make sure you're punished.” The little boy was very scared and ran away as fast as he could. He never returned to that village again, for fear of the cow's owner. Sadly, he never got to see the rare cow again.

Ben and Lily were twins who lived on a farm with their mom and dad. They liked to play with the animals in the barn, especially the baby pigs. One day, they decided to sneak some apples from the kitchen and feed them to the pigs. "Shh, don't let mom see us," Ben whispered as he grabbed two apples from the basket. "She will be mad if we spoil our lunch." "Okay, let's go to the barn," Lily said, following her brother. They ran across the yard and opened the barn door. They saw the pigs sleeping in a pile of hay. They tiptoed closer and held out the apples. "Here, piggy, piggy, have a treat," Ben said, smiling. He tossed an apple to the nearest pig. The pig woke up and sniffed the apple. It made a happy noise and bit into it. "Me too, me too," Lily said, throwing her apple to another pig. The pig also woke up and ate the apple. Soon, all the pigs were awake and hungry. They saw Ben and Lily holding more apples and ran towards them. "Uh-oh, they want more," Ben said, feeling scared. He and Lily tried to back away, but the pigs were too fast. They surrounded them and pushed them with their noses. They knocked them down and tried to take their apples. "Help, help, they are crazy!" Lily cried, holding her apple tight. She and Ben screamed for their mom and dad. Mom and dad heard the noise and ran to the barn. They saw the pigs chasing Ben and Lily and ran to help them. They picked them up and carried them out of the barn. They scolded the pigs and closed the barn door. "Are you okay, kids?" mom asked, hugging Ben and Lily. "Yes, mom, we are sorry," Ben said, feeling sorry. "We just wanted to feed the pigs, but they went crazy," Lily said, feeling sorry too. Mom and dad looked at the apples and shook their heads. "You know you shouldn't feed the pigs apples, they are bad for them. And you shouldn't spoil your lunch, either. You need to eat healthy food to grow strong and smart," dad said, sternly. "We know, dad, we won't do it again," Ben and Lily said, nodding. Mom and dad smiled and kissed them. "Come on, let's go wash up and have some soup. And maybe later, we can play with the pigs again, but only with their regular food," mom said, gently. "Okay, mom, we love you," Ben and Lily said, happily. They followed their mom and dad to the house, leaving the pigs to nap in the barn.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who loved kicking. He kicked the ball, he kicked the tree, he even kicked the clouds! One day, as he was walking, he saw something orange in the mailbox. It turned out to be a toy! He was so excited and kicked it as hard as he could. The toy flew away faster than he could ever imagine. "Oh no!" he said. He ran after it, but before he could catch it, it crashed into the window of a house. He knocked on the door and asked, "Do you have my orange toy?" A lady opened the door and said, "No, I'm sorry. You can't have it back." She was very firm. The boy was so disappointed. He slowly walked away, kicking stones and feeling sad. The end.
