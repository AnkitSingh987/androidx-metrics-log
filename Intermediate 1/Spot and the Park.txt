Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a big, crazy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the dirt all day long. One day, Spot saw a big sign in the park. The sign had a law that said all dogs must bathe before they play. The next day, Spot's mom gave him a big, soapy bath. Spot did not like the bath, but he knew he had to be clean to play in the park. He felt the water and soap wash away all the dirt from his fur. After the bath, Spot went to the park and played with his friends. They all had fun and followed the law. Spot now knew that having a bath was not so bad, and it was good to follow the rules. In the end, Spot was happy, clean, and ready for more fun days at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy loved to whistle all day long. He would sit on a branch and whistle happy songs. One day, Billy saw a big pitch. He was scared because it was so big. Billy wanted to play on the pitch, but he was too scared. He thought of a plan. He would whistle a brave song to make him feel strong. So, Billy took a deep breath and whistled his brave song. As Billy whistled, something unexpected happened. The big pitch started to move! It was not a pitch at all, but a big, friendly dog named Max. Max had been sleeping and Billy's whistle woke him up. Max was not scary, he just wanted to play. So, Billy and Max played together all day long.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a magic zipper. This zipper was not like any other zipper. It was difficult to see because it was very good at hide and seek. One day, Tim was playing with the zipper, and he thought it would be fun to hide it. He looked for a good place to put it. He found a small box and put the zipper inside. Then, he went to play with his toys. Later, Tim wanted to show his mom the magic zipper. But, when he went to find the box, it was difficult to see. He looked and looked, but he could not find it. Tim felt sad. His mom saw him and asked what was wrong. Tim told her about the magic zipper and how he cannot find it. His mom smiled and said, "Let's look together." They searched and found the box with the magic zipper. Tim was happy and hugged his mom. They played with the magic zipper together, and it was a fun day.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily went to a big store with her mom. They wanted to buy a new suit for Lily's dad. They walked and looked at many suits. Lily's mom asked her, "Which suit do you like for dad?" Lily pointed to a blue suit and said, "I like that one!" Her mom liked it too, so they bought it and went home. When they got home, Lily wanted to open the box with the suit inside. Her mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Lily opened the box, and something unexpected happened. A big, funny clown jumped out of the box! Lily and her mom laughed and laughed. The clown had brought the right suit for dad, and he also made them all happy with his funny tricks.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little car with four wheels. The car loved to wear a shiny red hat. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck said, "Hi! I like your hat." The car smiled and said, "Thank you! I love to wear it." The car and the truck became good friends. They played together every day. One day, while they were playing, something unexpected happened. The car's hat fell off and rolled away. The car was very sad. The truck saw the car's sad face and said, "Don't worry! I will help you find your hat." They looked and looked, but they could not find it. Then, they saw a little bike wearing the hat. The bike said, "I found this hat. It's so lovely, I wanted to wear it too." The car was happy to see the hat and said, "That's my hat! Can I have it back, please?" The bike gave the hat back to the car, and they all became friends. From that day on, they took turns wearing the lovely hat and played together happily.
