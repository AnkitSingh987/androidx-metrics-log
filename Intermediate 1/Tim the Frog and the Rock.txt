Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Frog and the Rock

One day, a little frog named Tim went for a hop. He liked to hop in a zigzag way. Tim was a very polite frog. As he hopped, he saw a big rock in his path. He thought, "I will hop over that rock soon." Tim met a small bug. The bug said, "Hello, polite frog! Can you help me? I am lost." Tim said, "Yes, I can help. Let's go find your home." They hopped together, still going in a zigzag way. As they hopped, they came to the big rock. Tim remembered that he wanted to hop over it. He said to the bug, "Hold on, little friend!" Tim hopped high and over the big rock. The bug was happy to be with Tim. They found the bug's home and said goodbye. Tim felt good for helping his new friend.

Lila liked to listen to stories. She had many books with pictures and words, and her mom or dad would read them to her at night. She liked to hear about brave heroes, funny animals, and faraway places. She wished she could go on adventures like them. But Lila did not have many friends to play with. She lived in a big house with a big yard, but there were no other kids nearby. Sometimes she felt lonely and bored. She wanted someone to talk to and share her stuff with. She wished she had a brother or a sister. One day, her mom told her a secret. She said, "Lila, I have some good news for you. You are going to have a baby sister soon. She is growing in my tummy right now. She will be here in a few months. You will be a big sister, and you will have someone to play with and love." Lila was very happy and excited. She hugged her mom and asked her many questions. She wanted to know what her sister would look like, what her name would be, and what she would like to do. She said, "Mom, I can't wait to meet her. I will teach her how to listen to stories, and I will share my stuff with her. I will be the best big sister ever." Lila waited and waited for her sister to come. She helped her mom get ready for the baby. She picked out some clothes, toys, and books for her. She made a card and a drawing for her. She sang songs and told stories to her mom's tummy. She hoped her sister would listen and like them. Finally, the day came when her mom and dad went to the hospital. Lila stayed with her grandma, who told her more stories and gave her cookies. She was nervous and curious, but also happy and proud. She knew her sister was coming soon, and she would not be lonely anymore. She wished she could see her and hug her and tell her she loved her. The next day, her mom and dad came back with a bundle in their arms. They smiled and said, "Lila, meet your baby sister. Her name is Mia. She is very small and cute, and she has your eyes and nose. She is your family, and she loves you very much." Lila ran to them and looked at the bundle. She saw a tiny face with dark hair and bright eyes. She heard a soft cry and a little snore. She felt a warm hand and a wet nose. She smelled a sweet scent and a new smell. She said, "Hi, Mia. I'm Lila. I'm your big sister. I'm so happy you're here. I have so many stories to tell you, and so much stuff to show you. You're not lonely anymore. Neither am I."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toy bear, Ben. One day, Ben fell on the floor. Lily picked him up and said, "Oh no, Ben! I am grateful you are okay!" Later, Lily and Ben played in the park. Lily told Ben, "I believe we can find a big tree to play under." They found the biggest tree and played all day. Ben said, "Thank you, Lily. I am grateful for our fun day." At night, Lily put Ben on her bed. She said, "I believe we will have sweet dreams, Ben." Ben replied, "I am grateful for you, Lily. Good night!" They both closed their eyes and slept happily.
