Category: Intermediate
Title: Angela the Girl and the Present

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Angela. She was three years old and had a very special friend. His name was Sam and he was always very good to her. One day, Angela was getting ready to have a birthday party. Sam had a present ready for her and he wanted to wrap it with a special card. He found the perfect card. It was purple and he thought it was perfect. Angela was really excited and so was Sam. He carefully wrapped the card with a perfect white ribbon. He handed the present to Angela and she was so happy. The party went off without a hitch and everyone had a great time. At the end of the day, when it was time to go home, Angela remembered something. She remembered that Sam hadn't given her the present he had been planning to give her. In a fit of anger and disappointment, Angela ripped up the purple card as everyone watched in horror. Sam had a sad look on his face and he never came back to Angela's house again.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit called Bob. Bob lived in a big field and he was very happy there. One day, Bob noticed something strange in the corner of the field. He spotted something original which he had never seen before. It was a hole! Bob hopped over to take a closer look. He discovered the hole was a way out of the field. He turned to his friend Sam the Squirrel. "I am going to escape!" said Bob. "But why? This field is your home!" replied Sam. "I want to explore and see the world!" said Bob. So, Bob bravely hopped into the hole and he escaped from the field. And that is how Bob the Rabbit went on his journey of exploration and adventure.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a big pear tree. The pears are ripe and yellow. "Look, Max, pears!" Lily says. "I love pears. Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Max says. "But how can we get them? They are too high." Lily has an idea. She sees a long stick on the ground. She picks it up. "We can use this stick to reach the pears. It is flexible. It can bend and stretch." Lily and Max take turns to hold the stick. They poke the pears with the stick. Some pears fall down. They catch them in their hands. They are happy. They sit under the tree and prepare to eat the pears. They wash them with water from their bottles. They peel them with their fingers. They share them with each other. "Mmm, these pears are juicy and sweet," Lily says. "Yes, they are. Thank you for sharing, Lily," Max says. They smile and hug. They are good friends. They enjoy the pears.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play with trains. They had a big box of toy trains and tracks that they could build in their room. Sometimes they pretended to be drivers and passengers, and they made sounds like "choo-choo" and "whistle". One day, they decided to make a big station with their blocks and cardboard. They worked hard to make it look nice, with windows and doors and signs. They put some of their trains inside the station, and some outside on the tracks. They were very proud of their station. But then, their big brother Tom came in. He was older and taller than them, and he did not like to play with trains. He liked to play with cars and balls and robots. He saw their station and said, "What is this? This is a poor station. It is not real. It is not fun. It is silly." He grabbed one of their trains and threw it across the room. It hit the wall and broke. Lily and Ben cried. They said, "Stop it, Tom! That is our train. That is our station. We made it. We like it. It is not poor. It is not silly. It is fun." Tom did not listen. He laughed and said, "No, it is not. It is a poor station. Watch this." He kicked their station with his foot. It fell apart and the blocks and cardboard scattered. Lily and Ben cried louder. They said, "Tom, you are mean! You are bad! You broke our station. You hurt our trains. Go away!" Tom did not care. He said, "I don't care. I don't like your station. I don't like your trains. I like my cars and balls and robots. They are better. They are real. They are fun. You are babies. You are silly." He ran out of the room, leaving Lily and Ben with their broken station and trains. They hugged each other and sobbed. They did not know what to do. They wished Tom would be nice to them. They wished he would play with them. They wished he would like their station and trains. They wished he would whistle with them.
