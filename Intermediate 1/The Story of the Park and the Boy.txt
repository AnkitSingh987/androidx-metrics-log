Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Park and the Boy

Once upon a time there was a small boy who had a very special word. He liked to tell everyone the word but could never remember what it was called. One day, he went for a walk in the park. There he met an old man. “What’s your name?” the old man asked. “I don’t know,” the little boy replied. The old man smiled. “That’s ok. Do you remember a special word?” The little boy nodded. “Yes! I do!” “What’s the word?” the old man asked. The little boy smiled. “Love.” The old man smiled back. “That’s a very special word,” he said. “Now, what’s your name?” The little boy smiled. “My name is Love!”

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and his best friend, a dog named Max. They would race around the yard, and whoever won would get a big victory. One sunny day, Tim and Max were playing in the yard when they saw a big, round ball. Tim said, "Let's poke the ball, Max!" Max barked, and they both poked the ball with their hands and paws. The ball rolled and rolled until it hit a tree. Tim and Max looked at each other and decided to race to the tree. They ran as fast as they could, but Max reached the tree first. Max wagged his tail in victory, and Tim laughed. "Good job, Max!" Tim said. Tim and Max played with the ball all day long. They poked it, chased it, and took turns winning races. As the sun began to set, Tim and Max were tired but very happy. That night, Tim and Max snuggled in bed, dreaming of their fun day together. They couldn't wait for more adventures and victories in the days to come.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an ugly frog. The frog was sad because he thought no one liked him. One day, the frog met a friendly lawyer. The lawyer was very kind and liked to help others. "Hello, Mr. Frog," said the lawyer. "Why are you so sad?" "I am sad because I am ugly," said the frog. "No one wants to be my friend." The lawyer thought for a moment and said, "You may be ugly on the outside, but you are a good frog. Let's be friends." The frog was happy to have a new friend. They spent a lot of time together. They played and laughed. The frog began to feel better about himself. One day, the frog did something he should not have done. He took a toy from another child. The frog felt bad and knew he needed to say sorry. He told the lawyer about his mistake. "I regret taking the toy," said the frog. "What should I do?" The lawyer said, "It is good that you feel sorry. Now, you must give the toy back and say you are sorry." The frog did as the lawyer said. He gave the toy back and said he was sorry. The child forgave the frog, and they became friends too. From that day on, the ugly frog had many friends and was never sad again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old man. He had a magic bottle. The bottle could talk and explain things to people. Everyone in the town loved the wise old man and his magic bottle. One day, a little boy came to the wise old man. He was sad because he did not know how to share his toys with his friends. The wise old man asked the magic bottle to explain how to share. The bottle told the little boy, "It is good to share. When you share, you make your friends happy. And when your friends are happy, you are happy too." The little boy went home and shared his toys with his friends. They played happily all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The magic bottle stopped talking! The wise old man was very sad. He did not know what to do. He asked the little boy to come back and help him. The little boy had an idea. He told the wise old man, "Maybe the magic bottle needs us to share our happiness with it." So they both shared their happy stories with the bottle. And then, the magic bottle started talking again! The wise old man and the little boy were very happy. The moral of the story is that sharing is not only good for making others happy, but it can also make you happy and even help fix problems.
