Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to have stamps. She had a big box with many fine stamps. They were all different colors and shapes. Sue was very happy with her stamps. One day, Sue went to the park to play. She took her stamps with her. At the park, she met a boy named Tim. Tim did not have any stamps. He was sad because he had nothing to play with. Sue thought about her stamps and had an idea. Sue decided to share her stamps with Tim. They both played with the stamps and had lots of fun. Tim was very happy, and Sue felt good about sharing. They became good friends and played together every day. The moral of the story is that sharing makes us happy and helps us make new friends.

Lily liked to make things with paper and tape. She liked to cut and fold and stick. One day, she found a big box in the garage. She had an idea. She wanted to make a robot. She took the box and some paper and tape. She cut holes for the eyes and mouth. She stuck paper tubes for the arms and legs. She drew buttons and wires on the paper. She wrapped the box with shiny paper. She made a robot. She was very proud of her robot. She wanted to show it to her mom and dad. She dragged the robot to the living room. She called them to come and see. She said, "Look, I made a robot!" But mom and dad were not happy. They were angry. They said, "Lily, what have you done? That box was not for you. That box had a gift for your grandma. You ruined it!" Lily felt sad and sorry. She did not know the box had a gift. She wanted to make a robot. She wanted to make mom and dad happy. But she made them angry. She cried. She wished she did not make a robot.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man. He lived near a mill. He liked to roll a big ball. One day, the bald man went to the mill. He saw a cat. The cat was playing with a small ball. The bald man wanted to play too. The bald man and the cat played together. They rolled the big ball and the small ball. They had a lot of fun. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to bounce all day long. Bounce lived in a small home with his mom, dad, and sister. Bounce wanted to be useful and help his family. One day, Bounce's mom said, "We need to get food from the store." Bounce had an idea. He said, "I can bounce to the store and get the food!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Bounce. Be careful and come back soon." Bounce was very happy to help his family. Bounce bounced to the store and got the food. On the way home, he saw a big hill. Bounce thought, "If I bounce down this hill, I will be home faster." So, Bounce bounced down the hill, and he got home very fast. His family was so happy to see him. Bounce was proud to be useful and help his family.

One day, a grumpy volcano was feeling very twisty. He twisted and turned all day long. He was so big and tall, but he was not happy. He wanted a friend to play with. A little bird came to the volcano and said, "Hello, Mr. Volcano! Why are you so grumpy?" The volcano replied, "I am lonely, and I want a friend to play with. But I twist and turn all the time, and no one wants to be my friend." The little bird thought for a moment and said, "I will be your friend, Mr. Volcano! We can play together, and I can help you twist and turn. I can fly around you and sing songs to make you happy." The grumpy volcano was so happy to have a friend. They played together every day. The little bird would fly around the volcano, and they would twist and turn together. The volcano was not grumpy anymore, and they were the best of friends.
