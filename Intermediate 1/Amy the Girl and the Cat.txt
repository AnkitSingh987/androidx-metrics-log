Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a cat named Max. Max was a brilliant cat. He was very smart and could do many things. One day, Amy wanted to bathe Max. She filled a tub with warm water and bubbles. Max did not like the water, but he loved Amy, so he let her bathe him. Amy was very gentle and made sure Max was clean. After the bath, Amy took a comb and brushed Max's fur. Max liked the comb because it felt nice on his fur. When Max was all clean and dry, he looked very pretty. Amy and Max were happy, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a favorite toy. It was a leather ball. He loved to play with the ball all day. One day, Max was playing with his ball when it rolled near a big, dangerous road. Max was scared. He did not want to go near the road, but he wanted his ball. Max started to panic. Then, Max's friend, a big dog named Buddy, saw Max. Buddy knew the road was dangerous too. He ran to Max and picked up the leather ball with his mouth. Buddy gave the ball to Max and they went back to play in the safe yard. Max was happy and no longer in panic. They played together all day, far away from the dangerous road.

Anna liked to play in the woods with her dog, Spot. They ran and jumped and chased each other. Sometimes they saw other animals, like birds and squirrels and rabbits. Anna was not afraid of them. She was kind and gentle. One day, Anna and Spot saw a big, gray animal in the trees. It had sharp teeth and a long tail. It was a wolf. Anna had never seen a wolf before. She was curious and wanted to get closer. Spot barked and growled. He wanted to protect Anna from the wolf. The wolf saw Anna and Spot. It was hungry and angry. It wanted to eat them. It came out of the trees and ran towards them. It was fast and strong. Anna and Spot were scared. They turned around and ran away. They hoped to find their way back home. As they ran, they saw a bright light in the sky. It was a fairy. She had wings and a wand. She was graceful and beautiful. She saw Anna and Spot and the wolf. She wanted to help them. She waved her wand and said a magic word. The wolf vanished. It was gone. Anna and Spot were safe. Anna and Spot thanked the fairy. She smiled and said, "You're welcome. Be careful in the woods. There are many dangers. Always stay close to your home and your friends." She waved her wand again and flew away. Anna and Spot followed her light and found their home. They hugged and kissed their mom and dad. They were happy and grateful. They never forgot the fairy and the wolf.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to race. He would race his toy cars, his friends, and even his dog. One day, Tim went to the subway with his mom. The subway was big and loud, but Tim was not scared. On the subway, Tim saw a man who was deaf. The deaf man could not hear the loud subway sounds. Tim wanted to be friends with the deaf man. He smiled and waved at him. The deaf man smiled back. Then, something unexpected happened. The subway stopped and everyone had to get off. Tim and the deaf man had to race to catch the next subway. They ran very fast. In the end, Tim and the deaf man won the race and caught the next subway. They became good friends and had a fun day together.

Tommy was sick. He had a fever and a cough. He did not like being sick. He wanted to play with his toys and his friends. But his mom said he had to go to the doctor. She said the doctor would make him better. Tommy was scared of the doctor. He did not like the white coat and the stethoscope. He did not like the needles and the pills. He did not like the smell and the noise. He felt helpless. He wanted to run away. But his mom held his hand and said it would be okay. They went to the doctor's office. There were many people there. Some were sick too. Some were not. Tommy saw a nurse. She had a smile and a badge. She said her name was Lisa. She said she would help Tommy. She said she would take his temperature and his blood pressure. She said it would not hurt. Tommy did not believe her. He thought she would scare him. He thought she would poke him and pinch him. He thought she would give him a shot. He cried and hid behind his mom. He said he did not want to go with Lisa. He said he wanted to go home. Lisa was kind. She did not get angry. She did not force Tommy. She said she understood. She said she was scared too when she was little. She said she had a trick to make it easier. She said she had a friend who would help. She said his name was Teddy. She showed Tommy a stuffed bear. He had a band-aid on his arm and a thermometer in his mouth. He looked cute and friendly. Tommy was curious. He liked bears. He liked stuffed animals. He wondered why Teddy had a band-aid and a thermometer. He asked Lisa. Lisa said Teddy was sick too. She said he had a fever and a cough. She said he had to go to the doctor too. She said he was brave and did not cry. She said he let her take his temperature and his blood pressure. She said he did not need a shot. She said he just needed some medicine and some rest. She said he felt better now. Tommy felt sorry for Teddy. He thought Teddy was nice. He thought Teddy was strong. He wanted to be like Teddy. He asked Lisa if he could hold Teddy. Lisa said yes. She gave Tommy Teddy. Tommy hugged Teddy. He felt Teddy's fur and his band-aid. He felt Teddy's warmth and his heartbeat. He felt less scared. He felt less helpless. He felt more brave. Lisa smiled. She said Tommy was a good boy. She said she was proud of him. She said she would not scare him. She said she would help him. She said she would make him better. She said she would be gentle and quick. She said she would give him a sticker and a lollipop. She said he could keep Teddy. Tommy nodded. He trusted Lisa. He trusted Teddy. He let Lisa take his temperature and his blood pressure. He did not hurt. He did not cry. He did not need a shot. He just needed some medicine and some rest. He felt better. He thanked Lisa. He thanked Teddy. He smiled. He was not scared of the doctor anymore.
