Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Knob

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a fearful boy who did not like to play outside. One day, he saw a big door with a knob. He was very curious about what was behind the door. Tim walked up to the door and touched the knob. He frowned because it was cold and he was scared. But he wanted to be brave, so he turned the knob and opened the door. Behind the door, it was very dark. Tim walked into the dark room. He heard a loud noise and it made him very scared. He wanted to leave, but the door closed and he could not find the knob. Tim was stuck in the dark room and he cried. The bad ending is that Tim never found his way out.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tommy. Tommy was a brilliant three year old. He lived in a small house in the woods with his parents. One day, Tommy's dad was getting ready to build a new table out of some of the wood in the woods. Tommy was very excited. He asked his dad what he would do with the wood. His dad said he would use it to make something special for Tommy. Tommy couldn't wait to see what it was. But when the time came to finish the table, Tommy's dad didn't make a table but instead he made a small stool. Tommy was so disappointed that he started to complain. He couldn't understand why his dad wouldn't make him something bigger. His dad said he was too small to use a big table. Tommy felt sad and he started to cry. His dad tried to cheer him up, but it was no use. Eventually, Tommy stopped complaining and went off to bed. Even though Tommy's dad tried to make something special for him, it had a bad ending. Tommy never forgot about that day and he never used the little stool his dad made for him.

One day, a little bird was sitting on a cliff. The bird saw a big tree and thought, "I want to live in that tree!" So, the bird flew to the tree and met a squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hello, little bird! Do you want to be my friend?" The bird said, "Yes, I want to be your friend. I like this tree. Can I live here with you?" The squirrel was happy and said, "Of course, little bird! We can live here together." They both were very happy. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud noise. They looked around and saw a big rock falling from the cliff. They were scared, but they didn't know what to do. Then, the little bird thought of an idea. The bird flew up high and caught the rock with its beak. The squirrel helped by pushing the rock away from the tree. They saved their home and became best friends forever.

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat something yummy. They went to the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. But the refrigerator was almost empty. There was only a cheap cheese and some old bread. "Can we eat this cheese?" Lily asked. "I don't know. It looks funny. It has green spots on it," Tom said. "Maybe it is still good. Let's try it," Lily said. She took the cheese and broke a piece. She gave it to Tom and took another piece for herself. They put the cheese in their mouths and chewed. But the cheese tasted very bad. It was sour and bitter and made their tongues hurt. They spit out the cheese and ran to the sink. They drank some water and tried to wash away the bad taste. But the water did not help. They felt sick in their stomachs. They started to tremble and sweat. They felt like they were going to throw up. "Mommy! Daddy! Help us!" they cried. But no one heard them. Mommy and Daddy were not at home. They had gone to the store to buy some food. They did not know that Tom and Lily had eaten the cheap cheese. They did not know that the cheese was bad and made them sick. Tom and Lily lay down on the floor and curled up. They wished they had not eaten the cheese. They wished Mommy and Daddy were here. They wished they had something yummy to eat. But they had nothing. They only had pain and fear. They cried and cried until they fell asleep.
