Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Swings

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a soft, fluffy cushion that she loved very much. She took her cushion everywhere she went. One day, Lily's mom warned her, "Be careful with your cushion, or you might lose it." Lily felt a little ashamed, but she promised to be more careful. The next day, Lily went to the park with her cushion. She played on the swings and slides, always holding her cushion tight. When it was time to go home, Lily remembered her mom's warning and made sure she had her cushion with her.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to wear her pretty red dress. One day, she went to a birthday party for her friend, Tim. At the party, Sue gave Tim a gift. It was a toy car. Tim was very happy with his gift. They played with the toy car and had lots of fun. Then, it was time to eat. They had spicy pizza with red sauce. Sue liked the spicy pizza, but Tim did not. They laughed and enjoyed the party. In the end, they were both happy and had a great day.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big tree with many fruits on it. They wanted to try some, but they were too high to reach. "Can we borrow a ladder from someone?" Lily asked. "Maybe we can ask the man who lives in the yellow house. He has a ladder in his garden," Ben said. They went to the yellow house and knocked on the door. A man with a long beard and a hat opened it. He looked unusual, but he smiled at them. "Hello, little ones. What do you want?" he asked. "Please, sir, can we borrow your ladder? We want to pick some fruits from the tree in the park," Lily said. The man nodded and said, "Sure, you can borrow my ladder. But you have to be careful and return it when you are done. And you have to share some fruits with me, too." "Thank you, sir. We will be careful and share with you," Ben said. They took the ladder and went to the tree. They climbed up and picked some fruits. They were juicy and sweet. They filled a basket with them and brought it down. They gave some fruits to the man and thanked him for the ladder. He thanked them for the fruits and said they were welcome to borrow his ladder again. Lily and Ben were happy. They had a new friend and a new snack. They went back to the park and ate some fruits. They shared some with the other children, too. They all agreed that the fruits were unusual, but very tasty.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who loved to hop. One day, while he was hopping in the woods, he found a big bell. The bunny thought, "This bell is so pretty! I want to show it to my friends." The bunny hopped back to his friends, carrying the bell. He showed it to his friends and said, "Look what I found!" His friends were very happy and they all took turns ringing the bell. They liked the sound it made. But then, something unexpected happened. When one of the bunny's friends rang the bell, it started to hop like the bunny! The hopping bell was very slow, but it made the bunnies laugh. They all hopped around with the bell, having lots of fun. They played with the hopping bell all day and it became their new favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Spot found a big red tent in the yard. "Wow, a tent!" said Spot. "I want to own this tent." Spot went inside the tent and found a ball. He picked up the ball with his mouth and ran outside. Spot saw a little girl named Sue. "Hi, Sue!" said Spot. "Do you want to play with me and my new tent?" "Yes, Spot!" said Sue. "I love your tent. Let's play together!" They played with the ball and the tent all day, and they were very happy.
