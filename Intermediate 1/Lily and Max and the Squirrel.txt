Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Squirrel

Lily and Max were best friends. They liked to play outside and explore new places. One day, they found a big hill with a steep edge. They climbed up the hill and looked over the edge. They saw water and rocks far below. "Wow, this is a cliff!" Max said. "It is very high and scary." Lily nodded. She felt a little afraid too. She held Max's hand and said, "Let's be careful. We don't want to fall down." They walked along the cliff and saw a big tree. The tree had a hole in its trunk. They peeked inside and saw a small animal. It had brown fur, a long tail and big ears. It looked at them with curious eyes. "Hello, who are you?" Lily asked. The animal made a soft sound and came out of the hole. It sniffed Lily and Max and rubbed its head against them. It was friendly and warm. "I think it is a squirrel," Max said. "It is very cute and soft." Lily smiled. She liked the squirrel too. She said, "Let's introduce ourselves. I am Lily and this is Max. We are your new friends." The squirrel nodded and made another sound. It seemed to understand them. It invited them to go inside the hole. Lily and Max followed it and saw a cozy nest. There were nuts and berries and leaves. "Wow, this is your home?" Max asked. The squirrel nodded and offered them some food. Lily and Max thanked it and ate some. They felt comfortable and happy. They had found a new friend and a new place. They decided to visit the squirrel again soon. They said goodbye and went back to their own home. They told their parents about their adventure and their new friend. They felt proud and excited. They had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a magic fan. The fan could make things disappear. Mia loved her fan and took it everywhere. One sunny day, Mia went to the park. She saw a big, distant tree. She wanted to make the tree disappear. She waved her magic fan at the tree and said, "Tree, disappear!" The tree vanished. Mia was happy and laughed. A little boy named Tim saw what happened. He came to Mia and asked, "Can your fan make things come back?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, it can." She waved her fan again and the tree came back. Tim clapped his hands and said, "Wow, your fan is amazing!" Mia and Tim became friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to write with her crayons. One day, Mia found a razor in the bathroom. She didn't know what it was but thought it would be fun to try to write with it. Mia tried to write on the wall with the razor, but it didn't work. She felt sad. Suddenly, her mom came into the room and saw the razor. Mia was scared that her mom would be mad, but her mom was grateful that Mia didn't get hurt. Her mom told Mia that the razor was not for writing, but for grown-ups to use. Mia learned that not everything can be used to write, and she was happy to go back to her crayons. From that day on, Mia only wrote with things that were safe for her to use.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal fly. The fly loved to help all his friends. He would fly around the big tree where they lived and play with them all day. One day, the fly heard a question from his friend, the little red bird. The bird asked, "Where is my yummy food?" The loyal fly wanted to help, so he tried to answer. He flew around the tree and looked everywhere for the bird's food. But, he could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened! The loyal fly saw the bird's food in a place he did not look before. It was hiding in a small hole in the tree! The fly went to the bird and told him where his food was. The little red bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, loyal fly!" They both played and ate the yummy food together. From that day on, the loyal fly and the little red bird were the best of friends.
