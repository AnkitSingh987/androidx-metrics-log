Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Human and the Cat

Once, there was a human named Tim. He had a pet dog named Spot. They loved to play together. One day, they went to the park. They were very happy. At the park, they saw a big cat. The cat was persistent. It wanted to play with them. Tim said, "We can all play together!" The cat, Spot, and Tim played with a ball. They had lots of fun. Then, they realized the cat was not a cat. It was a tiger! Tim said, "Oh no! We must go home!" The tiger was nice and did not hurt them. They all learned that even though someone looks different, they can still be a friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was a restless girl who liked to play all day. She had a big sack full of toys and loved to speak to her toys. One sunny day, Sue took her sack outside to play. She opened the sack and started talking to her toys. "Hello, Mr. Bear," she said. "Do you want to play with me today?" Mr. Bear nodded his head and smiled. Sue played with Mr. Bear and her other toys all day long. They had so much fun! But soon, the sun began to set and it was time for Sue to go inside. She put all her toys back in the sack and said, "Goodbye, Mr. Bear. I'll see you tomorrow." Then, Sue went inside to rest and dream of more fun days with her toys.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to lay in the sun and sleep all day. He never wanted to play with the other cats or help his family. One day, Tom saw a pretty blue bird high up in a tree. The bird was hanging a little toy for other birds to play with. Tom wanted the toy, but he was too lazy to climb the tree. He asked the blue bird to give him the toy, but the bird said, "No, you must climb up and get it yourself." Tom thought about it and decided to try. He slowly climbed the tree, even though it was hard work. When he got to the top, he took the toy and played with it. The blue bird smiled and said, "See, if you try and work hard, you can do anything." From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore. He played with the other cats and helped his family. The moral of the story is that we should always try our best and not be lazy.

One day, a curious little girl named Jane went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with a small pick on the ground. Jane picked up the pick and saw a boy named Tim walking by. She had an idea and said, "Tim, do you want to play catch with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Jane! I love catch!" They started to pass the pick back and forth. They were happy and having fun. As they played, a small dog came up to them. The dog wanted to play too. Jane and Tim looked at each other and decided to let the dog join in. They passed the pick to the dog, and the dog passed it back to them with its mouth. After playing for a while, they all became tired and sat down under the big tree. Jane, Tim, and the dog were now friends. They learned that sharing and playing together made them happier than playing alone. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to others, because it can lead to new friendships and more fun.

One day, a big truck came to a factory. In the factory, they made toys. A man named Tom worked there. He was very fit and strong. Tom liked to decorate the toys with pretty colors. One day, Tom met a small bird. The bird said, "Tom, can you help me? My nest is not pretty." Tom said, "Yes, I can help you. I will decorate your nest with pretty colors from the factory." Tom took the bird to the factory. They found many pretty colors. They worked together to decorate the nest. The bird was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The pretty colors started to wash away in the rain. The bird was sad. Tom said, "Don't be sad, bird. We can make your nest pretty again." They worked together to make the nest pretty with things from nature. They used leaves, flowers, and sticks. The bird's nest was pretty again, and it did not wash away in the rain. The bird was happy, and Tom learned that sometimes, the best things come from nature.
