Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a big, round pumpkin. The pumpkin was not happy. It felt terrible because it could not sing like the birds. One day, a little bird came to the pumpkin and asked, "Why are you sad?" The pumpkin said, "I feel terrible because I cannot sing like you." The little bird thought for a moment and said, "I can help you sing!" The little bird taught the pumpkin how to sing. They sang together every day. The pumpkin was not very good at first, but it tried very hard. After some time, the pumpkin learned to sing a simple song. The other pumpkins in the garden heard the singing pumpkin and were amazed. They all wanted to learn how to sing too. So, the little bird and the singing pumpkin taught them all. Soon, the garden was filled with the sound of singing pumpkins. They were all happy and did not feel terrible anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a purple flashlight that he loved very much. He would play with it every day and night. One day, Tim's mom told him, "We need to attach your flashlight to your bag, so you won't lose it." The next day, Tim went to the park with his mom and his purple flashlight attached to his bag. He played on the swings, the slide, and in the sandbox. The sun started to go down, and it was getting dark. Tim's mom said, "It's time to go home now, Tim." But Tim didn't want to leave yet. He had an idea. He took his purple flashlight off his bag and turned it on. The light was bright, and it made the park feel safe. Tim said, "Look, Mom! My flashlight can help us see in the dark. Can we stay a little longer?" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. Just a little longer." And they played in the park until it was time to go home.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom went to the airport. He wanted to see the big planes. At the airport, he met a nice dog named Max. Max was a big, happy dog who liked to help people. "Hi, I am Max," said the dog. "Do you need help?" Tom looked at Max and said, "Yes, I want to see the big planes. Can you show me?" Max was happy to help Tom. They walked together to see the planes. As they watched the planes, they saw a big sign. The sign said, "Do not go near the planes." But Tom was not a good reader. He did not know what the sign said. So, he went too close to the planes. Suddenly, a big wind from the plane blew Tom into the air! Max was scared, but he knew what to do. He jumped high and caught Tom in his mouth. They both were safe and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a small radio that he loved very much. It played nice songs that made Tim happy. One day, the radio started to make a terrible sound. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim's big sister, Jane, wanted to help. She encouraged Tim to try to fix the radio. They opened the radio and looked inside. They saw many parts, but they didn't know how to make the radio work again. They tried their best, but the terrible sound did not go away. In the end, Tim and Jane could not fix the radio. They were both very sad. The radio never played nice songs again. Tim missed the happy music, and he learned that not all problems can be fixed.

Tom and Anna like to play in the park. They like to run, jump, slide and swing. Sometimes they see dogs, birds and flowers. They are happy. One day, they go to the park with their mom. But they cannot see the park. They only see white. Mom says it is fog. Fog is like a big cloud on the ground. It makes everything look blurry. Tom and Anna are curious. They hold mom's hand and walk in the fog. They see shapes and shadows. They hear noises and voices. They wonder what is in the fog. They want to find out. They see a big building. It is modern. It has glass, metal and lights. It looks shiny and new. Mom says it is a museum. A museum has many things to see and learn. Tom and Anna are excited. They go inside the museum. They see paintings, sculptures, fossils and machines. They see colors, shapes, patterns and stories. They ask mom many questions. Mom tells them many answers. Tom and Anna are amazed. They have fun in the museum. They learn a lot. They forget about the fog. They are happy.
