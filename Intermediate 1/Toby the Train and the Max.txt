Category: Intermediate
Title: Toby the Train and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a little train named Toby. Toby was a nervous train because he had never left the train yard. One day, Toby saw a big train named Max. Max could shoot steam from his top. Toby wanted to be like Max. "Max, how do you shoot steam?" asked Toby. Max smiled and said, "You just go fast and let the steam out!" Toby wanted to try, but he was still nervous. Toby took a deep breath and started to move. He went faster and faster. Suddenly, he felt the steam inside him. He let the steam out and it shot from his top. Toby was so happy and not nervous anymore. Now, Toby could shoot steam like Max and they played together all the time.

Tom and Sam were two boys who liked to play on the beach. They liked to dig in the sand, splash in the water, and look for shells. One day, they saw a big, shiny shell that looked like a rock. They ran to it and tried to open it. "Maybe there is a pearl inside," Tom said. "We can sell it and buy candy." "Or maybe there is a monster," Sam said. "We should be careful." They pulled and pushed, but the shell was too hard. They looked for a stick or a stone to break it, but they could not find any. They decided to crawl under the shell and see if there was a hole. They crawled under the shell and saw a small opening. They peeked inside and saw something white and slimy. It was an oyster. They reached their hands to touch it, but they did not know that oysters can close their shells very fast. The oyster felt the boys' fingers and snapped its shell shut. It trapped their hands inside. Tom and Sam screamed and cried, but no one heard them. They were stuck under the shell, and the tide was coming in. They had found a cheap oyster, but it cost them their lives.

Once upon a time, there was a little pig. This pig was not like other pigs. He was huge! He was so big that all the other pigs would look at him and say, "Wow, you are a very big pig!" One day, the huge pig saw a big red ball. He really wanted to play with it. He tried to push the ball with his nose, but it was too big. He tried to kick it with his foot, but it was too heavy. The huge pig was sad because he could not play with the big red ball. Then, a little girl saw the huge pig. She wanted to help him. She got a stick and pushed the big red ball with it. The ball started to roll and the huge pig was so happy. He played with the ball all day long, and the little girl stayed to watch him. They became the best of friends, and the huge pig was never sad again.

Once upon a time, there was a big hotel. A bossy lady ran the hotel. She liked to raise her hand high when she talked. Everyone in the hotel listened to her. One day, a little boy came to the hotel. He saw the bossy lady raise her hand high. The boy said, "Why do you do that?" The bossy lady said, "I do it to show I am in charge." The little boy smiled and said, "I can do that too!" Later, the bossy lady had to leave the hotel for a short time. She told the little boy, "You can be in charge while I am gone. Just raise your hand high like I do." The little boy was so happy! He raised his hand high and everyone listened to him. When the bossy lady came back, she saw that the boy did a great job. She was very proud and they both raised their hands high together.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue loved to make food. She liked to make food that was colorful. One day, she wanted to make a special meal for her friend, Tim. Sue went to the store and got colorful food. She got red apples, green peas, and yellow corn. She was excited to make a yummy meal for Tim. When she got home, she cooked the food and put it on a plate. When Tim came over, he saw the colorful food on the table. He said, "Wow, Sue! This food looks so pretty and yummy!" They both sat down and ate the food. Tim and Sue were very happy because the colorful food was so tasty.
