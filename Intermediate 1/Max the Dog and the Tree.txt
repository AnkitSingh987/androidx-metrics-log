Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max had a pretty collar. He loved to run and play in the big yard. One day, Max saw a distant tree. He wanted to go and see it. Max ran to the tree and found a little girl. The girl was sad. Max wanted to help her. He kneeled down next to her and wagged his tail. The girl looked at Max and smiled. "Hi, Max," said the girl. "You have a pretty collar. Will you be my friend?" Max barked happily. From that day on, Max and the girl were best friends. They played together all the time and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a garage. He loved to play with his ball in the garage. One day, Spot's best friend, a little boy named Tim, came over to play. They went into the garage to find the ball. They looked and looked, but they could not find it. "Tim, we must find the ball," Spot urged. Tim nodded and they looked harder. Finally, they found the ball under a big box. They played and played until it was time for Tim to go home. Spot was happy that he had urged Tim to keep looking for the ball, and they had a great day together.

One day, a girl named Lucy and her dog, Max, went to a park. They saw a big grill where people cook food. Near the grill, there was a man with a brown hat. Lucy and Max went to say "hi" to the man. The man with the brown hat was very nice. He told Lucy he would cook some food on the grill for her and Max. Lucy was happy and Max wagged his tail. The man promised to make the food very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The grill started to jump and dance! The man with the brown hat was surprised too. He did not know that the grill was a magic grill. The grill cooked the food all by itself. Lucy, Max, and the man with the brown hat laughed and ate the magic food from the dancing grill.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk with her mom. They liked to see all the pretty things around them. Sue was very patient and liked to look at the flowers and trees. As they walked, the day turned to night. The sky was dark and full of stars. Sue and her mom were having a fun time, but they could not see very well in the night. Then, something unexpected happened. They saw a big, bright star fall from the sky. It landed right in front of them! The star was not a star at all, but a big, shiny rock. Sue and her mom were very surprised. They took the rock home and showed it to everyone. Now, they had a special rock from the night sky to remember their walk.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She loved to print pictures with her mommy. One day, she printed a big, pretty picture of her family. It was so nice, she wanted to put it in a frame. Lisa looked for a frame but found only a poor, broken one. She was sad. Her mommy saw her and said, "Don't worry, we can fix it!" They got some glue and fixed the frame together. It was like new! Lisa and her mommy put the picture in the frame. They hung it on the wall for everyone to see. Lisa was so happy, she clapped her hands and said, "I love my family!" The end.

Once upon a time, there was a polite dog named Silver. He loved to go on walks in the park with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They would play with a big red ball and have lots of fun. One sunny day, while they were playing, a new friend came up to them. It was a small, shy cat. The cat said, "Hi, can I play with you?" Silver and Lily smiled and said, "Of course! We love making new friends!" So, they all played together, having a great time. Silver and Lily were very happy to have a new friend to go on adventures with. And from that day on, the polite dog, the little girl, and the shy cat were the best of friends.
