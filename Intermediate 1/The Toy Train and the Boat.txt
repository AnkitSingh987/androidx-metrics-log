Category: Intermediate
Title: The Toy Train and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a toy train. The train was very happy. It liked to go fast. The train had a friend. The friend was a boat. The boat liked to sail on the water. One day, the train wanted to sail too. The boat had a plan. It made a fake lake with blue paper. The train went on the paper lake. The train was sad. It could not sail like the boat. The boat felt bad. It had an idea. The train and the boat played a new game. They raced each other. The train went fast on the tracks. The boat sailed fast on the water. They had fun and were happy friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a clumsy cat named Tom. Tom loved to eat cheese. One day, Tom saw a big piece of cheese on the table. He wanted to eat it so badly, but he could not reach it. Tom had a friend, a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry could help Tom get the cheese, but Tom and Jerry were not getting along. They were always fighting and chasing each other. Tom thought, "Maybe I can ask Jerry to help me get the cheese." Tom went to Jerry and said, "I am sorry for being mean. Can you help me get the cheese?" Jerry thought about it and said, "Okay, I will help you." They made a plan to get the cheese from the table. But Tom was very clumsy. He forgot the plan and accidentally knocked the cheese off the table. The cheese fell on the floor and broke into many pieces. Tom and Jerry were so sad. They could not eat the cheese anymore. In the end, they learned that they should work together and not fight. But it was too late, the cheese was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy cat named Fluffy. Lily loved Fluffy very much. They always played together. One day, Lily and Fluffy went to the park to play. They had a lot of fun. At the park, they met a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy dog named Spot. Tim wanted to play with Fluffy, but he was a bit selfish. He did not want to share his toy dog with Lily. Lily felt sad. She wanted to play with both toys. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Fluffy and Spot up into the sky. Lily and Tim were very scared. They waited for a minute, and then the wind stopped. Fluffy and Spot came back down from the sky. They were now friends! Lily and Tim learned that sharing was fun. They played with Fluffy and Spot together. They were all happy. They promised to always share and be good friends. And from that day on, they played together every day, having lots of fun.

Anna loved chocolate. She saw a big bar of chocolate on the table. It was her mom's chocolate, but mom was not here. Anna wanted to eat some chocolate. She tried to reach the chocolate, but it was too high. She got a chair and climbed on it. She reached the chocolate and took a big bite. But the chocolate was not good. It was very old and hard and bitter. Anna made a face and spit it out. She looked at the wrapper and saw a date. It was a very big number. It was an ancient chocolate. Mom had forgotten about it. Anna felt sick and sorry. She wished she had not eaten the chocolate. She heard mom coming back. She was afraid mom would be angry. She hid the chocolate and the chair and ran to her room. She hoped mom would not find out. She learned a lesson. She would not take things that were not hers. And she would not eat ancient chocolate.

Lily and Sam liked to play in the pool. They had many toys and floats. They were popular with the other kids. They always shared and had fun. One day, they saw a new kid sitting by the pool. He looked sad and lonely. His name was Ben. Lily and Sam wanted to be his friend. "Hi, Ben. Do you want to play with us?" Lily asked. Ben shook his head. He did not say anything. He just stared at the water. "Why not? The pool is fun. You can splash and swim and laugh," Sam said. Ben still did not answer. He looked scared. He did not like the water. He thought it was too deep and too loud. Lily and Sam felt sorry for Ben. They wanted to help him. They had an idea. "Look, Ben. We have a float. It is big and yellow and shaped like a duck. You can sit on it and be safe. We will stay with you and show you how to splash gently. It will not be scary. It will be fun," Lily said. She held out the float to Ben. Ben looked at it. He looked at Lily and Sam. They smiled at him. He felt a little bit curious. He felt a little bit brave. He nodded and took the float. He followed Lily and Sam to the pool. They helped him get on the float. They held his hands. They splashed softly. They talked to him. They made him laugh. Ben felt happy. He liked the water. He liked the float. He liked Lily and Sam. He splashed back. He said thank you. The pool was not silent anymore. It was full of joy and friendship. Lily and Sam and Ben were happy. They were popular. They were friends.
