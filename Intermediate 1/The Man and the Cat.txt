Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a man who loved to work. He made pasta every day. He was a rich man because he made the best pasta in the town. Everyone wanted to eat his pasta. The man was very happy. One day, a cat came to the man's house. The cat wanted to eat the pasta too. The man thought the cat was very cute. So, he gave some pasta to the cat. The cat ate the pasta and liked it a lot. The cat came back every day to eat more pasta. Then, one day, something unexpected happened. The cat brought a big bag of gold to the man's house. The cat was a magic cat! The cat said, "Thank you for the pasta. Now, you are even richer!" The man was very surprised and happy. From that day on, the man and the magic cat were best friends. They made pasta and shared it with everyone in the town.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Kitty. Kitty had a birthday coming up. She wanted to prepare a big party for all her friends. Kitty worked very hard to make her party fun. She made a big cake, blew up balloons, and put up decorations. All her friends came to the party and had a great time. At the end of the party, Kitty's friends were very happy. They said, "Thank you, Kitty, for preparing such a fun birthday party!" Kitty learned that when you work hard to make others happy, it makes you happy too. And that was the best gift of all.

Lily and Tom were friends. They liked to play and talk. One day, they went to the park with their moms. There, they saw a big tent. A man was talking in the tent. He had many pictures and books. He was giving a lecture. "What is he doing?" Lily asked. "He is telling people about animals and plants. He knows a lot. He is impressive," Tom's mom said. Lily and Tom wanted to hear the lecture. They asked their moms if they could go inside the tent. Their moms said yes, but they had to be quiet and listen. Lily and Tom went inside the tent. They sat on the floor. They looked at the pictures and books. They heard the man talk. He had a loud voice. He said many words they did not know. He showed them a snake, a frog, a flower, and a bird. Lily and Tom were bored. They did not like the lecture. They wanted to play and talk. They whispered to each other. "Let's go outside. This is not fun," Lily said. "OK. Let's go. But we have to be careful. The man might see us," Tom said. They got up and tried to sneak out of the tent. But they were not careful. They stepped on the snake. The snake was not a picture. It was real. It was angry. It bit Lily and Tom. They screamed. The man stopped talking. He saw them. He was mad. He yelled at them. "Who are you? Why did you come here? Why did you disturb my lecture? Why did you hurt my snake? Get out of here! Go to your moms! You are bad children!" he said. Lily and Tom ran out of the tent. They were scared and hurt. They cried. They found their moms. Their moms were worried. They saw the snake bites. They took them to the doctor. The doctor gave them medicine. He said they would be OK. But they had to stay in bed for a week. Lily and Tom were sad. They did not like the lecture. They did not like the man. They did not like the snake. They wished they had never met them. They learned a lesson. They should not go to places they do not know. They should not touch things they do not know. They should listen to their moms. They should be quiet and respectful. They should be good children.

Once upon a time there was a small, gray shield. It was very special. Every day the shield would sit in the corner and do nothing. One day, a monster came along and wanted to take the shield away. The shield was scared and tried to run away, but it was too late. The monster grabbed the shield and took it away. The shield was very sad, so it sat down and started to cry. But no matter how much it cried, it was still stuck with the monster. The monster kept the shield away from its home forever. The shield was so sad and it never got to see its corner again.
