Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to skip by the big water near her house. The big water was called the harbor. One day, Amy saw a small, frightened cat near the harbor. The cat was wet and cold. Amy wanted to help the cat. Amy said, "Don't worry, little cat, I will help you." She picked up the cat and went to find her mom. As they walked, Amy thought she heard a small sound. It sounded like someone saying, "Thank you." But it was very quiet, so she wasn't sure. When Amy and the cat got home, her mom helped them both get warm and dry. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat began to talk! "Thank you for saving me, Amy," said the cat. "I am a magic cat, and now we can be friends forever." Amy was very happy to have a new friend, and they all lived happily ever after.

Lily liked to cook with her mom. They made cakes and pies and soup. Lily helped to stir and pour and taste. She was a good helper. One day, they were making a salad. Lily washed the lettuce and the tomatoes. She saw a small insect on a leaf. It was green and had six legs. Lily was curious. She picked up the insect and looked at it. "Mom, what is this?" she asked. Mom looked at the insect and frowned. "That is a bug, Lily. It is not good for the salad. We have to throw it away." But Lily did not want to throw away the insect. She thought it was cute and funny. She wanted to keep it as a pet. "No, Mom. I like the bug. It is my friend. I want to play with it," she said. Mom shook her head. "No, Lily. The bug is not a friend. It is dirty and might make you sick. You have to let it go." Lily and Mom started to argue. Lily held the insect in her hand and did not listen to Mom. Mom tried to take the insect from Lily and did not listen to her. They were both angry and sad. It was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and it started to rain. The rain made a loud noise on the window. Lily and Mom stopped arguing and looked outside. They saw a big rainbow in the sky. It had many colors and it was bright and beautiful. Lily and Mom smiled. They forgot about the insect and the salad. They hugged each other and said sorry. "Mom, I love you," Lily said. "I love you too, Lily," Mom said. They decided to make a cake instead of a salad. They put the insect outside and hoped it would find a nice home. They cooked together and had fun. They ate the cake and it was yummy. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pig named Pinky. Pinky lived on a farm with many other animals. Pinky was a little jealous of the other animals because they could do things that Pinky could not do. The horse could run fast, the cow could give milk, and the chicken could lay eggs. One day, Pinky saw a big tree with yummy fruit. Pinky wanted to get the fruit, but the tree was too tall. Pinky asked the horse, "Can you help me get the fruit?" The horse said, "No, I can't climb trees." Pinky went to the cow and asked, "Can you help me get the fruit?" The cow said, "No, I can't reach that high." Pinky was sad, but then he had an idea. Pinky went to the tree and started to push it with all his might. The tree began to yield, and the fruit fell to the ground. Pinky was so happy that he could get the fruit all by himself. The other animals saw what Pinky did and were proud of him. Pinky was no longer jealous, because he learned that he could do something special too.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. They saw a pond with many fish. Some fish were big, some were small, and some fish looked weird. Mia liked the weird fish best because they were different. A boy named Tim saw Mia looking at the fish. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Do you like fish too?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, I like the weird fish the best. They are fun to watch." Tim agreed and they both laughed. Tim had a bag of bread with him. He offered some bread to Mia and said, "We can feed the fish together." Mia took the bread and they both threw little pieces into the pond. The fish swam fast to eat the bread. Mia and Tim enjoyed watching the fish eat and became good friends.
