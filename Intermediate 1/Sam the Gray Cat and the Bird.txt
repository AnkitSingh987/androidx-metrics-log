Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Gray Cat and the Bird

One day, a gray cat named Sam went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. As he went up, he saw a bird. The bird said, "Hi, I am Tim. What are you doing up here?" Sam said, "I like to climb trees. It is fun!" As they talked, they saw a screw hanging from a branch. Sam and Tim were surprised. "Who put that there?" asked Tim. As they looked at the screw, a big wind came. The wind blew the screw off the branch. It fell down and hit a big red button on the ground. The button made a loud noise, and a big door opened. Sam and Tim found a secret room with many toys inside. They played together all day and became best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim was scared. He lost his toy and did not know where it was. He began to worry. Tim looked around his room, but he could not find it. He felt very sad. Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim was sad and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my toy and I am scared. I looked everywhere and I can't find it." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, we will find it together." They looked and looked. It was almost noon, and they still could not find the toy. But then, Tim's mom saw something under the bed. She reached in and pulled out the toy! Tim was so happy, he hugged his mom and said, "Thank you!" Now, Tim was not scared or worried anymore.

Lily and Ben love to play with their cart. It is red and has four wheels. They can put their toys in the cart and push it around the yard. Sometimes they take turns riding in the cart, too. One day, they see a chubby cat sitting on the fence. The cat is black and white and has a fluffy tail. Lily and Ben want to pet the cat, but they are afraid it might scratch them. "Hello, cat," Lily says. "Do you want to play with us?" The cat looks at them and meows. It jumps down from the fence and walks towards them. Lily and Ben are happy. They think the cat likes them. They offer the cat some of their toys, but the cat is not interested. It sniffs the cart and climbs inside. It curls up and closes its eyes. It looks very cozy. "Look, Ben," Lily says. "The cat likes our cart. It wants to sleep in it." Ben nods. He thinks the cat is cute. He strokes the cat gently. The cat purrs. Lily and Ben smile. They decide to let the cat sleep in their cart. They sit next to the cart and watch the cat. They love the cat and the cat loves their cart. They are all happy.

Happy was a three year old boy that liked to play a lot. He had a basketball and would play with it every day. He loved to bounce it off the wall and dribble it between his legs. One day Happy was playing basketball when his mom came up to him. “Happy, it’s time to come inside,” said his mom. “But mommy, I want to keep playing,” replied Happy. “I know, but I’m busy and we can’t stay out any longer,” said his mom. So Happy put away his basketball and went inside with his mom. That night, he got ready for bed and dreamed about playing basketball with his friends. He imagined bouncing his ball off the wall and dribbling it between his legs. He smiled as he dreamed about fun basketball games and laughed as he thought about all the tricks he was going to do with his basketball. When the morning came, Happy got out of bed and smiled. He remembered his dream and couldn’t wait to play basketball again.

Once upon a time, there was a funny frog named Fred. Fred loved to tell jokes. He would hop around the pond and tell jokes to his friends. All his friends would laugh and laugh. But Fred had one problem. He would always repeat his jokes. One sunny day, Fred met a new friend, a baby bird named Bella. Fred was excited to tell Bella his best joke. He told her the joke, and Bella laughed so hard. But then, Fred told the joke again. Bella was a little frustrated. She asked Fred, "Why did you repeat the joke?" Fred didn't know why he always repeated his jokes. He felt sad and wanted to change. He asked Bella for help. Bella had an idea. She told Fred to think of a new joke every day. The next day, Fred tried to think of a new joke. He thought and thought. Then, he had a funny idea! He hopped over to Bella and told her the new joke. Bella laughed so hard she fell into the pond! All the friends laughed, and Fred was so happy. From that day on, Fred never repeated his jokes again, and he became the best joke-teller in the pond.
