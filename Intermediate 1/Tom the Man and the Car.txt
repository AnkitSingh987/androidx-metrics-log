Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Man and the Car

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a man named Tom. Tom liked to drive his big car. He felt very comfortable in it. One day, Tom decided to drive to the nearby park. On his way, Tom saw a little girl named Sue. Sue asked Tom, "Can you drive me to my house?" Tom said, "Yes, I can drive you there. Let's go!" Sue got in the car, and they started to drive to her house. But Tom did not know the way to Sue's house. They got lost. They drove and drove, but could not find the right way. Sue started to cry. Tom felt sad, too. In the end, they both stayed lost and never found Sue's house. The sun went down, and it was dark outside. They were not happy at all.

One day, a little girl named Lily was sad. She lost her toy angel. She looked everywhere. She could not find it. She said, "I need my angel." Her friend, Tom, was a smart boy. He wanted to help her. He said, "Let's look for your angel together." They looked in the house. They looked in the yard. They could not find the angel. Lily and Tom were very sad. They sat down on the grass. A big dog came to them. The dog had the angel in its mouth. The dog said, "Woof!" Lily and Tom were happy. They said, "Thank you, dog!" The dog wagged its tail and ran away. Lily and Tom played with the angel and had a fun day.

Once there was a poor family who had a little boy who was only three years old. On his third birthday, the little boy's parents wanted to deliver a present to him. The little boy woke up early in the morning and ran to the kitchen to see what was the present his parents had given him. When he arrived in the kitchen, he saw a big box with a bow on top of it. He was so excited! He opened the box and inside he found a toy truck. He was overjoyed! He then ran to his parents with the truck and asked, “What is this?” His parents replied with a smile, “It is your birthday present. Now you can use it to carry and deliver things!” The little boy drove the truck around the house and he was so happy, feeling no pain. Suddenly, he heard loud knocking coming from the door. He raced to see who it was. When he opened the door, he saw a lady with a basket. She was a neighbor in need. She asked if the family could deliver some food to her family. Of course, the poor family said yes and the little boy was eager to grab his truck and deliver the food! The little boy was so happy to be able to help the lady and he was proud that he could use his truck to deliver something. He had learned a valuable lesson about helping others

Lily is sick. She has a fever and a cough. Mommy gives her a pill to make her feel better. Lily does not like the pill. It is big and bitter. "Please, Lily, take the pill. It will help you," Mommy says. "No, no, no, I don't want it!" Lily cries. Mommy has an idea. She gets a bright red hat from the closet. It has a funny pom-pom on top. "Look, Lily, this is a magic hat. If you wear it and take the pill, you will be well soon," Mommy says. Lily likes the hat. It is soft and warm. She puts it on her head. "OK, Mommy, I will try the pill," Lily says. Mommy smiles and gives her the pill with some water. Lily swallows it quickly. "Good girl, Lily. You are very brave. Now you can rest and watch your favorite show," Mommy says. Lily snuggles with Mommy on the couch. She feels a little better. She likes the hat. She thinks it is magic.

Tim and Kim were twins who liked to play soccer. They wanted to win a trophy for their team. One day, they had a big game against another team. The other team was very good and fast. Tim and Kim tried hard to score goals, but they could not. The other team scored many goals and made them sad. Tim and Kim started to get angry and blame each other. They said mean things and pulled each other's shirts. They stopped working together and playing nice. The coach saw them and called them out. He said, "Tim and Kim, you are not helping your team. You are hurting your team. You need to be kind and share the ball. You need to help each other and cheer each other up. That is how you win a trophy, not by fighting and pulling." Tim and Kim felt sorry and hugged each other. They said, "We are sorry, coach. We are sorry, team. We will be kind and share the ball. We will help each other and cheer each other up." They went back to the game and played better. They scored some goals and had fun. The game ended and the other team won. They got the trophy and held it high. Tim and Kim felt sad, but they clapped and smiled. They said, "Good game, good game. You played very well. You deserve the trophy." The other team thanked them and said, "You played very well too. You were very nice and fair. You can hold the trophy too." They gave Tim and Kim the trophy and let them hold it for a while. Tim and Kim felt happy and proud. They learned that being kind and sharing is more important than winning. They learned that the trophy is not so distant if they work together and play nice. They learned that soccer is more fun when they are friends.
