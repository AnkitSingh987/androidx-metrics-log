Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Window

One day, a boy named Tim went to class. He sat with his friends, Sam and Sue. They liked to learn and play together. In class, they learned about colors, shapes, and animals. Suddenly, a bitter wind blew through the open window. Tim felt very cold. He looked outside and saw dark clouds. He shouted, "Look, it's going to rain!" The teacher closed the window to keep the class warm. Just then, a big, bright sun appeared in the sky. The dark clouds went away. The bitter wind stopped. Sam, Sue, and Tim were surprised. They all laughed and said, "Wow, that was unexpected!" The teacher smiled and said, "Now, let's go outside and play in the sunshine."

Once upon a time, there was a lonely queen. She lived in a big castle. The queen had no friends. She was very sad. One day, a little bird arrived at the castle. The bird said, "Hello, queen! Why are you sad?" The queen said, "I am lonely. I have no friends." The bird said, "I will be your friend!" The queen was happy. They played and had fun. The queen was not lonely anymore. And they were friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to imagine and play with her toys. One day, she was playing with her toy frog and thought, "What if my frog could talk?" So, Lily imagined that her frog could talk. The frog said, "Hi, Lily! I'm your friend, but be careful. There is a poison berry in the garden. Don't eat it, or you will feel sick." Lily listened to her frog friend and went to the garden. She saw the poison berry and remembered what her friend had said. She decided not to eat it, even though it looked yummy. In the garden, Lily met a selfish boy named Tom. Tom saw the poison berry and said, "I want to eat it! It looks so good!" But Lily told him, "No, Tom! My frog friend said it's poison and will make us sick." Tom listened to Lily and didn't eat the poison berry. They played together in the garden, and Lily shared her toys with Tom. They became good friends and always remembered to be careful with poison things and not be selfish.

Once upon a time, there was a modest boy named Will. He loved to smile and play with his friends. One day, Will saw a big, red ball in the park. He wanted to play with it, but it belonged to another kid. Will asked the kid, "Can I play with your ball?" The kid said, "No, it's mine." Will was sad, but he still wanted to play. So, he took the ball when the kid was not looking. Later, the kid found out that Will took his ball. The kid was very angry and told Will's mom. Will's mom was upset with him too. She said, "Will, you should not take things that do not belong to you. Now, you must give the ball back and say sorry." Will gave the ball back and said sorry, but the kid and his mom did not forgive him. They told everyone about what Will did, and no one wanted to play with him anymore. Will learned that it is important to be kind and not take things that belong to others.

One day, a big, loud dog named Max was playing in the park. Max liked to run and jump all day. The sun was hot and bright, and Max was very happy. Max saw a big stick on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his friend, a little girl named Lily. Max wagged his tail and showed Lily the stick. "Wow, Max! Let's play with the stick," said Lily. They played a game called "split the stick." Max and Lily took turns trying to split the stick in half. After a while, Max finally split the stick with a loud snap. Lily clapped her hands and laughed. "Good job, Max!" she said. They were both very proud of Max. They played in the park until the sun went down, and then they went home to rest.
