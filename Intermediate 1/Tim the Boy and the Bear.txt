Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy bear named Ben. Tim and Ben were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Tim saw that Ben was very dirty. Tim wanted to help Ben get clean. Tim's mom gave him a cloth to clean Ben. Tim said, "Ben, you are dirty. I will help you get clean." Tim used the cloth and cleaned Ben. Ben looked clean and happy. Tim felt good for helping his friend. Tim's mom saw what Tim did and said, "Good job, Tim! You encouraged Ben to be clean." Tim smiled and hugged his mom. From that day on, Tim and Ben always stayed clean and had fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. The sun was bright and the water was cold. Tim loved to play in the sand and make big castles. He saw a small crab and said, "Hello, Mr. Crab! Do you like my castle?" The crab looked at Tim and said, "Yes, but it needs something more. I think it needs some salt!" Tim was confused, but he wanted to make the crab happy. So he ran to his mom and asked for salt. Tim's mom gave him a small bag of salt. He ran back to his sand castle and put the salt on it. Suddenly, the sand castle started to grow and grow! Tim couldn't believe his eyes. He didn't recognize his own castle anymore. It was now a huge, real castle made of sand and salt. The crab laughed and said, "Now that's a castle fit for a king! Thank you, Tim!" Tim and his mom went inside the big castle and had a fun day playing together. They never forgot the day when something unexpected happened at the beach.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a gray bubble wand in her toy box. She ran to her mom and asked, "Mom, can I play with this bubble wand? Can I make bubbles?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Sally, you can play with the bubble wand. Let's go outside and make bubbles." Sally and her mom went outside to the yard. Sally's mom helped her put the gray bubble wand in the bubble mix. Sally waved the wand in the air and made big and small bubbles. The bubbles flew in the sky and she laughed. "Look, Mom! I made so many bubbles!" Sally said. A little boy named Tim saw the bubbles from his house. He ran to Sally's yard and asked, "Can I play with you and make bubbles too?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with me. My mom will allow it." Tim, Sally, and her mom played with the gray bubble wand and made lots of bubbles. They had so much fun making bubbles together.

One day, a little girl named Mia had a fancy calendar on her wall. It was full of pretty pictures and colors. She loved to gaze at it every day. She liked to count the days until her birthday. One sunny morning, Mia was looking at her calendar when she saw a new picture. It was a picture of a cat! "Mommy, look!" she said. "There is a cat on my calendar!" Her mommy came and looked at the calendar. "That is strange," her mommy said. "I did not see that cat there yesterday." The next day, Mia looked at her calendar again. This time, there was a dog next to the cat! "Mommy, now there is a dog too!" she said. Her mommy was very surprised. "I wonder where these animals are coming from," she said. Mia and her mommy decided to watch the calendar all day to see if they could find out. They sat and gazed at the calendar for hours. Then, just as the sun was going down, they saw something amazing. The animals on the calendar came to life and jumped out of the picture! Mia and her mommy laughed and played with the cat and dog. From that day on, they knew that their fancy calendar was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play chess with his dad. They would sit on the warm rug and have fun playing the game. One day, when they were playing, Tim's favorite chess piece, the knight, disappeared. Tim was very sad and didn't know what to do. Tim looked everywhere for the knight, but he couldn't find it. He asked his dad, "Dad, have you seen my knight?" His dad said, "No, Tim, I haven't seen it. But don't worry, we will find it together." They searched all around the house, but the knight was still gone. Finally, they found the knight under the sofa. Tim was so happy to have his knight back. He hugged his dad and said, "Thank you, Dad, for helping me find my knight!" The moral of the story is that when we lose something, it's important to stay calm and ask for help. Together, we can solve any problem.
