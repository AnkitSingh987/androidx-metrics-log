Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Pig

Lily liked to visit the farm with her mom and dad. She loved to see the animals and feed them some corn. Her favorite animal was the pig. The pig was big and pink and made funny noises. Lily wanted to pet the pig and give him a hug. Mom and dad told Lily to be careful with the pig. They said the pig was not like a dog or a cat. They said the pig could bite or kick if he was scared or angry. They said the pig had sharp teeth and strong legs. They tried to explain to Lily that the pig was not a toy, but a living thing. Lily did not listen to mom and dad. She thought they were silly and wrong. She thought the pig was friendly and cute. She thought the pig liked her and wanted to play. She waited until mom and dad were busy with the other animals. Then she ran to the pig pen and climbed over the fence. The pig was sleeping in the mud. He looked peaceful and happy. Lily smiled and walked towards him. She reached out her hand to touch his nose. She did not see the other pigs behind her. She did not hear their grunts and snorts. She did not feel their teeth and hooves. Mom and dad heard Lily scream. They turned around and saw the pigs attack her. They ran to the pig pen and tried to save her. But it was too late. The pigs had hurt Lily very badly. She was bleeding and crying. Mom and dad were sad and angry. They wished they had explained to Lily better. They wished Lily had listened to them. They wished they had never visited the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Winky. Winky was very good at giving winks. One day, Winky went for a walk in the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. While walking, Winky saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. She had lost her toy. Winky wanted to help, so he gave the girl a wink. The girl smiled and felt better. Together, they looked for the toy. Suddenly, they heard a scream. It was the toy! It was stuck in a tree. Winky climbed the tree and saved the toy. The girl was so happy. They all became good friends and played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny named Bobo. Bobo liked to jog in the park every day. He had many friends who would jog with him. They would laugh and play together. One day, while jogging, Bobo found a small, lost kitty. The kitty was sad and scared. Bobo wanted to help and comfort the kitty. He gave the kitty a big, warm hug. But then, something unexpected happened! The kitty turned into a big, pink butterfly! Bobo and his friends were very surprised. The butterfly thanked Bobo for his help and flew away. Now, every time Bobo and his friends jog in the park, they look for the pink butterfly to say hello.

Ben and Tom were brothers who liked to play with marbles. They had many marbles of different colors and sizes, and they kept them in a big jar. One day, they decided to go to the store with their mom and buy some candy. They took their jar of marbles with them, because they wanted to show them to their friends. At the store, they saw a lot of things that they liked. They saw toys, books, clothes, and snacks. They asked their mom if they could have some, but she said no. She said they only had money for candy and bread. Ben and Tom were sad, but they still wanted candy. They followed their mom to the candy aisle and picked some lollipops. While they were waiting in line to pay, they saw a man with a big coat and a hat. He looked at their jar of marbles and smiled. He said, "Hey, kids, those are some nice marbles you have there. Do you want to trade them for something?" Ben and Tom were curious. They asked, "What do you have?" The man opened his coat and showed them a shiny watch. He said, "This is a very special watch. It can tell you the time, the date, and the weather. It can also play music and games. It's worth a lot of money. Do you want to trade your marbles for it?" Ben and Tom were amazed. They had never seen such a watch before. They thought it was better than candy. They said, "Yes, we want to trade!" The man took their jar of marbles and gave them the watch. He said, "Thank you, kids. You made a good deal. Enjoy your watch." He walked away quickly, before their mom could see him. Ben and Tom were happy. They put the watch on their wrist and pressed some buttons. They heard some beeps and saw some lights. They thought it was cool. But then, something went wrong. The watch started to make loud noises and smoke. It burned their skin and hurt their arm. They screamed and dropped the watch. It broke into pieces on the floor. Their mom heard them and ran to them. She saw the watch and the marbles. She was angry and scared. She said, "What have you done? Where did you get that watch? Where are your marbles?" Ben and Tom were scared and sorry. They told their mom what happened. They said they traded their marbles for the watch. They said they thought it was a good deal. They said they didn't know the watch was bad. They said they wanted their marbles back. Their mom said, "You were very foolish and careless. You gave away your marbles for nothing. You lost your marbles and your candy. You could have been hurt badly. You can't get your marbles back. The man is gone. He tricked you. He was a bad man." Ben and Tom cried. They felt helpless and stupid. They wished they had never seen the watch. They wished they had never traded their marbles. They wished they had listened to their mom. They learned a hard lesson. They never played with marbles again.
