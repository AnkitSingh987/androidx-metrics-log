Category: Intermediate
Title: The Toys of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys all day. One day, his mom told him they were going to visit his grandma. Tim was very excited and started to pack his toys in a bag. He also found a small box of powder in his room. When Tim and his mom got to grandma's house, he showed her all his toys. He also showed her the powder. Grandma said, "Oh no, Tim! That powder is for cleaning, not for playing." Tim felt embarrassed and said, "I didn't know, grandma." Later, Tim and his mom went to the store to buy a new toy. Tim picked a toy car and was very happy. When they got back to grandma's house, Tim played with his new car and forgot all about the powder. He learned that some things are not for playing with, and he was never embarrassed again.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toys and eat ice cream. One day, his mom told him they were going on a trip on Sunday. Tim was so happy! Tim wanted to help his mom pack their bags for the trip. He tried to put his clothes and toys in the bag, but he was very clumsy. His clothes fell on the floor, and his toys rolled away. Tim's mom laughed and helped him pack the right way. On Sunday, Tim and his mom went on their trip. They had so much fun! They played in the park and ate yummy food. Even though Tim was clumsy, he was a very happy boy. And his mom loved him very much.

Once upon a time, there was a light rain. It made many puddles on the ground. One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw the puddles and had a fun idea. Lily ran to her toy box and took out her toy boats. She went back outside and put the boats in a big puddle. She added more and more boats to the puddle. The boats floated and moved around. Lily was very happy. As the rain stopped, the sun came out. The puddles started to go away. Lily picked up her boats and went inside. She knew she could play with the puddles and boats again the next time it rained.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a mitten on the ground. It was a quiet day and she wanted to do something fun. She thought, "I can decorate this mitten and make it pretty!" Lily went home and found some colorful things to put on the mitten. She used red, blue, and yellow to make it look nice. When she was done, she showed her mom. "Look, Mom! I decorated the mitten!" she said. Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Lily! You did a great job! Now you have a pretty mitten to wear when it's cold outside." Lily was so happy that she had made something beautiful and she couldn't wait to wear her new mitten.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He had a friend, a little bird named Tim. They lived in a big tree in a green park. One sunny day, Tom and Tim saw a big piece of cake on a table in the park. They wanted to eat it. Tom was too lazy to fly, so Tim flew down to the table. He wanted to touch the cake to see if it was soft. When Tim touched the cake, it was very soft and sweet. He called Tom to come and eat the cake with him. Tom was so excited that he forgot he was lazy. He jumped from the tree and ran to the table. They both ate the cake and had a great time. After eating the cake, Tom and Tim played in the park all day. They were very happy. From that day on, Tom was not lazy anymore. He loved to play with Tim, and they had many fun adventures together.
