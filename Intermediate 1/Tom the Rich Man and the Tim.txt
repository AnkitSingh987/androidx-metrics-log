Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Rich Man and the Tim

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had many spears in his big house. One day, he met a little boy named Tim. Tim saw the spears and was very excited. Tom said, "Do you like my spears, Tim?" Tim nodded and said, "Yes, I do! Can we count them?" So, Tom and Tim started to count the spears together. They counted one, two, three, four, five, and many more. When they finished counting, they found out that Tom had ten spears. Tim was very happy to learn how to count. Tom was happy too, because he made a new friend. From that day on, Tom and Tim met every day to count things and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy truck that he loved to play with. One day, he wanted to load his truck with something fun. He looked around his room and saw a big purple ball. Tim thought the purple ball would be perfect to load onto his truck. Tim tried to push the purple ball onto his truck, but it was too big. He was frightened that he might break his toy truck. So, he asked his mommy for help. His mommy had an idea. She told Tim to wait and went to the kitchen. Tim waited and soon his mommy came back with a smaller purple ball. She said, "Here, Tim! This purple ball is just the right size for your truck!" Tim was so happy. He loaded the small purple ball onto his truck and played all day long. And from that day on, Tim and the purple ball had many fun adventures together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a boy with a pretty scarf. Lucy wanted to say hi and introduce herself. She walked to the boy and said, "Hi, I'm Lucy. I like your scarf!" The boy smiled and said, "Hi Lucy, I'm Tom! My mom made this scarf for me. It keeps me warm when it's cold." Lucy and Tom played together all day. They went on the swings and down the slide. They had so much fun. After playing, they were hungry. They went to the park's food stand, but it was empty. There was no food left. Lucy and Tom were sad. But then, Lucy's mom came to the park with a big basket of food. They all sat down and ate together. Tom shared his scarf with Lucy, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful dog named Daisy. Daisy loved to play with her ball and watch TV. One day, Daisy saw a little girl named Lily on TV who could bow. Daisy thought it was fun and wanted to learn it too. Daisy went to her best friend, a cat named Whiskers. She said, "Whiskers, I saw a girl on TV bow. Can you teach me how to bow?" Whiskers thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I can help you." Whiskers showed Daisy how to bow. Daisy tried and tried until she could bow just like the girl on TV. They were both very happy and proud. Now, Daisy could play with her ball, watch TV, and bow like the girl on TV. She was a very special and beautiful dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Fluffy saw a big pot on the stove. The pot was making steam. Fluffy was very curious about the steam. Fluffy's mom saw him looking at the pot. She said, "Fluffy, be careful with the steam. It can make your ball disappear." Fluffy didn't want his ball to disappear, so he played far away from the pot. Later, Fluffy saw his friend Mousey. Mousey had a spoiled cheese. Mousey wanted to play with Fluffy's ball. But Fluffy remembered what his mom said. He didn't want his ball to disappear. So, Fluffy and Mousey played without the ball. They had fun, and Fluffy's ball stayed safe.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to ride the bus with their mom. They always sat near the front and watched the driver. The driver was a nice man who wore a blue hat and smiled a lot. He was attractive, Lily thought, but Ben did not care. One day, the bus was very crowded and noisy. Lily and Ben could not see the driver well. They felt bored and restless. They started to play a game of shout. They took turns shouting different words as loud as they could. "Dog!" shouted Lily. "Cat!" shouted Ben. "Red!" shouted Lily. "Green!" shouted Ben. The other people on the bus did not like the game. They looked annoyed and angry. Some of them told Lily and Ben to be quiet. But Lily and Ben did not listen. They thought it was fun. "Stop!" shouted the driver. He sounded very serious. He looked at Lily and Ben in the mirror. "You are making too much noise. You are disturbing the other passengers. You have to be polite and respectful on the bus. Do you understand?" Lily and Ben felt ashamed. They nodded and said sorry. They stopped shouting and sat quietly. They did not want to make the driver mad. They still liked him, even when he was stern. They hoped he would smile again.
