Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Pink Bunny and the Turtle

Once upon a time, there was a little pink bunny named Lucy. Lucy loved to hop and play all day long. One sunny day, she met a new friend named Timmy the turtle. Timmy was slow, but he was very kind. Lucy and Timmy played together every day. They would repeat their favorite game, hide and seek. Lucy would hop and hide, and Timmy would slowly find her. Then, they would switch and Timmy would hide while Lucy looked for him. They laughed and had fun all day long. As they played together day after day, Lucy learned to be patient and wait for Timmy. Timmy learned that even though he was slow, he could still have fun with his fast friend. The moral of the story is that we can learn from our friends and have fun together, even if we are different.

One day, Tom and his mom went to the theater. They were very excited to see a play. Before they left, they had to prepare. Tom's mom said, "Tom, put on your shoes and coat, we are going to the theater!" At the theater, Tom saw a man who was careless. The man dropped his popcorn on the floor. Tom's mom said, "Oh no, that man was not careful with his popcorn!" The play started, and Tom and his mom enjoyed it a lot. After the play, Tom said, "Mom, I want to be in a play too!" Tom's mom smiled and said, "Maybe one day, Tom. Just remember to always be careful and prepare well!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big fog come. Tim felt frightened. He did not know what to do. The fog was very thick, and he could not see. Tim heard a voice in the fog. It was a little girl named Sue. Sue said, "I am scared too. Let's hold hands and find our way home." They held hands and walked together. They felt better because they were not alone. After a while, the fog went away. Tim and Sue found their way home. They were happy they found each other. Tim said, "I am sorry I was scared." Sue said, "I was scared too, but we helped each other. Now we are friends." They both smiled and did not regret being frightened in the fog.

Lily was a young girl who liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She liked to mix and roll and cut the dough for the pastries. Her mom taught her how to make apple pies and cherry tarts and lemon bars. Lily was very proud of her pastries and wanted to share them with her friends. One day, Lily decided to take some pastries to the park and give them to the other kids. She put them in a basket and covered them with a cloth. She walked to the park with her mom and saw many kids playing and laughing. She felt happy and excited. She went to a bench and opened her basket. She saw a boy and a girl sitting on the swings. She smiled and waved at them. She said, "Hi, I'm Lily. Do you want some pastries? I made them with my mom. They are very yummy." The boy and the girl looked at her and then at each other. They did not know Lily. They did not trust her. They thought she was trying to trick them or make them sick. They said, "No, thank you. We don't want your pastries. Go away." Lily felt sad and hurt. She did not understand why they did not want her pastries. She did not mean to be bad or mean. She just wanted to be nice and make friends. She closed her basket and walked away. She cried and hugged her mom. Her mom hugged her back and said, "It's okay, Lily. Sometimes people are scared of things they don't know. They don't trust strangers. But you did a good thing. You were kind and generous. Maybe next time, you can find someone who is more friendly and open. Don't give up, Lily. You are a wonderful girl and I love you very much."
