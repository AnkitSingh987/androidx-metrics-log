Category: Intermediate
Title: Jazz the Regular Cat and the Magic

One day, a regular cat named Jazz was walking down the street. Jazz saw a seat near a big tree. She thought it looked like a nice place to sit and take a nap. Jazz jumped onto the seat and started to sleep. Suddenly, the seat started to move! It was not a regular seat; it was a magic seat! Jazz was very surprised. The magic seat took Jazz high up into the sky. Jazz was scared at first, but then she saw all the fun things she could see from up high. Jazz liked the magic seat and it took her back home. From that day on, Jazz and the magic seat went on many fun trips together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly tree. It was very tall and it would lean to one side. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the tree and wanted to climb it. As Tim started to climb the ugly tree, he saw smoke coming from a small fire nearby. The fire scared Tim, and he did not know what to do. He wanted to help put out the fire, but he was just a little boy. Tim called for help, and soon his mom and dad came running. They saw the smoke and the fire too. They quickly put out the fire with water. Tim was very happy that the ugly tree was safe, and he thanked his mom and dad for their help.

One day, a girl named Sue found a big, ugly dress in her mom's closet. She did not like how it looked. Sue wanted to make the dress pretty. So, she took out her scissors and began to cut the dress. She cut and cut until the dress was in two parts. Sue had split the dress! Sue's friend, Ann, came over to play. Ann saw the two parts of the dress and said, "What did you do?" Sue told her how she split the ugly dress to make it pretty. Ann had an idea. "Let's sew the two parts together and make a new dress!" she said. Sue and Ann worked hard to sew the two parts together. When they finished, they were very happy. The new dress was not ugly anymore. It was pretty and colorful! Sue's mom came in and saw the dress. She was surprised, but she smiled. "You made the dress beautiful," she said. Sue and Ann were proud of their work.

Once upon a time, there was a kind man named Tom. Tom liked to help people. He was very good at fixing things. He could repair toys, bikes, and even cars. People in the town loved Tom because he was always there to help them. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tom with a book. It was her favorite novel. But the book was torn and the pages were falling out. Sue was very sad. Tom saw the harsh way the book was treated and knew he had to help Sue. Tom worked hard to repair the book. He put the pages back in and fixed the cover. When he was done, the book looked like new. Sue was so happy that she hugged the book and thanked Tom. The people in the town learned that they should be kind to their things and take care of them. And they knew that if they needed help, Tom would always be there to lend a hand.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty house with a red roof. Inside the house, there were two friends named Cat and Dog. They liked to play together all day. But sometimes, they would fight over a toy. One sunny day, Cat and Dog were playing with their favorite toy. They both thought the toy was very attractive. They started to fight over the toy. The toy flew up in the air and landed on the roof of the pretty house. Cat and Dog knew they had to work together to get the toy back. They stopped fighting and helped each other climb up to the roof. When they reached the top, they found the toy and happily shared it. From that day on, Cat and Dog promised to share their toys and never fight again.
