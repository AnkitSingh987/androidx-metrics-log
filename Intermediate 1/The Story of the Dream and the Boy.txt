Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dream and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a young boy who had a very special dream. Every night he would close his eyes and wish really hard for it to come true. He wanted it more than anything in the world, but it was very expensive. The boy knew he needed to find a way to get the money for his dream. So he decided to start working hard to save up. Every day he worked, sold and saved, until finally he had enough money to buy his dream. The boy was so excited when he bought it. He hugged it tight and couldn't wait to show it off to his friends. But the next day he woke up and his dream had returned! All the money he had was gone. The boy was so sad, but he realised something. He realised that his dream didn't need money to be real and that he already had it. So he hugged his dream tight and never let it go. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very dirty boy. He loved to play in the mud and get his clothes all dirty. His parent would always tell him to clean up before coming inside. One sunny day, Tim was playing outside when he found a big rock. He thought, "I can split this rock!" So, he tried to split the rock with his hands, but it was too hard. Then, he saw his parent walking by and asked for help. "Parent, can you help me split this rock?" Tim asked. His parent smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's split it together." They both pushed on the rock and it split in half. Tim was so happy, and his parent was proud of him. After that, they went inside to clean up and have a snack.

One day, a little dog named Spot found a silly tie in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and ran home. Spot wanted to show his friend, a big cat named Fluffy, his new toy. Spot and Fluffy liked to play games with the tie. They would pull it, and sometimes Fluffy would wear it. One day, they played near the door. Spot pulled the tie and Fluffy pulled back. They were having so much fun. But then, the tie got stuck in the door. Spot tried to pull it out, but he couldn't. Fluffy tried to help, but the door shut on the tie. The tie was stuck, and they couldn't play with it anymore. Spot and Fluffy were sad. They lost their silly tie, and now they had to find a new toy to play with.

One day, a cat was playing with a ball. The cat was crazy and loved to jump high. The ball went very far and the cat ran to get it. But the cat did not see a big rock and it hit the rock with its head. The cat fell down and started to cry. A little boy heard the cat cry and went to help it. He saw the cat was hurt and did not know what to do. He ran to his mom and told her about the cat. The mom called an ambulance to help the cat. The ambulance came very fast. The cat was scared but the boy stayed with it. The ambulance took the cat to the doctor. The doctor helped the cat and it stopped crying. The boy was happy and the cat was okay. They played with the ball again, but this time, they looked for rocks.

Lia likes to play with blocks. She has many blocks of different colors and shapes. She can make towers, bridges, houses and more with her blocks. She is very adventurous and likes to try new things. One day, she decides to make a long row of blocks on the floor. She starts with a red block, then adds a blue block, then a yellow block, and so on. She wants to see how many blocks she can add to the row. She counts the blocks as she adds them. One, two, three, four, five... She is having fun, but she also wants to make the row more interesting. She looks around and sees some other toys. She adds a car, a ball, a doll and a book to the row. She thinks the row looks more colorful and fun now. She counts the toys as she adds them. Six, seven, eight, nine... She is happy with her row, but she also wants to make it more adventurous. She sees some pillows, blankets and chairs. She adds them to the row, making it higher and wider. She thinks the row looks more exciting and challenging now. She counts the big things as she adds them. Ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen... She is proud of her row, but she also wants to make it more fun. She climbs on top of the row and pretends it is a mountain, a train, a castle and a spaceship. She makes noises and stories as she plays. She thinks the row is the best thing she ever made. She smiles and laughs as she plays. She is very adventurous and happy.
