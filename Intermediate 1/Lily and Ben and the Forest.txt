Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Forest

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the forest near their home. They had a secret spot where they built a fort with sticks and leaves. They liked to pretend they were explorers or spies or fairies. One day, they heard a strange noise in the forest. It sounded like a growl or a roar. They got scared and ran to their fort. They hid inside and whispered to each other. "What is that?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Maybe a bear or a lion or a monster," Ben said. "Should we go and see?" Lily said. "No, we should stay here and be quiet. Maybe it will go away," Ben said. They waited and listened. The noise got louder and closer. They held each other and hoped it would not find them. Suddenly, they heard a familiar voice. It was their dad. He was calling their names. "Lily! Ben! Where are you? It's time to go home!" They peeked out of their fort and saw their dad walking towards them. He looked serious and worried. They ran out of their fort and hugged him. "Dad! We're here! We're sorry! We heard a scary noise and we hid in our fort!" Lily said. "What scary noise?" Dad asked. "It sounded like a bear or a lion or a monster," Ben said. Dad smiled and shook his head. "That was not a bear or a lion or a monster. That was me. I was trying to make you laugh. I was pretending to be a dinosaur. Did I scare you?" Lily and Ben looked at each other and then at their dad. They realized he was joking. They felt silly and relieved. They laughed and said, "Yes, you scared us! But it was funny too. You're a good dinosaur, Dad!" Dad hugged them and said, "I'm sorry I scared you. I love you, my little explorers. Now let's go home and have some cookies. And next time, don't go too far in the forest, okay?" Lily and Ben nodded and said, "Okay, Dad. We love you too. And we love the forest. And our fort. And dinosaurs. And cookies." They walked home with their dad, holding his hands and telling him about their adventures in the forest. They were happy and safe.

One day, a chicken and a dog were friends. They liked to play and talk together. Today, they wanted to discuss what game to play. The chicken said, "Let's play a boring game." The dog said, "No, I want to play a fun game." While they were talking, a big wind came. It blew the chicken up into a tree. The chicken was scared. The dog said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The dog found a long stick. He used the stick to help the chicken get down. The chicken was happy. They both said, "This was not a boring day!" And they played a fun game together.

In the morning when the sky was clear, Sarah rushed outside to play. She wanted to have lots of fun and make the most of the day. But when she got outside she realised something was wrong. Her bike was missing from the garden. She ran inside to tell her mum and dad, she was so sad and confused. Sarah’s mum and dad were very cross. They searched the house and garden but the bike was nowhere to be seen. They told Sarah that someone must have taken it and made her stay inside. Sarah was upset. She wanted to be playing outside in the fresh air and sunshine. But her mum and dad said it wasn’t safe now. Suddenly Sarah had an idea. She quickly ran outside and started calling for her friends. When they heard her story they all offered to help. Together they spread out searching the streets and nearby parks. Soon they found the bike abandoned in a park. Everyone was so happy and Sarah rushed home to tell her mum and dad the good news. From then on she remembered to never leave her bike outside, even on the clearest of mornings.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends on the floor. One day, Spot found a big box on the floor. He was very curious about what was inside. Spot's friend, Cat, came over and said, "What's in the box, Spot?" Spot replied, "I don't know, let's solve this together!" They both looked at the box and tried to open it. They pushed, pulled, and even jumped on it, but the box would not open. Then, Spot had an idea. He remembered seeing a key under his bed. He ran to his bed and found the key. Spot and Cat used the key to open the box. Inside, they found lots of fun toys to play with. They were so happy they solved the mystery of the big box. From that day on, Spot and Cat enjoyed playing with their new toys on the floor.
