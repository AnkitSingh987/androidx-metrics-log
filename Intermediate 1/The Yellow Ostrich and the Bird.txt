Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow Ostrich and the Bird

One day, a yellow ostrich went for a walk. It saw a big tree with yummy fruit. The ostrich tried to reach the fruit, but it was too high. A little bird saw the ostrich and wanted to help. The bird flew up and picked the fruit from the tree. It dropped the fruit down to the yellow ostrich. The ostrich was very happy and said "thank you" to the bird. They both ate the fruit together. After they ate, the bird and the ostrich played all day. They ran and jumped and had lots of fun. When the sun went down, they said "goodbye" and went to their homes. The fun day had to end, but they knew they would play again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. The cat liked to play with a cart. Every day, the cat would push the cart up and down the road. The cat was very happy. One day, the cat found a big box on the road. The cat was curious. It pushed the box with its cart. The box opened, and a little bird came out. The cat was so surprised! It had never seen a bird like this before. The bird was sad and started to weep. The cat wanted to help the bird. It let the bird ride in the cart. They played together all day. The bird stopped weeping and became happy. The cat and the bird became best friends. And from that day on, they always played together with the cart.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to wear her pretty red dress. One day, Amy went to school with her folder. In her folder, she had her drawings and her favorite stickers. At school, she met a boy named Tom. Tom was sometimes rude. He would take things from other kids and not say "please" or "thank you." Amy did not like when Tom was rude. One day, Tom saw Amy's folder and wanted to look at it. Amy said, "please be nice and don't be rude." Tom listened to Amy and was nice. He looked at her drawings and stickers and said, "thank you." From that day on, Tom was not rude anymore. Amy and Tom became good friends and shared their folders with each other.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a row of big boxes. They were not regular boxes. They were very big and colorful. Tim was very excited to see what was inside the boxes. Tim started to unpack the first box. Inside, he found a big, red ball. He played with it for a while, but then he wanted to see what was in the other boxes. So, he unpacked the second box. Inside, he found a big, blue ball. Tim was very happy to have two big balls to play with. But when he unpacked the third box, there was no ball. Instead, there was a note. The note said, "Surprise! You found me!" Tim looked around and saw his friends hiding behind a tree. They all laughed and came out to play with Tim and the big balls. It was a fun day at the park.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a long chain in his room. He did not know where it came from. He wanted to play with it, but it was too long. So, Tim thought of an idea. He decided to fold the chain to make it shorter. As Tim folded the chain, something unexpected happened. The chain turned into a big and beautiful toy train! Tim was very happy and played with the train all day. But then, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Tim did not want to share the train, so he hid it. Sue looked sad and asked where the train was. Tim felt ashamed for hiding the train. He decided to tell Sue the truth and share the train with her. They both played with the train together and had a great time. And from that day on, Tim always shared his toys with his friends.
