Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Tidy Cat and the Slide

One day, a tidy cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big, red button on the ground. Tim was very curious about the button. He looked at it, and then he looked around to see if anyone was watching him. Tim heard a small voice say, "Hi, I am Bee! Do you know what that button does?" Tim said, "No, I don't know. Should I push it?" Bee said, "I don't know, but I want to find out!" Tim and Bee decided to push the button together. When they did, something unexpected happened. A big, fun slide came out of the ground! Tim and Bee were so surprised and happy. They played on the slide all day and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her pretend kitchen. She would make yummy food for her toys and friends. One sunny day, Lily invited her best friend, Sam, to play with her. They both had fun in the pretend kitchen. They made pretend cake, pie, and cookies for their toys. After playing, Lily and Sam shared a big hug. They were happy to play together in the pretend kitchen. And Lily was not shy anymore.

One day, a big cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was trying to fill her cup with water. But the handle on the cup was broken. She was sad. Tom wanted to help Sue. He said, "I can fix your cup. Then you can fill it with water." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom found a strong stick and tied it to the cup to make a new handle. Now Sue could fill her cup with water. She was very happy. She said, "Now I can drink water by myself. I am independent!" But when she tried to lift the cup, the handle broke again! The water spilled everywhere. Tom felt bad. He did not want Sue to be sad. So he had an idea. He said, "I will hold the cup for you. Then you can drink the water." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom! We can be friends and help each other." And they were happy together.

Once upon a time, in a small house with a porch, there lived a calm cat named Daisy. Daisy loved to sit on the porch and watch the birds fly by. One sunny day, Daisy saw a big, red ball bouncing towards her. Daisy did not dare to touch the ball at first. She just watched it bounce and roll. Then, she thought it would be fun to play with the ball. So, Daisy jumped off the porch and pushed the ball with her paw. The ball rolled faster and faster, and Daisy chased it. As Daisy played with the ball, it suddenly popped! Daisy was surprised, but she saw that there was a new toy inside the ball. It was a soft, fluffy bird toy just for her. Daisy took the toy back to the porch and played with it all day long. The ball popping was an unexpected twist, but it made Daisy very happy.

One day, a brave boy named Tim went to meet his new tutor. His tutor was a kind lady named Miss Jane. They sat down to learn and have fun. Tim was happy to meet her and said, "Hello, Miss Jane! I am ready to learn!" While they were learning, Tim saw a big, red ball outside. He asked Miss Jane if they could play with the ball. Miss Jane said, "Yes, but first let's finish our lesson. Then we can have fun with the ball." Tim was excited and worked hard to finish the lesson. When they finished, they went outside to play with the big, red ball. But the ball was not there! Tim was sad. Suddenly, a little dog came running with the ball in his mouth. Tim and Miss Jane laughed. They did not expect to meet a new friend. They played with the dog and the ball all day. Tim was happy he met Miss Jane and the little dog.
