Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Jane

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. The sky was lovely and blue. Tim saw his friend, Jane. They both liked to play with their toy cars. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. It was thunder! Tim and Jane were scared. They ran to Tim's house. Inside, they felt safe. "Sorry I took your car," Jane said to Tim. "Can you forgive me?" "Of course," Tim said. "Friends forgive each other." The thunder stopped, and they went back outside to play. They were happy to be friends and share their toy cars.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom liked to climb trees and play with his friends. One day, Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He thought it would be fun to see how high he could go. As Tom climbed the tree, he saw something shiny. It was a firework! Tom was excited because he loved fireworks. They were bright and made loud noises. He wanted to show his friends the firework, so he carefully picked it up with his mouth. Tom climbed down the tree with the firework and showed it to his friends. They were all very happy and excited. They decided to save the firework for a special day when they could all watch it together. Tom felt proud that he found the firework and shared it with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy body named Bob. Bob loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, he saw a new friend named Tim. Bob wanted to introduce himself to Tim. Bob walked up to Tim and said, "Hi, I am Bob. Let's play together!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They played all day and had lots of fun. Bob was successful in making a new friend. Bob and Tim played every day. They were always happy and laughing. All the other kids wanted to be their friends too. Bob and Tim showed everyone how to be good friends.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to slide, swing and run. They saw a big tent. It was red and blue and had stars on it. "Let's enter the tent!" Lily said. "Maybe there is something fun inside." "OK!" Ben said. They ran to the tent and lifted the flap. They entered the tent. Inside the tent, they saw a man. He had a long neck and a funny hat. He was making balloons. He saw Lily and Ben and smiled. "Hello, little ones!" he said. "Do you want a balloon?" "Yes, please!" Lily and Ben said. The man took a balloon and blew it up. He twisted it and made it into a dog. He gave it to Lily. "Wow, thank you!" Lily said. She hugged the balloon dog. The man took another balloon and blew it up. He twisted it and made it into a cat. He gave it to Ben. "Wow, thank you!" Ben said. He hugged the balloon cat. The man took another balloon and blew it up. He twisted it and made it into a giraffe. He gave it to himself. "Wow, look at me!" he said. He put the balloon giraffe around his neck. He looked very funny. Lily and Ben laughed. They liked the man and his balloons. They were happy. The man was happy too. He liked to make children laugh. He was persistent. He made more balloons and gave them to Lily and Ben. They entered the tent and found a friend.

Lily was eager to help her mom make cookies. She loved cookies and wanted to learn how to make them. She put on her apron and climbed on a chair to reach the counter. Mom showed Lily how to mix the flour, sugar, butter and eggs in a big bowl. Lily stirred with a wooden spoon and smiled. She liked to make the dough. Mom said they had to roll the dough and cut out shapes with cookie cutters. Lily picked a star, a heart and a flower. Mom put the cookie shapes on a baking sheet and said they had to go in the oven. Lily wanted to see the oven, but Mom said no. She said the oven was very hot and could hurt Lily. She said Lily had to stay away from the stove and the oven. Lily nodded, but she was curious. When Mom went to get the milk from the fridge, Lily saw the oven door was open a little. She thought she could peek inside and see the cookies. She reached for the oven handle, but it was very hot. She burned her hand and cried out. Mom heard her and ran to her. She saw Lily's hand was red and sore. She hugged Lily and said she was sorry. She said she would put some ice on her hand and make it feel better. Lily cried and said she hated the stove and the oven. She said they were mean and bad. Mom said they were not mean and bad, but they were dangerous. She said Lily had to be careful and listen to Mom when she said no. She said she loved Lily and wanted her to be safe. She said they could still have cookies when they were done. Lily said she loved Mom and wanted to have cookies. She said she would not touch the stove and the oven again. Mom kissed her and said she was a good girl.
