Category: Intermediate
Title: The Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a frog who lived in a pond. He was very attractive and had sparkly eyes. One day, he wanted to meet new friends. So he hopped out of his pond and travelled through the forest. Suddenly, he stopped in front of an old oak tree. He saw a little caterpillar perched on a branch. The frog hopped close and said, "Hello there! I'm looking for friends. Will you be my friend?" The caterpillar smiled and spoke. "Why, yes! I'd love to be your friend. My name is Parsley!" The frog was so happy to have a new friend. He jumped for joy and said, "Thank you! I'm so glad to meet you". Together, they played games and had lots of fun. The caterpillar was so kind and had a spark of joy that made the frog even more attracted to him. They became close friends and enjoyed each other's company. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little zebra named Ziggy. Ziggy lived in a big forest with his friends. He loved to run, jump, and play all day long. But sometimes, Ziggy would behave rough with his friends. His friends would get sad and tell him to be nice. One day, Ziggy's mom told him to behave when they went to a big party. All the animals in the forest were going to be there. Ziggy promised his mom he would be good. He was very excited for the party. At the party, Ziggy saw his friends playing a fun game. He wanted to play too, but he remembered what his mom said. Ziggy tried his best to behave and play nice. His friends were happy, and they all had a great time. In the end, Ziggy learned that being nice and behaving was much better than being rough.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy arrow. He would run and jump with it all day long. Tim was very fit because he played a lot. One day, Tim found a big tree with a hole in it. He wanted to find what was inside the hole. Tim put his toy arrow into the hole and started to shake it. He hoped that something fun would come out. Suddenly, a cute squirrel came out of the hole. The squirrel looked at Tim and smiled. Tim was so happy to meet a new friend. From that day on, Tim and the squirrel played together in the forest. They had lots of fun and were always happy.

One day, a gray cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He jumped up and down, but he could not reach the tree. Tom saw a little girl named Sue. She was playing with her toy. Tom said, "Sue, can you help me climb the tree?" Sue said, "Yes, Tom. I will add some boxes for you to jump on." So, Sue put three boxes under the tree. Tom jumped on the boxes and then jumped on the tree. Tom was happy in the tree. But soon, he saw a big bird. The bird said a strange word. The word made the tree shake. Tom was scared and fell off the tree. Sue caught him and said, "Tom, be careful. Don't climb too high." Tom learned that it is good to have friends who help you, but you should always be careful.

Anna likes to help her mom in the kitchen. She likes to measure things with the cups and spoons. Mom says measuring is important for cooking. Today, they are making soup with beans and carrots and noodles. Anna measures the water and pours it in the big pot. Mom measures the beans and puts them in the water. Anna measures the carrots and cuts them with the plastic knife. Mom measures the noodles and breaks them in half. They wait for the soup to boil. Anna smells the yummy soup. She sees the steam coming out of the pot. Mom says the steam is hot and she should be careful. Anna nods and stays away from the pot. She looks around the kitchen. She sees the fridge, the stove, the microwave, the toaster, and the blender. She asks mom what they are. Mom says they are modern things that help them cook and keep food fresh. Anna says she likes modern things. She thinks they are cool and fun. The soup is ready. Mom turns off the stove and ladles the soup into bowls. She gives one to Anna and one to herself. They sit at the table and eat the soup. Anna says the soup is delicious. She likes the beans and the carrots and the noodles. She says she is a good helper. Mom says she is a great helper and she is proud of her. They hug and smile. They finish the soup and wash the dishes. They measure how much they love each other. They say it is a lot. They are happy.
