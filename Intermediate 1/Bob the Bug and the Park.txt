Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Bug and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a funny bug named Bob. Bob was a little bug who loved to play with his friends. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Bob was always happy and made everyone around him happy too. One day, Bob and his friends were playing in the park. They were having so much fun that they didn't notice the time. It was getting late and they forgot to go home. Bob's mom was very worried and went to look for him. She found Bob and his friends still playing in the park. Bob's mom told him that he should not forget to go home when it gets dark. She said it is important to remember to go home because it is not safe to play outside when it is dark. Bob and his friends learned a lesson that day. They promised to always remember to go home before it gets dark. From that day on, they played happily and safely, and never forgot to go home on time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia liked to play outside on the earth. One sunny day, Mia went outside to play. She saw a little bird sitting on a seat. Mia was very happy to see the bird. "Hi, little bird!" said Mia. "Do you like to sit on the seat?" The little bird looked at Mia and said, "Yes, I like to sit here. It is a nice seat." Mia and the little bird became friends. They played together every day. They liked to sit on the seat and look at the earth. They had lots of fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a fancy toy train. The train had a long track. Tim loved to play with his train every day. He would keep the train on the track and watch it go round and round. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the train and said, "Wow, what a fancy train! Can I play with it too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, but we must keep the train on the track." Tim and Sam played with the train all day. They made sure to keep the train on the track so it would not fall off. They had lots of fun watching the train go round and round on the track. As the sun went down, it was time for Sam to go home. Tim said, "I had fun playing with you and my train today. Let's play again soon!" Sam agreed and said, "Yes, I had fun too! I will come back soon to play with your fancy train." And so, Tim and Sam promised to play with the fancy train again. They knew that as long as they kept the train on the track, they would have lots of fun together.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. He was only three and was scared of monsters. He heard stories of monsters from his parents, but he never really believed in them until one day he had an adventure of his own. One day Jack was playing in the park and he heard a noise. It sounded like a monster! Jack didn't know what to do. He wanted to run away, but he managed to keep brave. He slowly approached the noise, and he saw what it was. It was a monster, but it wasn't scary at all! It was just a little puppy. Jack was so relieved. The puppy was timid and had a harsh bark, but Jack knew it was friendly so he picked up the puppy and hugged it. From that day forward, Jack managed to keep brave whenever he heard scary stories about monsters. He knew that most monsters aren't really so bad and he was sure he could handle anything.

One day, a furry cat named Kitty went to the kitchen. She saw a faucet. Kitty was very thirsty. She thought, "How can I get water from the faucet?" Kitty jumped and tried to turn the faucet on. "Meow," she said. But the faucet did not turn on. She tried again and again. Suddenly, a small bird flew in through the window. The bird saw Kitty and said, "Do you need help?" Kitty nodded her head. The bird flew to the faucet and turned it on for Kitty. "Thank you, bird!" Kitty said. She drank the water and felt happy. But then, the bird started to grow bigger and bigger. Kitty was surprised. The bird was not a bird at all! It was a magic friend who came to help Kitty. They played together all day and had lots of fun.
