Category: Intermediate
Title: John and Sally and the Gear

Once upon a time, there were two friends, John and Sally. Both of them had special gear that they donned whenever they would go out and play. One day, while they were out playing, they saw something that made them alert. It was a big scary monster! Sally and John were scared and wanted to run away. But then, they remembered their gear and how brave they could be. John and Sally were very brave, and they put on their gear. With their gear, they were ready to fight the monster. John and Sally worked together to fight the monster and eventually, they were able to scare it away. John and Sally were very happy and proud of themselves. They had fought off a scary monster, and they were victorious. From then on, they always wore their gear whenever they went out to play, just in case another monster came around.

One sunny day, a lazy cat named Tom saw a broken toy. He wanted to fix it. He asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him. "Max, can you help me fix this toy?" Tom asked. Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, Tom! I will help you." They both worked together to fix the toy. They moved the toy parts and put them back together. The toy was in motion again. After they fixed the toy, Tom and Max played with it. They laughed and had lots of fun. They were happy that they could fix the toy together. And from that day on, Tom was not so lazy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big festival in a small town. The sun was shining, and everyone was happy. Kids were playing, and people were dancing. But, it was cold outside. A little girl named Lily went to the festival with her mom. She saw a man kneel down to tie his shoe. Lily thought it was funny and wanted to try it too. But, her mom said, "No, Lily, you must stay close to me." Lily didn't listen to her mom. She ran away to kneel down like the man. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew Lily's hat away. She felt very cold and started to cry. But, her mom was not there to help her. Since Lily didn't listen to her mom, she got lost and felt cold. She was sad and scared. The festival was not fun for her anymore. The moral of the story is to always listen to your parents. They know what's best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Max. Max could lead people to find things. One day, Max met a man named Tom. Tom lost his wine, and he was very sad. Max said, "I can help you find your wine!" Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max sniffed and sniffed, then he led Tom to a big tree. Under the tree, Tom found his wine. Tom was very happy and said, "You are a good dog, Max!" From that day on, Max and Tom were best friends. Max helped Tom find things, and Tom gave Max treats. They learned that helping others is a good thing to do.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play with blocks and cars. One day, Tom has a new car. It is big and red and shiny. Lily wants to play with it. "Can I have your car, Tom?" Lily asks. "No, this is my car. You can play with the other cars," Tom says. "But I like your car. It is so pretty. Please, Tom, let me have it for a little while," Lily begs. Tom thinks for a moment. He likes his car, but he also likes Lily. He has an idea. "OK, Lily, you can have my car, but only if you give me your will," Tom says. "My will? What is that?" Lily asks. Tom explains. "Your will is a paper that says what you want to happen when you are gone. If you give me your will, I can have all your toys and your candy and your clothes when you are gone." Lily does not understand. She thinks Tom is joking. She laughs. "That is silly, Tom. I am not going anywhere. I am here with you. And you are my friend. You would not take my things," Lily says. Tom nods. He smiles. He is not joking. He is not a reliable friend. He is a greedy friend. He takes Lily's hand and gives her a pen. "Here, Lily, write your name on this paper. Then you can have my car," Tom says. Lily trusts Tom. She does not know what she is doing. She writes her name on the paper. Tom takes the paper and puts it in his pocket. He gives Lily his car. "Thank you, Tom. You are so nice. I love your car," Lily says. Tom does not say anything. He just watches Lily play with his car. He waits for his chance. He waits for Lily to be gone. Then he will have everything. He is happy. Lily is not. Lily is in danger. She does not know. She has made a big mistake. She has given Tom her will.
