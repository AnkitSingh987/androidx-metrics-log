Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to run and play outside with his friends. One day, it was very hot outside, but Tim still wanted to play. Tim saw a pretty ornament hanging on a tree. He wanted to show it to his friends. So, he picked it up and started to run towards his friends. As he ran, he felt the hot sun on his face. When Tim got to his friends, they all looked at the ornament. They thought it was very pretty too. They played with the ornament and had lots of fun. Then, they went inside to cool down from the hot sun.

One day, Jack was walking to the park with his mommy. He was so excited to go to the park and play with all his toys! But when Jack walked inside the park, he noticed something. He was the only kid there. "Mommy," said Jack. "Where are all the children?" Mommy sighed. "They aren't here," she said. "They are all on the stage." Jack was worried. He didn't know why all the children were on the stage. "Mommy, why are they up there?" he asked. Mommy smiled. "They're having a play," she said. "That big fat man up on the stage is the director and the children are his stars!" Jack was relieved. He was happy to know that there was a reason why all the children were on the stage. Now, he just couldn't wait until it was his turn to be up there.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to make things with her hands. One day, she found some harmless thread in her mom's sewing box. Sue started to make a pretty necklace with the thread. She put on beads, buttons, and a little bell. Sue was very happy with her necklace. She wore it all day and showed it to her friends. At the end of the day, Sue put her necklace away. She knew she could make more fun things with the harmless thread. Sue couldn't wait to see what she would make next.

Once upon a time, there was a mop named Molly. Molly was a very persistent mop. She loved to help clean up messes. One day, Molly saw a big spill on the floor. She said, "I can help!" So, Molly went to the spill and started to mop it up. She worked very hard and did not stop until the floor was clean. Later, Molly saw a man deliver a big box to the house. She wanted to help, so she asked the man, "Can I help you?" The man said, "Yes, please!" Molly held the door open while the man brought the big box inside. Molly was very happy that she could help. She knew that being persistent was a good thing. And from that day on, Molly continued to help clean up messes and make her friends happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big box of makeup. Lily loved to play with the makeup. She liked to put it on her face and feel pretty. One day, Lily saw her mom spray a bottle. She asked, "Mom, what is that?" Her mom said, "This is a spray for my face. It makes me feel nice." Lily wanted to try the spray too. She felt envious of her mom. Lily went to her room and found her makeup. She put some on her face. Then, she found a bottle to make her own spray. She filled it with water and sprayed it on her face. It made her feel very nice, just like her mom. Lily's friend, Bella, came over to play. Bella saw Lily's makeup and spray. Bella said, "Wow, Lily, you look so pretty!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you! Do you want to try my makeup and spray too?" Bella nodded, and they both played with the makeup and spray. At the end of the day, Lily and Bella felt very happy. They loved playing with makeup and spray. They felt beautiful, just like Lily's mom.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to fly high in the sky. Tim was not ready to fly yet, but he really wanted to try. His mom told him, "You need to wait, Tim. You will fly soon." Tim saw a television through a window. On the television, he saw big birds flying. Tim thought, "If they can fly, I can fly too!" So, he tried to fly, but he could not go high. He was sad and went back to his mom. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. You will be ready soon." The next day, Tim tried to fly again. This time, he flew high in the sky! He was so happy. His mom was right. Tim was ready to fly now, and he could be like the birds on the television.
