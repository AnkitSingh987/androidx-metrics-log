Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He was worried because it was very hot. He wanted to find some shade to stay cool. So, he walked to a big tree that had a lot of shade. Under the tree, he saw a big box. Tim was very excited and wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the box and found many toys. He played with the toys for a long time. Then, he saw a big truck toy that he wanted to keep. He tried to make the truck go, but it would not yield. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a little girl named Jane came to play under the tree too. Jane saw Tim and the big truck. She knew how to make the truck yield. Jane showed Tim how to do it, and the truck started to go. Tim was very happy, and they played together all day under the shade of the big tree. The plot twist was that Tim made a new friend, and they shared the toys in the box.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a purple spray bottle. She wanted to make a pretty image on the wall. So, she filled the bottle with water and some purple paint. Lucy went to the wall and started to spray. The purple water made a beautiful image of a big flower. Lucy was happy and smiled. She showed the flower to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I made a purple flower!" Lucy said. Tom looked at the flower and smiled too. "Wow, Lucy! It's so pretty!" he said. They both laughed and played near the purple flower all day.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there was a big oak tree. The oak tree was very old and wise. Many animals lived near the oak tree because it was a nice place to be. One day, a squirrel and a bird were talking under the oak tree. The squirrel said, "I love to spread acorns all around the forest. It is so much fun!" The bird replied, "I like to spread my wings and fly high in the sky. It is so beautiful up there." The squirrel and the bird became good friends. They played near the oak tree every day. They loved the peaceful forest and the big oak tree. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful farm. On this farm, there was a happy pig named Pinky. Pinky loved to play with his friends, the cow, the sheep, and the chicken. One day, Pinky and his friends were playing in the mud. The sheep said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Pinky was excited and said, "Yes, let's play!" They all started to play, but the chicken didn't want to join. The chicken was sad and felt ignored. Pinky saw the sad chicken and asked, "Why are you sad, chicken?" The chicken said, "I feel ignored because no one asked me to play." Pinky felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, chicken. We didn't mean to ignore you. Come play with us!" The chicken felt happy and joined the game. From that day on, Pinky and his friends made sure to always include everyone in their games. They played happily together on the peaceful farm, and no one felt ignored again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with healthy green leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but he had a question. He asked his mom, "Can I climb the tree?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim started to climb the tree. He felt happy and excited. As he climbed higher, he saw a little bird in a nest. The bird looked at him and said, "Hello, do you understand me?" Tim was surprised! He did not know that birds could talk. He said, "Yes, I understand you. My name is Tim." The bird said, "Nice to meet you, Tim. My name is Birdy. I have a question too. Can you help me find my friend? He is lost." Tim said, "Of course, I will help you!" They looked everywhere in the park. Finally, they found Birdy's friend hiding in a bush. Birdy was very happy and thanked Tim. Tim felt good that he could help his new friend. Then, he climbed down the tree and went home with his mom.
