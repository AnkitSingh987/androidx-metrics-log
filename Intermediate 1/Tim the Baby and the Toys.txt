Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Baby and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little baby named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mommy and daddy. He loved to play with his toys and sleep in his crib. One day, Tim saw a tiny bug crawling on the floor. He watched it as it moved closer to his crib. Tim wanted to become friends with the bug, so he reached out his little hand and gently touched it. The bug tickled Tim's fingers, making him giggle. The bug became Tim's new friend. They played together every day. Tim's mommy and daddy were happy that their chubby baby had found a friend in the tiny bug. And so, Tim, the bug, and the crib lived happily ever after.

Tom and Sue were friends. They liked to play with snow. They made a big snowman in the yard. They gave him a hat and a scarf and a carrot nose. They named him Bob. One day, they went to class. They learned about the sun and the seasons. The teacher said that the sun was very hot and made things warm. She said that when it was winter, the sun was far away and it was cold. But when it was spring, the sun came closer and it was warm. She said that snow and ice would melt when the sun was warm. Tom and Sue thought about Bob. They wondered if he would melt when the sun was warm. They felt sad. They liked Bob. They did not want him to go away. After class, they ran to the yard. They wanted to see Bob. But when they got there, they saw a big puddle. Bob was gone. His hat and scarf and carrot nose were on the ground. The sun was shining and it was warm. Tom and Sue cried. They missed Bob. They hugged each other. They decided to make a new friend. They picked up some rocks and sticks and leaves. They made a heavy rockman. They gave him a hat and a scarf and a carrot nose. They named him Rob. They smiled. They liked Rob. They knew he would not melt when the sun was warm. They played with him until it was time to go home. They said goodbye to Rob. They said they would come back tomorrow. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his bone. He would run and jump with his bone all day long. One day, Spot found a very special bone. This bone could glow! Spot was confused. He had never seen a bone like this before. He decided to show his friends. His friends were amazed by the glowing bone, and they all wanted to play with it. As they played, Spot realized that sharing his glowing bone made everyone happy. He learned that sharing with his friends was more fun than keeping the bone all to himself. And so, Spot and his friends played with the glowing bone together, and they all had a great time. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to walk to the park. He put on his helmet to ride his bike. Tim's helmet was big and strong. It was a reliable helmet that kept him safe. On his way to the park, Tim met his friend, Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tim! I like your helmet!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue! It keeps me safe when I ride my bike." Tim and Sue walked and played at the park all day. They had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim put on his reliable helmet and rode his bike back. He was happy he had a good helmet and a fun day with his friend Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy judge named Sam. Sam had been working all day, trying to decide cases and make the right decisions for everyone. But as Sam was trying his best, someone began to spoil his work. This someone was a sneaky little girl. She was only three-years-old, but it was clear she was making a problem for the judge. Every time he was trying to make a decision, she would make silly noises and try to make him laugh. But Sam was worried, because he had important decisions to make. So he tried his best to stay focused and stay serious, but something was always spoiling his work. One day, Sam decided he had enough of the little girl spoiling things for him. He told her she had to leave, but the little girl didn't want to go. She wanted to stay and help him with his work. So finally, Sam said to the little girl, "If you can stay serious and help me make decisions, then you can stay." The little girl was excited and did her best to help the judge. From that day on, she was a big help to Sam, and he taught her lots of things about being a good judge. The End.
