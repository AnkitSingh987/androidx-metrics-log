Category: Intermediate
Title: The Mountain and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. On top of the mountain, there was a nice frog. The frog was very sad. He wanted a friend to play with, but no one was available. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the mountain. She saw the sad frog and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Frog?" The frog said, "I am sad because I have no friends to play with." Lily felt bad for the frog. She wanted to make him happy. So, she gave the frog a big kiss. Suddenly, the frog turned into a happy boy. They played together all day on the mountain. From that day on, they were best friends.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. It was very cold, so his mom told him, "Tim, put on your scarf to stay warm." Tim did not want to wear his scarf because it was wet. He wanted to wear a dry scarf. But his mom said, "You must wear a scarf to go outside." Tim went to his room and found a dry scarf. He put it on and went outside to play. He saw his friend, Sam, who was also wearing a scarf. They played in the snow and had lots of fun. But soon, Tim's scarf fell off and got lost in the snow. Tim was sad and cold without his scarf. Sam said, "Let's find your scarf, Tim!" They looked and looked, but they could not find the scarf. Then, they saw a big snowman wearing Tim's scarf! Tim and Sam laughed and took the scarf from the snowman. Tim put the scarf back on, and they continued to play, happy and warm.

Once there was a little boy who loved to play with a brown motorcycle. He would take it outside every day and repeat the same thing over and over. He liked to pretend the motorcycle was real and would make engine noises as he rode it around. He would pedal it as fast as he could to his best friend’s house. On the way he would beep the horn and wave to everyone he passed. After playing for a while, it was time for him to go home. He would carefully pack up his motorcycle and repeat the same journey back to his house. He would give the motorcycle a goodbye hug and then take it inside to put it in its special spot. It was the best afternoon he could have ever hoped for.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sarah who loved to carry things. She had a basket and every day she would fill it with lovely things. Today, she went very fast. She was so excited that she giggled and laughed as she carried her basket of treats. As she went, she could feel the wind on her skin, and it made her feel happy. Sarah stopped at a bakery and asked for the most delicious cake. The baker gave her a special cake that made her mouth water. Sarah put the cake in her basket and carried it home. When she got there, she carefully lifted the cake out of the basket. It smelled so good! She took a big bite and it was so sweet and juicy. Sarah smiled, as she chewed and enjoyed every delicious morsel.

One day, Tom and his friend, Sam, were playing in the park. They found a big box with a screen on it. The screen had a picture of a cat. Tom said, "Look, Sam! A cat!" But Sam knew it was a fake cat because it was not moving. Tom and Sam wanted to play with the fake cat on the screen. They tried to fold the box to make the cat come out. They pushed and pulled, but the cat stayed on the screen. Sam said, "Tom, we can't get the cat out. It's just a picture." Tom was sad, but then he had an idea. "Let's draw our own cat!" So they found some paper and crayons. Tom folded the paper in half, and they both drew a cat on it. They laughed and played with their new paper cat. And even though it was not real, they had lots of fun.
