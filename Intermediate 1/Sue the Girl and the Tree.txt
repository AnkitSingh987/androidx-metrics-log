Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Tree

One day, a little girl named Sue found a soft, small pebble. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a soft pebble!" she said. Tom looked at the pebble and touched it. "Wow, it is soft!" he said. "Let's go show it to our other friends." Sue and Tom walked to the park, where their friends were playing. They showed the soft pebble to their friends. Everyone wanted to touch it. Then, it started to rain. The kids ran under a big tree to stay dry. Sue held the pebble tight in her hand. When the rain stopped, they all went back to play. At the end of the day, Sue took the soft pebble home. She put it on her shelf to remember the fun day she had with her friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went out to play. He wanted to discover new things. As he walked, he heard a noise. It was a bird singing a pretty song. Tim looked up and saw the bird sitting on a tree branch. He felt envious because he wanted to sing like the bird. "Hi, bird!" said Tim. "I like your song. Can you teach me to sing like you?" The bird looked at Tim and then sang its song again. Tim listened and tried to sing the same song. It was not perfect, but it was a start. Every day, Tim went to see the bird and learn more songs. They became good friends. Tim was happy to discover new things and make a new friend. The bird was happy too, because it had someone to sing with. And Tim was not envious anymore, because he could sing with the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom and dad at their restaurant. They made yummy food for people to eat. Lily liked to make the plates shine. She would wash and dry them until they were perfect. One sunny day, a nice lady came to the restaurant. She was very hungry. Lily's mom and dad made her a big plate of food. Lily wanted to help, so she brought the lady the shiny, perfect plate. The lady smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily! This plate is so shiny and perfect!" Lily felt so happy. She knew that helping her mom and dad at the restaurant was a good thing to do. From that day on, Lily always made sure that the plates were shiny and perfect for everyone who came to eat. And everyone loved to see Lily's happy smile as she helped at the restaurant.

Once upon a time, there was a man who had a fit. His fit filled him to the brim with fire. He wanted to show off his fire, so he ran outside and shouted. Everyone was scared and ran away. The man felt so big and strong! He filled up with more fire, and started to run around the village. The people were all very scared and barricaded their houses. The man felt invincible, but the fire inside him was much bigger than him. Suddenly, he couldn't hold it in anymore, and he exploded. Everything around him filled with fire - even the people's houses! The man was gone, and the fire kept burning until nothing was left.

Once upon a time, there was a soft, smooth rock. This rock lay in a big, green park. Many kids came to play near the rock every day. The rock liked to watch them have fun and laugh. One sunny day, a small bug came to the rock. The bug had an itch on its back. The bug asked the rock for help. The rock said, "I can help you with your itch. Just rub your back on me." The bug did what the rock said. The bug rubbed its back on the smooth rock. The itch went away, and the bug was happy. The bug said, "Thank you, rock. You are a good friend." From that day on, the bug and the rock were best friends. They always helped each other when they needed it. They were happy to have a friend in the big, green park.
