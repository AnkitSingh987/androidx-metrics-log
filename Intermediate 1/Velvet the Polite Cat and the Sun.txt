Category: Intermediate
Title: Velvet the Polite Cat and the Sun

One day, a polite cat named Velvet went for a walk. She liked to say "hi" to everyone she met. The sun was very hot that day, but Velvet was happy. She saw her friend, a dog named Fluffy. "Hi, Fluffy!" said Velvet. "Do you want to play with me?" Fluffy looked at the hot sun and said, "Okay, but let's find some shade so we don't melt!" They found a big tree and played under it. They had fun playing with a ball. Suddenly, they saw a big ice cream cone on the ground. Velvet and Fluffy were very happy and decided to eat it. But when they took a bite, they found out it was not ice cream. It was a rock painted to look like ice cream! Velvet and Fluffy's teeth hurt and they were sad. They learned that not everything is what it seems.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing on the swings and slides. Tim saw a key on the ground. He picked it up and showed his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a key!" Tim said. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Let's see if we can find who lost it." They walked around the park, asking people if they lost a key. A man with a sad face said it was his key. He had a bad day because he could not find it. "Here is your key," Tim said, giving the key to the man. The man smiled and thanked Tim. Tim felt happy to help and went back to playing with his mom.

Once upon a time, in a faraway land, there lived two siblings, Harry and Emma. They were very dependable and always looked out for one another. One day, they went on an adventure together to the nearby forest. There were lots of trees and berries scattered around. Emma asked Harry if she could have one, but he said no. Harry wanted to keep them all for himself. Angry, Emma scattered the berries into the air and ran away. The next day, the siblings decided to go back to the forest to apologise. But it was too late. All the berries had rotted away and the siblings had nothing to show for their adventure. The moral of the story is that sometimes, when we act selfishly, we make mistakes that can't be undone. It's important to be dependable and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a small bush. One day, the bush began to shake. It was very difficult for Billy to stay in the bush. He was scared. Billy saw a big cat. The cat wanted to catch him. Billy said, "Please don't catch me, cat! I like my bush." The cat did not listen. It tried to get Billy. A kind dog named Max saw what was happening. Max wanted to save Billy. He barked at the cat. "Go away, cat!" Max said. The cat got scared and ran away. Billy was happy. He said, "Thank you, Max! You saved me and my bush." Max smiled and said, "You're welcome, Billy. I'm here to help." From that day on, Billy and Max were best friends.

Lily was a big girl who liked to do things by herself. She was independent. She could dress herself, brush her teeth, and put away her toys. She did not like to ask for help or share with others. One day, her mom made dinner for the family. She put some chicken, rice, and carrots on Lily's plate. Lily did not like carrots. She stared at them and made a face. "Lily, eat your carrots. They are good for you," her mom said. "No, I don't want to. I don't like them," Lily said. "Lily, don't be rude. You have to try them. They are sweet and crunchy," her mom said. "No, I don't have to. I am independent. I can choose what I eat," Lily said. Lily pushed her plate away and crossed her arms. Her mom sighed and looked at her dad. Her dad smiled and picked up a carrot from his plate. He held it in front of Lily's nose. "Lily, look at this carrot. It is orange and bright. It is like a little sun. Do you like the sun?" he asked. Lily looked at the carrot and nodded. She liked the sun. It was warm and shiny. "Do you want to taste the sun?" he asked. Lily was curious. She opened her mouth and took a bite of the carrot. She chewed it and smiled. It was sweet and crunchy. She liked it. "See, carrots are not bad. They are good. You can eat more if you want," he said. Lily nodded and reached for her plate. She ate her carrots and her chicken and her rice. She was happy and full. "Good job, Lily. You are independent and brave. You tried something new and you liked it," her mom said. "Thank you, mom. Thank you, dad. I love you," Lily said. They hugged and kissed and finished their dinner. They were a happy family.
