Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sit in the sun all day. One day, while Tom was sitting, a little bird came and sat next to him. "Hi, cat!" said the little bird. "Why do you sit here all day?" Tom looked at the little bird and said, "I like to sit in the sun. It makes me feel warm and happy." The little bird liked the idea, so he sat with Tom in the sun. They sat together every day, and they became very good friends. They laughed, talked, and enjoyed the warm sun together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to draw with her crayons. She had a big box full of many colors. Sue would pick her favorite crayon and draw pretty pictures every day. One day, Sue found a magic crayon. It was very special and rich in color. When she picked it up, it sparkled like the stars. Sue started to draw with the magic crayon. As she drew, something amazing happened. Her pictures came to life! Sue drew a big sun, and it became warm and sunny outside. She drew tasty ice cream, and it appeared in her hand. Sue was so happy with her magic crayon. She used it to make her world full of fun and joy. And she shared her magic with her friends, making them happy too.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the woods. They liked to find new things and have fun. One day, they saw a big hole in the hill. It was a cave. They wanted to see what was inside. They went into the cave. It was dark and cold. They used a flashlight to look around. They saw rocks and bats and spiders. They heard water and wind. They were not scared. They were brave. They walked deeper into the cave. They saw two paths. One path went left and one path went right. They had to choose. Tom wanted to go left. He said it looked more interesting. Lily wanted to go right. She said it looked safer. They argued for a while. They did not agree. They did not listen to each other. They were deaf to each other's words. They got angry and sad. They decided to go their own way. They split up. Tom went left and Lily went right. They walked alone in the dark. They did not see or hear each other. They did not have fun. They missed each other. They wished they had stayed together. They learned a lesson. They should have been kind and respectful. They should have worked together and compromised. They should have chosen to be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Sam. Sam liked to sing. One day, Sam wanted to sing at the opera. He put on a fancy hat and went to the opera house. At the opera house, Sam met a big dog named Max. Max was the opera star. Sam said, "Max, can I replace you and sing at the opera tonight?" Max laughed and said, "Okay, Sam. You can try." Sam got on stage and sang a beautiful song. Everyone clapped and cheered. Max was happy to let Sam replace him for one night. Sam and Max became best friends and sang together at the opera house every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big jet in the sky. He wanted to whistle like the jet. He tried and tried, but he could not do it. Tim felt sad. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to whistle like the jet, but I can't." His mom smiled and said, "Keep trying, and you will learn." Tim was persistent. He tried again and again. One day, he could whistle just like the jet. Tim felt happy and proud. He showed his mom, and she said, "See? You did it because you did not give up." The moral of the story is to never give up and keep trying.
