Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Name

One day, a little girl named Amy found a pretty dress to wear. The dress had a label on it. The label had a name, but it was unknown to her. Amy did not know who the dress belonged to. She wanted to find out. Amy asked her friends if they knew the name on the label. They all said no. They did not know the name either. Amy felt sad but she did not give up. She knew there must be someone who knew the name. Finally, Amy asked her mom. Her mom knew the name! It was the name of a girl who lived far away. Amy's mom said they could send the dress to the girl. Amy was happy to help. She knew the girl would love to wear the pretty dress.

Sara loves jazz. She likes to listen to the music with her dad. He plays the saxophone and teaches her some notes. Sara wants to play jazz too, but she does not have a saxophone. She has a toy flute that she got for her birthday. One day, Sara sees a poster for a jazz show at the park. She asks her dad if they can go. He says yes, and they pack a picnic basket. Sara brings her toy flute along. She hopes to see some saxophones at the show. At the park, they find a nice spot on the grass. The show starts and Sara is amazed. She sees many instruments and hears many sounds. She sees saxophones, trumpets, pianos, drums, and guitars. She hears fast and slow songs, loud and soft noises, happy and sad tunes. She thinks jazz is very interesting. She also sees many people clapping and cheering for the musicians. She wants to join them. She takes out her toy flute and tries to play along. She does not know the songs, but she makes up her own notes. She has fun and feels proud. Her dad sees her and smiles. He says, "You are doing great, Sara. You have a good ear for jazz. I am proud of you. You are my little jazz star." He hugs her and gives her a kiss. He says, "Thank you for supporting me and my music. I love you." Sara hugs him back and says, "I love you too, dad. And I love jazz." They enjoy the rest of the show together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play near the stream. One day, he saw something shiny hanging on a tree. He thought, "I am so lucky!" Tim called his friend, Sue. "Sue, come see what I found!" Sue came running and saw the shiny thing too. They both wanted to get it. Tim tried to reach up, but he was too small. Sue had an idea. "Let's hang on the tree and grab it together!" They both climbed the tree and tried to hang on a branch. But, the branch was weak. It broke, and they fell into the stream. They got all wet and muddy. They did not feel lucky anymore. They went home, sad and dirty.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She had a present. The present was wraped in wrap. She was excited. But the wrap was dry. The girl wanted to open the present. But she could not get the wrap off. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said she had to wait. She said the girl had to be patient. The girl was not sure what that was. But then her mom told her, if she was patient, she would receive the present soon. The girl was happy. She waited patiently until she received the present. Finally, it was time! She ripped the wrap off, and the present was hers.

One day, a big, powerful wind came to the forest. All the little animals were scared. They needed to find a safe place to hide from the wind. They needed a shelter. A kind, old tree saw the little animals. The tree said, "Don't be scared, little ones. I can show you a safe place." The tree opened its branches wide and let the little animals come inside. The tree's branches were like a big, warm hug. Inside the tree, the little animals were safe from the powerful wind. They thanked the tree for its help. The tree was happy to help them. From that day on, the little animals knew that they had a friend in the kind, old tree.
