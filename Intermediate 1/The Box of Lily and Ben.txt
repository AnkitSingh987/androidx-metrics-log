Category: Intermediate
Title: The Box of Lily and Ben

Lily and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. One day, they saw a big crane near the park. The crane was very loud. It made a noise like "boom, boom, boom". "What is the crane doing?" Lily asked Ben. Ben shrugged. He did not know. He wanted to go closer and see. He took Lily's hand and they walked to the crane. The crane was lifting a big box. The box was brown and had holes. It smelled bad. Lily and Ben wrinkled their noses. "Yuck, what is in the box?" Lily asked. Ben peeked through a hole. He saw something green and fuzzy. It looked like a big ball. "I think it is a melon," Ben said. "But it is very old. It is rotting." Lily made a face. She did not like rotting things. They were gross and stinky. "Let's go back to the park," she said. "I don't like the crane or the box." Ben nodded. He agreed. They ran back to the park. They played with their friends and forgot about the crane and the box. They had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow cat named Sunny. Sunny loved to play with a long cord. One day, Sunny saw a big box in the room. Sunny was curious and wanted to see what was inside the box. Sunny tried to ignore the cord, but it was hard. The cord was so fun to play with. Sunny played with the cord near the box. Suddenly, the box started to move! Sunny was scared and jumped back. Then, the box opened and out came a cute puppy! The puppy was friendly and wanted to play with Sunny. Sunny was happy and they played together. From that day on, Sunny and the puppy shared the cord and played all day long.

Sara and Ben went to a pizza place with their mom and dad. They were very hungry and happy. They looked at the menu and saw many yummy things. Sara wanted cheese pizza and Ben wanted pepperoni pizza. Mom said, "You can choose one pizza to share, or you can have half and half." Sara and Ben looked at each other. Sara said, "I want cheese pizza. I don't like pepperoni." Ben said, "I want pepperoni pizza. I don't like cheese." They started to argue and whine. Mom and dad were not happy. They said, "Stop fighting. You can have both kinds of pizza if you are nice to each other." Sara and Ben did not listen. They kept saying, "I want, I want, I want." Then they saw another family at the next table. They had a big pizza with cheese and pepperoni and other things. They also had salad and breadsticks and juice. They looked very happy and they shared everything. They said, "Thank you" and "Please" and "You're welcome". They also clapped for the pizza man when he brought their food. Sara and Ben felt jealous. They wished they had a big pizza like that. They wished they were happy and nice like that. They looked at their menu and felt sad. Mom and dad saw them and said, "Do you see how they are? They are polite and kind and they enjoy their food. They are not greedy and selfish like you. If you want to have a good time, you have to learn to share and be grateful." Sara and Ben felt sorry. They said, "We're sorry, mom and dad. We were bad. We want to be good. Can we have half and half pizza and share it?" Mom and dad smiled and said, "Yes, you can. That's a good idea. We're proud of you." They ordered the pizza and waited. They said, "Thank you" and "Please" and "You're welcome". They also clapped for the pizza man when he brought their food. They ate and drank and talked and laughed. They had a good time. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring and gratitude is happiness.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot liked to play at the park with his friends. His favorite thing to play on was the slide. He would run up the steps and slide down fast, wagging his tail. One sunny day, Spot found a bone near the slide. He was very happy. Spot wanted to keep the bone safe, so he decided to bury it. He dug a hole with his paws and put the bone in it. Then, he covered it with dirt. Later, Spot's friend, a little girl named Lucy, came to play. She saw Spot near the slide and asked, "Spot, what are you doing?" Spot barked and wagged his tail. He showed Lucy the spot where he buried the bone. Lucy smiled and said, "Good job, Spot! You are so helpful." They played on the slide together and had lots of fun.
