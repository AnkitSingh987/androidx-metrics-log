Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Garden

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play in her garden. One day, she saw a small box hidden under a big rock. Mia was excited and wanted to see what was inside. She thought, "Maybe there is a reward in the box!" Mia moved the rock and opened the box. Inside, she found a note that said, "I am spicy and red. Find me, and I will be your reward." Mia thought, "What can be spicy and red?" She looked around the garden and saw a red pepper plant. Mia picked a red pepper and said, "Is this the spicy reward?" She took a tiny bite, and it was very spicy! Mia smiled and said, "I found the spicy reward!" She took the pepper inside to show her mom. Mia's mom was proud of her for finding the hidden reward. She decided to make a special dinner using the spicy red pepper. Mia learned that finding new things can be fun and rewarding.

Once upon a time, there was a kind witch. She was very attractive and had a big wand. She allowed children to come play near her home. Everyone felt safe around her because she believed weapons were not nice. One day, a girl found a big bucket in the witch’s garden. Inside it were lots of shiny pieces of metal. She was so excited and wanted to show them to the witch. But when the witch saw the pieces, she realized they were weapons. She asked the girl to put them back in the bucket and not look at them. The witch asked the children never to bring weapons to her garden. She told the children that everyone should be kind and not use weapons. They agreed and had lots of fun playing in the witch’s garden.

One morning, Tom woke up and did not feel good. He felt very tired, almost like he was dead. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I feel bad." His mom looked at him and said, "Oh no, Tom! Let's make you feel better. Do you want to eat some food?" Tom shook his head. "No, I don't want to eat. I just want to feel better." His mom hugged him and said, "Okay, let's rest today. We can play and have fun when you feel good again." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom." He went back to bed and slept. When he woke up again, he felt much better. He was so happy that he could play and have fun with his friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a slow crab. He lived near the sea. He liked to play in the sand and swim in the water. One day, he saw a big fish. The big fish was fast, and the crab wanted to be fast too. The crab decided to rush like the big fish. He tried to swim fast, but he was too slow. He tried to run fast, but his legs were short. The crab was sad because he could not be fast like the big fish. One day, the crab tried to rush across the road. He wanted to show the big fish that he could be fast. But the crab was too slow. A big car came and the crab could not get out of the way in time. The slow crab was hit by the big car and did not get to play with the big fish ever again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a dirty muffin on the ground. He picked it up and wanted to eat it. But his mommy saw him and said, "No, Tim! That muffin is dirty. You should not eat it." Tim felt sad and said, "But I want the muffin, mommy!" His mommy shook her head and said, "I will make you a new muffin, but you must surrender the dirty one." Tim thought about it and decided to give the dirty muffin to his mommy. He said, "Okay, mommy. I surrender the dirty muffin." His mommy smiled and made him a new, clean muffin. Tim was happy and ate the new muffin with a big smile on his face. From that day on, Tim learned not to eat dirty things from the ground.
