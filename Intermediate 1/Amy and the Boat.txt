Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy and the Boat

One day, a big boat arrived at the beach. A small girl named Amy saw the boat and felt fearful. She did not know who was on the boat. She hid behind a big rock and watched. A man came off the boat. He looked nice and had a big smile. He said, "Hello, is anyone here? I have a surprise!" Amy wanted to see the surprise, but she was still a little scared. She took a deep breath and came out from behind the rock. Amy said, "I am here. What is the surprise?" The man opened a big box from the boat. Inside the box were many colorful balls. Amy's fear went away, and she started to play with the balls. She had a lot of fun with the man and the balls from the boat.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little white rabbit. The rabbit was very happy and liked to play all day. The rabbit didn't know many things, but he always wanted to learn. One day, the white rabbit met a big bear. The bear was very loud and liked to yell. The rabbit was scared at first, but then he thought he could learn something from the bear. So, the rabbit asked the bear, "Why do you yell so much?" The bear looked at the ignorant rabbit and said, "I yell because it makes me feel strong and big." The rabbit thought it was a good idea to try it too. So, he took a deep breath and let out a tiny yell. The bear laughed and said, "You need to yell louder to be strong like me." The rabbit tried and tried, but he couldn't yell as loud as the bear. But that was okay. The rabbit learned that he didn't need to be loud to be strong. He could be strong by being kind and helping others. And that's what the rabbit did. He helped his friends in the forest, and they all loved him.

One day, a little cat named Kitty felt the urge to play. Kitty was very naughty and loved to run with speed. She looked for her friend, Doggy, and said, "Let's race to the big tree!" Doggy agreed and they started to run. Kitty ran very fast, but Doggy was faster. Suddenly, a big wind came and blew a hat in front of them. They both stopped and looked at the hat. "Wow! A magic hat!" said Kitty. They put the hat on and it made them both run even faster. They raced to the big tree and laughed. They were happy they found the magic hat and could play together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl and her Mommy. They went to the zoo and saw the elephants. The little girl thought the elephants were so big and beautiful. She wanted to give one of the elephants a hug. But Mommy said it was too dangerous and the little girl had to stay back. The little girl was so sad and she felt quite guilty. She wanted to give an elephant a hug so badly, but Mommy said no. She sadly walked away with her Mommy. Then something magical happened. As they were walking home, they received a surprise from the elephant. The little girl's eyes sparkled with joy and she received a beautiful white flower from the elephant. She was so happy and asked Mommy if she could give the elephant a un. Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, this time you can give him a hug." The little girl hugged the elephant and thanked him for her surprise. She knew the elephant was saying thank you too. The little girl and Mommy smiled and waved goodbye to the elephant. They both went home happy and full of memories.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big box of toys and they liked to make up stories with them. Lily had a soft bear and Ben had a green dinosaur. "Let's bury our toys and pretend they are fossils," Ben said. He dug a hole in the dirt with his hands and put his dinosaur in it. He covered it with more dirt and patted it down. "Okay, I'll bury my bear too," Lily said. She found a spot next to Ben's hole and started to dig. But she hit something hard and brown. She pulled it out and saw that it was a pine cone. "Look, I found a pine cone!" Lily said. She held it up and smiled. She liked pine cones. They were prickly and fun to touch. "No, that's not a pine cone, that's a bomb!" Ben said. He grabbed the pine cone from Lily and threw it away. "You can't bury that, it will explode!" Lily was angry. She liked the pine cone and she wanted it back. She ran after it and picked it up. "It's not a bomb, it's a pine cone! You're mean, Ben!" Ben ran after Lily and tried to take the pine cone from her. They pulled and tugged and shouted. They forgot about their toys and their game. Their mom heard the noise and came out to see what was wrong. She saw Lily and Ben fighting over the pine cone and their toys buried in the dirt. "What are you doing, kids?" she asked. She looked at them with a gentle face. She was not angry, but she was sad. "We're playing fossils, but Ben threw away my pine cone!" Lily said. "It's not a pine cone, it's a bomb!" Ben said. Mom shook her head. She took the pine cone from them and held it in her hand. She knelt down and hugged them both. "It's a pine cone, and it's a bomb, and it's whatever you want it to be," she said. "But it's not worth fighting over. You have so many toys and so much imagination. Why don't you share and play nicely?" Lily and Ben looked at each other and felt sorry. They said sorry to each other and to mom. They hugged her back and took their toys out of the dirt. They decided to play something else with the pine cone. They were friends again.
