Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Frog and the Hopped

Once there was a blue frog. He liked to hop around really fast. One day, he saw something that caught his eye. It was a yellow lollipop! He hopped over to it and looked around. Nobody was in sight, so he decided to taste it. The frog was so happy when it tasted so sweet! He hopped off to find more colors and tastes. He found all sorts of colors! He found some pink and some red. The frog hopped even faster when he saw the colors. He hopped over to each one to give them all a taste. Some were sweet and some were sour. Finally, the frog was done tasting all the colors of the lollipops and hopped away. He had a wonderful time tasting the colors, and he couldn't wait to go again.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. Spot liked to run, jump, and play with his ball. He was very happy. Suddenly, Spot saw a big cat. The cat looked very serious. Spot wanted to play with the cat, so he ran up to her and said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me and my ball?" The serious cat looked at Spot and said, "Stop! I do not want to play. I am too serious for play." Spot was sad, but he did not give up. He thought if he could make the cat laugh, then maybe she would want to play. Spot started to dance and make funny faces. The serious cat watched him and soon, she started to smile. Then, she laughed and said, "Okay, Spot, let's play together!" They played all day in the park, and the serious cat learned that it was fun to play and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an adorable pet cat named Kitty. One sunny day, Lily's mom asked her to go to the market to buy some apples. Lily was very excited and said, "Yes, Mommy! I will go to the market!" Lily and Kitty walked to the market together. They saw many people and things. Lily saw apples and picked some. She put them in a bag. Then, she saw a toy she wanted. She asked Kitty, "Can I buy this toy?" Kitty looked at Lily and said, "Meow!" That reminded Lily that she needed to save her money for the apples. So, she put the toy back. Lily bought the apples and went back home with Kitty. She gave the apples to her mom. Her mom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Lily! You did a great job at the market today." Lily smiled and hugged her adorable cat, Kitty. They both had a fun day at the market.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box of gray blocks. He was very happy. He started to play with the blocks. He made a tall tower with the blocks. The tower was so tall that it touched the sky. Tim saw a little bird on the top of the tower. The bird said, "Hello, Tim! I like your gray tower. Can you make a house for me with the blocks?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you!" He started to build a small house with the gray blocks for the bird. Tim wanted to make a roof for the bird's house. He found some paper. He tried to fold the paper to make a roof. He folded the paper once, twice, and three times. The roof was perfect. He put the paper roof on top of the gray block house. The bird was very happy with his new house. He said, "Thank you, Tim! I love my new house." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. I'm glad I could help you." They played together with the gray blocks, and they were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a warm, sunny day. A little girl named Mia wanted to nap under a big tree. She loved to nap there because it was so cozy and quiet. Mia also had a small bag of sugar with her. She liked to eat a little sugar when she woke up from her nap. As Mia napped under the tree, a tiny ant found the bag of sugar. The ant wanted to take the sugar back to his friends. The ant tried to carry the bag, but it was too big and heavy for him. So, he called his friends to help him. Together, they all carried the bag of sugar away from Mia. When Mia woke up from her nap, she saw that her bag of sugar was gone. She was sad and looked around for her sugar. Then, she saw the ants carrying her sugar away. Mia decided to follow the ants to see where they were going. The ants led Mia to a little hole in the ground. She looked inside and saw the ants sharing the sugar with their friends. Mia thought it was very nice of the ants to share, so she decided to let them keep the sugar. From that day on, Mia and the ants became the best of friends. They would share sugar and nap together under the big tree.
