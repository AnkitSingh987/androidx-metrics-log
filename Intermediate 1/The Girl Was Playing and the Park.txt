Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Was Playing and the Park

One day, a little girl was playing in the park. She was chasing a butterfly when she saw a flag on the ground. She was so excited. She picked up the flag and twirled it around. Suddenly, she felt a sharp pain. It was coming from the flag! It had bit her! The little girl ran to her mom and cried, "Mommy! The flag bit me!" Her mom replied, "The Flag didn't bite you, dear. That was a bee! That's why it hurt. A bee thinks the flag looks like a flower, so it was just being regular." The little girl hugged her mom tight and they laughed. From then on, she made sure to look out for bees when playing with flags.

Tom and Lisa are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big truck with corn. The truck driver is not there. He is in the shop. "Look, corn!" Tom says. "I love corn. Let's take some." "No, Tom," Lisa says. "That is not our corn. That is bad. We have to resist." "But I am hungry," Tom says. "And the corn looks yummy. No one will know." Lisa shakes her head. She is intelligent. She knows right from wrong. She says, "Tom, if you take the corn, you are stealing. Stealing is wrong. It makes people sad and angry. How would you feel if someone took your toys?" Tom thinks. He loves his toys. He would feel sad and angry if someone took them. He says, "You are right, Lisa. I don't want to steal. I want to be good. I will resist." They walk away from the truck. They find a bench and eat their sandwiches. They are happy and proud. They did the right thing. The moral of the story is: Resist temptation and do not steal. Stealing is wrong and hurts others. Be like Lisa, who is intelligent and knows right from wrong.

Molly and Tim were walking down the hall. Molly asked Tim what colour the walls were. He said, "They're black". Molly was happy. She liked the colour black. At the end of the hall, Tim saw a big door. He didn't know what was inside, so he asked Molly. She said she didn't know either. They decided to open the door and see what was inside. When they opened the door, they saw a big surprise! It was a black cat! Molly and Tim were so happy. They knew they had a new friend. They took the black cat and named it "Midnight". Midnight was part of their family now.

Once upon a time, there was a shiny needle. This needle was unusual because it could talk. One day, the shiny needle met a small boy named Timmy. The needle said, "Hi, Timmy! I am a talking needle. Can I be your friend?" Timmy was very happy to have a new friend. They played together every day. One sunny day, while they were playing, the shiny needle started to shine even more. It was very bright, like the sun. Timmy and the needle were very surprised. Suddenly, the needle turned into a beautiful fairy. The fairy said, "Thank you, Timmy, for being my friend. I was a fairy trapped in a needle, and your kindness set me free." Timmy and the fairy became best friends and had many more fun days together.

Lily had a toy bear that she loved very much. She called him Stitch, because he had a big stitch on his tummy where his fur was torn. Lily took Stitch everywhere with her, and sometimes she liked to chew on his ears when she was sleepy or bored. One day, Lily and Stitch went to the park with her mom and dad. They played on the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. Lily had so much fun, but she also got very hungry. She saw a big red apple on a picnic table and ran to get it. She did not see the sign that said "Do not touch". Lily took a big bite of the apple, but it was not sweet and juicy. It was hard and bitter. Lily spit it out and made a face. She looked at the apple and saw that it was not a real apple, but a plastic one. She felt silly and sad. She had chewed on a fake apple and hurt her teeth. She ran back to her mom and dad, who were looking for her. She showed them the apple and told them what happened. They hugged her and told her it was okay. They said they had a real apple for her in their bag. They also checked her teeth and said they were fine. Lily was happy again. She ate the real apple and felt much better. She also gave Stitch a big hug and thanked him for being her friend. She said he was very useful, because he always made her feel safe and cozy. Stitch smiled and nodded. He was glad to be Lily's toy bear.
