Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Park

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. But they did not like the big rat that lived under the bench. The rat was mean and scary. It had sharp teeth and a long tail. One day, the rat came out of its hole and saw Lily and Tom. It wanted to scare them away. It ran towards them, making a loud noise. Lily and Tom were fearful. They did not know what to do. "Help!" Lily cried. "The rat is coming!" "Run!" Tom said. "Run to the slide!" They ran as fast as they could, but the rat was faster. It caught up with Tom and pinched his leg. Tom felt a sharp pain and screamed. "Ow! Ow! Ow! The rat pinched me!" Lily heard Tom's scream and turned around. She saw the rat biting Tom's leg. She was angry and brave. She picked up a stick and hit the rat on the head. The rat let go of Tom and ran away. "Are you okay, Tom?" Lily asked. "Yes, thank you, Lily. You saved me!" Tom said. They hugged each other and ran to their mom. They told her what happened. She checked Tom's leg and put a band-aid on it. She praised Lily for being a good friend and a hero. She gave them both a hug and a kiss. They were happy and proud. They did not see the rat again. They played in the park without fear.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy car in his room. It was red and shiny. Tim loved to make the car go zoom across the floor. He would push it and watch it go fast. Tim was so happy with his toy car. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his toy car with him. Tim played with his car on the grass. He made it go zoom up and down the hills. But then, Tim felt uncomfortable. He was tired and wanted to go home. "Mom, I want to go home," Tim said. His mom said, "Okay, let's return home." Tim picked up his toy car and walked with his mom. When they got home, Tim played with his car again. He was happy to be home and not uncomfortable anymore.

Once there were two friends, Joe and Bill. Joe was wearing a jolly green uniform. Bill did not like Joe’s uniform and said, “I don’t want to be your friend anymore if you wear that uniform.” Joe was very sad and started to cry. He said, “But this is my uniform for my special job!” Bill felt bad about his mean words and said, “I’m sorry, I was being silly. I like your uniform, it’s jolly!” Joe was so pleased and smiled. They hugged and were friends again. They both agreed that Joe's uniform made him look jolly and they both felt very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Zip. He lived with a happy family. The family had a dad, a mom, and a little girl named Lily. They all loved to play together. One day, Zip and the family went to the park. They played catch with a ball. Zip was very fast and always brought the ball back. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. The family had a lot of fun. After playing, they all went home. Zip was tired but still wagged his tail. The family gave Zip a big hug and a treat. They all sat together and smiled. Zip was happy to be with his family.

Mary was a gifted three year old. She loved playing outside and reading books. One day, Mary was playing in the park when she saw a beautiful automobile. She asked her mom, "Mom, what's that?" Her mom answered, "That's an automobile! People use it to get around." "Wow!" Mary said, amazed. The next day, Mary decided to read her favorite book about automobiles. It came with colorful pictures that taught her all about how they work. As she read, she imagined riding around in one of those amazing cars. Mary was so happy she had read the book. She now knew what an automobile was, and could not wait to ride in one someday.
