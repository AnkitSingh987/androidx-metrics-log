Category: Intermediate
Title: The Seed Was and the Flower

One day, a little seed was in a hole. It was a comfortable hole with dirt and water. The sun was shining, and the seed wanted to bloom. It pushed and pushed, trying to grow big and strong. A worm came by and saw the seed. The worm said, "Hello, little seed! Are you trying to bloom?" The seed said, "Yes, I want to be a big flower!" The worm said, "I can help you. I will dig the dirt around you so you can grow." The seed was happy and thanked the worm. The worm dug and dug, making the hole bigger. The seed started to grow, and the worm was proud. But then, something unexpected happened. The seed did not become a flower. It became a big tree! The worm was surprised and said, "Wow, you are not a flower, but a tree!" The tree said, "Thank you, worm, for helping me grow. Now I can give shade to everyone!" The worm was happy, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was an enthusiastic kid who loved to play outside. One day, he went to a big landscape with lots of grass and trees. Tim was so happy to run and jump in this new place. As he played, he saw a big rock. Tim decided to climb it. When he got to the top, he saw a small pond. He was so excited and wanted to swim. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and pushed Tim into the water. Tim did not know how to swim. He tried to call for help, but no one was around. He started to cry, but the wind was too loud. Finally, Tim went under the water and did not come back up. The big wind blew away, and the landscape was quiet again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sophie. She was three years old and very brave. One day she was playing in her garden and she got really tired. She yawned really big. Just then, her mum came out to find her. She looked embarrassed and said, "Oh, Sophie! You shouldn't be so tired." Sophie looked embarrassed too, but she knew it was almost time for a nap. She followed her mum inside. Her mum said, "Sophie, it's time for your nap. Go lie down on your knee now." Sophie was tired and she wanted to yawn again. But she hugged her mum and did what she said. She crawled into bed and snuggled into her blanket. She yawned one last time and drifted off to sleep. Sophie knew it was time to rest and even if she was embarrassed, she was happy to lie down on her knee.

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a race. They wanted to see who was the best. The big dog said, "I will win. I am fast and strong." The small cat said, "I will try very hard. I am quick." The race began. The big dog was in the lead. The small cat was not far behind. They ran and ran. The big dog was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. The big dog saw a big red ball. He loved red balls. He stopped to play with the ball. The small cat did not stop. She ran very hard. She passed the big dog. The small cat won the race! The big dog was sad. The small cat said, "Next time, do not stop. Then, you might win too." The big dog smiled and said, "You are right. Let's race again!"

Once upon a time, there was a scary monster who lived in a big cave. He loved to eat corn all day. One day, the scary monster saw a big pile of corn in the middle of the forest. He was very happy and wanted to eat it all. As the scary monster started to eat the corn, a small bunny came out of the bushes. The bunny said, "Please don't eat all the corn. It's for everyone in the forest." The scary monster did not listen and kept eating the corn. The bunny was sad and went away. The next day, the scary monster found out that the corn was magic. It made him feel very nice and not scary at all. He felt bad for not sharing the corn with the bunny. So, the scary monster went to the bunny and said, "I surrender. I will share the magic corn with you and everyone in the forest." The bunny was happy, and they all lived happily ever after, sharing the magic corn.
