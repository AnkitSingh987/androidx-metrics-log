Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dull Clock and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a dull clock. It was not bright or shiny. The clock lived on a wall in a big room. Every day, it went tick-tock, tick-tock. The clock liked to tell time for everyone. One day, a girl named Sue came into the room. Sue was sad. She did not want to play. The clock cared about Sue. It wanted to help her feel better. The clock thought of a way to help. It started to tick-tock louder and faster. Sue looked up and saw the clock. She began to dance to the tick-tock sounds. Sue was happy again. The clock was glad it could care for Sue and make her smile.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a big oak tree. The oak tree had many friends. They were all very happy together. The sun was warm, and the sky was blue. One day, a harsh wind came. It blew and blew, and the oak tree's friends were scared. The oak tree wanted to help. It had a big idea. It said, "Let's unite and be strong together!" All the trees in the forest held hands. They were now a team. The wind blew again, but they did not fall. They were happy and safe. The oak tree and its friends were strong when they were united.

Alice and Ben loved to go on picnics. They packed healthy snacks and set up in the park. They laughed and smiled all afternoon. But then, they heard a loud noise. It was a big, roaring monster! Alice and Ben were startled, and a little bit scared. Alice and Ben tried to get away by running, but the monster was catching up. They were so scared! Suddenly, Ben had an idea. He took out all the healthy snacks from their picnic basket, and threw them at the monster. The monster loved the snacks, and with a happy smile on its face, it took all the snacks and walked away. Alice and Ben were relieved, and they laughed out of happiness at the silly plan that worked out. They hugged each other and resumed their picnic. They were very happy, and enjoyed their healthy snacks.

Once upon a time, in a big round house, there lived a nice dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toy ball. One sunny day, Spot went outside to play with his ball. He was so happy. As Spot played, he saw a yummy treat on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but he was not sure if it was okay. So, Spot decided to lay down next to the treat and wait for his friend, the little girl named Sue. Sue came outside and saw Spot laying by the yummy treat. She knew it was safe for him to eat, so she said, "Go ahead, Spot! Eat the treat!" Spot ate the treat and wagged his tail. He was very happy to have such a good friend like Sue. They played together all day long.

One day, a small bird named Tim was trying to organize his nest. He wanted to make it nice and cozy. The sky was getting dark and a big storm was coming. Tim was a little scared, but he knew he had to finish his nest before the rain came. As Tim worked, he saw a weak bug trying to find a safe place from the storm. The bug looked very tired and scared. Tim felt sorry for the bug and wanted to help. So, he said, "Hey bug, come stay with me. My nest will keep us safe from the storm." The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." They both went into the nest just as the rain started to fall. They were warm and dry inside. Tim and the bug became good friends, and they were not scared of the storm anymore. They knew they were safe together in the cozy nest that Tim had organized.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a restless dog who loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot saw a red ball in the yard. He ran to the ball and started to play with it. He was so happy and excited. As Spot played with the red ball, he saw a small fire near the big tree. Spot knew that fire could be bad, so he wanted to prevent it from getting bigger. He had an idea! Spot ran to the pool, jumped in, and got his fur all wet. Then, he ran back to the fire and shook his wet fur all over it. The fire went out, and Spot saved the day! But suddenly, the red ball started to move on its own! Spot was surprised and a little scared. Then, a friendly squirrel popped out from behind the tree. It was the squirrel who had moved the ball! Spot was not scared anymore. He and the squirrel became best friends, and they played with the red ball together every day.
