Category: Intermediate
Title: The Van and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big van. It was red and shiny. The van had many friends. One day, the van felt very sleepy. It wanted to take a nap. The van's best friend was a little bird. The bird liked to tease the van. The bird would fly around the van and make it laugh. The van liked the bird a lot. Today, the bird saw the sleepy van. The bird wanted to tease the van and make it laugh. But the bird knew the van needed a nap. So, the bird sang a soft song to help the van sleep. The van smiled and fell asleep, happy with its friend.

Once upon a time, there was a nice young man. He was honest and always kept his word. He went out one day to meet a new friend. As he arrived at the spot, he saw a group of youths. He knew that one of them would become his friend. He walked up and asked, "Hello, can I join you?" The youths nodded but did not say a word. He looked around and saw something he did not like. One of the youths had a knife in his hand. The honest young man began to feel scared. He said, "Goodbye," and started to leave. Suddenly, the youths grabbed him and held him tight. The honest young man realised that he had made a bad mistake. They had taken him for the wrong person and wanted to hurt him. No matter how much he tried to be honest, it was to no avail. The youths decided to take him away and still have him with them. His loyal and honest nature would be taken advantage of, forever.

One day, a little girl named Anna went to the park with her mom. They wanted to find blueberries. Anna was anxious because she had never picked blueberries before. Her mom said, "Don't worry, just look for the small blue fruits on the bushes." As they walked, Anna saw a bush with small blue fruits. She said, "Look, Mom! I found blueberries!" But when she picked one, it jumped out of her hand! It wasn't a blueberry, it was a tiny blue frog! Anna and her mom laughed. Anna kept looking, and soon she found real blueberries. She and her mom picked lots of them. They went home and made a yummy blueberry pie. They even gave a small piece to the blue frog, who became their new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a shy car named Carl. Carl lived in a small town with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to go on fun trips together. One sunny day, Carl and Lily decided to go to the park. They were excited to play and have a picnic. But before they left, Carl said, "Wait, Lily! We need fuel to go to the park." So, they went to the fuel store and filled Carl up with fuel. On their way to the park, they saw a big parade. They stopped to watch and clapped their hands. "This is fun! But we have to delay our trip to the park," said Lily. Carl agreed, and they enjoyed the parade. After the parade, Carl and Lily finally went to the park and had a great time playing and eating their picnic.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park to play. She saw a boy named Tom playing with a ball. Lily wanted to play too, so she asked Tom, "Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, but be careful with my ball." They played together and had lots of fun. But then, Lily was not careful and kicked the ball too hard. The ball went far away and got stuck in a tree. They tried to get the ball, but it was too high. Tom was sad and said, "You did not give my ball back. You were not careful." Lily was sorry and said, "I am sorry, Tom." They could not play anymore, and both went home sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the park with his friends. One sunny day, Tim saw a new toy in the park. It was a big, red ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, but he was too selfish to share it with his friends. Tim took the ball and ran away to a big tree to play by himself. He hid behind the tree so no one could find him. But then, Tim heard a noise. He decided to peek around the tree to see what it was. To his surprise, he saw a little girl playing with a toy car. The girl was having so much fun that Tim wanted to join her. Tim realized that he was being selfish with the ball and decided to share it with the girl. They played together and had a great time. In the end, Tim learned that sharing and playing together is much more fun than being selfish.
