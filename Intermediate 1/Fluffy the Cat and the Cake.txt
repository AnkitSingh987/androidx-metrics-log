Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Cat and the Cake

One day, a small cat named Fluffy wanted to cook a cake. She put on her cook hat and got a tray. Fluffy was so happy to make the cake. She knew it would be a fun day. In the kitchen, Fluffy saw a big, fierce dog. The dog barked loud. Fluffy was scared, but she knew she had to cook the cake. She thought of a plan to get the tray past the dog. Fluffy got a ball and threw it. The fierce dog ran to get the ball. Fluffy was fast and took the tray to the oven. The cake was cooked and it was yummy. Fluffy was happy she was brave and made the cake.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a big paw. He loved to run fast and enjoy playing with his ball. One day, Spot was playing with his ball in the park. While playing, Spot saw a big, red ball. It was much bigger than his ball. Spot thought, "I can play with this ball too!" So, Spot ran fast to the big, red ball and hit it with his paw. But, the big, red ball was not a ball! It was a balloon! The balloon popped and made a loud noise. Spot was scared, but then he saw his own ball. He went back to his ball and enjoyed playing with it again. Spot learned that sometimes, it is better to play with what you know and love.

One day, a nosy cat named Kitty saw a big package in the living room. She wanted to know what was in the package. She tried to open it with her paws, but it was too hard. She called her friend, Puppy, to help her. "Hey, Puppy, can you help me open this package?" Kitty asked. Puppy came running and saw the package. He used his teeth to tear the paper. Inside, they found a big, soft ball. Kitty and Puppy started to play with the ball. They would tease each other by taking turns hiding the ball. Kitty hid the ball under a chair, and Puppy found it. Then, Puppy hid the ball behind a plant, and Kitty found it. They had lots of fun playing with the ball. They learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than being nosy and opening packages alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with his toy ball. One day, Fluffy found a big box full of new toys. He was so happy and excited to play with them. Inside the box, Fluffy found a book called a novel. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it was a toy. So, he played with the novel too. He shook it, and little papers fell out. The papers had words on them that Fluffy couldn't read. The next day, Fluffy's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to visit. She saw the novel and the papers on the floor. She picked them up and put them back in the novel. She told Fluffy that the novel was a special kind of toy that could fill her mind with fun stories. Fluffy didn't understand, but he was happy to see Lily happy. From that day on, Fluffy would bring the novel to Lily, and they would read it together.

Once upon a time, there was a big race in a small town. Many animals came to race. A fast dog, a quick cat, and a slow snail wanted to win. They all wanted to be the best. The race began. The dog and the cat ran very fast. They did not see the snail. The snail was slow but fine. He did not give up. He kept going on and on. As the dog and the cat raced, they saw a big drain. They did not know what to do. The snail came and saw the drain too. The snail had an idea. He went into the drain and came out on the other side. The dog and the cat followed him. The snail won the race. The dog and the cat were happy for the snail. They all became good friends.
