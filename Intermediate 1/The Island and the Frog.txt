Category: Intermediate
Title: The Island and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a little island. On the island, there lived a sad frog. The frog was sad because he had a big, disgusting bump on his back. The other animals did not want to play with him because of the bump. The frog would often sit alone and suffer. One day, the sun was shining very bright. The frog felt very hot. He hopped into the cool water to cool off. He swam and swam, and soon he felt better. As he swam, the other animals watched him. They saw that the frog was a very good swimmer. The animals started to think that maybe they should play with the frog, even if he had a disgusting bump. The next day, the animals asked the frog if he wanted to play. The frog was so happy! He played with the animals all day long. And as they played, the animals realized that the disgusting bump did not matter. The frog was a good friend and a great swimmer. The frog did not suffer anymore, and everyone on the island was happy.

One day, a big dog with rough fur was walking in the park. He saw a small cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and crying for help. The big dog wanted to help the cat, so he tried to reach the cat with his shoulder. But, he could not reach the cat because the tree was too tall. Then, a little girl came to the park. She saw the big dog and the small cat. The little girl wanted to include the big dog in her plan to help the cat. She asked the big dog to stand on his back legs, so she could climb on his shoulder. The big dog was happy to help and did as the little girl asked. The little girl climbed on the big dog's shoulder and reached the small cat. She picked up the cat and climbed down. The cat was happy and thanked the big dog and the little girl. The big dog, the little girl, and the small cat became friends. They played in the park together all day. The big dog learned that even if he was rough, he could still help others and make new friends.

Ben and Lily wanted to go on a trip to the zoo. They liked to see the animals and learn their names. But first, they had to do a test at school. The test was about numbers and shapes. Ben and Lily were good at numbers and shapes, but they were frightened of the test. They thought the test was hard and scary. The teacher gave them the test papers and a pencil. Ben and Lily looked at the questions. They saw circles and squares and triangles. They saw one and two and three and four. They tried to remember what the teacher had taught them. They wrote their answers on the paper. They hoped they were right. When they finished the test, they gave the papers to the teacher. The teacher smiled and said, "Well done, Ben and Lily. You did a good job. You can go on the trip to the zoo now." Ben and Lily were happy and relieved. They had passed the test. They were not frightened anymore. They got on the bus with their friends and went to the zoo. They saw lions and tigers and bears. They saw monkeys and elephants and giraffes. They learned their names and their sounds. They had fun and laughed. They enjoyed the trip to the zoo. They forgot about the test. They were glad they had done it.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big truck drive by. Max was scared of the big truck. The truck was very loud and fast. Max hid behind a tree until the truck was gone. As Max walked, he smelled something yummy. It was meat! Max followed the smell to a nice man at a food stand. The man said, "Hi, little dog! Are you hungry? Here, have some meat!" Max was so happy. He ate the meat and wagged his tail. The nice man patted Max on the head. Max was not scared anymore. He knew that even though big trucks were loud, there were also nice people and yummy food in the world. And that made Max very happy.
