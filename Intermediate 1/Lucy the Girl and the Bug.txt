Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Bug

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play outside. She wore her favorite skirt. It was pink and fit her just right. She loved to twirl in it and watch it spin. As she played, she saw a small bug on the ground. "Hello, bug!" said Lucy. The bug did not say anything back. It was a tiny bug and could not talk. But it seemed to be in a panic. It went this way and that way, looking lost. Lucy wanted to help the bug. She sat down and watched it. The bug went in circles, still in a panic. "Don't worry, bug," Lucy said. "I will help you find your home." She picked up the bug and put it on a leaf. Together, they found the bug's home under a big rock. The bug was happy and not in a panic anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to go on the subway with his mom. The subway went under the ground and it was very fun. One day, Tim and his mom went to the subway. Tim was very excited, but he did not know how the subway worked. He was ignorant about it. His mom told him that it would go down and then rise up when they reached their stop. As the subway began to move, Tim felt it rise up. He was so happy that he clapped his hands. His mom smiled at him. They had a great day riding the subway together. And now, Tim knew a little more about how the subway worked.

Once upon a time, there was a strong lizard named Lizzy. She lived in a big tree with her family. They were all very happy. One day, Lizzy and her brother went to play in the park. They saw many rocks and wanted to count them. Lizzy started counting, "One, two, three, four..." She counted all the rocks and felt very proud. Lizzy's brother wanted to be strong like her, so they practiced counting together. They counted rocks, leaves, and even ants. Soon, they both became very good at counting. They went back home and told their family about their fun day. Everyone was happy and proud of Lizzy and her brother.

One day, a polite dog named Max was waiting for a taxi. He wanted to go to the park to play with his friends. The taxi came, and Max got in. The taxi driver said, "Welcome to my taxi, Max!" Max said, "Thank you! I want to go to the park, please." The taxi driver drove Max to the park. On the way, they saw many fun things, like kids playing and birds singing. When they got to the park, Max said, "Thank you for the ride!" The taxi driver said, "You're welcome, Max! Have fun playing!" Max got out of the taxi and played with his friends all day. They had a great time at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy bathtub. It lived in a small house with a little boy named Tom and his mom. Tom liked to play with his toy boat in the bathtub. One day, Tom's toy boat got stuck in the water. Tom tried to get it out, but he struggled. Tom called his mom for help. "Mom, my boat is stuck! I can't get it out," he said. His mom came into the room and saw the heavy bathtub with Tom's boat stuck in the water. She thought about how to help Tom. Tom's mom had an idea. She got a long stick and gave it to Tom. "Here, try to use this stick to get your boat out," she said. Tom used the stick and pushed the boat out of the water. He was happy that his mom helped him. Tom and his mom smiled and laughed. They had fun playing with the boat in the bathtub together.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia loved to create things. She made houses, trees, and animals with her toys. Mia's parent thought she was very interesting. One day, Mia's parent gave her a big box of toys. Mia was so happy! She started to create a town with the new toys. As she played, she found a magic wand in the box. Mia waved the wand and something unexpected happened. The toys came to life! They started to walk and talk. The toys had fun with Mia, but they also made a big mess in her room. Mia knew she had to clean up. She waved the magic wand and said, "Please help me clean up, toys!" The toys helped Mia clean the room, and they all learned that it is important to clean up after having fun. And with that, Mia's parent was very proud of her.
