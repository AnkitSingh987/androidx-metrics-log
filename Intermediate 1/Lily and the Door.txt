Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Door

One day, a little girl named Lily went to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a warm house with a big red door. Lily liked to play with her toys in the warm house. As Lily played, she saw smoke coming from the kitchen. "Grandma, what is that?" she asked. Grandma said, "I am making you a surprise, my dear." Lily was excited to receive a surprise. Soon, the smoke went away, and Grandma brought out a big plate of warm cookies. "Here is your surprise, Lily," she said. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Grandma!" They both sat down and enjoyed the warm cookies together.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a poor jacket with a broken zipper. He was sad because he could not close his jacket when it was cold outside. He told his mom, "Mom, my jacket is broken. Can you fix it?" His mom looked at the zipper and said, "I will try, Tim." While Tim's mom was trying to fix the zipper, she saw a little bug. The bug was stuck in the zipper! She carefully took the bug out and the zipper started to work again. Tim was so happy that his jacket was fixed. He said, "Thank you, Mom!" Later that day, Tim and his mom went outside to play. They made a small fire to keep warm. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bug from the zipper came back and went too close to the fire. The bug started to burn! Tim saw this and quickly got a cup of water. He put the water on the bug to stop it from burning. The bug was saved! Tim and his mom were happy that they could help the little bug. They all played together in the warm sun.

Once upon a time, there was a pale cat. The cat was hungry. It wanted to eat. The cat decided to go to the kitchen. In the kitchen, the cat saw a big cake. The cake looked yummy. The cat wanted to eat the cake. It jumped on the table to eat the cake. The cat ate the whole cake. But then, the cat felt sick. It ate too much cake. The cat was not happy. The end.

One day, a little puppy went for a walk. The puppy saw a boy with paint. The boy was painting on a big paper. The puppy wanted to paint too. So, the puppy asked the boy, "Can I paint with you?" The boy said, "Yes, you can paint with me!" The boy gave the puppy a brush. The puppy held the brush in its mouth. They painted many different things. They painted a tree, a house, and a sun. The puppy was happy to paint with the boy. After they finished painting, the boy and the puppy looked at the big paper. They saw all the different things they painted. The boy said, "We did a great job!" The puppy wagged its tail and barked, "Yes, we did!" They both smiled and had fun painting together.

Tom and Lily are playing with bricks. They want to build a big tower. They put one brick on top of another brick. They are happy. "Look, our tower is so high!" Tom says. "Yes, it is. Can we make it higher?" Lily asks. They find more bricks. They add more bricks to the tower. The tower is very high now. They are proud. "Wow, we did it! We completed our tower!" Tom says. "Yay, we are good builders!" Lily says. They clap their hands. They want to see their tower better. They stand on their toes. They look up at the tower. But the tower is too high. They feel dizzy. They lose their balance. They fall down. The tower falls down too. The bricks make a loud noise. "Ouch, that hurt!" Tom says. "Are you okay?" Lily asks. They rub their heads. They see the bricks everywhere. They are sad. "Our tower is gone!" Tom says. "It's okay, we can build it again." Lily says. They smile. They hug each other. They pick up the bricks. They start to build again. They are happy.
