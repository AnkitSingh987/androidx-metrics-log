Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Song

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to listen to music. He had a big box full of tapes. His favorite tape was an original song his mom made just for him. Tim played the original tape every day. One day, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw Tim's tapes and asked, "Can we listen to your tapes?" Tim said, "Yes! Let me load my favorite one." Tim loaded the original tape his mom made into the tape player. Sam and Tim danced and sang along to the music. After they finished playing, Sam said, "I like your original tape a lot! Can I take it home?" Tim thought about it and said, "No, I want to keep my special tape here, but we can make a new tape for you." So, Tim and Sam made a new tape together. They learned that sharing and creating new things together is more fun than taking something special away from a friend.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the park. They were good friends. The cat had thin skin and the dog had thick skin. They liked to play with a ball. The cat and the dog found a big ball. They wanted to play with it. The cat said, "I will throw the ball." The dog said, "No, I will throw the ball." They disagree on who should throw the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to move by itself! The cat and the dog were surprised. The ball was not a ball! It was a turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, I am a turtle. I am not a ball." The cat and the dog laughed. They played with the turtle instead.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the house. The cat saw a big vase with pretty fake flowers in it. The cat liked to tease the dog. So, the cat said, "I bet you can't jump high enough to touch the vase!" The dog did not like to be teased. He wanted to show the cat that he could jump very high. So, the dog tried to jump and touch the vase. But, he was not able to reach it. Just then, a little mouse ran into the room. The mouse saw the dog and the cat playing. The mouse wanted to play too. So, the mouse said, "I can help you touch the vase!" The dog and the cat were surprised. They did not think a little mouse could help them. But, the mouse ran up the wall and pushed the vase down. The dog and the cat were happy, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his ball in his room. One day, Tom's mom put up a new, thin curtain in his room. The curtain was red and pretty. Tom was so happy to see the new curtain. He wanted to show his ball the curtain too. So, he gave the ball a big kick. The ball went up, up, up, and then hit the curtain. The thin curtain moved and danced when the ball hit it. Tom laughed and clapped his hands. He kicked the ball again and again. The curtain danced every time the ball touched it. Tom and his ball had lots of fun with the new curtain.

One day, a big, impressive bird met a small, quick rabbit. The bird could provide food for the rabbit. The rabbit was very hungry. "Can you help me?" asked the rabbit. "I need food." "Of course!" said the bird. "I will provide you with some food." The bird flew away and soon came back with some food. But, something unexpected happened. The food was not for the rabbit. It was for a little mouse who was hiding nearby. The mouse came out and said, "Thank you, bird!" The rabbit was surprised but happy to make a new friend. They all shared the food and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a lively girl named Lily. Lily liked to find pretty things. One day, she found a big crystal. It was shiny and nice. Lily was very happy. Lily took the crystal home. She wanted to improve its shine. She cleaned it with water and a soft cloth. The crystal started to glow. It was a surprise! The glowing crystal made a sound. It was a magic crystal! It could talk! The crystal became Lily's new friend. They played and had fun together. And that's how Lily's life got better with her magic crystal friend.
