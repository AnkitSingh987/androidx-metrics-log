Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Long Snake and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a long snake named Sam. Sam loved to put his toys in a big box. One day, Sam found a new toy with a fun texture. It was soft and bumpy. He was very happy and wanted to put it in his box too. Sam had a friend, a small bird named Ben. Ben saw Sam's toy and wanted it. Ben took the toy when Sam was not looking. Sam was sad and looked everywhere for his toy. He asked Ben if he had seen it, but Ben said no. Later, Ben felt bad for taking Sam's toy. He decided to give it back and say sorry. Sam was very happy to have his toy back. They both learned that it is important to be honest and share with friends. And from that day on, Sam and Ben played with the fun textured toy together.

One day, a boy named Tom went to visit his grandma. She lived in a small house near the big park. Tom loved to visit her because she always had fun things to do. While he was there, Tom saw a big leather ball in the corner of the room. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Tom started to bounce the ball and spin around. He spun so much that he became dizzy. As Tom was spinning, the leather ball suddenly popped! Out came a tiny, friendly mouse. Tom was surprised and stopped spinning. He and the mouse became best friends and played together all day at grandma's house.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was very brave and kind. He wore a big, shiny hat with lots of colors. The soldier loved to play with a little girl. He would take her for walks and show her different kinds of flowers. She thought he was very charming. The girl loved spending time with the soldier. She would never forget the fun times they would have together. One day, the soldier had to leave. He gave the girl a big hug and a kiss on the forehead. He told her to always remember him. The girl was sad but she remembered the soldier’s hug and smile. She smiled and said goodbye. Now whenever the girl thinks of the soldier she remembers how charming he was.

Once upon a time, in a big wood, there was a little red house. In the house, lived a girl named Sue. Sue had a new book she wanted to read. One day, Sue went outside to read her new book under a big tree. As she read, she saw a little bird. The bird sang a song that went, "tweet-tweet-tweet." Sue smiled and thought, "I will read about this bird in my book." The next day, Sue went back to the tree with her book. She read and read, and then the little bird came back. It sang the same song, "tweet-tweet-tweet." Sue knew what the bird was saying, because she read about it in her book. The bird was saying, "I am your friend." Sue was so happy to have a new friend, and they played together in the wood every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a strong collar that he wore every day. He loved to run and play with his friends in the park. One day, while they were playing, Spot's collar broke. He was sad and did not know what to do. Spot's friends saw that he was sad and wanted to help. They looked for a new collar, but they could not find one. Just then, a big wind came and blew a pretty collar right to Spot's feet. It was a surprise! Spot and his friends did not know where it came from. Spot put on the new collar and it fit just right. He was so happy that he could play with his friends again. He looked up at the sky and said, "Thank you!" Spot and his friends played all day, and they knew that someone special had helped them.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat loved to yawn and play all day. One day, the cat heard about a fun festival in the park. The cat wanted to go to the festival and see all the fun things. At the festival, the cat saw many kids playing and laughing. There were balloons and yummy food. The cat yawned and stretched, feeling happy. The cat played with the kids and ate some tasty treats. As the sun went down, the cat yawned again. It was time to go home. The cat said goodbye to the kids and left the festival. The big purple cat went home, feeling tired but happy from the fun day.
