Title: The Pink Mole and the Garden

Once upon a time there was a pink mole. He was a very happy mole who liked to clap. He would wake up every morning and his first thought was to clap. He clapped all day long, clapping while he ran, clapping while he sniffed in the fresh air and clapping while he ate his lunch. One day, while he was out sniffing, he heard an even louder clapping sound. He stopped and looked around but he couldn't see who was making the noise. He continued sniffing until he finally found the source of the clapping. It was a little girl, only three years old. She was sitting in the grass clapping her hands together with a big smile on her face. The mole couldn't help but join in. He hopped up onto her lap and matched her claps. The girl laughed and clapped louder and louder. Soon the entire garden was filled with the sound of the mole and little girl clapping together. It was a beautiful sight and they clapped until the sun went down. The pink mole and little girl had become great friends. Every day they would meet up and clap together. The mole would bring all his mole friends to join in and soon the garden was filled with clapping moles. One day, a boy named Tim found a long string. He thought it was very fancy. Tim wanted to play and pretend with the string. He tied the string to his toy car and pulled it around the room. Tim was having so much fun. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the fancy string and said, "Wow! Can I play too?" Tim said, "Yes, let's pretend the string is a road for our toy cars!" They played together, laughing and having fun. As they played, the string began to move on its own. The toy cars followed the moving string. Tim and Sue were surprised! The string led them to a hidden toy box. Inside the toy box, they found more fancy strings and fun toys to play with. They were so happy and played all day long.

One day, a little boy named Tim was walking with his mom in the park. He saw a big bird with a long neck. Tim asked his mom, "What is that?" His mom said, "That is an ostrich, Tim." Tim was nervous to go near the ostrich. But he wanted to be brave. He went close to the ostrich and said, "Hi, ostrich. I am Tim." The ostrich said, "Hi, Tim. I am Ollie." Tim and Ollie became friends. They played in the park. But then, a big wind came. The wind wanted to take Ollie away. Ollie was scared. Tim tried to resist the wind. He held Ollie's long neck. The wind stopped. Tim and Ollie were safe. Tim's mom came and said, "You are very brave, Tim." Tim was happy. He and Ollie played in the park every day.

Mumma was very excited. She was getting ready for a special event. She put on a pretty dress and some sparkly earrings. Mumma took the little girl by the hand. "Let's go and see the event, darling," she said. The little girl was so excited. She loved events! The music was loud and the lights were dazzling. She jumped around with joy, feeling dizzy. Mumma saw her spinning in circles. She said, "We belong here. This is our special place. Let's dance together!" The little girl smiled and danced with her mother. She felt like she belonged in that moment. As she spun around, she felt so very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird loved to fly and play in the wind. The wind would blow and the bird would ride it, up and down, all around. One day, the bird met a small bug. The bug asked the bird, "How do you fly in the wind?" The bird wanted to explain, so it said, "I flap my wings and the wind helps me go up and down." The bug wanted to try too. It flapped its little legs, but it could not fly like the bird. The bird saw the bug try and said, "You need wings to fly in the wind like me." The bug was sad, but the bird had an idea. The bird found a big leaf and told the bug to hold on tight. Together, they flew in the wind, happy and free.
