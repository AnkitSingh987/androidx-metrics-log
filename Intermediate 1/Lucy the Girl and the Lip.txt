Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Lip

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, proud smile on her face. Lucy loved to talk and make her friends understand her. One day, Lucy hurt her lip while playing. She felt sad because her lip made it hard for her to talk. She tried to tell her friends what happened, but they did not understand her. Lucy's mom saw her sad face and gave her a hug. She told Lucy that her lip would get better soon. Lucy felt happy again. Soon, her lip was all better, and she could talk with her friends. They all understood her, and Lucy was proud of her big smile once more.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round metal ball. It was very boring because it could not move or play with the other toys. One day, it saw a small, brave toy car who dared to do fun things. The metal ball wanted to be like the toy car, so it dared to roll and play too. It rolled down a hill and went very fast. The other toys were surprised and happy to see the metal ball move. They all played together and had lots of fun. But then, the metal ball rolled too fast and broke a toy house. The metal ball felt sad and said sorry to the toy house. The other toys helped fix the toy house, and they all learned to play carefully. The moral of the story is to dare to try new things, but always remember to be careful and kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no friends to play with. He wanted to build something to help him find friends. Tim thought and thought, and then he had an idea. He would build a telephone to call and talk to new friends. Tim worked hard to build the telephone. He used a box, some string, and a cup. When he finished, he was very happy. He tried to call someone, but the telephone did not work. Tim was sad again. He tried and tried to fix the telephone, but it still did not work. Tim was very sad. He could not make any friends. In the end, Tim stayed sad and never found any friends to play with.

Lily liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She wore a pink apron with flowers on it. She helped her mom stir the soup, cut the bread, and set the table. She felt happy and calm. One day, Lily saw a big bottle on the counter. It had a label that said "Shrink". Lily was curious. She wanted to know what it did. She opened the bottle and smelled it. It smelled funny. She poured some on her apron. Suddenly, Lily felt very small. Her apron was too big for her. It covered her whole body. She could not see anything. She was scared. She cried out, "Mom, help!" Her mom heard her voice. She looked around the kitchen. She saw the bottle on the counter. She saw the big apron on the floor. She realized what happened. She ran to the apron and lifted it up. She saw Lily, tiny and trembling. "Oh, Lily, what did you do?" her mom asked. She hugged Lily and kissed her. She poured some water on Lily's apron. Lily started to grow back to her normal size. She felt relieved and happy. "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to see what the bottle did. I didn't know it would make me shrink," Lily said. "It's okay, Lily. I'm glad you're safe. But you have to be careful. You can't touch things that you don't know. They might be dangerous. Do you understand?" her mom said. "Yes, mom. I understand. I won't do it again. I love you, mom," Lily said. "I love you too, Lily. Now, let's have some soup. It's still warm," her mom said. They took off their aprons and sat down at the table. They ate their soup and bread. They smiled and talked. They felt happy and calm.

Once there was a king. He was a huge king. The king had many friends. One day, the king had a big question. He did not know the answer. The king asked his friends the question. They did not know the answer too. They all looked and looked for the answer. They asked many people. No one knew the answer. One day, a small kid came to the king. The kid knew the answer to the king's question. The king was very happy. The small kid became the king's best friend. The king and the kid had fun and played every day.
