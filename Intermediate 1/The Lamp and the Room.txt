Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lamp and the Room

Once upon a time, there was a little lamp. The lamp was mysterious. It sat in a room all by itself. The lamp liked to wake up when it was dark. One day, a boy found the lamp. He was happy. He took the lamp to his room. That night, the lamp woke up. It made the room bright and shiny. The boy liked the lamp. They played and had fun. The mysterious lamp and the boy became best friends. They were happy together, and the lamp woke up every night to make the boy's room bright.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a stubborn dog named Max. Max loved to race with his friends. One sunny day, Max and his friends were racing in the park. While they were racing, a thief took Max's favorite toy. Max saw the thief and was very sad. He wanted his toy back, so he stopped racing and ran after the thief. Max was fast and caught the thief. But Max did not know that the thief was just a little boy who wanted to play. The boy said sorry and gave the toy back to Max. Max was happy, and they all played together. The little boy was not a thief anymore, and Max had a new friend.

One sunny day, two little children were having a picnic in the park. The older one, Susie, was 7 years old. The other, Tommy, was only 3. Suddenly a very upset cloud came along and the seasons began to change. Susie told Tommy, "Don't worry, this will only last for a little while". The wind grew very strong and soon the park shrank. It seemed like the picnic was coming to an end. But then, a tiny rainbow appeared in the sky. Susie smiled and said, "See, the raincloud has gone away and now everything is OK again". Tommy was overjoyed. He clapped his hands and cheered, saying, "Yay, the park has not shrunk anymore!"

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy chess set. The chess set was original, and Tim loved it very much. He played with the chess pieces every day. One day, Tim's chess set vanished. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. Tim was very sad. He missed playing with his chess set. Tim's mom saw how sad he was. She helped him look for the chess set. They found it under his bed. Tim was so happy! He thanked his mom and played with his original chess set all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a very glad bird. He loved to soar high in the sky and feel the wind on his wings. Every day, he would fly and sing happily. One day, while Tim was flying, he found a big fan. The fan was on the ground, and it was not moving. Tim was curious and wanted to see what the fan could do. He used his beak to turn the fan on. When the fan started to move, it made a big wind. Tim was excited and decided to try to soar with the wind from the fan. He flapped his wings and began to fly higher and higher. The wind from the fan helped him go even higher than before. Tim was so happy that he found the fan. With its help, he could soar higher than ever. He knew that he would always be a glad bird, enjoying the sky and the wind on his wings. And from that day on, Tim and the fan were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big pit. The pit was very old and had been there for a long time. One day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Spot, were playing near the pit. Lily said to Spot, "Let's see if we can fit this ball into the pit!" She threw the ball, and it went into the pit. Spot barked and wagged his tail, excited to get the ball back. But the pit was too deep for them to reach the ball. An old man saw the two friends and came to help. "Don't worry, I will get your ball," he said. With a long stick, he reached into the pit and got the ball out. Lily and Spot were so happy! They thanked the old man and played together for the rest of the day.
