Category: Intermediate
Title: Leo the Cat and the Tree

One day, a big cat named Leo the Leopard felt very sad. He was miserable because he could not complete his big jump. He wanted to jump from one tree to another, but he was scared. He sat on the tree and cried. Then, a little bird named Bella flew by and saw Leo. Bella asked, "Why are you sad, Leo?" Leo said, "I want to complete my big jump, but I am scared. I don't want to fall." Bella thought for a moment and had an idea. Bella said, "I can help you, Leo! Hold on to my wings, and I will fly with you to the other tree." Leo was surprised. He never thought a little bird could help him. But he trusted Bella, so he held on to her wings. Together, they flew to the other tree. Leo was so happy! He completed his big jump with Bella's help. Now, Leo and Bella are best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play basketball. He took his basketball and went to the park. In the park, there was a big boy named Jack. Jack was not nice. He took Tim's basketball and would not give it back. Tim felt helpless. He asked Jack, "Please give me back my basketball." Jack laughed and said, "No, you can't have it." Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Just then, Tim's big sister, Sue, came to the park. She saw what happened and went to help Tim. Sue stood tall in front of Jack and said, "Give my brother his basketball back!" Jack was scared of Sue, so he gave the basketball back to Tim. Tim was happy and thanked his sister. Together, they played basketball and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bicycle. It was red and shiny. The bicycle was in a store. It was on display for everyone to see. Many kids wanted the bicycle, but no one could have it yet. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the bicycle. He loved it so much. He told his mom, "I want that bicycle, please!" His mom said they could not buy it today, but maybe one day. Tim was sad but he knew he had to wait. Tim went to the store every day to look at the beautiful bicycle. But one day, the bicycle was gone. Someone else bought it. Tim was very sad. He cried and cried. He wished he could have the beautiful bicycle, but now it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Max. Lily was a very honest girl. She always told the truth. One day, Lily found a bottle with a label that said "poison". She knew it was not good to touch. She ran to her mom and said, "Mom, I found poison in the house. What should we do?" Her mom was happy that Lily told the truth. She said, "Thank you, Lily. You did the right thing by telling me. We will throw it away so no one gets hurt." Later, Max the dog found the bottle of poison. He wanted to play with it. Lily saw Max and said, "No, Max! That is poison. It is bad for you!" Max stopped and did not touch the poison. Lily's mom saw what happened and said, "Lily, you saved Max from getting hurt. You are a very honest girl. We will not punish Max, but we will teach him not to play with dangerous things." And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a brother named Jim. They liked to play outside in the open air. One day, they went to the park with their mom. At the park, they saw a big box. Tim urged Jim to open the box. They did not know what was inside. When they opened the box, they found a lot of fun toys to play with. They were so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The toys started to move on their own! The toys were not just toys, but they were also friends. Tim and Jim played with the toys and made new friends. They all had a great time at the park.
