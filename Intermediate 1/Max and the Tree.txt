Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day. One sunny day, Max met a small, fast cat named Lucy. Max and Lucy stared at each other for a moment. They both wanted to play together. Max had an idea. He wanted to have a race with Lucy. They would run from the big tree to the little house. Max and Lucy got ready to run. They looked at the tree and the house. They were very excited. The race began, and Max and Lucy ran as fast as they could. Max was strong, but Lucy was very quick. They both ran and ran until they reached the little house. Max was tired, but happy. Lucy was happy too. They both had lots of fun in the race. From that day on, Max and Lucy were the best of friends.

Once a small boy named Tim wanted to get a haircut. He went to the barber with his mom. The barber had a big chair for Tim to sit on. Tim was happy to get his haircut. The barber started to cut Tim's hair. Tim looked in the mirror and said, "I want my hair short, please." The barber nodded and kept cutting. But then, Tim saw that the barber was cutting too much hair! Tim said, "Stop! That's too short!" The barber stopped and looked at Tim's hair. It was very short, but Tim did not like it. The barber felt sad but tried to fix it. He gave Tim a small toy to make him feel better. Tim smiled and knew that his hair would grow back soon.

Once upon a time, there was a humble boy named Tom. He lived in a small house near a big castle. Tom liked to whistle while he walked to the castle each day. One sunny day, Tom met a girl named Sue near the castle. She heard Tom whistle and said, "I like your whistle. Can you teach me how to whistle too?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course! Just pucker your lips and blow air out gently." Sue tried and tried, but she could not whistle. Tom said, "Do not worry. Keep trying, and you will learn." Sue smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. I will keep trying every day." And so, Tom and Sue became good friends, and they would whistle and play near the castle every day.

Once upon a time, there was a guard who wanted to be independent. Everyday, he went outside to catch butterflies. One day, he saw a butterfly that was especially beautiful. He said to himself,"I have to catch this butterfly." He chased the butterfly, but it kept on flying away from him. Then, he saw a little girl standing nearby. He said to the girl, "Can you help me catch the butterfly?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes! I can help you catch the butterfly!" Together, they chased the butterfly and they finally caught it! The guard was so happy and thanked the little girl. The guard said, "You are very independent. Thank you for helping me." The little girl smiled and said, "You're welcome!"

Once upon a time, there was a powerful boat. The boat loved to sail on the big water. One day, the boat saw some land far away. It wanted to go there and meet new friends. As the boat sailed, it met a fish. The fish said, "Hello, boat! Where are you going?" The boat replied, "I am going to the land to make new friends. Do you want to come with me?" The fish said, "Yes, I would love to!" The boat and the fish sailed to the land. When they got there, they saw many animals. They saw birds, cats, and dogs. The boat said, "Hello, friends! We have come to play with you!" The animals were happy to meet the boat and the fish. They all played together on the land. The boat and the fish made many new friends. They had so much fun. After a while, the boat and the fish said goodbye to their new friends. They sailed back to the big water, ready for their next adventure.
