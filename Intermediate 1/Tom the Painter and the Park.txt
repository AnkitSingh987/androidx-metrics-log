Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Painter and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. He liked to paint big and pretty pictures. He painted with many colors. Tom was happy when he painted. One day, Tom went to a noisy park to paint. There were many kids playing and laughing. The birds were singing too. It was hard for Tom to paint, but he tried his best. Tom painted a tree that seemed very big. He also painted the blue sky and the green grass. The kids stopped and looked at his picture. They liked it. Tom was happy and kept painting in the noisy park.

Lily and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Lily has a purple phone that can ring. Ben has a silly hat that has ears and a tail. They pretend to be different animals and make noises. Lily picks up the phone and says, "Hello, who is this?" Ben puts on the hat and says, "This is the lion. I am hungry. Can I come to your house?" Lily laughs and says, "No, you cannot. You are too big and scary. You will eat me and my toys." Ben says, "But I am a nice lion. I only eat grass and flowers. Please let me come. I want to play with you." Lily thinks for a moment and says, "Okay, you can come. But you have to be gentle and quiet. And you have to wear your silly hat. It makes you look funny." Ben says, "Thank you, Lily. I will be there soon. Bye bye." Lily hangs up the phone and waits for Ben. She hears a knock on the door. She opens it and sees Ben wearing his silly hat. He looks like a lion with ears and a tail. He roars softly and smiles. Lily giggles and says, "Hello, Mr. Lion. Welcome to my house. Do you want to play with my purple phone?" Ben nods and says, "Yes, please. I like your purple phone. It can ring. Can I call someone?" Lily says, "Sure, you can. Who do you want to call?" Ben thinks for a moment and says, "I want to call the elephant. He is my friend. He has a big nose and big ears. He can make loud noises." Lily says, "Okay, let's call the elephant. Maybe he wants to play with us too." They dial the number and wait for the ring. They hope the elephant will answer. They are having fun with their toys and their imagination. They are happy and silly.

One day, a sad boy went for a walk. He saw a leaf on the ground. The leaf was red and pretty. The boy picked it up and looked at it. He wanted to find more leaves like this one. The boy walked and walked. He met a girl. The girl said, "I recognize that leaf! It's from my tree!" The boy was happy to find the tree with the pretty leaves. He asked the girl to show him the tree. They walked together to the tree. But when they got there, the tree was gone! The boy and the girl were very sad. They looked at the ground and saw many pretty leaves. They knew the tree was cut down. The boy and the girl cried and went home, feeling sad that they could not find more leaves like the one the boy found.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted a glass of water. She felt very thirsty and uncomfortable. She asked her mom, "Mom, can you provide me a glass of water, please?" Her mom gave her a glass of water. Lily took a sip and felt better. She saw a little dog outside who looked thirsty too. Lily wanted to help the dog, so she went outside with her glass of water. When she got close, the dog turned into a magic cat! The cat said, "Thank you, Lily, for trying to give me water. I was testing you to see if you are kind. You passed!" The cat waved its tail and turned the water in the glass into candy. Lily was happy and shared the candy with her friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others, even if they are not what they seem.
