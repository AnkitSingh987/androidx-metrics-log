Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat food. His favorite food was cake. One day, Tim's mom told him that she had a serious surprise for him. Tim was very excited and couldn't wait to see what it was. Tim's mom brought out a big box. Tim opened it and saw a big cake inside. He was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat jumped out of the cake! Tim was very surprised and let out a big scream. The cat looked at Tim and started to laugh. Tim realized that the cat was not scary, but funny. He laughed too. The cat and Tim became good friends. They ate the cake together and played all day. Tim learned that sometimes, surprises can be both scary and fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. The doctor wanted to take an x-ray of Tim's arm. Tim was a bit scared, but his mom said it would be quick. In the waiting room, Tim saw a bowl of sour candy. He loved sour candy and it made him forget about the x-ray. He took one, and his mom said, "If you're brave, you can have more later." Tim felt better, and his fear started to increase less. Tim went to the x-ray room and was very brave. After the x-ray, his mom gave him more sour candy. Tim was happy he faced his fear, and the sour candy made it all better.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pair of scissors. Lily liked to cut paper and make pretty things with her scissors. One day, Lily found a flexible stick. It could bend and twist in many ways. Lily had a friend named Sam. Sam was sad because he did not have a toy to play with. Lily wanted to care for Sam and make him happy. She thought about how she could use her scissors and the flexible stick to make a toy for Sam. Lily cut the stick into small pieces with her scissors. Then, she tied the pieces together to make a fun toy for Sam. When Sam saw the toy, he was very happy. They played with the toy together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is to care for your friends and help them when they are sad. You can use what you have, like scissors and a flexible stick, to make something special for them.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to move and play all day long. One day, Tim went on a search to find a fragile toy he lost. He looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in the toy box. But he could not find it anywhere. Tim's mom saw him looking sad and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I can't find my fragile toy. I looked everywhere." Mom said, "Let's search together." So, they both started to look for the toy. As they were searching, Tim's dog, Spot, came in with the fragile toy in his mouth. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Spot!" Tim never expected that Spot would find his toy. From that day on, Tim and Spot became the best of friends and played together all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy cat named Tom. He loved to play with his ball in the kitchen. One day, while playing, Tom saw a sink. He had never seen a sink before. He thought it was a big bowl. Tom wanted to look inside the sink. So, he tried to kneel and jump in. But, he was too small. He tried again and again. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog named Max came in the kitchen. Max saw Tom trying to jump in the sink. He wanted to help. Max kneeled down next to Tom. Tom climbed on Max's back and jumped into the sink. They both laughed and became best friends. From that day on, they played together every day. And the sink was their secret play spot.
