Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Enthusiastic Cook and the Town

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a very enthusiastic cook named Tom. Tom loved to prepare yummy food for all his friends. He always made sure everyone was happy and full. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam said, "Tom, I heard about a new law in our town. The law says we must share our food with everyone." Tom thought that was a great idea. He wanted to help everyone in the town have yummy food too. So, Tom and Sam went to the town square. They prepared lots of food and shared it with everyone. All the people in the town were happy and said, "Thank you, Tom and Sam!" Tom and Sam smiled and knew they did a good thing by following the new law.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a whistle. He was very happy. Tim loved to blow the whistle. One day, he went to the deep park to play. In the park, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was studying the leaves on the ground. "Hi, Sue!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with my whistle?" Sue looked up and smiled. "Yes, I want to play!" she said. Tim and Sue played with the whistle. They blew it loud and soft. They had fun. But then, a big dog came running. The dog was angry because of the loud whistle sound. The dog took the whistle and ran away. Tim and Sue were very sad. They lost the whistle and couldn't play anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit ignorant. He did not know how to improve his skills. One day, Tim went to play with his friends in the park. He saw his friends playing a game. Tim wanted to join, but he did not know how to play. His friends tried to teach him, but Tim did not listen. One day, there was an emergency in the park. A little girl was hurt. Tim wanted to help, but he did not know how. His friends knew what to do because they had learned from their parents. They called for help and took care of the little girl. Tim felt sad that he could not help. Later, Tim's mom told him that he could improve by listening and learning from others. Tim decided to try harder and listen to his friends and family. Soon, Tim got better at many things. He was happy that he could help others in need. The moral of the story is to listen and learn so you can improve and be ready to help in an emergency.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big purple kite. She wanted to play with the kite outside. Lily ran to the park with her new toy. At the park, Lily held the kite and started to run. The purple kite went up, up, up in the sky. Lily was so happy to see the kite fly high. She loved how the kite danced in the wind. But then, a big gust of wind came. The purple kite started to fall, fall, fall from the sky. Lily was sad, but she did not give up. She picked up the kite and ran again. This time, the kite flew even higher than before. Lily smiled and knew that she could always try again if her kite falls.

Once upon a time, in a small wood, there was a little rabbit. The little rabbit loved to rush and jump all day. One day, the rabbit met a big, crazy fox. The crazy fox said, "Let's play a game!" The little rabbit was excited to play. They started to run and rush in the wood. The rabbit was fast, but the crazy fox was faster. As they played, the sky got dark. The little rabbit got scared and wanted to go home. But the crazy fox did not want to stop. The fox chased the rabbit, and the rabbit could not find his way home. The rabbit was lost in the wood, and the crazy fox was still chasing him. The little rabbit was very sad and scared, and he never found his way back home.
