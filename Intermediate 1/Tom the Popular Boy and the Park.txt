Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Popular Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his friends. They liked to throw a ball, play tag, and have fun. One day, Tom found a bottle in the park. He wanted to throw it away, but his friends said, "No, don't throw it! It's not nice to throw things that are not ours." Tom felt sad because he wanted to throw the bottle, but he didn't want to make his friends upset. Tom thought and thought, and then he had an idea. He asked his friends, "What if we find the owner of the bottle and give it back?" His friends liked the idea. They looked around the park and found a little girl who was crying. She had lost her bottle. Tom gave the bottle back to the little girl, and she was happy. Tom and his friends felt good because they helped someone, and they went back to playing their games.

Once there was a reliable magnet. The magnet loved to lean against things and hold them tight. One day, the magnet leaned against a box. Inside the box there were two toy cars and a balloon. The magnet was so strong that it held tight to the box. Next, the magnet leaned against a door. The door opened and a little girl came out. She was three years old and her name was Emma. Emma smiled when she saw the magnet and gave it a hug. The magnet was happy. No matter what it leaned against, the magnet was reliable and could always be counted on. From then on, Emma and the magnet were friends and they were always happy to be together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play in the mud. One day, it was very dull outside. Tom saw a big mud puddle and ran to it. Tom saw his friend Sue near the mud puddle. "Hi Sue!" said Tom. "Do you want to play in the mud with me?" Sue looked at the mud and said, "Yes! Let's play!" After playing in the mud, Tom and Sue were very dirty. They went to the store to buy soap. They bought the soap and went home. Tom and Sue washed their hands and faces. They were clean again. They had so much fun playing in the mud on a dull day.

Once upon a time, there was a modest cat named Tom. He had a big head and loved to play with his friends. Tom lived in a small house with his family. They were all very happy. One day, Tom and his friends decided to play a game. They wanted to scare each other and see who could be the bravest. They took turns hiding behind trees and bushes, trying to scare their friends. At the end of the game, Tom was the best at scaring his friends. His big head helped him make funny faces that made everyone laugh. They all had a great time, and Tom was very proud of himself. They played together until the sun went down, and then they all went home to their families.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to jog every day. He wanted to be very fit. One day, Tim's mom said, "Hurry, Tim! The big race is today!" Tim went to the big race. He was ready to jog fast. He felt fit. The race began, and Tim hurried to the front. He was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. A little dog joined the race! The dog was very fast. It passed Tim and won the race. Tim was not sad. He was happy for the dog. Tim learned that it's okay to not always win.

Once upon a time, in a big garden full of nature, there lived a pretty flower. This flower was very sad because it could not bloom like the other flowers. It tried very hard, but it just could not bloom. One day, a kind bee came to the sad flower. The bee said, "Do not worry, little flower. I will help you bloom." The bee went to find some sweet water for the flower to drink. The flower drank the water and felt much better. That night, it became dark in the garden. The moon and stars came out to watch. Suddenly, the sad flower started to bloom! It was so happy and thanked the kind bee. Now, the flower was not sad anymore and could enjoy the beautiful nature with its new friends.
