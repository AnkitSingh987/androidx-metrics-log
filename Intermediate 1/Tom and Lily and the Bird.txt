Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Bird

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big bird with many feathers. The bird was sitting on a branch and singing. "Look, a bird!" Tom said. "It has so many feathers. Can I touch them?" "No, Tom, you can't," Lily said. "The bird will not like it. The feathers are part of the bird. They help it fly and keep it warm." "But I want to see how they feel," Tom said. He did not listen to Lily. He climbed the tree and reached for the bird. The bird was troubled. It did not want Tom to touch it. It flapped its wings and pecked at Tom's hand. Tom was scared and hurt. He let go of the bird and fell to the ground. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom cried. He had a scratch on his hand and a feather in his hair. Lily ran to him and helped him up. "Are you okay, Tom?" Lily asked. She saw the feather and removed it from his hair. "You should not have done that, Tom. You made the bird angry and sad. You should say sorry to the bird." "I'm sorry, bird," Tom said. He looked at the bird and saw that it was calm again. It was still on the branch and singing. "I'm sorry, Lily. You were right. I should have listened to you. The feathers are not for me. They are for the bird." Lily hugged Tom and smiled. "It's okay, Tom. I'm glad you are sorry. And I'm glad you are not hurt too much. Come on, let's go and play something else. But no more climbing trees, okay?" "Okay," Tom said. He held Lily's hand and they walked away. The bird watched them and sang a happy song.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pale cat and a brown dog. They did not like each other. They would always quarrel and make noise. The house had a big roof where they would go to play and fight. One day, the pale cat and the brown dog went up to the roof. They started to quarrel very loudly. They jumped and pushed each other. The noise was so loud that the people in the house could not sleep. The pale cat and the brown dog were not careful. They kept fighting near the edge of the roof. Suddenly, they both fell off the roof. The cat and the dog got hurt very badly. They could not play or fight anymore. The people in the house were sad because their pets were hurt.

One day, a little boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon. He was very happy. The cartoon had a funny dog that would always sneeze. Tim would laugh every time the dog sneezed. Suddenly, Tim heard a loud sneeze from outside. He looked out the window and saw a big, heavy rock. He was very surprised. The rock had a face and it sneezed again! Tim could not believe it. A sneezing rock was not something he had seen before. Tim went outside to look at the sneezing rock. He felt brave. He touched the rock and it stopped sneezing. The rock was not a rock at all! It was the funny dog from the cartoon! The dog had come to life and was playing a trick on Tim. They both laughed and played together all day.

Mama was feeling very happy. She was about to supply something new for her daughter. She walked carefully to the store, so she didn't bump her toe. When she arrived, she saw so many neat and tidy things. She knew exactly what she wanted. She searched carefully until she found it: a big and pretty new dress! Mama was so excited. She was sure it would fit her daughter perfectly. She took it home and put it away for safekeeping. When it was time for the daughter to try it on, she couldn't wait. The little girl was happy when she saw the dress. It fit her perfectly, and the color was so bright and happy. Mama was delighted. The daughter twirled around and around, showing off her new dress. She was so proud that her mama had been able to supply her with such a neat and tidy dress.
