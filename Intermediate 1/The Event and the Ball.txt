Title: The Event and the Ball

Once, there was a big event in the town. All the kids were very excited. They wanted to have fun and play games. But one little girl, named Sally, was not happy. She was a spoiled girl who always wanted more. Sally went to the event with her mom. She saw a big, red ball. She wanted it very much. "Mom, I want that ball," she said. Her mom told her, "You have many balls at home, dear. You don't need another one." But Sally did not listen. She wanted the ball so much that she started to struggle to get it. Sally tried hard to get the ball, but it was too big for her. She pushed and pulled, but the ball did not move. Other kids saw Sally struggle and tried to help her. But Sally did not want help. She wanted the ball all for herself. Finally, the ball rolled away from Sally. It went very fast and hit a big cake. The cake fell on the ground and was ruined. All the kids were sad. The event was not fun anymore. Sally did not get the ball, and she made everyone unhappy. She was still a spoiled girl, and she learned nothing.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a pretty blouse with flowers on it. Lily liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, Lily saw a cat. The cat looked angry. Lily asked the cat, "Why are you angry?" The cat said, "I lost my bow. I am sad." Lily wanted to help the cat find its bow. They looked all around the park. At last, they found the bow under a big tree. The cat was so happy! Lily put the bow on the cat's neck. The cat said, "Thank you, Lily!" The cat was not angry anymore. Lily and the cat played together in the park. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends. They were both tall peppers. One was red and the other was green. The red pepper was always looking for fun things to do. One day, the green pepper said to the red pepper, “Let’s go for a walk. We can have some fun.” But the red pepper said, “No, it’s too dangerous. We could get hurt.” The green pepper said, “Don’t be silly. We should go outside. It will be fun.” The red pepper said, “No, let’s stay here. We need to be careful to prevent getting hurt.” So the green pepper listened and they stayed safe at home. They had a lot of fun playing together and they were happy they had been careful. The moral of the story is that it’s important to be careful and take precautions to prevent getting hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a cold day in a small town. A boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing outside. They were friends and liked to share secrets with each other. They wore big coats to stay warm. Tim said to Sue, "I have a secret." Sue was excited and asked, "What is it?" Tim whispered, "I saw a big fire last night. It was so big and bright, it burned and made everything warm." Sue's eyes grew big, and she said, "Wow, that's a big secret!" They continued to play outside, but now they had a fun secret to share. They talked about the big fire and how it burned. Even though it was a cold day, their secret made them feel warm and happy inside.

One day, a furry cat named Tom went outside to play. He liked to play in the sun and feel the warm air on his fur. The ground was full of dust, but Tom didn't care. He rolled in the dust and had so much fun. While playing, Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max was very big and liked to yell. Tom was scared of Max's loud yell, but he wanted to be friends with him. So, Tom walked up to Max and said, "Hi, I am Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Max looked at Tom and smiled. "Yes, I want to play with you!" Max yelled. Tom and Max played together in the dust and the sun. They chased each other and laughed. They were very happy to be friends, and Tom learned that Max's yell was not so scary after all.
