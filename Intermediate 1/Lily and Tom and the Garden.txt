Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Garden

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs. Sometimes they found worms, beetles, or spiders. They put them in a jar and gave them names. One day, they saw something shiny and long in the grass. It was a snake. Lily wanted to catch it and put it in the jar. She thought it was pretty and cool. Tom did not want to catch it. He thought it was scary and dangerous. He said snakes could bite and hurt them. Lily and Tom disagreed. They argued and shouted. "Let me catch it! It's my turn!" Lily said. "No, leave it alone! It's not a bug, it's a snake!" Tom said. The snake heard them and felt uncomfortable. It did not like the noise and the children. It wanted to be left alone. It hissed and slithered away. Lily and Tom stopped arguing and watched the snake disappear. They felt sorry for scaring it. They decided to play something else. They agreed to be nice to the snake and the bugs. They hugged and said sorry. They were friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a big van. Inside the van were two best friends. They were playing and having fun. Suddenly, one of the friends found a big box. She was so excited and asked her friend, "What do you think is inside?" But her friend had no idea. They opened the box and were disappointed. Nothing was inside! Sighing, they decided to close the box and go back to playing. But when they tried to close the box, they realized it wouldn't shut! After trying again and again, they realized something was blocking the opening. Her friend stuck her hand in and pulled out a big stick. Now that the stick was gone, the box shut with ease! The friends had solved the problem! They hugged each other, happy to have won against the big van.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to hang from the trees in the jungle. He would swing from branch to branch, laughing and playing all day long. One day, Momo saw something shiny around the waist of a big elephant. The shiny thing was a persistent little bug. The bug would not go away, no matter how much the elephant tried to shake it off. Momo wanted to help the elephant. So, he swung down from the trees and grabbed the shiny bug. The elephant was very happy and thanked Momo. From that day on, Momo and the elephant became best friends. They played together in the jungle every day, and the persistent little bug never bothered the elephant again.

Jenny and Sam were playing with blocks. They wanted to build a big tower. Jenny put one block on top of another. Sam did the same. They were happy. But then, Max came. Max was older and bigger. He did not like blocks. He liked cars. He saw Jenny and Sam's tower. He wanted to knock it down. He ran to the tower and pushed it with his hand. The blocks fell down. Jenny and Sam were sad. "Hey, why did you do that?" Jenny asked Max. She was angry. "Because I can. Blocks are boring. Cars are better." Max said. He was mean. "That's not nice. You ruined our tower. We worked hard on it." Sam said. He was upset. "I don't care. You can't stop me. I'm stronger than you." Max said. He was proud. Jenny and Sam did not like Max. They wanted to teach him a lesson. They had an idea. They whispered to each other. Then, they smiled. They picked up some blocks and hid them behind their backs. They pretended to be scared of Max. They said, "Please, don't hurt us. You are too strong. We are too weak." Max heard them. He laughed. He thought he had won. He said, "That's right. You are weak. I am strong. I am the best. You are the worst." He walked towards them. He did not see the blocks behind their backs. He did not know their plan. When he was close enough, Jenny and Sam threw the blocks at him. The blocks hit him on the head, on the nose, on the chest. They hurt him. He cried. He ran away. Jenny and Sam cheered. They had won. They had beaten Max. They had taught him a lesson. They hugged each other. They were happy. They picked up the blocks and started to build a new tower. They made it bigger and better than before. They used delicate blocks to make it pretty. They did not complain. They had fun. They were friends.
