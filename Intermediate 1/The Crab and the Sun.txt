Category: Intermediate
Title: The Crab and the Sun

One day, a funny little crab went to the shore. He liked to play in the sand and feel the waves on his legs. The sun was bright and the water was cool. The crab was very happy. As the crab played, he saw a big shell. He went close and heard a whisper. The whisper was soft, like the wind. The crab was curious and wanted to know who was talking. He put his ear near the shell and heard the whisper again. It was a tiny sea friend inside the shell! They talked and laughed all day. The funny little crab made a new friend at the shore.

Once upon a time, there was a big onion. The onion had many friends in the garden. But the onion was envious of the other vegetables because they all had fun jobs. The tomato was in the salad, the carrot was in the soup, and the potato was in the fries. The onion wanted to serve too. One day, the onion was talking to the tomato. The onion said, "I wish I could serve like you do in the salad." The tomato smiled and said, "You can serve too, onion! Just be patient and your time will come." The next day, the cook came to the garden. The cook picked the onion and took it to the kitchen. The cook used the onion to make a tasty dish. The onion was finally serving just like its friends. The onion was happy and not envious anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to go on walks with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. One day, they went on a very important walk to the park. On their walk, Max saw a big dog. The big dog said, "I am the king of the park. You must do a special trick to play here." Max was scared and started to tremble. Lily said, "Don't worry, Max. I know you can do it." Max thought of a special trick he could do. He ran fast, jumped high, and did a flip in the air. The big dog was happy with Max's trick. He said, "That was great! You can play in the park now." Lily and Max had a fun day at the park. They played and laughed, and Max was not scared anymore. They knew that their important walk was a big success.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Mimi. Mimi liked to play with a long tube that she found in the house. She would jump and roll around with the tube all day. One day, Mimi saw a little mouse inside the tube. The mouse was scared and wanted to go home. Mimi wanted to help the mouse. She thought of a plan to release the mouse from the tube. Mimi pushed the tube gently with her paw. The mouse slid out of the tube and ran back to its home. Mimi was happy that she could help the mouse. From that day on, Mimi and the mouse became good friends, and they played together every day.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with blocks. They had many blocks of different colors and shapes. They could make a stack of blocks as high as they wanted. One day, they decided to have a contest. Who could make the highest stack of blocks? They each picked a spot on the floor and started to build. They used big blocks and small blocks, round blocks and square blocks. They tried to balance their stacks carefully. "Look at my stack!" Lily said. "It is very high and pretty. I have red blocks and blue blocks and yellow blocks." "Mine is higher and prettier!" Ben said. "I have green blocks and purple blocks and orange blocks." They kept building and building, until their stacks were taller than them. They were very proud of their stacks. They looked at each other and smiled. But then, a playful cat came into the room. The cat saw the stacks of blocks and thought they were toys. The cat jumped on one of the stacks and knocked it over. The blocks fell to the floor with a loud noise. "Hey, that's my stack!" Lily cried. "Bad cat! Go away!" The cat did not listen. The cat ran to the other stack and pushed it with its paw. The blocks fell to the floor with another loud noise. "Hey, that's my stack!" Ben cried. "Bad cat! Go away!" The cat did not listen. The cat was having fun. The cat played with the blocks on the floor, rolling them and chasing them. Lily and Ben were very sad. They had worked hard to make their stacks. They had lost their contest. They looked at the cat and frowned. Then, they looked at each other and shrugged. They realized that the stacks were not that important. They were just blocks. They could make new stacks anytime. What was more important was that they were friends. They decided to forgive the cat and join the fun. They picked up some blocks and threw them gently at the cat. The cat caught them and threw them back. They laughed and played with the cat and the blocks. They had a good time. The moral of the story is: Don't be too attached to your things. They can be lost or broken. Be more attached to your friends. They can make you happy and help you. And sometimes, you can have fun with a playful cat.
