Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Pillow and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft pillow. This pillow was enormous! It lived in a little girl's room. The little girl loved her pillow very much. She would hug it and lay her head on it every night. One day, the little girl found a book. She wanted to read the book with her pillow. So, she sat down on the floor with her enormous pillow and opened the book. She looked at the words and tried to read them. As the little girl read the words, her pillow listened. They read the book together. The story was about a cat and a dog who were friends. The little girl and her pillow liked the story a lot. When they finished the book, the little girl was very happy. She hugged her enormous pillow and said, "Thank you for reading with me!" The pillow was happy too. From that day on, they read many books together and had lots of fun.

---

One day, a nice cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big case near a tree. Tim was very curious about the case. He thought, "I want to know what is inside." Tim went to his friend, a dog named Sam. He said, "Sam, I found a big case near a tree. Can you help me open it?" Sam was excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" They went to the case and tried to open it. It was very hard to open. Tim had an idea. "Let's jump on it!" he said. They both jumped on the case, and it opened! Inside, they found many nice toys to play with. Tim and Sam were very happy and played with the toys all day long.

---

Once upon a time, there was a wet cat. The cat was very sad because it was wet. The cat wanted to be attractive and dry. One day, the cat met a dog. The dog was very attractive and dry. The wet cat asked the dog, "How can I be attractive and dry like you?" The dog said, "You can replace your wet fur with dry fur." The cat thought this was a good idea. The cat replaced its wet fur with dry fur. The cat was happy and attractive. But then, the cat saw the dog playing in a puddle. The cat wanted to play too. The cat jumped in the puddle and got wet again. The cat learned that it was more fun to play and be wet than to be dry and not play. The moral of the story is that it is more important to have fun than to always look attractive.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to run and play with her friends in the park. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was the fastest. Lily was very excited and wanted to win. As they started the race, Lily ran as fast as she could. Her friends were close behind her. But then, Lily slipped on a wet leaf and fell down. She was sad, but she got up and kept running. She did not want to give up. Lily ran faster and faster, and soon she passed her friends. They all cheered for her. At last, Lily reached the finish line first. She felt the sweet taste of victory. Lily was so happy that she had not given up, even when she slipped.

Once upon a time, there was a monkey named Max. Max was always ready to play with his friends. One day, Max saw a little bird who looked sad. The bird let out a big sigh. Max asked, "Why are you sad?" The little bird said, "I lost my toy and I can't find it." Max wanted to help, so he said, "Let's look for it together!" They looked high and low, but they could not find the toy. The little bird sighed again, feeling very sad. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's ask our friends to help us look!" So, they asked all their friends to help. Together, they found the toy under a big tree. The little bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Max and friends!" Max was happy too. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can solve problems and make our friends happy.
