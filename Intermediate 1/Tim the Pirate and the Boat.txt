Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Pirate and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a pirate named Tim. Tim was a serious pirate. He liked to collect shiny things. He had a big boat and a funny hat. One day, Tim saw a shiny thing on an island. He said to his friend, "Look! A shiny thing! Let's go and collect it!" His friend said, "Yes! Let's go!" They went to the island and found the shiny thing. It was a big, shiny coin. Tim and his friend were very happy. They took the shiny coin back to their boat. They showed it to all their pirate friends. Everyone was happy because they had a new shiny thing to add to their collection. And from that day on, Tim and his friends kept looking for more shiny things to collect.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lonely boy named Tim. He had no friends to play with. One day, he found a small toy car. It could zoom very fast. Tim was very happy. Tim played with the car every day. One day, the car hit a big rock and broke. Tim was very sad. He wanted to heal the car, but he did not know how. Tim tried to fix the car with tape, but it did not work. He tried to ask for help, but no one knew how to heal the car. Tim was very sad and lonely again. The car never zoomed fast again.

One day, a big bossy cat saw a yellow ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball, so it ran to it. The cat hit the ball with its paw, and it rolled away. The cat chased the ball, having lots of fun. As the cat played, it heard a ring sound. The cat stopped and looked around. It saw a small bird near a bell. The bird said, "Can you help me ring this bell? I am too small." The bossy cat thought about it and said, "Okay, I will help you." The cat rang the bell, and something unexpected happened. The yellow ball grew bigger and bigger! The bird and the cat were very surprised. They played together with the big yellow ball and had lots of fun. The bossy cat learned that playing with friends was better than playing alone.

One day, a little angel was playing in the sky. She saw a big, rough rock. The angel wanted to make it pretty. She took some flowers and began to scatter them on the rock. A little bird saw the angel and said, "What are you doing?" The angel replied, "I am making this rock pretty with flowers." The little bird wanted to help, so it picked up flowers with its beak and scattered them too. Suddenly, the rough rock started to move. It was not a rock at all! It was a big turtle! The turtle said, "Thank you, angel and bird, for making my shell pretty." The angel and the bird were very surprised, but they were happy they could help the turtle. They all became good friends and played together in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to kick a ball. One day, Tom found a bag of oats. He had an original idea. He thought, "I can play with these oats!" Tom went to his friend, Sue. "Sue, let's play with the oats!" he said. Sue liked the idea. They started to kick the oats around. But soon, they noticed that the oats were making a mess. The ground was covered in oats. Tom and Sue felt bad. They knew they should not waste food. Tom and Sue decided to clean up the oats. They learned that it is important to respect food and not use it as a toy. They promised to only kick a ball from then on.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car. The car was very anxious because it had an exam today. The exam would show if the car could drive well. The car went to the exam place. A kind man talked to the car. He said, "Don't worry, little car. Just show me how you drive and you will do great!" The little car felt better. The car started to drive. It drove slow, then fast. It stopped and went again. The kind man smiled. The little car passed the exam! The car was so happy and not anxious anymore. It drove home to tell all its friends the good news.
