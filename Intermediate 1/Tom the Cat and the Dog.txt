Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tom. Tom liked to jump high and touch the ceiling. The ceiling was very hard, but Tom was strong. One day, Tom saw a big dog named Max. Max had a red ball. Tom wanted the red ball, so he tried to steal it. Max saw Tom and barked loud. Tom got scared and ran away. He jumped up and touched the ceiling. Max could not jump that high. Tom learned not to steal, and they became friends. They played with the red ball together, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there lived a tiny pink bird in a swamp. He was the only one of his kind and felt very lonely. Every morning, he tried to sing as loud as he could, but nobody seemed to hear him. One morning, the bird heard a strange sound coming from some bushes in the swamp. He hopped over and to his surprise, he saw a big pink elephant hiding there! The bird and the elephant looked at each other lovingly and smiled. The bird asked the elephant, “Why are you so quiet?” The elephant replied softly, “I am too shy to sing. I wish I could be brave like you.” The bird smiled and said, “All you need to do is try. Try singing, I promise you will be surprised.” The elephant closed his eyes and gave a huge bellow. To the bird's surprise, his song was beautiful and melodious! From that day onwards, the two friends sang together in the swamp every morning. Moral: All it takes is a little bit of courage to make something special. If you just try, you never know what can happen!

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She was very tired. She wanted to move her big chair to a new place. But the chair was too heavy. The queen needed help. The queen asked her friends to help her. They all tried to move the chair. But it was still too heavy. The queen was sad. Her friends were sad too. They all wanted to help the queen. Then, a little ant came. The ant said, "I can help you, queen." The queen was surprised. "How can a little ant help me?" she asked. The ant said, "I have many friends. We can all help you move the chair." The ant called all of his friends. They all worked together. They moved the chair to the new place. The queen was happy. The ant and his friends were happy too. They all helped the queen.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a rough toy bear. He loved his bear very much. One day, Tom and his bear went for a walk. They saw a big door. Tom was curious about what would happen if he opened the door. Tom knocked on the door. A kind old lady opened it. "Hello, little boy," she said. "What brings you to my door?" Tom asked her what would happen if he went inside. The old lady smiled and said, "You can come in and see for yourself." Tom and his bear went inside. They saw a room full of toys. Tom played with the toys for a while. Then, he remembered his rough bear. He felt bad for leaving his bear alone. So, Tom went back to his bear and gave it a big hug. The old lady saw this and smiled. She told Tom, "You have a good heart, little boy. It's important to care for your friends, even if they are rough and not as shiny as new toys." Tom learned a lesson that day. He always took care of his bear and his friends. The moral of the story is to love and care for your friends, no matter what they look like.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tom. Tom was a very happy cat who loved to play with his ball. One day, Tom saw a cell in the corner of his room. The cell was very small and had a tiny door. Tom went to the cell and tried to separate the bars with his paws. He wanted to see what was inside. But the bars were too strong. So, Tom thought of another way to separate them. Tom found a stick and used it to separate the bars. He pushed and pushed until the bars moved apart. Inside the cell, Tom found a small toy mouse. He was very happy and played with his new toy all day long. And from that day on, Tom and the toy mouse became best friends.
