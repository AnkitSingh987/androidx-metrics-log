Category: Intermediate
Title: The Beetle and the Bird

One day, a little beetle went for a walk. He was a fierce beetle with big strong legs. He liked to walk and see the world. But today, he was in a hurry to get home. He had a big surprise for his family. On his way, the beetle met a big bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Beetle! Can you help me? I need to find my nest." The beetle thought for a moment. He wanted to help the bird, but he also wanted to get home fast. Then, he remembered that helping others is important. So, he decided to delay his trip home and help the bird. The beetle and the bird looked and looked for the nest. Finally, they found it! The bird was so happy that he gave the beetle a ride home. When the beetle got home, he showed his family the big surprise. It was a beautiful flower he found on his walk. The beetle's family was so proud of him for being kind and helpful. The moral of the story is that being kind to others can bring you good things too.

Once upon a time, there was a big red jeep. The jeep loved to play in deep mud. One day, the jeep went to the park to play with his friends. Before he left, his mom told him not to forget his hat. The jeep said, "Okay, Mom!" and went to the park. At the park, the jeep played and played. He jumped in the deep mud with his friends. They had so much fun. But, the jeep forgot his hat at home. His mom would be sad. He felt bad and thought, "Oh no, what do I do now?" Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and saw the jeep was sad. The bird had found a hat on the ground. The bird gave the hat to the jeep. The jeep was so happy! He did not know it was his hat the bird found. He went home with a big smile. His mom was happy too. The jeep learned not to forget and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to slide down the big slide at the park. One day, Tom saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy car that Tom really liked. Tom thought about a trade. He had a toy truck that he could trade for Sue's car. He asked Sue if she wanted to trade toys. Sue said yes, and they both felt happy with their new toys. Later, Tom and Sue went to the slide together. At the top, Tom's toy car fell down the slide. It went very fast and hit a dead tree at the bottom. The tree shook and many leaves fell down. Tom and Sue laughed and played with their toys under the tree. They had a fun day at the park.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to mail a letter to his friend, Sam. He put the letter in a box and went to the mail place. Tim was very happy because he wanted to share a new language he learned with his friend. The language was fun and had many light words that made them both laugh. On the way to the mail place, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was very hungry and sad. Tim thought about the letter and the fun language. He wanted to help the dog, too. Tim had an idea. He took the letter out of the box and put some food for the dog in the box instead. He gave the box to the dog, and the dog was happy. Tim went home and told his mom about the dog. She was proud of him for helping the dog. Tim learned that it is good to share fun things with friends, but it is also important to help others when they need it. The next day, Tim and Sam played together and learned the new language. They also went to see the dog, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, in the deep dark forest, there lived a leopard. She lived happily in the forest and she wanted to keep it safe. One day, she heard something strange in the bushes. She crept forward and saw a struggling little creature. The little creature saw her and spoke. "Please help me, I am scared," she said. The leopard wanted to protect the little creature, so she said, "I will help you. Follow me and I will keep you safe." The leopard led the little creature to a hollow tree where it knew she would be safe. The leopard said, "I will come back tonight, but you must stay here until then." But that night, the leopard did not return. In the morning, the little creature went back to the bushes and saw the leopard lying dead on the ground. The little creature had a sad feeling in her heart. She knew that the leopard had lost her life protecting her and the forest. Even though the leopard was gone, the little creature was determined to struggle to keep the forest safe, just like the leopard had.
