Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car and the Hill

One day, a big car went to the fuel place. The car wanted to feel full and happy. The car talked to the fuel man. "I want fuel, please!" said the car. The fuel man put fuel in the car. The car felt good. Next, the car went to the park. There was a little bike there. The bike looked at the big car and said, "Wow, you are impressive!" The car said, "Thank you, bike!" They played together in the park. Then, something unexpected happened. The car and the bike found a big hill. The bike was scared, but the car said, "We can go up the hill!" They tried, but the car was too heavy. The little bike helped the car go up the hill. They were both happy and felt good.

Once upon a time, there was a big, fancy armchair in a little house. This armchair was very special. It could talk and nod its head. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room and saw the armchair. "Hello," said Lily. "Can you talk?" The armchair nodded its head. "Yes, I can talk," it said. Lily was so happy to have a new friend. They played games and told stories all day. Lily would sit in the armchair and they would laugh and have fun. At the end of the day, Lily said, "Thank you for being my friend, armchair." The armchair nodded and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I am always here for you."

Once upon a time there was a family of penguins. Every morning they would march to the edge of the sea and swim together. But one day something unexpected happened. One of the small penguins was being very selfish. He did not want to go in the sea with his family. His mother asked him “Why do you not want to march with us?” But the little penguin did not answer, he just kept standing there looking at the sea. His mother said “That’s enough now! You have to march with us!” and she gave him a gentle push. The selfish little penguin finally joined his family, and together they all marched through the sea, playing and having fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big test at school. He was scared because the test was very hard. Tim's mom gave him a hug and said, "You can do it!" On the day of the test, Tim found a big, shiny pen on the ground. It was an expensive pen. He thought the pen would help him do well on the test. But when he tried to use the pen, it did not work. Tim was sad and worried. He did not know how he would manage the test without the pen. Then, a little girl named Sue saw Tim and gave him her pen. Tim was happy and thanked Sue. They both took the test and did very well. Tim learned that he did not need an expensive pen to do well. He just needed a friend to help him. And that is how Tim and Sue became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She liked to talk to them and pretend they were real. She had a toy cat, a toy dog, and a toy bunny. They were all good friends. One day, the toy cat said, "I want a yummy treat." The toy dog said, "Me too!" But the toy bunny did not want a treat. This made the cat and dog sad. They wanted to find a way to make the bunny want a yummy treat too. Lily had an idea. She put her finger in some yummy jam and let the toy bunny taste it. The toy bunny said, "Wow, this is yummy! I want more!" The toy cat and dog were happy that the bunny liked the treat. They all had a fun time eating yummy jam together.

Once upon a time, there was a smelly bin. It lived in a small park. The bin was very sad because it was full of trash. The bin wanted to be clean and happy. One day, a kind girl named Lucy came to the park. She saw the smelly bin and wanted to help. Lucy started to remove the trash from the bin. She put the trash in a big bag. The bin felt better. After Lucy removed all the trash, the bin was clean and not smelly anymore. The bin was very happy. Lucy and the bin became good friends. They played in the park every day. And the bin was never smelly again.
