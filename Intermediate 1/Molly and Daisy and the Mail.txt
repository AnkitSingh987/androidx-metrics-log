Category: Intermediate
Title: Molly and Daisy and the Mail

Molly and Daisy were having a play date. Molly had a plan and she shouted to Daisy, "Let's go get the mail!" Daisy nodded her head and they ran to the mailbox. Molly opened the mailbox and pulled out all the mail. Daisy helped by putting the noisy envelopes in a neat stack. They looked through all the mail, but Molly was disappointed. "I wanted to find a surprise for us!" Daisy thought for a minute and then smiled. "Let's play house! I can be the mailman and you can be the person who gets the mail!" Molly thought this was a great plan and grinned. She ran inside to get some paper and envelopes. Daisy waited by the mailbox with a big smile on her face. Molly came back and handed Daisy some envelopes and a pen. She told Daisy to write something on each envelope and she would deliver them. Daisy loved the idea and started writing. Molly and Daisy soon had a stack of envelopes to deliver. Daisy put them in the mailbox and ran around the back yard, delivering the mail. Molly couldn't believe how much fun they had with just a simple plan.

Once upon a time, in a regular house, there lived a happy family. They had a big dog named Max. Max was a good dog who loved to play with his family. They would play in the yard and have lots of fun. One day, Max saw a big ball in the yard. He was so excited! Max ran fast to the ball and started to play with it. He played so hard that he accidentally knocked the ball into the house. The ball hit the house and made a loud noise. Max was scared! He didn't mean to destroy the house. The family came out and saw the mess. They were not mad at Max. They knew it was an accident. The family worked together to fix the house. Max helped too. He was very sorry. When the house was fixed, they all played together again. Max was more careful this time, and they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toy birds in the garden. They liked to make them fly and sing and pretend they were real. Ben had a blue bird and Lily had a pink bird. They were very happy. But then, a big wind came and blew their birds away. Ben and Lily ran after them, but they could not catch them. The wind took them far, far away, over the fence and into the woods. Ben and Lily were very sad. "Where are our birds?" Ben asked. "I don't know," Lily said. "Maybe they are lost." They looked at the woods and saw many trees and bushes and animals. They were scared. "Maybe there are bad things in there," Ben said. "Maybe they will hurt our birds." "No, no," Lily said. "Our birds are smart and brave. They will find a way back to us." They decided to wait and hope for their birds to come back. They sat on the grass and hugged each other. Meanwhile, their birds were flying in the woods. They were scared too, but they did not give up. They looked for a way back to Ben and Lily. They saw many things they did not know, like squirrels and owls and spiders. Some of them were friendly and some of them were not. One of them was a big black crow. He saw the toy birds and wanted to take them. He flew after them and tried to catch them. He was fast and strong and mean. "Leave us alone!" the blue bird said. "Go away!" the pink bird said. But the crow did not listen. He got closer and closer. He reached out his claws and grabbed the pink bird by the wing. He pulled hard and tried to separate her from the blue bird. "Help!" the pink bird cried. "Let her go!" the blue bird said. He flew at the crow and pecked him with his beak. He made a loud noise and scared the crow. The crow let go of the pink bird and flew away. He did not want to fight with the toy birds. He thought they were harmless, but they were not. The blue bird and the pink bird hugged each other. They were happy they were safe. They looked for a way back to Ben and Lily. They saw a bright light in the sky. It was the sun. They followed it and flew out of the woods. They saw the fence and the garden and Ben and Lily. They flew to them and landed on their hands. "Look, look!" Ben said. "Our birds are back!" "Yay, yay!" Lily said. "We missed you so much!" They kissed their birds and thanked them for being smart and brave. They played with them again and forgot about the big wind and the bad crow. They were very happy.
