Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy some cream for a cake. The store was big and had a lot of things. Lucy liked to look at all the things in the store. In the store, Lucy saw a dull toy. It was not fun to play with. She did not want the dull toy. She wanted a fun toy. But today, they were only there to buy cream. Lucy and her mom bought the cream and went home. They made a big, yummy cake. They put the cream on the cake. Lucy was happy because the cake was so good. She forgot about the dull toy and had fun eating the cake with her mom.

One day, a big, heavy bear named Ben went to visit his friend, a wide tree named Tim. Ben walked and walked until he found the wide tree. Tim was very happy to see his friend. "Hi, Ben!" said Tim. "I am happy to see you!" "Hi, Tim!" said Ben. "I am happy to visit you!" They played all day. They played hide and seek, and tag. The sun was very bright and they were very happy. When it was time to go home, Ben said, "Goodbye, Tim! I had fun today!" "Goodbye, Ben!" said Tim. "Come visit me again soon!" And with that, Ben walked back to his home, excited to visit his wide tree friend again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Bebe. Bebe was not feeling well. She needed medicine to feel better. Bebe went to her friend, a wise old owl. "I need medicine," said Bebe. The wise old owl gave her some medicine. "Take this, and you will feel better," said the owl. Bebe took the medicine and soon, she felt better. She was so happy that she sang a pretty song. All her friends heard her song and came to see her. They were happy that Bebe was feeling better too. And they all played together in the sunshine.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a friend named Lily. Lily was a cat. Spot and Lily liked to play together. One day, they played too much and Spot hurt his leg. Spot was sad and said, "Ouch, my leg hurts." Lily felt sorry for Spot. She wanted to help him feel better. She had an idea. Lily said, "I will give you a massage. It will make your leg feel good." Spot liked the idea. Lily used her paws to rub Spot's leg gently. Spot felt better and said, "Thank you, Lily. My leg feels good now." Spot and Lily were happy again. They played more, but not too much. They did not want Spot's leg to hurt again. In the end, Spot and Lily learned to play carefully. They were the best of friends, always helping each other when they needed it. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to a fancy place to eat with her mom. Sue was very excited to see the big menu with many yummy foods to pick. She looked at the menu and saw a lot of things she wanted to try. Sue's mom said, "Sue, you can only choose one thing to eat. Make sure you pick something you really like." Sue thought hard and picked a big, tasty burger. When the food came, Sue took a big bite and smiled. After they ate, Sue saw a sad boy sitting alone. She went to him and said, "Why are you sad?" The boy said, "I picked something I didn't like from the menu, and now I'm still hungry." Sue wanted to comfort the sad boy, so she shared her tasty burger with him. The boy was very happy, and they became good friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and share with others.

Lily and Ben like to play in the garden. They see many things that make them happy. They see flowers, birds, bees and butterflies. They also see ants. Ants are very small and busy. They work hard and carry things. One day, Lily and Ben find a big ant hill. They watch the ants go in and out. They see some ants with wings. They are the queen ants. They are very beautiful. They can fly and order the other ants. Lily and Ben want to be like them. They pretend to be queen ants. They make paper wings and stick them on their backs. They find some sticks and use them as crowns. They tell each other what to do. They order the flowers, the birds, the bees and the butterflies. They have fun. But they forget about the ants. The ants do not like Lily and Ben. They think they are mean and loud. They want them to go away. They bite their feet and legs. They make them itch and hurt. Lily and Ben cry. They run away from the ant hill. They take off their wings and crowns. They say sorry to the ants. They learn to be nice and quiet. They do not order anyone anymore. They play with the ants. They are friends.
