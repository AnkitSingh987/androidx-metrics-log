Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big friend named Tom. Tom was a very tall tree. Mia loved to play with Tom every day. One day, Mia was upset. She had lost her toy. She asked Tom, "Can you help me find my toy?" Tom wanted to help Mia. He sent a bird to find the toy. The bird flew high and low, looking for the toy. Finally, the bird found the toy near a small pond. The bird gave a wink to Tom and brought the toy back to Mia. Mia was so happy to have her toy again. She hugged Tom and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom was happy to help Mia and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell all the other animals what to do. One day, Tom saw a long line of ants. The ants were carrying food to their home. Tom wanted to gain more friends, so he decided to help the ants. Tom started to help the ants carry the food. The ants were happy to have Tom's help. The line of ants moved faster with Tom's big paws. The ants and Tom worked together and soon the food was at the ant's home. In the end, Tom was not bossy anymore. He learned that being kind and helpful was a better way to gain friends. The ants and Tom became good friends, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a girl called Holly. Holly loved life and was always so happy to see the world around her. One day, Holly was playing in the garden, when her grandmother came outside. Holly ran up to her and said, "Hello, nana! What are you doing here?". Her nana laughed and said, "Oh Holly, I have come to give you a special kiss. It's a kiss to say goodbye." Holly was surprised and asked, "Are you leaving me, nana?". Her nana hugged her and said, "Yes, for now. But don't worry, I'm always available for you and you can always find me if you need me." Holly hugged her nana tightly and said, "I'll miss you, nana. Thank you for the kiss." Then her nana smiled, kissed her one more time and left. Holly went back to playing but with a smile on her face, knowing that although her nana was not there, she was still available for her and would always be a part of her life.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a bossy cat and a little mouse. They were not friends, but they lived together. The cat always wanted to be in charge and tell the mouse what to do. The mouse did not like this, but he did not say anything. One day, the cat made some soup for dinner. The mouse smelled the soup and thought it smelled very good. The bossy cat told the mouse, "You can have some soup, but you must trust me and do what I say first." The mouse was hungry, so he agreed to do what the cat said. The cat told the mouse to go outside and bring back a big stick. The mouse did as he was told and brought back the stick. The cat then told the mouse to close his eyes and hold the stick tight. The mouse trusted the cat and did what he was told. But when the mouse closed his eyes, the bossy cat hit him with the stick and ate all the soup. The mouse was sad and learned not to trust the bossy cat again.

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming in the sea. He was very careless and did not look where he was going. He swam close to a big shark. The big shark saw Tim and wanted to eat him. Tim was scared and asked the shark, "Please don't eat me, Mr. Shark! I can support you if you are hungry. I can find food for you." The shark thought about it and said, "Okay, Tim. You can help me find food, but you must be careful." So, Tim and the shark swam together. Tim was not careless anymore. He looked around and found food for the shark. The shark was happy, and they became friends. From that day on, Tim and the shark helped each other and played together in the sea.
