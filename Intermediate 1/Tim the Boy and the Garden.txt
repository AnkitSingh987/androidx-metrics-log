Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Garden

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very unusual boy because he could talk to plants. Every day, he would go to his garden and talk to the plants in their own language. One day, Tim found a little seed. He thought it would be fun to plant the seed and watch it grow. So, he dug a small hole in the ground, put the seed in, and covered it with dirt. Every day, Tim would talk to the seed in its language, and it began to grow. The plant grew bigger and bigger, but it was not a nice plant. It had sharp teeth and was very mean. One day, the plant was so big and strong that it broke out of the garden. It went into the town and started to eat everything it saw. The people were very sad and scared. In the end, the town was destroyed, and it was all because Tim had grown the mean plant.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big box in his room. He was very happy and wanted to see what was inside. He opened the box and saw a robot. The robot was mild and friendly. Tim said, "Hello, robot!" The robot said, "Hello, Tim! I am your new friend. Let's play together." Tim and the robot played all day. They played with cars, balls, and blocks. They had lots of fun. At the end of the day, the robot had a surprise for Tim. The robot said, "I have something to show you." The robot opened a small door on its body and revealed a little toy car for Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, robot! You are the best friend ever!"

---

Sara was a little girl who liked to play with her dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily, a big doll with long hair and a pink dress. Sara liked to lift Lily and hug her and pretend she was a real lady. One day, Sara and Lily went to the park with Sara's mom. Sara saw a big slide and wanted to go on it. She asked her mom if she could take Lily with her. Her mom said yes, but be careful. Sara lifted Lily and climbed the stairs to the slide. She was very happy. But when Sara reached the top, she saw a big dog. The dog was barking and jumping and wanted to play. Sara was scared. She thought the dog would bite Lily or her. She held Lily tight and cried. Her mom heard her and ran to help her. The dog's owner also came and called the dog. He said he was sorry and that the dog was harmless. He just wanted to say hello. He said the dog liked dolls too. He showed Sara how the dog could lift a toy with his mouth and shake it. Sara saw that the dog was not mean, just playful. She stopped crying and smiled. She asked the dog's owner if she could pet the dog. He said yes, but be gentle. Sara reached out and touched the dog's fur. It was soft and warm. The dog licked her hand and wagged his tail. Sara laughed. She said the dog was nice and cute. She asked if the dog wanted to see Lily. The dog sniffed Lily and licked her too. Lily did not mind. Sara said they were friends. Sara lifted Lily and slid down the slide with her. The dog followed them and barked. Sara's mom and the dog's owner watched them and smiled. They were glad that Sara and the dog had fun. Sara said it was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot loved to help people. One day, Spot saw a man with a lot of luggage. The man looked tired and sad. Spot went to the man and wagged his tail. The man smiled and asked, "Can you help me with my luggage?" Spot barked happily and helped the man carry his luggage. They walked together, and the man was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Spot! You are a very smart and helpful dog!" Spot wagged his tail and felt proud. And they lived happily ever after.
