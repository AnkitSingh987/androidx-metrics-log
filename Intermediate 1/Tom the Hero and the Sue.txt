Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Hero and the Sue

Once upon a time, in a lovely little town, there lived a hero named Tom. Tom was a very nice and strong hero. He liked to help people and make them happy. One sunny day, Tom went for a walk. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tom wanted to help Sue. He said, "Don't worry, Sue. I will find your toy." Tom and Sue walked together. They looked under a big tree and behind a small rock. Then, they met a kind dog named Max. Max had Sue's toy in his mouth. Tom said, "Max, can you give Sue her toy back?" Max wagged his tail and gave Sue her toy. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom and Max. You are both my heroes!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome, Sue. We are happy to help!" And they all became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a sheep named Fluffy. Fluffy had a lot of soft wool. She loved to play with her friends, the dog and the cat. One day, the dog and the cat came to Fluffy. The dog said, "Fluffy, can we borrow some of your wool to make a warm bed?" Fluffy said, "Yes, you can borrow my wool. I have a lot, and I want to help you." So, Fluffy gave some of her wool to the dog and the cat. They made a warm bed and were very happy. They all played together and had a good time. But then, they saw a dead tree. The tree was sad because it had no leaves. Fluffy said, "Let's use some of my wool to make the tree happy, too!" So, they put the wool on the tree, and the tree looked pretty again. The moral of the story is, it's good to share and help your friends. When you share, everyone is happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little raft. The raft was very obedient. It always did what it was told to do. One day, the raft met a big number. The number wanted to play with the raft. The raft was happy to play with the number. They played and played all day long. The number and the raft became very good friends. They were always happy and had lots of fun together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw an expensive book about a comet. Tim loved to read about space, so he asked his mom, "Can I have this book, please?" His mom said, "That book is too expensive, but we can go to the library and find a book about comets there." At the library, Tim found a book about comets and started to read. As he read, something unexpected happened. The words in the book began to move and change. Suddenly, a little comet jumped out of the book! "Hi, I'm Comet," said the little comet. "I can show you all about comets in the sky." Tim and Comet became good friends. They had many fun days learning about comets and space. And Tim learned that sometimes, the best things in life are not expensive at all.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful shop. In the shop, there were many toys. One day, a girl named Lily went to the shop with her mom. They wanted to buy a toy for Lily's friend. In the shop, Lily saw a toy she liked. It was on display. "Mom, can I have that toy?" asked Lily. "No, Lily, we are here to buy a toy for your friend," said her mom. Lily was sad. She wanted the toy. So, when her mom was not looking, Lily took the toy. They went home. But when her friend saw the toy, she knew it was from the shop. She told Lily's mom. Lily's mom was very sad and angry. Lily had to go back to the shop and say sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce lion. The lion was going to help his friends, the mice. But they needed something to do it. So the lion went to find some steel. The lion had to look hard. He asked different animals if they had any steel to help his friends. Everyone said,"No!". Finally, the lion saw a turtle and asked it, "Do you have some steel to help my friends?" The turtle answered, "Yes I do! I have some steel to help your friends." The lion thanked the turtle and asked if he could borrow the steel. The turtle said yes. The lion used the steel to help his friends. The mice were so happy they cheered and cheered. The lion was so glad he helped his friends. The end.
