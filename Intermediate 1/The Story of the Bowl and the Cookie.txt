Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bowl and the Cookie

Once upon a time, a mommy was going to make cookies. She got out her bowl and put some flour in it. She stirred it around and then she said, "I need some milk to pour in it too." She took the milk and poured it into the bowl. Then she added some sugar, some eggs, and some butter and stirred it all together. Then mommy said to her little boy, "Do you want to help me make cookies?" The little boy said, "Yes!" Mommy asked him to pour some pitch into the bowl. He went to the cupboard and took out the pitch. He opened the pitch and carefully poured it into the bowl. The mommy smiled and said, "That's good. Now we need to mix everything together." They stirred it all together until it felt nice and thick. The little boy said, "Can I eat it now?" The mommy laughed and said, "No, we need to bake it first so it will taste good." They put it in the oven and it baked for a long time until it turned into nice, warm, yummy cookies. They both took out a cookie and the little boy said, "Yum! This tastes good!"

Once upon a time there was a chair. It was a noisy chair. It was noisy because it had things that moved in it. It included a swing and a squeaky wheel. The chair was very fun! A little girl named Sophie liked to play in it. She liked to swing back and forward in it and turn the squeaky wheel. It made her laugh. But there was one problem. The chair was too noisy. It made lots of noise when Sophie played with it. This was not good. Sophie's mum had an idea. She put some thick cushions around the chair to make it quieter. Now, it was less noisy and more fun for Sophie. She could play more quietly in her chair. The end.

Lily and Tom were playing with blocks in the living room. They liked to build towers and bridges and castles. Lily saw a red block and wanted to use it for her tower. But Tom had the red block and he did not want to share. "Give me the red block, Tom!" Lily said. "No, it is mine!" Tom said. They both pulled the red block and it flew out of their hands. It landed on the table where Mom had a candle. The candle was burning and it made the block catch fire. "Fire! Fire!" Lily and Tom shouted. Mom heard them and ran to the living room. She saw the burning block and the candle. She quickly grabbed a cloth and put out the fire. She was angry and scared. "What are you doing, Lily and Tom? You could have burned the house or your faces! You should never play with fire or candles!" Mom said. Lily and Tom felt sorry and scared. They did not mean to make a fire. They hugged Mom and said they were sorry. "I'm sorry, Mom. I wanted the red block," Lily said. "I'm sorry, Mom. I did not want to share," Tom said. Mom hugged them back and said she was glad they were not hurt. She said they should always be careful and listen to her. She said they should share and play nicely. She said she loved them. "I'm glad you are not hurt, Lily and Tom. You should always be careful and listen to me. You should share and play nicely. I love you," Mom said. Lily and Tom said they loved Mom too. They said they would be careful and listen to her. They said they would share and play nicely. They picked up their blocks and started to build again. They used the red block together. They were happy.

Once upon a time, in a small jungle, there lived a little girl named Lily. She loved to play hide and seek with her animal friends. One sunny day, Lily and her friends decided to play their favorite game. Lily started counting, "One, two, three, four, five..." As she counted, her friends ran to find good hiding spots. When Lily finished counting, she opened her eyes and started looking for her friends. She found the small bird in the tree and the little frog near the pond. As Lily looked for her last friend, the tiny mouse, she heard a voice she didn't know. "Hello, can I play too?" asked the voice. Lily looked around and saw a big elephant named Ella. Lily was surprised but happy to meet a new friend. "Yes, you can play with us!" she said. Ella joined the game, and they all had a great time playing hide and seek together. The small jungle was filled with laughter and fun as the new friends played all day long.
