Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the wild. The wild was full of big trees, pretty flowers, and fun animals. Tim loved to stare at the animals and watch them play. One day, Tim found a pan in the wild. The pan was round and shiny. He picked it up and took it home. Tim had a big idea. He wanted to make something fun with the pan. Tim filled the pan with water and put it in the sun. He stared at the pan and waited. Soon, the water got warm. Tim put his toy boats in the warm water and played all day. The pan made Tim very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who loved to play outside. He went out every day, running and laughing. One day, he got so caught up in playing that he forgot to come home in time. When he finally returned, he was wet from the rain and his mom was very mad. He regretted not coming home earlier. The little boy didn't want to go outside the next day because it was raining. He was so bored that he just wanted to stay inside and watch TV. But his mom said no. She showed him some fun things he could do inside and he was happy again. They spent the day painting and playing games. After that, the little boy remembered to come home when it was time. He never wanted to be wet and bored again!

Once there was a little girl named Mary. Mary loved to play with her dolls and she was very lively. One day, Mary was walking in her backyard when she spotted something strange. It was a big mark on the grass! She thought it looked funny and fun, so she ran over to see it. Mary was so excited. She got down on her knees and touched the mark. It felt rough and it was very cold. She couldn't understand what it was. Suddenly, Mary had a funny thought. She thought that maybe someone had made the mark to play a game. With that, Mary put her hands on it and started pushing. When she did, the mark moved! Mary couldn't stop smiling. She had found a lively game to play. She was so happy, she ran to get her dolls and began playing with the mark, pretending it was a magical creature. She thought it was the best game ever!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, harmless dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with all the kids in the town. One day, while playing, Spot found a hole that went underground. Spot was very curious and wanted to see what was inside. Spot went into the underground hole and found a small, scared rabbit. The rabbit was crying because it couldn't get out. Spot wanted to help, but he was too big to fit. He tried to shut the hole but couldn't. The kids heard the rabbit cry and came to see what was wrong. The kids worked together to help the rabbit. They used sticks and ropes to lift the rabbit out of the hole. The rabbit was very happy and thanked Spot and the kids. They all played together for the rest of the day. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can solve any problem.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The ball had a nose. One day, the ball wanted to teach its friend, a small blue ball, how to jump high. The big red ball with the nose tried to show the small blue ball how to jump. But the small blue ball did not jump high. The big red ball felt sorry for the small blue ball. The big red ball did not give up. It kept teaching the small blue ball how to jump high. Soon, the small blue ball learned to jump high too. They were both happy and played together all day.

One day, a little bird named Tim was flying in the sky. He saw a big, shiny ball. Tim wanted to keep it. He felt guilty, but he took it to his nest. In the nest, Tim's friend, Sam, saw the shiny ball. Sam said, "Where did you find this ball?" Tim said, "I found it in the sky. I wanted to keep it." Sam looked at Tim and said, "We should give it back." Tim and Sam flew back to the sky with the shiny ball. They saw a sad girl looking for her ball. Tim said, "Is this your ball?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" Tim and Sam felt happy for giving the ball back.
