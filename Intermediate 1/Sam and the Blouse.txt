Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Blouse

Sam was in the perfect blouse. He loved the bright colours and the soft feel of the fabric. But he wanted it to be even more special! "Mama," he cried, "I want to put glitter on my shirt!" His mama smiled and nodded her head. "Maybe you can succeed," she said. So with his mama's help, Sam carefully added a few sparkles to his blouse. He put his nose close to the fabric and breathed in deeply. He felt so proud and excited! The next day, Sam went to the school playground to show his new shirt to the other children. But when they saw it, they all ran away in fear. Surprised, Sam followed them with his eyes. He spotted one of his friends standing in front of a house. When he approached her, she gave a big smile. "Your blouse is perfect!" she exclaimed. Sam couldn't help but feel relieved and happy. His hard work had paid off! He was so glad that his blouse had succeeded in making his friends happy.

Lily and Ben went on a safari with Mom and Dad. They saw many big animals, like lions, elephants, and giraffes. Lily liked the giraffes best. They had long necks and spots. Ben liked the lions best. They had loud roars and sharp teeth. One day, they went on a jeep ride to see more animals. The jeep was bumpy and fast. Lily and Ben had to hold on tight. Lily had a camera. She wanted to take pictures of the animals. She saw a zebra and a monkey. She took pictures of them. But then, the jeep went over a big rock. Lily's camera flew out of her hand. She tried to catch it, but it was too late. The camera dropped to the ground. Lily was sad. She cried. She wanted her camera back. Dad stopped the jeep. He got out and picked up the camera. He checked it. It was not broken. He gave it back to Lily. He said, "Don't worry, Lily. Your camera is fine. You can take more pictures. But next time, hold on to it better." Lily smiled. She said, "Thank you, Dad. I'm sorry I dropped it. I will be more careful." She hugged her camera. She was happy again. They continued the jeep ride. They saw more animals. They had fun. But when the sun went down, it got dark. Lily and Ben were tired. They said, "Can we go back to the hotel? We want to sleep." Mom said, "Yes, we can. We had a long day. We saw many things. We will go back to the hotel and rest. Tomorrow, we will have another adventure." Lily and Ben nodded. They snuggled with Mom and Dad. They closed their eyes. They dreamed of the safari.

One day, a nice cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a top that reached the sky. Tom was very excite to climb the tree. As Tom climbed the tree, he met a bird. The bird said, "Hi, Tom! What are you doing?" Tom said, "I am climbing to the top. I want to see the sky." Tom and the bird became friends. They played in the tree all day. When the sun went down, Tom said, "I had a fun day with you." The bird said, "Me too, Tom. Let's play again tomorrow!"

Once upon a time there was a lady who loved to kick. Everywhere she went, she kicked. She kicked in the park, in the garden and even in the house. One day the weather was a bit strange. It was red outside and it felt different. The lady put on her shoes and decided to go outside to kick. The red weather was strange, but the lady kicked anyway. She kicked high, low, slow and fast. She was having fun! Suddenly, the lady heard a loud rumble. The red sky opened up and it started to rain. The lady raced back inside and said goodbye to her kicking fun. But she knew she'd come back soon to kick in the sun!

Once there was a noisy dog named Max. He liked to bark a lot. One day, Max heard a sound. It was an alarm. Max knew he had to provide help. Max ran to the house with the alarm. He saw a little girl outside. She was scared. Max barked and barked to let everyone know there was a problem. People came to see what was wrong. The people saw the alarm and the little girl. They knew they had to help. They turned off the alarm and made sure the girl was safe. Max had provided help by being noisy. Everyone was happy, and they thanked Max for being a good dog.
