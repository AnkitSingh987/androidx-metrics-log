Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Girl and the Fox

One day, a brave little girl named Daisy was walking in the forest. Suddenly, she saw a twig on the ground. As she touched it, it moved and she felt scared. “What was that?” asked Daisy. But then, an old fox came out of the bush. He was looking right at Daisy. “Don’t be afraid,” he said. Daisy was too scared to move, so she just kneeled down on the ground. The old fox smiled and said: “It was just a twig. It won’t hurt you. I want to help you.” Daisy felt better after she heard the fox's kind words. She thanked the old fox and decided to take the twig with her as a reminder not to be scared. She was no longer hurt, but brave.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a telephone in his house. He thought it was a silly toy to play with. He picked it up and started to pretend to talk to his friends. Suddenly, the telephone rang. Tim was surprised and answered it. A voice on the other side said, "Hello, is this Tim?" Tim replied, "Yes, who is this?" The voice said, "I am a silly monster, and I am coming to your house to make you weep!" Tim was scared and started to weep. He did not want the silly monster to come to his house. He told his mom about the call, but she did not believe him. She thought he was just being silly. Later that night, the silly monster came to Tim's house. Tim wept as the monster scared him and his mom. The monster made a big mess in their home and left. Tim and his mom were sad and had to clean up the mess. They wished they had not played with the telephone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to talk a lot. He talked to his friends, his toys, and his family. One day, Tim's mom told him they were going to watch a film. Tim was very excited because he loved films. Before the film started, Tim's mom said, "This film might be a little scary, but don't worry, I'll be right here with you." Tim was a brave boy, so he wasn't frightened. He was ready to watch the film with his mom by his side. As the film played, there were some scary parts. Tim felt a little frightened, but he remembered what his mom said. He talked to his mom about the scary parts, and she made him feel safe. In the end, Tim enjoyed the film and wasn't scared anymore.

Once there was a small worry. It lived in a tree and whispered in the wind. Every day it saw the birds soar and wished it could be free too. The worry was scared and wanted someone to talk to. One day, it heard a small voice below the tree. It was a little boy. The worry saw the boy's eyes and knew that he too had worries. The worry whispered to the boy, "You are not alone. I am here with you." The boy smiled and said, "Thank you". The little worry was so happy! It had found a friend. From then on, when the little boy had worries, the worry in the tree whispered to him and told him that he was not alone.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. He had a beautiful park near his house. The park had many trees, flowers, and a big cactus. The cactus was tall and green with lots of pointy parts. One sunny day, the wealthy man went for a walk in the park. He saw the big cactus and thought it was very pretty. He wanted to touch it, but he knew the pointy parts could hurt him. So, he just looked at it and smiled. As he walked in the park, he met a little girl. She was playing with a ball. The wealthy man and the little girl played together near the big cactus. They had lots of fun and laughed a lot. They became good friends and played in the park every day. And they always looked at the big cactus, but never touched it.
