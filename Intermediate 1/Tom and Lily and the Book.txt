Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Book

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the living room. There is a big armchair in the living room. The armchair is soft and cozy. Tom and Lily like to sit on the armchair and read books. One day, Tom and Lily find a new book on the armchair. The book has a picture of a lion on the cover. The lion looks strong and brave. Tom and Lily want to read the book. They both reach for the book at the same time. "Give me the book!" Tom says. "No, I want the book!" Lily says. They pull the book from each other. The book tears in half. Tom and Lily are sad and angry. They start to cry. Mom hears them cry. She comes to the living room. She sees the torn book and the twins on the armchair. "What happened?" Mom asks. "Tom and Lily tore the book!" They both say. Mom is not happy. She picks up the book and looks at it. She sees the title of the book. The book is called "How to Share". Mom smiles a little. She has an idea. She sits on the armchair with Tom and Lily. She gives each of them a half of the book. "Look, this book is about how to share. Sharing is when you let someone else have something you like. Sharing is nice and kind. Sharing makes you and others happy. Do you want to share?" Mom says. Tom and Lily look at each other. They feel sorry for tearing the book. They nod their heads. "Yes, we want to share." They say. Mom hugs them. She reads the book with them. They take turns to look at the pictures and the words. They learn how to share. They are happy. They love the book and each other.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their toys. They liked to pretend they were explorers, looking for treasure and animals. They had a big trunk that they filled with their things. The trunk was old and broken, but they loved it. One day, a mean boy came and kicked their trunk. He laughed and ran away. Lily and Ben were very sad. Their trunk was more broken than before. Some of their things fell out and got dirty. "Who will fix our trunk?" Lily asked. "We need it for our adventures." Ben thought for a moment. He remembered that his grandpa was good at fixing things. He was very dependable. He always helped them when they needed. "Let's go to grandpa's house," Ben said. "He can restore our trunk. He has tools and glue and paint." Lily nodded. She liked grandpa too. They picked up their things and carried their trunk to grandpa's house. Grandpa was happy to see them. He saw their trunk and smiled. "What happened to your trunk, my little explorers?" he asked. "A mean boy kicked it," Lily said. "Can you fix it, please?" "Of course I can," grandpa said. "I'll restore it to make it better than before. You can help me if you want." Lily and Ben were very happy. They helped grandpa fix their trunk. They cleaned it, glued it, painted it, and decorated it. It looked beautiful and strong. Grandpa gave them a hug and a kiss. "There you go," he said. "Your trunk is ready for more adventures. You are very brave and creative, my little explorers." "Thank you, grandpa," Lily and Ben said. "You are the best. We love you." They took their trunk and went back to the park. They continued to play and have fun. They were very proud of their trunk. They knew that grandpa was always there to help them. He was very dependable.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there was a tired tiger named Tim. He was tired because he had been playing all day with his friends. Tim was very good at jumping and running, but he wanted to try something new. One day, Tim saw a big bowl and many colors. He thought, "I will mix all the colors and make a new one!" So, he used his big paws to mix red, blue, and yellow. He mixed and mixed until he made a beautiful green color. Tim was so happy with his new green color that he painted his stripes with it. Now, he was a green tiger! All his friends loved his new look and they played together until the sun went down. Tim the green tiger was tired, but he was very happy with his new stripes.
