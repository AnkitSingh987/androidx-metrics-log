Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Dress

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very excited because her mom was making her a new dress. She loved dresses with lots of colors and pretty stitches. Lily wanted to help her mom make the dress, so she asked, "Can I help you, Mom?" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily! You can help me pick the colors and watch me sew the stitches." Lily was so happy to help her mom. She kneeled down next to her mom and watched as she sewed the dress with care. As they worked together, Lily learned about different stitches and colors. She felt proud to be a part of making her new dress. When the dress was finally done, Lily put it on and twirled around. She said, "Thank you, Mom! I love my new dress!" And they both smiled, knowing they made it together.

One day, a smart cat named Tom found a pretty cloth. He wanted to show his friend, a dog named Max. Tom ran to Max's house with the cloth in his mouth. He was so happy to show Max his new cloth. When Tom got to Max's house, he showed the cloth to Max. Max liked the cloth too. But Max wanted to keep the cloth for himself. Tom did not want to give the cloth to Max. They both wanted the cloth and didn't know what to do. Then, Tom had an idea. They could share the cloth and play together. Max liked the idea too. They both played with the cloth and had lots of fun. They were happy to share the cloth and be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball. One day, Spot saw a cat with a big ball. The cat was patient and did not want to share the ball. Spot was sad. Spot had an idea. He would yell to get the cat's attention. So, Spot yelled, "Hey, cat! Look at me!" The cat looked at Spot and the ball rolled away. Spot ran fast to get the ball. Spot was successful! He got the ball and was very happy. The cat was sad, but then Spot shared his new ball with the cat. They both played together and had fun. From that day on, Spot and the cat were good friends.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they decided to go to the valley near their house. The valley was full of colorful flowers, trees and birds. Lily and Ben loved to see all the different colors and hear all the different sounds. "Look, Ben, a yellow flower!" Lily said, pointing to a daisy. "It is so pretty!" "Yes, it is, Lily. And look, a red bird!" Ben said, pointing to a cardinal. "It is so bright!" Lily and Ben picked some flowers and made a crown for Lily and a necklace for Ben. They felt like a princess and a prince. They decided to skip around the valley and pretend they were in a fairy tale. They skipped and sang and laughed. "Skipping is so fun!" Lily said, feeling happy. "It is, Lily. And you are so fast!" Ben said, feeling proud. They skipped until they were tired and hungry. Then they sat under a big tree and ate some cookies and juice that they had brought with them. They shared their food and thanked each other. "This is the best day ever!" Lily said, smiling. "It is, Lily. You are the best friend ever!" Ben said, hugging her. They finished their snack and packed their things. They said goodbye to the valley and the flowers and the birds. They held hands and walked back to their house. They were happy and grateful for their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. He lived in a pond. The hippo liked to rise up from the water and look around. One day, he saw a fancy hat. The hat was on the ground near the pond. The hippo wanted to wear the fancy hat. The hippo tried to get the hat. He tried to use his nose to pick it up. But the hat was too far away. The hippo felt sad. He wanted the fancy hat so much. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the fancy hat into the pond. The hippo was so happy! Now, the hat was near him. He put the hat on his head. The hippo looked very fancy. He rose up from the water and showed his friends. They all clapped and smiled. The hippo was so proud of his fancy hat.
