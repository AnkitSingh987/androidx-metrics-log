Category: Intermediate
Title: Mum and the Shoes

Mum put on her shoes and took her scooter out of the cupboard. She was glad to see that it was still in perfect condition. "Do you want to help me remove the wheels?" she asked her 3-year-old daughter. The little girl nodded and together, they removed the wheels from the scooter. Mum gave her daughter the back wheel and asked her to put it carefully on the ground. The girl smiled and was glad that she had been able to help. They put the wheels back on the scooter and Mum said, "Let's take it for a ride!" The little girl was so excited, she couldn't wait to go out and ride her new scooter.

Sam liked to question everything. He always asked his mom and dad why, how, and what. Sometimes they answered him, and sometimes they said, "Not now, Sam, we are busy." One day, Sam saw his mom putting dirty plates and cups in the dishwasher. He wondered what the dishwasher did and how it worked. He wanted to ask his mom, but she was on the phone. So he decided to find out for himself. He waited until his mom went to the living room, and then he opened the dishwasher. He saw buttons and lights and a big door. He pushed a button and the door closed. He heard water and noise. He got scared and tried to open the door, but it was locked. He started to cry and bang on the door. His mom heard him and ran to the kitchen. She saw the dishwasher running and Sam inside. She pushed another button and the door opened. She took Sam out and hugged him. She was angry and relieved. "Sam, what were you doing in the dishwasher? You could have been hurt! That was very dangerous and naughty!" Sam sobbed and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I wanted to know what the dishwasher did. I was curious." Mom sighed and said, "I know, Sam, but you can't go inside the dishwasher. It is not a toy. It is a machine that cleans the dishes fast and hot. You can ask me questions, but you have to wait for the right time. And you have to listen to me when I tell you not to do something. Do you understand?" Sam nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I understand. I won't do it again. I love you." Mom smiled and said, "I love you too, Sam. Now let's go and have some cookies. And next time, you can help me load the dishwasher. But only when it is off."

Once upon a time, there was a big hill. On top of the hill lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was not very smart and did not know many things. People called him ignorant. One day, Tim met a kind bird. The bird said, "I can help you learn new things if you can manage to climb this hill." Tim wanted to learn, so he tried to climb the hill. Tim tried very hard and managed to climb the hill. The kind bird was happy and taught Tim many new things. Now Tim was not ignorant anymore. He thanked the bird for helping him. From that day on, Tim and the bird were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy dog with a fake tail. The toy's name was Spot. Amy loved Spot very much. They played together every day. One day, while playing, Spot's fake tail fell off. Amy was sad and cried. "Oh no, Spot's tail is gone!" she said. Her mommy heard her and came to see what was wrong. "Don't worry, Amy. I can fix it for you," said her mommy. Mommy took Spot and fixed his fake tail with some glue. She gave Spot back to Amy, and Amy was very happy. "Thank you, mommy!" she said. Now, Amy and Spot could play together again. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. He loved to play and laugh all day. One day, Tim's mom made a big waffle for breakfast. Tim looked at the waffle and smiled. He couldn't wait to eat it. But his mom said, "Wait, Tim! It is very hot. Don't touch it yet." Tim waited for a little while, but he really wanted to eat the waffle. He thought maybe it was not hot anymore. So, he touched the waffle. "Ouch!" he cried. The waffle was still hot, and it hurt his hand. Tim's mom heard him cry and came to help. She put a cold towel on his hand to make it feel better. Then, she said, "Now, let's wait a little longer for the waffle to cool down." Tim learned that he should listen to his mom and be patient. Finally, the waffle was cool enough to eat, and Tim enjoyed every bite.
