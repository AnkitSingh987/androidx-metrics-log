Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Stubborn Squirrel and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, lived a stubborn little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends, but he often lost his nuts. He would hide them in the ground, but forget where he put them. One sunny day, Sam met a wise old owl named Oliver. "Why do you always lose your nuts, Sam?" asked Oliver. Sam thought for a moment and said, "I don't know, I just forget where I put them." Oliver smiled and said, "Let me teach you a trick. When you hide your nuts, make a mark on the ground with your paw. That way, you will remember where they are." Sam liked the idea and thanked Oliver. From that day on, Sam used Oliver's trick and never lost his nuts again. He was very happy and played with his friends without worrying about his lost nuts. And the wild forest was filled with laughter and joy.

Tom and Lily are playing in the park. They like to run and slide and swing. They have fun with their friends. But then Tom falls from the slide. He hurts his arm. He cries and cries. Lily runs to him and hugs him. She says, "Don't cry, Tom. I am here. You will be okay." Lily calls for help. She sees a man with a phone. She says, "Please help. My brother is hurt. He needs a doctor." The man nods and calls an ambulance. Soon, the ambulance comes. It has a loud siren and a red light. The ambulance people are nice. They are careful with Tom. They put a bandage on his arm and give him a teddy bear. They say, "You are brave, Tom. You will be fine. We will take you to the hospital." Lily goes with Tom in the ambulance. She holds his hand and says, "I love you, Tom. You are my best friend. The doctor will make you better." Tom smiles and says, "I love you, too, Lily. You are my best sister. Thank you for helping me." They hug and kiss. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car was going fast. The car was going too fast. The car hit a tree. Crash! The car was a wreck. A little boy saw the wreck. He felt uncomfortable. He said, "Oh no! The car quit!" The boy wanted to help. He looked around for someone to help him. A big blue truck came. The truck driver saw the wreck. The driver said, "Don't worry, little boy. I will help!" The truck driver helped fix the car. The little boy was happy. The car could go again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was an honest girl who always told the truth. Lucy had a big diamond that she loved very much. The diamond was very pretty and shiny. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to decorate the house for a party. Lucy thought it would be fun to use her diamond to make the house pretty. She put the diamond on the table in the living room. The diamond sparkled and made the room look nice. Lucy's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw the diamond and said, "Wow, Lucy! Your diamond is so pretty!" Lucy was happy and said, "Thank you! I used it to decorate the house for the party." Tom and Lucy played together and had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore the world. One day she found a key in the park. She was so excited, because it was a magical key. The little girl wanted to find out what the key could do, so she went to an old tree and asked it if it knew anything about the special key. The old tree replied in a mild voice, "Yes, this key can open the door to a world of wonders. All you have to do is find the door and use the key to open it." The little girl was so happy and she thanked the old tree. She looked up and saw a door in the sky. She took the key in her hand, looked at it and said, "I love you, key!" The little girl used the key in the lock and it opened. She stepped through the door and explored the wonderful new world that awaited her. The end.
