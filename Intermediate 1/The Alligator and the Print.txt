Category: Intermediate
Title: The Alligator and the Print

Once upon a time, there was a little alligator. He was very silly, and made a lot of stupid choices. One day, he decided he wanted to learn how to print. He went to the library and asked the librarian how to do it. "It's easy," explained the librarian. "First, you turn on the computer. Then you press the print button. And finally, the printer will make a copy." The silly alligator followed the instructions. But when he pressed the print button, an big alligator came out of the printer! He was so shocked. "What happened?!" The librarian laughed. "That's what happens when you press print! You get a new alligator!" The alligator was embarrassed. He had been so stupid, he didn't realize what would happen. From that day on, he was much more careful when pressing buttons. And he never tried to print an alligator again!

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to count. He counted everything he saw. One day, Tom went to a shop to buy some food. He saw a big box of fish with a price on it. Tom wanted the fish, but he did not have enough money. Tom was sad that he could not buy the fish. He thought about what he could do. Then, he had an idea! He could count things for people and they could give him money. So, Tom went to his friends and asked if they needed help counting. His friends were happy to let Tom count for them. They gave him money for his help. Tom counted toys, shoes, and even how many steps it took to walk around the park. Soon, Tom had enough money to buy the fish at the shop. He was very happy and shared the fish with his friends. They all enjoyed the fish and thanked Tom for his help.

One day, a colorful dog went to the park. The dog was very happy. The dog had a number on its collar. The number was "5". The dog's owner, a little girl, played with the dog. Then, a big cat came to the park. The cat saw the colorful dog and the little girl. The cat wanted to play too. But the cat did not like the number "5". The cat wanted the number "3". This made the dog and the little girl sad. The little girl had an idea. She found a pen and changed the number "5" to a number "3" on the dog's collar. The cat saw the new number and was happy. Now, the colorful dog, the big cat, and the little girl all played together. They all became good friends.

Tim and Sam were brothers who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big tree with red fruits. They wanted to pick some fruits and eat them with cream. They climbed the tree and reached for the fruits. But the tree was not happy. It shook its branches and made a loud noise. Tim and Sam were scared and held on tight. They saw a big bird with sharp claws and a long beak. The bird was angry and wanted to eat the fruits too. It flew towards the tree and snapped at Tim and Sam. "Help! Help!" they cried. "Who will save us?" They heard a roar and saw a mighty lion. The lion was their friend. He lived in the forest and sometimes played with them. He heard their cries and came to help. He jumped on the bird and scared it away. He helped Tim and Sam get down from the tree. "Thank you, lion!" they said. "You are very brave and kind. You saved us from the bird." The lion smiled and said, "You are welcome, my friends. But you should be careful. Not all trees and birds are friendly. Some are dangerous and can hurt you. You should ask before you take something that is not yours." Tim and Sam nodded and said, "You are right, lion. We are sorry. We will not take fruits without asking again. We will share with you and the tree and the bird." The lion nodded and said, "That is good. Sharing is nice. Come, let us go to my den. I have some cream and honey. We can eat them together and have fun." Tim and Sam smiled and followed the lion. They learned a lesson. They learned to respect nature and to share with others. They were happy and grateful. They had a mighty friend who saved them.
