Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Magic and the Boy

One day, a boy went to the beach with his mom. The boy was eager to play in the sand. He saw a big anchor near the water. The boy said, "Mom, look at the big anchor!" The boy ran to the anchor and started to play. He tried to lift it, but it was too heavy. Suddenly, a crab came out from under the anchor. The boy was surprised and cried, "Oh no, a crab!" The crab looked at the boy and said, "Please don't cry. I am a magic crab. I can make a wish come true for you." The boy stopped crying and thought about his wish. He said, "I wish for a fun day at the beach with my mom." The magic crab clapped its claws, and the boy and his mom had the best day ever at the beach. They played in the water, built sand castles, and laughed together. The boy thanked the magic crab and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive box. Inside the box, there were many toys. One of the toys was a phone. The phone was small and blue. It was a toy phone, but it looked very real. One day, a little boy named Tim found the box. He was very happy to see all the toys. He picked up the phone and said, "Hello, who is this?" Tim pretended to talk on the phone with his friend, Sam. "Sam, I found a big box with many toys!" Tim wanted to take the phone with him everywhere he went. He tried to fit the phone in his pocket, but it was too small. He tried to fit the phone in his bag, but it was too full. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. Finally, Tim found a small box that was just the right size for the phone. He put the phone in the small box and carried it with him everywhere. Tim and the phone were very happy together, and they had many fun adventures.

One day, a big pillow and a small pillow were friends. They lived in a room. The big pillow was very wise, but the small pillow was ignorant. The small pillow did not know many things. The big pillow tried to teach the small pillow, but the small pillow did not listen. One day, they had a big problem. The bed they were on was very messy. The big pillow said, "We need to solve this problem. Let's fix the bed!" The small pillow did not want to help. The small pillow said, "No, I don't want to fix the bed. It's not my job!" The big pillow was sad, but it knew something would happen soon. Later, a girl came into the room. She saw the messy bed and decided to clean it up. She picked up the big pillow and said, "You are a good pillow. I will keep you." Then, she looked at the small pillow. She said, "You are a bad pillow. You did not help fix the bed. I don't want you." The girl took the small pillow away. The big pillow was sad to see the small pillow go, but it knew that the small pillow had to learn a lesson. The big pillow hoped that the small pillow would not be ignorant anymore and would learn to solve problems. The big pillow was happy that the bed was now clean and nice.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his toy car. The car needed a battery to go. Tim put the battery in the car and it started to move. He played with his car on the open grass, making it go fast and slow. A girl named Sue saw Tim playing with his car. She had a toy car too, but it was not moving. Sue went to Tim and said, "Can you help me? My car won't go." Tim looked at her car and saw that it needed a battery too. Tim requested his mom to buy a new battery for Sue's car. But when they put the new battery in Sue's car, something unexpected happened. Both cars started to move on their own, without anyone touching them. Tim and Sue laughed and chased their cars all around the park, having lots of fun.
