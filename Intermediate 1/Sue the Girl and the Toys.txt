Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a case with her favorite toys. She loved to play with them every day. One day, she met a boy named Tim who wanted to play with her toys too. Sue said, "Tim, if you want to play with my toys, you have to be polite and ask nicely." Tim thought about it and said, "Sue, may I please play with your toys?" Sue was happy that Tim was polite and said, "Yes, you can play with my toys." Together, they opened the case and released the toys. They played all day and had so much fun. Sue and Tim learned that being polite and sharing made playing together even better. From that day on, they became best friends and always remembered to be polite and share their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red couch. On this couch, there lived a happy cat named Fluffy and a jealous dog named Spot. They both liked to sit on the couch, but Spot always wanted to sit where Fluffy was sitting. One day, Fluffy was sleeping on the couch. Spot tried to ignore Fluffy, but he couldn't. He wanted to sit there too. So, Spot jumped on the couch, hoping Fluffy would move. But Fluffy didn't move! Instead, something unexpected happened. Fluffy woke up and looked at Spot. She didn't run away or hiss. Instead, she moved over and made room for Spot to sit. Spot was surprised. He sat down next to Fluffy and they both shared the couch. From that day on, they weren't jealous of each other anymore. They became best friends and always shared the big, red couch.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bob. Bob was a very good rabbit who always tried to behave. One day, he went to play with his friend, a bird named Sue. "Hi Sue," said Bob. "Let's play together, but we must behave, okay?" Sue agreed, and they started to play. They ran, jumped, and flew around, having lots of fun. As they played, a big, harsh wind came. It blew Sue up into the sky! Bob was scared, but he knew he had to save his friend. So, he jumped as high as he could, and grabbed Sue's wing. Together, they flew back to the ground, safe and sound. Sue was very happy that Bob saved her. From that day on, they knew they could always count on each other, no matter what. And they always remembered to behave, even when things got a little scary.

One day, a black cat was walking in the sun. The cat saw a big ball. The cat wanted to bring the ball home. The cat was very happy. But there was a thief. The thief wanted the big ball too. The thief tried to take the ball from the black cat. The cat did not want to give the ball. The black cat was very smart. The cat ran fast and the thief could not catch the cat. The cat brought the big ball home. The cat was happy and played with the big ball all day.

Lily liked to dance. She danced in the morning, she danced in the afternoon, she danced at night. She danced with her dolls, she danced with her dog, she danced with her mom and dad. She was happy when she danced. One day, Lily saw a bike with pedals. She wanted to try it. She asked her mom if she could. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful. Lily put on a helmet and got on the bike. She pushed the pedals with her feet. The bike moved. Lily felt the wind in her hair. She liked the bike. She saw a boy on the sidewalk. He was sad. He had no bike. He had no toys. He had no mom and dad. Lily felt sorry for him. She stopped the bike and got off. She walked to the boy and smiled. She said, "Do you want to play with me? You can have my bike. I can dance with you." The boy looked at Lily. He was surprised. He said, "Really? You are so generous. Thank you." He took the bike and put on the helmet. He pushed the pedals with his feet. The bike moved. He felt the wind in his hair. He liked the bike. Lily and the boy played together. They took turns on the bike. They danced on the sidewalk. They laughed and had fun. They became friends. They were happy.
