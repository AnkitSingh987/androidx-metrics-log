Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bench

Tim was a young boy who wanted a new friend to play with. He asked his mama if he could have a playdate with someone at the park near his house. Mama said yes, and Tim got very excited. Tim and Mama went to the park together and when they arrived, Tim could see a little girl sitting on the bench with her mama. Mama waved to the girl’s mama and the two moms agreed to let their children play together. Tim was so excited to meet the little girl. She had long brown curls and big brown eyes. She smiled at him and waved. So Tim waved back and signed ‘hello’. The girl smiled and signed back to him too. They quickly became best friends and played together for hours. Soon it was time to go but the girl was so sleepy she almost fell asleep. Her mama said goodbye to Tim and his mama and the two families said goodbye. Tim and his mama waved goodbye to his new friend and he couldn't wait to see her again. He knew they would have many more playdates and he’d have a new best friend to play with.

Lila and Ben are playing with blocks. They like to build towers and bridges and houses. Lila has a lot of red blocks and Ben has a lot of blue blocks. They share some yellow and green blocks. Lila wants to make a big tower with all the red blocks. She asks Ben to give her his red blocks. Ben says no, he needs them for his bridge. Lila gets mad and tries to take them from him. Ben holds them tight and says they are his. They start to pull and push and yell. The blocks fall down and make a loud noise. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. She sees the mess and the angry faces. She is not happy. Mom tells them to stop fighting and to yield. She says yield means to let go and to be kind. She says they have to share and take turns. She says they can use other blocks or other toys. She says they can also make something together. Lila and Ben feel sorry and say sorry. They hug and say they are friends. They decide to make a cell with the blocks. A cell is a small room with a door and a window. They pretend they are animals in the cell and make funny noises. They have fun and laugh. Mom smiles and says they are good kids. She gives them a snack and a drink. They eat and drink and feel happy. They also feel dizzy. Dizzy means their head spins and they feel silly. They lie down on the floor and close their eyes. They rest and dream.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a favorite seat in his room. It was a dull, brown chair. But Tom loved it very much. One day, Tom's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the dull seat and said, "Tom, why do you like that seat? It's so dull and not fun." Tom smiled and said, "Sam, this seat may be dull, but it's special to me. My mom and dad gave it to me for my birthday." Sam sat on the dull seat and found it very comfortable. He said, "Tom, you are right. This seat is very nice. I'm sorry I called it dull." Tom and Sam played all day and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is: Don't judge things by how they look. Sometimes, the most simple things can be very special.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved herbs. One day, she went to the market to pick the perfect herb to bring home. She searched for what seemed like forever until she found just the one she wanted. The herb was bright and tight, so she proudly picked it up and took it home. At home, the girl was so eager to enjoy her special herb, so she thought of a special dish. She gathered ingredients from the kitchen and mixed some together and replaced a few with her new herb. When the dish was ready, she couldn't wait to try it. She tasted it and it was delicious. Excited to show off her wonderful dish, the girl invited all of her family. They all were eager to try the girl's special herb meal. Everyone tried it and they all agreed that the herb was the perfect replacement! The little girl felt proud of her dish and smiled happily. She knew that this herb would become a special part of her cooking forever.
