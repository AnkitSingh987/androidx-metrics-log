Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Reef

Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. Fin lived in a big reef with his family. The reef was very pretty, with many colors. But, there was a part of the reef that was dangerous. One day, Fin saw another fish, named Bubbles, swimming close to the dangerous part of the reef. Fin swam to Bubbles and said, "Bubbles, don't go there! It's dangerous!" Bubbles looked scared and said, "Thank you, Fin! I didn't know." Just then, Fin saw a big shark coming! The shark wanted to eat them. Fin and Bubbles swam fast to hide in the reef. The shark tried to follow them, but it got stuck. The shark rubbed against the reef, trying to get free. It hurt the shark, so it swam away. Fin and Bubbles were safe now. They learned that it's important to listen to friends and stay away from dangerous places. They swam back to their families, happy to be safe and together. And they promised to always help each other.

One day, a restless dog named Spot wanted to cook. He put on his top and went to the kitchen. He looked at the big pot and thought, "I will make the best soup ever!" Spot's friend, Cat, came over and saw Spot cooking. Cat asked, "What are you doing, Spot?" Spot said, "I am cooking soup, but I need more things to make it good." Cat looked worried and said, "But Spot, you are a dog. Dogs can't cook!" Spot did not listen. He put more things in the pot. Suddenly, the pot fell over and made a big mess. Spot and Cat looked at each other. They knew they had to clean up. After they cleaned, Spot said, "Next time, I will ask for help when I want to cook." Cat agreed and they both learned a lesson.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a fierce lion. The lion was very strong and liked to destroy things. One day, he found a path in the forest. The path led to a small house. In the house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily saw the lion coming down the path. She was scared but wanted to talk to the lion. So, she went outside and said, "Please, Mr. Lion, don't destroy my house." The lion looked at Lily and thought about it. He decided not to destroy her house. Instead, they became friends and played together on the path. The fierce lion learned that he didn't need to destroy things to be happy.

John was playing outside in the garden. It was hot, but he was having lots of fun. Suddenly he saw something move out of the corner of his eye. It was a big door! He ran over to have a closer look. Then, he felt a cool breeze coming from inside the door. John just had to have a look inside! He put his hands on the door and he could feel it was cold. John started to push the door, but it didn't open. Then, he put his foot on the door and he slid it open. He stepped inside and the coolness felt so nice. It had been so hot outside! John saw a slide! It was brightly coloured and it looked so fun. He ran over to it and he couldn't wait to give it a go. He put one foot at the top and the other at the bottom. John held onto the sides of the slide and he started to slide. He slid faster and faster and he laughed as he went. It felt like he slid forever! When John finally stopped, he was out of the door and back outside into the garden. He ran around and played for the rest of the day, feeling so happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the evening. He would dig in the dirt and get all dirty. One day, Tim found a big rock. He thought it was very heavy. He tried to weigh it in his hands. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Tim, why are you so dirty?" Tim showed her the rock and said, "Look, Mom! I found a big rock. It is heavy!" His mom looked at the rock and smiled. "That's a nice rock, Tim. But it's time to go inside and get clean." Just then, something unexpected happened. The rock started to wiggle! Tim and his mom watched as the rock turned into a friendly little creature. The creature said, "Thank you for finding me, Tim! I was lost, but now I can go home." Tim and his mom were very surprised, but they were happy to help the creature find its way home. And from that day on, Tim always looked for more magical friends in the dirt.
