Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Drawing

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to read books and draw with her crayons. One day, while she was drawing, she heard a noise. It made her feel frightened. She went to her mom and said, "Mom, I heard a noise and I am scared." Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. It's just the wind outside." But the noise came again. This time, it was louder. Lily and her mom went to see what it was. They found a big, mean monster in the room where Lily was drawing with her crayons. The monster looked at them and growled. Lily and her mom tried to run away, but the monster was too fast. It caught them and took them away to its dark cave. They never saw their home or crayons again, and they were always frightened.

Tim and his dog Rex like to swim in the pond. They go there every day after school. Rex is a loyal dog. He always follows Tim and does what he says. One day, Tim sees a big mixer near the pond. It is making a lot of noise and dust. Tim is curious. He wants to see what it is doing. He tells Rex to stay and walks towards the mixer. Rex is worried. He does not like the mixer. He thinks it is dangerous. He barks at Tim, but Tim does not hear him. Rex decides to follow Tim. He does not want to leave him alone. Tim gets closer to the mixer. He sees that it is digging a hole in the ground. He wonders why. He does not see the wire that is on the ground. He steps on it and falls. He hurts his leg and cries. Rex hears Tim cry. He runs to him and licks his face. He tries to make him feel better. He also barks at the mixer, hoping it will stop. He is a loyal dog. He will not let Tim get hurt. The mixer driver hears Rex bark. He looks out and sees Tim and Rex on the ground. He stops the mixer and gets out. He runs to Tim and Rex and helps them. He says he is sorry. He did not know they were there. He says he will take them to the doctor. Tim and Rex are glad. They thank the mixer driver. They go to the doctor and get better. They still like to swim in the pond, but they stay away from the mixer. They are happy and loyal friends.

Molly was an obedient girl. She was always asked to help Mama cook. Mama said that cooking was an important job and only the most obedient children could help. Molly was so excited that she could! Mama said it was time to cook a meal. Molly followed Mama to the kitchen. Molly watched as Mama gathered all the ingredients from the cooler. She watched as Mama measured and stirred the ingredients in the pot. Molly wanted to help too. She helped stir and fold the ingredients into the pot. When the meal was finally done, it smelled so good! Mama put the delicious meal she and Molly cooked in the cooler. Later, everyone would enjoy the meal for dinner. Molly was so proud. She knew that she had been an obedient helper and the meal was sure to be delicious.

One day, a dog named Max went for a walk. Max was a very glad dog. He loved to hold a stick in his mouth and play with it. Max also liked to run and jump all day long. As Max was running, he felt a small pain in his heel. Max stopped and looked at his heel. He saw a tiny rock stuck in it. Max was sad and didn't know what to do. He sat on the grass and tried to take the rock out. Just then, a friendly bird saw Max and flew down to help. The bird picked up the rock with its beak and pulled it out of Max's heel. Max was so happy and thanked the bird. The bird was happy too, because it was the rock's shiny color that made it special. The bird took the shiny rock to its nest, and Max and the bird became best friends.
