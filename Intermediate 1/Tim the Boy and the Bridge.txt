Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bridge

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big board in his yard. He wanted to play with it. He put the board on two rocks to make a bridge. Tim was happy to walk on the bridge. As Tim walked on the board, he did not see a wet spot. He slipped and fell. Tim felt sorry and sad because he got hurt. He cried a little, but then he got up. Tim learned that he should be careful when playing. He wiped the wet spot off the board so he would not slip again. Tim was happy to play safely and not get hurt. The moral of the story is to always be careful when playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Hope. She lived in a small house with a nice family. Hope was a very useful dog. She liked to help her family with many things. One hot day, Hope saw a big cat in her yard. The cat was mean and wanted to take Hope's toys. Hope did not want the cat to take her toys, so she barked loud at the cat. The cat hissed at Hope and they began to fight. Hope tried her best to make the cat go away, but the cat was too strong. In the end, the cat took Hope's toys and ran away. Hope was very sad and wished she could have been more useful to protect her toys. The family tried to cheer her up, but Hope still missed her toys.

Once upon a time there was a cute little bear. He wanted to go on an adventure, but he was feeling tired. So, he decided to nap in a nearby tree. The little bear was deep in sleep when suddenly, he heard a loud squawking noise. 'What on earth could that be?' he thought to himself. He opened his eyes and saw a big, angry crow walking towards him. The crow spoke in a deep voice, “This is my tree. You must leave right now!” The little bear was scared so he quickly jumped off the tree. He tried to run away, but the tire tires made it hard for him. The crow chased after him and got closer and closer. Just then, the little bear remembered his nap. He yawned and started to feel more awake. He used all his energy to run faster, making it just far enough away from the crow. He made it back to his den and was able to have a peaceful nap. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a long, pretty dress. Lily loved to play games with her friends. One day, they decided to have a fun quiz. They wanted to see who knew the most about their favorite animals. Lily's friend, Tom, asked her a question. "What animal has a long neck and eats leaves?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "A giraffe!" Tom clapped his hands and said, "Good job, Lily!" They all laughed and played the quiz for a long time. After the quiz, Lily saw a box with a lock. She wanted to see what was inside. Her friend, Sam, had a key. He gave it to Lily, and she used it to unlock the box. Inside, they found a big, soft ball. They all played with the ball, and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a furry bird named Bob. Bob liked to soar high in the sky. He loved to feel the wind in his feathers. But there was one problem: Bob was scared of the big tree. One day, Bob found a helmet on the ground. He put the helmet on his head. It made him feel brave. He thought, "Now I can fly near the big tree and not be scared!" Bob started to soar near the big tree. He felt happy and strong with his helmet. But then, a mean squirrel came out of the tree. The squirrel tried to take Bob's helmet away! Bob did not want to lose his helmet. He flew faster and higher, away from the mean squirrel. The squirrel could not catch him. Bob felt proud and safe with his helmet. From that day on, Bob soared high in the sky with his helmet, not scared of the big tree or the mean squirrel. He was a brave, furry bird who loved to fly.
