Title: Mama and the Park

Mama was so excited as she put on her pink dress. She was about to go on a picnic with her three year old daughter, Amber. Amber twirled around, showing off her yellow dress. "Are you ready to go, sweetheart?" asked Mama. Amber nodded her head. So, the two of them set off, packed a picnic, and walked to the park. Once they arrived, they started to spread out the blankets. Amber saw a swing and said, "I want to swing!" Mama said "OK, have fun!" Amber ran over and jumped on the swing. She pushed herself higher and higher, feeling the wind in her hair. She sang a little song, and then decided to try something daring. As Mama watched her swing, Amber jumped off at the peak of her swing, giving her a hard shock. Mama tried to stay calm and said, "Oh no! That was a spoil! You must be more careful next time, Amber." Amber nodded, looking a little dull. Mama made sure that she was feeling ok, and then they continued their picnic. Amber was more careful about her surprises after that!

It was a sunny day. Ben and his mum were walking down the road. Ben asked, "Where are we going, mummy?" His mum smiled and said, "We're going to take a surprise." Ben was excited. He asked, "What is it?". His mum replied, "We'll soon find out! Let's keep walking." They kept walking until they reached the end of the road. There, Ben saw a broken box. His mum said, "Go on, Ben. Open it." Ben opened the box carefully. Inside, he saw a broken toy car. Ben was so excited. He shouted, "Wow! It's a car!" His mum revealed, "This car is yours. You can fix it up and play with it." Ben was so happy. He thanked his mum and hugged her. He wanted to play with his new car as soon as possible. He took the car and walked back home with his mum. It was a special day for Ben.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, heavy box that he needed to move. He tried to push it, but it was too heavy for him. Tim was sad because he could not move the box. Tim's mom saw that he was sad and wanted to help. She had an idea! She went to get some tape. She put the tape on the box and made handles for Tim to hold. Tim was happy to see the tape on the box. Tim's mom said, "Now you can supply the box to your room." Tim held the tape handles and tried to move the box again. This time, the box moved! Tim was very happy. He moved the box all the way to his room. Tim's mom was proud of him.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park with her mom. The sky was gloomy, and it looked like it might rain. Lucy saw a big ice-cream truck and wanted to get some ice-cream. Her mom said no, but Lucy did not want to refuse. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big, happy dog ran up to Lucy and licked her face. She forgot all about the ice-cream and started to play with the dog. The dog's owner, a kind old man, came over and said, "I see you like my dog, Fluffy. Do you want to play with her?" Lucy was so happy to play with Fluffy that she didn't care about the ice-cream anymore. The gloomy day turned into a fun day at the park. And the best part was, Lucy made a new friend in Fluffy the dog.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They wanted to buy some regular milk. Tim saw a milk bottle with a label on it. He could not read the label, so he asked his mom, "Mom, what does this label say?" His mom looked at the label and said, "This label says it is regular milk, Tim. This is what we need." Tim was happy to find the right milk. He put the milk in the cart and they went to pay. On the way home, Tim started to think. He asked his mom, "Mom, how did you read the label?" His mom smiled and said, "When you get older, you will learn to read labels too. I will teach you, Tim." Tim was excited to learn and couldn't wait to grow up.
