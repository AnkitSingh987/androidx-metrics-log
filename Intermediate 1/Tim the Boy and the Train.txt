Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Train

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy train that he loved to play with. One day, Tim's train needed coal to go fast. He asked his mom if he could get some coal for his train. His mom said, "Yes, I will allow you to get some coal." Tim went to the store with his mom to get the coal. But when they got there, they could not find any coal. Tim felt frustrated. He really wanted his train to go fast. Finally, a kind store helper saw Tim and his mom. The helper showed them where the coal was. Tim was so happy! They bought the coal and went home. Tim put the coal in his train, and it went very fast. Tim played with his train all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play baseball. He had a fragile ball that he took with him everywhere. Tim would throw the ball and run to catch it. He was very good at baseball. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He wanted to play baseball with his friends. Tim threw the fragile ball very far. He did not understand what would happen next. Instead of his friends catching the ball, a big dog ran and caught it in his mouth. Tim was surprised! The dog was now part of their baseball game. The big dog was very good at playing baseball, too. Tim and his friends had so much fun with their new friend, the big dog.

Once upon a time, there was a big bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to dance all day. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Tom wanted to catch Jerry for dinner. Tom chased Jerry around the house. Tom tried to dance to catch Jerry, but Jerry was too fast. Jerry ran under the table, and Tom bumped his head. Tom was very mad. Tom chased Jerry into the kitchen. Jerry found a big pot and hid inside. Tom could not find Jerry, but he was very hungry. Tom saw the big pot and thought it was his dinner. Tom ate the hot soup inside the pot. It was too hot, and Tom burned his tongue. Tom cried, and Jerry laughed. Tom did not have a good day.

Tim and Jen were twins who liked to play with toys. They had many toys, but their favorite was a big plane that could fly in the air. They took turns to make the plane zoom and swoop and loop. They had fun with the plane every day. One day, a new boy came to their house. His name was Rob and he was their cousin. He wanted to play with them, but he did not like the plane. He said the plane was boring and regular. He wanted to play with something else. He grabbed the plane from Jen and threw it on the floor. The plane broke into pieces. Tim and Jen were very sad and angry. They cried and yelled at Rob. They wanted their plane back. They tried to fix the plane, but it was too broken. They could not make it fly anymore. Rob did not care. He laughed and said he did not like the plane anyway. He went to play with his own toys. Tim and Jen did not want to play with Rob anymore. They did not like him. They felt lonely and hurt. They missed their plane. They hugged each other and trembled. They wished Rob would go away. They wished they had their plane back. They wished they could have fun again. But they could not. Their plane was gone and their day was ruined.

Once there was a little boy named Bob who loved blueberries. One day, he asked his mom if he could go out and pick some from the blueberry field. So his mom put on her hat and took Bob to the field. When they got there, Bob said, "Mom, I see something unusual!" His mom smiled and said,”What is it, sweetie?” Bob pointed to a big, fat bee hovering over a blueberry bush. He said, “That bee is feeding on the blueberries!” His mom nodded and said, “That’s right. It looks like the bee is enjoying a yummy snack!” Bob was so excited, he started picking berries and eating them right away. After a while, he noticed that the bee had gone. Bob asked his mom, “Where did the bee go?” His mom smiled and said, “You’ll find out soon enough.” The next day, when Bob went to pick some blueberries, he saw the bee again! This time, the bee had lots of baby bees with it, and they were all feeding on the blueberries! Bob was thrilled and said to his mom, “This is so unusual and cool!” His mom smiled and said, “Yes, it is, isn’t it?”
