Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rock and the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. It was a fun rock to play with. Many kids liked to play on the rock. They would climb and jump on it. The rock was very happy to have so many friends. One day, a healthy little boy came to play with the rock. The boy was strong and fast. He loved to run and jump. The rock knew that the boy would do something special. The rock was excited to see what would happen. The boy played with the rock for a long time. He climbed to the top and jumped off. He was so happy and had so much fun. Then, the boy had an idea. He ran and jumped from the ground onto the rock. The rock was so proud of the boy. The rock knew that the boy was special. And they had fun together forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Beaky. Beaky lived in a tree. He loved to sing when the sun came up. But, his friend, Bunny, liked to sleep late. One morning, Beaky sang a delicate song to wake up his friend Bunny. Bunny woke up and said, "Beaky, please don't sing so early. I want to sleep more." Beaky felt sad. He did not want to make his friend unhappy. So, Beaky and Bunny talked. They wanted to settle their problem. They thought and thought. Then, Beaky had an idea. He said, "I will sing a soft song for you, Bunny. This way, you can sleep more and I can still sing in the morning." Bunny liked the idea. From that day on, Beaky sang a soft song every morning. Bunny could sleep more and they both were happy. Beaky and Bunny stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a tall stack. It was so tall that it reached the sky! Every day, a little girl would come to the stack and use it. She would use a stick to climb up the stack, and sometimes she would even use rocks to build a path to climb higher and higher. The little girl would use her imagination and pretend the stack was a castle, and she was the brave princess who lived there. She would stack up the rocks, pretending they were walls around the castle, to keep her safe. When she was finished, the little girl would take one more look at her amazing tall stack. Then she'd climb down, heading back home. She knew that tomorrow she would come back and use the tall stack again!

One day, a zebra named Zara wanted to go on a trip. She had to pack her bag. She put her favorite toy, a ball, and some food in it. Zara thought her bag was perfect. Zara met her friend, a bird named Bob. "I am going on a trip," said Zara. "Can I come too?" asked Bob. "Yes, let's go!" said Zara. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. When they got to the tree, something unexpected happened. The tree started to talk! "Hello, Zara and Bob," said the tree. "I know a fun place to go. Climb up and I will take you there." Zara and Bob climbed the tree and had a great trip. They were very happy.

One day, a little frog named Tim hopped to a big tree. The sun was hot, and Tim felt thirsty. He saw a red cup with water near the tree. Tim tried to reach the cup, but it was too high. A big bird named Sue saw Tim. Sue asked, "Do you need help?" Tim said, "Yes, please. Can you get the cup for me?" Sue flew up and used her beak to snap the cup. She brought it down to Tim. Tim drank the water and felt better. He said, "Thank you, Sue!" Sue smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Let's play together!" They played and had fun all day, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a pirate. He had a big hat and a long, black coat. One sunny day, the pirate found a big ice cream cone. It was his favorite, chocolate flavor. He was so happy! But the sun was very hot. The pirate's ice cream started to melt. It dripped down his hand and made a big mess. The pirate felt embarrassed. He didn't want his friends to see him with a messy ice cream. So, the pirate ran to find some shade under a tree. He ate his ice cream very fast, so it wouldn't melt anymore. Then, he cleaned his hand and his coat. Now, the pirate was not embarrassed. He smiled and went back to play with his friends.
