Category: Intermediate
Title: The King and the Crown

Once upon a time, there was a king who had a big, shiny crown. The king was very happy with his crown. One day, he went out to play in the mud with his friends. They had a lot of fun, but the king's crown got filthy. The king's friend said, "Oh no, your crown is dirty!" The king did not want to listen. He chose to ignore his friend. He just wanted to keep playing in the mud. Later, the king went back to his castle. His mom saw the filthy crown and said, "You need to clean your crown before dinner." The king finally listened and cleaned his crown. It was shiny again. The king and his friends had a big dinner and they all laughed about the fun day in the mud.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a smelly table. The table was very old and had a lot of stains on it. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the house. She saw the smelly table and said, "Yuck! This table is smelly!" Lily wanted to make the table clean and nice. She got a cloth and some soap. She started to scrub the table. As she was cleaning, she saw something under the table. She bent down and discovered a small, red ball. "Wow!" she said, "I found a ball!" Lily played with the ball and had a lot of fun. She forgot about the smelly table. When her mom came home, she saw the clean table and was very happy. "Good job, Lily!" her mom said. They both played with the red ball and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big room with lots of toys and junk. Her favorite toy was a pink teddy bear. She loved the bear so much and always played with it. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came to play. They had lots of fun playing with all the toys. But then, Tim accidentally tore the pink teddy bear's arm. Lily was very sad and cried. Tim said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't mean to tear your teddy bear." Lily's mom heard them and came to help. She took the pink teddy bear and sewed its arm back on. The bear was as good as new. Lily was happy and hugged her mom. She also forgave Tim. They learned that it's important to say sorry when you make a mistake and to forgive others when they say sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big party coming up. She wanted all her friends to come. But she had a problem. She needed to give them an invitation to tell them about her party. Lily felt troubled. She did not know how to make the invitations. She asked her mom for help. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can make them together." They got paper, colors, and glue. They made pretty invitations for all her friends. Lily was happy. She gave the invitations to her friends. They all came to her party. They had lots of fun. They played games, ate cake, and laughed a lot. Lily's troubled face turned into a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell everyone what to do. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little mouse named Tim. One day, Tom found a big box. He wanted to fit inside the box, but it was too small for him. He asked Tim for help. Tim said, "I can fit in the box, but you are too big, Tom." Tom felt sad because he wanted to fit in the box too. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's find a bigger box!" They looked and looked, but they found zero big boxes. Finally, they found a big box that was just right. Tom could fit in the big box, and he was very happy. Tom said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." Tom was not bossy anymore, and they played together in the big box all day long.
