Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Tree

One day, a little dog went out for a walk. It was cold outside, but the dog did not care. He liked to walk and play. The dog saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. He was not scared. As the dog climbed the tree, he heard a snap. He did not know what it was. He kept climbing higher and higher. The dog was very high up now. He started to feel scared. The snap sound was louder now. The dog was too scared to climb down. He cried for help, but no one heard him. The snap sound got louder and louder. The branch he was on broke. The dog fell down and got hurt. It was a bad ending for the little dog.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird. The bird had pretty white feathers. It lived in a big tree near a small pond. The bird liked to fly and play with its friends. One day, the bird met a small, sad fish. The fish said, "I am sad because I cannot fly like you." The bird wanted to help the fish, so it had an idea. "I will fill a big leaf with water and carry you in it, so you can see the sky," said the bird. The bird found a big leaf and filled it with water. The fish swam into the leaf. The bird picked up the leaf with its beak and flew up into the sky. The fish was so happy to see the sky and trees. They both laughed and played together all day. The bird and the fish became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a jolly little town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to solve problems. One day, while playing in his yard, he found a big root. The root was in the way of his play area. Tim thought hard about how to solve the problem. He got a shovel and dug around the root. He pulled and pulled until the root came out. Now, Tim's play area was free of the big root. He was very happy and played all day long. His friends came over and they all had a fun time together. And that's how Tim solved the root problem in his jolly little town.

Once upon a time there was a very small, lonely girl named Jane. She lived all alone in a tiny house far away from everyone else. One day she decided to take a long walk in the park to try and find someone to play with. As she walked she listened to the birds singing and the wind rustling through the trees, but no one was around. She kept walking, but eventually the sky became dark and Jane felt very scared and alone. She stopped and said to herself, “I'm lonely and sad.” Suddenly, she heard a voice. “Hello Jane! Don't be scared. I'm here to take care of you.” Jane looked up and saw an old woman with a kind face. The old woman said, “My name is Mrs. Jones, and I know what it's like to be lonely. Here, take this for your heart.” Mrs. Jones opened her hand and in it was a small heart shaped locket. At that moment, Jane felt like her heart was no longer so lonely. She thanked Mrs. Jones, gave her a big hug, and she closed the locket around her neck. Since then Jane has been visiting Mrs. Jones every week and they play, talk and laugh until it's time to go home.

Sara and Ben were twins who liked to help their mom with the market. They each had a basket to carry fruits and vegetables. Mom told them to be careful and not drop their baskets. One day, they saw a big balloon in the sky. It was red and yellow and blue and green. It was impressive. Sara and Ben wanted to see it better. They ran after the balloon, forgetting their baskets. "Look, look, the balloon is so pretty!" Sara said. "Yes, yes, let's follow it!" Ben said. They did not see the big dog that was chasing them. The dog was hungry and angry. It saw the baskets with food. It jumped and bit the baskets. The fruits and vegetables fell out. The dog ate them all. Mom came looking for Sara and Ben. She saw the dog and the empty baskets. She was sad and angry. "Sara, Ben, where are you?" she shouted. Sara and Ben heard mom. They stopped running and turned back. They saw the dog and the baskets. They were scared and sorry. "Mom, mom, we are here!" they cried. Mom came to them and hugged them. She was glad they were safe. But she was also disappointed. "Sara, Ben, why did you drop your baskets?" she asked. "We saw a balloon, mom. It was impressive. We wanted to see it better. We forgot our baskets." they said. Mom shook her head. "Sara, Ben, you were not careful. You did not listen to me. You wasted our food. You made the dog happy and us sad. You were foolish." she said. Sara and Ben felt ashamed. They knew they did wrong. They learned a lesson. The moral of the story is: Do not let your curiosity make you forget your duty.
