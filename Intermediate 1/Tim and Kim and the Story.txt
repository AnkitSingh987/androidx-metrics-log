Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Kim and the Story

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play with their toys and run in the park. But sometimes they get tired and need a nap. One day, after lunch, Mom says, "Tim and Kim, it's time for your nap. Go to your room and lie down on your beds. I will read you a story." Tim and Kim nod and go to their room. They have cool beds with colorful sheets and pillows. Mom sits on a chair and reads them a story about a dragon and a princess. Tim and Kim listen and close their eyes. Soon, they fall asleep and have a dream. In their dream, they are the dragon and the princess. They fly in the sky and see many things. They see clouds, birds, mountains, and rainbows. They have fun and laugh. But then, they hear a loud noise. It is Mom. She says, "Tim and Kim, wake up. Your nap is over. It's time to get up and play." Tim and Kim open their eyes and see Mom. They smile and hug her. They tell her about their dream. Mom says, "That sounds like a cool dream. I'm glad you had a good nap. Now, let's go and have a snack." Tim and Kim jump out of their beds and follow Mom. They are happy and ready to play. They love their nap and their dream.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was an elderly spider. The spider was very old and wise. He lived in a corner of the room, where he made a big web. One day, a little girl saw the spider and was scared. She went to her mom and asked her to explain about the spider. Her mom told her not to be scared, because the spider was friendly and would not hurt her. The little girl listened to her mom and went back to the spider. She said hello to the elderly spider and they became friends. From that day on, the little girl was not scared of the spider anymore. They lived happily together in the small house.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to be organized. She had many cups in her room. One day, Sue saw a new cup on the table. She wanted to enter her room with the new cup. Sue picked up the cup and walked to her room. She was happy that she had a new cup. She put the cup on her shelf with her other cups. Sue looked at her cups and smiled. They looked so organized and nice. But then, something bad happened. Sue's cat jumped on the shelf. The cat knocked down all the cups. They fell and broke on the floor. Sue was sad. Her organized cups were gone.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock in a small room. The clock was very loud. It made a harsh sound. The sound went tick-tock, tick-tock all day and all night. Everyone in the house could hear it. One day, a little girl named Lucy went into the room. She looked at the clock and said, "Clock, why are you so loud?" The clock did not say anything. But, Lucy knew something was not right. She felt that soon, the clock would do something. Later that day, Lucy was playing with her toys when she heard a new sound. She went back to the room and saw that the clock was now quiet. A friendly face had appeared on the clock. The face said, "Thank you, Lucy, for talking to me. I will not be so loud anymore." Lucy was happy and played in the room with her new friend, the clock.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot liked to read books. He had a big book about a cauliflower. One day, Spot went to the park to read his book. He saw a big cauliflower growing in the garden. Spot was very happy to see a real cauliflower. Spot tried to pick the cauliflower, but he was so clumsy. He fell in the mud. Spot laughed and went home to read his book about the cauliflower. He learned that cauliflowers are good to eat. Spot had a fun day at the park with his book and the cauliflower.
