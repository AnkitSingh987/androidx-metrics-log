Category: Intermediate
Title: Penny the Pink Pig and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a pink pig named Penny. Penny had a big jar of coins. She liked to count and measure her coins every day. One day, she wanted to show her friend, Cat, how many coins she had. Penny went to Cat's house and said, "Look, Cat! I have a big jar of coins. I measure them every day!" Cat looked at the jar and smiled. But Cat had an idea. Cat wanted to take some coins from Penny when she was not looking. The next day, Penny went to play outside. Cat went to Penny's house and took some coins from the jar. Penny came back and saw that her coins were gone. She was very sad and cried. Cat did not say sorry, and Penny never found her coins again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He liked to help his dad in the garage. One day, his dad asked him to prepare some things. Tom was excited to help. Tom found a hammer on the shelf. It was not too big or too small. It was just right for his hands. He also found some nails and wood. The weather outside was mild, so they could work outside. Tom and his dad built a birdhouse together. They used the hammer and nails to put it together. When they were done, they hung it in a tree. They were very happy with their work.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big peach tree with many peaches. Tim wanted to get a peach, but they were too high for him to reach. He felt sad. A little bird saw Tim and flew down to speak with him. "Why are you sad?" asked the bird. Tim said, "I want a peach, but I can't get one." The bird thought for a moment and said, "I can help you. But first, you must promise to be kind and not call anyone stupid." Tim agreed, and the bird flew up to the tree. It picked a big, juicy peach and brought it down for Tim. Tim was so happy! He thanked the bird and promised to always be kind. From that day on, Tim remembered the bird's words. He never called anyone stupid and always tried to help others. And every time he ate a peach, he thought of the kind bird and the lesson it taught him.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went to play outside. He saw a big ball and decided to kick it. The ball went very far and landed near a little girl named Sue. "Hi!" said Tom. "Do you want to play with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes! I like to kick the ball too!" So Tom and Sue played with the ball all day. They kicked the ball high and low, and had lots of fun. After playing, they felt hot and tired. Sue's mom came outside with two cups of cocoa. "Here you go," she said. "Drink this to feel better." Tom and Sue drank the cocoa and felt cool and happy. They thanked Sue's mom and continued to play until the sun went down.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Penny. She was three years old and loved to explore the world with her friends. One day, Penny and her friends wanted to go out to eat. They asked Penny to lead them to the restaurant. Penny smiled and said, "Sure!" Penny lead them to a special restaurant with a big menu. Penny pointed to the menu and said, "Let's choose something healthy to eat!" All her friends agreed and chose a healthy option from the menu. After eating, they were all very happy. Penny had lead them to the perfect place to eat. She was so proud of herself for choosing the right restaurant.

Sarah was feeling helpless. She wanted to do something special for her mom, but she didn't know what. Then suddenly she had an idea. She ran to the kitchen and found a pan. She filled it with tiny stones and pretty things she had found in the garden. Then she grabbed some coloured paper and tissue and she began to wrap the pan. When her mother saw what Sarah had done she was so excited. "What a wonderful gift!", she said. Sarah smiled. She was so proud of herself. She had taken something small and made it into a big surprise for her mom. "Thank you so much!", said her Mom. "You have made me the happiest mom in the world." Sarah was so happy. She felt so proud and so helplessly thankful.
