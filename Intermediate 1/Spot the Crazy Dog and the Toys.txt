Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Crazy Dog and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a crazy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his toys. He had a big stack of toys in his house. One day, Spot decided to display his toys for all his friends to see. He put them in a line on the grass outside. He was very proud of his toy collection. All of Spot's friends came over to see the toys. They laughed and played with the toys all day long. Spot was so happy to share his toys with his friends. They all had a great time together.

Once upon a time, in a very organized town, there was a kind cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to talk to her friends. They all spoke the same language. One day, Kitty met a new friend, a dog named Doggy. Doggy did not speak the same language as Kitty. Kitty wanted to talk to Doggy, so she thought of a plan. She would request help from her wise friend, Owl. Kitty went to Owl and asked, "Can you teach me Doggy's language?" Owl said, "Yes, I can help you." Owl taught Kitty the new language. Kitty was very happy and thanked Owl. Now, Kitty could talk to Doggy in his language. They became best friends and played together every day. All the animals in the organized town were happy because they could talk to each other and be friends.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the mall with her mom. Mia was glad because she loved the mall. They went to buy a toy for her friend's birthday. Mia saw a big, red ball and wanted it for her friend. At the mall, Mia saw a funny man in a big hat. The man said, "Hi Mia! Do you want to see a trick?" Mia said, "Yes, please!" The man took the big red ball and made it vanish! Mia and her mom were very surprised. Then, the man in the big hat said, "Look behind you!" Mia and her mom turned around and saw the big red ball in a gift box. The man said, "Now you can give the ball to your friend!" Mia and her mom said, "Thank you!" They were very happy and went home with the gift.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. The sun was shining bright and warm. Tim was very happy. He saw a big tree and decided to sit under it. He felt the sun on his face and smiled. As Tim sat under the tree, he saw a small door on the tree. It was a little bit scary. Tim was brave, so he opened the door. Inside, he found a small room with a tiny bed and a little chair. He thought, "Maybe a tiny friend lives here." Tim went back outside and closed the door. He waited under the tree to see if the tiny friend would come home. The sun started to go down, and the woods became a little scary. Just then, a tiny squirrel came up to the tree. The squirrel opened the door and went inside. Tim was so happy to see the tiny friend. He knew that the little squirrel lived in the tree. From that day on, Tim would visit the squirrel in the woods. They became best friends, and the woods were not scary anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a smart boy named Tim. One hot day, Tim wanted to get some ice to cool down. So, he went to the big ice box in the kitchen. Tim saw his mom in the kitchen. He said, "Mom, can I get some ice?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. You can get some ice." Tim got the ice and put it in his cup. He felt very happy and cool. He shared the ice with his little sister. They both laughed and enjoyed the cold ice on a hot day.

Once upon a time, there was a cop. The cop was very nice. He liked to help people. One day, a cat disappeared. The cat's friend was worried. The cop saw the worried friend. He asked, "Why are you worried?" The friend said, "My cat disappeared." The cop said, "I will help you find the cat." The cop and the friend looked for the cat. They looked and looked. Then, they found the cat. The cat was hiding. The friend was happy. The cop was happy too. The cat did not disappear again.
