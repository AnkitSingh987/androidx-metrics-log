Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars and make a lot of noise. He would zoom the cars around the room and pretend they were racing each other. One day, Tim felt very tired. He decided to take a nap with his favorite toy car. While Tim was napping, his toy car started to talk! The toy car said, "I want to play a game. Let's number the other cars." Tim was so surprised that his toy car could talk. He rubbed his eyes, and said, "Okay, let's play the game." Tim and his toy car started to number the other cars. They counted one, two, three, and so on. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Tim's mom had dropped a plate in the kitchen. Tim and his toy car looked at each other and laughed. They knew it was just an accident. Tim wasn't tired anymore, and he was happy to have a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big cow named Bessie. Bessie loved to eat grass and make milk. One day, Bessie made some butter from her milk. She was very proud of her butter and wanted to share it with her friends. Bessie went to her friend, the little bird named Chirpy. "Chirpy, would you like some of my butter?" Bessie asked. "No, thank you," Chirpy said, "I cannot eat butter. My mom does not permit it." Bessie felt sad but understood Chirpy's mom's rules. As Bessie walked to find another friend to share her butter with, she saw a big shadow in the sky. It was a giant bird who wanted to eat Bessie's butter! Bessie was scared but then she had an idea. She called Chirpy for help. Chirpy flew up and talked to the giant bird. The giant bird agreed not to eat Bessie's butter and became friends with Bessie and Chirpy. From that day on, they all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, Jack was playing in the park. He saw a soldier walking by and Jack wanted to follow him. He quickly ran up to the soldier and asked, "Will you take me for a ride?" The soldier smiled and said, "Of course, let me tie my horse and we will go." Jack watched as the soldier tied the horse to a tree and thought it looked rather difficult. But the soldier did it with ease and soon they were on their way. They rode through the park and Jack felt very happy. But when they reached the top of the hill, he felt something strange. The soldier stopped and said, "Jack, this is the place." Jack looked around and asked, "What place?" The soldier replied, "This is the place I must leave you. This is where our journey must end." Jack's heart sank. He'd been so excited to follow the soldier and he didn't want the journey to end. But the soldier hugged him and said, "Don't worry, Jack. I'll be back someday." Jack waved goodbye to the soldier as he rode off. He knew it would be a long time before he saw the soldier again, but he was sure one day he would return.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat salt. Every day, he would put lots of salt on his food. Tim was very selfish and did not want to share his salt with anyone. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came to play. They played with toys and had fun. When it was time to eat, Sue saw Tim put lots of salt on his food. Sue asked, "Can I have some salt too?" Tim shouted, "No! This is my salt!" Sue was sad and did not want to play with Tim anymore. Tim saw that being selfish made his friend sad. He said, "I'm sorry, Sue. You can have some salt too." Sue was happy, and they shared the salt. From that day, Tim learned to share and was not selfish anymore. They played and ate together, and they became best friends forever.
