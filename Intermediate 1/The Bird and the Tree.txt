Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a bird in a cage. The bird was ready to fly. Every day, the bird looked out of the cage and saw the big sky. The bird wanted to start flying in the big sky. One day, the cage door opened. The bird was happy and flew away. The bird was flying high in the sky. The bird saw a big tree and sat on it. The bird was free and loved the big tree. But then, something unexpected happened. The big tree started to walk! The bird was surprised. The big tree was not a tree, it was a big green animal! The bird and the big green animal became friends. They played together every day. The bird was happy to have a new friend.

Anna and Ben are playing with their toys in the living room. Anna has a doll and Ben has a car. They like to pretend that the doll and the car are friends and go on adventures together. One day, Ben says to Anna, "I want to trade my car for your doll. My car is faster and cooler than your doll. It has more value." Anna thinks for a moment. She likes her doll very much. She says to Ben, "No, I don't want to trade. My doll is prettier and softer than your car. It has more value." Ben and Anna start to argue. They both think that their toy has more value than the other's. They raise their voices and increase their anger. They do not listen to each other. Mom hears them and comes to the living room. She sees them fighting over their toys. She says to them, "Stop, stop, stop! Why are you fighting? Your toys are both good and fun. They have the same value. You should share them and play nicely. That is more unusual and more value than fighting." Ben and Anna look at Mom. They feel sorry for being mean to each other. They say to Mom, "Sorry, Mom. You are right. We should share and play nicely. That is more unusual and more value than fighting." Mom smiles and hugs them. She says to them, "Good. I'm glad you understand. Now, why don't you make up a new adventure for your doll and your car? I'm sure they would like that." Ben and Anna nod and smile. They say to each other, "OK. Let's do that. Let's make up a new adventure for our doll and our car." They pick up their toys and start to play together. They are happy and friendly. They have more value than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a vendor named Tom. Tom was poor, but he was always happy. He sold toys to kids in the park. One day, a little girl named Lily came to his toy stand. She saw a pretty doll and wanted it. Tom saw Lily gaze at the doll for a long time. Tom wanted to give the doll to Lily, but he knew he needed money. He was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind lady came to the toy stand. She bought a lot of toys for other kids. She also bought the pretty doll for Lily. The lady was Lily's mom! Lily was very happy. Now, Tom was not poor anymore. He was able to buy more toys to sell. The kids in the park loved his toy stand. Tom was happy, and he always remembered the kind lady and little Lily. And from that day, Lily and her mom would always visit Tom's toy stand and buy more toys.

One day, a little boy was walking with his mom. He saw some friends playing together and he asked his mom to invite them to his house. His mom was worried, but he looked so happy that she said yes. At the house, the little boy's mom gave the friends some juice. They all laughed and had so much fun. When the friends left, the little boy was still smiling. He thanked his mom for inviting them over. His mom felt happy too, but she was still a little worried that the friends didn't have enough juice. But still, the little boy was so happy and excited about his play date that nothing could take away his joy. He was so happy that he had asked his mom to invite his friends over.
