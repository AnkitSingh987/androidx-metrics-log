Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the wet grass after it rained. One day, Tom met a little bird named Sue. Sue was sad because she couldn't play in the wet grass like Tom. Tom said, "Sue, why don't you imagine you're playing with me in the wet grass?" Sue closed her eyes and imagined she was playing with Tom in the wet grass. They laughed and played together. Sue felt happy because she could play with Tom in her mind. Tom felt happy too because he made a new friend. The moral of the story is that even if we can't do something, we can always imagine it and have fun with our friends.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toy fire truck in the garden. They liked to pretend they were firemen and rescue animals from danger. They had a stuffed bear, a plastic snake and a rubber duck as their friends. "Help, help!" Lily shouted, holding the bear. "My bear is stuck in the tree. He needs a fireman to save him." "I'm coming, I'm coming!" Tom said, driving the fire truck. He had a ladder and a helmet. He put the ladder against the tree and climbed up. He reached for the bear, but the ladder was too short. He could not get the bear. "Oh no, what can we do?" Lily asked, looking worried. "Maybe we can fix the ladder. We need something to make it longer," Tom said, looking around. He saw a broom leaning against the wall. He had an idea. He climbed down the ladder and took the broom. He tied the broom to the top of the ladder with a rope. He made the ladder longer. "Wow, you are so smart!" Lily said, clapping her hands. "Thank you. Now let's try again," Tom said, smiling. He climbed up the ladder again, with the broom. He reached for the bear and grabbed it. He brought it down and gave it to Lily. "Yay, you did it! You saved my bear!" Lily said, hugging the bear and Tom. "You're welcome. You are a good fireman, too," Tom said, hugging Lily. They were very happy. They decided to have a picnic with their friends. They had sandwiches, apples and juice. They shared their food with the bear, the snake and the duck. They thought it was delicious. They had a lot of fun. They loved being firemen.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He had a toy model of a big car. He loved the car very much. Every day, he played with it in his room. One gloomy day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. They played with the toy car together. As they played, Sam said, "I like your car, Tim! Can I pinch the wheels?" Tim replied, "Yes, you can pinch the wheels, Sam!" They pinched the wheels and laughed. They played with the car all day. When it was time for Sam to go home, they said goodbye. Tim was happy to have a friend who liked his toy car too.

Once upon a time, there was a little peanut. The peanut lived in a deep hole in the ground. One day, a big bird saw the hole and wanted to eat the peanut. "Yummy peanut, I want to eat you," said the big bird. The peanut was scared and did not want to be eaten. So, the peanut thought of a plan to stop the big bird. The peanut said to the big bird, "If you do not eat me, I will give you a big surprise." The big bird was curious and asked, "What surprise will you give me?" The peanut said, "I will give you a big, yummy cake." The big bird thought about it and agreed. The peanut was happy and the big bird did not eat the peanut. They became good friends and shared the big, yummy cake together. The peanut was safe and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a regular cat named Stitch. Stitch loved to play all day. He liked to run, jump, and chase his toy mouse. One day, Stitch was playing with his toy mouse. He threw it high into the air. But when it came down, Stitch could not find it. He looked under the table and behind the chair. Stitch was sad because he lost his toy mouse. Stitch's friend, a little girl, saw him looking sad. She wanted to help. They looked all around the house. At last, they found the toy mouse under a big box. Stitch was so happy to have his toy back. Now, he could play again. The little girl and Stitch played together for the rest of the day.
