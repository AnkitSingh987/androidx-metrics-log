Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tom found a long spear in the forest. He was very happy and took it home. He showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look, Sam! I found a long spear!" said Tom. Sam looked at the spear and wanted to play with it. Tom and Sam went to the park to play with the spear. They took turns throwing it and running to pick it up. They had a lot of fun. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. But then, Tom threw the spear too hard. It went very far, and they had to run to find it. When they found the spear, they saw that it had hit a big tree. The tree was very old and weak. It started to fall down. Tom and Sam tried to run away, but the tree fell on them. They were hurt, and the long spear was broken. The day was not fun anymore.

Lily liked to jog with her mom every morning. They would put on their shoes and hats and go outside. They would jog around the park and see the birds and the flowers. Lily felt happy and strong when she jogged. One day, Lily saw a big closet in the park. It was near the swings and the slide. It was brown and had a door with a lock. Lily was curious. She wanted to see what was inside the closet. She asked her mom, "Mom, can we look in the closet?" Her mom said, "No, Lily, we can't. That closet is not ours. It belongs to someone else. Maybe it has tools or toys or clothes. But we don't know. We have to respect other people's things. Come on, let's jog some more." Lily nodded, but she still wondered about the closet. She thought it looked interesting. She wanted to know what was inside. She decided to jog back to the closet when her mom was not looking. She waited for her mom to jog ahead of her. Then she ran to the closet and tried to open the door. But the door was locked. Lily could not open it. She pulled and pushed and kicked, but nothing happened. She felt frustrated and sad. She wished she had a key. She did not hear her mom calling her name. She did not see the man who came out of the bushes. He had a key. He had a big smile. He said, "Hello, little girl. Do you want to see what's in the closet?"

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a nest on a big tree. One day, Tim saw a fine twig on the ground. He thought it would be perfect for his nest. Tim flew down to get the twig. He saw a big cat sitting near the twig. The cat seemed nice, so Tim asked, "Can I take the twig for my nest, please?" The cat said, "Yes, you can have the twig." Tim was happy and took the twig to his nest. But when he got there, he saw the big cat had followed him. The cat jumped up and took Tim away. Now, Tim's nest was empty and he was gone.

One day, Daisy went to the beach and wanted to go for a swim. She ran to the shore and saw a tall wave. She stopped short, feeling scared at first. She wanted to see what was inside the wave, so she courageously took a few steps forward and reached out her arm. Suddenly, the wave started talking. "Hey there Daisy, do you want to come swimming with me?" it said with a friendly voice. Daisy was surprised, but decided it couldn't hurt to try. She smiled and said "Sure!". The wave then invited Daisy to climb on its back and take a ride. Daisy was a bit scared since the water looked so clean, but after some brief hesitation, she accepted the invitation. Daisy jumped on the wave and held on tight. The wave carried Daisy up and down the shore, like a rollercoaster! When Daisy finally came back to the shore, she felt excited and invigorated. She thanked the wave and waved goodbye to him. From that day onwards, Daisy was never scared of the beach again.
