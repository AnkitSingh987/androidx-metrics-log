Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Busy Ant and the Bob

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Tim. Tim liked to go on adventures. One day, he decided to go to a camp near his home. At the camp, Tim met a kind bee named Bob. "Hi, I'm Tim! Can I borrow some honey?" Tim asked. "Of course!" said Bob, "Here you go!" Tim was happy and thanked Bob for the honey. As they walked around the camp, Tim and Bob saw more friends. They played, shared, and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. Tim and Bob promised to meet again at the camp and have more adventures together.

Danny went to the store with his mom. He saw a big cactus and asked, "Mom, can I buy it?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can buy it." Danny was very excited and picked up the cactus. It was very tall and playful. He took it home and put it in his bedroom. He watered it every day and talked to it, too. Danny played games with the cactus. He pretended it was an airplane or a train. He imagined it flying or zooming around the room. Danny's mom smiled when she heard the noises of Danny playing with his cactus. One day, Danny took his cactus outside to play. He laughed and ran around the backyard, pretending he was a cactus too. His mom smiled, happy that Danny was so playful and imaginative.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big trophy in his room. It was shiny and pretty. He was so happy! He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house with the trophy. When Tim got to Sam's house, he showed Sam the trophy. Sam said, "Wow! That is a big trophy! Where did you find it?" Tim said, "It was in my room, just lying there!" They both looked at the trophy and smiled. But then, Sam felt frustrated. He wanted a trophy too. Tim saw that Sam was sad, so he said, "Don't worry, Sam! We can find a trophy for you too! Let's look in your room!" They went to Sam's room and looked everywhere. At last, they found a small trophy under Sam's bed. Now, both friends had a trophy and they were very happy.

One day, a sweet little girl named Lily went to the farm with her mom. She saw a big pile of hay and got very excite. Lily wanted to play in the hay and have fun. Lily said, "Mom, can I jump in the hay?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful." So, Lily jumped in the hay and laughed. She felt so happy to play in the hay. But, when Lily jumped, she found a small lost kitten in the hay. The kitten was scared, but Lily was gentle. She took the kitten to her mom, and they decided to take it home. The kitten was very happy, and Lily made a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a big class, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to tease his friends. He would laugh and make funny faces at them. His friends did not like it when Tom teased them, and they would get sad. One day, Tom and his friends went to the park. They saw a high tree with lots of pretty birds. Tom wanted to climb the high tree, but his friends said, "No, it's too high! You might fall!" But Tom did not listen, and he started to climb. As Tom climbed higher and higher, he started to get scared. He called out to his friends, "Help! I'm stuck!" His friends helped Tom get down from the high tree. Tom was so happy that he said, "I'm sorry for teasing you. I will be nice from now on." And they all played happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a famous structure. It was very big and tall. Many people liked to look at it. It was so special that it had a name. The name was Big Tall. One day, a little girl named Amy went to see Big Tall. She was so happy. She looked up and saw how tall it was. She wanted to touch it. Amy asked her mom if she could touch Big Tall. Her mom said yes. Amy walked up to Big Tall and touched it. She felt so small next to it. She knew she would remember Big Tall forever. She was so happy that she saw the famous structure with the special name.
