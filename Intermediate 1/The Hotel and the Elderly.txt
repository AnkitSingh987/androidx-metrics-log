Category: Intermediate
Title: The Hotel and the Elderly

Once upon a time, there was a big hotel. In the hotel, there was an elderly lady. She was very nice. The elderly lady liked to help all the people in the hotel. One day, a little girl came to the hotel. She was lost and did not know where her mom was. The elderly lady saw the little girl and wanted to help her. She said, "Don't worry, we will find your mom." The elderly lady and the little girl looked for the mom. They walked and walked. Then, the little girl saw her mom. She was so happy! But the elderly lady tripped and fell. She felt embarrass. The little girl and her mom said, "Thank you for helping us. We hope you are okay." The elderly lady smiled and said, "I am okay. I am happy that you found your mom." The little girl and her mom hugged the elderly lady. They were all happy in the big hotel.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a comfortable nest with his family. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One day, Ollie tripped and hurt his leg. He cried, "Ouch! My leg hurts!" His mom came running to help him. She looked at his leg and said, "Don't worry, Ollie. I will help you heal." Mom put a soft bandage on Ollie's leg to make it feel better. She told him, "You need to rest so your leg can heal." Ollie stayed in his comfortable nest for a few days. He was sad that he couldn't play with his friends. But soon, Ollie's leg started to feel better. He was so happy! His mom said, "See, I told you that you would heal." Ollie thanked his mom and went outside to play with his friends again. They all had a great time, and Ollie was careful not to hurt his leg again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a purple chimney. The purple chimney was very special because it was the only one in the town. Everyone loved to look at it. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to touch the purple chimney. He tried to climb up the house, but he fell and tore his pants. Tim was sad and started to cry. His mom heard him and came outside. She saw Tim's torn pants and gave him a hug. Together, they went inside to fix his pants. Tim learned not to climb on houses, but he still loved the purple chimney.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He liked to catch fish. One day, he went to the river to find fish. He saw a weird fish in the water. It was big and round. He wanted to catch it. The fisherman tried to catch the weird fish, but it was too fast. It would run away every time he got close. This made the fisherman sad. He really wanted to catch the weird fish. One day, the weird fish stopped running away. It came close to the fisherman. The fisherman was happy. But then, the weird fish started to talk! It said, "Please don't catch me. I am a magic fish. If you let me go, I will give you a wish." The fisherman was surprised. He let the weird fish go and made a wish. The fisherman and the magic fish became friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The car was very comfortable. It had a soft seat and a big wheel. The car liked to help people. One day, a boy named Tim needed to go to the park. He asked the car, "Can you lend me a ride to the park?" The car said, "Yes, hop in!" Tim got into the car and they went to the park. Tim had a lot of fun playing with his friends. When it was time to go home, Tim said, "Thank you, car, for lending me a ride. You are very comfortable!" The car was happy to help Tim. They went back home and Tim gave the car a big hug. They were the best of friends.
