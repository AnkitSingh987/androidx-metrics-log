Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Scarf

One day, a little girl named Lily found a scarf. It was a very pretty scarf. It had many colors on it. Lily thought that this scarf was powerful. She wanted to see what it could do. Lily put the scarf around her neck. She felt very warm and happy. She thought that the scarf made her powerful. She wanted to show her friends. So, she ran to find them. She told them all about her powerful scarf. Her friends were happy for her. They played together all day. They all took turns wearing the powerful scarf. It made them feel good. At the end of the day, Lily put the scarf away. She knew she would wear it again soon.

Janice was a little girl who loved to help her mom. One day Janice asked her mom, “What can I do?” Her mom said, “Can you serve up this lime?” So Janice took the lime and put it on the table. Janice was so proud of what she did, but suddenly she felt sad. Mom asked, “Are you ok?” Janice said, “I am a bit sad. I wanted to do more to help.” Mom smiled and said, “You did a great job serving the lime. You have helped me a lot!” Janice smiled and was happy again. She felt proud that she was able to help.

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a big fish. This fish had a tiny scale on its body. One day, the fish swam to a new place to settle. In this new place, the fish saw a small bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly high. The fish wanted to help the bird. The fish had an idea. The fish gave the bird its tiny scale. The bird put the scale on its wing. Suddenly, the bird could fly high! The bird was so happy. The fish and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who would jump all day long. His mom said to him, "Bunny, you need to stop jumping so much, or you'll get hurt!" But Bunny ignored her and kept jumping. Finally, his mom got angry and told him that if he didn't stop, he'd get a bad result. But Bunny just kept jumping. He was ignorant and thought nothing bad could happen. So he kept jumping, until finally one day he jumped too high and he fell really hard. He hurt his arm, and he started to cry. That was the result of his jumping. His mom was right! Now he knew that he should always listen to her, so that he would avoid bad results.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn fisherman. He lived near the sea. Every day, he went to the sea to catch fish. One day, he saw a big fish. He tried to catch it, but the fish was too fast. The fisherman did not give up. He kept trying. He wanted to talk to the fish, but fish can't talk. Then, something unexpected happened. The big fish stopped and looked at the fisherman. It opened its mouth and said, "Hello!" The fisherman was very surprised. Fish do not usually talk. The fisherman and the big fish became friends. They talked and played together every day. The stubborn fisherman was happy, and he learned that even fish can have something to say.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to help her mom with clothes. One day, Sue saw a small, guilty dog with dirty clothes. Sue wanted to help the guilty dog. She took the dirty clothes off the dog. Then, she washed the clothes with water and soap. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Sue put the clean clothes back on the dog. The dog thanked Sue for her help. Sue felt good because she helped the guilty dog. They became friends and played together every day.

One Sunday, a boy named Tim wanted to play with his big red ball. He tried to lift the ball, but it was hard. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me lift the ball?" Sam said, "Yes, I can help you!" They tried to lift the ball together, but it was still hard. They were sad because they could not play with the big red ball. Just then, Tim's big sister, Sue, came outside. She saw Tim and Sam looking sad. She asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim said, "We can't lift the big red ball. It's too hard." Sue smiled and said, "Let me help you." Sue helped Tim and Sam lift the big red ball. They were all happy and played with the ball together.
