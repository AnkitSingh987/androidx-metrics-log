Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben Find the Dog

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, they find a big box in the corner. They open the box and see many things inside. They see books, clothes, toys, and a frame. "What is this?" Anna asks, holding the frame. It is brown and has a glass in the middle. "It is a frame. You can put a picture inside," Ben says. He looks at the frame and sees a picture of a dog. The dog is black and white and has a big smile. "Wow, look at the dog! He is so cute!" Anna says. She likes dogs very much. She wants to keep the frame and the picture. "No, I want it! It is my frame!" Ben says. He likes dogs too. He grabs the frame from Anna. "Hey, give it back! It is mine!" Anna says. She pulls the frame from Ben. They start to fight over the frame. They tug and push and shout. The frame is hard and heavy. They are not careful. Suddenly, the frame breaks. The glass shatters and the picture falls out. Anna and Ben stop fighting. They look at the broken frame and the picture. They feel sad and sorry. They hear a voice from the door. It is their mom. "What is going on here? What is this mess?" Mom asks. She sees the broken frame and the picture. She smells something smelly. She picks up the picture and looks at it. She sees the dog. She knows the dog. "This is your grandpa's dog. His name was Spot. He was very old and smelly. He died last year. Your grandpa gave me this frame and picture to remember him. He loved Spot very much," Mom says. Anna and Ben feel more sad and sorry. They did not know the frame and the picture were so important. They did not mean to break them. They look at Mom and say, "We are sorry, Mom. We did not know. We were fighting over the frame. We both liked the dog. We wanted to keep it." Mom sighs and hugs them. She says, "I know you did not know. I know you liked the dog. But you should not fight over things. You should share and be nice to each other. You are twins. You are lucky to have each other." Anna and Ben nod and hug Mom. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We love you. We love each other. We will share and be nice." Mom smiles and says, "That's good. I love you too. Now, let's clean up this mess. And maybe we can find another frame and picture for you to keep. How about that?" Anna and Ben smile and say, "Yes, Mom. Thank you, Mom." They decide to help Mom clean up. They decide to be good twins. They decide to love the dog, even if he was old and smelly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a delicate toy computer that she loved to play with. Lucy liked to count all the colorful buttons on her toy computer. She would count them over and over again. One sunny day, Lucy went to the park with her toy computer. She was counting the buttons when a boy named Tim came up to her. Tim was not careful with things and grabbed Lucy's delicate toy computer. Lucy was scared that Tim would break her toy. But instead, Tim showed Lucy a new game on her toy computer. They both played the game together and had a lot of fun. Lucy learned that sharing her toy made her happy. The unexpected twist was that Tim did not break her toy but showed her a new way to play. From that day on, Lucy and Tim became good friends and always played together. And the moral of the story is, sharing can bring happiness and new friends.

Once upon a time, there was a duck. The duck was not like any other duck. This duck liked to eat prunes. The duck was trembling today because he was very scared. He noticed something dull in the pond. He wondered what it could be. He cautiously stepped closer and saw -it was a giant prune! He was so happy to find his favorite food. He grabbed the prune and started eating. Yum, it tasted so good! The duck was no longer trembling. He ate the prune and smiled. The End.
