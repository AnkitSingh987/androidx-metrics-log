Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Man and the Park

Once upon a time there was an old man named Bob. He was a very friendly man and always had a smile on his face. One day, Bob was walking down the street and he saw a lollipop on the ground. He thought it looked so delicious, so he picked it up. Bob went over to the park and sat down on a bench. He started to eat the lollipop. Suddenly, the sun was really bright and the heat was unbearable. Bob looked down and saw that his lollipop was melting! He started waving his arms around trying to make the heat go away. He looked up and saw a little girl standing in front of him. She asked him, “What’s wrong, old man?” Bob smiled at her and said, “My lollipop is melting and I can’t eat it now!” The little girl laughed and said, “Let me help! I can get some ice and put it on the lollipop so you can eat it.” Bob thanked the little girl and soon they were both enjoying the lollipop together.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy he loved a lot. One day, the toy broke, and Tim was very sad. He wanted to fix his toy, but he didn't know how. Tim went to his friend, Sam. Sam was good at fixing things. Tim asked, "Can you help fix my toy?" Sam looked at the toy and said, "I can try, but I need a tool from my house." Tim made a rude face and said, "No, fix it now!" Sam was sad and went home. Tim tried to fix his toy alone, but he couldn't. He was still sad and now had no friend to help him. The toy stayed broken, and Tim learned that being rude was not good.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a little soldier. This soldier was amazing. He could hop very high. One day, he went for a walk. As he walked, he met a bunny. The bunny said, "Hello, soldier! Can you hop like me?" The soldier smiled and said, "Yes, I can hop too!" The soldier and the bunny had a hopping race. They hopped and hopped. The soldier won the race. The bunny clapped for the soldier. They became best friends and hopped together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was a loyal dog who loved to play with his boot toy. One day, Max's best friend, a little girl named Lily, went to visit her grandma. While Lily was away, Max missed her a lot. He wanted to play with her, but he didn't know where she was. Max decided to look for Lily. He took his boot toy and went outside. On his way, Max met a mean cat. The cat wanted Max's boot toy. Max did not want to give it away. The cat tried to take the boot, but Max was brave. He barked loudly and scared the cat away. Max was very happy that he kept his boot toy safe. Soon, Lily came back, and they played together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy, but he was anxious about using the toilet. He was scared it might be hard to do. One day, his mom told him a secret. She said, "If you clap three times, a magic friend will come and help you." Tim thought about it all day. He wanted to try, but he was still scared. Then, he remembered the secret his mom told him. He went to the toilet and clapped three times. Just like magic, a friendly frog appeared! The frog said, "Hi Tim! I'm here to help you with the toilet." Tim smiled and felt less scared. The frog showed him how to use the toilet, and it was easy! Tim was so happy, he clapped again. The frog said, "Good job, Tim! You did it!" And with that, the frog disappeared. Tim was proud of himself and knew he could do it all by himself next time.
