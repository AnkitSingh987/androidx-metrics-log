Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was always hungry. One day, she went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, I am hungry. Can I have some food?" Her mommy gave her an apple and said, "Here you go, Lily. Eat this apple and you will not be hungry anymore." Lily ate the apple and felt much better. Later that day, Lily was playing in her room. She saw her toy bear sitting in the closet. She wanted to attach a ribbon to the bear's neck. She found a pretty ribbon and tied it around the bear's neck. Now, her toy bear looked very nice. Lily was happy and went to show her mommy the bear with the ribbon. Her mommy said, "Good job, Lily! Your bear looks so pretty now."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a friend named Sam. They liked to play near the hedge in the park. The hedge was very big and green. Tim thought the hedge was weird because it looked like a big green monster. One day, Tim and Sam were playing near the hedge. Tim said, "I recommend we play hide and seek." Sam agreed. Tim closed his eyes and began to count. Sam ran to find a hiding spot. Sam found a small hole in the hedge. He thought it was a great place to hide. As he crawled in, the hedge started to grow around him. "Help!" Sam cried. Tim heard Sam and ran to the hedge. But the hedge was too big and strong. Tim could not save Sam. Sam was stuck in the hedge forever. Tim was very sad. He never played near the hedge again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom and dad. One day, his dad asked him to fill a big cup with water. Tim was an obedient boy, so he said, "Yes, Dad!" Tim went to the kitchen and saw a small tool on the table. It was a spoon. He thought, "I can use this tool to fill the cup!" So, Tim started to fill the cup with water using the spoon. It took a long time, but he did not give up. Finally, the cup was full. Tim carried it carefully to his dad. His dad was happy and said, "Good job, Tim! You are a very helpful boy!" Tim smiled and felt proud. He knew that he was an obedient and helpful son.

Once upon a time there was a big family. The mother and father had a daughter. Every day, the daughter would run around and laugh. One beautiful day, the family went for a picnic in the park. The daughter was so excited. She ran around and played. Then, the father said it was time to go. The daughter didn't want to leave the park. She was having so much fun. But her mother said it was time to go and so they did. As they were leaving, the daughter saw a huge rock. She wanted to throw it, so she did. The rock flew up into the sky and the daughter laughed and laughed. The father was so proud of his daughter. He gave her a big hug and told her that she had thrown the rock really far. The daughter smiled, knowing that she had done something very big.

Once upon a time, there was a normal house. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. They were happy. But their chimney was very dirty. So, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to clean the chimney today." Tim and his mom started to clean the chimney. They used a big brush to clean it. They worked hard. After a while, they heard a small voice. "Help me!" said the voice. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They looked inside the chimney and saw a little bird. The bird was stuck. Tim's mom carefully took the bird out. "Thank you for saving me!" said the bird. Tim and his mom were happy they could help. Now, their chimney was clean, and they had a new friend.
