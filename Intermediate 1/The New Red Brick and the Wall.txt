Category: Intermediate
Title: The New Red Brick and the Wall

Once upon a time, there was a new red brick. It lived with its friends in a big pile. They all liked to play together. One day, a big wind came and blew all the bricks away. The new red brick was sad and wanted to find its friends. The new red brick went on a long walk to find its friends. It met a big, strong wall. The wall said, "I can help you find your friends, but first, you must help me prevent the wind from blowing me down." The new red brick agreed to help the wall. The new red brick and the wall worked together to stop the wind. They found a way to make the wall stronger. The wall was very happy and helped the new red brick find its friends. In the end, they all played together again, and the wind could not blow them away.

Once upon a time, there was a foolish dog named Spot. Spot liked to play on the roof of his house. He would jump up there and run around, barking at the birds and the wind. One day, it started to rain. The rain came down hard and fast. Spot was still on the roof, and he started to get very wet. The rain began to soak his fur, and he shivered in the cold. Spot's owner, a little girl named Lucy, saw him on the roof. She called him down, and Spot jumped off the roof into her arms. They both laughed as they went inside to get warm and dry. Spot learned not to play on the roof when it rained, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. Sarah was new to the neighbourhood and wanted to make some friends. She was feeling brave so she decided to go and find someone to play with. Sarah walked down the street and saw a park. She was so excited. But as she walked closer she began to tremble. It was dark and the park was all alone. Then, Sarah heard a noise. It came from Molly. Molly was a little girl who lived nearby. Sarah was so relieved to see another person. Molly took Sarah to the puddle in the park. The puddle was big and sparkly in the moonlight. The two girls splashed and laughed in the water. Molly was so excited. Sarah felt sleepy. They said goodnight and Sarah headed home. She was feeling brave and happy to have a friend. She jumped over the puddles and made her way home.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tom and his dog, Max. They loved to play together all day long. One sunny day, Tom found a long board in the backyard. He had a great idea for a new game to play with Max. Tom put the long board on two big rocks, making a fun slide. "Max, let's play on the board!" Tom said, smiling. Max wagged his tail and barked, excited to play. Tom helped Max climb up the slide, and then Max slid down the board, laughing and barking. They played on the long board all day, taking turns sliding and laughing. When the sun began to set, Tom and Max were tired but happy. They went inside the house and ate a big dinner. That night, as they slept, they both dreamed of more fun days playing together.

One day, a big horse named Bob went to the store. Bob wanted to buy hay for his little baby horse, Tim. Tim was very small and fragile. Bob knew he had to be careful with Tim. Bob saw a big stack of hay at the store. He knew that Tim would love it. But he also saw a big, dark cloud in the sky. It looked like rain would come soon. Bob was worried, but he bought the hay anyway. Bob started to carry the hay back to his home. The wind began to blow very hard. The rain started to fall. The hay got wet and heavy. Bob tried to hurry, but it was too late. The hay fell and broke. Tim didn't get his hay, and they both were very sad.
