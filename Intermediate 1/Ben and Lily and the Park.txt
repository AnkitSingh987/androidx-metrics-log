Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Park

Ben and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to run on the grass, swing on the swings, and slide on the slide. But their favorite thing was the fountain. The fountain was big and bright, and it made water go up and down like a dance. Ben and Lily liked to watch the water and make wishes. One day, they went to the park and saw something bad. The fountain was not bright anymore. It was dark and dirty, and it smelled bad. The water was not dancing, it was still and green. Ben and Lily felt sad and angry. "Who did this to the fountain?" Ben asked. "I don't know, but it's not fair!" Lily said. They looked around and saw a man with a big bag. He was throwing things in the fountain. They saw cans, bottles, papers, and food. The man was making the fountain rot. "Hey, stop that!" Ben shouted. The man looked at them and laughed. He said, "Why? It's just a fountain. It's not important. I can do what I want." Ben and Lily felt more angry. They said, "No, you can't! The fountain is important. It's beautiful and fun and it makes us happy. You are making it ugly and sad and it makes us mad. You have to stop!" The man did not listen. He said, "Go away, you little brats. This is none of your business. I have more things to throw in the fountain." He reached into his bag and pulled out a big box. He was about to throw it in the fountain, when something happened. The box was heavy and the man lost his balance. He fell into the fountain with a splash. He got wet and dirty and smelly. He screamed and tried to get out, but he slipped and fell again. Ben and Lily watched and smiled. They said, "That's what you get for being mean to the fountain. Now you know how it feels to rot." They ran to get help from a grown-up. They hoped the fountain would be bright again soon. They still had wishes to make.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dog named Max. Max was a patient dog. He always waited for his food and never barked too loud. Max liked to play with his toys and loved his family. One day, Max saw a newspaper on the ground. He thought it was a toy, so he picked it up in his mouth. Max did not know that he should not steal things. He ran around the yard, playing with the newspaper. Max's family saw him playing with the newspaper. They told Max that it was not a toy and that he should not steal. Max felt sad and gave the newspaper back. He learned not to take things that were not his. From that day on, Max was an even better dog, and his family loved him more and more.

Mark was a silly goose. He was a very nervous bird and he always wanted to fly. One day, he decided to fly away. He flew and flew until he couldn't see his home anymore. He was excited and scared at the same time. Suddenly, a big black cloud appeared in the sky. He was so nervous that he couldn't move. He knew he had to go back, but he was too scared to fly. He just sat in the sky, trembling. Suddenly, he heard a loud voice. "Hey! Come down! You don't belong up here!," said the voice. Mark was really scared and he was very nervous. He tried to fly back to his home, but it was too late. He was suddenly caught in a big storm and he was never seen again.

Lily and Ben were playing hide and seek in the garden. Lily was the seeker and Ben was the hider. Lily counted to ten and then looked for Ben. She saw his red shoes under the big tree. She ran to the tree and shouted, "I found you!" Ben came out from behind the tree and smiled. He said, "You are good at seeking, Lily. Now it's my turn to seek and your turn to hide." Lily nodded and ran to find a good hiding spot. She saw a lemon tree with many yellow fruits. She thought it was a perfect place to hide. She climbed the tree and hid behind the lemons. She hoped Ben would not find her. Ben counted to ten and then looked for Lily. He saw her pink dress behind the lemon tree. He sneaked to the tree and peeked behind the lemons. He saw Lily's face and said, "Boo!" Lily was surprised and laughed. She said, "You found me, Ben. You are good at seeking, too." Ben and Lily hugged and said, "We are both good at hide and seek. We are proud of ourselves." They picked some lemons and went to the kitchen to make lemonade. They shared the lemonade with their mom and dad and told them about their fun game. Mom and dad were proud of them, too.
