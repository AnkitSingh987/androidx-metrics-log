Category: Intermediate
Title: Pinky the Clumsy Cat and the Cake

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a clumsy cat named Pinky. Pinky was a very pink cat. She loved to play with her ball and run around the house. But Pinky had a big sneeze. Every time she sneezed, funny things happened. One day, Pinky saw a big cake on the table. She wanted to eat it, but she knew she should not. Pinky tried to be good but her nose started to tickle. She felt a big sneeze coming. She tried to stop it, but it was too hard. Pinky sneezed so big that the cake flew off the table and onto the floor. The cake made a big mess. Pinky felt sad and knew she had to clean it up. She got a mop and cleaned the floor. After that, Pinky learned to be more careful when she sneezed.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. There is a big hedge in the garden. The hedge has many green leaves. The leaves are soft and tickle. One day, Lily and Ben find a pair of scissors in the shed. They want to play with the scissors. They think the scissors are fun and shiny. "Let's cut the hedge!" Lily says. "We can make shapes with the leaves!" "OK!" Ben says. "I want to make a star!" They run to the hedge with the scissors. They start to cut the leaves. They cut and cut and cut. They make holes and gaps in the hedge. They laugh and giggle. But then, Mom comes out of the house. She sees the hedge. She sees the scissors. She sees Lily and Ben. She is not happy. "What are you doing?" Mom asks. "You can't cut the hedge with scissors! That's not safe! That's not nice! The hedge is hurt!" Lily and Ben stop cutting. They look at Mom. They look at the hedge. They see the mess they made. They feel sorry. "We're sorry, Mom," Lily says. "We just wanted to play." "We didn't know, Mom," Ben says. "We won't do it again." Mom sighs. She takes the scissors from them. She hugs them. "I know you didn't mean to be bad," Mom says. "But you have to be careful with scissors. They are not toys. They can hurt you and the plants. Next time, ask me before you use them. OK?" "OK, Mom," Lily and Ben say. Mom smiles. She kisses them. "Now, let's go inside and have some juice," Mom says. "And maybe later, we can help the hedge grow back."

Once upon a time there were two small children who lived in a valley surrounded by mountains. One day, the children decided to design a special picnic for their parents. The children were so excited to go on a picnic in the mountains! The two small children were very busy looking for the perfect spot to have the picnic. Finally, the children spotted a spot at the top of a mountain. The children quickly turned to each other and smiled. “Let’s go!” they said in unison. As they climbed the mountain, the sun began to set. The children felt a bit scared as the light grew dull, but they continued climbing. Finally, they reached the top and the picnic was ready. The children’s parents were so surprised and pleased. They hugged the children and said, “Thank you for designing such a special picnic. You two have made this moment so special.” The children beamed with pride, happy to have made their parents so happy. As they ate the picnic and watched the sun set, the children knew their adventure on the mountain had just begun!

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It was in a big garden. Many birds lived in the tree. They were happy. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the garden. She saw the tall tree and the birds. She wanted to decorate the tree. She said, "I want to make the tree pretty for the birds." Lily found some jam in her house. She put the jam on the tree branches. The birds came and ate the jam. They said, "Thank you, Lily, for the yummy jam and the pretty tree." The birds and Lily became good friends. They played in the garden every day. And the tall tree was the happiest tree in the world.
