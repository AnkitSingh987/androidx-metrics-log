Category: Intermediate
Title: The Duck Called Green and the Pond

Once upon a time there was a duck called Green. Green was safe in the pond and everyone was happy. One day Green wanted to explore the world. Green waddled to the edge of the pond and said to her friend Pig "I am going to go explore the world." Pig nodded and said "be safe out there." Green left the pond and started exploring. She looked around wondering what she should do next. Suddenly she saw a big pond with ducks! Excited, she flew towards them and shouted "What is your name?". The eldest duck said "I am Lola and am glad to meet you. We have been looking for a new friend. Welcome to our pond, Green. You are safe here with us." Green was so happy! She had found a new home and friends. From that day onward she was always happy, safe and with friends.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, he saw a big tree and wanted to become the best tree climber. Spot tried to climb the tree, but he was too small. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't do it. Spot's friends saw him and wanted to help. They thought of a plan to help Spot climb the tree. The next day, Spot's friends brought a big, soft hug. They put the hug around the tree to help Spot climb up. Spot tried again, and this time, he could climb the tree! He was so happy and thankful for his friends. Spot became the best tree climber and had lots of fun with his friends. They all played in the park together, and Spot always shared his big, soft hug with them. They all had a great time and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a cricket who loved mail. The cricket wanted to surprise their friends, so it thought of a spicy plan. The cricket quickly gathered some leaves, some rocks, and some twigs. It carefully placed everything in an envelope and mailed the surprise. When the envelope arrived, the friends were so excited! They opened it up and found a spicy surprise inside. The cricket had made them a spicy snack! The friends loved the surprise and thanked the cricket for its thoughtfulness. When the cricket heard this, it was so happy. Now it knew that it could surprise its friends with mail whenever it wanted.

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to make something different. He thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. "I will make a big space in my room for my toys!" Tom said. He was very happy. Tom started to move his toys to make space. He made a big space in the middle of his room. Suddenly, he heard a noise. It was a little green man. The little green man said, "Hello, Tom! I come from a different place. Thank you for making space for me!" Tom was surprised but happy. He played with the little green man all day. They had lots of fun in the new space. At the end of the day, the little green man said, "Goodbye, Tom! I will come back to play again!" Tom smiled and waved goodbye. He could not wait for his new friend to come back.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to look at things. He looked at the sky, the trees, and the animals. One day, Tim found a broken toy on the ground. It was a toy car. Tim wanted to fix the broken toy car. Tim asked his mom for help. She said they needed to do some math to fix the broken toy car. Tim and his mom sat down and counted. They counted one, two, three, four, five. They found out they needed five pieces to fix the broken toy car. Tim and his mom looked for the pieces. They found one under the table, two in the toy box, and two in the yard. They put the pieces together, and the broken toy car was fixed. Tim was so happy! He played with his fixed toy car all day long.
