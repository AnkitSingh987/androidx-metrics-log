Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who lived in a big house. One day, he wanted to borrow a book from his friend. So, he went to his friend's house to get the book. As he was walking home with the book, he saw lightning in the sky. The wealthy man was scared, but he kept walking. The lightning was bright and loud, making him jump. Then, something unexpected happened. The lightning hit the book in his hand, and it made the book magic! The wealthy man was surprised by the magic book. He read it and found out he could make things happen by reading the words. The man was happy that he borrowed the book because it made his life more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was anxious to go to the park. He put on his shoes and his mom helped him with his buckle on his belt. Tim's mom nodded and said, "Okay, let's go!" At the park, Tim saw a big dog. The dog had a buckle on its collar. Tim wanted to pet the dog, but he was a little scared. The dog nodded its head, as if it wanted Tim to come closer. Tim took a step closer and reached out to pet the dog. Just as Tim was about to pet the dog, the dog started to talk! The dog said, "Hi Tim, I am a magic dog! I can help you find a hidden toy in the park." Tim was so surprised! He never thought a dog could talk. They looked around the park and found a big, shiny toy car. Tim and the magic dog played together all day, and they became best friends.

Johnnie was walking in the park. He saw a long, green hill and he started to run. He noticed the grass was so green and soft. Near the top of the hill he stopped and noticed a strange shape on the ground. It was a shiny box! Johnnie opened the box and inside he found so many coins. They were golden and so different! He noticed they were flipped over. Johnnie flipped them over and saw they were rich coins! He was so excited! Johnnie ran back down the hill and went to find his mom. "Mom! Look what I found! Rich coins!" he said. His mom hugged him and said, "oh my goodness! What an amazing discovery!" She said. Johnnie was so proud of his discovery. He grinned from ear to ear.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was an incredible girl who loved to lift things. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Lucy saw a big rock. She wanted to lift the rock. She tried and tried, but it was too heavy. "Mom, can you help me lift the rock?" Lucy asked. Mom came over and helped Lucy lift the rock. Under the rock, they found a hidden lunch! They were so happy. They sat down and ate the yummy lunch together. And they lived happily ever after.

Sam and Sam’s mom were discussing because Sam wanted a wide, pink bed. Sam’s mom said no and Sam was very sad. He started to cry. His mom felt bad and tried to make him feel better. She said it was okay to talk about the pink bed. Sam’s mom said, “Let’s discuss how we can make this happen.” They talked about different ideas and ways that Sam could get a wide, pink bed. They agreed that Sam could do some extra chores around the house to earn the money for the bed. Sam was very excited and he got to work. After a few weeks, Sam had earned enough money to buy the wide, pink bed he wanted. He was so happy, and thanked his mom for helping to make his dream come true.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a little rose. The rose was red and pretty. But the rose was grumpy. It did not like to play with the other flowers. One day, a happy frog came to the garden. The frog saw the grumpy rose and wanted to be friends. The frog went to the rose and said, "Hi, rose! Let's play together!" But the grumpy rose said, "No, I don't want to play." The frog did not give up. It started to jump around the rose. Jump, jump, jump! The frog jumped so high and it made the rose laugh. The rose thought the frog was funny. The grumpy rose slowly became happy. It liked watching the frog jump. The rose and the frog became good friends. They played together every day, and the rose was grumpy no more.
