Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and the Sun

The sun was hot and bright as Bob walked up the stairs to reach the attic. He was feeling excited, as he had never been there before. He looked around and saw so many delicious looking things. Bob saw a sign that said "Do not eat any of these things", so he knew he had to be careful. Bob heard a noise and saw his dad standing in the corner of the attic. "Dad, what are all of these delicious things? Can I have some?" Bob asked. His dad smiled and said, "No Bob, you cannot. Nothing in this attic is for you." Bob was disappointed, but he still smiled. His dad patted his head and said, "It's OK, I'll encourage you with something else. How about a cool glass of juice? That's much better!" Bob smiled and hugged his Dad. But as soon as Bob hugged his Dad, he noticed his Dad's smile fading away. Bob looked down and saw that the delicious things in the attic were all over the floor. Bob's dad was mad and he scolded him for not listening. Bob was sad, but he knew he deserved it. He realized that he should have listened to Dad and not been tempted by the delicious food in the attic.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play with his ball. He loved to kick the ball high in the sky. He saw his friend, Sue, and asked her to play with him. "Hi Sue, do you want to play with me?" Tim asked. Sue said, "Yes, I want to play! I like to kick the ball too." They played and kicked the ball back and forth. They were having lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and took their ball in its mouth. Tim and Sue were sad. But the dog was not bad, it was a generous dog. It had a ball in its skin, and it gave the ball to Tim and Sue. They were happy again and played with the new ball. The dog stayed and played with them, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the park. He loved to swing, slide, and run around. The park was a good place to have fun. One day, Tim saw a big dog in the park. The dog was near the exit. Tim was scared of the dog. He wanted to go home, but he didn't want to go near the dog. Tim thought of a plan. He picked up a stick and threw it far away from the exit. The dog ran to get the stick. Tim ran to the exit and went home. He was happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to count. She would count her toys, her fingers, and her toes. One day, Lily went to the doctor's office with her mom. The doctor's office was called the "patient" place. At the patient place, there were many kids. Some kids were noisy. They would yell and run around. But Lily was a good girl. She sat quietly and waited for her turn. While she waited, she counted the chairs in the room. There were ten chairs! When it was Lily's turn, she went inside with her mom. The doctor was very nice. He asked Lily to count her fingers and toes. Lily counted, "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!" The doctor was happy. Lily was a smart girl. She knew how to count, even at the patient place with noisy kids around.

One day, a quiet girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They took some food to feed the ducks. The ducks were very happy and said, "Quack, quack! Thank you, Lily!" While they were at the park, Lily found a shiny coin on the ground. She picked it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Wow, Lily! That coin has a lot of value! We can buy something nice with it." Just then, a talking duck waddled up to them. The duck said, "That coin is magic! If you give it to me, I will give you a big surprise!" Lily and her mom were very surprised, but they decided to give the coin to the duck. The duck took the coin and jumped into the pond. Suddenly, the water turned different colors and the other ducks started to sing and dance. The duck came back with a big box of toys for Lily. She was so happy and thanked the duck for the fun surprise.
