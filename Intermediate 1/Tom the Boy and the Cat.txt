Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He had a fancy frame with a picture of a cat. One day, the frame fell and broke. Tom was very sad. Tom's friend, Sam, saw the broken frame and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I can heal it." Sam was very good at fixing things. Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Sam worked hard to fix the fancy frame. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat in the picture came to life! The cat jumped out of the frame and played with Tom and Sam. They all became good friends and had fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. Tim loved the beach because he could play in the sand and swim in the water. He saw a man sitting alone on the sand. The man looked sad, so Tim went to talk to him. "Hi, I'm Tim. Why are you sad?" asked Tim. The man said, "I am deaf, and I can't hear the waves. I regret not taking care of my ears when I was young." Tim thought for a moment and then smiled. Tim took the man's hand and led him to the water. Tim showed the man how to feel the waves with his feet. The man felt the water and smiled. Tim and the man played together all day. They did not need to hear to have fun at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a small squirrel. The squirrel was very fair and had a big fluffy tail. It liked to jump from tree to tree and eat nuts. One day, the squirrel saw a big dog in the park. The big dog saw the squirrel and started to point at it. The squirrel was scared, so it ran fast up a tree. The dog barked and jumped, but it could not reach the squirrel. The squirrel looked down and saw the dog getting tired. The squirrel thought it was safe, so it came down from the tree. But the big dog was waiting. It ran after the squirrel again. This time, the squirrel could not find a tree to climb. The big dog caught the squirrel, and the squirrel was very sad.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys and books. But they also like to give each other gifts. Sometimes they make gifts with paper and crayons. Sometimes they find gifts in the garden or the park. One night, Sara and Ben had a fancy idea. They wanted to give each other a special gift. They waited until their mom and dad were asleep. Then they got out of bed and went to their closet. They looked for the fanciest things they could find. Sara found a shiny necklace and a sparkly dress. She put them on and felt like a princess. Ben found a hat and a coat with buttons. He put them on and felt like a detective. They smiled at each other and whispered, "You look fancy!" They tiptoed to each other's room and put their gifts on the bed. Sara gave Ben a book about dinosaurs. She knew he loved dinosaurs. Ben gave Sara a doll with long hair. He knew she loved dolls. They hugged and said, "Thank you for the gift!" They went back to their own beds and snuggled with their gifts. They felt happy and sleepy. They closed their eyes and dreamed of fancy adventures. They did not hear their mom and dad wake up and see the gifts. They did not hear them laugh and say, "What a surprise!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play in her room with her toys. One day, she found a pretty crown. The crown was shiny and had many colors. Amy wanted to wear the crown and be a queen. Amy's friend, Ben, came to play. Ben saw the crown and thought it was cool. He wanted to wear it too. Amy didn't want to share, but she knew it was the right thing to do. So, she took off the crown and offered it to Ben. Ben was happy and put on the crown. They played together all day, taking turns wearing the crown. They pretended to be kings and queens, ruling over their toy kingdom. Even though sharing was tough for Amy, she learned that it was more fun to play with friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
