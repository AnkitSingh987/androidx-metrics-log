Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the World

One day, a boy named Tim found a little thing called a microscope. He did not know what it was, but it looked fun. Tim felt a strong urge to play with it. He took the microscope to his room and put it on his table. Tim looked through the microscope and saw a peaceful world with tiny animals and plants. He was very happy to see this new world. Tim wanted to show his friends the peaceful world too, so he asked them to come over. When his friends came, they all took turns looking through the microscope. They saw the tiny animals and plants in the peaceful world. They all felt the urge to protect this world and keep it safe. Tim and his friends promised to take care of the microscope and the peaceful world inside it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom did not believe in himself. He was always ashamed when he tried new things. His friends could run, jump, and play, but Tom was too scared to try. One day, Tom saw a bright light in the sky. It was a shooting star! He closed his eyes and made a wish. He wished that he could believe in himself and not be ashamed anymore. The next day, Tom felt different. He was not scared to try new things. He ran with his friends, jumped high, and played all day. Tom was happy, and he knew that the light from the shooting star helped him believe in himself.

---

Once upon a time, there was a strong man who lived on a ranch. He had big muscles and could lift anything. He had many animals on the ranch like cows, horses, and chickens. The man loved to take care of his animals and play with them. One day, a little boy came to the ranch. He did not know what a ranch was. The strong man wanted to explain to the little boy what a ranch is. He said, "A ranch is a big home for animals. We take care of them and give them food." The little boy was happy to learn about the ranch. He wanted to help the strong man. They fed the cows, brushed the horses, and collected eggs from the chickens. The little boy had so much fun at the ranch. He learned a lot from the strong man and loved the animals very much.

---

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play together. One day, they went to the farm with their parents. They saw many animals and plants. They were very happy. They came to a field of corn. The corn was tall and yellow. Tom and Lily wanted to pick some corn to eat. But their parents said no. They said the corn was not theirs. It belonged to the farmer. They had to ask the farmer first. Tom and Lily disagreed. They thought the corn was for everyone. They wanted to pick some corn anyway. They sneaked into the field when their parents were not looking. They each took a big ear of corn. But the farmer saw them. He was angry. He came to them and said, "What are you doing? This is my corn. You cannot take it without asking. That is stealing. That is wrong." Tom and Lily were scared. They dropped the corn and ran back to their parents. They said they were sorry. They learned their lesson. They learned to respect other people's things. They learned to be modest and not greedy. They learned to listen to their parents. They said they would never steal again. The farmer forgave them. He gave them each a small ear of corn to eat. They thanked him and shared it with their parents. They enjoyed the corn. It was sweet and juicy. They were happy again.

One day, a girl named Sue found her mom's makeup. She thought it would be fun to play with it. So, she took the makeup and went to her room. She lay on the floor and started to put the makeup on her face. She felt happy. Sue's little brother, Tim, saw her and asked, "What are you doing, Sue?" Sue said, "I am putting on makeup, like mom does." Tim wanted to try too. So, they both put on makeup and laughed. But then, they felt cold. They didn't know why. Sue and Tim went to mom and showed her their faces. Mom smiled and said, "You two look funny. But it's cold outside, and you should wear warm clothes, not play with makeup." Sue and Tim learned that they should dress for the weather and not play with things that are not for them. They took off the makeup, put on warm clothes, and went outside to play.
