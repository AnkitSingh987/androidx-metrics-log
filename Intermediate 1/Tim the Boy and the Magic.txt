Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the mud and get dirty. One day, while playing, he found a magic rock. The rock had a special power. Tim picked up the rock and said, "Wow! This rock is so cool! I wonder what the power is?" Suddenly, a friendly frog appeared and said, "Ribbit! This rock can make things disappear. But only use it for good, Tim." Tim was so excited. He looked at the dirty mud and said, "Rock, make the mud disappear!" And just like that, the mud was gone. Tim was clean and happy. He knew he would only use the rock's power to help others. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Poppy. Poppy was only three years old and she was out for a walk in the park. Suddenly, Poppy saw a buckle on the ground. It was a really pretty buckle, but it was also very dirty. Poppy wanted to pick up the buckle, so she bent down and pinch it. Then she saw a strange thing - the buckle began to move. It wasn’t a buckle at all - it was a tiny little mouse! The mouse said to Poppy: “Oh help me! I am stuck inside this buckle and I cannot get out!” Poppy was not scared, so she bent down and carefully opened the buckle. Then the little mouse thanked her and scurried away. Poppy smiled and put the buckle in her pocket. She was happy she could help the little mouse.

One day, a little dog named Spot found a big, soft toy. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck in a box. Spot tried to open the box. He pushed and pulled, but the box did not open. Spot saw his friend, Cat. He asked, "Cat, can you help me open the box?" Cat came over and used her claws to snap the box open. The soft toy was free. Spot was happy and said, "Thank you, Cat!" Spot and Cat played with the soft toy together. They had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that if we help each other, we can do things that are hard to do alone.

George was a poor man. One day, he found a telephone at the park. He was very happy and picked it up. On his way back home, George saw a lost puppy. He felt so sorry for the puppy. Without thinking further, he scooped it in his arms and brought the puppy back home. George decided to take care of the puppy. He fed the puppy and named him Rover. George took the telephone to call his friends and asked them, "Can you help me find a home for Rover?" His friends said, "Yes, of course! We will help you look for a home for Rover." George was so glad that he had found such kind friends and he hugged Rover tightly. From then on, George took good care of Rover and always made sure he had plenty to eat. George was very happy and thankful that he had found a friend who he could rely on.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn dog named Max. Max lived in a big lab with his friend, Dr. Jane. Dr. Jane liked to make things in the lab. Max liked to play with his toys. One day, Max looked out the window and saw it was raining. He wanted to go outside and play. Max asked Dr. Jane, "Can we go out and play in the rain?" Dr. Jane said, "No, Max. We have work to do in the lab." Max was sad but he had an idea. He went to his toy box and took out a big, red ball. Max threw the ball and it hit a big button in the lab. Suddenly, the lab started to rain inside! Max was so happy to play in the rain. Dr. Jane laughed and said, "Okay, Max. We can play in the rain for a little bit." Max and Dr. Jane played in the rain inside the lab. They had a lot of fun. After they played, they went back to work in the lab, all wet and happy.
