Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Green Bug and the Book

One day, a green bug named Bob lived in a small house. In the house, there was a big bookshelf with many books. Bob loved to read the books, but he was too small to reach them. He had to struggle every day to get a book. Bob had a friend named Sam, who was a little bird. Sam saw Bob struggle to get a book from the bookshelf. "I can help you, Bob," said Sam. "I can fly and get the books for you." Bob was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" Every day, Sam would fly to the bookshelf and get a book for Bob. They would sit together and read the books. Bob and Sam were very happy. They loved to read and learn new things. And they never had to struggle with the big bookshelf again.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the hall to play with her ball. She saw a box and felt frightened. She did not know what was inside. Amy called her friend Tim to come and see the box. "Tim, come here! I found a box and I am frightened. Can you help me?" Amy said. Tim walked to the hall and saw the box. "Don't worry, Amy. Let's open it together," Tim said. They opened the box and found a letter. It said, "Mail this letter to your friend." They were not frightened anymore. They felt happy to have a job to do together. Amy and Tim learned that it is good to help friends when they are frightened.

Once upon a time, in a large yellow house, lived a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Tim. They were not friends. Tom always tried to catch Tim. But Tim was very fast and smart. One day, Tom saw Tim near a large tree. Tom thought, "This is my chance to catch him!" Tom hid behind the tree and waited. Tim knew Tom was there. He had a plan. Tim called out, "Tom, I need your help. I can't climb the tree. Can you support me?" Tom thought for a moment. He wanted to catch Tim, but he also wanted to help. Tom decided to support Tim. He let Tim climb on his back to reach the tree. When they got to the top, they saw a big nest with baby birds. The baby birds were hungry and crying. Tom and Tim worked together to find food for the baby birds. They fed them and the baby birds were happy. Tom and Tim became friends. They learned that helping and supporting each other was better than fighting. From then on, they lived happily in the large yellow house.

Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily had a doll that she liked very much. She called her Anna. Ben had a car that he liked very much. He called it Zoom. "Look, Anna can dance!" Lily said, moving her doll in a graceful way. "She is very pretty and smart." "Zoom can go fast!" Ben said, pushing his car on the floor. "He is very strong and brave." They played for a while, until they smelled something bad. It was smoke. They saw smoke coming from the kitchen. They were scared. "Mommy! Daddy! Smoke!" they shouted, running to the door. Mommy and Daddy came quickly. They saw that a pot on the stove was burning. They turned off the fire and took the pot outside. They opened the windows to let the smoke out. "Are you okay, kids?" Mommy asked, hugging Lily and Ben. "Yes, we are okay," Lily said. "What happened?" Daddy asked. "We were playing with our toys," Ben said. "Did you touch the stove?" Mommy asked. "No, we didn't," Lily said. "Good. You should never touch the stove. It is very hot and dangerous," Daddy said. "We know," Ben said. Mommy and Daddy checked the toys. They saw that Anna and Zoom were very small. They had shrinked because of the smoke. "Oh no, your toys!" Mommy said. "They are so tiny!" Ben said. "Can you fix them?" Lily asked. "I don't know, honey. Maybe we can try," Daddy said. Lily and Ben felt sad. They loved their toys. They hoped they could make them big again. They hugged their toys and each other. Mommy and Daddy hugged them too. They said they were sorry and they loved them. They said they would buy them new toys if they couldn't fix them. They said they were proud of them for being brave and smart. They said they were happy they were safe. They said they were a family.
