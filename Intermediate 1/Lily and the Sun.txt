Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new red dress that she loved to wear. It was her favorite dress because it was so pretty and made her feel like a princess. One day, it was very hot outside. Lily wanted to play in the park with her friends. She put on her new red dress and went outside. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Lily and her friends played on the swings and the slide. They had so much fun. After playing, Lily and her friends were very hot and tired. They sat under a big tree to rest. Lily's mom brought them cold drinks and snacks. They all enjoyed their treats and felt much better. Then, they went back to playing in the park, and Lily wore her new red dress all day long.

---

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the beach with her mom. Mia saw a wide, big shell in the sand. "Look, Mom!" she said, "I found an oyster!" Mia's mom smiled and said, "Yes, you did! Let's open it and see what's inside." Mia and her mom opened the oyster. Inside, there was a small pearl. The pearl was shiny and round. Mia was very happy. She laughed and said, "I found a pretty pearl, Mom!" Her mom laughed too and said, "You did! It's very pretty, just like you!" Mia and her mom took the pearl home. They put it in a small box to keep it safe. Every day, Mia looked at her pretty pearl and remembered the fun day at the beach with her mom. She always laughed when she thought about finding the oyster and the pearl.

---

Once upon a time, in a pretty little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch the sun rise every morning. He would wake up early and go outside to see the sky change colors. It was his favorite part of the day. One morning, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was new to the town and was also watching the sun rise. "Hi, I'm Tim!" he said. "Hi, I'm Sue!" she replied. They both smiled and watched the sun together. After that, they became good friends. One hot day, Tim and Sue went to the park to play. They brought a cooler full of cold drinks and snacks. They played all day under the sun. When they got thirsty, they opened the cooler and shared the cold drinks. They had so much fun and were happy to have each other as friends.

---

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny day, a little girl named Jane went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a lock on it. Jane was very curious and wanted to find the key to open the lock. She looked around and found a key under a small rock. Jane was happy and used the key to open the lock. Behind the lock, there was a small door in the tree. She opened the door and saw a tiny room with a small table. On the table, there was a cup with a yummy drink. Jane was thirsty, so she drank the drink. It tasted so good! After she finished her drink, Jane closed the door and locked it again. She left the key under the rock and went back home with a big smile on her face.

---

One day, a happy boy went to the market with his mom. They saw a vendor selling big, round balloons. The boy loved balloons and wanted one very much. His mom bought a red balloon for him, and the boy was very happy. While walking, the boy saw a cat. He wanted to play with the cat, but the cat was scared. The boy tried to poke the cat with his balloon, but the cat ran away. The boy was sad and didn't understand why the cat didn't want to play. His mom told him that poking can hurt others and it's not nice. The boy learned to be gentle and kind to animals. From that day on, he never poked anyone or anything again. The boy was happy, and he played with his red balloon without poking.
