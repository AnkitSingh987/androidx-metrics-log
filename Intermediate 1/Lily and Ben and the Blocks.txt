Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Blocks

Lily and Ben were playing with blocks in their room. They wanted to build a big tower that reached the ceiling. They took turns to add more blocks on top of each other. They were very careful not to make the tower fall. "How many blocks do we need to finish the tower?" Lily asked. "I don't know. Let's measure it with the ruler," Ben said. He got the ruler from the drawer and held it next to the tower. "Look, the tower is 50 centimeters tall. The ceiling is 100 centimeters tall. We need 50 more centimeters of blocks." "Wow, that's a lot of blocks. Do we have enough?" Lily wondered. "Let's see. We have this box of blocks and this bag of blocks. Maybe we can use them all," Ben said. He opened the box and the bag and poured the blocks on the floor. They started to pick up the blocks and stack them on the tower. They were so busy building that they did not notice that it was getting dark outside. The sun was setting and the sky was turning red and purple. The room was getting darker and darker. "Ben, I can't see the blocks very well. It's too dark in here," Lily said. "Me too. Let's turn on the light," Ben said. He reached for the switch on the wall, but he bumped into the tower. The tower wobbled and then crashed to the ground. The blocks scattered everywhere. "Oh no! Our tower!" Lily cried. "Our tower is gone. We didn't finish it," Ben said. They looked at the mess of blocks and felt sad. They worked so hard to build the tower, but they did not make it to the ceiling. They wished they had more time and more light. "Maybe we can try again tomorrow," Lily said. "Maybe. But we have to clean up the blocks first," Ben said. They nodded and started to pick up the blocks and put them back in the box and the bag. They hoped that tomorrow they would have a better chance to finish their tower.

Once there was a little girl named Mia. She was very curious. One day, Mia saw a big cross on the other side of the street. She wanted to go see it. Mia's mom said, "I will permit you to cross the street, but first, we must look both ways." Mia looked left and right. She saw a big truck coming. Mia knew she had to wait. After the truck passed, Mia and her mom crossed the street safely. They reached the big cross. Mia was so happy and said, "Thank you, mom, for letting me cross the street!" Mia learned to always be careful when crossing the street.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tim. Tim was an incredible cat who loved to play and act in fun shows for his friends. One day, Tim found a big box in his yard. He knew it was something special and wanted to show his friends. Tim called all his friends to come and see the big box. They all gathered around, excited to see what was inside. Tim told them, "I am going to act in a show with this box. You will see something amazing!" His friends could not wait to see what Tim had planned. Finally, the time came for Tim's show. He jumped into the box and started to act. He pretended to be a king, a pirate, and a superhero. His friends were so happy and clapped their hands. Tim's show was incredible, just like he said it would be. They all knew that Tim the cat was the best actor in town.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom. At the park, she saw a boy named Ben. Ben had a journal with him. Lucy wanted to see the journal, but she didn't know how to ask nicely. Lucy went to Ben and said, "Give me your journal!" Ben thought that Lucy was being rude. He didn't want to share his journal with her. Lucy felt sad, and her mom told her to say sorry to Ben. Lucy said, "I'm sorry, Ben. Can I please see your journal?" Ben smiled and said, "Yes, you can look at it." They sat down together and looked at the journal. Soon, Lucy and Ben became good friends. They played together until it was time to go home. Lucy learned that it was important to be kind and not rude when asking for something.
