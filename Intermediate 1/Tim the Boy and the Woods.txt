Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Woods

One day, a little boy named Tim found a baseball. He liked to roll the ball on the ground. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam liked to play with the baseball too. They would take turns rolling it to each other. One sunny day, Tim and Sam were playing with the baseball. Sam rolled the ball too hard, and it went into the woods. They both looked for the baseball, but they could not find it. Tim was sad and Sam was sorry. Sam said, "I am honest, Tim. I did not mean to lose the baseball." Tim said, "It's okay, Sam. We can look for it again tomorrow." They both tried to be happy, but they missed the baseball. The next day, Tim and Sam went back to the woods. They looked and looked for the baseball. Finally, Tim found it under a big leaf. They were both so happy! Tim said, "We found it, Sam! Now we can play again." Sam smiled and said, "Yes, and I will be more careful when I roll the ball." They played with the baseball all day, and they were the best of friends.

One day, a big cat and a small cat were playing in the yard. They saw a smelly jug under a tree. The big cat said, "I want to take the jug home." The small cat said, "No, let me take it." They both wanted the jug. The big cat and the small cat started to fight. They were not being nice to each other. They both pulled the jug and it broke into pieces. The cats were sad. They did not have the jug anymore. A wise old dog saw what happened. He said, "Cats, you should have shared the jug. Now no one can have it." The big cat and the small cat learned that sharing is good. They said sorry to each other and became friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty purse that she loved very much. She took her purse with her everywhere she went. One day, Sue went to the park to play. While Sue was playing, she saw a big tree. She decided to cover her pretty purse with leaves so no one would take it. She then went to play with her friends. They played and played until the sun went down. When it was time to go home, Sue went back to the big tree to get her pretty purse. But when she moved the leaves, she found something unexpected! Instead of her purse, there was a big, pretty box. Inside the box, there was an even prettier purse! Sue was so happy and couldn't believe her eyes. She took her new pretty purse and went home, excited to share the story with her family.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat cereal every morning. One day, Spot forgot to eat his cereal before he went out to play. When Spot was outside, he saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree to see what was up there. Spot tried to climb, but he was not strong enough. He thought maybe if he had eaten his cereal, he would be strong. Spot felt sad that he could not climb the tree. He went back home and ate his cereal, but it was too late. The next day, Spot still could not climb the tree. Spot learned that forgetting to eat his cereal made him weak, and he could not do the things he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to twist his toy snake. One day, Tim saw a big ink bottle on the table. He thought it would be fun to twist the ink bottle too. Tim grabbed the ink bottle and twisted it. "Oh no!" he said, as the ink spilled all over the floor. Tim felt ashamed because he made a big mess. He tried to clean it up, but the ink spread everywhere. Tim's mom came in and saw the mess. She was not happy. "Tim, you should not play with things that are not yours," she said. Tim felt even more ashamed. He knew he should have asked before touching the ink. The moral of the story is to always ask before touching things that don't belong to you.
