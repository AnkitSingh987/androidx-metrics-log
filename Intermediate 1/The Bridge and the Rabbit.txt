Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bridge and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, there was a big bridge. The bridge was over a river. Many animals liked to play near the river. One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle wanted to have a race. They were very competitive. They wanted to see who could cross the bridge first. They asked their friends to watch and cheer. The race started, and the rabbit ran very fast. But he did not remember the way to the bridge. The turtle walked slowly but he remembered the way. The turtle crossed the bridge first and won the race. The rabbit was sad, but he learned to remember the way next time.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun who loved to go up and down every day. The sun was friends with the moon. The moon would come out when it was dark, and the sun would come out when it was light. One day, the sun said, "Moon, let's play a game. I will go up, and then you will come out when it is dark." The moon said, "Okay, Sun, that sounds fun!" So, the sun went up and shone brightly. All the animals and people were happy because they could see and play. When the sun went down, it was dark, and the moon came out. The moon was not as bright as the sun, but it was still pretty. The stars came out too, and they all danced in the sky. The next day, the sun came back up, and it was bright again. The sun and the moon kept playing their game, making it light and dark for everyone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a fierce dog. He liked to bark and play all day. One day, Spot saw a big stove in the kitchen. He had never seen a stove before. Spot thought the stove was a big toy. He wanted to play with it. But the stove was hot. Spot did not know that the stove was hot. He jumped up and touched the stove with his paw. "Ouch!" cried Spot. The stove had burned his paw. Spot's mom heard him cry and came running. She saw Spot's burned paw and felt sad. She told Spot that the stove was not a toy and that it was hot. Spot learned his lesson. He never went near the stove again. And they lived happily ever after.

Alice always loved to climb trees. She felt free and safe high up in their branches, reaching up their strong branches. One summer day, her mommy said she couldn't climb the trees anymore. She said there was a law that said she couldn't, and that it was too dangerous. Alice was sad and frustrated. She was so used to the warm safety of the trees. But she still followed the law. One day when Alice was feeling particularly sad, her daddy took her to the city park. There, they discovered a tall tree with strong branches. Before Alice could protest, her daddy said, "Honey, remember the law. You must stay safe." Alice was determined to find a way to reach the tree. Then, she noticed the rope swing hanging from a branch! She jumped onto the swing and began to soar high into the tree. Up, up she reached until she was close enough to touch the leaves. Alice laughed and smiled for the first time in weeks. Today, she was allowed to be free, and safe, as she reached up into the tree.

Once upon a time, Jack and his mum were playing in the garden. They played tag and they laughed so much they cried! The day was so great. Then Jack found something very special. He found a zip! He ran to his mum and said "Look Mum! Look what I found!" His Mum smiled and said "Well done Jack! Let's try and fix the zip, maybe you can put it on something and make it special." So Jack and his mum tried to fix the zip but, sadly, it wouldn't turn. They couldn't make it work. Jack was very sad and said "I wanted to make something special with the zip". His Mum gave him a hug and said "We can try again, don't worry". But then suddenly, there was a big crack of thunder and the sky turned very dark. Rain started to pour down. Jack and his mum ran inside with the zip but, sadly, the rain was too much and when they got home the zip was all ruined. They looked at the broken zip and Jack started to cry. His mum hugged him and said "It's ok, Jack. Let's have something nice to eat". Jack and his mum tried to fix the zip but it was no use. The zip was destroyed forever. Even though the day had started off so great, it did not have a happy ending.
