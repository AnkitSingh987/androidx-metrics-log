Category: Intermediate
Title: Cindy and the Funny

Once upon a time, there was a careless girl called Cindy. One day, Cindy decided to design something special. She made a plan in her head and set to work. She collected some bits and pieces from around her house. Then, she put them together to make something special – a funny little creature. She looked at it proudly and asked her family for their opinion. Her family thought it was really creative and clever. They said Cindy had made something really special. Cindy was thrilled and hugged the funny creature tightly. From that day on, Cindy was very careful not to be careless anymore. She made sure to take care of her funny creature and always listen to her family’s opinion. And they all lived happily ever after!

Tom and Anna like to play with blocks. They build towers and houses and cars with the blocks. They have many colors of blocks: red, blue, yellow, green and more. One day, Tom and Anna have a fight. Tom wants to use all the blue blocks, but Anna wants some too. Tom says, "No, they are mine! Go away!" Anna says, "No, they are ours! Share!" They pull and push the blocks. The blocks fall down and break. The blocks make a loud noise. Tom and Anna cry. Mom hears the noise and comes to see. She sees the broken blocks and the sad children. She says, "What happened here? Why did you break the blocks?" Tom and Anna say, "We had a fight. We wanted the blue blocks." Mom says, "That is not a good reason to break the blocks. You should not fight. You should be kind and share. The blocks are for both of you. Breaking the blocks is a terrible thing to do. You have to say sorry and fix the blocks." Tom and Anna feel bad. They say, "Sorry, Mom. Sorry, Tom. Sorry, Anna." They hug and make up. They pick up the broken blocks and put them back together. They say, "We will not break the blocks again. We will play nicely and share." Mom smiles and says, "That is good. I am proud of you. You learned a good word today: sorry. Sorry is a word that can fix many things. Now you can have fun with the blocks again." Tom and Anna say, "Thank you, Mom. We love you." They play with the blocks and have fun. They use all the colors of the blocks. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful man with a big mustache. His name was Bob. He liked to help people and fix things. One day, Bob saw a little girl crying. Her toy was broken. Bob wanted to help. He took the toy and used his tools to fix it. The little girl was so happy. She thanked Bob for being so kind. Bob went home with a big smile on his face. His mustache wiggled as he walked. He felt good because he had helped someone. And that is what thoughtful people with big mustaches do. They help and fix things for others.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big bell on a hill. Sue wanted to hear the bell ring. So, she walked up the hill to the bell. Sue pulled the rope to ring the bell. It made a loud sound. She was very proud of herself for ringing the bell. She rang the bell again and again. The sound made her happy. Soon, her friends heard the bell too. They came to the hill to see what was making the noise. Sue showed them the big bell. They all took turns ringing the bell. They were proud and happy too.

One day, an adorable ostrich named Ollie went to the park. Ollie loved to play in the sand. He saw a big pile of sand and ran to it. Ollie used his big feet to mix the sand. He mixed and mixed until the sand was soft and fun to play in. A little girl named Lily saw Ollie and walked over. She said, "Hi, Ostrich! Can I play with you?" Ollie nodded his head and they started to play together. They mixed the sand and made a big sand castle. As they played, more kids came to join them. They all mixed the sand and built a huge sand castle together. Ollie and the kids had a great time. When it was time to go home, they all said goodbye to their new friend Ollie. The adorable ostrich smiled and waved as the kids left the park.
