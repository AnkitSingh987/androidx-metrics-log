Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Strong Boy and the Bird

One day, a strong boy named Tom and his little sister, Sue, were playing in the park. They saw a big box with the word "case" on it. Tom and Sue were very curious about what was inside the case. Tom tried to open the case, but it was too heavy. Sue said, "Let me help!" Together, they pushed and pulled, but the case still would not open. They were sad and did not know what to do. Just then, a little bird flew by and landed on the case. The bird said, "I can help you open the case, but you must promise to share what's inside." Tom and Sue agreed. The bird sang a special song and, to their surprise, the case opened. Inside, they found lots of toys and games. They played with the bird and shared the toys, and they all became good friends.

One day, a cat and a dog had a race on a track. The cat was very fast, and the dog was very big. The sun was shining, but the wind was bitter. They both wanted to win the race. As they raced, the cat was in front of the dog. The dog tried to run faster, but the cat was too quick. The dog was sad because he thought he would lose the race. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and picked up the cat! The bird took the cat far away. Now, the dog was in front. The dog won the race and was very happy.

Lily liked her scooter very much. She rode it every day in the park with her mom and dad. She liked to go fast and make noises with her mouth. She felt happy and free on her scooter. One day, she saw a big boy with a bigger scooter. He had a helmet and stickers and a horn. Lily wanted his scooter. She asked her mom and dad if she could have it. They said no, it was not hers, it was his. Lily was sad and angry. She wanted his scooter. She waited until the big boy was not looking. Then she ran to his scooter and tried to take it. The big boy saw her and shouted. "Hey, that's mine! Give it back!" He ran after her and grabbed the scooter. Lily did not let go. She pulled and kicked and screamed. "No, it's mine! I want it!" The big boy was stronger and bigger. He pushed Lily away and made her fall. Lily hurt her knee and her elbow. She cried and cried. Her mom and dad came and picked her up. They were not happy. They scolded her and said she was naughty and rude. They said she had to say sorry to the big boy and his scooter. Lily did not want to say sorry. She was still angry and wanted his scooter. She was persistent. She marched to the big boy and his scooter. She tried to hit him and take his scooter again. The big boy was ready. He honked his horn and scared Lily. He hit her back and made her fall again. Lily hurt her head and her nose. She cried and cried more. Her mom and dad came and picked her up again. They were very angry. They said she was very naughty and very rude. They said she had to go home and no more scooter for a week. Lily did not like that. She did not like the big boy and his scooter. She did not like her mom and dad. She did not like anything. She had a bad day. She had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry wolf. The wolf lived in a big forest. He was very hungry and wanted to find some food. One day, the hungry wolf found a big, red apple. He was so happy! He picked up the apple and started to chew it. The apple was very yummy and sweet. The wolf chewed and chewed until the apple was all gone. After eating the apple, the wolf was not hungry anymore. He was very full and happy. The wolf went back to his home in the forest, and took a long nap. Now, every time the wolf got hungry, he would find a big, red apple to chew and be happy again.
