Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Leaf

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a tiny bug with a fierce face. Sally was not scared. She bent down to look at the bug. The bug had a little leaf. The leaf was mint green. Sally said, "Hi, little bug! I like your mint leaf." The bug whispered, "Thank you, Sally! I like it too." Sally and the bug became friends. They played in the park and had fun. As the sun went down, Sally said, "I have to go home now, little bug." The bug whispered, "I will miss you, Sally." Sally smiled and said, "I will come back to see you soon." Then, Sally went home, and the little bug went back to its mint leaf.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry little dog named Bob. He loved to bite things. One day, he saw a big book on the table. The book was a novel. Bob jumped up and grabbed the novel in his mouth. Bob's friend, a cat named Tom, saw him and said, "Bob, don't bite that book! It's not food!" But Bob was too hungry to listen. He kept biting the novel, making big holes in it. Bob's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was very sad and said, "Bob, you made a big mess and ruined the book! Now you can't have your favorite toy for a whole week." Bob felt bad and knew he should have listened to Tom. From that day on, he never bit a book again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his uncle. They played and had a lot of fun. Tim was an obedient boy and always listened to his uncle. While playing, Tim saw a big, red ball. He ran to his uncle and said, "Look, uncle! I find a ball!" His uncle smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Let's play with it." They kicked the ball back and forth, laughing and having a great time. After a while, it was time to go home. Tim hugged his uncle and said, "Thank you for playing with me, uncle. I love you." His uncle hugged him back and said, "I love you too, Tim. You are a very good boy."

Once upon a time there was a mum and a dad who wanted to lend a song to a friend. So they went to the park to look for their friend. As they arrived they saw their friend playing on the swing and another girl playing a game. The mum and dad asked their friend if they wanted to borrow the song. The friend said yes and the mum and dad gave the song to them. Then the two girls started to sing their song together. Everyone in the park was amazed. They sang and sang until they got to the end. Then they had a competition to see who could sing the best. They sang louder and louder until the mum and dad said who was the winner. At the end, the friend was the winner and she was very happy. They all had a great time singing and playing together. They all laughed and gave each other big hugs. Then, when it was time for them to go home, their friend thanked the mum and dad for lending them the song.

Once upon a time, there was a boy in blue pants. He was always very excited, and his friends would often encourage him to do things that he wanted to do. He always wanted to try new things, but he was too shy. One day, he noticed something down the street. It was a bright yellow railing along the sidewalk. He was curious and wanted to try it out, but he was too scared. His friends encouraged him to go for it. He took a deep breath, then stepped up and started sliding down the railing. As he went down, his pants became bright yellow too. He was so excited that he let out a huge shout of joy! His friends had encouraged him to try something new, and in the end he had a lot of fun. From then on, anytime he saw a similar railing, he remembered his journey down the yellow railing and was always happy and eager to slide around.
