Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Sugar

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog was very persistent. He liked to play all day. One day, he found a big bag of sugar. The dog was very happy. The little dog played with the bag of sugar. He put his nose in the sugar. Then, he sneezed. The sugar went everywhere. The dog sneezed again and again. He could not stop sneezing. The dog's sneezes made a big mess. The sugar was all over the floor. The dog's house was not clean anymore. The dog was sad. The end.

One day, two friends, Jack and Jane, were walking together down the street. Jack and Jane were taking a walk to enjoy the nice day. Jane said to Jack, "This day is so dull. Let's do something exciting!" Jack thought for a moment and stepped quickly, leading Jane to a nearby park. When they arrived, they could hear children laughing and running around. Jane looked around and said, "This is much better. It's not so dull here!" Jack smiled and said, "Life is much better when we take steps out of our comfort zone". Jane agreed with a nod and they both started to run around, laughing and playing together. The two friends smiled and laughed, enjoying the wonderful moments of their lives. They stepped out of their ordinary life, and stepped into an exciting park. They looked around and realized that dull wasn't so bad after all.

One day, a loyal dog named Spot went for a visit to his friend, a cat named Fluffy. They liked to play and have fun together. Spot and Fluffy were very happy to see each other. While they were playing, they found a big box. The box was closed and they wanted to know what was inside. Spot and Fluffy tried to solve how to open the box. After trying a few times, they finally opened the box. Inside the box, they found many toys to play with. Spot and Fluffy were very happy and played with the toys all day.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a fun maze. Many kids liked to play in the maze. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the green maze. He was having lots of fun running and finding his way through the maze. As Tim played, he found a pretty toy car on the ground. He liked the toy car very much and wanted to keep it. But then he thought, "Maybe someone lost this toy car." So, Tim decided to return the toy car to its owner. Tim asked other kids in the maze if the toy car was theirs. Finally, he found a sad little girl who had lost her toy car. She was so happy when Tim returned it to her. They both played with the toy car and became good friends. The moral of the story is that it's good to return things that don't belong to us, and doing so can make new friends.

Tim and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a small kite in the grass. It is red and blue and has a long tail. "Look, a kite!" Tim says. "Can we fly it?" "Yes, let's try!" Mia says. They run to a hill and hold the kite. The wind is strong and the kite goes up in the sky. "Wow, it is so high!" Tim says. "It is beautiful!" They take turns to hold the kite. They are happy and hug each other. But then, a big bird sees the kite. It thinks it is a fish. It flies fast and grabs the kite with its claws. It tears the kite and flies away. "NO!" Tim and Mia shout. "Our kite!" They are sad and cry. They hug each other again. They have no kite and no fun. They go home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a youth who loved to play and have fun. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car. He was so happy and excited. While playing, Tim saw a helpless baby bird on the ground. The baby bird had a tear in its wing. Tim wanted to help the baby bird, so he called his mom. His mom came and saw the baby bird too. Together, Tim and his mom took care of the baby bird. They made it feel better and helped fix the tear in its wing. Soon, the baby bird was happy and could fly again. Tim felt proud that he could help the baby bird.
