Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and Jill and the Bread

Once upon a time, there were two siblings, Jack and Jill. They were filled with joy and never seemed to quit laughing and playing. On a sunny day, Jack wanted to make breakfast. He wanted to make toast, but he noticed that he had used the last piece of bread. He told his sister, "Jill, I need to get some more bread from the store. Will you come with me?" Jill hopped onto Jack's back, and off they went on a wild adventure. They went to the store and bought bread, then hurried back home. Once home, Jack started toasting the bread. Suddenly it caught fire! Jill and Jack couldn't believe it. Suddenly, Jack had a plan. He took a pan of water and splashed it onto the fire, and it quit burning. The siblings laughed with glee. They had saved their toast, and now, breakfast was ready!

One day, a little fish was swimming in the ocean. He saw a big wave coming. The little fish was scared. He swam fast to tell his friend, the crab. "Big wave coming! We must hide!" said the little fish. The crab was hungry. He did not want to hide. He wanted to find food. But the little fish said, "No! We must hide now!" So, they both hid under a rock. They waited for the big wave to pass. But the big wave did not come. Instead, a big, kind whale came. The whale said, "I am sorry, little friends. I made the big wave. I did not want to scare you." The little fish and the crab were not scared anymore. They were happy. The whale shared his food with the hungry crab. They all became good friends and played in the ocean together.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy frog named Fred. He lived in a small pond with his friends. Fred was always grumpy because he wanted to be kissed by someone. He thought that a kiss would make him happy. One day, a big storm came to the pond. The sky was dark and there was lightning. All the animals were scared, but not Fred. He thought that maybe the lightning could give him a magic kiss. Fred hopped out of the pond and looked up at the sky. The lightning came down and gave him a tiny kiss on his nose. Suddenly, Fred was not grumpy anymore. He felt happy and jumped back into the pond with his friends. They all played together, and Fred was never grumpy again.

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince liked to jog. He jogged every day. One day, the sky was dark. But the prince wanted to jog. As he jogged, he saw a cat. The cat was under a tree. The tree was big. The prince said, "Hi, cat! Do you like to jog too?" The cat said, "Meow!" The prince and the cat jogged together. They jogged and jogged. Soon, the dark sky was not dark anymore. The sun came out. The prince and the cat were happy. They jogged back to the castle. They were friends forever.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go to the store. The store was a big place with many things. Tim's mom said, "We can go, but we can only get one thing. We can't get expensive things." At the store, Tim saw a big, red ball. He wanted to get it. But then, he saw a small, green frog. Tim liked the frog more. He asked his mom, "Can I get the frog, please?" His mom looked and said, "Yes, you can get the frog. It is not expensive." Tim was happy with his new frog. But when they got home, something unexpected happened. The frog started to talk! "Thank you for getting me," said the frog. Tim was very surprised. He and the frog became best friends and had many fun times together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a magic plant in her garden. The plant could disappear when she said the magic word, "Poof!" One sunny day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. They were playing ball, and the ball went into the garden. Tim went to get the ball, but he stepped on the magic plant. The plant disappeared! Lily was sad and said, "Oh no, my plant is gone!" Tim felt sorry for stepping on the plant. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I didn't know." Lily forgave him and said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's say the magic word together." They both said, "Poof!" and the magic plant came back. They were both happy and continued to play together in the garden.
