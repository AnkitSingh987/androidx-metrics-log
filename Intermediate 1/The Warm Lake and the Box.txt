Category: Intermediate
Title: The Warm Lake and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a warm lake in a big forest. Many animals liked to play in the lake. One day, a little duck found a key by the lake. The duck showed the key to his friends. They all wanted to unlock something. The animals looked for what the key could unlock. They looked near the lake and in the forest. Then, they found a big box. The little duck put the key in the box and turned it. The box unlocked. When the box opened, a big frog jumped out! The frog had been locked in the box for a long time. The animals were surprised but happy. The frog thanked the little duck for unlocking the box. Now, the frog could play in the warm lake with his new friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. Tom liked to wear a tight hat. One day, he went to the woods to find a big bear. In the woods, Tom met a small boy. The boy said, "Your hat is too tight. It does not fit." Tom did not like what the boy said. He wanted to show the boy that his hat was good. Tom saw the big bear and tried to catch it. But the tight hat made it hard for him to think. The bear saw Tom and growled. Tom was scared and ran away. The small boy helped Tom take off the tight hat. Tom learned that it was better to wear a hat that fit. He thanked the boy and they became friends. From that day on, Tom wore a hat that fit well and he was a happy hunter.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a mysterious turkey in his yard. The turkey was big and had many colors. Tim was very curious and wanted to talk to the turkey. He went outside and said, "Hello, Mr. Turkey! What are you doing here?" The turkey just looked at Tim and did not say anything. Then, Tim asked the turkey, "Do you want to be my friend?" The turkey nodded its head. Tim was very happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Tim's mom saw them playing and smiled. But then, something bad happened. The turkey got very hungry and wanted to eat Tim's food. Tim said, "No, Mr. Turkey! You cannot eat my food." The turkey got angry and started to chase Tim. Tim ran and ran, but the turkey was too fast. The turkey caught Tim and took him away. Tim's mom looked for Tim everywhere, but she could not find him. She was very sad and cried. The mysterious turkey had taken her little boy away, and nobody knew where they went. And so, the story ends with Tim's mom still looking for her lost son.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there lived a gentle cat named Whiskers. Whiskers loved to play with his friends, a dog named Spot and a bird named Chirpy. They played in the park every day. One day, while they were playing, they saw a little girl crying. Whiskers, Spot, and Chirpy wanted to help her. So, they went to the girl and Whiskers asked, "Why are you sad?" The little girl said, "I lost my toy. I can't find it." Whiskers, Spot, and Chirpy decided to help her find her toy. They looked everywhere in the park. At last, they found the toy under a big tree. Whiskers picked up the toy and gently offered it to the little girl. She was very happy and said, "Thank you, Whiskers, Spot, and Chirpy!" They all became friends and played together in the park every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a big red boot. She did not know who it belonged to. Sally thought, "I should mail this boot back to its owner." So, she put the boot in a big box. Sally went to the post office to mail the big red boot. On the way, she met her friend Tim. Tim said, "Hi Sally! What's in the box?" Sally said, "A big red boot. I am going to mail it to the owner." Tim thought it was a crazy idea, but he wanted to help his friend. At the post office, Sally and Tim met a man with only one red boot on his foot. He was sad. Sally thought, "This must be the owner!" She opened the box and showed him the big red boot. The man was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Sally and Tim! You found my missing boot!" Sally and Tim felt good for helping the man find his boot. They knew mailing the boot was not a crazy idea after all.
