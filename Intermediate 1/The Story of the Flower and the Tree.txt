Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Flower and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big, huge tree. Under the tree, there was a pretty flower. The flower was red and yellow. It was very happy to be under the huge tree. One day, a small bug came to the flower. The bug was tired. The flower said, "Relax, little bug. You can sit on me." The bug was happy and sat on the flower. They both enjoyed the sun and the wind. Soon, more bugs came to the flower. They all wanted to relax and have fun. The flower was happy to have friends. The huge tree was happy too. They all enjoyed a lovely day together.

Once upon a time, in a big stadium, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his red ball. One day, the ball went far away from him. He could not see it. Tim was sad. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Can you help me find my ball?" Sam said, "Yes! Let's look for it together." They looked high and low, but they could not find the red ball. The sky was clear and the sun was shining. Finally, they managed to find the ball under a big tree. Tim was so happy! He said, "Thank you, Sam!" They played with the red ball all day long in the big stadium. They had so much fun together.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the cold woods. She wore a big, warm coat to keep her warm. As she walked, she saw a lot of waste on the ground. Mia did not like the waste, so she picked it up and put it in a bag. While Mia was picking up the waste, she heard a noise. She looked around and saw a small, lost dog. The dog was cold and scared. Mia wanted to help the dog, so she took off her coat and wrapped it around the dog to keep him warm. Mia and the dog walked together until they met a kind man. The man knew the dog and took them both to the dog's home. The dog's family was very happy to see him and thanked Mia for her help. They all became good friends and promised to keep the woods clean and free from waste.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. Tim had a favorite fruit, and it was a grape. He would eat grapes every day after playing. One day, Tim saw a big grape on the ground. He wanted to eat it, but his mommy said, "No, Tim. That grape is dirty." Tim was sad, but mommy wanted to prevent him from getting sick. Later, mommy gave Tim clean grapes to eat. Tim was so happy. He shared his grapes with his friends, and they all played together. From that day on, Tim always listened to his mommy to stay healthy and happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They walked in the park and saw many things. Tim was very happy to see all the things around him. He saw a big tree, some flowers, and a pond. Then, he saw a cactus. He had never seen a cactus before. "Mom, what is that?" Tim asked, pointing at the cactus. His mom replied, "That is a cactus, Tim. It is a plant that grows in the desert. It has sharp points, so be careful not to touch it." Tim looked at the cactus and thought it was very interesting. He wanted to see more of them. But soon, he started to feel tired. His mom saw that he was tired and said, "Let's go home and rest, Tim. We can come back another day to see more cacti." As they walked back home, Tim thought about the cactus he saw. He knew he was too tired to see more today, but he was excited to come back again. He couldn't wait to see more cacti and learn about the desert. The next day, Tim and his mom went back to the park. Tim saw many more cacti, and his mom told him stories about the desert. Tim was very happy to learn about the cacti and the desert, and he wasn't tired anymore.
