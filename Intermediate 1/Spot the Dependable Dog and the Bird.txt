Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dependable Dog and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Spot. Spot always helped his friends. One day, his friend, a little bird, felt sick. Spot wanted to help the bird feel better. Spot found a thermometer to see if the bird was hot. He put the thermometer under the bird's wing. The bird was hot! Spot knew he needed to help the bird cool down. Spot had an idea. He found a balloon filled with cool air. Spot knew if he could release the air, it would help the bird. So, Spot let the air out of the balloon. The cool air made the bird feel better. The bird thanked Spot for being such a dependable friend.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer named Lily. She loved to dance all day long. Lily was very soft and kind to everyone she met. One sunny day, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom had a toy boat. He said, "Lily, do you want to sail with me?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to sail with you, Tom!" They went to the pond and put the toy boat in the water. The boat started to sail. Lily danced on the grass near the pond, and Tom watched the boat sail. They played and laughed together, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky fish named Fin. Fin lived in the big blue sea near a port. The port was a fun place where big boats and little boats would come and go. Fin loved to watch the boats and play with his fish friends. One day, a kind sailor named Sam came to the port. Sam saw Fin and thought he was very special. Sam wanted to teach Fin some new tricks. Fin was so excited to learn from Sam. Every day, Sam would come to the port and teach Fin. Fin learned how to jump high and swim fast. Fin was a very lucky fish to have a friend like Sam. They had so much fun together at the port. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a unique car. This car had a motor that could talk. The car and the motor were best friends. They liked to go on trips together. One day, they wanted to go on a trip to the park. The car said to the motor, "Let's start our trip!" The motor was happy and started to go. They saw many fun things on the way. They saw birds, dogs, and big trees. When they got to the park, they saw kids playing. The car and the motor were happy to be at the park. They had a great day together. The unique car and the talking motor went home and had a good night's sleep. They could not wait for their next trip.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy that could shoot. He liked to play with it all the time. It was fun to shoot at things like trees and rocks. Tim was very happy with his new toy. One day, a stranger came to Tim's house. The stranger was big and looked difficult to talk to. Tim was scared of the stranger, so he hid behind a tree with his toy that could shoot. Tim thought it would be fun to shoot at the stranger with his toy. But when he did, the stranger got very angry. The stranger came closer and took Tim's toy away. Tim was very sad and scared. The stranger left with Tim's toy and never came back. Tim learned that it was not good to shoot at strangers. He missed his toy and wished he had not done that. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tom and his pet dog, Buddy. Every evening, Tom and Buddy would play in the yard. They loved to run and jump together. The yard was their favorite place to be. One day, while they were playing, Tom saw a big, dangerous snake. He was scared and shouted, "Buddy, run! There's a dangerous snake!" Buddy heard Tom and ran away from the snake. Tom ran to his mom and told her about the snake. Tom's mom was brave. She knew how to keep the snake away. She told Tom, "We need to add a fence around our yard to keep the dangerous snake out." So, Tom and his mom worked together to add a fence. The fence made them feel safe. That evening, Tom and Buddy played in the yard again. They were happy and not scared of the dangerous snake anymore. Tom said, "Thank you, Mom, for adding the fence. Now, Buddy and I can play without being scared."
