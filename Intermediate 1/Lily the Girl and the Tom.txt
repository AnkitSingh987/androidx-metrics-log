Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Tom

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily had an orange cat named Tom. Lily loved to write in her journal every day. One day, Tom saw Lily's journal on the floor. Tom thought it was a toy and played with it. He tore pages from the journal. Lily came back and saw her journal on the floor. She was very sad and angry. "Tom, you ruined my journal!" she cried. Tom looked at her with big eyes, feeling bad for what he did. "Please forgive me, Lily," Tom said in a small voice. Lily did not want to forgive Tom. She was still very upset. "No, Tom, I cannot forgive you," Lily said. Tom felt very sad and went to hide under the bed. Lily's mom saw her sad face and asked what happened. Lily told her mom about Tom and the journal. Her mom said, "Sometimes we need to forgive when someone makes a mistake." But Lily did not listen to her mom. Lily and Tom did not play together anymore. They both stayed sad for a long time. The end.

One day, a big oak tree and a small bush lived next to a clean pond. The bush was always happy. But the oak tree would always complain. It did not like being so tall. One day, a strong wind came. The wind pushed the bush and the oak tree. The bush bent and swayed. But the oak tree was too tall and too stiff. It could not bend like the bush. The oak tree fell into the clean pond. It made a big splash. The bush felt sad for the oak tree. But the oak tree learned to not complain. It saw that being tall was not always good. The bush and the oak tree became friends. They lived happily by the clean pond.

Once upon a time, there was a rare bird named Blue. Blue was very wise and liked to teach. One day, Blue found a toy gun in the forest. He thought it would be fun to play with. Blue decided to teach the other birds how to play with the toy gun. They all had fun pretending to be heroes, but they did not know that they should be careful with it. One day, a little bird named Red was playing with the toy gun and it made a loud noise. All the birds got scared and flew away. Blue felt bad for scaring his friends. He learned that even toys can be scary if not used carefully. So, Blue decided to teach the birds a new game, like hide and seek. All the birds had fun and were happy again. The moral of the story is to always be careful with what you play with, and to learn from your mistakes.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They saw a big slide and wanted to try it. But there was a problem. A fierce dog was guarding the slide. It had sharp teeth and a loud bark. It made Lila and Ben tremble with fear. "Go away, dog!" Ben shouted. "We want to slide!" But the dog did not listen. It growled and showed its teeth. It looked like it wanted to bite them. Lila had an idea. She took a coin from her pocket. She threw it far away from the slide. The dog saw the coin and ran after it. It thought it was a shiny toy. "Quick, Ben! Now we can slide!" Lila said. They ran to the slide and climbed up. They slid down fast and had fun. They laughed and cheered. The dog came back with the coin in its mouth. It saw that Lila and Ben were on the slide. It was angry and barked again. But Lila and Ben did not care. They had tricked the dog. They were brave and smart. They slid down again and again. They enjoyed the slide. The dog could not stop them.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named John. John was an ordinary boy who lived in a small town with his mom and dad. One day, John decided to go for a walk in the woods. He was excited and wanted to explore. As he walked, he heard a voice. It said, "John, come here". He thought it was strange, but he decided to follow it. He followed the voice deep into the woods until he came to a clearing. There he met a strange man with a sly look in his eyes. The man asked John to sit down and John obeyed. The man then began asking John lots of questions. John got scared and wanted to leave. He tried to run away, but the strange man blocked his path. The man said, "If you don't stay, I'll hurt your parents". So, with fear in his eyes, John stayed put. John was never seen again, even after his parents searched for him. He had disappeared and no one knew what happened to him. Unfortunately, it was an ordinary day for John that had a bad ending.
