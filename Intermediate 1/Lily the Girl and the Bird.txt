Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. Lily was a dependable girl, and she always took care of her dress. One day, Lily was playing with her friend, Tim. They were having fun, when suddenly, her dress vanished! Lily cried, "Oh no, where is my dress?" Tim looked around and said, "I don't know, but we will find it." They searched and searched for the dress, but they couldn't find it. Just as they were about to give up, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird had Lily's dress in its beak! The bird said, "I'm sorry, I thought your dress was a pretty flower. Here, you can have it back." Lily was so happy to have her dress back, and she thanked the bird. From that day on, Lily and Tim had a new friend, the big bird, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there lived a nice doll. She lived in a peaceful room in an old house. One day, the doll got very broken. She couldn't move her arms or legs. The doll was very sad. She said, "I wish someone could help me." The next day, a nice girl called Lucy came to the room. She said, "I can help you!" Lucy began to repair the doll very carefully. She fixed the doll's arms and legs. When it was done, the doll was feeling better than ever. She could move her arms and legs again. The doll thanked Lucy for her help. She said, "Thank you for making me feel peaceful again."

One day, a little fish was swimming in the sea. The fish saw a big, shiny pick at the bottom of the water. The fish was scared, but it wanted the pick. It took a deep breath and said, "I can do it!" The fish swam down fast and took a dive to reach the pick. Just as it was about to grab it, a big crab said, "Stop! That's my pick!" The fish was scared and stopped. The crab looked at the fish and said, "You're brave for trying to get my pick. I will let you have it if you help me." The little fish agreed and asked, "What can I do for you?" The crab said, "I lost my friend, the starfish. Can you help me find her?" The fish was no longer scared and felt happy to help the crab. They swam together, and soon they found the starfish. As a thank you, the crab gave the pick to the little fish. The fish, the crab, and the starfish became good friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little monster named Momo. Momo was not like other monsters. He was light green and very nice. He had a big smile and loved to play with his friends. One day, Momo and his friends were playing in the park. They were having so much fun. Momo saw a little girl sitting alone. She looked sad. Momo wanted to help. He walked up to her and said, "Hi, I am Momo. Do you want to play with us?" The little girl looked at Momo and smiled. "Yes, I would like that," she said. So, Momo and the little girl played with his friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day long. As the sun started to set, Momo knew it was time to go home. Momo and his friends said goodbye to the little girl. They were happy they made a new friend. Momo went home and settled into his cozy bed. He was very tired from playing all day, but he was happy. Momo knew he was not like other monsters, and that was okay.

Once upon a time, in a warm summer, there was a thoughtful cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They would run and play in the sun all day. But one day, a big dog came to their home. The big dog said, "I want to play too!" Fluffy and Lily were scared. They did not want to disturb the big dog, but they did not want to play with him either. Fluffy whispered to Lily, "What should we do?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "We can try to play with the big dog, but we must be careful." So, Fluffy, Lily, and the big dog started to play together. But the big dog was not careful. He played too rough and ended up hurting Fluffy and Lily. They both cried and wished they had not played with the big dog. The big dog felt bad, but it was too late. Fluffy and Lily learned that sometimes it is better to be safe than to play with someone who might hurt them.
