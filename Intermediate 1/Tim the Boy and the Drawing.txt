Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drawing

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big box of crayons. He loved to draw and decorate with them. One day, Tim decided to make a special picture for his mom. Tim picked up his crayons and started to draw. He drew a big sun, some trees, and a happy family. He used many colors to make the picture pretty. Tim felt dizzy because he was drawing so fast. He wanted to finish the picture before his mom came home. When Tim was done, he showed the picture to his dad. His dad smiled and said, "Wow! You did a great job, Tim!" Tim felt proud of his picture. He could not wait to show it to his mom. When his mom came home, she loved the picture too. Tim was happy that he could decorate something special for his mom with his crayons.

Lila loved horses. She wanted to ride one every day. Her mom took her to a stable where many horses lived. There, Lila could choose a horse to ride. Lila looked at all the horses. They were big and strong and beautiful. Some were brown, some were black, some were white. Some had spots, some had stripes, some had stars. Lila wanted to ride them all. But one horse was different. He was smaller than the others. He had a long mane and a fluffy tail. He was gray with a white nose. He looked at Lila with kind eyes. He nickered softly. Lila walked up to him. She reached out her hand and touched his nose. He sniffed her and licked her fingers. Lila giggled. She felt a connection with him. She knew he was the one. She asked her mom if she could ride him. Her mom said yes. She helped Lila put on a helmet and a saddle. She lifted Lila onto the horse. She told her his name was Lucky. She said he was a gifted horse. He could do many tricks and run very fast. Lila smiled. She hugged Lucky's neck. She whispered in his ear. She said she was happy to choose him. She said he was her friend. Lucky nodded. He was happy too. He trotted around the stable with Lila. They had fun together. They were a perfect pair.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a book with a poem. A little girl named Lily loved to read the poem every day. One day, Lily found that the poem was gone. She felt sad. She looked everywhere, but could not find it. Then, she had an idea. She would replace the poem with a new one. Lily thought hard and wrote a new poem. It was about a cat and a dog who were friends. She put the new poem in the book. Now, the tidy room had a new poem to read. Lily was happy and read the new poem every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside in the sun. One day, he found a small door in his yard. The door was open. He could see a big, green garden on the other side. Tim was a good boy, but he was weak. He tried to shut the door, but it was too heavy for him. He did not want his mom to be mad, so he asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam was strong and could shut the door. When the door was shut, Tim's mom came outside. She saw the door and was happy. She told Tim and Sam that they did a good job. She said, "Always help your friends when they need it." The moral of the story is to help others when they need it, even if they are weak. It is good to be kind and work together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a tiny ant named Andy. Andy was very rude. He would not share his food with the other ants. He would push them and laugh. The other ants did not like Andy. One day, Andy found a shiny thread. He thought it was special. He wanted to protect it, so he hid it under his bed. The other ants saw him hide it. They talked and made a plan. The next day, the ants took the thread when Andy was not home. When Andy came back, he saw the thread was gone. He cried and cried. The other ants did not help him. The moral of the story is: if you are rude, you will not have friends to help you when you need them.
