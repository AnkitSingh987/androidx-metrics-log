Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Lily the Girl

One day, a little girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went to play in the park. They loved to dig in the soil and look for treasures. Lily had a small shovel and Max used his paws. They found many things like rocks and sticks. Suddenly, Lily saw a big hole in the ground. "Max, look! What is in there?" she asked. Max barked and looked into the hole. It was very deep and dark. Lily thought it might be dangerous, so she told Max, "We need to hurry and tell Mommy about the hole." Lily and Max ran to find Mommy. When they found her, Lily said, "Mommy, there is a big, dangerous hole in the park!" Mommy listened and held Lily's hand. They went to see the hole together. Mommy thanked Lily for telling her and said, "Good job, Lily! You were very brave to tell me about the hole. Now we can make sure no one gets hurt."

One day, a nice dog named Max went to the grocery store. Max liked to welcome his friends when they came to the store. He was a very good dog. The store had many things like apples, bananas, and cookies. Max loved to eat cookies. On a mild day, Max saw a big box at the store. He did not know what was in the box. Max was very curious. He wanted to see what was inside. He thought it could be a new friend. Max opened the box and found a small cat! The cat was scared, but Max was kind. He welcomed the cat and they became friends. Max and the cat played together and shared cookies. They were happy and everyone loved the new friends at the grocery store.

One day, a noisy bird was flying in the sky. The bird liked to sing very loud. It saw a tree with many leaves. The bird wanted to sit on the tree and sing. The bird saw a small boy near the tree. The boy was holding a label. The label had a picture of a cat on it. The boy looked at the bird and said, "Hello, noisy bird! Do you like my label?" The bird looked at the label and nodded its head. It liked the cat picture. The bird flew down and sat on the boy's hand. They both looked at the label together. Suddenly, the bird began to fall. It was scared and closed its eyes. The boy caught the bird before it hit the ground. The bird was happy and sang a thank you song. The boy smiled and said, "You're welcome, noisy bird!" They became good friends and played together every day.

One day, a big cat went to a small shop. The cat wanted to order a big, red ball. The shop man gave the cat the big, red ball. The cat was happy to play with the ball. But the ball was uncomfortable to hold in its mouth. Then, the cat met a small dog. The small dog wanted to play with the big, red ball too. They played with the ball all day. They became good friends. The cat and the dog did not feel uncomfortable with the ball anymore. But one day, the big, red ball popped. The cat and the dog were sad. They went back to the shop to order a new ball. The shop man gave them a big, blue ball. The cat and the dog played with the new ball. The big, blue ball was not uncomfortable. They were happy and played all day.

One day, a little fox went to the market. He saw an expensive toy he wanted. The fox did not have enough money. He was sad. The fox saw a man selling the toy. He went to the man and said, "Please, can I have the toy? I don't have money." The man said, "No, you need money to buy the toy." The fox was very sad and started to walk away. Then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew the toy out of the man's hand. The toy flew high in the air and landed near the fox. The fox was so happy! He picked up the toy and yelled, "Thank you, wind!" The man was surprised but let the fox keep the toy. The fox went home with a big smile on his face.
