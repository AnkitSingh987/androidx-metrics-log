Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a crazy dog who loved to play and run all day. One day, Spot saw a big ball on the left side of the yard. He ran to the ball and started to play with it. As Spot played with the ball, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was clapping her hands and laughing. Spot wanted to make Sue happy, so he ran to her with the ball. Sue clapped even more when she saw the ball. Sue and Spot played with the ball all day. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, Sue gave Spot a big hug. Spot was so happy that he made a new friend. And from that day on, they played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a mom and her little girl, Sue. Sue liked to watch her mom make coffee every morning. The coffee smelled good, but mom said it was for big people. One day, Sue wanted to try the coffee. She asked her mom, "Can I have some coffee, please?" Her mom said, "No, Sue. Coffee is not for little kids. It is sour and can make you feel bad." Sue felt sad but listened to her mom. To prevent Sue from feeling bad, her mom made her a yummy hot chocolate. Sue loved the hot chocolate and felt happy again. She knew her mom was right, and she would wait to try coffee when she was a big girl.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there were many animals. There were cows, pigs, and chickens. They all lived happily together. One day, a spoiled little boy came to the barn. He saw the animals and wanted to write his name on the barn wall. He took a big stick and started to write. The animals were not happy. The cows, pigs, and chickens came up with a plan. They would make the spoiled boy understand that it is not nice to write on the barn. They all worked together and painted the barn. The boy saw the beautiful barn and felt bad. He learned his lesson and never wrote on the barn again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved juice. Every day, he would drink juice from his cup. One day, Tim saw a big bottle of juice on the table. He wanted to drink it, but it was too big for him to hold. Tim had a plan. He would split the juice into two cups. He tried to pour the juice, but it was very messy. Juice spilled all over the table and floor. Tim was sad and worried. He knew his mom would be upset when she saw the mess. Tim heard his mom coming. He quickly grabbed a towel and started to clean the messy juice. When his mom saw him, she smiled. She was happy that Tim was trying to fix his mistake. They cleaned the mess together and then enjoyed the juice in their cups.

One sunny day, two friends, Jessie and Mark, were playing in the park. They wanted to go to the church, so they stretched their legs and started walking. Jessie asked Mark, "Why do we want to go to the church?" Mark smiled and said, "My teachers at school say that church is a dependable place to go. It is filled with lots of good people and it is a happy place." Jessie was excited and said, "Oh! Let's go!" They ran to the church and knocked on the door. A kind lady opened the door and welcomed them inside. They both looked around the church, taking in its beauty and happiness. Jessie asked, "This place is so dependable. What do people do here?" The lady smiled. "People come here to worship and praise our Lord. They also pray, help others and make friends." Jessie and Mark were glad they had found such a dependable place. They thanked the lady and said goodbye. They stretched their legs, ran out of the church, and went back to their playground.
