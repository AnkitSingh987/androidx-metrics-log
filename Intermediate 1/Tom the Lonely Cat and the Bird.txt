Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lonely Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat named Tom. Tom had no friends to play with. Every day at noon, Tom would prepare his lunch and eat alone under a big tree. One sunny day, Tom saw a little bird on the tree. Tom thought, "Maybe I can make friends with the bird." As Tom talked to the bird, he found out that the bird was also lonely. They decided to eat lunch together at noon. The next day, as they prepared their lunch, something unexpected happened. A big dog came to join them! The dog was also lonely and wanted to be friends. Now, Tom, the bird, and the dog were no longer lonely. They became best friends and ate lunch together under the big tree every day at noon.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle dog named Max. Max lived near a big factory. Every day, Max would go and play near the factory. He had many friends there. The factory made toys for kids. Max liked to watch the toys being made. One day, Max saw a big button on the factory wall. It said "increase". Max was curious and wanted to see what would happen if he pressed it. So, he jumped up and pressed the button with his paw. Suddenly, the toys in the factory started to grow bigger and bigger! Max was so surprised! He didn't know that the button would make the toys grow. The big toys were fun to play with, but they were too big for the kids. Max knew he had to fix it. He found another button that said "small". He pressed it, and the toys went back to their normal size. Max learned to be careful with buttons, but he still loved to play near the factory.

Once upon a time, there was a big tank in a room. The tank was full of water and had many fish. The fish were pretty and they liked to swim. The water in the tank was very clear, so people could see the fish well. One day, a little boy named Tim came to see the fish. He liked to watch them swim and play. Tim saw that the fish were showing a display. They were swimming in a circle, and it looked like they were dancing. Tim was very happy to see this. But then, something unexpected happened. A little frog jumped into the tank! The frog wanted to join the fish in their dance. The fish were surprised, but they let the frog dance with them. Now, the tank had fish and a frog, and they all danced together. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. The tank was now even more fun to watch.

Lily and Tom are playing in the kitchen. They want to cook a cake for mom. Lily is bossy. She tells Tom what to do. "Tom, get the eggs. Tom, get the flour. Tom, get the milk. Tom, get the sugar. Tom, get the bowl. Tom, get the spoon. Tom, get the pan. Tom, hurry up!" Lily says. Tom is tired of Lily's orders. He does not have fun. He wants to do something else. "Lily, I don't want to cook. I want to play with my cars. You are too bossy. You do everything yourself." Tom says. Lily is angry. She wants to cook with Tom. She thinks cooking is fun. She does not want to play with cars. She thinks cars are boring. "Tom, you are silly. Cooking is fun. Cars are boring. You have to help me. We are a team. Mom will be happy if we cook a cake for her." Lily says. Tom and Lily start to argue. They raise their voices. They make a mess. They spill the eggs, the flour, the milk, and the sugar. They drop the bowl, the spoon, and the pan. They do not notice mom coming home. Mom sees the kitchen. She sees Lily and Tom. She is not happy. She is angry. "Lily and Tom, what are you doing? Why are you making such a noise? Why are you making such a mess? You are not cooking. You are fighting. You are not a team. You are naughty." Mom says. Lily and Tom feel sorry. They stop arguing. They apologize to mom. They help mom clean the kitchen. They hug mom. They say they love her. Mom forgives Lily and Tom. She hugs them back. She says she loves them too. She says they can cook a cake together another day. She says they have to be nice to each other. She says they have to share and take turns. She says they have to have fun.
