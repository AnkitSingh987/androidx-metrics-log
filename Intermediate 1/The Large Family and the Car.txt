Category: Intermediate
Title: The Large Family and the Car

Once upon a time there was a large family who went on a vacation. They all went in a big car. On the way they stopped at a park. The park had a big open field. Everyone started running and playing. The family had so much fun. They all laughed and smiled. Everyone was very happy. Then the family found a big lake. It was so large and beautiful. They all waded in the lake and splashed around. Everyone was so excited. They rode on boats and swam for a long time. At the end of the day, the family opened up the car and everyone got in. They were all so tired and happy. They all slept peacefully the whole way home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful little bird named Blue. Blue had a friend, Old Turtle. Old Turtle was very slow, but he was wise. Blue loved to fly and scatter seeds in the garden. One day, Old Turtle told Blue, "If you scatter seeds near the big tree, something good will happen soon." Blue did not know what would happen, but he believed Old Turtle. So, Blue went to the big tree and scattered seeds all around. After some days, beautiful flowers grew near the big tree. The flowers made everyone in the garden happy. The other animals thanked Blue for being so helpful. Blue learned that listening to wise friends like Old Turtle can bring happiness to everyone.

One day, a boy and his dog went to play in the park. The park was full of fog. The boy could not see very far. He said to his dog, "Let's spend time playing in the fog!" While they played, they found a big, fake rock. The boy and his dog thought it was fun to hide behind the rock. They played hide and seek. Then, the fake rock started to move! The boy and his dog were very surprised. The rock was not a rock at all. It was a big turtle. The turtle said, "Hello, do you want to play with me?" The boy and his dog were happy to have a new friend to play with in the fog.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a whistle that he loved very much. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. They were all having fun, but Tim wanted to show them his whistle. "Look at my whistle!" Tim said to his friends. They all looked at him, confused. They didn't know what a whistle was. Tim blew the whistle and it made a loud sound. His friends were scared and ran away from him. Tim felt sad and didn't know what to do. He walked home, still holding his whistle. On his way, he met an old man. The old man saw Tim's sad face and asked, "What's wrong, little boy?" "I scared my friends with my whistle," Tim said, looking down. The old man thought for a moment and then said, "Maybe you should part with your whistle. It's not good to scare your friends." Tim thought about it and decided to give his whistle to the old man. The old man took the whistle and walked away. Tim went back to the park, but his friends were still scared of him. They didn't want to play with him anymore. The moral of the story is: think before you act, because your actions can have consequences that hurt others.

Jill and Jack were playing together on the playground. It was a cool day, and they were both wearing scarves. Jack said, "Let's spray each other with some water. That will be fun!" Jill agreed, so they took a water bottle and splashed each other. They laughed and laughed, happy to play in the cool air. Suddenly, Jill heard a loud noise coming from the playground. She looked up and saw a big truck, with a huge sprayer on top. It was spraying the playground. Jill screamed to Jack, "Run!" But it was too late. Jack got hit by the spray and was wet all over. Jill tried to shield him with her scarf, but it didn't help. Jack was sad now. His scarf was ruined and he was so wet. Jill felt bad for him and wished she had not agreed to play with the water. They went home feeling upset, and never sprayed each other again.
