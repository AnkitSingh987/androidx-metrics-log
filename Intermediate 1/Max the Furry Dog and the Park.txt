Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Furry Dog and the Park

One day, a furry dog named Max was walking in the park. He saw a big cup of cocoa on the ground. Max wanted to drink the cocoa, but he knew it was not for him. He looked around to see who it belonged to. Max saw a little girl named Lily crying. She had lost her cocoa. Max wanted to help her, so he took the cocoa in his mouth and led her to it. Lily was very happy to find her cocoa and thanked Max. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the cocoa away! Max and Lily were sad, but they decided to be friends and play together instead. They had lots of fun, and they forgot all about the cocoa.

One day, a big, hairy dog named Max went to the park. He saw a little girl eating a sweet treat. Max wanted a taste, so he made a plan to get some. Max walked up to the little girl and said, "Hi, I am Max. Can I have a taste of your treat?" The little girl smiled and said, "Sure, Max! You can have a bite." Max took a small bite and it was so yummy. He thanked the little girl and they played together in the park. Max was happy he made a new friend and got to taste the sweet treat.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A little girl named Amy went outside to play. She found a small box under a tree. She was very excited and ran to show her mom. In the box, there was a toy car and a battery. Amy's mom helped her put the battery in the car. The car started to move and Amy laughed. But then, the car began to whisper. Amy and her mom were surprised. The car whispered, "I can talk!" Amy and her mom looked at each other. They did not know the car could talk. They thought it was just a toy. Now, they had a new friend who could play and talk with them. It was a happy and unexpected day for Amy and her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little shop. The shop was not very good. It was hard for the shop to sell things. The shop wanted to improve. One day, a big, mean cat came to the shop. The cat wanted to take all the things in the shop. The shop did not want the cat to take the things. The shop was sad and scared. A nice dog saw the shop was sad. The dog wanted to help the shop. The dog chased the mean cat away. The shop was happy. The dog helped the shop improve by making it clean and pretty. Now, the shop was good and could sell many things.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot loved to think about bones. He would dream of all the bones he could find. One day, Spot saw a big bone under a tree. It was the biggest bone he had ever seen. He was very happy. Spot tried to pick up the bone, but it was too heavy. He thought hard and had an idea. He would ask his friend, Kitty, for help. Kitty was not as strong as Spot, but she was very smart. Spot thought she might have an idea to get the bone. When Kitty came, she looked at the bone and thought too. Then, she had an idea. She found a long stick and put it under the bone. With a push, the bone rolled out from under the tree. But, surprise! The bone was not a bone at all. It was a big, round rock that just looked like a bone. Spot was sad, but Kitty said, "That's okay. We can still play with the rock." And they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gentle monster. The monster was big and green, but he was very nice. All the kids in the town loved him. They played with him every day. One day, the kids and the monster made a plan. They wanted to have a big party. They needed to get balloons, cake, and games. The gentle monster helped the kids get everything they needed for the party. On the day of the party, everyone in the town came to have fun. The gentle monster played games with the kids and shared the cake. Everyone had a great time. The kids and the gentle monster became best friends and they all lived happily ever after.
