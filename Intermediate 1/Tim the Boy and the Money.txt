Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Money

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his friends outside. They would run, jump, and laugh all day long. Tim was always very glad when he could play with his friends. One day, Tim found some money on the ground. He was very excited and showed it to his friends. They all wanted to play with the money, too. So, they decided to play a game with the money. They took turns hiding the money, and the others would try to find it. As they played, they did not see a big dog coming near them. The dog saw the money and wanted it too. The dog took the money from Tim's hand and ran away with it. Tim and his friends were very sad. They could not play with the money anymore, and they were not glad. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big box. He wanted to wrap it as a gift for his friend, Sue. He felt happy thinking this would be a success. Tim found some paper and tape to wrap the box. As Tim was wrapping the box, his cat, Mittens, came to play. Mittens jumped on the paper and tore it. Tim felt guilty because he didn't want Sue to see the torn paper. He said to Mittens, "Oh no! You tore the paper. Now I need to find more." Tim looked for more paper and found some with colorful dots. He tried again to wrap the big box. This time, Mittens sat and watched. Tim finished wrapping the box and felt proud. He gave the gift to Sue, and she loved it. They both laughed and played, and Tim knew it was a success.

One day, a big, red top named Tim wanted to spin fast. He was ready to be the best top in the toy box. But, there was a problem. Tim was too big and could not spin well. He felt sad. Tim met a small, blue top named Sue. Sue was very good at spinning. Tim asked, "Sue, can you help me spin fast?" Sue said, "Yes, Tim! Let's try together." They tried and tried. Soon, Tim started to spin better. The toy box was full of silence as all the toys watched Tim and Sue spin together. They were so happy to see Tim spin fast. Tim and Sue became the best spinning friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a whistle. It was a reliable whistle. It always made a loud sound when he blew it. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He wanted to pay for a toy with his own money. He put the money on the table and the man at the store gave him the toy. When they got home, Tim played with his toy and blew his whistle. His mom and dad heard the whistle and knew that Tim was happy. They all laughed and had a fun day together. Tim loved his whistle and his new toy.

Once there was a boy named Jerry. He loved cartoons and always wanted to watch them. One day his mom said "let's go to the store, Jerry". Jerry was very excited and put on his shoes to go. The store was bright and colorful. Everywhere were all kinds of cartoon stuff like key-chains and t-shirts. Jerry was so excited he even forgot how nervous he felt when he walked into the store. As Jerry and his mom looked around the store, Jerry saw something shiny and wrapped in plastic. He asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said "Yes, Jerry, go ahead and choose one." He chose a cartoon key chain with a blue cartoon character smiling at him. Jerry was so happy he couldn't contain his joy. He hugged the key chain, thanked his mom and ran outside. His mom smiled at him and said "Now you will always remember this day." Jerry hugged his mom and said "Thank you mommy, I will never forget it!". And he held the key chain tight in his hands while they made their way home. Little did he know what was going to happen in the future and the role his magical cartoon keychain would play.
