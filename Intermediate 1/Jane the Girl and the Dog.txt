Category: Intermediate
Title: Jane the Girl and the Dog

One day, a little girl named Jane found a broken pick. She wanted to fix it. She asked her compassionate friend, Tom, to help her. Tom said, "Yes, I can help you. Let's fix it together!" They worked hard to fix the pick. When they were done, they were very happy. They went to the park to play with their fixed pick. They used it to dig in the sand and make a big castle. But then, a big dog came and knocked down their castle. Jane and Tom were sad. The dog looked at them and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to do that. Can I help you build a new one?" Jane and Tom were surprised that the dog could talk, but they said yes. The dog helped them build an even bigger and better castle.

One day, a kind boy named Tim went to the farm. He saw a chicken. The chicken was small and yellow. Tim liked the chicken very much. "Hi, chicken! Can you talk?" Tim asked. The chicken looked at him and said, "Yes, I can talk! My name is Chickie." Tim and Chickie talked and played all day. They had so much fun. They became best friends. One day, while they were talking, Chickie changed. Chickie became a big, pretty bird! Tim was so surprised. "I am not just a chicken, I am a magic bird!" Chickie said. Now, Tim and Chickie had even more fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play at the park. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He saw a big ball on the ground and wanted to play with it. Tim's friend, Lily, was also at the park. They decided to play together. Tim picked up the ball and said, "Let's play! I will strike the ball and you catch it." Lily agreed and they started to play. Tim struck the ball very hard, but it went the wrong way. The ball hit a little girl who was playing nearby. Tim felt very ashamed. He walked over to the girl and said, "I'm sorry. I didn't mean to hit you." The little girl smiled and said, "It's okay. Can I play with you too?" Tim, Lily, and the little girl played together for the rest of the day and they all had lots of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bubble toy. He loved to blow bubbles and watch them fly. He would whistle when the bubbles came out. The bubbles looked so pretty in the sky. Tim saw his friend, Sue, and called her. "Sue, come see my bubbles!" he said. Sue came running and watched Tim blow the bubbles. "Wow, they are so pretty!" Sue said. They played with the bubbles and whistled together. They were having so much fun. Then, one of the bubbles turned into a yummy candy. Tim and Sue were so surprised. They shared the candy and learned that sharing made them both happy. The moral of the story is that sharing is a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there were two brothers who were very different from each other. One brother loved playing with blocks and the other preferred reading books. One morning, their mom asked them what they wanted for lunch. The brother who liked blocks said he wanted something from the menu. The other brother said he wanted a disgusting sandwich. Mom wasn't happy. She said that the sandwich would rot and it wasn't a good idea. But the brothers were too stubborn to listen. "No, I want the sandwich!" said the brother. Mom sighed and sighed. She said, "Okay, I'll make you a sandwich but you have to eat it right away or it will rot." The brother smiled and thanked mom. He gobbled up his sandwich before it had time to rot. His brother looked at him with envy and wished he had listened to mom.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play in the snow. They made a big snowman with a hat, a scarf and a carrot nose. They gave him two buttons for eyes and a smiley mouth. "Hello, Mr. Snowman!" Ben said. "Do you want to play with us?" "Yes, please!" Lily said, pretending to be the snowman. "But I am cold. Can you take me inside?" Ben and Lily laughed. They knew the snowman could not go inside. He would melt near the fire. They saw the smoke coming out of the chimney. Their mom was busy making hot chocolate for them. "Sorry, Mr. Snowman," Ben said. "You have to stay outside. But we will come back soon. We will bring you some hot chocolate too." "OK, thank you," Lily said, still acting as the snowman. "But don't forget me. I will miss you." Ben and Lily hugged the snowman and ran inside. They drank their hot chocolate and ate some cookies. They told their mom about their snowman friend. She smiled and said they were very creative. "Can we go outside again, mom?" Ben asked. "We want to play with Mr. Snowman some more." "Sure, but put on your coats and boots," mom said. "It's very cold outside." Ben and Lily put on their warm clothes and went outside. They saw the snowman waiting for them. He still had his hat, his scarf and his carrot nose. But his buttons and his smiley mouth were gone. "Oh no, what happened?" Ben said. "Who took your eyes and your mouth?" Lily looked around and saw a squirrel on a tree. The squirrel had a button in its mouth and another one in its paw. It was nibbling on the carrot nose. "Hey, stop that!" Lily said. "That's our snowman's face!" The squirrel looked at them and ran away. It dropped the button and the carrot. Ben and Lily picked them up and fixed the snowman's face. They made him a new smiley mouth with some pebbles. "There, you look good again, Mr. Snowman," Ben said. "Thank you, Ben and Lily," Lily said, as the snowman. "You are very kind and very funny. I like you a lot." Ben and Lily laughed. They played with the snowman until it was dark. They had a lot of fun in the snow. They said goodbye to the snowman and went inside. They hoped he would still be there tomorrow.
