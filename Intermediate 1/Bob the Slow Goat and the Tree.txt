Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Slow Goat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a slow goat named Bob. Bob liked to rest under a big tree. One day, while Bob was resting, he saw a little boy named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, goat. Do you want to play with me?" Bob looked at Tim and nodded. They played together all day, running and jumping in the sun. But Bob got very tired because he was slow. As the sun went down, it was time for Tim to go home. But Bob was too tired to walk back to his tree. He tried to rest, but he could not. Tim said, "Sorry, goat. I have to go home now. Bye!" Tim left, and Bob was all alone. Bob tried to get up, but he was too tired. He could not find his way home. Bob was sad and scared. He stayed in the dark all night, and he never saw Tim or his tree again.

One day, a light butterfly was flying in the sky. It saw a little girl sitting on the grass. The girl was sad because she lost her toy. The butterfly wanted to help her. The butterfly said, "Let's pray for your toy to come back." The girl closed her eyes and prayed with the butterfly. They prayed together for the toy to come back. After they prayed, the girl opened her eyes and saw her toy next to her. She was so happy! She thanked the butterfly for helping her. The girl and the butterfly played together all day, and they became best friends.

One day, a colorful bird named Bob was flying in the sky. He saw a big tree with many oats on it. Bob was hungry and wanted to eat the oats. He flew down and sat on a branch. A small ant named Ann was on the tree too. She saw Bob and said, "Hi, I am Ann. Please don't eat all the oats. I need some too." Bob looked at Ann and said, "Okay, we can share the oats." As they were eating the oats, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the oats away. Bob and Ann were sad. They didn't want to quit eating, but there were no more oats. Bob thought for a moment and said, "Ann, let's go find more oats together." Ann agreed and they both went on a new adventure to find more oats. They became good friends and always shared their food.

One day, a brave cat named Tom went on a walk. Tom saw a big case under a tree. He was very curious. Tom said, "What is in the case?" Tom opened the case and found a big cake. He was very happy. Tom said, "I will taste the cake!" Tom took a bite of the cake. It tasted very good. Tom was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little mouse jumped out of the cake! The mouse said, "Surprise! I was hiding in the cake!" Tom was very surprised. The mouse and Tom became friends and shared the cake.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the sand with his toys. He had a rough toy truck that he would push around in the sand. One day, he wanted to make a sand cake, but he needed a teaspoon to do it. Tim asked his mom if he could use a teaspoon from the kitchen. His mom thought for a moment and then said, "Yes, I permit you to use the teaspoon, but be sure to bring it back when you're done." Tim was very happy. He took the teaspoon and went outside to make his sand cake. He used the teaspoon to scoop up the sand and put it in his toy truck. He did this many times until his sand cake was just right. When his sand cake was finished, Tim was so proud. He showed his mom the sand cake and she smiled. Tim thanked her for letting him use the teaspoon and remembered to bring it back to the kitchen. From that day on, Tim always asked for permission before using something that wasn't his. He learned that it was important to be respectful and to take care of the things he was allowed to use.
