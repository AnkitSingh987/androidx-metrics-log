Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Dog

Anna liked to help her mom in the garden. She liked to see the flowers and the fruits grow. She liked to water them and pick them. One day, her mom asked her to carry some tomatoes to the kitchen. Anna was happy to do it. She took a large basket and filled it with red tomatoes. They were round and shiny and smelled good. She lifted the basket and walked to the house. She was very careful not to drop any tomatoes. She wanted to make her mom proud. But on the way, she met a big dog. The dog was black and furry and had sharp teeth. He barked at Anna and ran towards her. Anna was scared and screamed. She dropped the basket and the tomatoes fell on the ground. The dog jumped on them and bit them. He made a mess of the tomatoes. He ruined them. Anna ran to the house and cried. She told her mom what happened. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Anna. You are safe. The dog was mean, but he can't hurt you. We can get more tomatoes later." But Anna was not okay. She felt sad and angry. She liked the tomatoes. She worked hard to carry them. She wanted to make her mom happy. But the dog spoiled everything. She hated the dog. She wished he would go away. She wished he would never come back. She wished he would be sorry.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy that he loved to play with every day. It was a ball that would light up when he hit it. Tim liked to strike the ball to see the bright colors. One day, Tim was playing with his ball near a big screen. The screen was dull and not very fun to look at. Tim thought it would be fun to make the screen light up like his ball. So, he decided to strike the ball at the screen. When Tim struck the ball at the screen, the screen lit up with all the colors of the rainbow! Tim was so happy and laughed as he played with his ball and the colorful screen. From that day on, Tim's dull screen was not dull anymore, and he had even more fun playing with his ball.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a mild boy who always wanted to help others. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was in trouble, and Tim wanted to help. Tim called out to the cat, "Don't worry, cat! I will help you!" Tim found a long stick and used it to prevent the cat from falling. He carefully pushed the cat until it was able to jump down from the tree. When the cat was safe, it looked at Tim and said, "Thank you, Tim, for stopping my trouble. You are a good friend." Tim felt happy because he had helped the cat. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they are in trouble.

Sara and Ben like to play pretend. They have a lot of junk in their room. They use the junk to make things. Sometimes they make a castle, sometimes they make a spaceship, sometimes they make a zoo. One day, they decide to make a circus. They use a big box as a tent. They use pillows as seats. They use stuffed animals as lions and elephants. They use scarves as costumes. They use pots and pans as drums. They take turns to be the ringmaster. The ringmaster tells the audience what to see. The ringmaster also makes loud noises with the drums. Sara is the ringmaster first. She says, "Welcome to the circus! Look at the amazing animals! They can do tricks! Watch out, they might roar or trumpet!" Ben is the audience. He claps and cheers. He also makes noises like the animals. He says, "Wow, that's so cool! I want to see more! Can I pet them?" Sara says, "Sure, you can pet them. But be careful, they might bite or kick!" They have fun playing pretend. They laugh and giggle. They forget about the junk. They only see the circus.
