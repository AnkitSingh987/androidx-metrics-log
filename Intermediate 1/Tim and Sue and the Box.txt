Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Sue and the Box

Tim and Sue were friends who liked to play together. One day, they found a big box of buttons in the attic. They decided to make a game with the buttons. "Let's count how many buttons we have," Tim said. "That will be our task." "OK," Sue agreed. "You start from that side, and I start from this side. We can meet in the middle." They began to count the buttons, one by one. They put them in piles of ten, to make it easier. They counted and counted, but the box seemed to have no end. After a while, Sue felt bored and tired. She did not like this task. She wanted to do something else. "Tim, can we stop counting?" she asked. "This is miserable. I want to play with the buttons, not count them." Tim looked at her and frowned. He liked this task. He wanted to know how many buttons they had. "No, we can't stop," he said. "We have to finish our task. Counting is fun. You just have to try harder." Sue did not agree. She did not think counting was fun. She thought it was hard and dull. She did not want to try harder. She wanted to have fun. She looked at the box and had an idea. She grabbed a handful of buttons and threw them in the air. They rained down on Tim and the floor. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tim shouted. "You messed up our task. Now we have to start over." Sue laughed. She did not care. She thought it was funny. She grabbed another handful of buttons and threw them again. "Stop it, stop it!" Tim cried. "You are ruining our game. You are a bad friend." Sue did not listen. She kept throwing buttons, until the box was empty. She felt happy and free. Tim felt angry and sad. He did not like this game. He did not like Sue. He ran out of the attic, leaving Sue alone with the buttons.

One day, a large dog named Max found a mop. Max liked the mop a lot. He played with the mop all day. He ran with it, and he jumped with it. Max was very happy. Max saw his friend, a cat named Sam. Sam said, "Wow, that mop is big! Can I play with it too?" Max said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, Max and Sam played with the mop. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. When Max and Sam were playing, the mop made a loud "snap" sound. They looked at the mop and saw that it was broken. Max and Sam were sad. But then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's fix the mop together!" So, they worked together and fixed the mop. Max and Sam were happy again, and they played with the mop all day.

Once upon a time, there was a 3 year old girl named Anna. She was playing with her toys when suddenly she heard a funny sound. She looked around and saw her dad with a razor in his hand. He was laughing out loud and Anna wondered what was so funny. "Dad, why are you laughing?" asked Anna. "I was joking with the razor," Dad said, "I was pretending it was a toy." Anna laughed too. She was glad she could joke around with her dad. Dad continued to make funny jokes with the razor and eventually they were both in stitches. They laughed until they were so tired that they had to go to sleep. Anna went off to her bedroom with a big smile on her face, knowing she could joke around with her dad any time she wanted.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Anna. She was only three years old, but she was very wise. She loved to explore her house and learn new things. One day, Anna went to the toilet. She was very proud of herself for learning how to use it. She said, "Yay, I did it!" Suddenly, Anna heard a voice say, "Good job. Thank you for using the toilet all by yourself!". Anna was shocked, who was talking to her? She cautiously looked around and saw a very small mouse standing near her. Anna was so surprised she almost screamed! The mouse said to Anna, "I'm Lee, thank you for being so brave and using the toilet all by yourself." Anna was no longer upset. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Lee."
