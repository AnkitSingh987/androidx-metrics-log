Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wide Tree and the Drum

Once upon a time, there was a wide tree. Under the tree, a boy named Tim found a drum. Tim loved to play the drum. He played it all day and all night. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came over. Sam said, "Can I play the drum too?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sam was sad and went home. Tim kept playing the drum, but he played it too much. One day, the drum broke. Tim tried to remove the broken part, but he could not fix it. Tim was sad and wished he had let Sam play too. The moral of the story is to share with others, or you may lose what you have.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tim found a leak in his room. He saw water on the floor and he was scared. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a leak in my room!" His mom was not happy. She said, "Tim, did you make the leak? If you did, I will have to punish you." Tim was sad and said, "No, mom, I didn't make the leak." His mom believed him and they went to fix the leak together. As they were fixing the leak, they heard a wild noise outside. They looked out the window and saw a big, friendly bear! The bear was the one who made the leak! The bear was trying to get honey from a bee's nest on the roof. The bear said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to make the leak." Tim, his mom, and the bear all became friends and they fixed the leak together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a fearful boy who always listened to his mom and dad. He never wanted to be punished. One day, Tim saw a shiny lamp in his room. He thought it was a toy, so he played with it. He threw it up and down, having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The lamp broke into pieces! Tim was very scared. He knew that if his mom and dad saw the broken lamp, they would punish him. So, he thought of a plan. He put all the pieces of the lamp under his bed and waited for his mom and dad to come home. When his mom and dad came home, they saw the broken lamp under Tim's bed. They were not mad at him. Instead, they told him that the lamp was magical! The lamp had the power to make Tim brave. Tim was so surprised and happy. From that day on, he was no longer a fearful boy.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big metal box in her house. She did not know what was inside the box, so she asked her mom, "Mom, what is in this big metal box?" Her mom said, "That is a printer, Mia. It can print pretty pictures for you to see." Mia wanted to try it, so she found a pretty picture of a lemon and asked the printer to print it. When the picture came out, it looked very nice, but then something strange happened. The lemon in the picture became real and fell off the paper! Mia picked up the lemon and tasted it. "Yuck!" she said. "This lemon is very sour!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's because lemons are always sour, Mia." From that day, Mia learned that even if something looks pretty, it might not always be nice to taste. And she also learned that her special printer could make things come to life!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a yellow lemon on it. Tim wanted to get the lemon, but he could not reach it. So, he called his dependable friend, Sam, to help him. Sam came to the park and saw Tim. "What do you need help with?" Sam asked. "I want the lemon on the tree, but I can't reach it," Tim said. Sam thought for a moment and then said, "Let's skip and see if we can shake the tree to get the lemon." They both skipped around the tree, and the lemon fell down! But, something unexpected happened. The lemon started talking! "Thank you for setting me free," said the lemon. Tim and Sam looked at each other in surprise. The talking lemon told them he was a magic lemon. He said, "I will give you one wish each for helping me." Tim and Sam were very happy. They both made a wish, and the magic lemon made their wishes come true. From that day on, Tim and Sam knew that anything is possible when you work together.
