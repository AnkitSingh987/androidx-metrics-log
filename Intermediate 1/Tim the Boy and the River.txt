Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the River

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk near the river. The sky was dark, and it began to rain. The rain came down hard, and the river started to rise. Tim and Spot saw that the water was getting very high. It was a dangerous flood. Tim said to Spot, "We need to go home and tell mommy about the flood. It's not safe here." Spot barked, and they ran back to their house. Tim told his mommy about the dangerous flood. Mommy said, "We must remain inside until the rain stops and the flood goes away." Tim, Spot, and mommy stayed safe inside their house. They played games and had fun while they waited for the rain to stop. After a while, the rain stopped, and the flood went away. Tim, Spot, and mommy were happy they remained safe inside their home.

One day, a little pen wanted to become successful. The pen was blue and could draw pretty lines. It lived in a big box with other pens and pencils. They all played together and drew many things. The little pen wanted to become the best pen in the box. It practiced drawing every day. It drew lines, circles, and squares. It drew trees, houses, and animals. The other pens and pencils watched and cheered for the little pen. After some time, the little pen became very good at drawing. Everyone in the box wanted to be like the little pen. The little pen was happy and proud. It had become successful, just like it wanted. And they all lived happily in the big box, drawing together.

Tommy was a painter. He liked to make pictures with colors on paper. He had many colors: red, blue, yellow, green, and more. He also had a big brush and a small brush. One day, Tommy saw a fancy wall in his house. It was white and smooth. He thought it would be fun to make a picture on it. He took his colors and his brushes and went to the wall. He started to paint a big sun, a blue sky, and some green grass. But his mom saw him. She was very angry. She said, "Tommy, what are you doing? You are not allowed to paint on the wall. That is very bad. You have to punish you. You have to clean the wall and say sorry. And you have to go to your room with no toys." Tommy felt sad. He did not mean to be bad. He just wanted to make a picture. He said, "I'm sorry, mom. I like to paint. But I will not paint on the wall again. I will only paint on paper. Please don't punish me." His mom said, "I'm glad you are sorry, Tommy. But you still have to clean the wall and go to your room. That is how you learn to not do bad things. Maybe later, I will give you some paper and you can paint a nice picture for me." Tommy nodded. He took a cloth and some water and cleaned the wall. It was hard work. He wished he had not painted on the wall. He learned his lesson. He went to his room and waited for his mom. He hoped she would forgive him and let him paint again. He loved to be a painter. But he also loved his mom. He wanted to make her happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very thirsty. He wanted to drink some water. Max went to the kitchen to find a glass. Max saw a glass on the table. But the glass was too high for him. Max tried to jump up, but he could not reach the glass. Max was sad. He asked his friend, the cat, for help. The cat said, "I can help you, Max." The cat jumped up on the table and pushed the glass down. The glass fell on the floor, but it did not break. Max was very happy. He said, "Thank you, cat!" Now Max could drink the water from the little glass. Max and the cat drank the water together. They were not thirsty anymore. Max and the cat became good friends. They played together every day. And they always helped each other when they needed it.
