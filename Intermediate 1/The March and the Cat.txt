Category: Intermediate
Title: The March and the Cat

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a march. Many people walked and played music. They were happy. A little boy named Tim wanted to join the march too. He put on his hat and shoes and went outside. As Tim walked in the march, he saw a little cat. The cat was sad. Tim wanted to help the cat. He picked up the cat and they walked together in the march. The cat started to feel happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The little cat did not yield to Tim. The cat jumped out of Tim's arms and ran away. Tim was sad, but he kept walking in the march. Suddenly, the cat came back with more little cats. They all wanted to join the march too. Tim was happy again, and they all walked together in the march.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a rake. The rake was big and original. It was a very special rake. Tom liked to play with his rake in the yard. One day, Tom saw a cat. The cat was on a tree. The cat was scared. Tom wanted to help the cat. He thought, "I will dare to help the cat with my rake." Tom said to the cat, "Don't worry, cat! I will help you with my rake!" Tom used his rake to reach the cat. The cat climbed onto the rake. Tom pulled the rake down. The cat was safe. Tom's mom saw what happened. She said, "Tom, you are very brave! You dared to help the cat with your rake." Tom felt happy. He knew his original rake was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a small nest made of twigs and a long wire. The wire helped keep the nest safe in the tree. Timmy loved his nest very much. One day, a big wind came and shook the tree. The wire fell off the nest, and Timmy was very upset. He didn't know what to do. Timmy's friend, Sammy the squirrel, saw Timmy was sad. Sammy asked, "Why are you upset, Timmy?" Timmy told Sammy about the wire and his nest. Sammy said, "Don't worry, I will help you fix it." They worked together to put the wire back on the nest. Timmy was happy again. He said, "Thank you, Sammy!" Sammy told Timmy to repeat after him, "Friends help friends." Timmy repeated, "Friends help friends." From that day on, Timmy and Sammy always helped each other. They learned that when you are upset, friends can make things better. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a huge horse named Bob. Bob liked to run and play in the big field. One day, a girl named Sue saw Bob and wanted to ride him. Sue was very nice, so Bob let her ride on his back. Sue and Bob went to a tree. Sue found a rope and tried to tie Bob to the tree. But the rope was too short. So, she looked around and found a longer rope. This time, Sue could tie Bob to the tree. They both were happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came and untied the rope. Bob was free again! The mouse wanted to be friends with Bob and Sue. They all played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, the huge horse, the girl, and the tiny mouse were the best of friends.

It was a hot summer day and two neighbours, Jack and Sue, were playing in a field. Jack had a sack and they were running around, playing tag. Sue said to Jack, "I bet I can win this game!" Jack smiled and said, "Okay, let's go!" They both ran as fast as they could until, eventually, Sue got Jack to stop. Sue was pleased with herself, and said she had won. But then Jack said, "What's in the sack?" Sue opened the sack, but inside it was a harmless snake. Sue screamed and dropped the sack. The snake slithered off into the grass and was gone. Sue was sad that she'd lost the game. The two neighbours stopped playing tag after that. As Sue watched the harmless snake make its way into the grass, she felt a sad, bad feeling inside her.
