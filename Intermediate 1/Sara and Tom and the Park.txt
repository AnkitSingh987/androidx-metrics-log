Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Tom and the Park

Sara and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. But today, the park is closed. There is a big sign that says "No entry. Park is under repair." Sara and Tom are sad. They want to have fun. "Let's go in anyway," says Tom. "The sign is boring. We can manage to play without getting hurt." "No, Tom," says Sara. "The sign is a law. We have to follow the law. The law is good. It keeps us safe and happy. If we break the law, we can get in trouble. And we can hurt ourselves or others." "But I'm bored," says Tom. "There is nothing else to do." "Sure there is," says Sara. "We can go to the library and read some books. We can go to the bakery and buy some cookies. We can go to the pond and feed some ducks. There are many things to do that are fun and follow the law." Tom thinks for a moment. He sees that Sara is right. He says sorry to Sara. He says he will follow the law. He says he will try to find fun things to do that are good. Sara smiles. She says she is happy. She says she is glad that Tom is her friend. They hold hands and walk away from the park. They have a good day. The moral of the story is: We should follow the law and respect the rules. The law is not boring. The law is good. It helps us to be safe and happy. And we can always find fun things to do that are good.

Once upon a time, there was a big garden. In the garden, there were many flowers. They loved to bloom and show their pretty colors. In the middle of the garden, there was a long cord. The cord was very useful. It helped the flowers stand tall and strong. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the garden. He saw the cord and thought it would be fun to play with. He pulled the cord and all the flowers started to shake. The flowers were scared, but they did not fall down. Then, something unexpected happened. When Tim pulled the cord, it made the flowers bloom even bigger and brighter. The flowers were so happy! They thanked Tim for making them more beautiful. Tim learned that sometimes, a little surprise can be a good thing.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. The sun was shining and it was a perfect day to play. Lily saw a big slide and she wanted to go on it. Lily climbed up the ladder and sat at the top of the slide. She was a little scared to go down. Her mom saw her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. You can solve this by going down slowly." Lily listened to her mom and started to slide down slowly. Soon, she was going faster and faster, and she was having so much fun! When she reached the bottom, she laughed and clapped her hands. She had solved her fear of the slide. Lily went on the slide again and again, and she had the perfect day at the park with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a big, clumsy dog named Max. Max loved to sing. One day, his friend Sam, a little cat, had an idea. Sam said, "Max, let's put on an opera!" Max was very happy and agreed to sing. They told all their friends about the opera. All the animals came to watch Max sing. Max was so excited, but he was also very clumsy. As Max started to sing, he moved around a lot on the stage. Suddenly, Max tripped on a toy and fell off the stage. All the animals gasped. Max was sad and embarrassed. The opera did not end well. From that day on, Max learned to be more careful when he sang.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth, round thing. It loved to play hide and seek with its friends. One day, it found a very good spot to hide. It was so good that no one could find it. The smooth thing was very happy. As time went by, the smooth thing started to feel lonely. It missed its friends and wanted to play with them again. But it was still hiding, and no one could find it. The smooth thing decided to come out of its hiding spot. When it came out, it saw that its friends were all sad. They had been looking for the smooth thing for a long time. The smooth thing felt bad and said sorry to its friends. They all hugged and played together again. The smooth thing learned that it is more fun to play with friends than to hide from them.
