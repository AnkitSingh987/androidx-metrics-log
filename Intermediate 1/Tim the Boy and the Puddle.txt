Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Puddle

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big puddle. The puddle had a leak. Tim was very curious about the leak, so he went closer to look at it. Tim saw two toys in the puddle. One toy was a pretty ball, but it was spoiled. The other toy was a fun car, but it was dirty. Tim had to choose which toy he wanted to play with. He thought very hard about it. Tim decided to pick the dirty car. He took it home and cleaned it up. Now, he had a fun car to play with, and he was very happy. The spoiled ball stayed in the puddle, and the leak kept making the puddle bigger. Tim learned that sometimes, you have to choose the best thing, even if it's not perfect.

Once upon a time, there was a kind sun who lived in the sky. He had a friend named Cloud. They liked to play together. The sun would shine and Cloud would move around the sky. One day, Cloud saw a little girl on the ground. She was sad because her toy was stuck in a tree. The kind sun wanted to help her. He thought of a plan with Cloud. The sun shined very bright and began to burn a little branch on the tree. It was not a big burn, just enough to break the branch. Cloud moved close to the tree and caught the toy as the branch broke. The little girl was so happy to have her toy back. The kind sun and Cloud were happy they could help the little girl. They continued to play together in the sky, always looking for ways to be kind to others on the ground. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, there was a modest painter who was doing some work in the house. He had all his tools out and was ready to start. Suddenly, he heard a loud sound and looked up to see that the wall had split! It was a big crack and he knew he had to fix it quickly. The painter got to work, carefully smoothing the wall and covering the split. He worked hard until the wall was fixed just right. The painter was very proud of his hard work. When the job was finished, the painter couldn't help but feel a little bit of pain in his heart. He wasn't sure why, but he slowly understood that the split in the wall reminded him of his own problems. The painter was happy that he could help the house and make it look beautiful again. He packed up his tools and went on his way - feeling a bit more modest than before.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Annie. She had an older brother and they were always together. One day they had a good idea - they wanted to have a trade. They ran to their dad and asked if they could have a trade. Dad said, “Yes, you can have a trade, but first you have to figure out what you want to trade”. So they both thought of some things to trade. The brother wanted to trade his toy truck, and Annie wanted to trade her doll. They showed these things to Dad and he was glad that they were thinking of having a trade. But then the brother teased Annie about her doll. “Your doll isn’t worth much,” he said. Annie got mad and said, “My doll is worth lots!” Dad saw that the brother was teasing, and he said, “Let’s have a fair trade - you can each have something that you like and both of you will be happy”. So the brother and Annie both got what they wanted, and they were glad that they had a trade. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to hold his toy chain. It was his favorite toy. One day, he went to play at the park with his friends. While playing, Tim met a new boy named Max. Max saw Tim's toy chain and wanted to play with it too. But Max was a little rude. He took the chain from Tim without asking. Tim felt sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Tim's friend, Lily, came to help. She told Max that it was not nice to take things without asking. Max said sorry and gave the chain back to Tim. They all learned to share and play together. And they became good friends.
