Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl Finds the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big heart and loved to show peace to everyone around her. She had a friend named Tim who was a bit spoiled. Tim liked to get his way all the time. One sunny day, Lily and Tim were playing with their toys in the park. "I want to play with your toy," Tim said to Lily. "But I am playing with it now," replied Lily. Tim was not happy and started to cry. Lily thought about how to show peace to Tim. She decided to share her toy with him. "Here, you can play with my toy for a little while," said Lily. Tim stopped crying and started to play with Lily's toy. They both played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim learned to share and not be spoiled. And Lily continued to show peace to everyone in the village.

Once upon a time, there was a soft ball named Bounce. Bounce loved to play with his friends. They would jump and roll all day long. One day, Bounce found a shield. It was big and shiny. Bounce wanted to show his friends, so he went to find them. He rolled and jumped with the shield by his side. When Bounce showed the shield to his friends, something unexpected happened. They all started to bounce higher and higher! They didn't know the shield had special powers. Bounce and his friends were so happy to play with the shield and jump high in the sky. And they all lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat olives. One day, he found a big jar of olives in the kitchen. Tim was very happy. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the jar of olives and asked, "What is that?" Tim tried to explain, "These are olives. They are small and yummy." Sam wanted to try one. Tim opened the jar and gave Sam an olive. Sam ate the olive and smiled. "This is good!" he said. Tim and Sam ate olives and played all day. They were happy and full. And that is the story of Tim, Sam, and the olives.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a helpful girl named Anna. She had a pretty veil that she loved to wear. One sunny day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Anna met her best friend, Lily. They both wanted to play on the swings, but there was only one swing left. Anna and Lily disagreed on who should use the swing first. They both wanted to play, but they could not agree. A wise, old lady saw the two friends disagreeing. She walked up to them and said, "Why don't you take turns on the swing?" Anna and Lily thought this was a good idea. They took turns and played happily together. The helpful girl, Anna, even let Lily wear her pretty veil. And so, they had a fun day at the park.

---

One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle wanted to buy a toy. They went to the toy shop together. The toy they liked had a price. They both wanted the toy, but they did not have enough money. So, they decided to talk. The rabbit said, "Let's split the price and share the toy. I will play with it in the morning and you can play with it in the afternoon." The turtle agreed, "Yes, that is a good idea. We can both have fun with the toy." So, the rabbit and the turtle split the price of the toy. They took turns playing with it every day. They were happy and became good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big bush and wanted to play with it. Tim was very careless and did not look around before he ran to the bush. As Tim got close to the bush, he heard a small voice say, "Please don't hurt me!" Tim stopped and looked around. He saw a little bug sitting on the bush. The bug was scared that Tim would hurt him. Tim said, "I'm sorry, I didn't see you there. I won't hurt you." The bug thanked Tim and they became friends. They played together all day. In the end, the little bug showed Tim a secret path to a beautiful garden that no one knew about.
