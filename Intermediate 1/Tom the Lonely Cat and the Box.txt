Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lonely Cat and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat named Tom. He had no friends and spent all day looking for someone to play with. One day, he met a dog named Max. Max was very friendly and said, "Hi Tom, do you want to play with me?" Tom and Max played all day and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened! They found a big box that had a hole in it. Tom and Max wanted to see if they could fit inside the box. Tom went first and he fit inside the box. Then Max tried, but he was too big to fit. Max was sad, but Tom had an idea. He said, "Max, let's find a bigger box for you." They looked and looked, and finally found a bigger box. Max fit inside the new box, and they both got a reward: a big bowl of yummy treats. Tom and Max were so happy, and they were best friends forever.

Lily loved her toy squirrel. She named him Nutty and took him everywhere. She hugged him, fed him, and played with him. Nutty was her loyal friend. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily brought Nutty with her. She saw some other kids playing with a big ball. She wanted to join them. She left Nutty on a bench and ran to the ball. But when she came back, Nutty was gone. She looked around and saw a boy holding him. He was walking away with his dad. "Hey, that's my squirrel!" Lily shouted. She ran after the boy. "Sorry, kid, he's mine now. I just bought him from a man who was selling toys," the boy said. "That's not true! He's not a toy, he's my friend! Give him back!" Lily cried. The boy's dad looked at Lily and then at the man who sold Nutty. He saw that the man was sneaky and mean. He realized that he had cheated them. "Son, I think we made a mistake. This squirrel belongs to the girl. We should give it back to her," he said. The boy was sad, but he knew it was the right thing to do. He handed Nutty to Lily. "Here, I'm sorry. I didn't know he was yours," he said. Lily smiled and hugged Nutty. She thanked the boy and his dad. She was happy to have her loyal friend back. She learned to never leave him alone again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a gloomy day. The sky was gray and the wind was cold. A little girl named Lily went to play outside with her friend, Tim. Lily and Tim found a big box of pastels. They were very happy and excited. Lily said, "Let's share the pastels and make a big, pretty picture together!" Tim smiled and nodded, "Yes, let's do it!" They sat down and started to draw with the pastels. The more they drew, the less gloomy the day became. The sky turned blue and the sun came out. Their big, pretty picture was full of bright colors and happy things. The whole town came to see their beautiful drawing and everyone felt happy. Lily and Tim learned that sharing can make a gloomy day bright and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toys and count numbers. One day, she found a big box in her room. She was very happy and excited. Amy opened the box and saw many colorful blocks inside. The blocks had numbers on them. She wanted to carry the blocks to her play area. So, she picked up the blocks, one by one, and carried them to her play area. She started to play and build with the blocks. She made a tall tower with the blocks, using the numbers to help her. Amy was very proud of her tower. She played with her blocks all day and had lots of fun. And that is the end of the sweet story about Amy and her number blocks.

One day, a little dog named Max was very eager to eat. He went to the kitchen and saw a big bag of sugar on the table. Max wanted to taste the sugar, so he jumped up and down to try to reach it. Max's friend, a cat named Lily, saw him jumping. She asked, "What are you doing, Max?" Max said, "I want the sugar!" Lily had an idea. She told Max, "Let's snap a stick and use it to get the sugar." Max and Lily found a stick and snapped it in half. They used the stick to pull the bag of sugar closer. They tasted the sugar and it was very sweet. Max and Lily were happy and full. They decided to share the sugar with their friends.
