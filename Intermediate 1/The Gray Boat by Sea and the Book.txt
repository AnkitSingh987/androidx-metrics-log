Category: Intermediate
Title: The Gray Boat by Sea and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a gray boat by the sea. A little boy named Tim saw the boat and wanted to go in. He asked his mom, "Can I go on the boat, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It is not safe to go on the boat alone." But Tim did not listen. He went on the boat when his mom was not looking. Tim saw a book on the boat and started to read it. The boat began to move away from the shore. Tim's mom saw him on the boat and shouted, "Tim, come back!" But the boat went far away, and Tim could not come back. His mom was sad, and Tim was lost at sea.

One day, a big bird with a red envelope in its beak arrived at a small house. Inside the house, a little girl named Lily and her mom were playing. The bird knocked on the window. Lily saw the bird and said, "Look, Mom! A bird with an envelope!" Lily's mom opened the window and took the envelope. She opened it and read the note inside. The note said, "I am sorry I cannot come to your party. Love, Grandma." Lily felt sad that her grandma could not come to her birthday party. Lily's mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. We will still have a fun party with your friends. And we can visit Grandma soon." Lily smiled and said, "Okay, Mom. Let's have the best party ever!" They both laughed and continued to plan the party together.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing in the yard. The cat wore a tight red hat, and the dog wore a tight blue hat. They saw a big box and wanted to see what was inside. The cat said, "Let's split the box open!" The dog agreed, and they both pushed on the box until it split open. When the box split open, smoke came out. The cat and the dog were very surprised. They did not know what to do. The smoke made them cough, so they ran away from the box. They hid behind a tree and watched the smoke. Then, a friendly frog jumped out of the smoke. The frog wore a tight green hat. The cat and the dog were not scared anymore. The frog said, "Hello, friends! I was in the box, and the smoke was my magic trick!" The cat and the dog laughed, and they all became friends. They played in the yard together, wearing their tight hats, and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely frog named Tim. Tim lived in a small pond with no other frogs to play with. Every day, Tim would jump from one lily pad to another, but he still felt sad and lonely. One sunny day, while Tim was jumping, he saw a shiny watch on the ground. He hopped over to it and picked it up with his little frog hands. Tim didn't know what it was, but he thought it was pretty and fun to play with. As Tim played with the watch, a little girl named Lucy came by the pond. She saw Tim with her lost watch and smiled. Lucy and Tim became friends, and they played together every day. Tim was not lonely anymore, and they both had lots of fun jumping and playing with the watch.

One day, a big powerful dog named Max found a gum near the park. He picked it up and started to chew it. He liked the sweet taste of the gum. As he walked, he saw his friend, a little cat named Lily. "Hi Lily!" said Max. "I found a gum and it is so sweet. Do you want to try it?" "No, thank you," said Lily. "I don't like gum. It can cover my fur and make me sticky." Max and Lily kept walking and talking. Suddenly, they saw a big tree with a kite stuck in it. The kite was very high and they could not reach it. Max had an idea. He took the gum out of his mouth and threw it at the kite. The gum was so sticky that it pulled the kite down from the tree. "Wow, Max! Your gum saved the kite!" said Lily. "I did not know gum could be so helpful." Max smiled and said, "Sometimes, things we don't like can surprise us and be useful. Now, let's go play with the kite!"
