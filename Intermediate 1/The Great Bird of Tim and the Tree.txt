Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Bird of Tim and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big tree with a pretty bird at the top. He wanted to show his friend, so he ran to find her. He found his friend, Sue, and said, "Look, Sue! I see a bird on the tree. Come with me!" They ran to the tree, and Tim pointed to the top. Sue saw the bird and smiled. But then, the bird looked worried. It could not fly down. Tim and Sue wanted to help. They found a long stick and gently pushed the bird off the branch. The bird flew away, happy and free. Tim and Sue felt good for helping the bird and went back to play.

Once upon a time, there was a famous race. Many animals wanted to win. A big lion, a fast rabbit, and a small mouse were all in the race. They all wanted to complete the race and be the best. The big lion said, "I am strong, I will win." The fast rabbit said, "I am quick, I will win." The small mouse said, "I will try my best." The race began, and they all ran as fast as they could. The big lion and the fast rabbit were ahead. The small mouse did not give up. He kept running. The big lion and the fast rabbit got tired. The small mouse passed them and completed the race first. He won! The other animals cheered for the small mouse. And they all learned that trying your best is important.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty little cat named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Lucy went out to play and felt the heat on her soft fur. She liked the warm feeling. Lucy saw a big red ball and guessed it would be fun to play with. She jumped and rolled the ball around the yard. Lucy was very happy playing with her new toy. She played until she was tired and needed a rest. After resting under a cool tree, Lucy went back home. She told her mom about the fun day she had playing in the heat with the big red ball. Her mom was happy to see Lucy so excited. They both knew it was a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a fragile flower. It had a strong root that held it in the ground. One day, a big ball came rolling fast and hit the flower. The flower did not break, but it was sad and scared. A kind bird saw the flower and wanted to help. The bird tried to push the ball away, but the ball was too big and heavy. The bird did not give up. It called its friends to come and help. Many birds came and together, they pushed the ball away. The flower was safe and happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball hit a tree and broke into many pieces. Inside the ball were many seeds. The seeds fell on the ground and started to grow. Soon, there were many new flowers around the tree. The fragile flower now had many friends, and they all lived happily together.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go on an adventurous trip. He told his mom, "I want to go far away and see new things!" His mom said, "Okay, Tim. Let's pack a bag and go." They packed a big bag with food, clothes, and toys. Tim was so excited! They walked and walked until they reached a point where they saw a big, beautiful rainbow. Tim said, "Mom, look! The rainbow is so pretty!" As they walked closer to the rainbow, they found a little puppy. The puppy was lost and sad. Tim and his mom did not expect to find a puppy on their trip. Tim said, "Mom, can we take the puppy home?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We can take the puppy home." So, Tim, his mom, and the puppy went back home. They had an adventurous day and made a new friend. Tim was very happy.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily and her dog, Max, went for a walk. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Lily said, "Max, let's play hide and seek!" Max barked and wagged his tail. Lily hid in the tree hole, and Max started to look for her. While Lily was hiding, she found a door on the floor. She opened it and saw a safe place with lots of toys. Lily said, "Wow, this is fun!" Max found Lily in the tree hole, and they both went into the safe place. They played with the toys and had a great time. But then, the floor split, and a big slide appeared. Lily and Max slid down the slide, and they landed in a room full of treats. They laughed and ate treats, and they knew that they had found a magic tree.
