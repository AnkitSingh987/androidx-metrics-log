Category: Intermediate
Title: Kiki the Clumsy Cat and the Wheel

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a clumsy cat named Kiki. Kiki loved to play with her ball. One sunny day, Kiki saw a big wheel near a house. The wheel was round and shiny. Kiki thought it would be fun to play with the wheel. Kiki tried to steal the wheel, but it was too heavy for her. She pushed and pushed, but the wheel would not move. "Oh no," said Kiki, "I cannot take this wheel. What should I do?" Just then, Kiki's friend, a little dog named Dodo, came running. "Kiki, what are you doing?" asked Dodo. Kiki told Dodo about the wheel and how she wanted to play with it. Dodo said, "We should not steal things, Kiki. Let's go and ask if we can play with the wheel." Kiki agreed, and they went to the house together. Kiki and Dodo asked the owner of the house if they could play with the wheel. The owner smiled and said, "Of course, you can play with the wheel. But remember, do not take things without asking." Kiki and Dodo promised, and they played with the wheel all day long. They learned that it is better to ask than to steal, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a humble fisherman named Tom. He lived in a small house near the sea. Every day, he went to the sea to catch fish. One day, Tom met a talking fish. The fish said, "Please, don't catch me, Tom. I will send you a gift if you let me go." Tom was surprised but he said, "Okay, little fish. I will let you go." Tom went home and told his mom about the talking fish. The next day, a big box came to their house. Inside the box, there were lots of toys and yummy food. Tom and his mom were so happy. They played with the toys and ate the food. They thanked the talking fish for the gift.

Lucy was tired after playing all day. But she really liked playing, so she was excited to draw in her new notebook. She grabbed her pencil and opened her notebook. Just as she placed the pencil to the paper, she sneezed! She couldn't believe it. "Ah-choo! Oh no," she said, closing the notebook. Lucy looked around to see if anyone saw, but no one was there. Suddenly, her best friend Jen appeared. "What happened, Lucy?" Lucy started laughing. "I sneezed and it made me drop my notebook!" Jen started laughing too. "That was so funny!" Jen said. Lucy put the notebook back in her bag and said "Let's go play something else now. I'm too tired to draw anymore." And off they went to find something else to do.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He liked to skip, so he skipped down the path. As he skipped, he saw a small flame in the grass. Tim was scared of the flame and didn't know what to do. He ran to his friend, Sam, who was playing nearby. "Sam, there's a flame in the grass!" Tim said. Sam looked at the flame and thought it was stupid for the flame to be there. They needed to put it out. Sam found a bucket of water and said, "Let's put the water on the flame!" They both went back to the flame and poured the water on it. The flame went out, and they were happy. They skipped back home, proud of what they did.

One day, a friendly cat went to get a big, round ball. The cat liked to play with the round ball. It was fun and made the cat very happy. But when the cat got to the place where the round ball was, it was gone! The cat was sad and did not know what to do. The friendly cat looked around and saw a little dog. The little dog had the round ball! The cat was mad and wanted to get the ball back. The cat and the dog did not like each other. They both wanted the round ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The little dog came up to the friendly cat and gave the round ball back. The cat and the dog started to play together with the round ball. They had lots of fun and became good friends. The cat and the dog learned that sharing the round ball made them both happy.
