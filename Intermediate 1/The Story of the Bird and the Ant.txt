Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Bird and the Ant

Once upon a time, there was a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird was sad and could not fly. The bird saw a helpful ant walking by. The bird asked the ant, "Can you help me fix my wing?" The ant said, "Yes, I can help you." The ant and the bird worked together to fix the bird's wing. The bird was very happy and excited. But the bird was also scared to try flying again. The bird started to tremble. The helpful ant said, "Don't be scared. I will be here to help you." The bird trusted the ant and tried to fly. With the ant's help, the bird could fly again. The bird and the ant became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a dish. The dish was very dry. It lived in a big house with many other dishes. One day, the dish went outside to play. It rolled and rolled on the grass. But soon, the dish got lost. It did not know how to go back home. A nice cat saw the lost dish. The cat said, "I will help you find your home." The cat and the dish walked and walked. Soon, they found the big house. The dish was happy to be home again. It was not lost anymore.

Once upon a time, there were twin sisters named Lily and Rose. They were very nice and always played together. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. One day, mom and dad decided to decorate their room. They wanted to make it look pretty for the twins. They painted the walls with pink and blue colors. They also put up some cute pictures on the walls. Lily and Rose loved their new room. They played in it all day and had a lot of fun. At night, they slept in their cozy beds, dreaming of all the nice things they could do in their pretty room. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Sleepy. Sleepy was very sleepy all the time. One day, Sleepy went for a walk down the road. As Sleepy walked, he saw a big tree. Sleepy wanted to reach the top. He climbed and climbed, but he was too sleepy to reach the top. Sleepy was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw Sleepy and wanted to help. The bird picked Sleepy up and flew to the top of the tree. Sleepy was so happy! Now, Sleepy could see the whole road. Sleepy and the bird became best friends and had fun together.

One day, a dog named Max wanted to perform a dance. Max put on a big, red wrap to look nice. He felt a little uncomfortable, but he wanted to dance for his friends. Max started to dance and spin around. His friends clapped and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The red wrap got caught on a tree and pulled Max back. Max was surprised and a little scared. His friends quickly helped him get free from the tree. They all laughed together and Max felt much better. From that day on, Max decided to dance without a wrap, so he could be more comfortable and have fun with his friends.

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play in their room. They have many toys and clothes. One day, they find a big black hanger in their closet. They wonder what it is for. "Look, Sam, a big black hanger!" Tim says. "What can we do with it?" "Maybe we can hang our clothes on it," Sam says. "Or we can use it as a hook." They try to hang their shirts on the hanger, but they are too small. The hanger is too big. They try to use it as a hook, but they cannot reach the ceiling. The hanger is too heavy. "Let's weigh the hanger," Tim says. "Maybe it is very heavy." They find a scale in the bathroom. They put the hanger on the scale. The scale shows a big number. They cannot read the number, but they know it is big. "Wow, the hanger is very heavy!" Sam says. "It is heavier than our toys and clothes." "Maybe it is not a hanger," Tim says. "Maybe it is something else." They take the hanger back to their room. They look at it carefully. They see a small sticker on the hanger. It has some words and a picture. They cannot read the words, but they recognize the picture. "Look, Sam, it is a picture of a bike!" Tim says. "The hanger is for a bike!" "Maybe it is Dad's hanger," Sam says. "Maybe he uses it to hang his bike in the garage." They decide to take the hanger to the garage. They find Dad's bike there. They try to hang the bike on the hanger, but they cannot lift it. The bike is too heavy. "Dad, Dad, we found your hanger!" Tim says. "We tried to hang your bike on it, but we couldn't." Dad comes to the garage. He sees the hanger and the bike. He smiles. "Thank you, boys, for finding my hanger," Dad says. "It is a special hanger for my bike. It helps me save space in the garage. But you are right, it is very heavy. You need to be careful with it. Do you want to help me hang the bike on it?" "Yes, Dad, we want to help!" Tim and Sam say. Dad lifts the bike and puts it on the hanger. He shows Tim and Sam how to hook the hanger on the wall. The bike hangs on the hanger. It looks cool. "Wow, Dad, your bike looks cool on the hanger!" Tim and Sam say. "Thank you, boys, you are very helpful," Dad says. "Now, let's go inside and have some cookies. You deserve a treat for finding my hanger." Tim and Sam are happy. They follow Dad to the kitchen. They have some cookies and milk. They tell Dad about their adventure with the big black hanger. Dad listens and laughs. He hugs them and tells them he loves them. They love him too. They are a happy family.
