Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Orange

One sunny day, a little bunny named Tim was playing in the park. He saw an orange ball bounce near him. Tim liked the ball and wanted to play with it. He tried to jump high to catch the ball, but it was too high for him. He tried again and again, but he could not reach it. Tim felt sad. Then, a stranger walked over to Tim. The stranger was a tall man with a big smile. He said, "Hi, little bunny! I see you want to play with the orange ball. Let me help you." Tim looked at the stranger and nodded his head. The tall man picked up the ball and handed it to Tim. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you!" He played with the orange ball and the stranger. They had a lot of fun. Then, the stranger bent down and whispered in Tim's ear, "I have a secret. I can help you spring high like me!" Tim was excited and wanted to learn. The stranger showed Tim how to jump high and soon Tim could reach the ball all by himself. Tim and the stranger played all day, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Toby. Toby was a happy child with a big heart. One day, Toby wanted to write a story. He grabbed some paper and a pencil, and he started to write. Toby wrote about a brave player. The player was going on a long journey to save a magical land from a gloomy dark force. Toby wrote about the player's adventures and his battle against the darkness. After Toby finished the story, he showed it to his mom. She was very proud of him and she praised Toby for his creativity. Toby was so happy that he smiled from ear to ear. Later in the day, Toby and his mom went to the park where Toby played with his friends. They had lots of fun! The end!

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to borrow a ball from her friend Tim. Tim had a big, high shelf with many toys. On the high shelf, there was a ball that Sue wanted to play with. Tim said, "Okay, you can borrow the ball, but be careful!" Sue tried to get the ball from the high shelf. She stood on her toes and reached up high, but she could not get it. Then, she saw a big box. Sue thought, "I can stand on the box to get the ball!" So, she stood on the box and reached up high again. This time, she got the ball! Just as Sue got the ball, something unexpected happened. The ball turned into a little bird! The bird said, "Thank you for helping me! I was stuck as a ball because of a mean witch's spell. But now, I am free!" Sue and Tim were very surprised. They played with their new bird friend all day. And from that day on, they knew that sometimes, when you borrow something, you can find a new friend too.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found an original coin. It was shiny and round. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show his friend, Sally. Tim went to Sally's house and said, "Look, I found a coin!" Sally looked at the coin and said, "Wow! It's so pretty!" They both smiled and laughed. One day, they saw a game at the park. The game was to toss the coin into a bucket. If the coin went into the bucket, they would win a prize. Tim and Sally decided to play the game. They took turns and tried their best. Finally, Tim tossed the coin and it went into the bucket! They won a big, soft teddy bear. Tim and Sally hugged the teddy bear and shared it. They were very happy and played with the teddy bear all day long.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue wandered in the park. She saw a big, red balloon. It was floating in the sky. Sue thought the balloon looked graceful as it moved in the air. Sue saw a boy named Tim who was sad. "Why are you sad?" Sue asked. Tim said, "My balloon flew away." Sue felt bad for Tim. Then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the red balloon back to Tim. He was so happy to have his balloon again. Sue and Tim played together with the balloon all day. They became good friends and had lots of fun.
