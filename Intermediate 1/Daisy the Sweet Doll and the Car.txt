Category: Intermediate
Title: Daisy the Sweet Doll and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a sweet doll named Daisy. Daisy was very small and pretty. She had a big smile and wore a pink dress. Daisy lived in a little house with her best friend, a fast car named Speedy. One sunny day, Daisy and Speedy went to play outside. They wanted to have a fun race. Speedy said, "I am fast, but you can ride on my back, Daisy!" Daisy was happy and climbed on Speedy's back. Speedy went very fast with Daisy on his back. They raced around the yard, laughing and having fun. Daisy held on tight as they went up and down hills. In the end, they were both very happy and tired. They went back to their little house and took a long nap together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had an unusual toy. It was a ball of string. She loved her ball of string. Amy took it everywhere she went. One day, Amy met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Amy's ball of string and said, "I hate string!" Amy was sad. She asked Tim, "Why do you hate string?" Tim said, "It is not fun. I like cars and trucks." Amy wanted to show Tim that string could be fun too. She tied one end of the string to a tree and the other end to a truck. Tim watched as the truck pulled the string tight. Then, Amy and Tim walked on the string like a tightrope. Tim smiled and said, "You are right, Amy. String can be fun!" They played with the string all day. From that day on, Tim did not hate string anymore. Amy and Tim became good friends, and they had many fun days playing with the unusual ball of string.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. Every night, when it was time to go to bed, he would weep. He would cry and cry because he did not want to be alone in the dark. One day, Tim's mom had an idea. She got him a night light. The night light made Tim's room a little bit bright. Tim was still scared, but not as much as before. Every night, Tim would look at the night light and feel a little bit better. He did not weep as much anymore. Soon, Tim was not scared of the dark at all. He could sleep all night without crying. Tim was a happy kid.

Sam and Mia were playing in the park with their toy cars. They liked to make noises and pretend they were driving fast. Sometimes they would crash into each other and laugh. "Beep beep! Out of my way!" Sam shouted as he zoomed his red car towards Mia's blue car. "No, you move! I was here first!" Mia said as she blocked his path with her car. They both pushed their cars harder, trying to make the other one yield. But neither of them wanted to give up. Suddenly, they heard a loud roar from behind the bushes. They turned around and saw a big rhinoceros coming out. It had a long horn and a thick skin. It looked angry and hungry. "Ah! A rhinoceros!" Sam and Mia screamed as they dropped their cars and ran away. The rhinoceros saw the toy cars and thought they were food. It charged at them and smashed them with its horn. It tried to bite them, but they were too hard and plastic. It spat them out and roared again. Sam and Mia hid behind a bench and watched the rhinoceros. They were scared and sorry for their cars. They wished they had not fought over them. "Sam, I'm sorry I didn't let you pass," Mia said in a small voice. "Mia, I'm sorry I was rude to you," Sam said in a small voice. They hugged each other and decided to be friends again. They hoped the rhinoceros would go away soon. They were very alert and waited for their mom and dad to come and get them. They learned to share and be kind to each other. They also learned to be careful of rhinoceroses.
