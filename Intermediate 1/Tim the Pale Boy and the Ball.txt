Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Pale Boy and the Ball

One day, a pale boy named Tim went to play outside. He wore a glove on his hand. He saw his friend, Sue, playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with Sue, but Sue did not want to share her ball. Tim said, "Sue, can I play with your ball?" Sue said, "No, Tim. I will not share my ball." Tim was sad because Sue refused to share her ball. He went back to his house and found his own ball to play with. Soon, Sue saw Tim playing with his ball. She wanted to play with Tim now. Sue said, "Tim, can I play with your ball?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Sue. We can play together." They both played with the ball and had lots of fun. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw a big tree with lots of shade. He wanted to play under the tree because the sun was very hot. Tim's mom said, "Yes, you can play there." Tim used a toy car to play in the shade. He liked how the tree kept him cool. As he played, he saw a hurt bird on the ground. The bird couldn't fly because its wing was hurt. Tim's mom saw the hurt bird too. She used her phone to call for help. Soon, a nice lady came to take care of the bird. Tim was happy that the bird would be okay. He played in the shade with his toy car, knowing the bird would get better.

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big cat named Luna. Luna was very upset. She lost her toy and could not find it. Max wanted to help Luna. Max said, "I will lead you to your toy." They walked and walked. They saw a quick bird fly by. The bird had Luna's toy in its beak. Max and Luna ran after the quick bird. They ran and ran until they caught the bird. Luna got her toy back. She was very happy. Max and Luna became best friends. They played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl, called Zoe. Zoe loved to design things. One day, Zoe had an amazing idea. She decided to design a reward that was fair for everyone. Zoe worked hard on her design. Several days later, Zoe had finished! She was so excited to show her family her reward. Zoe presented her design to her family and they were overwhelmed with joy. Everyone agreed it was a very fair reward. They all clapped and cheered for her. From then on, her family followed her design, and they all enjoyed equal rewards! Zoe was so happy. She was pleased that she had designed something that brought fairness and joy to everyone.

Lily and Ben are playing in the living room. They have many toys and books on the floor. Lily likes to make a big mess. Ben likes to keep things tidy. "Can I play with your file?" Lily asks Ben. She sees a file with colorful papers on the shelf. "No, you can't," Ben says. "That is my file. I use it for my drawings. You will make it dirty or tear it." "Please, Ben, please," Lily begs. "I will be careful. I just want to look at it." Ben thinks for a moment. He likes to share, but he also likes his file. He decides to permit Lily to play with his file, but only for a little while. "Okay, Lily, you can play with my file," Ben says. "But you have to promise to be gentle and put it back when you are done." "Thank you, Ben, thank you," Lily says. She takes the file and opens it. She sees many beautiful drawings of animals and flowers. She smiles and claps her hands. Lily and Ben play with the file for some time. They show each other their favorite drawings and make sounds of the animals. They have fun. But soon, their mom comes into the living room. She sees the toys and books on the floor. She sees the file on the couch. She is not happy. "Lily and Ben, what is this?" Mom asks. "Why is the living room so messy? And why do you have Ben's file? You know that is his special file." Lily and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry. They know they have to tidy up. "Sorry, Mom," Lily and Ben say. "We were playing with the file. It is very nice. But we will put it back and clean up the living room." Mom nods. She is glad that Lily and Ben are sorry and that they want to tidy up. She helps them to put the toys and books in their places and the file on the shelf. She hugs them and kisses them. "I love you, Lily and Ben," Mom says. "But next time, please ask me before you take something that is not yours. And please keep the living room tidy." Lily and Ben nod. They love Mom. They have learned their lesson. They will be more careful and respectful. They will keep the living room tidy.
