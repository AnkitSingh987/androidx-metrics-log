Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Tired Bird and the Rock

One day, a tired bird named Tim went to sleep on a big rock. He was so tired because he had been flying all day. Before he fell asleep, he told his friend, the ant, "I will wake up when the sun is up." The next day, the sun came up, and Tim did not wake. The ant saw this and thought of a plan. He went to find his friend, the wind. He asked the wind, "Can you help me wake up Tim? He is still sleeping on the rock." The wind agreed to help. The wind blew strong and fast. It made the rock move! The rock started to roll down a hill. Tim woke up and was very surprised. He looked around and saw that he was not on the rock anymore. He was on the ground, and the rock was far away. Tim and the ant laughed and played together. They were happy that the wind helped them have a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big calendar on his wall. He liked to look at the pictures and the days. One day, Tim saw that it was time to start something new. He wanted to be fit and strong. Tim asked his mom to help him. They went outside and started to run and play. They played catch with a ball and jumped high in the air. Tim was having so much fun. He felt his legs getting stronger and his heart beat faster. After a few days, Tim was very happy. He looked at his calendar and saw that he had been playing and running for a whole week. Tim was proud of himself. He knew that he was getting fit and strong. And every time he looked at his calendar, he smiled and thought about all the fun he had.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. His name was Sam. He used to love finding hidden sparks. Every day, he would go out looking for them. One day, as Sam was walking around looking for sparkles, he saw a naughty boy. The boy was trying to steal something from a shop. Sam stopped him and told him it was wrong to steal. The little boy said sorry and ran away. But as he went, he dropped something. It was a spark that was hidden in his pocket! Sam was so happy. He picked up the spark and looked at it closely. It shone brightly, just like all the other sparks he had found. Sam held onto the spark, and he was very glad that he had stopped the naughty boy from stealing it.

Lily loved to dance. She liked to spin and jump and twirl in her beautiful dress. She wanted to be a dancer when she grew up. She had a pair of shoes that helped her dance. They were pink and soft and had ribbons that she could wrap around her feet. One day, Lily saw a girl in the park who had no shoes. The girl was sad and cold. Her feet were dirty and hurt. Lily felt sorry for the girl. She wanted to help her. She thought of her shoes that helped her dance. She took them off and gave them to the girl. The girl was very happy. She thanked Lily and put on the shoes. She wrapped the ribbons around her feet. She felt warm and cozy. She tried to dance like Lily. She smiled and laughed. Lily was happy too. She saw that the girl was happy. She did not need her shoes to dance. She could dance with her bare feet. She danced with the girl. They became friends. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. You can make someone happy by giving them what you have. You can be happy without having everything you want. Dancing is fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was troubled because her room was full of dust. She wanted to care for her toys, but the dust made everything dirty. Amy thought, "I must clean my room and make my toys happy." Amy took a big cloth and started to wipe the dust away. She cleaned her toys one by one. Her teddy bear, Mr. Fluffy, was very happy. "Thank you, Amy," said Mr. Fluffy. "Now I can see and breathe better!" Amy smiled and continued to clean her room. When she was done, she sat down and played with her toys. They were all very happy and thanked Amy for caring about them. From that day on, Amy made sure to keep her room clean and her toys dust-free.
