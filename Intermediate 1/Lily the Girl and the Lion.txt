Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Lion

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to imagine things. She would pretend she was a princess, a superhero, or even a lion. Lily was a very persistent girl. When she wanted something, she would try and try until she got it. One day, Lily saw a big lollipop in the store. It was red and looked very yummy. She asked her mommy if she could have it. Her mommy said, "Not today, Lily." But Lily was persistent. She asked her mommy again and again. Finally, her mommy said, "If you can be a good girl and help me with the shopping, you can have the lollipop." Lily was so happy! She helped her mommy put food in the cart and carried a small bag. When they were done, her mommy gave her the big red lollipop. Lily imagined she was a superhero with a magic lollipop, and she felt very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to wait for the bus. It was a cold day, and Tim started to shiver. His mom gave him a big, warm hug to help him feel better. She said, "We will get on the bus soon, and you will be warm." While they waited, Tim saw a man selling toys. He saw a fake dog that looked like his dog at home. Tim asked his mom if he could buy the toy. His mom said, "Let's wait until after we ride the bus. Then, we can come back and get it." When the bus came, Tim and his mom got on. They sat down, and Tim felt warm again. He stopped shivering and smiled. Tim was happy to be on the bus and remembered the fake dog. He knew that after the bus ride, he would get the toy and play with it all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a sunny day, and the grass was wet from the rain last night. Tim loved to play in the wet grass. He liked how it felt on his feet. He saw a big, red ball near a tree and kicked it. The ball went very far and Tim laughed. As Tim was playing, he saw a naughty cat named Max. Max was always up to something. Max saw Tim and said, "Hi, Tim! Can I play with you?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Max! Let's play with the ball." Max and Tim ran to the ball and started to kick it back and forth. After a while, Max got tired and said, "Tim, can we do something else?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's hold hands and dance in the wet grass!" Max liked that idea. They held hands and danced around, laughing and having fun. Even though Max was naughty sometimes, he and Tim had a great day playing together.

One day, a little girl named Sue was playing with her toy bear, Bob. Sue was restless and couldn't sit still. She wanted a new taste in her mouth, so she went to her mom. "Mom, can I have a snack?" she asked. Her mom sighed and said, "Okay, but just a small one." Sue went to the kitchen and saw two snacks: an apple and a cookie. She wanted both, but her mom said she could only have one. Sue couldn't decide which one to pick. Bob, her toy bear, said, "Pick the apple, it's good for you!" Sue thought for a moment and then picked the apple. She took a bite and smiled. The apple was sweet and tasty. Sue was happy with her choice and shared it with Bob. They enjoyed the snack together and felt better.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going to visit his grandma's house. He needed to pack his bag. He put his clothes, toys, and a big red ball in the bag. On the way to grandma's house, Tim met a monster. The monster was big and green. Tim was not scared. He said, "Hello, monster! My name is Tim. What is your name?" The monster said, "I am a serious monster. My name is Sam." Tim and Sam became friends. They played with the big red ball. They laughed and had fun. Tim and Sam went to grandma's house together. Grandma was happy to see them. She made cookies for Tim and Sam. They all had a great time. And from that day on, Tim and the serious monster were best friends.
