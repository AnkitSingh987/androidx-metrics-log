Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Tree

One day, a boy named Tim had a big eraser. He liked to play with it. He took it to school every day. His friend, Sue, saw the eraser and wanted to play with it too. Tim said, "You can play with it later. I need it now." The next day, Tim's eraser was gone. He was sad and hurt. He looked for it but could not find it. Sue saw Tim was sad and asked, "What is wrong?" Tim told her about his lost eraser. Sue felt bad and wanted to help. Sue and Tim looked for the eraser together. They found it under a big tree. Tim was happy, and Sue was happy too. Tim said, "Thank you for helping me find my eraser. You can play with it now." They played with the eraser and had fun. They learned that sharing and helping can delay hurt feelings and make friends happy.

Lily liked to bake. She liked to mix flour and eggs and sugar and make yummy cakes. She liked to put icing and sprinkles on top and share them with her friends. But today, she had no friends to play with. Her best friend Anna was sick, and her other friends were busy. Lily felt lonely. She asked her mom if she could bake a cake, but her mom said no. She said they had no flour and eggs and sugar, and they had to go to the store later. Lily was sad. She wanted to bake now. She looked around the kitchen, but she could not find anything to bake with. She went to her room and saw her bookshelf. She liked her books, but she did not want to read them. She wanted to do something else. She had an idea. She took some books from the bookshelf and piled them on the floor. She pretended they were flour and eggs and sugar. She took a big bowl and a spoon and started to mix them. She said, "I am baking a cake, a big cake, a yummy cake." She was having fun, but she made a big mess. The books were everywhere, and some of them were torn and bent. She did not notice. She took a plate and put some books on it. She pretended they were the cake. She took some crayons and drew icing and sprinkles on them. She said, "My cake is done, it is beautiful, it is delicious." She heard her mom coming. She wanted to show her mom her cake, but her mom was angry. She saw the mess and the ruined books. She said, "Lily, what have you done? You have destroyed your books, and you have made a huge mess. You are not baking, you are breaking. You have to clean up and apologize." Lily was shocked. She did not mean to make her mom angry. She did not mean to destroy her books. She just wanted to bake a cake and have fun. She started to cry. She said, "I'm sorry, mom, I'm sorry. I was lonely, I had no friends, I wanted to bake a cake." Her mom saw her tears and felt sorry for her. She hugged her and said, "I'm sorry, too, Lily, I'm sorry. I know you were lonely, I know you wanted to bake a cake. But you can't use your books like that, they are not for baking, they are for reading. They are precious and valuable. You have to take care of them and respect them." Lily nodded and said, "I understand, mom, I understand. I love my books, I don't want to hurt them. I will clean up and fix them. I will be more careful next time." Her mom smiled and said, "That's good, Lily, that's good. I love you, and I'm proud of you. And you know what? We can still bake a cake, a real cake, a yummy cake. We can go to the store and buy some flour and eggs and sugar, and we can bake together. How does that sound?" Lily smiled and said, "That sounds great, mom, that sounds great. I love you, too, and I'm happy. Can we share the cake with Anna, when she feels better?" Her mom said, "Of course, we can, Lily, of course, we can. She is your friend, and she will love your cake. Come on, let's go to the store and bake a cake." Lily and her mom went to the store and bought some flour and eggs and sugar. They came back and baked a cake, a big cake, a yummy cake. They put icing and sprinkles on top and shared it with Anna, when she felt better. Lily was not lonely anymore. She was happy.
