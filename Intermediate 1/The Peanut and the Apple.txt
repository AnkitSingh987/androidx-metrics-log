Category: Intermediate
Title: The Peanut and the Apple

Once upon a time, there was a little peanut. The peanut was very small, but it was also great. It liked to play with its friends, the apple and the banana. One day, the peanut, the apple, and the banana went to the park. They wanted to play on the swings and slide. They had a lot of fun playing together. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. After playing, the peanut, the apple, and the banana were very tired. They sat down under a big tree to rest. They talked about their great day and how much fun they had. They all agreed that playing together was the best thing to do.

---

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a pretty ribbon. She wanted to tie it on her hair. So, she asked her mom for help. Her mom tied the pretty ribbon in Lucy's hair. Lucy was very happy. Lucy went to the park to play. She saw a big tree. Lucy thought she saw an angel in the tree. The angel had a big smile and was very nice. Lucy said "hello" to the angel. The angel said "hello" back. Lucy told the angel about her pretty ribbon. The angel looked at the ribbon and said, "It is very nice. You should be proud." Lucy was very proud of her ribbon. She thanked the angel and went home to tell her mom about the nice angel in the tree.

---

One day, a boy named Tom had a plan. He wanted to make everyone laugh. He thought, "I will do a crazy dance with my feet!" So, he put on his big, red shoes and went outside. Tom saw his friend Sue. He said, "Sue, watch me dance!" Tom started to dance with his feet going crazy. Sue laughed so much that she fell on the grass. Tom was happy that his plan worked. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and picked up Tom's red shoes. The shoes flew away! Tom and Sue looked at each other and laughed. They did not plan for that, but it made the day even more fun.

---

Once upon a time, there was a shy little boy named Tim. Tim was always too scared to talk to others. One day, while playing in his room, he found a toy missile. The missile was red and shiny. Tim felt very happy and excited. Tim wanted to show his new toy missile to his friends. He thought, "Maybe if I share my toy, I won't be shy anymore." So, Tim went to the park to find his friends. He saw them playing near the swings, and he walked up to them with his toy missile in hand. He said, "Look what I found! Do you want to play with my missile?" His friends were very happy and said, "Yes, we would love to play!" They all took turns playing with the missile. Tim didn't feel shy anymore. He learned that sometimes, you just have to be brave and not mind what others think. And that's how Tim and his friends had a great day playing with the toy missile.

---

One day, a little boy, Tim, found a talking toy. The toy could say many words. Tim was very happy. He played with the toy all day. But Tim was selfish. He did not want to share the toy with his sister, Sue. Sue saw the toy and wanted to play too. She asked Tim, "Can I play with the toy, please?" Tim said, "No, it is mine!" Sue was sad and her eyes filled with tears. She went to her room. That night, the toy began to tremble. It was scared of the dark. The toy called out, "Help, I am scared!" Tim heard the toy and went to help. But he was scared too. He did not know what to do. Tim thought of his sister, Sue. He knew she was brave. He went to Sue's room and said, "Sue, I need your help. The toy is scared." Sue smiled and said, "Let's help the toy together." Tim and Sue went to the toy and hugged it tight. The toy stopped trembling and felt safe. From that day on, Tim learned to share. He was not selfish anymore. Tim and Sue played with the toy together, and they were all very happy.
