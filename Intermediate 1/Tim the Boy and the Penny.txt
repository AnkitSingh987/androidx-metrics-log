Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Penny

One day, a small boy named Tim found a penny on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It was shiny and pretty. Tim had an idea. He wanted to see if the penny could spin. He put the penny on the table and gave it a little push. The penny began to spin. It spun faster and faster. Tim was very happy to see the penny spin. He clapped his hands and laughed. But then, something unexpected happened. The penny spun so fast that it started to grow bigger and bigger. Tim could not believe his eyes. The penny was now as big as a plate! Tim was surprised but he thought it was fun. He now had a big, shiny penny to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play chess. Chess was a game with big and small pieces. He liked to move the pieces on the board. One day, Tim's dad showed him an impressive chess board. The board was big and shiny. Tim was very happy. He wanted to play chess on the impressive board. Tim's friend, Sam, came to play chess with him. They played on the impressive board. Tim and Sam had fun. Tim's dad watched them play. He admired how well they played chess.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam was a proud squirrel who loved to find acorns. He would dare to climb the tallest trees to find the best acorns to eat. One sunny day, Sam saw the biggest acorn he had ever seen. It was on a very high branch of a very tall tree. Sam dared to climb the tree to get the big acorn. He climbed and climbed, higher and higher, until he reached the branch with the big acorn. Sam was so proud of himself for climbing so high. He held the big acorn tight in his little paws and began to climb down the tree. When he got to the ground, he showed all of his friends the big acorn. They all cheered for Sam, and they had a big party to celebrate. Sam felt very happy and proud that he dared to climb so high to find the big acorn.

One day, a little dog named Spot was playing in the park. He saw a big puddle and jumped in. He got very wet and dirty. Spot wanted to go home, but he didn't want to make his house dirty too. On his way home, Spot met a kind girl named Sue. Sue saw that Spot was wet and dirty. She had a clean towel with her. Sue wanted to help Spot, so she tried to give him the towel. At first, Spot did not accept the towel. He was scared. Sue was patient and talked to Spot in a soft voice. Soon, Spot felt safe and let Sue help him. Sue used the towel to clean Spot's fur. Spot was very grateful for Sue's help. They became good friends and played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time there was a lazy bear. He wore a big warm jacket all day. Every morning, he would wake up and find something to eat. He liked to feed on nuts, fruits and honey. One day the bear was walking around looking for food. Suddenly he saw a big beehive. He was so excited. The bear knew that the hive was full of honey and he couldn't wait to feed on it. The bear climbed up the tree and tried to get the honey. But it was much too hard to open beehive. He tried again and again, but still he could not manage to open it. The bear was sad but then he heard a voice from behind. It was a little boy who had seen the bear and wanted to help him. With the little boy's help, the bear was able to open the hive and get all the honey. The bear was so happy and thanked the little boy for all his help. He feasted on the honey for days and days. The end.
