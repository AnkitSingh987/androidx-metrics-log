Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Hunter and the Forest

Once upon a time, there was a hunter named Tom. Tom lived in a peaceful forest with many animals. He liked to rub his hands together when he was happy. One day, Tom met a big, brown bear named Ben. Ben was very friendly. Tom said, "Hi Ben! Do you want to play with me?" Ben nodded his head and they played together all day long. At the end of the day, Tom and Ben were very tired. They sat under a big tree and watched the sun go down. Tom rubbed his hands together and smiled. They were happy to be friends in the peaceful forest.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Ann. She had a big, dry rug in her room. Ann loved to play on the rug with her toys. One day, Ann wanted to invite her friend, Tom, to play with her. She asked Tom to come to her house. Tom was very happy and said yes. They played on the dry rug, sharing their toys and having a fun time. After playing, Ann and Tom were tired. They sat on the rug and talked. They learned that sharing and inviting friends to play made them both happy. The moral of the story is that sharing and being kind to others makes everyone happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a bike ride. He loved to twist the wheel and feel the light breeze on his face. As he rode, he saw a little girl named Sue sitting by the road. Sue looked sad. "Hi Sue, why are you sad?" asked Tim. "My wheel is broken, and I can't ride my bike," said Sue. Tim wanted to help. He looked at Sue's bike and saw that the wheel was twisted. He tried to fix it, but it was too hard. Tim had an idea. He ran to his house and brought back a big wrench. With the wrench, Tim twisted the wheel back into shape. Sue was so happy. She thanked Tim and they rode their bikes together, twisting their wheels and feeling the light breeze on their faces.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, red coat that she loved to wear. One day, Lucy's coat was not in her room. It was hidden! Lucy wanted to find her coat, so she started to look everywhere. Lucy went to the living room and started to count all the things she saw. She counted one chair, two tables, and three toys. But her coat was not there. Then, she went to the kitchen and counted four cups, five plates, and six spoons. But her coat was still hidden. Finally, Lucy went to her brother's room. There, she saw her coat on the floor! She was so happy to find it. She put on her big, red coat and went outside to play. From that day on, Lucy always remembered to put her coat away so it would not be hidden again.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a big tree. The big tree was very old and tired. One day, a man came to the forest with an axe. He wanted to cut the big tree down. The big tree did not want to be cut down. It wanted to restore its strength and keep living in the wild forest. So, the tree asked the man to leave it alone. But the man did not listen. The man used the axe to cut the big tree. The big tree fell down and could not restore. The wild forest was sad, and the big tree was gone forever.

One day, a lively dog went to search for a ball. He went to the park and saw a big tree. The dog looked up and saw a giant bird sitting on a branch. The bird had the ball in its beak. The dog barked and jumped to get the ball. The giant bird flew away with the ball. The dog chased the bird but could not catch it. The bird went far away and the dog was sad. The dog went back home without the ball. He learned that he should not try to take things from others. It is better to ask nicely and share. The dog was not happy, but he learned a good lesson.
