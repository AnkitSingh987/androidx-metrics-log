Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bulb and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a little bulb. The little bulb was light and shiny. The little bulb lived in a small box with other bulbs. They all liked to play together. One day, a big hand took the little bulb out of the box. The big hand put the little bulb in a lamp. The little bulb was very happy. The little bulb was allowed to shine bright and light up the room. The little bulb felt very special. But then, something unexpected happened. The little bulb saw a big mirror on the wall. The little bulb looked in the mirror and saw that it was not the only light bulb in the room. There were many other bulbs shining just as bright as the little bulb. The little bulb was not the only special one. But the little bulb was still happy, because now it had many new friends to play with and shine together.

One sunny day, a busy bee named Bob was flying around. He had big wings that went buzz, buzz, buzz. Bob loved to visit flowers and collect sweet nectar. As Bob was flying, he saw a little bird named Tina. Tina looked sad. She sat on a branch with a hurt wing. Bob flew to Tina and asked, "Why are you sad, Tina?" Tina looked at Bob and said, "I hurt my wing and can't fly." Bob wanted to help Tina. He thought and thought. Then he had an idea. "Tina, let's settle here and I will find help for you," said Bob. Bob buzzed around and found a kind spider named Sally. Sally could spin strong webs. Sally followed Bob to Tina. She spun a web around Tina's wing to make it feel better. Soon, Tina's wing was all better. She could fly again! Tina, Bob, and Sally became good friends. They played together in the sunny sky, happy and free.

One day, a friendly cat named Tom went to a new house. In the house, he found a bedroom. The bedroom had a big bed and a small bed. Tom liked the big bed. He jumped on the big bed and took a nap. When Tom woke up, he saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was happy to see Tom. She said, "Hello, friendly cat! Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "Yes, I do!" Sue and Tom played all day. They played with toys and ran around the house. After playing, Sue and Tom went to the bedroom to count their toys. Sue said, "Let's number our toys!" They counted one, two, three, four, five toys. Sue said, "We have five toys, Tom!" Tom was happy to know the number of toys. They put the toys away and went to sleep on the big bed.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big park. In the park, there was a fun game. Many kids would enter the line to play the game. They were all very glad to wait for their turn. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the line and wanted to play too. He entered the line and asked the girl in front of him, "What game is this?" The girl said, "It is a fun game where we throw a ball and try to hit a toy." Tim was very excited and couldn't wait for his turn. Finally, it was Tim's turn to play. He threw the ball, but he missed the toy. He was sad that he didn't win. Tim left the line and went home. He was not glad anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a curious little girl named Lily. She loved to play and explore the woods. One sunny day, she found a small seed on the ground. She picked it up and thought it looked like a fun thing to grow. Lily took the seed home and planted it in her backyard. She watered it every day and soon, a tiny green plant began to grow. Lily was so happy to see her plant grow bigger and bigger each day. One morning, as she went to water her plant, she saw something strange. The plant had grown a big skull on top! Lily was very surprised. She thought it was a toy skull, but when she touched it, she found out it was real. The skull began to talk to her and told her that it was a magic skull. It could grant her one wish. Lily thought hard and wished for a friend to play with. The skull granted her wish, and suddenly, a cute little puppy appeared. Lily and the puppy became best friends, and they played together in the forest every day.
