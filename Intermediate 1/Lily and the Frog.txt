Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Frog

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to visit her grandma. Lily loved her grandma very much. She had a wish to give her grandma a big, deep hug. On the way to grandma's house, Lily saw a big, deep hole. She was scared, but then a friendly frog came out of the hole. The frog said, "I can help you with your wish to give your grandma a big, deep hug." Lily was happy and let the frog help her. But when they got to grandma's house, the frog turned into a big, deep hug monster! The hug monster hugged everyone very tight. Lily was scared, but then she remembered her wish. Lily said, "I wish for the hug monster to go away." And just like that, the hug monster turned back into the friendly frog. The frog said sorry and hopped away. Lily and her grandma had a big laugh and shared a big, deep hug.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had an incredible toy named Mr. Bunny. They loved to play together every day. Lily liked to share her snacks with Mr. Bunny, even though he could not eat them. One day, Lily found an envelope on the floor. She did not know what was inside. She wanted to open it, but her mommy said no. Lily was sad, but she knew she had to listen to her mommy. She put the envelope on the table and went to play with Mr. Bunny. Later, her mommy said she could open the envelope. Lily was so happy! She opened it and found a fun sticker inside. She insisted that Mr. Bunny needed a sticker too, so she put one on him. They played all day with their new stickers, and they were very happy.

One day, a noisy dog named Max went to play outside. He saw a big tree and a little cat named Luna. Luna was up in the tree and could not get down. Max wanted to help Luna. Max said, "Luna, I will help you. I will win and get you down." Luna said, "Thank you, Max. I am scared." Max tried to climb the tree, but he was too big. So, he had another idea. Max found a long stick and used it to help Luna. He pushed the stick up to Luna, and she held on tight. Max pulled the stick down, and Luna came down too. Luna was happy and said, "Max, you are my hero. You won and saved me." They both went home as best friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with red and yellow leaves. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but his mom said, "No, Tim. It's not safe." Tim saw a spark on the ground near the tree. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! A spark!" But his mom was busy talking to a friend, so she ignored him. Tim felt sad. Tim saw a little girl named Sue eating a delicious apple. She saw Tim's spark and said, "Wow! That's a pretty spark!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you! Do you want to play with me?" Sue said, "Yes!" They played together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time there was a busy vehicle. It was a big blue bus full of happy people. The bus driver was very busy. He wiped the windows to keep the bus nice and clean. He wiped the floor to make sure that it was dry and safe. The bus driver drove the vehicle all around the streets. People hopped on and off. The bus driver greeted everyone with a wave and a smile. The bus driver was kept very busy all day, but he kept on wiping and driving the bus. At the end of the day he was tired, but the vehicle was clean and happy.

One day, a bird wanted to fly. It was on the roof. The bird was frustrated. It wanted to go to the big tree. The tree was far away. The bird said, "I want to fly to the big tree, but I can't." A cat heard the bird. The cat said, "I will help you!" The bird was scared but listened to the cat. The cat jumped up and down. It made the roof shake. The bird started to fly. It was flying to the big tree. The bird was happy. The cat was happy too. They became friends.
