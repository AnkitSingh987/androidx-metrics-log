Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Compassionate Fish and the Tiny

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate fish named Fin. Fin liked to help others. One day, he saw a little fish named Tiny stuck on a hook. Tiny was very sad and scared. Fin swam up to Tiny and said, "Don't worry, Tiny! I will help you." Fin began to think how to get Tiny off the hook. He tried to push and pull, but the hook was stuck. As Fin tried to help, a big fish came and saw them. The big fish was hungry. It saw Tiny and Fin and said, "Yum, two little fish for me to eat!" The big fish ate Tiny and Fin. They were gone, and the big fish swam away.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a small, lost pet. It was a cute cat. Sue wanted to help the cat find its home. Sue asked her friend Tom to help. Tom said, "Let's look for a house with a clear sign that says 'Lost Cat.'" They walked and walked. Sue saw a sign and whispered to Tom, "Look, there's a sign!" The sign had a picture of the lost cat. They took the cat to the house with the sign. A happy lady opened the door. "Thank you for finding my pet!" she said. Sue and Tom felt good for helping the cat find its home.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big goose near the pond. The goose was sad because it had lost its mate. "Hello, goose," Lily said. "Why are you sad?" The goose honked and looked at Lily with its black eyes. It was lonely and missed its mate. "Maybe we can help you find your mate," Tom said. "We can look around the park and see if we can see another goose." The goose nodded and followed Lily and Tom. They walked around the park and looked for another goose. They saw ducks, swans, pigeons, and squirrels, but no goose. "Maybe your mate flew away to another place," Lily said. "Maybe you can fly too and find them." The goose shook its head. It was too old and tired to fly. It had stayed in the park for a long time. "Then maybe you can make a new friend here," Tom said. "Maybe you can play with us or the other animals. We can share our bread and seeds with you." The goose smiled and honked. It liked the idea of having new friends. It decided to stay with Lily and Tom and the other animals. It was not so sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. He loved to play with his red ball. One sunny day, Tim went to the park with his mom. There, he saw a big tree with a point on top. Tim thought it was fun to kick his ball near the tree. As Tim kicked the ball, it went up and hit the point on the tree. Suddenly, the tree began to shake. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They didn't know what was happening. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly bird came out of the tree! The bird looked at Tim and his mom. It picked up the red ball with its beak and gave it back to Tim. Tim was so happy! He thought the bird was going to be mad, but it was just as jolly as Tim. They all played together in the park, kicking the ball and having fun.

Tim and Mia liked to play in the garden. They saw many flowers and bugs. One day, they found a caterpillar on a leaf. It was green and fuzzy. "Look, a caterpillar!" Tim said. "Can we keep it?" Mia nodded. "Yes, let's put it in a jar. It will be our pet." They ran to the house and got a jar. They put the caterpillar in the jar with some leaves and a stick. They made holes in the lid so it could breathe. They were very happy with their pet. They named it Coco. They watched it eat and crawl. They took turns holding the jar. But one day, they saw something different. Coco was not moving. It was wrapped in a white thing. It looked like a ball. "What happened to Coco?" Mia asked. "Is it sick?" Tim shook the jar. "Wake up, Coco! Play with us!" But Coco did not wake up. It did not play. It stayed in the white thing. Tim and Mia were sad. They thought Coco was gone. They did not want to play with the jar anymore. They left it on the table. The next day, their mom called them. "Tim, Mia, come here! I have a surprise for you!" They ran to the table. They saw the jar. But Coco was not in the jar. There was a hole in the white thing. And on the lid, there was a beautiful butterfly. It had wings with many colors. "Coco!" Tim and Mia shouted. "You are a butterfly!" Their mom smiled. "Yes, Coco is a butterfly. Caterpillars change into butterflies. It is called metamorphosis. Coco was not sick. Coco was finishing its change." Tim and Mia were amazed. They wanted to touch the butterfly. But their mom stopped them. "Be careful. Butterflies are very delicate. And some of them are dangerous. They can sting or bite. We have to let Coco go. Coco belongs to the garden, not to the jar." Tim and Mia understood. They loved Coco, but they wanted Coco to be free and happy. They opened the lid and let Coco fly away. They waved goodbye to Coco. They learned a lesson. They learned that some things change and some things stay the same. They learned that some things are for keeping and some things are for letting go. They learned that caterpillars are not pets, but butterflies are friends.
