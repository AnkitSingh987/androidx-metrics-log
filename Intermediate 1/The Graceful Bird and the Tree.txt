Category: Intermediate
Title: The Graceful Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird. The bird had many colors, just like a rainbow. The bird lived in a big tree. The tree was in a nice park. The bird liked to fly and sing. One day, the bird saw a little girl. The girl was sad. The bird flew down and said, "Why are you sad?" The girl looked up and said, "I lost my toy. I am sad because I cannot find it." The bird wanted to help the girl. The bird said, "I will help you find your toy." They looked all around the park. They looked near the swings. They looked near the slide. They looked near the pond. Finally, they found the toy. It was under a bush. The girl was happy. She said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird was happy too. They had a fun day together. The girl and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time, a playful cat named Kitty found a gear. The gear was big and round. Kitty liked the gear a lot. She wanted to show it to her friend, Doggy. Kitty went to Doggy's house with the gear. Doggy was happy to see Kitty. "Look what I found!" Kitty said. "It's a big gear!" Doggy looked at the gear and smiled. "Wow! Let's play with it!" he said. Kitty and Doggy played with the gear all day. They rolled it, pushed it, and even danced with it. They had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Kitty and Doggy were tired but happy. They knew they would have more fun with the gear tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house with his cat, Max. Tom loved Max very much, and they played together all day. One day, Tom was playing with Max when he saw an ashtray on the table. Tom said to Max, "Stop! We must not play near the ashtray. It is not safe." Max listened to Tom and they went to play in another room. Later, Tom's friend, Sara, came to visit. She saw the ashtray and asked, "Why is this here?" Tom told her, "I found it outside, but I don't use it. I keep it to remind me to be careful." Sara nodded and they all played together happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a good bear. He liked to relax under a big tree. Every day, the bear would sit and rest. He was very happy. One day, while the bear was relaxing, he heard a loud noise. The noise made the bear jump. It was a little bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The bear wanted to help the bird. The bear had an idea. He picked up the bird and put it on a high branch. The bird tried to fly again. This time, it flew! The bird was happy. But then, the bear saw something. The bird was not a bird, it was a butterfly! The butterfly thanked the bear and flew away. The bear went back to relax under the tree, feeling good about helping a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little berry. The berry was red and sweet. It lived in a big forest with many friends. The berry was very happy. One day, the berry saw a bird. The bird was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked the berry. "I am hungry," said the bird. The berry wanted to be important and help the bird. So, the berry said, "You can eat me. I will make you happy." The bird ate the berry and was very happy. The berry was important and helped the bird. Now, the bird could play with its friends again. The berry was gone, but it was happy to help the bird. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily were playing with their toy boats in the bathtub. They liked to pretend they were in the navy and had big missions. Ben had a blue boat and Lily had a pink boat. "Look, Lily, my boat is faster than yours!" Ben said, making his boat zoom across the water. "No, it's not! My boat is smarter than yours!" Lily said, making her boat turn and dodge. They kept playing until they heard a loud noise. It was their old dog, Rex, who had knocked over a vase in the living room. "Oh no, Rex!" Ben and Lily shouted, jumping out of the tub and running to see what happened. They found Rex lying on the floor, surrounded by broken pieces of glass. He was whimpering and bleeding from his paw. "Rex, are you okay?" Ben asked, kneeling down next to him. "Rex, we're sorry, we didn't mean to leave you alone," Lily said, stroking his fur. Rex licked their faces and wagged his tail. He was happy to see them, but he was also suffering from the pain. "Mom, Dad, help!" Ben and Lily cried, hoping their parents would hear them. Soon, their mom and dad came rushing in. They saw Rex and the mess and understood what happened. "Don't worry, kids, we'll take Rex to the vet. He'll be fine," their mom said, picking up Rex gently. "Can we come with you?" Ben and Lily asked, holding their toy boats. "Of course, you can. Rex needs your love and support," their dad said, smiling. They all got in the car and drove to the vet. Ben and Lily hugged their toy boats and prayed for Rex. They hoped he would get better soon and play with them again. They loved their old dog very much.
