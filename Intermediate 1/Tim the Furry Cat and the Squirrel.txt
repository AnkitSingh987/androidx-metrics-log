Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Furry Cat and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Tim. Tim loved to play in the garden. One day, he found a big ashtray. It was very dirty. Tim thought it would be fun to bury the ashtray in the ground. He dug a hole with his paws and put the ashtray inside. Then, he covered it with dirt. Tim felt happy and proud of his work. He went inside to take a nap. When Tim woke up, he went back to the garden. He saw a big surprise! The ashtray was not in the ground anymore. It was on top of the dirt and clean. Tim was shocked. He didn't know who cleaned the ashtray. Then, he saw his friend, the squirrel. The squirrel had dug up the ashtray and cleaned it. Tim and the squirrel played with the clean ashtray all day long.

Once upon a time there was a white dog called Speedy. One day, Speedy was playing in the garden when he found a pair of scissors. He ran up to the house to show his owners. "Look what I found!" said Speedy. His owners laughed. "That's very nice, Speedy. What are you going to do with them?" Speedy thought for a while. "I'm going to shrink them!" His owners laughed again. "You can't shrink scissors, Speedy. That's impossible!" But Speedy was determined to shrink the scissors. He held them tightly in his paws and closed his eyes. Suddenly, the scissors began to shrink! Speedy gasped with surprise. The scissors were now so small Speedy could fit them in his pocket. From then on, he always kept them with him. And whenever he wanted to use them, he could just shrink them back to their normal size!

Once upon a time, there was a little star. The star loved to shine very bright in the sky. Every night, the star would shine and make everyone happy. One day, the star saw a dirty rock on the ground. The rock was sad because it was not shiny like the star. The star wanted to help the dirty rock. It shone its light on the rock to make it feel better. The rock felt a shock of happiness. It was so happy that it started to shine too. The star and the rock became friends. Together, they shone bright in the sky and made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a little bored today. He saw a big open field and thought it would be fun to play there. In the open field, Tom met a girl named Lucy. Lucy was playing with a pretty toy. Tom asked, "Can I play with your toy?" Lucy said, "Yes, but you must value it because it is special to me." Tom and Lucy played with the toy all day. They were not bored anymore. They had so much fun together. In the end, they both learned to value their new friendship. And the open field became their favorite place to play.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play with his friend, Sam. They were in the park when they saw a man playing golf. Tim was confused. He had never seen golf before. Tim asked Sam, "What is that man doing?" Sam said, "He is playing golf. You hit the ball with a stick and try to get it in a hole." Tim wanted to try it too. They went to the man and asked if they could play. The man revealed a small golf club for kids. Tim and Sam took turns hitting the ball. They had so much fun playing golf.

One day, a big purple cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big billboard with a picture of a chair. Tom thought, "I want to sit on that chair." Tom walked and walked until he found the chair from the billboard. It was very big and very purple, just like him. He said, "Wow, this chair is perfect for me!" Tom tried to sit on the chair, but it was too high for him. A small bird named Tim saw Tom and said, "I can help you sit on the chair!" Tim flew down and picked Tom up with his strong wings. Together, they sat on the big purple chair. As they sat on the chair, they became friends. Tom learned that it is good to ask for help when you need it, and Tim learned that helping others can make you happy. From that day on, Tom and Tim were the best of friends, always helping each other and sitting on the big purple chair.
