Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a regular day, playing on the swings and slides. Tim was very happy. After playing, Tim was hungry. "Mom, can I have a snack?" he asked. His mom gave him a yummy snack. It was a banana. Tim ate the banana and felt better. Then, Tim saw a small door in a tree. He said, "Mom, look at the door!" His mom said, "Yes, it's a fairy door. Let's shut it." They shut the door and went home. Tim had a fun day at the park.

---

Once there was a noisy musician. He loved to play music all day. The musician had a big drum, a small guitar, and a shiny bell. He liked to play them very loud. All the people in the town knew when the musician was playing because they could hear the loud sounds. One day, the musician met a magic man. The magic man said, "I can make you disappear if you want." The musician thought this would be fun, so he said yes. The magic man waved his hand, and the musician disappeared. Now, the musician could play his music, and no one could see him. But he was still very noisy. The people in the town did not like it. They could not find the musician to ask him to be quiet. So, they had to live with the loud sounds forever. The musician never came back, and the town was always noisy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He went to the park with his mom. Tim was very happy to play outside. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. While playing, Tim noticed a small bug on the ground. The bug looked sad and miserable. Tim asked the bug, "Why are you so sad?" The bug said, "I lost my home and I don't know where to find it." Tim wanted to help the bug find its home. So, he asked his mom for help. They all looked around the park together. After a while, they found the bug's home under a big tree. The bug was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and mom!" Tim and his mom smiled and went back to playing at the park.

---

Once upon a time, there were two best friends named Tom and Sarah. Tom and Sarah were playing in the garden when Sarah noticed something shining in the grass - it was a beautiful bracelet! She shouted to Tom with excitement, "Look, Tom! I found a bracelet!" Tom ran to her and was surprised by the sparkly bracelet. He asked, "Where do you think it came from?" Sarah thought carefully and said, "Maybe it disappeared from the sky!" Tom and Sarah looked up, but they only saw a dry blue sky. They stayed there for a while, but the bracelet did not reappear. Tom and Sarah were very sad. But then Sarah thought of an idea. She said, "Let's try wearing the bracelet together." So Tom and Sarah both put on the bracelet and hugged each other. The two friends felt special wearing the bracelet together, so it didn't matter if it had disappeared from the sky or not.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a race. Many kids came to run. Tim was very excited. He wanted to win the race. His mom told him, "Mind the other kids when you run." The race started. All the kids ran fast. Tim ran as fast as he could. He saw his friend, Sam, running next to him. They both wanted to win. The race was incredible. Tim was having so much fun. In the end, Tim did not win the race. But he was happy. He had a great time with his friends. Tim's mom was proud of him. She said, "You did an incredible job, Tim. You minded the other kids and had fun!"

Mia and Ben are twins. They like to play with their baby brother, Leo. Leo is an infant. He is very small and cute. He sleeps a lot in his crib. One day, Mia and Ben are bored. They want to have some fun. They go to Leo's room. They see Leo sleeping in his crib. They think of a game. They decide to peek at Leo. Mia and Ben take turns to peek at Leo. They hide behind the door. They whisper to each other. They say, "One, two, three, peek!" They open the door a little bit. They look at Leo. They giggle softly. Leo hears the noise. He wakes up. He sees Mia and Ben peeking at him. He smiles. He makes a sound. He says, "Goo goo gaa gaa!" He kicks his legs. He waves his arms. He is lively. Mia and Ben are happy. They run to Leo. They hug him. They kiss him. They say, "Hello, Leo! We love you!" They play with Leo. They make him laugh. They have a lot of fun.
