Category: Intermediate
Title: The Elephant and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant. The elephant was very nice. He liked to provide food for his friends. One day, he found yummy fruit in the forest. He wanted to share it with his friends. The elephant went to his friend, the bird. "Bird, I have yummy fruit for you!" said the elephant. The bird was very happy. They ate the fruit together. The elephant then went to find more friends to share the yummy fruit with. But the elephant could not find any more friends. He looked and looked, but all his friends were gone. The elephant was sad. He had no one to share the yummy fruit with. In the end, the elephant was all alone, and the yummy fruit was left uneaten.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play together. But sometimes they disagreed about what to play. One day, they found a big box in the garage. It was heavy and had a picture of a van on it. "Let's make a van out of the box!" Ben said. He liked cars and trucks and wanted to drive the box-van. "No, let's make a house out of the box!" Lily said. She liked dolls and animals and wanted to live in the box-house. They both pulled the box to their side, but it was too heavy to move. They started to argue and shout. "Van!" "House!" "Van!" "House!" Their mom heard them and came to the garage. She saw the box and the twins. "What are you doing, Ben and Lily?" she asked. "We want to play with the box, but we disagree!" Ben said. "We can't agree on what to make!" Lily said. Mom smiled and had an idea. "Why don't you make both?" she said. "You can cut the box in half and make a van and a house. Then you can play together and visit each other." Ben and Lily liked mom's idea. They got scissors and tape and markers and started to cut and decorate the box. Soon they had a van and a house. They put them next to each other and played happily. "Vroom, vroom, I'm driving to your house, Lily!" Ben said. "Welcome, Ben, come in and see my dolls!" Lily said. They agreed that playing with the box was fun. They thanked mom for helping them and gave her a hug.

Once upon a time, there was a nice house. Inside, it had a nice window. One day, a little girl was in the house with her mommy and daddy. The girl wanted to look out the window to see the birds. Suddenly, there was a loud noise and a man appeared outside the window! He tried to cut the window with a big saw. The little girl was scared and started to cry. Her mom and dad saw the man and knew what he was doing. They ran outside. Daddy shouted at the man to stop and said that he must not cut the nice window. The man ran away and the girl was safe. Mommy hugged the little girl and dried her tears. They all felt happy that the window was safe and the little girl could look out it to see the birds again.

Sam was sick. He had a big cough and a hot head. He did not want to eat or play. His mom took him to the doctor. The doctor was nice. He looked at Sam and gave him some medicine. "Sam, you need to take this medicine every day. It will help you feel better," the doctor said. Sam did not like the medicine. It was bitter and yucky. He made a face and spat it out. "Sam, please try to swallow the medicine. It is good for you," his mom said. Sam shook his head. He did not want to try. He threw the medicine on the floor. The doctor and his mom were sad. They tried to make Sam take the medicine, but he did not listen. He cried and kicked and screamed. They took Sam to the nurse. The nurse was brilliant. She had a big smile and a funny hat. She had a syringe with a needle. "Sam, this is the nurse. She will give you a shot. It will make you feel better faster," the doctor said. Sam did not like the shot. He was scared of the needle. He tried to run away, but the nurse was faster. She held him tight and poked him with the needle. "Ow! Ow! Ow!" Sam yelled. He felt a sharp pain in his arm. He felt dizzy and sick. He fainted. The nurse checked his pulse and his temperature. She shook her head. "I'm sorry, doctor. The medicine was too strong for him. He had an allergic reaction. He is gone," she said. The doctor and his mom were shocked. They cried and hugged each other. They wished they had made Sam take the medicine. They wished they had not given him the shot. They wished he was still alive.
