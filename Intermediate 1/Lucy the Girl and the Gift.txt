Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Gift

One day, a girl named Lucy found a pink diary on her way home. She picked it up and saw that it had no name. She thought, "I need to find who this belongs to." Lucy asked her friend, Tom, "Do you know who lost this pink diary?" Tom said, "No, but let's ask others if they need it." They went around asking, but no one knew who it belonged to. Finally, they met an old man who said, "I know who needs this pink diary. It belongs to a girl named Mary." Lucy and Tom went to Mary's house and gave her the diary. Mary was so happy and said, "Thank you for giving it back to me. I was sad when I lost it." The unexpected thing was that Mary had a gift for the person who found her diary. She gave Lucy a beautiful pink dress. Lucy was very happy and learned that helping others can bring good things to you too.

One day, a big bottle was in a room. The bottle was very organized. It liked to keep things neat. Inside the bottle, there were many small toys. The toys were happy because the bottle was their home. A little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the bottle and said, "Hello, bottle! Can I play with the toys inside you?" The bottle said, "Yes, Tim. You can play with the toys, but please be gentle." Tim played with the toys and had lots of fun. He made sure to be gentle with them, just like the bottle asked. When he was done playing, he put the toys back inside the bottle. The bottle was happy and said, "Thank you, Tim, for being kind and organized." Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome, bottle. I will always be nice to you and your toys." From that day on, Tim and the bottle were best friends. They always played together and kept things neat and organized.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to organize his toys in his room. One day, Max decided to have a picnic with his friends. He wanted to share his toys with them. It was a hot day, and the sun was shining with a lot of heat. Max and his friends went to a big tree to have their picnic. They put a soft blanket on the ground under the tree. They all sat down and started to eat. Max's mom made them delicious sandwiches with cheese and ham. The sandwiches were so yummy that all the friends wanted more. They played with Max's toys and had a great time. After the picnic, Max and his friends said thank you to Max's mom for the delicious food and the fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy cars and run in the park. One sunny day, Tom and his friends went to the park to play. While playing, Tom fell down and hurt his finger. He started to cry because it hurt a lot. His friends came to help him and made him feel better. They told him not to worry and that his finger would be okay. Tom felt a little embarrass because he cried in front of his friends. But they did not laugh at him. They cared for him and told him that it was okay to cry when he was hurt. Tom was happy to have such good friends. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a large man named Tom. Tom had big muscles because he liked to work hard. Every day, Tom would help people in his town by lifting heavy things and fixing broken stuff. One sunny day, Tom went to the park to work. He saw a cat stuck in a tree. Tom thought he could use his big muscles to shake the tree and help the cat. He shook the tree, but the cat did not come down. Tom was sad and did not know what to do. Then, a little girl named Lucy came by. Lucy did not have big muscles like Tom, but she had a smart idea. She ran home and got a long stick. Lucy used the stick to gently poke the cat. The cat climbed down from the tree! Tom was so happy and thanked Lucy. They both learned that sometimes you don't need big muscles to help, just a good idea.
