Category: Intermediate
Title: Pippin the Gray Pony and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little gray pony named Pippin. Pippin lived in a big field with his friends. They loved to play and run all day. Pippin was very happy. One day, Pippin saw a big red ball in the field. He wanted to play with it, but he didn't want to share with his friends. So, Pippin decided to take the ball and disappear behind the big tree. Pippin played with the ball all by himself. At first, it was fun. But soon, he started to feel lonely. Pippin missed his friends and wanted to play with them again. So, Pippin brought the ball back to his friends and they all played together. From that day on, Pippin learned that it's more fun to share and play with friends than to be alone. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big parade. Many people came to watch. They saw a band march down the street. The band made a lot of noise. The people clapped and smiled. In the parade, there was a big, soft bear. The bear wore a hat and danced. The children laughed and waved. They liked the soft bear. When the parade was over, the people went home. The children talked about the fun parade. They liked the band, the noise, and the soft bear. They hoped to see the parade again soon.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there lived a happy, little bird. The bird had a beautiful, rough nest high up in a tree. Every day, the bird sang and played with his friends. One day, the bird saw a pretty rainbow in the sky. It had many colors like red, blue, and yellow. The bird wanted to add the colors of the rainbow to his rough nest to make it even more beautiful. The bird found red, blue, and yellow things in the forest. He added them to his rough nest. The nest looked just like the rainbow! The bird was very happy and showed his pretty nest to all his friends. They all loved it and played happily ever after.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play and laugh together. One day, they find a big box of clothes in the attic. They decide to try them on and pretend to be different people. Tom wears a hat and a coat. He pretends to be a doctor. He says, "Hello, Lily. I am a doctor. Do you need a check-up?" Lily wears a dress and a necklace. She pretends to be a princess. She says, "No, thank you, doctor. I am a princess. I need a crown." They have fun and make jokes. They say silly things and giggle. They take turns to wear different clothes and pretend to be different people. But then, Tom sees a pair of glasses. He thinks they are funny. He wears them and says, "Look at me, Lily. I am a teacher. Do you want to learn something?" Lily looks at him and frowns. She says, "No, Tom. Those are not funny. Those are my daddy's glasses. He needs them to see. You should not wear them. You should be kind." Tom feels bad. He did not know they were Lily's daddy's glasses. He did not mean to be unkind. He says, "I am sorry, Lily. I did not know. I was just making a joke. Please forgive me." Lily is patient. She knows Tom is her friend. She says, "It's okay, Tom. I forgive you. But you should be careful. Some things are not for jokes. Some things are important. You should respect them." Tom nods. He takes off the glasses and puts them back in the box. He says, "You are right, Lily. I should respect them. Thank you for being patient with me. You are a good friend." Lily smiles. She says, "You are welcome, Tom. You are a good friend, too. Let's play some more." They hug and play some more. They have fun and laugh together. They learn to be kind and respectful. They are happy.

One day, a thoughtful boy named Tim was playing in his room. He found a big box of colors. He thought it would be fun to draw on the wall. So, he took the colors and made a big, pretty picture on the wall. His mom came in and saw the picture. She said, "Oh no, Tim! We do not draw on the wall. We need to wipe it clean." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. Tim and his mom took a big cloth to wipe the wall. They worked together and soon the wall was clean again. Tim said, "I am sorry, Mom. Next time, I will only draw on paper." His mom smiled and gave him a hug. They both felt happy again.
