Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sam and the Dog

One day, Tom and Sam went to the park. They saw a big seat that could lift them up and down. They wanted to go on it very fast. Tom said, "Let's go on the seat and have fun!" Sam said, "Yes, let's do it!" They got on the seat and it started to lift them up and down. They were going fast and having lots of fun. All of a sudden, they heard a loud noise. A big dog came running to the seat. The dog wanted to play too. The dog said, "Can I play with you?" Tom and Sam were surprised. They did not know dogs could talk. They said, "Yes, you can play with us!" The dog got on the seat and they all went up and down, fast and slow, having fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom. The sea was very popular, and many kids liked to play there. Tim loved to swim and splash in the water with his friends. Tim's mom said, "Tim, I warn you. Do not go too far into the sea. It can be dangerous." Tim said, "Okay, mom. I will be careful." But Tim did not listen to his mom. He swam far away from the shore. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A big fish came and said, "Hello, Tim! I saw that you swam too far. I want to help you get back to your mom." Tim was surprised but happy. The big fish took Tim back to the shore, and Tim said, "Thank you, big fish!" From that day on, Tim always listened to his mom's warnings.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and read books. One day, she found a big statue in her yard. The statue was of a funny-looking man. Lily thought it was a new toy for her to play with. Lily played with the statue every day. She would dance around it and tell it stories. One day, she saw the statue move a little. She was very surprised! The statue was not just a toy, it was a magic statue! The magic statue could talk and play with Lily. But one day, Lily did not play with the statue. She was too busy playing with her other toys. The magic statue became sad. Lily saw the statue's sad face and felt regret. She promised to play with the statue every day. From that day on, Lily and the magic statue played together and were always happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk in the quiet woods. She wore her red shoes with a small heel. Sue saw a big box under a tree. She was very curious about what was inside. Sue tried to open the box, but it was hard. She asked a squirrel, "Can you help me open the box?" The squirrel nodded and jumped on the box. With a little push, the box opened! Inside the box, Sue found a pretty dress and a note. The note said, "For the girl with the red heel shoes." Sue smiled and put on the dress. She knew the quiet woods had a special surprise just for her.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He was enthusiastic about everything. He loved to play, run, and jump all day long. But there was one thing he did not like. Tim hated his mattress. It was old and not very soft. He wished for a new mattress to sleep on. One day, while Tim was playing outside, he saw a big truck pull up to his house. The truck had a picture of a mattress on it. Tim was so excited! His mom and dad must have bought him a new mattress. He ran inside to tell his mom and dad, "Thank you!" But when Tim went inside, he saw that the new mattress was not for him. It was for his baby sister, Lily. Tim was sad and surprised. He did not know that Lily needed a new mattress too. That night, Tim went to sleep on his old mattress, still wishing for a new one. The next day, Tim's mom and dad had a surprise for him. They saw how much he wanted a new mattress, so they bought one for him too! Tim was so happy and grateful. Now, both Tim and his baby sister, Lily, had new mattresses to sleep on. They slept very well from that day on, and Tim never hated his mattress again.
