Category: Intermediate
Title: Max and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play and run all day. He would exercise and jump in the park. Max was a very happy dog. One day, Max saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and cried, "Help, help!" Max wanted to help the cat. He thought and thought about how to rescue the cat. Max had an idea! He found a big stick and pushed it against the tree. The cat climbed down the stick and was safe. Max was tired but happy to help. The cat said, "Thank you, Max!" And they became best friends.

Jill was a three year old girl. She liked to swing in the park all day. One day, as she was swinging, she saw a bottle on the ground. She quickly hopped off the swing and picked it up. "What do you have there?" asked her mom. "I found a bottle!" said Jill, with a smile. "Let me take a look," replied her mom. The bottle was spoiled and it was no good. Her mom said, "I'm sorry, honey. This bottle is spoiled and we can't use it." Jill was sad and went back to the swing. But to her surprise, a large colorful toy was tucked underneath the seat. She grabbed it up and showed it to her mom. Her mom laughed and said, "Somebody must have left it here. Now you have a new toy to play with. Let's go home." Jill smiled and grabbed the toy and began to swing. She was so happy to have a new toy to play with. Playing in the park was even more fun now.

Once upon a time, there was a big, tough dog named Max. Max liked to run and play in the park. One day, Max saw a mystery box under a tree. He was very curious about the box. Max barked and said, "Woof! What's in the box?" A little bird heard Max and flew down. The bird said, "Tweet! I don't know, but let's open it!" Max and the bird tried to open the mystery box together. They pushed and pulled, but the box was tough. Suddenly, the box opened with a big "POP!" Max and the bird were surprised. But, oh no! There was a mean cat inside the box! The cat hissed and chased Max and the bird away. Max and the bird were sad and scared. They learned to be careful with mystery boxes.

Once upon a time, there was a furry bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to spring and hop all around the forest. One day, it got very cold outside, and Bobo wanted to find a warm place to stay. Bobo hopped and hopped until he found a big tree. Inside the tree, he saw a little bird named Chirpy. Chirpy was cold too. Bobo said, "Chirpy, can I stay with you in the tree? It is cold outside." Chirpy said, "Yes, Bobo, come in and we can be warm together." Bobo and Chirpy became good friends. They stayed warm in the tree and played together. When the cold went away, they went outside to spring and hop in the sunshine. They were very happy to have each other as friends.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old cat named Whiskers. Whiskers lived in a big house with his friend, a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy saw Whiskers race around the house. She thought it was funny and wanted to play with him. "Whiskers, let's race to the kitchen!" Lucy said. Whiskers liked the idea, so they raced down the hall. Lucy won, but Whiskers was not sad. He knew that there was a yummy treat waiting for them in the kitchen. Lucy's mom had made some butter for their bread. Whiskers loved butter, but he knew he should not eat too much. When they got to the kitchen, Lucy saw the butter on the table. She wanted to share it with Whiskers, but she remembered that her mom said not to give him too much. Lucy and Whiskers had a little bit of butter on their bread and enjoyed their snack. They were happy they had raced to the kitchen and found the yummy treat. And Whiskers was glad that Lucy was wise like him and knew not to give him too much butter.
