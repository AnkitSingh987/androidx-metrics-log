Category: Intermediate
Title: Yes and the Cat

One day, a cat and a dog were talking. The cat said, "I want to go on a regular walk." The dog agreed, "Yes, let's go!" So, they went for a walk in the park. As they walked, they saw a big box. The cat and dog were curious. They went to the box and opened it. Inside, they found a bird who said, "Help me escape!" The cat and dog wanted to help the bird. They opened the box wide and the bird flew out. But as the bird flew away, it turned into a big, colorful butterfly! The cat, the dog, and the butterfly all laughed and played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a small house with a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together every day. One day, Fluffy found a box of matches on the floor. Fluffy looked at the matches and said, "Lily, what are these?" Lily looked at the matches and said, "Fluffy, these are matches. They can make fire, but we must be careful." Fluffy and Lily decided to play a game. They took turns to pull a match out of the box. When it was Fluffy's turn, he pulled a match out with his paw. Lily laughed and clapped her hands. They played until there were no more matches left in the box. They put the matches away and played with their toys instead. Fluffy and Lily were happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a new, big box of crayons. The crayons were very happy to be in the box together. They liked to talk and play with each other. One day, a little girl named Amy found the box of crayons. She was so happy! She picked up a blue crayon and said, "Hello, blue crayon! Let's draw a pretty picture together." The blue crayon was happy too. Amy took the blue crayon and leaned it on the paper. She drew a big blue sky. Then, she picked up a green crayon and drew a pretty green tree. All the crayons took turns helping Amy draw a beautiful picture.

One day, a small cat named Sam set out to find a new bed. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree. In the tree, there was a small bird named Sue. Sam said, "Hi, Sue! Can you help me find a new bed?" Sue said, "Sure, Sam! I know a good place." They went to a small house with many beds. Sam tried a big bed, but it was too big. He tried a soft bed, but it was too soft. Then, he tried a small bed and it was just right. Sam was very happy. But then, a small dog named Max came in. Max said, "That's my bed!" Sam was sad, but he gave the bed back to Max. Max said, "Thank you, Sam. Let's find a bed for you too." They all looked together and found a small bed just right for Sam. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a large park. In the park, there was a big slide. Many kids loved to play on the slide. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw the slide and wanted to play on it. He ran to the slide and started to climb up. As he climbed, he felt something sharp on the slide. Tim stopped and said, "Ouch! Mom, there is something sharp on the slide." His mom came over and looked at the slide. She saw a small, sharp rock stuck on the slide. She carefully took the rock off and said, "There you go, Tim. The sharp rock is gone. Now you can play on the slide." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" He climbed up and slid down the large slide. He had so much fun playing on the slide all day with his friends.

One day, a mommy and her little girl named Lucy were walking in the park. The sun was shining and the birds were chirping. Lucy wanted to go on the playground, but her mommy said it was too hot and warned her to stay cool. So, Lucy decided to get a lotion from the ice cream parlor. She nearly ran to the ice cream parlor and when she got there, she asked the lady for some lotion. The lady said, "No, it's too tight. You can't get it." Lucy was sad and started to cry. Her mommy heard her, ran to the ice cream parlor, and asked the lady why she said no to Lucy. The lady said that the lotion was almost finished and there was only one left. She had to save it for someone else. Lucy was really disappointed and her mommy was too. They had to go home without the lotion.
