Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Gum

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they find a pack of gum on a bench. Lily picks it up and opens it. "Look, Ben, gum!" she says. "Do you want some?" Ben nods. He likes gum. Lily gives him a piece and takes one for herself. They unwrap the gum and put it in their mouths. They chew and blow bubbles. "This gum is yummy," Ben says. "And it makes pretty bubbles." Lily agrees. She likes the pink color of the gum. She thinks it makes her look pretty too. She smiles and blows a big bubble. "Wow, Lily, your bubble is so big!" Ben says. He tries to blow a bigger one, but his gum pops. "Oh no, Ben, your gum is gone!" Lily says. She feels sorry for Ben. She looks at her gum. She still has some left. "Here, Ben, you can have some of my gum," she says. She takes out her gum and breaks it in half. She gives one half to Ben and puts the other half back in her mouth. "Thank you, Lily, you are very nice," Ben says. He takes the gum and puts it in his mouth. He chews and smiles. "You're welcome, Ben, you are my friend," Lily says. She chews and smiles too. They hug and keep chewing their gum. They are happy. They have gum and they have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, people loved to watch fireworks. They would trust their friends to put on a big show. Everyone would clap and cheer when they saw the bright colors in the sky. One day, a new firework came to town. This firework was angry. It did not want to go up and make pretty colors. It wanted to stay on the ground and be left alone. But the people in the town did not know this. The day of the big show came. The people trusted their friends to make the fireworks go up. But when it was the angry firework's turn, it did not go up. It stayed on the ground and made a loud noise. Everyone was scared and ran away. The angry firework felt bad. It did not want to make the people scared. So, it decided to trust the people and go up in the sky. It made the biggest and most beautiful colors of all. The people clapped and cheered. From that day on, the angry firework was happy, and it loved being a part of the big show.

Once upon a time, there was a little pigeon. The pigeon liked to play in the park. One day, it saw a slide and wanted to try it. The pigeon flew to the top of the slide. It was a little upset because it was scared. But it wanted to be brave, so it tried to slide down. The pigeon went down the slide very fast. When the pigeon reached the bottom, it was so happy. It was not upset anymore. It wanted to slide again and again. The pigeon had lots of fun on the slide that day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They were having a fun time playing with a ball. Lily saw a big package near a tree. She ran to the package and tried to lift it. But it was too heavy for her. "Mom, come help me!" Lily said. Her mom came over and helped her lift the package. They opened it and found a cute puppy inside. The puppy was very happy to see them. The puppy was naughty and liked to play. It jumped and ran around the park with Lily. They played with the ball and had a great time. Soon, it was time to go home. Lily asked her mom, "Can we keep the puppy, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can keep the puppy. But we must teach it not to be naughty." Lily was very happy and hugged her new friend. They all went home together, ready to start a new adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a team of three friends: a dog, a cat, and a bird. They lived in a small house near a big tree. They were very lucky because they had a nice home and each other. One day, they wanted to have a big party. The dog said, "I will provide the toys." The cat said, "I will provide the food." The bird said, "I will provide the music." They were all very happy to help. The day of the party came, and the team was very busy. The dog got the toys, the cat got the food, and the bird sang songs. All their friends came to the party and had a great time. They were a lucky team and knew they could do anything together.
