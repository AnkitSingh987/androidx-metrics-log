Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Stone

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a lovely bird in a tree. Mia wanted to be friends with the bird, so she called to it, "Hello, bird! Come play with me!" The bird flew down and landed on a big stone. Mia picked up the stone and showed it to the bird. "Look, bird! This stone is so nice and smooth. Let's play with it together!" Mia and the bird played with the stone all day. They rolled it, threw it, and even used it to draw on the ground. At the end of the day, Mia and the bird said goodbye. They were both very happy to have a new friend.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big horse with a long tail. The horse was brown and white and had a shiny coat. "Wow, look at the horse!" Tom said. "He is so big and pretty. Can we pet him?" Lily nodded. She liked horses too. They walked up to the horse and said hello. The horse neighed and shook his head. "Hello, horse," Lily said. "What is your name?" The horse did not answer. He just snorted and looked at them. "Maybe he does not understand us," Tom said. "Maybe he speaks a different language." Lily thought for a moment. She remembered something her mom had mentioned. "Mom said horses like carrots," she said. "Maybe we can give him a carrot and he will be our friend." Tom agreed. He ran to the nearby shop and bought a carrot. He came back and held it out to the horse. "Here, horse, this is for you," he said. "Do you like carrots?" The horse smelled the carrot and smiled. He took it gently from Tom's hand and ate it. He licked his lips and nuzzled Tom and Lily. "Thank you, horse," Lily said. "You are very nice. We like you." The horse nodded and whinnied. He let Tom and Lily pet his long tail and his soft ears. They played with him until it was time to go home. "Bye, horse," Tom and Lily said. "We hope to see you again." The horse waved his tail and watched them leave. He was happy to have made new friends.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a cat named Max. They liked to play together all day long. One day, Lily's mom said it was time for Max to bathe. Lily filled a tub with water and put Max in it. Max did not like the water, but Lily made sure he was clean. That night, when it was dark outside, Lily and Max snuggled in bed. They were happy and clean, ready for another day of fun.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl named Daisy. She was excited because it was the day of her very first exam. She couldn't wait to see her classmates and show them her smarts. When Daisy arrived at the exam hall, she was a little nervous but still excited. She glanced around looked for a nice seat. Suddenly, she stumbled and almost fell! Her friends laughed at her, but Daisy was patient and managed to smile. In the exam hall, Daisy was so eager to answer the questions that she quickly banged her pencil on the paper. The teacher scolded her for being so impatient. Daisy was a little shocked at first, but then nodded and said, "Sorry, teacher." Daisy was determined to do her best on the exam. In the end, she managed to pass with flying colors. She was so proud and happy that she stumbled back to her seat! Even though she was a little impatient, she passed with flying colors!

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to own a special power. He wanted to be able to fly like a bird or run like a cheetah. Tim asked his mom, "Can I have a power, please?" His mom said, "Well, I don't know if I can give you a power, but let's try something fun." She took Tim to the park to play. At the park, Tim saw a big, red ball. He ran to the ball and kicked it very hard. The ball flew high up into the sky, and it didn't come back down. Tim was so surprised! He looked at his mom and said, "Mom, did you see that? I think I have a power now!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you have the power to kick the ball very high. You are strong and fast, and that's your special power." Tim was so happy. He played with the ball all day and showed his friends his new power. They all had fun, and Tim felt very special with his own power.
