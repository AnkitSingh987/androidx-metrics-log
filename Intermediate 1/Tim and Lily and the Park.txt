Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and Lily and the Park

Tim and Lily are playing in the park. They see a big red truck with a loud siren. It is a fireman truck. They run to see it. "Hello, fireman!" Tim says. "Can we see your truck?" The fireman smiles and nods. He is patient and kind. He lets Tim and Lily sit in the front seat. He shows them the buttons and the horn. He speaks to them about his job. "I help people when there is a fire," he says. "I use water and a hose to put out the fire. I wear a helmet and a coat to be safe." "Wow, that is cool!" Lily says. "You are brave and strong!" The fireman thanks them and gives them stickers. He tells them to be careful and not to play with fire. He says he has to go now. There is another fire to stop. "Bye, fireman!" Tim and Lily say. "Thank you for showing us your truck!" They wave to the fireman as he drives away. They are happy and excited. They pretend to be firemen and save the day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He wore his red hat and shoes. He took big steps to go fast. He liked to walk in the sun. Tim saw a big frog with red lips. The frog said, "Hi Tim, I can help you." Tim was surprised. He did not know frogs could talk. Tim asked, "What can you do?" The frog said, "I can jump very high. Step back and see." Tim took a step back. The frog jumped high into the sky. The red hat on Tim's head fell off. The frog caught it with his red lip. Tim was very happy. He and the frog played all day. They had so much fun together.

Once there was a boy named Tim. He loved to taste new things. One day, Tim saw a big cake on the table. He wanted to taste it, but he was too small. He felt helpless. Tim had an idea. He put on many pants to make him look bigger. He went to the table and tried to taste the cake. But he still could not reach it. Tim was sad. Then, a big bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird picked up the cake and gave it to Tim. Tim tasted the cake and it was yummy. The bird and Tim became friends and shared the cake. Tim was happy and not helpless anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat with a big sail. The boat was very old, or what we call ancient. It lived by the water, waiting for someone to sail it. One day, a small boy came to the water. He saw the boat and wanted to sail it. But the boat was too big for the little boy to move. The boy tried and tried, but he could not make the boat go. This made the boy very sad. The boy started to weep. He cried so much that his tears filled the water around the boat. The water got higher and higher, and soon it lifted the boat up. The ancient boat with the big sail floated on the water, and the boy was happy. He could now sail the boat, and he sailed it all day long. The boy and the boat became good friends, and they had many fun days together.

Once upon a time there was a smart monkey called Bobby. He had a special crystal that he always used to measure things. Whenever Bobby was out and about, he would always use his crystal to measure objects and see how long they were. One day, Bobby was out walking when he came across a river. He wanted to measure the water and so he used his crystal. But just as he was measuring, his crystal fell right into the water where it got swept away. Bobby was very sad and started to cry. A nearby wise owl spotted Bobby and flew down to him. The owl said, “Why are you crying?” Bobby explained what had happened and the owl said, “It is ok to cry when something bad happens. But remember, don’t dwell on it. You can always try to find a way to fix it.” Bobby understood the owl's words and then went off to find his crystal. He eventually found it and thanked the owl. Experiencing the loss of the crystal taught Bobby to be more careful with his belongings. The End
