Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Woods

Once upon a time, there was a little, adventurous cat. This cat loved to play and explore. One day, the cat went on a big adventure in the woods. In the woods, the cat saw a gray bunny. The bunny was hiding, but then it appeared! The cat and the bunny became friends. They played together all day. At the end of the day, the adventurous cat and the gray bunny went back to their homes. They were happy and tired. The cat and the bunny promised to play again soon. And they lived happily ever after.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was a daughter who loved to play and explore. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Lily was very happy. At the park, Lily saw a small bug on a leaf. She wanted to examine it closely. She looked at the bug with her big eyes and saw that it had many legs. Lily was not scared, but she felt a little ashamed because she didn't know what kind of bug it was. Lily asked her mom and dad about the bug. They told her it was a caterpillar. Lily felt better and was not ashamed anymore. She learned something new that day. From then on, Lily loved to examine all the bugs she found in the park. And she was never ashamed to ask questions again.

---

One day, a busy bird found a pretty feather. The bird wanted the feather but it was stuck in a tree. The bird tried to get the feather, but it was too high up. The bird felt sad. A big cat saw the bird and wanted to help. The cat said, "I can help you get the feather, please let me try." The bird was scared of the cat, but the cat looked nice. The bird said, "Please be careful." The cat climbed the tree and got the feather. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, cat!" The cat and the bird became friends. They played with the feather and had a fun day.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with his toys. So, he had to pack them in a bag. He took his ball, his car, and his teddy bear. Tim was very happy to play outside. While Tim was playing, he saw a bug. The bug was huge! It was as big as his teddy bear. Tim was scared at first, but then he saw that the bug was nice. The bug wanted to play with Tim too. The huge bug could do fun things. It could roll the ball with its legs. It could ride on the car. And it could even hug the teddy bear. Tim and the bug became friends. They played together all day. And that day, Tim learned that even big bugs can be nice friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse. He was a proud mouse who liked to help his friends. One day, he saw a big box. His friend, the cat, needed the box moved. The mouse wanted to lift the box for his friend. He tried very hard, but the box was too heavy. The mouse did not give up. He asked his other friends, the bird and the dog, to help him. Together, they all helped to lift the box. The cat was so happy and said, "Thank you!" The mouse was very proud that he could help his friend. They all played together and had a fun day.

---

John was at the park today. He was very excited! He saw the wide swings and knew what he wanted to do. He said to his mom, "Please can I swing now?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, of course you can John!" John was so happy and ran to the swing. He jumped on it and began to swing back and forth. He held on tight as the swing swayed wide. John was having so much fun! Suddenly, he heard his mom's voice. "John! Time to go now please." John was disappointed, but he slid off the swing and said goodbye to the park. He knew he would be back soon to play again. John and his mom left the park together, holding hands and smiling. John had had a fun day and knew he'd want to come back soon!
