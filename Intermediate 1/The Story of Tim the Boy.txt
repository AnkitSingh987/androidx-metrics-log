Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a normal boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a big ball that had a lot of weight. He wanted to play with it, but it was too heavy for him to lift. Tim had an idea. He would use his chair to seat on the ball. He pushed the chair close to the ball and tried to sit on it. But the ball was still too heavy, and it did not move. Tim did not give up. He asked his mom for help. She showed him how to roll the ball with his hands. Tim was very happy. Now he could play with the heavy ball and have fun. And from that day on, Tim and his mom played with the big ball together.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot lived in a cheerful house. Spot loved to play in his yard, but there was a fence all around it. Spot wanted to play with his friend, a big dog named Buddy, who lived next door. One day, Spot saw a hole under the fence. He thought, "I can go through the hole to play with Buddy!" So, Spot hurried and squeezed through the hole. But when he got to the other side, he saw a big cat. The cat hissed at Spot and chased him. Spot was scared, so he hurried back through the hole and into his yard. He was safe now. Spot learned that sometimes it's better to stay in his own yard. And from that day on, Spot and Buddy would play near the fence, happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the soil. He would dig and make mud pies all day long. One day, Tim saw a pretty red hat hanging on a tree. He wanted the hat, but he was too short to reach it. Tim felt ashamed that he could not get the hat. He wanted to show his friends the pretty hat. Tim thought hard and came up with a plan. He would use the soil to make a big hill so he could reach the hat. Tim worked all day to make the hill. He was very tired, but he did not give up. At last, the hill was big enough for Tim to reach the hat. He climbed up the hill and took the hat from the tree. Tim felt so happy and proud. He could not wait to show his friends the pretty red hat.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Amy. She wanted to buy a new kitchen. One day, she saw a black kitchen in a shop window. "Wow!" Amy said. "It's so beautiful!" So she went inside the shop, but the shopkeeper said the kitchen was too expensive. He said it was too much money for Amy to buy. Amy was very sad, so she started to cry. Suddenly, she heard a voice. "Why is she so sad?" Amy looked up and saw a big friendly dog. "I can't buy the kitchen," She said. The dog said "I can help you. I've found a piece of paper with money on it. You can use it to buy the kitchen!" Amy was very excited, she took the money and bought the black kitchen. She was so happy! The next day, Amy was playing with the dog in the new kitchen. She could not believe it was hers. She thanked the dog for his help. Amy and the dog were best friends from then on.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful desert, there was a little cactus named Catty. Catty loved to follow the sun as it moved across the sky. Every day, she would watch the sun come up and follow it until it went down. One day, Catty saw a little bird hopping near her. "Hello, bird," said Catty. "Do you like to follow the sun too?" "Yes, I do," said the bird. "I follow the sun to find food and water. But sometimes it's hard to find water in the desert." Catty thought about this and had an idea. "I have water inside me," she said. "If you help me follow the sun better, I can share my water with you." The bird agreed, and they became friends. The bird helped Catty follow the sun by showing her the best spots to catch the sunlight. In return, Catty shared her water with the bird. They both were very happy and lived peacefully in the desert together.
