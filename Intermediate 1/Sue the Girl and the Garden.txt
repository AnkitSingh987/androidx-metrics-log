Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Garden

One day, a little girl named Sue was very enthusiastic. She wanted to buy a big strawberry. She had some money to pay for it. So, she went to the store with her mom. At the store, she found a very big strawberry. But, when she tried to pay, she did not have enough money. Sue was sad. Her mom said they could come back another day. The next day, Sue found a small strawberry in her garden. It was a surprise! She did not need to pay for it. She was very happy and shared the strawberry with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a secret box. She loved her secret box very much. In the box, Sue would include a lot of her favorite things. She had a pretty red ball, a small toy car, and a big, soft bear. One day, Sue's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom saw the secret box and wanted to see inside. But Sue said, "No, it's not fair to look at my secret things." Tom was sad, but he understood. Then, something unexpected happened. Sue's big, soft bear started to talk! The bear said, "I think it's okay to share your secret box with Tom. Friends should share." Sue was surprised but listened to the bear. She opened the box and showed Tom all her favorite things. They played together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Sue and Tom became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was an old man. He went to the garden every day to look at the pretty roses. There were all different colors, but the old man's favorite rose was the long, red one. One day, the old man said to himself, "I should bring the beautiful rose inside". So he carefully plucked it from the bush and brought it indoors. When he showed it to his young neighbor, the little girl's eyes lit up with joy. She said to him, "Oh, can I have it? Please!" The old man smiled, "Yes, sure you can! I will order you this beautiful rose as a gift." The little girl hugged the old man and said, "Thank you! I love it!" The old man and the little girl smiled at each other, both happy with their gift.

Once upon a time, there was a little red bird named Tim. Tim loved to fly and sing. He had many friends in the forest. One day, while flying, Tim saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad and crying. Tim flew down to Sue and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sue looked at Tim and said, "I lost my way home. Can you help me?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I promise to help you find your home." Tim and Sue walked through the forest, looking for her home. They asked a big tree, "Do you know where Sue's home is?" The tree did not answer. They asked a small flower, "Do you know where Sue's home is?" The flower did not answer. Finally, they saw a little dog. They asked the dog, "Do you know where Sue's home is?" The dog barked and wagged its tail. It knew the way! Tim and Sue followed the dog, and soon they found Sue's home. Sue hugged Tim and said, "Thank you for keeping your promise." Tim smiled and flew back to his friends in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a popular port, there lived a happy boat. The boat loved to sail on the water and play with the fish. The boat had many friends at the port, like the big ship and the small canoe. One day, the boat saw a piece of wood that was rotting. The boat said to the wood, "Why are you so sad and rotting?" The wood replied, "I used to be a part of a big ship, but now I am old and not needed anymore." The boat felt sad for the wood and wanted to help. So, the boat talked to the big ship and the small canoe. They all agreed to make the wood a part of their group. The wood was happy to have new friends and stopped rotting. They all played together in the popular port, and they were the best of friends.
