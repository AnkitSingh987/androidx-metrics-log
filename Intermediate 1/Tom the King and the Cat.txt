Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the King and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a king named Tom. He liked to travel very much. One day, he wanted to go to a far away place. So, he got on his slow horse and started his trip. As King Tom went on his journey, he met a small cat. The cat said, "Hello, King Tom! I want to travel with you!" King Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can come with me, little cat." They traveled together for a long time. One day, they found a big box. King Tom opened the box, and guess what? The small cat turned into a big lion! The lion said, "Thank you, King Tom, for helping me. I was a lion under a spell, and you set me free!" King Tom was very happy, and they became good friends. They traveled together and had fun every day.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garage. They had many toys and games there, but their favorite was the old car. The car was red and shiny, but it did not work. Lily and Ben pretended to drive it to faraway places, like the zoo or the moon. One day, their dad came to the garage with a big truck. He said he had to take the car away, because it was broken and old. Lily and Ben were sad and angry. They did not want to lose their car. They begged their dad to let them keep it, but he said no. "Please, dad, please," Lily said. "The car is so charming. It has a nice smile and a soft horn. It is our friend." "Sorry, kids," dad said. "The car has to go. It is taking up too much space and it is not safe. Maybe we can find you another toy to play with." Lily and Ben did not listen. They ran to the car and hugged it. They said goodbye and thanked it for all the fun times. They cried and cried. Dad felt sorry for them. He knew they loved the car, but he also knew it was time to end it. He lifted them up and carried them to the house. He gave them a hug and a kiss and a cookie. He said he had a surprise for them. He took them to the backyard, where there was a big box with a bow. He opened it and showed them what was inside. It was a new bike, with two seats and two wheels. It was blue and shiny, and it had a bell and a basket. "Wow!" Lily and Ben said. "A bike!" They forgot about the car and ran to the bike. They wanted to try it right away. They put on their helmets and climbed on the bike. Dad helped them pedal and steer. They laughed and cheered. They rode the bike around the yard, feeling the wind and the sun. They pretended to go to faraway places, like the park or the stars. They had a lot of fun. They decided they liked the bike. It was not the same as the car, but it was also charming. It had a nice ring and a big smile. It was their new friend.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Ally. Ally was always asking lots of questions. One day Ally asked her Mommy if she could have a novel. Mommy said yes and gave Ally a shiny new book. Ally was so happy with her new novel that she gave her Mommy a big hug. Ally raced outside with her novel to find a nice spot to read it. She came across a big, hot rock. Ally decided to sit on the hot rock and started reading her novel. She got so lost in the story that she stayed there for hours. Finally, when it was dark outside, Ally asked her Mommy for a glass of water. With her novel in one hand and water in the other, Ally headed back to her room to read some more. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to swing at the park. One day, Tim and his mom went to the airport. They had to fly to see grandma. At the airport, Tim felt restless. He wanted to play, but there were no swings. Tim's mom had an idea. She held his hands and swung him back and forth. Tim laughed and felt happy. After some time, it was time to go on the big plane. Tim and his mom sat down and looked out the window. They saw the airport get smaller and smaller. Tim couldn't wait to swing at grandma's house.
