Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Amy. She had a toy that could change shape. One day, Amy saw a sad boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy. Amy wanted to help Tim feel better. Amy had an idea. She took her toy and made it change into a new toy for Tim. The new toy was only an inch tall, but it made Tim very happy. They played with the toy together and became friends. The moral of the story is that sharing can make others happy and change their day for the better.

Once there was a calm cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to follow his friend, a little girl named Lily. They liked to play together in the house. One day, Lily wanted to make a cake. She took a big pan from the kitchen. Fluffy followed Lily and watched her put the cake mix into the pan. "Fluffy, do you want to help me?" asked Lily. Fluffy nodded and helped Lily mix the cake. After they made the cake, Lily and Fluffy sat down to eat it. They talked and laughed together. Fluffy was happy he could help Lily. They had a fun day and enjoyed the yummy cake.

Once upon a time, there was a humble plane. It had a small bell on it that would ring when it was time to fly. The plane loved to fly high in the sky and see the world below. One day, the plane met a bird named Bob. The plane said, "Hi, I am a plane. I like to fly and ring my bell." The bird said, "Hi, I am Bob. I like to fly too. Can I fly with you?" The plane and Bob flew together, with the bell ringing happily. They saw big trees, little houses, and many animals playing. They had so much fun flying and being friends. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Annie. She was only three years old and loved to explore the world around her. One day, Annie decided to go for a walk in the park. On the way, she saw a big chair on the grass. She was very excited and decided to go and sit on it. Just as she was about to sit down, a big dog appeared. Annie became very worried and hid behind the chair. The dog walked closer to Annie and said, "Do not be scared, my name is Spot. I'm sorry if I scared you. Would you like to play?" Annie was still a bit scared, but she replied, "Okay." She smiled and started to make a seat for Spot out of the grass. Spot seemed happy and thanked Annie for making him a seat. Annie was no longer worried, and the two of them spent the rest of the afternoon playing in the park.

Lily likes to play with her pink watch. It has a big face and a strap that fits her wrist. She likes to look at the numbers and the hands that move. She pretends she is a teacher and tells her dolls what time it is. One day, she goes to the park with her mom and dad. She sees a boy on the swing. He has a blue watch that looks like hers. She wants to see it better. She runs to him and says, "Hi, I'm Lily. Can I see your watch?" The boy is nice. He says, "Hi, I'm Max. Sure, you can see my watch." He takes it off and passes it to her. She passes her pink watch to him. They look at each other's watches and smile. They have the same numbers and the same hands, but different colors. They decide to play together. They take turns on the swing, the slide, and the seesaw. They pass their watches back and forth and tell each other what time it is. They have fun and make a new friend. When it is time to go home, they hug and say goodbye. They promise to bring their watches again next time. Lily is happy. She likes her pink watch and her new friend. She thanks her mom and dad for taking her to the park. She can't wait to see Max again.
