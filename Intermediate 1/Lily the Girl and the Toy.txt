Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toy

One day, a little girl named Lily and her mom went to the shop. The shop was very pretty and had many nice things. Lily saw a big, attractive toy that she wanted to get. Lily asked her mom if she could get the toy. Her mom said they needed to discuss it first. They talked about if the toy was good for Lily and if they had enough money to buy it. In the end, Lily's mom said yes, and they bought the attractive toy from the shop. Lily was very happy and played with her new toy all day.

Jenny and her dad were playing in the park. Dad said, "Let's travel to the ocean". Jenny was very excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" They took the bus to the beach and they could see the large ocean in front of them. They looked around and saw a lot of things. Jenny saw a giant faucet and she ran over to check it out. "What is this Daddy?" she asked. Dad laughed and said, "This is a faucet. It's really big and it helps us get water from the ground." Jenny said, "Wow! That's amazing!" They stayed at the beach for the rest of the day and had a lot of fun. They watched the waves and built sand castles. They even found some seashells to take home. At the end of the day, the travel back home. Jenny was so happy and said, "Can we come back again soon Daddy?" Dad smiled and said, "Of course we can, I would love to." That was the best day ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the mail with his mom. It was a peaceful day. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Tim saw a big melon in the store. He asked his mom, "Can we buy the melon, please?" His mom said, "Yes, we can buy the melon." Tim was very happy. They bought the melon and went back home. Tim wanted to eat the melon right away. But when they cut the melon open, something unexpected happened. Inside the melon, they found a small, friendly bird. The bird said, "Thank you for letting me out of the melon!" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they were happy to help the bird. They all became good friends and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a big head and liked to play in the mud. One day, Tom's mom told him, "Tom, promise me you will stay clean today." Tom said, "I promise, Mom." Tom went outside to play with his friend, Sam. They saw a big mud puddle and wanted to jump in. But Tom remembered his promise to stay clean. He said, "Sam, I can't jump in the mud. I promised my mom I would stay clean today." Sam said, "Okay, Tom. We can play ball instead." As they played ball, the ball went into the mud puddle. Sam went to get the ball but slipped and fell into the mud. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the mud all over Tom. Now, Tom's big head was covered in mud too. When Tom went home, his mom saw his dirty head and was sad. Tom broke his promise and he felt bad.

Lisa loves strawberries. She likes to eat them with milk and sugar. She likes to pick them from the garden. She likes to smell them and feel them. One day, Lisa sees a big basket of strawberries on the table. She wants to eat them all. She does not want to share with her brother Tom. She takes the basket and runs to her room. Tom sees Lisa and follows her. He knocks on her door and says, "Lisa, can I have some strawberries, please? I like them too." Lisa says, "No, Tom, these are mine. Go away!" Tom says, "That is not nice, Lisa. We should share. Mom and Dad taught us to share." Lisa says, "I don't care. I want them all. You can't have any." Tom says, "Please, Lisa, just one. I am hungry." Lisa says, "No, no, no!" She gets angry and throws a strawberry at Tom. The strawberry hits Tom in the eye. It hurts. Tom cries. Lisa feels bad. She sees Tom crying. She sees the strawberry on the floor. She sees the juice on Tom's face. She says, "I'm sorry, Tom. I was selfish. I should have shared with you. Are you okay?" Tom says, "It's okay, Lisa. I forgive you. But don't throw strawberries. They are for eating, not for throwing." Lisa says, "You are right, Tom. They are for eating. And they are better when we eat them together. Do you want some?" Tom says, "Yes, please. Thank you, Lisa." Lisa and Tom hug. They take the basket and go to the kitchen. They wash the strawberries and put them in a bowl. They add some milk and sugar. They sit on the couch and eat the strawberries. They are delicious. They are happy. They are comfortable. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. Don't be selfish. Don't throw food. Be kind to your brother. Enjoy the strawberries.
