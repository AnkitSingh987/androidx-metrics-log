Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Park

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park with her mom. Mia saw a small, harmless pony. She wanted to play with it. Mia's mom said, "Go ahead, Mia. You can play with the pony." Mia went to the pony and said, "Hi, pony! Do you want to be my friend?" The pony nodded its head. Mia was happy. She encouraged the pony to run and jump. They played together all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The pony began to grow bigger and bigger! Mia was surprised. The pony was now a big, strong horse. Mia's mom came over and said, "Wow, Mia! Your love and encouragement helped the pony grow into a beautiful horse!" Mia was very proud of her new friend, and they continued to play happily in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was a very persistent dog. He loved to play and do tasks for his owner, Tim. One day, Tim told Max that he would receive a special treat if he could find a big stick in the park. Max and Tim went to the park to find the big stick. Max looked and looked, but he could not find it. Tim told Max to keep trying because he knew Max could do it. Max was excited and he wanted to find the stick so he could get the special treat. Finally, after looking everywhere, Max found the big stick. He was so happy! Tim was proud of Max for being so persistent. Just like Tim said, Max received the special treat for completing the task. They went home and Max enjoyed his treat, knowing that being persistent always pays off.

One day, a thin cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big cake on a table. Tom wanted to taste the cake. He asked a dog named Max, "Can I taste the cake?" Max said, "No, you can't taste it. It is for my friend's birthday." Tom was sad, but he still wanted to taste the cake. So, Tom made a plan to get the cake. He asked Max again, "Please, can I taste the cake?" Max said, "No, I told you, it's not for you." Tom was very sad but he did not give up. He thought of a new way to get the cake. Tom saw a little girl with a ball. He asked her, "Can you help me get the cake?" The girl said, "No, it's not nice to take things that are not yours." Tom was sad but he knew she was right. In the end, Tom did not taste the cake. He learned that it is important to listen to others and not take things that are not his.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. He was known for his wisdom and always helped the little animals when they had problems. All the animals loved Mr. Hoot and would come to him whenever they needed advice. One sunny day, two little squirrels, named Fluffy and Nutty, had a big fight. They both found a big, shiny nut and couldn't decide who should have it. Fluffy said it was his, but Nutty disagreed. They argued and argued, and finally, they decided to go to Mr. Hoot for help. Mr. Hoot listened to both Fluffy and Nutty and then told them a story about two friends who fought over a toy. In the end, they broke the toy and both were sad. Mr. Hoot said that the best thing to do was to share the big, shiny nut. Fluffy and Nutty agreed with Mr. Hoot's wisdom and decided to share the nut. They thanked Mr. Hoot and went back to playing happily together.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a big red car. Tim thought the car was very cool. He wanted to start the car and go for a ride. He asked his mom, "Can I start the car, please?" His mom said, "Okay, but be careful." Tim was very happy. He went to the car and started it. The car made a loud noise. Tim was having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The car stopped. It needed gas. Tim was sad. He didn't know what to do. He saw a deep hole in the ground. He looked inside and saw a big can of gas! Tim was surprised and happy again. He took the gas and filled the car. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Wow, Tim! You found gas in the deep hole! Good job!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" Tim and his mom went for a fun ride in the big red car.
