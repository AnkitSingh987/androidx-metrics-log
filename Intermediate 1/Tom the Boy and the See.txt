Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the See

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a happy boy, but he was troubled because he could not see well. His eyes were not like other kids' eyes. He wanted to play with his friends, but sometimes, he could not see the ball. One day, Tom's mom took him to a special doctor. The doctor looked at Tom's eyes and said, "I can help you see better, Tom." The doctor gave Tom a pair of glasses to put on his eyes. Tom was so happy, he could not wait to try them. When Tom put the glasses on his eyes, he could see everything! He was not troubled anymore. He went outside to play with his friends. They all played ball, and Tom could see the ball now. Tom and his friends had a lot of fun, and Tom was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny insect named Izzy. Izzy lived in a big tree with her friends. They liked to play and have fun all day long. One day, Izzy and her friends wanted to make a big party. They needed food to supply the party. But, they didn't have enough food for everyone. So, Izzy and her friends had to find more food. Izzy asked her friend, "Where can we find more food?" Her friend said, "We can go to the flower field. There is a lot of food there!" So, Izzy and her friends went to the flower field. But, there was a big bird who didn't want to share the food. Izzy and her friends were sad. They needed to find a way to get the food without making the bird angry. Izzy had an idea. She said, "Let's make the bird laugh with a funny dance!" So, Izzy and her friends did a funny dance. The bird laughed and laughed. He was so happy that he let Izzy and her friends take some food. In the end, Izzy and her friends had a big party with enough food for everyone. They were all very happy and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a pale little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, Tim would wake up early to see the sunrise. He loved to watch the sky change colors as the sun came up. One morning, Tim woke up and saw that the sky was still dark. He thought the sun was still sleeping. "Why is the sky still dark?" he asked his mom. His mom sighed and said, "The sun is hiding today, Tim. It will come up soon." Tim waited and waited, but the sun did not come up. He was sad and missed the pretty sunrise. So, he decided to sing a song to make the sun happy. As he sang, the sky began to change colors. The sun finally came up, and Tim was happy again. He knew that his song had helped the sun wake up, and he promised to sing to the sun every morning.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play hockey. He was very good at it. Tom was also very adventurous. He liked to try new things and go to new places. One day, Tom went to play hockey at the park. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. When he got to the top, he found a magic stick. Tom could not believe it! He took the stick and went back down. Tom used the magic stick to play hockey. It made him play even better! But then, something unexpected happened. The magic stick turned into a bird! The bird said, "Thank you for helping me, Tom. I was stuck as a stick, but your adventurous spirit set me free!" And they became the best of friends.

Lily liked to lend her toys to her friends. She had many toys, like dolls, cars, books and puzzles. She liked to share and play with others. That made her popular in her class. Everyone wanted to be her friend. One day, a new boy came to her class. His name was Ben. He looked sad and shy. He did not have any toys or friends. Lily saw him sitting alone in a corner. She felt sorry for him. She wanted to make him happy. She went to him and said, "Hi, I am Lily. Do you want to play with me? You can lend my toys if you like." She showed him her toys and smiled. Ben looked at her and smiled back. He said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very kind. I would like to play with you." They played together for a long time. They had fun with the dolls, the cars, the books and the puzzles. They laughed and talked. They became good friends. Lily was happy. She had helped Ben feel welcome. She had lent him her toys and her smile.
