Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy scissors that he loved to play with. He would tell his friends about his toy scissors all the time. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, saw the toy scissors. Sam felt envious because he didn't have a toy like that. Sam wanted to play with the toy scissors, too. Tim saw Sam looking at the scissors and felt happy. Tim told Sam, "We can play together with my toy scissors." Sam felt happy, too. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends and shared their toys with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Tilly. She was three years old and loved to explore the world around her. One day, Tilly went to the park and saw a tall building with a big door. She looked up at it and wondered what was inside. She decided to go and take a peek. When Tilly opened the door, she saw a judge sitting at a big desk. She felt a little shy, but the judge smiled at her and asked what she wanted. Tilly said, “I just wondered what’s inside this big building.” The judge laughed and said, “I’m the judge, and I make decisions about who wins or loses.” Tilly thought about this for a moment. She wondered why it all sounded so dull. The judge said, “It is dull, but my job is very important. I make sure everyone gets a fair chance.” Tilly smiled and thanked the judge. Then, she went off to explore the rest of the park.

Anna liked to play with her furry doll on the couch. She gave her doll a name, Lily, and pretended that Lily was her best friend. They talked and laughed and hugged on the couch. One day, Anna's brother Tom came into the living room. He saw Anna and Lily on the couch and said, "Move over, I want to watch TV." He grabbed the remote and turned on the TV. Anna was angry. She said, "No, Tom, this is my couch and my doll. Go away, you are mean." She held Lily close to her and glared at Tom. Tom was angry too. He said, "No, Anna, this is our couch and our TV. You are silly, dolls are for babies." He tried to push Anna and Lily off the couch. Anna and Tom started to fight. They yelled and pulled and kicked on the couch. Lily fell on the floor and Anna cried. Tom laughed and said, "Ha, ha, I win." Then, Mom came into the living room. She saw Anna and Tom fighting and Lily on the floor. She was not happy. She said, "Stop, stop, what are you doing? You are both naughty. You need to share the couch and the TV and be nice to each other. And you need to pick up Lily and say sorry to Anna." Anna and Tom stopped fighting. They looked at Mom and felt sorry. They said, "Sorry, Mom." They picked up Lily and said sorry to Anna. They hugged and said sorry to Tom. They decided to share the couch and the TV and play with Lily together. Mom smiled and said, "That's better. Now you are good." She kissed them and said, "I love you." Anna and Tom said, "We love you too, Mom." They all sat on the couch and watched TV. Lily was happy too. She liked to be with Anna and Tom and Mom. They were her family.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there were many kids playing. They were running, jumping, and laughing. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. One day, a new family came to the park. They were from an unknown place. The family had a big bag with them. They sat down on the grass and started to unpack the bag. Inside the bag, there were toys, food, and a ball. The kids in the park saw the new family and their toys. They came to say "hi" and play with them. They all played with the ball and shared the food. They had a lot of fun together. The new family became friends with the kids in the park. And from that day on, they all played together in the big park, under the bright sun.
