Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bald Man and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bald man named Tom. He had a big secret that no one knew about. Tom was very good at guessing things. He could guess what his friends were thinking, and he could guess what was inside a box without looking. One day, Tom's friends wanted to play a game with him. They wanted to see if he could guess the secret inside a big box. Tom was excited to play the game. He closed his eyes and tried to guess what was in the box. He thought very hard. Tom guessed that there was a big, scary monster inside the box. His friends opened the box, and he was right! The big, scary monster jumped out of the box and scared everyone. Tom and his friends were very sad and scared. They didn't want to play the guessing game anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max liked to dig and bury things in his yard. One day, Max found a soap in the house. Max took the soap outside and buried it in the yard. He was so happy with his new treasure. But then, Max got very dirty from all the digging. His fur was full of mud and dirt. Max's owner saw how dirty he was and decided it was time for a bath. Max didn't like baths, but he knew he had to get clean. When his owner couldn't find the soap, Max remembered where he buried it. He went outside, dug up the soap, and brought it back to his owner. Max learned that it's important to share and help others, even if it means giving up something you like.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They saw many colorful things at the park. There were pretty flowers, bright green trees, and a big blue sky. Amy loved to look at all the colors. Amy's mom said, "Look, Amy! Tonight there will be fireworks in the sky!" Amy was very excited. She had never seen fireworks before. She asked her mom, "What are fireworks like?" Her mom smiled and said, "They are big, colorful, and make loud sounds. You will love them!" That night, they went back to the park to watch the fireworks. The sky was dark, but soon it was filled with colorful lights. The fireworks went "boom" and "bang" in the sky. Amy laughed and clapped her hands. She loved the beautiful fireworks.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. Tim felt lucky because it was a sunny day. He wanted to have fun with his big red ball. He kicked the ball high into the sky. Tim laughed as he watched the ball go up and up. A big dog saw Tim's ball and wanted to play too. The dog ran and kicked the ball with its paw. The ball went even higher! Tim was surprised, but he was happy to have a new friend to play with. The ball came down and landed in a tree. Tim and the dog looked at the ball, but they could not reach it. They were sad. Then, a nice man came and saw the ball in the tree. He was tall and could reach the ball. The man took the ball out of the tree and gave it back to Tim. Tim and the dog were very happy. They played with the ball all day, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there was a little oak tree. The oak tree had many friends, like birds and squirrels. They all played together every day. The oak tree was very happy. One sunny day, a small bird was flying around the oak tree. The bird saw a big, scary shadow on the ground. The small bird began to panic. "Help! Help!" the bird cried out. The oak tree heard the bird and wanted to help. The oak tree called out to the bird, "Don't panic, little bird! I am here to help you!" The small bird flew to the oak tree and hid in its leaves. The shadow went away, and the bird was safe. The bird thanked the oak tree, and they continued to play together. The oak tree and the small bird were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
