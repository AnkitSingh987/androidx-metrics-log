Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Sue and the Toys

One day, Tom and Sue went to play in the park. They loved to run and jump. They saw a big box. It was a mystery. Tom was jealous because Sue saw the box first. They wanted to know what was inside the box. Sue pointed at the box and said, "Let's open it!" They opened the box and saw many toys. They were so happy. They played with the toys all day long. They shared the toys and were not jealous anymore. But then, something unexpected happened! The toys started to move by themselves. The toys were alive! Tom and Sue were so surprised. The toys became their new friends. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train loved to laugh and play with his friends. One day, the train saw an impressive, big track. It was very long and went up and down. The train wanted to go on the track and have fun. The little train told his friends about the big track. They all wanted to go too. They knew it would be a fun day. The little train felt happy but also a bit scared. He knew he had to be brave to go on the big track. The day came, and the little train and his friends went on the impressive track. They went up and down, laughing and having fun. The little train was brave, and he was so happy he tried the big track. In the end, they all had a great day and laughed a lot.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red oven. The oven had a friend named Bell. Bell could ring very loud. They lived in a kitchen, and they were very happy. One day, Bell saw the oven make a cake. Bell felt jealous. Bell said, "I want to make a cake too!" The oven said, "But Bell, you can't make a cake. You can only ring." Bell was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A cat came into the kitchen and tried to take the cake. Bell rang very loud. The cat got scared and ran away. The oven said, "Thank you, Bell! You saved the cake!" Bell was happy again. They both knew that they were special in their own way.

Lily liked to play in the garden. She liked to look for bugs and flowers. One day, she saw a frog. It was green and wet. It jumped on a leaf. Lily wanted to touch the frog. She was careful. She did not want to hurt it. She moved her hand slowly. The frog looked at her. It opened its mouth. "Ribbit," the frog said. Lily was surprised. She smiled. She thought the frog was talking to her. "Hello, frog," she said. "Do you want to be my friend?" The frog blinked. It said, "Ribbit, ribbit." Lily believed the frog said yes. She was happy. She picked up the frog gently. She held it in her hand. "Let's go to my house," she said. "I will show you my toys. We can play together." The frog nodded. It said, "Ribbit, ribbit." Lily and the frog went to her house. They had fun. They played with dolls and cars and blocks. They shared a snack. They laughed and sang. Lily and the frog were friends. They liked each other. They were careful and kind. They believed in magic.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They lived in a pretty house. They had a lot of toys and food. They were very wealthy. One day, the cat and the dog had a fight. They were not happy. They wanted the same toy. The cat said, "This is mine!" The dog said, "No, this is mine!" The cat and the dog stopped fighting. They wanted to become friends again. They shared the toy and played together. They were happy and smiled. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very adventurous. He liked to play outside and run around the big yard. One day, Max saw a window in his house. The window was open. Max wanted to see what was outside the window. Max jumped up and looked through the window. He saw a big tree and lots of birds. Max was so excited! He wanted to go outside and play with the birds. But, Max did not know how to open the door. Then, Max had an idea. He would lock the window and find another way out. Max locked the window and went to find his friend, a little girl named Lily. Lily knew how to open doors. Max barked and showed Lily the locked window. Lily was surprised. She did not know Max could lock windows. Lily opened the door for Max, and they both went outside to play with the birds. But when they got outside, they saw that the birds were actually big, colorful balloons! Max and Lily laughed and played with the balloons all day long.
