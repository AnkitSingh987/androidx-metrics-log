Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Toys

Sara was happy. She was going to visit her brother Tom. Tom lived in a big house with a big garden. Sara liked to play with Tom and his toys. She packed her bag with her doll and her book. She got in the car with Mom and Dad. They drove for a long time. Sara looked out the window and saw the sun go down. The sky became dark. Sara felt a little scared. She hugged her doll. "Are we there yet?" she asked. "Soon, sweetie," Mom said. "Don't worry, Tom is waiting for us. He has a surprise for you." Sara wondered what the surprise was. She hoped it was something nice. She liked surprises. They arrived at Tom's house. It was very dark. Sara could not see the garden or the toys. She saw a light in the window. She heard a noise. "BOO!" Tom shouted. He jumped out of the door. He wore a mask and a cape. He looked like a monster. Sara screamed. She dropped her doll and her book. She ran back to the car. She was very scared. "Tom, that's not nice!" Mom said. "You scared your sister. Say sorry." Tom took off his mask and his cape. He looked sorry. He did not mean to scare Sara. He wanted to make her laugh. "I'm sorry, Sara," he said. "It was a joke. I wanted to surprise you. I have a present for you. It's a teddy bear. Do you want to see it?" Sara stopped crying. She looked at Tom. She saw his face and his smile. She knew he was her brother. She knew he loved her. She felt a little better. She got out of the car. She picked up her doll and her book. She walked to Tom. She gave him a hug. "I'm sorry, too," she said. "I was scared. But I'm not scared anymore. Thank you for the present. I like teddy bears." Tom smiled. He gave her the teddy bear. He took her hand. He led her to the house. "Come on, Sara," he said. "Let's go inside. It's not dark in there. It's warm and cozy. We can play with our toys and read our books. We can have fun." Sara nodded. She followed Tom. She held her teddy bear. She felt happy. She was glad to visit her brother.

It was a brilliant day. The sun shone in a bright blue sky. Jimmy was walking in the park with his cane. He was looking for a friend to play with. Suddenly, he heard someone laughing. He looked around and saw a girl with a big block of ice in her hands. Jimmy thought it looked very interesting. The girl started to melt the ice in her hands with the warmth of the sun. But then the girl started running and Jimmy followed because he wanted to see what was so funny. But when Jimmy tried to catch up with her she ran faster. Jimmy was very confused. So he stopped and looked around. He saw that the girl had made a lake of melted ice and that she was running around it, laughing and jumping. Jimmy was filled with joy. He started running around the lake too, laughing with the girl. The girl was brilliant! She had created a magical scene in the park. Jimmy was so happy to run around the melted ice with the girl. It was a brilliant moment that he would never forget.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was learning how to use the toilet. But Lily was ashamed. She did not want her mommy and daddy to see her go to the toilet. One day, Lily's mommy told her a story about a brave princess. The princess was also learning how to use the toilet. The princess felt ashamed too. But the princess's mommy told her that everyone needs to go to the toilet, and it is okay. Lily listened to the story and decided to be brave like the princess. She went to the toilet and was not ashamed anymore. Lily's mommy and daddy were very proud of her. They told her that it is good to yield to new things and not be ashamed. And Lily learned that it is okay to be brave and try new things.
