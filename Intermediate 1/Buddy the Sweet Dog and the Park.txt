Category: Intermediate
Title: Buddy the Sweet Dog and the Park

One day, a sweet dog named Buddy put on a suit. He looked very nice in his suit. Buddy wanted to show his friends, so he went out of his house to find them. Buddy started to wander around. He looked for his friends near and far. He looked in the park and by the big tree. But he could not find them. Buddy felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. Buddy saw a big group of dogs wearing suits too! They were all happy and playing together. Buddy joined them and made new friends. And they all had fun in their suits.

One day, a curious little dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big box with a zipper on it. Max wanted to see what was inside the box. He pulled the zipper with his teeth and opened the box. Inside the box, Max found many toys. There were balls, dolls, and cars. Max thought it would be fun to organize the toys. He put all the balls in one corner, the dolls in another corner, and the cars in the last corner. Max was very happy with his work. He played with the toys and had a lot of fun. His friends came over and they all played together. They thanked Max for organizing the toys and making it easy for them to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a red bicycle. She loved to ride her bicycle every day. One day, Sue's bicycle had a flat tire. She was sad. A kind man named Tom saw Sue and wanted to help. Tom was very patient. He showed Sue how to fix the flat tire on her bicycle. Sue was so happy to have her bicycle fixed. She thanked Tom for his help. Now, Sue and Tom ride their bicycles together and have lots of fun. They always remember to be patient and help each other when they need it. And they lived happily ever after.

---

One day, a boy named Tim found a light hoop in his toy box. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. He ran to his friend, Sue, and said, "Look at my light hoop! Let's play with it!" Sue was not happy. She wanted to play with her ball, not the hoop. "No, I don't want to play with your hoop. I want to play with my ball," she said. Tim was sad, but he still wanted to play with Sue. Tim had an idea. "Let's play a game with both the hoop and the ball!" Sue thought about it and said, "Okay, let's try it." They played a game where they threw the ball through the hoop. They both had lots of fun and were happy to play together.

Timmy loves to imagine that he is a musician. He likes to play with his toy guitar and sing songs that he makes up. He pretends that he is on a big stage and that many people are clapping for him. One day, he sees a fat man playing a real guitar on the street. The man has long hair and a big beard. He plays loud and fast music that makes Timmy want to dance. Timmy goes closer to the man and watches him with wonder. "Hello, little boy," the man says to Timmy. "Do you like my music?" "Yes, I do!" Timmy says. "You are very good. I want to be a musician like you when I grow up." The man smiles and says, "That's great. Music is a wonderful thing. It can make you happy and express your feelings. Do you have a guitar?" Timmy nods and shows him his toy guitar. The man says, "That's a nice guitar. Can you play me a song?" Timmy feels shy, but he wants to impress the man. He strums his toy guitar and sings a song that he learned from his mom. The song is about a rainbow and a butterfly. The man listens and claps his hands. "That was beautiful," the man says. "You have a lovely voice and a good ear. You are a natural musician. Keep practicing and you will be amazing." Timmy feels proud and happy. He thanks the man and gives him a hug. The man gives him a coin and says, "This is for your music. You made my day brighter. Thank you." Timmy runs back to his mom, who was waiting for him nearby. He shows her the coin and tells her about the man. His mom says, "That's wonderful, Timmy. I'm glad you met a nice musician. Maybe you can play with him again someday." Timmy says, "Yes, maybe. But first, I want to go home and play more music. I love to imagine that I am a musician."
