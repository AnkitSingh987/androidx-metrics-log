Title: Tom and the Bathrobe

One day, Tom got a new bathrobe. It was very soft and warm. He loved it so much that he wanted to wear it all the time. He wore it to the park, to the store, and even to his friend's house. Some people laughed at him, but Tom did not care. He was happy in his bathrobe. At the park, Tom saw his friend, Jack. Jack said, "Why are you wearing a bathrobe? It's not bedtime!" Tom felt embarrassed, but he didn't want to take off his bathrobe. He said, "I like my bathrobe. It is soft and warm." Jack just shook his head and walked away. Tom wanted to succeed in making his bathrobe cool. He tried to do cool things like climb trees and ride his bike. But the bathrobe got in the way. It got stuck in the bike chain, and Tom fell off. He got hurt and cried. His bathrobe was dirty and torn. Tom went home, sad that his bathrobe was ruined and his day was bad.

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a reef. The reef was home to many fish. One fish named Bob was a very nice fish. He had many friends. Bob liked to play in the warm sea. One day, Bob heard about a faraway place with icy water. He wanted to see it. His friend Tim said, "Bob, be careful. Icy water is very cold!" Bob said, "I know, but I want to see it." Bob swam far away to find the icy water. When he got there, he felt the cold. He remembered what Tim said. But Bob was brave. He swam fast in the icy water and saw many new things. Then, he went back to the warm reef. He told Tim all about it. They were happy to be together again.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in the park. He saw his friend, Sam, fall down and start to cry. Tim ran over to Sam and asked, "Are you okay?" Sam looked at his knee and saw a small cut. "I want a bandage," Sam said, feeling a bit guilty for crying. Tim wanted to help, so he went to his mom and asked for a bandage. Tim's mom gave him a bandage, and he ran back to Sam. Tim put the bandage on Sam's knee. Sam smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim!" They both felt happy and went back to playing in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her red glove. One day, she got very dirty while playing. Her mom said, "Lucy, it's time to bathe." Lucy was scared of the water. She didn't want to bathe. But her mom told her, "Don't be scared, Lucy. I will be right here with you." So, Lucy got into the water with her red glove. As Lucy started to bathe, she saw that the water was turning red. She was surprised! Then, she looked at her red glove and saw that the color was coming off. Lucy laughed and showed her mom. Her mom laughed too and said, "See, Lucy? There's nothing to be scared of." The moral of the story is: don't be scared to try new things, because sometimes they can be fun and have surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Jack. He was only three years old. One day, Jack was sitting on his bed when he noticed that the mattress was rocking. He thought it was funny and wanted to join in, so he started rocking the mattress himself. He rocked back and forth, faster and faster. He had so much fun! Suddenly, the mattress suddenly stopped rocking. Jack looked around and saw his dad. His dad had been rocking the mattress while Jack was asleep. “Why were you rocking my mattress, Dad?” asked Jack. Dad smiled and said, “I wanted to rock you to sleep. I knew you had a long day, and I wanted you to have a good night's rest.” Jack smiled and said, “Thank you, Dad. That was really nice of you.”
