Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Pirate and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a pretty pirate named Lucy. She had a big hat and a red boat. Lucy loved to sail on the water and look for treasure. One day, Lucy saw a small island. She thought there might be treasure there. So, she said "please" to her boat and it sailed to the island. When she got there, she found a big box. Lucy opened the box and found many pretty things inside. There were shiny coins, colorful gems, and a soft, fluffy hat. Lucy was so happy! She said "thank you" to the island and sailed back home with her new treasures.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to do fun things. He played with his toys and ran outside with his friends. Tim was a happy boy. One day, it was Wednesday. Tim woke up and saw something incredible outside his window. It was a big, colorful rainbow! Tim was so excited. He called his friends to come and see the incredible rainbow. Tim and his friends played under the rainbow all day. They laughed and had so much fun. They knew that this was a special Wednesday. When the sun went down, the rainbow went away. But Tim and his friends would always remember the incredible rainbow they saw on that Wednesday.

Once there was a little girl named Marie. She was always very fair and was nice to everyone. One day she wanted to watch a special show on the TV. Her mum said that was ok, but first she had to do her chores. So, Marie put away her toys and tidied her room. Then she put on her special show, she was so excited. After the show she realised she hadn't been fair to her mum. She really should have done her chores first and then watched the TV. From that day on, Marie always put her chores first and was fair to her mum.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. They bought a cheap toy tank for Tim to play with. Tim was very happy and could not wait to play with his new toy. When they got home, Tim took his toy tank outside to play. He saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb it with his toy tank. Tim held the tank tightly and started to climb the tree. As Tim climbed higher, something unexpected happened. A real tank appeared next to the tree! The real tank was friendly and wanted to play with Tim and his toy tank. Tim was very surprised and climbed down from the tree. He played with the real tank and his toy tank all day long.

Tom and Anna liked to play by the sea. They liked to watch the big boats and the small fish. They liked to feel the sand and the water. They liked to hear the waves and the seagulls. One day, they saw something new. It was a big metal thing. It was shiny and heavy. It had a pointy end and a round end. It was an anchor. Tom and Anna did not know what it was. They gazed at it with wonder. "Where did it come from?" Tom asked. "Maybe it fell from a boat," Anna said. "Maybe it is a treasure," Tom said. They decided to touch it. It was cold and hard. They tried to lift it. It was too heavy. They tried to push it. It did not move. They tried to make a sound with it. It did not ring. They got bored of the anchor. It was not fun. It was just an ordinary thing. They left it and ran back to the sea. They played with the boats and the fish. They felt the sand and the water. They heard the waves and the seagulls. They were happy.

One day, two friends, Tim and Tom, went to play in the backyard. They liked to play with their toys and run around. But today, they started to quarrel. Tim wanted to play with the red ball, and Tom wanted to play with the same red ball too. "Give me the ball!" said Tim. "No, I want the ball!" said Tom. As they quarreled, they heard a loud noise. They were frightened. They stopped fighting and looked around. They saw a big, friendly dog come into the backyard. The dog had the blue ball in his mouth. Tim and Tom smiled. They stopped being frightened. They took the blue ball and played with the dog. They did not quarrel anymore. They had fun with their new friend in the backyard.
