Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a man who was very impatient. He had to get ready for a party, but the party decorations were in a big box. He wanted to hurry up and he decided to use a big scissors to cut out the decorations quickly. He took out a shiny ornament and cut it up into small pieces. He was so impatient he didn't even take the time to look at the ornament before cutting it up. Suddenly it made a loud noise and sparks started to fly out of it. He looked down and saw that he had cut the ornament into too many pieces. The man was so sad. He liked the ornament and he knew he couldn't fix it. He sat down and started to cry. Just then his little sister walked in. She looked down at all the pieces of the ornament and saw the man was crying. The little girl was very patient and knew what she had to do. She slowly started putting all the pieces of the ornament back together. With the help of tape, glue, and a lot of patience, she was able to make the ornament look like new. The man was so happy. He said thank you to his little sister and decided to be less impatient next time.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn. In the barn, there lived a reliable dog named Spot. Spot loved to watch over the animals in the barn. One day, while Spot was watching the animals, a little chick got lost. Spot saw the chick and knew he had to help. He looked and looked until he found the chick's mom. Spot brought the little chick back to its mom. The chick and its mom were very happy. From that day on, all the animals knew they could count on Spot to watch over them in the big red barn.

Anna and Ben are playing on the beach. They like to dig in the sand and look for shells. Anna finds a big shell that is shiny and colorful. She thinks it is very attractive. "Look, Ben, look what I found!" she says, holding up the shell. "Wow, that is a nice shell, Anna. Can I see it?" Ben says, reaching for the shell. "No, it is mine. I found it first. You can find your own shell," Anna says, pulling the shell away. Ben feels sad and angry. He wants to see the shell too. He thinks Anna is not being fair. He tries to grab the shell from Anna, but she runs away. She runs along the water, laughing. Ben runs after her. He wants to catch her and get the shell. He runs faster and faster. He does not see where he is going. Suddenly, he steps on something hard and sharp. He feels a pain in his foot. He screams and falls down. He looks at his foot and sees blood. He also sees a crab that is pinching his toe. The crab is angry and scared. It thinks Ben is trying to hurt it. "Ow, ow, ow! Help, help, help!" Ben cries. Anna hears Ben's scream. She stops running and turns around. She sees Ben on the ground, with a crab on his foot. She feels sorry for him. She drops the shell and runs to him. "Ben, are you okay? What happened?" she says, kneeling next to him. "A crab bit me. It hurts. Make it go away," Ben says, sobbing. Anna carefully takes the crab off Ben's toe. She throws it back into the water. She hugs Ben and says, "I'm sorry, Ben. I'm sorry I was mean to you. I should have shared the shell with you. It is not worth hurting you." Ben hugs Anna back and says, "It's okay, Anna. I'm sorry I tried to take the shell from you. It is yours. You can have it." Anna and Ben look at each other and smile. They are friends again. They walk back to the shell and pick it up. They decide to share it. They think it is more attractive when they are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys in her room. One day, she found a big mirror in her room. Lily was very happy to see the mirror. Lily looked at the mirror and saw her face. She made a harsh face and said, "Hello, mirror! I am Lily. Do you want to play with me?" The mirror did not talk, but Lily saw her face change when she talked. Lily played in front of the mirror all day. She danced, she sang, and she made funny faces. She had so much fun with the mirror. At the end of the day, Lily said, "Thank you, mirror, for playing with me. You are my new friend."
