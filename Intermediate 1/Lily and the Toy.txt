Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Toy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys. One day, she felt confused because she could not find her favorite toy. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my toy?" Her mom said, "I don't know, Lily. Let's look for it together." So, they started to look for the toy. They looked in her room, in the kitchen, and even outside. Then, they found the toy under the couch. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Mom!" Now, Lily could play with her toy and not feel confused anymore.

One day, Tom and Sue were playing in the park. They saw a big cookie on the ground. They both wanted the cookie. They began to quarrel about who should get the cookie. "I saw it first!" said Tom. "No, I did!" said Sue. They were both very angry. They did not want to share the cookie. Suddenly, a big dog came running up to them. The dog took the cookie in its mouth and ran away. Tom and Sue stopped quarreling and watched the dog eat their cookie. They both laughed and said, "We should have shared the cookie." Now, Tom and Sue always share and do not quarrel anymore. They learned that sharing is better than being angry.

Once there was a lazy bear who lived in the woods. One day, he was planning a festival. He was thinking of all the fun he could have. He decided to mix things up and make it special. He went out to get some sweets and snacks. At the festival, he met a funny bee. The bee said to the bear, "What are you doing?" The bear replied, "I'm having a festival and I'm mixing things up. Would you like to come?" The bee said, "Yes, I'd love to! Let's have some fun!" The bear and the bee had a great time. They mixed food, played games, and enjoyed each other's company. They both agreed that it was the best festival ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little red jeep. The jeep liked to go fast and play in the mud. One day, the jeep saw a big tree with a swing hanging from it. The jeep thought it would be fun to play on the swing. The jeep tried to get on the swing, but it was too big. A small, harmless bug saw the jeep and wanted to help. The bug had an idea. The bug went to find more friends to help the jeep get on the swing. Many bugs came to help. They all worked together to lift the jeep onto the swing. The jeep was very happy and swung back and forth. But then, something unexpected happened. The swing broke, and the jeep fell into a big, soft pile of leaves. The jeep was not hurt, and everyone laughed. The jeep and the bugs became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to spin around and around. He would spin so fast that he would get dizzy and fall down. Tim thought spinning was so much fun. One day, Tim went to the park to spin. He spun and spun, faster and faster. He was having so much fun that he did not see a big rock in his way. Tim tripped on the rock and fell down. He felt a little embarrassed, but he was okay. Tim's mom came over to help him up. She told him not to worry and that everyone falls down sometimes. Tim smiled and felt better. He got up and started to spin again, but this time, he made sure to watch out for rocks in his way.

Once upon a time, there was a big mall. In the mall, there was a little bug. The bug was very ugly, but it was a nice bug. The bug liked to crawl all around the mall. One day, the ugly bug met a small, cute cat. The cat was lost in the mall. The cat was sad and wanted to go home. The ugly bug wanted to help the cat, so they crawled together to find the way out. As they crawled through the mall, they met many nice people. The people helped the ugly bug and the cat find the way out. At last, they found the door and went outside. The cat was happy to be home and thanked the ugly bug. They became best friends and played together every day.
