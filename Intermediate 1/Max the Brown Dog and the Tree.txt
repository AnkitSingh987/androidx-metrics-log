Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Brown Dog and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. He loved the morning time. Every morning, Max would wake up and go outside to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. One morning, Max and Lily saw a big tree with a swing. Max barked and Lily said, "Do you want to play on the swing, Max?" Max nodded his head and wagged his tail. Lily helped Max onto the swing and gave him a push. Max was so happy, he barked and barked. After playing on the swing, Max and Lily went for a walk. They saw a pretty butterfly and chased it together. They played all morning until they were tired. Max and Lily sat under the big tree, smiling and laughing. They loved spending their mornings together.

Sam and Mia are twins. They like to play soccer with their dad. Sometimes they go to the big pitch near their house. The pitch has green grass and white lines. It also has two goals with nets. One day, Sam and Mia are very excited. Their dad tells them they are going to watch a real soccer game at the pitch. They will see big players and a lot of fans. Their dad says they have to behave well and listen to him. He also says they have to wear red shirts, because that is the color of their team. Sam and Mia put on their red shirts and follow their dad to the pitch. They see a lot of people wearing red or blue shirts. They hear a lot of noise and music. They find their seats and sit down. Their dad points to the players on the field. He says they are very good and fast. He says they have to cheer for them and hope they are successful. The game starts and Sam and Mia watch with big eyes. They see the players kick the ball, run, pass, and shoot. They see the ball go into the net or miss. They see the fans clap, shout, or boo. They see the referee blow his whistle and show cards. They see the score change on the board. Sam and Mia are very happy when their team scores a goal. They jump up and down and scream. Their dad hugs them and smiles. He says they are doing great and he is proud of them. He says they are behaving very well and he loves them. Sam and Mia hug him back and say they love him too. They say they like soccer and they want to be successful players too. Their dad says they can be anything they want if they work hard and have fun. He says they are his stars.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob loved to swim in the deep blue sea. He had many friends who would swim with him. One day, Bob saw a huge rock in the water. He thought it would be fun to carry the rock to show his friends. So, Bob started to carry the huge rock with his strong fins. As he swam with the rock, it became heavier and heavier. Bob was getting tired, but he did not want to give up. Suddenly, a big wave came and pushed Bob and the rock to the shore. Bob was stuck on the sand and could not swim back to the sea. His friends saw him and tried to help, but they could not carry the heavy rock. Bob was sad and could not swim in the sea with his friends again.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a small box. The box was wrapped with pretty paper. Amy was excited to see what was inside the box. She opened the box and found a little toy. The toy was an original toy, not like any other toy she had seen before. It was a tiny doll that could dance on her thumb. Amy played with the doll every day. She would put the doll on her thumb and they would dance together. They had lots of fun and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there were two furry friends - a dog and a cat. They lived together in the same house and were very good friends. One day, the dog and the cat were playing in the living room when the dog jumped up on the sofa to have a look at the ceiling. The cat was jealous and he said to the dog, "That's my spot!" The dog growled, "No, it isn't! I found it first!" The two friends began to quarrel. They started hissing and barking and they wouldn't stop. The quarreling went on for so long that the ceiling started to shake. Suddenly a unique little bird popped out from a tiny hole in the ceiling. It was so surprised to see the furry friends arguing! The dog and the cat stopped quarreling and looked up at the little bird. They were all friends now and the room was filled with peace and joy.
