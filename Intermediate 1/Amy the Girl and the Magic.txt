Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy she loved very much. One day, her toy got hurt. Amy was very sad. Amy's big brother, Tim, wanted to help. He tried to fix the hurt toy, but he made a mistake. Tim accidentally destroyed the toy. Amy cried and cried. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to move on its own! It was not destroyed at all. It was a magic toy! Amy and Tim were so happy, and they played with the magic toy all day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Rosie. Rosie had a blue box, and she loved it very much. Every day she took it out and played with it. One day Rosie was feeling very sad, because she didn't have as many friends as she wanted. She cried, and hugged her box tightly. Then, something magical happened. Her box opened and out flew a a little bird. It flew around the room and sang a wonderful song. When it finished singing, it hopped to the windowsill and spoke to Rosie. "You don't need lots of friends, Rosie," it said. "Just be kind and caring to the friends you do have and you will feel very happy." Rosie was amazed and smiled. She took care of her friends and was soon very happy. The little bird reminded Rosie that it is not the number of friends you have that matters, but how you treat them. She never forgot the moral lesson the bird had taught her, and the blue box remained a special thing in her heart.

One day, a charming little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim loved to play and run around. He also loved to watch the big birds in the sky. Tim's mom sat on a bench and watched him play. As Tim played, he saw a big aeroplane in the sky. It was flying very high. Tim was so excited, he ran to his mom and said, "Mom, look! A big aeroplane!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. That is a big aeroplane. It is going far away." Tim wanted to gain more knowledge about aeroplanes. He asked his mom, "Mom, can I learn more about aeroplanes?" His mom said, "Of course, Tim. We can go to the library and read books about aeroplanes." So, Tim and his mom went to the library and read many books about aeroplanes. Tim learned a lot and was very happy. He knew that one day, he would fly in a big aeroplane too.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was glad because he got a new toy car. The toy car could go with speed. He played with it every day. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his toy car to play. While he was playing, he saw a big tree. He thought it would be fun to hide his toy car in the tree. Tim put his toy car in the tree and went to play with his friends. When he came back, his toy car was gone! He looked and looked, but he could not find it. He was very sad. Then, he saw a squirrel. The squirrel had his toy car! The squirrel ran up the tree and locked the toy car in its nest. Tim tried to get it back, but the squirrel was too fast. Tim cried and went home without his toy car. From that day on, Tim never saw his toy car again. He was sad, but he learned to be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw a little red ball. He was glad to see the ball and wanted to play with it. Max picked up the ball with his mouth and started to lead his friends to the park. On the way, they saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his ball. Max went to Tim and said, "Is this your ball?" Tim was so happy to see his ball and said, "Yes, thank you, Max!" But then, a big wind came and blew the ball far away. Max and his friends ran fast to get the ball back. They found the ball near a big tree. The tree had a shield on it. The shield was magic! When Max touched the shield with the ball, the ball became bigger and bigger. Now, Max, Tim, and their friends could play with the big ball together. They were all very glad and had lots of fun.
