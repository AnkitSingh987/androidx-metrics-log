Category: Intermediate
Title: The Healthy Bird Was Flying and the Pebble

One day, a healthy bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a pebble on the ground. The bird wanted to play with the pebble. So, it flew down to pick it up. The bird held the pebble in its beak and started to fly up again. But the pebble was heavy. The bird started to fall. It was scared. Just then, a big wind came. It pushed the bird back up. The bird was happy. It did not fall. The bird and the wind became friends. They played together with the pebble.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a fun park with his mom and dad. There were many games to play. Tim saw a game where he could count balls. If he counted right, he would get a prize. Tim started to count the balls. One, two, three, four, five. He counted five balls! The man at the game gave Tim a prize. It was a big, cold ice cream. Tim was so happy. Tim, his mom, and his dad sat down to eat their cold ice cream. They talked about the fun games they played that day. Tim loved his prize and had a great day at the fun park.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Kitty. She liked to wear a pretty skirt. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. Kitty saw her friend, Doggy, and said, "Doggy, do you like my skirt?" Doggy nodded and said, "Yes, Kitty, your skirt is pretty!" Kitty felt happy. Later, Kitty and Doggy played with a ball. They had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, they said goodbye and went back to their houses. And they both had a good night's sleep.

Lily and Tom were playing in the garden. They liked to pretend they were explorers, looking for new lands and animals. They had a map, a hat, a binocular and a backpack. "Look, Tom, I see something shiny over there!" Lily said, pointing to a distant hill. "Maybe it's a treasure!" "Let's go and find out!" Tom said, excited. They followed the map and walked towards the hill. They had to cross a stream, a bridge and a field. When they reached the hill, they saw a big house with a fence. It looked old and quiet. They heard a voice from inside. "Who's there? Come in, come in!" the voice said. It was a friendly voice, like a grandma. Lily and Tom were curious. They opened the gate and walked to the door. They knocked and a lady opened it. She had white hair and a smile. "Hello, dear children. I'm Mrs. Rose. What are you doing here?" she asked. "We're explorers, looking for treasure!" Lily said. "Well, you're in luck. I have something for you. Come in, come in!" Mrs. Rose said. She led them to a cozy room with a fireplace and a table. On the table, there was a pot of tea, some cups, some cookies and a big book. "This is my treasure. It's a book of stories. I wrote them myself. Would you like to hear some?" Mrs. Rose asked. "Yes, please!" Lily and Tom said. They sat down and Mrs. Rose poured them some tea. She opened the book and started to read. She read stories about dragons, fairies, pirates and princesses. Lily and Tom listened with wonder. They felt warm and happy. They gained a new friend and a new adventure. They thanked Mrs. Rose and promised to visit her again. They went back to their garden, holding the book and the cookies. They had a lot to tell their mom and dad. They had a lot to dream about.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Jack. He was only three years old and was very sleepy. He loved playing with wooden boards. One day he told his mum that he wanted to make something special with his board. So his mum helped him to find all the tools he needed. Next, he found some glue and some paint and asked his mum to help. She assisted him and together they began to make something special with the board. Jack painted it with stars and moons and when he finished he was very happy. On the following day, Jack woke up feeling a bit sleepy but decided to finish the board. He worked very hard and put decorations all over. Then he put some figures of animals and the board was finally finished. Jack was very proud of his special board and showed it off to his friends. Everyone said it was very cool and had lots of fun playing with it. Jack was so happy that he had managed to finish the board.
