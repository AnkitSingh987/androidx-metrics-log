Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Frog

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a small girl named Lily. Lily liked to play in the woods near her house. One day, she found a small axe under a tree. She picked it up and said, "I will take this home." As Lily walked home, she heard a ring. She looked around and saw a green frog. The frog said, "Hello, can you help me? I am lost." Lily smiled and said, "I can help you. My house is close." Lily and the frog walked to her house. They played with the axe, making small cuts in the wood. The frog was happy and said, "Thank you, Lily. I had fun today." Lily said, "You're welcome. Come back anytime." The frog hopped away, and they both lived happily ever after.

Sara and Tom were playing with their blocks. They liked to build towers and houses and cars. But they did not like to share. Sara wanted all the blue blocks and Tom wanted all the red blocks. They started to fight and pull and push. They knocked down their blocks and made a big mess. "Stop it! Stop it!" their mom said. "You are breaking the law of the house. The law is to be nice and share with your brother and sister. If you don't, you will have to go to time out." Sara and Tom did not want to go to time out. They looked at their mom and then at their blocks. They felt sorry for fighting. They wanted to restore their blocks and their fun. "Sorry, mom," Sara said. "Sorry, Tom." "Sorry, mom," Tom said. "Sorry, Sara." They hugged and said they were sorry. Then they started to pick up their blocks and put them back in the box. They decided to share and mix the colors. They built a bright rainbow tower together. They smiled and laughed. Their mom was happy to see them playing nicely. She praised them and gave them a hug. She said, "You did a good job. You learned the law of the house. The law is to be nice and share with your brother and sister. That is how you restore peace and happiness."

Sara and Ben like to play in the wood behind their house. They imagine they are brave explorers, looking for treasure and animals. One day, they find a big gray rock with a hole in it. "Look, Ben, maybe there is something inside!" Sara says. "Maybe it is a cave for a bear or a fox!" Ben says. They go closer to the rock and peek inside the hole. They see something shiny and round. "What is that?" Sara asks. "I don't know, let's take it out!" Ben says. They reach inside the hole and pull out a metal coin. It has a picture of a man with a hat and a beard on one side, and some words on the other side. "Wow, this is a treasure!" Sara says. "Maybe it is from a long time ago, when the wood was different!" Ben says. They are very happy and excited. They imagine they are rich and famous, and that they have found a secret from the past. They put the coin in their pocket and run back to their house to show their mom and dad. They can't wait to tell them their story.

Once upon a time, there was a fair town. In this town, there was a man who would deliver things. One day, he had to deliver a big box. Inside the box, there was a skeleton. The man did not know this. The man was walking with the box when it started to rain. He ran to a big tree to stay dry. Under the tree, there was a cat. The cat was wet and cold. The man felt sad for the cat. The man opened the box to find something for the cat to stay warm. He saw the skeleton and was very scared. Then, the skeleton started to talk! It was a nice skeleton who wanted to help. The skeleton gave the cat a warm hug. The man, the cat, and the skeleton became good friends. They all lived happily in the fair town.
