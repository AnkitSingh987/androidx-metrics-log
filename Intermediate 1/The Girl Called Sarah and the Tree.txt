Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Sarah and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Sarah. She was feeling lonely. When she went outside to play, she saw a hand. It was a gentle hand and it asked her, "Would you like to come with me?" Sarah nodded, but she was a bit scared. The hand took Sally to a warm and sunny place. She could see a big tree and a beach. The hand said to Sarah, "Let's relax here together." Sarah said yes and lay down on the sand. She closed her eyes and relaxed. When Sarah opened her eyes, she saw the hand again. The hand said, "You were so relaxed. It was nice to see you happy." Sarah smiled and said, "Yes, it was. I'm no longer lonely. Thank you." The hand smiled back and said, "You're welcome."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to read. Every day, he would sit under a big tree and read his books. He was very eager to learn new things. One day, Tim saw a race happening in the park. The kids were running very fast. Tim wanted to join in the race too. He asked his friend, Sam, "Can I be in the race?" Sam said, "Yes, but you must be fast." Tim was not very fast, but he was eager to try. The race started, and all the kids ran as fast as they could. Tim was running, but he was not as fast as the others. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the kids' hats away. The kids stopped running to get their hats, but Tim kept going. He won the race! All the kids were happy for Tim. They learned that being eager and not giving up can help you win, even when you are not the fastest.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in the town. It was icy and cold outside. The billboard had a picture of a lost dog on it. The dog was sad and needed to find his way home. A little girl named Lily saw the billboard. She wanted to help the lost dog. Lily put on her coat and went outside to search for the dog. She looked under cars and behind trees, but she could not find the dog. Lily searched and searched until it was very dark. She was very cold and tired. She could not find the dog and had to go home. Lily was sad because she could not help the lost dog. The dog stayed lost and the story had a bad ending.

Anna was a child who liked to play with dolls. She had many dolls, but her favorite was Lily. Lily had long hair, a pink dress and a fragile necklace. Anna always talked to Lily and believed that Lily was her best friend. One day, Anna's brother Tom came into her room. He was bored and wanted to play with Anna. He saw Lily on the bed and grabbed her. "What a silly doll!" he said. "She can't talk or move. She's just a toy." Anna was angry and scared. She ran to Tom and tried to take Lily back. "No, Tom! Leave Lily alone! She's not a toy, she's my friend! She can talk to me and I can talk to her. I believe in her!" Tom laughed and pulled Lily away from Anna. He swung her around and threw her on the floor. The fragile necklace broke and the beads scattered everywhere. Lily's dress ripped and her hair tangled. Anna cried and ran to Lily. She hugged her and whispered, "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry." Tom felt bad when he saw Anna cry. He realized he had been mean and hurtful. He picked up the beads and the dress and went to Anna. "I'm sorry, Anna. I didn't mean to break your doll. I was just playing. Can I help you fix her?" Anna looked at Tom and saw that he was sorry. She wiped her tears and nodded. "Okay, Tom. You can help me. But you have to be gentle with Lily. She's fragile and she's my friend. And you have to believe in her, too."
