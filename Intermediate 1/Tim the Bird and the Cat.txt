Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree near a pretty river. Every day, Tim would fly around and play with his friends. They loved to sing and dance under the warm sun. One day, Tim saw a big cat trying to catch his friend, Sally the squirrel. Tim knew it was important to help Sally escape from the cat. So, Tim and his friends made a plan. They would work together to save Sally. Tim and his friends flew around the cat, making loud noises to scare it away. The cat got scared and ran away. Sally was able to escape and join her friends. They all danced under the sun, happy that they had saved their friend. They knew that working together was very important.

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a big lion. The lion was very strong and liked to roar. Every day, he would go to the woods and roar as loud as he could. All the animals in the woods would run and hide when they heard his big roar. One day, the lion found an axe on the ground. He did not know what it was, but he thought it looked fun. He picked up the axe with his strong paws and started to play with it. He swung the axe and hit a tree. The tree shook and many leaves fell down. The lion was happy with his new toy. He roared and played with the axe all day long. The animals in the woods were scared, but they saw that the lion was just busy playing. They came out of their hiding spots and watched the lion play. They all laughed and played together in the busy little town.

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. On the mountain, there lived a small, confused bird. The bird did not know how to fly. All the other birds could fly, but not this one. One day, the bird saw a big tree. It wanted to go to the top of the tree, but it could not fly. The bird tried and tried, but it remained on the ground. It felt sad and alone. A kind squirrel saw the bird and wanted to help. The squirrel told the bird to jump from branch to branch. The bird tried and soon, it was at the top of the tree. The bird was so happy and thanked the squirrel. From that day on, the bird and the squirrel were best friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was in her room. She liked to stare at the pretty carpet on the floor. It had many colors and shapes. Lucy loved to play on it with her toys. But one day, the sun was very harsh. It was so hot outside! Lucy's carpet became very warm too. She was sad because she couldn't play on it. So, Lucy went to her mom for help. Her mom had an idea. She opened the window and let the cool air in. The harsh sun went away and the carpet became cool again. Lucy was happy and played on her carpet with her toys all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a regular house with their dog. They liked to take care of their dog and play with him every day. One day, the man asked his wife, "Do you want to go to the park with our dog?" The wife said, "Yes, let's go!" So, they put on their shoes and went to the park with their dog. At the park, the man, his wife, and their dog played with a ball. They had a lot of fun. Then, they sat down to eat some food. They took care of each other and shared their food. After they finished eating, they went back home, happy and tired from their fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Her mum gave her a special present which was in a black box. When she opened it, she saw a smooth, shiny toy inside. It was a toy car! Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mum!" Mum replied, "You're welcome! Now, be careful with it!" Lily soon began to play with her new toy car. She pushed and pulled it, and it made beeping noises. She was having so much fun! Suddenly, the toy car broke. Lily was really sad. She looked up at her mum and said, "My toy car broke!" Mum said, "It's ok, sweetheart. We can fix it!" So, Mum and Lily worked together to repair the toy car. When it was all done, it was smooth and perfect again. Now, Lily could have lots of fun with her toy car.
