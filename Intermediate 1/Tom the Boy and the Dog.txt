Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Dog

One day, a boy named Tom went to play outside. He saw his mom doing laundry. "Mom, can I help?" he asked. "Promise to be careful?" she said. Tom promised. Tom and his mom were hanging the laundry when they heard a distant sound. It was a big dog barking! Tom was scared. "Don't worry," said his mom, "the dog is far away." But then, the big dog came running into their yard! Tom and his mom ran inside the house. The dog played with the laundry and made a big mess. Tom and his mom were sad that their laundry was ruined.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily and her deaf dog, Max. Max was a happy dog, but he could not hear when Lily called him. They loved to play together in the backyard. One sunny day, Lily and Max were playing outside. Max was running around and Lily was trying to hold his leg to stop him. "Hold still, Max!" Lily said with a giggle. Max wagged his tail and licked her face. Lily's mom came outside and said, "Lily, time for lunch!" Lily looked at Max and said, "Come on, Max. Let's go eat!" Max followed Lily into the house, wagging his tail. They both enjoyed their lunch and took a nap together. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was cold outside, so he wore a warm scarf. The scarf was red and very long. Tim liked his scarf because it made him feel tough. On his walk, Tim saw a little girl. The girl was cold and did not have a scarf. She looked sad. Tim wanted to help the girl. So, he went to her and ask, "Do you want to share my scarf?" The girl smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Tim and the girl shared the scarf. They both felt warm and tough. They played in the park together and had lots of fun. The girl was happy that Tim shared his scarf with her. From that day on, Tim and the girl were the best of friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They had a lot of fun playing with a big red ball. Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to start going home." Tim was sad, but he knew they had to leave. On the way home, they saw a big bird in a tree. The bird was very incredible. It was blue, green, and yellow. The bird said, "Hello, Tim!" Tim was very surprised. He didn't know birds could talk. The bird told Tim, "I can make you fly if you want." Tim was excited and said, "Yes, please!" The bird touched Tim's head with its wing. Suddenly, Tim started to float in the air. He was flying! Tim's mom couldn't believe it. They all laughed and played in the sky until it was time for Tim to wake up. It was all a fun dream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to eat pizza. One day, her mom made a big, yummy pizza for dinner. Sue was very happy. Sue wanted to help her mom. Her mom asked her to mind the pizza while it cooled down. Sue said, "Yes, mom!" and watched the pizza carefully. She made sure nothing happened to it. While Sue was watching the pizza, her dog, Spot, came running into the kitchen. He had been playing outside and was very dirty. Sue saw Spot coming and knew she had to protect the pizza. She quickly picked up the pizza and put it on a high shelf. Spot couldn't reach it, and the pizza stayed clean. Sue's mom was proud of her for minding the pizza and keeping it safe from the dirty dog.

It was a bright and cheerful day, and Sofie and her mom were out on a walk. Everywhere they went, they saw pretty flowers and trees. Sofie was wearing her favorite skirt, and it twirled around her when she ran around. As they kept walking, they heard a loud sound and saw something very special. It was a group of birds, and they were spreading their wings and flying in the sky. Sofie was so excited she started spinning and twirling around. When they got home, Sofie couldn't stop thinking about the birds she saw. She couldn't wait to go outside and spread her wings like them. Her mom said to go outside and she did. Sofie spread her arms out wide, imagining she was a bird. She spun, twirled and flapped her arms like wings. She felt so free and cheerful, like she was really flying! It was a magical moment, and now whenever Sofie wears her favorite skirt, she remembers the birds and how it felt to spread her wings and fly.
