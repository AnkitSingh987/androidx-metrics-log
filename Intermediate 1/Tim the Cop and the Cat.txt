Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cop and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a cop named Tim. He was a nice cop who liked to help people. Tim was always ready to take care of his town. One day, a cat climbed a tall tree and could not get down. The cat was scared. Tim saw the cat and knew he had to help. He was ready to take action. Tim went to the tree and climbed it. He took the cat in his arms and climbed down. The cat was safe and happy. Tim felt good because he was able to help. Everyone in the town loved Tim, the helpful cop.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a creative guard named Tim. He liked to make fun things from paper and wood. One day, Tim made a little bird from paper. He painted it with many colors. He was very happy with his bird. "Hello, little bird," Tim said to his new friend. "I will call you Rainbow." Rainbow would sit on Tim's shoulder as he did his guard job. They were very good friends. One day, when Tim was not looking, Rainbow flew away. Tim was very sad. He looked for Rainbow everywhere, but he could not find him. He asked his friends, "Have you seen Rainbow?" They all said no. Then, something unexpected happened. The next day, Rainbow returned! But he was not alone. He had brought many other paper birds with him. They were all different colors and sizes. Tim was so happy to see Rainbow and his new friends. "Thank you for coming back, Rainbow," Tim said. "And thank you for bringing your friends too." From that day on, Tim and his paper bird friends guarded the town together. They were the best and most colorful guard team ever.

One day, a big lion was walking in the forest. The lion was strong and dependable. All the animals liked him because he always helped them when they needed it. The lion saw a little bird who couldn't fly. The bird was sad and asked the lion for help. The lion wanted to help the bird, but he didn't know how to make the bird fly. He thought and thought, but he couldn't find a way. Then, a wise old owl came and told the lion to refuse to give up. The lion listened to the owl and kept trying. He found a way to help the bird fly again. The bird was happy, and all the animals knew that the dependable lion was their friend.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Grace and Jack. They were playing in the park when suddenly, the sky grew dark. Grace began to worry because rain was about to start, but then Jack said, "Don't worry. I hope we don't get wet!" Just then, the sky began to brighten again. "Look!" shouted Jack. "It's not raining!" Grace smiled and said, "Let's go to the meeting. It will be fun!" So Jack and Grace went to the meeting. Everyone was happy and smiling. They spent the whole day with their friends. At the end of the day, Grace and Jack waved goodbye and gave each other a big hug before they both walked home, feeling full of hope.

One day, a fat cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big red vehicle. The vehicle was shiny and loud. Tom thought, "I want to ride in the vehicle!" He could only imagine how fun it would be. Tom jumped in the vehicle and started to drive. He went fast and slow, up and down hills. He was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The vehicle stopped moving. Tom was sad and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, a friendly dog named Max came to help. Max knew how to fix the vehicle. Tom was surprised! He thought dogs and cats couldn't be friends. But Max showed him that they could. They fixed the vehicle together and went for a fun ride. Tom and Max became best friends, and they had lots of fun adventures in the big red vehicle.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a doll that she loved very much. The doll had a pretty dress and a big smile. Sue took her doll everywhere she went. One day, Sue was playing with her doll in the park. She heard a loud noise. It was a big, heavy truck driving by. Sue was scared and held her doll tight. The doll made her feel safe. Sue and her doll went home after playing in the park. Sue's mom made her a yummy snack. Sue shared her snack with her doll. They had a fun day together. Sue loved her doll and knew that they would always be best friends.
