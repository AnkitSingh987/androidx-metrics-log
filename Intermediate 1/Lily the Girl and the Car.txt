Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys all day. One day, she lost her favorite toy, a little red car. Lily was very sad and worried. Her parent saw her looking for the car. They wanted to help her. They looked under the bed, behind the couch, and in the toy box. But they could not find the little red car. The parent was worried too. Then, Lily had an idea. She asked her dog, Max, to help find the car. Max sniffed around the house and managed to find the little red car under a big chair. Lily and her parent were so happy. They thanked Max and played with the little red car together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a reliable friend. He liked to play and look at things. One day, Tim found a costume. It was a big, red costume. Tim put on the costume and went outside to play. His friends saw him and laughed. They did not know it was Tim. They thought it was a funny, big, red thing. Tim laughed too. He liked to make his friends laugh. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the costume away. Tim's friends were sad. They thought the funny, big, red thing was gone. But then, they saw it was Tim! They were happy again. They all laughed and played together.

Once upon a time there was a boy who lived in a big house. He liked to explore, so one day he decided to go outside and look for something fun to do. He went to the garden, and there he saw a long rope dangling from a very high tree. He wanted to try it, so he grabbed the rope and climbed up. When he looked around, he saw an apple in the branches above. He picked the apple and then carefully climbed back down. He was so excited to try it that he took a big bite. The apple tasted so sweet and juicy! He wanted to share it with his friends, so he collected somerope, tied it around the apple and threw it down to them. Everyone was so excited to taste it! They all thanked him for the apple, and commended him for being so brave to climb so high. He was happy that he had something to share with his friends.

Once upon a time, two children wanted to go to the park. They spotted a tall rail and were curious. 'Let's go near the rail! It looks so tall!', said one of the children excitedly. 'I want to go closer!', the other one said. So the two children carefully went near it, but suddenly a strange noise was heard. 'I think we should close now', the other one said. But it was too late. The rail shattered and the children got hurt. They began to cry. Their parents saw this and rushed to them. They were very sad and angry. 'It's not clear what happened here. You kids should have closed when you heard the noise', their father said sternly. And so the two kids never went near the rail again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play hide and seek with his friends. One sunny day, they went to the park to play. In the park, Tim saw a ladder. He thought it would be a good place to hide. So, he climbed up the ladder, and found a small tree house. He went inside and hid there. His friends looked for him everywhere, but they could not find him. They called out his name, and Tim heard them. He peeked out of the tree house and called back to them. They saw him, and they all climbed up the ladder to join him. They had a fun day playing together in the tree house.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big, purple ball near the swings. Lily wanted to play with the ball, so she picked it up and started to throw it in the air. "Let's play catch!" said a boy named Tim. He was a new friend from the park. Lily threw the ball to Tim, but the wind blew it away. The wind was harsh that day. They both ran after the ball, trying to catch it. As they chased the ball, Lily said, "I think the wind will blow the ball to the big tree!" They ran to the big tree, and just as Lily said, the purple ball was there. They both laughed and played catch under the tree, happy that they found the ball.
