Category: Intermediate
Title: Mark and the Bird

Once upon a time, a sweet little dog named Mark lived in a small house. Mark loved to play with his ball all day. He liked to make noise by barking and running around the house. One day, Mark heard a noise outside. He went to the door and saw a little bird. "Hello, bird," Mark said. "Do you want to play with me?" The bird looked at Mark and said, "I am too small to play with your ball, but I can sing a sweet song for you." Mark was happy to have a new friend. He listened to the sweet song of the bird and danced around. They played together every day, making noise and having fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. They slide on the slide, swing on the swings, and run on the grass. They have fun. But one day, it rains a lot. The park is wet and muddy. Anna and Ben still want to play, but their moms say no. They say it is too slippery and dangerous. Anna and Ben are sad. They decide to play in Anna's home instead. They go to Anna's home with their moms. They take off their shoes and coats. They go to Anna's room. They have many toys there. They play with dolls, cars, blocks, and books. They have fun. But then, Ben slips on a rug. He falls and bumps his head. He cries. Anna and their moms come to help him. They hug him and give him a band-aid. They say he is brave and strong. Ben stops crying. He smiles. He says to Anna, "Thank you for being my friend. Thank you for letting me play in your home. I am grateful for you." Anna says, "You are welcome. I am grateful for you too. You are my best friend." They hug again. They are happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily was very excited because she loved the park. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lily saw her friend, Max, playing with a ball. Max was good at catching the ball. "Hey, Max! Can you show me how to catch the ball?" Lily asked. Max smiled and said, "Sure, Lily! Just put your hands like this and watch the ball." Max threw the ball to Lily and she caught it. They played catch and had a lot of fun. After playing, they saw some leaves falling from the trees. It was fall and the leaves were very pretty. Lily and Max picked up some leaves and showed them to their moms. Their moms smiled and said, "Good job, kids! You found some beautiful leaves." Lily and Max were happy and proud of their fall leaves.

Once upon a time there was a rabbit who lived in the forest. The rabbit had an important job to do - he had to teach the other animals to rub when they were sad. One day, he tried to do his job with the deer. But the deer just didn't understand – no matter how hard he tried, the rabbit just couldn't get the deer to rub when it was feeling sad. The other animals in the forest were getting worried. What if the deer never learned how to rub? But then, one morning, the rabbit noticed something strange – the deer was rubbing! The rabbit was so happy that the deer had learned to rub! He knew that this meant the deer wouldn't be sad anymore. The moral of the story is that a little bit of teaching can go a long way! With some patience and care, you can help someone understand something they didn't know before - even if it seems impossible at first.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a pink paper on the floor. She picked it up and saw that it had a poem on it. Lily liked the poem because it had pretty words. She wanted to show it to her mom and dad. Lily ran to her mom and dad, holding the pink paper in her hand. But as she ran, her foot slipped on a toy. She fell down, but she did not cry. She got up and looked at the pink paper. The poem was still there, and it looked okay. Lily gave the pink paper to her mom and dad. They read the poem and liked it too. They were happy that Lily found the pretty poem. They put the pink paper on the fridge so everyone could see it. Lily felt proud and smiled.
