Category: Intermediate
Title: The Temple and the Tim

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a big temple. The temple was very pretty. Many people liked to go there. One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Mia went to the temple. They were friends and liked to play together. At the temple, they saw a long and wide path. Tim said to Mia, "Let's race to the end of the path!" Mia agreed, and they both got ready. They were excited to see who was the fastest. Tim and Mia started to race. They ran very fast. Their legs moved quickly, and they both tried their best. Mia was a little bit faster than Tim. She got to the end of the path first. Tim was happy for his friend, even though he did not win. Mia said, "That was fun! You were fast too, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, it was fun. Let's race again someday!" They both laughed and went back to the temple to play more games. They had a great day together at the temple.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. One day, Lucy's mom told them they were going on a vacation. Kitty and Lucy were very happy. Before they left, Lucy made Kitty promise to be good on the trip. Kitty promised, and they went on their way. They saw many fun things on their vacation. They went to the beach, played in the park, and ate yummy ice cream. One day, while they were at the beach, something unexpected happened. Kitty found a magic shell that could talk! The shell told Kitty that it could grant one wish. Kitty, being clever, wished for more yummy ice cream for her and Lucy. The shell granted the wish, and suddenly there was a mountain of ice cream. Lucy and Kitty were so surprised and happy. They shared the ice cream with all their new friends they made on their vacation.

Once there was a little ant named Tim. Tim lived near a big mill. He had many friends. One day, Tim felt ready for a big adventure. He told his friends, "I want to go see the mill!" Tim and his friends walked to the mill. They saw a big door. It was hard to open. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's all pinch the door together!" They pinched the door and it opened. Inside the mill, they saw a big wheel. It was turning fast. Tim and his friends watched it. They were happy they could see the mill. They knew they were brave and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy she loved very much. It was a soft bear. One day, Amy played with her bear too hard and it began to tear. Amy felt sad and helpless. She ran to her parent and said, "Mom, my bear is broken! Can you fix it?" Her mom looked at the torn bear and said, "I will try, but I need some time." While Amy waited, she played with her other toys. But she missed her bear. Then, something unexpected happened. Amy's mom called her and said, "Come here, I have a surprise for you!" Amy ran to her mom, and there was her bear, all fixed. Amy was so happy, she hugged her mom and said, "Thank you, Mom! I love you!" From that day on, Amy learned to be more careful with her toys. And she never forgot the day her mom saved her bear.

Once upon a time, there was a wide river. In the river, there was a little boat. The boat loved to go fast. One day, a big rain came. The river got very full. The boat wanted to go fast, but the rain made it hard. The boat was sad. It did not like to delay. The boat saw a little duck. The duck said, "Don't be sad, little boat. The rain will stop soon." The boat waited and waited. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. The boat went fast again. The boat was happy. The duck said, "See? Good things come to those who wait." The little boat learned to be patient. Now, the boat knows that sometimes it is okay to delay. The boat is happy and enjoys the wide river even more.
