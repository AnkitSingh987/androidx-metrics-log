Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Fancy Dog and the Kite

Once there was a fancy dog named Spot. Spot liked to lead his friends in exercise. They would run, jump, and play together. One day, Spot and his friends went to the park to play. At the park, Spot said, "Let's play a game! I will lead, and you all follow me." His friends were happy and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So, Spot began to run, and his friends followed him. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew a kite into Spot's face! Spot was surprised and stopped running. His friends laughed and said, "Spot, you look funny with a kite on your face!" Spot laughed too and said, "Now, I am a fancy kite dog!" They all laughed and played with the kite for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a bear named Bobo. Bobo loved honey very much. One day, he saw a big bee flying by. Bobo thought that the bee might know where to find honey. Bobo said, "Hi, bee! Can you help me? I want honey. Where can I find some?" The bee said, "Yes, Bobo! Come with me. I will show you where the honey is." Bobo was very happy and followed the bee. They came to a big tree. The bee said, "Look, Bobo! Up there is honey. But be careful, some honey is bad." Bobo climbed the tree and found two honey pots. One was good and sweet, but the other was bad and tasted yucky. Bobo only took the good honey and said, "Thank you, bee!" Then, Bobo and the bee became good friends.

Tom and Lily were playing with blocks. They wanted to make a big house for their toys. Tom had a lot of blocks, but Lily only had a few. Lily asked Tom if she could have some of his blocks. "Please, Tom, can I have some of your blocks? I need more to make my house," Lily said. "No, Lily, these are my blocks. You can't have any. Go find your own," Tom said. He was selfish and did not want to share. Lily was sad and angry. She looked around and saw a nail on the floor. She had an idea. She picked up the nail and went to Tom's house. She used the nail to poke holes in his blocks. She wanted to ruin his house. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom shouted. He saw Lily with the nail and his blocks with holes. He was shocked and mad. "I'm adding holes to your blocks. You are mean and selfish. You don't share with me, so I don't share with you," Lily said. She threw the nail at Tom and ran away. Tom and Lily both cried. They had hurt each other and their house. They were not happy. They learned that being selfish and mean was not fun. They wished they had been nice and shared.

Once upon a time, there was a famous man named Tom. He was very good at fixing things. One day, a little girl named Lily came to Tom with a broken frame. The frame was very special to her. Lily said, "Tom, can you repair my frame, please?" Tom looked at the frame and said, "Yes, I can fix it for you." Tom worked very hard to repair the frame. He put the pieces back together. When Tom was done, he gave the frame back to Lily. She was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom was happy too because he could help his friend. They both smiled and went to play together.

Once upon a time there was a charming little girl named Jeannie. She loved to explore the world around her. One day, she discovered an old mill by the river. She knelt down to take a closer look and noticed a tiny mouse inside. She smiled as she said, “Hello, little mouse! What are you doing in here?” The mouse put its tiny paws together and said, “I am trying to make flour so I can make yummy bread for my family.” Jeannie thought this was a wonderful idea and knelt down to help. Together, they managed to fill the mill with grains from the nearby fields. After a few hours, they had finished and they smiled with pride at the work they had done. Jeannie and the little mouse said their goodbyes, and the mouse ran off to share the flour with its family. Jeannie smiled to herself, charmed by the little mouse’s kindness. She waved goodbye and started walking home.
