Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a sad boy named Tim. He had no friends to play with. One day, he found a little bird in his yard. The bird was hungry, so Tim decided to feed it. He gave the bird some toast from his breakfast. "Thank you, Tim," said the bird. "Now we can be friends." Tim was so happy to have a friend. They played all day long in the sun. The next day, Tim went to find the bird to play again. But the bird was gone. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find his friend. He felt very sad. He sat under a tree and cried, missing his friend the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a colorful scooter that she loved to ride. One day, Lily wanted to ride her scooter to the park. On the way to the park, Lily saw her friend, Tim. Tim said, "Hi Lily! I like your colorful scooter. Can I try?" Lily said, "Yes, you can try my scooter." Tim tried to ride the scooter, but he was scared. Lily wanted to help Tim. Lily said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will encourage you." She held Tim's hand and showed him how to ride the scooter. Tim started to feel brave. He rode the scooter with Lily's help. They both had fun at the park, riding the scooter together.

One day, a soft cat named Kitty and a dog named Spot were playing in the yard. They saw a big seat under a tree. Kitty and Spot wanted to sit on the seat, but there was only room for one. Kitty said, "I will sit on the seat first, then you can sit after me." Spot did not like this idea. He wanted to sit on the seat now. They both tried to sit on the seat at the same time but could not fit. Kitty had an idea. She said, "Let's cut the seat in half, so we both can sit at the same time." They found a big stick and cut the seat into two pieces. Now, Kitty and Spot could both sit on their own part of the seat, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Bob. He liked to order pizza every day. Bob also liked to go to the gym to play and have fun. One day, while Bob was at the gym, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly dog came in! The dog wanted to play with Bob. They played ball and ran around together. Bob and the dog had a great time. After playing, Bob wanted to order pizza like always. But then, he had an idea. Instead of ordering pizza, he decided to share his food with the dog. They both ate and were very happy. From that day on, Bob and the dog became best friends. They went to the gym and shared food every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom cook in the kitchen. One day, Tim's mom was baking a cake. She asked Tim to pass her the cake mix. Tim was happy to help and passed the cake mix to his mom. Tim's mom put the cake in the oven. They waited and waited for the cake to bake. When the timer went off, Tim's mom took the cake out of the oven. But, oh no! The cake looked disgusting! It was all green and lumpy. Tim and his mom were very sad. They thought about what went wrong. Tim remembered that he passed the green mix, not the cake mix. They laughed and decided to try again. This time, they used the right mix. The cake came out of the oven looking perfect and delicious. Tim and his mom were so happy and enjoyed the yummy cake together.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier named Tom. Tom was a delicate man who liked to help people. One day, he saw a little girl named Lily who was sad. "Why are you sad, Lily?" Tom asked. Lily frowned and said, "I lost my toy." Tom wanted to help Lily. They looked for the toy together. They looked under trees and behind rocks. Soon, they found the toy under a bush. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom was happy that he could help. From that day on, Tom and Lily were good friends.
