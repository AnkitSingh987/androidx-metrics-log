Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Bunny and the Mountain

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a small bunny named Ben. Ben was a weak bunny who liked to play and clap all day. One day, Ben saw a big mountain far away. He wanted to go there and play. Ben told his friends about the big mountain. They all wanted to go with him. So, they started to walk to the mountain. On their way, they saw a big tree. Ben and his friends clapped and played around the tree. After a long walk, they finally reached the big mountain. They were all very happy and clapped their hands. They played and had fun on the mountain all day. When it was time to go home, Ben and his friends were not weak anymore. They were strong and brave because they had climbed the big mountain.

Sara liked to play princess. She had a crown, a dress and a wand. She pretended her room was a castle and her toys were her friends. But Sara was lonely. She wanted a real friend to play with. One day, Sara saw a new girl in the park. She had curly hair and a big smile. Sara wanted to say hello, but she was shy. She watched the girl play with a ball and a dog. Sara wished she had a ball and a dog. The next day, Sara brought her crown, her dress and her wand to the park. She hoped the girl would see her and want to play. But the girl did not see her. She was busy with her ball and her dog. Sara felt sad. The third day, Sara dared to do something. She put on her crown, her dress and her wand. She walked up to the girl and said, "Hi, I'm Sara. I like to play princess. Do you want to play with me?" The girl looked at Sara and smiled. She said, "Hi, I'm Lily. I like to play princess too. Do you want to share my ball and my dog?" Sara nodded. She felt happy. Sara and Lily became friends. They played princess, ball and dog. They pretended the park was their kingdom and they had many adventures. Sara was not lonely anymore. She had a real friend to play with.

One day, a big oak tree saw a small door with a lock. The oak tree felt guilty because it did not know who the door belonged to. It wanted to find the owner of the door. The oak tree asked its friend, the bird, "Do you know who this door belongs to?" The bird replied, "I don't know, but we can ask the other animals in the forest." So they went to ask the other animals if they knew who owned the door. As they asked more animals, nobody knew who the door belonged to. This made the oak tree and the bird feel sad. They wanted to find the owner so they could give the door back. Finally, they met a squirrel who said, "I know who the door belongs to! It belongs to the rabbit who lives in the hole under the oak tree." The oak tree and the bird went to the rabbit's hole and gave the door back to the rabbit. The rabbit was very happy and thanked them for finding his door. The oak tree and the bird felt proud and not guilty anymore. They had solved the problem and helped their friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little mouse who loved cheese. One day, he saw a big piece of cheese near his home. The cheese was yellow and round. The mouse was very happy. The little mouse went closer to the cheese. He wanted to take a big bite. But when he touched the cheese, it felt strange. It did not feel like real cheese. The little mouse thought the cheese was fake. Just then, a big cat appeared. The cat had put the fake cheese there to catch the mouse. The mouse was scared, but he had an idea. He took the fake cheese and threw it far away. The cat ran after the fake cheese, and the little mouse ran back to his home, safe and sound.
