Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Slide

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. Yesterday, they saw a big slide. It was wide and long and shiny. "Let's go on the slide!" Lily said. "OK!" Tom said. They ran to the slide and climbed up the steps. Lily went first. She sat on the slide and pushed herself down. She felt the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She heard a whoosh sound as she slid down. "Wow, that was fun!" Lily said. Tom followed her. He sat on the slide and pushed himself down. He felt the wind in his hair and the sun on his face. He heard a whoosh sound as he slid down. "Wow, that was fun!" Tom said. They smiled at each other and ran back to the steps. They wanted to go on the slide again and again. They had a lot of fun on the wide slide yesterday.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little cat named Kitty. She loved to play with her toy sack all day long. One day, she saw an open door and went outside to play. Kitty found a big tree and saw a bird on a branch. She said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to play with my sack?" The bird said, "Yes, Kitty! That sounds like fun!" So, they played together, tossing the sack back and forth. After a while, they were tired and happy. Kitty said, "Thank you, bird, for playing with me. I had a lot of fun!" The bird said, "You're welcome, Kitty. I had fun too!" They both went back to their homes, smiling and looking forward to playing together again.

Once upon a time, on a sunny spring day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. Lily felt proud of herself because she learned how to skip. She skipped all the way to the park. Lily saw her friends playing there, and she was very happy. As Lily skipped, she saw a big, green frog. The frog said, "Hello! I can skip too!" Lily was surprised because she did not know frogs could talk. The frog showed Lily how he could skip. They skipped together and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The frog turned into a little boy! The boy said, "Thank you, Lily! I was a frog because of a magic spell. When we skipped together, the spell went away!" Lily and the boy became good friends, and they skipped together every spring day.

One day, a playful bird named Blue was flying in the sky. He saw a big tree with a nest in it. Blue was happy because he wanted a nest to sleep in at night. He flew down to the nest and said, "Hello! Can I live here too?" A small bird named Red was in the nest. Red said, "Yes, you can live here with me. But, we need to find more sticks to make the nest bigger." Blue and Red went to look for sticks. They did not know that a big wind was coming soon. Blue and Red found many sticks and put them in the nest. They were very tired, so they went to sleep. In the night, the big wind came and blew the nest away. Blue and Red woke up and were scared. But then, something unexpected happened. A big, kind bird named Green saw them and said, "Don't worry, I will help you!" Green helped Blue and Red build a new nest that was even bigger and stronger than before. Blue, Red, and Green lived in the new nest together. They would laugh and play all day long. They knew that they were safe in their strong nest, even if another big wind came.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to dress in her favorite clothes. One day, she put on her big, warm coat because it was very cold outside. She also wore her red hat, gloves, and boots. Lucy was ready to go out and play in the cold. Lucy went outside and saw her friend, Tim. Tim was playing with a big blue ball. The ball was filled with air, like the oxygen we breathe. Lucy and Tim played with the ball, kicking it back and forth. They were having so much fun in the cold air. After a while, Lucy and Tim started to feel cold. They knew it was time to go inside. They said goodbye to each other and went back to their warm homes. Lucy took off her coat, hat, gloves, and boots. She felt warm and cozy again. Lucy was happy she had a fun day playing in the cold with her friend Tim.
