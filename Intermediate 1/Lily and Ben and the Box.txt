Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Box

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. They have chalk. Chalk is fun. They can draw on the sidewalk with chalk. One day, they find a big box of chalk. The box is old. The chalk is old too. Some of the chalk is broken. Some of the chalk is dirty. But Lily and Ben do not care. They are happy. They have many colors of chalk. They start to draw on the sidewalk. They draw flowers and stars and hearts. They draw their names and faces. They draw a big sun and a big moon. They draw a cat and a dog. They draw whatever they want. "Look at my cat!" Lily says. "It is black and white. It has a pink nose and green eyes. It is cute." "Look at my dog!" Ben says. "It is brown and yellow. It has a blue collar and red ears. It is funny." They laugh and draw more. They are having fun. They are using their imagination. They are making art with old chalk.

Once upon a time, there was a kangaroo. The kangaroo was very tired. He wanted to rest. The kangaroo was poor. He did not have a bed. He looked for a place to rest. He found a big tree. The kangaroo lay down under the tree. He closed his eyes. He was happy. The kangaroo slept and had a good rest.

Ben and Lily were playing in the garden with their water guns. They liked to soak each other and laugh. They also liked to hide behind the bushes and surprise each other. But one day, they saw a big red bike near the fence. It had shiny wheels and a long handle. It looked like a lot of fun. Ben and Lily wanted to try it. They ran to the bike and took turns to sit on it. They pretended to ride it and make noises. They did not see the sign that said "Do not touch". A man came out of the house next to the fence. He looked angry. He shouted at Ben and Lily. "Hey, you kids! Get off my bike! That's mine! You are not allowed to touch it!" Ben and Lily were scared. They jumped off the bike and dropped their water guns. They said sorry to the man. They felt guilty. They knew they did something bad. They ran back to their garden and told their mom what happened. Their mom was not happy. She said they should not touch other people's things without asking. She said they should go and apologize to the man again. Ben and Lily nodded. They picked up their water guns and walked to the fence. They saw the man cleaning his bike with a cloth. They said sorry again and gave him their water guns as a gift. The man smiled and thanked them. He said he forgave them and he was sorry for yelling. He said they could play with his bike sometimes if they asked first. Ben and Lily felt better. They made a new friend. They learned a lesson. They went back to their garden and played with their water guns. They did not soak each other too much. They were nice.

One day, a little boy and girl were playing outside on a sunny Sunday. They found a big open box near the park. They were very excited and wanted to see what was inside the box. The little boy thought it was full of toys, but the little girl thought it was full of candy. When they opened the box, they found a small book. The book had the word "value" written on the cover. They did not know what it meant. The little boy was sad because it was not toys, and the little girl was sad because it was not candy. They began to argue about what was better, toys or candy. Just then, a kind old man walked by and saw the children arguing. He asked them why they were upset. The little boy and girl told him about the book and how they wanted toys or candy instead. The old man smiled and said, "This book is very special. It teaches us to value the things we have, like friends and family, more than toys or candy." The little boy and girl listened to the old man and stopped arguing. They decided to keep the book and learn about value together. They played happily for the rest of the sunny Sunday, thankful for their new lesson and for each other.
