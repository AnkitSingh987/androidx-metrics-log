Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Ring

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to pretend and play with her friends. One day, she saw a big bull in the field. The bull looked rich because it had a shiny gold ring in its nose. Lily thought it would be fun to pretend to be rich like the bull. Lily walked up to the bull and said, "Hi, Mr. Bull! Can I pretend to be rich like you?" The bull looked at her and said, "Of course, Lily! But remember, being rich is not the most important thing in life. It is better to be kind and helpful." Lily and the bull played together all day, pretending to be rich. They had so much fun! But then, something unexpected happened. The gold ring fell out of the bull's nose! The bull was not really rich. It was just pretending, like Lily. Lily and the bull laughed and laughed. They realized that they didn't need to be rich to be happy. They just needed to be good friends and have fun together. And from that day on, Lily and the bull were the best of friends, always kind and helpful to each other.

Tim and Jen were friends. They liked to play in the woods. One day, they found a hut. It was old and dark. They wanted to see inside. "Let's go in," Tim said. "No, it's scary," Jen said. "Come on, it's fun," Tim said. He pushed the door. It opened with a creak. Tim and Jen went in. The hut was full of dust and webs. There was a bed, a table, and a chair. On the bed, there was a big blanket. "Look, a blanket," Tim said. He ran to the bed and pulled the blanket. Under the blanket, there was a snake. It was long and green. It had red eyes and sharp teeth. It hissed and bit Tim on the arm. "Ow!" Tim screamed. He dropped the blanket and ran to the door. Jen followed him. But the door was shut. It was locked. They banged on the door and cried for help. But no one heard them. The snake slithered to them. It was hungry and angry. It lay on the floor and waited for them. Tim and Jen were trapped. They were scared. They wished they never went in the hut.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a wild bird on a tree. The bird was hungry and looking for food. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she hurried back home to find some oats. At home, Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can I have some oats for the bird?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Lily took the oats and hurried back to the park. When Lily got to the park, the wild bird was still there. She gave the oats to the bird, and the bird was very happy. They became friends and played together in the park. And from that day on, Lily always brought oats for her wild bird friend.

One day, a young rhinoceros went for a walk in the forest. He had so much fun finding new things and seeing all the animals. Suddenly, while he was walking, he stumbled and began to fall. He fell and landed on a soft patch of grass. The rhinoceros got scared and tried to get back up but didn't know how. He laid there for a few minutes and then he saw a young rabbit hopping by. The rabbit stopped and looked at the rhinoceros. He asked, “Are you okay? You fell down.” The rhinoceros explained the situation and the rabbit offered to help him. He asked the rhinoceros to put his front foot on the soft grass and then the rabbit gently pushed him up from the rear. The rhinoceros was able to stand again. The rhinoceros thanked the young rabbit and they both said goodbye. The rhinoceros continued his walk and made sure not to fall again!

Once upon a time, there was a large goose named Gertie. Gertie lived on a farm with many other animals. She liked to play with her friends, the cows and the sheep. One day, Gertie and her friends wanted to have a race. They all lined up to start. Gertie said, "Ready, set, go!" and they all started to run. The cows were fast, and the sheep were faster, but Gertie was the fastest of all. She flapped her big wings and ran as fast as she could. As Gertie crossed the finish line, all her friends cheered for her. "You are so fast, Gertie!" said the sheep. Gertie was very happy to win the race. From that day on, Gertie and her friends had many more races and lots of fun together.
