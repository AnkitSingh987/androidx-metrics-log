Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Window

Once upon a time, in a busy little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside, but today it started to rain. She felt sad and stayed inside her house. Lily saw a little bird outside her window. The bird was wet from the rain and looked scared. Lily wanted to save the bird and give it a warm place to stay. She opened her window and called the bird. The bird flew inside and Lily saved it from the rain. They played together and became best friends. The rain stopped, and they went outside to play. Lily was happy she saved the bird and they had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was a daughter who loved her mom and dad. Lily liked to play outside in the light, where the sun was bright. One day, Lily found a big rock. She heard a voice say, "Hello, do you trust me?" Lily looked around and saw a small bug. The bug said, "I can help you find a big treasure if you trust me." Lily thought about it and said, "Yes, I trust you." The bug led Lily to a hidden treasure chest. Lily was so happy and excited. But when she opened the chest, she found a big, shiny mirror. She looked in the mirror and saw her mom and dad. They were the real treasure. The bug had shown her that the best treasure was the love of her family.

Lily was a gifted bee. She could balance on any flower and make sweet honey. She loved to fly around the garden and visit her friends. She was happy and busy. One day, she saw a new flower. It was big and red and smelled nice. Lily wanted to balance on it and get some nectar. She flew closer and closer. But she did not see the thorn. Ouch! The thorn poked Lily's wing. She felt a sharp pain and fell down. She could not fly anymore. She was scared and sad. She cried for help. But no one heard her. She was far from the hive and her family. She felt alone and cold. Then, she heard a soft voice. It was a ladybug. She saw Lily on the ground and came to help. She asked Lily what happened and listened to her story. She felt sorry for Lily and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, Lily. I can help you. I can carry you to your hive. You are not alone. You have a friend." She lifted Lily gently and put her on her back. She flew slowly and carefully to the hive. She found Lily's family and told them what happened. They thanked the ladybug and took care of Lily. They gave her some honey and a bandage. They told her she was brave and strong. Lily felt better. She smiled and thanked the ladybug. She said, "You are a gifted friend. You saved me. You balanced me on your back. You are kind and good." The ladybug smiled and said, "You are welcome, Lily. You are a gifted bee. You balance on flowers and make honey. You are happy and busy. You are my friend." They hugged and said goodbye. They promised to visit each other again. They were happy and grateful. They had a new friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He brought his toy boat to sail in the pond. Tim was very happy to play with his boat. In the pond, he saw a big fish. The fish was angry. It said, "I lost my diamond. Can you help me find it?" Tim wanted to help the fish. He looked around the pond for the diamond. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's boat found the diamond! The angry fish became happy. It said, "Thank you, Tim! You are very kind." Tim learned that helping others can make you feel good too. And the fish and Tim became good friends.

Once there was a bald man who loved to play chess. Every day he went to the park to do some chess. He played for hours, and lots of people watched him. One day, a little 3-year-old boy saw the man playing chess, and he was intrigued. He asked the bald man, "What do you do?" The bald man replied, "I play chess. Would you like to have a go at it?" The little boy wasn't sure what to do, but he wanted to try. He got out the chess pieces, and the bald man explained the rules. The little boy quickly learned to move the pieces around the board. He was doing really well! Soon, it was time for the little boy to go home. Before he left, the bald man asked him if he liked playing chess. He smiled and said, "Yes, I like it. Can I come back tomorrow and play more chess?" The bald man said, "Of course! I'm here every day playing chess. See you tomorrow!" The little boy was so excited to learn more about playing chess. He couldn't wait for the next day to come so he could play chess again.
