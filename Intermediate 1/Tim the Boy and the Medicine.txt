Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Medicine

One day, a little boy named Tim felt sick. His tummy hurt and he was sleepy. His mom gave him medicine to help him feel better. Tim did not like the taste of the medicine, but he knew it would make him feel better. Later, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Tim told Sam, "I had to take medicine today because I was sick and sleepy." Sam said, "I don't like medicine. It tastes bad. Let's play and forget about it." As they played, they found a big box. They opened it and saw a lot of candy inside. They were so happy! They started to eat the candy, but then Tim remembered the medicine. He thought, "If I eat too much candy, it will spoil the medicine and I will feel sick again." So, Tim told Sam, "We can eat some candy, but not too much. We don't want to spoil the medicine." Sam agreed, and they ate a little candy. They played all day and had lots of fun. Tim was glad he did not spoil the medicine, and he felt better the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a pretty carpet. The carpet was very soft and had many colors. In the room, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play on the carpet all day. One day, the cat and the dog had a fun game. They played with a ball. The cat hit the ball with her paw, and the dog caught it with his mouth. They played very tight to the carpet so they would not break anything. The cat and the dog got hungry. They went to the kitchen to find food. The cat served the dog a big bowl of dog food, and the dog served the cat a plate of cat food. They ate very fast and went back to play on the carpet again. They were happy and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was feeling anxious one day. He did not know why. His mom saw that he was sad. She had an idea to make Tim feel better. She said, "Tim, do you want to help me cook? We can make a yummy soup!" Tim liked to cook with his mom. He said, "Yes, mommy! I want to help!" They went to the kitchen. Tim saw a big onion on the table. He knew that his mom would use it in the soup. Mom cut the onion into little pieces. Tim put the pieces in a pot. They added more things to the pot. The soup began to smell good. Tim started to feel happy again. When the soup was done, Tim and his mom sat down to eat. They both enjoyed the yummy soup with the onion. Tim forgot why he was anxious. He was happy to spend time with his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max liked to play in the mud and soak his fur. He would run and jump all day long. One day, Max saw a big watch on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran back to his house. Max's friend, a little bird named Tim, saw the watch too. Tim asked Max to share the watch, but Max did not want to. He wanted to keep it all for himself. This made Tim very sad, and he flew away to find another friend to play with. Max felt lonely without Tim. He realized that sharing the watch would have made both of them happy. So, Max found Tim and gave him the watch. They played together and had lots of fun. The moral of the story is that sharing with our friends makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a huge bear named Bobo. Bobo had many friends, but his best friend was a little bird named Chirpy. They played together every day. One day, Chirpy was sad and started to cry. Bobo asked, "Why do you cry, Chirpy?" Chirpy said, "I lost my toy in the big tree, and I can't fly high enough to get it." Bobo wanted to help his friend. He stood up tall and reached for the toy in the tree. He was so huge that he could touch the top of the tree. He got the toy and gave it back to Chirpy. Chirpy stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are the best friend ever!" They were both happy and played together until the sun went down. From that day on, Bobo and Chirpy knew they could always count on each other.
