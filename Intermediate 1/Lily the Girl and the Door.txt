Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Door

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and baby brother. Lily had an unusual room. It was full of colors, toys, and a big bed. One day, Lily's baby brother was sick. He cried and cried, and Lily felt sad. She went to her unusual room and closed the door. She sat on her big bed and began to pray. "Please make my baby brother feel better," she said. Later, Lily's mom came into the room. "Lily, your baby brother is feeling better now," she said with a smile. Lily was very happy to hear this. She knew that her prayer had helped her baby brother. From that day on, Lily always prayed in her unusual room when she needed help.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Lily. She had pretty wings and a long beak. Lily liked to fly and play with her friends. One day, Lily found an envelope on the ground. She picked it up with her beak and showed it to her friend, Tim the dog. "Tim, look what I found!" said Lily. "What should we do with the envelope?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Let's give it to the person who lost it. They might be sad." Lily and Tim looked around and saw a little girl crying. They went to her and Lily gave her the envelope. The little girl smiled and thanked them. She then gave Lily and Tim a big cup of water to drink as a thank you. Lily and Tim drank the water and felt happy that they helped the little girl. They learned that it is good to help others when they are sad or in need.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived with his parent in a big tree. Tim's parent was very careful with him. They played together and had lots of fun. One sunny day, Tim's parent said, "Tim, it's time to learn how to soar!" Tim was excited. They went to a high branch and Tim's parent showed him how to spread his wings. Tim was a little scared, but he knew his parent would be careful. Tim tried to soar. He flapped his wings and jumped. At first, he fell a little, but then he started to soar! Tim was so happy. He soared up and down and all around. His parent was very proud of him. They soared together in the sky and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Nick and Sam. Nick and Sam liked to explore different places. One day they decided that they wanted to go on a big adventure. Nick and Sam looked at different places they could go. Then they decided they wanted to go to the forest. They were so excited and packed their backpacks with snacks and games. They arrived at the forest and knew that it was going to be a wonderful day. There was a special secret path that was organized just for them. They followed the path, until it led them to a clearing. When the friends reached the clearing, they were very happy to find a picnic set up for them. Nick and Sam knew it was a welcome from all the animals in the forest. They decided to stay and eat their snacks with the animals. It was the best day ever!

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play together, but sometimes they fight. Anna likes to draw and Ben likes to build. One day, they find a big box in the garage. They want to see what is inside. "Let me open it!" Ben says. He pulls on the tape. "No, it's mine!" Anna says. She pushes him away. They tug and push and yell. The box falls over and breaks. They see a lot of clothes and toys and books. They are for a baby. "Mama and Papa have a surprise for us," Anna says. She picks up a pink dress. "What is it?" Ben asks. He sees a blue car. "They are going to give us a sister," Anna says. She smiles. "A sister?" Ben says. He frowns. "Yes, a sister. She is in Mama's tummy. She will come out soon. She will be small and cute and we will love her," Anna says. Ben is not sure. He likes to be the only boy. He does not want to share his toys and books. He does not want a sister. "Mama and Papa will reveal her to us when she is ready," Anna says. She hugs the dress. Ben thinks. He loves Mama and Papa. He loves Anna too, even when they fight. Maybe a sister will be fun. Maybe he can teach her how to build and play. Maybe he can protect her and make her laugh. He looks at Anna. She is shy. She does not have many friends. She likes to stay at home and draw. Maybe a sister will be good for her. Maybe she will have someone to talk to and share with. He picks up the car. He gives it to Anna. "Here, you can have this. It is for our sister," he says. Anna smiles. She gives him the dress. "Thank you. You can have this. It is for our sister too," she says. They hug. They are happy. They are excited. They can't wait to meet their sister.
