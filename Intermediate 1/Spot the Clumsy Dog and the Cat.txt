Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Clumsy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Spot. Spot had a big body and loved to play with his friends. One day, Spot wanted to serve food to his friends at a party. Spot said, "I want to give my friends a fun time!" He put food on plates and carried them to the table. But Spot was clumsy, and he dropped the plates. The food went all over the floor. Spot's friends laughed and said, "Oh, Spot, you are so clumsy!" Just then, a cat came in and saw the food on the floor. The cat was hungry and started to eat the food. Spot's friends saw the cat and said, "Look, Spot! Your clumsy mistake helped the hungry cat!" They all laughed and played together. Spot was happy that he could help, even if it was by accident.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He had a little boat. Every day, he went to the sea to catch fish. The fisherman liked his job. He was good at it. One day, the fisherman went out to the sea. He saw a big fish. It was fast. The fisherman tried to catch the fast fish. He pulled and pulled on his net. But the fast fish was too strong. The net started to break. The fisherman was sad. He did not want his net to break. But the fast fish was too strong. The net broke. The fisherman went home. He fixed his net. The next day, he went back to the sea to catch more fish.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a stubborn girl who always wanted to do things her way. One day, she found a pretty glass in the kitchen. It was shiny and colorful. Lucy wanted to drink water from the glass. She tried to reach it, but it was too high. Her mommy saw her and said, "Do you need help, Lucy?" But Lucy shook her head and said, "No, I can do it!" She tried and tried, but she couldn't reach the glass. Finally, Lucy asked her mommy for help. Her mommy got the glass for her and filled it with water. Lucy drank the water and said, "Thank you, mommy!" Her mommy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lucy. It's okay to ask for help sometimes." And from that day on, Lucy learned to ask for help when she needed it.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play outside in her yard. One day, she found a regular patch of dirt. She thought it was a good spot to plant seeds. Lily asked her mom, "Can I plant seeds here?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can plant seeds and enjoy watching them grow." Lily was very happy. She planted the seeds and waited for them to grow. One day, Lily went outside to look at her patch. But instead of regular plants, she saw that candy had grown there! She was so surprised. Lily called her mom and said, "Look, the seeds grew into candy!" They both laughed and enjoyed the sweet treats together.

One day, a big cat saw a little mouse. The little mouse had a toy car. The big cat wanted to play with the toy car, but the little mouse said, "No, it's mine!" The big cat was sad, but he had an idea. He said to the little mouse, "I can lend you my ball if you let me play with your toy car." The little mouse thought about it and said, "Okay, we can share our toys." They played together and had fun. But then, the little mouse left his toy car on the floor. The big cat was not alert and stepped on the toy car, breaking it. The little mouse was very upset. In the end, the big cat and the little mouse learned that it is good to share, but they must also be careful with each other's things.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little squirrel named Tim. Tim loved to play and run around all day. One day, while playing, he found a shiny acorn on the ground. It was the biggest acorn he had ever seen! Tim picked up the acorn and started to carry it home. But the acorn was very heavy, and Tim got tired. He saw a big tree and decided to rest under it. As he sat down, he heard a loud noise. It was a big bird named Bob. "Hi, Tim! What do you have there?" asked Bob. "It's a big acorn!" said Tim. "Can you help me carry it home?" Bob agreed, and together they carried the acorn to Tim's house. After that, they became the best of friends and played together every day.
