Category: Intermediate
Title: Twinkle the Star and the Moon

Once upon a time, there was a happy little star named Twinkle. Twinkle lived in the sky and loved to play with his friend, Moon. One day, Twinkle and Moon were playing a game of snap. "Snap!" said Twinkle, as he jumped high in the sky. "Snap!" said Moon, as she jumped too. They laughed and played together, having so much fun. As they played, they met a new friend named Cloud. Cloud wanted to play too. "Can I play snap with you?" asked Cloud. "Yes!" said Twinkle and Moon together. They all played snap and had a great time. The sky was filled with their laughter. Twinkle, Moon, and Cloud became the best of friends. They played snap and other fun games every day. And they all lived happily ever after in the big, beautiful sky.

Once upon a time, in a big yellow house, there lived a little boy named Tim. He loved to sing and dance on his small stage. Every day, he would put on a show for his mom, dad, and little sister. They would all clap and cheer for him. One day, Tim's little sister, Lily, wanted to join him on the stage. She asked, or requested, if she could sing and dance with him. But Tim didn't want to share his stage. He said no, and Lily was very sad. This made their mom and dad sad too. Mom and dad talked to Tim and told him that sharing is good. They said that if he let Lily sing and dance with him, they could have even more fun together. Tim thought about it, and he agreed. So, Tim and Lily went on the stage together and sang and danced. They had a great time, and their mom, dad, and even their dog clapped and cheered for them.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Mia. She liked to play with her toy box. One day, she found a fake ring in her box. She put it on her finger and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mom! I have a ring! Can we play marriage?" asked Mia. Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, let's play. I will be the one to lift you up after the marriage." So Mia and her mom played marriage. Mia's mom lifted her up high in the air, and they both laughed. They had a lot of fun playing together. After playing, Mia took off the fake ring and put it back in her toy box. She knew it was just a game, but she loved spending time with her mom. From that day on, Mia and her mom played marriage many times, and they always had a great time.

One day, a little girl named Sally found a magic rock. She was very excite. Sally knew that this rock could make one wish come true. She thought hard about what her original wish would be. Sally met her friend Tim and said, "I found a magic rock! What should I wish for?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "You could wish for a big ice cream!" Sally liked the idea but wanted something even more original. As they walked, Sally and Tim saw a sad, lost puppy. They wanted to help it. Sally thought of her original wish. She held the magic rock and wished for the puppy to find its home. Suddenly, the puppy's owner appeared, happy and surprised to see his lost puppy. Sally and Tim were happy too. They knew that the magic rock had made an original and good wish come true.

One day, a big bossy cat named Tom saw some smoke. It came from a small house. Tom ran to the house to see what was wrong. He saw a little bird named Tim inside. Tim was scared. Tom said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will help you." Tim looked at the bossy cat and said, "But you are always mean to me. Why do you want to help?" Tom felt bad and said, "I want to be nice now." Tom and Tim worked together to make the smoke go away. They found out that the smoke came from a toy train. It was not a real fire! They laughed and played with the toy train. From that day on, Tom was not bossy anymore. He and Tim became good friends.
