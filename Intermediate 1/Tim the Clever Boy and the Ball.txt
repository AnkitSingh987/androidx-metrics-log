Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Clever Boy and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a clever little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his favorite object, a big red ball. One day, while Tim was playing with his ball, it went far away and got stuck in a big tree. Tim tried to rock the tree back and forth to get his ball back, but the tree was too big. Then, a small bird flew by and saw Tim trying to get his ball. The bird wanted to help, so it flew up to the tree and pushed the ball with its beak. But, instead of the red ball falling, a shiny gold ball fell from the tree! Tim was so happy and surprised. He thanked the bird and played with the new gold ball. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big rock in her yard. She thought it was special and wanted to keep it. So, she took a sheet and wrapped the rock in it. She showed it to her friend, Tom. Tom saw the rock and said, "Wow! Your rock is so cool!" He felt envious because he wanted a rock like that too. Sue was happy that her friend liked her rock. Together, they went to look for a rock for Tom. They found one under a tree. It was not as big as Sue's, but Tom liked it. He wrapped it in a sheet too. Now, they both had special rocks and they played with them every day.

One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to rise high in the sky. He was a serious bird who loved life. Tim always dreamed of touching the clouds. As Tim tried to rise, he saw a big, green leaf. He wanted to rest on it for a while. But when he landed on the leaf, something unexpected happened. The leaf started to rise with Tim! The leaf was not a leaf at all, but a friendly green balloon. Tim and the green balloon became best friends. They rose high in the sky together, touching the clouds and enjoying life.

One day, a boy named Tim found a map. The map was different. It was not like other maps. Tim wanted to know what the map showed. He asked his mom, "What is this map for?" His mom said, "This map is special. It shows you how to find things you value. Things that make you happy." Tim was excited. He followed the map to a big tree. Under the tree, he found a box. Inside the box, Tim saw many toys. But they were not new toys. They were his old toys that he lost. Tim was surprised. The map did not show him new things. It showed him things he already had. Tim learned that he should value the things he has. New things are not always better.
