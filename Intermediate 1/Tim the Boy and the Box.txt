Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Box

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. He would sit and laugh at the funny pictures on the screen. One day, Tim's mom had to send a letter. She asked Tim to take the letter to the mailbox. Tim said yes and took the letter. On the way, he saw a big, colorful box with a sign that said "Cartoon Show." He was so excited! Tim went inside the box, hoping to see his favorite cartoons. But, oh no! Instead of a fun cartoon show, it was a trap! The door closed, and Tim was stuck inside. The peaceful town was not so peaceful anymore. Tim's mom looked for him, but she could not find him. And that is the sad end of the story.

Lily and Tom were playing in the backyard. They saw some smoke coming from the house next door. They wondered what it was. "Let's go see!" Tom said. "No, we can't. Mom said we have to stay here," Lily said. "But I want to know. Maybe it's a fire. Maybe someone needs help," Tom said. He ran to the fence and looked over. He saw a man with a big hat and a long stick. He was making the smoke with the stick. He was blowing it at some boxes. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom shouted. The man looked at Tom and smiled. He said, "I'm making honey. I'm a beekeeper. The smoke makes the bees calm. Then I can take some honey from the boxes." "Wow, that's cool. Can I have some honey?" Tom asked. "Sure, come over. I'll give you some. And you can see the bees. They are very nice. They make the honey for us," the man said. Tom was happy. He wanted to see the bees and taste the honey. He forgot about Lily. He climbed over the fence and ran to the man. Lily was frustrated. She wanted to see the bees and taste the honey too. But she also wanted to obey Mom. She didn't know what to do. She guessed Tom would come back soon. She guessed the man was nice. She guessed the bees were not scary. She guessed the honey was sweet. But she was not sure. She wished Tom would come back. She wished he would share the honey with her. She wished he would not forget her. She wished he would say sorry.

Once upon a time there lived an old man and his three year old granddaughter. The old man loved his granddaughter very much, so he decided to take her on a special adventure. He brought her to the mountain and showed her a big pile of coal. The old man said, "Let's throw the coal into the river!" The little girl was so excited. She grabbed a handful of coal and threw it into the river. She laughed and squealed with delight as the coal splashed into the water. The old man smiled at her and said, "Young lady, coal isn't just for throwing into rivers. Let's go back home and I'll show you a better use for coal." When they reached home, the old man brought out a pot of soup. He said, "This is called a coal soup, and it tastes very tasty. I want you to try some and see how it tastes." The little girl was hesitant at first, but then she tried a spoonful of the soup and her face lit up with joy. She said, "Grandpa, this soup is delicious!" The old man and his granddaughter smiled at each other, knowing that this would be a memory they would cherish forever.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a lonely cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to stretch her legs and play with her toys. But she had no friends to play with her. One day, Kitty saw a little bird outside her window. The bird was trying to fly, but it couldn't. Kitty wanted to help, so she went outside and said, "Hello, little bird. I can teach you to fly." The little bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty! I want to learn." So, Kitty and the bird tried and tried. They stretched their wings and jumped up and down. After a while, the little bird could fly. It was so happy! Kitty was happy too, because now she had a friend. They played together every day and were never lonely again.
