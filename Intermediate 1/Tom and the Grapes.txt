Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Grapes

Tom is a strong boy. He likes to run and jump and play. He also likes to eat grapes. Grapes are sweet and juicy and good for him. One day, Tom goes to the market with his mom. He sees a big basket of grapes. He wants to fill his bag with grapes. He asks his mom, "Can I have some grapes, please?" Mom says, "Yes, you can have some grapes, but not too many. You have to share with others. And you have to pay for them, too." Tom nods. He takes a small bag and puts some grapes in it. He does not fill it too much. He is a good boy. He gives the bag to his mom. Mom pays the man for the grapes. Tom and his mom go home. Tom is happy. He has grapes for his snack. He says, "Thank you, mom, for the grapes. They are yummy." Mom smiles. She says, "You're welcome, Tom. I'm proud of you. You are a strong and kind boy."

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a winter day. The sun was not very bright, and the air was cold. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, wanted to play outside. The snow was white and fluffy. They had a plan to have fun in the winter. Tim and Spot put on their warm clothes. Tim wore a big coat and a hat. Spot had a little coat, too. They went outside to play in the snow. They saw a big hill. Tim and Spot decided to slide down the hill. They laughed and had a great time. After playing, Tim and Spot went to a store. They saw a toy that was very expensive. Tim wanted the toy, but he knew it cost too much. He did not feel sad, because he had a fun day with his plan in the winter. Tim and Spot walked back home, happy and tired from their day in the snow.

Once upon a time, there was a poor otter. The poor otter was very hungry. One day, a kind girl found the otter. She wanted to help the otter. The girl had a plan to feed the otter. She gave the otter a big fish. The otter was very happy. The girl and the otter became good friends. But one day, something unexpected happened. The otter did not want the fish anymore. The girl was sad. Then, the otter showed the girl a big pile of fish. The otter had learned to find food by itself. Now, the otter could help the girl too. They were both happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there lived a helpful ice cream man. He liked to give ice cream to all the kids in the place. The ice cream man had many flavors, like chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry. The kids loved the ice cream man and his tasty treats. One hot day, the sun was shining very bright. The ice cream man was giving ice cream to the kids, but the ice cream started to melt. The kids were sad because their ice cream was melting too fast. The helpful ice cream man had an idea. He went to a big tree and gave the kids ice cream under the tree. The shade from the tree helped keep the ice cream from melting too fast. The kids were happy again and thanked the helpful ice cream man for saving their ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her blocks. One sunny day, Amy went to the park to skip and play with her blocks. At the park, Amy saw a new friend named Tim. Tim was sad because he did not have any toys to play with. Amy said, "Do you want to play with my blocks?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, please!" Amy and Tim played with the blocks and had lots of fun. They built tall towers and made new shapes. They skipped around the park together, laughing and playing. Amy was very kind to share her blocks with Tim. In the end, Amy and Tim became best friends. They learned that sharing and being kind to others can make everyone happy. And that is why they always played together at the park.
