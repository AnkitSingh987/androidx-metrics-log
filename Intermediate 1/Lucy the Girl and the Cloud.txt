Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Cloud

One day, a little girl named Lucy went outside to play. She saw a big cloud in the sky. It was a sunny day, and the cloud was all alone. Lucy thought the cloud looked sad. She wanted to help it, but she didn't know how. Lucy saw her friend, Tom, walking by. She said, "Tom, do you see that big cloud? It looks sad. Can we help it?" Tom looked at the cloud and thought for a moment. "Maybe we can reverse the cloud to make it happy," he said. Lucy didn't know what "reverse" meant, but she wanted to try. They tried to be persistent and help the cloud. They jumped up and down, waved their arms, and even sang a song. Suddenly, the cloud started to change. It became smaller, and then it turned into a big, beautiful rainbow! Lucy and Tom were so happy that they could help the cloud. They knew that sometimes, all it takes is a little bit of love and care to make someone's day better.

Tim likes to play football with his friends. They have a big green field and a brown ball. They kick the ball and run after it. Sometimes they score a goal and shout "yay!" One day, Tim finds something unusual in the field. It is a big red balloon with a string. He thinks it is fun and he wants to play with it. He picks up the balloon and runs with it. He feels like he is flying. But his friends are not happy. They want to play football, not balloon. They say "Tim, come back! Give us the ball!" They chase Tim and try to get the ball. Tim does not want to give the ball. He likes the balloon. Tim has an idea. He attaches the string of the balloon to the ball. He thinks it is funny. He says "Look, now we have a flying ball!" He kicks the ball and the balloon. The ball and the balloon fly in the air. They go very high and far. But Tim's friends are not amused. They say "Tim, that is not fair! That is not how you play football! You ruined the game!" They are angry and sad. They cannot get the ball and the balloon back. They go away and leave Tim alone. Tim feels sorry. He did not mean to make his friends mad. He just wanted to have fun. He wishes he could play football with them again. He looks at the ball and the balloon in the sky. He says "Bye bye, flying ball. I hope you find a good home." He goes home and hopes his friends will forgive him.

Lily was a little girl who liked to grow flowers. She had a small garden in her backyard, where she planted seeds and watered them every day. She loved to see the flowers grow and bloom in different colors. One day, Lily saw a big blanket on the couch. It was soft and warm and had pretty patterns on it. Lily wanted to play with the blanket, so she took it and ran outside. She thought it would be fun to make a tent with the blanket and the flowers. But Lily was naughty. She did not ask her mom if she could use the blanket. She did not know that the blanket was special. It was a gift from her grandma, who made it by hand. It was very old and delicate, and could not get dirty or wet. When Lily's mom saw that the blanket was gone, she was very worried. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. She called Lily and asked her where the blanket was. Lily did not answer. She was hiding in her tent, under the blanket and the flowers. Lily's mom went outside and saw the tent. She was very angry. She pulled the blanket off the tent and saw that it was covered in dirt and water and petals. She scolded Lily and told her that she was very naughty. She took the blanket and the flowers away from her and sent her to her room. Lily was very sad. She did not mean to ruin the blanket. She did not know that it was special. She wanted to say sorry to her mom and her grandma. She wished she had asked before she took the blanket. She learned her lesson. She decided to be good and not take things without asking. She hoped that her mom and her grandma would forgive her.
