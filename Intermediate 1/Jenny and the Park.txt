Category: Intermediate
Title: Jenny and the Park

One day, Jenny and her mommy took a ride in their car. They drove to the park, and Jenny saw something exciting: ponies! Jenny had never ridden a pony before and she was excited. Jenny asked her mommy if she could ride one of the ponies. Her mommy said yes, so Jenny chose a big, brown pony named Robo. Robo was Jenny’s favorite right away! Jenny and her mommy took the pony for a ride around the park. They waved at the other ponies and their people, and Jenny laughed and smiled. When they were finished, Jenny gave Robo a big hug and told him goodbye. Jenny’s mommy helped her put Robo’s saddle in a file so it wouldn’t get bad. Jenny was so happy she had ridden a pony for the first time! She knew that’s something she would always remember.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty dog named Max. Max liked to run and play in the park. One day, Max saw a trail in the park. The trail had many trees and flowers. Max wanted to go on the trail. Max asked his mom if he could go on the trail. "Mom, can I go on the trail?" Max's mom thought about it. Then she said, "Yes, I permit you to go on the trail, but be careful and don't go too far." Max was very happy. He ran and played on the trail. But Max did not listen to his mom. He went too far. Max got lost on the trail. He could not find his mom. Max was very sad and scared. Max tried to find his way back, but he could not. The sun went down, and it got dark. Max was all alone in the dark. He missed his mom and wished he had listened to her. Max's mom looked for him, but she could not find him either. Max stayed lost on the trail, and the story had a bad ending.

One day a group of children was playing in the park. There was one hard game they wanted to play. It was called 'Increase'. One of the children said, "I know how to play increase! Let's play it. It will be fun!" The other children were excited and quickly joined the group. They started the game and ran around in circles until the leader called out, "Increase!" At that time, the children had to run faster. As they increased their speed, they couldn't stop laughing. The game got harder and harder. But the group of children kept playing and running around. It was a great way to get exercise and have fun! Finally, the leader said, "That's it! You all did a great job playing increase. I'm so proud of you." The children were glad they got to play together and had fun. They were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sleep all day and play all night. Tom did not own many toys, but he was happy. One day, Tom found a big red ball. He played with the ball all night. He was very tired the next day. Tom had a memory of the fun he had with the ball. Tom told his friend, a dog named Max, about the big red ball. Max wanted to play with the ball too. Tom and Max played with the ball together. They were not lazy anymore. They made many happy memories with the big red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish and a little fish. They were best friends. They liked to play together in the water. One day, they found a hidden cave. They wanted to see what was inside. The big fish said, "Let's go in!" The little fish was scared but said, "Okay, let's go!" They swam into the cave. Inside, they saw a net. The big fish said, "This net looks fun. Let's play with it!" The little fish and the big fish played with the net. They had lots of fun. But then, the big fish slipped into the net. He was stuck! The little fish was scared. He didn't know what to do. But then, he had an idea. The little fish swam very fast and pulled the net. The net opened and the big fish was free! They were so happy. They swam out of the cave and promised to be more careful next time. And they lived happily ever after.
