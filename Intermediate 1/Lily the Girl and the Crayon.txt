Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Crayon

One day, a little girl named Lily found a box of crayons. The box had many colors, but she liked the deep blue crayon the most. She used it to draw a big sea with fish and a boat. She was very happy with her drawing. Lily wanted to show her drawing to her mom. But when she went to find her mom, she saw that the deep blue crayon was gone! She was sad and looked everywhere for it. She thought she would never find it again. Then, she saw her little brother, Tim, holding the crayon. He was drawing a big sky with it. Lily was surprised and happy to see the crayon again. She asked Tim if they could draw together. Tim said yes, and they both gained a new friend to draw with. They had lots of fun drawing with the deep blue crayon together.

Sara liked to dress her toys. She had a doll, a bear, a frog and a goose. She put hats and scarves and shoes on them. She made them look pretty and funny. But the goose was bossy. He did not like Sara's clothes. He said, "No, no, no! I do not want this hat. It is too big. I do not want this scarf. It is too red. I do not want these shoes. They are too tight. I want to wear my own feathers. They are soft and white and warm." Sara was sad. She wanted the goose to play with her. She said, "Please, goose, do not be bossy. Let me dress you. It is fun. You can dress me too. We can be friends." The goose thought for a moment. He saw that Sara was nice and kind. He said, "OK, Sara, I will try. But you have to be gentle. Do not pull my feathers. Do not make me look silly. And you have to let me choose some clothes for you too." Sara smiled. She said, "OK, goose, I will be gentle. And I will let you choose some clothes for me too. But you have to promise me one thing. You have to say 'quack' when you like something. It is a funny sound. It makes me laugh." The goose nodded. He said, "OK, Sara, I will say 'quack' when I like something. It is a funny sound. It makes you laugh." And so Sara and the goose dressed each other. They tried different hats and scarves and shoes. They said 'quack' when they liked something. They laughed and played. They became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a farmer named Bob. Bob farmed every day to grow corn. Bob worked hard, but the corn was not growing as quickly as he wanted. One day, Bob noticed that the corn patch was very dull. He wondered why this was happening and what he could do to make it grow faster. Bob showed the dull corn patch to his friend, Sam. Sam thought it was very strange and had an idea. "Bob, try using some special fertilizer in your corn patch. I think it will help the corn to grow more quickly," said Sam. Bob thanked Sam and decided to try the special fertilizer. He spread the fertilizer all over the corn patch, and waited. The next day, Bob noticed lots of little sprouts coming up in the corn patch. He smiled and was very happy with the results. From then on, Bob always used special fertilizer to help his corn grow faster and brighter.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, serious dog named Max. Max loved Lily very much and always stayed by her side. One day, Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to play touch. Lily would run and touch Max, and then Max would run and touch Lily. They were having lots of fun. But then, Lily fell down and hurt her knee. Lily started to weep because her knee hurt. Max saw Lily weeping and felt sad for her. He went to her side and licked her face to make her feel better. Lily stopped weeping and hugged Max. They went home, and Lily's mom helped her feel better. After that, Lily and Max played more gently and never forgot to take care of each other.
