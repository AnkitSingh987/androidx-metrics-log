Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat peaches. One day, he saw a big peach tree in his yard. The tree had many big, yummy peaches. Tim was very happy. Tim wanted to eat a peach, but he was too small. He asked his big sister, Sue, to help him. Sue was naughty and said no. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He had an idea. Tim found a long stick. He used the stick to get a peach from the tree. He shared the peach with his dog, Spot. They both ate the peach and it was very yummy. Tim was happy, and Spot was happy too.

---

One day, a girl named Sue went to the park. She had long hair. In the park, she saw a big tree. She wanted to play there. Near the tree, she saw a boy named Sam. He had short hair. Sue said, "Hi, I am Sue. Want to play with me?" Sam said, "Yes, I want to play. I am Sam. Let's play together!" They were happy to meet each other. They played all day. They made a game with the tree. They found a ball that was available. They threw the ball and laughed. Sue and Sam became good friends.

---

Once upon a time, there was an old tree. The tree lived in a big forest with many other trees. The old tree had many friends, like the birds, squirrels, and bugs. They all loved to play and talk together. One day, a little bird flew to the old tree and said, "I found a shiny thing on the ground. It has a lock on it." The old tree asked, "What is it?" The little bird replied, "I don't know, but it is very pretty." The old tree and its friends wanted to open the lock. They tried many ways, but the lock did not open. Then, a wise owl said, "We need oxygen to open the lock. Oxygen is in the air we breathe." The animals all took a deep breath and blew on the lock. The lock clicked and opened! Inside the shiny thing, they found a beautiful treasure. The old tree and its friends were very happy. They shared the treasure and played together all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy dog named Max. Max liked to drive his little red car. He had a big sausage that he wanted to share with his friends. Max drove to his friend Cat's house. "I have a sausage to share," said Max. Cat was happy and jumped in the car. They drove to their friend Bird's house. At Bird's house, Max said, "I have a sausage to share with you too!" Bird was very happy and joined them in the car. They all drove to the park and ate the big sausage together. They had a fun day and were not bossy anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a queen. She lived in a big castle near a lake. The queen loved to swim in the lake. The water was perfect for her. She would swim every day with her friends, the fish. One day, the queen saw a big box in the lake. She swam to the box and opened it. Inside the box, she found a pretty hat. The queen put the hat on her head and swam back to the castle. The next day, the queen went to swim in the lake again. But this time, when she jumped into the water, she turned into a fish! The hat was magic! The queen was now a fish queen. She swam with her fish friends and they all had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big red strawberry. It lived in a small garden with many other fruits and vegetables. The strawberry was very happy because it was growing bigger and bigger every day. One day, a spoiled little boy saw the strawberry. He wanted to take it home with him. So, he picked it up and started to rock it like a baby. The strawberry was scared because it didn't want to leave the garden. The little boy's mom saw what he was doing and told him to put the strawberry back. The boy listened to his mom and put the strawberry back in the garden. The strawberry was happy to be back with its friends and continued to grow big and strong.
