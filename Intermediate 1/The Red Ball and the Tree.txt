Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Ball and the Tree

One day, a red ball went out to play. It rolled and rolled in the grass. The sun was shining, and the red ball was happy. Then, the red ball saw a big tree. It wanted to stop and rest. But, it did not know how to stop. So, it asked a bird, "How do I stop?" The bird said, "Just say, 'stop!'" The red ball said, "stop!" and it stopped by the tree. But, something unexpected happened. The tree was not a tree! It was a big, green dog! The dog was just standing very still. The red ball and the big, green dog became friends. They played in the grass all day. And when they wanted to stop, they just said, "stop!"

Lucy looked up, and saw a crane in the sky. She thought it was a very big bird, and she wanted to see it up close. She asked her mom if she could go get a closer look. Her mom said yes, so Lucy ran outside. She saw the big crane in the sky, and she watched it fly. Its wings were so big and it went up very high. Lucy was so happy she could see the crane from so close! Then Lucy saw that the crane had dropped something. It was lying on the ground, very close to where she was standing. She picked it up, and saw that it was very clean. She thought it was very special. Lucy smiled, and carefully put the item in her pocket. Now she would always remember her special experience with the crane.

Lily and her mom went to a big hotel for a holiday. Lily liked the hotel. It had a big pool, a nice garden, and many toys. Lily saw some flowers in the garden. They were pink and white and smelled good. Lily wanted to pick one for her mom. "Mom, can I pick a flower for you?" Lily asked. "No, Lily, you can't. The flowers belong to the hotel. They are not for us. We are only guests here. We have to be nice and not touch anything that is not ours," Mom said. "But they are so pretty. And you are so nice. You deserve a flower, Mom," Lily said. "Thank you, Lily, but we have to follow the rules. Maybe we can buy a flower later, when we go to the market. Come on, let's go to the pool. It's hot today," Mom said. Lily was sad. She really wanted to give her mom a flower. She thought the hotel was mean. They had so many flowers, and they did not share. Lily waited until Mom was not looking. She ran to the garden and picked a flower. She hid it behind her back and ran back to Mom. "Look, Mom, I have a surprise for you," Lily said. She showed Mom the flower. Mom was angry. She saw that Lily had picked a flower from the garden. She knew that the hotel would be angry too. She knew that they were poor and could not pay for the flower. She knew that they might have to leave the hotel and lose their holiday. "Lily, what have you done? You know you can't pick the flowers. You have broken the rules. You have made a big mistake. The hotel will be very mad at us. They might kick us out. They might call the police. How could you do this, Lily?" Mom said. Lily was scared. She did not know that picking a flower was so bad. She only wanted to make Mom happy. She started to cry. She said she was sorry. She said she loved Mom. She said she did not want to leave the hotel. She said she did not want to go to the police. But it was too late. The hotel manager saw Lily and Mom. He saw the flower. He was very mad. He said that Lily and Mom had to leave the hotel right now. He said that they had to pay for the flower. He said that he would call the police. He said that they were not welcome here. Lily and Mom had to pack their bags. They had to leave the hotel. They had no money. They had no place to go. They had no holiday. They had no flower. They only had each other. They were very sad. They wished they had never seen the flower. They wished they had never come to the hotel. They wished they had stayed at home.
