Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and the Man

One day, Jack saw a wealthy man playing a trumpet. Jack loved the sound it made and so he clapped loudly. The man stopped playing and smiled. "Would you like to try playing the trumpet?" he asked. Jack nodded, excitedly. He climbed onto a stool and blew into the trumpet. It made a funny noise! Jack laughed and clapped again. The man smiled again and said, "I can show you how to make wonderful music with this trumpet. Would you like to learn?" Jack nodded eagerly and so the man taught him how to make music. At first, Jack couldn't make any nice noises but soon he was playing beautiful tunes. He learned a lot from the wealthy man and soon Jack was clapping along to his music. It was wonderful!

One day, a cat named Tom found a long string. Tom loved to play with the string, but he did not know it was dangerous. He played with the string every day, and it made him very happy. One day, Tom's friend, a dog named Max, saw Tom playing with the dangerous string. Max knew that the string was not safe for Tom. So, Max decided to remove the string from Tom's home. When Tom saw that the string was gone, he was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. Max brought Tom a new toy to play with. It was a soft and safe ball. Tom and Max played with the new toy together, and they were both very happy. The dangerous string was gone, but their friendship became stronger.

Delia was excited. She was going to the park with her mom. The place was so nice and the sun was shining. As they drove to the park, Delia looked out the window. Everything looked so green and fresh. When they arrived at the park, Delia's mouth dropped open. It was so dirty and full of filth! Trash was everywhere. Delia started to cry. "Hey, don't worry," her mom said. "We'll clean it up and make it better." Delia and her mom started to work. They picked up all the trash and cleaned the path. After a few hours, the park was shining and clean. Delia smiled. "This is a wonderful place isn't it, Delia?" her mom said. "Yes, mommy," Delia said, happily. "Let's play!"

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the shore with her mom. It was a gloomy day, but they wanted to have fun. They saw a big boat near the shore. Mia saw a man on the boat. He looked sad. Mia asked her mom, "Can we bring him some food? He looks hungry." Her mom said, "Yes, let's bring him some food to make him happy." Mia and her mom brought the man some food. The man said, "Thank you for the food! I was very hungry." Mia smiled and said, "You're welcome! We wanted to make you happy." The man was happy, and the gloomy day turned bright. Mia, her mom, and the man had a fun day at the shore. They played in the sand and laughed all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat. The cat had a name. Her name was Kitty. Kitty liked to play and stretch. One day, Kitty went outside to play. The sun was pale and the sky was blue. Kitty saw a big tree. She wanted to stretch and climb the tree. Kitty stretched her legs and climbed up the tree. She was very happy. Then, Kitty saw a bird. The bird had a name too. His name was Chirpy. Chirpy and Kitty played together. They sang and danced in the tree. After a fun day, Kitty and Chirpy went home. They were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy who lived in a village. He loved to play on the swing in his garden each day. One morning when he went outside to play he saw a little white mouse running around in a hurry. The boy said "Hi, little mouse! What are you doing in such a hurry?" The mouse stopped and said "I am trying to find some food for my family. They're hungry and I want to help them". The boy smiled and said "I can help you too! Come with me". The boy and the mouse went to the boy's house and the boy reached into the cupboard. He took out a generous bag of food and handed it to the mouse. The mouse was so happy and said "Thank you so much! You are very generous". The boy smiled and said "It was my pleasure to help. We should always be kind to others and help when we can". The little mouse hopped off happily with the food and the boy went back to his swing. He had learned an important lesson that day - that we should always be generous and helpful to others.
