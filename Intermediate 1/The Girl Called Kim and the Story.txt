Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Kim and the Story

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Kim. She was very impatient and she wanted to eat her dinner quickly. As she was very impatient, she did not wait for the spaghetti to be cooked properly. Instead, she grabbed it from the pot and tried to eat it. But since it was not cooked properly, it was too hard. Kim got frustrated and started to cry. Seeing how upset she was, her mom came to help. Her mom gently took the spaghetti from Kim and split it into two parts. She put each part back into the pot for longer. Finally when the spaghetti was cooked properly, Kim ate it happily and enjoyed her dinner. Moral of the story: Patience is key! Never rush things, as it can lead to trouble. Wait for the right time and good things will happen!

Sara and Ben were playing with cubes in their room. They liked to build towers and houses with the cubes. They had many colors of cubes: red, blue, yellow, green, and more. "Look, I made a big tower!" Sara said, showing Ben her cube tower. "It is taller than me!" "That is nice, Sara," Ben said. "But I made a better house. It has a door and a window and a roof. See?" He pointed to his cube house. Sara looked at Ben's house. She felt angry. She wanted to have a better house too. She had an idea. She pushed Ben's house with her hand. The cubes fell down and made a loud noise. "Hey, why did you do that?" Ben shouted. "You disturbed my house! That is not nice!" Sara laughed. She did not care. She thought it was fun to disturb Ben's house. She tried to push her tower on Ben's head. "Stop it, Sara!" Ben said. "You are not a good sister! You are mean!" Ben was sad and angry. He did not want to play with Sara anymore. He ran out of the room and went to find Mom. Mom was in the kitchen. She was making cookies. She saw Ben crying. She asked him what was wrong. "Sara disturbed my house!" Ben said. "She pushed it down and laughed. She is not helpful. She is bad!" Mom hugged Ben. She felt sorry for him. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. That was not nice of Sara. She should not disturb your house. She should be helpful and kind. Why don't you come and help me with the cookies? You are a good helper." Ben nodded. He liked to help Mom with the cookies. He liked to mix the dough and cut the shapes. He felt better. He said, "Thank you, Mom. You are helpful and kind. I love you." Mom smiled. She said, "I love you too, Ben. And I love Sara too. She is not bad. She is just young. She needs to learn how to play nicely. Maybe later you can teach her how to build a house with the cubes. Would you like that?" Ben thought for a moment. He said, "Maybe. But only if she says sorry and does not disturb me again." Mom said, "That sounds fair. I'm sure she will say sorry and learn from her mistake. Now, let's make some cookies. Do you want chocolate or vanilla?" Ben said, "Chocolate, please!" Mom said, "Me too!" They laughed and made cookies together. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a fast dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play all day. One day, Spot found a big bone in the park. He was so happy! But then, another dog named Max came to the park. Max saw the bone and wanted it too. Max and Spot did not want to share the bone. They both wanted it for themselves. This made them very sad. Spot and Max decided to race for the bone. Whoever won the race would get the bone. They ran as fast as they could. In the end, Spot won the race. But when Spot saw Max was sad, he felt regret. He did not want the bone if it meant his friend was unhappy. So, Spot decided to share the bone with Max. They both learned that sharing was more fun than having something all to themselves. And they lived happily ever after.
