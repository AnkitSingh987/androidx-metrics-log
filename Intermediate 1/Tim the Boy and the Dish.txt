Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Dish

One day, a boy named Tim went to ride his bike. He saw a grumpy cat sitting on a dish. The cat looked sad and mad. Tim wanted to help the cat feel better. Tim said, "Hi cat, why are you so grumpy?" The cat said, "I am hungry and I don't like this dish. I want a new dish with yummy food." Tim wanted to help the cat, so he went to find some food. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find any food for the grumpy cat. The cat stayed grumpy and hungry on the dish. Tim felt sad that he could not help the cat. They both had a bad day.

One day, a boy named Tim was eager to build something. He wanted to make a big box to keep his treasure safe. His treasure was his favorite toys and a shiny rock he found in the park. Tim asked his dad, "Can you help me build a box for my treasure?" His dad said, "Yes, but first we need to find some wood and nails." They went to the store and got what they needed. Tim felt excited as they walked back home. At home, Tim and his dad started to build the box. They cut the wood and nailed it together. While they were working, Tim's little sister came and asked, "What are you making?" Tim said, "A box for my treasure!" When the box was done, Tim put his toys and shiny rock inside. He was very happy to have a safe place for his treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. He was a happy bird, and all his friends liked to hear him whistle. One day, Tim met a big, frightened cat named Max. Max was scared because he had been lost for a week. Tim wanted to help Max find his way home. So, they set off on a journey together. As they walked, Tim whistled a happy tune to cheer up Max. But, Tim did not see the danger ahead. A big, mean dog heard Tim's whistle and came running. The dog chased Tim and Max. They tried to run away, but the dog caught up to them. Sadly, Tim and Max did not make it home. The moral of the story is to always be careful and watch out for danger, even when trying to help others.

One day, a big, powerful dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a soft wrap on the ground. Max picked it up with his mouth and carried it home. He did not want to disturb anyone, so he walked very quietly. When Max got home, he saw his friend, a little cat named Daisy. He showed her the wrap. Daisy said, "Wow, that's a pretty wrap! Let's play with it!" Max nodded and they began to play together. As they played, they made sure not to disturb anyone. They took turns wrapping each other in the soft wrap. They had so much fun and played all day long. At the end of the day, they were tired but happy. They knew they had found a special wrap that made their day great.

One day, a little dog named Max was walking near a big palace. The palace was very pretty and had many rooms. Max liked to play in the wet grass outside the palace. He loved to run and jump in the water puddles. Max met a new friend, a cat named Luna. Luna lived in the palace. She liked to record her voice by talking into a small toy. "Max, want to play?" Luna asked. Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, let's play in the wet grass!" They played for a long time in the wet grass. Max and Luna were both very wet and tired. They went back to the palace to rest. Luna said, "Max, I want to show you something fun." She took out her toy and played the recording of her voice. Max was surprised and happy to hear Luna's voice from the toy. They laughed and played with the toy for the rest of the day.
