Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Drum

One day, a boy named Tim found a drum in his room. He was very happy to play with the drum. He hit the drum with his hands and made loud sounds. Tim's friend, Sam, came over to play too. They both played the drum and had fun. Sam said, "Let's talk to the drum and see if it talks back." Tim thought it was a funny idea. They both talked to the drum, but the drum did not talk back. They felt a little sad, but they kept playing. After a while, the drum started to talk! It said, "Hello, Tim and Sam! Thank you for playing with me. I am a magic drum!" Tim and Sam were very surprised. They asked the drum to help them be calm and quiet. The drum played soft music, and they all had a calm and happy day together.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, beautiful yard with a long fence. She loved to play there with her dog, Buddy. One day, Mia found a long, flexible stick in her yard. She thought it was very fun to play with. Mia and Buddy played with the stick near the fence. They threw it, caught it, and had lots of fun. But then, the stick got stuck in the fence. Mia was sad. She tried to get the stick out, but it was too hard. Buddy saw Mia was sad and wanted to help. He used his strong teeth to pull the stick out of the fence. Mia was so happy! She hugged Buddy and said, "Thank you, Buddy! We own the best stick ever!" And they played happily ever after.

Tim was a chubby boy who liked to play with his fireman toy. He had a red hat, a yellow coat and a big hose. He pretended to put out fires in his backyard with his hose. He liked to make siren noises and say "Fireman Tim to the rescue!" One day, he saw a real fireman truck on his street. It was shiny and loud and had a ladder. Tim ran to the sidewalk to see it better. He waved at the firemen who got out of the truck. They smiled and waved back. They said they were here to check the fire hydrant. Tim asked if he could see the truck. The firemen said yes and let him climb inside. Tim was so happy. He touched the buttons, the steering wheel and the horn. He put on a fireman helmet and pretended to drive. He felt like a real fireman. The firemen finished their work and said it was time to go. They thanked Tim for being a good helper and gave him a sticker. Tim thanked them for letting him see the truck. He got out of the truck and hugged the firemen. They said goodbye and drove away. Tim ran back to his backyard with his sticker and his toy. He stuck the sticker on his hat and played with his hose. He looked at the shiny truck as it disappeared. He said "Fireman Tim is the best!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play dress up. He had a big box of costumes. He had a costume of a cat, a dog, and a superhero. He liked to put on the costumes and pretend to be the animals or the superhero. One day, Tim wanted to seat on his chair, but his cat costume was on it. He tried to move the costume, but it was very persistent and would not move. Tim was sad. He wanted to sit on his chair and eat his snack. Tim asked his mom for help. His mom came and moved the persistent costume. Tim was happy. He sat on his chair and ate his snack. Tim learned that sometimes it's okay to ask for help when things are hard. From that day on, Tim and his costumes lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to his friend's house. His friend, Sam, was a loyal friend. They liked to play with toys and eat dessert together. "Can you lend me your toy car?" asked Tim. Sam said, "Yes, you can play with my toy car." They played with the toy car and had lots of fun. After playing, Sam's mom gave them some dessert. It was yummy ice cream. Tim and Sam ate the ice cream and were very happy. They knew they would always be loyal friends who shared toys and dessert.
