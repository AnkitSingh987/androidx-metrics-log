Category: Intermediate
Title: The Event and the Door

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big event. The event had fun games, yummy food, and lots of kids. A smart dog named Max lived in this town. Max loved to play with the kids at the event. One day, Max saw a door that was not closed. He knew that if the door stayed open, the event would not be fun. So, Max ran to find a grown-up to help close the door. He barked and wagged his tail to show the grown-up the open door. The grown-up saw the open door and closed it. The event was saved and all the kids were happy. They played games and ate yummy food. Max was a hero! Everyone thanked Max for being such a smart dog and helping to keep the event fun.

One day, a big cat with a sharp mouth met a small mouse. The cat said, "I am very hungry, and I will eat you now." The mouse was so scared and thought this was terrible. The cat opened his mouth wide to eat the mouse. But then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running, and he barked at the cat. The cat was now scared of the big dog. The mouse saw this and said, "Please, big dog, help me. The cat wants to eat me." The big dog said, "Don't worry, little mouse. I will help you." The dog barked louder, and the cat ran away, scared of the big dog. The mouse was so happy and thanked the big dog. They became good friends, and the cat never bothered the mouse again.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to find nuts in the big park. One day, Sam found a very big nut. It was the biggest nut he had ever seen! He wanted to take it to his home in the tree. Sam tried to pick up the nut, but it was too heavy. He felt helpless. He pushed and pushed, but it did not move. Then, Sam had an idea. He called his friend, Tim the turtle. Tim was very strong. Sam asked Tim to help him raise the nut off the ground. Tim came and helped Sam. Together, they raised the big nut and carried it to the tree. Sam was so happy. He thanked Tim for being a good friend. They shared the nut and had a great time. And from that day on, Sam and Tim became best friends forever.

Molly went on a walk in the park. She saw a funny looking squash. It was yellow and green. Molly wanted to pick it. She bent down and picked it up. When she looked closer she noticed that it was very icy. She could feel the cold through her fingers. Molly decided to take the squash home with her. She carefully carried it all the way home. Molly put the squash on the kitchen table. She looked at it for a long time before deciding to cut it open. Inside there were lots of small seeds - they were brown. Molly was happy. She had a big squash that was icy and cool. She couldn't wait to show her mum!

Once upon a time, there was a silly animal named Bobo. Bobo was a tall giraffe who loved to stand on his long legs. He lived in a big forest with many friends. One day, Bobo met a small animal named Toto. Toto was a tiny turtle who liked to walk very slow. Bobo said, "Hi Toto, want to play with me?" Toto looked up and said, "Yes, Bobo! Let's play together." They played a fun game called "Stand and Sit". Bobo would stand up tall, and Toto would sit down low. They laughed and had lots of fun. All the other animals in the forest saw them play and wanted to join. Soon, the whole forest was full of animals standing and sitting, being silly together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the garden. There are many flowers and trees in the garden. Some flowers have bulbs. Bulbs are round and brown. They hide under the dirt. One day, Tom and Lily find a bulb in the garden. They dig it out with a spoon. They look at it and wonder what it is. "Maybe it is a ball," Tom says. He throws the bulb in the air and tries to catch it. "Maybe it is a potato," Lily says. She pretends to eat the bulb and makes a funny face. They laugh and have fun with the bulb. They do not know that the bulb is a flower. A bee sees them and flies over. The bee knows that the bulb is a flower. The bee likes flowers. The bee is angry that Tom and Lily are playing with the bulb. The bee buzzes and says, "Stop! Stop! That is not a ball or a potato. That is a flower. You are silly. You are teasing the flower. Put it back in the dirt. Let it grow and bloom." Tom and Lily hear the bee and get scared. They drop the bulb and run away. They do not want to be stung by the bee. The bee picks up the bulb and flies away. The bee finds a good spot in the garden and plants the bulb. The bee hopes that the bulb will become a beautiful flower. Tom and Lily learn a lesson. They do not tease the bulbs anymore. They wait and watch them grow and bloom. They say sorry to the bee and the flower. They are not silly anymore. They are smart.
