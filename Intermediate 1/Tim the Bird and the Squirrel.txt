Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Squirrel

Once there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a small cage. He did not like the cage. He wanted to fly and play with his friends. One day, Tim's friend, a squirrel named Sam, came to visit. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you get out of the cage." Tim was very happy. He thought it was important to be free. Sam tried to open the cage, but it was too hard. Tim was still in the cage. Tim and Sam were very sad. They could not play together. The cage was still closed, and Tim was not free.

One day, a boy named Will went to play in the park. He liked to try new things. Will was an obedient boy, and he always listened to his mom and dad. Today, Will wanted to try to go on the big slide. He was a little scared, but he knew he could do it. Will climbed up the steps to the big slide. He sat down at the top and looked down. It was very high! Will took a deep breath and slid down. Whee! It was so much fun! He wanted to do it again. But when Will got to the bottom of the slide, something unexpected happened. Instead of landing on the ground, he landed on a big, soft cloud! The cloud took him up into the sky and showed him a whole new world of fun. Will had a great adventure on the cloud, and he knew that trying new things was always worth it.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the bakery with his mom. He saw a big, impressive muffin. Tim said, "Mom, I want that muffin, please!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. We can buy the muffin." They took the muffin home, and Tim was very happy. When they got home, Tim put the muffin on the table. He went to wash his hands. When he came back, the muffin was gone! Tim was sad and asked, "Mom, where is my muffin?" His mom looked around and saw their dog, Max, with muffin crumbs on his face. Max had eaten the muffin! Tim was sad, but then his mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can bake a new muffin together." Tim and his mom baked a new, even more impressive muffin. They shared it with Max, and they all enjoyed it together.

It was a nice day outside and Tom wanted to play. He saw his friend playing with a new exercise ball, and he asked if he could join in. "Yes, of course," his friend said. They started passing the ball back and forth. Soon, Tom noticed that the ball was getting bigger! "What's happening?" he asked his friend. "It's because you're passing the ball with so much energy," his friend replied. "The ball is getting bigger with every pass!" Tom was very excited. He kept passing the ball vigorously and soon enough, the ball was double its size! Tom and his friend had so much fun with their new, big exercise ball.

Once upon a time, there was a funny car. The funny car loved to play with kids. One day, the funny car went to the park to play with his friends. They played catch and ran around. They had so much fun. Soon, the funny car started to feel tired. The funny car said, "I guess I need gas." His friends went to find gas for him. They looked in the big red can, but there was no gas. They looked in the little blue can, but there was no gas. They looked everywhere, but they could not find gas. The funny car was very sad. His friends tried to make him happy, but the funny car could not play without gas. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The funny car's friends said goodbye and left him alone in the park. The funny car stayed there, feeling sad and lonely, because he had no gas.

One day, Ben and Lily went to the beach with Mom and Dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells. They saw a big crab walking on the rocks. Ben wanted to touch it, but Lily was fearful. She said, "No, Ben, it might pinch you!" Ben did not listen. He ran to the crab and reached out his hand. The crab saw Ben and was angry. It opened its claws and sprayed water at Ben's face. Ben was surprised and scared. He screamed and ran back to Lily. Mom and Dad heard Ben scream and came to see what happened. They saw the crab on the rocks and the water on Ben's face. They asked Ben if he was okay. Ben said, "Yes, but the crab sprayed me!" Mom and Dad hugged Ben and Lily and told them not to bother the crab. They said, "The crab was just protecting itself. It does not like to be touched by strangers. Let's leave it alone and find another place to play." Ben and Lily said sorry to the crab and walked away. They found a nice spot on the sand and made a castle with shells and seaweed. They had fun and forgot about the crab. They learned to respect the animals on the beach and not to be too curious.
