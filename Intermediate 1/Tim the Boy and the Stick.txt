Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stick

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a big stick on the ground. Tim was very happy and enthusiastic. He picked up the stick and started to play with it. He pretended it was a magic wand. Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw Tim with the stick and said, "Can I borrow your stick, please?" Tim said, "Yes, you can borrow my stick." Sue was very happy and they took turns playing with the stick. After a while, they got tired and put the stick down. They sat under a tree and talked about their fun day. They both loved playing with the stick and were glad they could share it. Tim and Sue promised to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived an elderly lady named Sue. Sue was very kind and loved to help her friends. She had a big, soft bed that she liked to rest on. One day, Sue heard a loud noise outside her house. She went to see what it was, and found a little bird who couldn't fly. The bird was very tired and needed to rest. Sue took the bird inside her house and let it rest on her big, soft bed. After a long sleep, the bird woke up feeling much better. The bird was very happy and thanked Sue for helping it rest. Sue was happy too, because she had made a new friend. From that day on, they played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Lily put on her soft bathrobe. She went outside to play in the yard. The sun was bright, and the grass was green. Lily liked to gaze at the sky and watch the birds fly by. She felt happy. As she was looking up, she saw a sweet little bird. The bird came down and said, "Hello, Lily!" Lily was very surprised. She did not know that birds could talk. "Hello, little bird," she said. The bird told Lily that it was lost and needed help to find its family. Lily wanted to help the bird, so she took off her bathrobe and made a soft bed for the bird to sit on. They went on an adventure to find the bird's family. In the end, they found the bird's family, and everyone was happy.

One day, a little girl named Jane went to the park. She saw a boy named Tom playing with a ball. Jane was glad because she wanted to play too. She walked up to Tom and asked, "Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play catch!" They threw the ball back and forth. They were having so much fun. Then, Tom threw the ball too high, and it got stuck in a tree. Jane and Tom were sad. A big man with a cut on his hand walked by. He saw the ball in the tree. He reached up and got the ball for Jane and Tom. They were so happy that they clapped their hands. Jane said, "Thank you!" The big man smiled and walked away. Jane and Tom played with the ball until it was time to go home.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to dress up. She had lots of clothes, but she was missing one thing - a shiny button! So the little girl went searching around her house. She looked and looked, but every drawer she opened was empty. Where could the button be? The little girl searched and searched, and soon she found the button! It was hiding under her bed! The little girl was so excited, she quickly put on her clothes and then quickly fastened the shiny button. She looked in the mirror, and she couldn't help but smile. The button was perfect! And the little girl never stopped searching for more buttons, always ready for her next dress-up adventure!

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a curious little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop around and explore new places. One sunny day, he decided to go on an adventure and see what he could find. As he hopped along, Ben came across a big tree. He looked up and saw a friendly bird named Tina. "Hello!" said Ben. "I'm Ben the bunny. Nice to meet you!" Tina smiled with her beak and said, "Hi Ben! I'm Tina the bird. Welcome to my tree!" Ben was very happy to have a new friend. They played together all day long. Ben showed Tina how to hop and she showed him how to sing with his mouth. They both learned new things and had a lot of fun. When the sun went down, they said goodbye and went back to their homes. Ben couldn't wait for the next day to play with his new friend again.
