Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a dry path in the park. He wanted to walk on it. As Tim walked on the path, he met a girl named Sue. Sue was trying to balance on a log. "Watch me, Tim!" she said. Tim watched as Sue balanced on the log, and he clapped his hands. "Good job, Sue!" Tim said. Tim and Sue played on the dry path all day. They had lots of fun balancing on logs, jumping, and running. At the end of the day, Tim and Sue were very happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved going on adventures and exploring. She often went out to find new, exciting things. One day, Lily decided to go on a special search. She wanted to look for something healthy and she was determined to find it. Lily talked to many people along the way. Some people shared their favorite healthy foods with her. Others recommended some exercises she could do to stay healthy. Finally, Lily reached the end of her search and she was surprised by what she found. She found out that the most important way to keep healthy was to stay active, eat healthy foods and get plenty of rest. Lily’s special search was a success. She had found what she was looking for. This was a very important lesson for Lily, as it taught her that taking care of her health is important.

One day, a big cat and a small dog had a race. The cat was very fast, but the dog was smart. The cat said, "I will win because I am big and fast!" The dog just smiled and said, "We will see." The race started and the cat ran very fast. The dog had a plan. He saw a big bag of food on the side of the road. The cat loved food and was a spoiled cat. The dog put the food on his shoulder and ran to the finish line. The cat saw the food and stopped to eat it. He forgot about the race. The dog won the race and everyone was happy. The cat learned that being spoiled and fast is not always the best way to win.

Once upon a time, there was a lamp. The lamp was very lucky. It lived in a nice room with a big window. The sun would shine in every day, and the lamp would be happy. One day, the lamp saw a little boy. The boy liked to run and play. He would not stop. The lamp liked to watch the boy run and play. They became good friends. One day, the boy was running too fast. He almost hit the lucky lamp! But the lamp said, "Stop!" The boy stopped just in time. The lamp and the boy were safe. They were both very happy and continued to be the best of friends.

Sara and Ben are twins. They like to play outside with their toys. But today, it is raining a lot. The rain makes big puddles on the ground. Sara and Ben cannot play outside. "Mom, can we play inside?" Sara asks. "Yes, you can, but you have to be tidy. Do not make a mess with your toys," Mom says. Sara and Ben go to their room. They take out their blocks, their cars, their dolls, and their books. They build a tower, a road, a house, and a library. They have a lot of fun. But then, they hear a loud noise. They look out the window. The rain has made a flood. The water is very high. It covers the street, the cars, the trees, and the houses. "Mom, look! There is a flood!" Ben shouts. Mom comes to their room. She sees the flood. She is scared. "Quick, we have to go upstairs. The water is coming inside," Mom says. Sara and Ben grab their toys. They follow Mom upstairs. They go to the attic. They are safe there. "Mom, are we going to be okay?" Sara asks. "Yes, we are, my loves. The rain will stop soon. The water will go away. We will be okay," Mom says. She hugs Sara and Ben. They hug her back. They wait for the rain to stop. They are glad they have each other. They are glad they were tidy.
