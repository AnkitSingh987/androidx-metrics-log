Category: Intermediate
Title: The Green Bird Found Key and the Door

One day, a green bird found a key. The key was shiny and pretty. The bird liked the key a lot. It took the key in its beak and flew away. The bird saw a small house with a door. The door was open a little bit. The bird went inside the house. It saw a box with a hole for the key. The bird put the key in the hole and turned it. The box opened and there was a toy inside. The bird played with the toy and had fun. Then, it was time to go. The bird shut the door of the house and flew away with the key. The green bird was very happy to have found the key and the toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern office. In this office, there were many people who worked together. They had a big box to unpack. Inside the box were new toys for the office. One day, two friends, Tom and Sue, wanted to unpack the box. But they both wanted to do it alone. They started to argue about who should unpack the box. This made everyone in the office sad. Then, their boss came and said, "Why don't you both unpack the box together?" Tom and Sue thought about it and agreed. They worked together and unpacked the box. Everyone in the office was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Amy. Amy loved the Earth and always tried to behave well. She played with her friends, shared her toys, and helped her mom and dad at home. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big tree that had fallen down. Amy felt sad for the tree and the Earth. She wanted to do something kind to help. Amy had an idea! She asked her mom to buy some seeds. They planted the seeds near the fallen tree. After some time, a twist happened! The seeds did not grow into a tree, but into a big, beautiful flower garden! The Earth was happy, and Amy learned that even small acts of kindness can make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a big red ball. The big red ball lived in a park in the east. Every day, the big red ball would play with the kids who came to the park. The big red ball loved to play with the kids and make them happy. One day, a new kid came to the park. The new kid did not understand how to play with the big red ball. The big red ball tried to show the new kid how to play, but the new kid still did not understand. The big red ball was sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A small blue ball came to the park. The small blue ball showed the new kid how to play with the big red ball. The new kid understood and started to play with the big red ball. The big red ball was happy again. The moral of the story is that sometimes, we need a little help to understand and enjoy new things.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom and dad around the house. One day, his mom asked him to help push a big box. Tim was excited to help, but he was not sure if he could do it. Tim's dad said, "You can do it, Tim! Just give it a big push!" Tim took a deep breath and pushed the box with all his might. The box started to move! Tim was so happy and proud of his success. Later that day, Tim's mom and dad gave him a surprise. It was a new toy car! Tim knew that his hard work had paid off. He played with his new toy car all day long, remembering how his thoughtful actions had made his mom and dad happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom went to the quiet shop. They wanted to buy a toy for Tim. When they got to the shop, they saw many toys. Tim saw a big red ball and wanted it. "Mom, can I have the red ball, please?" asked Tim. His mom said, "Yes, you can have it, but you must come with me to the other side of the shop first." Tim was so happy and followed his mom. But when they came back to get the red ball, it was gone! Tim was very sad. He looked around and saw another little boy holding the red ball. Tim's mom said, "It's okay, Tim. Let's find another toy for you." They looked around and found a blue ball. Tim was happy again, and they went home with the new toy.
