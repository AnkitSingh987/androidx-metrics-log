Category: Intermediate
Title: The Farm and the Horse

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On this farm, there were many animals. There was a cow, a pig, a horse, and a sheep. They all lived happily together. One day, the horse saw something incredible. A big pile of hay appeared in the middle of the farm. The horse said to the cow, "Look! There is new hay for us to eat! It appeared out of nowhere!" The cow and the horse were very excited. They ran to the hay and started to eat it. The pig and the sheep saw them and joined them too. As they ate the hay, the animals started to talk. They realized that they had never shared food before. They always ate their food alone. The animals felt happy eating together. They learned that sharing is good and makes everyone feel happy. From that day on, they always shared their food and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy dog named Max. Max liked to run and play all day. One day, Max saw a rail near a park. Max went to the rail to start a new game. Max started to walk on the rail, but he saw a big ball rolling fast. The ball was coming right at Max! Max jumped off the rail just in time. The ball rolled past Max, and he was safe. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball turned into a big, friendly cat! The cat said, "Hi, I'm Sam!" Max and Sam became best friends. They played on the rail and had fun all day.

Sara and Tom are friends. They love to play and watch movies together. One day, Sara wants to watch a new movie. She says, "Tom, let's watch the new movie. It has a princess and a dragon and a castle." But Tom does not want to watch the new movie. He wants to watch an old movie. He says, "No, Sara, let's watch the old movie. It has a cowboy and a horse and a hat." Sara and Tom start to argue. They say, "My movie is better. Your movie is boring. I don't want to watch your movie. You don't want to watch my movie." They are not happy. They are not friends anymore. Then, Mom comes and sees them. She says, "Sara and Tom, why are you arguing? Movies are for fun. You can watch both movies. You can take turns. You can share. You can learn from each other. That is what friends do." Sara and Tom think about what Mom says. They say, "You are right, Mom. We are sorry. We can watch both movies. We can take turns. We can share. We can learn from each other. That is what friends do." They hug and smile. They are happy. They are friends again. They watch the new movie and the old movie. They enjoy both movies. They love both movies. They love each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. His name was Tim. He liked to go for walks in the woods. One day, he found a twig. He was very grateful! He picked it up and thanked it for being so nice. He took it home and put it in his room. He wanted to keep it forever. Every day, he thanked the twig for being so special. Then, one day, the twig started to glow! Tim was so happy and he kept thanking the twig for bringing him joy. From that day on, whenever he felt grateful, he thanked the twig for reminding him of all the wonderful things in his life.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was an incredible fountain. The fountain had water that went up and down. It made pretty sounds. Kids liked to play near the fountain. One sunny day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Mia went to the park. They saw the fountain and ran to it. They laughed and splashed water on each other. They were very happy. "Look, Mia! The water goes so high!" said Tim. Mia laughed and said, "Yes, Tim! It's so fun!" They played near the fountain all day. The sun went down and it was time to go home. They waved goodbye to the fountain and went home with big smiles.
