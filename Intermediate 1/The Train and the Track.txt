Category: Intermediate
Title: The Train and the Track

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train had an interesting track to go on. The train really wanted to go on the track. The train started to move. It went over hills and through tunnels. The train saw many interesting things on the track. It saw big trees, small houses, and pretty flowers. At the end of the track, the train found a big party. All the train's friends were there. The train was very happy. It wanted to go on the interesting track every day.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. One day, he found a hidden box under his bed. He was very excited to see what was inside. When Tim opened the box, he found a lot of powder. He thought it was magic powder. Tim called his friend, Sam, to come and see the magic powder. Sam came running to Tim's house to see the hidden box and the magic powder. Tim said, "Let's use the magic powder to support our toy cars and make them go faster!" Sam agreed and they both sprinkled the powder on their toy cars. They raced their cars around the room, laughing and having fun. They played together all day, and the magic powder made their friendship even stronger.

One day, a funny bunny named Bob went on a walk. He hopped and skipped in the sun. He was very happy. Bob met a friend, a small cat named Tim. Tim said, "Hi, Bob! Look what I have!" Tim put his paw in his pocket and revealed a big, red ball. Bob and Tim played with the ball. They laughed and had fun. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. "Bye, Tim!" said Bob. "That was fun!" They both smiled and waved goodbye.

---

One day, a big, angry dog was in the park. He had a frown on his face. A little girl named Amy saw the dog and wanted to pet him. Her mom said, "No, Amy. That dog looks angry. We should stay away." Amy was sad but listened to her mom. They walked to a different part of the park. Suddenly, they saw a small cat. The cat was on a high point, stuck in a tree. Amy asked, "Mom, can we help the cat?" Her mom said, "Yes, let's try." They tried to reach the cat, but they were too short. The angry dog saw them and walked over. To their surprise, the dog was not angry anymore. He wanted to help the cat too. The dog stood on his back legs and lifted Amy up. With the dog's help, Amy could reach the cat. She took the cat down from the tree. The cat was happy, and the dog was happy too. They all became friends and played together in the park.

Mia and Ben were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to run, slide, swing and climb. They also liked to wave at the people and animals they saw. They waved at the dogs, the birds, the cars and the bikes. One day, they saw a big yellow balloon in the sky. It looked like a giant ball of sunshine. They wanted to wave at it too. They ran to the edge of the park and shouted, "Hello, sunshine balloon! We like you!" But the balloon did not wave back. It did not say anything. It just floated away. Mia and Ben felt sad. They wondered why the balloon did not want to be their friend. Then they heard a voice behind them. It was a man with a funny hat and a big basket. He said, "Hi, kids! That's my balloon. I'm going for a ride in the sky. Do you want to come with me?" Mia and Ben looked at each other. They were surprised and curious. They asked their mom if they could go. Their mom said yes, but only if they held her hand and wore a seat belt. They climbed into the basket with the man. He turned on a fire that made the balloon go up. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They saw the park, the houses, the trees and the river from above. They waved at everything they saw. They also saw the balloon's shadow on the ground. It looked like a big smile. Mia and Ben realized that the balloon was waving at them after all. They felt happy and silly. They laughed and said, "Sorry, sunshine balloon! We did not see your wave. We like you too!" The man smiled and said, "That's okay, kids. The balloon likes you too. It's just a little shy. Sometimes it gets embarrassed when people wave at it. But it's very friendly and kind. It likes to share its sunshine with everyone." Mia and Ben nodded and said, "We understand. We sometimes get embarrassed too. But we also like to share our sunshine with everyone. Thank you for taking us for a ride, mister. You are very nice." The man said, "You're welcome, kids. You are very nice too. And very brave. Not everyone likes to fly in a balloon. But you do. You are very special." They hugged the man and thanked him again. Then they landed back in the park and said goodbye to the balloon. They waved at it one last time and said, "See you soon, sunshine balloon! We hope you have a good day!" The balloon waved back and said, "See you soon, Mia and Ben! I hope you have a good day too!"
