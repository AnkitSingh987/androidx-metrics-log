Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Tom and the Grandma

Lily and Tom were very happy. Today, Grandma would arrive and stay with them for a week. Grandma always told them great stories and baked them yummy cookies. Lily and Tom helped Mom clean the house and make the beds for Grandma. Soon, they heard a knock on the door. It was Grandma! Lily and Tom ran to hug her and say hello. Grandma smiled and gave them each a kiss. She had a big bag with her. She said, "I brought you some presents, my dear ones. Let's go inside and see what they are." Mom helped Grandma carry her bag to the guest room. Lily and Tom followed them, curious and excited. Grandma opened her bag and took out two books, one for Lily and one for Tom. They were colorful books with pictures and words. Grandma said, "These are books for you to read and learn. I will read them to you every night before bed. Do you like them?" Lily and Tom nodded and thanked Grandma. They loved books. Grandma also took out a box of cookie dough. She said, "This is for us to bake together. Do you want to help me make some cookies for snack?" Lily and Tom clapped and said yes. They loved cookies. Grandma said, "All right, then. Let's go to the kitchen and turn on the stove. We will have some fun and make the house smell good." Lily and Tom followed Grandma to the kitchen. They helped her roll the dough and cut out shapes. They put the shapes on a tray and Grandma put the tray in the stove. They waited for a few minutes, then Grandma took out the tray. The cookies were golden and hot. Grandma said, "Be careful, they are very hot. Let them cool down a bit, then we can eat them." Lily and Tom waited patiently, then Grandma gave them each a cookie. They bit into them and smiled. They were delicious. Grandma said, "You did a great job, Lily and Tom. You are very good helpers. I'm so proud of you." Lily and Tom hugged Grandma and said, "We love you, Grandma. You are the best." Grandma hugged them back and said, "I love you, too, my dear ones. You are the best, too."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a bit weird because he loved to turn around and around all day. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw his friend, Sam. Sam asked, "Tim, why do you like to turn so much?" Tim smiled and said, "I don't know. It's just fun!" So, Sam tried to turn too. They both turned and turned until they felt dizzy and fell on the soft grass, laughing. After a while, more friends came to play. They all saw Tim and Sam turning and thought it looked fun. Soon, all the kids were turning and laughing together. Even though Tim was a little weird, his friends loved playing with him, and they all had a great day at the park.

Annie's dad was in the garage, looking for something. All Annie could see was a chain and she immediately wanted it. "Daddy, can I have the chain?" she asked, pointing to the shiny object. "This chain is not a toy, Annie," her dad said. "It's very strong and can be used to fix things." Annie thought for a moment and then said, "What if I promise to stay away from it? Can I have it?" Her dad laughed, then said, "That's a great idea, Annie! I can see you are very flexible." He handed her the chain and said, "You can have the chain, but you must promise to stay away from it, ok?" Annie said yes and her dad showed her where to put the chain to keep it safe. Annie was so happy, she couldn't wait to show her friends her new chain!

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy stamp. One day, Max's toy stamp rolled under a big chair. Max tried to get the stamp, but he could not reach it. Max's friend, Lily the cat, came to see what was wrong. Max said, "My stamp is under the chair. I cannot get it." Lily told Max to listen to her plan. "Max, I am small. I can go under the chair and get your stamp." Max was stubborn, but he listened to Lily. Lily went under the chair and got Max's toy stamp. Max was so happy and thanked Lily. From that day on, Max learned to listen to his friends and not be so stubborn. Max and Lily played together with the toy stamp every day.
