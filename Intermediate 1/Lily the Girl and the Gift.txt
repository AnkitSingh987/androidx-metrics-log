Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to draw with her pastel crayons. They were smooth and made pretty colors on the paper. Lily liked to draw big suns, little houses, and happy trees. One day, Lily made a very nice picture for her friend, Tom. She wanted to give it to him as a gift. So, Lily got some paper to wrap the picture. She put the picture in the middle of the paper and folded it around the picture. Then, she used a little bit of tape to hold the paper together. Now, the picture was wrapped and ready to give to Tom. Lily went to Tom's house and gave him the wrapped picture. Tom was very happy to get a gift. He opened the paper and saw the pretty drawing Lily made. Tom said, "Thank you, Lily! I love it!" They both smiled and played together, and from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a small squirrel named Bob. Bob loved to eat nuts. One gloomy day, Bob found a big, tasty nut under a tree. He was so happy! Bob said to himself, "I should bury this nut in the ground, so I can save it for later." He dug a small hole and put the nut inside. Then, he covered the hole with dirt. "There! Now my nut is safe." Later that day, Bob went back to the spot where he buried the nut. But, surprise! The nut was gone! Instead, there was a small plant growing in the hole. Bob was confused. He asked his friend, the bird, "Where is my nut?" The bird said, "Your nut is now a baby tree!" Bob was amazed. He never thought that burying a nut could make a new tree. From that day on, Bob and the bird took care of the baby tree, and they became the best of friends.

Lily and Ben were twins. They loved their dog, Spot. Spot was big and brown and very friendly. He liked to play with them and cuddle with them. He also liked to eat a lot. One day, it was Spot's birthday. Lily and Ben wanted to give him a present. They thought hard about what he would like. They decided to make him a cake. They asked their mom for help. She gave them a bowl, some flour, some eggs, some milk and some honey. She also gave them a bone to put on top of the cake. Lily and Ben mixed everything in the bowl. They poured the batter into a pan. They put the pan in the oven. They waited for the cake to bake. They smelled the sweet honey. They hoped Spot would like it. When the cake was ready, they took it out of the oven. They let it cool down. They put the bone on top of it. They wrapped it in a big red ribbon. They carried it to the backyard. They looked for Spot. They found him under a tree. He was sleeping. They woke him up. They said, "Happy birthday, Spot! We have a present for you!" They showed him the cake. Spot's eyes opened wide. He wagged his tail. He licked their faces. He barked happily. He ate the cake in big bites. He crunched the bone. He licked the plate clean. He looked at Lily and Ben. He smiled. He snuggled with them. He thanked them with his eyes. Lily and Ben hugged him. They said, "We love you, Spot! You are the best dog ever!" They thought it was the perfect day. Spot thought so too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play by the river near her house. One day, as Lucy was playing, she saw a big fish in the river. She asked her mom if she could borrow it. But her mom said 'No, Lucy. You can't borrow the fish.' Lucy tried to beg and plead, but her mom only said 'No'. Lucy was sad and didn't know what to do. Suddenly, she noticed a bright pink fish swimming in the river. Knowing that she couldn't borrow the first fish, she asked her mom if she could borrow this one. Her mom said 'Yes!' Lucy was very happy, and she thanked her mom for letting her borrow the pink fish. She put the fish carefully in a bucket, and she took it home and played with it all day.
