Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Cake

One day, a dog named Spot went out to play. He saw a little girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she could not make a cake. Spot wanted to help Sue. Spot said, "I can help you make a cake." Sue was happy and said, "Thank you, Spot!" They went to Sue's house and started to make the cake. They mixed flour, water, and sugar. But, they did not have any sweet sugar left. They only had bitter sugar. Spot said, "Let's use the bitter sugar." Sue said, "Okay, let's try it." They put the bitter sugar in the cake and baked it. When the cake was done, they tasted it. The cake was not sweet, but they liked it. They were happy they made the cake together.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a red wagon in her backyard. She was very happy and wanted to play with it. She pulled the wagon with a big smile on her face, but the wheels made a crazy noise. Sue did not know what to do. Sue saw her friend Tom and asked him, "Tom, my wagon makes a crazy noise. What should I do?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Let's sing a song to make the noise go away." So, Sue and Tom sang a happy song together while they pulled the wagon. The crazy noise was still there, but they didn't mind it anymore. After playing all day, Sue and Tom learned that sometimes things are not perfect, but if you have a friend and stay happy, you can still have fun. They went home feeling tired but happy. The moral of the story is that friends can help make things better and having fun is more important than being perfect.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to store his toys in a big box. One day, he found a long stick. It looked like a spear. He was very happy and played with it all day. Tim ran around with his spear. He was very dizzy. He spun and spun until he fell down. Tim was not happy. He wanted to play more with his spear. Tim got up and ran with his spear again. He was still dizzy. He did not see the big rock. Tim tripped and fell on his spear. It broke into two pieces. Tim was sad. His new toy was now gone.

Once upon a time there was a wise fox who wanted to do something special. He decided to sign a big stack of papers. Everyone was very curious what he was doing. He took out a pen and carefully signed each paper. When he finished, the stack was nearly as tall as he was! The fox was very proud of his work. He grabbed the stack of papers and put them on the ground. With a smile, he said, "This will help me do something special!" Then he took the stack and carefully placed it in the corner of the room. But none of his friends knew what the fox was up to. So the wise fox had to keep it a secret. One day, the fox went to the stack of papers and made a big surprise. He got out a marker and started drawing on each page of the stack. He made all sorts of funny shapes and designs. When he finished, all his friends were amazed. They asked him what he was trying to do. The fox whispered, "I wanted to make something special. Now everyone can see how wise I am!" The fox had done something magical. Everyone was so happy that the wise fox had been able to sign the stack of papers and turn it into something special. From then on, the fox was known for his wisdom and special skills.

One day, a smart swan named Sam went to the pond. He wanted to learn how to swim fast. His friend, a small fish named Fin, was very fast in the water. Sam asked Fin, "Can you teach me to swim fast?" Fin said, "Yes, I can help you. But first, you must learn to kick your legs hard." Sam tried to kick his legs, but it was not easy. He kept trying and trying. Soon, he got better at it. One day, a big race was going to happen at the pond. All the animals were going to swim fast. Sam was scared, but he remembered what Fin taught him. He kicked his legs hard and swam very fast. In the end, Sam won the race! All his friends were happy for him. Sam was glad that he learned to swim fast with the help of his friend Fin.
