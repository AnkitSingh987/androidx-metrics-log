Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Treasure

Sara liked to play with her toy pirate ship. She had many pirates, but her favorite was Jack. Jack had a red hat, a hook and a big smile. Sara thought he was attractive. One day, Sara wanted to play a new game. She said to Jack, "Let's go find some treasure. Maybe we can gain some gold and jewels." Jack nodded and said, "Aye, aye, captain. Where should we sail?" Sara looked around and saw a big box in the corner. She said, "Let's go to that island. It looks mysterious." Sara and Jack sailed their ship to the box. They climbed out and opened the lid. Inside, they saw many shiny things. Sara was happy and said, "Wow, look at all this treasure. We are rich!" Jack was happy too and said, "Yes, we are. But we have to be careful. There might be other pirates who want to take our treasure." Sara and Jack did not notice that Sara's brother Tom was watching them. Tom was older and bigger than Sara. He liked to tease her and take her things. He saw the treasure and wanted it for himself. He said to himself, "I will wait until they are busy and then I will sneak up and grab the treasure. They will never know what happened." Tom waited until Sara and Jack were looking at a necklace and a ring. He tiptoed to the box and reached for the treasure. But he did not see that Jack had a sword. Jack saw Tom and said, "Hey, you. Stop. This is our treasure. Go away." He swung his sword and hit Tom's hand. Tom yelled and dropped the treasure. He ran away, crying. Sara and Jack cheered and hugged. They said, "We did it. We defended our treasure. We are brave pirates." They put the treasure back in the box and sailed back to their room. They were happy and proud. They had gained a lot of treasure and a lot of fun.

One day, a bright sun was up in the sky. A little girl named Lucy went out to play. She saw a big bird in her yard. The bird was very pretty and had many colors. Lucy said to the bird, "Hello, pretty bird! Can you send a letter for me?" The bird said, "Yes, I can! Where do you want me to send it?" Lucy said, "Please send it to the east, where my friend lives." The bird took the letter and flew away. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird came back with a letter for Lucy. The letter was from her friend in the east. Lucy was very happy and said, "Thank you, pretty bird!" The bird said, "You're welcome!" and flew away.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a fan that she loved very much. Mia would carry her fan everywhere she went. The fan was her best friend. One hot day, Mia and her fan went to the park. They played on the swings and had lots of fun. Mia was very alert and always looked around to make sure her fan was safe. She wanted her fan to be happy. As the sun went down, Mia and her fan went home. They were tired but happy. Mia knew that her fan helped her stay cool on this hot day. She gave her fan a big hug and said, "Thank you, fan, for being my friend!"

One day, a boy named Tim found a tough toy in a drawer. He wanted to play with it, but it was stuck. He pulled and pulled, but the tough toy would not come out. Tim was sad. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I found a toy, but it's stuck." His mom said, "Let me try to help you." Tim felt happy. Mom urged Tim to pull the drawer out more. Together, they pulled and pulled. At last, the tough toy came out. Tim and his mom played with the toy all day. They had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy pistol. He would run around the yard and pretend to be a hero. One day, Tim found a spray bottle in the house. He had a great idea. He filled the spray bottle with water and took it outside with his pistol. Tim pretended his pistol could spray water like the spray bottle. Tim played with his friends in the yard. They all had fun spraying each other with water. They laughed and ran around. Tim's healthy friends loved the new game. They played until the sun went down.
