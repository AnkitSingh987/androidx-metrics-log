Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Lively Boy and the Park

One day, a lively boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a seat and sat down. He wanted to make a new friend. He saw another boy named Sam. Sam was sad because he had no one to play with. Tom got up from his seat and went to Sam. He said, "Hi, I am Tom. Do you want to be my friend?" Sam smiled and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend." But then, another boy named Max came. Max wanted the seat. Max said, "That is my seat. I want to sit there." Tom and Sam looked at each other. They did not want to fight with Max. So, they said, "Okay, you can have the seat, but do you want to be our friend too?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend too." Now, they all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boat. The boat lived in a place called a harbor. The harbor was a safe place for boats to rest. One day, the sky was gloomy. The sun was hiding behind the clouds. The little boat wanted to make the harbor happy. So, it thought of a plan. The little boat asked its boat friends to help. They all worked together to make the harbor pretty. They put bright flowers and colorful flags all around. Soon, the harbor was happy and pretty, even on a gloomy day. And all the boats were happy too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a weak boy, but he was very kind. He had a big dog named Sam. Sam was strong and loved to play with Tim. They were best friends. One day, Tim and Sam went to the park. They saw a big sign that said, "No Dogs Allowed." Tim had a question. He asked his mom, "Can Sam play here?" His mom said, "No, the sign says dogs are not allowed." Tim was sad, but he knew he had to follow the rules. As Tim played, he saw a little girl who was stuck in a tree. She was scared and crying. Tim wanted to help but he was too weak. He had an idea! He called Sam over to help. Sam jumped up and helped the girl down from the tree. Everyone was so happy! The park people saw how good Sam was and decided to change the rule. They put up a new sign that said, "Dogs Allowed." From that day on, Sam and Tim played happily in the park together. The moral of the story is that sometimes, rules can be changed when we show kindness and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. Polly was a very filthy parrot. She lived in a dirty tree house. One day, Polly saw a big car zoom by her house. The car went very fast. Polly wanted to see who was in the car. She flew to the car and saw a nice man. The man saw Polly and said, "Hello, little parrot! You are very filthy. Would you like to be my friend?" Polly nodded her head and said, "Yes, I want to be your friend." The nice man took Polly home and gave her a bath. Now, Polly was not filthy anymore. Polly and the nice man became best friends. They would zoom around in the car together, having lots of fun. Polly was very happy to have a new friend.

The sun was shining and it was a beautiful day. Jody was excited to play in the sand. Gently, she scooped up the mighty sand and spread it out on the ground. Her eyes lit up when she saw the shape of a bucket and a shovel take form. Just then, a large cloud moved in front of the sun and the sky went dark. Jody gasped and frowned as the rain started to pour. She tried to gather the sand back into the bucket, but it seemed like the rain wanted to keep it. But then suddenly the cloud moved away and the sun came back out! Jody cheered and the rain stopped. She was able to gather back the sand into the bucket and she was soon playing again. Jody was mighty happy that the sun was back!
