Category: Intermediate
Title: Joe the Boy and the Bird

Once, there was a little boy named Joe. He liked to play, and he loved to dive. One day, he was outside in the yard when he heard a funny noise. It sounded like a tiny violin. Joe wanted to find out more, so he looked up into the sky. There he saw a bird, singing and playing its violin. Joe was so excited and clapped his hands. He asked the bird, "Can I dive and play too?" The bird smiled and said, "Of course! Just flap your wings and try." Joe smiled and followed the bird's instructions. He flapped his arms and jumped up in the air. He dived and spun around in circles. Joe had so much fun. The bird clapped and cheered as Joe flew around. He had never seen anything like it before! Joe was so happy and thanked the bird for the fun adventure.

Once upon a time, in a colorful farm, there lived a big turkey. The turkey had many friends. They liked to play and have fun together. One day, the turkey and his friends were playing hide and seek. The turkey found a big bush to hide behind. He was very quiet so no one would find him. But then, he heard a small voice. "Please don't disturb me," said a little bug. The turkey looked down and saw the bug under a leaf. "I am sorry," said the turkey. "I did not see you there." The bug smiled and said, "That's okay. We can be friends." So the turkey and the bug played hide and seek together with their friends. They all had a great time and became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark night. The moon was hiding behind the clouds. All the people went to sleep, but one little boy named Timmy was awake. Timmy was scared of the dark. He called out to his mommy, "Mommy, can you come here, please?" Timmy's mommy came to his room. "What is it, Timmy?" she asked. "I am scared of the dark," Timmy said. "Can you stay with me until I fall asleep?" His mommy smiled and said, "Of course, my dear." So Timmy's mommy stayed with him until he fell asleep. The dark night was not so scary anymore because Timmy knew his mommy was there to protect him. And they all slept happily through the night.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim lived on a street with many other animals. He liked to play with his friends, but sometimes he felt scared. One day, Tim saw a big dog. The dog barked loudly, and Tim began to tremble. He didn't know what to do, so he hid behind a tree. His friend, a little bird named Sue, saw him shaking. Sue asked, "Tim, why are you shaking?" Tim replied, "I am scared of the big dog." Sue said, "Don't worry, I will help you." So, Sue flew up high and talked to the big dog. The dog said he just wanted to play. Tim and the big dog became friends, and they played together on the street. Tim was not shy or scared anymore. He was very happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, a little toy boat lived in a bathtub. The boat loved to travel from one end of the tub to the other. It had so much fun playing with the bubbles and splashing in the water. One day, the water in the bathtub was very bad. It was dirty and not fun to play in. The boat did not want to travel in the bad water. So, it waited for the water to be clean and nice again. Soon, the water was clean and the boat was happy. It started to travel in the bathtub again. The boat played with the bubbles and splashed in the water. It was a fun day in the bathtub for the little toy boat.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big clock. The big clock was in the middle of the town square. It was very old and started to lean to one side. The people in the town were worried that the big clock would fall. One day, a little girl named Sue went to the town square. She saw the big clock and said, "Oh no! The big clock is leaning! We need to fix it!" Sue asked her friends, "Can you help me make the big clock stand straight?" Her friends said, "Yes, we will help you!" They tried to push the big clock to make it stand straight, but it was too heavy. Then, they asked the grown-ups for help. The grown-ups used big sticks to make the clock stand straight again. Everyone in the town was happy and safe. They all thanked Sue and her friends for their help. The moral of the story is that if you see a problem, you should ask for help to fix it. Working together can make things better.
