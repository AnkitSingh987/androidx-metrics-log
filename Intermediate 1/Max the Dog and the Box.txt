Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Box

One day, a little dog named Max found a big box. The box had a surprise inside. Max was very happy. He wanted to see what was inside the box. He tried to open it, but the box was closed very tight. So, Max decided to shake the box. When Max shook the box, he heard a bad sound. It was like something was breaking inside. Max was scared. He did not want to break the surprise. He stopped shaking the box and thought about what to do. Max asked his friend, a wise old cat, for help. The cat told Max that sometimes it is better to wait and ask for help than to try to do things on his own. Max learned that being patient and asking for help is important. He felt good about learning this lesson. And when they opened the box together, the surprise was not broken.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. Lily loved to wear her favorite dress. It was pink and had flowers on it. She also had a bike with pedals. She liked to ride her bike in the park every day. One day, Lily saw a boy named Tim who was sad. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I don't know how to ride a bike with pedals. I always fall down." Lily wanted to help Tim. So she said, "I can teach you how to ride a bike. It's easy and fun!" Lily showed Tim how to sit on the bike and use the pedals. Soon, Tim was riding the bike with Lily. They both had lots of fun! Tim said, "Thank you, Lily! Now I can ride a bike too!" Lily was happy that she could help her friend. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel happy too.

One day, a friendly dog named Max went for a walk. He was very excite to see his friend, a cat named Luna. Max and Luna liked to play with a ball in the park. They would throw the ball and run to catch it. They had a lot of fun together. As they played, Max threw the ball very far. Luna ran fast to catch it. But, the ball went over a big hill. "I will go get it!" said Luna. Max waited for Luna to come back with the ball. Luna went over the hill and saw a big surprise. There was a party with many friendly animals. They had cake, balloons, and games. Luna was so excite! She told Max to come see. "Max, come here! There is a big party!" Max ran over the hill and saw the party too. All the friendly animals let Max and Luna join the party. They played games and ate cake. Max and Luna even won a prize for a game. It was a new ball to play with! They were so happy. The day was even more fun than they thought it would be.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toy, a missile. He loved his missile very much. It was his favorite toy. He would throw it in the air and watch it fly. One day, Tim was playing with his missile in the park. He threw it high in the air, but it went too far. Tim ran to find it, but he could not see it anywhere. He felt very sad and started to weep. He did not want to lose his favorite toy. A girl named Sue saw Tim crying and asked him what was wrong. Tim told her about his lost missile. Sue said, "Don't be sad. I will help you find it." They looked everywhere and finally found the missile behind a bush. Tim was so happy and thanked Sue for her help. He thought he was stupid for throwing the missile too far, but now he had a new friend to play with. They played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet duck. The sweet duck lived by a pond. The sweet duck liked to swim and play in the water. One hot day, the sweet duck got out of the pond. The sweet duck was wet. The sweet duck wanted to be dry. The sun was warm and helped the sweet duck get dry. When the sweet duck was dry, it went back to the pond. The sweet duck was happy to swim and play again. The sweet duck had a fun day at the pond.
