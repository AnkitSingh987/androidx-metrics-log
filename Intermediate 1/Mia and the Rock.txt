Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia and the Rock

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia lived in a small house. She had a pretty cloth that she loved to play with. One day, Mia went outside to play with her cloth. While playing, Mia saw a big rock. She thought it would be fun to lay her cloth on the rock. But when she tried to lay the cloth, she fell and hurt her leg. Mia started to cry because it hurt a lot. Mia's mom heard her cry and came to help. But she could not make the hurt go away. Mia was very sad and could not play with her cloth anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a cool faucet. The faucet was sad because no one would play with him. He would cry every day, making a big puddle on the ground. One day, a little girl named Lucy saw the faucet crying. She walked up to him and said, "Why are you crying, Mr. Faucet?" The faucet replied, "I am sad because no one wants to play with me." Lucy felt bad for the faucet and said, "I will play with you!" She turned the faucet on and off, making water come out. They played together all day, splashing and laughing. The faucet was happy and didn't cry anymore. And from that day on, Lucy and the cool faucet were the best of friends.

One day, a sweet cat named Lily went to school. She was very excited because today she would learn how to write on the big blackboard. Lily sat on her chair and waited for her teacher, Mr. Owl. Mr. Owl said, "Today we will learn to write on the blackboard. Who wants to try first?" Lily's paw rushed into the air. "Me, me, me!" she said. Mr. Owl smiled and said, "Okay, Lily, come to the front." Lily walked to the blackboard and picked up a white stick. She wrote her name with a big smile. All her friends clapped and cheered for her. "Good job, Lily!" said Mr. Owl. Lily felt very happy and proud.

One sunny day, Tom and his loyal dog, Spot, decided to have a picnic in the park. They brought a big basket with yummy food like apples, cookies, and sandwiches. They found a nice spot under a big tree and started to eat. While they were eating, a rabbit came up to them and said, "Can I have some food too?" Tom and Spot shared their food with the rabbit. They were all very happy and ate a lot. Suddenly, the rabbit said, "Watch this!" and it started to hop around really fast. In a second, the rabbit vanished! Tom and Spot looked at each other, surprised. They didn't know where the rabbit went. They searched everywhere, but the rabbit was gone. Tom and Spot finished their picnic, still wondering where their new friend had gone.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob loved to dance. He would dance in the sun, in the rain, and even in the snow. One day, while Bob was dancing, he heard a loud sound. It was a little bird named Tim singing a song. Bob went to Tim and asked, "Can you show me how to sing that song?" Tim was happy to help. He showed Bob how to sing the song loud and clear. They sang and danced together all day long, having lots of fun. Soon, other animals heard the loud singing and dancing. They came to join Bob and Tim. They all danced and sang together, making the forest a very happy place. And from that day on, the animals would meet every day to dance and sing their favorite loud song.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. He saw a ball and wanted to kick it. He ran to the ball and gave it a big kick. The ball flew high in the sky and landed far away. Tim went to find the ball. On his way, he saw a cold ice cream truck. He stopped and got a cold ice cream. He liked the sweet, cold treat. It made him very happy. As Tim walked, he found a small, shiny thing on the ground. It was a razor. He knew it was not a toy, so he did not touch it. He told his mom about the razor, and she said he did a good job. Tim felt proud and went back to play with his ball.
