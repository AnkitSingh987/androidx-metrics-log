Category: Intermediate
Title: The Fire and the Rabbit

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there was a big fire. The fire was very bright and loud. All the animals were scared and ran away from the fire. They did not know what to do. But there was a brave rabbit who had an idea. The rabbit had seen people clap their hands to put out small fires before. So, he thought if all the animals could clap their paws, they might put out the big fire. He told all the animals his idea, and they all agreed to try it. The animals lined up in front of the fire and started to clap their paws together. They clapped and clapped, and the fire got smaller and smaller. Finally, the fire was all gone! All the animals were so happy and they all applauded the brave rabbit for his smart idea. The forest was safe again, and the animals could go back to their homes.

One day, a cool little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore a red shirt with a big crane on it. Tim loved cranes because they were big and strong. His mom zipped up his jacket, and they walked to the park together. At the park, Tim saw a big pile of sand. He thought, "This looks like a fun place to play!" Tim started to dig in the sand with his hands, making a big hole. He wished he had a real crane to help him dig faster. As Tim was playing, a man walked by with a toy crane. The man saw Tim's red shirt with the big crane on it and smiled. He said, "Hey, little boy, do you want to play with my toy crane?" Tim's eyes lit up, and he said, "Yes, please!" The man gave Tim the toy crane, and he played with it all day long. The cool little boy with the crane shirt had the best day ever at the park.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a jog in the park. Tim saw a big red telephone. It was a very old and reliable telephone. Tim thought it was special. Later, Tim and Spot saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. She lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue find her toy. They all looked around the park, but they could not find it. Tim remembered the big red telephone. He thought it could help them. They went back to the telephone and asked for help. The telephone told them where the toy was. Tim, Spot, and Sue found the toy under a bush. Sue was happy again. The big red telephone was very helpful and reliable.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat named Kitty. She loved to play with her red ball. One day, Kitty saw a big basket in the room. She was very curious about it. Kitty jumped into the basket and said, "This is fun!" She played with her red ball in the basket. She threw the ball up in the air, but she missed it. The ball rolled away from the basket. Kitty jumped out of the basket and ran after the ball. She found it near a big tree. She saw a little boy named Timmy. Timmy said, "Hi Kitty! I like your basket. Can I play with you?" Kitty and Timmy played with the ball and the basket all day long. They had so much fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a laser. The laser made a bright light. Tim liked to play with his laser all day. One day, Tim's mommy said the light was too harsh. She told Tim to remove the laser from his eyes. Tim did what his mommy said because he loved her very much. Tim learned to play with his laser in a safe way. He played with his friends and they had lots of fun. Tim was happy and his mommy was happy too. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a strong boy named Tom went to the store with his mom. They needed to buy food for their kitchen. Tom liked to help his mom. He carried a big bag for the food. At the store, Tom saw his friend Sue. Sue said, "Hi Tom! I am here with my dad." Tom said, "Hi Sue! I am helping my mom." They both smiled and went to find their moms and dads. But then, something unexpected happened. Tom and Sue found a big, lost dog in the store! The dog was hungry and looking for food. Tom and Sue decided to help the dog. They gave the dog some food from their bags. The dog was happy and wagged its tail. Tom and Sue felt good for helping the lost dog.
