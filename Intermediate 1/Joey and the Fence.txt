Category: Intermediate
Title: Joey and the Fence

Joey was a little boy who liked to play outside in the nice weather. One day, he decided to go for a walk. But when he opened the door, he couldn't open it again! It was locked! He had to be flexible, so he decided to jump over the fence. He made it over the fence, but he ran into another problem-- there were two locked doors. He got so frustrated that he started to cry. He had to find a way out! All of a sudden, he remembered the key in his pocket. He quickly put it in the locks, and then he was free. But unfortunately, Joey's bad luck was not over yet. By the time he made it back home, it was dark and the weather had turned cold and rainy. He was so tired, he had to go to bed without having any dinner. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little match. The match was very tired because it had been used a lot. One day, the match wanted to start a fire to help its friends. The friends were cold and needed to be warm. The match tried to start a fire, but it was too tired. It asked its friends, the little rocks, for help. The rocks said, "We can help you start the fire!" The match was very happy and thanked the rocks. Together, the match and the rocks started a fire. The fire was warm and made all the friends happy. They danced and played around the fire. The match was still tired, but it was happy to help its friends. Everyone had a great time and the match knew it had done a good job.

Once upon a time in a large sky, there was a planet. On this planet, there were two friends, a boy and a girl. They liked to play and grab things. One day, they found a big ball. The boy wanted to grab the ball and the girl wanted to grab it too. They both tried to grab the ball at the same time. They pulled and pulled, but the ball was very big. This made the boy and the girl sad. They did not want to fight over the ball. Then, the boy had an idea. He told the girl that they could both grab the ball together. They both held on to the ball and played with it. They rolled the ball and had lots of fun. The boy and the girl were happy again. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot was famous because he loved to test things. One day, Spot found a big piece of metal in his yard. He wanted to see what it could do. Spot pushed the metal with his paw. It made a loud sound. He was very happy to learn something new. Spot decided to show his friends what he found. He took the metal to his friend, a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy also tested the metal and made the loud sound. They both laughed and played with the metal all day long. Soon, all the animals in the town wanted to see the famous metal that Spot found. They all came to test it and make the loud sound. Everyone was happy and had fun playing with the metal. And Spot became even more famous for finding the special metal.

Once upon a time, in a great big garden, there lived a little boy named Tim. He loved to play in the garden all day. He liked to run, jump, and play with his ball. One day, while he was playing, his mom warned him, "Tim, be careful! Don't go too far away." Tim played and played, but he did not listen to his mom's warning. He went far away from his home. As he played, he saw something big and green. It moved very fast. Tim was scared. He thought it was a big, mean monster. But then, the big green thing stopped. Tim looked closer and saw that it was not a monster at all. It was a big, friendly dog! The dog wagged its tail and wanted to play. Tim was happy and played with the dog. They ran and jumped together in the garden. From that day on, Tim and the big green dog were the best of friends.
