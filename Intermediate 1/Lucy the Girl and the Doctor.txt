Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Doctor

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very fearful of needles. One day, she had to leave her house to go to the doctor. At the doctor's office, Lucy saw a needle. She felt scared and wanted to go home. But her mom told her, "Don't worry, it will be quick and easy." The doctor gave Lucy a small pinch with the needle. It didn't hurt much. Lucy was happy that it was over. She was not fearful of needles anymore. And they went back home.

Once upon a time, a bald man named Tom loved to do yoga. He would hold his body in different shapes every day. All his friends liked to watch him and learn. One day, a big wind came when Tom was doing yoga in the park. The wind blew his yoga mat away. Tom was sad, but he did not give up. He tried to hold his yoga pose without the mat. His friends helped him by holding his hands and feet. As they all held on to each other, the wind stopped. They saw that by helping each other, they could do anything. Tom was happy, and his friends were happy too. They learned that working together can make hard things easy.

Tim and Sam were brothers who lived on a farm. They liked to play with the animals and run in the fields. But today, it was raining and they were miserable. They could not go outside and they had nothing to do inside. "Can we go to the barn?" Tim asked his mom. "Maybe we can see the cows and the horses." Mom shook her head. "No, the barn is too wet and muddy. You might slip and hurt yourself. And the animals are not happy either. They want to go out and eat grass." Tim and Sam sighed. They looked out the window and saw the gray sky and the puddles. They wished the rain would stop. Then, they heard a loud noise. It sounded like thunder, but it was not. They ran to the window and saw a big truck. The truck had a long trailer with a picture of a circus on it. The truck was driving to the barn. "What is that?" Tim asked. "Let's go and see!" Sam said. They put on their boots and coats and ran to the barn. They saw the truck stop and open the trailer. Out came a man with a red hat and a big smile. He waved at them and said, "Hello, boys! We are the circus! We were on our way to the town, but the road was blocked by a fallen tree. So we asked your dad if we could stay here for a while. He said yes, and he let us use the barn. Do you want to see what we have?" Tim and Sam nodded. They were curious and excited. They followed the man into the barn. There, they saw amazing things. They saw a lion, a tiger, a monkey, a bear, and an elephant. They saw clowns, acrobats, jugglers, and magicians. They saw balloons, flags, and lights. The man said, "We are going to put on a show for you and your family. You can help us if you want. You can feed the animals, or dress up as clowns, or learn some tricks. What do you say?" Tim and Sam smiled. They forgot about the rain and their misery. They said, "Yes, please! We want to help! We want to be part of the circus!" They had the best day ever. They turned from bored boys into happy circus stars. They had fun and made new friends. They thanked the man and the circus for coming to their barn. They hoped they would see them again. They learned that even on a rainy day, you can find magic and joy.

Once upon a time, there was a big log. It was in a forest. The log was sad because it could not move. It wanted to play with the other animals. One day, the log met a kind bird. The bird was excited to help the log. The bird had a plan. It called all its friends to help. They all came to the log. They started to push the log. The log began to rise into the air. The log was so happy. It could finally play with its friends in the sky. But then, something unexpected happened. The log was not a log at all! It was a big, big bird with wings like a log. The big bird thanked its new friends for helping it. Now, they all could play together in the sky. The log-bird was excited and happy.
