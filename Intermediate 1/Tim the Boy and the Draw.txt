Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Draw

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very creative. He loved to draw and paint. One day, he met a new friend named Sue. Sue liked to play with dolls, but she did not know how to draw or paint. Tim wanted to teach Sue how to draw and paint. But Sue was scared. She thought she would not be good at it. Tim said, "Don't worry, I will help you." They tried to draw and paint together, but Sue was still scared. She did not want to try. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's draw and paint on your dolls!" Sue liked this idea. They started to draw and paint on the dolls. Sue was happy. She learned to draw and paint with the help of her friend Tim. They became best friends and had lots of fun being creative together.

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to ride his bike. He saw a big model car in the store. It was a gloomy day, and the sky was dark. Tom wanted to buy the model car, but he did not have enough money. Tom asked his mom for money, but she said no. He felt sad and went to his room. He thought of a plan to get the model car. Tom took his bike and rode to the store to take the model car without paying. But when Tom got to the store, a big man saw him. The man knew Tom was doing something bad. He stopped Tom and took the model car away. Tom was very sad and scared. He rode his bike home, crying. He did not get the model car, and his mom was angry with him.

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to act in plays at school. One day, he got a new suit to wear in the play. The suit was big and red. Tim was very happy. In the play, Tim was a gentle king. He had to be kind to everyone. Tim practiced his lines and his act every day. He wanted to be the best gentle king ever. On the day of the play, something unexpected happened. When Tim put on his suit, it was too big! It fell down when he walked. Everyone laughed. Tim felt sad. But then, his friend Sarah had an idea. She found a belt to hold up the suit. Tim put on the belt and the suit fit. He acted as the gentle king and everyone clapped. Tim was happy and thanked Sarah for her help.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat yogurt. His favorite was the original flavor. One day, Tim's mom needed help in the kitchen. She asked, "Tim, can you help me make yogurt?" Tim was happy to help his mom. They mixed milk and the original yogurt to make more. Tim stirred the mix with a big spoon. They put the mix in a bowl and waited for it to become yogurt. Tim was very excited. While they were waiting, a cat came into the kitchen. The cat was hungry and saw the bowl of mix. The cat jumped up and ate some of the mix! Tim and his mom saw the cat and laughed. They did not get mad. Instead, they shared the yogurt with the cat. The cat was happy, and Tim learned that it was good to share and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big city. In the city, there were many houses, people, and cars. The city was always busy with lots of noise. One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to join her friends at the park. She put on her shoes and ran outside. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. At the park, Lucy and her friends had a yummy picnic. They ate sandwiches, fruit, and cookies. They played games and laughed. Lucy was very happy to join her friends in the city. They all had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big tree with many limes in her backyard. Lily liked to pick the limes and share them with her friends. One dark day, Lily saw a big lime at the top of the tree. She wanted to get it, but it was too high. She tried and tried, but she could not reach it. Lily was sad. Then, her friend Tom came to help. Together, they managed to get a long stick. They used the stick to reach the big lime. Finally, they got the lime and shared it with their friends. Everyone was happy and had a great time.
