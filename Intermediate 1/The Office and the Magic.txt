Category: Intermediate
Title: The Office and the Magic

Once upon a time, there was a big office. In this office, there was a hidden seat. The seat was very special. It was magic. The office was full of people who liked to work and play. One day, a kind man named Tom found the hidden seat. He sat on it and felt very happy. He knew this seat was magic. He told his friends about the seat, but they did not believe him. They thought it was just a normal seat. Tom felt sad. Tom decided to share the seat with everyone in the office. He wanted them to feel the magic too. One by one, his friends sat on the seat and felt happy. They learned that sharing is good. From that day on, they all shared the magic seat and played together. They also shared their toys and food. They learned that sharing makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pink car. The pink car liked to drive very fast. One day, the pink car was driving on the floor in the big room. It was having so much fun. A little toy came to play with the pink car. They played together on the floor. The pink car showed the toy how to drive fast. The toy was happy, but it did not know how to drive fast. The pink car saw the toy was sad. It wanted to help the toy. So, the pink car drove slow with the toy. They drove together on the floor. The toy was happy again. The pink car learned that it is good to help friends. It is not always about being fast. It is about being kind and having fun together. And the pink car and the toy lived happily ever after.

One day, Sam the dog saw a huge box. He did not know what was in it. He called his friend, Tim the cat, to come and look. "What do you think is in the box?" Sam asked Tim. "I don't know," said Tim, "but it smells nice, like perfume." They both laughed and joked about what could be inside. Later, they heard a big noise from the box. They were scared but wanted to see what was inside. They opened the box and found a big toy. They played with the toy all day and remembered how they joked about the box.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long, flexible leather string. He wanted to use it to play with his friends. So, he took the leather string to the park where his friends were playing. "Look what I found!" Tim said to his friends. "Let's use it to jump rope!" They all agreed and started to play. They were having so much fun. Suddenly, as they were playing, the leather string began to move on its own! It twisted and turned like a snake. Tim and his friends were very surprised. "What's happening?" they asked each other. The leather string then stopped moving and went back to being a normal jump rope. They all laughed and continued to play, knowing they had a magical leather string.

Once upon a time there was a big deep white pillow. Every day a big bear would come to the pillow and take a nap. He would stretch out his furry arms and legs and lay down. Then he'd close his eyes and start snoring. Every time he was done napping, he felt warm, happy, and refreshed. One day the bear needed a special nap. He went to the pillow and it looked extra deep and extra white. He stretched out his furry arms and legs and lay down. Then he closed his eyes and began to snore. He napped longer than usual, but when he woke up, he felt so rested. The deep white pillow was magical! All the bear wanted to do was take another nap. He would visit the pillow every day, stretch out his furry arms and legs, and close his eyes. And then he'd be off to dreamland.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. He liked to play outside and find new things. One day, he went to the park to find something fun. At the park, Tim saw an elderly man sitting on a bench. The man looked sad. Tim went to the man and asked him why he was sad. The elderly man said he lost his hat and could not find it. Tim wanted to help the man find his hat. Tim looked everywhere in the park. He looked under the slides and behind the trees. Then, he found the hat on top of a swing! Tim ran to give the hat back to the elderly man. But when he got to the bench, the man was gone! Instead, Tim saw a big, happy dog wearing the same hat! The elderly man was a dog all along! Tim laughed and played with the dog, happy to have a new friend.
