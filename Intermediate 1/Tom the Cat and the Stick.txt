Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Stick

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very troubled because he could not find his toy. He looked all around his house, but he could not see it anywhere. One day, Tom went to a party with his friends. At the party, there was a big table with food and drinks. Tom saw a big bottle of grape juice which looked like wine. He wanted to taste it, but he was too small to reach it. Tom had an idea. He found a long stick and used it to spread the grape juice on a piece of bread. He took a bite and it tasted very good. Tom was very happy and forgot all about his lost toy. He enjoyed the party with his friends and had a great time.

Lily liked music very much. She liked to sing and dance and play with her toy piano. One day, she heard a knock on the door. She ran to see who it was. "Hello, I have a package for you," said a tall man with a big smile. He wore a blue shirt and a hat with a letter on it. He held a big box in his hands. "A package for me?" Lily asked. She wondered what was inside. "Yes, it's from your grandma. She said it's a special gift for you. Can you sign here, please?" The man gave Lily a pen and a paper. Lily tried to write her name, but it was hard. She made some squiggles and dots. The man said it was good enough. He gave Lily the box and said goodbye. Lily thanked the man and took the box inside. She was very excited. She opened the box and saw a shiny thing with strings and a bow. It was a violin! "Wow, a violin!" Lily said. She picked it up and tried to play it. It made some funny noises. She laughed. Lily's mom came in and saw the violin. She smiled and hugged Lily. "Your grandma sent you a violin. She loves music too. She used to play the violin when she was young. She wants you to learn how to play it. Do you like it?" Mom asked. "I love it! Thank you, grandma!" Lily said. She gave the violin a kiss. Mom said she would find a teacher for Lily. She said Lily could make beautiful music with the violin. Lily was very happy. She wanted to make music for her mom and grandma and the tall man who delivered the package.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the park. One day, Tim saw a big green frog near the pond. The frog could spring high into the air. Tim was very excited and wanted to play with the frog. Tim said to the frog, "Hello, Mr. Frog! Can I play with you?" The frog looked at Tim and said, "Yes, but you must be careful. I need oxygen from the pond to breathe." Tim didn't know what oxygen was, but he knew he needed to be careful. So, Tim and the frog played together. They had fun jumping and springing in the park. Tim was careful not to hurt the frog. They played until the sun went down, and then Tim said goodbye to his new friend. The frog went back to the pond, and Tim went home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little rose in a big garden. The rose felt helpless because it was so small. One day, a kind bird saw the rose and wanted to help it. The bird flew down and said, "Little rose, I can help you. I will shake your stem to make you strong." The rose was happy and said, "Thank you, kind bird. I want to be strong and big like the other flowers." So, the bird began to shake the rose gently. Every day, the bird came to shake the rose. Slowly, the rose started to grow bigger and stronger. The other flowers in the garden saw this and were happy for the rose. One day, the rose was big and strong. It said, "Thank you, kind bird, for helping me. Now I am not helpless anymore." The bird smiled and said, "You are welcome, little rose. I am happy to help." From that day on, the rose and the bird became good friends. They played together in the big garden, and the rose was never helpless again.
