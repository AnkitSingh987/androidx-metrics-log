Category: Intermediate
Title: The Man and His Friend and the Oat

Once upon a time there was a man and his friend. The two were walking in the fields when the man saw something special – an oat! He was very curious about the oat and asked his friend “What is this?” “This is an oat,” his friend replied. “It is a very useful thing.” The man thought for a moment and then said “I want to try it! How much do I have to pay?” “Just one coin,” his friend replied. The man was delighted and he paid his friend the money. Then he took the oat and began to eat it. It was delicious! The man thanked his friend and said “This oat was very useful.” From that day on the man and his friend always went to the fields to look for oat, each time finding something new and useful.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lot of fog. The fog was so thick that people could not see where they were going. They had to be very careful when they walked outside. In this town, there was a big, strong dog named Max. Max liked to protect his family and friends. One day, while the fog was very thick, Max saw a little girl named Sarah. Sarah was lost in the fog and could not find her way home. She was scared and crying. Max knew he had to help Sarah, so he walked up to her and let her pet him. Sarah felt safe with Max and knew he would protect her. They were very careful as they walked through the fog. Max led Sarah back to her home, where her family was so happy to see her. They all thanked Max for being so brave and protecting Sarah. From that day on, Max and Sarah were the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy car. The car was red and shiny. Tim thought it was the perfect car to play with. He loved to zoom it around the room, making car noises as he played. Tim was very happy with his new toy car. Tim took his car everywhere he went. One day, he went to the park with his mom. He played with the car on the slides and swings. Then, Tim saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to race his car down the hill. He put the car at the top and let it go. The car went very fast down the hill. Tim laughed and clapped as he watched. At the bottom of the hill, there was a puddle. Tim did not see the puddle. The car raced down the hill and went right into the puddle. The car dropped into the water with a splash. Tim ran to the puddle and picked up his car. It was wet, but still perfect to him. Tim and his mom went home to dry the car. They had a fun day at the park with the perfect car.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a small house near a big hill. Mia liked to play outside with her red ball. She would throw the ball up the hill and watch it roll back down. One sunny day, Mia went out to play. The hill was very dry because it had not rained for many days. When Mia threw her ball up the hill, it got covered in dust. The ball was not red anymore, but brown from the dirt. Mia was sad to see her ball so dirty. She decided to wipe the dirt off the ball. She found a clean cloth and wiped the ball until it was red again. Mia was happy and went back to play with her clean red ball. She hoped that it would rain soon, so the hill would not be so dry.

Once there was a little girl, named Nora. She was three years old and liked to explore the world around her. One day, Nora found an interesting lock. It was bright and had many colors. She said to herself, "I must find a way to open this lock!" Nora went all around town in search of a key. She asked everyone she could, but none of them had the right key. Just as she was about to give up, Nora bumped into a kind man. He said, "I think I can help you. I can supply you with a key if you want." Nora was excited! She said, “Yes, please! I need a key.” The kind man gave Nora the key she needed. She put it in the lock and it opened! Nora was so excited about her useful new key. Now, Nora's friends always ask her for her useful lock. She is happy to have helped them open their own locks.
