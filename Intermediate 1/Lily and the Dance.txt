Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Dance

Lily loved to dance. She liked to move her feet and arms to the rhythm of the music. She wore a pink dress that twirled when she spun around. She felt happy and free when she danced. One day, she went to a dance class with her mom. There were other kids there, but they did not dance like Lily. They followed the teacher's steps and counted out loud. Lily thought that was boring. She wanted to dance her own way. She tried to join the class, but the teacher stopped her. "Lily, you have to follow the rules. You have to do what I say. You can't just dance however you want. You will make a mess of the dance." Lily felt sad and angry. She did not like the teacher. She did not like the rules. She ran out of the class and hid in the bathroom. She cried and prayed. She prayed that God would make the teacher go away. She prayed that she could dance her own way. Her mom came to find her. She hugged her and wiped her tears. She said, "Lily, I know you love to dance. And I love to watch you dance. But sometimes, you have to learn from others. You have to listen and cooperate. You have to respect the teacher and the other kids. You can't always do what you want. You have to think of others too." Lily looked at her mom. She thought about what she said. She felt sorry for praying mean things. She said, "Mom, I'm sorry. I was selfish. I didn't think of others. I will try to follow the rules. I will try to learn from the teacher. I will try to be nice to the other kids. But can I still dance my own way sometimes?" Her mom smiled and kissed her. She said, "Of course, you can. You can dance your own way when you are at home, or at the park, or with your friends. You can express yourself and have fun. But when you are in a class, you have to respect the structure and the discipline. You have to learn new skills and techniques. You have to be part of a team. That will make you a better dancer and a better person." Lily nodded and hugged her mom. She said, "Thank you, mom. I understand. I will try to do both. I will try to be a good dancer and a good person. I love you, mom." Her mom said, "I love you too, Lily. Now, let's go back to the class and apologize to the teacher. Maybe she will let you dance your own way for a little bit at the end." Lily agreed and followed her mom. She felt more calm and confident. She learned a valuable lesson that day. She learned that dancing is a gift from God, but it also requires respect, cooperation, and learning. She learned that she can pray for help, but not for harm. She learned that she can be herself, but also think of others. She learned that she can enjoy the rhythm of the music, and the rhythm of life.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy bear named Bobo. He lived in a big forest with many trees. Bobo loved to cook and make yummy food for his friends. One sunny day, Bobo decided to make a big pot of soup. He put all the tasty things he could find in the pot. He used a long stick to stir the soup. He stirred and stirred until all the things mixed well. Bobo tied a cloth around his waist to look like a chef. But Bobo was clumsy. When he was stirring the soup, he tripped on a rock. The soup went splat all over the ground. Bobo felt sad, but his friends came to help. They all laughed and helped Bobo clean the mess. Together, they made a new pot of soup. This time, Bobo was careful not to trip. They stirred the soup and shared it with everyone. All the animals in the forest had a fun time eating and playing together. And Bobo learned that even if he was clumsy, his friends would always be there to help him.
