Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Door

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see big dark clouds in the sky. They hear loud noises and see bright flashes. They are scared. It is a storm. Anna and Ben run to Anna's house. They knock on the door. Anna's mom opens the door. She hugs them and says, "Don't worry, it is just a storm. It will go away soon. Come inside and I will show you something." Anna's mom takes them to the living room. She shows them a big book with pictures of storms. She says, "Storms are powerful. They make wind, rain, thunder and lightning. But they also make rainbows, flowers and puddles. Storms are part of nature. They are not bad. They are just different." Anna and Ben look at the pictures. They see storms in different places and seasons. They see how storms can be beautiful and fun. They learn new words and facts. They are not scared anymore. They are curious. Anna's mom says, "Do you want to make a storm in a jar? It is easy and fun. I will show you how." She gets a jar, water, oil, food coloring and glitter. She fills the jar with water and adds a few drops of food coloring. She says, "This is the sky. What color do you want?" Anna and Ben choose blue. Then she adds oil on top of the water. She says, "This is the cloud. It is lighter than water, so it floats." She sprinkles some glitter on the oil. She says, "This is the rain and the lightning. They sparkle and shine." She closes the jar and shakes it. She says, "This is the storm. Watch what happens." Anna and Ben watch the jar. They see the oil and water mix and swirl. They see the glitter fall and flash. They see the colors change and blend. They are amazed. It is a storm in a jar. Anna and Ben say, "Wow, that is cool. Thank you for showing us. Can we make more?" Anna's mom says, "Sure, you can make more. You can use different colors and shapes. You can make your own storms. But remember, be careful and gentle. Storms are powerful, but they are also fragile."

Once upon a time, there was a rat named Sam. He was both small and sneaky. He liked to play hide and seek, and it was his favorite game. One day, Sam was walking near a deep pond. He wanted to go to the other side of the pond, but there was no bridge. So Sam tried to balance on a log to get across. He wobbled to one side, and then the other. Sam stayed focused and balanced with his eyes closed. He gently swayed back and forth. Finally, he made it to the other side! Sam was so happy and proud of himself. He had balanced on the log, and made it safely across the deep pond! From then on, Sam was the king of balance.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside with his friends. They were very enthusiastic about playing games like tag and hide and seek. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud sound. It was thunder! They were scared and ran to their homes. Tim's mom told him not to be afraid of the thunder. She said that it was just a sound made by the sky. Tim wanted to be brave, so he decided to go back outside and face the thunder. He told his friends that they should not be scared and they should not surrender to the thunder. They went outside and continued to play their games. Even though the thunder was still loud, they were not afraid anymore. They knew that they were brave and strong. They played happily until the sun came out and the thunder went away. From that day on, they were never scared of thunder again.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a new red bike. Tim loved to ride his bike fast. One day, he got a job to deliver things to people. Tim was very happy. Tim had a big bag to put things in. He also had a long rod. The rod helped him knock on doors. It made a loud sound. People would come out and get their things. One day, Tim had to deliver a big box. It was heavy. He put it on his bike and rode very slow. He used the rod to knock on the door. A little girl came out. She was happy to get the box. Tim felt proud. He rode his new red bike home with a big smile.
