Category: Intermediate
Title: Ducky the Duck and the Fish

One day, a cute duck named Ducky went to the pond to play. He loved to splash in the water with his friends. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. It was a great day to have fun. Ducky saw his friend, Bunny, sitting by the pond. Bunny looked confused. "What's wrong, Bunny?" asked Ducky. Bunny said, "I can't find my toy. I left it on the shelf in my home, but now it's gone!" Ducky wanted to help Bunny find the toy. They looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under rocks, by the trees, and in the tall grass. As they were searching, Ducky had a feeling that something was going to happen. Suddenly, they heard a big splash in the pond. They looked and saw a big fish holding Bunny's toy in its mouth! The big fish said, "I'm sorry, I thought this toy was food. I didn't mean to take it!" Bunny was happy to have his toy back. Ducky and Bunny thanked the big fish and continued to play by the pond. The big splash was a surprise, but it helped them find the missing toy.

One day, a pretty bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a nice home. The home was big and attractive. The bird wanted to live in the home. So, the bird flew down to the home. Inside the home, there was a little girl. The girl saw the bird and said, "Hi bird, do you want to talk to me?" The bird said, "Yes, I want to talk. I like your home. Can I live with you?" The girl was happy to have a new friend. She said, "Yes, bird, you can live with me. We can talk and play every day." The bird was happy too. They became good friends and lived in the attractive home together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play catch with her best friend, a dog named Max. They would play together every day, and when they were done, they would give each other a big hug. One day, Lily felt ready for a new game. She saw a butterfly and wanted to catch it. So, she and Max started to chase the butterfly. They ran and ran, but they could not catch it. They were so busy chasing the butterfly that they did not see the big hole in the ground. Lily and Max fell into the hole. They were scared and could not get out. They tried to jump and climb, but it was too deep. They hugged each other, but they were still stuck. No one came to help them, and they stayed in the hole for a long time. The sun went down, and it was dark and cold. Lily and Max were very sad and missed playing catch and giving hugs outside.

Once upon a time there was a furry jeep. It was the first of its kind. The jeep could balance in strange ways. One day the jeep went for a drive. It went over a hill and raced around a corner. It even drove through some puddles. It was so much fun! Eventually the jeep drove home. It was tired, but it was also very happy. And it balanced perfectly as it drove. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there were many tall plants. They were very happy. They loved to feel the sun on their leaves. One day, a big wind came. It was very strong. The wind started to scatter the seeds from the tall plants all over the garden. The plants were sad. They did not want their seeds to go away. The tall plants asked their friend, the sun, for help. The sun shone brightly and made the wind go away. The seeds came back to the tall plants. They were all happy again in their garden.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big, open field with pretty flowers. Tim started to wonder what was on the other side of the field. He walked across the field to find out. Tim saw a big tree with a shield leaning against it. He picked up the shield and heard a voice say, "Hello, I am a talking shield!" Tim was very surprised. The shield told Tim, "I can keep you safe if you take me with you." Just then, a big, scary bear came out of the woods! Tim held the shield in front of him. The bear tried to scare Tim, but the shield made a funny face at the bear. The bear started to laugh and didn't want to scare Tim anymore. Tim and the bear became friends, and they played together in the open field.
