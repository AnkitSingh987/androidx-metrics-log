Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Book

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim found a hidden book under his bed. The book had many pages with fun pictures. Tim loved to look at the pictures and make up stories in his head. One day, Tim saw his dog, Spot, chewing on one of the pages. "No, Spot!" Tim said, "Don't chew the book!" Tim took the book away from Spot and looked at the chewed page. It was a picture of a big tree with many fruits. Tim felt sad because the picture was not pretty anymore. He tried to fix the page, but it was still not the same. Tim learned that he should take better care of his things. The next day, Tim's mom saw the chewed page and asked him what happened. Tim told her the truth about Spot. His mom was not angry, but she told him a secret. The hidden book was a magic book! The chewed page was now a real tree outside in their yard. Tim and his mom went outside and saw the big tree with many fruits. They picked the fruits together and shared them with their friends. Tim learned that sometimes, unexpected things can bring happiness and sharing is a good thing to do.

Lily was a small girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a big bear, a red car, a yellow ball and a blue blanket. She liked to make stories with her toys and pretend they could talk. One day, she was playing with her bear and her blanket. She put the blanket over the bear and said, "You are a cave bear. You live in a dark cave and you are very brave." She made the bear roar and move under the blanket. Then she heard a knock on the door. It was her mom. She came in and smiled at Lily. "What are you doing, sweetie?" she asked. "I'm playing with my cave bear, mom. He is very brave and strong," Lily said. "Can I see your cave bear?" mom asked. Lily lifted the blanket and showed her mom the bear. The bear blinked at mom and mom blinked back. "He is very cute, Lily. But you know what? He might be a little hot under the blanket. Why don't you let him breathe some fresh air?" mom suggested. Lily thought for a moment. She loved her bear and she didn't want him to be hot. She nodded and said, "Okay, mom. I will let him breathe some fresh air." She took the blanket off the bear and hugged him. "You are still my cave bear, but you can also be my friend bear," she said. Mom smiled and kissed Lily and the bear. "You are very kind, Lily. And you have a great imagination. I'm proud of you," she said. Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, mom. I love you and I love my bear." She put the blanket on her bed and continued to play with her toys. She was happy and so was her bear.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty train. The train was very big and strong. It loved to go fast on the tracks. One day, the train saw a big pile of coal. The coal was very dirty. The train wanted to be clean, but it needed the coal to go fast. The train asked a little bird, "Can you help me? I want to be clean, but I need the coal to go fast." The bird said, "I can help you. I will find a way to make the coal clean." The bird flew away and found a big river. The bird brought water from the river to the coal. The bird washed the coal with the water. The coal became clean. The train was very happy. It took the clean coal and went very fast on the tracks. The train and the bird became good friends. They worked together to keep the train clean and fast. And they lived happily ever after.

Anna was a very enthusiastic 3 year old. She loved playing outside with her friends. One day she thought to herself, "I want to go on a raft ride." Anna asked her parents if she could go and they said yes. So she went to the nearby lake with her friends. They couldn't find a raft so they decided to build one together. They worked together and made the most beautiful raft. Anna's friends were impressed and they were excited to climb on and go. But then a problem occurred. The raft kept sinking. Anna and her friends tried their best to find a way to get the raft to stay above the water. They guessed and tried many things, but nothing seemed to work. Anna was starting to lose hope when suddenly one of her friends had an idea. “Wait! I think I know what we have to do.” They worked together and soon after the raft was able to float perfectly. Anna and her friends were so excited and that evening, they went on the most fantastic raft ride. It was a great experience, and they were all very enthusiastic!
