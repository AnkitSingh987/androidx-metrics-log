Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Window

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to run and play outside. Running was his favorite exercise. One day, while he was playing, he fell down and hurt his leg. His mom put a tight bandage on it to help it heal. Tim was sad because he couldn't run and play with his friends. He watched them from his window, wishing he could join them. His mom said he had to rest and wait for his leg to heal. So Tim waited and waited. One day, a funny thing happened. A little bird flew into Tim's room. The bird had a tight bandage on its wing too! Tim and the bird became friends. They both couldn't fly or run, but they could still have fun together. They played games and talked all day. And as they played, they both started to heal. Soon, Tim and the bird were all better and ready to play outside again. They had a big race to see who was faster. The bird won, but Tim didn't mind. He was just happy to have a new friend and be able to run again.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tiny. Tiny had a special power. When he would stay in one place, he could see far away things. One day, Tiny saw a big dog coming to his home. Tiny ran to his friend, Bunny. "Big dog is coming! We must hide!" he said. Bunny asked, "How do you know?" Tiny said, "I used my power when I stay still." Bunny believed Tiny, and they told all their friends to hide. The big dog came, but he could not find any of the small animals. They all stayed hidden and safe. The big dog left, and the small animals came out to play. They thanked Tiny for using his power to keep them safe.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pippin. Pippin was a very envious penguin. He always wanted what other animals had. One day, he saw a bird flying high in the sky. Pippin wanted to fly too. So, Pippin went to the bird and requested, "Please teach me how to fly." The bird agreed to help Pippin. They tried and tried, but Pippin could not fly. Pippin was sad, but he did not give up. Pippin kept trying to fly, but he could not. He was so envious of the bird that he did not see the ice crack under him. Pippin fell into the cold water. It was a bad ending for the envious penguin.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to bake a cake. She asked her mom, "Can I bake a cake, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but I will help you." Sue and her mom got all the things they needed to bake the cake. They got flour, sugar, eggs, and milk. Sue saw a box of salt and asked, "Do we need salt too?" Her mom said, "No, we don't need salt for the cake." They mixed everything together and put the cake in the oven. While they waited, Sue saw a dead bug on the floor. She told her mom, "Look, there's a dead bug!" Her mom cleaned it up and said, "Don't worry, the bug is gone now." When the cake was done, Sue and her mom took it out of the oven. They let it cool down and then put icing on it. Sue was very happy with her cake and said, "This is the best cake ever!"

One day, a bright sun was up in the sky. A little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sally, and they decided to play tag. As they were playing, they heard a loud alarm. "What is that?" asked Sally. "I don't know," said Tim, "but let's go see!" They ran to the sound and found a big red truck with lights. A kind firefighter saw them and said, "Don't worry, kids. We are just here to help a cat stuck in a tree." Tim and Sally watched as the firefighter saved the cat. They were happy to see the cat safe. At the end of the day, Tim and Sally went home. They were tired but had a lot of fun playing and seeing the big red truck. They couldn't wait to play again tomorrow.
