Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little dog. The dog had no ears. He could not hear. He was deaf. The dog liked to dance. He danced all day. One day, the dog went to the park. He danced on the grass. He danced under the trees. He danced near the pond. The dog was very happy. But the dog could not hear. He did not know there was a big car coming. The car was fast. The dog kept dancing. The car hit the dog. The dog was hurt. The dog could not dance anymore. The end.

---

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tim was a bit scared because the tree was very tall. He looked up and stared at the tree for a long time. While he was staring, a little bird came and sat on a branch near Tim. "Hello, little bird," said Tim. "I want to climb this tree, but I am scared." The bird looked at Tim and said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I can help you climb the tree." Tim was very surprised that the bird could talk. He thought it was a dream. But the bird was real! With the help of the bird, Tim started to climb the tree. They went higher and higher. When they got to the top, Tim saw something he did not expect. There was a big bag of candy on a branch! The bird said, "This is for you, Tim. You were brave and climbed the tree. Now you get a prize." Tim was very happy. He shared the candy with the bird and his friends. The poor boy was not scared anymore. From that day on, Tim and the bird became best friends. They played together at the park every day.

Once there was a three year old girl named Amy. She loved to watch her daddy play golf in the weekend. Every time daddy hit the ball into the hole, Amy would clap and laugh. One day, daddy asked Amy to come and play a game of golf with him. She was so excited she said yes straight away. On the golf course, Amy tried her best and was soon able to hit the ball into the hole. When she did, Daddy applauded and hugged her. Amy was so happy that she learned an important lesson that day. Whenever she achieved something and worked hard, it was important to appreciate her efforts and applaud her successes.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Millie was so excited to go outside to play. She put on her tennis shoes and went outside with her friends. Millie smiled as she put her hands in the air and started to jog around the park. Millie noticed a tall tree in the corner of the park, and off in the distance she heard a quiet sound. She stopped and made her way quietly to the tree. Millie's friends followed her closely. When they reached the tree, they saw a little bird perched on one of its branches. The little bird was busy stitching a small nest with twigs and leaves. Millie smiled and asked the bird, "What are you doing?". The bird spun around and replied, "'I'm making a cozy home for my little family." Millie's eyes lit up, and she and her friends continued to jog around the park, happily imagining about the bird's little family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys. One day, he found a weak toy. The toy was an object that looked like a car. Tim wanted to make the car strong. Tim asked his dad for help. His dad said they could fix the weak car together. They used glue and tape to make the car strong. Tim was happy to see his car become strong. When they finish fixing the car, Tim played with it all day. He raced the car around the house. The car was not weak anymore. Tim and his dad were happy because they made the car strong together.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom loved to watch TV all day long. One day, Tom saw a show about a cat who could raise its paw very high. Tom wanted to do that too. Tom practiced raising his paw every day. He would raise it up high and then put it down. He did this many times. One day, while Tom was practicing, a ball rolled into his house. The ball was stuck under the TV. Tom had an idea. He remembered how he practiced raising his paw. He raised his paw high and pushed the TV up. The ball rolled out from under the TV. Tom was so happy that his practice had paid off. Now, Tom not only watched TV all day, but he also raised his paw high to play with the ball. Tom was a very happy thin cat. And that's the end of the story.
