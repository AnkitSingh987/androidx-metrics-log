Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Adventurous and the Mom

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was very adventurous. She loved to explore the world around her. One day, she asked her mom to let her go for a walk. Her mom said yes and off she went. The little girl walked down the street, taking in all of the sights, smells and sounds. She felt so adventurous. She walked around for a while, exploring every corner she could find. Then, before she knew it, it was time to go home. Her mom was so proud of her for being so adventurous. She was so happy that she gave her a big hug and told her to go for a walk again soon.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were friends. The big dog could not hear. He was deaf. The little cat could hear very well. They liked to play together. One day, they went to play near the waste. The waste was dirty and smelly. The big dog liked to play in the waste. The little cat did not like the waste. The big dog wanted the little cat to play in the waste too. The little cat did not want to. The big dog and the little cat talked about what to do. They had an idea. They would include a new game. The new game was to clean the waste. They would make the waste go away. They worked together and made the waste go away. Now, they could play in a clean place. The big dog and the little cat were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a young bird. The bird lived in a small cage. The cage was in a big house. The bird did not like the cage. The bird wanted to fly and play with other birds. One day, the bird saw a big cat. The cat was outside the house. The cat looked at the bird. The cat wanted to eat the bird. The bird was scared. The bird gazed at the cat and thought of a plan. The bird began to sing a pretty song. The cat liked the song. The cat came closer to the cage. The bird sang louder. The cat opened the cage with its paw. The bird flew away quickly. The cat was sad. The bird was free and happy.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to school. In her classroom, there was a big blackboard. The teacher used the blackboard to write and draw things for the kids to see. Amy liked to look at the blackboard and learn new things. During playtime, Amy and her friend, Tim, played near the blackboard. Tim said, "Let's draw on the blackboard!" Amy was scared. She said, "No, the teacher will be mad." But Tim did it anyway. He drew a funny face on the blackboard. When the teacher came back, she saw the funny face on the blackboard. She asked, "Who did this?" Tim was embarrassed. He said, "I did it, I'm sorry." The teacher was not mad. She said, "Next time, ask me first." Tim and Amy learned to ask before doing things.

One day, a little car named Zoomy was driving on an empty road. Zoomy loved to zoom fast. He saw a rope on the road and thought it would be fun to play with. Zoomy drove over the rope and pulled it with his wheels. He zoomed around in circles, making the rope twist and turn. Zoomy was having so much fun playing with the rope. After playing for a while, Zoomy felt tired. He stopped zooming and parked near a big tree. He left the rope under the tree and went to sleep. Zoomy had a happy day playing with the rope on the empty road.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved the sunshine. Every morning, he would rise with the sun and sing a happy song. One day, Tim saw a big, red apple on the ground. It looked very tasty. Tim went to his friend, the squirrel, and said, "Look at that tasty apple! Can you help me get it?" The squirrel agreed and helped Tim push the apple to his tree. They both were very happy and excited to eat the apple. But when they got to the tree, a big, mean cat was waiting for them. The cat saw the tasty apple and wanted it too. The cat jumped on the apple and took it away from Tim and the squirrel. They were very sad and cried. The sunshine was not so happy anymore, and they did not rise with the sun the next day.
