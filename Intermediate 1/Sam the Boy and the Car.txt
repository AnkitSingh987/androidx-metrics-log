Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Boy and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam. Sam loved to play with his toy car. One day, he saw a clear box on the table. It was an ashtray. He thought it was a cool toy! So, he played with it and put his toy car inside. Sam wanted to show his mom the new toy he found. He ran to his mom and tried to pull the ashtray from the table. But it was stuck! He pulled and pulled, but it would not move. Sam was sad because he could not show his mom the ashtray. Then, something unexpected happened. Sam's mom came into the room and saw him trying to pull the ashtray. She smiled and said, "Sam, that's not a toy. It's for grown-ups." She took the ashtray and put it away. Then, she gave Sam a big hug and a new toy car to play with. Sam was happy and played with his new car all day long.

One day, a big cat and a little dog were playing in the park. They were running and jumping. They were very happy. The cat said, "I can jump higher than you!" The dog said, "No, you can't!" They decided to have a jumping contest. They organized a line to jump over. The cat jumped first. He jumped very high and did not fall. Then, it was the dog's turn. The dog jumped, but he fell on his head. The cat laughed and said, "I told you I can jump higher!" Just then, a small bird came and said, "I can jump higher than both of you!" The cat and the dog did not believe the bird. The bird jumped and flew very high. The cat and the dog were very surprised. They learned that even small friends can do big things.

Lily and Ben like to play with their toy kitchen. They have pots and pans, plates and cups, and pretend food. They like to serve each other yummy meals and tea. One day, they decide to visit their grandma, who lives in a big house. They bring their toy kitchen with them, and set it up in the living room. They want to surprise grandma with a special lunch. They look around the house for things to cook. They find some bread, cheese, apples, and cookies. They also find a jug of milk in the fridge. They cut the bread and cheese, slice the apples, and arrange the cookies on a tray. They pour some milk into a pot and put it on the stove. They wait for the milk to get hot, but it takes a long time. They get bored and start to play with their toys. They forget about the milk on the stove. The milk starts to boil and spill over. It makes a big mess and a loud noise. Grandma hears the noise and comes to the living room. She sees the milk all over the stove and the floor. She is not happy. She tells Lily and Ben to clean up the mess. She also tells them that the milk is too hot to drink, and that they should never leave the stove on. Lily and Ben feel sorry. They say sorry to grandma and help her clean up. They also say sorry to each other for not paying attention. They decide to serve grandma cold milk instead of hot milk. They also serve her bread, cheese, apples, and cookies. Grandma smiles and hugs them. She says that they are sweet and that she loves them. She says that they can still play with their toy kitchen, but they have to be careful and ask for help. Lily and Ben nod and agree. They enjoy their lunch with grandma and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big and impressive bird. The bird had a special style. It could leave a trail of colorful feathers when it flew. The bird loved to show off its style to all its friends. One day, the bird heard a loud sound. The bird knew this sound meant something was coming. The bird decided to leave a trail of feathers to help its friends find it later. The bird flew away, leaving the colorful feathers behind. Later, the bird's friends saw the feathers. They followed the trail and found the bird. They were happy to see the impressive bird with its special style. The bird had used its feathers to bring its friends together.
