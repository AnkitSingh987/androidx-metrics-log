Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Bird

One day, a girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw her friend, Tom, who was trying to tie his shoe. "I can help you," Sue said. Tom was happy and said, "Thank you, Sue!" Together, they tied the shoe. They walked to the west, where the sun was going down. They saw a big tree with a useful rope hanging from it. "Let's play on the rope!" said Sue. Tom and Sue played on the rope and had lots of fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took the rope in its mouth. "Oh no!" said Tom. "Our rope is gone!" But the bird did not fly away. It showed them a new place to play with an even better rope. Tom and Sue were very happy and played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dance. She would dance in her room, in the yard, and even at the park. One day, Mia saw a fierce lion at the park. The lion looked sad. Mia went up to the lion and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Lion?" The lion replied, "I need to dance for my friends, but I don't know how to dance." Mia smiled and said, "I can teach you how to dance!" Mia showed the lion how to dance. They danced together every day. The lion became very happy. Soon, it was time for the lion to dance for his friends. The lion danced so well that his friends cheered and clapped. The lion thanked Mia for teaching him how to dance, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, open house. In this house, there lived a nice family. The family had a mom, a dad, a little girl named Mia, and a dog named Max. They were always happy to welcome friends to their home. One day, Mia and Max were playing with a red ball. Mia threw the ball high up in the air, and it landed on the top of the house. Mia and Max looked at each other and did not know what to do. They wanted to get the ball back, but the top of the house was very high. Just then, a friendly bird flew by and saw the ball on the top of the house. The bird picked up the ball with its beak and dropped it down to Mia and Max. They were so happy and thanked the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! It said, "You're welcome!" Mia and Max were very surprised, but now they had a new friend who could talk. They all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a bear went to take honey from a tree. The bear liked honey a lot. It was warm and sweet. The bear was very happy when he found honey. He took the honey and put it in a big jar. The bear went to his home with the jar of honey. He wanted to eat the honey with his friends. He called his friends to come and have a honey party. The bear and his friends were very excited. But when they opened the jar, there was no honey inside! It was full of warm water. The bear was sad and confused. Then a little bee came and said, "I took the honey to share with my family. I'm sorry, Mr. Bear." The bear was not angry. He was happy that the bee shared the honey with her family. And the bee promised to bring more honey for the bear and his friends next time.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily and her sister named Rose. They loved to play in their big, wild backyard. One day, Lily had an idea. She said to Rose, "Let's have a tea party with our toys!" Rose thought it was a good idea. She suggested, "Let's pick some pretty flowers for our tea party too." So, they went to the wild garden and picked the most beautiful flowers they could find. At the tea party, Lily and Rose had so much fun with their toys. They laughed and shared their yummy snacks. After the tea party, they gave each other a big hug and said, "We love playing together!"
