Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and Ben and the Shoes

Anna and Ben were playing with their dolls in the living room. They liked to dress them up and make them talk. Anna had a pink dress for her doll and Ben had a blue shirt and pants for his doll. "Let's go to the park with our dolls," Anna said. "They can play on the swings and the slide." "OK," Ben said. "But we have to put on our shoes first." Anna and Ben went to their room and looked for their shoes. Anna found her red shoes with the velcro straps. She put them on easily and fastened them. Ben found his brown shoes with the leather laces. He tried to put them on, but they were too tight. "Ow, ow, ow," Ben said. "These shoes hurt my feet. They are too small." Anna looked at Ben's shoes. She saw that the leather laces were knotted and tangled. She tried to help Ben untie them, but they were too hard to pull. "Resist, Ben, resist," Anna said. "We have to get these shoes off." Ben resisted. He pulled and twisted his feet, but the shoes did not come off. He started to cry. "Mommy, mommy, help," Ben shouted. "My shoes are stuck." Mommy heard Ben's cry and came to their room. She saw Ben's shoes and smiled. "Ben, these are not your shoes," Mommy said. "These are Daddy's shoes. They are too big for you. That's why they are so tight." Mommy took off Ben's shoes and gave him his own shoes. They were green and had buttons. Ben put them on and pushed the buttons. They made a beep sound. "Thank you, Mommy," Ben said. "These shoes are much better. They fit me and they make noise." "You're welcome, Ben," Mommy said. "Now you and Anna can go to the park with your dolls. But be careful and have fun." Anna and Ben hugged Mommy and ran to the living room. They picked up their dolls and went to the door. "Bye, Mommy," Anna and Ben said. "We love you." "I love you too," Mommy said. "Have a good time."

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe. It was unique because it could talk. It had many pretty clothes inside. The wardrobe lived in a room with a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily wanted to wear a dress with a bow. She asked the wardrobe for help. The wardrobe said, "I can help you tie the bow!" So, the wardrobe showed Lily how to tie the bow on her dress. Lily was very happy. She thanked the wardrobe for teaching her to tie the bow. From that day on, Lily and the wardrobe became best friends. They played and had fun together every day. And Lily always had the prettiest bows on her dresses.

Once upon a time, a soft cat named Kitty lived with her mother. They loved to play and hug each other. Kitty and her mother were very happy. One day, when they were playing, Kitty saw a big box. She went to the box and tried to open it. Her mother told her to be careful. Kitty paused for a moment, then she opened the box. Inside the box, there was a soft, fluffy pillow. Kitty and her mother were so surprised! They thought the box would have something scary inside. But now, they could both take a nap on the soft pillow together. They laughed and enjoyed their new gift.

Once, there was a little bird called Sam. He liked to fly around singing, but one day he couldn't move. He had a nut stuck in his wing! "Help!", he squawked. "I can't fly!" Just then, a clumsy little caterpillar crawled out of the grass and said, "I can help you, little bird. My name is Alice. I can help you get the nut out". With one quick tug, Alice pulled the nut from Sam's wing. He was so grateful, he started to sing. Alice danced around in excitement hearing Sam's cheerful song. She smiled and thanked him for the beautiful music. Sam flew away, but he promised to come back and sing for Alice as a thank you for helping him. The friendship between this little bird and caterpillar would last forever.
