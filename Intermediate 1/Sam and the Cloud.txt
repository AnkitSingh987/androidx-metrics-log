Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and the Cloud

Sam was playing in her backyard one bright and clear noon day. She looked up and gazed at the big, blue sky. Her heart felt full of joy and she gave a wide, happy smile. Suddenly, she heard a voice from high above in the sky. She heard it say, "Hello Sam! How are you today?" Sam was so surprised. She stopped and looked up and gazed, searching to see who was speaking. High up in the sky, she saw a big, white cloud with a friendly face. The cloud said to Sam, "I am a magical cloud. If you close your eyes and make a wish, I will turn it into reality!" Sam thought for moment and then smiled. She closed her eyes and made her wish. When she opened her eyes, the magical cloud was gone, but a piece of a rainbow had appeared in the sky. Sam was delighted. She thanked the magical cloud and started running and twirling around in circles in the backyard. From that day on, whenever Sam felt sad, she would look up at the sky and gaze until she could find her magical cloud and a rainbow in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a boy who heard about a festival. He was very excited and he wanted to go. He chose to go very fast. The boy ran to the festival with his arms wide open. When he arrived, he saw that the festival was closed. He was so angry and sad. He shouted loudly and cried. No one was there to help him. He was all alone. Then he heard an old man who was very kind. He told the boy that he should always be careful and not be so fast. The boy thanked the old man and walked away sadly. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tom went outside to play. He saw a big tree with a little bird on a branch. Tom asked the bird, "Do you know where my ball is?" The bird did not answer Tom's question. Tom felt sad. Then, a nice girl named Sue came to play with Tom. Sue said, "I found your ball! It was under the bush!" Tom was glad. They played with the ball, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, the little bird flew down and took the ball in its beak. Tom and Sue were surprised. The bird said, "Now I know where your ball is!" Tom and Sue laughed, and they all played together.

Sara was a daughter who liked to help her mom in the kitchen. She liked to pour water, milk, and juice into cups and bowls. She liked to stir and mix and taste. She liked to make yummy things for her family. One day, her mom had a baby boy. His name was Ben. Sara was happy to have a brother, but she was also jealous. She wanted to help her mom with the baby, but her mom said no. She said the baby was too small and fragile. She said Sara had to be careful and gentle. She said Sara had to wait until the baby was bigger. Sara felt sad and angry. She did not want to wait. She wanted to pour water, milk, and juice for the baby. She wanted to stir and mix and taste his food. She wanted to make him happy and smile. She thought her mom loved the baby more than her. One day, when her mom was busy, Sara saw the baby's bottle on the table. It had milk inside. Sara had an idea. She grabbed the bottle and ran to the baby's crib. She wanted to pour the milk for the baby. She thought he would like it. But the baby did not like it. He started to cry and cough. He spit out the milk. He was wet and cold. Sara was scared. She did not mean to hurt the baby. She wanted to help him. She wanted to be a good sister. She heard her mom coming. She dropped the bottle and ran away. She hid under her bed. She felt sorry and ashamed. She did not know what to do. Her mom found the baby and the bottle. She saw what happened. She was angry and worried. She cleaned the baby and comforted him. She checked if he was okay. She was relieved that he was not hurt. Then she looked for Sara. She found her under the bed. She saw her tears and fear. She understood how she felt. She was not angry anymore. She was sorry too. She hugged Sara and lifted her up. She said, "Sara, I love you. You are my daughter. You are a good sister. But you cannot pour milk for the baby. He is too small. He cannot drink like you. He needs a special bottle. You can help me in other ways. You can sing to him, read to him, play with him. You can pour water, milk, and juice for yourself and for me. You can stir and mix and taste our food. You can make us happy and smile. You are a big girl. You are a big help. Do you understand?" Sara nodded. She said, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry, baby. I love you. I love you both. Can I hug the baby?" Her mom smiled. She said, "Of course, you can. But be careful and gentle. He is your brother. He loves you too." She carried Sara to the crib. She let her hug the baby. The baby stopped crying. He looked at Sara. He smiled. Sara smiled too. She felt happy and proud. She was a daughter. She was a sister. She was a helper. She was loved.
