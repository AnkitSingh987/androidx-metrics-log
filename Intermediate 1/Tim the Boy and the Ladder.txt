Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ladder

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big ladder. The ladder seemed very tall. Tim wanted to climb the ladder to see what was at the top. Tim started to climb the ladder. He went up and up. When he got to the top, he saw a rich man's house. The rich man had many toys and a big yard to play in. Tim was happy to see this. But then, something unexpected happened. The rich man came out of his house and saw Tim. The rich man was not angry. He was happy to see Tim. He said, "I have no one to play with. Will you be my friend?" Tim was happy to be his friend. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big mountain. The mountain was very fancy because it had many pretty colors. The people in the town loved to look at the mountain every day. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to help his mom. His mom was sad because she lost her favorite hat. Tim thought the hat might be on the fancy mountain. So, he went to look for it. Tim walked and walked up the mountain. He found the hat near a big tree. He was very happy to help his mom. He took the hat and went back down the mountain. When he gave the hat to his mom, she was so happy. They both smiled and hugged each other.

One day, a big, loud train went down the track. It made a lot of steam as it went. The train liked to roll fast. It was a sunny day and the train was happy. Then, the train saw a little ball on the track. The ball said, "Please don't roll over me!" The train stopped fast. The little ball was safe. The train said, "I will not roll over you, little ball." The little ball was not just a ball. It was a magic ball! The magic ball said, "Thank you for not rolling over me. I will give you a wish." The train wished to roll even faster. The magic ball made the wish come true. The train was very happy and rolled faster than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who lived in a world of wonder. Every morning she loved to explore new places. One morning, she came across a big tree that was so shiny and smooth. She wanted to touch it but something told her not to. It was as if the tree was talking to her. So she left it behind. Later that day, she stumbled upon a big box. It smelled so spicy! She wanted to open it but something told her not to. It was as if the box was talking to her. So she left it behind. As the days passed, the little girl kept exploring and finding new places. One day, she found an even bigger box. It was glowing and she wanted to touch it. But something told her not to. It was as if the box was talking to her. So she left it behind. Finally, the little girl reached the end of her journey. When she opened the last box, she found the most amazing thing! It was the same shiny, smooth tree she had seen at the beginning of her journey! Except this time the tree had beautiful, soft skin that she could touch. It was spicy to the touch - the perfect blend of sweet and sour! Now she knew why the box, tree and box were speaking to her - they were trying to keep her safe and show her the world! All she had to do was leave them behind.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with dry leaves on the ground. Tim wanted to play with the leaves, so he picked up a big pile and threw them into the air. The leaves fell all around him, making him laugh. A girl named Sue came up to Tim and said, "Please, can I play with the leaves too?" Tim said, "Yes, but we need to count to zero first." They both counted, "Three, two, one, zero!" Then they threw the leaves into the air together. But then, a big wind came and blew the leaves away. Tim and Sue were sad. They looked at each other and said, "Let's find more dry leaves to play with!" They searched the park and found more leaves. They played together, making new friends and having fun.
