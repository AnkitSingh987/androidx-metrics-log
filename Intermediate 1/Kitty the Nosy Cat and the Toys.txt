Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Nosy Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a little street, there lived a nosy cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to know what everyone was doing. She would go from house to house and peek through the windows. One day, Kitty saw a little girl named Sue playing with her toys. Sue looked lonely, so Kitty went to her and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" Sue was happy to have a friend and answered, "Yes, let's play together!" From that day on, Kitty and Sue played every day on the street. They had so much fun that Kitty forgot to be nosy. They became best friends and lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily was very hungry. She went to the table to eat her lunch. But when she looked at her plate, it was empty. She was sad because she wanted to eat. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can I have some food? My plate is empty." Her mom said, "I am sorry, Lily. We have no more food in the house. We have to go to the store." Lily and her mom went to the store to buy food. When they got there, they saw a big dog blocking the door. Lily was scared, but her mom said, "Don't worry, I will make the dog go away." Her mom clapped her hands and the dog ran away. They bought food and went back home. Lily and her mom put the food on the table. Lily was happy to eat her lunch. She ate and ate until her plate was empty again.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there lived a famous wizard. He was very powerful and wise, and every day he was worked hard doing his magic. One day, he felt very tired and decided to nap. Just then, he had a dream. In the dream, he saw a card that he was meant to find. He knew that the card was important, but he didn’t know why. When he woke up, he felt calmer, but also excited to find the card. The wizard decided to search the land for the card. He searched high and low, but he couldn’t find it. He was about to give up, when finally he found the card in the last place he thought to look - his pocket! When he looked at the card, he realized that the card was a ticket to a famous festival. The wizard was so happy, and the next day he set off for the festival. With the card, he was able to attend the festival and it was pure joy! He was never so glad to have taken a nap!

Lily and Tom were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big red truck with a ladder and a hose. It was a fire truck. The fire truck stopped near their house and some men got out. They wore yellow hats and coats. "What are they doing?" Lily asked Tom. "Maybe there is a fire," Tom said. "Let's go see." They ran to the fire truck and saw the men roll out the hose and point it at a house. Smoke was coming out of the chimney. The men climbed the ladder and went to the roof. "Be careful!" Lily shouted. One of the men nodded and smiled. He had a big bag with him. He opened the bag and took out something shiny. It was a hook. He used the hook to pull out the chimney. Then he dove into the hole. Lily and Tom gasped. They wondered what he was doing. They waited and waited. They heard some noises from inside the house. Then they saw the man come out of the hole. He was holding a cat. The cat was black and dirty. It was coughing and meowing. The man climbed down the ladder and gave the cat to a lady. The lady was crying and hugging the cat. She thanked the man and the other men. They said they were happy to help. They said the cat had climbed into the chimney and got stuck. They said the smoke was from the cat's fur. Lily and Tom clapped and cheered. They thought the man was very brave and kind. They wanted to pet the cat, but the lady said the cat needed to go to the vet. She said the cat was not feeling well. She said the cat's name was Smokey. Lily and Tom said goodbye to the lady and the cat. They said goodbye to the men and the fire truck. They went back to their house. They told their mom what they had seen. They said they wanted to be firemen when they grew up. They said they liked to help animals. Their mom smiled and hugged them. She said they were very good and sweet. She said she was proud of them. She said they could have some cookies and milk. She said they should be quiet and let Smokey rest. She said Smokey was lucky to have such a good friend.
