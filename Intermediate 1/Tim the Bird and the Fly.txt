Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Bird and the Fly

One day, a little bird named Tim was in his nest. He wanted to study how to fly. He saw his mom and dad flying high in the sky. Tim thought it looked fun. "Mom, I want to fly too," said Tim. "Can you teach me?" His mom said, "You are still too little, Tim. You need to wait." But Tim was silly and did not want to wait. Tim tried to fly on his own. He flapped his wings and jumped out of the nest. But he could not fly. He fell down and hurt his wing. His mom and dad came to help him. They were sad because Tim did not listen to them.

Once upon a time there was a little frog. He was very excited because he had just received a special package. The little frog opened the package and inside was a beautiful, green ball. He jumped with glee and said, "This looks like so much fun!" The little frog was so excited that he decided to dive right in. He grabbed the green ball and jumped into the air. He waved his arms and legs to stay afloat and then he started to swim. The little frog loved to dive around in the water with the green ball. He dove down to the bottom of the pond, where he found a secret cave. Inside, he found so many interesting things - like shells, rocks and bugs! The little frog had such a wonderful adventure! He swam back to the surface with his new findings and the green ball still in his hands. As he climbed out of the pond, the little frog couldn't wait to show his parents what he had found!

Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Tim. Tim liked to travel. One day, he went on a trip to see a new shore. Tim walked and walked until he found a big, sour lemon. The lemon was on the sand near the water. Tim thought it was a fun toy, so he played with it. After playing, Tim looked around and saw his new shore. It was so pretty! He saw many new friends to play with. Tim was so happy he traveled to the new shore.

Once upon a time, a small cat named Tiny lived in a big house. Tiny liked to rub his soft fur on everything. He had a powerful friend named Max, and Max was a big dog. Tiny and Max liked to play together in the yard. One day, Tiny saw a new sight in the yard. It was a big, red ball. Tiny said to Max, "Look at that big, red ball! Let's play with it!" Max said, "Yes, let's play! But be careful, Tiny. The ball is very big!" Tiny and Max played with the big, red ball all day. They had so much fun! But then, the ball rolled away very fast. Tiny and Max ran after it. They remembered to be careful, just like Max said before. The ball rolled into a bush, and Tiny and Max stopped just in time. They found the ball, and they were so happy! Tiny and Max played with the big, red ball every day. They knew that being careful was important. And they always had fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside and find new things. One day, he saw a big stone in his yard. He had an idea to build something with the stone. Tim found more stones and started to build. He built a small wall with the stones. It took a long time, but Tim did not give up. He was very happy with his wall. A little bird came and sat on the wall. Tim and the bird became friends. They played together every day. Tim and the bird had lots of fun with the stone wall that he built.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue. They were friends who loved to play together. One day, they found a big, shiny spoon. They both wanted to play with the spoon, but there was only one. Tim said, "Let's share the spoon, Sue." Sue was grateful for Tim's idea. They took turns playing with the spoon, and they were both happy. As they played, they found out that the spoon was magic. When they shared the spoon, it made their toys come to life. They had so much fun playing with their toys and the magic spoon. In the end, they learned that sharing made everything more fun.
