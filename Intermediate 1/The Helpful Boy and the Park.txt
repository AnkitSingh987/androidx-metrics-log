Category: Intermediate
Title: The Helpful Boy and the Park

Once there was a helpful boy. He was playing in the park when he saw a clown. The clown was juggling and smiling. The boy liked the clown and wanted to buy it. The boy asked his dad for money so he could buy the clown. Dad gave him the money. The boy ran back to the park and gave the money to the clown. The clown was very happy and gave the boy a hug. The boy went home and put the clown in his bedroom. The boy and the clown were happy to be friends. The boy played with his clown every day and the clown was very helpful. He taught the boy how to juggle and do tricks. The boy and the clown were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a gate. One day, the gate was left open. The animals saw the open gate and went out to play. The cow and the pig found a tree with sour apples. The cow said, "I like these sour apples!" The pig said, "Me too!" They ate and ate until their bellies were full. The farmer saw the open gate and the animals playing. He knew he had to save them from getting lost. He called, "Come back, animals!" The cow and the pig heard him and ran back to the farm. The farmer closed the gate and the animals were safe. They all lived happily on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little girl named Lily. She loved to play and make things with her hands. One day, she found a big roll of tape in her house. She was very happy and thought of a fun idea. Lily decided to make a beautiful crown with the tape. She used the tape to stick colorful papers together. She made a big circle and put it on her head. It looked so pretty! Lily felt like a queen. Lily's friends saw her clever crown and wanted one too. So, Lily helped them make their own crowns with the tape. They all had fun and played together like kings and queens. They were very happy and proud of their clever tape crowns.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely dog named Spot. He lived in a small house with his friend, Tom the cat. They liked to play and run outside. One day, Spot had an urge to go far away from his house. Spot said to Tom, "I want to leave and see new things." Tom said, "Don't go, Spot. We are happy here." But Spot did not listen. He quit playing and left his friend Tom. He walked and walked and saw many new things. As Spot went farther, he got lost. He felt sad and missed Tom. He tried to find his way back, but he could not. In the end, Spot was still lonely and lost. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and be happy with what you have.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends. They would run and jump in the park all day. Max was big and strong, and all his friends liked him. One day, Max saw a little stand in the park. It had many colorful toys for dogs to play with. Max and his friends went to the stand and picked their favorite toys. They played with the toys all day long, wagging their tails and barking happily. As the sun went down, Max and his friends were tired but very happy. They had a great day playing with the toys from the stand. Max felt like the most powerful dog in the park. He knew that playing with his friends was the best way to have fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She wanted to help her mom cook. Her mom said, "Amy, you can try to cook with me today." Amy was very happy. Amy's mom took out a big pan. It was an expensive pan. Amy and her mom put food in the pan. They cooked and cooked. The food smelled good. Amy tried to stir the food in the pan. She was careful. Then, Amy's friend Tom came to play. Tom wanted to touch the pan too. But Amy said, "No, Tom. This is an expensive pan. We need to be careful." Tom listened to Amy. They all ate the food together. It was yummy. The moral of the story is to be careful with things that are important and to listen to your friends when they give good advice.
