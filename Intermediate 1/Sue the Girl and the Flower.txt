Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Flower

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a delicate flower that was wet from the rain. Sue wanted to touch it, but she didn't know if she should. Sue asked her mom, "Can I touch the wet flower?" Her mom said, "Be very gentle, Sue. It is delicate." Sue touched the flower very softly. But then, something unexpected happened. The flower started to talk! It said, "Thank you for being gentle, Sue." Sue and her mom were very surprised. They didn't know flowers could talk.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim saw an elderly man with a long stick. The man used the stick to point at birds in the sky. Tim wanted to do that too, but he did not have a stick. Tim's mom saw that he was sad. She had an idea. She told Tim to wait and went to the car. She came back with a long stick. Tim was so happy! He took the stick and started to point at the birds like the elderly man. But then, Tim saw a big dog. The dog wanted his stick. The dog jumped and tried to take it away from Tim. Tim was scared. But the elderly man came and helped Tim. He showed Tim how to point the stick high, so the dog could not reach it. Tim did it, and the dog went away. Tim felt like a success. He thanked the elderly man, and they both pointed at the birds together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was 3 years old and wanted to be a brave player. "Mommy, can I be a player?" asked Sarah. "Yes, of course!" her mom said with a smile. So Sarah put on her fancy dress and ran outside to play. She promised to have lots of fun and never stop playing. But Sarah soon got tired and needed to sleep. "I will never break my promise," she said, hugging her mom. So Sarah kept on playing until she fell asleep on the grass, dreaming of a land filled with players and fancy games.

One day, a bee named Billy was in his hive. Billy was a fast bee. He liked to fly around and play with his friends. They would race each other and have fun. Billy always managed to win the races because he was so fast. One day, Billy and his friends were racing, and they saw a big bear. The bear said, "I want to race too!" Billy and his friends were scared, but they said, "Okay, you can race with us." They all started to race, and the bear was very fast too. As they raced, the bear managed to get ahead of Billy. Billy was surprised and thought, "How can the bear be so fast?" But then, Billy saw that the bear was not running anymore. The bear stopped and said, "I just wanted to make new friends. I am lonely." Billy and his friends felt sorry for the bear and invited him to join their hive. From that day on, they all played and raced together as friends.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the zoo with her mom. They saw many animals like big elephants, tall giraffes, and funny monkeys. Mia was very happy. At the zoo, Mia received a brilliant red balloon from a nice man. She held it tight so it would not fly away. Mia and her mom walked and talked. "Look at the lions, Mia!" her mom said. Mia loved the lions. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and popped Mia's balloon! Mia was sad, but the bird was sorry. The bird gave Mia a pretty feather to make her feel better. Mia smiled and said, "Thank you, bird!" Her day at the zoo was still fun, even with the surprise.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big red boot. He loved his boot very much. Every night, he would put the boot next to his bed before he went to sleep. One morning, Tim woke up and saw that his boot was not next to his bed. He looked all around his room but could not find it. He felt sad and wanted to find his boot. Tim went outside and saw his boot far away, near a big tree. He ran to the tree, picked up his boot, and felt very happy. From that day on, Tim always made sure his boot was close to him when he went to sleep.
