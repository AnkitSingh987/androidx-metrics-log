Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sun

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He liked to imagine things. He thought about a big, yellow sun in the sky. He wanted to play outside. As Tim played, he saw a big box. He did not know where it came from. Tim was very surprised. He wanted to see what was inside the box. Tim opened the box and saw a big, yellow ball. It was like the sun he imagined! He was so happy. Tim played with the ball all day. The sun ball made his day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful fish. His name was Max. Max loved to swim in the deep, blue sea. He was always looking for a new event to make his swimming more fun. One day, Max came across a beautiful beach. He swam up to the shore and asked a passing bird, “what’s happening here?” The bird replied, “Today is a very special event! Every year, all the powerful creatures of the ocean come together to race. Will you join?” Max happily accepted the invitation! He lined up beside the other powerful swimmers and waited for the race to start. When the race began, Max gave it his all and won the event! Max was so proud of himself and swam back to the beach, with a big smile on his face. From then on, he would always remember his big win, and visit the beach whenever he felt like a swim.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. Tom had a big house, many toys, and a long rope. One day, Tom saw a little girl named Sue playing outside. Sue was not wealthy like Tom, but she was happy. Tom went to Sue and said, "Hi Sue! Do you want to play with my rope?" Sue was excited and said, "Yes, Tom! I would love to play with your rope!" They played with the rope all day, and they had so much fun. That night, Sue went to bed and had a dream. In her dream, she was playing with Tom and his rope again. They were laughing and having a great time. When Sue woke up, she was happy because she had a fun dream. She knew that even if she was not wealthy like Tom, she could still have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, heavy toy box full of fun toys. One day, Tim wanted to move his toy box to a new spot in his room. Tim tried to push the heavy toy box, but it was too hard. He felt sad and sat down on the floor. His mom came into the room and saw Tim. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I want to move my toy box, but it's too heavy. I can't do it." His mom smiled and said, "Let's try together. I will help you." Tim and his mom both pushed the heavy toy box. With their help, the toy box moved to the new spot. Tim was very happy and thanked his mom. From that day on, Tim learned that life was easier when you try things together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big yellow hat. He loved the hat so much. He wore it all day and all night. He showed it to his friend, Sam. "Look at my yellow hat!" said Tim. Sam liked the hat too. He wished he had one just like it. One day, Tim got a letter in the mail. It was from his grandma. She said she was coming to visit him. Tim was very happy. He wanted to show his grandma his yellow hat. He knew she would like it too. When grandma came, Tim ran to her with his yellow hat. "Look, grandma! I have a yellow hat!" he said. Grandma smiled and gave Tim a big hug. "I have a surprise for you," she said. She opened her bag and took out another yellow hat! It was just like Tim's hat. She gave it to Sam. Now, Tim and Sam both had big yellow hats. They were so happy and played together all day with their yellow hats.
