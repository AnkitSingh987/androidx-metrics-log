Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a yummy candy bracelet. She loved her bracelet very much. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. Before she left, her mom told her, "Sally, be careful with your bracelet." At the park, Sally met a big, scary dog. The dog saw her yummy bracelet and wanted it. The dog said, "Give me your bracelet!" Sally was scared, but she did not want to give her bracelet to the dog. She thought of what her mom said and knew she had to be brave. Then, Sally had an idea. She said, "I will give you my bracelet, but first, you have to do a trick!" The dog was excited and did a big jump. When the dog jumped, Sally quickly hid her bracelet behind her back. The dog looked everywhere, but he could not find it. Sally said, "You did a good trick, but I tricked you too!" The dog was not angry, but laughed. He said, "You are smart, little girl. I surrender." And they became friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. It was a nice Wednesday. Tim liked to find unusual things on his walks. Today, he found a big, red ball. He was very happy. Tim played with the big, red ball all day. He threw it up and down, and he rolled it around. But then, something unexpected happened. The big, red ball hit a tree and broke. Tim was very sad. A nice lady saw Tim and wanted to comfort him. She gave him a big, soft hug. Then, she showed him a secret. The big, red ball was not broken. It was two smaller balls. Tim was happy again. He played with his new balls and had a great day.

One day, a bald man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a bird in it. The bird was singing a nice song. Tom asked the bird, "Why are you singing?" The bird did not answer. Tom was sad. He wanted to know why the bird was singing. He walked to the town and met his friend Sam. Sam was a nice man who knew many things. Tom asked Sam, "Why did the bird not answer me?" Sam said, "I think the bird does not know the law of talking to people." Tom and Sam went back to the tree. They saw that the bird was gone. They were surprised. They looked around and saw the bird on the ground. The bird said, "I am sorry I did not answer you. I did not know the law. But now I do, and I will talk to you." Tom and Sam were very happy. They talked to the bird and learned many new things. They all became good friends and had a great time together.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She wanted to see something new and fun. As she walked, she saw a big, red ball. It was hot from the sun. Amy liked the hot ball and wanted to play with it. Amy kicked the hot ball and it rolled far away. She ran after it and saw a rare, pretty bird. The bird was blue and yellow. Amy had never seen a bird like that before. She thought it was very special. The rare bird flew away, and Amy felt happy to have seen it. She picked up the hot ball and went back home. She told her mom about the rare bird and the hot ball. Her mom smiled and said, "What a fun day you had!"

Once upon a time, there was a driver named Tom. He drove a big bus with many people in it. Tom was a very good driver, but one day he felt uncomfortable. His tummy hurt, and he did not know what to do. Tom did not want to lose his job, so he kept driving the bus. But his tummy hurt more and more. The people on the bus saw that Tom was not feeling good. They wanted to help him. A kind lady on the bus gave Tom some water to drink. Tom drank the water and felt a little better. He said thank you to the kind lady. After that, Tom's tummy did not hurt so much. He was happy he did not lose his job, and he kept being a good driver.
