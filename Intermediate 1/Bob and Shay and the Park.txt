Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and Shay and the Park

Once upon a time there were two friends, Bob and Shay. They were always together and were always having lots of fun. One day they were in the park playing and they got into a quarrel. Bob said something to hurt Shay’s feelings and Shay started to cry. Bob was very sad he had made Shay cry. Shay’s dad came to help them. He said that a healthy friendship means to forgive each other and to patch up the quarrel. He asked Bob to help crack open a walnut for a sweet treat for them all. Bob cracked open the walnut, and he and Shay shared it between them. After that Bob and Shay started to laugh and play nicely again. From then on, Bob and Shay stayed the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Tilly. She loved to eat cream. One day, Tilly saw a big bowl of cream on the table. She was very happy and jumped up to get it. But Tilly did not know that the cream belonged to her friend, Ben the dog. When Ben came home, he saw the empty bowl and was very sad. He did not know that Tilly ate his cream. Ben thought that he was ignorant and had forgotten to eat it. Tilly felt bad and wanted to tell Ben the truth, but she was scared. Later, Tilly found a new bowl of cream and gave it to Ben. She said, "I'm sorry, Ben. I ate your cream." Ben was surprised, but he chose to forgive Tilly. They shared the new cream and stayed best friends forever. And from that day on, they always shared their treats with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a gift. He could urge his friends to share their toys. Tim liked to help, and he was always available to play with his friends. One day, Tim saw two of his friends, Sam and Sue, fighting over a toy. Sam wanted to play with the toy, but Sue did not want to share it. Tim knew he had to help his friends. Tim went to Sam and Sue and urged them to share the toy. He said, "Sam, Sue, let's share the toy and play together." Sam and Sue listened to Tim and decided to share the toy. They played together and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim's gift helped his friends to be happy and play together. Everyone was glad that Tim was always available to help. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim had a huge goat. The goat was his best friend. They played and ate together every day. Tim loved his goat very much. One day, a girl named Sue asked Tim to lend her his huge goat. She wanted to play with the goat too. Tim was not sure, but he wanted to be nice. So, he let Sue take his goat to her house for one day. Sue took the goat home and played with it. But then, something unexpected happened. The goat started to shrink! It became very small. Sue was scared and didn't know what to do. She took the small goat back to Tim. When Tim saw his goat, he was very surprised. But he was not mad. He still loved his goat, even if it was small. Tim and Sue became friends, and they all played together with the small goat. And that's how Tim learned that it's not the size of the goat that matters, but the love you have for it.

Once upon a time, in a big, blue sea, there was a pretty reef. Many fish lived there, and they loved to play together. One day, a big, dangerous shark came near the reef. The fish were very scared. A brave fish named Tim saw the shark and wanted to help his friends. Tim had a plan. He swam up to the shark and said, "Hey, Mr. Shark, I know a place where you can find lots of food. Follow me!" The shark thought about it and decided to follow Tim. Tim led the shark far away from the reef. They swam and swam until they reached a place where the water was deep and dark. Tim said, "Here, Mr. Shark, you can soar and find lots of food." The dangerous shark was happy and left the fish alone. Tim went back to the reef, and all the fish were safe and happy. They thanked Tim for being so brave and smart, and they continued to play together in their beautiful reef home.
