Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Tree

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his toy. His toy was a small, persistent dog named Spot. They liked to walk together in the park. As they walked, they saw a big tree. Tim and Spot wanted to play near the tree. "Let's play here, Spot," said Tim. Spot barked and wagged his tail. They played and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew Spot up into the tree. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim asked a tall man for help. The man tried to get Spot out of the tree, but he couldn't reach. Tim felt sad, but then he had an idea. He asked the man to lift him up high. The man lifted Tim, and Tim was able to grab Spot. They were so happy to be together again. They thanked the man and went back to their walk.

Once upon a time, there was a gloomy forest. In this forest, there was a little spirit. This spirit was very sad because it had no friends. It would float around the trees, looking for someone to play with. One day, the spirit found a small toy. It was a toy car with a string. The spirit had an idea. It could attach the string to itself and pull the car around. Then, it would have something fun to do. The spirit played with the toy car all day. It felt happy and not gloomy anymore. Soon, other spirits saw the fun and came to play too. They all became friends and had a great time playing together in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play catch with his friends. They would throw a ball and try to catch it. Tim was very good at this game. One day, there was a big catch game in the park. The prize was a perfect shiny trophy. Tim wanted to win the trophy, so he practiced every day. His friend, Sam, said, "You can do it, Tim! You're the best at catch!" The day of the game came, and Tim was ready. He played catch with his friends and caught the ball every time. At the end of the game, Tim won the perfect trophy. He was so happy, and his friends cheered for him. Tim said, "Thank you, friends! Let's play catch again tomorrow!" And they all played catch happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his best friend, a little boy named Tim. One day, they found a mask in the park. It was a pretty mask, with many colors. They decided to keep it and play with it later. The next day, Tim and Spot went to the park again. They saw a sad girl named Lily. Lily was looking for her lost mask. Tim and Spot knew they had to help her. They remembered the mask they found the day before, and thought it might be Lily's. Tim and Spot showed Lily the mask they found. She was so happy! But the mask was broken. Tim, Spot, and Lily worked together to fix the mask. They used glue and tape to make it as good as new. Once the mask was fixed, Lily thanked Tim and Spot for their help. From that day on, Tim, Spot, and Lily became best friends. They played in the park every day and had lots of fun. And they always remembered how they helped each other and fixed the mask.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a gray toy. It was a microscope! He was very happy. Tim liked to look at things with his new toy. He saw many small things that he could not see before. One day, Tim went to the park with his microscope. He saw a big tree and wanted to look at its leaves. He lay under the tree and looked up. He saw a small gray bug on the leaf. It was so small, he could only see it with his microscope. But then, something unexpected happened. The gray bug started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim was surprised. The bug was not a bug at all. It was a tiny gray bird! The bird flew away and Tim laughed. He loved his new toy and the fun things it showed him.
