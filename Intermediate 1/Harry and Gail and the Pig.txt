Category: Intermediate
Title: Harry and Gail and the Pig

Once upon a time there were two friends, Harry and Gail. One day, they decided to have a picnic. So they gathered some food and took a picnic basket. “We need something else to make this picnic special,” said Gail. Harry thought for a moment. “I know! Let’s get a pig!” So Harry and Gail went to the farm to get a pig. At the farm, they met a farmer who had a lucky pig. “Can you supply us with your lucky pig?” asked Harry. The farmer nodded. “Sure, take him with you!” So Harry and Gail were lucky. They took the pig to the picnic, and it made the day very special for them. They had a lot of fun with their pig. The end!

Once upon a time, in a land full of yummy fruit, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat fruit every day. She liked apples, bananas, and grapes. Lily would always say "please" when she wanted some fruit. One sunny day, Lily went to the fruit garden with her mom. She saw a big, yummy apple on a tree. Lily asked her mom, "Can I have the apple, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily!" She picked the apple and gave it to Lily. Lily was very happy. After eating the yummy apple, Lily and her mom continued to walk through the fruit garden. They saw more yummy fruit like oranges and strawberries. Lily said "please" each time she wanted some fruit. Her mom was very proud of Lily for being so polite. They went home with a big basket full of yummy fruit, and Lily shared it with her friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her mom. Sue wanted to know where the bathroom was. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is the bathroom?" Her mom said, "The bathroom is down the hall, on the left." Sue went to the bathroom. She saw a rare toy on the floor. It was a small, blue car. Sue picked it up and smiled. She wanted to play with the car. Sue went back to her mom. She showed her mom the rare car. Her mom looked at it and then shrugged. "I don't know whose car that is," her mom said. Sue didn't care. She played with the car all day long.

One day, a big bear named Bob wanted to invite his friends to his house. He wanted to have a fun party on Wednesday. Bob was very excited to see his friends. On Wednesday, all of Bob's friends came to his house. They played games, ate food, and had lots of fun. But then, something serious happened. A little mouse came into the house and scared all of Bob's friends. The little mouse said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to scare you. I just wanted to join the party too." Bob thought about it and decided to invite the little mouse to the party. Everyone was happy and they all played together. The little mouse became their new friend, and they all had a great time.

Molly was out for a stroll by the beach with her mom. She looked around and saw an attractive puddle. She asked her mom to listen as she squished her toes in the puddle. Molly's mom smiled and said, "It sounds like you're having fun playing in the puddle." Molly replied, "Yes! This puddle is so much fun. I want to stay here all day." Molly's mom chuckled and said, "No, we can't stay here all day." She spread her arms out and said, "But listen to all the birds. They are so happy and I bet they'd be glad if you joined in by singing a song with them." Molly laughed and then sang out a song as she skipped around the puddle. Even though she had been sad about leaving the puddle, she ended up having an even more fun time playing and singing with the birds.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little train. It loved to go on the track every day. The train would go fast and slow, and all the kids would wave and cheer. One day, the train saw a big tree on the track. The train knew it had to stop. So, it slowed down and stopped just in time. The train couldn't go on the track with the tree in the way. A nice man came to help. He had a big truck that could move the tree. The man said, "I will move the tree for you, little train." The train was very happy. The man moved the tree off the track, and the train could go again. The train said, "Thank you, nice man!" The man said, "You're welcome, lovely train!" The train went on the track again, and all the kids waved and cheered. The train was happy to yield for the tree and keep everyone safe. And they all lived happily ever after.
