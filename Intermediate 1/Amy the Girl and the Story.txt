Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to gather her toys and play with them all day. One day, she found a broken photo of her family on the floor. Amy was sad and wanted to fix it. She went to her mommy and said, "Mommy, look! The photo is broken. Can we fix it?" Mommy looked at the broken photo and shook her head. "I'm sorry, Amy. It's too broken to fix. We must be careful with our things." Amy felt very sad and cried. She learned that when we don't take care of our things, they can break and be gone forever. The moral of the story is to always be careful with the things we love.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bobo saw a big pot on the ground. He thought it was a perfect place to rest. Bobo flew down and sat on the pot. He felt very happy. Just then, a little girl named Amy came by. She saw Bobo on the pot and said, "Hello, chubby bird! Why are you sitting on my pot?" Bobo replied, "I like this pot. It is a good place to rest." Amy smiled and said, "You can rest on my pot, but be careful. The pot has a surprise inside." The next day, Bobo was flying high in the sky again. He remembered the pot and the surprise inside. He flew down to see what the surprise was. When he opened the pot, he found yummy seeds inside. Bobo was so happy and ate the seeds. From that day on, Bobo and Amy became best friends, and Bobo always knew where to find a tasty treat.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to decorate his vest. He had an easy plan. He would use colors and stickers to make it look nice. Tim was very excited to start. As Tim began to decorate, something unexpected happened. His dog, Spot, came into the room and saw the stickers. Spot thought they were treats and began to eat them. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. Tim had an idea. He would use his colors to draw pictures of treats on his vest. Spot would not be able to eat them. It was an easy plan. So, Tim finished decorating his vest with the pictures. Spot looked at the vest but did not try to eat it. Tim was happy, and they both had fun.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. The sky was gray and it began to rain. Mia did not like the harsh rain, so she ran to a big tree for cover. The rain was cold and wet, but the tree kept her dry. Under the tree, Mia found a small boy named Tim. Tim was eating gum. He said, "Hi, my name is Tim. Do you want some gum?" Mia smiled and said, "Yes, thank you!" They both chewed the gum and it made them happy. As they stood under the tree, the rain stopped. Mia and Tim became good friends. They played together until the sun came out. They were happy they had met under the tree on a harsh, rainy day.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little girl. Everywhere she went, she wanted to explore and find out more. One busy day she saw a pear on the ground and she was delighted. She picked it up and said “Look, it’s a pear!” Her mum smiled and said “Yes, it’s a nice pear, isn’t it? I think it’s time for lunch now.” The little girl responded “No, I want to keep it!” Mum replied, “I’m afraid you can’t keep it. It’s time for lunch.” The little girl was determined and insisted “I want to keep it!”, but her mum was insistent and the little girl had to give up the pear and go and have her lunch. The little girl was disappointed, but she knew that her mum was right.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. It was a wet day, and there were many puddles on the ground. Tim liked to jump in the puddles and watch the water splash. At the park, Tim saw a row of ducks near the pond. He wanted to gather some bread to feed them. He asked his mom, "Mom, can we give the ducks some food?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. Let's go get some bread for the ducks." They went home to get the bread and came back to the park. Tim and his mom stood in front of the row of ducks and threw the bread to them. The ducks were very happy, and Tim was happy too. After feeding the ducks, they went home, and Tim told his dad all about the fun day at the park.
