Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Hat

One day, a dog named Spot wanted to act in a play. Spot was very glad because he loved to play and have fun. He put on a funny hat and went to the street to show his friends. In the street, Spot met his friend, a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy saw Spot's funny hat and she started to laugh. She thought Spot looked very silly. Spot was happy to make Fluffy laugh, so they both acted in a funny play together. All the animals in the street came to watch Spot and Fluffy act. They laughed and clapped as the two friends acted and played. At the end of the play, everyone was glad and had a big party. Spot and Fluffy were very happy to make their friends smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little car. The car was red and very fast. One day, the car went on a long trip. It drove and drove until it was far from home. The car began to wonder where it was. The car saw a big truck. The truck was angry because it was out of oil. The car wanted to help the truck, so it went to find some oil. The car drove and drove until it found a store with oil. The car bought the oil and went back to the truck. The truck was very happy. The car gave the truck the oil, and the truck was not angry anymore. The car and the truck became friends, and they went on many fun trips together.

Once upon a time, there was a fat sheep named Woolly. Woolly lived on a big farm with many friends. They liked to play and eat grass all day long. One day, Woolly was eating grass when something happened. A little girl named Lucy came to the farm. She saw Woolly and said, "Hello, fat sheep! I like your wool. Can I play with you?" Woolly was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" They played all day and had lots of fun. Lucy liked Woolly's soft wool and Woolly liked to make Lucy laugh. They became best friends and played together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his ball outside. He saw his friend, Sam, and wanted to pass the ball to him. So, Tim threw the ball, and Sam caught it. They were having a lot of fun. Then, they heard a loud noise. It was an emergency! A little cat was stuck in a tree. Tim and Sam looked at each other and knew they had to help. They were both very thoughtful and wanted to save the cat. Tim said, "Sam, let's pass the ball to the cat. Maybe she will grab it and come down." Sam thought it was a good idea. So, they passed the ball to the cat. The cat grabbed the ball and climbed down the tree. The cat was safe, and Tim and Sam were happy. They knew they had done a good thing by helping the cat in an emergency.

One day, a little girl named Sue was feeling restless. She wanted to go outside and play, but her mom said she had to stay inside and listen to a lecture. Sue did not like lectures. They were boring and made her want to wiggle. Sue's mom unlocked the door to the room where the lecture was happening. She said, "Sue, you must sit and listen. It is important." Sue sat down, but she was still restless. She looked around the room and saw a big red ball. She really wanted to play with it. Just as Sue was about to get up and play with the ball, a funny thing happened. A big dog walked in and started to talk! The dog said, "Hello, I am here to give the lecture!" Sue was so surprised. She forgot about the ball and listened to the dog talk. She was not restless anymore. The dog's lecture was fun and Sue had a great time.

One day, a cool cat named Tom went for a walk. He wore a big red bow on his neck. As he walked down the street, he saw a dog named Max. Max was a funny dog who liked to laugh. "Hi Max," said Tom. "Do you like my red bow?" Max looked at Tom's bow and started to laugh. "Yes, Tom! It looks very cool on you!" Max said, still laughing. As they walked together, they saw a big box. They did not know what was inside. Tom and Max opened the box, and out came a big bunch of balloons! The balloons lifted Tom and Max up into the sky. They laughed and laughed as they flew high above the town, holding their balloons. And everyone below looked up and saw the cool cat and the funny dog, flying with their big red bow and balloons.
