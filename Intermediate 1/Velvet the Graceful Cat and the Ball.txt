Category: Intermediate
Title: Velvet the Graceful Cat and the Ball

One day, a graceful cat named Velvet went for a walk. She was soft and pretty. Velvet liked to play with her friends in the park. In the park, Velvet met a dog named Max. Max said, "Hi Velvet, do you want to play with me?" Velvet said, "Yes, Max. Let's play catch!" Max held a ball in his mouth and threw it to Velvet. As they played, a big wind came and took the ball away. Velvet and Max were sad. But then, a nice bird flew down and gave them the ball. They were happy and played all day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Lily paused for a moment and looked up at the tree. It was very tall, but she was brave. As she climbed, Lily saw a bird in the tree. The bird was a color she did not know. "Hello, bird," said Lily. "What kind of bird are you?" The bird looked at her and said, "I am an unknown bird. I am the only one like me." Lily and the unknown bird became friends. They played in the tree and had lots of fun. When it was time for Lily to go home, she said goodbye to her new friend. "Bye, unknown bird. I will come back and play with you again soon."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her cat, Mittens. One day, Lily was playing with Mittens in the backyard. It was a clear and sunny day. Lily's mom called out to her, "Lily, can you help me hang the clothes outside?" Lily said, "Yes, mom!" She ran to help her mom. They took the clothes out of the bin and hung them on the line together. Mittens watched them and played with the clothes as they hung in the sun. After they finished hanging the clothes, Lily's mom said, "Thank you, Lily! You are a big help!" Lily smiled and felt proud. She went back to playing with Mittens in the yard. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a big green cactus. The cactus had sharp points on it. Amy did not dare to touch the cactus. She knew it could hurt her. Amy walked a bit more and felt cold. She saw a small, cold river. She put her hand in the water and felt the cold. She did not dare to swim in the cold river. Amy walked back home and told her mom about the cactus and the cold river. Her mom said, "Good job, Amy! You were smart not to touch the cactus or swim in the cold river." Amy was happy she made the right choices.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the beach with his mom and dad. They wanted to build a big sandcastle. Tom found a big, fit stick to help him build. He dug and dug, and the sandcastle grew bigger and bigger. While digging, Tom found a big, heavy anchor. He showed it to his mom and dad. They were very surprised. They decided to put the anchor on top of the sandcastle to make it look cool. Just then, a big wave came and washed the sandcastle away. But the anchor stayed in place. The wave left a big hole where the sandcastle was. Tom, his mom, and dad laughed. They did not expect that to happen! They had a fun day at the beach.

Once upon a time, a little seed wanted to grow big and strong. It wanted to be independent and not need help from anyone. It was in the ground, waiting for the sun and rain to help it grow. One day, a bird came by and found a shiny whistle on the ground. The bird picked up the whistle and blew it. Suddenly, a big cloud appeared and it started to rain. The little seed was very happy because it could now grow. The little seed grew and grew, but something unexpected happened. Instead of becoming a big tree, the seed turned into a big whistle! The bird was surprised too. The seed was now a big, independent whistle that made a loud sound when the wind blew. And they all lived happily ever after.
