Category: Intermediate
Title: Anna and the Park

Anna was a small girl who liked to wander. She liked to see new things and meet new people. One day, she wandered too far from her home. She saw a big park with many flowers and swings. She wanted to play there. She ran to the park and saw a boy with a watch. The watch was shiny and had many colors. Anna liked the watch. She went to the boy and said, "Hello, I am Anna. Can I see your watch?" The boy looked at Anna and smiled. He said, "Hello, I am Ben. Sure, you can see my watch. It is a gift from my dad. He said it can tell time and play music. Do you want to hear?" Anna nodded. She was curious. Ben pressed a button on his watch and a song started to play. Anna and Ben danced and laughed. They had fun. But then, Anna heard a voice. It was her mom. She was calling her name. She sounded worried. Anna stopped dancing and looked around. She saw her mom running to her. She was angry and scared. "Anna, where have you been? I have been looking for you everywhere. You can't wander off like that. It is dangerous. You could get lost or hurt. You have to stay close to me. Do you understand?" Anna felt sorry. She did not mean to make her mom worry. She said, "I am sorry, mom. I just wanted to see the park. And I met Ben. He has a nice watch. He is my friend." Her mom hugged her and said, "I am glad you made a friend, but you have to be careful. You have to tell me where you are going. And you have to ask before you touch other people's things. That is polite. Do you understand?" Anna nodded. She said, "I understand, mom. I will be careful and polite. Can I say goodbye to Ben?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can. But then we have to go home. It is getting late." Anna ran to Ben and said, "Bye, Ben. Thank you for showing me your watch. It is very cool. Maybe we can play again." Ben said, "Bye, Anna. You are welcome. I like your dress. It is very pretty. Maybe we can play again." Anna and Ben hugged and waved. Then Anna took her mom's hand and they walked home. Anna was happy. She had a new friend and a new story to tell. She still liked to wander, but she learned to be more careful and polite.

One morning, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a messy pile of sand. Tim wanted to play and bury his toy car in the sand. He thought it would be fun to hide it and find it again. As he was playing, a little girl named Sue came over. She saw Tim's toy car and wanted to play too. "Can I play with you?" she asked. Tim said, "Yes, let's bury the toy car and find it together!" They took turns hiding the toy car in the messy sand and finding it. They laughed and played all morning. Tim and Sue became good friends, and they loved playing together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Jack. He was playing in the park when he saw a pastry on a bench. He quickly ran over and pinched it, but then the parkkeeper came over. "That's not yours!" the parkkeeper said. Jack blushed and said, "I'm sorry. It looked so yummy!" The parkkeeper smiled and said, "Then why don't you have it? Take it on one condition - you have to share it with me!" Jack smiled and agreed. He ate the pastry together with the parkkeeper, and they talked and laughed until the pastry was all gone. Jack thanked the parkkeeper, waved goodbye, and ran off to play some more. He felt quite lively with a belly full of pastry!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was walking in the park with his mom. He saw a big statue. The statue was of a man with a terrible face. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. So, Tim managed to walk closer to the statue. He looked at the terrible face and thought, "Maybe the man is sad." Tim wanted to help the man feel better. He found a pretty flower and put it in the statue's hand. Suddenly, the statue started to move! It was not a statue at all, but a man who had been frozen. The man smiled at Tim and said, "Thank you for making me feel better." Tim was happy that he managed to help the man. From that day on, the man and Tim became good friends.
