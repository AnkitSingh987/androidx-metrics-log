Category: Intermediate
Title: The Duck and the Dog

One day, a little duck went for a wander. The duck saw a big, pretty pond. The duck wanted to swim in the pond. But there was a big, fancy gate around the pond. The duck did not know how to get in. The duck saw a big dog near the gate. The dog said, "I can help you get in, but you must give me something." The duck thought hard. Then the duck gave the dog a pretty feather. The dog took the feather and opened the gate. The duck was very happy and jumped into the pond. But then, something unexpected happened! The pond was not deep at all. It was just a big puddle! The duck was sad but then saw the dog laughing. The duck and the dog became friends and played in the puddle together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big dream. He wanted to go to space. He knew it would be hard, but he wanted to try. Spot worked and worked to build a great big rocket. He asked his friends to help. They all worked together. It took many days, but they did not give up. One day, the rocket was ready. Spot and his friends counted, "One, two, three!" Spot got in the rocket and went up, up, up! He succeeded! He was so happy to be in space. Spot looked down at his friends and waved. They were all proud of Spot, the little dog who went to space.

Once upon a time, there was a jet that flew through the sky. All day, it would travel alone in the vast sky, feeling lonely. When it became dark, the jet would look down at its home. Down there, it could see the people and houses glowing with lovely light. The jet always wished it could join in the warm glow, but it knew its home was in the sky. One night, the jet flew to the nearest town and it found that all the people had gone to bed. So the jet flew close to their houses and stared down at the glowing windows. In that moment, the jet felt like it was part of the town. It felt so happy that it started to glow too. The people in the town awoke to find the jet’s shining light. They were filled with joy to see this bright star in their sky. Then, the people realized that no matter how lonely they felt, there was still something special that could make them all happy. The moral of this story is that when you feel lonely, you can still be a light in someone else’s life.

Lily was a kid who loved to sing. She sang all the time, at home, at school, and in the park. She sang loud and happy songs that made people smile. One day, Lily's teacher told her that there was a big show at school. All the kids could sing or dance or tell jokes on a stage. Lily was very excited. She wanted to sing her favorite song for everyone. She practiced every day with her mom and dad. They helped her learn the words and the tune. They told her she was a great singer and hugged her. The day of the show came. Lily put on a pretty dress and a shiny bow. She waited with the other kids backstage. She saw some of them were nervous, but she was not. She was ready to sing. When it was her turn, she walked on the stage and smiled. She saw her mom and dad in the front row. They waved at her and cheered. She saw her teacher and her friends and many other people. They all looked at her and waited. She opened her mouth and sang her song. She sang loud and happy and clear. She sang with all her heart. She felt good and proud. When she finished, everyone clapped and cheered. They applauded her. They liked her song. She heard them say, "Wow, Lily, you are amazing! You are a star!" She bowed and smiled. She felt happy and warm. She ran to her mom and dad and hugged them. They hugged her back and said, "We are so proud of you, Lily! You did a wonderful job!" She thanked them and her teacher and her friends. She was a little sleepy, but she was very happy. She had sung her song and made everyone happy. She had done her best. She was a kid who loved to sing.
