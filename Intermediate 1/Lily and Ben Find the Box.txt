Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben Find the Box

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. One day, they find a big box in the corner. They open the box and see many things inside. They see a hat, a coat, a bag, and a stethoscope. They are happy and curious. "Look, Ben, we can play doctor!" Lily says. She puts on the hat and the coat. She takes the stethoscope and the bag. "You can be my patient. Lie down on the bed." "OK, Lily, I will be your patient." Ben says. He lies down on the bed. He pretends to be sick. He coughs and sneezes. "Doctor, I don't feel well. What is wrong with me?" Lily puts the stethoscope on Ben's chest. She listens to his heart. She looks at his eyes and his ears. She opens his mouth and says "Ahh". She takes a thermometer from the bag and puts it under his tongue. She waits for a minute and then looks at the thermometer. She frowns. "Oh, no, Ben, you have a fever. You are very hot. You need to go to the hospital." Lily says. She takes the thermometer out of his mouth. She puts it back in the bag. She helps Ben get up from the bed. "Come on, Ben, we have to hurry. The hospital is far away." They walk out of their room. They go down the stairs. They see their mom in the kitchen. She is making lunch. She sees them and smiles. "Hello, Lily and Ben. What are you doing?" She asks. She notices their clothes and their bag. She laughs. "Are you playing doctor?" "Yes, mom, we are playing doctor. But Ben is very sick. He has a fever. We have to go to the hospital." Lily says. She holds Ben's hand. She looks worried. Mom hugs them and kisses them. She says "Don't worry, Lily and Ben. Ben is not really sick. He is just pretending. You don't have to go to the hospital. You can stay here and have lunch. I made your favorite soup. It will make you feel better." Lily and Ben look at each other. They realize they were just playing. They smile and say "OK, mom, we will stay here and have lunch. Thank you for the soup. We love you." They take off their hat and coat. They put them and the bag back in the box. They go to the kitchen and sit at the table. Mom serves them the soup. It is hot and light. They eat the soup and feel warm and happy. They talk and laugh with mom. They forget about the hospital. They are glad they have each other. They are the best twins.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to write a letter to her friend, Tom. She got her paper, pen, and sat at her table. Mia was very excited to write the letter. But, she was also a messy writer. As Mia wrote, her words got messier and messier. She tried to make the words look nice, but they were still messy. Mia was sad. She wanted her letter to look good for Tom. Then, Mia's mom came into the room. She saw Mia's sad face and asked what was wrong. Mia told her mom about her messy writing. Her mom smiled and said, "Mia, you have the power to make your writing better. Just practice and try your best." Mia felt happy and tried again. With practice, her writing got better and better.

Once upon a time, there was a big, elderly cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived near a big gate. Every day, Fluffy would walk by the gate and say hello to her friend, a little bird named Chirpy. One day, Fluffy saw Chirpy with a pretty bird. Chirpy said, "Fluffy, this is my friend, Tweetie. We are going to marry!" Fluffy was happy for Chirpy and Tweetie. She wanted to help them get ready for the big day. On the day of the marry, Fluffy and Chirpy went to the gate. They waited for Tweetie, but she did not come. They looked and looked but could not find her. Suddenly, they saw a big dog running away with Tweetie in his mouth! Fluffy and Chirpy were scared, but they knew they had to save her. Fluffy and Chirpy chased the big dog. They ran and ran until they caught up to him. Fluffy jumped on the dog's back and Chirpy flew into his face. The big dog was so scared that he dropped Tweetie and ran away. Fluffy, Chirpy, and Tweetie were happy that they were safe. They went back to the gate and had the marry. They all lived happily ever after, and the big dog never bothered them again.
