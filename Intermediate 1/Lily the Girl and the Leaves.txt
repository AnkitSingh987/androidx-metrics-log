Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Leaves

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a boy named Tom. Tom was sad. Lily asked, "Why are you sad, Tom?" Tom said, "I lost my toy yesterday. It is difficult to find it." Lily wanted to help Tom. They looked for the toy together. They looked under the slide, behind the trees, and near the swings. They could not find the toy. They were tired and sat down. Just then, a big wind came and made the leaves wave. The leaves moved away and there was the toy! Tom was so happy. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily was happy too. They played together all day. The moral of the story is: helping others can make you happy and sometimes the unexpected can happen when you work together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore his red coat because it was cold outside. Tim saw a big pond with ducks in it. He wanted to play with the ducks, so he asked his mom, "Can I go near the pond?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't get too close." Tim walked slowly to the edge of the pond. He saw a duck with a tight blue ribbon on its neck. The duck looked funny, and Tim laughed. The duck then did a big dive into the water. Tim clapped his hands and said, "Wow, that was fun!" The duck came back up from the water and shook its feathers. Tim wanted to see the duck dive again. He asked, "Can you dive one more time, please?" The duck nodded and did another big dive. Tim laughed and clapped again. Tim's mom called him, "It's time to go home, Tim!" Tim waved goodbye to the duck and left the park with his mom. He couldn't wait to tell his friends about the funny duck with the tight blue ribbon that could dive into the water.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful lion. He had a big roar. The lion lived in the jungle. He had many friends there. The lion was the king of the jungle. One day, the lion found a new way in the jungle. He walked and walked. He saw many new things. He saw a big river. The lion had never seen a river before. The lion had a friend, a small bird. The bird could fly high in the sky. The bird saw the lion near the river. The bird flew down to the lion. The bird and the lion had a fun day by the river. They played and played. Then they went back home, happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly shop. It had many things like toys, balls, and yummy food. A girl named Amy loved to go to the shop. She would walk there with her mom. One day, Amy and her mom went to the shop. Amy saw a big red ball. She said, "Mom, can I have the red ball, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, you can have the red ball." Amy was very happy. She played with the red ball all day. She shared it with her friends too. They all had fun together. The friendly shop made everyone happy.

One day, in a classroom, a little girl named Ann had a question. She raised her hand and asked her teacher, "Can I look for the hidden toy?" The teacher smiled and said, "Yes, Ann. You can look for the hidden toy, but remember to share it with your friends." Ann started to look for the hidden toy. She looked under the tables and behind the chairs. Her friends watched her and started to help her look for the toy too. They all wanted to find the hidden toy and play with it. Finally, Ann found the hidden toy under a big box. It was a beautiful doll. She was so happy and showed it to her friends. But then, something unexpected happened. The doll started to talk! The doll said, "Thank you for finding me, Ann. Now, I want to tell you a secret. The best way to be happy is to share with your friends." Ann and her friends were surprised. They learned that sharing is important and it makes everyone happy. From that day on, they always shared their toys and played together. And they never forgot the talking doll's secret.
