Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim Finds the Ball

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to a new city with his mom and dad. They wanted to see new things and have fun. Tim was very excited to explore the big city. One day, they went to a big park in the city. Tim saw a big, red ball and wanted to play with it. He ran to the ball and kicked it very hard. But, oops! The ball flew and hit a man. Tim felt embarrass. He said sorry to the man. The man smiled and said it was okay. Tim learned to be more careful when playing. He had a great time in the new city with his mom and dad. They saw many fun things and met nice people. They all went home with happy memories of their new city adventure.

One day, a little pink cat named Lucy went for a walk. She liked to play outside. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. Lucy was very happy. As Lucy walked, she saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. But there was trouble! A small bird was stuck in the tree. The bird was sad and scared. Lucy wanted to help the bird. Lucy started to climb the tree. She was very brave. When she got close to the bird, she used her paw to point at the way down. The bird followed Lucy's paw and they both got down safely. The bird was happy and thanked Lucy. They became good friends and played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird who had a nest on a branch. The bird was very proud of her nest. She worked hard to make it the best nest in the tree. One day, a big wind came and blew the nest off the branch. The little bird was embarrass. She did not want her friends to see her nest on the ground. The little bird worked hard to fix her nest. She put it back on the branch and made it even better than before. She was proud of her nest again. And all her friends were happy for her.

Once upon a time, in a wide field, there was a lot of corn. A little boy named Tim lived near the field. He liked to play there with his toy car. One day, while playing, Tim noticed something shiny in the corn. He walked closer to see what it was. It was a tiny, pretty bird with a hurt wing. Tim felt sad for the bird. He knew he had to help it. So, he took the bird home and showed it to his mom. With love and care, they helped the bird get better. One day, while Tim was playing with the bird, it started to sing a beautiful song. All of a sudden, the corn in the field grew taller and stronger. Everyone in the town was happy and thanked Tim for helping the bird. The moral of the story is that when we help others, good things can happen to us too.

Once upon a time, there was a humble old man. He lived in a small house all by himself. The old man walked with a cane because his legs were not very strong. One sunny day, the old man went for a walk. He used his cane to help him walk down the path. As he walked, he saw a big, pretty butterfly. The butterfly was flying near some flowers. The old man wanted to see the butterfly up close. The old man turned to follow the butterfly. He walked slowly with his cane. The butterfly flew around, and the old man smiled. After a while, the butterfly flew away. The old man was happy to see the butterfly. He turned around and went back to his small house. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. They played in the sand and built a big sand castle. Tim was very happy. A poor little crab came up to Tim and said, "Please don't disturb my home." Tim looked at the crab and said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know this was your home." Just then, something unexpected happened. The sand castle started to shake and twist. The sand castle turned into a big, friendly sand monster! The sand monster said, "Thank you for not disturbing the crab's home. Let's all play together!" Tim, his mom, the crab, and the sand monster played all day at the beach. They had lots of fun and became good friends.
