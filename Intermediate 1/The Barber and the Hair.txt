Category: Intermediate
Title: The Barber and the Hair

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a barber. He had a new shop. The barber liked to cut hair. One day, a big man came to the shop. He had long hair. The barber said, "I can cut your hair." The big man sat down. The barber started to cut. But the big man did not like it. He said, "Stop! I don't like this." The barber was sad. He did not know what to do. So, he just gave a shrug. Then, a little girl came in. She had messy hair. The barber smiled and said, "I can make your hair nice." The little girl sat down. The barber cut her hair. She loved it! The girl was happy, and the barber was happy too. The end.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a toy. This toy was a powerful robot. Tim loved to play with his robot every day. He would tell the robot to do things like dance and sing. The robot made Tim very happy. One day, Tim was playing with his robot when he heard a voice. "Hello, Tim," said the voice. Tim looked around and saw that it was his robot talking to him! "I can talk now," the robot said. Tim was very surprised. Tim and the robot became good friends. They would play and tell each other stories every day. Tim was happy to have a talking robot friend, and they had lots of fun together. And that's how Tim's toy robot became his best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty cat named Tom. Tom loved to play outside on Sundays. One Sunday, he saw a little mouse named Jerry. Tom wanted to catch Jerry, but Jerry was very fast. Tom said, "I will catch you, Jerry!" Jerry laughed and said, "You can try, but you will not get me!" They played a game of catch all day. Tom tried and tried, but he could not catch Jerry. At the end of the day, Tom felt sad. He said, "I regret not catching you, Jerry." Jerry smiled and said, "That's okay, Tom. We can play again next Sunday." And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to go fast and zoom around the town. He had many friends, but sometimes he would zoom too fast and they could not keep up with him. One day, Zoomy went to a wide park to play with his friends. They all wanted to race, but Zoomy knew he would win. So, he thought of a plan to make the race fun for everyone. Zoomy decided to give his friends a head start, so they could all have a chance to win. The race began and Zoomy's friends went ahead. After a while, Zoomy started to zoom and caught up with his friends. In the end, one of his friends won the race and they were all happy. Zoomy learned that being thoughtful and helping others can make everyone feel good.

Once upon a time, there was a kind dog named Spot. Spot was very excite to go to the park. He loved to run and play with his friends in the sun. The park was a fun sight for all the kids and pets. One day, Spot saw a new friend at the park. It was a little bird. Spot wanted to play, so he ran to the bird. The bird was scared and flew away. Spot felt sad, but he still wanted to play. He tried to find other friends, but they were busy. Spot did not give up. He saw a big tree and had an idea. He tried to climb the tree to find the bird. But Spot was not good at climbing. He fell down and hurt his leg. Spot could not play anymore. The kind dog had a bad day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a brave knight called Sir Percy. He had a shiny sword that could cut through anything. He went on an adventure to save a princess. Sir Percy walked through the dark forest when suddenly he heard a loud roar. He was scared and started to shiver. He picked up his sword and held it tight. Suddenly, he saw a big dragon! The dragon was angry and roaring at Sir Percy. "Oh no!", thought Sir Percy. He knew he had to fight the dragon with his sword. He bravely stepped forward and held up his sword in front of him. The dragon stepped forward and blew a puff of hot smoke out of its nose. Sir Percy started to shiver even more. Then, the dragon opened its mouth and blew a soft breeze from its mouth. Sir Percy was surprised and he looked at the dragon. The dragon said, "I won't hurt you, brave knight. I only want to help you on your journey!" Sir Percy smiled and thanked the dragon. The dragon flew away and Sir Percy continued his journey, feeling a lot brave. He found the princess and they both lived happily ever after.
