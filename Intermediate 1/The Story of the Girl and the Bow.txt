Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Girl and the Bow

Once there was a little girl, who was very independent. She loved to make things on her own. One day, the little girl wanted to try and make a bow, so she got some ribbon and started tying it together. She tried and tried, but it was hard! After many attempts, she finally managed to get the bow ready. The little girl was so proud of her success, she wanted to show it to everyone. She went around the house and asked her parents "look what I made!". Her parents were very impressed and gave her a big hug. The little girl showed off her bow to all her friends the very next day. Her friends were amazed by her skill and they all cheered. She was so happy that she had managed to be so successful, all by herself. The little girl had truly become very independent, and she loved it. She would keep making things on her own and show off her hard earned skills proudly. The little girl had found her success.

Jack was walking in the park when he saw Alison. She was laughing and eating a big onion. Jack couldn't believe he didn't think of eating an onion first. He was so jealous! "Hi Alison!" Jack said. "Those onions look so delicious. May I have a bite?" Alison smiled. "Thanks for asking!" She said and gave Jack a piece. Jack enjoyed the onion. It was so delicious. "You're so lucky to be eating this!" Jack said. "I'm jealous!" "Don't be!" Alison responded. "You can come and eat with me anytime!" Jack was so pleased. They ate onions together every weekend and had a lot of fun.

One day, a friendly dog named Max had a birthday. He was very happy. Max wanted to have a big party with all his friends. He asked his mom, "Can I have a party with cake and games?" His mom said, "Yes, but you need to take a nap first." Max didn't want to nap, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he went to his bed and closed his eyes. But Max couldn't sleep. He was too excited about his birthday party. Max tried to nap, but he heard a noise outside. It was his friend, the cat. The cat was playing with a ball. Max wanted to play too. He thought, "If I don't nap, I can't have my party." Max knew what to do. He closed his eyes and thought about his birthday party. Soon, Max fell asleep and had a good nap. When Max woke up, his mom said, "Good job, Max! Now we can have your birthday party." Max's friends came over, and they all had a fun time. They played games, ate cake, and Max was happy he took his nap.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They saw a big hill with many trees and flowers. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Lila!" Ben said. "Maybe we can find a treasure or a fairy or a dragon!" "OK, Ben!" Lila said. "But we have to be careful and not get lost!" They held hands and started to climb the hill. They saw many birds and bugs and animals. They heard many sounds and smells. They felt happy and curious. They reached the top of the hill and looked around. They saw a big valley with many houses and cars and people. They saw a big sign that said "Welcome to the City". They saw many lights and colors and shapes. "Wow, Ben!" Lila said. "Look at all the things in the valley! They are so pretty and shiny and different!" "Yes, Lila!" Ben said. "They are very nice! The valley is displaying many things for us to see!" They sat on the hill and watched the valley for a long time. They saw the sun go down and the stars come out. They saw the valley change from day to night. They felt tired and sleepy. They decided to go back to the park. They climbed down the hill and walked to the slide. They saw their mom and dad waiting for them. "Hi, Lila and Ben!" Mom said. "Did you have fun in the park?" "Yes, Mom!" Lila and Ben said. "We climbed a hill and saw a valley! The valley was displaying many things for us to see!" "Wow, that sounds amazing!" Dad said. "You are very brave and adventurous! But now it's time to go home and rest. The park is empty and dark." "OK, Dad!" Lila and Ben said. "But can we come back tomorrow and see the valley again?" "Maybe, Lila and Ben!" Mom said. "But now let's go home and have a story and a hug and a kiss!" They got in the car and drove away. They waved goodbye to the park and the hill and the valley. They felt happy and sleepy. They dreamed of the valley and the things they saw.
