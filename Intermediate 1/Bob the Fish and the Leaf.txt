Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Fish and the Leaf

One day, a fish named Bob was excited to play with his friends. They all lived in a big reef. The reef was their home. Bob and his friends liked to swim and play games together. But one day, when they were playing, Bob got hurt. He swam too fast and hit a rock. It made him very sad. His friends were worried about him. They wanted to help Bob heal. Bob's friends all worked together. They found a big leaf from a sea plant. They put the leaf on Bob's hurt spot. After some time, the leaf made Bob heal. He felt better and could play with his friends again. They were all so happy to be together in their reef home.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Binky. Binky lived in a big forest with many friends. Binky was very kind and always helped his friends when they needed it. Binky's friends loved him very much because he was a good friend. One day, Binky heard his friends mention a spirit in the forest. The spirit was sad because nobody talked to it. Binky wanted to help the spirit feel better. So, he went to find the spirit and talk to it. When Binky found the spirit, he said, "Hello, I am Binky. I want to be your friend." The spirit smiled and said, "Thank you, Binky. I am happy now." Binky and the spirit became good friends. They played together every day. Binky's friends also became friends with the spirit. Everyone was happy and learned that being kind to others can make them happy too. The forest was a better place because Binky showed everyone the importance of being a good friend.

Once upon a time, a cheerful boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. Tim was not sick, but he needed to get an x-ray. He was a little scared, but his mom told him not to worry. She said it would be fast and easy. In the x-ray room, Tim saw a big machine. The doctor told him to stand still and smile. The machine made a little noise, and then it was done. Tim was happy it was over, but then something unexpected happened. The doctor looked at the x-ray and laughed. Tim's x-ray showed a big, funny smile on his face. The doctor said it was the first time he saw an x-ray like that. Tim and his mom laughed too. They went home feeling very happy and surprised.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a smart dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball. One day, Spot's ball rolled into the kitchen and fell into the sink. Spot was sad because he could not get his ball out. Spot had an idea. He saw a chair and thought he could use it to remove the ball from the sink. He pushed the chair to the sink and climbed up. He was very close to the ball, but he still could not reach it. He needed something more to help him. Spot looked around and saw a big spoon. He grabbed the spoon with his mouth and used it to remove the ball from the sink. Spot was very happy to have his ball back. He learned that being smart and using things around him could help solve his problems. And from that day on, Spot played with his ball, knowing he could always find a way to get it back if it got stuck again.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play outside when it is cold and there is snow. They make snowmen and snowballs and have fun. One day, they see a big hill with lots of snow. They want to slide down the hill on their sleds. Anna says, "Let's do it! It looks attractive and exciting!" Ben says, "I don't know. It looks dangerous and steep. Maybe we should not do it." Anna does not listen to Ben. She thinks he is scared and silly. She takes her sled and runs to the hill. She slides down very fast and screams with joy. Ben watches her and feels worried. But then, something bad happens. Anna does not see a big rock hidden by the snow. She hits the rock with her sled and falls off. She hurts her leg and cries. Ben runs to help her. He calls for help and stays with her until help comes. Anna is sorry for not listening to Ben. She says, "You were right, Ben. It was not attractive and exciting. It was dangerous and steep. I should have listened to you. I was foolish and reckless." Ben says, "It's okay, Anna. I'm glad you are not hurt more. I'm still your friend. Next time, let's be careful and safe when we play with snow." Anna says, "Yes, Ben. Thank you for being my friend. Next time, I will listen to you." They hug and smile. They learn a lesson.
