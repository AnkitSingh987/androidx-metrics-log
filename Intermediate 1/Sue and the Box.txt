Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Box

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue found a big box. Sue wanted to open the box. Inside the box, she found a comb. The comb was very pretty. Sue went to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a good comb in the box!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Sue! Let's use it to comb your hair." Sue and her mom combed her hair with the good comb. They made her hair look nice and neat. Sue was very happy. She loved her new comb and used it every day.

Once upon a time, there was a small hippo named Ray. Ray was bored and wanted something to do. He went out looking for adventure and soon saw a big pond in the distance. He waddled closer and saw a tiny boat waiting for him. Ray stepped inside and grabbed the oars, but he couldn't carry the boat very far. He was too small. Suddenly, he heard a voice calling out, "Carry me in, Ray!" Ray looked around and saw a giant hippo, three times his size. He was a little scared, but he managed to carry the giant hippo into the boat. Together they sailed off in the pond! They had a lot of fun and even a few races. The giant hippo was much faster but Ray managed to keep up with him. When it was time for the giant hippo to go back home, he thanked Ray for the adventure and said, "Don't forget, if ever you're bored again, come back and find me. We'll have the best day out ever!" Ray smiled and waved goodbye as he watched his new friend sail away. He couldn't wait for the next time they would sail together!

Once upon a time, there was a jolly frog named Fred. He liked to jump and play by a little stream. The water was cool and clear, and Fred loved to look at his face in it. One sunny day, Fred met a big fish named Sam. Sam said, "Fred, can you help me open this box?" Fred hopped over and said, "Sure, I can help!" They tried to open the box, but it was hard. They pushed and pulled, but the box would not open. Just then, a little bird named Tina flew down from a tree. Tina said, "I know how to open the box!" She pecked at the box with her beak, and it popped open! Inside the box, there were beautiful, shiny stones. Fred, Sam, and Tina were so surprised and happy. They played with the shiny stones all day long by the little stream.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an unusual toy car. It was big, red, and shiny. Tim loved to play with his car on the floor. He would push it and watch it pass by. One day, Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the unusual toy car on the floor. "Wow, what a fun car!" she said. "Can I play with it too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, let's play together!" They took turns making the car pass on the floor. They laughed as the car went fast and slow. Tim and Sue had a great time playing with the unusual toy car. And from that day on, they became best friends who loved to play together.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play with toys and draw pictures. One day, they find a big box in the park. They open the box and see a lot of shiny things inside. They see rings, necklaces, bracelets, and earrings. They are very happy and want to keep them. "Wow, look at these!" Anna says. "They are so pretty and sparkly!" "Yes, they are!" Ben agrees. "Let's take them home and show them to our moms and dads!" They start to fill their pockets and bags with the shiny things. They do not see a small paper in the box. The paper has a message on it. The message says: "These are not toys. They belong to someone else. Please do not take them. They are very important and valuable. Thank you." A man is walking by the park. He sees the box and the paper. He also sees Anna and Ben with the shiny things. He is very angry and sad. He runs to them and shouts: "Hey, stop! What are you doing? Those are mine! Give them back!" Anna and Ben are scared and confused. They drop the shiny things and run away. They do not understand why the man is so mad. They think they did nothing wrong. The man picks up the shiny things and puts them back in the box. He also takes the paper and reads it again. He sighs and shakes his head. He wishes Anna and Ben had read the message. He wishes they had been honest and respectful. He blinks his eyes and feels a dry tear on his cheek. He hopes they will learn their lesson. The moral of the story is: Do not take things that are not yours. Always read and follow the messages. Be honest and respectful to others.
