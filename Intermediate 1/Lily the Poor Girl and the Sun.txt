Title: Lily the Poor Girl and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a poor girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play outside in the sun. One day, Lily found a pretty jewel in the grass. It was shiny and bright. She was very happy and showed it to her mom and dad. They all smiled and were very proud of her. That night, Lily put the jewel in a box and shut it tight. She went to bed and had sweet dreams. The next day, she shared her jewel with her friends. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a brilliant girl. She had lots of files filled with important things. One day, the girl made a mistake and she was punished. She couldn't go out to play like she usually did and she was very sad. She looked at her files and saw that all her important things were gone. She started to cry and ran to her mom for help. But her mom was too angry and she couldn't help her. The girl was left alone and feeling very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird called Bob. Bob had big dreams of flying high in the sky. He wanted to soar like the other birds. One day, Bob decided that he was ready to take on his dreams. He stood outside a door and looked up. He saw the birds high above, gliding so gracefully. He said to himself, “I can do it! I can soar too!” So Bob spread his wings and started flapping them. He was so determined to make his dream come true. He flapped and flapped and flapped, but he couldn’t seem to get any higher. He felt so discouraged and sad. But then he heard a voice, “You need help, don’t you? Let me help you.” Bob turned and saw an attractive butterfly. The butterfly said, “Let’s fly together. I’ll help you soar”. So Bob and the butterfly flew up into the sky. With the help of his friend, Bob was soon soaring up in the sky with the other birds. The moral of this story is that teamwork is attractive: it can help everyone become successful. With help, our dreams can come true.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside and get dirty. One day, Tim was playing with his friends in the mud. They were all having fun, but their clothes were very dirty. After playing, Tim's stomach started to hurt. He went to his mom and told her about his stomach. His mom asked him to hold his stomach and take deep breaths. She knew that Tim had eaten some dirt while playing, and that was why his stomach hurt. Tim's mom told him that he should always wash his hands before eating. She said that dirty hands can make our stomachs hurt. Tim promised to wash his hands from now on. The moral of the story is to always keep our hands clean, so we don't get sick.

Sara and Tom were going on a trip with their mom and dad. They had to load their bags and toys in the car. Sara wanted to bring her big teddy bear, but Tom said it was too big. Sara was sad and cried. "Please, Tom, let me bring my teddy bear. He is my best friend," Sara said. "Fine, but you have to share your snacks with me," Tom said. Sara agreed and hugged her teddy bear. They loaded their things in the car and said goodbye to their grandma. She gave them a kiss and a cookie. They drove for a long time and saw many things. They saw cows and horses and trees and flowers. They sang songs and played games. They asked their mom and dad when they would get to the hotel. "Soon, sweeties, soon. We are almost there," their mom said. They finally arrived at the hotel. It was very big and shiny. Sara and Tom were amazed. They got out of the car and followed their mom and dad inside. They saw a man at a desk. He gave them a key and a smile. "Welcome to our hotel. You have a nice room on the fifth floor. Enjoy your stay," he said. Their dad thanked him and took the key. He looked at the paper the man gave him. He frowned. "Wow, this hotel is very expensive. We have to pay a lot of money for one night," he said. Their mom shrugged. "Well, it's the only hotel in town. And it's a special occasion. We are here to celebrate your birthday, remember?" she said. Their dad smiled and nodded. "You're right. It's worth it. Come on, kids, let's go to our room. Maybe we can see the pool and the playground later," he said. Sara and Tom cheered and followed their mom and dad to the elevator. They pressed the button and waited. They were excited to see their room and have fun. They loved their trip and their hotel. They didn't care if it was expensive. They were happy.
