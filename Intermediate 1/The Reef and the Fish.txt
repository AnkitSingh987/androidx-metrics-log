Category: Intermediate
Title: The Reef and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big reef in the sea. Many fish lived there. They were all friends. They liked to play and swim together. The reef was their home. One day, a serious fish came to the reef. He was not like the other fish. He did not want to play. He did not like to share. He wanted to keep the reef for himself. The other fish were sad. The serious fish saw how sad the other fish were. He thought about it. He wanted to be a good friend. So, he learned to share the reef. They all played and swam together again. They were happy. The serious fish learned that sharing is good. Sharing made them all happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big navy boat. On the boat, there was a foolish man named Tom. Tom liked to lean on the side of the boat to see the fish in the water. One sunny day, Tom was leaning on the side of the boat, looking at the fish. A big fish saw Tom and thought it would be fun to play a trick on him. The big fish jumped out of the water and splashed Tom. Tom was very wet and laughed. He thought the big fish was funny. Tom leaned over the side of the boat again to see if the big fish would splash him again. But this time, when Tom leaned over the side, the big fish did not splash him. Instead, the big fish gave Tom a beautiful shell. Tom was very surprised and happy. He showed the shell to his friends on the boat. From then on, Tom and the big fish became good friends. And Tom learned that even a foolish man can have a very special friend.

Jim was a 3 year old boy who couldn't decide what to do. He wanted to go to school, but he wasn't sure which one. Jim asked his mom, "Which school should I choose?" His mom smiled and said, "That's up to you, Jim." So, Jim looked around and chose the rarest school he could find. It was such a special school as it had a slide in the playground and five swings too! Jim was so excited to go to school. His mom said, "I'm very proud of you for making your own choice." Jim walked into the school with a big smile on his face. He was the happiest he had ever been knowing he had made his own choice.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and her dog, Spot. One day, Lily and Spot went for a walk in the woods. They saw a small house with a witch inside. Lily said to the witch, "Hello, can I come in?" The witch said, "No, you are too little." Lily insisted, "But I am big and strong!" The witch did not want to let Lily in. Lily and Spot went home and told her mom and dad. They were angry with the witch. The next day, Lily's dad went to the witch's house. He told the witch, "Let my little girl in." The witch said, "Okay, but only if she is nice." Lily and Spot went back to the witch's house. They were nice and the witch let them in. They all became friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily was very excited. She had just learned how to use the toilet all by herself. Lily wanted to tell her best friend, Tim, about her new skill. Lily went to Tim's house and said, "Tim, guess what? I can use the toilet now!" Tim was happy for Lily and said, "Wow, that's great! I can use the toilet too!" They both felt proud and wondered if they could teach their other friends how to use the toilet. Together, Lily and Tim went to their friend Sue's house. They told Sue about using the toilet. Sue was excited to learn, so Lily and Tim showed her how. Soon, all three friends were using the toilet like big kids. They were very proud of themselves and wondered what they could learn next.
