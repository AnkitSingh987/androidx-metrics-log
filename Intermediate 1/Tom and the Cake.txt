Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Cake

One day, Tom the cat was in the kitchen. He wanted to make a cake. He put flour, sugar, and eggs in a big bowl. Then, he used a spoon to stir it all up. As he stirred, he felt very happy. Tom's friend, Sue the dog, came into the kitchen. She saw Tom and asked, "What are you doing?" Tom said, "I am making a cake." Sue looked confused. She shook her head and said, "But you don't know how to make a cake." Tom didn't listen to Sue. He kept stirring the mix. He wanted to show Sue that he could make a cake. But when he tried to put the cake in the oven, he made a big mess. The mix went all over the floor. Sue looked at the mess and said, "I told you, you don't know how to make a cake." Tom felt sad. He knew Sue was right. But Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's make the cake together." They cleaned up the mess and started over. This time, they made the best cake ever.

Lisa and Tom were friends who liked to play together. They had a toy kitchen with pots and pans and spoons and plates. Sometimes they pretended to cook yummy food for each other, like soup and cake and pizza. One day, Lisa had an idea. She said to Tom, "Let's play marriage. You can be the husband and I can be the wife. We can cook for each other and say nice things and hug and kiss." Tom thought that sounded fun. He said, "Okay, let's play marriage. You are a charming wife and I am a lucky husband. What do you want to cook for me?" Lisa smiled and said, "I want to cook you a big salad with lettuce and tomatoes and cheese and dressing. And you can cook me a hot dog with ketchup and mustard and relish. And we can share a chocolate pudding for dessert." Tom nodded and said, "That sounds delicious. Let's go to the toy kitchen and start cooking. And then we can eat and talk and hug and kiss." Lisa and Tom went to the toy kitchen and pretended to cook their food. They put the plates on the table and sat down. They said, "Bon appetit!" and ate their food. They said, "This is so good!" and "You are a great cook!" and "I love you!" and "I love you too!" They hugged and kissed and smiled. They were happy to play marriage.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a curious cat named Tom. Tom loved to play and explore. One day, he saw a line of ants marching outside. Tom was very curious about the ants and wanted to be friends with them. Tom tried to welcome the ants by giving them food. The ants were happy and took the food back to their home. Tom felt good because he made new friends. He watched the ants march back and forth, carrying the food. Every day, Tom would give the ants more food and watch them march. They became good friends and had lots of fun. Tom learned that being curious can lead to making new friends, and he was always happy to welcome new friends into his life.

Once upon a time, in a land full of wonder, there was a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to help her friends and family. She was always ready to lend a hand when someone needed it. One sunny day, Lily and her friend Tom were playing in the park. They saw a little bird trapped in a net. The bird was scared and couldn't fly away. Lily and Tom wanted to help the bird. They had to remove the net to set the bird free. Lily and Tom worked together to take the net off the bird. They were very careful not to hurt it. With the net gone, the bird was free! It flew away, happy and safe. Lily and Tom felt proud that they were kind and helped the bird. They knew that helping others was the best thing to do.
