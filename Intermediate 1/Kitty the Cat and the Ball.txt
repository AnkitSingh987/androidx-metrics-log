Category: Intermediate
Title: Kitty the Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with yarn. One day, she found a big ball of yarn. It was very fun to play with. Kitty wanted to share the yarn with her friend, Bunny. But, she did not know how to share it in a fair way. She thought and thought. Then, she had an idea. Kitty managed to break the yarn into two parts. She gave one part to Bunny. Now, both friends could play with the yarn. They were very happy and played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, his ball fell into a big pit. Spot was sad because he could not get his ball back. Spot's friend, a bird named Tweet, saw him and wanted to help. Tweet had an idea. She told Spot to separate some sticks and put them in the pit. Spot did as Tweet said and put the sticks in the pit. With the sticks in the pit, the ball began to move up. Spot was surprised and happy. The ball came out of the pit! Spot and Tweet played together with the ball. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben like to play with tubes. They have many tubes of different colors and sizes. They like to put things inside the tubes and see what happens. One day, they find a big purple tube in the garage. They wonder what can fit inside the purple tube. They try to put their toys, their shoes, and their hats inside the tube, but nothing fits. The tube is too small. "Maybe we can fit inside the tube," Lily says. She tries to crawl inside the tube, but she gets stuck. She can only put her head and one arm inside the tube. She cannot move. "Help me, Ben!" Lily cries. "I cannot get out of the tube!" Ben tries to pull Lily out of the tube, but he cannot. He is too weak. He does not know what to do. "Mom! Dad!" Ben shouts. "Lily is stuck in the tube!" Mom and Dad hear Ben's shout. They run to the garage and see Lily in the tube. They are surprised and worried. "Don't worry, Lily. We will get you out of the tube," Mom says. She and Dad push and pull the tube until Lily pops out. Lily is happy and relieved. She hugs Mom and Dad. "Thank you, Mom and Dad. I'm sorry I got stuck in the tube," Lily says. "It's okay, Lily. We're glad you're safe. But next time, be careful with the tubes. They are not for playing," Dad says. Lily and Ben nod. They say sorry to Mom and Dad. They promise to not play with the tubes again. They put the tubes back in the garage and go to play with something else.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim wanted to learn how to tie his belt. He asked his mom to teach him. His mom was very happy to help. They sat down and Tim's mom showed him how to tie the belt. Tim tried and tried, but it was hard. His mom said, "It's okay, keep trying." Tim did not give up. Finally, Tim tied his belt all by himself. He was very proud. His mom was proud too. They hugged and Tim wore his belt all day long.

One day, a little girl named Sue had a bad dream. She called it a nightmare. In her nightmare, she saw a big ice monster that could freeze everything it touched. Sue was very scared of the ice monster. When she woke up, she told her mom about her nightmare. "Mom, I had a nightmare about an ice monster that could freeze everything," Sue said. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, it was just a dream. You are safe and warm here with me." That night, Sue went to sleep again. But this time, her nightmare came true! The ice monster appeared in her room. Sue was very scared, but then she remembered her mom's words. She bravely walked up to the ice monster and gave it a warm hug. To her surprise, the ice monster started to melt. It turned out that the ice monster was just cold and needed a warm hug to feel better. Sue and the ice monster became friends, and they played together every day. The ice monster never froze anything again, and Sue never had a nightmare about it.
