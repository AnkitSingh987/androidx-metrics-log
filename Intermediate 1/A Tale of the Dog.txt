Category: Intermediate
Title: A Tale of the Dog

One day, a big, impressive dog and a small cat were friends. They liked to play and talk. The dog heard a noise and did not know what it was. The cat said, "I will explain what the noise is." The dog was happy to learn. The cat and the dog went to find the noise. They looked up and down, left and right. The noise was getting louder. The cat said, "I think I know what the noise is. It is a big, hungry bear!" The dog was scared but the cat was brave. The cat and the dog walked closer to the noise. They saw a big, hungry bear! The bear saw them too. The cat said, "Run, dog, run!" But the dog was too scared to run. The bear ate the dog. The cat was sad, but learned to be more careful with noises.

One day, a girl named Amy and her twin sister, Lily, went to play outside. They saw a big, red door. They wanted to see what was behind the door. Amy said, "Let's enter the door, Lily!" Lily nodded and they both went in. Inside, they found a big room full of toys. Amy and Lily were very happy. They played with the toys and had lots of fun. Suddenly, they heard a small cry. They looked around and saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and didn't know how to get down. Amy and Lily wanted to help the cat. They were compassionate girls who loved animals. Amy said, "Don't worry, cat! We will help you!" They found a long stick and used it to help the cat come down from the tree. The cat was very happy and thanked them with a soft purr. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me, Amy and Lily! I was stuck in that cat body, and you saved me. I will give you both a wish for being so kind and compassionate." Amy and Lily were very surprised but happy. They wished for more fun days with their new fairy friend. From that day on, they had many more adventures with the fairy and became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing with her toys. She had a big toy box, but she could not reach her favorite toy, a small red ball. Amy was impatient and wanted to play with it now. Amy's mom saw her and asked, "What's wrong, Amy?" Amy said, "I can't reach my ball." Her mom had an idea. "Why don't you use your thumb to help you get it?" Amy thought for a moment and then tried to use her thumb to reach the ball. But something unexpected happened. When she touched the ball with her thumb, it started to bounce all by itself! It bounced higher and higher until it hit the ceiling. Amy and her mom laughed and clapped their hands. They played with the magic bouncing ball for the rest of the day, and Amy learned that her thumb could do amazing things.

Once upon a time, there was a big, gentle dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends. One day, he found a big, soft mattress in the yard. Spot jumped on the mattress and started to bounce up and down. Spot's friends saw him bouncing and wanted to play too. They all jumped on the mattress with Spot. They laughed and played together. But then, the mattress started to rip. Spot knew they had to stop. Spot told his friends, "Stop! The mattress is ripping!" They all stopped bouncing and got off the mattress. Spot felt sad, but his friends gave him a big hug. They knew they could find other fun games to play together. And they did, all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a dirty box in the grass. Tim wanted to see what was inside the box. He kneeled down and opened the box. Inside, there was a file with papers. Tim looked at the papers. They had drawings of animals. He saw a cat, a dog, and a bird. Tim thought the drawings were pretty. He took the file inside to show his mom. His mom looked at the file and smiled. She said, "I made these for you when you were a baby." Tim was happy and surprised. He thought he found something new, but it was a special gift from his mom. Now, Tim and his mom can look at the pretty animal drawings together.
