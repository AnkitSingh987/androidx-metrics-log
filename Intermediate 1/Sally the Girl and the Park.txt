Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a big, fluffy dog named Max. They loved to play together in their big, green yard. One sunny day, Sally and her dog Max went to a park. At the park, they saw a man who was a judge. The judge was there to pick the best dog. Sally wanted to join the fun, but she was scared. She thought Max was not good enough. She said to her mom, "Max is terrible at tricks. I don't want the judge to laugh at us." Her mom told her, "Don't worry, just try your best and have fun." So, Sally and Max joined the other kids and dogs. They all showed their tricks to the judge. When it was Sally's turn, she mentioned to the judge that Max could only do one trick. The judge smiled and said, "That's okay, let's see what Max can do." Sally called Max and said, "Sit, Max!" And Max sat down. The judge clapped and said, "Good job, Max!" Sally was so happy. She learned that it was okay to try, even if you think you are not the best.

Once upon a time, a girl named Lily lived in a big house. The house had many stairs. Lily liked to dance on the stairs. She thought it was safe and fun. One day, her friend Tom came over to play. "Let's dance on the stairs!" said Lily. Tom looked at the stairs and was scared. He said, "I don't know, Lily. It doesn't look safe." Lily said, "Don't worry, I do it all the time." So, they started to dance on the stairs. They were having fun, laughing, and dancing. Suddenly, a big, soft pillow appeared at the bottom of the stairs. Lily and Tom were surprised. They had never seen the pillow before. They looked at each other and smiled. Now, they knew they could dance on the stairs and be safe. The pillow would catch them if they fell. They danced and played all day long, and had the best time ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to guess the color of things. One day, she met an intelligent dog named Max. Max could talk and guess colors too! Sue said to Max, "Let's play a game. I guess the color of the sky, and you guess the color of the grass." Max barked, "Okay!" Sue guessed, "The sky is blue." Max guessed, "The grass is green." They both were right. Then, they saw a big tree. Sue wanted to climb it. Max said, "Be careful." Sue did not listen. She climbed up high. But, she fell down. Sue was hurt. Max barked, "I told you to be careful." Sue learned to listen to her intelligent friend Max next time.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside, but today it started to rain. She was sad because she could not play in the park. Her mom said, "Stay inside, Lily, and be obedient." Lily stayed inside and looked out the window. She saw a big black dog in the rain. The dog looked lost and wet. Lily wanted to help the dog, so she put on her rain boots and coat, and went outside. Lily was kind to the dog and brought it inside. The dog was very happy and obedient. Lily's mom saw how good the dog was and let Lily keep it. The rain stopped, and they all played outside together. The moral of the story is to be kind and help others, even when things don't go as planned.

One day, a fat frog was walking in the woods. He spotted a sheet on the ground and decided to follow it. He hopped closer and closer until he met a little duck who was quacking happily! The frog greeted the duck, asking who she was. She said, "I am trying to find a big pond to live in. If I can find one, I'll be safe from predatory animals". The frog said, "I know a pond that's perfect! Follow me and I'll take you there". The little duck nodded in agreement and they followed the sheet. After a while, they arrived at the edge of a big pond. The duck was so excited. She thanked the frog and said she would look after her new home forever. The frog smiled, content to have made such a good friend.
