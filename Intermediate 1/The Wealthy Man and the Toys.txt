Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wealthy Man and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big mustache. He liked to talk a lot. One day, he met a little girl. She wanted to discuss her toys. The wealthy man did not want to discuss toys. He wanted to discuss his mustache. The little girl was sad. She wanted to tell him about her toys. He did not listen. Then, something unexpected happened. The man's mustache fell off. The little girl laughed. The man was sad. The moral of the story is: always listen to others, or you might lose something you love.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a pink cat named Sally. Sally was a grumpy cat who did not like to play with others. One day, Sally saw a big ball in the park. She wanted to play with it, but she was too grumpy to ask anyone. Sally saw a little boy named Timmy playing with the ball. She thought, "Maybe Timmy will let me play with the ball if I ask nicely." But Sally was not good at being nice. Instead, she said, "Give me the ball!" Timmy looked at Sally and said, "No, you are grumpy. I don't want to play with you." Sally was sad and did not know what to do. She sat down and thought about how she could make Timmy change his mind. Then, she had an idea. Sally went to Timmy and said, "I'm sorry for being grumpy. Can you please delay your game and let me play with you?" Timmy looked at Sally and saw that she was trying to be nice. He said, "Okay, Sally. You can play with us." Sally was very happy. She played with Timmy and the ball all day long. She learned that being nice was much better than being grumpy. From that day on, Sally was never grumpy again, and she made many new friends in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a toy made of rubber. It was a very attractive toy. Sue liked her toy a lot. She played with it every day. One day, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Sue's toy and said, "Wow, that's a nice toy! Can I play with it too?" Sue said, "Yes, you can play with it, but be careful not to bite it." Tim and Sue played with the rubber toy together. They had lots of fun. They laughed and smiled. They made sure not to bite the toy. After a while, it was time for Sue to go home. She said to Tim, "I have to go now, but we can play again tomorrow." Tim said, "Yes, I would like that. I will not bite your toy, I promise." The next day, Sue and Tim played with the rubber toy again. They were very happy. They made a new friend and had a fun toy to play with. And they never bit the toy.

Once upon a time, a jolly dog named Buddy loved to play games. His favorite game was fetch. Buddy would run and catch the ball every day with his friends. They had so much fun playing games together. One day, Buddy was playing fetch with his friends when he started to suffer. His paw hurt a lot. Buddy could not play the game anymore. His friends did not know what to do. They were sad and worried about Buddy. Then, a little girl named Lily saw Buddy and his friends. She knew just what to do. Lily found a soft bandage and wrapped it around Buddy's paw. Soon, Buddy felt much better. He could play the game again! Buddy and his friends were so happy. They learned that helping each other is important and makes everyone happy.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play games. Today they play a game with cards. The cards have pictures of animals on them. Lily and Ben take turns to pick a card and say the name of the animal. "Your turn, Lily," Ben says. He gives her a card. Lily looks at the card. She sees a big gray animal with a long nose and big ears. She knows the name of the animal. She is gifted with words. "Elephant!" she says. She smiles. "Good job, Lily!" Ben says. He claps his hands. He picks another card for himself. He sees a small brown animal with a fluffy tail and a black nose. He does not know the name of the animal. He is not gifted with words. "What is this?" he asks. He shows the card to Lily. Lily looks at the card. She knows the name of the animal. She is kind to Ben. She helps him. "Squirrel!" she says. She points to the card. "Thank you, Lily," Ben says. He repeats the word. "Squirrel." They play more cards. Lily knows all the names of the animals. Ben knows some of them. He learns new words from Lily. They have fun. Lily wins the game. She has more cards than Ben. She is happy. She is gifted with cards. "Yay, Lily!" Ben says. He is happy for her. He is gifted with friendship. They hug each other. They are good friends. They share their cards. They are gifted with love.
