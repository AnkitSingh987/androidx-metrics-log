Category: Intermediate
Title: Fred the Green Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Fred. Fred loved to play chess with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They would play chess every day near the pond. Lily would encourage Fred to do his best in the game. One sunny day, while they were playing chess, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the chess pieces into the pond. Fred and Lily were very sad. They thought they could not play chess anymore. But then, a group of friendly fish swam up to the surface of the pond. They had all the chess pieces on their heads! The fish wanted to play chess too. So, Fred and Lily played chess with the fish, and they all had fun. The wind had brought them new friends to play with, and now they could all encourage each other to do their best in the game.

Once upon a time there was a family who had to go out. Mommy, Daddy and little 3 year old Roberta had to go to the park. They got in their car and went for a drive. Once they arrived Roberta was so excited. She kept clapping her hands and running around. She was so happy to be in the park. Daddy found a nice spot and spread out the blanket. Roberta wanted to play. Mommy and Daddy set her up with a ball and some other toys. In the mean time Daddy had a surprise. He had a present for Roberta! It was a spicy pink dress. Roberta was so excited when she saw the present and tried it on right away. She loved it and put it on. Mommy said, “It looks lovely Roberta. How do you like the spicy colour?” Roberta said, “I like it! It's very spicy!” The family then spent the day laughing and playing at the park. What a great day they had!

Once upon a time, there was a blue cat. The cat was very hungry. It went to find food. The cat walked and walked. Then, it saw a big tree. The cat looked up and saw a bird. The cat said, "Bird, can you help me find food?" The bird said, "Yes, cat. I can help you." The bird flew down from the tree. It helped the cat look for food. They walked and walked. Then, they saw a big house. The house had a lot of food. The cat and the bird went to the house. They ate the food. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat and the bird heard a big noise. They had to pause. They looked around and saw a big dog. The dog was not happy. The dog said, "This is my food! You can't eat it!" The cat and the bird were scared. They ran away from the house. The dog chased them. They ran and ran. But the dog caught them. The cat and the bird were very sad. They had to give the food back to the dog. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a family of bunnies. They lived in a big, warm hole under a tree. The family loved to play and eat together. One day, Mommy Bunny told her little bunnies to lay down for a nap. They were very tired from playing all day. While the bunnies slept, a bitter wind blew outside. The wind was so strong that it made the tree shake. It shook and shook until something fell from the tree. It was a big, round thing. It landed right by the bunny hole. When the bunnies woke up, they saw the big, round thing. They didn't know what it was, but they wanted to find out. They pushed and rolled it into their hole. The bunnies thought it was a new toy to play with. As they played, the big, round thing started to crack. The bunnies stopped and looked. What could be inside? They waited and watched. Then, out came a tiny, wet bird! The bunnies were so surprised. They didn't know that their new toy was an egg. The bunnies decided to help the bird. They kept it warm and fed it. Soon, the bird grew big and strong. The bunnies and the bird became best friends. They played and ate together, just like a family. And they all lived happily ever after.
