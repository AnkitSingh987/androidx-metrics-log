Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a nervous boy. One day, Tim had to take a package to his friend, Sam. Tim walked to Sam's house. On the way, he saw a big dog. The dog barked at Tim. Tim was scared and hid. This made him delay his walk. He waited until the dog went away. Tim got to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam opened the door and said, "Hi, Tim! What do you have?" Tim said, "I have a package for you." Sam was happy and thanked Tim. Tim felt brave and was not nervous anymore. They played together, and it was a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Sam. Sam had a big pick. Sam liked to dig with his pick. One day, Sam saw a filthy rock. He wanted to own the rock. Sam tried to pick up the rock, but it was too heavy. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it. Sam was sad. He wanted to own the rock so much. Sam asked his friend, Tom, to help him. Together, they lifted the filthy rock. Sam was happy. Now, Sam and Tom both own the rock. They cleaned the rock and shared it.

Once upon a time, there was a little house with a big window. The window was very clean and shiny. The sun would shine through the window and make the room bright and warm. One day, a little bug came to the window. The bug had brilliant wings that sparkled in the sunlight. The bug would blink its wings and make the room even more bright. The little bug and the window became friends. They would play together every day. The bug would blink its wings and the window would shine the sun on the bug. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little pupil named Tim. Tim was a careful boy. He always looked both ways before he crossed the street. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They were having a lot of fun. In the park, Tim saw a small bird. The bird looked scared. Tim said, "Mom, can we protect the bird?" His mom said, "Yes, let's be careful and help the bird." They went close to the bird and saw that its wing was hurt. Just then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running towards the bird. Tim was scared, but he wanted to protect the bird. He stood in front of the bird and said, "No, dog! Leave the bird alone!" The dog stopped and looked at Tim. It didn't want to hurt the bird. It just wanted to play. So, Tim, the dog, and the bird all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat named Lucy. Lucy liked to play and run all day. She had a lot of fun with her friends. One day, Lucy's mom asked her to do some work. She needed to pick up her toys and put them away. But Lucy did not want to do that. She wanted to play outside with her friends. So, Lucy went outside and forgot about her work. She played and played with her friends. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The sky turned dark, and it started to rain. All the cats had to run home. When Lucy got home, her mom was not happy. Lucy remembered that she forgot her work. She felt sad. But then, her mom had an idea. They could pick up the toys together! So Lucy and her mom worked together, and it was fun. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Lucy learned that work can be fun when you do it with someone you love.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Tilly. Tilly was very hungry, and she wanted something delicious to eat. She saw an apple tree in her garden that was full of big, juicy apples. Tilly asked her dad if she could pick some apples, and he said yes. He gave her a big bucket to pick the apples in. Tilly started picking the apples, one by one, and putting them in the bucket. When she reached the last apple, something strange happened. The branch of the tree was too weak, and it snapped off. The branch fell on Tilly’s dad’s head, and he started to cry out in pain. It was an emergency! The dad quickly straightened out the situation and thanked Tilly for being so obedient. He said, “No matter what, always yield to adults. That way we can make sure there are no emergencies! And you can have those delicious apples."
