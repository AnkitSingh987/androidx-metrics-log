Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a man with a unique mustache. It was big and curly. One day, he went to bathe in a pond. A little frog saw him and said, "Hello, man with the unique mustache!" The man looked at the frog and said, "Hello, little frog! I am here to bathe in the pond. Do you want to join me?" The frog said, "Yes, I would like that!" So, they both went into the water and had fun. While they were playing, something unexpected happened. The man's mustache fell off! The frog saw it and said, "Oh no, your mustache is gone!" The man looked sad, but then he laughed. He said, "It's okay, little frog. It was just a fake mustache. I still had fun playing with you!" They both smiled and continued to play in the pond.

Once upon a time, in a lonely house in the east, there lived a small cat. The cat liked to play and jump all day. One day, the cat saw a big red ball outside the house. The cat wanted to play with the ball. The cat went outside to get the ball. But the ground was wet from the rain. The cat did not know this. As the cat ran to the ball, it started to slip on the wet ground. The cat tried to stop but it could not. The cat slipped and fell into a big hole. The cat was very sad and scared. It could not get out of the hole. The cat stayed in the hole and no one came to help. The cat was lonely and sad in the hole forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big, impressive tree. Under the tree, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to make pie. One sunny day, she made a yummy pie with red apples. Amy wanted to share her pie with her friend Tom. Tom was a friendly cat. Amy told Tom, "I trust you to take this pie to our friends." Tom said, "Yes, I will do it." So, Tom took the pie and walked to their friends. Tom gave the pie to their friends. The friends were very happy. They all ate the pie together. It was yummy and they all had fun. Amy was happy because she trusted Tom and they shared the pie with their friends.

One day, Jack was playing golf. The golf course was very big and he was having lots of fun. His dad came and said: "Let's go, it's time for lunch." Jack wanted to stay and play for a bit longer so he made a big sign that said "No". His dad was not happy about this and said to Jack "If you can't follow instructions, then you can't play golf ever again!" Jack was very sorry and wanted to finish playing but his dad said it was too late. He had already made his decision, so they both went home. Jack was so upset that he could not play golf, he felt very sad and he had a big cry. Jack never played golf again and was so sad that he hadn't listened to his dad.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a regular day. He went to school and played with his friends. One day, Tim found a gum on the ground. It was a special gum. Tim wanted to eat the gum, but he was not sure. Tim went to his friend, Sue, and showed her the gum. Sue said, "I will encourage you, Tim. Eat the gum and see what happens!" Tim ate the gum and something unexpected happened. Tim started to jump very high. He could jump as high as a house! Tim and Sue played and jumped all day. They were very happy. The gum made Tim jump high, but it did not last forever. At the end of the day, Tim was back to his regular self. Tim and Sue learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be fun.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little grey wolf. The wolf liked to play with his friends, the birds and the rabbits. They liked to run and jump all day long. One day, a big magic box appeared in the forest. The little wolf was very alert. He looked at the box and wondered what was inside. He called his friends, the birds and the rabbits, to come and see the box too. Together, they opened the magic box. Inside, they found a lot of colorful balls. They took the balls out and played with them all day long. The little wolf and his friends were very happy. They had so much fun with the magic box and the colorful balls.
