Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had an incredible pet. Her pet was a small, soft cat named Kitty. Sue loved Kitty very much. They played together every day. One sunny day, Sue and Kitty went to the park. They saw other kids with their pets, too. Sue felt happy. She saw a boy with a big dog. The big dog wanted to play with Kitty. Sue was scared, but Kitty was brave. Sue said, "No, you can't play with Kitty!" The big dog did not listen. Sue had to demand, "Go away, big dog!" And the big dog went away. Sue and Kitty were safe. They went home and had a fun day. Sue knew her incredible pet, Kitty, was the best friend ever.

One day, a girl named Sue went on a walk. She saw a pretty flower. The flower was unique. It was big and pink. Sue liked the flower a lot. Then, a bee appeared. The bee liked the flower too. It made perfume from the flower. The perfume smelled nice. Sue and the bee were happy. Sue and the bee became friends. They played near the flower every day. They loved the unique flower and its perfume. They were very happy together.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big brown dog. The dog is friendly. He wags his tail and licks their faces. "Can we pet him?" Lily asks. "Sure, but we have to test him first," Ben says. "How do we test him?" Lily wonders. Ben has an idea. He picks up a stick and throws it. The dog runs after it and brings it back. "Good boy!" Ben says. He pets the dog's head. Lily wants to try too. She finds a ball and tosses it. The dog chases it and returns it. "Good girl!" Lily says. She rubs the dog's belly. The dog is happy. He likes Lily and Ben. He gives them a wink. Lily and Ben giggle. They have a new friend. They play with the dog until it is time to go home. They wave goodbye and promise to come back soon. The dog winks again and barks. He hopes to see them again.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree in the forest. The tree was so big that all the animals liked to play around it. One day, a little squirrel named Squeaky wanted to measure how big the tree was. So, he asked his friend, a wise old owl named Oliver, to help him. Oliver said, "I can help you, Squeaky. Let's start at the bottom and go all the way to the tip of the tree." They began to measure the tree together, but when they reached the middle, they heard a loud noise. It was a big surprise! A family of birds was living inside the tree. The mommy bird said, "Why are you making noise? My babies are trying to sleep." Squeaky and Oliver said sorry and told the bird family that they were just measuring the tree. The mommy bird laughed and said, "You don't need to measure it. It's the biggest tree in the forest!" So, Squeaky and Oliver stopped measuring and played with the bird family instead.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She lived in a green house with her mommy and daddy. One day, Mia's mommy said, "We are going on a holiday!" Mia was very happy. On the holiday, they went to a big park. In the park, there was a swing. Mia loved to swing. She said, "Mommy, can I swing?" Her mommy said, "Yes, you can swing!" Mia got on the swing and her daddy pushed her. She went up and down, up and down. She was so happy. Then, it was time to go home. Mia said, "I had so much fun on our holiday!" Her mommy and daddy smiled and said, "We did too, Mia!"

One day, a big turkey named Tom lived on a farm. He liked to yell very loud. All the other animals did not like it. They would tell Tom to be quiet, but he did not listen. Tom was not a very nice friend. One sunny morning, Tom saw a big red ball. He wanted to play with it. He yelled very loud to call his friends. But no one came. They were all tired of his loud yelling. Tom felt sad and alone. He wished he had not yelled so much. The next day, Tom decided to be quiet. He did not yell when he saw his friends. They liked it and played with him. Tom felt happy and ashamed at the same time. He learned that being loud was not always good. From that day on, Tom did not yell so much, and he became a good friend to all the animals on the farm.
