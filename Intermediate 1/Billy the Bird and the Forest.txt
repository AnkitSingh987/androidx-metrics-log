Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Bird and the Forest

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little bird named Billy. Billy was a happy bird who loved to play with his friends. One day, Billy and his friends found a big net on the ground. They were curious about what it was. They didn't know that the net was there to catch them. A kind man named Mr. Smith came to the forest to measure the trees. Mr. Smith saw Billy and his friends playing with the net. He knew that the net was not a toy, and it could hurt the little birds. So, Mr. Smith thought of a way to help them. Mr. Smith took the net away and put it in his bag. He then gave Billy and his friends some yummy seeds to eat. Billy and his friends were very grateful to Mr. Smith for saving them from the net. They sang a happy song for him as a thank you. From that day on, Billy and his friends always remembered to be careful in the forest.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big table in the park. The table was very tall. He wanted to climb on it and slide down. Tim asked his mom, "Can I slide on the table, please?" His mom said, "Not now, but maybe later." Tim was sad, but he played with his ball instead. Later, it started to rain. Tim and his mom ran under the big table to stay dry. Tim was grateful for the table. He said, "Thank you, table!" And when the rain stopped, Tim got to slide down the table just like he wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with sand. Every day, she went to the park and built sand castles with her friends. They had so much fun making big towers and small houses. One day, a big boy came to the park. He saw Sue's sand castle and wanted to break it. Sue was sad and scared. She didn't want her castle to break. Sue's friends came to help her. They all stood in front of the castle to keep it safe. The big boy tried to break the castle, but Sue and her friends were strong. They didn't let him break it. The big boy went away, and Sue's castle was safe. Sue and her friends were happy. They played with the sand and had fun all day.

Once upon a time there was a poor house. All the things in the house were old and broken and it was a very sad place. One day, the family living there decided to try and improve their home. They started to look around to see what they could do. Suddenly, they noticed that there was a lot of waste lying around. They decided to pick up all the waste and throw it away. It was hard work, but they kept trying. After they had finished, they looked around and noticed a huge improvement. All the rubbish had gone and the house was so much tidier and brighter! The mother of the family said, "Look at how much better our house is now! Thank you for helping us improve it." The family was very happy and thanked each other for their hard work. From then on, they kept the house tidy and shining, so it never looked as poor as it did before.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a big problem. Her hair was very messy. She wanted it to look nice like her friend, Mia. Mia had pretty hair. Lucy felt sad. She wanted to add something to her hair to make it look nice. Lucy's mom saw that she was sad. Her mom was very compassionate. She wanted to help Lucy. So, she got a pretty hairband and added it to Lucy's hair. Lucy's hair looked much better now. She was very happy. But when Lucy went to play, the hairband fell off. Her hair was messy again. Lucy's friend, Mia, saw this and wanted to help. Mia was also compassionate. She shared her hair ties with Lucy. They both had nice hair now. They were very happy and played together all day.
