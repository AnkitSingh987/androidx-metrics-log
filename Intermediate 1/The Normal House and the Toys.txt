Category: Intermediate
Title: The Normal House and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a normal house. In this house, there was a big curtain. Behind the curtain, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toys. One day, Tim did something bad. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, you should not do that." Tim felt sad. His mom had to punish him. She said, "You must go to your room and think about what you did." Tim went to his room and sat on his bed. He thought about what he did. He knew it was not good. Tim said to himself, "I will not do that again." Then, his mom came in and hugged him. She said, "I am glad you learned your lesson, Tim." From that day on, Tim never did that bad thing again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play in the park. One day, he found a big file in the grass. Max was very curious about the file, so he picked it up with his mouth. Max decided to take the file home. On his way, he saw a tall tree. Max wanted to see if he could shake the tree. He put the file down and started to shake the tree with all his might. The tree shook and shook, and lots of leaves fell to the ground. Suddenly, a big branch broke off the tree and fell down. The branch landed on the file and broke it! Max was very sad because the file was now bad. He had to leave the broken file behind and go home without it. The day did not end well for Max.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a colorful box. She did not know what was inside. She wanted to open it and see. She asked her friend, Tom, "What do you think is in the box?" Tom said, "I don't know. Let's open it and find out." They opened the box and found a test inside. The test had many colors and shapes. Lucy and Tom wanted to do the test, but they did not know how. Lucy had an idea. She said, "Let's start by matching the colors and shapes." They tried, but it was hard. They could not do it alone. They needed help. Lucy and Tom asked their friend, Sam, to help them. Sam was good at colors and shapes. Together, they all worked on the test. They matched the colors and shapes, and they finished the test. In the end, they were happy that they worked together. They learned that when they help each other, they can do anything.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a jolly alligator. He liked to play with his friends, the ducks, and the frogs. They would swim and splash in the water all day long. One day, the jolly alligator saw a big ball. He called his friends, "Hey, ducks and frogs! Come see this big ball!" They all came to see the ball and decided to play with it. They pushed the ball with their noses and kicked it with their feet. As they were playing, something unexpected happened. The ball popped and out came a tiny mouse! The mouse said, "Thank you for freeing me from the ball!" The jolly alligator and his friends were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend. From that day on, they all played together and had lots of fun in the big pond.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk with her aunt. They walked in the cold, icy air. Lily saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. Her aunt said, "Be careful, Lily. The ground is icy." As they played, a magic rabbit appeared. The rabbit said, "I can give you one wish, but you must be kind and share." Lily thought for a moment and wished for a big, warm coat to keep her and her aunt warm. Suddenly, two big, warm coats appeared. Lily and her aunt put them on and felt very cozy. They thanked the magic rabbit and went back home. Lily learned that if you are kind and share, good things can happen.
