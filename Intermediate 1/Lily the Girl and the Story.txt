Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Story

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Lily. She loved to wear colorful clothes. One day, she found a new, pretty dress in her closet. It was a gift from her mom. Lily was so happy! Lily went to the park to play with her friends. She met her friend, Tom. Tom said, "Wow, Lily! Your dress is so colorful!" Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom! I like it too!" They played together all day, and Lily told Tom a story she learned from a book. It was called a novel. The story was about sharing and being kind to others. Tom listened carefully and said, "I want to be kind and share too!" From that day on, Lily and Tom always shared their toys and treats with their friends. They learned that sharing and being kind made everyone happy. And every time Lily wore her colorful dress, she remembered the story and its lesson. The moral of the story is: Sharing and being kind to others brings happiness to everyone.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a nice bedroom with many toys. Lily liked to play in her bedroom all day. She had a friend named Tom. Tom was a cat. One day, Tom could speak! Lily was very happy to talk with Tom. They spoke about fun things like toys and games. But then, Tom wanted to play with Lily's toys. Lily did not want to share her toys with Tom. Lily and Tom were not happy. They did not want to fight. So, they thought about how to fix the problem. They decided to take turns playing with the toys. Lily played first, then Tom played. They were happy again and spoke about their fun day together.

Anna and Ben were playing in the sand. They had a bucket and a shovel. They wanted to make a big castle. "Let me have the bucket, Anna," Ben said. "I need to fill it with sand." "No, let me have it," Anna said. "I want to make the tower." They both pulled the bucket and it broke. Sand spilled everywhere. They were sad and angry. "Look what you did!" Ben shouted. "You broke our bucket!" "It's your fault!" Anna shouted back. "You pulled too hard!" They heard a bark. A hairy dog came running to them. He wagged his tail and licked their faces. He liked the sand. He jumped on their castle and knocked it down. "Hey, stop that!" Ben said. "That's our castle!" But the dog did not listen. He rolled in the sand and shook his fur. He made them laugh. "Maybe he wants to play with us," Anna said. "He's a funny dog." They forgot about the bucket and the castle. They threw a stick and the dog fetched it. They made a new friend. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He liked to run and jump in the wind. One day, Tim found a big red ball. He was very happy and played with it all day. While Tim was playing, he saw a little girl named Sue. She looked sad and guilty. Tim asked her, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I lost my red ball." Tim looked at his big red ball and thought for a moment. Then, Tim did something unexpected. He wiped his hands on his pants and gave the big red ball to Sue. She was so happy and smiled at Tim. They both played with the ball together and became good friends. And from that day on, they always shared their toys and played together.

One day, a boy named Tim had to pack his bag. He had a new book and a big exam at school. He was a little scared. He put his book in the bag and went to school. At school, his friend Sam said, "I did not pack my book for the exam!" Tim said, "You can look at my new book with me." Sam was happy and they went to the exam together. During the exam, a funny thing happened. A little dog ran into the room! The dog took Tim's new book and ran away. Everyone laughed and the teacher said, "No exam today!" Tim and Sam were happy and played with the dog.
