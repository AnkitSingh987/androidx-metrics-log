Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy and the Bird

Once upon a time, a little bird named Timmy wanted to soar high in the sky. He tried and tried, but he just couldn't fly. One day, he met a scary skeleton named Mr. Bones. Mr. Bones was sad because everyone was scared of him. "Hello, Mr. Bones. Why is everyone scared of you?" asked Timmy. "I don't know, Timmy. I just want to be friends with them," said Mr. Bones. Timmy had an idea. "Why don't we help each other? I can teach you how to be nice, and you can teach me how to soar!" So, Timmy and Mr. Bones worked together. Timmy showed Mr. Bones how to smile and be kind, and Mr. Bones helped Timmy practice flying. Soon, Timmy could soar high in the sky and Mr. Bones had many friends. They were both very happy. The moral of the story is that when we help each other, we can do great things and make new friends.

One day, a little cat with white fur went out to play. The sun was bright and the cat was happy. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Up, up, up the cat went. When the cat got to the top, it looked around and saw a big bird. "Hi bird!" said the cat. "I can see a lot from up here. It's so bright!" The bird looked at the cat and said, "Hi cat! Be careful up here. The tree is very tall." The cat nodded and continued to look around. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew and the cat lost its balance. The cat shut its eyes tight, scared that it would fall. But the big bird was quick and grabbed the cat with its beak. The bird flew down and put the cat safely on the ground. The cat was very thankful to the bird for saving its life. "Thank you, bird!" said the cat. "You are a good friend." From that day on, the cat and the bird were best friends. They played together every day under the bright sun, and the cat never climbed the tall tree again.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sam, who wore pants with many colors. Tim liked the pants and wanted to have them too. They played all day and had lots of fun. The sun was very harsh, but they did not care. Tim asked Sam, "Can I have your pants? They are so nice." Sam said, "No, my mom gave them to me." Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He asked again and again, but Sam always said no. Tim felt bad and stopped playing with Sam. The next day, Tim's mom gave him new pants with many colors. Tim was so happy. He went outside to play and saw Sam. Tim said, "Look, I have nice pants too!" Sam smiled and said, "That's great! Let's play together again." They played and had fun. Tim learned that being patient and waiting for good things to come is better than being sad and not playing with friends.

Danny was tired. He wanted to take a nap but his mom wanted him to do his homework first. Danny didn't want to do it so he asked his mom if he could just watch instead. Mom said, "No Danny, you have to do your homework first then you can watch." Danny thought for a minute and then he said, "Mom, can you help me with my homework? Then I can do it and watch afterwards." Mom smiled and said, "Yes Danny, I will help you. Good job for thinking of this solution." So Danny and his mom sat together and his mom gave him a pencil to help him with his homework. After a while, Danny finished his homework and he was very happy. Now he could watch and take a nap. This story has a moral: When you are tired, it's ok to take a break, but you can also think of creative ways to get your job done. With help and determination, anything is possible!

Once upon a time, a big cat with a bad tooth lived in a deep forest. The cat was very sad because its tooth hurt a lot. It could not eat or play with its friends. One day, a small bird saw the cat and wanted to help. The bird had an idea to make the cat feel better. The bird told the cat to complete a task. The cat had to find a dead tree and hit its bad tooth on it. The cat thought it was strange, but it wanted the pain to go away. So, the cat found a dead tree and hit its tooth on it. Suddenly, the tooth fell out! The cat was very surprised. At first, the cat was happy because the pain was gone. But soon, it realized it could not eat well without its tooth. The cat became weak and could not play anymore. The bird felt sad for the cat too. The moral of the story is to think twice before doing something, even if it seems like a good idea at the time.
