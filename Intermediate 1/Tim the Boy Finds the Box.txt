Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Box

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big red box with a lock on it. Tim was very patient and wanted to see what was inside. His mom said, "Wait, Tim. We need to unlock it first." Tim's mom found a key and unlocked the box. They opened it and saw a big hot air balloon! Tim was so happy and surprised. He said, "Wow, mom! Can we go up in the sky?" His mom said yes, and they climbed into the hot air balloon. They went up, up, up into the sky. Tim looked down and saw his friends. They all waved at him. Tim felt like he was on top of the world.

Once upon a time, there was a panda named Pandy. Pandy lived in a big, green forest. He had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Pandy met a scary bear. The bear was big and loud. Pandy was afraid. The scary bear said, "I am not mean. I just look scary. My name is Bearry. Can we be friends?" Pandy thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, we can be friends." They both smiled and started to play together. It was a hot day, and Pandy and Bearry were getting very warm. They found a cold river and jumped in to cool off. The water felt so good that it made them melt. Pandy and Bearry laughed and splashed each other. They were happy to be friends and play in the river on a hot day.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a big bush with red berries on it. Mia was very patient and picked the berries one by one. She put the berries in her basket and smiled. Mia saw a small bird near the bush. The bird looked sad and hungry. Mia wanted to help the bird, so she gave it some berries. The bird ate the berries and felt better. Mia was happy to help the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird grew bigger and bigger! It was not a bird, but a magic bird! The magic bird hugged Mia and said, "Thank you for being patient and kind." Mia and the magic bird became best friends and played in the park all day.

One day, a cat named Tom and a dog named Max were playing in the yard. Tom saw a shiny faucet in the garden. He turned it on and water came out. Max looked at the water and was envious. He wanted to play with the water too. "Can I play with the water?" Max asked Tom. Tom did not want to share, so he said, "No, it's mine!" Max was sad, but he had an idea. He picked up a stick and decided to strike the faucet to make the water come out faster. When Max hit the faucet, the water started to spray everywhere! Tom and Max were both wet and laughing. They played in the water together, forgetting about their fight. They were no longer envious of each other and had fun playing in the water all day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big drawer in her room. Inside the drawer, there were many toys. Lily loved to play with her toys every day. One day, Lily wanted to play with her friend, Tom. Tom came to Lily's house, and they went to her room. Lily opened the drawer and said, "Let's grab a toy and play!" Tom was excited and said, "Yes, let's play with the toys!" They both grabbed a toy from the drawer. Lily picked a soft teddy bear, and Tom chose a small toy car. They played together and shared the toys. They were very fair to each other. Lily said, "We can play with my toys, and then we can play with your toys at your house." Tom agreed and said, "That's a great idea, Lily! We can have fun at both our houses." They played together all day, taking turns and being fair with each other. And they became the best of friends.
