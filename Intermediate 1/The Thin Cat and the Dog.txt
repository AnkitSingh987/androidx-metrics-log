Category: Intermediate
Title: The Thin Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat liked to store food in his little house. One day, he saw a big jail. The thin cat was curious. He went inside the jail to see what was there. Inside, he found a lot of food. He was very happy and started to store the food in his little house. But then, a big dog came. The dog was the guard of the jail. The dog saw the thin cat and got very angry. The dog chased the cat and caught him. The thin cat was put in the jail, and he could not get out. The cat was very sad, and his house was empty without him.

Once upon a time, there was a silly mule. The mule lived on a farm with many animals. The mule liked to play and have fun all day. One day, the mule wanted to play with the other animals. He went to the cow, but the cow was eating. The mule did not want to disturb the cow. So, he went to the sheep. The sheep were sleeping. The mule did not want to disturb the sheep either. Finally, the mule found a dog who wanted to play. They played all day and had lots of fun. The mule was happy. From that day on, the silly mule and the dog were best friends. They always played together and never disturbed the other animals.

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree. Under the tree, there was a little poppy. The poppy was very happy. Every day, the sun would shine on the poppy and help it grow. One day, a big storm came. The poppy started to worry. It was scared of the wind and the rain. The huge tree tried to protect the poppy, but the storm was too strong. The wind blew and blew. The rain fell hard. The poppy tried to be brave, but it was too small. In the end, the storm was too much for the little poppy. The poppy could not stand up any more, and it fell down.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with her friends in the park. One day, while playing, Fluffy found a toy sword. It was small and shiny. Fluffy liked the sword very much. Fluffy showed the sword to her friends. They all wanted to play with it. Fluffy's friend, Bunny, said, "I recommend we take turns with the sword." All the friends agreed, and they took turns playing with the sword. After a fun day of playing, Fluffy and her friends went home. Fluffy put the white sword in a special place in her room. She knew that tomorrow, she and her friends would play with the sword again. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly girl named Sue. Sue loved to have fun and play with her toys. One day, Sue found a big table in her room. The table was very high, and she could not see what was on it. Sue called her friend Tom and asked, "Can you help me see what is on the table?" Tom said, "Yes, let's find something to rise up on." They found a box and put it near the table. Sue climbed on the box and could now see the top of the table. On the table, there was a big, red ball. Sue and Tom tried to get the ball, but the box broke! Sue and Tom fell down and cried. They were sad because they could not play with the big, red ball.

One day, a little girl was feeling sad. Her mum saw her and wanted to comfort her. "What's wrong, my love?" she asked. The little girl said, "I'm scared of frogs!". Mum chuckled and said, "Frogs are actually quite interesting. Look at this one." She showed her daughter a cute frog hopping around the garden. The little girl was amazed! She thought that frogs were scary, but this one was so cute! Mum said, "See, frogs can be friendly. Let's try to make friends with it!" She gently moved her hand towards the frog. The little girl did too. The frog was happy and hopped around the two of them. The little girl and her mum enjoyed spending time with it together. When the frog finally hopped away, the little girl no longer felt scared. She was so relieved and content. She hugged her mum and said, "Thank you for comforting me."
