Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play and run in the park. One day, Max saw a big cat. The cat looked guilty because it took a toy from another dog. Max wanted to help get the toy back. Max started to bark at the cat. His voice got louder and louder. He wanted the cat to give the toy back. The cat looked at Max and began to increase the distance between them. Max kept barking and did not give up. Finally, the cat dropped the toy and ran away. Max was happy that he helped get the toy back. The other dog wagged its tail and thanked Max. From that day on, Max and the other dog became best friends and played together in the park every day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. Her name was Daisy. Daisy loved flowers and going for walks in the park. One day Daisy was walking in the park and she found a beautiful inchworm. She was so excited she picked up the inchworm and held it in her hands. "Hello tiny worm," Daisy said. "Do you need some help?" The inchworm looked at Daisy and nodded. Daisy smiled and said, "My grandmother always tells me that a little love can help heal anything. So let me give you a hug!" Daisy hugged the inchworm tightly and was amazed when the inchworm's little body began to heal. After a while, the inchworm transformed into a beautiful butterfly. "Bye bye little worm," Daisy said, as she watched the butterfly fly away. "Happy healing!"

Mummy was in the bathroom. She opened her makeup box and looked inside. There was a broken mirror. Mummy said, "That needs to be repaired." She looked around and found a hot glue gun. She switched it on and got some hot glue. She carefully repaired the broken mirror. Mummy smiled when the repair was finished. She put her makeup box away and turned off the hot glue gun. She said, "That was a good repair!"

Lily liked to paint. She had many colors and brushes. She painted on paper, on walls, on rocks, and on her face. She painted flowers, stars, rainbows, and animals. But her favorite thing to paint was fish. One day, she went to the park with her mom. She saw a pond with many fish. They were big and small, red and blue, striped and spotted. Lily wanted to paint them. She asked her mom for paper and colors. Her mom gave her a smile and a hug. She said, "Okay, Lily, you can paint the fish. But don't get too close to the water. And don't touch the fish. They are not pets. They are wild." Lily nodded. She sat on the grass and looked at the pond. She saw a chubby fish with yellow scales and a long tail. It swam near the surface and made bubbles. Lily thought it was funny and cute. She picked a yellow color and a big brush. She started to paint the chubby fish on her paper. She was so busy painting that she did not hear a voice behind her. It was a boy. He was older than Lily. He had brown hair and green eyes. He wore a hat and a backpack. He said, "Hey, what are you doing?" Lily turned around. She saw the boy. She said, "I am painting a fish. See? It is chubby and yellow. Do you like it?" The boy looked at Lily's paper. He frowned. He said, "That is not a fish. That is a blob. You can't paint. You are too little. You should give me your colors and brushes. I can paint better than you." Lily felt sad and angry. She did not like the boy. She did not want to give him her colors and brushes. She liked her fish. She said, "No, you can't have them. They are mine. And this is a fish. You are mean. Go away." The boy did not go away. He reached for Lily's paper. He said, "Give me that. I will show you how to paint a real fish." Lily did not let go. She pulled her paper back. She said, "No, stop. You are bad. Leave me alone." The boy and Lily tugged and pulled. They shouted and cried. They made a lot of noise. The fish in the pond heard them. They did not like the noise. They swam away. The chubby yellow fish was the last to go. It looked at Lily and the boy. It shook its head. It thought, "Humans are silly. They don't know how to paint or play. They should learn from us. We are happy and free." It swam to the other side of the pond. It joined its friends. It made more bubbles. It forgot about Lily and the boy.
