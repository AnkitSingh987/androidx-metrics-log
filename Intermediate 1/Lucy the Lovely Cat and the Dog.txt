Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Lovely Cat and the Dog

One day, a lovely cat named Lucy was playing in the kitchen. She saw a shiny knife on the table. Lucy was curious and wanted to touch it. She looked around and saw her friend, the dog named Max. "Max, can you help me get the knife?" Lucy asked. Max looked at the knife and knew it was not a toy. "No, Lucy, we should not touch it," Max said. Lucy did not like this answer and asked Max to repeat what he said. "We should not touch the knife, Lucy. It is not safe," Max said again. Lucy still wanted the knife, so she jumped up to get it. But something unexpected happened! The knife fell off the table and landed on a big, soft pillow. Lucy and Max looked at each other and laughed. They knew they were lucky that nothing bad happened. From that day on, Lucy and Max promised to never touch the knife again.

Sara and Ben like to play with the big wheel. The big wheel has many colors and numbers on it. They take turns to spin the wheel and see what number it stops on. "Look, I got six!" Sara says, pointing to the red number on the wheel. "That is a big number. I win!" "No, you don't win," Ben says, shaking his head. "You have to spin again and get the same number. That is the rule." Sara frowns. She does not like this rule. She thinks Ben made it up. She spins the wheel again and hopes for six. But the wheel stops on four, a blue number. "Ha, ha, I win!" Ben says, clapping his hands. "You did not get the same number. Now it is my turn." Ben spins the wheel and smiles. He gets eight, a yellow number. He thinks eight is the best number. It is the biggest and the most attractive. "Wow, I got eight!" Ben says, jumping up and down. "That is the best number. I win!" "No, you don't win," Sara says, crossing her arms. "You have to spin again and get the same number. That is the rule." Ben looks at Sara. He does not like this rule. He thinks Sara made it up. He spins the wheel again and hopes for eight. But the wheel stops on one, a green number. "Ha, ha, I win!" Sara says, laughing. "You did not get the same number. Now it is my turn." Sara and Ben keep spinning the wheel and changing the rule. They do not agree on who wins. They do not notice that their mom is watching them. She smiles and shakes her head. She thinks they are both silly and cute. She loves them very much.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was a very lucky girl. She had a job to do every day. Her job was to scatter seeds in the garden for the birds to eat. One day, while Lucy was doing her job, a little bird came to her. The bird said, "Thank you for the seeds, Lucy. You are so kind!" Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. I am happy to help." As the days went by, more and more birds came to the garden. They all thanked Lucy for her job. Lucy felt happy and lucky to have so many new friends. And the birds were happy too, because they had a friend who cared for them.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to count. She would count everything she saw. One day, Lily found a book. It was a novel, but she did not know what it was. Lily asked her mom, "What is this?" Her mom said, "It is a novel. It is a story in a book." Lily wanted to read the novel, but she did not know how to read. She asked her mom to read it to her. Her mom started to read the novel. But the story was about a deaf boy. Lily did not know what deaf meant. Her mom explained, "Deaf means you cannot hear." Lily felt sad for the boy in the story. But as they read more, Lily saw that the boy was happy because he could count just like her. In the end, Lily learned that everyone is special in their own way.
