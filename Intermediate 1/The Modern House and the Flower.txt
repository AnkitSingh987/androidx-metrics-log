Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modern House and the Flower

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In this house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved flowers. She had many flowers in her room. One day, Amy found a new flower in her garden. "Who do you belong to?" she asked the flower. The flower said, "I belong to you, Amy." Amy was very happy. She took the flower to her room and put it with her other flowers. Amy's friend, Ben, came to play. He saw the new flower and said, "Wow, what a pretty flower!" Amy told Ben the story of the flower. They both laughed and played with the flowers all day.

One day, a helpful cat named Sam was in the kitchen. Sam saw a piece of toast on the table. The toast had some jam on it. Sam wanted to eat the toast, but it was too high for him to reach. Sam had a friend, a little bird named Tim. Sam called out, "Tim, can you help me get the toast?" Tim flew down and said, "Yes, I can help you!" Tim picked up the toast with his beak and gave it to Sam. Sam was very happy. He took a bite of the toast and got jam on his face. Tim saw the jam and said, "Sam, you have jam on your face!" Sam replied, "Oh no! What should I do?" Tim was helpful and said, "I will wipe your face with my wing." Tim wiped Sam's face clean. They both smiled and enjoyed the toast together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was anxious because he lost his favorite toy. He looked for it in the house, but he could not find it. So, Spot went outside to look for it. Spot saw a well and thought his toy might be there. He went near the well and looked inside. Spot was scared, but he did not surrender. He wanted to find his toy so much. He tried to reach for it and fell into the well. Nobody heard Spot's cries for help. He was stuck in the well for a long time. Spot was sad and scared. He could not get out of the well. This story has a bad ending, and Spot stayed in the well.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big paw. One day, Spot went to the park to play with his friends. Spot saw a ball and he wanted to play with it. Spot ran to the ball with his big paw. He tried to pick it up, but it was bitter and yucky. Spot did not like the bitter ball. He wanted to find another toy to play with. Spot saw his friend, a cat named Whiskers, playing with a nice toy. Spot went to Whiskers and asked if he could play too. Whiskers said yes, and they played together all day. Spot was happy he found a new toy and a friend to play with.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom was very wealthy. He had a big house and many pets. He loved to feed his pets every day. Tom also loved to paint pretty pictures with bright colors. One day, Tom saw a small, hungry bird outside his window. He thought, "I will feed this bird and make it my friend." So, Tom went to the store to buy bird food. While he was there, he also bought a new color of paint. It was shiny and gold. Tom went home and fed the bird. The bird was happy and became Tom's friend. Then, Tom painted a beautiful picture using the shiny, gold paint. The painting was of the bird eating the food he gave it. Tom and the bird were very happy, and they lived together as best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Pippin. Pippin was a very useful penguin. He loved to help his friends. One day, Pippin found a big, green leaf. He had an idea. He thought, "I can use this leaf to help my friends." Pippin showed the leaf to his friend, Bunny. Bunny asked, "What can we do with this leaf, Pippin?" Pippin said, "We can chew it and make it soft. Then, we can give it to Birdy. Birdy can use it to make her nest warm." So, Pippin and Bunny chewed the leaf. They made it very soft. They took it to Birdy. Birdy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Pippin and Bunny! This soft leaf is perfect for my nest." Pippin and Bunny were proud. They knew they had done something good. They had used the leaf to help their friend. And that is how Pippin the penguin and Bunny learned that being useful is a great thing.
