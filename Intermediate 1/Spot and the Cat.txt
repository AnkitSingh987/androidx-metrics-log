Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a red collar. He liked his collar because it was comfortable. Spot wore his collar every day. One day, Spot wanted to have a party. He wanted to invite all his friends. Spot invited his friends Cat, Bird, and Fish to his party. They all said yes and were very happy. On the day of the party, Spot's friends came to his house. They played games, ate treats, and had a fun time. Spot was happy that he invited his friends to his party. They all had a great day together.

---

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw something on the ground. He went to examine it. It was a pair of sunglasses. They were big and black. Tim thought they were cool. Tim put on the sunglasses. He looked in the mirror. He saw his face with the sunglasses. He thought he looked scary. Tim smiled. He liked looking scary. Tim went outside with the sunglasses. His friend saw him. She said, "You look scary!" Tim laughed. He took off the sunglasses. He gave them to his friend. She put them on. Now, she looked scary too. They both laughed and played together, wearing the scary sunglasses.

---

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Bob. He liked to eat and sleep all day. Bob had many friends in the yard, like the birds and the mice. One day, Bob and his friends made a plan. They wanted to have a big party. They talked about what food to eat and games to play. Bob said, "We need to make an order so we know what to do." So, they made a list. First, they would play games. Then, they would eat yummy food. And last, they would sing and dance. The fat cat and his friends were very excited for their party. They had fun and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little cell. The little cell was very enthusiastic. It loved to play with its friends. One day, the little cell met a big cell. "Hi big cell, do you want to play with me?" the little cell asked. "Yes, little cell, let's play!" said the big cell. The little cell and the big cell played all day. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. When it was time to go home, the little cell felt sad. "Will you come back tomorrow and play with me?" the little cell asked the big cell. "Of course, little cell! I will come back and play with you," said the big cell. The little cell was very happy. It went home and told its mom and dad about the big cell. The little cell could not wait to play with the big cell again. That night, the little cell went to bed and closed its eyes, dreaming of more fun days with its new friend, the big cell.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dinosaur. He lived in a big forest with his friends. The smart dinosaur loved to play with all his friends every day. One day, the smart dinosaur found a big, pretty balloon. He wanted to play with it, but he did not want to lose it. So, he had an idea. He would attach the balloon to his tail. The smart dinosaur attached the balloon to his tail and went to show his friends. They all played together, and the balloon stayed with the smart dinosaur. They had so much fun, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little girl named Amy. Amy had a toy bear named Ben. They played all day and had lots of fun. One day, Amy saw a crack on the wall. She wondered how it got there. Amy showed the crack to Ben. Ben looked at the crack and frowned. He did not like the crack. Amy felt sad too. They wanted to make the crack go away. Amy and Ben thought hard. They had an idea! They put a pretty picture over the crack. Now, they could not see the crack anymore. Amy and Ben smiled and went back to playing happily. The crack was gone, and they had a lovely day.
