Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Bird

One day, Tom went to the field to collect rocks. He liked rocks a lot. He put the rocks in a big bag. The sun was hot, and Tom was tired. But he wanted more rocks. As he looked for more rocks, he saw a big hole in the field. He went to look at the hole. Inside the hole, he found many shiny rocks. Tom was very happy. Tom put the shiny rocks in his bag too. But when he tried to lift the bag, it was too heavy. Tom was sad. Then, a big bird came and helped Tom. The bird carried the heavy bag of rocks for Tom. They went home together, and Tom was happy again.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the beach with her mom. They saw a lot of sand and water. They also saw a small, harmless crab. The crab was red and had big eyes. Lily liked the crab and wanted to play with it. Lily's mom said, "Lily, be kind to the crab. It is harmless and just wants to serve its family, like you and me." Lily and the crab played in the sand. They made a big sand castle together. Lily's mom watched and smiled. The sun was warm and the water was cool. They had a lot of fun at the beach. At the end of the day, Lily said goodbye to the crab. She told the crab, "Thank you for playing with me. I hope you can serve your family well." The crab waved its claw and went back to its home in the sand. Lily and her mom left the beach with happy hearts. They knew they would come back to the beach again soon.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to clap his paws and dance. One day, Remy went on a search to find his best friend, Tilly the turtle. He looked under the trees, behind the bushes, and near the pond. Remy started to feel tired from his long search. He sat down under a big tree to rest. Just then, he saw Tilly walking slowly towards him. Tilly was sad because she had lost her way home. Remy wanted to help Tilly, but he was too tired to search more. Remy had an idea. He clapped his paws and danced to cheer up Tilly. Tilly smiled and started to dance too. They danced and clapped together, and soon Remy didn't feel tired anymore. They both searched together and found Tilly's home. The moral of the story is that when you feel tired, sometimes having fun with a friend can give you the energy you need to keep going.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw a big slide and a hoop. Tim was so happy. He ran to the slide and went down it. "Wee!" he said as he slid down. He did it again and again. Next, he saw a poor, sad dog near the hoop. The dog had no friends. Tim wanted to help the dog. He took a ball and threw it through the hoop. The dog jumped and caught the ball. They played together. "Good dog!" said Tim. The dog wagged its tail. Tim and the dog became best friends. They played with the hoop every day. The poor dog was not sad anymore. Tim and the dog had so much fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big motorcycle. He was very happy and enthusiastic. He wanted to ride it, but he was too small. So, he asked his mom, "Can I ride the motorcycle, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. You are too little. But you can dance around it and have fun." Tim was sad, but he decided to dance around the motorcycle. As he danced, something unexpected happened. The motorcycle started to dance too! It jumped up and down, side to side, just like Tim. Tim was surprised and laughed. He danced with the motorcycle all day long, and they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue car. The car had four thick tires. One day, the car was driving down the road when it hit a big rock. The car stopped because one of the tires was not round anymore. The car's driver, a nice man, knew he had to remove the bad tire. He got out of the car and looked at the tire. He saw it was not round and needed to be fixed. The man was sad because he did not know how to remove the tire. A helpful friend saw the man was sad and stopped to help. The friend knew how to remove the bad tire. They worked together and removed the tire. The man was happy and thanked his friend. They put a new tire on the car and drove away, happy and safe.
