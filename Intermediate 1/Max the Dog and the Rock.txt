Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Rock

One day, a big dog named Max went for a walk in the park. Max loved to look at everything around him. He saw a big tree, a small bird, and a pretty flower. Max was very happy. As Max walked, he felt a pain in his paw. He looked down and saw a small rock stuck in his paw. It hurt a lot. Max tried to get the rock out, but he couldn't. He was very sad. Suddenly, a tiny mouse named Tim saw Max's pain. Tim wanted to help. Max didn't think the tiny mouse could help him, but Tim was very smart. Tim used a stick to get the rock out of Max's paw. Max was so happy and surprised! He and Tim became best friends.

Anna was sick. She had a fever and a sore throat. She did not want to eat or play. She only wanted to lie in bed and watch cartoons. Her mom was compassionate. She gave Anna some medicine and a glass of water. She put a thermometer in Anna's mouth to check her temperature. She said, "You are very brave, Anna. I know you don't feel well, but the medicine will help you get better soon." Anna did not like the thermometer. It was cold and hard. She complained, "Mom, I don't want this. It hurts my mouth. Can I take it out?" Her mom said, "No, Anna, you have to keep it in for a little while. It will tell me how hot you are. If you are too hot, you might need to see the doctor." Anna did not want to see the doctor. She was scared of the doctor. She said, "No, mom, I don't want to see the doctor. He will poke me with a needle. Can I just watch cartoons?" Her mom said, "Yes, Anna, you can watch cartoons, but only after I see the thermometer. It will beep when it is done. Then you can take it out and I will give you a hug." Anna waited for the thermometer to beep. She hoped it would beep soon. She wanted to take it out and watch cartoons. She wanted to feel better. She loved her mom. Her mom was compassionate.

Once upon a time, there was a red car. The red car had a flat tire. The car was sad because it could not go. A man saw the red car and said, "I can fix your tire!" The man had a big smile. The red car was happy. The man tried to fix the tire, but he did not know how. The red car said, "Oh no! I still can't go!" The man was sad too. They could not fix the tire, and the red car stayed sad.

Once there was a little girl named Suzy. She loved to wander in the garden and explore. One day, she saw a bright, round orange pumpkin and she smiled. She couldn't contain her excitement and started running towards it. "Hello pumpkin!" Said Suzy. The pumpkin replied, "Hi Suzy!" in an excited voice. "Do you smell yummy?" asked Suzy. The pumpkin said, "Yes! I'm so yummy!" Suzy was so happy that she hugged the pumpkin. She took the pumpkin inside her house and started to prepare it for dinner. She put the pumpkin in the oven and waited for it to cook. When the pumpkin was done, it smelled so good. Suzy took it out of the oven and started to cut it with a knife. She peeled away the orange skin and scooped out some yummy pumpkin seeds. Then she served it on a plate and said, "Dinner time!" The whole family enjoyed the delicious yummy pumpkin. Suzy was so glad she decided to wander in her garden that day.

One day, a big man in a suit was walking down the street. He had a frown on his face. He looked scared. A little boy saw the man and wanted to help. The little boy walked up to the man and said, "Hi, why are you scared?" The man looked down at the boy and said, "I lost my dog. I don't know where he is." The boy wanted to help the man find his dog. They started to look for the dog together. As they walked, they heard a bark. They turned around and saw the dog! But, the dog was wearing the man's suit! The man and the boy laughed. The man was not scared anymore. They all went home happy.
