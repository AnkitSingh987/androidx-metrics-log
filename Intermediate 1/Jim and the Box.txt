Category: Intermediate
Title: Jim and the Box

Jim looked out the front window of his house and noticed something was strange. He saw a green box by the door. He ran out and opened the box excitedly. Inside, he found a jar of jam. Jim was so excited he could hardly contain himself. He shouted out "Welcome!" He took the jam back inside and ran to his mom. He said to her, "Mom, look what I found outside—jam!" His mom smiled and thanked him for being so excited. She suggested that they invite the jam over for a visit. The next day, the family welcomed the jam inside. They showed it around the house and made sure it was comfortable. Later on, they all ate together happily. As they were enjoying the jam, Jim noticed something strange was happening. The jam was turning green. His mom smiled and said, "That jam was a special green jam from a secret store. I thought it would be a great surprise for you today." Jim smiled excitedly and thanked his mom. He loved his surprise and was happy to share it with his family.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Sam who was very lazy. He didn't like to move at all and never wanted to exercise. Every day he would beg his mom not to go to the gym. One day, his mom said to him, "Sam, I promise that if you come to the gym with me, I'll buy you a surprise!" Sam thought about it and finally agreed. At the gym, it wasn't easy for Sam. He had to walk around and carry some heavy objects. He was so tired that he fell asleep in the middle of it. When they got home, his mom told him, "You kept your promise and kept going even when it was hard. So here's your surprise! A big, delicious chocolate cake!" Sam was so happy and excited. He had had lots of fun at the gym and received a great reward. From that day on, Sam stopped being lazy. He ventured out of the house and had new adventures with his mom. He was much happier and healthier!

Tom and Jen are twins. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big slide. They want to go on it. "Let's go on the slide!" Tom says. "OK, but be careful!" Jen says. They run to the slide. Tom goes first. He twists his body as he slides down. He thinks it is fun. Jen goes after him. She twists her body too. But she twists too much. She falls off the slide. She hurts her arm. She cries. "Ow, ow, ow!" Jen says. Tom hears her cry. He runs to her. He sees her arm. It is red and swollen. "I'm sorry, Jen!" Tom says. "Are you OK?" "No, I'm not OK!" Jen says. "You made me twist too much! You are a bad brother!" Tom feels sad. He did not mean to make Jen twist too much. He wants to help her. He sees a lemonade stand nearby. He has some coins in his pocket. He goes to the stand. He buys a cup of lemonade. He brings it to Jen. "Here, Jen, drink this!" Tom says. "It will make you feel better!" Jen takes the cup. She drinks the lemonade. But it is very sour. She makes a face. She spits it out. "Yuck, yuck, yuck!" Jen says. "This is the worst lemonade ever! You are a bad brother!" Tom feels worse. He did not know the lemonade was sour. He wanted to make Jen happy. He does not know what to do. A lady sees them. She is a nurse. She works at the park. She comes to them. She sees Jen's arm. She has a bandage and some ice. She puts the bandage and the ice on Jen's arm. She smiles at them. "Hello, children!" the lady says. "What happened?" Tom tells her what happened. He says he is sorry. He says he loves Jen. Jen stops crying. She sees Tom's face. He looks sad. She feels sorry too. She says she loves Tom. She says she is sorry. She says he is a good brother. The lady hugs them. She says they are good children. She says they should be careful on the slide. She says they should be kind to each other. She says they should share the lemonade. She gives them another cup of lemonade. It is sweet and cold. They drink it. They smile. The end. Moral value: Be careful on the slide. Be kind to your siblings. Share the lemonade.
