Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom loved to dance. One day, he saw an unusual bird sitting on a tree branch. The bird was very colorful and pretty. Tom wanted to dance with the bird. Tom said, "Hey bird, let's dance together!" The bird looked at Tom and said, "Okay, but you have to sit first." Tom sat down on the grass. The bird flew down and landed on Tom's head. Tom and the bird started to dance. They danced and danced. The bird sang a happy song. Tom and the bird had so much fun. They danced all day long, and they became the best of friends.

One sunny day, a big, red ball went for a roll. It rolled down a hill, faster and faster. It rolled through the land, making an impressive trip. The ball was very happy. On its way, the ball met a little dog. The little dog said, "Hi, ball! Can I roll with you?" The ball said, "Yes, let's roll together!" So, the little dog and the big, red ball rolled down the hill together. They rolled through the land, having a lot of fun. They rolled over soft grass and through cool water. At the end of the day, the little dog and the big, red ball were very tired. They said goodbye and went to sleep, happy from their impressive day of rolling.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red fire truck. It was amazing and shiny. The fire truck had a job to do. It had to teach little kids about fire. The kids loved the fire truck and listened carefully. One day, the fire truck went to a school. The kids were happy to see it. The fire truck started to teach the kids about fire. It said, "Fire is hot. Fire can hurt you. We must be careful with fire." The kids nodded their heads and said, "Yes, fire truck, we will be careful." But then, something unexpected happened! A little cat came running up to the fire truck. The cat said, "I know about fire too! I can help teach the kids!" The fire truck was surprised. Cats don't usually talk! But the fire truck said, "Okay, cat. You can help me teach the kids." So the cat and the fire truck taught the kids about fire together. The kids learned a lot and had fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lila and Tom were twins who liked to play in the garden. One day, they found a big sack behind the shed. They opened it and saw many candles of different colors and shapes. "Wow, look at these!" Lila said. "They are so pretty and shiny. Can we take some?" "I don't know," Tom said. "Maybe they belong to someone. Maybe they are for a special day." Lila did not listen. She grabbed a red candle and a blue candle and put them in her pocket. Tom took a green candle and a yellow candle and did the same. They ran back to the house and went to their room. They closed the door and lit the candles with a match. They put them on the table and watched them glow. "They are so nice," Lila said. "They make our room look like a fairy tale." "Yes, they do," Tom said. "But I feel bad. We should not have taken them. What if someone misses them?" Just then, they heard a knock on the door. It was their grandma, who lived with them. She opened the door and saw the candles. "Children, what are you doing?" she asked. "Where did you get these candles?" Lila and Tom looked at each other. They felt ashamed. They told their grandma the truth. They said they found the candles in a sack in the garden and took them without asking. Their grandma was not angry. She was sad. She said, "These candles are very special. They are from my old country. I brought them with me when I came here. They are for a festival that I celebrate every year. It is a festival of light and hope. I was going to share them with you and teach you about it. But now, some of them are gone." Lila and Tom felt very sorry. They hugged their grandma and said they were sorry. They said they did not know. They said they would give the candles back. Their grandma smiled and said, "It's okay, my dears. I forgive you. You made a mistake, but you learned from it. That is how you become wise. Come, let's go to the garden and put the candles back in the sack. Then, we can light them together and celebrate the festival. I will tell you all about it." Lila and Tom nodded and followed their grandma. They put the candles back in the sack and thanked her for being so kind. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to respect other people's things and to be curious about other cultures. They also learned that their grandma was very wise and loved them very much.
