Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Ball of Tim and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an incredible sock. This sock was not like any other sock. It could reverse time when Tim put it on his foot. Tim liked to play with the sock and see what would happen. One day, Tim was playing outside with his friends. They were playing with a ball. Tim's friend threw the ball very high, and it went into a tree. Tim said, "Oh no! The ball is stuck in the tree!" His friends were sad because they could not reach the ball. Then Tim remembered his incredible sock. He put it on his foot and reversed time. The ball came out of the tree and went back into his friend's hand. Tim's friends were surprised and happy. They said, "Wow, Tim! How did you do that?" Tim just smiled and said, "It's a secret." They all laughed and continued playing with the ball, having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a creative girl named Lucy. She loved to draw and paint all day. Lucy liked to feel the texture of different things, like soft fur or bumpy rocks. One day, Lucy needed a new idea for her art. She went for a walk and found a big, colorful bird. The bird had soft feathers and a bumpy beak. Lucy thought the bird would be a great idea for her art. She started to draw the bird, but something unexpected happened. As she was drawing, the bird changed colors! It went from red to blue to green. Lucy was surprised, but she kept drawing. Now, her art was even more creative than before. Lucy and the bird became friends, and they made beautiful art together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Dizzy. He was called Dizzy because he would run around in circles and make himself feel dizzy. One day, Dizzy was playing in the park when he saw a big ball. He wanted to play with the ball, but it was too big for him to carry. Dizzy asked his friend, a big dog named Max, for help. "Max, can you help me carry the ball?" Dizzy said. Max nodded and said, "Yes, Dizzy, I can help you." So Max used his strong legs to help Dizzy carry the ball. Together, they played with the ball all day long. They were very happy and tired. At the end of the day, Dizzy said, "Thank you, Max, for helping me carry the ball. You are a good friend." Max smiled and said, "You're welcome, Dizzy. I'm always here to help you." And they went home to rest their tired legs.

Once upon a time, there was a little dove. The dove liked to fly high in the sky. One day, the dove saw a boy playing in the park. The boy was very alert, always looking around. The dove flew down and landed near the boy. The boy saw the dove and smiled. He wanted to play with the dove. So, he picked up a small stick and threw it. The dove flew after the stick and brought it back to the boy. They played together all day. The boy would throw the stick, and the dove would bring it back. They both smiled and had lots of fun. The sun started to set, and it was time for the boy to go home. He waved goodbye to the dove, and the dove flew back into the sky. They both knew they would play together again another day.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red cart. The cart was very happy. It loved to roll around the town. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the cart. He wanted to tell his mom about it. Tim's mom said, "Go play with the cart, but be careful." Tim played with the cart all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The cart started to talk! It said, "I can tell you a secret." Tim was very surprised. The cart told Tim that it was a magic cart. It could make wishes come true. Tim wished for a big ice cream. The bright red cart made his wish come true. Tim and the cart were very happy and played together every day.
