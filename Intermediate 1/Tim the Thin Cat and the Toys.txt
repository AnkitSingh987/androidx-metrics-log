Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Thin Cat and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a thin cat named Tim. Tim was a very good cat who liked to help others. One day, Tim saw his friend Sam, a dog, trying to fix his toys. Sam looked sad because his toys were broken. "Sam, do you need help?" asked Tim. Sam looked at Tim and said, "Yes, but I don't have the right equipment to fix my toys." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I know where we can find the equipment. Trust me, I can help you." Tim and Sam went to the village shop where they found the equipment they needed. They bought glue and tape to fix the toys. They worked together and fixed all of Sam's toys. Sam was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. From that day on, Sam and Tim became best friends. They learned that by trusting each other and working together, they could solve any problem. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to read a book. She went to her tidy room and looked at her books on the shelf. The book she wanted was on the top shelf, but she was too small to reach it. Lily saw a ladder in the corner of her room. She said, "I can use the ladder to get the book!" She climbed up the ladder and got the book she wanted. She was very happy. Lily sat down and started to read her book. Her mom came in and said, "Good job, Lily! You used the ladder to get your book and kept your room tidy." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!" They read the book together and had a great time.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Daisy. Daisy was playing in the park and wanted to show her friends something. She found two peaches and said to her friends, "Look! I found two peaches!" Her friends replied, "Wow Daisy, you are a smart girl! Do you want us to help you?" Daisy smiled and said, "Yes please!" So, the friends all gathered around the peaches. They all put their hands together and began to slowly lift the peaches. When they reached the top, they cheered! The peaches were now strong and blue and they supported Daisy proudly. Daisy couldn't help but smile and said, "Thank you, my friends. This is the best peach ever!" Her friends agreed and they all admired the beautiful blue peach. Daisy and her friends hugged and celebrated their success!

Once upon a time, in a big box, a rat named Tim lived. Tim was a kind rat who liked to help his friends. One day, a little bug came to Tim's box. The bug looked sad. "Can you lend me some food?" the bug asked. "I am very hungry." Tim wanted to help his new friend, so he gave the bug some of his food. The bug was happy and went away. The next day, the bug came back with more bug friends. They all wanted food. Tim was kind, so he gave them food too. But soon, there was no food left for Tim. The bugs did not care, and they left Tim all alone. Tim was sad and hungry. The clear moral of the story is to be careful when helping others and not to give away all that you have.

Lena and Ben are friends. They like to play together. They have a lot of fun. One day, they find a big box in the park. They open it and see many things inside. They see a dress, a suit, a ring, a cake, and some flowers. They think it is a marriage box. Lena and Ben decide to play marriage. Lena puts on the dress and Ben puts on the suit. They put the ring on each other's finger. They say, "I do". They cut the cake and share it. They smell the flowers and smile. They think they are married now. Lena and Ben are very happy. They think they are gifted. They have a marriage box and each other. They hug and kiss. They say, "We love you". They play marriage until it is time to go home. They take the box with them. They want to play marriage again tomorrow.
