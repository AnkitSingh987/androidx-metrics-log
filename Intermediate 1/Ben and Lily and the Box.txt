Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Box

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play pretend. One day, they found a big box of costumes in the attic. They tried on different hats, scarves, and glasses. Ben found a fake mustache and stuck it on his face. He pretended to be a detective and said, "I am looking for clues. Do you have any secrets, Lily?" Lily giggled and shook her head. She found a pink dress and a tiara. She pretended to be a princess and said, "I am very shy and kind. I like to help animals and people. Do you want to be my friend, Ben?" Ben nodded and smiled. He liked to be Lily's friend. They played together for a long time, making up stories and adventures. They had a lot of fun. But then they heard their mom calling them. "Ben, Lily, it's time for lunch. Come downstairs and behave, please." Ben and Lily quickly took off their costumes and put them back in the box. They ran downstairs and hugged their mom. She smiled and said, "You look very happy. What were you playing?" Ben and Lily told her about their pretend game. They showed her the fake mustache and the tiara. Their mom laughed and said, "You are very creative and clever. I'm glad you had fun. But don't forget to wash your hands before you eat." Ben and Lily nodded and went to the bathroom. They washed their hands and their faces. They were hungry and ready for lunch. They thanked their mom and ate their sandwiches. They behaved very well. They were good twins.

One day, a girl named Sue put on her modest blouse. Sue loved her blouse because it was pretty and soft. She wanted to show her blouse to her friends. So, she went to the park to play. At the park, Sue saw her friends. They were all playing a fun game. The game was to roll a big ball. Sue wanted to play too. But when she tried to roll the ball, she fell down. Her blouse got dirty. Sue was sad. Sue's friends saw that she was sad. They wanted to help her. They had an idea. They all took off their blouses and rolled them in the dirt. Now, all their blouses were dirty too. They all laughed and played together. Sue was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an adorable pet cat named Mimi. They loved to play together in the sun. One day, Lily and Mimi found a big, deep well near their house. They looked inside and saw lots of water. Lily had an idea to share the water with all her friends. Lily called her friends to come and see the well. They all came with their cups and shared the water from the well. They laughed and played together, and Mimi the cat joined in the fun. Everyone was happy because they shared the water from the well.

Once upon a time, there was a big rabbit. This rabbit was very powerful. It could jump high and run fast. It lived in a nice home with a kind girl. The girl loved her rabbit a lot. She would feed it, play with it, and take care of it every day. She would say, "I am happy to own my rabbit." The rabbit loved the girl, too. They were best friends. One day, the rabbit saw a big tree with a cat stuck in it. The cat was scared. The powerful rabbit jumped high and helped the cat come down. The cat said, "Thank you, rabbit!" The girl was proud of her rabbit. They all played together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a busy bee named Bob. Bob loved to fly around and slap his wings. He was always very busy, making honey and helping his friends. One day, Bob found a jar with a label on it. The label had a picture of a bee. Bob was very excited and said to his friend, "Look, I found a jar with a bee on it!" His friend said, "Be careful, Bob. That jar might be trouble." But Bob didn't listen. He wanted to see what was inside the jar. He slapped his wings and flew closer. When he opened the jar, a big spider jumped out! The spider was not nice and wanted to eat Bob. Bob's friend tried to help, but it was too late. The spider caught Bob and ate him. Bob's friend was very sad and said, "I wish Bob had listened to me. Now he is gone." And that is the end of the story.
