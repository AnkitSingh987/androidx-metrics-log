Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car of Tim the Boy

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He loved to make the car go fast and pretend he was the driver. Tim wanted to put his toy car on the high shelf, but he could not reach it. Tim saw his big sister, Sue, and asked, "Can you help me reach the high shelf?" Sue smiled and said, "Of course, little brother!" She reached up and put the toy car on the shelf for Tim. Later, Tim was sad because he wanted to play with his toy car again, but it was still on the high shelf. Just then, a compassionate cat named Fluffy jumped up onto the shelf, knocked the toy car down, and Tim could play with it again. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, Fluffy!" The cat meowed and Tim knew that Fluffy was happy to help.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to a distant place. They saw a big temple. They wanted to go inside and see what was there. Tim said, "Let's go in, Spot!" But Spot disagreed. He did not want to go inside the temple. Tim was sad, but he went inside alone. Spot waited outside for him. Inside the temple, Tim found a big box. He opened it, and a magic bird came out. The bird said, "Thank you for letting me out, Tim. I will give you one wish." Tim wished for a big, fun playground for him and Spot to play in. The bird made the wish come true, and they played all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a tall giraffe named Gina. She lived in a big safari with her friends. They liked to play and have fun all day. But one day, a new animal came to the safari. His name was Tommy the tiger. Tommy was very loud and wanted to play all the time. Gina and her friends were not sure if they liked Tommy. One day, Tommy wanted to play a game, but Gina and her friends wanted to rest. Tommy was sad and went away. Gina felt bad for Tommy. She had an idea to help everyone settle and be happy. She talked to her friends and they agreed to try her idea. The next day, Gina told Tommy they would play a new game. They would play "quiet time" first, and then they would play his loud game. Tommy agreed to try it. They all played "quiet time" and then had fun playing Tommy's loud game. In the end, everyone was happy and settled. Gina's idea helped them all be friends and have fun together.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play with cars and balls. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big fountain with water. The water is shiny and cold. "Look, Tom, water!" Sam says. He runs to the fountain. He wants to drink the water. "Wait, Sam, no!" Tom says. He knows the water is not good to drink. It is dirty and has germs. He follows Sam. But Sam does not hear Tom. Sam is deaf. He cannot hear any words. He only sees Tom's mouth move. He thinks Tom wants to play. He smiles and puts his hand in the water. He brings the water to his mouth. "Sam, stop!" Tom says. He grabs Sam's hand. He shakes his head. He points to his bottle. "Drink this, Sam. This is good water. That is bad water." Sam looks at Tom. He sees Tom's face. He sees Tom's bottle. He understands. He drops the water. He takes Tom's bottle. He drinks the good water. He says "thank you" with his hands. Tom smiles. He says "you're welcome" with his hands. He hugs Sam. They are still friends. They go back to their moms. They play with their cars and balls. They are happy.

Once upon a time there lived three friends: a girl called Holly, a boy called Tom and a cat called Misty. Holly was a poor girl and she only had one blouse to wear. Whenever Holly and her friends went on adventures, Holly was always anxious. She worried that someone would make fun of her because of her poor blouse. One day when Holly and her friends were walking in the woods, Holly encountered a small creature called Red Bird. Red Bird was a beautiful bird with a red beak and feathers. The bird said to Holly, "I can help you. Trust me". Holly nodded her head in agreement and Red Bird told Holly to take off her blouse and put it on the ground. Holly did as the bird said and Red Bird spread it's wings and flew around it. All of a sudden the blouse was changed into a sparkling, new one. Holly was ecstatic and her friends were amazed. From then on Holly learnt an important lesson: that you should always trust those who offer to help you. She was also thankful to Red Bird for teaching her that even if someone doesn't have a lot, they can still be happy.
