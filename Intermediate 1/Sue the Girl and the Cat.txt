Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to draw on her blackboard. One day, she drew a cat on the blackboard. The cat was very pretty. Suddenly, the cat appeared off the blackboard and said, "Hello, Sue! I am a magic cat. I can do tricks. Do you want to see?" Sue was very surprised and said, "Yes, please!" The magic cat did a trick and made a big ice cream appear. Sue was so happy! She shared the ice cream with the cat. They had lots of fun and became good friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big barrel in his yard. Tim wanted to look inside the barrel, but he could not see the top. Tim tried to climb the barrel. He put one foot up and then the other. But then, he began to stumble. Tim fell down on the soft grass. He was not hurt, but he felt a little sad. Just then, Tim's mom came out of the house. She had a big cup of something. She said, "Tim, do you want to try this?" Tim took a sip. It was bitter! Tim made a funny face. His mom laughed, and Tim laughed too. They forgot about the barrel and went inside to find something sweet to eat.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toys all day. One day, Tom found a key on the floor. It was a big key and he thought it was crazy that he had never seen it before. Tom wanted to know what the key could open, so he looked for a door or a box. But first, he decided to wash the key because it was dirty. Tom took the key to the sink and washed it with soap and water. The key became shiny and clean. Tom searched the whole house for something to open with the key. He tried every door and box he could find, but the key did not fit. Tom felt a little sad, but then he had a crazy idea. He put the key under his pillow before going to bed. That night, Tom had the most amazing dreams about magical lands and adventures. The key had opened the door to a world of imagination.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a toy kayak that he loved to play with in the water. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sue playing by the water too. She looked sad. Tim asked Sue, "Why are you sad?" Sue said, "I don't have a toy to play with in the water." Tim thought for a moment. He didn't want to be selfish, so he decided to share his toy kayak with Sue. As they played together, Tim and Sue became good friends. They laughed and splashed in the water with the toy kayak. From that day on, they always played together and shared their toys. And they were never selfish again.

Mom was feeling cold, so she decided to put on a scarf. The scarf was made of a soft, dry fabric. As she wrapped it around her neck, she asked her three-year-old daughter, "Can you help me with this?" The daughter smiled and said, "Yes!" She took off her own scarf and carefully wrapped it around her mommy's neck. Mommy said, "Thank you, that's perfect!" The daughter's old scarf wasn't as comfortable as mommy's, but she didn't mind. She said, "Do you need me to replace it when it gets too cold?" Mommy smiled and said, "Yes, thank you. I would love that." The daughter hugged her mommy and said, "I will always replace your scarf when it's too cold." Mommy smiled and thanked her daughter.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to help her mom in the garden. They would raise many vegetables together. They grew big tomatoes, tall corn, and green peas. Mia's favorite vegetable was the big tomato. One day, Mia saw a fake bug in the garden. She screamed, "Mom, look! A big bug!" Her mom laughed and said, "That's just a fake bug, Mia. It's not real. It won't eat our vegetables." Mia was happy that the bug was fake. She and her mom continued to raise their vegetables. They had a big feast with all the vegetables they grew. Mia shared her big tomatoes with her friends, and they all loved them. From that day on, Mia wasn't scared of fake bugs anymore.
