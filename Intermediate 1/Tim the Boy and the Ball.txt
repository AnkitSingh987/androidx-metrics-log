Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ball

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to play with his ball. He saw his friend, Sue, on the other side of the park. Tim said, "Hi Sue! Let's play!" Sue said, "Ok, Tim! Let's get the ball and play!" They played and had fun. They laughed and ran. But then, something not normal happened. The ball went up into a tree! Tim and Sue looked up at the ball in the tree. They did not know what to do. Then, a big bird flew down and got the ball. The bird gave the ball back to Tim and Sue. Tim and Sue said, "Thank you, bird!" They played with the ball again. This time, they played near the trees, so the ball would not get stuck. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl. She was three years old and very curious. Every day she would explore and discover new things. One day she went for a walk with her mom. She saw a beautiful flower and wanted to pick it. She reached out and was about to take it, when her mom said "No, don't forget, we don't take other people's flowers". The little girl was very disappointed. Then, her mom winked and said, "Let's go home and I'll get you a new flower". The little girl was so excited! When they got home, her mom gave her a new flower. It was so pretty and the little girl was so happy. From that day on, she always remembered not to take other people's flowers. Every time she saw a flower, she would wink and remember the time her mom gave her a new one.

One day, a mop named Mimi lived in a small house. Mimi was a very good mop. She liked to clean the house every day. Mimi made the house look nice and shiny. The sun was hot outside, but Mimi did not mind. She was happy to help. Mimi had a friend named Tom. Tom was a big mop. Tom lived in a big house. Tom wanted Mimi to accept his help to clean the big house. Mimi was scared because the big house was very big. But Tom said, "Do not worry, Mimi. We can do it together." Mimi and Tom worked together to clean the big house. They were hot and tired, but they did not stop. They cleaned and cleaned until the big house was clean. Mimi was happy she accepted Tom's help. Now, Mimi and Tom were very good friends. They cleaned houses together and had fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big parade. All the people and animals march in the parade. They were happy and having fun. There was a prize for the best marcher. A little dog named Spot wanted to win the prize. He practiced his march every day. He marched fast and slow. He marched high and low. Spot wanted to be the best marcher in the parade. On the day of the parade, Spot marched with all his friends. They all marched their best. At the end of the parade, the judges gave the prize to a big horse. Spot was sad that he did not win. But his friends told him, "You did a great job, Spot! We all had fun marching with you." Spot smiled and knew that he was still a winner because he had his friends.

One day, a little girl named Sally went with her mom to the grocery store. They wanted to buy things to bake a cake. Sally was very excited because she loved to bake with her mom. At the store, they met a sad old lady. She was looking for food but did not have enough money. Sally's mom said, "Let's help her. We will buy her some food and bake a cake for her too." Sally smiled and said, "Yes, let's do that!" Sally and her mom baked a cake for the old lady and gave her some food. The old lady was grateful and said, "Thank you so much!" Sally felt happy that they could help someone. She learned that it is good to help others and share what you have.
