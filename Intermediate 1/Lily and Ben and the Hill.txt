Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Ben and the Hill

Lily and Ben are playing in the park. They see a big hill with green grass. They want to roll down the hill. "Let's roll, Lily!" Ben says. "OK, Ben!" Lily says. They run to the top of the hill and lie down on their backs. They hold hands and start to roll. They roll faster and faster. They feel the wind on their faces and the sun on their skin. They laugh and scream. They reach the bottom of the hill and stop. They are dizzy and happy. They sit up and look at each other. They have grass and dirt on their clothes and hair. "That was fun, Ben!" Lily says. "Yes, it was, Lily!" Ben says. They hug each other. They feel warm and cozy. Lily sees a man with a camera. He is taking photos of the flowers. She has an idea. "Ben, let's ask the man to take a photo of us!" Lily says. "OK, Lily!" Ben says. They stand up and run to the man. They smile and wave. "Hello, mister!" Lily says. "Can you take a photo of us, please?" The man looks at them and smiles. He sees how happy they are. "Sure, kids!" he says. "You look like you had a lot of fun!" He holds up his camera and points it at them. "Say cheese!" he says. "Cheese!" Lily and Ben say. The man presses a button. The camera makes a click sound. He shows them the photo on a small screen. "Wow, look, Ben!" Lily says. "We look so good!" "Yes, we do, Lily!" Ben says. They thank the man and take the photo. They hold it in their hands and look at it. They see their bright eyes and big smiles. They see the hill and the sky behind them. They are happy. They have a photo of their fun day. They put the photo in their pocket and run back to the hill. They want to roll again.

Once upon a time, in a small kitchen, there was a box of pasta. The pasta was very naughty. It liked to jump out of the box and play all day. The pasta had a lot of friends in the kitchen, but its best friend was a red spoon. One day, the naughty pasta had a fun idea. It wanted to attach itself to the red spoon and go on a big adventure. So, the pasta and the spoon went around the kitchen, visiting all their friends. They saw the cups, the plates, and even the big pot. At the end of the day, the pasta and the spoon were very tired. They had so much fun on their adventure. The pasta decided to go back into the box and the spoon went back to its spot. They both knew they would have more fun the next day.

Once upon a time, Grandpa went to the forest to find blueberries. He was trying to find enough for his special recipe. Grandpa had to be tough and he could not give up. He counted each blueberry that he found. He counted and counted. Finally, Grandpa had enough blueberries to take home. He was so happy. On the way back, Grandpa gave some of his blueberries to the animals he saw. The birds, the rabbits, and even the deer ate the sweet blueberries and thanked Grandpa for his kindness. Grandpa smiled and waved. When Grandpa got home he hugged his grandkids and told them about the adventure he had gone on. Grandpa showed them the blueberries he had found. The kids laughed and clapped their hands together. Grandpa was so proud of himself for counting all the blueberries and being tough enough to make it home. He knew his special recipe was going to be delicious!

One day, a boy named Tim found a modest tube in his toy box. He did not know what it was. He asked his mom, "What is this tube, mom?" His mom said, "It is a magic tube, Tim. You can name anything you want, and it will come out of the tube." Tim was so happy. He named a big red ball, and it came out of the tube. He named a yummy cake, and it came out too. Tim played with the tube all day. He named more toys and treats, and they all came out of the tube. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim named a big green frog, and it came out of the tube. The frog was not like the other things Tim named. It could talk! The frog said, "Thank you for naming me, Tim. Now I am free from the magic tube. I will give you one wish." Tim thought hard and said, "I wish for more magic tubes!" The frog laughed and gave Tim more tubes. Tim and the frog played and had fun all day.
