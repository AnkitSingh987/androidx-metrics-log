Category: Intermediate
Title: Zip and the Park

One day, a big, wide cat named Zip went to the park. Zip loved to spin around and around. He would spin so fast that he would get dizzy. As Zip was spinning, a little girl named Mia came up to him. She asked, "Can I spin with you, Zip?" Zip said, "Yes, let's spin together!" So they both began to spin and spin, laughing and having fun. After they stopped spinning, Zip and Mia sat down on the soft grass. They looked up at the big, blue sky and smiled. They were happy to have made a new friend. And from that day on, Zip and Mia would always spin together in the park.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He put on his coat and zipped it up. Tim liked to be organized, so he made sure his toys were put away before he went outside. As he walked, he saw a big tree. Tim thought it would be fun to climb it. He zipped up his coat even more to stay warm. He carefully climbed the tree and sat on a branch, looking at the world around him. After a while, Tim climbed down and continued his walk. He saw a little dog and they played together. They ran and jumped, having lots of fun. Tim felt happy and tired, so he walked back home. He took off his coat and unzipped it. Tim was glad he had an organized and fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a big slide. All the kids loved to play on the slide. One day, a big truck came to the park. The truck had an incredible surprise inside. The truck driver opened the back of the truck. He started to unpack many big boxes. The kids watched him and were very excited. They could not wait to see what was inside the boxes. The driver finished unpacking the boxes. Inside, there was a new slide! The new slide was even bigger and more incredible than the old one. The kids were so happy. They played on the new slide all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little puppy named Max was playing in the park. He saw a big ball high up in a tree. Max wanted to get the ball down, but he didn't know how. Max saw a boy named Tim. Max asked Tim, "Can you help me get the ball?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you." They tried to jump and reach the ball, but it was too high. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's reverse and run fast to jump higher!" Max and Tim went back, then ran fast and jumped high together. They got the ball down and played happily in the park.

Once upon a time, there were two friends walking together in a park. One was very delicate and the other was very strong. They were having a lot of fun together, walking on the path, kicking leaves and laughing. Suddenly, the delicate friend stopped and started to cry. Surprised, the strong friend asked, "What is wrong?". The delicate friend replied, "I'm scared of getting hurt!" The strong friend hugged the delicate friend. "It's okay. I will always care for you and make sure no one ever hurts you". The delicate friend smiled, feeling safe and happy. They continued to walk on the path, hand in hand, and they knew that no matter what, they would always care for each other.

Lily and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. But sometimes they make too much noise and Mom tells them to be quiet. One day, they find a big book with many pictures and words. They want to read it, but they do not know all the words. So they ask Mom to help them. Mom comes to their room and sits on the bed. She opens the book and points to a word. She says the word and asks Lily and Ben to repeat it. Lily and Ben repeat the word after Mom. They do this for many words. They learn new words and have fun. They also try to say the words in a funny way, like a robot or a bird. They giggle and laugh. But then they hear a knock on the door. It is Dad. He says, "Shh, be quiet, please. I have a big headache. Can you read the book in a low voice?" Lily and Ben say, "Sorry, Dad. We will be quiet." They nod and smile. They close the book and hug Mom. They whisper, "Thank you, Mom, for reading with us. We love you." Mom whispers, "I love you too, my sweet twins. You are very smart and kind. Now, let's be quiet and let Dad rest." She kisses them and tucks them in. They close their eyes and dream of the big book and the new words.
