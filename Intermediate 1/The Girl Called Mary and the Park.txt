Once upon a time, there was a bird who loved to fly. He saw people playing a game called golf. The bird did not know what golf was. He was ignorant about the game. One day, the bird saw a girl playing golf. He flew down and asked, "What are you doing?" The girl said, "I'm playing golf." The bird wanted to try, but he had no hands to hold the golf stick. The girl had an idea. She tied the golf stick to the bird's back. The bird tried to fly and hit the golf ball. At first, it was hard, but after a while, the bird got good at it. The girl and the bird played golf together and had lots of fun. The bird was happy that he learned something new.

Sara and Ben went to the big water with Mom and Dad. They saw many fish and boats and birds. They liked the big water. Mom and Dad said they could go on a boat and see dolphins. Sara and Ben were very happy. They got on the boat and put on life vests. The boat man said he knew where the dolphins were. He drove the boat fast and made big waves. Sara and Ben laughed and held on tight. They looked for dolphins in the water. Soon they saw a fin. Then another. Then many. The dolphins were jumping and playing near the boat. Sara and Ben clapped and cheered. The boat man said they could let the dolphins come close and touch them. He gave them some fish to feed the dolphins. Sara and Ben leaned over the boat and held out the fish. The dolphins came and took the fish from their hands. They felt the dolphins' skin. It was smooth and wet. The dolphins made noises. They sounded happy. One dolphin was very fat. He had a big belly. The boat man said he was a daddy dolphin. He said the daddy dolphin had a baby dolphin inside his belly. He said the baby dolphin would come out soon. Sara and Ben were amazed. They wanted to see the baby dolphin. The fat dolphin came close to the boat. He looked at Sara and Ben. He opened his mouth and gave them a fish. Sara and Ben smiled and said thank you. They gave the fish back to the fat dolphin. They hoped he would share it with his baby dolphin. They thought the fat dolphin was very nice. They liked the dolphins. They had a great day at the big water.

One day, a big dog and a little cat were playing in the park. The dog was very careful not to step on the cat. The cat liked to chase the dog and the dog liked to chase the cat. They were very happy playing together. Suddenly, there was a big silence. The dog and the cat stopped playing and looked around. They saw a little bird on the ground. The bird could not fly. The dog and the cat wanted to help the bird. The dog said, "I can carry the bird." But the cat insisted, "No, I will carry the bird." They both wanted to be the one to help the little bird. Then, the little bird started to laugh. It was just pretending it couldn't fly! The dog and the cat were surprised, but they all laughed together and went back to playing in the park.
