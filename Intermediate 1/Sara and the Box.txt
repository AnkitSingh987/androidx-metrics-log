Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and the Box

Sara liked to go to the park with her mom. She liked to see the flowers, the swings, and the birds. Her favorite birds were the doves. They were white and soft and made a nice sound. One day, Sara saw a dove on the ground. It looked hurt. It had a broken wing and could not fly. Sara felt sad for the dove. She wanted to help it. She asked her mom if they could take it home. Her mom said yes, but they had to be very careful. They wrapped the dove in a towel and put it in a box. They took it to their car and drove home. Sara held the box on her lap. She talked to the dove and said nice things. At home, they put the box on the table. They gave the dove some water and some seeds. They called the vet and asked what to do. The vet said they had to weigh the dove and give it some medicine. He said they could use a kitchen scale to weigh the dove. Sara helped her mom weigh the dove. It was very light. They gave it some medicine with a dropper. They put the box in a warm and quiet place. They hoped the dove would get better soon. The next day, Sara checked on the dove. It looked more alert and happy. It ate some seeds and drank some water. It moved its wing a little bit. Sara was glad. She smiled and said, "You are a brave dove. I hope you can fly again soon." The dove cooed and looked at Sara with its bright eyes. Sara felt a warm feeling in her heart. She loved the dove.

One day, a big ugly rat came to play with a little boy named Tim. Tim did not like the ugly rat because it was big and scary. But the ugly rat wanted to show Tim that it was nice and fun. The ugly rat said, "I can show you something fun!" Tim was scared, but he wanted to see what the ugly rat could show him. So, they went outside to play. The ugly rat showed Tim how to play a fun game with a ball. Tim and the ugly rat played and laughed all day. In the end, Tim found out that the ugly rat was not scary at all. The ugly rat was just lonely and wanted a friend. Tim and the ugly rat became best friends, and they played together every day. The ugly rat was not so ugly after all, because it had a big, kind heart.

Once upon a time, there was a big river. The river was very pretty. The sun would shine on it and make it sparkle like a shiny toy. Many animals liked to play near the river. They liked to splash and swim. One day, a rare bird came to the river. The bird was very pretty too. It had many colors on its wings. The other animals liked the rare bird. They wanted to be friends with it. The rare bird and the other animals played by the river every day. They all had fun together. The sun would shine on them and the river would sparkle. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a pale cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with his ball. One day, Fluffy saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Fluffy started to climb the tree but he had a struggle. He tried and tried, but it was hard. Fluffy did not give up. He wanted to reach the top of the tree. He knew he could do it if he took his time. After a long time, Fluffy made it to the top of the tree. He was so happy! He looked down and saw his ball on the ground. Fluffy climbed back down the tree and played with his ball. He knew that if he tried hard and took his time, he could do anything.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog. The brown dog liked to play with a magazine. He would jump and run with the magazine in his mouth. The brown dog was happy. One day, the brown dog took the magazine to a big door. The door was open. He went inside with the magazine. The door closed and it was dark. The brown dog did not know how to go back. The brown dog was sad. He tried to open the door, but it was locked. He could not go out. The brown dog cried and cried. But nobody heard him. The brown dog stayed in the dark room with the locked door and the magazine.
