Category: Intermediate
Title: The Girl Called Amy and the Lake

Once upon a time there lived a little girl called Amy. She was three years old and had golden curls that bounced when she ran around. One day she asked her mum, "Mummy will we go for a walk today?". Her mum smiled and said, "Yes, I promise you, we'll go for a walk tomorrow, on Wednesday". On Wednesday morning, Amy could hardly contain her excitement and woke early. She and her mum got ready and set off. While they were walking, they passed by a park. Amy asked her mum, "Can we go to the park?". But her mum said, "No not today. I promised you a walk and that's what we're doing". Amy sighed and continued walking, a little bit grumpy. Suddenly, through the trees, she could see a lake glimmering in the sunlight. Amy asked her mum again, "Can we go to the lake?" her mum replied, "If you are reliable and if we have time, then yes". Amy brightened and smiled. She held her mum's hand tightly and they walked happily together. When they arrived at the lake, Amy looked up at her mum and said, “Mummy, I am reliable and I always keep my promises”. Her mum smiled and hugged her, “I know you doAmy, I know".

One day, a cool crane was by the river. The crane liked to catch fish. It would stand very still and watch for fish. When it saw a fish, it would snap its beak very fast to catch it. The cool crane had a friend named Duck. Duck liked to swim in the river. Duck and the cool crane would play together. They would race to see who was faster. Duck was fast in the water, but the cool crane was fast in the air. One day, the cool crane saw a big fish. It wanted to catch the fish for Duck and itself. The cool crane waited and waited. Then, it saw the big fish near the water. The cool crane snapped its beak and caught the fish. Duck and the cool crane shared the big fish and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. He loved to stand and watch his mom make lunch. One day, mom made a cheap lunch with bread and cheese. Tom was happy to eat it. Tom went to the park with his cheap lunch. He saw a big tree and thought, "I will eat my lunch under that tree." As he stood under the tree, he heard a voice say, "Hello, can I have some lunch too?" Tom looked up and saw a talking bird! The bird was very hungry. Tom shared his cheap lunch with the bird. They became good friends. From that day on, Tom and the bird always ate lunch together under the big tree. And they always shared their food, no matter how cheap or simple it was.

One day, a duck named Dilly arrived at a big pond. Dilly was a careless duck who did not look where he was going. He liked to play and have fun all day. The other ducks at the pond were nice and liked to play too. "Watch out, Dilly!" said a big duck named Bucky. "You need to look where you are going." Dilly did not listen and kept playing without a care. One day, Dilly was playing near the edge of the pond. He did not see a big hole in the ground. As he ran, he fell into the hole. "Help, help!" cried Dilly. The other ducks heard him and came to help. Bucky and the other ducks worked together to get Dilly out of the hole. "Thank you for saving me," said Dilly. "I will be more careful from now on." The other ducks were happy that Dilly was safe. They all played together, but this time Dilly looked where he was going. The moral of the story is to always be careful and look where you are going, so you can stay safe and have fun with your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red clock. The clock lived in a little boy's room. The little boy loved his clock so much. Every day, the boy would talk to the clock and say "please tell me the time." One day, the clock stopped working. The boy was very sad. He asked his mom for help. His mom said, "please be nice to your sister, and I will help you fix the clock." The boy did not like to play with his sister, but he wanted his clock to work again. The boy played with his sister, and they had a lot of fun. They laughed and played all day. Then, the mom fixed the red clock. The boy was very happy. He learned that being nice to others can make you happy too. And from that day on, the boy was always nice to his sister.
