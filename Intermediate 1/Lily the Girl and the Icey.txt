Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Icey

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to talk to her friends. One day, she met a weird new friend. This friend was not a person or an animal. It was a piece of ice! The ice could talk too! It said, "Hi, I am Icey. I am not like other ice. I can talk and play." Lily was very happy. She played with Icey all day long. They talked about fun things and had a great time. But then, the sun came out. Icey started to feel hot. Lily knew that ice melts in the sun. She didn't want to lose her new friend. So, she took Icey inside her house to keep it cool. Lily and Icey played inside and talked every day. They were very happy together.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big truck with many things in it. Tim wanted to have fun, so he went to the truck. He saw a man load more things into the truck. Tim was very excited. Tim asked the man if he could help. The man said, "Be careful, little boy." Tim tried to be careful, but the things were too heavy for him. He wanted to have fun, so he kept trying to load the truck. Tim tried to load a big box, but it was too heavy. The box fell and broke. The man was not happy. Tim was sad because he wanted to have fun. But now, the man was angry, and Tim had to go home.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet town. In this town, there was a big bell. Every day, the bell would ring to tell the people when it was time to eat. One day, the people were ready to eat. They waited for the bell to ring, but it did not. They looked up and saw a big bird sitting on the bell. The bird was too heavy, so the bell could not ring. The people did not know what to do. But then, a little girl had an idea. She gave the bird some food. The bird ate the food and flew away. The bell rang, and everyone was happy. They all sat down to eat and thanked the little girl for her help.

One day, a boy named Tom went for a walk in his country. The sky was gloomy, and big gray clouds were in the sky. Tom saw a big tree with yummy red apples. He wanted to grab one to eat. Tom said, "I want to grab that apple!" He jumped high to grab the apple. But he could not reach it. He was sad. Then, a friendly bird flew down from the tree. The bird said, "I can help you grab the apple!" Tom was very happy. The bird flew up and grabbed the apple for Tom. But something unexpected happened. The apple turned into a shiny gold coin! Tom was so surprised! He thanked the bird and took the gold coin home. From that day on, Tom and the bird were the best of friends.

One day, a colorful bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a hedge with many red berries. The bird was very hungry, so she flew down to eat the berries. The bird met a small mouse near the hedge. The mouse said, "Hello, bird! Can I offer you some cheese?" The bird said, "No, thank you. I want to eat the berries on the hedge." The bird started to eat the berries. Suddenly, the berries turned into shiny stones! The bird was surprised. The mouse said, "I told you! The berries are magic. They turn into stones when you eat them." The bird was sad because she was still hungry. The mouse offered the cheese again. This time, the bird said, "Yes, please!" The bird and the mouse became friends and shared the cheese. They were happy and full.

Once there was a lonely bowl. It sat on a shelf with no other bowls to talk to. One day, a girl named Lucy saw the lonely bowl. She took it off the shelf and said, "Why are you so lonely, bowl?" The bowl did not answer, but Lucy knew it was sad. Lucy had an idea. She would perform a show for the lonely bowl to make it happy. She danced and sang, and the bowl seemed to smile. Just then, something unexpected happened. The bowl started to talk! "Thank you, Lucy, for making me happy," said the bowl. From that day on, the bowl was never lonely again. Lucy and the bowl became best friends. They played and talked every day. And sometimes, Lucy would perform a new show just for the bowl.
