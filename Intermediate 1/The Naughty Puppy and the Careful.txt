Category: Intermediate
Title: The Naughty Puppy and the Careful

Once upon a time there was a naughty puppy. He liked to run around and have lots of fun, but he never listened. One day he was running so fast he didn't see that a big sneeze was coming. He sneezed so hard it made him fall over! He was so embarrassed. From then on, the naughty puppy learned to be careful and listen. Whenever he had a sneezy feeling, he would remember the big sneeze that embarrassed him and he would stop and be careful. The puppy taught an important lesson: it's important to be careful, even when you're having fun. If you don't listen, you can get into trouble – like the naughty puppy did!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a farm with his mom. The farm was incredible! There were big cows, little chicks, and funny pigs. Tim loved to watch them play and eat. While walking around the farm, Tim saw a big red barn. He wanted to go inside, but his mom said, "No, Tim. Stay with me." Tim felt sad but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he stayed with her and they continued to look at the animals. Later, Tim saw the big red barn again. He wanted to go inside so much, but he remembered what his mom said. He thought about it and decided to repeat her words, "Stay with me." Tim knew it was important to listen to his mom. They had a great day at the incredible farm, and Tim learned that listening to his mom was the best thing to do.

Once upon a time, in a big valley, there lived a curious little bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play all day long. He enjoyed exploring the valley, finding new places to play with his friends. One day, Ben saw a big hill he had never seen before. He was curious and wanted to see what was on the other side. Ben hopped up the hill, higher and higher, until he reached the top. When he looked down, he saw a beautiful lake with lots of fish swimming in it. Ben was very happy to find the lake, but he also knew he should go back home. He remembered his mommy always said, "Be safe and stay close to home." Ben realized the importance of listening to his mommy and staying safe. So, he hopped back down the hill and returned to the valley. From that day on, Ben always stayed close to home and enjoyed playing with his friends in the valley. And the moral of the story is to always listen to your parents and stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a plastic toy named Mr. Bunny. Mia loved to play with Mr. Bunny every day. One day, Mia and Mr. Bunny went to the park. They saw a big boy named Tom. Tom was not kind. He wanted to scare Mia and Mr. Bunny. Tom said, "Boo! I am going to take Mr. Bunny away from you!" Mia was scared and sad. Mia said, "Please, Tom, do not take Mr. Bunny. We just want to play." Tom saw that Mia was scared and felt bad. He said, "I am sorry, Mia. I will not take Mr. Bunny. Let's all play together." Mia, Mr. Bunny, and Tom played at the park and had lots of fun.

Tim and Kim are twins. They like to play with wood and tools. They have a big box of wood and a small box of nails. They want to make a house for their toys. They find a big piece of wood and put it on the ground. They find four small pieces of wood and nail them to the big piece. They make a square. They find another big piece of wood and nail it on top of the square. They make a roof. They are happy with their house. They put their toys inside. They have a bear, a doll, a car and a ball. They pretend the toys are a family. They make funny voices for them. But then the wind blows hard. The roof falls off the house. The toys fall out. Tim and Kim are sad. They cry. Dad comes and sees them. He hugs them and says, "Don't cry. I will help you." He picks up the roof and the nails. He says, "We need to support the roof. We need to nail it better. We need to use more nails and more wood." He shows them how to nail the roof to the square. He adds more wood to make the house stronger. He says, "Now the wind can't blow it away. Now your toys are safe." Tim and Kim smile. They thank Dad. They hug him. They play with their toys again. They make funny voices for them. They have fun. They are happy.
