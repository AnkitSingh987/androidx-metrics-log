Category: Intermediate
Title: A Wonderful Bird with Tim and Window

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy blocks. He was building a big stack. He wanted to make it very tall. Tim was a bit scared that the stack would fall. Tim's friend, Sue, came to play with him. Sue saw the stack and said, "Wow, that's a big stack! But it looks like it might fall. I recommend putting more blocks at the bottom." Tim thought that was a good idea. They added more blocks to make the stack strong. As they were playing, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird saw the stack and thought it was a tree! It landed on the top of the stack. Tim and Sue were surprised. They laughed and said, "Silly bird, that's not a tree!" The bird flew away, and they continued to play with their strong stack.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to eat delicious food. One day, Spot's mom set a big plate of yummy treats on the table. Spot was very happy. Spot saw a white napkin next to the plate. He thought the napkin was a treat too. So, he ate the napkin! His mom saw this and said, "Oh no, Spot! That's not a treat!" Then, something strange happened. Spot started to feel funny. He turned into a big, colorful bird! Now, Spot could fly high in the sky. He loved his new wings. Spot's mom was surprised but happy to see him having fun. And from that day on, they always made sure to keep napkins away from Spot.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to explore. One day, they went to the park with their mom and dad. They saw a big hill with many trees and flowers. They wanted to climb the hill and see the landscape. "Can we go up there, mom?" Ben asked. "Sure, but be careful and stay close to us," mom said. Ben and Lily ran up the hill, holding hands. They felt the wind in their hair and the sun on their faces. They saw birds and butterflies and bugs. They reached the top of the hill and looked around. They saw the park, the road, the houses, and the sky. They saw a big lake that sparkled like a diamond. They saw an old bridge that crossed the lake. "Wow, look at that!" Lily said, pointing to the bridge. "It's so old and pretty," Ben said. They raised their arms and shouted, "We did it! We climbed the hill!" Mom and dad smiled and clapped. They joined Ben and Lily and hugged them. "You did great, kids," dad said. "You saw a beautiful landscape." Ben and Lily nodded. They felt happy and proud. They liked to explore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to take pictures with her camera. One day, Mia's mom said they were going to the park. Mia was very happy! Mia was impatient to go to the park. She wanted to bring her camera to take pictures of the pretty flowers and the big trees. She put her camera in her bag and waited for her mom. When her mom was ready, they went to the park together. At the park, Mia took her camera out of her bag. She took pictures of the flowers, the trees, and the birds. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Mia!" They had a fun day at the park with the camera.

Once upon a time, there was a modest octopus. He liked to explore different underwater places. One day, he swam to a large iceberg in the ocean. He touched it and it started to freeze. The octopus was startled and shouted, “Help! I'm stuck!” But no one heard him. He was all alone. Just then, a friendly dolphin swam by. “What’s wrong?” asked the dolphin. The octopus explained his problem. The dolphin laughed, “I have a secret to set you free. You must sing a special song!” So the octopus took a deep breath and sang. Suddenly, the ice melted away and the octopus was free. He thanked the dolphin and swam off happily.
