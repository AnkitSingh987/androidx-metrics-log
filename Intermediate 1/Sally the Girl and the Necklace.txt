Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Necklace

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park with her mom. Sally saw an enormous tree with many birds. She wanted to feed the birds, so she asked her mom, "Can I feed the birds, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can, but be gentle." Sally took some bread from her mom and started to feed the birds. The birds were very happy and came closer to her. As Sally was feeding the birds, she found a shiny piece of jewelry on the ground. It was a pretty necklace. She showed it to her mom and said, "Look, I found a necklace!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's beautiful, Sally. You can wear it if you want." Sally wore the necklace and continued to feed the birds. She felt very happy and special that day. The birds were happy too, and they sang a sweet song for Sally.

Lila and Tom were twins who loved to play outside. They liked to run, jump, sing and explore. One day, they found a stream in the woods. It was clear and cool, and it made a nice sound. "Look, Lila, there are flowers by the stream!" Tom said. He pointed to some purple and yellow blooms. "They are so pretty!" "Yes, they are, Tom. And they smell good, too!" Lila said. She picked one and put it in her hair. "Do you think they are a gift from the stream?" "Maybe, Lila. Maybe the stream is gifted and can make flowers grow." Tom said. He picked one and gave it to Lila. "Here, this is for you. You are gifted, too. You can sing and draw and make me laugh." "Thank you, Tom. You are gifted, too. You can run fast and climb trees and tell stories." Lila said. She hugged Tom and gave him a kiss. "You are the best brother ever." They smiled and played by the stream, enjoying the flowers and each other. They felt happy and loved. They did not know that their mom was watching them from behind a tree, feeling proud and grateful. She thought they were the most gifted twins in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, it was very wet because it rained. The sky was dull and gray. Tim put on his boots and went outside to play in the wet puddles. Tim saw a big puddle and jumped in it. Splash! The water went all over. He laughed and jumped in more wet puddles. Tim's boots were wet, but he did not care. He was having so much fun in the rain. Soon, the rain stopped and Tim was tired. He went back inside to finish his snack. His mom gave him a towel to dry his wet boots. Tim was happy he played outside, even if it was wet and dull.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a pretty forest. A small, soft bunny lived under the tree. The bunny had a velvet blanket to keep warm at night. One day, the bunny met a big bear. The bear looked sad. The bunny asked, "Why are you sad, big bear?" The bear let out a big sigh and said, "I am cold, and I don't have a blanket." The bunny thought for a moment and said, "You can share my velvet blanket with me, big bear." The bear smiled and said, "Thank you, little bunny." From that day on, the bunny and the bear became best friends. They shared the velvet blanket and played together in the pretty forest. And they both were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lucky little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a toy scale to weigh things. She loved to weigh all her toys and see how heavy they were. She would say, "Wow, my teddy bear weighs more than my toy car!" One day, Lucy went to the store with her mom. They saw many things with different prices. Lucy saw a big, shiny apple. She asked her mom, "Can I buy this apple?" Her mom said, "Yes, but first, let's weigh it and see the price." Lucy put the apple on the scale. The scale showed the weight and the price. Her mom said, "Good job, Lucy! Now we know how much the apple costs." Lucy felt very lucky to have her toy scale. She smiled and said, "I love my scale, and I can't wait to weigh more things at home!"
