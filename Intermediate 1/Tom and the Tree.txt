Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Tree

One sunny day, Tom and his mom went on a picnic. They took a big red ball to play with. They also took some yummy food to eat. They found a nice spot under a big tree to sit and enjoy their picnic. Tom's mom said, "Tom, don't ignore the ants. They want to eat our food too." Tom looked at the ants and said, "No, ants! You can't have our food." But the ants did not listen. They kept coming closer to the food. Tom had an idea. He took the big red ball and rolled it on the ground. The ants saw the ball and thought it was fun. They began to follow the ball, leaving the picnic alone. Tom and his mom laughed and enjoyed their picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends and eat toast. One day, Kitty saw her friend, Bunny, looking sad. Kitty asked Bunny, "Why are you sad?" Bunny said, "My toast disappeared! I don't know where it went." Kitty wanted to help her friend. So, Kitty and Bunny looked everywhere for the missing toast. They looked in the kitchen, under the bed, and even in the garden. Finally, they found the toast behind a big tree. Bunny was so happy to have her toast back. She shared it with Kitty, and they both had a yummy snack. From that day on, they always looked out for each other and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play with jewelry. One day, he found a shiny necklace in the park. He was very happy and played with it all day long. The next day, Tom met a little dog named Max. Max wanted to play with the necklace too. They decided to fight for it. They chased each other and tried to grab the necklace from each other's mouth. Suddenly, a big wind came and took the necklace away. Tom and Max stopped fighting and watched as the wind carried the necklace far away. They became friends and learned that it was better to share and play together than to fight over things.

One day, a thin cat named Tim went for a walk. He liked to march with his tail up high. Tim marched all around the big tree and then to the small pond. He saw his friends, the ducks, swimming in the water. They were happy and splashing around. Tim had a gift for his best friend, Sam the dog. The gift was a big, red ball. Tim marched to Sam's house with the ball in his mouth. He was very excited to give Sam the gift and play together. When Tim got to Sam's house, he gave the red ball to Sam. Sam was very happy and wagged his tail. They played with the ball all day, running and jumping in the sun. Then, they lay down on the soft grass, tired and happy. Tim and Sam were very good friends.

One day, a cat and a dog went to play outside. It was a cold day, and the ground was starting to freeze. They saw a big shield near the trees. The cat said, "Look, a shield! Let's use it to slide on the ice!" The dog agreed, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So they took the shield and went to the frozen pond. They took turns sliding on the ice with the shield. The cat went first and slid very fast. The dog was excited and said, "Wow, this shield is very useful!" After playing for a while, they started to feel cold. They decided to go back home and tell their friends about the fun they had with the shield. They knew that the shield was not only useful, but also a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a dog. The dog liked to move a lot. He lived in a modern house with his family. The dog was very happy. One day, the dog went to play outside. He saw a big box. The dog was curious. He moved closer to the box. Then, something unexpected happened. The box started to move too! The dog was surprised. He moved back and barked at the box. The box stopped moving. The dog moved closer again, and the box moved too! The dog was scared, but he wanted to know what was inside the box. So, he opened the box. Inside the box was a little robot dog! The robot dog could move and bark like a real dog. The dog was not scared anymore. He was happy to have a new robot friend to play with. They moved and played together all day long.
