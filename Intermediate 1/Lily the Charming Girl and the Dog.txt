Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Charming Girl and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a charming little girl named Lily. She loved to skip and play with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends were playing in a big room with many doors. They were having so much fun! But then, they heard a loud noise. They all stopped and looked around. They saw a big dog barking at them. The dog was not happy. Lily and her friends were scared. They wanted to find the exit to get away from the dog. Lily said, "Let's skip to the exit!" Her friends nodded and they all started skipping together. They found the exit and went outside. The dog couldn't follow them. They were safe! Lily and her friends were so happy they could skip away from the dog. They played outside and had even more fun. The charming little girl and her friends learned that when they work together, they can solve any problem.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play outside. One day, they see a big orange cloud in the sky. It looks like a giant carrot. "Wow, look at that cloud!" Lily says. "It is so orange and funny!" "Maybe it is a magic cloud," Ben says. "Maybe it can make wishes come true." Lily and Ben run to the cloud. They want to touch it and talk to it. They hope it will be nice to them. "Hello, orange cloud!" Lily says. "We are Lily and Ben. We like you. Do you like us?" The orange cloud does not say anything. It just floats in the sky. Lily and Ben feel sad. They want the cloud to be their friend. "Please, orange cloud, say something!" Ben says. "We want to play with you. We want to invite you to our house. We have cookies and toys and books. You will like them." The orange cloud still does not say anything. It just moves away from Lily and Ben. It goes to another part of the sky. Lily and Ben feel very sad. They do not understand why the cloud does not want to be their friend. "Maybe the cloud is shy," Lily says. "Maybe it does not know how to talk. Maybe it is lonely." "Maybe we can try again tomorrow," Ben says. "Maybe the cloud will be happier then. Maybe it will say hello to us." Lily and Ben hug each other. They are still friends. They go back to their house. They hope the orange cloud will come back. They hope it will be their friend too.

One day a big storm came. It was very scary and had lots of lightning. A little girl named Sarah wanted to pick the lightning. She thought it would be fun. So Sarah put on her raincoat and boots and went outside. But when she got close to the lightning, it was too big and too bright. The lightning scared Sarah and she tried to run away. But it was too late. In a big flash, the lightning picked Sarah up and took her away. No one ever saw or heard from Sarah again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Every day, Amy's mom would make a fine cake with cream on top. Amy loved the cake very much. One day, Amy's mom was making the cake. She asked Amy, "Do you want to help me make the cake?" Amy was very happy and said, "Yes, mom!" So, Amy helped her mom mix the cake mix and watched it rise in the oven. When the cake was done, they put cream on top. Amy's mom said, "You did a great job helping me, Amy!" They shared the cake with Dad, and they all enjoyed it together.

Once upon a time, there was a little train. The train loved to run fast on the tracks. Every day, it would go through the town and make the people happy. One sunny day, the train was running very fast. It was working hard to go faster and faster. Then, something unexpected happened. The train saw a big tree on the tracks. It was too late to stop! But then, a little bird came and pushed the tree off the tracks. The train was so happy and thanked the bird. The bird smiled and flew away. From that day on, the train and the bird became best friends. The train learned that even the smallest friends can make a big difference.
