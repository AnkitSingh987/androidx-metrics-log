Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Curious Cat and the Cup

One day, a curious cat named Tom saw a cup. The cup was on a table. Tom wanted to play with the cup. He jumped on the table and started to spin the cup with his paw. The cup went round and round. It was fun to watch. Then, a little mouse named Jerry came out from a hole in the wall. Jerry saw the spinning cup and wanted to play too. He climbed up the table leg and joined Tom. Together, they spun the cup faster and faster. But, as the cup spun, something unexpected happened. The cup started to shake and wiggle. Suddenly, a tiny door opened on the side of the cup. Out came a little toy clown! The clown danced and laughed. Tom and Jerry were very surprised. They had never seen a cup like this before. They played with the clown and the cup all day long, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty liked to climb trees and look at the view. One day, Kitty climbed a very tall tree. She saw a big, blue sky and green grass below. Kitty was happy. Kitty saw a little bird in the tree. The bird was weeping. Kitty asked, "Why do you weep, little bird?" The bird said, "I lost my mommy. I am sad." Kitty wanted to help the little bird. Kitty had an idea. She told the bird, "Climb on my back. We will find your mommy." The little bird climbed on Kitty's back. They looked at the view together. They saw the bird's mommy in another tree. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, clever Kitty!" They became good friends.

One day, a big cat named Tom and a little mouse named Jerry were playing in the park. They were running and jumping with joy. Tom was very strong and had lots of power, but he was also very guilty because he always chased Jerry. As they played, Tom saw a big tree and said, "Let's climb the tree!" Jerry agreed, and they started to climb. They climbed higher and higher, and Jerry was scared. "Tom, I am scared. I want to go down," said Jerry. "Okay, let's go down," Tom replied. But as they were climbing down, Tom slipped and began to fall. Jerry was scared and shouted, "Tom, be careful!" Tom hit the ground, and Jerry quickly climbed down to help him. Tom felt guilty for making Jerry scared. He said, "I am sorry, Jerry. I should have been more careful." Jerry smiled and said, "It's okay, Tom. We can still have fun on the ground." So, they continued to play and had a great time together.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They had a big box of paper and scissors. They liked to cut the paper into different shapes and colors. "Look, I cut a star!" Lily said, holding up a yellow paper star. "That's nice, Lily. I cut a heart!" Ben said, showing a red paper heart. They cut more shapes and put them in a pile. Then they had an idea. "Let's play search!" Lily said. "We can hide the shapes and find them." "OK, that sounds fun!" Ben said. "You hide first, and I'll count to ten." Lily took some shapes and ran to hide them. She put a star under a flower pot, a heart behind a tree, and a circle in the birdhouse. Then she came back and said, "Ready!" Ben opened his eyes and started to search. He looked around the garden and saw the shapes. He found the star, the heart, and the circle. He was happy. "I found them all, Lily!" Ben said. "You are good at hiding. Now it's my turn to hide." Ben took some shapes and hid them. He put a square under a rock, a triangle in the mailbox, and a diamond in the grass. Then he came back and said, "Ready!" Lily opened her eyes and started to search. She looked around the garden and saw the shapes. She found the square, the triangle, and the diamond. She was happy. "I found them all, Ben!" Lily said. "You are good at hiding, too. This is a fun game." They hugged and smiled. They were good friends. They decided to share their shapes and make a big picture. They were generous. They had a lot of fun.
