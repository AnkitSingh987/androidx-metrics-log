Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big field of wheat near his house. The wheat was tall and golden. Tom wanted to play in the wheat field. Tom put on his shoes and ran to the wheat field. But when he got there, he saw that the ground was wet. It had rained the day before. Tom did not want to get his shoes wet, but he still wanted to play. Tom thought for a moment, and then he took off his shoes. He played in the wet wheat field with his bare feet. He ran and jumped and laughed. Tom had so much fun playing in the wet wheat field. When it was time to go home, he put his shoes back on and went back to his house. Tom could not wait to play in the wheat field again.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Max. Max loved to play with his favorite toy, a piece of cardboard. One sunny day, Max took his cardboard to the park to play. At the park, Max saw a big seat. He thought it was the perfect place to play with his cardboard. Max jumped on the seat and started to play. He slid the cardboard back and forth, making fun sounds. Soon, other friendly animals came to see Max play. They all wanted to join in the fun. Max shared his cardboard with them, and they all took turns sliding on the big seat. They played all day and became good friends.

Ben and Mia went to the toy shop with Mom. They saw many toys that they liked. They wanted to buy a big truck and a doll house. But Mom said they were too much money. She said they had to look for toys with a low price. Ben and Mia looked around the shop. They saw a small car and a teddy bear. They had a low price. Ben and Mia liked them. They showed them to Mom. Mom smiled and said they were great toys. She said they could buy them. Ben and Mia were happy. They took the car and the teddy bear to the counter. The shop lady was nice. She said hello and welcome. She put the toys in a bag. She gave Mom the change. She said thank you and have a nice day. Ben and Mia said thank you and goodbye. They left the shop with Mom. They held their new toys. They were excited to play with them at home. They had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange frog named Hoppy. Hoppy loved to hop all day long. He would hop from one big rock to another, having so much fun. One day, while Hoppy was hopping around, he found a menu on the ground. The menu had pictures of yummy food. Hoppy thought the food looked very good. Hoppy decided to take the menu to his mom. He hopped all the way home, holding the menu in his mouth. When he showed the menu to his mom, she was so happy. They looked at the menu together and picked something yummy to eat. From that day on, Hoppy and his mom always had fun looking at the menu and trying new foods.

Anna was a spoiled girl who always wanted more toys. She had a big room full of dolls, cars, books and games, but she was never happy. She always frownd when her mommy and daddy said no to her. One day, Anna saw a new toy on TV. It was a shiny rocket that could fly in the air and make noises. Anna wanted it very much. She ran to her mommy and said, "Mommy, mommy, I want the rocket! Please buy it for me!" Mommy shook her head and said, "No, Anna, you have enough toys already. You don't need the rocket. It is too expensive and too dangerous. Why don't you play with what you have?" Anna was very angry. She threw a tantrum and said, "I hate you, mommy! You are mean and bad! I want the rocket! I want the rocket!" Mommy was very sad. She said, "Anna, you are being very rude and ungrateful. You should be thankful for what you have. There are many children in the world who have no toys at all. They have no food, no water, no home. They live in poor lands where there is war and sickness. They would be happy with just one doll or one car. You should think about them and be kind and generous." Anna did not listen. She kept screaming and crying. She ran to her room and slammed the door. She did not care about other children. She only cared about herself. The next day, Anna woke up and saw a big box on her bed. She thought it was the rocket. She was very excited. She opened the box and saw a note from mommy. It said, "Dear Anna, I have a surprise for you. I have donated all your toys to a charity that helps children in need. They will send them to a faraway land where they will make many children happy. I hope you will learn to share and appreciate what you have. Love, mommy." Anna was shocked. She looked inside the box and saw nothing. All her toys were gone. She had nothing left. She felt a big pain in her heart. She realized how selfish and spoiled she had been. She wished she could have her toys back. She wished she could say sorry to mommy. She wished she could be a good girl. She ran to mommy and hugged her. She said, "Mommy, mommy, I'm sorry. I was wrong. I was spoiled. I don't need the rocket. I don't need any toys. I only need you. Please forgive me. Please love me." Mommy smiled and kissed her. She said, "I forgive you, Anna. I love you. You are my precious girl. I'm proud of you. You have learned a valuable lesson. You have learned to be thankful and compassionate. You have learned to be happy with what you have." Anna smiled and said, "Thank you, mommy. I love you too. You are the best mommy in the world. You have given me the best gift ever. You have given me your love."
