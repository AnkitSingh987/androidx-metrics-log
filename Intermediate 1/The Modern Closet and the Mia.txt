Category: Intermediate
Title: The Modern Closet and the Mia

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a modern closet. The closet was very big and had many colorful clothes inside. It was in a little girl's room. The little girl's name was Mia. One day, Mia heard the closet talk. It said, "Hello, Mia! I am your friend, Mr. Closet. I can talk and play with you." Mia was so happy to have a talking closet. They became good friends. Mia and Mr. Closet played games and talked every day. They had lots of fun together. Mia's friends came over, and they all played with the talking closet. Everyone had a great time, and they all loved the modern closet.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern farm. On the farm, there were many pumpkins. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm. He wanted to find the best pumpkin. Tim saw a very big pumpkin. He thought it would be the best one. Tim tried to open the pumpkin. When he opened it, he saw something inside. It was a small, black cat. The cat was not happy. It was mad that Tim opened the pumpkin. The cat jumped out of the pumpkin and ran away. Tim was sad. He knew he should not have opened the pumpkin. The cat was gone, and now Tim had a bad day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to bring things to her friends. One day, Lily found a shiny crystal. It was very pretty and she wanted to show it to her best friend, Tim. Lily went to Tim's house and gave him the crystal. Tim thought it was so cool! They decided to go on an adventure to find more crystals. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with lots of fruit. The fruit looked yummy, so they picked some to eat. But when they tasted it, it was sour! They made funny faces and laughed. Even though the fruit was sour, they had a great time on their adventure. And they always remembered the day they found the shiny crystal.

Lily loved parades. She liked to see the big balloons, the pretty costumes, and the loud music. She always wanted to go to the parade with her mom and dad, but they said she was too little. They said she had to wait until she was older. One day, Lily saw a poster on the wall. It said there was a parade in the park today. She was so excited. She ran to her mom and dad and pointed at the poster. "Look, look, parade!" she said. "Can we go, please?" Her mom and dad looked at each other. They knew Lily wanted to see the parade, but they also had a lot of work to do. They didn't have time to take her to the park. They felt bad for Lily, but they said no. "We're sorry, Lily, but we can't go today. Maybe next time, okay?" Lily was very sad. She cried and cried. She didn't understand why they couldn't go. She thought they didn't love her. She ran to her room and slammed the door. Her mom and dad felt sorry for Lily. They wished they could make her happy. They thought of a way to surprise her. They called their friend, who was a clown. They asked him if he could come to their house and bring some balloons and toys. He said yes. He said he was on his way. Lily heard a knock on the door. She didn't want to open it. She was still angry and sad. She heard her mom and dad say, "Lily, come here. We have a surprise for you." Lily was curious. She opened the door and saw a clown. He had a big red nose, a funny hat, and a charming smile. He had a bunch of balloons and toys in his hands. He said, "Hi, Lily. I'm here to make you laugh. Do you want to play with me?" Lily was amazed. She couldn't believe her eyes. She forgot about the parade. She smiled and said, "Yes, yes, yes!" She ran to the clown and hugged him. He gave her a balloon and a toy. He made her laugh with his jokes and tricks. He was very nice and funny. Lily's mom and dad watched from the window. They were happy to see Lily happy. They said, "We love you, Lily. We're sorry we couldn't go to the parade, but we hope you like this surprise." Lily heard them and said, "I love you, too. Thank you for the surprise. It's better than the parade."
