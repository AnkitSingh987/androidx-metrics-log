Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wise Owl and the Rock

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a tree near a big rock. One day, he met a small triangle. The triangle was lost and didn't know how to get home. "Hello, wise owl," said the triangle. "Can you help me find my way home?" The wise owl looked at the triangle and said, "Of course, little triangle. I will help you. Follow me." They walked and walked, but the wise owl didn't know where the triangle's home was. They saw a big rock and the wise owl had an idea. "Let's sit on this rock and think," he said. As they sat on the rock, a big wind came and blew them away. The wise owl and the triangle were lost forever. They never found the triangle's home and they never saw their friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bull. The bull lived on a farm with other animals. They all played together and had fun. One day, the bull noticed a small mouse. The mouse was scared of the big bull. The bull wanted to be friends with the mouse, but he did not know how. The bull tried to be nice to the mouse. He gave the mouse some food and played with it. Soon, the mouse was not scared of the bull anymore. They became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a furry bear. He wanted to win a race so he asked a bunny to race with him. The bunny said yes, and they agreed the exit of the forest would be the finish line. On the day of the race, the furry bear was very excited. He was ready to try and win. The bunny hopped along and was much faster than the bear. The bear was getting tired and the bunny was almost at the exit. Suddenly, the bear had an idea. He hugged the bunny and snuggled him tight. The bunny could not hop away. He could not reach the exit! So, the bear won the race. The furry bear was very happy he won. He laughed and smiled and hugged the bunny once more for being a good race partner. From then on, the two were friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an old pan that he loved to play with. He would take the pan and pretend to cook food for his toys. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the old pan and wanted to play with it too. Tim did not want to share his pan. They both wanted to take the pan and play with it. Tim and Sam were sad. They did not know what to do. Then, Tim's mom came to help. She said, "Why don't you both take turns playing with the old pan?" Tim and Sam thought it was a good idea. Tim let Sam play with the pan first. Then, it was Tim's turn to play with the pan. They both had fun and were happy. They learned to share and play together. And they both loved the old pan.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She was happy because she was going to marry Tom, a nice boy. They loved each other and wanted to be together forever. Lily's mom told her, "You will marry in the big chamber, where everyone can see your love." On the day of the wedding, Lily and Tom were in the chamber with their family and friends. They were so happy that they could not stop smiling. Lily's dad said, "This is a very special day. We are all here to see you marry and be happy together." But then, a big storm came. The wind blew very hard, and the rain fell down like tears. The storm was so strong that it broke the chamber. Everyone was sad and scared. Lily and Tom could not marry that day. The storm left the chamber broken, and it could not be fixed. Lily and Tom were very sad, and they could not marry. Their happy day turned into a sad day, and they never got to be together like they wanted.
