Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Leaves

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a rake. Tim wanted to play with the rake, but his mom said, "No, we have to leave soon." Tim was sad, but he listened to his mom. At the park, it was very noisy. There were many kids playing and laughing. Tim saw a big pile of leaves. He wanted to jump in the leaves, but he didn't have the rake. Then, something unexpected happened. A dog ran up to Tim with the rake in its mouth. The dog dropped the rake at Tim's feet. Tim was very happy. He used the rake to make the pile of leaves bigger. Then, he jumped in the leaves with the dog. They played together until it was time to leave.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma had a bathrobe that she loved very much. It was soft, warm, and a pretty pink color. She wore her bathrobe every day and did not want to wear anything else. Emma was very stubborn. One day, Emma went for a walk outside. She saw a big tree and started to wonder what was up there. She thought she saw something shiny and wanted to find out what it was. So, Emma tried to climb the tree, but it was not easy in her bathrobe. Emma's mommy saw her and said, "Emma, you need to wear your clothes to climb the tree. Your bathrobe is not safe for that." But stubborn Emma did not want to change. She tried and tried, but she could not climb the tree in her bathrobe. Finally, Emma listened to her mommy and changed her clothes. Then, she climbed the tree and found a shiny, beautiful bird up there. Emma was happy, and she learned that sometimes it is good to listen to mommy.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. The tree was a home for many birds. The birds liked to live in the tall tree because it was safe and had lots of room. One day, a new bird came to the tree. The new bird was small and shy. The other birds saw the new bird and said, "Hi! Do you want to share our home with us?" The new bird was very happy and said, "Yes, I would love to share your home!" So, the birds helped the new bird make a nest in the tall tree. They shared their food and played together. As the days passed, the birds became good friends. They learned that sharing their home made them all happy. They sang songs and played games in the tall tree. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a generous boy named Tim. He had a small, red kayak. Tim loved to share his toys with his friends. One day, he took his kayak to the river with his best friend, Sam. They wanted to play and have fun in the water. Tim and Sam took turns using the kayak. They paddled and splashed in the river. They laughed and played all day. But then, they saw a big, scary fish. The fish wanted to take the kayak. Tim and Sam did not want to give it up. The big, scary fish told Tim and Sam to surrender the kayak. Tim and Sam were scared. They did not want to lose their kayak. But they had to listen to the big, scary fish. They gave the kayak to the fish. The fish swam away with the kayak, and Tim and Sam were very sad. They went home without their kayak and did not play in the river again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play and tease his friends. He would run around and make them dizzy. They would laugh and play all day. One day, Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, came over to play. "Let's play!" said Tweet. Spot and Tweet ran around and around until they both felt dizzy. They laughed and sat down to rest. After playing, they were very hungry. Spot's mom gave them some food to eat. They ate the yummy food and felt much better. Then, Spot and Tweet played some more until it was time for Tweet to go home. They said goodbye and promised to play together again soon.
