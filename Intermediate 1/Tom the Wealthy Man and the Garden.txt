Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Wealthy Man and the Garden

One day, a wealthy man named Tom lived in a big house. He had a big garden with lots of plants. In his garden, there were many red tomatoes. Tom loved to eat tomatoes. One day, a little girl named Sue came to Tom's house. She said, "I need a tomato for my mom. Can you please give me one?" Tom said, "Yes, you can have one." Sue was very happy and took the tomato to her mom. But when Sue got home, the tomato turned into a big, funny toy. It was not a real tomato! Sue was surprised but she played with the toy and had lots of fun. Tom didn't know that his tomatoes could turn into toys, but he was happy that Sue was happy.

Sara and Tom were playing in their room. They liked to pretend they were animals. Sara was a cat and Tom was a dog. They chased each other and made noises. "Meow, meow, I am a cat. You can't catch me, dog!" Sara said. "Woof, woof, I am a dog. I will catch you, cat!" Tom said. They ran around the room, jumping on the bed and the sofa. They had fun, but they also made a mess. They knocked over the lamp, the books and the toys. "Stop, stop, you are being silly!" Mom said. She came into the room and saw the mess. She was angry. "Look what you did! You broke the lamp, you tore the books and you scattered the toys. This is not how you play. This is not where you belong. You belong outside, not inside. Go to the yard, now!" Sara and Tom felt sad. They did not want to go outside. They wanted to stay inside and play. They did not mean to make a mess. They said sorry to Mom, but she did not listen. She took them by the hand and led them to the door. "Bye, bye, cat and dog. You can play outside, but not inside. And don't come back until you learn to behave." Mom said. She closed the door behind them. Sara and Tom looked at each other. They felt lonely and cold. They wished they could go back to their room. They wished they could play with their toys. They wished they could look at the ceiling and see the stars. They wished Mom was not angry. They wished they were not silly.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy iron. He liked to play with it all day. One day, Tim put his toy iron on a high shelf. He thought it would be safe there. Later, Tim went to get his toy iron from the high shelf. But it was gone! It seemed to disappear. Tim was sad and asked his mom, "Mom, where did my toy iron go?" His mom did not know. They looked everywhere for the toy iron. Then, Tim's little sister, Sue, came into the room. She was holding the toy iron! Sue said, "I took it to play. I'm sorry." Tim was happy to see his toy iron again. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big bus. The bus had a driver. The driver was very important because he took kids to school. The bus was yellow and fun. One day, the bus saw a big hill. The driver knew he had to resist going too fast. He wanted to keep the kids safe. So, the driver went slow and steady down the hill. The kids were happy and safe. The driver was proud of himself. He knew that being a good driver was important. They all got to school on time, and everyone had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had an enormous jacket that his mom gave him. It was very big and very warm. He loved his jacket very much. One day, Tim went to the park with his friends. They played with a ball and had lots of fun. But when it was time to go home, Tim could not find his jacket. He looked everywhere, but it was gone. Tim was very sad. He told his mom about his lost jacket. His mom wanted to help, so she sent a message to all the moms in the town. She asked if anyone had seen Tim's enormous jacket. The next day, Tim's friend, Sarah, found the jacket near the park. She gave it back to Tim, and he was very happy. Now, Tim and his enormous jacket were together again.
