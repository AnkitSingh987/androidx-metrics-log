Category: Intermediate
Title: Sara and Ben and the Train

Sara and Ben like to play with trains. They have a big track with many turns and bridges. They have many trains of different colors and sizes. Sara likes the pretty trains with flowers and stars. Ben likes the fast trains with lights and sounds. One day, Sara and Ben are playing with their trains on the track. Sara wants to use the pink train with the flower stickers. But Ben wants to use it too. He says it is his turn. Sara says no, it is her turn. They both pull the train and try to take it from each other. The train breaks in two. Sara and Ben are sad and angry. Mom hears the noise and comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken train and the unhappy children. She asks them what happened. Sara and Ben tell her their story. Mom says they should not fight over the train. She says they should share and take turns. She says they should be kind and gentle with their toys. She says they should say sorry to each other and to the train. Sara and Ben feel sorry for what they did. They say sorry to each other and to the train. They hug and make up. Mom helps them fix the train with some glue and tape. She says they can play with it again when it is dry. She says they can use another train for now. She gives them a blue train with a rainbow sticker. Sara and Ben like the blue train. They say thank you to Mom. They push the blue train on the track and have fun. They learn to share and take turns. They are happy and so is Mom.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. She saw a clown making hard balloon animals for the kids. The clown had a big red nose and funny shoes. Lily wanted a balloon animal too. She asked the clown, "Can I have one, please?" The clown smiled and made her a balloon dog. Lily was very happy. At home, Lily played with her balloon dog all day. Her mom said, "Time for bed, Lily." But Lily insisted, "No, I want to play more with my balloon dog!" Her mom laughed and let her play a little longer.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named True. True loved to play outside in the sun. One day, the sun did not rise. It was dark and sad. True was not happy. He wanted the sun to come back. True had an idea. He would clean his room to make the sun happy. True picked up his toys and put them away. He made his bed and swept the floor. True's room was very clean. True went outside and looked up at the sky. Slowly, the sun began to rise. It was bright and warm again. True was so happy. He knew that his clean room made the sun happy too. Now, True could play outside in the sun all day.

Once upon a time, there was a shy cat named Lily. Lily was always scared to go outside and play with the other cats. One day, her friend, a dog named Max, came to her and said, "Lily, let's play outside together!" Lily was scared, but she agreed to go with Max. They went outside and saw a taxi. The taxi was going very fast, and it made a loud noise. Lily wanted to delay going near the taxi because she was shy and scared. Max said, "Don't worry, Lily, I will be with you." Max and Lily walked slowly near the taxi. The taxi stopped, and the driver smiled at them. Lily was not so shy anymore. She played with Max and even made new friends. Now, Lily was a happy cat who loved to play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a new toy cart. He loved to play with it all day. Tim would put his toys in the cart and push it around the house. One sunny day, Tim decided to take his cart outside. He wanted to find more things to put in his cart. As he was walking, he saw a big, soft ball. Tim thought it would be fun to play with, so he picked it up and gave it a squeeze. To his surprise, the ball made a loud honk sound! Tim laughed and squeezed the ball again. He put the honking ball in his cart and kept walking. Soon, he met his friend, Sue. She saw the honking ball in Tim's cart and wanted to play too. They took turns squeezing the ball and laughing at the funny sound it made. They had a great time playing together with the new cart and the unexpected honking ball.
