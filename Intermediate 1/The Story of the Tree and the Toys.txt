Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Tree and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a dependable mom and her daughter. They lived in a small house with a big tree in the yard. The mom worked very hard to take care of her daughter. The daughter loved her mom very much. One day, the mom told her daughter to sit under the big tree. She said, "I need to go and get some food. You stay here and be good." The daughter said, "Okay, mom. I will sit here and wait for you." The dependable mom went to get food, and the daughter sat under the big tree. She played with her toys and sang songs. Soon, the mom came back with a big bag of food. They both sat under the tree and ate their food. They were very happy, and they loved each other very much.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Susie who loved playing with her toys. One day, her mom told her that her aunt was visiting. Susie was very excited to meet her aunt and put on her prettiest dress. When Susie was finally face to face with her aunt, the aunt smiled and said, "I brought you a present!" Susie was eager to open her present, but her mom said, "you have to thank your aunt first." Susie smiled and said, "Thanks Auntie!" The aunt opened the present and revealed a toy train. Susie's eyes lit up and she asked, "Can I play with it?" Her aunt said, "Yes, but you must understand how to use it first. Once you understand how to put it together, you can play with it." Susie was eager to learn and her aunt taught her how to set up the train and use the tracks. Susie was so excited when she finally understood how to play with the train. She thanked her aunt for teaching her and showed her mom the toy. Susie and her mom played with the train happily for the rest of the day.

Lily and Tom were playing with their dolls and cars in the living room. They liked to pretend that they were the king and queen of a big nation. They had many toys and books and clothes. They were very wealthy. One day, a knock came on the door. Lily and Tom ran to see who it was. It was their friend Sam, who lived next door. Sam had a big smile on his face and a backpack on his back. "Hi, Lily and Tom!" Sam said. "Do you want to come with me to the park? I have a surprise for you!" Lily and Tom looked at each other. They liked Sam, but they also liked their game. "What is the surprise?" Lily asked. Sam opened his backpack and showed them a big book. It had many pictures and words in it. It was a book about different nations in the world. "This is the surprise!" Sam said. "My mom gave me this book for my birthday. It tells you about how people live in different places. Some are wealthy, some are poor. Some are hot, some are cold. Some are big, some are small. Do you want to see?" Lily and Tom were curious. They had never seen a book like that before. They wanted to learn more about the world. "Okay, we will come with you," Tom said. "But we have to ask our mom first." They ran to their mom, who was in the kitchen. They told her about Sam and his book and the park. "Can we go, mom?" Lily asked. Their mom smiled and nodded. She was happy that they wanted to read and explore. "Yes, you can go," she said. "But be careful and come back before dark." Lily and Tom hugged their mom and thanked her. They grabbed their coats and followed Sam to the park. They were excited to see the surprise and have fun with their friend.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful spider. One day the spider was trying to get to the top of the window, but just couldn't reach it. Then a 3 year old child came along and saw the spider. She asked the spider, "What are you trying to do?" The spider replied, "I want to get to the top of the window, but I can't reach it." The child smiled and said, "Let me help you! I can get you to the top!" So the child carefully reached up and put the spider on the top of the window. The spider thanked the child and said, "I'm so happy you helped me! You are so kind and beautiful!" The child smiled and said, "It was my pleasure! I was happy to help!" And they both went on their way, and everyone lived happily ever after. The end.
