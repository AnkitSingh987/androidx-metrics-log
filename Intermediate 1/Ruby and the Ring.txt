Category: Intermediate
Title: Ruby and the Ring

Once upon a time a curious little girl named Ruby was staying at a hotel. She was sitting in the lobby looking around when suddenly the phone began to ring. She hopped off her chair and ran over to the counter to answer it. "Hello?" she said in her sweet little voice. "Hello, is this the hotel?" replied the voice on the other end. Ruby smiled and said "Yes, this is the hotel. What can I do for you?" The voice replied, "I was wondering if I could book a room for tonight?" Ruby was happy she could help. "Yes, we have rooms available!" she said cheerfully. The voice thanked her and hung up. Ruby stood there, feeling very proud of herself. She had just helped someone on the phone! She was very curious about what else she could do at the hotel. The End.

Michelle loved her days at her dad's office. He was the boss and the staff were always so nice. Today, they had a special treat. Michelle ran to the front desk, eager to find out what was happening. "What's going on today?" Michelle asked the receptionist. "We are having a picnic! The staff is organizing it and I've heard that the food will be really tasty," the receptionist replied. Michelle couldn't wait! She ran around the office helping the staff organize the picnic. The staff was very grateful for her help. Soon enough, everything was ready and it was time for the party. Michelle had a great time eating the tasty food and playing with the staff. She gave them all a big hug and thanked them for organizing such a wonderful picnic.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was going on a journey with his mom and dad. It was an important trip, so Tim had to wear his special clothes. On the journey, Tim saw many fun things. He saw big trees, cute animals, and pretty flowers. He was very happy to be on this important journey with his mom and dad. They walked and walked, and Tim's special clothes started to feel hot. Tim was very tired and hot, so he took off his special clothes. But then, he got lost in the big forest. Tim looked for his mom and dad, but he could not find them. He was very sad and scared. In the end, Tim did not find his mom and dad, and he was all alone in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a slow mouse named Tim. Tim loved cheese more than anything else. He lived in a small house near a big kitchen. One day, Tim saw a big piece of cheese on the table in the kitchen. He wanted to enjoy the cheese, so he thought of a plan. He waited until everyone was asleep, then he slowly and quietly went to the table. Tim climbed up the table leg and reached the top. He saw the cheese and was very happy. He took a big bite and enjoyed the cheese very much. Tim ate and ate until he was full. Then, he slowly went back to his small house, feeling happy and full of cheese.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. They see a big tree with many branches. Tom says, "Let's climb the tree. It looks fun." Mia says, "OK, but be careful. Don't fall." They hold the branches and climb up the tree. They see many birds and leaves. They feel the wind and the sun. They are happy. They say, "Wow, the park is so attractive from here. We can see everything." But then, they hear a loud bark. A big dog is running towards the tree. It looks angry. It wants to catch them. Tom and Mia are scared. They say, "Oh no, a dog. How can we get down?" They look for a way to get down. But the branches are too high and too thin. They can't jump. They can't slide. They are stuck. They say, "Help, help. Someone, help us." A man hears them. He sees the dog and the tree. He runs to the park. He has a leash. He says, "Don't worry, kids. I'm here. The dog is mine. His name is True. He won't hurt you. He just wants to play. But he is too big and too loud. I'm sorry. Let me get him." He puts the leash on True. He pulls him away from the tree. He says, "True, sit. Stay. Good boy." He looks at Tom and Mia. He says, "Are you OK? Do you need help to get down?" Tom and Mia say, "Yes, please. Thank you, mister. You are very nice." The man helps them to get down. He says, "You're welcome, kids. But be careful next time. Climbing trees can be dangerous. And always ask your parents before you go to the park." Tom and Mia say, "OK, we will. Thank you again. Bye, mister. Bye, True." They wave to the man and the dog. They go back to their homes. They tell their parents what happened. They say, "We had an adventure today. We climbed a tree. We saw a dog. His name was True. He was big and loud. But he was also friendly. And a nice man helped us. The park is attractive. But we learned to be careful." Their parents hug them and say, "We're glad you're safe. We love you."
