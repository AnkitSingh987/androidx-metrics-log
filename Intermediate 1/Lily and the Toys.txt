Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lily found a big bag in her room. She thought it would be fun to fill the bag with toys and take them outside to play. Lily and her friends played all day with the toys from the bag. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. But then, the sky turned gray and it started to get cold. Lily and her friends did not want to stop playing, but they knew they had to go inside before the cold spoiled their fun. Lily put all the toys back in the bag and went inside with her friends. They were sad that the cold had spoiled their fun, but they were happy that they had the bag of toys to play with inside. They played with the toys all day, and they had lots of fun even though it was cold outside.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Mia went to the kitchen. She saw a smooth, round ball on the table. She picked it up and felt how smooth it was. Mia's mom came into the kitchen and saw her holding the ball. "Mia, what do you see?" she asked. Mia smiled and said, "I see a smooth ball, Mommy!" Mia and her mom played with the smooth ball in the kitchen. They rolled it back and forth to each other. They laughed and had a lot of fun. After playing, they put the ball away and went to eat some yummy food. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Polly. She was so excited because it was almost New Year's Day. One day, Polly asked her mom if she could get a firework for the holiday. Her mom said, "Yes, I will mail you one." Polly waited and waited, but no firework came. She was so sad she began to cry. But then, on New Year's Day, a special packages arrived in the mail. Polly opened it and found a pretty firework inside. It was the prettiest firework Polly had ever seen. Polly was so happy! She ran outside and lit the firework. It made colors and a loud sound. Polly loved it. What a happy day!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, Tim saw a big box in his room. It was closed and he wanted to see what was inside. Tim tried to open the box, but it was hard. Tim thought and thought. Then, he realized he could use a cut to help him open the box. He asked his mom for help. She gave him a safe tool to make a cut in the box. Tim was happy and excited. Tim made a small cut and opened the box. Inside, he found a new toy car! But, there was a problem. The toy car was broken. Tim was sad. His mom saw him and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can fix it together." They worked together and fixed the toy car. Tim was happy again, and he played with his new toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to carry his toy bear everywhere he went. One day, he went to the park with his mom and his toy bear. They saw a harmless bug on a leaf. Tim was not scared of the bug because it was small and friendly. "Mom, can we play a quiz about bugs?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Sure, Tim! Let's see how much you know about bugs." So they started their quiz. Tim got all the answers right because he knew a lot about bugs. After the quiz, they had a fun day at the park. Tim carried his toy bear and played with the harmless bug. They all went home happy and tired. Tim learned that even small things can be fun and exciting.

One day, a girl named Lily had a dream. In her dream, she met a new friend. The friend was a fair and nice cat. The cat said, "Hello, I am Kitty. Let me introduce myself." Lily and Kitty played together in a big park. They were very happy. But then, a big dog came. The dog was not happy. He wanted to play with Lily too. The dog said, "I want to play, but you have a new friend." Lily thought for a moment. She wanted to make everyone happy. She had an idea. She said, "Why don't we all play together? We can be friends too." The dog agreed, and they all played together in the park. In the end, Lily, Kitty, and the dog became good friends. They played and laughed together. Lily's dream was full of fun and love. When she woke up, she knew she had a great dream.
