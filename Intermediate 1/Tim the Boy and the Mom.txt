Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Mom

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He had a toy phone and liked to call his friends. Tim's mom heard him talking and came into the room. She saw that Tim was using a harsh voice when he talked to his friends on the toy phone. "Tim, why are you using a harsh voice?" asked his mom. Tim said, "I want to sound like a big boy with a big brain, so my friends will think I'm smart." His mom smiled and said, "You don't need to use a harsh voice to show you have a big brain. Just be kind and share what you know." Tim thought about what his mom said. The next time he played with his toy phone, he called his friends with a nice voice. He shared fun things he learned with them. His friends liked talking to Tim even more now. Tim learned that being kind was better than being harsh.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a colorful rod that he loved to play with. One day, he went to a park with his mom and dad. He took his colorful rod with him to show his friends. In the park, Tim and his friends played with the colorful rod. They took turns to look at it and touch it. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the colorful rod away. Tim and his friends looked for the colorful rod but they could not find it. Tim felt very sad, but his mom and dad told him not to worry. They said that it is important to share and be kind. Tim learned that even if he lost his colorful rod, he still had his friends. They all played together and had fun. And that was more important than any toy. The moral of the story is to value friends and be kind to others.

Once upon a time, in a small forest, there lived a little bunny named Ben. One sunny day, Ben went out to find some food. He hopped and hopped until he saw a big bush with red berries. Ben thought, "I want to eat those berries!" Ben went closer to the bush and started to eat the berries. But after eating one, he made a terrible face. "These berries taste terrible!" he said. He didn't want to eat any more of the terrible berries. So, Ben hopped away to look for something else to eat. As he hopped along, Ben saw a big tree with lots of green leaves. He thought, "Maybe I can eat these leaves." So, Ben ate some leaves and they tasted much better than the terrible berries. Ben was happy and full. He hopped back to his cozy home in the forest, and took a long nap.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the train station with his mom. They were going to visit grandma. The station was big and busy with many people. Tim thought it was incredible. At the station, Tim saw a man with a hat. The man said, "Hi, can you help me?" Tim nodded. The man asked, "Can you remove my hat for me?" Tim reached up and took off the man's hat. "Thank you," said the man. Tim smiled and gave the hat back to the man. Then, Tim and his mom got on the train to go see grandma. They had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty beetle. He loved to play pranks on his friends and they called him Mr. Mischief. One day, Mr. Mischief was playing in the garden when he came across a beautiful flower. He decided he wanted to make it his own. "I'm going to pick that pretty flower," Mr. Mischief said. Just then, an old lady walking by spotted Mr. Mischief. "Oh no! You mustn't pick the flower!" she said. Mr. Mischief was determined to pick the flower and replied, "Yes I am!" The old lady was very firm. She said, "No, you mustn't, because it's the only way to prevent more beetles from growing in the garden." Mr. Mischief didn't want any other beetles to grow, so he listened to the old lady and left the flower alone. He was very sad, but he had done the right thing, and all the other bugs praised him for it.
