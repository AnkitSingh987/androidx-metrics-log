Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob and Tim and the Stick

Once upon a time, two friends, Bob and Tim, were playing together. Tim said, “Let’s do something fun. What shall we do?” Bob thought for a moment and then suggested, “What about playing hockey?” Tim was surprised. “Hockey? That sounds exciting. But I don’t have a hockey stick.” Bob looked around and found a hairy stick lying near their feet. He picked it up and said, “Look, Tim! We can use this!” Tim was delighted. He smiled and took the stick from Bob. Then he said, “Let’s play!” And so Bob and Tim had a wonderful time playing hockey with the hairy stick.

Once upon a time, there was a little candle. The candle was weird because it could talk. It lived in a small house with a nice family. The family loved the candle very much. Every night, they would light the candle and listen to it tell funny stories. One day, the candle became sad. It did not want to talk anymore. The family tried to help the candle, but they did not know what to do. The candle just sat in the corner of the room, not saying a word. Then, a big wind came and blew the candle over. The candle started a fire in the house. The family tried to put out the fire, but it was too big. The house burned down, and the candle was gone forever. The family was very sad.

Lily and Tom like to play with balls. They have many balls of different colors and sizes. They like to throw them, kick them, and roll them. Sometimes they play catch with each other, or with their dog Max. One day, they see a big net in the park. It is hanging from two poles. It looks like fun. They run to the net and try to throw their balls over it. Some balls go over, and some balls get stuck. They laugh and try again. A man comes to the net. He is angry. He says the net is his. He says he uses it to play a game called tennis. He says the balls are not for the net. He says the balls are cheap and bad. He takes the balls and puts them in a bag. Lily and Tom are sad. They want their balls back. They say the balls are theirs. They say they like the balls. They say the balls are not cheap and bad. They say the man is mean. Max sees the man and the bag. He does not like the man. He likes the balls. He runs to the bag and bites it. The bag breaks and the balls fall out. Max barks and moves his tail. He picks up a ball and runs away. Lily and Tom follow him. They are happy. They say Max is good and smart. They say the man is silly and rude. They play with their balls and their dog. They forget about the net and the man.

Frankie was so excited. Tonight, his mum had promised him a special dinner. Frankie couldn't wait to see what it was. He rushed around the house, helping his Mum to carry all the yummy food to the table. At last, dinner was ready. Frankie couldn't believe his eyes when he saw the original dinner his Mum had made. There was fried chicken and chips, plus lots of nice healthy salads too. Frankie was so hungry, he gulped it all down in no time. After dinner, Frankie and his Mum went outside for a walk. They had to carry all the dirty dishes back to the kitchen first. Frankie wasn't very pleased about that, but his Mum said it was important! Frankie helped his mum to carry all the dishes back, then went outside for a lovely walk in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He was standing by a window, looking out. He noticed a car in the distance. He asked his mom what it was. She said, "That's a car sweetheart." He watched as the car drove away. Then he said to his mom, "I want a car when I'm big!" His mom smiled and replied, "You need to be patient, love. Big things take time." The little boy thought about this for a moment. He finally said, "You're right, mom. I should be patient!" The moral of the story is that patience is a virtue. We may want something right away, but sometimes it's better to wait until the time is right. Watch and wait, and you will get what you desire in the end.
