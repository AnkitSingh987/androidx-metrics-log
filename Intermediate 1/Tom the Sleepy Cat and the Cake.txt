Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Sleepy Cat and the Cake

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big tree and decided to climb it. At the top of the tree, he found a shiny jewel. Tom was very happy and wanted to show his friend, Sam the dog. Tom said, "Sam, look what I found! A shiny jewel!" Sam looked at the jewel and said, "Wow, that is very pretty. But be careful, Tom. It may rot if we don't keep it safe." Tom and Sam put the jewel in a small box and hid it under a rock. They went to play and forgot about the jewel. The next day, they went to check on the jewel. When they opened the box, they were surprised! The jewel was gone and there was a yummy cake inside. Tom and Sam looked at each other and laughed. They didn't know how the jewel turned into a cake, but they were happy. They shared the cake and had a fun day together. The sleepy cat and his friend had a big surprise, and they learned that sometimes unexpected things can be fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the mud. One day, Tim went outside to play. The sun was shining, and it was a calm day. Tim found a big puddle of mud and jumped in it. He laughed as the mud splashed all around him. Tim's mom saw him playing in the mud. She called out to him, "Tim, come inside now! You are very dirty!" Tim did not want to stop playing, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. He walked back to the house with mud all over his shoes and clothes. When Tim got inside, his mom said, "You need to clean up. You cannot play in the mud like that." Tim felt sad. He knew his mom would punish him for getting so dirty. He went to the bathroom and washed the mud off his hands and face. Then, he changed into clean clothes. Tim's mom saw that he was clean and said, "Now you can go back outside, but no more playing in the mud." Tim nodded his head and went back outside. This time, he played on the swings and stayed away from the mud. From that day on, Tim knew that he should not play in the mud if he did not want to be punished. He still had fun playing outside, but he made sure to stay clean. Tim's mom was happy, and they both enjoyed the calm days together.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a snake on the ground. The snake was long and green. Tim knew it was a healthy snake because it looked strong and happy. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, can I request to play with the snake?" His mom looked at the snake and said, "Yes, Tim. But be careful and don't go too close." Tim went back to the snake and talked to it. He said, "Hi, snake! I want to be your friend." The snake looked at Tim and they played together. They had a lot of fun in the park. Tim and the snake became good friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a fat pig. His name was Bob. He was walking in the forest one day. All of a sudden Bob started to sneeze. Bob was very surprised by his sneezing. Bob asked the trees, "Why did I sneeze?" The trees replied, "It must be a secret!" So Bob kept walking, trying to figure out what the secret was. He asked the birds and the bees but they just chirped and hummed and wouldn't tell him anything. Finally Bob asked an owl, "What is the secret?" The wise owl hooted, "Eat less food and you will be less fat, this is your sneezing secret!" Bob was so happy he had learned the secret that he ran home and started eating less food. He was soon no longer fat and he was never sneezing again.

Once upon a time, there was a big giant. He was nice and had a loyal dog. The dog was his best friend. They played and had fun all day. One day, the giant wanted to borrow a ball from a little boy. He asked, "Can I borrow your ball?" The little boy said, "Yes, you can." The giant and his loyal dog played with the ball. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed into a big cake! The giant, the loyal dog, and the little boy were surprised. They all laughed and ate the yummy cake together. And they became best friends forever.
