Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Dog of Tim and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tim went to the gym with his mom. The gym was a fun place where people could play and run. Tim liked the gym because it had a big ball he could bounce on. The gym was not too hot or too cold, it was mild inside. Tim saw his friend Sue at the gym. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to comfort Sue, so he said, "Don't be sad, Sue. We can find your toy together." They looked all around the gym for Sue's toy, but they could not find it. Then, a big dog came into the gym. The dog had Sue's toy in its mouth! Tim and Sue were happy to see the toy, but the dog did not want to give it back. The dog ran away with Sue's toy and they never saw it again. Tim and Sue were sad, but they still had fun playing at the gym together.

A little girl named Sue asked her mom to take her to the park. Her mom said yes, but only if Sue told her the truth. Sue hesitated, unsure of what to do. At first, Sue was afraid that if she said the truth, her mom would find out something bad. But then, she remembered the light in her mom's eyes, and her gentle smile. Sue took a deep breath, and then requested the truth. Her mom was very proud that her daughter asked for the truth, so she hugged Sue tightly and said, "You can go to the park, and I'm proud of you." Sue smiled and hugged her mom back. She was glad that she had asked for the truth, and that her mom was so understanding. Sue skipped out of the house and off to the park, happy to be on her way. She knew that asking for the truth was always the right way to go.

One day, Tom and Anna went to the park with their mom. They saw a big hill with a hole in it. They wanted to see what was inside. "Can we enter the hole, mom?" Tom asked. "Only if you are careful and stay close to me," mom said. They walked up the hill and entered the hole. It was dark and cold. They used a flashlight to look around. They saw many rocks of different shapes and sizes. Some were shiny, some were smooth, some were sharp. "Wow, look at this rock!" Anna said. She picked up a round rock that had red and blue spots on it. "It is so pretty!" "Let me see!" Tom said. He reached for the rock, but Anna pulled it away. "No, it is mine!" Anna said. They started to fight over the rock. They did not hear a loud growl behind them. A wild animal was hiding in the hole. It had big teeth and claws. It did not like the noise that Tom and Anna were making. It jumped out of the shadows and roared. "Ah!" Tom and Anna screamed. They dropped the rock and ran to their mom. The wild animal chased them. "Help, mom, help!" they cried. Mom saw the wild animal and grabbed Tom and Anna. She ran out of the hole with them. She threw a stick at the wild animal to scare it away. "Are you okay, my loves?" mom asked. "Yes, mom, we are okay," Tom and Anna said. They hugged their mom and said they were sorry for fighting. They learned their lesson. They did not enter the hole again. They did not want to meet the wild animal again. They played with other things in the park. They shared and had fun. They were happy.

Once upon a time there was a happy family. There was a mom, a dad and two kids. One day the mom and dad were very excited. They started to plan a special day. They were going to marry! The mom put on a white dress and the dad put on a suit. On the day of the wedding, the kids were so excited. They brought a birdcage with them. It was white and pretty. The birdcage was a surprise for the mom and dad. The wedding was very special. Everyone was smiling and looking happy. At the end of the ceremony, the mom and dad said "I do" and everyone cheered. The family was really happy. They all hugged each other and went home to celebrate. They put the birdcage in the window so everyone could see it. It was a reminder that the family was married. And they all lived happily ever after!
