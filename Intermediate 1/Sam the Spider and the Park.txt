Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Spider and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a playful spider named Sam. Sam loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Sam met a new friend named Timmy. Timmy was a little bug who liked to play too. Sam said to Timmy, "Let's play together!" Timmy was happy and said, "Yes, I want to play too!" So, Sam and Timmy played all day. They played on the swings and the slide. They had lots of fun. Then, Sam and Timmy got hungry. Sam wanted to provide food for his new friend. Sam found some yummy leaves for Timmy to eat. Timmy was very happy and said, "Thank you, Sam!" They ate their food and played some more. At the end of the day, Sam and Timmy were tired but happy. They had so much fun playing together. They said goodbye and went to their homes. Sam and Timmy knew they would play together again soon. And they became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mysterious vehicle. It was big and shiny. People did not know where it came from. They called it the "mysterious vehicle". One day, a nice man came to the town. He wanted to sell the mysterious vehicle. The people in the town were very excited. They all wanted to see the vehicle up close. The nice man showed the mysterious vehicle to everyone. It had big wheels and bright lights. The people in the town loved it. At the end of the day, a happy family bought the mysterious vehicle. They drove it around the town, and everyone waved and smiled.

One day, a lovely cat and a little dog were playing in the park. They were running and jumping. The cat saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. The dog wanted to play more, so he said, "Cat, please don't go up! Let's play more!" The cat did not stop and went up the tree. The little dog was sad and sat down. Then, a nice girl came to the park. She saw the sad dog and went to him. "Why are you sad, little dog?" she asked. The dog said, "My friend cat went up the tree and left me here." The girl looked at the tree and saw the cat. She called the cat, "Come down, lovely cat! Your friend is sad. Play with him!" The cat came down and they all played together. The cat learned that it is good to care for friends and not leave them alone.

Alice and her mom were looking out at the night sky. "Mommy, what are those things?" Alice asked. "Those are stars, Alice," her mom answered. Alice pointed, "And that one?" "Ah," her mom smiled, "That one is a comet. It's red." Alice watched the red comet for a moment then asked, "Can we pour it?" Her mom shook her head, "No, we can't pour it - it's too far away." Alice squinted, "Oh. I wish we could pour it like juice." Her mom laughed, "Me too, sweetheart. But for now, we'll just have to enjoy watching it." Alice smiled and kept looking up at the sky. Together, they watched the beautiful red comet in the night sky.

One day, Tim had a new eraser. It was big and pink. All his friends were envious. They all wanted to have it. Tim was very happy and proud. At school, Tim's friend, Sam, asked, "Can I keep your eraser?" Tim said, "No, it is mine. You can't keep it." Sam was sad, but he understood. Tim kept the eraser in his bag. Later, Tim went to play outside. When he came back, his eraser was gone. He was very sad. He asked his friends if they took it. They all said no. Tim did not know what to do. Then, a little bird flew by. It had the eraser in its beak. The bird dropped the eraser into Tim's hands. Tim was so happy and surprised. He thanked the bird. From then on, Tim shared his eraser with his friends. They were all happy together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was restless. She wanted to play and have fun. One day, she found a jar of jam in the kitchen. Mia loved jam. She wanted to make a game with the jam. Mia took the jam and started to clap her hands. Each time she clapped, she would say "jam, jam, jam!" Mia clapped and danced around the room. She was having so much fun with her new game. Mia's mom saw her playing with the jam. She laughed and clapped too. They both played the jam game together. Mia was not restless anymore. She was happy and had fun with her mom and the jam.
