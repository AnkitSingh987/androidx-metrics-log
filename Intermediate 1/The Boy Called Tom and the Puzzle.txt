Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Tom and the Puzzle

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Tom. He was three years old and loved playing in the rain. One day, the sky started to fill up with grey clouds. Soon, the clouds opened and it began to rain heavily. Tom ran outside with excitement. He noticed a task that had been left outside from the night before. It was an interesting one- a puzzle. Tom didn't know how to complete it, but he was excited to try. He ran over to his mom, who was watching him from the window. “Mom! I want to try to do the task!” he shouted. Mom smiled. “Okay, but be careful in the rain. Go back inside if it's too wet.” Tom nodded eagerly. “I will!” He ran back to the task and focused hard. With his mom watching him, Tom worked on the puzzle until the rain stopped. When the sun came out, Tom had finished the task. He was very proud of himself!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pretty dress with lots of colors. She loved her dress very much. One day, she played outside with her friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. But then, Amy fell down and got her dress dirty. Amy felt very sorry. She went home to her mom and showed her the dirty dress. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we can clean it." So, they went to the sink and Amy's mom helped her rub the dress with soap and water. They rubbed and rubbed until the dress was clean again. After the dress dried, Amy put it back on. She was so happy to have her pretty dress clean again. She promised to be more careful when playing outside. Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, we all make mistakes. Just remember to have fun and be safe." And Amy went back outside to play with her friends, with a big smile on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a smart bird named Tim. Tim lived in a big tree in the park. One day, Tim saw a twig on the ground. He thought the twig would be perfect for his nest. So, Tim picked up the twig in his beak and flew back to his tree. On his way to the tree, Tim met his friend, Sam the squirrel. Sam saw the twig in Tim's beak and said, "Hey Tim, what are you doing with that twig?" Tim answered, "I am going to use it for my nest. I want to cover it so my eggs stay warm." But as Tim and Sam were talking, a big wind came. The wind blew the twig out of Tim's beak. The twig flew far away and landed in a puddle. Tim and Sam looked at the wet twig and felt sad. They knew they could not use the wet twig for the nest anymore. The nest would not be warm and cozy for the eggs. And that is how the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little car. The car had four round tires. The car loved to go fast and play with its friends. One day, the car saw that its tires were very dirty. The car wanted to be clean and shiny. The clever car had an idea. It went to the big water puddle to clean its tires. The car went splash, splash, splash! The water made the tires all wet and clean. The car was very happy. It could go fast and play with its friends again. But then, something unexpected happened. As the car went fast, it saw a big, big tire in the road. The big tire was very dirty too. The clever car had another idea. It pushed the big tire to the water puddle. Splash, splash, splash! The big tire was also clean now. The car had a new friend, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Marcus. He lived with his Mom and Dad. One day, Mom told Marcus that she and Dad were going out for the day and he had to stay with old Mrs. Johnson. Marcus was so excited because he loved Mrs. Johnson. Mom said to Marcus, "I need your help. Can you send something to Mrs. Johnson for me?" Marcus said, "Yes!" He went to the bathroom and got a big bottle of shampoo. He put it in a basket and went to Mrs. Johnson's house. Marcus rang the doorbell and Mrs. Johnson said, "Hello, Marcus! What have you brought me?" Marcus said, "Mom sent shampoo!" Mrs. Johnson thanked him and said, "Your Mom is so thoughtful!" Marcus smiled and waved goodbye as he went back home. He was happy that he had sent something to old Mrs. Johnson. He knew she would enjoy it!
