Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa and the Flowers

Lisa and her mom went to the big store. Lisa liked to look at the pretty things and touch the soft things. Mom wanted to buy a new perfume. She said perfume was a smell that made people happy. They walked to the perfume place. There were many bottles with different colors and shapes. Mom picked up one and sprayed it on her wrist. She smelled it and smiled. "This one is amazing," she said. "Do you like it, Lisa?" Lisa smelled mom's wrist. She did not like it. It smelled like flowers, but not the good kind. It smelled like the flowers that made her sneeze. She made a face and shook her head. "No, mom. It is yucky." Mom laughed and put the bottle back. She picked up another one and sprayed it on her other wrist. She smelled it and frowned. "This one is not good either," she said. "It is too strong. It hurts my nose." Lisa smelled mom's other wrist. She liked it a little bit. It smelled like candy, but not the sweet kind. It smelled like the candy that made her teeth hurt. She nodded and said, "It is okay, mom. But not the best." Mom nodded and put the bottle back. She looked at the other bottles and sighed. "It is hard to find a good perfume," she said. "Maybe we should try another place." Lisa saw a bottle that looked different. It was small and round and shiny. It had a picture of a cat on it. Lisa loved cats. She reached for the bottle and sprayed it on her hand. She smelled it and smiled. It smelled like home. It smelled like mom. She recognized it. It was mom's old perfume. The one she used to wear before it was gone. Lisa ran to mom and showed her the bottle. "Look, mom. Look what I found. It is your perfume. The one you love. The one I love. It is amazing." Mom looked at the bottle and gasped. She recognized it too. It was her perfume. The one she got from dad. The one he said was special. The one she thought was lost. She smiled and hugged Lisa. "You are amazing, Lisa. You found my perfume. The one I love. The one you love. Thank you, Lisa. Thank you so much."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play in her garden. One day, she saw a tomato plant. It was small and sad. It was a miserable tomato plant. Amy wanted to help the tomato plant. She gave it water and sun. She sang to it every day. The tomato plant began to grow. It was not miserable anymore. It was a happy tomato plant. One morning, Amy went to the garden to find something. She saw a big, red tomato on the plant. She was so happy! She picked the tomato and showed it to her mom. They ate the tomato together. It was the best tomato they ever had.

One day, a nosy cat named Kitty saw a big box. The box had a top on it. Kitty wanted to see what was inside the box. She tried to open the top, but it was hard to do. She kept trying and trying. Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, to help her open the box. Doggy was strong and could open the top. But Doggy said, "No, Kitty. We should not open the box. It is not ours." Kitty did not listen and kept trying to open the top. Soon, Mommy came and saw Kitty trying to open the box. She said, "Kitty, you should not be nosy. It is not good to look in things that are not yours." Kitty felt sad and said sorry to Mommy. Mommy hugged Kitty and said, "It is okay, but remember to always ask before you touch things that are not yours." The moral of the story is: it is important to ask before touching things that do not belong to you.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. He was very glad because he loved to play there. At the park, he saw many kids playing and laughing. Tom wanted to join them and make new friends. Tom saw a girl named Sue. She was playing with a ball. Tom went up to her and said, "Hi, I'm Tom. Can I play with you?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I'm Sue. Let's play together!" They played catch with the ball and had lots of fun. After a while, Sue wanted to chew some gum. She took out a piece of gum and gave one to Tom. But Tom didn't know how to chew gum. He tried to swallow it and started to choke. Sue quickly knelt down and patted Tom's back, and the gum came out. Tom was so thankful to Sue for helping him. Sue said, "Tom, you should never swallow gum. It's not good for you." Tom nodded and said, "Thank you, Sue. I will remember that." They continued to play and had a great day at the park. The moral of the story is to always be careful with what you eat and to help your friends when they need it.
