Category: Intermediate
Title: The Town and the Max

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, a big dog named Max loved to play in the mud. One day, Max's owner said, "Max, you need a bath!" Max did not like baths, but he knew he had to get clean. That night, as Max was going to bathe, the town felt a shake. The shake made Max feel helpless. It was an earthquake! The people in the town were scared. Max knew he had to do something to help them. The next day, Max went to the park. He saw a big hole from the earthquake. Max thought, "I can help by filling the hole with mud!" So, Max played in the mud and filled the hole. The town was happy, and Max felt like a hero. After that, Max did not mind taking a bath, because he knew he could always help his town.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate bird named Bella. Bella lived in a nest, up high in a tree. She loved to fly in the sky and play with her friends. One day, Bella saw a little bug on a leaf. The bug looked sad and could not move. Bella knew she had to help the bug. Bella thought of a plan to help the bug. She knew she could not pick up the bug with her beak because it would weigh too much. So, Bella found a strong leaf and put the bug on it. She then picked up the leaf with her beak and flew high up in the sky. Bella found a nice, safe spot for the bug to live. She gently placed the bug on a new leaf in a big tree. The bug was so happy and thanked Bella for being so kind. Bella felt good about helping her new friend. From that day on, Bella and the bug became the best of friends. The moral of the story is to always be compassionate and help others when they need it.

Sara and Ben were twins. They liked to learn new things. One day, their mom took them to the museum. The museum had many things to see and touch. Sara and Ben were excited. They saw big bones of animals that lived long ago. They saw shiny rocks and stars. They saw paintings and statues. They saw people who talked about the things in the museum. Sara wanted to ask them questions. Ben was shy. He did not want to talk to the people. Sara raised her hand and asked a question. The person answered her. Sara smiled. She asked another question. The person answered her again. Sara was happy. She asked many questions. The person was nice and patient. Ben watched Sara. He wanted to ask a question too. But he was afraid. He thought the person would not like him. He thought the other kids would laugh at him. He thought his question was silly. He kept quiet. Then, he saw something that made him curious. He saw a big glass case with a mummy inside. The mummy was wrapped in cloth. It had a mask on its face. It looked scary and old. Ben wondered who the mummy was and how it got there. He looked at the sign next to the case. It had many words that he did not know. He wished he could ask the person. He looked at Sara. She was still asking questions. She did not see the mummy. He looked at the person. The person was looking at him. The person smiled and said, "Do you have a question, young man?" Ben felt his heart beat fast. He wanted to run away. But he also wanted to know. He took a deep breath. He opened his mouth. He asked his question. The person answered him. Ben listened. He learned something new. He felt proud. He smiled. Sara saw Ben. She saw the mummy. She saw the person. She ran to join them. She said, "Wow, Ben, you asked a question! That's awesome! What did you learn?" Ben told her what he learned. Sara was impressed. She said, "That's so cool! I want to learn more too. Can we ask more questions?" The person said, "Of course. I'm happy to answer your questions. You are both very smart and curious. I like that. Come, let me show you more things in the museum." Sara and Ben followed the person. They asked more questions. They learned more things. They had fun. They were not shy anymore. They were happy.
