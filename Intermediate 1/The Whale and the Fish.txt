Category: Intermediate
Title: The Whale and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a big whale. The whale lived in a peaceful sea. The sea was very blue and calm. The whale had many friends in the sea. They all liked to play together. One day, the whale and his friends decided to have a party. They wanted to include everyone. They invited the fish, the crabs, and even the little sea horses. Everyone was excited for the party. At the party, they all played fun games and ate yummy sea plants. The whale was so happy that everyone was included. They all danced and played until the sun went down. The peaceful sea was filled with joy and laughter. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Amy's mom asked her to fold a napkin. Amy was very happy to help. Amy's mom was making a big pot of vegetable soup for dinner. Amy was a little careless and dropped a carrot on the floor. But she picked it up quickly and put it in the pot. Amy's mom smiled and said, "Good job, Amy!" After they finished cooking, Amy and her mom sat down to eat the soup. Amy was proud of her folded napkin and the vegetables in the soup. They both enjoyed their dinner, and Amy couldn't wait to help her mom again.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bobo. Bobo had strong muscles. He liked to run and play all day long. One day, Bobo saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and embarrassed. Bobo wanted to help the cat. He tried to use his muscles to push the tree down. But the tree was too big. Bobo had an idea. He would prevent the cat from falling by catching her in his soft fur. Bobo stood under the tree and told the cat to jump. The cat was scared, but she trusted Bobo. She jumped and landed in Bobo's soft fur. The cat was safe and happy. They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. The cat had a weak tie on its neck. The dog said, "Why do you use that weak tie? It looks like it will break soon." The cat said, "I like this tie. It is my favorite. Let's go play by the big tree." They ran to the big tree and started to play. They jumped and played for a long time. The sun was shining, and they were very happy. Then, something unexpected happened. The weak tie broke! The cat was sad and said, "Oh no! My tie is gone!" But the dog was smart. He found a new tie under the big tree. The cat was so happy and said, "Thank you, dog! I will use this new tie from now on." And they played and played, happy with the new tie.

One day, a frog and a duck were playing near a swamp. They were very good friends. The frog liked to jump, and the duck liked to swim. They always had fun together. The frog said, "Let's play a game. I will jump, and you will swim. We will see who can go the fastest." The duck agreed, and they started the race. The frog jumped, and the duck swam as fast as they could. Suddenly, the frog heard a shout. It was the duck! The duck was stuck in some mud in the swamp. The frog stopped jumping and went to help the duck. The duck was embarrassed because it needed help from the frog. The frog and the duck worked together to get the duck out of the mud. They were both very happy when the duck was free. They laughed and continued to play in the swamp, knowing that they would always help each other no matter what.

Ben and Mia were best friends. They liked to play outside and explore new things. One day, they decided to go to the lake with their parents. They packed some snacks, water, and toys in a big bag. When they got to the lake, they saw many ducks, fish, and frogs. They also saw a big, hairy dog. The dog was friendly and wagged its tail. Ben and Mia wanted to pet the dog, but they asked their parents first. "Can we pet the dog, please?" they said. "Sure, but be gentle and careful. The dog might not like it if you pull its fur or ears," their parents said. Ben and Mia approached the dog slowly and said hello. The dog licked their faces and sniffed their hands. They stroked its soft, hairy coat and played fetch with a stick. The dog was happy and ran around. The dog reminded Ben of his grandpa's dog, Rex. Rex was also big and hairy, but he was brown and the lake dog was black. Ben told Mia about Rex and how he liked to play with him when he visited grandpa. "I like Rex, too. He is very nice and funny. He reminds me of my teddy bear, Bob. Bob is also brown and hairy, but he is small and the lake dog is big," Mia said. They laughed and hugged the dog. They had a lot of fun with the lake dog. They thanked the dog and its owner for letting them play. They said goodbye and went back to their parents. They ate their snacks, drank their water, and shared their toys. They enjoyed the lake and their friendship.
