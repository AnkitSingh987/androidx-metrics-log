Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He had wide eyes that sparkled when he saw new things. One day, Tim saw a big dog playing in the park. He wanted to play with the dog, but he was scared. Tim asked his mom to explain why the dog was so big. His mom said that some dogs are big and some dogs are small, just like people. She told Tim that the dog was friendly and that he could play with it if he wanted to. Tim took a deep breath and walked up to the dog. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim learned that even if something looks scary, it might not be. The moral of the story is to not judge things by how they look.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a young boy. This youth was kind and loved to help others. One day, while he was walking, he met a big, talking bird. The bird was very sad. The bird said, "I lost my baby bird! Can you help me find her?" The youth wanted to help, so he said, "Yes, I will help you." They looked and looked for the baby bird. Then, the youth had an idea. He told the big bird to order the other birds to help look for the baby bird. The big bird ordered the other birds to help. They all flew around, looking for the baby bird. Soon, they found her! The big bird was so happy and thanked the youth. The baby bird was safe, and everyone was happy. The youth learned that when we help others, we can make a big change. The big bird learned that when we ask for help, we can find what we need. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, attractive house. In front of the house, there was a porch. On the porch, there was a cat. The cat was sad. It was sad because it had a hurt paw. The cat suffered because of the hurt paw. One day, a little girl saw the cat on the porch. She said, "Hi, cat! Why are you sad?" The cat told the girl about its hurt paw. The little girl wanted to help the cat feel better. The little girl went home and told her mom about the cat. Her mom gave her a band-aid for the cat's paw. The girl went back to the porch and put the band-aid on the cat's paw. The cat was happy now. The cat and the little girl became good friends. They played on the porch every day, and the cat did not suffer anymore.

One day, a big, heavy rock sat on a flag. The flag was sad because it could not fly. A bird saw the flag and wanted to help. The bird said, "Don't worry, flag, I will help you!" The bird tried to lift the heavy rock, but it was too hard. The bird called its friends to help. Many birds came and tried to lift the rock, but it was still too heavy. The birds did not give up. They all tried again and again. Finally, the birds lifted the heavy rock off the flag. The flag was so happy that it started to laugh. The flag could now fly high in the sky. The birds laughed too, happy that they could help their friend.

One day, a playful cat named Kitty found a pen. Kitty wanted to write with the pen. She looked around and saw a big refrigerator. Kitty thought it would be fun to write on the refrigerator. Kitty jumped up and started to write on the refrigerator. She wrote a funny face. Kitty's friend, Doggy, came into the room. Doggy saw what Kitty was doing and said, "Kitty, you are silly! What are you doing?" Kitty replied, "I am writing on the refrigerator. It is fun! Do you want to try?" Doggy thought about it and decided to join Kitty. They both wrote funny faces and had a great time.

One day, a cute little bunny was hopping in the woods. He saw a big log and decided to sit on it. The sun was shining, and the bunny felt very happy. A squirrel came by and saw the bunny. He said, "Hi, bunny! What are you doing on that log?" The bunny replied, "I am just sitting here, enjoying the sun." The squirrel had an idea. "Let's play a game," he said. "We can fold leaves and see who can make the biggest pile!" The bunny thought that was a great idea. So, they started to fold leaves and make piles. They laughed and played, folding leaves and making piles. The sun began to set, and they had made many big piles of folded leaves. The bunny and the squirrel were very tired but happy. As the sky turned dark, they said goodbye and went to their homes. The cute little bunny hopped back to his burrow, and the squirrel climbed up a tree. They had a fun day and knew they would play together again soon.
