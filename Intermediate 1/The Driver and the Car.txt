Category: Intermediate
Title: The Driver and the Car

Once upon a time, there was a driver in a bright yellow car. She loved to drive for hours and hours along the long winding roads. One day, the driver saw a sign up ahead. It read, "Longest Drive!". She couldn't help but feel intrigued by the challenge. She decided she would take the sign's challenge and complete the longest drive! The driver got in her car and began the long drive. She drove slowly and carefully, taking the extra long winding roads. After a couple of hours, the driver felt exhausted but continued to drive. Finally, after hours and hours of driving, the driver heard a loud noise. She had completed her longest drive! The driver stepped out of her car and cheered with joy! She had done it - she had completed the longest drive!

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play in the park. They swing, slide and run. They have fun. But one day, the park is closed. There is a big sign that says "No play". Lily and Tom are sad. They do not know what to do. They walk to Lily's house. They see Lily's mom. She says "The park is closed because they are fixing it. It will be open soon. Do not be sad. You can play here. I have a surprise for you." She takes them to the backyard. There is a big box. She says "Open it". Lily and Tom open the box. They see a big teddy bear. It is soft and brown. It has a red bow. They are happy. They hug the teddy bear. They say "Thank you, mom". Lily's mom says "You are welcome. You can share the teddy bear. It is easy to be nice. Now you can play with it. But remember to wait for your turn. And be careful. Do not break it." Lily and Tom nod. They take turns to hug the teddy bear. They play with it. They forget about the park. They have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big red ball. One day, she went outside to play with her ball. She threw the ball and it went far, far away. Lucy was patient and looked for her ball. Lucy walked to the east, where the sun wakes up. As she walked, she saw a big tree. Lucy thought her ball might be there. But when she looked, she found a blue ball instead. Lucy realized this was not her ball, but she liked the blue ball too. Lucy took the blue ball and walked back home. When she got there, she saw her big red ball waiting for her. It was on the grass, near her house! Lucy was so happy. Now she had two balls to play with. And that day, she played with both her red and blue balls, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy dog named Max. Max had a big house and many toys. He was very happy. One day, Max heard a sound with his ear. He went outside and saw a little cat. The cat was sad and had no toys. Max wanted to help the cat. Max gave the cat some of his toys. They played together and became friends. In the end, Max and the cat were both happy. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a dark statue. The statue was a big man. He was strong and tall. Many kids played near the statue every day. They looked at the statue and wanted to be strong like him. One day, a small boy named Tim tried to climb the statue. He wanted to reach the top. But he was too small. Tim tried and tried but could not succeed. He was sad and sat down near the statue. Then, something unexpected happened. The dark statue began to talk! It told Tim that being strong is not just about being big and tall. It is about being kind and helping others. Tim listened and learned the lesson. He went to help his friends and became strong in his heart. And that is how Tim succeeded.
