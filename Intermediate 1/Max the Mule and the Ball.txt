Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mule and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Max. Max was a very selfish mule. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Max went to the park to play with his ball. He saw his friend, the dog named Sam, and Sam wanted to play with Max's ball too. Max did not want to share his ball with Sam. Max felt big and strong. But then, something happened. Max tried to kick the ball very hard, but he missed it. Max fell down and his face turned red. Max felt embarrass. All the other animals at the park saw Max fall and they laughed. Max was sad and he learned that being selfish was not good. He decided to share his ball with Sam and all the other animals at the park. They all had fun playing together. Max was not selfish anymore and he made many new friends.

Jenny liked to play with her model cars. She had many cars of different colors and sizes. She liked to make them race and crash on the floor. She also liked to pretend that they were talking to each other. One day, she found a new car in her toy box. It was shiny and red, with black stripes and a big spoiler. It looked very fast and cool. Jenny did not know where it came from. Maybe her mom or dad gave it to her as a surprise. She decided to call it Blaze. She took Blaze out of the toy box and put it on the floor. She wanted to see how fast it could go. She pushed it hard and watched it zoom across the room. It was faster than any of her other cars. She smiled and clapped her hands. But then, something strange happened. Blaze did not stop at the wall. It kept going and crashed into the door. Jenny heard a loud bang and saw a dent on the door. She gasped and ran to see if Blaze was okay. She picked it up and saw that it had a button on the side. She pressed it and heard a voice. "Hello, I am Blaze, the spy car. I have a secret mission to complete. Do you want to help me?" Jenny was surprised and scared. She did not know what a spy car was. She did not know what a secret mission was. She did not know who the voice was. She wanted to play with her other cars, not with this unknown car. She wanted to put it back in the toy box and forget about it. But before she could do that, the voice said again. "Quick, we have to go. The bad guys are coming. They want to take me and my secrets. You have to hide me or surrender me. What will you do?" Jenny did not know what to do. She did not want to hide the car or surrender the car. She did not want to deal with the bad guys or the secrets. She just wanted to play. She started to cry and dropped the car on the floor. Her mom heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. She saw the dent on the door, the car on the floor, and Jenny's tears. She hugged Jenny and asked her what happened. Jenny told her mom everything. She told her about the new car, the button, the voice, the mission, the bad guys, and the choice. She told her mom that she did not like the car and that she was scared. Her mom listened and nodded. She told Jenny that it was okay to be scared and that she did not have to play with the car if she did not want to. She told Jenny that the car was not a real spy car, but a toy car that made sounds and stories. She told Jenny that the voice was not a real person, but a recording that played when the button was pressed. She told Jenny that the mission, the bad guys, and the choice were not real, but part of the game. She told Jenny that the car was a gift from her uncle, who thought she would like it. Jenny felt better and calmed down. She understood that the car was not unknown, but a model. She understood that the car was not dangerous, but fun. She understood that the car was not a spy, but a friend. She decided to give the car another chance and play with it again. She picked up the car and pressed the button. She heard the voice. "Hello, I am Blaze, the spy car. I have a secret mission to complete. Do you want to help me?" Jenny smiled and said yes. She and Blaze went on an adventure together. They raced and crashed and talked and laughed. They hid and surrendered and escaped and won. They had a lot of fun. The end.
