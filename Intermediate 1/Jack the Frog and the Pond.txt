Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack the Frog and the Pond

Once there was a little frog named Jack. Jack was a dependable frog and he always did what was right. One day, Jack wanted to go for a walk. He stretched and hopped out of his pond. But then he realized he had no way to get around. He could not jump, so he started to crawl. As Jack was crawling, he spotted a way to get around. It was a four-legged creature walking nearby. Jack knew just what to do! He quietly crept up to the creature and hopped onto its back. He lay low and the creature did not even notice. Jack's plan had worked! As the creature walked along, Jack was able to travel and see the world. Everywhere he went, Jack made sure to be dependable. After his journey was over, Jack crawled off and hopped back home to his pond.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little dog named Spot. Spot had a white ball that he loved to play with every day. One day, Spot's white ball went missing, and he was very sad. Spot looked everywhere for his white ball. He asked his friend, Kitty, "Did you see my white ball?" Kitty said, "No, but I will help you find it." They looked and looked, but they could not find the white ball. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot found a blue ball under a tree. He decided to replace his lost white ball with the blue one. Spot and Kitty played with the blue ball all day and had lots of fun. In the end, Spot learned that even though he missed his white ball, he could still have fun with something new.

Once upon a time, there was a mule named Max. Max lived on a farm with many friends. Max was a serious mule. He liked to do his work and help his friends. One day, Max was walking around the farm when he saw a big tree. The tree had many red apples. Max wanted to eat the apples, but they were too high for him to reach. Max thought of an idea. He would shake the tree. Max went to the tree and started to shake it. The apples began to fall down. Max was happy, and he ate the apples. But then, something unexpected happened. When Max shook the tree, a little bird fell out of its nest. Max was sad and worried about the bird. He wanted to help the bird get back to its nest. Max had another idea. He put the bird on his back and walked to the tree. He shook the tree again, and the bird flew back to its nest. Max was happy that he could help his new friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim did not understand why he could not play with the expensive toy in his crib. He was sad and started to cry. His mom heard him and came into the room. "Why are you crying, Tim?" she asked. Tim pointed at the toy and said, "I want to play with it, but I don't understand why I can't." His mom looked at the toy and then at Tim. She said, "That toy is expensive and can break easily. I will get you a new toy that is better for you to play with." Tim stopped crying and smiled. His mom brought him a new toy that was not expensive and perfect for him to play with. Tim was happy and played with his new toy all day. The conflict was over, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big red stable. In the stable, there was a horse named Brownie. Brownie was anxious. He wanted to go out and play. One day, Brownie was talking to his friend, the cat. "Why are you anxious, Brownie?" asked the cat. "I want to go out and play, but I am scared," said Brownie. The cat told Brownie, "Don't be scared. Let's go out and play together." So, Brownie and the cat went outside to play. They were having fun when a ball came flying and struck Brownie's leg. Brownie was not hurt, but he was surprised. The ball was thrown by a little girl named Lily. She was looking for her lost ball. Lily said, "I'm sorry, Brownie. I didn't mean to strike you with the ball." Brownie was happy that Lily said sorry. They all became friends and played together every day. And Brownie was not anxious anymore.
