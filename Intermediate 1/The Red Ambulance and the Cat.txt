Category: Intermediate
Title: The Red Ambulance and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big red ambulance. It was always alert and ready to help. The ambulance had a nice driver named Tom. Tom loved to help people and make them feel better. One day, the ambulance was driving fast to help a sick boy. On the way, the ambulance saw a cat stuck in a tree. The ambulance stopped and Tom got out. He wanted to help the cat, too. Tom climbed the tree and saved the cat. The cat was happy and Tom felt good. Then, something unexpected happened. The ambulance got tired and needed to rest. Tom was worried. How could he help the sick boy now? But the cat had an idea. The cat called all of its friends, and the animals worked together to push the ambulance. With their help, the ambulance started moving again. Tom smiled and thanked the animals. They all reached the sick boy and helped him feel better. From that day on, Tom, the ambulance, and the animals became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little cow called Beth. Beth was very playful and loved to bounce. Everywhere she went she would bounce up and down, happy and excited. One day, Beth caught sight of a long spell in a nearby tree. She wondered what the spell was but she was too small to reach it. She thought for a moment before she got an idea. She carefully bounced as high as she could and managed to grab the spell! Beth asked the spell, “What can you do?” The spell answered in a whispery voice, “I can make your bounces longer. Would you like to try?” Beth nodded eagerly and the spell began to work. Suddenly Beth’s bounces became much longer! She laughed and bounced around, overjoyed with her achievement. The long spell had helped Beth to bounce higher than ever before. From then on, she was always the bounciest cow in the entire land!

Lily and Sam were playing in the park. They liked to make things with sticks, stones and leaves. They were very creative. They made a house, a car and a cake. "Look at our cake!" Lily said. "It has berries and nuts on top. Do you want some?" "Yes, please!" Sam said. "It looks yummy. Thank you for sharing." They pretended to eat the cake. They made funny noises with their mouths. They laughed and smiled. Suddenly, they saw a big dog. It was brown and furry. It had a long tail and a wet nose. It appeared from behind a tree. It ran towards them. It wanted to play. "Wow, a dog!" Lily said. "Hello, dog. What is your name?" The dog barked. It wagged its tail. It licked Lily's face. She giggled. "Hi, dog. You are friendly. Do you want to play with us?" Sam said. The dog nodded. It sniffed their cake. It looked tasty. It took a bite. "Hey, that's our cake!" Lily said. "You can't eat that. It's not real. It's made of leaves." The dog spit out the leaves. It made a funny face. It did not like the taste. It looked sorry. "It's okay, dog. We forgive you. You didn't know." Sam said. "But you can't have our cake. You can have this stick instead. Do you like sticks?" The dog barked. It liked sticks. It took the stick from Sam. It ran away. It came back. It dropped the stick. It wanted Sam to throw it. "Okay, dog. Let's play fetch. Ready?" Sam said. He threw the stick. The dog ran after it. It caught it. It brought it back. They played fetch for a long time. Lily and Sam had fun with the dog. They were happy. They made a new friend. They forgot about their cake. They did not mind. They had something better. They had a dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a shiny crown that she loved to wear. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. At the park, Lily saw a boy named Tim. Tim was weak and could not balance well. He tried to climb on the seesaw but kept falling. Lily went to help him. She said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you balance." Lily held Tim's hand and helped him climb on the seesaw. They played together and had lots of fun. Tim was happy and thanked Lily for helping him. Now, they both wore their crowns and played together every day.
