Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Firework

One day, a boy named Tom found a weird thing. It was a firework. Tom did not know what it was. He thought it was a toy. He wanted to play with it. Tom showed his mom the firework. She told him it was not a toy. Tom's mom said they could use the firework at night. When it was dark, they went outside. Tom's mom told him to hold the firework. She helped him light it. The firework went up in the sky. It made big, bright colors. Tom was happy. He liked the firework. It was fun to watch. He wanted to find more fireworks. Tom's mom said they could get more for a special day. Tom could not wait for that day to come.

Once upon a time there was a little rabbit that was very hungry. The rabbit was looking for food when it saw a shiny red berry. Excited, the rabbit grabbed it and ate it. But the berry was not food; it was poison! The rabbit became very sick and had to lay down. Suddenly, a fox appeared and saw the rabbit lying there. The fox was very hungry and grabbed the rabbit. But then the fox was scared away by a loud noise. The noise was made by a kind lady who saw the fox trying to take the rabbit. She quickly grabbed the rabbit and brought it to her house. She gave it some yummy carrots and the rabbit started to feel better. When the rabbit was finally better, the kind lady let it go back into the woods. From then on, the rabbit never ate anything before taking a closer look!

One day, a little fish named Tim was swimming near the shore. Tim saw a big rock and wanted to dive. He got ready, took a deep breath, and dove down into the water. As he dove, he saw many colorful fish and plants. Tim met a friendly crab named Carl. Tim was confused and said, "Hi, I am Tim. I am lost. Can you help me find my way back to the shore?" Carl smiled and said, "Sure, Tim! Follow me, and I will show you the way." They swam through the water, passing more pretty fish and plants. Soon, Tim and Carl reached the shore. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Carl, for helping me!" Carl waved goodbye, and Tim swam back to his family.

Jen and Ben were twins who liked to play in the garden. They had a big space with flowers, trees and a swing. They also had a small space with a sandbox and a toy house. Jen liked the small space more, because she could make cakes with sand and pretend to be a chef. Ben liked the big space more, because he could run and jump and swing high. One day, Jen and Ben decided to swap spaces and try each other's games. Jen went to the big space and Ben went to the small space. Jen tried to swing, but she felt scared. She did not like to go too high. She wanted to go back to the small space. Ben tried to make cakes, but he felt bored. He did not like to play with sand. He wanted to go back to the big space. They saw each other and called out. "Jen, I don't like the small space. Can we swap back?" Ben asked. "Ben, I don't like the big space. Can we swap back?" Jen asked. They nodded and ran to each other. They hugged and said sorry. "I'm sorry, Jen. I should have trusted you. You know what you like best," Ben said. "I'm sorry, Ben. I should have trusted you. You know what you like best," Jen said. They smiled and went back to their spaces. They played happily and shared their games. Jen gave Ben a sand cake and Ben gave Jen a flower. They both said "yummy" and laughed. They were happy twins who trusted each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who was only 3 years old. His mommy gave him a special peanut to tie. The little boy quickly tied a big knot with the peanut, but it came undone. He looked around for something else to tie, but there was nothing. Suddenly, the little boy noticed a fake peanut on the ground. He picked it up and tried to tie the knot again. This time, it stayed tied! He smiled in triumph, happy with his work. The little boy was so proud that he ran to show his mommy. When he showed it to her, he boasted, ‘Look mommy! I tied it!’ His mommy smiled and praised him, saying ‘Good job! You are so clever!’ The little boy felt so proud that he ran off to show all of his friends. The little boy had successfully tied a peanut, even though it was fake!
