Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Amy. She liked to sort her toys by texture. Some toys were soft, like her teddy bear. Others were hard, like her blocks. She had a lot of fun playing with them. One sunny day, Amy went to the park with her mom. She saw a new toy on the ground. It had a mild color, not too bright. Amy picked it up and felt its texture. It was not too hard, but not too soft. She liked it very much. Amy took the new toy home and started to sort her toys again. She put the soft toys in one pile, the hard toys in another pile, and her new toy in the middle. She was very happy with her sorted toys and played with them all day long.

Lily and Ben were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They had a big pot, some spoons, and some things from the fridge. "What are we making today, Lily?" Ben asked. "Soup!" Lily said. "We need water, carrots, and cauliflower." "Cauliflower?" Ben said. He did not like cauliflower. It was white and funny-looking. He made a face. "Yes, cauliflower. It is good for you. And it makes the soup creamy," Lily said. She put some water in the pot and turned on the stove. She asked Ben to help her cut the carrots and the cauliflower. Ben did not want to cut the cauliflower. He wanted to cut something else. He looked around and saw a banana. He liked bananas. They were yellow and sweet. He took the banana and peeled it. "Lily, can I put the banana in the soup?" he asked. "No, Ben! Bananas do not go in soup. They are for dessert. Put the banana back and cut the cauliflower," Lily said. "But I do not like cauliflower. It is yucky. Can we make something else?" Ben said. "No, Ben. We are making soup. And you have to try it. Maybe you will like it. It is helpful to try new things," Lily said. She finished cutting the carrots and the cauliflower and put them in the pot. She stirred the soup and smelled it. It smelled good. She poured some soup in two bowls and gave one to Ben. "Here, Ben. Try the soup. It is hot, so blow on it first," she said. Ben looked at the soup. He saw the white bits of cauliflower. He did not want to eat them. But he saw that Lily was eating her soup and smiling. She seemed to like it. He did not want to make her sad. He wanted to be helpful. He took a spoon and blew on the soup. He took a small sip. He was surprised. The soup was not yucky. It was creamy and tasty. He liked the carrots and the water. And he even liked the cauliflower. It was soft and mild. He took another sip. And another. He finished his bowl and asked for more. "Lily, this soup is yummy. Thank you for making it. I like cauliflower now," he said. Lily smiled. She was happy that Ben liked the soup. She was proud of him for trying something new. She gave him more soup and said, "You are welcome, Ben. I am glad you like it. You are very helpful. And you know what? I have a surprise for you. We have bananas for dessert."

Ben and Lily were playing in the park with their pet dog, Max. Max was a big and friendly dog, but he liked to chase things. Sometimes he chased balls, sometimes he chased birds, and sometimes he chased cars. "Look, Max, I have a ball!" Ben said, holding a red ball in his hand. "Do you want to play?" Max barked and wagged his tail. He loved to play with balls. Ben threw the ball as far as he could, and Max ran after it. Lily clapped her hands and cheered. "Good boy, Max!" she said. "Bring the ball back!" Max caught the ball in his mouth and ran back to Ben and Lily. He dropped the ball at their feet and licked their faces. They laughed and hugged him. "Let me throw the ball now!" Lily said, picking up the ball. She threw the ball, but not very far. It rolled onto the road, where there were many cars. "Max, no!" Ben shouted. "That's dangerous!" But Max did not listen. He saw the ball and wanted to get it. He ran onto the road, not looking at the cars. The cars honked and screeched. Ben and Lily were scared. They hoped Max would be okay. A big car stopped just in time, before it hit Max. The driver got out of the car and picked up Max. He carried him back to the park, where Ben and Lily were waiting. "Are you the owners of this dog?" the driver asked. "He almost got hit by my car. He should not run on the road. That's very dangerous." Ben and Lily nodded. They were sorry and thankful. They took Max from the driver and hugged him. "Thank you for saving Max," Ben said. "We're sorry he ran on the road. We won't let him do that again." The driver smiled and patted Max. He gave him the ball and said, "He's a lucky dog. And a brave one. But he needs to be careful. The road is not a place to play. Remember that, okay?" Max barked and wagged his tail. He understood. He was happy to be safe and with his friends. He licked the driver's hand and then Ben and Lily's faces. They laughed and hugged him again. They thanked the driver and said goodbye. Then they played with Max in the park, away from the road. They had fun and were careful. They loved their pet dog, Max.
