Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Mechanic and the Car

One day, a mechanic named Tom was fixing a car. He had a big tool box with many tools. He was working hard to make the car run well. Tom heard a "snap" sound. He looked around and saw a little boy. The boy said, "I'm sorry, I broke your ruler." Tom smiled and said, "It's okay, I can fix it." The boy was happy and thanked Tom. While Tom was fixing the ruler, he found a clear, shiny stone in the tool box. He showed it to the boy, and they both were very surprised. The stone was magic! When they touched it, they could fix anything with just a tap. Tom and the boy became the best fixers in town, and they were always happy to help others.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, a little girl named Sue played with her friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed all day. The sun was shining, and they felt happy. One day, a new boy named Tim came to the park. He was shy and didn't know how to play with others. Sue saw Tim and walked up to him. "Do you want to play with us?" she asked. Tim nodded his head and smiled. As they played, a big wind came and blew their ball far away. Sue, Tim, and their friends looked for the ball, but they couldn't find it. They felt sad and thought their fun day was over. But then, something unexpected happened! A kind squirrel that lived in the park found their ball and brought it back to them. The children were so surprised and happy. They thanked the squirrel, and Sue said, "You can play with us too!" The squirrel nodded and joined their game. From that day on, the park was filled with peace and joy. Sue, Tim, their friends, and the squirrel played together every day. They learned that even when things go wrong, something good can happen if you keep a happy heart.

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo lived in an old tree in the jungle. The tree was very, very old. It was ancient. Momo loved to play and jump around all day. One day, Momo was playing with his friends when he lost his favorite toy. Momo was sad. He asked his friends, "Did you see my toy?" His friends shook their heads. They didn't know where it was. Momo looked and looked for his toy. He looked in the trees, on the ground, and even in the river. Finally, he found his toy under a big leaf. Momo was so happy! He hugged his toy and thanked his friends for helping him. They all played together until the sun went down.

One day, a small boy named Tim found a rough scarf on the ground. He picked it up and thought, "I can give this scarf to my mom!" He was very happy. But then, a big girl named Sue saw Tim and said, "Hey, that's my scarf! Give it back!" Tim was scared, but he did not want to give the scarf back. He said, "No, I found it. I want to give it to my mom." Sue was angry and they started to fight. They pulled the scarf, and it began to rip. They both knew something bad would happen if they kept fighting. Just then, a bird flew down and took the scarf away. Tim and Sue were surprised. They looked at each other and started to laugh. They both said sorry and became friends. They learned that fighting was not good and that they should share. The rough scarf brought them together, and they played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to play in the water. One day, Ducky found a big tub with a faucet. The faucet had easy-to-turn handles. Ducky was very happy. Ducky said, "I want to bathe in the water from the faucet!" So, Ducky turned the handles and water came out. Ducky jumped into the tub and splashed around. Ducky was having so much fun! Then, Ducky's friend, Froggy, came by. Froggy saw Ducky in the tub and said, "Can I bathe with you, Ducky?" Ducky smiled and said, "Yes, come on in!" So, Froggy jumped into the tub too. They both played and splashed in the water. They were very happy.
