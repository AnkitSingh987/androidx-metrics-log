Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Sam

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy car made of metal. Tim loved to play with his car all day long. One day, while playing, the car got stuck in the mud. Tim tried to pull the car out, but it was stuck tight. Tim saw his friend, Sam, walking by. "Sam, help me," said Tim. "My car is stuck in the mud." Sam came over and looked at the car. "You should turn the car this way," Sam said. Together, they turned the car and it came out of the mud. After they got the car out, Tim said, "Thank you, Sam. That was a stupid thing I did." Sam laughed and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all do stupid things sometimes." They both smiled and continued to play with the metal car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to listen to her radio. Every day, she would turn it on and dance to the music. One day, the radio played a funny joke. Mia laughed and laughed. Mia's friend, Tom, came over to play. Tom was upset because he lost his toy. Mia wanted to help Tom feel better. She turned on her radio and waited for the funny joke. When the joke played, Tom listened and started to laugh too. Now, Tom was not upset anymore. Mia and Tom danced to the music and laughed at the jokes on the radio. They played all day and had lots of fun. From that day on, whenever Tom felt upset, Mia would play the radio and they would laugh together.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a big root. A little girl named Amy lived near the tree. She liked to play with the tree every day. One day, Amy met a small, lost bird. The bird was sad. Amy asked, "Why are you sad, bird?" The bird said, "I can't find my mom. I'm lost and hungry." Amy felt bad for the bird. Amy gave the bird a bitter fruit from the tree. The bird ate the fruit and felt better. Then, Amy and the bird hugged each other. They became good friends. Amy helped the bird find its mom, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a three-year old boy who loved to go to the beach. He could watch the waves for hours. One day the boy was running along the shoreline and suddenly he saw a bright light. He cupped his hands around his eyes to get a better look, and he saw something sparkly. He wanted to get it, so he ran closer and tried to grab it, but it was just out of reach. He started to cry and out of the corner of his eye he noticed a wave coming closer. He looked up and saw that the wave had picked up the sparkly object. When the wave reached him, he put out his hands and the wave gently put the sparkly object into his hands. He was filled with light! He tore up the beach searching for more fun things to discover. Eventually he found a shell that was just the same colour as the sparkly object he had found earlier. He was so happy! He ran over to his mum and showed her the sparkly thing and the shell. His mum smiled and said, "What a wonderful day for you!"

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to play with her toy tap. She would pretend to fill cups with water and give them to her stuffed animals. One day, while Mia was playing, her big brother Tom came in. He wanted to play a trick on her. He put something sour in the toy tap. It was lemon juice! He thought it would be funny to see her face when she tasted it. Mia did not know about the trick. She filled a cup with the toy tap and took a sip. Yuck! It was so sour! Mia was sad and did not know why her toy tap was sour. Tom saw her face and laughed. But their mom came in and saw what happened. She warned Tom that it was not nice to play tricks on his sister. Tom said sorry to Mia, and they played nicely together after that.
