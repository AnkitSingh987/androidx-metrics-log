Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Dog and the Loud

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, Spot saw a big, red ball. The ball was very loud when it bounced. Spot liked the loud ball. Spot's mom said, "Stay close, Spot. Do not go too far away." Spot wanted to stay close, but the loud ball was so fun. Spot chased the ball and it went far away. Soon, Spot could not see his mom. She was out of sight. Spot was scared. He wanted to find his mom. He looked and looked until he saw her. She was happy to see him. Spot promised to stay close and never go out of sight again. They played with the loud ball together and had a great day.

Once upon a time there were two friends called Emma and Henry. They were very happy together and would play every day. One day they wanted to make a castle but they only had one pillow. They both wanted to make their own castle, so they started to fight. They kept on fighting but could not agree. Suddenly, they noticed an ancient pillow that had been left behind by someone long ago. Emma and Henry both wanted it, so they started to fight again. Their fight was very noisy and they kept on fighting. Then a wise old man came along and said, "You two should not fight, you should share the pillow". Emma and Henry agreed and hugged each other as they decided to make the castle together. And they all lived happily ever since!

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play together, but sometimes they disagree. One day, they want to play different games. Tom wants to play with cars and trucks. Lily wants to play with dolls and tea sets. They start to argue. Tom says, "Cars are fun. Dolls are boring." Lily says, "Dolls are nice. Cars are noisy." They get angry and sad. They do not want to share or take turns. Mom hears them and comes to see what is wrong. She says, "Why are you fighting? You can play with both cars and dolls. Or you can play something else. There are many games to choose from." Tom and Lily do not listen. They say, "No, we want to play our own games. We do not like the other games." They turn away from each other and cross their arms. Mom sighs. She says, "Okay, then. If you do not want to play together, you can play alone. But you have to stay in your room until you are ready to be nice. You can march to your room now." Tom and Lily do not like this. They do not want to play alone. They do not want to stay in their room. They want to play with Mom. But they are too stubborn to say sorry. They march to their room, stomping their feet. They sit on their beds, playing with their own toys. But they soon find out that it is not fun. It is boring. They miss each other. They miss Mom. They wish they did not disagree. They wish they could play together again.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate dog named Buddy. Buddy had a big head and a kind heart. He loved to help his friends in need. One day, he saw a little bird who was sad. Buddy went to the bird and asked, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I am hungry and cannot find any food." Buddy wanted to help the bird, so he offered to find food for the bird. The bird was happy and said, "Thank you, Buddy!" Buddy searched all day for food, but he could not find any. He was tired and sad. As the sun went down, Buddy knew he had failed his friend. The little bird stayed hungry, and Buddy felt very bad.

Once upon a time, there was a big, reliable dog named Max. Max loved to play with his little boy, Tim. They would run, jump, and scatter things all around the yard. One sunny day, Tim and Max were playing with a ball. They were having so much fun. As they were playing, they saw a new thing in the yard. It was a big box. Tim looked at Max and said, "What is that thing, Max?" Max barked, and they both went to see the big box. They were very curious. When they got to the box, they heard a sound. It was a tiny meow. Tim opened the box, and out jumped a little kitten. Tim and Max were so surprised! They didn't expect to find a new friend in the box. The kitten was happy to see them too. From that day on, Tim, Max, and the kitten would play together, scattering things all around the yard, and having lots of fun.
