Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Bird

One day, a gray cat named Tom went for a walk. The sky was gray too. Tom saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. When he started to climb, he heard a loud thunder sound. Tom was scared but kept going up. Tom met a bird on a branch. The bird said, "Be careful Tom, it will rain soon. You should go home." Tom didn't listen and kept climbing higher. The thunder sound came again, but Tom didn't care. The rain started and the tree got wet. Tom tried to go down but he slipped on the wet branch. He fell down and got hurt. Tom cried, and wished he had listened to the bird.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little dog named Bobo. Bobo had blue spots all over his body. He loved to play and run around with his friends in the park. One day, Bobo saw a big blue ball. He wanted to play with it, but he did not know how. So, he decided to bite the ball. The ball popped with a loud noise, and Bobo got scared. His friends told him that he should not bite things that were not his. Bobo felt sad, but he learned his lesson. From that day on, he never bit anything that was not his. He played nicely with his friends and shared his toys. Bobo became a good dog, and everyone loved him. The moral of the story is to not take or bite things that are not yours.

Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Emma. She was three years old and loved to explore her world. One day, Emma was playing in her garden when something strange caught her eye. She looked closer and noticed it was a small fire. She was amazed! "What is that?" Emma asked. "It's called a fire," said her dad. "Watch as I stir it and it will create smoke." Emma watched with wide eyes as her dad stirred the fire. Then, he added something into the flames. She could see lots of fake smoke coming out of the fire. "Wow!" said Emma. She was so amazed that she just had to take a closer look. She reached out towards the fire. "Oh no!" said her dad, as he quickly pulled her hand away. "That is much too hot. Don't touch it!" Emma nodded, her eyes still wide with amazement. She was fascinated by the fire and its fake smoke.

Once upon a time, there was a famous cat named Tom. Tom was big and liked to play with his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. They lived in a house with a big yard. Every year, they had a race to see who was the fastest. One day, Tom said to Jerry, "This year, I will win the race!" Jerry laughed and said, "We will see, Tom!" They both got ready for the race. They ran and ran, but Jerry was always faster than Tom. Tom wanted to prevent Jerry from winning the race. He thought of a plan. He put a big rock in the path where Jerry would run. When the race started, Jerry ran very fast. But he did not see the rock. He tripped and fell down. Tom ran past Jerry and won the race. Jerry was sad and hurt. Tom felt bad for what he did. He knew it was not right to cheat. But it was too late. Their friendship was broken, and they never raced again.

Once upon a time, there was a very excited dog named Spot. He lived in a big house with a kind family. One day, Spot saw a small door in the house that he had never seen before. He was curious and wanted to see what was behind the door. Spot opened the door and found a little patient mouse named Squeak. Squeak was sad because he could not escape from the small room. Spot wanted to help Squeak, so he came up with a plan to help him get out. Spot and Squeak worked together to escape the room. But when they opened the door, they found a big surprise! The family had set up a fun play area just for them. They were so happy to play together, and from that day on, Spot and Squeak became the best of friends.
