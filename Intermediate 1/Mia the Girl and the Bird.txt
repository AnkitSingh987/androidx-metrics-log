Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Bird

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing with her toys in her room. She heard a big noise outside her window. Mia saw an enormous bird in her yard. The bird was very big and had many colors. Mia went to her mom and said, "Mom, there is a big bird in our yard!" Her mom looked out the window and saw the enormous bird too. She told Mia, "We should lock the door, so the big bird won't come in our house." Later, a stranger came to their door. He was a nice man who lost his big bird. Mia's mom talked to the stranger. "Is this your big bird?" she asked. The stranger said, "Yes, thank you for finding my bird!" Mia and her mom helped the stranger take the big bird home.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a tree with a thick trunk. The tree loved to watch its flowers bloom. Every day, the tree would grow more and more pretty flowers. The tree was very happy. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the tree with the thick trunk and the pretty flowers. The little girl wanted to give the flowers to her mom. She picked some flowers and ran home. The next day, the tree saw something unexpected. The flowers the little girl picked had grown into new trees with thick trunks! Now, there were more trees with pretty flowers. The garden was full of blooming trees, and they all were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big pencil. He loved his pencil very much. One day, Tim wanted to draw a picture. He was very careful with his pencil. Tim drew a big sun in the sky. He drew a big tree too. He was happy with his picture. But then, Tim's pencil broke. He felt sad. Tim sighed and looked at his broken pencil. Tim tried to fix his pencil, but it was too broken. He could not draw anymore. Tim was very sad. He put his broken pencil away and went to bed. His picture was not finished, and he could not draw anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear and a little rabbit. They were good friends. One day, they found a soft cloth in the woods. They both wanted the cloth to keep them warm. The big bear and little rabbit did not know what to do. They both wanted the cloth, but they did not want to fight. The big bear had an idea. "Let's share the cloth," he said. The little rabbit liked the idea. So, they took the cloth and walked slow back to their homes. They took turns using the cloth to keep warm. They were happy because they shared the cloth and stayed friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat tiger who lived in a big jungle. He loved to swing in the trees every day. One day, he decided to take a break and lay down in the grass. Suddenly, he saw a big, shiny jet in the sky. He got so excited that he started to swing in the trees again! The tiger said, "Wow, what a beautiful jet!" The jet replied, "Thank you tiger! I'm so happy you are enjoying my flight!" The tiger thanked the jet and said, "I'll swing in the trees all day and watch you in the sky!" The jet smiled and exclaimed, "That's a wonderful idea! I'll be sure to fly low so you can always see me!" The tiger continued to swing in the trees and watch the beautiful jet fly in the sky. They were the best of friends!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always anxious. He did not like to go outside and play with his friends. One day, his mom told him to go outside and get some fresh air. Tim did not want to go, but he did what his mom said. Outside, Tim saw a big cat. The cat was mean and Tim hated it. The cat looked at Tim and started to chase him. Tim ran as fast as he could, but the cat was faster. Tim was scared and his lungs were tired from running. In the end, the cat caught up to Tim and scratched him. Tim cried and went back inside. His mom saw his scratch and hugged him. She said it was okay to be scared, but Tim should be brave next time. Tim did not want to go outside again. He stayed inside and kept feeling anxious.
