Category: Intermediate
Title: The Huge Tree and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a huge tree. It was so big that all the animals loved to play around it. One day, a little bunny and a squirrel were playing near the tree. The squirrel said, "Let's race to the top of the tree before the sunset!" The bunny agreed, and they started to zip up the tree. They climbed and climbed, helping each other along the way. As they got closer to the top, they saw the beautiful sunset. The sky was full of pretty colors like pink, orange, and yellow. When they reached the top, the bunny and the squirrel were so happy. They looked at the sunset together and smiled. They learned that by helping each other, they could do big things. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can achieve our goals and enjoy the beauty of life.

One day, a boy named Tom found a guitar. The guitar was not old, it was modern. Tom loved to play the guitar. He played it every day. He played it so much that he became very good at it. One day, Tom went to play outside with his guitar. He saw a big tree and sat under it. He played and played. Then, a bird came and sat on the tree. The bird said, "Hi Tom, you play the guitar very well. Can you play a song for me?" Tom said, "Yes, I can play for you." Tom played a song for the bird. While he played, something unexpected happened. The bird started to sing along with the music. The bird's voice was so beautiful that everyone in the park stopped to listen. They all clapped for Tom and the bird. From that day on, Tom and the bird remained best friends and played music for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a little green ball. It was a mysterious ball because it could talk. One day, a boy named Tim found the ball in his yard. Tim was very happy to find the talking ball. Tim asked the ball, "Can I play with you?" The green ball said, "Yes, let's play! You can kick me and I will come back to you." So, Tim and the green ball played all day. Tim would kick the ball and the ball would roll back to him. As the sun began to set, Tim said, "I have to go home now." The green ball said, "Thank you for playing with me. I had fun." Tim smiled and said, "I had fun too. Let's play again tomorrow." And so, Tim and the mysterious green ball became best friends and played together every day.

Ben and Lily liked to play at the beach. They liked to make sand castles and dig holes and splash in the water. They always had fun with their mom and dad. One day, they saw a big red ball on the sand. It looked like a balloon. Ben and Lily ran to the ball and kicked it. It bounced and rolled. They laughed and chased it. But the ball was not a balloon. It was a crab. A big, angry crab. It did not like to be kicked. It snapped its claws and made a loud noise. Ben and Lily were scared. They screamed and ran away. Their mom and dad heard them and came to see what was wrong. They saw the crab and the ball. They understood what happened. They picked up Ben and Lily and hugged them. They said it was okay. They said the crab was just trying to protect itself. They said they should not kick things that are alive. Ben and Lily felt embarrassed. They said they were sorry. They said they did not know it was a crab. They said they would be more careful next time. Their mom and dad smiled and kissed them. They said they loved them. They decided to leave the beach and go home. They said goodbye to the crab and the ball. They hoped they would not meet them again. They learned a lesson that day. They learned to respect the animals at the beach.
