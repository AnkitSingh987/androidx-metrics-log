Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Regular Boy and the Hill

Once upon a time, there was a regular boy named Tom. Tom had a toy skull. He loved to play with his toy skull every day. One day, Tom saw a big hill. He wanted to gain the top of the hill. So, Tom and his toy skull started to climb the hill. It was hard, but Tom kept going. When Tom and his toy skull reached the top, they were very happy. They looked at the pretty view. Tom and his toy skull went back home and played more. They had a fun day.

A small boy named Jack wanted to go on a rafting trip with his family. He was excited to get on the raft and go floating down the river. But when the family arrived at the river, Jack's mom told him he couldn't come. Jack felt sad. He didn't understand why he couldn't go. He asked his mom, "Can I still come?" His mom smiled and said, "No sweetie, this is too dangerous for you. You're too young and too weak to stay on the raft. You need to stay here and wait for me. I promise we'll go rafting again sometime soon." Jack was a bit disappointed, but he knew his mom was right. So he said goodbye to his family and stayed there while they went rafting. He watched them have fun floating down the river and was excited for when he could go on a rafting trip too.

One day, a boy named Tim found a long glove. The glove was magic. Tim put on the glove and felt very happy. He wanted to show his friends the glove, so he went to find them. Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a ball. Tim said, "Look at my long glove!" Sam was amazed. Tim wanted to show Sam what the glove could do. So, he said, "Watch this!" Tim pointed at the ball, and the glove made it shoot into the air. Sam laughed and clapped his hands. Tim and Sam played with the long glove all day. They made the ball shoot up and down, and they had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tim took off the glove and put it away. He couldn't wait to play with it again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named John and Sandy. They went on a walk through a muddy field. As they walked, they heard a voice. It said, "You must not pass". John and Sandy were scared. They stopped to listen. Then the voice said again, "You must not pass". The voice sounded filthy. John said to Sandy, "Let's go back. The voice sounds dangerous." But Sandy said, "No. I want to see who's talking". So they kept walking. Soon they saw a big pig living in the mud. And the pig said loudly, "You must not pass". John and Sandy laughed. Then they said goodbye and went on their way.

Once there was a clever little girl. Her name was Izzy and she was three years old. She loved playing outside in the park with her friends. Today, Izzy and her friends were playing tag. Izzy was very good at the game and won every time. Suddenly, she saw a big cone in front of her. Izzy was very curious and decided to explore it. She peeked in the cone and saw some tasty treats. Izzy wanted to take the treats, but she remembered to behave. Instead, she carefully climbed on the cone. With her clever mind, she figured out a way to get the treats without taking them. She used some of the sticks around to grab them. Her friends cheered for her. The cone was now filled with tasty treats for everyone to enjoy. Izzy's friends were very impressed by her clever thinking. They all grabbed a few treats from the cone and enjoyed a tasty snack together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a quick dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with his friends. One day, while playing, Max saw a small, fearful cat near a big tree. Max wanted to be friends with the cat, so he went to say hello. The cat was scared and did not want to play. Max did not know what to do, so he just gave a little shrug. He thought maybe the cat needed some time to feel safe. After some time, Max tried again to be friends with the cat. This time, the cat was not so scared. They started to play together and became good friends. The other animals saw Max and the cat playing and they all became friends too. The moral of the story is to be patient with others and give them a chance to feel safe. Sometimes, all it takes is a little time and understanding to make new friends.
