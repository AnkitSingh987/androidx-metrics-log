Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben the Kid and the Bowl

One day, a kid named Ben wanted to help his mom bake a cake. He saw a big bowl, a spoon, and some eggs on the table. He thought he could fit the eggs in the bowl and stir them with the spoon. He climbed on a chair and reached for the eggs. "Ben, what are you doing?" his mom asked. She saw him holding an egg in his hand. "I want to help you, Mom. I can fit the eggs in the bowl," Ben said. "Thank you, Ben, but that is not how we make a cake. The eggs will break if you fit them in the bowl. You have to crack them first and then pour them in," his mom explained. "Oh, I see. Can I crack the eggs, Mom?" Ben asked. "Sure, you can. But be careful, the eggs are fragile. Here, let me show you how to do it," his mom said. She took an egg and tapped it gently on the edge of the bowl. Then she opened it and let the egg go in the bowl. "Wow, that looks fun. Can I try?" Ben said. "Yes, you can. Here is another egg. You hold it like this and tap it like this," his mom said. She gave him an egg and helped him crack it. Ben felt happy and useful. He cracked the egg and poured it in the bowl. He smiled at his mom. "Good job, Ben. You are a great helper. Now we can stir the eggs and add some flour and sugar and make a yummy cake," his mom said. "I love you, Mom. And I love cake," Ben said. "I love you too, Ben. And I love your help," his mom said. They hugged and stirred the bowl together.

Once upon a time there was a big tower. It was made out of bricks and stones and it was very tall. One day, the tower's door opened, and a little girl entered. She had curly blonde hair, and she was wearing lots of bright colors. Suddenly, an angry monster came out of the tower. He had big teeth and claws and he was very scary. He roared at the little girl and she was scared, but she didn't want to back down. The little girl put on a brave face and faced the monster. She said, "I'm not afraid of you!", and the monster got even angrier. He waved his claws and growled, but the little girl kept her cool. The little girl smiled and said, "I only came to the tower because I heard there was a secret inside. Do you know where it is?" The monster was surprised by the little girl's courage, so he decided to help her. He told her where to go and she thanked him before going inside the tower. The little girl discovered a secret room that was full of treasures! She grabbed a few items and then ran back out of the tower, leaving the angry monster behind.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a wise old owl. The owl loved to eat his meal at night. One day, he found a mouse and said, "I will eat you for my meal tonight." The mouse was scared, but she had an idea. She said, "Please, wise owl, let me go. I will find you a better meal if you protect me." The owl thought about it and agreed. The mouse found a big, juicy bug for the owl to eat. The owl was very happy and said, "Thank you, little mouse. I will protect you." But then, something unexpected happened! The mouse grew bigger and turned into a cat! The cat said, "Thank you, wise owl, for protecting me. I am a magic cat, and now I will protect you too." The wise owl and the magic cat became best friends. They protected each other and shared their meals in the big forest. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, Tom the rabbit was playing in the garden. His mom came out of the house holding a razor in her hand. Tom noticed the razor, and he asked what it was for. His mom replied: "A razor is for shaving. I'm going to shave my face so I look nice". Tom said: "Can I have one?" His mom replied: "No, it's too dangerous. You might slip and hurt yourself!" Tom felt distant. He knew that he couldn't have a razor. But he thought of a different way! He went and found a stick, and he carved it until it was very smooth. Tom smiled with joy as he held the stick up to his mom. "Mommy!" he exclaimed, "Now I have my own razor!" His mom smiled. She taught Tom an important lesson: it is better to find a creative solution to a problem than to do something dangerous.
