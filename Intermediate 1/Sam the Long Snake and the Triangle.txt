Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Long Snake and the Triangle

One day, a long snake named Sam met a triangle. The triangle was very sad. Sam asked, "Why are you sad, triangle?" The triangle said, "I am sad because I always fall down. I want to stand up tall like you, Sam." Sam had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. We can help each other not fall down." The triangle liked the idea, so they started to play. Sam held the triangle up with his long body, and the triangle held Sam up too. But then, something unexpected happened! A big wind came and blew them both up into the air. They were scared at first, but then they found out they could fly together. Sam and the triangle became the best of friends, and they never fell down again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was very cute and loved going on special adventures. One day, Sarah's mom showed her a lollipop. It was a big, round lollipop with lots of colorful stripes. Sarah was so excited! She saw how fun the lollipop looked, so she asked to try it. Her mom smiled and said, yes. Sarah carefully took the lollipop and licked it. It tasted so sweet! She smiled and spun the lollipop around in her hand. Then, Sarah showed her mom how she could make the lollipop twirl! Her mom was so happy that Sarah was having fun. When Sarah finished the lollipop, her mom gave her a hug. She said, "Sarah, you looked so cute with the lollipop. You really made me smile today." Sarah smiled and said, "I had a lot of fun eating the lollipop with you, Mommy. Let's go on more adventures together!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy telephone that she loved very much. One day, her family had to pack their things and move to a new house. While packing, Lucy could not find her toy telephone. She was very upset. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and behind the sofa, but she could not find it anywhere. At the new house, Lucy's mom found the toy telephone in one of the boxes. She gave it to Lucy, and Lucy was so happy. She played with her telephone all day and was not upset anymore. The End.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park in summer. They run and jump and laugh. They see a big slide. They want to go on the slide. "Me first!" says Ben. He runs to the slide. He climbs up the ladder. He slides down fast. He smiles. "Me next!" says Lily. She runs to the slide. She climbs up the ladder. She slides down fast. She smiles. They take turns on the slide. They have fun. They see a boy. He is sad. He sits on a bench. He has no one to play with. "Who is he?" asks Lily. "I don't know," says Ben. "Let's go and say hi," says Lily. She is kind. She runs to the boy. Ben follows her. "Hi, I'm Lily. This is Ben. What's your name?" says Lily. "Hi, I'm Sam. I'm new here. I have no friends," says Sam. "You can be our friend. Do you want to play with us?" says Ben. "Really? Yes, please!" says Sam. They run to the slide. They climb up the ladder. They slide down fast. They smile. They are friends. They play in the park in summer. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, brown coat that he loved to wear. One day, Tim went outside to play and saw lots of pretty leaves on the ground. Tim decided to collect the leaves. He picked up red, yellow, and orange leaves. He put them all in his big, brown coat. Tim's friend, Sam, saw him and asked, "What are you doing, Tim?" "I am collecting leaves in my big, brown coat!" Tim said. Sam thought it was a fun idea and joined Tim. They both collected as many leaves as they could. When they were done, Tim and Sam looked at all the leaves they had collected. They had so many! They laughed and played, throwing the leaves up in the air. They had a great time with their big, brown coats full of leaves.
