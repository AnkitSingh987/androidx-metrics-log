Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Restless Boy and the Pond

One day, a restless little boy named Tim wanted to feed the ducks at the pond. He asked his mom, "Can we go feed the ducks today?" His mom said, "Yes, but we must be quick. I heard there might be an earthquake soon." Tim and his mom went to the pond with a big bag of bread. They threw the bread to the ducks and the ducks were happy. Tim laughed as the ducks quacked and ate the bread. But he remembered what his mom said about the earthquake, so he kept an ear out for any strange sounds. Suddenly, the ground shook a little. Tim knew it was the earthquake his mom talked about. He said, "Mom, we need to go home now!" They quickly left the pond and went home. When they got home, the earthquake was over and everything was safe. Tim was glad that he listened to his mom and they went home in time.

One day, a prince was in his big castle. He was a very reliable prince. All the people in the town loved him. He always took care of them and helped them when they needed it. One sunny day, the prince walked in the town. He saw a little girl crying. The prince asked, "Why are you crying, little girl?" The girl said, "I lost my toy. Can you take me home?" The prince said, "Yes, I will take you home." The prince took the girl's hand and they walked together. On the way, they found the girl's toy. The girl was very happy. They got to her home and the girl's mom said, "Thank you, reliable prince!" The prince said, "You're welcome!" and went back to his castle. The moral of the story is to always help others when they need it. The unexpected thing was that the prince found the girl's toy on the way home.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was fearful of rain. Yesterday, it rained a lot. Tim stayed inside his house and looked out the window. He saw the rain falling down. Tim's friend, Sam, came to his house. Sam said, "Tim, let's play outside!" Tim said, "No, I am scared of the rain." Sam said, "Don't be scared, Tim. I will show you something fun." Tim and Sam went outside. The rain had stopped. They saw a big, pretty rainbow in the sky. Tim was not scared anymore. He looked at the rainbow and said, "Wow, the rain made something beautiful!" From that day on, Tim was not fearful of the rain anymore.

One day, two friends, Peter and Sarah, were playing by a lake. It was winter and the lake was icy. Suddenly, Peter wanted the handle of Sarah's toy boat. Sarah said no and held onto the handle tightly. Peter was so angry that he started to fight with Sarah for it. He tried to grab the handle away from Sarah but she still wouldn't let go. In the end, the fight ended when Sarah finally agreed to share the handle, and they held it together and played happily with the boat.

One day, a silly cat named Tom found a dish. The dish was round and shiny. Tom liked the dish very much. He wanted to show it to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom went to Max and said, "Look at my dish! It is so shiny!" Max looked at the dish and smiled. "Wow, Tom! That is a nice dish!" Max said. They both liked the dish a lot. Later, Tom and Max saw a bird singing on a tree. They liked the bird's song. After the bird finished singing, Tom and Max clapped their paws together. They were applauding the bird. The bird was happy and sang again. Tom and Max kept applauding and had a fun day with their new dish and bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a big, green bug in his room. It was disgusting! Tim was scared of the bug, but he wanted to be brave. Tim made a promise to himself that he would get rid of the bug. He thought of a plan. Tim took a big deep breath, and he got ready to squeeze the bug. But then, he remembered what his mom said about being kind to all living things. Tim knew he should not hurt the bug. Instead, Tim found a cup and a paper. He put the cup over the bug and slid the paper under it. Then, he took the bug outside and let it go. The bug was happy, and Tim was proud of himself. The moral of the story is to be brave, but also be kind to all living things, even if they look disgusting.
