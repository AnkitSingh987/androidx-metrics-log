Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Apple

One day, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a small stream. The water in the stream was clear and cold. They felt happy. Tim said, "Look, Spot! Let's rush to the stream and play!" They ran fast to the water. They splashed and had fun. The sun was warm and the water felt nice. After playing, they were hungry. Tim's mom gave them a yummy snack. It was a big, red apple. They shared the apple and ate it all. Tim and Spot smiled and said, "Thank you, Mom!"

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, she found a big sunflower in her yard. It was so pretty and yellow. Lily wanted to find something that would match the sunflower. She looked around her room and found a yellow dress. Lily wore the dress and went outside to show her friends. Her friends loved the dress and said it was a perfect match for the sunflower. They all played together, and Lily felt very happy. After playing, Lily's friends went home. Lily looked at the sunflower and thought about her day. She learned that when you share your happiness with others, it makes everyone feel good. And that was the moral of Lily's sunflower story: sharing happiness with others makes the world a brighter place.

Once upon a time, there was a little kid named Tim. Tim was a bald kid with a big smile. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Tim got very dirty while playing in the mud with his friends. "Tim, you need a wash!" said his mom. Tim knew his mom was right, so he went inside to wash himself. He filled the tub with warm water and got into the tub. He washed his hands, his feet, and his bald head with soap. After he was all clean, Tim went back outside to play with his friends. They were so surprised to see how clean and shiny his bald head was. "Wow, Tim! Your head is so clean!" said his friend, Sam. Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I had a good wash!" Then, they all played happily together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Anna. Anna liked to play outside in the sunshine. One day, she saw a twig rolling in the grass. She followed the twig and it rolled all the way to a small pond. By the pond sat an old man. Anna said to the old man "hello, what are you doing?" The old man smiled and said "I'm fishing. Would you like to join me?" Anna was very excited and said "yes please!" The old man taught Anna how to fish. He was so honest with her and taught her all the tricks of being a good fisher. Afterwards, Anna said goodbye to the old man and thanked him for teaching her to fish. She picked up the twig and started to roll it back home. Anna felt proud of herself. She had learned a new skill from the old man and was ready to try it out with her family.

One day, a little crab went for a walk by the sea. The crab was very careless. He did not look where he was going. He was always looking at the sky and the pretty birds. A kind fish saw the careless crab. The fish wanted to show the crab how to be safe. The fish swam up to the crab and said, "Hello, little crab! Let me show you how to be careful." The crab listened to the kind fish. They walked together by the sea. The fish showed the crab how to look around and be safe. The crab was not careless anymore. They became good friends and had fun playing near the sea.

Once upon a time, in a quiet forest, there lived a little lion named Leo. Leo loved to roar very loudly. One day, Leo found a big sheet. He thought it would be fun to play with it. Leo took the sheet and hid behind it. He saw his friend, a small bird named Bella. Leo thought it would be funny to surprise her. So, he jumped out from behind the sheet and roared, "ROAR! Hi, Bella!" Bella was scared at first, but then she saw it was Leo. She laughed and said, "Oh, Leo! You scared me! Let's play with the sheet together." So, Leo and Bella played with the sheet all day long. They had lots of fun and made the quiet forest a happy place.
