Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam the Smart Sheep and the Grass

Once upon a time, there was a smart sheep named Sam. Sam liked to wander and look for yummy grass to eat. One sunny day, Sam went for a walk in the big field. As Sam wandered, he saw his friend, the little bird named Bob. Bob flew down and said, "Hi Sam! Where are you going?" Sam replied, "I'm just wandering around, looking for some tasty grass. Want to come with me?" Bob said, "Yes! Let's go!" Together, Sam the smart sheep and Bob the little bird wandered through the field. They found the yummiest grass and had a fun day. From that day on, they always wandered together, and they were the best of friends.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a shiny thing on the ground. It was a yellow diamond. Lily picked it up and said, "Look, Tom, I found a pretty stone!" Tom wanted the stone too. He said, "Give it to me, Lily, it's mine!" Lily said, "No, Tom, I found it first. You can't have it!" Tom was angry. He pushed Lily and grabbed the stone. Lily fell and hurt her knee. She cried and said, "Tom, you are mean! Give me back my stone!" But Tom did not mind. He ran away with the stone. He did not see the big dog that was barking at him. The dog chased Tom and bit his leg. Tom dropped the stone and screamed. The dog ran away with the stone. Lily and Tom were both sad and hurt. They did not have the stone or a friend. They learned that being greedy and mean is not good.

Once upon a time, there was a new pirate. He lived on a big ship. The pirate liked to move fast on the water. He had a big hat and a pet bird. The bird was his best friend. One day, the pirate saw a big box in the water. He thought it was treasure. The pirate and his bird went to get the box. They put the box on the ship and opened it. Inside the box, they found a big, shiny key. The pirate and his bird were very happy. They thought the key would open a treasure chest. But when they tried the key on every chest, it did not work. The pirate and his bird were sad. Then, the pirate saw a door on the ship. He had never seen it before. The pirate tried the key on the door, and it opened! Inside the room, there was a big, soft bed and many toys. The pirate and his bird were so happy. They found a new room to play in, and it was their best treasure ever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside in the snow. One day, it was very icy outside. Lucy put on her coat, hat, and gloves to play. Lucy saw her friend, Tom, outside. They played and had fun in the snow. After a while, they felt cold and tired. They decided to relax for a minute under a big tree. Lucy and Tom talked and laughed as they rested. Soon, they felt warm and happy. They learned that taking a break is good. When you are tired, it is important to relax, even if just for a minute. This way, you can have more fun and be safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim was very restless. He could not sit still. His mom was a patient lady. She tried to tell him a story to help him calm down. "Tim, do you want to hear a story about a big surprise?" she asked. "Yes, Mom!" Tim said, sitting down to listen. "Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to find surprises. One day, she saw a big, shiny box. She could not wait to see what was inside. She asked her mom if she could open it." Tim listened as his mom told the story. The little girl in the story opened the box, and inside was a small, fluffy dog. The dog jumped out and licked her face. The little girl was so happy! She did not know she would find a new friend in the box. As the story ended, Tim's mom said, "Now, I have a surprise for you too!" She brought out a big, shiny box, just like in the story. Tim's eyes grew wide as he opened the box. Inside was a soft, cuddly teddy bear. Tim was so happy and hugged the bear tight. He had not expected a surprise like the girl in the story. From that day on, Tim was not as restless, and he loved to hear his mom tell stories.
