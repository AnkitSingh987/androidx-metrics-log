Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Friends

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his friends. One day, he wanted to invite his friends to his house for a fun playdate. When his friends came over, they saw a fake toy toilet in the playroom. They thought it was funny and wanted to play with it. They took turns pretending to use the toilet, and they all laughed. After a while, Tim's little sister came into the playroom. She did not know the toilet was fake. She tried to use it and made a big mess. Tim and his friends were sad because they had to clean up the mess. The playdate ended, and everyone went home.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very foolish dog. Yesterday, he saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. Spot started to run after the big red ball. He ran and ran, but he could not catch it. The ball went up a hill and down a hill. Spot was very tired, but he did not give up. At last, Spot caught the big red ball. He was so happy! He took the ball home and played with it all day. Spot learned that even if you are foolish, you can still have fun and be happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very happy because today she would receive a special treat. She loved pasta more than anything in the world. Mia's mom knew how much she loved pasta, so she decided to make it for her. As Mia's mom was cooking the pasta, Mia came into the kitchen and said, "Mom, what is that yummy smell?" Her mom smiled and said, "I am making pasta for you, Mia. I know it is your favorite." Mia's eyes lit up and she clapped her hands. "Yay! I can't wait to eat it!" she said. When the pasta was ready, Mia sat down at the table with her mom. They both enjoyed the delicious pasta together. Mia was so happy that she received her favorite meal. After they finished eating, Mia gave her mom a big hug and said, "Thank you, Mom! I love pasta and I love you!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a sister named Emma. They liked to play together. One day, they went to the park. At the park, they saw a big tree. Lily wanted to climb the tree. She asked her sister, "Can I climb the tree?" Emma said, "Yes, but be careful!" Lily started to climb. She went up and up. Emma watched and smiled. When Lily was done, she came down. The tree was dry and easy to climb. They both laughed and went home. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a pale boy named Tim. Tim liked to pack his toys in a big box. One day, he found a pair of scissors on the table. He thought it would be fun to play with the scissors. Tim's mom saw him with the scissors and said, "No, Tim! Scissors are not for play. They can hurt you." But Tim did not listen to his mom. He kept playing with the scissors and packing his toys. Later, Tim wanted to cut a paper with the scissors. He was not careful and cut his finger. It hurt a lot, and he cried. His mom came and helped him. She said, "I told you not to play with scissors, Tim." From that day on, Tim never played with scissors again. He learned that scissors can be dangerous and should be used with care. And he always listened to his mom after that.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a cheap toy that he loved very much. It was a long rod with a big star at the end. Tim liked to wave it around and pretend he was a hero. One day, Tim and his toy rod went to the park. He saw a small bug in the grass. The bug was scared of a big bird that wanted to eat it. Tim wanted to protect the bug and be its hero. Tim waved his toy rod at the big bird. The bird got scared and flew away. The bug was safe now, thanks to Tim and his cheap rod. Tim felt very happy because he could protect the little bug.
