Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Grass

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They played on the swings and slides. Tim felt dizzy after spinning around on the merry-go-round. He sat down on the grass to feel better. Tim saw a big tree with many apples. He wanted to pick an apple to eat. His mom said, "The tree will yield apples if you shake it." Tim shook the tree with all his strength, and apples fell to the ground. Tim picked up the biggest apple and gave it to his mom with a big smile. His mom was very happy and gave Tim a big hug. They sat on the grass, ate the apples, and enjoyed the rest of their day at the park.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a kind cow named Daisy. Daisy had a patch of white on her head that all the other cows could recognize. Daisy loved to play with her friends in the field. One sunny day, Daisy met a new friend named Betsy. Betsy was a small brown cow with a big heart. Daisy said, "Hi, I am Daisy! I have a white patch on my head. Do you want to play with me?" Betsy smiled and said, "Yes, Daisy! I would love to play with you." Daisy and Betsy played together all day long. They ran, jumped, and laughed in the big green field. They were very happy to be friends. From that day on, Daisy and Betsy always played together, and everyone could recognize their kind friendship.

One day, a boy named Tim found a weird toy. It could shoot little balls. Tim was very happy with his new toy. He went outside to play with it. Tim saw a bin and wanted to shoot the balls into it. But a big dog came and stood in front of the bin. The dog did not want Tim to shoot the balls into the bin. Tim was sad and did not know what to do. Tim tried to shoot the balls anyway, but the dog jumped and caught the balls in its mouth. The dog then ran away with the balls. Tim was very sad and could not play with his weird toy anymore.

One day, a generous cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw a little bird shiver in the cold. Lily felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. "Hello, bird. You shiver. Are you cold?" asked Lily. The bird nodded and said, "Yes, I am cold. I need a warm place to stay." Lily thought for a moment and said, "Come with me. I will take you to my warm home." The bird was happy and followed Lily. When they got to Lily's home, the bird was not cold anymore. The bird said, "Thank you, Lily. You are very generous." They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with a big yard. He loved to play outside, but he did not have any friends to play with. Spot would run and jump all by himself, but he would miss having someone to share his fun with. One sunny day, Spot saw a big cake on a table in the yard. The cake looked so yummy! Spot wanted to eat the cake, but he knew it was not for him. He sat down and watched the cake, hoping that someone would come and share it with him. Soon, a little girl named Sue came into the yard. She saw Spot and smiled. They played together, running and jumping in the sun. When they got tired, Sue said, "Let's eat cake!" Spot wagged his tail and barked happily. They shared the cake, and from that day on, Spot was not lonely anymore. He had a friend to play with and share his fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pretty vase in his house. It was big and blue. He liked to look at it every day. His mom told him, "Do not touch the vase. It can break." One day, Tim was playing with his ball. He threw it high in the air. The ball hit the vase. The vase fell and broke. Tim felt guilty. He was sad and scared. He thought his mom would be mad at him. Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the broken vase. She did not get mad. She said, "Stop crying, Tim. I have a secret." She showed him a new vase. It was even bigger and prettier. The old vase was not special. The new vase was a magic vase. Tim was happy and surprised. His mom was not mad, and now they had a magic vase.
