Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Clever Dog and the Box

One day, a clever dog named Max went to the park. He took his red ball and his spade with him. He loved to dig and play with his toys. The sun was shining, and the park was full of happy kids and animals. Max found a nice spot to sit under a big tree. He started to dig a hole with his spade. He dug and dug, and the hole got bigger and bigger. Soon, he found a small, shiny box. Max was very excited. Max took the box and showed it to his friends. They all thought it was very special. They played with the box, the ball, and the spade all day long. When it was time to go home, Max buried the box again, so he could find it next time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a brave boy who loved to play with his toys. One night, Tim heard a loud roar outside his window. He was scared, but he wanted to see what made the noise. Tim took a candle and went outside. He saw a big lion with a thorn in its paw. The lion roared again. Tim knew he had to be brave to help the lion. So, he walked up to the lion and gently pulled the thorn out of its paw. The lion stopped roaring and looked at Tim with happy eyes. Tim and the lion became best friends. They played together every day. And from that day on, Tim was known as the bravest boy in the village.

Once upon a time, there was a white tent in a camp. The camp was in a big wood. A boy and a girl went to the camp to play. They loved to run and jump in the camp. The sun was out, and the sky was blue. But then, the sky turned gray. It started to rain. The boy and the girl went into the white tent. They did not like the rain. The rain made them sad. They wanted to play outside, but they could not. The rain did not stop. It rained and rained. The camp was wet and muddy. The boy and the girl could not play outside anymore. They had to go home. They were very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big drawer in her room. Inside the drawer were many toys. Amy liked to play with her toys and share them with her friends. One day, Amy's mom said, "Amy, you have been very patient waiting for your new toy. I will buy you one today." Amy was very happy. She wanted her toy collection to increase. Amy and her mom went to the toy store. There were so many toys! Amy picked a toy car. She said to her mom, "I like this car. It will be a good friend for my other toys." Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, it will." When they got home, Amy put the new toy car in the drawer with her other toys. Now her drawer was full of even more fun things to play with. Amy was very happy and played with her toys all day.

Once upon a time, there was a big, modern house. In this house, there was a small box. This box was very special. It was red and shiny. Every day, a little boy named Tim would look at the box and wonder what was inside. One sunny day, Tim decided to open the box. He was very excited. As he opened the box, a tiny cat jumped out! Tim was so surprised. The cat was very fast and began to run all around the house. Tim laughed and started to run after the cat. As they ran, they found a secret door in the house. The cat ran through the door and Tim followed. Inside the secret room, they found a magical land with lots of toys and treats. The cat turned into a big, friendly lion. They played and had fun all day long. Tim and the lion became best friends, all thanks to the special box.
