Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Box

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys and books and puzzles, but sometimes they got bored. One day, they saw a big box in the corner of the room. It was their dad's box of tools and wires and things. They wanted to see what was inside. "Let's crawl to the box and open it," Tom said. "Maybe we can find something fun." "No, Tom, we can't do that," Lily said. "Dad said the box is not for us. It has sharp and dangerous things. We might get hurt or break something." But Tom did not listen to Lily. He was naughty and curious. He crawled to the box and lifted the lid. He saw many shiny and colorful things. He grabbed a strip of metal and pulled it out. It was long and bendy and made a funny noise. "Look, Lily, I found a snake!" Tom said. He waved the strip of metal in the air. "That's not a snake, Tom, that's a wire," Lily said. "Put it back, Tom, it's not a toy. It can shock you or start a fire." But Tom did not listen to Lily. He was naughty and silly. He wrapped the strip of metal around his arm and pretended to be a superhero. He ran around the room and shouted. "Zap, zap, I'm Super Tom! I can shoot lightning from my arm!" Suddenly, he felt a pain in his arm. He screamed and dropped the strip of metal. It had sparks and smoke coming out of it. It had burned his skin and his shirt. He started to cry. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She was scared and sorry. She called for their mom and dad. They came quickly and saw what had happened. They were angry and worried. They took Tom to the bathroom and put some cream on his arm. They threw away the strip of metal and closed the box. They scolded Tom and Lily for being naughty and careless. "Tom, Lily, you should never touch dad's box of tools and wires and things," mom said. "They are not for playing. They are for working. They can hurt you or damage the house. You are lucky you are not more hurt. You could have been electrocuted." "Electro-what?" Tom asked. "Electrocuted means to get a big shock from electricity," dad said. "Electricity is what makes the lights and the TV and the fridge work. But it can also be very dangerous if you don't use it properly. You should never play with wires or plugs or sockets. You should always ask us before you touch anything that has electricity." Tom and Lily nodded. They felt ashamed and sorry. They apologized to their mom and dad. They promised to never touch dad's box of tools and wires and things again. They learned their lesson. They hugged their mom and dad and said they loved them. Mom and dad hugged them back and said they loved them too. They said they were glad they were okay. They said they were proud of them for being brave and honest. They said they hoped they would be more careful and obedient next time. Tom and Lily smiled. They said they would. They went back to their room and played with their toys and books and puzzles. They did not get bored. They had fun. They were happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big slide there. Tim wanted to go on the slide, but he was scared. His mom said, "Don't worry, I will help you." Tim and his mom climbed up to the top of the slide. The view from up there was very nice. They could see the whole park. Tim said, "Wow, I like this view!" Then, Tim and his mom got ready to slide down. As they started to slide, a big, soft pillow appeared at the bottom of the slide. It was not harsh at all. They didn't know where it came from, but it made the slide even more fun. Tim and his mom laughed and said, "That was a fun surprise!" They played on the slide all day long.
