Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and the Frog

Once upon a time, there was a prince. The prince was not happy. He felt dull. He wanted to find a way to get happy. One day, the prince went for a walk. He saw a frog. The frog said, "Hello, prince. Why are you sad?" The prince said, "I am dull. I want to get happy." The frog said, "I can help you. Get a big ball. Play with the ball. You will be happy." The prince got a big ball. He played with the ball. The prince was happy. He said, "Thank you, frog." The frog said, "You're welcome, prince." They played together and were happy.

---

Once upon a time, there was a big red car. The car could drive very fast. A little boy named Tim loved to play with the car. Tim would push the car and watch it go zoom! One day, Tim saw a big scale. He thought it would be fun to drive the car on the scale. So, he put the car on the scale and pushed it. The car went fast, but then it made a terrible noise. The scale broke into many pieces. Tim was very sad. His car was broken, and the scale was broken too. He could not play with his car anymore. Tim learned that driving the car on the scale was a bad idea.

---

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, Lily decided she wanted to organize a trip to the park. Lily told her mom, "Mommy, I want to go to the park today." Her mom said, "Okay, Lily. Let's get ready." Lily put on her shoes, picked up her toys, and put them in a bag. At the park, Lily played with her toys. She had so much fun. She was happy she organized the trip all by herself. When it was time to go home, Lily told her mom, "I want to organize more trips!" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. You did a great job today."

Once upon a time, there was a unique duck who lived by a lake. The duck liked to write with his feet in the mud. He would make shapes and draw his friends. One day, the duck met a big frog. The frog saw the duck write in the mud. The frog wanted to write too. They wrote their names side by side in the mud. The duck and the frog became best friends. They played by the lake every day. They wrote and drew in the mud together. They were happy and loved their unique way of having fun.

One day, a little mouse named Tim found a big piece of cheese in the kitchen. He was very happy and wanted to take the cheese to his family. But the cheese was too big for him to carry. He tried and tried, but he could not move it. Tim had a friend named Sam, who was a deaf dog. Tim went to Sam and asked for help. "Sam, can you help me take the big cheese to my family?" Sam could not hear Tim, but he saw him pointing at the cheese. Sam understood and nodded. Together, they tried to move the cheese. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. The cheese started to roll by itself! Tim and Sam were surprised. The cheese rolled and rolled until it stopped near Tim's family. They were all happy and said thank you to Sam. They shared the cheese and had a big feast.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Mary and Joe. Mary had a nice motion that she wanted to share with Joe and so she recommended it. Joe liked the motion and they both had a lot of fun playing together. But then, Joe got frustrated and decided to make his own motion. Mary saw this and did not like it. She told Joe that her motion was nice and that he should use it instead. But Joe didn’t listen. Suddenly, the motion Joe was making went wrong and everything became a big mess. Mary was very sad and Joe felt bad. In the end, they both learned that it's always better to listen to a friend’s recommendation rather than doing something on their own.
