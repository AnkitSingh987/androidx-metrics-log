Category: Intermediate
Title: The Large File and the Park

One day, there was a large file. It was a bright, sunny day and the file seemed to sparkle in the sunshine. So, the file started to talk. It asked, "Who will take me?" A little girl heard the talking file and was very excited. She stepped closer and asked, "Can I take you?" The file was happy and said, "Yes, please!" So, the little girl took the large file and asked, "Where should I take you?" "Take me to the park," the file replied. The little girl smiled and said, "Ok, let's go!" She took the file to the park, and they had a wonderful day. They talked, laughed and played together. The little girl had a lot of fun with her large file!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. But, she always left her room messy. Her mom asked her to put her toys in order, but Lily did not listen. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, if you don't clean your room, I will wash your toys away." Lily did not want her toys to be washed away, so she started to put them in order. She put her dolls in one place, and her cars in another place. When she was done, Lily saw how nice her room looked. She felt happy and proud. From that day on, Lily always kept her room clean and put her toys in order. The moral of the story is to always keep your things in order and listen to your mom.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to hang a picture on the wall. He needed a nail to do it. Tim asked his big brother, Sam, for help. Sam was very reliable and always helped Tim. They looked for a nail in the house, but they couldn't find one. Tim was sad. Sam told Tim not to worry, they would find a way. They saw a tree with a strong branch outside. Sam had an idea. They could hang the picture on the tree branch! Tim was happy with the idea. They went outside and hung the picture on the tree branch. The picture looked nice, and Tim was happy. They didn't need a nail after all. The moral of the story is that when you have a problem, you can always find a solution if you work together with someone reliable.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a key on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a key!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "Good job, Tim! Let's see if we can find what it opens." Tim and his mom went around the house trying the key in different things. They tried doors, toy boxes, and even the dog's collar. But the key didn't open anything. Tim was sad and wanted to quit, but his mom said, "Don't quit, Tim! We will find what it opens." Just then, Tim's dog, Sam, came into the room. Sam was a very obedient dog and always listened to Tim. Tim had an idea. He showed the key to Sam and said, "Sam, can you help us find what this key opens?" Sam wagged his tail and sniffed the key. Then, he ran to the backyard. Tim and his mom followed Sam to a small box hidden under a bush. Sam wagged his tail and barked. Tim used the key to open the box. Inside, they found a note that said, "You found it! This is a special box for you to keep your treasures in. Love, Dad." Tim smiled and hugged Sam. They had found the secret box, and it was all because they didn't quit.

One day, a little girl named Amy found a box. The box had a big word on it: "Present." She was very happy. She wanted to see what was inside. But the box was tied with a strong rope. Amy tried to open the box. She pulled and pulled, but the rope was too tight. She saw her friend, Tim. She asked, "Tim, can you help me open this present?" Tim said, "I have a useful tool to help you." He took out a small pair of scissors. Tim used the scissors to cut the rope. The rope was now free. They opened the box together. Inside the box, they found many toys. They were so happy and played for many hours. They learned that when you work together, things are easier and more fun.
