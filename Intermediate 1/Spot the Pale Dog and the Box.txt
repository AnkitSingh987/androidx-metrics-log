Category: Intermediate
Title: Spot the Pale Dog and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a pale dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. He liked to run and jump with his friends. Spot had a style of playing that made everyone happy. He was a good dog. One day, Spot saw a big box. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he went to the box and started to peek in. He saw something that looked like fun. It was a big, round ball. Spot thought it would be a great toy to play with. Spot took the ball out of the box and started to play with it. He was having so much fun, but he did not know that the ball was not a toy. It was a big, round bomb. The bomb went off and made a big boom. Spot was not okay. His friends were sad. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a brave boy named Tom. He loved to go on adventures and discover new things. One day, while playing in the woods, he found a big, shiny axe. Tom picked up the axe and decided to use it to help his friends. He saw a big tree with a cat stuck on top. Tom used the axe to cut the tree, and the cat jumped down safely. His friends clapped and cheered for him. Tom felt happy and proud. He knew that he was brave and could help others with his new axe. From that day on, Tom always carried the axe with him, ready to discover more ways to be a hero.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there lived a king and a queen. The palace was very pretty. The king and queen were nice and loved to play with their little cat. One day, a man came to the palace with a big, fake toy cat. He said, "Look at my cat! It can dance and sing!" The king and queen were excited and wanted to see the toy cat dance and sing. The man made the toy cat dance and sing for the king and queen. They laughed and clapped. The little cat saw the toy cat and said, "I can dance and sing too! Watch me!" The little cat danced and sang, and the king and queen loved it even more. The king and queen told the man, "Thank you for showing us your toy cat, but we love our little cat more." The man left the palace, and the little cat was happy. The little cat gained many new friends and played with them all day long. From that day on, the little cat, the king, and the queen lived happily in their palace, playing and having fun together. They knew that real friends were better than any fake toy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to build with her toy blocks. She had many blocks in different colors. Her favorite color was blue. One day, Lily got a new stamp from her mom. The stamp was a picture of a happy sun. Lily liked her new stamp very much. She decided to use her stamp to make her blocks even more fun. Lily stamped the happy sun on all her blocks. She then started to build a big tower with her blocks. The tower had the happy sun on every block. Lily was very proud of her tower. She showed it to her mom, who smiled and said it was the best tower she had ever seen.

Once upon a time, there was a daddy who had two kids. They liked to play but did not like to share. One day, daddy had an idea to help them learn a good lesson. Daddy had a hard rock, and he told the kids to try and break it. They tried, but they could not do it alone. Daddy said, "Now, unite and try again." So, they held hands and worked together. With their teamwork, they broke the rock. The kids learned that when they unite and help each other, they can do hard things. They started to share and play together. And they all lived happily ever after.
