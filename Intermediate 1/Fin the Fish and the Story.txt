Category: Intermediate
Title: Fin the Fish and the Story

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He loved to dive deep into the water and then jump up to make a big splash. Fin was a flexible fish, and he could twist and turn in the water very easily. Fin had a friend named Sam, who was a seagull. Sam loved to surf on the waves and fly high in the sky. Sam saw Fin diving and splashing, and he wanted to try it too. But Sam was not as flexible as Fin, and he found it hard to dive and splash like his friend. Fin saw Sam trying to dive and said, "Sam, you are great at surfing and flying, but diving is not easy for you. We should be happy with what we can do and not try to be like others." Sam listened to Fin and went back to surfing on the waves. He was happy with what he could do, and he didn't try to be like Fin anymore. They played together, each doing what they were good at, and they were both very happy. The moral of the story is to be happy with who you are and what you can do, and not to try to be like others.

One day, a little lamb was playing with his friends in the field. They were jumping over the hay and running around the trees. The lamb was having fun, but he was also very curious. He wanted to see what was behind the big fence. He saw a hole in the fence and decided to go through it. He thought he would find something new and exciting. But he was wrong. He found a big dog that was barking and growling at him. The lamb was scared and tried to run away, but the dog was faster and caught him by his wool. The lamb cried and cried. He wanted his friends and his mom. He wished he never went through the hole. He felt very embarrassed and sorry. The dog was about to bite the lamb, when he heard a loud voice. It was the farmer, who came to see what was the noise. He saw the dog and the lamb and shouted at the dog. He took the dog away and put him in a cage. He picked up the lamb and carried him back to the field. The lamb was still shaking and crying. He saw his friends and his mom waiting for him. They were worried and happy to see him. They ran to him and hugged him. They comforted him and told him it was okay. They said they were sorry he was hurt and scared. They said they loved him and he was brave. The lamb felt better. He thanked the farmer and his friends and his mom. He learned his lesson and never went through the hole again. He was happy to play in the hay and be with his friends. He was not embarrassed anymore. He was proud and grateful.

Once upon a time, there was a funny dog named Spot. Spot had a big nose. He loved to sniff and find things with his nose. His nose could find anything. One day, Spot saw a tall tree. He wanted to see what was up there. Spot tried to stand on his back legs to see the top of the tree. But he was not tall enough. He tried to jump, but he could not jump high enough. Spot felt sad. He wanted to see what was up there so badly. Then, Spot had an idea. He found a big box that was just the right size for him. It was a fit! Spot stood on the box and now he could see the top of the tree. He saw a pretty bird singing a happy song. Spot wagged his tail and felt happy too. The end.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a fake toy cat. He loved to play with it all day. One night, as Tim slept, he had a bad dream. In his dream, he saw a big, scary monster. The monster was a nightmare. In the nightmare, the monster chased Tim. He was very scared. Then, his fake toy cat came to life! It grew big and strong. The cat entered the nightmare to save Tim. The cat fought the big, scary monster. Tim thought his cat would lose. But then, something unexpected happened. The monster became a tiny, cute kitten! Tim and his cat were not scared anymore. They played with the tiny kitten and had fun. Tim woke up and knew his fake toy cat had saved him in his dream. They were best friends forever.
