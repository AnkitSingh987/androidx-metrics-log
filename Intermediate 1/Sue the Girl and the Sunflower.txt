Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl and the Sunflower

One day, a little girl named Sue found a sunflower seed. She was so happy and wanted to plant it. She dug a hole in her yard and put the seed in. Every day, she gave it water and watched it grow. Sue knew that one day, the sunflower would be big and pretty. Sue's friend Tim saw her sunflower and said, "My sunflower is bigger than yours!" Sue looked at Tim's sunflower and saw that it was filthy. She said, "Your sunflower may be bigger, but it is not clean like mine." One hot day, the sun was very strong. Sue's sunflower started to burn because it was so hot. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Tim came over and saw Sue's sunflower burning. He had an idea. Tim took some water and put out the fire on Sue's sunflower. He said, "I'm sorry I was mean before. We can help each other make our sunflowers better." Sue and Tim became good friends and took care of their sunflowers together. They learned that it was better to be kind and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a parrot named Polly. She lived in a big tree. Polly loved to sit on a branch and talk to her friends. One day, she saw a worried cat named Tom. Tom said, "Polly, I can't find my toy. Can you help me?" Polly wanted to help her friend, so she flew around to look for the toy. They looked in the grass, under the tree, and near the flowers. But they could not find the toy. Then, they saw a big dog named Max. Max was playing with Tom's toy. Tom and Polly asked Max to give the toy back. But Max did not want to share. He ran away with the toy and Tom was very sad. The moral of the story is to always share your things with your friends, so everyone can be happy.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there was a sad boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Tim set out on a journey to find a friend. As Tim walked, he met a dog. "Will you be my friend?" Tim asked the dog. The dog wagged its tail and said, "Yes, I will be your friend!" Tim was happy, but they needed a place to play. Tim and the dog found a big tree. "This is a great place to play!" said Tim. But then, they saw a big cat. The cat was mean and did not want them to play there. Tim and the dog were sad. "Please, can we play here?" asked Tim. The cat thought for a moment and said, "Okay, you can play here, but you must be nice to me." Tim, the dog, and the cat all played together and became good friends. From that day on, Tim was never sad again.

One day, a busy mom was cleaning her computer. Her little boy, Tim, was watching her. He wanted to help his mom. "Can I help you, Mom?" Tim asked. "Sure, Tim. You can clean the computer too," said the mom. So, Tim took a cloth and started to clean. He was happy to help his mom. Suddenly, the computer made a funny noise. It started to talk! "Thank you, Tim and Mom, for cleaning me," said the computer. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They did not know that the computer could talk. They all laughed and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Bob. Bob liked to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a small hammer. One day, Bob's mom warned him, "Be careful with the hammer, Bob. You might hurt someone if you are careless." Bob played with the hammer all day. He did not listen to his mom's warning. He was very careless. He swung the hammer around and hit a big tree. The tree started to shake. Then, something unexpected happened. A lot of birds flew out of the tree. They were not happy with Bob. The birds took the hammer away from Bob. They hid it in their nest. Bob was sad, but he learned his lesson. He knew that he should listen to his mom and be careful with his toys.
