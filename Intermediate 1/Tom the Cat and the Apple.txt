Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Apple

One day, a big cat named Tom went on an adventurous walk. He was very tired, so he yawned. He saw his friend, a small dog named Max. Max was looking at a menu on the ground. Max said, "Hi, Tom! I found this menu. It has many yummy foods. But I don't know what to pick." Tom looked at the menu and saw a big, red apple. He said, "Max, let's share the apple. It is good for us." So, Max and Tom went to find the apple. They found it and shared it. They were very happy. The moral of the story is that sharing is caring and good things happen when we help each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom and took his toy car with him. Tim played with his car on the slides and swings. He was very happy. While playing, Tim met a new friend, Sally. They played together, taking turns with the toy car. They laughed and had a lot of fun. But then, Tim lost his toy car. It was not in his pocket or on the ground. Tim was sad, and Sally helped him look for the car. They saw a big tree with unusual leaves. The leaves were blue and shiny. Tim and Sally looked under the tree and found the toy car. Tim was so happy that he hugged Sally. They played with the car until it was time to go home. From that day on, Tim and Sally were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy bow and arrow. One day, Tim wanted to improve how far his arrow could go. Tim thought very hard. He had a brilliant idea! He would use a bigger bow to make the arrow go farther. Tim asked his dad to help him make a bigger bow. With the bigger bow, Tim's arrow went so far! Tim was very happy. He had improved his arrow with his brilliant idea. Tim and his dad spent the whole day playing with the new bow and arrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to study and learn new things. One day, he found a big, open field filled with carrots. Remy was so happy because carrots were his favorite food. Remy saw another rabbit named Rosie in the field. Rosie said, "Remy, we should share these carrots with our friends!" Remy thought about it and said, "Yes, Rosie, that's a good idea. Let's take some carrots to our friends." So, Remy and Rosie took some carrots to their friends. They all enjoyed the tasty carrots together. After they ate, Remy and Rosie told their friends how they found the open field. They all decided to study and learn more about the field, so they could find more carrots together. The moral of the story is that sharing with others makes everyone happy, and studying helps us learn new things.

Ben was a clumsy boy. He often fell down and broke things. His mom and dad were not happy with him. They said he had to be more careful. One day, Ben saw a cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and meowed loudly. Ben wanted to help the cat. He found a ladder and climbed up the tree. He reached for the cat, but he slipped and fell. The ladder fell too. "Help! Help!" Ben shouted. He was hurt and scared. A man heard Ben's cry. He ran to the tree and saw Ben and the cat. He was a fireman. He had a big red truck and a long hose. He used the hose to make a soft cushion of water on the ground. He told Ben to jump. "Don't be afraid, little boy. I will catch you. You are a hero for saving the cat," the fireman said. Ben was surprised. He did not think he was a hero. He thought he was clumsy. But he trusted the fireman. He jumped and landed on the water. The fireman picked him up and gave him a hug. The cat jumped too and rubbed against Ben's leg. "Thank you, thank you!" Ben said. He was happy and proud. The fireman smiled and said, "You're welcome, welcome. You did a good job. You are not clumsy. You are brave and kind." Ben's mom and dad came and saw what happened. They were worried and relieved. They hugged Ben and the fireman. They thanked the fireman for saving their son and the cat. They said they were sorry for being angry with Ben. They said they loved him. Ben smiled and said, "I love you too. And I love the cat. Can we keep him?" The fireman said, "Sure, why not? He needs a home and a friend. And you need a surprise." Ben was very happy. He had a new friend and a new pet. He named the cat Hero. He was not clumsy anymore. He was a hero too.
