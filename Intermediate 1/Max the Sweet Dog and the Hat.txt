Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Sweet Dog and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a sweet dog named Max. Max loved to wear a big red hat. One day, Max went for a walk in the park with his red hat on. In the park, Max saw a little nail on the ground. Max thought it was a toy and picked it up with his mouth. He played with the nail, but then it got stuck in his hat! A kind bird saw Max and wanted to help. The bird flew down and tried to pull the nail out of Max's hat. But as it pulled, the hat got bigger and bigger! Max was so surprised. The bird had turned his hat into a big red umbrella. Now, Max could stay dry when it rained. Max and the bird became best friends and played in the park every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a lucky arrow. He was very happy. He wanted to play with it. Tim saw a big ball in the yard. The ball could bounce very high. Tim thought it would be fun to play with the ball and the lucky arrow. Tim took the arrow and the ball outside. He threw the arrow up in the air and watched it come down. Then, he tried to bounce the ball. The ball went up and up. Tim was having a lot of fun. But then, something bad happened. The lucky arrow fell down and hit the bouncy ball. The ball popped and could not bounce anymore. Tim was sad. He lost his bouncy ball and his lucky arrow did not help him.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They needed to buy a hanger to hang up Sue's pretty dress. It was easy to find the hanger, and Sue was happy. At the store, Sue saw a nice toy she wanted. "Mom, can I have this toy, please?" Sue asked. Her mom said, "Yes, but we must pay for it first." When they went to pay, a little bird flew into the store. The bird picked up the hanger and flew away! Sue and her mom were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They did not need the hanger anymore, but they still had a fun day at the store.

Once upon a time, there was a class full of kids. They liked to play, sing, and learn. One day, a new kid came to class. His name was Sam. Sam had a big sneeze. When he sneezed, it was very loud. The other kids did not like it. They said it was disgusting. Sam felt sad. One day, the class was playing outside. A big wind came and blew dirt into the air. All the kids started to sneeze, just like Sam. They all laughed and sneezed together. Now, they liked Sam's big sneeze. It was not disgusting anymore. They were all friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Tara. Tara liked to pretend. On Wednesday, her mom took her to the park to play. Tara saw a little boy playing alone in the sandbox. She sat down beside him, but he didn't seem to notice her. Tara asked him to play with her, but he stayed silent. His eyes looked so sad. Tara was a bit confused, so she asked her mom why the boy wasn't talking. Her mom said the boy was ignorant and didn't know how to talk or play with other kids. Tara didn't know what to do, so she started to pretend. She pretended that she was a princess, and that the boy was a prince. The boy's eyes lit up, and he started to pretend too. They played together in the sandbox all afternoon!

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her friend Tom's house. Tom's house had a large upstairs with many rooms. Lily and Tom loved to play hide and seek together. Lily said, "Let's play hide and seek, Tom!" Tom was excited and said, "Okay! You hide first, and I will try to find you." So, Lily ran upstairs to find the best hiding spot. She found a big room with a large bed and hid under it. Tom started to look for Lily. He looked in the kitchen, the living room, and then went upstairs. As Tom was about to see Lily under the large bed, he heard a funny sound. It was a talking cat! The cat said, "Hi Tom, I can help you find Lily!" Tom was very surprised but happy to have a new friend. Together, Tom and the talking cat found Lily under the large bed. They all laughed and played hide and seek all day long. From that day on, the talking cat became their best friend, and they had many more fun adventures together.
