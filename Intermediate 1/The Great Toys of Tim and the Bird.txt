Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Toys of Tim and the Bird

One day, a little boy named Tim found a gear in his toy box. He wanted to make something with it. He asked his mom, "Can I make a toy with this gear?" His mom said, "Yes, you can try." Tim tried to make a toy car with the gear, but it was not easy. His friend, Sam, came over and saw him. Sam said, "I can make a better toy than you." This made Tim feel miserable. They both started to make their own toys with gears. While they were making their toys, a little bird flew in through the window. The bird picked up the gear and flew away with it. Tim and Sam were surprised! They stopped fighting and worked together to make a new toy without the gear. In the end, they made a fun toy and played together happily.

One day, a little girl named Amy was very excited. She wanted to go on a march with her friends. They planned to walk around the park with signs. The signs would say nice things to make people happy. Amy made a sign that said "Smile!" She showed it to her friend Tom. Tom said, "I like your sign, but I don't want to march." This made Amy sad. She wanted Tom to be excited too. Amy thought for a moment. She said, "What if we march and then play on the swings?" Tom smiled and said, "Okay, I will march with you!" They marched around the park with their signs, making people happy. After, they played on the swings and had a fun day.

One day, a family went to the park. Mum and Dad were pushing the kids on the swings, and the kids were playing hide and seek. As the family played, someone noticed them - a nosy old lady. She smiled and waved at the family. Dad sighed and said to the kids, "We should probably go now." The kids said goodbye to the old lady and the family went home. On the way home, the kids talked about the nosy old lady at the park. The dad sighed again as he thought about how nosy people can be sometimes. Mum wrapped her arm around Dad and said, "But isn't it nice to know that someone cares about our family?" Dad smiled and agreed. The family went home, tired but happy. Dad and the kids all sighed in contentment.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big basket near a tree. The basket has many fruits and snacks inside. "Wow, look at the basket!" Anna says. "Can we have some?" "I don't know," Ben says. "Maybe it belongs to someone." They look around, but they do not see anyone. The basket looks very yummy and available. "Let's have one apple each," Anna says. "No one will mind." "Okay," Ben says. "But we have to be quick." They run to the basket and grab an apple each. They bite into the juicy fruits and smile. "Yum, yum, yum!" they say. But then, they hear a loud voice. "Hey, you two! What are you doing with my basket?" They turn around and see a man. He looks angry and scary. He is wearing a hat and a coat. He has a big dog with him. "Oh no!" Anna and Ben say. "We are sorry, mister. We thought the basket was available." They drop the apples and run away. They are scared and sorry. They do not want to steal or make trouble. They learn a lesson. They should not take things that are not theirs. They should ask before they have something. They should be careful and respectful.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and make a big mess. One day, Tim found a cup in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to play with the cup. Tim filled the cup with water and carried it to his room. He played with the cup, pouring water on his toys and on the floor. The room became very wet and messy. Tim laughed and continued to play with the water and the cup. Suddenly, Tim felt a sneeze coming. He sneezed so hard that the cup flew out of his hands. The water splashed all over him and the room. Tim's mom heard the noise and came to see what happened. She saw the messy room and the wet Tim. She was not happy. Tim had to clean up the mess and go to bed early.
