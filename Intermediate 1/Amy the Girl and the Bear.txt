Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy bear named Mr. Bear. Amy loved Mr. Bear so much. She would squeeze him tight every night before she went to sleep. Mr. Bear made her feel safe. One day, Amy and Mr. Bear went to the park. They saw a big tree. Amy said, "Mr. Bear, let's climb the tree!" Mr. Bear said, "Okay, Amy. It looks easy. Let's do it!" So, they climbed the tree together. They had a lot of fun. As they climbed down, Amy's foot slipped. She was scared. Mr. Bear said, "Don't worry, Amy. I will help you. Just hold on tight and squeeze my hand." Amy did as Mr. Bear said. They got down from the tree and Amy felt safe again. They went home, and Amy gave Mr. Bear a big hug. They were happy and safe together.

Once there was a family who lived in a small house. They had a simple and ordinary table which they used to eat their meals. One day, the family had a visitor. He was a very special friend who they wanted to make feel welcome. Ma said to the visitor, “Would you like to sit at the table?” The visitor replied, “Yes, I would like that very much.” Ma said, “Let me offer you something to eat.” She brought out some delicious food and set it down on the table. The visitor thanked her and said, “This looks delicious! Thank you for the offer.” Ma smiled and said, “My pleasure! Enjoy your meal.”

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey. The monkey liked to play all day. He would jump, run, and laugh with his friends. But one day, while they were playing, something unexpected happened. The little monkey saw a big ball rolling fast towards them. He was worried. He knew he had to make it stop. So, he thought of a plan. The monkey ran and jumped on the ball. The ball stopped moving. His friends were safe. But then, the ball started to move again! The monkey was not on the ball anymore. His friends laughed and clapped. The ball was not a ball, it was a big, round toy! They all played with the new toy and had lots of fun. The little monkey was happy and not worried anymore.

Lilly was a 3 year old girl who loved to play. One day, she found a dark room in her house. She was curious to find out what was inside. Lilly walked into the room and saw quiz supplies scattered around. She was excited to play with them. But as she picked up the supplies, something happened. The room started to get very dark. It was almost like something did not want her to play there. Lilly was scared, but bravely decided to keep playing. Lilly was able to find some flashlights and tried to light up the room. After a few attempts, she was able to make it bright enough to keep playing. Lilly had a wonderful time playing with the quiz supplies. She didn't know what caused the dark room but was glad she was able to play.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a restless lizard named Liz. Tim and Liz loved to play together in the yard. One day, they saw a big, shiny box near the tree. "Look, Liz! Let's open it!" said Tim. Liz nodded and they both ran to the box. Tim tried to open it, but it was hard. He used his hands to tear the tape on the box. Inside, they found a big, red ball. Tim and Liz played with the ball, having lots of fun. Suddenly, the ball bounced high in the air and hit a tree. The tree started to shake, and many little lizards came out from the tree! They all looked like Liz. Tim and Liz were very surprised. Now, they had many new friends to play with in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a weak frog. He lived near a big pond. One day, the frog saw a big bug. The frog was hungry, but he was too weak to catch the bug. The frog met a friendly bird. The frog said, "Help me, please. I want to eat the big bug, but I am too weak." The bird nodded and said, "I will help you, frog." The bird flew fast and caught the big bug. The bird gave the bug to the weak frog. The frog ate the bug and felt strong. The frog and the bird became best friends. They played together every day by the big pond.
