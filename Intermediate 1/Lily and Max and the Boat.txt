Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Boat

Lily and Max were twins who liked to play in their room. They had many toys, but their favorite thing was the big mattress on the floor. They liked to jump on it, roll on it, and pretend it was a boat or a castle. One day, they had a fight over the mattress. Lily wanted to use it as a boat and sail to a faraway island. Max wanted to use it as a castle and defend it from dragons. They both pulled and pushed the mattress, trying to make it their way. "Stop it, Max! It's my boat!" Lily shouted. "No, it's my castle! Go away, Lily!" Max yelled. They were so loud that their mom heard them from downstairs. She came up to see what was wrong. She saw the twins fighting over the mattress and frowned. "What are you doing, Lily and Max? Why are you fighting?" she asked. They both stopped and looked at their mom. They felt a little ashamed. "We want to play with the mattress, but we want different things," Lily said. "Yeah, we can't agree," Max said. Mom smiled and had an idea. She said, "Why don't you try something unusual? Why don't you share the mattress and play together? You can take turns being the captain of the boat and the king of the castle. Or you can make up a new game that uses both ideas. What do you think?" Lily and Max thought about it. They liked mom's idea. They said, "Okay, mom. We'll try that. Thank you." They hugged mom and then hugged each other. They said, "Sorry for fighting. Let's play nice." They moved the mattress to the middle of the room and started to play. They had fun making up stories and adventures with the mattress. They learned to share and cooperate. They were happy and so was mom.

Once upon a time, a cute little dog named Spot lived in a small house with a big family. One day, the family had to go on a long trip. They needed someone to drive them, so they asked a new friend to help. They wanted to introduce the new friend to Spot, the cute dog. The family said to Spot, "Spot, this is our new friend, the driver. He will help us go on our trip." Spot wagged his tail and barked, "Woof!" The driver smiled and petted Spot. They became friends very fast. Spot could tell that the driver was a nice person. The day of the trip came, and the family got into the car. Spot jumped in, too. The driver started driving, and they all went on a fun adventure. Spot and the driver played games and had fun together. In the end, the family was happy they had the driver as a new friend, and Spot was happy because he had a new friend, too.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing in her room. She had an ordinary toy house with tiny people inside. She liked to make the tiny people talk to each other. She would use her voice to make them sound happy, sad, or mad. Lily heard a sound outside her window. She looked out and saw a big, fluffy cat. The cat was trying to catch a bird. The bird was trying not to disturb the cat. Lily opened the window and said, "Hey, cat! Leave the bird alone!" The cat stopped and looked at Lily. It said in a soft voice, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to disturb you. I was just playing with the bird." Lily smiled and said, "That's okay, but be nice to the bird. It's just an ordinary day, and we should all be friends." The cat agreed and left the bird alone. Lily went back to playing with her toy house. She made the tiny people have a big party to celebrate the ordinary day. The bird flew away, happy that the cat didn't disturb it anymore.

Lily liked to sing. She sang all day long. She sang at home, at school, and at the park. She sang loud and happy. She did not care what others thought. One day, she saw a big bird. The bird had smooth feathers and bright colors. Lily wanted to sing with the bird. She ran to the bird and said, "Hello, bird. Can I sing with you?" The bird did not like Lily. The bird did not like to share. The bird gave Lily a shock. The bird pecked Lily on the nose. Lily cried and ran away. Lily felt sad and hurt. She did not want to sing anymore. She did not want to talk to anyone. She hid in her room. Her mom came to see her. She saw Lily's nose. She hugged Lily and said, "What happened, Lily? Why are you so sad?" Lily told her mom about the bird. She said, "The bird was mean to me. The bird did not want to sing with me. The bird hurt me." Her mom said, "I'm sorry, Lily. The bird was not nice. But you are nice. You have a beautiful voice. You should not let the bird stop you from singing. Singing makes you happy. Singing makes others happy. Singing is a gift. You should share your gift with the world." Lily listened to her mom. She felt better. She said, "Thank you, mom. You are right. I will not let the bird make me sad. I will sing again. I will sing with my friends. I will sing with anyone who wants to sing with me." Lily and her mom smiled. They hugged and kissed. They sang a song together. They sang loud and happy. They did not care what others thought. The moral of the story is: Don't let others discourage you from doing what you love. Be kind and generous with your talents. Sing your heart out.
