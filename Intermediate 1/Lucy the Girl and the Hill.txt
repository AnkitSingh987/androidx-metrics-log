Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Hill

One day, a girl named Lucy and her dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a big hill and wanted to play on it. Lucy and Spot ran up the hill, but Lucy tripped on a broken toy. "Ouch!" she cried. Spot saw Lucy was sad and began to panic. He wanted to help Lucy feel better. Spot ran to find help. He saw a boy named Tom. "Help!" barked Spot, "Lucy is hurt!" Tom followed Spot to Lucy. Tom had some salt with him. He said, "Here, put this salt on your cut. It will help." Lucy put the salt on her cut and it felt better. She smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom!" Now, Lucy, Spot, and Tom could play together on the hill.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with yarn. One sunny day, Fluffy found a big ball of yarn in a basket. She was very happy and started to play with it. As Fluffy played with the yarn, she heard a small voice. It was a little mouse named Squeaky. Squeaky was frightened and said, "Please don't hurt me, Fluffy. I didn't mean to scare you." Fluffy looked at Squeaky and said, "I won't hurt you. Let's play together with the yarn." Fluffy and Squeaky played with the yarn all day long. They had so much fun and became good friends. At the end of the day, Fluffy said, "I forgive you for scaring me, Squeaky. We all make mistakes, but it's important to forgive and be kind to each other." The moral of the story is to always forgive others and be kind.

One day, a dove was flying in the sky. It saw a big tree with rough bark. The dove wanted to spend time on the tree. It flew down and sat on a branch. A big cat was under the tree. The cat saw the dove and wanted to catch it. The dove was scared of the cat. The cat jumped to catch the dove, but it missed. The dove flew away from the tree. It found a new tree that was far from the big cat. The dove spent the day on the new tree, happy and safe from the cat.

One day, a little dog named Spot was in his yard. He saw dark clouds in the sky. Spot started to worry. He knew that bad weather was coming. He needed to find a shelter to stay dry and safe. Spot looked around and saw a big tree. He thought the tree would be a good shelter. But when he got close, he saw a big cat. The cat said, "This is my tree! You can't stay here!" Spot felt sad and kept looking for another shelter. Finally, Spot found a small house. He went inside and met a friendly frog. The frog said, "You can stay here with me!" Spot was so happy. They both stayed in the house until the bad weather went away. Spot and the frog became best friends and never had to worry about finding shelter again.

John was a little boy who had a toy rock. He was very proud of it, because it was reliable and he was sure it would never break. Everyday, he played with it in the garden, pushing and pulling it around. One day, John had an exciting idea. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, I want to complete a race with my rock". Mom smiled and said, "That's a great idea! We can have a race in the garden together". John was so happy, he said, "Come on rock. Let's go complete the race with my mom!". He put the rock in his pocket and they went to the garden. Along the way, they saw lots of flowers and birds. When they got to the garden, they set up the race course. They agreed that the rock was to cross the finish line first to be the winner. John and his mom lined up the starting line, grinning with excitement. John started running, pushing the rock in front of him. When the rock crossed the finish line, everyone cheered. John was very proud, and he hugged his mom, thanking her for helping him complete the race.
