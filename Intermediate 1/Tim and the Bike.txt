Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Bike

Once upon a time, a lonely boy named Tim lived in a small house. Tim was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, his mom asked him to go to the grocery store to buy some food. Tim was excited because he could ride his red bike there. At the grocery store, Tim saw a cute dog. The dog was wagging its tail and seemed to want to play. Tim wanted to be friends with the dog. He bought the food his mom asked for, and then he went to the dog. Tim asked the dog, "Do you want to ride on my bike with me?" The dog barked happily and jumped on Tim's bike. Together, they rode back to Tim's house. When they got home, Tim's mom saw the dog and smiled. She knew the dog would make Tim happy. From that day on, Tim and the dog were best friends. They rode the bike and played every day. Tim was not lonely anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird in a cage. The cage was filthy and the bird was sad. The bird wanted to fly and play with other birds, but it was stuck in the cage. One day, a big cat came to the cage. The cat wanted to eat the bird. The bird was scared and did not know what to do. The cat tried to open the cage, but it could not. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running and barked at the cat. The cat got scared and ran away. The dog was a nice dog and it helped the bird. The dog pushed the cage with its nose and the door opened. The bird was free and happy. It flew away and found a tree with other birds. The bird was safe and could eat and play with its new friends. The dog and the bird became friends and the dog promised to protect the bird from the cat.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pet cat named Daisy. Daisy was an obedient cat. Mia loved to pet Daisy's soft velvet fur. They played together every day. One day, Mia and Daisy saw a big red ball. Mia said, "Daisy, let's play with the ball!" Mia threw the ball, and Daisy went to catch it. But the ball got stuck in a tree. Mia and Daisy looked up at the ball. Mia asked her dad for help. He said, "I will release the ball from the tree." Dad got a long stick and pushed the ball out of the tree. The ball fell down, and Mia caught it. Mia said, "Thank you, Dad!" She hugged her dad and Daisy. Then, Mia and Daisy played with the big red ball all day long. They were very happy.

Once upon a time there was a little bunny. He liked to jog and he was very fast. One day, he was poking around in the grass looking for something to eat. Suddenly, he felt something poke him. He turned around to see a big snake. The snake said, "Hey there, why don't you come over here and have some lunch with me." The little bunny was scared, but he was also very hungry, so he decided to do it. When the bunny came close, the snake disappeared with a wink and a smile. The bunny started to jog away, but it was too late. The snake had caught him! He quickly wrapped himself around the bunny and said, "Now you are my lunch!". The bunny was so scared and cried out for help, but no one was coming. The bunny felt so helpless and sad. He had no way to escape and he knew the snake was going to eat him. In the end, the snake ate the bunny. He was very fast and brave, but it was the end of the little bunny's journey.

Lily and Tom were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were chefs and make yummy food. They had pots and pans and spoons and bowls. They also had some fruits and vegetables that Mommy gave them. "Let's make a soup!" said Lily. "We can include carrots and potatoes and apples and bananas!" "That sounds good!" said Tom. "But how do we cook it? We can't use the stove. It is too hot and not safe for us." Lily thought for a moment. Then she had an idea. "We can use the microwave!" she said. "It is like a magic box that makes things warm. Mommy uses it sometimes." "OK!" said Tom. "But we have to be careful. We have to ask Mommy first and use a plastic bowl. And we have to wait until it beeps and then use oven mitts to take it out." Lily and Tom ran to Mommy, who was in the living room. They told her their plan and asked for permission. Mommy smiled and said yes. She helped them put their soup in a plastic bowl and put it in the microwave. She set the timer and told them to watch and listen. Lily and Tom waited eagerly. They heard the microwave hum and saw the soup spin. They smelled the fruits and vegetables. They heard the beep and saw Mommy take out the bowl with oven mitts. She put it on the table and gave them spoons. "Here you go, my little chefs!" she said. "Enjoy your soup!" Lily and Tom tasted their soup. It was warm and sweet and colorful. They liked it very much. They thanked Mommy and gave her a hug. They felt happy and proud. They had made a soup with their own hands. And they had used the microwave, not the stove, because it was safer.
