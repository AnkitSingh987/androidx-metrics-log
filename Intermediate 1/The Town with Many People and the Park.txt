Category: Intermediate
Title: The Town with Many People and the Park

There was a town with many people. They all wanted to make their town better. They wanted to improve it. They had a big meeting to talk about it. One man said, "We can make a big park with flowers and trees." The people thought it was a brilliant idea. They all worked together and made the park. The town was now more beautiful. The people were happy. They knew they could make their town even better if they worked together.

Once there was a man who went for a walk in the forest. He found a bee perched on a leaf. It was a fierce bee, with a big striped body. The man was excited and he wanted to display the bee to his friends. So he picked up the bee and put it in a jar. Suddenly, the bee became very angry. It started to buzz louder and louder. It seemed to be trying to break the lid of the jar. The man was frightened and he tried to put the lid back on, but the bee kept on buzzing. The man dropped the jar and ran away. The fierce bee had escaped! It flew away far into the sky, never to be seen again. The man was sad because he had lost the bee he wanted to display. He sat down on the ground and cried. The end.

Sara and Ben are friends. They like to play with shapes. Sara has a circle. Ben has a square. They put their shapes on the floor. "Look, my circle is round. It has no corners," Sara says. "My square is better. It has four corners. It is strong," Ben says. They start to argue. They do not want to share their shapes. A big boy comes. He sees the shapes. He takes them. "Hey, give them back. They are ours," Sara and Ben shout. The big boy does not reply. He runs away with the shapes. Sara and Ben are sad. They cry. A teacher comes. She sees them crying. "What is wrong, Sara and Ben?" she asks. "A big boy took our shapes. We were arguing. We did not share," Sara and Ben say. The teacher nods. She has an idea. She takes out a long rope. She makes a circle with it. She puts it on the floor. "Look, this is a big circle. It has no corners. It is round," she says. She takes out a long stick. She makes a square with it. She puts it on the floor. "Look, this is a big square. It has four corners. It is strong," she says. She smiles. She says, "You can both play with the circle and the square. They are different, but they are both good. You can learn from each other. You can have fun together. You can be friends." Sara and Ben look at each other. They feel sorry. They nod. They say, "Thank you, teacher. You are right. We can share. We can be friends." They hug. They play with the circle and the square. They are happy. The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. We can respect and appreciate our differences. We can be friends.

One day, a modest queen lived in a big castle. She liked to make pretty things for her people. She made clothes, toys, and yummy food. The queen was very nice and loved to help others. One day, a little girl named Lily came to the castle. She wanted to talk to the queen. "Hello, Queen," said Lily. "Can you help me make a dress for my doll?" The queen smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! Let's make the dress together." They worked together to make the dress. It was so much fun. When they were done, Lily said, "Thank you, Queen! You are the best." The queen was very happy. She loved to make things for her people, and she loved to see them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a friend named Tom. One day, Lucy got an invitation to a birthday party. Lucy was very excited and told Tom, "I got an invitation! Let's go to the party together!" Tom was sad because he did not get an invitation. Lucy did not want to share her invitation. She was being selfish. At the party, Lucy ate a lot of cake and played games. She did not let Tom join her. Tom gazed at the party from outside the window, feeling very sad and lonely. In the end, Lucy got a tummy ache from eating too much cake. She wished she had shared her invitation with Tom. Now, she was feeling sick and sad, and Tom was still lonely. They both had a bad day.
