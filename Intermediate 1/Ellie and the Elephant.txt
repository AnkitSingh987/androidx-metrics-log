Category: Intermediate
Title: Ellie and the Elephant

Once upon a time, there was a big, friendly elephant named Ellie. Ellie had an enormous toy telephone that she loved to play with. It was her favorite toy, and she would pretend to talk to her friends on it. One sunny day, Ellie was playing with her toy telephone in the mud. She was having so much fun, but her telephone got very dirty. Ellie knew she had to wash it to make it clean again. Ellie took her toy telephone to the river and carefully washed it with water. She scrubbed and scrubbed until it was shiny and clean. Ellie was so happy that her telephone was clean again, and she continued to play with it every day. And from that day on, Ellie always made sure to wash her toys when they got dirty.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved cake more than anything in the world. One day, he heard his mommy talking about a hidden cake in the house. Tim's eyes got big and he knew he had to find it. Tim went on a hunt for the hidden cake. He looked under his bed, behind the couch, and even in the toy box. But he could not find the cake. Tim felt sad and really wanted to eat the cake. Finally, Tim had an idea. He went to his mommy and requested, "Mommy, can you please help me find the hidden cake?" His mommy smiled and took his hand. Together, they found the cake in the kitchen. Tim was so happy, and they both enjoyed the yummy cake.

One day, a little gray monster went for a walk. He was not a big scary monster, but a small and friendly one. He liked to play and have fun with his friends. Today, he wanted to find new friends to play with. As he walked, he saw a little girl. She was scared at first, but the gray monster smiled and said, "I am nice. Let's play!" The little girl was happy and they played all day. They played with a ball, they ran, and they laughed. The gray monster was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The little girl's mom came and saw the gray monster. She was scared and told the little girl to come home. But the little girl said, "No, Mom! The gray monster is my friend!" The mom looked at the gray monster and saw that he was nice. In the end, they all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a humble little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was four years old and loved to play outside with his friends. They liked to throw balls and run around the park, laughing and having fun. One day, Tim found a big, shiny ball. He was so excited to show his friends. They all gathered at the park and took turns to throw the ball high in the sky. The ball would go up, up, up, and then come back down for them to catch. They played with the ball all year long, in the sun and in the snow. As the year went by, Tim and his friends grew older and learned many new games. But no matter how big they got, they always remembered the fun they had with that humble, shiny ball. They knew that simple things could bring them the most joy.

Tom likes to read books. He has many books in his room. He reads them every day. He learns new words and stories. One day, Tom goes to the park with his mom. He brings his favorite book in his pocket. It is a book about animals. He wants to read it under a big tree. At the park, Tom sees a squirrel. The squirrel is hungry. It looks for nuts and seeds. It sees Tom's pocket. It thinks there is food in it. It runs to Tom and jumps on his pocket. "Hey, stop!" Tom says. "That is not food. That is my book!" The squirrel does not listen. It bites Tom's pocket. It tears the book. Tom is sad. He cries. "Mom, mom, help!" Tom calls. "The squirrel ruined my book!" Mom hears Tom. She runs to him. She hugs him. She sees the book. "I'm sorry, Tom," Mom says. "The squirrel did not know. It was hungry. Maybe we can fix the book. Or we can get you a new one." Tom sniffles. He looks at the squirrel. The squirrel looks sorry. It runs away. "OK, Mom," Tom says. "I forgive the squirrel. But next time, I will not bring my book to the park. I will read it at home."
