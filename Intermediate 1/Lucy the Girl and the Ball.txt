Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Ball

One day, a little girl named Lucy was hungry. She went to the fridge to find something to eat. But the fridge was too big, and Lucy was too small. She saw a yummy cake on the top shelf. Lucy wanted the cake, but she could not reach it. She tried to jump, but she was not tall enough. She started to struggle. Lucy's friend, Tom, came into the room. Tom was a tall boy. He saw Lucy struggle and asked, "What is wrong, Lucy?" Lucy said, "I want the cake, but I am too small." Tom smiled and said, "I can help you!" He reached up and got the cake for Lucy. They both felt proud. Lucy and Tom sat down to eat the cake. But when they took a bite, they found out it was not a cake at all! It was a big, round ball of cheese! They both laughed and learned that things are not always what they seem. The moral of the story is that you should not judge something by how it looks. Sometimes, you need a friend to help you see the truth.

One day, a squirrel named Tim found a big, cold nut. Tim was so happy! He wanted to take the nut to his warm home. But, he saw his friend, the rabbit, named Sam. Sam was sad because he was hungry. "Sam, look at this big nut! Let's hurry and eat it together!" said Tim. Sam's face lit up, and they both went to Tim's home to share the nut. In Tim's warm home, they broke the nut and ate it. The nut was yummy and made them feel warm and happy. They played and laughed together all day. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends, always sharing and helping each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to skip and play outside. She had a pretty skirt that she wore every day. Sue was a stubborn girl, and she always wanted to do things her way. One sunny day, Sue met a new friend named Tim. Tim said, "Let's play catch!" Sue said, "No, I want to skip and wear my skirt." Tim said, "Okay, let's skip together!" So, Sue and Tim skipped and laughed together. After playing, Sue learned that it was good to listen to others and try new things. She was still stubborn, but she knew that sometimes, it was better to share and play with friends. Sue and Tim became best friends, and they always had fun together.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing outside. The sky was gray and the wind was strong. Tim said, "I think we should be careful. The wind is very strong today." Sue looked up at the sky and said, "Yes, you are right. Maybe a hurricane is coming. Let's go inside and tell mom and dad." They went inside and told their mom and dad about the strong wind. Dad looked out the window and said, "I suggest we all stay inside today. We can play games and have fun together." While they were playing a game, they heard a loud noise from outside. They looked out the window and saw a big truck. The truck was fixing a broken tree. The tree was not strong enough for the wind. Tim and Sue were happy they were inside and safe. They learned to be careful when the wind is strong and to listen to their dad's suggestions.

One day, a little girl named Sue wanted to build something fun. She thought and thought, and then she had a great idea! She would build a big tower with pastry. Sue went to the kitchen and found some yummy pastry. She started to build the tower on a big plate. The pastry tower was getting taller and taller. Sue was very happy. Then, Sue found some blue paint. She thought it would be fun to make her pastry tower blue. So, she painted it all blue. Sue showed her blue pastry tower to her mom and dad. They all laughed and clapped. Sue was very proud of her blue pastry tower.
