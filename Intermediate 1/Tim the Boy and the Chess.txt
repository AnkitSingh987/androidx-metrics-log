Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Chess

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play chess with his friends. Chess was a fun game with lots of pieces that moved in different ways. Tim wanted to improve at chess, so he practiced every day. One day, Tim felt very hungry while playing chess. His tummy rumbled like a big truck. He told his friend, "I need to eat something before we play more chess. I am too hungry!" His friend said, "Okay, let's go find some food." They went to the kitchen and found some yummy apples and bananas. They ate the fruit and felt much better. Now they were not hungry anymore. They went back to play chess, and Tim played even better than before. Tim was happy that he improved at chess and had a full tummy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was very curious and loved exploring. One day, she spotted a lock on the ground. She picked it up and put it in her pocket. The little girl and her parents were walking in a field. It was very sunny and hot. The ground was dry and the field was empty. The little girl kept the lock in her pocket. The little girl and her parents came across a river. The river was wide and the water sparkled in the sun. The little girl wanted to see what was on the other side so she took out the lock. She dropped it into the water and watched it bob away. After exploring on the other side, the little girl decided to go home. As they were walking, they spotted the lock lying in the middle of their path. The little girl picked it up, dried it off and put it back in her pocket. She was glad that the lock was safe. The little girl and her parents continued on their way home. From then, the little girl's pocket was never empty. She kept the lock wherever she went and never forgot to take it with her.

Sara loves to make cards for her friends. She has many colors of paper, stickers, and crayons. She likes to draw hearts, stars, and flowers on her cards. She writes her name and a nice word, like "love" or "fun". One day, Sara wants to make a card for her friend Tom. She knows that Tom likes cars and trucks. She finds a cute sticker of a red car and puts it on a blue card. She writes "Tom" and "zoom" on the card. She thinks Tom will like her card very much. She puts the card in her backpack and goes to school. She is very happy and excited. She wants to give the card to Tom at lunchtime. She thinks he will smile and say thank you. But when she gets to school, she sees that Tom is not there. She asks her teacher where he is. Her teacher says that Tom is sick and he has to stay at home. Sara feels sad and disappointed. She misses Tom and she wants him to feel better. She thinks of an idea. She asks her teacher if she can mail the card to Tom. Her teacher says yes and helps her write Tom's address on an envelope. She puts the card and a stamp inside the envelope and seals it. She gives the envelope to her teacher, who says she will mail it for her. Sara hopes that Tom will get her card soon. She hopes that he will like it and that it will make him happy. She hopes that he will come back to school and play with her. She thinks that her card is a cute and kind way to say hello.

John and his mommy were playing in the park. John had a red truck and liked to use the bricks in the park to build towers. He built one with three thick bricks, and it was very tall. John's mommy said, "That looks great, John. Let's do one more!" John smiled and put a fourth brick on the tower. It was too thick and the tower soon fell down. John's mommy said, "That's okay, John. We'll do it again tomorrow." John was sad. He started to cry, but his mommy said, "Let's go home now." John had no choice but to leave the park. He was so sad he wouldn't have the chance to do it again tomorrow.
