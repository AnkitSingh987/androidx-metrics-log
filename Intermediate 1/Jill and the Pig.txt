Category: Intermediate
Title: Jill and the Pig

Jill was feeling guilty. She had been given a pig by her grandmother and had promised to take care of it. But she had to leave for vacation with her family and wasn't sure what to do. She asked her dad, "What should I do with the pig, Daddy?" Her dad smiled knowingly. "Leave it with the farmer down the street. He'll look after it while you're away." Jill nodded and gave the pig a big hug before getting in the car. She waved to the pig as they drove away, feeling a little guilty but also relieved to know it was in good hands. When they returned, Jill was delighted to see that the pig had grown much bigger. She thanked the farmer for taking such good care of her pet. The farmer smiled and said, "No problem. That's what friends are for." Jill gave the pig one more big hug before running off to play. She was glad she had left it in the care of the farmer. Even though she had felt guilty before, it had been the right thing to do.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to a stage. The stage was in a big park. Tim saw a distant tree and wanted to go there. He started walking to the tree. On his way, Tim met a small bird. The bird said, "Hi! Where are you going, Tim?" Tim said, "I am going to the distant tree near the stage." The bird said, "I will go with you!" They walked and walked until they reached the tree. Tim and the bird looked at the stage from the tree. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim said, "Let's go back to the stage and play with the other kids!" The bird said, "Yes! Let's start walking back!" And so, Tim and the bird walked back to the stage and played with the other kids all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his rope toy. One day, Spot saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was sad because she lost her ball. Spot had an idea. He said, "Lily, let's imagine we are on a big adventure to find your ball!" Lily smiled and they started to look for the ball. They looked in the park, behind trees, and under benches. Then, something unexpected happened. Spot found a big box with a note. The note said, "Pull the rope!" Spot pulled the rope and the box opened. Inside was not only Lily's ball, but also many new toys for them to play with! They were so happy and played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot was a happy dog who liked to play with his friends. One day, Spot and his friends went to the park. They were excited to run and play. At the park, Spot and his friends saw a new sight. It was a big, colorful ball. They all wanted to play with it. But they began to quarrel about who would play with the ball first. The quarrel made them sad and upset. Then, Spot had an idea. He said, "Let's all play with the ball together!" His friends agreed, and they stopped the quarrel. They played with the big, colorful ball and had a lot of fun. Spot and his friends learned that it was better to share and play together.

Once upon a time, there was a wise lion. He lived in a big forest. All the animals liked the wise lion. They knew he was very smart and kind. One day, a little bird came to the wise lion. The bird was very sad. "Wise lion, I lost my family. Can you help me find them?" the bird asked. The wise lion thought for a moment and said, "I will help you, little bird." They looked and looked. Soon, they found the bird's family. The little bird was so happy! The wise lion realized that helping others made him happy too. All the animals in the forest learned from the wise lion. They helped each other and were happy. The wise lion taught them a good lesson: when you help others, you will be happy too.
