Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Mighty Mule and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a mighty mule named Max. Max lived on a farm with many other animals. Max was very big and strong. He liked to help his friends on the farm. One day, Max and his friends had a big job to do. They had to move a big pile of hay from one side of the farm to the other. Max was excited to help, but some of his friends were not so sure. They thought Max was too big and strong, and they might not be able to keep up with him. Max started to move the hay with his mighty legs. His friends tried to help, but they were getting very tired. Max saw that his friends were tired, so he told them to rest. Max knew that everyone needs to rest sometimes, even mighty mules like him. While his friends rested, Max kept working. He moved the hay all by himself. His friends watched and cheered him on. When Max was done, his friends were so happy and thankful for his help. From that day on, Max and his friends worked together on the farm. They learned that it was okay to rest and ask for help when they needed it. And Max, the mighty mule, was always there to lend a helping hoof.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She liked to observe things around her. She saw a big tree, a pretty flower, and a small bug. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Then, Amy saw a round ball. It was red and blue. She picked it up and played with it. She kicked the ball and it went far. She ran to get it and saw an empty box. The box was big and brown. Amy put the round ball in the empty box. She pushed the box all the way home. At home, she showed her mom and dad the round ball and the empty box. They all played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there were two friends; Patty and Jen. Patty had a bowl of milk and Jen was very nervous. She said to Patty, “Should you drop the milk?” Patty said, “Why not?” Jen was still nervous, so she said, “I don’t know. What should I do? How do you drop milk?” Patty put the bowl on the ground and said, “This is how you drop milk.” Jen was still a little bit nervous, but she watched as Patty lightly tipped the bowl of milk and it slowly spilled out onto the ground. After a few seconds, all the milk had been spilled and Patty asked Jen, “See? That was easy!” Jen nodded and said, “Yes. It was easy to drop the milk.” Patty smiled and the two friends giggled together, happy that the milk had been safely dropped.

Once upon a time there was a big, old barn. It had been there for many years. Every day, Farmer Sam would look after the animals in the barn and make sure they were happy. One day, Farmer Sam was feeling very tired. He was ready for a rest. Just then, something special arrived. There, at the barn door, was a puppy! She was small, and cute. Farmer Sam thought she was the most beautiful puppy he had ever seen. He smiled and welcomed the puppy into the barn and gave her a warm cuddle. She made him feel a bit less tired already. From that day on, Farmer Sam and the puppy were the best of friends. They looked after the animals in the barn together, and played games. Farmer Sam was never too tired anymore!

Tom and Lily were playing in the park. They saw a big ladder near a tree. They wanted to climb the ladder and see the birds. "Let's go up the ladder!" Tom said. "OK!" Lily said. They ran to the ladder and started to climb. They were very careful and held on tight. They reached the top of the ladder and looked around. They saw many birds on the branches. They were singing and flying. "Wow, look at the birds!" Lily said. "They are so pretty and noisy!" Tom said. But one bird was not singing or flying. It was sitting on a nest. It was a deaf bird. It could not hear the other birds or the children. The deaf bird saw Tom and Lily. It was scared. It thought they wanted to take its eggs. It opened its beak and tried to peck them. "Ouch, the bird is biting us!" Tom said. "Let's go down the ladder!" Lily said. They quickly climbed down the ladder. They ran away from the bird. They were sad and hurt. "Sorry, bird, we did not want to hurt you!" Tom said. "We just wanted to see you!" Lily said. The bird calmed down. It realized they were not bad. It wished it could hear them and say sorry. It went back to its nest and watched over its eggs.
