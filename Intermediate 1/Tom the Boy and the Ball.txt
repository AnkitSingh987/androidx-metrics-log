Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Ball

One day, a boy named Tom and his loyal dog, Max, went to play outside. They liked to play with a big, round ball. Max would run and catch the ball when Tom threw it. They played for a long time and Max did not seem to get tired. Tom saw a big tire near a tree. He thought it would be fun to roll the tire down a hill. He said to Max, "Let's roll the tire down the hill and see how fast it goes!" Max barked, and they went to the top of the hill. Tom pushed the tire and it rolled down the hill very fast. But, something unexpected happened! The tire hit a big rock and bounced back up the hill. Tom and Max were surprised! They laughed and played with the tire until they were both very tired.

One day, a little girl named Amy felt terrible. She was sad because her favorite bathrobe was old and had a big hole. She loved her bathrobe very much and wanted to make it new again. Amy had an idea. She asked her mom, "Can we restore my bathrobe, please?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, we can try to fix it together." They worked hard to sew the hole and make the bathrobe look nice again. When they were done, Amy was very happy. She wore her bathrobe all day and felt much better.

Molly was very frightened when she opened the cupboard. Inside she could see something moving. She was scared that it would come out, so she called for her mom. Molly's mom came right over and she looked inside. She reached in and carefully removed the little mouse that was in there. It was scared and jumped onto Molly's mom's shoulder. Molly was relieved and started laughing. Her mom had pulled it out so quickly and easily that Molly wasn't scared anymore. Then Molly and her mom both started to laugh together. They put the little mouse outside where it could find its own little home. Molly was so happy that she had been brave enough to open the cupboard. She had learned that there is nothing to be frightened of as long as you are brave.

Jimmy was outside looking at the sky. He had his cap on, and he was taking in every detail of the sky. He heard a voice behind him. He turned around and saw an old man gazing up at the sky. Jimmy was a nosy child, so he asked the old man, “What are you looking at?” The old man smiled and said, “I’m gazing at the stars. They’re so beautiful.” Jimmy was fascinated and asked, “Can I gaze too?” The old man said, “Yes, of course. You just need to take off your cap first.” Jimmy eagerly took off his cap and stared up at the sky. He couldn’t believe the beauty of the stars. The old man smiled at Jimmy and said, “Isn’t it wonderful?” Jimmy nodded and said, “Yes, it is.”

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a big stage. On this stage, people would come to sing, dance, and play games. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the stage with his toy gun. He loved to play and shoot with his toy gun. On the stage, Tim met a little girl named Sue. Sue was playing with her toy car. Tim said, "Hi Sue, want to play shoot with me?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, let's play!" So, Tim and Sue played together, shooting their toy gun and driving the toy car. As they played, a big wind came and blew their toys away. Tim and Sue looked at each other, sad that their toys were gone. But then, a friendly dog came running up to them. The dog had their toys in its mouth! Tim and Sue were so happy. They laughed and played with the dog, now having more fun than ever. The peaceful town's stage had become a place of joy and friendship for Tim, Sue, and their new dog friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim had a nest in a big tree. One day, Tim found a twig for his nest. The twig was very sharp. Tim did not know what to do with the sharp twig. Tim asked his friend, Cat. "What do I do with this twig? It is too sharp," said Tim. Cat looked at the twig and said, "Oh no, don't use that twig. It might hurt you. Let's find another one." Tim and Cat looked for a new twig. They found a soft, nice twig. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, Cat!" But Tim felt embarrassed because he did not know the first twig was sharp. Cat smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We all learn new things." Tim put the new twig in his nest. His nest was now safe and cozy. Tim and Cat played together in the big tree. They were very happy friends.
