Category: Intermediate
Title: Sally the Girl and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to eat blueberries. One day, she went to the park with her mommy. She saw a big blueberry bush and wanted to pick some to eat. Sally's mommy said, "No, we can't pick them. They might be terrible." But Sally insisted. So, her mommy let her pick just one blueberry. Sally picked the biggest one she could find. She took a big bite, and her face turned all funny. The blueberry was terrible! Sally didn't like it at all. She learned to listen to her mommy, and they went home to eat good blueberries from the store. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a big, soft bed in her room. Every night, she would jump into her bed and snuggle under her warm blanket. Sue loved her bed so much. One day, it was very cold outside. Sue's mom said, "Don't go out and spoil your fun by getting cold!" But Sue didn't listen. She went outside and played in the cold air. Soon, her hands and feet felt very cold. Sue ran back inside her house. She was so cold! She went to her room and jumped into her big, soft bed. The warm blanket made her feel better. From that day on, Sue listened to her mom and didn't spoil her fun by playing in the cold.

One day, a little girl named Lily went for a walk in the park. She saw a big tree with lots of fruit on it. The fruit was round and green. Lily wanted to try the fruit, so she picked one from the tree. Lily took a bite of the fruit and made a funny face. "Yuck!" she said, "This fruit is sour!" She didn't like the taste at all. Her friend, Tom, saw her and asked, "What's wrong, Lily?" "I tried this fruit, but it's too sour," Lily told Tom. Tom laughed and said, "That's because it's not ready to eat yet. You have to wait until it's ripe and sweet." Lily and Tom decided to sit under the tree and rest for a while. They talked and laughed until the sun went down. They promised to come back to the tree when the fruit was ripe and sweet. And when that day came, they enjoyed the sweet fruit together.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful cat. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat went to the park to play. At the park, the cat met a dog. The dog said, "Do you want to play with me?" The cat said, "Yes, let's play!" So, the cat and the dog played together. But the cat played too rough. The dog got sad and ran away. The cat felt regret. The cat said, "I'm sorry, dog. I didn't mean to play rough." The dog came back, and they played nicely. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Stuffy. He lived in the forest with his family and was very happy. One day, he got a small scratch on his paw. He tried to ignore it, but it kept hurting. Stuffy's mom saw his hurt paw, and she brought him to the doctor. The doctor put a cozy bandage on Stuffy's paw and said it would make it all better. Stuffy was still a bit worried, so his mom gave him a hug and said it would be okay. Stuffy hugged her back and shrugged. The next morning, Stuffy's paw felt very comfortable. He took off the bandage and could run and play like before! He was so happy he ran around the forest and played with his friends all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. On a bright March morning, Lucy's mom and dad explained to her that today was a special day. They said, "Today is march and it is a special day for all the deaf people in the world." Lucy curiously asked, "Mom, what is deaf?" Her mom smiled and kindly explained, "Deaf means when people cannot hear. We will march together to show that we care about all the deaf people in the world." Lucy eagerly agreed and said, "I want to show them I care too!" Her dad smiled and said, "Let's go march together!" So, Lucy, her mom and her dad all marched together in support of the deaf people in their town. Lucy felt proud to show that she supported all the deaf people in the world and she knew that taking part in the march was a great way to show this.
