Category: Intermediate
Title: Emma the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma liked to play with her toys and her dog, Max. One day, she saw her mom cook in the kitchen. She thought, "I want to cook too!" Emma had an idea. She went outside and found some lumber. It was easy for her to carry. She brought the lumber inside and said, "Mom, can I cook with you?" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, Emma. Let's cook together!" They used the lumber to make a little table. Emma and her mom cooked yummy food on the table. Max watched and wagged his tail. From that day on, Emma and her mom cooked together on their little table made from lumber. They had lots of fun and made many tasty treats. And Max always got a little bite too.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cap. It was a very nice cap. The cap had a big button on it. The cap liked to play with its friends. One day, the cap wanted to play with a ball. The cap tried to attach the ball to the button. The cap pushed and pushed, but the ball would not attach. The cap was sad. But then, a friend came to help. The friend helped the cap attach the ball to the button. The cap was happy. They played with the ball all day, and the cap was very thankful for its friend's help.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird. The bird loved to sing all day long. It lived in a big tree near a house. The people in the house liked the bird, but sometimes it was too noisy. One day, the bird saw some pasta on the ground. It was spring, and the people in the house were eating outside. The bird flew down and picked up the pasta with its beak. It was very happy and started to sing even louder. The people in the house came outside to see what was happening. They saw the noisy bird with the pasta and laughed. They decided to give the bird more pasta to eat. The bird was very happy and sang a thank you song to the people. The people and the bird became good friends, and they all enjoyed the spring together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He saw a pepper on the table. Tim felt hungry and wanted to eat it. He picked up the pepper, but it was very hot. He sighed. Tim's mom saw him and said, "Don't eat the pepper, Tim. It will make you angry." But Tim did not listen. He took a big bite of the pepper. Suddenly, his face turned red and he felt very angry. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's mom started to laugh. "I was just joking, Tim," she said. "The pepper won't make you angry. It's just very spicy." Tim started to laugh too. He realized the pepper didn't make him angry after all.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a gray cap that he loved very much. He wore it every day. One day, Tom saw a girl with a pretty pink cap. "I like your cap," Tom said to the girl. "Thank you," said the girl. "I like your gray cap too." Tom wanted to change his cap to a pink one, just like the girl's. Tom went home and asked his mom, "Can I change my cap to a pink one?" His mom said, "Yes, let's try." So, they put the gray cap in a big tub with pink paint. When they took it out, it was not pink, but green! Tom was surprised, but he liked his new green cap. He wore it every day and showed it to the girl with the pink cap. They both laughed and played together, happy with their own special caps.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a garden. In the garden, he grew big and small plants. Tim loved his garden very much. One day, Tim found an expensive toy in the garden. The toy was shiny and pretty. Tim was very happy. He played with the toy all day and all night. But the next day, something unexpected happened. The toy was gone! Tim looked everywhere in the garden. Then he saw a little squirrel. The squirrel had the expensive toy! The squirrel liked the toy too. So, Tim and the squirrel played with the toy together. They were both happy and had a lot of fun in the garden.
