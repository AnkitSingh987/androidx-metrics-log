Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lisa. She had a toy cat that she loved very much. One day, the cat's tail came off. Lisa was very sad. Lisa's mom saw a long, dull thread on the floor. She thought it could help fix the cat's tail. But the thread was too high for Lisa to reach. She tried to jump and grab it, but she couldn't. Lisa felt more sad. Then, Lisa had an idea. She found a big pillow and put it on the floor. She stood on the pillow and tried again. This time, she could reach the thread! Lisa's mom used the thread to fix the cat's tail. Lisa and her toy cat were happy again, and they both lie down to take a nap.

Once upon a time, there was a big book. The book was about the history of a town. The town had many interesting things. People liked to read the book to learn about the town. One day, a girl named Lily found the book. She wanted to include her own story in the book. Lily loved to play with her dog, Spot, in the park. She thought it would be a fun story to add. Lily wrote her story and put it in the book. Now, the big book had a new interesting story. It was about Lily and Spot playing in the park. The people in the town loved to read Lily's story too.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a helpful man named Tom. Tom loved to skip and play with his friends. One day, Tom found a big axe in the forest. He thought he could use it to help his friends. Tom took the axe and went to his friend's house. His friend, Jim, needed help to cut a tree. Tom wanted to show how helpful he could be, so he tried to cut the tree with the axe. But Tom did not know how to use the axe well. As Tom tried to cut the tree, the axe slipped from his hands. It flew through the air and hit Jim's leg. Jim cried out in pain. Tom was very sad that he hurt his friend. The village people became angry at Tom for being careless with the axe. They told Tom to leave the village and never come back. Tom had to live alone in the forest, feeling sad and lonely, because he could not be helpful to his friends anymore.

Once upon a time there were two twins. Their names were Jack and Jill. They were very tight, like two pieces of the same puzzle. One day, Jack and Jill decided to go for a walk in the park. When it was time to go home, Jack and Jill split up. Jack went one way, and Jill went the other way. They were both a little bit scared, but they were brave. When they got home, they realized they'd split something else too. They had split a piece of bread and a bowl of apples that they had been eating together in the park! They both laughed at their mistake. Jack and Jill were happy to be so tight that they even split things together. From then on, they never split apart again.

Once upon a time, there was a big clock on the wall. It was not a fancy clock, it was a cheap clock. But the clock was very important because it told the time for everyone in the house. One day, the clock started to struggle. It could not tell time anymore. It tried very hard, but the clock hands would not move. Everyone in the house was sad because they did not know what time it was. The people in the house worked together to fix the clock. They took turns and helped each other. After a while, they made the clock work again. The clock was happy to tell time, and everyone in the house was happy too. They all learned that even cheap things can be important and that working together can fix problems.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot saw a big ball. The ball was on a rough path. Spot wanted to find the way to the ball. Spot started to walk on the rough path. He led his friends, a cat and a bird, to the ball. They followed Spot as he showed them the way. The cat and the bird were happy to play with the ball too. When they got to the ball, they all played together. Spot, the cat, and the bird had a great time. They played until the sun went down. Then, Spot led the way back home. They all had fun and slept well that night.
