Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy and the Pillow

Once upon a time, there was a big room with a soft carpet. A little girl named Lucy lived in this room. Lucy was a clumsy girl who liked to play on the carpet all day long. One day, she saw a small chair and thought it would be fun to seat on it. Lucy moved the chair to the carpet and tried to sit on it. But she was clumsy, so she fell off the chair. She was not hurt, but she was sad. Lucy's mom saw her fall and had an idea. She told Lucy to wait and went away for a short time. When Lucy's mom came back, she had a surprise for Lucy. She had a big, soft pillow for Lucy to seat on. Lucy tried the pillow on the carpet and it was perfect for her. Now Lucy could play on the carpet without falling off the chair. She was so happy, and her mom was happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. A little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a faucet by his house. Tim had a big smile on his face. One day, Tim had a fun idea. He turned the faucet on and water came out. He played in the water and got all wet. His mom saw him and said, "Tim, you should not waste water." But Tim did not listen. The next day, Tim turned the faucet on again. But this time, no water came out. Tim was sad. He had no water to play with. His mom told him, "Because you wasted water yesterday, there is no more water today." Tim learned his lesson. He promised to not waste water again.

Once upon a time, there was a hidden camp in the woods. It was a secret place where animals would play and have fun. They would keep their toys and games there, so no one else could find them. One day, a little bunny named Tim hopped to the camp. He saw his friends, a squirrel and a bird, playing with a ball. Tim asked if he could play too. They all played together, laughing and having a great time. As the sun went down, the animals knew it was time to go home. They put their toys away to keep them safe for the next day. Tim, the squirrel, and the bird said goodbye and went to their homes. They couldn't wait to play at the hidden camp again.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree by the ocean. The tree had many friends who lived with it. They were all happy together. One day, a little bird came to the tall tree. The bird was sad. It had lost its way and did not know how to get home. The tall tree and its friends wanted to help the little bird. The tall tree realized it could see very far from its top. It looked around and saw the little bird's home. The tall tree told the little bird where to go. The little bird was very happy and flew back to its home. The tall tree and its friends were happy too. They knew they had done a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a child named Tim. Tim had an enormous toy named Big Bear. They liked to play with each other all day long. One day, Tim and Big Bear went to the park to play with other children. At the park, they saw other children playing with their toys. Tim saw a toy he liked more than Big Bear. He wanted to take the toy away from the other child. Tim tried to snap the toy from the other child's hand. The other child started to cry. Tim's mom saw what happened and told him it was not nice to take toys from others. Tim felt sad for what he did. He gave the toy back to the other child and said sorry. Tim learned that it is important to share and be kind to others. From that day on, Tim and Big Bear played happily with all the children at the park.
