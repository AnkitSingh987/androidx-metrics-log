Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue the Girl Finds the Box

One day, a little girl named Sue went to her room to unpack her bag. She found a box inside. The box was covered in soft velvet. Sue was very happy and excited. She called her mom and said, "Mom, look at this pretty box I found!" Her mom came to see the box. She said, "Sue, that box is not yours. It is bad to take things that are not yours." Sue felt sad and said, "I am sorry, Mom. I did not know it was bad." Just then, the box started to shake. Sue and her mom were very surprised. They opened the box and found a small cat inside! The cat looked up at them and said, "Thank you for finding me. I was lost and scared." Sue and her mom were so happy to help the cat. They learned that even when something seems bad, something good can come from it.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the sea with his mom and dad. They wanted to have fun and play in the water. Tim was very excited, because he loved the sea. As they played near the water, Tim saw a dolphin. The dolphin had smooth skin, but the sea was rough that day. Tim watched the dolphin jump in the air and admired how it played in the waves. Tim wished he could be friends with the dolphin. He waved at it and the dolphin waved back with its tail. Tim and the dolphin played together all day, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a clean, new pair of shoes. He loved the image of the shoes on his feet. But Tim did not know how to tie his shoes. So, he asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you tie my shoes?" Tim asked. His mom showed him how to tie them. Tim tried, but he could not do it. He felt sad. One day, Tim went to play outside with his shoes untied. He tripped on the laces and fell down. Tim's shoes got dirty and his knees hurt. He cried and went back home. Tim learned that he should ask for help when he needs it.

One day, a cheerful little girl named Lucy went for a walk with her mom. They saw a man sitting on a bench, reading a newspaper. Lucy liked the colorful pictures in the newspaper, so she asked her mom if she could look at it too. "Mom, can I look at the newspaper, please?" Lucy asked. Her mom said yes and asked the man if they could borrow the newspaper for a moment. The man smiled and gave it to them. As Lucy was looking at the newspaper, a gust of wind blew it out of her hands. The newspaper flew up into a tree! Lucy and her mom tried to get it down, but it was too high. Just then, a friendly bird flew by and saw the newspaper stuck in the tree. The bird flew up and grabbed the newspaper in its beak. It flew down and gave the newspaper back to Lucy. She was so happy and said, "Thank you, bird!" The bird chirped and flew away. Lucy, her mom, and the man on the bench all laughed and clapped. They didn't expect a bird to save the day!

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Sue. She had a big box with a silver key. Sue loved the key and wanted to find where it would fit. She looked all around her house but could not find a lock for the key. One day, while playing in the yard, Sue found a small door in a tree. She tried the silver key, and it fit! Excited, she opened the door and saw a tiny room with a little tea set. Sue thought she found a secret place for her toys. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse wearing a hat came out of the tree. The mouse said, "Thank you for opening my door! I lost my key and could not get in." Sue was surprised! The silver key belonged to the mouse, not her toys. Sue and the mouse became friends and had a tea party together.
