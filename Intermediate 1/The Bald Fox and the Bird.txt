Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Fox and the Bird

One day, a bald fox went for a walk. He saw a small hole in the ground. The fox wanted to see what was inside. "I wonder if I can fit in there," he thought. The bald fox tried to fit in the hole. He pushed and pushed, but he could not fit. A bird saw the fox and flew down to talk to him. "Why are you trying to fit in that hole?" the bird asked. The bald fox said, "I want to see what is inside. But I can't fit." The bird laughed and said, "Maybe you can find a bigger hole to fit in." The bald fox smiled and said, "Yes, I will find a bigger hole." And they both went on their way, looking for a bigger hole.

Once upon a time, there was a wide room with a big, colorful floor. In this room, there were two friends, a cat and a dog. They liked to play together all day. One day, the cat said to the dog, "Let's play a game. We must remain in the wide room and not go outside." The dog agreed and said, "Okay, I will stay with you and play." They played and played, but soon the dog saw a ball outside the room. He wanted to play with it, but he remembered that he had to remain inside. The cat saw the dog looking at the ball and said, "Don't go outside. We must stay in the wide room." But the dog did not listen. He went outside to play with the ball. The cat remained sad on the floor. Soon, the dog got lost and could not find his way back to the wide room. He was very scared and sad. The moral of the story is, always listen to your friends and follow the rules.

Once upon a time, there was a dancer. She was very organized. Every day, she would close her box of toys and dance in her room. She loved to dance and play. One day, she heard a knock on her door. "Who is it?" she asked. "It's me, your friend!" said a voice from outside. The dancer opened the door and saw a big bear. The bear wanted to dance too, but he was not organized. He left his toys all over the place. The dancer taught the bear how to be organized. They closed their toy boxes and danced together. They had so much fun! The bear learned that being organized made playing more fun. The dancer and the bear became best friends and danced together every day. And they always closed their toy boxes when they were done playing. The moral of the story is: being organized can make playing more fun and help you make friends.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in a big pond. Fin was a lucky fish because he had many friends who played with him. They liked to swim fast, jump high, and play hide and seek in the water. One day, while playing with his friends, Fin saw a big zip on a big bag near the pond. Fin was curious and wanted to see what was inside the bag. He swam close to the bag and used his nose to open the zip. When the zip was open, Fin saw many yummy treats inside the bag. He called his friends to come and see. They all shared the treats and had a fun time. They felt very lucky to find the bag with the yummy treats. After they ate, they played more games and swam around the pond, happy and full.

A little boy named Tim had a big zip bag. He loved to put things in it and take them out. One day, Tim felt sick. His mom gave him medicine to make him feel better. But Tim did not like the taste of the medicine. He had a creative idea to make it taste better. Tim put the medicine in his zip bag. Then, he added some sweet juice and shook the bag. The medicine mixed with the juice. Tim opened the zip bag and took a sip. It tasted much better! But then, his mom saw what he did. She was not happy. She told Tim that he should not mix the medicine with other things. Tim was sad but he understood. Next time, he would take the medicine the right way.
