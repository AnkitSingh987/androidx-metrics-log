Category: Intermediate
Title: The Boy Called Dave and the Momma

Once upon a time, there lived a little boy called Dave. Dave loved to stay up late, even when his momma said he had to go to sleep. One night, Dave was setting up his fort made of pillows and blankets. "I'm gonna stay here all night," he said. But then his momma came in, shaking her head. "No way, Dave! You need to get dressed for school tomorrow." Dave started to pout and said, "But I wanna wear my long fashion dress!" Momma smiled and said, "That's not what moms do, Dave. You need to stay in your pajamas." Dave crossed his arms and replied, "No! I'm gonna stay up all night!" Momma sighed and said, "I'm sorry, Dave, but you have to go to bed. We can talk about the long fashion dress after school tomorrow, okay?" Dave smiled and said, "Okay, Momma." He quickly got dressed and jumped into bed, happy that he would still get to wear his long fashion dress.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an orange cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her friends. They liked to run, jump, and take big steps together. One day, Kitty and her friends found a new place to play. It was a big hill with lots of steps. They were all excited to play there. But, a big dog named Max lived on the top of the hill. Max did not want to share the steps with Kitty and her friends. Kitty and her friends did not want to fight with Max. They wanted to be friends with him. So, they invited Max to play with them. Max was very happy to join them. They all played together on the steps and had lots of fun. From that day on, Kitty, her friends, and Max played together every day. They learned that it is better to share and play together than to fight. And they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: It is better to share and be friends than to fight.

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play in their mom's room. They liked to look at her shiny things and smell her pretty smells. One day, they found a bottle of perfume that was pink and sparkly. Sara wanted to spray it on herself, but Tom said no. "That is mom's perfume. She will be mad if we use it. It is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who go to parties and dance," Tom said. Sara did not listen. She thought Tom was stupid and boring. She wanted to feel like a grown-up. She took the bottle and sprayed it all over her dress and hair. She smiled and said, "Now I smell like mom. I am so pretty and fancy." Tom was scared. He knew mom would come back soon and see what Sara did. He tried to take the bottle from her, but Sara ran away. She ran to the living room, where dad was reading a book. She wanted to show him how pretty she was. But dad did not think she was pretty. He smelled the perfume and made a face. He said, "Sara, what have you done? You smell like a skunk. You used mom's perfume, didn't you? That was very naughty. Mom will be very upset." Sara felt bad. She realized she made a mistake. She wished she listened to Tom. She started to cry and said, "I'm sorry, dad. I just wanted to be like mom. I didn't mean to be naughty." Dad sighed and hugged her. He said, "I know, sweetie. But you don't need perfume to be like mom. You are already beautiful and smart and kind, just like her. Perfume is not for kids. It is for grown-ups who know how to use it. Come on, let's go wash it off and say sorry to mom." Sara nodded and followed dad. She hoped mom would forgive her. She learned her lesson. She would never use perfume again. She would rather play with Tom and his toys. They were more fun and less smelly.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to try new things. She thought trying new things was very interesting. One day, Sue saw a big curtain in her house. She had never seen it before and wanted to play with it. Sue walked up to the curtain and pulled on it. The curtain opened, and she saw a big room. The room was full of toys and games. Sue thought it was the most interesting place she had ever seen. She ran into the room and started to play with all the toys. Sue played in the room all day. She tried every toy and game she could find. She had so much fun trying new things. When it was time for dinner, Sue closed the curtain and went to eat. She was very happy she found the interesting room behind the curtain.
