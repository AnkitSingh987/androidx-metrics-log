Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Ship

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to pretend he was an astronaut. He had a big rocket toy that he played with all the time. Tim was very independent and liked to play by himself. One day, Tim's mom said they were going on a trip. Tim was excited because he could bring his rocket toy. He knew that his rocket would help him have a fun adventure. Tim's mom smiled and told him that he would see something special on their trip. When they got to the park, Tim saw a big surprise. There was a real rocket ship! Tim's eyes opened wide as he looked at the huge rocket. He knew that his toy rocket had led him to this amazing place. Tim felt like a real astronaut and was so happy that his pretend play had come true.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy room. In the room, there was a big pot. The pot was very old and dirty. One day, a little girl named Jane found the pot. She wanted to see what was inside. Jane leaned over the pot and looked inside. She saw a little bug. The bug was sad and said, "Hello, I am a sad bug. I am sad because I have no friends." Jane felt bad for the bug and wanted to help him. Jane took the bug outside and found him new friends. The bug was happy and said, "Thank you, Jane, for helping me find friends." Jane was happy too. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobo. Bobo was cold, so he went to find a warm place to lay. He saw a house with a chimney. Bobo thought the chimney would be a nice, warm spot to lay. Bobo flew near the chimney and said, "Hello, chimney! Can I lay here to stay warm?" The chimney answered, "Yes, Bobo! You can lay here and stay warm with me." So, Bobo laid near the chimney and felt warm and cozy. He was happy to have found a friend in the chimney. They talked and laughed together. From that day on, Bobo and the chimney were the best of friends.

Tom and Anna liked to play in the park. They liked to climb the big rocks and look for shiny minerals. One day, they found a very big and very shiny mineral on top of a rock. They wanted to take it home and show their mom and dad. "Look, Anna, this is the best mineral ever!" Tom said. "It is so big and so shiny!" "Yes, Tom, it is very pretty!" Anna said. "But how can we get it down? It is too high for us." Tom had an idea. He saw a long stick on the ground. He picked it up and said, "I can use this stick to push the mineral down. Then we can catch it and put it in our bag." Anna thought that was a good idea. She said, "OK, Tom, you push and I will catch. Be careful, though. Don't fall." Tom nodded and climbed up the rock. He reached the stick to the mineral and pushed hard. The mineral started to move. Tom pushed harder. The mineral rolled off the rock and fell down. "Anna, catch it!" Tom shouted. Anna tried to catch it, but the mineral was too big and too heavy. It hit her on the head and knocked her down. She cried out in pain. Tom heard her cry and looked down. He saw Anna lying on the ground, bleeding from her head. He saw the mineral next to her, still shiny but now stained with blood. He felt scared and sorry. He had pushed too hard. He had hurt his sister. He wanted to help her, but he did not know how. He tried to climb down the rock, but he slipped and fell. He hit his head on the stick and blacked out. No one saw them or heard them. They lay on the ground, unconscious and bleeding. The sun set and the night came. The park was dark and cold. The mineral was still there, but it did not matter anymore. Tom and Anna had not been successful. They had been foolish. They had been unlucky. They had been hurt.
