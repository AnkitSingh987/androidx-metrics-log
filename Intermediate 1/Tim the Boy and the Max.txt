Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Max

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. One day, while Tim was whistling, he heard a sound from under his bed. He was scared, but he went to look. Tim saw a big, green monster with a broken arm. The monster said, "Hi, I am Max. I am not a bad monster. I just need help with my broken arm." Tim wanted to help Max, so he went to find his mom. Tim's mom helped Max with his broken arm. Max was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim and mom." Tim and Max became best friends. They played and whistled together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to enjoy walks with his best friend, a girl named Lucy. Every day, they would go on a walk together and have so much fun. One day, Lucy took Spot on a walk to a high hill. The hill was very big, and they could see a lot from the top. Spot was so happy to be on the high hill with Lucy, and he wagged his tail. As they walked back home, Spot and Lucy enjoyed playing with a ball. They laughed and had a great time. When they got home, they were tired but happy. They enjoyed their walk and couldn't wait to do it again.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn cat named Tom. Tom loved to catch things. He would catch balls, mice, and even leaves. One day, Tom saw a red ball. It was very hot outside, but Tom wanted to catch the red ball. Tom said to his friend, the dog, "I want to catch the red ball. Can you help me?" The dog said, "Yes, Tom. I will help you catch the red ball." So, Tom and the dog went outside to catch the red ball. They ran and ran, trying to catch the red ball. It was very hot, and they were tired. But Tom was stubborn and did not want to give up. Finally, Tom jumped high in the air and caught the red ball. Tom and the dog were very happy. They went inside to cool down and play with the red ball.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw his shadow on the ground and thought it was a friend to play with. "Hello, shadow!" Tim said. "Do you want to play with me?" The shadow did not say anything, but it followed Tim as he played. They played all day long, and as the sun went down, the shadow grew bigger and bigger. Tim got scared and wanted the shadow to shrink. He ran inside his house and hid under his bed. But the shadow was still there, and it was even bigger now. Tim cried and cried, but the shadow did not go away. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very generous boy. He liked to help people and share his toys. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. While playing, Tim saw an old lady. She was walking with a big bag. Suddenly, she stumbled and dropped her bag. Tim ran to help her. He picked up the bag and gave it back to her. The old lady was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are so kind." The old lady wanted to give Tim a reward. She opened her bag and, to Tim's surprise, it was full of candy! She gave Tim some candy and said, "Share this with your friends." Tim was very happy and shared the candy with everyone. The moral of the story is: always be kind and helpful, and good things will happen to you.

Once upon a time, there was a painter named Tom. Tom was very good at painting. He made brilliant pictures with many colors. People loved his art. One day, Tom wanted to paint a big picture, but he needed a new brush. Tom went to the store to buy a new brush. He met his friend, Sue. Sue said, "I have a gift for you, Tom!" Tom was happy to receive the gift. Sue gave him a big, new brush. Tom said, "Thank you, Sue! This is perfect for my big picture." Tom went home and started to paint with his new brush. He painted a beautiful picture with many colors. When the picture was done, everyone loved it. They said, "Tom, you are a brilliant painter!" Tom was happy and thanked Sue for the perfect gift.
