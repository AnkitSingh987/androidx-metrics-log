Category: Intermediate
Title: The King and the Gift

Once upon a time, there was a king. The king was very brilliant. He lived in a big castle. One day, the king wanted to send a gift to his friend. So, he picked a pretty toy and put it in a box. He asked his helper to take the box to his friend's house. The helper walked and walked until he reached the friend's house. The friend was very happy to get the gift from the brilliant king. They played with the toy and had a fun day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a happy girl who loved to play with her friends. One day, she went to the forest to play with her best friend, a cute bunny named Binky. They played hide and seek, and Lily had so much fun. While they were playing, Lily forgot the way back home. She started to cry and said, "Binky, I don't know how to go back home. I'm scared!" Binky hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Lily. I will help you get back home safe." Binky and Lily walked together, looking for the way home. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Binky said, "I think I remember this tree. Your house is close!" Lily felt happy and safe with her friend. As they walked, they saw a big rock. Binky said, "Yes, I know this rock! Your house is just around the corner!" And just like that, they found Lily's house. Lily hugged Binky and said, "Thank you for helping me get home safe. You are the best friend ever!" From that day on, Lily never forgot the way home. She and Binky played in the forest every day, and they always made sure to stay safe and remember the way back home.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the garden. She saw a cauliflower there. It was a light green color. Mia wanted to pick it and take it home. A little bird was sitting on the fence. The bird saw Mia and tried to warn her. "No, no!" said the bird. "Don't pick the cauliflower!" But Mia did not listen to the bird. She picked the cauliflower and took it home. At home, Mia put the cauliflower on the table. Suddenly, the cauliflower started to grow bigger and bigger! It was not a normal cauliflower; it was a magic cauliflower! Mia and her family were very surprised. They all laughed and played with the big cauliflower.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big sack near the slide. They are curious. They want to see what is inside the sack. "Let's open it!" Ben says. He runs to the sack and pulls the string. Mia follows him. She is alert. She thinks there might be something bad in the sack. The sack opens. Out comes a big, loud, angry dog. It has sharp teeth and red eyes. It wants to bite Ben and Mia. It jumps at them and barks. "Help! Help!" Ben and Mia scream. They are scared. They try to run away, but the dog is faster. It catches them and bites them hard. They cry and bleed. No one hears them. No one helps them. The dog has power. It hurts Ben and Mia. They are in pain. They wish they never opened the sack. They are sorry. They are sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very careful dog. He liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, Spot found a big red ball. He wanted to play with it, but it was tied to a tree. Spot tried to untie the ball, but the knot was too tight. Then, a nice girl named Ann came to help. Ann was very good at tying and untying things. She carefully untied the knot and set the ball free. Spot was very happy and played with the ball all day long.

Sara liked to play with yarn. She had many colors of yarn in her basket. She liked to make things with yarn, like hats and scarves and dolls. One day, she found a new yarn in her basket. It was unusual. It was not one color, but many colors. It was not smooth, but fuzzy. It was not thin, but thick. Sara wanted to examine the unusual yarn. She took it out of her basket and held it in her hands. She felt it and looked at it. She wondered what it was and where it came from. She asked her mom, who was sitting on the couch. "Mom, what is this yarn?" Sara asked. "That is a special yarn, Sara. It is called rainbow yarn. It is made from the wool of a special sheep. The sheep has many colors of wool. It is very rare and very soft. Do you like it?" Mom said. Sara nodded. She liked the rainbow yarn. She thought it was beautiful and cozy. She wanted to make something with it. She asked her mom, who was smiling. "Mom, can I make something with this yarn?" Sara asked. "Of course, Sara. You can make anything you want with this yarn. What do you want to make?" Mom said. Sara thought for a moment. She had an idea. She smiled and said, "I want to make a rainbow scarf for you, Mom. You are special, like the rainbow yarn. And you are always warm and soft, like the rainbow yarn. I love you, Mom." Mom hugged Sara and said, "I love you too, Sara. And I would love a rainbow scarf from you. You are very sweet and creative, Sara. You make me happy." Sara and Mom smiled and kissed. Then Sara took the rainbow yarn and her needles and started to make the rainbow scarf. She was happy. She liked to play with yarn. She liked to make things with yarn. She liked to make things for Mom.
