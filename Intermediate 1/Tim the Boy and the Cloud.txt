Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cloud

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived near a big temple. One day, he saw a dark cloud coming to the temple. Tim ran to his friend, Sue. "Sue, can I borrow your umbrella?" he asked. Sue said, "Yes, you can borrow it, but please bring it back." Tim took the umbrella and ran to the temple. The dark cloud was getting closer. Tim was scared, but he wanted to save the temple from the rain. As the rain started to fall, Tim opened the umbrella and held it over the temple. The rain stopped, and the dark cloud went away. Tim and Sue were happy, and they played together in the sun.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there was a dependable king. The king had a young son named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy horse and his ball. One day, Tom was playing with his ball near the palace walls. The ball went far away and Tom ran to get it. As he picked up the ball, he saw a big cat. The cat looked mad and wanted to slap Tom. Tom was scared and ran back to the palace. Tom told his dad, the king, about the big cat. The king said, "Do not worry, Tom. I will keep you safe." The king went outside and told the cat to go away. The cat listened to the king and left. Tom was happy and thanked his dad. They played with the ball together and had a fun day.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big bridge over a river. They wanted to cross the bridge and see what was on the other side. They asked their mom if they could go. Mom said yes, but be careful and hold hands. Lily and Ben were happy and ran to the bridge. They started to walk on the bridge, but it was very high and shaky. Lily felt dizzy and scared. She stopped and held Ben's hand tighter. Ben saw that Lily was dizzy and scared. He said, "Don't worry, Lily. I'm here with you. Let's pray to God and ask Him to help us cross the bridge. God loves us and He will keep us safe." Lily nodded and closed her eyes. She prayed to God and asked Him to help them cross the bridge. She felt a warm and calm feeling in her heart. She opened her eyes and smiled at Ben. Ben smiled back and said, "Let's go, Lily. We can do it." They walked slowly and carefully on the bridge, holding hands and praying to God. They reached the other side and saw a beautiful park with flowers and trees. They thanked God for helping them cross the bridge and for the beautiful park. They played and had fun until mom came to pick them up. They hugged mom and told her about their adventure. Mom was proud of them and said, "You are very brave and faithful, Lily and Ben. God is always with you and He answers your prayers."

Once upon a time, there was a restless little dog named Max. Max loved to run and play all day long. One day, Max found something new in the kitchen. It was round and smelled different. Max did not know it was an onion. Max picked up the onion and started to chew on it. "Yuck!" said Max, as he did not like the taste. Max's friend, a little girl named Lucy, saw Max chewing on the onion. "Max, onions are not for dogs!" said Lucy. Lucy took the onion away from Max and gave him a tasty bone instead. Max was happy to chew on the bone. He wagged his tail and thanked Lucy. From that day on, Max never chewed on onions again.

Molly was going to the beach. She was so excited, she couldn't wait to dip her toes in the warm, blue water! She hopped out of bed, put on her favorite green dress and grabbed her hat. She ran outside to her Dad and said, "I'm ready, let's go!" But then Dad said, "I'm sorry, Molly. We can't go to the beach today." Molly frowned and said, "Why not, Daddy?" Dad said, "Molly, it's raining outside. Do you have another hat that you can bring with you?" Molly had a lot of hats, but she shook her head sadly. She had left all of her hats at school and she didn't have any other hats. But then Dad said, "I think I have an old hat in the closet that you can bring. Let's go see!" Molly followed Dad to the closet and sure enough he found a yellow hat that was just right for her. Molly put on the hat and smiled. She said, "Thank you, Daddy! Now I'm happy and I can go to the beach!"
