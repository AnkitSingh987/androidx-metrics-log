Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa the Girl and the Bear

Once upon a time, in a regular house, there lived a girl named Lisa. She had a soft, velvet teddy bear that she loved very much. Every night, she would hug the teddy bear and promise to keep it safe. One day, Lisa went to the park with her teddy bear. She met a boy named Tom. Tom looked at the velvet teddy bear and said, "I like your teddy bear. Can I play with it?" Lisa said, "Yes, but you must promise to be nice to it." Tom played with the teddy bear, but then something unexpected happened. The teddy bear began to talk! It said, "Thank you for letting me play with Tom, Lisa. I was feeling lonely." Lisa and Tom were both very surprised. They promised to play with the teddy bear every day, and they all became the best of friends.

Once, there was a big rat. The rat was gifted. It could talk like people. One day, the rat met a boy. The boy said, "Hello, rat. Can you talk?" The rat said, "Yes, I can talk. I am gifted." The boy was happy. He said, "Wow! You are a special rat. Let's be friends." The boy and the rat played all day. The boy asked the rat, "Can you dance?" The rat did not know. It shrugged and said, "I don't know. Let's try." They danced and had fun. The boy and the gifted rat were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big wardrobe in his room. Tim loved to play with his toys in his room every day. One day, Tim wanted to stretch and reach his favorite toy on top of the wardrobe. But he was too small and frightened to climb up. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "I want my toy, but it's too high and I'm scared." His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you get your toy." She stretched her arm and grabbed the toy for him. Tim was very happy. But when Tim's mom gave him the toy, something unexpected happened. The toy started to talk! It said, "Thank you for saving me from the high wardrobe! I was frightened too!" Tim and his mom were surprised, but they laughed and played with the talking toy all day long.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a new friend named Lily. Tim wanted to say hello, so he walked up to her. He said, "Hi, I am Tim." This was how Tim introduced himself. Lily gave Tim a big smile. They were happy to meet each other. Tim and Lily played together all day. They ran and jumped and laughed. But then, they saw a little dog. The dog was restless, running around in circles. Tim and Lily wanted to help the dog, so they went closer. When they got near the dog, something unexpected happened. The dog turned into a big, friendly bear! Tim and Lily were surprised, but they were not scared. The bear gave them a big smile too. Now, they had a new friend to play with at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a big soft bear. The bear liked to ride his bike in the park. One day, while riding his bike, he saw a big statue. The statue was of a man on a horse. The bear looked at the statue and thought it was fun. He wanted to ride the horse too. So, he got off his bike and climbed on the statue. He pretended to ride the horse like the man in the statue. The bear had a lot of fun. After a while, he got tired and went back to his bike. He rode home, happy and ready for a nap.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. Amy saw lots of candy on the shelf. She asked her mom, "Can I have some candy, please?" Her mom said, "No, not today." Amy was sad but she had an idea. She went to the candy shelf and took a candy when her mom was not looking. She felt a little embarrassed but she wanted the candy so much. When they got home, Amy's mom found the candy in her pocket. She was not happy. "Did you take this candy?" she asked. Amy nodded and said, "I'm sorry, Mom." Her mom said, "It's important to not take things without asking. Let's go back to the store and pay for the candy." Amy learned her lesson and promised to never repeat it again.
