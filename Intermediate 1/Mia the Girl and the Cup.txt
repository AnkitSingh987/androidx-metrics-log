Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a girl named Mia. Mia loved to listen to her radio every day. She liked to dance and sing along with the songs that played on the radio. One day, Mia was in the kitchen with her mom. Her mom was pouring some juice into a cup for Mia. Mia was very happy because she liked juice a lot. While her mom poured the juice, Mia was listening to her radio and dancing around the kitchen. After Mia finished her juice, she went outside to play with her friends. They played in the sun and had lots of fun. Mia told her friends about her radio and how she liked to dance while her mom poured her juice. At the end of the day, Mia and her friends were tired but happy. They went back to their homes and slept well, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time, there was a big mountain. A little boy named Tim lived near the mountain. He liked to play with his dog, Sam, and they would go on adventures together. One day, Tim and Sam went to the mountain to explore. Tim said to Sam, "Be careful, Sam. We don't want to fall." They walked up the mountain very slowly. Tim was always careful when he climbed the mountain. As they climbed, they found a magic rock. Tim picked up the rock and rubbed it. A fairy appeared and said, "Hello, Tim and Sam! Because you were careful and found my rock, I will give you a wish. What do you want?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I wish for more fun adventures with Sam!" The fairy smiled and granted Tim's wish. From that day on, Tim and Sam had many fun adventures together, and they always remembered to be careful.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a great big cat named Tom. Tom was always cold and would shiver all the time. One day, he saw a small ball of thread on the floor. Tom had an idea. Tom picked up the thread and started to wrap it around himself. He wrapped and wrapped until he was covered in a warm, thread blanket. Now, he did not shiver anymore. Tom was very happy. Tom's friend, a small bird named Sue, flew in and saw Tom in his thread blanket. Sue said, "Wow, Tom, you look great! You're not shivering anymore." Tom smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue. This thread keeps me warm and cozy." And from that day on, Tom and Sue played together, and Tom never shivered again.

Anna loved her puppy, Max. He was fluffy and brown and liked to play. Anna's mom gave Max a new toy. It was a ball that could bounce very high. Max was very happy. He ran after the ball and jumped to catch it. "Good boy, Max!" Anna said. "You are so smart and fast!" But Max did not want to share his ball. He growled when Anna tried to take it. He ran away from her and hid under the bed. Anna was sad. She wanted to play with Max and his ball. "Max, come out!" Anna said. "Don't be mean. It is not nice to be selfish." Max did not listen. He stayed under the bed and chewed his ball. He was a spoiled puppy. He thought the ball was only his. Anna's mom saw what happened. She was not happy. She went to Max and took his ball. She said, "Max, you are being naughty. You have to share with Anna. She is your friend. If you don't share, you will lose your ball." Max whimpered. He did not want to lose his ball. He loved his ball. He looked at Anna. She was still sad. He felt sorry. He wagged his tail and came out of the bed. He licked Anna's face and gave her his ball. Anna smiled. She hugged Max and said, "Thank you, Max. You are a good boy. I love you." Max barked. He was happy. He loved Anna. He learned to share. He and Anna played with the ball and had fun. They were not spoiled. They were happy.
