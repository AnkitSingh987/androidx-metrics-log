Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Fat Cat and the Sun

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom loved to play in the sun all day. One day, he saw a twisty rope hanging from a tree. Tom was very curious and wanted to play with the twisty rope. Tom jumped up and grabbed the twisty rope with his paw. He twisted and turned, having so much fun. But then, the sky became dark, and big raindrops started to fall. It was a shower! Tom didn't like getting wet, so he ran to find a place to hide. He found a small house with a nice, dry spot under it. Tom hid there until the shower stopped. When the sun came back out, Tom went back to play with the twisty rope. He played and played until he was tired. Then, he went home to take a nap, happy to have had such a fun day.

Tom and Lily are brother and sister. They like to play outside in the garden. One day, they see a big hole in the fence. They are eager to see what is behind it. They crawl through the hole and discover a new place. It is a wood with many trees and animals. They hear birds singing and squirrels running. They smell flowers and grass. They feel happy and curious. They explore the wood and find a stream. They see fish swimming and frogs jumping. They splash water and laugh. They make a boat with a leaf and a stick. They watch it float away. They have fun in the wood until it gets dark. They hear their mom calling them. They crawl back through the hole and run to the house. They hug their mom and tell her about their adventure. She smiles and says they are brave and clever. She gives them a warm bath and a cozy bed. They fall asleep and dream of the wood.

One day, a messy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a stream with pretty fish. Tom wanted to play with the fish. He blinked his eyes and jumped in the water. The fish swam away fast. Tom felt sad. Then, Tom saw a big box near the stream. He blinked his eyes and went to look. Inside the box, he found a pretty hat. He put the hat on his head and felt happy. Tom went back to the stream to show the fish. When Tom got to the stream, something unexpected happened. He blinked his eyes, and the fish came back. The fish liked Tom's hat too! They all played together in the stream. Tom and the fish were now friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her stuff. She had a big box full of toys. One day, she had to leave her room to eat lunch. Sue felt frightened because she did not want anyone to touch her stuff while she was gone. Sue's big brother, Tom, was always playing tricks on her. Before she left, Sue hid her stuff under her bed. She thought this would keep it safe from Tom. Then, she went to eat her lunch. While eating, she felt better knowing her stuff was hidden. When Sue finished eating, she ran back to her room. She looked under her bed and saw her stuff was still there. But, she found a note from Tom that said, "I knew you would hide your stuff, so I didn't touch it!" Sue was surprised and happy that her stuff was safe. She learned that sometimes, things we are frightened of can turn out just fine.

Once there was a little girl. She was three years old and she loved music. She had never seen a piano before, but she knew what one was. One day she was walking down the street and saw an empty room. The room was so quiet and it made her curious. She walked in and saw a big, shiny piano in the corner. She knew right away what it was; it was a piano! She was so excited and wanted to try it. She started to press the keys and it sounded so beautiful. She instantly recognized the sweet sound of the piano. She spent the rest of the day playing with the piano and making music. She loved the sound it made and it filled the previously empty room. She knew the piano was something special and she would never forget it.
