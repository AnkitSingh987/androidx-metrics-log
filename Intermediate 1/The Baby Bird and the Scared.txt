Category: Intermediate
Title: The Baby Bird and the Scared

Once upon a time, there was a baby bird. The baby bird was scared. It was scared because it wanted to fly but did not know how. The baby bird lived in a nest with its mom and dad. They loved the baby bird very much. One day, the baby bird hoped to fly. It asked its mom and dad for help. They said, "Don't be scared, baby bird. We will teach you how to fly." The baby bird was happy. It was ready to learn. The baby bird's mom and dad showed it what to do. They flapped their wings and jumped in the air. The baby bird tried too. At first, it was hard. But soon, the baby bird was flying! It was not scared anymore. The baby bird was so happy and proud.

Once upon a time, there was a poor, little cat named Velvet. She lived in a small house with her friend, a kind mouse named Tim. They did not have much, but they were happy together. One day, Velvet and Tim were playing outside when it started to rain. "Oh no, we need to go inside," said Tim. Velvet agreed, and they both ran back to their tiny house to stay dry. As they sat inside, Velvet had an idea. "Let's start making a big, warm blanket to keep us cozy," she said. Tim liked the idea, and they began to work together. They made the best blanket ever, and they were never cold or wet again.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small cabin with his mom and dad. One day, Tim and his mom went for a walk in the woods. They saw a line of ants marching on the ground. Tim asked his mom, "Why do the ants march like that?" His mom said, "They march to find food and bring it back to their home." As they walked, they saw a big, colorful bird. The bird was also marching with the ants. Tim and his mom were very surprised. They had never seen a bird march with ants before. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you marching with the ants?" The bird said, "I am learning how they find food. I want to be a good helper like the ants." Tim and his mom smiled. They thought the bird was very smart. They walked back to their cabin and told Tim's dad about the bird. Tim's dad laughed and said, "That bird must be a very good friend to the ants." From that day on, Tim watched the bird and the ants march together, and he learned how to be a good helper too.

Tommy liked to hop in the garden. He saw many flowers and bugs. He saw a green caterpillar on a leaf. He wanted to play with it. "Hello, caterpillar," he said. "Do you want to hop with me?" The caterpillar did not answer. It was busy eating the leaf. Tommy was sad. He picked up the caterpillar and put it in his pocket. "Come on, caterpillar. We will have fun," he said. He hopped to the pond. He saw some ducks and frogs. They were noisy. They quacked and croaked. Tommy liked the noise. He wanted to join them. "Hello, ducks and frogs," he said. "Do you want to hop with me?" The ducks and frogs did not answer. They were busy swimming and jumping. Tommy was sad. He took out the caterpillar and threw it in the pond. "Come on, caterpillar. We will have fun," he said. But the caterpillar did not have fun. It could not swim. It sank to the bottom of the pond. It was cold and dark. It was scared. It could not breathe. It died. Tommy did not know. He hopped away. He looked for more friends. He did not find any. He was alone. He was sad. He did not have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big box of crayons. Tim loved to draw with his crayons. One day, he found an incredible bouncy ball. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he took it outside. As Tim played with the bouncy ball, he had an idea. He went inside and got his crayons. He drew a big smiley face on the ball. Tim said, "Now you have a face, ball!" The ball seemed to smile back at him. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to talk! It said, "Thank you, Tim! I love my new face!" Tim was so surprised. The ball became Tim's best friend. They played and drew together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.
