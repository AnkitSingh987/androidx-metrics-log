Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a big, selfish cat. The cat lived in a house with a small kitchen. The cat loved to eat all the food in the kitchen. The cat did not like to share. One day, the cat saw a little bird outside. The bird was dirty and needed a bath. The selfish cat wanted to help the little bird. The cat thought that if it helped the bird, the bird would be its friend and bring more food. The cat took the bird to the kitchen and helped it bathe in a small bowl of water. The bird was very happy and thanked the cat. The cat learned that it was good to share and not be selfish. They became best friends and shared food in the kitchen. And they lived happily ever after.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved going on adventures. One day she decided to march to a big open field. As she walked in the field, she saw a humble old car. She knew it must have belonged to someone special. She decided to march closer to the car and noticed it had four shiny wheels. She decided to march around the car while imagining what it would be like to drive. She knew she was too young to drive the car, but she thought it was so much fun to pretend.

One day, a little shark swam in the sea. He liked to play with his fish friends. But sometimes, the shark was careless. He would swim too fast and not look where he was going. One day, while playing, the shark did not see a big rock. He swam very fast and hit the rock. "Oh no!" said the shark, "I break my fin!" His fish friends swam to help him. They wanted to make him feel better. Then, something unexpected happened. A little crab walked up to the shark. "I can help you," said the crab. The crab held the shark's broken fin and it started to heal. The shark could swim again! The shark and his fish friends were happy. They learned to be more careful when they played in the sea.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy had a bed that she loved very much. It was old and not pretty, but it was her favorite place to sleep and play. Lucy's mom and dad wanted to make her bed look nice again. They wanted to restore it. Lucy's mom and dad worked hard to fix the bed. They painted it with a pretty color and put on new sheets. They even put a fun toy at the end of the bed for Lucy to play with. Lucy watched them work and felt very happy. When the bed was all fixed, Lucy was so proud of her mom and dad. She loved her new bed even more. That night, Lucy slept in her pretty bed and had the best dreams ever. And her mom and dad were proud too, because they made Lucy's bed beautiful again.

Once upon a time, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play all day. One day, he saw a big hill and wanted to run up to the top. Remy was a little worried because he had never climbed such a big hill before. But he was brave and wanted to try. Remy started to run up the hill. It was hard work, but he did not give up. He kept going, even when he felt tired. Finally, Remy reached the top of the hill. He was so happy that he finished climbing the big hill. He looked around and saw his home far away. It was a beautiful view. Remy learned that even if he was worried about something, he could still try and finish it. He knew that being brave and not giving up was important. From that day on, Remy was never scared to try new things. And that is how Remy became the bravest rabbit in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big dream. He wanted to fly high in the sky like a bird. Every day, he would look up at the sky and wish he could fly. One day, Tim met a black cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! I can help you with your dream." Tim was very happy. He asked, "How can you help me?" The cat said, "You need to trust me. Close your eyes and jump." Tim was scared, but he trusted the cat. Tim closed his eyes and jumped. He felt the wind on his face and opened his eyes. He was flying! The black cat was flying with him. They flew high in the sky, and Tim's dream came true. Tim was very thankful to the cat and they became best friends forever.
