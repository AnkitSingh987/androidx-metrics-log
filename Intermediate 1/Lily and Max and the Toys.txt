Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and Max and the Toys

Lily and Max are playing in the living room. They have many toys and books on the floor. Mommy comes in and says, "It is time to clean up, kids. Put your toys and books in the folder." Lily and Max do not want to clean up. They want to keep playing. They say, "No, Mommy. We are not done. We want to play more." Mommy says, "You can play more later, but now you have to put your things away. The folder is on the table. It is big and blue. You can fit many things in it." Lily and Max are still not happy. They start to cry and scream. They are very loud. Mommy does not like the noise. She says, "Stop crying and screaming, kids. You are making my head hurt. If you do not put your things in the folder, you will have no dessert tonight." Lily and Max do not want to miss dessert. They love dessert. They stop crying and screaming. They start to put their toys and books in the folder. They are not very fast. They are still sad. Mommy sees that they are trying. She says, "Thank you, kids. You are doing a good job. When you are done, you can have some ice cream. Ice cream is yummy and cold. Do you like ice cream?" Lily and Max nod. They like ice cream. They smile a little. They finish putting their things in the folder. They give the folder to Mommy. Mommy says, "Well done, kids. You are very good. Come on, let's go to the kitchen and have some ice cream." Lily and Max follow Mommy. They are happy. They forget about playing. They think about ice cream. They say, "Yay, ice cream!"

Once upon a time there were two friends, Jack and Jill. Jack loved to sing and Jill wanted to learn. Jack and Jill decided to make a video together. Jill: "Jack, can we make a video?" Jack: "Yes, let's make one!" So they made a video. Jack sang a song and Jill danced. They were so happy, they looked like they were in a music show! Suddenly something strange happened. Jack was so excited he accidentally turned the video into a red colour! Jill: "Oh no! How can we fix this?" Jack thought for a few moments and then he smiled. Jack: "I know. Let's start again and take it slow this time." So they made the video a second time and this time it stayed the same colour. The moral of the story is, if something doesn't go quite as planned don't panic, try being patient and taking it slow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was adventurous and loved to play outside. One sunny day, Mia went to the park to study the world around her. At the park, Mia found a bubble wand. She dipped it in the soapy water and blew into it. Big, beautiful bubbles floated into the sky. Mia laughed and clapped her hands as she chased the bubbles around the park. Mia's mom watched her play with the bubbles. She was happy to see her adventurous little girl study and learn about the world. And that day, Mia had the most fun with the bubbles at the park.

Once there was a bright boy called Luke. He was always playing and laughing and wanted to do new things. One day, he wanted to help his mom around the house, so he decided to clean out the bin. As soon as he opened the bin, he found a big bug. He was scared but he was brave and he quit! His mom saw what he did and was very impressed. She said, "Well done, Luke! I am proud of you for taking on a challenge and not being scared." Luke was so excited and replied, "Yes! I was brave and I quit!" His mom smiled and said, "That's right. You are a very bright boy with a brave heart."

Ben and Lily were playing in the living room. They liked to pretend they were lions and roar at each other. They used the curtain as their mane and hid behind the sofa. "Roar! I am the king of the jungle!" Ben shouted. "Roar! I am the queen of the jungle!" Lily replied. They ran around the room, chasing each other and making loud noises. They knocked over a lamp and a vase. They did not see that Mom was watching them from the kitchen. "Ben! Lily! Stop that right now!" Mom said. "You are making a big mess and you are not behaving!" Ben and Lily stopped and looked at Mom. They saw that she was angry and had a frown on her face. "Sorry, Mom," they said together. Mom came into the living room and picked up the lamp and the vase. She checked if they were broken. Luckily, they were not. "You two need to be more careful and quiet when you play," Mom said. "You can pretend to be lions, but you don't have to be so fierce and loud. And don't use the curtain as your mane. It is not a toy. It is for the window." Ben and Lily nodded. They felt bad for making Mom upset. "Can we still play, Mom?" Ben asked. "Yes, you can, but only if you behave," Mom said. "And clean up your mess when you are done." Ben and Lily hugged Mom and said thank you. They decided to play something else. They put the curtain back on the window and picked up their toys. They whispered to each other and smiled. They still had fun, even without being fierce lions.
