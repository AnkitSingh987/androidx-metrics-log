Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hat

One day, a boy named Tim had a hat. He loved his hat so much. It was a big hat. Tim wore his hat every day. But one day, he felt miserable. He did not want to wear his hat. He did not know why. Tim went outside to play. He saw a big puddle. Tim wanted to step in the puddle. He took a big step and splashed in the water. It was so much fun. He forgot about his hat and his miserable feelings. Then, something unexpected happened. Tim saw a frog jump out of the puddle. The frog had a hat too! The frog's hat was just like Tim's hat. Tim laughed and felt happy. He put his hat back on. Now, Tim and the frog were friends with the same hats.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little cat named Dare. Dare was not like other cats, because he was not afraid of anything. He loved to play and explore new places. One sunny day, Dare saw a big tree and wanted to climb. His friend, Bird, told him, "Dare, be careful! You might feel pain if you fall." But Dare did not listen and started to climb the tree. As he climbed higher and higher, he felt very proud of himself. Suddenly, Dare slipped and fell to the ground. He felt a lot of pain and started to cry. Bird flew down and said, "I told you to be careful, Dare. But don't worry, I will help you." Bird then surprised Dare by using her wings to gently lift him up and carry him home. From that day on, Dare learned to listen to his friends and be more careful in his adventures.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard was dark and hard to see at night. It was near a shop where people go to pay for things they want. One day, a little boy named Tim and his mom walked by the billboard. Tim asked, "Mom, why is the billboard so dark?" His mom looked at the billboard and said, "I don't know, Tim. Maybe it needs more light." Tim had an idea. He went to the shop and paid for a bright light. He put the light near the billboard. Now, everyone could see the billboard at night. Tim's mom was very proud of him for helping. They both smiled and went home.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. The sky was full of big, white clouds. Tim liked to look at the clouds and find shapes in them. He saw a cloud that looked like a big, wet dog. Tim laughed and said, "Look, Mom! A wet dog cloud!" Mom came to see the cloud. She smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. It does look like a wet dog." Just then, the cloud started to rain. Tim and Mom got wet from the rain. They ran back inside the house to stay dry. When they got inside, Tim's mom said, "Tim, we need to lock the door." Tim locked the door, and they both watched the rain fall outside. Suddenly, the wet dog cloud floated down from the sky and turned into a real, live dog! The dog wagged its tail and barked at Tim and Mom. They were very surprised but happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a naughty boy. Every day he did things that were not nice. One day he asked his mum for a novel. She told him no, but he asked again and again. Finally she said 'okay'. The novel was scary and made the naughty boy scared. He wanted to stop reading, but he was too embarrassed to say anything. He tried to look away but the pictures were so creepy that he could not stop looking. When he had finished the novel, the naughty boy was very scared. Mama saw this and felt sorry for him. She hugged the naughty boy tightly and asked why he had been so naughty. The naughty boy felt better after that. He knew that the novel was too scary and he vowed to never ask his mum for a novel again.
