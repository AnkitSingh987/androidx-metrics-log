Category: Intermediate
Title: Sam and Mia and the Rock

Sam and Mia are playing in the park. They see a big rock near the slide. They want to climb on it. "Can you help me up?" Mia asks Sam. "OK, hold my hand," Sam says. He pulls Mia up on the rock. They sit on top and look around. They see a cable hanging from a tree. It looks like a swing. They want to try it. "Can we go on that?" Sam asks Mia. "Let's go!" Mia says. They slide down from the rock and run to the cable. Sam grabs the cable and tries to swing. But the cable is too long for him. He can't fit on it. He falls to the ground. "Ouch!" Sam says. He rubs his knee. "Are you OK?" Mia asks. She runs to Sam and hugs him. "I'm OK," Sam says. He smiles at Mia. "Maybe we can find another swing," Mia says. "Or another rock," Sam says. They hold hands and look for more fun things to do in the park.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Emily. She was very thoughtful and often thought about things for a very long time. One day she decided she wanted to open a pumpkin. She went to the pumpkin patch and found the biggest pumpkin she could. Emily was very excited and ran back home with the pumpkin. She put it down and began to look for something to open it with. But just then, Emily noticed the pumpkin was moving. She was scared and stepped back. When the pumpkin opened, out jumped a big, scary monster. Emily screamed and ran away. The monster shouted out. "Why did you open me? Now I am free and able to make lots of trouble!" Emily was so scared she could not answer. She ran all the way home and never opened another pumpkin again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside. One day, Tim saw a big tree with a tasty red apple on the tip of a branch. He wanted to eat it. Tim tried to reach the apple, but it was too high. His friend, Sam, saw him and said, "Let me help you, Tim!" Sam urged Tim to climb on his back. With a big smile, Tim said, "Thank you, Sam!" Together, they reached the apple. Tim took a big bite and shared it with Sam. They both thought it was very tasty. From that day on, they were the best of friends and always helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to sell toys. He had a big tray full of toys. He was very happy. One day, a big dog came and took Tom's tray. Tom was very sad. He needed his tray to be successful. He asked the dog, "Please give me my tray back." The dog did not want to give the tray back. Tom had an idea. He gave the dog a toy. The dog was happy and gave Tom his tray back. Tom was successful and sold all his toys. Everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little island in the sea. On the island lived a small boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the sand and swim in the water. He had a big friend, a whale named Wally. Tim and Wally played every day. One day, Tim found a hard rock on the beach. The rock had a pretty picture on it. Tim showed the rock to Wally. Wally said, "This rock is special. Keep it safe, and something good will happen." The next day, the sun began to rise, and Tim took the rock to the beach. He put it on the sand and waited. Suddenly, the sand around the rock began to rise. It grew and grew until it became a big sandcastle! Tim was so happy. He knew the rock was magic, just like Wally said. Tim and Wally played in the sandcastle all day long.

One day, a girl named Lily was playing with her notebook. She liked to be organized and write in her notebook every day. She had a friend named Tom who liked to play and shout a lot. Sometimes, Tom would shout so loud that Lily could not think. One day, while Lily was writing in her notebook, Tom came to play. He shouted, "Lily, let's play!" But Lily said, "No, Tom, I want to write in my notebook and be organized." Tom was sad and went away. Later, Lily felt bad for not playing with Tom. The next day, Lily had an idea. She went to Tom and said, "Tom, let's play a game where we shout and write in my notebook." Tom was happy and they played the game. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew away all the pages of Lily's notebook. Lily was sad, but Tom said, "Don't worry, Lily. We can find the pages and put them back in your notebook." They worked together and found all the pages. The moral of the story is that sometimes it's good to take a break and have fun with friends.
