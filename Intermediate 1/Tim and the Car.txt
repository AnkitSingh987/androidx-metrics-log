Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Car

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a trip with his mom and dad. They drove in their car to a big park with lots of trees, grass, and birds. Tim was very happy to go on this trip. When they got to the park, it was time to unpack the car. Tim helped his mom and dad take out the bags, food, and toys. They found a nice spot to sit and have a picnic. Tim's mom had a special toy for him. It was a big, red ball. Tim liked to play with the ball because it was fun and dependable. He knew it would always bounce back to him when he threw it. Tim's mom and dad watched as he played with the ball, laughing and having a great time. After playing, Tim was hungry. His mom gave him a sandwich with his name on it. Tim thought it was so cool to see his name on the sandwich. He ate it all up and felt very full. Then, they all laid down on a soft blanket and looked at the clouds in the sky. Tim felt happy and loved, knowing he had a dependable family who cared for him.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a kind cow named Betsy. Betsy loved to eat grass and play with her friends. One day, Betsy's owner, Farmer Brown, wanted to make cream. He asked Betsy to help him. Betsy and Farmer Brown worked hard to make the cream. They raised their hands high to mix the cream in a big bowl. Betsy was very happy to help. The cream was smooth and sweet. They shared the cream with all their friends in the town. Everyone in the town loved the cream. They thanked Betsy and Farmer Brown for sharing. Betsy learned that when you help others and share, everyone is happy. The town was peaceful and full of love. And that is the end of the story.

Tom and Anna were playing a game. They had to roll a ball and knock down some cans. If they knocked down all the cans, they would get a prize. Tom rolled the ball first. He knocked down four cans. He was happy. He said, "Look, Anna, I almost got them all!" Anna rolled the ball next. She knocked down five cans. She was happier. She said, "Look, Tom, I got more than you!" Tom rolled the ball again. He knocked down six cans. He was the happiest. He said, "Look, Anna, I got them all! I win the prize!" Anna rolled the ball again. She knocked down six cans too. She was also the happiest. She said, "Look, Tom, I got them all too! I win the prize!" They ran to the table where the prizes were. They saw a lot of toys and books and stickers. They wanted to pick one. But when they got closer, they saw a sign. The sign said, "Sorry, all prizes are gone. Come back tomorrow." Tom and Anna looked at the table. It was empty. There was nothing left. They felt sad. They said, "Oh no, we are too late. There is no prize for us." They sighed. They walked away. They decided to play another game. Maybe they would have more luck next time.

Once upon a time there was a truck that was driving down the road. It was pretty, with yellow and blue paint. In the back of the truck was a very hungry puppy. The puppy saw some grass and started to chew on it. Then he saw something even more delicious. He saw some delicious cookies in the back of the truck! He quickly hopped in, grabbed the cookies and started to chew them. But the truck driver saw him and he was not happy. The driver quickly stopped the truck and scolded the puppy for trying to eat the cookies. The puppy whimpered as the driver grabbed the cookies and put them back in the truck. But the driver decided to be kind. He gave the puppy a big, juicy bone to chew on. The puppy was so happy and he thanked the driver with wags of his tail. And so, the puppy and the driver drove off into the sunset with the puppy happily chewing away.
