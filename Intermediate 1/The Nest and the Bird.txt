Category: Intermediate
Title: The Nest and the Bird

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a nest. In the nest, lived a small bird named Tim. Tim was a fit bird who loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, Tim's best friend, a squirrel named Sam, came to visit him. Sam said, "Tim, let's play! But don't panic if you can't keep up." Tim laughed and said, "I won't panic, Sam. Let's go!" They played and played until it was time to go back to the nest. When they got back, they were both tired but happy. They said goodnight to each other and went to sleep. The next day, they played some more, and Tim never panicked.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They liked to play a game. The game was to find a fake bone that Tim hid. Spot would look for the bone while Tim would relax on the grass. Tim said, "Spot, I will hide the bone now. Go and look for it!" Tim hid the bone under a big tree. Spot ran around, looking for the bone. But then, Spot saw something else. Spot saw a real bone, not the fake one! He dug it up and brought it to Tim. Tim was very surprised. "Spot, this is not the fake bone! You found a real one!" They both laughed and played with the real bone. They had more fun with the real bone than the fake one.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big house and many toys. Tom loved to play outside with his friends. One sunny day, Tom and his friends decided to create a tunnel in the park. They used their small shovels and dug a hole in the soft dirt. As they dug deeper, they made a long tunnel that went from one side of the park to the other. Tom and his friends had so much fun playing in their new tunnel. They crawled through it, played hide and seek, and pretended they were on a big adventure. It was a day they would always remember.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lily. She was so excited one day because she was going to find a well. Lily walked and walked until she found the well. She looked down into the deep, dark hole. She wanted to go inside the well, but she knew it was dangerous. Lily's mom saw her and shouted, "Lily! Don't go in there, it's very slippery!" But Lily was too impatient. She said, "I'm not stupid Mom, I can do it!" She stepped forward and slipped on the edge of the well. She screamed as she started to fall down, down, down into the deep, dark hole. But luckily, Lily was safe! Her mom had reached out and grabbed her just in time. Lily was embarrassed and said "I'm sorry Mom. I won't do that again, I promise!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's okay Lily. I'm just glad you're safe!"

Once upon a time, there was a good clown named Bobo. He lived in a big, colorful tent. Bobo loved to make kids laugh and smile. One day, a little girl named Lily came to see Bobo. She was sad because she lost her toy. Bobo wanted to help Lily find her toy. They looked all around the tent for it. Bobo saw the toy on a high shelf. He said, "Lily, I see your toy!" Bobo grabbed a long stick and used it to grab the toy from the shelf. He gave the toy back to Lily. Lily was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are a good clown!" Bobo smiled and said, "You're welcome, Lily. I'm glad I could help!" From that day on, Lily and Bobo became good friends. They played and laughed together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived on a farm with her mom and dad. They grew lots of corn in their field. Amy loved to watch the corn grow tall and strong. One day, Amy was walking through the corn field when she met a small bird. The bird was sad and said, "I don't understand how to find food." Amy felt bad for the bird and wanted to help. She said, "Don't worry, little bird. I will show you where to find food." Amy took the bird to the corn field and showed it how to eat the corn. The bird was very grateful and said, "Thank you, Amy! Now I understand how to find food." The bird was happy, and Amy felt good about helping her new friend. From that day on, the bird and Amy became best friends. They played together in the corn field every day. The little bird was always grateful for the corn that Amy had shown it, and Amy was happy to have a new friend who understood her.
