Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Book

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to play hide and seek with her friends. One day, she found a big book with many words. In the book, there was an unknown word that she had never seen before. Lily asked her mom, "What is this unknown word?" Her mom said, "I don't know, but maybe if we play hide and seek, we will find out." Lily got excited and asked her friends to join her. They all played hide and seek in the park. Lily found all her friends, but she still did not know the unknown word. Then, she saw a wise old owl in a tree. The owl said, "The unknown word is 'love.' When you play with your friends, you are showing love." Lily smiled and hugged her friends, happy to know the unknown word.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a fake toy paw that he liked to play with. He would carry the toy paw around in his mouth and show it to all his friends. One day, Spot met a new friend named Bella. Bella saw the toy paw in Spot's mouth and said, "What is that?" Spot replied, "This is my fake paw. I like to play with it." Bella thought it was funny and wanted to play too. Spot and Bella played with the fake paw all day. They would take turns holding it in their mouths and running around. At the end of the day, Spot decided to accept Bella as his new best friend. They promised to play together every day and share the fake paw.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird. The bird loved to fly high in the sky. One day, the bird saw a big rock on the ground. The bird wanted to raise the rock up high. It tried to lift the rock with its beak, but the rock was too heavy. The bird did not give up. It thought of a plan. The bird asked its friends for help. They all worked together to raise the rock. They pushed and pulled until the rock moved. The colorful bird was so happy, and they all played around the big rock all day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a ball. It was a very bouncy ball. Lucy felt lucky to find it. She played with the ball all day. She would throw it and watch it bounce up and down. Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, it is time to come inside." Lucy brought her bouncy ball inside. She saw the television in the living room. Lucy had an idea. She wanted to see if the ball would bounce on the television. So, she threw the ball at the television. Oh no! The ball broke the television. Lucy was sad and scared. Her mom came into the room and saw what happened. She said, "Lucy, you should not throw things at the television. It is not safe." Lucy felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Mom." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Lucy. We can fix the television. But remember to play with your ball outside only." Lucy learned that it is important to play with her toys in the right places. She promised to be more careful next time. And Lucy still felt lucky to have her bouncy ball, but now she knew where to play with it.

Tim and Lily were best friends. They liked to play with toys and eat snacks. One day, they found a big bag of gummy bears in the kitchen. They were very happy and took some gummy bears to share. But Tim was greedy. He wanted more gummy bears than Lily. He took some from Lily's hand when she was not looking. He put them in his mouth and chewed them fast. Lily saw him and was sad. She said, "Tim, that is not nice. You took my gummy bears. You should share with me." Tim did not listen. He said, "No, they are mine. I like gummy bears more than you. Go away, Lily." He pushed Lily and made her cry. Lily ran to her mom and told her what Tim did. Mom was compassionate. She hugged Lily and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. Tim was wrong. He should not take your gummy bears or push you. You are a good friend. You deserve to have gummy bears too." Mom took Lily back to the kitchen. She saw Tim with a big pile of gummy bears. He had eaten almost all of them. He looked sick and unhappy. He had a tummy ache from eating too many gummy bears. Mom said, "Tim, look what you did. You ate too many gummy bears and now you feel bad. You also hurt Lily's feelings and made her cry. You were not a good friend. You need to say sorry to Lily and give her some gummy bears." Tim realized he was wrong. He felt sorry for Lily and for himself. He said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I was greedy and mean. I should have shared with you. Here, you can have some of my gummy bears. I don't want them anymore." Lily smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I forgive you. You are still my best friend. But next time, let's share and be nice to each other." Tim and Lily hugged and said sorry to Mom. Mom was proud of them. She said, "That's good, Tim and Lily. You learned your lesson. Sharing is caring. Gummy bears are fun, but friends are more important. Now, let's go and play with your toys. But first, drink some water and brush your teeth."
