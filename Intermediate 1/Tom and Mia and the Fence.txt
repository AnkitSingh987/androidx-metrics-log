Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Fence

Tom and Mia like to play in the garden. They have a big hose that can spray water. They pretend that the hose is a fire dragon and they are brave knights. One day, they see a big shadow behind the fence. It is a monster! It has rough fur, sharp teeth and red eyes. It growls and tries to jump over the fence. "Help! Help!" Mia cries. "The monster wants to eat us!" Tom grabs the hose and turns it on. He sprays water at the monster. "Go away, monster! You are not welcome here!" The monster is surprised and angry. It does not like water. It shakes its head and runs away. "Yay! We did it!" Tom cheers. "We scared the monster away!" Mia hugs Tom. "You are a brave knight, Tom. And you have a fire dragon!" They laugh and play with the hose. They are happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was an unusual cow. She liked to wear funny hats. One day, Bessie found a big red hat. She put it on her head and went for a walk. As Bessie walked, she met a little boy named Tim. Tim looked at Bessie and said, "Why do you wear a hat, cow?" Bessie replied, "I like to be different and have fun!" Tim smiled and said, "I like your hat, Bessie!" Bessie and Tim became good friends. They played together every day. Bessie showed Tim that it's okay to be unusual. And they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house near a big hill. Every day, he would go out and play near the hill. He liked to roll down the hill and feel the wind in his hair. Tim had a lot of fun on the hill. One day, Tim saw a big red ball on the hill. He hoped he could play with it. He ran up the hill and kicked the ball. The ball went flying down the hill. Tim laughed and chased the ball. He played with the ball all day long. Later, Tim's mom called him back to the house. She had made a yummy dinner for him. It was his favorite food, but it was spicy. Tim liked spicy food. He ate all the food and thanked his mom. Tim went to bed happy, thinking about the fun day he had on the hill.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an enormous tree. Under the tree, there was a pastel-colored house. In the house lived a girl named Lily and her big cat, Tom. One sunny day, Lily and Tom went to play outside. They saw a big ball under the tree. Lily said, "Tom, let's play with the ball!" Tom looked at the ball and said, "Meow!" They played and played, but Tom insisted on playing more. "Tom, it's time to go home," said Lily. Tom meowed loudly, wanting to play more. But Lily said, "No, Tom, we need to go home." So, Lily and Tom went back to their pastel house under the enormous tree. They had a good day and were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a tall oak tree. The oak tree was very strong and had many leaves. Animals loved to play under the oak tree because it gave them shade from the sun. One day, a scary fire started in the forest. The fire was hot and it began to burn the trees. The animals were scared and ran away from the fire. They did not want to get hurt. The tall oak tree was very brave. It tried to protect the animals by stopping the fire. The scary fire came close, but the oak tree did not burn. The fire went away and the animals were safe. They were very happy and thanked the oak tree for being so strong and brave.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy frog named Sam who lived in a swamp. Sam loved to jump and play all day with his friends. They would splash in the water and chase each other around the swamp. One day, Sam met a new friend named Lily. She was a pretty butterfly with big wings. "Hi, I'm Sam. Do you want to play with me?" asked Sam. Lily said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Sam!" So, they played together all day long. After a while, Sam and Lily were very tired. "Let's rest under this big tree," said Sam. So, they sat down and rested together. They talked and laughed as they watched the sun go down. And from that day on, Sam and Lily were best friends who always played and rested together in the swamp.
