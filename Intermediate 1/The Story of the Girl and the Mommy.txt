Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Girl and the Mommy

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and her little girl. The little girl was three years old and full of joy. One day, mommy told the little girl that she needed to clean her room. The little girl believed mommy and went to her room to clean. As the little girl was cleaning, she felt pain in her hands. The little girl asked, "Mommy, why do I have pain in my hands?" Mommy smiled and said, "It's ok, my sweet girl. Cleaning can be hard, but it's important. And look! You made your room so brown and clean." The little girl was so very proud of her hard work. She believed in herself and her mommy, and that made her feel very special.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy liked to play with her ball and run around the house. One day, Fluffy found a bottle of glue. She didn't know what it was, but she thought it was fun to play with. Fluffy played with the glue and it got all over her paws. She tried to walk, but her paws would stick to the floor. Fluffy didn't like this and started to cry. She didn't know how to make the glue go away. Then, it started to get very cold in the house. Fluffy's paws began to freeze. She couldn't move at all. She was stuck to the floor and very sad. No one came to help Fluffy and she stayed stuck there. This is the end of the story and it is a sad one.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toy phone. One day, she found a big orange in the kitchen. Sue had an idea. She would call her friend, Tom, and tell him about the orange. Sue picked up her toy phone and called Tom. "Hi Tom!" she said. "I found a big orange. It is very fine. Do you want to see it?" Tom said, "Yes, Sue! I will come over soon." Sue waited for Tom. She looked at the orange and smiled. She knew Tom would like the orange too. When Tom came over, he saw the orange and said, "Wow, Sue! That is a fine orange." They both laughed and played with the orange all day. And they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big yellow sun. The sun was very hot, and it made the sky very bright. The sun was friends with the wind. The wind liked to blow and make things move. They liked to play together in the sky. One day, the wind and the sun decided to mix and make a big storm. The storm had loud sounds and bright lights. The rain came down very fast, and the wind blew very hard. All the animals and people had to go inside their homes to stay safe and dry. After the storm, the big yellow sun came out again. The sky was bright and blue. The wind stopped blowing, and everything was calm. The animals and people came out of their homes. They were happy that the storm was over, and they could play outside again.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a delicate cobweb. It was in a corner where no one could see. The cobweb was home to a tiny spider named Bob. Bob was a friendly spider who liked to play with his friends, the ants. One day, Bob and the ants were playing a game of tag. They were running around, laughing and having fun. Suddenly, they heard a loud "STOP!" It was Mommy Ant, who was worried that they would break the delicate cobweb. Bob and the ants stopped playing and looked at the cobweb. They knew it was special and didn't want to hurt it. So, they decided to play a different game that wouldn't hurt the cobweb. They played hide and seek instead, and everyone had a great time. The delicate cobweb stayed safe, and Bob and the ants learned to be careful when playing near it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wallet on the ground. He picked it up and looked inside. There was some money and a card with a name on it. Tim thought it would be thoughtful to find the person and give the wallet back. Tim asked his mom, "Can we find the person who lost the wallet?" His mom said, "Yes, we can try." They looked for the person in the park, but they could not find him. Then, Tim saw a sad man sitting on a bench. Tim walked up to him and whispered, "Is this your wallet?" The man looked at the wallet and smiled. "Yes, it's mine! Thank you so much!" Tim felt happy that he helped someone, and the man was no longer sad.
