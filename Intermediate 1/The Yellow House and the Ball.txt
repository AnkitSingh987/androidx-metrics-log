Category: Intermediate
Title: The Yellow House and the Ball

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a yellow house. In the yellow house, there lived a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved her yellow house and her nice neighbor, Mr. Brown. One sunny day, Lucy went outside to play. She saw Mr. Brown and wanted to play with him. They decided to play ball together. Lucy threw the ball and Mr. Brown tried to strike it. As they played, the ball went very high in the sky. Lucy and Mr. Brown both looked up to see where the ball would come down. They laughed and had fun playing together. Lucy was happy to have a kind neighbor like Mr. Brown. They played until the sun went down, and then Lucy went back to her yellow house.

Once upon a time, there was a big sky. In the sky, there was a comet. The comet was very original. It was shiny and bright. It moved fast in the sky. One day, the comet met a star. The star said, "Hello, comet! Can I join you?" The comet said, "Yes, I permit you to come with me." So, the star and the comet went together in the sky. They played and had fun. They made the sky look beautiful. All the people on the ground looked up and smiled. The comet and the star were very happy. They were best friends in the sky.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make a big tower with his toy blocks in his backyard. He thought it would be fun to see how high he could make it. Tim started to put one block on top of another. He was very happy with his tower. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and knocked over Tim's tower. Tim felt sad and called the bird stupid. He did not like that the bird messed up his tower. So, Tim started to make the tower again, hoping the bird would not come back. As Tim was building his tower again, he saw the bird fly back with something shiny in its beak. The bird dropped the shiny thing near Tim's tower. It was a pretty toy car! Tim was surprised and happy. He realized that the bird was not stupid, but was trying to help him by bringing a new toy. Now, Tim and the big bird played together in the backyard, making towers and racing the toy car.

One day, a little ant named Tim was walking in the sun. It was very hot. Tim was looking for food to take back to his ant hill. He found a big, sweet apple on the ground. Tim was very happy. As Tim was trying to move the apple, he saw big, dark clouds in the sky. "Oh no, it will rain soon!" said Tim. His friend Sam came by and said, "Tim, you should come back to the ant hill. It will not be safe out here when it rains." But Tim did not want to leave the apple. The rain started to fall. Big drops of water came down from the sky. Tim tried to move the apple, but it was too heavy. The rain got stronger and the ground began to flood. Tim was scared and called for help, "Sam, please help me!" But it was too late. The water washed Tim and the apple away. Sam was sad, but he knew that he had to go back to the ant hill to be safe. Tim should have listened to his friend and left the apple behind.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Zigzag. Zigzag was a lucky dog. He had a big yard to play in and a nice family to stay with. He liked to run and play all day. One day, Zigzag saw a big red ball in the yard. He was so happy and started to play with it. He would run and jump and stay close to the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball started to move on its own! Zigzag was surprised but he still wanted to play. He followed the ball as it went in a zigzag path. Soon, he found out that the ball was not moving on its own. It was his friend, the wind, who was playing with him! Zigzag felt so lucky to have the wind as a friend. From that day on, Zigzag and the wind played together in the yard, having lots of fun.
