Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cow

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They wanted to observe the cows. Tim loved cows. They were big and had soft noses. At the farm, Tim saw a cow that was dizzy. The cow walked in circles and did not look happy. Tim asked his mom, "Why is the cow dizzy, Mom?" His mom said, "I don't know, Tim. Let's ask the farmer." The farmer told Tim and his mom that the cow was just playing. It was a silly cow who liked to spin around. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He was happy the cow was just having fun. They watched the cows until the sun went down and then went home.

Once upon a time, there was a mop. The mop was not nice. It was rude. The mop did not like to help. It liked to make a mess. One day, the mop was with its friends. They were all talking and laughing. Then, something happened. A big wind came and blew the mop away. The mop was scared. It did not know where it was going. The wind took it to a place where there were many things on the ground. The mop looked around. It saw that it could gather the things. The mop started to gather the things. It was not rude anymore. It wanted to help. The mop worked hard to gather all the things. Soon, the mop's friends came to the place. They saw the mop working. They were surprised. They did not know that the mop could be nice. The mop had changed. Now, it liked to help and be kind. The mop and its friends were happy together, and they all learned that being nice is better than being rude.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was a very independent girl who liked to play by herself. She had a toy named Mr. Symbol, who was a funny little toy with a smiley face. One day, Amy and Mr. Symbol went to the park to play. They were having lots of fun on the swings and slides. Then, Amy decided to play a game with Mr. Symbol. She would drop him from the top of the slide and catch him at the bottom. "Ready, Mr. Symbol?" Amy asked. "Yes, let's play!" Mr. Symbol replied. Amy climbed to the top of the slide and dropped Mr. Symbol. She quickly ran to the bottom and caught him just in time. They both laughed and played the game again and again. After a while, it was time to go home. Amy and Mr. Symbol said goodbye to the park and walked back to their house. They were very happy to have had such a fun day together. And from that day on, they played the drop and catch game every time they went to the park.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a colorful party. At the party, there were many big balloons. The balloons were red, blue, and yellow. Everyone at the party was happy and having fun. A funny clown came to the party. The clown had a big red nose and big shoes. The clown said, "Hello, kids! I am here to perform for you!" The kids clapped their hands and laughed. The clown started to make animals with the colorful balloons. He made a dog, a cat, and a giraffe. The kids were very happy. They said, "Thank you, clown, for the fun show!" The clown smiled and waved goodbye. The party was a big success.

One day, a strong boy named Tom found a basket. He wanted to replace his old basket with this new one. The new basket was big and could carry many things. Tom went to the park with his new basket. He saw his friend, Sue, who had a small basket. "Sue, look at my new basket. It is strong and can carry many things," Tom said. Sue was sad because her basket was not strong. Sue asked Tom, "Can you help me replace my basket with a strong one like yours?" Tom wanted to help his friend. They went to the store and found a strong basket for Sue. Now, both friends had strong baskets and were very happy.
