Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Park

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a large slide. Tom wants to go on the slide, but Lily is scared. "Come on, Lily, it is fun!" Tom says. "No, Tom, it is too high. I do not want to go." Lily says. Tom goes on the slide by himself. He slides down fast and laughs. He feels brave. "Look at me, Lily, I did it!" Tom says. Lily looks at Tom. She feels sad. She wants to be brave too, but she is still scared. "Tom, you are mean. You do not care about me." Lily says. Tom feels bad. He does not want to hurt Lily. He wants to be her friend. "Lily, I am sorry. I do care about you. You are my friend." Tom says. He goes to Lily and hugs her. He says, "Do you want to try the slide with me? We can go together. I will hold your hand." Lily thinks. She is still scared, but she trusts Tom. She says, "Okay, Tom, I will try. But you have to be nice." Tom and Lily go to the slide. They hold hands. They slide down slowly and carefully. They feel happy. "Yay, Lily, you did it!" Tom says. "Yay, Tom, we did it!" Lily says. They hug each other. They feel proud. The other kids in the park see them. They clap their hands. They applaud Tom and Lily. They say, "Well done, Tom and Lily, you are brave!" Tom and Lily smile. They thank the other kids. They play together. They have a good day.

One day, Mommy was walking with her three-year-old girl. The girl saw a strawberry. She didn't know what it was, so she asked Mommy. "Mommy, what's that?" "That's a strawberry." The girl was excited. She wanted to try it! "Can I have one?" asked the girl. "Sure," said Mommy, "but first we have to find some. It's deep in the ground." The girl was determined to find some. She found a big patch of strawberries and told her Mommy. "Look, Mommy! I found some!" Mommy smiled. She knew her daughter had done a great job. They picked some strawberries and enjoyed them together. From that day on, the girl knew what a strawberry was.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tom. He liked to play outside in every season. His favorite season was summer, when it was warm and sunny. One day, Tom was playing with his ball. He threw it up high in the sky. But he missed the ball when it came down. The ball rolled far away, and Tom ran after it. Tom saw the ball near a big tree. He was happy to find it. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird came down and took the ball away. Tom was sad and cried. He never saw his ball again.

Once upon a time, there was a proud king who had a big staff of helpers. They all worked hard to make the king happy. One day, the king found out that one of his helpers had done something wrong. He called all of his staff to find out who did it. The king said, "Who did the bad thing? I will punish the one who did it." All the helpers were scared. They did not want to be punished. One helper, a little girl named Lily, was very brave. She said, "I did it, but it was an accident. I am sorry." The king looked at Lily and thought for a moment. Then he said, "Lily, I will not punish you. I am proud of you for telling the truth. You are a good helper." All the other helpers were surprised. They thought the king would be angry. But instead, he was happy because Lily was honest. From that day on, everyone in the staff worked harder and always told the truth.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was sad and ashamed because he had a hurt wing. He could not fly with his friends. Every day, he sat alone and watched the pretty sunset. One day, a kind rabbit named Lucy hopped by. She saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told her about his hurt wing. Lucy said, "I can help you heal your wing!" Tim was happy and thanked Lucy. Together, they found a soft leaf to wrap around Tim's wing. They waited for days and watched the sunsets together. Soon, Tim's wing was healed. He was not ashamed anymore. Tim and Lucy played and watched sunsets together forever.
