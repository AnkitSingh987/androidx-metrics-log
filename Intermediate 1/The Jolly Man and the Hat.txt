Category: Intermediate
Title: The Jolly Man and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a jolly man. He had a big hat and a long strip. The man liked to wave to his friends. Every day, he would walk down the street and wave to everyone he saw. One day, the jolly man went to the park. He saw kids playing and birds flying. He waved to them all. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew his hat off his head! The long strip flew away too! The jolly man was sad. But then, a little girl found his hat and strip. She waved them in the air. The jolly man saw her and smiled. He ran to the girl and thanked her. They both waved the strip together and laughed. The jolly man was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely mouse named Tim. Tim lived in a tiny house near a big tree. He had no friends to play with, so he felt sad. One day, Tim went out to find some cheese for his dinner. He loved cheese more than anything. As Tim walked, he noticed a big piece of cheese on the ground. He was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. A big cat jumped out from behind the tree! Tim was scared, but the cat did not want to hurt him. The cat said, "I am lonely too. Can we be friends and share the cheese?" Tim was surprised. He never thought a cat could be his friend. But, he was happy to share the cheese. So, Tim and the cat became friends and ate the cheese together. From that day on, they were never lonely again, and they shared many more adventures and lots of cheese.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a wide toy box. In the box, he had many toys. One day, Tim was playing with his toys when he saw a small, hurt bird outside his window. The bird could not stand because it had a hurt leg. Tim wanted to help the bird. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, the bird is hurt. It can't stand. Can we help it?" Mom nodded and said, "Yes, Tim. Let's get a bandage to help the bird." Tim and his mom went outside with a bandage. They put the bandage on the bird's hurt leg. The bird looked at them and said, "Thank you, Tim and Mom. Now I can stand again!" Tim and his mom smiled. They were happy they could help the bird. From that day, the bird became their friend and visited them every day.

Lulu and her mum were walking along the shore. Lulu loved running along the sand, it felt so soft between her toes. She stopped suddenly and pointed out to see. "Mum! Look, a beautiful bird on the shore," said Lulu. Mum smiled. She kneeled down. "Yes, it's so pretty," Mum said softly. The bird's feathers were soft and sweet. It looked so alone. Lulu wanted to help the bird, so she gently let it perch on her hand. Suddenly the bird began to weep. Tears rolled down the bird's soft wings. Mum took Lulu's hand and the bird fluttered away. Mum wiped away her own tears. "It's okay," she said. "We are all here for each other even though sometimes we're apart." Lulu hugged her mum tight and took her hand. They continued walking along the shore. Lulu knew that even though the bird had gone, her Mum was sweet and would never leave her side.

One day, a bald man went to the beach. He liked to play in the surf. The sun was bright, and the water was cool. He was very happy. He saw a big wave coming. So, he jumped on the wave and rode it. The bald man felt like he was flying. He waved to his friends on the beach. They waved back and clapped. The bald man played in the surf all day. He had so much fun. When it was time to go home, he waved goodbye to the waves. He couldn't wait to come back and play again.

Jill was feeling very spoiled. She had been given lots of new toys to play with and a brand new diary to write down all her secret thoughts. She loved spending time in her bedroom and would lie in her bed with her diary open, looking through all the pages. One day, she was feeling so happy that she decided to tell a little lie. She wrote in her diary that she owned a small pony, even though she knew it wasn't true. After that, she put her diary away and went off to play with her toys. The next day, Jill's mom found her diary and saw the lie Jill had written. She was a bit mad at first, but then she understood how happy it made Jill to pretend to own a pony. So her mom decided to take her to a farm and surprise her with a ride on a real pony. Jill was so excited when she saw the pony! She was thrilled that her lie had come true! She got to brush the pony and chat with the farmer, and spend the whole day in the sunny fields. Jill still has her diary and she still loves writing in it. But she hopes that she will never have to lie again!
