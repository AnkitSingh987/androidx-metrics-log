Category: Intermediate
Title: Amanda and the Fox

Once upon a time a little girl called Amanda was walking through the park. She saw a fox. The fox said, "Hello Amanda." Amanda said, "Hi fox!" The fox asked Amanda if she would like to have a picnic. She said, "Yes!" The fox grabbed some food from a nearby tree and put it in a basket. Inside the basket there was some cheese and sauce. The fox said, "Let's wrap this up so we can take it with us." So together they wrapped the basket with a piece of cloth. When they arrived at the picnic spot they spread the cloth on the grass. Amanda put the basket on the cloth. Then they started to eat. The cheese was creamy and the sauce was nice and sweet. Amanda said, "The food is so good!" The fox smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it." They ate until they were full and then packed up the basket and wrapped it back up again. They had a wonderful picnic and said goodbye to each other. Amanda walked home with the basket in her hand and a smile on her face.

Ben and Lily are playing in the living room. They have many toys, but they like the phone best. The phone has a cord that they can pull and twist. They pretend to talk to their friends and family. "Hello, this is Ben. How are you?" Ben says into the phone. "I'm fine, thank you. This is Lily. What are you doing?" Lily says into the other phone. "I'm playing with the phone. It's fun. Do you want to play with me?" Ben says. "Yes, I do. Can you bring the phone to me?" Lily says. Ben tries to bring the phone to Lily, but the cord is too short. He pulls and pulls, but the cord is hard and does not stretch. He gets angry and yells. "No, I can't. The cord is too hard. It won't let me. You come here!" Ben says. Lily gets sad and cries. She does not like to hear Ben yell. She wants to play with him, but she does not want to move. She whines. "No, you come here. I don't want to move. You are mean!" Lily says. Mom hears Ben and Lily fighting. She comes to the living room and sees them with the phone. She is not happy. She takes the phone away from them and puts it on the table. "Stop fighting, Ben and Lily. The phone is not a toy. It is for talking to people who are far away. You can't bring it to each other. You can play with something else. How about the blocks?" Mom says. Ben and Lily look at the blocks. They are colorful and have different shapes. They think they are more fun than the phone. They nod and smile. "Okay, Mom. We will play with the blocks. Sorry for fighting. We love you." Ben and Lily say. Mom hugs them and kisses them. She is happy. She loves them too. She leaves them to play with the blocks. They build a tower and a house. They share and help each other. They are good friends. They forget about the phone.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to visit her friend, the giant. The giant lived in a big house in the forest. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. Lucy was very happy to see her friend. When Lucy got to the giant's house, she knocked on the door. The door opened, and the giant came out. The giant was very big, but he was also very kind. Lucy was not frightened of the giant because they were good friends. They played together all day, and the giant showed Lucy his big garden. They picked flowers and chased butterflies. When it was time for Lucy to go home, the giant gave her a big hug. Lucy said goodbye and promised to visit the giant again soon.

Once upon a time, in a big, green forest, there lived a lazy alligator. He liked to sleep all day and did not like to work. One day, he met a very big and strong bear. The bear liked to work hard and help his friends. One day, the lazy alligator and the hard-working bear had a quarrel. The alligator said, "You work too much, and I don't like it!" The bear said, "You are too lazy, and I don't like it!" They were both very angry at each other. A few days later, the lazy alligator was sleeping by the river when he heard a loud sound. It was the big, strong bear! The bear was stuck in the river, and he needed help. The alligator, even though he was lazy, remembered their quarrel and knew he had to help his friend. So, the alligator swam to the bear and helped him out of the river. The bear said, "Thank you, alligator!" From that day on, they were friends and did not quarrel anymore.
