Category: Intermediate
Title: The Dog and Cat and the Bird

One day, a big dog and a little cat went for a walk. They saw a box with the word "salt" on it. The dog said, "I want to eat the salt!" The cat said, "No, it is not safe. We should ask mom first." They went to ask mom. On the way, they heard a loud "snap!" They saw a big tree had fallen down. The dog and cat were scared. They ran back to the box of salt. When they got there, they saw a little bird. The bird said, "I made the snap sound. I'm sorry I scared you. I was trying to break a stick to make a nest." The dog and cat felt better. They all went to ask mom about the salt. Mom said, "It's not safe to eat the salt. It's for cooking. Let's make a big meal together." The dog, cat, and bird helped mom cook. They had a fun day and learned that it's always good to ask mom first.

Once upon a time, there was a brave man. This man lived in a small house near the woods. He liked to help people and animals. One day, the brave man heard a scream in the woods. He ran to see who needed help. He found a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird said, "Help me, please!" The brave man gently picked up the bird and took it to his house. When they got to the house, something unexpected happened. The little bird turned into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for being so kind. I will give you a wish!" The brave man was very happy and wished for more ways to help others.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pipe in his yard. He picked it up and smiled. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house and knocked on the door. Sam came out, looking alert. "Look, Sam! I found a pipe!" Tim said, showing his friend the pipe. Sam smiled too, but then he saw a big dog coming. The dog wanted the pipe. The dog barked at Tim and Sam, trying to get the pipe. They were scared. "Let's give the dog the pipe," Sam said. Tim agreed, and they gave the pipe to the dog. The dog was happy, and he stopped barking. Tim and Sam smiled and played together. They were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a thick tree. It was so big that all the birds liked to live in it. One day, a little bird named Tim wanted to go on a trip. He had a plan to take a taxi to see his friend, who lived far away. Tim waited and waited for the taxi to come. When the taxi came, he was very happy. He got in and told the driver where he wanted to go. The driver was a kind man who liked to help little birds like Tim. The taxi went very fast, and soon Tim was with his friend. They played and had a lot of fun. When it was time to go home, Tim took the taxi again. He was happy he had made a good plan.

Once upon a time, in a modest little house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy loved to eat celery. Every day, she would munch on her crunchy celery sticks. One day, Amy heard about a race in her town. The prize for the winner was a big bag of celery. Amy wanted to win that prize. So, she put on her shoes and went to the race. Many kids were at the race, but Amy did not give up. She ran as fast as she could. At the end, she was very happy because she won the race! She got the big bag of celery and shared it with her friends. They all enjoyed the tasty celery together.

Once upon a time, there was a quick little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to paint with his friends. They would paint pictures of the sun, trees, and flowers. Remy's paintings always had bright colors that made everyone happy. One day, Remy found a rich, red paint in the forest. He was very excited to paint with it. He called his friends and they all came to see the new paint. They decided to paint a big picture together, using the rich red paint to make their art even more beautiful. As they painted, their picture became more and more amazing. The rich red paint made the sun look like it was shining, and the trees and flowers looked even more pretty. When they were done, Remy and his friends were very proud of their work. They hung the painting in the middle of the forest, so everyone could see it and enjoy the beautiful colors.
