Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Mouth

Once upon a time, in a tidy little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to pretend and play with her toys all day. She had a big toy box full of fun things to play with. One day, Lily found a toy called Mr. Mouth. Mr. Mouth was a funny toy with a big, happy face. Lily picked up Mr. Mouth and said, "Hello, Mr. Mouth! Do you want to play with me?" Mr. Mouth smiled and said, "Yes, Lily! I would love to play with you!" Lily and Mr. Mouth played all day. They pretended to be in a big castle, and Mr. Mouth was the king. They pretended to be in a boat on the water, and Mr. Mouth was the captain. They had so much fun. When it was time for bed, Lily put Mr. Mouth back in the tidy toy box and said, "Goodnight, Mr. Mouth! I will see you tomorrow!" Mr. Mouth smiled and said, "Goodnight, Lily! I can't wait to play again!"

Lila and Tom were twins who liked to help their elderly grandma in the garden. Grandma grew many kinds of fruit in her garden, like apples, pears, and grapes. She also had a big basket that she used to fill with fruit when it was ripe. One day, Lila and Tom decided to surprise their grandma by filling the basket with fruit for her. They went to the garden and picked some apples, pears, and grapes. They put them in the basket and carried it to the porch, where grandma was sitting in a rocking chair. "Look, grandma, we filled the basket with fruit for you!" Lila said, smiling. "That's very kind of you, my dears," grandma said, patting their heads. "Thank you for helping me. You are very good children." But then, a big dog came running from the street and saw the basket of fruit. He barked loudly and jumped on the porch, trying to get the fruit. Lila and Tom were scared and ran behind grandma, who tried to shoo the dog away. "Go away, you naughty dog!" grandma said, waving her cane. "This is not your fruit, this is our fruit!" The dog did not listen and kept barking and jumping. He knocked over the basket and the fruit spilled on the floor. He grabbed an apple and ran away, leaving the rest of the fruit behind. Lila and Tom were sad and angry. They had worked hard to fill the basket with fruit for their grandma, and now the dog had ruined it. They started to cry. "Shh, shh, it's okay, my dears," grandma said, hugging them. "Don't cry. It's not your fault. The dog was hungry and greedy. He did not know how to share or be polite. He was a bad dog." "Can we fill the basket again, grandma?" Lila asked, wiping her tears. "Of course, we can, sweetheart," grandma said, smiling. "We still have plenty of fruit in the garden. And we can also give some to our neighbors, who are kind and friendly. They will appreciate our fruit and share with us if we need it. They are good neighbors." Lila and Tom nodded and smiled. They learned that it is good to help others and share what you have, and that it is bad to be hungry and greedy and take what is not yours. They also learned to love their grandma and their neighbors more. They filled the basket again with fruit and gave some to their neighbors, who thanked them and gave them some cookies in return. They were happy and proud.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to class. He had a wide smile on his face because he was excited to learn. In his class, there were many kids who liked to play and learn too. His teacher, Miss Jane, said, "Today, we will pack a box with toys for kids who don't have any." All the kids were happy to help. They put many toys in the box, like balls, dolls, and cars. As they were packing the box, they heard a noise. They looked up and saw a big, friendly dog. The dog had a note on its collar. The note said, "I am a toy dog. Can I be in the box too?" The kids laughed and said, "Yes, you can be a toy for the kids too!" So, they put the toy dog in the box and finished packing. They were happy to help others and make new friends.
