Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to eat yogurt. One day, she found a big cup of yogurt in the fridge. She was very happy. But when she tried to open it, the yogurt spilled all over her dress. Lily's dress was filthy. Lily didn't want her mommy to see her filthy dress. She thought, "I need to fix this!" So, she went to her room and found her favorite toy, a little toy car. She put the toy car in the yogurt and started to play. The yogurt was like mud, and the toy car drove through it. Lily's dress was getting even more filthy. Lily's mommy came into the room and saw the mess. She wasn't mad. She said, "Lily, let's fix your dress together." They went to the bathroom and washed the dress. Then, they went back to the kitchen and mommy gave Lily a new cup of yogurt. Lily was very happy, and she promised to be more careful next time.

One day, a girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a bear. The bear was unusual. It had a big, red ball on its nose. Mia was not scared. She walked to the bear and said, "Hi, bear! Do you want to play?" The bear nodded and said, "Yes, I want to play. Can you help me ride my bike?" Mia smiled and said, "Of course, bear! Let's ride bikes together." They found two bikes and started to ride. The bear was very happy. As they rode their bikes, they saw other kids at the park. The kids were surprised to see an unusual bear riding a bike. They all wanted to play with Mia and the bear. They had a fun day at the park, riding bikes and playing together.

Tom liked to run and play in the park. He had a lot of fun with his ball and his dog. One day, he saw a new thing in the park. It was a big pole with a shiny top. Tom wanted to touch it and see what it was. He ran towards the pole, but he did not see the girl who was also running towards it. She had long hair and a pretty dress. She also wanted to touch the pole and see what it was. They both reached the pole at the same time and bumped into each other. They fell down and dropped their things. Tom and the girl looked at each other and said sorry. They helped each other get up and picked up their things. Tom saw that the girl had a doll and the girl saw that Tom had a ball. They smiled and decided to share their toys. They played together with the pole, the ball, and the doll. They had a lot of fun and became friends. Tom thought the girl was attractive and the girl thought Tom was attractive. They liked each other and wanted to play again. They asked each other their names and where they lived. They found out that they lived in the same street and went to the same school. They were happy and hugged each other. They said goodbye and ran back to their homes. They told their parents about their new friend and the pole. They asked if they could go to the park again tomorrow. Their parents said yes and smiled. They knew that Tom and the girl had met in a special way and that the pole had brought them together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma was nervous because she had lost her favorite toy. It was a toy with a long history in her family. Her mom had played with it when she was a little girl too. Emma looked everywhere for her toy. She asked her friend, Tom, "Have you seen my toy?" Tom shook his head and said, "No, I have not seen it. Let's look for it together." So, Emma and Tom searched high and low for the toy. They looked under the bed, behind the couch, and in the closet. Finally, they found the toy in the yard. Emma was so happy, she hugged Tom and said, "Thank you for helping me find my toy!" From that day on, Emma made sure to never lose her special toy again. She learned that friends are always there to help when you are nervous or sad. And that's the end of the story.
