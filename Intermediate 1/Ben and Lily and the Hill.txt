Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily and the Hill

Ben and Lily were best friends. They liked to play in the park near their homes. One day, they saw a big hill with a lot of grass and flowers. They wanted to climb the hill and see what was on the other side. "Let's go, Ben!" Lily said. "Maybe we can find a new land to play in." "OK, Lily, but be careful. The hill is very high and steep. We have to test the ground before we step on it. It might be slippery or have holes." Ben said. He was a thoughtful boy who always looked out for Lily. They held hands and started to climb the hill. They tested the ground with their feet and avoided the rocks and the thorns. They saw some birds and butterflies on the way. They also heard some strange noises, like growls and roars. "What is that, Ben?" Lily asked, feeling scared. "I don't know, Lily. Maybe it's some animals that live on the hill. Don't worry, they won't hurt us. We are almost at the top." Ben said, trying to be brave. They reached the top of the hill and looked around. They saw a beautiful land with trees, lakes, and flowers. They also saw some animals that they had never seen before. They saw a big lion, a tall giraffe, a striped zebra, and a funny monkey. "Wow, Ben, look at that! It's a new land! It's like a zoo, but without cages. Can we go and play with the animals?" Lily said, feeling excited. "Sure, Lily, but we have to be gentle and quiet. The animals might be shy or scared of us. We have to respect their home." Ben said, being thoughtful. They walked down the hill and approached the animals. The animals were curious and friendly. They let Ben and Lily touch them and feed them. Ben and Lily had a lot of fun playing with the animals. They felt like they had found a new land to explore. They were very happy.

One day, a boy named Tom was playing with his toy car. He loved his toy car very much. It was red and shiny. Tom was having lots of fun. Tom saw his friend, Lily, playing with her toy doll. Lily said, "Tom, let's play together!" Tom said, "Okay, but we have to take turns." They agreed to play in order. First, Tom played with his car, then Lily played with her doll. As they played, a big, tough dog came running up to them. The dog grabbed Tom's toy car and ran away. Tom and Lily were very sad. They did not know what to do. Then, the dog came back with the toy car. It was not broken. The dog just wanted to play, too! They all played together and had a great day.

Mickey and Jane were excited to play with their new bicycles. They had been hoping for this day for weeks. Mickey said "Let's have a race. I bet I can beat you!" Jane smiled and said "I'm ready!" The two of them got on their bikes and snapped the brakes. They started pedalling as fast as they could and Jane raced ahead. Mickey tried to pedal faster so he could catch up, but it was no use. Jane had already crossed the finish line. Mickey stopped and felt embarrassed. He had lost the race and knew Jane was the winner. Jane gave him a cheerful smile and said "That was a great race! It was really fun!" Mickey smiled back and said "Yeah, it was fun." Jane gave him a big hug and said "Let's play again tomorrow!" They smiled, snapped their brakes and peddled off together in search of a new adventure.

Mia and Jake were best friends since preschool. Today they were playing in the park with Mia’s toy train. Mia was pushing the train and Jake was walking beside her. Suddenly, Mia noticed a rail a few metres away. She pointed at it and said “Jake look!” Jake crossed the road and spread his arms wide as he shouted “I'm the rail!” Mia couldn’t believe her eyes, she ran across the road and tried to spread her arms but Jake got to it first. Mia got angry when Jake wouldn’t let her play. “Leave it Mia,” Jake said in a serious tone, “It’s my rail now.” Mia crossed her arms, grumpy, and stomped back to her train. Jakes said “Sorry Mia!”, but she didn’t respond. Jake then noticed her lip trembling and said “let’s build something together, Mia!” Mia looked up, her eyes brightened and said “Really?!”. Jake nodded and they went to work, building a big track with their toy trains. They spread some rocks around the track and added many different details. Mia and Jake had so much fun together, and the rail was forgotten. Together, they created something magical.
