Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Dog and the Cat

One day, a big dog and a little cat were in a messy yard. They did not like the mess. The dog said, "Let's do a clean up!" The cat said, "Yes, let's do it!" So they started to clean up the yard together. As they were cleaning, they found a big toy. The dog wanted the toy, and the cat wanted the toy too. They started to fight over the toy. The dog said, "This toy is mine!" The cat said, "No, it is mine!" Suddenly, a bird came and said, "Stop the fight! You should share the toy!" The dog and the cat looked at each other and said, "You are right, we should share." So, they stopped fighting and played with the toy together. They had fun and the yard was clean. They learned that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time there was a farmer who lived on a farm. Every day he worked hard to take care of his animals and plants. One day he looked at his wheat field and saw it was dry. He was worried. He needed to get water to make it grow again. So, he put on his special wide hat and grabbed his bucket. The farmer walked to the river and filled his bucket with water. He also grabbed a bag of wheat and went back to the field. He poured water everywhere, taking care to not miss any of the plants. When the farmer finished pouring water, he took the bag of wheat and poured it in the middle of the field. This way, the wheat could help the plants grow. The farmer smiled and was glad that he had saved his wheat field. The farmer went back home, feeling happy. He knew that the wheat would help the field grow and his animals would get something to eat. He was no longer worried.

Lily and Max were playing in the kitchen. They liked to pretend they were spies and had guns. They used spoons and forks as their guns. They hid behind the table and the chairs. "Bang, bang, I shoot you, Max!" Lily said, pointing her spoon at her brother. "No, you miss, I shoot you, Lily!" Max said, throwing his fork at her. They laughed and ran around the kitchen. They saw the dishwasher. It was big and blue. It had a door and buttons. "Look, Max, a secret base!" Lily said. "Let's go inside and see what's there!" They opened the door and climbed into the dishwasher. It was dark and wet. They saw plates and cups and knives. "Wow, this is cool!" Max said. "Maybe we can find some clues or treasures!" They started to touch and move the things inside the dishwasher. They did not know that Mom was watching them. She was not happy. "Hey, you two, what are you doing in there?" Mom said, turning on the light. "Get out of the dishwasher right now! You can hurt yourself or break something!" Lily and Max were scared. They dropped their spoons and forks and got out of the dishwasher. They looked at Mom. She was angry. "Sorry, Mom, we were just playing," Lily said. "We didn't mean to be bad," Max said. Mom sighed. She hugged them and said, "I know you were just playing, but the dishwasher is not a toy. It is for washing dishes, not for hiding or shooting. You have to be careful and listen to me. Do you understand?" Lily and Max nodded. They said, "Yes, Mom, we understand. We won't do it again." Mom smiled. She said, "Good. Now, why don't you go and play in your room? I have to finish the dishes." Lily and Max said, "Okay, Mom, we love you." They ran to their room and played with their toys. They still liked to pretend they were spies, but they did not use the dishwasher as their secret base anymore. They learned their lesson.

One day, Tim and his mom went for a walk. They saw a big tree. Tim noticed something shiny by the tree. It was a little spirit. The spirit was very polite and said "hello" to Tim and his mom. The spirit told them it was lost and needed help. Tim and his mom wanted to help the spirit. They looked around to find the spirit's home. After walking for a bit, they found a small house. The spirit was happy and said "thank you" to Tim and his mom. They all went inside and had a fun day playing together.
