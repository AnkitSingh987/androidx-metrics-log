Category: Intermediate
Title: Rolly the Ball and the Sally

One day, a little ball named Rolly went out to play. Rolly loved to roll around in the soil. He felt lucky to have a big yard to play in. As he rolled, he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was sad. Rolly rolled up to Sally and asked, "Why are you sad?" Sally looked at Rolly and said, "I lost my toy in the soil. I can't find it." Rolly wanted to help Sally. So, they both started to look for the toy in the soil. After a while, Rolly found the toy. He rolled it to Sally. Sally was so happy! She said, "Thank you, Rolly! You are a lucky ball and a good friend." From that day on, Rolly and Sally played together every day in the soil. They were both very lucky to have each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little red car with a big motor. The car was sad and miserable. It wanted to help others, but no one needed a little red car. One day, the little red car heard a cry. It was a tiny cat stuck in a tree. "Help me, please!" said the tiny cat. The little red car wanted to save the cat, but it didn't know how. The little red car thought and thought. Then, it had an idea! It used its big motor to drive very fast and hit the tree. The tree shook and the tiny cat fell onto the soft grass. The cat was saved and happy! The little red car was not miserable anymore. It had found a way to help others.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Max. Max was very tired one day. He wanted to take a nap. So, Max found a soft and pretty blouse on the floor. He thought it was a good place to sleep. Max curled up on the blouse and started to nap. Soon, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw Max sleeping on her blouse. Lily said, "Max, that's my blouse! You can't nap there!" Max woke up and looked at Lily. He knew he should not sleep on her blouse. So, Max got up and found a different spot to nap. Lily was happy, and Max had a good nap. They both had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with bubbles. She would blow bubbles every day. She also liked yummy treats like cookies and ice cream. One day, Lily saw a big bubble in her yard. It was the biggest bubble she had ever seen! She tried to pop the bubble, but it would not pop. The bubble seemed to refuse to be popped. Lily was very confused. Then, something unexpected happened. The big bubble started to talk! It said, "Hello, Lily! I am a magic bubble. I can give you one yummy treat if you don't pop me." Lily was very surprised, but she agreed. The magic bubble gave her a big ice cream cone. Lily was very happy and never tried to pop the magic bubble again.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom loved to have picnics in the park. One sunny day, he called his friend, Sue. "Sue, let's have a picnic today!" he said. Sue was happy and said, "Yes, Tom! I will bring food!" Before the picnic, Tom wanted to find the best spot in the park. He saw a big tree with lots of shade. He thought, "I will measure how much shade the tree has." He walked around the tree and saw that it had a lot of shade. He said, "This is the perfect spot for our picnic!" Later, Tom and Sue had their picnic under the big tree. They ate yummy food and had lots of fun. When they were done, Tom said, "Sue, I'm glad we had our picnic here. The shade from the tree was perfect!" Sue agreed and they both laughed. They knew they would have many more picnics under the big tree.

Once upon a time, there was a slow dove. The dove lived in a big tree. The dove had a friend, a little girl. The little girl liked to cook. They played together every day. One day, the slow dove saw a big cat. The big cat wanted to eat the dove. The dove was scared. The little girl saw the big cat too. She wanted to help her friend, the slow dove. The little girl had an idea. She went to her home and started to cook. She cooked a yummy fish. The big cat smelled the fish. The big cat liked fish more than the dove. The big cat ate the fish and left the slow dove alone. The slow dove was safe, and they played together again.
