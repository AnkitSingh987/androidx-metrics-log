Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball and the Cup

Once upon a time, there was a ball. The ball loved to bounce. One day, the ball saw a cup of milk on a table. The ball thought, "I want to bounce near the milk." The ball had a bad feeling. It felt guilty. But the ball wanted to bounce near the milk anyway. So, the ball went bounce, bounce, bounce, getting closer to the milk. Then, the ball went bounce, bounce, bounce, and hit the milk. The milk fell off the table. The milk went all over the floor. The ball was sad. It knew it should not have bounced near the milk. Now, there was a big mess and no more milk.

Once upon a time, there was a panda named Pandy. Pandy was anxious because he wanted to escape from the zoo. He missed his home in the big forest with all his panda friends. One day, Pandy saw a big hole in the zoo wall. He thought, "This is my chance to escape!" So, Pandy went through the hole and ran away. He was so happy to be free and went to find his friends in the forest. But when he got to the forest, he saw that all his panda friends were gone. Pandy was sad and confused. Then, he heard a noise and saw his friends coming towards him. They had also escaped from the zoo! They were all so happy to see each other and played together in the forest. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to join her friends and play outside in the big world. One sunny day, Amy saw a new friend. His name was Tim. Tim was sad because he didn't have anyone to play with. Amy said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to join me and play together?" Tim's face lit up, and he said, "Yes, I would love to join you!" They went to the playground and had lots of fun playing on the swings, slide, and seesaw. After playing, they got hungry and decided to eat some fruit. Amy had a sweet apple, and Tim had a sour lemon. Tim made a funny face when he tasted the sour lemon, and they both laughed. They had a great day and became best friends. And from that day on, they always joined each other to play and explore the big world together.

One day, a happy dog named Max went for a walk down the street. Max liked to walk and see all the things around him. He saw trees, cars, and other animals. Max was very happy, but he did not know that something was waiting for him at the end of the street. Max met a little bird who was not happy. The bird sat on a branch and said, "I am sad because I can't find my friend." Max wanted to help the bird, so he said, "I will help you find your friend. Let's look together!" They looked all around the street, but they could not find the bird's friend. The little bird started to complain. "I miss my friend so much! Where could she be?" Max had an idea. He remembered seeing a bird's nest in a tree at the start of the street. Max said, "Let's go back to the start of the street. I think I know where your friend is." They went back to the start of the street and found the bird's friend in the nest. The little bird was so happy to see her friend again. Max was happy too, because he helped his new friends. From that day on, Max and the birds became best friends and played together on the street every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a mighty boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gun. He would run around the yard, making loud noises and pretending to shoot at things. One day, Tim saw some smoke in the sky. He thought it was a dragon. He grabbed his toy gun and ran to see what was making the smoke. As he got closer, he saw that it was just a small fire in a neighbor's yard. Tim knew he had to help. He aimed his toy gun at the fire and pretended to shoot at it. He made loud noises and shouted, "I am mighty Tim, and I will stop this fire!" His mom saw him and smiled. She knew Tim was a mighty helper. Together, they put out the fire and saved the day.
