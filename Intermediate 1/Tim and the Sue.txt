Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Sue

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to walk. He walked to the park, to the store, and to his friend's house. One day, Tim saw a big man in a uniform. The man was a police officer. Tim liked the uniform and wanted to be a police officer when he grew up. The next day, Tim went to school. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad. Tim asked her why she was sad. Sue said her toy was gone. Tim wanted to help Sue. He put on his pretend police uniform and went to find Sue's toy. Tim walked all around the school. He looked in the hall, the playground, and the lunchroom. Finally, Tim found the toy under a table. It was a little dirty, but not bad. Tim gave the toy back to Sue. She was so happy! Tim felt like a real police officer in his uniform. And from that day on, Tim and Sue were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. The rock had a friend named Wheel. They loved to play together. One day, Rock said, "Let's have a race!" Wheel was excited and said, "Yes, let's do it!" They went to a hill and started the race. Wheel rolled down the hill fast, and Rock rolled down too, but not as fast as Wheel. At the bottom of the hill, Wheel was happy because he won the race. He felt successful. Rock was proud of his friend and said, "Good job, Wheel!" They both laughed and played more games together.

Once upon a time, there was a furry cat named Sam. Sam liked to serve tea to his friends. One day, Sam invited his friend, a little bird named Lily, to his house for a tea party. As they were having tea, Lily found a nail on the floor. She picked it up and said, "Sam, I found a nail. What should we do with it?" Sam thought for a moment and then had an idea. Sam said, "Let's use the nail to hang a picture on the wall." So they found a pretty picture and used the nail to hang it up. They were very happy and went back to their tea party. From that day on, Sam and Lily had many more fun tea parties together.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there lived a big bear named Bob. Bob loved to eat berries. One day, while he was walking, he found the biggest berry bush he had ever seen. Bob said, "Wow! Look at all the berries! I must protect them so no one else can eat them." He sat down by the bush and watched it all day and all night. One morning, a little bird named Tina came by. She saw the big berry bush and wanted to eat some berries too. "Can I have some berries, please?" she asked Bob. Bob thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can have some berries, but we must protect them together." Tina agreed, and they became friends. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the berries away! Bob and Tina were sad, but they learned that they couldn't protect everything. They decided to look for more berries together, and they became the best of friends.

One day, a polite boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a shiny rock on the ground. It was a mineral! Tim picked it up and put it in his pocket. Tim saw his friend Sally and said, "Look what I found!" Sally looked at the mineral and said, "Wow, that's pretty!" Tim wanted to show his mom, so he and Sally walked to his house. As they walked, Tim dropped the mineral. It broke into two pieces! But instead of being sad, Tim and Sally saw that inside the mineral was a tiny, shiny star. They both smiled and knew they found something very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. Every day, he would go outside and play in his big yard. He loved to run, jump, and climb trees. One sunny day, Tim saw a man in his yard. The man was a thief! Tim was scared, but he wanted to stop the thief. He thought of a plan. He would make friends with the thief and then tell his mom. So, Tim went up to the thief and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" The thief was surprised. He said, "Okay, let's play." They played together for a while. Tim saw that the thief was not so bad. Tim asked, "Why do you take things?" The thief said, "I have no food or toys. I just want to be happy." Tim felt sad for the thief. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's go to my house. My mom can help you." The thief agreed, and they went inside. Tim's mom gave the thief food and a job. The thief was happy, and he stopped taking things. Tim and the thief became good friends, and their love for each other increased.
