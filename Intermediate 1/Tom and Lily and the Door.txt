Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily and the Door

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play games. They had a big box of toys in their room, but they also liked to find new things to play with. One day, they saw a shiny trophy on the shelf in their dad's office. They wanted to touch it and pretend it was theirs. But the trophy was behind a glass door with a lock. They needed a key to open it. They looked around the office and saw a bunch of keys on a hook. Tom reached for them, but Lily stopped him. "No, Tom, those are dad's keys. He will be mad if we take them. We have to ask him first," she said. She was very bossy and always told Tom what to do. Tom did not like that. He wanted to be brave and unlock the door by himself. He did not want to wait for dad. He pushed Lily away and grabbed the keys. He ran to the glass door and tried to find the right key. He put one key in the lock and turned it, but nothing happened. He tried another key, and another, and another. Lily followed him and watched him. She was scared and angry. She wanted to tell him to stop, but she also wanted to see the trophy. She hoped he would find the right key soon. But he did not. He tried all the keys, but none of them worked. The lock was still locked. He was sad and frustrated. He threw the keys on the floor and started to cry. Lily felt sorry for him. She hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tom. We can play with something else. Maybe dad will let us see the trophy later. Come on, let's go back to our room." They picked up the keys and put them back on the hook. They left the office and went to their room. They did not see dad coming home. He saw the glass door and the keys and knew what they had done. He was not happy. He decided to teach them a lesson. He took the trophy and hid it in a new place. He locked it with a different key. He waited for them to ask him about it. He wanted to see if they would be honest and sorry. He wanted to see if they would learn to respect his things. He wanted to see if they would be good twins.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy loved to fly high in the sky. He wanted to soar up in the clouds like his Uncle Bob. Uncle Bob was a big, strong bird who could fly very high. Timmy was proud of his uncle and wanted to be just like him. One day, Timmy saw a big tree. He thought, "If I can fly to the top of the tree, maybe I can soar like Uncle Bob." Timmy tried and tried, but he could not fly to the top. He was sad, but he did not give up. He knew he would soar one day. The next day, Timmy saw a big hill. He thought, "If I can run up the hill, maybe it will help me fly like Uncle Bob." Timmy ran up the hill and tried to fly. This time, he soared high in the sky! Timmy was so happy and proud. He knew that if he kept trying, he could be just like Uncle Bob. And from that day on, Timmy soared high in the sky, just like his uncle.

Once upon a time, a three year old and his parents went on an adventure. They went to the park and played on the swings, walked around the lake, and made lots of new friends. At the end of the day they stopped in front of a large stage. They saw microphone, a DJ, and lots of people gathered around. The three year old was so excited to see a real microphone! He asked his parents if he could try it out. His parents were hesitant, but the DJ promised he would help the boy. So, his parents agreed, the DJ helped and the three year old began to sing. He wasn't very good, but the crowd began to clap and cheer anyway. Then, the DJ insisted that the three year old keep singing. He kept singing over and over again, until the microphone began to sputter and spark! The three year old gasped as light shed from the microphone, and everyone ran away from the stage. The three year old burst into tears, not understanding what had just happened. He had ruined the microphone! The bad ending was that the DJ would not forgive him.
