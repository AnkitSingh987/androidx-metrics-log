Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Cheerful Crab and the Fish

One day, a cheerful crab named Tim found a shiny shell on the beach. He was so happy because he wanted a new home. He picked up the shell and started to walk away. But then, a little fish named Finn swam up to him. "Hey, that's my shell!" said Finn. Tim and Finn started to disagree about whose shell it was. They both wanted the shiny shell for their home. As they were talking, a big wave came and took the shell away. Tim and Finn were sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, friendly whale named Willy swam up to them. He had seen the shiny shell and brought it back to them. "Here is the shell," Willy said. "But you must learn to share." Tim and Finn agreed to share the shell and became best friends. They thanked Willy and lived happily in their shared shell home.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a noisy dog named Max. Max loved to play and run around all day. One day, Max saw a big cat in the yard. The cat was very pretty and had a long tail. Max wanted to be friends with the cat. Max went to the cat and said, "Hi, I am Max. Do you want to play with me?" The cat looked at Max and said, "I am Lily. I will play with you, but I need help. I am very hungry, and I want some sauce to eat." Max wanted to care for Lily, so he went to the kitchen to find some sauce. He found a big jar of sauce and brought it to Lily. Lily was very happy and ate the sauce. She said, "Thank you, Max. This sauce is very yummy." After eating the sauce, Max and Lily played together all day long. They became best friends and always took care of each other. The house was noisy with their laughter and fun, but everyone was happy because Max and Lily were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big ostrich named Olly. Olly lived in a comfortable nest with his family. Every day, Olly would stretch his long neck to look for food. One day, while Olly was stretching his neck, he saw a small bird named Billy. Billy had a short neck and could not reach the tasty leaves on the tree. Olly asked, "Do you need help, Billy?" "Yes, please!" said Billy. Olly stretched his long neck and picked some leaves for Billy. Billy was very happy and thanked Olly. They became good friends and played together every day. From that day on, Olly and Billy would always help each other. They learned that being kind and sharing made them both very happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the farm with his mom. They saw a lot of wheat in the field. The wheat was tall and clean. Tom liked to play in the wheat. Tom saw a duck in the field. The duck said, "Quack! I am lost. Can you help me?" Tom wanted to help the duck. He took the duck's wing and started to lead it to the pond. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the wheat all around. Tom and the duck could not see the pond. They were scared. Tom and the duck held hands and walked slowly. They found a big rock. They climbed on the rock and saw the pond. Tom and the duck were happy. They went to the pond and the duck said, "Thank you, Tom!"

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. It lived in a big park with lots of grass. The smooth rock had no name. It was sad. One day, a nice man with a staff came to the park. The man saw the smooth rock and smiled. He said, "You need a name. I will call you 'Smoothie.'" Smoothie, the smooth rock, was very happy. The man with the staff put Smoothie in his pocket. They went for a walk in the park together. Now, Smoothie had a name and a friend.
