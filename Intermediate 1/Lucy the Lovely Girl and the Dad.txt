Title: Lucy the Lovely Girl and the Dad

One day, a lovely little girl named Lucy went to her dad's shed. She saw many tools. She wanted to touch them. Her dad said, "Be careful, some tools can hurt." The next day, Lucy went back to the shed. She saw a soft, red tool. She touched it. It was nice to touch. She showed it to her dad. "Look, dad! I found a nice tool," she said. Her dad smiled. "That's a paint brush, Lucy," he said. "I was looking for that. Now we can paint your room together." Lucy was so happy. She knew that touching the red tool was a good idea.

Once upon a time, there was a proud ship. The ship was big and strong. Many people liked the ship. One day, the ship was going to welcome new friends. The friends were happy to come on the ship. The sky was dark. The ship went on the water. The people on the ship saw something far away. They did not know what it was. They thought it was a friend, so they went to it. But it was not a friend. It was a big wave. The wave was very big and strong. The proud ship tried to go away, but it could not. The big wave came and the ship went down. The people were sad. The proud ship was gone.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. One day, Tom's mom asked him to organize his toys. "Tom, please put your toys away," she said. Tom felt foolish because he had left his toys all over the floor. Tom went to his room and saw a big mess. He looked at the curtain and had an idea. He pulled the curtain across the room to hide the toys. He thought he was very smart. When his mom came to check on him, she saw the curtain hiding the toys. She shook her head and said, "Tom, hiding your toys is not the same as organizing them. It's important to learn to clean up after yourself." Tom learned that trying to hide his mess was not the right thing to do. From that day on, he always organized his toys and kept his room clean. The moral of the story is to always clean up after yourself and not try to hide your mess.

Adam was very lazy. He had to take a trip, but he didn't want to pack any luggage. So he split his backpack in two pieces thinking it would be enough to carry his things. He went to his mom and said, "Mom, look. I split my backpack in two smaller pieces." His mom smiled and said, "Oh, Adam, that's not enough to take all your things. You need to split the two pieces of your luggage too." Adam frowned. "But it'll take too long," he said. "No, it won't," his mom said. "I'll help you. Just be patient." So Adam and his mom split the two pieces of luggage in two more pieces. It was hard work, but it was worth it in the end. Adam was excited to have enough space for all of his things and he was excited for his trip.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy who had a statue of a horse. He loved it very much, so he always played with it. One day, he went too far and broke the statue. He felt so guilty that he screamed and ran away. As he was running, he heard an angry voice calling his name. It was his mom. She said, "That statue belonged to your grandmother. You have to be responsible for the things that are given to you." The little boy felt so guilty and scared that he screamed again and ran faster. He kept running and running until he saw a big, dark cave. He went inside to hide, but as soon as he stepped in, he realized it was a mistake. It was an old man's home and the old man was very angry. The old man yelled, "How dare you intrude my home! You are an irresponsible boy!" The little boy was so scared that he screamed and ran away. He ran until he could no longer hear the old man's voice. The moral of this story is that we should be responsible for the things we are given and we should be more careful when making decisions. Otherwise, we can find ourselves in very bad situations.
