Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy the Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy had a friend named Benny the squirrel. They lived in a big tree near a small town. One day, Timmy and Benny saw a man spreading seeds on the ground. They were very happy and ready to eat the seeds. "Let's go eat the seeds!" said Timmy to Benny. They ran to the seeds and started to eat them. But then, they saw a big sign that said, "Do not eat the seeds!" They were scared because they didn't want to go to jail. "We should not eat these seeds," said Benny. "We don't want to go to jail." So, they stopped eating the seeds and went back to their tree. They learned that it is important to follow the rules and not take things that don't belong to them. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a purple ball. This ball loved to bounce all day long. It would bounce high and low, all around the town. The purple ball was very happy. One day, while bouncing, the purple ball found a piece of paper. The paper was on the ground, near a big tree. The purple ball picked up the paper with its bouncy body and took it home. At home, the purple ball put the paper on the table. It used crayons to draw pretty pictures on the paper. The purple ball drew a big sun, a green tree, and a blue sky. The purple ball felt proud of its drawings and hung the paper on the wall. Now, the purple ball had a beautiful picture to look at while it bounced happily ever after.

One day, Mom was feeling worried. She looked at her family and said "We need to tie this rope around the tree." Dad and the little girl tied it tight and the rope made a big knot. Dad asked, "Why do we need this rope?" Mom said, "We need to tie the rope around the tree because it will help keep us safe." The little girl was worried. She asked, "What if it rains?" Mom said, "We will stay warm and dry inside our tent. Our family will be safe here." The family tied the rope to the tree. Then they put the tent up and Dad started a fire to keep them warm. The family was now safe and happy. They all felt much better and Mom was no longer worried.

Once upon a time there was a boat. It was bright and shiny and waiting for something to happen. One day, a man came and got in the boat. He was going to take it for a drive. The man turned the wheel of the boat and started to drive. The water moved around the boat and made it go really fast. Everywhere they went looked so exciting. After they had been driving for a while, the man decided that it was time to stop. He found a quiet spot in the sea and switched off the engine. Suddenly it was very quiet and the man got very bored. So, he decided to make some noise. He shouted and clapped his hands. Then he sang a happy song and suddenly the boat was a lot of fun!

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Rich. He had a big house, and he loved to dance. Mr. Rich was very happy, but he had no friends. One day, Mr. Rich saw a little girl named Sally. Sally had a pretty dress and was dancing in the park. She looked so happy. Mr. Rich wanted to be her friend, so he went to talk to her. "Hi, Sally! I like your dance. Can I dance with you?" asked Mr. Rich. Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Mr. Rich! Let's dance together!" They danced and laughed in the sun. Their skin felt warm and happy. After they danced, Sally and Mr. Rich sat down to rest. Mr. Rich said, "Thank you, Sally. I had fun dancing with you. Do you want to be my friend?" Sally smiled and said, "Yes, Mr. Rich! I want to be your friend." From that day on, Mr. Rich and Sally were best friends. They danced and played together every day. Mr. Rich learned that having a friend is better than being wealthy. And they lived happily ever after.
