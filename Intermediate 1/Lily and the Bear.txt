Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Bear

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy named Bear. Bear was very polite and always said "please" and "thank you." Lily and Bear played together every day. One day, Lily and Bear found a long tape. They wanted to play with it, but they did not know what to do. Lily wanted to use the tape to make a dress for Bear. Bear wanted to use the tape to make a road for his toy cars. They could not agree on what to do with the tape. Lily and Bear decided to ask their friend, Cat, for help. Cat listened to both ideas and thought for a moment. Then, Cat had a great idea. They could use the tape to make both a dress and a road! Lily and Bear loved the idea. They worked together and had lots of fun. In the end, they learned that sharing and working together can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy that she liked a lot. It was not a normal toy. This toy could talk! One day, the toy said, "Lily, let's try to play a new game." Lily was happy and said, "Yes, let's try!" They played a game of hide and seek. The toy hid, and Lily tried to find it. Lily looked under her bed, behind the door, and in the closet. She could not find her toy. Then, she heard a small noise. It was her toy! Lily found it in a box with other toys. They laughed and played more games together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to play with her toys. She had a big box full of them. One day, she decided to find the toys that match. She was very patient and took her time. Amy looked at all her toys. She found a red car and a red ball. "These match!" she said with a big smile. She looked again and found a blue truck and a blue hat. "These match too!" she said, clapping her hands. Amy kept looking and being patient. She found more toys that match. She had so much fun playing with her matching toys. At the end of the day, she put all her toys back in the box. Amy was happy and proud of her matching game.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Bob. He lived in a small house with his pet potato. Bob loved his potato very much. They played together every day. One sunny day, Bob wanted to write a letter to his friend, Tim. He picked up a pen and paper. But he did not know what to write. So, he asked his potato for help. The potato thought for a moment and then told Bob to write about their fun days together. Bob wrote the letter and then read it to his potato. They both liked the letter very much. They put the letter in an envelope and sent it to Tim. Tim loved the letter too, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was always getting into trouble. One day, he went outside to play in the cold. He saw a big, red apple on the ground. Tim picked up the apple and said, "I am going to eat this apple!" But his friend, Sue, said, "No, Tim! Don't eat it! That apple is trouble." Tim did not listen to Sue. He took a big bite of the apple. Suddenly, his tummy felt bad. Sue was right, the apple was trouble. Tim felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Sue. I should have listened to you." From that day on, Tim always listened to his friends and stayed out of trouble. He learned that sometimes, things that look good can be bad. And he never ate a cold apple from the ground again.

Once upon a time, there was a big orange lion. He lived in a green forest. The lion liked to play with his friends. His friends were a small bird, a fast rabbit, and a slow turtle. One day, the lion and his friends wanted to play a new game. They decided to play "spring". They took turns jumping high into the air. The bird flew up, the rabbit hopped high, and the turtle tried his best to jump. The orange lion was very good at "spring". He jumped the highest of all his friends. They all laughed and had fun. They played "spring" all day long. The lion and his friends were very happy.
