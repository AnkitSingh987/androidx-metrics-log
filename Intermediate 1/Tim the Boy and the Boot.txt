Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Boot

One day, a boy named Tim found a big red boot in his room. He thought it was boring and didn't want it. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, I found this boring boot. Can we send it away?" His mom looked at the boot and said, "Yes, we can send it to your cousin Jake. Maybe he will like it." The next day, they sent the boot to Jake. When Jake got the boot, he was very happy. He put it on and started to dance. As he danced, something unexpected happened. The boring boot turned into a fun magic boot! Jake called Tim and said, "Thank you for sending me the boot. It's not boring at all. It's a magic boot that makes me dance!" Tim was surprised and happy that his boring boot was now a magic boot for his cousin. From that day on, they both loved the magic boot and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a big tent in a distant place. It was a very nice tent. Many people liked to go there and play. One day, a little girl named Mia went to the tent. She saw her friends acting like animals. They were having so much fun. Mia wanted to act like an animal too. Mia acted like a cat. She said, "Meow!" Her friends laughed and clapped. They all acted like animals together. They played in the tent all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a pet cat that he loved very much. One day, Tim found a wild cat outside. The wild cat was very thin and hungry. Tim wanted to help, so he decided to feed the wild cat. Tim went inside and asked his mom, "Can I feed the wild cat, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful. The wild cat is not like our cat." Tim took some food and went outside. He put the food near the wild cat. The wild cat ate the food quickly. Tim wanted to play with the wild cat. He took his chess game outside. Tim tried to play chess with the wild cat, but the wild cat did not know how to play. The wild cat knocked the chess pieces down. Tim was sad, but he knew that the wild cat was still hungry. He went inside and got more food for the wild cat. The wild cat was happy, and Tim learned that not all cats like to play chess.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Anna. She had a pretty blouse that she loved to wear. One day, she saw something unusual in her garden. It was a big, colorful butterfly. Anna called her friend, Tom. "Tom, come see this big butterfly!" Tom came running and saw the unusual butterfly too. They both wanted to touch it, but the butterfly flew away. They decided to split up and look for it. Anna went left, and Tom went right. While looking for the butterfly, Anna's blouse got caught on a bush. It started to split! Anna was sad. Tom heard her cry and came running to help. Tom looked at the split blouse and said, "Don't worry, Anna. My mom can fix it." They went to Tom's house, and his mom sewed the blouse back together. Anna was happy again. In the end, Anna and Tom didn't find the unusual butterfly, but they had fun playing together. And Anna learned that even when things go wrong, friends can help make it better.

One day, a bright sun was in the sky. A girl named Sue went to the store. She wanted to buy a pastry. Sue saw a big pastry with red jam inside. She was very happy. Sue went to the man at the store. She said, "I want this pastry, please." The man said, "You need to pay two coins for the pastry." Sue gave the man two coins and took the pastry. Sue went home and showed the pastry to her mom. Her mom said, "That's a big pastry! Let's share it." Sue and her mom ate the pastry together. It was very yummy. They both smiled and had a good day.
