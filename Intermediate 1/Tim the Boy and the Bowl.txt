Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Bowl

One day, a boy named Tim went to play outside. It was very cold and icy. Tim saw his uncle Jack in the yard. Uncle Jack was trying to freeze water in a big bowl. Tim wanted to help his uncle. "Why do you want to freeze the water, Uncle Jack?" Tim asked. Uncle Jack said, "I want to make a big ice block for our game." Tim wanted to play the game too, but he was too small. This made Tim sad. Tim had an idea. He asked his uncle, "Can I help you make the ice block?" Uncle Jack smiled and said, "Yes, you can help me, Tim." They worked together to freeze the water. Tim was happy to help and be part of the game.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a pigeon. The pigeon had a tight string around its leg. The pigeon looked sad. Tim wanted to help the pigeon. Tim went to the pigeon and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." He tried to take off the tight string, but it was too hard. Tim did not quit. He tried again and again. At last, Tim's mom called him to come home. Tim had to quit helping the pigeon. He said, "I'm sorry, I can't help you anymore." The pigeon was still sad. Tim went home, and the pigeon stayed with the tight string on its leg.

Once upon a time, there was a big dinosaur. The dinosaur was very sleepy. He wanted to find a place to sleep. The dinosaur saw a big tree. He thought it was a good place to sleep. But a little bird urged the dinosaur to find a better place. The bird said, "This tree is too small for you." So, the sleepy dinosaur walked and walked. He found a big cave. The dinosaur went inside the cave and fell asleep. The little bird was happy that the dinosaur found a good place to sleep. They both had a good night's sleep.

Once upon a time there was a girl. She was small and liked to explore. One day, she decided to go on an adventure. She entered a big building that was made of steel. Inside, she found lots of interesting things. She saw toys, a big slide and even swings! She stayed in the building for a long time and was very happy. At the end of the day, she was tired so she decided to go home. She said goodbye to the building and as she left, she couldn't wait to come back and explore again. The end.

One sunny day, a little bird named Tim was flying high in the sky. It was Tuesday, and Tim was looking for a place to settle and rest. He saw a big tree and thought it would be a good spot to relax. As Tim landed on a branch, he saw a friendly squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Are you here to settle and rest?" Tim replied, "Yes, Sam! I have been flying high all day and I am tired." They both spent the Tuesday afternoon together, playing and talking. The sun began to set, and it was time for Tim to fly home. Tim said, "Thank you, Sam, for a fun day. I will come back soon!" And with that, Tim flew high into the sky, excited to see his new friend again.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a birdcage in her room. It was a pretty birdcage with a little bird inside. The bird was patient and sat very still. Sue wanted to play with the bird, so she asked her mom, "Can I play with the bird, Mom?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be gentle. The bird likes to rock in its cage." Sue was excited and started to rock the birdcage back and forth. The bird seemed to like it and began to sing a happy song. Sue rocked the birdcage more, and the bird sang even louder. Suddenly, the birdcage door opened, and the bird flew out. Sue was surprised and didn't know what to do. The bird flew around the room, singing and playing. In the end, Sue's mom came into the room and helped her catch the bird. They put the bird back in the birdcage, and Sue learned to be more careful when playing with her new friend.
