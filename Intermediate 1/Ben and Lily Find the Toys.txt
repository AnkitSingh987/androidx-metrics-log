Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Lily Find the Toys

Ben and Lily are twins. They share a room and a bed. But they do not like to share their toys. Ben has a lamp that he likes very much. It is blue and has stars on it. Lily has a doll that she likes very much. It is pink and has a dress on it. One night, Ben and Lily are getting ready for bed. Ben turns on his lamp and puts it on his side of the bed. Lily sees the lamp and wants it. "Lily, can I have your lamp?" she asks Ben. "No, Lily, it is my lamp. You have your doll," Ben says. "But I want your lamp. It is so pretty. And my doll is not tough. She is soft," Lily says. "I don't care. It is my lamp. And my lamp is tough. It is not soft," Ben says. They start to pull the lamp from each other. They make a lot of noise. They wake up their mom and dad. "Ben, Lily, what are you doing?" their mom says. "They want my lamp!" Ben says. "They want my doll!" Lily says. Their mom and dad are not happy. They take the lamp and the doll away from them. "Ben, Lily, you need to learn to share. You can't have everything you want. And you can't fight over toys. You are twins. You should be nice to each other," their dad says. He gives them each a hug and a kiss. He tells them to say sorry to each other. "Sorry, Ben," Lily says. "Sorry, Lily," Ben says. Their mom and dad turn off the light and leave the room. Ben and Lily cuddle up in their bed. They are quiet and sleepy. They do not want the lamp or the doll anymore. They just want each other.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk in the park. He saw a big box with a pretty ribbon. Tim felt happy and excited. He wanted to see what was inside the box. He thought it would be easy to open. Tim pulled the ribbon to reveal what was inside. The box opened and Tim was surprised. He thought he would find a toy or a treat, but instead, there was a funny look on his face. There was a mirror inside the box! Tim laughed and laughed. He didn't expect to see his own face in the mirror. He thought it was a joke. Tim closed the box and put the ribbon back on. He left the box in the park for someone else to find and have a good laugh too.

Lily and Ben were very hungry. They wanted to eat spaghetti for dinner. Mommy said she would make spaghetti for them, but she had to work on her computer first. Lily and Ben waited and waited, but Mommy did not stop working. "Mommy, we are hungry!" Lily said. "Can we have spaghetti now?" "Not yet, sweetie. I have to finish this email. It is very important." Mommy said. Lily and Ben sighed. They played with their toys, but they were not fun. They watched TV, but they were not interesting. They yawned and yawned, but they were not sleepy. They just wanted spaghetti. "Mommy, please, can we have spaghetti now?" Ben said. "We are very hungry!" "Okay, okay. I'm almost done. Just a few more minutes." Mommy said. Lily and Ben smiled. They hoped Mommy would make spaghetti soon. They went to the kitchen and saw a big pot on the stove. They thought it was spaghetti. They were very happy. "Look, Ben, spaghetti!" Lily said. "Mommy made it for us!" "Yay, spaghetti!" Ben said. "Let's eat it!" They grabbed two forks and ran to the pot. They lifted the lid and saw something gray and slimy. It was not spaghetti. It was worms! "Ah, worms!" Lily screamed. "Mommy, why did you make worms?" "Ah, worms!" Ben screamed. "Mommy, this is not spaghetti!" Mommy heard their screams and ran to the kitchen. She saw the pot and the worms and the forks and the kids. She was very shocked and angry. "What are you doing?" Mommy yelled. "That is not spaghetti! That is bait! I was going to go fishing with Daddy tomorrow! How did you get into the pot?" Lily and Ben dropped their forks and cried. They were very scared and sorry. They did not know it was bait. They thought it was spaghetti. "Mommy, we are sorry!" Lily said. "We were hungry!" "Mommy, we are sorry!" Ben said. "We wanted spaghetti!" Mommy shook her head and sighed. She was not happy. She had to throw away the worms and the pot and the forks. She had to clean the kitchen and the kids. She had to make new spaghetti and new email. She had to cancel the fishing trip and the fun. "Next time, wait for me to make dinner. And never touch the stove without me. And never eat worms. Do you understand?" Mommy said. "Yes, Mommy." Lily and Ben said. They learned their lesson. They never ate worms again. They never had spaghetti again. They never had fun again. The end.
