Category: Intermediate
Title: Tuna the Purple Fish and the Slide

Once upon a time, in a big sea, there was a purple fish named Tuna. Tuna loved to play with his friends. They liked to swim and jump in the water. Tuna was very happy. One day, Tuna and his friends found a big slide in the sea. It was so much fun! They all took turns sliding down the slide. Tuna went down the slide very fast. He laughed and laughed. After they played on the slide, Tuna and his friends went home. Tuna's mom gave them all some yummy food to eat. They were very tired, but they had a great day. Tuna loved playing with his friends and could not wait to slide again.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They were both 3 years old and loved to play together. One day, they were walking in the park and noticed a big tree with a lot of colorful fruits hanging from its branches. "Let's try some," said Jack. "No," said Jill. "Why not?" asked Jack. "I don't know the name of this fruit, so I don't want to try it," answered Jill. Jack thought for a moment and then said, "My mom always says that it's better to try something new before you decide if you like it or not. I recommend that we try the fruit." Jill hesitated and then decided to try the fruit. To her surprise, the fruit was delicious! She was so happy that she agreed with Jack. The moral of this story is that it pays to be open-minded and not be afraid to try something new. You never know how wonderful something could be if you don't first give it a try.

Tom and Anna were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw a big hole in the ground near their house. They wanted to see what was inside the hole. "Can we go and look?" Tom asked their mom. Mom shook her head. "No, you can't. The hole is deep and dangerous. You might fall and hurt yourself. Stay away from it, please." Tom and Anna were sad. They wanted to explore the hole. They waited until mom was busy in the kitchen. Then they sneaked out of the house. They took a cane from the porch. They thought they could use it to poke the hole. They ran to the hole and looked down. It was dark and scary. They could not see the bottom. Tom held the cane and leaned over the hole. He tried to touch the edge with the cane. "Be careful, Tom!" Anna said. "You might fall!" Tom did not listen. He wanted to see how deep the hole was. He pushed the cane harder. But the cane was too short. It slipped from his hand and fell into the hole. Tom lost his balance and fell too. "Tom!" Anna screamed. She ran to the hole and looked for her brother. She could not see him. She could only hear him cry. "Help! Help! I'm stuck!" Anna was scared. She did not know what to do. She ran back to the house and told mom what happened. Mom was angry and worried. She called dad and the firemen. They came quickly and brought a rope and a ladder. They lowered the rope into the hole and pulled Tom out. He was dirty and bruised, but not badly hurt. The firemen also found the cane and gave it back to him. Mom hugged Tom and Anna. She was glad they were safe. But she was also very upset. "You were very naughty and foolish," she said. "You did not obey me. You did not stay away from the hole. You put yourself in danger. You could have died. You are not permitted to play outside for a week. You have to stay in your room and think about what you did." Tom and Anna felt sorry. They knew they had done wrong. They said sorry to mom and dad and the firemen. They went to their room and cried. They wished they had never seen the hole. They learned their lesson. They decided to never disobey mom again.
