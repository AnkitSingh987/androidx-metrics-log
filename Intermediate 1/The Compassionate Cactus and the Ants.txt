Category: Intermediate
Title: The Compassionate Cactus and the Ants

Once upon a time, in a big desert, there was a compassionate cactus. It lived with its friends, the ants. One day, a big wind came and blew the ants' food away. The ants were sad and hungry. The compassionate cactus wanted to help the ants. It thought and thought. Then, it had an idea! It knew that if it let its fruit rot, the ants could eat it. So, the cactus let its fruit rot and fall to the ground. The ants were so happy to find the rotting fruit. They ate and ate. The compassionate cactus was happy to help its friends. The ants learned that when friends help each other, they can solve big problems. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big grill near a tree. Tim's mom said, "We can cook hot dogs on the grill for lunch!" Tim was happy because he loved hot dogs. Tim's mom put the hot dogs on the grill. The grill was very hot, so Tim's mom told him to stay back. Tim saw a ball on the ground. It was tight and round. He picked it up and started to play. While playing, Tim saw a dog. The dog wanted to play too. Tim called, "Come here, dog!" The dog ran to Tim and they played with the ball. When the hot dogs were ready, Tim, his mom, and the dog ate lunch together. They all had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Mia. Mia liked to pray every night before bed. She also had a special diary where she wrote about her day. One day, Mia lost her diary. She looked everywhere but could not find it. Mia was very sad. She prayed that she would find her diary soon. The next day, Mia's little brother, Sam, found the diary. But it was not the same diary. This diary was magic! When Mia wrote in it, her wishes came true. Mia was very happy and shared her magic diary with Sam. They had lots of fun together.

One day, a little girl and her daddy decided to travel to a fancy place. They put on their fancy clothes and got ready to go. The little girl was very happy to spend the day with her daddy. They walked and walked until they found a big park. The park was very fancy with lots of pretty flowers and trees. The little girl and her daddy played on the swings and the slides. They had so much fun together. After playing in the park, they went to a fancy ice cream shop. The little girl and her daddy each got a big ice cream cone. They sat and ate their ice cream, feeling very happy. The little girl loved the special day with her daddy.

One day, a kind girl named Lily went to see a parade. She was very excited to see all the big floats and hear the loud music. She held her mom's hand as they walked to the parade. As they watched the parade, a little blue car zoomed by. "Wow!" said Lily, "I like the zoom car!" Her mom smiled and said, "Yes, it is a fun car!" But then, something unexpected happened. The little blue car stopped zooming and started to cry. "Oh no!" said Lily. "Why is the zoom car sad?" The kind girl walked up to the car and asked, "Why are you sad, zoom car?" The car said, "I lost my way and now I can't find my friends in the parade." Lily wanted to help, so she said, "Don't worry, zoom car! My mom and I will help you find your friends!" They all looked together, and soon they found the car's friends. The zoom car was happy again, and they all enjoyed the parade together.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with a glass ball. He would roll the glass ball all day long. One day, Tom was rolling the glass ball outside. It was sunny and warm. Tom was very happy. He rolled the glass ball fast and far. But then, the glass ball went into the woods. Tom was scared, but he wanted his glass ball back. He went into the woods and found the glass ball. Tom was very happy and he went back home with his glass ball.
