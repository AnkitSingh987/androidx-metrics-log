Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Gifted Boy and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a gifted boy named Tom. Tom was very good at making things. One day, Tom made a big board with many seats on it. He wanted to share his board with his friends. Tom took his board to the park. All his friends came to see it. They were very happy. They took turns sitting on the seats. They played and laughed all day long. Tom felt happy too. He liked sharing his board with his friends. The moral of the story is that sharing makes everyone happy. When we share, we show our friends that we care about them. Sharing is a gift that we can give to others.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim found a heavy ornament in his toy box. It was round and shiny. Tim liked it very much. He showed it to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" Tim said. Mom looked at the ornament and smiled. "That's a special ornament, Tim. It can record your voice," she said. Tim was excited. He held the ornament close to his mouth and said, "I love you, Mom!" The ornament recorded his words. Then, Tim played it back. The ornament said, "I love you, Mom!" in Tim's voice. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. Tim and Mom put the heavy ornament on their tree. They played the recording for Dad when he came home. Dad smiled and hugged Tim. They all loved the special ornament. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, while flying, he saw a big, hard rock. He thought it was a toilet for birds. Bobo wanted to tell his friend, Toto, about it. Bobo flew to Toto's tree and said, "Toto, I found a big toilet for birds! It is a hard rock. Let's go and see it!" Toto was very excited and said, "Yes, let's go!" They both flew to the big, hard rock. When they got there, they realized it was not a toilet but just a big rock. They laughed and said, "Silly us! It's just a hard rock, not a toilet!" Then they flew back to their trees, still laughing about their funny mistake.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max loved to dream at night. In his dreams, he would run and play with his friends in the park. One day, Max saw his friend, a wise old owl named Mr. Hoot. Mr. Hoot was giving a talk to the other animals. Max wanted to learn too, so he sat and listened. Mr. Hoot said, "To be happy, we should not be jealous of others. We should be happy for them and their fun things." Max thought about this and knew Mr. Hoot was right. He remembered feeling jealous when he saw his friend, Daisy the cat, playing with a new toy. Max knew he should be happy for Daisy instead. That night, Max had a dream. In his dream, he was playing with Daisy and her new toy. Max was happy for Daisy and enjoyed playing together. When Max woke up, he was excited to tell Daisy what he learned from Mr. Hoot's talk. Now, Max and Daisy play together all the time, and Max is never jealous of his friends.

One day, a busy bee named Ben and a little ant named Amy were in the garden. They both loved to work and help their families. The sun was shining and the flowers were pretty. Ben the bee said, "I think we should start collecting food now." Amy the ant had a different opinion. She said, "No, we should start building our homes first." They did not agree and began to argue. A wise old ladybug saw them and said, "Why not work together? You can both help each other." So, Ben and Amy agreed to work together. They became good friends and the garden was a happy place.

Sara liked to play with her yellow ball. She would throw it, catch it, bounce it and roll it. She had fun with her ball every day. One day, she saw a new girl in the park. The girl had a red ball. Sara wanted to play with the red ball. She went to the girl and said, "Can I have your ball?" The girl said, "No, this is my ball. You have your own ball." Sara did not listen. She tried to grab the red ball from the girl. The girl got angry and pulled the red ball back. They started to fight over the ball. Sara ignored her yellow ball. She did not care about it anymore. She only wanted the red ball. She was foolish. While they were fighting, a dog came to the park. The dog saw the yellow ball. The dog liked the yellow ball. The dog ran to the yellow ball and took it in his mouth. He ran away with the yellow ball. Sara and the girl stopped fighting. They saw the dog with the yellow ball. They tried to chase the dog, but the dog was too fast. They could not catch the dog. Sara was sad. She lost her yellow ball. She wished she had not ignored it. She wished she had been nice to the girl. She learned a lesson. She should be happy with what she has and not be greedy for what others have. She should not be foolish.
