Category: Intermediate
Title: Bobby the Boat and the Harbor

Once upon a time, there was a little boat named Bobby. Bobby lived in a big harbor with many other boats. The harbor was very wide and had lots of water. Bobby loved to float on the water with his friends. One day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard, and the waves were very big. Bobby and his friends were scared. They knew they would need help to stay safe in the wide harbor. They remembered that there was a strong and tall lighthouse near the harbor. The lighthouse saw the little boats in trouble and shined its bright light to show them the way. Bobby and his friends followed the light and found a safe place to hide from the storm. They were so happy that the lighthouse was there to help them. After the storm was gone, Bobby and his friends went back to playing in the wide harbor, knowing that they would always have help when they needed it.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to play outside. She saw some equipment in her yard. There was a swing, a slide, and a seesaw. Lucy loved to play on the equipment with her friends. Lucy's mom asked her to polish the equipment. She wanted it to be clear and shiny for her friends to play on. Lucy got a cloth and some water. She started to clean the swing first. She rubbed the cloth on the swing to make it clear and shiny. Next, Lucy polished the slide and the seesaw. She worked hard to make them clear too. When she was done, the equipment looked very nice. Lucy's friends came over to play. They all had a fun time playing on the clear, shiny equipment.

Once upon a time, there was a musician. He played a big drum. He liked to make loud and harsh sounds. He lived in a small house near the sea. One sunny day, he went to the beach. He saw a big crab. The crab had big claws. The crab waved its claws at the musician. The musician waved back. The musician had an idea. He played his drum near the crab. The crab danced to the beat. They had fun together. The musician and the crab became good friends. They played and danced on the beach every day.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely car. The car liked to drive fast. One day, the car went for a drive in the sunshine. It was a fun day. The car was happy. As the car drove, it saw a vine. The vine was big and green. The car wanted to play with the vine. So, the car drove close to the vine. The car and the vine became friends. Then, something unexpected happened. The vine could talk! The vine said, "Hello, lovely car!" The car was surprised. The car and the vine talked and played all day. They had lots of fun. And they became best friends forever.

Lily was a normal girl who liked to play with her toys. She had a big doll, a small car, and a soft bear. She also had a mouse, but it was not a toy. It was a real mouse that lived in her room. Lily liked the mouse, and the mouse liked Lily. They were friends. One day, Lily wanted to play with her mouse. She looked under her bed, behind her desk, and in her closet. She could not find the mouse. She was sad. Where was her mouse? She asked her mom, but her mom did not know. She asked her dad, but her dad did not know. She asked her brother, but her brother did not care. Lily decided to look for her mouse outside. She put on her shoes, her coat, and her hat. She opened the door and went to the garden. She saw many things: flowers, birds, bugs, and a cat. She did not see her mouse. She was very sad. She wanted to go back to her room. Then, she heard a squeak. She looked around and saw a hole in the wall. She went closer and saw a nose. It was her mouse! The mouse saw Lily and came out of the hole. It ran to Lily and climbed on her hand. Lily was very happy. She hugged her mouse and said, "I found you! I missed you!" She took her mouse back to her room. She gave it some cheese and some water. She made a seat for it out of a box and a cloth. She put it next to her bed. She said, "You are my best friend. I love you, mouse." The mouse squeaked and licked her face. It was happy, too. They played together until it was time to sleep. Lily and her mouse had a good day.
