Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Cow

Once upon a time, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom lived on a ranch with his mom and dad. The ranch had many red animals. Tom loved the ranch. One day, Tom's mom said, "Tom, I want to introduce you to our new red cow." Tom was very excited. He went outside and saw the big red cow. Tom said, "Hi, red cow! I am Tom." Tom wanted to play with the red cow. He ran around the cow, laughing and having fun. But, the red cow did not like it. The cow got mad and chased Tom. Tom was scared and cried. His mom and dad heard him and came running. They tried to stop the red cow, but it was too late. The red cow had hurt Tom. Tom was sad, and everyone on the ranch was sad too. The end.

Lily liked to play with string. She had many colors of string in her box. She made bracelets, necklaces, and pictures with string. She wondered what else she could make with string. One day, she saw a big ball of string in the corner of the room. It was gray and dirty and ugly. She did not like it. She asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "That is old string. We use it for the garden. It is not for playing." Lily was curious. She wanted to see the garden. She followed her mom outside. She saw many flowers and plants and bugs. She liked them. She also saw the old string. Her mom used it to tie the plants to sticks. She said, "The string helps the plants grow tall and strong." Lily was amazed. She looked at the old string. It was not ugly anymore. It was useful and helpful. She said, "Can I use some string for my plants?" Her mom smiled. She said, "Of course. Here, take some string. But be careful. Don't cut yourself or tangle yourself." Lily was happy. She took some string and ran to her room. She had a new idea. She wondered what she could make with string and plants. She thought it would be beautiful.

One day, a tidy cat named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big silver box. Tim was very curious. He went closer to the box and saw a red button. He pressed the button with his paw. "Hello, I am a record box!" said the box. "I can record your voice if you talk to me." Tim was surprised. He had never heard a box talk before. So, Tim talked to the box. He said, "I am a tidy cat, and I like to play." The box recorded his words. Then, the box played Tim's words back to him. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The box started to talk like Tim! It said, "I am a tidy cat, and I like to play." Tim was shocked. He didn't know the box could do that. Tim and the silver box became friends. They played and talked together every day. The box always made Tim laugh with its funny voice. And Tim was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a rare bird named Bobo. Bobo was very strong because he had big muscles. He could fly very high and very fast. All the other birds looked up to him and wanted to be his friend. One day, a little bird named Lulu wanted to play with Bobo. She flew up to him and asked, "Can I play with you, Bobo?" Bobo looked at Lulu and said, "You are too small, Lulu. You do not have big muscles like me." Lulu felt sad, but she did not give up. She went home and practiced flying every day. She got stronger and stronger. One day, she went to Bobo again and asked, "Can I play with you now, Bobo?" Bobo saw that Lulu had grown big muscles too. He smiled and said, "Yes, Lulu, let's play together!" From that day on, Bobo and Lulu became best friends. They played together every day and showed everyone that big muscles or small, everyone can be friends.
