Category: Intermediate
Title: Love the Black Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a little black cat named Love. Love liked to play and have fun. One sunny day, Love went outside to play with her friends. As they played, Love saw a big red ball. She wanted to get it, so she started to hurry. Love ran and ran, but the ball kept moving away. She didn't give up and kept trying to catch the ball. Finally, Love caught the ball and brought it back to her friends. They were all so happy and clapped for Love. Love felt proud and happy that she didn't give up. They all played with the big red ball until the sun went down, and then they went home to rest.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, lived a happy potato named Pat. Pat had many friends who lived in the garden too. They all liked to play and have fun together. One day, while Pat was taking a nap, a big bird came and tried to take Pat away. Pat's friend, a little bug named Ben, saw the bird and yelled, "Pat, wake up! A big bird is trying to take you!" Pat woke up just in time and wiggled free from the bird. Pat and Ben were scared but happy that they were safe. Later, Pat and Ben told their friends about the big bird. They all decided to make a plan to keep safe. The next time the big bird came, they would all yell and scare it away. And that's just what they did. The big bird never came back, and Pat and all the friends in the garden lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wise old owl. The owl loved to have fun with his friends. One day, he decided to have a big party. He invited all his animal friends to come and play. At the party, there was a little mouse who was very shy. He wanted to make new friends, but he was too scared. The wise owl saw the scared mouse and had an idea. He asked the other animals to play a game of hide and seek. The little mouse was very good at hiding. He found a small hole and hid inside. All the other animals looked for him, but they could not find him. The wise owl knew where he was but did not tell anyone. Suddenly, the little mouse jumped out and scared all the animals. They all laughed and were surprised. From that day on, the little mouse was not scared anymore. He made many new friends at the party. The wise owl was happy to see everyone having fun together. The moral of the story is to not be scared to make new friends and that even the smallest one can surprise everyone.

Anna was very happy. It was her birthday today. She had a big cake with four candles. She had many presents from her family and friends. She had a new doll, a new book, a new dress and a new hat. She wanted to play with them all. But there was one present that she did not open yet. It was a big box with a red ribbon. It was from her uncle Tom. He said it was a very unusual present. He said it was a surprise. He said she would love it. Anna was curious. She wanted to see what was inside the box. She asked her mom if she could open it. Her mom said yes, but she had to be careful. She said uncle Tom liked to play jokes. She said maybe the box had something funny or scary in it. Anna did not mind. She liked funny and scary things. She took the box to her room. She put it on her bed. She untied the ribbon. She lifted the lid. She looked inside. She saw something furry and brown. It had big ears and a long tail. It had sharp teeth and claws. It was a bear. A real bear. It was sleeping in the box. Anna screamed. She dropped the lid. She ran out of the room. She was very scared. She trembled all over. She did not like this present. She did not like this joke. She did not like uncle Tom. Her mom heard her scream. She came to her room. She saw the box. She saw the bear. She was very angry. She called uncle Tom. She told him to come and take the bear away. She told him it was a very bad joke. She told him it was a very unusual present. She told him he was a very silly uncle.
