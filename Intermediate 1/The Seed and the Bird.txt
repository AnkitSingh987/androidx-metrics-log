Category: Intermediate
Title: The Seed and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a little seed. The seed wanted to grow big and strong. It felt helpless because it was so small. The seed wished to become a tall tower one day. One day, the seed met a friendly bird. The bird said, "I can help you grow, little seed!" The seed was very happy. The bird took the seed to a nice spot with soft dirt and lots of sun. The bird put the seed in the ground and covered it with dirt. The seed started to grow. It grew a little bit each day. Soon, the seed became a small plant. The plant grew taller and taller. One day, the plant was as tall as a tower! The seed was not helpless anymore. It was big, strong, and very happy.

One day, a pink monkey went to the park. He saw a big tree and wanted to test how high he could climb. The pink monkey started to climb the tree. He went up and up until he was very high. He looked down and saw his friend, the yellow bird. "Hi, pink monkey! What are you doing up there?" asked the yellow bird. The pink monkey said, "I am testing how high I can climb this tree!" The yellow bird was excited and wanted to test how high he could fly. So, he flew up and up, higher than the pink monkey. Just then, something unexpected happened. The pink monkey saw a big, green leaf. He touched the leaf, and it changed color! It turned pink, just like him! The pink monkey was very surprised. He called out to the yellow bird, "Look, I can change the color of the leaves!" The yellow bird flew down to see the pink leaf. They both laughed and played, turning more leaves pink. Soon, the tree was full of pink leaves. The pink monkey and the yellow bird had so much fun that day, and they learned that they could do amazing things when they tested themselves.

One day, a dove sat on a tree. The dove gazed at the deep blue sky. It saw a big cloud and said, "Wow, that is a big cloud!" A cat heard the dove and came to the tree. The cat said, "Hi, dove! I want to see the big cloud too!" The cat climbed the tree and sat next to the dove. They gazed at the deep blue sky together. But then, the big cloud changed! It became a big, funny face. The dove and cat laughed and laughed. They did not expect the cloud to do that. The dove and cat became friends and gazed at the sky every day.

John was walking outside in the park when he noticed a big birdcage. He went closer to take a better look and heard a chirp inside the cage. He saw a little bird inside it and he asked the bird, "What's your name?". The little bird said, "My name is Tweet". John smiled and asked Tweet if it wanted to come out and play. Tweet happily said, "Yes, please!" John opened the door of the birdcage and Tweet flew out. Tweet was so happy and sang happily as it flew around John. John started following the little bird with his eyes and noticed a really big tree with lots of green leaves. John said to Tweet, "Let's fly to that big tree over there! We can have so much fun." Tweet was so excited and replied, "Great! Let's go!" The two of them flew to the big tree and Tweet started playing between the leaves. John just watched and smiled at the little bird having so much fun. It was such a nice moment for both of them.

Once upon a time, there was a musician who liked to play music. He had a thick rope to tie his drum. One day, he went to the park to play for the kids. At the park, a boy came up to him and said, "Can you tie my shoe?" The musician said, "Yes, I can help you." He tied the boy's shoe with a big knot. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you!" Later, the musician started to play his drum. But the thick rope broke, and the drum fell on the ground. The music stopped, and everyone was sad. The bad ending was that the musician could not play music anymore. The moral value is to always take care of your own things first before helping others.
