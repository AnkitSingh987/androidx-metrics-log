Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Rock

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One day, he found a stick. He used the stick to strike a big rock. The rock made a loud sound. Tom was happy and played with the stick all day. Tom wanted to see how big the rock was. He used his stick to measure it. The rock was very big. Tom thought it was the biggest rock he had ever seen. He wanted to show the rock to his friend, Sue. As Tom was waiting for Sue, it started to get dark. The sky was full of big, dark clouds. Tom was scared, but he wanted Sue to see the rock. When Sue came, they looked at the rock together. Suddenly, a bright light came from the sky and struck the rock. The rock broke into many little pieces. Tom and Sue were sad. They did not have the big rock to play with anymore. The dark sky and the bright light had taken it away. They went home and wished they had played with the rock when it was still there.

Once upon a time, in a small town, two friends, Tom and Tim, loved to play chess. They played chess every day. They liked to fight in the game to see who was better. One day, they played chess in the park. They were having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the chess pieces away. Tom and Tim were sad and hurt. But then, a little dog came running to them. The dog had a chess piece in his mouth. The dog had found all the chess pieces! Tom and Tim were happy again. They played chess with the dog and had more fun than ever before. The dog was their new friend, and they never fought again.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play pretend. Today, they pretend to be sailors in the navy. They wear blue hats and shirts. They have a big cardboard box. The box is their ship. "Ahoy, matey!" Ben says to Anna. "Are you ready to sail the sea?" "Yes, captain!" Anna says. "But I am a little nervous. What if we see a shark?" "Don't worry, sailor!" Ben says. "We have a sign to scare them away. Look!" He shows Anna a paper with a picture of a mean face. "This sign says 'go away, shark!'" Anna smiles. She thinks the sign is funny. She holds the sign in front of her. "Go away, shark!" she says. They put the sign on the front of the box. They get inside the box. They pretend to row with sticks. They sing a song. "Row, row, row your boat, gently down the stream. Merrily, merrily, merrily, merrily, life is but a dream." They have fun. They do not see any sharks. They are brave sailors in the navy.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot loved to jog every day. One day, while jogging, Spot found a huge bone. He was very happy and took the bone home. Spot's friend, a small cat named Fluffy, came to visit. Fluffy saw the huge bone and wanted some too. Spot thought for a moment and decided to cook the bone to share with Fluffy. They cooked the bone together and had a great time. In the end, Spot and Fluffy enjoyed the cooked bone. They were both very happy because they shared. The moral of the story is to always share with your friends, and you will both be happy.

Lily liked to play with her trumpet. She got it from her grandpa, who played it in a band. Lily wanted to be like grandpa and make loud and happy sounds with her trumpet. One day, Lily saw a big hole in her trumpet. She was sad and angry. How did it happen? She asked her mom, who said that her brother Ben had played with it and broke it. Lily was very mad at Ben. She wanted to fill the hole with something and make her trumpet work again. She looked around the house and found some things to fill the hole. She tried a crayon, a button, a sock, and a banana. But none of them worked. They only made her trumpet look funny and sound worse. Lily was frustrated and cried. Ben heard her cry and came to see what was wrong. He saw her broken trumpet and felt sorry. He said he was sorry for breaking it and he wanted to help her fix it. He said he had some tape and glue in his room. He asked Lily if she wanted to try them. Lily was still mad, but she also wanted to play with her trumpet again. She nodded and followed Ben to his room. They worked together to tape and glue the hole. They waited for it to dry and then tried to play the trumpet. It sounded better, but not perfect. Lily smiled and thanked Ben. Ben smiled and hugged Lily. They learned to share and forgive.
