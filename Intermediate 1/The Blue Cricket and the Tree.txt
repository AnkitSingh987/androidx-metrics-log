Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Cricket and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little blue cricket. He lived in a tiny house under a big tree. The blue cricket had many friends like ants, bees, and butterflies. One sunny day, the blue cricket lost his way home. He felt very sad and scared. He did not know what to do. So, he sat down and started to pray. He asked for help to find his way back to his tiny house. As the blue cricket prayed, a kind ladybug heard him. She wanted to help him. The ladybug took the blue cricket by the hand and showed him the way back to his tiny house. The blue cricket was very happy and thanked the ladybug. From that day on, the blue cricket and the ladybug were best friends.

Tom and Sam are friends. They like to play in the park. One day, they see a big dog. The dog is black and brown. It has sharp teeth and a loud bark. "Look, a dog!" Tom says. "Let's go and pet it." "No, Tom, don't!" Sam says. "The dog might bite you. It is not our dog. We don't know if it is nice or mean." But Tom is ignorant. He does not listen to Sam. He runs to the dog. He wants to touch its fur. The dog does not like Tom. It growls and snaps at him. Tom is scared. He screams and runs away. The dog chases him. "Help, help!" Tom cries. "The dog is after me!" Sam runs to Tom. He grabs his hand. He pulls him to a safe place. The dog stops and goes away. "Are you okay, Tom?" Sam asks. "Did the dog hurt you?" Tom is shaking. He has a scratch on his arm. He is sorry. "I'm okay, Sam," he says. "But I was stupid. I should have listened to you. You were right. The dog was not nice. It was mean." Sam hugs Tom. He is glad that Tom is okay. "It's okay, Tom," he says. "You made a mistake. But you learned from it. Next time, you will be more careful. And we will still be friends." Tom smiles. He is happy. He has a good friend in Sam. The end.

It was very cold outside and Jack was shivering. He looked out his window and could see two people. He recognized them as his two best friends, Greg and Sarah. They were getting married. Jack watched them exchange a kiss and he shouted out in excitement. He was so happy to see them happy together. Jack's mum came in the room and said, "It sure looks like Greg and Sarah are getting married. Do you know what this means Jack? They are going to pay each other in marriage." Jack was confused and asked what his mum meant. She explained, "It means they will take care of each other, love each other and always be there for one another." Jack smiled and said, "That sounds like a wonderful way to pay for marriage."

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big earthquake. The ground shook and the people were scared. They did not know what to do. The earthquake made a big mess and the village needed help. A kind man named Tom wanted to help fix the village. He was not selfish. Tom admired the strong people in the village. He wanted to be like them. So, he began to help clean up the mess the earthquake made. The people in the village admired Tom too. They saw how he was not selfish and wanted to help. They all worked together to fix the village. In the end, the village was even better than before. The people were happy and thankful for Tom's help.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. He was very good at finding animals in the woods. The hunter had a special trick. He could whistle. When he whistled, the animals would come to him. One day, the hunter met a big, flexible snake. The snake could bend and twist in many ways. The hunter was not scared. He wanted to be friends with the snake. So, he whistled a happy song. The snake liked the song and danced to it. The hunter and the snake became good friends. They played together in the woods every day. And they both lived happily ever after.
