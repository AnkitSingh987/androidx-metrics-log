Category: Intermediate
Title: The Grumpy Fisherman and the Boat

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy fisherman. He lived in a small house by the sea. Every day, he went out to catch fish in his little boat. One day, the fisherman was very tired. He decided to sleep in his boat. While he slept, the boat floated far away from his home. When the grumpy fisherman woke up, he was lost. He tried to find his way back, but he couldn't. He was sad and grumpy forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a job to help his mom in the garden. One day, Tom saw two bugs fighting on a leaf. They were very competitive and didn't want to share the leaf. Tom asked his mom why the bugs were fighting. She told him that sometimes, bugs fight over things they want. But it is not nice to fight, and it is better to share and be kind to others. So, Tom learned from the bugs that fighting is not good. He decided to be kind and share with his friends. And from that day on, Tom was a better friend and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to be kind and share, instead of being competitive and fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat named Tom. Tom was very selfish. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tom found a long strip of cloth in his room. He thought it was a great toy to play with. Tom's friend, a small dog named Max, came to visit. Max saw the strip and wanted to play with it too. But Tom did not want to share the strip. He said, "No, it's mine!" Max was sad and asked Tom to share, but Tom just repeated, "No, it's mine!" Max left Tom's house feeling very sad. Tom continued to play with the strip all by himself. But then, the strip got stuck on a high tree branch. Tom tried to get it down, but he couldn't. He needed Max's help, but Max was not there. Tom was all alone and could not play with the strip anymore.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to the park. She saw a mysterious big box near a tree. Sally stared at the box, wondering what was inside. She walked closer to take a better look. As she got near the box, a funny cat jumped out. The cat said, "Hello, Sally! I have a special order for you." Sally was surprised that the cat could talk, but she was curious about the order. The cat told Sally, "My order is to give you this magic ball." The cat handed her a shiny, colorful ball. "When you play with it, you will make new friends." Sally thanked the cat and played with the magic ball. Soon, many kids came to play with her, and Sally made lots of new friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a log. The log was very old and miserable. It did not like being alone. It wanted to have friends. One day, a little bird came to the log. The bird was sad too. The log asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I lost my home in the storm." The log said, "You can live with me." The bird was happy. They became friends. Then something strange happened. The log started to change. It became a big tree with many branches. The bird made a nest in the tree. More birds and animals came to live in the tree. The tree was not miserable anymore. It was happy with all its new friends. The moral of the story is: when you help others, good things can happen to you too.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved going to school. She liked to play with her friends, learn new things, and have fun. One day, Amy was playing a game with her friends. They would say a word and she would repeat it. Amy's friend said the word "sad." Amy repeated, "sad." But when she said it, she started to feel sad too. She didn't know why, but she couldn't stop feeling sad. Her friends tried to make her feel better, but nothing worked. Amy was still sad. The next day, Amy went to school again. She hoped she would feel better. But when she got to school, she was still sad. She couldn't play with her friends or learn new things. Amy stayed sad all day. And when she went home, she was still sad. The end.
