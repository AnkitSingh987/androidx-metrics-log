Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Toys

One day, a boy named Tom went to play with his friends. They all liked to play with fake toys. They had a lot of fun with their fake cars and fake dolls. Tom had a toy phone that he used to call his friends. He would pretend to talk to them and they would laugh. One day, while they were playing, Tom's toy phone started to ring. He thought it was a joke, but he picked it up and said "hello". To his surprise, a voice on the other end said "hello" back. Tom was very confused. He didn't know that his fake phone could really call someone. Tom told his friends about the call. They didn't believe him at first, but then the phone rang again. This time, they all heard the voice on the other end. They were all very surprised and excited. They had no idea that their fake toys could really work. They started to play with their fake toys more and more. They found out that they could really call people and have fun with their fake cars and dolls. They learned that sometimes, things that seem fake can be real and fun to play with. In the end, Tom and his friends had a great time playing with their fake toys that turned out to be real. They knew that they would always remember this special day of play.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a sister named Emma. Lily and Emma liked to play dress up. They would wear their mom's big clothes and shoes. It was a lot of fun. One day, Lily wanted to wear her sister's favorite dress. Emma was upset. She did not want Lily to wear her dress. Lily was sad, but she put the dress back. Later, Lily found a box in the closet. Inside the box was a new dress. It was just like Emma's favorite dress but in a different color. Lily put on the new dress and showed Emma. Emma was happy to see Lily in the new dress. Now, they both had a favorite dress to wear. They played dress up together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a hidden hotel in the woods. It was a fun place where animals liked to play. The hotel had a big pool where they could splash all day. One sunny day, a little frog named Tim went to the hotel. He wanted to splash in the pool with his friends. They played games and had a lot of fun. After a while, Tim got tired and went to his room. He took a nap and dreamed of more fun days at the hidden hotel. The next day, Tim and his friends splashed in the pool again, and they were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat peaches. One day, he saw a big peach on the table. He wanted to eat it, but it was too high for him to reach. He felt uncomfortable because he could not get the peach. Tim had a smart idea. He found a long stick and tried to snap the peach off the table. He pushed and pushed, but the peach did not move. Tim felt sad because he could not get the peach. Just then, his big sister, Sue, came into the room. She saw Tim and the peach. Sue was tall and could reach the peach. She picked it up and gave it to Tim. Tim was so happy! He thanked Sue and they both sat down to eat the delicious peach together.

Once upon a time there was a faithful dog named Able. He was a very loyal pet who loved to perform tricks. Every day Able’s owner John, would give him some treats to reward him for his good behaviour. John owned a pan which he used to make pancakes. One day, John was preparing pancakes for breakfast and he couldn’t find his pan. He looked in the living room, the kitchen and even in the garden but he still couldn’t find it. John had almost given up when he heard a strange noise. He went to take a look outside and to his surprise he saw Able with the pan in his mouth! John laughed and said, “What are you doing Able? Where did you find this pan?” Able barked and dropped the pan at John’s feet. He then sat down and patiently waited for his treats. John thanked Able for his loyalty and gave him a big hug. He rewarded Able with a few treats and they both enjoyed their pancakes.
