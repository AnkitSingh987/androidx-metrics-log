Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cap

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a soft, blue cap that he loved to wear. He wore it every day when he played outside. One sunny day, Tim went to play with his friend, Jane. Jane saw Tim's cap and said, "I like your cap, Tim! Can I try it on?" Tim said, "Yes, you can try it on, but be careful. It is my favorite cap." Jane put on the cap, and it felt soft on her head. While they were playing, a big wind came and blew the cap away. Tim and Jane were sad. They looked for the cap but could not find it. Then, they saw a bird. The bird had the cap in its beak. Tim and Jane asked the bird, "Can you please give us the cap back?" The bird was kind and gave them the cap. Tim decided to attach a string to the cap, so it would not blow away again. From that day on, Tim and Jane played happily with the soft, blue cap always safe on Tim's head.

The sun was rising slowly in the sky. It was a beautiful morning. Emma was watching the sun from her bedroom window. "Oh, look Mum," said Emma. "It looks great!" Mum smiled down at Emma. "Yes, it does indeed. Now you'd better get ready. We have lots of fun things to do today," Mum said. Emma was so excited. She put on her favourite dress and grabbed her watch. "Come on, let's go, Mum!" Mum and Emma went outside and started walking. The sun was getting brighter and brighter. As they walked, Emma saw a big field full of wildflowers. She was so amazed by how beautiful it looked. "Wow, Mum, it's so great! Just look at all these colours!" Emma said. Mum smiled and agreed. "Yes, it is a sight to behold." They stayed in the field for a while, watching the sun rise higher in the sky. "Well, it's time to go now, Emma," Mum said. Emma nodded, feeling happy and excited as she watched the sun rise in the sky. They had had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to gather acorns and play in the big tree. One day, Sam found an acorn that was uncomfortable to hold. It was big and bumpy. Sam went to his friend, Birdie the bird, and said, "Birdie, this acorn is uncomfortable. Can you help me replace it with a better one?" Birdie agreed and they both went to look for a new acorn. They found a smooth and shiny acorn under the tree. Sam was happy and thanked Birdie for helping him replace the uncomfortable acorn. They played together in the big tree and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to rock on his chair while reading the newspaper. One day, he felt uncomfortable sitting on the chair. He tried to find a better place to sit, but he could not. Tim saw his friend, Sam, sitting on a big rock. Tim wanted to sit on the rock too. But Sam said, "No, this is my rock!" Tim was sad and angry. He tried to push Sam off the rock, but Sam pushed back. They both fell off the rock and got hurt. Tim and Sam cried. Their moms came to help them. The moms told them, "You should share and be kind to each other." But Tim and Sam did not listen. They were too busy fighting. In the end, both Tim and Sam lost their friend and were very sad. The moral value of the story is to always share and be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a very special boy. He was gifted beyond compare. He was walking through the park one day and he stepped into a clearing. In the middle of the clearing was a wide lake. On the other side of the lake was a little boat. The boy pointed to the boat and said, "What's that?" His mom smiled and said, "It's a boat. Would you like to go sailing?" The boy was so excited! He jumped up and pointed eagerly to the boat, saying, "Let's go!" They ran to the edge of the lake and hopped in the boat. The gifted boy and his mom sailed around the wide lake, laughing and talking. They enjoyed their day together, and then went home.
