Category: Intermediate
Title: Wednesday the Grumpy Cat and the Lucy

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Wednesday. She lived in a small house with a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved Wednesday very much, but sometimes Wednesday was not very nice. One day, Lucy said to Wednesday, "Why are you so grumpy all the time? I love you and I want to play with you." Wednesday looked at Lucy and thought for a moment. She decided to try to be less grumpy and play with Lucy. They played all day, and Wednesday started to feel happy. She liked playing with Lucy and being loved. From that day on, Wednesday was not grumpy anymore. She and Lucy played and laughed together every day, and they lived happily ever after.

One day, a girl named Sue went outside to play. She saw a rake on the ground. Sue picked up the rake and started to clean the leaves. As she raked, the wind blew the leaves around. It was loud and fun. Sue found a big red apple under the leaves. She picked it up and took a bite. The apple tasted very good. Sue wanted to share the apple with her friends. She took the apple and the rake and ran to find her friends. When Sue found her friends, they were playing with a big, funny-looking ball. Sue showed them the apple and they all wanted to taste it. But when they took a bite, they found out it was not an apple! It was the funny-looking ball! They all laughed and played with the ball and the rake. It was a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Ann was very excited. She put on her red shoes and went outside to play. She saw a big sign near the park. The sign said, "Do not slip on the wet grass." Ann wanted to play on the grass, but she did not want to slip. Ann thought of an idea. She took off her red shoes and put on her boots. Now she could play on the grass and not slip. She ran and jumped on the wet grass, feeling very happy. But then, a big dog came to the park. The dog saw Ann's red shoes and took them away. Ann was sad and did not know what to do. She asked her friends for help. Together, they looked for the big dog and found him. They got Ann's red shoes back and everyone was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a persistent dog named Max. Max lived in a lab with a kind man named Tom. Tom and Max were best friends. One day, Tom said to Max, "Let's try to find a big ball." Max wagged his tail, and they went outside to look for a big ball. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and in the grass. Max saw a big ball in a tree. He tried and tried to get it down. He jumped high, but he could not reach it. Tom saw Max trying hard. He said, "Good try, Max!" Tom helped Max by getting a long stick. They both tried together, and they got the big ball down. Max was happy, and Tom was proud of his persistent dog. They played with the big ball all day long.

One day, a nosy little girl named Lily went for a walk with her mother. They saw a big tree and a small house. Lily wanted to see what was inside the house. Her mother said, "No, Lily, we should not go in there. It is not our house." But Lily did not listen. She opened the door and went inside. She started to shiver because it was very cold. Lily saw a big bear sleeping in the house. She was scared and ran back to her mother. "Mom, there is a bear in the house!" she said. Her mother went to look, but the bear was gone. Instead, there was a big, warm blanket on the floor. Lily's mother said, "This is not a bear, it is a blanket. You were scared for no reason." They laughed and went back home, and Lily learned not to be so nosy.
