Category: Intermediate
Title: Fluffy the Gentle Cat and the Clock

Once upon a time, there was a gentle cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her toy clock. One day, Fluffy was playing with her clock when a little boy named Tim came to visit. "Hi Fluffy," said Tim. "Can I play with your clock too?" Fluffy looked at Tim and said, "Okay, but please don't disturb my clock." Tim and Fluffy played together with the clock. They had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The clock started to make a loud noise. Tim and Fluffy were very surprised. "Oh no! We disturbed the clock!" said Tim. Fluffy looked worried. But then, the clock stopped making noise. Instead, it started to sing a happy song. Tim and Fluffy laughed and danced to the song. They learned that sometimes, when you disturb something, it can turn into a fun surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy chain. He would run and jump with it all day long. One day, Max's chain broke. He was very sad. He wanted to finish playing with his toy. So, Max went to his friend, a little girl named Lucy. Lucy was good at fixing things. Lucy saw Max's broken chain and said, "I can help you, Max!" She took some glue and fixed the chain for Max. Max was very happy! Max and Lucy played with the fixed chain all day. They had so much fun! When they were tired, they went home to rest. Max was glad his chain was fixed, and he could finish playing with it.

Once upon a time, there was an ugly bull. The bull lived on a big farm. The bull was not happy because he was ugly. One day, the ugly bull met a little girl. The little girl wanted to play a game. The game was to number the cows. The ugly bull liked the game. The ugly bull and the little girl played the game every day. They numbered the cows and had fun. The ugly bull became happy. The little girl and the ugly bull were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl called Jane. She was 3 years old and loved ice-cream. Jane couldn't wait to try all of the different flavours at the ice-cream shop. There she was, at the ice-cream shop. Jane looked around, trying to decide what flavour to try first. But then, she saw it! A big scoop of bright yellow ice-cream, with swirls of chocolate. Jane was excited and ran over to get it. "Give it to me!" She said, with her tiny hands already reaching out to grab it. The shop keeper smiled at her and replied, "No Jane. That's a dull ice-cream. I can't give it to you". Jane was a bit sad, but the shop keeper said, "I'll give you a different one; a bright and colourful ice-cream". Jane brightened up, thanked the shop keeper and sat down to enjoy her treat. And that's how Jane had a yummy ice-cream, instead of the dull one.

Once upon a time, in a small place, there was a soft bunny. The bunny liked to raise his ears when he heard a sound. He had many friends who lived with him in the place. One day, the soft bunny and his friends went to play near the big tree. They played a game of hide and seek. The soft bunny was very good at hiding. He found a small hole near the big tree and hid inside. But, the soft bunny did not know that the hole was the home of a mean snake. The snake came back to his hole and saw the soft bunny inside. The snake was very angry and scared the soft bunny away. The soft bunny ran back to his friends, but they were all gone. The soft bunny was very sad and alone.

Lily and Ben were playing with their mom's clothes. They liked to try on different hats and scarves and pretend to be different people. Lily found an enormous coat that was red and soft. She put it on and wrapped it around her. She felt very warm and cozy. "Look at me, Ben!" she said. "I am a big bear!" Ben laughed and said, "You are not a bear, you are Lily! Bears don't wear coats!" "Yes, they do!" Lily said. "This is my special bear coat. It has buttons!" She showed Ben the shiny buttons on the front of the coat. They were round and smooth and made a clicking sound when she pressed them. "Can I try it?" Ben asked. "OK, but be careful. It is very big," Lily said. She took off the coat and gave it to Ben. Ben tried to put it on, but it was too big for him. The sleeves were too long and the collar was too high. He could not see anything. "Help, Lily! I can't see!" he said. Lily giggled and said, "You are a silly bear, Ben! You have to fold the sleeves and the collar. Like this." She helped Ben fold the sleeves and the collar of the coat so that they fit him better. She also buttoned the coat for him. "There, now you can see!" she said. "Thank you, Lily!" Ben said. "Now I am a big bear too!" They hugged each other and roared like bears. They had fun playing with the enormous coat.
