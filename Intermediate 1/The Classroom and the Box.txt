Category: Intermediate
Title: The Classroom and the Box

Once upon a time, there was a classroom. In the classroom, there were many kids. They all liked to play and learn. One day, they found a big box. They did not know what was inside the box. They wanted to look. When they opened the box, they saw many fake toys. There were fake cars, fake dolls, and fake animals. The kids were very happy. They took the toys and started to play. They made funny sounds and laughed a lot. Soon, it was time to go home. The kids put the fake toys back in the box. They said goodbye to their friends. They could not wait to come back to the classroom and play with the fake toys again.

Once upon a time, there was a big stadium. The stadium was very expensive. Many people liked to go there to watch games and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the stadium with his friends. They wanted to pretend they were playing a game. Tim was very excited. He had never been to the stadium before. Tim and his friends ran around the stadium, pretending to be players. They laughed and had a great time. Tim thought the expensive stadium was the best place ever. He couldn't wait to go back and play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys in his room. One day, he found a big, red plastic ball in his toy box. Tim was very grateful for the new toy. Tim sat on the floor and rolled the ball back and forth. He had so much fun with his new toy. Then, his mom came into the room. She saw Tim playing with the ball and smiled. She was happy that he was having a good time. Later, Tim went outside to play with his friends. He took the big, red plastic ball with him. They all sat in a circle and rolled the ball to each other. Everyone was grateful to play with Tim's new toy. They all had a great day and played until the sun went down.

Lily hates her coat. It is too big and too red. She does not want to wear it when she goes outside. She wants to wear her pink dress and her shiny shoes. But Mom says she has to wear her coat. It is cold and windy outside. Mom says she will get sick if she does not wear her coat. Mom puts the coat on Lily and zips it up. "No, no, no!" Lily cries. She tries to unzip her coat. She wants to take it off. She does not like how it feels. She does not like how it looks. "Lily, stop that!" Mom says. She holds Lily's hand and walks to the car. "We are going to the park. You will have fun there. You will see your friends. You will play on the swings and the slide. You will like your coat when you are there. It will keep you warm and cozy." Lily does not listen. She still hates her coat. She pulls on the zipper. She makes it open. She feels the cold air on her skin. She shivers. She feels a drop of rain on her nose. She looks up. She sees dark clouds in the sky. She hears thunder. She sees lightning. Lily is scared. She does not like the rain. She does not like the thunder. She does not like the lightning. She wants to go back home. She wants to be safe and dry. She looks at Mom. She sees Mom's coat. It is blue and soft. It is not too big and not too red. It looks nice. It looks warm. Lily says, "Mom, can I wear your coat?"

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Kelly who loved animals. She had a big, smelly bulldog named Rex. Every day, Kelly took Rex for a walk and let Rex run wild and explore new places. One day, Kelly and Rex were walking in a nearby park when they saw something strange. It was a handle sticking out of the ground. Kelly got closer to investigate, but suddenly Rex barked loudly and started to pull her away. He kept barking and Kelly could tell he was trying to warn her. Frightened, Kelly quickly ran away with Rex and they both went home. Later, Kelly told her parents about the handle. Her parents knew it was a gas pipe and warned her to stay away. Kelly was sad that she couldn't explore the park again, but was thankful that Rex had protected her. Kelly never forgot her brave bulldog and from then on, listened very carefully to everything Rex warned her about.
