Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily and the Park

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They had a picnic with yummy food. Lily loved the good taste of the sandwiches her mom made. They had a fun time playing and eating. After they ate, Lily saw a little boy named Timmy crying. She asked him, "Why are you sad?" Timmy said, "I forget where my mom is." Lily wanted to help him, so she took Timmy's hand and they went to look for his mom. They found Timmy's mom near the swings. She was so happy to see him. She said, "Thank you, Lily, for helping my boy." Timmy's mom gave Lily a big hug. After that, Lily, Timmy, and their moms played together at the park. They all had a good time and became friends.

Sara and Tom are twins. They live in a big house with a modern bedroom. They have many toys and books and games. They like to play together every day. One day, their mom says, "Sara and Tom, you have to stay in your bedroom today. I have to work on the computer and I need quiet. You can play with your toys and books and games, but do not make noise. Do you understand?" Sara and Tom nod and say, "Yes, mom, we understand." They go to their bedroom and close the door. They start to play with their toys and books and games. They have fun and forget about mom. But after a while, they get bored. They want to see what mom is doing. They want to go to the living room and watch TV. They want to go to the kitchen and get a snack. They want to go to the backyard and play with the dog. They whisper to each other, "Let's go out of our bedroom. Mom will not notice. She is busy on the computer. Come on, it will be more fun." They open the door and sneak out of their bedroom. They go to the living room and turn on the TV. They go to the kitchen and grab some cookies. They go to the backyard and throw a ball to the dog. But mom hears the noise. She stops working on the computer and goes to check on Sara and Tom. She sees that they are not in their bedroom. She sees that they are making a mess in the house. She sees that they are disobeying her. She is angry and sad. She says, "Sara and Tom, what are you doing? I told you to stay in your bedroom. Why did you not listen to me? You are not being good children. You are being naughty and rude. You have to go back to your bedroom and clean up your mess. And you have to say sorry to me." Sara and Tom feel ashamed and sorry. They say, "We are sorry, mom. We were bored and we wanted to have more fun. We did not mean to make you angry and sad. We will go back to our bedroom and clean up our mess. And we will not do it again." They go back to their bedroom and clean up their mess. They hug mom and say, "We love you, mom. We are sorry, mom. We will listen to you next time." Mom hugs them and says, "I love you, too, Sara and Tom. I am glad you are sorry. I know you are good children. But you have to learn to stay in your bedroom when I ask you to. It is for your own safety and respect. Sometimes, mom has to work and you have to be quiet. Do you understand?" Sara and Tom nod and say, "Yes, mom, we understand. We will stay in our bedroom when you ask us to. We will be quiet and good. We will play with our toys and books and games. And we will have fun in our modern bedroom." Mom smiles and says, "That's good, Sara and Tom. I am proud of you. Now, you can play in your bedroom until dinner time. And then, we can watch a movie together in the living room. How does that sound?" Sara and Tom smile and say, "That sounds great, mom. Thank you, mom. We love you, mom." They play in their bedroom until dinner time. And then, they watch a movie together in the living room. They are happy and grateful. They have learned a lesson. They have learned to stay in their bedroom when mom asks them to. And they have learned to appreciate their modern bedroom and their toys and books and games. And they have learned to love and respect their mom.
