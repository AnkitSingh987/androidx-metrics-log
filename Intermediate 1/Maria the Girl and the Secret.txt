Category: Intermediate
Title: Maria the Girl and the Secret

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Maria. She loved to explore her house, and one day she found something really neat - a grocery! It was tucked away in a hidden corner of the room, and Maria was so excited to find it. She ran to tell her mother. "Mama, look what I found!" Maria said. Her mother smiled. "That's great, Maria. Let's keep it a secret, ok?" she said. Maria agreed and kept the grocery hidden in the corner. She took great care of it. Whenever she wanted to play with it, she'd take it out and play with it. But when she was done, she'd always tuck it away safely, keeping it hidden and away from prying eyes. Maria loved her hidden grocery, and her Mama was always happy that she was taking good care of it. The end.

One day, a girl named Lily went to her mom's room. She saw a box with pretty jewelry inside. Lily wanted to wear it, so she put on a necklace and a ring. She felt very happy and warm inside. Lily went to show her friend, Max. "Look at my pretty jewelry!" said Lily. Max liked the jewelry too. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and took the necklace from Lily's neck. Max and Lily were sad. They wanted to get the necklace back. They ran after the bird and saw it go into a tree. Max tried to climb the tree, but he was scared. So, Lily closed her eyes and wished very hard for the necklace to come back. Suddenly, the bird flew down and gave the necklace back to Lily. She was so happy and warm inside again. Max and Lily thanked the bird and went home to tell their moms about their adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pet cat named Tom. Amy loved to feed Tom every day. One day, she saw a cut on Tom's paw. She felt sad for him. Amy's mommy told her that they would go to the park tomorrow. She said, "Maybe we will find something there to help Tom feel better." Amy was excited and hoped to find something for Tom. The next day, they went to the park. Amy saw a little boy with a bandage on his knee. She asked her mommy if they could get one for Tom. Her mommy said yes. They went home, and Amy put the bandage on Tom's cut. Tom felt better, and Amy was not ashamed anymore. She was happy that she could help her friend.

Tom and Mia were friends who liked to play near the sea. They saw a big yacht with many people on it. They wanted to go on the yacht and have fun. "Let's go on the yacht, Mia. It looks nice and shiny," Tom said. "But how can we go on the yacht, Tom? We don't know the people. They might be bossy and mean," Mia said. "We can sneak on the yacht when they are not looking. Come on, it will be fun," Tom said. He took Mia's hand and ran to the yacht. They climbed on the yacht and hid behind some boxes. They saw many things on the yacht, like toys, food, and clothes. They wanted to play with the things, but they were afraid of the people. "Look, Tom, there is a big cake on the table. It looks yummy. Can we have some?" Mia asked. "Okay, but be quick. We don't want the people to see us," Tom said. They ran to the table and grabbed some cake. They ate the cake and smiled. It was sweet and soft. They liked the cake. But then they heard a loud voice. It was a man who was on the yacht. He saw them and he was angry. "Hey, you two! What are you doing on my yacht? You are not invited. You are stealing my cake. Get off my yacht now!" the man shouted. He was big and scary. He had a red face and a black hat. He looked like a pirate. He came to them and grabbed them by their arms. He was very bossy and mean. Tom and Mia were scared. They wanted to run away, but they could not. They blinked their eyes and cried. They wished they had not gone on the yacht. They wished they had listened to Mia. They were in big trouble. The man took them to his boat and sailed away. They did not know where he was taking them. They did not know if they would ever see their home and their parents again. They were very sad and sorry. The end.
