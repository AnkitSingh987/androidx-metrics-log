Category: Intermediate
Title: The Witch and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a witch. She was not nice. She liked to spoil things. One day, she saw a ball. The ball was pretty. The witch wanted to spoil the ball. A girl came to play with the ball. The witch hid the ball. The girl was sad. She wanted her ball back. The witch laughed. She did not want to return the ball. A big dog saw the girl cry. The dog went to the witch. The dog barked loud. The witch got scared. She gave the ball back. The girl was happy. The witch learned not to spoil things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom had a big stomach because he loved to eat. One day, Tom saw a cheap toy in the store. It was a small toy that could stand on its own. Tom wanted the toy very much. Tom asked his mom if he could have the toy. His mom said, "Yes, but only if you help me clean the house." Tom was happy and started to help his mom. They cleaned the house together, and Tom's stomach started to feel hungry. After they finished cleaning, Tom's mom bought the cheap toy for him. Tom was very happy to have the toy that could stand. He played with it all day and forgot about his hungry stomach. Tom learned that helping his mom was fun and that cheap toys can make him happy too.

Molly was a young girl who loved spending time outdoors. One day, she went out to explore the woods near her home. As she was walking, she noticed a small collar lying on the ground. She bent down to take a closer look. Molly picked up the collar and examined it. It was an old red collar with a golden bell attached. She wondered who it belonged to. "Maybe I'll find out," she said. Suddenly, Molly heard a noise coming from behind her. It was a young puppy. He was small and furry, and wearing the same collar. Molly realized the collar must belong to him. The puppy looked up at Molly with big eyes and wagged his tail. He seemed happy to find her. Molly smiled and held up the collar. The puppy barked excitedly and ran off into the woods.

Once upon a time, there was a big, messy room. In this room, there was a shiny trophy. The trophy was very special. It could blink its eyes. Yes, the trophy had eyes! One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the trophy and said, "Wow! What a shiny trophy!" The trophy blinked its eyes. Tim was surprised. "Did you just blink?" he asked. The trophy blinked again and said, "Yes, I can blink and talk!" Tim and the trophy became friends. They played and had fun in the messy room. They laughed and talked all day. Tim was very happy to have a new friend. And the trophy was happy to have someone to play with in the messy room.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sally. They loved to play together every day. One hot day, Sally felt the heat and said, "Bobo, I am hot. Can you open the door and let the cool air in?" Bobo barked happily and ran to the door. Bobo used his nose to open the door, and a cool breeze came inside. Sally smiled and said, "Thank you, Bobo! You are such a dependable friend." After the door was open, Sally and Bobo went outside to play. They ran and jumped in the grass, laughing and having fun. As the sun began to set, they went back inside, tired but happy. They knew they could always depend on each other, no matter what.

One day, a boy named Tim went to ride his bike. He liked to ride fast and go far. Today, he wanted to find a pie. His mom made the best pie, but it was boring. He wanted to try a new pie. Tim rode his bike to a big tree. He saw a pie up high in the tree! It was not boring. It was a big, red pie. Tim wanted to eat the pie, but it was too high. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. Then, a big bird came and took the pie away. Tim was sad. He rode back home with no pie. When he got home, his mom had a new pie. It was not boring. It was a big, blue pie! Tim was so happy to eat the new pie with his mom.
