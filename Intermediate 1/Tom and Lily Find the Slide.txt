Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Lily Find the Slide

Tom and Lily are twins. They like to play in the park with their mom and dad. One day, they see a big slide. It is very wide and shiny. Tom and Lily want to go on the slide. "Can we go on the slide, mom?" Tom asks. "Sure, but you have to wait your turn and be careful," mom says. Tom and Lily run to the slide. They see a line of kids waiting to go on the slide. Tom and Lily join the line. They are very excited. Soon, it is their turn. Tom goes first. He climbs up the stairs and sits on the slide. He holds on to the sides and slides down. He feels the wind in his hair and the sun on his face. He laughs and yells, "Whee!" Lily goes next. She climbs up the stairs and sits on the slide. She holds on to the sides and slides down. She feels the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She laughs and yells, "Whee!" Tom and Lily are happy. They have done their task. They have gone on the wide slide. They hug each other and run to their mom and dad. "Did you have fun on the slide?" dad asks. "Yes, we did!" Tom and Lily say. They tell their mom and dad about their slide adventure. Mom and dad smile and clap. They are proud of Tom and Lily. They love their twins.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to sing and eat dessert. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to play with her friends. While playing, Lily felt uncomfortable in her shoes. She sat down on the grass and said, "My feet hurt!" Her friend, Tim, came over and asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" She told him about her shoes. Tim said, "Take off your shoes and play without them!" Lily did that and felt much better. Lily was so happy that she started to sing a song. All her friends joined her, and they sang together. After they finished singing, Lily's mom came with a big plate of dessert. They all sat down and enjoyed the sweet treats. Lily smiled and thought, "What a great day!"

Once there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to play with her mom's makeup. One day, she found a big bag of makeup in her mom's room. Sue wanted to show her friends, so she decided to carry the bag to the park. At the park, Sue and her friends put on the makeup. They put on too much and did not look modest. They looked funny with makeup all over their faces. They laughed and played, but soon their faces felt itchy and dirty. Sue's mom came to the park and saw the mess. She told Sue that it's good to be modest and not use too much makeup. Sue and her friends learned that being modest is better. They washed their faces and felt happy and clean.

Once upon a time, in a small country, there lived a girl named Lucy. She loved to play outside with her friends. One day, while they were playing, they noticed a big tree with many pretty flowers. Lucy wanted to pick some flowers to take home, but her friends told her to be careful. There were bees flying around the tree, and they did not want Lucy to get hurt. But Lucy did not listen and went closer to the tree. As Lucy tried to pick the flowers, a bee flew near her. She got scared and ran away, but she tripped and fell. Her friends helped her up and told her that they should all be more careful next time. Lucy learned her lesson and promised to be more careful when playing outside.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Mimi. Mimi loved to sing songs all day long. One day, Mimi met a bird named Bobo. Bobo could sing songs too. They became good friends. Mimi and Bobo wanted to act in a play. They practiced their songs and acted together. Mimi said, "Bobo, we will be great in the play. Let's practice our big song for the end." They practiced their big song many times. They knew it would be a big surprise for everyone. The day of the play came. Mimi and Bobo were so excited. They acted and sang their songs. At the end, they sang their big song together. Everyone clapped and cheered. Mimi and Bobo were very happy. Their hard work paid off, and they had the best day ever.
