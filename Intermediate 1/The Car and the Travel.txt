Category: Intermediate
Title: The Car and the Travel

Once upon a time there was a car. The car liked to travel. One day the car decided to travel very fast. It went so fast that it made a lot of dirt around it. The car was having lots of fun. Then, it saw an old man. The old man said, "Car, car! Don't travel so fast. You're making too much dirt!" But the car didn't listen. It kept driving faster and faster, making more dirt. Suddenly, there was a big bang! The car stopped working and couldn't travel anymore. The car was sad. It asked the old man to help, but the old man shook his head. He said, "Sorry car, you should have listened. Now you can't travel anymore." The car was very sad. It had to stay still in the dirty place and never travel again.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little dog. They lived in a house with a nice family. The cat and the dog did not like to share their toys. One day, they had a quarrel about a ball. The cat said it was his ball, but the dog said it was his ball. The mom saw them fighting and she felt guilty. She wanted them to be friends and play together. She told them a story about a memory she had. In the memory, the cat and the dog were playing nicely with the ball. They were happy and having fun. The cat and the dog listened to the story. They thought about the memory and decided to share the ball. They played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, they were the best of friends and never had a quarrel again.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play alone in his room. One day, Tim found a magic belt that could change things. Tim put the belt around his waist and said, "I want a big toy car!" Suddenly, a big toy car appeared in his room. Tim was very happy and played with the car all day. Later, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw the magic belt and asked, "Can I try it?" Tim said, "No, it's mine!" Sam was sad and went home. Tim felt bad and tried to change things back, but the magic belt didn't work anymore. Tim was sad and lonely without his friend.

One day, a dog named Spot went for a jog in the park. Spot liked to run and play. He saw a thin stick on the ground. Spot picked up the stick with his mouth and wagged his tail. Spot saw a girl named Sue. Sue was playing with a ball. Spot ran to Sue with the stick in his mouth. He wanted to play with Sue. Sue saw Spot and smiled. "Hi Spot!" said Sue. "Do you want to play?" Spot barked and dropped the stick at Sue's feet. Sue picked up the stick and threw it far away. Spot ran fast to get the stick and brought it back to Sue. They played together all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was feeling sick. His mom wanted to use a thermometer to see if he had a fever. But Tim was scared of the thermometer. He said, "No, Mom! I don't want it!" Mom said, "Please, Tim. We need to see if you are sick." But Tim was slow to answer. He thought about it and said, "Okay, Mom. But you have to be gentle." Mom was very gentle with the thermometer. She put it under Tim's arm and waited. Tim was brave and didn't cry. The thermometer beeped, and Mom looked at it. She smiled and said, "Good news, Tim! You don't have a fever!" Tim was happy and felt a little better. He thanked his mom for being gentle with the thermometer. From that day on, Tim never refused to use the thermometer again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. One day, Tim's mom made a big, yummy plate of spaghetti for him. Tim was so happy! He knew he would eat it all. Before Tim started to eat, he saw an empty cup on the table. He remembered that he needed to drink some water with his meal. So, Tim went to the kitchen to fill his cup with water. He was excited to drink and eat his spaghetti. When Tim came back to the table, he saw that his spaghetti was gone! He was sad and confused. Then, he saw his dog, Max, with spaghetti on his face. Tim laughed and knew that Max ate his spaghetti. Tim's mom made more spaghetti for him and they all ate together. Tim was happy again and remembered to always keep his food close.
