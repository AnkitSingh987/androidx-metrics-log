Category: Intermediate
Title: The Family and the Box

Once there was a family who needed to sort out their home. Dad had an idea of sorting things into boxes. He said, "Let's try and sort out the kitchen and put everything in boxes!" Mum and the children all agreed and began to sort. Dad was sorting plates and put them into the big box with a big smile on his head. Mum was sorting spoons and put them in small boxes with a lovely smile. The children also joined in and helped to sort things. They sorted through the toys and put them into a medium box. One of them asked, "What else can we sort?" "We can sort our books," Dad said. He was proud that he had the idea of sorting the home and said, "Let's keep sorting. It's lovely to see our home become organized!" Everyone smiled and began to sort their books. They put the books into big boxes, with big smiles on their heads. They got a lot of work done and enjoyed spending time together sorting all the things in the house. They finished the job with big hugs to each other, grateful for the love they shared.

Once upon a time, there was a strong monster named Bob. He wanted to reach the mall very badly. But he was too scared to go there. One day Bob decided to be brave and take a chance. He walked to the mall full of courage. When he reached the mall, Bob was filled with joy. He wanted to go shopping and buy treats. But then he remembered why he wanted to go to the mall in the first place. It wasn't to buy treats; it was to be brave and reach out to new experiences. Bob smiled and was very proud of himself. He learned that being strong and trying his best was more important than he thought.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a fancy switch on the wall. It was shiny and pretty. A little girl named Lily lived in the house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily's mom said, "Lily, it's time to bathe. You are dirty from playing outside." Lily went to the bathroom and turned on the water. She saw the fancy switch on the wall and thought it was so pretty. While Lily was in the bath, she asked her mom, "Mom, what does the fancy switch do?" Her mom smiled and said, "That switch makes the room bright when it's dark. It's called a light switch." Lily was happy to learn about the fancy switch and enjoyed her bath.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a row of apple trees. The trees had red and green apples. The apples were very yummy. One day, a little boy named Tom went to the farm. He saw the row of apple trees. He wanted to pick the apples. But some apples were not good. They started to rot. Tom did not like the rotting apples. Tom felt sad and thought the farm was boring. He wanted to make the farm fun. So, he picked up the good apples and put them in a basket. Then, he took the basket to his friends. They all ate the yummy apples and played together. The farm was not boring anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to examine things. One day, she found a fragile box in her room. Lily was very careful with the box because she knew it could break. Inside the box, Lily found a pretty book. The book had many words in it. Lily liked to read the words and learn new things. She read the book every day and loved it very much. One day, Lily's friend, Tom, came to play. They looked at the book together. Tom was gentle with the fragile book, too. Lily and Tom read the words and had a lot of fun. They were happy to share the special book and learn together.

Mia and Sam liked to play on the beach. They liked to make sand castles and look for shells. One day, they saw something shiny and pink in the water. It looked like a flower. "Look, Mia, a flower!" Sam said. "Let's go see it!" Mia said. They ran to the water and splashed in. They got closer and closer to the pink thing. But it was not a flower. It was a jellyfish. "Ouch!" Sam cried. He felt a sting on his leg. He looked down and saw a red mark. He was scared and hurt. "Sam, are you okay?" Mia asked. She saw the jellyfish and knew it was bad. She remembered what Mom had told them. "Don't touch the jellyfish, they can hurt you!" She grabbed Sam's hand and pulled him out of the water. She ran to Mom and told her what happened. Mom was worried and angry. She wrapped Sam's leg with a towel and put some cream on it. She hugged him and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. You'll be okay. But you have to be careful on the beach. Some things are not what they seem. Some things are beautiful but dangerous." Mia nodded. She felt sorry for Sam. She looked at the jellyfish and thought, "You are not a flower. You are a mean jellyfish. I don't like you."
