Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Hat and the Coat

Once upon a time, there was a big, important lawyer. He liked to wear a big hat and a long coat. One day, he went for a walk in the park. He saw many kids playing and having fun. As he walked, he saw a little boy hiding behind a tree. The boy jumped out and said, "Boo! I scare you!" The lawyer was not scared. He smiled and said, "You are a brave boy, but I am not scared." The boy looked sad. He wanted to scare the important lawyer. So, the boy had an idea. He said, "If I can't scare you, can I be a lawyer like you?" The lawyer thought for a moment and said, "Yes, you can be a lawyer too." The boy was very happy. He put on a big hat and a long coat just like the important lawyer. They walked together in the park, and the boy learned how to be a good lawyer. And sometimes, they even scared each other just for fun.

Lily and Ben were very happy. They were going on vacation with Mom and Dad. They packed their bags and got in the car. They drove for a long time, but they did not mind. They sang songs and played games. They arrived at a big house near the lake. It was very pretty and peaceful. There were many trees and flowers and birds. Lily and Ben ran out of the car and hugged Mom and Dad. They wanted to explore the house and the lake. "Can we go to the lake, Mom?" Lily asked. "Yes, but first we have to unpack and eat lunch," Mom said. "OK, Mom," Lily said. She and Ben helped Mom and Dad with the bags. They put them in their rooms and then went to the kitchen. Mom made sandwiches and juice for everyone. They ate and drank and thanked Mom. "Now, can we go to the lake, Mom?" Ben asked. "Yes, but you have to wear your hats and sunscreen," Mom said. "OK, Mom," Ben said. He and Lily put on their hats and sunscreen. They took their towels and toys and followed Mom and Dad to the lake. It was very blue and shiny. There were boats and ducks and fish. Lily and Ben were very excited. They played in the water and on the sand. They made a castle and a moat. They searched for shells and stones. They fed the ducks and watched the fish. They had a lot of fun. They heard Mom call them. "It's time to go back, kids," Mom said. "OK, Mom," Lily and Ben said. They gathered their things and dried themselves. They hugged Mom and Dad and said, "We love the lake. It's the best vacation ever." Mom and Dad smiled and said, "We love you, too. And we love the lake. It's very peaceful and beautiful." They walked back to the house, holding hands. They were very happy. They were on vacation.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day and play all night. He had a friend, a dog named Max. Max was always happy and liked to run and play with Tom. One day, Max met a pretty dog named Bella. Max liked Bella a lot and wanted to be with her forever. Max asked Tom, "Do you think Bella would like to be with me in a marriage?" Tom nodded his head, saying yes. Max and Bella had a big party for their marriage. All their friends came to celebrate. Tom, the lazy cat, even woke up early to be there. Max and Bella were very happy, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to a classroom to learn and play with his friends. The classroom was full of toys, colors, and happy kids. One day, Tim was playing with a ball. He liked to kick the ball very high in the air. His friends watched and clapped when Tim kicked the ball. They all wanted to kick the ball too. But then, an angry boy named Max came to the classroom. Max did not want to share the ball. He kicked the ball away from Tim and his friends. Tim and his friends were sad. They talked to Max and asked him to be kind. Max listened and said sorry. They all played together and had fun in the classroom.
