Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Powerful Cat and the Puddle

One day, a powerful cat named Tom went out to play. It started to rain. Tom did not like the rain, but he wanted to have fun. Tom saw a big puddle. He wanted to jump in it. He ran fast and jumped high. But when he landed, he felt his feet slip. Tom fell down into the water. He was wet and cold. Tom went back home. His mom saw him and helped him. She gave him a warm towel and a big hug. Tom learned to be careful when he plays in the rain.

One day, there was a mysterious cloth in the park. Little Anna wondered what the cloth was. She asked her mom if she could go look at it. "Yes, but don't touch it," Mom said. So Anna went over to the cloth. She stood by it and looked at it. She noticed it was different colours and shapes. "What is this?" Anna asked. Suddenly, a voice came from the cloth. "Can you answer my riddle?" it said. Anna was surprised. She had never heard a cloth talk before. "Um... what is the riddle?" she asked. "What has four legs, but can't walk?" Anna thought for a moment, and then she had the answer. "A chair!" she shouted out. The cloth began to move. "You are correct!" it said. Anna smiled. She had just solved the mysterious cloth's riddle.

Once upon a time, there was a quiet duck. The duck lived near a pond. The duck had many friends. They all played together. One day, the duck's friend, a frog, was sad. The frog's toy had disappeared. The duck wanted to help the frog. They looked for the toy near the pond. They asked their friends if they saw the toy. But no one knew where the toy was. The duck and the frog kept looking. At last, they found the toy near a big rock. The frog was happy again. The duck and the frog played with the toy. They were glad they found it. The quiet duck and the frog were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a big stadium, there lived a dog named Spot. Spot was an obedient dog who loved to help his friends. One day, Spot saw his friend, Cat, who was stuck in a tree. Spot called out, "Cat, do you need help?" Cat said, "Yes, Spot, please help me!" Spot tried to think of a way to help Cat get down from the tree. He looked around and saw a big ball. Spot had an idea. He pushed the big ball to the tree and tried to help Cat jump onto the ball. But the ball was too bouncy. When Cat jumped, he fell off the ball and got hurt. Spot was sad that he could not help Cat. Now, Cat had to go to the doctor because of his fall. Spot learned that sometimes, even when you try to help, things can still go wrong.

Once upon a time, there was a little animal named Bobo. Bobo lived in a big, open field with his animal friends. They played together every day under the warm sun. Bobo loved his friends, but sometimes he did not behave well. One day, Bobo's friend, Lulu the bunny, said, "Bobo, you need to behave. If you don't behave, something bad might happen to you." Bobo just laughed and said, "I don't care, Lulu. I just want to have fun and play." The next day, Bobo and his friends were playing near the edge of the open field. Bobo still did not behave. He ran too close to the edge, even though Lulu told him to be careful. Suddenly, Bobo fell into a deep hole. His friends tried to help, but they could not reach him. Bobo was scared and sad. He wished he had listened to Lulu and behaved better. Now, he was stuck in the hole with no way out. Bobo learned the hard way that not behaving can lead to bad things happening.

Once upon a time, in a busy forest, there was a tall pine tree. The pine tree had many friends. One day, a little bird came to live in the tree. The bird was very happy in the tree, and they became good friends. The bird said to the tree, "You are so tall and strong. I want to marry you." The tree was happy and said, "Yes, let's marry." All the animals in the forest came to see the tree and the bird marry. They were all very happy. But one day, a big storm came. The wind blew very hard. The tree tried to stay strong for the bird. The bird held on tight to the tree. After the storm, the tree and the bird were still together. They learned that if they work together, they can face anything. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can face any problem.
