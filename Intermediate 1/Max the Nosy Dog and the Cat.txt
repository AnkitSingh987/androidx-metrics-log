Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Nosy Dog and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little dog named Max. Max loved to sniff and dig in the dirt. One day, Max found a shiny copper coin in the dirt. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his best friend, Sam the cat. Max went to Sam's house to show him the copper coin. Sam was very nosy too, and he wanted to play with the coin. Max said, "Okay, but we have to be careful." So, Max and Sam played with the coin in Sam's yard. They tossed it in the air and tried to catch it. While they were playing, a big wind came and took the copper coin away. Max and Sam tried to run and catch it, but it was too fast. They were very sad. Max and Sam learned that they should not have been nosy and played with the coin. They wished they could go back and release the coin in a safe place. But now, the copper coin was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved flowers. One day, she saw a flower that did not bloom. Sue felt sad for the flower. She wanted to help it. Sue went to the store to buy some water for the flower. She needed money to buy the water. But she did not have any money. Sue felt guilty. She took some money from her mom's purse. Sue bought the water and gave it to the flower. The next day, Sue went to see the flower. It had bloomed! But the flower was not just any flower. It was a magic flower! The flower told Sue, "Thank you for helping me. I will give you a wish." Sue wished for more money to help more flowers. The magic flower made her wish come true. Sue learned that doing good things can bring unexpected surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat who lived on a farm. All the animals on the farm were very happy. One day, the little goat found something special. It was a shiny red chilli! It looked so tasty and spicy! The little goat was so excited. He couldn't wait to try it. But deep down he was afraid. He nervously put the chilli close to his nose, and took a small sniff. Suddenly, he started to tremble, and quickly put it back down. The goat's friend, a pig, noticed and waved his hoof. "Why don't you try it?" he said. The goat had an idea. He picked up the chilli again and put it in his mouth, then stuck his thumb out to stop it from going any further. The pig smiled. "Go on, try it!" The goat hesitated, then mustered all his courage and took a brave little bite. Surprisingly, it wasn't as spicy as he'd thought it would be! The little goat smiled and jumped around with joy. He'd put his fears aside and found out that there was nothing to be so scared of! He had discovered something incredible. He was now brave and happy, thanks to the tasty spicy chilli!

Once upon a time, in a green park, there was a little girl named Lucy. Lucy loved summer. She liked to play outside in the sun. Her favorite thing to do was swing on the swings. She would swing high up in the sky and feel the wind on her face. One sunny day, Lucy went to the park to swing. She saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was swinging too. They both liked to swing very high. They laughed and had fun together. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, green bird came and sat on Lucy's swing! Lucy and Tim stopped swinging and looked at the bird. The bird started to swing with them! They all laughed and had fun together. The big, green bird became their new friend, and they played together all summer long.

Once upon a time, there was a black pick. It was set in the middle of a field on a bright day. Everyone in the land hated the black pick, but none of them knew why. One day, a little boy, who was only three, came to the pick. He picked up the black pick, looked at it carefully and said, "This pick doesn't seem so bad. I don't hate it." Everyone who heard the little boy was shocked. Then they started to think and realized that maybe they didn't need to hate the black pick. All the people in the land agreed and soon enough, they all had a smile on their faces and the pick was the pride of the land. The End.
