Category: Intermediate
Title: Timmy and the Orange

Timmy was so excited to take his new motorcycle for a ride. He said, "I'm gonna zoom all around town on my motorcycle!" His dad said, "Be careful Timmy, a motorcycle is fast and can be dangerous if you're not careful." Timmy ignored his dad and started the engine. He was so proud of his new motorcycle, it was orange and shiny! As he zoomed around town, his friends watched in awe. He wanted to show off so he drove even faster. But soon Timmy noticed a spicy smell in the air. His engine was too hot! The motorcycle slowed down and then stopped completely. Timmy was really sad but luckily his dad was there to help. It was too late though, the motorcycle was broken. Timmy cried and said, "I wish I had listened to daddy. Now I can't zoom anymore."

Tim and Sam are brothers. They like to play with toys and cars. One day, they find a switch on the wall. They do not know what it does. "Let's see what happens when we push it," Tim says. "No, don't do that. Mom said we should not touch it," Sam says. But Tim does not listen. He pushes the switch. Nothing happens. He pushes it again and again. "Stop it, Tim. You are being bad," Sam says. But Tim does not stop. He laughs and pushes the switch more. Then, something terrible happens. The switch makes a loud noise and sparks fly out. The switch breaks and the lights go out. The room is dark and scary. "Tim, what did you do?" Sam cries. "I don't know. I was just playing," Tim says. They hear Mom coming. She is angry and worried. She sees the broken switch and the dark room. She sees Tim and Sam. "Who did this?" she asks. "It was Tim. He pushed the switch and broke it," Sam says. "Tim, how could you? You know you are not supposed to touch the switch. It is dangerous. You could have hurt yourself or Sam. You are in big trouble, young man," Mom says. She takes Tim by the hand and leads him to his room. She says he has to stay there until Dad comes home. She says he has to learn his lesson and not be naughty. She says she is very disappointed in him. Tim feels sad and sorry. He wishes he had listened to Sam. He wishes he had not pushed the switch. He wishes he could say sorry to Mom. He wishes he could play with Sam. He wishes he could have fun. But he can't. He has to stay in his room. He has to wait for Dad. He is scared of what Dad will say. He knows he did a terrible thing. He knows he has to be taught a hard lesson. He knows he has a bad ending.

Mum and Dad came home with a big bundle. Everyone was confused. They took it into their bedroom and started to unpack it. When they finished, there was a sparkling blue box. It made everyone grin. Mum opened the box, and out tumbled a brand new toy train set. Everyone was excited and happy. Dad quickly set up the train track and showed the little ones how it worked. The kids were amazed! It rolled around the track, going around and around, puffing out tiny clouds of smoke. Mum, Dad and the kids were so happy and excited. They spent the rest of the day with the blue train and exploring what else it could do.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet named Bounce. Bounce was very excited to see the big sky. He wanted to play with the stars and the moon. Bounce was always happy and loved to play with his friends. One day, Bounce saw a big star. The star was sad because it was all alone. Bounce wanted to help the star feel happy. So, he went to the star and said, "Let's play together!" The star smiled and they started to play. They had so much fun and became good friends. As they played, Bounce and the star saw more friends who were sad and alone. Bounce and the star invited them to play too. Soon, all the comets, stars, and the moon were playing together and everyone was happy. The sky was full of laughter and joy. Bounce learned that it was good to help others and make friends. And everyone in the sky was happy because they learned to play together and share their happiness.
