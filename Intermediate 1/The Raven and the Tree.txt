Category: Intermediate
Title: The Raven and the Tree

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a raven. This raven was very popular. All the other birds liked to play with the raven. They played games and had fun every day. One day, it was very hot outside. The raven started to complain. He said, "It's too hot. I don't want to play anymore." The other birds were sad. They did not want the raven to be unhappy. So, the birds thought of a plan. They found a big leaf and used it to make some shade for the raven. Now, the raven was not hot anymore. He was happy and stopped complaining. They all played together again and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a nice house with a big attic. In the attic, there lived a friendly cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to keep things neat and clean in her attic home. One sunny day, a little bird named Tweetie flew into the attic. Tweetie saw how nice and clean the attic was. Tweetie said, "Hello, Kitty! Your attic is so nice. Can I stay here with you?" Kitty thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Tweetie, you can stay. But you must help me keep the attic nice and clean." Tweetie agreed, and they became good friends. Every day, Kitty and Tweetie worked together to keep the attic nice. They played and had fun in their clean home. They were very happy and loved living in the nice attic together.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little boy named Tim. He could read big books and do hard math. One day, Tim saw a sign at school. The sign said, "Talent Show Today!" Tim wanted to be in the talent show, but he was scared. Tim's friend, Sue, wanted to help him. She said, "Tim, you can do it! You are gifted!" But Tim was still scared. He said, "No, I can't do it." He refused to be in the talent show. Sue was sad, but she did not give up. Sue had an idea. She said, "Let's do it together, Tim!" Tim thought about it and said, "Okay, let's do it." They went on stage and showed everyone how gifted they were. Tim was happy that he did not refuse to be in the talent show anymore.

One day, a little mouse went out to play. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. The mouse saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As the mouse climbed, he met a bird. The bird said, "Hi, mouse! Why are you here?" The mouse said, "I want to see the bright sky up close." The bird blinked and said, "I can show you the sky. Just hold onto my back." The mouse held onto the bird, and they flew up high. They saw the bright sky and the big, green world below. But then, something unexpected happened! The bird turned into a big, friendly cat. The mouse was scared at first, but the cat said, "Don't be scared, mouse. I just wanted to show you the sky, too." The mouse and the cat became good friends. They played together every day under the bright sky, and the mouse was never scared of the cat again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there were two friends, Tom and Tim. They both liked to play with tops. One day, they found a bright top in the park. It was so pretty, they both wanted it. Tom said, "I saw it first!" But Tim said, "No, I saw it first!" They began to quarrel about who should get the top. They got very mad at each other. A wise old man walked by and saw them fighting. He said, "Why not share the bright top and play together?" Tom and Tim thought about it and decided to share. They played with the top and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom and Tim learned to share their toys and never quarrel again. The moral of the story is that sharing is better than fighting.

Once upon a time, there was a little player. He loved to play with his ball. One day, he saw a big toy store. He went inside to find more toys to play with. In the store, he found a fake toy ball. It looked like his real ball, but it was not the same. The player nodded his head and said, "I want this ball too." He took the fake ball and went to the counter to pay. The player went home with his new fake ball. He played with it, but it was not as fun as his real ball. The fake ball broke after a few days. The player was sad and wished he never bought the fake ball.
