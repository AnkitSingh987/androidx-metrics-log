Category: Intermediate
Title: Mia the Girl and the Butterfly

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk with her mom. They walked in a big park with lots of trees and flowers. Mia saw a clean patch of grass and said, "Mom, can we sit here?" Her mom said, "Yes, Mia. This is a nice clean spot. Let's sit down and have our snack." As they sat on the clean patch, Mia saw a pretty butterfly. She wanted to touch it. She reached out her hand, but the butterfly flew away. Mia felt sad. Her mom said, "Don't be sad, Mia. Butterflies are gentle. We should not touch them. They need to fly free." Mia smiled and said, "Okay, Mom. I won't touch the butterfly. I will just look at it and let it fly." They sat on the clean patch, watching the butterflies and enjoying their snack.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot met a new friend, a cat named Fluffy. They played together with the ball. Spot and Fluffy found a book with numbers and spells. They tried to read it, but it was hard. Spot said, "Let's learn to number and spell together!" Fluffy agreed, and they started to learn. They counted one, two, three, and more. They spelled cat, dog, and ball. They were happy and proud. Spot and Fluffy played and learned together every day. They became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little girl named Sarah. She was walking through the park when she suddenly noticed something strange. There were piles of funny-looking toes everywhere! She asked her mom what was going on and her mom told her that everyone was helping to sort the toes. Sarah was really excited and asked her mom if she could help. Her mom said “yes,” so Sarah started to sort the toes. She soon discovered that some toes were bigger than others! She was so pleased with herself she started to dance around. Suddenly, Sarah saw her friend Grace on the other side of the park. She waved and shouted “Hi Grace!” Grace waved back and shouted “Let’s play together!” So, Sarah and Grace began to play with the toes. They made a jolly game of piling them up and knocking them down. They laughed and laughed as the toes flew everywhere. At the end of the day, Sarah and Grace walked away with big smiles on their faces. They had sorted the toes and had a lot of fun too!

Anna and Ben are playing in the garden. They have a tent, a blanket and some toys. They pretend they are on an adventure in the jungle. "Look, Ben, a lion!" Anna says, pointing to a big cat on the fence. "We have to escape!" Ben nods and grabs a toy gun. He makes a loud noise and scares the cat away. "Quick, Anna, follow me!" He runs to the tent and pulls Anna inside. They close the flap and sit on the blanket. They are safe now. They smile at each other. "We are successful, Anna. We escaped the lion!" Ben says. "Yes, we are, Ben. We are brave and smart!" Anna says. They hug and laugh. Then they see a basket with some cups and a pot. Anna's mom left it for them. "Look, Ben, tea!" Anna says. "Let's have a tea party!" Ben agrees and pours some tea for both of them. They sip and chat. They enjoy their adventure and their tea. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous bear. The bear loved to eat oats. One day, he found a big bag of oats near his home. He was very happy. The bear decided to eat some oats, but he wanted to save some for later. He put the bag in a safe place and went to play with his friends. They played hide and seek in the forest. When the bear came back home, he found that the bag of oats had disappeared! He was very sad. He looked everywhere for the bag. At last, he found it near a tree. The wind had blown it away. The bear was happy again and ate some more oats. And from that day on, he always made sure to keep his bag of oats in a safer place.
