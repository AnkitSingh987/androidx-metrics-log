Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Boy and the Park

One day, a boy named Tom and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They saw a big tent. It was red and blue. Tom said, "Look, Spot! That tent is perfect for us to play in!" They ran to the tent and went inside. Inside the tent, they found a big toy box. Tom and Spot played with the toys. They played catch and laughed. Then, Tom saw a big red ball. He threw it for Spot to catch. Spot ran fast, but he could not stop. He bumped into the tent wall. Tom said, "Stop, Spot! Be careful!" They laughed and played some more. They had so much fun in the perfect tent. At the end of the day, they went home, happy and tired.

Ben and Mia like to play with their printer. They print pictures of animals and cars and flowers. They cut them out and stick them on the wall. They have a lot of fun. One day, they find a new picture on the printer. It is a picture of a skunk. It has black and white fur and a big tail. Ben and Mia do not know what a skunk is. They think it is a funny cat. "Look, Mia, a funny cat!" Ben says. He picks up the picture and shows it to Mia. "Let's print more funny cats!" Mia says. She presses the button on the printer. The printer makes a noise and prints another picture of a skunk. Ben and Mia print more and more pictures of skunks. They stick them all over the wall. They are very happy. But then, they hear a knock on the door. It is their mom. She opens the door and smells something bad. She sees the pictures of skunks on the wall. She knows what a skunk is. She is not happy. "Ben! Mia! What have you done?" she says. "These are not funny cats. These are skunks. They are very smelly animals. They spray a bad smell when they are scared. You have to take down these pictures. They make the room stink." Ben and Mia are surprised. They did not know that skunks are smelly. They did not mean to make the room stink. They are sorry. "Sorry, mom," they say. They take down the pictures of skunks. They throw them in the trash. They promise not to print any more skunks. Mom is still mad, but she loves Ben and Mia. She gives them a hug and a kiss. She tells them to wash their hands and go outside. She opens the window and sprays some perfume. She hopes the smell will go away soon.

Once upon a time, there was a soldier. He was a very nice soldier. He wanted to find a friend. One day, he walked in the park. He saw many people, but no one was available to play with him. Then, he heard a ring sound. He looked around and saw a little bell. The soldier picked up the bell and rang it again. It made a pretty sound. He liked the bell and thought it would be fun to play with. The soldier and the bell played all day. They had so much fun together. They became the best of friends. The soldier was happy he found a friend in the bell. They played and rang the bell every day, and they were never alone again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to pass a big red ball to her friends. They would play with the ball every day at the park. One day, Lily and her friends found a new park. This park had a special point where they could see the whole town. Lily thought it was a great place to play with the big red ball. Lily's friend, Sam, was very reliable. He could catch the ball every time Lily passed it to him. They had lots of fun at the special point. They played with the big red ball until the sun went down. Then, they went home with big smiles on their faces, excited to play again tomorrow.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She liked organizing things and would organize her toys every day. One day, Amy’s daddy took her to the beach. Amy was very excited, and when they arrived she noticed a lot of funny grey round things on the sand. Her daddy told her they were oysters. Amy wanted to collect some and organize them in a pile. She loved how yellow they were and wanted to show her mummy and daddy. So Amy started to gather them, and organize them in a big yellow heap. “See Daddy!” shouted Amy, proudly. ”Wow Amy, they look great!” said her Daddy. And ever since that day, Amy loved to organize her oysters and show her family her amazing yellow piles!
