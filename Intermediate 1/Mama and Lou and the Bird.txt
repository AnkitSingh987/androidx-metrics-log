Category: Intermediate
Title: Mama and Lou and the Bird

Mama and Lou were walking in the park. Lou saw lots of yummy food. "Can I have some, Mama?" asked Lou. Mama smiled, "Yes, of course." Mama took out a muffin from her bag and gave it to Lou. Lou was so excited. He quickly ate the muffin. But then Lou noticed something strange. He saw a giant bird standing near him. Lou was scared. Mama saw the bird too. "Don't worry," Mama said, "The bird won't hurt us." Lou stopped being scared and smiled. He held out his muffin and said, "Want some food, bird?" The bird came close and opened its big beak. Lou tried to feed the bird his muffin. But then the bird quickly flew away. Lou felt so sad. He started to cry. Mama hugged him tight and said, "Don't worry, we will find another muffin for you."

One day, a little green frog was hopping around a pond. He hopped up to a big sign and read it. Frog: "What does this sign say?" He hopped from side to side, trying to figure out what it said. Suddenly, he heard a voice. Voice: "Rise up high and you will see!" The frog looked up and sure enough, there was a rainbow rising into the sky! The frog hopped up and up until he reached the rainbow. He smiled and then he hopped back down to the sign. Frog: "That was so nice! I rose up and saw the rainbow!" The frog smiled and hopped away. He had learned something new that day!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a favorite sock. The sock was red and dry. He loved to wear it every day. One day, Tim went outside to play. He saw a big dog. The dog was very nice. Tim observed the dog as it played in the yard. He thought, "Maybe the dog will like my sock too!" Tim took off his red sock and gave it to the dog. The dog was so happy! They played together all day. The dog took the sock in its mouth and ran around the yard. Tim laughed and chased the dog. At the end of the day, the dog gave the sock back to Tim. The sock was not dry anymore. It was wet from the dog's mouth. But Tim didn't care. He had so much fun playing with the dog. He knew that the sock would be dry again soon, and they could play together another day.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Alice. She was three and full of curiosity. One day when Alice was playing in the garden she saw a stranger. He was tall and looked friendly. Alice stared at the strange man, but he didn't look back. Alice had a tear in her eye. She was scared and didn't know what to do. But then the stranger smiled at Alice and she felt safe. He was kind and honest. Alice said hello and the stranger replied. They smiled at each other and then the stranger waved goodbye. Alice watched the stranger walk away, but she wasn't scared – she felt happy. She knew the stranger was honest and she knew they would be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong farmer named Sam. He had a big, busy farm and lots of animals. Every day he had to push around the farm to take care of all his animals and plants. One day, when Sam was pushing his wheelbarrow, he met a little chick. The chick was scared and didn't know how to get back home. Sam asked her, "Where is your home, little one?" The chick said, "I don't know. I'm lost!" Sam insisted, "You have to push and find your way back home. It's important to stay strong and never give up!" The chick listened to Sam and pushed until she found her way home. The little chick was so happy! Sam was pleased and said, "It's important to stay strong and never give up, even when you are lost. Just keep pushing and you will find your way home." The moral of the story is that you should always stay strong and never give up, even when you are lost. If you keep pushing, you can find your way back home.
