Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cone

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a perfect day at the park. He played with his friends, and they had so much fun. Tim's mom said, "Let's get a cone!" Tim was so happy. Tim and his mom went to the ice cream truck. They got a big cone with ice cream on top. Tim took a big bite. He loved it. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew by and took a bite of Tim's cone too! Tim was scared at first, but then he laughed. The little bird was just hungry. Tim shared his cone with the bird. They ate the ice cream together. In the end, Tim made a new friend, and they all had a perfect day.

One day, a boy named Tom and his dog, Spot, went to play at the park. Tom liked to shout and run with Spot. They were very happy playing together. Spot was different from other dogs because he could talk. "Tom," said Spot, "I need to tell you a truth. I am not like other dogs. I can do something special." Tom looked at Spot and asked, "What can you do?" Spot said, "I can fly!" Tom was very surprised. He did not think that dogs could fly. But then, Spot started to fly up in the sky. Tom shouted, "Wow, Spot! You are amazing!" Spot flew around the park and all the other kids saw him. They shouted too, because they were so happy. Tom and Spot had a fun day at the park. They knew that being different was a good thing. They went home and told their mom about their day. She hugged them and said, "I am proud of you both!"

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a green egg on the ground. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sue. "Look what I found!" he said. Sue looked at the green egg and said, "It's so rare! Let's lay it down in a safe place." They found a soft spot under a big tree and lay the green egg there. As they sat and watched, something unexpected happened. The green egg started to shake and crack. A baby bird came out of the egg! Tim and Sue were so happy and surprised. They knew they had found a very special, rare green egg.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful cat named Lucy. Lucy lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Mia. They loved to play together every day. One day, Mia could not find her favorite toy. She looked everywhere, but it was not there. Mia said to Lucy, "I can't find my toy. Can you help me?" Lucy nodded her head and began to look for the toy. She searched all around the house, but the toy was not in any of the rooms. Lucy thought very hard about where the toy could be. Finally, Lucy remembered that they played near the couch the day before. She went to the couch and looked under it. There, she found Mia's favorite toy! Mia was so happy that Lucy found her toy. They played together all day long, and from that day on, they always checked under the couch when something was missing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She was a curious girl who liked to go on walks. One day, Mia went for a walk in the woods. She saw a big tree and thought, "I have not seen this tree yet." Mia walked up to the tree and saw a tiny door on it. She was very curious about what was behind the door. She opened the door, and to her surprise, she met a little squirrel. The squirrel said, "Hello, Mia! I have been waiting to meet you." Mia was very happy to meet her new friend, the squirrel. They played together in the woods all day long. When it was time to go home, the squirrel gave Mia a big hug and said, "I knew we would meet today, and I am so glad we did." Mia smiled and went back home, excited to tell her family about her new friend.
