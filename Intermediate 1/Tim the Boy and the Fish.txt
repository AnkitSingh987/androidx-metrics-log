Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Fish

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to swim in the pool. One hot day, Tim went to the pool with his mom. They had so much fun. They splashed in the water, played with beach balls, and laughed together. Tim saw a little fish in the pool. The fish was angry. It said, "I want to sleep, but I can't because of all the noise!" Tim felt bad for the fish. He told his mom, "Let's be quiet so the fish can sleep." They whispered and played gently in the pool. Soon, the angry fish was happy. It closed its eyes and went to sleep. Tim and his mom continued to have fun, making sure not to wake the fish. And they all had a great day at the pool.

Once upon a time there were two children. They were friends and loved to play together. One day they went to the park. The children saw a big swing. They both wanted to go on the swing. They laughed as they went up high and swung in circles. Next they noticed a big bucket of chalk near the swing. The chalk was different colours and the children used it to draw different shapes on the ground. Finally, they went over to the pond. It was very deep. They looked down and saw fish. They both laughed and watched the fish swim. The two children had a great day playing together at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a pet cat named Fluffy. Mia and Fluffy loved to play together. They would run and jump in the sun all day. Fluffy was a calm cat and always made Mia happy. One day, Mia saw that Fluffy was hungry. She wanted to feed Fluffy to make her feel good. Mia went to the kitchen and found cat food. She put the food in a bowl and gave it to Fluffy. Fluffy ate the food and licked her lips. Mia felt happy that she could help her friend. After Fluffy ate, she felt better. Her body was full and she was not hungry anymore. Mia and Fluffy went outside to play again. They played in the grass and had lots of fun. And when the sun went down, they went inside, ready for a calm night's sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and her best friend, a dog named Max. They played all day in their big backyard. Lily and Max liked to include all their friends in their games. One sunny day, they were playing hide and seek. Lily said to Max, "I will count, and you go hide." Max ran off to find a hiding spot. Lily counted to ten and then went to look for Max. She looked behind the trees, under the bushes, and in the flower beds. But she could not find Max anywhere. Then, Lily saw a shiny rock on the ground. She picked it up and saw that it was gold! She was very surprised. Just then, Max jumped out from behind a bush and barked, "You found me and the gold!" They both laughed and were happy. They took the gold rock to their mom, who told them it was a special treasure. From that day on, Lily and Max knew that their backyard was a magical place where anything could happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a new toy cat that she loved very much. She would hold her toy cat all the time and take it everywhere she went. One day, Lily and her toy cat went for a walk in the park. As they walked, they saw a big box. Lily was so shocked to see it there. She went to the box and opened it. Inside the box was a real cat! The real cat looked just like her toy cat. Lily was so happy to find a new friend. She held both her toy cat and the real cat in her arms. From that day on, Lily, her toy cat, and the real cat were the best of friends. They played together and had lots of fun. And that was the best surprise Lily ever had.
