Category: Intermediate
Title: The Bald Man and the Friends

Once there was a bald man. He was surprised because he had found a network of friends. He thought he was all alone but he was wrong. The network of people brought the bald man lots of joy. He was surprised by all the new friends he made. He thought that he would never have so many friends. The bald man laughed and tossed his head. He was happy to have so many new buddies. He liked playing with the network of friends and meeting new ones every day. The bald man was delighted. He never thought he would have so many friends. He was truly surprised by this beautiful network of people that gathered around him. There was joy in the sky and friendship was everywhere.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot loved to help his family. One day, Mom asked Spot to bring a toy from the cupboard. Spot wagged his tail and ran to the cupboard. Spot looked at the cupboard and saw many toys. He did not know which toy to bring. He thought, "Should I bring the ball or the bone?" Spot was confused. He did not want to make Mom sad. So, Spot decided to ask his friend, the cat. "Cat, which toy should I bring to Mom?" asked Spot. The cat said, "Bring the ball. Mom will like it." Spot trusted the cat and took the ball from the cupboard. Spot brought the ball to Mom. Mom smiled and said, "Good job, Spot! You picked the right toy." Spot felt happy and wagged his tail. The cat was right, and Spot knew he could trust his friend. From that day on, Spot and the cat became best friends, always helping each other.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy dog named Max. Max liked to run and play in the big yard. One day, Max saw a ball and tried to catch it. But Max was so clumsy, he fell into a mud patch. Max was very dirty, but he was happy. Max wanted to play more, so he paused for a moment to think. He saw a big tree with a swing. Max went to the swing and pushed it with his nose. The swing went back and forth. Max tried to jump on the swing, but he was too clumsy and fell on the grass. Max laughed and rolled around. Max's friend, a little girl named Lily, came to play with him. She saw Max in the mud patch and laughed. Lily helped Max clean up and they played together. Max may be a clumsy dog, but he was always happy and made everyone around him smile.

One day, a boy named Tom went to play basketball. The sky was gloomy, but he still wanted to play. He took his ball and went to the park. In the park, he saw a big basketball hoop. He was very happy. Tom started to play with his ball. He tried to throw it into the hoop. He missed, but he did not give up. He tried again and again. Then, a girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tom playing and asked, "Can I play too?" Tom said, "Yes, you can play." They played together and had lots of fun. They laughed and threw the ball into the hoop. But then, something unexpected happened. A man came to the park and said, "You have to pay to play here." Tom and Sue were sad. They did not have any money. They looked at each other and had an idea. They took the ball and went to another park. There, they played basketball for free and had even more fun. The gloomy day turned into a happy day for Tom and Sue.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a soft velvet doll that she loved very much. One day, she saw an amazing new doll in the store. She asked her mom, "Can I have the new doll?" Her mom said, "We can get it, but you must replace the old doll with the new one." Lily thought about it and said yes. She got the new doll and put her old velvet doll away. She played with the new doll for a while, but soon she missed her old doll. The new doll was not as soft and warm as the old one. Lily felt sad. Lily's mom saw her sad face and knew what to do. She got the velvet doll from the closet and gave it back to Lily. Lily hugged the doll and was very happy. The moral of the story is that new things may look amazing, but they can't always replace the love and comfort of the things we already have.
