Category: Intermediate
Title: The Rabbit and the Fox

Once there was a small rabbit. The rabbit wanted to get to the other side of the forest. So it started walking, but then it stumbled and fell. Looking up, it saw a cross in the distance. The rabbit was happy and started hopping towards the cross. The rabbit hopped and hopped but it was going slow. It kept stumbling along the way. Suddenly, a big fox appeared! The fox was so fast! The rabbit was scared and started hopping faster, but it still stumbled. The fox came closer and closer and was going to catch the rabbit. The rabbit was so scared and didn't know what to do. It finally reached the cross and was about to get away from the fox - when the fox got it and ate it! The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play games with his friends. They played all day, and Tim was a good player. He always tried to be honest when he played with his friends. One day, Tim and his friends played a game called "Keep the Ball". In this game, they had to keep the ball away from the other team. Tim's friend, Sam, said, "Tim, let's play fair and be honest players." Tim nodded and they started the game. They ran, laughed, and tried their best to keep the ball. Tim's team was doing great, but then, Tim saw his friend, Sam, trip and fall. Tim stopped and helped Sam get up. "Are you okay, Sam?" Tim asked. Sam smiled and said, "Yes, thank you, Tim. You are a good friend and an honest player." Tim felt happy that he could help his friend, and they continued to play the game. In the end, Tim's team won, but everyone had fun and played honestly.

One day, Daddy wanted to organize his toys. He had many toys that he loved. He put all the balls in one box, and all the cars in another box. Daddy was very happy with his work. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew all the toys out of the boxes! The balls and cars were all mixed up again. Daddy felt guilty because he did not close the boxes. But then, a little bird came and helped Daddy. The bird picked up the toys with its beak and put them back in the boxes. The bird even closed the boxes for Daddy. Daddy was so happy and thanked the bird. Now, Daddy and the bird are best friends.

Once upon a time, there lived a little girl called Jane. Every day she went to the park to play. One day when she was playing, she saw some white powder all over the ground. She wondered what it was! So, she asked her dad. Her dad said, "It's icy powder. Be careful, Jane, it can make the ground slippery!" But Jane didn't listen. She started running and, sure enough, she slipped on the icy powder and fell down. Jane was very scared when she fell. But then she heard a voice. It was her dad! He said, "It's ok, Jane. You slipped because of the icy powder." Jane felt better. She looked around and saw the sunny sky and knew that everything was going to be alright.

One day, a little boat named Bob lived in a harbor. Bob was a regular boat, not too big and not too small. He had many friends in the harbor, like fish, birds, and other boats. One day, Bob's friend, a big boat named Tom, said, "I'm going far away for a long time. I will miss you, Bob." Bob was sad. He did not want his friend to go away. He said, "I will miss you too, Tom." While Tom was away, Bob felt lonely. He missed his friend a lot. The fish and birds tried to make Bob feel better, but it was not the same. Bob wanted his friend back. One day, Tom came back to the harbor. Bob saw him and was so happy. They both said, "I missed you!" Now, Bob and Tom could play together again, and the harbor was a happy place.
