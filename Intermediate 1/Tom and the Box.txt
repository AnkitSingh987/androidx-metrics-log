Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and the Box

One day, a big, lazy cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a long track in the grass. He thought, "I will follow this track and see what I find." As he walked, Tom met a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "No, I am too lazy to play. I just want to walk on this track." They kept walking and saw a big box. The box had a note. The note said, "This box is for you. It includes a surprise." The bird said, "Let's open it!" Tom said, "Okay, let's see what's inside." They opened the box and found a big cake! But, the cake jumped out of the box! It was a jumping cake! The cake said, "I am a magic cake! I can jump and play with you!" Tom and the bird laughed. They played with the cake all day. Tom was not lazy anymore.

Jack was playing in his yard one day when he saw something very surprising. A man in a uniform was walking down the street. Jack thought the uniform looked very interesting, so he decided to go closer and have a look. Jack followed the man in the uniform until they stopped outside a big, bad building. The man opened the door and stepped inside. Jack was so surprised that he didn't know what to do. He wondered what the man was doing inside the bad building. Jack wanted to find out, so he waited outside until the man came out. A few minutes later, the man came out with a big smile on his face, and he offered Jack a ride in the uniform car! Jack was so happy and he jumped in the car. The man drove Jack to the park, and they spent the rest of the day playing together. Jack was so surprised by the nice thing the man in the uniform had done! From that day on, they remained good friends, and Jack learned a valuable lesson about not judging people by the way they look.

Once upon a time, there was a big, happy dog named Yoga. Yoga loved to run and play in the park. One day, Yoga saw a graceful cat doing something new. The cat was moving and stretching in a fun way. Yoga wanted to learn how to do that too. Yoga went to the cat and asked, "What are you doing? Can I play too?" The cat replied, "I am doing yoga. It makes me feel good. You can try it too, Yoga!" So, Yoga tried to copy the cat. They moved and stretched together. Yoga found it hard at first, but soon, it became easier. As they were doing yoga, something unexpected happened. Yoga's tail started to wag very fast. The wagging tail began to count numbers! One, two, three, four, and so on. Yoga and the cat were amazed. They found out that Yoga's wagging tail could count numbers while doing yoga. Yoga and the cat became good friends and did yoga together every day. And every time they did, Yoga's tail would count the numbers for them.

Once upon a time, there was a frog. The frog lived near a pond. The frog liked to jump and play. One day, the frog saw a bird. The bird could fly high in the sky. The frog felt envious. The frog wanted to fly too. The frog said to the bird, "I wish I could fly like you." The bird said, "You can jump high. That is fun too." The frog thought about it. The frog liked to jump. The frog said, "You are right. Jumping is fun." The bird and the frog became friends. They played together every day. The bird would fly and the frog would jump. They were happy. The frog was not envious anymore. The frog learned that being a frog was fun too.

One day, a little seed was in the ground. It wanted to grow big and tall. It was very excited to see the sun and feel the rain. A fly named Buzz came by and saw the little seed. Buzz said, "Hello, seed! I can help you grow. I will bring you water and tell you a fun story." The seed was happy and said, "Thank you, Buzz! I can't wait to grow with your help." Buzz brought water to the seed every day. The seed started to grow. It grew and grew until it was a big, tall tree. But one day, when Buzz came to visit, the tree could talk! The tree said, "Buzz, thank you for helping me grow. I have a surprise for you!" The tree had a magic leaf that could make Buzz grow, too! Buzz was excited and touched the leaf. Buzz grew and grew until he was as big as the tree. Now, Buzz and the tree could play and talk together all day long. They were the best of friends.
