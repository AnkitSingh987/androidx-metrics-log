Category: Intermediate
Title: Ducky the Duck and the Pond

One day, a little duck named Ducky wanted to play in the pond. He loved to splash in the water. But, his friend Bunny did not like to get wet. Ducky wanted to find a way to play with Bunny. They tried to play near the pond. Ducky was stubborn and kept splashing water at Bunny. Bunny got mad and hopped away. Ducky felt sad that his friend was mad at him. Ducky thought of a new way to play. He found a dry spot near the pond and called Bunny over. They played on the dry spot and had fun. Ducky did not splash water at Bunny anymore. They were happy friends again.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly fish named Fin. Fin loved to swim all day in the big blue sea. He had many friends who liked to swim with him. They played fun games and had a great time. One day, while Fin and his friends were swimming, the water started to get very cold. Fin and his friends did not like the cold water. They swam faster to stay warm, but the water got colder and colder. Soon, the water was so cold that it started to freeze. Fin and his friends were scared. They swam as fast as they could to find warm water. After a long swim, they found a place where the water was warm and nice. Fin and his friends were happy again, and they continued to swim and play in their new warm home.

Once there was a little boy named Tom. He had a very curious mind and loved to explore the world around him. One day, Tom was walking around in the park when he saw something that made him curious. It was a strange sight in the distance. Tom wanted to take a closer look but he was scared of what he might find. He slowly crept up to the sight, but as he got closer he became embarrassed. It was his teacher! She was sitting in the park, all alone and very quiet. Tom panicked and quickly turned to run away. But he was too late! The teacher had already seen him. "Hello Tom", said his teacher. "What are you doing here?" Tom was so embarrassed that he couldn't answer. He just stood there in silence. "I think it's time for you to go home now, Tom" his teacher said. Tom walked slowly back home. He was sad and embarrassed. He had been caught doing something that he should not have been doing. From then on, Tom was very careful to stay away from that sight. He didn't want to be embarrassed ever again.

Tom is a big boy who likes to play with his toys. He has many cars, trains, and planes that he can zoom and crash. He also has a scale that he can use to measure the weight of his toys. He likes to see how heavy they are and compare them. One day, he meets a new friend at the park. Her name is Lily and she has a doll that she carries everywhere. She sees Tom playing with his scale and she is curious. "Hi, I am Lily. What are you doing?" she asks. "Hi, I am Tom. I am measuring the weight of my toys. Do you want to try?" he says. "Sure, that sounds fun. Can I measure my doll?" she says. "Okay, let me see. Your doll is very light. She weighs less than my car. See?" he says, showing her the numbers on the scale. "Wow, that is cool. Can I measure your plane?" she says. "Sure, here you go. My plane is very heavy. It weighs more than your doll and my car. See?" he says, showing her the numbers on the scale. "Wow, that is cool. Your plane is very serious. It can fly very fast and far. Can we play with it?" she says. "Okay, but be careful. It is my favorite toy. We can make it fly in the air and land on the grass. Do you want to try?" he says. "Yes, please. That sounds fun. Thank you for sharing your toys with me. You are a nice friend." she says. They play with the plane and the doll for a long time. They have fun and laugh. They are happy to meet each other and become friends.
