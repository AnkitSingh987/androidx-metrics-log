Category: Intermediate
Title: The Blue Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little blue bird. He was all alone, and he had nowhere to go. He fluttered around outside, looking for a place to settle down. Then, he spotted a cobweb in the corner of a tree. The cobweb looked so soft, it was just perfect for a little bird like him. He landed and started shaking the cobweb until it was nice and comfy. Just then, a voice called out from above. It was an old wise owl. "What are you doing?" asked the owl. "I'm shaking the cobweb, so it's nice and comfy!" exclaimed the little blue bird. The owl smiled and said, "Very nice work, little one. That's how you make a real home!" The little blue bird was so happy. He curled up in his new home and went to sleep, content and comfortable. And ever since then, the little blue bird has been living happily in his cobweb home.

Once upon a time, there was a big gym. In the gym, there were many kids. They all liked to play and have fun. One day, a new kid came to the gym. His name was Tim. Tim was a harmless boy who wanted to make friends. In the gym, there was a toy box. Inside the toy box, there was a sharp toy. The sharp toy was not safe for the kids to play with. Tim found the toy and showed it to the other kids. He said, "Look, I found a sharp toy. We should tell a grown-up." The other kids agreed with Tim. They said, "Yes, Tim. That is a good idea. Let's tell a grown-up about the sharp toy." So they went to find a grown-up. The grown-up took the sharp toy away and made the gym safe again. Tim and the other kids were happy. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends. They were called Lucy and Sam. Every day, Lucy and Sam would play together in their garden, with lots of fun. One day, Sam had an idea. He said, "Let's make a tent!" "Yes, great idea!" Lucy replied. The two friends started looking for things to make their tent. They found some sticks and a big green blanket. Then Lucy said, "We need an anchor to keep it strong." Sam told her, "I know just the thing. Here, I'll use this big rock to anchor our tent. Now our tent is super extra strong." The two friends laughed with joy, and they made the tent together. The tent was so strong that nothing could tear it down. Lucy and Sam were very happy. They stayed in the tent until it was time to go back home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a creative boy named Tim. Tim loved to draw and make things. He had a friend named Sally. Sally liked to play with Tim. They had lots of fun together. One day, they played a game called "Disappear Tuesday." On Tuesday, Tim and Sally played in the park. They ran and laughed. Then, Tim said, "Let's play Disappear Tuesday!" Sally asked, "What's that?" Tim said, "We close our eyes and count to ten. When we open our eyes, we try to find something that disappeared!" Sally closed her eyes and counted to ten. When she opened her eyes, she saw that Tim's hat had disappeared! "Tim, your hat is gone!" she said. Tim looked around and saw his hat on a dog! The dog had taken it while they were counting. They laughed and played with the dog. The dog was their new friend. And that's how Disappear Tuesday became their favorite game.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard was near the road. On a hot day, a man named Tom was walking by the billboard. Tom was very hot and tired. Tom saw a truck near the billboard. The truck was loading many things. Tom walked to the truck driver and said, "Hello! Can I help you load the truck?" The truck driver was happy and said, "Yes, please! Thank you!" Together, Tom and the truck driver loaded the truck. They worked and talked under the hot sun. After they finished, the truck driver gave Tom a cold drink. They sat under the billboard to rest. They were happy to be friends and help each other on a hot day.
