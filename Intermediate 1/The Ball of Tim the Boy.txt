Category: Intermediate
Title: The Ball of Tim the Boy

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toys and run in the park. One day, Tim saw a package by the door. He was very excited and wanted to open it. Tim asked his mom, "Can I open the package, please?" His mom said, "Not yet, Tim. We must wait for Dad to come home." Tim tried to resist, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. He played with his toys and waited for his dad to come home. Finally, Dad came home and Tim could open the package. It was a big, red ball! Tim was so happy. He played with the ball all day long. And that night, Tim went to bed with a big smile on his face, dreaming of all the fun he would have with his new ball.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little seed. The seed was sad because it was broken. It wanted to grow big and strong like the other plants around it. One day, a friendly bird came by and saw the broken seed. The bird said, "Don't be sad, little seed. I will help you grow." The bird picked up the seed with its beak and placed it in a nice spot in the ground. The bird then found some water and gave it to the seed. After a while, the seed started to grow. It grew a tiny green leaf, and then another one. Soon, it was a big, strong plant with lots of green leaves. The plant was so happy that it could grow, even with a broken ear. The bird came back and saw the plant. "You did it," said the bird. "I knew you could grow, even if you were broken." The plant and the bird became good friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to pick berries in the big forest near her home. One day, she found a very special berry. It was big, red, and mighty. Sue knew this berry was not like the others. Sue took the mighty berry home to her mom. Her mom told her that this berry had the power to restore things. They decided to try it on Sue's old, broken toy. Sue touched the berry to her toy, and it became new again! They were so happy. From that day on, Sue and her mom used the mighty berry to restore many things in their town. They helped their friends and neighbors. Everyone was so thankful for the magic of the mighty berry. And Sue learned that even the smallest things can have great power.

Lila and Sam are at the beach with Mom and Dad. They like to play in the sand and splash in the water. They see big waves coming and going. They hear the waves make a loud sound. "Look, Lila, the waves are saying hello!" Sam says. "Hello, waves!" Lila says. She waves her hand at the water. "Can you repeat what the waves say?" Sam asks. Lila listens to the waves. She hears them go "whoosh, whoosh, whoosh". "Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!" Lila repeats. "Good job, Lila! You sound like a wave!" Sam says. They laugh and clap their hands. Mom and Dad come to join them. They have a big towel and a picnic basket. "Are you ready to eat, kids?" Mom asks. "Yes, we are ready!" Lila and Sam say. They sit on the towel and open the basket. They see sandwiches, fruits, cookies, and juice. "Yummy, yummy, yummy!" Lila and Sam say. They eat and drink and enjoy the sun. They thank Mom and Dad for the food. "Can we play some more, please?" Lila and Sam ask. "Sure, you can play some more. But be careful of the waves, okay?" Mom says. "Okay, Mom. We will be careful." Lila and Sam say. They run back to the water and have more fun. They wave at the waves and repeat their sound. They are happy and silly. They love the beach.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little rabbit named Remy. Remy was a calm rabbit who liked to hop and play with his friends. One day, Remy found a horn while playing near a tree. He picked it up and blew it. It made a loud noise that scared all his friends. Remy's friends started to panic. They ran and hid behind the trees and bushes. Remy felt sad because he didn't want to scare his friends. He wanted to find a way to make them feel better and not be scared of the horn. Remy had an idea. He took the horn and hopped to each of his friends. He showed them the horn and how to blow it. When they saw that it was just a horn making the noise, they all felt better. They took turns blowing the horn and laughing. In the end, Remy and his friends played happily together with the horn, and they were not scared anymore.
