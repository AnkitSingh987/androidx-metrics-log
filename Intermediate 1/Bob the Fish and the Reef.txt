Category: Intermediate
Title: Bob the Fish and the Reef

One day, a fish named Bob lived in a noisy reef. The reef was his home, and he had many friends. They liked to play and swim all day in the bright blue water. Bob was a happy fish, and he loved his home. One morning, Bob woke up and felt a tickle in his nose. He tried to ignore it, but the tickle got bigger and bigger. Suddenly, Bob let out a big sneeze! All his friends heard the sneeze and came to see if he was okay. Bob's friends asked if he was sick, but Bob said he was fine. They all laughed and went back to playing in the noisy reef. They swam and played all day, and Bob did not sneeze again. They were happy fish in their beautiful home.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a long rake. He loved to use the rake to clean his yard. One day, while Tim was raking the leaves, he saw a big dog. The dog was not behaving well. It was running around and making a mess. Tim said to the dog, "You should behave and not make a mess." The dog looked at Tim and wagged its tail. Tim had an idea. He showed the dog how to use the rake. The dog watched and learned. Together, Tim and the dog cleaned the yard. People in the town saw Tim and the dog working together. They were very happy. The dog learned to behave, and Tim learned that helping others can make things better for everyone. The town was now clean, and everyone was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a dream. In his dream, he saw a big, tasty cake. Tim wanted to eat the cake, but he needed to find it first. Tim walked and walked until he met a talking cat. The cat said, "I know where the tasty cake is, but be careful not to burn it!" Tim and the cat went to a big oven. Inside the oven was the tasty cake. Tim was so happy! But then, something unexpected happened. The cat pushed a button and the oven got very hot. "Oh no!" said Tim, "The cake will burn!" Tim quickly opened the oven door and saved the cake. The cat said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to do that." Tim and the cat shared the tasty cake, and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat. The cat loved to sit by the window. Every day, the cat would watch the birds fly by. The cat wanted to play with the birds, but they were too high up in the sky. One day, a little bird came to the window. The cat asked the bird to play with him. The bird said, "No, I don't want to play with you. You are too fat." The cat felt sad, but he did not disagree. He knew that he was a fat cat. The next day, the cat decided to exercise. He ran and jumped and played. Soon, the cat was not so fat. The little bird came back to the window. This time, the bird wanted to play with the cat. They played together and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to paint. One day, he found a big, wild tree. The tree had many colors on it. Tim wanted to complete the paint on the tree. Tim tried to paint the tree, but a squirrel said, "No! This is my tree!" Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He wanted to make the wild tree more beautiful. Tim thought of a plan. He gave the squirrel some nuts. The squirrel was happy. Tim asked, "Can I paint the tree now?" The squirrel said, "Yes!" Tim completed the wild tree paint. They both loved the tree now.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy named Mr. Bunny. One day, Lily and Mr. Bunny went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a big spider making a web. The spider was harmless and just wanted to make a pretty network. Lily wanted to be friends with the spider. So, Lily said to Mr. Bunny, "Let's introduce ourselves to the spider." They went up to the spider and said hello. The spider was happy to meet them. Now, Lily, Mr. Bunny, and the spider were friends and played together at the park every day.
