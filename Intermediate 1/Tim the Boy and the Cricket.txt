Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Cricket

One day, a little boy named Tim found a cricket in his yard. He liked the cricket a lot. It was a good cricket. It was green and could jump very high. Tim wanted to play with the cricket all day. But Tim's big sister, Jane, did not like the cricket. She said it was too loud. She told Tim to let the cricket go. Tim was sad. He did not want to let his good cricket go. He wanted to keep playing with it. Tim had an idea. He told Jane that if she kneel down and look at the cricket, she might like it too. Jane did what Tim said. She kneel down and looked at the cricket. She saw how good it was. Jane and Tim played with the cricket together. They were both happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to follow his best friend, a little boy named Tim. They played together every day. One day, Tim got a big, new mattress. Spot felt envious, because he wanted a new mattress too. Spot said to Tim, "Can I sleep on your new mattress?" Tim said, "Of course, Spot! We can share it." That night, Spot and Tim slept on the big, new mattress together. They both felt happy and cozy. The next day, Spot and Tim played outside. They ran and jumped, and Spot followed Tim everywhere. When it was time to sleep, they went back to the big, new mattress. Spot was not envious anymore, because he knew he could always share with his best friend, Tim.

Sara and Ben were making pizza with Mom. They had dough, cheese, sauce and toppings. Mom showed them how to roll the dough with a big stick. Sara and Ben tried to copy Mom, but their dough was not smooth. It had holes and bumps and tears. "Listen, Sara and Ben," Mom said. "You have to be gentle with the dough. If you push too hard or too fast, it will break. You have to roll it slowly and evenly, like this." Mom rolled the dough again and made it smooth and round. Sara and Ben watched Mom and listened to her words. They tried to roll their dough gently and slowly. They were careful not to make holes or bumps or tears. Soon, their dough was smooth and round too. "Good job, Sara and Ben!" Mom said. "You listened and learned. Now you can put cheese and sauce and toppings on your pizza. What do you want on your pizza?" Sara and Ben smiled and said what they wanted. They had fun making their pizza with Mom. They learned that listening and being gentle can help them do things better. They also learned that pizza is yummy.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a big tree with a high branch. Lily wanted to climb the tree and sit on the high branch. She thought it would be fun. As she climbed, Lily saw a radio on the ground. She picked it up to examine it. The radio had a red button. Lily pressed the button and the radio started talking. It said, "Hello, Lily! I can help you find a hidden toy." Lily was surprised and happy. She followed the radio's voice to a bush. Under the bush was a toy box. Inside the box, Lily found a magic wand. She waved the wand and it made her fly. Now, Lily could sit on the high branch without climbing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big bag of rice. He wanted to know how heavy the bag was. So, he went to his mom and asked her to help him weigh the bag. His mom put the bag on a big scale. The scale showed that the bag was very heavy. One day, Tim saw an open box in his room. He thought it would be fun to pour the rice into the box. So, he opened the bag and poured all the rice into the open box. The rice filled the box up to the top. Tim was very happy to see the rice in the box. Tim's mom came into the room and saw the rice in the open box. She smiled and said, "Now we can cook the rice for dinner!" Tim and his mom cooked the rice together. They had a yummy dinner with the rice. Tim was very proud that he could help his mom with the rice. And they all lived happily ever after.
