Category: Intermediate
Title: The Princess and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a princess. She loved to play games. Her favorite game was match. She played match with her toys, her clothes, and her shoes. One day, the princess wanted to play a new game. She wanted to find hidden things. She asked her friends to play with her. They all said yes. They looked for hidden things in the castle. They found a hidden toy under a chair. They found a hidden shoe behind a door. They also found a hidden dress in a box. The princess and her friends had so much fun playing the hidden game. They played match with the hidden things they found. The princess was very happy.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little rabbit named Bobo loved to play. One day, Bobo found a big, purple stone. It was rough and shiny. Bobo liked the purple stone so much, he wanted to hide it so no one could take it. Bobo thought hard about where to hide the purple stone. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. "This is a good place to hide my stone!" Bobo said. So, he put the purple stone in the hole and went to play with his friends. As Bobo played, he forgot about the purple stone. The next day, it rained a lot. The big tree with the hole filled up with water. When the rain stopped, Bobo remembered his purple stone. He ran to the tree and saw that the hole was full of water. He looked inside and saw the purple stone was now smooth and shiny. The rain had washed away the rough parts. Bobo was so happy to have an even better purple stone now!

Tal was a three year old girl who had a very special box. It was a big, blue box with a golden lock. Tal loved to open it, especially on special occasions. One day, Tal set out to play in the park with her friends. She took the special box with her and put it in her bag. But while she was playing, her bag had an accident, and the box fell into the dirt and got dirty! "Oh no," Tal said, "My box is spoiled!" Suddenly, Tal spotted an old man nearby. He had a kind smile on his face, and he said to Tal, "Don't worry, I can help you." The old man gave Tal some wet cloths and told her to clean the box. With some hard work, very soon the box was clean. "There," the old man said, "Your box is no longer dirty!" Tal smiled and said, "Thank you very much!" The old man smiled and nodded, and Tal took her clean box and ran home. From then on, Tal was very careful to keep her special box clean.

Once upon a time, there was a popular fish named Fin. Fin had a big smile and a shiny scale. All the little fish liked to play with Fin. One day, Fin and his friends played in the sea. They swam and laughed all day. The sun went down, and it was time for the fish to go home. But, Fin's shiny scale was gone! Fin was sad and worried. He looked and looked for his shiny scale. Then, something unexpected happened. A little crab walked up to Fin and gave him a big hug. The crab said, "I found your shiny scale!" Fin was so happy, he smiled a big smile. The little crab was a new friend, and they all played together.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy dog named True. True liked to sleep all day and play all night. He had a best friend, a cat named Moon. They lived in a small house in a big green park. One sunny day, True and Moon wanted to play. True wanted to play catch, but Moon wanted to play hide and seek. They could not agree on what to play. They both said "no" to each other's ideas. They started to disagree. Then, a wise old owl saw them disagreeing. He told them to take turns playing each game. First, they played catch and had lots of fun. Then, they played hide and seek and laughed a lot. True and Moon learned that it is okay to disagree, as long as they find a way to play together. From that day on, they always took turns and were the best of friends.
