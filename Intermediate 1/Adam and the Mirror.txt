Category: Intermediate
Title: Adam and the Mirror

Adam was three years old. Every morning he woke up and wanted to have a healthy breakfast. He loved to pay attention to what he ate. He would often look in the mirror after he ate and feel good about himself. One morning, Adam was feeling extra healthy. He had a big breakfast of oatmeal and fresh fruit. After he was done eating, he went to go look in the mirror. He was so happy with what he saw. All of his hard work was paying off. He gave himself a big smile in the mirror. He felt so proud. He knew that if he kept eating healthy, he would be feeling great for a long time. He waved goodbye to his reflection and skipped down the stairs. It felt so good to know he had chosen a healthy breakfast!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She liked to look up at the sky and think about the stars. One day, Mia saw a big yellow ball in the sky. She thought it was a new planet! Mia told her friends about the yellow planet. They all wanted to go there. So, they built a big box with wings to fly up to the sky. Mia and her friends went inside the box and started to fly. They flew higher and higher until they reached the yellow planet. The planet was made of yummy yellow ice cream! Mia and her friends ate the ice cream and played all day. They had so much fun on the yellow planet.

Lily and Tom were playing in the park. They saw a big hill with a hole in it. "Look, a cave!" Tom said. "Let's go inside and see what is there." They ran to the hill and crawled into the hole. It was very dark and cold. They could not see anything. "I don't like this cave. It is too black," Lily said. "I want to go back." Tom tried to be brave. He said, "Maybe we can find something fun in the cave. Maybe there is a treasure or a dragon or a fairy. Let's keep going." They walked deeper into the cave. They heard a loud roar. It sounded like a monster. They were very scared. They turned around and ran back to the hole. But they could not find it. They were lost in the black cave. They started to cry. They hugged each other and said, "We are sorry. We should not have come to the cave. We want to go home." Then they saw a light. It was a flashlight. It was their dad. He had followed them to the cave. He heard their cries and came to help them. He said, "Don't worry, kids. I am here. I will take you out of the cave. You are safe now." Lily and Tom were happy to see their dad. They said, "Thank you, dad. You are our hero. You saved us from the monster." Dad smiled and said, "There is no monster in the cave. It was just an echo of your voices. But you were very brave to explore the cave. Next time, let me know where you are going. And don't go too far into the dark." They nodded and said, "OK, dad. We learned our lesson. We will not go to the black cave again. We will stay in the park and play with the flowers and the birds and the sun." They hugged their dad and walked out of the cave. They became happy again. They went home and told their mom about their adventure. Mom gave them a hug and a kiss and a cookie. They ate the cookie and went to bed. They dreamed of nice things. They did not dream of the black cave.

Once upon a time, there was a mommy and a baby. The baby was so cute and sweet, and the mommy loved her very much. Every day, the mommy would watch the baby playing and laugh. One day, the baby’s daddy was watching as the baby played. He loved to watch her too. He saw something and said to the mommy, “I think she’s going to do something good.” The mommy looked worried. She said, “What do you think she’s going to do?” But the daddy just smiled and said, “Wait and see.” The mommy was a little nervous. But finally, the baby did something good. She reached up and gave her daddy a hug! The daddy smiled. He looked at the mommy and said, “You see? I knew she would do something good.” The mommy smiled back. She was filled with love and watched her baby and daddy hug. It was a special moment that the mommy would never forget.
