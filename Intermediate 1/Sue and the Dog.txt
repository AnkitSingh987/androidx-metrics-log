Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pet dog named Max. They loved to play together in the mild sun. One day, they found a shiny copper coin on the ground. Sue was very happy. Sue wanted to raise the coin up high so everyone could see it. She put it on a big stick and raised it up. Max barked and jumped because he was happy too. They took the copper coin home and showed it to their mom. Their mom smiled and said, "Good job, Sue and Max!" They all had a fun day with the shiny copper coin.

Tom and Sam were brothers. They liked to travel with their mom and dad. They went to many places and saw many things. They had fun and learned a lot. One day, they went to a big city. There were many cars and people and noises. They stayed in a hotel with a pool. They wanted to swim, but mom said no. She said they had to go to the hospital. She said Tom needed an operation. Tom was scared. He did not want an operation. He did not know what it was. He asked Sam to help him. Sam said he had a plan. He said they could hide in the hotel and not go to the hospital. He said they could find a hidden place and wait until mom and dad left. They packed their bags and sneaked out of their room. They looked for a hidden place. They found a closet with a door. They went inside and closed the door. They thought they were safe. But they were not. The closet was not a closet. It was an elevator. It started to move. It went up and down and up and down. It stopped at different floors. The door opened and closed. People came in and out. They saw strange faces and heard strange words. They were scared and confused. They wanted to go back to their room. They wanted to see their mom and dad. They wanted to cry. But they could not. The elevator was broken. It did not stop at their floor. It did not listen to their buttons. It did not hear their screams. It kept moving and moving and moving. They were trapped. They did not know what to do. They wished they had gone to the hospital. They wished they had not hidden. They wished they had listened to their mom. They were sorry. But it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous factory. In the factory, there were many big machines. One day, a little girl named Amy went to the factory with her dad. Her dad worked at the factory. Amy saw her friend Tom at the factory. "Hi Tom!" she said. "Do you want to play?" Tom said yes, and they started to play hide and seek. Amy hid behind a big machine, and Tom tried to find her. But he could not see her. "I miss Amy," Tom said. Just then, the big machine made a funny noise. It was not a scary noise, but it was a surprise. Amy and Tom looked at the machine, and they saw a big, yummy cake coming out of it! They could not believe their eyes. They ran to the cake and started to eat it. The enormous factory was not just for big machines, but it also made cakes!

Once upon a time, there was a busy mom. She had a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to talk a lot. One day, Amy wanted to play with her mom. But her mom was very busy. Amy found her mom's bathrobe. She put it on and ran around the house. She pretended to be a superhero. She talked to her toys and told them she would save the day. But Amy was not careful. She tripped on the bathrobe and fell down the stairs. She was hurt and cried. The busy mom ran to help her. But it was too late. Amy's day of fun was over.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue. They loved to play together. One day, they found a big red ball in the park. They were very happy and wanted to play with it. Tim said, "Let's discuss how to play with the ball." Sue agreed, and they talked about how to play. They decided to play catch. They threw the ball to each other, but Tim was frustrated because he could not catch the ball. Sue tried to help him, but he was still frustrated. Then, a big dog came and took the ball away. Tim and Sue were sad because they could not play anymore. They went home with a bad ending to their day, hoping to find a new ball to play with tomorrow.
