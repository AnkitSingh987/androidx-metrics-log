Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Sun

One day, a little dog named Max went for a walk. He liked to run and play in the sun. Max was a happy dog, but he was also a little bit rude. Sometimes, he would take toys from other dogs without asking. While walking, Max saw a big tree. He hurried to the tree because he knew there was a treat hiding there. Max loved treats! He dug and dug until he found a yummy bone. Max was very happy with his treat. But then, another dog named Lucy came by. She looked sad. Max asked her what was wrong. Lucy said that the treat was hers. Max felt bad for being rude. He gave the treat back to Lucy, and they both played together. Max learned that sharing was more fun than being rude.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Tom and Lucy. Tom and Lucy did lots of fun things together, like playing games and talking. But one day, they wanted to try something different. "Let's do something new," said Tom. "Like what?" asked Lucy. "Let's make a plan," Tom said. So Tom and Lucy began to discuss. They thought of lots of ideas but nothing felt right. Suddenly, Tom had an idea. "Let's make a pen," he said. "But how?" asked Lucy. Tom grinned. He had a plan. He searched the room and found some hard rocks, some string and some bits of wood. They worked together to shape the rocks and wood into a pen. When the pen was complete, Tom and Lucy were so proud. They had done it! It had been hard, but together they had done it. The next day, Tom and Lucy were back to playing games and talking. But now, when they wanted to plan something together, they knew they could do it, even if it was hard. They knew they could make anything they wanted.

One day, a little girl named Daisy went for a walk. She felt very lucky because it was a sunny day. Daisy saw a pretty flower by the road. She wanted to save it and take it home. As Daisy reached for the flower, a big wind came. It blew the flower far away. Daisy was sad but she did not give up. She ran after the flower to save it. Daisy chased the flower until she found a big tree. The flower was in the tree! A nice bird saw Daisy and helped her. The bird picked up the flower and gave it to Daisy. Daisy felt lucky again. She saved the flower and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy sword that he loved to play with. But one day, his sword got broken. Tim was very sad. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, my sword is broken. Can you fix it, please?" His mom looked at the broken sword and said, "I will try, Tim." She took the broken sword and went to work. Mom tried to fix the sword, but it was not easy. Tim watched her and said, "Mom, can I help?" She smiled and said, "Yes, you can hold the sword while I fix it." Tim held the sword very carefully. Mom fixed the sword and gave it back to Tim. He was so happy! Tim played with his sword all day. He would swing it in the air and say, "I am a brave knight!" He would repeat this over and over again. From that day on, Tim took good care of his sword. He made sure it never got broken again. And every time he played with it, he remembered how his mom had helped him fix it.

Once upon a time, there was a red house. In the red house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with water. His favorite thing to do was to turn on the faucet and watch the water come out. One day, Tim's mom saw him playing with the faucet. She said, "Tim, you must quit playing with the faucet. You are making a big mess!" Tim looked at the water on the floor and knew his mom was right. Tim stopped playing with the faucet and helped his mom clean up the mess. He said, "I am sorry, mom. I will not play with the faucet again." His mom smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. Now we can go outside and play with your red ball instead." Tim was happy to play outside with his mom and his red ball. And he never played with the faucet again.
