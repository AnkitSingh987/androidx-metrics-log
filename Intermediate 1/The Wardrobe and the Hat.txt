Category: Intermediate
Title: The Wardrobe and the Hat

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a room. The wardrobe was full of pretty clothes. It was easy to open and close. One day, a little girl named Lily found the wardrobe. Lily loved playing dress-up. She put on a red dress and a blue hat. She was so happy. As Lily played, something magical happened. The wardrobe became a door to a fun land. Lily went inside and played with new friends. They had a great time. When it was time to go home, Lily said goodbye and went back through the wardrobe. She knew she could always come back to play again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved pizza more than anything. One day, he and his mom went to the pizza shop to get some pizza for dinner. When they got there, Tim saw that the pizza shop was very organized. All the toppings were in neat rows. Tim said, "Mom, I want to take that pizza with the cheese and the red things on it!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. That's a pepperoni pizza. Let's get it." After they got their pizza, Tim and his mom went home. They sat down and ate their pizza together. Tim was so happy, he said, "This is the best pizza ever, Mom!" His mom smiled and said, "I'm glad you like it, Tim. We can come back to the organized pizza shop again soon." And they lived happily ever after, eating pizza.

Once upon a time there was a small frog. He hopped around all day long but he was getting very tired. He was about to give up when he saw a tray on the ground. He hopped closer to take a look and found that it was full of tasty bugs. Excitedly he said to himself: "Wow! This looks delicious, but I'm too small to reach it". Just as he finished speaking another animal, a tall giraffe, stood next to him. The giraffe smiled and said:" Do not worry, I'll help you reach the tray". She bent her neck and picked up the tray. The frog had the best meal of his life. The moral of the story is that if you stay determined and ask for help if needed, you can overcome any challenge.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play in the snow. They make a big snowman and throw snowballs at each other. They laugh and have fun. "Let's go to the hill and slide!" Mia says. She grabs her sled and runs to the hill. Tom follows her with his sled. They put on their gloves and hats to keep warm. The hill is steep and slippery. Mia goes first. She sits on her sled and pushes off. She slides down the hill very fast. She feels the wind on her face and the snow under her sled. She smiles and shouts, "Whee!" Tom waits for Mia to reach the bottom. Then he sits on his sled and pushes off. He slides down the hill very fast too. But he is careless. He does not steer his sled well. He slides into a tree. He bumps his head and drops his glove. He cries, "Ow!" Mia hears Tom cry. She runs to him and helps him up. She sees his glove on the ground. She picks it up and gives it to him. She says, "Are you okay, Tom? I'm sorry you hit the tree. Here is your glove." Tom rubs his head and puts on his glove. He says, "Thank you, Mia. I'm okay. I was careless. I should have watched where I was going. Next time I will be more careful." Mia hugs Tom and says, "That's okay, Tom. We all make mistakes. I'm glad you're okay. Let's go back to the snowman and play some more." Tom nods and smiles. He says, "Okay, Mia. Let's go. You're a good friend." They hold hands and walk back to the snowman. They play in the snow until it is time to go home. They say goodbye and promise to play again soon. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sarah who lived in a big, big city. She had a big, red bag that her mom gave her. One day Sarah was walking through the city and met a small, cute dog. The dog was very, very hungry. Sarah smiled and opened up her bag. Inside was a yummy sandwich! The dog yipped with excitement and quickly gobbled up the sandwich. "Thank you," said the dog. "I'm feeling much better." Sarah smiled and said, "You're welcome!" They both carried on with their day and became the best of friends. The end.
