Category: Intermediate
Title: Ben and Sam and the Toys

Ben and Sam are brothers. They like to play with cars. They have many cars, but they have only one taxi. The taxi is yellow and black. It is their favorite car. One day, they want to play with the taxi at the same time. Ben says, "I want the taxi. It is mine." Sam says, "No, it is mine. I saw it first." They pull the taxi from each other. They pull very hard. The taxi breaks. It splits in two. Ben and Sam are sad and angry. They cry. Mom hears them cry. She comes to see what is wrong. She sees the broken taxi. She says, "Oh no, what happened?" Ben and Sam tell her what happened. Mom says, "You should not fight over toys. You should share and take turns. Fighting can hurt you and your toys. Look, now you have no taxi to play with." Ben and Sam feel sorry. They say, "We are sorry, Mom. We were bad. Can you fix the taxi?" Mom says, "I will try, but I don't know if I can. The taxi is very broken. Maybe you can find another toy to play with together." Mom goes to get some glue. She tries to fix the taxi. She puts the two parts together. She holds them for a long time. She hopes the glue will work. Ben and Sam wait and watch. They hope the taxi will be whole again. After a while, Mom lets go of the taxi. She says, "Look, I did it. The taxi is fixed. It is not perfect, but it is still a taxi. You are lucky. Next time, be careful and kind with your toys and each other." Ben and Sam are happy. They say, "Thank you, Mom. You are the best. We will share and take turns. We will not fight again." They hug Mom and the taxi. They play with the taxi together. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a funny bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to play near the chimney of a little house. Every day, he would fly and rise high up in the sky. One day, Bobo saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was playing outside the little house. She was laughing and having fun. Bobo wanted to play with Lily, so he flew down from the chimney. Bobo and Lily played together all day. They laughed and had a great time. When the sun went down, Bobo flew back to the chimney. He was happy to have a new friend. And every day, Bobo and Lily played together, making each other laugh and smile.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree with a small door. Tim was very curious. He wanted to see what was behind the door. He walked up to the door and tried to open it. But it was closed very tight. He pushed and pushed, but the door would not open. Then, a tiny bug came up to Tim. The bug said, "Hi! I can help you open the door, but I need a little help too. My food is on a high leaf, and I can't reach it. It is only an inch away, but I am too small." Tim agreed to help the bug. He picked the bug up and put it on the leaf with the food. The bug was very happy. In return, the bug helped Tim open the door. When the door opened, Tim saw a big room with a lot of ice cream. But the ice cream was bitter! Tim did not like bitter ice cream. He was sad. The bug felt bad and said, "I am sorry, Tim. I did not know the ice cream would be bitter. Let's go find some sweet ice cream together!" Tim smiled and they went on a new adventure to find sweet ice cream.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to play outside in the snow. One day, she found a journal on the ground. The journal was very pretty, with a picture of a snowman on the front. Sally took the journal inside her house. She wanted to write about her fun day in the snow. As she wrote, she thought about how she helped her mom make yummy cookies. The cookies were sweet and made her smile. The next day, Sally went outside to play again. She saw the snowman she made with her friends. The snowman was very big and had a big smile. Sally remembered how they all had to freeze when they played a game. They laughed and had so much fun. Sally was happy to have her journal to remember all her fun days.
