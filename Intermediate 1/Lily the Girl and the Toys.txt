Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy phone that she loved to play with. One day, her toy phone became broken. Lily was very sad because she could not play with her phone anymore. Lily's mommy saw her crying and asked her what was wrong. Lily told her mommy that her phone was broken. Her mommy said she would help fix it. They looked for things to fix the phone. They would include tape, glue, and a new button. Lily's mommy fixed the broken phone with the tape, glue, and new button. Lily was so happy to have her toy phone working again. She played with her phone all day and remembered to be careful with it. From that day on, Lily always took good care of her toys.

One day, in a big pretty forest, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She loved to look at the trees and flowers. She was very happy. Mia saw a big tree and thought it would be fun to climb. When she got to the top, she saw something far away. It looked like a big cat with a long tail. Mia was excited to see it. Mia climbed down the tree and ran to where she saw the big cat. As she got closer, she saw that it was a big lion! The lion looked sad. Mia wanted to help, so she shouted, "Hello, Mr. Lion! Why are you sad?" The lion looked at Mia and said, "I lost my pretty crown. I need it to be the king of the forest." Mia wanted to help the lion find his pretty crown. They looked everywhere in the forest. Then, they heard a shout from a tree. It was a little bird! The bird had the lion's pretty crown! The bird said, "I found this pretty crown on the ground. I wanted to wear it, but it is too big for me." The lion was happy to have his crown back, and Mia was happy to help her new friend. They all played together in the pretty forest.

One day, Mommy told Jack that he should try a lime. It looked small and green. Jack was scared. He said he didn't want to try it. Mommy said that she would take a small bite if he tried it. Jack agreed, but he was still nervous. He squirted lime juice in his mouth and it tasted sour. Jack spat it out and started to cry. He said he was too weak to eat it. Mommy said that was alright. He didn't have to eat the lime if he didn't want to. Jack felt better. He hugged Mommy and said she was the best. He had been scared, but Mommy had helped him overcome the fear. He felt happy and strong.

Once there was a little girl named Mia. She was three years old and very excited. She was always looking forward to something different and fun. One day while Mia was playing in the park she saw a paper on the ground. She picked it up and noticed it was a drawing. Mia was so excited to see the picture and took it home. When Mia got home she showed it to her mom. Her mom was so proud, but also a bit confused because it wasn't Mia's drawing. She asked Mia where she got it. Mia said, "I found it in the park. Can I keep it?" Her mom replied, "Of course you can keep it. I'm sure whoever drew it would be glad to share it with you." Mia was so happy and thanked her mom for forgiving the person who made the drawing. From then on she was so excited to go to the park and look for more special paper she could keep.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet dancer named Lily. She loved to twirl and jump all day long. Lily was very good at dancing and made everyone happy when they watched her. One day, Lily went to a big party. There were lots of people and yummy food. Lily wanted to help, so she decided to serve the food to the people. She put on a pretty apron and began to serve the snacks. As Lily served the food, she danced and twirled around the room. Everyone clapped and cheered for the sweet dancer. They loved watching her serve the food while dancing. And from that day on, Lily became known as the sweet dancer who served food with a smile.
