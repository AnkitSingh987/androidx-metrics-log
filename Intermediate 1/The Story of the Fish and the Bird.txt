Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Fish and the Bird

One day, a big fish with many scales swam in the water. The fish had a friend, a small crab. The crab was very reliable. They liked to play together. The fish said, "Let's play hide and seek. I will cover my eyes and you hide." The crab agreed and went to hide. The fish started to count, "One, two, three." The crab found a shell and hid inside. The fish finished counting and went to look for the crab. The fish looked everywhere but could not find the crab. The fish was sad, but then something unexpected happened. A bird flew down and said, "I saw where the crab is hiding. I will show you." The fish was happy and followed the bird. The bird showed the fish the shell where the crab was hiding. The fish found the crab and they both laughed. They played together all day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He wanted to discover new things. He saw birds, trees, and flowers. He was very happy. As he walked, he saw a big tiger. The tiger was clumsy. It tripped on a rock and fell. Tim was scared, but the tiger looked sad. Tim thought, "Maybe the tiger is nice." Tim said, "Hi, Mr. Tiger. Are you okay?" The tiger looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I am okay. I am just clumsy. Thank you for asking." Tim and the tiger became friends. They played together in the park all day.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park. He saw a dirty bench near the trees. He wanted to sit on it, but it was too dirty. Tom had an idea. He ran back to his house to get a cloth. Tom came back to the park with the cloth. He cleaned the dirty bench until it was nice and clean. Now, Tom could sit on the bench and look at the pretty trees. A girl named Sue came to the park. She saw Tom sitting on the clean bench. Sue said, "Wow, you made the bench clean! Thank you, Tom!" Tom smiled and said, "I value a clean bench. Now we can both sit and enjoy the park." Sue sat next to Tom, and they both had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat. Her name was Lily. She lived in a small house with a girl named Sue. Sue and Lily loved to play together. They played with a ball of yarn. The yarn was soft and blue. One day, Lily saw a big dog. The dog wanted to play with Lily and Sue. Lily did not know if she wanted to play with the dog. She looked at Sue and gave a little shrug. Sue said, "It's okay, Lily. The dog is nice." So, Lily, Sue, and the big dog played with the blue yarn. They had a lot of fun. The pretty cat, the girl, and the big dog became good friends.

Lily liked to help her mom with the mail. Every day, she would run to the mailbox and bring the letters and cards to the living room. She would sit on the big armchair and look at the pictures and words. Sometimes, she would find a letter for her, with her name on it. She felt very happy when that happened. One day, Lily saw a big envelope in the mail. It was for her mom, but it had a sticker with a bear on it. Lily loved bears. She wanted to see what was inside. She thought her mom would not mind if she opened it a little bit. She took the envelope to the armchair and tore a corner. She peeked inside and saw a book. A book with bears! Lily was so excited. She pulled the book out of the envelope and opened it. It was full of pictures and stories about bears. Lily could not read the words, but she made up her own stories. She imagined she was a bear, living in the forest, eating honey and berries, and playing with other bears. She forgot about the mail and the envelope. Her mom came home from work and saw the mail on the floor. She saw the torn envelope and the book on the armchair. She was not happy. She had ordered the book for her sister, who was sick and loved bears. She had wanted to surprise her with a nice gift. She was going to mail it to her today. Now, she had to find another envelope and write a new note. She called Lily and asked her what she had done. Lily looked at her mom and saw her angry face. She felt very sorry. She said she was sorry and that she liked the book and the bear sticker. She said she wanted to share it with her aunt. Her mom sighed and hugged her. She said she understood, but that she should not open other people's mail. She said she would mail the book to her aunt, but she would also buy another book for Lily. A book with bears. Lily smiled and said thank you. She helped her mom tidy up the mail and the envelope. She learned a lesson and got a gift. She was happy.
