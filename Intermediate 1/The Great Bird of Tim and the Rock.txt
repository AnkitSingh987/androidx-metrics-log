Category: Intermediate
Title: The Great Bird of Tim and the Rock

One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny copper coin on the ground. He was very happy. He wanted to show it to his best friend, Sam. So, he put the coin in his pocket and ran to Sam's house. When Tim got to Sam's house, Sam was very sleepy. Tim said, "Wake up, Sam! I have something to show you!" Sam rubbed his eyes and sat up. Tim showed Sam the copper coin. Sam said, "Wow, it's so shiny! Let's play with it outside." As they were playing, a big wind came and blew the copper coin away. Tim and Sam were very sad. They looked and looked, but they could not find the coin. Then, a little bird flew down and said, "I saw where the wind took your coin. It's under the big rock." Tim and Sam went to the big rock and saw the coin was covered with leaves. They moved the leaves and picked up the coin. Tim and Sam were very happy. They thanked the little bird and played with the coin all day. From that day on, they always put their special things in a safe place, so the wind could not take them away again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a harmless pet cat named Fluffy. They lived in a small house with a big yard. Tim and Fluffy liked to play outside together. One day, Tim and Fluffy were playing on one side of the yard. Tim threw a ball and said, "Go get it, Fluffy!" Fluffy ran after the ball and brought it back to Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Good job, Fluffy!" They played this game many times, and Tim would always repeat, "Go get it, Fluffy!" As the sun started to set, Tim's mom called from the other side of the yard, "Tim, it's time to come inside!" Tim said, "Okay, Mom!" He picked up Fluffy and walked back to the house. They had a fun day playing together and couldn't wait to do it again tomorrow.

One day, a pretty bird named Lily was flying in the sky. She was very happy. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Lily liked to sing pretty songs. Lily saw a big tree and decided to rest. She saw a squirrel on the tree. The squirrel said, "Hi, I am Sam. I have been living in this tree for one year." Lily said, "Hi Sam, I am Lily. I like your tree." Sam and Lily became friends. They played and talked. But soon, it was time for Lily to go home. She did not want to leave Sam. Lily tried to resist, but she knew she had to go. Sam said, "Don't worry, Lily. You can come back and play with me again." Lily smiled and flew away, knowing she would see her friend Sam again soon.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to bounce on her bed. She would jump up and down, up and down, all day long. One day, while she was bouncing, she saw a poor, sad cat outside her window. The cat was cold and hungry. Lily went outside and said, "Hello, poor cat! Do you want to come inside and watch television with me?" The cat meowed and followed Lily into the warm house. They sat on the soft couch and watched their favorite show on the television. As they watched, Lily had an idea. She said, "Cat, do you want to bounce with me?" The cat meowed again, and they both went to Lily's bedroom. They bounced on the bed together, laughing and having fun. The poor cat was not sad anymore, and Lily had a new friend to bounce with.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Tim. Tim was a very incredible penguin. He could dance, sing, and even paint. All the other penguins liked to watch Tim do his amazing things. One day, Tim saw a big, shiny fish. He wanted to catch the fish for his dinner. But the fish was very fast. Tim tried and tried, but he could not catch the fish. He felt sad and regret that he could not catch the fish. Then, Tim's friend, a little bird named Sue, came to help him. Sue was very smart. She had a plan to catch the fish. Sue told Tim to dance and sing, while she went to get the fish. Tim danced and sang, and the fish stopped to watch. Sue quickly caught the fish, and they both had a big, yummy dinner. Tim was very happy and thanked Sue for her help. They were both incredible friends.
