Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom and Mia and the Heart

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play and joke with each other. One day, they find a small red heart on the ground. It is made of paper. "Look, Mia, I have a heart for you!" Tom says, and gives the heart to Mia. "Thank you, Tom, you are very nice!" Mia says, and smiles. But then, Tom takes the heart back and runs away. "Hey, that is not fair! Give me back my heart!" Mia says, and chases Tom. Tom laughs and says, "I was only joking, Mia! It is not a real heart, it is just paper!" Mia catches up with Tom and grabs the heart. She tears it in half and says, "Now you have no heart, Tom! How do you like that?" Tom stops laughing and looks sad. He says, "I am sorry, Mia, I did not mean to make you angry. I just wanted to have fun with you. Can we still be friends?" Mia feels sorry for Tom. She says, "Yes, Tom, we can still be friends. But you should not joke like that. It is not nice to take things from people. Here, you can have half of the heart. I have the other half. We can share it." Tom and Mia hug and make up. They put the two halves of the heart together and say, "We have a big heart, because we are friends!"

Once there was a girl named Lily. She had a pretty dress. The dress was modest. Lily loved to play with her toy car. The toy car could zoom very fast. One day, Lily put her dress on her toy car. She wanted to see if the car could still zoom. The car started to zoom with the dress. It went faster and faster. Then, something unexpected happened. The car zoomed so fast, it flew into the sky! Lily was amazed. The car and the dress flew around like a bird. Lily laughed and clapped. The car and the dress came back down. The car was still zooming fast. Lily was very happy with her magical dress and car.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a big box of fake toy cars. He loved to play with them all day. One day, Tim decided to add more cars to his box. He asked his mom for help. She said, "Let's go to the store and find some new cars to add to your box." Tim was very happy and excited to start. At the store, Tim and his mom looked at many toy cars. Tim picked up a shiny red car and a big blue truck to add to his box. He couldn't wait to start playing with them. They bought the cars and went back home. When Tim opened his box to add the new cars, he saw something unexpected. All his fake toy cars turned into real, tiny cars! Tim and his mom were so surprised. They laughed and played with the real cars all day. From that day on, Tim's toy box was full of magic and fun.

One day, a little boy named Jim was sitting in his room. He was very curious about the world around him. He saw a strange doll in the corner and decided to examine it. Jim took a closer look at the doll. Its eyes sparkled and Jim could not resist the temptation to touch it. He gently put his hand over the doll and asked it, "Are you alive?" Suddenly he felt the doll moving beneath his fingers. He was so surprised and jumped back in shock. He quickly recovered and asked the doll, "Can you talk?" The doll stared back at him without answering. Jim thought this all was very strange, but he didn't give up, and spoke to the doll once again. He asked, "Where did you come from?" He waited for a response, but none came. The doll's mysterious behaviour made Jim even more curious. He looked around and wondered what more he could discover in this room. His adventure was just about to begin.

Once upon a time, a cute little dog named Max lived in a small house. Max loved to play on the soft carpet with his toys. One day, his friend, a cat named Lily, came to visit him. "Max, let's play on the carpet!" said Lily. Max and Lily played and had a lot of fun. They laughed and chased each other around the house. Suddenly, they heard a noise outside. They looked out the window and saw a big truck arrive. The truck had a big box in the back. Max and Lily were very curious about what was inside the box. The box was brought inside the house and opened. To their surprise, it was a new carpet! Max and Lily were so excited to play on the new carpet. They didn't know that the carpet was magic. As they played on the magic carpet, it started to move! Max and Lily were amazed. They held on tight and the carpet took them on a fun adventure. They laughed and played as they flew through the sky, and they had the best day ever.
