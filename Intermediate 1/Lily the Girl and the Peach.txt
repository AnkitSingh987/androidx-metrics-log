Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Peach

One day, a little girl named Lily found a big, smelly peach. She wanted to eat it, but it was too big for her to hold. Lily tried to pick it up, but it was very hard. She felt sad because she really wanted to eat the peach. Lily asked her friend, a small dog named Max, to help her. Max tried to bite the peach, but it was too smelly. Max did not want to eat the peach anymore. Lily was sad again because she still wanted to eat the peach. Then, Lily had an idea. She asked her mom to cut the peach into small pieces. Her mom did it, and the peach was not smelly anymore. Lily and Max ate the peach together, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two friends – Sam and Mike. Sam needed a way to get to his house, but there was no path. Mike saw how sad Sam was, so he decided to help. He asked Sam to close his eyes. When Sam opened them, he saw a big, red path in front of him! Sam was so happy! He quickly ran down the path and found his way home. The moral of the story is that you should help your friends in need, even if it takes a little effort. Sometimes with a little patience and kindness, we can find the right way to do things!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a crazy love for shoes. He had red shoes, blue shoes, and even yellow shoes. But he had no shoes with toes. Tim knew that if he could find shoes with toes, he would be so happy. One day, Tim went to the store with his mom. He saw a pair of shoes that had toes! Tim was so excited that he asked his mom if he could spend his money on them. His mom said yes, so Tim bought the shoes with his own money. The next day, Tim wore his new toe shoes to the park. He ran and played all day long. He felt so happy and proud of his new shoes. And just as he had thought, the shoes with toes made him the happiest boy in the world.

Lily liked to watch cartoons on TV. She liked the funny animals and the songs. She did not like to share the TV with her brother Ben. Ben liked to watch other shows. He liked the cars and the robots. One day, Lily and Ben were home with their mom. Mom said they could watch TV for a little while, but they had to take turns. Lily went first. She chose her favorite cartoon. She laughed and clapped. Ben waited patiently. He played with his toys. When Lily's cartoon was over, Ben said, "My turn now, Lily. Please give me the remote." Lily did not want to give him the remote. She wanted to watch another cartoon. She said, "No, Ben. I want to watch more cartoons. Go away!" She held the remote tightly. Ben felt sad and angry. He said, "That's not fair, Lily. You had your turn. You have to share. Mom said so." Lily did not listen. She said, "Shut up, Ben. I don't care what mom said. I don't care what you want. I only care about me and my cartoons." She turned up the volume. Mom heard the noise. She came to the living room. She saw Lily and Ben fighting over the remote. She saw Lily being rude and selfish. She saw Ben being humble and polite. She was very upset. She said, "Lily, how could you be so mean to your brother? You should be ashamed of yourself. You have to learn to share and be kind. You are not the only one who likes to watch TV. You have to respect others. Give me the remote right now. You are in big trouble, young lady. You are not watching any more cartoons today. You are going to your room. And you are going to apologize to Ben." Lily realized she had made a big mistake. She felt sorry and scared. She cried and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I'm sorry, Ben. I was wrong. I was selfish. Please forgive me. Please let me watch cartoons." But it was too late. Mom took the remote and turned off the TV. She took Lily by the hand and led her to her room. She said, "No, Lily. You have to face the consequences of your actions. You have to learn your lesson. Maybe next time you will be more humble and generous. Maybe next time you will think of others before yourself." The moral of the story is: Sharing is caring. Being humble is better than being selfish. Respect others and they will respect you.
