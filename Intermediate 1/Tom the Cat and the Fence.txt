Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Cat and the Fence

One day, a cat named Tom was walking on a fence. He was very good at balance. Tom liked to walk on the fence every day. The fence was long and fun to walk on. Tom was a happy cat. One day, while Tom was walking on the fence, he saw a big bird. The bird was big and blue. Tom wanted to say hello to the bird. But the bird was on the other side of the fence. So, Tom kept walking on the fence to get closer. As Tom got closer, the big bird started to change. It was not a bird! It was a big blue balloon! The wind was making the balloon move like a bird. Tom was surprised but happy. He played with the big blue balloon and had a good day.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a mysterious box in her room. She did not know where it came from. It was a mystery. She wanted to know what was inside the box. She tried to open it, but it was shut very tight. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can you help me open this box?" Her mom said, "Let's try together." They tried to open the box, but it was still shut. They tried and tried, but it would not open. Then, Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's ask dad for help." So they went to dad and showed him the mysterious box. Dad looked at the box and smiled. He said, "I know how to open it." He pushed a secret button on the box, and it opened! Inside the box, there were lots of fun toys and treats for Lily. She was so happy and surprised. The mystery of the box was solved, and Lily had a great day playing with her new toys.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly frog named Fred. He lived in a swamp with his friends. They liked to play and jump all day long. One day, a new animal came to the swamp. It was big and green. Fred and his friends did not recognize it. "Hello," said the big green animal. "I am Sam. I am new here. Can I play with you?" Fred and his friends were scared. They thought Sam might be mean. But Sam just wanted to play. So, they let him join in their games. They played hide and seek, and Sam was very good at hiding. Then, something unexpected happened. Sam started to change. He became smaller and smaller. Soon, Sam was not big and green anymore. He was a little green frog, just like Fred and his friends. They all laughed and jumped together. Now, they were all jolly frogs in the swamp, and they played together every day.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big, wide potato in his yard. He was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim ran to Sue's house and knocked on her door. Sue came out and Tim said, "Look at my wide potato!" Sue looked at the potato and then she pointed to a big hole in the ground. "I found this hole in my yard," she said. Tim and Sue were very curious about the hole and the wide potato. They decided to put the potato in the hole and see what would happen. As soon as they put the potato in the hole, something unexpected happened. The wide potato started to grow and grow until it became a huge potato tree! Tim and Sue were very surprised and happy. They climbed the tree and found more wide potatoes. They picked them and shared them with everyone in their town. And that's how Tim and Sue became the best potato tree friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, round loop near the slide. Tim wanted to play with the loop, but it was too high for him to reach. He asked his mom, "Can you help me get the loop, please?" His mom said, "Of course!" She lifted Tim up, and he grabbed the loop. It was a loud, shiny loop. Tim loved the sound it made when he hit it with his hand. He decided to send the loop down the slide. Tim placed the loop at the top of the slide and let go. The loop went down the slide very fast. When it reached the bottom, it made a loud noise. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He said, "Again, again!" His mom smiled and helped him send the loop down the slide many more times. They had a lot of fun at the park that day. Tim couldn't wait to come back and play with the loud, shiny loop again.
