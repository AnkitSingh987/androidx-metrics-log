Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Squirrel

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a very clear thinker. He always knew what he wanted. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big slide and swings. Tim liked to play on the swings. While Tim was swinging, a kind squirrel came up to him. The squirrel said, "I recommend you try the big slide. It's lots of fun!" Tim was surprised to hear the squirrel talk. But he thought it was a good idea. So, he went to the big slide. Tim climbed up and got ready to slide down. But as he started to slide, something unexpected happened. The slide turned into a big, soft pillow! Tim laughed and laughed as he bounced on the pillow. The squirrel had played a fun trick on him. From that day, Tim and the squirrel became best friends, and they played at the park together all the time.

Tom and Lily were playing in the yard. They liked to pretend they were pirates and sail in a big barrel. They used sticks for swords and hats for helmets. One day, a big truck came to their house. It had many boxes and bags on it. The truck driver got out and said, "Hello, kids. I have a delivery for you. Do you want to help me?" Tom and Lily ran to the truck. They wanted to see what was inside the boxes and bags. Maybe there were toys or books or candy. They helped the truck driver carry the boxes and bags to the porch. When they were done, the truck driver said, "Thank you, kids. You are very helpful. Do you want a reward?" Tom and Lily nodded. They hoped the reward was something fun. The truck driver smiled and said, "Well, I have something different for you. How about this?" He pointed to the back of the truck. There was a big barrel with a lid on it. Tom and Lily gasped. They ran to the barrel and opened the lid. Inside, there was a small puppy. It was brown and white and had a red collar. It wagged its tail and licked their faces. Tom and Lily hugged the puppy. They were so happy. They thanked the truck driver and named the puppy Spot. They decided to make him their pirate captain. They put him in the barrel and sailed away. They had a new friend and a new adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a big frog. The big frog lived by a river. The river was very pretty. The big frog liked to jump and play by the river. One day, the big frog saw a box by the river. The box had a bow on it. The big frog was happy to receive a gift. He opened the box and found a ball inside. The big frog played with the ball by the river. He had a lot of fun. The big frog was very happy. He loved his new ball and the pretty river.

Once upon a time, in a big land, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lot of toys. One day, Tim was playing with his toys in his room. He had so much fun, he did not see that his toys were all over the floor. His toys were scatter everywhere. Then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw the scatter toys and said, "Tim, you need to clean up your toys." Tim felt sorry, but he did not want to clean up. He wanted to keep playing. So, he said, "Okay, Mom," but he did not clean up. Later, Tim's dad came home. He walked into Tim's room and saw the scatter toys. But, something unexpected happened. Tim's dad did not get mad. Instead, he started to play with the toys too! Tim and his dad had a lot of fun playing together. They laughed and played until it was time for bed. At bedtime, Tim said, "I'm sorry, Dad. I should have cleaned up my toys." Tim's dad smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We had fun. But tomorrow, we will clean up together." Tim was happy that his dad was not mad and they hugged. The next day, Tim and his dad cleaned up the scatter toys. They put them away in their right places. From that day on, Tim always cleaned up his toys after playing. And sometimes, his dad would still come in to play with him.
