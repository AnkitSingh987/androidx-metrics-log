Category: Intermediate
Title: The Taxi and the Driver

Once upon a time, there was a taxi. The taxi was yellow and big. The taxi driver was a little foolish. He liked to balance on one foot while he waited for people to get in his taxi. One day, a little girl and her mom needed a ride. They saw the taxi driver balancing on one foot. The little girl said, "Mommy, look at the man. He is funny!" Her mom said, "Yes, dear. He is a little foolish, but he can take us home." The taxi driver took them home. He drove very well, even though he was a little foolish. The little girl and her mom were happy. They said, "Thank you, taxi driver!" The taxi driver smiled and said, "You're welcome. Have a nice day!" Then, he went back to balancing on one foot, waiting for more people to get in his taxi.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to nap under a big tree. One day, while Spot was napping, he had a dream. In his dream, he saw an image of a big bone. Spot woke up and said to his friend, Cat, "I saw a big bone in my dream! I want to find it!" Cat said, "Let's go look for it together!" So, Spot and Cat went on a fun adventure to find the big bone. As they walked, Spot stepped on a rock and his paw felt hurt. "Ouch!" cried Spot. Cat helped Spot by finding a soft leaf to put on his paw. Soon, Spot felt better and they continued their search. In the end, they found the big bone and shared it as a special treat.

One day, a girl named Lily went to play outside. She saw a big ball and wanted to catch it. She ran and ran, but the ball was very fast. Lily felt bad because she could not catch the ball. Then, Lily saw a boy named Tim. He was sad too. His hair was stuck in a tree. Lily wanted to help Tim. She tried to pull his hair out, but it was too hard. Tim felt bad because his hair was still stuck. Lily had an idea. She asked Tim to jump and catch the ball. Tim jumped high and caught the ball. The ball hit the tree and Tim's hair came out. They both felt happy. They played with the ball and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to make things with his hands. One day, he decided to make a museum in his room. He wanted everyone to see his beautiful things. Tim's best friend, Sue, came to see his museum. She saw all the pretty things Tim made. Sue felt jealous because she wanted to make pretty things too. She asked Tim if he could teach her how to make them. Tim was happy to help his friend. They spent the day making things for Sue's museum. Soon, Sue had a beautiful museum too. Tim and Sue were happy to have their own museums and they were not jealous anymore. They played together and shared their pretty things with each other.

One day, a loyal dog named Spot saw a big plane in the sky. Spot was very happy and wanted to see the plane up close. He asked his friend, a little bird named Tweet, "Can you take me to the plane?" Tweet said, "Yes, come with me!" So, they went on a long trip to find the plane. They walked and walked until they saw the big plane on the ground. Spot and Tweet were so excited to see it. But when they got close to the plane, they saw that it was not a real plane. It was a big cake that looked like a plane! Spot and Tweet were very surprised. Spot took a bite of the cake and said, "Mmm, it tastes so good!" They both laughed and enjoyed the tasty plane cake together.

Once there was a little girl named Anna who wanted to explore the world. One day, she decided to take a walk. She put on her shoes and started towards the sunny park filled with trees and flowers. As she walked, Anna noticed a small pond with many birds swimming and chirping. She excitedly walked closer while being careful so that she didn't slip on the wet grass. Suddenly, she heard a voice asking her to stop. It was her mom. She said, "Anna, be careful. You can see the birds, but don't go too close to the pond." Anna smiled and nodded. She walked away holding her mom's hand. She liked exploring the world, but she was happy to know that her mom was watching her with care.
