Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lady and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was an old lady. She was very clean and tidy. She liked to observe her neighborhood. One day, she was observing some children playing in the street. Suddenly, one of them lost their ball. The old lady saw where it went and smiled. She picked up the ball and held it in front of the children. The children ran to her, begging her to give them the ball back. But the old lady was very stubborn. She refused to give the ball back. She said that she wanted something in return. The children were sad and confused. Nobody had anything to give her. Then, the old lady told them that if they worked hard, saved their money and were very clean in the future, then she might give them the ball. The children were very sad and they knew they would never get the ball back. They learned an important lesson that day. It's important to be kind and generous to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red boot. It lived in a small house with a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with the boot. They would run, jump, and laugh together. One day, Tim and the boot went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a tree. Tim wanted to climb the tree, but he was too small. The boot had an idea. The boot leaned against the tree, and Tim climbed on top of the boot. Tim was so happy that he could reach the tree branches. But then, Tim got stuck in the tree. He felt frustrated and scared. The boot wanted to help Tim. It leaned against the tree again, and Tim held onto the boot. Together, they slid down the tree and landed safely on the ground. Tim hugged the boot and said, "Thank you, boot! You are my best friend!"

Once upon a time, in a big country, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very anxious. She wanted to succeed in making the best cake for her mom's birthday. Lucy asked her friend, Tom, to help her. "Tom, can you help me make a cake for my mom?" Lucy asked. Tom said, "Yes, Lucy, I will help you. Let's make the cake together." They worked hard and mixed all the things they needed for the cake. They put the cake in the oven and waited. When the cake was done, they took it out and put pretty things on it. Lucy and Tom were happy. They succeeded in making the best cake for Lucy's mom.

Once there was a lonely otter. He lived in a big lake. He had no friends. One day, he saw a light in the water. The light was glowing. The otter was curious and swam closer to the light. As he got close, he saw a small fish. The fish said, "Hello, I am a glowing fish. Why are you lonely?" The otter replied, "I have no friends." The fish said, "I will be your friend if you help me." The otter helped the fish get some food. They became good friends. The otter was not lonely anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The fish started to glow brighter and brighter. The otter asked, "Why are you glowing so bright?" The fish said, "I am a magic fish. When I am happy, I glow bright. Thank you for being my friend and helping me." The otter was happy too. They played together every day. The moral of the story is: be kind and help others, and you will find friends.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a thick tree with a big branch. Tim thought it would be fun to climb the tree. So, he began to climb up the thick tree. As Tim climbed higher, he found a screw on the branch. He picked it up and put it in his pocket. He continued to climb and play on the tree. Then, he heard a voice from below. It was his friend, Sam. "Hey, Tim! Can I play with you?" Sam asked. Tim said, "Yes, come up!" Sam started to climb the tree, but he was scared. He didn't want to climb too high. Tim wanted Sam to play with him on the big branch, but Sam was too scared. Tim thought about what to do. He remembered the screw in his pocket. He showed it to Sam and said, "Look, I found a screw! Let's play with it on the ground." Sam was happy and climbed down the tree. Tim and Sam played with the screw on the ground. They pretended it was a magic key that could open any door. They had lots of fun playing together, and they forgot about the big, thick tree.
