Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Green Frog and the Pond

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Tim. He lived in a small pond with his family. Tim was a very happy frog who loved to jump and play with his friends. One day, Tim had to take an exam. It was a test to see how high he could jump. All the frogs in the pond had to take the exam. Tim was a little scared, but he knew he could do it. Tim went to the pond and waited for his turn. When it was time, he took a big breath and jumped as high as he could. He struck his legs out and stretched them far. Tim jumped so high that everyone in the pond clapped and cheered. He passed the exam and felt very proud. And from that day on, Tim was known as the best jumper in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty lion named Leo. Leo was very big and strong. He lived in a hot place with lots of trees and grass. Leo liked to play with his friends, the little birds and the cute monkeys. One day, Leo found a small box. The box had a button on it. When he pushed the button, the box could record sounds. Leo thought it was fun. He went around the hot place and recorded the sounds of his friends. Leo recorded the birds singing, the monkeys chattering, and even the wind blowing through the trees. He was very happy with his new toy. At the end of the day, Leo and his friends sat together and listened to the sounds they had recorded. They all laughed and had a great time. From that day on, the mighty lion and his friends loved to play with the little box in their hot home.

Once upon a time, there was a messy dog named Max. Max loved to play in the mud. One day, Max played in the mud all day long. He was very happy, but his ears were very messy too. Max's friend, the cat, saw Max and said, "Max, your ears are so messy!" Max looked at his ears and knew he needed to be clean. So, Max went to the pond to wash his ears. Max put his ears in the water and splashed around. The water made Max's ears clean and not messy anymore. Max was happy to have clean ears again. From that day on, Max always made sure to clean his ears after playing in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She was very excited today, because her mom had promised to take her to the park! When they arrived, Jane saw a big playground with lots of exciting things: a wide slide, a swing, and a big, colorful rocking horse. Jane immediately ran over to it and started to rock, up and down, up and down. She was having so much fun! But after awhile, the rocking horse began to feel a bit boring. That's when Jane's mom said, "Hey, what do you say we go to the other side of the park? I hear there's a wide pond there, and that sounds like fun!" So off they went, and Jane soon forgot all about the rocking horse. She watched the ducks swim in the wide pond, and chased the butterflies in the grass. It was so much more fun than the rocking horse! Before she knew it, it was time to go home. Jane was sad to leave, but she was happy that she got to go to the park and explore. She knew she'd come back again soon!

Once upon a time, there was a red ball. It lived in a park with a big tree. One day, a girl named Lily came to play. She had a red glove. She picked up the red ball with her red glove. Lily said, "Hi, red ball! Do you want to play with me?" The red ball was happy. It wanted to play with Lily. So, they played catch together. They had lots of fun. Then, Lily had to pause. She saw her mom waving at her. "Lily, it's time to go home!" her mom said. Lily put her red glove on the red ball. She said, "Bye, red ball. I will come play with you again soon." The red ball was happy and waited for Lily to come back.
