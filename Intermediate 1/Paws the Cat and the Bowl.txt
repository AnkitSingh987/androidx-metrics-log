Category: Intermediate
Title: Paws the Cat and the Bowl

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Paws. Paws loved to eat pasta. Every day, he would ask his mom for pasta. "Mom, can I have pasta?" Paws would say. His mom would smile and say, "Yes, Paws, you can have pasta." One day, Paws was very tired. He had played with his friends all day long. He wanted to sleep. So, he ate his pasta and went to his bed. Paws had a happy dream. In his dream, he was eating a big bowl of pasta. He smiled in his sleep. When Paws woke up, he felt good. He was ready to play with his friends again. And, of course, he was ready to eat more pasta.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly bird with big wings. The bird loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. He was always happy and liked to sing songs. One day, the jolly bird saw a little girl who was sad. She had lost her toy and could not find it. The bird wanted to help, so he went to the girl and asked, "Can I help you find your toy?" The girl was happy and said, "Yes, please!" The jolly bird used his wings to fly all around, looking for the toy. He asked his friends, the other animals, if they had seen the toy. Finally, he found it in a tree. The bird brought the toy back to the girl, and she was very happy. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a lovely little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to settle down and play with her toys every day. She had a best friend named Tim. They played together all year. One day, Lily and Tim had a big fight. "I don't want to play with you anymore!" said Lily. Tim felt sad and went away. Lily was all alone with her toys. As the days went by, Lily missed Tim a lot. She wanted to say sorry, but Tim had moved to another town. Lily never saw Tim again. Now, Lily had to play all by herself, and she was very sad.

One day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went to the fair. They saw a big mixer. It was mixing colors. Tom was very happy. He wanted to print a picture with the colors. Tom asked the man at the mixer, "Can I print a picture?" The man said, "Yes, but you need to wait for the colors to mix." Tom and Spot waited and watched the mixer go round and round. They knew something fun would happen soon. Finally, the colors were mixed. Tom printed a picture of Spot. It was very pretty with all the colors. Tom and Spot went home and showed the picture to their mom. She loved it, and they all knew that the fair was a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Sally was playing outside. She was running and jumping when she fell down. Her knee was hurt, and she began to cry. Sally's mom heard her cry and came to help her. Sally's mom looked at her hurt knee and said, "We need to clean it and put some medicine on it." Sally was scared because she thought the medicine would hurt. But her mom told her, "Don't worry, it won't hurt. It will help your knee feel better." Sally's mom put the medicine on her knee, and it didn't hurt at all. Soon, Sally's knee felt better, and she was happy. She could play outside again without any pain. Sally was very thankful for the medicine and her mom's help.

One day, a strong sun was shining in the sky. It was very hot. In a park, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a big ice cream cone. The ice cream started to melt because of the sun. Tim saw his friend Sue. She was playing with a toy. Sue looked at Tim's ice cream and said, "Oh no, your ice cream is melting!" Tim was in shock. He did not want his ice cream to go away. Tim and Sue thought of an idea. They went under a big tree. The tree gave them shade. Tim's ice cream stopped melting. They were happy and played together.
