Category: Intermediate
Title: Lisa and Ben and the Toys

Lisa and Ben are twins. They like to play with their toys in their room. They have many toys: dolls, cars, blocks, books, and more. They think they have the best toys in the world. One day, their mom says they have to go to the store with her. She says they need to buy some food and clothes. Lisa and Ben do not want to go. They want to stay and play with their toys. They say, "No, mom, we do not want to go. We want to play. We have fun here." Mom says, "I know, but we have to go. It will not take long. And maybe you will see something new and interesting at the store. Come on, let's go." She takes their hands and leads them to the car. At the store, Lisa and Ben see many things they have never seen before. They see toys that are bigger, brighter, and more colorful than theirs. They see clothes that are softer, warmer, and prettier than theirs. They see food that is sweeter, juicier, and tastier than theirs. They want to touch everything, but mom says, "No, we cannot buy these things. They are too expensive. They are a luxury. We only buy what we need." Lisa and Ben do not understand. They ask, "What is a luxury, mom? Why can we not have these things? They look so nice. We want them." Mom says, "A luxury is something that you do not need, but you want. It is nice to have, but not important. We do not have a lot of money, so we cannot buy luxuries. We have to be careful and save our money for things that we need, like food and clothes and rent. Do you understand?" Lisa and Ben nod, but they are not happy. They think mom is mean. They think they need these things. They think they deserve these things. They think their toys are not good enough. They think their clothes are not good enough. They think their food is not good enough. They go back to the car with mom. They are quiet and sad. They do not want to play with their toys anymore. They do not want to wear their clothes anymore. They do not want to eat their food anymore. They only want the things they saw at the store. On the way home, they see a boy on the street. He is dirty and thin. He has no shoes, no coat, no hat. He has no toys, no books, no blocks. He has nothing. He is sitting on the sidewalk, holding a tin can. He is asking people for money. He is hungry and cold. Lisa and Ben look at him. They feel something in their hearts. They realize something. They realize that they are lucky. They realize that they have a lot. They realize that they do not need luxuries. They realize that they have everything they need. They realize that they should be happy and grateful. They turn to mom and say, "Mom, can we give him some money? Can we help him? He has nothing. He needs something. He needs food and clothes and a home. He needs a mom." Mom smiles and says, "Yes, you can. That is very kind of you. That is very generous of you. That is very flexible of you. You are changing your mind. You are learning. You are growing. I am proud of you." She stops the car and gives them some coins. They get out and run to the boy. They give him the coins and say, "Here, this is for you. We hope you can buy some food and clothes and find a home. We hope you can be happy. We are sorry for you." The boy smiles and says, "Thank you. Thank you very much. You are very nice. You are very good. You are making me happy. You are giving me hope." Lisa and Ben hug him and say, "You are welcome. You are welcome very much. You are nice too. You are good too. You are making us happy too. You are teaching us something." They go back to the car with mom. They are not quiet and sad anymore. They are loud and happy. They want to play with their toys again. They want to wear their clothes again. They want to eat their food again. They do not want the things they saw at the store anymore. They have everything they need. They have more than they need. They have a luxury. They have a mom.
