Category: Intermediate
Title: Lucy the Girl and the Park

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a loose tooth that wiggled every time she talked. One day, Lucy went to the park to play with her friends. While they were playing, Lucy's friend Tom saw her wiggly tooth. Tom said, "Lucy, your tooth is moving! Can I help you lift it out?" Lucy was scared but said, "Okay, Tom, you can try." Tom gently tried to lift Lucy's tooth, but it didn't come out. They played some more and soon forgot about the tooth. When Lucy got home, her mom saw the wiggly tooth and helped her lift it out. Lucy was so happy to have her tooth out and put it under her pillow for the tooth fairy.

One day, Jack was playing with his toy train. He felt like it belonged to him - it was his! Suddenly, his sister came into his room and took the train away. Jack was very upset. He wanted his train back. He tried to get it back. He begged, but she wouldn't listen. He asked her nicely, but still she wouldn't give it back. Jack didn't understand why she was being so mean. He was very upset. He looked around and noticed his sister had left the room. He quickly grabbed a bean from the kitchen and put it in the train. That way it would still belong to him. But when his sister came back, she saw the bean and got really angry. She threw it away and said he could never have the train again. Jack was sad and very upset. He never got his train back.

Once upon a time, there was a pink bunny. The bunny was very harmless and loved to play with all the animals in the forest. The pink bunny had many friends, like the bird, the cat, and the dog. One day, the pink bunny wanted to teach his friends a new game. He called all his friends to come and play. The bird, the cat, and the dog came running to see what the pink bunny had to show them. The pink bunny taught his friends how to hop and jump like him. The bird, the cat, and the dog all tried to hop and jump like the pink bunny. They all had a lot of fun playing the new game. The pink bunny was very happy that he could teach his friends something new. And they all played together in the forest, hopping and jumping like the harmless, pink bunny.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dancer. She loved to dance all day. The dancer had a friend named Tim. Tim liked to watch her dance. One day, Tim asked the dancer, "Can you stay and play with me?" The dancer said, "Yes, I can stay and play with you!" They played fun games and danced together. They were happy and had a great day. The lively dancer and Tim were the best of friends.

One day, Tom and his dog, Spot, went to play in the park. The sun was shining, and they were having lots of fun. Tom saw a big ball and kicked it. "I want to make a score!" he said to Spot. Tom kicked the ball again, but it went into a dark place behind some trees. "Oh no! It's too dark, and I can't see the ball," said Tom. Spot barked and ran to the dark place. Tom followed Spot, and they found a big pool of water. Spot jumped into the water and started to bathe. Tom laughed as he watched Spot splash around. Just then, a big frog jumped out of the water, holding the ball in its mouth. "Ribbit! Here is your ball," said the frog. Tom and Spot were very surprised and happy. They thanked the frog, and Tom made a big score with the ball. They played with the frog until it was time to go home.

One day, a girl named Sue found a brilliant paper. On the paper was a poem. Sue liked the poem a lot. She read it over and over. The poem made her feel happy. Sue wanted to show the poem to her friend, Tim. She ran to Tim's house. But on her way, something unexpected happened. The wind blew very hard. The brilliant poem flew out of Sue's hand. Sue tried to catch it, but she could not. Sue was sad. She thought the poem was gone. But then, Tim came running. He had the poem in his hand. The wind blew it to him. Sue was happy again. Tim had saved the brilliant poem. They read the poem together and laughed.
