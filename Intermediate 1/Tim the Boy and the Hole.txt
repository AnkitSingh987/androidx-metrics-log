Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Hole

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big hole in his yard. He looked at the hole and wondered what was inside. He put his toy car near the hole and said, "I want to see what's in the hole!" A small, funny-looking bug came out of the hole. The bug looked at Tim and said, "I will show you what's in the hole, but you must give me something cheap first." Tim thought for a moment and then ran inside his house. Tim came back with a cheap, old toy he did not play with anymore. He gave it to the bug and said, "Here you go, now show me what's in the hole!" The bug took the toy and went back into the hole. After a while, the bug came back with a shiny, new toy car for Tim. Tim was so happy and said, "Thank you, bug!" The bug smiled and went back into the hole. Tim played with his new toy car all day, and from then on, he always took care of his toys, even the cheap ones.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Amy. She was three years old, and always wanted to help. One day, her mommy asked her to look after a little box. "I want you to care for this box, OK?" said mommy. "It's very important and needs to stay safe." Amy was happy to help and said, "Yes mommy! I will take care of it." Amy looked after the box very carefully, but then one day something strange happened. A fake rabbit came running up to her. The rabbit did a quick spin and said, "Can I borrow the box, please?" Amy frowned. "What are you doing here?" she asked. "Mommy said I have to keep the box safe." The rabbit did another quick spin. "Oh come on," it said. "I just want to borrow it for a little while." Amy shook her head. "No," she said. "I'll take care of it, like mommy said." The fake rabbit sighed, but then it smiled and said, "You're very smart. I like that!" With that, the rabbit hopped away and left the box with Amy. From then on, Amy took care of the box even more carefully - happily knowing she had done the right thing.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was playing in her garden with her ball. Suddenly, a car appeared. Lucy's mommy was standing close by and she shouted "No! That car is dangerous". Lucy was scared but she was also curious. She asked her mommy: "Why is it dangerous?". Her mommy replied "It's too dangerous to cross the street! We must stay here and wait until it's gone!" Sure enough, after a few moments the car disappeared. Lucy was relieved and she ran back to playing with her ball. The end!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived with his mom, dad, and old aunt. His old aunt loved to tell stories. One day, Tim found a big toy. It was a big red truck. Tim loved to play with the big red truck. One day, Tim was playing with his big red truck in the living room. His old aunt was sitting in her chair, watching him play. Tim's big red truck bumped into the chair. The chair shook a little. Tim's old aunt said, "Be careful, Tim. Don't destroy the chair." Tim looked at his old aunt and said, "I'm sorry, aunt. I will be careful." Then, Tim played with his big red truck some more. He was very careful not to destroy anything. His old aunt smiled and told him a story about a big red truck just like his. Tim and his old aunt were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a modern toy rod. He loved to play with it all day. One day, his friend Sam came to play with him. Tim said, "Sam, look at my modern rod. It's so fun to play with!" Sam was excited and wanted to play too. But Tim did not want to share his rod. He wanted to keep it all to himself. So, Sam felt sad and went home. Tim's mom saw what happened. She told Tim, "You should share your toys with your friends. It's nice to share, and it will make them happy." Tim thought about what his mom said. He decided to share his modern rod with Sam. The next day, Tim went to Sam's house. He said, "Sam, I'm sorry for not sharing. Let's play with my modern rod together." Sam was happy, and they played all day. From that day on, Tim always shared his toys with his friends. He learned that sharing is caring, and it makes everyone happy.
