Category: Intermediate
Title: Billy the Bird and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy wanted to build a modern nest. He found some wire to help him design it. He was very excited to make his new home. One day, while Billy was working on his nest, a big cat named Tom saw him. Tom wanted to catch Billy. Billy saw Tom and knew he had to finish his nest fast. He worked very hard, using the wire to make his nest strong. Billy finished his nest just in time. Tom tried to catch him, but the wire made the nest too strong for Tom. Billy was safe in his modern nest. He was very happy and proud of his design.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man. He had white hair and a long beard. He lived in a small house near the park. The elderly man was very kind and liked to help people. One day, the elderly man went for a walk in the park. He saw a little girl crying. She was lost and didn't know how to get home. The elderly man wanted to help her. He held her hand and said, "Don't cry. I will help you find your home." The elderly man and the little girl walked around the park. They asked people if they knew where her home was. Finally, they found her house. The little girl was very happy. She hugged the elderly man and said, "Thank you for helping me." The elderly man smiled and went back to his house. He was glad he could help the little girl.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom to buy a toy. He was very happy because he loved new toys. They walked through the store and saw many things. They saw a big ball, a small car, and a funny hat. Tim could not choose which toy he wanted to buy. While they were in the store, Tim saw a naughty cat. The cat was playing with a toy mouse. The cat was fast and jumped around. Tim thought the cat was funny and wanted to play with it. But the cat ran away and hid behind a big tree outside. Tim followed the cat to the tree and saw something amazing. Behind the tree, Tim found a place full of nature. There were many trees, flowers, and animals. Tim saw birds singing, rabbits playing, and butterflies flying. He forgot about the toys in the store and played with the animals instead. Tim's mom found him and smiled. They decided not to buy a toy that day. Tim learned that sometimes, nature can be more fun than toys.

Anna and Ben like to play sport in the park. They run and kick a ball and have fun. One day, they see a big dog in the park. The dog is black and has sharp teeth. The dog looks at them and growls. "Let's go away, Ben. The dog is scary," Anna says. But Ben is persistent. He wants to observe the dog. He thinks the dog is cute and wants to pet it. "No, Anna. The dog is nice. Look, he is wagging his tail. He wants to play with us," Ben says. He walks towards the dog and holds out his hand. The dog barks and jumps on Ben. He bites Ben's arm and makes him cry. Anna screams and runs to get help. The dog runs away and leaves Ben bleeding. Anna comes back with a man who calls an ambulance. Ben is hurt and has to go to the hospital. He is sad and scared. Anna is sorry and angry. She says to Ben, "You should have listened to me, Ben. The dog was not nice. He was mean and dangerous. You were silly and stubborn. Now you are in pain and trouble." Ben says nothing. He wishes he had not observed the dog. He wishes he had played sport with Anna. He learns a hard lesson.

One day, a big bird named Bob wanted to soar high in the sky. He loved to fly and look at the world below him. But Bob had a problem. His leg was tied to a heavy anchor. He felt guilty because he could not fly with his friends. Bob tried to lift the anchor, but it was too heavy. He felt sad and sat down. Then, a little ant named Amy saw Bob. She wanted to help him. Amy called all her ant friends. Together, they pushed and pushed the heavy anchor. Suddenly, the anchor moved! It was a surprise. Bob was so happy. He could soar high in the sky again. He thanked Amy and her friends. Bob learned that even little friends can do big things. And the guilty feeling was gone.
