Category: Intermediate
Title: Amy the Girl and the Cake

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She had long hair that she loved to brush. Her mom called her from downstairs, "Amy, hurry! It's time to eat!" Amy felt confused because it was not time for dinner yet. Amy ran down the stairs with her hair flying behind her. She saw her mom in the kitchen making a big cake. "Mom, why are we eating cake now?" she asked. Her mom smiled and said, "It's a surprise for your dad's birthday!" Amy wanted to help her mom with the cake. She put on a big apron and tried to tie her hair back, but it was still in her face. Her mom saw her struggle and said, "Let's put your hair up in a ponytail, so it won't get in the cake." Amy's mom helped her tie her hair back and they finished making the cake together. When dad came home, they all sang "Happy Birthday" and enjoyed the cake. Amy was happy she could help her mom and keep her hair out of the cake.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy who lived near a lake. Every day he would go to the lake to play. He loved to watch the swans swim in the water. One day, the little boy was sitting by the lake playing when he noticed one of the swans was acting very rude. He was pushing the other swans around and didn't share the food. The other swans were scared of the rude swan. The little boy felt sorry for the scared swans and he wanted to help them. He thought for a moment and then he had an idea. He grabbed some food and put it in the water around the swans, so the rude swan could no longer stop the other swans from eating. The little boy watched as the swans ate the food, and he was very happy that he could help them. He realized that he was kind and he could make a difference. He smiled and waved goodbye to the swans as he left the lake. He was proud of himself and his good deed.

One day, a pale bird sat on a tree. The bird started to sing a song. The song was very nice. A little girl named Lily heard the song and looked up at the bird. She smiled and said, "Hello bird, I like your song." The bird liked Lily too. It flew down and twisted its head to look at her. "Thank you, Lily," said the bird. "I like to sing songs. Do you want to sing with me?" Lily nodded her head and they started to sing together. Lily and the bird sang many songs. They twisted and danced around the tree. They were very happy. The sun went down, and it was time for Lily to go home. She hugged the bird and said, "Goodbye, my friend. I will come back to sing with you again."

Once upon a time, there was a person named Tom. Tom was a very frustrated person. He wanted to start a garden, but every time he tried, the plants would not grow. One day, while Tom was trying to plant seeds, a little bird flew down and said, "Hi, Tom! I see you are frustrated. Can I help you start your garden?" Tom looked at the bird and said, "Yes, please! I don't know why my plants won't grow." The bird told Tom, "You need to give your plants more water and sun. That will help them grow big and strong!" Tom listened to the bird and started to give his plants more water and sun. Soon, his garden began to grow, and Tom was not frustrated anymore. He was happy and thanked the bird for helping him.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy cat named Tom. Tom had a big zipper on his bag. One day, Tom went to the park with his bag. He was going to play with his friends. At the park, Tom saw his friends. They were all playing together. Tom opened his bag with the big zipper. Inside the bag, he found his toys. He had a ball, a car, and some paints. Tom wanted to play with the paints. Tom took out the paints and started to mix the colors. He mixed red and blue to make purple. He mixed yellow and blue to make green. Tom was having so much fun mixing the colors. His friends came over to see what he was doing. Together, they all mixed the colors and painted beautiful pictures. Tom was not grumpy anymore. He was happy to play with his friends and share his paints. The big zipper on his bag helped him keep all his toys safe. And they all played happily ever after.
