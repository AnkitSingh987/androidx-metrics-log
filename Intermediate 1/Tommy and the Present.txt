Category: Intermediate
Title: Tommy and the Present

Tommy was a three year old on a beautiful day. He was out in his backyard playing with the lovely clay that his parents had bought for him. He was smiling as he made little figures out of it and pretending that it was alive. Suddenly he heard a noise behind him and he turned around. It was his father! He was smiling and holding a little puppy in his arms. Tommy screamed in delight, "Oh daddy! Is this for me?" His father nodded with a smile, "Yes, it's all yours. His name is Lucky." Tommy was so happy that he couldn't believe it. He hugged the puppy and said, "Oh, I'm so lucky to have you!" Lucky quickly settled into the family and they all adored him. At night, Tommy would have him sleep near his bed and he would give him a big hug and whisper, "Good night, my lovely Lucky". Tommy was so happy to have his new best friend that he'd often give him a little present of clay. What lovely friends!

Once upon a time, there was a big, hot swamp. In the swamp, there lived a frog named Fred. Fred was a very nice frog who liked to help others. One day, Fred met a lost bird named Betty. Betty needed help to find her way back home. Fred said, "I will lead you home, Betty!" So, they started their journey together. As they walked, they found a big surprise. They saw a magic rock that could talk! The rock said, "I can help you too!" The rock showed them a secret path that led Betty home very fast. Fred, Betty, and the magic rock became best friends and had many fun adventures together.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play at the park. He saw a big ball and wanted to kick it. So, he ran fast and kicked the ball hard. The ball went flying high in the sky. Tim looked at the ball and noticed it had a different texture. It was bumpy, not smooth like his other balls. He picked it up and showed it to his friend, Sam. Tim said, "Look, Sam! This ball feels funny. Do you want to kick it too?" Sam felt the ball and said, "Yes, it is not like our other balls. But it is fun to kick!" They took turns kicking the ball and had a great time. Then, they tried to fit the ball through a hoop. It was too big! They laughed and kept playing with their special ball.

Ben and Lily like to play with rockets. They have a big box of rockets that can fly and make noise. They also have a small rocket that can burn. They only use the small rocket when Mom and Dad are with them. One day, Ben and Lily want to play with the small rocket. They ask Mom and Dad if they can. Mom and Dad say yes, but they have to be careful. They say the small rocket is not easy to use. It can be hot and hurt. Mom and Dad take Ben and Lily to the backyard. They find a safe place to put the small rocket. They give Ben and Lily a long stick with a match on the end. They tell them to light the match and touch the small rocket. Then they tell them to run away fast. Ben and Lily do what Mom and Dad say. They light the match and touch the small rocket. The small rocket makes a hiss and a spark. Then it flies up in the air. It makes a loud boom and a bright flash. Ben and Lily clap and cheer. They think the small rocket is fun. Mom and Dad hug Ben and Lily. They tell them they did a good job. They also tell them to never play with the small rocket alone. They say the small rocket is dangerous and only for special days. Ben and Lily nod and say okay. They are happy and proud. They love to play with rockets.

Once upon a time, there was a generous boy named Tom. He liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tom was playing with his toy truck. He saw his dad load big boxes into the car. Tom wanted to help, so he ran to his dad. "Dad, I want to help you load the boxes," said Tom. His dad smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom. You are very generous." Tom picked up a small box and put it in his toy truck. Then, he pushed the truck to the car. After they finished, Tom made a wish. He wished he could be big and strong like his dad. His mom heard him and said, "Tom, you are already strong and generous. We are so proud of you." Tom smiled, knowing that his wish had come true.
