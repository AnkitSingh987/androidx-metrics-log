Category: Intermediate
Title: John and the Swim

John was at the beach with his family. He wanted to go for a swim but his Mama said he was too young. Mama said he had to wait until he was a bit older. John started to feel guilty because all the other kids around him were swimming. John wanted to increase his chances of being able to swim. So he asked his Papa, "Please Papa, can I go in the water and swim with them? I promise to be careful." Papa looked at John, smiled and said, "OK John, but you must follow the rules. Don't go too far and always stay close to the beach". John was so happy he jumped up and down. He ran and jumped into the water and swam with all the other kids. He felt so guilty for being able to swim, but he was having too much fun to care. Mama and Papa were so proud of John for following the rules and swimming responsibly. They were happy to see him having so much fun.

One day, a wide river was flowing through a forest. It was full of oxygen, and the animals were happy. Suddenly, two frogs saw a frog on the other side of the river. It was very wide, so they couldn't cross it. "Let's go over there," said one of the frogs. But the other frog said, "We can't cross. The river is too wide." The first frog didn't listen and tried to cross the river. But it was too deep, and he started to sink. The other frog saw this and yelled, "Don't ignore me! You need to stay here!" The first frog listened and stayed where he was. The other frog was glad that he didn't ignore his advice. They both stayed safe on their side of the river.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Jack. Jack was three years old. He was playing in the street when he was stopped by a bad man. The bad man said in a very loud voice, "Hey little boy, do you want to talk to me?" Jack was scared but he was brave, so he said, "Yes I do." The bad man smiled and said, "Good, I have something to show you. Come with me." Jack followed the bad man to an old house in the woods. The bad man opened the door and said, "Come in." Jack was very scared, but he was brave so he went in. Inside the house, Jack saw a huge pile of teaspoons. The bad man said, "These teaspoons are all mine. I want you to give me one of them." Jack was scared, so he said, "No, I don't want to." The bad man got very angry and shouted, "Give me one teaspoon now!" Jack was really scared, so he took one teaspoon. The bad man said, "That's my terrible teaspoon!" and he grabbed Jack and threw him out of the house and drove away in his car. Jack was never seen again, and the bad man never got caught. It was a terrible ending for poor Jack.

Once upon a time, there was a bright red bag with a big zip. The bag could talk and loved to help people. One day, the bag met a little girl named Sue. Sue had a problem. She could not part her toys into two groups. "Hi Sue, I can help you part your toys," said the bright red bag with a smile. "Thank you, red bag!" Sue said. She put her toys into the bag. The bag used its big zip to part the toys into two groups. Sue was very happy. From that day on, Sue and the bright red bag were best friends. They played together every day and had lots of fun.

Ben likes to design things. He has many crayons and papers. He draws shapes and colors. He makes his own symbols. A symbol is a picture that means something. One day, Ben wants to design a symbol for his family. He thinks hard. What can he draw? He loves his mom, his dad, and his baby sister. He loves their home and their dog. He loves to play and hug and laugh. He has an idea. He draws a big circle. The circle is his family. They are always together. He draws a small house inside the circle. The house is their home. They are happy there. He draws a bone next to the house. The bone is for their dog. He loves their dog. He draws four hearts inside the circle. The hearts are for his mom, his dad, his sister, and himself. He loves them very much. He shows his symbol to his family. They smile and hug him. They like his symbol. They say it is very special. It is not a regular symbol. It is a Ben symbol. They put it on the fridge. Ben is proud. He likes to design things.
