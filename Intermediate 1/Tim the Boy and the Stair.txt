Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Stair

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who liked to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim wanted to go up the big stair in his house. But he was too small. Tim asked his mom, "Can you help me go up the stair?" His mom said, "You can try, but be careful!" Tim tried to manage the big stair all by himself. He put one foot up, then the other foot. He did this again and again. Soon, he was at the top of the stair. Tim felt very happy and proud. He called out, "Mom, I did it! I managed to go up the stair all by myself!" His mom said, "Good job, Tim! You did it!" Tim smiled and knew he was a big boy now. He could do many things if he tried hard.

Once upon a time there was an honest little girl named Sophie. Sophie was walking in the forest when she saw something shining. She looked closely and it was a piece of steel in the sun. Sophie was so happy and she wanted to see it melt. She asked her friend, Bobo the rabbit, for help. Bobo said "Let me help you. I have a fire in my hole!" Sophie said, "Oh wow! Let's put the steel in the fire!" So Sophie and Bobo put the steel in the fire. As it heated up, it started to melt. Sophie and Bobo watched as it became liquid. They both smiled and said "Wow, it melted!" Sophie thanked Bobo and took the piece of melted steel home. She was very proud of her honest self for finding it. She looked at it with admiration and never forgot the adventure she and Bobo had.

One day, a king lived in a big castle. The king was very loud. He liked to yell and sing. Everyone in the castle could hear him. The king had a big job to do. He needed to supply food to his people. The king went to the market to get food. He bought apples, bananas, and bread. He put them all in a big bag. Then, he went back to the castle. He was very happy. He thought his people would be happy too. But when he got back to the castle, something unexpected happened. The food was gone! The king was very sad. He looked around and saw a little mouse. The mouse had taken the food! The mouse was hungry too. The king laughed and shared the food with the mouse. The people were happy, and the mouse was happy too.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Tom and Jerry. Tom wanted to make a yummy drink with a lemon. He asked Jerry to help him. At Jerry's house, they prepared the lemon by cutting it into pieces. They added the lemon slices to a cup of water. That made the drink. Tom asked Jerry if the drink was sweet. Jerry told him it was not sweet, but he could make it sweet by adding some sugar. Tom said he did not have any sugar, but Jerry knew a way. He said he could put some honey in the drink. But it was not sweet enough. Then Jerry said they could get some sugar from a store. Tom and Jerry went to the store and bought some cheap sugar. Then they added the sugar to the drink and it was sweet! Tom and Jerry laughed and said that the drink was delicious. They had a great time together preparing it.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big bin. This bin was very popular. All the people liked it. They put their trash in the bin every day. The bin was always full, but it was never sad. It was happy to help keep the town clean. One day, a naughty boy came to the town. He did not like the popular bin. He wanted to destroy it. He tried to push the bin down. But the bin was too strong. The bin did not fall. The boy was angry, but he could not destroy the popular bin. The people in the town saw the naughty boy trying to destroy the bin. They told him to stop. They said the bin was good and helpful. The boy felt bad. He said sorry to the bin and the people. From that day on, the boy was nice and helped the popular bin. The town stayed clean and happy.
