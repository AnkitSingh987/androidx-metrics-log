Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim and the Cat

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a trip with his family. They went to the country to see the animals and play outside. Tim was very excited because he loved animals. He saw a big, harmless dog and wanted to play with it. "Can I play with the dog, Mom?" Tim asked. "It looks harmless and fun!" Mom said, "Yes, but be careful, Tim. Don't go too far away." Tim played with the dog and they had lots of fun. They ran and jumped and laughed. But then, the dog saw something in the bushes. The dog ran to the bushes and Tim followed him. Suddenly, a big surprise came out of the bushes! It was a mean, angry cat. The cat hissed and tried to scratch Tim and the dog. They got scared and ran away. Tim cried and the dog barked. Mom found Tim and the dog, and they all went home. Tim was sad because the day had a bad ending. He learned that sometimes surprises can be scary and not all animals are harmless.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Sam. Sam was big and liked to play with his toys. His favorite toy was a motor car. He loved to make the motor car go fast. One day, Sam saw a big motor car outside his house. It was shiny and looked fun to play with. Sam wanted to take the motor car for a ride. He thought he could be like a grown-up and drive fast. Sam took the motor car and started to drive. He went very fast and felt happy. But he did not know how to stop the motor car. Sam tried to make the motor car stop, but it was too hard. The motor car went too fast and hit a tree. Sam was sad and scared. The motor car was broken, and Sam was hurt. He learned that he should not take things that are not his and should always ask for help.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was just an ordinary boy who liked to play with his friends. Today, Tim hoped to find a new friend to play with. As Tim walked down the street, he saw a big dog. The dog looked sad. Tim said, "Hi dog! Do you want to play with me today?" The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim was excited to play with his new friend. But as they played, Tim saw a sign that said, "Do not play with the dog." Tim did not listen and kept playing. Soon, the dog bit Tim's hand. Tim cried and ran home. His mom said, "You should always follow the rules, even if you hope for something else." And Tim learned that not all ordinary days turn out how we hope.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he found a new object in his room. It was a big rock. Tim was very happy to find the rock, and he started to play with it. As Tim played with the rock, something unexpected happened. The rock began to move! Tim was very surprised. The rock moved more and more, and then it started to talk. "Hello, Tim!" said the rock. Tim could not believe his ears. He had a talking rock! Tim and the rock became very good friends. They played games and had fun together. But one day, the rock told Tim a secret. "I am not just a rock," it said. "I am a magic rock!" Tim was amazed. The magic rock could do many things, like change colors and make funny noises. Tim and the magic rock had even more fun together, and they were the best of friends forever.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the bus stop. He wanted to ride the big bus to the park. Tim was very excited. At the bus stop, he saw a big bus coming. It was red and shiny. Tim got on the bus and found a seat near the window. As the bus moved, Tim saw many things outside. He saw a dog running, and kids playing. Then, he saw a lady with a big bag. She was giving food to people. Tim thought the lady was very generous. He wanted to be like her when he grew up. When the bus stopped at the park, Tim got off. He saw a small bug on a leaf. Tim wanted to examine the bug. He looked at it closely and saw it had many legs. Tim liked the bug and the park. He had a fun day and went home on the big bus.
