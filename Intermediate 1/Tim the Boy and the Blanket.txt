Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Blanket

Once upon a time, in a small cheerful house, there lived a boy named Tim and his mom. Tim had a big, soft blanket that he loved very much. He would take his blanket everywhere he went. One day, Tim and his mom went to the park to play. Tim brought his blanket with him. At the park, Tim met a new friend named Sam. They played together and had lots of fun. Then, Sam saw Tim's blanket and asked, "Can I see your blanket, please?" Tim said, "Yes, you can see it, but please be nice to my blanket." Sam took the blanket and started to slap it on the ground. Tim felt sad and said, "Please don't slap my blanket. That's not nice." Sam stopped and said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I didn't know it would make you sad." Tim and Sam learned that it is important to be kind to others and their things. They played together again and had more fun at the park.

One day, a cat named Tom found a big pot. Tom was very confused. He did not know what was inside the pot. So, Tom put his head inside to see. When Tom looked inside, he saw a lot of dust. The dust made Tom sneeze very hard. The sneeze was so big that the pot fell over. Tom was surprised to see what came out of the pot. Out of the pot came many colorful balls. The balls bounced all around Tom. He thought they were just dust, but they were not. Tom played with the balls all day and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a loyal dog named Buddy. They loved to play in the park together. One day, they found a big triangle toy in the park. "Look, Buddy! A triangle!" said Amy. Buddy wagged his tail and seemed very happy. They played with the triangle all day long. They threw it, rolled it, and even jumped over it. Later, another little boy came to the park. He looked sad. "That's my triangle," he said. "I lost it yesterday." Amy thought for a moment. She did not want to make the boy sad. "Here, you can have it back," she said, giving the triangle to the boy. The boy smiled and said, "Thank you, Amy!" Buddy wagged his tail and seemed happy too. From that day on, Amy, Buddy, and the little boy became good friends. They learned that sharing and being kind to others can make everyone happy. And that was the best thing they could do.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play. He saw some kids playing hockey. He wanted to play too. Tim wandered over to them and asked, "Can I play with you?" The kids looked at Tim and saw his ugly, old shoes. They laughed and said, "No, you can't play with us. Your shoes are ugly and old." Tim felt sad and walked away. As Tim wandered around, he met a kind man. The man saw Tim's sad face and asked, "Why are you sad?" Tim told him about the kids and his ugly shoes. The kind man smiled and said, "Don't worry. You can play with me." They played hockey together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little brown dog named Max. Max lived with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play in the park together. One day, Max got sick and needed an operation. Lily was sad and scared. In the hospital, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I hate operations, they are scary." Lily said, "I am scared too, but we have to be brave for Max." Tim and Lily decided to be brave together for Max and their new friendship. The doctor came out and said, "The operation was a success! Max is all better now." Lily and Tim were so happy. They went to see Max, and there was a surprise! Max had a new friend, a little brown cat named Bella. Max and Bella played together, and Lily and Tim laughed. Now they all had new friends, and they played happily ever after.
