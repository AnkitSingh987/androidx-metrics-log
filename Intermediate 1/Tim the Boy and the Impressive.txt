Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Impressive

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat lollipops. One day, he heard his mom mention a big, impressive lollipop at the store. Tim was very excited and could not wait to see it. The next day, Tim and his mom went to the store. There, Tim saw the impressive lollipop. It was so big and colorful! He asked his mom if he could have it. His mom said yes, but told him to be careful because it was so big. Tim started to eat the lollipop. It was very yummy. But as he ate it, he began to feel not so good. He ate too much too fast. In the end, Tim had a big tummy ache and did not feel well at all. He learned that sometimes, even the most impressive things can have a bad ending if you are not careful.

Once upon a time, there was a poor little dog named Max. Max did not have a home to stay in. He was always walking around, looking for food and a warm place to sleep. One day, Max found a little girl named Lily. Lily saw that Max was cold and needed something to wear. She said, "Max, I have a warm coat for you to wear. You can stay with me." Max wagged his tail and was very happy. Max wore the warm coat and stayed with Lily. They played together every day. Max was not poor anymore because he had a friend and a warm place to stay. And they lived happily ever after.

Molly was so excited! Her mum had just told her that she was getting a box of toys today. She jumped up and down, clapping her hands and shouting "Yay!" When the box arrived, Molly's dad helped her to unpack it. Inside, there were all kinds of amazing toys. There was a hopscotch set, a big top hat and a crazy spinning top. Molly loved them all, and she couldn't decide which one to play with first. Then a voice said "I think you should play with this one". Molly looked around, but no one else was in the room. She slowly turned her gaze to the box, and saw the little blue spinning top smiling at her. Molly gasped and said "Oh, hello! Are you a talking toy?". The spinning top nodded and said "Yes, of course I am! Why don't you play with me first?". Molly was so excited to be playing with a talking toy that she couldn't wait to start. She grabbed the spinning top, and the two of them had a crazy, fun-filled day, full of spinning, laughing and playing.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to shake his chain. One day, Spot was very sad because he could not find his chain. He looked and looked, but it was gone. Spot did not know what to do. Then, Spot had an idea. He would ask his friend, the big bird named Flap, to help him find his chain. Flap flew high up in the sky and looked around. He saw something shiny in a tree. Flap flew down and told Spot about the shiny thing. Spot and Flap went to the tree and saw that it was Spot's chain! But it was not alone. There was another little dog with the chain. The other dog was shaking the chain too! Spot was not sad anymore. He had found his chain and made a new friend. They both shook the chain together and had lots of fun.

Anna and Ben wanted to mail a card to their grandma. They made the card with paper, crayons and stickers. They wrote "We love you, Grandma" and drew hearts and flowers. They put the card in an envelope and wrote Grandma's name and address. They asked their mom to take them to the mail box. Mom said yes, but they had to hurry. It was almost time for the mail truck to come. Anna and Ben put on their shoes and coats and ran to the car. They were anxious to mail their card. They hoped Grandma would like it. Mom drove them to the mail box. It was a big blue box with a slot. Anna and Ben took turns to put their card in the slot. They pushed it hard until it disappeared. They smiled and waved at the mail box. They said "Bye, card! Have a good trip!" They got back in the car. Mom said they did a good job. She said Grandma would be very happy to get their card. She said maybe Grandma would call them or send them a card back. As they drove away, they saw the mail truck coming. It stopped at the mail box. A man got out and opened the box. He took out the mail, including Anna and Ben's card. He put it in his truck and drove away. Anna and Ben did not see this. They were busy talking about Grandma and what they would do when they saw her. They did not know that their card was on its way to Grandma. They did not know that they had just made Grandma's day.
