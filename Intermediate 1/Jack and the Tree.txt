Category: Intermediate
Title: Jack and the Tree

One day, Little Jack found a pine tree in the forest. He was so excited that he wanted to help the pine tree. He asked the pine tree, "Can I help you?" But the pine tree said nothing. Little Jack tried again and again, but still no answer. Little Jack was very sad. Then he heard a voice. It was a little bird that was sitting on the pine tree. The little bird said, "I can help you. I know how to help this pine tree!" Little Jack was so excited. The little bird flew down and showed Little Jack how to keep the pine tree healthy. This included watering and taking care of it. Little Jack was very happy. Every day, Little Jack helped the pine tree and it became bigger and healthier. Whenever Little Jack visited the pine tree, the little bird came to say hello. They were the best of friends.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big cooler in his yard. He did not know what was inside. He felt excited but a little bit scared. He called his friend, Sam, to come and see it. Sam ran over as fast as he could. "What is in the cooler?" Tim asked Sam. Sam did not know either. They both felt a little bad because they knew they should not open things that were not theirs. But they were so curious. They decided to question Tim's mom. "Mom, can we open the cooler?" Tim asked. "I don't know whose it is, but I want to see what is inside." Tim's mom thought for a moment. "No, it is not ours. We should not open it," she said. Tim and Sam were sad, but they knew she was right. Just then, a man came into the yard. He looked worried. "Have you seen my cooler?" he asked. Tim and Sam looked at each other, then showed the man where they found the cooler. The man was happy to get his cooler back, and he thanked the boys for not opening it. Tim and Sam felt good that they did the right thing.

Sara and Tom are playing in the living room. They have many toys, but they like the rug best. The rug is rare. It has many colors and shapes. Sara and Tom pretend the rug is a map of a faraway land. "Look, this is a mountain," Sara says, pointing to a red triangle. "And this is a river," she says, tracing a blue line. "I see a castle," Tom says, finding a yellow square. "And a dragon," he says, spotting a green circle. They make up stories about the mountain, the river, the castle and the dragon. They have fun. Then they hear a voice from the kitchen. It is Mom. She calls them. "Sara, Tom, time for lunch!" Sara and Tom stop playing. They get up and run to the kitchen. They are hungry. "Can we play with the rug later?" Sara asks. "Of course, my loves," Mom says. "The rug is always here for you." Sara and Tom smile. They love the rug. And they love Mom.

Once there was a little boy who wanted to be a lawyer when he grew up. He always loved to joke around, and he thought it was so much fun! One day he and his friends were playing in the park when they found an icy lake. The little boy got very excited, and said: "It looks like so much fun!" He ran up to the edge, ready to jump in and have a swim. But right before he jumped, he heard someone call out: "Wait!" He looked around and saw a lawyer walking up to him. The lawyer smiled and said, "You can't jump in the icy lake. It's against the law." The little boy was a bit disappointed, but he understood. The lawyer laughed. He said, "I know a joke that might make you feel better. It'll be just for you." The little boy was so happy, and before he knew it the lawyer was telling him a funny joke. The little boy laughed, and thanked the lawyer for making him feel better. He promised to remember the joke and tell it to his friends. And ever since then, the little boy loved to make others laugh just like the lawyer had done for him.
