Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Sweet Cat and the Dog

Once upon a time, there was a sweet cat named Lily. Lily liked to play catch with her friends in the park. One day, while playing catch, Lily found a paper on the ground. It was a license, and it had a picture of a dog on it. Lily thought it would be fun to find the dog in the picture, so she asked her friends to help her. They looked everywhere, but they could not find the dog. Then, something unexpected happened - a big wind came and took the license away. Lily and her friends chased after it, and they finally caught the license in a tree. When they got the license back, they saw a little dog under the tree. It was the dog from the picture! Lily and her friends were very happy to find the dog and return the license. The dog's owner was so thankful that he gave them all a treat. The moral of the story is that when you work together and help others, good things will happen.

One day, a restless little dog went for a walk. He saw a big tree with lots of shade. The dog was hot, so he went under the tree to rest. Under the tree, he saw a cat. The dog asked the cat, "Can I sit here too?" The cat said, "Yes, but don't make noise. I like quiet." The dog sat down, but he was still restless. He wanted to play. The dog started to bark and run around. The cat got mad and hissed at the dog. The dog didn't listen, and the cat left the tree. Now, the dog was alone in the shade, and he felt sad.

Once upon a time, there lived a little boy called Ben. He was only three years old and he loved to design things. One day he had an idea. He wanted to design a bean! He went to grab his pencils and paper and got to work. "I'm going to make a bean," he said to himself. He drew a yellow circle and added two eyes to it. Then, he added two long ears. He had done it - he had designed a bean! Ben ran to show his mum the bean he had designed. His mum smiled and said, "That's a very honest bean, Ben!" Ben was very proud of himself and laughed. "I want to design more things," he said. Mum hugged him and said, "Of course you can, Ben. You are a very creative boy." Then she added, "But remember, always be honest in your designs." Ben nodded, smiled and hugged his mum. Then, he went off to design something else.

Once upon a time, there was a popular gym. Many kids loved to play there. They would run, jump, and have fun all day. One day, a big change came to the gym. A mean man became the new boss. The mean man did not like kids. He wanted the gym to be only for grown-ups. He took away all the fun things for the kids to play with. The kids were sad and did not want to go to the gym anymore. One by one, the kids stopped coming to the gym. The gym became empty and quiet. The mean man was happy, but the gym was not popular anymore. In the end, the gym had to close down because no one wanted to go there. And that was the sad end of the once popular gym.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn little girl named Sue. She lived in a small house with her mom and her dog, Max. One day, Sue saw a big cobweb in the corner of her room. She didn't like it, so she asked her mom to help her clean it. "No, Sue, you must do it yourself," said her mom. Sue didn't want to, but she hoped she could clean it all by herself. She got a broom and tried to reach the cobweb. But she was too small and couldn't reach it. Sue was sad, but she didn't give up. She climbed on a chair to get closer to the cobweb. But the chair was not strong, and it broke. Sue fell down and hurt her arm. Her mom came running and hugged her. "I told you to be careful, Sue," her mom said. Sue cried, and the cobweb was still there.
