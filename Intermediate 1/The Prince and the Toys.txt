Category: Intermediate
Title: The Prince and the Toys

Once upon a time, there was a prince. He lived in a big castle. The prince was not very nice. People said he was terrible. One day, the prince met a girl. The girl was sad. She lost her toy. The prince wanted to help her. He said, "Let us number the toys you have. Maybe we will find the lost one." The girl and the prince counted the toys. They found the lost toy. The girl was happy. The prince was not terrible anymore. They played together and became good friends.

---

One beautiful day, Nellie was playing in the park with her toy. She had a bright orange ball to play with. She threw it in the air and then tried to catch it, but her little hands were too slow. Suddenly, Nellie saw a colourful card on the ground. It was very pretty and interesting. She lifted the card and saw that it had a man on it and some words. She couldn't read the words, but she could tell that the man was smiling. Nellie decided to keep the card and show it to her mommy. She was so proud of it. When she arrived home, she showed the card to her mommy. "My goodness, it is a magic card!" her mommy said. "This card is very powerful. It can do amazing things if you wish it to." Nellie was so excited. She knew that she had something powerful in her hands. She was eager to see what the card could do. She waved her hand above the card, closed her eyes and wished for something. The next morning, Nellie was very surprised. When she opened the card, there was a bright blue butterfly inside! It flew out of the card and hovered in the air above her. Nellie was amazed and filled with awe. She never expected the card to be so powerful!

---

Sam and Mia liked to play with their toy cars and boats. They had a big blue car that could drive fast and a red boat that could float on water. They also had a shiny anchor that they could attach to the boat. One day, they decided to play a game. They pretended that the car was a pirate and the boat was a treasure. The car wanted to catch the boat and take the treasure, but the boat wanted to escape and keep the treasure. "Arrr, I am the pirate car and I will drive after you, boat!" Sam said, making a loud noise with his mouth. "No, you can't catch me, pirate car! I am the boat and I have a secret weapon!" Mia said, smiling. "What secret weapon?" Sam asked, curious. "This!" Mia said, and threw the anchor behind the boat. The anchor landed on the floor and made a loud clang. The car tried to drive over the anchor, but it was too difficult. The anchor was heavy and sharp, and it made the car stop and get stuck. "Ha ha, I tricked you, pirate car! Now you can't catch me, and I can keep the treasure!" Mia said, happy. "Aw, you are too smart, boat! You win this time, but I will try again next time!" Sam said, pretending to be sad. They laughed and hugged each other. They liked to play together, even when they were pirates and boats. They put the car and the boat and the anchor back in their toy box and went to have a snack. They were good friends.

One day, a child found a smooth rock. He liked it a lot. He wanted to lift it and take it home. But, the rock was too heavy for him. He tried and tried, but he could not lift it. He asked his friend, a big dog, to help him. The dog tried to lift the rock too, but it was too heavy for the dog as well. The child was sad. He did not want to leave the smooth rock. Then, a kind bird saw the child and the dog. The bird wanted to help them. The bird found a stick and told the child and the dog to lift the rock together. They tried again, and this time, they could lift the smooth rock with the help of the stick. The child was very happy. He thanked the bird and the dog, and took the smooth rock home.
