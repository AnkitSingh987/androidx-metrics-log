Category: Intermediate
Title: The Story of the Fox and the Farm

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful country where many animals lived. In this country, there was a big farm with lots of yummy food. One day, a naughty fox wanted to steal some food from the farm. The fox sneaked into the farm and saw a hen. The hen said, "Don't steal our food, Mr. Fox! That's not nice!" But the fox didn't listen and took some food anyway. The hen was sad and told her friends about the fox. The next day, all the animals on the farm made a plan to stop the fox. They all talked and said, "We must work together to stop the fox from stealing our food!" So, they set a trap for the fox. When the fox came back to steal more food, he fell into the trap. All the animals came out and said, "Mr. Fox, stealing is wrong. We all need to share and be kind to each other." The fox felt sorry and promised not to steal again. From that day on, all the animals in the beautiful country lived happily and shared their food with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in the mud with her friends. One day, after playing, Lily had the urge to wash her hands. But her friends said, "No, let's play more!" Lily thought about it and decided to listen to her friends. As they continued to play, a little bird flew down and landed on a tree nearby. The bird saw how dirty they were and said, "You should wash your hands, or else something bad might happen." The friends laughed and didn't listen to the bird. Later that day, the friends went home and ate dinner without washing their hands. The next day, they all felt sick. They remembered what the bird said and realized they should have washed their hands. From that day on, Lily and her friends always washed their hands before eating. They learned that it's important to stay clean and listen to good advice.

Once upon a time, there was a big pond. In the pond, there was a flexible frog. The frog liked to jump and play all day long. One day, the frog saw a pretty bug. The bug could spin around and around. The frog wanted to spin too. So, the frog and the bug became friends. They played together by the pond. They spun and jumped all day. The frog was happy to have a fun friend. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a nosy cat named Tom saw a mop. The mop was big and wet. Tom wanted to test the mop, so he pushed it with his paw. The mop fell and made a big splash. Tom's friend, a dog named Max, came running. "What are you doing, Tom?" Max asked. Tom said, "I'm playing with the mop. It's fun!" Max wanted to play too, so they both pushed the mop around the room. As they played, they cleaned the floor. Mom came in and saw the clean floor. She was happy. "Good job, Tom and Max!" she said. They were proud and kept playing with the mop.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. The sky was gloomy and it looked like it might rain. Tim saw a red ball near the slide and wanted to play with it. Tim's mom said, "Wait, let me check the thermometer first." She looked at the thermometer and saw that it was cold outside. "Put on your coat, Tim," she said. Tim put on his coat and went to play with the red ball. After a while, a girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Tim playing with the red ball and said, "That's my ball!" Tim felt sad but gave the ball back to Lily. "Thank you for returning my ball," Lily said with a smile. Tim and Lily then played together until it was time to go home.

John was an ordinary boy who really wanted to work. He started to look all around, but he could not find a job. One day, he saw an igloo, so he thought he could work there. He went to the igloo and knocked on the door, but no one answered. He looked around and saw a window. He opened it and went inside. John saw that there were lots of things inside he could use to work. He was so excited! He decided to start cleaning the place. But when he was done cleaning, he heard a loud noise. Suddenly, two big people stepped into the igloo and yelled at him. They said he could not work there. Then they ran away, with John behind them. John was very sad, as he was not able to do the work he wanted. He never found an ordinary job again.
