Title: Sue the Girl and the Drum

One day, a girl named Sue was playing with her toy band. She had a toy drum, a toy guitar, and a toy piano. She loved her toy band very much. Sue wanted to show her friends her toy band, so she decided to raise them high in the air for everyone to see. Sue went to the park with her toy band. She saw her friend Tim. "Look at my toy band!" Sue said to Tim. Tim looked at the toys and said, "Wow! I like your toy band. Can I play with them too?" Sue was anxious because she didn't want to share her toy band. But she said yes to Tim anyway. Tim started to play with the toy drum. He hit it very hard. Suddenly, the drum broke! Sue was very sad because her toy band was not the same anymore. Tim said sorry, but Sue's toy band was still broken. Sue went home with her broken toy band, feeling very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree with his family. He loved to fly, but he was too small and felt helpless. Every day, he watched the other birds fly high in the sky. One day, Billy saw a big bird flying in the sky. The big bird saw Billy and said, "Why are you not flying with us, little bird?" Billy replied, "I am too small and can't fly high like you." The big bird smiled and said, "You can change that. Just be brave and try." Billy thought about it and decided to try. He jumped off the tree, flapped his wings, and started flying. The sky changed colors as he flew higher and higher. Soon, he was flying with the big bird and his friends. The other birds cheered for Billy, and he felt so happy. He realized that he was not helpless anymore. The moral of the story is that if you are brave and try, you can change and do amazing things.

One day, a girl named Lily was playing with her friend, Tim. They were having a fun time drawing pictures with their crayons. They both wanted to make the best picture ever, so they became competitive. Lily said, "My picture will be the best!" Tim replied, "No, mine will be the best!" As they were drawing, it started to rain. The rain came through the open window and began to soak their pictures. "Oh no, our pictures are getting wet!" cried Lily. Tim said, "We need to save them!" They quickly closed the window and grabbed their wet pictures. Instead of being upset, they saw that the rain had made their pictures even more beautiful. The colors were now mixed together in a pretty way. They both agreed that they didn't need to compete anymore. They could just have fun and make art together. And from that day on, Lily and Tim always enjoyed making beautiful pictures, rain or shine.

Once upon a time, there was a little crab. The crab liked to watch things. One day, the crab saw a big fish. The big fish was very nice. The crab thought, "I want to be friends with the big fish." The crab went to the big fish and said, "Hi, I am a crab. I saw you and you look nice. Can we be friends?" The big fish smiled and said, "Yes, we can be friends. I am a generous fish. I like to help my friends." The next day, the crab and the big fish played together. They had a lot of fun. The big fish said, "I have a surprise for you. Close your eyes." The crab closed its eyes. When the crab opened its eyes, it saw a big pile of food. The crab was very happy. The big fish said, "I told you I am a generous fish. I wanted to share my food with you." The crab and the big fish ate the food together, and they were the best of friends.

One day, a big white dog and a little brown cat played a game. They wanted to see who could run the fastest. The big white dog thought he would win because he was so big and strong. The little brown cat was not so sure, but she wanted to try her best. Before the race, the big white dog whispered to the little brown cat, "I will let you win this time." The little brown cat just smiled and said, "We will see." They got ready to start the race, and off they went! The big white dog ran very fast, but he did not look back to see where the little brown cat was. As the big white dog got close to the finish line, he slowed down so the little brown cat could win. But when he looked back, he saw the little brown cat was already at the finish line! She had taken a shortcut and won the race. The big white dog was very surprised, but he was happy for his friend. From that day on, they both knew that anyone could win if they tried their best.
