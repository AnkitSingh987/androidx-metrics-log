Category: Intermediate
Title: Sue and the Oil

Once upon a time, there was a little town with different colored houses. In one of the houses lived a girl named Sue. Sue was a brave girl who liked to help others. One day, Sue saw a big truck spill oil on the road. The oil was black and sticky. Sue knew that the oil was not good for the animals and plants. She wanted to save them from the oil. Sue ran to her friend Tom's house. She told Tom, "We need to save the animals and plants from the oil!" Tom agreed and they both went to the road. They used big brushes to clean the oil. Soon, the road was clean and the animals and plants were safe. Sue and Tom were happy that they saved the animals and plants. They knew that they did a good job. The people in the town were proud of Sue and Tom for their help. From that day on, Sue and Tom became best friends who always helped others.

One day, a boy named Tom was very worry. He was worry because his ice cream was in the freezer. He thought someone might eat it. Tom was sorry he did not eat it before. Tom went to play outside. He saw his friend, Sam. Sam was sorry too. Tom told Sam about the ice cream. Sam said, "Let's go get it now!" They ran to the house fast. When they got to the freezer, the ice cream was gone! Tom was very sad. But then, his mom came in. She had a big smile. She said, "I put the ice cream in a new box!" Tom and Sam were happy. They ate the ice cream and were not worry anymore.

One day, a pretty girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. She saw a big, round lollipop. Lily wanted the lollipop very much. She asked her mom, "Can I have the lollipop, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but you need to pay for it." Lily gave the store man her money. The store man said, "Thank you!" and gave her the lollipop. Lily was very happy. When they got home, Lily opened the lollipop. But it was not a lollipop! It was a toy in the shape of a lollipop. Lily was sad at first, but then she played with the toy and had lots of fun. In the end, she was happy with her new toy.

Anna and Ben are playing hide and seek in their house. Anna is the seeker and Ben is the hider. Ben runs to find a good spot to hide. He sees a closet in his room. He opens the door and goes inside. He closes the door and sits on the floor. He hopes Anna will not find him. Anna counts to ten and then says, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looks for Ben in the living room, the kitchen, and the bathroom. She does not see him. She goes to his room and sees his blue bed, his blue rug, and his blue toys. She thinks, "Maybe he is hiding in something blue." She looks under the bed, behind the curtains, and in the toy box. She does not see him. She sees the closet and thinks, "Maybe he is hiding in there." She walks to the closet and opens the door. She sees Ben sitting on the floor. She says, "I found you!" Ben says, "You are good at seeking!" They both laugh and hug. They decide to switch roles and play again. Anna goes to hide and Ben goes to seek. They have fun playing hide and seek.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a pretty dress that she loved to wear. One day, she wanted to go to the park and play with her friends. Lucy was so excited that she had to rush to put on her dress. At the park, Lucy saw her friend Tommy. Tommy was eating a delicious ice cream cone. Lucy asked, "Can I have a lick of your ice cream, please?" Tommy said, "Sure, Lucy!" and gave her a lick of his ice cream. As Lucy was licking the ice cream, she did not see a big mud puddle in front of her. She stepped in it and splashed mud all over her pretty dress. Lucy was very sad and cried because her pretty dress was dirty. She had to go home and change her dress, and could not play with her friends anymore that day.
