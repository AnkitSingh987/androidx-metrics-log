Category: Intermediate
Title: The Sun and the Powder

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big sun that would shine every day. The sun made everyone happy. The people in the town loved to play outside and enjoy the sunshine. One day, a boy named Tim found some special powder. The powder was very colorful and pretty. Tim wanted to show his friends the powder, but he didn't know that it was dangerous. He took the powder to his friends and they all looked at it, amazed by its colors. Tim's mom saw them playing with the dangerous powder and quickly took it away. She told them that it was not safe to play with. The kids were sad, but they understood. They went outside to play in the sunshine instead, and they were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big bird. The bird loved to soar high in the sky. One day, the bird saw something on the ground. It was a big pile of disgusting trash. The bird did not like the trash, but it saw a toy in the pile. The bird flew down to the trash to get the toy. It picked up the toy with its beak. The bird was happy with the toy, but it was sad about the trash. The bird thought, "I need to do something to help." The bird told its friends about the trash. They all worked together to clean up the trash. In the end, the result was a clean and pretty park. The bird and its friends were very happy. They could play with the toy and soar in the sky without seeing the disgusting trash anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. The people in the town were very happy. They danced, sang, and played games. But, there was a little boy named Tim who was restless. He did not want to play with the other kids. Tim's mom had a shiny, pretty thing called a trophy. She wanted to show it at the festival. She asked Tim to polish it. Tim did not want to do it. He wanted to play, but he knew his mom would be sad if he did not help. Tim tried to polish the trophy, but it was hard. He could not make it shiny. He felt bad. Then, his friend Lily came and saw him. She wanted to help. Together, they polished the trophy until it was very shiny. Tim's mom was happy, and Tim and Lily went to play at the festival.

One day, a little boy named Tim found an old ticket. He was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sue. Tim called Sue to come and see the old ticket. When Sue saw the old ticket, she was excited too. They both wondered what the ticket was for. Tim and Sue decided to ask Tim's mom about the old ticket. Tim's mom told them that the old ticket was for a fun fair. Tim and Sue were so happy. They went to the fun fair together and had a great time!

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play in the mud. Every day, Max would get very dirty. Max's fur was always brown and sticky. One morning, Max's friend, the sun, came to bring wake. Max jumped out of his bed and ran outside. He saw a big puddle of mud. Max played in the mud all day long. Max was very happy. In the evening, Max's mom called him inside. Max's mom saw his dirty fur. She said, "Max, you are very dirty! Let's take a bath." Max took a bath and his fur was clean again. Max went to bed and dreamed of more fun days in the mud.

Once upon a time, there was a young bird named Tim. Tim had soft feathers and lived in a big tree with his family. Tim's mom and dad would support him while he learned how to fly. One day, Tim saw a big red ball in the grass. He wanted to play with the ball. So, he jumped from the tree to fly down to the ball. But, Tim was not good at flying yet. As Tim tried to fly, he fell down and hit the ground. Tim felt sad and hurt because he could not play with the ball. His mom and dad came to help him, but they knew that Tim needed more time to learn how to fly. The bad ending made Tim very sad, and he wished he could fly better.
