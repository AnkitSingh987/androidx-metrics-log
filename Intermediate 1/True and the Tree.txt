Category: Intermediate
Title: True and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a little monkey called True. True loved to bounce around his tree. One day, True was feeling particularly energetic, so he bounced even higher than usual. He bounced so high that he went over the tree and landed in a harsh desert. True was scared, so he called out for help. Suddenly, he heard a voice from above. It was an eagle! True was so relieved. The eagle said, "Do not worry, True. Just keep bouncing and you will find the way back to your tree!" True trusted the eagle, so he started to bounce. Little by little, soon True was back in his tree, safe and sound. The eagle had told him the truth, and True was thankful. From then on True was careful not to bounce so high again!

Tom and Lily are friends. They like to play together. One day, they see a big box in the yard. They want to see what is inside. "Let's try to open it," Tom says. He pulls on the lid, but it is too heavy. He grunts and pushes, but it does not move. "Can I help?" Lily asks. She is smaller than Tom, but she is strong. She joins him and they both pull and push. The lid moves a little, but not enough. They are tired and sweaty. They sit on the grass and drink some water. They look at the box and wonder what is inside. "Maybe we need a tool," Tom says. He remembers that his dad has a hammer in the garage. He runs to get it. He comes back with the hammer and a screwdriver. "Let's try again," he says. He uses the screwdriver to loosen the screws on the lid. He taps them with the hammer. He makes a lot of noise. Lily covers her ears. She does not like the noise. She waits for Tom to finish. He takes off the screws and puts them in his pocket. "Ready?" he asks. He lifts the lid with the screwdriver. Lily helps him with her hands. They open the box and look inside. They see a lot of pillows and blankets. They are soft and colorful. They smell nice. They are surprised and happy. "Wow, look at this!" Tom says. He picks up a pillow and hugs it. It is comfortable. "Maybe someone made this for us," Lily says. She picks up a blanket and wraps it around her. It is warm. They smile at each other. They have done a hard task. They are proud of themselves. They decide to make a fort with the pillows and blankets. They crawl inside and play. They have fun. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was an old man and a young boy. The old man said to the boy, "I will give you a yellow symbol if you sleep right now". The little boy was confused, so he asked, "What is a symbol?". The old man replied, “A symbol is something special. It will bring you luck". Hearing this, the boy went to his bed and fell asleep. When he woke up, he saw that the old man had left a yellow symbol on the kitchen table. He was very excited and felt very lucky. So each night, the boy would sleep, and in the morning he would find a yellow symbol on the kitchen table. The boy was so happy that he always slept soundly. He was so thankful for all the yellow symbols the old man gave him.

Anna and Ben are twins. They like to play in the garden. One day, they find a big bucket of water and some sponges. They have an idea. "Let's soak the sponges and throw them at each other!" Anna says. "OK, that sounds fun!" Ben says. They fill the sponges with water and start to toss them. They laugh and scream as they get wet. They run and hide behind the trees and the flowers. Anna sees Ben behind a rose bush. She sneaks up on him and throws a sponge at his head. Ben feels the water drip down his face. He is surprised and drops his sponge. "Got you!" Anna says. "Hey, that's not fair!" Ben says. He picks up his sponge and chases Anna. He catches her and squeezes the sponge over her hair. Anna feels the water soak her hair and her shirt. She is shocked and drops her sponge. "Got you back!" Ben says. They look at each other and start to giggle. They are both soaked and happy. They hug and say sorry. "That was fun!" Anna says. "Yes, it was!" Ben says. They hold each other's palms and walk back to the house. They are glad they have each other. They are the best twins ever.
