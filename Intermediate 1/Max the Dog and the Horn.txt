Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog and the Horn

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his toy horn. He would run around the park, horn in his mouth, and make loud honks. All the other dogs would bark and chase Max, trying to play with the horn too. One day, Max saw a big machine in the park. It was a printer! Max watched as the printer made pretty pictures. A kind man was using the printer. Max had an idea. He wanted to print a picture of his horn. Max went to the kind man and showed him the horn. The man smiled and helped Max print a picture of the horn. Max was so happy! He took the picture home and showed it to his family. They all thought it was very nice. That night, Max's mom made a delicious dinner. Max and his family sat together and ate the yummy food. Max looked at his horn picture and felt happy. He knew that he had a special day, and he would always remember the fun he had with his horn and the printer.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball in the park. One day, Max saw a little cat named Luna playing with his ball. Max was sad. He wanted his ball back. Max went to Luna and said, "Give me my ball!" This was a demand. Luna was scared, but she did not want to give the ball back. She said, "No, I want to play too!" Max and Luna did not know what to do. They both wanted to play with the ball. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's play together!" Luna liked this idea. So, Max and Luna played with the ball together in the park. They had a fun event. They became good friends and were not sad anymore. From that day on, Max and Luna always played together, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little cat. The cat liked to play with a ball. One day, the cat saw the mailman. The mailman had a bag with lots of mail. The cat wanted to help the mailman. The mailman said, "Let's number the mail together!" So, the cat and the mailman counted the mail. They counted one, two, three, and more. After they numbered the mail, the cat and the mailman gave the mail to the people. The people were happy to get their mail. The cat was happy to help. The mailman said, "Thank you, cute cat!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a long string that she loved to play with. One day, she met a boy named Max. Max saw the string and wanted to play with it too. Lily said, "I don't want to share my string. It's mine!" Max felt sad because Lily was being selfish. He wanted to play with the string too. Then, Max had an idea. He said, "Let's imagine we both have strings and we can play together!" Lily thought about it and agreed. They both played with their imaginary strings and had lots of fun. Lily learned that sharing is better than being selfish.

Once upon a time, in a great big forest, there lived a little rabbit named Remy. Remy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Remy and his friends decided to play a game. They wanted to see who could jump the highest. The winner would get a big victory. The first friend to jump was a squirrel. He jumped high, but not high enough. Next was a bird, who flew up high, but it was not a jump. Then, it was Remy's turn to jump. Remy took a deep breath, and with a snap of his strong legs, he jumped higher than any of his friends. All of Remy's friends clapped and cheered for him. Remy felt so happy and proud of his great jump. He won the victory and got a big, sweet carrot as a prize. From that day on, Remy knew that with a snap of his legs, he could do great things. And they all played happily in the big forest.
