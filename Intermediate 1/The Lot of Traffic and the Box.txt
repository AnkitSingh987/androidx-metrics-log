Category: Intermediate
Title: The Lot of Traffic and the Box

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lot of traffic. Cars, buses, and bikes all went up and down the road. The people in the town were not happy. The noise from the traffic was very loud. They could not hear each other talk. It was like they were all deaf. One day, a kind man came to the town. He saw the people were sad. He wanted to help them. He had a big box with him. Inside the box was a special display. The display showed when it was safe to cross the road. The kind man put the display by the road. The people in the town watched. They saw the display turn red and green. When it was red, they knew it was not safe to cross the road. When it was green, they knew it was safe to cross. The people were very happy. The display helped them cross the road without getting hurt by the traffic. The town was not as noisy as before, and the people could hear each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a small island. On this island, there lived a big, heavy elephant. The elephant liked to walk around the island every day. One day, while the elephant was walking, he saw a little bird. The bird was sad because it could not fly. The heavy elephant wanted to help the little bird. So, the elephant and the bird walked together around the island. They walked and walked, and soon the bird felt happy. The bird tried to fly again and this time, it could fly! The elephant and the bird became best friends, and they walked around the island together every day.

One day, a little dog named Spot was very naughty. He liked to run and jump and play. He had four legs that helped him go fast. Spot was always happy and loved to make friends. One day, Spot saw a big ball. He wanted to play with it. He ran fast to the ball, but he did not see a big hole in the ground. His leg fell in the hole and he could not move. Spot was sad and scared. He needed help to prevent being stuck forever. Then, a little cat named Fluffy saw Spot. Fluffy was not friends with Spot because cats and dogs are not always friends. But Fluffy knew she had to help. She ran to the hole and pulled Spot's leg out. Spot was so happy and surprised! From that day on, Spot and Fluffy were the best of friends. They learned that being naughty can sometimes lead to unexpected friendships.

One day, a smart cat named Sam went for a walk. Sam liked to observe things around him. He saw many sights, like big trees and little bugs. Sam saw a small bird and said, "Hello, bird! I like to look at sights. Do you like to look too?" The bird said, "Yes, I like to look at sights from high up in the sky!" As they talked, a big wind came and blew the bird far away. Sam was sad, but then he had an idea. He found a big leaf and held on tight. The wind blew again and Sam flew up high in the sky! Now, Sam and the bird could look at sights together from high up. They were both very happy and had fun looking at all the sights from the sky.

Once upon a time, in a small, humble house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat jelly. He had a big jar of jelly that he would eat every day. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you need to clean your room. It is very messy." Tim didn't want to clean his room, but he knew he had to listen to his mom. So, he cleaned his room and put all his toys away. After cleaning, Tim went to the kitchen and saw his big jar of jelly. He asked his mom, "Can I have some jelly now?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, you can have some jelly. You did a good job cleaning your room." Tim was very happy, and he enjoyed his jelly even more because he knew he had worked hard to earn it.
