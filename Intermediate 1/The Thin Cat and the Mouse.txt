Category: Intermediate
Title: The Thin Cat and the Mouse

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat was hungry and wanted to eat a mouse. So, the cat thought to prepare a trap for the mouse. The cat put some cheese in the trap and waited. The mouse saw the cheese and went near the trap. But the mouse was smart. The mouse knew the cheese was in a trap. Then, something unexpected happened. A big bird saw the cheese too. The bird took the cheese and flew away. The cat and the mouse were both surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. The cat and the mouse became friends and played together.

One day, an old lady with long hair sat on a chair. She saw a little girl playing with her toy. The old lady said, "Hi, little girl! Can I tell you a story?" The little girl looked at her and said, "Yes, please tell me a story!" The old lady started to tell a story about a big dog and a little cat who were friends. They played together and had fun. But then, something unexpected happened! The big dog found a magic hat. The magic hat could make the big dog's hair change color. The big dog's hair turned blue, then red, then green! The little cat and the big dog laughed and laughed. They loved the magic hat. The little girl listening to the story was so happy. She said, "Wow! I wish I had a magic hat too!" The old lady smiled and said, "Maybe one day, you will find a magic hat too." And as they laughed and talked, the old lady's hair started to change color, just like the big dog's hair in the story. The little girl clapped her hands and laughed, knowing that the old lady must have a magic hat too.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to climb trees and play all day. One day, Momo saw a big, tall tree and decided to climb it. As Momo climbed higher and higher, he saw a police car below. The police officer looked up and saw Momo in the tree. He called out, "Hey, funny monkey! You are very high up. Be careful!" Momo smiled and said, "I love to climb! It's so much fun!" The police officer laughed and said, "You are a very brave and funny monkey, Momo. But always remember to be safe when you climb." Momo agreed and continued to have fun climbing trees every day, always remembering to be safe.

One day, a boy named Tim went for a walk. He was eager to meet new friends. As he walked down the street, he saw a big, blue car. Tim knew it was a cop car. He wanted to meet the cop. Tim walked up to the car and saw the cop inside. The cop had a big hat and a shiny badge. Tim said, "Hi, I'm Tim. I want to be your friend." The cop smiled and said, "Hello, Tim. I'm Officer Mike." Tim and Officer Mike talked for a while. Tim told him about his toys and his favorite games. Suddenly, Tim's mom came running. She was worried and said, "Tim, you can't just walk away from home! I was so scared." Tim felt sad because he made his mom worry. Officer Mike told Tim, "It's important to stay close to your mom and not wander off." Tim said sorry to his mom and they went home. Tim didn't get to be friends with the cop and he was sad. He learned that he should always stay close to his mom and not go too far away.

One day, a girl named Sue went to play outside. She saw her friend, Tom, with some chalk. Sue asked, "Can I play too?" Tom said, "Yes, let's draw on the ground!" They drew a big open sun and a happy tree. But then, the sky got dark. Sue and Tom looked up and saw big gray clouds. Sue said, "I think it will rain soon." Tom just shrugged and said, "Maybe." They kept drawing with the chalk. Then, rain started to fall. Their chalk pictures started to wash away. Sue and Tom were sad. They had an idea. They ran under a tree to stay dry. They waited under the tree, and soon the rain stopped. The sun came out again. Sue and Tom smiled. They went back to their chalk drawings. The rain had left a big open space for new pictures. They drew a big rainbow and laughed.
