Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Healthy Youth and the Ball

One day, a healthy youth named Tom went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sam, who was also a healthy youth. Tom wanted to play a fun game with Sam. Tom had a ball with him. Tom said, "Hey Sam, let's play catch!" Sam was happy and said, "Yes, let's play!" So Tom and Sam started to throw the ball to each other. They threw the ball high and low, and they both laughed as they played. As they played, a little girl named Lily came to the park. She saw Tom and Sam playing catch and wanted to join. Tom and Sam said, "Sure, come play with us!" Now, Tom, Sam, and Lily all threw the ball together, and they had a great time. They were all happy and healthy youths playing in the park.

Once upon a time there was a young boy. His name was Billy. He was only three years old. He had a dependable belt he liked to wear. It was red and made of cotton. Billy would stretch out his belt by stretching it around his waist. This made him feel strong. Every day he would stretch his belt and around the house he went. He felt like he could do anything! But one day, Billy was running around and he accidentally stretched his belt too hard. It broke! Billy was very sad. He didn't know what to do. But then, his mom came in with a present for him. It was a brand new dependable belt. Billy put it on and smiled. He knew it would never stretch too far!

Once upon a time, there was a cute little boat. The boat loved to sail on the water. It would go up and down on the waves, and the wind would blow its sail. The boat was very happy when it was sailing. One day, the boat saw a grocery floating in the water. The grocery had many yummy things like apples, bananas, and cookies. The boat wanted to help the grocery, so it sailed closer and took the grocery on its deck. The cute little boat sailed back to the shore with the grocery. When it got there, the people were so happy to see the boat and the grocery. They clapped and cheered for the boat. The boat felt very proud and happy to help. From that day on, the boat and the people were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot was a clean dog with a hurt heel. He had a dream to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw his friend, Kitty. "Kitty, I want to play, but my heel hurts," Spot said. Kitty told Spot, "You must rest and wait for your heel to get better." Spot did not want to wait. He tried to play, but his heel hurt more. Spot felt sad. He knew he should have listened to Kitty. Days went by, and Spot rested his heel. He remembered Kitty's words and waited. Soon, his heel felt better. Spot was happy because he could play with his friends again. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and be patient.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy had a big dream. He wanted to find the most beautiful egg in the world. One day, Billy met a wise old owl. "Hello, Mr. Owl," said Billy. "Do you know where I can find the most beautiful egg?" The owl thought for a moment and said, "Yes, Billy, but it is in a dangerous place. You must be very careful." Billy was brave and wanted to find the egg. He flew to the dangerous place. He saw the egg and it was so pretty! But, a big snake was near the egg. Billy knew he had to be quick and smart. Billy waited for the right moment and grabbed the egg. The snake was angry, but Billy was too fast. He took the egg back to his nest and showed all his friends. They all agreed it was the most beautiful egg they had ever seen. Billy was so happy, his dream had come true.
