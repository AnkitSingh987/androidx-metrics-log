Category: Intermediate
Title: Max the Dog Finds the Ball

One day, a big dog named Max went to the park. Max loved to exercise and play with his ball. He was an obedient dog and always listened to his mom. While Max was playing, a sneaky cat named Tom came and said, "I will steal your ball!" Max did not want the cat to take his ball, so they started to argue. "No, it's my ball!" said Max. "But I want it," said Tom. Just then, a little girl came and saw the cat and dog fighting. She thought of a fun game they could all play together. The girl said, "Why don't we all play with the ball? We can take turns and have fun!" Max and Tom liked the idea and stopped fighting. They played together and had a great time. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy. He wanted a new toy, so he asked his mom. His mom gave him a box. The box had a handle on the top, so he carried it back to his room. Inside the box was something special, but he wasn't sure what it was. It had shiny colors that made him smile. He put it on the floor and opened the box. He could see it was a toy car. It was brand new and it was his favorite color, blue. He picked up the car and was about to play with it, but he saw something else. A small, shiny key. He put the key in the car and turned it. Suddenly, the car started to move! He pushed down the handle and the car moved all around the room. He was so excited! But then something unexpected happened. Suddenly, the car started to rot. The handle was rusted and the car broke into pieces. He was sad, but he remembered something. That new key! His mom had warned him about something that could happen with the key, but he didn't realize what it meant until now. The key was magical, and it could make new things from old things. He hugged the broken pieces of the car and smiled. He was sure it would be new and just as awesome when it was all put back together.

Lily and Tom went to camp with their mom and dad. They liked camp a lot. They slept in a big tent, played with sticks and stones, and saw many animals. One night, they saw a huge fire in the sky. It was very bright and made a loud noise. "What is that, mom?" Lily asked. "It is a rocket, sweetie. It is going to space," mom said. "Can we go to space too?" Tom asked. "Maybe one day, honey. But now we have to go to bed. It is late," dad said. Lily and Tom did not want to go to bed. They wanted to see more rockets. They waited until mom and dad fell asleep. Then they got out of the tent and ran to the fire. They thought it was fun. But the fire was not fun. It was very hot and dangerous. It was not a rocket. It was a bomb. It was made by bad people who wanted to hurt others. Lily and Tom did not know that. They got too close to the fire. The fire burned them and made them glow. They screamed and cried, but no one heard them. They died in the fire. Mom and dad never saw them again. They were very sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little squirrel named Sam. Sam loved to eat peanuts. Every day, he would find peanuts on the ground and eat them all up. Sam was a very happy squirrel. One day, Sam met a girl named Lily. Lily had a big bag of peanuts. Sam was very excited. Lily saw Sam and decided to feed him a peanut. Sam was so grateful. He ate the peanut and did a little dance. From then on, Lily would come to the park every day to feed Sam peanuts. Sam was always grateful and would do his happy dance. Lily and Sam became the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.
