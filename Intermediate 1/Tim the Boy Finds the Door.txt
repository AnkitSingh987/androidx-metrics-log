Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy Finds the Door

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. It was a cold and icy day. Tim wore a big coat to keep warm. He saw his friend, Sue, who lived next door. Sue was holding a flashlight. "Hi, Sue! What are you doing with that flashlight?" Tim asked. "I want to find my lost toy in the dark shed," Sue said. "But I can't manage to open the door." Tim wanted to help Sue. They both pushed the door together. With a big effort, they managed to open it. They went inside the dark shed. Sue turned on the flashlight and shined it around. They looked everywhere for Sue's lost toy. Finally, they found it under a box. Sue was very happy. She said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me!" Together, they left the shed and played in the icy yard with Sue's toy. They had a fun day, and Tim was glad he could help his friend.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim loved to celebrate his birthday. Every year, his mom and dad would throw a big party for him. Tim was very excited for his birthday this year. On the day of his party, Tim found a big switch on the wall. He did not know what it did. He wanted to see, so he flipped the switch. Suddenly, all the lights went off! "Oh no!" said Tim. "This is bad. What do I do now?" Then, something unexpected happened. Tim's dad came into the room with a big cake and lots of candles. When he saw the dark room, he said, "Perfect! Now we can sing 'Happy Birthday' with the candles glowing brightly." Tim realized that flipping the switch made his birthday even more special.

One day, a little rabbit named Tim wanted to prepare a party for his friends. He wanted to make it in his underground home. Tim was very happy and excited. Tim asked his friends to come to his party. His friends were a cat, a dog, a bird, and a frog. They all said yes and could not wait for the party. They knew it would be fun. On the day of the party, Tim's friends came to his underground home. They played games and ate yummy food. But then, the cat got hurt while playing. Tim and his friends helped the cat feel better. After that, they all had a great time at the party.

One day, a small boy named Sam wanted to go to the park. His mom was very busy and told him that they couldn't go. Sam began to complain, asking if they could go later in the day. His mom explained that it wouldn't be possible, as she had many things to do. Upset, Sam stomped his foot and ran outside to an old pit in the backyard. He slumped down against the side of the big pit and began to sulk. Just then, he heard something rustling around in the pit! Curious, Sam leaned over the edge and he saw a huge, brown bear. The bear gave an excited yelp and Sam immediately sat up. He was scared at first, but then he realized the bear was so gentle and kind. Sam held out his hand and the bear gave him a gentle nudge. Sam was so grateful to have a new friend that he smiled and forgot all about complaining. He and the bear stayed in the pit, playing together until the sun went down. From that day forward, Sam was never too quick to complain. He knew that he could find something to be grateful for if he looked hard enough.

One day, a big cat and a small mouse were in the house. The cat said, "Mouse, leave this house now!" The mouse was quick and harmless. The mouse did not want to leave. The cat and the mouse did not like each other. They were not friends. The cat wanted the mouse to go away. The mouse did not want to go. They began to fight. The cat was big and strong. The mouse was small and weak. The cat caught the mouse. The mouse could not run away. The cat said, "I told you to leave. Now you will be my food." The mouse cried, but it was too late. The cat ate the mouse. The story had a bad ending.
