Category: Intermediate
Title: Tim the Boy and the Loud

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy stand in his room. Tim loved to play with his toys and make loud noises. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you can play with your toys, but please don't make loud noises. Baby sister is sleeping." Tim said, "Okay, Mom. I will not be loud." Tim played with his toys at the stand. He wanted to make loud noises, but he remembered what his mom said. So, Tim played quietly. Soon, baby sister woke up and started to cry. Mom came in and said, "Good job, Tim! You were not loud. Now, I permit you to make loud noises with your toys." Tim was happy that he could play loud again. He played with his toys and made loud noises. Baby sister also liked the loud noises and stopped crying. They all had fun together.

One day, a cool cat named Tom found a map. The map was big and had many lines on it. Tom wanted to gain new friends, so he decided to follow the map. He walked and walked until he saw a big tree. Tom met a little bird named Sue under the tree. "Hi, I am Tom. I found a map and it brought me here. Do you want to be my friend?" Tom asked. Sue said, "Yes, I want to be your friend! Let's follow the map together and find more friends." As they walked, they saw a big box. They were very surprised. Tom and Sue opened the box and found many toys inside. They played with the toys and had a lot of fun. They didn't need the map anymore because they had each other and the toys. Tom and Sue were happy to have gained a new friend and a box of toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy named Mr. Bear. They liked to play all day. Lily was calm and happy with Mr. Bear. One day, Lily found a magic wand. She waved the wand at Mr. Bear. Mr. Bear began to shrink! He became very small. Lily was sad because she could not play with Mr. Bear now. Lily had an idea. She waved the wand at herself. Lily started to shrink too! Now, she was small like Mr. Bear. They played together and had a great time. After a while, Lily waved the wand again, and they both grew big. They played together happily ever after.

Lily and Max liked to play in the backyard after dinner. They had a tent, a flashlight and some toys. They pretended they were camping in the woods. One night, it started to rain. Lily and Max ran inside the tent. They turned on the flashlight and made funny faces. But then they heard a loud noise. It was a big, hairy dog. The dog was wet and hungry. It smelled the food in the tent. It ran towards the tent and tried to get in. Lily and Max were scared. They screamed for help. Mom and Dad heard them and came outside. They saw the dog and the tent. They grabbed a broom and a hose. They shooed the dog away with the broom. They sprayed the dog with the hose. The dog was soaked and ran away. Lily and Max were safe and happy. Mom and Dad hugged them and took them inside. They dried them off and gave them hot cocoa. They said they were brave and smart. They said they could play in the tent again, but only when it was sunny. Lily and Max agreed. They said they loved Mom and Dad. They said they did not like big, hairy dogs.

Once upon a time there was a brother and sister who were best friends. They were full of energy and always very excited to gather and play together. One day, the brother thought of a great game to play. He said to his sister, "Let's gather all the cushions in the house and build a fort in the living room!". The sister was so excited and they both began to gather. But soon they became very messy by knocking things over as they raced around collecting cushions. When they were finished their fort was very big and messy, but it was also quiet lot of fun. Together they laughed and played in their cushion fort until they fell asleep.
