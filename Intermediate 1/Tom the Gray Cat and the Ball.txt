Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Gray Cat and the Ball

Once upon a time, there was a gray cat named Tom. Tom had a friend, a bald bird named Bob. They lived in a big yard with lots of trees and grass. Tom and Bob liked to play and run around in the yard. One day, Tom and Bob found a big ball. They both wanted to own the ball. Tom said, "I want the ball!" Bob said, "No, I want the ball!" They both tried to grab the ball at the same time, but they could not agree on who should own it. They decided to ask their friend, Sam the dog, to help them. Sam said, "Why don't you both play with the ball together?" Tom and Bob looked at each other and smiled. They realized that they could both own the ball and share it. So, they played with the ball together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Tom and Bob always shared their toys and played together happily.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy sail. He liked to play with it in the water. The sail was red and blue. Tim liked to watch it go. One day, Tim took his sail to the park. He put it in the water. The water was rough. The sail went far away. Tim could not reach it. He was sad. A big dog saw Tim. The dog was nice. It swam to the sail. The dog reached the sail and brought it back to Tim. Tim was happy. He said, "Thank you, dog." They played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was very playful and always liked to joke around. Today, Tim's mother took him to the market. She wanted to buy some things for their house. Tim wanted to go very quickly and kept asking his mother if they were done yet. He was impatient! But when they were in the market, Tim had a lot of fun playing with the different things there was for sale. He was so happy that he started joking and being silly. He made everyone in the market laugh! Even his mother couldn't help but laugh. When it was time to leave, Tim was sad but also very proud of himself. He had made everyone happy and showed them how much fun it is to joke around.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. One day, his mom made a big plate of spaghetti for dinner. Tim was very happy. As Tim sat down to eat, his mom warned him, "Be careful, Tim. It is dark outside, and you might spill your spaghetti if you go out to play." But Tim didn't listen. He took his plate and went outside to play with his friends. While playing, Tim tripped and spilled all his spaghetti on the ground. He was very sad. His friends saw what happened and said, "You should have listened to your mom, Tim." Tim learned that it is important to listen to warnings from others.

Once upon a time, there was an unusual candle. It was said that it brought good luck to anyone who held it. One day, a little girl called Susie decided to find out. "Let's take the candle, Susie!" her mum said. Susie reached out and grabbed it. Its warm light made it feel nice in her hands. Just then, a gentle voice said, "Surrender the candle". Susie and her mum looked around, but couldn't see anyone. Again the voice said, "Surrender the candle". Susie looked at her mum confused. She didn't know what surrender meant. "It means give something back," her mum said. So Susie took a deep breath, and gave the candle back. The next day, Susie woke up to find a box of chocolates by her bed. Susie laughed and said, "Mum, I think the candle brought us good luck!" They both enjoyed the chocolates and Susie thanked the candle for its help.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had an adorable dog named Max. Lily and Max loved to play together. One day, they found a big book in the house. The book had many pictures and words. Lily wanted to read the book, but Max wanted to play. Lily said to Max, "Let's read the book first, then we can play." Max didn't want to wait. He jumped and knocked the book out of Lily's hands. The book fell on the floor and opened to a page with a picture of a big cup. Just then, it started to pour rain outside. Lily and Max looked out the window and saw the rain coming down. Max felt sad because he couldn't play outside now. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's read the book together and learn about the rain." Max agreed, and they both sat down to read the book. They learned about the rain and had a fun time reading together. After the rain stopped, Lily and Max went outside to play. They were happy because they had learned something new and still got to play together.
