Category: Intermediate
Title: Tom the Bossy Cat and the Bird

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to sit by the window and watch the birds outside. One day, he saw a little bird sitting on a tree branch. Tom wanted to catch the bird, so he opened the window and jumped out. "Hey, little bird, I am going to catch you!" Tom said. The little bird was scared and flew away. Tom chased the bird, but he could not catch it. He was sad and tired. Then, he met a big dog named Max. Max saw Tom and said, "Why are you so sad, bossy cat?" Tom told Max about the little bird he wanted to catch. Max had an idea. "Let's work together to catch the bird," Max said. Tom agreed, and they became friends. Max and Tom tried to catch the bird together, but the little bird was too fast for them. Then, something unexpected happened. The little bird came back and said, "Why are you trying to catch me? I just want to be friends." Tom and Max were surprised, but they liked the idea. So, the bossy cat, the big dog, and the little bird became friends and played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Anna was a gifted girl. She could do many things that other kids could not. She could read big books, play the piano, and draw beautiful pictures. But her favorite thing to do was to fly. She had a special furniture in her room. It was a big chair with wings. She called it her flying chair. She liked to sit on it and pretend that she was flying in the sky. She would imagine that she was a bird, or a fairy, or a superhero. One day, she decided to try something new. She wanted to fly outside her window. She put on her helmet and her goggles, and she tied a rope to her chair. She opened the window and pushed her chair out. She held on to the rope and jumped after it. She felt the wind in her hair and the sun on her face. She saw the trees, the houses, and the cars below. She waved to the birds and the clouds. She was so happy and free. She flew higher and higher, until she reached the stars. She looked at the stars and wondered what they were. She wanted to touch them and talk to them. She let go of the rope and reached out her hand. But then, she realized that she had made a mistake. She had no way to get back to her chair. She started to fall. She screamed and cried. She was scared and sorry. She wished she had not been so silly. She wished she had stayed in her room. She closed her eyes and waited for the end. But then, she felt something soft and warm. She opened her eyes and saw that she was in her bed. She looked around and saw that she was in her room. She saw her flying chair, her books, her piano, and her pictures. She realized that it was all a dream. She was relieved and grateful. She hugged her teddy bear and said a prayer. She learned a lesson. She decided that she would never fly outside her window again. She decided that she would only fly in her imagination. She decided that she was still a gifted girl, but also a wise one.

One day, a little girl named Amy went outside to play. It was a cold day, but she wore her warm coat. She saw a small drain near her house. She heard a tiny voice coming from the drain. "Help me, please!" said the voice. Amy looked down and saw a little frog. The frog was cold and wet. Amy wanted to help the frog. She reached down and picked him up. Amy took the frog inside her house. She made a warm bed for him. The frog said, "Thank you, Amy!" They played and laughed all day. At the end of the day, the frog was warm and happy. Amy was happy too because she made a new friend.
