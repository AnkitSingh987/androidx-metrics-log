Category: Intermediate
Title: Lily the Girl and the Cookie

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, ugly apron that she loved to wear. The apron was green and had a big pocket in the front. Lily liked to wear her apron when she helped her mom in the kitchen. One day, Lily wanted to help her mom make cookies. She put on her ugly apron and started to mix the cookie dough. But the dough was very sticky, and Lily started to struggle. She tried to mix it with a spoon, but it was too hard. Lily's mom saw her struggle and came to help. She showed Lily how to mix the dough with her hands. They both laughed as they made a big mess in the kitchen. When the cookies were done, they were the best cookies ever. And Lily was very proud of her ugly apron.

Once upon a time, in a small world, there lived a little boy named Tim and his friend, a talking bird named Bob. They loved to play and make things together. One day, they decided to make a big cake for everyone in the world. "Let's make the cake sweet and yummy!" said Tim. But Bob wanted to make a joke. "Let's make the cake disgusting!" said Bob, and he put mud and bugs in the cake mix. They baked the cake and shared it with everyone. When people ate the cake, they said, "Yuck! This cake is disgusting!" Tim felt sad and Bob felt sorry. They learned that making jokes can hurt others. So, they made a new cake, a sweet and yummy one, and everyone was happy. The moral of the story is to be kind and think about how your actions can affect others.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a messy room. He did not like to clean. His mom always asked him to put his toys away. Tim did not want to do it. So, he thought of a plan. He asked his friend, the talking palm tree, for help. The palm tree was very kind. It said, "I will help you, Tim. But you must promise to keep your room clean." Tim agreed. The palm tree used its big leaves to clean Tim's room. It was not messy anymore. But then, something unexpected happened. The palm tree got very tired. It could not talk or help Tim anymore. Tim felt sad. He realized that he should clean his room by himself. From that day on, Tim always kept his room clean and took care of his friend, the palm tree.

Once upon a time, a little flea named Tim lived in a big, broken tree. Tim liked to jump and play all day long. One day, he saw a big ant walking by the tree. Tim thought, "I want to see where the ant goes!" So, he decided to follow the ant. Tim jumped and jumped, following the ant as it walked through the grass. They went past a small pond, where a frog sat on a log. The ant stopped at a big hill. Tim was very tired from all the jumping, but he was happy he had followed the ant. But then, something unexpected happened! The big hill started to move! Tim and the ant were surprised. The big hill was not a hill at all – it was a giant turtle! The turtle looked at Tim and the ant and said, "Hello, little friends! Do you want a ride?" Tim and the ant smiled, and they climbed on the turtle's back. They had a fun day riding around with their new, big friend.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a pretty bird and said, "I admire you, bird." The bird was happy and flew to Tim. The bird said, "I have a quiz for you, Tim. If you answer it right, I will give you a tasty treat." Tim was excited and said, "Okay, I will try." The bird asked, "What is the best thing to give to others?" Tim thought and thought. Then he said, "Love and care!" The bird clapped its wings and said, "Yes, Tim! You are right!" The bird gave Tim a tasty cookie. Tim shared the cookie with the bird. They both were happy. The moral of the story is to always share love, care, and treats with others.
