Category: Intermediate
Title: Mommy and the Sun

The sun was shining in the sky and all the birds were singing. Mommy, Daddy and their little three-year-old daughter were having a picnic in the park. The family sat together and enjoyed their sandwiches and snacks. They were playing games and laughing. Then, suddenly, Daddy saw something. He pointed to the sky and said, “Oh no, it’s a storm coming!” Mommy and Daddy quickly gathered all their things and put them in a bag. Daddy said to his daughter, “We have to hurry and go home now.” The little girl was worried. But Daddy said, “Don’t worry, I’ll protect you.” The family ran quickly and made it home just as the rain started. Daddy got the grill out and put it over their windows to protect them from the rain. The family was safe now, but they were still worried. So, Daddy said, “It’s alright now, we’ll stay here until the storm is over.” The family was safe and once again they were all laughing and smiling.

Once upon a time, there was a pink car. The car had a gear. The gear helped the car go fast or slow. One day, the car met a big truck. The truck was blue. The pink car said, "Hello, big blue truck! Can you help me open my door? I can't open it." The blue truck said, "Yes, I can help you, little pink car." The blue truck used its big wheel to open the car's door. Now, the pink car could go fast or slow with its gear. The pink car and the blue truck became good friends. They played together every day. They had lots of fun, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoom. Zoom loved to go fast. He would race with his friends all day long. Zoom was always happy when he could go fast. One day, Zoom's mom told him that he needed to be careful. She said that he should not go too fast when he was near the big road. Zoom listened to his mom and promised to be careful. Zoom played with his friends, but he remembered to remain slow and careful near the big road. He did not want to get in trouble. Zoom learned that being careful was important, and he could still have fun with his friends. And they all played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside. One day, the sun was shining, and Spot went out to play. Spot saw a big puddle on the ground. He ran to the puddle and jumped in it. The water splashed all around, and Spot was very happy. Soon, Spot's friend, a cat named Fluffy, arrived. Fluffy saw Spot playing in the puddle. Fluffy decided to join Spot, and they both played in the puddle together. They had lots of fun and were the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. It was a hot day, and Sue felt very warm. She saw a man with a cart. The man had icy cream. Sue wanted some icy cream to cool down. Sue asked her mom, "Can I have some icy cream, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can have some." Sue was very happy. She went to the man with the cart and asked, "Can I have some icy cream?" The man said, "Of course! What flavor do you want?" Sue picked her favorite flavor, which was pink. She got her icy cream and thanked the man. Sue and her mom sat on a bench and enjoyed their icy cream together. Sue felt much cooler and happier after eating her icy cream.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw his friend, Sam, with some equipment. Sam had a ball, a bat, and a glove. They wanted to play a game to be healthy and have fun. "Let's play catch," said Sam. Tim and Sam threw the ball back and forth. They laughed and had a great time. After a while, they got tired and wanted to try something new. Tim said, "Let's not quit playing. Let's try the bat now." They took turns hitting the ball with the bat. They played until the sun went down. They were happy and healthy from playing all day.
