Title: My School Bag
Category: Beginner

This is a story about a bag. It is not an ordinary bag. It is my school bag. I carry it with me five days a week. It goes everywhere I go on a school day. 

My school bag is blue and grey. It has two soft straps. The straps go over my shoulders. It has three zippers. The zippers open three different pockets. A small pocket on the front, a middle pocket, and a very big pocket at the back. 

Every morning, I must lift my bag. I put it on my back. It is always heavy. Sometimes, it is very, very heavy. My mother says, "Your bag is too heavy. You will hurt your back." But I need everything inside it. 

What is inside my heavy school bag? Let us look inside. 

First, let us open the big pocket at the back. Zzzzip. 
This is the heaviest part of the bag. Here, I keep my school books. I have many thick books. I have a big red Mathematics book. I have a blue English book. I have a green Science book. I also have three thin notebooks. One notebook has lines for writing. One notebook has squares for math. And one is blank for drawing. 
Also in the big pocket is my homework folder. It is a yellow plastic folder. I keep my finished homework inside it. I do not want my homework to get dirty or bent. The yellow folder keeps it safe and flat. 

Now, let us close the big pocket and open the middle pocket. Zzzzip. 
This pocket is for my food. School is a long day. I get hungry. 
I have a lunch box in this pocket. My lunch box is green. Inside the lunch box, there is a sandwich, an apple, and a small bag of chips. My mother makes my sandwich every morning. Usually, it is a cheese and tomato sandwich. 
Next to the lunch box is my water bottle. It is a silver metal bottle. It keeps my water cold all day. I drink a lot of water after I play football at recess. 
Sometimes, I find an old cookie crumb in the bottom of this pocket. I need to clean it more often. 

Finally, let us open the small pocket on the front. Zzzzip. 
This pocket is for my small, important things. 
Here is my pencil case. It is a black pencil case. It holds my tools for learning. I open the pencil case. Inside, I have three sharp lead pencils. I have two blue pens and one red pen. I have a pink eraser to fix my mistakes. I have a small sharpener to make my pencils sharp again. And I have a short plastic ruler to draw straight lines. 

Also in the small front pocket, I have some other things. I have a packet of tissues. If I sneeze, I need a tissue. 
I have a small key. It is the key to my house. I wear it on a string, but sometimes I keep it in the bag. 
And at the very bottom of the small pocket, there is a secret. It is a small plastic dinosaur. It is a green T-Rex. I know I am ten years old, but I like my small dinosaur. Sometimes, when the class is boring, I put my hand in my pocket and touch the dinosaur. It makes me smile. It is my secret friend. 

I close all three zippers. My bag is packed. It is ready. 
My bag is like a small house. It holds everything I need for my day. It holds my work, my food, and my tools. 
When it rains, my bag keeps my books dry. The blue and grey material is strong. 
When I run for the bus, my bag bounces on my back. It says, 'Thump, thump, thump.' 

At the end of the day, I carry my bag home. It is still heavy. But when I get home, I take the bag off my shoulders. Ah, that feels good. I drop the bag on the floor in the hallway. It makes a loud 'thud' sound. 
My bag rests on the floor all evening. It sleeps while I eat dinner and watch television. 
But the next morning, I will pack it again. I will put it on my back again. My heavy, blue and grey school bag is my best helper. I cannot go to school without it.
