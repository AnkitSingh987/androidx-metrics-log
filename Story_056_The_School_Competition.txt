Title: The School Competition
Category: Beginner

It is the month of May. The weather is warm and sunny. At Riverdale Primary School, the students are very excited. Every May, the school holds a big art competition. All the children from the different classes can enter the competition. They must paint a picture of their favorite place in the city. 

Lucy is nine years old. She loves to paint. She has a big box of paints at home, and she uses them every weekend. When her teacher, Mr. Green, told the class about the competition, Lucy knew she wanted to join. 

"What are you going to paint, Lucy?" her friend Emma asked her during the lunch break. 
"I am going to paint the big park in the center of the city," Lucy answered. "I love the big oak tree and the small duck pond. It is my favorite place."
"That sounds beautiful," Emma said. "I am going to paint the public library. I love the tall windows."

That afternoon, Lucy went home and started her painting. She worked very hard. She used bright green paint for the grass and the leaves on the big oak tree. She used light blue paint for the sky and the duck pond. She even painted two small brown ducks swimming in the water. 

She worked on her painting for three days. When she finished, she was very proud of it. The colors were bright, and the picture looked very peaceful. 
On Friday morning, she carefully carried her painting to school. She gave it to Mr. Green. 
"This is a lovely painting, Lucy," Mr. Green said. He smiled and put it on a large table with all the other paintings. There were many beautiful pictures. Some students painted the bridge, some painted the tall buildings, and some painted the train station. 

The headmaster of the school, Mr. Davis, was the judge of the competition. He looked at all the paintings on Friday afternoon. He walked around the table slowly. He looked at the colors, and he looked at the details. The children watched him through the window of the classroom. They were very nervous. 

"I hope I win," a boy named Tom whispered. "The first prize is a new set of professional paints."
Lucy wanted the new paints too. Her old paints were almost finished. But she knew that many students painted beautiful pictures. Winning would be very difficult. 

On Monday morning, the whole school gathered in the big hall. It was time to announce the winners of the art competition. 
Mr. Davis stood on the stage. He held a microphone in his hand. Next to him was a table with three prizes: a big set of paints, a medium set of paints, and a small set of drawing pencils. 

"Good morning, students," Mr. Davis said. His voice was loud and clear. "Thank you all for joining the art competition. We had over fifty paintings this year. They were all wonderful. You all worked very hard, and you should be proud."
The hall was completely silent. The students waited for the names. 

"The third prize goes to... Tom, for his painting of the train station!" Mr. Davis announced. 
Everyone clapped. Tom walked up to the stage with a big smile and took the drawing pencils. 
"The second prize goes to... Emma, for her painting of the library!" 
Emma gasped. She ran up to the stage and took the medium set of paints. Lucy clapped very hard for her friend. She was so happy for Emma. 

"And now, for the first prize," Mr. Davis said. He paused for a moment. He looked at the paper in his hand. "The first prize goes to... Lucy, for her beautiful painting of the park!"

Lucy could not believe it. Did he say her name? 
"Go on, Lucy!" Emma whispered, pushing her shoulder gently. "You won!"
Lucy stood up. Her legs were shaking a little bit. She walked down the aisle and up the steps to the stage. She felt very hot, but she was smiling a massive smile. 
Mr. Davis shook her hand. "Congratulations, Lucy. Your painting is full of light and happiness. You are a very talented young artist."

He handed her the big box of professional paints. The box was heavy and shiny. It looked wonderful. 
"Thank you very much, Mr. Davis," Lucy said into the microphone. Her voice was quiet, but everyone heard her. 
The whole school clapped and cheered. 

After the assembly, Mr. Green put the three winning paintings on the wall in the main hallway. Everyone could see them. 
Lucy looked at her painting of the oak tree and the duck pond. She looked at the new box of paints in her hands. 
She did not win because she was lucky. She won because she loved painting, and she tried her very best. She knew exactly what she was going to do when she went home. She was going to open her new box of paints and start a brand new picture.
