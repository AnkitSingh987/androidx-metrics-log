Title: The debate over economic data Part Three

The debate over economic data

The low IMF grade was mainly because of the delay in rebasing. The IMF tends to be tougher on emerging markets  -  China also got a C. But the challenges they face, including frequent external shocks in the last decade, need to be acknowledged. Constructive suggestions that recognise the magnitude of the task are welcome but it is unfair to attack a professional cadre, and their standards of probity, in order to score political points.

By Ashima Goyal The writer was a member of the previous MPC
