Title: OnTime Every Time Tagline IndiGo cant hide Part Three

'On-Time, Every Time': Tagline IndiGo can't hide behind

Right now, IndiGo must prove that its famous tagline was more than clever marketing. "On-Time, Every Time" needs to be more than a nostalgic echo of better days. In the eyes of the travelling public, IndiGo's real flight path to credibility, to trust, to recovery begins not on the runway but in the way it speaks, apologises, and reconnects with the people who made it the airline it is today.

Until then, the crisis continues, not just in airports and departure halls, but in the way an entire nation perceives a brand that once defined efficiency and today stands exposed in silence. By Chaitanya K Prasad The writer is a former civil servant, who writes on cinema and strategic communication. With inputs from Zoya Ahmad and Vaishnavie Srinivasan; views are personal
