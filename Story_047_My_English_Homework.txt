Title: My English Homework
Category: Beginner

It is Wednesday evening. Dinner is finished. Tom is in his bedroom. He is sitting at his desk. He has a lot of homework tonight, but he wants to start with his English homework. English is his favorite subject. 

Tom opens his school bag. He takes out his green English folder. He opens the folder and takes out a piece of paper. This is his homework for tomorrow. 
At the top of the paper, it says 'Reading Comprehension'. 
Under the title, there is a short story. Under the story, there are five questions. Tom must read the story and answer the questions. 

Tom takes a deep breath. He starts to read. 
He reads slowly. He wants to understand every word. 
The story is about a boy named Jack. Jack lives on a farm. He has a dog named Spot. Spot is a black and white dog. Every morning, Jack and Spot walk in the fields. They look at the cows and the sheep. Jack likes the farm, but he wants to visit the big city one day. 

Tom finishes reading the story. It is a simple story. He understands it very well. He smiles. 
Now, he looks at the questions. 
Question one: 'Where does Jack live?'
Tom looks back at the story. He finds the answer in the second sentence. 
He takes his blue pen. He writes his answer on the empty line. He writes a full sentence. 
'Jack lives on a farm.'

He looks at question two: 'What is the name of Jack's dog?'
Tom remembers this. But he looks at the story again to be sure. He wants to spell the name correctly. 
He writes his answer. 
'The name of Jack's dog is Spot.'

He looks at question three: 'What color is the dog?'
Tom writes the answer. 'The dog is black and white.'

He looks at question four: 'What animals do Jack and Spot look at in the fields?'
Tom looks at the middle of the story. He finds the words 'cows' and 'sheep'. 
He writes his answer. 'They look at the cows and the sheep.'

Now, there is only one question left. Question five. 
Question five: 'Does Jack want to live on the farm forever?'
Tom stops. He must think about this question. The story does not use the word 'forever'. 
He reads the last sentence of the story again. 'Jack likes the farm, but he wants to visit the big city one day.'
If he wants to visit the city, maybe he does not want to live on the farm forever. 

Tom writes his answer carefully. 
'No, he wants to visit the big city one day.'

Tom puts his pen down. He is finished. But he is not ready to put the paper away. A good student always checks his work. 
He reads his answers from the beginning. 
'Jack lives on a farm.' The spelling is correct. The sentence has a capital letter at the start and a full stop at the end. 
'The name of Jack's dog is Spot.' Correct. 
'The dog is black and white.' Correct. 
'They look at the cows and the sheep.' Correct. 
'No, he wants to visit the big city one day.' Correct. 

Tom is very happy. His English homework is completely finished, and he thinks there are no mistakes. He feels proud. 
He puts the piece of paper back into his green folder. He puts the green folder back into his school bag. 
He still has math homework to do. Math is not his favorite subject. It is difficult for him. But he feels strong now. He finished his English homework perfectly. He is ready to try the difficult math. He takes his red math book from his bag and opens it.
