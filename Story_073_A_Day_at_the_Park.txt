Title: A Day at the Park
Category: Beginner

It is a beautiful Saturday morning in early June. The sky is bright blue, and there are no clouds. The sun is shining, but it is not too hot. A gentle wind is blowing through the trees. It is the perfect day to go to the park. 

Ten-year-old Ben and his younger sister, Mia, are very excited. Mia is six years old. They run out of their house and wait for their father. 
"Hurry up, Dad!" Ben shouts. "We want to go to the park!"
Their father comes out of the house. He is carrying a blue backpack. 
"I am coming, I am coming," he laughs. "I had to pack some water and snacks. Playing at the park is thirsty work."

They walk down the street together. The park is only five minutes away from their house. As they get closer, they can hear the sounds of children laughing and shouting. 
They walk through the big iron gates of the park. The park is very large. There is a lot of green grass. There are tall trees that provide cool shade. In the middle of the park, there is a big playground. 

"Can we go to the swings?" Mia asks immediately. She loves the swings more than anything else. 
"Yes, you can go to the playground," their father says. "I will sit on this bench under the tree. I can see you from here."

Ben and Mia run across the grass to the playground. The playground is full of bright colors. There are red swings, a yellow slide, and a blue climbing frame. The ground is covered with soft rubber to keep the children safe if they fall. 

Mia runs straight to the red swings. There is one empty swing. She jumps onto the black seat. 
"Ben, push me! Please push me!" she calls out. 
Ben walks behind her. He puts his hands on her back and pushes gently. Mia swings forward. 
"Higher!" she shouts. 
Ben pushes harder. Mia swings higher into the air. Her hair flies behind her. She kicks her legs towards the blue sky. 
"I am flying like a bird!" she laughs loudly. 

Ben pushes her for a few minutes. Then, he wants to do something else. 
"Okay, Mia, you can swing by yourself now," he says. "I want to go on the big slide."
Ben walks over to the yellow slide. It is a very tall slide. He has to climb a long ladder to get to the top. He grips the metal bars tightly and climbs up, step by step. 

When he reaches the top, he looks down. The ground looks far away. He feels a little flutter in his stomach, but he is not scared. He is excited. 
He sits down on the smooth yellow plastic. He pushes himself forward. 
"Wheeee!" he shouts as he slides down. He goes very fast. He reaches the bottom in two seconds and lands on his feet with a soft 'thud'. 
It is so much fun that he runs immediately back to the ladder to do it again. 

While Ben is on the slide and Mia is on the swing, their father watches them from the bench. He smiles. He likes seeing his children happy and active. 

After half an hour, Ben and Mia are tired. They run back to the bench. Their faces are red, and they are breathing heavily. 
"Dad, we are very thirsty," Ben says. 
Their father opens the blue backpack. He takes out two bottles of cold water. He gives one to Ben and one to Mia. 
They drink the water quickly. It feels very cold and refreshing. 

"Do you want to play a game now?" their father asks. He takes a white and black football out of the backpack. 
"Yes!" Ben says. "Let's play football on the grass."

They walk to a large, empty area of green grass. They set up two small bags as the goalposts. 
Ben and his father play against each other. Mia tries to kick the ball too, but she mostly just runs around and laughs. 
Ben kicks the ball hard. His father tries to catch it, but he misses. The ball goes between the two bags. 
"Goal!" Ben shouts, jumping up and down. "I scored a goal!"
"Good shot, Ben," his father says, clapping his hands. 

They play football until they are all very tired and very hot. They fall down on the soft green grass and look up at the sky. A day at the park is full of running, jumping, and playing. It is the best way to spend a sunny Saturday morning with your family.
