Title: My First Library Card
Category: Beginner

My name is Anna. I am eight years old. I love to read books. I have many books in my bedroom. But I have read all of them. I want to read new books. 

Today is Saturday. My mother has a surprise for me. 
"Anna," she says after breakfast. "Put on your coat. We are going to a special place."
"Where are we going?" I ask. 
"We are going to the public library in the city," she says. "And today, you are going to get your very own library card."

I am very excited! A library card is like a magic key. With a library card, you can borrow any book in the library for free. I quickly put on my red coat and my shoes. 
My mother and I walk to the bus stop. We take the bus to the center of the city. 
The public library is a very big building. It is made of grey stone. It has wide steps in front. We walk up the steps and go through the big glass doors. 

Inside, it is very beautiful. The ceiling is high. There are thousands of books. There are many people walking quietly or sitting in comfortable chairs. 
"Where do we go?" I whisper to my mother. 
"We go to the front desk," she whispers back. 

We walk to a long, wooden desk. A man is sitting there. He has a friendly face. 
"Good morning," my mother says to the man. "My daughter would like to get a library card today."
The man smiles at me. "Good morning," he says. "How old are you?"
"I am eight years old," I say proudly. 
"That is a perfect age for a library card," he says. 

The man takes a piece of paper from his desk. It is a form. 
"I need you to fill out this form," he says to my mother. "I need your name, your address, and your phone number. And I need Anna's name."
My mother takes a pen. She writes our address on the paper. She writes my name in big letters: A - N - N - A. 
She signs her name at the bottom. She gives the paper back to the man. 

The man looks at the paper. "Thank you," he says. Then he looks at me. "Now, Anna. Can you write your own name?"
"Yes, I can," I say. 
He gives me a small piece of thick paper. It is the library card! It is yellow and has a barcode on it. There is a white line on the back. 
"Please write your name on the white line," he says. "Use your best handwriting."

I take the pen from him. I hold it carefully. I write 'Anna' on the white line. I write it very neatly. 
"Excellent," the man says. "Now, this is your card. You are a member of the library. You can borrow five books at a time. You can keep them for three weeks. But remember, you must bring them back on time."
"I promise," I say. 

I hold the yellow card in my hand. It feels very important. It has my name on it. It is mine. 
"Now," my mother says. "Do you want to find some books?"
"Yes!" I say. 

We walk to the children's section. It is a big area with colourful shelves and small tables. There are so many books! 
I look at the picture books. I look at the animal books. I look at the story books. 
I choose three books. I choose a book about a cat. I choose a book about space. I choose a book with funny poems. 

I carry the three books to the front desk. My mother walks behind me. 
I put the books on the desk. I give the man my new yellow library card. 
The man smiles again. He takes my card. He scans the barcode on my card. Beep! 
Then he scans the barcode on the first book. Beep! 
He scans the second book. Beep! 
He scans the third book. Beep! 

He gives me back my yellow card. He gives me the three books. 
"There you go, Anna," he says. "Enjoy your reading."
"Thank you very much," I say. 

I put my new library card safely into my small purse. I carry my three new books in my arms. They are a little heavy, but I do not mind. 
My mother and I walk out of the big library. We walk down the wide steps. We go to catch the bus home. 
I am very happy. Today is a great day. I have three new books to read. And I have my very own library card. I can come back to this magic place again and again.
