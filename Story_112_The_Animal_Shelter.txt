Title: The Animal Shelter
Category: Beginner

The drive to the local animal shelter took twenty minutes. It was located in an industrial area on the edge of the city. As their car pulled into the small parking lot, Leo and Maya could already hear the noise. Even through the closed car windows, the sound of dozens of dogs barking was very loud. 

They got out of the car and walked towards a long, low brick building. A large sign above the glass door read 'City Animal Rescue Center'. 
When they pushed the door open, the noise was almost deafening. It was a chaotic mix of deep, booming barks and high-pitched yelps. The smell inside the building was a strong mixture of wet dog fur and cleaning chemicals. 

A young woman in a blue uniform stood behind a reception desk. She smiled warmly when she saw the family. 
"Hello," she said loudly over the noise. "Can I help you?"
"Yes, please," their father replied. "We are looking to adopt a dog today."
"That is wonderful news," the woman said. "Have you ever owned a dog before?"
"Not as a family," their mother answered. "We are looking for a medium-sized dog, or perhaps a puppy, that is good with children."

"I have exactly the right area for you to look at," the woman said, coming out from behind the desk. "Please follow me. I will show you the family kennels."
She led them down a long hallway. On both sides of the hallway, there were large cages with strong metal bars. Inside almost every cage was a dog. 

As the family walked down the aisle, the dogs reacted differently. Some dogs ran to the front of their cages, wagging their tails furiously and barking for attention. They pushed their wet noses against the metal bars, hoping to be touched. Other dogs were very shy. They sat quietly in the back corners of their cages, watching the people with sad, nervous eyes. 

Maya looked at a large, black dog that was barking loudly. "He is very noisy," she whispered to her brother. 
"He is just excited," Leo replied, but he felt a little overwhelmed by the noise and the smell. It was sad to see so many animals locked in cages, waiting for someone to choose them. 

The woman in the blue uniform stopped in front of a section of smaller cages. 
"These are our younger dogs and puppies," she explained. "They have a lot of energy, so they need a family that will play with them and train them properly."

Leo and Maya looked into the first cage. There were three small, fluffy white puppies sleeping in a pile of blankets. They looked incredibly cute, but they were fast asleep. 
They moved to the next cage. Inside, a medium-sized dog with light brown fur and floppy ears was jumping up and down excitedly. 
"Oh, look at this one!" Maya pointed. 
The dog ran to the metal bars and licked Maya's fingers through the gaps. Its tail was wagging so fast that its entire body was shaking. 

"He is very friendly," the woman noted, looking at the card on the cage. "He is about six months old. He was found wandering on the streets last month. We call him Barnaby. Would you like to meet him outside the cage?"
"Yes, please!" Leo and Maya said at the exact same time. 
"Okay," their father agreed. "Let's see how he interacts with us in a quiet space."
The woman smiled, took a green leash from her pocket, and unlocked the heavy metal door. The real introduction was about to begin.
