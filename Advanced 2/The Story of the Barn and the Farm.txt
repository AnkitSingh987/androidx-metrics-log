Once upon a time, there was a beautiful red barn on a big farm. The barn was home to many animals. There was a cow named Daisy, a pig named Pinky, and a horse named Harry. One day, Daisy said to Pinky and Harry, "We need to prevent the rain from coming into the barn." Pinky and Harry agreed. They all worked together to stop the rain. They put big bags near the door. After they finished, they all looked at their work. "We did it!" said Harry. "Now, the rain can't come into our beautiful barn." Daisy, Pinky, and Harry were happy. They knew they were safe and dry in their beautiful red barn.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a guitar. He was very proud of his guitar. He played the guitar every day. One day, Tim saw a big box. The box had a picture of a spring on it. Tim was very curious about the box. Tim asked his mom, "What's in the box?" His mom said, "It's a surprise, but you need to wait until tomorrow." Tim was excited but also a little sad. He wanted to know what was inside the box right now. The next day, Tim opened the box. Inside, there was a big toy! It was a jumping toy with a spring. Tim was very happy. He played with the jumping toy all day long. But then, something unexpected happened. The jumping toy broke his proud guitar! Tim was sad, but he learned that he needed to be more careful with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She had a big key. It was shiny and silver. She wanted to play with her key. She put her key in a bucket of water. She watched it soak. She liked watching it getting wet. It was gentle. Sarah took her key out and dried it with a towel. She held it tight and smiled. She felt happy when she saw her dry key. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a best friend named Tom. They liked to play together every day. They played with their toys, ran in the park, and laughed a lot. One day, Mia found a spicy pepper in the kitchen. She didn't know it was spicy. Mia took a big bite and it was very hot! Mia started to weep because her mouth was on fire. Tom saw Mia crying and wanted to help her. Tom gave Mia some cold water to drink. The cold water made Mia feel better. Mia stopped weeping and smiled at Tom. They knew they would always help each other. Mia and Tom stayed best friends forever.

One day, a lively cat named Tom went to the shop. He wanted to help his friend, Sam the dog. Sam was not feeling well, so Tom wanted to buy him a toy to make him feel better. At the shop, Tom found a big, red ball. He knew Sam would love it! But when Tom tried to pick up the ball, it rolled away. Tom chased the ball all around the shop. It was very unexpected! Just when Tom was about to catch the ball, it rolled out of the shop. Tom followed it and found out that Sam was already outside playing with the ball! It turned out that Sam was feeling better and had come to the shop to surprise Tom. They both laughed and played with the ball together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Susan. She liked to store all of her favourite things in the cupboard in her bedroom. One day, she decided to go out and get some more things to store. Susan went to a big store and looked around. She found lots of amazing things for her cupboard. Then, as she was about to leave, she heard strange music coming from a small room at the end of the store. Curious, Susan opened the door and was surprised to see a dancer! He was twirling around inside the room and making scary faces. Susan was so scared that she ran out of the store as quickly as she could. But she still remembered the dancer, twirling around and making those scary faces!
