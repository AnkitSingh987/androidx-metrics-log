One day, in a big garden, a little flower was about to bloom. It was a great day because all the other flowers were already big and pretty. The little flower wanted to be just like them. A boy named Tim saw the little flower and wanted to help it bloom. He went to his dad's equipment box and took out a small watering can. Then, he filled it with water and gently poured it on the little flower. "You can do it, little flower!" Tim said. The next day, Tim went back to the garden and saw something unexpected. The little flower had not only bloomed, but it had also turned into a magical talking flower! "Thank you, Tim!" said the flower. "Because of your help, I am now the prettiest and most special flower in the garden!" Tim and the magical flower became great friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She had a soft, fluffy cat named Kitty. Sue and Kitty loved to play together. One day, Sue found a big book with many pages. She wanted to show it to Kitty. Sue opened the book and said, "Look, Kitty! Let's read this book together." Kitty looked at the pages and started to crawl on them. Sue laughed and said, "Silly Kitty, you can't crawl on the pages!" Kitty jumped off the book and cuddled next to Sue. They looked at the pictures on the pages together. Sue pointed at a picture of a dog and said, "This is a dog, Kitty." Kitty looked at the dog and purred softly. As they turned the pages, they saw more animals. Sue taught Kitty about each one. They had a fun time learning together. When they finished the book, Sue said, "Kitty, I'm happy we read the book together. Let's do it again tomorrow!" Kitty purred, and they both went to sleep, dreaming about the animals they saw on the pages.

Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and sing. They saw a hedge with many green leaves. They wanted to see what was behind it. "Let's go and look," said Lily. "OK," said Ben. They ran to the hedge and pushed the leaves apart. They saw a big pond with water and fish and frogs. They were happy and clapped their hands. "Wow, look at the fish!" said Ben. "They are so smooth and shiny," said Lily. They leaned over the pond and tried to touch the fish. But the fish were fast and swam away. They tried to catch the frogs. But the frogs were jumpy and hopped away. They heard a loud voice behind them. "Hey, what are you doing here?" said the voice. They turned around and saw a man. He was angry and had a stick. He was the owner of the pond. "Get away from my pond!" he said. "You are scaring my fish and frogs!" Lily and Ben were scared and ran away. They ran back to the hedge and through the leaves. They ran to their house and told their mom what happened. "Mom, we saw a pond with fish and frogs, but a man yelled at us and chased us away," said Lily. "That was not nice of him," said mom. "But you should not go to other people's ponds without asking. They might not like it." "We are sorry, mom," said Ben. Mom hugged them and gave them a cookie. "Next time, ask me first, OK?" she said. "OK, mom," they said. They ate their cookie and played with their toys. They forgot about the pond and the man. They were happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty ladder. It was in a big, green yard. The ladder wanted to be clean and shiny. It did not like being dirty. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily saw the dirty ladder. She wanted to help the ladder. Lily got a big bucket of water and a soft cloth. She started to clean the ladder. As Lily cleaned the ladder, something nice started to happen. The ladder became clean and shiny. The ladder was very happy. Lily was happy too, because she made a new friend. From that day on, Lily and the ladder played together in the big, green yard.
