Once there was a very smart cat named Tim. Tim was so intelligent, he could read and write. He lived in a small house with a big blackboard. Tim loved to write on the blackboard every day. One day, Tim wanted to write on the top of the blackboard. But he was too small to reach it. So, he had an idea. He would stretch his body to make himself taller. Tim stretched and stretched until he was tall enough to write on the top of the blackboard. He was so happy! Now, he could write and draw anywhere he wanted. Tim's friends came to see his blackboard and they were amazed. They all played and learned together, thanks to Tim's stretching and smart thinking.

Once upon a time, there was a barber named Tim. Tim was very useful in his town. He helped people look nice by cutting their hair. Everyone loved to visit Tim the barber. One day, a big box came to Tim's shop. Tim had to push the box inside. Inside the box, there were new chairs for people to sit on while Tim cut their hair. Tim was very happy with the new chairs. Tim's friends came to see the new chairs. They took turns sitting on them. Tim pushed each chair to make sure it was just right. Everyone said "Thank you, Tim!" for being so useful. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty dress. It had many stitches and was very nice. The dress loved to hang on a hanger. One day, the dress met a scarf. The scarf was also pretty. "Hi, scarf," said the dress. "Do you like to hang too?" "Yes, I do," said the scarf. "But I am sad because I don't have stitches like you." The dress wanted to help the scarf. So, the dress took out some of its stitches and gave them to the scarf. The scarf was happy and said, "Thank you, dress!" But then, the dress started to fall apart. It lost too many stitches. The dress could not hang on the hanger anymore. The scarf felt bad for the dress. The moral of the story is to be careful when helping others. Sometimes, you can give too much and hurt yourself.

Once upon a time there was a kind farmer who cared a lot for his animals. He had a flock of chickens, a cow, and a big green barn. Every day he collected fresh eggs from his chickens and gave them to the little town nearby. One day, the farmer heard a strange noise coming out of his barn. He went inside to take a look, and saw that a mischievous little fox had gotten inside and was running around causing a ruckus. The fox begged the farmer to forgive him. He said, "Please forgive me for getting into your barn. I was just so hungry and your eggs looked so delicious!" The farmer smiled and said, "I forgive you little fox. You can stay here and help me take care of my animals. After all, everyone makes mistakes and deserves a second chance." The little fox was so happy to know that he was forgiven! Later, the fox and the farmer became best friends and the fox even helped the farmer collect his delicious eggs from the chickens.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. One hot day, Amy and her friend Tim went to the park to collect rocks. They liked to find rocks with pretty colors and shapes. Amy found a big, red rock, and Tim found a shiny, blue rock. They were very happy with their new rocks. At the park, they saw a mint plant. Tim said, "I like mint. It smells good." Amy agreed, "Yes, it does smell good." They picked some mint leaves to take home. They wanted to show their moms the mint and the rocks they collected. But then, something strange happened. The shiny, blue rock started to wiggle and shake. It changed into a little blue bird! Amy and Tim were so surprised. The bird said, "Thank you for finding me. I was stuck as a rock, but now I am free!" Amy and Tim smiled and waved goodbye as the little blue bird flew away. They knew they had made a new friend and had a story to tell their moms.
