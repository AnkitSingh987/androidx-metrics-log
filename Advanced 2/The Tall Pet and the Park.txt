Once upon a time there lived a tall pet. He was a lovable pet and always wanted to do something fun. One day, he was walking down the street when he heard someone whisper. He stopped and listened more carefully. Suddenly he heard a voice saying, "Let's have a picnic". The pet was so curious that he followed the small voice. He followed the soft whisper to a big green park. When he reached the park, he saw a family with a picnic blanket on the grass. They had a giant basket of food and were whispering to each other. The pet was so excited and he jumped up and down. The family saw him and smiled. One of them whispered, "Would you like to join us for a picnic?" The pet nodded and ran to the picnic blanket. They were all happy and laughed as the pet enjoyed the picnic with them.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big orange ball stuck in the mud. Tim wanted to play with the ball, so he tried to pull it out. But the ball was stuck very deep in the mud, and it was hard for Tim to pull it out by himself. Just then, a friendly dog came by. The dog saw Tim trying to pull the ball out of the mud. "Woof! Can I help you?" asked the dog. Tim was happy to have help. Together, they both tried to pull the orange ball out of the mud. As they pulled and pulled, something unexpected happened. The ball was not just a ball, it was a big orange balloon! When they finally pulled it out of the mud, the balloon started to float up into the sky. Tim and the dog were very surprised, but they laughed and played with the big orange balloon all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a nice lady. She liked to cook. One day, she wanted to cook something new. She saw a big bird with a pretty wing. The bird said, "You can cook with my wing, but you must be kind to me." The nice lady said, "Yes, I will be kind to you." So, she took the wing and started to cook. She put the wing in a big pot. She added some sweet and some sour things to make it yummy. The smell was very good. When it was ready, the nice lady shared the food with the bird. They both ate and smiled. The bird said, "Thank you for being kind to me." The nice lady and the bird became good friends. They cooked and ate together every day.

One day, a little monkey named Momo found a sweet banana. Momo was very happy. He wanted to eat the sweet banana right away. But first, he wanted to show his friends. Momo went to find his friend, Lulu the bunny. Lulu saw the sweet banana and liked it too. Momo and Lulu wanted to share the sweet banana. They decided to say thank you to the banana tree. Momo and Lulu went to the big banana tree. They looked up at the tree and said thank you. Then, they both did a little bow. The tree was happy too. Momo and Lulu ate the sweet banana together and played all day.

One day, a sweet girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a tree with lots of prunes on it. Lucy loved prunes, so she picked some to eat. As she ate the prunes, she felt very happy. Then, a little bird came to the tree. The bird also liked prunes. But, the bird could not reach the prunes. Lucy wanted to help the bird. She picked a prune and gave it to the bird. The bird was very happy and ate the prune. Suddenly, the bird started to change. It became a big, beautiful bird. The bird was really a magic bird! The magic bird was so happy that Lucy helped it. It started to sing and dance. Lucy and the magic bird danced together. All the people in the park saw them and started to applaud. Everyone was happy and had a fun day.
