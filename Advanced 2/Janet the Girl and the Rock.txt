Once there was a girl named Janet. She wore dry pants and they made her feel comfortable. Janet went out to play in the garden and she found a big rock. She picked up the rock and it felt heavy in her hands. Janet was so excited that she ran to her mom and said, "Mom! Look what I found, a big rock!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Janet. What do you want to do with it?" Janet thought for a moment and said, ". I can see a castle in the sky, can I make it with this rock?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, you can. You can make whatever you imagine. Go ahead and try!" Janet could not wait to start. She picked up the rock and placed it on the dry grass. She started smashing and smashing the rock until it became tiny pieces. She was careful not to get too close and the pieces flew everywhere. Suddenly, she had a stack of rocks and it now looked like a real castle! Janet was so proud and happy. When her mom saw the castle, she said, "Wow, Janet! You made a real castle! I knew you had the imagination to do it. I'm so proud of you!" The castle was the symbol of Janet's courage and strong determination. From that day on, Janet always remembered her special rock castle.

Sammy was in the park on Tuesday. He felt very open. Just then, a group of his friends showed up. They started to giggle and point at his shirt. Sammy was embarrassed. He asked his friends, "Why are you laughing?" The friends replied, "Your shirt is too small, Sammy. It makes you look silly!" Sammy wished he had worn a different shirt. He felt very embarrassed. The other children started to laugh even more. Suddenly, Sammy's mother arrived. She noticed her son's embarrassment and said kindly, "It's alright Sammy, don't worry. We can always come back on a different day!" Sammy was so relieved. He smiled at his mother and held her hand as they walked away.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bunny named Bob. Bob loved to hop and spring all day long. He had lots of fun with his friends in the big green park. One day, Bob found a long stick. He thought it was a good stick to measure how far he could spring. Bob would hop and his friends would use the stick to see how far he went. Each time, Bob tried to spring even farther. But one day, when Bob tried to spring, he found that he could not. All his friends were surprised. They tried to help Bob, but nothing worked. Then a little bird whispered in Bob's ear, "You are not really a bunny, you are a bird, just like me!" Bob looked at himself and saw that he had wings! Bob was not a chubby bunny, but a chubby bird! He flapped his wings and began to fly. His friends were so happy for him. From that day on, Bob and his friends played in the park, with Bob flying high above them.

Once upon a time, there was a big green castle. In the castle lived a king, a queen, and their little boy. The little boy loved to play with his toys and put them all over the castle. One day, the little boy and his friend, a talking cat, decided to have a fun game. "Let's put all the toys in the castle's garden!" said the talking cat. The little boy agreed and they both went to the garden. They put the toys on the green grass and started to play. They had so much fun playing with the toys in the sunshine. The king and queen watched them from the castle's window, smiling and laughing. The little boy and the talking cat played until the sun went down, and then they picked up the toys and put them back in the castle. They went to sleep, dreaming of more fun days at the green castle.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to visit his grandma. Grandma was very happy to see Tim. She wanted to show him something incredible. Tim was excited to see what it was. Grandma took Tim to the kitchen. On the table, there was a big dessert. It was a cake with lots of colors. Tim's eyes got big and he smiled. He thought the cake was incredible. They sat down and ate the dessert together. Tim loved the cake. It was sweet and yummy. He thanked his grandma for showing him the incredible dessert. They had a great day together.
