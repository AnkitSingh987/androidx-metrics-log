Once upon a time, there was a thin girl named Lucy. She was playing in her room when her mom came in. "Lucy, I have something for you." Mom put a box on the bed. It was big and made of shiny wood. "What is it?" Lucy asked. "It's a secret! But if you open it, you'll find out," said Mom. Lucy wondered what was in the box. She opened it and found a doll. The doll had long, thin legs and a yellow dress. It looked like a real girl. Lucy was excited. "Where did you get this?" "It was under the bed," Mom said with a smile. From that day on, Lucy played with her doll every day. She liked to put the doll under her bed and pretend it was sleeping. It was her secret.

Once there was a dog named Spot. Spot had a long tail. He loved to wag his tail when he was happy. He liked to play with his friends, the cat and the bird. One day, Spot saw a dead bug on the ground. He did not know what it was, so he asked his friends. The cat said, "It is a dead bug." Spot felt sad for the bug. Spot and his friends decided to help the bug. They used their tails to give it a soft bed. Spot knew the bug was dead, but he felt good helping it. Spot, the cat, and the bird played together and wagged their tails, happy they were good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat celery. One day, he went to the garden to find some. But when he got there, he saw that all the celery was gone! Bob felt very sad and anxious. Bob went to his friend, the squirrel named Sally. He told her, "Sally, I miss the celery from the garden. It's all gone!" Sally said, "Don't worry, Bob. I saw some celery in the big park. Let's go there." Bob and Sally went to the big park. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find the celery. Then, they saw a big bird named Ben. Ben had the celery in his nest! Bob and Sally asked Ben, "Can we have some celery, please?" Ben said, "Sure, let's share it." Bob, Sally, and Ben ate the celery together. They were all happy and not anxious anymore. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little house in a big forest. The house was old and needed repair. A kind family lived in the house. They had a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. One day, a big storm came. The sky was not clear, and the rain fell hard. The wind blew the trees and the leaves. The family was scared. The storm broke the house even more. After the storm, the family knew they needed to repair the house. Mom, Dad, and Lily worked together to fix the house. They put the broken pieces back together. They made the house strong again. The sun came out, and the sky was clear. The family was happy and safe in their repaired house. The moral of the story is when we work together, we can fix anything.

One day, Little Tommy was running to the store with his mom. He was extra excited since his mom had said he could pick out whatever type of yogurt he wanted. "What flavour do you want?" Little Tommy's mom asked. Little Tommy said he wanted the strawberry yogurt, his all-time favorite. When they got to the store, Little Tommy zoomed over to the strawberry yogurt, but it was all gone. Disappointed, he said "That's OK, I'll just have blueberry instead." Little Tommy and his mom grabbed the blueberry yogurt and headed back home. On their way, Little Tommy's mom noticed he seemed very sleepy. "I think it's time for a nap," she said. So Little Tommy cuddled up in his blankie and took a nap. Little Tommy's mom heard his breathing get deeper and knew he was fast asleep. But Little Tommy had a surprise when he woke up! His blueberry yogurt had shrunk in size! He was so amazed that it was just the right size for him to finish! Little Tommy smiled as he finished up the last spoonful. His mom had been right - a nap really was the best way to make everything shrink!
