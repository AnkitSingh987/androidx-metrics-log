Once upon a time there was a wild dog. He wanted to meet someone new. He ran and ran until he saw a big towel. He barked and barked at the towel, but it did not move. He tried to nudge it with his nose but it was too heavy. Suddenly, a small creature appeared from behind the towel. It was a friendly cat! They said hello to each other and became the best of friends. Every day the wild dog and the cat would meet behind the towel and play together until the sun set. They were together so often that people called them the wild friends.

Once upon a time there was a family who lived in a house with a broken fireplace. One day, the mother asked her little three-year-old daughter if she wanted to borrow some wood from their neighbours. The little girl was very excited and nodded her head up and down eagerly. So the mother and daughter put on their warmest coats and set off to the neighbours house. When they arrived, the little girl knocked on the door and the friendly neighbour asked her, "What can I do for you dear?" The little girl answered, "We need to borrow some wood for the fireplace at our house." The friendly neighbour smiled and said, "Of course, come right in and take what you need." The little girl and her mother were very grateful and thanked the friendly neighbour before taking enough wood back to their house to get the broken fireplace up and running. The girl was so excited that from then on, she was always happy to help others and pay it forward!

Once upon a time, there was a huge house. This house was not like any other house. It was a special kind of structure. Many animals lived in this house. They were all friends and played together every day. One day, a little dog named Max was playing with his friends in the house. They were playing a game of hide and seek. Max was very good at hiding. But, this time, Max hid so well that he started to feel lost. He couldn't find his way back to his friends. Max was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. So, he walked and walked until he saw a big tree. Max climbed the tree and looked around. From up high, he saw the huge structure. Max was so happy! He climbed down and ran back to his friends. They were all glad that Max was not lost anymore. They hugged Max and played together again.

One day, a little boy named Tim found some junk in his yard. He saw a gentle cat playing near the junk. Tim was happy to see the cat and wanted to play with it. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, can I play with the cat near the junk?" His mom said, "No, Tim. You should stay away from the junk. It can be dangerous." But Tim did not listen to his mom. He thought the cat was too gentle to be dangerous. The next day, Tim went to the junk to play with the gentle cat. They had fun playing together. But as they were playing, the cat knocked over a big pile of junk. The junk fell on Tim and he got hurt. Tim cried and his mom came running. She helped him up and said, "I told you to stay away from the junk, Tim. You should always listen to your mom." Tim learned that he should listen to his mom, even if things look safe and fun.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom was very kind and liked to help people. One day, he heard about a fun event in the park. It was a big party for all the kids in the town. Tom wanted to go and help make the party great. On the day of the event, the sky was dark with big clouds. Tom was worried that rain might stop the fun. So, he thought of a way to prevent the rain from ruining the party. He found a big, strong tent and put it up in the park. This way, even if it rained, the kids could still have fun under the tent. When the kids arrived at the park, they saw the big tent and were very happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced to fun music. And then, it started to rain. But the rain did not stop the party because of Tom's tent. The kids stayed dry and had a great time. Everyone thanked Tom for preventing the rain from stopping their fun. And from that day, Tom became a hero in the town.
