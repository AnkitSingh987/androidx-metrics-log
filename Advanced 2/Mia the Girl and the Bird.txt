Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to touch things. One day, she saw a pretty ornament on a tree. It was shiny and round. Mia wanted to touch it, but her mom said, "No, Mia! Don't touch the ornament." Mia was sad, but she listened to her mom. She went to play with her ball instead. While she was playing, a healthy, big bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw the shiny ornament too. The bird picked up the ornament in its beak and flew away with it. Mia's mom saw the bird take the ornament. She was very surprised. "Oh no!" she said. Mia looked at her mom and asked, "Can I touch the ornament now?" Her mom laughed and said, "I guess so, but you'll have to find the bird first!" Mia and her mom went on an adventure to find the bird and the ornament, and they had a lot of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his football. One day, he met a big, powerful dog. The dog wanted to play with the football too. Tim was scared because the dog was so big. Tim said, "Please, dog, don't take my football!" But the dog did not listen. The dog took the football and ran away. Tim was sad and wanted his football back. Tim asked his mom for help. She said, "Please, dog, give Tim his football back." The dog listened to Tim's mom and gave the football back. Tim was happy and said, "Thank you!" Now, Tim and the big, powerful dog play football together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. She lived in a big house with an elderly lady. The elderly lady was kind and loved Kitty very much. But Kitty wanted to go outside and play with her friends. One day, Kitty saw a network of strings near the window. She thought this was her chance to escape. So, she climbed up the strings and jumped out of the window. Kitty was so happy to be outside and play with her friends. But soon, it started to get dark and Kitty got scared. She didn't know how to get back inside the house. Kitty's friends helped her find the window with the network of strings. She climbed back up and went inside. The elderly lady was so happy to see Kitty again. From that day on, Kitty learned to be happy inside the house and play with her friends outside only when the elderly lady allowed her.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a fun day planned today. He wanted to play with his toys, eat yummy food, and watch his favorite show. He was very excited for today. But Tim did something he should not do. He took a cookie from the cookie jar without asking. His mom saw him and felt guilty. She did not want to spoil his day, but she knew he needed to learn. So, mom told Tim that he could not watch his favorite show today. Tim was sad, but he knew he did something wrong. He said sorry to his mom and promised not to do it again. Mom hugged him and said they could still have fun today.

Lily was a brave girl and she was ready for her first dive! She walked to the beach with her family, her little feet making footprints in the sand. She stepped close to the water, her toes wiggling. She was ready for her dive. Suddenly, a wave came to the beach. It was too big for Lily, and she stumbled. She tried to shout for help, but the water was too loud. The wave swept up to the shore and took her away! Lily's family rushed to the beach and started searching for her. They looked in the water and on the sand, but they couldn't find her anywhere. They were so sad. Lily had been ready for her dive, but she wasn't ready for the big wave. She was gone and never seen again.
