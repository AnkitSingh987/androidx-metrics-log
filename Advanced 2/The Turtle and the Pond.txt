Once there was a turtle. It had a tail that was very different from other turtles. Every day, it wished that its tail would change. Finally, the turtle had an idea. It swam past the pond and over the meadow. It crossed the bridge and went down the path until it found a magical river. The turtle jumped in and swam until it reached a waterfall. Suddenly, the turtle felt a tingle all over its body. Its tail started to change colors and grew longer than before. The turtle was so happy to see its tail had changed! When the turtle returned to the pond all the other turtles were amazed. The turtle showed them its different tail and all the other turtles agreed that it was beautiful. From then on the turtle was proud of its tail. It never wished for it to change again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a grumpy old man named Mr. Brown. He did not like to talk or play with others. He always sat alone in his little house. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the town. She wanted to make friends with everyone. When she saw Mr. Brown, she wanted to be his friend too. Lucy asked her mom, "How can I make Mr. Brown happy?" Her mom said, "I recommend you give him a book about history. He loves history very much." So, Lucy went to the store and bought a history book for Mr. Brown. When Mr. Brown got the book, he was very happy. He read the book and smiled. Lucy and Mr. Brown became good friends. They talked about history and played together. Now, Mr. Brown was not grumpy anymore, and the town was a happier place.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a bird class. All the little birds went there to learn how to fly. One little bird was clumsy. His name was Tim. Tim tried to fly, but he always fell. The other birds laughed at him. Tim was sad, but he did not give up. He tried and tried, but he could not fly like the others. One day, a big wind came. It blew all the birds out of the tree. Tim was scared, but he tried to fly again. This time, he flew! He helped his friends back to the tree. They were happy and said sorry for laughing at him. The moral of the story is to never give up, even when others make fun of you.

Once upon a time, there was a big park. In the park, there was a statue. The statue was different. It had a big hat and a funny face. Kids liked to play near the statue. They laughed at the funny face. One sunny day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw the statue and wanted to play with it. She tried to open the big hat on the statue. Lily pulled and pulled, but the hat did not open. Lily's friend, Tom, came to help. They both pulled on the hat. This time, the hat opened! Inside the hat, they found a small toy. Lily and Tom were so happy. They played with the toy and the statue all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a delicate toy bird that she loved very much. One day, the toy bird flew away and got stuck in a tree. Lucy was very sad and didn't know what to do. A friendly squirrel saw Lucy and asked, "Why are you sad?" Lucy said, "My toy bird is stuck in a tree. I don't know how to rescue it." The squirrel wanted to help Lucy, so it climbed the tree and got the toy bird for her. The squirrel gave the toy bird back to Lucy. She was so happy! Lucy said, "Thank you for rescuing my toy bird." She took a box and put her delicate toy bird inside to keep it safe. From that day on, Lucy and the squirrel became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a good king who lived in a big palace. The palace had many rooms and a big garden with pretty flowers. The king loved to play in the garden when the weather was nice. One day, the king found a little frog in the garden. The frog could spring very high. The king liked the frog and they became friends. They played together every day, and the frog would spring high into the air to make the king laugh. As the days went by, the king and the frog had lots of fun. They played hide and seek, and the frog would always spring out to surprise the king. They were very happy in their big palace, and they lived happily ever after.
