Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Buddy. He lived in a small house with a nice family. Buddy loved to play with his ball and run around the yard. One day, Buddy saw a little bird on the fence. He stared at the bird, and the bird stared back at him. They were friends now. The bird told Buddy about a war between the birds and the squirrels in the park. Buddy wanted to help his new friend. So, he went to the park with the bird. The birds and squirrels were fighting for a big tree. Buddy barked and scared the squirrels away. The birds were happy, and the war was over. From that day on, Buddy and the little bird were best friends. They played together every day, and the family loved their dependable dog and his bird friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a small light flower. She waited and waited for the sun to come out so she could bloom. Then one day the sun appeared in the sky and it felt so good! The flower opened up and was the prettiest flower ever! The flower saw a little girl who was very happy when she saw the flower. The girl came over to the flower and said: “I love you, flower. I will take care of you.” The girl watered the flower every day and the flower blossomed in the sun. Everyone in the neighborhood admired the flower, it was so beautiful. Then one day, a big storm came and the wind blew the flower away. The girl looked everywhere for the flower, but she could not find it. She was so sad and started to cry. The flower was gone. It would never bloom again.

Once upon a time, there was a cool cat named Tom. Tom loved to rest under a big tree in the park. He also liked to watch people walk by. One day, Tom saw a girl named Sally. Sally was playing with her makeup. She put on red lips and big eyes. Tom was curious and wanted to try too. When Sally saw Tom, she laughed and said, "Cats don't wear makeup!" But Sally had an idea. She put some makeup on Tom's face, and he looked very funny. They laughed and played together. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the makeup away. Now, Tom's face was clean again! Tom and Sally were surprised, but they didn't mind. They knew they could always play and have fun, with or without makeup. And from that day on, they became the best of friends.

One day, a little girl named Sally went outside to play. She liked to skip and say words out loud. She was a very happy girl, but today she felt a little serious. Sally saw her friend Timmy sitting under a tree. Timmy looked sad, so she skipped over to him. "Hi Timmy, why are you sad?" asked Sally. Timmy looked up and said, "I lost my ball. I can't find it anywhere." Sally wanted to help Timmy find his ball, so they both started to look for it. They looked behind the tree, under the bushes, and even in the sandbox. As they were looking, a big, mean dog came into the park. The dog saw Sally and Timmy and started to run towards them. Sally and Timmy were scared and tried to run away. They ran as fast as they could, but the big, mean dog caught up to them. The dog took Timmy's ball and ran away. Sally and Timmy were very sad. They didn't find the ball and the big, mean dog took it away. They walked back to the tree and sat down, feeling sad about their bad day.

One day, a fancy cat named Kitty went for a walk. She saw a big switch on the wall. Kitty did not know what the switch did, but she wanted to find out. She jumped up and hit the switch with her paw. All of a sudden, it started to rain inside the house! Kitty was very wet and did not like it. She jumped up and hit the switch again, but the rain did not stop. Kitty was sad and did not know what to do. Then, a little mouse named Timmy came out from his hole. He saw Kitty and the rain. Timmy was a very smart mouse. He ran to the switch and hit it with his tiny paw. The rain stopped! Kitty was so happy and thanked Timmy. From that day on, Kitty and Timmy were best friends, and they never touched the switch again.
