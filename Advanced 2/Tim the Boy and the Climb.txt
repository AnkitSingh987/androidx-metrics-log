Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to climb trees. One day, he saw a very big tree and wanted to climb it. He started to climb, but he fell down and hurt his leg. Tim's mom took him to the doctor. The doctor was very nice. He looked at Tim's leg and put a bandage on it. Tim felt better, but he was ashamed that he fell from the tree. The next day, Tim went back to the tree. He wanted to climb it again. This time, he climbed slowly and carefully. When he reached the top, he was very happy. He learned to be careful and not to be ashamed of his mistakes.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. They saw a wild cat near the trees. The cat was big and had pretty colors. Lily gazed at the cat and wanted to play with it. Lily asked her mom, "Can I play with the wild cat?" Her mom said, "No, Lily, it might not be safe. Let's draw a picture of it instead." So, Lily took a piece of paper and a crayon. She drew the wild cat with a big smile on its face. Lily and her mom laughed as they looked at the drawing. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old and loved to explore. One day she saw a colorful drawer and asked her mom, "What's in this drawer?" Her mom said, "Why don't you open it and find out?" The little girl opened the drawer and was so excited to find lots of little treasures. She had found colorful beads, a sparkly necklace, and a magical butterfly. The little girl was so excited that she asked her mom, "Can I keep these?" Her mom nodded and said, "Yes, these can be your little treasures." The little girl was so happy she ran off to show all of her friends her colorful find.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very happy because her friend Tom was coming over for a visit. Lucy wanted to finish her painting before Tom arrived. When Tom came, he saw the painting and said, "Wow, Lucy! Your painting is so pretty!" Lucy smiled and said, "Thank you, Tom! I just finished it before you came." They played and laughed all day. They had so much fun during the visit. When it was time for Tom to go home, they both were sad. But they knew they would see each other again soon.

One bright morning, Max and his mama went to the beach. When they got there, Max was very excited. He saw the model sailboat and said, "Mama! Look!" His mama smiled and said, "That model boat is very nice, isn't it?" Max nodded and said excitedly, "Can I play with it?" His mama smiled again and said, "Yes, of course you can!" Max was so excited! He took the model boat and ran to the ocean. He started to splash with it in the water. He loved the feeling of the cool water on his feet. He laughed out loud and said, "Look, Mama! I'm helping the sailboat go sailing!" Max's mama smiled and said, "That's very helpful, Max! Have fun!" Max smiled and said, "I am! Thanks, Mama!" He kept on playing with the sailboat and enjoyed the sun, the beach, and the cool water.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn girl named Lily. She loved to wear her uniform to school. One day, she met a boy named Tim. Tim was a nice boy, but he did not like to wear his uniform. Lily and Tim started to quarrel because they did not agree. The next day, Lily and Tim went to school. They saw a big, scary dog. The dog was very mad. Lily and Tim were scared, but they still did not stop their quarrel. They were too stubborn to see the danger. Then, something unexpected happened. The big, scary dog chased Lily and Tim. They tried to run away, but the dog caught them. The dog took their uniforms and ran away. Lily and Tim were sad because they lost their uniforms. They learned that being stubborn and quarreling was not good.
