Peter was a compassionate three year old. He loved to play with his dog Jack and explore their backyard. One day, Peter and Jack were running around when Jack kicked a bulb with his foot. Peter was so mad that his dog had made the bulb break and started to yell at Jack. Jack looked sad, so Peter talked to him. "Why did you kick the bulb," he asked. Jack whimpered and looked away. Peter took a closer look at his dog and he noticed that Jack's paw was hurting. That made Peter feel very bad. Feeling compassionate, he gave Jack a hug and a treat. Peter put Jack to bed and went back outside to clean up the broken bulb. He was careful not to step on any pieces, but he was sad. He had only wanted to play, not break things. Peter sat down and thought about what he had learned. He realized that being compassionate is the most important thing. Now, whenever Peter and Jack play together, Peter makes sure to check on his dog to see if his paws are OK.

Once upon a time, there was a lady who loved to run. She was very fast. Every day, she would put on her shoes and go for a run in the park. One day, while running, she met a little boy. The boy said, "Lady, can you teach me to run fast like you?" The lady smiled and said, "Of course! I will show you how to increase your speed." They started to run together. The lady taught the boy to move his legs fast and to breathe in and out. The boy was happy to learn from the lady. As they ran, the boy became faster and faster. Soon, the boy was running almost as fast as the lady. They raced each other, laughing and having fun. The boy said, "Thank you, lady, for teaching me to run fast. I will always remember you." From that day on, the boy and the lady became good friends. They ran together every day, and the boy's speed continued to increase. Everyone in the park knew them as the fast-running friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a girl named Lily. She was a very original girl. She liked to help her mom with the laundry. One day, while they were doing laundry, Lily's mom asked her to support the clothes basket. "Can you support this basket for me, Lily?" her mom asked. "Yes, Mommy," Lily said, holding the basket with her little hands. As they walked to the laundry room, Lily saw her favorite shirt. It was a pretty, pink shirt with a big heart on it. She was so happy to see it clean again. Lily and her mom folded the laundry together, and they had a lot of fun. In the end, Lily learned that helping her mom and supporting her with the laundry made her feel good. She knew that she was a big help, and she felt proud of herself. And her mom was happy too, because they got to spend time together.

Once upon a time, there was a little bike with a big pedal. The bike loved to go fast. It could shoot down the road like a race car. The bike was very happy. One day, a little boy named Tim saw the bike. He wanted to ride it. Tim said, "Hi, bike! Can I ride you?" The bike said, "Yes, Tim! I want you to ride me. I am independent and can go very fast!" Tim got on the bike and pushed the pedal. They started to shoot down the road. Tim felt the wind in his hair. He was so happy. The bike was happy too. They were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a humble girl named Lily. She had a big heart and loved to help others. One day, she went for a walk in the woods and saw a little bird stuck in a tree. Lily knew she had to rescue the bird. Lily climbed the tree carefully, step by step. When she reached the bird, she saw the wonder in its eyes. She knew the bird was scared, but she spoke softly to it. "Don't worry, little bird, I'll help you," said Lily. With a gentle hand, Lily freed the bird from the tree. The bird flew away, happy and free. Lily felt proud of her rescue. She knew that even a humble girl could do great things. From that day on, Lily and the little bird became the best of friends.
