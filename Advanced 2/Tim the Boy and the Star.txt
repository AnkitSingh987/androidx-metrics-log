Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had an original cap that he loved very much. It was blue and had a big red star on it. Tim wore his cap every day. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play. He met a new friend named Sam. Sam looked at Tim's cap and said, "Wow, I like your cap! It's so original!" Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam! Do you want to play with me?" Sam nodded, and they played together all day. After playing for a long time, Tim and Sam felt very tired. They decided to take a nap under a big tree. They put their caps on the grass and closed their eyes. When they woke up, they felt very happy and ready to play again. Tim and Sam became best friends and always wore their caps when they played together.

One day, a poor boy named Tim went for a walk in the woods. He saw a big pine tree. He thought it was very pretty. Tim wanted to take a part of the tree home with him, so he tried to tear a small branch. Just then, a little squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel said, "Please don't tear my home!" Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, little squirrel. I didn't know this was your home." As they talked, the wind blew hard and the pine tree shook. Something shiny fell from the tree. The squirrel and Tim looked at it. It was a gold coin! The squirrel said, "You can have this for not tearing my home." Tim was very happy and thanked the squirrel. They became good friends and Tim was not poor anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pale blue dress that she loved to wear. One sunny day, Amy went to her garden to plant a bulb. She dug a hole, put the bulb in it, and covered it with dirt. A few days later, Amy saw a worm in her garden. The worm said, "Hello, Amy! I live in the ground, and I saw your bulb. I think it will rot if you don't water it." Amy thanked the worm and ran to get some water. She poured the water on the bulb, hoping it would not rot. After a while, the bulb grew into a beautiful flower. The flower was so big and colorful. Amy was so happy that she had listened to the worm. The worm came out of the ground and said, "Good job, Amy! You saved the bulb from rotting." Amy smiled and thanked the worm again. They became good friends, and Amy learned to take care of her garden with the help of her new friend, the worm.

Once upon a time, there was a popular girl named Lily. She loved to play in her garden every day. The garden was full of pretty flowers and green trees. Lily liked to zip around the garden, running and laughing. One sunny day, Lily and her friends played a fun game in the garden. They zipped around, trying to catch each other. The wind blew gently, and the flowers danced with them. They all laughed and had a great time. As the sun began to set, Lily and her friends sat under a big tree. They talked and shared yummy snacks. The garden was their favorite place to be. They knew they would come back to play and zip around again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat muffins. Her mom made her healthy muffins with fruits inside. Lily liked to eat the muffins for breakfast. One day, Lily and her mom went to a park. They took a big blanket and some muffins. They sat on the blanket under a big tree. Lily ate her muffin and felt very happy. After eating, Lily and her mom played in the park. They ran and laughed. When they were tired, they went back to the blanket. They laid down and relaxed under the big tree. Lily thought it was a perfect day.
