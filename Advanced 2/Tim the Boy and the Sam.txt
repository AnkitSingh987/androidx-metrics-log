Once upon a time, in a small place, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim was very restless. He loved to run, jump, and play all day long. One day, Tim's mom asked him to give a toy to his friend, Sam. So Tim picked up the toy and went to Sam's house. On his way, he saw a big tree and decided to climb it. He had so much fun climbing the tree, but he forgot to give the toy to Sam. Later, Tim remembered that he had to give the toy to Sam. He climbed down the tree and ran to Sam's house. When he got there, he gave the toy to Sam. Sam was very happy, and they played together all day long. And for a little while, Tim was not restless anymore.

Sam and Jen were friends who liked to bake. They baked cakes, pies, cookies and muffins. They baked for their families, their teachers and their friends. They were very good at baking and everyone loved their treats. One day, they decided to bake something new. They wanted to bake a swamp cake. They thought it would be fun and different. They looked for a recipe in their books, but they could not find one. So they made up their own recipe. They used green food coloring, chocolate, marshmallows, gummy worms and frogs. They mixed everything in a big bowl and poured it into a cake pan. They put it in the oven and waited. While they waited, they talked about how their swamp cake would look and taste. They imagined it would be gooey and slimy and yummy. They hoped everyone would like it and think they were very clever. They were excited to share their swamp cake with their friends. But when they opened the oven, they saw something terrible. Their swamp cake had exploded! The cake pan was broken and the cake was everywhere. It was on the oven, on the walls, on the floor and on them. It was a big mess. It smelled bad and looked worse. They were shocked and sad. Their swamp cake was a disaster. They heard a knock on the door. It was their friends. They had come to see their swamp cake and to have a taste. They saw Sam and Jen covered in cake and the kitchen in chaos. They laughed and laughed. They thought it was a joke. They said it was the funniest thing they had ever seen. They said Sam and Jen were very funny and successful bakers. They helped them clean up the mess and ate some of the cake. It was not very good, but they did not mind. They had a lot of fun. Sam and Jen smiled and laughed too. They realized their swamp cake was not a failure, but a surprise. They were happy to have such good friends. They decided to bake something else next time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red gate. It was in front of a pretty garden. Many people would walk by and admire the gate. They loved how it looked. One sunny day, a small, fragile bird flew near the gate. It was a very little bird with soft feathers. The bird wanted to rest on the gate. But something unexpected happened. As the bird landed on the gate, the gate started to move! The gate was not a gate at all. It was a big, friendly dog, painted to look like a gate. The dog and the little bird became good friends, and they played together in the pretty garden. Everyone who walked by now admired not only the gate, but also the special friendship between the dog and the fragile bird.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a big puddle. The cat was very careful not to get wet. The dog loved to splash and play in the water. The cat said, "Don't splash me, dog! I don't like it!" The dog just laughed and said, "Okay, cat!" The next day, they saw a dull can near the puddle. They did not know that the can had oil in it. The cat said, "Be careful, dog. That can might be bad." The dog just laughed and said, "Okay, cat!" On the third day, the dog wanted to play in the puddle again. He ran and jumped, but this time he hit the can. The oil spilled into the puddle. The cat said, "I told you, dog!" The dog got sad because the puddle was not fun to splash in anymore. The cat and dog learned to be more careful and listen to each other.
