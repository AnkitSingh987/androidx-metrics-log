Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to be organized. She put her toys in boxes and her clothes in the closet. One day, Mia's mom gave her a pretty veil. Mia loved the veil and wore it every day. One day, Mia went to play outside. She played with her ball and ran with her friends. But then, Mia lost her veil. She was very sad and looked everywhere for it. Mia asked her friends to help her find the veil, but they could not find it. Mia went home and told her mom about the lost veil. Her mom said, "Don't worry, Mia. We will find it." They looked in Mia's organized room and found the veil under the bed. Mia was happy and put the veil back on her head. She learned to be more careful with her things.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a nice cat named Sam. Sam loved to serve food to all his friends. Every day, he would make easy snacks and share them with everyone. Sam's friends were very happy. One sunny spring day, Sam decided to have a big party in the park. He invited all his friends and started to make food for everyone. Sam worked hard to serve the best snacks he could. All his friends came to the party and were very excited to eat the food. But, when they started to eat, something unexpected happened! The food was not easy to eat at all. It was very hard and not yummy. Sam's friends were confused and a bit sad. Then, Sam's friend, a wise owl, found out that Sam had used the wrong recipe! They all laughed and decided to make new, easy snacks together. In the end, they had a great time at the party and learned that it's okay to make mistakes.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big dog named Max. Mia and Max loved to play together. They played in the park, in the yard, and in the house. One sunny day, Mia and Max went for a walk. Mia put a tight collar on Max and held a lead. They walked and walked until they saw a big tree. Under the tree, there was a small red ball. Mia kicked the ball with her foot and Max ran to catch it. Mia and Max played with the red ball for a long time. They were very happy. When it was time to go home, Mia held the lead tight and they walked back together. They were tired but had a lot of fun.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the garden. He saw a big, red tomato. He wanted to pick it and eat it. But, his friend, the bird, told him, "Listen, Tim. The tomato is not ready yet." Tim felt sad. He wanted the tomato now. He looked around and saw an empty basket. He had an idea. He would wait for the tomato to be ready. Every day, Tim and the bird would look at the tomato. They would talk and have fun. One day, the bird said, "Look, Tim! The tomato is ready!" Tim was so happy. He picked the tomato and put it in the basket. He shared it with the bird. They both enjoyed the yummy tomato. From that day on, they always listened to each other and had fun in the garden.

Max had a very clear idea about what he wanted for breakfast; chocolate. His mom had always said that breakfast was the most important meal of the day, so he had decided today was a good day for chocolate. He asked his mom, “Mommy, can I have chocolate for breakfast?” “No, Max. You can’t have chocolate for breakfast,” she said as she ruffled his hair. Suddenly, a big smile spread across Max’s face. He knew why his mom had said no. It was because she was going to give him a chocolate anyway, and it was going to be a surprise! She handed him a yummy banana shaped like a smiley face. On the top it had some creamy chocolate with sprinkles that Max couldn’t wait to eat. Max couldn’t contain his joy. He ran into his dad’s arms, “Daddy, I’m eating chocolate for breakfast!” He took a big bite and started to chew. It tasted even better than he had imagined. Max’s dad smiled, “See I told you that mommy always had a surprise for you. It’s good to be excited about breakfast, isn’t it?” Max happily nodded his head. Finally, he was able to have chocolate for breakfast!
