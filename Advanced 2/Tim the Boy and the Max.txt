Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toy cars. He would make roads in the dirt and drive his cars all day long. One day, a big dog named Max came to Tim's yard. Max was a spoiled dog who liked to disturb Tim's play time. Max would run through Tim's roads and mess them up. This made Tim sad. Tim's mom saw what was happening and had an idea. She gave Max a big bone to chew on. Max loved the bone and stopped disturbing Tim's play. Tim was happy again and they all played together in the yard.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to perform a show for his friends. He put on his favorite clothes and went outside. He was very excited. As he started to perform, his friend Sue said, "Tim, your clothes have a big hole! It looks hurt." Tim looked down and saw a big hole in his clothes. He was very sad, but he decided to keep performing. Then, a big wind came and blew Tim's clothes away. All his friends laughed at him. Tim felt very bad and ran home. He didn't want to perform ever again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little girl who loved to shake things. One day, she found an envelope on the ground. She loved the feel of it so she shook it and quickly realised that there was something inside. She smiled with glee and opened it, revealing a piece of candy inside. She was overjoyed and quickly ran to show her mum. Her mum was so happy to see her daughter so happy and hugged her tightly. They both enjoyed the snack and the little girl was delighted with her new find. She thanked the envelope for bringing her the delicious candy and smiled her biggest grin.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy, a little match. One day, Tom lost his match and felt very sad. Tom went to his friend, a dog named Max. Tom said, "Max, I lost my match. Can you help me find it?" Max was a good friend and said, "Yes, Tom, I will help you." Together, Tom and Max looked for the match. They looked in the grass and under the tree. Then, they managed to find the match near the big rock. Tom was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max! You are a great friend." They played with the match all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was getting ready for her birthday party. She had a big cake with candles on top. She was very excited. Jane's mommy told her, "Jane, we need to load the car with all the party things. Are you ready?" Jane said, "Yes, Mommy! I am ready!" So, they loaded the car with balloons, games, and the big cake. At the party, Jane and her friends played games and ate yummy food. When it was time to blow out the candles on the cake, Jane's mommy said, "Be careful, Jane!" But Jane was too excited. She blew out the candles too hard, and the cake fell on the floor. Everyone was sad, and the party was not fun anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a castle on a hill. All of a sudden, a big firework appeared in the sky. It was tight and sparkly! The castle guard noticed the firework. "What a big firework!" he shouted. The firework kept getting bigger and bigger until it was too big to fit in the sky. The guard was so happy that he called for the King! The King came outside and said, "Let's go watch the firework!" Everybody followed the King and they all gathered around the firework. It was so tight that nobody could get close to it. They all stood back and watched it burst into a million colours. The King shouted, "Wow! That was the best firework ever!" Everybody agreed and they all clapped their hands. The firework was so tight that it stayed in the sky for the whole night. The end!
