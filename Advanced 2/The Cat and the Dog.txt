Once upon a time, in a blue house, there was a big cat. The cat had a friend, a small dog. They liked to play all day. They had many toys and they shared them. One night, the cat and the dog saw a blue ball outside. They went to play with it. They had so much fun. They played until the night was very dark. When it was time to go back to the blue house, the cat and the dog were tired. They had a good night's sleep. They dreamed about the fun they had with the blue ball. And they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, while playing, he saw a smelly sock on the ground. "Yuck!" said Tim, "Who would leave a smelly sock here?" Tim looked around and saw a big dog. The dog was looking at the sock too. The dog said, "That's my sock! I like to grab it and play with it." Tim laughed and said, "Okay, let's play together!" Tim picked up a stick and pointed to the dog. "I'll throw this and you can grab it!" he said. The dog wagged its tail and barked happily. Tim threw the stick and the dog ran to grab it. They played all day and became good friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Jack and Jill. They always loved playing together. One day, they were playing in the park when they saw a strange object. Jack asked: "What is it?" Jill replied "It looks like a player!" They both decided to offer it a try. They each took a turn playing with it. The player was fair, giving each one an equal chance to win. When their playtime finished, Jack and Jill hugged each other. They had lots of fun playing together. The end.

Once there was a smart sunflower. It liked to clap its petals together. Every morning it would stand tall and clap its hands. The sunflower knew how to be happy! One day, a butterfly flew down and asked the sunflower to come on an adventure. So the sunflower said yes, and they jumped on the butterfly's wings. They flew high into the sky. The sunflower was so excited to explore! The butterfly and sunflower flew to a lake. They saw turtles swimming in the lake and had so much fun watching them. Then they flew to a field full of yellow flowers. The sunflower couldn't believe how many of its friends it had. The sunflower was so happy that it started to clap. Its petals moved fast and it spun in circles. Eventually, the butterfly and the sunflower decided it was time to go back home. They flew off, clapping the whole way. The sunflower was so smart. It had so much fun during its adventure and knew how to clap just like its butterfly friend.

One day, a fast dog named Max went for a walk. He saw a big room where people were doing yoga. Max was very curious and wanted to join them. So, he entered the room. Inside, Max saw a nice lady. She was doing yoga too. Max said, "Hello, can I do yoga with you?" The nice lady smiled and said, "Yes, Max. You can join us." Max tried to do yoga, but it was hard for him. He was a fast dog, but yoga was slow and calm. Max kept trying and soon he got better. The nice lady and the people in the room were happy to see Max do yoga with them.

Once upon a time, there was a small boat that was about to sail away. The boat had a battery in it which provided the power for it to sail. Suddenly, a seagull flew above the boat and said "Hey! Where are you sailing to? I want to come too!" The boat replied "No, I'm not going anywhere! I'm just enjoying the wind." The seagull was a little confused and said: "Why? You have a battery, you should be able to go anywhere!" The boat answered "No, that battery is too stupid. It doesn't get enough power to make me sail very far." The seagull thought for a moment and said: "That's okay! I can help you get enough power. We can fly around the boat and when we both flap our wings, the battery will be able to get enough energy to make the boat sail." And so the seagull and the boat sailed away together, enjoying their adventure and the wind, thanks to that stupid battery.
