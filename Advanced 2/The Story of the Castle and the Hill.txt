Once there was an impressive castle on a high hill. There was a king who lived there with his family. Every day, the king and his family were happy and safe. One day, the king decided to build an even bigger castle. But to do that, he needed more gas. So, he ordered some gas to be brought to the castle. However, the gas was too heavy and it made the castle fall. The castle fell down faster than anyone could imagine and the family were not safe anymore. Everyone was sad and scared. The family were all very upset that the impressive castle had fallen. But no one could do anything about it, because the gas had gone.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a sack with him. Inside the sack, there were yummy cookies. Tim loved cookies. He wanted to eat them all. But his mom said, "Wait, we will share with friends." At the park, Tim saw his friends playing. He put the sack down and went to play too. They all played and had fun. Tim's mom watched them play. After a while, Tim's mom called them to eat the cookies. When they opened the sack, they found no cookies. Instead, there were yummy fruits! They all were surprised. They didn't know how the cookies turned into fruits. But they ate the fruits and loved them. They felt happy and healthy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, colorful board that she liked to keep in her room. The board was very special because Lily could draw anything she wanted on it. One day, she drew a funny, weak cat with a big smile. "Hello, weak cat!" Lily said to her drawing. "Why do you look so weak and funny?" To her surprise, the weak cat on the board started to talk. "I am weak because I need some food," the cat said. Lily was very shocked, but she wanted to help the cat. Lily drew some food on the board for the weak cat. The cat ate the food and became strong and happy. Lily and the cat played together all day long. From that day on, Lily knew that her special board was magical, and she could make her drawings come to life.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Abigail. She had a modern comb that was just for her. Every morning, Abigail would run to get her comb and display it proudly. She would spread it out on her dressing table and admire how pretty it was. She would comb her hair just like her mom had showed her. She would always feel so special when she saw the way the comb sparkled in the sunlight. One day, Abigail was very excited because it was her birthday. She was going to have all of her friends over to celebrate. When they arrived, Abigail got out her modern comb and displayed it for all her friends to see. Everyone thought it was beautiful and Abigail felt so proud. From then on, Abigail always displayed her modern comb proudly. She would comb her hair each morning and her friends would always comment on how nice she looked. Abigail loved her modern comb very much and it was the perfect thing to make her feel special.

Ben liked to number his toys. He had one car, two dolls, three balls, and four books. He put them in a line on his bed and counted them every day. He liked to hear his own voice say the numbers. He felt smart and happy. One day, his mom told him to clean his room. She said he had too many toys and he should give some away. Ben did not want to do that. He was lazy and did not like to work. He said no to his mom and hid under his bed. He thought she would not find him there. But his mom was not fooled. She looked under the bed and saw Ben and his toys. She was angry and sad. She said Ben was selfish and naughty. She took all his toys and put them in a big bag. She said she would give them to other kids who needed them more. Ben cried and begged his mom to stop. He wanted his toys back. He wanted to number them and hear his voice. But his mom did not listen. She took the bag and left the room. Ben was left alone with no toys, no voice, and no fun. He felt dumb and sad. He wished he had listened to his mom and cleaned his room.
