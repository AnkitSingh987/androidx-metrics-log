One day, a slow snail was on a big leaf. The snail saw a grape. The grape was big and round. The snail said, "I want to eat the grape. But it is so far away." The snail moved slow to the grape. The snail saw a bug. The bug was polishing a rock. The snail said, "Hi bug! Can you help me get the grape?" The bug said, "Yes, I can help you." The bug stopped polishing the rock. The bug and the snail went to the grape. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The grape rolled away! The snail and the bug ran after the grape. They ran and ran, but the grape was too fast. The snail and the bug were sad. But they did not give up. They kept trying to get the grape. And one day, they got it. They ate the grape and were very happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a kind girl named Lily. Lily liked to care for others. She was very compassionate to everyone. She had a pretty veil that she wore on her head. One day, Lily found a sad cat. The cat was hungry and lost. Lily wanted to help the cat. She gave the cat some food and put her veil on the cat's head. The cat was happy and wore the veil. Then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for caring for me, Lily. Because you were compassionate, I will give you a gift." The fairy waved her wand, and Lily's veil turned into a beautiful crown. Lily learned that when you care for others, good things can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a big red chimney. The chimney was very proud of itself. It was tall and strong. But, the chimney was also a little jealous of the other houses with pretty chimneys. One day, a little bird came to the chimney. The bird wanted to make a nest. The bird started to fold small sticks and leaves. The chimney was happy to have a friend. The bird made a beautiful nest on top of the chimney. But then, something unexpected happened. The other houses saw the pretty nest on the big red chimney. Now, they were jealous of the chimney with the bird's nest. The chimney was not jealous anymore. It was happy and proud to have a special friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy gun. He would run around and shoot at things, making loud noises. His favorite color was purple, and he had a purple shirt that he wore every day. One day, Tim's mom made a big dessert for a party. It was a yummy cake with purple frosting. Tim saw the cake and wanted to eat it. But his mom said, "No, Tim. The cake is for the party. You can have some later." Tim was sad, but he went to play with his toy gun. He was running and shooting, not looking where he was going. Suddenly, he bumped into the table with the cake on it. The cake fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Tim's mom was very angry, and the party was ruined. Tim learned that he should be more careful when playing with his toy gun. But now, there was no dessert for anyone, and everyone was sad.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play outside. One day, they saw an elderly lady who lived in a small place near their house. She was sitting on a chair and looked sad. "Hello, lady. Why are you sad?" Anna asked. "Hello, children. I am sad because I have no one to talk to. My family is far away and I have no friends here," the lady said. "That is not good. We can be your friends. We like to talk and play," Ben said. "Really? That is very kind of you. What are your names?" the lady asked. "I am Anna and this is Ben. We are twins. We live in that big place over there," Anna said, pointing to their house. The lady smiled and invited them to sit with her. They talked and played for a while. The lady told them stories about her life and they told her about their school and hobbies. They had a lot of fun. Before they left, the lady said, "Thank you, Anna and Ben. You made me very happy today. You are very good children. I will pray for you and your family." "Thank you, lady. You are very nice. We will pray for you too. We will come back and visit you soon," Ben said. Anna and Ben hugged the lady and ran back to their house. They told their parents about their new friend and how they made her happy. Their parents were proud of them and said, "You did a good thing today. You showed kindness and compassion to someone who needed it. That is what God wants us to do. He will bless you for it." Anna and Ben felt happy and thanked God for their new friend. They learned that it is good to help others and make them smile. They also learned that they can pray for anyone, even if they don't know them well. They looked forward to seeing the lady again and playing with her.
