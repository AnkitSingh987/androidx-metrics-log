Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ella. Ella was very patient. She liked to play ball with her dog, Max. They played in the house. One day, Ella and Max were playing in the house. Ella would pass the ball to Max, and Max would pass it back. They were having so much fun. Then, Ella threw the ball too high. "Oh no!" said Ella. The ball hit the ceiling and made a big hole. Ella and Max looked at the hole. They were scared. Just then, a little bird flew out of the hole. "Hi, I'm Birdy!" said the bird. Ella and Max were surprised but happy. They had a new friend to play with. From that day on, Ella, Max, and Birdy played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big bag of makeup. She loved to play with it and make her face look incredible. One day, she decided to pack her makeup in a small box and take it to her friend's house. When Lily got to her friend's house, they played with the makeup for hours. They put on red, blue, and green colors. They laughed and had so much fun. Lily's friend said, "Wow, our faces look incredible! Let's show our moms." Before they left the room, Lily saw a big, shiny mirror. She looked into the mirror and saw her incredible face. She knew her mom would be so surprised to see her. She smiled and said, "I can't wait to show my mom!" Lily and her friend ran to find their moms. When they saw their incredible faces, the moms laughed and clapped. They said, "You two look so amazing! We didn't know you could make such incredible faces with makeup." Lily and her friend were so happy and proud of what they had done.

Once there was a big bear named Billy. He lived at the edge of the forest. One warm and sunny day, he heard a strange noise from the trees. It was a very sweet smell, like perfume. He had never heard anything like it before. Billy wanted to find out what the special noise was. He looked around but couldn't find it. He asked his best friend, Rabbit, if he had heard it too. Rabbit said, "Yes, I have heard the noise. It sounds like flowers." Billy was very excited. He walked into the forest to find the source of the special noise. He followed the sound until he came to a big and beautiful flower. He smelled the sweet perfume of the flower, and he knew it was the source of the noise. Billy was very happy. He waited in the forest until sundown when the perfume scent filled the air and the stars shone in the night sky. He enjoyed the special smells and sights of the forest, and he never forgot the perfume.

There was a little girl named Jenny. She was always so sorry when she did something wrong. She had a special mirror in her room. She liked to look in the mirror and pretend she was on stage performing for a big crowd. One day, Jenny got mad and broke the mirror. She was so sorry but it was too late. She was scared to tell her parents what she had done. She tried her best to hide the shattered pieces of the mirror. The next morning, Jenny's mom noticed the broken mirror. She asked Jenny what had happened. Jenny couldn't look at her mom and she started to cry. Jenny missed her mirror so much. She knew nothing could make it better. Jenny's mom was very sad and disappointed. She hugged Jenny and tried not to be mad. Jenny was so sorry, but the mirror was gone forever.
