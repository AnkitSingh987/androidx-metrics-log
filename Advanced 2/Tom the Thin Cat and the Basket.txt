Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Tom. Tom liked to learn new things. One day, he found a basket near a big tree. Tom went inside the basket to play. He jumped and rolled in the basket. Suddenly, the basket started to move! It went up and up, into the sky. Tom was scared, but then he saw a big bird with the basket in its beak. The bird took Tom to its nest. Tom made friends with the bird and they played together. Tom was happy he learned something new and unexpected.

Once upon a time, in a normal little house, there was a girl named Lily. She loved to talk to her toys. She had a big toy bear named Teddy. Lily and Teddy were best friends. One night, Lily had a dream. In her dream, she and Teddy went to a big park. They played on the swings and slides. They talked and laughed all day. It was so much fun! When Lily woke up, she told Teddy all about her dream. They both wished they could go to the park for real. So, Lily asked her mom if they could go. Her mom said yes, and they all went to the park together. Lily and Teddy were very happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tree with lots of apples. Tim wanted to pick an apple, but they were too high. His mom said, "Wait, I have an idea!" She took out a sharp knife to help cut an apple from the tree. As they were picking apples, Tim saw a man eating a spicy sandwich. The man started to cough and looked like he was in pain. Tim's mom told him, "Spicy food can be too hot for some people. It's important to be careful when trying new things." Suddenly, the man fell down and couldn't get up. Tim's mom called an ambulance to help him. The ambulance came quickly, and the helpers took the man to the hospital. Tim and his mom felt good that they could help. Later, Tim and his mom talked about their day. Tim's mom said, "Today, we learned two important things. First, we should always be careful with sharp things like knives. Second, we should be careful when trying new things, like spicy food." Tim nodded and said, "Yes, mom. I will always remember to be careful and help others when they need it."

Once there was a little girl, who was very attractive. She loved to play in the park and she had one toy that she especially cherished. It was her most prized possession. This toy was all hers, she loved it and thought it was very special. One day the little girl went to the park, and as she was playing she remembered that she left her toy at home. She was very concerned as she had just realised that her toy was gone. The little girl wondered who had taken her toy. She started to think that maybe someone had taken her toy away because they liked it too. She started to worry and fret. Suddenly, she heard someone in the park say that they had found the little girl's toy. When she looked, she saw it was an unattractive boy who had taken it. He said it was his now because it was "mine". The little girl began to cry, as she couldn't believe her toy had been taken away. The little girl went home with a heavy heart, as she never saw her toy again. She learned an important lesson that day, to always keep an eye on her possessions.

The old man was very rude to the three year old and every day he would shout and destroy things. He was always so angry! The three year old wanted to help the old man, so one day he decided to try something. He took some sticks, some strings and some stones and he built a pitch. The old man was surprised when he saw what the three year old had done. He smiled for the first time in a long time. The old man then decided to play a game of pitch with the three year old. They laughed and had so much fun. After a few days, the old man was no longer rude. The three year old had destroyed the old man's anger with fun and games!
