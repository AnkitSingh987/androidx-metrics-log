Once upon a time, there was a big spider. She was very busy weaving her cobweb. She wanted to make it important and strong. One day, a little bird came to visit the spider. He could see that the spider was tired so he asked what he could do to help. The spider said that she needed food and the bird was happy to provide. For many days, the little bird provided the spider with tasty treats. The spider was very happy and thanked the bird. Together, the spider and bird worked hard to make the cobweb strong and important. They worked until the cobweb was beautiful. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, soft blanket that she loved very much. Lily took her blanket everywhere. It was her best friend. One day, Lily and her blanket went to a picnic. Mommy made a tasty lunch for them. The lunch included yummy sandwiches, sweet fruits, and cold milk. Lily and her blanket sat under a big tree and ate their lunch. But then, a mean wind came and took Lily's blanket away. She tried to catch it, but the wind was too fast. Lily was very sad. She cried and cried, but her blanket was gone forever. The end.

Tom liked to imagine he was a fast rocket. He would run around the yard and make zoom noises. He had a red cape that was his rocket flame. He wanted to fly to the moon and see the stars. One day, he found a big box in the garage. It was heavy and had a picture of a bike on it. Tom wondered what was inside. He hoped it was a bike for him. He tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy. He felt sad. The next day, it was his birthday. He woke up and saw his mom and dad smiling. They said they had a surprise for him. They took him to the garage and opened the box. It was a bike for him! It was shiny and blue and had a bell. Tom was so happy. He hugged his mom and dad and thanked them. They helped him put on his helmet and taught him how to ride the bike. Tom felt scared at first, but soon he was pedaling and balancing. He felt the wind in his hair and the sun on his face. He rang his bell and made zoom noises. He imagined he was a fast rocket. He flew to the moon and saw the stars. He was the happiest boy in the world.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a red scooter that he loved to ride. One day, while playing outside, Tim saw a big, hairy dog. The dog looked sad and lost. Tim wanted to help the dog, so he got off his scooter and walked to the dog. He could hear the dog whimper softly. Tim knew he had to be kind and gentle with the dog. He patted the dog's head and talked to him in a soft voice. The dog wagged its tail, happy to have a friend. Together, Tim and the dog went around the neighborhood to find the dog's home. They found a house with a picture of the hairy dog on the door. The dog's family was so happy to see him! Tim learned that being kind and helpful made him feel good inside. And the dog was happy to be home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a bossy cat named Tom. Tom loved to play music on his little piano. One day, he met a tiny mouse named Jerry. Jerry was scared of Tom, so he would tremble when he saw him. Tom said, "Hey Jerry, do you want to play music with me?" Jerry was still scared, but he liked music too. So he said, "Okay, I will try." Tom played the piano and Jerry played a small drum. They made beautiful music together. Jerry stopped trembling and they both had fun. Now, they are best friends who love to play music every day.
