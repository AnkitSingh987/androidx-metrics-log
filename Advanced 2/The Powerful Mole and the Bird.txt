Once upon a time, there was a powerful mole. He lived in a big hole in the ground. The mole was very strong and could dig very fast. One day, the sun was very hot. It made the ground start to melt. The mole saw a little bird and said, "Bird, the ground is melting, and my home is in danger! What should I do?" The bird looked at the mole and said, "You are powerful! You can dig a new hole somewhere else." The mole tried to dig a new hole, but the ground was too soft. He could not dig a new home. The mole was very sad. The ground melted more and more, and the mole's home was gone. The powerful mole had no place to live, and he was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot liked to run and play in the yard. He was very fast, but sometimes, he was slow too. One day, Spot saw a fence. The fence was there to prevent him from going to the other side. Spot did not like the fence, but he knew it was there to keep him safe. Spot decided to play near the fence. He ran fast and then he ran slow. He had a lot of fun playing by the fence. And he knew that the fence was there to help him stay safe and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toy cars and eat yummy snacks. One day, he went to the fridge to find something to eat. The fridge was big and cold, and it had a light inside. Tim opened the fridge and saw a funny looking box. He asked his mom, "What is this box?" His mom said, "That is a magic box that can make things happen." Tim was very excited and wanted to try it. He took the box and wished for more toy cars. Suddenly, the box started to shake and the fridge light began to blink. The door opened and out came a parade of toy cars! Tim was so surprised and happy. He played with his new toy cars all day long, and he knew that the magic box in the fridge was very special.

Once upon a time, in a lovely little house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to paint with colors. She had red, blue, yellow, and green. Every day, she would make pretty pictures with her colors. One day, Lily met a little bird who couldn't sleep. "Why can't you sleep, little bird?" asked Lily. The bird said, "I need a cozy nest, but I don't have any colors to make it pretty." Lily wanted to help the bird, so she shared her colors with him. Together, they made a beautiful nest with all the colors. The bird was so happy and said, "Thank you, Lily!" That night, the bird slept in the lovely, colorful nest. Lily learned that sharing with others can make them happy. And when she went to sleep, she knew she had done something good.

One day, a girl named Sue found a long rag on the floor. She picked it up and started to play with it. Sue liked to play with her new toy. She would make it fly like a kite and swing it around like a rope. One sunny day, Sue took her long rag outside to play. She started to swing the rag around and around. Suddenly, the rag began to change! It grew bigger and bigger. Sue was very surprised. The long rag turned into a magic cape! Sue put on the cape and started to fly high in the sky. She could see her house and all her friends from up above. Sue had so much fun flying with her magic cape. When it was time to go home, she took off the cape and it turned back into a long rag. Sue knew she had a special secret that made her very happy.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who had a very fragile trophy. She had worked very hard to get it and was so proud. Every day she would hold it in her hands and carry it around. One day, the little girl wanted to show her trophy to her friends. She grabbed it carefully, but she accidentally dropped it on the floor. The trophy was falling apart. She was so sad. She put the pieces back together, but it didn't fit like it used to. That night she cried and cried, hugging the broken pieces close to her. But then something amazing happened. Her mom came in with some glue and special tools and she was able to fix the fragile trophy. The little girl now could show her trophy to her friends and be proud. She hugged the trophy tightly and thanked her mom for helping. The End
