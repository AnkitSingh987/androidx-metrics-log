Once upon a time there was a small, furry creature who went for a walk. He looked around and everywhere he went he saw that everything was lonely. Even the cliff he was walking past showed signs of loneliness. The creature wanted to bring something to the cliff to make it feel less lonely. He used his furry paws to look around for something to bring. He found a little flower and thought it would be perfect. He carefully picked it up and ran towards the cliff. He was determined to put a smile on the cliff's face. He climbed up the side of the cliff and held the flower up high. When he did, a huge gust of wind blew against him and the flower flew away. He was so sad. He had tried so hard to make the cliff feel less lonely. Suddenly a ray of sunshine came out from behind the clouds and the cliff was happy. The creature learned that even when things seem lonely, the sun will always bring hope and happiness at the end of the day.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the kitchen. He wanted to help his mom. She was making a big pot of sauce for dinner. Tim watched as his mom stirred the sauce. It smelled very good. He asked, "Can I help, Mom?" She said, "Yes, you can watch the sauce while I get more things." Tim watched the sauce very carefully. Suddenly, he heard a loud noise. It was his dog, Max. Max had knocked over a chair. Tim ran to help Max, but he forgot about the sauce. When he came back, the sauce was all over the floor. Tim was sad. He said, "I'm sorry, Mom." Mom smiled and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can clean it up and make more sauce together." Tim and his mom cleaned the mess and made a new pot of sauce. They had a fun time cooking and Tim learned to always watch the sauce.

Once upon a time, there was an attractive angel named Angie. She had big, pretty wings and a shiny halo. Angie lived in a beautiful garden with lots of flowers and trees. One day, a sneaky fox came to the garden. He saw Angie's shiny halo and wanted it for himself. The fox said, "Hello, pretty angel. Can I see your halo?" Angie was nice and let the fox see her halo. But the fox was not nice. He tried to steal the halo from Angie. Angie was sad and cried, "Please don't take my halo!" But the fox did not listen. He stole the halo and ran away. Angie was very sad and her garden was not happy anymore. The flowers and trees missed Angie's shiny halo too. And they all wished the sneaky fox never came to their garden.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a cat named Tom. He loved to play near the drain outside the house. One day, his ball got stuck in the drain. Tom was sad. Tom asked his friend, a dog named Max, to help him. Max said, "Tom, I will help you get your ball, but you must promise to share your food with me." Tom refused. He did not want to share his food. Max was sad, but he did not help Tom. Tom tried to get his ball out of the drain by himself, but he couldn't. The ball stayed in the drain forever. Tom was sad, and he learned that he should have shared his food with Max.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy turtle named Tom. Tom had a friend named Rabbit. Rabbit was very fast. One day, they wanted to have a race. Tom knew he would need to try hard to win. The race started, and Rabbit ran very fast. Tom walked slowly, but he did not stop. Rabbit was so far ahead that he thought, "I can take a rest. Tom is too slow." So, Rabbit lay down and fell asleep. Tom kept walking and saw Rabbit sleeping. He walked past Rabbit, and soon, he was ahead. When Rabbit woke up, he saw Tom near the finish line. Rabbit ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. Tom won the race! Rabbit learned that he should not be too sure of himself.
