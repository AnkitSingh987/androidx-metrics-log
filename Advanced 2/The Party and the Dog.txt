Once upon a time, there was a big party. At the party, there was a funny clown. The clown had a red nose and big shoes. All the kids at the party liked the clown because he was honest and kind. One day, the clown was part of a game. The game was to find a hidden toy. The clown and the kids looked everywhere. They looked under chairs and behind doors. The clown was sure he would find the toy first. But then, something unexpected happened. A small dog ran into the room. The dog had the hidden toy in its mouth! The kids laughed and clapped. The clown was happy too. He did not win the game, but he was still the best clown at the party.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She loved to play outside and sing for her friends. One day, when Amy was playing outside, she heard a strange sound coming from a deep cave. She crept closer to the cave and heard a beautiful but mysterious singing. Amy was amazed and curious and she wanted to see who was singing. She carefully stepped inside the cave and saw a brown bear. The bear was singing a spell! Amy was a little scared but the bear noticed her and stopped singing. "Why are you singing this spell?" asked Amy. The bear looked at her and said: "This spell is powerful and can help me create wonderful things. I sing this spell to make them real." Amy smiled and sang along with the bear. She was so happy and she knew that the spell was like magic. They sang and sang until the spell was as deep and powerful as it could be.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park with her mom. Sue saw a big yellow statue in the middle of the park. She wanted to play with the statue, so she asked her mom if she could go near it. Her mom said yes, but told her to hurry because they had to go home soon. Sue ran to the yellow statue and started to touch it. She thought it was very pretty. Suddenly, something unexpected happened. The statue started to move! Sue was very surprised and a little scared. But then, the statue smiled at Sue. It was not a statue at all! It was a nice man dressed in a yellow costume. He was a street performer who pretended to be a statue. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. The man in the yellow costume bowed and went back to being a statue. Sue ran back to her mom, excited to tell her about the fun surprise at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little orange fish named Fin. He liked to swim in a small stream near his home. One day, while he was swimming, he saw a big fish named Biggie. "Hi, Biggie! Can I join you?" asked Fin. Biggie said, "Of course, Fin! Let's swim together." So, Fin and Biggie swam in the stream, playing and having fun. As they swam, they saw something strange. It was a big, shiny rock. They had never seen anything like it before. Fin and Biggie swam closer to take a look. When they touched the rock, it started to move! The rock was not a rock at all! It was a big, friendly turtle named Shelly. Shelly had been sleeping under the mud. Fin and Biggie were very surprised, but happy to meet their new friend. From that day on, Fin, Biggie, and Shelly played together in the stream every day. They had lots of fun and were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was feeling miserable one day. He did not want to play with his toys or talk to his friends. He just sat by himself, looking very sad. His mom saw Tim and asked, "Why are you so miserable, Tim?" Tim opened his mouth and said, "I don't know, Mom. I just feel sad and don't want to play." Mom thought for a moment and said, "I have an idea! Let's play a guessing game." Tim looked at his mom and smiled a little. "Okay, Mom. I will try to guess what you are thinking." Mom put her hand on her mouth and whispered, "I am thinking of something that is very big and has a long neck." Tim thought and thought, and then he shouted, "I guess it's a giraffe!" Mom clapped her hands and said, "Yes, Tim! You guessed it right!" They played the guessing game for a while, and Tim started to feel happy again. His miserable feeling went away, and he laughed and played with his mom. From that day on, every time Tim felt sad, he would play the guessing game with his mom, and it always made him feel better.
