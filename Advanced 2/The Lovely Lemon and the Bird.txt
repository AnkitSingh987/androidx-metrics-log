Once upon a time there was a lovely lemon. Every day it would sit in the sun, watching the birds fly by as they sang their songs. One day, a mixed up bird flew by the lemon. She was tired and confused, not understanding why she was so mixed up. The lemon smiled at the bird and said, “Don’t worry, I understand.” The bird looked confused, not understanding what the lemon was saying. The lemon laughed and said, “Come have a rest on my branch and I’ll tell you a story.” The confused bird felt better as it listened to the stories about birds and lemons, understanding for the first time that everything is lovely. The day was sunny when the bird flew away, thanking the lemon for its help and understanding. The lemon smiled and said, “You’re welcome, my friend.”

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was almost four years old. Lily was scared of the big, loud mixer in the kitchen. Her mom used it to mix things. One day, Lily's mom said, "Next year, you will be big enough to use the mixer." Lily thought about being big enough to use the mixer next year. She wanted to be brave, but she was still scared. Her mom said, "Don't worry, I will help you when the time comes." One day, next year, Lily's mom said it was time to use the mixer. Lily was still scared, but her mom was there to help her. They put the cake mix in the big bowl and turned on the mixer. It was loud, but Lily was brave. Lily and her mom mixed the cake together. The mixer wasn't so scary anymore. Lily was happy and proud of herself. She was a big girl now. And from that day on, Lily was never scared of the mixer again.

Tom and Lily like to play with their toy boat. They take it to the pond and sail it on the water. They pretend they are pirates and look for treasure. One day, they see a big orange ball in the sky. It is the sun, but it looks very bright and round. Tom and Lily think it is a gold coin. "Look, Lily, a gold coin!" Tom says. "We can get it and be rich!" "How can we get it, Tom?" Lily asks. "It is too high and too far." Tom has an idea. He takes out his camera and points it at the sun. He presses the button and makes an image of the sun on the camera screen. "See, Lily, I got the gold coin!" Tom says. "Now we can buy anything we want!" Lily looks at the image and smiles. She thinks Tom is very clever. She hugs him and says, "You are the best pirate, Tom! And I am the best pirate too!" They sail their boat back to the shore and show their image to their mom and dad. They are very proud of their treasure. Mom and dad are very happy and give them a hug and a kiss. They tell them they love them very much. Tom and Lily love them too. They are a happy pirate family.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a colorful candle. The candle had many pretty colors, like red, blue, and yellow. It was a very special candle. It could talk and play with the kids in the house. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to play with the candle. He said, "Candle, let's play hide and seek!" The candle was happy to play with Tim. They played and had lots of fun. But then, Tim's sister, Sue, wanted to play too. She said, "Candle, can I play with you and Tim?" The candle did not want to play with Sue. It refused to play with her. Tim was sad that his sister could not play with them. He told the candle, "Please, let Sue play with us. We can all have fun together." The candle thought about it and agreed. They all played together and had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a nice horse named Happy. Happy lived on a big farm with many friends. They liked to play all day. One day, Happy and his friends decided to have a race. They lined up in a row and waited for the start. A loud "Go!" sent them running as fast as they could. Happy and his friends laughed and had fun. In the end, all the friends crossed the finish line together. They were all winners in the race. Happy and his friends hugged and went back to playing on the farm. They knew that being together made the race even more fun.
