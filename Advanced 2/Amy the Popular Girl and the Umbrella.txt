Title: Amy the Popular Girl and the Umbrella

Once upon a time, there was a popular girl named Amy. She had a lot of friends. Amy had a pretty umbrella that she loved. One day, it was raining outside. Amy took her umbrella to school. At school, there was a boy named Tim. Tim did not have an umbrella. He was sad because he did not want to get wet. Tim saw Amy's umbrella and wanted it. He thought of a plan to grab it from her. When Amy was not looking, Tim grabbed her umbrella. Amy saw Tim take it and was very sad. Tim felt bad for making Amy sad. He gave the umbrella back to Amy and said sorry. Amy forgave Tim, and they became friends. From that day on, they shared the umbrella whenever it rained.

Once upon a time, in a big garden, there lived a little bug. The bug loved to play in the wet grass after it rained. The rain made the grass shiny and fun to jump in. One day, the sun was shining and the bug wanted to play. But the grass was still wet from the rain. The bug asked his mom, "Can I play in the wet grass?" His mom said, "Yes, I permit you to play, but be careful." So, the little bug went outside to play. He jumped and danced in the wet grass. He was so happy! After playing, he went back home and told his mom all about his fun day.

One day, a big dog named Spot went to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was a nice lady who helped animals feel better. Spot was not feeling well, so his mom took him to see her. The veterinarian had a wide room with lots of toys and treats for animals. Spot liked the wide room because he could play and have fun. He leaned on a big, soft bed to rest while he waited for the nice lady to come in. When the veterinarian came in, she gave Spot a treat and checked his ears and paws. She gave him some medicine to make him feel better. Spot was happy to see the nice lady and wagged his tail. Soon, he felt all better and went home with his mom.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As he started to climb, he saw something strange. He saw a monster! The monster was big and green. Tim was scared, but he wanted to be brave. Tim walked up to the monster and said "hello". The monster looked at Tim and smiled. The monster pointed to a big bowl of fruit. The fruit was sour, but Tim and the monster liked it. They ate the sour fruit together and laughed. After they ate, Tim and the monster played games. They ran and jumped and had so much fun. Tim realized that the monster was not scary at all. The monster was just lonely and wanted a friend. Tim and the monster became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a hunter. The hunter liked to spring high in the air. He would jump up and down, playing in the sun. One day, the hunter saw a big, tasty apple on a tree. He wanted to eat it. He tried to spring up and grab it, but it was too high. The hunter did not give up. He practiced his spring jump every day. One day, he jumped so high that he got the tasty apple. He was very happy, and he ate the tasty apple.

One day, a shy cat named Kitty went to play in the backyard. She saw a big ball and wanted to move it. But the ball was very heavy. Kitty tried and tried, but she could not move the ball. A dog named Max saw Kitty and asked, "Do you need help?" Kitty was shy, but she said, "Yes, please help me move the ball." Max came over and together they pushed the ball. The ball began to move! They played with the ball in the backyard all day. They had so much fun. In the end, Kitty was happy she asked for help. The moral of the story is: don't be afraid to ask for help when you need it.
