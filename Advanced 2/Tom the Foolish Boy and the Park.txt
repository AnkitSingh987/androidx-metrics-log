Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a foolish boy named Tom. Tom liked to run and play all day long. One day, Tom found a figure in the park. The figure was shiny and pretty. Tom did not know what it was, but he wanted to keep it. Tom took the figure to his house and showed it to his mom. "Look what I found, Mom!" he said. His mom did not know what it was either, but she told Tom to be careful. Tom did not listen to his mom. He wanted to play with the figure. Tom took the figure outside and started to play with it. He threw it up in the air and caught it. Suddenly, it started to get very cold. Tom started to freeze. The figure was magic and it made everything cold. Tom could not move and he was stuck in the cold. The end.

One day, a boy named Tom found a paint box. Tom felt lucky. He wanted to make pretty pictures. He took out the paint and a big paper. He painted a sun, a tree, and a house. Tom was happy with his picture. Tom showed his picture to his friend, Sue. Sue liked the picture too. She said, "Wow, Tom! You are lucky to find the paint box. Can I paint too?" Tom said, "Yes, let's paint together!" Sue and Tom painted all day. They made many pretty pictures. They read a book about painting. They learned how to mix colors. They had fun and felt lucky to have each other as friends.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She liked to be creative and make things with her toys. She built a big tower with her blocks and put her stuffed animals around it. Suddenly, she heard a voice calling her from outside. "Lucy, come out and play!" It was her friend, Tom. Lucy went outside and saw Tom with a big box. "What's in the box?" she asked. Tom smiled and said, "I have a fun game for us to play!" Tom opened the box and took out a big sheet of paper and some colorful paint. They both got creative and painted a big picture together. They laughed and talked while they painted. When they were done, they called their moms to come see their artwork. Their moms were so proud of their creative work and hung the picture on the wall. Lucy and Tom were happy and had a great day playing together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to speak with her cat, Tom. They liked to play with a big, heavy ball. The ball was red and blue. One day, Lily found a book with many spells. She wanted to do a spell to make Tom speak like a person. Lily read the spell out loud. Tom looked at her and meowed. After the spell, Tom started to speak. He said, "Thank you, Lily!" They both laughed and played with the heavy ball. They talked and played all day long. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a filthy bathroom. It was very dirty. The people who lived in the house did not like the dirty bathroom. They wanted to make it clean and pretty. One day, they decided to design a new bathroom. They picked a color they liked. They chose blue. They painted the walls blue. They put in a new sink and a new tub. They hung a pretty curtain. The bathroom looked very nice. The people in the house were happy. They loved their new bathroom. They made sure to keep it clean. They did not want it to be filthy again. They lived happily in their house with their clean, pretty bathroom.

One day, Miss Linda took her 3 year old student, Leslie, to the museum. Leslie was so excited to explore the museum because she had never been before. She expected to find many interesting things. They arrived at the museum and Miss Linda bought a ticket for access. Leslie was so happy and could feel the excitement bubbling inside her. She jumped and skipped all the way to the front door. But when Miss Linda opened the door, something unexpected happened. Everything they saw was dull. There were no surprises, no new inventions, and no interesting things. Leslie couldn't believe her eyes. She started to panic. Miss Linda saw Leslie's panic, so she tried to calm her down. She said, "Don't panic, Leslie. I promise it will get better." But Leslie wasn't convinced. "What if it doesn't get better?" she asked in an anxious voice. Miss Linda smiled and patted Leslie's shoulder. "Don't worry," she said. "Just trust the ticket and see what happens." Leslie breathed deeply and smiled. She knew that whatever happened, she would get through it with Miss Linda. And that was enough to make her lose her fear and forget her panic.
