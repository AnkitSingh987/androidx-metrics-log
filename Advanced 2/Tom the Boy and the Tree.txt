Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his original toy sword. He would swing it around and pretend to be a hero. One day, Tom went to a big party. There, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tom wanted to help, so he decided to serve Sue with his hero skills. Tom took his sword and looked everywhere for Sue's toy. He looked under the table, behind the chairs, and even in the bushes. Finally, he found the toy under a big tree. Sue was so happy, and they played together for the rest of the party. Tom felt like a real hero, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a restless cat named Tom. Tom loved to run and play all day long. One day, Tom heard some news. A big race was happening in the park. Tom wanted to win the race, so he practiced every day. On the day of the race, Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Max was also in the race. They both stood at the start line, ready to run. Tom said, "Good luck, Max!" Max replied, "Thank you, Tom. Good luck to you too!" The race began, and Tom and Max ran as fast as they could. They passed trees, flowers, and other animals cheering for them. At the finish line, a man was there to measure who won. Tom and Max crossed the finish line at the same time. The man said, "It's a tie! You both win!" Tom and Max were very happy. They had so much fun in the race. They both got a big ribbon to wear. From that day on, Tom and Max became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a beautiful princess. She was getting ready to design the most impressive castle in the land. She took some woods, some rocks and some sticks, and she used her imagination to design the most beautiful castle. Once the castle was done, she decided to make a salad. She took a big bowl, some lettuce and some tomatoes, and then she mixed them together. After the salad was done she looked around and saw a confused bunny. The princess was confused, too. She thought: "What is that bunny doing here?" The bunny said, "I'm looking for a home. Can I stay in your castle?" The princess nodded and said, "Yes, of course you can stay here in my castle." The bunny was very happy and the princess smiled. She was so glad to have made the princess's castle and to give the bunny a new home.

Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bob. Bob was a popular bird. All the other birds liked him. They liked to play with him and talk to him. Bob was a happy bird. One day, Bob and his friends went to play in the park. They played all day long. They played tag, hide and seek, and catch. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Bob and his friends were having so much fun. At the end of the day, the sun went down and it was time to go home. Bob and his friends said goodbye to each other. They were tired but happy. They could not wait to play together again soon.

Mommy was in the kitchen. She had a bowl and a spoon. She was mixing something. It looked yummy. She had a blue belt around her waist. The belt was special. Mommy was making pancakes. She was mixing sugar, eggs and flour. She stirred the mixture with the spoon. It looked perfect. She added some blueberries too. Mommy put some butter in a pan. She poured some of the mixture in the pan. Then she put them in the oven. Mommy waited until the pancakes were done. Mommy took them out of the oven. They were golden and perfect. She put them on a plate and added some maple syrup. Yum! Mommy put her blue belt on and gave them to me. They were delicious!
