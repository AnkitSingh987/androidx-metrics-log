One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big book on the bench. The book had the word "history" on it. Tim's mom said, "Look, Tim! A history book!" Tim grabbed the book and opened it. Inside, there were many pictures of people who could not hear. They were deaf. Tim asked his mom, "Why can't they hear?" His mom said, "They were born deaf, but they can still do many things." As they turned the pages, something unexpected happened. The pictures in the book came to life! The deaf people in the book started talking to Tim and his mom using their hands. They showed Tim and his mom how to talk with their hands too. Tim and his mom were very happy to learn something new and make new friends.

Once upon a time there was a bug who needed a rest. The bug was very tired and decided to take a nap underneath a tree. But then a bigger bug came along. The big bug said, "You have to go away! This is my tree, and I don't want you to use it for resting!" The bug felt very sorry and responded, "Please, I'm so tired and I just need a place to rest." The big bug was sad but saw how tired the little bug was and said, "Okay, you can stay here as long as you need. But when you're done, you have to go!" The little bug thanked the big bug and said, "Thank you. I'm sorry for disturbing you." The big bug replied, "It's okay, just be sure to rest and then leave when you're done." The little bug was so grateful and found a comfortable spot under the tree. It found its rest and was soon dreaming of its next adventure. The end.

One day, a girl named Lucy went to her room to examine her toys. She loved to play with her toys and make sure they were all fit and happy. She had a special folder where she kept pictures of her toys, so she could remember their names and faces. While Lucy was looking at her folder, something unexpected happened. A strong wind blew through her window and took the folder away! Lucy was very sad and didn't know what to do. She looked out the window and saw her folder flying high in the sky. Lucy had an idea. She asked her toy bird, named Blue, to help her get the folder back. Blue was a magic bird and could fly very fast. Blue flew up into the sky and caught the folder just in time. Lucy was so happy to have her folder back, and she knew that her toys were not just fit and happy, but also magical!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big rain. The rain went on and on, and soon there was a flood. The water went up and up, and the animals were scared. They all ran to a big hill to be safe. Among the animals, there was a little bird. The bird was not original like the other birds. It could not fly high. But it had a big heart. The bird saw a little ant who was stuck in the flood. It wanted to help the ant. The bird flew down and picked up the ant with its beak. It took the ant to the big hill where all the other animals were safe. The other animals saw the bird's kind act and learned that even if you are not original, you can still help others. The moral of the story is that you don't have to be original or special to help others and make a difference.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy called a pistol. It was white and he loved to play with it. He would run around the house, pointing the pistol and making "bang bang" sounds. Tim's mom always told him to be careful with his toy and not to point it at people. One day, Tim went to the park to play with his friends. He brought his white pistol with him. He showed his friends the pistol and they all wanted to play with it too. Tim's mom told him to let his friends have a turn with the pistol. So, Tim let them play with his toy. While they were playing, Tim's mom talked to them about being kind and sharing with others. She said it's important to let others have a chance to play with our toys. Tim and his friends learned that sharing is good and makes everyone happy. From that day on, they always shared their toys with each other and had lots of fun together. The moral of the story is to share and be kind to others, so everyone can have fun and be happy.
