Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a special gift. She could talk to animals. Lily was very attractive, and everyone liked her. She had many friends. One day, Lily went to the park. She saw two birds fighting. They were taking turns to peck each other. Lily felt sad for them. She wanted to help, so she talked to the birds. She told them that fighting was not good. They should be friends and play together. The birds listened to Lily. They stopped fighting and became friends. They took turns to play on the swing. Everyone in the park was happy to see them play. The moral of the story is that talking and being kind can solve problems and make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play pretend with his friends. They would pretend to be many things, like animals, heroes, and sometimes even thieves. One day, Tim and his friends pretended to be thieves. They took things from each other and hid them. Tim took his friend's toy and put it in his room. But Tim's friend got very sad. He cried because he loved his toy so much. Tim's mom saw this and felt bad for the little boy. Tim's mom told Tim that it's not nice to pretend to be a thief and take things from others. She said that it could make others feel sad and spoiled their fun. Tim understood the lesson his mom taught him. He gave back the toy to his friend and said sorry. From that day on, Tim and his friends never pretended to be thieves again. They learned that taking things from others is not good, and it's important to be kind and share. And they all lived happily, playing together and having fun.

One day, a graceful cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she went up the tree, she saw a file. The file was stuck in the tree. Lily thought, "How did that get there?" Lily tried to get the file, but it was hard. She asked a bird friend, "Can you help me get the file?" The bird said, "Yes, I will help you." The bird flew to the file and tried to pinch it with her beak. With a pinch, the bird got the file out of the tree. The bird gave the file to Lily. Lily said, "Thank you, bird!" They both smiled and were happy. Lily took the file home, and they played together all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ella. She was very proud, and loved taking baths with her favourite soap. One day, when she was in the bathroom, she heard a noise. Ella asked, "Who's there?" A voice replied, "It's me, the soap! I'm so glad you're using me to take your bath. Welcome!" Ella couldn't believe it! She was so surprised that the soap was talking to her. She was very proud to have such a special soap. The soap said, "I'm here to talk to you about taking a bath. Together we can make sure your body is always clean and healthy." Ella was so excited that the soap wanted to talk to her. The two of them talked about cleaning her body with soap, and all the fun activities parents can do together with their kids. Ella was so happy that the soap was there to welcome her.

One day, a girl named Sally wanted to create a new game. She went to her baby brother's room and saw his crib. She thought it would be fun to make a game with the crib. Sally began to play with the crib, and her brother laughed and clapped his hands. As Sally played with the crib, she found something disgusting under it. It was green and slimy. Sally didn't like it, but her baby brother thought it was funny. He laughed and tried to touch it. Sally moved it away from him, but the green slime started to grow bigger. Suddenly, the green slime was not disgusting anymore. It turned into a big, fluffy, and friendly monster. The monster wanted to play with Sally and her brother. They all played together and had so much fun. The crib became a magical place where they could play with their new monster friend.
