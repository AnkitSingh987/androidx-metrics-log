One day, a big ball and a small ball were playing in the park. They liked to roll around and have fun. They saw a shiny jewel on the ground. It was different from other things they had seen. The big ball said, "I want the jewel!" The small ball said, "No, I want it!" They both wanted the jewel and did not want to share. They started to roll very fast at the jewel to see who could get it first. But then, something unexpected happened. A little girl picked up the jewel before they could get to it. She looked at the big ball and the small ball and said, "Why are you fighting? It's better to share and be friends." The big ball and the small ball realized she was right. They decided to play together and be happy, even without the jewel.

One day, a girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. They saw a big steam train. "Choo choo!" said the train. Amy and her mom got on the train and sat down. They were very happy. The train went fast. Amy saw a man with a big hat. He looked serious. "What do you have?" asked Amy. The man showed her a big box. Inside the box, there were many ice creams. "Would you like to taste one?" he asked. Amy picked a pink ice cream. But when she tasted it, it was not cold! It was hot like steam! "Oh no!" said Amy. The serious man laughed. "This is a special ice cream. It is warm, like the steam train we are on!" Amy and her mom laughed too. They had a fun day on the steam train with the warm ice cream.

One day, a little girl called Mary was walking in a forest. She looked around, and saw a tall fence. The fence was deep and went on and on. Mary heard a voice coming from the other side of the fence. She was puzzled - who was it? "Hello!" called the voice. "Come join me." Mary thought for a moment, and then decided to go through the deep fence. She stepped through it and saw a big furry creature. The creature said, "Welcome! My name is Bob and I live in this forest." Mary was amazed. She and Bob made many friends, and Mary was so happy that she eventually forgot all about the deep fence.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Jojo. He liked to hop around his pond, looking for tasty insects. One day, Jojo saw a dish on the grass. He hopped closer to take a better look. "What's this?", asked Jojo, who was a bit ignorant about what the dish was. The dish answered: "I'm a dish! You can put food in me!" Jojo was delighted and hopped on top of the dish. He hopped up and down. "Can you also hop like me?", asked Jojo. The dish answered with a slow shake of its handle: "No, I don't hop, but I can make you feel happy if you put some food in me!" Jojo thought this over, then decided it sounded like a good idea. So, he hopped away and gathered some delicious insects and put them in the dish. He happily hopped around, content that he had made a new friend.

Once there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to eat strawberries. One day, she saw a big, red strawberry in her yard. She wanted to pick it, but it was very stubborn and would not come off the plant. Lily started to complain. "I want the strawberry, but it won't let me have it!" she said. Her mom heard her and said, "Don't worry, sweetie. Maybe the strawberry will be ready tomorrow." The next day, Lily went to the yard again. She saw the stubborn strawberry was still there, but it looked even bigger and redder. She tried to pick it again, and this time, it came off the plant easily. Lily was so happy and knew that waiting was a good idea. Lily took the big strawberry to her mom. They washed it and cut it into pieces. Lily and her mom shared the yummy strawberry, and it was the best one they ever had. They both smiled, and Lily learned that sometimes, you just have to be patient.
