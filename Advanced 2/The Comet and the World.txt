Once upon a time there was a comet in the sky. One day it started to move, and it came closer and closer to the earth. Suddenly, the ground started to shake and everything froze. Everything was organized in the frozen world: people, animals and trees all stayed still. One brave boy thought he could do something. He wanted to make the world move again. He ran up to the comet and shouted at it. He tried to tell the comet to go away and leave the earth alone. But the comet didn't listen. Instead it made a loud noise and the world got darker and darker. The boy was scared and he held his breath. He knew that if something didn't happen soon, the world would stay frozen forever. Unfortunately, nothing ever changed. The world stayed frozen and the comet kept moving. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved playing outside in the sunshine. Tom was walking in the park when he stumbled upon a pebble in his path. He bent down to pick it up and decided he would keep it as a souvenir. Suddenly, Tom heard a voice. It was his mother. "Tom, be careful! Don't pick up strange pebbles," she warned. Tom replied, "But why Mom? This pebble is so shiny and pretty, and I like it!" His mom shook her head. "It might seem nice now, but it would be a stupid thing to do. You should always listen when your parents tell you something." Tom nodded and put the pebble back down on the ground. "Okay, I won't pick it up again," he agreed. Then, he ran off to play with his friends, feeling safer now that he had listened to his mom. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree with many limes. A little boy named Tim saw the tree and wanted to get a lime. He asked his friend, Pat, to help him. Tim said, "Pat, can you help me get a lime from the tree?" Pat said, "Yes, let's get a long stick to reach the lime." They found a long stick and got a big lime. They saw that the lime was very generous with juice. They decided to spray the juice on their food. Tim and Pat laughed and enjoyed their meal with the lime juice. They were happy they found the lime tree.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Tom. Tom was always tired and would yawn a lot. He lived in a small house with his friend, a little mouse named Jerry. One day, Tom and Jerry went to find some food. They saw a big cake on a table. The cake looked delicious. Tom and Jerry wanted to eat the cake, so they climbed up the table to get it. Tom took a big bite of the cake and yawned. Jerry did the same. Suddenly, the cake began to shake and fell off the table. Tom and Jerry fell too. The cake broke into pieces on the floor, and Tom and Jerry were sad. They did not get to eat the delicious cake after all.

One day, a girl named Sue found a big glove in her room. She wanted to play with it. She put the glove on her hand and went outside. Sue saw her friend Tom playing with a toy truck. Tom was trying to load small rocks into the truck. "Hi Tom, can I help you?" Sue asked. Tom looked at her big glove and said, "Yes, your glove can help us load the rocks faster!" So, they started to load the rocks into the truck. After a while, Sue started to feel dizzy from all the work. Suddenly, the glove began to move on its own! It picked up all the rocks and loaded them into the truck very fast. Sue and Tom were so surprised! They looked at each other and laughed. The big glove had a magic power that helped them finish their work. From that day on, Sue and Tom played together with the magic glove and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim and his sister, Sue, were in their room. They looked up at the ceiling and saw a big, pretty picture on it. They wanted to talk about the picture. They sat down and started to discuss it. Tim was very patient, waiting for Sue to say what she thought about the picture. Sue said it looked like a happy sun with a big smile. Tim agreed and said the sun was there to make them feel good. They both felt happy looking at the pretty picture. The next day, they told their friends about the picture on the ceiling. Their friends also wanted to discuss it. They all learned to be patient and listen to each other's thoughts. The moral of the story is to be patient and listen when talking with others.
