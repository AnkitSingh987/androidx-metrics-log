Once upon a time, there was a big room with a bright board. The board was very dirty. A girl named Lucy wanted to clean it. She got a cloth and started to clean the board. The board became very clean and shiny. One day, a little bird came to the room. The bird saw the bright board and thought it was a big pond. The bird wanted to take a bath in the pond. It flew to the board and tried to splash in the water. But there was no water! The bird was confused. Lucy saw the bird and started to laugh. She knew the bird thought the board was a pond. She decided to help the bird. Lucy got a small bowl of water and put it near the board. The bird was happy and took a bath in the bowl. Lucy and the bird became good friends.

One day, a little seal was swimming in the sea. He was scared because he was alone. He did not like being alone. The little seal saw a big fish and said, "Hello, big fish! I am scared. Can you swim with me?" The big fish did not like to swim with scared seals. He said, "No, I do not swim with scared seals. Go away!" The little seal was sad, but he did not complain. The little seal swam and swam until he saw a big shark. The big shark had a big smile. The little seal did not know that sharks like to eat seals. He said, "Hello, big shark! I am scared. Can you swim with me?" The big shark said, "Yes, little seal. I will swim with you." The little seal was happy, but he did not know that the big shark was not a good friend. They swam together, and the little seal was not scared anymore. But then, the big shark ate the little seal. The little seal should have been more careful. The big shark swam away, and the sea was quiet again.

One day, a fast car wanted to zoom in a big race. The car was very fast and loved to zoom all day. The driver was happy and excited to race. They wanted to show everyone how fast they could zoom. At the race, there was a judge. The judge would watch the cars and pick the fastest one. All the cars wanted to be the fastest and win the race. The fast car was sure it would win because it could zoom so fast. The race began and the fast car zoomed ahead of all the others. It was almost at the end when something unexpected happened. A big rock was in the way and the fast car could not zoom around it. The other cars zoomed past the fast car and finished the race. The fast car did not win and felt very sad. The judge picked another car as the fastest. The fast car learned that sometimes, even if you can zoom fast, you might not always win.

Once there was a puppy named Spotty. Spotty loved playing and chasing things around the garden. Every day, Spotty would mark the grass and chase around the butterflies. One day, Spotty wasn't feeling so well. His legs were achy and he didn't want to play. Spotty's mommy and daddy decided it was time to take Spotty to the veterinarian. The veterinarian was very busy. She was taking care of lots of other animals too. She took a look at Spotty and gave him some medicine. She said he should feel better in no time. Spotty's mommy and daddy took him home. Spotty wasn't allowed to play for a couple of days. But he was so eager to get back to running and chasing, he wouldn't listen. One day, Spotty wasn't feeling well again. His legs were aching and he was very sad. Spotty's mommy and daddy had to take him back to the veterinarian. The veterinarian told Spotty's mommy and daddy that Spotty needed to rest. She said that Spotty was too busy playing and needed to take a break. Spotty listened carefully and promised to be better. The moral of the story is that it's important to take breaks, even if we are having fun. If we don't give our bodies a chance to rest, we might not be able to do the things we love.
