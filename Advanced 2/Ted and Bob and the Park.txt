Ted and Bob were two best friends. They were walking in the park one day and they saw a big board. On the board was a big piece of candy. The candy looked really yummy and so Ted and Bob wanted to try it. When they got close to the board, Ted reached for the candy. But Bob said that Ted should not take it. Bob said that it might be sour. So they decided to wait and find out. Soon a lady walked up to the board. She grabbed the candy and put it in her mouth. Ted and Bob watched but they couldn't tell if the candy was sour. The lady smiled at Ted and Bob. "It's sour candy" she said. Ted and Bob met the lady's eyes with disappointment. But she just smiled and said that it's always good to try something new. Ted and Bob thanked the lady and walked away. They knew that it's always important to think before doing something. They were happy to have met the kind lady in the park.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a mighty dog named Max. Max loved to run and play with his friends. Every day, they would run in the green forest, chasing each other and having fun. One day, Max saw a big green ball. He wanted to play with it, but his friends told him, "No, Max! That ball is not ours. We should not take it." But Max did not listen. He took the green ball and started to run with it. As Max was running, he tripped and fell. The green ball rolled away, and Max was hurt. His friends came to help him, but they were sad. Max learned that taking things that are not yours can lead to a bad ending. The moral value of the story is to always listen to your friends and not take things that do not belong to you.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom had a book with many interesting pictures. He liked to look at the pictures every day. One day, Tom saw a picture that reminded him of a number. The number was three. Tom wanted to find three things like in the picture. He went outside to look for them. He saw a cat, a dog, and a bird. Tom was very happy. He found the three things just like the number in the picture. But then, the cat chased the bird, and the dog chased the cat. They ran very fast and went far away. Tom was sad because he lost the three things. He went back home and looked at his book again.

One day, a little gray bunny was hopping in the forest. The bunny saw a big hole. The bunny was very curious and wanted to see what was inside the hole. So, the bunny went to the hole and called out, "Hello! Is anyone there?" A grumpy voice came from the hole, "Why do you disturb me?" It was a big, gray bear who lived in the hole. The bear did not like to be disturbed. He liked to sleep all day. The bunny said, "I am sorry, Mr. Bear. I did not mean to disturb you. I just wanted to see who lived in the hole." The bear was still grumpy, but he decided to forgive the bunny. The bunny and the bear became friends, and they played together in the forest every day. The bear learned that it was okay to be disturbed sometimes, and the bunny learned to be more careful when exploring new places.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to sing. One day, she found a microphone in her toy box. She was very happy and excited to sing with it. Amy decided to sing in front of her friends. But when she started to sing, the microphone disappeared! Amy felt embarrassed. She did not know what to do. Her friends were waiting for her to sing. Then, something unexpected happened. A magic bird came and gave Amy a new microphone. Amy was surprised and happy. She sang for her friends and everyone clapped. The magic bird had saved the day!
