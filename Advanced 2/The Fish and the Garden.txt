Once upon a time, there was a small fish who lived in a beautiful reef. He had never been outside of the reef and was feeling very nervous. One day, he decided to explore what was outside. He said goodbye to his family and all of his friends, and started to swim away. He began to explore the ocean, and he saw so many incredible things. He saw a coral garden, and a school of colorful fish. He even found some sunken treasure! But suddenly, he noticed something strange. A huge creature was moving towards him, and he froze in fear. He quickly heard a voice, "Don't be so nervous! I'm not going to hurt you. My name is Rex, and I'm here to help you find your way around the ocean." Rex asked the fish what he was looking for, and the fish told him he wanted to be able to explore and see the ocean. Rex smiled and said, "That's a great goal. I can help you design a map of the ocean so you won't get lost! Then you can explore safely everywhere." The fish thanked Rex and he was so excited. Soon he was out exploring, enjoying the beauty of the reef and the big blue ocean with the help of his design. And he never again felt nervous about venturing outside the reef.

Lily and Max are friends. They like to play together at school. Today, they are hungry. They go to the lunch room with their teacher. "Look, Lily, we have sandwiches and apples for lunch. Do you like them?" Max asks. "Yes, I do. They are normal and good. What do you have on your sandwich?" Lily answers. "I have cheese and ham. Do you want to try some?" Max offers. "OK, thank you. I will give you some of mine too. I have peanut butter and jelly." Lily says. They trade some of their sandwiches and eat them. They are happy and full. They drink some milk and water. Then, they go back to their classroom. "Did you have a good lunch, Lily and Max?" their teacher asks. "Yes, we did. We had normal and yummy lunch. We shared our sandwiches and apples." Lily and Max say. "That's very nice of you. Sharing is caring. I'm glad you enjoyed your lunch." their teacher says. Lily and Max smile. They are good friends. They like normal lunch.

One day, a small cat named Tim went to play outside. He saw a modest stack of rocks in the yard. Tim was very curious. He wanted to examine the rocks. Tim looked at the rocks very closely. He saw that one of the rocks had a picture on it. The picture was of a big, shiny key. Tim thought that maybe the key was hiding somewhere. He decided to look for it. Tim looked under the rocks, in the grass, and behind the trees. Finally, he found the shiny key under a bush. The key opened a small box that was hidden in the yard. Inside the box, Tim found a beautiful toy just for him. He was so happy that he examined the rocks and found the key.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful bird named Bob. Bob liked to sing and make pretty sounds. He lived in a big tree with his best friend, Sam the squirrel. They liked to spend time together and play games. One day, Bob and Sam were playing hide and seek. Bob closed his eyes and counted. Sam ran to find a good hiding spot. When Bob opened his eyes, he started to look for Sam. He listened for any sound that Sam might make. "Where are you, Sam?" Bob asked. He heard a little sound and flew towards it. But it was not Sam. It was a big, mean cat. The cat saw Bob and wanted to catch him. Bob tried to fly away fast, but the cat jumped and caught him. The cat was very happy to have caught the colorful bird. But Bob and Sam were very sad because they could not play together anymore. And that was the bad ending of their fun day.
