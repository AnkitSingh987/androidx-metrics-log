Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a long, fun trip coming up with his family. He was very excited to go on the big plane for the first time. Tim's mom told him to listen and be good so they could all have a great time. Before they left, Mom gave Tim a very important job. She asked him to hold their passports safe. Passports are special books that help people go on trips far away. Tim felt proud and promised to take good care of them. On the day of the trip, Tim listened to his mom and held the passports tight. He didn't let go of them, even when he saw a big, shiny toy at the airport. When they got to the big plane, the nice lady asked for the passports. Tim gave them to her with a big smile. The family had a great trip, and Tim learned that listening and being responsible can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a family of rocks. They lived in a small rock house. The family had a big rock, a medium rock, and a little rock. The little rock was very obedient. He always did what the big rocks told him to do. One day, the big rocks said, "Little rock, please go to the store and buy some food for dinner." The little rock said, "Yes, I will do that." So, the little rock went to the store. He walked and walked until he got there. At the store, the little rock saw a big, shiny rock. It was not like any rock he had ever seen before. The shiny rock said, "Hello, little rock! I am a magic rock. I can make one wish come true for you." The little rock was very surprised. He thought about his family and said, "I wish for my family to be happy forever." The magic rock smiled and said, "Your wish is granted." Then, the little rock went back home with food for dinner. When he got home, he told his family about the magic rock. The family was so happy and they all hugged each other. From that day on, the rock family was always happy, and they loved their obedient little rock even more.

One day, a small cat named Tom found a cane in the park. He picked it up and started to act like an old man. He walked slow and talked with a funny voice. Tom's best friend, a dog named Max, saw him and started to laugh. "Why are you acting like that?" Max asked Tom. Tom said, "I found this cane and I want to play with it. Let's pretend we are old men." So, Max found another cane and they both acted like old men. But then, a big wind came and blew their canes away. Tom and Max were sad and wanted their canes back. They looked and looked but could not find them. Just when they were about to give up, a bird flew down and gave them their canes back. The bird said, "I saw you having fun and wanted to play too. Can I be an old man with you?" Tom and Max smiled and said, "Yes, let's all act like old men together!" So, they all played and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there lived a unique lizard named Lizzy. Lizzy was not like the other lizards. She had a beautiful, rainbow tail that everyone loved to look at. She was very happy and loved to play with her friends. One day, Lizzy and her friends were playing a game. They had to pay with leaves to play the game. Each friend gave one leaf to play. Lizzy gave a big, green leaf from her favorite tree. They all laughed and had fun playing the game. After they finished playing, Lizzy and her friends said "thank you" to each other for the fun day. They went back to their homes in the garden. Lizzy looked at her rainbow tail and smiled. She knew she was unique, and that made her very happy.
