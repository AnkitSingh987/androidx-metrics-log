Once upon a time, there was a bird named Bob. Bob was building a nest. He wanted to set the nest very high in a tree. Bob liked high places because he could see all his friends from up there. Bob worked hard to make his nest. He used sticks, leaves, and even some soft grass. He set the nest on a high branch, and it looked great. Bob was happy and proud of his nest. One day, a big wind came and blew the high nest down. Bob was sad, but his friends came to help. Together, they set a new nest even higher in the tree. Bob was happy again and loved his new high nest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Will. Will was an incredible kid who loved to play and laugh with his friends. One day, he went to the park with his mom to play on the swings. While playing, Will saw a big dog. The dog came close to him and started to bark. Will got scared and began to cry. His friends saw him and said, "Don't be embarrassed, Will. The dog just wants to play." Will's mom came over and patted the dog. The dog stopped barking and wagged its tail. Will stopped crying and smiled. He realized that there was no need to be embarrassed. From that day on, Will and the dog became great friends and played together at the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big rain. It was a terrible rain. It made everything wet and dirty. The sun was hiding behind the clouds, and the animals were hiding too. One day, the rain stopped. The sun came out and started to wash everything. The sun washed the trees, the grass, and the flowers. The animals came out to play, and they were happy. As the sun washed the sky, a beautiful rainbow appeared. The rainbow had many colors, and it made everyone smile. The terrible rain was gone, and now everything was clean and bright. The animals played under the rainbow, and they were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy oven. One day, he put a pretend cake in the oven and said, "I will make the best cake ever!" After a while, Tim heard a loud noise. It was his rocking chair. The rocking chair spoke to him and said, "Hello, Tim! Can I have some cake too?" Tim was surprised but happy to share his cake with the rocking chair. Tim took the pretend cake out of the oven and gave a piece to the rocking chair. They both laughed and enjoyed the cake together. From that day on, Tim and his rocking chair became the best of friends, sharing many more pretend cakes and having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a man who wanted to catch an automobile. He set off on a journey to find one. The man walked for a long time, and soon he arrived at a distant place. There he saw an amazing automobile! So he ran towards it. The man shouted, "I will catch the automobile!" But then, a voice came from the automobile. "No!" it said. "You can't catch me!" The man replied, "Why not? I want to take you with me!" The voice laughed. "You don't have the keys," it said. "So you can't catch me!" The man was sad. He said goodbye to the automobile, and started his journey back home. He thought to himself, one day I will catch that distant automobile.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big box that she liked to play with. The box was a good fit for her toys. Lucy played with her box every day, and it made her very happy. One day, Lucy went to her friend's house to play. She had so much fun, but she forgot to bring her box with her. When it was time to go home, Lucy felt sad. She missed her box and wanted to play with it again. When Lucy got home, her mom saw that she was sad. She asked, "What's wrong, Lucy?" Lucy told her mom that she missed her box. Her mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, your box is right here!" Lucy's face lit up with joy, and she played with her box all night long.
