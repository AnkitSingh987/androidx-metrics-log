Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a powerful king. He had a big, pretty castle. The king loved to spread joy all around the town. He gave people yummy food, soft blankets, and fun toys. One day, the king had an idea. He wanted to have a match. He told all the people in the town to come and play. They would see who was the best at running, jumping, and playing games. The day of the match came, and everyone was excited. The sun was shining, and the sky was blue. The people played games and had fun. The king was happy to see everyone smiling. At the end of the day, the king gave out prizes to the winners. The town was full of joy, and everyone lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl named Sally. Sally had a basket. In the basket, there were pretty flowers. Tim wanted to play with Sally. "Can I play with you?" Tim asked. Sally was careful with her flowers. She did not want Tim to mess them up. "No, you can't play with me," Sally said. She refused to let Tim play. Tim was sad. He ran away from Sally. He did not see a big hole in the ground. Tim fell into the hole. No one could help him get out. Sally and her basket of flowers went home.

Maya and her mom were in the hospital. Maya looked around and marveled at all the colorful things in the room. "Mom, why are we here?" she asked. "We're here because I love you and I care about you," her mom replied. Maya was so happy; she hugged her mom tightly. She loved that her mom cared about her so much. The nurses came in and asked Maya questions, but she was too shy to answer them. Her mom answered for her, making sure all the nurses heard how much she cared for Maya. The nurses smiled and patted Maya's head, then gave her a colorful sticker. Maya looked around the hospital and smiled to herself. She knew her mom had brought her here because she cared.

Once upon a time, there was a big desk in a room. A small boy named Tim played near the desk every day. Tim liked to pretend he was a lion and would roar loudly. He would say, "Roar! I am a big lion!" One day, Tim found it difficult to open the desk drawer. He pulled and pulled, but it would not open. He tried to roar like a lion to make the desk open. "Roar!" he said, but the desk did not listen. Then, something unexpected happened. The desk started to roar back at Tim! "Roar!" said the desk. Tim was surprised and scared. But soon, he found out that it was just his cat, Fluffy, hiding inside the desk. Fluffy had learned to roar like Tim. They both laughed and roared together.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to wake up early and watch the sunrise. Every day, he would find a new spot to see the beautiful colors in the sky. Tom was always looking for an original place to enjoy the view. One day, Tom saw a big hill and thought, "That would be a great place to watch the sunrise!" So, he woke up very early and climbed the hill. When he reached the top, he saw a little girl named Sue. Tom asked, "Did you come to see the sunrise too?" Sue nodded and said, "Yes, but I'm scared. I've never been up this high before." Tom tried to help Sue feel better, but she was still scared. They sat down and waited for the sunrise together. As the sky turned pink and orange, Tom and Sue watched the sunrise. But then, dark clouds came and covered the sun. It began to rain, and they couldn't see the sunrise anymore. Tom and Sue were sad and wet. They had to go back down the hill without seeing the beautiful sunrise they had hoped for.
