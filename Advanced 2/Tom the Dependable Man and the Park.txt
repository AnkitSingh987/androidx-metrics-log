Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dependable man named Tom. Tom worked as an ambulance driver. He was always there to help people when they needed him. One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in the park. She fell down and hurt her knee. Lucy cried and cried for help. Tom heard her cry and came to answer her call. He took her in his ambulance and drove her to the hospital. There, the doctor fixed Lucy's knee, and she stopped crying. Lucy learned that if you need help, you should call out for it. People like Tom, the dependable ambulance driver, will come and answer your call. The moral of the story is that it's good to ask for help when you need it, and there are dependable people who will be there for you.

Jessie was very excited. It was a bright, sunny day and she was going to a picnic with her Grandma. Grandma offered to let Jessie pick out her own dress for the outing. Jessie was so excited about her new dress that she ran around the house in it until she noticed something strange. Her zipper on the back of the dress wouldn't close! She started to panic, but Grandma told her not to worry. Grandma grabbed her sewing kit and very quickly fixed the zipper. It was as good as new in no time. Jessie was so thrilled that she twirled around the room admiring her bright dress. Grandma smiled and before they knew it, they were off to the picnic.

One day, a boy found a toy rocket. It was the only one available. He wanted to play with it. He took the rocket to the park. At the park, he put the rocket in a big puddle. He wanted to see the rocket sail. The rocket started to sail in the water. The boy was happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The rocket sailed out of the puddle and into the sky! The boy was so surprised. The rocket went higher and higher. Now, the boy had a new game to play. He wanted to catch the rocket when it came back down.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to paint. He took his brush and paint, and started to paint the moon. Tim loved the moon. It was big and bright in the night sky. Tim had a loyal dog named Max. Max was always with Tim. They played and had fun together. One day, while Tim was painting, Max said, "I want to help you paint the moon!" Tim was very happy. He gave Max a brush and paint, and they started to paint the moon together. As they painted, something unexpected happened. The moon started to look sad. Tim and Max looked at each other, confused. They tried to paint the moon happy again, but it didn't work. The moon stayed sad. Tim and Max felt sad too. They didn't understand why the moon was sad, and they couldn't make it happy again.

Once upon a time there was a jolly cat. Every day, she loved to play with her friends in the garden. One day, she smelled something strange in the air. She knew it was oil. She didn't know why this oil was everywhere, but it made her and her friends very sad. They couldn't play in the garden anymore. The jolly cat was mad. She wanted to know who had put all this oil in the garden. So she asked her friends. One by one, they all said, "It wasnt me!" The jolly cat thought and thought until one day, she figured it out. It was a grouchy bear who had put the oil in the garden. She told her friends, and they were very happy. The jolly cat said, "Let's go talk to the grouchy bear and make him take the oil away, so we can play in the garden again!" Her friends agreed and they all marched to the grouchy bear's house. When they arrived, they said, "Please take the oil away" The grouchy bear thought for a while and then said, "Alright. I will take the oil away." The jolly cat and her friends were so happy. Now they could play again in the garden. They thanked the grouchy bear and ran off to play in the garden. And they all lived happily ever after.
