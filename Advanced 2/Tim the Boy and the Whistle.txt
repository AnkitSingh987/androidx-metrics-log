Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to whistle all day long. He would whistle while he played, and he would whistle while he worked. One day, Tim found a big red button on the floor. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a button!" he said. His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! But remember, we must always put things back where they belong." Tim wanted to keep the button, but he knew his mom was right. He looked around and saw his neighbor, Mr. Brown, had a red coat with a missing button. Tim went to Mr. Brown's house and gave him the button. Mr. Brown was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." As Tim walked back home, he whistled a happy tune. He felt good because he had helped someone. And from that day on, Tim always remembered to do the right thing and help others when he could. The moral of the story is: It's important to be kind and help others.

Once upon a time, there was a big, soft rug. The rug was in a room where a little boy named Timmy played. Timmy liked the rug because it was so soft and fun to sit on. He would admire how pretty and colorful it was. Timmy had a dog named Spot. Spot was a little bit crazy. He liked to run and jump all around the room. One day, while Timmy was playing on the rug, Spot ran into the room. He was going very fast and jumped onto the rug. But when Spot jumped on the rug, something unexpected happened. The rug moved and showed a secret door on the floor. Timmy and Spot were very surprised. Timmy and Spot opened the secret door and found a little room with lots of toys. They played together in the secret toy room. They had so much fun that they forgot all about the rug. From that day on, Timmy and Spot loved the secret toy room even more than the rug.

There was a fish and a dolphin who were very good friends. They would always swim side by side, have underwater adventures and tell each other stories of what they had done that day. One day, the fish and the dolphin wanted to look their best to impress their friends, so they both decided to use shampoo. The fish used a modest amount but the dolphin used an extravagant amount, so much that it spilled out into the sea. The fish was not impressed and began to argue with the dolphin. The dolphin could not understand why the fish was so angry with them, and soon enough the two were ready to fight. That night, all the other sea creatures gathered around to watch the fight. They were surprised to see the fish and the dolphin both fighting so fiercely, it had never happened before. The fish had used its modest amount of shampoo and had won, proving that sometimes less is more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play sport. His favorite sport was to kick a ball. He was very proud of how good he was at kicking the ball. Every day, he would go outside and play with his friends. One sunny day, Tim and his friends were playing their sport. They were having so much fun. Tim leaned back to kick the ball really hard. But, something unexpected happened. Instead of the ball flying far, it went up into a tree. Tim and his friends were so surprised. They all looked at the tree, not knowing what to do. Then, a big bird flew down from the tree. The bird had the ball in its beak. It dropped the ball right in front of Tim. Tim and his friends were so happy. They thanked the bird and continued to play their sport. From that day on, Tim knew that even when things go wrong, there can be a happy ending.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a belt that he loved very much. But one day, his belt became dull. Tim was sad and wanted to replace his belt. Tim went to the store to find a new belt. He saw many belts, but he didn't like any of them. Tim felt sad and thought he would never find a belt as good as his old one. Then, something unexpected happened! A nice lady at the store showed Tim a magic belt. She said it was a special belt that could change colors. Tim was so happy! He put on the magic belt, and it changed from red to blue to green. Tim loved his new belt even more than his old one. And that's how Tim found the best belt ever.
