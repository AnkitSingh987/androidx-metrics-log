Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with cables. One day, Kitty found a big cable in the house. Kitty played with the cable and found a big machine. The machine could print pretty pictures. Kitty wanted to print a picture of her friend, Doggy. So, Kitty pushed a button on the machine. The machine started to print a picture of Doggy. Kitty was very happy. She showed the picture to Doggy and said, "Look, Doggy! I printed a picture of you!" Doggy smiled and said, "Thank you, Kitty! You are so clever!" They played together and had a fun day.

Once there was a little boy who loved to look at the stars in the night sky. He often wished he could touch one of the stars. One night he wished even harder. Then, all of a sudden, a star came down from the sky and and started to talk to the little boy. The star asked the boy if he wanted to go on a special adventure. The boy said yes! So the star picked up the little boy and flew very high into the night sky. It was a beautiful journey, and the sleepy little boy was very happy. But then, the star started to cry. It said it was very sorry and needed forgiveness. The little boy was very sad. He asked the star why it was so sorry. But the star only said it was too late for forgiveness. The little boy was very sad. He knew there was nothing he could do to help the star. He watched as the star flew away.

Anna and Ben were best friends. They liked to play in the park, draw pictures and read books. One day, they found a big letter on the ground. It was red and shiny and had a star on it. "Wow, what an amazing letter!" Anna said. "I wonder what it says." "Let's open it and see," Ben said. He tore the letter and looked inside. There was a piece of paper with some words on it. "What does it say?" Anna asked. Ben tried to read the words, but they were too hard. He only knew some of them. He read: "This is a magic letter. If you say the word 'disappear', you will go to a wonderful place. But be careful, you can only come back if you say the word 'appear'." "Wow, that sounds fun!" Anna said. "Let's try it. Maybe we will go to a place with candy and toys and animals." "OK, but we have to remember the word 'appear'," Ben said. "Or we will be stuck there forever." They held hands and said together: "Disappear!" Suddenly, everything went dark. They felt a strange wind and heard a loud noise. They opened their eyes and saw a new place. It was not wonderful at all. It was scary and ugly and cold. There were no candy and toys and animals. There were only rocks and thorns and monsters. "Help!" Anna cried. "This is a bad place. I want to go home." "Me too," Ben said. "Let's say the word 'appear' and go back." They held hands and said together: "Appear!" But nothing happened. They said it again and again, but nothing changed. They were still in the bad place. They looked at the piece of paper, but it was gone. They had lost it when they tore the letter. They realized they had made a big mistake. They had opened a bad letter that lied to them. They had said a bad word that trapped them. They had forgotten a good word that could save them. They started to cry. They wished they had never found the letter. They wished they had never said 'disappear'. They wished they could see their home and their parents and their friends again. But they couldn't. They were stuck in the bad place forever. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little bunny. The bunny loved to hop and play. One day, the bunny decided to go on an adventure. He wanted to find the big mountain he had heard about. As the bunny hopped and hopped, he saw the mountain. It was very big! The bunny was so happy. He hopped faster and faster to get to the mountain. When he got there, he saw something he did not expect. There was a big frog on the mountain. The frog was sad. The bunny asked the frog, "Why are you sad?" The frog said, "I miss my mommy. She is far away." The bunny wanted to help the frog. He had an idea. He said, "I will give you a kiss. Maybe it will help you feel better." The bunny kissed the frog. Then, something unexpected happened! The frog turned into a beautiful bird. The bird said, "Thank you, bunny! Your kiss broke the spell. Now, I can fly to my mommy." The bird flew away, and the bunny was happy. He hopped back home, knowing he had done a good thing.
