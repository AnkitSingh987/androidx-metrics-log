One day, a little rabbit named Remy was hopping in the woods. He had long ears and a fluffy tail. Remy was looking for food to eat. Remy noticed a big bowl of salad under a tree. The salad had green leaves, red tomatoes, and orange carrots. Remy was very happy to find the salad. Remy ate the salad with his friends, a squirrel and a bird. They all enjoyed the yummy salad. After eating, they played together all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to go for a walk with her dog, Spot. Spot was a harmless dog who liked to play and have fun. One sunny day, Lily and Spot went for a walk in the park. They saw a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of dogs, but Lily told him, "Don't worry, Spot is harmless. He won't hurt you." Tim slowly came closer to Spot, and they started to play together. After a while, Tim wanted to teach Spot a new trick. He said, "Spot, repeat after me: Woof!" Spot barked back, "Woof, woof!" Lily and Tim laughed and clapped their hands. They all had a great time playing together in the park.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big bottle of ink. She wanted to show it to her friend, Tom. So, she started to carry the ink to Tom's house. As she walked, she felt dizzy because the ink was very heavy. When Lucy got to Tom's house, she said, "Look, Tom! I found this big ink!" Tom was excited and said, "Wow! That's so big! Let's make a picture with it!" They both started to draw with the ink, but Lucy still felt dizzy. After a while, Lucy said, "Tom, I feel dizzy. Can you help me carry the ink back to my house?" Tom said, "Of course, Lucy! I will help you." They carried the ink together, and Lucy didn't feel dizzy anymore. They were happy to have each other's help and had a fun day with the big bottle of ink.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was scared of the dark. One night, he saw a scary shadow on his wall. He thought it was a big, scary monster. Tim was very afraid. Tim's mom gave him a jug of water. She said, "If you see the scary shadow again, throw the water on it." Tim took the jug and went back to his room. He saw the scary shadow again. This time, he was brave. He managed to throw the water on the shadow. The water made the shadow go away. Tim was not scared anymore. He knew he could be brave and manage to face his fears. From that night on, Tim was not scared of the dark anymore. He was a brave little boy.

Lily was a little girl who liked to talk a lot. She talked to her mom, her dad, her brother, her friends, and even her toys. She talked about everything she saw, everything she did, and everything she wanted. She talked so much that sometimes she did not listen to others. One day, it was Lily's birthday. She was very happy and excited. She wanted to have a big party with cake, balloons, and presents. She talked to her mom about her party all morning. She did not let her mom talk back. She did not hear her mom say that they had to go to the store to buy some things for the party. When Lily and her mom got in the car, Lily kept talking. She did not see the big truck that was coming fast. She did not hear her mom scream. She did not feel the crash. She only felt a big pain in her head. Lily woke up in a hospital. She saw her mom and dad crying. She saw a doctor and a nurse. She saw wires and machines. She was very scared. She wanted to talk, but she could not. She tried to move, but she could not. She felt very sad and sorry. The doctor told Lily's parents that she had a bad injury in her brain. He said that she might not talk or move again. He said that they had to wait and hope. Lily's parents hugged her and prayed for her. Lily learned a hard lesson. She learned that talking is not always good. She learned that listening is also important. She learned that life is precious and fragile. She learned that she should be grateful and careful. She wished she could tell her mom and dad that she loved them and that she was sorry. She wished she could have her birthday party. She wished she could talk again.
