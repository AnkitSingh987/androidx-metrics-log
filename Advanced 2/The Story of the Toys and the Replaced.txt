Once upon a time there was a little girl who was very envious of the order in her friend's house. She wanted her house to be tidy too, so she decided to replace all her things to make it happen. First, she replaced the toys in the playroom. She sorted the lego and put all the action figures on the shelf. Then she replaced the books so all the stories were nice and tidy. Next she replaced all the cushions and blankets on the sofa. Finally, she replaced her bed sheets so that her room was very neat and organised. The little girl was very proud of herself for creating such order. And the best thing was that her house was just as neat as her friend’s!

Once upon a time, there was a big, intelligent dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to wear a fun hat on his head. He loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Buddy saw a group of people doing yoga in the park. They were moving their bodies in different ways. Buddy thought it looked like fun, so he decided to try it too. Buddy tried to copy the people doing yoga. He stretched his legs and wiggled his tail. All of his friends came to watch and they laughed. Buddy had so much fun doing yoga and wearing his hat. Everyone thought he was a very smart dog.

Once upon a time, in a small open house, there lived a muffin. This muffin was very sad because he had no friends. He wanted to join others and play with them. One sunny day, a little girl came to the open house. She saw the muffin and wanted to eat it. But then, the muffin started to talk! "Please don't eat me! I want to join you and be your friend," said the muffin. The little girl was very surprised. The little girl and the muffin became best friends. They played and had fun every day. The muffin was not sad anymore. And the little girl learned that friends can be found in the most unexpected places.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a bottle of lotion. He did not know what it was, so he asked his mom. "Mom, what is this?" Tim showed her the bottle. His mom looked at it and said, "That is lotion, Tim. It is important to use it on your skin to keep it soft." Tim wanted to use the lotion, so he asked his mom to help him. His mom opened the bottle and put some lotion in Tim's hand. She showed Tim how to rub his hands together to spread the lotion. Tim liked how the lotion made his hands feel soft and smooth. Later that day, Tim's friend, Lily, came over to play. Tim wanted to share his new lotion with her. He said, "Lily, I own this lotion. It is important to use it on your skin. Do you want to try?" Lily nodded and Tim helped her put some lotion on her hands. They both laughed and played, enjoying their soft, smooth hands.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box. Inside the box was a microscope. A girl named Lucy wanted to see small things with the microscope. She asked her mom to order it for her. Her mom said yes and ordered the microscope. When the microscope came, Lucy was very happy. She looked at a lot of small things. She saw a tiny bug and a small flower. The microscope made everything look big. One day, Lucy's friend Sam came to play. They looked at more small things with the heavy microscope. Sam and Lucy had a lot of fun. They both loved the microscope. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his basketball. One day, it was very dark outside. Tim could not see well, but he wanted to play with his basketball. As Tim played, he heard a loud sound. His basketball hit a big tree and broke a small branch. Tim was sad and scared. He knew he needed to fix the branch. He went to his dad and said, "Dad, I broke a branch. Can you help me fix it?" His dad smiled and said, "Yes, Tim. We can fix it together." They went outside and found a strong stick. They tied the stick to the broken branch with a rope. The branch was fixed. Tim learned that it is important to tell the truth and fix his mistakes. He also learned to be more careful when playing in the dark.
