One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a long vine hanging from a tree. Tim wanted to swing on the vine, but it was filthy. He asked his friend, Sam, to help him clean it. "No, I refuse to help you clean the vine," said Sam. "It's too dirty!" Tim was sad, but he decided to clean the vine by himself. He got some water and soap and started to scrub the vine. As he cleaned it, something unexpected happened. The vine began to wiggle and move! Tim was scared at first, but then he saw that the vine was not a vine at all. It was a friendly snake! The snake thanked Tim for cleaning him and they became good friends. From that day on, Tim and the snake played together every day.

Tom and Lily were friends who liked to play together. They lived in a big house with a big garden, because their parents were wealthy. They had many toys and books and clothes, but they also liked to explore the garden and look for bugs and flowers. One day, they found a strawberry patch in a corner of the garden. They were very happy, because they loved strawberries. They picked some and ate them. They were sweet and juicy and red. "Yum, these are the best strawberries ever!" Tom said. "I agree, they are so tasty!" Lily said. "Let's play a game. We can pretend we are farmers and these are our crops. We have to take care of them and sell them at the market," Tom suggested. "OK, that sounds fun. I will be the farmer's wife and you will be the farmer. We have to water the strawberries and keep the birds away," Lily agreed. They played for a long time, pretending to water the strawberries with a watering can, and making scarecrows out of sticks and leaves. They also pretended to load the strawberries in a basket and take them to the market, where they sold them for a lot of money. They had so much fun, they forgot about the time. They only remembered when they heard their parents calling them for lunch. "Come on, Tom and Lily, it's time to eat. What have you been doing?" their parents asked. "We have been playing farmers. We found a strawberry patch and we ate some and sold some. They were delicious!" Tom and Lily said. Their parents smiled and praised them for being creative and adventurous. They also gave them some more strawberries for dessert, which made Tom and Lily very happy. They decided that playing farmers was their new favorite game.

One day, a mighty dog named Spot went for a walk. The sun was shining, and the park was quiet. Spot liked the quiet because he could hear the birds sing. As he walked, he saw his friend, a little cat named Fluffy. "Hi, Fluffy!" Spot said. "Do you want to play with me?" "Yes, let's play!" Fluffy replied. They started to run and play together, having lots of fun. Spot could run very fast. He would speed around the park, and Fluffy would try to catch him. Suddenly, while they were playing, a big wind came. It blew and blew, and it picked up Spot and Fluffy into the air! They were scared, but they held onto each other. The wind took them high up into the sky. They could see the whole park from up there. Then, just as quickly as it started, the wind stopped. Spot and Fluffy floated gently back down to the ground. They were happy to be safe and back on the ground. They looked at each other and laughed. "That was fun!" Fluffy said. "Let's play some more!" Spot agreed, and they continued to play together in the quiet park, having a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to travel with her mom and dad. One day, Lucy saw a brilliant red ball. She wanted to play with it. Lucy asked her mom if she could play with the red ball. Her mom said yes, but told her to be careful. Lucy played with the ball all day. She threw it up and down, and all around. But, Lucy did not see a big tree in front of her. Lucy threw the ball, and it hit the tree. The ball fell down and hit Lucy's thumb. It hurt a lot. Lucy's mom came and helped her. She told Lucy to be more careful next time. Lucy learned to watch where she was going when she played.
