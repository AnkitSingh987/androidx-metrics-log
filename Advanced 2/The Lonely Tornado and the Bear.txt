Once upon a time, there was a lonely tornado. He was very sad because he had no friends. The tornado wanted to play with the trees and the animals, but they were all scared of him. One day, the tornado met a big bear. The bear was not scared of the tornado. The bear said, "Hello, tornado. Do you want to play with me?" The tornado was very happy and said, "Yes, I do!" The bear and the tornado played together all day. They had so much fun. When it was time to eat, the bear shared his food with the tornado. They became best friends and the tornado was not lonely anymore.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went to the park. They wanted to spend time playing. Tim saw a big strip of grass where they could play. He was very excited. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue said, "Be careful, Tim. I saw a big hole in the grass strip." Tim thanked Sue and they all played together. Later, Tim saw the hole that Sue talked about. He was careful not to fall in. They spent the whole day playing and had lots of fun. Tim was happy that he listened to Sue and was careful.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tim. Tim loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, Tim found a big folder under his bed. He didn't know what was inside, but he was very curious. Tim called his friend, a little mouse named Sam. "Sam, come look at this folder I found!" Sam ran over to see the folder. They opened it together and saw many colorful papers inside. Tim and Sam decided to use the papers to make fun pictures. As they were making their pictures, Tim found a big bowl and a spoon. He began to stir the colorful papers in the bowl. "What are you doing, Tim?" asked Sam. Tim said, "I'm making a magic potion with the papers!" They laughed and played until they were very sleepy. Tim and Sam went to bed, happy with their fun day.

One day, there was a sleepy boy. His name was Jake. Jake was walking near a garage and he wanted to take a look inside. He was curious about what he could find. He took a deep breath and opened the door. As he stepped into the garage, he noticed a small box on the floor. He went over to take a closer look. Suddenly, Jake heard a loud noise. He was so scared that he tried to part from the garage, but he couldn't. He was stuck. Jake started to cry and screamed for help, but it was no use. He was stuck in the garage until the morning. After a long night, Jake was very tired and sleepy. He was so sleepy that he fell asleep in the garage. When the morning came, he found out that the door had been closed and he couldn't get out. He was trapped in the garage for the whole day. In the evening the door opened and Jake finally could part from the garage. It was a bad ending for Jake.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to her friend's house to play. They wanted to make a pretty picture to hang on the wall. They found a nice frame to put the picture in when they were done. Amy and her friend worked hard to make the picture look nice. They used many colors and drew flowers and trees. When they were done, they put the picture in the frame. They were very proud of their work. They wanted to show their moms, so they tried to stand the frame on the table. But, the frame fell and the glass broke. Amy and her friend felt embarrassed. Their moms came and saw the broken frame. They hugged the girls and said, "It's okay, we can fix it." The girls smiled and knew they had made a good picture.

Joey and his mom were at Jerry's Pet Store. Joey had his eye on a big, furry dog. Mom said, "Let's ask Mr. Judge what he would recommend." Mr. Judge was the store owner. He smiled when he saw Joey. He said, "I have just the dog for you, Joey." He went to the back of the store and came out with a big, furry golden retriever puppy. Joey was so excited. He hugged the puppy and his mom said, "We'll take him!" Joey took his new puppy home. Every day he’d take him for walks, feed him, and play with him. Joey was so thankful for his furry new friend. He’d always remember that Mr. Judge recommended this puppy for him and how happy it made him.
