One day, a curious youth named Tim went for a walk. He saw a big tree and a small bird. The bird was on the ground, looking sad. Tim wanted to help the bird. Tim asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird pointed at the tree and said, "I lost my nest. It is up there, but I can't fly high enough to get it back." Tim looked at the tree and thought of a plan. He climbed the tree, grabbed the nest, and gave it back to the bird. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." The moral of the story is that helping others can make you and them happy. And sometimes, being curious can lead to good things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to collect rocks. One day, he found a big rock with a switch on it. He was curious and a bit anxious, but he decided to take it home. At home, Tim showed his mom the rock with the switch. "Look, Mom! What does it do?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Let's find out!" She flipped the switch, and the rock began to glow. They were both amazed. Tim and his mom decided to share the glowing rock with their friends and neighbors. Everyone loved the rock and it made them all happy. Tim learned that sharing his special find made him feel even better than keeping it to himself. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can bring happiness to everyone.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a box of makeup. She was very happy. She wanted to play with the makeup and look pretty. But Lucy's mom said, "No, Lucy. That makeup is spoiled. It is not good to use." Lucy was sad. She wanted to keep the makeup. She thought of a plan. She decided to bury the makeup in her garden. She dug a hole and put the makeup in it. Then, she covered the hole with dirt. A few days later, Lucy went to the garden. She wanted to see if the makeup was still there. To her surprise, she saw a pretty flower growing from the spot where she buried the makeup. Lucy ran to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! The spoiled makeup turned into a pretty flower!" Her mom smiled and said, "Wow, Lucy! That is very unexpected! Sometimes, even spoiled things can bring beauty into the world."

Lila and Tom were best friends. They liked to play in the park every day. They liked to swing, slide and run. But their favorite thing was to hang from the monkey bars. They felt strong and happy when they did that. One day, a new boy came to the park. His name was Ben. He was bigger than Lila and Tom. He wanted to play with them, but he was not very nice. He pushed Lila and Tom off the monkey bars and said, "This is my spot. You are too small and weak to hang here. Go away!" Lila and Tom felt sad and angry. They did not want to play with Ben. They wanted to hang from the monkey bars. They tried to tell him to stop, but he did not listen. He laughed at them and said, "You are silly and stupid. You don't know anything. I know everything. I have wisdom." Lila and Tom did not know what wisdom meant. They thought it was something good. They wanted to have wisdom too. They asked Ben, "What is wisdom? How do you get it?" Ben smirked and said, "Wisdom is when you know everything and you are always right. You get it by being big and strong and smart. You don't have wisdom. You are small and weak and dumb. You will never have wisdom." Lila and Tom felt bad. They thought they were not good enough. They wished they were big and strong and smart like Ben. They wondered if they could ever have wisdom. Then, they saw a lady walking by. She had long, white hair and a kind smile. She looked very old, but also very beautiful. She saw Lila and Tom and said, "Hello, children. What are you doing?" Lila and Tom told her about Ben and the monkey bars and wisdom. They asked her, "Do you know what wisdom is? Do you have wisdom?" The lady nodded and said, "Yes, I know what wisdom is. And yes, I have wisdom. But wisdom is not what Ben told you. Wisdom is not when you know everything and you are always right. Wisdom is when you learn from everything and you are always kind. Wisdom is not when you are big and strong and smart. Wisdom is when you are humble and gentle and brave. Wisdom is not something you get by pushing others away. Wisdom is something you get by helping others along. Wisdom is not something you have. Wisdom is something you share." Lila and Tom listened to the lady and felt a warm feeling in their hearts. They understood what she meant. They realized that Ben was not wise at all. He was mean and rude and selfish. They decided that they did not want to be like Ben. They wanted to be like the lady. They wanted to have wisdom. They thanked the lady and gave her a hug. They said, "You are very wise and very beautiful. We want to be wise and beautiful too. Can you teach us how to be wise and beautiful?" The lady smiled and said, "Of course, I can. But you already have wisdom and beauty inside you. You just need to let them shine. Come, let's go to the monkey bars. I will show you how to hang and how to share." Lila and Tom followed the lady to the monkey bars. They saw Ben still hanging there, alone and bored. They asked him, "Do you want to play with us? We have a new friend. She is very wise and very beautiful. She can teach us all how to be wise and beautiful too." Ben looked at them and at the lady. He felt curious and confused. He wondered what they were talking about. He said, "OK, I will play with you. But who is she? And what is wisdom? And what is beautiful?" The lady said, "I am a grandma. And wisdom is when you learn from everything and you are always kind. And beautiful is when you are humble and gentle and brave. And you can be wise and beautiful too, if you want to. Come, let me show you." And so, Lila and Tom and Ben and the grandma played together on the monkey bars. They hung and swung and laughed and talked. They learned and shared and helped and cared. They became friends and had fun. And they all felt wise and beautiful.
