One day, a little dog named Max was very sad. He had a toy that he loved, but it was spoiled. Max could not play with his toy anymore. He wanted a new toy, but he had to wait for Tuesday to come. Max did not like to wait. On Tuesday, Max's mom said, "Today is Tuesday! We will go to the store and get a new toy." Max was so happy. He could not wait to go to the store. He wagged his tail and jumped up and down. At the store, Max saw many toys. He picked a big red ball. He was very happy with his new toy. Max and his mom went home, and Max played with his red ball all day. He forgot about his spoiled toy. Max was happy that Tuesday came.

Once upon a time, there was a very important lady. She lived in a tall house and walked in big gardens. One day, when she was walking in the garden, she saw a butterfly. She quickly chased it. The butterfly flew away, but suddenly it disappeared. It had gone into a big, dark hole. The important lady wanted to get closer to see where the butterfly had gone. Suddenly, she heard a loud voice. It was coming from the hole. “Don’t come in!” it said. “It’s dangerous!” The lady paused. Then she thought to herself, “It can’t be that dangerous. I’ll just take a look. What’s the worst that can happen?” So she decided to ignore the warning and continued to walk towards the hole. But it was a mistake. When she got closer, the ground started to shake and the hole opened wider. The important lady fell right in! She got stuck and couldn’t get out. And that’s how she was never seen again. The moral of the story is: always listen to warnings!

Once upon a time, there was a big room called a chamber. In the chamber, there were many toys for kids to play with. One day, a little boy named Tim went into the chamber to play. Tim saw a toy car and wanted to play with it. But a foolish girl named Sally did not want to yield the toy car to Tim. She wanted to play with it too. They both got upset because they both wanted the toy car. Then, Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's share the toy car!" Sally agreed and they both played with the toy car together. They learned that sharing was more fun than being foolish and not yielding. And they played happily in the chamber ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a messy farm. On this farm, there was a little girl named Sara. Sara wanted a horse more than anything in the world. Every day, she would ask her mom and dad for a horse. They would say, "Maybe one day, Sara." One day, Sara saw a big box near the barn. She knew something was in the box. She thought, "Could this be the day I get my horse?" She felt very excited and happy. Sara opened the big box and there was a beautiful horse inside! She was so happy to receive her horse. She named him Buddy. Sara and Buddy played on the messy farm and had lots of fun together. And from that day on, Sara and Buddy were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a zebra named Zara. Zara was a very nice zebra. She had many friends in the zoo. One day, Zara saw an orange ball near her home. She liked the orange ball very much. Zara wanted to play with the orange ball. She asked her friend, the lion, if he would play with her. The lion said, "Yes, I will play with you, Zara. But I do not have a ball." Zara was very happy. She said, "I can lend you my orange ball. We can play together." So, Zara and the lion played with the orange ball all day. They had a lot of fun. They laughed and ran around. When the sun went down, they were very tired. Zara said, "Thank you for playing with me, lion. I am happy to lend you my ball." The lion smiled and said, "Thank you, Zara. You are a very good friend."
