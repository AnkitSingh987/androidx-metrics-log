Once there was a girl who loved to drink juice. Every day she would go to the kitchen to get a glass of juice. Then one day she found a letter on the floor. It was very exciting and she couldn't wait to open it. When she opened it, a disgusting smell came out. She was very disappointed and started to cry. Then she saw something even worse; a big brown bug was crawling out of the letter. The girl was terrified and screamed. She quickly threw the letter away, ran to the kitchen and grabbed her juice cup. She drank it all in one gulp to make the bad feeling go away. Sadly, the juice was old and didn't taste right. The girl got sick and she had to stay in bed for a few days. She was very sad that she had to miss out on her favourite activity - drinking juice.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Amy's room had a big window. She loved looking out the window at the pretty flowers in her yard. One day, Amy's mom gave her a tiny seed. She told Amy that it could grow into a beautiful flower. Amy was very excited. She put the seed in a pot and put it on the window. Every day, she gave it water and talked to it. After a few days, the seed started to grow. It grew into a big, beautiful flower. The flower was very special because it looked like an expensive jewel. Amy was so happy. She knew that her love and care helped the flower grow big and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a plan. He wanted to find a new way to play with his toys. He told his friend, Sam, about his plan. Sam said, "I am envious of your plan, Tim! I want to find a new way to play too!" So, they both went to the toy box to start their plan. But, when they opened the toy box, they found a secret door! They did not plan for this. Tim and Sam looked at each other, and then they went through the door. They found a whole new world of toys to play with! They were so happy and played all day long.

One day, Bill was excited, because he was going to play a game with his friends. He was competing to see who could win. Bill had a special page he was holding in his hand. Then, Bill's friends arrived. They all began to play. It was a lot of fun! But suddenly, something happened. Bill's special page got broken. His friends said, "Oh no, Bill's page is broken! How can he win now?" Bill was sad that he couldn't win. Then, Bill thought of a brilliant idea. He said to his friends, “I know how I can win. We can make a new page together and I can be the winner." His friends agreed, and they all worked together to create a new page. Bill won the game and they all cheered! At the end of the day, Bill was so happy. He had won, and he was also glad that everyone was able to work together to come up with a solution.

One day, a dog named Spot saw a helpless cat stuck in a tree. Spot wanted to rescue the cat. He looked at the tree and saw a pattern. The pattern was made of the branches. Spot thought he could climb the tree using the pattern. Spot started to climb the tree. He was almost at the top when something unexpected happened. The cat jumped down from the tree all by itself! Spot was so surprised that he lost his balance and got stuck in the tree. Now, it was the cat's turn to help. The cat found a long stick and pushed it up to Spot. Spot grabbed the stick and the cat pulled him down. They both were safe on the ground. Spot and the cat became good friends, and they learned that they could help each other when they needed it.
