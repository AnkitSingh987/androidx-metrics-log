Once upon a time, in a big garden, there was a pretty flower. The flower was very patient. It waited for the sun to come out and play. One day, a boy came to the garden. He liked to throw things. He saw the patient flower and wanted to play with it. The boy gently picked the flower and threw it in the air. The patient flower danced in the wind. It was very happy. The boy and the flower played all day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved playing with his rope. One day, he had a fun idea. He wanted to raise his toy high up in the sky. Tim tied one end of the rope to his toy and the other end to a big tree. He started to pull the rope up. The toy went higher and higher. Tim felt very happy. But Tim was foolish. He forgot to hold the rope tight. The toy flew away with the wind. Tim was sad, but he learned to be more careful next time.

Once upon a time, there was a human boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He liked to play with his toys and look at the stars in the sky. One day, while Tim was playing, he found a special rock. This rock could glow. Tim took the glowing rock home and showed it to his mom and dad. They were very surprised. They had never seen a rock that could glow before. They decided to keep it safe in their house. One night, while everyone was sleeping, the glowing rock started to change. The rock became a friendly glowing creature! The creature was very intelligent, just like Tim. The creature became Tim's best friend and they played together every day. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl was walking in the park with her Mommy. She was so excited! All the flowers were so colorful and beautiful, and the birds were singing so loud. The little girl went to a big tree and started reaching her fingers to try to touch a bird. Her Mommy smiled at her and said, "Let's walk a bit more, sweetie". So, the girl and her Mommy walked and then stopped when they saw a big pond. The little girl saw some ducks swimming and said, "Mommy, look! Look at the ducks!". Mommy smiled and said, "Let's try to find a finger that's the same size as the duck's." So they started looking around and, suddenly, the little girl found a big, yellow finger in the water. Mommy said, "That one looks perfect! Let's go show it to the ducks!" So they started walking towards the ducks, and the girl held up the yellow finger. When they got close, the little girl made a loud noise and the ducks quickly swam away!

Once upon a time, there was a fancy lollipop. It was very big and very sweet. The lollipop lived in a candy store with lots of other treats. Every day, the lollipop would wait for a friend to come and play. One day, a little girl named Lucy came into the candy store. She saw the fancy lollipop and thought it was so pretty. Lucy wanted to win the lollipop, so she asked her mom if she could try. Her mom said yes, and Lucy was very happy. Lucy played a game to win the fancy lollipop. She tried very hard and did her best. Finally, Lucy won the game and got the fancy lollipop! She was so excited to have a new friend. Lucy and the lollipop went home together, and they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to whistle. One day, he walked to his friend's house. He stood in front of the door and whistled. His friend's mom opened the door. "Hi, Tim! It's polite to knock on the door," she said. Tim said sorry and went inside to play with his friend. Later, they went outside to play. Tim saw a big dog. He thought it would be fun to whistle at the dog. So, he whistled loudly. The dog didn't like it and started to chase Tim and his friend. "Run!" Tim's friend shouted. They ran and ran, but the dog caught up. It bit Tim's leg. Tim cried and his friend's mom came out to help. The moral of the story is to be polite and not whistle at dogs. It's better to be kind and careful with animals.
