Once upon a time, there was a little cat named Kitty. Kitty was very thirsty, so she went to the store to order some milk. She saw a big cow behind the counter and asked, "Can I have some milk, please?" The cow said, "Of course!" and gave Kitty a big glass of milk. As Kitty started to drink her milk, a silly dog named Max came into the store. Max saw Kitty drinking the milk and thought it was stupid. He laughed and said, "Why are you drinking milk? That's not for cats!" Kitty felt sad and stopped drinking her milk. But then, something unexpected happened. The cow behind the counter spoke up and said, "Max, you are wrong! Milk is good for cats too!" Max was surprised and felt a little silly. He apologized to Kitty and they both enjoyed a big glass of milk together. From that day on, Max learned not to call others stupid and Kitty and Max became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty dog. He liked to perform tricks and act silly everyday. But one day, he got very sick and he needed to go to the veterinarian. He had to have a special doctor that would take care of him. The naughty dog was scared of the veterinarian and he tried to run away. But his owners grabbed him and brought him to the vet's office. When he arrived, the vet had to perform many different tests on him. Afterward, the vet said that the naughty dog needed medicine. But the medicine was very expensive. His owners couldn't afford it. So, in the end, the naughty dog had to be put to sleep. He died without ever receiving the medicine that he needed.

Anna and Ben like to go to the market with Mom. They see many things at the market. They see fruits, vegetables, bread, cheese, eggs, and flowers. They also see people who sell and buy things. Mom gives them some money to buy something they want. Anna and Ben walk around the market. They see a big red balloon. They want the balloon. They go to the man who sells the balloon. He says the balloon costs two coins. Anna and Ben have two coins. They give the coins to the man. He gives them the balloon. Anna and Ben are happy. They hold the balloon together. They walk back to Mom. But then a strong wind comes. The wind blows the balloon away. Anna and Ben are sad. They cry. They want their balloon back. The balloon flies high in the sky. It flies over the market. It flies over the houses. It flies over the park. It flies over the pond. It flies over the bridge. It flies over the hill. It flies over the forest. In the forest, there is a bird. The bird is not ordinary. The bird is big and colorful. The bird has blue feathers, yellow beak, and green eyes. The bird sees the balloon. The bird likes the balloon. The bird flies after the balloon. The bird catches the balloon with its beak. The bird does not pop the balloon. The bird is gentle. The bird flies back to the market. The bird sees Anna and Ben. They are still crying. The bird feels sorry for them. The bird flies down to them. The bird gives them the balloon. Anna and Ben are surprised. They see the bird. They see their balloon. They stop crying. They smile. They thank the bird. The bird smiles too. The bird flies away. Anna and Ben are happy again. They hold the balloon together. They run to Mom. They show her the balloon. They tell her about the bird. Mom is amazed. She hugs them. She says they are lucky. She says the bird saved their balloon. She says the bird is not ordinary. She says the bird is special. Anna and Ben agree. They say the bird is their friend. They say they love the bird. They say they love their balloon. They say they love Mom. They say they love the market.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to go to the gym. At the gym, he would play and have fun. Tim wanted to be strong like his big brother, Sam. One day, Tim saw a girl at the gym. She was trying to lift a heavy ball. Tim wanted to help her. He walked over and said, "Let's lift it together!" The girl smiled and said, "Thank you!" Together, they lifted the heavy ball. Tim felt strong and happy. The girl said, "You are a good helper, Tim!" Tim smiled and said, "We are strong together!" They played at the gym all day and became good friends.
