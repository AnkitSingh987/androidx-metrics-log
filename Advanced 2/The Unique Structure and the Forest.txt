Once upon a time, there was a unique structure in the middle of the forest. It was a big and special house where many animals lived together. The animals were very happy in their home. One day, a big truck came to the forest. The truck was there to load many things from the forest. A little squirrel saw the truck and was scared. The squirrel went to the big structure and told the other animals, "A big truck is here to take things away!" The animals in the unique structure wanted to help. They all went to the truck and talked to the driver. "Please don't take our things," they said. The driver listened to the animals and decided not to load anything from their forest. The animals were very happy and thanked the driver. They all went back to their big, special house and continued to live happily together.

Tom and Lily are friends who like to joke with each other. They play games and try to make each other laugh. Sometimes they are competitive and want to win. One day, they go to the park with their moms. They see a big cooler with drinks and snacks. They are thirsty and hungry, so they run to the cooler. "Can I have a juice, please?" Tom asks his mom. "Me too, me too!" Lily says. Their moms give them each a juice box and a cookie. They sit on the grass and enjoy their treats. "Hey, Lily, do you want to hear a joke?" Tom says. "Sure, Tom, tell me a joke," Lily says. "Okay, here it goes. Why did the chicken cross the road?" Tom says. "I don't know, why?" Lily says. "To get to the cooler!" Tom says and laughs. Lily thinks for a moment, then shakes her head. "That's not a joke, Tom, that's silly. The chicken would not want a juice or a cookie. The chicken would want seeds or worms," Lily says. Tom frowns. He thinks his joke is funny. "No, Lily, you don't get it. It's a joke because it's unexpected. The chicken does something different than usual. That's what makes it funny," Tom says. Lily shrugs. She does not think it's funny. "Well, I have a better joke, Tom. Do you want to hear it?" Lily says. "Okay, fine, tell me your joke," Tom says. "Okay, here it goes. What do you get when you cross a cooler and a competitive kid?" Lily says. "I don't know, what?" Tom says. "A cool kid!" Lily says and laughs. Tom rolls his eyes. He thinks his joke is better. "That's not a joke, Lily, that's lame. The cooler and the kid have nothing to do with each other. That's what makes it boring," Tom says. Lily smiles. She thinks her joke is clever. "No, Tom, you don't get it. It's a joke because it's a pun. The cooler and the kid sound the same. That's what makes it funny," Lily says. Tom and Lily look at each other. They realize they have different kinds of jokes. They decide to agree to disagree. They finish their juice and cookies and go back to playing. They still like to joke with each other, but they also respect each other's humor. They are friends who have fun.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Pete. Pete liked to be silly and have fun, so one day he ran away from home. He ran around and around until he found a big barrel. The barrel was big, round, and harmless. Pete climbed into the barrel and it started to roll away. Pete was so excited, he didn't know what would happen next. The barrel lead Pete down a road and across a bridge. After a while Pete realised that he was going much too fast, he might get hurt! So he held onto the sides and closed his eyes tight. The barrel finally stopped at the bottom of a big hill. Pete opened his eyes and was relieved to find himself in a safe place. He scrambled out of the barrel and ran home, happy and safe. From then on, Pete liked to go exploring, but always made sure that he was never too far away from home. He never forgot the lesson he learned from the barrel: stay safe and have fun!
