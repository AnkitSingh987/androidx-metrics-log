Tom and Anna want to go to the park. They ask Mom if they can go. Mom nods and says yes, but they have to wait. She has to finish some work first. Tom and Anna are restless. They do not like to wait. They look for something to do. They play with their toys, but they get bored. They draw some pictures, but they run out of paper. They sing some songs, but they forget the words. They look at the clock. It is still not time to go. They go to Mom and ask her again. Mom says she is almost done. She tells them to put on their shoes and coats. Tom and Anna are happy. They run to their room and get ready. They come back to Mom and show her. Mom smiles and says they are good. She closes her laptop and picks up her bag. She tells them to follow her. They go to the car and get in. Mom drives to the park. Tom and Anna see the swings, the slide, the sandbox and the trees. They are excited. They thank Mom and jump out. They run to the playground and have fun. They do not have to wait anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Mark. Mark had a special job; he would examine things. One day, he had to examine an inch! Mark knew that it was a unique inch, as it was special and different from all the other inches he had seen before. He examined it really carefully, noticing the different colours and shapes of it. Mark was really surprised when his inch began to move! It started moving around on its own, and Mark couldn't believe it. He kept examining the inch to find out why it was so unique. Soon enough, Mark realized that his inch was really special because it was alive! It was a magical inch, and it even said hello to him! Mark thought it was the best job ever to examine such a unique inch. He and his inch became friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to dance to music. But one day, the music was boring. Sue did not want to dance. She was sad. Sue had a friend named Tom. Tom wanted to make Sue happy. He had a plan. He went to find a big bug. He wanted to pinch the bug to make it sing. Tom thought the bug's song would be fun for Sue. Tom pinched the bug, but something unexpected happened. The bug got mad and bit Tom. Tom cried and ran away. The bug did not sing. Sue was still sad, and now Tom was hurt too. The plan did not work, and they both had a bad day.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to play. She saw a big box in her room. Lily thought it looked like a car. So, she decided to drive the box like a car. Lily said to her cat, "Come on, Kitty! Let's drive in my car!" Kitty jumped in the box with Lily. They drove around the room, having fun. Everything was normal and happy. Suddenly, the box started to move on its own! Lily and Kitty were surprised. They drove faster and faster. Then, the box stopped. They looked around and saw they were in a new, fun playroom. "Wow!" said Lily. "That was unexpected!" They played all day and had lots of fun.

Once there was a little fish who lived in a tank. The tank was very organized. The fish liked to study the rocks and plants in his tank. One day, the little fish saw a new toy in the tank. It was a small castle. The fish was very happy. He started to study the castle. He swam in and out of the windows. The little fish had a lot of fun studying the castle. He felt proud of his organized tank. Every day, he would study and play in his tank. And he lived happily ever after.

Lily loved to play in her garden. One day she saw a blue butterfly. She watched it flutter and dance around the garden. As Lily followed the butterfly, she noticed the flowers blooming in the garden. "Oh, how pretty!", she said. Then, she saw an old rake. She picked it up and began to rake the area around the flowers. The sun was shining and it was a warm summer day. "Hi there!" said a friendly voice. Lily looked up and saw an old man standing at the end of the garden. "I see you are raking the area," he smiled. "Yes, I am," said Lily. "It's such a nice day and I wanted to help the flowers bloom." The old man chuckled. "You are a very nice young lady. I think the garden looks very nice now. Would you like a scoop of ice cream? It's blueberry, my favorite," he said. Lily smiled. "Yes, please!" she said. They had a nice afternoon eating ice cream and talking about the beauty of blooming flowers. Lily learned that sometimes things must be taken care of, just like the rake had helped the space around the flowers bloom.
