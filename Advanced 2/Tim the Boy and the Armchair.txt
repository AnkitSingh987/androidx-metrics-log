One day, a happy boy named Tim went to his grandma's house. He loved to play with his toys there. Grandma had a big, soft armchair that Tim liked to sit in. He would sit in the armchair and play with his cars and trucks. Tim was enthusiastic about playing in the armchair. He would drive his cars up and down the armchair's arms. He would make them jump off the armchair and land on the floor. Sometimes, he would close his eyes and pretend he was driving the cars himself. When it was time to go home, Tim didn't want to leave the armchair. He hugged the armchair and said, "Bye armchair! See you soon!" Tim's mom said, "We'll come back next week to play again." Tim smiled and waved goodbye to the armchair as they left grandma's house.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to create a symbol. This symbol would show that he was an independent boy. He thought hard about what to make. He wanted it to be special. Tim drew a big sun with a smiley face. He showed it to his mom, dad, and little sister. They all liked it, but his sister said, "That's not a twist!" Tim thought she was right. He wanted something with a twist. So, Tim drew a snake with a twisty tail. He showed it to his family again. This time, they were all happy. The snake was a surprise! It was a symbol of Tim being independent and having a twist. Now, everyone knew Tim was a big, independent boy.

Once upon a time, there was a large dog named Max. Max liked to play outside and make lots of noise. He loved to run and jump in the sun. One day, Max saw a pretty dress hanging near a house. The dress was red and shiny, and Max wanted to play with it. Max jumped up and grabbed the dress with his mouth. He pulled it down and ran around with it. The dress was long and fun to play with. Max made a lot of noise as he played with the dress. He was very happy. But soon, a girl came out of the house. She was very sad because her pretty dress was now dirty and broken. Max felt bad for making her sad. He tried to give the dress back, but it was too late. The girl was still sad, and Max could not play with the dress anymore. The day ended with both the girl and Max feeling sad.

Billy was a wild little boy, and he loved to stare at the prices all around him. Everywhere he went, he stared at the prices of things and dreamed of buying them all. One day, Billy saw a toy he wanted, so he asked his mom if he could buy it. His mom said it was too expensive. That made Billy very angry, so he decided to stare at the price of the toy until his mom changed her mind. The whole day, his mom refused to buy him the toy, but when night fell she suddenly agreed. Billy was so excited, and he couldn't wait to buy it. Unfortunately, when Billy went to the store and asked for the toy, the shopkeeper said he was all out of them. Billy was so disappointed and angry that he started to cry. Billy's experience taught him that sometimes wild things have a bad ending.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Kay. Her mum wanted to take her to the park. But Kay was a bit bored and said "No, I don't want to go to the park". So her mum said: "Please come with me, it will be fun!" But Kay just shook her head and said "No, I don't want to. It's too boring!". So her mum said: "Come on, just wait, it won't be so boring, I promise!" Eventually, Kay agreed and got into the car. When they arrived at the park, there were so many exciting things for Kay to explore! She ran around, looking at everything and having such a great time. But then, Kay and her mum heard music coming from afar. "Let's go and look at it," her mum said. So they followed the music and found a street musician playing a guitar. Kay was very excited and started to dance. The musician smiled and continued to play. So Kay and her mum listened and danced to the music. Kay loved it so much she asked the musician to "repeat" it again. And the musician did! Kay and her mum listened and danced until they were too tired and then they went back home. Kay was so happy she didn't find going to the park boring anymore.
