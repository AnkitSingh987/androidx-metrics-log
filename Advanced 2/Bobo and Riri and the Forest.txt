Once upon a time, in a quiet and beautiful forest, there lived a big bear named Bobo and a little rabbit named Riri. They were the best of friends and played together every day. One sunny day, while they were playing hide and seek, Bobo accidentally stepped on Riri's foot. Riri cried out in pain and said, "Ouch! You hurt me, Bobo!" Bobo felt very sad and sorry. He looked at Riri with big, sad eyes and said, "I'm so sorry, Riri. I didn't mean to hurt you. Can you forgive me?" Riri looked at her friend and saw how sorry he was. She wiped away her tears and said, "Yes, Bobo, I forgive you. We are friends, and friends forgive each other." They hugged and continued to play in the beautiful, quiet forest, happy to be friends once again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to watch television all day. One day, Tim saw a big dog on the television. The dog looked scared. Tim felt sorry for the dog, so he pointed at the television and said to his mom, "Look, the dog is scared!" His mom looked at the television and said, "Yes, Tim. The dog is scared because it is lost. We should always take care of our pets and not let them get lost." Tim thought about what his mom said. The next day, when he went outside to play with his dog, he made sure to hold on to the leash. He did not want his dog to get scared and lost like the dog on the television. As Tim played with his dog, he saw his friend Lucy. He told her about the scared dog on the television. Lucy also promised to take care of her pets. From that day on, Tim, Lucy, and all their friends learned to take care of their pets, so they would not get scared and lost. They learned that it is important to be kind and responsible pet owners.

One day, a boy named Tim found a potato in his yard. He liked the potato and wanted to keep it. Tim thought of a fun game to play with his new friend, the potato. He decided to attach a string to the potato and pull it around. As Tim pulled the potato, it made a noisy sound on the ground. His little sister, Sue, heard the sound and came to see what was happening. "What's that noise?" she asked. Tim told her about his friend, the potato, and the game he was playing. Sue wanted to play too, so Tim attached another string to the potato. They both pulled the potato around, making it even more noisy. They laughed and played until it was time for dinner. And from that day on, Tim and Sue always had fun playing with their noisy potato friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big, round moon in the sky. It would shine bright at night and make everyone feel happy. The moon seemed to watch over everyone while they slept. One day, a careless little boy named Tim was playing outside. He looked up at the sky and saw the moon. He wanted to touch the moon, but he knew it was very far away. So, he decided to jump as high as he could to reach it. Tim jumped and jumped, but he could not touch the moon. He felt sad, but then he saw the moon seemed to smile at him. Tim knew that the moon was still there to watch over him, even if he couldn't touch it. And with that, he went to bed, feeling happy and safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Tom. Tom loved playing with his friends outside. He hadn't seen them for a long time. Finally one day, Tom saw his friends playing up the street. He was so excited! He was about to go and join them, when he remembered something. It was cold outside and he had no jumper. Tom started to get a bit sad, but then he had an idea. He ran up to his mom, and asked her for a hug. His mom smiled and said yes. As Tom hugged his mom, he started to feel warm. Tom was so happy. He asked his mom if he could take the hug outside with him. His mom said yes, and showed him what to do. He had to close his eyes and squeeze really tight. When he opened his eyes, Tom saw that the hug had shrunk down so much it could fit in his pocket! He put the small hug in his pocket and ran outside. He felt much warmer now and was able to play with his friends until it was time to go home. Tom was glad he was able to take his mom's hug outside with him. He knew it would keep him warm and safe.
