One day, a little bug named Tim wanted to crawl to the top of a big hill. Tim was a very alert bug, always looking around and listening. He started to crawl up the hill, moving his tiny legs as fast as he could. As Tim crawled, he saw a big pile of leaves. He thought it would be fun to play in the leaves, so he crawled over to them. Inside the pile, he found a file. The file was shiny and new. Tim picked it up and said, "Wow! What a pretty thing!" Just then, a friendly bird named Sue flew down next to Tim. She saw the file and said, "Hi Tim! That's a nice file you found. Can I play with it too?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, Sue! Let's play together!" So Tim and Sue played with the file, taking turns to slide it through the leaves. They had lots of fun and became best friends. After their playtime, they crawled and flew back to their homes, excited to play again another day.

Jimmy was so impatient. He wanted to go whale watching. Every day he asked his mom if they could go. Finally, one day his mom said yes, and they went to the beach. Jimmy was so excited as he watched the horizon. After a few minutes, he saw a whale in the distance. He shouted, "Welcome whale!" as the whale came closer. He was so happy to see the whale, and he started to wave. The whale waved back, and Jimmy was thrilled. Jimmy watched the whale for a long time, until the whale swam too far away, and Jimmy had to go home. He was so happy, and he was already looking forward to his next adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small, noisy town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to jump and spring all day long. He would spring on his bed, spring in the park, and even spring when he was happy. One day, Tim saw a big trophy in a shop. The trophy was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted the trophy, but he had no money. So, he thought of a plan to get the trophy. Tim decided to have a big spring race with his friends. Everyone was excited and came to watch. They all jumped and sprang as fast as they could. Tim sprang the highest and won the race. He was so happy, and his friends gave him the shiny trophy. From that day, Tim and his trophy were always together, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big maze, there lived a curious little mouse named Mimi. Mimi liked to play and explore the maze all day. She had lots of fun running in and out of the many paths. One day, Mimi found a big supply of yummy cheese in the middle of the maze. She was so happy! Mimi decided to share the cheese with her friends, so they could all have a big cheese party. Mimi and her friends had a great time at the cheese party. They laughed, played, and ate lots of cheese. The curious little mouse and her friends were very happy in their maze home. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and her mom were going to visit grandma. They had to take a taxi to the train station. Lily liked taxis. They were yellow and fast and had a funny horn. They waited on the sidewalk and waved at the taxis that passed by. But none of them stopped. Lily was sad. She wanted to see grandma soon. "Mom, why don't they stop?" she asked. "Maybe they are full, or they have to go somewhere else, honey. Don't worry, we'll find one soon," her mom said. Then, a big, black taxi came. It was not yellow like the others. It had a sign that said "unknown". Lily was curious. She had never seen a taxi like that before. "Look, mom, a taxi!" she said. Her mom looked and frowned. She did not like the unknown taxi. It looked old and dirty and scary. "Mom, can we take it?" Lily asked. Her mom hesitated. She did not want to miss the train, but she also did not trust the unknown taxi. "Hello, do you need a ride?" the taxi driver said. He had a big beard and a hat. He smiled, but his teeth were yellow and crooked. Lily's mom did not like his smile. She thought he was not nice. "Um, yes, we do. How much to the train station?" she asked. "Ten dollars," the taxi driver said. Lily's mom thought that was too much. She knew that the yellow taxis charged less. "That's too much. Can you lower the price?" she asked. The taxi driver shook his head. He did not want to lower the price. "Take it or leave it, lady. I have other customers waiting," he said. Lily's mom did not believe him. She did not see any other customers. She thought he was lying. She looked at her watch. They had to hurry. They had no choice. "Fine, let's go," she said. She took Lily's hand and opened the door. They stepped into the unknown taxi. Lily was excited. She wanted to see what was inside. But as soon as they stepped in, the door slammed shut. The taxi driver locked it. He laughed and drove away. "Ha ha ha, you fell for it, lady. This is not a taxi. This is a trap. I'm going to take you somewhere far away and sell you for money. You'll never see grandma again," he said. Lily and her mom were scared. They screamed and cried. They tried to open the door, but it was stuck. They were trapped in the unknown taxi. They wished they had waited for a yellow taxi. They wished they had never stepped into the unknown taxi. They wished they had listened to their gut.
