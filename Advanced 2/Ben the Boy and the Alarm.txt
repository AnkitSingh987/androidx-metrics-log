Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Ben. Every morning, his mum would give him an alarm. But Ben did not like it. He thought the alarm made too much noise and scared him. One morning, Ben told his mum that he did not want to have the alarm. His mum said: "It is easy, Ben. The alarm will help you wake up in the morning". Ben shook his head and said: "No, I don't want it". Soon it was time for Ben to go to bed. But he couldn't sleep. He was too afraid of the alarm. Then he had an idea. He snuck out of bed and disconnected the alarm! Ben was so relieved. He finally felt calm and got a good night's sleep. In the morning, Ben was ready to go! He was so happy that he didn't have to worry about the loud alarm. He was also pleased that he found an easy solution to his problem.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom lived on a farm with many animals. He liked to sleep all day under a big tree. One day, Tom saw a sign near the tree. The sign said, "No lazy cats allowed." Tom did not like the sign. He wanted to keep sleeping under the tree. So, he asked his friend, a horse named Sam, for help. Sam had a long whip that he used to help the farmer. Tom thought the whip could help take down the sign. Sam agreed to help Tom. They went to the sign and Sam used his whip. The whip hit the sign and it fell down. Tom was very happy. Now, he could sleep under the tree again. From that day on, Tom and Sam were the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was an ostrich who wanted to learn how to fly. He asked the birds in the sky why they could fly and he couldn't. They replied, “We were lucky to be born with wings. You were not so lucky.” The ostrich was sad and he started to suffer. Just then, a lucky rabbit hopped along. The ostrich asked the rabbit, “How did you get so lucky?” The rabbit answered, “I have special powers that help me get what I want.” The ostrich was excited and asked the rabbit to help him learn how to fly. The rabbit shook his head and said, “I'm sorry, I can't help you. You have to find your own luck.” The ostrich was disappointed, but then he remembered something the birds said. He thought, “Maybe I can find my own luck and learn how to fly." And so the ostrich set off to find his own luck. The ostrich eventually found his luck, and he learned how to fly. He was so happy and every day he thanked the lucky rabbit for inspiring him to find his luck.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to surprise her daddy. Her daddy worked in an office. Every day, he would go to the office to do his job. One day, Lily decided to make a surprise for her daddy. She made a beautiful drawing with lots of colors. She wanted to give it to him when he came home from the office. She hid the drawing behind her back, waiting for him. When her daddy came home, he looked upset. Lily asked, "Daddy, why are you upset?" Her daddy said, "I had a hard day at the office." Lily smiled and showed him the surprise drawing. Her daddy's face lit up, and he hugged her tight. "Thank you, Lily," he said. "You made my day better!"

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her room. She saw her wide wardrobe. She wanted to observe what was inside. She opened the doors and looked at her clothes. As she was looking, she saw a small door at the back of the wardrobe. It was a secret door! Lily was very surprised. She opened the secret door and went inside. Inside the secret door, Lily found a fun playroom with lots of toys. She played with the toys and had a great time. Then, she heard her mom call her. Lily went back through the secret door, closed it, and went to her mom. She did not tell anyone about the secret playroom in her wide wardrobe. It was her special secret.
