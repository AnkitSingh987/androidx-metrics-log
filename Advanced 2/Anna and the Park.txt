Anna liked to jog with her dad every morning. They would put on their shoes and hats and go outside. They would jog around the park and say hello to the birds and the flowers. Anna felt happy and strong when she jogged with her dad. One day, they saw a man with a big truck in the park. The man was taking out boxes and bags from the truck and putting them on a table. Anna was curious and asked her dad, "What is he doing?" Her dad said, "He is supplying food and water for the people who are running in the race today. They need to eat and drink to have energy and stay healthy." Anna said, "Can we run in the race too?" Her dad said, "No, sweetie, the race is for grown-ups who jog very fast and very far. We jog for fun and for regular exercise. Maybe when you are older, you can run in the race if you want to." Anna said, "Okay, dad, I understand. But can we watch the race and cheer for the runners?" Her dad said, "Of course, we can. That sounds like a great idea. Let's jog to the finish line and find a good spot to see the race." Anna and her dad jogged to the finish line and waited for the runners to come. They clapped and shouted and smiled as they saw the runners cross the line. Anna thought that jogging was a wonderful thing to do, with or without a race.

One day, Bill the rabbit was walking along the field when he saw something pink and shiny. He quickly hopped over and discovered it was a pistol. Bill was very excited, and wanted to show the pistol to all his friends. But then, he heard a voice. "That is my pistol," said the voice. It was Liz the fox. Bill had not seen her coming. Liz was very angry. "I found this pistol first," said Bill, trying to settle the situation. But Liz wasn't happy. "You must give the pistol back to me," she said. Bill knew this was a tricky situation. He didn't like the idea of giving the pistol back to Liz, so he came up with an idea. "I shall keep the pistol, but I'll give it back to you whenever you want to borrow it," said Bill. Liz thought about this for a moment and then smiled. She agreed that this was a good way to settle the dispute. From then on, Bill could keep the pistol for himself, and Liz could borrow it whenever she wanted. They smiled at each other, happy the dispute had been so easily settled.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was very good at flying high and fast. One day, Tim wanted to test how hard he could fly. He wanted to wake up early and start his test. The next morning, Tim woke up when the sun was just coming up. This was his wake time. He felt excited and ready to test his flying. He jumped out of his nest and started to fly high in the sky. Tim flew very hard and fast. He was so happy to see how hard he could fly. After flying for a long time, Tim got tired. He went back to his nest and took a nap. When he woke up, he felt proud of his test. Tim knew he was a great bird who could fly very hard.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a silly rabbit. The silly rabbit liked to play all day. He would hop, skip, and jump around the trees. One day, he found some soft soil near a big tree. The silly rabbit had an idea. He would load the soil into his tiny cart. He put the soil in the cart, one scoop at a time. The cart got full and heavy, but the silly rabbit was strong. He pulled the cart all around the forest. He showed his friends the soil. They all laughed and played with the soil. They had a fun day, and the silly rabbit was very happy. The end.
