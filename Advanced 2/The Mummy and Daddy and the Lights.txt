Once upon a time there was a mummy and a daddy. They were making dinner in their kitchen when the lights suddenly went off. "Mummy, what's happened?" asked the little one. Mummy said, "The lights have gone off, darling. We'll have to turn the switch back on." The little one then ran off to find the switch. He quickly found it and pulled it hard. The lights came back on and the mummy and daddy started to laugh. "That was silly," said daddy. "We should have ordered you to pull it more gently." The mummy and daddy gave the little one a big hug and they all went back to cooking their dinner.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her friends. They liked to choose games to play together. One day, they played with a big toy called a network. The network was a big web made of rope. They could climb on it and have fun. The network was very busy with all the children playing on it. Lily and her friends laughed and smiled as they climbed up and down. They felt like they were on a big adventure. The sun was shining, and the day was perfect for playing outside. After a while, it was time to go home. Lily and her friends said goodbye to the busy network. They promised to come back and play again soon. Lily went home with a big smile on her face, excited to tell her mom and dad all about her fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. One day, it rained a lot and made many puddles on the ground. After the rain stopped, Tim put on his rain boots. He wanted to go outside and play. His mom helped him unpack his raincoat from the closet. Tim put on his raincoat and went outside to find the biggest puddle. Tim found a big puddle near his house. He jumped and splashed in the puddle. The water went up and down, and Tim laughed. He played in the puddle until he was very tired. Then, he went back home to rest. Tim had a fun day playing in the puddles.

Once upon a time, in a big jungle, there lived a little monkey named Momo. Momo loved to build things with his friends. One sunny day, Momo and his friends decided to build a big tower. Momo asked his friend, Toto the elephant, "Can you help me build the tower?" Toto said, "Yes, Momo! I will help you build the tower." So, Momo and Toto started to build the tower with big blocks. As they built the tower, Momo and Toto made sure to be fair. They took turns putting the blocks on the tower. The tower grew taller and taller. Momo and Toto were very happy with their big tower. At the end of the day, Momo and Toto looked at their big tower in the jungle. They were very proud of what they built together. They knew that working together and being fair made their tower so great. From that day on, Momo and Toto always played together and built many more things in the jungle. They learned that being fair and helping each other made everything more fun.

One day, a big bird named Bob wanted to fly high in the sky. He tried and tried, but he could not fly high. Bob was sad. He wanted to be successful and touch the sky. One sunny day, Bob saw a red ball. He had an idea. He would jump on the ball and slip into the sky. Bob was excited. He jumped on the ball and slipped high into the sky. He was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Bob started to fall back down. He was scared. As he fell, a big, soft cloud caught him. The cloud was his friend. Bob was safe and happy. He learned that sometimes, even when things do not go as planned, everything can still be okay.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a red ball. The red ball loved to play with the kids. They would shoot the ball high into the sky, and it would come back down. The red ball and the kids had a lot of fun. One day, a thick fog came to the town. The fog was so thick that the kids could not see the red ball. They still wanted to play, so they tried to shoot the ball into the sky. But the fog was too thick, and they could not see where the ball went. The kids looked and looked for the red ball, but they could not find it. The red ball was lost in the fog. The kids were very sad. They missed their red ball and could not play anymore. The fog stayed in the town, and the red ball was never found again.
