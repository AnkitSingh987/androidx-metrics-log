One day, a bear named Tom went out to find honey. He loved honey very much. Tom walked and walked, and then he saw a big tree. The tree had a hole with honey inside. Tom was very happy. Tom tried to get the honey, but it was icy and cold. He did not like the icy honey. So, Tom called his friend, a bird named Sue. Sue was good at making things warm. Sue came and helped Tom. She used her warm wings to make the icy honey warm. Soon, the honey was not icy anymore. Tom and Sue ate the warm honey together. They were very happy and full.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally felt gloomy because she had no friends to play with. She spent her days alone in her room, wishing for someone to share her toys with. One day, Sally found a small toy heart under her bed. It was red and shiny. Sally thought that maybe this heart could make her happy. She spent her time talking to the heart, and to her surprise, it talked back! The heart became her new best friend. Together, they spent their days playing and laughing. The gloomy feeling went away, and Sally felt happy again. The little heart taught her that even in the darkest times, a friend can bring light and happiness into our lives.

Once upon a time, there was a persistent little mouse named Max. Max lived in a big house with his friend, Sam the cat. Max liked to play tricks on Sam to scare him. One day, Max saw a sink full of water. He thought it would be fun to scare Sam. He hid behind the sink and waited for Sam to come. When Sam walked by, Max jumped out and yelled, "Boo!" Sam was scared and said, "Max, you got me!" Max laughed and said, "I like to scare you, Sam!" Sam smiled and said, "You are a persistent little mouse, Max!" They both laughed and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a room. It was a mild day, and the sun was shining. Inside the wardrobe, there were many clothes. There was a little shirt that was always scared. The shirt would tremble when the wind blew. One day, a small mouse came into the room. The mouse saw the wardrobe and went inside. The little shirt heard the mouse and started to tremble. The mouse saw the shirt and said, "Why are you so scared?" The little shirt told the mouse about the wind. The mouse said, "I can help you not be scared." The mouse and the shirt became friends. They played together, and the shirt did not tremble anymore. The moral of the story is: with the help of friends, we can face our fears.

Once upon a time, there was an intelligent mole. The mole lived in a small hole in the ground. He had many friends in the forest. One day, the mole decided to start digging a new hole. While he was digging, he met a friendly rabbit. The rabbit said, "Hi, mole! What are you doing?" The mole replied, "I am starting a new hole to live in." Together, the mole and the rabbit dug the hole. They had a lot of fun and became best friends. The intelligent mole and the rabbit lived happily ever after in the forest.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an unusual cat named Tim. Tim loved to play with tape. One day, while playing with his tape, he saw a new cat move in next door. Tim wanted to be friends with the new cat. Tim took his tape and went to the new cat's house. He knocked on the door, and when the new cat opened it, Tim said, "Welcome to our town! My name is Tim. Do you want to play with my tape?" The new cat smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play with your tape! My name is Lily." Tim and Lily played with the tape all day long. They had so much fun and became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Lily always played together and shared their toys.
