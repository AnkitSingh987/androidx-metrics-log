One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to eat pizza. He asked his mom for pizza, but she said no. Tim was sad and began to complain. He thought it was stupid that he could not have pizza. Later, Tim's mom went to the store. Tim had a plan. He wanted to make his own pizza. He put bread, cheese, and sauce on a plate. But when he tried to cook it, something unexpected happened. The pizza did not look like a real pizza. It was all messy and strange. Tim's mom came home and saw the messy pizza. She laughed and said, "I have a surprise!" She opened a box and there was a real pizza inside. Tim was so happy. He learned that complaining and trying to do things on his own can be silly. Sometimes, it is better to wait and be surprised.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the park. He had a toy that could spray water. He liked to spray water on the trees and plants. Tim saw a girl named Sue playing with a bag of powder. The powder was sour, and it made her face look funny. Tim said, "Hi, Sue! Can I try your sour powder?" Sue said, "Yes, but it's very sour!" Tim tried the powder and made a funny face too. They laughed and played with the sour powder and the water spray toy. Then, a big wind came and blew the sour powder into the air. The powder mixed with the water spray and made a big, colorful cloud. Tim and Sue were surprised and happy. They watched the cloud float away and said, "Wow! That was fun!" They became best friends and played together every day.

One day, Tom and his mom went out to play. They saw a big, yellow taxi near their house. The taxi was very tidy. Tom and his mom got inside the taxi to go to the park. At the park, Tom played with his ball. He threw it high up in the air. "Look, Mom! I can throw the ball so high!" Tom said. His mom clapped and smiled. Tom felt happy. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the ball away. Tom and his mom ran after it. The wind blew the ball back to the taxi. Tom tried to open the taxi, but it was locked. The taxi driver came and unlocked the door. Tom got his ball back and they all laughed. The day was fun and full of surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a big red oven. It lived in a small house with a kind family. The oven loved to cook yummy food for the family. One day, a hard, round ball arrived at the house. The family was very happy to see the ball. The little boy and girl played with the hard ball in the yard. They laughed and had fun. The oven watched them play and wanted to join the fun too. But the oven was too big and could not move. So, it stayed inside the house and felt a little sad. The next day, the family had an idea. They made a big, soft ball out of dough and put it in the oven. The oven cooked the dough and it became a big, yummy bread. The family ate the bread and thanked the oven. The oven felt happy and loved again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was a very selfish boy. He did not like to share his toys with his friends. One day, Tom's mom told him a story about a fire. Tom listened very carefully. In the story, there was a big fire in the forest. All the animals were scared and running away. A kind and brave elephant named Ellie saw the fire and knew she needed to help. She called out to the other animals, "Let's work together to put out the fire!" The other animals listened to Ellie and started to help. They all worked together to put out the fire. Tom's mom told him that Ellie was not selfish like him. She shared her water with the other animals to put out the fire. Tom felt embarrassed. He realized that he should not be selfish and should share his toys with his friends. The next day, Tom played with his friends and shared his toys. They all had lots of fun together. Tom learned that sharing is good and being selfish is not.
