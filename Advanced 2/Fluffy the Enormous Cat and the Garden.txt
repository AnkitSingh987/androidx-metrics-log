Once upon a time, there was an enormous cat named Fluffy. Fluffy was so big that he could not fit in his little cat bed. Fluffy loved to eat vegetables, like carrots and peas. One day, Fluffy went to the garden to find some vegetables to eat. He saw a big carrot and tried to take it. But the carrot was stuck in the ground! Fluffy pulled and pulled, but the carrot would not come out. Fluffy started to complain. He was sad because he could not get the carrot. Just then, a little girl named Lily came to help. She pulled the carrot with Fluffy, and together they got the carrot out of the ground. Fluffy was so happy and thanked Lily. They both ate some vegetables and became good friends.

One day, a bossy boy named Tim went to play with his friends. He wore his favorite red shirt. He loved to march like a soldier when he played. Tim met his friends, Sam and Lily, at the park. They wanted to play hide and seek. But Tim said, "No, we will march like soldiers today." Sam and Lily didn't want to march, but Tim was very bossy. Sam and Lily got tired of marching. They said, "Tim, we want to play hide and seek now." Tim didn't like that, but he saw his friends were sad. So, he said, "Okay, we can play hide and seek." They all played hide and seek, and Tim learned that it was more fun to play together. He was not bossy anymore, and they all had a great day.

Once upon a time there was a fan who was very independent. She did not like when anybody told her what to do. She only wanted to do things her own way. One day, she decided to go outside. She went to the park, skipping and laughing. But it was very hot outside, so she was very hot and sweaty. Then she saw a big tree with lots of leaves. She wanted to cool down so she jumped up and grabbed a leaf. When she pulled it, the leaf was released from the tree, like a big fan! It cooled her down and it made her feel much better. From that day forward, she knew that she could release her own fan whenever she was too hot. She even took it with her to the park, so she could be cool when she wanted! She was so satisfied with her independent fan that she never stopped using it.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally. She was very proud of herself and loved going on adventures. One day she decided to take a walk in the forest. As Sally went deeper into the woods, she heard a strange sound. Sally quickly rushed to the source of the sound, and she saw a big bear! She was so scared, but the bear just smiled at her. The bear said, "Do not be afraid, I just wanted to see if you were brave enough to explore these woods." Sally was proud of herself for not running away and facing the bear. She said, "Yes, I was brave enough to explore the forest!" The bear laughed and said, "Good! That is why I am proud of you too. Now go and have more fun! Sally thanked the bear and ran off, happy and proud of herself. She promised herself she would go on more adventures and never be afraid.

Once there was a boy named Terry. He was playing in the snow when he saw an icy bulb. He was surprised and wanted to find out what kind of bulb it was. So Terry reached out and touched the icy bulb. It felt cold and slimy. He exclaimed, "What is this?" His mom heard his question and laughed. She said, "That's an ice bulb! When it heats up in the sun, it will melt and you can see what is inside!" Terry was so excited he couldn't wait. He asked his mom, "Can I look now?" But his mom said, "No. You'll have to wait till the sun melts it. It will be a surprise!" Terry was now even more curious and couldn't wait to see what was inside the bulb. He spent hours watching the sun melt the icy bulb. And finally it was ready! Terry was so excited he quickly opened the bulb and found a beautiful blue crystal inside. He was so surprised!
