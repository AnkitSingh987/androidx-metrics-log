Once there was a little girl named Jenny. She loved to talk to everyone she met. One day, she went to a special place. It was white and very pretty. Jenny was very excited! She started to talk to all the animals she saw in this special place. She said hello to the birds, rabbits and even the squirrels. She even talked to the flowers. And they all replied to her in their own language. Jenny was so happy that she stayed there for the whole day, chatting away. Eventually, the sun started to go down, so Jenny had to go back home. But she was already planning to come back to that special white place the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. One day, his mustache became broken. He was very sad. He asked his friend, "What can I do to fix my broken mustache?" His friend replied, "Let's go to the store and buy something to help." At the store, they found a special comb to fix the broken mustache. The man was very happy. He said, "Thank you, friend!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a rare toy with one big eye. Lily loved her toy very much. She named it One-Eye. One day, Lily and One-Eye went to the park. They saw many kids playing. Lily wanted to play too. She asked the kids if they could count with her. The kids said yes. Lily, One-Eye, and the kids sat in a circle. They started to count. One, two, three, four, five. They counted all the way to ten. Lily and the kids had a lot of fun. They all became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was smart and loved to play outside with her friends. One day, Lucy and her friend Tom were playing with a ball near the dark forest. Tom said, "Lucy, be careful! The ball might go into the dark forest." But Lucy didn't listen and kicked the ball too hard. The ball went into the dark forest, and they were both scared. Lucy said, "I'm sorry, Tom. I didn't mean to." Tom forgave her and said, "It's okay, Lucy. Let's be brave and find the ball together." They went into the dark forest, found the ball, and played happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle bunny named Lily. She loved to hop and play in the sunny meadow. One day, she saw a big tree with no leaves. Lily had an idea to make the tree pretty. Lily went to find yellow flowers to decorate the tree. She hopped and hopped until she found a field full of yellow flowers. Lily picked some flowers and hopped back to the tree. She was very happy. Lily put the yellow flowers on the tree. It looked so pretty! All her friends came to see the tree. They all loved it! They played and danced around the tree all day. And from that day on, the tree was the happiest tree in the meadow.

Josh was thirsty and went out to search for water. He came across a mineral pond and was so excited. He carefully took a sip and he knew it was just what he needed. Josh thanked the mineral pond, but it didn't respond to him. He felt a bit lonely and thought he could use a hug. He looked around and saw a big, brown bear cruising through the forest. Josh paused and thought it wouldn't be safe to hug a wild bear! But the brown bear looked very serious so he took a chance and walked up to him. He wrapped his arms around the bear's neck and gave him a big hug. The bear seemed to smile and returned Josh's hug! Josh was so happy as it was the best hug he had ever received. He thanked the brown bear and went back home with a big smile.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very impatient. He wanted to play outside all day. One day, Tim found a wide, big box. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim tried to open the box. He used his hands. He used his feet. But the box would not open. Then, Tim found a sharp rock. He thought the rock could help him open the box. Tim used the sharp rock to open the wide box. Inside, he found a big, bouncy ball. Tim was so happy. He played with the ball all day. And he was not impatient anymore.
