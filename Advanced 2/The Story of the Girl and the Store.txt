Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to store things. She would collect rocks, feathers, and sticks. One summer day, she wanted to store the feeling of summer into a special thing. So she went to the nearest store and asked for a cloth. The storekeeper handed her a bright yellow cloth. The little girl looked up at the storekeeper and said, "It's so hot!" The storekeeper laughed and said, "Yes, it is hot outside today. Make sure you keep it somewhere cool." The little girl thanked him and took the cloth home. She held it close to her heart and let the feeling of summer sink in. Then, she stored it away safely in her room. From then on, the little girl kept the cloth safe. Whenever she missed summer or wanted to store something special, she would take it out and wrap it around herself.

Once upon a time, a furry dog named Max went to the airport with his family. Max was very excited to fly on a big plane. He wagged his tail and barked, "Woof, woof! I can't wait!" At the airport, Max saw many people and heard many sounds. The loud noises made Max panic. He ran away, and his family could not find him. Max's family was sad and worried. They looked everywhere for him. Finally, Max's family found him hiding under a chair. They hugged Max and said, "It's okay, Max. We are here to keep you safe." Max learned that it's important to stay close to his family and not to panic when he's scared. And from that day on, Max always stayed close to his family, no matter where they went.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot liked to crawl around and explore. One day, Spot saw something really special: chocolate! Spot couldn't believe his eyes. He wanted to take a bite, but then he heard a noise. "No, Spot, that's bad!" said his owner. "That chocolate isn't for you." Spot was sad, but then he had an idea. He started to crawl out of the room, with the chocolate in his mouth. Spot's owner saw what Spot was doing, but he was too slow to stop him. He knew he had been bad for leaving the chocolate lying around, and he was worried about what would happen to Spot when he ate it. But it was too late. Less than a minute later, Spot had eaten the whole thing. Spot's owner sighed. He was sad that Spot had been bad, but he was relieved that he hadn't gotten sick from eating the chocolate.

Once upon a time, there was a green ostrich named Ollie. Ollie lived in a big forest with many other animals. Ollie liked to play with his friends, the rabbit, the frog, and the squirrel. One sunny day, they all went to the big tree to watch a show. The squirrel was very good at jumping, so he wanted to show his friends. Everyone sat down and watched. The squirrel jumped high and far, and they all clapped. They were happy to see their friend do a good job. After the squirrel's show, Ollie the green ostrich wanted to try too. He took a big step back and ran very fast. Ollie jumped high in the air, and his friends watched. When he landed, everyone clapped for him too. They all laughed and played together, happy to have fun with their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a famous dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run in the park. One day, Spot found a file. The file was big and had many papers in it. Spot wanted to find the owner of the file, so he shouted, "Who lost this file?" A little girl named Sue heard Spot and came running. Sue said, "That's my file! Thank you, Spot!" Spot and Sue became good friends. They played together every day in the park. Spot was happy he found the file and helped Sue. Everyone knew the famous dog named Spot.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a favorite book. It was a big, old, filthy book. Tim loved to read it every day. One day, Tim's mom saw the filthy book. She didn't like it. "This book is too dirty," she said. She took the book away. Tim was very sad. Tim's dad saw him crying. "Why are you sad?" he asked. Tim told him about the filthy book. Tim's dad had an idea. "Let's clean the book," he suggested. So they did. They made the book nice and clean. Tim's mom was happy, and Tim could read his favorite book again.
