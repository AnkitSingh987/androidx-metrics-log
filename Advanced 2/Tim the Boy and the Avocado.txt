Once upon a time, in an ordinary house, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat avocado. Every day, he would ask his mom for some avocado on his toast. One day, while eating his avocado toast, Tim saw a little green creature in the garden. The creature was shaped like an avocado! Tim went outside and said, "Hi, who are you?" The creature smiled and said, "I am Ava, the avocado fairy." Tim and Ava played in the garden all day. They had so much fun. Tim asked Ava, "Can you come to my house every day?" Ava said, "Yes, I will come and play with you every day, Tim." From that day on, Tim and Ava became best friends. They played together every day and Tim always had avocado on his toast. The ordinary house became a magical place, filled with laughter and fun.

Once upon a time there was a big fat cat. He was always looking for something to eat, but he was never very lucky. One day, he saw something strange. It was a mattress pushed against the wall of a house. He pushed against it and the mattress seemed to be alive! The fat cat was so surprised that he said "Who are you?". The mattress replied, "I'm a mattress. What are you?" The cat smiled and said, "I'm a fat cat. Nice to meet you!" They talked for a while and had a great time together. After that, the fat cat could often be seen pushing against the mattress to chat with his new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tiny ant named Andy. He was an inch long and loved to join his friends to play. One day, Andy went to the park to meet his friends, but he was late. He felt embarrassed because all his friends were waiting for him. When he got to the park, his friend Bee said, "Andy, why are you late? We were waiting for you!" Andy was shy and said, "I am sorry, I was slow today." His friends smiled and said, "That's okay, Andy. Let's play together now." So, Andy and his friends played all day long. They joined in games like tag and hide-and-seek. They had so much fun, and Andy was happy that he could join his friends. From that day on, Andy tried to be on time, so he wouldn't feel embarrassed again.

Once upon a time there was a boat that sailed the seven seas. The boat had a yellow anchor that it used to keep the boat still. On a hot day, the boat was out at sea. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and the anchor was pulled from the boat and sank to the bottom of the ocean. The boat floated away and everyone was scared. The captain was furious and shouted to the crew. He hated that the anchor was lost and he did not know how his boat could ever sail without it. All of a sudden, the crew spotted something yellow in the water. It was the anchor! The crew members carefully pulled the anchor onto the boat and the captain was very happy. The crew tied the anchor to the deck and the captain smiled. Now the boat could sail again. Everyone cheered and let out a big "hurrah"!

One day, a boy named Tim had a bicycle. He loved to ride it far away. One morning, his mom said, "Tim, do not go to the distant place today." But Tim did not mention this to his friend, Sam. Tim and Sam went to the park with their bicycles. They saw a big hill. "Let's go up the hill!" said Sam. Tim thought of what his mom said, but he did not tell Sam. They went up the hill together. At the top of the hill, they saw the distant place. They rode down very fast. Tim fell off his bicycle and got hurt. He cried and wished he had listened to his mom.

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat spaghetti for dinner. Mom cooked spaghetti in a big pot. She put sauce and cheese on top. It smelled good. "Can we help you, Mom?" Tom and Lily asked. "Sure, you can help me set the table. But be careful with the plates and forks. They are not toys," Mom said. Tom and Lily took the plates and forks from the cupboard. They walked to the table. But Tom was careless. He did not look where he was going. He bumped into Lily. The plates and forks fell on the floor. They made a loud noise. "Oh no! Look what you did, Tom!" Lily cried. "I'm sorry, Lily. I did not mean to," Tom said. Mom came to see what happened. She saw the mess on the floor. She was not happy. "Tom, Lily, you need to clean this up. You were not careful. You could have hurt yourselves or broken the plates and forks. Go get the broom and the dustpan," Mom said. Tom and Lily felt bad. They said sorry to Mom. They got the broom and the dustpan. They cleaned the floor. They picked up the plates and forks. They set the table. They learned to be more careful. Mom forgave them. She smiled and hugged them. She served them spaghetti. They ate and enjoyed it. They thanked Mom for the dinner. They said they loved her. Mom said she loved them too. They were happy.
