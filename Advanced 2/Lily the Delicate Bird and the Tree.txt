Once upon a time, there was a delicate bird named Lily. She lived in a big tree near a pretty park. Lily loved to sing and play with her friends all day long. One day, when the sky turned dark, Lily could not find her way home. She was scared and alone. She could hear noises in the dark, but she did not know what they were. Then, a kind friend named Tom came to help her. Tom was a big, strong dog who lived in the park. He heard Lily's cries and came to find her. Together, they found their way back to Lily's tree, and they were happy. From that day on, Lily and Tom were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a woman named Sally. Sally was incredible. She loved to dance and slip on her shoes. One day, Sally went to the park. At the park, Sally met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Sally's shoes and said, "Wow, your shoes are so cool!" Sally smiled and said, "Thank you! I love to dance and slip on my shoes." Sally and Tim played in the park. They ran, jumped, and laughed. But then, Sally slipped on a rock. She fell down and hurt her leg. Tim was sad and said, "Oh no, are you okay?" Sally tried to stand up, but her leg hurt too much. She could not dance anymore. Sally and Tim were both very sad. They went home, and Sally's incredible shoes stayed in the closet.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a small mouse. They lived in a tight, cozy house. The big cat and the small mouse were good friends. The house was full of noise when they played together, and they both belonged there. One day, the big cat and the small mouse went for a walk. They saw a big box. The big box made a funny noise. The big cat and the small mouse were scared. They did not know what it was. They thought something unexpected was inside the box. The big cat and the small mouse opened the box. Inside the box, they found a big dog. They were scared at first, but the big dog was nice. He did not want to hurt them. The big dog belonged with them. Now, the big cat, the small mouse, and the big dog all live together in the tight, cozy house. They made even more noise when they played, but they were all happy.

Once upon a time, in a land full of colors, there was a big white dog. The dog was very loyal to his best friend, a little boy named Tom. They played together every day. One sunny day, Tom wanted to build something fun. "Let's build a big fort!" he said to the white dog. The dog wagged his tail and barked happily. They found some big boxes and started to build their fort. As they built the fort, the white dog helped Tom by holding the boxes in place with his paws. They worked together until the fort was all done. Tom and the loyal white dog crawled inside and laughed. They had so much fun in their new fort, and they were the best of friends forever.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a nice park. In the park, there was a broken bench. One sunny day, a boy named Tim and his friend, Lily, went to the park to play. As they played, Tim found some paint near the broken bench. He had an idea. "Lily, let's paint the broken bench and make it pretty," said Tim. Lily agreed, and they started to paint the bench with the colors they found. After a while, the broken bench was colorful and beautiful. Tim and Lily were very happy. They played in the park all day, and the broken bench became their favorite place to sit and talk. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a great lecture with his mom. The lecture was about big animals. Tim loved animals, so he was very excited. He looked around and saw many other kids with their parents. Tim knew it was going to be a fun day. During the lecture, a man showed pictures of big animals on a screen. Tim saw a big elephant, a tall giraffe, and a strong lion. He was so happy to learn about these animals. Suddenly, Tim shouted, "Look, Mom! A big elephant!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, that's an elephant. Remember to use a quiet voice inside." Later, the man asked the kids to come up and play a game. The game was to guess the animal by its sound. Tim was very good at this game. He remembered how the man talked about the animals and their sounds. When it was Tim's turn, he heard a loud roar. He thought about the strong lion from before and said, "It's a lion!" Everyone clapped, and Tim felt very proud.
