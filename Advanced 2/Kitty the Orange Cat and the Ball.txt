Once upon a time, there was a little orange cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to study life. She would watch the birds and the bugs in the garden. One day, she saw a big orange ball in the sky. "Wow!" she said, "That looks fun!" Kitty asked her friend, Doggy, "What is that big orange ball?" Doggy said, "I think it's a toy. Let's play with it!" They tried to jump and catch the ball, but it was too high. They were sad, but they did not give up. The next day, they saw a little boy with a big orange balloon. They asked him, "Can we play with your toy?" The little boy said, "Sure, but this is not the big orange ball in the sky. This is just a balloon." Kitty and Doggy played with the balloon and had lots of fun. They learned that even if they can't reach the big orange ball in the sky, they can still have fun with their new friend and the orange balloon.

One day, a little owl named Ollie felt very hungry. He flew around the big tree to find some food. As he looked around, he saw a small mouse on the ground. Ollie thought, "That mouse will be good to eat!" Ollie swooped down and said, "Hello, little mouse. I am very hungry. Can you help me find some food?" The mouse looked up and yawned. "I am sleepy, but I can help you find food," said the mouse. Together, Ollie the owl and the mouse searched for food. They found a big pile of yummy bugs. Ollie and the mouse ate the bugs and became full. They were not hungry anymore. Ollie said, "Thank you, little mouse, for helping me find food." The mouse smiled and said, "You're welcome, Ollie. Now, let's go to sleep." And they both slept happily under the big tree.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sandra. She was three years old, and was always out to try new things. One day she decided she wanted to try to build something. She wanted to build a house out of gold. Sandra went outside to find some gold and she came across a large pile of gold. She was so proud of herself and decided to use it to build her house. Sandra worked hard all day, and when the sun had set she had built a small house. She was so proud of it. She ran inside to show her mom and dad. "Mom, Dad! Look what I made," Sandra said happily. Her mom and dad were impressed. "You did a great job, Sandra," her dad said. "I'm so proud of you." Sandra was beaming. She had succeeded in her goal. She knew that she could do anything if she just tried her best. She was proud of herself.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old man who lived in a cabin in the woods. One day, he heard a loud shout coming from outside. When he went to investigate, he saw a little three-year-old boy who was running around and shouting with excitement. The wise old man asked the boy, "What are you shouting about?" The little boy replied, "I found some candy in the cabin, and I am so excited!" The wise old man smiled and said, "Well, you are certainly a wise young man to have found some candy in the cabin. Just remember to enjoy it and not eat it all at once!" The wise old man then watched as the little boy was happily shouting and running around with the candy in his hand. The wise old man knew that the little boy had enjoyed a special treasure and was very wise to use it with respect.

Once there was a boy named Bob. Every day, he had to go to his school which he didn't like very much. Every day his teacher would tell him to create something, but the projects could never be what Bob wanted to make. The teacher was very mean to Bob and she would always scold him if he didn't complete his projects on time, or if he made a mistake. Bob never wanted to be at school and he wished he could just have some fun instead of having to sit in his boring classroom. One day, Bob decided he didn't want to create anything anymore, so he just sat in the corner and pretended he wasn't there. His teacher got very angry at him and told him he needed to do all his projects or else he would get into a lot of trouble. But Bob was so tired of being told what to do, he just stayed in the corner and didn't listen. As a result, he got in a lot of trouble and was given a very bad punishment. From then on, Bob never wanted to go back to school again. The end.
