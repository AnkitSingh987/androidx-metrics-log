Once upon a time, there was a big girl named Sue. Sue had a pretty bracelet. She loved her bracelet very much. One day, she went to the park to play. She wore her bracelet on her arm. At the park, Sue met a small girl named Lily. Lily saw Sue's bracelet and liked it too. She asked Sue if she could bring the bracelet to the park the next day. Sue said yes, she would bring the bracelet again. The next day, Sue and Lily played at the park. Sue brought the bracelet like she said she would. Lily was very happy. They played and had fun all day. In the end, Sue let Lily wear the bracelet. They became best friends and shared the pretty bracelet together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a busy day playing with her toys and her best friend, Tom. After they played, Lily went to her room to write in her diary. In her diary, Lily wrote about her fun day with Tom. She liked to remember all the good times they had together. Tom was a funny boy who always made Lily laugh. One day, Tom dared Lily to jump in a big puddle. Lily was scared at first, but she did it anyway. They laughed and laughed as they splashed in the water. The next day, Lily and Tom were playing again. Lily said, "Tom, I dare you to climb the big tree in the park." Tom was scared, but he wanted to be brave like Lily. So, he climbed the tree with Lily cheering him on. They had another fun day together, and Lily wrote about it in her diary. That night, Lily's mom read her diary and smiled. She was happy that Lily had a good friend like Tom. And Lily was happy too, because she had a diary to remember all the fun times they had together. They were the best of friends, and they always dared each other to be brave and try new things.

One day, a boy named Tom found a wide box. He opened it and saw a toy laser. Tom was very happy. He wanted to show it to his friend, Jane. Tom ran to Jane's house and knocked on the door. "Look, Jane! I found a toy laser!" Tom said. Jane looked at the laser and shrugged. "I don't know what it does," she said. They decided to play with it outside. As they played, the laser made a loud noise. Suddenly, a big hole appeared in the ground. Tom and Jane were scared. They didn't know what to do. They tried to fill the hole with dirt, but it didn't work. In the end, Tom and Jane were sad. They knew they should not have played with the laser. The big hole stayed in the ground, and they could not fix it.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a guitar in his room. He picked it up and started to play. It made a funny sound, like a sour note. Tim wanted to learn how to play the guitar well, so he practiced every day. One day, while Tim was playing, his friend Sam came over. Sam saw the guitar and said, "I can play that too!" Sam picked up the guitar and started to play. But just like Tim, Sam played a sour note too. Tim and Sam both wanted to play the guitar well, but they didn't know how. They decided to ask their friend, Max, for help. Max knew how to play the guitar very well. Max showed them how to hold the guitar and how to play the right notes. Tim and Sam practiced with Max, and soon they were playing beautiful music together. They were so happy that they could play the guitar without any sour notes. They played and played, making the music zoom through the air, filling their room with happy sounds.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy aeroplane. It flew high, higher than the clouds. It reached all the way up to the sky. Every day, the aeroplane flew around, saying hello to all the trees, the birds and the sun. One day, the aeroplane saw something very shiny and special. It was a star all the way up in the night sky! The aeroplane wanted to reach the star, so it stretched its wings wide and flew as fast as it could. Finally, the aeroplane reached the star. It was so high in the sky, it felt like the sky was never ending. The aeroplane was so pleased with itself that it smiled and flew back home. The end!
