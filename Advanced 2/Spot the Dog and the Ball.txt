Once upon a time, there was a dog named Spot. Spot had four legs, just like a normal dog. He liked to run and play with his ball every day. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, while Spot was playing, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and changed Spot's ball into a big, round balloon. Spot was surprised, but he still wanted to play. He tried to bite the balloon, but it went up, up, up into the sky. Spot was sad because he lost his ball. He sat down on his legs and looked up at the sky. Then, a friendly bird flew down and gave Spot a new ball. Spot was so happy! He jumped up and down on his legs and played with his new ball. From that day on, Spot and the bird were best friends, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little bird. This bird loved to play with a long wire. The wire was thin and shiny. The bird liked to twist it and make shapes with it. One day, the bird found a magic seed. The seed could make things shrink. The clever bird thought it would be fun to shrink the wire. So, the bird put the seed near the wire and said the magic word, "Shrink!" The wire became very, very small. The bird was happy and played with the tiny wire all day. But then, the bird missed the big wire. The clever bird thought hard and found another magic seed. This seed could make things grow. The bird put the new seed near the small wire and said the magic word, "Grow!" The wire grew back to its big size. The clever bird was happy to have the big wire again. The bird played with the wire every day and had lots of fun.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a big book. It was a "dictionary". Sue wanted to be independent, so she tried to read the book by herself. She asked her mom, "What does 'reveal' mean?" Her mom said, "It means to show something that was hidden." Sue liked that word and wanted to reveal something too. Sue went to the park with her friends. They played hide and seek. When it was her turn to find her friends, she said, "I will reveal you!" She found all her friends and they laughed. Then, a big wind came and blew the dictionary away. Sue was sad, but her friends helped her find the book. When they found it, they saw that the wind had turned the pages to a new word. It was "independent". Sue smiled and said, "I want to be independent too!" They all laughed and played together, happy to learn new words.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to splash in the pond all day. One day, while Ducky was playing, he saw a tiny needle on the ground. Ducky didn't know what it was, so he went to ask his friend, Bunny. Bunny was a fearful rabbit who was always afraid of new things. When Ducky showed Bunny the needle, Bunny got scared. He told Ducky to be very careful with it because it could hurt them. Ducky and Bunny decided to take the needle to their friend, Owl. Owl was very wise and knew many things. Owl told them that the needle could be used to fix things, but they need to be careful. So, Ducky and Bunny put the needle in a safe place and went back to the pond to splash and play together.

Once upon a time, in a quiet forest, there lived a little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to fly and play with his friends. One day, it started to rain. All the animals in the forest ran to find a place to hide. Bobby saw a hurt bunny who could not run fast. He decided to help the bunny find a safe place to stay. Bobby and the bunny found a big tree to hide under. They stayed there until the rain stopped. When the rain was gone, the bunny thanked Bobby for helping him. Bobby felt happy that he could help his new friend. The other animals saw Bobby's kindness and they all became friends. The moral of the story is to always help others when they are in need.
