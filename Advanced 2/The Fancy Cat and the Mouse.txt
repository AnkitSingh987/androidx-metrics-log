Once upon a time, there was a fancy cat. The cat had a big, red mug. She liked to drink milk from her mug every day. One day, the cat left her mug on the table. A little mouse saw the mug and wanted to drink from it too. The mouse climbed up the table and took a sip of milk. The cat saw the mouse and ran to the table. She shut the door and said, "No more milk for you, little mouse!" The mouse was sad, but the cat let him go. They both learned to share the milk from the fancy mug. And they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big river. Many animals lived near the river. One day, a bossy duck came to the river. The duck wanted to be the leader of all the animals. He said, "I am the boss here. You must listen to me." All the animals did not like the bossy duck. They did not want to listen to him. They wanted to live happily near the river. The kind rabbit said, "We can all be friends. We do not need a boss." The bossy duck did not like what the rabbit said. He got angry. But the other animals agreed with the rabbit. They wanted to be friends and help each other. So, the animals decided to settle near the river together. They played, shared food, and had fun. The bossy duck saw how happy they were. He wanted to be happy too. So, he stopped being bossy and became friends with the other animals. From that day, all the animals lived happily near the river. They settled there, played together, and took care of each other. And the bossy duck learned that being friends was better than being a boss.

Once upon a time, there was a big rock. It was a fair rock, not too big and not too small. The rock lived in a nice park with lots of grass and trees. Every day, kids would come to the park and play near the rock. They would run, jump, and laugh. The rock liked to watch them have fun. One day, a little girl named Sue came to the park. She saw the rock and thought it was a nice place to sit. Sue sat on the rock and started to tap her feet. She tapped and tapped and made a fun beat. The rock liked the beat and started to move a little. Sue was surprised but kept tapping her feet. As Sue tapped her feet, the rock moved more and more. Then, something unexpected happened. The rock began to dance! Sue and the rock danced together in the park. The other kids saw them and joined in. They all danced and laughed together. From that day on, the rock was known as the dancing rock, and the park became a very special place for everyone.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived an old man with a cane. He was very kind and loved to help others. One day, he saw a dirty dog near his house. The dog looked sad and hungry. The old man said to the dog, "Come here, little dog. Let me help you." He took the dog inside his house and gave it a bath. He used his cane to pick up a towel and dry the dog. After that, he gave the dog some food to eat. Soon, the dog felt better and started to wag its tail. They became good friends and lived happily together. The dog learned to pick up things for the old man, and the old man took care of the dog. The people in the village saw how happy they were and learned the moral value of helping others, even if they are dirty or in need.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a hairy cat. This cat loved to eat melon. Every day, the cat would go to the market and look for the biggest melon it could find. One day while looking for melons, the cat met a little mouse. The mouse was also looking for melons. The cat mentioned to the mouse, "I love melons too! Let's find a big one together." The mouse nodded, and they both searched for the biggest melon they could find. They found a very big melon and decided to share it. The hairy cat and the little mouse sat down under a tree, and they ate the melon together. They were both very happy and became good friends. From that day on, they always shared their melons and had lots of fun together.
