Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a colorful house. In the house, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to fold paper into fun shapes like dogs, cats, and birds. One day, Sally went for a walk with her mom in the city. They saw many colorful things like big cars, tall buildings, and pretty flowers. Sally had an idea. She wanted to fold paper to look like the city. Sally and her mom went home. They found colorful paper and started to fold it. They made small cars, tall buildings, and pretty flowers. When they were done, they had a tiny, colorful city made of folded paper. Sally was very happy and showed her new city to all her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Skip. Skip loved to skip all day long. He lived near a big pile of lumber, where he played with his friends. One sunny day, Skip and his friends were playing near the lumber. They were having lots of fun. They laughed and skipped around the big pile of wood. They didn't see the little door hidden behind the lumber. While they were playing, the little door opened, and out came a tiny, soft mouse. The mouse was scared at first, but then he saw Skip and his friends having fun. He wanted to join them too. The mouse said, "Hi, I'm Tim. Can I play with you?" Skip and his friends were surprised, but they were happy to have a new friend. They all played and skipped together, and they had the best day ever. From that day on, Tim the mouse became a part of their group, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a green park, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to spin around and around. One sunny day, Sally went to the park to play. At the park, Sally met a boy named Tim. Tim looked sad. Sally asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "I tried to spin like you, but I fell down. It was terrible!" Sally wanted to help Tim. She said, "Let's spin together, Tim. Hold my hand and we will spin slowly." Tim held Sally's hand and they began to spin. They spun around and around, and Tim did not fall down. They both laughed and played together all day in the green park. And Tim never felt terrible again.

One day, a little girl named Ann noticed something in the kitchen. She saw a big jar of butter on the table. Ann was very hungry and wanted to eat the butter. But the butter was not clean. There was dirt on it. Ann said to her mom, "Mom, I want to eat the butter, but it is not clean. What should I do?" Her mom looked at the butter and saw the dirt. She knew that they needed to clean the butter before Ann could eat it. Mom and Ann worked together to clean the butter. They used a clean cloth and wiped off the dirt. After they cleaned the butter, Ann was happy. She said, "Thank you, Mom! Now I can eat the butter!" Ann ate the clean butter and her tummy was full.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a comet in the sky. The comet was very big and bright. Tim felt uncomfortable because he had never seen a comet before. He ran to his mom and said, "Mom, there is a big thing in the sky! It makes me feel funny." His mom smiled and said, "Don't worry, Tim. That is a comet. It is very far away and cannot hurt you." Tim felt better, but he still looked at the comet with big eyes. One night, the comet did something unexpected. It fell from the sky and landed in Tim's backyard! Tim saw that the comet was hurt. He said, "Mom, the comet needs help!" So, Tim and his mom went outside to heal the comet. They put a big band-aid on it, and the comet felt better. The comet thanked Tim and his mom, and then it flew back into the sky. Tim was happy that he could help the comet, and he was not uncomfortable anymore.
