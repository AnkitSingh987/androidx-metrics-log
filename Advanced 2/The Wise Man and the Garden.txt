Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a wise old man. He had a big garden with many plants. In the garden, there was a special potato. This potato was not like the others. One day, a little girl came to visit the wise old man. She saw the special potato and smiled. The wise old man smiled too. He knew the potato was special because it could make people happy. The little girl and the wise old man became good friends. They would play in the garden and eat the special potatoes. Each time they ate a potato, they would smile and laugh. The whole village soon learned about the special potatoes and everyone would come to the garden to eat them and be happy.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to win a reward. The reward was for the best drawing. Tim thought it was easy. He started to draw a big, happy sun. His friend, Lily, came over and said, "I want to win, too!" She started to draw a pretty flower. They both worked hard on their drawings. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew their drawings away! "Oh no!" said Tim. "What do we do now?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Let's not quit. We can draw together!" So they drew a big, happy sun and a pretty flower together. It was the best drawing ever. They both won the reward and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big hospital. It was a lonely hospital because it had no friends. The hospital was sad and wanted friends to play with. One day, a kind man came to the hospital. He wanted to help the hospital. The kind man said, "I will design a park for you. Then, you will have many friends to play with." The hospital was very happy. The kind man worked hard to design the park. Many kids came to play at the park. The kids were the hospital's friends. The hospital was not lonely anymore. The hospital and the kids played together, and they were all happy.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful forest, there lived a little bird named Luna. Luna loved to fly and sing with her friends. One day, while flying, Luna met a big, wise owl. The owl said, "Luna, be careful with your lung, don't fly too high or you will get hurt." Luna did not listen to the owl. She wanted to fly higher and higher. Her friends tried to stop her, but she did not want to listen. "I can do it," she said. Luna flew so high that the sky became dark and the air became thin. Luna's lung hurt, but she did not stop. She wanted to touch the stars. Suddenly, Luna could not breathe and she fell down. Her friends cried and said, "We told you not to fly so high." Luna closed her eyes and never woke up again. The peaceful forest was sad and quiet without Luna's songs.

One day, a big bear and a little bird were friends. They liked to play together. The bear was good at finding food, and the bird liked to sing. The bear said, "I am tired. I want to sleep." The bird said, "Okay, I will sing to help you sleep." The bird sat on a pole and sang a nice song. The bear closed his eyes and started to sleep. While the bear was sleeping, a big wind came. The wind was so strong that it moved the pole. The bird was scared and called for help. The bear woke up and saw the bird in trouble. The bear got up and saved the bird from the moving pole. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, bear!" And they both went back to sleep.

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. It was a cold day, but he put on his warm coat and hat. He saw a tiny spark on the ground and it made him excited. Tim wanted to see what the spark could do. He encouraged the spark to grow bigger and brighter. He said, "You can do it, spark!" The spark grew bigger and turned into a warm and friendly fire. The fire helped Tim stay warm on the cold day. They played together and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim was happy he encouraged the spark and made a new friend.
