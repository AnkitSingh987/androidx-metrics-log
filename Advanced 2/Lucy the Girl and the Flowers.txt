Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She went with her mom and dad to a hotel. The hotel was big and pretty. Lucy was excited to be there. In the hotel, Lucy saw a big red button on the wall. She wanted to touch it, but her mom said, "No, Lucy! Don't touch the button. It's naughty to touch things that don't belong to us." Lucy listened to her mom and did not touch the button. Later, Lucy and her mom went to the hotel garden. They saw pretty flowers and green grass. Lucy saw a butterfly and tried to touch it, but it flew away. Lucy laughed and played in the garden. She was a good girl and had a fun time at the hotel.

Mama and Daddy were examining their backyard together. "It looks strong," said Daddy. "Let's make sure it stays that way." Mama gave a nod. She pointed and said, "I think we should put a fence here, and slide over there." Daddy laughed. "That sounds like a great idea. We can build it together." Mama was excited. They gathered materials and started to work. Together, they put up the fence and made it very strong. The backyard looked beautiful when they were done. Daddy smiled and said, "Our backyard is safe now." Mama smiled too. They hugged and went inside. Mama said, "Thank you for helping me make our backyard strong." Daddy laughed. He said, "It was my pleasure."

Once upon a time, there was a powerful girl named Sue. Sue loved to run and jump. One day, she saw a big tree with a shiny medal up high. Sue wanted to reach the medal. Sue asked her friend, Tom, "Can you help me reach the medal?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." They tried to reach the medal, but it was too high. They needed to think of a new way. Suddenly, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird picked up the medal in its beak and gave it to Sue. Sue and Tom were so happy. They never thought a bird would help them. They thanked the bird and shared the shiny medal.

Once upon a time, there was a big bear. He always seemed really grumpy. He always wore a suit and never laughed. He lived by himself in the woods. One day, the bear saw something in a tree. It was a bird - a beautiful little yellow bird. He had never seen one before. The bear frowned. "What are you doing here? You don't belong here!" he said in a harsh voice. The bird was scared. He flew away, leaving the bear alone again. The bear seemed sad. He wanted the bird to stay, but his words had been too harsh. He wished he'd been kinder. The bear looked up at the sky and sighed. Then he slowly walked away. He knew he should be nicer, but it seemed like he had already lost the one that he wanted most.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy wanted to make a pie. It was very important to her because she wanted to surprise her mom. She asked her big brother Tom to help her. Lucy said, "Tom, can you support me in making the pie?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, Lucy! Let's make the pie together." They worked together to make the pie. Lucy mixed the dough and Tom put in the fruit. When they were done, they put the pie in the oven. They waited and waited until it was ready. When the pie was done, they took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! They were very proud of their pie. They knew their mom would be happy. Their mom came home and saw the pie. She was so surprised and happy! She said, "Thank you, Lucy and Tom! This pie is very important to me because you made it together." They all sat down and enjoyed the pie as a family.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, messy room. She did not like to clean it. One day, her mom told her to unpack her toys. Lily did not want to, but she had to listen to her mom. As Lily started to unpack her toys, she found a small box. She thought it was a secret box. She was very excited. Lily opened the box and saw a note inside. The note said, "If you want a clean room, say these words: 'Clean, clean, make my room clean!'" Lily said the words, but her room did not get clean. Instead, it got messier and messier. Toys and clothes were everywhere. Lily was sad. She learned that there are no secrets to make things easy. She had to clean her room by herself.
