One day, a boy named Tim went to the doctor with his mom. Tim was not feeling well. The doctor said, "I think we need an x-ray." Tim did not know what an x-ray was, but his mom said it would help him feel better. At the x-ray room, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue and Tim did not agree on many things. They disagreed on what color was the best, and what games were the most fun. But they both wanted to feel better. Then, something strange happened. When the x-ray was done, Tim and Sue felt much better. They were very grateful to the doctor. And even though they disagreed on many things, they became good friends. They learned that sometimes, it is okay to disagree and still be friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She liked to play with her pastel crayons. Lucy had a comfortable bed, where she would sit and draw pretty pictures. One day, Lucy drew a big, pretty butterfly. She wished it could fly. So, Lucy closed her eyes and started to pray. She prayed very hard, hoping her butterfly would come to life. Suddenly, the butterfly flew off the paper! Lucy was very surprised. She learned that if you pray and believe, good things can happen. And from that day on, Lucy and her butterfly became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who loved to bake. Every morning, he would walk to the local store, pick out the different ingredients he needed and then go home to bake. One day, he decided to make a basket of goodies for his mum as a special surprise. He decided to bake some cookies, a cake for his mum, and some other treats he knew she would love. Once he had finished baking, he put the treats into a pretty basket and was about to take it to his mum when he saw his neighbour. His neighbour was poor and had nothing to eat. The boy thought of how he could share his kind gesture with the neighbour. He gifted the attractive basket to the neighbour, telling him to share it with his family. The neighbour was extremely happy and thanked the boy. The moral of this story is that it is important to be kind and generous to others, even if it is a simple act. Every act of kindness is special and can go a long way.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Yesterday, she found a big, tasty apple under a tree. She was very happy and wanted to eat it. But first, she needed to clean the apple. Lily took the apple to a small pond to soak it in the water. She put the apple in the water and watched it get clean. But then, a big frog jumped on the apple and pushed it away. Lily tried to catch the apple, but it was too far away now. Lily was very sad because she lost her tasty apple. The frog just sat on a rock and looked at her. The apple floated away, and Lily never got to eat it. And that is the end of the story.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play together. One day, they decide to play sell. They find some toys and books and put them on a table. They make a sign that says "Sell. One coin for each thing." They wait for some people to come and buy their things. But no one comes. They are sad. "Maybe we need to go to a different place," Lily says. "Maybe we need to go to the town." "The town?" Ben asks. "What is the town?" "The town is a big place with many people and houses and shops," Lily explains. "It is very interesting. My mom took me there once. Maybe we can sell our things there." "OK, let's go to the town," Ben agrees. "But how do we get there?" They look around and see a bus. The bus has a sign that says "Town". They run to the bus and get on. They give the driver two coins and find a seat. The bus starts to move. Lily and Ben look out the window. They see many new things. They see cars and trees and animals and bridges. They see a big river and a big park. They see a tall tower and a big clock. They are very excited. "Look, Ben, look!" Lily says. "This is the town. It is so interesting. Do you think we can sell our things here?" "I don't know, Lily, I don't know," Ben says. "But I want to see more. This is fun." They smile and hold their things. They are happy. They are going to the town.
