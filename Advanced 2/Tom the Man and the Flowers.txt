One day, an elderly man named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big land with many trees and flowers. Tom liked this land, so he walked on it. As he walked, he saw a little boy named Tim. Tim looked angry. He said, "Stop! This is my land!" Tom did not want to make Tim sad, so he said, "I am sorry, I did not know this was your land." But Tim did not want to share. He wanted the land all to himself. So, he pushed Tom away. Tom fell down and got hurt. He felt very sad and went back to his home. Tim kept the land, but now he was all alone.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a scared little rabbit named Remy. Remy was scared of everything. One day, Remy went to look for his best friend, a pine tree named Pippin. Remy and Pippin loved to play together every day. On his way to find Pippin, Remy saw a big, dark cloud. It made him feel more scared. He wanted to delay going to Pippin, but he knew his friend was waiting for him. So, Remy took a deep breath and hopped faster to find Pippin. When Remy found Pippin, he said, "I was scared of the big, dark cloud, but I didn't delay coming to see you." Pippin smiled and said, "I'm so happy you came, Remy. We can be scared together, but we'll always be safe with each other." And they played happily under the big pine tree all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Abigail. She was very excited to go to school and learn new things. When Abigail arrived at school, the teacher showed her the blackboard. The teacher said, “Abigail, for the first lesson, I want you to cover the blackboard with chalk drawings!” Abigail was so excited that she jumped up and down and screamed in delight. She covered the blackboard with all sorts of beautiful drawings. Her teacher was so impressed with her art that he praised her. At the end of the day, Abigail had learned a new skill and was even more excited than when she had first come to school. She waved goodbye to her teacher and gave him a big hug. Then she turned around and ran out of the classroom, still excited about what she had learned that day.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a game. He went to his friend, Sue, and asked, "Do you want to play a game with me?" Sue said, "Yes, I want to play too!" They started playing a fun game. But then, a new kid named Max saw them play. Max was envious. He wanted to play the game too. Max said, "Can I play the game with you?" Tim and Sue looked at each other and said, "Of course, Max! You can play with us!" While they were playing the game, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew down from the sky. The bird wanted to play the game too! Tim, Sue, and Max were surprised but happy. They all played the game together and had lots of fun.

One day, a fisherman went to the river. He wanted to catch some fish. The sun was bright, and the water was calm. The fisherman felt happy. As he was fishing, he saw a harmless snake. The snake said, "Hi, can I have a fish?" The fisherman said, "Sure, you can have one." The snake was happy and thanked the fisherman. Then, the fisherman tried to step closer to the water. But he did not see a wet rock. He slipped and fell into the river. The harmless snake saw this and quickly swam to help him. The snake saved the fisherman, and they both became good friends.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy had a toy bear. She loved her bear very much. They played all day and slept together at night. One day, Lucy's bear got a little hole in it. Lucy felt sad because her bear had pain. Lucy's mom saw the pain in the bear and wanted to help. She found some glue to fix the hole. She told Lucy, "I will pour the glue on the bear's hole, and it will feel better." Lucy was happy that her mom could help her bear. The glue worked, and the bear's pain was gone. Lucy and her bear played together again. They were both very happy. The bear's hole was fixed, and it was not a persistent problem anymore. Lucy learned that when you have a problem, it's good to ask for help.
