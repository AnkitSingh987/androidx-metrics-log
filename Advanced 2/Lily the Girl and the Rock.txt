Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to organize her toys. One day, she found a big hole in the ground. It was a mine. Lily thought, "I can find nice rocks in the mine." The next day, Lily went to the mine with her friend, Tim. They found many pretty rocks. They put the rocks in a bag to take home. Lily said, "We will organize these rocks later." Tim was excited. He knew something good was going to happen. At home, Lily and Tim organized the rocks by color. They found a shiny rock that was different from the others. Lily's mom said, "This rock is special. It can make us healthy." They were very happy. They put the special rock in a safe place. From that day on, Lily and her family were always healthy and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Cat. Cat was three years old and had never been away from home for more than a few minutes. One day, Cat's mum took Cat out for the day. Cat was excited and scared at the same time. They arrived at the restaurant and looked at the menu. Cat was very frightened when she saw the words. Mum asked Cat what she wants to eat. Cat shook her head and said she didn't know. Mum gave Cat a big hug and said “It’s ok. I will help you choose something nice.” She then wrapped her arms around Cat, and Cat felt a bit better. In the end, Cat chose a delicious pizza and asked for lots of extra cheese. Everyone laughed because she was so excited and Cat smiled too. It was a day Cat would never forget!

Once upon a time, there was a very smart little girl named Clover. She had a happy life living in the countryside with her family. Every morning there was a special activity she would do, it was her favorite time of the day. Clover would go outside and play in the wind. On windy days, the wind was very strong and blew her hair around. On those days, Clover never stopped playing. After a while, the wind would start to tire her out. That's when she would take a break and head inside. Once inside, she would go over to a special box she kept near a window. Inside the box, she kept a thick rope wrapped around a bell. When the wind blew hard, she would tug the rope with all her might, to make the bell ring. The sound of the bell ringing filled the house with joy. Clover's family was so proud of her. Even though the bell was loud, everyone knew it was a sign that she was using her energy smartly. Whenever the thick wind blew, Clover would often ring the bell and everyone in the house would smile. Clover's parents taught her a valuable lesson that day - use your energy wisely. Even though it was fun to play in the wind, Clover also knew when to take a break. This is important to remember in life, as we all need to take care of ourselves in order to stay healthy and happy.

Sam and Lily liked to play with their toy boats in the pond. They each had a red boat and a blue boat. They would race their boats and see who was faster. Sometimes Sam won, and sometimes Lily won. But they always had fun. One day, they met a new boy at the pond. His name was Max. He had a big yellow boat that looked very fast. He asked Sam and Lily if they wanted to race with him. They said yes, and they each picked a boat. Sam picked his red boat, Lily picked her blue boat, and Max picked his yellow boat. They lined up their boats at the edge of the pond and counted to three. Then they pushed their boats into the water and watched them go. Max's boat was very fast. It zoomed ahead of Sam's and Lily's boats. Max laughed and said, "I'm the best! You're too slow!" Sam and Lily felt sad. They didn't like how Max was talking to them. They tried to catch up with their boats, but Max's boat was too fast. He reached the other side of the pond first and shouted, "I win! You lose!" Sam and Lily didn't want to play with Max anymore. They said, "We quit! You're not nice. You're too competitive. We don't like you." They took their boats and walked away from the pond. They decided to play somewhere else, where they could have fun and be kind to each other.
