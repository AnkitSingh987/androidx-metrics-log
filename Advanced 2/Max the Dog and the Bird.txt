One day, a dog named Max was walking in the park. He saw a big jail. Max was scared, but he wanted to see what was inside. He walked closer and started to shiver because it was very cold. Inside the jail, Max found a hurt bird. The bird was sad and could not fly. Max wanted to help the bird. He thought of a plan to get the bird out of the jail. Max pushed the jail door with all his strength. The door opened, and the bird was free! But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to grow bigger and bigger. It was not a real bird, but a big balloon! Max was surprised, but he was happy that the balloon was not a hurt bird. He played with the balloon in the park and had lots of fun.

One day, a girl named Sue found a costume. The costume was very pretty and attractive. Sue was so happy and wanted to wear it to the big party. But, she saw that the costume was broken. Sue felt sad and didn't know what to do. Sue asked her friend, Tim, "Can you help me repair my costume?" Tim said, "Yes, I can help you!" They worked together to fix the costume. They used glue, tape, and a needle with thread. Soon, the costume was fixed and looked nice again. At the party, everyone loved Sue's costume. But then, something unexpected happened. The costume started to glow! It turned out that the costume had magic powers. Sue was surprised and happy. She thanked Tim for helping her repair the costume and they had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful boy named Tim. Tim loved to play catch with his friends. One day, Tim had a great idea. He wanted to make a video of them playing catch. He thought it would be fun to watch later. Tim asked his friend, Sam, "Do you want to make a video of us playing catch?" Sam said, "Yes, that sounds like fun!" So, they went outside with a ball and Tim's video camera. They started to play catch and Tim's mom helped by holding the camera. As they played, Tim and Sam laughed and had a great time. They were very good at catch. When they were done, they went inside to watch the video. They liked seeing themselves on the screen and laughed at the funny faces they made. They were happy they made the video and could watch it anytime they wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was very independent. She liked to do things by herself. One day, Amy had a scary nightmare. She woke up and felt scared. Amy went to her mom and said, "Mommy, I had a bad dream. Can I sleep with you?" Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay, Amy. I have an idea. Let's record your happy thoughts before bedtime. That way, you won't have bad dreams anymore." The next night, Amy and her mom sat down to record her happy thoughts. They talked about her favorite toys, her friends, and fun times at the park. After they finished, Amy went to bed with a big smile on her face. That night, Amy had another dream. But this time, it was not a nightmare. It was a happy dream about her favorite toy, a big fluffy bear. The bear could talk and they played together all night long. When Amy woke up, she was so excited to tell her mom about her happy dream. From that day on, Amy and her mom recorded her happy thoughts every night. Amy never had a nightmare again. She felt brave and strong, knowing she could make her dreams happy all by herself.

Anna and Ben love to bake with Mom. They help her to mix the flour, the eggs, the sugar and the milk in a big bowl. Mom says they are making a cake for Dad's birthday. She tells them to be careful and not make a mess. Anna and Ben are very excited. They want to make the cake look nice and tasty. They find some sprinkles, some nuts, some chocolate and some candles in the cupboard. They ask Mom if they can use them. Mom says yes, but they have to wait until the cake is baked and cooled. Mom puts the cake in the oven and sets the timer. She tells Anna and Ben to wash their hands and clean the table. She gives them each a brush and a cloth. Anna and Ben start to scrub the bowl, the spoons, the cups and the plates. They sing a song while they work. Ding! The timer rings. Mom opens the oven and takes out the cake. It smells good and looks golden. She puts it on a rack to cool. She tells Anna and Ben to be patient and not touch the cake. She says she has a surprise for them. Anna and Ben wonder what the surprise is. They think maybe it is a toy, a book, a balloon or a sticker. They finish cleaning the table and put the brush and the cloth away. They sit on the floor and wait for Mom. Mom comes back with a huge smile. She has a surprise for them. It is not a toy, a book, a balloon or a sticker. It is something better. It is Dad! He is home early from work. He hugs Mom and kisses Anna and Ben. He says he is happy to see them and he thanks them for the cake. Anna and Ben are very happy. They show Dad the cake and the decorations. They help Mom to put the sprinkles, the nuts, the chocolate and the candles on the cake. They sing "Happy Birthday" to Dad and help him to blow out the candles. They cut the cake and share it with Mom and Dad. They say it is the best cake ever. They all laugh and hug. They love to bake with Mom and Dad.
