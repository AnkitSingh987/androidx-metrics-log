Once upon a time, there was a very popular boy. Everyone knew his name and he was always happy. One day, he was playing in the park with his best friend when he fell on the ground. His friend called for help and soon, an ambulance arrived. The people inside the ambulance had to rush to get the boy to the hospital. When the boy and his friend arrived, the doctors worked hard to help him. But sadly, they weren't able to save him. The popular boy had died. Everyone felt very sad and the ambulance drove away. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a small bug named Ben. Ben was an interesting bug because he could glow in the dark. Every night, when the sky was dark, Ben would glow to light the way for his friends. One day, Ben and his friends went into a big cave. The cave was very dark and full of silence. Ben's friends were scared, but Ben knew he could help them. He glowed very bright, and the cave was not dark anymore. Ben's friends were happy, and they all walked through the cave together. As they walked, they saw another bug that was lost and scared. Ben used his glow to help the lost bug find its way out of the cave. When they all got out, the lost bug thanked Ben for his help. Ben learned that being different can be good, and helping others is important.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a dull lizard on the ground. The lizard was not bright and colorful like other lizards. Tim thought it was special. Tim said to the lizard, "Hi, lizard! Do you want to be my friend?" The lizard did not say anything. It just sat there and ignored Tim. Tim felt sad, but he did not give up. He tried again and said, "Please, lizard, I want to be friends." This time, the lizard looked at Tim and nodded its head. They played together all day and became best friends.

One day, a blue car and a red car had a race. They wanted to see who was the fastest. The race was from their home to the library. On their way, they saw a big tree in the road. The blue car and the red car did not know what to do. They were both scared. They did not want to crash into the tree. The blue car and the red car worked together to move the tree. They pushed and pushed until the tree was out of the way. They were happy they could help each other. Then, they finished the race to the library. They both won because they helped each other.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man who had a big house. He had many things, but he was not happy. One day, he found a brush in his garden. He did not know what it was for. A little girl saw the man and said, "That is a magic brush. If you do good things with it, you will be happy." The man wanted to be happy, so he used the brush to help others. He cleaned their houses, and he made them pretty pictures. The people were happy, and the man was happy too. But one day, the man lost the brush. He was very sad and began to weep. The little girl came back and said, "You do not need the brush to be happy. You can still help others and be happy." The man learned that being kind and helping others is what makes you happy, not having many things.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy box. A little boy named Tim wanted to see what was inside the box. He tried to pull the box, but it was too heavy for him. Tim was sad because he really wanted to know what was inside. One day, Tim had an idea. He would ask his friends to help him pull the heavy box. So he asked his friends, Sam and Lily, to help him. They all stood by the wall and tried to pull the box together. They pulled and pulled, and the box started to move! Finally, the box was away from the wall. Tim, Sam, and Lily opened the box, and inside they found a big, bouncy ball! They were so happy and played with the ball all day. They learned that when they work together, they can move even the heaviest things.
