Once upon a time, there was a pretty cat named Lily. Lily loved to watch birds in the big tree near her home. One day, Lily saw a new bird in the tree. The bird was big and pretty too. Lily wanted to be friends with the bird. Lily went to the tree and said, "Hi, bird! I am Lily. Let's be friends." The bird looked at Lily and said, "Hi, Lily! I am Ben. We can be friends, but you must follow one law. You must not catch any birds in this tree." Lily agreed to the law and they became good friends. They played and watched the sky together every day. One day, Ben was gone. Lily looked everywhere but could not find him. Then, she saw a small egg in the tree. The egg cracked and a tiny bird came out. It was Ben! He was not a big bird, but a baby bird all along. Lily was so surprised, but she loved her friend even more. They continued to play and watch the sky together, and Lily always followed the law.

He ate some more berries and played with his friends in the woods. They all had a great time together. When it was time to go home, Bobo hopped back to his cozy little burrow, feeling full and happy.

Then, something unexpected happened. The frog came back to Amy. The frog said, "I am sorry for being rude. Let's be friends." Amy was so happy. She and the frog played near the stream all day. They became the best of friends.

It was an ancient tree. The tree was rot and fall down. The bull and the bird were sad. They said, "We can't play here." But then, they saw a new tree. It was small and green. The bull and the bird were happy. They played near the new tree all day long.
