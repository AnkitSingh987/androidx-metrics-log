Tom and Lily are twins. They live on a farm with their mom and dad. They like to help with the animals and the plants. They also like to play and eat yummy food. One day, mom makes a big cake for them. She says, "This is a special cake for you, Tom and Lily. You can share it after lunch." Tom and Lily are very happy. They love cake. But when lunch is over, they have a problem. They both want the same piece of cake. The piece with the most frosting and sprinkles. They start to fight over it. They pull and tug and shout. Mom hears them and comes to the kitchen. She sees the cake and the twins. She is not happy. She says, "Stop fighting, Tom and Lily. You have to separate the cake and share it. Or you will get no cake at all." Tom and Lily stop fighting. They look at the cake and at mom. They feel sorry. They say, "Sorry, mom. We were greedy. We will separate the cake and share it." They cut the cake into two equal pieces. They give each other a hug and a smile. Mom smiles too. She says, "That's good, Tom and Lily. You are kind and generous. Now you can enjoy your yummy cake." She gives them each a plate and a fork. They eat their cake and say, "Thank you, mom. This is the best cake ever." They are happy.

One day, a little kangaroo went for a walk. The sun was shining and it was a perfect day. The kangaroo liked to hop and play in the grass. He was very happy. On his walk, the kangaroo saw a big tree. He wanted to climb the tree, but he was too small. He tried and tried, but he could not get up. The kangaroo felt sad. Just then, a bird flew by and saw the sad kangaroo. The bird mentioned, "Hey, little kangaroo, don't be sad. You can hop high and play in the grass. That's what makes you special." The kangaroo smiled and felt happy again. He hopped away, enjoying the perfect day.

Once upon a time, there was a generous girl named Ruby. Ruby lived in the west and she loved to talk. Every day, she would have long conversations with her friends and neighbors. One day, Ruby noticed something strange. Her friends and neighbors suddenly became very quiet. Ruby sensed something was wrong and wanted to help, so she decided to talk to them. At first, no one spoke but after a while, they started to tell Ruby their worries. They were scared that something bad was going to happen in the west and they didn't know what to do. Ruby smiled and told them, "If we all stay generous and spread kindness, nothing bad can happen. We can all make the west better by being kind to each other." Ruby's friends and neighbors were glad. They thanked Ruby and began spreading generosity and kindness to everyone they met. From that day forward, the west was a much brighter place. The moral of the story is that generosity and kindness can help make the world a better place.

Once, a squirrel was in a tree. The squirrel was very sad. He was miserable because he had no friends. One day, a small bird saw the squirrel and asked, "Why are you so sad?" The squirrel said, "I have no friends. I want to join and play with others, but they don't like me." The bird felt bad for the squirrel and wanted to help. The bird said, "I will be your friend. We can join and play together." The squirrel was very happy. They played together every day. But one day, while they were playing, a big cat came. The cat saw the squirrel and the bird and wanted to catch them. The cat caught the squirrel and the bird. They were very scared. The cat took them away, and they were never seen again. The moral of the story is to always be careful when playing and watch out for danger.

One day, a popular boy named Tom wanted to play. He saw his friends playing with a ball near his home. Tom liked to throw the ball very far. His friends liked to watch him throw. Tom said, "Watch me throw the ball!" But when he threw the ball this time, it broke a window. Everyone was scared. The owner of the home came out and was very mad. Tom and his friends did not know what to do. Then, Tom said, "I am sorry. I will help fix the window." The owner was surprised. He thought Tom would run away. But Tom did the right thing. The owner was happy and Tom learned to be careful when he played.
