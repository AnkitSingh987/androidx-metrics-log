Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his toy ball. One day, Spot went outside to play. The sun was shining, and Spot felt very calm and happy. Spot saw a button on the ground. He sniffed the button and then sneezed. The sneeze was so big that the button flew high in the air. Spot watched the button go up, up, up. He was very surprised. Then, something unexpected happened. The button turned into a big, colorful bird. The bird landed on Spot's head and started to sing. Spot was happy and wagged his tail. From that day on, Spot and the bird were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big boat in a port. The boat was very dry. One day, a small girl named Lily went to see the boat with her mom. As they walked closer, Lily saw a funny cat on the boat. The cat had a big sneeze. "Achoo!" went the cat. Lily laughed and said, "Mom, the cat sneezed!" Her mom said, "Yes, Lily, the cat sneezed. Let's give it some water to help with the dry air." They gave the cat water, and the cat stopped sneezing. The cat was happy, and Lily was happy too.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved the color pink. She had a pink dress, pink shoes, and a pink room. Lily always had a big smile on her face. One day, Lily's friend, Tim, came over to play. They played with their toys and had a lot of fun. Then, Lily showed Tim her pink room. Tim said, "I don't like pink. I like blue." Lily looked at Tim and said, "I disagree, pink is the best!" Tim and Lily talked about their favorite colors. They both had big smiles on their faces. They realized that even though they like different colors, they can still be friends. From that day on, Lily and Tim played together and always shared their smiles.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny called Bobo. Bobo loved to hop around in the summertime. Every day, he would hop around in the sun, enjoying the beautiful season. But one day, Bobo realized that he was feeling very lonely. He missed having friends to play with. So Bobo decided to ask his dad for help. "Dad, I'm so lonely," said Bobo. "Can you help me find some friends?" "Oh, sure!" said his dad. "Let's go and find some friends to play with!" So Bobo and his dad hopped around the garden, looking for Bobo's friends. Finally, Bobo spotted four little bunnies hopping around in the garden. But as he got closer, Bobo realized that the bunnies were deaf! Bobo was very excited about having found some friends, but he was a little concerned about how he would communicate with them if they couldn't hear him. His dad suggested that Bobo could sign with the deaf bunnies. So they both learnt some signs together and soon Bobo was able to talk with his new friends. From then on, Bobo and the deaf bunnies made sure to get together and have lots of adventures every season. And so, with the help of his dad, Bobo was never lonely again!

Once there was a boy named Tim. Tim was very smart. He liked to learn new things every day. One day, Tim's mom told him he would get a big box. Tim was very excited to see what was inside the box. The next day, Tim got the big box. He opened it and saw a toy robot. The robot was called Will. Will was a very smart robot. Tim and Will became good friends. They played and learned together every day. Tim and Will were very happy. They knew that they were smart and could do many things. Tim's mom was happy too. She knew that Tim would learn a lot with his new friend, Will.

Dave went to school. He was three years old. He saw some cool blocks on the floor. They were green. He wanted to learn how to build something cool. So he asked his teacher, "Can you show me how to make something cool?" The teacher smiled. She said, "Yes! Let's start with the green blocks." She showed Dave how to stack and connect the blocks. He watched carefully until he was able to make something all by himself. He was so proud! He said, "Look, I made a cool castle!" The teacher gave him a high five. She said, "Nice job! You learned something new today!" Dave was so happy. He had learned a cool new skill.
