Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to build. He built with blocks, sticks, and anything he could find. One day, Tim found a pepper. It was red and shiny. Tim had an idea. He wanted to build with the pepper. Tim went to his friend, Sue. He said, "Sue, let's build with this pepper!" Sue was not sure, but she wanted to help. So, they tried to build a tower with the pepper. But, the pepper made them sneeze. They felt miserable. Then, something unexpected happened. The pepper turned into a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for trying to build with me. I was stuck as a pepper, but now I am free!" Tim and Sue were happy. They played with the fairy and forgot about being miserable.

Once upon a time, there was a big chocolate. It was so big that it could fill a whole room. Everyone loved this big chocolate because it was very yummy. But there was one problem. The chocolate was a little bit sour. No one knew why, but it was still very good to eat. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to see the big chocolate. She wanted to eat some, but first, she had to clean her hands. Lucy knew that it was important to be clean before eating. So, she washed her hands with soap and water, and then she was ready to eat the chocolate. Lucy took a small bite of the sour chocolate, and it tasted so good. She shared it with her friends, and they all loved it too. They all ate the chocolate together and had a great time. And after they were done eating, they remembered to clean their hands and faces, just like Lucy had done before.

Once upon a time, there was a young cat named Tim. Tim was very small and loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, he saw a big dog named Max. Max was so big that Tim felt a shock when he saw him. "Hello, Max!" said Tim. "Do you want to play with me?" Max smiled and said, "Yes, Tim! I would love to play with you!" They played catch with the ball. Tim was fast and Max was strong. As they played, Tim started to gain speed. He ran faster and faster, and Max could not catch him. They laughed and had so much fun. At the end of the day, Tim and Max were tired but happy. They had made a new friend. From that day on, they played together every day and became the best of friends.

One day, Sam and his mom went to a big place with many people. They had big luggage with them. They were going on a trip. Sam felt uncomfortable in the big place with so many people. He held his mom's hand tight. Sam saw a long slide in the big place. He said, "Mom, I want to slide on that!" His mom smiled and said, "Ok, Sam. Let's go slide." They put the big luggage near the slide. Sam went up and slid down. He laughed and said, "Again, mom!" They slid many times. Sam felt happy and not uncomfortable anymore. They had fun before going on their trip.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily had a big, red umbrella that she loved very much. One day, it was raining outside, and Lily decided to go for a walk with her umbrella. As she walked, she saw a weak, wet bird sitting under a tree. The bird looked very sad. Lily went up to the bird and said, "Why are you so sad, little bird?" The bird replied, "I am weak and wet, and I have no place to hide from the rain." Lily felt bad for the bird, so she opened her umbrella and let the bird peek under it. The bird was happy and thanked Lily. They both sat under the umbrella until the rain stopped. From that day on, Lily and the bird became good friends. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help others when they need it.
