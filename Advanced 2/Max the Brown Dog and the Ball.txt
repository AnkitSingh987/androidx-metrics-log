Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends in the park. One day, Max met a new friend named Lily. Lily was a small, white cat. Max and Lily played together all day long. Max wanted to show Lily a fun trick. He said, "Lily, I know a special spell. I can make a big ball appear!" Lily was excited and wanted to see the trick. Max tried to remember the spell, but he forgot the words. He felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, Lily. I can't remember the spell." Lily smiled and said, "That's okay, Max. We can still have fun together." They played in the park until the sun went down. As they were getting ready to go home, Max suddenly remembered the spell. He said the magic words, and a big, colorful ball appeared. Max and Lily laughed and played with the ball until it was time to go home. They knew they would always be good friends, with or without magic spells.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful leopard named Leo. Leo loved to race with his friends in the jungle. One day, he met a small rabbit named Remy. "Hi Remy," said Leo. "Do you want to race with me?" Remy looked at Leo and said, "Yes, let's race to that big tree over there!" Leo and Remy started to race. Leo ran very fast, but Remy was slow. Leo looked back and saw that Remy was far behind. He thought, "I should help my friend." So, Leo went back and ran with Remy. They raced to the tree together. Leo and Remy were happy. They learned that it was more fun to race together than to win alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play games with her friends. One day, they decided to play a race game. Lucy wanted to win, but she was a little anxious. Lucy's big sister, Emma, saw her feeling worried. Emma wanted to support Lucy. She said, "Don't worry, Lucy. Just do your best and have fun. I'll be here to cheer for you." Lucy smiled and felt better. The race began, and all the kids ran as fast as they could. Lucy ran with all her might. She didn't win, but she had fun. Emma hugged her and said, "I'm proud of you, Lucy." They all laughed and played more games together.

Tom and Lily were hungry. They wanted to eat something yummy. They saw a bakery with many pastries in the window. They ran to the door, but it was closed. "Oh no!" Tom said. "The bakery is not open. We cannot get any pastries." Lily looked around. She saw a big trash can next to the bakery. She had an idea. "Maybe there are some pastries in the trash can," she said. "Let's look." Tom and Lily lifted the lid of the trash can. They saw some old bread, some banana peels, and some paper. They also saw a pastry. It was a croissant. It was a little dirty, but it looked good. "Look, Tom!" Lily said. "There is a pastry. It is a croissant. We can share it." Tom nodded. He reached for the croissant. He rubbed it on his shirt to clean it. He broke it in half and gave one piece to Lily. "Thank you, Tom," Lily said. She took a bite of the croissant. It was a little stale, but it was sweet and buttery. "You're welcome, Lily," Tom said. He took a bite of his piece. It was a little hard, but it was tasty and flaky. Tom and Lily smiled. They ate the croissant. They were happy. They found a pastry in the trash can. It was the only one available. It was their lucky day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. Her name was Lucy and she was very shy. She loved looking up into the cloudy night sky. She would search for the stars, wondering about the planets far away. One night, Lucy wished she could go to one of the planets. She wished she could go explore one of them, but she knew it was impossible. She felt sad and looked up into the night sky again. Suddenly, Lucy noticed something different. A sparkle, like a star! It was bright and beautiful. Lucy cheered, she found a planet! She kept looking at it, and the star kept getting brighter and brighter until Lucy could make out its shape. Lucy was so excited, but there was one thing she couldn't do. She missed her mom and dad and really wanted to tell them about the planet that she found. She waved goodbye to the star and headed off to bed. The next day when Lucy told her mom and dad about the planet, they were so amazed. She showed them where it was in the night sky, and all three of them looked up at it together. Lucy smiled, happy to be with her family and to have found her special star.
