Once upon a time there were two friends – one was called Bob and the other was called Clara. One day Bob and Clara were playing together in Clara's garden. Bob stirred the soil with a stick to make a little hole, and then he said, "I'm going to put a surprise in this hole! We'll see what happens!" Clara said, "But what kind of surprise should we use?" Bob thought for a second and said, "Why don't we try a seed?" So Clara and Bob planted a seed in the soil and then left it to grow in the warm sunshine. A few days later, Bob and Clara went back to the garden and saw that something had grown! Clara was so excited that she ran over to the plant and gave it a big hug! The plant was a beautiful flower. But then Bob noticed something else. He could see that the soil near the flower was hard and harsh. Bob said to Clara, "Why don't we stir this soil with our hands to make it easier for the flower to grow?" Clara thought this was a good idea, and so she did as Bob said. The soil became much softer and the flower was able to grow much better. Through Bob and Clara's efforts, the soil was softened and the flower was much happier. This story teaches us that being kind is always the best solution
