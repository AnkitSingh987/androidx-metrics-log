Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. He loved to play in the mud and make a big mess. One day, he found a big, shiny mug in the mud. It was the most beautiful mug he had ever seen. Tim wanted to show the mug to his mom, but he knew she would be mad if he brought the messy mug into the house. So, he decided to wash it in the river. As he washed the mug, it started to heal the scratches and dents it had. The mug became even more beautiful and shiny. Tim brought the clean, shiny mug to his mom. She was so happy to see the beautiful mug and how clean Tim was. Tim learned that taking care of things can make them better, just like the river healed the mug. And from that day on, Tim tried his best to be less messy.

Once upon a time there was a big phone. The phone was special because it could mark things. One day a little girl, who was only three years old, got to take the big phone. She was so excited and wanted to mark something with it right away. The little girl went searching around the house for something to mark. She eventually came across the kitchen and decided to mark a piece of paper with a picture on it. She put the piece of paper in the big phone, and pressed down on the screen. A few seconds later the little girl saw her picture, with a funny mark right in the middle. The little girl was so happy with the mark. She went around the house and showed everyone the big phone and the mark she had made. Everyone was amazed and clapped for her. The little girl was so proud of her mark, and the big phone that she had used to make it.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was a very lucky girl. She had a big box full of toys. One day, Sue wanted to play with her toys. She opened the box and saw that her toys were all mixed up. Sue decided to sort her toys into different piles. As Sue sorted her toys, she found a toy she did not remember. It was a small, pretty bird. Sue was confused. She had no memory of this bird. She thought, "This is unexpected! Where did this bird come from?" Sue showed the bird to her mom. Her mom smiled and said, "This bird was my toy when I was a little girl! I put it in your toy box by mistake." Sue was happy to learn about the bird. Now, she had a new toy to play with and a special memory to share with her mom.

Once there was a boy named Bob. He had a dog. The dog was very old and the owner was very kind. One day, Bob took his dog for a walk. It was a very warm day and soon the dog was very hot and dirty. His fur was filthy and his nose was covered in dirt. Bob decided to take the dog to the river to give him a bath. The water was cool and the dog felt much better. Bob finished scrubbing the dog's fur and then the two of them were ready to go home. The dog was much cleaner and smelled nice. Bob was very happy with the work he had completed and the smile on the dog's face was the best reward. From then on, the owner and dog were always very close.

One day, a little boy named Tim went with his mom to a meeting. At the meeting, there were many moms and dads talking. Tim felt bored and thought the meeting was stupid. Tim saw a little girl named Sue sitting close to him. He said to her, "This meeting is stupid. Do you want to play?" Sue nodded her head and they went outside to play. While they were playing, they found a big, shiny rock. They picked it up and it started to talk! The rock said, "Thank you for finding me. I will give you a wish." Tim and Sue looked at each other, surprised. They wished for the meeting to be fun, and suddenly, the meeting turned into a big party! Everyone was happy and dancing. Tim and Sue learned that even stupid things can turn into something fun.
