Bobby was so excited to play outside in the sunshine. He had been dreaming about it all morning. He put on his sneakers and ran out the door. But before he knew it, the day's activities had grown too big. Bobby became so dizzy, his vision started to blur. "I need to take a nap," he thought. Before Bobby knew it, he was curled up in a comfy bed with a blanket over him. As Bobby dozed off, he heard a voice calling him outside. "Come on Bobby, you have to restore your energy!" But Bobby was too tired to answer. Moments later, Bobby awoke feeling restored and refreshed. He ran outside and enjoyed the sunshine. As he played, he laughed as he remembered the voice calling him out of the house to restore his energy. He knew it was his parents, looking out for him the whole time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big desk in his room. He liked to play with his toys on the desk. One day, Tim's mom told him to be obedient and clean his room. Tim did not want to clean his room. He said, "No, I will not surrender to you, Mom!" His mom was sad, but she left him alone. The sky outside turned dark and cloudy, but Tim did not notice. Later, Tim was playing with his toys on the desk. He heard a loud boom from outside. It was a big storm! The storm shook the house, and the desk fell over. Tim's toys broke, and he was very sad. If only he had been obedient and cleaned his room, his toys would be safe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a compassionate girl named Mia. She had a magic wrap that could make things vanish. Mia loved to help her friends and family with her special wrap. One sunny day, Mia's friend, Tim, was very sad. His toy had broken into pieces. Mia wanted to help him. She thought, "I can use my magic wrap to make the broken toy vanish and make Tim happy again." So, Mia took her wrap and made the broken toy vanish. Tim was amazed and happy to see the broken toy gone. Mia felt good that she could help her friend. From that day on, Mia used her magic wrap to solve problems and make her friends and family happy. Everyone loved Mia and her magic wrap.

Tommy loved to dream. Every night, he would close his eyes and see amazing things. He would dream of flying in the sky, or swimming with fish, or playing with dinosaurs. He liked to tell his mom and dad about his dreams in the morning. One night, he had a very impressive dream. He dreamed that he was a lemon. He was yellow and round and sour. He rolled around on a big green hill, and he met other lemons. They were all happy and friendly. They played games and sang songs and told jokes. But then, a big hand came from the sky. It picked up Tommy and squeezed him hard. Tommy felt a lot of pain. He tried to scream, but only juice came out. He woke up with a start. He was in his bed, and his mom was holding him. She had a glass of water and a spoon. "Tommy, are you okay?" she asked. "You were crying in your sleep. Did you have a bad dream?" Tommy nodded. He told her about his dream of being a lemon. He said he was scared and hurt. "I'm sorry, sweetie," she said. "That sounds like a terrible dream. But it was only a dream. It's not real. You're not a lemon. You're my brave and smart boy. And I love you very much." She gave him a hug and a kiss. She gave him some water and some honey. She said honey was good for sore throats. She tucked him in and sang him a lullaby. Tommy felt better. He closed his eyes and hoped to have a nicer dream. He hoped to dream of something sweet, like candy or cake. Or maybe a lemonade.
