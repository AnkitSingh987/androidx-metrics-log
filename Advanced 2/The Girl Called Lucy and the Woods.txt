Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Lucy. She was a very brave girl, but she was worried because she wanted to do something that she had never done before. So one day, Lucy decided to take a walk in the woods. It was a bright day and the trees were all green. In the middle of the woods, Lucy saw something that caught her eye - a beautiful blue house. Lucy was excited, but also a bit scared. Suddenly, Lucy heard a voice from the house. "If you want to come in, you must first remove something," the voice said. Lucy was very worried, but she felt brave, so she decided to find out what she had to remove. She stepped closer and noticed a big blue stone in front of the house. Lucy bravely went up and removed the stone - and the door of the house opened! Inside, Lucy found a little surprise waiting for her! It was a blue toy she had always wanted! At that moment, Lucy felt a little less worried. She realised that there was nothing she couldn't do if she was brave and determined.

Anna loves to sign with her aunt. Her aunt is deaf, so she uses her hands to talk. Anna learns new signs every time she visits her aunt. She likes to sign her name, her favorite colors, and her favorite animals. One night, Anna sleeps over at her aunt's house. She brings her teddy bear and her blanket. Her aunt reads her a story and signs it to her. Then they say good night and turn off the light. It is very dark in the room. Anna feels scared. She hears a noise outside the window. She thinks it is a monster. She wants to call her aunt, but she cannot make any sound. She remembers that her aunt can sign. She reaches for her aunt's hand and signs "help". Her aunt wakes up and sees her sign. She hugs her and signs "don't worry". She turns on the light and shows her that the noise is just a tree branch. She signs "no monster". Anna feels better. She signs "thank you" and "I love you" to her aunt. Her aunt signs the same back to her. They cuddle and fall asleep.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a popular man named Tom. He had a pipe that he loved very much. Every day, he would sit on his chair and use his pipe. All the people in the town knew him and his pipe. One day, Tom lost his pipe. He was very sad and looked everywhere for it. He asked his friends if they had seen his pipe, but no one knew where it was. Tom began to hate not having his pipe with him. Then, something unexpected happened. A little dog found Tom's pipe in the park. The dog picked it up and brought it back to Tom. Tom was so happy to see his pipe again. He thanked the little dog and they became best friends. From that day on, Tom didn't just love his pipe, he also loved his new friend, the little dog.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable tree. It had many leaves and strong branches. Under the tree, a little boy named Tim and his dog, Sam, liked to play. One day, it started to rain. Tim and Sam ran under the tree to stay dry. Tim said, "Sam, we can stay here until the rain stops." Sam barked and wagged his tail. They both liked the sound of the rain on the tree's leaves. After a while, the rain stopped. Tim and Sam came out from under the tree. Tim touched his skin and said, "Look, Sam, we are still dry!" Sam barked happily. They knew they could always count on the dependable tree to keep them safe and dry.

Once upon a time, there was a huge moon in the sky. It was so big and bright that everyone could see it. One night, the moon looked down at the Earth and saw a little girl named Mia. She was getting ready for bed. Mia put on her favorite pajamas. They were soft and warm, and they had pictures of the moon on them. She loved to wear them every night. As she looked out her window, she saw the huge moon and said, "Hello, Moon! I like to wear my moon pajamas just like you!" The moon heard Mia and smiled. It said, "Hello, Mia! I am so happy you like to wear your moon pajamas. They look very nice on you!" Mia smiled and thanked the moon. She felt so special and happy that the moon talked to her. Every night, Mia would wear her moon pajamas and talk to the huge moon. They became the best of friends. And every night, the moon would shine brightly, making sure Mia felt safe and warm as she slept.
