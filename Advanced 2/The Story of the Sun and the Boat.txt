Once upon a time, there was a girl who loved the beach. She was always so happy to go, wearing her favorite pink dress. On this particular day, the sky was blue and the sun was warm. But when the girl got to the beach, her friends seemed to ignore her. She sat on the sand, feeling sad. The girl was about to leave when something made her stop. An old boat had been washed up to the shore. She ran over and peeked in the boat--there were lots of treasures, but the best was a bright pink shell. The girl held up the shell and it sparkled in the sun. She smiled and waved the shell above her head. Suddenly, her friends clapped and cheered. They had been ignoring her to set up her special surprise! The girl and her friends enjoyed the rest of their day on the beach. The pink shell was a treasure that would stay with the girl forever.

Once upon a time, there was a hairy bug. The bug liked to crawl on the ground. One day, the bug found a big shell. The bug was very happy. The hairy bug said, "I want to live in this shell." So, the bug crawled into the shell. Inside the shell, the bug met a little crab. The crab said, "This is my home." The bug and the crab became friends. They played together in the shell. One day, while they were playing, the shell started to move. They were surprised. The shell was not a shell, but a big turtle! The turtle said, "Hello, friends! I am glad you found my back. Let's go on an adventure!" The bug, the crab, and the turtle became best friends. They went on many fun adventures together.

One day, a brown dog named Max found a cube in the park. He picked it up with his mouth and ran around. Max saw his friend, a cat named Lily, and went to her. "Look, Lily! I found a cube," Max said. Lily looked at the cube and said, "It's nice, Max. But be careful, it may open." Max played with the cube all day. He ran, jumped, and even swam with it. But he always kept it close to him. He remembered what Lily said about the cube opening. At the end of the day, Max was very tired. He lay down with the cube close to him. Suddenly, the cube opened! Inside was a big, tasty bone. Max was so happy and ate the bone. He was glad he listened to Lily and kept the cube close.

Once upon a time, in a small garden, there was a little red tomato. The tomato had many friends like the noisy bird, the big tree, and the tiny ant. They all lived happily in the garden. One day, the noisy bird was flying high in the sky. He saw the little tomato and wanted to talk to it. So, he flew down and said, "Hello, little tomato! I watch you every day. You are so red and pretty!" The tomato felt happy and said, "Thank you, noisy bird! I like to watch you fly and sing, too!" Later, the tiny ant came to visit the tomato. They talked and laughed together. The big tree watched them and felt happy that all his friends were having fun. In the end, the little tomato, the noisy bird, the big tree, and the tiny ant all played together in the garden. They were the best of friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a humble cat named Tom. Tom loved to sing and dance. He wanted to be on stage for everyone to see. One day, Tom's mom said, "You can go on stage, but you must be good." Tom was happy! He was allowed to be on stage. He practiced his singing and dancing all day. The day came for Tom to be on stage. All the people came to watch. But when Tom got on stage, he did not sing or dance. He told a funny joke! Everyone laughed and clapped. Tom was happy. He found out he was a great joke teller and not just a singer and dancer.
