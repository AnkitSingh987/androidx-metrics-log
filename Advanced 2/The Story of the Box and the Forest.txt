Once upon a time, there was a big, dull box made of lumber. It lived in a forest with many trees. The box was not happy because it could not play with the other animals. One day, a little boy came to the forest. He saw the big, dull box and wanted to make it happy. So, he thought of a way to make the box feel better. He decided to make the box melt and become something new. The little boy got some sun rays and made them shine on the box. The box started to melt and became a beautiful, shiny puddle. The puddle was very happy and could now play with all the other animals in the forest. They all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a walk. Spot was nervous because he did not want to get dirty. He saw a big puddle of mud in front of him. Spot was scared to walk through the mud. Spot tried to jump over the mud, but he did not jump far enough. He landed right in the middle of the mud! Spot was very embarrass. He was all dirty now. Spot walked home feeling sad. When he got home, his mom saw him and laughed. She said, "Don't worry, Spot. We can clean you up." She gave him a bath and soon, Spot was clean again. Spot was happy and not nervous anymore.

One day, a boy went to the market with his mom. They saw a vendor selling toys. The boy saw a big, red ball. He wanted it very much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the ball, please?" His mom nodded and said, "Yes, you can have it. But remember, it is important to take good care of it." The boy was very happy. He played with his new ball all day. He bounced it high and low. He threw it up in the sky and caught it again. He loved his new ball very much. But one day, the boy was not careful. He threw the ball too far, and it landed in a big lake. The ball floated away, and the boy could not get it back. He was very sad. His mom told him, "I am sorry, but you did not take good care of your important ball. Now it is gone." The boy cried and wished he had been more careful with his special toy.

Once upon a time, there was a playful dog named Toby. He loved to run and play in the big yard near his home. One day, while playing, Toby saw a hedge. It was big and green. Toby thought it was fun to hide behind the hedge and jump out to surprise his friends. One sunny day, Toby was playing by the hedge again. He saw his friend, a little bird, flying by. Toby wanted to surprise the bird, so he hid behind the hedge. When the bird came close, Toby jumped out with a big smile. But instead of laughing, the bird got scared and flew away. Toby was sad. Toby thought about what happened and decided to turn into a kind dog. The next time he saw his friends, he didn't jump out to surprise them. Instead, he greeted them with a wagging tail and a friendly smile. His friends were happy, and they played together all day long. Toby learned that being kind is better than scaring others, and everyone had more fun together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a gloomy bedroom. In this bedroom, there lived a little bird named Tim. Tim was a happy bird, but he did not like how dark his room was. One day, Tim decided to whistle a happy song to make his bedroom feel less gloomy. He whistled and whistled, and soon, the sun came out. The sun's light made the bedroom bright and happy. Now, Tim's bedroom was no longer gloomy. Tim felt so happy that he whistled even more. Every day, Tim would whistle his happy song, and his bedroom stayed bright and full of light. And they all lived happily ever after.
