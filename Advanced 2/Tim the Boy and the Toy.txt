Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a big idea. He wanted to make a useful toy for his little sister, Sue. Tim thought hard and tried to understand what Sue would like to play with. One day, Tim found some sticks and strings. He had an idea! He could make a toy that would move when Sue pulled the strings. Tim worked very hard to make the toy. He wanted it to be useful and fun for Sue. When the toy was done, Tim gave it to Sue. Sue was happy and started to play with the toy. But then, the toy broke! The sticks fell apart and the strings got tangled. Sue was sad and Tim felt bad. His idea was not as useful as he thought. Tim learned that he needed to understand better what makes a good toy before trying again.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a wise gorilla named Gogo. Gogo liked to wonder about many things. One day, Gogo was walking and saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and scared. Gogo asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I am lost. I can't find my way home." Gogo wanted to help Lily. He knew the forest very well. So, Gogo and Lily walked together to find her home. As they walked, they saw a big tree with lots of yummy fruit. Gogo and Lily were hungry, so they ate some fruit. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! It said, "Thank you for eating my fruit! Now I can grow more!" Gogo and Lily were very surprised. The talking tree then showed them the way to Lily's home. They were happy and thanked the tree. Gogo and Lily became best friends and always wondered about the forest together.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing with her friend, Tom. They wanted to make a special picture for their teacher. They had many colors of paint and big white paper. Amy said, "Let's mix the colors to make new ones!" Tom agreed, and they began to mix the paint. They mixed red and blue to make purple. They mixed yellow and blue to make green. They were very slow and careful so they didn't make a mess. After mixing the colors, they painted a big rainbow on the paper. They were so proud of their work. They showed their teacher, who smiled and said, "Thank you, Amy and Tom! I love the pretty colors you mixed for this picture. You did a great job!"

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fragile boy who loved to play in his room. One day, Tim found a big tap in his room. He had never seen it before. Tim was very curious about the tap. Tim wanted to turn the tap, but he was too scared. He thought that if he turned the tap, something bad might happen. Tim's big sister, Sue, saw that he was scared. Sue wanted to help Tim, so she said she would turn the tap for him. Sue turned the tap, and water came out. Tim was still scared, but he saw that nothing bad happened. Tim and Sue played with the water and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim was not scared of the tap anymore. He learned that it was okay to be scared, but sometimes you just have to be brave and try new things.

Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a little mouse. The mouse was small and loved to play all day. One day, the mouse saw a large piece of cheese on the table. The mouse thought, "Wow! That cheese looks so yummy! I want to eat it all." The mouse climbed up the table leg and went to the cheese. Just as the mouse was about to take a big bite, a big cat came into the room. The cat saw the mouse and said, "You should not eat that cheese. It is not yours." The mouse felt regret and knew the cat was right. The mouse said, "I am sorry, big cat. I will not eat the cheese." The cat and the mouse became friends and played together in the large house. They shared the cheese and lived happily ever after.
