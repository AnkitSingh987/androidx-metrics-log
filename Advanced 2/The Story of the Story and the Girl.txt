Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to wander. One day, she found a sweet pastry that looked very yummy and delicious. She was so excited and ready to eat it! She said to her mom, "Mommy, can I have this pastry?" Her mom answered, “Not yet, you have to wait until dinner time!” But the little girl was so excited that she couldn't wait any longer. She wanted to have it right away! So she snuck the pastry to a corner and ate it all by herself. But what happened next was unexpected. The little girl got a stomach ache and was not feeling well. She cried and cried until mommy came to take care of her. Moral of the story: Patience is a virtue. It is always wiser to wait and enjoy something when the time is right.

Anna and Ben like to play with their toy cars. They have many cars of different colors and sizes. They are very organized. They keep their cars in a big box with a lid. One day, they decide to make a car wash for their cars. They fill a bucket with water and soap. They pour some water on a sponge and rub their cars. They make bubbles and splash each other. They laugh and have fun. But they do not see that their cat, Milo, is watching them. Milo is curious and naughty. He wants to play with the water and the cars. He jumps on the box and pushes the lid. The lid falls and the cars escape. They roll out of the box and onto the floor. "Oh no!" Anna and Ben say. "Our cars!" They run to the box and try to catch their cars. But Milo is faster. He chases the cars and bats them with his paws. He makes them go under the sofa and the table. "Stop, Milo!" Anna and Ben say. "You are messing up our car wash!" But Milo does not listen. He thinks it is a game. He meows and purrs. He is having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom loved to sleep all day in the sun. One day, Tom woke up and saw his friend, a little duck named Ducky. Ducky was wet from swimming in the pond. Ducky asked Tom, "Can you lend me a towel to dry off?" Tom, being lazy, did not want to get up. But he knew that friends help friends. So, he got up and found a towel for Ducky. Ducky used the towel to dry off and thanked Tom for his help. Tom felt happy that he helped his friend. From that day on, Tom learned that it is good to help friends, even if you are a lazy cat. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Bella. She was very happy. One day she wanted to wear her favourite blouse. Problem was that it was too small for her! Bella started to cry. Her mum hugged her and said, "Don't cry, sweetie. Let's look for something else to wear." Bella nodded, wiped her tears and was alert. They soon found a new blouse that fit Bella nicely. She was very happy now and danced around in joy. Mum said, "It's important to remain alert and see the situation in a positive light." The moral of the story is to always stay positive and look for solutions!

One day, a little girl found a violin. She wanted to play it, but she did not know how. So, she decided to try. She picked up the violin and put it under her chin. She tried to make a nice sound, but it only made a loud noise. The girl was sad, but she did not give up. The girl tried again and again. She wanted to fill the empty room with beautiful music. But each time, the noise was not nice. Her big brother heard the noise and came to see what was happening. He saw his sister with the violin and laughed. "You can't play the violin," he said. The girl felt bad, but she did not want to stop trying. She practiced every day, even when her brother laughed at her. She kept trying, and one day, the noise started to sound like music. The empty room was now filled with the sweet sound of the violin. The girl was happy, and her brother was surprised. She showed him that if you try and never give up, you can do anything.
