One day, a little cat named Kitty was playing outside. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Kitty went up the tree and saw a light bird. The bird said, "Hi Kitty, what are you doing here?" Kitty replied, "I am climbing the tree to see the world." Kitty and the bird became friends. They talked and played all day. But as the sun went down, Kitty saw that it was getting dark. She wanted to go home but didn't know how to get down from the tree. Kitty was scared. The light bird saw that Kitty was scared and wanted to help her escape the tree. The bird said, "Don't worry, Kitty. I will show you the way down." The bird flew down the tree and Kitty followed. Soon, they reached the ground. Kitty was happy to be safe and thanked the bird. They said goodbye and Kitty went home to her family.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird lived in a big tree near a well. Every day, the bird would fly around and play with its friends. One day, the little bird saw a sad boy sitting by the well. The boy looked at the bird and said, "I lost my toy. Can you help me find it?" The bird wanted to help, so they looked around together. After a while, the bird found the toy near the tree. The boy was so happy! He said, "Thank you, little bird!" Then, he gave the bird a big hug. From that day on, the boy and the yellow bird were best friends. They played together every day by the well.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy little penguin who wanted to find out a secret. He thought if he could kneel down and look close enough, he would find a patch that had the secret hidden inside it. He knelt down, on the cold white ice, and looked around for the patch. After searching for a long time, he finally spotted the patch. It was in the shape of a heart, and he was excited to find out the secret inside it. He looked around to make sure no one was watching, then he carefully opened the patch. Inside he found something magical - it was a big piece of shiny ice. He couldn't believe his eyes. He quickly put the patch back and ran home to tell all his friends about his special find. From that day on, he never forgot about the magical patch and he was even more nosy when it came to looking around for secrets.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch cartoons on his TV. His favorite cartoon was about a funny dog who could talk and do tricks. One day, Tim was watching his cartoon when he noticed something new. A new friend was in the cartoon! This friend was a cute cat who could sing and dance. Tim was very happy to see the new friend. Tim told his mom about the new friend in the cartoon. His mom was happy that Tim noticed the new friend. They watched the cartoon together and laughed at the funny dog and the cute cat. The little boy and his mom had a great time watching the cartoon. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a high hill. On the hill, there was a big pit. The pit was very deep. Many animals lived near the pit. They liked to play on the high hill. One day, a little boy came to the high hill. He saw the pit and wanted to store his toys there. He put his ball, his car, and his bear in the pit. The animals watched the boy store his toys. The next day, the boy came back to play with his toys. The animals joined him. They had lots of fun playing with the toys from the pit. They played all day on the high hill. The boy and the animals became good friends. They played together every day, and they were very happy.
