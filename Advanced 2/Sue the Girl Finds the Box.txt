Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her toys. One day, she saw a box near her bed. Sue was very happy. She opened the box and found a fragile flute inside. "Wow, a flute!" Sue said. Sue wanted to play with the flute, but she knew it was fragile. She was careful with it. Sue's friend, Tim, came over to play. Tim saw the flute and said, "What a nice flute! Can I play with it?" Sue thought for a moment. She knew the flute was fragile, so she said, "Yes, but we must be careful. The flute is fragile." Tim agreed, and they played with the flute together. They played with the flute all day. Sue and Tim were very careful with it. They took turns playing it and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they put the fragile flute back in the box. Sue was happy she could share her new toy with her friend.

Once upon a time there was a three year old girl named Lulu. She was playing in the park near her house when she found a temple. It was very different than any other temple she had ever seen. It was tall and shiny and much bigger than the other temples she had seen before. Lulu couldn't resist the urge to explore it, so she held the door open and stepped inside. It was dark and mysterious inside. She wondered what kind of secrets lay in wait. Suddenly she heard a voice. "What are you doing here?" a man asked. Startled, Lulu looked up to see who was talking to her. It was a big scary man. She tried to answer him but she couldn't find her voice. The man became angry and he grabbed her arm and said, "You must leave now or else you'll get in big trouble!" Lulu started to cry and she tried to break free from his grip. But the man was too strong and she couldn't hold herself any longer. He dragged her away from the temple and she never saw it again.

Once upon a time, there was a fake gorilla that loved to dance. Everywhere it went, it would move it's arms and legs around. The gorilla would move so fast that it almost looked like it was flying. One day, the gorilla heard some music playing and suddenly started to dance faster than ever before. It twirled and whirled around the room, jumping and swaying its arms. The gorilla's dancing was so good that it made everyone in the room laugh and smile. They clapped their hands and stomped their feet as the gorilla kept on grooving. When the music stopped, the gorilla happily smiled and bowed. Everyone applauded and cheered. The fake gorilla had danced its heart out.

Once upon a time, there was a mixer named Mixy. Mixy loved to mix things together to make healthy drinks for his friends. One day, Mixy found a new fruit to mix. He was so excited to show his friends. Mixy went to his friend, Spray the elephant. Spray was sad because he was hot and tired. Mixy had an idea. He mixed the new fruit with water to make a cold, healthy drink. He knew it would help Spray feel better. Mixy gave the drink to Spray. Spray drank it and felt much better. Then, Spray used his trunk to spray water on Mixy to help him cool down too. Mixy and Spray were both happy and learned that helping each other is important. And that's how Mixy and Spray became best friends.

One day, a little dog named Max was very sleepy. He wanted to take a nap, but his friend, a big cat named Tom, wanted to play. Tom said, "Max, I want to teach you a new game!" Max was not happy, but he didn't want to make Tom sad. Max tried to play the game with Tom. The game was to jump on one leg. Max was sleepy, but he tried his best to jump. He fell down many times, but Tom helped him get up. Tom said, "You can do it, Max!" Max was tired, but he didn't want to give up. After a while, Max got better at jumping on one leg. He was still sleepy, but he was happy to learn a new game. Tom was happy too. They played the game for a long time. When they were done, Max was very tired. He went to his bed and took a long nap. Tom was proud of his friend and took a nap too. They both had a good day.
