Once upon a time, there was a cute puppy. He liked to play outside with his friends. One day, he found a long, thin thing on the ground. It was a tie. The cute puppy picked up the tie with his mouth. He wanted to show his friends. He ran around the yard, wagging his tail. His friends saw the tie and thought it was fun. Together, the cute puppy and his friends took turns using the tie. They played tug-of-war and tied it around a tree. They had a great day playing with the tie. And when it was time to go home, the cute puppy carried the tie with him, ready for more fun the next day.

John went to the mechanic's shop because his car needed repairs. When the mechanic saw John's car he shook his head. "Your car looks very poor," the mechanic said. He took the car and weighed it. "It's very heavy," the mechanic said. "It will cost a lot to fix it." John sighed. He wished he could make it lighter. He thought and thought until he had an idea. He took off all the heavy things from the car. Then he went back to the mechanic and asked him to weigh it. The mechanic was amazed. He said, "It's much lighter now! The repairs will cost much less." John was very happy. He thanked the mechanic for his help. Now he could get his car fixed and it wouldn't cost too much.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big mustache. He was very proud of it. Every day, he would look in the mirror and say, "I love my mustache!" One day, the man wanted his mustache to grow more. He wished for it to increase. So, he asked his friend, "How can I make my mustache bigger?" His friend told him to put special cream on it. The man was very happy and said, "Thank you!" But, the cream did something unexpected. Instead of making his mustache bigger, it made him smaller! Now, the man was tiny, but his mustache still looked big. He was still very proud of his mustache. And, all his friends thought it was funny and laughed with him.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to help his mom cook. One day, they were making a big cake. Tim's mom said, "We need to add a teaspoon of sugar." Tim was so excited to help. He picked up the teaspoon and put the sugar in the bowl. As they mixed the cake, Tim's mom said, "We need to increase the heat in the oven." Tim turned the heat up, but he turned it up too much! The oven started to make scary noises. Tim and his mom were a little scared, but they knew they had to be brave. Then, something unexpected happened. The cake in the oven started to grow bigger and bigger! It grew so big that it pushed the oven door open. Tim and his mom were surprised but happy. They laughed and shared the giant cake with all their friends. And from that day on, Tim knew that sometimes, even scary things can lead to something fun and yummy.

Once upon a time, there was a kind girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom, dad, and little brother, Tim. Lily loved to play with Tim and help her family. They were all very happy. One day, Lily and Tim found a door in their house that led to an attic. They went up to the attic and found many old toys. They started to play with the toys and had a lot of fun. While playing, Lily found a snowman toy that could talk. The snowman said, "Please take me outside or I will melt." Lily and Tim took the snowman toy outside to keep it from melting. They played with the snowman and their other toys in the yard. The snowman was happy that he didn't melt because Lily and Tim were very kind to him. In the end, Lily, Tim, and the snowman became good friends. They played together every day and had a lot of fun. Their family was happy to have the snowman as a new friend, too. And they all lived happily ever after.
