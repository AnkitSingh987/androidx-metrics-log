One day, a boy named Tim had a slow computer. He wanted to play a game, but the computer would not let him. The computer was too slow. Tim was sad. Tim's mom saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim said, "My computer is slow, and I cannot play my game." His mom told him, "Stay here, and I will help you." Tim's mom went away and came back with a big box. Tim was very surprised. Inside the box was a new, fast computer! Tim was so happy. Now he could play his game and have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little alligator named Ally. Ally was very persistent. She liked to play with her friends in the pond. One sunny day, Ally saw a new friend, a duck named Ducky. Ally swam up to Ducky and said, "Hi! Do you want to play with me?" Ducky looked at Ally and felt scared. Ducky had never seen an alligator before. Ducky decided to ignore Ally and swam away. Ally was sad and didn't understand why Ducky ignored her. Ally's mom saw what happened and told her, "Don't worry, Ally. Maybe Ducky just needs some time to get to know you." The next day, Ally saw Ducky again. Ally remembered what her mom said and tried to be nice. She swam up to Ducky and said, "Hi, Ducky! I brought you some yummy food to share." Ducky saw the food and thought it looked good. Ducky decided to give Ally a chance. Ally and Ducky ate the food together and had lots of fun. They played in the pond all day long. From that day on, Ally and Ducky were best friends. They learned that being persistent and kind can make new friends, even if it takes time.

Once upon a time, there was a powerful dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball all day. He would run, jump, and roll around with his ball. Max was very happy. One sunny day, Max had a goal. He wanted to bury his ball in the garden. He dug a big hole with his paws. Max put the ball in the hole and covered it with dirt. He was very proud of his work. Later, Max wanted to play with his ball again. He went to the garden and dug up the ball. Max played and played until he was tired. At the end of the day, Max knew he had reached his goal. He was a powerful and happy dog.

Lily and Ben were playing in the sand. They had a spade, a bucket and some shells. They wanted to make a castle. "Let's pretend we are the king and queen of the castle," said Lily. "You can dig the moat and I can decorate the walls." "OK," said Ben. He took the spade and started to dig a hole around the bucket. He threw the sand out of the hole. Lily took the shells and stuck them on the bucket. She made a door and a window. They were having fun until a big wave came and washed away their castle. The bucket and the spade were gone. The shells were scattered. The hole was filled with water. "Oh no!" said Lily. "Our castle is gone!" "Don't be sad," said Ben. "We can make another one. Or we can pretend we are pirates and look for treasure." "OK," said Lily. She smiled and took Ben's hand. They ran along the shore and looked for shiny things. They found a coin, a bottle cap and a feather. They put them in an empty can. "Look, we have treasure!" said Ben. "We are the best pirates ever!" They hugged and laughed. They forgot about the castle. They were happy with their treasure.

One day, a colorful frog named Bob saw a big button on the ground. The button was shiny and round. Bob was very curious about it. Bob went to his friend, Sam the squirrel. "Sam, I found a big button. What do we do with it?" asked Bob. Sam thought for a moment and said, "Let's push it and see what happens." Bob and Sam went back to the button. They both jumped on it with all their might. Suddenly, they began to spring up and down, higher and higher. They laughed and played, enjoying the fun surprise the button brought them. As the sun began to set, Bob and Sam said goodbye to the button and went to their homes. They would always remember the day they found the magical button that made them spring high into the sky.
