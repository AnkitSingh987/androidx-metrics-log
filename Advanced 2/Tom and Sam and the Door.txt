Tom and Sam were brothers who liked to play in the woods. They had a secret fort made of lumber that they found near the old mill. They had a rule: never go near the mill, because it was dangerous and scary. One day, Tom dared Sam to go inside the mill and bring back something cool. Sam was afraid, but he did not want to look like a baby. He said, "OK, I will go, but you have to wait for me here." He walked to the mill and opened the door. Inside, it was dark and dusty. Sam saw a lot of old machines and tools. He looked around for something cool, but he only found junk. He was about to leave, when he heard a loud noise. It was a big dog, barking and growling. It had escaped from its cage and saw Sam. It ran towards him, showing its teeth. Sam was very scared. He screamed, "Help! Help!" He ran to the door, but it was locked. He had trapped himself inside the mill with the dog. He looked for another way out, but he could not find one. He wished he had never come here. Tom heard Sam's scream. He ran to the mill and tried to open the door, but it was locked. He saw the dog through a window, chasing Sam. He felt ashamed. He had made Sam go inside the mill, and now he was in trouble. He had to help him. He looked for something to break the door. He saw a big piece of lumber. He picked it up and smashed the door with it. It broke open. Sam saw Tom. He was so happy. He ran to him and hugged him. They both ran out of the mill, away from the dog. They were safe. They went back to their fort. They were both shaking and crying. They said they were sorry to each other. They promised to never go near the mill again. They hugged again. They were glad they had each other.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to drink milk and play on the furniture. One day, Fluffy was drinking milk near the sofa. Suddenly, Fluffy heard a noise. It came from under the furniture. Fluffy was scared, but she wanted to see what it was. She looked under the sofa and saw a little mouse. The little mouse was scared too. But then, something unexpected happened. The mouse said, "I don't want to eat your food, I just want to be your friend." Fluffy was surprised but happy. They became best friends and played on the furniture together.

One day, a playful cat named Kitty found a notebook under a big tree. Kitty liked to play with the notebook. She would push it with her paw and watch it slide on the ground. Kitty had so much fun with the notebook. One day, a little boy named Timmy saw Kitty playing with the notebook. Timmy wanted to play too. He asked Kitty, "Can I play with you and the notebook?" Kitty meowed happily and they played together. They took turns pushing the notebook and laughing. But then, the notebook got wet in a puddle. The pages were all wet and Kitty could not play with it anymore. Kitty started to cry. Timmy wanted to help his friend. He said, "Don't cry, Kitty. I will find you a new notebook to play with." Kitty stopped crying and smiled at Timmy. They went to find a new notebook together and continued to have fun.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Tom. Tom had a big waist and he liked to eat a lot. He lived in a small house with a nice lady. The nice lady always gave him food, and Tom loved to eat. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was very fast and he liked to run around the house. Tom wanted to catch Jerry, but he was too fat. Tom said to Jerry, "I will catch you, little mouse!" But Jerry just laughed and said, "You can't catch me, fat cat. You don't understand how fast I am!" Tom tried to catch Jerry, but he was too slow. He ran and ran, but Jerry was always faster. Tom got very tired and sad. He wanted to catch Jerry, but he couldn't. The nice lady saw Tom was sad and said, "You need to eat less and exercise more, Tom. Then you might catch Jerry." But Tom didn't listen. He just kept eating and eating. In the end, Tom never caught Jerry. He got even fatter and couldn't move at all. The nice lady tried to help him, but Tom didn't understand that he needed to change. And so, Tom stayed fat and sad, and Jerry kept running around the house, always too fast for Tom to catch.
