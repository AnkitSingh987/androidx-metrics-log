Once upon a time, there was a happy family. They smiled a lot and were very grateful for each other. Every Sunday they were all together and they felt united. But one day, something bad happened. Dad became very sad and he stopped eating. He became very quiet, and the family was very worried. One Sunday, Dad said he had to go away. He said he felt bad inside and needed some time away. Everyone said goodbye and watched until Dad was out of sight. The family was very worried and missed Dad very much. But each Sunday, they still felt united, even though Dad wasn't there. But sadly, Dad never returned. Though the family was very sad, they were still grateful for the time they had spent together. They still loved each other and were happy to be united, even though Dad was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim had a crib in his room. The crib was his favorite place to sleep. One day, the sky became dark outside. Tim was not scared, he liked the dark. In the dark, Tim saw a little star. The star said, "Hello, Tim! I like to dance when it is dark. Do you want to see?" Tim was happy and said, "Yes, please!" The little star danced for Tim. It was a fun dance to watch. When the star finished dancing, Tim wanted to say "good job." So, he clapped his hands. This is called applaud. Tim said, "I like your dance, little star!" The little star was happy and thanked Tim. Then, the star went back into the sky, and Tim went back to sleep in his crib.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty red scarf that she loved. One day, she went to the park with her mom. Lily saw a big tree and wanted to play near it. Her mom said, "Okay, but don't lose your scarf!" Lily nodded her head. Lily played near the big tree, but she forgot about her scarf. The wind blew and took her scarf away. Lily was sad and started to cry. Her mom said, "Don't worry, we will find it." They looked all around the park, but they could not find the scarf. Just then, a nice squirrel came by with something in its mouth. It was Lily's red scarf! The squirrel had found it near a tasty nut. Lily was so happy and thanked the squirrel. She promised to be more careful next time. And from that day on, Lily and the squirrel became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate zebra named Zara. She loved to climb hills and play with her friends. One day, Zara and her friends went to climb the big hill near their home. As they climbed, they saw a little cat stuck in a tree. The cat was scared and couldn't come down. Zara wanted to help the cat, so she climbed the tree. Her friends watched her and cheered. When Zara reached the cat, she saw that it was not a cat at all! It was a tiny baby zebra, just like her! She helped the baby zebra down from the tree. They all became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a proud little bird named Blue. Blue loved to travel and see new places. One day, Blue found a shiny key on the ground. Blue was very happy and wanted to show it to his friend, Red the squirrel. Blue flew to Red's tree and said, "Look, Red! I found a key!" Red looked at the key and said, "Wow, Blue! That's a nice key! What will you do with it?" Blue thought for a moment and said, "I will travel and find the door it opens!" So, Blue and Red went on a fun trip to find the door. They saw many pretty flowers and tall trees. They played together and laughed a lot. After a long day, they found a little door in a tree trunk. Blue used the key and opened the door. Inside the tree, they found a room full of yummy treats and colorful toys. Blue and Red were so happy and played together all day. They knew they would always remember their special adventure with the key.
