Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. Today, she was feeling very curious, so she asked her mom to explain what corn was. Her mom smiled and said, “Corn is a type of food that comes in yellow, white, and orange colors. It's very crunchy and sweet!” The little girl was so excited! She wanted to learn even more about corn, so she asked, “Mommy can we go to the farm and see it growing?”. Her mom nodded. “Yes, we can go to the farm. It is usually very busy there. You'll get to see the corn growing in the fields.” The little girl was happy and she couldn't wait to see everything! She was so excited to see the beautiful fields of corn and to learn more about this delicious food.

One day, a cat and a dog were playing near a big tree. The cat was calm and liked to sit in the sun. The dog liked to run and jump. They were good friends. The dog found a long rod on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and said, "Look, cat! I found a rod. Let's play with it!" The cat looked at the rod and said, "Okay, let's play!" But when the dog tried to play with the rod, he sneezed. The sneeze was so big that the rod flew into the air! It landed on a high branch of the tree. The cat and the dog looked up and saw a big bird sitting on the rod. The bird said, "Thank you for the new toy!" The cat and the dog were surprised but happy to make a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a clever little rabbit named Bobby. Bobby loved to hop and play all day long. One day, Bobby saw a big stair in the woods. He wanted to climb the stair to see what was on top. Bobby met a friend, a squirrel named Sally. Sally said, "Bobby, don't go up the stair! It might delay our playtime." Bobby thought about it and said, "You're right, Sally. Let's play first, then we can climb the stair together." Bobby and Sally played for a long time. They had so much fun! Then, they went to the stair and climbed it together. At the top, they found a beautiful view of the woods. They were happy they waited and climbed the stair together. The moral of the story is to listen to your friends and be patient. Good things come to those who wait.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant named Andy. Andy was a stubborn ant. He wanted to move a big leaf all by himself. The leaf was many inches long. Andy tried very hard, but the leaf would not move. Andy's friend, Bee, saw him trying to move the leaf. Bee said, "Andy, let me help you move the leaf." But stubborn Andy said, "No, I can do it by myself." Bee watched as Andy tried again, but the leaf still did not move. Finally, Andy was tired and said, "Okay, Bee, please help me move the leaf." Together, they pushed and the leaf moved! Andy thanked Bee for helping him. They both learned that sometimes it is better to ask for help.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a green alligator. He liked to swim and play with his friends, the fish and the frogs. One day, the alligator found something new in the pond. He wanted to discover what it was, so he swam closer to take a look. As the alligator got near, he saw that it was a big, shiny thing. He thought it was a toy, so he tried to play with it. But the shiny thing was not a toy, and it made a loud noise that scared all his friends away. The alligator felt very stupid for playing with something he did not know. Later, the alligator's friends came back, and they all talked about the shiny thing. They learned that it was not safe to play with things they did not know. From that day on, the alligator and his friends were always careful and never played with unknown things again. And they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is to be careful and not to play with things you do not know.
