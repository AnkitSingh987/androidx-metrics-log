One day, a boy named Tim found a dirty balloon. He wanted to make it clean, so he filled a big bowl with water. He put the balloon in the water to soak. Tim was very happy to see the balloon getting clean. After some time, Tim took the balloon out of the water. He wanted to play with it. He tried to blow air into the balloon, but it was not easy. He blew and blew, but the balloon did not get big. Tim felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The dirty balloon changed color! It became a bright, shiny red balloon. Tim was so surprised and happy. He tried to blow air into the balloon again, and this time it got big! Tim played with his new red balloon all day long.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a new store. In the store, there was a big, shiny crystal. A little girl named Lily saw the crystal and thought it was very pretty. She had a question for her mom about the crystal. Lily asked her mom, "Can I have the crystal, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can have the crystal." Lily was very happy. She took the crystal home and put it on her table. The crystal made her room look very nice. Every day, Lily looked at her crystal and smiled. Sometimes, she had more questions about the crystal, like where it came from and why it was so shiny. Her mom would try to answer her questions. Lily loved her crystal, and it made her happy every day.

Tom and Lily were playing in the park with their mom. They liked to slide, swing and run on the grass. They saw a man sitting on a bench with a big bag. He looked sad and lonely. "Mom, can we say hello to him?" Lily asked. "Sure, but be polite and careful," Mom said. Tom and Lily walked to the man and smiled. "Hi, we are Tom and Lily. What is your name?" Tom said. "Hello, I am Sam. You are very cheerful," Sam said. "Why are you sad?" Lily asked. "I lost my wallet. It had my money and my cards and my photos. I don't know where it is," Sam said. "That is bad. We can help you look for it," Tom said. "Really? That is very kind of you," Sam said. They looked around the bench, the trash can, the fountain and the trees. They did not find the wallet. "Maybe someone took it," Lily said. "Who would do that?" Tom asked. "Someone who is not nice and not honest," Sam said. They saw a woman walking fast with a big bag. She looked nervous and scared. She dropped something from her bag. It was the wallet! "Look, there is your wallet!" Lily shouted. "Hey, stop! That is mine!" Sam yelled. The woman ran away, but Tom and Lily chased her. They caught up with her and grabbed her bag. "Give it back! Give it back!" Tom and Lily said. "Let go! Let go!" the woman said. Mom and Sam came and helped them. They removed the wallet from the woman's bag and gave it to Sam. "Thank you, thank you, thank you! You are amazing!" Sam said. He opened his wallet and checked his money, his cards and his photos. They were all there. "Here, take this. It is a reward for your help," Sam said. He gave Tom and Lily some money and a candy. "Wow, thank you! You are very generous," Tom and Lily said. They hugged Sam and said goodbye. They went back to their mom and showed her their money and candy. "You did a great job, kids. I am very proud of you. You were brave and helpful and honest," Mom said. They smiled and ate their candy. They felt happy and proud. They had a great day in the park.

Lily and Ben were twins who liked to play near the pond. They knew there were alligators in the pond, but they were not afraid. They thought the alligators were sleepy and slow. One day, they saw a big alligator sunning on the shore. They wanted to touch it and see what it felt like. They crept closer and closer, being very quiet. They did not want to wake the alligator up. "Look, Lily, it has scales and teeth!" Ben whispered. "Let me see, let me see!" Lily whispered back. She reached out her hand to touch the alligator's tail. But the alligator was not sleepy or slow. It was waiting for a snack. It snapped its jaws and swung its tail. Lily and Ben screamed and ran away. Lily's hand slipped on the wet grass and she fell down. The alligator chased after her. "Help, help!" Lily cried. Ben heard his sister and turned around. He saw the alligator getting closer to Lily. He was scared, but he loved his sister. He picked up a stick and threw it at the alligator's head. The alligator was surprised and stopped. Ben ran to Lily and helped her up. They ran back to their house as fast as they could. They told their mom what happened. She hugged them and said, "I'm glad you're safe, but you were very naughty. You should never go near the pond or the alligators. They are dangerous and can hurt you. You should listen to me and stay away from them." Lily and Ben felt sorry and said, "We're sorry, mom. We won't do it again. We learned our lesson." The moral of the story is: Listen to your parents and don't play with dangerous animals.
