One day, a little bear went on an adventure. He wanted to find something tasty to eat. He walked and walked until he spotted something behind a big rock. It was an apple tree with a big basket of apples. The little bear was so excited, they looked so tasty that he couldn't wait to try one. He picked one up and took a big bite. Delicious! The little bear wanted to eat them all, but he wanted to make sure no one else could get as lucky as he was. So he counted them out loud. "One, two, three, four…all the way to ten!" The little bear had found all the apples – ten in total. He was so happy with the tasty treats he'd found! With a smile on his face, the little bear went on his way, carrying the basket of apples with him.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Jazz. Jazz loved to play and turn around in the sun. One day, Jazz went out to play in the big yard. While Jazz was playing, he heard a sound. It was a small bird high up in a tree. The bird sang a pretty song. Jazz liked the song and wanted to be friends with the bird. So, Jazz tried to turn into a bird too. Jazz closed his eyes and wished very hard. When he opened his eyes, he was not a bird. But, he saw his shadow on the ground. It looked like a bird! Jazz was happy and sang with the bird in the tree. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a queen. The queen was very kind and had an adorable pet cat. The cat was small and liked to play with the queen. They were very happy together. One day, the cat went out to play in the garden. It saw a butterfly and ran after it. The cat ran and ran but could not catch the butterfly. It was getting late, and the cat was far from the castle. The cat was lost and did not know how to return home. It was cold and dark outside. The queen was very sad and cried because her adorable cat was not with her. The queen looked for her cat every day, but she could not find it. The cat never returned to the castle. The queen was sad and lonely without her adorable cat. And they never saw each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Tom. Tom loved to walk and play all day. One day, he saw a big stick and wanted to try something new. He wanted to balance on the stick. Tom started to walk on the stick very carefully. He was doing great and felt very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird landed on the stick, making it shake. Tom wobbled and almost fell, but he held on tight. The little bird said sorry to Tom, and they both laughed. Tom and the bird became friends. They played together every day, and Tom taught the bird how to balance too. They had lots of fun and were always jolly. And that's how Tom learned that new friends can come from unexpected places.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot loved to help people in his town. One day, Spot saw a cat stuck in a tree. He ran to the tree and helped the cat get down. The cat was very happy. One day, a boy named Tim lost his ball. He started to complain. Spot heard Tim and wanted to help. Spot looked everywhere for the ball. He looked under cars, behind trees, and in bushes. Then, Spot saw the ball on a roof! Spot climbed up and got the ball for Tim. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! A police officer saw Spot on the roof. The police officer thought Spot was in trouble. He ran to help Spot get down. When Spot was safe, the police officer realized that Spot was a helpful dog. The police officer gave Spot a big hug. From that day on, Spot and the police officer were friends. The moral of the story is that being helpful can make new friends.
