Once upon a time, there was a little otter. The otter liked to play in the water. He was very careful when he played. One day, he found a big thing to wrap around him. It was soft and warm. The otter showed his friends the big thing. They all wanted to wrap it around them too. They took turns wrapping it around their bodies. They were all very happy and warm. But then, a big wind came. It took the big thing away from the otter and his friends. They were all very sad. They tried to find the big thing, but it was gone forever. The otter and his friends were cold and missed the big thing.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy car. It liked to zoom up and down the road. All the people in the town would hear the car go zoom, zoom, zoom. The car was very fast and loved to show off. One day, the noisy car saw a hedge. The hedge was big and green. The car thought it would be fun to zoom through the hedge. But the car did not know that the hedge was home to many little animals. As the noisy car zoomed through the hedge, the little animals got scared. They ran away from their home. The people in the town saw this and felt sad for the little animals. They asked the noisy car to stop zooming through the hedge. The noisy car learned that it was not nice to scare the little animals. It stopped zooming through the hedge and found a new place to play. The little animals were happy and went back to their home. The people in the town were happy too. The moral of the story is to think about how our actions can affect others and to be kind to everyone around us.

Sam and Pam were twins who lived near the sea. They liked to play on the sand and swim in the water. One day, their mom told them there was a big storm coming. She said it was called a hurricane and it could make the wind blow hard and the water rise high. She said they had to pack their things and go to a safe place. Sam and Pam did not want to leave. They said they were not afraid of the storm. They said they wanted to stay and watch the waves. Their mom was angry and sad. She said they had to listen to her and do what she said. She said the storm could hurt them and their home. She said they had to be smart and careful. But Sam and Pam did not listen. They waited until their mom was busy and ran away. They went to the beach and hid behind a rock. They laughed and said they were brave and clever. They said they would see the hurricane and have fun. They did not see their mom looking for them and crying. The hurricane came and it was very bad. The wind howled and the water crashed. The rock did not protect Sam and Pam. The water swept them away and they were gone. Their mom never saw them again. She was very sad and lonely. She wished they had listened to her and stayed with her. The story has a bad ending. Sam and Pam did not survive the hurricane. The story has a moral value. Sam and Pam should have obeyed their mom and respected her wisdom. They should have valued their safety and their family more than their curiosity and fun. They were poor in judgment and character.

One day, a big dog named Max was very hungry. He went to his friend, a bossy cat named Lucy. Lucy had a big, yummy steak. Max asked Lucy, "Can I have some steak, please?" Lucy said, "No, you have to play a game first. You must throw a ball and catch it three times." Max was sad, but he wanted the steak. So, he started to play the game. He threw the ball once, twice, and then three times. He caught it every time. Suddenly, a bird flew down and took the steak away! Max and Lucy were very surprised. They looked at each other and said, "Let's work together to get the steak back." They became friends and learned that sharing and teamwork were more important than being bossy.
