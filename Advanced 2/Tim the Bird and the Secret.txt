Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was a troubled bird because he could not sing well. All the other birds would laugh at him. Tim felt sad and wished he could sing like his friends. One day, Tim met a wise old bird who told him a secret. "To sing well, you must practice every day," said the wise old bird. So, Tim decided to practice his singing each day, just an inch away from his nest. He sang and sang, and slowly, he got better. After many days, Tim's friends heard him sing. They were surprised and happy for him. They all sang together, and Tim was no longer a troubled bird. He was happy because he could sing beautifully with his friends.

Once upon a time, in a little village, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom and dad. One day, Lily's mom asked her to fill a big pot with water from the well. Lily went to the well and saw that the water was dirty. She was sad because her mom needed clean water. Just then, a big frog jumped out of the well. The frog said, "I can help you make the water clean." Lily was surprised, as she didn't know frogs could talk. But she wanted to help her mom, so she said, "Yes, please help me." The frog jumped into the well and made the water clean. Lily filled the pot with clean water and took it back to her mom. Her mom was very happy and thanked Lily. From that day on, the frog and Lily became best friends, and the village always had clean water.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Amy. She had a big toy box full of fun toys. One day, Amy saw her mommy's medicine on the table. She thought it was candy. Amy was feeling naughty, so she decided to choose the medicine. Amy put the medicine in her mouth, but it tasted yucky! She spit it out and started to cry. Her mommy heard her cry and came running into the room. When mommy saw the medicine, she knew what had happened. Mommy told Amy that medicine is not candy and that she should never touch it. Amy felt sorry for being naughty. She promised to listen to her mommy and never touch the medicine again. From that day on, Amy played only with her toys and never touched anything that was not hers.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy gun. He was very happy when he played with it. One day, Tom saw a big box. He thought it would be fun to hide in it. The box was a fit for him, so he went inside. While Tom was in the box, his friend Sam came to play. Sam did not see Tom, but he saw the toy gun. Sam wanted to have the gun, so he picked it up and started to play with it. Tom heard Sam and wanted to come out of the box to play too. Tom tried to get out of the box, but it was too tight. He was stuck! Tom called for help, but Sam was too busy playing with the gun to hear him. Tom was sad that he could not play with his friend and his toy gun. The day did not end well for Tom because he stayed stuck in the box.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally was very stubborn. One day, she found a big book called a novel. Sally wanted to read the novel all by herself. Sally tried to read the novel. But, she did not know some words. Her big sister, Jane, saw her and said, "Sally, let me help you read the novel." Sally was stubborn and said, "No, I can do it myself!" Sally tried and tried to read the novel. She felt sad because she could not read it. Jane saw Sally was sad and said, "Sally, please forgive me. I want to help you." Sally looked at Jane and said, "I forgive you, Jane. Let's read the novel together." So, Jane and Sally read the novel together. They were very happy. Sally learned that it was okay to ask for help when she needed it.
