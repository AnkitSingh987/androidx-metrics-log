Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ellie. Ellie was 3 years old and she was very curious. One day, she was walking around her house when she stumbled across an old jar. It was very colorful, with all kinds of different colors painted on it. Ellie was intrigued and wanted to know more about the jar. She asked her mom what the jar was for and her mom told her it was a special jar. She said that if Ellie put something special in it, it would keep it for her. Ellie was so excited about the jar, she decided to start filling it up with her favorite things. She put in colorful buttons, tiny ribbons, and little rocks she would find when playing outside. Once she had filled it up, Ellie’s mom said it was time to tell someone else about the jar. That’s when Ellie decided to go to her little friend’s house to tell them all about it. She was so excited when her friend got just as excited as she was about the jar and wanted to make their own. Ellie was so pleased to see how excited her friend was and was glad that she had decided to tell them all about her special jar.

Once upon a time, in a big green field, there was a little bee named Buzzy. Buzzy lived in a hive with all his bee friends. They would fly around and find flowers to make sweet honey. One day, Buzzy was flying and saw a big scary bird. The bird looked at Buzzy and said, "Hey, little bee! I want to eat you!" Buzzy was very scared and flew back to his hive as fast as he could. When Buzzy got back to the hive, he told his friends about the big scary bird. His friend, Daisy, said, "Don't worry, Buzzy. We will all rest in the hive and be safe. The bird can't get us here." Buzzy and his friends stayed in the hive and rested. They were not scared anymore because they were safe together.

Once upon a time, there was a curious little cat. The cat liked to play near the hedge. One day, the cat saw a big dog. The dog was loud and scary. The cat was sad because it could not play near the hedge. The cat had a friend, a small mouse. The mouse was very smart. The cat told the mouse about the big dog. The mouse had an idea. It said, "Let's whisper to the dog and make him go away." The cat liked the idea. The cat and the mouse went near the hedge. They whispered to the dog, "Go away, big dog! This is our home." The dog was scared of the whispers. It ran away fast. The cat and the mouse were happy. They could play near the hedge again.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lily. She loved to cook with her mom in the kitchen. They would make yummy food together. Lily liked to cook because it made her feel like a big girl. One day, Lily and her mom decided to cook a big meal for the whole family. They put on some fun music and danced while they cooked. The music made them feel happy and excited. They cooked and danced all afternoon. When the meal was ready, Lily's family came to eat. They all enjoyed the food that Lily and her mom cooked. They laughed and talked while they ate. Everyone was very happy. After dinner, they all danced to the fun music. It was a great day for Lily and her family.

Daddy was busy trying to fix his car. He had been working on it for a few hours now, but he couldn't manage to get it done. He went inside and said to Daisy, his 3 year old daughter, “I need to get a new part for my car, can you come with me?” Daisy was thrilled and hopped right away into the car. On the way to the shop, Daddy explained to her what he was trying to fix. When they arrived, they found the exact part they needed. Daisy felt a bit sad though. She remarked, “But how will you know if it's the right one? It might have a different taste.” Daddy laughed and said, “Don't worry, we'll test it. I think it's the right one but if the taste is too harsh, I'll have to find something else.” So Daisy and Daddy tested the part and it was indeed the perfect one! They were so happy and Daddy thanked Daisy for her help. It was the best day of the week!
