Once upon a time there was a young boy called Matthew who loved to play outside with his friends. He was very happy and enjoyed playing with his friends. One day his friends asked him if he wanted to play with their new toy – a pistol. Matthew was unsure if he should or not. He was a bit scared and felt a bit upset but he said yes. His friends showed him how to use the pistol. It made loud noises and Matthew was scared but he decided to keep playing. Later that day Matthew's teacher asked the class that if you fire a pistol it can change things. Matthew understood and felt embarrassed. He had made a mistake and he was very upset. Matthew learnt a lesson that day – be careful when you make decisions because even small choices can change things and it might not always turn out the way you want it to. It's better to think twice before taking a risk.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat spaghetti. One day, Tim's mom made him a big plate of spaghetti for lunch. Tim was so happy and felt safe at home with his mom. While Tim was eating his spaghetti, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse ran into the kitchen! Tim saw the mouse and began to cry. The mouse was scared too, and it ran away. Tim's mom saw what happened and had an idea. She took a piece of cheese and put it outside, far away from the house. The mouse found the cheese and was so happy. It decided to live outside, and Tim felt safe again. From that day on, Tim and the mouse both enjoyed their food without any more surprises.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim was very lazy. He did not like to do any work. He just wanted to play all day. One day, Tim's mom told him to improve and be a good boy. She said, "Tim, you need to help me clean the house and learn new things." But Tim did not want to do that. He just wanted to play with his toys and watch TV. Because Tim did not improve, he did not learn how to do many things. He did not know how to clean his room or make his bed. Tim's house became very messy and dirty. His mom was sad, and Tim did not have any friends to play with. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful boat. The boat was navy blue. It lived in the sea with many fish friends. One day, the boat met a big fish. The big fish said, "I am sad. I made a bad choice and now I regret it." The boat asked, "What did you do?" The big fish said, "I ate all the small fish. Now I have no friends." The beautiful navy boat felt sorry for the big fish. It said, "Don't worry, big fish. We can be friends." The big fish was happy to have a new friend. They played together in the sea and had lots of fun. From that day, the big fish never ate its small fish friends again. It learned from its mistake. The beautiful navy boat and the big fish were best friends forever. They played and swam in the sea, happy and free.

Once upon a time, there was a toy robot. The robot needed a battery to move. The robot was very flexible. It could dance and jump. A little boy named Tim loved the robot. One day, Tim could not recognize his robot. The robot was not moving. Tim was sad. He asked his mom for help. His mom said the robot needed a new battery. Tim and his mom went to the store. They looked for a battery. They could not find the right one. The store had no more batteries. Tim and his robot were sad. The robot could not dance or jump anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a happy girl named Lily. She had a pretty blouse that she loved to wear. The blouse was red, blue, and yellow. It made her feel like a rainbow. One day, Lily met a cat in the park. The cat was soft and nice. Lily asked the cat, "What is your name?" The cat said, "My name is Snowy." Lily and Snowy played together all day. They ran, jumped, and laughed. At the end of the day, Lily said, "Snowy, I had so much fun today! I want to see you again." Snowy agreed and they became best friends. Now, Lily and Snowy play together all the time, and they are always happy.
