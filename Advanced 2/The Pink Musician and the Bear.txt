Once upon a time, there was a pink musician. He played the drum. He loved to make loud music. People liked to dance to his music. But one day, a big bear came to the town. The bear did not like the music. The music made the bear angry. The bear wanted the music to stop. The pink musician was scared. He did not want to stop playing his drum. The bear went up to the pink musician. The bear said, "Stop the music!" But the pink musician did not stop. He loved to play his drum. The bear got angrier. The bear grabbed the drum and squeezed it. The drum broke. The pink musician was sad. He could not play his drum anymore. The bear went away. The people in the town were sad too. They missed the music. The pink musician never played again.

One day, a little frog named Tim was hopping in the hot sun. He was very hot and tired. Tim wanted to cool down, so he hopped to a pond. The pond was cool and nice. Then, the rain started to fall. The rain was cold, but Tim liked it. He danced and played in the rain. The rain made the hot day feel better. After a while, the rain stopped, and the pond started to freeze. Tim saw the ice and was excited. He slid on the ice and had so much fun. The hot day turned into a fun day for Tim the frog.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Pepper. Pepper loved to learn new things. He lived in a big house with a little girl named Lily. They were best friends and played together every day. One day, Pepper and Lily were playing in the yard when a big, mean cat came by. The cat wanted to take Pepper's favorite toy. Pepper did not like that, and he tried to stop the cat. But the cat was too fast and ran away with the toy. Pepper and Lily were very sad. But then, an unexpected thing happened. A friendly bird saw what happened and wanted to help. The bird flew high in the sky and found the cat. The bird talked to the cat and told him that taking things was not nice. The cat felt bad and gave the toy back to the bird. The bird brought the toy back to Pepper and Lily. They were so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, they all became friends and learned to share and play together.

One day, a girl named Sue found a big orange purse. She was very happy. Sue wanted to show her friend, Tom. She ran to his house and said, "Tom, look at the big orange purse I found!" Tom looked at the purse and said, "Wow, that's a big purse! Let's add some things inside." So, Sue and Tom added toys, rocks, and leaves into the purse. They were having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A small cat jumped out of the purse! Sue and Tom were very surprised. The cat said, "Thank you for finding my home. I was lost." Sue and Tom were happy they could help the cat and they became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly lady who had a pretty patch. She loved her patch very much. One day, she saw a little girl playing near her patch. The girl was trying to match the flowers in her hands. The elderly lady said, "Hello, little girl! What are you doing?" The girl replied, "I am trying to match these flowers. I want to make them look the same." The elderly lady smiled and said, "Come, I will help you match the flowers in my patch." Together, they picked flowers and matched them. They had a lot of fun. The elderly lady and the little girl became good friends. They played in the patch every day and matched flowers together.

Amy was playing outside with her toys. She was having so much fun! Suddenly, an ant came crawling up her leg. She screamed and started to panic. She quickly grabbed a toy shovel and swept the ant away. But suddenly the ant came back with five of his friends. Amy started to panic even more. She tried to shoo away the ants with her hands, but they kept coming back. Panicking, Amy ran towards the front door to get away from the ants. But the door was closed! She started to cry and pulled on the door handle, but it wouldn't open. She was completely surrounded by the ants and she didn't know what to do. Just then, her mom opened the door and saw Amy surrounded by ants. She quickly grabbed her daughter and ran into the house, slamming the door shut behind her. Amy was safe, but she was still very scared. The door stayed closed and the ants were still outside. So unfortunately, Amy's fun outside was ruined. She learned that she had to be careful around ants, and always make sure the door was open before playing outside.
