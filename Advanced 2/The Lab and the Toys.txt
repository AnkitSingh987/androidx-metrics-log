Once upon a time, there was a big lab. In the lab, people would build and make things. One day, a man named Tom wanted to build something new. He wanted to show everyone that he was the best builder. Tom worked very hard in the lab, but he did not share his toys with others. He was a spoiled man. He thought he was better than everyone else. He did not want to be friends with anyone. One day, while Tom was building, a big wind came and knocked down all his work. Tom was very sad. A kind girl named Lucy saw Tom and helped him build again. Tom learned that being nice and sharing is better than being spoiled. From that day on, Tom shared his toys and made many friends. The moral of the story is that sharing and being kind is more important than being the best.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her friend, a dog named Max. Kitty and Max liked to play and stay together all day. They were very happy. One day, Kitty and Max saw a big box in their house. They did not know what was inside. Max wagged his tail and Kitty purred. They knew something fun was going to happen soon. The next day, Kitty and Max found a big party in their house. There were many friends who came to play and have fun. Kitty and Max were so happy to see everyone. Kitty helped serve yummy food to all her friends. Max played with all the toys. At the end of the day, Kitty and Max were very tired but they had a great time at the party. The big box was a surprise party for Kitty and Max, and they loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bird named Billy. Billy liked to teach other birds how to fly. One day, Billy met a little bird named Timmy who could not fly. "Hi Timmy, I can teach you how to fly," said Billy. Timmy was very happy and said, "Yes, please teach me!" They started to practice flying together. After they practiced, they would eat a snack. One day, while they were eating their snack, a big cat came. The cat wanted to eat both birds. Billy and Timmy tried to fly away, but Timmy was not fast enough. The cat caught Timmy and ate him. Billy was very sad because he could not save his friend.

Sam and Lily were friends who liked to play outside. One day, they decided to skip to the park and have a picnic. They packed some bread, cheese, and fruit in a bag and put on their hats and shirts. "Are you ready, Lily?" Sam asked. "Yes, Sam, let's go!" Lily said. They skipped along the road, holding hands and singing songs. They saw some birds, flowers, and cars on the way. They were happy and excited. When they got to the park, they found a nice spot under a big tree. They spread a blanket on the grass and took out their food. They ate and drank and shared their stories. "Sam, this is a very useful shirt," Lily said, pointing to Sam's shirt. "It has a pocket where you can keep things." "Thank you, Lily," Sam said. "You are right, it is a useful shirt. I keep my toy car in the pocket. Do you want to see it?" "Yes, please, Sam," Lily said. Sam took out his toy car and showed it to Lily. They played with it for a while, making vroom-vroom noises. Then they put it back in the pocket. They finished their picnic and packed their bag. They skipped back to their homes, saying goodbye to the park and the tree. They hugged each other and thanked each other for the fun day. "See you tomorrow, Lily," Sam said. "See you tomorrow, Sam," Lily said. They went inside their houses and told their moms and dads about their adventure. They were tired but happy. They had a good nap and dreamed of skipping and playing again.
