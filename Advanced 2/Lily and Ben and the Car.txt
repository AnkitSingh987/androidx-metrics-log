Lily and Ben were going to the zoo with their mom. They were very excited to see the animals. They got in the car and put on their seat belts. Mom said, "Always buckle up, it is safe and smart." On the way, they saw a big sign that said "Zoo". It had pictures of a lion, a monkey, a giraffe and a penguin. Lily said, "I want to see the lion, he is the king of the jungle." Ben said, "I want to see the monkey, he is funny and can swing on the trees." Mom said, "We can see both, and many more. But I recommend we start with the penguin, he is my favorite. He is very intelligent and can swim fast and slide on the ice." They parked the car and walked to the entrance. They bought their tickets and got a map. Mom said, "Let's follow the map, it will show us where the animals are." They followed the map and found the penguin exhibit. They saw many penguins in a big pool. Some were swimming, some were diving, some were standing on the rocks. One penguin came close to the glass and looked at them. He tilted his head and blinked his eyes. Lily and Ben smiled and waved at the penguin. They said, "Hello, penguin, you are so cute and smart." The penguin nodded and made a sound. He seemed to say hello back. Mom said, "He likes you, he is very friendly. Do you want to feed him some fish?" Lily and Ben said, "Yes, please!" They got some fish from a bucket and threw them into the pool. The penguin caught them and ate them. He thanked them with another sound. Lily and Ben were very happy. They said, "We love the penguin, he is the best. Thank you, mom, for recommending him." Mom said, "You're welcome, my loves. I'm glad you enjoyed it. Now, let's go and see the other animals." They left the penguin exhibit and continued their adventure at the zoo.

One day, a little bird was sitting in a cold tree. The bird was sad because it could not sing a song. All the other birds could sing, but this little bird could not. A kind squirrel saw the sad bird and wanted to help. The squirrel went to the bird and asked, "Why are you sad?" The bird said, "I can't sing a song like the other birds." The squirrel tried to explain how to sing, but the bird still could not do it. The squirrel had an idea. It found a nice, warm place for the bird to sit. The bird felt better in the warm place. The squirrel explained how to sing again. This time, the bird tried and could sing a beautiful song. The bird was happy, and the squirrel was happy too. They sang songs together all day long.

Once upon a time, in a warm and sunny land, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toy weapon, a little sword. He would pretend to be a brave knight, fighting dragons and saving the day. One day, while Tim was playing, it started to rain. Tim's mom called out to him, "Tim, come inside! It's raining, and you'll get all wet!" Tim didn't want to stop playing, but he didn't want to get wet either. So, he picked up his toy sword and ran inside. When Tim was inside, he told his mom all about his adventures with his toy weapon. His mom listened and smiled, happy to see her little boy having so much fun. The rain stopped, and the sun came out again. Tim's mom said, "You can go back outside now, but remember to come in if it starts to rain again!" Tim hugged his mom, grabbed his toy sword, and went back outside to continue his brave adventures.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. He was a very hungry boy, so he went out to find food. He went to a park and saw a lot of delicious things, like candies and chips. Jack had a strong urge to eat them all. While he was looking around and picking out snacks, he suddenly tripped and fell. Everyone at the park looked at him, and Jack felt embarrassed. However, he got up and kept on walking. Soon Jack found a sandwich in a trash can. Though it was yucky, he was so hungry that he ate it anyway. After he ate the sandwich, he was not embarrassed anymore. He was full, and that made him feel better. The End.
