Title: A Tale of the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little mouse. The big cat liked to eat, but the little mouse wanted to teach the big cat a lesson. One day, the big cat said, "I am going to eat you, little mouse!" But the little mouse was not scared. He said, "You are stupid, big cat! I will teach you a lesson!" The little mouse had a plan. He found a big hole and hid inside. When the big cat came to eat the little mouse, he fell into the hole. The big cat could not get out of the hole. The little mouse laughed at the big cat. But then, another big cat came and ate the little mouse. The big cat in the hole was sad because he could not help his friend. The moral of the story is: do not call others stupid, or you might get hurt too.

Anna and Ben wanted to decorate the big tree in the garden. They had many shiny things to hang on the branches. They also had a star for the top. "Look, Anna, I found a tap!" Ben said. He pointed to a metal thing on the wall. "We can use it to make water for the tree." Anna nodded. She liked water. She ran to the tap and turned it. But nothing came out. She turned it harder. Still nothing. "Maybe the tap is broken," Ben said. He tried to turn it too. But he only made a loud noise. "Shh, Ben, you will wake up Grandpa!" Anna said. She looked at the house. Grandpa was sleeping on the sofa. He was very old and wise. He knew many things. They tiptoed to the house and opened the door. They saw Grandpa snoring softly. They also saw a big jug of water on the table. "Let's take the water for the tree," Anna whispered. She picked up the jug and carried it outside. Ben followed her with the shiny things. They poured the water on the tree and hung the shiny things on the branches. They also put the star on the top. The tree looked very pretty. Grandpa woke up and saw them. He smiled and came outside. "What a lovely tree!" he said. "You did a good job, Anna and Ben. You are very clever and kind." Anna and Ben hugged Grandpa. They were happy. They liked to decorate the tree. They also liked to make Grandpa proud.

Once upon a time there was a mommy and a baby. Mommy loved the baby so much and wanted to make her happy. One day, Mommy rubbed the baby's back and gave her a massage. The baby loved it and felt so sleepy. But, just as Mommy was finishing the massage, the baby heard a loud noise. It was the doorbell! Mommy ran to the door and opened it. There was a man outside. He said he was there to prevent bad things from happening. Mommy didn't know what to do. Mommy told the man to leave but he wouldn't go. Mommy was scared and angry. She tried to keep the man away but he wouldn't leave. So Mommy had to take the baby away from the massage and back inside. The baby was so sad because she was so sleepy. But the man wouldn't leave, so Mommy had to take the baby away and the bad man stayed outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toy blocks. She would build a big stack with them. One day, her friend Tim came over to play with her. While playing, Tim accidentally knocked over Lily's stack of blocks. The blocks fell down and one of them was broken. Lily was very sad. Tim felt bad and said, "I promise I will be more careful next time." The next day, Tim came back with a new block to replace the broken one. They played together and built an even bigger stack. Lily was happy again, and they both promised to take care of each other's toys. From that day on, they played happily and always kept their promises.
