Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max liked to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Max saw a little girl named Lily. She was painting with many colors. Max went to Lily and said, "Hi! Can I play too?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes! You can help me paint." Max was very happy. Lily offered Max a brush with blue color. Max took the brush and started to paint with Lily. They painted a big tree with green leaves and a blue sky. Max and Lily were very proud of their painting. They showed it to their friends, who also liked the colors. Max and Lily became best friends and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a big window. He liked to look out the window at the park. He saw kids playing and having fun. Tim wanted to go to the park too, but his mom said they would go later. Tim did not like to wait. He felt hate for waiting. Tim's mom was making a delicious cake for a party. She told Tim they would go to the park after the cake was done. Tim looked at the cake and thought of a plan. He wanted to make the cake cook faster, so they could go to the park sooner. Tim turned up the heat on the oven. The cake started to smell good. Tim's mom came into the kitchen and saw the oven was too hot. She took the cake out and it was burned. Tim felt sad. His mom said they could still go to the park, but they had to buy a new cake first. Tim learned that waiting is not always bad, and sometimes it can be better than rushing.

John was very excited - he was going to design his very own aeroplane! He worked very hard to make sure all the pieces were organized. After much careful planning and many colourful sheets of paper, John finally put all the pieces together. He jumped for joy - he had done it! John couldn't wait to tell his parents about his project, so he ran up to them in the living room and shouted "Look Mum, Look Dad! I designed an aeroplane!" His parents smiled with pride. His mum said, "That's great John! I can see you worked really hard and it is a very organized design". John just smiled and replied, "Thanks Mum, it was so much fun to do!"

Once upon a time, there was a land. In this land, there was a hidden place. This hidden place had many toys. One day, a boy named Tim wanted to make a new toy. He went to the hidden place to find something to make his toy. In the hidden place, Tim found a big box. He opened the box and saw a magic wand. He picked up the wand and said, "I want to make a toy!" Suddenly, the wand made a bright light. The light made a big, funny toy. Tim was happy to see the toy. He played with it all day. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy turned into a big, friendly bear. Tim was surprised, but he loved his new bear friend. They played together in the hidden place and had lots of fun.

Once there was a bird. His wings were big and he wanted to soar. He flapped his wings quickly and lifted off the ground. He flew higher and higher until he was in the clouds. He could feel the cool breeze around him. The bird wanted to do something really special. He saw a thread and he decided to wrap it around himself. He felt lighter and he knew he could soar even higher. He followed the thread as it twisted and turned like a rollercoaster. The bird found himself at the highest point flying above the clouds. It was easy and he felt so free. He flew around in circles and loops, feeling the wind go past him. He twirled and twisted in the sky. The bird eventually flew back down to the ground to rest. It was an amazing experience for him, and he knew he could soar wherever he wanted.
