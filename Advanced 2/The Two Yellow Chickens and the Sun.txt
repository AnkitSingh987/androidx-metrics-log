Once there lived two yellow chickens, a shy one and a brave one. The brave one was never scared to move around, jumping here and there. The shy one, on the other hand, stayed in the corner, scared to move. One day, the brave chicken said to the shy one, "Let's move together to the other side of the yard. Come on. It'll be fun!" The shy chicken was unsure and scared but the brave chicken encouraged it to move. The shy chicken slowly moved and eventually, it started jumping and running with the brave chicken. They ran, flew and hopped all around the yard, pecking and playing, until the sun set in the horizon. By the end of the day, the shy chicken was no longer scared to move around. This story illustrates the importance of facing our fears and not letting them affect us. If the brave chicken didn't encourage the shy one, it would have lived its entire day in the corner of the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a happy little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his board. He would run, jump, and roll with it all day long. One day, while playing with his board, Spot saw a big tree near the pond. Spot's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, lived in the tree. Chirpy was very sad because he lost his nest. Spot wanted to help Chirpy. He had an idea! He thought, "Maybe my board can save Chirpy's home." Spot said to Chirpy, "Don't worry, Chirpy! My board will save your home." He put the board under the tree. Chirpy was scared at first, but then he saw how the board could help him. Chirpy built a new nest on the board. The next day, it rained a lot. The pond water went up, but Chirpy's nest stayed dry on the board. Chirpy was so happy that Spot saved his home. They played together with the board every day, and they became the best of friends.

Anna and Ben wanted to decorate the big cake for Mom's birthday. They had a lot of frosting, sprinkles, and candles. They put the cake on the table and started to spread the frosting with a knife. "Let me do it, Anna. You are too slow," Ben said. He grabbed the knife from Anna and made a big swirl on the cake. "No, Ben. It's my turn. Give it back," Anna said. She pulled the knife from Ben and made a smaller swirl on the cake. They kept fighting over the knife and the frosting. They did not see that the frosting was dripping from the table to the floor. They did not hear that the dog was licking the frosting from the floor. They did not notice that Mom was coming into the kitchen. Mom saw the mess on the table, the floor, and the dog. She saw the cake with the uneven frosting and the missing candles. She saw Anna and Ben with frosting on their faces and hands. She was very angry. "Anna and Ben, what have you done? You ruined the cake and the kitchen. And look at the dog. He ate the candles and now he is bald. How could you be so careless and selfish?" Mom yelled. Anna and Ben felt very sorry. They did not mean to make Mom angry. They wanted to make her happy. They hugged Mom and said they were sorry. "We wanted to decorate the cake for you, Mom. We love you. Please don't be mad," Anna and Ben said. Mom sighed and hugged them back. She was still angry, but she loved them too. She said they had to clean up the mess and take the dog to the vet. She said they had to make a new cake and decorate it nicely. She said they had to learn to share and cooperate. "Maybe next time, you can ask me to help you decorate the cake. I can show you how to do it without making a drain on the floor and a bald dog," Mom said.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Ann. She loved chocolate very much. One day, she got a big chocolate bar from her mom. She was so happy! Ann wanted to save her chocolate for later. She put her chocolate on the table in her room. But Ann was careless. She left her window open. A strong wind blew into her room. The wind blew the chocolate off the table. It fell on the floor and broke into pieces. Ann came back and saw her broken chocolate. She was very sad. The chocolate was all dirty and she could not eat it anymore.
