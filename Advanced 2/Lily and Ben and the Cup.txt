Lily and Ben were playing with their toys in the living room. They had a lot of fun making noises and pretending to be animals. Lily had a tray with some plastic cups and plates. She liked to pretend she was having a tea party with her dolls. "Ben, do you want some tea?" Lily asked, holding a cup. "Yes, please," Ben said, smiling. He took the cup and pretended to drink. "Mmm, this is good tea." Lily poured some more tea from a pot. She was careful not to spill. She wanted to be a good hostess. Suddenly, they heard a loud crash from the kitchen. They turned and saw their mom standing by the door. She looked angry and scared. She was holding a broken vase. There was water and flowers on the floor. "What happened?" Lily asked, dropping the pot. "I was trying to put this vase on the shelf, but it slipped and fell," Mom said. "It was your grandma's vase. It was very old and special. Now it's broken. I'm so sorry." Lily and Ben felt sad. They loved their grandma and her vase. They wanted to help their mom. "Can we fix it?" Ben asked. "I don't think so, honey," Mom said. "It's too broken. Some pieces are missing. They must have disappeared." Lily and Ben looked around. They saw some shiny bits on the floor. They were pieces of the vase. They ran to pick them up. "Look, Mom, we found some pieces!" Lily said, holding a tray with the bits. "Maybe we can glue them back together," Ben said, hopeful. Mom smiled and hugged them. She was happy that they tried to help. "Thank you, my sweeties," Mom said. "You are very kind and smart. Maybe we can try to fix it later. But for now, let's clean up this mess and have some real tea. How does that sound?" Lily and Ben nodded. They were glad that their mom was not angry anymore. They helped her clean up the water and the flowers. Then they went to the table and had some real tea and cookies. They still felt sad about the vase, but they also felt happy to be with their mom. They knew that she loved them and their grandma. And they loved her too.

Once upon a time, in a land of many colors, there lived a selfish king. The king loved all the colors, but he did not want to share them with anyone. He wanted to keep all the colors for himself. One day, a little girl came to the king and asked if she could guess his favorite color. The king thought this would be fun, so he said yes. The little girl guessed and guessed, but she could not find the right color. The king laughed and said, "You cannot guess my favorite color because I love all the colors, and I keep them just for me!" The little girl was sad, but she had an idea. She asked the king if she could try one more time. The king agreed, and the little girl said, "Your favorite color is the color of sharing!" The king was surprised, and he realized that he had been selfish. He decided to share all the colors with everyone in the land. From that day on, the king and his people lived in a world full of colors, and they were all very happy. The king learned that sharing was the best color of all, and he was not selfish anymore. And everyone in the land knew that the little girl had taught the king a very important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to watch videos on his mom's phone. His favorite video was about a funny dog that made him laugh. One day, Tim was feeling anxious because it was time to sleep, but he didn't want to stop watching his video. He asked his mom if he could watch one more video before bedtime. His mom said yes, but only one more. Tim watched the last video and then went to bed. As he closed his eyes, he thought about the funny dog and it made him feel less anxious. Soon, Tim fell asleep and had happy dreams about the funny dog from his favorite video.
