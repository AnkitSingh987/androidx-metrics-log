Once upon a time, there was a large bulb. The bulb knew it could do something special. Every day, it would look in the sky and dream of what it could do. One day, the bulb found the courage to perform. It glowed with a light so bright that the sky smiled. Everyone around was filled with joy. The bulb kept performing for days and nights. It was so happy to show everyone its light. People stopped to watch and cheer each time the bulb performed. Soon the bulb's light faded and its performance ended. But the joy and happiness it created stayed with everyone who saw it. And the bulb was happy too, knowing it had done something special.

Once upon a time, there was a huge bear named Ben. He lived in a big forest with his friend, a tiny bird named Tim. They loved to play together all day long. One day, Ben saw a sign that said, "Free Lotion." He thought, "I want to get some lotion for Tim and me." So, Ben and Tim went to get the lotion. When they got there, they saw a long line of animals waiting. Ben and Tim decided to wait too. As they waited, they saw a small squirrel crying. The squirrel was too short to see the sign. Ben and Tim felt sad for the squirrel. They decided to help the squirrel by letting him sit on Ben's big shoulder. The squirrel was very happy and could now see the sign. At last, it was their turn to get the lotion. But when they opened the bottle, they found out it was empty! They were sad, but then they remembered the squirrel. They felt happy because they helped a friend. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel good, even when something unexpected happens.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to tease his old cat, Fluffy. He would run around the house and make funny faces at her. Fluffy would just look at him and meow. One day, Tom found a big glass in the kitchen. He thought it would be fun to play a trick on Fluffy. He put the glass on the floor and hid behind the door. When Fluffy came into the room, she saw the glass and walked up to it. She looked inside and saw something shiny. Suddenly, the glass started to shake! Tom was scared, but he peeked out from behind the door. He saw a tiny fairy come out of the glass! The fairy looked at Fluffy and said, "Thank you for finding me! I was trapped in that glass for a long time." Fluffy just meowed and looked happy. The fairy looked at Tom and said, "I saw you teasing Fluffy. That wasn't very nice. But, since you helped me, I will give you one wish." Tom thought for a moment and said, "I wish I could be friends with Fluffy and never tease her again." The fairy smiled and waved her wand. From that day on, Tom and Fluffy were the best of friends and never teased each other again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to run and play outside. One day, Tim wanted to record how fast he could run. Tim ran as fast as he could. He ran so fast, he won a big, shiny medal. Tim was very happy and proud of his medal. He showed it to his mom, dad, and friends. That night, it was dark outside. Tim put his medal next to his bed. He went to sleep with a big smile on his face, dreaming of running even faster next time.

Once upon a time, there was a big spike in a small park. All the kids wanted to play with it, but it was too high to reach. They tried to jump, but they could not get it. They were sad. One day, a kind girl named Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's make a tall ladder to get the spike!" All the kids were happy with the idea. They found sticks and ropes and made a ladder together. The ladder was very rare and special. They put the ladder on the tree and climbed up. The kids got the spike and played with it all day. They were so happy, and they learned that working together can fix problems.
