Once upon a time, there was a big festival in the town. Everyone was happy and excited. But Tom, a little boy, felt gloomy. He didn't want to go to the festival because he was scared he would embarrass himself. Tom's mom said, "Don't worry, Tom. We will go together and have fun." Tom felt a little better and went to the festival with his mom. They played games, ate yummy food, and watched a fun show. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew away all the colorful decorations. Everyone was surprised and sad. But Tom wasn't scared or embarrassed anymore. He said, "Let's fix the decorations and make the festival fun again!" Everyone worked together and made the festival even better than before. Tom felt happy and proud.

One day, a nice dog named Ben went for a walk. He saw a big round ball in the park. Ben was very happy and wanted to play with the ball. He ran to the ball and started to push it with his nose. A little girl named Lily saw Ben playing with the round ball. She walked up to Ben and said, "Hi, do you want to play with me?" Ben wagged his tail and barked, "Yes, let's play!" Lily and Ben played with the round ball all day long. They had so much fun together. When it was time to go home, Ben bowed to Lily to say thank you. Lily smiled and said, "You're a good dog, Ben. Let's play again tomorrow!" Ben barked happily and they both went home.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had many fruits. Birds and animals loved the tree. They all played near the tree. One day, a careless boy came. He saw the fruits. He wanted to steal the fruits. The boy climbed the tree to take the fruits. The tree did not like this. The tree shook its branches. The careless boy fell down. He learned not to steal. The tree and its friends lived happily.

Lily and Max were playing in the park. They liked to swing and slide and run. They saw a big pink balloon in the sky. It was very pretty and shiny. They wanted to catch it. They ran after the balloon. They did not see the wild dog. The dog was big and black and angry. It barked and chased them. Lily and Max were scared. They screamed for help. A man heard them. He came with a stick. He shooed the dog away. He was very brave and kind. He gave Lily and Max a hug. He also gave them the pink balloon. It had fallen from the sky. Lily and Max were happy. They thanked the man. They played with the balloon. They did not see the wild dog again.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to cook. One day, he decided to cook a big meal for all his friends. He was very enthusiastic about it. So, he wrote an invitation for his friends to come over to his house. Bobo went to his friend, a small, blue bird named Lulu. He said, "Lulu, I am cooking a big meal. Here is your invitation. Please come to my house." Lulu was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bobo! I will come." On the day of the big meal, all of Bobo's friends came to his house. They saw the big table with lots of food. They all sat down and ate the yummy food that Bobo cooked. Everyone was happy and had a great time. After they finished eating, Bobo's friends said, "Thank you, Bobo, for the invitation and the yummy food!" Bobo was happy that his friends enjoyed the meal. They all played together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Bobo and his friends would often cook and share meals together. They were always enthusiastic about trying new foods and spending time with each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to build with her toy blocks. She had many blocks in different colors like red, blue, and yellow. Building with blocks was easy and fun for her. One day, Sue found a big box in her room. Inside the box was a new toy. It was a stamp! The stamp had a picture of a pretty flower on it. Sue was so happy and excited to play with her new stamp. Sue had an idea. She wanted to make her blocks even more fun. She used the stamp to put pretty flower pictures on her blocks. Now, when she built with her blocks, they had flowers on them too. Sue loved her new blocks and played with them all day long.
