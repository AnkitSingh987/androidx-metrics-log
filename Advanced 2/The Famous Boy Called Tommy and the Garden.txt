Once upon a time there was a famous boy called Tommy. Tommy was three years old and liked to skip. One day, Tommy was skipping in his garden when he heard the phone ring. He ran to the telephone to answer it. "Hello," said Tommy. "Hello, Tommy," replied the voice. "This is Grandma. I'm at the airport, can you come and get me?" Tommy thought for a moment and then said, "No, I have to skip." Grandma was angry. "Tommy, I'm your grandma. You have to come and get me!" Tommy shook his head. He really wanted to keep skipping. Grandma sighed. "Tommy, if you come and get me then I'll take you to get your favorite ice cream." Tommy thought for a moment and then smiled. He knew he had to go. "Okay Grandma, I'll be there soon." So Tommy put on his shoes and skipped all the way to the airport. He was so excited to see Grandma and get his favorite ice cream. The end.

Once there was a little boy called Jack. He wanted to go for a walk but he didn't know where to go. Then he had a great idea. His dad told him about a nice forest that was not too far away. So he set off on a journey to find the forest. On the way there, he looked around and saw a lot of different things like animals, butterflies and trees with bright colors. He kept walking and walking until he reached the forest. The forest looked very nice. There were tall trees and colorful flowers. Jack was very excited and he couldn't wait to explore further. Suddenly, he saw a beautiful butterfly in the distance. He pointed towards it and said "look at that pretty butterfly". He started to walk around the forest and play. He climbed the trees, picked flowers and chased the butterflies. Jack was having so much fun that he didn't want to leave. He stayed in the nice forest until the sun went down. Then he went home with a big smile on his face, happy to have had such a wonderful day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia went on a safari with her mom and dad. They saw many animals like lions, elephants, and monkeys. Mia was very happy. One day, Mia saw a sad monkey. The monkey was all alone. Mia stared at the monkey and asked her mom, "Why is the monkey sad?" Her mom said, "He is lonely because he has no friends." Mia wanted to help the sad monkey. She asked her mom and dad if they could find a friend for the monkey. They all looked around the safari. They saw a group of monkeys playing. Mia's dad said, "Let's take the sad monkey to the other monkeys." Mia, her mom, and her dad took the sad monkey to the other monkeys. The sad monkey became happy when he met the other monkeys. They played together and had fun. Mia smiled and said, "Now the monkey is happy and has friends." From that day on, the monkey was never sad again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to a store with his mom. The store was not big, but it had many things. Tim saw a modest red umbrella. He liked it a lot. He said, "Mom, can I have this umbrella?" His mom looked at the umbrella and said, "I recommend we buy it. It will keep you dry when it rains." So, they bought the umbrella, and Tim was very happy. The next day, it rained. Tim took his new umbrella to school. His friend, Sam, saw it and said, "Wow, I like your umbrella!" Tim said, "Thank you! My mom helped me pick it out." They both stayed dry under the umbrella, and they played all day.

Once upon a time, there was a compassionate little girl named Sue. Sue loved to help others and make new friends. One day, she went to the park to play. At the park, Sue met a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because his ball was stuck in a tree. Sue wanted to help Tim, so she asked him, "Can I help you get your ball?" Tim said, "Yes, please!" Sue tried to reach the ball, but it was too high. She thought and thought, then she had an idea. She found a long stick and used it to poke the ball. The ball fell down and Tim was very happy. They played together and became good friends. Sue felt good because she could help Tim. They had lots of fun and played until it was time to go home.
