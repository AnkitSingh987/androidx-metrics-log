A seal and a penguin were friends. They liked to play together and have fun. But one day, something bad happened. There was a big wave that made the seal and penguin separate. The penguin was scared and sad. It tried swimming back to the seal, but it couldn't find it. The seal was also scared and fearful. It was swimming far away and couldn't see the penguin. The penguin called out to the seal, "Where are you? I'm so scared and I miss you." The seal replied, "I'm here! Come find me. I miss you too." The two were very happy when they found each other. They swam and hugged, and never wanted to be separated again. They were glad to be back together and be friends once more.

Tim felt good that he was gifted and could help the glove. Now, the glove was nice and they could play with it.

Once upon a time, in a hot and sunny forest, there lived a little tiger. He liked to play and run around all day. But the little tiger was always full of questions. One day, the little tiger asked his mom, "Why is it so hot?" His mom smiled and said, "The sun makes it hot, my dear." The little tiger was happy with the answer, but he still had more questions. The little tiger went to his friends and asked them questions too. They all shared what they knew. The little tiger learned that asking questions was a good way to learn new things. Soon, the little tiger became very wise. He knew many things about the forest and the animals living there. The other animals started to ask the little tiger questions too. They all learned from each other and became good friends. The moral of the story is that asking questions can help us learn and grow. When we share what we know, we can all be wise and live happily together.

Once upon a time, there was a big, scary, dangerous monster. Every night, the monster would wake up and roam around the neighbourhood. One night, it stumbled upon a mailbox. The monster stared at the mailbox and thought it looked quite yummy. So he opened it. But when he opened it, he realised it was empty. Just then, a little girl who was playing nearby heard the noise. She didn't know what the sound was and she was so scared! So she shouted, “Who's there?” The monster replied, “It's me, the dangerous monster.” The little girl was very frightened but bravely asked, “What are you doing with my mailbox?” The monster replied, “I thought I'd find something yummy inside! But it's empty.” The little girl said, “There's no food here, just letters for my mommy and daddy. So please leave my mailbox alone!” The monster nodded and said, “Okay, I'll leave it alone. Sorry for being so dangerous!” So the monster left and the little girl was so relieved. From then on, she was extra careful when walking near her mailbox. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a fish named Fin. Fin loved to surf. Every day, he would wake up and surf the waves. One day, Fin saw a deep hole in the sea. He was scared but curious. He thought, "I will come back here tomorrow and see what is inside." The next day, Fin went back to the deep hole. He found a big, shiny pearl inside. He was so happy that he decided to show it to all his friends. They all loved it and had a great party to celebrate Fin's find. And from that day on, Fin was known as the bravest fish in the sea.

The big bird and the small bird started to play with the cat. They found out the cat was nice and just wanted to be friends. They all played together and sang a song.
