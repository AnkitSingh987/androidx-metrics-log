Once upon a time, there was a powerful hippo. Whenever it wanted, the hippo liked to dance. One day, the hippo saw a little bird flying in the sky and it wanted to dance with the bird. So, the hippo jumped up high and twirled its arms and legs in the air. The bird was so surprised, it didn't know what to do. It started flying around the hippo and catching the breezes with its wings. The hippo smiled a wide smile and kept on dancing. Everywhere it moved, the hippo's powerful feet made the ground rattle and shake. The bird watched in amazement as the hippo moved gracefully around the sky. But then the hippo quickly stopped. It had danced enough. The hippo waved goodbye to the bird and ran off, happy that it could still dance. The bird flew home feeling happy too, its eyes sparkling with joy.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. Lily loved to play with her toy chest full of fun things. One day, Lily found a big red button inside her toy chest. She didn't know what it was for. "Mommy, what is this?" she asked. Her mommy looked at the button and said, "That is a record button, Lily. It can save your voice when you talk or sing." Lily was excited. She pressed the record button and sang her favorite song. The button saved her song. When she pressed it again, she heard her voice sing the song. Lily was so happy. She played with her new toy and recorded many songs for her mommy and daddy to hear.

Once upon a time, there was a small town with clean houses and happy people. They loved to play outside and have fun. One day, dark clouds came and it started to rain. The rain did not stop for many days. The town started to flood because of all the rain. The water was everywhere, and the people had to move to a safer place. They were sad to leave their clean houses behind. They needed help to make the water go away. A big, strong wind came and blew the rain clouds away. The sun came out and the water started to dry up. The people could move back to their clean houses again. They were so happy and thankful. From that day on, they always remembered to be ready for a flood.

Once upon a time, there was a famous frog. This frog was very good at jumping. He could jump higher than all the other frogs. One day, the famous frog wanted to show everyone how high he could jump. The famous frog went to the big tree. He said, "I will jump to the top of the tree and sleep there." All the other frogs came to watch. They wanted to see the famous frog jump to the top of the tree. The famous frog jumped and jumped. He was almost at the top of the tree. But then, he got very tired. He could not jump anymore. The famous frog fell down from the tree. All the other frogs were sad. The famous frog could not jump high anymore.

One day, Tim and his toy dog, Spot, were playing on the carpet. Tim liked his dog Spot because he was a reliable friend. They were having so much fun together. Tim would toss a ball and Spot would run to catch it. Suddenly, Tim's mom called out, "Tim, it's time for lunch!" Tim picked up his toy dog and ran to the kitchen. He was so excited for lunch that he didn't see the ball on the floor. Tim tripped on the ball and drop Spot on the carpet. "Oh no, Spot!" Tim said, worried. He picked up his toy dog and checked if Spot was okay. Spot was just fine. Tim's mom came over and said, "Be careful, Tim. Now let's have some lunch." Tim and Spot sat down to eat, happy that they were still together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a very careful girl. She always made sure her toys were put away, and she never ran too fast. One day, Sue went to a big park with her mom. Sue saw many kids playing and having fun. She wanted to play too. Sue's mom told her to be careful and stay close. Sue saw a boy who was sad. He lost his toy. Sue asked him, "What is your name?" The boy answered, "My name is Tim." Sue wanted to help Tim find his toy. They looked everywhere, being very careful not to step on any other toys. Finally, they found Tim's toy under a big tree. Tim was so happy! He thanked Sue for being careful and helping him. Sue and Tim became good friends and played together all day.
