One day, a big cat named Tom found a ball of yarn. Tom loved to play with yarn. He would go to the yarn and bat around it with his paws. Tom played with the yarn all day and all night. One morning, Tom woke up and the yarn was gone. He was sad. Tom looked everywhere for his yarn. He looked under his bed, in the kitchen, and even outside. But he could not find it. Then, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry. Jerry was playing with the yarn! Tom was surprised. He thought only big cats liked yarn. Tom and Jerry became friends. They would go to the yarn and play with it together. And they had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely cat named Tom. Tom lived near a grocery shop. One day, Tom saw a big bag of food inside the shop. Tom wanted to take the food home, but he knew it was wrong to take things that were not his. One day, a little girl named Susie came to the grocery shop. Susie saw Tom and wanted to help him. Susie asked her mom to buy some food for Tom. Susie's mom said yes. Susie was happy, and Tom was happy too. Susie and Tom became good friends. They played together every day. One day, Susie and Tom found a lost kitten. They took the kitten to the grocery shop and gave it food. The kitten was not lonely anymore. Everyone was happy. The end. Moral: It is good to help others and share what we have.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went for a walk. They saw a big waste bin near the park. Tim was a serious boy who liked to keep the park clean. He saw some trash on the ground and picked it up to put it in the waste bin. "Spot, we must not forget to clean the park," said Tim. Spot barked happily, wagging his tail. They continued to walk and pick up trash, putting it in the waste bin. As they walked, they saw a big box near the waste bin. Tim and Spot were surprised. They looked inside the box and found a lot of toys! Tim said, "Wow, Spot! We did not expect to find toys here!" Spot barked and jumped around the box. They took the toys home and shared them with their friends. Tim and Spot were very happy that they did not forget to clean the park.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his toy cars and draw pictures. He had a lot of friends, and they all liked to play together. One day, Tom made a pretty picture for his grandma. He wanted to mail it to her so she could see it. Tom's mommy helped him put the picture in an envelope and write grandma's address on it. They went to the mailbox and put the envelope inside. Tom was so happy that he mailed his picture to his grandma. He knew that she would love it, and he couldn't wait to hear what she thought of it.

Once upon a time, there was a small patch of land. It was weak and had been neglected for many years. One day, a small boy named Julian saw it. He wanted to help make it better. So he wished for someone to send him a patch of grass. He went to his mom and asked, “Mommy, can someone send me a patch of grass to fix the weak land?” His mom smiled and said, “Of course, angel. I will call a special delivery man and he will send you a patch.” Julian was so excited. He waited and waited until the special delivery man came with the patch. Julian quickly put it in the weak land. The patch was magical! It made the land stronger and greener. Julian was so proud of how he helped the land.

Once upon a time there was a crab. He was very old and tired, so he decided to take a rest. Suddenly, he heard something coming from the sea. It was a new crab! She was offering him a much needed vacation. The old crab happily accepted her kind offer. The new crab took the old crab with her on a new adventure. Together, they travelled across the sea. They saw lots of new places and creatures. The old crab was very excited to experience something new. At the end of their journey, the old crab was feeling much better. He thanked the new crab for this wonderful adventure. The old crab returned home feeling happy and refreshed. He would never forget his new friend who offered him a gift of a new experience.
