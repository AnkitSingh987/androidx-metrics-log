Once upon a time, in a wild forest, there lived a little bunny named Binky. Binky loved to hop and play with his friends, the birds and the squirrels. They would play hide and seek, and Binky was very good at finding his friends. One day, Binky found a new object while playing. It was round and shiny, and he did not know what it was. Binky wanted to show his friends, but he could not find them. Binky began to panic. He hopped and hopped, looking for his friends, but they were not in their usual hiding spots. Finally, Binky found his friends by the big tree. They were all looking at the shiny object Binky had found. Binky was happy that he found his friends and showed them the object. They all laughed and played with the shiny object, and Binky was not scared anymore. They had a great day playing in the wild forest together.

Once upon a time, there was a tall man. He was walking down a long hallway. He came to an exit and he wanted to keep going. He looked for a way out, but he couldn't find one. He looked up and saw a very tall door. He wanted to keep walking so he opened the door. He walked through the door, and then he saw a huge staircase. Again, he wanted to keep walking, so he walked up the stairs. At the top of the stairs was another tall door. He opened it and he saw a big moon in the sky. He was so happy to find the exit. He was glad he was able to keep going and find the way out. He thanked the moon and went back down the stairs. He never forgot the tall exit that he found.

One day, a tall boy named Tim went for a walk. He wanted to observe things around him. He saw a big tree, a small dog, and a red ball. Tim loved to see all the things in his town. As he walked, Tim found a letter on the ground. He picked it up and looked at it. It had a picture of a cat on it. Tim wanted to find the person who lost the letter. He asked a girl he saw, "Is this your letter?" The girl looked at the letter and said, "No, that is not my letter. But I think it is my friend's letter. She loves cats." Tim and the girl went to find her friend. When they found her, she was so happy to get her letter back. Tim felt good that he could help.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dog named Max. Max liked to perform tricks for his friends. He could jump, roll, and even dance. One day, Max saw a big automobile on the street. It was red and shiny. Max thought, "I want to perform a trick with that automobile." Max had a plan. Max practiced and practiced his tricks. He got better and better. Finally, Max was ready. He jumped on the automobile and performed a dance. All his friends clapped and cheered. Max was happy and proud.
