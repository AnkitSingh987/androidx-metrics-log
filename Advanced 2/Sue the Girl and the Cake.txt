Once upon a time, there was a big reef in the sea. In the reef, there were many fish. One fish was rich. He had many shells. His name was Rich Fish. One day, Rich Fish met a new fish. The new fish was sad. Rich Fish asked, "Why are you sad?" The new fish said, "I have no shells." Rich Fish smiled and said, "I will give you some of my shells." The new fish was happy. He smiled too. They swam together in the big reef. They played and shared shells with other fish. All the fish in the reef were happy and smiling.

Once there was a little girl named Lucy. She was happy and loved to play. One day, she was playing in her room with her favorite toy when she heard a noise. Lucy went to check it out, but when she got to the door, she saw something bad. There was a monster under her bed! The monster tried to scare her, but Lucy was brave. She ran to her dad and said, "A monster is under my bed!" Her dad was brave, too. He grabbed a sheet to use as a cape and charged at the monster. He said, "Go away!" and the monster ran away in fear. Lucy smiled and was very happy. She ran to her dad and hugged him. He said, "Good job, brave one! Everything is okay now." They hugged and smiled and Lucy was safe again.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She saw a big tree with a shiny buckle hanging from it. Mia was worried. She did not know where the buckle came from. She wanted to find the owner of the buckle. Mia walked around and asked her friends, "Do you know who lost this buckle?" Her friends shook their heads. They did not know. Mia felt sad. She wanted to help the person who lost the buckle. Just then, a fairy appeared! The fairy said, "Thank you, Mia, for finding my buckle!" Mia was happy. She gave the buckle to the fairy. The fairy smiled and flew away. Mia felt good that she helped her new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy was going to a fun party with her family. She was anxious because she wanted to wear a pretty dress. Her mom told her that they would unpack her favorite dress from the box. Amy and her mom went to the room where the box was. They opened the box and started to unpack. Amy was so happy when they found her pretty dress. It was pink with flowers on it. Amy put on her pretty dress and looked in the mirror. She felt like a princess. She went to the party with her family and had lots of fun. She danced and played with her friends. Everyone said her dress was very pretty. Amy was so happy she had unpacked her dress.
