One day, a blue ball and a cheap toy car met in a park. The blue ball loved to roll around all day. The cheap toy car had small wheels that went fast. They both liked to play and have fun. The blue ball said, "Let's roll down the big hill together!" The cheap toy car agreed, and they went to the top of the hill. They were excited to race each other. The blue ball said, "Ready, set, go!" and they both started to roll down the hill. As they rolled down, the cheap toy car's wheels began to shake. The blue ball noticed and said, "Be careful, car!" But the cheap toy car couldn't stop. They rolled faster and faster, and then, the cheap toy car's wheels broke off. The blue ball stopped rolling and looked at the broken toy car. The cheap toy car was sad and couldn't play anymore. The blue ball felt bad, and they both wished they hadn't raced down the big hill.

One day, the group of children were playing in the park. They were having fun until they heard a loud noise. They all turned around and saw a big, scary creature! All the children were very fearful. One of the children, Lucy, started to cry. "Don't be scared," said her teacher. "Let me tell you a story." The teacher told them a story about animals who lived far away. They were brave and kind and protected each other. After hearing the story, the children weren't so fearful anymore. The teacher then asked the group of children what they wanted to do. After some discussing, they decided to name the creature and be friends with it. The children waved hello to the creature and it waved back. Everyone was happy that they faced their fear and made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to walk in the park. One day, it was very cold outside. The sky was dull and gray. Tim put on his warm coat and hat. He was ready to go for a walk. Tim walked in the park. He saw a little dog. The dog was cold too. Tim wanted to help the dog. He took off his hat and put it on the dog. The dog was not cold anymore. The dog wagged its tail and walked with Tim. They walked together and had fun. Tim and the dog became good friends. Even though the day was cold and dull, Tim and the dog were happy. They walked in the park every day, and they were never cold again.

Mimi looked at her mixer thoughtfully. She knew she had to make a cake with it for her birthday. Mimi was very excited because this would be her first time baking with the mixer. Mimi's mom came over to help. Together, they put the ingredients together and poured them into the mixer. It whirred and hummed, mixing the ingredients until it became a thick, cake batter. Mimi's mom asked, "Do you want to try it?" Mimi smiled as she dipped her finger in the batter and tasted it. "It's delicious!" Mimi and her mom poured the cake batter into a pan and put it in the oven. Just a few minutes later, it was finished! Mimi and her mom smiled as they admired it. The cake looked and smelled wonderful. Mimi grabbed a fork and took a big bite. It was sweet, moist, and delicious. Mimi was delighted and exclaimed, "Yum! Can I eat the whole cake?" Mimi's mom laughed and said, "No, you cannot eat the whole cake! But I think you can have one more piece for being so thoughtful."

Once upon a time there was a playful cat called Tom. He was having a special day, it was his birthday. Tom was excited to celebrate with his friends and family. So Tom headed outside to find them. As he reached the door, he realised it had started to rain. Tom stopped and said: "Oh no, what will I do?" At that moment, his older brother opened the door and said "Come on, Tom. I'll take you to your party"! Tom was so happy and quickly followed his brother outside. It was raining a lot, but his brother had a big umbrella. So they stayed dry and ran to the party. All of Tom's friends were there, and they were so happy to see him. They hugged him and wished him a happy birthday. Everyone sang and danced and ate cake. It was such a fun party and Tom had the best day ever!
