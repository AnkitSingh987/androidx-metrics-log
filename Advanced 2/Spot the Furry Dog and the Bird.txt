Once upon a time, there was a furry dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his ball and run in the park. One day, Spot found a little bird with a hurt wing. The bird could not fly. Spot said, "Little bird, let me help you." He gently picked up the bird in his mouth and carried it to his house. Spot's mom used her hand to settle the bird on a soft bed. She said, "We will take care of you until you can fly again." Every day, Spot and the bird played together. They became best friends. When the bird's wing was better, Spot and his mom took the bird back to the park. The bird said, "Thank you, Spot, for being my friend and helping me." Then, the bird flew away. Spot felt happy because he helped his friend.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a toy car. He loved the car so much. He wanted to attach a string to the car, so he could pull it around. But Tim did not know how to tie the string. "Mom, can you help me?" Tim asked. Mom came over and helped Tim attach the string to the toy car. Tim was very happy and played with the car all day. Later, Tim's sister, Sue, saw the car and wanted to play with it too. But Tim did not want to share. They both got upset and began to cry. Mom heard them and came into the room. "Why are you upset?" Mom asked. Tim and Sue both pointed at the toy car. Mom thought for a moment, and then she had an idea. "Let's attach another string to the car," Mom said. "Then both of you can pull it together." Tim and Sue liked the idea. They stopped being upset, and they played with the car together, laughing and having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big event in a small town. Everyone was happy and excited. They could not wait to see what would be revealed. The town had worked hard to make the event fun for all. The day before the event, the people of the town saw a rough-looking man. He was not from the town, but he wanted to help. At first, they were scared, but then they saw that he was kind. The man helped them set up for the big event. On the day of the event, the rough man revealed a big surprise. He had made a beautiful painting for the town. Everyone was so happy and thanked the man. The event was the best they ever had, and they learned not to judge someone by how they look.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Tim. Tim loved to eat radishes from the garden. One day, he found a very big radish. It was so big that it was hard for Tim to carry it. He tried to weigh the radish on a scale, but it was too big for the scale. Tim was very sad because he could not eat the big radish. His friend, Sam the squirrel, saw Tim and asked him what was wrong. Tim told Sam about the big radish and how he could not eat it. Sam had an idea to help Tim. Sam and Tim worked together to cut the big radish into small pieces. They shared the radish with all of their friends in the forest. Everyone was grateful to Tim and Sam for sharing the yummy radish. Tim was happy that he could share the big radish with his friends, and he learned that working together can make big problems small.

Once upon a time there was a small, yellow ball. It would roll and it would roll, all across the grassy field. On this particular day, the little ball felt very lonely. He missed all the other things that usually came to play. As he continued to roll, the little ball wondered what he could do. Suddenly, he saw a small leaf blowing in the wind. It had a message written upon it that said 'The news is coming!'. This made the little ball very excited, so he followed the leaf. After a while, he reached a big tree and heard voices coming from the top. He climbed up and reached the top, where he saw a group of birds gathered around. "What kind of news are you talking about?" the little ball asked. One of the birds turned to him and said "A new park is going to be opened tomorrow. We can roll and play there!" The little ball was so happy! Finally he wouldn't be so lonely anymore. He decided that tomorrow he would definitely go to the opened park and have lots of fun!
