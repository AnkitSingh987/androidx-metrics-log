Sara likes fish. She likes to look at fish in the water. She likes to eat fish with rice. She likes to draw fish with crayons. One day, she sees a big fish in the market. It is red and shiny. She wants to buy it. "Mommy, can we buy the fish?" Sara asks. "Sorry, honey, the fish is too big. It will not fit in our pot. We need a smaller fish," Mommy says. Sara is sad. She wants the big fish. She thinks it will be delicious. She looks at the fish again. She has an idea. "Mommy, can we buy the fish and cut it in half? Then it will fit in our pot. We can eat one half today and one half tomorrow," Sara says. Mommy smiles. She thinks Sara is smart. She agrees to buy the fish and cut it in half. Sara is happy. She helps Mommy carry the fish home. They cook the fish with rice and vegetables. Sara eats a lot of fish. She says it is delicious. She thanks Mommy for the fish. She draws a picture of the fish with crayons. She gives it to Mommy. Mommy hugs Sara. She says she loves her. Sara loves Mommy too. They are happy.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy lady who had a big house. She liked to help the kids in her town. One day, she decided to start a class. In the class, she would teach the kids how to attach things together. The kids were very happy to join the class. They would come to the big house every day to learn. The wealthy lady would show them how to attach paper, leaves, and sticks together. The kids would make fun things like hats, houses, and animals. One day, the kids wanted to say thank you to the wealthy lady. They worked together to attach a big heart made of paper and sticks. They gave it to the lady and she was very happy. The class became a fun place for all the kids in the town.

Once upon a time there was a dolphin. He was a very special dolphin who could do something very weird. He could display colours! Every time the dolphin moved, he would display a different colour. All the other animals in the ocean were very surprised and fascinated. So, one day, all the animals decided to have a party and invite the dolphin to come and show off his colours. Everyone was so excited! The dolphin put on a great show. He displayed all the colours of the rainbow and everyone was amazed! The dolphin was so happy that he display all his colours, and all the animals cheered. After the party, everyone waved goodbye to the dolphin and thanked him for the wonderful show.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play with their scooters in the park. They zoom and turn and laugh. They have fun. But one day, the sky is gray and it rains. Lily and Ben cannot go to the park. They are sad. They sit on the couch and look out the window. "Can we play with our scooters inside?" Ben asks. "No, we can't," Lily says. "Mommy says scooters are for outside. They can make the floor dirty." "Then what can we do?" Ben says. Lily thinks. She has an idea. She smiles. "I know!" she says. "We can bathe our scooters!" Ben likes the idea. He smiles too. They take their scooters to the bathroom. They fill the tub with water and soap. They put their scooters in the tub. They scrub and rinse and splash. They make bubbles and sing. They have fun. Mommy comes to the bathroom. She sees Lily and Ben and their scooters. She is surprised. "What are you doing?" she asks. "We are bathing our scooters!" Lily and Ben say. "Why are you bathing your scooters?" Mommy asks. "Because they are dirty," Lily says. "Because it is raining," Ben says. Mommy laughs. She hugs Lily and Ben. "That's very nice of you," she says. "But you know what? You are dirty too. You need a bath as well." Lily and Ben agree. They take off their clothes and join their scooters in the tub. They bathe and play and laugh. They have fun.
