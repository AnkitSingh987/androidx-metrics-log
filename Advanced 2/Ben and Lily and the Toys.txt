Ben and Lily were twins who shared a bedroom. They liked to play with their toys, read books, and draw pictures on their walls. Sometimes they also liked to make a big mess and jump on their beds. One day, their mom told them to clean their bedroom before dinner. Ben and Lily did not want to clean. They wanted to keep playing. They said no to their mom and ran away. Their mom was not happy. She said they had to listen and do what she asked. Ben and Lily hid under their enormous bed. They thought their mom could not find them there. They whispered and giggled and hoped she would forget. But their mom did not forget. She came into their bedroom and looked for them. She saw their feet sticking out from under the bed. She smiled and said, "I found you!" Ben and Lily were scared. They thought their mom would be mad at them. They crawled out from under the bed and said sorry. Their mom hugged them and said, "It's okay. I love you. But you still have to clean your bedroom. It's part of being a good kid. Can you do that for me?" Ben and Lily nodded. They felt bad for making their mom unhappy. They wanted to make her happy. They said, "Yes, mom. We can do that. We will help each other and clean our bedroom. We will support each other and be a good team." Their mom smiled and said, "That's wonderful. I'm proud of you. You are good kids. And good twins. Now, let's get to work. And then we can have dinner and dessert." Ben and Lily smiled too. They got up and started to pick up their toys, books, and pictures. They worked together and supported each other. They cleaned their bedroom and made it nice and tidy. They felt good and happy. They loved their mom and their bedroom. And they loved each other.

Once upon a time, there was a family with a mom, a dad, and a little girl named Lily. They lived in a small house. The mom and dad worked hard every day, but they were always happy. One day, the mom and dad decided to buy a dishwasher. They thought it would help them finish their work faster. When they got the dishwasher, they were excited. They put their dirty dishes in it and turned it on. The dishwasher was mild, and it did not make a lot of noise. Lily saw her mom and dad using the dishwasher and she wanted to help too. She started putting her toys in the dishwasher to clean them. Her mom and dad saw what she was doing and they laughed. They told Lily that the dishwasher was only for dishes, not toys. Lily learned that it is important to use things for what they are made for. And from that day on, the family was even happier because they all helped each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Mia. Mia had a lemon tree in her yard. The tree was rare because it gave big, sweet lemons. Mia loved her lemon tree very much. One day, a big, mean squirrel came to Mia's yard. The squirrel wanted to eat all the lemons from her tree. Mia saw the squirrel and she was sad. She didn't want to lose her special lemons. So, Mia had an idea. She said, "Mr. Squirrel, please do not eat all my lemons. I will give you some if you promise not to take all of them." The squirrel thought about it for a moment. The squirrel decided to surrender and not eat all the lemons. He agreed to Mia's idea. Mia was happy and the squirrel was happy too. They both shared the rare lemons from the tree. From that day on, Mia and the squirrel became good friends, and they always shared the sweet lemons from the rare lemon tree.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird. The bird was very special, because he could make things glow. He had a pot of magical oil which he would sprinkle around to make things glow. One day, the little yellow bird decided to use his special oil to make some flowers glow. He sprinkled the oil around their petals, and they started to sparkle in the sunlight. Everyone was very amazed! Everywhere the little yellow bird went, he used his magical oil and made things glow. The little yellow bird's home town was soon full of glowing things. Even the sun seemed to glow brighter when the little yellow bird was around. Everyone was very happy and excited. The little yellow bird was given a special medal to thank him for his special powers. Everyone cheered and applauded as he smiled and the magical oil sparkled around him.
