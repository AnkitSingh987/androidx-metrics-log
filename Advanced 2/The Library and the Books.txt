Once upon a time, there was a little library. In the library, there were many books. The books were very happy together. One day, it started to rain. The rain came inside the library. The books did not like the rain. The rain made the books hurt. They needed help. A big cover came to the library. The cover said, "I will help you, books." The cover put itself over the library. The rain could not hurt the books anymore. The books and the cover were happy. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. Lily was a happy girl who loved to celebrate with her friends. One day, Lily and her friends decided to have a party in the park. While they were at the park, Lily saw a scary-looking tree. It had big, dark branches that looked like hands. Lily's friends were scared, but Lily was brave. She went up to the tree and touched it with her hand. To her surprise, the tree was not scary at all. It was just a tree. Lily and her friends learned that sometimes things that look scary are not really scary. They had a great party and celebrated under the tree. From that day on, they were not scared of the tree anymore. The moral of the story is that you should not be scared of things just because they look scary.

Jill and her mom were walking in the park one day. Jill saw a patch of unusual-looking grass and asked her mom, "What is that?" Her mom said, "It's a surprise. We'll have to wait and see." They kept walking until they reached the patch of grass. Suddenly, a creature arrived. Jill was frightened at first, but then she noticed it was very friendly. The creature said, "I am here to give you a magical surprise." Jill was filled with anticipation. The creature asked Jill to come closer and gave her an unusual gift. Jill unwrapped the present and was so excited to find a magical butterfly. "Thank you!" said Jill. The creature smiled and then vanished. The grass patch that Jill found taught her that there can be something special hiding in the most unusual places.

Lily and Ben were playing in the park with their dolls. They liked to pretend that their dolls were having a tea party. They had a small table, some cups and plates, and a teapot. They filled the teapot with water from the fountain. "Would you like some tea, Miss Daisy?" Lily asked her doll. "Yes, please, Miss Lily. You are very kind," Ben said in a high voice for his doll. They poured some water into the cups and pretended to sip it. They smiled and laughed. But then, a big boy came to the park. He was rude. He saw Lily and Ben and their dolls. He wanted to play with them, but not in a nice way. "Hey, what are you doing?" he shouted. "That's a silly game. Dolls are for babies. Let me show you a better game." He ran to the fountain and filled the teapot with something else. It was poison. It was green and smelly. He did not tell Lily and Ben. He came back to the table and smiled. "Look, I have some special tea for you. It's very tasty. Try some," he said. He poured some poison into the cups and pushed them to Lily and Ben. Lily and Ben did not like the big boy. They did not like his smile. They did not like his voice. They did not like his tea. They smelled the poison and saw the green color. They knew it was bad. "No, thank you," Lily said. "We don't want your tea. It's not nice. It's poison. You are rude. Go away." "Yeah, go away," Ben said. "You are not our friend. You are mean. Leave us alone." They picked up their dolls and moved to another place. They did not want to play with the big boy. They wanted to play with their dolls. They wanted to have a nice tea party. They were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy pig who was wallowing in the mud. He enjoyed feeling the mud melt away as he moved around. One day, he decided he was too hot and needed to cool off. He decided to roll in the mud and felt it start to melt away under him. As the mud melted, he felt cooler and lay there contentedly. His friends, the cow and the sheep, watched him from a distance. They were very impressed by his lazy behaviour and thought it was a great way to cool off. Eventually, the sun went down and it got cooler. The lazy pig got up and shook off the mud. He snuggled down with his friends and they went to sleep feeling very content. The next day, the pig and his friends returned to the same spot, but the mud had all melted away. They were a bit sad, but knew it would be back soon enough.
