Once upon a time, there was a little house with a rough chimney. In the house lived a boy named Tim and his mom. Tim liked to play outside with his red ball. One day, Tim's ball went near the rough chimney. He saw a long string on the ground. Tim wanted to tie the string to the ball, so it would not go far away. He asked his mom to help him tie the string. Mom helped Tim tie the string to the ball. Now, Tim could play without losing his ball. They both laughed and played together near the rough chimney. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a brother and sister wanted to build a big tower with their blocks. They both thought it was very important to make the tallest tower ever. They started to build and build, putting one block on top of the other. The brother said, "I will put this block here." The sister said, "I will put this block there." They worked together and their tower got taller and taller. They were so happy. But then, a big wind came and blew the tower down. All the blocks fell on the floor. The brother and sister were very sad. They tried to build it again, but the wind kept blowing it down. They never got to build the tallest tower ever.

One day, a fast car named Speedy loved to speed on the road. Speedy was a little foolish sometimes. He didn't like to stop when he should. His best friend was a sweet girl named Sugar. Sugar liked to watch Speedy race, but she worried about him. One sunny day, Sugar told Speedy, "You need to be careful when you speed. You might get hurt." Speedy just laughed and said, "I am the fastest car. Nothing can hurt me." Sugar shook her head and said, "You are being foolish." The next day, Speedy went to race again. He was going very fast, but he remembered what Sugar said. This time, he slowed down when he needed to. He did not get hurt. Speedy learned that being foolish was not good. He thanked Sugar for helping him be safe.

Once upon a time, a red bird lived in a big tree. The red bird loved to gather things. One day, the red bird found a tool. The tool was shiny and new. The red bird was very happy. The red bird showed the tool to all its friends. The friends liked the tool too. They all played with the tool. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A little girl saw the red bird and the tool. She said, "That is my tool!" The red bird was surprised. The little girl took the tool back. The red bird was sad. But the little girl said, "We can share the tool!" So, they all played together and had a great time.

One day, Bill and Ben were playing together in their village. They were running around and laughing. Suddenly, Bill slipped on the grass and got hurt. He was very upset and started to cry. Ben was so sad to see his friend in pain. He knew he had to do something to help Bill. So he ran to the village shop and bought some special things to fix his friend. When Ben came back he told Bill not to worry anymore. Ben and Bill marked a big X in the ground, then Ben took out the bandages to cover up Bill's cuts and bruises. With some special lotion from the shop and Ben's help, Bill felt much better. The two friends smiled at each other, then went back to running around and playing again. From that day, the X in the ground was a special mark that reminded the boys of the time that their friendship was so strong that it could even heal a hurt.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly bunny named Wednesday. Wednesday was very old, but she still loved to hop and play with her friends. One sunny day, Wednesday went out to hop in the soft grass. She hopped and hopped, and she felt very happy. She saw her friends, a little bird and a small squirrel, and they all hopped and played together. After a long day of hopping and playing, Wednesday and her friends were tired. They said goodbye and went to their homes. Wednesday hopped back to her cozy hole, feeling happy and loved.
