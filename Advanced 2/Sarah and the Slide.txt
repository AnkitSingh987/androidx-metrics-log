Sarah was excited. She saw the big slide and ran over to it. She was so happy that she wanted to go down it right away. She put her hands on the slide and started to push. The slide was so fun. She felt the cool metal and heard the squeak as she pushed. The harder Sarah pushed, the faster the slide went. Finally, she reached the bottom and said, "Whee!" She was so excited, she wanted to go again. She put her hands on the slide and started to push. Sarah pushed and she pushed and before she knew it, she was at the top of the slide again. She looked down and smiled. She was so excited to go down the slide again. She pushed off the slide and giggled as she rushed to the bottom. Sarah loved going down the slide. Every time was just as much fun as the time before. She loved feeling the cool metal, hearing the squeak and feeling the wind as she quickly flew down the slide. She was so excited to go down the slide again and again.

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play games with his friends. They would play all day and have fun. Tom was very good at games and always wanted to win. He was competitive. One day, Tom saw an avocado. He had an idea for a new game. He called his friends and said, "Let's play a game! We will tie the avocado to a tree and see who can hit it with a ball." All his friends agreed and they started to play. Tom tried very hard to win. He threw the ball many times, but he could not hit the avocado. His friends laughed at him. Tom got angry and threw the ball harder. The ball hit the avocado and it fell down. But the avocado broke and made a big mess. Tom's friends were upset and left. Tom was sad and alone.

Once upon a time, there was a little house. This house was different because it could lean. It liked to lean from side to side. One day, a cat came to the house. The cat saw the house lean to the left. The cat was happy and played with the house. They had fun together. The next day, a dog came to the house. The dog saw the house lean to the right. The dog was happy and played with the house too. The cat, dog, and house all played together and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She loved to eat strawberries. One day, she saw a big strawberry on the ground. Mia was very happy. Mia picked up the strawberry and took it to her mommy. "Mommy, can I eat this strawberry?" she asked. Her mommy did not answer. Mia thought that meant yes, so she took a bite of the strawberry. Mia's mouth started to hurt. The strawberry was bad. She cried and cried. Her mommy came running to help her, but it was too late. The bad strawberry made Mia feel very sick.

Once upon a time, in a big, old house, there was a little, friendly spider. The spider made a pretty cobweb in the corner of a room. The cobweb grew bigger and bigger every day. The spider was very proud of it. One day, a big, strong ant came into the room. The ant saw the big cobweb and thought it was in his way. The ant said, "I don't like your cobweb, Spider. I want to fight you." The little spider was scared but did not want to fight. The spider said, "Please, Ant, let's not fight. We can share this room and be friends." The big ant thought about it and decided to give it a try. They both lived in the room together and became good friends. The ant helped the spider make the cobweb even bigger and prettier. The spider helped the ant find food. They were happy and never fought again. The moral of the story is that it is better to be friends and help each other than to fight.
