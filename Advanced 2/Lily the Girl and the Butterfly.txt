Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small, clean house with her mom and dad. Lily loved to play outside with her toys and her dog, Spot. One sunny day, Lily and Spot were playing in the garden. Lily saw a pretty, colorful butterfly. She asked the butterfly, "Where do you live?" The butterfly didn't answer, but it flew away. Lily and Spot followed the butterfly to see where it went. The butterfly led them to a beautiful, clean pond. Lily saw many fish in the pond. She asked the fish, "Do you know where the butterfly lives?" The fish didn't answer, but they swam around and played with Lily and Spot. They had a fun day by the clean pond, and Lily learned that sometimes, you don't need an answer to have a good time.

Once upon a time, there was a white cat. The cat had a small helmet. She loved to wear her helmet all day. One day, she met a big dog. The dog wanted to play with her. The cat was scared, but the dog urged her to play. The cat and the dog played all day. They had a lot of fun. The sun went down, and it was time to go home. The cat was happy she played with the dog. She knew her helmet kept her safe. The next day, the cat saw the dog again. But this time, the dog had a helmet too! The dog told the cat that he wanted to be safe like her. Now, they both wore helmets while they played together. The cat and the dog became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Sally who liked to bake. Every day, she would take out her big bowl and mix together flour, sugar, milk and butter to create yummy treats. One day, Sally baked an extra special treat. She made a delicious jail cake! The cake was tall, with thick frosting and decorations of bars and a mini jail cell. Sally took her cake and ran to the nearby jail. She wanted to share her cake with all the prisoners. When she arrived, Sally showed the jail guard the cake. He smiled and said, "It smells good. Can I have a slice?" Sally smiled and said, "You bet! Everyone should get to enjoy my regular jail cake." With that, Sally passed out pieces to the guard and the prisoners. They all enjoyed the sweet treat and thanked Sally. She was happy to share her special creation with others.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys. One day, he wanted to find his toy car. He knew it was in the cabinet. But, the cabinet was very high. Tim was too small to reach it. Tim tried to do something. He got a chair to stand on. But, the chair was still too short. Tim felt sad. He really wanted his toy car. He needed help. Then, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw him trying to reach the cabinet. She smiled and helped him. She got the toy car for Tim. Tim was very happy. He said "thank you" to his mom. Now, they could play together.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely fish. This fish loved to swim all day. One day, while swimming, the fish saw a youth playing near the water. The youth looked lonely too. The fish wanted to help the youth, so it swam closer. The youth saw the fish and smiled. They started to play together in the water. They were both happy and not lonely anymore. They became good friends. One day, while they were playing, something unexpected happened. The fish started to change! It turned into a beautiful bird! The youth was surprised. Now, they could play in the water and fly in the sky together. The lonely fish and the youth were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. He loved to play and jump all day. One day, Tim found a big skull in his yard. The skull was very old and had been there for a long time. Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, look what I found!" His mom looked at the skull and said, "Wow, that is very old! We should raise it up so everyone can see it." So, Tim and his mom got a big stick and some rope. They tied the skull to the stick and raised it up high. All the kids in the neighborhood came to see the skull. They thought it was very cool. Tim felt proud of his find and had a lot of fun showing it to his friends. The skull became a special part of their playtime, and they all had many lively adventures with it. And they all lived happily ever after.
