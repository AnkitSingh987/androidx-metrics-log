Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot had four paws and a wagging tail. He loved to run and play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Spot met a new friend named Fluffy. Fluffy was a cute cat with soft fur. Spot said, "Hi Fluffy, do you want to play with me?" Fluffy said, "Yes, I would love to play with you, Spot!" Spot and Fluffy played a game where they had to number their paws. They counted, "One, two, three, four!" They laughed and had so much fun with their paws. They were very happy to be friends and play together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play and discover new things. One sunny day, Lily put on her purple dress and went for a walk with her mom. As they walked, they found a pass in the forest. Lily was excited and wanted to see what was on the other side. Her mom said, "Let's go and discover what's there, Lily!" They walked and walked until they reached the other side of the pass. When they got there, they saw a big, beautiful purple flower. Lily was so happy to discover the flower. She picked it up and gave it to her mom. They both smiled and went back home, feeling happy about their special day in the forest.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bright sun. The sun would wake up every morning and shine its light all around. It made the sky blue and the grass green. The sun loved to make the world bright and happy. One day, the sun saw a dark cave. The cave was very sad because it was always dark inside. The sun wanted to help the cave, so it called out, "Hello, cave! I want to make you bright too! Can I come in and shine my light?" The cave was happy to hear the sun's words. It said, "Yes, sun! Please come in and make me bright!" So, the sun went inside the cave and shined its light. The cave became bright and happy, just like the sun. From that day on, the sun and the cave became good friends. Every morning, the sun would wake up and shine its light inside the cave. The cave was no longer dark and sad, but bright and full of joy. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, Bob's Mommy said they were going to the store. Bob was so excited! He wanted a new toy and he knew Mommy would get him something nice. When they got to the store, Bob found a shiny red truck in the toy aisle. He didn't have enough money to buy it, so he asked Mommy if he could get it. She said no, not today. Bob was so sad! He was about to cry, when Mommy had a better idea. She said if he did a good job taking care of the yard, he could get the new truck one day. Bob was happy to work in the yard and help his family. He worked really hard, pulling weeds and tending the garden. After a few weeks, Bob's Mommy said they were going back to the store. She said this time, they would buy the shiny red truck Bob wanted. Bob was so excited! He couldn't believe his hard work had paid off. He rushed over to the toy aisle and grabbed the truck. He hugged Mommy and thanked her for being so kind. Thanks to his hard work, Bob didn't have to suffer anymore. He could finally have the new truck he worked so hard for!

Once upon a time, there was a lonely turkey. The turkey lived in a big farm. The turkey had no friends. One day, the turkey had a plan. The turkey wanted to build a big house. The turkey started to build the house. The turkey found wood and put it all together. But a big wind came. The wind blew the house down. The turkey was sad. The turkey tried again. This time, the turkey built a stronger house. The wind came again, but the house did not fall. The turkey was happy. Soon, other animals came to see the house. They wanted to be friends with the turkey. The turkey was not lonely anymore.
