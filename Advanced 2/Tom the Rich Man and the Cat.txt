Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. Tom had a big house and many things. He liked to travel to far places. He always took his passport with him. One day, Tom saw a little cat outside his house. The cat was cold and wet. It was trembling. Tom felt sad for the cat. He wanted to help the cat. He thought, "Maybe the cat can come with me on my next trip." The next day, Tom took the cat and his passport. They went to a warm place with a big sun. The cat stopped trembling. It was happy and warm. Tom was happy too. They had a great time together.

Once upon a time, there was a dirty automobile. It was very sad because it was always dirty. No one wanted to take it for a ride. The automobile wanted to be clean and shiny like the other cars. One day, a kind girl saw the dirty automobile. She felt bad for the car. She decided to take a bucket and a sponge to help clean the car. The girl scrubbed and scrubbed until the automobile was clean and shiny. Now, the automobile was very happy. The girl took the clean automobile for a ride around the town. Everyone saw how beautiful the car looked. The automobile was no longer sad and now had a friend who took care of it. They lived happily ever after.

One day, a little frog named Tim was slow. He liked to gather rocks by the pond. Tim had a friend named Sam. Sam was a fast frog. They liked to play together. One day, Tim said, "Let's gather rocks!" Sam said, "Yes, but I will gather more!" They both went to the pond. They were very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim found a big, shiny rock. It was not a rock, it was a magic rock! Tim touched the rock and he became a fast frog too. Sam was surprised. Now, they both could gather rocks fast and play together even more.

Jimmy was feeling playful one day, so he decided to go outside and count his heels. He would take one big step and then count the heel of his left foot. He enjoyed counting his heels, as it always filled him with joy. As he was counting his heels, he noticed something strange in the sky. It was a bright light, glowing in the dark sky above him. He was curious, so he went to his parents to ask them what was happening. His mom smiled and said, “It’s the moon, Jimmy. It’s a special part of the sky that comes out at night.” Jimmy was so excited, he ran outside to count his heels and look at the moon. He could see the moon in the sky, smiling back down at him. He was amazed at how it was both so big and so far away. The next night, Jimmy went outside to count his heels again. This time, he noticed something different about the sky. It was filled with twinkling stars, each of them sparkling like a diamond in the night sky. He knew now that the moon and stars were special friends of his, always watching over him and keeping him safe. He was so ecstatic that he shouted with joy. Jimmy continued counting his heels and looking up the amazing sky. He knew he was in for something special and he couldn't wait to find out what it was.

One day, a boy named Tim went out to play. It was a sunny day, and Tim wanted to jump in the puddles. He put on his shoes and ran outside. As he jumped in the puddles, his shoes got heavy and wet. Tim liked how the water splashed when he jumped. "Tim, don't soak your shoes!" his mom called from the door. "You won't have dry shoes to wear later." But Tim didn't listen. He kept jumping and laughing. Then, something strange happened. A big frog jumped out of a puddle and onto Tim's shoe. Tim was surprised and stopped jumping. The frog looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for making my home bigger with the splashes. I will help you dry your shoes." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, frog." The frog waved his hands, and Tim's shoes were dry again. Tim went home with dry shoes, and he was happy. From that day on, Tim and the frog were good friends.
