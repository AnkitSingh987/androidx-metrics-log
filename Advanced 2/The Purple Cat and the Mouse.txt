Once upon a time, there was a big purple cat. The cat had a staff. The staff was very long. The cat liked to walk around with the staff. One day, the cat lost the staff. The cat was sad and had a big frown. The cat looked everywhere for the staff. The cat looked under the bed, in the box, and behind the tree. The cat could not find the staff. The cat was very sad. The cat did not want to be without the staff. Then, the cat saw a little mouse. The mouse had the staff! The cat was surprised. The mouse said, "I found your staff. I wanted to play with it." The cat was happy. The cat and the mouse became friends. They played with the staff together. The cat's frown went away.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She loved to go on a safari with her mom and dad. They would see many animals like lions, elephants, and birds. One day, while they were on the safari, Lily saw a little monkey. The monkey was trying to balance on a thin branch. Lily watched and laughed as the monkey wobbled back and forth. The monkey saw Lily and wanted to be her friend. So, it jumped down from the tree and walked over to her. Lily and the monkey played together all day. They had so much fun on their safari adventure, and Lily made a new friend.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play in the woods. They found a big pine tree with many cones and needles. They wanted to record their voices and pretend they were talking to the tree. "Hello, pine tree, how are you today?" Lily said into her toy phone. She pressed a button to record her voice. "I'm fine, thank you, Lily. You are very kind and sweet," Tom said into his toy phone. He pressed a button to record his voice. They laughed and took turns to record more questions and answers. They did not see a big bear that was sleeping behind the pine tree. The bear was hungry and angry. He did not like the noise that the children made. The bear woke up and roared. He saw Lily and Tom and ran towards them. The children were very scared and anxious. They dropped their toy phones and ran away. But the bear was faster and caught them. He ate them up and went back to sleep. The moral of the story is: Do not disturb the animals in the woods. They might be dangerous and hurt you. Be careful and quiet when you play outside.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very intelligent. He loved to learn and play with his toys. One day, Tim found a bottle of oil in the kitchen. He didn't know what it was for, but he thought it looked interesting. Tim took the oil to his room and decided to stand his toy car on it. He thought the car would slide down the oil and go very fast. Tim said to his car, "Are you ready to go fast?" The toy car didn't say anything, but Tim knew it was ready. Tim poured the oil on the floor and put his toy car at the top. The car slid down the oil and went very fast, just like he thought! Tim laughed and clapped his hands. He was so happy that his intelligent idea worked. Tim played with the oil and his toy car all day long.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little house, there lived a happy family. Mom, Dad, and little Timmy loved to cook yummy food together. They had a big oven in their kitchen. The oven was very good at making food hot and tasty. One day, Mom and Dad wanted to make a surprise for Timmy. They put a cake in the oven for him. Timmy was playing with his toys and did not know about the cake. Mom and Dad wanted to make sure the cake stayed a secret. But, Timmy saw the oven was on and wanted to help. He opened the oven door to look inside. The cake was not ready yet, and the hot air came out. The cake did not cook right, and it was spoiled. Timmy was sad that he spoiled the cake. Mom and Dad saw that Timmy was sad. They hugged him and said it was okay. They all decided to make a new cake together. They used the oven again, and this time, they made the best cake ever. The cheerful family ate the cake together and had a fun time.
