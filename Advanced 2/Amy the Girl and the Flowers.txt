Title: Amy the Girl and the Flowers

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy liked to play outside with her friends. She loved to see the pretty flowers and the big trees. One day, Amy saw a little cat. The cat was very careful when it walked. It did not want to step on the flowers. Amy thought the cat was very cute. She wanted to give the cat some love. Amy went to the cat and gave it a big hug. The cat was very happy. It purred and rubbed its head on Amy's leg. Amy knew that the cat loved her too. They became the best of friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was very troubled, because she couldn't find a tomato in the garden. No matter how hard she looked, there were no tomatoes to be found. One day, the little girl decided to take a rest under the big tree. As she rested, she heard a strange sound. It sounded like someone was munching on something delicious. Could it be a tomato?! The little girl looked up and saw a big, fat squirrel eating a red tomato. The little girl was so excited. She had finally found a tomato! The little girl was no longer troubled. She smiled as she watched the squirrel eat the red tomato. She had finally found the thing she'd been looking for.

Once there was a little girl named Essie. She was 3 years old and she loved to play. One day she was playing with her dolls when her mom was shaking the dirty TV. Essie ran to her mom and asked why she was shaking the TV. Her mom said the TV was dirty so she had to shake it. Essie thought that was funny and wanted to help. So Essie stood up on a chair and started shaking the TV. She shook it so hard that mom had to help her so the TV wouldn't break. When they both stopped shaking, Essie looked at the TV and saw that it was much cleaner! Mom was so proud of Essie for helping clean the TV. From then on, Essie would help mom shake the TV every week.

Rosie was feeling jolly one day. She wanted to do something fun so she decided to go pick the biggest novel she could find. She went to the library and looked around. Then she spotted the most jolly novel! It was bright pink with a big, sparkly yellow star on it. Rosie was so excited, she grabbed it right away. When Rosie got home, she opened the novel and started to read. It was about a group of animals that had a lot of adventures together. Rosie smiled and laughed as she read, it was such a fun story! After reading the novel, Rosie was so happy. She was so glad she had picked a fun, jolly novel! From then on she always made sure to pick a jolly novel whenever she wanted to read something.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside in nature. One day, she found a big tree with a small hole in it. Lily thought, "I wonder what's inside." The next day, Lily went to the tree with her friend, Tom. They looked at the hole and saw something shiny inside. Tom said, "Let's come back tomorrow with a long stick to get it out." Lily agreed, and they went home. The next day, Lily and Tom came back with a long stick. They put the stick in the hole and pulled out a shiny, gold key. They were so happy! They knew they found something special. From that day on, Lily and Tom loved to explore nature together, looking for more hidden treasures.

One day, a thoughtful cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a big box with a hole in it. Tom was curious and looked inside. He saw a lot of mail. Tom thought, "I want to help put the mail in the right homes." So, he picked up the mail with his mouth and walked to the first house. He knocked on the door and a little girl opened it. "Hi, I have your mail," said Tom. The little girl looked at him and said, "Thank you, cat. But you should not touch our mail." Tom felt sad but still wanted to help. He went to the next house and knocked on the door. A big dog opened it and said, "Go away, cat! You should not touch our mail." Tom felt very sad now. He went back to the big box and shut the hole. He thought that he was being helpful, but no one liked what he did. Tom went back home with a heavy heart and never tried to help with the mail again.
