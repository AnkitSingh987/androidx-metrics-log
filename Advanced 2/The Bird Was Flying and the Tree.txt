One day, a small bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big tree. The tree could support the bird's nest. The bird was grateful for the tree. The tree was happy to help the bird. A big wind came. The wind wanted to push the tree down. The bird was sad. The bird said, "Please don't push my tree down. It is my home." The wind did not listen. The wind kept trying to push the tree down. The bird asked its friends for help. The friends came to support the tree too. They all pushed back against the wind. The wind got tired and stopped. The bird and its friends saved the tree. The tree was still a good sight for the bird's nest. The bird was very grateful to its friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Zoomy. Zoomy loved to drive fast on the road. One day, Zoomy met a big truck named Biggy. Biggy was carrying many different minerals. Zoomy asked, "Biggy, what are those different things you have?" Biggy replied, "These are minerals. They are special rocks from the ground. I am taking them to a place far away." Zoomy wanted to help Biggy. So, they decided to drive together. Zoomy drove fast, and Biggy drove slow. They talked and laughed as they drove down the road. At last, they reached the place where the minerals needed to go. Biggy said, "Thank you, Zoomy, for helping me drive." Zoomy smiled and said, "You're welcome, Biggy. It was fun to drive with you!" From that day on, Zoomy and Biggy became best friends. They loved to drive together and help each other with their different jobs. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a penguin named Poppy. Poppy lived in a cold place with her penguin friends. They liked to play games and eat fish. One day, Poppy felt uncomfortable. She wanted to find a warm place. Poppy walked far away from her friends. She found a big box. Poppy went inside the box. The box was warm and nice. Poppy felt happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The big box started to move. Poppy was scared. The big box stopped moving. Poppy went outside the box. Her friends were there. They said, "Poppy, you vanished!" They told her the box was magic. It took her back to her friends. Poppy was happy to be with her friends again. They played games and ate fish together.

Once upon a time, there was a little ghost named Boo. Boo lived in a big house with his ghost friends. They liked to play hide and seek. Boo was very good at hiding because he was very small and fragile. One day, Boo found a magic potion in the big house. He drank it and started to shrink. He became even smaller and smaller. Boo was so happy because now he could hide in even tinier places. His friends would never find him! Boo and his friends played hide and seek again. They looked and looked but could not find Boo. He was hiding in a tiny hole in the wall, thanks to the magic potion. When Boo finally came out, his friends were so surprised! They all laughed and played together, happy that Boo was the best at hiding.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a smelly crayon. She wanted to color with it, but it smelled so bad! Lucy said, "Yuck! I don't like this crayon. It is smelly!" She showed the crayon to her friend Tom. Tom sniffed the crayon and said, "Ew! That is a smelly crayon. Let's ask Mom how to fix it." They went to Lucy's mom and asked for help. Lucy's mom said, "Don't panic. We can clean the crayon and make it smell better." Lucy and Tom helped Mom clean the crayon with soap and water. They scrubbed it until the smell was gone. Now, the crayon was clean and ready to use. Lucy and Tom were happy they could color with the crayon without it being smelly anymore. They learned that sometimes, asking for help can fix a problem.
