One day, a little boy named Tim was watching his favorite cartoon on TV. He loved the funny animals and the silly things they did. Tim laughed and clapped his hands as he watched the cartoon animals play. In the cartoon, the animals were playing with colorful balls. They would throw the balls high in the air and scatter them all around. Tim thought it was so much fun and wanted to play too. He went to his room and found his own colorful balls. Tim felt a little helpless at first because he didn't know how to scatter the balls like the cartoon animals. But then, he had an idea. He put all the balls in a big box and shook it really hard. The balls flew out of the box and scattered all over the room. Tim laughed and clapped his hands, just like when he watched the cartoon. Now, he could play just like the funny animals on TV.

Once upon a time a young girl called Amy was sitting in her garden and she was very sad. She was weeping because she didn't have enough muscle to play with her friends. "Why are you crying, Amy?" her dad asked, seeing her sobbing in the grass. Amy looked up at her dad with tears in her eyes and replied sadly, "I want to play, but I don't have the muscle to keep up with my friends". Her dad hugged her tightly and said, "Don't worry, I have something special for you". He brought out a bright green kite and held it up for her to see. Amy gasped as she saw the beautiful green kite fluttering in the breeze. She wiped away her tears and her face lit up with excitement. "Now you don't need muscle to fly your own kite," her dad said with a smile. Amy smiled back and grabbed the kite, running up and down the garden with it. Later that afternoon, the sky was filled with the bright green kite flying through the air. Amy never wept again because she had a wonderful new way to play.

One day, a large dog named Max went for a jog in the park. He liked to run and play with his friends. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. It was a perfect day. As Max was jogging, he saw his friend, a cat named Lily. Lily was sitting under a tree. "Hi Lily!" said Max. "Do you want to play with me?" Lily looked up at Max and said, "No, thank you. I am serving tea to my dolls." Max was curious and went to see what Lily was doing. She had a small table with cups and plates. She poured pretend tea from a little pot. "Would you like some tea, Max?" asked Lily. Max nodded and sat down to join the tea party. They had a great time together, serving tea and eating pretend cake. After the tea party, Max and Lily went back to jogging and playing in the park. They were happy to be friends and enjoyed their day together.

Once there was a little girl called Lucy who lived near the ocean. Every day she would go to the beach and play. One day, Lucy noticed a net lying near the water. She picked it up and held it in her hands. It was so heavy! "What a heavy net!", she said. Suddenly, she heard a voice from behind. "It was my net!", said the voice. Lucy looked behind and saw another little girl. She had long blonde hair and big blue eyes. "What are you going to do with it?" asked the little girl. Lucy thought for a moment and suddenly had an idea. "Why don't we twist it together, so it won't be so heavy?", said Lucy. The little girl agreed and so the two of them twisted the net tightly. Together they were able to carry the net much more easily. The two little girls walked along the beach, carrying the net. The moral of the story is that if we work together, we can do much more than if we try to do something alone.
