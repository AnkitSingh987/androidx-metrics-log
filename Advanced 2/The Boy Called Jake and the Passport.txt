Once upon a time there was a boy called Jake. He was three and he was very excited, because he was going on holiday. Joe asked his mum, "Do I have a passport?" His mum smiled. "Yes, you do. It's in the drawer." Jake ran to the drawer and opened it. He saw a blue passport and his face lit up. He hugged the passport tight. When he arrived at the airport, it was time for him to show the passport to the lady at the desk. The lady said, "Let's see how flexible you are". Jake smiled and showed her all the silly poses he could do. The lady laughed and said, "You win! You can have your passport now." Jake was so happy. He was going on an adventure. He waved goodbye and was off.

One day, a big cat and a small dog were playing in the park. They were having a lot of fun. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Then, a rude boy came to the park. He screamed loudly at the big cat and the small dog. The big cat said, "Why are you screaming at us? We are just having fun!" The rude boy did not listen and screamed again. The big cat and the small dog decided to be kind to the rude boy. They asked him, "Do you want to play with us?" The rude boy stopped screaming and started playing with them. Soon, they all became friends. The moral of the story is to be kind even when others are rude.

Once upon a time there was a boy. His name was Jack and he had a very special pet. It was a unique trap. Jack loved the trap so much he treated it like a friend. One day, Jack decided to take the trap on an adventure. He walked with the trap through the woods, until he came across a small stream. At the stream he thought his trap might like to play. Jack was so excited, he threw the trap into the stream. It floated down the stream and disappeared! Jack was so sad and cried out for help. Just then a big, friendly beast appeared. He asked Jack what was wrong and Jack told him what happened. The beast said he could help and gave Jack the trap back. Jack was so relieved and grateful he hugged the beast. So the trap was saved and Jack and his pet were once again happy. They never went on an adventure without one another again!

Once upon a time, there was a star that was hidden in the sky. It lay high up, twinkling and calling out to the children below. The children would look up and wonder what the star was up to. One day, a little girl named Bella looked up at the star and said, "I wish I could go there". A little bird heard her wish and flew up to the star. When he arrived, he asked the star if it would grant the little girl's wish. The star said it was too dangerous for Bella to come up, so the bird promised to keep it a secret. He flew back down, and told Bella what the star said. She was sad, but thanked the bird for watching out for her. The days and weeks passed, but the star never faded from the sky. Bella soon forgot her wish and moved on with her life. Until one day, the little bird showed up with a surprise. He had gone to the star and asked if it would grant Bella's wish. The star had said yes! The star was hidden no more, and it invited Bella to visit. Bella said yes with a smile, as she realized her wish had come true. The star was so excited it began to twinkle brighter than ever before.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was not feeling well. His mom said he needs to take medicine to feel better. Spot did not like the taste of medicine, but he knew it would help him. Every day, Spot's mom would give him the medicine and say, "Repeat after me: 'I will take my medicine because it helps me feel better.'" Spot would repeat, "I will take my medicine because it helps me feel better." He took the medicine and slowly started to feel better. One day, Spot went to a fair with his friends. They had so much fun playing games and eating yummy treats. Spot was happy he took his medicine, because now he felt well enough to play with his friends at the fair.
