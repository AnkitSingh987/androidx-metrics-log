Title: Amy the Girl and the Pot

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a toy pot. This pot was very special. It was her original pot. One day, Amy took her pot outside to play. She saw a cat. The cat saw the pot and liked it too. The cat said, "Can I come play with your pot?" Amy said, "Yes, cat, you can come play with my pot." So the cat came and they played with the pot together. They had a lot of fun. Then, it was time to go home. Amy said, "Bye, cat. Thanks for playing with my pot." The cat said, "Bye, Amy. I had fun with your original pot." And they both went home, happy from their fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Tom. He had a big factory where he made toys. One day, he decided to visit his factory and see how the toys were made. When he went to the factory, he saw many people working hard to make the toys. Tom was very happy. He saw a big box in the corner and thought, "I will open this box later." After watching the workers, Tom went to the big box. He opened it and found a big, shiny toy car. The workers had made it just for him! Tom was so happy and thanked them. The visit to the factory was very special.

Once upon a time, there was a spike. It was very organized. It liked to learn new things. Every day, the spike tried to learn something new. One day, it tried to learn something too hard. It was very difficult, and the spike couldn't do it. The spike started to get angry and frustrated. It tried and tried, but it couldn't do it. It started to get even more angry. The spike became so angry, it started to shake and hit the ground. Suddenly, there was an earthquake and everything fell down. The spike was destroyed and had to be thrown away. That's what happened when the spike tried to learn something too hard. The bad ending was that the spike was destroyed.

One day, a friendly cat named Tom went to play outside. He saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Tom was very good at balance. He climbed the tree very carefully. When he got to the top, he saw a little bird. The bird said, "Hi, I'm Bob! What are you doing up here?" Tom replied, "I like to climb trees and balance on the branches. It's fun!" Bob told Tom about his favorite food, sauce. Tom wanted to try it too. So, they went to Bob's house. Bob's mom gave Tom some sauce to taste. Tom loved it! They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a clever little girl called Jenny. Every day she combed her hair before she went to school. One day Jenny was standing in the bathroom and looking in the mirror, when she decided to change her hair. She was feeling adventurous and wanted to stand out from her friends. Jenny took her comb and made a part on the side of her head. She experimented with it, trying out different hairstyles. She felt so pleased with herself. Suddenly she heard a voice, and it was her mum. "Jenny, what are you doing?" her mum asked. "I'm changing my hair," Jenny answered proudly. "Oh, that's clever! You look very cute with your new style," her mum said. Jenny smiled, feeling very happy that her mum had noticed her new look. She put her comb back in the drawer and went off to school, feeling very pleased with her new style.

Once upon a time, there was a big peach. It was in a tree. A boy named Tim saw the peach. He wanted to eat it. One day, Tim went to the tree to get the peach. But the peach was gone! It did disappear. Tim was sad. He wanted to find the peach. Tim looked for the peach. He saw a squirrel. The squirrel had the peach! The squirrel was nice. It gave the peach back to Tim. Tim was happy. They ate the peach together.
