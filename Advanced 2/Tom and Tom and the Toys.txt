Once upon a time there were two friends: Tom and Tom. Tom was a very gifted boy, and Tom was not as gifted. Tom was very generous, and he liked to store things in his shed, such as toys and old television sets. One day, Tom asked Tom if he wanted to take one of his old television sets and store it in his own shed. Tom eagerly agreed, so the two friends went to Tom's shed and found an old television set. Tom was so excited to have a television of his own that he forgot to ask Tom if it was okay to take it. He rushed home with the television set and put it in his room. The next day, when Tom came to visit him, Tom was very upset that he had taken the television without asking. Tom told him that it was not okay to take things without permission, and the two friends had an argument. In the end, Tom realised that taking things without permission is wrong, and Tom was really sad that he had caused an argument with his friend. He decided to put the television in Tom's shed back and apologised to him. The story has a bad ending, but a very important moral value - always ask for permission before taking something that doesn't belong to you!

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the fancy store with his mom. They needed to buy vegetables for dinner. Tim saw a big carrot and wanted it. He asked his mom, "Can we get this big carrot, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. We need to buy other vegetables too." Tim was sad, but he helped his mom pick more vegetables. They put them in the cart and went to pay. While they were waiting in line, Tim saw a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. He asked his mom, "Can I have the toy gun, please?" His mom said, "No, we came here for vegetables, not toys." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. They paid for the vegetables and went home. Tim helped his mom cook dinner, and even though he didn't get the toy gun, he felt happy eating the vegetables with his family.

One day, a polite plane named Pete was flying in the sky. He loved to play games with his friends. Today, he wanted to play hide and seek with his best friend, a little bird named Bella. Pete said, "Bella, let's play hide and seek. I will hide and you will find me, okay?" Bella was excited and said, "Yes, Pete! I love this game!" Pete went to hide behind a big cloud. He waited and waited for Bella to find him. Bella looked up and down, left and right. Finally, she found Pete behind the big cloud. "I found you, Pete!" she said with a big smile. Now, it was Bella's turn to hide. She found a spot inside a tree. Pete looked for Bella, but he could not find her. He called out, "Bella, where are you?" Bella giggled and said, "Here I am, Pete!" She came out of the tree and they both laughed. Pete and Bella played hide and seek all day long. They had so much fun together. When the sun went down, they said goodnight and went to their homes. Pete and Bella could not wait to play together again the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a wide island. On the island lived a big, happy bear. The bear liked to play with his friends, the birds and the fish. One day, the bear wanted to make a big cake. He asked his friends to help. The birds helped to add eggs, and the fish helped to add water. Together, they mixed everything in a big bowl. The cake was very yummy. The bear and his friends had a fun party. They ate the cake and played all day long. The wide island was filled with happiness and laughter.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Blue. Blue lived in a high tree. Every day, he would wake up and sing. One day, Blue woke up late. "Oh no! I missed the time to sing!" he said. He tried to sing, but the other birds were not there to sing with him. Blue felt sad. Blue went to find his friend, Cat. "Cat, I need help. I woke up late and missed the time to sing," said Blue. Cat thought for a moment and said, "Let's ask the other birds to sing with you now." Blue and Cat asked the other birds, and they all agreed to sing together. Blue was happy again, and they all sang their beautiful songs.
