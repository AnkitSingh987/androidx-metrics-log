Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. He rode it every day. One day, Tim saw a big jug by the road. It was a very big jug. Tim was curious about the jug. Tim stopped his bike and looked at the jug. He was sad because the jug was empty. He wanted to find something to put in the jug. Then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew down and landed on the jug. The bird looked at Tim and said, "Don't be sad. I have a gift for you." The bird sang a beautiful song. The song made Tim very happy. The jug was not empty anymore. It was now filled with the bird's beautiful song. Tim and the bird became good friends and they rode the bike together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was three years old and loved to talk to her friends. One day, Sarah was telling her friends about the new park in town. She mentioned that nuts were her favorite snack. "Yum! Nuts are so much fun," her friend told her. Sarah was excited. She said, "I know! I can't wait to go to the park and play on the swings. We can bring the nuts with us and eat them while we play!" Then they all decided to go to the park. Everyone had a great time playing on the swings and eating nuts. When they got home, they told all their other friends about the park and how much fun they had. It wasn't long before the park became popular with all the children in the neighbourhood. Sarah was proud that she was the one who had mentioned nuts to her friends and started the trend of visiting the park.

Once upon a time, there was a soft cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy vehicle. She would push it all around the house, making it go fast and slow. One day, Kitty's friend, a little boy named Tim, came over to play. Tim wanted to play with the toy vehicle too. Kitty did not want to share at first, but then she thought, "I will let Tim play with my toy vehicle." As they played together, something unexpected happened. The toy vehicle started to grow bigger and bigger! Soon, it was big enough for Kitty and Tim to ride in. They looked at each other, surprised and excited. Kitty and Tim climbed into the big toy vehicle and went on a fun adventure all around the house. They laughed and played together, having the best time ever. And from that day on, Kitty always let Tim play with her toy vehicle, because sharing made their playtime even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree. It was very big and strong. The tree was in a park with many other trees. But this tree was the tallest of all. One day, a little bird came to the tall tree. The bird wanted to act like a big bird. It tried to carry a big weight with its beak. The weight was very heavy. The bird wanted to show the other birds how strong it was. The tall tree saw the little bird trying hard to carry the weight. The tree wanted to help the bird. The tree shook its branches and the weight fell on a big leaf. The little bird was happy and said thank you to the tall tree. The bird and the tree became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a slow wolf. The wolf was not like the other fast wolves. He would always complain that he could not catch any food. One day, the slow wolf saw a little rabbit. The rabbit was fast, but the wolf thought he could catch it. He tried his best, but the rabbit ran away. The wolf was sad and complained again. Later, the wolf saw the rabbit again. This time, the rabbit was stuck in a hole. The slow wolf walked up to the rabbit and helped it out. The rabbit was so thankful that it gave the wolf some food. The wolf was happy and did not complain anymore.
