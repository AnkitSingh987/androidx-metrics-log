Once there was a little puppy who always wanted to play. One day she wanted to play tag, but she couldn't find anyone to play with. As the puppy looked around, she suddenly heard something. It sounded like singing! She followed the sound and found a lively bird on a branch. The puppy was so excited to find a playmate. She barked and the bird flew down from the tree. "Let's play!" said the bird. The puppy thought it was a great idea and they both started to play tag. The puppy was having so much fun. But then the bird told her there was something even more fun they could do. He said they could play with cream! The puppy wasn't really sure what cream was, but she believed the bird when he said it would be fun. So the puppy followed the bird to the kitchen, and they soon found a jar of cream. The puppy and the bird spent the whole day playing in the cream, running around and laughing. The little puppy was so happy to have found such a lively friend in the bird. It was the best day ever!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia was very excited because today she was going to learn how to sew. Her mom gave her a big needle to use. Mia was so happy and couldn't wait to start. Mia's mom said, "Listen, Mia. You need to be very careful with the needle. It is sharp and can hurt you." Mia nodded and promised to be careful. She started to sew a pretty dress for her doll. As Mia sewed, a tiny mouse came out from a hole in the wall. The mouse saw the shiny needle and thought it was a toy. The mouse took the needle and ran back to its hole. Mia looked around and saw that her needle was gone. She was sad and didn't know what to do. Mia's mom saw her looking sad and asked what was wrong. Mia told her mom about the missing needle. They both looked around and found the mouse's hole. They saw the mouse playing with the needle like it was a toy. Mia and her mom laughed and were not mad at the mouse. They gave the mouse a small piece of cheese as a thank you for making them laugh. The mouse gave the needle back to Mia, and she finished sewing the pretty dress for her doll. They all became friends and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tom. Tom was a normal boy who liked to play outside. One day, he found a big flashlight in his room. He was very happy. Tom wanted to show his flashlight to his friend, Sam. So, he ran to Sam's house and knocked on the door. "Sam, look what I found!" Tom said. Sam came out and saw the flashlight. "Wow, Tom! That's a big flashlight!" Sam said. That night, Tom and Sam went outside to play with the flashlight. They played hide and seek in the dark. Tom used the flashlight to find Sam. They had so much fun. Tom was happy that he could gain a new toy and share it with his friend.

Jimmy was a very happy little boy. He loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Jimmy was playing and he found a very big lollipop. It was pink and it looked delicious. He wanted to eat it right away. But then his mother said, "Jimmy, it's important for you to ignore that lollipop. You should save it for later." Jimmy didn't really want to listen, but he did as his mother said and ignored the lollipop. The next day, Jimmy was very excited when he woke up. He had a feeling that something special was about to happen. He went downstairs and saw a surprise party his mother had prepared for him. Everyone was there, including his friends from outside. Jimmy was so excited to see them. His mother had also made lollipops for the party. Jimmy was so happy! He couldn't believe his mother had remembered to save the lollipop for him. Jimmy was so grateful and he hugged his mother. He knew how important it was to listen to her and ignore the lollipop the day before.
