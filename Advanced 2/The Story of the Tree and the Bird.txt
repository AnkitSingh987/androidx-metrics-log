Once upon a time, there was a big, old tree. This tree was very crazy. It liked to dance and sing all day. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird wanted to build a nest. The bird said, "Old tree, may I build my nest here?" The tree said, "Yes, but you must dance and sing with me!" The bird agreed, and they danced and sang together. They had so much fun. One day, a bossy squirrel came to the tree. The squirrel tried to order them around. "Stop dancing and singing!" said the squirrel. But the old tree and the bird did not listen. They kept dancing and singing. The squirrel got tired and left. They lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. She liked to run and jump in the park. One day, the sky turned gray and rain started to fall. The rain was rough, and it made Sue very sad. Sue went inside her house and started to weep. She did not like the rain because it made her wet and cold. Her mom saw her weep and came to give her a hug. "Don't worry, Sue," her mom said. "The rain will stop soon, and you can play outside again." Sue waited and waited for the rain to stop. Finally, the sun came out and the rain went away. Sue was happy again. She put on her shoes and went outside to play. She ran and jumped in the park, just like before. Sue knew that even if the rain was rough, it would always go away, and she could play outside again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to learn new things. One day, she went with her mom to pick blueberries in the forest. Blueberries were Amy's favorite fruit. Amy's mom said, "Amy, it is important to only pick the blue ones." Amy nodded and started looking for blueberries. She saw many colors, but she remembered to only pick the blue ones. Amy met a squirrel in the forest. The squirrel said, "Hi, Amy! I like to eat blueberries too!" Amy smiled and shared some of her blueberries with the squirrel. They both went home happy, having learned something new and enjoyed their tasty blueberries.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big purple ball. She loved to play with her purple ball every day. Mia's best friend was a small cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with the purple ball too. One day, Mia could not find her purple ball. She looked everywhere but it was not there. Mia was very sad. She did not know where her purple ball was. Tom saw Mia was sad and wanted to help her find the ball. Mia had an idea. She went to her mom and asked to use the phone. Mia called her friend Sam to ask if he had seen the purple ball. Sam said he did not know where the ball was, but he would help Mia look for it. Mia, Tom, and Sam looked all around and finally found the purple ball under a big tree. Mia was so happy and thanked Tom and Sam for helping her. They all played with the purple ball together and had a great day.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved spaghetti. He ate it every day. But one day, Tom felt uncomfortable in his tummy. His mom encouraged him to rest. The next day, Tom still felt uncomfortable. His mom made more spaghetti, thinking it would make him feel better. But Tom's tummy hurt more. His mom encouraged him to eat, but Tom could not. On the last day, Tom's tummy hurt the most. He did not want spaghetti anymore. But his mom encouraged him to eat it. Tom took a bite and his tummy hurt even more. Tom cried and wished he did not eat the spaghetti.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. One evening, she went to the park to play. Lily saw a big red balloon stuck in a tree. She was confused. How did the balloon get there? Lily wanted to help. She found a long stick and tried to reach the balloon. With a big push, she was able to release the balloon from the tree. The balloon was free! But something unexpected happened. As the balloon floated away, it turned into a big, friendly bird! The bird thanked Lily for helping it. It turns out, the bird was stuck as a balloon by a naughty wizard. Lily and the bird became best friends and played together every evening.
