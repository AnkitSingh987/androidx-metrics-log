Once upon a time, in a small, humble house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his mom. Tim was a good boy, but sometimes he would refuse to do what his mom asked him to do. One evening, Tim's mom asked him to help her clean up the toys in the living room. "Tim, please pick up your toys," she said. But Tim refused. "No, I don't want to!" he replied. His mom felt sad because Tim didn't want to help. Later that night, Tim saw that his mom was tired from cleaning all alone. He felt sorry for not helping her. So, he decided to pick up his toys and put them away. His mom was very happy and gave him a big hug. From that day on, Tim learned that it is important to help others and not to refuse when someone needs help. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She was three years old. One day, she wanted to go camping. She asked her mum, "Can I go camping?" Her mum said, "Yes, you can go camping. Let's go." So they went together to the forest. Lucy saw a bright tent. She was excited and said, "Mummy, can we pull it and set it up?" Her mummy smiled and said, "Yes, let's do it!" So they pulled the tent and set it up. Lucy went inside the tent and was very happy. She said, "Mummy, this tent is so bright! Can we stay here tonight?" Her mummy said, "Yes, of course. Now let's make a fire and roast some marshmallows." So they did. Lucy was so glad she could go camping with her mummy. She had the most wonderful time!

Once upon a time, in a distant land, there lived a dog named Spot and a cat named Mittens. They were best friends and always played together. One day, they decided to go on a big adventure. As they walked, Spot said to Mittens, "We need to trust each other on this adventure." Mittens nodded and said, "Yes, we must trust and help each other." They walked and walked, and soon they found a big tree with lots of birds. Spot and Mittens wanted to play with the birds, so they made an order. Spot would bark and scare the birds, and Mittens would catch them. They played and played until they were tired. Then, they went back home, happy to have trusted each other on their big adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim loved to climb trees. One day, he saw a big tree with many branches. He thought, "I want to climb this tree!" So, Tim started to climb the big tree. As Tim climbed, it started to rain. The rain made the tree wet and slippery. Tim was scared but he kept climbing. He wanted to see what was at the top. When he got to the top, he found a big box. The box was full of rich, shiny things. Tim was very happy! Tim wanted to take the box home. But, the rain made the box too heavy. Tim had an idea. He opened the box and shared the rich, shiny things with his friends. They all helped him carry the box down the tree. The rain stopped, and the sun came out. Tim and his friends were happy, and they played together with their new shiny things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Peter. Peter had a best friend called Joe, and they liked to do everything together. One day, Peter and Joe were going on a holiday together. They were so excited that they decided to skip all the way there! On the way they saw a very angry dog blocking their way. Joe was scared, but Peter was brave. He turned to Joe and said, “Let’s keep skipping. The dog won’t hurt us.” Joe shook his head. “I’m not so sure. That dog looks really angry!” But Peter kept skipping, and soon enough the dog went away. When they finally arrived at their holiday destination, Peter and Joe were exhausted. But they were also very happy – and full of relief!
