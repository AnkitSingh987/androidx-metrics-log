It was a special night. The stars were out and the moon shone brightly in the night sky. Little Sam was so excited he could hardly contain himself. He looked up at the sky and saw a beautiful firework in the distance. "Look! A firework," he said to his mum and dad excitedly. His mum smiled and said, "Yes Sam, that's a firework. It's a beautiful welcome for the night." Just then, Sam heard a loud noise from the skies above. He looked up and saw dark clouds appearing and getting closer. Sam's face was troubled. He said, "What's that noise? Is it a bad firework?" Mum was startled and quickly looked up. "No, Sam," she said, "It's a storm. Let's go inside and stay safe." She quickly ushered him towards the house. Although Sam was scared, he felt safe knowing that his mum and dad were there and they would protect him. After a while, the storm passed and they were able to welcome the night peacefully once more.

Once upon a time, there was a big mixer. The mixer was very important. It helped mix things for people to eat. One day, it needed to mix cake for a fun party. Mommy and Daddy put all the things in the mixer. They put in eggs, milk, and sugar. The mixer mixed and mixed until it was all done. The cake mix was ready for the oven. After the cake was baked, everyone at the party loved it. They said "Thank you, mixer!" The mixer was so happy to help. It knew that it was important and needed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was very excited to help her mom and dad wash their clothes. They had a big basket full of dirty clothes to wash. Lily wanted to be a big help! One day, Lily found a big black rock in the yard. She didn't know it was coal. She thought it was a fun toy! She played with it all day, and then she put it in the basket with the dirty clothes. When mom and dad started to wash the clothes, they didn't see the coal. The clothes went round and round in the water. Suddenly, all the clothes turned black! Mom and dad were very surprised. Lily felt bad that she made the clothes black. She said sorry to her mom and dad. They all laughed and hugged. They knew it was just an accident. Together, they washed the clothes again and made them clean. And from that day on, Lily learned to always check the basket before washing clothes.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to wear her favorite hat. One day, she found a long tape in her room. Lily had an idea to use the tape to make her hat look even better. Lily put the tape on her hat and went outside to show her friends. But something unexpected happened. When the wind blew, the tape flew off her hat and got stuck in a tree. Lily was sorry because she didn't want to lose her tape. Then, a friendly squirrel came and took the tape from the tree. The squirrel brought the tape back to Lily. She was so happy! But instead of putting the tape back on her hat, Lily decided to share it with the squirrel. They both played with the tape, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy car. One sunny day, Tim went outside to play with his car in the yard. As Tim played, he saw a poor little ant on the ground. The ant was walking under the hot sun. Tim felt bad for the ant because the sun was so hot it could burn. Tim decided to help the poor ant. He picked it up and put it in the shade under a big tree. Now, the ant was safe from the burning sun. The ant was happy and Tim was happy too. They both continued to play, and they became good friends.
