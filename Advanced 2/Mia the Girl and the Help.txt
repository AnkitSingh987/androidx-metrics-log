Once upon a time, there was a small girl named Mia. Mia was a generous girl who liked to help others. One day, Mia saw a telephone on the ground. She picked it up and heard a voice. "Hello, can you help me?" the voice said. Mia said, "Yes, I can help you." The voice told Mia to step on the stones to find the owner of the telephone. Mia stepped on the stones, one by one, until she found a lady. The lady said, "Thank you, Mia, for finding my telephone!" Mia felt happy that she could help. The lady was so grateful that she gave Mia a big hug. From that day on, Mia and the lady became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a purple cat named Luna. Luna loved to play outside, even when it was cold. One day, she went out to play in the cold with her friend, a little boy named Tim. "Tim, let's play in the cold," said Luna. Tim smiled and said, "Okay, Luna! Let's play!" They played in the cold all day, making snowballs and having fun. As the sun went down, Luna and Tim got tired. They went back inside to get warm. Luna's nose was cold, but she was happy. She had a fun day playing with her friend in the cold.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a sister named Rose. They liked to play with their harmless pet cat, Fluffy. One day, Lily said, "Rose, let's bathe Fluffy!" Rose agreed, "Yes, Fluffy is dirty. Let's make her clean!" So, they took Fluffy and put her in a tub of water. They used soap and gently washed Fluffy. When they were done, Fluffy was clean and happy. Lily and Rose laughed and played with their clean pet cat. From that day on, Lily and Rose always made sure to bathe Fluffy when she was dirty. They loved their harmless pet and took good care of her. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to fold paper. One day, he found a new piece of paper with a funny image on it. The image was of a green frog with a big, round nose. Tim thought it was so silly! Tim wanted to show his friend, Sarah, the funny image. So, he took the paper with the frog to her house. But on the way, he stepped in a big, disgusting mud puddle. The mud got all over the paper and the frog. Tim was sad. When Tim showed Sarah the muddy paper, she helped him clean it. They washed the disgusting mud away and the funny frog was clean again. Tim and Sarah laughed at the silly image. Then, they folded the paper into a frog shape and played with it all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had a toy he loved very much. But Tim was spoiled. He did not like to share his toy with others. One day, a new girl came to play. Her name was Sue. Sue saw Tim's toy and said, "Can I play with your toy, please?" Tim did not want to share. He said, "No, it is mine." Sue was sad, but she had an idea. She went to her bag and took out a toy. Sue offered her toy to Tim and said, "If you let me play with your toy, you can play with mine." Tim thought about it and said, "Okay." They played together and had lots of fun. In the end, Tim learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. The moral of the story is that it is good to share and be kind to others.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo. He lived in a light, sunny place. The hippo liked to play with his friends. One day, he saw a new animal. The new animal looked a little scary. The hippo wanted to be friends with the new animal. He went to ask the new animal to play. The new animal said no. The hippo was sad. He tried to ask again and again, but the new animal still said no. The new animal got mad at the hippo. He did not want to be friends. He pushed the hippo into the water. The hippo was very sad. He swam away and did not play with the new animal again.
