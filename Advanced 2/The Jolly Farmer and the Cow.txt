Once upon a time, there was a jolly farmer. He had a big farm with many animals. The farmer loved to sing and dance with his animals. One day, the farmer's cow said, "Let's play a game!" The farmer thought it was a good idea. He asked, "What game should we play?" The cow thought for a moment and then said, "I suggest we play hide and seek!" All the animals on the farm were very excited to play. They all hid while the farmer closed his eyes and counted. "One, two, three, four, five!" the farmer said. Then he opened his eyes and started to look for his animal friends. The farmer found the cow behind a big tree, the pig in the mud, and the sheep in the tall grass. They all laughed and had so much fun. The jolly farmer and his animals played hide and seek until the sun went down. They all went to sleep, happy and tired from their fun day.

Once upon a time, a curious little sheep named Mimi lived in a big green field. Mimi loved to play with her friends and eat the sweet grass all day. She had soft, fluffy wool that kept her warm when the wind blew. One day, Mimi saw a big red ball near the fence. She wanted to play with it, but her mom told her to mind the fence. Mimi was a good little sheep and listened to her mom, but she still wanted to see the ball up close. The next day, Mimi asked her friend, a small yellow bird, to help her get the ball. The bird flew over the fence and pushed the ball into the field. Mimi was so happy! She played with the ball all day, and her friends joined in too. Mimi knew it was important to mind her mom, but she also learned that with a little help, she could still have fun and be safe.

Once upon a time, there was a man who had a shop. In the shop, there was a lonely shelf. The man wanted to sell things from the shelf, but no one came to buy them. The shelf was sad, and the man was sad too. One day, a little girl came into the shop. She saw the lonely shelf and felt bad for it. She wanted to help the man, so she had an idea. She went home and made pretty pictures with bright colors to put on the shelf. The next day, the little girl came back to the shop with her pictures. She put them on the lonely shelf, and it looked very nice. People walking by saw the pretty shelf and came in to see what the man was selling. The man was so happy that people were coming to his shop. The shelf was not lonely anymore, and the man sold many things. The little girl was happy too, because she had helped the man and the lonely shelf. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there was a swan. The swan was very lucky. She had many friends who lived with her in the pond. They all played together every day. The swan was very happy. One day, a new flower came to the pond. The flower was called Bloom. Bloom was very pretty, but she was sad. She did not have any friends. The lucky swan saw Bloom and wanted to help her. The swan talked to Bloom and asked her to play with her and her friends. Bloom was happy to play with them. Soon, Bloom had many friends too. The swan, Bloom, and all their friends played together every day. They were all very happy. The lucky swan learned that helping others can make you even happier. And Bloom learned that friends make you feel warm and loved. They all lived happily in the big pond, playing and laughing together. And that is how the lucky swan and Bloom became the best of friends.

Kelly was a young girl who wanted to look cool. She wanted a pair of sunglasses. She asked her mom for them and her mom gladly supplied them for her. Kelly was so excited to see her new sunglasses. They were tall and dark. She put them on and looked at herself in the mirror. She took them off and tried them on again, adjusting their position to make them look more stylish. She looked very cool in them and couldn't wait to show off her new look. Kelly and her mom went outside and the sun was bright. Kelly smiled and put her sunglasses back on. Now she was ready to show off her cool style.
