Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to dance. One day, she was out in the garden, dancing and twirling. Just then, her mom called her over, and said, “Let me remove your skin.” The little girl was scared and asked, “Why would you remove my skin?” Her mom sighed and replied, “It’s weak. I just want to make sure your skin remains healthy and strong.” But the little girl didn’t believe her mom. She started crying and shouted, “No! I don’t want you to remove my skin!” She stepped back and crossed her arms. Her mom said softly, “It’s ok, I just want to help you. I promise I won’t hurt you.” Finally, the little girl agreed and said, “Ok, but only if you promise me you won’t hurt me or take away too much skin.” Gently, her mom removed a small bit of the little girl’s skin without hurting her. The little girl was relieved and hugged her mom tight. From then on, the little girl took care of her skin so that it stay healthy and strong.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Max. He was a very curious bird and he loved to observe everything. One day, he was flying over a field and he noticed something interesting. From his angle in the sky, he saw a cross. It was made of wood and it was very old and filthy. Max flew down to get a closer look. He knew it was a special place as he could see people had left things on the cross. He began to pick them up one by one and examine them - there were flowers, coins, pretty necklaces, and even a tiny toy car. As Max admired the things on the cross he heard a voice behind him. He turned and saw a small old lady. She said to him in a kindly voice, "Hi Max, don't you think it's nice how people leave things on the cross?" Max nodded and replied, "Yes, it's very nice". The old lady smiled and thanked Max for his kind words. Max said good-bye and began to fly away. As he flew off, Max remembered to always keep on observing the world around him. He had discovered something very special that day!

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. Tim had a kite to play with. They found a big tree with a hidden spot behind it. Tim and his mom hid behind the tree and played with the kite. As they played, a girl named Sue came to the park. Sue saw Tim and his mom hiding behind the tree. Sue asked, "What are you doing?" Tim said, "We are playing with my kite!" Sue wanted to play too, so they all played with the kite together. Suddenly, the wind blew the kite up high into the sky. The kite touched a big bird! The bird grabbed the kite in its beak and flew away. Tim, Sue, and Tim's mom were sad, but then they laughed. They did not expect the bird to take the kite. They all went home and planned to play with a new kite next time.

One day, a little girl named Lily was scared. She saw a big shadow in her room. Lily did not know what it was. She felt her heart go fast. She wanted to cry, but she did not. Lily thought, "I will pray for help." So she closed her eyes and said, "Please, make the shadow go away." When she opened her eyes, the shadow was still there. But she saw it was just her toy bear, Ben. It was not scary at all! The light made it look big and scary. Lily laughed and picked up Ben. She started to dance with him. They made fun motions together. Lily was not scared anymore. She was happy and played with Ben until bedtime.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Seth. Seth loved watching his favorite show on the television. One day, the television did something very strange. The show started to freeze! Seth was very surprised and felt a bit scared. It was so weird. Seth's mom saw what was happening and she came over to help. She started pushing buttons and adjusting the settings on the television. After a few minutes, she seemed to figure out what the problem was. She pushed one last button and the show started playing again. Seth was so excited and relieved. From that day on, Seth was always careful to turn off the television before he left the room. He didn't want it doing anything weird again!
