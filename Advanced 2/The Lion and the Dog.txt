Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a big lion. The lion loved to roar very loud. He roared when he was happy, and he roared when he was sad. One day, the lion went for a walk near a creek. The water in the creek was clear and cold. The lion liked to walk by the creek because it was quiet and he could hear his roar very well. As the lion walked, he met a small, obedient dog. The dog liked the lion and wanted to be his friend. The lion liked the dog too, and they played together by the creek. The lion roared, and the dog barked, and they were happy together.

One day, a big, hairy dog went for a walk. The dog had a key in its mouth. The dog liked to carry the key. It was a shiny key. The dog was very happy. The hairy dog saw a little girl. The little girl was sad. She had lost her key. The dog wanted to help her. So, the dog dropped the key in front of the girl. The girl was very happy. She thanked the dog. But then, something unexpected happened. The girl's mom came and said, "That's not our key!" The hairy dog picked up the key again and walked away. The dog found another key on the ground. This was the girl's key! The dog took the key to the girl. Now, the girl and her mom were very happy. The hairy dog had saved the day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a teddy bear named Ted. Ted was old and dirty, but Sue loved him very much. They played together every day. One day, Sue's mom saw the dirty teddy bear and said, "Sue, this teddy bear is disgusting. We need to remove the dirt." Sue was scared but her mom promised to be careful. So, Sue's mom washed Ted, and he became clean and soft again. Sue was so happy to see Ted clean again. She gave him a big hug and said, "Ted, I love you even when you're dirty, but it's nice to have you clean again." From that day on, Sue learned that it's important to take care of the things she loves. And Sue and Ted lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a graceful deer named Lily. Lily had many friends who played with her every day. They loved to run and jump together. One day, Lily's friend, the wise owl, came with some news. He said, "Lily, there is a big party tonight, and all the animals are invited!" Lily was very happy and excited to go to the party. At the party, all the animals were having fun. They were dancing, singing, and eating yummy food. Lily saw her friend, the squirrel, and said, "Hello, do you want to dance with me?" The squirrel frowned and said, "No, I am too shy to dance." Lily smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." So, Lily and the squirrel danced together, and soon the squirrel was not shy anymore. They all had a great time at the party, and Lily was happy that she could help her friend. And from that day on, the squirrel was never shy again.

Lily liked to draw images with her crayons. She enjoyed making colorful pictures of her family, her friends and her toys. She was very proud of her images and liked to show them to everyone. One day, she brought her images to school to share with her classmates. She hoped they would like them too. She put them on the table and waited for her teacher to start the show and tell. But then, Max came and saw her images. He did not like them at all. He said they were ugly and silly. He laughed at them and tore them into pieces. He threw them on the floor and stepped on them. Lily was very sad and angry. She felt embarrassed and hurt. She ran to her teacher and told her what Max did. She cried and hugged her teacher. The teacher was very mad at Max. She scolded him and made him pick up the pieces. She told him to say sorry to Lily and to make a new image for her. She told him to be kind and respectful to others. Lily was still sad, but she felt a little better. She thanked her teacher and accepted Max's apology. She looked at his new image. It was a flower with a smiley face. She smiled back and said it was nice. She enjoyed making a new friend.
