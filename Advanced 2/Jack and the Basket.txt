Little Jack was very restless. He ran around the yard with a big basket in his arms, and it made him very happy. He loved carrying things around. He ran for a very long time, and the sun was high in the sky. After a while, the basket was feeling very hot, and it started to melt. Little Jack was so sad that he had melted his basket. He didn't know what to do. But then, Little Jack had a wonderful idea. He grabbed some flowers from the garden and he put them inside the basket. The flowers looked so pretty! He ran to the kitchen and got some glue. Then, he glued the flowers onto the basket. Now it looked like a new basket! Little Jack was so happy! From then on, Little Jack used his new basket to carry all his things around the yard. And despite being a little restless, he was always careful not to let the basket get too hot.

One day, a boy named Tim and a girl named Sue were playing with blocks. They had red blocks, blue blocks, and yellow blocks. They were building a big tower together. They liked to share their blocks with each other. As they built the tower, they found a clear block. It was a special block because they had never seen a clear block before. They put the clear block on top of the tower. The clear block made the tower look very nice. Then, something unexpected happened. The clear block started to glow. Tim and Sue were very surprised. The glowing block made their tower grow bigger and bigger. They were happy because now they had more blocks to share with each other. They played with the big tower all day and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a robot. The robot was very boring. It did not dance, sing, or play with kids. It just stood there, doing nothing all day. One day, a little girl named Amy saw the robot. She did not like it. She said, "I hate this boring robot! It does not play with me." Amy was very sad. Then, a magic fairy heard Amy. The fairy waved her wand and the robot changed. It was not boring anymore! The robot played with Amy and made her laugh. They became best friends and played together every day.

Sara and Ben are playing in the garden. They see a big well with a bucket and a rope. Sara wants to look inside the well. She says to Ben, "Can you help me? I want to see the water." Ben is a helpful brother. He says, "OK, but be careful. The well is deep and dark." He holds the rope and lowers the bucket. Sara stands on the edge of the well and leans over. She sees the water shining in the bucket. But then, Sara slips and falls into the well. She screams, "Help! Help! I'm in the well!" Ben is scared. He tries to pull the rope, but it is too heavy. He says, "Don't worry, Sara. I'll get help." He runs to the house and tells Mom and Dad. They are worried too. They follow Ben to the well. Dad grabs the rope and pulls hard. He lifts Sara out of the well. She is wet and cold, but not hurt. Mom hugs her and says, "Thank God you're OK. You were very brave." Sara says, "I'm sorry, Mom. I was curious. I won't do it again." She looks at Ben and says, "Thank you, Ben. You were very helpful. You saved me." Ben smiles and says, "You're welcome, Sara. You're my sister. I love you." They hug and go back to the house.

Once upon a time there was a purple cone. The cone was very special and it could do amazing things. One day, two children went outside to play. They saw the purple cone in the garden and set the cone down on the ground. The children were so excited that they began to jump around the cone. Then, something magical happened. Suddenly the cone began to sprout little purple wings. With a single flap, the cone was flying in the sky! The children clapped and cheered. They watched in amazement as the cone flew high above them in the sky. Finally, the cone set down in the garden and the children ran to give it a hug. Then, the cone flew back up into the sky and disappeared. The two children were so happy they hugged each other and ran off to play, never forgetting their special purple cone!
