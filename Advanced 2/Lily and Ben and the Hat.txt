Lily and Ben were very happy. They were going to see the zoo with Mom and Dad. They liked to see the animals and hear their sounds. They wanted to dress in their best clothes for the tour. "Look, Lily, I have a blue shirt and a red hat," Ben said, showing his outfit to his sister. "I have a pink dress and a yellow bow," Lily said, twirling in front of the mirror. "We are so pretty!" Mom and Dad smiled and said they were ready to go. They got in the car and drove to the zoo. It was a sunny day and the sky was blue. But when they got to the zoo, they saw a big sign. It said: "Zoo closed. Sorry for the inconvenience." Lily and Ben were very sad. They wanted to see the animals so much. They started to cry and ask why. Mom and Dad said there was a problem with the water pipes. The zoo was wet and muddy and not safe for the animals or the people. They tried to cheer up Lily and Ben. They said they could go to the park or the library instead. But Lily and Ben did not want to go anywhere else. They only wanted to see the zoo. They cried and cried in the car. They did not care about their best clothes anymore. They were wet from their tears. They had a bad day.

One day, a little boat was sailing in a small pond. The boat was happy to sail and play with the ducks. It saw a drain in the pond and thought, "I should stay away from that drain." The sun was shining, and the boat had a fun day. But then, the wind blew the boat closer to the drain. The boat became upset and scared. It tried to sail away, but the wind was too strong. Just when the boat was about to go into the drain, a big duck swam by. The duck saw the upset boat and pushed it away from the drain. The boat was safe and happy again. It thanked the duck and they both played together in the pond.

Once upon a time, there was a large village. All the people in the village were friendly and nice. One day, a wise teacher arrived in the village. He was wearing a long robe and a tall hat. The wise teacher was carrying a basket of delicious fruit. He walked through the village handing out the fruit to the people. Everyone was very grateful for the delicious fruit. However, the wise teacher was not only giving out fruit. He told the people: "I am here to teach you something important." The people were very curious and asked the teacher what he wanted to teach them. The teacher said: "Be kind and generous to one another. Help each other and share what you have." The people in the village understood the message and began to help each other and share the delicious fruit. The wise teacher smiled and left the village. Everyone in the village remembered his words and began to treat each other with kindness and generosity. They all agreed that it was much better to share and help each other.

One day, a little dog named Spot went for a long run. He saw a big cross on a hill. Spot was very curious about the cross. He thought, "I want to see the cross up close!" So, he ran up the hill to get a better look. When Spot got to the top of the hill, he met a big, friendly bird. The bird asked, "Why did you run up here, little dog?" Spot said, "I wanted to see the big cross!" The bird smiled and said, "You are brave to run up this big hill. I have a secret to tell you. The cross has magic powers. It can make good things happen if you are kind and brave." Spot thought about what the bird said. He wanted to be kind and brave. Spot and the bird became good friends. They played and ran all day long. At the end of the day, Spot and the bird went to the cross and made a wish. They wished for more fun days together. And just like the bird said, the magic cross made their wish come true.
