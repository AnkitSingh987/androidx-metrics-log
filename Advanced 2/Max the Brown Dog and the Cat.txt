One day, a brown dog named Max went to play outside. He saw a big sink with water. Max was very thirsty, so he drank from the sink. The water was cool and nice. Max's friend, a cat named Lily, came to play too. She saw Max drinking from the sink. Lily asked, "What are you doing?" Max said, "I am drinking water. I recommend you try it too. It is very good!" Lily went to the sink and drank the water. She liked it too. They played all day, and when they were thirsty, they drank from the sink. At the end of the day, Max and Lily went home, happy and not thirsty anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw a stream and felt very thirsty. Tim was also very hungry. He wanted to find food and water. He walked and walked, but he could not find any food. Tim saw a big bird and asked, "Can you help me find food? I am very hungry." The big bird said, "Yes, I can help you. Follow me." So Tim followed the big bird to find food. But the big bird was not nice. It took Tim to a dark place. Tim was scared and asked, "Where is the food?" The big bird said, "I have no food for you. I am hungry too, and now I will eat you." And the big bird ate Tim.

Lily loved to go to the park with her owner, Sam. Sam was a successful writer who worked at home. He liked to take Lily for walks every day and play with her in the park. Lily was a happy dog who enjoyed running, jumping and chasing balls. One day, when they were in the park, Lily saw a big, fluffy cat sitting on a bench. Lily wanted to say hello to the cat, so she ran towards it. The cat did not want to say hello to Lily. It hissed and scratched at her. Lily was scared and hurt. She ran back to Sam, who hugged her and checked her for wounds. Sam was angry at the cat and its owner, who was reading a book and did not care. He told the owner to control his cat and keep it away from other animals. The owner shrugged and said his cat could do what it wanted. He did not like dogs or their owners. He was a mean and selfish person who did not have any friends. Lily and Sam left the park and went home. Sam gave Lily a treat and a toy. He told her she was a good and brave dog. He said he was sorry for what happened and that he loved her very much. Lily licked his face and wagged her tail. She loved Sam very much too. They were a happy and successful pair.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big tire in his yard. He had a fun idea. Tim asked his friend, Sam, to come over and play with the tire. Sam came over and saw the tire. He said, "Wow, this is a rare find! What can we do with it?" Tim smiled and said, "Let's rock on it!" They both sat on the tire, holding hands. They rocked back and forth, laughing and having fun. Soon, they were tired but happy. They knew they had found a special toy to play with.

Sylvia was having a hard time getting her three year old son, Jake, to bed. Every night he would create an escape from his bedroom and come wandering into the living room. He often asked Sylvia why he had to stay in bed. On this night, Sylvia had had enough. She said to Jake in a firm voice, "Now Jake, you need to mind me and go back to bed right now. It's time for you to sleep." Jake looked up at his mom with a sad look but nodded his head and slowly trudged back to his bedroom. He had learned his lesson: every night he was to stay in bed and not make a noisy escape from his bedroom. But sleep didn't come easy for Jake. His mind was full of all the fun things he could be doing if he would only make another escape from his bedroom. Every night he yearned for thoseescapes, but he knew he must listen to his mom. Then one night, after hours of tossing and turning, an idea suddenly came to Jake: he could play a game in his bedroom. With a smile of excitement, he settled into his bed and soon fellasleep. From then on, every night Jake made up a new game before going to bed so that his escapes would be within the boundaries of his bedroom. He was happy to have found a way to make bedtime something he looked forward to.
