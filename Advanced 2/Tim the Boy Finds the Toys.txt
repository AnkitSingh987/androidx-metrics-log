Once upon a time, in a large house, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim was a happy boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, Tim found a pin on the floor. It was small and shiny. He picked it up and showed it to his mom. "Mom, look what I found!" Tim said. "It's a pin. Can I keep it?" His mom looked at the pin and said, "Yes, you can own it, but be careful. Pins can be sharp." Tim was very excited to own the pin. He decided to use it to make a big picture on a large piece of paper. He drew a sun, a tree, and a house with a big red door. When he finished, Tim showed his picture to his mom. She smiled and said, "What a beautiful picture! You did a great job using the pin." Tim was proud of his picture and put it on the fridge for everyone to see. From that day on, he always kept the pin safe and used it to create many more beautiful pictures.

Once upon a time, there was a little animal. The animal had four legs and a long tail. It liked to crawl on the ground. The animal lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, the animal saw something shiny and icy on the ground. It was very cold to touch. The animal wanted to play with the icy thing. So, it started to crawl and push the icy thing around the forest. As the animal played, the sun came out and the icy thing started to get smaller. The animal was sad to see it go away. But then, it found new friends to play with in the forest. They all crawled and played together, and the animal was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia did not like broccoli. One day, her mom made her a big plate of broccoli for dinner. Mia looked at the broccoli and felt guilty. She knew she should eat it, but she did not want to. Mia had an idea. She took the broccoli outside to play. It was a very hot day. The sun was shining and the ice cream man was driving by. Mia left the broccoli on the ground, hoping it would melt like ice cream. She went inside to get some water. When Mia came back outside, she saw something unexpected. Her broccoli was gone! Instead, there was a big green monster. The monster was friendly and smiled at Mia. He said, "Thank you for the yummy broccoli! It did not melt, but I ate it all up!" Mia was surprised but happy. She made a new friend and did not have to eat the broccoli. From that day on, Mia shared her broccoli with the friendly green monster, and they played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a strong dog named Max. Max loved to eat food from a plate. One day, Max smelled something yummy. It was in the big house. Max wanted to find it. Max went into the big house. He saw a plate on the table. The plate had hot food on it. Max knew he had to be careful. He did not want to burn his mouth. Max waited for the food to cool down. After waiting, Max jumped up and ate the yummy food from the plate. It did not burn his mouth because he was patient. Max was happy and went back to his little house. He knew that being patient and strong was a good thing.

Once upon a time, there was a red wagon. A girl named Lily liked to play with the wagon. One day, she took the wagon to a fair. At the fair, Lily saw a big tree. She wanted to climb it. Lily left her wagon under the tree. She climbed up, up, up! At the top, she saw a bird. The bird whispered to Lily, "I like your wagon." Lily smiled and whispered back, "Thank you, bird." When Lily climbed down, her wagon was gone! She looked around and saw a boy with her wagon. Lily walked up to the boy and asked, "Is that my wagon?" The boy said, "No, this is my wagon. But we can share." So, Lily and the boy played together and had fun at the fair.
