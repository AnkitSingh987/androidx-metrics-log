Sam and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to swing, slide and run. But one day, they saw something unusual. There was a big tree with a hole in it. And in the hole, there was a paper with some words on it. It was a message. "Look, Lily, a message!" Sam said. "What does it say?" Lily could read some words. She looked at the paper and said, "It says, 'Find the treasure under the bridge. But be careful of the troll.'" "A treasure? And a troll?" Sam said. "That sounds exciting. Let's go and find it." They ran to the bridge. It was a wooden bridge over a small stream. They looked under the bridge, but they did not see any treasure. They only saw a big pile of leaves. "Maybe the treasure is under the leaves," Lily said. She started to move the leaves with her hands. But then, they heard a loud roar. It came from under the bridge. It was the troll. He was big and hairy and angry. He did not like anyone to touch his leaves. "Who dares to disturb my rest?" the troll shouted. He came out of the bridge and saw Sam and Lily. He glared at them with his red eyes. Sam and Lily were scared. They dropped the paper and ran away. They did not want to play with the troll. They did not want to find the treasure. They just wanted to go home. The troll picked up the paper and looked at it. He smiled. He had written the message himself. He liked to trick people and scare them away. He liked to rest under the bridge with his leaves. He did not need any treasure. He was happy.

Once there was a brave little fireman. He was very loyal to his job and always tried his best. One day, the fireman decided to dare himself to do something special. He took a hand held hose and climbed up the tallest ladder he had ever seen. When he reached the top, he could see the whole town. Everyone was so proud of the little brave fireman and he was so loyal to his job. Everyone cheered for him, because he was so brave and loyal!

One day, a little boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw the stars in the sky and thought about the universe. He knew the universe was big and full of stars, just like the ones he saw. Tim saw a cat sitting under a tree. He walked to the cat and said, "Hello, cat! Do you know about the universe?" The cat looked at Tim and said, "I know the universe is big, but I just like to sit here and watch the stars." Tim thought the cat was a little bit stupid for not wanting to learn more about the universe. But he still liked the cat. So, Tim decided to sit with the cat and watch the stars together. They both enjoyed looking at the stars and talking about the universe.

One day, a little girl named Sue went outside to play. She saw a bird sitting on a tree branch. Sue thought the bird looked silly. She wanted to get a closer look at the bird, so she walked closer to the tree. As Sue got closer, she saw the bird examine a big red ball. The bird picked up the ball with its beak and flew away. Sue was very surprised. She did not expect the bird to take the ball. Sue started to chase the bird to get her ball back. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird dropped the ball, and it bounced high into the sky. The ball turned into a big, pretty balloon. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. The silly bird had given her a fun surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a strong girl named Lucy. Lucy liked to draw patterns with her crayons. She would draw circles, squares, and lines that made pretty pictures. One day, Lucy found an apple in her yard. The apple was not nice. It was old and had started to rot. Lucy did not like the yucky apple, so she picked it up to throw it away. Lucy's mom saw her and said, "Good job, Lucy! You are a strong girl for picking up that yucky apple." Lucy smiled and went back to drawing her patterns, happy that she could help her mom.
