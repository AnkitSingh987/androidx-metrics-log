Once upon a time there was a tiny little flower that bloomed in a field. The flower was so original, with its soft pink petals and a bright yellow centre. One day, a farmer walked by and saw the flower. He had an idea. He reached down and pulled the flower out of the ground by its stem and put it in his pocket. The farmer took the flower home and put it in a box on his shelf. He didn't forget the flower, but he didn't think it was special either. Years passed and the flower was forgotten. But one day the farmer grabbed some leather and decided to make a belt for his jeans. He remembered the flower, so he took it out of the box and placed it between two pieces of leather. He wanted the flower to be a part of the belt for everyone to see. The flower was squashed as the farmer pressed the leather around it. No one knew what happened to the original flower. The belt was finished and the farmer wore it proudly. But the flower never bloomed again.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely little flea named Fifi. Fifi lived in a big green tree with her family. She loved to jump and play with her friends all day long. One day, Fifi's mom told her, "Fifi, we need to pack our things. We are moving to a new tree." Fifi was sad but she helped her mom pack their bags with toys and clothes. When they got to the new tree, Fifi saw a new friend. She said, "Hi, my name is Fifi. Do you want to play?" The new friend said, "Yes, I am Tim. Let's play together!" Fifi and Tim played all day and became best friends. Fifi was happy in her new home.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat named Zipper. Zipper lived in a small house with a nice family. One day, Zipper saw a big ball in the yard. He thought it would be fun to play with the ball. Zipper ran fast and tried to strike the ball with his paw. But, when he hit the ball, it did not move. Zipper was very confused. He tried to strike the ball again, but it still did not move. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball opened up and out came a new friend for Zipper! It was a little dog named Pop. Zipper and Pop played together all day long. From that day on, Zipper and Pop were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, in a big universe, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a warm heart and loved to help others. One day, she found a box in her room. It was a very special box. Inside the box, there was a tiny bird. The bird was sad because it wanted to fly in the sky. Lily wanted to help the bird. She thought, "I will release the bird so it can be happy again." Lily took the box outside and opened it. The bird flew out and up into the sky. It was so happy to be free. Lily felt warm inside because she helped her new friend. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a man with a big, smooth mustache. He lived in a small house near the park. One day, he went for a walk and met a little girl. The little girl looked at his mustache and said, "Wow! Your mustache is so big and smooth!" The man smiled and said, "Thank you! I like to turn it up at the ends." He showed her how he could turn his mustache up and down. The little girl laughed and clapped her hands. They became good friends and played in the park every day. The man would turn his mustache up and down to make the little girl laugh. They had lots of fun together, and the man's smooth mustache always made everyone smile.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Lizzie and Beth. They wanted to have a fun day together. First, Lizzie and Beth went shopping. Lizzie bought a delicious ice-cream cone. Beth bought some yummy candy. They both enjoyed the yummy treats. Next, they bought some stamps and stuck them on a post. It was fun to put stamps on the post. Then they put the post into a big box and paid the postman. The postman smiled at them and said goodbye. Finally, they went to Lizzie's house for dinner. Lizzie's mom had cooked a delicious dinner for them. They ate and laughed and had the best day ever. The end.
