Once upon a time, there was a car. It was really old and really filthy. The car was very sad because it didn't have any fuel. It wouldn't be able to drive anywhere! Suddenly, the car heard a noise. It was a voice! It was a little girl! She had seen the car and wanted to help it. She said, "I can get you fuel. I'll come back soon." The car was very happy. It nodded its head. The girl ran off, but came back a few minutes later. She brought fuel and filled up the car. Now the car was ready to go! It was so happy. The little girl smiled and waved goodbye as the car drove off. The car was so thankful to the little girl. It couldn't help but nod its head in thanks one last time.

Once there was a very pretty girl. She loved to dress up in her prettiest clothes. One morning, she put on her sparkly dress and ran out the door. The girl immediately noticed the noisy traffic. She stood at the side of the road and listened to the cars honking and rushing by. “Oh my,” the girl said. “The traffic is so loud today.” Just then, her mother rushed out of the house. “Let’s go, darling,” she said. “We mustn’t be late!” The girl smiled and grabbed her mother’s hand. Together they ran across the street, dodging the traffic. When they reached the other side, the girl looked back at the busy street. “That was exciting!” she said. “Thanks, Mommy.” Her mother smiled and squeezed her hand. “It sure was,” she said. “You look really pretty in your dress too.” The girl beamed with pride. She felt so special in her sparkly dress. With that, the girl and her mother walked off down the street, arm in arm.

Once there was a man named Tom who had a magnet he really liked. It was useful for holding things, like the keys to his house. He was always so happy to have it. Tom's neighbor, Jane, hated his magnet. She wished she had one just like it. Everyday, she would come by and make mean jokes about it. Tom said one day to Jane, “Why do you hate my magnet so much?” Jane said something mean and laughed. Tom kept his magnet safe and refused to let Jane have it. But one day, when Tom wasn’t home, Jane sneaked into his house and took the magnet. Tom was so sad. He realized what had happened and he knew he would never see the magnet again. He felt so lost without it. He never got it back.

Summer was jolly. Every day, two kids would run outside and play in the sun together. The two best friends, Sam and Sarah, were always running around. They chased each other in circles and giggled as they did. One day, they were running around when Sarah noticed a big, red ball. "Hey, let's play with the ball!" She shouted. Sam smiled and the two friends ran with the ball. They kicked it around and laughed as it rolled away. Suddenly, Sarah ran faster than Sam and caught the ball. “Ha! I won!” She shouted. Sam smiled as he followed her. The two kids continued to run around, playing with the ball and exploring the summer together.

Once upon a time, there was a modest bunny. He lived a very peaceful life in a cozy den in the forest. One day, he heard a loud noise coming from a nearby field. He hopped over to investigate the noise, and he saw a giant run stomping around. She was screaming to the sky, "I need to release my brain!" The bunny approached her cautiously and said, "Please, may I help you?" The giant was so grateful that someone was listening, that she burst into tears. Through her tears, she explained that she felt she was stuck, and could not get her thoughts and feelings out of her head. "I need to release my brain!", she said. The bunny, who was very wise for his age, said, "Maybe what you need is to take a few moments to write down your thoughts. That way, your brain can have some relief." The giant was so relieved to find a solution, and she thanked the modest bunny for the helpful advice. She sat down and wrote in her journal, and felt a sense of peace as she released her brain.
