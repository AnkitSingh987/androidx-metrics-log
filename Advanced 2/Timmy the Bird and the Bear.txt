Once, in a peaceful forest, there lived a little bird named Timmy. Timmy loved to eat berries from the berry bush. Every day, he would fly to the bush and eat until his belly was full. Timmy heard a noise in the forest but he did not know what it was. The noise came every day when Timmy went to eat berries. The noise was big and scary, and it made Timmy feel afraid. But he still wanted to eat the berries, so he tried not to think about the noise. One day, when Timmy was eating berries, he saw a big, friendly bear. The bear was making the noise! The bear liked to eat berries too. Timmy and the bear became friends, and they would eat berries together every day. The forest was peaceful again, and Timmy was not afraid of the noise anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was upset one evening because his toy car was broken. He wanted to play with it, but it just wouldn't move. Tim asked his mom for help. "Mom, can you fix my car?" he said. His mom looked at the car and had an idea. She went to the kitchen and got a big bowl of ice cream. She gave the ice cream to Tim and said, "Eat this, and your car will increase in size and be fixed." Tim was confused but ate the ice cream. As he ate the ice cream, something unexpected happened. His toy car started to grow! It increased in size until it was big enough for Tim to ride. Tim was so happy and thanked his mom. They both laughed and played with the big toy car all evening.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a playful dog. The dog loved to run and jump all day long. One day, the dog found a big log. He thought it would be fun to play with the log. The dog tried to roll the log, but it was too big. He tried to push the log, but it would not move. The dog had an idea. He would sack the log and take it to his home. He found a big sack and put the log inside. The dog was very happy. He started to run back to his home with the sack. But then, something unexpected happened. The log fell out of the sack and rolled down a hill. The dog tried to catch the log, but he could not. The log rolled into a river and floated away. The dog was very sad. He had lost his new toy.

Once upon a time, in a big desert, there was a little cactus. The little cactus was not happy. It wanted to be big and mighty like the other plants. It tried to grow tall, but it was still small. One day, a small bird came and sat on the little cactus. The bird was hurt and needed help. The little cactus loved the bird and wanted to help. It gave the bird water and a safe place to rest. The bird got better and flew away. The little cactus learned that even if it was not big and mighty, it could still help others. It was happy to be small and full of love. The moral of the story is that you don't have to be big and strong to be important and helpful.
