One day, a small ant and a big bear met in the woods. The small ant was sad. The big bear asked, "Why are you sad, small ant?" The small ant said, "I suffer because I need to move my food to my home. But my food is a huge leaf, and I can only move it an inch at a time!" The big bear wanted to help the small ant. But the big bear was too big to move the huge leaf without breaking it. The small ant and the big bear thought hard about what to do. Then, the small ant had an idea. "Big bear, you can help me by pushing a big rock. The big rock can help move the huge leaf!" The big bear pushed the big rock, and the huge leaf moved more than an inch. The small ant was very happy. The big bear and the small ant worked together to move the huge leaf all the way to the small ant's home. They became good friends and helped each other when they needed it.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly dog named Spot. Spot was a good friend to everyone in the park. All the kids and other dogs loved to play with him. One day, Spot saw a little girl crying. She was sad because she lost her toy. Spot wanted to help her. He searched all around the park and found a toy. He brought it to the little girl. The girl was happy and hugged Spot. Spot liked to supply fun to all his friends. He would play with the kids and other dogs, making everyone happy. Spot was the best elderly friend anyone could have.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Cindy. Cindy loved fancy things. She dreamed of having a fancy bed with lots of pillows and blankets. One day, her dream came true and Mommy and Daddy bought her a fancy bed. Cindy was so excited she couldn't wait to unpack it. She wanted to have a fun time making her bed but Mommy said she was too little to unpack it herself. Cindy was sad, but Mommy said she needed to be patient and wait for a grownup to help her. So, Cindy waited patiently and eventually Mommy and Daddy came to unpack the fancy bed together with Cindy. Cindy was so happy they were all doing it together. Mommy and Daddy showed Cindy how to be patient and wait for their help. Cindy learnt an important lesson - when we work together, wonderful things can happen.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack. He lived in a small house in the woods. The winter was cold, and the snow made everything look ugly. One day, Jack went outside to play with his toy car. Suddenly, an old man appeared from behind a tree and said, "I have a test for you! If you pass, you can have lots of candy!" Jack was so excited about the candy that he agreed to take the test right away. The old man asked, "Can you find a special rock I hid in the woods?" Jack searched the woods, but he couldn't find the rock. When it started to get dark and the snow started to fall, Jack finally decided to give up. He was about to go home when he bumped into the old man. The old man said, "You didn't give up, even when it was getting dark and cold. That is the best test you could have taken. Congratulations, you passed my test!" The old man handed Jack a big bag of candy. Jack was so happy to have all that candy! The moral of the story is: it is much better to persevere and not give up in the face of difficult challenges than to run away from them.

One day, a cat and a mouse were in the kitchen. The cat saw a big, thick piece of cheese on the table. The cat wanted to eat the cheese, but it was too high for him to reach. "Mouse, can you help me get the cheese?" asked the cat. The mouse thought about it and said, "Yes, but you have to share it with me." The cat agreed, and they made a plan. The mouse climbed up on the cat's back and jumped onto the table. The mouse pushed the cheese off the table, and it fell to the floor with a loud "break!" The cat and the mouse were very happy. They sat down and ate the thick cheese together, sharing it like good friends.
