One day, Emma and her best friend Lily were playing outside. They had so much fun running around together! Suddenly they heard a loud noise coming from the street. Emma looked and saw a stranger. He was pushing a big red tricycle. Emma was a bit scared. Lily asked the stranger, "What are you doing?". The stranger smiled and said, "We're going to have a race!". Emma and Lily smiled back. They were excited to join the race. The three of them raced down the street, with the stranger leading them. Emma was pedaling as fast as she could, but the stranger was faster. The faster they went, the dizzier Emma felt. Before Emma knew it, the stranger crossed the finish line first. He laughed and said, "You both did a great job for your first race!". Emma and Lily beamed with pride. They had so much fun racing with the stranger.

One day, a little girl named Amy was playing in her room. She was tired of her toys and thought they were boring. She looked around and saw a big, old armchair in the corner of her room. Amy had an idea. She decided to make the armchair her new friend. Amy walked up to the armchair and said, "Hi, Mr. Armchair! Will you be my friend?" To her surprise, the armchair answered, "Yes, Amy! I would love to be your friend." They started to talk and play together. The armchair was not boring at all! As they played, Amy's mom came into the room. She saw Amy talking to the armchair and smiled. "Amy," her mom said, "I'm happy you found a new friend." Amy smiled back and said, "Me too, Mom! Mr. Armchair is the best friend ever!" From that day on, Amy and Mr. Armchair had lots of fun together and were never bored again.

It was a warm day outside. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. So the three year old little girl decided to go out the front lawn to play. She ran outside to the comfortable grass and began to gather her toys. For hours on end, the little girl played with her toys on the lawn. She rolled around and laughed in the sun. It was a perfect day. However, the little girl noticed something strange. Dark storm clouds had started to fill the sky. The wind began to pick up and the air got colder. She quickly gathered up all her toys and rushed back inside. Just as she locked the door, it began to rain. It was a heavy downpour, with thunder and lightning. The little girl was relieved that she had gathered her toys up in time. She looked out the window and smiled, feeling safe and cozy inside her dry, comfortable home.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw many kids playing and having fun. Lily wanted to play too, but she was shy. She just stood there and stared at the kids playing. Lily saw a girl wearing a pretty dress. She liked the girl's fashion. Lily wanted to be friends with the girl. Lily took a deep breath and walked over to the girl. She said "hi" and the girl said "hi" back. They started to play together and became friends. As they played, they found a lemon tree. The lemons were big and yellow. They picked some lemons and decided to make lemonade. When they tasted the lemonade, it was very sour! They laughed and made a funny face. Then, they added more sugar and it tasted much better. Lily and her new friend had a great day at the park, and the sour lemonade made their day even more special.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max had a label on his collar that said "Max". Max had a friend named Sam. Sam was a small cat. Max and Sam liked to play together. One day, Max and Sam found a big, bitter fruit. Max said, "Sam, do you want to eat this fruit?" Sam said, "No, Max. It looks bad. Let's remain here and play." Max did not listen to Sam. He ate the fruit. Max felt sick. His tummy hurt. Sam was sad. Max should have listened to Sam. Now, Max could not play with Sam. Max had a bad ending.
