Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a best friend named Sam. They liked to play together every day. One day, Tim had to go away with his family. He was very sad because he would miss Sam. Before he left, Tim said to Sam, "Don't worry, I promise I'll be back soon." Sam looked at Tim and said, "I'll be good while you're gone, but I'll miss you a lot." While Tim was away, Sam felt sad and lonely. He wanted to play with Tim, but Tim wasn't there. One day, Sam saw a little girl crying. She had lost her toy. Sam decided to help her find it. They looked everywhere and finally found the toy. The little girl was so happy! She thanked Sam and they became friends. Sam and the little girl played together every day. They had lots of fun. But Sam still missed Tim. He hoped Tim would come back soon. Finally, the day came when Tim returned home. Sam was so happy to see him! They hugged and played together all day. Tim was proud of Sam for being good and helping the little girl. They promised to always be best friends and never worry about being apart again.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man with a big mustache. He loved to build things with his hands. One day, he wanted to build a big house for his family. He started to build the house with wood and nails. The elderly man worked very hard. His mustache would wiggle when he hammered the nails. The house was almost done, and it looked great. But then, something unexpected happened. The elderly man's mustache started to grow bigger and bigger. It grew so big that it could help him build the house. The mustache picked up the wood and nails and finished the house. The elderly man was very happy, and his family loved their new house.

Once upon a time, there was a man and his wife. They lived in a small house with their black cat. The man worked hard every day, and the wife took care of the house. The black cat liked to play with a ball and sleep in the sun. One day, the man came home and looked very sad. He let out a big sigh. His wife asked him what was wrong. The man said that his work was not going well, and he was worried. The wife gave him a hug and told him not to worry. She said they would find a way to make things better. The next day, the wife made a plan. She sold some of her things and bought a new tool for the man. The man used the new tool at work, and it helped him a lot. He was happy again, and so was his wife. The black cat played with them, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little rabbit named Tim was playing with his friends in the forest. They were playing hide and seek. Tim was very good at hiding. He could disappear behind the trees and bushes. His friends were always trying to find him, but they never could. Tim's mom told him to promise her that he would be home before it got dark. Tim said, "Yes, Mom, I promise." But Tim was having so much fun playing with his friends, he didn't want to leave. He was impatient to play more games. As the sun went down, Tim's friends went home. Tim was all alone in the dark forest. He was scared and cold. He remembered his promise to his mom. Tim ran home as fast as he could. When he got home, his mom was worried. She hugged him tight and said, "Please remember to keep your promises, Tim." Tim learned that keeping promises is very important.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely onion who lived in a big garden. The onion was sad because all the other plants had friends, but he had none. Every day, the onion wished for a friend to talk and play with in the garden. One day, a little girl came to the garden. She saw the lonely onion and said, "Hello, onion! Why are you so sad?" The onion replied, "I am sad because I have no friends to play with." The little girl felt bad for the onion and decided to help him. The girl left the garden and came back with a small plant. She planted it next to the lonely onion. Soon, the plant grew into a beautiful flower. The onion was happy and said, "Thank you for bringing me a friend!" The flower and the onion became best friends, and they were never lonely again.
