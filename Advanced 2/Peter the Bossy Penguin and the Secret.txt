Once upon a time, there was a bossy penguin named Peter. He lived in an igloo with his friends. Peter liked to plan games and tell his friends what to do. One day, Peter had a big plan for a fun day outside. Peter told his friends about the plan. They would play hide and seek, then have a big snowball fight. Peter's friends were excited, but they wanted to help plan too. Peter didn't like that. He wanted to be the only one to plan. The day came for the fun plan. Peter's friends played hide and seek, but they didn't like that Peter was bossy. So, they all made a secret plan. When it was time for the snowball fight, they all threw snowballs at Peter! Peter was surprised and laughed. He learned that it is more fun when everyone helps to plan.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a dream that she would find a big potato. She wanted to be rich and thought the big potato would help her. One day, Sue went to the garden to look for the big potato. She dug and dug, and then she found it! Sue was so happy! She took the big potato home and showed it to her mom and dad. They were happy too. But then, something unexpected happened. The big potato started to grow and grow! It became so big that it filled the whole house! Sue and her family didn't know what to do. The potato was too big to eat! Then, Sue had an idea. She asked her friends and neighbors to come and take some potato. Everyone was happy, and Sue learned that sharing made her feel rich.

Once upon a time, there was a bright little boat. It liked to sail on the big blue sea. The boat had a friend named Tom Thumb. Tom Thumb was very small, but he was very brave. One day, Tom Thumb and the bright little boat went sailing. They saw a big fish in trouble. The fish was stuck in a net. Tom Thumb wanted to help the fish. The boat was scared, but Tom Thumb said, "We can do it!" Tom Thumb and the bright little boat worked together. They pulled the net and set the fish free. The fish was very happy. It said, "Thank you, Tom Thumb and little boat!" They all became good friends. The moral of the story is that even if you are small or scared, you can still help others and make a big difference.

Once upon a time, there was a horse. The horse was very big and strong. He liked to go for walks in the park and eat yummy carrots. One day, the horse wanted to show some of his skills so he decided to put on a show. He started to dance and jump around. Everyone clapped and cheered as they watched the impressive display. The horse felt very proud of himself. He decided to reward himself with a yummy reward. He trotted over to the fruit stand and chose some juicy apples. They tasted so delicious! The horse was very happy. He had learned a lot from his show and now he was enjoying a yummy reward. He enjoyed it so much, that he wanted to do it all again someday!

Once upon a time, there was a big meeting. The meeting was for all the animals in the forest. They wanted to unite and be friends. The sun was shining and the day was dry. At the meeting, the big lion and the tiny mouse had a fight. The lion was very mad at the mouse. All the animals were scared. They did not know what to do. They did not want to be friends anymore. The meeting was not going well. Then, something unexpected happened. A big rain came and made everything wet. The lion and the mouse stopped fighting. They worked together to find a dry place for all the animals. The rain helped the animals unite and be friends again. The meeting ended with all the animals happy and safe.

One day, a little bird wanted to travel far away. The bird knew it would be hard, but it wanted to see new things. It said goodbye to its family and started to fly. While flying, the bird felt some pain in its wing. It was very sad, but it did not want to give up. The bird tried to keep going, but it was very hard. The bird had to stop and rest. After resting, the bird felt better. It was happy and ready to travel again. The bird saw many new things and met new friends. Even though it was hard and there was some pain, the bird was glad it traveled far away.
