Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play with clay. They had many colors of clay, but Lily's favorite was pink and Ben's favorite was blue. One day, they decided to make a cake with their clay. They rolled and shaped the clay into a big circle and put it on a plate. "Let's mix some colors to make the icing," Ben said. He took some pink and blue clay and squished them together. He made a new color that was purple. He was very happy with his purple icing. "Wow, that's pretty," Lily said. She was envious of Ben's purple icing. She wanted to make her own color, too. She took some yellow and green clay and mixed them together. She made a new color that was brown. She was not happy with her brown icing. It looked like mud. "Yuck, that's ugly," Ben said. He laughed at Lily's brown icing. He thought his purple icing was much better. He put his purple icing on the cake and made swirls and dots with his fingers. Lily was angry at Ben. She thought he was mean and rude. She wanted to ruin his purple icing. She waited until he was not looking and took a turn. She grabbed his purple icing and threw it on the floor. She smiled and felt proud. "Hey, what did you do?" Ben said. He saw his purple icing on the floor and his cake without icing. He was sad and mad. He cried and yelled at Lily. He wanted his purple icing back. Lily and Ben started to fight. They pushed and pulled and threw clay at each other. They made a big mess and broke their cake. They were not friends anymore. They were sorry and wished they had shared their colors.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a potato. It was not an ordinary potato. It had a face and could talk! Tim was very surprised. The potato said, "Hi, I am Mr. Potato! Can you help me find my family?" Tim and Mr. Potato went for a walk. They looked for other potatoes with faces. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. Inside the hole, they found a family of potatoes with faces! They were all very happy to see Mr. Potato. "Thank you for helping me," said Mr. Potato. "Now I can stay with my family." Tim was happy to help. He said goodbye and left Mr. Potato with his family. Tim went back home and told his mom about his adventure. From that day on, Tim knew that even ordinary things could be special.

One day, a little bug named Tim wanted to see the sky. He wanted to crawl up a hill to get a clear view. But the hill was too big for him! Tim was sad. Then, Tim saw a helicopter. The helicopter was big and could fly high. Tim asked the helicopter, "Can you help me see the sky?" The helicopter said, "Yes, I can help you!" The helicopter picked up Tim and they flew up high. Tim could see the clear sky now. He was very happy. The helicopter and Tim became good friends. They loved to fly and see the sky together.

Once upon a time, in a small nation, there was a hidden place. In this place, there were many trees and animals. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to play in the hidden place. While Lucy was playing, she saw a big bear. The bear was sad because everyone would ignore him. Lucy did not ignore the bear. She went to him and asked, "Why are you sad, Mr. Bear?" The bear said, "No one wants to play with me because I am big and scary." Lucy thought for a moment and then said, "I will play with you, Mr. Bear!" They played all day, and the bear was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The bear turned into a little boy! The boy said, "Thank you, Lucy, for playing with me. I was a bear because I was under a spell, and your kindness broke the spell." From that day on, Lucy and the boy became best friends. They played in the hidden place every day, and no one ever ignored the boy again.
