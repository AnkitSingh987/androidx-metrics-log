One day, a little girl named Sue found a tray. She wanted to show her mom. Sue was very brave, so she walked all the way to her mom in the kitchen. She said, "Look, Mom! I have a tray!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's a nice tray, Sue! What will you do with it?" Sue thought for a moment and said, "I will show my toys the tray too!" So, Sue took her tray and went to her room. In her room, Sue put her toys on the tray. She showed them how they could sit on it and play. Sue and her toys had lots of fun with the tray. Sue felt happy and brave with her new tray.

Once upon a time, two friends lived in a town. They liked to play together every day. One day, they were running around and having fun until one of them accidentally bumped into the other. The second friend got angry and yelled at the first friend. The first friend said, "I'm sorry. Please forgive me." The second friend said, “No, I'm mad. I don't want to be your friend anymore.” The first friend wanted to stay friends, so she ran to get a yellow flower from the garden. She brought it back and gave it to the second friend with a smile. The second friend felt sorry for the first friend, so he took the flower and said, "I forgive you. Let's be friends again!" The two friends hugged and ran around the town together, laughing and playing again. They were the best of friends. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Paul. Paul was three years old and he loved to explore. Today, he was playing outside in the garden with his toy cars. Suddenly, the ground started to shake. Paul was scared so he started to cry. His mom saw him running to the house and she said “Paul, it's just an earthquake. Don’t be scared, it will be over soon.” Paul was still scared. He asked his mom, “Will you punish me?” His mom smiled and said, “No, Paul. I won’t punish you. I will just keep you safe.” Paul felt better, but he was still scared. The ground was shaking slowly, but Paul knew it would get stronger soon. His mom went to the corner of the house, and held tightly to the wall while the ground was shaking. The earthquake soon stopped, and Paul felt relieved. He knew that his mom was right - the earthquake didn’t last long and she had kept him safe.

Once upon a time, there was a flexible dancer named Lily. She loved to dance and wander in the big park near her home. Every day, she went to the park to dance and play. One sunny day, Lily was dancing and wandering in the park when she saw a big, green frog. She had never seen a frog before, so she was very surprised. The frog looked at her and said, "Hello, I can dance too!" Lily could not believe it. She had never met a dancing frog before. So, the frog showed her his dance moves. He was very good and flexible, just like Lily. They danced together all day long in the park. From that day on, Lily and the dancing frog were the best of friends, and they danced together every day.

John was holding a black grape in his hand. He wanted to attach it somewhere. His mum noticed. Mum: What are you going to do with the grape? John: Attach it. Mum smiled. She thought it would be fun to see what her son was going to attach the grape to. John started looking around the house, trying to find something to attach the grape to. Everywhere he looked, he seemed to come across something that he could not attach the grape to. Finally, he came across a black balloon that was lying on the floor. He smiled. John: I will attach it here! John carefully attached the grape to the black balloon. As soon as he had done it, the balloon flew up into the sky. John and his mum watched in awe as he had been able to make the grape fly.
