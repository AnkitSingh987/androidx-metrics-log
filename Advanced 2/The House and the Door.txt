Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a bathroom. The bathroom was always available for everyone to use. One day, a family arrived at the house. They were happy to see the bathroom. The family went into the bathroom one by one. They liked the bathroom very much. But, something unexpected happened. When the last person went in, the bathroom door closed by itself. The family was surprised. They tried to open the door, but it would not open. Then, they heard a loud noise. The bathroom door opened, and they saw a big, friendly bear. The bear had been hiding in the bathroom. The family and the bear became friends, and they all lived happily in the big house.

Dave and Mom were walking down the street. Mom was carrying a fancy box with a red ribbon on top. "What's that box, Mommy?" asked Dave. "It's a surprise, sweetie," mom replied. They got to the park and sat down. "Can I open it now?" asked Dave. "Yes, Dave. Go ahead," said mom. Dave pulled the ribbon off and opened the box. Inside was a fancy new grill! Dave was thrilled and ran to show it to all his friends. Suddenly, mom called out "Oh no, there's no charcoal!" Dave stopped in his tracks and looked at Mom with a sad face. "But Mom, I want to use the grill now," he whined. Mom smiled. "Don't worry, Dave. I'll go to the store to buy some charcoal and in no time, you'll be able to unpack the grill and make yourself a delicious dinner!" Dave cheered up and ran around the park until mom came back with the charcoal.

One day, a farmer was walking through the quiet farm. The sun was out and everything was peaceful. The farmer heard something strange. He looked around and saw a puddle of water. It was soaking the grass. The farmer knew that it had rained the night before, so he thought the puddle was from the rain. He smiled, knowing that rain is always a good thing on the farm. He walked closer to the puddle and saw a bright, yellow duck. The duck must have been thirsty because it was drinking all the water in the puddle. The farmer laughed and said hello. The duck quacked in response and the farmer watched as it waddled away into the quiet fields. The farmer was happy that the rain had made a puddle for the thirsty duck and that the farm was quiet enough for the duck to find it and get a drink. He knew that nature was doing its job. So he went on his way, making sure to be quiet too.

Tom and Lily were playing outside in the garden. They liked to run and jump and laugh. They saw a big, powerful rainbow in the sky. It had many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. "Wow, look at the rainbow!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and bright!" "I want to drink the rainbow!" Tom said. "Maybe it tastes like candy or juice!" "No, you can't drink the rainbow!" Lily said. "It is too high and too far. And it is not for drinking. It is for looking." Tom did not listen to Lily. He ran to the shed and got a ladder. He put the ladder against the fence and climbed up. He reached out his hand to touch the rainbow. "Tom, stop! Come down!" Lily shouted. "It is not safe! You will fall and hurt yourself!" But Tom did not hear Lily. He wanted to drink the rainbow. He leaned forward and opened his mouth. Suddenly, a big cloud came and covered the sun. The rainbow disappeared. Tom lost his balance and fell off the ladder. He landed on the grass with a thud. "Ow, ow, ow!" Tom cried. He felt pain in his arm and his leg. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She was scared and sorry. "Tom, are you okay?" Lily asked. "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to yell at you. I just wanted to protect you." Tom looked at Lily and sniffed. He felt sad and silly. "I'm sorry, too," Tom said. "I was wrong. You can't drink the rainbow. It is not for drinking. It is for looking. And for sharing with you." Lily smiled and kissed Tom's cheek. She helped him get up and go inside. They told their mom what happened. She gave them a band-aid and a hug. She also gave them a glass of water and a cookie. "Next time, listen to your sister," Mom said. "She loves you and knows what is best for you." Tom and Lily nodded and said sorry. They drank their water and ate their cookie. They looked out the window and saw the sun come out again. They saw a new, powerful rainbow in the sky. It had many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple. They smiled and held hands. They learned their lesson. They did not want to drink the rainbow. They just wanted to look at it. And to share it with each other.
