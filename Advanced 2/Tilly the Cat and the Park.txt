Once upon a time, in a land full of colors, there lived a chubby cat named Tilly. Tilly loved to play with her ball and chase butterflies in the sunny park. One day, she found a big box with a symbol on it. The symbol on the box was a big red heart. Tilly didn't know what it meant, so she asked her friend, a wise old owl named Ollie. "What does this symbol mean?" Tilly asked. Ollie looked at the symbol and replied, "It means love, Tilly. Love is a warm feeling we have for our friends and family." Tilly was very happy to learn about the symbol of love. She decided to share the love with her friends. Tilly drew big red hearts on paper and gave them to all her friends in the park. They all smiled and hugged Tilly, feeling the love she shared. From that day on, Tilly and her friends always remembered to share love and kindness with everyone they met.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to touch soft things. She had a white teddy bear that she would touch all the time. Mia also loved cookies. Her favorite cookies were chocolate chip. One day, Mia's mommy made a big plate of cookies. Mia looked at the cookies and her eyes got big. She wanted to eat all of them. But her mommy said, "Mia, you can only have one cookie now. We will save the rest for later." Mia took one cookie and ate it. It was so yummy! She wanted to touch the other cookies, but she remembered what her mommy said. So, Mia went to play with her white teddy bear instead. She was happy because she listened to her mommy and still got to touch something soft.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. The palace was very tidy. In the palace, there lived a king, a queen, and a little prince. The little prince liked to play with his toys, but he did not like to put them away. One day, the king and queen went away for a while. The little prince played all day. He made a big mess in the palace. His toys were everywhere. When the king and queen came back, they did not recognize the palace. They were very sad. The little prince saw his mom and dad sad. He knew he had to make the palace tidy again. So, he picked up all his toys and put them away. The king and queen were happy to see the tidy palace. They hugged the little prince and all was good again.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park with her mom. Her mom had a big gray purse. Amy's mom said, "Amy, behave and play nice with your friends. I will sit here and watch you." Amy played with her friends and had fun. She saw her mom's gray purse on the bench. Amy thought, "I want to see what is inside the purse." But she remembered her mom told her to behave. So, she did not touch the purse. After playing, Amy and her friends sat near the gray purse. A little boy tried to take the purse. Amy said, "No, that is my mom's purse! We must behave." The little boy listened to Amy and put the purse back. Amy's mom saw this and was very proud. She gave Amy a big hug and said, "Thank you for being a good girl and behaving."

One day, in the hot summer, a little boy named Tom saw a big gray cloud in the sky. He was sad because he wanted to play outside with his friend, Sam. Tom said, "Sam, the gray cloud will delay our play time." Sam looked up and said, "Don't worry, Tom. Maybe it will go away soon." They waited and waited, but the gray cloud stayed. Then, something unexpected happened. The gray cloud started to rain, but it was not water. It was raining candy! Tom and Sam were very happy. They played in the candy rain and ate some too. The gray cloud did not delay their fun after all.
