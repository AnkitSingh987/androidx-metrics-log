Once upon a time there were two caterpillars, Sam and Tim. Sam was an old caterpillar who knew lots of tricks, while Tim was a young Caterpillar who was always asking Sam questions. One day they were hanging in a tree when Tim said to Sam, "I wish I could hang as long as you!" Sam smiled and said, "Practice makes perfect!" The next day Tim was hanging from a branch and he was amazed he could hang for so long. He thought he was better than Sam. So the following day, Tim boasted to Sam, "I can hang longer than you. I'm better than you!" Sam shook his head and said, "It's better to learn from others, than to be envious of them." Tim thought about what Sam said and realized he was right. So Tim started to practice and with Sam's help, he got better and better at hanging. The moral of the story is “It's better to learn from others, than to be envious of them.”

One day, a little girl named Sally saw a brown package on her way home. She picked it up and wanted to find out who it belonged to. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I make this package find its home?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sally. Let's ask our friends if they know who it belongs to." Sally and her mom went to their friend Tom's house. They asked Tom, "Do you know who this brown package belongs to?" Tom said, "No, but let's ask more friends to help." Sally, her mom, and Tom went to their friend Jane's house. They all asked Jane, "Do you know who this brown package belongs to?" Jane said, "Yes! It's my package! Thank you for finding it!" Sally felt happy to help her friend. She learned that it's good to help others and make them happy.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Sue. Sue loved to dance and sing. She had a good rhythm when she danced. One day, Sue met a boy named Tom. Tom liked to dance too, but he did not have a good rhythm. Sue said, "Hi Tom, I like your dance. But I can help you dance better with rhythm." Tom said, "Thank you, Sue. I want to learn." Sue and Tom danced and sang together. They practiced every day. Soon, Tom had a good rhythm too. They were very happy dancing together. One day, a big dance show came to town. Sue and Tom danced together in the show. They were the best dancers. Everyone clapped for them. Sue and Tom were very happy. The moral of the story is: when we help each other, we can learn and grow better together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy who loved to grow flowers. She wanted to make the world pretty with her fancy flowers. She had a small garden where she grew all her flowers. Every day, she would water them, talk to them, and watch them grow. One day, she saw a big, scary bird in her garden. The bird was eating her fancy flowers. Amy was sad and didn't know what to do. She wanted to save her flowers and make the world pretty again. Amy had an idea. She gave the bird some food that was not her flowers. The bird liked the food and stopped eating the flowers. The bird became Amy's friend and helped her grow more fancy flowers. Together, they made the world pretty and full of color.

Once upon a time there was a bear. He had a big paw and he was very strong. One day he wanted to bake a cake, so he went to the kitchen. He got some eggs, flour, sugar, and butter and started to bake. But the bear made a mistake and he put in too much sugar. When the cake was done, it tasted too sweet and he got angry. He stomped his big paw and the kitchen shook. The bear was still angry, so he went outside and shouted. He shouted so loud that the birds flew away and the other animals ran away into their homes. The bear went back into the kitchen and thought about what he could do. Then he remembered the eggs and mixed them into some bread. He put it in the oven and got a nice sweet bread. He took the sweet bread outside and shared it with all the animals. They were very happy and thanked the bear. The bear was also happy and his angry face changed into a nice smile. The bear had learned an important lesson- when you make a mistake, use your head and try again!
