Once upon a time, there was a huge ant. This ant was very big and very strong. The huge ant had many friends. They all lived together in a big network under the ground. The network was like a big town for ants. They were all very happy. One day, the huge ant saw a big piece of food. The huge ant wanted to take the food back to the network. But the food was too big for just one ant to carry. The huge ant tried and tried, but it could not move the food. Then, the huge ant had an idea. It asked all its friends in the network to help. They all came out and worked together. They all pushed and pulled the big piece of food. They did not stop until they finished moving the food to the network. The ants were all very happy. They shared the big piece of food with everyone in the network. They learned that when they work together, they can finish big jobs. The huge ant was very proud of its friends. They all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is that working together can help us finish big tasks.

One sunny day, a cheerful little fish named Finny was swimming in the sea. He loved to play with his friends and make big waves with his tail. Finny saw his friend, Crabby the crab, on the sand near the water. He swam closer to say hi. "Hi, Crabby!" Finny said with a big smile. "Do you want to play with me and make waves?" Crabby looked at Finny and said, "Yes, I want to play, but I can't swim like you." Finny had a fun idea. "You can poke the water with your claw and make little waves," he said. Crabby liked the idea and started to poke the water with his claw. The little waves crashed on the sand, and they both laughed. Finny and Crabby played together all day, making waves and laughing. As the sun began to set, they said goodbye and promised to play again soon. Finny swam away, feeling happy to have such a good friend like Crabby.

One day, a little boy named Tim found some clay. He thought it looked tasty, so he took a bite. "Yuck!" he said, spitting it out. "This is not tasty at all!" Tim's mom saw him eating the clay and said, "Tim, you should not eat clay. It is not good for you. I must punish you for doing that." Tim was sad, but he knew his mom was right. As his punishment, Tim had to clean up all the mess he made with the clay. While he was cleaning, he accidentally broke his favorite toy. Tim was very sad and wished he had never eaten the clay.

Once upon a time, there was a busy ant named Andy. Andy was always working hard to help his family. He had a big job to find food for everyone. One day, Andy found a big mattress. It was soft and nice to sleep on. Andy thought it would be fun to rest on it for a little while. But Andy's mom told him not to play on the mattress. She said it was not safe. Andy did not listen to his mom. He played on the mattress all day. He forgot to find food for his family. His family was very hungry and sad. When Andy's mom found out, she had to punish him. She told Andy that he could not play with his friends for two days. Andy felt very bad. He learned that he should always listen to his mom and do his job. From that day on, Andy was never too busy to help his family.

Once upon a time, there was a poor girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lily had a beautiful veil that her mom gave her. She loved to wear it on her head and dance around the house. One day, Lily wanted to move her veil to a special place. She took it outside and hung it on a tree. The wind blew and made the veil move like a pretty dance. Lily laughed and clapped her hands, watching the veil move in the wind. But then, the wind blew too hard and the veil flew away. Lily felt very sad and cried. Her mom and dad saw her crying and came to help. They looked everywhere, and finally, they found the veil stuck in a bush. They gave it back to Lily and she was happy again. From that day on, Lily only danced with her veil inside the house, where the wind could not take it away.
