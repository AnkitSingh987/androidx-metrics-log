One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a deep hole in the ground. Tim was very curious about the hole, so he looked inside it. He saw something shiny at the bottom. Tim wanted to get the shiny thing, but he was scared to go into the deep hole. Tim met a girl named Sue at the park. Sue had a big cup of juice with her. Tim told Sue about the shiny thing in the hole. Sue said, "Let's use my juice to fill the hole. Then the shiny thing will come up, and we can get it!" They poured the juice into the hole, but it was not enough to reach the top. Tim and Sue found a big box and put it near the hole. They put a soft seat on the box. Tim said, "Sue, you sit on the seat and hold my hand. I will reach into the hole and get the shiny thing." Sue sat on the seat, and Tim reached into the hole. He got the shiny thing! It was a beautiful, shiny key. They were so happy! The park keeper saw Tim and Sue with the key. He said, "Thank you for finding the lost key to the park gate! Now we can lock it at night to keep the park safe." Tim and Sue smiled, proud of their teamwork. The moral of the story is that when we work together and help each other, we can solve problems and do great things.

One day, a sweet little bird was flying in the sky. The bird saw a big shiny thing high up. It was a comet! The bird wanted to tell his friends about the comet. So, he flew down to find them. The sweet bird met his friend, a nice cat. The bird said, "I recommend you look up at the sky. There is a big shiny comet!" The cat looked up, but he did not see the comet. The bird was sad. He thought his friend did not believe him. Then, something unexpected happened. The comet came closer and closer to the ground. The cat saw the comet and was very surprised. The bird and the cat watched the comet together. They were both happy and excited. The comet was not just a comet, but a big shiny spaceship! The spaceship opened, and out came a friendly alien who wanted to be friends with the bird and the cat. They all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl. The owl liked to watch the little animals in the forest. He would sit on a branch and watch them play. One day, the wise owl saw a small rabbit with a hurt lip. The rabbit was sad and did not want to play. The wise owl wanted to help the rabbit feel better. The wise owl got a soft leaf and put it on the rabbit's lip. The rabbit felt better and said thank you to the wise owl. Now, the rabbit could play again. The wise owl was happy to watch the rabbit play with the other animals.

Once upon a time, there was a brave little frog who lived in a pond. One day, the frog noticed that the water in the pond was getting very deep. He tried to swim to the surface of the pond, but he was struggling. The frog heard a voice calling him from the top of the pond. He looked up and saw a beautiful dragonfly. The dragonfly laughed and said to the frog, “Don’t worry, little one. I can help you out of this deep pond.” The dragonfly pointed to a strip of lily pads. The dragonfly said, “You need to climb on the lily pads and they will take you to the surface. Then you can hop out of the pond and be free.” The frog was happy. He hopped on the lily pads and struggled to keep his balance. But gradually he became stronger as he hopped closer to the surface. Finally, he was free. He thanked the dragonfly and hopped away. The frog was so proud of himself. He learned that if you struggle hard enough, you can escape from even the deepest of places.
