Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside with his friends. One day, he went to the park to meet his best friend, Sam. They were very happy to see each other. In the park, they found a big, dizzy merry-go-round. They took turns spinning it very fast. Tim and Sam laughed and laughed as they spun around. They felt dizzy, but they loved the feeling. After playing on the merry-go-round, Tim and Sam saw a man taking a photo of a pretty flower. They asked the man if they could be in the photo too. The man said yes and took a photo of Tim and Sam by the flower. They smiled big and said "cheese!" Tim and Sam had a fun day at the park and couldn't wait to play together again.

Once upon a time, there were two little friends named Joe and Kayla. They liked to play together all the time. One day, Joe found a fake yarn ball on the ground. He showed it to Kayla and they decided to clean it up. Joe got a wet cloth and Kayla got the spray. They worked together to make the fake yarn look like new again. It was even more colorful when they were done! After they finished their cleaning, they decided to play a game. They each held one side of the yarn and ran around until they got tired. Joe and Kayla had so much fun that they forgot the yarn was fake. They hugged it together and promised to always be friends. The End.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big red ball. She loved to play with it every day. One day, she went to the park to play with her ball. At the park, Lily saw a boy named Tim. Tim was tired and wanted to sit on a seat. But there was only one seat in the park, and Lily's ball was on it. Tim asked Lily to move her ball so he could sit down. But Lily didn't want to. She wanted to display how high she could bounce her ball on the seat. Tim was sad because he was tired and needed to sit down. So, he asked Lily again, "Please, can I sit on the seat?" Lily thought for a moment. She knew it was important to be kind and share. So, Lily took her ball off the seat and let Tim sit down. Tim was happy and thankful to Lily. They both became good friends. From that day on, Lily and Tim played together in the park and shared everything. They learned that sharing and being kind made them both happy. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little seal went to play at the beach. The seal was very popular with all his friends. They loved to play in the water and splash each other. The sun was shining, and the water was warm. As they played, they saw a big fish swim by. The fish stared at the seal and his friends. The seal asked, "Who are you?" The fish said, "I am a big fish. I want to play too!" The seal and his friends were happy to have a new friend. But then, the big fish did something unexpected. He jumped out of the water and started to fly! The seal and his friends were surprised. They did not know that fish could fly. They all stared at the flying fish and laughed. Now, the big fish was popular too, and they all played together at the beach.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a popular lawyer named Bob. He was very good at his job, and everyone liked him. Bob had a big display in his office with many books and pictures. The display was very pretty, and people loved to look at it. One day, a little girl named Sally came to visit Bob. She wanted to see his display. Sally said, "Hi Bob! Can I see your display, please?" Bob smiled and said, "Of course, Sally! Come in and look at my display." Sally went inside Bob's office and saw the beautiful display. She liked the colors and the pictures. Sally said, "Wow, Bob! Your display is so pretty!" Bob was happy that Sally liked his display. From that day on, Sally and Bob became good friends, and they always enjoyed looking at the display together.
