Once upon a time, there was a warm, fuzzy cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play in the sun all day. One day, Fluffy found a ball. The ball was big and red. Fluffy liked the ball a lot. Fluffy played with the ball all day. Fluffy would drop the ball and then run after it. The ball would roll and roll, and Fluffy would chase it. Fluffy was so happy to have a new toy to play with. But then, Fluffy got an itch. The itch was on Fluffy's ear. Fluffy tried to scratch the itch, but it was hard to reach. Fluffy dropped the ball and rubbed the ear on the ground. The itch went away, and Fluffy was happy again. Fluffy picked up the ball and went back to playing in the warm sun.

One day, a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom went to the park. They wanted to catch butterflies. They had a net to catch them. Lily caught a big, pretty butterfly. She was very happy. Tom was jealous because he did not catch one. Tom said, "I want to catch a butterfly too." Lily said, "Let's try again." They tried to catch more butterflies. This time, Tom caught one too. It was small but very pretty. Tom was happy now. Lily had an idea. She said, "Let's take a photo with our butterflies." They took a photo with the butterflies. Then, they let the butterflies go. They were not jealous anymore. They were happy and had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time there was a brave little girl called Jill. She loved to explore new places, play with friends and take care of animals. One day, when she was out exploring, Jill came upon an old house. She noticed a bright flame coming from inside, so she decided to take a closer look. As she peered inside the window, Jill saw a beautiful fire burning in the fireplace. She knew she must take care of it. Using some old logs and some easy matches, Jill was able to carefully build up the flame. After a few minutes, the whole fireplace was filled with a warm, orange light. The heat was comforting and cosy. Jill was so happy with her work. The flame was now safe and she had taken care of it. She smiled and ran off to play with her friends.

One day, a fast car went zoom down the road. A little boy named Tim saw the car and was envious. He wanted to go fast too. He asked his mom, "Can I have a fast car like that?" His mom replied, "You are too little for a car, but we can get you a toy car to play with." Tim was happy with his toy car, but he still wanted to go fast. One day, as Tim was playing with his toy car, a magic fairy appeared. She said, "I can make your toy car go fast like the real car. But you have to promise to always be nice to others." Tim promised, and the fairy waved her magic wand. Suddenly, Tim's toy car zoomed around the room, faster than any car he had ever seen. Tim was so happy and knew he would always be nice, just like he promised the fairy.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max liked to play with his toys, but he was bored with his old equipment. He wanted to find new toys to play with. One day, Max saw a big box near the door. Max thought, "Maybe there are new toys in the box!" Max went to the box and tried to open it. He felt a little scared because the box was very big. Max started to tremble, but he wanted to see what was inside. He thought about the new toys he might find, and that made him brave. Finally, Max opened the box. Inside, he found lots of new and fun toys! Max was so happy that he had been brave and opened the box. Now, he could play with his new equipment and not be bored anymore. And Max knew that sometimes, being brave can lead to fun surprises.
