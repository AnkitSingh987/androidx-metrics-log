One day, a little cat named Tom found a bottle of ink. Tom was very curious about the ink. He wanted to show it to his friend, Sam the dog. So, Tom went to Sam's house. The sky was gloomy, and it looked like it would rain soon. Tom knocked on Sam's door. Sam opened the door and said, "Hi Tom! What do you have there?" Tom replied, "I found this ink. I want to show you." Sam was excited and invited Tom inside. They played with the ink, drawing pictures on paper. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Then, the rain started to fall outside. Tom and Sam looked out the window and saw the gloomy sky. They were happy to be inside, warm and dry, playing with the ink together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a delicate toy bunny that she loved very much. Every day, she would play with her bunny and take it everywhere she went. One day, Lily's mom told her to be patient while she was making lunch. Lily sat with her bunny and waited. But she started to feel bored. She wanted to play outside with her friends. So, she asked her mom, "Can I go outside now?" Her mom said, "Not yet, Lily. Be patient, lunch will be ready soon." Lily decided to wait a little longer. She played with her bunny and told it stories. Soon, her mom called her for lunch. Lily was happy she had been patient. She learned that good things come to those who wait. And from that day on, Lily always tried to be patient, just like her mom had taught her.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had a toy snake that was flexible. She loved her toy snake very much. Every evening, she played with her toy snake before bedtime. One day, Lily's toy snake disappeared. She looked everywhere in her room but could not find it. She felt very sad and missed playing with it in the evening. Her mom and dad helped her look for the toy snake too, but they could not find it. After a few days, Lily found her toy snake under her bed. She was so happy! From that day on, Lily learned to always put her toys away after playing with them. The moral of the story is to always keep your things tidy, so they don't disappear.

Once upon a time, there was a cute cat. The cat loved to provide help to its friends. One day, the cat found a big print on the ground. The print was from a big dog. The cat was scared of the big dog. The cute cat wanted to help its friends, so it went to find the big dog. The cat and the big dog had a big fight. The big dog was very strong, and the cute cat was very small. In the end, the cute cat could not win the fight. The big dog was too strong. The cat's friends were sad. The cute cat was very brave, but it could not help its friends this time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who liked to wander. He was curious about the world, and always wanted to discover new things. One day, he decided to go out for a walk. He looked around and the trees and birds were very interesting. He was so excited he jumped up and down. As he walked, his head filled with so many questions. He had so much to learn and see. He wanted to find out what was around the bend and behind the trees. The little boy just kept walking and watching. He wanted to explore everything he saw. He went up and down, over hills and across meadows. He was having so much fun, he completely forgot the time. Finally, the little boy realized it was getting dark. He knew it was time to go home. So he walked back, wondering about all the interesting things he had found. When he got to his house, his mom smiled and said, "Welcome home! Did you have a good time wandering?" The little boy smiled and nodded. He had had an amazing time!
