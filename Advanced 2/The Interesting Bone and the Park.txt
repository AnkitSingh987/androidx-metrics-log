Jack and his mum were walking in the park when Jack saw an interesting bone. He wanted to pick it up and have a look at it. He asked his mum if he could take it. His mum said yes but to be sure to write down where he found it. Jack went to get a pen and paper, and he wrote down the exact location. Then he went back to the bone and picked it up. It was disgusting! It was all slimy and smelly! Jack quickly put the bone back down in the exact same spot he found it. Jack and his mum left the park, and Jack made sure to never pick up another disgusting bone again. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little pigeon named Pippin. Pippin was ready to fly high in the sky. But one day, Pippin's wing got hurt. He could not fly anymore. This made Pippin very sad. Pippin's friend, a kind squirrel named Sammy, saw Pippin's hurt wing. Sammy said, "Don't worry, Pippin. I will help you restore your wing!" Sammy found some soft leaves and wrapped them around Pippin's wing. Pippin felt better but still could not fly. One day, Pippin and Sammy were playing near a big tree. Suddenly, a magic butterfly appeared! The butterfly said, "I can help you, Pippin. I can make your wing better!" The butterfly touched Pippin's wing with her magic. Pippin's wing was all better! Pippin was so happy and thanked the magic butterfly. Now, Pippin could fly high in the sky again with his friend Sammy.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his red ball. One day, Spot was playing with his red ball when it went into the woods. Spot ran after the ball, but he could not find it. Then, Spot saw a big frog. The frog had the red ball in its mouth. Spot said, "Please, Mr. Frog, give me my ball back." The frog looked at Spot and opened its mouth. But instead of the red ball, a big, round stomach came out! Spot was very surprised. The big, round stomach was full of air, and it started to float up into the sky. Spot jumped on the stomach and it took him for a ride. Spot and the stomach flew high above the trees, and Spot saw his red ball on the ground. Spot and the stomach floated back down, and Spot got his red ball back. From that day on, Spot and the big, round stomach were the best of friends.

One day, a cool cat named Sam was walking in the park. He saw a big tree with a loop hanging from a branch. Sam wanted to play with the loop, so he jumped to reach it. As Sam jumped, he heard a small voice say, "Hi! Can I play too?" It was a little mouse named Tim. Sam said, "Yes, let's play together!" They both took turns jumping to touch the loop. Then, Sam had an idea. He said, "Let's strike the loop with a stick and see if it makes a sound!" Tim agreed, and they found a small stick. They took turns hitting the loop, and it made a fun sound. They laughed and played until the sun went down. Sam and Tim became good friends, and they played with the loop every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. One day, Amy found a big hole in her backyard. She looked inside and saw a secret underground world. It was icy and cold, but very pretty. Amy wanted to tell her mom and dad about it. Amy went inside and told her mom and dad about the icy underground world. They did not believe her. They thought it was just her imagination. So, Amy decided to show them. She took her mom and dad to the backyard and showed them the big hole. When her mom and dad looked inside, they saw the icy underground world too. They were very surprised and happy. They all went down to explore the underground world together. They found a stage where animals were dancing. The animals saw Amy and her family and started to applaud. Amy's mom and dad joined in and applauded too. They all had a great time in the unexpected icy underground world, and it became their secret family place.
