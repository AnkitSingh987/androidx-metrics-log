Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. He had many cars of different colors. Tim wanted to have more fun with his cars, so he thought of a game. He wanted to match the colors of his cars to the colors of things in his room. One day, Tim saw a big clear box in his room. He thought it would be fun to put his cars in the box. But, his sister, Lily, saw the box too. She wanted to put her dolls in the box. Tim and Lily both wanted the box for their toys, so they started to argue. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. We can match the colors of our toys to the colors in our room. If I find more matches, I get the box. If you find more matches, you get the box." Lily agreed. They started to play the game and had a lot of fun. In the end, Tim and Lily both found the same number of matches. They decided to share the clear box for both their cars and dolls. They learned that sharing and playing together was more fun. And they had a great time matching colors and playing with their toys.

Once upon a time there was a wild girl. She liked to explore in the woods and look for jewelry. One day she found a special golden necklace. She thought it was so pretty and she wanted to keep it. So she put it in her pocket and tried to run away from the woods. But she was too slow. Suddenly, she heard someone calling her name. It was a mean woman. She wanted the necklace so she grabbed the girl and took it away from her. No matter how much the girl cried and begged, the mean woman wouldn't give it back. Then she said goodbye and ran away. The wild girl was so sad that she never went back to the woods. She would never be able to find jewelry again. The bad ending was that she was left with no jewelry.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to make a pretty picture. She took out her paper and colors. Lily was very excited to start her picture. She started to draw a big sun, a blue sky, and green grass. Then, Lily's little brother, Tom, came into the room. Tom was curious about what Lily was doing. He said, "What are you making, Lily?" Lily told him, "I am making a picture, Tom!" Tom wanted to help, but he accidentally knocked over the colors. Lily felt frustrated. Lily took a deep breath and said, "It's okay, Tom. Let's clean up." They picked up the colors together. Then, they started the picture again. This time, they made an even prettier picture together. Lily and Tom were very happy with their new picture.

Once upon a time, there was a long, old snake named Sam. Sam lived in a big tree in the forest. He had many friends, like the birds, the bugs, and the squirrels. Sam was very happy living in the tree, but he thought it was a little plain. He wanted to make it look pretty. So, Sam decided to decorate his tree. Sam asked his friends to help him decorate the tree. The birds found pretty leaves and put them on the branches. The bugs painted the bark with bright colors. The squirrels hung shiny stones from the twigs. Everyone worked together to make the tree look beautiful. When they were done, Sam and his friends looked at the tree. It was now the prettiest tree in the forest. Everyone was very happy with their work. They had a big party under the tree to celebrate. Sam and his friends danced and played games all day long. And from that day on, the long, old tree was the most beautiful home for Sam and his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside with his toys. One day, he saw an expensive toy car in the store. Tim wanted the car so much, but he did not have enough money. One day, while playing outside, a bug appeared near Tim. The bug was very small and had wings. Tim had an idea. He asked the bug for help to get the expensive toy car. The bug agreed to help Tim, but only if Tim could find the bug's lost friend. Tim and the bug looked everywhere for the lost friend. They looked under rocks, behind trees, and in the grass. Finally, they found the lost friend hiding in a flower. The bug was very happy and thanked Tim for his help. As promised, the bug helped Tim get the expensive toy car. The bug used its wings to fly up high and get the car from the store shelf. Tim was so happy to have the car and thanked the bug. From that day on, Tim and the bug became best friends and played together all the time.
