Once upon a time, there was a rat who had an adventurous spirit. He wanted to explore the world around him and have some fun. One day, while the rat was playing in the grass, he stumbled across a humble little house. It looked so comfortable and inviting that he decided to take a peek inside. When he opened the door, he saw a young girl playing with her toys. The rat was entranced and he asked her, "What are you playing?" The girl said nothing and just looked away nervously. She was too scared to speak. The rat was a bit surprised, so he asked her again. This time he said, "Don’t be scared, I’m only a rat. I just want to play with you." The girl still stayed quiet, but slowly she started to warm up to the rat. Soon enough, the rat and the girl were playing together - they had become fast friends. From then on, they played every single day and their friendship kept growing. The rat was finally living a life full of adventure!

Mark was a very charming three-year-old. He was naughty, but his family still loved him. Everyone could predict that one day, he would get into trouble. One day, Mark was playing outside when he heard his mother call: "Mark, come inside and close the door!" Mark ran inside and slammed the door, but he didn't close it properly. Mum saw what Mark did and said, "Now, Mark, you must always close the door when you come in." Even though Mark was sorry, he knew he was in trouble. He thought his mother would punish him. Mark tried to stay out of trouble the next day, but he was so curious! As he was playing, he heard his mother calling from the kitchen. "Mark, come inside now and make sure to close the door behind you." Mark knew he wasn't in trouble this time and he remembered about closing the door. He quickly ran inside and shut it tightly. His mother appeared around the corner, looking proud. She said "Well done Mark! I'm proud of you for listening and for closing the door." Mark felt very proud and he knew that, from then on, he would always remember to close the door.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car that he loved to play with. It was his favorite toy and he called it "mine". Every day, he would take his toy car outside and play with it. One day, Tim saw his friend, Sam, playing with a toy truck. Tim wanted to play with the truck too. So, he took the truck from Sam without asking. Sam was sad and started to cry. Tim felt bad and knew he should not have taken the truck. Tim gave the truck back to Sam and said, "I'm sorry. I should have asked you first." They decided to play together and share their toys. Tim learned that it is important to ask before taking something that is not his. They played together all day and had lots of fun, being slow and careful with each other's toys. And they became even better friends.

Once upon a time there were two friends, Ted and Sue. They were playing in the park and having lots of fun when suddenly, a big black bear appeared. It was very big and very scary. Ted and Sue were very scared and ran away shouting. The bear followed them and said in a loud voice, "If you don't stop running I will make a big, bad threat". Ted and Sue stopped running and nodded. The bear then said "I will applaud if you do what I say". Ted and Sue thought they had no choice and agreed. The bear gave them a long list of bad things they had to do. Ted and Sue did all the bad things, but the bear still did not applaud. Ted and Sue were sad and confused. Then the bear said, "I lied. I said I would applaud but I won't. This was all a trick!" Ted and Sue were very upset. They knew they had been tricked and felt very sad. They realised they should never have listened to the bear.
