Once upon a time, there was a little infant named Tim. Tim wanted to be independent, just like his big sister, Jane. Tim and Jane lived in a small house with their mommy and daddy. One day, Tim and Jane wanted to play outside. Jane said, "Tim, let's leave the house and play in the yard." Tim said, "Yes, I want to be independent like you, Jane." So, they left the house and went outside to play. While playing, Tim and Jane found a small puppy. The puppy was lost and wanted to find its mommy. Tim and Jane helped the puppy look for its mommy. Together, they found the puppy's mommy, and the puppy was happy. Tim felt proud because he was independent and helped the puppy.

Tom likes to write. He has a big book and a red pen. He writes his name, his mom's name, and his dog's name. He writes many words and pictures. He is very proud of his book. One day, he goes to the park with his mom and his dog. He brings his book and his pen. He wants to show his book to his friends. He puts his book in his backpack and buckles it. He is very careful with his book. At the park, he sees his friend Lily. Lily is playing with a ball. Tom runs to her and says, "Hi, Lily! Do you want to see my book? I wrote many words and pictures. It is very serious." Lily smiles and says, "Sure, Tom! I want to see your book. Can I write in it too?" Tom nods and opens his backpack. He takes out his book and his pen. He gives them to Lily. Lily opens the book and looks at it. She sees Tom's name, his mom's name, and his dog's name. She sees many words and pictures. She likes Tom's book. She says, "Wow, Tom! Your book is very nice. You write very well. Can I write my name too?" Tom says, "Yes, Lily. You can write your name. But be careful. Don't write on my words and pictures. They are very serious." Lily says, "OK, Tom. I will be careful. I will write my name here." She points to a blank space in the book. She writes her name with the red pen. She writes L-I-L-Y. She shows it to Tom. Tom smiles and says, "Good job, Lily! Your name is very pretty. Thank you for writing in my book. You are a good friend." Lily smiles and says, "Thank you, Tom. You are a good friend too. Your book is very serious. Can we write more words and pictures together?" Tom says, "Yes, Lily. We can write more words and pictures together. Let's write a story. A story about us and our dogs. It will be a fun story." Lily says, "Yes, Tom. Let's write a story. A story about us and our dogs. It will be a fun story." They write a story in Tom's book. They write about their names, their dogs, and their park. They write many words and pictures. They have a lot of fun. They are very happy.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends, Bob and Jack. They spent every day playing together. One day, though, they disagreed on what to do. Jack wanted to play outside, but Bob wanted to stay inside. Jack was very determined and said he was going outside no matter what. Bob was unhappy and uncomfortable with this. Then, something else happened to make the situation worse - a missile came flying in from the sky! They both screamed and ran away. After they had gone a safe distance away, Jack was angry at Bob for not agreeing with him about playing outside. But Bob was sure that it was better for them to stay inside when there were missiles flying around! Jack's angry feelings disappeared and he was glad that he and Bob were still best friends. They decided to go play somewhere safer after that.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to make pretty things. One day, she made a beautiful bracelet with many colors. It had red, blue, and yellow beads. She was very happy when she finished it. Sally had a friend named Timmy. Timmy saw the bracelet and wanted it. But Timmy was a bit selfish. He didn't want to ask Sally to make one for him. Instead, he took Sally's bracelet when she was not looking. When Sally found out her bracelet was gone, she was very sad. Timmy saw how sad Sally was and felt bad. He knew he should not have taken her bracelet. So, Timmy gave the bracelet back to Sally and said sorry. Sally was happy to have her bracelet back. She decided to teach Timmy how to make his own bracelet. They both made many bracelets together and had lots of fun. They learned to share and not be selfish. And they became the best of friends.
