Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play all day. He would run, jump, and laugh with his friends. One day, Tim was playing with his toy car when his mom called him. "Tim, it's time to stop playing and come inside for dinner!" she said. Tim was hungry, so he put his toy car down and went inside the house. At the dinner table, there was a big plate of yummy food. Tim ate and ate until his plate was empty. His mom was happy to see him eat well. After dinner, Tim went back outside to play with his toy car until it was time for bed.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a normal house with her mom and dad. One day, Lily was playing ball in the living room. As she was playing, the ball hit a pretty vase on the table. The vase fell and broke into pieces. Lily was scared and said, "Oh no, what do I do now?" Lily went to her mom and told her what happened. Her mom looked at her and said, "It's okay, Lily. I forgive you. We all make mistakes sometimes." They hugged and cleaned up the broken vase together. From that day on, Lily learned to be more careful when playing inside the house.

Once, a girl named Grace went on a vacation with her family. Grace was a graceful girl who loved to dance. They went to a big park with lots of trees and flowers. Grace was very happy to be there. One day, while Grace was dancing in the park, she saw a big, pretty bird. The bird was very graceful too, just like Grace. They danced together and had lots of fun. Grace wanted to own the bird and take it home with her. But then, something unexpected happened. The bird started to talk! It said, "Grace, I am not a real bird. I am a fairy!" Grace was very surprised. The fairy told her that she could not own her, but they could still be friends. So, Grace and the fairy spent the rest of the vacation dancing and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable cat named Miss Kitty. She loved to play with her leather ball. One sunny day, Miss Kitty went outside to play with her ball. As Miss Kitty played, she missed the ball and it rolled far away. She ran to find it, but it was gone. Miss Kitty was sad and didn't know what to do. She looked and looked for her ball, but could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened! A little bird flew down with the leather ball in its beak. The bird had found the ball and brought it back to Miss Kitty. She was so happy and thanked the bird. From that day on, Miss Kitty and the bird became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was an elderly man who loved to surf. He lived by the sea in a small house. Every day, he would go to the beach to find big waves to surf on. One day, while the elderly man was surfing, he saw a big fish in the water. The fish said, "Hello, do you want to surf with me?" The elderly man smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to surf with you, big fish!" So, they surfed together and had lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big fish opened its mouth and ate the elderly man! The fish was not a nice friend after all. And that is the end of the story.

Once upon a time, in a big green park, there was a helpful dog named Bow. Bow loved to play with his red ball and eat juicy cherries from the cherry tree. One sunny day, Bow saw a little girl named Mia, who was trying to reach the cherries on the tree. Mia looked up and said, "I want cherry, but tree is too tall." Bow wanted to help Mia, so he thought of a plan. Bow picked up his red ball and threw it at the cherry tree. The ball hit the branch and made some cherries fall down. Mia was very happy and said, "Thank you, Bow! Now I can eat cherries." Bow wagged his tail and smiled. He was happy that he could help Mia. They both sat under the cherry tree and enjoyed eating cherries together. From that day on, Bow and Mia became best friends and played in the park every day.
