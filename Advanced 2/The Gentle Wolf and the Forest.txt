Once upon a time in a big forest, there lived a gentle wolf. He was called Wolfy. Every morning and evening, he used to count the number of animals living in the forest. One morning, as usual, he started counting the animals. He noticed a sheep and said, "One." Then he saw two rabbits and said, "Two." Suddenly Wolfy noticed another wolf. He turned to the other wolf and said, "Do you want to be part of my numbers?" The other wolf smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to." Wolfy and the other wolf played and laughed together, counting the animals and birds in the forest. When it was time for Wolfy to go home, the other wolf said, "I have learnt one very important thing from you. I have learnt that it's better to make friends than to just count numbers." Wolfy smiled and said, "I'm glad that you understand. Being gentle and kind to others will always help us in life." Moral of the story - It's important to be kind and gentle to others. Don't just count numbers, make friends!

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a girl named Sue. Sue liked to stir her soup. She had a big spoon for stirring. Sue had a mom who sewed with a needle. Her mom was very good at sewing. Sue knew her mom was reliable. One day, Sue saw a hole in her favorite toy. She was sad. She asked her mom to help her. Her mom said, "I will fix it with my needle." Sue was happy because she knew her mom was reliable. Sue's mom sewed the hole in the toy. While she sewed, Sue stirred her soup. She was happy to stir her soup and watch her mom sew. When her mom finished sewing, Sue's toy was fixed. Sue knew her mom was reliable, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily was very adventurous. She loved to play and explore the house. One day, she found a big stove in the kitchen. The stove was hot, but Lily did not know. Lily wanted to play with the stove. She saw a big pot on the stove. She tried to open the pot, but it was too hot. Lily did not want to give up. She was very adventurous. She tried to close the pot, but it was still too hot. Lily did not listen to her mommy, who told her not to play with the stove. She got hurt and cried a lot. Mommy was sad too. From that day, Lily learned not to play with the stove. It was a bad ending for adventurous Lily.

Once upon a time there was a little boy named Peter. Peter loved to play with his toy train. One day Peter's train stopped working. He tried to make it go but it wouldn't move. "What's the matter?" asked mum. "The train won't go!" said Peter. "Let me have a look," said mum. She examined the train and said, "I think there is no steam. We need to keep it patient and filled up with steam." So mum took Peter to the steam station. The station was filled with hissing noises and clouds of steam. Mum and Peter filled the train with steam until it bubbled and gurgled. Then they took the train outside and watched it chug around the track. Peter smiled and said, "Thanks mum, I will keep it patient and make sure it has enough steam!"

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big red ball. She liked to bounce the ball. She would bounce it high and low, fast and slow. Lucy loved her big red ball. One day, Lucy saw a mirror on the wall. She looked at the mirror and saw something different. She saw a little girl with a big red ball just like hers. "Hi, my name is Lucy. Do you want to play?" she asked the little girl in the mirror. The little girl in the mirror said, "Yes, let's play!" Lucy and the little girl in the mirror started to bounce their red balls together. But then, something unexpected happened! When Lucy bounced her ball, the little girl in the mirror didn't bounce her ball. Instead, she bounced a big blue ball! Lucy was surprised, but she liked the different ball. They played together, bouncing their red and blue balls, and they became the best of friends.
