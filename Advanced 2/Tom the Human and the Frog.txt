Once upon a time, in a green forest, there lived a human named Tom. Tom liked to think a lot. He thought about the trees, the birds, and the animals that lived in the forest. One day, Tom met a green frog. The frog was sad because it could not find its family. Tom wanted to help the frog, so he thought very hard. He thought about where the frog's family could be. Tom and the green frog looked around the forest. They looked under rocks and behind trees. Finally, they found the frog's family by the river. The green frog was very happy, and so was Tom. They had a fun day playing together in the forest.

Mummy was very eager to find the kitten. She had dropped it at the park earlier that morning, but now it was nowhere to be seen. She looked at little Tommy and said, "Come on, Tommy! Let's look for your kitty!" Tommy just smiled and ran off, eager to find his pet. The pair searched high and low all over the park, but could not seem to find the kitten. As they were about to give up and call it a day, Tommy heard a meow coming from a bush. He ran over, excitedly, and sure enough, there was the kitten! He quickly scooped it up and ran back to his Mummy. "Look, Mummy! I found kitty!" he said as he hugged it close. Mummy was so happy and gave Tommy a big hug. "Well done, my boy! You did a great job finding kitty!" she said. From that day on, Tommy knew he could always rely on his determination and eagerness to get the job done.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a mighty tiger. The tiger had many friends. They liked to play games and have fun together. One day, the tiger's friends decided to surprise him. They hid behind trees and waited for the tiger to come. When the tiger walked by, they jumped out and shouted, "Surprise!" The tiger was so happy and laughed with his friends. After playing all day, the tiger and his friends sat down to rest. They talked about how much fun they had and how good it felt to make someone happy. The friends learned that making others happy can make you happy too. And from that day on, they always tried to be kind and surprise each other with fun and love.

Once upon a time, there was a small farm. On the farm, there was a small cow named Mimi. Mimi loved to eat hay. One day, Mimi saw a big pile of hay and wanted to eat it all. Mimi's friend, a small bird named Titi, came to the farm. Titi saw Mimi eating the hay and said, "Mimi, you should not eat all the hay. It is not good to eat too much." Mimi did not listen and kept eating the hay. Titi was sad because Mimi did not listen. The next day, Mimi felt bad. She had eaten too much hay and her tummy hurt. Titi came to Mimi and said, "Mimi, you need to change. You should not eat too much hay." Mimi listened to Titi and said, "You are right, Titi. I will change and not eat too much hay anymore." From that day on, Mimi only ate a little hay and felt much better.

Once upon a time, there was a small boy named Tim. He loved to play in the sand. One day, Tim went to the beach with his mom and dad. He built a big sand castle with his toys. He was very happy. A persistent little crab saw Tim's sand castle. The crab wanted to live in the castle. But Tim didn't want the crab to break his castle. He thought of a plan. He would freeze the sand castle to make it strong. Tim asked his mom for some ice. He put the ice on the sand castle. The ice made the sand cold and hard. The crab tried to break the castle but it could not. Tim was happy that his castle was safe. The crab found a new home in a shell. Tim, the crab, and his family had a fun day at the beach.
