One day, daddy and his little girl were playing in the park. Daddy ran around making funny faces and silly jokes to make the girl laugh. He ran faster than the girl, and she chased him all around the playground. Suddenly, daddy stopped and crouched down. "Where are you going, Daddy?!" asked the little girl, her eyes wide with anticipation. Daddy looked around and motioned for the little girl to come closer. "Look!" said Daddy, pointing at the pond. The little girl squinted her eyes and looked close. Suddenly, she saw it: a little baby duckling, waddling around all alone. The girl gasped in delight, and started to urge Daddy to get the little duckling. "It looks so cute, Daddy! Let's get it!" However, Daddy shook his head. "No way, silly," he said. "We can't just take it away from its mommy. We have to let nature do its work." The little girl pouted and sighed, disappointed. However, Daddy patted her head and said, "Don't worry, honey. I'm sure if we come back tomorrow, the baby duckling will have grown a bit bigger." The little girl nodded her head and smiled, content with Daddy's suggestion. The two of them left the park, eagerly awaiting their next visit.

Lily was a little otter who liked to play in the river with her friends. One day, she saw a big fish swimming near the bank. She wanted to catch it and share it with her friends. She prepared to jump on the fish, but then she heard a loud splash. "Who are you?" Lily asked, turning around. She saw an unknown otter who was bigger and darker than her. He had a scar on his nose and a mean look in his eyes. "I am Rex, and this is my river. You are not welcome here. Go away, or I will bite you!" the unknown otter said, showing his sharp teeth. Lily was scared, but she did not want to give up her fish. She said, "No, this is our river. We live here with our family and friends. You are the one who should go away. You are not nice!" Rex growled and swam towards Lily. He tried to grab her tail, but Lily was quick and dodged him. She saw her friends coming to help her. They all barked and splashed at Rex, making him angry and confused. "Leave us alone, Rex! You are not stronger than us. We are a team!" Lily's friends said. Rex realized he was outnumbered and outmatched. He decided to swim away and look for another river. He muttered, "Fine, I will go. But I will be back. You will see!" Lily and her friends cheered and hugged each other. They were happy and proud. They thanked Lily for being brave and smart. They shared the big fish and enjoyed their meal. They learned that they could face any unknown danger if they stuck together.

One day, a little car wanted to go up a big hill. The car was scared because it was not easy for it. The car had a friend, a big truck, who wanted to help. The truck said, "You can do it, little car! I will encourage you to go up the hill." The little car felt happy and brave with the help of the truck. They started to go up the hill. The little car went slow, but the truck stayed with it. The truck said, "Keep going, little car! You can do it!" The little car went up, up, up the hill. At the top, the little car felt proud and said, "Thank you, big truck, for encouraging me!"

Once upon a time, there was a humble flower. The flower was little and lived in a big field. The flower loved the sun and the rain. The flower did not move, but it was happy. One day, a big wind came. The wind was strong and made the flower move. The flower was scared. The wind took the flower up, up, up into the sky. The flower saw the big field below and was sad. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind took the flower to a new field. The new field had many flowers, just like the humble flower. The flower was not alone anymore. The flower was happy again.
