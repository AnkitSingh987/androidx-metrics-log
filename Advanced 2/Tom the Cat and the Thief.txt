Once upon a time, there was a nice cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a little girl named Sue. They played together every day. One day, while they were playing, a thief came into the house. The thief wanted to take Sue's toy. Sue was sad and cried, "No, please don't take my toy!" Tom saw the thief and wanted to help his friend. Tom ran to the thief and kicked him. The thief was surprised and dropped the toy. Sue picked up her toy and hugged Tom. She said, "Thank you, Tom! You are my hero!" From that day on, they played and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a hot jungle, there lived many animals. There were big elephants, small monkeys, and colorful birds. They all played together and had fun in the jungle. One day, a little monkey wanted to play with the big elephants. But the big elephants did not allow the little monkey to play with them. They said, "You are too small, little monkey. You cannot play with us." The little monkey was very sad. Then, a kind bird saw the sad little monkey and said, "Do not be sad, little monkey. I will play with you." So, the kind bird and the little monkey played together. They had lots of fun. Soon, the big elephants saw how much fun they were having and felt sorry. They said, "Little monkey, you can play with us too." And from that day on, all the animals in the hot jungle played together and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He liked to run, jump, and climb. One day, Tim found a big tree in his yard. It was very tall and looked powerful. Tim wanted to climb the big tree. He put his foot on the first branch and pulled himself up. He climbed higher and higher. Tim felt happy and strong like a powerful superhero. When Tim got to the top, he looked around and saw his play area below. He waved to his mom and dad, who were watching him. Then, he climbed back down. Tim felt proud and couldn't wait to play and climb again tomorrow.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very excited because today was his birthday party. He wanted all his friends to come and play with him. Tim was very persistent in making sure everything was ready for the party. During the party, Tim and his friends played a lot. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Tim was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim was running and he fell down. All his friends stopped and looked at him. Tim was sad that he fell, but then his friends started to laugh. Tim was confused. Why were they laughing? Then, one of his friends pointed up. Tim looked up and saw that when he fell, he hit a big pinata. All the candy from the pinata fell down on him and his friends. They all laughed and ate the candy together. The party was even more fun because of the surprise candy.

One day, a girl named Amy went to the park. She saw her friend Tom. Tom had a big pizza. It was a brilliant pizza with lots of colors. Amy wanted some pizza too. Tom said, "We can share, but first, let's play on the swings." Amy and Tom played on the swings. They had a lot of fun. They also played on the slide and the see-saw. When they were done playing, they went back to the pizza. But the pizza was gone! They looked around and saw a dog with the pizza in its mouth. Amy and Tom were sad. They should have eaten the pizza first. They learned that they need to take care of their things. Sharing is good, but they should be careful too. Next time, they will eat their pizza first and then play.

Once upon a time, there was an adventurous lizard named Lizzy. She loved to play and explore in her big backyard. One day, she met a new friend named Tommy, who was a turtle. They wanted to play together. "Let's play shake the tree!" said Lizzy. Tommy agreed, and they went to a tree in the yard. They took turns shaking the tree, and leaves fell all around them. They laughed and had fun. Suddenly, while they were shaking the tree, a big bird came out of the tree and flew away. Lizzy and Tommy were very surprised. They didn't know the bird was living in the tree. They looked at each other and laughed. Now they had a fun story to tell their friends about their adventurous day.
