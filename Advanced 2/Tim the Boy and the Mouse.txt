Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little boy named Tim and his cat, Mimi. They loved to play together. One day, Tim found a fake toy mouse. He thought it would be fun to play a game with Mimi. Tim set up a trap with the fake mouse. He put it in a box and called Mimi. "Mimi, come here! Look what I found!" Mimi ran to Tim and saw the toy mouse in the box. Mimi was excited and tried to catch the mouse, but it was not real. Mimi reminded Tim, "This is not a real mouse, but it is fun to play with!" They laughed and played with the fake mouse all day long. The trap turned into a fun game for both Tim and Mimi. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a high house on a hill. In this house, there lived a girl named Lily and her dog, Max. They were best friends and loved to play and hug each other every day. The house was very big and had many rooms to play in. One day, Lily and Max were playing hide and seek in the house. Max was very good at hiding, and Lily had to look in every room to find him. While she was looking, she saw a door she had never seen before. It was a little door, just big enough for her to go through. Lily opened the door and went inside. It was very dark and she could not see Max. She called out for him, but he did not come. Lily was scared and wanted a hug from Max. She tried to go back out the door, but it was locked. Lily was stuck in the dark room and could not get out. She cried for Max and her mommy, but no one could hear her.

Imagine a brilliant monkey. He was so clever, he could do all sorts of tricks. He would jump around and swing from tree to tree. He made everyone around him laugh. One day, he decided to do something extra special. He used his tail to tie a knot in a long rope. Everyone was amazed. He had made a giant swing! He was so proud of himself, he leapt onto the swing and started to soar. He flew so high that he could see the entire world from the top of the world. Everyone below shouted with amazement and joy. The monkey enjoyed his moment of glory for as long as it lasted. Eventually, he had to come back down to earth. But he had given everyone a brilliant surprise!

Once upon a time, there was a big gorilla named Bobo. Bobo lived in a regular house with his friend, a small bird named Tiki. They liked to play games and eat fruit together. One day, Bobo found a sharp stick in the yard. He showed it to Tiki and said, "Look, Tiki! I found a sharp stick. What can we do with it?" Tiki thought for a moment and said, "Let's use it to draw in the dirt!" As they were drawing in the dirt, a big wind came and blew the sharp stick away. Bobo and Tiki were sad. They looked and looked, but they could not find the sharp stick. They decided to sit under a tree and rest. Suddenly, the tree started to talk! "I have your sharp stick, Bobo and Tiki," said the tree. Bobo and Tiki were very surprised. The tree gave them back the sharp stick and said, "Be careful with this. It can hurt if you're not careful." Bobo and Tiki thanked the tree and promised to be careful with the sharp stick. From that day on, they had a new friend, the talking tree.

One day, a girl named Amy got a new lotion. She was very happy. The lotion smelled like flowers. She wanted to show her friend, Tom. Amy went to Tom's house. "Look at my new lotion," she said. Tom smelled it. "Wow, it smells good!" he said. They played with the lotion, putting it on their hands. Then, something happened. The lotion made their hands change colors! Amy's hands turned blue and Tom's hands turned green. They were surprised and laughed. They showed their colorful hands to their moms and dads. Everyone thought it was funny. From that day on, Amy and Tom loved playing with their magic lotion.
