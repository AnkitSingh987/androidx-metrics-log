One day, a famous cat named Tom went to the park. He saw a big glass by a tree. Tom was very thirsty, so he walked to the glass to drink. As he got close, he leaned on the tree to look inside the glass. Just then, a small bird named Sue flew down and said, "Tom, don't drink that! It's not water, it's soap!" Tom was surprised and said, "Oh, thank you, Sue! I almost drank it!" Sue and Tom became good friends. They played in the park all day. And from that day on, they always checked the glass before they drank from it.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. She had a big, shiny jewel. It was very pretty and original. Lily loved her jewel so much. Every day, she would polish it to make it even more shiny. One day, while Lily was polishing her jewel, a little bird came to her. The bird said, "Lily, can I see your shiny jewel?" Lily said, "Yes, you can look, but please don't touch." The bird looked at the jewel, but then something unexpected happened. The bird took the jewel in its beak and flew away! Lily was very sad. She didn't know what to do. But then, the bird came back. It had a surprise. The bird had found another jewel! Now, Lily had two shiny, original jewels. She was so happy, and she promised to share her jewels with her new bird friend.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy little girl named Lucy. She loved to design things with her crayons. One day, Lucy wanted to make a new friend. So she thought of a fun idea. Lucy decided to design a funny vegetable friend. She took a big piece of paper and her crayons. She drew a big round face with a smile. Then she added some long green arms and legs. Lucy was very happy with her new friend. Lucy showed her vegetable friend to her mom and dad. They smiled and said it was the best vegetable friend they had ever seen. Lucy played with her new friend all day. They had fun and laughed together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small nation, there lived a selfish king. He always wanted everything for himself. The people in the nation were not happy with the king. One day, a wise old man came to the king. The wise man told the king that he must learn to stand up for his people, not just for himself. The king did not like what the wise man said, but he listened. As time went by, the king started to change. He learned to stand up for his people and not be selfish. The nation became a happy place, and the people loved their king. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man named Mr. Green. He lived in a big house with his family. Mr. Green liked to stretch his arms wide and say, "I have so much, I am so lucky!" But he never shared with others. One day, Mr. Green and his family went on a holiday. They went to the beach and played in the sand. Mr. Green saw a little boy who had no toys to play with. The little boy looked sad. Mr. Green thought about how lucky he was to have so many things. He decided to share his toys with the little boy. They played together and had lots of fun. The little boy was very happy. When the holiday was over, Mr. Green learned that sharing made him feel good. He started sharing with others in his town. Everyone was happy and thankful. The moral of the story is that sharing with others can make you and everyone around you happy.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a boy named Mark. Mark liked to sell his toys to his friends. He had a small table where he put all his toys for sale. The sun was shining and the birds were singing. It was a happy day. One day, Mark wanted to sell his favorite toy car. He put a big sign that said "Toy Car for Sale." His friends saw the sign and came to look at the toy car. They all wanted to buy it because it was very pretty and shiny. Mark's friends gave him some money, and he sold the toy car to them. They were all very happy, and they played with the toy car together. Mark felt good that he could share his toys with his friends. The town stayed peaceful and everyone had a fun time playing with their new toy car.
