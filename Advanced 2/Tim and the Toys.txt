One day, a boy named Tim went to the toy store with his mom. He wanted to choose a new toy to play with. They looked at all the toys, but Tim could not decide which one he wanted. Then, Tim saw a harmless missile toy. It was made of soft foam, so it would not hurt anyone. He thought it would be fun to play with. He showed the missile to his mom and said, "I choose this toy, please." His mom said yes, and they bought the harmless missile. Tim played with it all day and had lots of fun. He was happy with his choice and shared the toy with his friends too.

Billy was a young boy who loved to explore. Every day he wanted to try something new and different. One day, he asked his mom if he could go to the grocery store all by himself. His mom said no, it was too dangerous and she didn't permit it. Billy was very disappointed but he accepted her decision. The next day, Billy again asked his mom if he could go to the grocery store. His mom thought about it long and hard. Eventually, she said he could go, but only with his younger brother. Billy was very excited and he hugged his mom. Billy and his brother went to the grocery store. It was a very ordinary grocery store, but Billy was still excited. As they were walking around they saw a big white truck. The driver said he had candy and toys in the back of the truck and he offered to give them some. Billy and his brother were tempted, but they remembered their mom had said to not talk to strangers. The driver then began to get angry. He said that if they didn't take the candy and the toys, he would be very mad. Billy and his brother got scared and they ran away quickly. They were so scared that they didn't think to tell their mom or anyone else. The next day, Billy and his brother were missing. Billy's mom was very upset and she went looking for them. She searched for days but could not find them.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a suit. He did not like the suit. He thought it was boring. Tim's mom said, "You must stay and wear the suit today." "But I don't like it," said Tim. "It's boring!" Mom said, "You have to wear it for the party. Everyone will be there." Tim put on the boring suit. He went to the party. At the party, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam wore a suit too. They played together and had fun. They did not think their suits were boring anymore. They stayed at the party and had a great time.

One day, a cat named Tom was sitting on a rug. He was very alert, always looking around. He did not want any trouble. Tom liked to mind his own business. Suddenly, a big bird came into the house. Tom was very surprised. The bird flew around and landed on the rug. Tom did not know what to do. He was not sure if he should be scared or if he should play with the bird. Then, the bird started to talk! Tom could not believe it. The bird said, "Do not be scared, Tom. I am here to be your friend." Tom was very happy. He and the bird became best friends and played on the rug all day.

Once upon a summer, a little girl named Lucy wanted to go outside and play with her friends. The sun was bright, but the sky was still a little wet. Lucy's mom said, “No, it's too wet. You can't go outside.” But Lucy didn't give up. She looked around and thought of a way to play with her friends. Then she had an idea. She asked her mom, “Can I serve them lemonade?” Her mom smiled. “Yes, that's a great idea. We have plenty of lemonade in the fridge.” So Lucy quickly grabbed some glasses and juice and brought them to her friends. When her friends saw the glasses, they cried out “Lemonade!” Everyone was so happy. They all enjoyed the summer day, even if it was a little wet. Lucy was a fantastic server, and she was proud that she found a way to play with her friends after all.
