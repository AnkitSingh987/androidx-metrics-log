Once upon a time, there was a jolly red car named Zoom. Zoom loved to go fast. He would fill up with gas and race down the street. One sunny day, Zoom went to the park with his best friend, a blue bike named Bree. They played together, going fast and slow, up and down the hills. Zoom and Bree had so much fun. As the sun went down, Zoom and Bree said goodbye. Zoom went home, feeling very happy. He knew he would see his friend again soon for more fun and games.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a silver bird and wanted to play with it. Lily was a bit anxious, but she walked up to the bird and said, "Hi, bird! Do you want to dance with me?" The silver bird looked at Lily and said, "Yes, I would love to dance with you!" So they danced and danced, and had lots of fun. Suddenly, the silver bird turned into a big, friendly bear! Lily was very surprised and said, "Wow, you are a bear now! Why did you change?" The bear smiled and told her, "I was a bird who needed a friend to dance with. Your kindness turned me back into a bear. Thank you, Lily!" Lily and the bear became best friends, and they learned that being kind to others can bring happiness and good things.

Molly was a happy lady. One day, she decided to celebrate. She put on her fanciest dress and went outside to talk to her friends. They were all very excited and the nosy neighbours watched as they celebrated. Molly sang a song and her friends clapped. They laughed and talked and enjoyed the party. Suddenly, a little girl called Lucy came running up. She looked so excited and she asked Molly what was going on. Molly laughed and explained, “We are celebrating. Do you want to join us?” Lucy nodded her head and Molly smiled. Together, the lady, her friends and the little girl celebrated the happy day. They sang and danced and had lots of fun. It was a wonderful celebration!

Once upon a time there were two friends, a cat and a mouse. The mouse said to the cat, "Let's go climb the hill." The cat said, "Ok!" They both ran to the top of the hill and stopped to take in the view. The mouse looked out and sighed. He said, "It's so wide! I see the trees, the flowers and the pepper plants!" The cat smiled and said, "Let's get some pepper for dinner." The mouse agreed, so they ran down the hill and started to pick the peppers. The cat said, "These peppers are so thin. I can carry two at once!" The mouse said, "Let me help you carry them." So they filled their arms with peppers and laughed as they climbed back up the hill. When they got to the top, they both sighed with joy. They had enough peppers for dinner and enjoyed the view one last time before they ran back home.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to read books. In her room, she had a big bookcase full of her favorite books. Every day, she would pick a book and read it before bedtime. One day, Lily was feeling upset. She did not know what to do. She went to her bookcase to find a book that would make her happy. As she pulled a book, something surprising happened. The bookcase moved and showed a secret door! Lily was so surprised! She opened the door and found a room full of toys and games. She played with the toys and forgot why she was upset. From that day on, whenever Lily was sad, she would go to the secret room and play. And every time, she would leave with a big smile on her face.

One sunny day, a humble dog named Max went for a walk in the park. Max loved to play with his ball and chase butterflies. While playing, he saw a girl with a big box. The girl was taking pictures of the pretty flowers. Max went to the girl and said, "Hi! What are you doing?" The girl smiled and said, "I am taking pictures of the flowers. They are so pretty!" Max looked at the flowers and saw how pretty they were. He wanted to be in a picture too. The girl said, "Would you like to be in a picture, Max?" Max wagged his tail and said, "Yes, please!" The girl took a picture of Max with the flowers. She showed Max the picture and they both laughed. Then they played together and became good friends.
