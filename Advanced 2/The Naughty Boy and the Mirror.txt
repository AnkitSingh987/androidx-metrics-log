Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy. He was always getting into trouble. One day, his mom had the best idea. She brought a special mirror into his bedroom. Every day she asked him to look at the mirror and talk to it. His mom wanted him to encourage himself. She said things like "you can do it!" and "you are strong!" The little boy liked his mirror. He started to think positive things about himself. He said "I can do it!" and "I am strong!" The mirror encouraged him to be better. Whenever he felt silly or naughty, he looked in the mirror and remembered his mom's words. The little boy had lots of fun with his mirror. He even talked to it when he was happy. He felt so good about himself. With the help of his mom and his mirror, he became a better and happier kid. The end.

One day, a girl named Amy wanted to own a book. She went to the store with her mom to find a new book. Amy saw a big healthy tree and under the tree, she found a novel. She was very happy and picked it up. Amy said to her mom, "Look, I found a novel! Can I keep it?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can own it. It's a very healthy book. Let's read it together." When they started reading the novel, a funny thing happened. The words in the book began to jump and dance around the page. Amy and her mom were very surprised. They did not know that the novel was magical. They laughed and had a lot of fun reading the dancing words.

One day, a wise cat went for a walk. The cat saw a big step. The cat thought, "I can jump on the step!" The cat jumped and landed on the step. The cat felt very happy. While on the step, the cat saw a big red ball. The cat wanted to play with the ball. The cat tried to slap the ball with its paw. The ball moved a little bit, but not much. The cat tried again and again. Then, something unexpected happened. The ball changed! It turned into a big, happy dog. The dog said, "Thank you for playing with me, wise cat!" The cat was very surprised, but happy to have a new friend. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lucy lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Every day, Lucy would help her mom with the dishwasher. She loved to wake up early and open the clear door of the dishwasher to help put the clean dishes away. Lucy's mom always told her that one day, she would find a big surprise inside the dishwasher. One morning, Lucy woke up very early. She was so excited to see what was inside the dishwasher today. She ran to the kitchen and opened the clear door. Inside, she saw a big, shiny, red ball! Lucy was so happy. She knew this was the surprise her mom had talked about. Lucy played with the red ball all day long. She bounced it, threw it, and rolled it. Her mom and dad watched her play and smiled. They were happy that Lucy was having so much fun. That night, as Lucy went to bed, she couldn't wait to wake up and help with the dishwasher again. She knew that every day could bring a new surprise.

Once upon a time, there was a soft bunny named Ben. Ben loved to hop and play with his friends in the big green park. They liked to play a fun game called "send the ball". In this game, they would kick a big round ball and try to send it into a goal. The more goals they got, the more points they would score. One sunny day, Ben and his friends were playing "send the ball" in the park. Ben's friend, Tina the turtle, was very good at the game. She could send the ball far and score lots of points. Ben wanted to be good, too, but he was not as fast as Tina. Ben felt sad, but his friends told him, "Don't worry, Ben! You can be good, too. Just keep trying!" So, Ben practiced and practiced. He learned to send the ball with a soft kick, and soon he could score points just like Tina. Ben was so happy, and his friends cheered for him. They all played "send the ball" together, and they had lots of fun.
