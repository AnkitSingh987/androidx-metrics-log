Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to be organized. He always put his toys away and made his bed. Tom had a red cap that he loved very much. One day, Tom came home from playing outside. He was very tired. Tom went to his room and lay down on his bed. He forgot to put his red cap away. It was on the floor next to his bed. In the morning, Tom woke up and looked for his red cap. He found it next to his bed. Tom remembered that he forgot to put it away. He felt sad. Tom picked up his red cap and put it in its special place. From that day on, Tom always remembered to put his cap away.

Once upon a time, there lived two cats – Amy and Noah. Noah was always getting into trouble. One day, Noah saw a pretty black flame and he curiously went towards it to take a closer look. Amy shouted, "Noah, don't go near that flame! It's too dangerous, you could get burned!". Noah ignored Amy and kept walking towards the flame. Then, suddenly a scary voice said," If you don't step away, I will make the flames even bigger! It's a threat!" Noah got scared and quickly stepped back. He learnt his lesson and realised that he must stay away from anything that has fire. Amy said, "Noah, it's better to be safe than sorry. We mustn't break the rules or else something bad could happen." Noah agreed and said, "You're right Amy! I will never disobey again." From then onwards, Noah and Amy always followed the rules and listened to the advice of their elders.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant who loved to work. He would work all day in the hot sun, carrying food to his ant friends. One day, he met a lazy grasshopper who did not like to work. "Why do you work so much?" asked the grasshopper. "Come and play with me." But the ant said, "No, I must work. Winter is coming, and we need food." The grasshopper laughed and said, "You worry too much. I will be fine." But the ant did not listen and kept working. Soon, winter came, and it was very cold. The ant and his friends were warm and had food because they had worked hard. The grasshopper was cold and hungry. He went to the ant's home and asked for help. But the ant said, "You did not work, so you do not get food." The grasshopper was very sad and went away. In the end, the grasshopper was dead because he did not work and prepare for the winter.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy cat named Tom. Tom lived in a small house with his best friend, a motor car named Max. Tom loved to sleep all day, while Max liked to go fast. They were very different, but they liked each other a lot. One sunny day, Max said to Tom, "Let's go play outside! I can shoot fast like a rocket!" Tom yawned and said, "Okay, let's go!" So, they went outside to play together. Tom climbed on Max's back and they started to go fast. Tom felt the wind on his face and he liked it. As they played, they saw their friend, a little bird named Sue. Sue was flying high in the sky. Max said, "Look, Tom! Sue can shoot up high too!" Tom and Max watched Sue fly and they all played together. They had a fun day, and even lazy Tom was happy to play outside with his friends.

Amanda and her mom were at the park. Amanda said, "Mommy, can I play?" Her mom replied, "Yes, here is some oil so you can fold the leaves together." Amanda looked around and saw plenty of leaves. She grabbed the oil bottle and started folding the leaves together. She wanted to make a mighty tower that she could climb up. Amanda used her little hands and folded the leaves together. Soon, she had folded enough leaves and created a sturdy tower. It was so mighty that Amanda could climb all the way to the top. She smiled proudly from the top and waved to her mom. Her mom cheered and shouted, "You did it, Amanda!"
