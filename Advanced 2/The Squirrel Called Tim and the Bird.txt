Once upon a time, there was a squirrel called Tim who lived in the woods. He was very generous and would give out nuts to all his friends in the woods. One day, Tim found a really big nut that he could not reach. So he asked a big blue bird for help. "Hello there!", he said. "Can you please help me reach this nut?" The blue bird flew down and kindly agreed to help Tim reach the nut. But when the bird flew back up with the nut in its beak, it flew away and didn't give the nut back to Tim. Tim was very sad, as he had no nut to give to his friends. He was also very angry with the blue bird, for not being generous and taking the nut away. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big billboard. The billboard was wide and colorful. It showed a picture of a happy cat. The cat was smiling and holding a big box. The box had the word "deliver" on it. Everyone who saw the billboard liked it very much. One day, a little boy named Timmy walked by the billboard with his mom. Timmy looked at the happy cat and said, "Mom, what does 'deliver' mean?" His mom smiled and said, "Deliver means to take something from one place to another, like a package or a letter." Timmy thought about it and said, "I want to deliver something too!" His mom thought it was a good idea. So they went home and made a package for Timmy's friend, Sally. Timmy put a toy car and a drawing inside the package. Then, he and his mom went to Sally's house to deliver it. When they got to Sally's house, Timmy knocked on the door. Sally opened it and saw Timmy holding the package. "What's this?" she asked. Timmy said, "I wanted to deliver something, so I made a package for you!" Sally was very happy and thanked Timmy. They played with the toy car together and had lots of fun. From that day on, Timmy loved to deliver packages to his friends. And every time he walked by the wide, colorful billboard, he smiled at the happy cat and felt proud.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a cool pencil. The pencil was red and blue. Tim liked the pencil a lot. He wanted to draw with it all day. But he had to hurry, because it was time for lunch. Tim's sister, Sue, saw the cool pencil too. She wanted it. Tim did not want to share. They both wanted to draw with the cool pencil. This made them sad and mad. Mom saw Tim and Sue fighting. She said, "You both can use the cool pencil. Tim, you can draw before lunch. Sue, you can draw after lunch." Tim and Sue were happy. They both got to use the cool pencil.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a motor. This motor was part of a big red car. The car had a driver named Tom. Tom loved to drive his big red car around town. People would wave and smile when they saw Tom and his car. One day, Tom met a new friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim was very persistent. He asked Tom if he could ride in the big red car. Tom said, "Yes, but you have to accept my help to get in." Tim was happy and accepted Tom's help. They had a fun ride together. The next day, Tim saw the big red car again. He remembered how much fun he had with Tom. Tim asked Tom for another ride. Tom smiled and said, "If you still accept my help, we can go on another ride." Tim agreed, and they had another great day together. From that day on, Tom and Tim became the best of friends, all because Tim was persistent and accepted Tom's help.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a small nest on a roof. One day, the wind blew very hard. The roof started to rise and fall. Billy felt dizzy. Billy was scared. He did not want to fall off the roof. He knew he had to fly away to a safer place. But he was still dizzy and scared to fly. The wind kept blowing and the roof kept rising and falling. Then, Billy's friend, a big bird named Tommy, saw him. Tommy flew to the roof and helped Billy. They flew away together to a big tree. The tree was safe and strong. Billy was happy and not dizzy anymore.
