Once upon a time there was a brave guard. He used to travel all around the kingdom, making sure everyone was safe. One day, as he was travelling, he came across a compassionate old lady. She was very alone and in need of help. The guard spoke to her kindly and asked her why she was there. She told the guard a sad story about how she had been travelling for weeks and had become very tired. The guard was full of compassion for the old lady. He offered to keep travelling with her so she could have someone to help her along the way. The old lady thanked the guard with a big smile. Together they continued to travel until they safely got to the old lady's home. The guard was very happy to have been able to help the old lady and to show her the compassion needed to make her journey better.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there was a lot of hay. A cow named Bessie lived on the farm. Bessie liked to eat hay. Bessie was very big and flexible. She could bend her body and reach the hay easily. One day, Bessie's friend, a small mouse named Mimi, came to visit. Mimi saw Bessie eating the hay. Mimi said, "Bessie, why do you eat so much hay?" Bessie said, "I love hay. It is yummy and it makes me strong." Later that day, the farmer came to weigh Bessie. He wanted to see how big she was. Bessie was scared. She did not want the farmer to know she ate so much hay. So, Bessie used her flexible body and hid some hay under her belly. The farmer weighed Bessie and said, "You are just the right size, Bessie." But then, something unexpected happened. The hay under Bessie's belly fell out! The farmer saw the hay and laughed. He said, "Bessie, you are very clever. But it is okay to eat hay. That is what cows do." Bessie and Mimi laughed too. They were happy that the farmer was not mad.

Once upon a time, there was a tutor named Mr. Fox. He had many students who loved to learn. Mr. Fox was very good at teaching. One day, he wanted to play a game with his students. He told them to hide in the deep forest and he would find them. The students ran and hid in the deep forest. They were very excited. Mr. Fox started to look for them. He found some students behind trees and others under bushes. But there was one student he could not find. It was a little mouse named Tim. Tim had hidden very well, but he was scared. He was so deep in the forest that he could not hear Mr. Fox or the other students. Tim started to cry. Mr. Fox heard him and went to find him. He told Tim not to be scared and took him back to the others. The students learned that it was important to stay close to their friends and not go too far away. They also learned to listen to their teacher, Mr. Fox. They thanked him for teaching them this lesson. From that day on, they always stayed close to each other and listened to Mr. Fox.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. He had a best friend named Sam. They liked to play together every day. One day, Tim's mom told them to behave while playing outside. While they were playing, Tim saw a little bug. He wanted to touch it. "Be careful with your finger, Tim!" said Sam. Tim didn't listen and touched the bug with his finger. The bug bit him, and it hurt a lot. Tim cried and ran to his mom. She saw his finger and knew what happened. "I told you to behave, Tim," she said. She put a bandage on his finger, and it felt better. Tim learned his lesson and always behaved when playing outside.

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk. She saw a dirty figure on the ground. Amy looked at it and said, "What are you?" The dirty figure looked up and said, "I am a toy. I am lost and need help to find my home." Amy picked up the toy and they walked together. They looked for the toy's home, but they could not find it. They were sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the dirt off the toy. The toy was not a toy at all. It was a magic fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for helping me, Amy. I will take you to my home now." And they both flew away to the fairy's home, happy and full of joy.
