Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a toy dog named Max. Lily loved Max very much, but Max was jealous of the TV. Lily liked to watch her shows and Max wanted to play with her. One day, the TV stopped working. Lily was sad and didn't know what to do. Max saw that Lily was sad and wanted to help. Max had an idea to make Lily happy again. Max found a ball and brought it to Lily. He wagged his tail and barked. Lily saw Max and smiled. She played with Max and the ball, and they had so much fun. Max was happy that he could help Lily, and he wasn't jealous of the TV anymore. They played together and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She loved exploring the beach and searching for seashells. On one particular day, Lucy found something special. "Wow! What is this?" Lucy shouted. "Let me guess," said her daddy. "It's a jellyfish!" Lucy smiled and looked at the jellyfish carefully. She saw it moving around and noticed how flexible it was. "Can I pick it up?" asked Lucy. "Yes, as long as you're careful. It's very delicate," replied her daddy. So Lucy carefully picked up the jellyfish and held it in her hands. She was amazed by how light and flexible it was. "This is so cool!" she exclaimed. "Yes, it is!" said her daddy with a smile.

Once upon a time, in a big pond, there lived a swan named Lily. Lily loved to drink water all day long. One day, while Lily was drinking water, she saw a frog named Fred. "Hi, Fred!" said Lily. "Do you want to drink water with me?" Fred looked at Lily and said, "Yes, Lily! But can we try something new? I know a place where the water is spicy!" Lily was curious and said, "Spicy water? I want to try!" So, Lily and Fred went to the place where the spicy water was. They took a big drink and their mouths felt hot! "Oh no!" said Lily. "This water is too spicy for me!" Fred laughed and said, "It's okay, Lily. Let's go back to our pond and drink the cool water." Lily and Fred went back to their pond and drank the cool water. They were happy to have tried something new, but they liked their pond water best.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to make a modest fort. She took her toys and some big boxes. She put them all together to make her fort. Sue was very happy with her fort. She decided to take a nap inside. While Sue was napping, her friend Tom came over. Tom saw the fort and thought it was cool. He wanted to play inside too. Tom said, "Can I play in your fort, Sue?" But Sue was still napping and did not hear him. Tom went inside the fort to play. Suddenly, the fort started to shake! The big boxes fell down and the toys went everywhere. Sue woke up and saw what happened. She was not mad at Tom. They both laughed and decided to make an even bigger fort together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Cat. Cat was three years old and she loved to explore. One day, she decided to go on an adventure. On her journey, Cat stumbled upon an original post. She wondered what might happen if she touched it. Then, a small voice said, "What are you doing?" Cat turned around and saw her brother, Sam. Sam said, "That post is special. You shouldn't touch it." But Cat just couldn't resist. She touched the post and something magical happened. She found herself in a beautiful garden filled with colourful flowers. Cat and Sam explored the garden together. They had the most wonderful afternoon! Afterwards, they returned home, and Cat swore never to touch an original post again.

Anna likes to read. She has many books in her room. She reads stories about animals, princesses, and pirates. She also has a diary. A diary is a book where she writes her secrets and feelings. She does not let anyone read her diary. One day, her brother Tom comes into her room. He is bored and wants to play. He sees Anna's diary on her bed. He is curious and picks it up. "Hey, what is this?" he asks. Anna hears him and runs to him. She is angry and scared. "Tom, give me that! That is my diary! You cannot read it!" she shouts. Tom laughs and holds the diary high. He tries to open it. "Why not? What are you hiding? Are you writing about me?" he teases. Anna jumps and tries to grab the diary. She is very upset. "Tom, stop it! That is not nice! That is not fair! That is mine!" she cries. Tom thinks it is funny to see Anna so mad. He does not care about her feelings. He is not smart. He is mean. But then, he hears a loud bark. It is Rex, their dog. Rex likes Anna more than Tom. Rex sees that Tom is making Anna sad. Rex does not like that. Rex runs to Tom and jumps on him. He makes him drop the diary. Anna catches the diary and hugs it. She thanks Rex and pets him. She is happy and relieved. Tom is surprised and scared. He falls on the floor. He does not like Rex. He thinks Rex is big and scary. "Rex, get off me! Go away!" he yells. Anna smiles and sticks out her tongue. She is smart. She knows how to protect her diary. She also knows how to read. She reads her diary and forgets about Tom. She is happy.
