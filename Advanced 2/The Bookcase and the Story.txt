Once upon a time, there was a big bookcase. It was very organized. All the books were happy on the bookcase. They liked to be in their right place. One day, a new book came. The new book was big and wanted a spot on the bookcase. The big book tried to find a spot, but there was no room. The other books did not want to move. They liked their spots. The big book was sad. It wanted to be stored on the bookcase too. The big book thought and thought. It had an idea! The big book started to talk to the other books. It told them a fun story. The other books liked the story. They all laughed and had fun. Then, something unexpected happened. The books moved a little to make room for the big book. Now, the big book had a spot on the organized bookcase too. All the books were happy together.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim had a journal. He loved to draw in it every day. One day, while he was drawing, a girl named Sue came over. Sue wanted to see what Tim was drawing. But Tim did not want to show her. Sue felt sad. Later, Tim saw Sue kneeling down, looking at a flower. Tim was sorry for being rude. He walked over to Sue and said, "I am sorry for not showing you my drawing. Will you be my friend?" Sue smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend." From that day on, Tim and Sue played together every day. One day, they found a magic flower. It could talk! The flower said, "Thank you for being kind to each other. I will give you a wish." Tim and Sue wished to be friends forever. The flower made their wish come true. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Buddy. Buddy loved his family very much. He also loved to admire his bedroom. It was full of toys and a big, soft bed. One day, Buddy's family went out to play. Buddy stayed in his bedroom. He heard a strange sound. Buddy was scared, but he knew he had to protect his family's home. Buddy went to find the sound. It was a little bird who was lost. Buddy helped the little bird find its way home. The little bird thanked Buddy and flew away. When Buddy's family came home, they were so proud of him. They saw that Buddy was brave and kind. The moral of the story is to always be loyal and help others, even when you are scared.

Once upon a time, there was a big, ugly bug who lived in a corn field. He had no friends because everyone was scared of him. The ugly bug was very sad and lonely. One day, a small, cute bird came to the corn field. The ugly bug was scared, but the bird said, "Hi, I'm not scared of you. Let's be friends and play together." The ugly bug was very happy and they played all day long. Later, the ugly bug and the bird found a big part of corn on the ground. They were both hungry and wanted to eat it. But then, a big wind came and took the corn away. The ugly bug and the bird were sad, but they still had each other. They knew that their friendship was more important than the corn, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to walk with her aunt. They walked to the park and played on the swings. They walked to the store and bought ice cream. They walked to the pond and fed the ducks. Sally and her aunt had so much fun together. One day, Sally met a boy named Timmy. Timmy was a selfish boy. He did not like to share his toys or his snacks. Sally wanted to play with Timmy, but he always said no. Sally was sad, but her aunt told her to be patient. Sally and her aunt walked to the park again. They saw Timmy sitting all alone. Sally's aunt talked to Timmy and told him that sharing is good. Timmy listened and decided to share his toys with Sally. They all played together and had fun. From that day on, Timmy was not selfish anymore.
