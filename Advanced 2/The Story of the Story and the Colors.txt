Once upon a time, in a beautiful land, there were many colors. The colors were friends and liked to play together. They made the land bright and happy. Everyone loved the colors and how they made everything look nice. One day, a new color came to the land. This color was different and not as bright. Some of the colors did not want to play with the new color. They tried to resist and stay away. But, the new color was kind and wanted to be friends with everyone. Slowly, the other colors started to play with the new color. They found out that the new color was fun and could make beautiful things too. In the end, all the colors played together and made the land even more beautiful. The moral of the story is that we should not resist someone just because they are different. We can all be friends and make the world a beautiful place.

Once there was a tree. It was tall and strong, full of green leaves. Everyone who saw it was envious because it was so beautiful. One day, a little girl spotted the tree and decided that she wanted the same leaves for her house. To do this, she went to the store to get some paper and markers. The girl picked up the paper and began to match the leaves from the tree. She spent the entire day carefully tracing each leaf and colouring them in. When she was finished, she had a brand new set of leaves! The girl was so proud of her work that she decided to put the leaves up on a wall in her room. She wrote a word to go with them - “Match” - so that everyone who saw them would know where they came from. The little girl was envious of the tree, but now she has her very own set of leaves. They always remind her of the beautiful tree, and what she can achieve when she puts her mind to it.

One day, a little girl named Maddie went on a walk outside. She was so excited to explore all the flowers and the green grass. She took a deep breath and whispered, "This is the best day ever!" Maddie kept walking, and soon, she found a strange object. It was shaped like a heart! She was so happy to find it and she quickly showed it to her mom. When Maddie's mom saw it, she smiled, her eyes lighting up. "Maddie, this is a special heart! It can tell us if someone is feeling impatient." Maddie looked at her mom with big eyes, excited to find out what the heart could do. Together, they fiddled and turned with the heart. Suddenly, the heart lit up and started to beat faster. Maddie and her mom knew that someone was feeling impatient!

One day, a little boy named Tim found an ugly pencil on the ground. He picked it up and saw that it was very old and dirty. Tim wanted to show the pencil to his best friend, Sam. Tim ran to Sam's house with the ugly pencil in his hand. When he got there, he said, "Look, Sam! I found an ugly pencil!" Sam looked at the pencil and laughed. "It is very ugly," he agreed. "Let's draw with it." They took turns drawing with the ugly pencil. Tim drew a big sun and Sam drew a funny cat. They laughed and had fun with the ugly pencil. Even though it was ugly, it made them happy to draw together. And that is how Tim and Sam learned that even ugly things can be fun and special.

Ben and Mia are friends. They like to play with their bikes. They have red bikes with two wheels and pedals. They can ride fast and slow, and turn and stop. One day, they see a big hill. They want to go up the hill and see what is on the other side. But the hill is too steep and hard. They try to pedal, but they get tired and stop. They look at each other and feel sad. Then, they have an idea. They decide to unite their bikes. They tie a rope from Ben's bike to Mia's bike. They say, "Now we are a team. We can help each other. We can do it!" They start to pedal again. They push and pull with the rope. They work together. They go up the hill. They feel happy and proud. They reach the top of the hill. They see a charming view. They see a lake and a forest and a rainbow. They smile and hug. They say, "We did it! We are a great team. We are friends."
