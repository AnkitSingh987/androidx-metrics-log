Once upon a time, there was a foolish little squirrel. He wanted to print something, so he ran to the printing shop. The shopkeeper gave him some paper. He then asked for some milk, but the shopkeeper said there wasn't any. The squirrel was very disappointed and asked 'Why not?' The shopkeeper laughed and said, 'You're so foolish, squirrel! Printing doesn't need milk, it needs ink.' The squirrel felt embarrassed, but still wanted the milk. He begged and pleaded with the shopkeeper, hoping he would get some. But the shopkeeper refused and said he could only give him ink. The squirrel went back home without the milk and without any printing done. He was very sad and never went back to the printing shop again.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a little girl named Lily. One day, Lily found a little key under her bed. She did not know what the key was for, but she knew it was special. She put the key in her pocket and went to play outside. While playing, Lily saw a little bird. The bird was trying to join its friends in a tree, but it could not fly. Lily felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. She remembered the little key in her pocket and wondered if it could help the bird. Lily took the key out of her pocket and held it up to the sky. Suddenly, the key began to shine. It turned into a magic key! The magic key helped the little bird fly up and join its friends in the tree. Lily was so happy that she could help the bird. The little key was not just special, it was magic!

Once upon a time, there was a little cricket. The cricket liked to jump and play in the calm grass. The cricket had many friends like ants, bees, and butterflies. One day, the cricket and his friends wanted to play a game. They decided to separate into two teams. The cricket said, "I will be on one team, and my friend ant can be on the other team." The other bugs joined the teams too. The cricket's team and the ant's team played a fun game of tag. They all laughed and had a great time. In the end, they all agreed to play together again soon. The little cricket was happy to have such good friends.

Once upon a time, there was strong Paul. He was a brave boy and he wanted to win a race. He went up to a big hill and saw lots of other boys and girls ready to solve the race. Everyone was very excited. Paul said, "I am the strongest and I will win!" Just then, another boy came up and said, "No, I will win!" Paul looked very mad and said, "Let's race and we will see who can solve it first!" So, the race began. Paul ran very fast, but the other boy was faster. Paul couldn't catch up. In the end, the other boy won the race. Paul was very upset and he cried. Everyone else felt bad for Paul, but there was nothing they could do. Paul felt so sad, he went home and never raced again.

Anna and Ben like to play hide and seek. They take turns to hide and seek. Anna counts to ten and Ben runs to hide. He finds a big box in the garage and climbs inside. He thinks Anna will not find him there. Anna finishes counting and says, "Ready or not, here I come!" She looks for Ben in the house, in the garden, in the shed. She cannot find him. She wonders where he is. She goes to the garage and sees the big box. She has an idea. She tiptoes to the box and knocks on it. She says, "Hello, is anyone there?" Ben hears her voice and stays quiet. He thinks he is very clever. Anna knocks again and says, "Please, can you help me? I am looking for my friend Ben. He is hiding from me and I miss him." Ben feels sorry for Anna. He thinks she is sad. He opens the box and says, "Surprise! I am here!" Anna smiles and says, "I found you! You are a good hider, Ben. But I am a good seeker, too." Ben laughs and says, "You are, Anna. You are very smart." They hug and say, "You are my best friend." They decide to play again. Ben counts to ten and Anna runs to hide. She finds a pale curtain in the living room and hides behind it. She thinks Ben will not find her there.
