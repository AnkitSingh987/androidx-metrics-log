Lily and Tom were playing in the backyard. They liked to dig in the dirt and look for bugs and rocks. Lily found a shiny thing in the dirt. She picked it up and showed it to Tom. "Look, Tom, I found a shiny thing!" Lily said. Tom looked at the shiny thing. It was long and thin and had a sharp edge. He knew what it was. It was a razor. His dad used a razor to shave his face. Tom told Lily to be careful. "Lily, that is a razor. It is not a toy. It can cut you. Give it to me. I will give it to dad," Tom said. Lily did not want to give the razor to Tom. She liked the shiny thing. She wanted to keep it. She said no to Tom. "No, Tom, it is mine. I found it. It is pretty. I want to keep it," Lily said. Tom and Lily started to fight over the razor. They pulled and pushed and shouted. The razor slipped from their hands and fell on the ground. The sun was hot and made the razor hot too. Lily reached for the razor. She touched the hot razor. She felt a pain in her hand. She screamed. "Ow, ow, ow! It hurts, it hurts, it hurts!" Lily screamed. Tom heard Lily scream. He saw her hand. It had a red mark and a drop of blood. He felt sorry for Lily. He ran to her and hugged her. "I'm sorry, Lily. I'm sorry. Are you okay? Let me see your hand. We need to tell mom. She will make it better," Tom said. Lily cried and hugged Tom. She was scared and hurt. She wished she had listened to Tom. She did not like the razor anymore. She wanted mom. Tom and Lily ran to the house. They told mom what happened. Mom cleaned Lily's hand and put a band-aid on it. She hugged Lily and Tom and told them to be more careful. She threw the razor in the trash. She said razors are not for children. They are dangerous. Lily and Tom learned their lesson. They said sorry to each other. They decided to play with something else. They found a ball and a stick. They had fun. They forgot about the razor.

Once upon a time there was a fat port. Every day, sailors would visit and relax. They would sit under the bright blue sky and gaze out to the horizon. One day, a small boy came to the port. He could not understand why the sailors were relaxing, as there was so much to explore and do. “What are ya doin’ sittin’ here?” He said to the sailors. The sailors looked up and smiled. “We’re just taking a break from our travels,” they replied. The boy frowned. “Why don’t we have a race?” he asked. The sailors laughed and shook their heads. “We’re too tired, little one,” they said. The boy looked out to the horizon with anticipation in his eyes. He knew, in that moment, that his racing with the sailors would eventually be fulfilled. And years later, when the boy had grown to be an old man, the fat port was still a stop for sailors. But now, instead of just relaxing, the sailors were racing each other. The old man smiled. He remembered that day long ago, when his adventure had been foreshadowed with the sailors at the port.
