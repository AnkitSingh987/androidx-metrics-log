Once upon a time, there was a cute cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her ball in the yard. One day, Kitty saw a big jar near the fence. She was very curious about the jar. Kitty tried to pass the jar to her friend, Doggy. But when she pushed the jar, something unexpected happened. The jar started to shake and wiggle. Kitty and Doggy were surprised and a little scared. Suddenly, the jar opened and out came a tiny, friendly mouse. The mouse said, "Hi, I am Mousy! I was stuck in the jar. Thank you for helping me!" Kitty and Doggy were happy to make a new friend. From that day on, they all played together in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to play with her toy box. One day, she found a rough piece of paper with many colors on it. She wanted to show her mom. Amy ran to her mom and said, "Look, Mom! I found a rough paper with colors!" Her mom smiled and said, "That's great, Amy! Let's attach it to the fridge so everyone can see it." Together, they attached the rough paper with colors to the fridge. Amy was so happy. She clapped and said, "Yay! Now everyone can see my pretty colors!" From that day on, Amy loved to find more things with colors. She would attach them all to the fridge. Her mom was always happy to help her. They had lots of fun together.

Once there was a kind and wise old man. He liked to eat nuts and he had many. He wanted to share them with his friends. One day, the old man laughed as he put a nut on top of a tall tree. His friends were amazed. He said, "When the nut falls - whoever gets it will be very lucky!" The old man's friends laughed as they all watched the nut fall. One friend was lucky and caught the nut in his hands. He smiled and said "Thank you!" The wise old man laughed. He said, "Remember what I taught you: Sharing makes us all happy!" The friends laughed and went away with the nut. They shared it amongst themselves. The old man was happy that his friends learned a lesson that day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to count numbers. One day, Tim was counting from one to ten. But he felt uncomfortable because he could not remember one number. He missed it and was very sad. Tim asked his friend, the cat, for help. The cat tried to help Tim, but she did not know the missing number too. They both thought hard, but still could not find the answer. They decided to ask the wise owl who lived in the big tree. The owl listened to their problem and then said, "You have missed the number five!" Tim and the cat were so happy to know the missing number. But then, something unexpected happened. The wise owl told them that he had a secret. The owl could change the numbers! The wise owl changed the numbers, and now the number five was number three. Tim and the cat were surprised but excited. They thanked the wise owl and went home to practice counting with the new numbers. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there lived a king. The king loved to walk every day. He would walk in his big garden and say hello to his friends. He was a regular king, not too big, not too small. One sunny day, the king went for a walk in his garden. He saw his friend, the cat. The cat said, "Hello, king! Let's walk together." The king and the cat walked and talked. They had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the king far away. The cat was very sad. The king was gone, and he could not come back. The cat had to be the new king, but he missed his friend a lot.
