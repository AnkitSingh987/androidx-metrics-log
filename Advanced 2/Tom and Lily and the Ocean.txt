Tom and Lily went to the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and splash in the water. But they also wanted to watch the ocean, because it was big and blue and wild. "Look, Lily, a big wave!" Tom said, pointing to the ocean. "It's so high and loud!" "Wow, Tom, it's amazing!" Lily said, clapping her hands. "Can we go closer and watch it?" "No, no, Lily, we have to stay here," Tom said, shaking his head. "The ocean is wild, and it can be dangerous. Mom and dad said we have to be careful and listen to them." "But I want to see the wave, Tom," Lily said, pouting. "It's so pretty and fun!" Lily did not listen to Tom, and she ran to the ocean. She wanted to watch the wave, but she did not see the other waves behind it. The ocean was wild, and it grabbed Lily and pulled her in. "Help, help, Tom, help!" Lily cried, as the water covered her head. Tom heard Lily, and he was scared. He ran to the ocean, and he tried to grab Lily. But the ocean was wild, and it pushed Tom back. Tom and Lily were in trouble. Mom and dad saw Tom and Lily, and they were very worried. They ran to the ocean, and they shouted for help. A lifeguard heard them, and he came with a big float. He went into the ocean, and he rescued Tom and Lily. He brought them back to the beach, and he checked if they were okay. Mom and dad hugged Tom and Lily, and they were very happy. They thanked the lifeguard, and they scolded Tom and Lily. "Tom and Lily, you were very naughty," mom said. "You did not listen to us, and you went to the ocean. The ocean is wild, and it can hurt you. You have to be safe and smart, and stay away from the big waves." "We're sorry, mom, we're sorry, dad," Tom and Lily said. "We were curious, and we wanted to watch the ocean. But we were foolish, and we did not think. We will never do that again, we promise." Mom and dad kissed Tom and Lily, and they forgave them. They told them they loved them, and they wanted them to be happy. But they also wanted them to be careful, and to respect the ocean. The ocean was wild, and it had many secrets. But it also had many dangers, and it was not a toy. Tom and Lily learned their lesson, and they never ran to the ocean again. They still liked to watch the ocean, but they did it from a safe distance. They enjoyed the beach, and they had fun. But they also remembered to be safe and smart, and to listen to mom and dad. The ocean was wild, and they respected it.

Once upon a time, there was a big sunflower. It was very selfish. It did not like to give its seeds to anyone. It thought, "These seeds are only for me!" One day, a little girl came to the sunflower. She said, "Please, sunflower, give me some seeds. I want to plant them and grow more sunflowers." But the selfish sunflower said, "No! These seeds are mine!" The next day, a big wind came. It blew the sunflower's seeds away. The sunflower was sad. It had no more seeds. The little girl saw the sunflower and said, "Don't be sad, sunflower. I will give you some seeds. We can share." The sunflower was happy, and it learned to not be selfish anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to learn and be creative. One day, he wanted to make a sandwich all by himself. He asked his mom, "Can I make a sandwich?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim! Let's learn how to make a creative sandwich together." They went to the kitchen and found bread, cheese, and some fruits. Tim thought hard about how to make his sandwich special. First, Tim put cheese on the bread. Then, he added some apple slices. He smiled and said, "Look, Mom! I made a creative sandwich!" His mom was proud of him. They sat down and enjoyed the tasty sandwich together.
