Once upon a time, there was a big car. A man would drive the car very fast. One day, he saw a big pile of ash near the road. The man did not care and continued to drive fast. As he drove, the wind blew the ash all around. The ash went into the car and made the man's eyes hurt. He could not see well, but he did not stop. He kept driving fast. Soon, the man's car hit a big tree. The car was broken and the man was hurt. He should have slowed down when he saw the ash. But he did not, and now he was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a man with an orange mustache. He lived in a little house near the big park. The man loved to raise his mustache high up in the air when he was happy. One sunny day, the man went for a walk in the park. He saw a cute dog playing with a ball. The dog was running and jumping, and the man thought it was very funny. He raised his orange mustache high up in the air and laughed. The dog saw the man's orange mustache and thought it looked like a toy. The dog ran over to the man and tried to grab the mustache with his mouth. The man raised his mustache higher, and they played a fun game of catch. The man and the dog became good friends, and they played in the park every day.

Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful little rabbit named Remy. Remy lived near a big hill. Every day, Remy would hop and play on the hill. One sunny day, Remy saw a big box on the hill. The box had a close sign on it. Remy was very curious. He thought, "What could be inside the box?" Remy wanted to open the box, but he knew he had to wait. The next day, Remy went back to the hill. The close sign was gone! Remy opened the box and found lots of yummy carrots inside. He was so happy! Remy knew that being patient and thoughtful was a good thing. Now, he had a big surprise to share with all his friends.

Once there was a little boy named Tim. He was playing with his favorite toy car when he saw something soft on the ground. He picked it up and said, "What is this?" There was no answer. He looked around but there was no one around. He split the soft thing in two and inside there was an emergency button. He started to press it. He didn't even know what it meant. Suddenly all his toys started to talk. Tim was so scared he screamed and ran away. The emergency button had accidentally split open the door to another world and all the toys had escaped! The toys ran around looking for Tim, as they were really angry he had split open the emergency door they were using to escape the city. Eventually Tim was caught by the toys and they put him in a cage. He screamed and begged them to let him go, but they would not listen. And that is the bad ending. Tim was stuck in the cage forever and ever.
