One day, a big pot and a small pot were friends. The big pot could write nice words. The small pot was envious. It wanted to write too. The small pot said, "I want to write like you, big pot." The big pot said, "Okay, let's have a write race." The small pot was happy. They started to write. The big pot wrote fast. The small pot tried hard but could not write well. The small pot was sad and angry. It pushed the big pot. The big pot fell and broke. The small pot was scared. The small pot learned that being envious and angry can hurt friends.

Once upon a time, there was a tidy room. In the room, there was a radio. The radio played happy songs. A little girl named Lucy lived in this room. Lucy loved to dance to the songs on the radio. One day, Lucy's mom had a surprise for her. She hid the surprise behind her back. Lucy was excited to see what it was. She jumped and clapped her hands. Her eyes were big and bright. Lucy's mom showed her the surprise. It was a new, shiny radio! Lucy was so happy. She hugged her mom and said thank you. Now, Lucy could dance to even more songs. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there were two little friends. They were named Sam and Peter. Sam wanted to build a car. But unfortunately, he did not have enough material. Sam asked Peter, "Can you help me build the car?". Peter shook his head and said, “Sorry buddy, I'm not available". Sam got sad and said, “I wish I had enough stuff to build a car”. Then came the fog. It was thick and smoky. The fog engulfed Sam and Peter and Sam shouted, “Where did this fog come from?” Peter got worried and answered, “I don't know.” Suddenly, the fog slowly disappeared and left some materials behind. Now, Sam and Peter had enough stuff to build a car. So, they started building the car with happiness and joy. As soon as the car was made, Peter learned a valuable lesson and said, “Sometimes things are not available if we don't look hard enough, but if we have patience and wait, things will work out.” Sam and Peter hugged each other and smiled. They were happy to have the materials to build a car!

Once upon a time, there was a mom and a daughter who lived on a farm. Every day, the daughter was very obedient and always helped her mom around the farm. But she wanted to do something special to show her mom how much she loved her. So one day, the daughter decided to pick some flowers from the garden. She put them all in a pretty bouquet and bowed to her mom, offering her the bouquet. The mother was so surprised and so happy! She hugged her daughter and thanked her for being obedient and thoughtful. From that day forward, the daughter and the mom went on lots of adventures. They went to the park, watched the sunset, and played together. They were so happy, and the bouquet of flowers was with them always.

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Spot. Spot liked to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Spot saw a big red ball and wanted to play with it. Spot tried to pick up the ball with his mouth, but it was too big. He felt upset. Then, he had an idea. He found a long string and decided to tie it to the ball. Spot pulled the string with his teeth and the ball rolled along. Spot was so happy that he could play with the big red ball. He showed his friends how he tied the string to the ball. They all laughed and played together. Spot was not upset anymore, and they all had a fun day in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. He lived in a small house with a big garden. Tom loved to grow plants and flowers. One day, Tom found a magic seed. He planted it in the garden and watered it. The seed began to grow very fast! It grew taller and taller until it reached the sky. Tom decided to climb the plant. Up in the sky, he found a big box. Inside the box, there were many numbers. Tom played with the numbers and had lots of fun. Then, he took some numbers home and shared them with his friends. Everyone was happy and loved the magic numbers.
