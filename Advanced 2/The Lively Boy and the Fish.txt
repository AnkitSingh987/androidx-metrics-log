Once there was a lively little boy. He owned a well. The well was something he was very proud of. But one day a bad thing happened. He noticed that the water in his well had turned a funny colour. He ran to get his family, but it was too late. The water was bad and so were the fish living inside. The family were all very sad, but his well was ruined forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She loved to wander around and explore. One day, she decided to wander into a magical forest. It was full of beautiful trees, animals, and flowers. As she walked, she spotted a unique angel. She had never seen an angel before, so she decided to take it with her. The little girl carried the angel back home with her. She was so excited to show it to her friends. But soon she realized that something was wrong. The angel started trembling and seemed to be in trouble. The little girl quickly took the angel back to the forest. As they arrived, she noticed something strange - the angel seemed to glow! Then the angel flew away into the sky, leaving the little girl alone and sad. The little girl had lost her special angel forever. She learned a valuable lesson that day about the importance of leaving nature alone.

Tom is a lazy cat. He likes to sleep all day on the sofa. He does not like to play with his toys or run in the garden. He only wakes up when he is hungry or thirsty. One day, Tom hears a loud noise outside. He opens his eyes and sees a big dog barking at the window. The dog looks angry and scary. Tom is afraid of the dog. He jumps off the sofa and runs to hide under the bed. "Tom, where are you?" his owner, Anna, calls. She comes into the room and sees the dog at the window. She opens the door and tells the dog to go away. The dog runs away, wagging his tail. Anna looks for Tom under the bed. She sees his fluffy tail sticking out. She pulls him out gently and hugs him. "Don't be scared, Tom. The dog is gone. He was just playing. He is my friend's dog. He came to visit us. He is a nice dog. Do you want to meet him?" Tom shakes his head. He does not want to meet the dog. He does not like dogs. He likes to sleep on the sofa. He puts his head on Anna's neck and purrs. Anna smiles and strokes his fur. "OK, Tom. You can sleep on the sofa. But maybe one day you will try to play with the dog. He is fun and friendly. He likes to play with cats. He is not lazy like you."

Once upon a time, in a wide, big park, there was a tiny bug. The tiny bug loved to pinch. He pinched his friends, and he pinched his family. He thought it was fun, but it made everyone sad. One day, a new bug came to the park. She was a tour guide, and she showed bugs around the park. She took them to see the tall trees and the pretty flowers. The tiny bug wanted to go on the tour too, but he was scared that he would pinch the new bug. The tiny bug decided to try very hard not to pinch. He went on the tour with the new bug, and they saw so many fun things. They saw a big, wide pond with ducks, and they saw a big hill with kids playing. The tiny bug was having so much fun that he forgot all about pinching. At the end of the tour, the new bug thanked the tiny bug for not pinching her. The tiny bug felt very happy and proud of himself. From that day on, he never pinched anyone again. The tiny bug learned that it is better to be kind and make friends than to pinch and make them sad.

Tommy was a 3 year old boy. He loved to dream about far away places. One day, Tommy had a dream about a big noisy shop. He dreamed of playing with the toys he saw in the shop. In the morning, Tommy went to look for the noisy shop he had dreamed about. He asked his mom if they could go to the shop. Mom said no. Tommy was very disappointed. He was sad that he would never get to play with the toys he had seen in his dream. Tommy cried and went back home. He was so sad that he could not go to the noisy shop that he had dreamed of.
