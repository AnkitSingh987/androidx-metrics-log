Once upon a time, there was a happy little boy called Sam. He loved to explore the world around him. One day he decided to explore the forest near his house. He wasn't allowed to go alone and had to bring his mom's license. Sam took the license and went to explore the forest. He was excited to look for bugs and bugs and collect pretty stones. All of a sudden, he heard a loud noise in the bushes. He was very scared! He shouted out, "Mama! I'm scared!" He was so scared he started to cry. Then, out of the bushes came a friendly black and white cat. The cat had a bright collar of white fur around its neck. Sam's fear quickly disappeared. The cat meowed and said, "Don't be scared Sam, I'm here to keep you safe." Sam smiled, feeling much more happy and relaxed. He thanked the cat and went on to explore the forest, with the cat tagging along.

Once upon a time, in a big city, there was a brilliant sun. The sun was very bright and made everyone happy. The city had many houses, trees, and people. The people loved the sun and played outside every day. One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk with her mom. They had a lot of fun. They saw many things in the city. They saw big cars, pretty flowers, and happy dogs. Amy and her mom laughed and smiled as they walked. As they walked, they met a nice old man. The man gave Amy a big, red balloon. Amy was very happy to have the balloon. She held it tight as they walked back home. The brilliant sun shined on them, and they had a great day in the city.

Once there was a little boy who loved the beach. He wanted to go out and feel the sand and the cool breeze. One day he went with his mum and dad. He couldn't wait! His feet ran quickly over the sand. He stopped and grabbed a bucket and spade. He started building a sandcastle. He pushed and pulled the sand, carefully selling and shaping his castle. With each scoop of sand he felt happy and calm. The sun was getting strong and the little boy was feeling hot. He turned and looked out to the sea. He saw something glimmering in the sun. It was an ice! He started to pull it out of the water and rushed to show it to his mum and dad. When the little boy got to shore his mum and dad were so surprised. His mum said, ‘you pulled that ice out of sea! We are so proud of you!’ The little boy smiled with joy and hugged his mum and dad. He knew that he had done something special. He felt so happy and calm!

One day, a very organized cat named Tim lived in a small house. He liked to keep everything clean and nice. Tim loved to take a shower every day. He would close the door and sing happy songs. One day, while Tim was taking a shower, he heard a loud noise. He was scared, so he closed the shower and ran to see what happened. He saw a big mess in his living room. All his toys were on the floor. Tim was sad because his house was not organized anymore. Tim started to clean, but then he heard a small sound. He looked and saw a little mouse. The mouse was sorry for making the mess. He wanted to be friends with Tim. So, Tim and the mouse cleaned the house together. They became best friends and played with the toys. Now, Tim's house was organized and he was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, in a small house, a wise cat and a little girl lived. They loved to play and have fun together. One day, they wanted to find a yummy cake to eat. They looked in the kitchen, but there was no cake. They looked in the living room, but still no cake. Then, they went outside and found a big, beautiful cake under a tree. The wise cat and the little girl were so happy to find the cake. They sat down and ate the cake together. It was the best cake they ever had. From that day on, they knew they could find anything if they looked together.
