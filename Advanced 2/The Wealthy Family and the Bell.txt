Once upon a time, there was a wealthy family. Every morning, the father would wake up to the sound of a bell ringing from the kitchen. He knew it was his servant ringing the silver bell to tell them breakfast was ready. One morning, the father went to the kitchen to find the servant ringing the bell more frantic than usual. When he asked what was going on, the servant pointed to the kitchen table and said, "A large insect flew in through the window, sir. It won't leave!" The father was a calm person and he slowly approached the table. Sure enough, there was a massive insect in the middle of the table. He tried to scare it away but it just wouldn't go. So he rang the bell again and the servant quickly came in and chased away the insect. Once the insect was gone, the father smiled and said, "Well done. That was quite a rush for such a wealthy family!" The servant nodded, grateful that the large insect was gone. And from then on, the family remembered to keep their windows shut on warm days!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a girl named Mia. Mia had a big box that she wanted to mail to her friend, Sam. Sam lived far, far away. Mia was excited to send the box because it was full of fun toys that they could play with together. Mia went to the post office to mail the box. But, there was a problem. The box was too big! Mia felt sad because she wanted to be successful in sending the toys to Sam. She thought of a way to fix the problem. Mia went home and found a smaller box. She put the toys in the smaller box and went back to the post office. This time, the box was the right size. Mia was happy that she found a way to mail the box. She was successful in sending the toys to her friend, Sam.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her toy pan. One day, she wanted to gain a new friend to play with. Kitty went to the park and saw a dog. The dog said, "Hi, I am Doggy. Want to play?" Kitty said, "Yes, let's play with my pan!" They played and had fun. But then, a big wind came. The wind took the pan away. Kitty and Doggy tried to catch it, but they could not. The pan was gone. Kitty and Doggy were sad.

One day, a wet dog named Spot went to the park. He saw a big red ball. Spot wanted to play with the ball. He ran to the ball and hit it with his nose. The ball went very far. Spot saw a little girl named Amy. Amy was sad because she lost her ball. Spot wanted to make her happy. He said, "I can perform a trick for you with the ball." Amy smiled and said, "Okay, show me!" Spot ran to the ball and hit it with his wet nose. The ball went up in the air. Spot jumped and caught it. Amy clapped and laughed. Spot gave the ball back to Amy. They played together all day.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a sleepy cat. Tim loved to watch his cat sleep all day. The cat was very soft and had pretty colors. Tim and the cat were best friends. One sunny day, Tim and the cat went to play outside. The sleepy cat saw a big tree and climbed it fast. Tim was so happy to watch his cat climb. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat turned into a beautiful bird and flew away! Tim was sad, but then he saw the bird fly back to him. The bird was still his best friend. They played together all day long. At night, the bird turned back into the sleepy cat. Tim learned that his cat was magic, and they had many more fun adventures together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a new toy, a marble. The marble was shiny and pretty. Amy loved her marble and took it everywhere she went. One day, Amy met a boy named Sam. Sam was painting a picture. "What are you doing?" asked Amy. "I am painting a picture of a cat," said Sam. "Can I paint with you?" asked Amy. "Yes, you can," said Sam. Amy and Sam painted together. Amy painted a picture of her marble. It was a fun day. They painted many pictures and played with the marble. Then, they became best friends.
