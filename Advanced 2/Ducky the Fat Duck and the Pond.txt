Once upon a time, there was a fat duck named Ducky. Ducky loved to enjoy his days at the pond. He would swim and play with his friends all day long. The pond was a happy place for all the animals. One day, a new duck came to the pond. His name was Little Duck. Little Duck was not fat like Ducky. He was small and thin. Some of the animals at the pond laughed at him. They said he was too small to play with them. But Ducky did not laugh. He knew it was not nice to laugh at others. Ducky went to Little Duck and asked him to play. They had a lot of fun together. Soon, all the animals saw how much fun they were having. They stopped laughing at Little Duck and joined them in their games. The pond was a happy place once again. The moral of the story is to be kind to others, no matter how different they are. Everyone can enjoy playing together and being friends.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy bear, Ben. They had lots of fun together. One day, Tim found an unusual box in his room. It was big and red. He was very curious. Tim opened the box and found a treat inside. It was a big, shiny ball. Tim was so happy! He squeezed the ball and something unexpected happened. The ball started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim and Ben were very surprised. The big ball kept growing until it filled the whole room. Tim and Ben had to think fast. They climbed on top of the ball and squeezed it together. The ball started to get smaller and smaller. Soon, it was back to its normal size. Tim and Ben learned that their unusual ball could grow and shrink when they squeezed it. They played with their new toy all day long and had so much fun.

Once upon a time there was a small pony who lived in a stable. Her name was Daisy and she loved to run. One day she wanted to win a race, so she asked her best friend, Sally the Sheep for help. "Will you help me win a race?" Daisy said to Sally. Sally smiled. "Of course I will!" So Daisy and Sally practiced running every day. Finally, the day of the race arrived. Daisy was scared but Sally was there to cheer her on. When the race started, Daisy worked hard. She ran faster than ever before and when she crossed the finish line, she had won! Daisy was so happy that she danced in the stable with Sally. They both had the best time ever!

One day, a poor boy named Tim went to the market. He saw a big red tomato. He wanted to eat it very much, but he did not have any money. Tim was very sad. A kind man saw Tim and asked, "Why are you sad, little boy?" Tim said, "I am hungry, and I want that red tomato, but I have no money." The kind man smiled and said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The kind man bought the tomato and gave it to Tim. Tim was very happy to receive the tomato. He said, "Thank you so much, sir!" The kind man said, "You're welcome. Always remember to help others when you can." Tim went home and shared the tomato with his family. They were all very happy. From that day on, Tim always tried to help others, just like the kind man. The moral of the story is: we should always be kind and help others when we can.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Max. Max liked to play with a ball of yarn. One day, Max found a needle in the yarn. He did not know what it was, so he played with it. Max liked to mix the needle and yarn together. One day, Max was playing with the needle and yarn. He mixed them so much that something unexpected happened. The yarn and needle turned into a big, colorful blanket! Max was so surprised. He did not know how it happened, but he was happy. Max showed the blanket to his friends. They all wanted to play with it too. Max shared the blanket with his friends, and they all had a fun time. They played on the blanket all day. Max was happy that the needle and yarn turned into something so fun.
