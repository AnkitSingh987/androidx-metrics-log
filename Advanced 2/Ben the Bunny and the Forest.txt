Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Ben. Ben lived in a small hole with his family. He had a lot of brothers and sisters, but he was the smallest. Ben was sad because he thought he was boring. One day, Ben saw a hanger on the ground. He had an idea. Ben started to hop onto the hanger. He hopped and hopped until he could hop very high. Ben was so happy that he could hop high with the help of the hanger. Soon, all the other animals saw Ben hopping high with the hanger. They thought it was fun and wanted to try it too. Ben showed them how to hop with the hanger. Now, Ben was not boring anymore. He was the most fun bunny in the forest. And they all lived happily ever after. The end.

One day, a girl named Mia went to play outside. She took her hoop with her. Mia liked to make the hoop roll. It was fun. Mia saw a big dog. The dog looked at Mia's hoop. Mia was frightened. She did not want the dog to take her hoop away. But the dog did not take the hoop. The dog used its nose to make the hoop roll. Mia was not frightened anymore. She and the dog played with the hoop together. They had fun and became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted little bird who could sing very well. Every morning, the bird would wake up early and sing a beautiful song. This made everyone in the forest very happy. One day, the bird wanted to complete a new song for his friends in the forest. He worked hard all day to make the best song ever. The bird was very excited to share his new song with everyone. But, something unexpected happened. When the bird started to sing his new song, all his friends started to laugh! The bird was confused and sad. Then, a wise old owl told the bird that he was singing the song backward. The bird tried again, but this time he sang the song the right way. Everyone in the forest loved the new song, and they all clapped and cheered for the gifted bird.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was a very troubled girl because she always lost her things. One day, she lost her favorite toy, a small powder-blue bear. She looked everywhere for it, but she could not find it. Lucy was very sad. Lucy's mom saw her sad face and said, "Why don't you think about where you last saw your bear?" So Lucy thought hard. She remembered that she was playing with her bear in the garden yesterday. Lucy felt happy because now she knew where to look. Lucy went to the garden and saw something blue under a big flower. She went closer and found her powder-blue bear! She was so happy that she hugged the bear and danced. Lucy learned that when she is troubled, she should think about what to do. From that day on, Lucy always thought before she did something, and she never lost her things again.

One day, a charming robot came to a little town. The robot was big and shiny. It had bright lights and could talk. The people in the town were happy to see the robot. They liked to watch it dance and sing. The robot made friends with a little boy named Tim. Tim and the robot played every day. They played ball, ran, and jumped. They had lots of fun together. One day, Tim asked the robot, "Can you rot like an apple?" The robot said, "No, I cannot rot. I am made of strong metal. But I can help you learn about rot." Tim and the robot went to the garden. They found an apple on the ground. It was brown and soft. The robot told Tim, "This apple is rotting. It is not good to eat." Tim learned about rot from his charming robot friend. They had fun and learned together. The people in the town loved their robot, and the robot loved them too.
