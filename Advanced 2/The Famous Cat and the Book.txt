Once upon a time, there was a famous cat. This cat was very good at jumping and running. One day, the cat found a big book called a novel. The cat wanted to read the novel, but it was very big. The famous cat tried to carry the novel to its home. But the book was too big and heavy. As the cat walked, it started to stumble. The cat stumbled and the novel fell on the ground. The cat tried again and again to carry the novel. Every time, the cat stumbled and the novel fell. At last, the cat asked a friend to help. Together, they carried the novel home. The famous cat was very happy and read the novel with its friend.

Once upon a time, there was a mighty big tree. The tree had many pretty flowers on it. One day, a little girl named Mia saw the tree. Mia said, "I want to put a flower in my hair." She picked a flower from the tree. Mia put the flower in her hair and smiled. But then, the mighty big tree got very sad. It started to cry. All the flowers fell down from the tree. The tree had no more flowers left. Mia felt bad and the tree stayed sad forever.

One day, a little girl named Sue was in the kitchen with her daddy. They were making a cake for mommy's birthday. Sue was very excited to help. Daddy said, "First, we need to measure the flour." Sue held the cup and poured the flour in. Next, they added a thick, sweet mix to the bowl. Sue stirred it all together. Daddy said, "Good job, Sue!" Sue smiled, feeling proud of her work. She couldn't wait to see mommy's face when she saw the cake. When the cake was done, Sue and daddy put candles on it. They sang "Happy Birthday" to mommy. Mommy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Sue and daddy!" Sue learned that helping others can make you feel good too. And that was the best part of mommy's birthday.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park. She saw a tree with tasty fruit. She wanted to eat it, but the fruit was too high. Lily tried to jump, but she could not reach. A big bird saw Lily and wanted to help. The bird flew up and took the fruit. The bird gave the fruit to Lily. She was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The fruit started to freeze in her hand! Lily was surprised. She looked at the bird and the bird looked at her. They both didn't know what to do. Then, they saw a little boy with a big smile. He had a magic wand! The boy had made the fruit freeze. He waved the wand again, and the fruit was not frozen anymore. Lily, the bird, and the boy all laughed and shared the tasty fruit.

Once upon a time, there was a big hippo named Hippy. Hippy lived in a pond with his friends. They loved to play in the water all day. They splashed and laughed, and everyone was glad. One day, the sun was very hot. It made the water go away, and the pond became dry. Hippy and his friends were sad because they could not play in the water anymore. They needed to find more water to make their pond wet again. Hippy and his friends worked together. They all helped to bring water from a far river. It was hard work, but they did not give up. Soon, their pond was wet again, and they were glad. They learned that when they worked together, they could make things better. The moral of the story is: Working together makes hard things easier.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky lived near a pond. One day, Ducky met a new friend, Froggy. Froggy was green and liked to jump. Ducky was yellow and liked to swim. "Let me show you my pond," said Ducky to Froggy. Froggy was happy and said, "Yes, please show me!" So, Ducky and Froggy went to the pond together. They played in the water and had lots of fun. But then, they saw something terrible. A big, mean fish came to the pond. The fish wanted to eat Ducky and Froggy. Ducky and Froggy were scared. They swam and jumped away from the fish. They hid in the tall grass until the fish went away. After that, Ducky and Froggy were safe and happy again. They played at the pond every day, but they always looked out for the big, mean fish.
