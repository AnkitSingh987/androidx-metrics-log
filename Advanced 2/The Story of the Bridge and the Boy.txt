One day, a boy had a dream. In the dream, he met a giant. The giant was very big and had long legs. The boy was scared, but the giant was nice. They played together and had fun. The giant took the boy to a long bridge. The bridge was so long, they could not see the end. They walked and walked, but they did not get tired. They talked and laughed as they walked. Then, something unexpected happened. The giant became small, just like the boy. Now, they were the same size. The boy was not scared anymore. They played even more and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy bear named Ben. Ben did not like to work. One day, snow started to fall from the sky. It was very cold outside. Ben knew he had to work to get ready for winter. He needed to find food and make his bed warm. Ben went to his friend, a squirrel named Sam. Sam was not grumpy. Sam liked to work. Ben said, "Sam, help me get ready for winter." Sam said, "Okay, Ben. I will help you find food and make your bed warm." They worked together to find nuts and berries. They also found soft leaves to make Ben's bed warm. The snow kept falling, but they did not stop working. After they finished, Ben was not grumpy anymore. He was happy because he had a warm bed and food for winter. Ben said, "Thank you, Sam, for helping me work." Sam said, "You're welcome, Ben. Now we can both be warm and happy in the snow." And they were both warm and happy all winter long.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Daisy. Daisy loved to carry things for her friends. One day, she met a little bird with a hurt wing. "Can you help me carry my food?" asked the bird. "I can't fly." "Of course!" said Daisy. She was always happy to help her friends. Daisy carried the food for the bird every day. The bird's wing got better, and they became best friends. The other animals saw how dependable Daisy was and they all wanted to be her friend too. The moral of the story is: when you are dependable and help others, you make many friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to wave at people and say "hi". Amy was very kind to everyone. One sunny day, Amy found a colorful gem in her backyard. It was shiny and pretty. Amy wanted to show her friends the colorful gem. She went to the park and waved at her friends. They all gathered around to see the gem. Amy's friends loved the gem too. They all wanted to touch it and play with it. Amy had an idea. She decided to share the gem with her friends. They all took turns holding the colorful gem and playing with it. Soon, they were all laughing and having fun together. The friends learned that sharing made everyone happy. And from that day on, they always shared their toys and played together.

Tommy and his mommy were walking in the park. Tommy was looking for something furry. Suddenly, he saw it! It was a little bunny, with soft fur. Tommy chased after it, but it started to run away. Tommy started to cry because the bunny was disappearing. His mommy said, "Don't worry, Tommy. We will just wait here, and the bunny will come back." They waited and waited, but the bunny didn't come back. Tommy started to cry again. His mommy said, "Let's go look for the furry bunny. Maybe it went to find some food." So they started to look. Tommy was so excited when he finally saw the bunny again. It was so furry and soft. Tommy reached out and touched the bunny! The bunny hopped away, but Tommy was happy to have seen it. He smiled and thanked his mommy for helping him find it. They hugged and then went home. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a toy guitar. He loved to play it every day. It made him very happy. One day, Tim's friend Sam came over to play. Sam saw the guitar and thought it was amazing. He asked Tim if he could try it. Tim said yes, and Sam played the guitar too. They had so much fun playing together. Later, Sam's mom asked him what he wanted for his birthday. Sam told her about the guitar. His mom said she would recommend getting one for him. On his birthday, Sam got a guitar just like Tim's. They played their guitars together and had the best time ever.
