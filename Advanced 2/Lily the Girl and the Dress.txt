Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to wear her favorite dress. It was a pretty dress with lots of colors. Lily was so happy when she wore it. She wanted to wear it to school in her classroom. One day, Lily's mom said, "Tomorrow is a special day at school. You can wear your favorite dress!" Lily was very excited. She knew that her classroom would be a fun and peaceful place with all her friends. The next morning, Lily put on her pretty dress and went to school. When she got to her classroom, she saw all her friends wearing their favorite clothes too. They all had a great day together, just like Lily knew they would.

Ben was a monkey who loved bananas. He lived in a big tree with his family and friends. Every day, he would swing from branch to branch and look for ripe bananas to eat. One day, he saw a bunch of bananas hanging from a vine. He was very hungry and happy. He climbed up the vine and grabbed the bananas. But as he was about to peel one, he heard a loud roar. It was a lion who also wanted the bananas. The lion was big and gray and scary. He had sharp teeth and claws. He growled at Ben and said, "Those are my bananas. Give them to me now!" Ben was afraid of the lion. He did not want to fight. He wanted to share the bananas. He said, "Please, Mr. Lion, can we have some bananas too? There are many bananas here. We can both eat some." The lion did not like to share. He said, "No, these bananas are mine. They belong to me. You are a monkey. You belong in the tree. Go away or I will eat you!" Ben did not know what to do. He did not want to lose the bananas. He did not want to be eaten. He looked around and saw his friends in the tree. They were watching and waiting. They had an idea. They started to make loud noises and throw things at the lion. They threw sticks and stones and nuts and leaves. They shouted, "Go away, lion! Leave Ben alone! He is our friend. He belongs with us!" The lion was angry and confused. He did not like to be bothered. He did not like to be hit. He said, "Stop it, you annoying monkeys! You are making me mad. You are making me hurt. You are not worth these bananas. I will find another snack." The lion let go of the vine and ran away. He did not want to deal with the monkeys anymore. He looked for a quieter place to hunt. Ben was relieved and grateful. He thanked his friends for saving him. He said, "You are the best friends ever. You are brave and smart and kind. You helped me when I was in trouble. You belong with me. And I belong with you." He shared the bananas with his friends. They ate and laughed and hugged. They were happy and full. They belonged together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big purple bottle. She loved her bottle very much. It was her favorite thing. One day, Mia couldn't find her purple bottle. She looked everywhere. Mia was very sad. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my purple bottle?" Her mom said, "I don't know, Mia. Where do you think you left it?" Mia thought very hard. She remembered playing in the park. Mia said, "I think I left it at the park!" So, Mia and her mom went to the park. They looked all around. And there it was! Mia's purple bottle was under a big tree. Mia was so happy. She picked up her purple bottle and gave it a big hug. From that day on, Mia always remembered to keep her purple bottle close. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who was walking through the forest. Suddenly, he felt something strange under his feet. It was slimy and slimy to touch, making him feel uncomfortable. The little boy looked down to see a giant palm. It had lots of pointy fingers reaching up high. He stared at it in awe, until he noticed it was reaching out and trying to grab him. He tried to resist, but he couldn't. Even though he was scared, the little boy jumped at the palm. He grabbed its fingers and started shaking it. The giant palm screeched and finally let go, leaving the little boy alone. The boy was relieved but also a little bit sad. He had resisted the giant palm, but it was still uncomfortable. He decided to take its branch with him, as a reminder of his brave adventure.
