Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to sing songs all day long. One day, he heard a new song from his friend, Tommy. Bobby thought the song was stupid, but he wanted to try singing it too. Bobby decided to add his own words to the song to make it better. He sang the new song loudly and proudly. All the other birds listened to Bobby's song and started to sing it too. Soon, the whole forest was filled with the sound of the new song. In the end, Bobby learned that even if something seems stupid at first, it can be made better by adding your own touch. The moral of the story is to always give things a chance and be open to trying new things.

One day, a little girl named Mia wanted to prepare a big surprise for her mom. She went to her room and found her favorite toy figure. Mia had an idea to make her mom happy. She was worried her mom would not like it, but she tried her best. Mia took her toy figure and put it in a box. She wrapped the box with pretty paper and put a bow on top. She was excited to give the surprise to her mom. Mia hid the box under her bed and went to play outside. Later, Mia's mom went to her room to clean. She found the box under the bed and thought it was a gift for her birthday. She opened the box and saw the toy figure. Mia's mom was so happy and surprised. Mia came inside and saw her mom with the box. They both laughed and hugged. The surprise was not what Mia planned, but it still made her mom happy.

Tom and Mia are on a big boat with their mom and dad. They are going to a new place far away. Tom and Mia like to watch the water and the birds and the other boats. Sometimes they see fish jumping or dolphins playing. One day, the boat stops at a port. A port is where boats can park and people can get on and off. Mom and dad say they want to go see the port and buy some things. Tom and Mia want to go too, but mom and dad say they have to stay on the boat with a nice lady who will watch them. Tom and Mia are not happy. They want to see the port. They look out the window and see many people and cars and shops and noises. They also see a lot of trash and dirt and smoke. The port is very smelly. Tom and Mia hold their noses. "Yuck, I don't like the port," Tom says. "Me neither, it's too smelly and loud," Mia says. They decide to play with their toys and books instead. They hope mom and dad will come back soon and take them away from the port. They miss the water and the birds and the fish. They think the port is boring and bad. They like the boat better.

Once upon a time, there was a little pony called Socksy. She was brave and adventurous, and she loved to zoom around the meadow. One day, Socksy thought she'd try something new and exciting. She hopped onto a leather saddle, and held on tight. All of a sudden, she zoomed away! Socksy was so excited that she didn’t realise where she was going. Suddenly, Socksy felt helpless and scared. She had zoomed right into the middle of a deep, dark forest, and she didn't know how to get out. The little pony started to cry out for help. But a voice replied, "If you want out of this forest, you'll have to do what I say!" Socksy was very frightened, but she wanted to get home, so she nodded her head. The voice belonged to the King of the Forest- a huge, scary eagle. He told Socksy that he'd help her get home if she gave him her most treasured possession: her lovely, leather saddle. Socksy was sad, but desperate. She had to do it, so she gave the King of the Forest her saddle, and zoomed off. The next day Socksy was back in the meadow, feeling helpless without her saddle. She never tried to zoom into the dark forest again.
