Once upon a time, there was a little pine tree. The pine tree lived in a big forest with other trees. One day, a squirrel came to the pine tree and said, "I want to fit in your branches, but they are too uncomfortable." The pine tree wanted to help the squirrel. So, the pine tree tried to make its branches soft and nice. The squirrel tried to fit in the branches again, but it was still uncomfortable. The squirrel said, "I will come back later." The next day, the squirrel came back to the pine tree. The pine tree tried to make its branches even softer. The squirrel tried to fit in the branches again, but it was still very uncomfortable. The squirrel said, "I will come back one more time." On the last day, the squirrel came back to the pine tree. The pine tree did its best to make the branches soft. But the squirrel still could not fit in the branches. It was too uncomfortable. The squirrel was sad and left the pine tree. The pine tree felt bad that it could not help the squirrel. And they never saw each other again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big festival. All the people in the town were happy. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced to fun music. But there was one little girl named Mia who was fearful. She was scared to join the fun because she thought she would not be good at the games. Mia's mom saw her looking sad and asked, "Why are you not playing, Mia?" Mia said, "I am fearful, Mom. I don't want to play the games." Her mom hugged her and said, "It's okay to be fearful, but you can still try. Choose a game that you like, and I will play with you." Mia looked around the festival and saw a game where you throw a ball at some pins. She thought it looked fun. So Mia and her mom went to play the game together. Mia threw the ball and knocked down all the pins! She was so happy and not fearful anymore. After that, Mia and her mom played more games and had lots of fun at the festival. They ate ice cream and danced to the happy music. Mia learned that it was okay to be fearful, but trying new things can be fun too! From that day on, Mia was never fearful at the festival again. She always chose to try new games and have fun with her friends and family. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a silly chicken. The chicken was so silly that it would always forget to shut the gate of the farm when it went out for a walk. One day, the chicken decided to take a long walk. Before it left, the farmer said, "Chicky, don't forget to shut the gate before you go!" But, when the silly chicken returned, the gate was wide open! "Oops! I forgot to shut it!", cried the chicken. The farmer smiled and said, "That's okay Chicky. Just remember that it's important to always shut the gate when it's open." The silly chicken nodded and said, "I will, I will!"

One day, a little girl named Mia found a bar of soap. It was a very amazing soap. It was so bright and shiny. Mia wanted to use the soap to wash her hands. She picked it up and started to walk to the sink. On her way, the soap began to talk! It said, "Hi, Mia! I am a talking soap. Can we be friends?" Mia was very surprised. She had never seen a talking soap before. She said, "Yes, we can be friends!" They played together all day. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The soap started to get smaller and smaller. Mia was worried. She didn't want her new friend to go away. The soap said, "Mia, don't be sad. I will always be with you, even when I'm all gone." Mia felt better. She knew that her amazing talking soap friend would always be in her heart, even when it was all used up. And from that day, Mia always remembered her special friend, the talking soap.
