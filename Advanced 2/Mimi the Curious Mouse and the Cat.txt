Once upon a time, there was a curious little mouse named Mimi. Mimi loved to explore and play in the big house she lived in. One day, Mimi found a small pile of dust in a corner of the house. She thought it was strange because the house was always very clean. Mimi decided to quit playing and find out who made the dust. She asked her friends, the cat and the dog, if they knew who made the dust. The cat said, "I don't know, but we should clean it up." The dog agreed, "Yes, let's clean it up and make the house nice again." So, Mimi, the cat, and the dog worked together to clean up the dust. They found out that the dust came from a small hole in the wall. They asked the people in the house to fix the hole and keep the house clean. The people thanked them for their help and fixed the hole. The moral of the story is that when we work together, we can solve problems and keep our home clean and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Max. Max loved to play outside with his friends. One day, Max saw a rake in the yard. The rake was big and had many teeth. Max had an idea. He urged his friends to come and play with the rake. They all took turns jumping over the rake, laughing and having fun. Max was very happy. In the end, Max and his friends were tired but had a great day. They all said thank you to Max for his clever idea. They knew they could always count on Max for fun games.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable little dog. His name was Max. Every morning, Max would go on long walks around the neighbourhood. He never wanted to stay in one spot too long. One day, Max went out on a walk and didn't come back. His family called and called, but Max was nowhere to be seen. Days passed by, and still no sign of Max. Then, one morning, everybody heard an ambulance turn onto the street. Max was inside! He had found a way to escape! The family rushed outside, and there was Max - panting and tired, but safe and sound. They were so happy to see him and everybody cheered. Max was home, safe and sound. He had been dependable once again, and the family was so lucky to have him. They were so proud.

One day, in a jolly green forest, a little bunny named Tim hopped around. He loved to admire the pretty flowers and big trees. Tim was very happy in his home. Tim met a jolly squirrel named Sam. Sam said, "Hi, Tim! Do you want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, Sam! Let's play together!" They played and had a lot of fun. But then, Tim saw a trap on the ground. He was scared. Sam said, "Don't worry, Tim. I know how to help you." Sam got a big stick and opened the trap. They were safe now. Tim and Sam were very happy and hugged each other. They played in the jolly forest and admired the pretty things around them every day.

Once upon a time, there was a rat named Jack. He was very small, but he was brave and adventurous. One day, Jack decided that he wanted to fly. He jumped off the ground and flapped his arms, but nothing happened. He tried again and again, but still nothing happened. Then, he saw a bird flying in the sky. He called to the bird, "Can you teach me how to fly?" The bird laughed and said, "Fly? No, rats can't fly! Ha, ha!" Jack felt scared. But then a naughty thought popped into his head. He said to the bird, "Maybe I can't fly now… but I bet I can fly better than you!" The bird was so surprised, it flew away without even answering. Jack was a bit disappointed, but he was also happy that he had been brave enough to talk to the bird. From then on, he was always very proud of himself.
