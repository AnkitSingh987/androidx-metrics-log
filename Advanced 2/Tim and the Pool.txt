Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim wanted to ride his bike to the pool. He put on his helmet and said to his mom, "Mom, I want to ride to the pool!" His mom smiled and said, "Okay, but be careful. It can be dangerous." Tim rode his bike and saw a big dog. The dog was near the pool. Tim was scared, but the dog wagged its tail and said, "Don't be scared. I'm a nice dog. I can help you if it's dangerous." Tim was happy and said, "Thank you, dog!" They went to the pool together. But when they got there, the pool was full of ice cream! Everyone was surprised and laughed. Tim and the dog had a fun day at the ice cream pool.

Once upon a time there was a little boy. He was feeling very anxious because he could hear a storm outside his window. The storm was so loud! He was scared and his heart was beating fast. He closed his eyes and wished it would end soon. But the storm kept going and going, and the boy felt more and more scared. He wanted to go hide somewhere. He decided to go to the closet and make a secret den there. It was dark and cosy, and he could feel safe in there. The storm kept roaring outside, but the little boy was no longer anxious. In his den, he was safe and calm. He stayed in there until the storm eventually ended. Then he was happy - the storm was gone, and he knew he would be safe.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a great big copper pot. The copper pot was very shiny and lived in a nice house with a girl named Sally. Sally loved the copper pot very much. One day, Sally met a boy named Tom. Tom was a nice boy, and he liked Sally a lot. Tom said, "Sally, I want to marry you. Will you be my wife?" Sally was very happy and said, "Yes, I will marry you, Tom." Sally and Tom got married and they lived together with the great big copper pot. They used the pot to cook yummy food for their friends and family. Everyone in the town loved the food from the copper pot. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a messy boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys, but he never put them away. His room was always a big mess. One day, his mom asked him to complete a promise. She said, "Tim, if you clean your room, I will take you to the park." Tim wanted to go to the park, so he said, "Okay, Mom, I promise to clean my room." But Tim did not really want to clean. So, he just hid his toys under his bed and in the closet. He thought his mom would not find out. Later, when his mom came to check his room, she saw the mess under the bed and in the closet. She was very sad and said, "Tim, you did not keep your promise. We cannot go to the park today." Tim felt bad and cried, but he learned that he should always keep his promises.

Little Ben wanted to measure things. He wanted to measure his house, his toys and his friends. One day, his mom gave him a special glass. It was big and round and very lucky. Little Ben took the glass and went to measure everything he could find. He measured his bed, his blocks and his books. He even tried to measure the clouds in the sky. After measuring everything, he put the lucky glass back in his room and went to bed. He felt very happy because he could measure anything he wanted.

Once upon a time there were two birds. One was green and one was blue. They were very different. The green bird loved to go outside but the blue bird stayed inside. One day, they decided to go on an adventure together. Outside it was cold and the sky was dark, so they started to shiver. Then the wind began to blow and the birds started to freeze. They hugged each other to stay warm. Soon they were so cold, they couldn't move. Suddenly, a big bird flew down and picked them up in its big wings. It brought them to its nest and put them in a warm bed. The green and blue bird stayed there until it was sunny outside again. Then the big bird flew away. The green and blue birds waved goodbye. They were different, but they were still friends.
