Sara and Ben are playing in their room. They have many toys, but they want to make something new. They see the curtain on the window. It is light and soft. "Let's make a tent with the curtain!" Sara says. "OK, how do we do that?" Ben asks. Sara thinks for a moment. She has an idea. She pulls the curtain from the rod. She folds it in half. She puts one end on the bed and the other end on the chair. "Look, we have a tent!" Sara says. Ben crawls under the curtain. He likes the tent. It is cozy and dark. He invites Sara to join him. "Come in, Sara. It is fun in here!" Ben says. Sara crawls under the curtain too. She smiles at Ben. They pretend they are camping in the woods. They tell stories and sing songs. They have a lot of fun with the curtain.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to pack his bag for a trip. He put his good shirt, pants, and socks in the bag. He also put his favorite toy, a soft bunny, inside. The bunny had a nice texture that Tim loved to touch. While Tim was packing, his friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam saw the bag and said, "What are you doing, Tim?" Tim replied, "I am packing for a trip with my family." Sam looked sad and said, "I will miss you." But something unexpected happened. When Tim finished packing, he found out that his family's trip was not today, but next week! Tim was happy because he could still play with Sam. They both laughed and played all day. And the soft bunny with the nice texture stayed in the bag, waiting for the real trip.

Once upon a time there was a little owl named Jonathan who lived in the woods. Jonathan loved the trees and being outdoors. Every day he would fly around his forest in search of food and friends. One day, while Jonathan was flying he heard a noise. He couldn't believe his ears when he realized what it was - a grown owl was cutting down the big trees in his forest. Jonathan was so sad and uncomfortable. He wanted to ask the grown owl why he was cutting down the trees. The grown owl said to him, “I'm changing the forest to something different. Sometimes we need to change things so that life can be better.” Jonathan still felt unhappy about the changes, but he understood. That day, Jonathan changed his outlook. He realized that sometimes when things change, it can lead to something positive. He learned that change is not always bad or uncomfortable. So Jonathan ended up feeling much better about the big changes being made in his forest and he found a way to make the best of the situation. He accepted the change and it made him happier. Jonathan understood that sometimes change can lead to something great.

Once there was a boy who wanted to find a beach, but it seemed too distant. So he called out to the sky and asked, "Where can I find a beach?" Suddenly, an answer floated down from the clouds. It said, "Follow the sun, and you will find a distant beach." So the boy followed the sun until he reached a distant beach. He ran into the sun-warmed sand and splashed in the salty water. He laughed and smiled with joy. It was the most beautiful beach he had ever seen. The waves moved gently like a slow dance and the air was sweet and warm. It was as if the beach was made just for him. He could stay there forever, and he never wanted to leave. As he watched the sun start to set, the boy knew that he would remember this day at the distant beach, always.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a soft, cuddly toy cat named Stitch. Mia and Stitch were best friends. They played together all day and slept together at night. One day, they went to the park to play. Mia was having fun on the swings, but Stitch was sad. A loud dog barked at him and scared him. Mia saw Stitch was sad and hugged him tight. She said, "Don't worry, Stitch. I will keep you safe." Mia and Stitch played more and soon, it was time to go home. At home, Mia's mom made them yummy food. Stitch felt better now. He was happy to have a friend like Mia. Before going to bed, Stitch said, "Thank you, Mia, for keeping me safe and being my friend." Mia smiled and hugged Stitch. They went to sleep, knowing they had each other.
