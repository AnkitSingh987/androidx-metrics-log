Once upon a time, there was a compassionate girl named Lily. She loved to play with her colorful blocks. One day, Lily wanted to make a pretty pattern with her blocks. She started to match the colors and shapes to make a big tower. As Lily was building, her little brother, Tim, came into the room. He saw the tower and wanted to play too. But Tim was too small and didn't know how to match the blocks like Lily. He tried to add a block, but it didn't fit the pattern. The tower fell down with a loud crash! Lily was upset, but she knew Tim didn't mean to ruin her tower. She said, "Tim, let's build a new tower together. I will show you how to match the blocks and make a nice pattern." Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Lily!" They worked together, and soon they had built an even bigger and prettier tower.

Once upon a time, there was a big hut in the woods. In the hut lived a kind man, a nice lady, and a little boy. They were a happy family. Every day, the boy would rise early and play outside. One day, the boy found a big box near the hut. He was very excited and ran to tell his mom and dad. They all went to see the box together. The man said, "Let's open the box tomorrow." The boy was so happy and could not wait. The next day, the family went to open the big box. They were all very surprised. Inside the box was a big, friendly bear! The bear became their new friend. They played and had fun together every day. The big hut was now even happier with their new big friend.

Lily liked to collect rocks. She had many rocks of different colors and shapes in her room. She liked to show them to her friends and tell them where she found them. One day, her teacher said they would have a quiz about rocks. Lily was very happy. She knew a lot about rocks. She thought she would get a good mark. But when she got the quiz, she felt anxious. The quiz had many hard words that she did not understand. She did not know what igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic meant. She did not know what fossils, crystals, and minerals were. She looked at the pictures, but they did not help. She tried to guess, but she was not sure. She wished she had learned more about rocks. She wished she had read the books that her teacher gave them. When the teacher collected the quiz, Lily felt sad. She knew she did not do well. She hoped her teacher would not be angry. She hoped her friends would not laugh. She wished she could go home and play with her rocks.

One day, a curious little cat named Kitty saw a big bowl of water. She wanted to touch it, but she was scared. Her friend, Doggy, told her, "Don't disturb the water, Kitty. Something might happen." Kitty thought about what Doggy said, but she still wanted to touch the water. She waited until Doggy went away. Then, she put her paw in the water. The water moved and made a big splash! Doggy heard the splash and came running back. He saw Kitty all wet and laughed. "I told you not to disturb the water!" he said. Kitty was wet, but she had fun. She learned that sometimes it's okay to be curious and try new things.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play outside. He had a big, red ball. One day, he was playing with his ball when he saw a sandwich on the ground. It was an easy sandwich to see because it was big and colorful. Tim picked up the sandwich and said, "Whose sandwich is this?" A small bird flew down from a tree and said, "That's my sandwich! I dropped it when I was flying." Tim gave the sandwich back to the bird, and they became friends. The bird showed Tim a special game. It was called "Freeze." They would run around and play, but when the bird said "freeze," they had to stop and not move. Tim and the bird had a lot of fun playing Freeze and eating the sandwich together. They were happy and laughed a lot.
