Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki was a very eager kangaroo. She loved to jump and play all day long with her friends. One day, while Kiki was playing, she met a little bird named Billy. "Hi, I'm Billy," said the bird. "Do you want to play with me?" Kiki was so eager to make a new friend. She said, "Yes! Let's play together!" Kiki and Billy played for hours. They ran, they jumped, and they laughed. But then, they saw a big, scary bear. The bear wanted to play too, but Kiki and Billy were scared. "Please, Mr. Bear, we surrender," said Kiki. "We don't want to play with you." The bear felt sad, but he understood. Kiki and Billy went back to playing, and they were happy again. And even though they didn't play with the bear, they knew that sometimes it was okay to surrender and say no.

Once upon a time, there was a fat dinosaur named Dino. Dino was very big and he had lots of friends. They all lived in a nice place with many trees and a big river. One day, Dino and his friends saw a sign near the river. The sign had a picture of a dinosaur and a big red X. Dino did not know what the sign meant, but he thought it was not good. He told his friends to stay away from the river. A few days later, it rained a lot and the river got very big and fast. Dino's friends wanted to play near the river, but they remembered the sign and what Dino said. They all stayed away from the river and played somewhere else. The river got too big and fast, and it washed away the place where Dino and his friends used to play. But because they listened to Dino and the sign, they were all safe. The moral of the story is to always pay attention to signs and listen to your friends when they try to keep you safe.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to win a trophy. He liked to run very fast. He wanted to show his friends how fast he could run. Tim's mom said, "You can win a trophy in a race." Tim went to the race with his mom. All the kids were there. They were ready to run. The race started and Tim ran as fast as he could. He was faster than all the other kids. Tim was so happy. But then, something unexpected happened. A little girl named Sara fell down. Tim stopped running and went back to help her. He said, "Are you okay, Sara?" Sara said, "Yes, thank you, Tim." They both ran to the finish line together. Tim did not win the trophy, but he felt happy that he helped Sara. His mom was very proud and said, "You are a winner in my heart."

Once upon a time, there was a big bird named Ollie. Ollie was an ostrich. He was a very dependable friend. He lived in a warm place with lots of sun. One day, it rained a lot. The ground was very wet. Ollie's friend, a little dog named Dotty, was sad. She did not like the wet ground. Ollie wanted to help Dotty. He had an idea. Ollie used his big wings to fan the ground. He worked very hard to dry the ground. Then, something unexpected happened. As Ollie fanned the ground, he found a shiny rock. It was a pretty rock that made Dotty happy. Ollie and Dotty played on the dry ground with the shiny rock. They had a lot of fun. They were happy because they had each other and the pretty rock.

Matt had a difficult day. He was angry and sad. His mom saw him and asked, "What is wrong, Matt?" Matt responded, "I am having a bad day mom." His mom said, "Why don't you go play and I will make us a sandwich?" Matt went outside and started playing. After a while, he heard his mom calling him. She said, "Look at you! You saved me, I was about to burn the sandwich!" Matt was so happy and they sat down together to enjoy the delicious sandwich.
