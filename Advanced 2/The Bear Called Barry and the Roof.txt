Once there was a bear called Barry. He was a very nice bear, but he was very hungry. One day, he decided to go on an adventure to find food. Barry wandered for days until he found a house with a big roof. He was so excited he decided to lay down underneath the roof to take a rest from his long journey. The roof kept the sun off him, so he was very happy. But then Barry heard a noise - the owner of the house was coming back! The owner was very upset that someone was resting on their roof. They told Barry to leave right away. Barry was so sad, but he knew he had to listen. He quickly left, feeling very hungry and a little bit scared. He soon forgot about the roof and continued on his journey, hoping to find something to eat. And so he did, and they lived happily ever after!

One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. He saw a rake on the ground. Tim wanted to use the rake to make a big pile of leaves. He started to rake the leaves. As Tim raked the leaves, his friend Sue came over. "Can I help?" she asked. "Yes!" said Tim, and they both raked the leaves together. They made a big pile of leaves and smiled. Then, Tim and Sue ran and jumped into the pile of leaves. They laughed and played. But Tim did not see a stick in the leaves. He fell on the stick and it hurt. Sue said, "Are you okay, Tim?" Tim said, "Yes, but it hurt a little." They took the stick out of the leaves and played some more.

Once upon a time, there was a rat named Tim. Tim was a confused rat. He lived in a big house with his rat family. They all liked to play and eat together. But one day, Tim and his family had to separate. Tim went to live in a small room. He missed his family a lot. In the small room, Tim found a big box. He did not know what was inside. He thought maybe it was a surprise for him. He was excited and wanted to find out. One day, Tim's family came back to the house. They were so happy to see Tim again. They all went to the small room and opened the big box together. Inside the box, they found lots of yummy food. Tim and his family ate the food and played together. They were not confused or separate anymore. They were a happy rat family again.

One day, a big, scary bear went for a walk in the woods. He was very hungry and wanted to find some food. He saw a little girl picking up berries and putting them in her basket. The bear thought the berries looked yummy, so he walked up to her. "Hello, little girl," said the bear. "Can I have some berries, please?" The little girl was scared, but she said, "Okay, Mr. Bear. I will share my berries with you." She took some berries out of her basket and scattered them on the ground for the bear to eat. The bear ate the berries and felt very happy. He was not so scary anymore. The little girl smiled at the bear, and they became friends. From that day on, they played together in the woods and shared their berries. And the bear was never scary again.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max wanted to go on a journey. He asked his friend, a big cat named Tom, to go with him. Max said, "Tom, let's go on a journey! But we must behave, okay?" Tom agreed, "Yes, Max! We will behave and have fun!" They walked and walked, and soon they found a fake bone. Max looked at it and said, "This bone is fake, but that's okay. We can still play with it!" Tom and Max played with the fake bone and had a great time. After playing, they continued their journey. They saw many things like trees, flowers, and birds. They behaved very well and had a lot of fun together. At the end of the day, Max and Tom went back home. They were happy and tired from their journey. They promised to go on another journey soon, and always behave.
