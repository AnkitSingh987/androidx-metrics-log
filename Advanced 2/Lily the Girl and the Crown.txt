Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She liked to visit her grandma. Every time she went, she wore her favorite crown. It was made of hard, shiny plastic. Lily loved her crown very much. One day, Lily and her grandma went for a walk. They saw a big dog. The dog was very friendly. It wagged its tail and licked Lily's hand. But then, the wind blew Lily's crown off her head! Lily and her grandma looked for the crown. They looked under a tree, behind a rock, and even in a puddle. At last, they found the hard crown stuck in a bush. Lily was so happy to have her crown back. She and her grandma went home and had a nice cup of warm milk.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little boy. He liked to visit lots of different places with his family. One day, his family decided to visit the marble factory. At the factory, the boy saw lots of pretty colorful marbles. He wanted to take some home with him. But his mum said no, and told him it was naughty to take something without asking. The boy was very unhappy and cried and whined. He would not stop, no matter how many times his parents told him that it wasn't allowed. Suddenly, the boy spotted the most beautiful marble. It was pink with gold sparkles and he just had to have it. The naughty little boy reached out and grabbed it before anyone could stop him. His parents saw what he did and scolded him for being so naughty. But, not wanting to make the boy too sad, they decided to buy the marble for him. The boy was excited and took the marble with him on all his visits. He kept it safe and treasured it for many years to come.

One day, a silly boy went to the barber. He wanted a new haircut. The barber said, "What kind of haircut do you want?" The boy thought for a moment and then said, "I want a haircut that looks like a tree!" The barber laughed and said, "That is a silly idea, but I will try." He started to cut the boy's hair. The boy was very happy. He closed his eyes and began to pray. He prayed that his new haircut would look like a tree. But something unexpected happened! When the barber was done, the boy's hair did not look like a tree. Instead, it looked like a big, funny hat! The boy and the barber laughed and laughed. They knew it was a very silly haircut, but they both loved it.

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. They wanted to make a yummy snack. The cat said, "Let's organize the food and find the knife to cut it." The dog found a big knife and said, "Here is the knife." The cat found some bread and cheese. They were very fast at organizing the food. The dog cut the bread and the cat cut the cheese with the knife. They put the cheese on the bread and made a yummy sandwich. They were very happy and ate the sandwich fast.

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, Spot could not find his ball. He looked and looked, but the ball was gone. Spot was very sad. Spot saw his friend, Cat, playing with a ball. "That's my ball!" Spot said. "Give it back!" But Cat did not want to give the ball back. Cat liked the ball too. They both wanted the ball and did not want to share. "Give me the ball!" Spot demanded. Cat did not like being told what to do. But Cat saw how sad Spot was. So, Cat gave Spot his ball back. Spot was very happy. He said, "Thank you, Cat. You are a good friend." They played with the ball together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah. She was very curious and one day she decided to explore her garden. She took a big step forward and entered the misty garden. Everything was so wet and the air was so damp. Sarah walked around, admiring the wet plants and trees. Suddenly, she heard a noise coming from the back of the garden. She crept closer and saw a dry patch of land with a little door in the middle. She cautiously reached out her hand to open the door. To her surprise, a small toy popped out and greeted her with a smile. Sarah was so excited and gave the toy a big hug. She continued to explore the garden, feeling even more adventurous now. As the sun was setting, Sarah walked back home having had a wonderful day.
