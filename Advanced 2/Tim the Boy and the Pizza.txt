Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to eat pizza. One day, his mom made a big pizza for dinner. Tim was very happy. Tim had a little sister named Sue. Sue was sometimes foolish. She liked to disturb Tim when he was eating. Tim did not like this at all. He told Sue, "Please do not disturb me when I eat pizza." Sue listened to Tim and did not disturb him. They both enjoyed their pizza together. Tim was happy that Sue was not foolish anymore. They laughed and had a good time. And from that day on, they always ate pizza together without any disturbance.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play with his drum. He liked to feel the rhythm when he played. He played his drum in an original way that made everyone happy. Tim loved to play his drum for his friends and family. One day, while Tim was playing his drum, a cat came to listen. The cat started to dance to the rhythm. Tim was very surprised because he had never seen a cat dance before. He thought it was funny and started to play even louder. As Tim played louder, more animals came to dance. Soon, there was a big party with many animals dancing to the rhythm of Tim's drum. It was an unexpected and fun day for everyone. And Tim learned that even animals can enjoy the rhythm of his original drum playing.

Once upon a time, there were twin boys named Tom and Tim. They lived in a small house with their mom and dad. Tom and Tim loved to play outside, even when it was cold. One cold day, Tom and Tim put on their warm coats and went outside to play. They built a big snowman with a carrot nose and stick arms. Tim said, "I want a hat for our snowman!" Tom agreed, "Yes, a hat would be nice!" They went inside to ask their mom for a hat. "Mom, can we have a hat for our snowman?" Tim asked. Mom smiled and said, "Sure, but you must clean your room first." Tom and Tim cleaned their room quickly and then demanded, "We are done, can we have the hat now?" Mom gave them the hat, and they put it on their snowman. Tom and Tim played outside with their snowman all day. When it was time to go inside, they said goodbye to their snowman and went in to have warm soup with their mom and dad. They were happy to have had such a fun day in the cold.

Once upon a time, in a land of snow, there was a friendly penguin named Pippin. Pippin lived in a small igloo. He loved to play with his friends all day. One day, Pippin found a big box outside his igloo. He wanted to see what was inside. Pippin tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy. He needed help from his friends. Pippin asked his friends, a seal and a polar bear, to help him lift the box. Together, they lifted the box and opened it. Inside, they found a fun toy for everyone to play with. They were all very happy and played together all day.

One day, a boy went to the park. He saw a big, fair tent. Inside the tent, there was a pie contest. The boy wanted to win the contest. He thought, "I will make the best pie!" The boy made a pie with apples and sugar. He put the pie on a table with other pies. He waited for the judge to taste the pies. The judge tasted all the pies. The boy was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. A dog ran into the tent! The dog jumped on the table and ate the boy's pie. The boy was sad. He thought he lost the contest. But the judge saw the dog eat the pie. The judge said, "That pie must be very good if the dog likes it so much!" The boy won the contest after all.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy dog named Bobo. Bobo lived in a small house with a red door. Every morning, Bobo would wake up and play with his toys. One day, Bobo saw a big ball outside the door. He wanted to play with it, but the door was closed. Bobo started to struggle to open the door. He pushed and pushed, but the door would not open. Bobo's friend, a little bird named Chirpy, saw him struggling. Chirpy flew down and helped Bobo open the door. Together, they pushed and the door opened. Bobo and Chirpy played with the big ball all day. They had lots of fun. The moral value of the story is: when we help each other, we can do things that are hard to do alone.
