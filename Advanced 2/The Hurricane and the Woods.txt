Once upon a time, there was a hurricane. It put all the trees in a twist and made the sky so dark. But hidden away in the woods was a small town. The people of the town had put it in a special place, so the hurricane could not find it. The hurricane searched high and low but could not find the town. Every day the people of the town were safe and happy. One day they decided to put up a giant sign. It said 'Welcome to our hidden town!' The people of the town were so proud of their sign and they were happy that they were safe from the hurricane. The end.

Once upon a time there was a young prince who didn't feel well. He had a sick heart, and he couldn't make it heal. He was very sad. So the prince went on a quest to find something to make him feel better. He asked every shopkeeper he met, but no one could help him. Finally, he stumbled into a grocery. It was a pretty grocery, with a kindly shopkeeper who knew just how to help the prince. She offered him some special healing herbs. The prince took the herbs and put them on his sore heart. Slowly, but surely, the herbs were able to heal the prince’s heart. He was so happy! From then on he decided to always trust his own special healing power. He knew that the pretty grocery had given him the tools he needed to take care of himself. The prince went home feeling better than ever, and he knew this message was true: true healing comes from within!

One day, a little girl named Amy found a mitten on the ground. She wanted to find the person who lost it. She asked her friend Sally, "Did you lose a mitten?" Sally said, "No, but let's wrap it in a box and give it to someone who needs it." They put the mitten in a box and went to find someone who was cold. On their way, they saw a naughty dog named Max. Max liked to play tricks on people. He saw the box and thought it would be fun to take it. Max took the box and ran away very fast. Amy and Sally were sad, but they did not give up. They chased Max until he got tired. When Max stopped, something unexpected happened. Max opened the box, and inside were two mittens! Max felt bad for being naughty, so he gave the mittens to Amy and Sally. Amy and Sally were very happy. They found a cold man and gave him the mittens. The man was grateful, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim and his sister, Sue. They liked to play outside in the sun. One day, they decided to have a race to see who was faster. "Ready, set, go!" said Tim, and they both began to speed down the path. Sue was very careful not to trip, but she wanted to win, too. They both ran as fast as they could, but Sue was a bit faster than Tim. As they finished the race, Sue said, "I won, Tim! But you were fast, too!" Tim smiled and said, "Good job, Sue! You were very careful and fast!" They both laughed and decided to race again, having fun together in the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun in the sky. It made everything look happy. A little girl named Lisa was playing in her yard. She saw a big board near the fence. Lisa had an idea. She called her friend Sam and said, "Let's paint the board with pretty colors." Sam loved the idea. They took their paints and brushes and started to paint. They painted flowers, trees, and animals on the board. When they were done, they stood back to admire their work. "Wow, it looks so pretty!" said Sam. Lisa smiled and said, "Yes, it does. I love our bright and colorful board." They were very happy and proud of their beautiful board.

One day, a cat and a dog went for a walk. They saw a big stack of toys near a tree. The cat and dog wanted to play with the toys. They ran to the stack and started to play. While they were playing, they found a sour apple under the toys. The cat and dog did not like the sour apple. They made a funny face and tried to hide the apple. But then, something unexpected happened. The sour apple started to talk! It said, "I am a magic apple. If you eat me, you will get a big surprise!" The cat and dog were scared at first, but they decided to share the magic apple. They took a bite and the sour apple turned sweet. Suddenly, the cat and dog could talk to each other like people! They were so happy and played with the toys all day. The magic apple made their walk even more fun than they thought it would be.
