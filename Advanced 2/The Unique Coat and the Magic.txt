Once upon a time, there was a unique coat. It was red and blue. The coat could lock like a door. It was a magic coat. One day, a little boy found the coat. He put it on and felt very warm. He showed the coat to his friends. They all liked it. They wanted a magic coat too. The little boy and his friends played outside. It was cold, but the coat kept him warm. When he went home, he locked the coat. He was happy to have a unique coat.

Once upon a time there was a girl named Kayla. She was 3 years old and was very excited. Today was a fine day and she was going to see some fireworks. Kayla went outside to look at the firework in the sky. She saw lots of colors and shapes. She stared up in the night sky in awe. Suddenly, something bad happened. A firework shot down and Kayla got scared. She screamed and ran away. But it was too late. The firework exploded near her and she got hurt. Kayla was sad and crying. Everyone was sad that she got hurt. She was taken to the hospital to check if she was okay. The doctors said she was going to be fine but everyone was very sad.

Once there was a little girl, who was three years old. She was playing in the yard with her dress on when she started to get tired. She wanted to go inside and take a nap. But suddenly, her puppy came running up to her. She was so excited to see him, she forgot about being tired. She started to play with her puppy and didn't want to go inside. The little girl and the puppy ran around and played in the yard for a long time. But soon the little girl was so very tired that she had to stop playing and lay down in the grass. She was so tired she couldn't keep her eyes open. But the puppy wasn't tired at all. He kept wanting to play and kept barking at her to wake her up. The little girl was confused. She wanted to play with her puppy, but she was too tired. Finally, the little girl found a solution. She started eating some snacks that she had in her pocket. Suddenly, she was awake again. She was able to eat her snacks and play with her puppy at the same time. After a few minutes, she was happy and not so tired anymore. She could go inside and take a nap after all. The little girl and the puppy had lots of fun playing together, and she didn't feel so tired anymore.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, scary dog. The dog barked loudly. Tim was scared and felt pain in his tummy. Tim's mom saw his face and asked, "What's wrong, Tim?" Tim said, "Mom, the dog is scary and my tummy hurts." His mom hugged him and said, "Don't worry, the dog won't hurt you." Then, a nice man came and took the scary dog away. Tim felt better and the pain went away. He learned that talking about his feelings can help make the scary things go away.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to take pictures with her camera. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. At the park, Lily saw a big, disgusting bug. It was slimy and had lots of legs. She wanted to take a picture of it. Her mom said, "No, Lily, it's too yucky!" But Lily insisted. She said, "Please, Mom, I want to take a picture!" Finally, her mom said yes. Lily took a picture of the disgusting bug with her camera. She was so happy. When they went home, Lily showed the picture to her friends. They all said, "Eww!" But Lily just laughed and said, "It's so cool!" From that day on, Lily loved to take pictures of all kinds of things, even the yucky ones. And everyone knew that Lily was the bravest girl with a camera.
