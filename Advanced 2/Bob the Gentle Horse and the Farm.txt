Once upon a time, there was a gentle horse named Bob. Bob lived on a small farm with a kind farmer. Bob liked to help the farmer and play with the other animals. The farmer loved Bob and took good care of him. One day, the farmer went to the market and got a big bag of treats. The treats were for Bob and the other animals. The farmer told Bob not to eat all the treats, or he would spoil his dinner. Bob wanted to be a good horse, but the treats looked so yummy. Bob thought about what the farmer said, but he couldn't stop thinking about the treats. He ate one, then two, then three. Soon, Bob ate all the treats. When dinner time came, Bob was not hungry. The farmer was sad that Bob spoiled his dinner. Bob felt bad and promised not to eat too many treats again. The moral of the story is to listen to others and not be greedy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy cat named Kitty. She lived in a small house with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play together every day. One sunny day, Lucy and Kitty were playing in the garden. Lucy said, "Kitty, let's play a game! We can choose to tell the truth or tell a lie. If we tell the truth, we get a treat!" Kitty liked treats, so she nodded her head excitedly. Lucy asked, "Kitty, are you a clumsy cat?" Kitty thought for a moment and said, "Yes, I am clumsy." Lucy smiled and said, "Good job, Kitty! You told the truth!" She gave Kitty a treat. They played the game all day, choosing to tell the truth and enjoying their treats together.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a smelly envelope in his backyard. He didn't know where it came from, but he wanted to find out. So, he decided to raise his hand and ask his mom about the envelope. "Mom, what is this smelly envelope?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the envelope and said, "I don't know, Tim. Let's open it and see what's inside." Inside the envelope, there was a note. The note said, "Dear Tim, I am your friend, the smelly skunk. I live in the woods near your house. I am sorry for the smell. I hope we can be friends." Tim smiled and showed the note to his mom. Tim and his mom went to the woods to find the smelly skunk. They saw him near a big tree. Tim said, "Hello, smelly skunk! I got your note. We can be friends." The smelly skunk smiled and said, "Thank you, Tim. I am happy to have a friend like you." From that day on, Tim and the smelly skunk played together in the woods. They had lots of fun and became best friends. And Tim learned that even smelly things can be good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Daisy. Daisy lived on a big farm with many other animals. Daisy was an open and friendly cow. She loved to play with her friends all day long. One day, Daisy's farmer came to her and asked for her help. He needed Daisy to provide cream for a cake he was making. Daisy was very happy to help her farmer. She wanted to make the best cream ever. Daisy worked hard to make the cream. She gave the cream to the farmer and he used it to make a big, yummy cake. The farmer shared the cake with all the animals on the farm. They all loved the cake and thanked Daisy for her help. Daisy was very proud and happy that she could help her friends.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable doctor named Dr. Good. He was very kind and always helped people feel better. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to see him. She had a hurt leg and needed support to walk. "Hello, Lucy," said Dr. Good. "I see your leg needs help. I will give you support so you can walk again." "Thank you, Dr. Good," said Lucy. She was happy that the dependable doctor would help her. Dr. Good put a soft band on Lucy's leg. The band gave her leg the support it needed. Soon, Lucy could walk again. She was very happy and thanked Dr. Good for his help. Everyone in the town loved the dependable doctor because he always made them feel better.
