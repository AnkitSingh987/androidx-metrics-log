Once upon a time, there was a big factory. In the factory, many people worked together. They made toys for kids. The factory was known to be very reliable. All the toys were strong and never broke. One day, a new worker came to the factory. He was a clown who could perform tricks. He said, "Hello, I am here to make the kids happy with my tricks!" The workers were surprised but they let him perform for them. They all laughed and clapped their hands. They thought the clown was fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The clown was not just a clown. He was a magic clown! He said, "I have a gift for this factory!" With a wave of his hand, all the toys came to life. The toys could now walk and talk. The workers were amazed. They knew that the kids would love these magic toys even more. The factory became even more reliable and famous, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a bald soldier named Tom went for a walk. He was very tired, so he yawned a big yawn. As he walked, he saw a little girl named Lily. She was playing with her toy car in the park. "Hi, Lily!" said Tom. "What are you doing?" Lily looked up and smiled. "I am playing with my car, Tom. Do you want to play too?" Just as they started to play, they heard a loud noise. They looked up and saw a big bird in the sky. The bird was not like other birds. It was very big and had a long neck. Tom and Lily were scared. But then, the big bird spoke. "Don't be scared, Tom and Lily. I am a friendly bird. I just want to play too!" So Tom, Lily, and the big bird played together in the park. The bald soldier was not tired anymore, and they all had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big refrigerator. It was full of many different foods. One day, the foods started to talk to each other. The apple said to the orange, "We are so different. I think we should separate." The orange agreed, "Yes, we are not the same. Let's go to different places in the refrigerator." So, the apple went to the top shelf, and the orange went to the bottom shelf. They were now separate and felt better. But soon, they began to miss each other. The apple called to the orange, "I miss you. Can we be friends again?" The orange replied, "Yes, I miss you too. Let's be friends, even if we are different." From that day on, the apple and the orange stayed together in the refrigerator. They learned that it was okay to be different and still be friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there were two friends called Ben and Sam. Ben was brave and Sam was curious. They were both very good friends. One day, the two friends went for a walk and came across a wide pit. Ben was frustrated and wanted to cross it, but it was too wide and deep. But then, Sam had an idea. She picked up a long stick and handed it to Ben. Then, Sam picked up another stick and they put them together to make a bridge. The sticks united to make a bridge across the pit. The two friends crossed the bridge safely and hugged each other with joy. They were so happy that they both united to make the bridge. Everyone was so proud of them.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big, disgusting bug. The bug was a threat to the other bugs. Tim wanted to help them. Tim tried to go near the bug, but it moved away. He tried again and again, but the bug kept going away. Then, Tim had an idea. He got a stick and went near the bug. He thought the stick would scare the bug away. But something unexpected happened. When Tim went near the bug with the stick, the bug jumped on the stick. It was not scared at all. Instead, the bug started to clean the stick. Tim saw that the bug was not a threat. It was just trying to help clean the park. Tim felt happy and played with the bug. They became friends and helped keep the park clean together.
