Once upon a time there was a man who had two daughters. He loved them both very much and wanted the best for them. He decided to prepare a huge surprise for his youngest sister. The man worked very hard to get the surprise ready. He bought some new toys and made a big cake. Finally, the surprise was complete and he called both his daughters in for the occasion. He was so excited to see their reactions. The youngest sister was delighted with the surprise. She shouted and ran around in excitement. She asked her father what he had prepared for her. Suddenly, the man screamed in pain. He had forgotten to tie his shoelaces and had tripped on them. He had hurt his ankle very badly. The two sisters were very sad. They were sadder than ever before. They knew that the huge surprise had been ruined. The man was very sad too, but he was glad that he had made his two daughters happy by trying to prepare the surprise.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a wallet on the floor. He picked it up and saw that it was full of money. Tim was very happy and wanted to buy a lot of toys. But he knew it was important to find the owner of the wallet. Tim went to his mom and showed her the wallet. His mom said, "We must find the person who lost this wallet. It is not right to keep it." Tim agreed and they went to the park to look for the owner. In the park, they met a sad man named Mr. Brown. Tim asked, "Did you lose a wallet?" Mr. Brown said, "Yes, I did! I was so worried. Thank you for finding it." Tim gave the wallet back to Mr. Brown. Mr. Brown was very happy and allowed Tim to pick a toy as a thank you gift. Tim learned that it is important to be honest and help others.

Lily liked to make jewelry with her mom. They used beads, strings, and clips to make necklaces, bracelets, and earrings. Lily liked to choose the colors and shapes of the beads. She liked to make jewelry that was fancy and shiny. One day, Lily and her mom were making jewelry for Lily's grandma. Lily wanted to make a special necklace for her. She picked some pink and purple beads and a heart clip. She asked her mom to help her string the beads. She was very careful not to drop any. When they were done, Lily looked at her necklace. She was very proud of it. She said to her mom, "Look, mom, I complete the necklace for grandma. It is very fancy and pretty. Do you think she will like it?" Her mom smiled and hugged her. She said, "I think she will love it, Lily. You did a great job. You are very creative and thoughtful. Let's put it in a box and wrap it with a ribbon. Then we can give it to her when we visit her tomorrow." Lily nodded and helped her mom pack the necklace. She was very excited to see her grandma and give her the jewelry. She hoped her grandma would wear it and think of her. She loved her grandma very much.

Once upon a time, in a big brown house, there lived a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside with her friends. One day, while they were playing, they found a big brown trunk under a tree. Sue and her friends were very excited. They wanted to know what was inside the trunk. Sue tried to open it, but it was locked. They looked for a key, but could not find one. Then, a small brown dog entered the yard. The dog had a shiny key in its mouth! The dog gave the key to Sue. She used the key to open the trunk. They thought there would be toys inside, but there were only old clothes. Sue and her friends were sad at first. But then, they had an idea. They put on the clothes and played dress-up! They had a fun day, and the small brown dog played with them too.
