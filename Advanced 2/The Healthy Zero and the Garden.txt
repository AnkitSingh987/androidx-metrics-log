Once upon a time, there was a healthy zero. He lived alone in a small house and rarely ever left his home. One day, he decided he would take a chance and mail himself to a far-off place. So, he grabbed an envelope and put himself inside. The zero was very excited about his journey, even though he wasn't sure what would happen. He hoped whatever it was, it would be something wonderful. After a long journey, the zero arrived at his destination. He found himself in a beautiful garden full of vibrant flowers and busy bumblebees. The zero was amazed at the sight and felt like he was finally home. As he looked around, he noticed something else. A friendly little girl was playing nearby. The zero and little girl became friends. Every day, they would play and explore the garden together. The healthy zero had finally found his true home. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his best friend, Sam. They played with their toys and had a lot of fun. One day, Tim saw Sam playing with a new toy. Tim wanted to play with the toy too, but Sam said no. Tim was sad and angry. Tim thought about taking the toy when Sam was not looking. But Tim knew that was not an honest thing to do. He decided to talk to Sam and tell him how he felt. Tim told Sam he was sad and wanted to play with the new toy too. Sam listened and felt bad for not sharing. Sam said sorry to Tim and let him play with the new toy. Tim was happy and forgave Sam. They played together and had a lot of fun. From that day on, they always shared their toys and played together. They learned that being honest and forgiving each other made them even better friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a pretty apron with a hidden pocket. Amy liked to store her toys in the pocket. One day, she went to the park with her mom. At the park, Amy met a new friend named Tim. They played on the swings and in the sandbox. Amy showed Tim her apron with the hidden pocket. "Look!", she said, "I have a toy in my pocket!" Tim liked Amy's apron and they played with the toy together. They had so much fun at the park. When it was time to go home, Amy put her toy back in the hidden pocket. She said goodbye to Tim and went home with her mom.

Once upon a time, there was a funny little boy named Tim. Tim loved to joke and make his friends laugh. He was very creative and always had new ideas for fun games. One day, he decided to play a game with the number zero. Tim drew a big zero on the ground with chalk. He told his friends, "Let's see who can jump over the zero the most times!" All his friends loved the idea and they took turns jumping over the zero. They laughed and joked as they played. At the end of the day, Tim and his friends were very happy. They had a lot of fun playing with the zero and being creative. They knew they could always count on Tim to come up with new and funny games to play.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He found a big black rock. "What is this?" he asked his mom. "That is coal, Tim," she said. "It is not normal to find coal in your room. Let's clean your room and see if we find more." As they cleaned, they found more coal. "Why is there so much coal here?" Tim asked. "I don't know," said his mom. "Let's keep cleaning and maybe we will find out." After they cleaned, they found a small door in the wall. They opened it and saw a little man. "Hello!" he said. "I am a coal elf. I hid the coal in your room. I am sorry for the mess. I will help you clean." They all cleaned together and the room was normal again. The coal elf became Tim's friend and they played together every day.
