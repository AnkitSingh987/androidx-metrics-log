Once upon a time, there was a grumpy princess who lived in a castle. Every day the princess would yell at everyone and make them feel really sad. One day, the princess heard a noise from upstairs. It was repeating over and over again. The princess was curious, so she ran up the stairs to investigate. When she got to the top, she was surprised to see a small puppy barking. The princess smiled and said "Hello, little puppy. What are you doing up here?" The little puppy said "I'm just playing. I like to repeat things!" The princess was delighted and started to play with the puppy. Soon, the grumpiness began to melt away and everyone in the castle was happy again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went to a big park with lots of trees and flowers. Tim saw a small, unknown animal. It was very cute and had soft fur. Tim wanted to say hi to the animal. "Hello, little animal!" said Tim. The animal looked at him and said, "Hi, I am lost. Can you help me find my mom?" Tim looked at his mom and she nodded. They all started to look for the animal's mom together. As they walked, they found a big rock. The animal's mom was behind the rock! The animal ran to its mom and they hugged. The little animal looked at Tim and said, "Thank you for helping me!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome!" Then, Tim and his mom walked back home, happy that they had helped their new friend.

One day, a little girl was out walking with her mum. They walked down the street together, and the little girl was looking at all the things around her. She saw a large, yellow rubber duck in the shop window. She stopped and stared at it with wide eyes. The duck was so big and bright that she couldn't take her eyes away. Mum smiled at her and asked if she wanted to go in and get it. But the little girl shook her head and stepped back, looking a bit fearful. She wasn't sure if she wanted the big, yellow rubber duck. Mum smiled and said, "That's okay. Let's keep walking." But the little girl kept glancing back over her shoulder at the duck. She wished she could get it but was still a bit scared. Mum put her arm around her and said, "It's okay. Whatever you decide is fine with me." The little girl smiled and let her mum continue to hold her as they walked away. She was no longer scared and knew that if she wanted to get the big, yellow rubber duck, she could.

Tommy was very excited to go to the park. He got dressed and put his shoes on, then he ran all around the house. His mom was so thoughtful and said “Tommy, why don’t you take your finger-puppets with you to the park?” Tommy smiled and put them in his pocket. When he got to the park, he found a nice big tree. He laid down on the grass next to it and held up his finger-puppets. They were so funny and made him laugh. As he was playing, he heard a buzzing noise. He looked up and saw a bee flying around the tree. It was so beautiful, so he decided to lie still and watch it. Suddenly, a little voice said “I can help you find the bee”. Tommy jumped up and saw a friendly girl standing there. She was holding out a thoughtful gift - a little toy bee. She smiled and said “Let’s go find the real bee together”. Tommy said “Yes!” and off they went. They laughed and talked and looked for the bee until the sun was setting. In the end, they never found it, but Tommy still had a great time. He thanked the girl as she said goodbye, and he went home with his finger-puppets and his toy bee.

One day, a big lion and a little ant met. The lion was very busy. The ant asked, "Why are you so busy?" The lion said, "I am getting ready for war with the other animals." The ant wanted to help. The lion laughed, "You are too small to help in the war." The ant said, "I can teach you something to help you win." The lion did not believe the ant, but he let the ant teach him. The ant taught the lion how to be very quiet when he walked. The lion used this to surprise the other animals. The other animals did not want to fight anymore. The lion and the ant became best friends. The lion learned that even small friends can help in big ways.
