Once upon a time, there was a weak little bird who could not fly. The bird lived in a big tree with many other birds. They all could fly, but the weak bird could not. The bird was sad because it was the season when all the birds had to move to a warm place. One day, a big wind came and blew the weak bird out of the tree. The bird was scared, but then something unexpected happened. A group of ants saw the bird and wanted to help. The ants said, "We can unite and help you get to the warm place." The ants and the weak bird worked together. They made a plan to use leaves and sticks to make a little boat. The ants helped the weak bird get on the boat and pushed it to the river. The weak bird floated down the river, and the ants walked beside it. After many days, they reached the warm place where the other birds were. The weak bird was very thankful to the ants for helping. The ants and the weak bird became good friends. They showed everyone that even the smallest and weakest can do big things when they unite and work together. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play golf with their dad. They had a small golf set with balls and clubs that they could use in the backyard. One day, they decided to decorate their golf set with stickers and paint. They wanted to make it more creative and fun. They took out their art supplies and started to stick and paint different things on their balls and clubs. They made stars, flowers, animals, and letters. They were very happy with their work and showed it to their dad. "Dad, look at our golf set! We decorated it with stickers and paint. Do you like it?" Ben asked. Dad smiled and said, "Wow, you are very creative, Ben and Lily. I like your golf set very much. It looks very colorful and cheerful. But do you know what else is important when you play golf?" "What, dad?" Lily asked. "Being fair and honest. Golf is a game where you have to follow the rules and respect your opponents. You have to count your strokes and not cheat. You have to be polite and not make noise when someone else is playing. You have to be a good sport and not get angry or boastful. Do you understand?" Dad explained. Ben and Lily nodded and said, "Yes, dad. We understand. We want to be fair and honest when we play golf. We want to be good golfers and good people." Dad hugged them and said, "I'm proud of you, Ben and Lily. You are not only creative, but also kind and smart. Now, let's go and play some golf with your beautiful golf set. And remember, have fun and be fair!"

Once upon a time, there was a lonely boy named Tom. Tom had no friends to play with. One day, Tom saw a big race happening in the park. He wanted to join the race and make new friends. The race would include running, jumping, and laughing. Before the race, Tom practiced running and jumping every day. He was getting better and better. He knew that if he practiced a lot, he would have a chance at victory. Tom was excited and couldn't wait for the race day. Finally, the race day came. Tom was ready to run and jump with all the other kids. As he raced, he laughed and had so much fun. In the end, Tom won the race and felt the joy of victory. All the other kids cheered for him, and Tom was not lonely anymore. He made many new friends that day, and they all played together happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a big palace, there was a king. The king had a dog named Sam. Sam was not very smart. He did many stupid things. One day, Sam saw a cat. He wanted to chase the cat. The cat ran away very fast. The cat ran into the palace. Sam urged to follow the cat. He ran after the cat, but he knocked over many things in the palace. The king was not happy. He told Sam to stop chasing the cat. But Sam did not listen. He kept running after the cat. At last, Sam caught the cat. But the palace was a big mess. The king was sad. He told Sam that he must learn to listen. Sam felt bad for making the king sad. He promised to be a good dog and listen to the king. The moral of the story is to always listen to others and not to do stupid things.
