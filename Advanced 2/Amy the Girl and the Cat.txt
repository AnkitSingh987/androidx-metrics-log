Title: Amy the Girl and the Cat

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had a big porch in front of her house. She liked to play on the porch with her toys. One day, she saw a small, hungry cat on the porch. The cat was looking for food. Amy said, "Hi, cat! Are you hungry?" The cat meowed and came closer to Amy. Amy went inside her house and got a bowl of milk for the hungry cat. The cat drank the milk very fast. It was very happy. After the cat finished drinking the milk, it went to Amy and gave her a kiss on her hand. Amy smiled and said, "You're welcome, cat. I'm happy to help you." From that day on, the cat and Amy became best friends and always played together on the porch.

One day, a boy named Tim found a toy. The toy was a bald man in a little boat. Tim liked the toy very much. He wanted to see the toy sail on the water. Tim took the toy to a pond. He put the toy in the water and said, "Sail, sail, little toy!" The toy started to sail. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened. The toy grew big! The bald man in the boat was not a toy anymore. He was a real man! The man said, "Thank you, Tim, for helping me. I was a toy, but now I am free!" Tim was surprised but happy to make a new friend. They both smiled and watched the boat sail together.

One day, a little boy named Tim got a new jacket. It was a shiny jacket. Tim loved his new jacket and wanted to show it to his friends. Tim went to the park and saw his friend, Sue. "Look at my shiny jacket!" said Tim. Sue looked at his jacket and said, "Wow, it is so shiny!" Tim felt happy and not ashamed of his new jacket. Then, the sun began to shine. Tim and Sue played in the park, and Tim's shiny jacket sparkled in the sun. They had a fun day together, and Tim was very proud of his shiny jacket.

One night, Jessica was walking in the woods. The bright stars twinkled above her, and the night felt magical. Suddenly, Jessica heard a noise. She stopped and peered into the darkness. There, in front of her, was a huge bear! The bear growled and waved her paws. It was time to escape! Jessica ran as fast as she could, but the bear was too close. Just as Jessica thought she was safe, the bear caught her! Jessica was terrified as the bear rose up to its full size. But then, just then, Jessica heard a different sound. It was a wise old owl, perched high in a nearby tree. "Don't worry, Jessica," the owl warned. "If you move slowly, you can escape." So, Jessica did as the wise owl said. She moved slowly and the bear eventually became bored. The bear turned and lumbered off into the dark night and Jessica made her escape!

Once upon a time there was a girl named Lucy. She was three years old. One day she was playing in the garden when a guard came along. He had a big smile on his face. Lucy was a bit scared at first, but then she noticed that he was being very sweet. She smiled back at him. The guard spoke to Lucy in a kind voice. He asked her how she was doing and if she wanted some ice cream. Lucy nodded yes enthusiastically. The guard went away and came back with a big bowl of ice cream. He said he had made it. It was pink and very sweet. Lucy thanked him and enjoyed every bite. When Lucy finished the ice cream she said goodbye to the guard. She waved to him and he waved back. She knew that he would always be there to make sure she was safe. The end.

Once upon a time there was a clumsy bear. He wanted to make some soup for dinner, but he forgot to add water. He had all the other ingredients, but he needed the water to make the soup nice and yummy. He looked around for water, but there was none. He thought and thought, then he had an idea. He ran to the river, but it was too far away. He then had an even better idea. He hopped over to a nearby pond and filled his pot with pond water. He hopped back to his house and added the water to his soup. It smelled delicious! He was so proud of himself. The soup was the best soup he ever made.
