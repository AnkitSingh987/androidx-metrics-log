One day, a fast rabbit and a slow turtle were in a race. They wanted to see who could run to a big tree first. All the animals came to watch and cheer for them. As they started the race, the rabbit ran very fast. The turtle walked very slow. The rabbit was so far ahead, he thought, "I can take a rest and still win." So, he stopped and fell asleep. The turtle did not give up. He kept walking, slow and steady. He walked past the sleeping rabbit. The other animals saw this and started to admire the turtle. The rabbit woke up and saw the turtle near the finish line. He said, "Oh no! I need to run fast!" But it was too late. The turtle crossed the finish line and won the race. All the animals cheered for the turtle and admired his hard work. The rabbit learned that being fast is not always the best way to win.

Once upon a time, there was a fast leopard. He loved to run and play in the big forest. The fast leopard had many friends, like the nice bird and the funny monkey. They all liked to play together. One day, the fast leopard found a big box. The box had a lock on it. The fast leopard was very curious. He wanted to open the lock and see what was inside. The nice bird and the funny monkey told the fast leopard not to open the lock. They said it may not be safe. The fast leopard did not listen. He opened the lock and looked inside the box. A big wind came out of the box. The wind blew the fast leopard, the nice bird, and the funny monkey far away. They could not find their way back home. They were very sad and missed their friends in the big forest.

Once there was a little boy named Sam. He was really curious about the world. One day, he decided to go on an adventure to find the truth. He looked all around his house but couldn't find it. He was getting frustrated when he got an idea. He thought if he could escape his house, he'd be able to find it! Sam went to his backyard and peeked out. He saw the flexible fence that surrounded his backyard. He smiled and knew he could escape. He climbed the fence, but it was quite hard. He tried and tried until he eventually made it over and he was free! He began his adventure, wandering around looking for clues. He found a grassy open field and he wanted to explore every corner of it. As he walked, he heard someone call his name. It was his mom! She asked Sam why he was trying to escape. Sam explained that he wanted to find the truth. His mom smiled and hugged him. She said the truth is within you, and the only way to find it is to be flexible. Sam nodded and thanked his mom. He felt like he had learnt a lesson. From then on he was determined to be flexible and find the truth.

John and Joan were walking through the park. It was a warm day, so they both decided to take a break and sit down on the grass. As they rested, John saw a beautiful rose. He wanted to pick it to show Joan, so he got up, plucked it and ran back to her. "Look Joan," he said with a smile, "I found a rose for you!" Joan was so happy. She thanked John and hugged him. Together, they went on their walk again, with John holding the rose proudly in his hands. As they walked, they discovered a perfect place to bury the rose. With John's help, Joan started to carefully dig a hole in the soft earth, and when it was deep enough, she placed the rose inside and patted down the earth. "Now the rose will stay safe and warm here forever," Joan said, smiling at John. John nodded, happy that the rose was now safe in its little spot. He loved to help Joan like this. Together, they brushed their hands off, and went on their way.
