Once upon a time, there was a clever cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play with her favorite toy, a big red button. She would push the button with her paw and watch it spin around and around. One day, Kitty couldn't find her button. She looked under her bed, in her toy box, and behind the couch. But the button was nowhere to be found. Kitty was very sad and didn't know what to do. Then, Kitty decided to ask her friend, Doggy, for help. Doggy was good at finding things. Together, they searched the whole house. Finally, they found the button under a big pile of leaves in the yard. Kitty was so happy! She thanked her friend Doggy and they played with the button all day long.

One day, a little bird named Blue was flying near the beach. She saw the big blue waves and felt the bitter cold wind. Blue was a happy bird, and she loved to sing. She sat on a rock near the water and began to sing her favorite song. As Blue sang, a small fish named Fin heard her from under the water. Fin liked the song very much and wanted to be friends with Blue. He jumped out of the water and said, "Hi, I am Fin. I like your song. Can we be friends?" Blue was happy to have a new friend. She said, "Yes, we can be friends, Fin! I am Blue. Let's sing and play together." So, Blue and Fin sang and played by the big blue waves all day. They were very happy, and they did not feel the bitter cold wind anymore.

Once upon a time, there lived a boy named Tony. He was an impatient little boy and he could never sit still. One day, Tony walked outside and saw a mosquito buzzing around. He grabbed his toy plane and said, "I'm gonna get you, pesky mosquito!" He flew the plane around the mosquito, excitedly trying to catch it. He didn't give up and kept zip, zipping the plane back and forth around the mosquito. Suddenly, the mosquito flew away! Tony was very disappointed, but he knew he tried his best. He put his plane away and said, "Maybe next time I'll be able to catch you!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Alex. One day, Alex was walking in the park, when she noticed something unusual. In the park was a big blue apple tree. Alex couldn't believe her eyes! She ran up to the tree and smiled. She looked up at it and nodded her head. Just then, from behind the tree hopped a cute little blue bird. The bird looked at Alex and chirped. Alex smiled and nodded her head. She knew exactly what the bird was saying - it wanted an apple. Delighted, Alex quickly plucked an apple from the tree and handed it to the bird. Again, the bird chirped happily and nodded its head in thanks. As the bird flew away, Alex nodded and waved goodbye. She knew she had made a new friend.

One day, a unique bird named Bobo could speak like people. Bobo lived in a big tree near a train station. He loved to watch the trains come and go. He heard people talk about tickets when they got on the train. Bobo wanted to ride the train too. So, he went to the train station and found a ticket on the ground. He picked it up with his beak and got in line with the people. When it was his turn, he gave the ticket to the train man. The train man was very surprised to see a bird with a ticket. He smiled and let Bobo get on the train. Bobo was so happy. He looked out the window and saw many new things. When the train stopped, he flew back home to his tree. He told all his bird friends about his fun train ride.

Once upon a time, there was a big light room. In the room, there was a tall stand. On the stand, there was a big red button. No one knew what the button did. Everyone was scared to touch it. One day, a small boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the red button on the stand. He was not scared. He wanted to know what the button did. So, he climbed up the stand and pushed the button with all his might. When Tim pushed the button, something unexpected happened. The room started to fill with toys! There were balls, dolls, and cars everywhere. The button was a magic toy button! All the kids came running into the room, and they played with the toys all day long. They were so happy that Tim had found the magic button.
