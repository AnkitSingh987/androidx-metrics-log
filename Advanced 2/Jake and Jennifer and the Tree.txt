Once upon a time there were two best friends, Jake and Jennifer. One day they played in the park and they saw a pine tree. Jake and Jennifer liked to play a game. They liked to see who could bounce the highest. "I bet I can bounce the highest!" said Jake. "No, I bet I can!" said Jennifer. They started jumping on the grass and racing to the pine tree. Jake was the first to get to the pine tree and he started bouncing on the trunk. Jennifer wasn't far behind. She was bouncing around the pine tree and having a great time! But then something happened! As soon as Jennifer stopped bouncing, the pine tree started shaking. She was so scared! But then, she heard a voice coming from the tree saying, "It's ok, Jennifer. You are safe here". Jake and Jennifer looked at each other in surprise. It was the pine tree talking! "It's ok," said the pine tree again. "You can keep bouncing around my trunk. I will keep you safe." And that's what Jake and Jennifer did. They kept bouncing around the pine tree and laughed, knowing that they were safe there.

Once upon a time, there was a loyal dog named Spot. Spot had a friend named Tom. They liked to play and run all day. One day, they found a big box in the park. They did not know what was inside. "Let's open it!" said Tom. They opened the box and found a magic hat. Tom put the hat on Spot. Suddenly, Spot changed into a big bird. Tom was very surprised. "What happened?" asked Tom. Spot could still talk. "I don't know, but I can fly now!" Spot said. Tom and Spot laughed and played with the magic hat. They had lots of fun with their new surprise.

Once upon a time, in a small farm, there was a thick field full of corn. The corn was tall and green. The sun was bright and the sky was blue. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the corn field. He saw the corn and wanted to play. He ran into the thick corn field and began to shake the corn with his hands. The corn danced and swayed with Tim's touch. As Tim shook the corn, he found a little bird hiding in the field. The bird was scared, but Tim was kind. He helped the bird find its way out of the thick corn field. The bird was happy and flew away. Tim felt good and went back home, knowing he had a fun day in the corn field.

Once upon a time there was an ordinary little girl called Ann. She was three years old, and she loved to decorate her room. Every day, Ann would go outside and search for a special piece to add to her collection. One day, Ann found a strange piece on the ground. It was round, colourful and shiny. She thought it looked special, so she decided to take it home. When she showed her mum the piece she had found, her mum warned her. "Ann, this is not a special piece," she said. "It's an ordinary piece. You should only decorate your room with special pieces." Ann was disappointed, but she understood. She learned that it's important to be picky and choosy when decorating her room. Decorating with only special pieces made it look even better. The moral of the story is that it's important to be careful when decorating your home. Even the smallest piece can make all the difference!

One day, a little boy named Tim was eager to play outside. He wanted to unlock the door to go out, but he could not find the key. He looked under the rug, behind the chair, and even in his toy box. Tim was sad because he could not find the key. Then, he saw his mom with scissors in her hand. She was cutting paper to make a pretty picture. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can you help me find the key to unlock the door?" His mom smiled and said, "Of course, Tim. Let's look together." They looked all around the house, and then they found the key in the kitchen. Tim was so happy! He grabbed the key and unlocked the door. But when he opened the door, he saw that it was raining outside. Tim was sad again because he could not play outside. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can play inside and make pretty pictures with the scissors." Tim smiled and they had fun making pictures together.
