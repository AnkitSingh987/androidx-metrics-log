One day, a boy named Tim and his mom went on a vacation. They went to a big beach with lots of sand and water. Tim was very happy to start playing in the sand. While Tim was playing, a rude boy came over and said, "This is my spot! You can't play here!" Tim was sad, but he didn't want to fight. So, Tim and his mom moved to another spot on the beach. At the new spot, Tim met a nice girl named Sue. Sue said, "Do you want to play with me?" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I would like that!" They played all day and had lots of fun on their vacation.

Once upon a time, there was a cold and icy day. On this day, a girl was going on a journey. She prepared her clothing, her blanket and her food and packed her bag. As the day wore on, the girl went farther and farther on her journey. The icy wind blew and the grey sky outside was dim. But the girl kept walking. Soon, the girl's heart was full of happiness. She could feel it increasing more and more. Finally, she arrived at the end of her journey. She turned around and looked behind her, and the entire sky was bright and sparkling with stars! There, she knew she had reached her destination. Thus, the girl's joy and happiness had increased throughout her journey!

Once there was a little boy that lived in a small house. His name was Danny. Every evening, Danny's mom would ask him if he wanted to pray. Danny really liked to pray and he always said yes. Danny and his mom knelt down in the corner of the room and Danny put his hands together. His mom smiled and said, "Say a prayer, Danny." Danny smiled back and closed his eyes. He began to pray to God for all the things he wanted in life: love, peace and health. Then Danny asked for something special. He asked for power. He wanted to use this power to do interesting things. After the prayer, Danny opened his eyes. His mom hugged him and said, "You have the power inside you already, my boy - never forget that!"

Jill and Jack were out in the garden playing tag. They were laughing and having fun. Then, Jill stopped and looked intently at the ground. Jack asked, "What are you doing?" Jill replied, "I found something really cool! Look!" She was holding a beautiful bug with lots of tiny, red dots. "It's really pretty," Jack said. Jill nodded and said, "I'm going to learn all I can about it. I can learn what kind of bug it is, what it likes to eat and what its skin looks like." Jack smiled and said, "You can learn that?" Jill nodded and said, "Yes. I can learn a lot about it. Look, here's a picture of its skin. Isn't it beautiful?" Jack smiled and said, "It sure is!" The two friends continued to explore the bug together and learn all about it. They had lots of fun doing it.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Paul and Amy. They loved to play together every day. One day, Amy decided to give Paul a special treat. She went to the store and bought some honey. When Paul saw the honey, his eyes lit up with joy. "Wow, honey!" Paul exclaimed. "Can I have some?" "Yes, you can, but you have to climb a high tree to get it," Amy replied with a smile. Paul and Amy laughed as they raced each other to the tree. Paul reached it first and was about to climb it when Amy yelled, "Wait, I have an idea - let's play a game of Honey Adventure! I'll hide the honey in the tree and you find it." Paul was so excited. He spent hours searching for honey, but he found it in the very last place he looked - a high branch of the tree. He proudly carried the honey back to Amy, who cheered and hugged him. Paul beamed with happiness, knowing he had the best friend ever.
