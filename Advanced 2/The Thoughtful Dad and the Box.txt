Once upon a time, there was a thoughtful dad. He wanted to design a big surprise for his little girl. He thought a lot about how to make her happy. One day, he had an idea. He went to the garage to find some things. He found a big box, some paint, and a brush. He started to design a beautiful house for her dolls. The little girl saw her dad working in the garage. She was so happy when she saw the dollhouse. She hugged her dad and said "thank you". They played together all day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily and her best friend, a teddy bear named Tim, lived in a small house. They loved to play together all day. One day, they found a box with matches in it. Lily said, "Look, Tim! What are these?" Tim the teddy bear said, "I don't know, Lily. Let's find out!" They took one match and tried to light it. Suddenly, there was a loud noise. The match made a big fire. Lily and Tim got scared and hugged each other tight. Lily's mom heard the loud noise and came running. She saw the fire and quickly put it out. She hugged Lily and Tim, and said, "Matches can be very dangerous. You should never play with them." Lily and Tim learned their lesson and promised never to play with matches again. From that day on, they only played with safe toys and had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. One sunny day, he saw a big box. The box was closed. Tom wanted to open the box. Tom tried to open the box, but it was hard. He pushed and pushed. Then, the box opened! Inside the box, there was coal. Tom was sad. He wanted to find a toy, not coal. But then, something unexpected happened. The coal started to shine. It was very clear and bright. The coal turned into a big, clear diamond! Tom was very happy. He found a beautiful diamond, not just coal.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim lived in a clean, small nest high up in a tree. Every morning, he liked to watch the sunrise. The pretty colors in the sky made him very happy. One day, Tim saw a big box near his tree. He was curious and went to look inside. When he went in, the box shut behind him. He was scared and wanted to go back to his nest. Tim tried to push the box open, but it was too heavy. He called for help, and his friends, the squirrels, heard him. They worked together to open the box. Tim was free again, and he was very thankful. The next morning, Tim watched the sunrise with his friends, and they all felt happy.

Once upon a time, in a big zoo, there was a hairy rhinoceros. His name was Ronny. Ronny had many friends, but he felt sad because his best friend, a big elephant named Ellie, did not want to play with him. One day, Ronny went to Ellie and said, "Let's play, Ellie!" But Ellie did not say anything. She just walked away. Ronny felt very sad because Ellie ignored him. The next day, Ronny tried again. He said, "Please, Ellie, let's play today!" This time, Ellie looked at Ronny and said, "Okay, let's play!" They played all day and had a lot of fun. From that day on, Ellie never ignored Ronny again, and they were the best friends in the whole zoo.

Once upon a time, there was a little jet. The jet was very intelligent. It could fly high and fast. One day, the jet wanted to visit its friend, the bird. The jet went to the bird's home. The bird was happy to see the jet. They played together in the sky. They had lots of fun. The bird said, "You are so fast and smart, jet!" After playing, the jet and the bird were tired. They sat on a big tree. They talked and laughed. The jet was happy it visited its friend. They promised to play together again soon. And they lived happily ever after.
