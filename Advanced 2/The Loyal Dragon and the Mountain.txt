Once upon a time there was a very loyal dragon. Every day, he would go to the top of the tallest mountain and peek out, looking for something special. One day, his wish came true! In the distance, he saw a big, friendly-looking dragon. He was so excited! The loyal dragon quickly flew down the mountain to meet the other dragon. When they got closer they both let out a big, happy roar! The two dragons became the best of friends – they even had their own secret play spot! Every day, the two dragons would play and laugh and have so much fun together. They both particularly loved playing hide and seek. The loyal dragon would always peek out from behind the trees and surprise his friend! They would spend hours at their secret play spot until the night stars came out. The loyal dragon loved his special, new dragon friend and wouldn't forget their wonderful times together ever!

One day, a little boy named Tim was very excited. He loved to watch the rain outside his window. Today, there was a big storm with lots of lightning. Tim liked to count the seconds between the lightning and the loud noise it made. Tim's mom saw him looking at the storm. She asked him if he wanted to go outside and play in the rain. Tim was very happy and said yes. They put on their raincoats and boots and went outside. Tim jumped in the puddles and laughed. Then, something unexpected happened. The lightning stopped, and the rain went away. Tim was sad because he wanted to play more. But then, he saw a big, pretty rainbow in the sky. Tim and his mom were very excited to see the rainbow. They played outside even more and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play outside in the sun. One day, he saw a big hose in the yard. Spot wanted to play with the hose, but he was frightened. He decided to hide behind a tree and watch the hose. As Spot watched the hose, he saw a little bird fly over to it. The bird was not frightened. It played with the hose and had lots of fun. Spot saw that the hose was not scary at all. He came out from behind the tree and started to play with the hose too. Spot and the bird played with the hose all day. They splashed in the water and had a great time. Spot learned that he should not be frightened of new things. He should be brave and try them. This made Spot a very happy dog.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a rubber ball. It was a mighty ball that could bounce very high. Tim loved to play with the ball every day. He would throw it up in the air and watch it bounce all around. One sunny day, while Tim was playing with his ball, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because she lost her toy. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better, so he said, "Sue, do you want to play with my mighty rubber ball?" Sue looked at Tim and smiled. She said, "Yes, I would love to play with your ball!" They played together and had lots of fun. The mighty rubber ball made both Tim and Sue very happy. From that day on, they became the best of friends and played with the rubber ball every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a funny cat named Tom. Tom liked to play with junk. One day, Tom found a big box of junk near his house. He was very happy. Tom saw his friend, a dog named Max. Tom asked Max, "Do you want to play with my junk?" Max said, "Yes, I want to play too!" So, Tom and Max played with the junk together. They had lots of fun. After playing, they were tired. Tom asked Max, "Do you want to eat?" Max said, "Yes, I am hungry." Tom and Max ate some food and then they took a nap. They had a good day playing with the junk and being funny friends.
