Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to grow things in her garden. One sunny day, she decided to raise a pepper plant. She wanted to be independent and do it all by herself. Sue planted the pepper seed in the ground and watered it every day. Soon, a tiny green plant started to grow. Sue was so happy! She talked to the plant and said, "Grow big and strong, little pepper plant!" One day, Sue saw a big, red pepper on her plant. She was so proud of her independent work. She picked the pepper and shared it with her family. They all said, "Yummy! This is the best pepper we have ever eaten!" The moral of the story is that when you work hard and do things by yourself, you can be proud of your accomplishments.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play in the park every day. He would run, jump, and get dirty in the mud. One day, while playing, Tim saw a big hill. He thought it would be fun to climb it. As Tim went up the hill, he saw his friend, Sam. Sam was also dirty from playing in the mud. "Hi, Tim! Do you want to help me increase this big pile of mud?" Sam asked. "Yes, let's do it together!" Tim replied. They both laughed and started to make the mud pile bigger and bigger. When the mud pile was very big, Tim and Sam slid down the hill. They had so much fun that they did it again and again. At the end of the day, they were both very dirty, but very happy. They promised to play together in the wide green park every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the farm with his mom. They saw many animals like cows, pigs, and chickens. Tim's mom said, "Let's pick a pumpkin from the pumpkin patch!" They walked to the pumpkin patch and saw many big, round pumpkins. Tim saw a messy pumpkin with dirt all over it. He said, "I want this one!" His mom said, "Are you sure? It's very messy." Tim said, "Yes, I like it!" While they were walking back, the messy pumpkin started to wiggle. Suddenly, it turned into a funny pumpkin man! Tim and his mom were very surprised. The pumpkin man said, "Thank you for picking me! I am a magic pumpkin! I will clean up my mess and give you three wishes!" Tim and his mom were so happy. They thanked the pumpkin man and made their wishes. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a modern house. In this house, there was a big family. They loved to welcome new friends. One sunny day, a butterfly came to the house. The butterfly was very pretty. The family saw the butterfly and smiled. They wanted the butterfly to be their friend. The family welcomed the butterfly into their home. They played and laughed together. The butterfly was happy to have new friends. And the family was happy too. They loved their new butterfly friend.

One day, a little girl named Sally went to play outside. It was cold, so she wore her warm jacket. The jacket was red and had a sweet picture of a cat on it. Sally loved her jacket very much. While playing, Sally saw her friend Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his toy car. Sally wanted to help Tim find his car. They looked around the park, under the slide, and by the swings. Then, Sally had an idea. "Let's turn around the big tree," she said. "Maybe your car is there." They walked around the big tree and found Tim's toy car! Tim was so happy that he hugged Sally. "Thank you for helping me," Tim said with a big smile. Sally and Tim played with the car together, and they had a fun day. And Sally's sweet jacket kept her warm all day long.

One day, a big, impressive ship was in the water. The ship had a lot of friends. They all played together. But one little boat was sad. The little boat did not have friends. The little boat wanted to play too. The big ship saw the little boat and paused. The big ship thought, "I can help the little boat." The big ship went to the little boat and said, "Let's play together!" The little boat was very happy. All the boats played together and had fun. The moral of the story is, always be kind to others. When you see someone sad, you can help them. When you help others, you make new friends.
