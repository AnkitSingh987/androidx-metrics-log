One day, a boy named Tim went outside to play. The sun was shining and the sky was blue. Tim saw a big cloud in the sky. The cloud was not easy to miss. It was very big and dark. Tim was not scared. He wanted to see what it was. The big cloud came closer and closer. Tim saw that it was a tornado. The tornado was spinning very fast. Tim wanted to enter the tornado and see what was inside. He thought it would be a fun adventure. Tim ran to the tornado and entered it. Inside, he saw many things flying around. There were leaves, sticks, and even a toy car. Tim was happy and laughed as he spun around with the tornado. After a while, the tornado stopped spinning and Tim was back on the ground. He had a great time and went home to tell his mom about his adventure.

Once upon a time, there was a princess who seemed very sad. One day, a kind fairy appeared and gave the princess a magic bag. The princess was so excited! The fairy said the magic bag could make the princess' wishes come true. The princess was thrilled. She put her hand in the bag and wished for her sadness to go away. Suddenly, the fairy seemed angry. She said the magic bag could only make one wish. Then the fairy was gone. The princess was sad, but then she realised that she already made a wish: for her sadness to go away. The princess smiled and she was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a big red barn on a farm. In the barn, there lived many animals. They were all very happy. One day, a little adventurous cat named Timmy came to the barn. Timmy wanted to make new friends. He went to the other animals and said, "Hello! Can I play with you?" The animals liked Timmy and they all played together. They ran, jumped, and had so much fun. After playing, the animals were very happy with Timmy. They wanted to say thank you. So, all the animals stood up and started to clap their hands and paws. They were applauding Timmy for being a good friend. Timmy was very happy too, and they all lived happily ever after.

Tim and Lily were playing with their toy ship in the bathtub. They liked to pretend they were pirates and sailors, and they made funny noises and splashed water. They had so much fun that they did not hear their mom calling them for dinner. "Tim, Lily, hurry up! It's time to eat!" their mom shouted from the kitchen. But Tim and Lily did not listen. They wanted to play more with their ship. Suddenly, they heard a loud bang. The ship hit the faucet and broke into pieces. Water started to leak from the faucet and fill the tub. Tim and Lily were scared and wet. They cried and shouted for help. Their mom came running and saw the mess. She was angry and sad. She turned off the water and took Tim and Lily out of the tub. She wrapped them in towels and dried them. "Why didn't you listen to me?" she asked. "You could have hurt yourself or flooded the bathroom. And you broke your ship. How do you feel now?" Tim and Lily felt miserable. They knew they had been naughty and careless. They said sorry to their mom and hugged her. "We feel bad, mom. We love our ship. We love you more. We will listen to you next time," they said. Their mom smiled and forgave them. She gave them a kiss and took them to the kitchen. She had made their favorite soup and bread for dinner. She told them that they could get a new ship if they behaved well and helped with the chores. Tim and Lily learned their lesson. They realized that playing is fun, but listening to their mom is important. They enjoyed their dinner and thanked their mom. They went to bed happy and dreaming of their new ship.

One day, a dog named Max went on a tour with his friends. They went to a big park to play. Max was very happy. He loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. While playing, Max saw a big door. He wanted to enter and see what was inside. But his friends said, "No, Max! Don't go in there!" Max did not listen. He wanted to see what was inside. So, he entered the big door. Inside, Max found a big cat. The cat was not nice. It was hurt and angry. The cat hissed at Max and tried to hit him. Max was scared. He ran out of the door and back to his friends. They all hugged Max and said, "We are glad you are safe!" From that day on, Max never went into places he did not know.
