One day, a boy named Tim went to the museum with his mom. He was so proud because he could tell her all about the big dinosaurs he saw. Tim loved to learn about them and wanted to see more. As they walked, Tim saw a big room with a sign that said "No Kids Allowed." He wanted to get in and see what was inside. His mom said he couldn't go, but Tim didn't listen. He ran into the room when his mom was not looking. Inside, Tim saw a big dinosaur bone on the floor. He was scared and wanted his mom. He ran out of the room and found his mom. She was not happy, but she hugged him. Tim was sorry and promised not to go into any more rooms he wasn't allowed.

One day, a girl named Lily saw her mom put on makeup. Lily wanted to try it too. She asked her mom to explain how to use the makeup. Her mom told her it was for big girls and not for little kids like her. Lily was sad but she listened to her mom. In the distant room, Lily found her sister's makeup. She thought maybe she could try just a little bit. When no one was looking, she took some makeup and started to put it on her face. She made a big mess with the makeup. It was all over her face and clothes. Then, something unexpected happened. Lily's mom came into the room and saw the mess. She was very angry with Lily. Lily tried to say sorry, but her mom said she had to clean up the mess and go to her room. Lily cried as she cleaned up the makeup. She knew she should have listened to her mom. The day had a bad ending for Lily.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was very excited because today was the day she would gather pretty rocks with her best friend, Tim. They both loved to look for rocks in the park. As they were walking in the park, Tim saw a small pill on the ground. He picked it up and said, "Look, Sue! I found a pill!" Sue looked at it and said, "We should give it to a grown-up. It's not good for us to play with." But Tim wanted to keep it. Suddenly, a tiny bird flew down from a tree and took the pill from Tim's hand. The bird flew away and dropped the pill in a pond. Tim was sad, but Sue said, "It's okay, Tim. We can still gather pretty rocks together." They continued to gather rocks and had a wonderful day in the park. They learned that it was better to play with rocks and not with things they didn't know. And they were happy that the tiny bird helped them stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Spot. He lived with a family who loved him very much. They played together, ate together, and slept together. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, the family went on a walk in the woods. Spot saw a big, dark hole. He knew it was not safe. So, he warned the family by barking loudly. They all stopped to see what was wrong. The family saw the big hole too. They thanked Spot for being so clever and helping them stay safe. They all went back home and had a big party to celebrate. Spot was a hero, and the family loved him even more.

Once upon a time there were two friends, John and Max. They were playing outside and it was starting to rain. John was very impatient and he wanted to go home. Max asked, “Where are you going?” John replied, “I want to go home before it rains too much.” Max said, “Let’s split, you can go home and I’ll stay.” John said, “Ok,” and he ran off quickly. But unfortunately, John was so impatient that he slipped on the wet ground and sprained his leg. Because he was in so much pain, he had to stay where he was until someone found him. Once the rain had stopped, Max came looking for John and found him in pain. Max called for help and soon John was taken back to his home where he could get better. Moral of the story: Patience is important - sometimes rushing can be dangerous.
