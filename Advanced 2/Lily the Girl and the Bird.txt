Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved to sleep and eat pears. She had a big pear tree in her yard. Every day, she would pick a pear and share it with her best friend, Max the cat. One sunny day, Lily and Max were sitting under the pear tree. Lily said, "Max, I am sleepy. Let's take a nap." Max meowed happily and they both lay down under the tree. The sun was warm and the grass was soft. Soon, they were both fast asleep. While they were sleeping, a little bird flew down and saw the two friends. The bird was hungry and wanted a pear. Lily and Max woke up and saw the bird. Lily said, "Hi bird, do you want a pear?" The bird chirped and nodded its head. Lily picked a pear from the tree and gave it to the bird. The bird was very happy and flew away with a full tummy. Lily and Max smiled, knowing they had made a new friend.

The dark night was starting to set in. The sun started to disappear in the sky and stars twinkled above. Little Sarah was alert as she watched the sky from her bedroom window. It was quiet, except for an occasional chirp from the birds and the rustling of leaves. Sarah stood there, wondering what would happen next. Suddenly, a light shone brightly in the sky. Sarah's eyes grew wide as a loud noise filled the air. Fireworks were shooting brightly in the sky. Sarah smiled as the fireworks lit up the night sky. The lights and noise united together to create a magical show in the sky. Little Sarah danced and twirled around, laughing as the beautiful fireworks wowed in the night sky. She was happy and alert, filled with joy and wonder as the fireworks united together to create the most beautiful scene she had ever seen.

Once upon a time, there was a big elephant. The elephant had a long trunk. He lived in a green forest. The forest was safe and nice. The elephant had many friends. One day, a big storm came. The wind was very strong. It started to destroy the trees. The elephant was scared. He wanted to make sure his friends were safe. The elephant used his strong trunk. He pushed the trees away from his friends. The storm went away. The forest was safe again. The elephant and his friends were happy. They played and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to visit the park. The park had many pretty flowers. She liked to look at them and smell them. One day, Sue saw a perfect flower. It was big and pink. She wanted to take it home. She asked her mom if she could pick the flower. Her mom said, "Yes, you can." Sue picked the perfect flower and took it home. She put it in a vase with water. Sue was very happy. She loved her perfect flower.

Once there was a little girl named Sid who loved to run. One day, she decided to race another girl, but she lost. Sid felt embarrassed by this. Her mummy hugged her and said, "It's ok, Sid. You're so brave for racing! That's your reward for doing something brave." Sid felt a little better. Mummy then said, "Let's play a harmless game. Can you think of one?" Sid thought for a minute and then smiled. "Let's hide-and-seek! I can hide and you can seek, Mummy," she said. They played and Sid had such fun! In the end, Mummy even gave her a sweet reward; a lolly. Sid was happy to get the reward and was no longer embarrassed.

Once upon a time, there was a normal boy named Tim. Tim liked to read big books with many pages. He had a cat named Cat. Tim and Cat were best friends. One day, Tim found a new book. This book was very special. It could help him gain more friends. Tim was very happy. He read the book with Cat. The book told Tim to share his toys with other kids. Tim did what the book said. Soon, many kids came to play with Tim. But Tim saw that Cat was sad. Cat missed playing with Tim. Tim learned that it's good to gain new friends, but he should not forget old friends. Tim played with Cat and the other kids. Everyone was happy again.
