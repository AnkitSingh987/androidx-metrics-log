Lily loved dinosaurs. She had many dinosaur toys and books. She wanted to see a real dinosaur one day. But her mom said dinosaurs were gone a long time ago. One day, Lily and her mom went to the park. Lily saw a big hill with a sign that said "Dinosaur Dig". She ran to the hill and saw some people digging with tools. They were looking for dinosaur bones. "Can I dig too?" Lily asked one of the people. "Sure, you can use this small shovel," the person said and gave Lily a shovel. "But be careful, the hill is harsh. It has rocks and dirt that can hurt you." Lily nodded and started to dig. She was very happy. She hoped to find a dinosaur bone. She dug and dug until she saw something white and hard. She touched it with her shovel. It felt like a bone. "I found a bone! I found a bone!" Lily shouted. She tried to pull the bone out of the hill. But it was too big and heavy. She could not manage to move it. "Mom, help me!" Lily called to her mom. Her mom came and saw the bone. "Wow, Lily, you found a dinosaur bone!" her mom said. "That's amazing! But it's too big for us to take home. Let's leave it here for the people to study. They will be very happy too." Lily was sad. She wanted to keep the bone. But she understood that it was important to share. She smiled and said, "OK, mom. Maybe they will let me see it again. And maybe I will find another dinosaur bone someday." Her mom hugged her and said, "I'm sure you will, Lily. You are a great dinosaur digger. I'm very proud of you." They thanked the people and left the hill. Lily looked back at the bone and waved goodbye. She felt happy and excited. She had seen a real dinosaur bone. And she had a story to tell.

Once upon a time, there was a long train. The train had many cars. One day, the train had to load a lot of toys for the kids in the town. The train was very happy to help. On the way to the town, the train saw a fire. The fire was big and scary. The train wanted to help put out the fire. So, the train went to the river and loaded water in its cars. The train was very fast and strong. It went back to the fire and helped put it out. The town was safe and the kids were happy. The train was a hero. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big stove. The stove was very special. It was the original stove in a small house. The house was in a big forest. In the house lived a nice lady. One day, the nice lady wanted to cook a yummy meal. She looked at the big stove and said, "I guess I can cook something good." She put a pot on the stove and started to cook. The smell was very good. Soon, all the animals in the forest came to the house. They wanted to eat the yummy meal too. The nice lady was happy to share. They all ate together and had a great time. The original stove made everyone happy.

Mia and Maria were best friends. They did everything together! They went to school together and would play together at their house too. One day, Mia had an idea. She said to Maria, "Let's plan to go swimming in the pool this weekend!" Maria thought it was a great idea and they both got very excited. Mia called her parents to ask if they could go swimming and they said yes. Mia and Maria started to plan their journey to the pool. Mia's mom was very helpful and told them what they would need to bring with them. She also said that she and Mia's dad would be waiting for them when they arrived. On the day of their trip Mia and Maria were so excited! They arrived at the pool and dressed in their swimming suits. They smiled to each other and jumped in the pool together. They played for hours, swimming and diving, and it was the best day ever. The next week, Mia and Maria were already planning their next swimming trip. They were so happy and thankful they had each other!
