Once upon a time, there was a happy zebra who lived in a meadow. The zebra was very healthy, with strong legs and bright eyes. Everyday, the zebra would go out in the meadow and dance. As it danced, its stripes glittered in the sunshine. The zebra loved to dance so much. It would dance, and dance, and dance! It would twirl and leap, and sometimes even stand on its hind legs. It was so fast and graceful, just like a ballet dancer. Every morning, the zebra was joined in the meadow by two little bunnies. The bunnies had seen the zebra's dancing and wanted to join in. So each day, the three friends would dance and laugh together in the meadow. It was a happy, healthy time. In the evenings, the three friends would have a big hug. Then they would say "Goodnight" and go their separate ways. But each morning, the three friends would come back to the meadow, and start dancing again.

Once upon a time, there was a tall tree in the middle of a beautiful green field. All of the animals in the forest liked to visit the tree and admire it's beauty. Every day, the tree would be alive with birds, butterflies and other animals from the forest. One special day, the tree bloomed brightly. All the animals noticed the tree's new bloom and jumped for joy. The birds and other animals flew around the tree and wiggled their legs in celebration. Suddenly, a squirrel appeared and said, "Stop! Please, don't be too fast. Take time to appreciate the beauty of the bloom and savor the moment!" All of the animals listened to the squirrel and stopped to admire the bloom. They all understood the moral: to take time to savor the special moments in life.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lizzy. She was only three years old but she was very proud. One day her dad took her to the park and told her there was a special surprise for her there. She was so excited she ran ahead of him and when she got to the gate of the park she saw a lock. She asked her dad what it was and he smiled and said “Now that’s part of the surprise my Lizzy.” Lizzy wanted to know more but all her dad said was “just stay here while I take care of the lock”. He bent down to give her a kiss before standing up to try and open the lock with a key. Lizzy watched and waited, and soon the lock clicked! Lizzy ran in and saw the surprise. It was a tiny puppy! The pup ran straight to lizzy and licked her hand. Lizzy was so proud. She hugged the pup and turned to her dad and kissed his cheek. He smiled and said, “Now you have someone to take care of and love like I take care of and love you”.

Once upon a time, there was a big billboard in a small town. The billboard was very normal. It had a picture of a cat and a dog on it. One day, a little girl named Amy walked by the billboard. She saw the cat and dog and said, "Hi, cat! Hi, dog!" She reached up and tried to rub the cat's head, but the billboard was too high. A nice man saw Amy trying to rub the cat's head. He said, "Do you need help?" Amy said, "Yes, please!" The man lifted Amy up so she could rub the cat's head. Then, Amy rubbed the dog's head too. Amy was very happy. She said, "Thank you!" to the man. The man smiled and said, "You're welcome!" From that day on, every time Amy walked by the billboard, she would wave and say "Hi!" to the cat and dog.

One day, a little girl named Mia found an unusual pink fruit in her garden. She had never seen this fruit before. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. "Look, Tom! I found a pink fruit. What is it?" Mia asked. "I don't know, Mia. It looks unusual. Maybe we can taste it?" Tom said. Mia took a small bite of the fruit. "Yuck! It tastes bad!" she said. Tom tried it too, and he didn't like it either. They both made funny faces because of the bad taste. Mia and Tom didn't want the unusual pink fruit in their garden, so they decided to throw it away. They put the fruit in a bag and threw it in the trash. After that, they went to play with their toys and forgot about the pink fruit. The next day, Mia's mom found the unusual pink fruit in the trash. She told Mia and Tom that it was a special fruit that needed to be cooked before eating. Mia and Tom learned that sometimes, things can be good if you know how to use them right.
