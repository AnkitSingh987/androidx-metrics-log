One day, a boy named Tim went to a cabin with his mom and dad. The cabin was small and cozy. Tim saw a big pot in the kitchen. He wanted to help his mom make soup. Tim asked, "Can I stir the soup, please?" His mom said, "Yes, you can help, but it is difficult. Be careful." Tim tried to stir the soup, but it was very hot. He was scared to touch the pot. He started to cry. His dad came in and saw Tim crying. He said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will help you." Tim and his dad stirred the soup together. It was not difficult anymore. They all had a nice dinner and Tim was happy.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to collect rocks. He loved rocks because they were shiny and fun to play with. While he was looking for rocks, he found a big piece of steel. Tim thought it was useful and decided to take it home. When he got home, Tim showed the steel to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found this useful steel!" he said. His mom looked at it and said, "Wow, Tim! That's great! Let's see what we can do with it." Tim and his mom tried to use the steel for many things. They tried to make it into a toy, but it was too heavy. They tried to use it as a chair, but it was too hard. Just then, Tim's dad came home and saw the steel. "Wow, that's a big piece of steel!" he said. "I have an idea!" Tim's dad took the steel and made a surprise for Tim. He turned the steel into a slide for the playground. Tim was so happy and couldn't believe it. He played on the slide all day long and thanked his dad for making something so fun and useful from the steel he found.

Once there was a naughty little bee who lived in a beautiful valley under the sunshine. He would fly around the valley everyday and one day he thought, "I'd like to be cheeky!". So he flew to a nearby flower and took a sip of its sweet nectar. Oh how he enjoyed it! Next, the bee flew to a tree and before long he was having fun buzzing around. But he was getting a bit too naughty and soon the other animals in the valley noticed. "Hey!", said the fox. "The bee is being very naughty. We must do something about it!" So all the animals in the valley chased the naughty bee until he was buzzing so high that he was out of sight. That's when they decided to take a break and they all sat down. The bee looked down from the sky and thought, "Well, maybe I should stay here in the sky and watch the valley from above. That way, I can be careful and not get too naughty." So, the bee flew up to the sky and watched the valley from above. He was never naughty again!

Once upon a time, there was a little fish who loved to swim. The fish saw a butterfly flying above him. The fish felt envious of the butterfly because it could fly high up in the sky. One day, the butterfly saw the sad fish and asked, "Why are you sad?" The fish said, "I want to fly like you." The butterfly said, "I will help you fly if you help me swim." The fish and the butterfly tried to help each other. The fish jumped out of the water and tried to fly, but he could not. The butterfly tried to swim in the water, but she could not. Then, something unexpected happened. A kind fairy saw them and said, "I will help you both." She waved her magic wand, and the fish became a butterfly, and the butterfly became a fish. Now, the fish could fly high in the sky, and the butterfly could swim in the water. They were both very happy and played together every day.

Once upon a time there was a big and enormous tree. Its leaves were so tall they almost touched the clouds. At the bottom of the tree sat a little girl. She loved to sit there every day and simply stare up into the blue sky. One day as the little girl was staring up, she saw something strange. It was a giant tea pot floating in the sky! She couldn’t believe her eyes. She had never seen anything like it before. The little girl wanted to believe it was real, but she didn’t want to get too close in case the tea pot came down crashing. She was scared. But then she thought, if it was real, she would never have this chance again. So, with courage in her heart, she ran towards the enormous tea pot and yelled, “Hello! Are you real?” Miraculously, in response, the tea pot opened and hot, delicious tea started pouring out! The little girl was so happy. She now knew she had to believe in the power of her dreams.
