Once upon a time, there was a big town. In the town, there was a house. The house was important. One day, a thief came to the town. The thief wanted to take things from the important house. The thief arrived at the house at night. He was very quiet. He opened the door and went inside. He saw many nice things. He wanted to take all of them. But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat came out of the dark. The cat was not scared of the thief. The cat went to the thief and started to play with him. The thief liked the cat. He didn't want to take things anymore. The thief and the cat became friends. The thief stayed in the town and never took things again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She liked to polish the floor every day. She worked really hard and did a great job. But one day, she hurt her finger. It started to hurt really bad and made her feel a lot of pain. She couldn't bear the pain so she decided to take a warm bath. The water was hot and she thought it would make her finger feel better. But that didn't help the pain at all. It kept hurting and she was really sad. So she never got to polish the floor ever again.

Once upon a time there was a brave, little girl. She had a shiny spear and was not scared of anything. One day, she decided to explore a mysterious forest. As she entered the forest, she felt a chill in the air. All of sudden, a scary monster appeared! The little girl was scared, but she remembered her spear. She bravely took out her spear and rubbed it on the monster. The monster immediately turned into a friendly, fluffy kitten! The little girl was so happy, she rubbed its head and took it home with her. The girl and her fluffy kitten are still friends today.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lisa. Lisa had a toy that could glow. She loved her toy very much. The toy had a big number on it. The number was three, just like Lisa's age. One day, Lisa's friend Tom came to play. Tom saw the glowing toy and wanted to play with it too. Lisa didn't want to share her toy. Tom said, "Please, Lisa, can I play with your glowing toy?" Lisa thought about it and said, "Okay, but only for a little bit." While they played, the toy broke. Tom was scared. He didn't want Lisa to be mad at him. But Tom was an honest boy. He said, "I'm sorry, Lisa. I broke your toy." Lisa was sad, but she saw that Tom was sorry. She said, "It's okay, Tom. We can fix it together." So, Lisa and Tom worked together and fixed the glowing toy with the number three on it. They learned that sharing and being honest are important. From that day, they became best friends and always shared their toys.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth stone. It was round and shiny. A little girl named Emily found the stone. She liked it a lot. She held it in her hand and stared at it all day. One day, Emily saw a bee. The bee was flying near a flower. Emily wanted to show the bee her smooth stone. She walked closer to the bee and stared at it. The bee stared back at her. Emily tried to give the bee her smooth stone. But the bee did not want the stone. The bee was scared. It stung Emily's hand. Emily cried and dropped the stone. The bee flew away. Emily's hand hurt a lot, and she lost her smooth stone. The end.

Once, there was a happy, enthusiastic girl. Her name was Lilli. She liked to go to the stable to see her friends, the ponies. One day, Lilli wanted to do something special. She had an idea; she wanted to lift all the ponies by herself. So, Lilli went to the stable and tried lifting all the ponies. She gave it her best try, but it was too hard for her. Her friends, the ponies, wanted to help, but they were too little. Just when Lilli was about to give up, a big, strong man came along. He asked her what she was doing. Lilli told him, and the man said, "Leave it to me!" and he lifted all the ponies. Lilli smiled. She was so happy that the man had helped her. All the ponies were excited too, and together they celebrated. From then on, Lilli was always very enthusiastic whenever she went to the stable.
