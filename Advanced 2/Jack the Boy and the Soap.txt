Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jack. He was very foolish, and always liked to play. One day, Jack's mom gave him some soap and a bathtub to spend the afternoon. She said, “Now, Jack, you need to spend the soap carefully, so you don’t use it all up. Do you understand me?” Jack nodded his head. He was very excited to spend the soap, so he ran off to the bathtub. When Jack finally got there, he used all the soap in one go. He had made a big, soapy mess! His mom shouted from the kitchen. “Jack, you were so foolish! You spent all the soap in one go!” Jack felt embarrassed and he said, “I am sorry, Mom. I won’t be foolish again.” His mom smiled at him and said, “That’s alright Jack. Now, let’s get some more soap so you can have a nice bath.”

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little spark. This spark was very curious and liked to question everything. It lived in a warm and cozy house with its family. One day, the spark went outside and saw something new. It was an icy pond. The spark had never seen ice before and had many questions about it. It wanted to know how it felt and what it could do. So, the spark jumped onto the icy pond to find answers. But, the spark didn't know that ice was very slippery. It slipped and fell into the icy pond. The spark was very cold and scared. It tried to get out, but it couldn't. The little spark was lost forever in the icy pond. The story has a bad ending, but it teaches us a very important lesson. Always be careful when trying new things and ask for help if you don't know what to do.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pretty blouse with many colors. Lily loved her blouse very much. One day, Lily went to the park to play with her friends. She left her interesting blouse on a bench. A bird saw the blouse and thought it was very pretty. The bird decided to steal the blouse and take it to its nest. Lily came back to the bench and saw that her blouse was gone. She was very sad. Then, she looked up and saw the bird with her blouse. Lily asked the bird to give her blouse back. The bird felt sorry and gave the blouse back to Lily. She was very happy and thanked the bird. From that day, Lily and the bird became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a furry, brown dog. His name was Fido and he loved to race. One day, Fido was keen to go racing. He ran to the nearest store and waited at the door. The storekeeper came out and said, "Sorry, Fido, no dogs allowed". Fido tried his best to explain, "But I just want to race!" The storekeeper shook his head, "No, no. No racing inside the store". Fido didn't give up. He thought hard and came up with an idea. He started to race around the store, going faster and faster. The storekeeper couldn't help but laugh and let Fido keep racing. Fido was so happy, he was still racing around the store by the time the sun went down.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom liked to build things with his toys. One day, he decided to build a big tower with his blocks. While Tom was building, his friend, the ugly duck, came to visit him. The ugly duck was not feeling well. He was sick and needed some medicine to feel better. Tom wanted to help his friend. He stopped building his tower and went to find medicine for the ugly duck. He gave the medicine to the duck, and soon the duck felt better. They were both happy and went back to building the tower together. And they had a great day playing and building.

One day, a boy named Tim wanted to play a sport. He liked to be healthy and have fun. Tim asked his mom, "Can I go out and play with my friends?" His mom said, "Yes, but first you must dress in your sport clothes." Tim put on his shirt, shorts, and shoes. He was ready to play. He ran outside to meet his friends. They started to play a game of ball. They were all having fun. Then, something unexpected happened. A big dog came running into the game. The dog picked up the ball in its mouth and ran away. The kids were sad because they could not play without the ball. They decided to follow the dog. The dog led them to a big park. There were many kids playing different sports in the park. Tim and his friends joined in and played all day. They had more fun than they ever imagined.
