Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to go on big trips. Sue was very adventurous. One day, Sue saw a big bookshelf in her room. She wanted to find a book to read. Sue looked at the books on the bookshelf. She picked up a book with a picture of a cat. As she opened the book, the cat jumped out! Sue was so surprised. The cat sighed and said, "Thank you for letting me out!" Sue and the cat became best friends. They went on many fun trips together. They had lots of adventures. But one day, they found out the cat was really a princess! The cat had to go back to her kingdom. Sue was sad, but she knew her friend had to go. They promised to meet again someday.

The sun shone very brightly in the park. Two children, Tony and Tara, were playing there. They laughed and giggled and shouted with excitement. Suddenly, Tony heard a distant whisper. It sounded like someone was leaking a secret. He was curious and wanted to know what was being said. He carefully crept closer so he could see who was whispering. He found two big kids at the far end of the park, speaking in low voices. Tony couldn't hear what they were saying, but he knew it was something he wasn't allowed. Tara saw Tony and hurried over. She asked him why he was listening to the big kids. He told her he wanted to know what their secret was, but the kids saw him and ran away. Tony was sad. He wanted to find out what the secret was. Tara told him that some secrets aren't meant to be shared. She gave him a hug and they went back to their game.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to clap her hands and dance. One day, Lily went to the park to play. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. But her mom said, "No, Lily, that is dangerous." Lily was sad, but she started to clap and dance under the tree. As she clapped, a big squirrel came down from the tree. The squirrel wanted to clap and dance too. Lily and the squirrel had fun playing together. Then, something unexpected happened. The squirrel found a big, red button on the ground. It pushed the button, and the tree turned into a slide! Lily and the squirrel were so happy. They took turns going down the slide, clapping and laughing together. The dangerous tree was now a fun slide for everyone to enjoy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a man who was very generous. He was giving away toys to all the kids. Tim's mom said, "Go say thank you to the man." Tim went to the man and said, "Thank you for the toy!" The man smiled and said, "You're welcome, little one." Later, Tim's mom saw a thief take a toy from the generous man. She told Tim, "I saw the thief take a toy. Let's go tell the man." When they told the man, he said, "Thank you for mentioning the thief. I will get the toy back." But then, something unexpected happened. The thief came back and said, "I'm sorry for taking the toy. I didn't have any toys when I was a kid. Can you forgive me?" The generous man said, "Yes, I forgive you. Let's be friends and give toys to kids together." And they did.

One day, a boy named Thomas went to the park. He saw a big tree and thought it was brilliant! He was very excited. So, he started to jog around it. He jogged around the tree again and again. He was so happy that he could jog. Thomas went around the tree faster and faster. He was out of breath, but he kept on going. He was loving the feeling of his body running around the tree. He slowed down and stopped after jogging around the tree 4 times. He smiled and patted the tree. He was so happy to have been able to jog around it. It was the best day ever.
