Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had many friends, but they all liked to be the best at everything. They were always competitive. Tim and his friends liked to learn about history. They liked to read books about old things that happened long ago. One day, Tim and his friends found a big, old book about history. They wanted to see who could read the fastest. They started to read the book, but they were not really understanding the stories inside. They were just trying to be the best and the fastest. Then, Tim's mom gave them some gum to chew. It made them slow down and enjoy the taste. Tim and his friends started to read the history book slowly, and they began to understand the stories. They stopped trying to be the best and just enjoyed learning. The moral of the story is that sometimes it is better to slow down and enjoy things, rather than trying to be the best all the time.

Once upon a time, there was a small puppy with a wagging tail. It lived in a big park where other animals were playing. But one day, the puppy felt a bit scared. It started to tremble. Suddenly, it heard a loud noise! The puppy looked up and saw a big, scary bird. It had a long yellow beak, an angry glare and a sour temper. The puppy was so scared that its tail started to wag faster. The big bird flew closer and closer as the puppy trembled with fear. It was so scared that it couldn't move! But then, something wonderful happened. The big bird flew away, leaving the puppy safe and sound. The puppy was so relieved that it wagged its tail even faster. From that day on, it was always brave and didn't tremble anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play and pretend. One day, Lucy put on a big, green coat. She said, "I am a big, green monster with green skin!" Her friend Tim came over to play. He saw Lucy in her big, green coat. Tim said, "Wow, you are an intelligent monster!" Lucy laughed and said, "Yes, I am! Let's pretend to be monsters together!" So Tim and Lucy played and pretended all day. They had so much fun being big, green monsters. They laughed and played until the sun went down. And they knew they were the best of friends.

One day, a girl named Lily and her mom went on a picnic. They found a quiet spot under a big tree. They sat down and opened their basket. Inside, there were yummy sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. As they ate, a squirrel came by. Lily and her mom watched the squirrel climb the tree. It jumped from branch to branch. Lily clapped her hands and said, "I applaud the squirrel! It is so good at climbing!" After the picnic, Lily and her mom walked home. They were happy they had a fun day together. They could not wait to go on another picnic and see more animals to applaud.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim liked to look at the moon. The moon was big and bright. It made the night mild and nice. Tim wanted to take the moon home. One night, Tim tried to steal the moon. He got a big net to catch it. He threw the net up high. The net fell down, but the moon was still in the sky. Tim was sad. Then, a wise old man came by. He told Tim that the moon is for everyone to share. We should not steal things, but enjoy them together. Tim learned that sharing is good. He never tried to steal the moon again. The town was happy and they all enjoyed the mild nights under the bright moon.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She saw a big tree with sweet peaches on it. Lucy was very excited. She wanted to eat a peach. But the peaches were too high for her to reach. Lucy saw a boy named Tom. She asked, "Can you help me get a peach?" Tom said, "Yes, I will help you." They tried to get the peach together. But they still could not reach it. A tall man saw Lucy and Tom. He asked, "Do you need help?" Lucy said, "Yes, please help us get a peach." The tall man got a peach for them. Lucy and Tom were very happy. They shared the sweet peach and became good friends.
