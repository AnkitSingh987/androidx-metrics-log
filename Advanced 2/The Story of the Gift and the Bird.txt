One day, a little green bug found a big gift. The bug was very happy. He wanted to open the gift right away. But his friend, a small red bird, told him to wait. "Delay opening the gift," said the red bird. "It will be more fun if we open it together later." The green bug agreed and they went to play. They played all day long. The sun began to set, and the green bug remembered the gift. He was excited to open it with his friend, the red bird. They went back to the gift and opened it together. Inside was a big, shiny ball. The green bug and the red bird played with the ball and had so much fun. They were happy they waited to open the gift together.

Lily and Ben were friends who liked to play on the shore. They liked to build sand castles, look for shells and splash in the water. One day, they found a big, shiny shell that was very original. It had many colors and shapes on it. "Wow, look at this shell!" Lily said. "It is so pretty and different. Can we share it?" Ben looked at the shell and wanted it for himself. He thought it was too special to share. He said, "No, I found it first. It is mine. You can find another shell." Lily felt sad and angry. She said, "That is not fair. We are friends. We should share things. You are being mean and selfish." Ben ignored Lily. He did not care about her feelings. He only cared about the shell. He put it in his pocket and ran away. Lily cried and followed Ben. She wanted to talk to him and make him change his mind. But Ben did not listen to her. He ran faster and faster, until he reached the end of the shore. There, he saw a big wave coming. He did not see the wave, because he was looking at the shell. He did not hear the wave, because he was ignoring Lily. He did not feel the wave, until it was too late. The wave crashed on Ben and knocked him down. He dropped the shell and it broke into pieces. He tried to get up, but the water was too strong. He screamed for help, but Lily was too far away. Lily saw what happened and was scared. She wanted to help Ben, but she did not know how. She ran to the nearest adult and told them what happened. They called for help, but it was too late. Ben was gone. He never saw Lily again. He never said sorry to her. He never shared the shell with her. He only had the shell for a moment, and then he lost it and everything else. He made a bad choice and had a bad ending.

Mia was running around the house, showing off her amazing new dress. As she twirled, the skirt of the dress spun around her. She felt so special. Mia's mom called out to her, "Mia! Come here please!" Mia rushed to the living room and was soon looking at her mom, who had a big surprise. "Look what I have!" said her mom, as she took out a box of makeup. It contained colorful eyeshadow, blush and lipsticks. Mia couldn't believe it. Mia asked, "What's that?" Her mom said cheerfully, "It's makeup! It will make you look even more beautiful!" Mia thought it was amazing and thanked her mom. But then she felt embarrassed after hearing her mom say she was already beautiful before. "You don't need makeup to be beautiful Mia. You are already amazing," her mom said as she hugged her. Mia smiled and hugged her mom back. She was so happy to know that she was already special and amazing, even before the makeup.

One day, a little girl named Mia went outside to play. It started to rain, so she put on her jacket. Her jacket was pink and had a graceful butterfly on it. Mia loved her jacket very much. Mia saw her friend, Tom, who lived next door. Tom was wearing his blue jacket. They both liked to play in the rain. Mia said, "Hi, Tom! Do you want to play with me in the rain?" Tom said, "Yes, Mia! Let's jump in the puddles!" Mia and Tom jumped in the puddles and laughed. The rain made the puddles bigger and more fun to play in. They were happy to have their jackets to keep them warm and dry. After playing in the rain, they went inside to have hot cocoa and cookies.
