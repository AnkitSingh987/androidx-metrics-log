Once there was a girl named Molly. She liked to be very silly and she liked to wear bright colours. Today she decided to wear her prettiest dress. It was bright pink with lots of ribbons. Molly went out to the street to get the mail. But today something strange happened. There was a big dog in the mailbox. He was so shy he wouldn't come out. Molly tried to coax him away but he wouldn't budge. Molly didn't know what to do. Then she had an idea. She slowly pulled something out from her pocket. "Do you like cookies?" she asked in a gentle voice. The dog slowly walked out from the mailbox. He happily ate the cookie Molly offered him. Molly smiled, happy that the dog was finally safe.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a quiz at school. She was very good at quizzes and everyone thought she was impressive. But today, she was worried because she forgets things sometimes. At school, her teacher, Mrs. Brown, gave the quiz to all the kids. Lucy looked at the quiz and started to feel scared. She forgot some of the answers! Her friend, Tommy, saw her and said, "Don't worry, Lucy. Just try your best." Lucy took a deep breath and tried to remember. She answered all the questions on the quiz. When Mrs. Brown gave back the quizzes, Lucy saw that she did well. She was so happy and her friends were proud of her. They said, "Good job, Lucy! You are impressive!"

One day, a little otter went to the river to play. He wanted to choose a toy to bring with him. He looked at his toy box, but it was empty. The otter felt sad. The otter's friend, a duck, saw him and asked, "Why are you sad, otter?" The otter said, "My toy box is empty. I have no toys to play with in the river." The duck had an idea. "Let's choose things from nature to play with!" They found sticks, leaves, and stones. They had a fun day playing in the river together. The otter was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a close family who loved to do incredible things together. They liked to play games, eat yummy food, and laugh a lot. There was a mommy, a daddy, and a little girl named Lily. One day, they went to a park close to their house. They played on the swings, the slide, and the seesaw. Then, they saw a big sign that said, "No playing ball." This was a new law in the park. Lily was sad because she loved to play ball. Lily had an idea. She asked her mommy and daddy if they could play ball in their backyard. They thought it was an incredible idea! So, they went home and played ball together. They had so much fun, even better than playing in the park. The new law in the park led them to find a new fun place to play ball, and they were happy together.

One day, a big red car was driving on the road. It went very fast and made a loud noise. The car wanted to pass the other cars. The driver was serious because he wanted to win the race. As the car went faster, the wheels went round and round. Smoke came out from the back of the car. The smoke was gray and it went up in the air. The car passed all the other cars on the road. At the end of the race, the big red car was the first one to pass the finish line. The driver was very happy and smiled. All the people clapped and cheered for the big red car. The car and the driver were the best.

Once upon a time, there was an enormous sunflower. It was so big, it could not stand up by itself. The sunflower was sad because it wanted to see the sun and dance with the wind. One day, a strong ant saw the sunflower and wanted to help. The ant tried to carry the sunflower, but it was too heavy. The ant called his friends, and they all came to help. They tried to carry the sunflower together, but it was still too heavy. Then, a kind bird saw the ants and the sunflower. The bird had an idea. It called all its bird friends, and they came with strong sticks. The ants and the birds worked together. They used the sticks to carry the enormous sunflower. The sunflower was happy to see the sun and dance with the wind. The ants and the birds became good friends.
