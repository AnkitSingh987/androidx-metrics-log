Once there was a famous dog named Max. Max liked to eat herbs. One day, Max found a new herb. He thought it would be very tasty. Max was very happy and excited to eat the herb. Max's friend, a cat named Lily, saw Max with the herb. Lily reminded Max that some herbs can be bad for dogs. Max did not know this, so he decided not to eat the herb. Max and Lily went to play with their ball. Later, Max and Lily found out that the herb was not bad for dogs. It was a magic herb! The herb could make dogs and cats talk like people. Max and Lily ate the magic herb and started talking like people. They became even more famous and everyone loved them.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a swing and wanted to play. The sun was shining, but the wind was harsh. Tim did not care. He sat on the swing and started to go back and forth. As Tim was swinging, a big cloud came. The cloud was very cold. It made everything freeze! Tim was scared. The swing stopped because it was frozen. Tim could not move. Then, a warm sunbeam came through the cloud. The sunbeam was like a hug. It made the ice melt. The swing was not frozen anymore. Tim could play again. He was very happy and kept swinging all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. Bella had colorful feathers and she loved to fly high in the sky. One day, Bella saw a big box arrive at the house below. She was curious to see what was inside. In the box, there was a bottle of lotion. The lotion smelled sweet like flowers. Bella watched as a little girl named Lily opened the box and took the lotion out. Lily put the lotion on her hands and rubbed them together. Her hands became soft and smooth. Bella wanted to try the lotion too. So, she flew down to the ground and landed next to Lily. Lily saw Bella and smiled. She gently put some lotion on Bella's feathers. Bella felt so happy and her feathers became soft like Lily's hands. They became friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. The palace was very pretty. In the palace lived a king and a queen. One day, a black cat came to the palace. The king and queen did not like the black cat. They said, "Go away, black cat!" The black cat did not go away. It stayed in the palace. The king and queen were mad. They tried to catch the black cat, but it was too fast. One day, the queen saw the black cat and ran after it. She did not see the water on the floor. The queen slipped on the water and fell down. She was hurt and cried. The king saw what happened and felt sad. He knew he should have cleaned the water. The black cat went away. The king and queen learned that they should keep the palace clean and not blame others for their own mistakes. They also learned to be kind to all animals, even if they don't like them.

One day, Tom went to play outside. He saw a big stack of smelly leaves. He thought it would be fun to jump in them. So, he ran and jumped into the stack. The leaves went everywhere! When he got up, he saw a small bug. The bug was stuck in the smelly leaves. Tom felt bad for the bug. He wanted to help it. So, he picked up the bug and put it on a green leaf. The bug was happy to be free. It looked at Tom and said, "Thank you!" Tom smiled and said, "You're welcome!" Then, he watched the bug walk away. Tom felt good for helping the bug. He went back to playing in the leaves, but made sure not to hurt any more bugs.

One day, a little boy named Timmy and his friend Sally went outside to play. They wanted to pretend they were in an enormous forest. Timmy said, "Let's be brave explorers!" Sally agreed and said, "Yes, let's find a big flame!" They walked around their backyard, pretending it was a big forest. They saw trees, flowers, and even a little squirrel. They laughed and played, looking for the enormous flame. As they played, Timmy and Sally saw a small fire in a fire pit. Timmy said, "Look, Sally! We found the flame!" They sat by the fire, feeling proud of their adventure. They pretended to be brave explorers, having found the enormous flame in the big forest. And they played happily until it was time to go inside for dinner.
