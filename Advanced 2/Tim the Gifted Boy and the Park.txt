Once upon a time, there was a gifted little boy named Tim. He loved to play with his toys and run in the park. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big tree with pretty leaves. He wanted to touch the leaves, so he tried to jump high. But he could not reach them. Tim felt sad. Then, a nice bird saw Tim and wanted to help. The bird flew up to the tree and dropped a leaf into Tim's hand. Tim looked at the leaf in his palm and smiled. The gifted boy and the bird became good friends, and they played together all day long.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jo. Jo was lively, always running and playing. One day, Jo went to the market to pick up some olives for her family. As she was looking for olives, she bumped into a boy named Tom. Jo was shy and scared, so she didn't say anything. Tom was kind and he waved. Jo looked at Tom, smiled and waved back. Suddenly, Jo got an idea - to share her olives with him. She asked "Do you want some olives?" Tom, thinking quickly, said, "No thank you, Jo. I already have some in my basket." Jo immediately regretted not saying something before. Tom reappeared the very next day and this time, Jo asked him before he could say no, "Do you want to come play?". Tom smiled and said yes. The two friends ran off together, laughing and playing as lively as ever.

Tom and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. They had a lot of toys, but their favorite was a big red comb. They used the comb to make funny hairstyles for themselves and their dolls. They had a lot of fun with the comb, but they also fought over it sometimes. One day, their mom told them to clean their room before dinner. She said they had to complete the task or they would not get any dessert. Tom and Lily did not want to miss dessert, so they started to put their toys away. But they both wanted to keep the comb for themselves. "Give me the comb, Lily!" Tom said. "It's my turn to use it!" "No, it's mine!" Lily said. "You had it for too long!" They pulled and tugged on the comb, but neither of them would let go. They got angry and started to yell at each other. Their mom heard them and came to their room. "What are you two doing?" she asked. "You are supposed to be cleaning, not fighting!" She saw the comb in their hands and took it away from them. She said they were being crazy and selfish. She said they did not deserve the comb or dessert. She put the comb on a high shelf and told them to finish their task. Tom and Lily were very sad and sorry. They wished they had shared the comb and listened to their mom. They tried to reach the comb, but it was too high. They could only look at it and cry. They had lost their favorite toy and their dessert. They had a bad ending.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He wore his favorite pants. He played with his ball and his toy car. He was very happy. Suddenly, Tim saw something in the sky. It was a big bird. The bird came down and took Tim's pants! Tim was very upset. He did not believe what just happened. Then, the bird came back. The bird dropped the pants on the ground. But, they were not Tim's pants! They were magic pants! Tim put on the magic pants and he could jump very high. Tim was not upset anymore. He was very happy and played all day.

Once upon a time there was a mommy, daddy and baby. They were a very happy family who loved being together. One day, mommy and daddy wanted to go out, so they asked baby to stay in and be a good baby. Baby said yes, but when the door shut behind mommy and daddy, it was too noisy. After a few minutes, baby started to cry. Baby wanted to go out too. So he went to the door and shouted: "Open the door!" But no one came to open the door for baby. So baby kept trying, banging on the door, and calling out for someone to open the door. But no one came. Baby got even more noisy, and soon the whole neighbourhood could hear his cries. But still no one came to open the door. Eventually, baby got tired and went to sleep. The next morning when mommy and daddy came home, the door was still shut and baby was still inside, all alone. The End.
