One day, a little boy named Tim woke up. He was confused because he could not find his toy car. He looked under his bed, in his toy box, and behind the couch. But the toy car was not there. Tim felt sad. Tim asked his mom for help. They looked everywhere in the house. Then, Tim remembered he played with the toy car outside. So, they went to the yard to look for it. Tim saw something shiny near a tree. He was sure it was his toy car. But when Tim got closer, he saw it was not his toy car. It was a toy robot with one leg! Tim took the robot inside and showed it to his mom. She smiled and said, "Let's fix the robot's leg." They found a leg from an old toy and fixed the robot. Now, Tim had a new friend to play with, and he was not sad anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little potato. This potato lived in a big garden with many other potatoes. They liked to play easy games like hide and seek. The little potato was very happy. One day, a big bird came to the garden. The bird saw the little potato and wanted to eat it. The little potato was scared and started to cry. The other potatoes heard the cry and came to help. The potatoes all worked together to chase the big bird away. After the bird was gone, the little potato stopped crying. They all went back to playing their easy games and were happy again. And the little potato knew that with friends by its side, there was no need to cry.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sue. Sue was a regular girl, just like you. She had a friend named Tom. Tom had a big thumb. One day, they went to the park to play. At the park, they saw a man. He made a sign with his hands. Sue and Tom did not know what the sign meant. They asked the man, "What is that sign?" The man said, "It is a sign for 'hello'." Sue and Tom learned the sign and said 'hello' to the man. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom's big thumb started to glow! The man was surprised and said, "Your thumb is magic!" From that day on, Sue and Tom used the sign for 'hello' and Tom's magic thumb to make new friends.

One day, a little caterpillar named Tim was very upset. He saw his friends wear pretty clothes, but he had none. Tim wanted to wear something nice too. He crawled under a big leaf and started to cry. A kind girl named Amy saw Tim the caterpillar crying. She asked, "Why are you upset, little caterpillar?" Tim said, "I want to wear pretty clothes like my friends, but I don't have any." Amy thought for a moment and smiled. Amy picked some colorful flowers and made a tiny hat for Tim. She put it on his head and said, "Now you can wear this pretty hat!" Tim stopped crying and smiled. He was so happy to wear his new hat. Tim thanked Amy, and they played together all day. Tim the caterpillar was not upset anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a lively dog named Spot. Spot loved to run, jump, and play with his friends. He also loved to drink water from his big, blue bowl. One sunny day, Spot was very thirsty after playing outside. Spot went inside his house to drink some water. But when he got to his bowl, he saw that his favorite cushion was in the way. The cushion was soft and fluffy, and Spot loved to sleep on it. He wanted to move the cushion so he could drink his water. Spot pushed and pulled the cushion with his paws. He tried very hard, and finally, the cushion moved away from his bowl. Spot was so happy! He drank lots of water from his big, blue bowl. After that, Spot laid down on his favorite cushion and took a nice, long nap.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a favorite cloth. It was soft and pretty. She took the cloth everywhere. One day, she saw a boy with an unknown cloth. It was a different color. Sue wanted that cloth too. Sue asked her mommy for the unknown cloth. Her mommy said, "You already have a cloth, Sue. You don't need another one." Sue was sad but listened to her mommy. The next day, Sue saw the boy again. He was crying because he lost his cloth. Sue felt bad for the boy. Sue went to the boy and gave him her favorite cloth. The boy was happy, and they became friends. Sue learned that it's nice to share and help others. She didn't need the unknown cloth after all.
