Once upon a time, in the sky, there was a big round planet. The planet was very sad. It did not have any friends to play with. One day, a small star came near the sad planet. The star said, "Hello, I am a star. Why are you so sad?" The planet said, "I am sad because I have no friends. Will you be my friend?" The star smiled and said, "Yes, I will be your friend!" And just like that, a new friend appeared for the sad planet. They played together and the planet was not sad anymore. From that day, the planet and the star were always together. They were very happy and the sky was full of joy.

Once upon a time, there was a little penguin. He lived in a cold place with his family. The penguin loved to talk to his friends. They would play and talk all day long. One day, the penguin wanted to talk to a new friend. He saw a big bird and said "Hi!" But the big bird did not talk back. The penguin felt sad and embarrassed. The penguin went back to his friends and told them what happened. They all said, "Don't worry, we still like to talk to you!" So, the penguin felt happy again and kept talking and playing with his friends.

Once upon a time, there was a painter. He loved to paint all day. He was very happy. One day, he saw a rich man. The rich man had a big house. The painter wanted to paint the big house. The painter asked the rich man, "Can I paint your big house?" The rich man said yes. The painter was very excited. He got his paint and brush. He started to paint the big house. As the painter was painting, he saw a bunny. The bunny could spring very high. The painter liked the bunny. The bunny liked the painter too. They became friends. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Fluffy saw his friends running fast. They had a lot of speed. Fluffy wanted to join them. "Can I join you?" Fluffy asked his friends. His friends said, "Yes, Fluffy, come play with us!" Fluffy was very happy to join his friends. They all ran and played together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took Fluffy's friends away. Fluffy tried to run with speed to catch them, but he was too chubby. Fluffy was very sad that he could not save his friends. The wind took them far away, and Fluffy was all alone.

Once upon a time, there was a big, red box. Inside the box was a pretty jewel. The jewel was locked in the box, and no one could see it. One day, a girl named Lily found the box. Lily was a creative girl. She loved to draw, paint, and make things. When she saw the box, she wanted to unlock it. She tried and tried, but she could not open it. Then, she had a good idea. She asked her friend, Tom, to help her. "Tom, can you help me unlock this box?" Lily asked. Tom said, "Yes, I will try." Tom was very strong. He pulled and pulled, and the box opened! Inside was the pretty jewel. Lily and Tom were so happy. They shared the jewel, and it made them feel creative and special.

Once there was an ant who was very special; it was pink. Every day it watched all the other ants, and wished it looked just like them. One day, something very special happened. All the other ants gathered around the pink ant and cheered. The pink ant felt really happy. Then something even more special happened. The pink ant started to change. Its colour became more and more like all the other ants. Soon it was black and brown, just like them. The ants were so happy that the pink ant was now like them that they threw a big party. They sang and danced together, and the pink ant was so happy. From that day on, the ant and all the other ants were best friends. They remained that way forever.
