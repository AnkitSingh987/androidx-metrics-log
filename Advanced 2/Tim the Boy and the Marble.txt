One day, a little boy named Tim found a shiny marble. He was very excited. He did not know who it belonged to. Tim showed the marble to his friend, Sam. Sam said, "I know that marble! It is mine!" Tim did not want to give the marble back to Sam. They both wanted to keep the marble. They began to argue. They did not know what to do. Then, their friend, Lily, came to help. She said, "You both can play with the marble together!" Tim and Sam agreed. They were excited to play with the marble and be friends again.

Once upon a time, a kind old man went for a walk on a hot summer day. He felt very hot, so he stopped at a wide tree and took a nap. When he woke up, he saw a big cardboard box. He was surprised! He opened the box and saw that there were many toys inside. He was so happy and decided to take them to the children in his village. The old man was excited to give the toys to the children and he was hoping to make them happy. On his way there, he suddenly remembered that he had to lead the group of children. He knew the children were too young to know which way to take and he had to show them the right way. The old man smiled, but he still kept going. When the group of children arrived at the other village, the old man was so proud. He knew that his effort had lead the children in the right direction. The old man went back home happily. The moral of the story is that if we lead by example, it will help us make the right decisions and lead us to the right path.

Once upon a time, there was a purple dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his ball. One day, while playing, his ball broke. Spot was very sad. He wanted to replace his ball, but he didn't know how. Spot went to his friend, a wise cat named Whiskers. Whiskers knew a lot about x-rays. Whiskers told Spot, "I can help you find a new ball with my x-ray." Spot was happy and said, "Please help me, Whiskers!" Whiskers used the x-ray to look for a new ball. They found one under a big tree. Spot was so happy to replace his broken ball. Now, Spot and Whiskers could play together again. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little monkey named Momo was very hungry. He looked for food everywhere. He found a big, yellow banana on the ground. Momo was so happy! He tried to snap the banana to eat it, but it was very hard. Momo felt frustrated because he could not snap the banana. He tried again and again. His hands were tired. Momo did not know what to do. He thought maybe if he climbed a tree and dropped the banana, it would snap. Momo climbed the tree with the banana. He went to the top and dropped the banana. It fell to the ground with a big noise. Momo climbed down fast to check the banana. But when he got there, he saw the banana was all smashed and not good to eat. Momo was very sad and still hungry.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to eat a pie. Her mommy made a big pie and put it high on the table. Lucy was too small to reach the pie. She saw her dog, Max, and had an idea. Lucy said, "Max, help me get the pie!" Max knew he should not, but he wanted to taste the pie too. They worked together. Lucy made Max crawl like a horse. She got on his back and they moved close to the table. Lucy reached for the pie and took it down. Both Lucy and Max ate the pie and it was yummy. Mommy came into the room and saw the empty plate. She looked at Lucy and Max. They both looked guilty. Mommy said, "Who ate the pie?" Lucy and Max did not say anything. Mommy was not happy, but she knew they learned their lesson. Next time, they would wait for mommy to share the pie.
