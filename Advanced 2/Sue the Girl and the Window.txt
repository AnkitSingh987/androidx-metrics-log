One day, a girl named Sue wanted to write a letter. She took her pen and paper and sat near the window. The sun was light and warm on her face. Sue began to write. She made a big loop on the paper. As she did, something unexpected happened. The loop jumped off the paper and became a real big loop! Sue was very surprised. She looked at the loop and thought, "What can I do with a big loop?" Then, she had an idea. Sue took the loop outside and played with it. She jumped in and out of the loop, and it made her very happy. In the end, Sue didn't write a letter. Instead, she had a fun day playing with her new big loop. And that was the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a team of animals. They liked to play games in the big park. One day, a new animal came. His name was Whistle the bird. Whistle wanted to play with the team, but he felt confused. He did not know how to play their games. Whistle went to the team and asked, "Can I play with you?" The team said, "Yes, come play with us!" They started to play a game called "Catch the Ball." Whistle was happy but still confused. He did not know how to catch the ball with his wings. One of the animals, a kind dog, saw Whistle's confused face. The dog said, "Don't worry, Whistle. I will teach you how to play." The dog showed Whistle how to use his beak to catch the ball. Whistle tried and tried, and soon he could catch the ball too. The team was happy to have Whistle play with them. They all had fun and played games together every day. Whistle was not confused anymore. He learned how to play with his new friends, and they all became the best team in the big park.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bell. It loved to ring all day. All the animals in the forest knew when the bell rang, it was time to eat. The bell was on the tip-top of a tall tree. One day, a little bird was flying by the tree. He saw the noisy bell and wanted to play with it. He knew if he could ring the bell, all the animals would come. So, he flew to the tip of the tree and waited. Soon, the time came for the bell to ring. The little bird was so excited. He flew up and rang the noisy bell. All the animals heard the bell and came running for their food. The little bird was so happy to help. From that day on, the little bird rang the bell every day and all the animals loved him for it.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, a rabbit and a mouse. They owned a beautiful orange planet. Every day they played together, feeling so happy and content. One day, the rabbit said, “Let’s go explore our planet!” The mouse replied, “Yes, let’s go!” So, they hopped around the orange planet, discovering new and wonderful things. They played tag, rolled down hills and even climbed up trees. At the end of the day, the mouse said to the rabbit, “Our planet is the best! I’m so glad we own it.” The rabbit laughed, “Me too! Now, let’s go home and get some rest!” And so, the two friends went home and fell asleep, dreaming of all the wonderful adventures they would have on their very own orange planet.

Once upon a time there was a fancy slide. It was made of brightly coloured plastic and it sparkled in the sunlight. Everyone wanted to have a go on it. One day it started to rain and some rain water trickled down the slide. It poured and poured until a huge puddle had formed at the bottom. Nobody wanted to go on the wet slide. Suddenly a little girl had an idea. She decided to pour oil on the slide to help the water slide away. However, this made the puddle even bigger and made the slide very slippery. Everyone was scared to go on it, but the little girl was brave and she slid down the slide. She looked very fancy as she glided down, that is until she couldn't control her speed and was flung off the end of the slide! The little girl was very sad, but she had learned an important lesson. Never pour oil on slides!
