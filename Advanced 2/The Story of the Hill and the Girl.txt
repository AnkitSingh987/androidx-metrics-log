Once upon a time, there was a little girl and a big hill. The little girl wanted to climb the hill, so she started to walk towards it. But when she got to the bottom of the hill, an angry man stopped her. The man said, "No! You cannot climb this hill!" and he was very angry. The little girl was scared, so she stayed at the bottom of the hill. She looked up at the hill, and it seemed so big. She wanted to climb the hill so badly, but the angry man wouldn't let her. The little girl was very sad, but she was too scared to do anything. Suddenly, a kind lady came to help. The kind lady was not angry and she said, "It's alright. You can climb the hill with me." The little girl was very happy, and they started to walk up the hill together. The hill seemed much less scary now, and soon they made it to the top. The little girl was so happy that she had made it to the top of the hill.

One day, a little boy named Tim went on a walk with his dog, Max. They went to the park to play with a ball. It was very easy for Max to catch the ball because he was fast. Tim and Max were having a lot of fun. Then, Tim saw a big flame in the park. He was scared and called out, "Help! Fire!" A nice man came running to help. He had a bag that included a big blanket to put out the fire. The man put the blanket on the flame and the fire went away. Tim said, "Thank you for helping us!" The man smiled and said, "You're welcome. I am a firefighter. It's my job to help when there is a fire." Tim was happy that the firefighter saved the day. From that day on, Tim wanted to be a firefighter when he grew up.

One day, a big whale appeared in the sea. The whale was angry. He swam fast and splashed water everywhere. All the fish and sea animals were scared. A little fish saw the angry whale and wanted to help. The little fish swam up to the whale and said, "Why are you angry, Mr. Whale?" The whale looked at the little fish and said, "I am angry because I am lost. I cannot find my way home." The little fish wanted to help the angry whale find his home. They swam together through the sea, asking other sea animals for help. After a while, they found the whale's family. The whale was happy to be home and was not angry anymore. The little fish and the whale became good friends.

Once upon a time there was a fisherman. He was very brave and he always wore a red shirt. Everyday, he would get in his boat and row away. One day, he was in a far away land, when he saw a big, red fish. He wanted to catch it so he started rowing towards it. He rowed and rowed until he got very close. But when he tossed his net in the water, the fish was too smart and it jumped away. Then a wise old owl said, “you have to be smarter than the fish if you want to catch it. Don’t just show your strength, use your brains.” The fisherman thought about it and he knew the old owl was right. So the next day, instead of rowing fast, he rowed slow and the fish got so curious that it swam right into the net. The moral of the story is that you don’t always have to be strong to get what you want. Sometimes you have to use your brain!

Once upon a time, in a jolly little house, there lived a jolly little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys and use her hands to make things. One sunny day, Lily went to the kitchen to get a drink of water from the faucet. As she turned the faucet, something unexpected happened. Instead of water, out came colorful bubbles! Lily was so surprised and happy. She started to play with the bubbles, laughing and clapping her hands. Then, Lily had an idea. She called her mom to show her the bubbles. But when her mom came and turned the faucet, only water came out. Lily was confused, but she just laughed and said, "Silly faucet!" And they both had a jolly time playing with the water.
