Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a map. The map showed him where to find a big, yummy cake. Tim wanted to serve the cake to his friends at his birthday party. Tim followed the map to a dark forest. He was frightened, but he knew he had to be brave to find the cake. He walked and walked until he found a small house. Inside the house, there was the big, yummy cake! Tim served the cake to his friends at his birthday party. Everyone was so happy and loved the cake. Tim was proud that he was brave and found the cake for his friends. They all had a great time at the party.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom was a curious boy who loved to discover new things. One day, he found an axe in the woods. It was shiny and sharp. Tom was very happy and showed the axe to his friends. His friends were envious because they did not have an axe like Tom's. Tom and his friends decided to play with the axe. They took turns chopping wood. Suddenly, the axe broke into two pieces! Tom and his friends were sad and scared. They did not know what to do. They thought they would be in trouble for breaking the axe. Tom decided to tell his dad what happened. His dad was not angry. Instead, he was proud of Tom for being honest. He told Tom and his friends that it was okay to make mistakes, but it was important to tell the truth. The friends learned a valuable lesson and promised to always be honest. From that day on, they were no longer envious of each other and shared everything they had.

Once upon a time, there was a big lake. Near the lake, a dog and a cat lived. The dog was nice, but the cat was selfish. The cat loved to play tricks on the dog. One day, the cat found a big bone. The cat wanted to play a trick on the dog. So, the cat told the dog to bury the bone by the lake. The dog was happy and went to bury the bone. The dog dug a deep hole by the lake. But the lake water started to fill the hole. The dog tried to get the bone out, but it was too late. The water took the bone away. The dog was sad, and the cat laughed. The cat's trick worked, but the dog lost the bone.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a yummy cookie on the table. He wanted to eat it, so he reached for it. Just then, a tiny star fell from the sky and landed on the cookie. Tim was very surprised. The star said, "Hi Tim! I am a magic star. If you let me stay on this cookie, I can make it even more yummy for you!" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, magic star. You can stay." Tim closed his eyes and counted to three. When he opened them, the magic star had grown big and bright. The cookie was now the most yummy cookie Tim had ever seen. He took a big bite and smiled. The magic star had made his day extra special.

Once upon a time, there was a little duck named Ducky. Ducky was very nervous to go to the big fountain in the park. All the other ducks went there to swim and play. Ducky wanted to join them, but he was scared. One day, Ducky decided to be brave and go to the fountain. When he got there, he saw all the other ducks splashing and having fun. Ducky started to panic. He was too scared to jump in the water. Then, Ducky's friend, Bunny, came to help. Bunny held Ducky's wing and together, they jumped into the fountain. Ducky was so happy! He splashed and played with all the other ducks. From that day on, Ducky was never nervous to go to the fountain again.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy telephone. He loved to play with it and pretend to talk to his friends. One day, Tim was feeling sad because he lost his favorite toy car. His mom saw him crying and said, "Don't worry, Tim. We will find your toy car." She gave him a big hug to comfort him. Tim felt better and decided to play with his toy telephone. As he was playing, the telephone started to ring! Tim was surprised because it was just a toy. He picked it up and heard a voice. "Hello, I am Mighty Mike! I heard you lost your toy car. I will help you find it!" Tim was so happy and thanked Mighty Mike. Mighty Mike told Tim where to look, and Tim found his toy car under his bed. Tim was amazed that his toy telephone had helped him. From that day on, Tim knew he had a mighty friend in his toy telephone.
