Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. It lived in a big forest. One day, the rock saw a long cable on the ground. The rock said, "What is this?" At night, something unexpected happened. The cable started to glow! The rock was very surprised. It said, "Wow! You can glow!" The glowing cable and the smooth rock became best friends. They played together in the forest every day. And every night, the cable would glow, and they would have fun. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a troubled pigeon. This pigeon was sad because he had no friends. He would fly around the park all day, looking for someone to play with. One sunny day, the pigeon saw a little girl playing with a ball. She saw the troubled pigeon and asked, "Do you want to play with me?" The pigeon was so happy to accept her offer. They played with the ball, and the pigeon was not sad anymore. After playing, the little girl and the pigeon became best friends. They would play in the park every day, and the troubled pigeon was no longer sad. The little girl had taught the pigeon how to accept love and friendship.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big garden with many different fruits. One day, Lily wanted to eat a melon. So, she went to the garden to search for one. Lily saw many different fruits in the garden. She saw red apples, yellow bananas, and green grapes. But she did not see any melons. Lily was sad, but she did not give up. She kept searching for the melon. Finally, Lily found a big, round melon hiding under some leaves. She was very happy! Lily picked the melon and took it to her mom. They cut the melon and shared it. It was the best melon they ever had.

Once upon a time, there was a big purple box. It had a bossy note on it. The note said, "Do not open!" But a little girl named Amy found the box in her room. One day, Amy's mom went out. Amy was alone at home. She looked at the box and thought, "I want to open it!" So, she opened the box. Inside, there was a big purple hat. She put it on her head and something unexpected happened. When Amy wore the hat, she became a bossy queen! She could tell everyone what to do. But then, she saw her mom come home. Amy was scared. She took off the hat and put it back in the box. The bossy queen was gone. Amy learned that being bossy was not fun. She was happy to be herself again.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy new clothes for Lily. The store was very busy with many people. Lily saw a pretty dress that she liked. She said, "Mom, can I try this dress on?" "Yes, you can," her mom said. Lily put on the dress and looked in the mirror. She felt happy in the dress. She wanted to become a princess in that dress. Lily's mom bought the dress for her. They went home, and Lily wore her new dress all day. She played with her toys and pretended to be a princess. She had a lot of fun in her new clothes.

Once upon a time, in a little cabin in the woods, there lived a modest old man named Bob. He had a big heart and liked to give things to others. One day, while walking in the woods, he saw a small, sad girl sitting on a rock. "Hi, little girl," said Bob. "Why are you sad?" "I lost my toy," the girl replied with tears in her eyes. "It was my favorite toy." Bob felt sorry for the girl and wanted to help her. So, he said, "Let's go to my cabin. I will give you a new toy." The girl's eyes lit up, and she followed Bob to his cabin. When they got there, the girl saw her favorite toy sitting on the table! She was so happy and couldn't believe her eyes. "Thank you, Bob!" she said, hugging her toy tightly. "You're the best!" Bob smiled and realized that sometimes, the best things we can give are the things we find along the way.
