Once upon a time there was a famous team of four. Each day they would go out together on a jolly adventure. On this day they decided to go to the park. When they arrived they spread out blankets and snacks on the grass. They relaxed and started to chew on the treats they brought. They laughed and talked and had fun in the warm sunshine. Then they noticed something moving in the grass. It was a tiny little bug. The kids were excited and started to cheer. One of them wanted to help the little bug, so they got a small bowl of water and put it in front of the bug. They watched with amazement as the bug happily chewed on the water and splashed around. They had such a great time at the park that day. The famous team will always remember this special moment where they helped a tiny little bug.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some grapes. Lily loved grapes because they were sweet and yummy. When they got to the store, Lily saw a big display of clean grapes. She was very excited. Lily said, "Mom, can we get the grapes from the display? They look so clean and good!" Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily. Let's get some grapes for you." They picked up a big bag of grapes from the display and took them home. Lily and her mom washed the grapes and put them in a bowl. They sat down and ate the grapes together. Lily was very happy and said, "These grapes are the best grapes I have ever had!"

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a healthy bunny named Bobo. Bobo loved to hop and play with his friends in the sunshine. One day, it started to get very cold and snow began to fall. The village started to freeze! Bobo and his friends didn't know what to do. They were so cold and couldn't play outside anymore. Bobo's mom said, "Bobo, you and your friends can play inside our warm house today." Bobo and his friends were happy and went inside to play. As they played, Bobo's mom told them a story about how it's important to help others when they are cold or sad. Bobo and his friends learned that being kind and helping others is what makes a village strong and happy. They promised to always help each other, no matter what. And when the sun came out again, they played in the warm village, remembering to always be there for their friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy judge named Mr. Bear. He lived in a big house in the woods. Mr. Bear liked to stir his soup every day. He was always very serious and never smiled. One sunny day, Mr. Bear was stirring his soup when he heard a knock on his door. He opened the door and saw a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and needed help to find her way home. Mr. Bear was not happy, but he decided to help her. As they walked through the woods, Mr. Bear started to feel less grumpy. Lily made him laugh and smile. They found Lily's house, and her mom was so happy. Mr. Bear was not grumpy anymore, and he learned that helping others made him feel good. From that day on, Mr. Bear became a kind and happy judge.

One day, a boy named Tim saw a big plane. He wanted to enter the plane and fly high in the sky. Tim asked his mom, "Can I enter the plane, please?" His mom said, "No, Tim. It might be hurt." Tim was sad but he still wanted to enter the plane. He saw a small door on the plane and went inside. Inside the plane, he met a nice bird. The bird was lost and wanted to go home. Tim said, "I will help you, bird." Tim and the bird tried to fly the plane. But, the plane did not go up. Instead, it went down and turned into a boat. Tim, the bird, and the plane were now on a river. They were all surprised! Tim and the bird became friends and they found the bird's home. Tim learned that sometimes things can be hurt, but good things can still happen.
