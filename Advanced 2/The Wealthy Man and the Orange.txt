Once upon a time, there was a wealthy man. He had a big house and many things. He also had a big orange tree in his yard. The tree had many big, round oranges. One day, the man went for a walk. He liked to wander near his house. As he walked, he saw a little girl. She was looking at the orange tree. The girl was hungry. The man saw this and felt sad. He wanted to help her. So, he picked an orange from the tree and gave it to the girl. The girl was very happy. She said, "Thank you!" and ate the orange. From that day on, the man and the girl became good friends. They would wander and play near the orange tree. They were both very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clever girl named Mary. She loved to play with her cardboard toys. One day, her mom said, "Mary, I want to introduce you to a new friend, Tom." Tom came over to play with Mary. They played with the cardboard toys together. But Tom wanted to play with Mary's favorite toy, a cardboard car. Mary did not want to share. "No, that's mine!" Mary said. Tom was sad. Mary's mom saw this and said, "Mary, you need to share with Tom." So, Mary shared her cardboard car with Tom. They played together and had fun. Mary and Tom became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Timmy. Timmy lived in a big tree with many branches. He liked to jump from branch to branch, singing happy songs. One day, Timmy saw a crazy squirrel running around the tree. "Hey, squirrel!" Timmy called out. "Why are you running like crazy?" The squirrel stopped and looked at Timmy. "I'm trying to find my nuts!" he said. "I hid them in the tree, but I can't find them now." Timmy wanted to help the squirrel. He thought and thought, and then he had an idea. "I can help you find your nuts!" he said. "I can see all the branches from up here!" So Timmy and the squirrel worked together. Timmy looked from branch to branch and the squirrel climbed up and down. They managed to find all the nuts the squirrel had hidden. The squirrel was so happy that he danced around the tree. "Thank you, Timmy!" he said. "You are a good friend!" From that day on, Timmy and the squirrel were best friends. They played in the tree every day, and the squirrel never ran like crazy again.

Little Bobby was a very curious 3 year old boy. He loved to explore and learn new things. One day, he discovered an old machine in his backyard. It confused him. What was it? He wanted to find out! So, he stretched his hands out towards the machine and was very careful when he touched it. A few drops of rain began to fall. Suddenly, the machine started to make a humming sound and it began to vibrate. Little Bobby was scared and he quickly stepped away from the machine. But then, the machine began to stretch out! Soon, the old machine had become a tall tower with two slides, a swing, a monkey bar and more. Little Bobby was excited! He realized it was a playground and he quickly started to play. He laughed and shouted as he moved from one ride to the other. Little Bobby had a lot of fun! He was so happy and relieved that he had discovered the playground. Little Bobby had no idea he would find such an amazing surprise!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a long street. On this street, there was a girl named Lily. Lily liked to help her mom and dad. She was a useful girl. One day, Lily found a big egg on the street. She picked it up and took it home. Lily and her mom lay the egg in a soft, warm place. They waited and waited. One day, the egg started to crack. A baby bird came out of the egg. Lily and her mom were so happy. They took care of the baby bird until it was big and strong. The bird grew up and became Lily's friend. They played together on the street every day. The bird was also useful, just like Lily. It helped her pick up things and clean the street. Lily learned that being useful and kind made her happy. The people in the town were proud of Lily and her bird. They all learned to be useful and kind too.
