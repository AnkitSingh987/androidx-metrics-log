Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to play. He wanted to learn how to use the equipment at the park. The park had a slide, a swing, and a seesaw. Tim was very excited to try them all. At the park, Tim met a girl named Sue. Sue was playing on the slide. "Hi," said Tim. "Can you teach me how to use the equipment?" Sue smiled and said, "Of course! Let's start with the slide." They went down the slide together, laughing and having fun. Next, they went to the swing. Tim was a little scared, but Sue helped him. They were swinging high when something unexpected happened. A bitter wind blew, and it started to snow! Tim and Sue were so surprised. They laughed and played in the snow, making snow angels and having a snowball fight. The park had become a winter wonderland, and they learned to have fun in the snow too.

Once upon a time, there was a big, wide igloo. In the igloo, there lived a nice boy named Tim and his little dog, Spot. Tim and Spot loved to play in the snow all day. They made snowballs and had fun. One day, while Tim and Spot were playing, they saw a big box. They opened the box and found many toys inside. Tim was so happy! He wanted to give the toys to all his friends. But, when Tim and Spot went to find their friends, they saw that everyone had their own big box of toys! It was a big surprise for everyone. They all played with their new toys and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a tiny house near the market. Every day, Kitty would go to the market to buy food for her family. One sunny day, while Kitty was at the market, she saw a little bird in a cage. The bird looked sad. Kitty asked the bird, "Why are you sad?" The bird replied, "I want to fly and be free, but I am stuck in this cage." Kitty wanted to help the bird. She thought of a plan to save the bird. Kitty found the key to the cage and opened it. The bird was very happy and said, "Thank you, Kitty, for saving me!" They became good friends and played together every day at the market.

Once there was a little girl called Tina. She was very small and had big brown eyes. One day she was walking in the garden, when she saw something tall. It was a pencil! She was excited, so she turned around and ran to pick it up. Tina was so happy when she held it tight in her hand. She decided to use it to draw a picture. She sat down on the grass and started to draw. When she was done, she looked up and saw the wooden fence around her house. She smiled, for the fence was now full of the pictures she had drawn with the pencil. Tina had a lot of fun that day. She never forgot the tall pencil that turned her picture day into a special one.

Once upon a time, there was a yellow bird. The bird was hungry, so it set out to find food. It flew high and low, looking for something to eat. One day, the bird found a sandwich. It looked so good! The bird picked up the sandwich and flew back to its nest. It set the sandwich down and got ready to eat. Just then, a little squirrel came by. The squirrel was hungry too. The bird saw the squirrel and decided to share the sandwich. They both ate the sandwich and were happy. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little mole. He lived in a hole under a big tree. The mole was very original because he liked to think a lot. He thought about the sun, the moon, and the stars. One day, the mole met a bird. The bird asked, "What do you think about all day, little mole?" The mole said, "I think about the sky and how big it is." The bird said, "I can show you the sky. Come with me!" So, the mole and the bird went on an adventure. They went up the tree and looked at the sky. The mole was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The tree started to shake. It was a big bear! The bear wanted to play with the mole and the bird. The mole and the bird were scared at first, but then they realized the bear was friendly. They all played together and had lots of fun. And from that day on, the mole, the bird, and the bear were the best of friends.
