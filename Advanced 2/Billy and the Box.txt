Billy was very excited to get his new paint set. He opened up the box and all the pots of paint were heavy! He couldn't wait to get started right away. However, his mum said that he would need to behave and wait until the weekend before he could start playing with the paint. At first, Billy felt disappointed, but he remembered that he had promised to be a good boy and listen to his mum. So he kept himself busy by playing with other toys until the weekend came. As soon as his mum said it was okay, Billy grabbed his paint set and started painting. It was so much fun! He painted all day, and some lovely pictures too. Billy was so proud of himself for behaving and being a good boy. He was happy that he could now paint and make beautiful pictures.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim went to school. He wore a uniform. It was blue and white. Tim felt happy in his uniform. One day, Tim saw a cat outside his classroom. The cat was alert. Tim gazed at the cat. The cat gazed back at Tim. They looked at each other for a long time. Tim said to the cat, "Hi, cat! Do you like my uniform?" The cat meowed and walked away. Tim felt happy. He made a new friend that day.

Once upon a time, there was a colorful dragon. He lived in a big cave. One day, the dragon was sleeping. A small boy named Tim came to the cave. He saw the dragon and wanted to play. Tim said, "Wake up, dragon! Let's play!" The dragon opened his eyes and looked at Tim. The dragon was not mad. He was happy to have a friend. Tim and the dragon played all day. They played with a big ball. The dragon used his tail to throw the ball. Tim laughed and clapped. The dragon was happy too. At the end of the day, Tim said, "Goodbye, dragon. Let's play again soon!" The dragon smiled and went back to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a dog called Spike. He was a white and tan colour and he always had a lot of energy. One day, Spike was playing in the garden when he found a rare bone. He was very excited and started to chew on the bone. Suddenly, a little girl called Emily appeared. Emily started to complain and said that the bone was hers. Spike looked sad and shook his head. Emily said, "That's my bone! Please give it back." Spike reluctantly put the bone down and Emily smiled. Emily thanked Spike and said, "I promise to give you a bone again, but this time it won't be so rare!" Spike happily nodded his head and ran off to play again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park with his mom. They saw a big, black dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play with the dog, so he asked his mom, "Can I play with the dog, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but be careful." Tim ran to the dog and grabbed the ball. He threw the ball far away. The black dog ran fast and caught the ball with its mouth. The dog brought the ball back to Tim, wagging its tail. As they played, the black dog licked Tim's face. Tim laughed and wiped his lip. He said, "You're a good dog!" They played until the sun went down. Tim and his mom went home, and Tim couldn't wait to play with the black dog again.

Once upon a time there lived a brave little girl named Dolly. She lived in a small forest with lots of animals. One sunny morning, Dolly wanted to take a walk. She saw a big, white goose and decided to follow it. The goose flew away and Dolly followed it. She was starting to get anxious as the goose was flying further and further away. “Where are you going?” Dolly yelled as she followed the goose. But the goose didn't answer. Dolly started to run faster and she decided it was time to catch up with the goose. Finally, she did and the anxious goose asked her, “Why are you following me?” Dolly smiled and replied, “I wanted to go on an adventure with you!" The goose was happy and they decided to go together. The two friends explored the forest and had fun playing together. Dolly was not anxious anymore and she had a wonderful time with her new friend, the goose. The end.
