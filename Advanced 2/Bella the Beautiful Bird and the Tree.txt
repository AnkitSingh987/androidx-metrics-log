Title: Bella the Beautiful Bird and the Tree

Once upon a time, there was a beautiful bird named Bella. She lived in a big tree with her family. Bella loved to fly high in the sky during the day. But at night, she would go back to her tree and sleep. One night, Bella met a new friend named Timmy. Timmy was a small bug who lived on the same tree. He said, "Hi Bella, do you like the night?" Bella nodded her head and said, "Yes, I do. It is quiet and nice." Timmy and Bella would play together every night. They would look at the stars and listen to the sounds of the night. Bella learned that the night was just as beautiful as the day. And they were happy friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a large bird named Bobo. Bobo had big wings and he loved to soar high in the sky. He liked to watch the little kids play in the park. One day, Bobo saw a pretty frame on the ground. The frame was shiny and had a picture of a happy family. Bobo wanted to give the frame back to the family, but he did not know where they were. Bobo flew and flew, holding the frame in his beak. He showed the frame to his animal friends and asked if they knew the family. Finally, a kind squirrel told him where the family lived. Bobo soared to their house and gave the frame back. The family was so happy and thankful to Bobo.

Once upon a time, in a big yard, there was a clever dog named Max. Max liked to play with his big red ball. One day, Max saw a big tire in the yard. Max had an idea. He wanted to jump through the tire. Max tried to jump through the tire, but he was scared. He asked his friend, the cat, for help. The cat said, "You can do it! Just take a big jump and go through the tire." Max thought about what the cat said and got ready to jump. Max took a big jump and went through the tire! He was so happy that he did it. Max learned that if you try and don't give up, you can do anything. The cat was proud of Max, and they played all day in the yard.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very careful boy. He always made sure his toys were put away and he was nice to his friends. One day, Tim had a bad dream, a nightmare. In the nightmare, a big monster wanted to spread Tim's toys all over the floor. Tim woke up and felt scared. He went to his mom and dad's room. "Mom, Dad, I had a nightmare," Tim said. His mom hugged him and said, "It's okay, Tim. It was just a dream. The monster can't spread your toys." In the morning, Tim's mom helped him make a plan. They decided that every night, Tim would be extra careful to put his toys away. That way, even if the monster came, there would be nothing for it to spread. Tim felt better and knew that being careful would keep the monster away. From then on, Tim never had that nightmare again.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Amy had a plan. She wanted to do something fun each day for a week. Amy was stubborn, so she knew she would do it. On Monday, Amy played with her ball. On Tuesday, she painted a picture. On Wednesday, she made a big tower with her blocks. On Thursday, she went to the park with her mom. On Friday, she played with her dog. Amy was very happy with her plan. But on Saturday, something unexpected happened. A big, funny clown came to Amy's house. The clown made her laugh and showed her new games. Amy forgot about her plan and played with the clown all day. She had so much fun that she didn't mind changing her plan. And that's how Amy learned that sometimes it's okay to change your plans when something new and fun comes along.
