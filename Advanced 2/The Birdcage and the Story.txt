Once upon a time, in a hidden place, there was a birdcage. Inside the birdcage, there was a little bird. The little bird was sad because it wanted to fly and play outside. One day, a girl named Lily found the hidden birdcage. She saw the sad little bird and said, "Why are you sad, little bird?" The little bird replied, "I am sad because I want to fly and play outside, but I am in this birdcage." Lily wanted to help the little bird. She opened the birdcage and let the little bird fly free. The little bird was so happy! It said, "Thank you, Lily! You are very kind." Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, little bird. Remember to always be kind to others, too." The little bird flew away and started to play with other birds. It learned the importance of being kind to others, just like Lily was kind to it. And they all lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: Always be kind and help others when they need it.

Once upon a time there were two brothers. They loved to play together and do fun things. One day at the farm, the brothers wanted to play hide and seek. So, one brother hid in the barn behind some hay. The other brother couldn't find him, so he started to stretch. His arms and legs stretched out to all corners of the barn. Finally, his eyes caught something and he saw his brother! He was hiding in the hay and it was getting kind of dirty. The brother had a great laugh and all the hay started to shake. They spent the whole day playing and having fun. When it was time to go, the brothers hugged each other and said goodbye. They knew they'd meet again soon, but until then they would have to stretch their imaginations to keep playing together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a bossy cat named Tom. Tom liked to tell everyone what to do. One day, Tom saw a little mouse named Jerry near the exit of a store. Tom wanted to catch Jerry, but Jerry was too fast. Tom thought of a plan to catch Jerry. He asked a big dog named Max to help him. Max was not bossy like Tom, but he wanted to be friends. Tom tried to explain his plan to Max, but Max did not like the plan. Max told Tom that it was not nice to be bossy and catch little mice like Jerry. Tom felt sad and thought about what Max said. He decided to stop being bossy and try to be friends with Jerry. Tom went to Jerry and said sorry. Jerry forgave Tom and they became friends. From that day, Tom learned that being nice and kind was much better than being bossy.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very eager to go to her class. She loved learning new things with her friends. Her mom said, "Sue, today you will learn something new and fun!" In class, the teacher told the kids that they were going to play a game called "Resist the Treat." The teacher put a yummy cookie on each kid's table. She said, "You must not eat the cookie until I say so." Sue and her friends looked at the cookies and tried to resist eating them. Sue's friend, Tim, could not resist and ate his cookie. The teacher said, "Oh no, Tim! You did not resist the treat!" Soon, the teacher said, "Now, you can eat the cookies!" Sue and her friends were very happy. They ate their cookies and learned how to resist.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom liked to carry his toy bear everywhere he went. One day, Tom went to the park with his mom and his bear. At the park, Tom met a girl named Sue. Sue asked Tom, "Can I play with your bear?" Tom said, "Yes, you can play with my bear." Sue and Tom played together and had a lot of fun. But then, Tom's bear got lost. Tom and Sue looked everywhere for the bear. They felt guilty because they lost the bear. Finally, they found the bear under a tree. Tom and Sue were happy again, and they learned to take better care of their toys.
