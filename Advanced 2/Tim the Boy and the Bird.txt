One day, a little boy named Tim walked in the woods. He saw a big pine tree. The tree was very big and tall. Tim was hungry, so he looked for something tasty to eat. Tim found a big, red apple under the pine tree. He picked it up and said, "This looks tasty!" Just then, a small bird flew down and said, "Please don't eat that apple. It's mine!" Tim did not want to complain, so he gave the apple to the bird. Tim kept walking and found a tasty cake. He was so happy. He started to eat the cake, but the bird came back. The bird said, "That cake is not good for you!" Tim did not listen to the bird. He ate the cake, and it made him feel sick. The bird was right, and Tim was sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max lived in a house with a family. They were nice to Max. Max liked to help them. One day, Max saw the dishwasher. It was open and full of dishes. Max wanted to help. Max jumped high to reach the dishwasher. He used his mouth to take out the dishes. Max led the family to the clean dishes. They were happy that Max helped. They gave Max a treat for being a good dog. Now, every day, Max helps with the dishwasher. He jumps high and leads the family to the clean dishes. They all love Max very much. And Max loves them too. They are a happy family.

Once upon a time, there was a little star who wanted to explore the sky. So one day, the star decided to start his journey. He flew around the sky and eventually he spotted a beautiful comet in the distance. The comet was so majestic, the star couldn't resist getting closer and closer. Suddenly, the comet noticed the star, and it started to make its way towards him. The star was overwhelmed with joy and spread out its wings. However, when it noticed that the comet was coming closer and closer, the star got very embarrassed. The star tried to hide, but it was too late; the comet had seen him. The star was so embarrassed, but the comet reassured him by saying, "It's perfectly all right. I am here to be your friend." The star was so relieved. From that day forward, the star and the comet were good friends and explored the sky together.

One day, a little girl named Amy was nervous. She had lost her brush. She looked everywhere for it. She looked under the bed, in the toy box, and even behind the door. But she could not find it. Amy asked her friend Tom, "Have you seen my brush?" Tom said, "No, but I hope you find it." They looked for the brush together. They looked in the garden, in the kitchen, and even in the big tree. But they could not find it. Then, they saw a big dog. The dog had the brush in its mouth. They tried to get the brush back, but the dog ran away. They could not catch it. Amy and Tom were sad. They had lost the brush forever. The moral of the story is: always put your things away in their right place, or you might lose them.

Once upon a time, in a land of cold and snow, there was a winter. This winter was harsh. The wind blew strong, and the snow piled up high. All the animals in the forest had to find ways to stay warm and safe. One day, a little bird named Timmy was trying to find food. Timmy was very cold and hungry. He began to complain to his friends, "I don't like this harsh winter. It's too cold, and I can't find any food." His friends, a squirrel named Sally and a rabbit named Bobby, agreed with Timmy. Sally, Bobby, and Timmy decided to work together to find food and stay warm. They found a cozy cave and shared their food with each other. The friends learned that even though the harsh winter was hard, they could be happy if they helped each other.
