One day, a good cat named Sam found a tube in his house. He looked at it and thought, "What can I do with this tube?" Sam decided to play with the tube. He put his paw in it and felt something tickle his nose. Sam said, "I think I am going to sneeze!" And then, he sneezed very loud. The tube made a funny sound when Sam sneezed. He laughed and played with the tube some more. Later, Sam's friend, a dog named Max, came over to play. Max saw the tube and said, "What is this?" Sam told Max about his sneeze and the funny sound. Max wanted to try too. Max sneezed into the tube, but instead of a funny sound, a little bird flew out! Sam and Max were very surprised. They laughed and played with the bird and the tube all day.

Once upon a time, there were two boys who loved to play hockey. Every day, the boys would go outside with their hockey stick and puck and play in the sunshine. One day, the boys decided to play a game of catch. The boys took turns throwing the hockey puck back and forth. It felt soft as it sailed through the air! The boys started running to catch the puck and have so much fun! Back and forth the boys threw the puck, giggling and smiling as they tried to catch it! They were so happy and enjoyed their game. Suddenly, one of the boys got the puck in his hand and shouted, "I caught it!" The other boy clapped and cheered, happy that his friend had caught the hockey puck. The two boys went back inside, smiling and happy after their game of catch.

Once upon a time, in a small house, lived a boy named Tim. Tim had a tight blue shirt that he loved to wear every day. One day, while playing outside, he saw a big cat. The cat was not nice. It was a threat to the small birds in the garden. Tim wanted to help the birds. He thought of a plan to gain the cat's friendship. He went inside the house and took some fish from the kitchen. He gave the fish to the cat. The cat liked the fish and started to like Tim too. Now, the cat was not a threat to the birds anymore. Tim and the cat became good friends. They played together every day. The birds were happy, and they sang beautiful songs. Tim's tight blue shirt became a symbol of friendship and peace in the garden.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a happy little ant named Andy. Andy loved to dance more than anything. Every day, he would do his original dance and make all his friends smile. One day, as Andy was dancing, he saw a big rock in his way. He thought, "I can use this rock in my dance!" So, he climbed up the rock and started to dance on top of it. But suddenly, he felt a pinch on his foot. "Ouch!" he cried out. Andy looked down and saw a little crab named Carl. Carl said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to pinch you. I just wanted to join your dance!" Andy smiled and said, "That's okay, let's dance together!" So Andy and Carl danced on the rock, making everyone in the forest happy. And from that day on, Andy and Carl became the best of friends, always dancing and making others smile.

One day, two friends, Ben and Craig, were playing together in Ben's bedroom. It had great toys and a nice, soft floor. Ben found something special in the corner of the room – an old treasure chest! Both boys got excited and opened the chest together. But when Craig touched the chest, it started to tremble! Craig stepped back in surprise. "What's going on?" he asked Ben. Ben smiled and said, "Don't worry, it won't hurt us." Ben knew what was in the chest – it was full of great candy! Craig remember that Ben said the chest would tremble when opened, and he began to smile. "Let's see what's inside!" he exclaimed. Ben opened the chest and inside there was a pile of colourful sweets. Craig couldn't believe his eyes. He grabbed a handful of candy and went to the floor – it was so soft and comfortable! Then he lay down on the floor and started enjoying the treat. The two boys had a great time with the candy. Craig learned a lesson that day – you never know what surprises could be hidden in an old treasure chest. And sometimes, when danger seems close, it's really just a sign of something great!
