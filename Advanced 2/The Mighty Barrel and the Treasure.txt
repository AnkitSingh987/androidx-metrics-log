Once upon a time, there was a mighty barrel. It was very big and made of wood. Tom was a brave little boy. He wanted to explore the mysterious barrel. He poked it and it sent a loud rumble throughout the room. Tom was scared and confused. Just then, an old wise man appeared in the corner of the room. He had a big white beard and a long robe. The old wise man said to Tom, "Do not be afraid. I know why this barrel will not open. You must release the lock on the barrel!" Tom was so excited. He couldn't believe that he could open the barrel. He grabbed the lock and released it with all his might. The barrel opened with a loud creak. Tom was overjoyed! Inside the barrel were all sorts of shiny pieces of treasure. He quickly grabbed the treasure and ran away with it, feeling happy and rich.

Once upon a time, in a big park, there was a gentle dog named Max. Max liked to play with a long rope. One day, Max saw a big box in the park. Max was curious and wanted to enter the big box. Max went near the big box and left his rope outside. Max entered the box, but the door closed behind him. Max was scared and wanted to get out. He did not know what to do. Then, Max remembered his rope. Max put his paw through a small hole in the door and pulled the rope. The rope helped Max open the door. Max was happy and learned that his rope was not just for play, but could also help him when he needed it.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little house, lived a kind lady named Sue. She loved to store lots of tea in her kitchen. Sue had many friends who liked to visit her for a tea party. One sunny day, Sue invited her friends over for a tea party. They all sat in her cozy garden, drinking tea and eating yummy cookies. They talked and laughed, having a great time together. As they enjoyed their tea, a small bird flew down and sat on the fence. It looked sad and hungry. Sue's friends wanted to shoo it away, but Sue said, "No, let's share our tea and cookies with the bird." So, they gave the bird some crumbs and a little cup of water. The bird was very happy and sang a beautiful song for them. Sue's friends learned that sharing with others can bring joy and happiness. From that day on, they all became kinder and shared their things with others, making the world a more cheerful place.

One day, a boy named Tim went to school. It was a hot day, and he was very sweaty. In his class, there was a big blackboard. The teacher wrote on it with white chalk. Tim liked to watch the teacher write. At lunch time, Tim wanted to play with his friends. But he forgot his lunch in the class. So, he went back to get it. When he got to the door, it was locked. Tim was sad because he was hungry. Tim tried to open the door, but it was still locked. He asked his friends to help, but they could not open it too. Tim was very sad and hungry. He did not get his lunch that day. The day ended bad for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to go to the theater with her mom and dad. One day, they went to see a show with a big, ugly monster in it. During the show, the ugly monster saw Lily and came down from the stage. Everyone was scared, but Lily was brave. She walked up to the monster and kneeled down in front of him. The monster kneeled down too, and they looked at each other. Suddenly, the ugly monster started to laugh and dance. It turned out that he was not scary at all. He was just lonely and wanted a friend. Lily and the monster became best friends, and they played together in the theater every day.
