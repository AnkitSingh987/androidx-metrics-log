One day, a little boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a tap near a big tree. Tim was thirsty, so he went to the tap to drink some water. But when he tried the water, it was bitter. Tim did not like the bitter water, so he shouted, "Yuck!" A little girl named Sue heard Tim's shout. She ran over to him and asked, "Why did you shout?" Tim told her about the bitter water from the tap. Sue said, "I know where to find sweet water. Follow me!" Sue took Tim to another tap near the swings. They both drank the water and it was not bitter. Tim and Sue were happy. They played in the park all day and drank sweet water when they were thirsty. They became good friends and always played together.

Once upon a time, there was a shy little ball named Bouncy. Bouncy was very good at one thing: bounce, bounce, bounce! Bouncy lived in a quiet room where there was a lot of silence. Bouncy would bounce all day long, but Bouncy was lonely. One day, while Bouncy was bouncing around the room, something unexpected happened. Bouncy found a secret door in the wall! Bouncy was very excited and went through the door. On the other side, Bouncy found a big, bright room full of other balls just like Bouncy! Bouncy was so happy to have new friends to bounce with. Bouncy was not shy anymore. All the balls would bounce, bounce, bounce together and there was no more silence. The room was full of laughter and fun. Bouncy and the other balls lived happily ever after, and they all loved to bounce together.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a pair of cheap sunglasses. He loved to wear them when the sun was bright. Tim's sunglasses were red and shiny. One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He wanted to hold his sunglasses instead of wearing them. Tim held his sunglasses tightly in his hand as he played. He ran, jumped, and laughed with his friends. While playing, Tim dropped his sunglasses on the ground. He felt sad, but his mom said, "Don't worry, we can get you new sunglasses." Tim smiled, and they went to the store to find new sunglasses. Tim picked out a blue pair, and he was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a strong ant named Andy. He liked to carry things on his back. One day, he saw a big buckle on the ground. It was shiny and pretty. Andy wanted to carry the buckle to his home. He put it on his back and started to walk. It was very heavy, but Andy was strong. He walked and walked, and he did not give up. At last, Andy reached his home. All his friends were happy to see the shiny buckle. They knew Andy was strong and could carry anything. They all cheered and clapped for him. Andy felt proud and happy.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a big mountain. Many people liked to go to the mountain to play and have fun. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to go to the mountain too. Tim asked his mom if he could go. She said yes, but he must be patient and wait for his dad. So, Tim waited and waited. He was very patient. When his dad was ready, they went to the mountain together. They had a great time at the mountain. They played and laughed all day. Tim was happy that he was patient and waited for his dad. They went home tired but with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a big, intelligent cat named Tom was walking in the woods. He saw a little bird in a tree. The bird was singing a happy song. Tom wanted to talk to the bird, so he climbed up the tree. "Hello, bird," said Tom. "I like your song. But please don't disturb my nap." The bird looked at Tom and said, "Okay, I will be quiet." Tom started to take a nap on the tree branch. But suddenly, the branch broke! Tom and the bird had a big fall. They fell down, down, down to the ground. When they hit the ground, Tom was very sad. He could not walk anymore. The bird was sad too, because it could not fly. They both learned that sometimes, even when you try to be nice, bad things can happen.
