Once upon a time, there was a messy room. Toys were everywhere. A little boy named Tim lived in the house. Tim loved to play with his toys, but he did not like to clean up. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, it's time to clean your room." Tim started to search for his toy box. He looked under the bed, behind the door, and even in the closet. He found his toy box behind a big chair. Tim began to put his toys away. He picked up his cars, his blocks, and his teddy bear. As Tim cleaned, he found a door with a knob. Tim had never seen this door before. He turned the knob and opened the door. Inside, there was a secret playroom. It was clean and had lots of new toys. Tim was so happy! He promised to keep his room clean so he could play in the secret playroom.

Once upon a time, there was a modest little girl named Lily. She liked to play in her yard every day. One sunny day, she went outside to play with her ball. While playing, Lily saw a small bug. The bug was crawling on the grass. Lily was very excited to discover the bug. She watched the bug as it moved around the yard. Lily decided to show the bug to her mom. She carefully picked up the bug and brought it inside. Her mom smiled and told Lily that the bug was a ladybug. Lily was happy to learn about the ladybug and let it go back to its home outside.

One day, a wise cat named Wednesday went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Up she went, higher and higher, until she reached the top. She looked around and saw her friend, a little bird, sitting on a branch. "Hi, bird!" said Wednesday. "What are you doing up here?" The bird looked at her and said, "I am looking for water to drink. I am very thirsty." Wednesday thought for a moment and said, "I can help you find water." Just then, something unexpected happened. A big cloud came and started to spray water on them. They were both surprised, but happy because the bird could now drink the water. They laughed and played in the rain, enjoying the cool water. Soon, the rain stopped, and they climbed down the tree. They were both very wet but very happy. "Thank you, Wednesday," said the bird. "You are a very wise and good friend." Wednesday smiled and said, "You're welcome, bird. I am always here to help you." And they both went on their way, knowing they would always be friends.

One day, a large cat put on a costume. The costume was very funny. The cat looked like a big, big bird. The cat went for a walk in the park. The cat saw a little girl and wanted to play with her. The little girl saw the cat and did not know it was a cat. She thought it was a big bird. The girl was happy to play with the big bird. They played and laughed together. The cat blinked its eyes and the girl blinked back. Then, something unexpected happened. The costume fell off the cat. The little girl was surprised. She did not know it was a cat all this time. The cat was happy too. Now, they could play together without the costume. They played and laughed more, and the cat and the girl became best friends.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play together. But sometimes they also liked to poke each other and make each other angry. One Sunday, they were playing with their blocks in the living room. Anna wanted to build a tall tower, but Ben wanted to knock it down. "Stop it, Ben!" Anna said. "I want to make a tower!" "No, I want to make a crash!" Ben said. He poked Anna's tower with his finger and it fell down. Anna was frustrated. She poked Ben back and he cried. "Mommy, Anna poked me!" he shouted. Mommy came from the kitchen and saw the mess. She was not happy. She picked up Ben and hugged him. Then she looked at Anna and said, "Anna, why did you poke your brother?" "Because he poked my tower!" Anna said. Mommy sighed. She put Ben down and said, "Both of you, listen to me. Poking is not nice. It hurts and it makes people sad. You need to share your toys and play nicely. Do you understand?" Anna and Ben nodded. They said they were sorry to each other and hugged. Then they decided to build a tower together, without poking. Mommy smiled and gave them a kiss. She was glad they were friends again.
