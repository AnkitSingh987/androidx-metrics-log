Once upon a time, there was an elderly cat named Lucy. Lucy had a diary where she wrote about her days. She liked to sit in the sun and write in her diary with her paw. One day, Lucy met a nice dog named Jack. Jack was very kind and always played with Lucy. They became best friends. Lucy wrote about Jack in her diary every day. After some time, Lucy and Jack decided to marry. They had a big party with all their friends. They played games, ate yummy food, and danced. From that day on, Lucy wrote happy stories about her life with Jack in her diary.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an old toy named Mr. Bear. Tim loved Mr. Bear, but he was worried because Mr. Bear was very old and not as fun as the new toys. One day, Tim saw a new toy in the store. He wanted the new toy very much. He asked his mom, "Can I have the new toy, please?" His mom said, "You have Mr. Bear, and he is still good." Tim was sad, but he took Mr. Bear home. That night, Tim was playing with Mr. Bear. He noticed that Mr. Bear had a secret pocket. Inside the pocket, Tim found a key. Tim was very excited. He remembered there was an old box in his room. He used the key to open the box. Inside the box, there were many fun toys and games. Tim was so happy, and he knew that Mr. Bear was the best toy ever.

Once upon a time, there was a busy little bee buzzing around. The bee was looking for nectar and could not understand why the flowers had no nectar left. The bee flew to a nearby stream and met a friendly frog. "Hello!" said the frog. "Hi!" the bee replied. The frog told the bee that the only way to restore the life of the flowers was to add some water to the stream. The bee was excited and flew quickly back to the flowers. But as soon as the bee added the water, it seemed as though it had done too much. The flowers were suddenly drenched with water and their vibrant colours were gone. The bee was sad, and slowly flew away. It was too late to restore the life of the flowers, and the bee realised it had been too busy to think things through.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a weird frog. The frog had a big, round belly and long, wavy legs. He loved to hop around and play all day. One day, the frog met a tiny bird. The bird said, "Hello, Mr. Frog! Why do you look so weird?" The frog replied, "I don't know, but I like being weird. It makes me special!" The bird liked the frog and wanted to be his friend. They played together every day. But one day, the bird had to leave the hut to find food for her family. The frog was sad but understood. The frog waited for the bird to come back. He missed his friend very much. After a few days, the bird returned, and they were happy to play together again. The weird frog and the tiny bird stayed best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She had a big, black cat named Luna. Sally and Luna were best friends. They played together every day. One day, Sally and Luna were playing hide and scare. Luna hid behind a tree and waited for Sally to find her. When Sally walked by, Luna jumped out and said, "Boo!" Sally laughed and said, "Luna, you scared me! Now it's my turn to hide." Sally hid behind a bush and waited for Luna to find her. When Luna walked by, Sally jumped out and said, "Boo!" Luna jumped and then laughed. They played hide and scare all day long. At the end of the day, Sally and Luna were tired. They sat down under a tree and talked. Sally said, "Luna, we had so much fun today. I'm glad we can laugh when we scare each other." Luna agreed, and they both learned that it's okay to be scared sometimes, as long as you can laugh about it with a friend.
