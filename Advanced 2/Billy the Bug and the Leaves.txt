Once upon a time, there was a little bug named Billy. Billy lived in a colorful cobweb in a big tree. He loved to watch the birds fly and the leaves dance in the wind. One day, while Billy was watching the birds, a big wind came. It blew and blew, and the colorful cobweb started to shake. Billy was scared and said, "Oh no! What will happen to my home?" Just then, a friendly bird named Betty flew down to the cobweb. She saw Billy and said, "Don't worry, little bug. I will help you!" Betty grabbed the cobweb with her beak and held it tight. The wind stopped blowing, and the cobweb was safe. Billy was so happy and said, "Thank you, Betty! You saved my home!" From that day on, Billy and Betty were the best of friends. They would watch the birds and the leaves together, and Billy learned that even the unexpected can bring new friends.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Pau and Mid. Pau had a guitar which was very special to him. Whenever he strummed its strings, it made him very happy. One day, Mid saw the guitar and became very envious. She wanted to have a guitar of her own. So Pau kindly offered to share his guitar and supply Mid with one too. He said that he was happy to share it with her. Mid was very pleased and happy. She thanked Pau for being so generous and the two of them got to play their guitars together. The moral of this story is that no matter how big or small, it is good to share with each other and be generous. Always try to put a smile on someone's face and remember that there are more happy moments when you share with each other.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy car with a handle. He loved his car very much. He would push the car around the house all day long. One day, Tim's big sister, Sue, took the car from him. "I want to play with it too," she said. Tim was sad and angry. He wanted his car back. "Give it back!" he cried. Sue just laughed and ran away. Tim chased Sue around the house. They ran and ran until they were both tired. Finally, Sue stopped and gave Tim his car back. "I'm sorry," she said. Tim was happy to have his car again. He gave Sue a big kiss on the cheek. They both smiled and went back to playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a big table in a room. The table had many colors. On the table, there was a piece of paper. The paper was very pretty. A little girl named Lily saw the paper and wanted to play with it. Lily took the paper and started to fold it. She folded it one time, two times, and three times. Now, the paper looked like a fake bird. Lily was very happy with her fake bird. She showed it to her mom and dad. They smiled and clapped for her. Lily put the fake bird on the table. She played with it every day. She made it fly around the room. She loved her fake bird very much. And the table with many colors was always there for her to play and make more fun things.

One day, a big bunny went to visit his friend, a small bird. The bird was sad because her nest was broken. The big bunny wanted to help, so he said, "Let's repair your nest!" They worked together to fix the nest. The big bunny used his strong legs to find sticks. The small bird used her beak to put the sticks in place. They were very happy when the nest was fixed. But then, something unexpected happened! The big bunny tried to go inside to see the nest, but he was too big! The small bird felt ashamed because her friend could not fit. The big bunny had a great idea. He said, "Let's make a bigger nest for both of us!" So, they worked together again and made a big nest where they could both play and be happy.
