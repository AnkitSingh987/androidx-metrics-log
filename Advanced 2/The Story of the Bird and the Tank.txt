Once upon a time, there was a big, messy tank. It was very strong and could destroy many things. It lived in a town where everyone was scared of it. The tank loved to make a mess and break things. One day, the tank was rolling down the street, ready to destroy more stuff. But then, something unexpected happened. A tiny, little bird flew up to the tank and landed on its big, messy side. The bird was not scared of the tank at all. The tank was surprised that the little bird was not scared. It made the tank think about what it was doing. The tank decided to stop being messy and destroying things. Instead, the tank and the little bird became friends. They went on fun adventures together, and the town was happy again.

Once upon a time, in a big wild forest, there lived a small bunny. The bunny liked to play with his friends, but he was scared of the rough ground. One day, the bunny decided to be brave and play on the rough ground. While playing, the bunny met a big bear. The bear was not nice. The bunny was scared and wanted to go back to his friends. The bear said, "Surrender, little bunny. You cannot go back to your friends." The bunny did not want to surrender, but he was too scared to run away. So, he thought of a plan. He told the bear that there was a big party with lots of food in the forest. The bear liked food, so he followed the bunny to the party. But there was no party. The bunny tricked the bear! The bear was very angry and chased the bunny. The bunny tried to run away, but he could not run fast on the rough ground. The bear caught the bunny and the bunny could not go back to his friends. The bunny was very sad and wished he had never played on the rough ground.

Once upon a time, there was a playful cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a big palace with her best friend, a little girl named Lucy. They loved to play together all day long. One sunny day, Kitty and Lucy were playing in the garden. Kitty was running and jumping, and Lucy was laughing. Then, Kitty got tired and wanted to lie down. She found a soft spot under a tree and lay down. Lucy said, "Kitty, why did you lie down?" Kitty replied, "I am tired, Lucy. Let's rest for a while." After a short rest, Kitty and Lucy went back to the palace to have a yummy snack. They had a fun day playing together, and they couldn't wait to play again tomorrow.

One day, a little boy named Tim had a big surprise. He woke up and saw a big box in his room. Tim was very happy. He jumped out of bed to look at the box. The box was dirty, but Tim didn't care. He wanted to see what was inside. Tim opened the box and found a toy car. The car was red and shiny. Tim loved cars, so he was very excited. He played with the car all day. He raced it around his room and down the hall. At the end of the day, Tim was tired. He put his car on the table and went to bed. When he woke up the next day, he saw his car and remembered the surprise. Tim smiled and knew he would have fun playing with his car again.

Once upon a time, in an ordinary town, there lived a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. One day, his mom gave him a helmet. She said, "Promise me you will wear this helmet when you ride your bike." Tim promised his mom he would wear the helmet. But sometimes, he forgot. One day, he rode his bike without the helmet. He went very fast. He saw his friends and wanted to show them how fast he could go. Tim rode faster and faster. But then, he fell off his bike. Tim got hurt because he did not wear his helmet. His mom was sad, and Tim was sad too. He learned that he should always keep his promise and wear his helmet.
