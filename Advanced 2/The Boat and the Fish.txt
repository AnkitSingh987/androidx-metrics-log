Once upon a time, there was a big boat. It had a big anchor. The boat liked to go on regular trips. One day, the boat met a little fish. "Hi, little fish!" said the boat. "What do you think of my big anchor?" "I think it is very big and strong," said the little fish. "But why do you need it?" The boat smiled. "I use my anchor to stay in one place when I need to stop. It helps me stay safe." The little fish and the boat became best friends. They went on many regular trips together. The little fish liked to swim next to the boat and the boat liked to show off its big anchor. They had lots of fun and were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She lived in a small house with her mom and dad. Lucy loved to play with her toys and draw pretty pictures. One day, she wanted to improve her room by drawing on the ceiling. She thought it would look nice with her drawings. Lucy found a chair and climbed up to reach the ceiling. She started to draw a big, icy snowman. It was a cold day, and Lucy thought an icy snowman would look fun. As she drew, something unexpected happened. The icy snowman on the ceiling started to move and came to life! The icy snowman said, "Hello, Lucy! Thank you for drawing me. I am a magic snowman, and I can make your drawings come to life!" Lucy was so surprised and happy. She drew more pictures on the ceiling, and they all came to life. Her room became a magical place full of her new friends. Lucy and her new friends had so much fun playing together.

One day, a little girl named Amy wanted to play hide and seek with her friends. She was very happy because she loved this game. She knew that if she hid well, she could win. Amy found a big tree to hide behind. Her friend, Tom, started counting. "1, 2, 3, 4, 5," he said. Amy was quiet and still behind the tree. She hoped Tom would not find her. Tom looked and looked for Amy. He could not find her. Finally, he called out, "Amy, where are you? I give up!" Amy jumped out from behind the tree and said, "Here I am! I win!" They both laughed and were happy. They played hide and seek all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to walk and play with his drum. Every day, he would walk around his house and make loud noises with his drum. Tim was very happy. One day, as Tim was walking and playing his drum, he saw a fragile bird on the ground. The bird was hurt and could not fly. Tim wanted to help the bird, so he stopped playing his drum. He picked up the bird very gently and took it to his mom. Tim's mom helped the bird feel better. The bird was not just a normal bird, it was a magic bird! To thank Tim for helping, the bird changed his drum into a magic drum. Now, every time Tim played his magic drum, it made everyone around him happy. Tim and the magic bird became best friends, and they walked and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim found an interesting toy in his room. It was a telephone. Tim liked to play with the telephone. He would pretend to call his friends and have fun talks with them. One day, Tim wanted to take his telephone outside to play. But it did not fit in his pocket. Tim was sad. He thought hard about how to make it fit. Then, he had an idea! He could put the telephone in his backpack. Tim put the telephone in his backpack and went outside. He played with his telephone under the big tree. He called his friends and talked about the fun things they would do together. Tim was happy that his telephone could fit in his backpack. The interesting telephone made his playtime more fun.
