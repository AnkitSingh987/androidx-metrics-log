Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a toy named Mr. Robot. Mr. Robot was enthusiastic and loved to play with Tim. They would play games and have fun all day. One day, Tim saw a cord on the floor. He didn't want anyone to trip on it. So he asked Mr. Robot, "Can you help me protect everyone from the cord?" Mr. Robot said, "Yes, Tim! I will help you." Tim and Mr. Robot picked up the cord and put it away safely. They were happy to keep everyone safe. From that day on, Tim and Mr. Robot always made sure to protect their friends and family from anything that could hurt them.

Once upon a time there was a boy called Jack. One day Jack wore his new coat for the first time and was so proud. But as he walked to school, it started to rain. As he looked in his bag, he realised he had left his umbrella at home! Jack quickly ran to a nearby shop. He knocked on the door, "Please let me in, I need shelter from the rain," he shouted. A kind old man answered the door. He saw that Jack was in a hurry, so he was very polite and welcomed him inside. Jack thanked him and said, "Can I borrow your umbrella? I promise I'll bring it back!" The old man smiled and said, "I'm sorry Jack, I still need it." Jack was very sad and started to walk home in the rain. He regretted not bringing his umbrella with him. When he arrived home, all his clothes were wet and his new coat was ruined. He wished he had been more careful and never left his umbrella at home. He never wore the coat again and he always felt a little bit of regret.

Once upon a time, there was a big mall. Many people went to the mall to buy things. One day, a big, patient dog named Max went to the mall with his friend, a little girl named Lily. They wanted to buy a toy for Lily's birthday. As they walked through the mall, Max saw a big sign that said, "Do not destroy!" Max didn't know what it meant, but he was a good dog, so he didn't touch anything. Lily and Max found a toy store and went inside. They saw many toys, but Lily couldn't decide which one she wanted. Lily asked Max, "Which toy should I pick?" Max looked around and saw a small toy house. He barked at it, saying, "This one!" Lily picked up the toy house, but as she did, she accidentally knocked over a big toy tower. The tower fell down and made a loud noise. All the people in the store looked at Lily and Max. Lily was scared, but Max was patient. He helped Lily pick up the toys and fix the tower. The store owner saw how good Max was and said, "Thank you for helping. You can have the toy house for free!" Lily and Max were so happy. They left the mall with the toy house, and Lily had the best birthday ever.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little boy named Tim. Tim loved to wear his favorite vest every day. It was blue and had a picture of a big red car on it. Tim thought that his vest made him look very smart. One day, Tim went to bed very early because he was very sleepy. The next morning, he did not wake up when the sun came up. His mom came into his room and said, "Tim, wake up! It is time to start the day!" Tim rubbed his eyes and slowly woke up. He was still a little sleepy, but he knew it was time to get up. Tim put on his smart blue vest with the big red car on it. He was so happy to wear it again. He went to the kitchen and ate some breakfast with his mom. They had a fun day playing together, and Tim felt very smart in his special vest.

Once upon a time there was a curious little girl named Billy. She loved to dream and always thought of fun and wonderful things. One day, Billy decided to pick some velvet flowers and bring them home. Billy beamed with excitement and said, "Mommy, I found the prettiest flowers! I want to put them on my bed." Mommy smiled and said, "Oh, that's lovely! Where did you find them?" Billy smiled, "I found them outside, in the garden. I dreamed of having velvet flowers on my bed!" Mommy chuckled, "Well, then, let's go and make those dreams come true!" So Mommy and Billy hung the velvet flowers on Billy's bed and Billy went to sleep, dreaming of all the wonderful things she could do with them.
