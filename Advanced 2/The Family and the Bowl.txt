Once there was a family who lived in a big house. Every day the father served dinner, but the family were always feeling miserable. One day the mother said it was time to start something new. So they all got together to make dinner. The father served up some delicious soup and everyone was happy. They each had a big bowl and after dinner was finished, everyone felt much better. Now the family start dinner together every night. Even though sometimes dinner is not as tasty as the father's soup, everyone feels much less miserable. And that is why the family serves dinner together.

Once there was a boy named Jack. He was three years old. Jack's mom gave him some sour candy. But Jack was so excited he forgot to take it out of the wrapper. He went outside and started playing. Jack dug a hole in the ground. Suddenly, he remembered the candy in his pocket. He jumped up and put his hand in his pocket. But it was gone! Jack looked everywhere but he couldn't find it. Jack was so sad. He yelled, "Where is my candy?" He looked in the hole he had dug. And there it was! Jack was so happy. He reached in the hole and grabbed the sour candy. He took a big bite and smiled.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient slide in a big park. The slide was very old and tall. All the kids loved to play on it every day. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim and his friend, Sally, went to the park to play. They ran to the slide and took turns sliding down. They laughed and had lots of fun. After playing for a while, Tim saw a small bird on the ground. It looked hungry. Tim said to Sally, "Let's feed the bird some bread!" Sally agreed, and they ran to find some bread. They found some bread and broke it into small pieces. They went back to the bird and said, "Here, bird! Eat this bread!" The bird ate the bread and chirped happily. Tim and Sally smiled, happy they could help the bird. They played on the ancient slide until the sun went down, and then they went home. They would always remember the day they fed the bird at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very impatient boy. He always wanted things right away. One day, Tim found a big tube in his yard. He did not know what it was for, but he wanted to play with it. Tim tried to put the tube on his head, but he could not manage. He tried to put the tube on his arm, but he could not manage that either. Tim was very sad. He wanted to play with the tube, but he could not find a way to do it. Then, Tim had an idea. He tried to be patient and think hard about how to use the tube. Suddenly, he found a way to play with it! He put the tube on the ground and rolled a ball through it. The ball went fast and far! Tim learned that being patient can help him find new ways to have fun. And that was the day Tim became a more patient boy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to eat yummy food. Every day, he would go to the kitchen to find something to eat. One day, Tim saw a big door with a shiny knob. He wanted to see what was behind the door. Tim tried to turn the knob, but it was too high for him. He tried to jump, but he could not reach it. Tim did not give up. He found a small chair and stood on it. This time, he could reach the knob. Tim was happy that he could succeed. When Tim opened the door, he found a big room full of yummy food. He was so excited! But then, something unexpected happened. All the yummy food started to talk! They were all friendly and wanted to play with Tim. Tim was surprised, but he made new friends with the yummy food. From that day on, Tim and his yummy friends had a lot of fun together.
