One day, a big dog named Max had a bad itch. Max tried to blink it away, but the itch did not go away. Max was sad and did not know what to do. Max saw a little bird and said, "Little bird, can you help me? I have a bad itch and I cannot make it go away." The little bird looked serious and said, "Yes, Max, I will help you. Follow me to the magic tree." Max and the little bird went to the magic tree. The little bird said, "Max, close your eyes and blink three times." Max did what the bird said. But when Max opened his eyes, he was not a dog anymore. He was a small bug! Now Max was even more sad. He still had the itch, but now he was a small bug and not a big dog. The little bird said, "I am sorry, Max. I tried to help you, but the magic tree did something wrong." Max blinked his tiny bug eyes and knew he could never be a big dog again.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a big hill and wanted to climb it. The sky was gloomy, but Tim did not care. He wanted to have fun on the hill. As Tim went up the hill, he saw a girl named Sue. Sue was sad because her toy had fallen down the hill. Tim wanted to help, so he went down the hill to find the toy. The hill was steep, but Tim was brave. Tim found the toy and gave it back to Sue. Sue was happy, and they both played on the hill together. The sky was still gloomy, but Tim and Sue did not care. They had fun and became good friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a polite little bee named Bella. Bella loved to prepare honey in her small cell, which was her home. She worked hard every day to make the sweetest honey for her friends. One sunny day, while Bella was preparing honey, her friend, a tiny ant named Andy, came to visit her. "Hello, Bella!" said Andy. "Can you please share some honey with me?" Bella smiled and said, "Of course, Andy! I am always happy to share with my friends." Bella gave Andy some honey, and they both enjoyed eating it together. After they finished, Andy thanked Bella for being so kind and sharing her honey. Bella felt happy that she could help her friend. From that day on, Bella and Andy became best friends, and they always prepared and shared their food together.

Once upon a time, there was a big ranch. On the ranch, there lived a weak cow named Molly. Molly was not like the other cows. She could not run and play with them. Molly felt sad. One day, a kind farmer saw Molly. He said, "Molly, do not be sad. I will help you." The farmer gave Molly some special food. The food made Molly strong. Molly was happy. The next day, Molly went to the field. She tried to yield to the other cows. But something unexpected happened. Molly could fly! The other cows were amazed. Molly was not weak anymore. She was the strongest cow on the ranch.

One day, a little duck named Dolly was walking near a deep drain. She saw her friend, a small frog named Freddy, lay by the drain. Dolly wanted to play with Freddy. "Hi Freddy, do you want to play with me?" Dolly asked. Freddy looked up and smiled. "Yes, Dolly! Let's play near the drain. We can jump over it and see who goes the farthest!" Dolly and Freddy took turns jumping over the deep drain. They laughed and cheered each other on. Dolly jumped very far, but Freddy jumped even farther. They were both very happy playing together. As the sun started to set, Dolly and Freddy knew it was time to go home. They said goodbye to each other and promised to play again soon. Dolly walked back to her pond, and Freddy hopped back to his lily pad. They both had a fun day playing by the deep drain.
