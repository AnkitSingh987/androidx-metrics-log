Once upon a time, in a big sea, there lived a wise old fish named Tuna. Tuna had many friends in the sea. One day, Tuna met a small fish who was sad. "Why are you sad?" asked Tuna. The small fish said, "I can't find my family. I'm lost." Tuna thought for a moment and said, "I will help you find your family." Tuna and the small fish swam together. They saw a big rock. Tuna told the small fish, "Let's cover our eyes and hide behind the rock." They covered their eyes and waited. Soon, the small fish's family swam by. The small fish was very happy. "Thank you, Tuna," said the small fish. "You are a wise and good friend." And from that day on, the small fish knew that asking for help was a good thing to do.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was upset because she lost her beef. She looked everywhere for it but could not find it. Bessie was very sad. One day, Bessie met a dog named Max. Max was a big, strong dog who liked to protect his friends. Max saw that Bessie was upset and asked her what was wrong. Bessie told Max about her lost beef. Max wanted to help Bessie, so they looked for the beef together. They looked under rocks, in the grass, and behind trees. Finally, they found the beef near a big tree. Bessie was so happy! Max promised to protect Bessie and her beef, so she would never be upset again. Bessie and Max became best friends and lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Millie. She was so happy, but one day her world was snapped. Millie and her friends were playing when a bad cloud appeared above them. It was dark and the sky was full of thunder and lightning. Millie was scared and ran back home. She said to her mom, “I’m afraid of the bad cloud!” Her mom smiled and said, “Don’t worry, Millie. The bad cloud won’t last forever. Life is like this bad cloud sometimes. When it gets dark and scary, just remember that it won’t last forever.” Millie was surprised to hear this. She realized that life could be hard sometimes, but it wouldn't last forever. She smiled and said, “Ok mom, I promise I’ll be brave and keep looking forward.” Moral: Life can be hard sometimes, but don't give up. Be brave and keep looking to the future.

One day, a red car named Speedy loved to speed. Speedy was very fast and liked to race with his friends. He always won because he was the fastest car in town. One day, Speedy saw a big bag of popcorn on the road. He wanted to eat it, but he was going too fast. So, he tried to stop, but he could not. Speedy hit the popcorn, and it went everywhere! The popcorn filled the air like snow. But then, something unexpected happened. The popcorn made Speedy slow down. He was not fast anymore. His friends caught up to him and they all raced together. Now, Speedy did not always win, but he was happy because he could have fun with his friends. And they all loved to eat popcorn together.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a girl named Lily. She had a big calendar on her wall. Every day, she would look at it and see what fun things she could do. One day, Lily saw that it was her friend Tom's birthday. She wanted to make him happy. So, she went to her mom and said, "Mom, can we have a party for Tom? Please let us have a party today." Her mom smiled and said, "Of course, Lily! We can have a party for Tom. Let's bake a cake and invite all your friends." Lily and her mom baked a big cake and invited all their friends. They played games, ate cake, and had a lot of fun. Tom was very happy, and he said, "Thank you, Lily! This is the best birthday ever!" From that day on, Lily always checked her calendar to make sure she never missed a chance to make her friends happy. And the peaceful little town had many more fun parties and happy days.
