Once upon a time, there was a big castle. In the castle, there was a terrible dragon. The dragon liked to lie down and sleep all day. The people who lived near the castle were scared of the dragon. One day, a brave girl named Lily came to the castle. She wanted to make the dragon go away. Lily walked up to the dragon while it was lying down. She was not scared. Lily asked the dragon, "Why are you so terrible?" The dragon opened its eyes and said, "I am not terrible. I am just lonely." Lily felt sad for the dragon. She decided to be its friend. The dragon was happy and not terrible anymore. They played together every day, and the people near the castle were not scared anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a big ball called Earth. Earth had many friends like Sun, Moon, and Stars. They all liked to play together in the sky. One day, Earth wanted to play a new game. The game was called "Miss". Earth would spin around, and her friends would try to catch her. But they had to be careful not to bump into her. Sun, Moon, and Stars took turns trying to catch Earth. Sometimes they missed her, and sometimes they got close. They all laughed and had fun playing the game. Earth felt happy to have such good friends. And they played "Miss" until it was time to go to sleep.

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tom. Tom was three years old, and he was eager to learn new things. One day, Tom was walking in a park when he found a big stone. He picked it up and it felt fair. Tom was very curious. He wanted to know all about the stone so he took it home and asked his mom. “Mom, what is this?” he asked, holding up the stone. His mom smiled. “That’s a record stone,” she said. “It holds a special record. Every time you and I do something nice together, the stone records it and keeps it forever.” Tom was very excited. He wanted to record some special memories. So, every day he and his mom went to the park and recorded happy memories. Tom was so proud of his record stone and all the memories he collected. And from then on, Tom and his mom always had fair memories to cherish.

One day, a big, filthy bird sat on a roof. It saw a little girl named Sue walk by. Sue looked up and saw the bird. She wanted to help the bird become clean. Sue went to the store with her mom to buy some soap. She said, "Mom, can we buy soap to clean the bird?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue. We can help the bird." They bought the soap and went back to the house. Sue used the soap to clean the bird. The bird was so happy and clean. It flew away and came back with a pretty flower for Sue. Sue and the bird became best friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk in the woods with her mom. They saw a berry bush with big, red berries on it. Mia wanted to pick some berries to eat, so she reached out to poke the berries. Just then, a fierce squirrel jumped out from behind the bush. The squirrel looked at Mia and said, "These are my berries! You can't have any!" Mia was scared and ran to her mom. Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, Mia. The squirrel is just protecting its food. Let's find another berry bush to pick from." Mia felt better, and they went on their walk, looking for more berries.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Jake and a guitar. Jake was very ignorant and never wanted to clean his guitar. Every day, his guitar became dirtier and dirtier. One day, his dad asked him, "Why don't you clean your guitar?" Jake grumbled, "I don't want to!" His dad said, "If you want to play it, then you must take care of it too. If you clean it now, you can enjoy your guitar for a very long time." Jake listened to his dad's advice and started to clean his guitar. He wiped and polished it until it sparkled. Then, he started strumming the strings and it sounded beautiful. Jake learned a lesson that day. He was not ignorant anymore and realised that to enjoy what he owns, he must take care of it and clean it regularly.
