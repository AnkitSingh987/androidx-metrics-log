Once upon a time, there was a friendly dog. The friendly dog liked to travel. One day, the friendly dog went on a long walk. As he walked, he saw white snow on the ground. The friendly dog liked the snow. The friendly dog played in the snow. He jumped and ran around. The friendly dog was having fun. He saw a big hill and wanted to go up. The friendly dog started to climb the hill. But the hill was too steep. The friendly dog slipped on the snow. He fell down the hill. The friendly dog was hurt and sad. He could not play in the snow anymore. The friendly dog went home with a bad ending.

Anna and Ben are twins. They are three years old. Today is their birthday. They are very happy. They want to celebrate. "Mom, can we go to the museum?" Anna asks. She likes to see the big animals and the shiny rocks. "Sure, sweetie. The museum is a good place to have fun and learn," Mom says. She packs some snacks and drinks for them. They get in the car and drive to the museum. Ben is excited. He wants to see the big trains and the old cars. They arrive at the museum and buy their tickets. Mom gives them each a sticker that says "Happy Birthday". They feel special. They explore the museum and see many things. They see a big dinosaur skeleton, a colorful butterfly garden, a shiny rocket, and a funny clown. They laugh and ask Mom many questions. They also meet other kids who are celebrating their birthdays. They share their snacks and drinks and play together. They make new friends. They have a good time at the museum. They learn a lot and have fun. They thank Mom for the birthday treat. They go back to the car and sing "Happy Birthday" to each other. They hug Mom and say they love her. They are happy. They celebrate.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Tom. Tom liked going to the beach with his mom and dad. His mom and dad would always let him explore and use his imagination. One day when they went to the beach, Tom saw something glistening in the sand. It was an oyster! It was harmless, so Tom picked it up and opened it. Inside he found a shiny pearl! Tom's mom and dad let him keep the pearl, and he kept it carefully in his pocket. He was so excited to show his friends the pearl. He told everyone that went on the beach, and shared the pearl with everyone he met. Tom and his family returned to the beach many times and took lots of friends too. He showed them all the oyster, and let them look inside. They all found lovely pearls, which made them all very happy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a peaceful forest. In the forest, there was a big tree with many roots. Animals lived near the tree and loved to play. One day, a little bird came to the tree. The bird was sad because it lost its family. The bird sat on a root and began to pray. As the bird prayed, something unexpected happened. The big tree started to shake! The animals were scared and ran away. But the little bird kept praying on the root. It did not stop praying. Then, the tree stopped shaking. The animals came back to see what happened. To their surprise, they found the bird's family sitting on the tree! The bird was very happy. The animals learned that praying can bring good things. From that day, they all prayed together in their peaceful forest home.

One day, a kind cat named Tom went for a walk. He saw a little bird with a hurt wing. Tom felt sad for the bird and wanted to help. Tom took the bird to his friend, a wise old owl. The owl gave the bird a wink and said, "I can fix your wing." The owl wrapped the bird's wing with a soft leaf. The bird felt better and said, "Thank you, kind friends." The bird was so happy, she started to sing a pretty song. Tom and the owl began to clap and dance. They were all very happy to be friends and help each other.
