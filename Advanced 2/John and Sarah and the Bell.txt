Once upon a time, there were two friends, John and Sarah. They both loved playing basketball. One day they heard the bell ding and school was finished. They ran outside to play basketball. John said, "Let's have a fair game." Sarah replied, "Yes! That sounds fun!" They each grabbed a basketball and started playing. They each tried their best to shoot the ball through the hoop. They played for hours and hours until it started to get dark. At the end of the game, they both agreed it was fair. John exclaimed, "We had so much fun!" Sarah smiled and said, "Yes, this was a very fair game!" The two friends decided to meet up again the next day and finish their game.

Once upon a time, in a deep, deep forest, there was a brave fireman named Tom. He had a big red truck and wore a big red hat. Tom loved to help people and animals when they needed him. One sunny day, Tom saw a little bird with a hurt wing. He knew he could help the bird heal. But just as he was helping the bird, he saw smoke in the deep, deep forest. There was a big fire! Tom knew he had to stop the fire and save the forest. Tom put the little bird in his big red truck and drove very fast to the fire. He used the water from his truck to put out the fire. The forest was saved! After the fire was gone, Tom took the little bird to a safe place and helped its wing heal. The little bird was so happy and thanked Tom for saving the forest and helping it heal.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookshelf in a little room. The bookshelf had many books on it. Some books were hidden behind other books. There was a little girl named Amy who loved to read the books on the bookshelf. One day, Amy's big brother Tom came into the room. He saw Amy reading a book. Tom liked to tease Amy. He said, "I can find a hidden book that you can't find." Amy did not like when Tom teased her. She said, "No, you can't!" Tom started to look for a hidden book on the bookshelf. Amy looked too. They both looked and looked. Then, Amy found a hidden book behind some other books. She showed it to Tom. Tom stopped teasing Amy. They both sat down and read the hidden book together. They were happy to find a new book to read.

Once upon a time, there was a unique little bird named Blue. Blue loved to fly high in the sky and play with his friends. He was very happy. One day, Blue started to worry. He saw that his friends could sing, but he could not. Blue felt sad and thought that maybe his friends would not love him because he could not sing like them. Blue decided to try something new. He danced and played with his friends in a different way. They all loved Blue's unique dance and played with him all day. Blue learned that being different is okay, and his friends loved him for who he was. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Mia went for a walk. She wanted to discover new things. Mia saw a red ball, a yellow flower, and a green frog. She was very happy to find these things. Then, Mia found a long, thin thing called a thermometer. It helps to know if something is hot or not hot. She wanted to try it. Mia put the thermometer in the sun. The sun was very hot. The thermometer showed that it was hot outside. Mia took the thermometer home to show her mom. Her mom was happy to see Mia's new discovery. They put the thermometer on the wall. Now, Mia can see if it is hot or not hot every day.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a powerful elephant. The elephant had a long trunk. He loved to hug his friends with his trunk. All his friends in the forest liked his hugs. One day, while the elephant was walking, he saw a little bird. The little bird was sad. The elephant wanted to hug the bird to make it happy. But the bird was too small for a hug from the powerful elephant. The elephant was sad too. Then, the elephant had an idea. He used his trunk to pick up a soft leaf. He wrapped the leaf around the little bird like a hug. The little bird started to smile. The elephant and the bird became best friends. They played together every day, and the powerful elephant learned that even little hugs can make a big difference.
