Once upon a time, there was a big house. In the house, there was a small attic. The attic was a comfortable place for a little girl named Mia and her toys. Mia liked to welcome her toys to the attic every day. One day, Mia went to the attic. She saw a big box. She did not know what was in the box. Mia opened the box and found a big, soft bear. She was so happy! Mia welcomed the bear to her attic. Mia played with the bear in the attic. They had so much fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The bear started to talk! The bear said, "Thank you for welcoming me to your attic, Mia." Mia was so surprised, but she was happy to have a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He had an orange toy car that he loved to play with. He would make the car go zoom around his room. He played with his car every day. One day, Tim saw his friend Sue playing with her toy car. Sue's car was not orange, but it could also zoom. Tim wanted to play with Sue's car, but he did not want to share his orange car. Sue looked sad because she wanted to play with Tim's car too. Then, Tim remembered that it is good to share. He decided to let Sue use his orange car, and Sue let Tim use her car. They played together and had lots of fun. They made their cars zoom all around the room. Tim and Sue were happy because they learned that sharing is fun and makes everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom and dad. Tim loved to play outside in his wide backyard. One sunny day, Tim found a beautiful crystal while playing in the dirt. It was shiny and pretty, and he was very happy. Tim showed the crystal to his mom. "Wow, Tim! That's a very nice crystal," she said. "But remember, you should always share your things with others." Tim nodded and went back outside to play. Later, Tim saw his friend Sally. She was sad because she lost her favorite toy. Tim thought about what his mom said and decided to give Sally his crystal to replace her lost toy. "Here, Sally. You can have my crystal," he said. Sally's face lit up, and she smiled. "Thank you, Tim! You are a good friend." They played together with the crystal all day long. Tim was happy because he learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy. And that was the moral value of the story: sharing is caring.

Once upon a time, a boy named Tim went for a jog. He liked to jog in the park. Tim was very happy. He had a yummy sandwich in his bag. He planned to eat the sandwich after the jog. While jogging, Tim saw a big tree. He felt uncomfortable. He thought he saw something move behind the tree. Tim stopped jogging and looked closer. A funny squirrel came out from behind the tree. The squirrel was holding Tim's sandwich! Tim was surprised. He did not know how the squirrel got his sandwich. The squirrel ran away with the sandwich. Tim chased the squirrel. They ran and ran. The squirrel was fast, but Tim was faster. Finally, Tim caught the squirrel. He got his sandwich back. The squirrel looked sad. Tim felt sorry for the squirrel. He broke the sandwich in half and shared it with the squirrel. They both ate the sandwich together. Tim and the squirrel became good friends.

Tom was sorting through some things that he had found. He looked at the mitten, it was thin and looked nice. "I could use this," he said. Then his mother came into the room. "Tom, why do you have my mitten?" she asked. Tom looked up, "I found it, I thought it was nice." His mother smiled, "Yes, it is nice, but it's my mitten, I lost it this morning." Tom gave the mitten to his mother. "I'm sorry, I should have asked," he said. His mother smiled, "Sorting through things is good, but we should always ask before we use something that isn't ours." Tom nodded and hugged his mother. He was glad she had gotten her mitten back.
