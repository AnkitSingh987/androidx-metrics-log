Once upon a time, there was an old man who liked to polish things. He would spend hours in his workshop, polishing his treasures. One day, he found the most beautiful chain. It was so shiny, so clean! He spent the whole day polishing it. He was thrilled with his work, but what happened next was a surprise! The old man took his chain outside, and before he knew it, the chain had been taken away. He was so sad he had to go home without it. The old man was a little bit wiser. He knew to always keep his most precious things safe. He learned his lesson, but it was too late for his chain!

Lila was very happy. She had an invitation to Anna's birthday party. Anna was her best friend. She liked to play with Anna and share her toys. The invitation had a picture of a big cake with candles. Lila wanted to eat some cake and sing happy birthday. But Tom was not happy. He did not have an invitation. He was Anna's brother. He wanted to go to the party too. He was envious of Lila. He thought Anna liked Lila more than him. He was angry and sad. He decided to do something bad. He went to Lila's house and knocked on the door. Lila opened the door and smiled. She said, "Hi, Tom. Do you want to play with me?" Tom said, "No, I don't. I want your invitation. Give it to me now!" Lila was surprised and scared. She said, "No, Tom. This is my invitation. Anna gave it to me. You can't have it. Go away!" Tom was furious. He pushed Lila and grabbed the invitation. He ran away with it. He laughed and said, "Ha ha! Now I can go to the party and you can't!" Lila cried and shouted, "Tom, you are mean! Give me back my invitation! That's not fair!" But Tom did not listen. He ran to his house and hid the invitation under his pillow. He thought he was clever and happy. But he was wrong. That night, something strange happened. The invitation started to glow. It glowed brighter and brighter. It made a loud noise. It said, "Tom, you are naughty! You stole Lila's invitation. You hurt her feelings. You can't go to the party. You have to say sorry to Lila and give her back the invitation. Or else!" Tom was scared. He did not like the glowing invitation. He did not like the loud noise. He did not like the words. He felt sorry for what he did. He wanted to make Lila happy again. He took the invitation and ran to Lila's house. He knocked on the door. Lila opened the door and saw Tom. She was still sad and angry. Tom said, "Lila, I'm sorry. I was wrong. I was envious of you. I should not have taken your invitation. Here, take it back. Please forgive me." Lila was surprised and happy. She took the invitation and hugged Tom. She said, "Tom, thank you. I forgive you. You are my friend. Do you want to come to the party with me?" Tom smiled and nodded. He said, "Yes, I do. Thank you, Lila. You are very kind." The invitation stopped glowing. It was quiet and normal. It said, "Tom and Lila, you are good. You are friends. You can go to the party. Have fun!" Tom and Lila went to the party together. They ate some cake and sang happy birthday. They played with Anna and the other children. They were happy and had fun. They learned to share and care. They were good friends. The end.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a smart dog named Max. Max loved to build things with his toys. His favorite toy was a big, round, orange ball. Max would play with his orange ball all day long. One sunny day, Max was building a tower with his toys. He put the orange ball on top of the tower. He was very happy with his tower. But then, a strong wind came and blew the orange ball far away. Max was sad and went to look for his orange ball. Max looked everywhere for his orange ball. He looked behind trees, under cars, and in the park. Just when Max was about to give up, he saw his orange ball on top of a big hill. Max was so happy! But when he got to the top of the hill, he found out that the orange ball was not his. It was a big, round, orange pumpkin! Max was surprised, but he knew that he could still build something with the pumpkin. So, Max rolled the pumpkin down the hill and built a new tower with it. Max was happy and proud of his new tower, and he learned that sometimes, unexpected things can be fun too.
