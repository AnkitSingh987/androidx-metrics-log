Once upon a time, there was a naughty fox. He did not listen to his parents and was always getting into trouble. One night, the mischievous fox was running around in the forest. Suddenly, he came across a skull! The fox shivered with fear and stepped back. He wanted to turn around and leave, but he was too scared to move. Suddenly, a voice called out, "What are you doing here?" The fox looked up and saw an old man in the shadows. The man had a dark cape and an evil smile. The fox tried to run away, but the man grabbed him by the tail. The fox was so scared that he couldn't move. The old man cackled and said, "Naughty fox, you won't be seeing your parents ever again!" The fox shivered in fear. The old man then dragged the fox away into the darkness, never to be seen again.

One sunny day, Pete was outside playing in the garden. He saw a big nosy tree and went over to have a look. He tried to climb the tree but it was too slippery. Then Pete had an idea; he started to spray the trunk of the tree with water. He sprayed and sprayed until the trunk became covered in water. After a few minutes, Pete was able to climb the tree. When Pete was near the top he saw something shining in the branches. He reached out to grab it, but suddenly a voice came from behind him. It said, "That's mine! You can't have that!" Pete looked down and saw a mean looking old lady. She said, “You were being nosy. That’s why I said ‘no’ to you.” Pete quickly climbed down the tree and ran away. The old lady was very nosy, but Pete was glad he was able to escape without getting into trouble.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. She saw a pretty plant with green leaves and pink flowers. Sue really wanted to take the plant home, so she asked her mom, "Can I have this plant, please?" Her mom said yes, and they took the plant home. At home, Sue put the plant near her bed. But after a few days, the plant looked sad and confused. It was not as pretty as before. Sue didn't know what to do. She asked her mom, "Why is the plant sad?" Her mom told her, "The plant needs water and sun to be happy." Sue started to give the plant water and put it near the window for sun. The plant became happy and pretty again. Sue learned that taking care of something is important. The unexpected thing was that the plant grew bigger and bigger, and soon it had many more pretty flowers. Sue was very happy and knew that when you take care of something, it will grow and be beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The green frog loved to clap. He would clap all day long. The green frog also loved to eat popcorn. One day, the green frog went to a party. At the party, there was a big bowl of popcorn. The green frog was very happy. He clapped and clapped. Then, he ate some popcorn. It was very yummy. After the party, the green frog went home. He was very tired from all the clapping and eating popcorn. The green frog went to bed and had a good sleep. He dreamed about clapping and eating more popcorn.

Once upon a time, there was a big, angry cat. The cat had a toy that it loved to play with all day. One day, the toy broke and the cat was very sad. The cat's friend, a little mouse, saw the broken toy. The mouse wanted to help, so it tried to repair the toy. It worked very hard to fix it. The cat watched and hoped the toy would be good as new. When the toy was fixed, the mouse found some ink. It used the ink to draw a happy face on the toy. The cat loved the new look! The cat and the mouse played together with the toy, and the angry cat became a happy cat. They were the best of friends.
