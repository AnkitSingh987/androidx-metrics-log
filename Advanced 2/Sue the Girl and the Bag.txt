One day, a girl named Sue found a fancy bag. She was very happy. Sue wanted to show her friend, Tom. So, she decided to carry the bag to Tom's house. When Sue got to Tom's house, she said, "Look, Tom! I found a fancy bag!" Tom looked at the bag and said, "Wow, that is a nice bag! Can I have it?" Sue thought for a moment, but she wanted to keep the bag. "No, Tom, I want to keep it," Sue said. Tom was not happy. He wanted the fancy bag too. So, when Sue was not looking, Tom took the bag and ran away. Sue was very sad. She lost her fancy bag and her friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very hungry one morning. He wanted to go to school and eat his lunch. On his way to school, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to attach a swing to the tree. He said to his friend, "Let's attach a swing to this tree!" His friend said, "Yes, let's do it!" They found a rope and a seat. They attached the swing to the tree. Tim and his friend took turns swinging. They laughed and had fun. After playing, they went to school. Tim was still hungry. He opened his lunch box and ate his sandwich. He was happy to have food in his belly. Tim and his friend played on the swing every day before school. And they were never hungry again.

One day grandma took her grandchild to the park. The little one was really excited and skipped happily around the big green lawn. "Look at this, grandchild," said grandma, showing him a teaspoon in the grass. The grandchild's eyes grew wide in amazement. "What is it?" he asked. "It's a teaspoon," said Grandma, beaming. The grandchild examined it carefully. He picked it up and tried to skip with it in his hand. "Be careful, child," said Grandma. "It's a serious thing." The grandchild nodded solemnly. Then he carefully put it back in the grass and went on skipping happily around the park.

Peter wanted to please his mom. He thought of a good idea! He wanted to find a huge mailbox for his mom. So Peter started his search. He went to the store and asked the lady behind the counter. She said she didn't have a huge mailbox. So Peter was sad. He continued his search, but the mailboxes he found were not huge. They were too small. At the end of the day, Peter was still far from finding what he wanted. His mom would be so disappointed. As Peter was walking home, he noticed a huge mailbox in a garden. He was so excited! But when he stepped closer, he realized the mailbox was broken. Peter felt sad. He had hoped to please his mom, but he was unable to. He went home and hugged his mom, but the mailbox remained broken.

Once upon a time, a girl called Lucy and her family went on a special holiday to the beach. The sun was shining and the air was warm. When they got to the beach, it was so lively! There were lots of people playing in the sand, splashing in the sea and eating ice-creams. Lucy's mum gave her a big red bucket and a colourful spade. Then she showed her how to use them to cut a castle shape in the sand. Lucy was so excited! She made the castle bigger and bigger, and added towers and flags. It looked like a real fairytale castle! When it was time to go home, Lucy said goodbye to her castle. But she knew she could come back to the beach another day and build it again.

Once upon a time, there lived an old man and an old woman. They were both very wealthy and owned many things. Every day, the old man hung a big zero from the ceiling. He thought it was very attractive. One day, a curious young boy saw the zero and asked the old man: "What is that?" The old man replied: "That is a zero. It means nothing." The boy thought for a moment and said: "If that is nothing, then why do you hang it up?" The old man smiled and said: "It's a reminder to me that no matter how wealthy I am, if I do not use my wealth to do good and help others, then my wealth is really worth nothing at all." The young boy nodded and learned an important lesson that day. It is not enough to be wealthy. You should strive to use your wealth to make the world a better place.
