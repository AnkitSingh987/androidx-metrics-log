Once there was a small girl named Marie who lived with her mom and dad. Every day, Marie's mom and dad would tell her stories. They told her stories about animals, far off lands and even her own adventures. One day, her mom and dad told her a special story: they told her that if she looked under the big mineral they had in the garden, she would find a special surprise! Marie was excited to look for the surprise. She looked everywhere! She dug holes and searched under rocks and trees. But no matter how hard she looked, she could not see any signs of the surprise. Marie was feeling sad. But then, one sunny day, she finally found it! Under that big mineral, there was a small box. Inside the box was a shiny rock. Marie was so glad to have finally found the surprise. Marie was so glad that she showed her mom and dad the surprise. They were just as glad as Marie was to see the surprise - it was a special mineral that would protect her from all the bad things in the world. Marie keep the mineral near her, so no matter what happened, she would always be safe.

Once upon a time, in a wide green park, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a shiny medal hanging from a tree. He wanted it so much, but he was too small to reach it. Spot's mom said, "I will permit you to try and get the medal, but you must be very careful." Spot thought and thought about how he could get the medal. He asked his friends, the birds, to help him. They agreed and they all made a plan. The next day, Spot and his bird friends went to the tree. The birds flew up and held Spot's tail with their beaks. They lifted him up, up, up, until he could reach the medal. Spot was so happy! He thanked his bird friends and wore his shiny medal with pride. From that day on, Spot and his bird friends played together every day in the wide green park.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play the violin. She played it every day. Lily was an honest girl. She always told the truth. One day, Lily met a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to play the violin too. "Can you teach me to play?" he asked. Lily said, "Yes, I can help you. But you must be honest like me." Tom agreed. They played the violin together. But then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and took the violin away. Lily and Tom were very sad. They looked for the violin, but they could not find it. The wind was too strong. In the end, they could not play the violin anymore.

Lily and Ben were at the beach with their mom and dad. They liked to play in the sand and look for shells and rocks. They also liked to swim in the water, but they had to be careful of the jellyfish. Jellyfish were soft and pretty, but they could sting and hurt. One day, Lily saw a big jellyfish near the shore. She wanted to touch it and see how it felt. She ran to the water and reached for the jellyfish. Ben saw her and shouted, "Lily, no! That's a jellyfish! It can sting you!" But Lily did not listen. She thought Ben was just being mean and bossy. She grabbed the jellyfish and lifted it up. She felt a sharp pain in her hand and dropped the jellyfish. She started to cry and ran back to the sand. Ben followed her and saw her hand was red and swollen. He felt sorry for her and said, "Lily, I'm sorry you got hurt. But you should have listened to me. I was trying to help you. Jellyfish are not toys. They are animals that need to be left alone." Lily looked at Ben and realized he was right. She said, "Ben, I'm sorry I did not listen to you. You are my brother and you know more than me. You were being important and kind. Thank you for caring about me." Ben smiled and hugged Lily. He said, "You're welcome, Lily. I love you and I want you to be safe. Next time, let's stir the water with a stick and see what we can find. Maybe we can find some fish or crabs or starfish. But no more jellyfish, okay?" Lily nodded and said, "Okay, Ben. Let's do that. I love you too." Mom and dad came and saw Lily's hand. They put some cream on it and gave her a bandage. They also praised Ben for being a good brother and helping Lily. They said, "You two are very brave and smart. We are proud of you. You learned a valuable lesson today. You learned to respect nature and to listen to each other. That's very important." Lily and Ben agreed and hugged mom and dad. They had a fun day at the beach, but they also learned something new. They learned to stir the water, not the jellyfish.
