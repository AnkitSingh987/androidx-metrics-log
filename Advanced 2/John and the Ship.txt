Sara liked to play with her mom's bracelet. It was shiny and had many colors. She wanted to wear it and show it to her friends. But her mom did not permit her to take it. She said it was too big and too precious for Sara. One day, Sara saw the bracelet on the table. Her mom was not in the room. Sara thought she could wear it for a little while. She put it on her wrist and ran outside. She felt happy and pretty. She saw her friend Anna in the park. Anna liked the bracelet too. She asked Sara if she could try it on. Sara said yes. She wanted to share her joy. She gave the bracelet to Anna. Anna put it on her wrist and smiled. But then, a big dog came running. It barked and jumped. Sara and Anna were scared. They ran away from the dog. But the bracelet fell off Anna's wrist. It landed on the grass. The dog saw it and thought it was a toy. It picked it up with its mouth and ran away. Sara and Anna cried. They lost the bracelet. They did not know what to do. They went back to Sara's house. They told Sara's mom what happened. Sara's mom was angry and sad. She said Sara was naughty and careless. She said she should not have taken the bracelet without permission. She said the bracelet was a gift from her grandma and it was very special. Sara felt sorry and ashamed. She said she was sorry and she loved her mom. She said she wanted to find the bracelet and give it back to her. She asked her mom to forgive her. Her mom hugged her and said she forgave her. She said she loved her too. She said they would try to find the bracelet together. She said they should be careful and healthy and not go near big dogs. She said they should always ask before they take something that is not theirs. Sara and Anna nodded and said they understood. They hoped they would find the bracelet and make everything right.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big building. In this building, there were many animals. One day, a fat cat came to the building. The fat cat wanted to play with the other animals, but they did not want to play with him. They did not like how big and fat he was. They would ignore him when he tried to play. The fat cat was very sad. He wanted to have friends and play games like the other animals. One day, he saw a little mouse. The mouse was in trouble. He was stuck in a hole and could not get out. The fat cat wanted to help the mouse, but he remembered that the other animals did not like him. The fat cat thought about it and decided to ignore the mouse, just like the other animals ignored him. He walked away, leaving the mouse in the hole. The mouse stayed stuck in the hole and could not get out. The fat cat never made friends with the other animals, and the mouse stayed stuck in the hole forever. This was a sad ending for both the fat cat and the little mouse.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a noisy boy who liked to play with his toys all day. He had many toys, but he did not like to share. Tim would say, "These are my toys, I own them and no one else can play with them!" One day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He brought his favorite toy car in his hand. While playing, Tim saw a little girl named Sally. Sally wanted to play with Tim's toy car. Tim did not want to share, but his mom told him, "Tim, you should share your toys with others. It is good to be kind." Tim was not happy, but he let Sally play with his toy car. As they played, something unexpected happened. Tim and Sally began to have fun together! They laughed and played, and Tim learned that sharing could be fun too. From that day on, Tim was not a noisy boy who kept his toys to himself. He shared his toys with others and made many friends. The moral of the story is that sharing is a good thing, and it can bring happiness to everyone.
