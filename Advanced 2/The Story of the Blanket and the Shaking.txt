Once there was a little girl. She liked to shake in the morning. She enjoyed shaking her arms and legs. Every day she did shaking exercises. She had a special shaking blanket. It was bright red and very thick. She loved to wrap the blanket around her and shake back and forth. She would laugh and giggle while she did it. One day, the little girl wanted to do something special. She grabbed her shaking blanket and wrapped it around her. Then, she started shaking harder than ever. She shook so hard that the thick blanket started to move around. The little girl was so happy that she shook even harder. She was having so much fun that she didn't want to stop. Finally, she stopped to take a break. She was so happy that she could do her favorite exercise any time she wanted!

Once upon a time there were two sisters. The older sister, Anna, so very jealous of her little sister, Lexi. Anna wanted to show her little sister that she could do things too. “Today, I’m going to use the mixer to make a cake,” Anna announced one day. “Would you like to help?” Lexi excitedly nodded her head and ran to get the mixer. Lexi and Anna then carefully used it to mix all the ingredients together. With the last mix, the muffins were ready. Anna smiled at Lexi proudly. “I’m glad you could help me, Lexi. You did a great job!” The jealous Anna forced a smile and looked away. She knew that Lexi could do things just as well as her, but she just wanted to make sure. Meanwhile, Lexi was so happy to help her big sister and was proud of what they had accomplished!

One day, a little girl named Amy went for a walk down the road. She saw many pretty flowers on the side of the road. Amy wanted to pick a flower to give to her mom. She looked at all the flowers and picked a big, red one. As she walked, Amy met a silly dog. The dog was jumping and playing. The dog wanted to play with Amy. So, Amy and the dog played together. They had lots of fun running and laughing. After playing, Amy and the dog walked back down the road. They went to Amy's house to show her mom the big, red flower. Her mom was happy and gave Amy a big hug. The silly dog wagged its tail, and they all had a fun day together.

One day, a little white cat named Lily went outside to play. She saw some clay by the big tree. Lily thought it would be fun to make something with the clay. Lily started to make a ball with the clay. A small dog named Max saw Lily and wanted to play too. Max ran up to Lily and said, "Can I play with you and the clay?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's make something together!" They both played with the clay and made a big tower. But then, the tower fell down and broke. Lily and Max began to yell, "Oh no! Our tower!" They decided to try again, but this time they made a small house with the clay. They were very happy with their house. Lily and Max played together all day, and they became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to dream. Every night, she would dream about playing with her friends in a big, fun park. Mia was a healthy girl who liked to run and jump with her friends. One day, Mia found a long rope in her yard. She had an idea! She called her friend Ben and said, "Ben, come here! Let's play with this rope!" Ben came over and they decided to play a game of tug-of-war with the rope. Mia and Ben pulled and pulled on the rope, both trying to win. But then, the rope broke! Mia and Ben both fell down. They were sad and started to cry. Mia's mom came outside and saw them crying. She said, "Don't worry, kids. I will fix the rope for you." Mia's mom tied the broken rope together, and it was as good as new. Mia and Ben were happy again. They thanked Mia's mom and continued to play with the rope. They learned that sometimes things break, but with help from others, they can be fixed. And so, Mia and Ben played happily ever after.
