Once upon a time, there was a happy little girl named Lucy. She loved to create things with her toy blocks. One day, Lucy decided to build a big tower with her blocks. She wanted her tower to reach the sky! As Lucy was building the tower, her friend Tom came to play. "What are you doing?" Tom asked. "I am creating a tower with my blocks," Lucy said with a big smile. Tom wanted to help, so he started to build with Lucy. They were having so much fun together. As they built the tower, they used a scale to make sure it was tall and straight. The tower got taller and taller, and Lucy and Tom were very happy. They laughed and played as they built the tower. In the end, they looked up at their huge tower and knew they had created something amazing together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy had a favorite pair of shoes. They were red and shiny. She loved to wear them all the time. One day, Amy went to the park to play. While she was running, she felt a twist in her foot. She looked down and saw that her shoe had a twist too. Amy was sad and didn't know what to do. A harmless little bug saw Amy and wanted to help her. The bug told Amy, "Don't be sad. I can help you fix your shoe." Amy smiled and let the bug help her. Together, they untwisted the shoe and Amy could run and play again. She was very happy and thanked the bug. The moral of the story is that even the smallest and most harmless friends can help us in big ways. We should always be kind to everyone, no matter how small they are.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went on a trip with her family. They packed their luggage with clothes, toys, and snacks. Lucy's favorite toy was a glowing ball that her mom said was perfect for the trip. On the way, they stopped at a park to play. Lucy took out her glowing ball and started to play catch with her brother, Tom. They laughed and had lots of fun. The ball was so bright that everyone at the park could see it. As it got dark, Lucy and Tom didn't want to stop playing. The glowing ball lit up the park like a big, bright star. A little boy named Sam saw the ball and asked, "Can I play too?" Lucy and Tom said, "Yes!" and they all played together. The glowing ball made their trip perfect, and Lucy made a new friend.

Lily liked to collect magazines. She had many magazines in her room. She liked to look at the pictures and the words. She wanted to learn more words. One day, she saw a new magazine at the store. It had a big picture of a dog on the cover. Lily loved dogs. She asked her mom if she could buy the magazine. "Sorry, Lily, we don't have enough money for that today," her mom said. "Maybe next time." Lily felt sad. She really wanted the magazine. She looked at the dog again. It had brown fur and black spots. It looked friendly and soft. She had an idea. She waited until her mom was busy. Then she took the magazine and hid it under her coat. She walked out of the store with her mom. She thought she was very clever. But when she got home, she found out that the magazine was not what she expected. It was not a magazine about dogs. It was a magazine about gardening. The dog was only on the cover. The rest of the pages had pictures of plants and flowers and tools. Lily did not like plants and flowers and tools. She liked dogs. And the magazine was not friendly and soft. It was rough and scratchy. It hurt her skin when she touched it. It made her itch and sneeze. Lily felt bad. She had taken something that was not hers. She had lied to her mom. She had wasted her time. She did not like the magazine at all. She decided to tell her mom the truth. She hoped her mom would not be angry. She hoped her mom would forgive her. She hoped she could find a real magazine about dogs someday.
