Molly was very excited. Today was a special day! In her bedroom, she started to unpack a box. Inside, there was a wonderful surprise - a soft toy rhinoceros. It was pink and had a big, squishy nose. Molly gave it a big hug and a kiss. She loved it so much! Molly played for hours with her soft rhinoceros. They took a pretend journey around the world, and visited lots of exciting places. Molly imagined the rhinoceros running across the grass, and laughed as it tried to climb a tree. Soon it was time to finish the day, so Molly unpacked her little rhinoceros and put it back in its box, ready for the next adventure.

Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a cold volcano. The cold volcano was very big and tall. It was friends with a little bird. They liked to talk and play together. One day, the little bird said, "I want to pick some berries for us to eat." The cold volcano said, "Yes, I would like that. Let's go find some berries!" So, the little bird flew around, and the cold volcano looked with its big eyes. Soon, the little bird found a bush with red berries. It picked the berries and brought them back to the cold volcano. They shared the berries and had a fun time eating them together. After they finished eating, the cold volcano said, "Thank you, little bird, for picking the berries for us." The little bird smiled and said, "You're welcome, cold volcano. I'm happy we are friends." And they both lived happily ever after.

One day, a little girl named Lily found a smooth stone. It was small and shiny. She liked it a lot. Lily showed the stone to her mom. Her mom said, "That's a magic stone. If you say 'shrink', it will make things smaller." Lily wanted to try it. She held the stone and said, "Shrink!" Suddenly, her toy car became very tiny. Lily was happy and surprised. She played with her tiny car and the smooth stone all day. Later, Lily's brother Tom saw her tiny car. He asked, "Why is your car so small?" Lily showed him the smooth stone and said, "This is a magic stone. It can make things smaller when I say 'shrink'." Tom touched his lip and thought for a moment. Then he said, "That's amazing! Can we play together?" Lily smiled and said, "Yes, let's play with the magic stone and my tiny car!" So, they played and had fun with the smooth stone and the tiny car all day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a boy named Tim. He had a hidden secret. Tim had a big muscle that could save people. One day, Tim saw a cat stuck in a tree. He knew he could help. Tim used his big muscle to climb the tree. He saved the cat and brought it down. The cat was happy and went home. Tim felt good about helping. He knew his hidden muscle was special. He promised to use his muscle to save others. Everyone in the town loved Tim and his big muscle. And they all lived happily ever after.

Lily and Ben are playing in the kitchen. They have a big box and some string. They want to make a fridge for their toys. "Look, Ben, I can tie the string to the box. Then we can open and close it like a real fridge," Lily says. "Good, Lily, you are very smart. Can I help you?" Ben asks. "Yes, you can. Here, take this end of the string and tie it to the other side of the box. Then we can put our toys inside and pretend they are food," Lily says. They work together and soon they have a nice fridge. They put their teddy bear, their car, their ball and their doll inside. They are very happy. "Let's play, Ben. You can be the daddy and I can be the mommy. We can make dinner for our toys," Lily says. "OK, Lily. What do we have in the fridge?" Ben asks. "We have a teddy bear sandwich, a car salad, a ball soup and a doll cake. Yummy!" Lily says. They take out their toys and pretend to eat them. They make funny noises and laugh. They have a lot of fun. They are good children.
