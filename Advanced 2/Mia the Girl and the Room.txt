Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia loved to keep her room clean. One day, Mia's mom told her, "If you keep your room clean, you can have a fun playdate tomorrow." Mia was excited and made sure her room was very clean. She put away all her toys and clothes. That night, Mia had a bad dream, a nightmare. In her nightmare, her toys came to life and made her room messy. When Mia woke up, she saw that her room was still clean. She was happy it was only a nightmare. Mia's mom came into her room and said, "You did a great job keeping your room clean! You can have your playdate today." Mia had a fun playdate with her friend, and they played all day. Mia's mom was proud of her for not letting the nightmare spoil her day. Mia learned that keeping her room clean made her happy and that nightmares couldn't stop her from having fun.

Once upon a time, there was a brown dog named Ben. He loved to play with his best friend, a little girl named Lily. They were always happy together. One day, Ben and Lily found a big piece of paper in the yard. Lily said, "Let's draw on the paper!" They took some colors and began to draw. Ben drew with his tail, and Lily drew with her hands. Suddenly, the wind came and slapped the paper away! Ben and Lily were sad. They looked everywhere for the big piece of paper, but it was gone. Then, they heard a voice. It was the wind! The wind said, "I'm sorry I took your paper. I will bring it back." The wind blew the paper back to Ben and Lily, and they were happy again. They continued to draw and play together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Amy. Amy liked to cook. One day, Amy saw an unknown bird outside her window. The bird was sad because it was hungry. Amy wanted to help the bird. Amy said to the bird, "Do not be sad, I will cook for you." She went to the kitchen and cooked some food for the bird. The bird was very happy and ate the food. Then, the bird wanted to stay with Amy. Amy had an idea. She took a hanger and put it near her window. The bird sat on the hanger and lived with Amy. They became good friends and cooked together every day. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small hut, there lived a kind and fair girl named Lily. Lily had many friends who lived near her hut. They loved to play together every day. Lily always made sure to include everyone in their games. One sunny day, Lily and her friends decided to have a picnic near the hut. They brought yummy food to share with each other. Lily made sure to include everyone's favorite treats. They laughed and played together all day long. As the sun began to set, Lily and her friends said goodbye and went back to their homes. They felt happy and loved. They knew that their friend Lily was very special because she was fair and always included everyone. And they all looked forward to more fun days together near the hut.

Once upon a time, there was a potato. His name was Toby. Toby loved to play and have fun. But one day, something very scary happened. Toby was walking in the woods when he heard a loud noise. He was so scared he ran and hid behind a tree. Then he heard a voice say, "Hello, Toby!" Toby looked around and saw a giant monster! The monster came closer and asked Toby, "Do you want to come with me?" Toby said "No!" But the monster came closer and tried to grab him with his big, scary hands. Toby ran as fast as he could, but the monster was too fast and caught him. Toby never saw his family again. The end.

Ben was a little boy who liked to play with his toys. He had a lot of cars, trains, and blocks that he could make into different shapes. He also had a cane that he found in the garage. He liked to pretend that it was a sword or a wand or a fishing rod. One day, he was playing with his cane in the living room. He was swinging it around and making noises. He did not see his mom coming in with a tray of cookies. She was trying to be calm and not spill the cookies. She wanted to surprise Ben with a treat. But Ben did not see her. He swung his cane too hard and hit the tray. The cookies flew in the air and landed on the floor. The tray made a loud noise. Ben and his mom were both scared. They both screamed. Mom dropped the tray and ran to Ben. She hugged him and asked him if he was okay. Ben was okay, but he felt bad. He said he was sorry for hitting the tray and making a mess. He said he did not mean to scare his mom. Mom said it was okay. She said she was sorry for screaming and scaring him. She said she was glad he was not hurt. She said they could clean up the mess together. She said she still loved him. Ben said he loved her too. He said he would be more careful with his cane. He said he would share his cookies with her. They smiled and hugged. They were calm and happy.
