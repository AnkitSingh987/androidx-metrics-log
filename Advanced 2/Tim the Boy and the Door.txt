Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very bored. He lived in a small house with a big door. The door had a big knob. Tim would turn the knob every day to go out and play. But today, Tim could not go out. It was raining. Tim's mom saw him being bored. She wanted to help. So, she sent a letter to a friend. The friend sent back a box. Tim opened the box and found a toy. It was a magic toy. Tim was very happy. Tim played with the magic toy. The toy could turn into anything he wanted. First, it turned into a ball. Then, it turned into a car. Just then, the toy turned into an umbrella. Tim took the umbrella, opened the door, and went outside to play. The rain was not boring anymore. And Tim was not bored anymore. He had a lot of fun playing in the rain with his magic umbrella.

Once upon a time, there was a large plane. The plane had a big job. It had to take people from one place to another. One day, the plane was getting ready to fly. It was very excited. Before the plane could go, it had to make sure it had everything. It had its wings, its wheels, and its seats for people. But, the plane forgot something important. It forgot its fuel! Without fuel, the plane could not fly. A little boy saw the plane and said, "Why are you not flying, Mr. Plane?" The plane told the boy, "I forgot my fuel." The boy helped the plane get fuel, and the plane was very happy. Now, the large plane could fly and take people where they needed to go. And the plane never forgot its fuel again.

Once upon a time, there was an old grandma who loved to wear makeup. Everywhere she went, she would always bow to those who said hello and she always had a cheerful look on her face. One day, a small three year old boy spotted her and ran up to her. He said "Hello Grandma! Can I try your makeup?" Grandma smiled and said "Of course! Let's go inside and I'll show you how it works." The little boy followed Grandma inside and she showed him how to use the makeup. He was so excited that he wanted to put it on her face. "Let me try, Grandma," he said eagerly. Grandma smiled and said, "Yes, you can do it. Just remember the most important part: bow your head and make sure the makeup covers your entire face." The little boy listened carefully and he did as Grandma said. After he was done, Grandma looked in the mirror and smiled. She said, "That's wonderful! You sure are a talented young makeup artist." The little boy beamed with pride and said, "Thank you, Grandma!" And from then on, the little boy was always by Grandma's side, helping her with her makeup and bowing to those who said hello.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He loved to play ball on the pitch with his friends. One day, a new boy named Sam came to play. Sam was very good at playing ball, and all of Tim's friends liked to play with Sam. Tim felt jealous. He did not like that his friends liked Sam more than him. So, he went up to Sam and said, "I don't want you to play with us anymore. Go away!" Sam felt sad and left the pitch. Later, Tim's friends found out what he said to Sam. They were mad at Tim and decided not to play with him anymore. Tim was all alone on the pitch, and he started to weep. He realized he made a mistake, but it was too late.

Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny lived in a big tree with her family. One day, while Sunny was playing with her friends, she saw something strange. A big yellow ball was in the sky. Sunny was very curious about the mystery ball. Sunny asked her friend, "Do you know what that yellow ball is?" Her friend said, "No, I don't know. Let's go find out!" So, Sunny and her friend flew closer to the ball. As they got closer, the ball started to disappear. Sunny and her friend were very surprised. Just when they thought the ball was gone, it came back! The yellow ball was the sun! Sunny and her friend laughed and played in the sunshine. They were happy to have solved the mystery of the big yellow ball. From that day on, Sunny and her friend enjoyed playing in the sun every day.
