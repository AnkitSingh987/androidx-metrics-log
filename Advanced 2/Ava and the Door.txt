Title: Ava and the Door

Once upon a time, a little angel named Ava lived in the sky. Ava was a very available angel, always ready to help her friends. She had big wings and a bright smile. One day, Ava saw a little girl named Lily who was sad. Lily had lost her toy. Ava flew down to the ground to talk to Lily. "Why are you sad?" asked Ava. "I lost my toy," said Lily. "Don't worry," said Ava, "I will help you find it." Ava and Lily looked everywhere for the toy. They looked under the bed, in the closet, and behind the door. Finally, they found the toy under a big tree. Lily was so happy! She hugged Ava and said, "Thank you!" Ava felt good after helping Lily. She had gained a new friend. From that day on, Ava and Lily played together every day, and they both lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a cow named Bessie. Bessie was a wild cow who lived in a big field. She had many friends like birds, dogs, and other cows. Bessie loved to run and play with her friends all day. She would jump and smile as she felt happy. One sunny day, Bessie met a little girl named Lily. Lily was wearing a pretty dress and had a big smile on her face. Bessie walked up to Lily and said, "Hi, I am Bessie. Do you want to play with me and my friends?" Lily smiled back and said, "Yes, I would love to play with you and your friends!" Bessie and Lily played all day in the big field. They ran, jumped, and laughed together. Bessie showed Lily how to be wild and free like her. Lily taught Bessie how to pick flowers and make pretty crowns. As the sun began to set, Bessie and Lily said goodbye to each other. They promised to play together again soon. Bessie and Lily smiled as they knew they had made a new best friend.

Once upon a time, there was a healthy bug named Tim. Tim was a purple bug. He liked to crawl around in the garden. He had many friends like the red bug, the blue bug, and the green bug. One day, while Tim was crawling, he found a big leaf. He thought, "I will crawl under this leaf and take a nap." But when Tim crawled under the leaf, he saw something unexpected. There was a big, shiny rock. Tim called his friends to see the rock. They all crawled under the leaf together. Suddenly, the rock started to move! The bugs were scared. But then, they saw it was not a rock. It was a big, shiny bug! The shiny bug said, "Hello, I am Sam." Tim and his friends were happy. They had a new friend, the shiny bug. They all crawled and played in the garden together. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a cheerful little town, there lived a boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy phone. He would pretend to call his friends and have fun talks with them. One day, while playing with his toy phone, it began to ring. Tim was surprised because his toy phone never rang before. Tim answered the toy phone and heard a voice on the other side. The voice said, "I am a threat, and I will take all the toys in the town." Tim was scared but he knew he had to do something to save his toys and his friends' toys. He thought of a plan to trick the threat. Tim called the threat back on his toy phone and said, "I have a big party with lots of toys, you can come and take them all." The threat was happy and agreed to come. When the threat arrived, Tim and his friends were waiting with big nets. They threw the nets on the threat and caught it. It turned out that the threat was just a little mouse who was lonely and wanted to play. Tim and his friends laughed and invited the mouse to join their playtime. From that day on, the mouse became their new friend and they all played happily together.
