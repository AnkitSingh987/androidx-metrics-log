Once upon a time, there was a bald man named Tom. Tom liked to make things with paper. One day, he made a big paper hat to wear on his head. Tom went to the park and saw a little girl named Sue. Sue saw Tom's paper hat and said, "Wow! I like your hat. Can you make one for me too?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I can make you a paper hat." Tom took out some paper and made a beautiful hat for Sue. They both wore their paper hats and played in the park all day. They were very happy and became good friends. From that day on, they always made fun things with paper together.

One day a tiny baby crawled into a lovely garden. The baby was so happy there! He crawled around the garden and it felt like he could go forever. He looked up and saw a beautiful shimmering butterfly. Its wings were so colourful and lovely. He wanted to see it up close, so he kept crawling to it. As he kept getting closer, the butterfly flew away. But then a tiny blue bird flew up to the baby. It was so small and soft and the baby thought it was the most lovely thing he had ever seen! He giggled and crawled to it. The bird played with him and made the baby so happy. When it was time to leave the garden, the baby said goodbye to his tiny bird friend. He couldn't stop grinning! He was special and he knew his visit to the garden had been full of love and adventure. He waved goodbye to the garden, crawling away with a smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to build things with his toy blocks. One day, he wanted to build something different. He thought about what to build and then he got an idea. Tim decided to build a big horn with his blocks. He put one block on top of another until it looked like a horn. Tim was very happy with his horn. He showed it to his mom and she smiled. She thought it was a great idea. Later, Tim played with his friends. They saw the horn and thought it was fun. They all wanted to build different things too. One friend built a house, another built a car, and another built a tree. They all had a great time building and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a weird toy named Mr. Wiggles. Mr. Wiggles could dance. Mia loved to play with Mr. Wiggles and watch him dance. One day, Mia told her friend, Tom, about Mr. Wiggles. "I have a toy that can dance!" Mia said. Tom was excited to see the dancing toy. Mia knew that something fun would happen when Tom saw Mr. Wiggles dance. The next day, Mia brought Mr. Wiggles to show Tom. When Tom saw Mr. Wiggles dance, his eyes grew big. He was so happy. "Wow, that's so cool!" Tom said. Mia and Tom danced with Mr. Wiggles all day long. They had so much fun. As a result, Mia and Tom became best friends. They loved to play and dance with Mr. Wiggles. From that day on, Mia, Tom, and Mr. Wiggles were always together, dancing and having fun. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty little squirrel named Timmy. Timmy loved to rush around the big tree in the park. He was always looking for peanuts, his favorite food. One sunny day, Timmy found a big peanut on the ground. He was very happy and wanted to eat it right away. But, his mommy told him to share it with his sister, Tina. Timmy did not want to share the peanut. Timmy tried to hide the peanut from Tina, but she saw him being naughty. Tina told their mommy, and mommy said they had to share the peanut. Timmy was sad, but he knew sharing was the right thing to do. Timmy and Tina shared the peanut, and they were both happy. They learned that sharing made them feel good. From that day on, Timmy and Tina always shared their peanuts and played together in the park.
