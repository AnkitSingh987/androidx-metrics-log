There was a bald painter. He smiled all the time and loved his work. Every day he painted pictures with bright colors on the walls of buildings. One day the painter was walking through the park when he saw a little toddler. The toddler had a frown on her face. The painter stopped and knelt down to her. He smiled and asked her what was wrong? The toddler said she was sad because she had hurt her knee. The painter laughed and said he could make it better. So he brought out a big bucket of paint and some brushes. He mixed the colors until he found the perfect shade to match the little girl's dress. Then he painted a big yellow sun and a few dancing stars on her knee. The toddler laughed and smiled. She gave the painter a hug and thanked him for making her feel better. The painter smiled and waved goodbye until the little girl was out of sight. He was so happy that he had brought a smile to her face and he continued on his way, still smiling.

Ben and Lily were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a big van in the street. It was red and shiny and had many buttons and wheels. Ben and Lily wanted to see what was inside the van, so they opened the door and climbed in. The van was full of boxes and bags and tools. Ben and Lily started to touch everything and make noises. They found a rope and decided to tie it around their waists. They thought it was fun to pretend they were explorers in a jungle. They did not see the man who owned the van coming back. The man was angry when he saw Ben and Lily in his van. He shouted at them and told them to get out. He said they were stupid and naughty and could have hurt themselves or broken his things. He untied the rope from their waists and pushed them out of the van. He slammed the door and drove away. Ben and Lily were scared and sad. They ran back to their house and told their mom what happened. Their mom hugged them and said they were lucky they were not hurt. She said they should never go inside a stranger's van without asking. She said they should be careful and respectful with other people's things. She said they should learn from their mistake and not do it again. Ben and Lily said they were sorry and they understood. They said they would not be stupid and naughty again. They said they would play with their own toys and not bother anyone. Their mom smiled and said she loved them. She gave them a cookie and a kiss and told them to go and have fun.

It was a beautiful sunny day and Jim was outside. He was in the garden and wanted to tie a knot. He had a long piece of string and he was ready to make a knot. He asked his mum, "Mum, can you help me tie a knot?" His mum looked at him with a smile and said "Of course I can help you, Jim. You grab the string and hold it like this and I will do the knot." Jim watched his mum tie a beautiful knot. Just then, he spotted something in the sky. It was a comet! It was so bright and colourful and was coming closer to the ground. Jim and his mum watched the comet as it flew closer. Suddenly, Jim could smell something smelly in the air. It was like burning stinky socks! The smell was very strong. Jim and his mum were so confused. What was that smelly comet? They watched the smelly comet float away until they couldn't see it anymore. Then they went back inside the house and the funny smelly comet was soon forgotten.

One day, a clever cat named Penny went for a walk. She liked to look for fun things to play with. Penny loved the sun on her face and the grass under her feet. While walking, Penny found a big box. She did not know what was inside. She tried to open it, but it was stuck. Penny began to panic. She did not want to leave the box, but she did not know how to open it. Just then, a friendly dog came over to help. He pushed the box with his nose, and it opened! Penny and the dog were surprised to see a lot of toys inside. They played together all day and became best friends. Penny learned that sometimes, unexpected things can lead to new friends and fun adventures.
