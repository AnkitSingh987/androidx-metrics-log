Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was a bit nervous to go to the park. She had never been there before. But she really wanted to play and meet new friends. When Sue got to the park, she saw a boy named Tim. Tim was playing with a ball. Sue walked over to say hi, but she tripped and fell. Her knee hurt a lot. She felt very sad. Then, something unexpected happened. A talking bird flew down and said, "Don't be sad, Sue! I can help you feel better!" The bird gave Sue a magic band-aid for her knee. Sue felt better and was happy. She played with Tim and the talking bird, and they all became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was an adventure. A purple club was floating in a river. A brave girl took a deep breath and jumped into the cold water. She grabbed the club, and was about to rescue it, when a big fish came to get it. The fish was very strong and pulled the club away from her. The little girl cried for help, but no one could help her. She cried and cried, but it was too late. The fish was swimming away with the club in her mouth. The girl was so sad about losing the club. She thought, "If only I had been faster, I could have rescued my beloved purple club." The little girl never got her club back.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very persistent, and she always wanted to know secret things. One day, she saw a door that was locked. She knocked very loudly and shouted, “Let me in! I want to see the secret inside!” But no one ever answered her. Jane kept on shouting and knocking, but the door would not open. She was so determined that she tried every day. Finally, a kind old woman saw the little girl. She said, “Jane, it is wrong to shout and ask for secrets. Secrets are special things that should not be shared with everyone. Instead of shouting, why don’t you be patient and find someone who will share their secrets with you?” Jane listened to the old woman’s advice. She stopped shouting and started asking people politely if they would share their secrets with her. Soon she had many wonderful secrets to share with her friends. Jane learned that the best way to get secret things is to be patient and kind. She remembered this lesson for the rest of her life.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She lived in a small house with her mommy and daddy. One day, Lily went outside to play. While she was playing, she saw something shiny under a big rock. She moved the rock and peeked under it. Lily found a small box with gold inside! She was very happy and ran to show her mommy. "Mommy, look what I found!" Lily said. Her mommy looked at the box and said, "Oh, Lily, this gold belongs to our neighbor, Mr. Brown. He must have lost it. We should give it back." Lily felt sorry for Mr. Brown and agreed to return the gold. They went to Mr. Brown's house, and he was very thankful. From that day on, Lily and Mr. Brown became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big, strong, and reliable horse named Max. He lived on a farm with a kind girl named Lily. Lily loved Max very much. They played together every day. One day, Lily wanted to teach Max a new trick. She tried and tried, but Max could not do it. Lily let out a big sigh. She felt sad. But she did not give up. She knew Max could learn the trick. The next day, Lily tried again to teach Max the new trick. This time, Max did it! Lily was so happy. They both smiled and played together. Lily knew that her reliable friend Max could do anything if they worked together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy wanted to play. She went outside and saw the sky. It was very cold. Lucy felt the air freeze around her. She put on a big coat to stay warm. Lucy looked up and saw the ceiling of her house. She saw a bird on the ceiling. The bird was honest and nice. The bird said, "Hello, Lucy!" Lucy said, "Hello, bird!" They talked and laughed. Lucy and the bird played all day. They had fun and were happy. When the sun went down, Lucy said, "Bye, bird!" The bird said, "Bye, Lucy!" Lucy went back inside her warm house.
