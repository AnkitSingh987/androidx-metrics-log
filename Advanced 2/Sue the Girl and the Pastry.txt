One day, a girl named Sue went to the shop. She wanted to buy a pastry. She saw many pastries, but one was unknown to her. It was big and round. Sue thought it looked tasty. She bought the unknown pastry and went home. At home, Sue's mom saw the pastry. She said, "I will provide a plate for your pastry." Sue put the pastry on the plate. She was very happy. She took a big bite of the pastry. But something unexpected happened. Inside the pastry, Sue found a note. The note had a big twist. It said, "You win a fun trip!" Sue and her mom were very surprised. They never thought a pastry could provide a trip. They laughed and got ready for their fun trip.

Once upon a time, there was a big tiger. The tiger lived in a wide forest with many trees. The tiger had many friends like the monkey, the elephant, and the bird. One day, the tiger wanted to dance. He asked his friends to dance with him. They all said yes. The monkey climbed down from the tree, the elephant came from the river, and the bird flew down from the sky. They all danced together. The tiger danced fast, the monkey danced on his hands, the elephant danced with his big legs, and the bird danced in the air. They had so much fun dancing in the wide forest. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Suzy. Suzy had a scary nightmare one night. In her dream, she saw a big, dark room with a locked door. Suzy was scared, but she knew she had to be brave. Suzy found a key to unlock the door. She put the key in and turned it. The door opened and the scary nightmare went away. Suzy felt happy and safe again. From that day on, Suzy knew that she could face her fears and unlock her scary nightmares. She was a brave little girl and never let her nightmares scare her again.

Karen was a little girl who lived in the woods. She loved the seasons of the year and spent a lot of time outdoors. One day, Karen decided to explore the forest, but it had turned cold and the fierce wind made it difficult to walk. Karen stopped and turned to her mom who was standing next to her. "Mommy, can I go further?" she asked. "No, it's too cold and the wind is too fierce," her mom replied. "Let's go back home now." Karen obeyed her mom and they returned home. Soon, the season changed to spring and the fierce winds had turned into a gentle breeze. Now, Karen could explore the forest as much as she wanted.

Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a bear. The bear loved to mark trees with his big paws. He would walk around the forest, marking the trees he liked best. One day, as the bear was marking a tree, he saw a little girl. The little girl was holding something shiny and expensive. The bear was curious and wanted to see what it was. "Hi, little girl," said the bear. "What is that shiny thing you have?" "Hello, bear!" said the little girl. "This is my expensive necklace. My mom gave it to me for my birthday." The bear and the little girl became friends. They played together in the forest, and the bear showed the little girl how to mark trees. They had lots of fun, and the little girl's expensive necklace sparkled in the sun.

One day, a boy and a girl went for a walk. They saw a big wreck in the park. The wreck was cold and made of metal. The boy and girl were very curious. The boy said, "Let's go near the wreck and see what it is." The girl agreed, and they walked closer. As they got near, the boy pointed at the wreck and said, "Look! There's a door." The girl opened the door and, to their surprise, they found a big room full of toys! They played and had fun, but then they heard a voice. It said, "Hello, I am the toy fairy, and this is my secret toy room. You can take one toy each." The boy and girl were very happy and said, "Thank you, toy fairy!" They took their toys and went home, amazed by their unexpected adventure.
