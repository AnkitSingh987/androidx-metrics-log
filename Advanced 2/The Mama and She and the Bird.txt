Once upon a time, there was a mama and she had a baby. Mama and baby were in the park one day, when Mama heard a noise. Mama didn't know what it was, so she made a guess. She thought it might be a noisy bird, so she looked up in the tree. But it wasn't a bird, it was Papa! Papa was playing an easy game and he was having lots of fun. Mama waved at him and Papa waved back. He had been playing hide-and-seek and he was very good at it. So Mama, baby and Papa went home. They all had a big hug, and Mama said, "Let's play a game tomorrow". Papa said, "Yippee!", and the baby smiled. They all had a wonderful time together guessing and playing easy games.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim had a secret. He found a weird ice cream cone. It was not like other ice creams. This one could talk! The ice cream said, "Hello, Tim! I can be any flavor you want, but you must eat me fast, or I will melt!" Tim was very happy. He loved ice cream. He wished for a yummy chocolate flavor. Tim ate the ice cream very fast, so it did not melt. He told his friends about the weird ice cream cone. They all wanted to find one too. But it was Tim's special secret. And he smiled every time he thought about it.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to ride his bike. But he had a fragile helmet. His mom told him, "Tim, we need to supply you with a new helmet. Your old one is not safe." Tim and his mom went to the store to buy a new helmet. They found a strong helmet that would keep his head safe. Tim was happy with his new helmet. One day, Tim was riding his bike with his friends. They saw a big hill and wanted to go down it. Tim remembered that his mom said his new helmet would keep him safe. He wore his helmet and went down the hill. Tim had fun and was safe because of his new helmet. The moral of the story is to always wear a helmet when riding a bike to stay safe.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely frog named Fred. Fred lived near a pond. He liked to eat mosquitoes. One day, a new mosquito came to the pond. Her name was Molly. Fred said to Molly, "I like to eat mosquitoes. Can I eat you?" Molly was scared. She said, "Please don't eat me, Fred. I am lonely too. Can we be friends instead?" Fred thought about it and then said, "Okay, let's be friends." Fred and Molly became good friends. They played together every day. They were not lonely anymore. They learned that being friends was better than eating each other. And they lived happily ever after. The moral of the story is: it is better to be friends than to hurt each other.

One day there was a jolly angel. She wanted to go for a walk. She didn't know where to go. She asked the sun, "Where shall I go?" But the sun couldn't guess. So the angel asked the trees, "Where should I go?" But they couldn't guess either. Then the angel had an idea. She decided to go and ask the birds. She flew over to them and asked, "Where should I go?" And the birds had a guess. "Why don't you fly over to the pond?" they said. So the jolly angel flew over to the pond. When she got there she saw some fish swimming around. It was so much fun! She splashed and played in the water until it was time to go home. It was a jolly day for the angel! She was so glad that the birds had helped her with their guess. They were her very special friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind boy named Tom. Tom had a violin that he loved very much. He played the violin every day and made beautiful music. He was a compassionate boy, always helping his friends and family. One day, Tom was playing his violin in the park. A little bird came and sat on his shoulder. The bird started to sing along with Tom's violin. This was unexpected, but Tom liked it. He trusted the little bird and they became friends. Tom and the bird played music together every day. But one day, the bird did not sing. Instead, it played a tiny flute! Tom was surprised, but he liked the new sound. They played music together with the violin and the flute, and everyone loved their songs even more.
