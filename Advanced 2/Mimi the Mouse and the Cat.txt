Once upon a time, in a big maze, there was a small mouse named Mimi. Mimi wanted to find her way out of the maze, but she felt lost. She thought the maze was stupid because it was hard to find her way. One day, Mimi met a big cat named Tom. Tom said, "I can help you get out of the maze, Mimi." Mimi was scared of Tom, but she wanted to get out of the maze. So, she said, "Okay, Tom, please help me." Tom and Mimi started to walk through the maze together. They took many turns, but they still could not see the way out. Mimi said, "Tom, this is hard. I do not like this stupid maze." Tom agreed, but he told Mimi not to give up. Finally, they saw a bright light at the end of the maze. Mimi was so happy, and she thanked Tom for his help. They both walked out of the maze together, and Mimi was free at last.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the farm with her mom. They saw many animals and plants. Sue liked the farm a lot. She saw a field with corn. Sue picked some corn and put it in a bag. Sue saw a boring old man sitting near the corn field. She walked up to him and said, "Hi, I am Sue. I like your corn." The old man smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue. Did you know that this corn can talk?" Sue did not believe him. She thought he was joking. The old man took a piece of corn and said, "Hello, corn. Please talk to Sue." To Sue's surprise, the corn said, "Hi, Sue. I am corn. Nice to meet you." Sue was so happy. She had never seen talking corn before. She told her mom about it, but her mom did not mention the talking corn to anyone. Sue and her mom went home with a big secret. They promised to visit the talking corn again soon.

Once there was a clumsy rhinoceros. He was always bumping into things and making a mess. One day he was walking in the forest and he stumbled over a large root. He fell to the ground and was very embarrassed. The rhinoceros saw a rabbit hopping nearby. He was so embarrassed that he bowed his head in shame. But the rabbit smiled and said "It's OK, everyone makes mistakes." The rhinoceros was so happy that the rabbit was nice to him. He thanked the rabbit and asked if there was anything he could do to repay him. The rabbit asked for an adventure and the rhinoceros was excited. They went on a long journey and visited many interesting places. The rhinoceros was clumsy still, but he was having so much fun. Eventually, the sun set and he knew it was time to go home. The rhinoceros thanked the rabbit again and bowed to say goodbye. The rabbit waved and said "Hey, happy adventures until we meet again!" And with that, the rhinoceros said goodbye to his new friend.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there was a big bell. The bell would ring every day to tell the people when it was time to eat. One day, the bell stopped ringing. The people did not know when to eat. They were sad and hungry. A kind man named Tom wanted to help. He went to the bell and saw that it was broken. He said, "I will fix the bell so we can eat again!" Tom worked hard to fix the bell. The people watched and hoped. Finally, Tom fixed the bell. But when it rang, it made a funny sound. Instead of a loud "DING," it went "meow!" The people laughed. "A cat is inside the bell!" they said. They took the cat out and the bell rang loudly again. The people ate and were happy. The cat found a new home with Tom, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim went for a walk with his mom. They went to the park to have fun. Tim saw a big red button on the ground. He was scared to touch it. "Mom, what is that?" he asked. His mom said, "It's just a button, don't be scared. Let's observe it." They looked at the button and saw it was shiny and pretty. Tim's mom told him that it was okay to pick it up. Tim picked up the button and put it in his pocket. They played at the park all day. When it was time to go home, Tim remembered the button. He showed it to his mom, and she smiled. "See, it was just a button. There was nothing to be scared of," she said. Tim was happy he found the button. He learned that sometimes, it's okay to be scared, but it's also important to observe and learn. From that day on, Tim was not scared of buttons anymore.
