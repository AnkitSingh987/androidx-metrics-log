Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to help her mom cook in the kitchen. One hot day, they decided to make a yummy cake together. As they mixed the cake batter, Lily's mom said, "Lily, can you please open the door? It's very hot in here." Lily went to the door and opened it wide. A cool breeze came in, making the kitchen feel nice. When the cake was ready, they put it in the oven to cook. They both waited and watched. Soon, the cake was done. Lily and her mom took it out of the oven and let it cool. They were very happy and proud of the cake they made together.

One day, a little cat found a pair of sunglasses. The cat liked the sunglasses a lot. She thought they were very cool. She wore the sunglasses and went to play with her friends. She felt very happy. The cat and her friends played a game. They had to number the toys from one to ten. The cat was having fun, but she could not see well with the sunglasses. She made a mistake while numbering the toys. Her friends laughed at her. The cat felt ashamed. She took off the sunglasses and tried again. This time, she numbered the toys right. Her friends clapped and cheered for her. The cat learned that sometimes it is better to be able to see well than to look cool.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a hero named Tom. Tom was a reliable friend to all the animals. Every day, he would feed the animals in the town. The animals loved Tom very much. One day, a little bird came to Tom and said, "Tom, my friend, I am hungry. Can you feed me?" Tom smiled and said, "Of course, little bird!" He gave the bird some food and the bird was very happy. As Tom continued to feed the animals, more and more of them came to him. They knew they could always count on their hero Tom. The town was full of happy animals, and they all lived together in peace.

Once upon a time, in a big nation, there was a high hill. On top of the hill, there lived a little bird named Blink. Blink had many friends and they liked to play together. One day, while Blink was playing with his friends, a big wind came. It blew all of them far away from the high hill. Blink was very sad and he said, "Oh no, where are my friends? I miss them so much." Blink decided to look for his friends. He flew high in the sky and searched everywhere. Suddenly, he saw something strange. It was a big, round thing that looked like a big fruit. Blink went closer and saw that it was a big balloon! Inside the balloon, he found all his friends. They were all happy and said, "Surprise! We wanted to give you a fun ride in the sky!" Blink was very happy to see his friends, and they all had a fun time flying high in the sky in the big balloon. From that day on, they had many more adventures together.

Once upon a time there was a very nice teacher. She decided to go on a special journey on a Subway to teach everyone a rare skill. The teacher sat down and the Subway started to move. She smiled as she heard the Subway moving under the ground. When the teacher arrived she saw a classroom full of people. She taught them how to use the rare skill she carried. She made everyone laugh and smile as she taught. At the end of the day, everyone said goodbye to the teacher as she left the Subway. They were all very happy that she had come to teach them, and they thanked her for taking the time to come and teach them the rare skill!

One sunny Sunday, a little boy named Tim felt very bored. He did not have any fun toys to play with. He sat on the floor and looked around his room for something to do. Tim saw his dog, Spot, lying on the floor. He had an idea! He walked over to Spot and began to rub his soft fur. "Do you like that, Spot?" Tim asked. Spot wagged his tail and licked Tim's face. Tim laughed and continued to rub Spot's fur. As they played together, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim and Spot having fun. "You two are so cute!" she said. Tim smiled and said, "I'm not bored anymore, Mom! Spot is my best friend!" They all laughed and enjoyed the rest of their Sunday together.
