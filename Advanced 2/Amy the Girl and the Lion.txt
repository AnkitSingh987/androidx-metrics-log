Title: Amy the Girl and the Lion

One day, a little girl named Amy went outside to play. She saw a big, red tomato in her garden. Amy liked tomatoes, so she picked it up. As she walked, she skipped with joy because she was happy. Amy saw her friend, Tim, who was playing with a toy lion. The lion was fierce, but Tim was not scared. Amy showed Tim the tomato she found. Tim looked at the tomato and said, "Wow, that is a big tomato!" Amy and Tim decided to share the tomato. They went to the kitchen and asked Tim's mom to cut it. They ate the tomato and it was very tasty. They were happy they could share the big, red tomato.

Once upon a time, there was a big cave. A bear lived in the cave. The bear was careful. He liked to keep his cave clean and safe. One day, a little bird flew into the cave. The bear did not own the bird. The bird was lost. The bear wanted to help the bird find its way home. They looked for the bird's home together. As they looked, they found a hidden door in the cave. They opened the door and saw a big treasure. The bird was not lost, it was a magic bird! The bird wanted to show the bear the treasure. They were both happy and became best friends.

Once there was a little boy named Jack. He was feeling very troubled and he didn't know why. So one day, Jack's mom decided that he needed a shower. "Jack," his mom said. "Come on, let's get a shower!" Jack felt scared and confused. He didn't understand why he had to get a shower. "But why?" he asked. His mom smiled and said, "Because it will make you feel better. You'll feel better after a nice shower." So Jack and his mom went to the bathroom and got in the shower. Jack was still a little scared, but he trusted his mom, so he stayed with her. The shower was warm and soothing, and soon Jack was feeling much calmer. When they were finished, Jack was feeling more relaxed and content. He had gotten a much-needed shower, and it had helped him feel better.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the beach with his mom. The sun was bright and the sand was warm. Tim liked to play in the sand and make sand castles. His mom said, "Tim, don't go too far. Stay close to me." Tim played in the sand and found a big shell. He thought it would be perfect for his sand castle. As he was walking slow back to his mom, he saw a big rock. Tim had an idea. He would lie the rock on top of his sand castle to make it even better. As Tim picked up the rock, he heard a small voice say, "Please don't take me away!" Tim was surprised. The rock could talk! It was a turtle, not a rock! Tim said, "I'm sorry, I didn't know you were a turtle." The turtle smiled and said, "That's okay. Can I help you with your sand castle?" Tim and the turtle became friends and they built the best sand castle ever together.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Sue had a big, red balloon. It was her original balloon, and she loved it very much. She would play with it all day, and it made her very happy. One day, Sue took her balloon to the park. She ran and jumped with her balloon, having lots of fun. Then, she saw a big tree and wanted to see how high her balloon could go. So, she let it go up, up, up into the sky. But something unexpected happened. A big bird saw Sue's balloon and thought it was a yummy treat. The bird flew fast and gave the balloon a big slap with its wing. Pop! The balloon burst into tiny pieces, and Sue was very sad. Her original balloon was gone, and she went home with a frown on her face.

Once upon a time, there was a competitive dancer. She loved to dance and was always practice hard, so that she could be the best dancer. One day, she was training with her partner when she realised that she had lost her special tutu. It was her favourite tutu and she needed it for the competition in two days! Sad and worried, she asked her friend: "What will I do?!" Her friend said: "Don't worry! Just focus on practicing and maybe the tutu will come back." So the dancer kept practicing, and on the day of the competition, her tutu magically floated down from the sky. She gasped in wonder and put on the tutu, ready to show the world what a competitive dancer she was. She won the competition and enjoyed her moment of glory. She knew that hard work and focus can make any dream come true!
