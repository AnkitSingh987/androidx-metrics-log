Once upon a time, there was a clever monkey who lived in a big tree. He loved to hang from the branches and play all day. One day, he found a spade near the tree. He thought it was a fun toy, so he took it. The clever monkey showed the spade to his friend, the bird. The bird said, "Be careful with that! It can be dangerous!" But the monkey did not listen. He wanted to play with the spade and dig holes in the ground. The clever monkey started digging holes under the tree. He dug and dug, making a big mess. Suddenly, the tree began to shake. It fell down with a loud crash! The clever monkey and the bird lost their home. The moral of the story is: always listen to your friends, and be careful with things you don't understand.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little deer. The deer had many friends who liked to play and run with her. They had lots of fun together. One day, while they were playing, they saw a big, scary thing. It had long legs and big eyes. The deer and her friends were very scared. They did not know what to do. Then, a kind mama deer came to help them. She hugged the little deer and her friends. The mama deer told them, "Do not be scared. I am here to help you." The scary thing went away, and the little deer and her friends were happy again. They knew that a hug from a mama deer could make everything better.

Once upon a time, there was a barber. He made sure everyone's hair was cut nicely. One day, he had a very special customer. It was an icy day and the customer wanted to wrap a scarf around his neck. The barber said, "There's a nice, warm scarf for you. It will keep you nice and warm!" The customer thanked him and asked, "Are you sure it's warm enough?" The barber smiled and replied, "Yes, it's made of soft, warm wool and I just wrapped it really tightly." The customer was delighted, "Oh thank you! It looks beautiful!" The barber smiled as the customer wrapped himself up in the scarf and happily left the shop. The barber laughed to himself as he thought how warm and cozy his customer must have felt on the icy day.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. The tide was high, and the waves were big. Fin liked to play in the water and spray it with his tail. He had a lot of fun with his friends. But then, a big mean shark came. The shark wanted to play too, but he played too rough. He scared Fin and his friends. They did not want to play with the shark. The shark was sad and did not understand why no one wanted to play with him. Fin had an idea. He said to the shark, "If you play nice, we can all play together." The shark agreed, and they all played in the high tide, spraying water and laughing. The shark learned how to be a good friend, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a big, heavy bus. This bus loved to work and help people. Every day, the bus would drive around the town and pick up people who needed to go places. One day, a little boy named Tim got on the bus. He was going to the store with his mom. Tim said, "Mom, this bus is so heavy!" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, Tim, it is a very big bus. It works hard to take us where we need to go." As the bus drove around town, it stopped to pick up more people. The bus was happy to work and help everyone. At the end of the day, the bus went to sleep, ready to work again tomorrow.
