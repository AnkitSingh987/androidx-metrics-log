Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big question. He wanted to know how to win a medal. He saw his big sister with a shiny medal and he was envious. Tim wanted a medal just like hers. Tim asked his sister, "How did you get that medal?" His sister smiled and said, "I won it in a race. If you want a medal, you have to be fast." Tim was excited. He wanted to be fast, too. He practiced running every day. One day, there was a race for little kids. Tim joined the race. He ran as fast as he could. He came in first place! Tim won a shiny medal. He was so happy. Now, he had a medal just like his sister.

Once there was a little girl who was very, very curious. She wanted to learn all about the world. One day, her mommy said, "You can't go there! It's not safe!" But the little girl wanted to go. So, she asked her mommy, "Please, can I go?" Her mommy said, "No! But I'll permit you to go to the garden." The little girl said, "Yay!" The little girl jumped and ran to the garden. She smelled the flowers and heard the birds. She even saw a butterfly that was all rainbow. The garden was so beautiful, and then the little girl heard something. It was coming from the lung of a very silly donkey! The donkey was so crazy! She had so much fun with him. When the little girl went to bed, she had all kinds of wonderful dreams about the crazy donkey and the beautiful garden.

Once upon a time, there was a lively town. In this town, people loved to have fun. They liked to dance, sing, and play all day. One day, the town decided to have a big parade. Everyone was very excited. The parade was full of colors and sounds. There were big balloons and people dressed in fun costumes. As they danced down the street, the sun was shining bright. The people in the parade were getting very hot. A kind man saw that the people were hot. He had an idea. He took a big bucket of water and threw it on the people. They were all soaked! They laughed and kept dancing in the parade. The lively town had a great day, and everyone was happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to collect bows. She had many bows in her room. Some were big, some were small, and some were very fragile. Her favorite bow was a fragile one with pretty colors. One day, Lily went to the park with her mom. She wore her favorite fragile bow in her hair. While playing, she met a boy named Tim. Tim saw Lily's bow and said, "Wow, I like your bow! Can I see it?" Lily said, "Yes, but be careful. It is fragile." Tim held the bow, but it slipped from his hands. The bow fell and broke. Lily was sad, and Tim felt bad. Tim had an idea. He ran home and came back with a big box. Inside the box were many bows! Tim said, "I'm sorry I broke your bow. I collect bows too. You can have any bow you want!" Lily picked a new bow and smiled. From that day, Lily and Tim became best friends, and they collected bows together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl. She was three years old and loved to play outside. Every day she went to the park with her mom. Her favorite thing to do was go on the slide. She loved to slide down and feel the wind on her cheeks. She was so happy when she was on the slide! One day, as they were walking to the park, the little girl saw something unique. It was a special slide that looked much bigger than the usual one. She asked her mom if she could go on the slide. Her mom said yes, so the little girl was so excited. But when she went to the slide, the little girl was confused. She had never seen a slide like this before. She didn't understand how it worked. After a few minutes, her mom explained that she had to sit at the top and then slide down. The little girl understood what her mom said and she felt so proud. She climbed to the top of the slide and sat down. She pushed off and slid down - she had so much fun! It was the most unique slide she had ever gone on. From then on, the little girl went to the park every day and took turns going on the special slide. She loved it and looked forward to it every day.
