Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to bake with her mom. They would make yummy treats together. Lily liked to be original and try new things. One day, Lily and her mom decided to bake a big cake. They measured the cake to be one inch tall. Lily was so excited to see her cake grow in the oven. She knew it would be the best cake ever. When the cake was ready, Lily and her mom took it out of the oven. It smelled so good! They put icing on the cake and added some fun decorations. Lily was proud of her original cake. She shared it with her family, and they all loved it.

Once upon a time, there was a unique toy phone. It loved to ring all day. The toy phone had a friend named Paper. They played together every day. One day, the toy phone rang in a funny way. It was not the same ring as before. Paper and the toy phone were confused. They did not know what was happening. They decided to find out why the ring changed. As they looked around, they found a little button on the toy phone. Paper touched the button, and the toy phone rang in its old way again. They were happy to find the button. They played together and laughed, enjoying the old ring sound. From that day on, Paper and the toy phone knew they could change the ring if they wanted to.

One day, a boy named Tim and his dad wanted to build a big fridge. They wanted to make it tough so it would not break. They worked hard all day to make the fridge. When they finished, Tim's friend, Sam, came over. Sam looked at the fridge and said, "Wow, that's a tough fridge! Can I help you build one too?" Tim and his dad agreed to help Sam build his own tough fridge. While they built the fridge, Tim and Sam learned that working together made the job easier. They had fun and finished the fridge quickly. In the end, they learned that helping each other is important and makes things better for everyone.

One day, a little fish swam in a big pond. The fish felt very important because it was the fastest fish in the pond. It liked to swim fast and play with all the other fish. One day, the fish met a big, wise fish. The wise fish said, "You need to relax sometimes. It is important to rest too." The little fish did not know how to relax, so the wise fish showed it how to float and rest. The little fish learned to relax and rest. It still liked to swim fast and play, but now it knew that it was important to relax too. All the other fish in the pond learned to relax, and they were all happy.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a clever kitten named Fluffy. Fluffy loved to play with her ball of yarn. One day, Fluffy could not find her ball of yarn. She looked everywhere, but it was gone. Fluffy went to her friend, Bunny. "Bunny, did you see my yarn?" she asked. Bunny shook her head. "No, Fluffy. I did not see it." Fluffy was sad, but she did not complain. She knew she had to keep looking. Finally, Fluffy found her ball of yarn under a big tree. She was so happy! Fluffy and Bunny played with the yarn all day long. And they never complained again.

Once upon a time, there was a big fish named Bob. Bob loved to dive deep in the water. One day, Bob saw a small fish named Tim who was sad. Tim was sad because he lost his ball in a deep pitch. Bob wanted to help Tim because he was an honest fish. Bob and Tim went to the deep pitch to find the ball. They looked and looked, but they could not find it. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wave came and pushed them out of the deep pitch. After the wave, Bob and Tim saw a big crab holding the ball. The crab was very nice and gave the ball back to Tim. Tim was so happy that he had his ball back. Bob and Tim became best friends and played with the ball every day.
