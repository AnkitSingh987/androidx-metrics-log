Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a big, soft bed with her favorite sheet. The sheet had pretty flowers on it. Lucy loved her sheet very much. One night, it rained a lot. Lucy's room got wet from the rain. She woke up and saw her sheet was wet too. She felt sad and started to cry. Her mommy heard her crying and came to her room. Mommy said, "Don't cry, Lucy. I will comfort you." She took the wet sheet and gave Lucy a new, dry one. Lucy smiled and hugged her mommy. Then, they both said goodnight and Lucy went back to sleep. She was happy and cozy with her new sheet.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a favorite shoe that she wore all the time. One day, she went to the park to play with her friends. While playing, she left her shoe in the sandbox. She went home and could not find her shoe. Lily was sad. She asked her mom, "Mom, where is my shoe?" Her mom said, "You must have left it at the park. Let's go and find it." They went back to the park and found an honest boy named Tim. He saw the shoe and knew it was not his. He gave the shoe back to Lily. She said, "Thank you, Tim!" Lily learned that it is important to be honest and to take care of her things.

One day, a little girl wanted to go for an adventure. She wanted to explore the wild outdoors, so she took her wagon and crawled outside. Along the way, she found many wild animals, from zebras to tigers. She was so excited she ran around and clapped her hands. Suddenly, the little girl heard a deep growl. She looked down and saw a wild bear. Terrified, she froze. "Don't worry. I won't hurt you," said the bear. The little girl didn't know what to do, so she bravely opened her wagon and offered the bear some food. The bear thanked her with a smile then crawled away. The little girl felt relieved, but as she walked away, she heard the bear growl one more time. She quickly turned around and saw the bear crawl back towards her with a furious face. The bear lunged towards her, and the little girl screamed in fear. She tried to run away, but the bear was too fast. The little girl never explored the wild outdoors again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy who loved to help his mom and dad. One day, Tim's dad asked him to clean the post outside their house. Tim was very excited and happy to help. Tim took a big cloth and a bucket of water. He started to clean the post. While he was cleaning, a silly bird came and sat on the post. The bird looked at Tim and said, "You are stupid for cleaning this post. It will just get dirty again." Tim didn't listen to the bird and kept cleaning. When Tim finished cleaning the post, it looked shiny and new. His mom and dad were very proud of him. They knew that Tim was not stupid, but a very smart and helpful boy. And they all lived happily ever after.
