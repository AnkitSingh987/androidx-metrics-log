One day, a little girl named Lily found a flower in the garden. The flower was very pretty and important. Lily loved the flower and wanted to keep it safe. Lily showed the flower to her friend, Tom. "Look at my flower, Tom!" she said. Tom liked the flower too. He wanted to touch it. "Can I rub the flower?" he asked. Lily thought for a moment and said, "No, Tom. If you rub the flower, it might break." Tom was sad, but he listened to Lily. They played together and had fun. Later, they saw a big wind blow through the garden. Many flowers were broken and gone. But Lily's important flower was still safe because they did not rub it. The moral of the story is to be careful with things that are important to you and to listen to your friends when they give you good advice.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was playing in her room. She felt anxious because she had a big question. She wanted to ask her mom, but her mom was busy in the kitchen. So, Lucy went to her brother Tom and asked, "Tom, can you answer my question?" Tom was happy to help. He said, "Yes, Lucy, I can try to answer your question." Lucy asked, "Why do we have curtains on our windows?" Tom thought for a moment and said, "Curtains help keep the sun out, and they give us some privacy." As they were talking, a big gust of wind blew open the window and the curtain flew up in the air. To their surprise, they saw a little bird sitting on the window sill. The bird looked lost and scared. Lucy and Tom decided to help the bird find its way back home. After they helped the bird, Lucy and Tom felt very happy. The unexpected visit from the bird taught them that helping others can make you feel good. The moral of the story is: always be kind and help others when you can.

One day, a lazy cat named Tom was lying by the door. He did not want to go out and play. His friend, a small dog named Max, came up to him. Max said, "Tom, come play with me outside!" Tom did not want to move. He said, "No, Max, I want to stay by the door. I am too lazy to play today." Max was sad. He wanted to play with Tom, but Tom would not move. Max thought of a plan. He ran outside and found a big, red ball. Max brought the ball to Tom and said, "Look, Tom! I have a fun ball to play with!" Tom looked at the ball and thought it was fun. He did not want to be lazy anymore. Tom got up and went outside with Max. They played with the red ball all day and had lots of fun together.

One day, a nosy cat named Tom went for a walk. He liked to see what was going on in his town. As he walked, he saw a big bag of mail. It was very heavy. Tom wanted to help carry the mail, but he was too small. Tom saw a nice lady named Jane walking by. He said, "Please help me carry this mail. It is too heavy for me." Jane smiled and said, "Of course, I will help you, Tom." Together, they picked up the big bag of mail. As they carried the mail, they began to feel very happy. They were helping people get their letters and packages. Tom and Jane walked all around town, giving the mail to everyone. At the end of the day, they were tired but felt very good. They were happy to help their friends and neighbors.

Once upon a time, there was a cheerful girl named Lily. She loved to dream and play with her friends. One day, she found a letter under her bed. She was very excited! Lily opened the letter and it said, "Hello, Lily! I am a magic friend. I want to play with you. Say 'please come' and I will be there." Lily said, "Please come!" and a big, friendly bear appeared. They played all day and had so much fun. At bedtime, Lily asked the bear, "Will you stay with me?" The bear said, "I can't stay, but I will be in your dreams." Lily was sad, but she went to bed and had a happy dream about her new magic bear friend.
