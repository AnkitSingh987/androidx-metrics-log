Once upon a time, there were two friends called Jenn and Tess. They both had a lot of fun playing together every day. One day, Jenn asked Tess, “do you want to try something different?” “Yes!” said Tess excitedly. “What do you want to do?” “Let's guess which tutor has the cute dog,” Jenn suggested. The two friends went to the tutors, each one with a different type of dog. It was hard to guess which one was the cutest. So they asked the tutors: “Who has the cutest dog?” To their surprise, the tutors replied together, “That's up to you to decide. Look carefully and make up your mind.” Jenn and Tess looked at the two dogs, one by one, and soon they realised that both dogs were equally cute! The two friends learnt a valuable lesson that day: being different is special, and everyone deserves respect.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a neighbor named Mr. Brown. Tim liked Mr. Brown because he was kind and always smiled. One day, Tim found a ball in his yard. He knew it was not his ball. Tim felt guilty because he thought it was Mr. Brown's ball. He wanted to give it back to him. Tim decided to send the ball back to Mr. Brown. He rolled the ball across the grass and it went right to Mr. Brown's yard. Mr. Brown saw the ball and smiled. He knew Tim was a good friend. They played with the ball together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a strong boy named Tim. Tim liked to open big books and read stories. One day, he found a story about a talking cat. The cat could talk to people and make them laugh. In the story, Tim met the talking cat. The cat said, "Hello, Tim! Let's play a fun game." Tim was very happy to play with the talking cat. They played all day and had a lot of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The talking cat said, "Tim, I have a secret. I am not a cat, but a magic dog!" Tim was very surprised, but he still loved his new friend. They played more games and had the best day ever.

One day, two little girls were playing together. One said to the other, "Let's make an incredible joke!" The other girl replied, “What kind of joke?” The first girl said, "We need a sheet!" They went to the other room and looked around. Then, they found a big white piece of paper. They both grabbed it and ran back to the living room. The girls put the paper on the floor. The first girl started acting like a monster. The second girl laughed so hard that she started rolling around on the floor. The first girl asked, "Did you see the incredible joke I just made?" The second girl nodded and said, "Yes! It was the best joke ever!"

Once there was a little boy who liked to whisper. He would sit and whisper to himself every day. One day he was given a special present from his grandma. It was wrapped up in ordinary brown paper. When he opened it, he found a toy airplane inside. He was so excited he almost shouted out loud. But then he remembered he liked to whisper, so he whispered "thank you" to his grandma. He was so happy about his present, and he played with the airplane all day long, quietly whispering to himself the whole time.

One day, a large dog named Max wanted to win a race. He wanted to get a special license to show he was the best. Max practiced running every day. He was very fast and strong. One day, Max went to the big race. He saw a small cat named Lily. Lily was also in the race. Max laughed and said, "You are too small to win." Lily just smiled and said, "We will see." The race began and Max ran very fast. He was sure he would win. But then, something unexpected happened. Max saw a big ball and stopped to play with it. While Max was playing, Lily ran past him and won the race. Max was surprised and learned that being large and fast doesn't always mean you will win.
