One day, a girl named Mia found a light veil in her room. She put it on her head and went outside to play. Mia saw a ball and wanted to kick it. She ran fast and kicked the ball high in the sky. As the ball went up, something unexpected happened. The light veil started to make Mia fly! She was so surprised and happy. Mia flew higher and higher, holding the veil tight on her head. She looked down and saw her friends playing below. Mia decided to fly down and show her friends the magic veil. But when she took it off her head, she stopped flying. Mia learned that she could only fly when the veil was on her head. She and her friends took turns wearing the veil and flying in the sky. They had so much fun playing with the magic veil all day.

Once upon a time, there was a duck named Delilah. Delilah was very excited, as she was going to visit the pond for the first time. When she got there, she saw the pond was big and heavy with water. She flapped her wings and quacked out of sheer joy. Suddenly, two geese came in and started quacking. They didn't like how Delilah was behaving, and they started to chase her away. Delilah began to run as fast as she could, but the geese were gaining on her. Just as the geese were about to get her, Delilah's momma duck flew in and quacked loudly. She flew straight at the geese and made them back away. She said to them, "Behave yourselves, or else I'll make you regret it!" The geese flew away quickly, and Delilah was safe. She was so happy she had been rescued. She hugged her momma duck, thanked her, and they both happily continued exploring the big, heavy pond.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a big, fierce dog named Max. They loved to play together. One day, Lily found a pretty candle in her house. She loved the candle and wanted to show Max. Lily called Max to show him the candle. Max was very excited and wanted to play with it too. Max tried to grab the candle with his mouth, but he was too strong. He started to tear the candle with his teeth. Lily was sad because her pretty candle was broken. The next day, Lily's mom saw the broken candle. She knew Max had torn it. But she had a surprise for Lily. She gave Lily a new, even prettier candle. This time, Lily knew not to show Max the candle. She learned that some things are not for playing with big, fierce dogs.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Max. Max had a pretty collar on his neck. He liked to play with his ball and run around the yard. Max was a happy dog. One day, Max did a stupid thing. He saw a squirrel and ran after it. Max ran so fast that his collar came off. The squirrel got away, but Max was sad because he lost his collar. Max started to weep. His friend, a little girl named Lily, saw Max crying. She hugged Max and said, "Don't cry, Max. We will find your collar." They looked all around the yard and found the collar under a bush. Max was happy again, and he promised not to chase squirrels anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play outside. His favorite thing to do was to slide down the big hill near his house. He would slide all day long with his friends. They had so much fun together. One day, the sky began to get dark early. Tom and his friends were still playing on the hill. They did not want to stop sliding. They thought the dark sky was fun and exciting. They did not know that something unexpected was about to happen. As Tom slid down the hill, he saw a big, shiny star fall from the sky. It landed right in front of him. Tom and his friends were amazed. They had never seen a star up close before. The star was so bright that it made the dark sky turn light again. Tom and his friends knew that this was a very special day. They would always remember the day they slid under the dark sky and found a fallen star.
