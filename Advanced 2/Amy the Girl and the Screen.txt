Title: Amy the Girl and the Screen

One day, a little girl named Amy found a brilliant toy in her room. She did not know where it came from. The toy had a screen and many buttons. Amy was very excited to play with it. Amy pushed a button and the screen opened. A funny little man appeared on the screen. He said, "Hello, I am Tom, the talking toy. What do you want to play today?" Amy was surprised but happy to have a new friend to play with. Amy and Tom played games and sang songs. Then, something unexpected happened. Tom jumped out of the screen and became a real, tiny person! Amy and Tom played together in her room and had lots of fun. They became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She lived in a house made of bricks. Amy loved to play outside and eat healthy food like apples and carrots. One day, Amy found a broken brick in her house. She thought, "I need to replace this brick!" So, Amy went to the store to buy a new brick. She picked the best brick she could find and brought it home. When Amy put the new brick in the house, something unexpected happened. The brick turned into a talking frog! The frog said, "Thank you for picking me! I was a frog stuck in a brick, but now I am free!" Amy and the frog became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. She lived in an ordinary house with her mom and dad. Jane loved to bake cookies with her mom. One day, her mom said, "Jane, today we will bake cookies for a special contest!" Jane was very excited. She put on her apron and helped her mom mix the dough, roll it out, and cut it into fun shapes. They put the cookies in the oven and waited for them to bake. When the cookies were ready, they took them out and decorated them with icing and sprinkles. At the contest, there were many other kids with their cookies too. Jane's cookies looked very pretty and yummy. The judges tasted all the cookies and talked to each other. Finally, they said, "Jane, your cookies are the best! You win the trophy!" Jane was so happy and proud. She took her trophy home and showed it to her dad. They all enjoyed the cookies and had a great day.

Sara and Tom were twins who liked to play with their toys. One day, they found a new toy in their room. It was a flashlight with a red handle and a yellow button. Sara wanted to play with it, but Tom grabbed it first. "Mine!" Tom said. "I saw it first!" "No, mine!" Sara said. "I want to see it glow!" They pulled and pushed, but neither of them would let go. The flashlight fell to the floor and broke. The handle came off and the button stopped working. Sara and Tom looked at the broken toy and felt sad. "Sorry," Tom said. "I was too busy wanting it for myself." "Me too," Sara said. "I was not nice to you." They hugged and said they were sorry. Then they went to their mom and told her what happened. Mom was not happy, but she was glad they told the truth. She said they could share another flashlight if they promised to be careful and kind. Sara and Tom learned that sharing is better than fighting, and that toys are more fun when they glow.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Ann. She had a toy, a rag doll, named Grace. Grace was a very graceful doll, with a pretty dress and a big smile. One day, Ann went to the park with her mom. At the park, Ann saw a new friend, a boy named Tom. Ann wanted to introduce Tom to Grace, her rag doll. So, Ann walked up to Tom and showed him her doll. Tom thought Grace was a nice doll, and he showed Ann his toy car. They played together at the park all day. Ann and Tom became good friends, and they always had fun playing with Grace the rag doll and the toy car. And they all lived happily ever after.
