Once upon a time, there was a purple fish named Finn. Finn loved to swim in the big blue sea. One day, while he was swimming, he saw a shiny gem on the sea floor. Finn wanted the gem, so he swam down to pick it up. But when he touched the gem, something unexpected happened. The gem turned him into a big fish! Finn was so surprised and happy. As a big fish, Finn made many new friends. But soon, he missed being a purple fish. So, he swam back to the gem and touched it again. Just like that, he turned back into a purple fish. Finn was happy to be himself again and swam away with a big smile.

Once there was a thin mechanic. He spent all his days working on cars and trucks in his garage. He loved fixing things and worked very hard every day. One day, he noticed a strange noise coming from one of the trucks. He was curious and wanted to know what was wrong. He looked closely at the engine and saw that a part was broken. The mechanic wanted to repair the truck, so he went to his toolshed to get his tools. He spent the morning taking apart the engine and fixing the broken part. The truck was soon running again. The mechanic was proud of his work and decided to stay in his garage and take a rest. He remained in the garage until the end of the day, admiring the truck and all he had done.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a weird toy named Mr. Wiggles. Mr. Wiggles could talk and play games with Lily. They had a lot of fun together. One day, Lily saw her friend Tim playing a new game. She wanted that game too. So, she asked her mom for the new game. Her mom said, "You can have the new game, but you have to give away one of your old toys." Lily thought about it and decided to give away Mr. Wiggles. When she got the new game, Lily played with it all day. But she started to miss Mr. Wiggles. She realized that the new game was not as fun as playing with Mr. Wiggles. She felt sad for giving him away. Lily learned that it is important to be happy with what you have. Sometimes, new things are not always better than the old ones. And that is the story of Lily and Mr. Wiggles.
