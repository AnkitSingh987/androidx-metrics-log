Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was only 3 years old and always loved to play outside. One snowy day, she set off on an adventure. She was wearing her warmest coat and her most colorful mittens. She arrived at her grandparents' house feeling anxious. She was excited to see them, but at the same time she was a little bit scared. As she walked up to the door, she heard her grandpa calling out. "Lucy, is that you? Come on in!" Lucy stepped inside and her grandpa gave her a big hug. He said, "I have something very special for you. Wait right here." He went and retrieved a toy train. The train was bright and shiny and Lucy was so excited. "Can I play with it, Grandpa?" she asked. Grandpa smiled and said, "Of course! But let's put your mittens on first so your hands stay warm, okay?" Lucy smiled as she put on her mittens and eagerly grabbed the train. She ran off to play and have fun with her grandpa. She was so happy and no longer anxious. She was once again living her adventure with the special toy train.

One day, Mary and her dad were out for a walk on the beach. Mary saw a gray bird struggling in a net and flew over to her dad. She said, "Daddy, can you release the bird?" Her dad replied "Yes, baby we should help the bird. We should do the right thing." So they walked over to the gray bird and her dad carefully removed the net. The bird was fit and ready to fly again. Then Mary's dad said, "Mary, we have just released the bird, what do you think about it?". Mary replied, "I am happy that we could help the bird, but next time it may be a person in need". The father smiled, he knew Mary had learned the important moral lesson that it is always important to help those in need.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny who was very hungry. Every day, he went out in search of food, but nothing seemed to satisfy his hunger. One day, the little bunny hopped down a path and came across a large clock. It was big and made a loud tick-tock sound. The bunny hopped around it and he noticed something. On top of the clock were five big carrots. "Oh, wow! Carrots!" the little bunny cried with joy. He reached up to the top of the clock and started to raise the carrots down. This made the clock even louder, but the bunny didn't mind. He was so happy and filled with satisfaction. The little bunny then hopped off down the path, feasting on the carrots. He was happy to have found something to fill his hungry tummy.

One day, a little boy named Tim was with his mom in the kitchen. Tim's mom was making pasta for dinner. Tim liked pasta a lot. He gazed at the pasta as it cooked in the pot. The sky outside was dark, and Tim knew it was time for dinner. Tim's mom looked at him and said, "Tim, can you help me? I need you to get the pasta out of the pot." Tim was scared because the pot was hot. He didn't want to get hurt. But he wanted to help his mom. Tim looked at the pot and had an idea. He grabbed a big spoon and tried to get the pasta out. But, the pasta jumped out of the pot and onto the floor! Tim was surprised. His mom laughed and said, "It's okay, Tim. We can clean it up and make more pasta." Together, Tim and his mom cleaned the floor and made more pasta. They had fun, even though it was a little messy. In the end, they ate their pasta, and it was the best pasta they ever had.

Lily and Tom are friends. They like to play together. But they cannot play now. Lily is sick. She has a cold. She stays at home with her mom. Tom misses Lily. He wants to make her happy. He thinks of a good idea. He draws a picture for Lily. He draws a sun, a flower and a heart. He writes "I miss you" on the picture. He puts the picture in an envelope. He asks his dad to send the picture to Lily. Lily's mom brings the mail. She sees an envelope for Lily. She gives it to Lily. Lily opens the envelope. She sees the picture. She smiles. She likes the picture. She thinks Tom is a brilliant friend. She calls Tom on the phone. She says "Thank you for the picture. It is very nice. I miss you too." Tom is happy to hear Lily's voice. He says "You are welcome. I hope you feel better soon. Do you want to do some exercise with me?" Lily says "Yes, I want to do some exercise. What can we do?" Tom says "We can do some jumping, some clapping and some singing. It will make us strong and happy." Lily says "That sounds fun. Let's do it." They do some exercise on the phone. They jump, they clap and they sing. They laugh and have fun. They feel better. They are brilliant friends.
