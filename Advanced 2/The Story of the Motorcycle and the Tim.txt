One day, a big motorcycle was on the road. It went very fast. A little boy named Tim saw the motorcycle and wanted to ride it. He blinked his eyes and wished very hard. Tim walked up to the motorcycle and said, "Can I ride you?" The motorcycle replied, "I don't know if you can. You are very small and don't know much about me. You are ignorant about motorcycles." Tim felt sad but he wanted to learn. He asked the motorcycle, "Will you teach me?" The motorcycle agreed and said, "Yes, I will teach you. But first, you must grow bigger and stronger." So, Tim worked hard every day to learn about motorcycles and grow bigger. One day, he was big enough to ride the motorcycle. They went on a fun ride together, and Tim was very happy.

One day, a snake named Sam went to the park. Sam was a serious snake. He liked to write in his book. At the park, he saw a big tree. Sam thought it was a good place to write. He went under the tree and took out his book. A little boy named Tim saw Sam the snake. Tim was not scared. He said, "Hi, snake! What are you doing?" Sam said, "I am writing in my book." Tim asked, "Can I see?" Sam showed Tim his book. The words were all wiggly, like a snake. Just then, a big wind came. It blew Sam's book away! Sam and Tim were sad. They looked for the book. They found it in a bush. But something unexpected happened. The wiggly words were now straight! Sam and Tim laughed. They were happy. From that day on, Sam and Tim were best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little comet named Sparky. Sparky was very tired from flying around the sky all day. He wanted to find a place to rest. One day, Sparky met a big star named Stella. "Hello, I am tired. Can I rest here?" Sparky asked. "Yes, you can stay with me," said Stella. They became good friends. Sparky was so happy, but he knew he could not stay forever. One day, Sparky said, "I must go now." Stella was sad but understood. As Sparky flew away, he seemed to disappear into the sky. Stella learned that it was important to help others, even if it made her sad. And Sparky learned that friends are always in our hearts, even when they disappear.

Once upon a time, there was a thin cat. The cat liked to play with a package. One day, the cat saw the package in the house. The cat was very happy. The cat played with the package all day. The cat did not want to leave the package. The cat and the package were best friends. But one day, the package had to go away. The cat was sad. The cat had to leave the package. The cat said goodbye to the package. The cat missed the package, but the cat made new friends. The cat was happy again.

One day, a boy named Tim found a big eraser. It was a disgusting eraser. It was dirty and smelly. But Tim wanted to play with it because it was a magic eraser. Tim took the eraser outside to play. He met his friend, Sam. They wanted to shoot the eraser into the sky. They thought it would be fun to see how high it could go. So, they threw the eraser up, up, up into the air. The disgusting eraser went so high that they could not see it anymore. Tim and Sam laughed and clapped. They were so happy to watch the eraser shoot into the sky. And they knew that the magic eraser was gone forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside. He would run, jump, and have lots of fun. One day, Tim was playing on the porch. He saw a big cup. The cup was full of a drink. Tim was very thirsty from playing. He picked up the cup and took a big sip. The drink was very sour! Tim made a funny face. He did not like the sour drink. Tim's mom saw him and laughed. She said, "That's lemon juice, Tim!" Tim laughed too. From then on, Tim always asked what was in a cup before he drank it.
