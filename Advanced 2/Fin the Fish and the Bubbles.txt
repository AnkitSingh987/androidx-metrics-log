One day, a little fish named Fin lived in a reef. He had a friend, a big fish named Bubbles. They liked to play and swim together. Bubbles was very useful because he helped Fin when he was in trouble. One day, while they were playing, a mean shark came. He wanted to eat Fin. Bubbles said to Fin, "Trust me, I will help you." Fin was scared but he trusted Bubbles. Bubbles swam fast and made a big splash. The mean shark got scared and swam away. Fin was happy and said, "Thank you, Bubbles! You are a good friend." They played and swam together, knowing they could trust each other.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a restless dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to run and play all day long. One day, Buddy saw a big race for dogs. He wanted to win and feel the victory. On the day of the race, Buddy met a new friend named Daisy. Daisy was a small and shy dog. She was scared of the race. Buddy told Daisy, "Don't worry, I will help you. We can run together." Daisy smiled and felt happy. During the race, Buddy and Daisy ran very fast. They were almost at the finish line when Daisy got tired. Buddy knew he could win, but he wanted to help his friend. So, he stopped and covered Daisy with his body to give her some rest. Together, they crossed the finish line. They did not win the race, but they had a great time and became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sarah. She wanted to make her own bow and arrow so she could practice her skills. She went outside to look for materials and she found some sticks, string and feathers. Sarah quickly made a bow and some arrows. She picked up the bow and showed it to her little brother. He was so excited and wanted to have a go. Sarah agreed and set up a target for him to shoot at. After a few minutes of practice, he was ready to shoot. He pulled the string back but after his first attempt, he began to complain. "This bow is too hard", he said. Sarah smiled and put her hand on his shoulder. "Let me show you something," she said. She carefully took the bow from his hands and pointed it away in the distance. She pulled the string and released the arrow. It flew through the air and landed close to the target. The brother was amazed. He looked at Sarah with wide eyes and a big smile. Sarah grinned back and said "now you try". He took the bow and tried again. This time, with a bit of help from Sarah, his arrow flew further and hit the target. The brother was so proud of himself and excitedly thanked Sarah. She gave him a hug and said "you did great!" From that day forward, he never complained about doing too much work.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a new kid playing by himself. Tim wanted to introduce himself and make a new friend. He walked up to the new kid and said, "Hi, I'm Tim. What's your name?" The new kid smiled and said, "I'm Sam. Nice to meet you!" As they played together, Tim suddenly stepped on something disgusting. He looked down and saw a big, squishy bug under his foot. "Eww!" he said, "I stepped on a bug with my foot!" Sam laughed and said, "That's yucky, but don't worry, it happens!" Then, something unexpected happened. The bug started to grow bigger and bigger! Tim and Sam were scared. But the big bug was not mean. It said, "Thank you for letting me out of my tiny bug body! I am a magic bug, and I will give you both a wish for helping me." Tim and Sam looked at each other, amazed. They made their wishes, and the magic bug made them come true. From that day on, Tim and Sam were best friends, and they never forgot the magic bug that brought them together.
