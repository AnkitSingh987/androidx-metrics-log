One day, a little bug named Tim wanted to crawl to the big park. He saw a long piece of tape on the ground. He thought, "I can use this tape to help me get to the park faster!" So, Tim began to crawl onto the tape. As Tim crawled, he met a big bird named Sue. Sue said, "Hi, Tim! Where are you going?" Tim replied, "I'm going to the big park!" Sue looked at the tape and said, "Oh no, Tim! That tape is sour! It's not good for you." But Tim did not listen to Sue. He thought the tape was fun and kept crawling. When Tim reached the park, he felt sick. His tummy hurt from the sour tape. He could not play with his friends. Tim was sad and wished he had listened to Sue. The day ended badly for Tim because he did not listen to his friend's advice.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a cool blue blanket that she took everywhere with her. She loved her blanket very much. One day, Lily met a boy named Tim. Tim said, "I hate blankets! They are not cool." Lily felt sad that Tim did not like her cool blanket. She wanted to show him how nice blankets can be. The next day, it started to rain. Lily and Tim were outside playing. They got very wet and cold. Lily had an idea. She took out her cool blue blanket and shared it with Tim. They both felt warm and dry under the blanket. Tim said, "I was wrong. Blankets can be cool. I like your blanket now." Lily was happy that she could show Tim how nice blankets can be. From that day on, Tim and Lily became best friends, and they both loved the cool blue blanket.

Jill followed Mommy through the kitchen. It looked funny. The kitchen seemed bigger and there were so many colors. Jill could see tall piles of flour, sugar, and eggs. Mommy was busy so Jill asked her, "What are you doing?" Mommy smiled and said, "I'm making a pie." Jill was so excited and couldn't wait to see the finished product. Jill followed Mommy back to the kitchen. She watched as Mommy mixed up the ingredients and carefully placed the pie inside the oven. Jill asked, "Can I help?" But Mommy just smiled and said, "Not this time." Mommy let her help set the timer and they waited together. Finally, the timer went off and the kitchen smelled yummy. Mommy opened the oven and took out the pie. It was tall and looked delicious. Jill said, "Yum!" She followed Mommy to the dinner table and they teased the pie. Mommy cut a slice for each of them and they enjoyed the special treat. It was the best pie ever!

One day, Bill had a brilliant plan. He wanted to make his own calendar! He got out all of his crayons and markers and thought carefully about what he should draw. He planned to make a special calendar with different colors and shapes. But when he actually tried to make it, Bill felt helpless! He just couldn't get his colors and shapes to look like the calendar he had in his head. He was getting frustrated, and he was about to give up when suddenly, he figured out how to make it just right. Bill was so excited! His calendar was so beautiful, he almost couldn't believe what he had done. He proudly hung up his calendar in his bedroom, and he felt like he had accomplished something amazing.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play in the kitchen. He loved to wave at his elderly grandma when she cooked. Grandma always smiled and waved back. One day, Tim saw a big cupboard in the kitchen. He wanted to see what was inside. He opened the cupboard and found many fun things. There were pots, pans, and spoons. Tim played with them and made a lot of noise. Grandma heard the noise and came to see what Tim was doing. She saw Tim playing and laughed. She waved at Tim and said, "Let's cook together!" Tim was very happy and waved back. They cooked a yummy meal and had a great time.
