Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She liked to play in her garden. One day, she found a small, round stone. Lucy liked the stone because it was smooth and nice to touch. She could feel it in her hand. Lucy took the stone with her everywhere. She showed it to her mom and dad. They said it was a reliable stone. It would always be there for her. Lucy was happy because she had a new friend. Lucy and the stone went on many fun adventures. They played in the park, went on slides, and even had a tea party with her toys. The stone was always there for Lucy. She knew she could count on it. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful village, there lived a boy named Tom. Tom loved to play with his spear. He would throw it and run to catch it. One day, while playing, Tom met a new friend named Sam. Sam said, "Hi! I like your spear. Can I play with you?" Tom said, "Yes, let's play together!" As they played, they saw a big bird. The bird looked at them and they recognized it was a friendly bird. The bird said, "I like how you play. Can I join you?" Tom and Sam said, "Yes, let's all play together!" And so, Tom, Sam, and the big bird played happily in the peaceful village. They had lots of fun with the spear and became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a rich man named Tom. He had a big house and lots of toys. One day, Tom met a small boy named Tim. Tim was worried because he did not have any toys to play with. Tom said, "Don't worry, Tim! I have many toys in my big house. We can play together!" Tim was very happy to hear this. They went to Tom's big house and played with the toys. One day, while they were playing, they heard a loud strike! They ran outside and saw a big tree had fallen down. Tom and Tim were scared, but they knew they had each other. They held hands and went back inside to play with their toys. And they were not worried anymore.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dark night. The moon was hiding, and the stars were asleep. A little boy named Tim was very hungry. He wanted to eat something yummy. Tim thought about pizza. He loved pizza with cheese and tomato. But he did not have any pizza at home. His friend, Sam, lived next door, and he had pizza. Tim went to Sam's house and asked, "Can I have some pizza, please?" Sam said, "I will give you pizza if you help me clean my room." Tim didn't want to clean the room, but he was very hungry. So, he decided to surrender and help Sam. They cleaned the room together, and it became shiny and tidy. Then, Sam shared his pizza with Tim, and they both were happy. The moral of the story is that sometimes we need to do things we don't want to do to get what we want. When we help others, we can get good things in return.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a clever kangaroo named Kiki. Kiki had many friends, but her best friend was a little bird named Benny. Kiki and Benny loved to play together and plan fun games. One sunny day, Kiki and Benny planned to have a big race. Kiki said, "Benny, let's see who is faster, you or me!" Benny agreed, "Okay, Kiki! Let's race to the big tree and back!" They both got ready, and when they said "Go!" they started to run and fly as fast as they could. But suddenly, as Kiki was hopping and Benny was flying, a big wind came and blew Benny far away! Kiki didn't know where Benny went. She stopped hopping and looked around. She saw Benny far away in a tree, looking scared. Kiki had a plan. She hopped as fast as she could to the tree and used her strong legs to shake the tree. Benny was able to fly back down, and they were both safe. Kiki and Benny didn't finish their race, but they didn't care. They were just happy to be together again. From that day on, they knew that their friendship was more important than any race or game. And they always made sure to plan their games carefully, so nothing unexpected would happen again.
