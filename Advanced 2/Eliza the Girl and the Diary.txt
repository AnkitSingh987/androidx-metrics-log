Once upon a time there was a little girl named Eliza who was always getting into trouble. One day she spotted a diary in her granny's room and decided to take it without asking. Eliza opened the diary and began to read it. Suddenly, she felt dizzy, and she fell and the diary snapped shut in her hands. Frightened, Eliza ran to show her granny the diary. Granny was very disappointed and said, "Eliza, this diary belongs to me and you should not take things that are not yours." Eliza felt sorry and apologised. From that day onwards, Eliza never took anything that did not belong to her. She learned an important lesson: it is wrong to take something that does not belong to you, as it might make other people feel bad.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a big garage. Inside the garage, there were many things to play with. There were balls, bikes, and even a little car. One day, a boy named Tim wanted to be healthy. He knew he could use the things in the garage to help him. Tim went into the garage and saw a big red ball. He picked up the ball and started to play. He threw the ball up and down, and then he kicked it around. He played with the ball for a long time. After playing with the ball, Tim felt happy and healthy. He knew that using the things in the garage was a good way to stay strong. From that day on, Tim played in the garage every day, and he grew up to be a very healthy boy.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a loyal dog named Max. They played together every day. Tim had a shiny coin that he loved very much. He would show it to Max and they would play with it. One day, while playing with the coin, it rolled away and vanished. Tim was very sad. He looked and looked, but he could not find the coin. Max saw that Tim was sad and wanted to help. Max looked for the coin too. After some time, Max found the coin under a big tree. He picked it up with his mouth and brought it to Tim. Tim was so happy that Max found the coin. They played with it and had more fun. Tim knew that Max was a loyal friend who would always help him.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to sing and talk all day. He lived in a small chamber in a big tree. Bobo had many friends who lived in the tree too. One day, Bobo's friend, a squirrel named Squeaky, asked him to deliver a nut to his friend on the other side of the forest. Bobo was happy to help. He took the nut and flew across the forest, singing and talking loudly as he went. When Bobo reached the other side of the forest, he found Squeaky's friend waiting for him. He gave the nut to the friend, and they both smiled. Bobo felt good for helping his friend. Then, he flew back to his noisy chamber in the big tree, singing and talking all the way home.

Once upon a time, there was a big, rough bear. He lived in a cave underground. One day, he went out to look for food. He walked and walked, but he could not find any food. Then, he saw a little girl. She was picking flowers in the forest. The bear went to her and said, "I am hungry. Give me your food, or I will punish you!" The little girl was scared, but she had an idea. She said, "Wait, Mr. Bear! I know where there is a lot of food. Follow me!" The bear was surprised, but he followed her. They walked and walked until they found a big tree with lots of fruit. The bear was happy and ate all the fruit. He did not punish the little girl, and they became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a fat cat named Bob. Bob loved to play with his ball. One day, Bob saw a big step in his way. Bob wanted to get his ball, but the step was too high for him. Bob tried to jump, but he could not. He dropped down and felt sad. Then, his friend, a little bird, came to help. The bird flew up and got the ball for Bob. Bob was so happy! He thanked the little bird. They played together all day long. The fat cat and the little bird became best friends. And they never let a big step stop their fun again.
