Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and dog, Spot. Tim loved to play with his toy cars. His favorite car was a big, red automobile. One sunny day, Tim went to play outside with Spot. They saw their friend, Sally, who lived in a distant house down the street. Tim had an idea. He said, "Sally, come play with me and Spot!" Sally was happy and said, "Okay, I will come." Tim, Sally, and Spot played with the red automobile all day. They invited more friends to join them. Everyone had a great time. At the end of the day, they all went home, tired but happy.

Once upon a time, in a little town, there was a new boy named Tim. Tim was very excited to make new friends. He wanted to play with the kids in the town. Tim's mom told him, "Behave well, and you will make many friends." Tim listened to his mom and went to the park. At the park, he saw other kids playing. Tim behaved nicely and shared his toys with the other kids. They all had fun together. The kids in the town liked Tim, and he made many new friends. And they all played happily every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little baby named Tim. Tim was a very independent baby. He liked to do things by himself. He had a small crib in his room where he slept every night. One sunny day, Tim was playing in his crib. He heard a sound and wanted to know what it was. So, Tim decided to peek out of his crib. He stood up and looked over the edge of the crib. When Tim peeked out, he saw a cute puppy playing with a ball. The puppy saw Tim and came over to the crib. Tim laughed and clapped his hands. They played together all day and became best friends. And from that day on, Tim and the puppy always played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a boy named Tim had a big urge to zip his jacket. But the jacket's zip was not reliable. Tim tried to zip it, but it did not work. He was sad because he wanted to go out and play. Tim's friend, Sue, saw him being sad. She wanted to help him. Sue tried to zip the jacket too. But, the jacket's zip was still not reliable. They both felt sad and did not know what to do. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the jacket away. Tim and Sue were surprised. They looked at each other and laughed. They did not need the jacket anymore. They played outside and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was an ordinary boy who liked to play with his toys. One day, he was playing with his toy cars near a drain in the backyard. A little frog jumped out of the drain and said, "Hi, Tim! Can you help me? I need to find a place to relax." Tim thought for a moment and said, "I know the perfect spot for you!" Tim took the frog to a small pond near his house. The frog was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim! This is the best place to relax." Tim and the frog became good friends, and they played together every day.

Jack was walking through the forest and he saw something strange. He noticed a wolf sitting obediently nearby. Jack couldn't believe it and he slowly went over to the wolf. Jack asked the wolf, "Why are you sitting here so obediently?" The wolf replied, "I am waiting for the hunter. He is my friend and he promised to take me for a walk today." Jack was surprised and he asked, "Won't he be scared of you?" The wolf smiled and said, "No, he knows I am very obedient." Jack was happy and he decided to stay with the wolf until his friend showed up. Sure enough, shortly after, the hunter arrived with a leash and took the wolf for a walk. Jack waved goodbye to the wolf and continued his walk through the forest.
