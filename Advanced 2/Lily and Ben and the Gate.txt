Lily and Ben were playing in the garden. They liked to run and jump and make noise. They saw a big gate that led to the road. The gate was old and rusty and had a sign that said "Do not touch". "Let's go and see what is behind the gate," said Ben. He pushed the gate with his hands. It made a loud creak. "No, Ben, we have to be obedient," said Lily. "Mum and Dad said we have to stay in the garden. The road is dangerous. There are cars and trucks and bikes. They can hurt us." But Ben did not listen. He pushed the gate harder. It made a louder creak. Then it broke. The gate fell to the ground with a bang. It was destroyed. Lily and Ben were scared. They heard Mum and Dad coming. They knew they were in trouble. "What have you done?" Mum and Dad shouted. They saw the broken gate and the sign. They were angry and sad. "We are sorry, Mum and Dad," Lily and Ben said. They hung their heads. They wished they had been obedient. They learned their lesson. They never touched the gate again.

Once upon a time, there was a modest house. In the house lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to think and play all day long. One day, Lily found a passport. It was a small book with pictures. Lily wanted to go on a trip with the passport. She thought it would be fun. So, she put on her shoes and went outside. Lily walked and walked, but she did not know where to go. As Lily walked, she got lost. She could not find her way back to her modest house. Lily started to cry. She wished she had not left her house with the passport. The sun went down, and it was dark. Lily was still lost and could not go back home.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the park. She saw a pretty rock and wanted to examine it. She picked up the rock and looked at it closely. It was a perfect rock with many colors. While Amy was looking at the rock, she heard a voice. It was a small bird in a tree. The bird said, "Hello, Amy!" Amy was very surprised but happy to talk to the bird. They became good friends and played in the park every day. Amy and the bird loved to sing together. Amy thought the bird had the most perfect voice. They were very happy and had lots of fun.

Lily and Ben went to the zoo with Mom and Dad. They saw many animals, like lions, elephants, and monkeys. But Lily's favorite was the gorilla. She liked how big and strong he was, and how he played with his friends. "Can we see the gorilla again?" Lily asked Mom. "Sure, honey. Let's go back to his cage," Mom said. They walked to the gorilla cage, where a big crowd was watching. The gorilla was sitting on a rock, eating a banana. He looked bored and sad. "Hello, gorilla!" Lily said. She waved at him and smiled. The gorilla looked at Lily and threw his banana peel at her. It hit her on the head and made her cry. "Hey, that's rude!" Ben said. He was angry at the gorilla for hurting his sister. The gorilla laughed and clapped his hands. He thought it was funny to make Lily cry. "Stop it, gorilla!" Mom said. She picked up Lily and hugged her. "You're not nice. You should be sorry." The gorilla did not care. He kept laughing and clapping. He did not like people. He wanted to be alone. But then, something happened. A loud noise came from the sky. It was a helicopter. It flew over the zoo and dropped a big net over the gorilla cage. The net caught the gorilla and lifted him up. "Help! Help!" the gorilla screamed. He was scared and confused. He did not know what was happening. The crowd gasped and watched. Lily and Ben saw the gorilla in the net. He looked small and weak. "Where are they taking him?" Lily asked Mom. "I don't know, honey. Maybe to another zoo. Maybe to a better place," Mom said. Lily felt sorry for the gorilla. She wiped her tears and said, "Bye, gorilla. I hope you're happy." The gorilla heard Lily's voice. He stopped screaming and looked at her. He saw her kind face and felt something in his heart. He felt sorry too. He reached out his hand and waved at Lily. He did not clap or laugh. He did not throw anything. He just waved. Lily waved back and smiled. She did not hate the gorilla. She just wanted him to be nice. The helicopter flew away with the gorilla. Lily and Ben never saw him again. But they always remembered him. And they hoped he learned to be nice.
