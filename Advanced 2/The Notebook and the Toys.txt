Once upon a time, there was a big notebook. The notebook was very sorry. It was sorry because it was alone. It wanted friends to unite and play with. One day, the notebook saw a group of toys. They were having fun together. The notebook wanted to join them. It asked the toys, "Can I unite and play with you?" The toys said, "Yes, come and play with us!" The notebook was very happy. It played with the toys all day. Then, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the notebook away. The toys tried to catch the notebook, but they could not. The notebook was lost and could not unite with the toys anymore. In the end, the notebook felt very sorry again. It was far away from its new friends, and it could not play with them anymore. The toys were sad too, as they missed their new friend, the notebook.

One day, a girl named Amy went to play on her porch. She saw her friend, Tim, walking by. Amy called out to him, "Hi Tim! Want to play with me?" Tim said, "Yes, I want to play! Can we use my new ball?" Amy and Tim played with the ball for a while, having lots of fun. They were laughing and running around. As they played, they smelled something yummy. They followed the smell and found a big cake on a table. They looked around, but no one was there. Amy said, "Let's eat the cake!" Just as they were about to eat the cake, a squirrel jumped out of the cake! They were so surprised! The squirrel said, "That was my home! Now I need a new one." Amy and Tim felt bad, so they helped the squirrel find a new home. They found a nice tree for the squirrel to live in. The squirrel said, "Thank you!" and gave them each a yummy nut. They all became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a red bird. The red bird had a comb. The red bird liked to comb its feathers. Every day, the red bird would comb and sing. One day, a cat came. The cat liked to disturb the red bird. The cat would jump and make noise. The red bird could not comb and sing. But then, something unexpected happened. The cat found a comb too! The cat started to comb its fur. The red bird and the cat became friends. They both combed and sang together. And no one disturbed them again.

One day, a little bird named Tim was very sad. He could not sing. All the other birds could sing, but not Tim. He tried and tried, but he could only make a dry sound. Tim was not happy. Tim's friend, a big cat named Sam, saw that Tim was sad. Sam wanted to help. "Why are you sad, Tim?" Sam asked. Tim told Sam that he could not sing. Sam had an idea. "I will help you sing, Tim. Let's try to remember how to sing," Sam said. Sam and Tim sat under a tree. They tried to remember how to sing. "La, la, la," sang Sam. Tim tried to sing too. "La, la, la," sang Tim. Tim's voice was not dry anymore. Tim was happy. He could sing now. Tim and Sam sang together. They made a beautiful memory, and they were the best of friends.
