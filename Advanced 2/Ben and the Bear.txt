Once upon a time, there was a big, soft bear named Ben. Ben loved to help his friends. One day, his friend, a little bunny named Lily, was very sad. Ben wanted to comfort Lily. He gave her a big hug and a nice comb. Lily liked the comb because it made her fur smooth and pretty. This made Lily feel a little better. Together, Ben and Lily went to play with their friends in the big park. They had so much fun that Lily forgot all about being sad. Ben was happy that he could help his friend feel better. And from that day on, they were always there to comfort each other.

One day, a little girl named Sue found a small piece of copper. She took it home and put it in her room. That night, she heard a tiny voice. It was the copper talking to her! Sue was very surprised. The copper told Sue it was ashamed because it was dirty. Sue wanted to help the copper, so she cleaned it with water and a cloth. The copper was shiny and happy again. They talked and played all night. But the next day, when Sue showed her friends the talking copper, it did not talk. Sue's friends did not believe her. Sue was sad, but when she got home, the copper talked again. It told Sue it was just shy. Sue smiled and knew they would be friends forever.

One day, Grandma took her grandkids to the park. They had so much fun playing on the swings and slides. While they were there, Grandma noticed a very big tree. She pointed at the tree and said, "Look how big that tree is! I bet it weighs a lot." The grandkids weren't sure what Grandma meant but she explained that even though it looked big and heavy, it might not weigh very much. On the way back home, Grandma stopped at the hardware store. She said, "Let's see if the tree weighs what I think it weighs." The grandkids weren't sure what this meant, so they followed Grandma inside. The hardware store was filled with strange items, including something called a "screw." Grandma grabbed the screw and explained to the grandkids that they would use it to weigh the tree. They went back to the park and attached the screw to the tree. Grandma said, "It looks like the tree weighs an enormous amount." The grandkids clapped and cheered. Grandma had proven her point - everything isn't always as it seems!

Once upon a time, there was a little player. He loved to spin around and around. He liked to spin so much that he would spin all day long. One day, the player went outside to spin. The sun was very bright and the wind was harsh. But the player did not care. He just wanted to spin and have fun. As the player spun, he saw a big tree. The tree had strong branches and lots of leaves. The player thought it would be fun to spin on the tree. So, he climbed up and started to spin. The wind blew and the leaves danced. The player was happy, spinning in the tree, and he played there all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with a big window. Every day, Tim looked out of the window and saw the brilliant sun in the sky. One day, as Tim was looking out of the window, he saw a twist in the sky. It was a big, twisty cloud that looked like a snake. Tim was very excited to see this twisty cloud. As Tim watched the twisty cloud, something unexpected happened. The cloud started to change colors! It went from gray to pink, then to blue and green. Tim was so happy to see the twisty cloud change colors. The cloud then went away, and Tim knew that he had seen something very special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to find something special. She spent her days searching, looking for something that was true and real. She climbed hills and crossed rivers, but she could not find what she was searching for. At last, she found something hidden in a soft meadow. When she took it in her hands, she knew it was the something she had been searching for. It was true and real, and it made her smile. She hugged it close, and brought it home. When the little girl touched the soft object, it glowed with a bright light. She was so happy she laughed and danced in the sun. Every day she looked at it and thought of how true and special it was. She knew she would keep it for always.
