Once upon a time, there was a popular towel named Tim. Tim was very good at what he did. He helped people when they were wet. Everyone liked Tim because he was so helpful. One day, Tim was hanging in the sun. He saw a big ball coming to him. The ball hit Tim and he fell on the ground. Tim was sad because he could not do his job now. Then, a little girl picked Tim up. She did not know Tim was a towel. She thought Tim was a cape. She put Tim around her neck and started to run. Tim felt happy again because he could help the little girl be a superhero. Now, Tim was not just a towel, he was a cape too. And that was the best day for Tim.

Once upon a time, there was a wild cat. The cat liked to run, jump, and catch things. It lived in a house with a boy and his family. The boy liked to play with the wild cat. One day, the boy saw a big fridge in the kitchen. He opened the fridge and saw many yummy things inside. The wild cat saw the fridge too and wanted to catch something from it. The boy tried to stop the wild cat, but it was too fast. The wild cat jumped into the fridge and tried to catch the food. But the fridge door closed, and the wild cat was stuck inside. The boy was sad because he could not get the wild cat out. The wild cat should have listened to the boy and stayed away from the fridge.

One day, a boy named Tom went to the park with his mom. They saw a big tent. Tom was very excited. He said, "Mom, look! A tent! Can we go see it?" Mom said, "Yes, but be careful, Tom." Tom and his mom went to the tent. Inside, they saw a man making funny faces. Tom laughed and clapped his hands. The man said, "Hello, I am a funny man. Do you like my funny faces?" Tom said, "Yes, I like them a lot!" After they left the tent, Tom and his mom went to play on the swings. Tom was very careful when he played. He had a fun day at the park with his mom and the funny man in the tent. They went home happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a little ant who lived on the ground. The ant was sad because he had no friends to play with. He walked around the ground every day, looking for someone to be his friend. One day, the ant saw a big bug. The ant said, "Hi! Do you want to be my friend?" The big bug looked down at the little ant and said, "No, you are too small. I will pinch you!" The ant was very sad and walked away. As the ant walked, he saw a tiny bug on the ground. The tiny bug looked sad too. The ant asked, "Do you want to be my friend?" The tiny bug said, "Yes, I am sad because no one wants to play with me." Then, the tiny bug showed the ant a secret: it could change colors! The ant and the tiny bug became best friends, and they played together every day. The big bug saw them and was sad because he had no friends. The ant and the tiny bug decided to let the big bug play with them too, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to sing. Every day she went to the park in her cart and sang. Her singing was so beautiful people from all over heard it and were amazed. One day, an old man from the town heard her singing and gave her a special gift - an original guitar. She was so happy and immediately began playing it and singing. Her singing grew even more beautiful and the people of the town loved to hear her. The old man was so happy his gift had made someone so happy and came every day to listen to her sing. After that, the girl became very famous and everyone loved her original songs. The little girl and her cart, the original guitar and beautiful singing all became a part of the town. Every time she sang, everyone smiled and enjoyed the original music.
