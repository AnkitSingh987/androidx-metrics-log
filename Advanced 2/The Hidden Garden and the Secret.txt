Once upon a time, there was a hidden garden. In this garden, there were many herbs. One day, a little boy named Tim found the garden. He saw a big herb and wanted to take it home. Tim called his friend, Sara, to come see the herb. When Sara came, they both looked at the herb. They thought it was very pretty. They wanted to take it home to show their friends. Together, Tim and Sara took the herb from the hidden garden. They showed it to all their friends. Everyone was happy to see the herb. The hidden garden became their secret place to play.

Once upon a time there was a young driver who enjoyed driving in her bright car. She drove everywhere, from mountains to oceans. One day, as she was driving, she noticed a sign saying “Turn Here”. She thought to herself “Why not? Let’s turn and see what’s down that road!” So she turned the car and followed the road. Eventually the driver stopped at a village. There were children playing on the bright green grass, with the sun shining brightly on them. One of the children, who was only 3 years old, walked up to the driver. The young driver smiled and asked “What are you doing here?” The 3-year old replied “We’re playing today, and having fun!” The driver smiled and said “That’s really nice. You should remember that it’s always a good idea to explore and try new things. Who knows what you might find!” The child smiled and thanked the driver for their advice. The driver drove away, feeling happy - she had shared a moral lesson with the child about the importance of exploring and trying new things. Who knows what wonders the 3-year old would find if they kept turning down different roads!

Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Tim. Tim had a big fan in his room. He liked to watch it go round and round. One day, Tim wanted to touch the fan. He knew it was high up, so he had to step on something to reach it. Tim found a chair and put it under the fan. He stepped on the chair and reached up. Just then, his mom came in the room. "Tim, no!" she said. "The fan can hurt you." Tim felt sad, but he knew his mom was right. Later, Tim was playing with his toy car. Suddenly, the car started to talk! "I have a secret," the car said. "I can make your fan safe to touch." Tim was surprised. He asked the car, "How can you do that?" The car told Tim to put it near the fan. Tim did as he was told, and the car used its magic. Now, Tim could touch the fan, and it didn't hurt him. His mom was happy, and they played with the fan and the magic car all day.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable plant named Lily. Lily lived in a big garden with many other plants. They all loved Lily because she was very strong and always helped them. One day, a kind bee named Buzz came to the garden. He saw Lily and thought she was very pretty. Buzz said, "Hello, Lily! You are so nice. Will you marry me?" Lily said, "Yes, Buzz! I will marry you." So, Lily and Buzz had a big party. All the plants and bugs came to have fun. They danced and ate yummy food. From that day on, Lily and Buzz were very happy together. They were the best friends in the whole garden.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with no friends. Every day, he would look out the window and wish for someone to play with. One day, a nice girl named Sally came to visit Spot. She saw that he was lonely and wanted to be his friend. Sally brought some meat to feed Spot. Spot was very happy and wagged his tail. From that day on, Sally and Spot became best friends. They played together and were never lonely again. And every day, Sally would feed Spot his favorite meat. They lived happily ever after.
