One day, a grumpy cat and a happy dog went to the park. They saw a cooler under a big tree. The cat and dog were very hot, so they wanted to see what was inside the cooler. The cat and dog started to fight over who could open the cooler first. As they fought, they did not see a little bird fly down and land on the cooler. The bird opened the cooler and found cold drinks inside. The grumpy cat and the happy dog stopped fighting when they saw the bird with the cold drinks. They all shared the cold drinks and became friends. The cat was not grumpy anymore, and they all played together in the park.

Once upon a time, Mary and her dad were in their old van, going on a drive. Mary's dad said, "We need to be careful; we need to attach something to the van." Mary asked her dad, "What do we need to attach?" Her dad smiled and said, "We need to attach a rope, so we can bring some things with us. That will be very useful. You need to be careful and make sure the rope is secure." Mary was very proud and wanted to help. She grabbed the rope and carefully attached it to the van. After she finished, she looked up and said, "I did it!" Her dad praised her and said, "Good job! Now we are ready for the drive!" They drove away and Mary enjoyed the ride, knowing she had been careful to attach the rope.

One day, a big cloud came to the sky. The cloud was sad. It wanted to tell the sun something. The sun asked, "Why are you sad, big cloud?" The big cloud said, "I want to make rain, but I don't know how." The sun smiled and said, "I will tell you how to make rain. You need to be full of water. Then, let the water fall down to the ground." The big cloud was happy. It filled up with water and made rain. The rain fell on the trees, the flowers, and the grass. The big cloud was not sad anymore. It had made rain and had help from the sun.

Once upon a time, there was a little sheep called Fluffy. She had soft and fluffy wool, just like her name. Fluffy lived in a meadow with her friends. Every day they would play hide and seek and laugh lots. One day, they came across a big spicy pepper. It smelled so yummy, they all wanted to try it. So Fluffy leaned over to have a bite. The pepper was so spicy that it made Fluffy's eyes go wide! Just then, she spotted a cool patch of shade just past the pepper patch. She leaned in that direction and made her way to it. Fluffy and her friends had a nice time lounging in the shade and playing. But from then on, Fluffy was always a bit more careful with spicy peppers!

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a cat named Fluffy. Fluffy had a big, soft nose. She loved to rub her nose on everything. One day, Fluffy saw a big, red ball in the yard. She wanted to play with it. Fluffy went up to the ball and rubbed her nose on it. The ball started to roll away. Fluffy chased the ball and kept rubbing her nose on it. The ball rolled faster and faster. Suddenly, the ball hit a vase and it broke into pieces. Fluffy's mom came running and saw the broken vase. She looked at Fluffy and said, "Did you break the vase?" Fluffy felt guilty and hung her head. "Yes, Mom, I did," she said. Fluffy's mom was not angry. She hugged Fluffy and said, "It's okay. Next time, be more careful when you play." From that day on, Fluffy learned to be careful when she rubbed her nose on things. She still loved to play, but she made sure not to break anything. Fluffy and her mom lived happily in their little house, playing with the big, red ball together.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a big urge to wrap a gift for her friend, Tim. Wrapping gifts was difficult for Lucy, but she wanted to try her best. She found a pretty box and some paper to wrap the gift. Lucy's mom saw her trying to wrap the gift. "Do you need help?" her mom asked. Lucy said, "No, I want to do it by myself." So, her mom let her try. Lucy tried and tried to wrap the gift, but it was difficult. The paper kept tearing and the tape was sticky. Finally, she asked her mom for help. Together, they wrapped the gift and made it look nice. Lucy was happy and couldn't wait to give the gift to Tim.
