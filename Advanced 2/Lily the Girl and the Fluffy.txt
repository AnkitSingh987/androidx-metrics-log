Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily had an adorable pet cat named Fluffy. Lily loved to play with Fluffy all day. One day, Lily and Fluffy found some boxes in the house. Lily had a fun idea to make a big stack of boxes. Lily and Fluffy started to stack the boxes, one on top of the other. The stack got higher and higher. Fluffy wanted to stop, but Lily kept insisting, "More, more!" Fluffy did not like the big stack, but Lily wanted it to be even bigger. The stack of boxes got very tall. Suddenly, the stack fell down with a big crash. Fluffy tried to run away, but the boxes fell on him. Lily was very sad. She learned not to insist on things that can be dangerous.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He liked to play with his toy robot. Tim's toy robot was big and had a fake smile on its face. Tim loved his robot very much. One day, Tim saw his toy robot fight with another toy. Tim said, "Robot, why are you fighting?" The robot stopped and said, "I am fighting because I want to see who is stronger." Tim did not like that his robot was fighting, so he thought of a plan. Tim put a big weight on the other toy, so it could not move. The robot tried to fight, but the other toy did not move. The robot was confused. Tim said, "Robot, fighting is not good. Let's be friends with the other toy." The robot agreed and they all played together. The robot learned that being friends was better than fighting.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a little girl named Lily. She had a big, round bathtub that she loved to bathe in every day. One day, Lily was very upset because she could not find her favorite bath toy. Her mommy and daddy tried to help her look for it, but it was nowhere to be found. The house was full of silence as they all searched. Lily started to cry because she missed her toy so much. Then, her mommy had a great idea. She took Lily to the store and bought her a new bath toy. When they got home, Lily was so happy to bathe with her new toy. The silence was gone, and the house was filled with the sound of Lily's laughter.

One day, a strong man named Tom wanted to eat a pie. He went to the store and bought a pie. Tom was so happy to eat the pie when he got home. When he got home, Tom put the pie in the oven to warm it up. He sat down and waited for the pie to be ready. But he forgot about the pie, and it started to burn. "Oh no, my pie!" cried Tom. Tom's friend, Sam, came to visit. Sam smelled the burned pie and said, "Don't worry, Tom. I have a surprise for you." Sam opened his bag and took out a new pie. They both laughed and enjoyed the new pie together.

One day, a little fish named Fin was swimming in the sea. He saw a big shark named Sam. Fin was scared, but Sam was nice. They became friends and liked to play together. One day, Sam said to Fin, "I want to be successful. I want to be the best swimmer in the sea." Fin thought for a moment and said, "Let's have a race! If you win, you will be the best swimmer." Sam demanded, "Ready, set, go!" They swam very fast. Sam was big and strong, but Fin was small and quick. They swam and swam, and soon they were near the finish line. In the end, Sam and Fin reached the finish line at the same time. They were both happy and laughed. They knew that they were both successful because they had tried their best and had fun together.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Amy. She was three years old and she liked to go for a walk in the park. One day she was walking in the park and she saw a lady bird sitting in the east. She smiled at the lady bird and said 'Hello'. But the ladybird was very rude. She shouted 'Go away! I don't want you here.' Amy was very sad. She didn't understand why the ladybird was so rude. She sat down and started to cry. Suddenly, a friendly rabbit hopped over to Amy. He said 'Don't cry, little one. There's no need to be sad'. The rabbit gave Amy a big hug and she felt much better. She thanked the rabbit and they both sat in the east and enjoyed the sunshine.
