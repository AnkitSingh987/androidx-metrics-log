Dolphin was very playful. He loved to swim and jump in the ocean. He often wished that he could turn into a human, so he could go on land and play with the other children. One day, he decided to make his wish come true and followed his dream. He swam up to the shore and as far onto the land as he could go. Suddenly, he felt different. He looked down at his body and saw it had changed - he had turned into a human! He was so excited to explore the world of humans! He ran around, playing and laughing with the other children. But soon, the sun began to set and the other people started to go home. Dolphin was scared. How was he going to get back to the sea? Just then, he heard a voice behind him. "Little Dolphin, don't worry. Just wish and I will turn you back into a sea creature like you were before," the kind voice said. So Dolphin did as he was told and soon, he felt himself getting smaller. He looked down and saw that he had turned back into his normal dolphin form. He happily swam away, thankful that he had been able to turn back to the wonderful ocean. He no longer wished to be a human - he was happy to just be a playful dolphin.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Daisy. Daisy was very grumpy one day. She went for a walk to feel better. As Daisy walked, she saw a big, red ball. She thought, "I will play with the ball and maybe I will not be grumpy." But Daisy did not observe where she was going. Daisy kicked the ball and it went far away. She ran to get it, but did not see the big hole. Daisy fell in the hole and could not get out. She was very sad and grumpy. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Amy. Amy liked to sort her toys. She had a whip toy that she loved to play with. She played with her whip toy outside. One day, Amy went to the park. The park had a rough slide. Amy was scared to go on the rough slide. But she took her whip toy with her. Amy climbed up the slide. She held her whip toy tight. She went down the rough slide with her whip toy. Amy was happy. She played at the park all day with her whip toy.

John and his mom walked down the street one day to the barber shop. John was very excited because he wanted to get his hair cut. As they walked, they could smell the sweet smell of fresh-cut hair coming from the shop. When they got to the barber shop, the barber was already waiting. He had a big smiling face and he said, "Hello John! Are you ready for a wild new haircut?" John smiled and clapped his hands. He was so excited he couldn't even say a word. His mom said, "Yes, he is ready. Go ahead and give him the haircut he wants!" The barber went to work, snipping and cutting away. He finished in no time and the haircut was just the way John wanted it. John and his mom thanked the barber and smiled as they walked out of the shop. They were both very happy with John's stunning new wild look.

Lily was restless. She did not want to go to bed. She wanted to play with her toys and watch TV. But Mom said it was time to sleep. "Please, Mom, just five more minutes?" Lily begged. "No, Lily, you need to rest. You have a big day tomorrow. You are going to see the big machine at the museum. Remember?" Mom said. Lily remembered. She was excited to see the big machine. It was a train that could go very fast and make loud noises. She had seen it in a book and wanted to see it for real. "Okay, Mom, I will go to bed. But can you tell me a story about the big machine?" Lily asked. "Sure, sweetie. I will tell you a story. But you have to close your eyes and remain quiet, okay?" Mom said. Lily nodded and snuggled under the covers. Mom sat next to her and began to tell her a story. "Once upon a time, there was a big machine that loved to travel. It would go from one place to another, carrying people and things. It was happy and proud of its work. But one day, it broke down. It could not move anymore. It was sad and lonely. It remained in the same spot for a long time, waiting for someone to fix it." "What happened next, Mom?" Lily asked. "You have to wait and see, Lily. That's the end of the story for tonight. Tomorrow, we will go to the museum and see the big machine. Maybe we will find out how it got fixed. But now, you have to sleep. Good night, Lily. I love you." Mom said. "Good night, Mom. I love you too. And I love the big machine." Lily said. She closed her eyes and fell asleep, dreaming of the big machine. She was not restless anymore. She was happy.
