Once upon a time, there was a taxi. The taxi was yellow and had thick tires. It liked to help people go from one place to another. One day, the taxi saw a big tree. The tree was leaning to one side. It looked like it might fall down. The taxi wanted to help the tree. The taxi asked its friends for help. They all came and pushed the tree. The tree stood up straight again. The tree was very happy. The taxi and its friends felt good about helping. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we feel good too.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a little train. The train was always ready to go. It loved to help people get to where they needed to be. Every day, the train would chug along the tracks, taking people from one place to another. One day, the train saw a small cat stuck on the tracks. The train knew it had to save the cat. As it got closer, the train said, "Little cat, please move! I don't want to hurt you!" But the cat was too scared to move. The train stopped just in time. It was very close to the cat, but it did not hit it. The people on the train came out to help. They picked up the cat and moved it away from the tracks. The cat was safe, and the train could go again. Everyone was happy and thanked the train for being so ready to help. The train felt proud that it saved the little cat.

Once there was a little robot who wanted to go on an adventure. So he passed through the door of his house, and started walking. He soon came to a wild forest, full of birds and flowers. The robot was a bit scared, but he kept walking, determined to find something exciting. Soon, he saw a group of wild animals playing in the grass. He wanted to join them, but he was too afraid to say anything. The animals noticed the little robot and came closer. The robot was so surprised! But he managed to say hello, and soon he was playing with them. They ran around, chasing each other and having lots of fun. The robot had a great time with all the wild animals. He was sad to have to leave, but he knew he had to get back home. So he said goodbye to his new friends and passed back through the door of his house. He knew that the adventure would stay with him forever.

One day, a little girl named Mia went to the park. She saw a person sitting on a bench. The person looked sad. Mia wanted to be polite, so she said "hello" to the person. But the person did not say anything back. Mia felt sad too. Mia tried again, but the person still did not talk. Mia thought the person was ignoring her. She did not like that. But Mia did not give up. She went to find a pretty flower. Mia came back with the flower and gave it to the person. The person looked at Mia and smiled. They said "thank you" to Mia. Mia was happy that the person was not ignoring her anymore. They became friends and played in the park together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot had a big red ball that he loved to play with. One day, Spot's friend, a small cat named Fluffy, came over to play. Fluffy saw the big red ball and wanted to play with it too. Spot was happy to share and let Fluffy borrow the ball. As they played, they saw a tree with a big hole in it. Spot and Fluffy thought it would be a useful place to hide the ball. They put the ball in the hole and went to play other games. They knew the ball would be safe in the useful hole. After a while, Spot and Fluffy wanted to play with the big red ball again. They went back to the tree with the hole, but the ball was gone! They looked around and saw a squirrel holding the ball. The squirrel had found the ball and thought it was a big nut. Spot and Fluffy told the squirrel that it was their ball. The squirrel was sorry and gave the ball back. Spot and Fluffy learned that even the most useful hiding spots can be found. They all played together happily ever after.
