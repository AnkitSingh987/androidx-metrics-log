Lily and Ben like to collect things. They collect rocks, shells, leaves and flowers. One day, they go to the farm with their mom and dad. They see many animals and plants. They also see a big pile of potatoes. "Can we collect some potatoes?" Lily asks. "Sure, but be careful. They are heavy and dirty," mom says. Lily and Ben each take a basket and start to pick up potatoes. They find big ones, small ones, round ones and funny ones. They fill their baskets with potatoes. "Look, I found a fancy potato!" Ben says. He shows Lily a potato that has many bumps and colors. "Wow, it looks like a monster!" Lily says. She laughs. They show their fancy potato to mom and dad. Mom and dad smile. "That's a very special potato. Maybe we can make something yummy with it later," dad says. Lily and Ben are happy. They like to collect potatoes. They wonder what other fancy things they can find on the farm.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had a pink room with lots of toys. Lily loved to play with her toys and look at the pretty pink walls. One day, Lily's mom came into her room with a new thing. It was a bulb. "Look, Lily," her mom said, "This is a new bulb for your room." Lily was excited to see what the bulb would do. Her mom put the bulb in the light and turned it on. The bulb made the room glow in a soft pink light. Lily was so happy. She pointed at the light and said, "Mommy, I love it! My room is even more pink now!" Lily and her mom smiled and played in the pink light until it was time for bed.

One day, a small fish named Tim was swimming in the warm water. He loved to dive deep in the water to find junk. Tim had a big box of junk that he found at the bottom of the sea. He liked to play with the junk and make new things. One day, while Tim was diving for junk, a big shark named Sam came to take Tim's junk. Sam wanted the junk for himself. Tim was sad and didn't know what to do. He didn't want to lose his junk. Tim had an idea. He went to find more junk, but this time he found a very big piece of junk. Tim brought the big junk to Sam the shark. The big junk was so big and heavy that it made Sam sink. Sam was stuck and couldn't swim. Tim helped Sam and they became friends. Now, they both dive for junk and play together in the warm water.

One day, a boy named Mark went to the grocery with his mom. Mark was a creative kid who loved to draw. He brought his crayons and paper with him to draw pictures while his mom shopped. As they walked through the store, Mark saw a big, round, orange pumpkin. He thought it would be fun to draw a face on it. So, Mark asked his mom, "Can I draw a face on the pumpkin, please?" His mom said, "Yes, but only if you promise to be careful." Mark carefully drew a happy face on the pumpkin. But then something unexpected happened. The pumpkin started to talk! It said, "Thank you, Mark! You gave me a face so I can see and talk to you!" Mark and his mom were very surprised, but they made a new friend that day.

One day, a curious cat named Mimi went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. Mimi used her sharp claws to go up, up, up. When she got to the top, she saw a big bird nest with three little eggs. Mimi got close to the eggs and started to whisper, "Hello, little eggs. What is inside you?" She was very curious and wanted to know. The eggs did not say anything, but Mimi knew that baby birds were inside. She waited by the nest, hoping to see them hatch. As Mimi sat by the nest, she saw a big shadow on the ground. She looked up and saw a big bird coming. Mimi was scared, but she did not run away. She stayed to protect the eggs. The big bird landed on the tree and whispered, "Thank you, Mimi, for watching my eggs." The big bird was the mommy bird, and she was happy that Mimi was kind. Mimi and the mommy bird became friends, and they took care of the eggs together. When the eggs hatched, the baby birds had both feathers and fur! Mimi was surprised, but she loved the baby birds just the same. They were a special mix of bird and cat, all because of Mimi's big heart and curious brain. From that day on, Mimi and the mommy bird lived together in the big tree, raising their special family. They whispered stories to the baby birds each night, teaching them about the world. And everyone who saw them knew that love and kindness could create something truly unexpected and beautiful.
