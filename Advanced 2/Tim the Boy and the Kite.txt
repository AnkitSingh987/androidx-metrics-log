Once upon a time, there lived a small and humble boy. Every day, he would come to the same spot to drink from a pond. Every day he would take a big sip from the pond, and the same amount - one inch. One day, the boy decided he could get more out of his drinking if he took two inches. So, he gulped twice as much as he usually did, thinking he would be twice as satisfied. But instead, he ended up spilling more than he had drank, and lost the precious water. The Boy was so upset and embarrassed, but then he realized that he should have been content with what he had. He promised himself that he would go back to drinking only one inch, like he used to. The boy learned it was better to be content with what he had and not risk losing something precious. The moral of the story is that it's important to stay humble and take what you need, nothing more.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little bunny named Binky. Binky lived in a big forest with his family. He loved to play with his friends and hop around the trees. One day, Binky and his friends decided to play a game of hide and seek. Binky was very good at hiding, so he found a great spot behind a big rock. While he was hiding, he heard some loud noises. It sounded like a war between the animals in the forest. Binky was scared and didn't know what to do. He wanted to run away, but he didn't want to embarrass himself in front of his friends. So, he stayed behind the rock and hoped the noises would stop. But the noises didn't stop. They got louder and louder. Binky's friends found him and they all started to cry. The war between the animals was too scary, and they didn't know how to make it stop. In the end, the war didn't stop, and the forest became a very sad and scary place. Binky and his friends couldn't play and have fun anymore. They wished they could go back to the happy times before the war.

They played together and had fun. At night, Cozy kept Sue warm and safe. Sue was happy to have a famous blanket as a friend. They lived happily ever after.

Sara likes to play with her dolls. She has many dolls, but her favorite is Lily. Lily has long, yellow hair that Sara likes to brush and braid. Sara also likes to wash Lily's hair with shampoo. She has a small bottle of shampoo that smells like flowers. One day, Sara decides to give Lily a bath in the sink. She fills the sink with warm water and bubbles. She holds Lily in her arms and gently puts her in the water. She squeezes some shampoo on her hand and rubs it on Lily's hair. She makes a lot of foam and laughs. But then, Sara hears her mom calling her name. She has to go downstairs for lunch. She quickly rinses Lily's hair and wraps her in a towel. She wants to take Lily with her, but she can't find the small bottle of shampoo. She looks around the sink, but it is not there. She feels sad and worried. She runs downstairs with Lily and asks her mom if she has seen the shampoo. Her mom says no, but she helps Sara look for it. They go back to the bathroom and search everywhere. They find the shampoo under the sink, where it rolled when Sara was washing Lily's hair. Sara is happy and relieved. She hugs her mom and says thank you. She holds the shampoo and Lily and goes to have lunch. She thinks Lily's hair smells very nice.
