Francy was walking down the path when she spotted something. It was a tall shadow standing against the trees. She stood there, starring at the shadow. "I wonder who that is?", she thought to herself. Suddenly, the shadow started to move. It stretched out its arm and waved. "Hello Francy!" it said. Francy couldn't believe her eyes. "Who are you?", she asked the shadow, her eyes wide open with excitement. The shadow smiled warmly. "My name is Wilma and I'm your dependable friend. I will be here when you need me, no matter what." Francy was filled with happiness. She reached out and hugged the shadow then smiled. "Thank you for being my dependable friend," Francy said. The shadow smiled again and said, "I will always be with you, Francy."

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to play with her mom's makeup. Her mom was at work, so she thought it was a good time to try it. She opened the makeup box and saw many pretty colors. Lily was very excited and started to put the makeup on her face. "Wow! I look so pretty!" Lily said to herself. But she was careless and made a big mess. The makeup was all over her face, her clothes, and the floor. She didn't care about cleaning it up because she was having so much fun. When her mom came home, she saw the mess and said, "Lily, you should not play with my makeup without asking me. You need to learn to care about your things and not be careless." Lily felt sad and said, "I'm sorry, mom. I will be more careful next time." Her mom hugged her and they cleaned up the mess together. The moral of the story is to always be careful with things that are not yours and to care about keeping your things clean and tidy.

One day, a big dog named Max went to play in the park. Max loved to run and jump. He was very excited to see his friend, a small cat named Lily. Max said, "Hi Lily! Let's play and have fun!" Lily was happy too. Max and Lily played in the dirt. They got very dirty. Their muscles were tired from all the fun. They laughed and said, "We need a bath!" Max's mom came to the park and saw the dirty friends. She said, "Oh no, you two need a bath!" Max and Lily went home to get clean. They had a fun bath with lots of bubbles. After the bath, they were not dirty anymore. Max and Lily felt happy and their muscles were not tired. They said, "We had a great day!"

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a wise elephant. The wise elephant had a big trunk. He used his trunk to help his friends. One day, a small bird fell into a deep hole. The bird was scared and cried for help. The wise elephant heard the bird's cries and went to save him. He used his big trunk to reach down into the hole. The wise elephant's trunk was strong and long. He saved the small bird from the hole. The bird was very happy and thanked the wise elephant. From that day on, they became best friends. The wise elephant and the small bird played together every day in the big forest.

Once upon a time, there was a graceful bird named Bella. Bella loved to fly high in the sky. She was very happy. One day, Bella saw a lot of waste on the ground. She felt sad and began to worry. Bella flew to her friend, Timmy the turtle. "Timmy, look at all the waste on the ground! What can we do?" Bella asked. Timmy thought for a moment and said, "Let's clean it up together, Bella." So, Bella and Timmy went around picking up the waste. They worked hard and soon, the ground was clean again. Bella was no longer worried, and they both felt proud. From that day on, Bella and Timmy took care of their home and kept it clean.

One day, a little girl named Mia was playing in her room. She saw a small purple box on her shelf. Mia was very curious about what was inside the box. She wanted to touch it, but her mom had told her not to touch things without asking first. Mia went to her mom and asked, "Can I touch the purple box in my room?" Her mom gave her a wink and said, "Yes, but be careful." Mia was very happy and ran back to her room. Mia touched the purple box and opened it. Inside the box, there was a big spider! The spider jumped out and scared Mia. She screamed and ran out of her room. The spider followed her, and Mia was very sad. She wished she had never touched the purple box.
