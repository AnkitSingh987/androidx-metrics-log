Once upon a time, there was a little shark. The shark loved to eat yummy fish. He swam in the big sea to find his food. One day, the little shark lost his way. He could not find his home. He was sad and hungry. He wanted to eat a yummy fish. The little shark met a big fish. The big fish was nice. He helped the little shark find his home. They swam together and found the shark's home. The little shark was happy. He said thank you to the big fish. Now, the little shark could eat a yummy fish again.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily loved to play with her toys. Her favorite toy was a small, blue car. She would take it everywhere she went. One sunny day, Lily went to the park to play with her blue car. She saw another child named Tim. Tim had a big red ball. Lily asked Tim, "Can I play with your red ball?" Tim said, "Yes, if I can play with your blue car." Lily and Tim played together all day long. They would take turns with the red ball and the blue car. They laughed and had lots of fun. At the end of the day, they were both very happy. They had made a new friend and learned how to share their toys.

Max and Sandy were best friends. They loved to play together every day. One day, Sandy found an unusual bracelet on the ground. She was so excited and tried it on her wrist. Max saw the bracelet and also wanted to try it. Sandy refused, and said it was only for her. Max felt upset. He decided to try something sneaky. He grabbed the bracelet from Sandy's wrist and ran away. Sandy was angry and ran after him. She tried to get the bracelet back but Max was too fast. Finally, Sandy figured out a plan. She bought a different unusual bracelet for Max in the market. Max was surprised and tried it on. He was happy to have a new bracelet so he was willing to return Sandy's. Max and Sandy hugged each other and said sorry. They both agreed to share the bracelets, and were best friends again.

One day, a big sun came out. It was so hot that it could melt ice. A deaf dog named Spot and a small cat named Fluffy were friends. They loved to play in the park. They played all day long. One day, they saw a big ice cream. They both wanted it. Spot said, "I want the ice cream!" But Fluffy said, "No, I want it!" They were not happy. They did not want to share the ice cream. So, they started to fight. Then, something unexpected happened. The hot sun began to melt the ice cream. Spot and Fluffy stopped fighting. They saw the ice cream melting and felt sad. They learned that fighting was not good. They decided to share the love and be friends again.

Once upon a time, there was a group of animals living in a big forest. They were all friends and played together every day. In this group, there was a little rabbit who was very proud of how fast he could run. One day, while they were playing, a big, scary animal came into the forest. All the animals were scared and wanted to escape. The little rabbit said, "Don't worry, friends! I will help you escape from the big, scary animal." The little rabbit ran very fast to the big, scary animal and said, "Hey, you! Let my friends go!" The big, scary animal laughed and said, "You are too small to stop me!" But the little rabbit was not scared. He ran around the big, scary animal so fast that it got dizzy and fell down. The group of animals cheered and said, "Thank you, little rabbit! You saved us!" The little rabbit felt very proud and happy that he could help his friends escape. From that day on, the animals played together and lived happily in the big forest.

Greg gasped as he saw the present wrapped with a big sparkling bow. He ran to his mom and said, "Mine?" His mom smiled and said, "Yes, go ahead and open it!" Greg opened the present and was so excited to see a brand new toy car. He jumped up and said, "Mine! Mine!" His mom said, "Run and race it around the house, but be careful and don't let it go too fast." Greg raced the car around the living room, the kitchen and the hallway. He was so restless. He couldn't get enough of it. Finally, his mom said, "Greg, you need to wrap up the car now. It's time for a nap." Greg looked at his mom and said, "OK, Mom." Greg took the car and wrapped it up carefully, before heading to his bedroom for a nap.
