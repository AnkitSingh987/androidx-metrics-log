One day, a driver was driving his car. He was angry because he could not find his toy. The toy was very important to him. He looked everywhere in the car, but he could not find it. He was very sad. Then, he saw a big dog on the road. The dog had something in its mouth. The driver stopped the car and looked at the dog. The dog was holding his toy! The driver was very surprised and happy. The driver got out of the car and took the toy from the dog. The dog was not angry. The dog was happy to help. The driver and the dog became good friends. Now, the driver was not angry anymore. They all played with the toy together and had a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a baby bird. He was very young and small, and didn't know much about the world yet. He chirped and flapped his wings, and he was so happy! One night, the little bird was looking for a place to sleep. He looked around, but he couldn't find a spot. He started to feel tired and lonely. Suddenly, he saw a cozy nest high up in a tree. He decided to try to get to the nest and he flapped his wings. The little bird eventually flew up to the nest. He curled up inside and it felt so warm and safe. He snuggled deep down into the soft feathers. His head sank onto the hard prey and he felt so relaxed. As the birds floated off to dreamland he heard a voice. "Hi little bird. Here is your cozy nest for you to sleep in". The little bird smiled and said, "Thank you! I'm so happy I found my nest". And with that, he fell asleep.

Once upon a time, there was a big room with an ancient blackboard. The blackboard was very old and had many marks on it. In the room, there were kids who did not like the blackboard. They would say, "We hate that blackboard!" One day, a new kid came to the room. He saw the blackboard and thought it was special. The new kid said, "I like the blackboard. It is old and has many stories to tell." The other kids did not agree. They still did not like the blackboard. The new kid wanted to show them that the blackboard was good. He took a piece of chalk and drew a big, happy sun on the blackboard. The other kids saw it and started to smile. They took turns drawing on the blackboard. Soon, they all liked the blackboard too. The ancient blackboard was now a place for fun and happy drawings. And the kids never said they hated the blackboard again.

Once upon a time, there was an old man who loved to bike around his neighborhood. Every day he would pedal his bike slowly, taking time to admire the flowers and trees along his route. One day, however, the old man began to feel very tired. He was used to pedaling for hours each day, and the fatigue was starting to catch up with him. He decided he would need to take a nap before he could keep going. So, the old man stopped and prepared to go to sleep. Just then, a young boy of three came running up and asked the old man why he had stopped. He said he was taking a nap, and the boy asked why. The old man explained that if he didn't take a nap to rest, he wouldn't have enough energy to pedal for the rest of the day. The young boy was very serious when the old man finished speaking. He thought for a moment and finally remarked that taking a nap can be good sometimes. The old man was surprised to hear such thoughtful words coming from such a young child! The old man realized the moral of this story – even if it means stopping to take a break, you should always take the time to rest and take care of yourself.

Once upon a time, there were two friends named Tom and Sue. They loved to play together. One day, they found a big sail in Tom's backyard. They wanted to play with it, but they both wanted to hold it. Tom said, "Let's share the sail!" Sue agreed, and they both held the sail together. They pretended to be on a big ship, sailing on the water. They laughed and had so much fun. Tom and Sue felt competitive, so they decided to have a race. They wanted to see who could run the fastest while holding the sail. They counted, "One, two, three, go!" and they ran as fast as they could. In the end, they both reached the finish line at the same time. They laughed and said, "We both won!" They learned that sharing and playing together was more fun than trying to win alone. From that day on, Tom and Sue always shared their toys and played together happily.
