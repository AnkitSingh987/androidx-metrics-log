Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a wealthy girl named Lily. She had many pretty clothes to wear. One day, she saw a beautiful dress on a shelf in a shop. She wanted to wear it to her friend's birthday party. Lily asked her mom, "Can I have the dress on the shelf, please?" Her mom said, "No, we have many clothes at home. You don't need a new dress." Lily was sad and angry. She didn't want to wear her old clothes to the party. At the birthday party, Lily wore her old dress. She saw her friend, Mia, wearing the beautiful dress from the shop. Lily was jealous. Mia saw Lily's sad face and said, "Lily, don't be sad. We can share my dress next time!" Lily smiled and they played together. They had lots of fun at the party.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play in the garden. One day, she found a small patch of flowers. The flowers were sad because nobody cared for them. They needed water and love. Lucy did not want to be selfish. She wanted to help the flowers. So, she brought water and gave it to the flowers every day. She also talked to them and gave them love. Slowly, the flowers became happy and grew big and strong. All the other kids saw how Lucy cared for the flowers. They learned from her and started to care for the garden too. The garden became more beautiful than ever. The kids learned that if they were not selfish and cared for others, everyone could be happy.

Timmy was walking through the forest when he heard a noise. He felt fearful and his heart began to race. He looked around to see what it was and there in front of him was a big, grey wolf! Timmy's legs began to shake. He remembered what his mommy had said - never talk to strangers. He began to walk backwards slowly, hoping the wolf wouldn't follow him. But the wolf followed him, closer and closer. Just then, Timmy remembered what his daddy had said - make a lot of noise! So he started to shout and scream. He waved his arms and jumped up and down. The wolf was so scared that he ran away. Timmy was so relieved. He had remembered what his mommy and daddy had said and it had helped him stay safe. He was so happy and thankful that he started to skip through the forest - he wouldn't forget what he had learned today!

Once upon a time, there was a kind bird. The bird had a sharp beak. The bird lived in a big tree near a small house. One day, the bird saw a can of oil under the tree. The can was open. The bird was curious, so it flew down to look at the oil. The bird thought the oil was shiny and pretty. The bird saw a little girl who lived in the small house. The girl was sad because her bike was squeaky. The kind bird used its sharp beak to pick up some oil. The bird put the oil on the bike's chain. The girl was very happy and thanked the kind bird. They became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fair cook named Tom. Tom loved to mix herbs in his food. One day, he found a new herb in the forest. He thought it would make his food taste even better. He was very excited to try it. Tom went to his friend, Ann, and said, "I found a new herb! Let's mix it in our food for the big party tonight!" Ann looked at the herb and felt unsure. She said, "I don't know, Tom. Maybe we should ask someone first." But Tom did not listen. He was too excited to try the new herb. At the big party, everyone ate the food with the new herb. At first, they all said it was very tasty. But soon, everyone started to feel sick. They all had to go home early. The next day, Tom and Ann felt sad. They knew they should have asked someone about the herb before using it. The moral of the story is: always ask for help when you're unsure about something.
