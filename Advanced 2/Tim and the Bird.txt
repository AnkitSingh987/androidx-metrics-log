One day, a boy named Tim went to visit his friend, Sam. Sam was a funny and sometimes foolish boy. They liked to play and make things together. Today, they wanted to make a big tower with blocks and glue. They put glue on the blocks and stacked them very high. The tower was taller than them! But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird flew into their room and hit the tower. The tower fell down and the blocks went everywhere. Tim and Sam were sad, but then they saw the bird was stuck in the glue. They helped the bird get out of the glue and cleaned it up. The bird was happy and flew away. Tim and Sam learned to be more careful with their glue and not to be foolish. They built a new tower, but this time, they did it outside where no birds could get hurt.

Once upon a time, there was a big bookshelf in a little girl's room. The bookshelf was full of many colorful books. The little girl loved to read her books every day. One day, the girl's friend came to play at her house. The friend saw the bookshelf and said, "Wow! I am envious of your bookshelf. I want one too!" The little girl smiled and said, "You can have one too. Let's ask your mom to get you one." The next day, the friend got a new bookshelf too. Both friends were very happy. They decided to celebrate with a party. They read their favorite books and shared them with each other. They had lots of fun at their bookshelf party.

One day, a little cat named Kitty went for a wander in the big park. Kitty saw a tall tree and wanted to climb it. So, Kitty climbed up the tree to see what was at the top. When Kitty got to the top, she saw a big nest with a frightened little bird inside. The little bird was scared because it could not fly yet. Kitty wanted to help the bird and not scare it more. Kitty had an idea. She climbed down the tree and found the bird's mom. The mom bird was so happy to see her baby safe. The mom bird thanked Kitty for being kind. And that day, Kitty and the birds became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a strong young man called Joe. He lived in a big city and liked to stay active. Every day he liked to jog around the neighbourhood. It made him feel really good. One day, Joe thought that he wanted to go further and run for a long time. He made sure he had all the things he needed, like a hat and some water, and then he set off. On his jog, Joe stopped to take a little break. He looked down and noticed his waist felt a bit tight. He had been jogging harder today and it was making him a bit sore. He thought it was a good feeling and he knew his body was getting stronger. Joe kept going and when he got back home he was feeling just fine. He stretched carefully so he wouldn't hurt himself, and then he looked in the mirror. He could see his waist was getting slimmer and he was so happy. Joe's efforts had paid off. He was sure that if he kept on jogging he'd be super strong.

Once upon a time, there was a small blue spider who liked to climb up the sides of buildings and tease the birds which flew around. The little spider sometimes got too brave, and the birds soon learned to avoid it when they flew by. Just when the spider thought it was safe to stay and swing on the side of the building, a big blue bird came by and tried to scare it away. The spider hissed and wriggled its legs, until the bird left and it was safe again. The little spider kept its eyes open for more birds and kept on teasing them, until it was night time and the spider had to go back home.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play and run all day. One day, he saw a big red ball. Spot ran to the ball and played with it. He was very happy. Then, Spot saw a boy named Tim. Tim was sad because he lost his magazine. Spot wanted to help Tim. So, Spot offered to find the magazine for Tim. Tim smiled and said, "Thank you, Spot!" Spot looked everywhere for the magazine. He found it under a big tree. He picked it up and ran back to Tim. Tim was very happy to have his magazine back. He gave Spot a big hug. They became best friends and played together every day.
