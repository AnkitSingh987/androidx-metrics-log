One day, a fancy cat went for a walk. The cat saw the big, round moon in the sky. The cat wanted to go to the moon. She thought, "How can I open the door to the moon?" The cat walked and walked. She found a big, red balloon. The cat had an idea. She jumped into the balloon and it went up, up, up. The cat thought she would soon open the door to the moon. But then, something unexpected happened. The wind blew the balloon away. The cat did not go to the moon. Instead, she landed in a big tree. The cat was happy to be in the tree. She found a new home and many new friends. The cat did not need the moon anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big radio. He loved to listen to it all day. He would dance and sing with the music. The radio made him very happy. One day, Tim's mom said, "Tim, you need to eat your healthy food before I permit you to play with your radio." Tim did not want to eat his healthy food. He wanted to play with his radio. But he knew he had to listen to his mom. So Tim ate his healthy food. It was not so bad after all. When he finished, his mom said, "Good job, Tim! Now you can play with your radio." Tim was so happy. He turned on the radio and danced and sang all day long.

Johnny liked kicking things. He loved finding something soft and kicking it until it flew up in the air. One day, he found an ornament in his room. He thought it was funny because it had a silly face on it. So he decided to give it a kick. He put his foot back and then pushed it forward. The ornament flew up and hit the ceiling. Johnny laughed and ran around the room excitedly. Next, Johnny tried kicking things off the shelf. He kicked a cute toy cat and it flew across the room. Johnny was giggling with glee. Finally, he went back to the ornament. He wound up his foot and kicked it hard. The ornament flew high and eventually settled underneath the bed. Johnny smiled and ran off to find something else to kick.

Once upon a time, there was a big palace. In the palace, there lived a kind king and queen. They had a little girl named Lily. Lily liked to play with her toys, but one day, her toy horse was broken. Lily was very sad about her broken toy horse. She asked her mommy and daddy to fix it. The king and queen promised to help her. They looked for glue to put the broken toy horse back together. They worked hard and fixed the broken toy horse. Lily was very happy and thanked her mommy and daddy. She promised to be careful with her toys. From that day on, Lily played happily in the palace with her toy horse.

Once upon a time, there was a fast bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat fruit. His favorite fruit was the big red apple. One day, Bob saw a big tree with many red apples on it. But, there was a problem. The tree was in the yard of a big, grumpy bear named Ben. Bob wanted the apples, but he was scared of Ben. So, he thought of a plan. He decided to include Ben in his plan. Bob went to Ben and said, "Let's share the apples. I will run fast and pick them for us." At first, Ben was angry. But then, he thought about it and agreed. Bob ran fast and picked the apples. They both ate the apples together and were happy. The moral of the story is to share and include others. When we share, everyone can be happy.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big mystery. No one knew where all the toys went. All the kids were sad and jealous of each other's toys. One day, a little girl named Lucy saw a cat with a toy in its mouth. She said, "Hey, cat! That's my toy! Allow me to have it back, please!" The cat dropped the toy and ran away. Lucy followed the cat to a secret place. There, she found all the missing toys! The cat was taking the toys because it wanted to play too. Lucy and the cat became friends and shared the toys with all the kids. The mystery was solved, and no one was jealous anymore.
