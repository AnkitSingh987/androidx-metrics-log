Once upon a time, there was a big mouse named Tom. Tom lived in a little house near a big cheese shop. He loved cheese very much. Every day, he would go to the shop and look at all the cheese. One day, Tom had an idea. He wanted to measure how much cheese he could eat. He went to the cheese shop and said, "Hello, I want to measure how much cheese I can eat. Can you help me?" The shop owner smiled and said, "Of course, Tom! Let's find out." Tom started eating cheese, one piece at a time. He ate and ate, but then something unexpected happened. Tom ate so much cheese that he became very big! He was now bigger than the cheese shop itself! The shop owner was surprised and said, "Oh no, Tom! You are too big now. I don't have enough cheese for you." Tom was sad, but then he had another idea. "I will go and find more cheese in the world," he said. And so, big Tom went on a journey to find more cheese. He made many new friends along the way and shared his love of cheese with them. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a curious patient who was always looking for ways to feel better. One day he heard about a magical place that could cure all his ailments. He excitedly journeyed there to get help. When he arrived, he noticed a strange, smelly plant growing in the corner. He wondered what it was, and he was told it was a rot-plant. He was told to eat it and it would make him feel better. The patient was cautious, but he was also desperate for a cure, so he tried it. To his surprise, the rot-plant made him feel much worse. He felt sick and dizzy and he couldn't stand up. He had made a bad decision and there was nothing he could do. The patient had to be carried away and he was never seen again. His curiosity had caused him to make a mistake, and he paid the price. Unfortunately, the patient's story ended sadly.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Max. Max was very obedient. He always listened to his mom and dad. One day, Max's mom told him, "Max, you must stay in the yard and not run away." Max nodded and promised to be good. Later, Max saw a small bird stuck in a tree. The bird cried, "Help me, please!" Max knew he should stay in the yard, but he wanted to rescue the bird. So, Max went to the tree and helped the bird get free. The bird said, "Thank you, Max! You saved me!" Max felt happy about his victory, but he was worried about not being obedient. He ran back to the yard as fast as he could. When Max's mom saw him, she said, "Max, I saw you rescue the bird. I am so proud of you for helping!" Max learned that sometimes it's okay to break the rules if it means helping someone in need.

Once upon a time, in a big harbor, there lived a wealthy man. He had a big boat. He loved to order people to do things for him. One day, he told his helpers to get his boat ready. He wanted to go for a ride. As the boat moved through the water, the wealthy man saw a little fish. The fish was very sad. The man asked the fish, "Why are you sad?" The fish said, "I lost my family. I can't find them." The wealthy man wanted to help the fish. He ordered his helpers to look for the fish's family. They looked and looked, but they couldn't find the fish's family. The wealthy man was sad, too. Then, something unexpected happened. The little fish jumped high into the air and turned into a big, beautiful bird! The bird said, "Thank you for trying to help me. I was a bird under a spell. Your kindness broke the spell." The wealthy man was very surprised. He learned that being kind is important. From that day on, he was nicer to everyone. He didn't just order people around. He helped them, too. And they all lived happily ever after.
