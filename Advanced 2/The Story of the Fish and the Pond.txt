Once upon a time there was a perfect marriage between two fish. They lived in a deep pond far away from everyone else. Every day they would dive around the pond together and play games. One day, another fish came to join them. This new fish was very different as it could jump out of the pond and soar high above it. The two fish were amazed at how this fish could bravely dive up and out into the sky. The two fish wanted to do what the new fish could do. They asked the new fish to take them on a dive up and out of the pond. The new fish was hesitant, but agreed to show them how to do it. But the two fish were too scared to jump out of the pond that day. The new fish encouraged them to be brave and to have courage. But the two fish were too afraid to jump. The new fish told them that only through courage can things be made perfect. Although the two fish did not dive out of the pond that day, the new fish's words of wisdom helped the two fish to stay devoted to their marriage, even when things were hard. And this is why it is important to have courage and to be brave.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a deer named Dara. Dara had a big horn on her head. She was a graceful deer, and all her friends liked her. One day, Dara met a wise old bird named Bob. Bob said, "Dara, you can improve your running if you practice every day." Dara liked the idea and started to practice running with her friends. Days went by, and Dara became a faster runner. Her friends were happy for her. Dara thanked Bob for his help. "Thank you, Bob, for helping me improve my running," Dara said. And they all lived happily in the big forest.

One day, a quiet dolphin named Dolly lived in the sea. Dolly loved to play and swim with her friends. She liked to display her tricks, like jumping high and flipping in the air. One sunny day, Dolly met a little fish named Finny. Finny was shy and did not know how to play. Dolly wanted to help Finny have fun. She said, "Hi Finny, do you want to play with me and learn some tricks?" Finny smiled and said, "Yes, please! I want to play and learn tricks too!" So, Dolly showed Finny how to jump high and flip in the air. They played all day long, and Finny learned many tricks. At the end of the day, Finny said, "Thank you, Dolly! I had so much fun today!" Dolly was happy to help her new friend. From that day on, Dolly and Finny played together every day, and the sea was full of happy, playful friends.

One day, a small cat named Kitty was playing with her toy. She saw a big carpet in the room. Kitty wanted to roll on the carpet. So, she jumped on it and started to roll. As Kitty rolled on the carpet, she heard a tiny voice say, "Help!" Kitty stopped rolling and looked around. She saw a little bug who looked helpless. The bug said, "I am stuck and can't move. Can you help me?" Kitty wanted to help the bug. But, as she tried to help, the carpet started to roll by itself! Kitty and the bug were both surprised. They both rolled on the carpet together, laughing and having fun. In the end, the bug wasn't stuck anymore, and Kitty made a new friend.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Jess. Jess and her mom were doing some cleaning in the house. Jess watched as her mom used the mop to clean the floors. Jess asked her mom, "What are you doing?" Her mom replied, "I'm mopping the floors so they are nice and clean." Jess wanted to help out and asked, "Can I mop too?" Her mom nodded and gave Jess a mop of her own. Jess was so excited to help. She was getting ready to mop when her mom warned her, "Remember, be gentle with the mop. Use light and gentle strokes. You don't want to be too harsh, ok?" Jess nodded and got to work. The floor looked so much nicer once she had finished mopping. Jess and her mom were both very proud. They had made something so nice happen together.
