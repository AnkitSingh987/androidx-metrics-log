Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a big book called a dictionary. This dictionary was very interesting. It had many words and their meanings. Tim loved to read his dictionary every day. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the dictionary and said, "Wow, that's a big book! What is it?" Tim replied, "It's my interesting dictionary. It helps me learn new words!" Sam was excited and wanted to read it too. As they read the dictionary together, they found a page with a word missing. Tim said, "Oh no, we need to replace the missing word!" They looked and looked, and finally found the right word. They replaced the missing word with a sticker. Tim and Sam were happy they fixed the dictionary. They continued to read and learn more words together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom loved to paint. He would paint every day. One day, Tom wanted to make an original painting. He thought and thought about what to paint. Tom saw his friend, Sam, painting a tree. "I want to paint a tree too!" Tom said. Sam looked at Tom and smiled. "Why not paint something different?" Sam asked. Tom thought about it and decided to paint a big, red sun. When Tom finished his painting, he was proud of the result. He showed his painting to Sam. Sam was happy to see Tom's original painting. "Good job, Tom!" Sam said. "Your painting is very special." The moral of the story is: Be original and create something unique.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his drum. He would play it all day long. It made him very happy. His drum was red and it made a loud noise when he hit it. One mild day, Tim went to the park with his mom. He took his drum with him. He played his drum near the big tree. Suddenly, he saw something in the tree. It was a small bird. The bird looked at Tim and started to sing a song. Tim was very surprised. Tim played his drum along with the bird's song. They made a beautiful sound together. Tim's mom and other kids came to listen. Everyone clapped and danced to the music. Tim and the bird were so happy. They had made a fun surprise for everyone at the park.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to play together. They have a big map of the world on the wall. They point to different countries and say their names. "Look, this is China," Ben says. "It is very big and far away." "And this is France," Anna says. "It is small and near. My grandma is from France. She speaks French." "Wow, that is cool," Ben says. "Can you speak French too?" Anna shakes her head. "No, I only know a few words. Bonjour, merci, au revoir." Ben smiles. "That is good. You can teach me. I want to learn French." Anna feels happy. She likes to share her grandma's language. She encourages Ben to repeat the words after her. "Bonjour, merci, au revoir." "Bonjour, merci, au revoir," Ben says. He tries to sound like Anna. Anna claps her hands. "You are doing great. You are a fast learner." Ben blushes. He feels proud. He likes to learn new things. He thanks Anna for teaching him. "Merci, Anna. You are a good teacher." Anna hugs Ben. She feels proud too. She likes to help her friend. She says goodbye to him. "Au revoir, Ben. See you tomorrow." "Au revoir, Anna. See you tomorrow," Ben says. He waves to her. They both look at the map again. They wonder what other countries and languages they can learn. They are curious and happy. They are friends.

One day, a little girl named Sue went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to turn around it. As she turned, she found a lollipop on the ground. Sue picked it up and felt very happy. She knew that someone must have dropped it. She met a boy named Tom who was very sad. Tom said, "I lost my lollipop and now I am not calm." Sue thought about the lollipop she found. She knew it was Tom's lollipop. Sue gave the lollipop to Tom and said, "Here, I found your lollipop!" Tom was so happy and became calm again. They both smiled and played together. From that day on, Sue and Tom were the best of friends.
