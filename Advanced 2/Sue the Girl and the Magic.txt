One day, a little girl named Sue went to the garden to study. She saw a pretty red rose. Sue liked the rose very much. She wanted to show the rose to her best friend, Tom. "Look, Tom!" said Sue. "I found a pretty rose. Isn't it nice?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, Sue. It is very nice. You are a dependable friend for showing me the rose." But then, something unexpected happened. The rose started to talk! The rose said, "Hello, Sue and Tom! I am a magic rose. I can help you with your study." Sue and Tom were very surprised. They never saw a talking rose before. They were happy to have a new friend. The magic rose helped Sue and Tom study every day. They learned a lot from the rose and had fun together.

Lily was a little girl who liked to play in the garden. She liked to look at the flowers and the bugs. One day, she saw a caterpillar on a leaf. It was green and fuzzy and had many legs. Lily wanted to touch the caterpillar. She reached out her hand and gently picked it up. The caterpillar tickled her palm. Lily smiled and said, "Hello, caterpillar. You are so cute. Do you want to be my friend?" The caterpillar did not say anything. It was busy eating the leaf. It was very hungry. It needed to eat a lot of leaves to grow big and strong. Then it could make a cocoon and become a butterfly. Lily did not know that. She thought the caterpillar was just eating for fun. She said, "Caterpillar, you eat too much. You will get a tummy ache. Why don't you share your leaf with me? I am hungry too." The caterpillar did not want to share. It knew that eating the leaf was important for its life. It tried to wiggle away from Lily's hand. But Lily held it tight. She said, "Don't be greedy, caterpillar. Sharing is nice. Here, I will take a bite of your leaf. Mmm, yummy." Lily bit the leaf. It was not yummy. It was bitter and chewy. Lily made a face and spat it out. She said, "Yuck, caterpillar. Your leaf is gross. How can you eat that? I don't want to be your friend anymore. You can have your leaf back." Lily put the caterpillar back on the leaf. The caterpillar was glad. It ate the leaf as fast as it could. It hoped that Lily would leave it alone. It wanted to be a butterfly. It did not care about Lily. It thought, "Silly girl. You don't know anything. Eating the leaf is important for me. It is my dream."

One day, a boy named Tom was very hungry. He wanted to eat spaghetti. So, he asked his mom, "Mom, can you make spaghetti for me?" His mom said, "Yes, I will send you to the store to buy it." Tom felt lucky because he loved going to the store. He went to the store and bought spaghetti. But when he came back, he saw a sad dog. The dog was hungry too. Tom thought about the dog and decided to share his spaghetti with the dog. Tom and the dog ate the spaghetti together. They both felt happy and full. Tom learned that sharing with others can make you feel good. And the dog was lucky to find a friend like Tom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was different from the other kids. He had a special power. He could save people when they were in trouble. One day, Tim heard a loud noise. It was a big wake from a boat. He saw a little girl in the water. She was scared and needed help. Tim jumped into the water and swam to her. He said, "Don't worry, I will save you!" Tim grabbed the little girl's hand and pulled her to the shore. The little girl was so happy that Tim saved her. She said, "Thank you for saving me!" Tim smiled and said, "You're welcome! I'm glad I could help." From that day on, Tim and the little girl became best friends. They played together every day. Tim was happy to be different because he could save people and make new friends.
