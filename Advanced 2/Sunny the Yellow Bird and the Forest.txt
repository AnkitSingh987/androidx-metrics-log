Once upon a time, there was a little yellow bird named Sunny. Sunny lived in a big tree with his friends. They played all day and sang songs. One day, Sunny saw a fire in the forest. He was scared, but he knew he had to help. Sunny flew to his friends and said, "Fire! We must help put it out!" His friends nodded and they all flew to the river to get water. They worked together to carry water in their beaks and dropped it on the fire. Soon, the fire was out and the forest was safe again. Sunny and his friends were very happy that they could help. They sang a happy song and played together for the rest of the day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Mia. Mia liked to play with her friends. They were very popular. Mia and her friends loved to play dress-up. One day, Mia found a box of shiny jewelry. There were necklaces, bracelets, and rings. Mia and her friends were very happy. They put on the jewelry and felt like princesses. Mia's little brother, Tom, wanted to play too. He kneeled down and asked Mia if he could wear some jewelry. Mia said yes and shared her jewelry with Tom. They all played together and had lots of fun.

One day, a bald man named Tim went on a trip. He took a big bag with him. Inside the bag, he had many things like clothes, books, and toys. When he got to the place, he started to unpack his bag. He took out a soft cloth to clean his things. As he was unpacking, a small bird flew by and saw the cloth. The bird thought the cloth was very pretty. It flew down and said, "Hi, can I have your cloth, please?" Tim was surprised. He did not know that birds could talk. He said, "Okay, you can have it." The bird took the cloth and flew away. Tim went back to unpacking his bag. Suddenly, the bird came back. It had turned into a magical fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you for giving me the cloth. I will give you a wish because you were so kind." Tim was very happy. He wished for a new friend to play with. The fairy made his wish come true, and Tim had a new friend to play with on his trip.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily was going on a trip with her family. She had a big luggage to pack all her toys and clothes. Lily was excited to play with her toys on the trip. Before they left, Lily's mom said, "Lily, please shut your luggage so we can go." Lily closed her luggage, but she did not do it right. She left a small part open. Lily's mom did not see it. On the trip, Lily was very graceful. She danced and played with her toys. But when they got to their destination, they saw that Lily's luggage was open. All her toys and clothes were gone! Lily was sad because she lost her favorite toys.

Once upon a time, in a quiet little town, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to draw pictures and put them in envelopes. She gave the envelopes to her friends and family to make them happy. One day, Lily's mom asked her to order more envelopes from the store. Lily went to the store and said, "I want to order envelopes, please." The store man smiled and gave her a big box of envelopes to take home. Lily went back to her quiet house and drew more pictures. She put the pictures in the new envelopes and gave them to her friends and family. Everyone was happy, and Lily felt proud of her work.

Once upon a time, there was a big dog named Max. Max was very patient. He liked to play with his ball on the ground. One day, Max saw a big tree with a ball stuck in it. Max wanted to get the ball down. He had an idea. Max decided to shake the tree. He pushed the tree with his strong body. The tree moved a little. Max did not give up. He shook the tree again and again. Finally, the ball fell from the tree to the ground. Max was so happy. He picked up the ball in his mouth and ran to his home. Max played with his ball all day long. He was a very patient and happy dog.
