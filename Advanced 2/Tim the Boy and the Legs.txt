One day, a boy named Tim wanted to create something fun. He had a toy box with many things inside. He found two sticks and thought, "I can make legs for a toy!" Tim took the sticks and put them on a toy car. Now the car had legs! He was very happy and showed his mom. She smiled and said, "Good job, Tim!" Tim played with his car with legs all day. He ran with it and made it jump. But, after playing so much, Tim felt dizzy. He sat down and rested. When he felt better, he played some more with his car with legs. And they had a great day together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little leaf. The little leaf was green and happy. It lived on a tall tree with many other leaves. One day, the wind came and started to blow. The little leaf was frightened. It did not want to leave its tree. It said, "Please, wind, do not take me away!" But the wind did not listen and blew harder. The little leaf held on tight to the tree, but it could not stay. It flew away with the wind. The little leaf was sad, but soon it made new friends. It met other leaves and they played together. The little leaf was not frightened anymore.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Sarah. She was three and loved to explore. One day, Sarah had an adventure to the deep, dark woods. She wanted to stay there all day. So she made a colourful blanket out of flowers and lay down on it. She was so excited to stay in the woods. Just then, Sarah saw a butterfly. It was so beautiful. She chased after it. "Stay still, butterfly!" she called. But the butterfly kept on flying. So Sarah stayed still and waited. Suddenly, Sarah saw something that made her smile. It was a buttercup! The petals were bright yellow and the centre was full of butter. It was delicious. She carefully picked it, ate it and stayed still to enjoy the taste. Then Sarah headed home. She was happy to have had her deep adventure.

One day, a little mouse named Tim went to choose some cheese. He went to the big cheese house. There were many kinds of cheese there. Tim saw a yellow cheese, a white cheese, and an unknown cheese. The unknown cheese was a funny color. It was not yellow or white. It was something new. Tim wanted to try the unknown cheese. He took a small bite. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! The unknown cheese made Tim feel very funny. He felt like he was growing bigger and bigger! Now, Tim was a big mouse, not a little mouse. He was so big that he could not fit in the cheese house anymore. Tim was sad. He did not want to be a big mouse. Then, he saw a small piece of the yellow cheese. He thought, "Maybe if I eat the yellow cheese, I will become small again." Tim took a bite of the yellow cheese. To his surprise, he started to feel smaller and smaller! Soon, he was a little mouse again. Tim was very happy. From that day on, he never chose the unknown cheese again.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a dependable dog named Max. Max was very good at helping people. One day, Max found a bottle of glue on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and carried it with him. Max saw a little girl named Lisa, who was trying to fix her broken toy. Lisa was sad because her toy was not working. Max knew he could help her with the glue he found. He went to Lisa and gave her the glue. Lisa was very happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Later, a big wind came and blew down a tree. The tree blocked the road, and no one could go through. The people in the town did not know what to do. Then, Max had an idea. He went to the tree and put glue on it. The people were confused, but Max demanded them to help him push the tree. Everyone pushed the tree together, and the glue made the tree stick to the ground on the side of the road. The people were surprised and happy, and they all thanked Max for his smart idea.
