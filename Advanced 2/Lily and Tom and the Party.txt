Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a girl named Lily and a boy named Tom. They were very happy because they were going to have a big marriage party. Everyone in the village was excited too. They all helped to make the party the best one ever. One day, as they were getting ready for the marriage, the weather became very icy and cold. The village people started to worry. They did not want the icy weather to spoil the big day. They all thought hard about what they could do to keep the marriage party warm and happy. Then, a kind woman named Sue had a great idea. She told everyone to bring their warm blankets and clothes to the marriage party. They all worked together to make the party place warm and cozy. The icy weather did not spoil the marriage party, and Lily and Tom had a wonderful time. Everyone in the village danced and laughed, and they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a cat named Tom went to deliver a big box. The box was for his friend, a dog named Max. Tom walked and walked until he got to Max's house. He knocked on the door and said, "Max, I have a box for you!" Max was very happy to see the box. He opened it and found a high hat and a drum. Tom and Max decided to start a band. They played loud and fun music for all their friends. Everyone liked their band and danced to their music. One day, a little bird came to listen to their band. The bird was sad because it could not play music. Tom and Max wanted to help the bird. They found a small whistle for the bird to play. The bird was so happy and joined their band. The music was even better now with the bird. The moral of the story is that when you help others, everyone can be happy and have fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a blue bus. The blue bus went on a tour every day. It took kids to see many fun things. The kids were always happy on the tour. One day, a big boy named Tom went on the tour. Tom was not nice. He liked to tear things. He saw a little girl named Sue with a pretty hat. Sue said, "I like your hat." Tom said, "I will tear your hat." Sue was sad. Tom tore Sue's hat. The kids on the tour were sad. The blue bus stopped. The tour was over. Everyone went home with a bad day.

One day, a boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw his bike and decided to go for a ride. But first, he knew he needed his helmet to keep his head safe. He looked everywhere for his helmet but could not find it. "Mom, where is my helmet?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Look in your room, Tim. It might be there." Tim went to his room and found his helmet under his bed. He put it on and went outside to ride his bike. Tim rode his bike up and down the street. He was having so much fun! After a while, Tim started to feel tired. He decided to go home and rest. When he got home, he put his bike away and took off his helmet. He went inside and told his mom, "I had fun, but now I'm tired." His mom smiled and said, "That's okay, Tim. It's time for a nap anyway."

Once upon a time, there was a graceful cat named Lily. She liked to imagine she was a princess. She would dance and jump all day long. One day, Lily met a friendly dog named Max. Max liked to give orders. He told Lily to sit, and she did. He told Lily to jump, and she did. They played together and had a lot of fun. Lily and Max became best friends. They imagined they were in a big castle. Lily was the princess and Max was the king. They played all day and lived happily ever after.
