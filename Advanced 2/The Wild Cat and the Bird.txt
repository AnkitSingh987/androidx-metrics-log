Once upon a time, there was a wild cat. This cat was very slow. It lived in a big forest with many trees. The wild cat had no friends. One day, the slow cat met a small bird. The bird was hurt. The cat wanted to care for the bird. So, the cat took the bird to its home. The cat gave the bird food and water. The bird got better. The bird and the cat became good friends. They played together every day. The wild cat was not alone anymore. The cat and the bird were very happy.

One day, a little girl named Lucy found a big chest in her room. "Wow, this is incredible!" she said. She wanted to know what was inside, but it was locked. Lucy asked her mom for help. "Mom, can you recommend how to open this big chest?" she asked. Her mom thought for a moment and said, "Maybe we can use a key. Let's look for one." So they searched the house for a key that could open the chest. Finally, they found a small key hidden in a drawer. Lucy put the key in the lock and turned it. The chest opened! Inside, there were many colorful balloons. The balloons started to float out of the chest and filled the room. Lucy and her mom were surprised and laughed. They did not expect the balloons to come out of the chest. They played with the balloons all day. Lucy's mom said, "This was an incredible day. I'm glad we found the key to open the chest." Lucy smiled and hugged her mom. She was happy they had this fun adventure together.

One day, a big family went to a tall building. They wanted to go up to the top floor. They saw an elevator. The elevator was cheap and old. The family was not sure if it would work well. They got into the elevator. The door closed. The elevator started to go up. Then, it stopped. The family was scared. They did not know what to do. They pushed the buttons but nothing happened. They were stuck. A man outside heard them. He called for help. After some time, help arrived. The people who came to help opened the elevator door. The family was happy to be out. They thanked the helpers and went home. They decided to use the stairs next time.

Once upon a time, there was a lawyer. He liked to gather pretty rocks. One day, he found a very rare rock. It was shiny and big. He was very happy. The lawyer showed the rare rock to his friends. They all wanted to see it too. They gathered around and looked at the shiny rock. They all liked it a lot. But then, something unexpected happened. The rare rock started to move! It was not a rock at all. It was a turtle! The turtle walked away, and the lawyer and his friends laughed. They were surprised by the twist, but still had fun gathering rocks together.

Once upon a time, there was a nurse named Jane. She loved to help people and make them feel better. Jane worked in a big, white building where people went when they were sick or hurt. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the building. He was sad because he had a tight feeling in his tummy. Jane saw Tim and said, "Hi, Tim! Let's see what we can do to make you feel better." Jane took Tim to a room and gave him some medicine to help with the tight feeling. Then, she told him a funny joke that made Tim laugh. "Why did the chicken cross the road?" Jane asked. "To get to the other side!" Tim laughed and laughed. He started to feel better already. After a little while, Tim's tummy felt all better. He was so happy that he hugged Jane and said, "Thank you, Jane! You made me feel so much better!" Jane smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. I'm glad I could help." From that day on, Tim and Jane were good friends. They laughed and played together whenever Tim came to visit the big, white building. And they all lived happily ever after.
