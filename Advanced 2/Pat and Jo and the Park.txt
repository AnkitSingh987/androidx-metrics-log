Once upon a time there were two friends, Pat and Jo. Jo was a fast runner and Pat was slow. One day, Pat and Jo were playing by the park when they saw an open door. Pat wanted to go in, but Jo said they shouldn't. Pat argued that they should, but Jo remained firm. In the end, Pat decided to go in, but slowly he went. Inside, Pat saw something shiny, but as he tried to take it, a person came out of a nearby room! Pat wanted to run out, but the person was too fast. Pat tried to move fast, but he was too slow. In the end, Pat couldn't escape and had to remain there. Jo was very sad, but he could do nothing to help his friend. Pat was in trouble and the sad ending remained.

Once upon a time, there was a dog named Tom. Tom had a best friend. His friend's name was Frank. One day, the two friends were playing in the yard. Frank noticed something strange on the ground. It was a big, green avocado. Frank decided he wanted to mail the avocado to his grandma. So, he put the avocado in an envelope and stamped it. He ran to the post box and mailed it away. Tom was very jealous of his friend. He wanted to mail something too. So, he took the hose and sprayed his neighbor's clean car. The car became very wet. The neighbor was not happy. He came outside and saw Tom. He said "What did you do? That car was clean!" Tom couldn't explain why he had done it. He ran away crying. Frank and Tom never got to mail anything else again. They were not friends anymore. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a favorite mug that she liked to drink from every day. The mug was big and blue, with a picture of a smiling sun on it. One day, Mia wanted to drink some water, but she could not find her mug. She looked all around her house, but it was not there. Mia was very sad. She asked her mom for help to find her mug. Her mom said, "Let's open the cupboard and look inside." Mia opened the cupboard, and there it was! Her favorite mug was hiding behind a big, clear bowl. She was so happy to find it. Mia took her mug and filled it with water. She drank the water and smiled at the sun on her mug. Mia and her mug were together again, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lily. Lily loved to skip and play outside. She had a good heart and was very kind to everyone. One day, while Lily was skipping, she found a talking bird. The bird said, "I have wisdom to share with you." Lily was surprised but wanted to hear what the bird had to say. The bird told Lily, "If you are good and kind, you will find a big surprise." Lily was excited and continued to be good and kind. One day, while skipping, she found a big box. She opened the box and there was the bird again! The bird said, "I am the surprise! I am a magic bird, and I will be your friend forever." Lily was so happy, and they played and skipped together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey. The turkey was very restless. It liked to run and jump all day. The turkey had a friend, a little boy named Tim. Tim and the turkey played together every day. One day, the turkey and Tim saw a big, mean dog. The dog wanted to fight the turkey. The turkey did not want to fight, but it wanted to protect Tim. So, the turkey puffed up its feathers and made a loud noise. The big, mean dog was scared of the loud turkey. The dog ran away and did not fight. Tim hugged the turkey and they were both very happy. They played together, and the restless turkey was a hero.
