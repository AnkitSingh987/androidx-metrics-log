Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a very organized boy. He always put his toys away when he was done playing with them. One day, Tim could not find his favorite toy car. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not see it. Tim started to cry. His mom heard him cry and came to help. "What's wrong, Tim?" she asked. Tim said, "I can't find my toy car!" His mom said, "Let's look together. It will only take a minute." So, they looked and looked. And in just a minute, they found the toy car under the couch. Tim was so happy! He stopped crying and thanked his mom. From that day on, Tim always made sure his toys were organized so he could find them easily.

Once upon a time, there was a green frog named Tim. Tim was a proud frog who loved to hop and play all day. He lived in a pond with his friends, the fish and the ducks. One day, Tim found a long string. He wanted to learn how to tie a knot. He asked his friend, the duck, to help him. The duck showed Tim how to loop the string and pull it tight. Tim tried and tried, but he could not tie the knot. Tim did not give up. He asked his friend, the fish, to help him. The fish showed Tim how to twist the string and make a bow. This time, Tim tried again and he tied the knot! Tim was so proud of himself for learning how to tie the knot. From that day on, Tim and his friends played a new game. They would tie knots and bows with the long string. They had so much fun together, and Tim was always proud of the new skill he had learned.

Once upon a time, there was a big wardrobe in a room. It was a special wardrobe. It liked to be independent and do things by itself. It had many pretty clothes inside. The wardrobe loved to clap its doors to make the clothes dance. One day, a little girl named Lily came into the room. She saw the wardrobe and wanted to play with it. She opened the doors and saw all the pretty clothes. Lily wanted to try them on. When she put on a dress, the wardrobe started to clap its doors. As the wardrobe clapped, something unexpected happened. The pretty clothes came to life! The clothes started to dance with Lily. They danced and twirled around the room. Lily and the clothes were having so much fun. But then, the wardrobe stopped clapping. The clothes stopped dancing and went back inside the wardrobe. Lily was sad that the clothes stopped dancing. She clapped her hands, and the clothes started dancing again! Now, Lily knew the secret of the special wardrobe. She made a promise to visit the wardrobe every day to dance with the clothes. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a bright sun who loved to shine all day. He would light up the sky and make everyone feel warm and happy. But sometimes, the bright sun would forget to shine. On those days, it would be dark and cold. One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw the bright sun was not shining. Lily wanted to help the sun remember to shine. She thought, "Maybe if I give the sun a touch, it will remember to be bright again." Lily found a big, tall tree and climbed all the way to the top. She reached up as high as she could and touched the sun. The sun felt her warm touch and remembered to shine. The sky became bright and warm again. Lily was very happy, and so was everyone else. From that day on, the sun never forgot to shine again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim was an adventurous bird who loved to fly around and explore. One sunny day, Tim decided to find a new house. Tim flew high and low, looking for the perfect house. He saw a big tree with a small hole. Tim flew inside and said, "Hello! Is anyone home?" A tiny mouse peeked out and said, "Hi, I am Mimi. This is my house." Tim smiled and said, "Nice to meet you, Mimi. I am looking for a house too." Together, Tim and Mimi looked for a house for Tim. They found a cozy nest on a branch. Tim was happy and started to whistle a happy song. Mimi clapped her tiny hands and danced. From that day on, Tim and Mimi were best friends, and they had many fun adventures together.
