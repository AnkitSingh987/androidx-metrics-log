Once upon a time, in a big green forest, there lived a little bunny named Bob. Bob had a lot of friends, like the birds, the squirrels, and the other bunnies. They all liked to play together. One sunny day, Bob and his friends decided to play a game of hide and seek. Bob was the one who had to find his friends. He started to look for them. He looked behind the trees, under the bushes, and even by the river. But he could not find any of his friends. Then, Bob saw a long neck behind a bush. He knew it was his friend, the giraffe. He said, "I found you!" The giraffe laughed and came out from the bush. They both went to find the other friends. At last, they found all of them, and they all played together until the sun went down.

One day, a fancy cat named Lily went for a walk. She saw her friend, a dog named Max, sit on the grass. Lily went to sit next to Max. They both looked at the sky and saw big clouds. "Look, Max! I see a car in the sky!" said Lily. Max looked and said, "Me too! It's a gas car!" They both laughed and played, not knowing that something fun was about to happen. Soon, a big gas balloon came down from the sky. It was the same car they saw in the clouds! The car had a big smile and said, "Hi, Lily and Max! Want to go for a ride?" Lily and Max jumped into the car and flew up into the sky. They had a great day with their new friend, the gas car.

Once upon a time, there was a thin pumpkin. It was so thin that it could barely stand. One day, a little girl saw the pumpkin and said to it, "Hello, thin pumpkin!" The pumpkin didn't reply, so the little girl decided to look inside. When she looked inside the pumpkin, she gasped with surprise. Inside the pumpkin was a tiny little bird! The little girl said to the bird, "Hello, tiny bird. What are you doing inside this pumpkin?" The little bird replied, "I got stuck in here! Please help me get out!" So the little girl carefully lifted the bird out of the pumpkin. Then she said, " farewell tiny bird!" The little bird thanked the girl and then flew away. The little girl watched it go, feeling very proud of herself.

Little Lisa was very excited when her parents told her they were taking her to the zoo. She had never been before and couldn't wait to see all the different animals. When they got to the zoo, Lisa was amazed by the sights. She loved the big lions and the big giraffes, but her favorite things of all were the zebras. She loved to look at the stripes and dream of what it would be like to have a zebra of her own. Lisa ran to the zebra pen and begged her mom and dad to let her in. They warned her to behave and to not get too close. Lisa said she was being very tidy but when no one was looking, she got closer and closer to the zebras. When Lisa reached out to pet the zebra, it kicked and ran away. Lisa started to cry and her parents were very angry. They told her that she had not behaved and that she was in big trouble. Little Lisa learned her lesson and from then on, she always behaved wherever she was.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there lived a little tiger. The little tiger loved to play and run all day long. His favorite game was to rush through the trees and jump into the leaves. One day, while the little tiger was rushing through the forest, he saw a big, bad wolf. The bad wolf was very scary and wanted to eat the little tiger. The little tiger was very scared and did not know what to do. The little tiger tried to run away from the bad wolf, but the bad wolf was too fast. The bad wolf caught the little tiger and ate him up. And that was the end of the little tiger. The forest was very sad without the little tiger to play and rush through the trees.
