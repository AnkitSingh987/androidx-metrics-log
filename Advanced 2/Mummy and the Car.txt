Mummy had something very exciting to explain to Dan. "What is it, Mummy?" asked Dan. Mummy smiled and said, "It's a network." Dan looked confused. Mummy could see he didn't know what a network was, so she explained. "A network is like a lot of roads that you can drive on. You can go from one place to another on the roads." Dan was so happy. He loved driving in the car and now he could imagine lots of roads that he could drive around on. "Can I go on the network now, Mummy?" he asked eagerly. Mummy laughed. "Not now sweetie," she said. "But we can go for a drive and you can show me which roads you think make up the network." Dan was very happy at this idea. He clapped his hands and said, "Let's go!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sara. She loved to play with her soft toy named Velvet. Velvet was a pretty, red rabbit. Sara and Velvet were best friends. One sunny day, Sara and Velvet went to the park to play. They saw a big tree with tasty apples. Sara picked some apples for her and Velvet to eat. The apples were sweet and juicy. While they were eating, they heard a sound. It was a little boy named Tim. Tim was playing with his toy car. Sara and Velvet went to meet Tim. They all became friends and played together all day long.

One day, a yellow cat named Sam was playing in the yard. He saw a big hose on the ground. Sam thought it would be fun to play with the hose. He jumped on it and started to roll around. Just then, a little girl named Amy came outside. She saw Sam playing with the hose and said, "Sam, do you want to play with water?" Sam meowed and nodded his head. Amy turned on the water and the hose began to rush with water. Sam and Amy laughed as they played with the hose. But, suddenly, a big frog jumped out of the hose! Sam and Amy were very surprised. The frog said, "Thank you for helping me out of the hose!" Sam and Amy smiled, happy to have a new friend to play with. They all played together in the yard until the sun went down.

One day, a girl named Lily went to the park. The park was empty, and she felt lonely. She saw a guard standing near the gate. The guard looked bored and tired. Lily walked up to the guard and said, "Hi! I am Lily. Do you want to play with me? I can invite my friends too!" The guard smiled and said, "That sounds nice, but I have to stay here and watch the gate." Lily had an idea. She invited her friends to the park, and they all brought toys to play with. They played near the guard so he could watch them and feel happy. The park was not empty anymore, and everyone had a great time. The guard was glad that Lily invited her friends, and he felt less lonely too.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a hoop that he loved to play with every day. One day, Tim saw a girl named Sally playing with her hoop too. Tim wanted to play with Sally, so he walked up to her and asked, "Can I play with you?" Sally looked at Tim and said, "Yes, but you have to behave. My hoop is very special to me, and I don't want it to get spoiled." Tim nodded and promised to be careful. They played together, rolling their hoops and laughing. Tim behaved very well and did not spoil Sally's hoop. They had so much fun that they became best friends. From that day on, Tim and Sally played with their hoops every day, always remembering to behave and take care of their special toys.

One day, a monkey went to the woods. He saw a big tree with bitter fruit. The monkey wanted to make the fruit sweet. He thought, "I will add sugar to the fruit." The monkey went to his friend, the bird. The bird had sugar. The monkey asked, "Can I have sugar to add to the fruit?" The bird said, "No, the sugar is mine." The monkey was sad but he had an idea. He took the sugar when the bird was not looking. The bird saw the monkey and was very mad. They fought over the sugar. In the end, the monkey and bird were both hurt. The fruit stayed bitter and no one was happy. The monkey learned that taking things without asking is bad.
