One day, a little person went for a walk. The sky was gray, but the person was happy. They liked to look at the pretty flowers and trees. They walked and walked until they saw a big box. The box was closed, but they could hear a tiny sound inside. The little person was curious about the sound. They bent down and tried to peek inside the box. They couldn't see anything, but the sound was still there. They wanted to know what was making the sound, so they opened the box. When the box was opened, many pretty butterflies flew out. The little person laughed and clapped their hands. The gray sky became sunny and bright. The butterflies made the world more beautiful. The little person learned that sometimes, good things can come from places we don't expect.

Once upon a time there was a little girl called Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing with her toys. One day, Lucy decided to play hide and seek. She put on a red glove and ran around the garden trying to find a good place to hide. "Found you!" shouted her mum. Lucy jumped up, she was so surprised. She felt a bit ashamed because she thought she had done a good job at hiding. "Why did you find me?" she asked her mum. "Because I'm very good at hide and seek," her mum answered. Lucy smiled and took off the red glove. Now that she knew the answer, she thought it would be fun to play hide and seek again. She grabbed the glove and ran off to find a new hiding spot!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Jane. Jane was very thoughtful. One day, she got an invitation to a party. Jane was very happy and excited. She had a lot of questions about the party. Jane asked her mom all her questions. "What do I wear? Can I bring a toy? What games will we play?" Her mom helped her find a pretty dress and a toy to bring. Jane was ready for the party. At the party, Jane had fun playing games and eating cake. But then, something bad happened. Jane lost her toy. She looked everywhere, but she couldn't find it. Jane was very sad. The party ended, and Jane went home without her toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big lion. The lion was not like other lions. He was rare. He could read. He liked to read books. He read books under a big tree every day. One day, a little bird saw the lion reading. The bird was curious. The bird asked the lion, "What are you doing?" The lion said, "I am reading a book. It is fun." The bird wanted to read too. The lion helped the bird learn to read. They read books together. They became good friends. They read books under the big tree every day. And they were very happy.
