There was a girl named Lily. She had a pencil. The pencil was fair and nice. Lily loved to draw with her pencil. One day, Lily drew a cat. She showed it to her mom. Her mom said, "Good job! Can you repeat it?" Lily smiled and drew another cat. It looked like the first cat. Lily drew many cats. She gave them to her friends. Her friends liked the cats. They all said, "Thank you, Lily!" Lily was happy. She loved her fair pencil.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Alice. She had a special tool that her dad had made for her. Every day, Alice would use the tool to scatter leaves in the garden. One day, Alice heard a voice. She looked around and saw a little elf. She was very surprised! Alice asked, "Who are you?" The elf smiled and said, "My name is Tinker. I am here to play with you!" Alice didn't know what to say, so Tinker said, "Let's play a game! I'll hide and you have to find me!" Alice agreed and before she could blink, Tinker had vanished. Alice searched and searched, but she couldn't find Tinker anywhere. Then, she saw a trail of leaves scattered around the garden. She followed the trail and it led her to Tinker, who was hiding behind a tree. Alice was so pleased that she had found him and they played together until it was time for Alice to go home. From then on, Alice and Tinker were the best of friends. Alice used her tool to scatter leaves in the garden and it became a game for the two of them to find each other. Alice and Tinker spent many days together, playing and laughing. They were the best of friends, thanks to Alice's little tool.

Once upon a time, there was a famous model named Mark. He was very good at making people smile. One day, Mark went to a big park to play with his friends. While they were playing, Mark and his friends saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. It barked at them and made them feel scared. They did not know what to do. Mark had an idea. He made a funny face and danced around. The big dog stopped barking and started to laugh. The dog became nice and played with Mark and his friends. They all had fun together and were not scared anymore.

One day, a wise old owl sat on a tree. He looked at the pretty landscape. He saw green grass, blue sky, and a big lake. He sighed, feeling happy. A little bird flew by and saw the wise owl. "Why do you sigh?" the little bird asked. "I sigh because I love this place," said the owl. "It is so pretty and makes me happy." The little bird looked at the landscape too. He saw the green grass, the blue sky, and the big lake. He smiled and sighed, just like the wise owl. They both sat on the tree, happy together, looking at the pretty landscape.

Once upon a time, there was a elderly man who loved to joke. He would always be out every morning in his front yard and joke with the kids as they walked to school. One day, the man thought it would be fun to play a joke on someone who wasn't expecting it. He thought about it for a while, and came up with an idea. He decided to hide behind the mailbox and jump out and scare the first person he saw. The elderly man thought it would be funny, but it didn't end up being so. As the man jumped out from behind, he scared an old lady who lived next door. She stumbled and fell, and got a big scratch on her arm. She was very upset and started to cry. The elderly man felt very bad and apologised to the old lady. But that didn't help. He had gone too far with his joke. The elderly man never played a joke like that again. He learned that jokes aren't always appropriate, and that some jokes can end badly.
