Once upon a time there was a little girl named Emma. She was very excited to go out in the garden and explore. As she peeked around she found a big basket with lots of colorful things. She was so curious and she wanted to know what was inside. Emma went to her mother and asked, "What's in the basket?" Her mother said, "If you show me you're ready, I will tell you!" Emma got very excited. She ran to her bedroom and changed into her play clothes. When she was dressed and ready to go, she ran back to her mom, who said, "The basket has all kinds of surprises for you! Be sure to use your imagination and explore!" Emma went out and with her own two hands, carefully lifted the basket and looked inside. She found all sorts of things - a kite, colorful ribbons, candy and so many new things. The moral of the story is that if you are ready and show your enthusiasm, you will be rewarded with something wonderful.

Once upon a time, there was a hungry pigeon. His name was Pete. Pete lived in a big park with many trees. He had many friends, but his best friend was a little girl named Amy. One sunny day, Pete saw Amy in the park. She was eating a sandwich. Pete flew to Amy and said, "Hi Amy! I am hungry. Can I have some of your sandwich?" Amy smiled and said, "Of course, Pete! You can have some." Amy gave Pete a piece of her sandwich. Pete ate it and felt very happy. They played in the park all day. They laughed and had fun. When it was time to go home, Pete said, "Thank you, Amy! You are a good friend." Amy hugged Pete and said, "You're welcome! See you tomorrow, Pete!" From that day on, Pete and Amy played together every day. They shared food and had a lot of fun. They were the best of friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved to play outside. One day, she walked into her backyard and saw a big hoop on the ground. It was wide and made of plastic. Lucy smiled, clapped her hands and called out "Who wants to play with me?" No one answered, so Lucy decided to play by herself. She picked up the hoop and started running around with it, spinning it around and making it hop. Soon, her brother Jack heard her and ran into the backyard. He asked Lucy "What's that?" Lucy smiled and said "It's a hoop! Wanna play with me?" Jack smiled and said "Of course, let's see who can spin it the longest!" So, Lucy and Jack ran around the backyard with the hoop, giggling and spinning it as fast as they could. Suddenly, Lucy's wide hoop touched Jack's foot and made him lose balance. Lucy started to laugh, and Jack chirped “Hey! I won, you fell!” Lucy and Jack laughed and played with the hoop until it was time to go inside. Lucy was glad to have a new friend to play with.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play and talk. One day, Lily has a new book. She shows it to Ben. "Look, Ben, I have a new book. It has many stories. Do you want to hear one?" Lily asks. "Yes, please. I like stories. What is the book about?" Ben says. "It is about animals. They do funny things. They also learn lessons. I will read you one story. You can guess what the lesson is." Lily says. She opens the book and reads a story. It is about a monkey who likes to tease other animals. He makes fun of their tails, their ears, their colors. He thinks he is the best. But one day, he meets a lion. The lion is not happy with the monkey. He roars and chases him. The monkey is scared. He runs and runs. He tries to hide behind a tree. But the lion finds him. He is about to catch him. "Please, please, don't eat me. I am sorry. I will not tease anyone again. I will be nice and kind. Please, let me go." The monkey begs. The lion stops. He looks at the monkey. He says, "I will not eat you. But you have to learn your lesson. Teasing is not funny. It is mean and hurtful. You should respect other animals. They are different, but they are also good. You should be friends with them, not enemies. Do you understand?" The monkey nods. He says, "Yes, I understand. Thank you for sparing me. I will change my ways. I will be a good monkey." The lion nods. He says, "Good. Now go and apologize to the animals you teased. And don't do it again." The monkey runs away. He is happy to be alive. He is also sorry for his actions. He goes to the animals he teased. He says sorry to them. He asks them to be his friends. They forgive him. They are happy to have a new friend. Lily closes the book. She says, "That was the story. Did you like it?" Ben says, "Yes, I liked it. It was funny and exciting. But it also had a lesson. I think I know what it is. Can I guess?" Lily says, "Sure, you can guess. What is the lesson?" Ben says, "The lesson is, don't tease others. Be nice and kind. Respect differences. Make friends, not enemies." Lily says, "That's right. You guessed it. That is a good lesson. We should follow it. We should be good friends to each other and to everyone." Ben says, "Yes, we should. Thank you for reading me the story. It was a good story. Can you read me another one?" Lily says, "Yes, I can. I have many stories. Let's read another one. Maybe you can guess the lesson again." They smile and hug. They are happy. They love stories. They love each other.
