Once upon a time, in a land far away, there was a magic tree. This tree was incredible because it could change with the season. It could appear to be a different tree each time. One day, a little boy named Tim went to the magic tree. In the summer, the tree had big green leaves. Tim played under the tree and felt happy. In the fall, the tree's leaves turned orange and yellow. Tim loved to jump in the big pile of leaves. During winter, the tree was covered in white snow. Tim would make snowballs and have fun with his friends. In the spring, the tree had pretty pink flowers. Tim liked to smell the flowers and feel the soft petals. The magic tree taught Tim that each season is special and brings new things to enjoy. The moral of the story is to appreciate and enjoy the changes in life, just like the seasons.

Mia and Leo were playing with their toy planes in the backyard. They liked to make them soar in the air and pretend they were pilots. Mia had a red plane and Leo had a blue plane. They were both very proud of their planes. One day, a big wind came and blew their planes away. Mia and Leo ran after them, but they could not catch them. They saw their planes fly over the fence and into the neighbor's yard. They were very sad and scared. They did not know if they could get their planes back. They knocked on the neighbor's door and asked if they could have their planes. The neighbor was a nice lady who liked children. She said yes, but she asked them to do something for her first. She said she had a big quilt that she was making for her granddaughter, but she needed some help. She asked Mia and Leo to touch the different fabrics and tell her what texture they felt. She said texture meant how something felt, like soft or rough or smooth. Mia and Leo agreed to help. They touched the quilt and said the words they knew. They said some fabrics were soft like a bunny, some were rough like a tree, some were smooth like a glass, and some were fuzzy like a peach. The neighbor was very happy and thanked them. She said they had good eyes and good hands. She gave them their planes and some cookies. Mia and Leo were very grateful and happy. They thanked the neighbor and hugged her. They ran back to their backyard and played with their planes again. They made them soar in the air and pretend they were pilots. They said they had a new friend and a new word. They said they liked texture.

Once upon a time, in a big, big forest, there was a smart little bird. This bird loved the color yellow. One day, the bird saw a yellow flower and flew down to look at it. As the bird was looking at the flower, a big, big bear came up to the bird. The bear said, "I want to take this yellow flower home." The bird did not want the bear to take the flower, so it tried to resist. "No, no, Mr. Bear, please don't take my yellow flower!" said the bird. But then, something unexpected happened. The yellow flower began to talk! "Please don't fight," said the flower. "I can make more yellow flowers for both of you to enjoy." The bear and the bird were very surprised. They agreed to let the flower stay, and soon the forest was full of beautiful yellow flowers for everyone to enjoy.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man named Tom. He had a job to deliver books to people. He liked his job because it made people happy. One day, Tom had to deliver a book to a little girl named Lily. Lily loved books and was very excited. Tom knocked on Lily's door. Lily opened it and said, "Hi! Are you here to deliver my book?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, I am. Here is your warm book." Lily took the book and hugged it. The book was warm because it was a sunny day. Lily said, "Thank you, Tom!" Tom said, "You're welcome. Have fun reading!" Lily went inside her house and started to read her new book. She felt happy and warm inside. Tom walked away with a big smile, glad that he could make Lily happy.
