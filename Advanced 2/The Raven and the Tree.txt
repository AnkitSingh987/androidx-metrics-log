Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little raven. He was ready to learn how to fly. He looked at his mom and dad, and they told him it was time to try. The little raven went to the edge of the tree. He was scared, but he knew he had to try. He spread his wings and jumped. As he flew, he tried to balance in the air. He wobbled a little but kept going. As he flew, he saw his mom and dad flying with him. They were so proud of him. The little raven felt happy and knew he could fly high in the sky. And from that day on, he was never scared to fly again.

Once there was a little girl called Molly. One day Molly was walking in the park when she saw a feather on the ground. She bent down to pick it up and had a big smile on her face. Molly loved feathers! Suddenly, Molly noticed a big heavy dog walking towards her. It was big and scary! Molly was scared, so she kicked the feather away. The feather flew away and the dog chased it. Molly was so happy to see the dog running away. The feather flew farther and farther away, and the heavy dog just couldn't catch up. Molly was very happy and clapped her hands. She laughed as the dog ran away and the feather flew away in the wind. Molly was glad that she had kicked the feather away.

Once upon a time, in a little house, there lived a kind girl named Lucy. She loved to make soup for her friends. One day, Lucy made a big pot of soup. The soup was hot and yummy. Lucy was excited to share it with her friends. In the woods near Lucy's house, there were wild animals. A little rabbit was hopping around. The rabbit smelled the soup and wanted to taste it. But the rabbit was scared to go near the house. So, Lucy had an idea. She would save some soup for the rabbit. Lucy put some soup in a small bowl and took it outside. She saw the rabbit and said, "Here, little rabbit! This soup is for you." The rabbit came closer and tasted the soup. It was very happy. The rabbit liked the soup so much that it told all the wild animals in the woods about Lucy's soup. The next day, Lucy found many wild animals waiting outside her house. They all wanted to taste her soup. So, Lucy made more soup and shared it with all the wild animals. They were all happy and thanked Lucy for her kindness. From that day on, Lucy and the wild animals became good friends. They all enjoyed her soup and played together in the woods. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a strong prune tree. It was surrounded by other weak trees in the forest. The prune tree always said, "I have the strongest branches! I can handle anything." One day, a big storm came and all the trees were shaking. Suddenly, the weak trees started to break. But, the prune tree was not afraid. The prune tree said to the other trees, "Let's decide what to do." All the trees discussed, including the prune, and they decided that they should all try to bend and weather the storm together. At the end of the storm, the prune said to the other trees, "See! I was right! Together, we are strong!"

Once upon a time, there was a little puppy named Max. Max was very good at playing, but he was a bit difficult when it was time for a nap. One day, Max's mom said, "Max, it's time for your nap now." Max did not want to nap, so he ran away to play. He found a big tree and started to dig a hole. As he dug, he found something shiny. It was a key! Max picked up the key in his mouth and ran back to his mom. "Look, Mom, I found a key!" Max barked. His mom was surprised and smiled. "Good job, Max! Now, let's take a nap and we can find out what the key opens later." Max agreed and they both took a nap together. The difficult puppy learned that napping could be fun, too.
