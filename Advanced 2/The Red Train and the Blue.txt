Once upon a time, there was a big red train. It liked to go fast on the rails. One day, a small blue train came. The big red train felt jealous. It wanted to be the only train on the rails. The big red train thought of a plan. It would settle in the middle of the rails, so the small blue train could not pass. The small blue train saw the big red train blocking the way. It was sad and asked the big red train to move. The big red train said no. The small blue train tried to push the big red train, but it was too heavy. The small blue train could not go anywhere. It was stuck. The big red train was happy, but the small blue train was very sad.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. The tree had a lot of branches. One day, a little bird came to sit on a branch. The bird was very serious. The bird wanted to mix things up. So, it started to sing a silly song. All the other birds stopped and listened. They were very surprised. The serious bird was not serious anymore. It was now the funny bird. All the other birds laughed and joined in the song. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a gifted dog named Buddy. Buddy could do many tricks. He could jump high, roll over, and even dance. Buddy always tried to behave well. One day, Buddy went for a walk with his friend, a little girl named Emma. They walked into a mist. The mist was thick, and they could not see. Buddy was scared, but he stayed close to Emma. Suddenly, they heard a loud noise. Buddy and Emma were afraid. But, it was not a monster. It was a big, friendly elephant! The elephant was lost too. They all became friends and helped each other find their way out of the mist. In the end, they were happy and safe.

Once there was a little girl named Clara. She was three and lived with her parents in a lovely house. Clara had a lot of furniture in her house and it was very colorful. Clara liked to play around the furniture and pretend it was a castle. One day, Clara's parents took her to the park. Clara was so excited. The park was an icy field. Clara and her parents walked around and Clara said, "It's so cold and icy!" Clara's mom smiled and told her, "It's alright. We'll go inside soon. But first, we can still explore the icy field!" So Clara explored the icy field and ran around it. Suddenly she slipped and fell down. Clara started to cry but her parents came quickly and hugged her. Her dad said, "It's alright, Clara. You'll be fine soon. We'll go home now." Clara was sad, but she was happy to know she'd soon be in the warm house with all the furniture. So Clara and her parents went home, and sure enough she was soon playing around her furniture again, just like before.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He was a fearful boy who was scared of many things. One day, Tim went to a big park with his mom. They saw many kids playing and having fun. Tim wanted to play too, but he was scared. Then, Tim's mom said, "I will introduce you to a new friend." Tim's mom introduced him to a girl named Lily. Lily was a kind girl who had a special spirit. She was not scared of anything. Lily said, "Hi Tim, let's play together and have fun!" At first, Tim was scared, but as they played, he became less fearful. They played on the swings, the slide, and in the sandbox. Suddenly, a big dog came running towards them. Tim was scared, but Lily was not. She said, "Don't be scared, Tim. The dog is nice." The dog stopped and wagged its tail. It was a friendly dog, and Tim was not scared anymore. Tim learned that with a brave spirit like Lily, he did not have to be fearful.
