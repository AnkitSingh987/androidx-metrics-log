Once upon a time, there was a wise pigeon. This pigeon had a lot of friends. They played in the park every day. One day, the pigeon saw a twisty thing on the ground. It was long and thin. He did not know what it was. The pigeon picked up the twisty thing with his beak. He showed it to his friends. They all looked at it and tried to guess what it was. One friend thought it was a worm. Another friend thought it was a stick. They were all wrong. Then, something unexpected happened. The twisty thing started to move! It was not a worm or a stick. It was a baby snake! The wise pigeon and his friends were surprised. They let the baby snake go back to its home. And from that day on, they all learned to be more careful with twisty things.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy who loved to explore. One day, he decided to explore a big drain. The drain was dark and scary, but the brave boy was determined. He asked his mom if he could explore the drain, and she allowed him to go. He was so excited. He grabbed his flashlight and completed all of his safety checks. He was ready to explore! He walked through the dark drain, carrying his flashlight in front of him. He saw all sorts of exciting things in the drain. He saw frogs, snakes and even a big fish! He also found bits of old coins. He was so brave as he encountered each new surprise. Finally, he made it to the end of the drain. He was relieved to be back in the sunshine. His mom had been worried, but she was proud of him when she saw him. He had been a brave explorer, and she was so proud.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She loved to mix things in her kitchen. One day, she wanted to make a big bubble. So, she got a bowl and put water and soap in it. She was very patient as she mixed it all together. Sue took a straw and blew into the mix. She saw many little bubbles come out. She was happy, but she wanted a big bubble. Sue was patient and tried again. This time, she blew very slow and gentle. A big bubble started to grow! The big bubble floated in the air, and Sue watched it with a big smile. She was proud of her big bubble. It was the biggest bubble she had ever seen. Sue knew that being patient and mixing well had helped her make the big bubble.

Once upon a time, in a gloomy forest, there was a pirate. The pirate had a big hat and a long sword. He liked to walk around the forest to look for treasure. One day, the pirate found a big flower that was about to bloom. He wanted to see the flower bloom, but it would not open. The pirate felt sad because the flower stayed closed. The pirate tried to make the flower bloom by talking to it, giving it water, and singing to it. After a while, the flower started to open. The pirate was happy, and the flower was not gloomy anymore. They became good friends, and the pirate found the treasure of friendship.

One day, a shy bird named Tim was sitting on a branch. He saw a big fruit hanging from a tree. Tim wanted to eat the fruit, but he was too scared to get it. His friend, Sam the squirrel, saw him and said, "Tim, why don't you get the fruit?" Tim was afraid, but he wanted the fruit so bad. He took a deep breath and flew to the tree. He tried to get the fruit, but it was too big for him. He pushed and pulled, but it would not come off. Sam saw him and said, "Let's split the fruit in half!" Together, they pushed and pulled, and the fruit split in half! They were so happy and started to eat. But then, something unexpected happened. The fruit was magic! When they ate it, they could talk like people! Tim and Sam were so surprised and happy. They knew they would have many fun adventures with their new magic. From that day on, Tim the bird and Sam the squirrel were the best of friends. They used their magic to help other animals and have fun. And Tim was never shy again.
