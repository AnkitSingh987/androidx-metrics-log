Once upon a time, there was a mommy and her little three year old girl. They were to go to the movies one special day together. The little girl smiled as she hopped with joy. She said to her mommy, "Can I order a movie?" Her mommy smiled and said, "Sure, Sweetie. But what kind of a movie do you want to order?" The little girl thought for a moment. Then she said, "I want to order a troubled movie!" Her mommy gave her a puzzled look. "What do you mean by a troubled movie?" she asked the little girl. The little girl pointed at the screen. "I want to order that movie! With the man and the lady who are very troubled!" Her mommy understood and said, "Alright, let's order that movie!" The mommy and the little girl watched the movie, and as the movie finished, they both looked deeply moved. As they were walking out of the theater, the little girl looked up to her mommy and said, "I'm so glad we ordered that troubled movie together!"

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved to explore. One day she was looking around and found an old structure. She had never seen anything like it before. She studied it carefully and wondered, "What could this be?" She couldn't figure it out. She shrugged and decided to explore it further. The little girl opened the door of the structure and found a bright, warm room inside. She looked around in amazement and couldn't believe what she was seeing. Then, she realized this was a special place meant for her. She was so grateful and couldn't thank enough whoever had made this for her. She explored the structure and found books, toys, and other exciting things. She stayed there for a long time, discovering more new things and having lots of fun. Finally, when it was time for her to go, she was sad to leave, but she knew she had a lot of happy memories in the special structure.

Once upon a time, there was a kind aunt. She loved to teach her niece and nephew. They liked to learn from her. She was very useful to them. One day, she taught them how to plant seeds. They put seeds in the ground. They gave the seeds water. They watched the seeds grow into big plants. The niece and nephew were happy. They thanked their aunt for teaching them. They knew how to grow plants now. They were useful too. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a brilliant chain in the park. It was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted to show his friend, Sam, the chain. Tim went to Sam's house and said, "Look, Sam! I found a shiny chain. Let's play with it and act like pirates!" Sam was excited and said, "Yes, let's act like pirates and find treasure!" They put on pirate hats and took turns wearing the chain. They pretended to sail a big ship and search for more treasure. They had lots of fun acting like pirates with the brilliant chain. At the end of the day, Tim and Sam put the chain back in the park for other kids to find and play with. They knew they could always act like pirates and have fun together, even without the chain.
