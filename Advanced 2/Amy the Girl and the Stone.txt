Title: Amy the Girl and the Stone

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy loved to gather pretty stones and put them in her fancy bag. One sunny day, she went to the park to look for more stones. While Amy was looking for stones, she felt a pain in her foot. She saw a small rock in her shoe and took it out. Then, she found a very shiny stone and put it in her bag. She was so happy! But when Amy got home, something unexpected happened. The shiny stone was not a stone at all! It was a magic key that opened a secret door in her room. Amy could not believe it! She used the key and found a new world full of fun and games.

Once upon a time, a little red boat liked to travel. It went all around the sea. One day, it saw a crazy big fish. The fish was so big that it scared the little red boat. The little red boat wanted to go back to the shore. But, the crazy big fish followed it. The little red boat went faster and faster, but the big fish did not stop. The little red boat was very scared. At last, the little red boat got to the shore. But, it was going too fast. It hit the shore very hard. The little red boat broke into pieces. The crazy big fish went away. The little red boat could not travel anymore. The moral of the story is that we should always be careful and not go too fast when we are scared.

Once there was a boy named Tom. Tom liked to scatter seeds in the yard. He hoped that the seeds would grow into big plants. One day, while scattering seeds, Tom saw a distant hill. He thought it would be fun to go there. Tom walked to the hill and found a big tree. Under the tree, he saw a box. He opened the box and found a reward inside. The reward was a shiny toy car. Tom was very happy. Tom ran back home and showed the toy car to his mom. "Look, Mom! I found a reward!" he said. "That's great, Tom!" his mom said. "Now you have a new toy to play with." Tom played with the shiny car all day and was very happy.

Once upon a time there was a tired little bear cub who needed to sleep. He looked around for a good place to rest, but he couldn't find one. Everywhere was so dangerous. Finally, he remembered his cave back home. He thought it would be safer there. So he went back and soon he was inside and safe. The cave was cozy and quiet. Soon he was snuggled up and sleeping like a baby. He fell into a deep sleep and dreamed of running through the meadow and playing with his friends. When he woke up, he was rested and happy. He smiled and yawned, and decided to go back out and explore. But first, the bear cub knew he needed to find a safe place to sleep.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to visit her dad at the university. She was very excited to see the big school where her dad worked. When they got there, her dad said, "Hold my hand, Lucy. I don't want you to get lost." She held his hand tightly as they walked. As they walked, Lucy saw a miserable cat sitting alone near a tree. She wanted to help the cat feel better. "Dad, can I help the cat?" she asked. Her dad said, "Sure, but be careful." Lucy went to the cat and gave it a gentle pet. The cat started to purr and looked happy. Suddenly, the cat changed into a magical fairy! The fairy said, "Thank you, Lucy, for being so kind. I was turned into a cat by a mean witch. Because you helped me, I will grant you one wish." Lucy thought for a moment and said, "I wish for everyone at the university to be happy." The fairy waved her wand, and everyone at the school started to smile and laugh. Lucy and her dad were amazed and they knew it was a day they would never forget.
