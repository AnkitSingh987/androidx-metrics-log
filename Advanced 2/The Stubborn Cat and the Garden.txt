Once upon a time there was a very stubborn cat. His name was Pickles and he was always refusing to do what anyone told him. Pickles didn't like strangers and he never wanted anyone new to come near him. One day, an unfamiliar cat appeared in the garden. He was a big, tabby cat and very friendly. Pickles was scared and started to hiss at the stranger. "Why are you so mean to me?" asked the tabby cat. Pickles didn't answer, but just carried on hissing. "I only want to include you in our game," said the tabby cat kindly. Pickles thought for a while and finally agreed to join in the game. From then on, Pickles was no longer so stubborn. He was happy to include the stranger in all their activities. The end.

One day, a polite fish named Bob lived in a reef. He liked to balance on his tail. He would show his friends how he could balance for a long time. Bob saw a new fish named Tim. Tim was shy and did not talk to anyone. Bob wanted to make friends with Tim. So, he swam to Tim and said, "Hi, I am Bob. Do you want to play with me?" Tim looked at Bob and said, "Yes, I want to play. But I don't know how to balance like you." Bob smiled and said, "I can teach you how to balance. It's easy and fun!" Bob and Tim played together and learned to balance. They became good friends and had a lot of fun in the reef. All the other fish liked them because they were polite and kind.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sarah who loved tuna. Every week her mom would deliver her a big can of tuna. Sarah was very thankful and couldn't wait to eat it. This week, Sarah was extra excited; when her mom delivered her the tuna, it was in a big box with a big ribbon! Sarah asked her mom, "What is in the box?" Her mom smiled and said, "It's a special gift for you. Just open it and see." Sarah carefully opened up the box. She was so happy when she saw the can of tuna! Sarah smiled and said, "Thank you mom, it's so thoughtful of you." Her mom said, "You are always welcome my child. Have a lovely day."

Once upon a time, there was a small cat named Tiny. Tiny had a job to deliver things to people in his town. He loved his job very much. One day, Tiny had to deliver a bathrobe to Mr. Fox. The bathrobe was big, soft, and blue. Tiny put the bathrobe in his bag and went on his way. Tiny walked and walked until he got to Mr. Fox's house. He knocked on the door and Mr. Fox opened it. Tiny gave the bathrobe to Mr. Fox. Mr. Fox was very happy and thanked Tiny. Tiny felt good about helping Mr. Fox and went back home to rest.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy who had an important job. He drove a vehicle around town, and he was very careful to always follow the rules. But one day, something unusual happened. Just as the boy was about to turn left, a pesky bird flew in front of his vehicle. He had to stop so suddenly, he started to frown. The little boy was very brave and he didn't want to give up. He tried one more time to turn left, but the pesky bird came flying back again. The little boy wasn't ready and again he had to stop. He frowned even harder this time. The third time, the little boy was prepared. He looked all around before he decided to turn left and the pesky bird didn't come back. The boy smiled with relief, happy that he had finished his important job.

Once upon a time, there was a large tree in the park. A little bird named Tim lived in the tree. Tim loved to balance on the branches and sing happy songs. One day, a squirrel named Sam came to visit Tim. Sam saw Tim balancing on a branch and said, "Wow, Tim! You are really good at balancing! Can you teach me how to do that?" Tim replied, "Of course, Sam! Just find a branch and try to keep your body straight. Make sure you measure the branch before you start, so you know it is not too big or too small for you." Sam found a branch that was just right for him. He tried to balance like Tim, but it was hard at first. Tim helped Sam, and soon they were both balancing on the branches and having fun together. They played in the large tree all day and became the best of friends.
