Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was three years old and loved playing in the garden. One day, she discovered a hidden mailbox in the garden. She dared to open it and found something very special inside - a small shiny object. She didn't know what it was but knew it was valuable. Lucy held the shiny object in her hand and showed it to her mom. Her mom was very excited and told Lucy the shiny object was a lucky coin. She said it was very valuable. Lucy quickly put the lucky coin back in the hidden mailbox and shut the door. She thought about the surprise she'd found and knew that she would always value it. The end.

Once upon a time, there lived a bear and a rabbit. The bear was very big and scary. The rabbit was small and scared. One day, the bear was walking near a river and saw a nice rug that someone had left behind. He immediately decided to give it to the rabbit. The rabbit was very thankful to the bear and said, "Thank you for the nice rug. I was so scared to go near the river, I was afraid something might happen." The bear smiled and said, "It is important to share with others and not to be scared. Kindness is always rewarded". The rabbit nodded in happy agreement, understanding the moral of this story. From then on, the bear and the rabbit were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She went to class with her friends. They liked to play with toys and laugh together. Sue had a toy car she liked to push around the room. Her friends loved it too. One day, Sue's friend Tim said, "Can I push the car?" Sue said, "Yes, but be careful. It's very dry today." Tim pushed the car, but it went too fast and hit a wall. The car broke into pieces. Sue and Tim were sad. Just then, their teacher came and said, "Don't worry, I can fix it!" She put the pieces back together, and the car was even better than before. It could now jump and spin. Sue and Tim were so happy, they played with the car all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very adventurous. He liked to play outside with his friends every week. They would run, jump, and laugh together. One day, Tim found a box of matches. He did not know what they were, but they looked fun. Tim showed the matches to his friends. They all wanted to play with the matches too. Tim's mom saw them with the matches. She told them that matches were not for playing. Matches could be dangerous. Tim and his friends listened to her. They put the matches away and played a safe game instead.

Once upon a time, there was a big blackboard in a fun room. The blackboard was very creative. It liked to help kids draw and write. One day, a small girl named Lily came to the room. She was happy to see the blackboard. She wanted to draw a big sun with a yellow chalk. The blackboard knew that the room had a big fan. The fan would blow away the chalk dust. The blackboard wanted to protect Lily's drawing. So, it thought of a smart plan. It told Lily to draw the sun in a special spot. Lily did not know why, but she listened to the blackboard. When Lily finished her sun, the fan turned on. The chalk dust began to fly away. But, the blackboard's plan worked! The special spot protected the sun. Lily was very happy. She thanked the creative blackboard for keeping her sun safe. From that day on, Lily and the blackboard became best friends.

One day, a big dog and a small cat had a race. The big dog thought he would win because he was strong. The small cat was weak, but she was also fast. The big dog said, "I will win the race because I am strong." The small cat said, "I may be weak, but I am fast. Let's see who wins." They started to run. The big dog ran fast, but the small cat ran faster. They went up and down hills and through the grass. The small cat won the race. The big dog was sad, but he learned to value the small cat's speed. The big dog and the small cat became friends. They learned that being strong or weak did not matter. What mattered was being kind and having fun together.
