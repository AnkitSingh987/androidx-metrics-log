One day, Tim went to school. His teacher had a big blackboard in the room. Tim thought school was boring. He wanted to play with his friends outside. During break time, Tim and his friends ran outside to play. They played a fun game of tag. Tim was running fast and laughing with his friends. Then, Tim did not see a rock on the ground. He stumbled on the rock and fell down. His friends helped him get up. They all went back inside and Tim was not bored anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a polite cat named Kitty. Kitty lived in a small house with her friend, a tiny mouse named Inch. They were best friends and always played together. One day, Kitty and Inch were playing outside. They saw a big dog. The big dog looked at them and said, "Do you know where my ball is?" Kitty and Inch did not know where the ball was. They shook their heads and said, "Sorry, we don't know." The big dog was not happy. He wanted his ball back. He got mad and started to chase Kitty and Inch. They ran and ran, but the big dog was too fast. In the end, the big dog caught them, and they were very sad.

Once upon a time, there were two friends, Sam and Mike. Sam was an amazing Runner. He could run faster than anyone else. Mike was a bit slower, but still a good runner. One day, they challenged each other to a race. They both lined up at the start line, ready and eager to win. They took off running and Mike was in the lead. But as they got closer to the finish line, Sam was starting to catch up. Just as he was about to overtake Mike, Sam stepped on something hard with his toe and stumbled. He fell on the ground and Mike crossed the finish line first. Sam was really sad, but Mike was really happy. He was so excited to have won the race. He shouted, "I won the race. This is so amazing!" Sam rolled over and said, "Well, that was an amazing way for me to lose." And so, Sam lost the race in a pretty unfortunate way.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a squirrel. The squirrel was not happy. He had a terrible day. He lost his nuts and could not find them. He looked and looked, but the nuts were gone. The squirrel was very tired. He wanted to rest. He went to his home in the tree. The home was soft and warm. The squirrel lay down and closed his eyes. He began to rest. While the squirrel was resting, a bird came by. The bird saw the nuts on the ground. The bird picked up the nuts and took them to the squirrel's home. When the squirrel woke up, he saw the nuts. He was so happy! His terrible day was now a good day. The squirrel and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a great big book. This book was called a dictionary. The dictionary was full of many words. It lived on a big shelf with other books. One day, a magic cat came to the room. The cat had a special power. It could make things shrink. The cat saw the great big dictionary and thought it was too big. So, the cat used its power to make the dictionary shrink. Now, the dictionary was small. It was happy because it was easier to hold and read. The other books on the shelf liked the small dictionary too. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to listen to music. One day, she found an open box in her room. Inside the box, there was a shiny music toy. Lily was very happy and wanted to play with it. Every day, Lily played with her music toy. She would wind it up and it would play a pretty song. But one day, when she wound up the toy, the music started to change. It played a new song that Lily had never heard before. She was surprised but liked the new song too. Lily told her mom about the music toy and the new song. Her mom smiled and said, "I have a secret to tell you. I put a magic music toy in the open box. It can change songs when you really love the music." Lily was so excited and couldn't wait to see what other songs her magic music toy would play.
