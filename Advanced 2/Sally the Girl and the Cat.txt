Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. Sally loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, Sally's mom gave her a package. Inside the package, there was a big spoon. Sally was so happy! Sally's mom told her to stir the soup with the big spoon. Sally stirred the soup carefully. She did not want to make a mess. While stirring, a rude cat jumped on the table. The cat wanted to eat the soup! Sally shooed the rude cat away. She did not want the cat to eat their soup. Sally's mom was proud of her. They finished making the soup together. Sally learned that she could be a big help in the kitchen.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing outside. He saw a small bird with a hurt wing. The bird had a bandage on it. Tim felt sad for the bird. Tim went to his mom and said, "Mom, the bird has a hurt wing. Can we help it?" His mom said, "Yes, Tim. We can try to help the bird. Let's go see it." They went to the bird and Tim's mom looked at the bandage. She said, "Oh, this bandage is not put on right. We must fix it." Tim's mom took off the old bandage and put on a new one. She was very careful. Tim asked his mom, "Mom, can we number the days until the bird is better?" His mom said, "That's a good idea, Tim. We can number the days and see how long it takes for the bird to fly again." After a few days, the bird started to fly again. Tim felt very happy. He learned that it was foolish to think the bird would not get better. Now, Tim and the bird were good friends.

Once there was a brilliant bird named Burt. He loved to pick up shiny things and take them far away. One day Burt found a beautiful opera playing in a park. He was so mesmerized by the music that he decided to pick it up and take it. When Burt flew away with the opera, he didn't realize the people in the park were very sad. Without their music the park felt empty. Burt flew to the top of a mountain and started to play the opera. He found it even more beautiful up there. However, he noticed some animals were very sad. It turned out, the animals missed their music. Burt realized that even though he was able to pick up the opera, it was not his to keep. He flew back to the park and placed the opera back where he found it. Everyone was so happy, and the music filled the park with joy. Burt learned a big lesson that day. He knew that even though it was brilliant to pick up things, it was not always the right thing to do.

Once upon a time, in a big castle, there was a king. He was a mysterious king. He had a big garden with many colorful flowers. One sunny day, the king was in his garden. He saw a little boy. The boy was sad. The king asked, "Why are you sad, little boy?" The boy said, "I want to spray water on the flowers, but I don't have a water can." The king wanted to help the boy. He said, "I have a water can. Let's spray water on the flowers together." The king and the boy started to spray water on the flowers. Then, something unexpected happened. When they sprayed water on one flower, it turned into a big, beautiful butterfly. The butterfly said, "Thank you, king and boy. I was a butterfly stuck in a flower, but your water helped me." The king and the boy were very happy. They became good friends and took care of the garden together. The garden was full of more butterflies and everyone lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a hot land, there was a brave girl named Lily. She liked to play outside in the heat. But sometimes, it was too hot for her. One day, Lily found a big spray bottle. She thought it would be fun to fill it with water. She took the spray bottle outside and played in the heat. As Lily played, she sprayed water on her face to cool down. She felt brave and happy. She also sprayed water on her friends, and they all laughed and played together in the heat. They had a great time, thanks to the brave girl and her spray bottle.
