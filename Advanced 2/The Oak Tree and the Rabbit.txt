Once upon a time there was an oak tree in the middle of a forest. The tree was tall and strong. Everyday, a rabbit would come and sit under the oak tree. He enjoyed the peacefulness of the forest. One day, a squirrel came along and asked the rabbit, "Can you help me? I'm looking for an acorn to match this one I have. It was a patient mission, but I must find it." The rabbit looked around and soon found a perfect match. He handed it to the squirrel. The squirrel was so relieved and thanked the rabbit. "You have been so patient. Thank you so much!" The rabbit just smiled in return. He was happy to help his friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim went to the park with his mom. He wore his favorite jacket. It was a sunny day, and Tim was happy. In the park, Tim saw a big tree. He wanted to pick apples from the tree. Tim's mom said, "Be careful, Tim." Tim was frightened, but he wanted to try. He picked one apple, and then another. He was so proud! But then, something unexpected happened. A little squirrel came down from the tree. It picked up Tim's jacket and ran away! Tim and his mom chased the squirrel. They found the jacket on a bench. The squirrel just wanted a warm place to sleep. Tim and his mom laughed, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a smart rabbit. This rabbit had many animal friends. They liked to play together every day. The rabbit was very fast and could jump high. One day, the smart rabbit and his animal friends found a big fruit. They all wanted to eat it. But, they didn't know how to share it. The rabbit had an idea. "Let's split the fruit!" said the rabbit. All the animal friends agreed. The smart rabbit split the fruit into equal parts for all his animal friends. They all enjoyed the tasty fruit together. After they ate, they played and laughed all day. The rabbit and his animal friends were very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a helpful dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with a basketball. He would run and jump and have fun. He also knew how to whistle. When Spot whistled, all his friends would come to play. One day, Spot found a big hole in the ground. He thought it would be fun to hide his basketball in the hole. Spot whistled for his friends to come and play. They all looked at the hole and wondered what was inside. Spot jumped into the hole to get his basketball. But the hole was too deep, and Spot could not get out. His friends tried to help, but they could not reach him. Spot was stuck in the hole, and his friends were sad. The helpful dog and his basketball were lost forever.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who liked to explore new places. One day she asked her mom if she could go to the woods to find a tiger. Her mom said no because it was too dangerous, but the girl was persistent. Finally, her mom gave in and agreed to take her to the woods. When they got there, the little girl heard a loud roar and excitement filled her heart. She saw a big tiger and quickly asked it to come home with her. The tiger growled and stepped closer. The little girl was frightened but she was still very persistent. She kept asking the tiger to come home with her but the tiger just growled louder. Sadly, the little girl's mom had to pull her away from the tiger and they ran back home.

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. This jug was very fancy. It lived in a small house with a girl named Sue and her mom. One day, Sue wanted to pour water from the jug. She asked her mom, "Mom, can I pour water from the fancy jug?" Her mom said, "Yes, Sue, but be careful." Sue took the jug and poured water into a cup. She was happy and proud. She showed her mom the cup of water. Her mom smiled and said, "Good job, Sue! You did it!" They both drank the water and had a fun day.
