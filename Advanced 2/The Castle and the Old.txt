Once upon a time there was a castle. It was very fine with lots of windows and walls. Every day, a kindly old man would come to the castle to measure it. He wanted to make sure it was the finest he had ever seen. One day, when he came to measure the castle, it was no longer there. All that was left was a pile of rocks. The old man was very sad. He had taken such great care with the castle and now it was gone. He looked everywhere for the castle but could find nothing. After a long day of measuring, the old man returned home. Sadly, he realized he would never see the castle again.

Once upon a time, there was a nice man named Tom. Tom was a mechanic. He fixed cars all day and loved his job. One day, a lady came to Tom with a broken car. Tom looked at the car and knew what to do. He fixed the car very fast. The lady was so happy! The lady wanted to thank Tom. She gave him money to pay for his help. Tom was happy too. He used the money to buy ice cream for his family. Everyone loved the nice mechanic Tom.

One day, a girl named Lucy had a doll. She loved her doll very much. The doll was patient and always sat with Lucy. They played together every day. One day, Lucy's mom said, "Lucy, we need to send your doll to your cousin, Lily. She needs a friend to play with." Lucy was sad, but she knew her cousin needed the doll. So, they put the doll in a box and sent it away. A few days later, Lucy got a box from her cousin. She opened it and saw a new doll inside. The new doll was also patient and looked like her old doll. Lily sent a note that said, "Thank you for the doll, Lucy. Now we both have a friend to play with." Lucy smiled and played with her new doll.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a big microscope in her room. She didn't know what it was, so she started to wonder. She looked through it and saw tiny things that she never saw before. Mia was very happy and excited. Mia went to her friend Tom, who was a fat cat. She said, "Tom, look at this big thing I found! It's called a microscope." Tom looked at it and said, "Wow, Mia! That looks fun!" They both took turns looking through the microscope and saw many tiny things. Mia and Tom played with the microscope all day. They looked at leaves, bugs, and even Tom's fur. They were amazed by the tiny world they found. At the end of the day, they were both very tired but happy. They knew they would have many more fun days with the big microscope.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old owl who loved to paint. Every day, the owl would paint beautiful pictures of the sky, trees, and flowers. One day, the owl decided to paint a picture of a big steam train. As the wise owl painted the steam train, something unexpected happened. The steam train jumped out of the painting and started to move! The train went "Choo Choo" and puffed out steam. The owl was very surprised and didn't know what to do. Then, the wise owl had an idea. He quickly painted a track for the steam train to follow. The train happily followed the track, and the owl painted more and more tracks. In the end, the owl and the steam train became good friends, and they had lots of fun painting and playing together.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her delicate toy car. One day, she took her car to the big stadium where people played games. At the stadium, she met a boy named Tom. Tom wanted to play with Lily's car too. They decided to have a race to see who was faster. Lily was very excited to gain a new friend. They raced their toy cars around the stadium. Lily's car was fast, but Tom's car was faster. In the end, Tom won the race. Lily was happy for her new friend and they played together all day at the stadium.
