"Look, Mummy!" said Sarah excitedly. She had found a book in the living room. Mummy smiled and said, "That's a lovely book! I'm so glad you found it." Sarah held the book up. It was big and thick. "It's so flexible! Can I set it on the table?" Mummy nodded. "Yes, go ahead and set it on the table. I'm sure you'll have lots of fun with it!" Sarah carefully placed the book on the table. When she saw how it fit so nicely, she smiled. "Can I open it?" she asked. "Of course!" said Mummy. "Go ahead and explore!" Sarah opened the book and it was filled with colourful pictures and fun words. She squealed with delight and started to read. Mummy smiled to herself. It was nice to see Sarah having so much fun with a book. She was so flexible and always eager to learn new things.

One day, a small boy named Tim went for a walk. He saw many pretty flowers. Tim wanted to pick a flower for his mom. He saw a big red flower and picked it. As he walked, he saw a little bird with a broken wing. The bird said, "Help me, please." Tim put the bird in his hand and walked back to his house. When they got home, Tim's mom saw the bird and said, "Oh no, we must help the bird!" Tim put the bird in a box and gave it some food. The bird ate the food with its mouth. Tim and his mom took care of the bird until its wing was fixed. One day, the bird started to talk like a person! It said, "Thank you for helping me, Tim." Tim was so surprised! The bird was a magic bird. It gave Tim a wish for being so kind. Tim wished for more flowers to pick for his mom. Now, their garden was full of pretty flowers.

Once upon a time, there was a big brown dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to play and run around. One day, Buddy went with his family to the zoo. The zoo had many animals, like lions, monkeys, and birds. At the zoo, Buddy saw a little boy holding a balloon. The balloon was red and shiny. Buddy wanted to play with the balloon, so he asked the boy, "Can I play with your balloon, please?" The boy smiled and said, "Yes, you can play with it, but be careful." Buddy was so happy to play with the balloon. But while he was playing, he accidentally let the balloon go. The balloon started to fly away. Buddy felt sad that he had released the balloon. The little boy saw Buddy's sad face and said, "It's okay, Buddy. We can get another balloon." Buddy and the boy got a new balloon and continued to have a fun day at the zoo. They saw many animals and played together. Buddy learned to be more careful with balloons, and he made a new friend at the zoo.

Once upon a time, there was an adorable little monkey named Momo. Momo lived in a big tree with his family. Every day, Momo would play with his friends and eat bananas. One day, Momo saw a very big banana high up in the tree. He wanted to eat it, but he could not reach it. Momo asked his friend, the bird, "Can you help me get the banana?" The bird said, "Yes, I can help you." So, the bird flew up and tried to get the banana for Momo. But, when the bird touched the banana, something unexpected happened. The big banana fell from the tree and broke into many little bananas. Momo and the bird were very surprised. They called all their friends, and everyone ate the little bananas together. It was a fun day for Momo and his friends.

Sara and Tom were friends. They liked to play in the park. They had a lot of fun on the swings, the slide and the seesaw. But one day, the park was closed. There was a big sign that said "No Entry". Sara and Tom were sad. "Why is the park closed?" Sara asked. "Maybe they are fixing something," Tom said. "Or maybe they are making something new," Sara said. They decided to walk around the fence and see if they could find out. They saw some men with big machines and tools. They were digging and building and painting. Sara and Tom were curious. "What are they doing?" Tom asked. "Let's go closer and see," Sara said. They sneaked behind a tree and peeked at the men. They saw something shiny and round and tall. It looked like a big flower. Sara and Tom gasped. "Wow, what is that?" Sara asked. "It looks like a giant sunflower," Tom said. "Maybe it can grow and grow and touch the sky," Sara said. They wanted to touch it and climb it and play with it. They forgot about the sign and the fence and the men. They ran towards the big flower. But before they could reach it, they heard a loud voice. "Hey, you kids, get away from there!" the voice shouted. It was one of the men. He saw Sara and Tom and ran after them. He looked angry and scary. Sara and Tom were scared. "Run, run!" Tom said. They turned around and ran as fast as they could. They ran back to the fence and jumped over it. They ran all the way home. They were out of breath and out of fun. "That was not fun," Sara said. "That was dull," Tom said. They wished the park would open again soon. They missed the swings, the slide and the seesaw. They did not want to see the big flower again. They learned their lesson. They would not sneak around the fence again. They would wait until the park was ready. Maybe then, they would have fun again.
