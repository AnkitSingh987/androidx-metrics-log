One day, a little cat named Luna wanted to become a big helper in her town. She thought that if she made a law, everyone would be happy. Luna went to her friend, a wise old owl named Oliver. Luna said, "Oliver, I want to make a law to help everyone. What should it be?" Oliver thought for a moment and then said, "Luna, the best law is to be kind and share with others." So, Luna went to the town and told everyone about the new law. "We must all be kind and share with each other," she said. The people in the town thought it was an adorable idea and agreed to follow the law. From that day on, everyone in the town was kind and shared with each other. They all became good friends and lived happily together. Luna was proud of her law and felt happy that she could help her town.

Once upon a time, there was a little bird named Tim. Tim loved to sing. He would sing all day long. Tim had many friends who liked to sing with him. They would sing together on the side of a big tree. One day, Tim met a new friend. The new friend was a bug named Sam. Sam was confused. He did not know how to sing. Tim wanted to help Sam. He told Sam to try and sing with him. They tried to sing together, but Sam was still confused. Tim told Sam to listen to the other birds sing. Sam listened and soon he started to sing too. Now, Tim, Sam, and all their friends could sing together on the side of the big tree. They were all very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a clumsy fish. The fish loved to swim around and listen to music. Every day the fish swam around and listened to music, but one day he swam too close to the shore. Suddenly, the clumsy fish was scooped up and put in a bucket. The music stopped playing as the fish was struggling to get free. Unfortunately, the fish was so clumsy, he could not escape. Soon after, he was taken away and cooked for dinner! The clumsy fish was never seen again and the music faded away.

Tom and Mia are friends. They like to play and laugh together. One day, they enter a big tent. The tent is for a circus. They want to see the clowns and the animals. Inside the tent, they see a lot of people. They also see a big ring. In the ring, there are some clowns. The clowns are funny. They wear big shoes and big noses. They make jokes and do tricks. Tom and Mia laugh a lot. One of the clowns is very clumsy. He trips and falls a lot. He tries to juggle some balls, but he drops them. He tries to ride a bike, but he crashes. He tries to spray some water, but he gets wet. He makes everyone laugh more. Tom and Mia think he is the best clown. They clap and cheer for him. The clumsy clown sees them and smiles. He waves at them and throws them a ball. Tom and Mia catch the ball and wave back. They are happy. They like the circus. They like the clumsy clown.

Once upon a time, there was a little goat. The goat liked to play all day. One day, the goat found an empty box. The goat was curious and wanted to see what was inside. The goat opened the box, but it was empty. The goat was sad. The goat decided to repair the empty box. The goat found some pretty paper and glue. The goat covered the box with the pretty paper. The goat was happy with the new box. The goat showed the new box to its friends. The friends liked the box, too. They all played with the box together. They put toys inside the box and took them out again. The goat and its friends had a lot of fun with the repaired box. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a toy telephone that she loved very much. One day, she wanted to play with her friends, so she took her telephone and went outside. Lily saw her friend, Tim, playing with his ball. She went to him and said, "Tim, let's play with my telephone!" Tim smiled and said, "Yes, I like your pretty telephone, Lily!" They played together, taking turns to put the telephone to their ears and pretend to talk. They laughed and had so much fun. After a while, they got tired and sat down under a big tree. Lily put her pretty telephone down and they both enjoyed a nice rest.
