Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play outside in the sun. One day, while playing, she saw a rare bird. The bird was big and blue. Sue wanted to remember the bird, so she ran to get her mom. When Sue and her mom came back, the bird was gone. They were sad. Sue's mom said, "Let's wait and maybe the bird will come back." They waited and waited. Then, something unexpected happened. Rain started to fall. Sue and her mom ran inside their house. After the rain stopped, Sue and her mom went outside. They saw the big, blue bird again! It was sitting in a tree. Sue was so happy. She learned that sometimes, you have to wait for special things.

Once there was a little girl called Jenna. She was three years old and loved to explore the world around her. Today was a gloomy day and she decided to take a dive into the vegetable patch in her garden. She walked to the patch and saw lots of vegetables; carrots, tomatoes, squash, and cabbage. Jenna got down on her hands and knees and put her hands in some mud. She started digging. As she was digging, she heard a voice ask, "What are you doing?" Jenna looked up and saw a big, brown rabbit sitting next to the vegetables. Jenna said, "I'm diving into the vegetable patch. I want to see what I can find!" The rabbit said, "Can I help you? I love to dive too!" Jenna smiled and said, "Yes please!" The two of them dug and dug until they found a carrot! Jenna was so excited. She thanked the rabbit and took the carrot home. From then on, Jenna and the rabbit became the best of friends. They had lots of fun diving into the vegetable patch together.

Once upon a time, there was a nice driver. He drove a big bus. One day, he saw an unknown animal on the road. It was small and furry. The driver stopped the bus to help the animal. "Hello, little one," said the driver. "I will provide you with a safe place to stay." The animal was happy and went on the bus. The driver and the animal became good friends. One day, the animal found a magic hat. It put the hat on its head. Suddenly, the animal turned into a big and friendly bear. Everyone was surprised and happy. The driver, the bear, and the people on the bus had a fun party together.

One day, a light dancer named Lily was jumping and spinning in the park. She was so happy and full of fun. Lily was the best dancer in the park. A little boy named Tim saw Lily and wanted to dance too. He went to Lily and said, "Please, can you teach me to dance like you?" Lily smiled and said, "Of course, I can teach you. Just follow me and do what I do." But Tim was not a good dancer. He tried to jump and spin like Lily, but he fell down. Tim started to cry. Lily felt sad for Tim and thought of a way to help him. Lily had an idea. She took Tim's hand and said, "Let's dance together. I will hold your hand, and we can jump and spin as one." Tim stopped crying and smiled. They danced together, holding hands, and Tim became a good dancer too. Tim and Lily were now the best dancers in the park, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, in a faraway country, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a fit boy who loved to run and play. One day, while playing, he found a big, shiny key. He showed it to his mom and asked, "Mom, what is this key for?" His mom looked at the key and said, "I remember when I was a little girl, I heard about a treasure box hidden in our country. This key might open it. But no one knows where the box is." Tim was so excited. He wanted to find the treasure box. The next day, he went on a long walk to look for it. On his way, he met a wise old man. The old man told Tim, "I know where the treasure box is. It is under the big oak tree near the river." Tim thanked the old man and ran to the big oak tree. He dug and dug until he found the treasure box. He used the shiny key to open it. Inside, he found lots of toys and treats. Tim was so happy. He ran back home to share the treasure with his family and friends.
