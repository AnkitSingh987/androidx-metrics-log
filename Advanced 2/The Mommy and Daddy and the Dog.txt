Once there was a mommy and a daddy who wanted to have a baby. So, they went to the doctor's office and he made a special plan. After a few months, the mommy and the daddy were very excited when their baby was born. The baby was small and very cute. But the baby's eyes were very deep. No matter how hard the mommy and the daddy tried, they weren't able to make the baby laugh and smile. They became a bit worried that their little baby didn't seem happy. One day, the mommy and the daddy heard someone laughing and smiling in their house. They quickly ran inside and found that the baby was smiling and having lots of fun with the family dog. The mommy said, "Look how united our family is! We have our baby and our dog, and they both bring us lots of joy!" From that day onward, the mommy, the daddy, the baby and the dog were always together and they always shared lots of love and laughs.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy had very long hair. She loved her hair very much. One day, Amy wanted to change her hair. She wanted to look like her friend, who had short hair. So, she found some scissors and cut her hair all by herself. When Amy looked in the mirror, she did not like what she saw. Her hair was not pretty like her friend's hair. It was messy and uneven. Amy felt ashamed. She did not want her friends to see her hair like this. She started to cry. Amy's mom heard her crying and came to see what was wrong. When she saw Amy's hair, she gave her a big hug. She told Amy that it was okay and that everyone makes mistakes. Amy's mom took her to a hair person, who fixed Amy's hair and made it look nice again. Amy was happy and learned that it is always better to ask for help when she wants to change something.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a pair of sunglasses on the ground. He thought they were very cool, so he put them on. He walked around, feeling very happy with his new sunglasses. Tim saw his friend, Sue, who had a small cut on her arm. "What happened?" Tim asked gently. "I fell down," Sue said, feeling sad. Tim wanted to help Sue feel better, so he told her, "Don't worry, it will heal soon." Sue looked at Tim's sunglasses and smiled. "Can I try them on?" she asked. Tim said, "Sure!" and gave the sunglasses to Sue. When Sue put on the sunglasses, she felt happy too. They played together all day, and soon, Sue's cut healed. They were very happy and knew that their friendship was special.

Once upon a time, in a big tree, there was a little monkey named Momo. Momo was not happy. He was sad because he had no friends to play with. One day, Momo saw a great big lion named Leo. Momo wanted to be friends with Leo. But Momo was scared. He thought, "Will Leo like me?" Momo went to Leo and said, "Hi, I am Momo. Can we be friends?" Leo looked at Momo and smiled. "Yes, Momo, we can be friends!" Momo was so happy, he jumped up and down. But then, Momo fell down from the tree. He started to cry. Leo heard Momo cry and ran to help him. "Don't cry, Momo," said Leo. "I will help you." Leo picked up Momo and put him back in the tree. Momo stopped crying and said, "Thank you, Leo. You are a great friend." From that day on, Momo and Leo were best friends. They played together every day. All the other animals saw them and wanted to be friends too. Soon, Momo and Leo had many friends to play with. The moral of the story is: Don't be scared to make new friends. Friends can help you when you are sad or in trouble. And when you have friends, you will never be alone.

One day, a little girl named Lily wanted to bake a cake. She was very careful when she mixed the flour, sugar, and eggs. She asked her mom, "Can I bake the cake all by myself?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful with the oven. It is very hot." Lily was excited and she knew she had to be careful. While the cake was baking, Lily wrote a poem. The poem was about a big, yummy cake. She read it to her mom and her mom smiled. But then, they smelled something burning. They both ran to the oven and saw that the cake was burnt! Lily was sad, but her mom said, "Don't worry, we can bake another cake together and be more careful this time." So they baked a new cake and it turned out perfect. Lily read her poem again and they all enjoyed the cake while they listened.
