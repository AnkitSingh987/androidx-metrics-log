One day, a boy named Tim went with his mom to a big building. They needed to go up to the top floor. Tim was very excited because he had never been in an elevator before. "Are you ready, Tim?" his mom asked. "Yes, I am ready!" Tim shouted. When they got into the elevator, Tim saw a big button with the number 10 on it. That was the top floor! Tim leaned on the button and the elevator started to go up. Tim's mom said, "Don't lean on the button, Tim. It's not a good idea." But Tim didn't listen. He leaned on the button again and again. The elevator started to shake and make loud noises. Tim's mom looked worried. She knew something bad was going to happen. Suddenly, the elevator stopped moving. The lights went out and it was very dark. Tim and his mom were stuck! They had to wait a long time for help. When they finally got out, Tim's mom said, "Next time, please listen when I tell you not to do something." Tim felt sad because he knew he should have listened to his mom.

Bobby was a little boy who loved to go to school. All his classmates were so nice and they always had a lot of fun together. One day, the teacher told them a story about a wealthy family. She said the family had lots of money and nice things. Bobby thought it was exciting to hear about them. After the story was done, the teacher asked Bobby to listen very carefully. He did and he heard a noise coming from outside. It was a puppy barking! Bobby was so excited. He waved his hands and shouted out that he wanted to go see the puppy. The whole class laughed and the teacher said they could go and take a look. When they got to the yard, there was a big, fluffy puppy! Bobby was so happy. He cuddled the puppy and smiled. This was the best school day ever! The teacher reminded them to always listen and to be careful. Bobby said he would and then he went back to class with his friends. He learned a lot that day and had fun too!

Once upon a time, there was a big orange whale. The whale loved to play in the blue sea. Every day, the whale would splash and jump with his fish friends. One day, the orange whale wanted to do something new. He closed his eyes and started to imagine. He thought about flying in the sky like a bird. He imagined himself with big wings, soaring high above the sea. The whale told his fish friends about his dream. They all thought it was a fun idea. So, they played a game together. They pretended to be birds, swimming fast and jumping high out of the water. The orange whale was happy because he could imagine and play with his friends. And they all had a great time.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the zoo with his mom. They saw many animals, but Tim's favorite was the big lion. The lion had a long mane and a loud roar. Tim felt lucky to see the lion up close. Tim asked his mom, "Can I talk to the lion?" His mom smiled and said, "You can try, but the lion may not reply." Tim walked up to the lion's cage and said, "Hi, Mr. Lion. You are big and strong." To Tim's surprise, the lion replied with a soft "roar." Tim was so happy that the lion talked to him. Tim and the lion became good friends. They talked and played every time Tim went to the zoo. Tim knew he was lucky to have a lion friend who could reply to him. And the lion was happy to have a little boy who was not scared of his roar.

Ben was an adventurous boy who liked to explore nature. He always had many questions about the animals, the plants and the rocks he saw. He wanted to learn everything he could. One day, he went to the park with his mom and saw a big pond. He saw ducks, fish and frogs in the water. He ran to the edge of the pond and asked his mom, "Can I touch them? Can I feed them? Can I catch them?" His mom said, "No, Ben. You have to be careful. The water is deep and you might fall in. The animals might bite you or get scared. You have to respect nature and leave them alone." But Ben did not listen. He was too curious and excited. He took a stick and tried to poke a frog. The frog jumped away and splashed water on Ben. Ben got wet and cold. He tried to grab a fish, but the fish was too fast and slippery. Ben slipped and fell into the pond. He got soaked and muddy. He cried for help. His mom heard him and ran to him. She pulled him out of the pond and hugged him. She said, "Ben, are you okay? You were very naughty and foolish. You did not listen to me. You could have hurt yourself or the animals. You have to be more careful and respectful of nature. Nature is beautiful and wonderful, but also dangerous and wild. You have to ask questions, but also follow the rules." Ben felt sorry and ashamed. He said, "I'm sorry, mom. I was wrong. I won't do it again. I love nature, but I also love you. You are right. I have to be more careful and respectful of nature. And I have to listen to you. You know more than me. You are smart and kind." His mom smiled and said, "I love you too, Ben. You are a good boy and a good learner. You are adventurous and curious, but you also have to be safe and responsible. Come on, let's go home and change your clothes. And maybe we can read a book about nature and learn more about it." Ben nodded and said, "Okay, mom. Thank you. You are the best mom ever." The end.
