Once upon a time, there were two best friends. One was named Sarah and one was named Tommy. They were both very cute and loved to play together. One day, Tommy had a special toy. It was a funny toy that made a scary noise when you shook it. Tommy was so excited to show Sarah. "Look Sarah!" Tommy said. "It scares! Can you hear it?" Sarah was scared, but also curious. "Wow Tommy," said Sarah. "Is it really scary?" Tommy nodded and smiled. He shook the toy harder, and it made an even scarier noise. Sarah laughed and said, "That's so cute! I love it!"

Once upon a time, there was a messy person named Bob. Bob liked to print pictures. One day, he wanted to print a big picture of a cat. He went to his friend, Sue, and said, "Sue, can you help me print a cat picture?" Sue said, "Yes, Bob! Let's print the cat picture together." They started to print the cat picture, but something unexpected happened. The printer made a funny noise and printed a dog picture instead! Bob and Sue looked at the dog picture and laughed. They thought it was funny that they got a dog picture instead of a cat picture. Bob said, "I like this dog picture, Sue. It's a nice surprise!" The moral of the story is that sometimes, things don't go as planned, but that can be fun and make you happy too.

John and his friend Sally were having a great day playing together. Suddenly, John made a joke and began to laugh. He was so proud of his joke, he started to tell more. “John, don’t joke like that," warned Sally. "It sounds mean." John didn't listen and he kept joking. Even when Sally's mom called from the kitchen and asked them to come in for lunch, John kept joking. When John and Sally arrived in the kitchen, John noticed something funny in the fridge. So he joked about it. Sally's mom was angry and she said, "John, joking is not nice. You need to stop." But John just kept joking and he laughed even louder. Sally's mom was so angry, she made John go home. He felt very sad and he was no longer proud of his joke.

Anna and Ben were twins who liked to play in the park. They had a favorite slide that was big and fast. They took turns to slide down and laugh. One day, a new boy came to the park. He was bigger than Anna and Ben and he had a mean face. He wanted to slide too, but he did not wait for his turn. He pushed Anna and Ben away and slid down by himself. He did not care if they were hurt or sad. Anna and Ben did not like the new boy. They wanted to tell him to stop, but they were afraid. They looked at the sky and saw a bright star. They wished the star would help them. The star heard their wish and smiled. It sent a gentle breeze to the park. The breeze made the slide slippery and the new boy slid too fast. He lost his balance and fell off the slide. He landed on the grass and cried. Anna and Ben felt sorry for the new boy. They ran to him and asked if he was okay. The new boy was surprised and ashamed. He said he was sorry for being mean and asked if they could be friends. Anna and Ben nodded and helped him up. They invited him to slide with them, but only if he waited for his turn. The new boy agreed and learned to be obedient. The star winked at them and faded away. It was happy that Anna and Ben were kind and brave, and that the new boy was sorry and changed. It hoped they would always play nicely and share the slide.

Once upon a time, there was a big, thick tree in a warm forest. The tree was very strong and could support many little animals. One hot day, a small bird named Timmy came to the tree. Timmy said to the tree, "Hello tree! Can you support me and my nest? The heat is too much on the ground." The tree smiled and said, "Yes, Timmy, I can support you. Come, make your nest on my branch." So, Timmy made a cozy nest on the tree's branch. He felt much better in the shade of the tree. The heat was not a problem for him anymore. Timmy and the tree became very good friends. They were happy to support each other.
