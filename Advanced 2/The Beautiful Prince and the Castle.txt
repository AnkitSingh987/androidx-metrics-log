Once upon a time, there was a beautiful prince who lived in a modern castle. He was walking through the castle one day when he heard a loud noise coming from outside. He decided to investigate, so he grabbed a key and ran out of the castle. When the prince arrived outside, he saw a group of children playing loudly. He was about to scold them when he noticed that one of the kids was crying and looking very sad. The prince paused and asked why the child was so unhappy. The child replied that they were playing a game without her and were not including her. The prince smiled and told the other children that everyone should be included and that the game could not continue until the crying child was having fun too. The other kids agreed and they all played together until it was time to go home. The prince smiled and waved goodbye as they all walked away. The prince was proud that he had solved the problem and he returned to the castle with a peaceful feeling in his heart.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily liked to prepare food for her family. She loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom gave her a new job. She had to mix the powder to make a cake. Lily was very happy. She took the big bowl and put the powder in it. She stirred and stirred, making the powder into a thick mix. She felt like a modern chef, just like on TV. Her mom was proud of her. When the cake was ready, Lily and her family sat down to eat it. They all loved the cake. Lily's mom said, "You did a great job, Lily!" Lily smiled and knew she would help her mom in the kitchen again.

Sara and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to slide, swing, and run. But their favorite thing was to dig in the sand. They had shovels and buckets and made big holes and piles. One day, they found something shiny in the sand. It was a round thing with a hole in the middle. It had strange marks on it. Sara and Ben wondered what it was. "Maybe it is a ring for a giant," Sara said. "Maybe it is a wheel for a very old car," Ben said. They wanted to keep it, but they did not know how to share it. They both grabbed it and pulled. "Give it to me!" Sara demanded. "No, it is mine!" Ben demanded. They pulled and pulled, but the shiny thing did not move. It was stuck in the sand. They pulled harder and harder, but the shiny thing did not budge. They pulled so hard that they fell down. They looked up and saw a man standing over them. He had a hat and a coat and a big bag. He smiled and said, "Hello, children. Do you know what you have found?" Sara and Ben shook their heads. "This is a very special thing," the man said. "It is an ancient coin. It is very old and very rare. It comes from a far away land. It is part of the earth's history." Sara and Ben were amazed. They had never seen a coin like that before. They felt sorry for fighting over it. "Can we see it?" Sara asked. "Of course," the man said. He took a tool from his bag and gently dug out the coin. He cleaned it with a cloth and showed it to them. It was shiny and beautiful. "Wow," Ben said. The man told them stories about the coin and the land it came from. He said he was a collector and a teacher. He said he loved to find ancient things and learn about them. Sara and Ben listened and learned. They forgot about their fight. They thanked the man for showing them the coin and telling them the stories. They asked if they could keep the coin. The man smiled and said, "No, children. This coin belongs to a museum. It is too precious to keep. But I will give you something else. Something you can share and enjoy." He reached into his bag and took out two small toys. They were cars, but not like the ones Sara and Ben had. They were very old and very rare. They had strange marks on them, just like the coin. "These are ancient toys," the man said. "They come from the same land as the coin. They are part of the earth's history, too. They are for you, Sara and Ben. A gift for finding the coin and being curious and kind." Sara and Ben were delighted. They thanked the man again and hugged him. They took the toys and played with them in the sand. They made roads and bridges and towns. They shared and smiled and had fun. They learned that ancient things are not only shiny and rare, but also interesting and valuable. They learned that the earth has many stories to tell, and that they can discover them if they look and listen. They learned that sharing is better than demanding, and that kindness is better than fighting. They learned a lot that day, Sara and Ben. And they had a lot of fun, too.
