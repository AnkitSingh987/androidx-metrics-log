Once upon a time there was a big, reliable tree. Little Tommy loved to gaze at the big tree. One day, Tommy saw a glass on the ground near the tree. He was amazed and wanted to pick it up. "Mama, look!" Tommy shouted. Mama carefully came over and said "Let's have a look." She picked up the glass and inspected it. "This looks like an interesting item," Mama said. "Let's take it home." So Tommy and Mama went home and placed the glass on the window sill. Tommy would often go to the window to gaze at the glass. It was so shiny and pretty! But one day, Tommy noticed a crack in the glass. He was sad and wanted to fix it. "Don't worry, Tommy," Mama said, giving him a hug. "This glass is very reliable. We can still use it. Now, let's go outside and gaze at the big, reliable tree!" This made Tommy feel better and he smiled for the first time that day.

One day, a sweet cat and a dog went to a big hotel. They wanted to have fun and play. The cat had a red ball. The dog had a blue ball. They liked to roll their balls and run after them. The cat said, "Let's roll our balls and see who is faster!" The dog said, "Yes, I want to play!" So, they rolled their balls down the long hall. The dog ran fast, but the cat was faster. They laughed and played all day. Then, something unexpected happened. A tiny mouse came out and rolled a green ball. The cat and the dog were surprised. The mouse said, "Can I play with you?" The cat and the dog said, "Yes, let's all play together!" So, they all rolled their balls and became good friends. The hotel was a fun place for all of them.

Once upon a time, there was a big armchair in a small room. A little boy named Tim loved to sit on the armchair. It was so soft and cozy. Tim would sit there every day and read his favorite book. One day, Tim's friend, Sally, came to visit. They played and laughed all day. Then Sally saw the armchair and asked, "Can I sit on the armchair too?" Tim said, "Yes, but be careful. It is very special to me." Sally sat on the armchair, but she was not careful. She spilled her drink on the armchair. The armchair was wet and dirty. Sally felt very sorry. She said, "I am so sorry, Tim. I didn't mean to make the armchair dirty." Tim was sad, but he knew that Sally was sorry. He said, "It's okay, Sally. We can clean it together." So they cleaned the armchair, and it looked like new again. From that day on, Tim and Sally were more careful with the armchair. They learned that it is important to be careful with things that are special to others. And they lived happily ever after, sitting on the big armchair and reading their favorite books together.

One day, a little girl was feeling very tired. She was so tired, that she yawned very big. Then, she went to sleep. When she woke up, she saw something that made her very happy. It was a big, smelly cauliflower! She was so excited that she quickly picked it up and hugged it. She knew that she could make something special with the cauliflower. She ran to the kitchen and began to cook. When it was finished cooking, it smelled so good that she yawned again. She ate the cauliflower and it was delicious! She was so happy, she hugged the cauliflower and yawned once more.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He lived in a small house with his mom, dad, and his unique pet, a wide, green frog. Tom loved to play with his friends and his frog in the park. One day, Tom's friends went away to visit their grandma. Tom was sad and lonely. He started to complain to his mom, "I have no one to play with. I miss my friends." His mom said, "Why don't you play with your frog? He's your friend too." Tom thought about it and decided to play with his frog. They played games and had so much fun. Tom learned that he could have fun even when his friends were not around. He also learned that it's not good to complain about things, but to find happiness in what he has. From that day on, Tom stopped complaining and enjoyed playing with his unique, wide frog. He understood that friends can be found in the most unexpected places. And, he was grateful for the love and friendship he had with his pet frog.
