Once upon a time, there was a happy girl. She was named Jacky and she was 3 years old. Every day Jacky would play and have fun with her friends. But one day, Jacky wanted to do something extra special. Jacky asked her dad if she could write something. Her dad gave her a piece of paper and a pencil. Jacky was so excited! She started to write very carefully. As she wrote, a spark appeared in the air. It made Jacky jump with surprise! The spark was crazy and it danced around the room. Jacky and her dad were so excited that they laughed and laughed. Jacky kept writing and soon she had written a very special story. The story was all about the spark. She smiled and showed her dad what she had written. He gave her lots of hugs and kissed. Jacky was so proud of herself and was very happy. In the end, the spark had taught Jacky a valuable lesson - she could do anything if she put her mind to it!

Once upon a time, there was a little red car named Cara. Cara loved to go fast and listen to music. One day, Cara met a big blue truck named Tim. Tim was very useful because he could carry many things. Cara said to Tim, "Hey Tim, let's go on a trip together and listen to fun songs!" Tim agreed, and they started their journey. As they drove, they sang and listened to their favorite tunes. But they forgot one important thing – a license! A nice police officer stopped them and said, "Cara and Tim, you need a license to drive on the road." Cara and Tim felt sad, but they knew the officer was right. They promised to get a license and be safe on the road. From then on, they always remembered to have their license and continued to have many fun adventures together, listening to music and being useful friends.

Tom and Lily were going to see their grandma on a plane. They were very happy and excited. They packed their bags and put on their coats. Mom helped them zip their coats and said, "Are you ready to go?" "Yes, yes, yes!" Tom and Lily said. They got in the car and drove to the airport. They saw many planes in the sky. They pointed and said, "Look, look, look!" At the airport, they had to wait in a long line. They were bored and tired. They wanted to play and run. But Mom said, "No, no, no. You have to stay with me and be quiet." Tom and Lily tried to be good, but they were restless. They saw a big man with a hat and a beard. He looked funny. Tom giggled and said, "Look, look, look!" The man heard Tom and turned around. He was not happy. He was rude. He said, "What are you looking at, kid? Stop laughing at me!" Tom was scared and sad. He said, "Sorry, sorry, sorry." Lily was angry and brave. She said, "You are mean, mean, mean. You should not yell at my brother. He did nothing wrong. You are rude, rude, rude." The man was surprised and ashamed. He said, "Oh, oh, oh. You are right, little girl. I was rude, rude, rude. I am sorry, sorry, sorry. Please forgive me, me, me." Tom and Lily smiled and said, "OK, OK, OK. We forgive you, you, you." Mom was proud and relieved. She said, "Good, good, good. You are very kind and smart, Tom and Lily. Now, let's go to our plane, plane, plane." They walked to their plane and boarded. They found their seats and buckled their belts. They looked out the window and saw the clouds. They said, "Wow, wow, wow!" They were ready to fly and see their grandma. They were happy and excited. They said, "Yay, yay, yay!"

Once upon a time, there was a little seal who lived by the sea. The seal had a friend, a big bird. They liked to play and swim together. One day, while they were playing, the seal found a sour apple on the beach. The seal called to the bird, "Look what I found! It's a sour apple!" The bird flew down and took a bite of the apple. The bird's face showed that it was very sour. They both laughed and continued to play. As the sun began to set, the seal and the bird said goodbye. The seal went back to the sea and the bird flew up to the sky. They knew they would see each other again the next day, to play and maybe find more sour apples.
