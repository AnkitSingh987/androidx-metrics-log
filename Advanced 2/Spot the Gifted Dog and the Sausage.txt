Once upon a time, there was a gifted little dog named Spot. Spot was very good at finding things. One day, Spot's family wanted to prepare a big meal. They needed help to find the sausage they would cook. Spot went to the kitchen and looked everywhere. He looked under the table, in the cupboards, and behind the chairs. Finally, Spot found the sausage in the big cold box. He was so happy! Spot's family was very proud of him. They cooked the sausage and had a big meal together. Spot got a special treat for being so helpful. They all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big, loud truck. It loved to go fast and make noise. The truck had a special chamber in the back to carry things. One day, the truck was going down the road, when it saw a little cat. The truck wanted to help the cat, so it tried to reverse. But it was not good at going backwards. The truck went back and forth, trying to be careful. The cat watched, scared of the big, loud truck. In the end, the truck could not help the cat. It tried too hard to reverse and hit a tree. The tree fell on the truck's special chamber. The truck was sad and broken, and the cat ran away.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was very thick and loved to play outside. One day, Spot saw a newspaper on the ground. He picked it up with his mouth and ran to his best friend, a little girl named Lily. "Look, Lily! I found a newspaper!" said Spot. Lily took the newspaper and opened it. Inside, there was a big picture of a storm. Lily looked at the picture and then at the sky. The sky was getting dark. Lily warned Spot, "We need to go home now, Spot. A big storm is coming!" Spot listened to Lily and they both ran back to their house. They got inside just in time before the rain started to fall. Spot and Lily were safe and warm inside their house, happy that they listened to the warning in the newspaper.

Once upon a time, there was a nice and loyal dog whose name was Max. Max loved to play with everyone who stopped by. One day, Max asked the owner if he could go outside and play. The owner said yes and Max went to the yard. On the way, Max saw an alarm. It looked like a really fun toy so he wanted to play with it. Max asked his owner if he could play with it. The owner said no and reminded Max that the alarm was there for safety. Max was so loyal that he didn't protest. That night, Max stayed close to his owner and stayed awake to be sure no one could harm them. Max was a loyal dog and everyone was grateful that he was watching out for them.

Once upon a time there was a little girl named Anna who lived in a very small house. Anna had a special wish, she wanted to travel around the world. Everyday she would look at the blue sky and imagine different places around the world. One day, Anna decided to make her wish come true. She took a piece of paper and wrote about her wish to travel around the world on it. She asked her mum to mail the letter for her. “Mum, can you mail my wish away?” Anna asked. Mum looked at her daughter and smiled “Of course darling.” She said. That night, Mum took the letter and put it in the mail box. A few days later, Anna got a big surprise. She opened the mailbox and found a package. Inside the package was a map of the world that Anna would be able to use to travel around. Anna was so happy. Her wish had come true. She couldn't wait to start travelling!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She was a very creative girl. She liked to draw and paint all day. One day, she found a big melon in the kitchen. She wanted to do something fun with it. Lily had an idea. She took the melon to her room. She used her colors to paint a face on it. She made it look like a happy friend. She put a hat on the melon head. It was very funny. Lily wanted to show her mom. She hid the melon friend behind her back. She walked to the living room where her mom was. Lily peeked from behind the door. Her mom did not see her yet. Then, Lily jumped out and showed her mom the melon friend. Her mom laughed and clapped. They had a fun day with the creative melon friend.
