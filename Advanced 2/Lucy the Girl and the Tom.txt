One day, a little girl named Lucy went to her room to unpack her bag. She found a note inside. The note was from her friend Tom. It said, "Let's play in the mud!" Lucy got very excited. Lucy ran outside and saw Tom. They both laughed and said, "Let's get filthy!" They jumped in the mud and had so much fun. They got dirt all over their clothes and faces. Suddenly, Lucy's mom came outside. She looked at them and said, "Oh no! You two are so filthy!" But instead of getting mad, she laughed too. She took a hose and sprayed Lucy and Tom clean. They all had a fun day playing in the mud together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to learn about new things. One day, his mom took him to a place called the museum. It was a big building with lots of things to see. When they got to the museum, Tim started to look at all the things. There were big animals, like a dinosaur, and small animals, like a bug. Tim was very happy. But then, he saw a painting on the wall. He wanted to touch it. When he did, the painting fell down. Tim felt ashamed. His mom came over and helped him pick up the painting. She told him it was okay, but he should not touch things in the museum. Tim learned his lesson and they had a good day at the museum.

Fiona was very hungry. She looked all around, but she couldn't find anything to eat. She began to feel dizzy. She walked a little further and suddenly, she saw something bright and orange peeking out of the ground. She knew right away that it was a carrot! She was so excited, she quickly pulled it out of the ground. She took a big bite and it was so sweet. She felt much better and wasn't dizzy anymore. From then on, whenever Fiona was hungry, she went to look for carrots!

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a thin dog named Spot. Spot was sad because he could not raise his head high like the other dogs. He tried every day, but his neck was too weak. One year went by, and Spot was still sad. One day, Spot met a wise old bird named Mr. Feathers. Mr. Feathers saw how sad Spot was and told him, "Spot, if you want to raise your head high, you need to help others." Spot didn't know how he could help, but he wanted to try. The next day, Spot saw a little girl crying because her ball was stuck in a tree. Spot had an idea! He climbed the tree and got the ball for the girl. She was so happy and thanked him. Spot felt good helping her, and he noticed that his neck felt stronger. Spot kept helping others, and soon he could raise his head high like the other dogs. The twist was that by helping others, Spot also helped himself. The moral of the story is that when we help others, we become stronger too.

Once there was a long road. It was long and winding and it was one that no one had ever been down before. One day, a little boy decided to take a journey down the road. He brought some avocados with him to snack on. As he walked, the little boy noticed that the further down the road he went, the more reversed the roads seemed to become. First the path twisted in an unexpected way, then branches formed in the opposite direction. He couldn't figure out why the path was getting reversed. The little boy eventually reached the end of the road. He was surprised to find that there was a playground at the end of the long road. He realized that the path had been reversed because the person who designed the park wanted people to explore it, so they helped make it fun by reversing some of the paths! The little boy had a great time playing in the playground and he was so glad that he had ventured down the long reversed road. He munched on his avocados and enjoyed the time that he spent at the playground.
