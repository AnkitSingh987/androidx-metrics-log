Lily and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to swing on the swings and slide on the slide. They also liked to look at the sky and pretend they were in space. "Look, Lily, there is a big ball in the sky. It is yellow and bright. What is it?" Ben asked. "That is the sun, Ben. It is a star. It gives us light and warmth. It is very far away," Lily said. "Wow, a star. Are there more stars in the sky?" Ben wondered. "Yes, Ben, there are many stars in the sky. But we can only see them at night, when the sun goes to sleep. Some stars have planets around them. Planets are like big rocks that hang in space," Lily explained. "Planets? What are planets like?" Ben asked. "Well, some planets are hot and some are cold. Some have rings and some have moons. Some have water and some have air. And some have life, like us. We live on a planet called Earth. It is blue and green and beautiful," Lily said. "Earth is a planet? I did not know that. That is amazing. Can we go to other planets?" Ben asked. "Maybe, Ben, maybe. But we need a special ship to go to space. And we need a special suit to breathe. And we need to be very brave and smart. It is not easy to go to other planets," Lily said. "I want to go to other planets. I want to see what they are like. Maybe I can find some silly aliens to play with. That would be fun," Ben said. Lily laughed. She liked Ben's imagination. She thought he was silly too, but in a good way. She hugged him and said, "Maybe one day, Ben, maybe one day. But for now, let's enjoy our planet. It is a wonderful place to play."

Once upon a time, there was a big flood in a small town. All the people were sad because they could not go out and play. They had to stay in their houses. It was very boring for everyone, especially the kids. One day, a little girl named Lucy had an idea. She told her mom to order a big boat. Lucy wanted the boat to take all the kids in the town to a fun place. Her mom thought it was a great idea, so she ordered the boat. The boat came and all the kids got in. They thought they were going to a fun place, but then something unexpected happened. The boat took them to a big, beautiful park that was not flooded. The kids were so happy and surprised. They played all day and had lots of fun. The boring days were over, and the town was happy again.

Once upon a time, there was a baby named Tim. Tim liked to crawl all around his house. One day, he found a camera on the floor. The camera was big and shiny, and Tim was very curious. Tim was confused about what the camera was for. He tried to crawl with the camera in his hands, but it was heavy. He decided to show his mommy the new thing he found. When Tim's mommy saw the camera, she smiled. She took a picture of Tim as he crawled. Tim was happy to learn what the camera did. From that day on, Tim and his mommy took many pictures together as he grew up.

Once upon a time there was a mummy and her little boy. He loved painting and the mummy loved watching him paint. One day, his mummy said, "Let's paint something special together. What should we paint?" The little boy thought for a moment and he knew the answer - a rainbow! They went to the store and got some warm colours of paint. The mummy said, "Let's start with the blue." The little boy excitedly began to cover all the blue paint on the paper. But suddenly, the paint ran out. The little boy was sad. The mummy said, "Don't worry, we will buy more. We'll finish the rainbow together". They went back to the store and bought more paint. This time, the little boy had a plan. He said, "Let's cover the rainbow first, then we'll fill it up with colours". The mummy nodded and watched as the little boy started to cover the rainbow with the warm colours. Finally, the rainbow was finished and the little boy and mummy were proud. They stood back and admired their work. The little boy smiled and said, "Let's paint again tomorrow, Mum!"
