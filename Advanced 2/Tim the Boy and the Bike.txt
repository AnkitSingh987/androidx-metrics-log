Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was anxious to ride his bike. He wanted to succeed and ride it all by himself. But first, Tim needed to wear his helmet to keep his head safe. Tim put on his helmet and started to ride his bike. At first, it was hard, and he fell down a few times. But Tim did not give up. He got back on his bike and tried again. After a while, Tim started to get the hang of it. He was able to ride his bike without falling down. Tim was so happy he succeeded! He rode his bike all day, and his helmet kept his head safe.

Once upon a time, there was a little car named Beep. Beep was eager to go fast. He loved to race with his friends. Beep had a big tank, full of fuel. That fuel helped him go very fast. One sunny day, Beep and his friends had a race. They all lined up, ready to go. Beep was so excited. He wanted to win the race. Beep's engine started to burn the fuel. He went faster and faster. Beep was going so fast, he passed all his friends. He was so happy. Beep crossed the finish line first. He won the race! Beep was very proud. His friends were happy for him, too. They all played together after the race.

Once upon a time there was a girl who was very creative. She loved to draw and paint with her colourful crayons. One day, she found a small spool of thread in her mommy's sewing box. The girl was so excited and she couldn't wait to play with it. She grabbed some scissors and started to cut the thread into short pieces. Then she began to make a necklace. When she was finished, she was so proud of her work but she was very dirty too. So, she decided to wash her hands. She filled a bucket with soapy water and she splashed her hands in the bubbles. She felt so good and clean. The girl dried her hands on a towel, hung the necklace around her neck and showed it to her mommy. Mommy was so amazed by her daughter's creativity! The girl was so happy and she was so proud of her necklace. She wore it every day and treasured it. The End!

Once upon a time, there was a three year old who wanted to be impressive. He asked his mom to explain to him how he could do it. His mom smiled and told him she had just the thing to help him. She pulled out a stamp and told him if he had this, he could do some impressive things. The three year old asked what the stamp was for. His mom explained to him that if he got the stamp, he could get into places that usually only grown-ups can go. The three year old was so excited that he grabbed the stamp and ran off to explore. He couldn't believe all the impressive places he could go. He had seen grown-ups go to these places, but now he could too! The three year old was so happy that he had the stamp. Now he could go places, see sights, and do things that grown-ups do. He realized that the stamp was special, and he would never forget how important it was for him to have it and how it made awesome things happen.

Rosa was walking through the park. It was gloomy and still. She wondered what was hiding around the corner. She took a peek, just in case. Beside a tall tree, Rosa saw a small number. What could it be? Rosa was curious. She carefully moved closer to take a better look. The number was 5! She smiled and tried to think why she'd seen a number in the park. What could it be for? As Rosa pondered, she noticed two others walking down the path. She knew right away they weren't friends - their faces were too gloomy. Rosa wondered who they were. She had to take a secret peek, so she hid behind the tree. The two people stopped at the number 5. Rosa finally understood why the number was there. The people had met up at the spot! Rosa knew then that the people were up to no good. The gloomy atmosphere made it obvious. Rosa quickly ran away. The number 5 had been a sign of danger - and she'd been lucky to figure it out in time!
