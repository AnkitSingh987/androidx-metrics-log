One day, Tom was very excited. He was going to be tested at the fountain. He thought it would be fun. Tom jumped into the fountain. The water was cold and wet, but Tom didn't mind. He splashed around in the fountain and then stopped. He looked around to see if anyone was watching him. When he was sure no one was around, he tried the test. He dunked his head underwater, counted to ten, and then came back up. He was so proud of himself. He had passed the test! Tom was so excited that he smiled and did a happy dance in the fountain. He stayed in the fountain until the sun was setting, and then he got out. He was wet, but he was content. Tom had enjoyed testing himself, and he loved the feeling of accomplishment that came with it. From then on, Tom loved to test himself at the fountain.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim went to the park to play. He saw his friend, Sam, and they decided to play catch. Tim had a big red ball that he would throw to Sam, and Sam would throw it back to Tim. While they were playing, a man came to the park. He had a big orange ball. The man asked Tim and Sam if they wanted to trade balls. Tim and Sam thought it would be fun, so they said yes. They gave the man their big red ball and took the big orange ball. Tim threw the big orange ball to Sam, but something unexpected happened. When Sam caught the ball, it turned back into the big red ball! Tim and Sam were surprised and confused. They looked around for the man, but he had disappeared. It turned out the man was a magician who liked to play tricks on people. Tim and Sam laughed and continued to play with their big red ball, happy to have had a little adventure.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. She saw a big strawberry on the ground. Sue was confused. She had never seen a strawberry so big. Sue picked up the strawberry and said, "Wow! I want to show this to my friend Tom!" So, she went to find Tom. When she found Tom, she showed him the strawberry. Tom said, "That's a big strawberry! Let's lay it down here and think about what to do with it." While they were thinking, a big bird flew down from the sky. The bird saw the strawberry and wanted it. The bird picked up the strawberry and flew away. Sue and Tom were sad. They didn't get to keep the big strawberry.

Once upon a time, there was a fisherman. He wore an orange hat. Every day, he went to the sea to catch fish. He liked to throw his net into the water. One day, while the fisherman was throwing his net, he saw a big fish. He was very happy. He pulled the net back and saw that the big fish was orange too. It was a very special fish. The orange fish looked at the fisherman and said, "Please let me go." The fisherman was very surprised. He did not know that fish could talk. He thought about it and said, "Okay, I will let you go." The orange fish was happy and thanked the fisherman. They became good friends and the fisherman never threw his net at the orange fish again.

Once upon a time, there was a smooth rock. This rock lived in a big forest with many trees. One day, a little girl named Lily found the rock. She picked it up and said, "You are so smooth! I will take you home with me." When Lily got home, she put the smooth rock on her table. Then, she saw a skeleton toy in her room. The skeleton was sad because it had no friends. Lily wanted to help the skeleton, so she said, "Don't be sad, Mr. Skeleton! I will be your friend and I will encourage you to make more friends." The next day, Lily took the skeleton and the smooth rock to the park. She played with them and introduced them to other kids. The kids liked the skeleton and the smooth rock, and they all became friends. Soon, the skeleton was not sad anymore. It had many friends, and it was all because Lily encouraged it. The smooth rock was happy too, because it had a new home and new friends. And they all lived happily ever after.
