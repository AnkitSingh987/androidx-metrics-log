One day, a girl named Lucy wanted to design a big, fancy house. She wanted it to be a luxury house. Lucy drew many pictures of her dream house. She used many colors and made it look very pretty. Lucy worked very hard to make her house perfect. Lucy showed her pictures to her friends. They all liked her house design. But one friend, Tom, said it needed to be tough too. Lucy thought about it and agreed. She wanted her house to be both pretty and strong. So, Lucy went back to her pictures and added some tough parts to her house. She added big walls and strong doors. When she showed her new design to her friends, they all loved it even more. But then, something unexpected happened. Lucy's little brother found her pictures and thought they were for coloring. He colored all over her luxury house design. Lucy was sad, but she knew she could make an even better design next time.

Once upon a time, there was a driver named Tom. Tom was driving his big truck on a sunny day. He was happy as he drove along the road. Suddenly, Tom saw a helpless little kitten on the side of the road. He stopped his truck and got out. He wanted to help the kitten. Tom kneeled down next to the kitten and gently picked it up. Tom took the kitten home and gave it food and a warm bed. The kitten was happy and not helpless anymore. Tom and the kitten became best friends, and they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a big tower in a small town. In that tower, lived a kind man named Tom. Tom loved to spread joy and happiness to everyone in the town. Every day, he would give out hugs, smiles, and nice words to make the people happy. One day, a persistent little bird came to the tower. The bird wanted to be friends with Tom. But Tom was scared of the bird. He did not want to be friends with the bird because he thought it might hurt him. So, he closed his door and did not let the bird in. The bird did not give up. It came back every day, trying to show Tom that it was a good friend. One day, the bird brought a beautiful flower and left it at Tom's door. When Tom saw the flower, he felt happy and decided to give the bird a chance. They became the best of friends. The moral of the story is to not judge someone before you know them. Sometimes, the most unexpected friendships can bring the most joy.

One day, a little girl named Amy was eager to play outside. She put on her shoes and ran to the door. But, she could not find her ball. She looked everywhere and was very sad. Then, Amy remembered that she put her ball in a drawer in her room. She went to her room and opened the drawer. There it was! Her ball was inside the drawer. She was very happy and picked it up. Amy called her friend, Tim, to come and play with her. Tim came to her house and they played with the ball all day. They had a lot of fun and laughed a lot. At the end of the day, they were both very tired but happy.

Once upon a time, there were two twins, Tom and Tim. They lived in a small, comfortable house with their mom and dad. Tom and Tim loved to play together and have fun all day. One day, Tom and Tim were playing a game. Tom said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Tim said, "Yes, that sounds fun!" So, they started to play. Tom hid first and Tim tried to find him. Tom found a good hiding spot and stayed very quiet. Tim looked and looked for Tom. He looked in every room of the house, but he could not find him. Tim said, "Tom, where are you?" Then, he heard a little giggle from behind the couch. Tim looked behind the couch and found Tom. They both laughed and hugged each other. Tom and Tim loved playing games together and being twins.
