Once upon a time, there was a mum and a baby. It was a sunny day and the baby wanted to go outside to play. The mum said, "Okay, but be alert and don’t forget your umbrella in case it rains." The baby happily ran outside and began to play with his toys. However, the skies soon darkened and it started to rain. The mum realized that the baby had not brought his umbrella and she ran outside to save him. She shouted, "Baby! Come back inside, it's raining!" The baby was scared but he heard the mum and ran back inside. The mum hugged him and said, "I'm so happy I saved you from getting wet. You must be more alert next time and remember to bring your umbrella." The baby nodded, remembering to always be alert and bring his umbrella when going outside. The End.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big toy box. One day, Tim wanted to find his favorite toy car. He looked in his toy box, but it was empty. Tim was sad and started to cry. Tim's mom told him to look around the house. Tim looked and looked, but he could not find his toy car. He started to struggle with his feelings. Tim felt angry and upset. He did not know what to do. Tim spent a lot of time looking for his toy car. But, he never found it. In the end, Tim's toy car was lost forever. Tim was very sad and learned that sometimes, things don't have a happy ending.

Once upon a time, there was an ancient desk. It was big and brown. The desk lived in a room with many toys. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the room. He saw the desk and said, "Hello, desk! You are very big." The desk did not reply. Tim felt sad. Then, a toy named Bob jumped on the desk. Bob said to the desk, "Please talk to Tim. He is sad." The desk replied, "Okay, I will talk to Tim." Tim was happy, and they all played together.

One day, a big cat went to find food. He wanted to be successful in his hunt. He saw a little mouse and thought, "This will be easy!" The cat tried to catch the mouse, but the mouse was too fast. The cat was not successful. Then, a big bird appeared in the sky. The bird had a look at the cat and the mouse. The bird thought, "I can help the cat catch the mouse." The bird swooped down to grab the mouse, but the mouse hid in a hole. The cat and the bird became friends. They thought they could work together to catch the mouse. But then, something unexpected happened. The mouse came out of the hole with a big piece of cheese. The cat and the bird looked at each other and decided to eat the cheese instead. The mouse was happy to share, and they all became friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Jack who had a very special coat. One day, he wore his coat and went out for a walk. He came to a field where there was a group of birds chirping and singing. Jack wanted to talk to the birds, so he said, "Hello, little birds! Can you talk to me?" The birds were a bit scared, so they just chirped. But then one of they said, "Yes, we can talk! What do you want to know?" Jack was very surprised. He said, "Can you guess what color my coat is?" The birds flew around his coat and then one of they said, "Your coat is blue! You must be very gifted to be able to talk to us!" Jack beamed with pride and said, "Yes, I am gifted! Thank you birds, for talking to me!" Then, he went back home with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time, there was a family who wanted to go on a vacation. They were so excited to bake together in their new kitchen. On their first night, they heard a loud noise coming from outside. The father went to investigate and found that their garden was full of dead plants. He was very surprised and sad. The family decided to go and talk to their neighbor, who admitted that he had been baking late at night. The neighbor said that he didn't care if he ruined the family's garden. The family was so disappointed. They thought their vacation was going to be so nice, but it didn't turn out the way they expected. The family had to spend their vacation in the dead garden. They were not able to bake together, and the vacation was ruined.
