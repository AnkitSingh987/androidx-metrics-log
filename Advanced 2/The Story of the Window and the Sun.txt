Once upon a time, there was a big, comfortable bed. It lived in a room with a window that faced east. Every morning, the sun would shine through the window and wake up the bed. One day, the bed had a talk with the sun. The bed said, "Sun, can you please not shine so bright in the morning? I want to sleep more." The sun replied, "I'm sorry, bed. I need to rise in the east to start the day." The next day, the bed had a talk with the window. The bed said, "Window, can you please not let the sun in so early? I want to sleep more." The window replied, "I'm sorry, bed. I need to stay open so the room can be bright and happy." The bed felt sad that it could not sleep more. But then, the bed had an idea. It asked a curtain to help. The curtain said, "I can help you, bed. I can cover the window when the sun comes up, and you can sleep more." From that day on, the curtain covered the window every morning, and the bed was able to sleep more. The bed, the window, and the curtain were all happy. The moral of the story is that sometimes we need to ask others for help to find a solution to our problems.

Once upon a time, there was a popular dog named Spot. Spot loved to play with his friends in the park. One sunny day, Spot and his friends were playing near a big puddle. Spot's friend, a little bird named Tweet, said, "Let's jump in the puddle and soak!" So they jumped and splashed in the puddle. They had so much fun soaking in the water. After playing, Spot's owner came to take him home. Spot's owner saw that his belt was wet from the puddle. "Oh, Spot! Your belt is all wet from soaking in the puddle," said his owner. Spot wagged his tail, happy to have had such a fun day with his friends.

One day, a quiet little mouse named Tim found a big prune. He was so happy because he loved to eat prunes. Tim wanted to take the prune to his home. But the prune was too big for him to carry. Tim had an idea. He would pinch the prune with his small hands and try to roll it. He pinched and pushed, and the prune started to roll. Tim was very excited. He kept pinching and pushing the prune all the way to his home. When he got home, Tim shared the big prune with his family. They all loved it and were very happy. Tim felt proud that he could bring the big prune home. From that day on, Tim was not just a quiet little mouse, but also a very helpful one.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big yellow toy truck. He loved to play with it all day long. One day, while playing with his truck, Tim accidentally broke the tip of the truck. Tim was very sad and cried. His mom saw him crying and asked, "Why are you crying, Tim?" Tim showed his mom the broken truck and said, "My truck is broken, and I can't play with it anymore." Mom hugged Tim and said, "Don't worry, we can restore your truck." Mom took some glue and fixed the broken tip of the truck. Tim was very happy and thanked his mom. From that day, Tim learned to be careful with his toys and that it's okay to ask for help when something is broken.

One day, a girl named Sue wanted to have a party. She thought it would be fun to make a modern invitation. Sue asked her mom, "Can I print invitations for my friends?" Her mom said, "Yes, that is a great idea!" Sue made a pretty drawing for the invitation. She was excited to print it. She put the paper in the printer and pressed the button. But, something unexpected happened! The printer printed a picture of a big, funny cat instead of her drawing. Sue was surprised and laughed. Sue showed the funny cat invitation to her mom. Her mom laughed too and said, "This is a great twist! Your friends will love it." Sue sent the funny cat invitations to her friends. At the party, everyone laughed at the funny cat and had a great time.
