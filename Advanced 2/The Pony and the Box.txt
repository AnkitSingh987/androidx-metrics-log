Once upon a time, there was a little pony. The pony was very bossy. He liked to tell all his friends what to do. One day, the bossy pony was playing with his friends in the field. The pony saw a big box in the field. He stared at it for a long time. He told his friends to come and look at the box. They all stared at the box too. They were not sure what was inside. The bossy pony opened the box. Inside, there was a tiny kitten. The kitten was not bossy at all. The kitten was very nice. The bossy pony and his friends played with the kitten. They all became good friends. And the bossy pony learned to be nice too.

Once upon a time, there was a gentle barber named Bob. He loved to clean hair and make people look nice. Bob had a small shop in the town where he worked every day. One day, a little boy named Tim came into the shop with his mom. Tim had messy hair and needed a haircut. Bob said, "Hello, Tim! I will clean your hair and make it look nice." Tim was a little scared, but he sat in the chair and let Bob start cutting his hair. As Bob cut Tim's hair, he talked to him in a soft voice. Tim started to feel less scared. When Bob finished, Tim's hair looked very nice and clean. Tim said, "Thank you, Bob! I like my new haircut." Bob smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim! Come back soon."

Once upon a time, in a big forest, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo was very excited because he found a new loop to play in. The loop was made of soft grass, and it was the perfect place for Bobo to hop and jump. One sunny day, Bobo was playing in the loop when he saw his friend, Lulu the squirrel. Lulu was sad because she lost her favorite nut. Bobo wanted to help, so they looked and looked for the nut. They found it near a big tree, but it had a small bite in it. Lulu was upset and thought Bobo took a bite of her nut. Bobo felt bad and said sorry to Lulu. He didn't mean to make her sad. Lulu looked at Bobo and saw that he was truly sorry. She decided to forgive him. They hugged and played together in the loop. From that day, Bobo and Lulu became best friends and always played together in their favorite loop. And they always shared their nuts and treats with each other.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a girl named Lily. Lily was very intelligent. She loved to cook for her family. One day, she wanted to make a special dinner for her mom and dad. Lily went to the kitchen and started to cook. She mixed and stirred, and soon the yummy smell filled the house. Her little brother, Tim, came into the kitchen. He saw Lily cooking and asked, "What are you making?" "I'm cooking dinner for Mom and Dad," Lily said with a smile. "I want it to be a surprise!" Later, Lily and Tim went outside to play. They found a big red bike with pedals. They took turns riding the bike. They pedaled fast and slow, laughing and having fun. When it was time for dinner, Lily and Tim went back inside. They sat at the table with their mom and dad. Everyone loved the food that Lily cooked. They were so proud of their intelligent daughter. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very eager to learn about tools. One day, he saw his dad using a hammer. Tim asked, "Dad, what is that?" Dad smiled and said, "This is a hammer, Tim. It is a tool we use to hit nails into the wood." Dad wanted to explain more, but he knew Tim was only three years old. Tim watched his dad use the hammer. He saw how the hammer hit the nail and the nail went into the wood. Tim clapped his hands and said, "I want to try!" Dad said, "Okay, Tim. But be careful." He gave Tim the hammer and a big nail. Tim held the hammer and tried to hit the nail. He missed a few times, but then he hit the nail just right. Tim was so happy. He said, "I did it, Dad! I used the hammer!" Dad hugged Tim and said, "Good job, Tim! You're a great helper." And they both laughed and continued working together.
