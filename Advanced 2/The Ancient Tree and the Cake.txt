Once upon a time, there was a big ancient tree. It lived in a happy forest. The tree had a secret. Inside the tree, there was a magic crystal. One day, a kind girl named Lily found the tree. She saw the crystal and picked it up. It was very pretty. Lily wanted to do something special with the crystal. Lily had an idea. She decided to bake a cake with the crystal on top. She made the cake and put the crystal on it. The cake became magic, too. When Lily and her friends ate the cake, they all felt very happy. The magic crystal made everyone smile.

One day, a red ball was rolling down the street. It rolled from one side of the street to the other side. The red ball met a blue ball on the other side. "Hi, I am Red Ball," said the red ball. "Do you want to play with me?" "Sure, I am Blue Ball," said the blue ball. "Let's change sides and roll together." So, the red ball and the blue ball rolled together. They changed sides many times, laughing and playing. They had so much fun and became best friends. And from that day on, the red ball and the blue ball always played together on both sides of the street.

One day, a girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some pasta for dinner. Sue saw a big bag of pasta on a high shelf. She wanted to help her mom, so she tried to pull the bag down. As Sue pulled the pasta, the bag opened! Pasta fell all over the floor. Sue was sad and scared. She thought her mom would be mad. But her mom just smiled and said, "It's okay, we can clean it up." Sue and her mom cleaned the pasta off the floor. Then they bought a new bag of pasta. When they got home, Sue's mom showed her how to open the bag the right way. She pulled the top of the bag open slowly and carefully, so the pasta didn't spill. Sue learned that it's important to be careful when you pull things, especially if they can open and make a mess. She also learned that it's okay to make mistakes, as long as you learn from them and help fix them.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the shop with her mom. Sue loved going to the shop because she could see so many colorful things. She liked to look at the toys, but her mom said they were expensive. Sue saw a big toy cat on a shelf. She wanted to play with it. She took it off the shelf and said, "Hi, cat! Do you want to play with me?" The toy cat did not say anything, but Sue played with it anyway. While Sue was playing, her big brother Tom came up behind her. He wanted to scare her for fun. Tom said, "Boo!" Sue jumped and laughed. She said, "You scared me, Tom!" Tom laughed too, and they both played with the toy cat. Sue's mom smiled and said, "Okay, we can buy the toy cat. But only because you two are playing so nicely together." Sue and Tom were very happy and thanked their mom.

Once upon a time, there was a bean. This bean was very special, as it had the power to become anything you wished. A three-year-old child wished for the bean to become a sparkly toy car. But when the bean became the car, something serious happened. The car started to roll away and it didn't stop. It rolled off a cliff and broke into tiny pieces. Everyone was very sad, but it was too late. The special bean was gone forever.

One day, a zebra named Ziggy went to play with his friends. They all wanted to watch a race. The race was for fast animals. Ziggy was competitive and wanted to win. Before the race, Ziggy talked to his friend, the lion. "I want to win," said Ziggy. "I will run very fast." The lion smiled and said, "You can do it, Ziggy!" The race started and all the animals ran fast. Ziggy was in the front. But then, something unexpected happened. A big bird flew down and picked Ziggy up. The bird took Ziggy away from the race. Ziggy was sad that he could not win the race. But then, the bird took him to a new place with many zebras. They were all playing and having fun. Ziggy made new friends and was happy. He learned that winning is not always the most important thing.
