Once upon a time, there was a big yacht. It was a very nice yacht. The yacht had a friend. The friend was a little boat. They liked to play in the sea. One day, a big storm came. The storm was very bad. The wind blew hard. The waves were high. The little boat was scared. The big yacht was scared too. They wanted to go home. The big yacht had an idea. It would send a message for help. The big yacht sent the message to the sea. The sea was kind. It sent help to the big yacht and the little boat. Soon, the storm was over. The big yacht and the little boat were safe. They were happy to be home. They played in the sea again.

Once upon a time, there was a big factory. The factory was very modern. It had many big machines. People worked at the factory to make toys for kids. One day, a girl named Lucy went to the factory. Lucy loved to read books. She brought her favorite book with her. Lucy wanted to show the factory workers her book. While Lucy read her book, something unexpected happened. The machines started to make new toys. These toys were different. They were book toys! The workers were surprised and happy. Lucy had made the factory even better with her love for reading.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He had a big job to do. He had to give an envelope to his mom. But Tim was a bit forgetful. He was always losing things. This time, he did not want to forget. He did not want to feel ashamed. So, he put the envelope in his bag and went to find his mom. On his way, Tim saw a big dog. The dog was playing with a ball. Tim wanted to play too. So, he stopped and played with the dog. They had a lot of fun. But, Tim forgot about the envelope. He left his bag near the dog. After playing, Tim went to find his mom. But, he did not have the bag. Tim felt very ashamed. He did not know what to do. He went back to find the dog and his bag. But, the dog was not there. Tim was very sad. Tim went home and told his mom about the envelope. She was not happy. She was very mad. Tim felt very bad. He knew he should not have played with the dog. He knew he should not have forgotten the envelope. Now, his mom was mad, and Tim felt very ashamed.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim had a wallet. The wallet was heavy. He liked to carry it everywhere. One day, Tim went to the park. He played with his friends. They ran, jumped, and laughed. Tim forgot about his heavy wallet. It was time to go home. Tim looked for his wallet but could not find it. He was sad. His friends wanted to surprise him. They looked for the wallet too. They found the heavy wallet under a big tree. Tim's friends gave it to him. "Surprise!" they said. Tim smiled and thanked his friends. They all went home happy.

One day, a little girl named Lily went outside to play. She saw the sky was very blue and pretty. Lily had a toy that could spray water. She thought it would be fun to spray the water up to the sky. Lily started to spray water up high. Her friend, Tom, saw her and asked, "What are you doing, Lily?" She said, "I am trying to spray water up to the sky. I want to make it more pretty!" Tom thought it was a fun idea, so he joined her. They sprayed water up to the sky together. Suddenly, something unexpected happened! The water turned into delicious candy as it fell back down. Lily and Tom were so happy and surprised. They laughed and ate the candy together, enjoying their fun day under the sky.

Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there lived a wise old owl. The owl liked to store all kinds of wisdom in his big head. He knew many things about the world, and he loved to help others learn too. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to the wise owl. She wanted to learn something new. The owl was happy to help. He shared some of his wisdom with Lucy. He told her about the sun, the moon, and the stars. Lucy listened carefully and learned a lot. Lucy went back to her lively town and shared the wisdom the owl had given her. The people in the town were happy to learn new things too. They thanked Lucy and the wise owl for sharing their wisdom. And from that day on, everyone in the town became a little bit wiser.
