Once upon a time there was a young girl named Sarah. She was very excited because her parents had bought her an expensive mixer. She had wanted a mixer for a long time and so was very happy! Sarah unpacked the mixer and was amazed by how shiny and new it looked. She wanted to try it out right away, so she rushed to the kitchen and quickly found some food. She filled the mixer with some fruits and vegetables and switched it on. The mixer immediately began to whirr and spin and very soon it had turned all the food into a delicious smoothie! Sarah was delighted at the results and wanted to show off her work. She went to the living room and put the smoothie on display for everyone to see. Everyone in the family was so impressed by Sarah's concoction. They said she had done a great job with the mixer and praised her for being so creative. Sarah felt so proud of herself and was so glad her parents had bought her the expensive mixer.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was an intelligent dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to help people. He was very good at it. One day, he saw a little girl named Sally. Sally was trying to sit on a seat, but it was too high for her. Buddy wanted to prevent Sally from falling. So, he ran to her and put his back under the seat. Now, the seat was not too high for Sally. She could sit on it without any trouble. Sally was very happy and said, "Thank you, Buddy!" From that day on, Buddy and Sally became best friends. They played together and helped each other. Everyone in the town loved Buddy for being such a helpful and intelligent dog. The end.

One day, a boy named Tom found a big raft by the river. He wanted to go on an adventure to the unknown place across the water. Tom asked his friend, Sue, to go with him. "Let's go on the raft and see what's on the other side," said Tom. Sue was scared, but she wanted to be brave. "Okay, let's go," she said. They got on the raft and started to push it into the water. As they floated across the river, they saw a big bird in the sky. The bird did not like them on the raft. It tried to push them back to land. Tom and Sue were scared, but they did not want to give up. They clapped their hands to scare the bird away. The bird heard the loud claps and flew away. Tom and Sue were happy that they won. They reached the unknown place and it was beautiful. They played and had fun all day, and then they went back home on the raft.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was ignorant, which means he did not know many things. One day, Tim wanted to change his snack. He wanted to make it hot. Tim saw the stove and thought, "I can make my snack hot!" So, Tim put his snack on the stove. The stove was very hot. He did not know that the stove could be dangerous. Tim's mom came into the kitchen and said, "Tim, no! The stove is too hot for you!" But it was too late. The snack was burned and Tim was sad. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a wise old herb in the garden. All the other plants loved to talk to the wise herb because it knew many things. The wise herb told the plants to be kind and share with their friends. One day, a small bug came to the garden. The bug was hungry and wanted to eat the plants. The wise herb saw the bug and talked to it. The herb told the bug about being kind and sharing. The bug listened and learned from the wise herb. The next day, the plants saw the bug again. But this time, the bug did not want to eat them. The bug wanted to help the plants grow by giving them water. The plants were happy and thanked the wise herb for talking to the bug. The wise herb smiled and said, "Always remember to be kind and share with others."
