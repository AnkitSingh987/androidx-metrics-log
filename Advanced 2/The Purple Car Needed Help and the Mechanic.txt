One day, a purple car needed help. The car went to the mechanic. The mechanic said, "I can fix your car." The purple car and the mechanic had a talk. The car said, "I want to go fast." The mechanic said, "No, you should go slow." They disagree. In the end, the purple car and the mechanic became friends. They agreed to go not too fast and not too slow. The purple car was happy, and the mechanic was happy too.

Once upon a time, a big cat named Tom lay in the sun. He was very happy. Tom had a heart full of love for his friends. He liked to play with them all day. One day, Tom met a small mouse named Tim. Tim was very scared because Tom was big. But Tom did not want to hurt Tim. He wanted to be friends. So, Tom gave Tim a tight hug to show his love. From that day on, Tom and Tim played together every day. They were very happy. All their friends saw how much they loved each other. And they all lived happily ever after.

Ben and Lily were playing in their room. They had many toys, but they wanted to try something new. They saw a big box in the corner. It was from a new chair that Mom had bought. "Let's play with the box!" Ben said. "We can make it into a car or a house or a rocket!" "Okay!" Lily said. "But how do we fit in the box? It looks too small for both of us." Ben had an idea. He pushed the box to the middle of the room. He cut a hole in one side with his scissors. He told Lily to go inside the box. Then he cut another hole in the opposite side. "Now we can both fit in the box!" Ben said. "We can look out of the holes and pretend we are driving or flying or exploring!" Lily liked the idea. She crawled into the box and peeked out of the hole. She saw Ben's face smiling at her. She smiled back. "This is fun!" Lily said. "Where do you want to go?" Ben thought for a moment. He wanted to go somewhere exciting and different. He looked around the room and saw a poster of a dinosaur on the wall. "Let's go to the land of dinosaurs!" Ben said. "We can see big and scary and friendly ones!" "Wow!" Lily said. "That sounds cool! But how do we get there?" Ben had another idea. He grabbed a stick and pretended it was a steering wheel. He told Lily to hold on tight. He turned the stick left and right, making vroom and beep sounds. "We are going to the land of dinosaurs!" Ben said. "Hold on, Lily, it's going to be a bumpy ride!"

Once upon a time, there was a small town. In this town, there was a big radio. People loved to listen to the radio. One day, the radio stopped working. Everyone was worried. A kind girl named Lucy had an idea. She said, "We can unite and fix the radio!" So, all the people worked together. They looked for the problem. They found a little bird inside the radio. The bird was lost. The people helped the bird find its way home. The bird was happy. Then, the radio started working again. The people were surprised and happy. They learned that when they unite, they can solve any problem. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once Upon A Time, there was a little girl who loved to act. Every day, she would pick out a box from her toy shelf, and imagine she was some sort of great hero. She would go on grand adventures and make up stories as she went. But one day, the little girl found a box that was different from all the others. She had a great feeling of excitement when she opened it. Inside, she found a costume, some props, and a big note that said, "Be the great hero!" So the little girl acted out her greatest adventure ever. She imagined fighting dragons and destroying monsters, and being the bravest person she could be. She even made funny sounds and funny faces while pretending. When it was all done, the little girl was so proud of herself. She had acted great, and she was very happy. And every now and then, she would still take that special box down from her shelf and remember her grand adventure.
