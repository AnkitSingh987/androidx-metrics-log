One day, a bear named Bob saw a hive up in a tree. The hive was full of sweet honey. Bob thought it would be easy to steal the honey. So, he climbed the tree to get to the hive. As Bob got closer to the hive, he saw a bee named Buzz. Buzz said, "Please don't steal our honey, Bob. We worked very hard to make it." Bob felt sad for the bees and did not want to take their honey anymore. Bob climbed down from the tree and said, "I am sorry, Buzz. I will not steal your honey." Buzz was happy and thanked Bob. From that day on, Bob and Buzz became good friends, and they always shared the honey.

Once upon a time, there was a friendly cat named Tom. Tom lived in a little house with his best friend, Sam. One day, Tom and Sam wanted to relax and have fun. Tom said, "Let's play hide and seek!" Sam agreed and started to count. Tom ran and hid inside a cabinet. He was very quiet and waited for Sam to find him. Sam looked everywhere and finally found Tom in the cabinet. "I found you, Tom!" Sam said with a big smile. They both laughed and continued to play. After playing, Tom and Sam relaxed together, happy to have such a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Lily was playing with her toys. She was very happy. Her mom was in the kitchen making food. Lily saw a fragile glass cup on the table. She wanted to play with it, but her mom said, "No, Lily. That is fragile. It can break." Lily was sad. She went to the living room and saw the television. She asked her mom, "Can I watch television, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful. Don't touch the television." Lily watched the television and laughed. She saw a funny cat on the screen. She wanted to touch the cat. Lily forgot what her mom said. She touched the television and it fell. The television broke and Lily was sad. Her mom was not happy.

Once upon a time, there was a messy little bird named Bobby. Bobby loved to play with his friends in the trees. Every day, they would fly around and play games. One morning, Bobby woke up early to see the sunrise. The sky was full of colors. It was a beautiful display. Bobby was so happy, he wanted to tell his friends. He flew to his friend, Sally the squirrel, and said, "Sally, come see the sunrise! It's so pretty!" Sally looked at the sky and smiled. She said, "Wow, Bobby! You're right. The sunrise is so nice. Thank you for showing it to me." They both sat and watched the sunrise together. Then, they went to play with their friends in the trees.

One day, a little yellow robot was walking in the park. The robot loved to wave at everyone it saw. It would say, "Hi, I am a yellow robot. Do you want to play?" A small boy saw the robot and said, "Hi, yellow robot! I want to play with you." They played catch with a red ball. They were having so much fun. The robot would wave at the boy when it was his turn to catch the ball. Then, something unexpected happened. The yellow robot's arm fell off while it was waving! The boy was surprised and said, "Oh no, yellow robot! Your arm fell off!" The robot looked sad and did not know what to do. But the boy had an idea. He found some tape and put the robot's arm back on. The robot was happy and said, "Thank you, little boy! Now I can wave again!" They continued to play catch and wave at each other, having a great day in the park.

Once there were two brothers. They both loved the great shore. Every day, they went to the shore and had lots of fun. One day, they saw something that made them stop and smile. On the shore were two small boats made out of paper. The boys knew what to do. They picked up the boats and carefully folded them in their hands. Then, they ran to the shore and sent the boats off on the water. The boats floated across the shore and the brothers watched with excited smiles. The boats journeyed all around the shore and the brothers couldn't believe what they saw. They saw a great big whale, a bright red lobster and all sorts of wonderful things in the water. Once it was time to go, the brothers picked up their boats, folded them carefully and put them away. From that day on, the brothers always remembered their great adventure on the shore.
