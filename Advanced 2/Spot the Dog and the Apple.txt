Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to bathe in the sun. One day, Spot saw a tall pole in the park. Spot thought, "I want to see what is on top of that pole!" Spot asked his friend, a big bird named Flap, to help him. Flap said, "Okay, Spot! Hold on to me, and I will fly you up to the top of the pole." Spot held on tight, and Flap flew him up. At the top of the pole, they found a sour apple. Spot and Flap were happy to find the sour apple. They shared it and ate it together. After they ate the apple, they played in the park all day. Spot knew that he and Flap were the best of friends. And from that day on, they always looked for new things to find and share.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim liked to study. He wanted to learn many things. But Tim was still ignorant about some things. One day, Tim found a big red yet. He did not know what a yet was. He thought it was a toy. So, he took it home to play with it. Tim played with the yet. But then, something unexpected happened. The yet opened up and a big angry bear came out. The bear was not happy to be in Tim's house. The bear chased Tim and he was very scared. In the end, Tim learned that he should not play with things he does not know about.

Once upon a time, there lived a wolf in the woods. He wanted to find a nice place to settle. So he wandered around looking for a cozy spot. One day, he discovered a hilarious tree. The tree was so funny - it had a big smile on its face! The wolf thought it was a perfect spot to settle. So he ran up to the tree and said, "Friend tree, can I settle here?" The tree smiled and replied, "Yes, of course!" The wolf was so excited. He quickly started making his home. He built a cozy den underneath the tree and snuggled up for a nap. From then on, the wolf settled in his new home in the funny tree. He was happy and content. He even made some new animal friends in the woods!

Once upon a time, there was a light swan named Lily. Lily lived in a pond near a park. She liked to swim with her friends and eat bread that children throw to her. One sunny day, a little boy named Tim came to the park with his mom. Tim saw Lily and her friends swimming in the pond. He wanted to play with them. Tim picked up a small piece of bread and throw it to Lily. "Here, Lily!" he said. Lily swam fast to catch the bread and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Every day, Tim and his mom came to the park to see Lily. They would throw bread and watch Lily and her friends eat and swim. Tim and Lily became best friends, and they all lived happily ever after.
