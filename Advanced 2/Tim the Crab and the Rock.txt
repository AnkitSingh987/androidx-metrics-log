Once upon a time, there was a little crab named Tim. Tim lived near the water with his family. They liked to play in the sand and swim in the sea. Tim was a happy crab, but sometimes he did silly things. One day, Tim saw a big rock. He thought it would be fun to climb up and see what was on top. Tim climbed and climbed, but when he got to the top, he found that it was very dry. Tim felt stupid for climbing up there. Tim wanted to go back to his family, so he climbed down from the rock. He was happy to be back in the wet sand with his family. They all laughed and played together. Tim learned that it's okay to do silly things, as long as he learns from them.

One day, Emma was playing outside in the park. Suddenly, she heard a loud noise and got scared. She started to feel really sad and started to cry. Then, a nurse heard her and quickly came over to see what had happened. The nurse asked Emma what was wrong. Emma said that her favorite teddy bear had disappeared. The nurse smiled and said: “Don’t be sad, Emma. Let’s look for it together.” The nurse and Emma searched for a long time, but they couldn’t find the teddy bear. Finally, the nurse said: “I’m so sorry, Emma. I’m afraid it’s gone forever.” Emma felt even more sad and started to cry even more. The nurse was so sorry and she gave Emma a big hug. After a few moments, Emma felt better. She looked up at the nurse and said: “Thank you.” The nurse smiled and said: “You’re very welcome, Emma.”

One day, a red jeep and a blue jeep were friends. They liked to play together. But one day, they had to separate. The red jeep had to go far away. The blue jeep was sad. The blue jeep was persistent. He said, "I will find you, red jeep!" So, the blue jeep went on a long trip to find the red jeep. He asked other cars, "Have you seen the red jeep?" They said, "No, we have not." Finally, the blue jeep found the red jeep. But there was a surprise! The red jeep was now green! The red jeep said, "I changed my color, but I am still your friend." The blue jeep was happy, and they played together again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had an adorable pet cat named Kitty. They loved to play together. One day, Sue and Kitty were playing a game. The game was to see who could find the most toys. Sue wanted to win and have victory. Sue looked and looked for toys. She found one toy under the bed, and another toy behind the door. Kitty looked too, but she could not find any toys. Sue felt sad for Kitty, so she tried to help Kitty find more toys. They looked together, but they still could not find any more toys. Sue wanted to restore the fun in their game, so she decided to share her toys with Kitty. But when they went back to play, they found that all the toys were gone! Sue and Kitty were very sad. They never found out who took the toys, and they never had victory in their game.

Once upon a time, in an ancient tree, there lived a little squirrel. The squirrel loved peanuts more than anything. Every day, he would find peanuts and hide them in his tree. One day, the squirrel found a very big peanut. He was so happy! He wanted to hide the peanut in his tree, but it was too big. So, he put the peanut on the ground and went to find a friend to help him. The squirrel found his friend, the rabbit. He reminded the rabbit about the big peanut and they both ran back to the tree. But when they got there, the peanut was gone! They looked around and saw a tiny mouse with the peanut. The mouse had found a way to carry the big peanut all by himself! The squirrel and the rabbit were surprised. They didn't know that the tiny mouse could do that. The mouse shared the big peanut with the squirrel and the rabbit. They all became friends and learned that even the smallest friend can do big things.
