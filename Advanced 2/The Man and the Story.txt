Once there was a man who was always very busy. He never took the time to stop and enjoy what was around him. One day, he was walking through the darkness when he saw something shiny. He shouted, "Please can I have it?" It turned out to be coal and he was thrilled. He slipped the coal into his pocket and kept walking. He was so happy to finally have something to call his own. As he went further, he found more coal and he kept collecting it. Finally, he stopped and looked at what he had. He realized that his pockets were full and weighed down. No matter how much coal he had found, it wasn't enough. He felt sad and disappointed. The moral of this story is to never be greedy and always be thankful for what you have. If the man had stopped and been content with the coal he had, he would have been happier in the end. Greed can only lead to darkness, so please be content with what you have.

Once upon a time, a kind crocodile lived in a big pond. He had many friends who played with him every day. They liked the crocodile because he was nice and shared his toys with them. One day, something strange happened. The crocodile woke up and saw that he was not in the pond. He was in a big forest! The crocodile was scared and missed his friends. He did not know how this could happen. But then, the kind crocodile met a big bird who could talk. The bird told the crocodile that he had moved him to the forest by accident. The bird said sorry and took the crocodile back to the pond. The crocodile was happy to be home with his friends again. From that day on, the crocodile and the bird became best friends, and they all played together in the pond.

One day, a pale dog named Bob went for a jog. It was a hot day, so he wore a hat. As he jogged, he saw a cat. The cat was sitting under a tree. The tree had big, green leaves. "Hi, cat," said Bob. "Why are you under the tree?" "I am hot," said the cat. "The tree helps me cool down." Bob thought that was a good idea. So, he stopped jogging and sat under the tree with the cat. They talked and laughed. They became good friends. After some time, the sun went down. The day was not hot anymore. Bob and the cat said goodbye. Bob went home, happy that he had made a new friend. He knew they would play together again soon.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the swamp near their house. They liked to splash in the water, catch frogs and make mud pies. One day, they saw a funny bird in the swamp. It had a long neck, a big beak and a lot of feathers. They wanted to see it closer. "Hello, bird!" Tom said. "What are you doing in the swamp?" The bird looked at them and said, "I am looking for food. I am very hungry." "Can we help you?" Lily asked. "We have some bread and cheese in our bag." The bird nodded and said, "Yes, please. Come closer and give me some." Tom and Lily walked towards the bird, holding their bag. They did not see the big teeth in the bird's beak. They did not know that the bird was not a bird, but a crocodile. The crocodile waited for them to come near, and then he snapped his jaws and ate them both. The swamp was quiet again. The crocodile was full and happy. He lay on the bank and dried his skin in the sun. He did not care that he had killed Tom and Lily. He only cared that he had found food. He was a bad crocodile.

Once upon a time, there was a polite girl named Amy. She loved to go to the market with her mom. One day, they went to the market to buy some food. At the market, Amy saw a man selling oysters. She had never seen oysters before. They looked interesting. Amy asked her mom if they could buy some oysters. Her mom said yes, so they bought some oysters. They took them home and had a fun time eating them. Amy was happy and polite, and she said thank you to her mom. And they lived happily ever after.
