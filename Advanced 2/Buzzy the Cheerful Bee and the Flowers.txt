Once upon a time, there was a cheerful bee named Buzzy. Buzzy loved to make honey. Every day, Buzzy would fly to flowers, take their sweet stuff, and make honey. One day, Buzzy met a big bear named Benny. Benny said, "I love honey too! Can I have some?" Buzzy said, "Yes, you can have some, but you must not take all my honey. I need it for my bee friends." Benny agreed and went to Buzzy's home to get honey. But Benny was hungry and did not listen to Buzzy. He ate all the honey! Buzzy was sad and needed to prevent this from happening again. Buzzy thought of a plan. He told Benny, "I will make more honey, but you must help me. You can carry me to more flowers, so I can make honey faster." Benny agreed, and they became friends. From that day on, Benny carried Buzzy to many flowers. Buzzy made lots of honey, and Benny only ate some, not all. They were happy, and Buzzy's bee friends had enough honey too.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a dark sky. He was sad because he wanted to play outside. As he looked out the window, he saw a bright light moving fast. "Wow!" Tim said, "Is that a comet?" Tim ran to his mom and said, "Mom, I saw a comet!" His mom smiled and said, "That's great, Tim! Comets don't come by very often." Tim asked, "Can we leave the house to see it better?" His mom said, "Yes, let's go." Outside, Tim and his mom looked at the comet. It was getting bigger and bigger. Then, something unexpected happened. The comet changed its shape and became a big spaceship! The spaceship landed near Tim and his mom. Out of the spaceship came a friendly alien. The alien said, "Hello! I'm here to play with you!" Tim and his mom were surprised, but happy to make a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to go to the park with her mom. One day, while they were walking, Lily saw a big lemon tree. She was very excited and wanted to pick a lemon. Lily said to her mom, "I want to get a lemon, please!" But her mom looked at the tree and felt fearful. "Be careful, Lily," her mom said. "The tree is very tall, and the lemons are high up." Lily listened to her mom and did not try to reach the lemons. They continued to walk and found a small lemon on the ground. Lily picked it up and smiled. She was happy to have a lemon, and her mom was glad that Lily was safe. They went home and made lemonade together, and it was the best lemonade they ever had.

One day, a little bug named Tim went on an adventurous trip. He put on his tiny dress and went outside. Tim was excited to see what he could find. As he walked, he met a big bug named Sam. Sam said, "Hey, I like your dress! But I don't think you should go on this trip. It's too scary for a little bug like you." Tim felt sad, but he wanted to be brave. He said, "I can do it! I want to see new things." Tim and Sam went on the trip together. They saw big trees and pretty flowers. But then, they saw a scary bird! The bird wanted to eat them. Tim and Sam were scared, but they worked together. They hid under a leaf until the bird flew away. Tim and Sam were happy they were safe. They went back home and told all their bug friends about their adventurous day.

Once upon a time, there was a smart dog named Spot. He loved his owner, a little girl named Sue. They played together all the time. One day, Spot saw a big ball in the yard. He knew it was not his, but he wanted to play with it. Spot waited for Sue to come home from school. When she came home, she saw the big ball too. Sue was very happy and played with the ball. Spot knew that the ball had an owner, but he did not know who it was. He felt sad because he knew the owner would be sad too. The next day, a little boy named Tim came to the yard. He was weeping because he lost his big ball. Spot saw Tim and knew he was the owner. He brought the ball to Tim, and Tim was very happy. Sue was proud of Spot for being so smart and helping Tim. They all played together, and everyone was happy.
