Once upon a time, in a lively little town, there was a big pizza party. All the kids were very excited to eat pizza. They laughed and played games while they waited for the pizza to come. When the pizza finally arrived, a naughty cat jumped on the table. The cat wanted to eat the pizza too! The kids didn't want the cat to eat their pizza. They needed to prevent the cat from getting the pizza. So, the kids made a plan. They gave the cat some cat food and played with it. The cat was happy with its own food and toys. The kids saved their pizza and enjoyed the party. They ate the pizza and played more games. The cat played with them too, and everyone had a great time.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a good boy, but sometimes he did things he should not do. One day, Tim saw a big spoon in the kitchen. He knew he should not touch it, but he wanted to play with it. In the same house, there was a fierce dog named Max. Max was a big dog who liked to bark and play. Tim knew that Max would get mad if he took the spoon, but he took it anyway. Tim played with the spoon, but he was scared that Max would find out. Later, Max saw the spoon in Tim's room. Max was very mad and barked at Tim. Tim knew he was in trouble. His mom heard the noise and came to see what was wrong. She saw the spoon and knew that Tim had done something bad. Tim's mom had to punish him, so she took the spoon away and told Tim he could not play outside today. Tim was sad, but he knew he should not have taken the spoon. From that day on, Tim learned to listen and never took things he should not touch again.

Once upon a time, there was a little bathtub that lived in a big house. The bathtub was very flexible. It could bend and stretch to fit all the toys and bubbles inside. Every day, it would welcome the children to play and have fun. One day, a new toy came to the house. It was a little rubber duck. The bathtub was so happy to welcome the duck to join the other toys. The duck liked the bathtub a lot because it was so flexible and could hold all the toys and bubbles. The children loved to play in the bathtub with the rubber duck and all the other toys. They laughed and splashed, making the bathtub happy. And every night, the bathtub would sleep, dreaming of more fun days to come.

Once upon a time there was a dog named Spot. He was a normal dog who lived with his family in a nice house. One day he was in the garden, enjoying the sun. Suddenly he heard a noise coming from the back of the house. It was like something was being added. Spot looked over to see what it was – he was surprised to find something shiny and new! It was a big, open box. Spot ran over to his owners and said, "Look! What is this new box?" His owners looked down and smiled. "It's a present for you, Spot!" they said. "It has lots of toys inside – would you like to add some?" Spot was so happy he could hardly believe it. He jumped in and started adding all the toys that he saw. He had so much fun – it was not a normal day for Spot!

Lily liked to play in her yard. She had a swing, a slide and a sandbox. She also had a lot of toys, like dolls, cars and balls. She was happy with her toys, but sometimes she wanted to try new ones. One day, she saw a big truck in the yard next door. It was red and shiny, and it made loud noises. Lily thought it was incredible. She wanted to play with it. She asked her mom if she could borrow it from the neighbor. Her mom said no. She said the truck was not a toy, but a tool. She said the neighbor needed it to fix his house. She said Lily could not borrow it, because it was not hers, and it was dangerous. Lily was sad. She did not understand why the truck was not a toy. She thought it looked like fun. She decided to wait until the neighbor was gone, and then sneak into his yard and borrow the truck. She waited and waited, until she saw the neighbor leave in his car. She ran to his yard and climbed into the truck. She pushed some buttons and pulled some levers. The truck started to move. It was very fast and loud. Lily was scared. She did not know how to stop it. The truck crashed into the fence, the tree and the mailbox. It made a big mess. The neighbor came back and saw what happened. He was very angry. He yelled at Lily and her mom. He said Lily was naughty and she had to pay for the damage. Lily was sorry. She realized she had made a big mistake. She learned that she should not borrow things without asking, and that some things are not toys. She apologized to the neighbor and her mom. She promised to never do it again. She went back to her yard and played with her own toys. She was happy with them, and she did not want to borrow anything else.
