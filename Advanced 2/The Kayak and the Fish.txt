Once upon a time, there was a small kayak. The kayak was blue and loved to go on the water. One day, the kayak wanted to find an unknown place. The kayak was very excited. The kayak met a big fish. The fish said, "I can help you weigh yourself." The kayak was happy. They weighed the kayak and found out it was not very heavy. The fish said, "Now you can go to the unknown place." The kayak went to the unknown place. It saw many new things. The kayak had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, the kayak went back home. The kayak told all its friends about the unknown place and the big fish. They all wanted to go there too.

Once upon a time, in a peaceful town, there was a big zipper. It was a very special zipper. All the people in the town loved the zipper. One day, a little girl named Anna came to see the zipper. She said, "Wow! This is a big zipper!" She wanted to touch it. But her mom said, "No, Anna. Don't touch the zipper." Anna didn't listen. She touched the zipper and pulled it down. The town started to shake. The peaceful town was not peaceful anymore. People were scared and said, "Oh no! What did Anna do?" The town went dark and the big zipper was gone. Everyone was sad. Anna learned to listen to her mom. But the peaceful town was never the same again. The end.

Lily and Ben were playing with bubbles in the park. They had a big bottle of soap and a wand with a hole. They dipped the wand in the soap and blew air through the hole. Out came many bubbles, big and small, shiny and colorful. "Look, Lily, I made a huge bubble!" Ben said, holding the wand high. He waved it gently and the bubble floated away. "Wow, Ben, that's amazing!" Lily said, clapping her hands. She tried to make a big bubble too, but hers popped as soon as she blew it. "Oh, no, why did it pop?" Ben smiled and said, "Maybe you blew too hard. You have to blow softly and slowly, like this." He showed Lily how to blow a graceful bubble that danced in the air. Lily nodded and tried again. This time, she blew softly and slowly, and a graceful bubble came out of her wand. She was so happy that she squealed with joy. "Yay, I did it, I did it!" She said, jumping up and down. She and Ben watched the bubble fly away, until it disappeared in the sky. They continued to play with bubbles, making different shapes and sizes, and having fun. They displayed their bubbles to each other and to the birds and the flowers. They felt like they were in a fairy tale.

Once upon a time, there was a big drop. Everything was getting wet and the ground was getting soggy. One thing that stayed dry was a hidden TV. The TV was in a place most people couldn't reach. It had been there for as long as anyone could remember. The drop kept going and going until one day all the wetness stopped. It was then that the people noticed the hidden TV. They all looked up and saw the TV in its secret place. It stayed perfectly dry! The people were amazed and even more excited when they pressed the power button and the TV suddenly came to life. The people were delighted and never forgot how the TV stayed safe during the big drop.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the park. She had a big bucket with her. Lucy wanted to play in the sand with her bucket. Lucy saw a furry cat near the sand. The cat was very cute and soft. Lucy started to make a big sand hill with her bucket. The furry cat watched her and wanted to help. The cat used its paws to push the sand into the bucket. They were having lots of fun together. Suddenly, the furry cat moved the sand and revealed a hidden toy. It was a shiny ball! Lucy and the cat were very happy. They played with the ball and the bucket all day. In the end, the cat was not a cat, but a magic friend who could turn into any animal.
