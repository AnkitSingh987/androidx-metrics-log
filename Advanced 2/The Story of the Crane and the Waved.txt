Once there was a little girl. She was very persistent. Every day, she watched for a special crane in the sky. It was her only wish to see the crane. One day, the crane flew by. The little girl was so happy! She waved at the crane and the crane waved back. The little girl loved the crane more than anything else. She was so excited when it flew by every day. She always waved at it and the crane always waved back. After a while, the crane stopped coming. The little girl was sad. But she kept being persistent. Every day she looked in the sky and waved, just in case the crane came back. Then one day--the crane was back! The little girl was so happy that the crane was here. She loved the crane very much and waved at it every day. And they became the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a broken radio. He loved to listen to music, but the radio did not work. He was sad. One day, Tim's friend, Sam, came to play. Sam saw the broken radio and said, "I can help you save your radio, Tim!" Tim was happy. They worked together to fix the broken radio. After some time, the radio was not broken anymore. Tim and Sam turned it on, and they heard music. They danced and laughed. "Thank you for helping me save my radio, Sam!" said Tim. Sam smiled and said, "You're welcome, Tim. Let's always help each other."

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She had a big folder with many pretty pictures inside. Amy loved her folder so much, she took it everywhere she went. One day, Amy went to the park with her mom. She played with her friends and had a lot of fun. But when it was time to go home, she left her folder on the bench. The folder felt sad and lonely. Later, a spoiled dog came to the park. He saw the folder and wanted to chew on it. Just as the dog was about to bite the folder, a kind boy saw what was happening. He ran fast and rescued the folder from the dog. The boy found Amy's name on the folder and took it back to her. Amy was so happy to have her folder back, and she thanked the boy for his help.

Once upon a time, there was a nice girl named Lucy. She liked to cook yummy food for her friends. One day, Lucy went to a class to learn how to cook even better. In the class, Lucy met a funny cat. The cat could talk! The cat said, "I want to cook too!" Lucy and the cat became good friends. They cooked together in the class and had a lot of fun. One day, the cat cooked a big cake. It was a magic cake! When Lucy and her friends ate the cake, they started to float in the air! They all laughed and had a great time flying around. The cat's magic cake made the day very special.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn boy named Tim. Tim did not like to eat anything but spaghetti. His mom tried to give him other food, but he would say no. Tim only wanted spaghetti all day, every day. One day, something funny happened. Tim's mom made spaghetti for dinner, but she put a new color on it. It was green! Tim looked at the green spaghetti and was not sure if he wanted to eat it. His mom told him it was a special kind of spaghetti. Tim was brave and tried the green spaghetti. He found out that he liked it! From that day on, Tim was not as stubborn about his food. He learned that trying new things can be fun and yummy. And that is the story of Tim and the green spaghetti.

One day, a little girl named Amy went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy a pastry. Amy saw a pastry that looked very yummy. It had pink icing and sprinkles on top. Amy asked her mom if they could buy it. Her mom said yes, and they went home. At home, Amy and her mom sat down to eat the pastry. But when they took a bite, it seemed very bad. The pastry tasted disgusting. Amy and her mom did not like it at all. They felt sad because they had been so excited to eat it. Amy and her mom threw the disgusting pastry in the trash. They were very upset. They had wasted their money on something that was not good. They decided that next time, they would be more careful when picking out a pastry to eat.
