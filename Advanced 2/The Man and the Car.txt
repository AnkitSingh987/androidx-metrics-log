Once upon a time there was a man. He was independent and he liked to do things his way. One day he decided to go for a drive. He drove his car to the top of a very big cliff. When he got to the top he decided to reverse his car down the cliff. He thought it looked like fun. But when he started to reverse, he got into trouble! His car got stuck in the middle of the cliff and he was so scared. He tried and tried to get his car out, but he couldn't do it. Then suddenly something magical happened. Out of nowhere, a big, white bird flew over the cliff and landed near the man's car. It was magical because the bird made the car move in reverse all by itself. Soon the car was safely at the bottom of the cliff. The man was so happy that he could be independent again. He thanked the big, white bird for helping him and then drove his car happily away.

Once upon a time, there was a store. In the store, there were many things. The store had a job to supply toys to kids. One day, a big toy bear was in the store. The bear was dead. The bear could not play or talk. A little girl came to the store. She saw the dead bear and felt sad. She wanted to play with the bear. She picked up the bear and gave it a big hug. Something unexpected happened. The bear came to life! The bear was not dead anymore. It could play and talk now. The little girl was so happy. They played together and had lots of fun. The store now had a new job. It supplied magic toys to kids. And they all lived happily ever after.

One day, a big black dog named Max went for a walk. He walked and walked until he saw a little boy. The boy was sad because he lost his toy. Max wanted to help, so he looked around. He saw a toy on the left side of the road. Max picked it up and gave it to the boy. The boy was happy and said, "Thank you, Max!" Max felt good that he could help. The boy's mom came and saw Max. She had a small box that could record sounds. She asked Max to say something. Max barked and the box recorded his bark. The mom played it back and Max was surprised to hear his own voice. He didn't know he could be recorded like that. The boy and his mom thanked Max again and went home. Max learned that it's good to help others, and that sometimes, you can learn new things about yourself.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Bob. Bob liked to stand on his back legs and dance. One day, he found a big folder on the floor. He was very happy and wanted to play with it. Bob stood on the folder and started to dance. But, suddenly, the folder opened up and Bob fell inside! He was very surprised and didn't know what to do. Inside the folder, there were many colorful papers. Then, something unexpected happened. The colorful papers turned into big, jolly birds! They helped Bob get out of the folder. The birds and Bob became friends. They danced and played together all day long. From that day on, Bob and the birds had lots of fun and were always happy.

Once there was a small green frog who wanted to stay in the wood forever. He hopped around looking for just the right spot, but he couldn't seem to find one. Everywhere he went, the wood seemed too crowded. One day he came across a small clearing. He decided this was the place. But then, he made a foolish mistake. He hopped too close to some birds and they flew away, making lots of noise. The small green frog knew he had made a mistake, but he was too scared to move. He jumped up into a bush and waited, hoping the birds wouldn't come back. Luckily, the birds didn't see him and he was able to stay in the wood. He stayed for many years, finding lots of tasty bugs to eat. The small green frog was very happy there, and he learned that being foolish doesn't always mean bad things happen. From that day on, he always reminded himself to stay careful!
