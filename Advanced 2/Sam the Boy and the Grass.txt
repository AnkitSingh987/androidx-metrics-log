Once there was a boy named Sam. He was happy and he liked to play all day. One day he went outside to play in his backyard. He saw a beautiful pistol lying in the grass. He couldn't believe his eyes. He asked his mom if he could pick it up. His Mom said yes but she looked very worried. Sam happily picked it up and admired it. He was so excited he ran to show it to his brother. But when his brother saw it he became very envious. He then said to Sam, "Why did you get the cool pistol and not me?!" Sam didn't know what to say. He then noticed the pistol was starting to rot. He told his brother that they better throw it away. His brother sadly agreed and they got rid of the pistol before it rotted away.

One day, a boy named Tim was very sleepy. He went to the haircut place with his mom. Tim sat on the big chair and the nice lady started to cut his hair. Tim was so sleepy, he fell asleep in the chair. The nice lady kept cutting his hair. When Tim woke up, he looked in the mirror and said, "I guess I was very sleepy!" But then, Tim saw something unexpected. He had a big, funny haircut! The nice lady made a mistake because Tim was sleeping. Tim's mom laughed, and Tim laughed too. They all thought it was a funny surprise.

One day, a little girl named Sara went to play in her backyard. She liked to run and jump in the soft green grass. The sun was warm and bright in the sky. Sara saw her friend, a little bird, sitting on a tree branch. The bird looked tired. "Why are you tired, bird?" Sara asked. The bird said, "I am tired because I tried to fly high, but I fell from the sky." Just then, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful balloon floated into the backyard. Sara and the bird looked at the balloon with big eyes. They had an idea. Sara tied the tired bird to the balloon. The balloon lifted the bird into the sky without falling. The bird was happy and not tired anymore. Sara and the bird played in the backyard all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Lucy. She liked to cook very much. Today, Lucy wanted to cook something special. She asked her mom for help and together they went to the store. At the store, they bought lots of nice ingredients. When they got home, they started to cook. First, they took out their pots and pans and then they stirred together the ingredients. They made a delicious cake. When the cake was done, Lucy was so excited. She put it on the table and gave her mom a big hug. She thanked her mom for helping her cook. Then, she put candles on the cake and took it to the post office. At the post office, Lucy gave her cake to the mailman. He was so happy to get the nice cake. He said he was very pleased to get a treat. Lucy smiled and said she was happy to have cooked the cake for him. The mailman gave Lucy a big hug and thanked her for the cake. Lucy said goodbye and skipped home. It was a fun day for Lucy and she was very proud of the cake she cooked.

Once upon a time there was a small kid named Bob. Bob lived with his Mom and Dad and he had a lot of toys to play with. One day, Mom and Dad decided to take Bob to the park. When they arrived at the park, Bob saw other kids playing and he wanted to join them. But he was a bit scared because they looked bigger and older than him. So, Dad came up with an idea. He said, "Let's play a game of tag. Bob, why don't you be It?" Bob liked the idea and quickly agreed. He started chasing the other kids around and he really enjoyed it. He even managed to tag a few of them. Soon, it was time for Bob to go home. As he was leaving, one of the kids said, "Hey Bob, you're so bossy. You made all the motions". All the other kids laughed and Bob felt proud. He had made all the decisions and managed to tag almost everyone. He smiled and it was time for him to go home. He had enjoyed playing at the park and made a lot of new friends.
