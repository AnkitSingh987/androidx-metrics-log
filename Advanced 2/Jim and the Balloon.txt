Jim was an ignorant boy, he could not understand much at all. He would just sit, watch and ignore the world around him. One day while he was lying in bed he heard a loud noise. It was so loud it made him jump. He looked around but he could not figure out where it was coming from. So he ran out to the yard and kept listening. Suddenly he heard it again - it was coming from the sky! He looked up and saw a bright colorful balloon coming his way. He was so excited that he waved his arms and laughed! The balloon flew closer and closer until it landed right in front of him. Jim was so surprised he couldn't believe it! He quickly grabbed the balloon, ran inside and showed it to his mom. His mom smiled and said, 'That is a very special watch. Now you have something to show the world you are an ignorant boy no longer!' Jim was so happy he hugged his mom and never let go of the balloon watch.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a thief. This thief liked to take things that did not belong to him. He would go out when it was dark and steal toys from the kids. The kids were very sad when they woke up and saw their toys were gone. One day, a big, loud dog came to the town. The dog was very good at finding things. The kids asked the dog to help them find the thief. The dog agreed and started to look for the thief. At night, the dog found the thief trying to take more toys. The dog barked very loud and the thief was frightened. The thief quit stealing toys and ran away. The kids got their toys back and were very happy. The loud dog stayed in the town to protect the kids and their toys. And the thief never came back.

Once upon a time, there was a little nosy dog named Spot. Spot loved to dig holes in the ground. One sunny day, Spot found a big hole in his yard. He was very excited and called his friend, Kitty the cat. "Kitty, come see the big hole I found!" said Spot. Kitty came running and looked at the hole too. They both wondered what could be inside. As they were looking, a nice bird flew down and said, "Hello, friends! I see you found my hole. I have a gift for you!" The bird gave Spot and Kitty a pretty ball to play with. They were so happy to receive the ball. Spot and Kitty played with the ball all day long, near the big hole. They thanked the bird for the gift and promised to be kind to all their friends. From that day on, Spot and Kitty had many fun adventures together. And they always remembered the big hole and the nice bird who gave them their favorite toy.

Once upon a time, there was a big turkey named Tom. Tom lived on a farm with many other animals. Tom was not happy because he had a dull, old seat in the barn. He wanted a new seat to sit on. One day, Tom saw a nice seat in the farmer's house. He said to his friend, the cow, "I want that seat. It is not dull like mine." The cow said, "But Tom, that seat is not for us. It is for the farmer." Tom did not listen to the cow. He went to the farmer's house to take the nice seat. The farmer saw Tom and said, "Tom, you cannot have my seat. It is for people, not for turkeys." Tom felt sad and went back to the barn. The other animals saw that Tom was sad. They all worked together to make his dull seat better. They put soft hay and colorful feathers on it. When Tom saw his new seat, he was very happy. He thanked his friends and learned that it is good to share and help each other.

Once upon a time, there was a lovely farmer. He had a big farm with many animals. He liked to take care of them and play with them. One day, the lovely farmer found a shiny rock. He wanted to make it even more shiny. So, he decided to polish it. He rubbed it and rubbed it until it was very shiny. After the rock was polished, the lovely farmer showed it to his animals. They all liked the shiny rock, and they were very happy. The lovely farmer and his animals lived happily ever after.
