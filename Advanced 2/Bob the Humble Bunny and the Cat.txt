Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a humble bunny named Bob. Bob loved to eat vegetables. One day, Bob found a big, green vegetable under a tree. Bob wanted to share the vegetable with his friend, Cat. He went to Cat's house and said, "I found a big vegetable! Let's eat it together!" Cat looked at the vegetable and thought for a moment. Then, Cat suggested, "Let's ask Dog to join us too!" Bob and Cat went to Dog's house and showed him the vegetable. Dog was very happy and said, "Yum! Let's eat it now!" But, when they tried to eat the vegetable, it was very hard. They could not bite it. They tried and tried, but the vegetable would not break. The friends were sad because they could not eat the big, green vegetable. In the end, they had to leave the vegetable under the tree and go home with empty tummies.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play at the park near the harbor. Tim's favorite thing to do was to swing on the swings. He thought it was incredible how high he could go. The harbor had big boats and lots of water. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to swing. He saw a new friend named Sarah. Sarah liked to swing too. They had fun swinging together. The boats in the harbor looked pretty as they played. After swinging, Tim and Sarah sat and looked at the boats. They talked about the incredible boats and the fun they had. They made a plan to come back to the park and swing again. They couldn't wait to have more fun at the park near the harbor.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little girl named Lucy. She had a big, soft cushion that she loved very much. Lucy would sit on the cushion every day to read her favorite books. One day, Lucy's friend Tom came over to play. He saw the cushion and thought it looked fun! Tom asked, "Can I sit on the cushion too?" Lucy smiled and said, "Yes, we can part it and both sit on it!" So, Lucy and Tom sat on the cushion and read books together. They had lots of fun and laughed all day. When it was time for Tom to go home, he said, "Thank you for sharing your cushion with me, Lucy!" Lucy smiled and said, "You're welcome! Come back soon!" Lucy's mom saw how well they had played together and was very happy. She made them some sweet cookies as a treat. Lucy and Tom enjoyed the cookies and promised to share the cushion again the next time they played together. And they lived happily ever after.

Tom and Lily were playing with their toys in the living room. Tom had a big truck and Lily had a doll. They were having fun until Tom saw something shiny on the floor. "Look, Lily, a screw!" Tom said, picking up the small metal piece. "I wonder where it came from." "Maybe from your truck," Lily said, looking at Tom's toy. "It has many screws on it." Tom examined his truck and saw a hole where a screw was missing. He tried to fit the screw back in, but it was too hard. He needed a tool to turn the screw. "Can I use your screwdriver, dad?" Tom asked, running to his father, who was reading a book on the couch. "Sure, son, but be careful with it. And mind your sister, don't hurt her with the screwdriver," his father said, handing him the tool. Tom thanked his father and ran back to his truck. He was excited to fix his toy. He put the screw in the hole and started to turn the screwdriver. "Tom, can I help you?" Lily asked, coming closer to him. "No, Lily, this is my truck and my screw. Go play with your doll," Tom said, pushing her away. Lily felt sad and angry. She didn't like Tom's attitude. She wanted to play with him and his truck. She looked around and saw something enormous on the shelf. It was a big red ball that their uncle had given them for Christmas. Lily had an idea. She climbed on a chair and reached for the ball. She held it with both hands and threw it at Tom's truck. "Hey, what are you doing?" Tom shouted, as the ball hit his truck and made it fall over. The screw and the screwdriver flew out of his hands. "That's what you get for being mean to me," Lily said, sticking out her tongue. Tom was furious. He got up and chased Lily around the living room. They started to yell and fight. Their father heard the noise and came to see what was going on. He saw the mess and the broken toys. He was not happy. "Tom, Lily, stop it right now!" he said, in a loud voice. "Why are you behaving like this? You should share your toys and play nicely. And you should not throw things or use tools without supervision. You could have hurt yourselves or damaged something." Tom and Lily stopped and looked at their father. They felt ashamed and sorry. They knew they had done wrong. "We're sorry, dad," they said, in a low voice. "Come here, both of you," their father said, opening his arms. "I love you, but you need to learn to respect each other and your things. Now, let's clean up this mess and see if we can fix your toys. And then, maybe we can play with the ball together. How does that sound?" Tom and Lily nodded and hugged their father. They apologized to each other and helped to tidy up the living room. They hoped their toys were not too damaged. They also hoped their father would still let them play with the ball. They learned a lesson that day: to mind their manners and their toys.
