Molly the Rabbit skipped through the woods on a sunny day. She had a mighty sight of all the tall trees and long, green grass. Molly stopped skipping and looked around. "Hmmm," she thought, "Where should I go next?". Suddenly, she heard a loud voice from behind her. It was her friend Little Bear. "Come Molly! Let's go jump in the river," Little Bear said. Molly smiled and said, "Yes! Let's go!". They both skipped to the river and jumped in. The water felt cool and mighty on their fur. Together, they saw all the sights in the woods as they splashed around in the river. At the end of the day, both Molly and Little Bear were very happy. With a great big skip, they both hopped out of the river and promised to come back and see all the sights the next day.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bunny named Daisy. Daisy loved to hop and play with her friends in the green grass. One day, Daisy found a big carrot in the ground. She wanted to share it with her friends. Daisy went to her friend, the bird, and insisted, "Let's share this big carrot!" But the bird said, "No, I like worms more." Daisy felt sad but hopped to her friend, the squirrel. She insisted, "Let's share this big carrot!" But the squirrel said, "No, I like nuts more." Daisy felt sadder but hopped to her friend, the mouse. She insisted, "Let's share this big carrot!" The mouse said, "Yes, I like carrots too!" Daisy and the mouse shared the big carrot and were very happy. They played and laughed together. The other friends saw them and felt sorry for not sharing. The moral of the story is that sharing with friends can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play outside in the mild sun. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a dog. The dog was big and had a long tail. Tim did not recognize the dog. The dog came close to Tim and started to play with him. Tim was very happy. But then, something unexpected happened! The dog changed into a cat! Tim was so surprised. He did not know that the dog was a magic pet. Now, Tim and the magic cat played together in the park every day. And they were very happy.

Molly was a furry cat. She was out for a walk on a cold winter day. She had some mittens on to keep her paws warm and fuzzy. Suddenly, she heard a strange sound coming from the other side of the park. Her ears perked up, and she was curious to see what the sound was. She slowly inched closer to the noise. "Hisssss!" A sharp sound greeted her. It was a snake! Molly was scared and backed away quickly. The snake, however, was not so easily deterred. It kept slithering closer and closer, making the same sharp sound as it moved. Molly knew she had to do something to stay safe. She quickly grabbed a stick and started poking at the snake. It soon became clear that the snake was not a fan of the stick. Molly kept poking and prodding the snake until it finally slithered away. Molly was relieved that she was able to stay safe. She continued on her walk with her warm and fuzzy mittens, feeling proud of herself.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a pink dress that she loved to wear. One day, she went for a walk in the park with her mom. As they walked, Sue saw something on the ground. She stumbled and picked it up. It was a diary! Sue was so happy to find it. She wanted to read the words inside, but she didn't know how to read yet. Sue took the diary home and showed it to her dad. He read the words to her. The diary was full of funny stories about a talking cat! Sue and her dad laughed and laughed. They didn't know that the cat who wrote the diary was hiding in their house, listening to them read.
