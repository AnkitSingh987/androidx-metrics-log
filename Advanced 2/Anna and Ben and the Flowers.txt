Anna and Ben were playing with their crayons and paper. They liked to draw animals and flowers and cars. But sometimes they made mistakes and wanted to change their drawings. They had one eraser to share, but it was very small and old. "Can I have the eraser?" Anna asked Ben. She wanted to erase a big blue circle that she did not like. "No, I need it first," Ben said. He wanted to erase a green line that he drew by accident. "But I asked first," Anna said. She was impatient and tried to grab the eraser from Ben's hand. "Hey, stop it!" Ben said. He was impatient too and pulled the eraser back. They both tugged and pulled the eraser until it broke in half. They looked at the pieces and felt sad. Now they had no eraser at all. "I'm sorry, Anna," Ben said. He gave her one piece of the eraser. "I'm sorry, Ben," Anna said. She gave him the other piece of the eraser. They tried to use the pieces, but they were too small and did not fit well on their crayons. They could not erase anything. "Maybe we can ask Mom for a new eraser," Anna said. "Maybe we can share it better next time," Ben said. They hugged and went to find Mom. They hoped she had a big and new eraser for them.

Once upon a time, there was a little frog named Fifi. Fifi loved to play in the wet pond with her friends. They would jump and splash all day long. The wet pond made Fifi's mouth feel cool and fresh. One day, Fifi's friend, a fish named Fin, mentioned a fun game. "Let's see who can jump the highest," said Fin. Fifi and her friends loved the idea. They all lined up near the edge of the wet pond, ready to jump. Fifi took a big breath and jumped as high as she could. Her mouth was wide open with a big smile. She won the game! All her friends clapped and cheered for her. They played the game over and over again, laughing and having fun in the wet pond.

Once, there was a big dog named Ben. Ben loved to play with his ball. He would throw it and catch it all day long. He liked to play by himself and did not want to share his ball with anyone. Ben was a selfish dog. One day, while Ben was playing, a little cat named Mia came to play with him. Mia tried to play with the ball, but Ben did not want to share. Mia felt sad and walked away. Ben was happy to have his ball all to himself. But then, something unexpected happened. As Ben was playing, he saw Mia dancing to a rhythm. Mia was having so much fun that Ben wanted to join her. Mia showed Ben how to dance and they danced together. Ben felt happy and complete. He realized that sharing and playing with friends was much more fun than being selfish. From that day on, Ben and Mia became best friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a big planet. It was very big and it belonged to everyone. It was filled with lots of things to explore and play with. One day, something very bad happened. The planet started to get very dizzy and shake. Everyone was scared, but they worked together to try and protect it. A group of brave people went to the planet and worked hard to make the planet feel better. All the animals and plants were happy to have them there, so they made the planet shine again. When the planet was safe, everyone cheered! They thanked the brave people for protecting the planet for them. From that day on, everyone worked together to keep the planet safe and clean. They wanted it to stay strong and healthy forever. The End.

Mom's friend was visiting and had a surprise for Lilly. Lilly was only three and very excited. Mom's friend had a knot for her. It was made of colourful strings. Lilly laughed and clapped her hands. "Can you tie it into a pretty bow?" asked Mom's friend. Lilly giggled and tried to tie the knot. She twisted and turned the strings but it wouldn't make a pretty bow. Mom's friend laughed and said, "Let me show you how." She tied the knot into a wonderful bow. "I can do it too," said Lilly, and Mom's friend teased her. "I bet you can't tie as good as me," she said, wiggling her eyebrows. Lilly stuck out her tongue and tried harder. After a few tries, she made a beautiful bow. Mom's friend was so surprised she gave Lilly a sour candy. Lilly loved it and smiled. She was happy she could make a knot into a pretty bow all by herself.
