Title: Anna and Ben and the Cat

Anna and Ben are going to visit their grandma by plane. They have to pack their luggage. Anna has a big pink bag. Ben has a small blue bag. "Anna, what are you putting in your bag?" Ben asks. "I am putting my books, my crayons, and my paper. I like to write stories and draw pictures," Anna says. "Can I see your stories?" Ben says. "Sure, here is one. It is about a fat cat who likes to eat fish," Anna says. She shows Ben her paper with words and drawings. "Wow, that is a good story. And a funny cat. Can you write one for me?" Ben says. "Okay, what do you want your story to be about?" Anna says. "Maybe about a brave dog who likes to play ball," Ben says. "Okay, I will write it for you. But first, we have to finish packing. We don't want to be late for the plane," Anna says. They put their books, crayons, and paper in their bags. They also put their clothes, their toys, and their snacks. They zip their bags and carry them to the car. Their mom and dad are waiting for them. "Are you ready to go, kids?" their mom says. "Yes, we are ready. We have our luggage and our stories," Anna says. "Great, let's go then. Grandma will be happy to see you and your stories," their dad says. They get in the car and drive to the airport. They are excited to fly in the plane and see their grandma. They hope she will like their stories too.

Once upon a time, there was a restless little bunny named Ben. Ben lived in a cozy home with his mom and dad. One day, Ben decided to go on an adventure. He hopped and hopped, far away from his home. While hopping, Ben met a big bear named Bob. Ben said, "Hi, I'm Ben. I'm on an adventure!" Bob replied, "Hi, Ben! I'm Bob. I'm restless too. Can I join you?" Ben was happy to have a friend and they both continued hopping together. As they hopped, the sky turned dark. Ben and Bob were scared and cold. They remembered their warm homes and wanted to go back. They hugged each other for comfort. Then, they found their way back home, where their moms and dads were waiting. They hugged their moms and dads, happy to be home again. From that day on, Ben and Bob went on many adventures together, but always returned home for a warm hug.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a hot day. The sun was shining brightly, and everyone was feeling very warm. All the people in the village were trying to find ways to stay cool. They were playing in the water, eating cold treats, and sitting under trees for shade. On this hot day, a little girl named Mia wanted to join her friends at the village pond. She put on her swimsuit and ran to the pond as fast as she could. When she got there, she saw her friends splashing and laughing in the water. Mia was so happy to join them and cool off on this hot day. After they played in the water, Mia and her friends sat under a big tree to eat some cold ice cream. They laughed and talked about their fun day in the village. As the sun began to set, the hot day turned into a cool evening. Mia and her friends said goodbye and went home, excited to play together again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a small, curious cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play and find new objects to play with. One day, as Kitty was walking, she saw a shiny object on the ground. Kitty was so excited and picked it up with her paw. Kitty went to her friend, Doggy, and showed him the shiny object. Doggy looked at the object and said, "Oh, Kitty, that doesn't belong to you. We should find who it belongs to." Kitty and Doggy went around asking their friends if the shiny object belonged to them. They asked Birdy, Froggy, and even Fishy, but no one knew who it belonged to. Finally, they asked Bunny, who said, "Yes, that's my shiny object! Thank you for finding it." Kitty and Doggy were happy that they helped their friend Bunny. They all played together with the shiny object and had a fun day.
