Once upon a time, there was a big navy boat. It was very strong and adventurous. The boat loved to go on long trips on the blue sea. The people on the boat were happy and liked to have fun. One sunny day, a little girl named Amy came to visit the navy boat. She was excited to see the big boat and meet the people. Amy saw a man wearing a big hat. He was the captain of the boat. The captain smiled and said, "Hi Amy! Welcome to our navy boat!" Amy looked around and saw a big wheel. She asked, "What is that?" The captain said, "That is the wheel we use to point the boat where we want to go." Amy wanted to try, so the captain let her hold the wheel. They pointed the boat to a pretty island. Together, they went to the island and had a fun day. They played games and found pretty shells. When the sun went down, they went back to the navy boat. Amy said, "Thank you for the adventurous day!" The captain smiled and said, "You're welcome! Come back soon!" From that day on, Amy loved the navy boat and the adventurous captain. She would always remember the fun day they had together.

Once upon a time in a big jungle, there lived a lion and a monkey. They were best friends and played together all day. One day, they had a quarrel. The lion said, "I am the king of the jungle!" The monkey said, "No, I am the king of the jungle!" They were both hurt by each other's words. They decided to stop being friends and went their separate ways. The lion went to the left side of the jungle, and the monkey went to the right side. One day, while they were both playing alone, they heard a loud noise. It was a big elephant who had fallen into a deep hole. The lion and the monkey saw each other and knew they had to work together to help the elephant. They forgot their quarrel and worked as a team. The lion used his strong paws to dig, and the monkey used his long tail to pull the elephant out. The elephant was so happy and thanked the lion and the monkey. The lion and the monkey realized that being friends was more important than being the king of the jungle. They hugged each other and promised to never quarrel again. From that day on, they were best friends forever and played together in the jungle.

One day, a boy named Tim had a toy bear named Bob. Tim loved to play with Bob all day. But one day, Bob got a big tear on his side. Tim felt guilty because he pulled Bob too hard. Tim's mom saw the tear and said, "We need to do an operation to fix Bob." Tim was scared but his mom insisted it would be okay. They went to the kitchen to find thread and needle. Tim's mom started the operation on Bob. She tried to sew the tear but it was too big. Tim watched and hoped that Bob would be okay. But, the tear was too big to fix. Tim's mom said, "I'm sorry, Tim. I can't fix Bob." Tim was very sad and cried. He wished he had been more careful with Bob. From that day, Tim learned to be gentle with his toys.

Once upon a time, there was a young girl called Sarah. She was three. One day, Sarah was looking around her house. In one corner, Sarah noticed something hidden! It was a faucet. Sarah had never seen it before and she was very curious. Sarah ran to find her mom. "Mom, mom! Look what I found!" she said. Her mother smiled and asked, "What is it, Sarah?" Sarah smiled and said, "It's a faucet, mom!" Her mother laughed and said, "Yes it is a faucet, but it has been hidden for a reason. Do not touch it." But Sarah didn't recognize that she must not touch it. She was so excited to find it. She reached out and touched it. Suddenly, water came shooting out of it! It flooded the house and the living room had to be rebuilt. Sadly, Sarah learned a lesson that day. She had not recognized that she must not touch the hidden faucet!
