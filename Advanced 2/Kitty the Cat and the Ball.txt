One day, a little cat named Kitty was playing by a window. She saw a big red ball outside. Kitty wanted to play with the ball, so she ran out the door. She was alert and excited. As Kitty played with the ball, a little boy named Timmy came by. Timmy saw Kitty and said, "Hi Kitty! Can I play with you and the ball?" Kitty was happy and they played together. They were having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The ball suddenly disappeared! Kitty and Timmy looked around, but they could not find it. They were sad. Then, they heard a noise by the window. It was Kitty's mom! She had the ball and said, "I found the ball behind the bushes. Now you can play again!" Kitty and Timmy were very happy and continued to play with the ball.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly man named Tom. Tom had a big, red tap in his yard. Every day, he would go outside and turn the tap to water his plants. The plants grew big and strong, and Tom was very happy. One day, Tom found a big, brown apple under a tree. The apple was not good to eat. It had started to rot. Tom did not want the apple to make his other plants sick. So, he picked it up and threw it far away from his yard. The next day, Tom went back to his tap and watered his plants again. They grew even bigger and stronger. Tom was very jolly, and he knew that his plants were happy too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a lawyer named Bob. He was a good man who helped people. One day, he had to deliver a letter to a big, scary house. Bob was scared, but he knew he had to be brave. As Bob walked to the scary house, he saw a little girl crying. She was scared too. Bob told her not to worry and that he would help her. The little girl said her cat was stuck in the scary house. Bob knew he had to help the girl and her cat. Bob and the girl went into the scary house. They found the cat and delivered the letter. The house was not scary anymore. They were all happy and went home. The moral of the story is to be brave and help others, even when you are scared.

Once upon a time, there was a nice little girl named Sarah. She loved to collect pretty roses. So one day, Sarah went outside to the garden and found the most beautiful rose that she had ever seen. Sarah was so excited that her eyes twinkled and she blinked with anticipation. She carefully plucked the rose from its stem and held it close to her heart. But then Sarah heard a distant voice! It said, "That rose looks delicious--may I have it please?" Sarah looked up and saw a large, hungry wolf standing nearby. The wolf's eyes blinked as he waited for an answer. Sarah was scared but brave. She said, "No, this is my rose." The wolf was angry. His eyes blinked twice as he said, "Give it to me now," and he stretched out his long claws towards Sarah. Sarah closed her eyes and tried to run away but it was too late. The wolf swooped down, grabbed the rose, and then ran away. When Sarah opened her eyes, the rose was gone. Sarah sadly returned home with no rose in her hands. She didn't understand why the wolf was so mean. The only thing she could do was blink back the tears.

One day, a cat named Max was very hungry. Max saw a stove in the kitchen. On the stove, there was a big, dirty pot. Max wanted to see what was inside the pot. He thought there might be food in it. So, Max started to stretch his body to reach the pot. Max's friend, a dog named Sam, saw Max stretching. Sam said, "Max, wait! The stove is hot. It can hurt you!" Max stopped stretching and listened to Sam. He did not want to get hurt. Sam was a good friend for helping Max. Just then, Mom came into the kitchen. She saw the dirty pot and cleaned it. Then, she put some yummy food in it for Max and Sam. They both ate and were happy. Max learned that it is important to be careful and listen to friends. And Sam learned that helping friends is a good thing to do.
