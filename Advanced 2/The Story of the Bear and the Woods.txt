Once upon a time, there was a big bear and a little bear. They both loved to eat yummy food. One day, they found a pot in the woods. The pot was full of yummy soup. The big bear and the little bear wanted to eat the yummy soup. They knew they should share the pot of soup. So, they sat down together and took turns eating from the pot. As they shared the yummy soup, they became very happy. They laughed and talked while they ate. Soon, the pot was empty. The big bear and the little bear smiled at each other, knowing they had shared something special.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play outside. One day, while playing, she saw a big, dangerous snake. She was scared and ran back to her house. Lily told her mom, "Mom, I saw a dangerous snake outside!" Her mom said, "Oh no! We must shut the door so the snake can't come in." They shut the door and felt safe inside their house. That night, Lily had a dream. In her dream, she was playing outside and the snake was her friend. They played together and had fun. When Lily woke up, she was not scared of the snake anymore, but she knew to be careful around dangerous things.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet little bunny named Bob. Bob loved to play all day. He would hop and run in the big green park. One day, Bob saw a red alarm near a big tree. He did not know what it was, but he wanted to touch it. Bob's mommy saw him near the alarm and said, "Stop, Bob! Do not touch the alarm. It is not for playing." Bob was sad but he listened to his mommy. He went back to playing with his friends in the park. Later, a loud noise came from the alarm. It was telling everyone that it was time to go home. Bob and his friends went back to their homes with their mommies and daddies. Bob was happy that he listened to his mommy. The moral of the story is to always listen to your mommy and daddy, because they know what is best for you.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play in the park. Today is a hot day, so they want to eat ice-cream. They go to the ice-cream truck and buy two cones. Lily chooses chocolate and Ben chooses vanilla. They sit on a bench and lick their ice-creams. They are happy and cool. But then a big dog comes. The dog is hungry and wants their ice-creams. He barks and jumps at them. Lily and Ben are scared and run away. "Help! Help!" they shout. "The dog is chasing us!" They see a trash can. They have an idea. They throw their ice-creams in the trash can. The dog stops and sniffs the trash can. He finds the ice-creams and eats them. He is happy and full. Lily and Ben are safe. They are sad that they lost their ice-creams, but they are glad that the dog is not angry anymore. "Let's go back to the ice-cream truck and buy new ones," Lily says. "OK," Ben says. "But this time, let's choose a different flavor. Maybe strawberry or mint." They hold hands and walk back to the ice-cream truck. They buy new ice-creams and enjoy them. They are happy and cool again. They think that throwing their ice-creams was easy and smart. They are good friends.

Once upon a time there was a grumpy little bunny. He lived in a dark forest and he was always unhappy and grumpy. One day the light came and the little bunny was happy. He saw the light and remembered how happy it made him feel. But then something sad happened. The light went away and the little bunny became grumpy again. He felt lost and alone until he remembered why he loved the light. The light reminded him of happy times spent with his friends, playing together in the sunshine. And then he knew that even if it was dark, he could still be happy. The little bunny taught us an important lesson. No matter how grumpy we may feel, we should remember to look for the light! That will remind us of all the wonderful things which make us truly happy.
