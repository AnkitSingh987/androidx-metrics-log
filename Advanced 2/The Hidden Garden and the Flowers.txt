Once upon a time, there was a hidden garden. In the garden, there was a big hose. The hose was very long and helped to give water to all the pretty flowers. One day, the hose had a little split. The water did not go to the flowers anymore. The flowers became sad and thirsty. A kind girl saw the split in the hose. She fixed it with some tape. The water started to flow again. The flowers were happy and thanked the girl. The hidden garden was pretty and full of life once more.

One day, a cute dog named Max was playing in the park. Max was very happy. He liked to run, jump, and play with his ball. Max saw a big tree and wanted to rest under it. He was very tired from all the fun. Under the tree, Max found a cup of juice. Max was thirsty, so he drank the juice. It was sweet and tasty. But then, Max started to feel guilty. He knew he should not have taken the juice. It was not his. Max heard a little girl named Lily. She was looking for her juice. Max wanted to say sorry. He walked to Lily and sneezed. The sneeze made Lily laugh. Max gave her a big, happy lick. Lily forgave Max, and they played together in the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to dance. One day, her mom took her to a dance class. The class was in a big room with a lot of kids. Lily was happy to be there. In the class, the kids danced to fun music. Lily met a new friend named Tim. Tim said, "I like to dance too! Do you want to dance with me?" Lily said, "Yes!" They danced together and had a lot of fun. The room was mild and nice. But then, something bad happened. Lily and Tim danced too fast and bumped into each other. They both fell down and got hurt. They cried and their moms came to help them. The dance class was over and they had to go home. Lily and Tim were sad because they could not dance anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was only three-years-old. One day, Lucy wanted to go on an adventure. She asked her mum if she could take her passport. Her mum said 'Yes' and gave her the passport. Lucy was very excited! She packed her bag and was ready to go. On her way, she stopped to cook some food. She was calm and focused. Suddenly, two mean thieves came and snatched her passport away! Lucy was very upset and started crying. The thieves were so mean! They took Lucy's passport and ran away. Lucy never got to go on her adventure. She was so sad.

Once upon a time, there was a panda named Pandy. Pandy loved to play in the light sun. He would roll and jump in the grass all day long. His best friend was a little bird named Chirpy. One day, Pandy and Chirpy found a big cup in the grass. The cup was full of water. Pandy had an idea. He wanted to pour the water on the flowers to help them grow. Chirpy thought it was a good idea too. So, Pandy and Chirpy worked together to pour the water on the flowers. The flowers grew big and pretty in the light sun. Pandy and Chirpy were very happy to see their work help the flowers. They played together in the beautiful garden all day long.

Once upon a time, there was a smart little bird named Billy. Billy lived in a big tree in the park. He had many friends who played with him every day. One day, a strong wind came and blew all the leaves off the trees. Billy's friends were scared and didn't know how to get home. But Billy was smart and knew what to do. Billy told his friends to follow him. They all flew together, and the wind helped them get home fast. When they reached their homes, they were very happy. They knew that having a smart friend like Billy was great.
