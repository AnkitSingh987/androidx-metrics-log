Once upon a time, there lived a gifted girl called Daisy. Daisy lived in a house full of flowers. Every month, Daisy's mommy would allow her to pick one special flower from the garden. One day, Daisy spotted a lovely pink flower she had never seen before. She couldn't wait to show her mommy. But her mommy said Daisy could not pick it. She said the flower was too special, that it was a magical flower and it was not allowed to be picked. Daisy felt sad but she accepted her mommy’s decision. That night, Daisy wished for the magical flower to grant her one special wish. The next day, when Daisy walked into her garden, she was surprised to see that the magical pink flower had grown really big! Daisy was amazed and happily picked the flower to use it to make a special gift. When Daisy presented the gift to her mommy, her mommy was so happy. The magical flower had granted Daisy's wish and allowed her to make her mommy the perfect gift.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He did not like to eat beef. His mom would cook it, but he thought it was dull. One day, his mom made a new dish for dinner. It was a big surprise. Tim sat down to eat. He took a bite and it tasted very good. He asked his mom, "What is this?" His mom smiled and said, "It is a special meal just for you!" Tim ate and ate. He was very happy. After he finished eating, his mom told him the surprise. "Guess what, Tim? You ate beef and you liked it!" Tim was shocked. He never thought he would like beef, but his mom made it taste good. From that day on, Tim learned to try new things, even if he thought they might be dull.

Once, there was a boy named Tim. He liked to play outside. One day, he saw some light smoke near a big tree. Tim was curious, so he walked to the tree. As he got closer, he saw a little cat. The cat was scared and said, "Help me, please! I am stuck in the tree, and the smoke is making me cough." Tim wanted to help the cat, but he was too small to reach it. Then, Tim had an idea. He found a big seat near the tree. He pushed the seat under the cat. The cat jumped down onto the seat and was safe. Tim and the cat were happy, and they became good friends. The smoke went away, and they played together all day.

One day, a sleepy cat named Tom went for a walk. He walked down the hall of his house. Tom saw a big box. He was curious about what was inside the box. Tom asked his friend, Sam the dog, "What is in the box?" Sam said, "I don't know, let's weigh it." They tried to weigh the box, but it was too heavy for them. Tom and Sam asked their friend, Liz the bird, for help. Liz was very strong for a small bird. Together, they weighed the box. Inside, they found a big, soft bed for Tom. Tom was so happy and said, "Thank you, friends!" Tom took a long nap on his new bed, and he was not sleepy anymore.

One day, Mike and Amy were looking for something to do. Suddenly, Mike had an idea. "Let's go catch butterflies!" he said. "Yes!" said Amy, and the two of them went outside. They found a big group of butterflies hidden in the garden. Mike and Amy both ran as fast as they could to catch as many as possible. They both giggled as the butterflies flew around them. After a few minutes, they both had managed to catch several! Mike and Amy laughed as they showed each other the beautiful colours of the butterflies they had caught. They were so happy that they had found this hidden group of wonders!

Once upon a time, there was a pilot named Tom. He had a wide hat that he wore every day. Tom loved to fly his big plane in the sky. One day, Tom wanted to count how many times he could fly around the big tree. He started his plane and went up, up, up into the sky. He flew around the tree one, two, three times. Tom was very happy. He flew his plane back down to the ground. He told all his friends that he flew around the big tree three times. They all clapped and cheered for Tom, the pilot with the wide hat.
