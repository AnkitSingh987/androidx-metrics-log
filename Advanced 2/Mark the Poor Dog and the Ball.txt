Once upon a time, there was a poor dog named Mark. Mark lived in a small house with a nice family. They loved Mark, but they did not have a lot of things. Mark was happy, but he was always very hot in the summer. One day, the family got a cooler. The cooler was big and blue. They put cold things in the cooler, like ice and cold drinks. Mark liked to sit next to the cooler because it made him feel cooler too. Mark was a smart dog. He found a way to mark the cooler as his own. He took a red ball and put it on top of the cooler. Now, everyone knew that the cooler was Mark's special spot. Mark and his family were very happy, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there lived a brave boy named Tim. He loved to help his mom and dad with their work. One day, his mom asked him to organize the food in the kitchen. Tim saw some beef on the table. He thought it would be a good idea to put it in the big cold box. He put the beef in the cold box and felt happy. He knew his mom would be proud of him. The next day, Tim's mom made a yummy dinner with the beef. She thanked Tim for being brave and helpful. Tim felt very proud of himself and knew he had done a good job organizing the kitchen.

One day, a girl named Lily wanted to paint a model. The model was high up on a shelf. Lily asked her big brother Tom for help. "Tom, can you help me get the model? It is too high for me," said Lily. Tom got the model for Lily, but he wanted to paint it too. They both wanted to paint the model, but there was only one paintbrush. "I want to paint it first," said Lily. "No, I want to paint it first," said Tom. They were not happy. Then, something unexpected happened. Their dog, Spot, came into the room and took the paintbrush! Spot started to paint the model with his tail. Lily and Tom laughed. They decided to let Spot paint the model, and they would watch. Now, everyone was happy.

One day, a little girl was walking outside with her mom when she saw a leak. It was coming from a sour orange tree nearby and was running all over the ground. The little girl decided to follow the leak. Her mom stopped walking and said, “What are you doing? That leak doesn’t look safe.” The little girl smiled and said, “I’m just gonna follow it, Mommy. I wanna see where it goes.” Her mom shook her head and said, “Okay, but be careful and don’t get too close.” So the little girl followed the leak. It went around the corner, down the street, and then crossed over a bridge. She followed it all the way to a park. The little girl was amazed. She had never been to the park before, and it was full of fun things to do. She watched the ducks swimming in the pond and explored the paths in the woods. The leak seemed to have disappeared, but the little girl was happy that she followed it, because she had discovered a whole new world. She couldn't wait to come back!

Once upon a time, there was a bright little ghost. He lived in a big, old house. The ghost liked to play with his friends, the cat and the dog. They had lots of fun together. One day, the ghost wanted to buy a toy. He went to the toy store with his friends, the cat and the dog. They saw many fun toys. The ghost picked a red ball. The cat picked a soft mouse. The dog picked a big bone. They took their toys home to play. The bright little ghost was happy. His friends, the cat and the dog, were happy too. They played with their new toys all day long. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous dog named Bobo. Everyone liked Bobo because he was very nice and very good at tricks. Bobo had a best friend named Timmy. Timmy was a little boy who loved to play with Bobo every day after school. One day, Timmy's mom said they were going away for a week. Timmy was sad because he would miss Bobo. So, Timmy told Bobo that they would not see each other for a week. Bobo was sad too, but he had an idea. He insisted that Timmy take his favorite toy, a red ball, with him on the trip. Timmy liked this idea and took the red ball with him. Every day, when Timmy played with the red ball, he thought of Bobo and felt happy. And when the week was over, Timmy and his family came back home. Timmy and Bobo played together again, and they were both very happy. They knew that even when they were apart, they could still feel close by thinking of each other.
