Once upon a time, there was a healthy horse named Harry. Harry loved to pull a big red wagon. He liked to help people by carrying their things in his wagon. One sunny day, Harry met a little boy named Tommy. Tommy had a toy sword and a cape. He was playing pretend. "I am a hero," said Tommy. "Will you help me save the day?" Harry nodded his head and let Tommy climb on his back. Together, they went on an adventure. They found a big tree with a sign that said "surrender." Tommy told the tree, "We will never give up!" Then, they rode around the tree and went back home. Harry and Tommy were very happy. They had saved the day.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to a garden. She saw a pretty rose. Lily wanted to take the rose home. But her mom said, "Stay polite and don't pick the rose." Lily was sad but she listened to her mom. She did not pick the rose. Then, a big wind came. The wind wanted to take the rose away. Lily did not want the rose to go away. She asked her mom, "Can I help the rose?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can." So, Lily stayed by the rose. She held the rose and the wind did not take it. The rose was safe. Lily was happy and her mom was proud.

One day, a big cat named Tom went for a walk. He took a step in the grass and saw a tiny bug. The bug was an inch long and had a silly smile. Tom was curious about the bug and wanted to talk to it. "Hi there, little bug," said Tom. "Why do you have a silly smile on your face?" The bug giggled and replied, "I am happy because I can dance! Do you want to see me dance?" Tom nodded his head and the bug started to dance. It took tiny steps and twirled around. Tom laughed and clapped his paws. He thought the bug was very funny. After the dance, Tom and the bug became friends. They played together every day. Tom learned that even the smallest creatures can bring big joy. And the bug was happy to have a new friend who liked its silly dance.

Once upon a time, there was a big, dependable dog named Buddy. Buddy liked to gather sticks and play in the yard. One sunny day, Buddy found a tunnel in the woods. Buddy called his friend, Kitty. "Hey, Kitty! Come see the tunnel I found!" Kitty came running, excited to see the tunnel. They both looked inside, but it was very dark. They were scared but wanted to know what was inside. As they went into the tunnel, they heard a loud noise. Suddenly, the tunnel began to shake! They ran out as fast as they could, but the tunnel fell down. Buddy and Kitty were safe, but now they knew that not all tunnels were fun to play in.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim liked to play with his toy cars on the road near his house. One day, he saw a new road that he had never seen before. It seemed like a fun place to play. Tim took his toy cars to the new road. He saw many other kids playing there too. They all had fun playing with their toy cars on the road. Tim looked around and saw a big toy car. It was the only one available for him to play with. Tim played with the big toy car on the road. He was very happy. The other kids liked the big toy car too. They all played together and became friends. From that day on, Tim and his friends always played on the new road with their toy cars.

Once upon a time there was a mighty little bear who was loved by everyone in his neighborhood. One day, he was walking through the park with his scarf on when he saw a group of children playing. He stopped to watch them and smiled. Suddenly, one of the children spotted him and called out, "Hey! Come and play with us!" He was so happy he couldn't resist and quickly joined the game. Together, they ran and laughed and played for a long time. They had so much fun together! At the end of the day, the little bear hugged each one of the children. With a big smile, he waved goodbye and ran home with his scarf flying behind him. From that day on, everyone knew that the mighty little bear could always make them smile.
