Once upon a time, there was a little motor. The motor was very flexible. It could move in many ways. It liked to help people a lot. It was a good motor. One day, the motor met a crab. The crab was sad. It could not find its toy. The motor wanted to help the crab. They looked for the toy together. They were a good team. As they looked, the motor saw the toy stuck in a tree. The motor used its flexible arm to pinch the toy. The crab was happy. They played together all day. They became best friends.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy nurse. She worked in a big house where she took care of many kids. The kids did not like the nurse because she was always sad and angry. One day, a little girl named Mia came to the big house. Mia was always happy and liked to help everyone. She saw the grumpy nurse and wanted to make her smile. Mia thought of a plan to remind the nurse how to be happy. Mia played with the other kids and laughed a lot. She shared her toys and gave the nurse a big hug every day. Soon, the grumpy nurse started to smile more and more. Mia reminded her that being happy is easy when you help others and share love.

One day, a big dog named Max went to the city with his family. Max saw a big building with an elevator. He did not understand what an elevator was. His family wanted to go up in the elevator to see the city from the top. Max said, "What is this big box? I don't understand." His mom said, "Max, this is an elevator. It will take us up to the top of the building." Max was scared, but he wanted to be brave. When they got inside the elevator, Max saw a big red button. He thought it was a toy, so he pressed it. The elevator stopped! Max and his family were stuck. They were scared, but then they heard a voice. It was a friendly man who said, "Don't worry, I will help you." The man fixed the elevator, and they all went to the top of the building. Max learned that the elevator was not scary, and he was happy he tried something new.

One day, a small cricket went to a big tree. He saw a big bird on a branch. The cricket was scared, but he had a request. He wanted the bird to be his friend. "Hello, big bird," said the small cricket. "Can we be friends? I have a request for you." The big bird looked down at the small cricket and said, "Okay, we can be friends. What do you want?" The small cricket was so happy. "I want to play a game with you. Let's see who can jump the highest," said the cricket. The big bird agreed to play the game. They took turns jumping. The small cricket jumped high, but the big bird jumped even higher. Then, something unexpected happened. When the big bird jumped very high, he saw a lot of small crickets on the ground. The big bird was hungry. He forgot about being friends and ate all the small crickets. The small cricket who had made the request was very sad. He learned that not everyone can be a friend.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a big, old house with a secret attic. The attic was a fun place to play. Tim loved to play there with his toys. One day, Tim found a small, tough box in the attic. He opened the box and saw a magic cup inside. The cup was very special. Tim's mom said, "That cup can make any drink you want. Just say the drink's name, and it will fill up." Tim was very excited. He held the cup and said, "Juice!" And just like that, the cup was full of juice. Tim took a big drink and smiled. The juice tasted so good. From that day on, Tim and his friends loved to play in the attic. They would take turns asking the magic cup for their favorite drinks. The attic became the most fun place to be, and they all lived happily ever after.
