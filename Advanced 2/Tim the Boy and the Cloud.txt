One day, a little boy named Tim saw an aeroplane in the sky. The aeroplane was big and shiny. Tim liked to watch the aeroplane fly high in the sky. He wished he could fly too. As Tim watched, the aeroplane went behind a big cloud. The aeroplane did not come out from the other side of the cloud. Tim was sad because the aeroplane disappeared. He wanted to find the aeroplane and make sure it was safe. Tim asked his mom to help him find the aeroplane. They walked around the park, looking up at the sky. Then, the big cloud moved, and the aeroplane came out. Tim was happy to see the aeroplane again. He knew it was safe and could keep flying in the sky.

Once upon a time there was a little boy called Tom. Tom had a purple sweater and loved it very much. One day, Tom wanted to make a print of his sweater so he could show his friends. Tom asked his mum if he could have a print of the sweater. Mum said, “Yes, Tom. You can have a print of the purple sweater.” Tom hurried off to the print shop and asked for a copy of his sweater. The shopkeeper smiled and said, “Of course, Tom! That purple sweater is lovely.” Tom was very happy and soon he had a special purple print of his sweater. He rushed home to show off his new print and show all of his friends. Tom’s mum was very proud of him and said, “Well done, Tom! You have a special purple print of your sweater.”

Once upon a time, there was a big jug. The jug was very pretty and expensive. It lived in a nice house with a kind family. The family took very good care of the jug because they loved it. One day, a little cat came into the house. The cat saw the jug and wanted to play. The cat jumped and hit the jug. The jug started to tremble, shaking back and forth. The cat was scared and ran away. The family heard the noise and came to see what happened. They saw the trembling jug and held it to make it stop. They were happy the jug did not break. The family put the jug in a safer place, so the cat could not reach it again. The cat learned not to play with the expensive jug, and they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a young bird named Billy. Billy lived in a cage in a big house. He was a very happy bird. One day, a little girl named Lucy came to visit the house. She saw Billy in his cage and said, "Hi, bird! I want to play with you." Billy was excited and wanted to play too. Lucy opened the cage and took Billy out. They played together and had lots of fun. Then, Lucy wanted to make a cake. She let Billy help her mix the cake batter. They mixed and mixed until the cake was ready to bake. After the cake was done, they shared it and became very good friends.

One day, a little girl named Mia found a brilliant ticket on the ground. It was shiny and pretty. She picked it up and showed it to her friend, Tom. They were very happy. Mia and Tom wanted to find out what the ticket was for. They walked to a big tree and saw a squirrel. Mia whispered to the squirrel, "Do you know what this ticket is for?" The squirrel looked at the ticket and whispered back, "It is for a fun party." Mia and Tom went to the party with the brilliant ticket. They played games, ate cake, and had a great time. At the end of the party, they thanked the squirrel for helping them. They went home with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a shark and a fish were friends. They liked to talk and play together. The shark was big and the fish was small. The shark was very creative. He made fun games for them to play in the water. One day, they saw a big ship. The shark had an idea. He told the fish, "Let's make the ship think we are pirates!" The fish thought it was a fun game. They put on pirate hats and started to talk like pirates. "Arrr, matey!" they said. The ship saw the shark and the fish. The people on the ship got scared. They thought the shark and the fish were real pirates! They gave the shark and the fish their treasure. The shark and the fish were surprised. They did not want the treasure, they just wanted to play. They gave the treasure back and said sorry. The people on the ship laughed and said it was a good game. The shark and the fish swam away, happy to have made new friends.
