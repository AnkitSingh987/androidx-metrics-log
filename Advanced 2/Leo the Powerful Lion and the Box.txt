Once upon a time, there was a powerful lion named Leo. He lived in a big forest with his friend, a small bird named Chirpy. One sunny day, Chirpy found a big box near the river. He flew to Leo and said, "I found a big box! Let's open it!" Leo liked the idea and went with Chirpy to see the box. When they reached the box, Leo used his strong paws to open it. Inside, they found a lot of yummy food. Chirpy said, "I recommend we share this food with our friends!" Leo agreed and they both called their friends to enjoy the food together. All the animals in the forest came and had a big feast. They were very happy and thanked Leo and Chirpy for sharing the food. From that day on, they all became even better friends.

Sara likes to skip. She skips to school, she skips in the park, she skips at home. She has a big smile when she skips. She feels happy and free. One day, Sara sees a big stick on the ground. She thinks it is a staff, like the ones she sees in fairy tales. She picks it up and pretends to be a wizard. She waves the staff and says magic words. She skips with the staff in her hand. But then, a bad man sees Sara and her staff. He is angry and mean. He wants the staff for himself. He runs after Sara and tries to grab the staff. He shouts at Sara and scares her. Sara is sad and afraid. She does not want to give the staff to the bad man. She holds the staff tight and runs away. She looks for help. She sees a police officer near the road. She runs to him and tells him what happened. The police officer is good and kind. He listens to Sara and hugs her. He tells her she is brave and smart. He goes to the bad man and stops him. He takes the staff from him and gives it back to Sara. He tells the bad man to go away and not bother Sara again. Sara is happy and relieved. She thanks the police officer and gives him a hug. She skips with the staff in her hand. She feels safe and free. She has a big smile when she skips.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Fluffy. Fluffy lived in a big room with his best friend, a little boy named Timmy. They loved to play together all day. One day, Timmy said, "Let's put on a show for Mommy and Daddy!" So, Timmy and Fluffy worked hard to make a fun show. They danced and sang songs. Fluffy even learned to jump through a hoop. Timmy knew their show would make Mommy and Daddy happy. Finally, it was time for the show. Timmy and Fluffy did their best dance and sang their favorite song. Fluffy jumped through the hoop just like they practiced. When the show was over, Mommy and Daddy clapped their hands and said, "Good job, Timmy and Fluffy!" Timmy and Fluffy were so happy that they made Mommy and Daddy applaud.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play outside, even on gloomy days. One day, he went out to play in the park with his friends. They ran, jumped, and had lots of fun. While playing, Tim fell down and hurt his knee. He felt sad and didn't want to play anymore. His friends tried to cheer him up, but Tim just wanted to go home. As he walked home, he saw a small, lost kitten. Tim decided to help the kitten find its way home. He forgot about his hurt knee and felt happy to help. They walked together, and soon, they discovered the kitten's house. The kitten's family was very thankful to Tim for bringing their kitten home. The moral of the story is that helping others can make you feel better, even when you are feeling sad or hurt. And sometimes, unexpected things can happen that will change how you feel.

One day, a curious little boy named Tim saw a sign on the fridge. The sign had a picture of a big, red apple. Tim wanted to know what the sign said, so he asked his mom. "Mom, what does the sign on the fridge say?" Tim asked. His mom looked at the sign and smiled. "It says, 'Find the big, red apple and you will get a fun surprise!'" she told him. Tim was very excited. He looked all around the house for the big, red apple. He looked under the bed, behind the couch, and even in the bathroom. Finally, he found the apple in the fridge next to the sign. But when he picked it up, it was not a real apple – it was a toy apple! Tim was surprised and laughed. His mom came into the kitchen and said, "Good job finding the apple, Tim! Your surprise is that we are going to the park to play today!" Tim was so happy that he found the apple and got to go to the park.
