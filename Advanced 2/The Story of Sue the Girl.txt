One day, a little girl named Sue went to the park. It was a wet day, but Sue liked to play outside. She saw a big tree and went to sit under it to stay dry. Sue saw a little boy named Tim. He was holding a box. Tim said, "I have a number game. Do you want to play?" Sue said, "Yes, I like number games!" They played the game together. The game had treats inside the box. After they played the game, Tim said, "Now we get treats!" But when they opened the box, the treats were wet! They laughed and said, "Oh no! The wet day made our treats wet too!" Sue and Tim became friends and played more games at the park.

Once upon a time, a little boy named Tim loved to watch cartoons on TV. He would sit and laugh at the funny characters. One day, he saw a cheap toy in a shop. It looked like his favorite cartoon character, so he bought it with his money. Tim took the toy home and played with it all day. He made it move and dance, just like in the cartoons. Then, something unexpected happened. The toy started to move by itself! Tim was very surprised. The toy became Tim's best friend, and they played together every day. But one day, the toy stopped moving. Tim was sad, but he realized that he had many other friends to play with. So, he put the toy on a shelf and went outside to play with his friends. The toy had taught him that real friends are better than any cartoon.

One day, a tired boy named Tom went to a cabin in the woods with his mom and dad. The cabin was small and cozy. Tom liked to play with the number blocks his mom gave him. Tom was playing with his blocks when he heard a knock on the door. He opened the door and saw a big, friendly bear. The bear said, "Hello, can I play with you?" Tom was surprised but said, "Yes, let's play with my number blocks!" As they played, the bear showed Tom a new game. They had to put the blocks in order from one to ten. Tom was having so much fun with his new friend. But then, something unexpected happened. The bear sneezed really loud and all the number blocks went flying! Tom and the bear laughed and picked up the blocks together. They played until it was time for the bear to go home. Tom was happy he made a new friend and couldn't wait to tell his mom and dad about his fun day with the bear.

Once upon a time, there was a small cube. The cube was very original because it could change colors. It had many friends who loved to play with it. They were always excited to see what color the cube would be each day. One day, the cube's friends had a big party. They all played games and had fun. The cube was excited to show off its new color at the party. But, when it got there, it saw that another cube had the same color. The original cube was sad because it was not special anymore. Then, something unexpected happened. The other cube started to change colors too! The two cubes became friends and learned that being original is not just about being different. The moral of the story is that being kind and sharing your special gifts with others is what truly makes you original.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. She had a big, round basket. Mia loved her basket very much. She would put all her toys in it and carry them around. One day, Mia went to the park with her basket. She played with her toys under a big tree. The sun began to rise high in the sky. Mia felt warm and happy. Suddenly, a strong wind blew. Mia's basket started to roll away. She ran after it to keep it safe. The basket stopped near a big rock. Mia was happy to find her basket and hugged it tight. She knew her toys were safe inside. From that day on, Mia and her basket had many more fun days at the park.
