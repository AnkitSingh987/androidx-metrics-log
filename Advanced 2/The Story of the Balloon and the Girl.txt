Once upon a time, there was a little girl who loved balloons. She had a bright red balloon to replace her old one which had gone 'pop'. She held it close, feeling very happy. The little girl decided to take her balloon outside and she skipped along, watching it float above her in the sky. But as she was walking, an unknown wind blew the balloon away. The little girl gasped with surprise. Suddenly, the little girl saw something strange. It was a green balloon, bigger than her red one, and it was coming towards her. She watched as the green balloon moved closer and closer until it reached her and then it seemed to land in her hands. The little girl smiled with delight. The little girl thanked the unknown wind for replacing her balloon with a new, bigger one. From then on, she never let it float away and always kept it close. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a big, hairy elephant named Ella. Ella had a long trunk that she used to pick up things. She lived in the forest with her friends. One day, Ella met a little girl named Lily. Lily was lost and needed help to find her way home. "Can you help me?" Lily asked Ella. "Yes, I can help you," Ella said with a smile. Ella decided to lead Lily back to her home. As they walked together, Ella used her long trunk to move things out of the way. They saw many animals like birds, squirrels, and rabbits. Finally, Ella and Lily found Lily's home. Lily was so happy to be back. "Thank you, Ella!" Lily said. Ella was happy to help her new friend. And from that day, Ella and Lily became best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big blue ball. This ball was the world. It was very pretty and had many friends. The friends were the sun, the moon, and the stars. One day, the world felt cold and shivered. The sun saw the world shivering and said, "World, why are you cold?" The world replied, "I don't know, Sun. But I am very cold and I need help." The sun thought about how to help the world. The sun had an idea. "I will wrap you in a warm blanket, World!" The sun took a big, soft blanket and wrapped it around the world. The world felt warm and cozy. "Thank you, Sun!" said the world. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby cat named Tim. Tim liked to study. He would look at his books all day. Tim wanted to go on a big bus. He needed a ticket to go on the bus. One day, Tim found a ticket on the ground. He was very happy. Tim went to the bus stop and waited. He could not wait to go on the bus. He had never been on a bus before. When the bus came, Tim got on. But something unexpected happened. The bus was full of dogs! The dogs were nice and wanted to study too. Tim was surprised but happy. He made new friends and they all studied together on the bus.

Tom and Lily liked to play in the park. They liked to run and slide and swing. They also liked to feed the geese that lived by the pond. The geese were big and white and made loud noises. One day, Tom and Lily saw a new goose. It was brown and had a wide neck. It looked different from the other geese. Tom and Lily wanted to pet it. They walked slowly to the brown goose. The brown goose did not run away. It looked at them with its small eyes. Tom reached out his hand to touch the brown goose. The brown goose opened its wide mouth and bit Tom's finger. Tom cried out in pain. He pulled his hand back. The brown goose had borrowed his finger for a moment. It did not want to share. Lily ran to Tom and hugged him. She said sorry to him. She said the brown goose was mean. She said they should go back to the other geese. The other geese were nice. They liked to eat bread and seeds. Tom and Lily walked away from the brown goose. They did not want to play with it anymore. They went to the other geese and fed them. The other geese made happy noises. Tom and Lily smiled. They liked the other geese. They did not like the brown goose. The brown goose was bad. It liked to borrow fingers.
