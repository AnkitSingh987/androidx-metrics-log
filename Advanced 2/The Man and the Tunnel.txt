Once there was an elderly man who wanted to go for a swim in the river. He took a dip in the water, making big splashes with his arms. Just then, a young boy came running along the riverbank. He was about three years old and he had never been swimming before. He asked the man, “Will you take me in the water?” The elderly man smiled and said, “Of course!” He taught the boy how to splash in the river. As they were swimming, the boy noticed a tunnel in the river. He asked, “What is that?” The man explained, “That’s a tunnel. It’s like a path that the river takes.” The boy went closer to take a better look. He was curious and wanted to explore it. Seeing this, the elderly man said, “Let’s swim through the tunnel!” So the two of them swam towards the tunnel. The boy was a bit scared but excited at the same time. Then, they made their way through the dark and narrow tunnel and when they came out, the boy was filled with joy. He had just gone on an exciting adventure!

One day, a little boy named Tim found a clean phone on the floor. It was shiny and pretty. Tim wanted to play with the phone, so he picked it up. Tim saw a big bed in the room. He wanted to lie down on the bed with the phone. So, Tim climbed onto the bed and lay down. He played with the phone, pushing the buttons and making fun noises. After a while, Tim's mom came into the room. She saw Tim with the clean phone and smiled. "Good job, Tim! You found my phone and it's so clean!" she said. Tim was happy he could help his mom and play with the clean phone.

Once upon a time, there was a smart cat named Tom. Tom loved to bathe in the sun all day. He wore a big red boot on his paw. One day, a dog named Max came to play with Tom. Tom did not want to play. He wanted to bathe in the sun. Max wanted Tom's red boot. Max said, "Give me your boot, Tom!" Tom said, "No! It is my boot!" They both got mad. Tom had an idea. He said, "Let's play a game. If you win, you can have my boot. If I win, I can bathe in the sun." Max agreed. They played and Tom won. Tom got to bathe in the sun and keep his red boot. Max went away, and they both were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. Tim was a strong boy who loved to play baseball. One day, he went to the park to play with his friends. At the park, Tim saw a big dog. The dog had a baseball in its mouth. Tim said, "Hey dog, can I have the baseball?" The dog wagged its tail and gave Tim the baseball. Tim and his friends played with the baseball and the dog. As they played, the dog used its paw to mark a spot in the dirt. Tim and his friends looked at the spot. They dug in the dirt and found a big box. Inside the box were more baseballs! They were all very surprised and happy. Now, they could play baseball all day long with the dog.

Once upon a time, there was a little star. This star was very creative. It could change colors and make fun shapes in the sky. All the kids loved to look up and see the star at night. One day, the star began to worry. It thought that maybe it was not good enough. It saw other stars that were bigger and brighter. The little star wanted to be like them. But then, the star remembered that being creative made it special. It didn't have to be like the other stars. It could be happy just being itself. So, the little star kept changing colors and making fun shapes. The kids still loved to watch it every night. And the little star was happy again.

One day, after a successful fishing trip, Johnson was heading home with his catch when he noticed a small honeybee hovering above a flower. He watched it until it disappeared around a big tree. Curious, he followed it. When he came around the tree, he saw a huge beehive. He was astonished! He immediately called his 3-year-old daughter Anna so she could see the magnificent sight. “Look Anna! A beehive! Can you believe it?” he asked. Anna was very excited. She watched the honeybees flying in and out of the beehive. However, after a while they all disappeared. “Daddy, where did they go?” asked Anna. “Well Anna, they must have gone out to get some honey so they can make more!” “That’s so cool! I can’t wait to come here again and see if they were successful on their honey mission.” said Anna, full of anticipation. The next day, when Johnson and Anna returned to the beehive, sure enough, it was full of honey and the bees were happily buzzing around happily buzzing around. Johnson and Anna observed the beehive with awe, feeling very successful in their own mission to find the beehive.
