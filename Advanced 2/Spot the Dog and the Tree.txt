Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot loved to run and play with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big tree and wanted to learn how to climb it. Spot asked his friend, Bird, to help him learn. Bird said, "I can teach you the truth about climbing trees. It is not easy for dogs, but I will help you." Spot was very happy and excited to learn. They tried and tried, but Spot could not climb the tree. Bird told Spot the truth, "Dogs can't climb trees like birds can." Spot was sad, but he understood. He knew he was still a cute dog who could run and play. So, Spot and Bird played together on the ground, and they were happy.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to eat blueberries. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. They had a big basket of blueberries with them. Sue saw a big bird in the park. The bird was hungry too. Sue wanted to help the bird, so she tried to release the blueberries from the basket. But, she dropped the basket and all the blueberries fell on the ground. Sue felt ashamed because she made a mess. Her mom and dad told her it was okay. They picked up the blueberries together and shared them with the bird. The bird was very happy, and Sue learned how to share with her new friend.

The clouds were growing gloomier each day. It was time for little Tommy to wait at the bus stop. The sun was nowhere to be seen, and there was no one around. Tommy waited, getting cold and anxious, but the bus never came. Suddenly, the sky opened and it started pouring rain. Tommy wanted to go home, but he was scared, so he decided to wait at the bus stop until the rain stopped. He waited and waited, as the rain kept pouring and the clouds grew ever darker. Finally, Tommy heard a loud rumble. "Oh no, a thunderstorm!" he cried. As the thunder rolled and the lightning flashed, Tommy ran for shelter. But it was too late. The rain and thunder were getting stronger and stronger, and Tommy was too scared to move. Just then, a gust of wind blew and the clouds opened up. The sun shone and the sky cleared. But little Tommy was nowhere to be seen. He had been swept away in the wind, never to be seen again.

Sara was a wealthy girl who had many toys and books. She liked to read novels, which were long stories with many pages. She had a big sack where she kept her novels. She liked to take one novel every night and read it in her bed. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. She saw a boy who was playing with a ball. He looked sad and lonely. Sara wanted to be his friend. She went to him and said, "Hi, I am Sara. What is your name?" The boy looked at her and said, "I am Ben. I have no friends here. I just moved to this town." Sara smiled and said, "Do you like to read novels? I have many novels in my sack. You can borrow one if you want." Ben's eyes lit up and he said, "Really? I love novels. They make me happy and forget my problems. Can I see your sack?" Sara nodded and opened her sack. She showed him her novels and let him choose one. Ben picked a novel with a picture of a dragon on the cover. He said, "Thank you, Sara. You are very kind. Can I read this novel with you?" Sara said, "Of course. Let's sit on that bench and read together." They sat on the bench and opened the novel. They read about the dragon and the knight and the princess. They laughed and gasped and wondered. They became friends and forgot the time. They had fun reading the novel.

Once there was a little girl named Snook. She had a secret but she was afraid that she would tell someone. One day the little girl was feeling lonely and sad when her mommy came over and gave her a big hug. Mommy knew that Snook had a secret but she comforted her anyway. The little girl felt better after the hug and felt like she could trust her mommy. Snook knew that she had to keep her secret safe but she also wanted to tell someone. She soon found the perfect person to tell, her friend Todd. Todd was always really nosy, but he knew how to keep a secret and he was a great listener. Snook was very excited to tell him her secret so she asked him if he was ready to hear it. Todd was very excited and listened closely to every word. Snook was so happy to have someone to share her secret with, and Todd was so excited to be the one Snook chose. They talked and laughed until Snook was all comfy and ready to go to bed. They said goodnight, and Snook knew her secret was safe with Todd.
