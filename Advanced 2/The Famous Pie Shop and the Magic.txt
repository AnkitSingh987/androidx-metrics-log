Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a famous pie shop. The shop was run by a kind lady named Sue. She made the most yummy pies. Everyone loved her pies. One day, a little boy named Tim came to the shop. He said, "Sue, I want to help you make pies." Sue smiled and said, "Okay, Tim. Let's make pies together." They started to make pies. Tim was very happy. As they were making pies, they found a big problem. They were out of pie filling! Tim was sad, but Sue said, "Don't worry, Tim. We can solve this." They went to the market to buy more filling. At the market, they found a magic pie filling that made their pies talk! The pies became even more famous, and everyone was surprised and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a bright, sunny day. A little boy named Tim and his dog, Spot, went outside to play. They liked to run and jump in their big yard. One day, they saw that the gate was broken. Tim said, "Oh no! We need to fix the gate." So, Tim and Spot tried to fix the gate together. They pushed and pulled until the gate was fixed. Now, Tim and Spot could play in their yard again. They were very happy. The bright sun shined down on them as they played all day long. And the fixed gate kept them safe in their yard.

One day, a little boy named Tim and a little girl named Sue went to the park. They saw a big apple tree with many apples on it. They both wanted the same apple, but they did not want to share. They were very busy trying to get the apple. Tim and Sue began to discuss who should get the apple. They talked and talked, but they could not agree. While they were busy discussing, a bird flew down and took the apple away. Tim and Sue were sad because they did not get the apple. They learned that they should share and not fight over things. They promised to share the next time they found something they both wanted.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Hannah. She was three years old and had bright blue eyes. One day, she was walking in a meadow with her parents. Suddenly, she saw the most beautiful veil she had ever seen. It was brown and shone in the sunshine. She could not resist it. Hannah ran towards it and pulled it off a bush. "No, dear!" Her mother said. "That veil does not belong to us." But it was too late. As she pulled the veil, a dark shadow appeared in the sky and a loud thunder shook the meadow. Heavy dark clouds began to fill the sky. Hannah was terrified. The dark clouds began to pour down rain and a strong wind started to blow. Hannah was soaking wet and shivering with cold. The veil was nowhere to be seen. It had been taken away by the strong gusts of wind. Hannah's parents were cross with her because she had taken something that did not belong to her. They only just managed to get home safely in the middle of the terrible storm. Hannah learned her lesson and never took something that did not belong to her again.

One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to climb a small hill. He put on his shoes and went outside. The sun was shining and the grass was green. Tim was very happy. As he climbed the hill, he saw a big tree with a score on its trunk. Tim knew that his big brother had made the score with a stick. Tim felt safe because he knew his brother had been there too. At the top of the hill, Tim saw his house and his family. He waved to them and they waved back. Tim felt good and safe. He climbed back down the hill and ran home to tell his family about his adventure.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a fancy crystal. It was big and shiny. Everyone loved the crystal. It was in the middle of the town for all to see. One day, a boy named Tim came to the town. He saw the crystal and wanted to touch it. He asked his friend, Sam, "Can I touch the crystal?" Sam said, "You must promise to be careful." Tim promised, "I promise to be careful." Tim reached out to touch the crystal, but he was not careful. The crystal fell and broke into many pieces. Everyone was sad because the fancy crystal was gone. Tim was sorry, but it was too late. The town was not happy anymore.
