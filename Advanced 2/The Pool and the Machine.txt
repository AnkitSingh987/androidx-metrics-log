Once upon a time, there was a big pool. In the pool, many kids and an elderly man liked to swim. The elderly man was slow, but he was happy. One day, a big machine came to the pool. The machine could swim too! All the kids wanted to play with the machine. They laughed and splashed in the water. The elderly man watched and smiled. He swam with the kids and the machine. They all had fun together. The pool was a happy place for everyone.

Once upon a time, there were two best friends called Lucy and Billy. They were playing together with a toy car. Both were so happy when suddenly, they disagreed. Billy wanted to push the toy car and Lucy wanted to pull it. Billy said, "I want to push it!" "No, I want to pull it!" Lucy said. Billy tried to push the car, but it was too heavy. He grunted and said, "It's too heavy!" Lucy thought for a moment and then said, "Let's work together. I'll pull and you push. Ready? 1, 2, 3!" So they both pushed and pulled the car until they reached the finish line. They both cheered and then hugged each other. Lucy and Billy agreed that it was more fun and easier to work together. They smiled, went back to playing with the toy car, and didn't disagree anymore.

Once upon a time, there was a nosy cat named Tom. Tom liked to wake up early and walk around the town. One day, while walking, Tom saw a big police car. The police car had a nice man inside. The man said, "Hi, Tom! I am a police man. I help people." Tom liked the police man and wanted to help too. So, Tom went with the police man in the big car. They helped people and had fun. Tom was very happy. He loved being a nosy cat and helping the police man.

Lily and Tom were friends who liked to play at school. They liked to draw on the blackboard with colorful chalks. They made pictures of animals, flowers, and stars. They were happy and proud of their drawings. One day, it was Lily's birthday. She brought a big cake and some balloons to share with her classmates. She wanted to celebrate with them. She put the cake and the balloons on a table near the blackboard. She said, "Look, I have a cake and balloons for my birthday! Let's eat and have fun!" Tom was happy for Lily, but he also felt a little jealous. He wished he had a cake and balloons too. He thought, "Why does Lily have everything? I want to have something special too." He looked at the blackboard and saw Lily's drawing of a butterfly. He had an idea. He took a chalk and drew a big X over the butterfly. He thought, "Now Lily's drawing is ruined. She will be sad and I will be happy." But Lily saw what Tom did. She was very angry and sad. She ran to the blackboard and tried to erase the X. But the chalk was fragile and broke in her hand. She cried, "Tom, why did you do that? You are a bad friend! You spoiled my drawing and my birthday!" Tom felt sorry for what he did. He saw that Lily was really hurt. He realized that he was wrong. He said, "Lily, I'm sorry. I was jealous of you. I wanted to have a cake and balloons too. But I shouldn't have ruined your drawing. That was mean and selfish. Please forgive me." Lily looked at Tom. She saw that he was sincere. She said, "Tom, I forgive you. I understand that you felt jealous. But you don't have to be. You are my friend and I want to share my cake and balloons with you. You can have something special too. Come, let's celebrate together." Tom and Lily hugged and made up. They ate the cake and played with the balloons. They also fixed the drawing of the butterfly. They learned that being jealous is not good. They learned that being kind and generous is better. They learned that friendship is more important than things. They were happy and grateful.
