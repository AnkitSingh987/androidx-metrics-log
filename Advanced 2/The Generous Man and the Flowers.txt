Once upon a time, there lived a generous man. He often painted pictures for people in his village. One day, the generous man decided to help the children. He got his paint and brush and went to a playground. The generous man started to paint. He painted beautiful pictures of animals, flowers, and trees. Some of the children came to watch. They were so excited that their toes started to tap. The generous man then asked the children if they wanted to help paint. The children were so happy to help with such a fun task. They each got their own brush and painted bright colors all over the playground. They even painted on their own toes! The generous man was so proud of the children's artwork. Everyone was happy with the colorful playground. It was a great day, thanks to the generous man!

Ben and Lily like to play with their toy animals. They have many animals: a cat, a dog, a cow, a pig, a lion, a bear, and more. They make noises and pretend the animals talk to each other. One day, they decide to have a tea party with their animals. They set up a table and chairs in the garden. They fill a pot with water and pour it into cups. They give each animal a cup and a plate. They say, "Cheers!" and sip their water. But then, Lily sees a big problem. She says, "Ben, look! The lion is not happy. He does not like tea. He likes meat. He is hungry and angry. He wants to eat the other animals!" Ben says, "No, Lily, that is not true. The lion is a regular animal. He is nice and friendly. He likes tea and cookies. He does not want to eat the other animals. He wants to play with them." Lily and Ben start to argue. They pull the lion from each other. They say, "He is mine! He is mine!" They make loud noises and cry. Their mom hears them and comes to the garden. She says, "What is going on here? Why are you fighting over the lion? He is a toy, not a real animal. You have to share and be kind to each other." She takes the lion and puts him back on the table. She says, "Look, the lion is not hungry or angry. He is smiling. He likes tea and cookies. He likes all the other animals. He likes you and you. He wants you to be happy and have fun." Lily and Ben look at the lion. They see his smile. They feel sorry for fighting. They say, "Sorry, mom. Sorry, lion. Sorry, Ben. Sorry, Lily." They hug each other and hug the lion. They say, "We love you, lion. We love you, mom. We love you, Ben. We love you, Lily." They sit down and have their tea party. They make noises and pretend the animals talk to each other. They say, "Cheers!" and sip their water. They are happy and have fun.

One day, John was walking through the woods in search of a surprise. He was feeling happy, but soon became frustrated as he couldn't find anything. He was about to give up when he suddenly heard a voice. John: "Who's there?" Mystery Voice: "I'm a magic fairy!" John was surprised. He had never seen a magic fairy before! Magic Fairy: "I can help you find your surprise! I'm a master of vanishing things!" John was excited! He was sure the magic fairy could help him find what he was looking for. John: "Please help me find my surprise!" The magic fairy waved her wand and suddenly the surprise appeared! John was so happy and thanked the magic fairy for her help. John: "Thank you for making my surprise vanish and reappear!"

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing with his toy car. He liked to make the car go fast and then yell, "Stop!" Tim was glad when the car stopped right away. Tim's mom was in the kitchen making food. She heard Tim playing and asked, "Tim, do you want to help me make lunch?" Tim said, "Yes!" and ran to the kitchen. He stopped his car and put it down. While they were making lunch, Tim's mom told him about their body parts. She said, "We have lungs inside us to help us breathe." Tim thought that was cool. He was glad to learn about his lungs. After they finished making lunch, Tim went back to play with his toy car. He played with it all day and every time he made it stop, he thought about his lungs and how they help him breathe. Tim was glad he learned something new.
