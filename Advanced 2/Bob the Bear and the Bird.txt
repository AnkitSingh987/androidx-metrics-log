Once upon a time, there was a big bear named Bob. Bob was very sad because he had no friends. One day, Bob went for a walk and saw a fake bird. Bob said, "Hi, bird! Will you be my friend?" The fake bird did not say anything, but Bob did not know it was fake. Bob tried to touch the fake bird, but it fell down. "Oh no! I am sorry, bird," said Bob. Just then, a real bird came and saw what happened. The real bird said to Bob, "You did not hurt me. That was a fake bird. I am a real bird." Bob was happy to gain a new friend. The real bird and Bob played together every day. They had so much fun, and Bob was not sad anymore. And from that day, they were best friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a big insect. The big insect lived in a small house. One day, the big insect met a little cat. The cat was sad. The big insect asked the cat, "Why are you sad?" The cat said, "I can't find my toy. I think it is in the big tree." The big insect wanted to help the cat. So, they went to the big tree. The big insect tried to climb the tree to find the toy. But the tree was too big. The big insect fell down and could not move. The cat cried, and the big insect did not get up. The toy was still in the tree, and they did not have a happy day.

One day, a big rock and a small rock were sitting by the river. The big rock said, "Look, I can twist and turn in the water!" The small rock watched as the big rock twisted and turned, splashing water everywhere. "I want to try too," said the small rock. "But I'm scared." The big rock told the small rock not to be scared. "It's perfect fun!" said the big rock. So, the small rock tried to twist and turn like the big rock. But the small rock twisted too much and fell into the river. The water carried the small rock far, far away. The big rock was sad because the small rock was gone. The big rock learned that it is not always good to do what others do, especially if it can be dangerous.

Once upon a time, there was a unique bunny who loved to hop. Bunny wanted to learn about history, but the big words in the books were too hard for him. One sunny day, Bunny hopped to his friend, Bird. "Can you help me learn history?" Bunny asked. Bird thought and said, "Let's hop to the park and I will tell you a simple story." At the park, they sat under a big tree. Bird told Bunny about a brave king who saved his people. Bunny listened and smiled. He learned something new and it made him happy. From that day on, Bunny and Bird hopped to the park every day to learn more history. Bunny loved the simple stories and felt proud to know about the past. Bunny's unique way of learning history made him the smartest bunny in the land.

One sunny day, three friends were walking around a farm. They were looking for an adventurous thing to do, but there wasn't much around. Then suddenly, the sky began to grumble and a few raindrops fell. The friends saw the rain and they got very excited. They started to run and dance in the rain. They splashed and cheered, and got very wet, but they didn't care. After a while, the rain stopped and the sun came out. The sun dried the friends quickly, and they were off again, exploring the farm. They saw lots of cool things and even made up some games. At the end of the day, the friends all said goodbye, with smiles on their faces. Rain had brought them lots of adventure!

Jake was getting ready to go outside and play. He pulled on his clothes and looked in the mirror. He shrugged, they looked fine. His Mom said, "Where are you going?" Jake replied, "Outside, I'm going to play!" Mom sighed and said, "Don't forget: fine is not safe. Always be careful when you play. You can't be too safe." Jake shrugged, not sure what she meant. He said goodbye and stepped outside. At first, it was all fun and games. Then, things took a turn for the worse. Suddenly, he realized he was in danger and that his Mom's cautionary words about being safe had been a warning. He had been too casual and now he was in trouble. Luckily, with some quick thinking, he managed to avoid the danger and get back home safe. As soon as he walked through the door, his Mom gave him another cautionary look. She said, "Fine is not always safe. Remember that!" Jake nodded, understanding now what she had meant before. He promised to always be more careful and never again shrug off the importance of staying safe.
