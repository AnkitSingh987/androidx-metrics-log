Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big parade. Everyone was happy and excited. The sun was shining, and the kids were laughing. They could not wait to see the parade. The people in the town had been planning the parade for a long time. But there was one little boy named Tim who was very restless. He could not sit still. He wanted to be the center of attention. So, he thought of a plan to spoil the parade. He wanted to make a big mess and stop the parade. Tim started to throw things and make a mess. The people in the town became sad. They did not like what Tim did to the parade. Tim saw how sad everyone was and felt bad. He realized that he should not have spoiled the parade. Tim decided to say sorry and help clean up the mess he made. The people in the town were happy again. They forgave Tim and continued the parade. Tim learned that it is not good to spoil things for others just to get attention. He promised to be a good boy and never spoil anything again.

One day, a big, dependable dog named Max went for a walk. Max wanted to find a safe place to play. He walked and walked until he saw a small girl named Lily. Lily looked sad. Max asked, "Why are you sad, Lily?" Lily said, "I am lost. I don't understand how to get home." Max wanted to help Lily. He said, "I will help you find your home. I am a dependable dog." As they walked, they saw a big tree with lots of red balls. Max thought they were balls to play with, but they were not balls. They were apples! The tree was an apple tree! They both laughed and picked some apples to eat. Max and Lily kept walking and found Lily's home. Lily was so happy and said, "Thank you, Max, for helping me. You are a dependable dog and now I am safe." Max wagged his tail and was happy he could help Lily. From that day on, they became best friends and always played together in the safe place Max found.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to the store with her mom. They wanted to buy some food. Sue saw a huge, round ball. She wanted to play with it. Her mom said, "We can buy the ball if you want." At home, Sue played with the huge ball. She threw it up in the air and caught it. She kicked it and rolled it. Sue was so happy. She made a funny face and laughed. Sue's friend, Tim, came over to play. He saw the huge ball and said, "Wow! That's a big ball!" They played with the ball all day. They had so much fun. When it was time for Tim to go home, Sue and Tim both made funny faces and laughed.

Once upon a time there was a boy who was very adventurous. He loved to explore and one day he decided to explore a metal cave. When he arrived he saw a big metal door and it was slightly open. He decided to go inside and when he did, he saw a big scary monster. The boy was very scared but he was also brave. He said to the monster, "Please don't eat me!" The monster replied, "Don't worry, I'm not going to eat you. You appear to be very adventurous and I can see that. I have a special task for you." The boy said, "What is it?" The monster said, "I need you to find a magical metal key and bring it back to me. If you do this, I will reward you with something very special. So, what do you say?" The boy answered, "I say yes! I'm excited to go on this adventurous quest for the magical metal key!" The monster smiled and said, "I'm sure you can handle it. Good luck!"

Once upon a time, there was a chubby little girl called Chloe. She loved nothing more than playing her guitar. One day, as she was playing her guitar, her mom called her from the kitchen and said: "Chloe, come here!" Chloe came running into the kitchen with her guitar. But when she got there, her mom looked mad. She said to Chloe: "Why are you playing the guitar again? You should do your homework instead!" Chloe looked sad but she didn't say anything. Her mom said: "You should take your guitar upstairs and come back down once you've done your homework." Chloe reluctantly took her guitar upstairs and started her homework. However, when she came back down, her mom was even madder. It turned out Chloe was two hours late! Her mom was so angry, she took her guitar away and said: "No more guitar playing for you until you learn to do your homework on time!" Chloe was very sad. But no matter how hard she tried, her mom wouldn't let her play the guitar again.
