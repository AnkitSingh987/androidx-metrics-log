Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Lily. She had a big, soft armchair in her room. Lily loved to sit in the armchair and read her favorite books. One day, Lily saw a little bird outside her window. It was singing a sweet song. "Hi bird," said Lily, "I like your song, but I don't understand it." The bird looked at Lily and chirped again. Lily opened the window and the bird flew in. It sat on her armchair and sang a new song. "I understand now," said Lily with a smile. "You want to be friends!" The bird nodded its head and they became best friends. From that day on, Lily and the bird would sit in the armchair together, reading books and singing songs.

Once upon a time, there was a fast elephant. He always wanted to get almost everywhere really quickly. One day, he had to go on a very long journey, so he left early in the morning. On the way, he met a wise old turtle. The turtle asked, "Where are you going in such a hurry?" The elephant nodded, and motioned at the long journey ahead. The turtle said, "Why don't you take your time on such an important journey? If you go too fast, you may miss what's most important." The elephant thought about this and knew that the turtle was right. He lowered his head, slowing down from his fast pace. He made sure to take in all of the sights on his journey and to appreciate all of the little things. At the end of his journey, he was almost at his destination. To remind himself to never be in such a rush, he put his thumb on the side of his trunk. From that day on, he always looked at his thumb when he felt the urge to move too quickly. He realized that sometimes it's better to take your time and appreciate what's around you.

One day, a little fly was flying in the sky. The fly saw a big tree and wanted to rest on it. So, the fly went to the tree and sat on a leaf. The fly was very tired. Soon, a big wind came and blew the leaf. The fly felt helpless and was scared. The fly tried to hold on to the leaf, but it was too hard. The wind was too strong for the little fly. Just then, a kind bird saw the helpless fly. The bird flew to the fly and said, "Hold on to me, I will help you." The fly held on to the bird, and the bird took the fly to a safe place. The fly was very happy and said, "Thank you, kind bird, for helping me." The bird and the fly became good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly cat named Kitty. Kitty loved to play all day. She liked to surprise her friends with fun games. One day, Kitty saw a wide box in the room. She thought it would be fun to hide inside it. Kitty jumped in the box and waited for her friend, Doggy, to come. Doggy walked into the room and saw the wide box. He said, "What is in this box?" Kitty jumped out and yelled, "Surprise!" Doggy laughed and said, "You got me, Kitty! Let's play together!" They played all day and had so much fun. Kitty and Doggy were very happy. They loved to surprise each other and be jolly friends.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a tall man named Tom. Tom was a taxi driver. He loved to drive his yellow taxi and help people go to different places. One day, Tom saw a little boy named Tim who was lost and crying. Tom asked, "Why are you crying, Tim?" Tim said, "I lost my mom, and I don't know where she is." Tom wanted to help Tim and said, "Don't worry, we will find your mom in my taxi." They went around the town and finally found Tim's mom. She was so happy to see her son safe and sound. But Tim's mom was not happy with Tim for running away. She said, "Tim, you should not run away from me. I will punish you by not giving you ice cream today." Tim felt sad but understood that he did something wrong. The moral of the story is to always stay close to your parents and not run away, or you might get punished.
