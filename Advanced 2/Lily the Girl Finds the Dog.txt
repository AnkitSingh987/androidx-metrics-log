One day, a little girl named Lily went to the park with her mom. Lily had a big, comfortable bag with her. Inside the bag, she had a bottle of water to drink and some toys to play with. At the park, Lily saw a small dog. The dog looked thirsty. Lily asked her mom, "Can I give the dog some water to drink?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can share your water with the dog." Lily opened her bag and took out the bottle of water. She poured some water into a small bowl and the dog drank it. The dog wagged its tail and seemed happy. Lily felt happy too, because she helped the dog. After playing at the park, Lily and her mom went back home with the comfortable bag.

Once upon a time, there was a 5 year old named Lucy. One day, she was walking in the park with her mom when they saw two little girls. They were twins, and they were both very fast runners. "Look at those two!" said Lucy's mom. "Maybe they would like to play?" So, Lucy's mom introduced herself and Lucy to the two little girls. They introduced themselves as twin sisters Marjorie and Maddie. The girls all got along very well, so they decided to play together. They all ran around the park, laughing and having a great time. When it was time to go home, they promised to play again soon. Lucy's mom gave them each a hug, and said goodbye. "Bye Marjorie and Maddie!" Lucy said. "I can't wait to play with you again."

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was very persistent. He did not like to go to bed at night. One day, Tim's mom said, "It is time to go to bed, Tim." But Tim disagreed. "No, I don't want to go to bed!" Tim said. His mom tried to tell him that it was late and he needed to sleep. But Tim still disagreed. He wanted to play with his toys and watch his favorite show on TV. Finally, Tim's mom said, "If you go to bed now, you will wake up early and have more time to play tomorrow." Tim thought about it and agreed. He went to bed and had a good night's sleep. The next day, he woke up early and had lots of time to play with his toys and watch his favorite show. He learned that sometimes it is good to listen to your mom, because she knows what is best for you.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little girl named Lily. She loved to play outside and admire all the things in her yard. One day, she saw a big rock that she had never seen before. It was so big that she could not move it. She thought it was a special rock. Lily told her friends about the big rock. They all wanted to see it too. So, they all went to Lily's yard and looked at the rock. They all thought it was a special rock too. They played games around the rock and had so much fun. One day, Lily and her friends found a treasure map. They followed the map to a big X. The X was right by the big rock! They dug and dug, and they found a treasure chest. Inside the chest were pretty jewels and gold coins. Lily and her friends were so happy. They knew that the big rock was special, and it had led them to a great treasure.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sarah. She was very naughty. One day, Sarah was eating some yummy cake and she was balancing all of it on her stomach! Suddenly, her mummy walked in and saw what she was doing. Mummy said, "Sarah, how naughty! You must not balance food on your stomach." Sarah was a bit sad because she had been having lots of fun. She saw her puppy near the door, so she decided to try balancing him on her stomach instead! Mummy was cross. She said, "Sarah! That is enough now. You must not balance your puppy like that. Now go and play outside for a while." Sarah was a bit sad and she went outside to play. She thought about balancing things carefully and not doing naughty things. She soon forgot all about her tasty cake and balancing her puppy.
