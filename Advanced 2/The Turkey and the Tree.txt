Once, there was a big turkey. The turkey was very important. It lived on a farm with many friends. The turkey liked to serve food to its friends. It was a very kind turkey. One day, there was no food to serve. The turkey was sad. Its friends were hungry too. The turkey wanted to help its friends. It looked for food everywhere on the farm. But it could not find any food. Then, the turkey saw a big tree. The tree had many fruits. The turkey asked the tree for help. The tree was kind and gave fruits to the turkey. The turkey was happy. It served the fruits to its friends. All the animals on the farm were happy and full. The turkey was a hero.

One day, a little girl named Lucy had a dream. In her dream, she met an elderly lady who lived in a small house. The elderly lady was very kind and loved to help others. Lucy liked her a lot. The elderly lady said, "Lucy, I want to give you something special. I have a big cup of hot cocoa. If you share this cocoa with someone who needs help, you will both be happy." Lucy took the cocoa and said thank you. The next day, Lucy saw a sad boy in the park. She remembered her dream and the elderly lady's words. She went to the boy and said, "Would you like to share my cocoa?" The boy smiled and said yes. They both drank the cocoa and felt happy. Lucy learned that sharing with others can make everyone happy.

Once upon a time, there was a cute dog. The dog had a toy motorcycle. The dog loved to play with the motorcycle. He would drive it around and around in the yard. One day, the dog saw a big hill. The dog wanted to drive the motorcycle up the hill. He thought it would be fun. So, the dog started to drive the motorcycle up the hill. But the hill was too steep for the dog and the motorcycle. The motorcycle fell down the hill. The dog tried to catch it, but he could not. The motorcycle broke into pieces. The dog was very sad. He could not play with his favorite toy anymore.

Ben and Lily were twins who loved to play outside. They had a brilliant kite that was red and yellow and had a long tail. They liked to run with the kite and watch it fly high in the sky. One day, they went to the park with their mom and dad. They saw a big pond with ducks and fish. They wanted to fly their kite near the pond, so they asked their mom and dad if they could. Their mom and dad said yes, but told them to be careful and not go too close to the water. Ben and Lily ran to the pond with their kite. They found a spot where the wind was strong and let go of the kite. The kite soared up and up, and they laughed and clapped. They followed the kite with their eyes, but they did not see where they were going. Suddenly, Ben tripped on a rock and fell. He let go of the kite string, and the kite flew away. Lily tried to catch the kite, but she was too late. The kite dived into the pond and sank. Ben and Lily cried and cried. They had lost their brilliant kite. Their mom and dad heard them and came to see what was wrong. They saw the kite in the water and felt sorry for them. They hugged them and said they would buy them a new kite. But Ben and Lily did not want a new kite. They wanted their old kite back. They were very sad. The end.

One day, a little girl named Lily went to her grandma's house. She saw a big cabinet in the kitchen. The cabinet was brown and had many doors. Lily was very curious about what was inside the cabinet. Lily asked her grandma, "What is in the cabinet?" Grandma smiled and said, "Let's open it and see." They opened the cabinet and found many tasty treats inside. There were cookies, candies, and fruits. Lily's eyes grew big and she was very happy. Lily and her grandma took some tasty cookies and sat down to eat. They talked and laughed together. Lily loved spending time with her grandma and eating the treats from the cabinet. It was a fun day for both of them.
