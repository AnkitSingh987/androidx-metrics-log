Once upon a time, in a distant land, there was a small fairy named Fifi. Fifi had a best friend, a big dog named Dodo. They played together every day and had lots of fun. One sunny day, Fifi and Dodo had a quarrel. Fifi wanted to play with a red ball, but Dodo wanted to play with a blue ball. "I want the red ball," said Fifi. "No, I want the blue ball," said Dodo. They both got very sad and stopped playing. Fifi and Dodo sat under a tree and thought about their quarrel. They realized that they didn't need to fight over the balls. They could play with both balls and have even more fun. So, Fifi and Dodo said sorry to each other and started playing again. They played with the red ball and the blue ball and had the best day ever.

Once upon a time, in a little lake, there was a little boat. The little boat was very happy. It liked to float on the water all day. One day, a frog saw the little boat and wanted to play with it. The frog said, "Hello, little boat! Can I play with you?" The little boat said, "Yes, let's play together!" So, the frog jumped on the little boat, and they played on the water. They were very happy. But one day, a big bird saw the little boat and wanted to steal it. The big bird flew down and tried to take the little boat away. The frog was scared and said, "No, you cannot steal my friend, the little boat!" The big bird was surprised and said, "I am sorry, I did not know the little boat was your friend." The frog and the big bird became friends too. They all played together on the water and had lots of fun. And they lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a small, ugly doll that she loved very much. The doll had a pretty cloth dress with red and blue flowers on it. One day, Lucy decided to invite all her friends to a tea party. She wanted her ugly doll to come too. She set up a small table with cups and plates, and put the doll in a chair. Her friends came over with their own dolls and toys. They all sat down and had a fun tea party together. Lucy and her friends had a great time at the tea party. They laughed and played, and the ugly doll with the pretty cloth dress had fun too. In the end, Lucy's friends thought the ugly doll wasn't so ugly after all. They all went home happy, and Lucy knew that her doll was special no matter what.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a lady. She was nice and had many friends. One day, she woke up and found out she had become unknown. No one knew her. She was sad and didn't know what to do. She went to her friend's house and knocked on the door. Her friend opened the door and said, "Who are you? I don't know you." The lady was very sad and said, "I am your friend, but I became unknown." Her friend didn't understand and closed the door. The lady walked around the town, trying to find someone who knew her. Then, she met a little girl who said, "I know you! You are the nice lady who always smiles." The lady was happy and hugged the little girl. Just then, she became known again, and everyone remembered her. They all laughed and played together, and the lady was never unknown again.

Once upon a time, there was a grumpy old man. He had a sunflower in his garden, and it was the biggest one that anyone had seen. One day, a little three-year-old boy who lived nearby stopped by the old man's garden. He saw the sunflower and thought it looked really beautiful. He said to the old man, "Can I pick the sunflower?" The grumpy old man frowned and said, "No, you shall never dare pick my sunflower!" The little boy was so sad that he couldn't pick the sunflower. He wanted to so badly, but he knew he couldn't disobey the old man. The little boy went back to his own garden and planted a small sunflower seed. Every day he would water it and keep it safe, until one day it bloomed into a beautiful sunflower just like the one in the old man's garden. The little boy was so proud of his sunflower, and ran to show the old man. The old man smiled and said, "You did very well, I am proud of you for growing your own sunflower." That day, the little boy and the old man became friends and shared their love for sunflowers.
