Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sally. She was very enthusiastic and loved to play outside. One day, she went to the park with her mom and dad. They played on the swings, slid down the slide, and ran around in the grass. Sally was very happy. At the park, Sally saw a post with a sign on it. She asked her mom what it said. Her mom told her it was a name, "Tommy." Sally thought it was a funny name and laughed. She said, "I want to name something too!" So, Sally and her mom and dad went home and found a little rock. Sally named the rock "Bobby." She was so proud of her new friend. They played together all day and had lots of fun. And that is the story of Sally and her rock named Bobby.

One day, a little girl named Sue went to visit her elderly grandma. Grandma was in her room. Sue wanted to help her dress. "Grandma, let me help you dress," Sue said. Grandma smiled and said, "Thank you, Sue." In the kitchen, Sue saw a tray with yummy food. She wanted to give it to her grandma. Sue picked up the tray and walked to her grandma's room. But, on the way, she tripped and the tray fell. The food went everywhere. Sue was sad and said, "Oh no! What do I do now?" Just then, a friendly dog came in. The dog saw the food on the floor and started to eat it. Sue was surprised and laughed. She told her grandma, "The dog ate the food from the floor!" Grandma laughed too and said, "That's okay, we can make more food together." So, Sue and her grandma had a fun day making food and playing with the dog.

One day, Tim and his mom went to the park. It was a sunny day, and Tim was very excited. He loved to play on the swings and slide. When they got to the park, they saw that it was wet. It had rained the night before. Tim's mom said, "Tim, be careful. The slide might be difficult because it's wet." Tim listened to his mom and played on the swings instead. He saw his friend, Sam, at the park too. They played together and had a lot of fun. They waited for the slide to dry, and then they slid down with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a girl named Lily found a new cushion. It was very pretty with many colors. She wanted to show her mom, so she took it to the kitchen. Her mom was cooking food for dinner. While Lily's mom was cooking, the cushion began to move. It was a surprise! The cushion turned into a big, friendly cat. Lily and her mom were so happy to see the cat. The cat said, "I am a magic cat. I can help you cook." So, the cat helped Lily's mom cook dinner. They all had fun and made the best dinner ever. From that day on, the magic cat stayed with Lily and her mom, and they all lived happily together.

Once there was a boy named Andrew. Andrew loved to spin around like a top and play with his yarn. He had lots of different colors – red, purple, pink, blue. He always chose his favourite color of purple. One day, his friend Tim came over. Tim wanted to spin around with Andrew and they both loved playing with Andrew’s yarn. But when Andrew chose his favourite color of purple, Tim got jealous. He said he wanted the purple yarn too! Andrew told Tim that he could choose whichever color he liked. Tim chose pink yarn to play with and they both had lots of fun spinning around with their different colors. The moral of the story is that it is better to share than be jealous. When we share, everybody gets to play.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink cocoa before bedtime. Every night, he would ask his mom to make him a warm cup of cocoa. His mom would smile and make it for him. Tim liked to drink his cocoa while sitting in his big chair. One night, while Tim was drinking his cocoa, he saw something scary. It was a big shadow on the wall. Tim was scared and wanted to hide. He closed his eyes tight and started to blink really fast. When he opened his eyes, the scary shadow was still there. Tim called his mom, and she came running into the room. She saw the scary shadow too. But then, she turned on the light and showed Tim that it was just his toy bear. Tim felt silly and laughed. He finished his cocoa and went to bed. From that night on, Tim was not scared of shadows anymore.
