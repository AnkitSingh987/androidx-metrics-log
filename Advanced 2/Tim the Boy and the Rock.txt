Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He loved to play outside in nature. One day, Tim saw a big, fair rock. He wanted to take it home. Tim tried to pick up the rock, but it was too heavy. He asked his friend, Sam, for help. Sam said, "I will help you, but you must share the rock with me." Tim did not want to share, so he said, "No, it is my rock." Tim tried to carry the rock alone, but he could not. He realized that he needed help. He went back to Sam and said, "I am sorry. I will share the rock with you." But Sam was mad and did not want to help anymore. In the end, Tim did not get the big, fair rock. He was sad and alone. The moral of the story is that it is better to share and have friends than to be greedy and alone.

Once upon a time, there lived a patient owl. He lived in a small tree in the middle of a big forest. Every day, he would look for food. Suddenly, he saw a funny looking animal. The owl was curious and wanted to know what the animal was. He flew closer and asked the animal what his name was. The animal explained that he was a squirrel. The owl was so excited about his new friend that he asked the squirrel to tell him about his home in the tree. The squirrel patiently explained that his home in the tree was full of yummy acorns. The owl thought this sounded delicious and wanted to learn even more. He asked the squirrel to show him which trees had the best acorns. The squirrel happily agreed and explained to the owl that the trees that had the biggest and roundest acorns were always the best. The owl thanked the squirrel and went off to find the perfect tree. From then on, the owl always had plenty of food and he was very happy.

Once upon a time, there was a stubborn chicken named Chicky. Chicky lived on a farm with his friends. Chicky loved to jump all day long. He would jump over the fence, over the hay, and even over his friends. One day, Chicky met a new friend named Cowy. Cowy was big and slow. Chicky said, "Cowy, do you want to play with me?" Cowy said, "Yes, Chicky, but I can't jump like you." Chicky thought for a moment and then had a great idea. Chicky and Cowy played a new game. Chicky would jump, and Cowy would try to catch him. They laughed and played all day. Chicky learned that even stubborn chickens can have fun with friends who are different. And Cowy learned that it's okay not to jump, as long as you're having fun with your friends.

Once upon a time, there was a big red bus. The bus had many friends who would ride on it every day. One day, a naughty little boy named Tim came to ride the bus. Tim was always doing silly things and making a mess. The bus wanted to teach Tim a lesson about being good. So, the bus thought of a plan. The bus would lend Tim a toy car to play with, but only if Tim promised to be nice and not make a mess on the bus. Tim agreed to the bus's plan and played with the toy car. He was very careful not to be naughty. The bus was happy that Tim learned to be good, and they became friends. From that day on, Tim always behaved well on the bus, and they had lots of fun together.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. He loved to sleep all day and play all night. One day, he was taking a nap under a big tree when he heard a loud noise. Tom woke up in shock and looked around to see what made the noise. He saw a little bird named Polly sitting on a branch. Polly was crying. Tom asked, "Why are you sad, Polly?" Polly said, "I can't find my mom. I am lost." Tom felt bad for Polly and wanted to help her. Together, they went on a search for Polly's mom. As they walked, they saw a big dog. The big dog started to chase them. Tom and Polly ran fast, but then Tom had to pause. He had an idea. He climbed up a tree and helped Polly fly up too. The big dog couldn't reach them, so he left. To their surprise, Polly's mom was in the tree! She had been watching them the whole time. Tom and Polly were so happy to find her, and they all became good friends.
