One day, a little boy named Tim found a big switch on a wall. He wanted to touch it, but his mom said, "Tim, resist! Do not touch the switch!" Tim was an intelligent boy, so he listened to his mom. Later, a cat came by and saw the switch. The cat said, "Meow, what is that?" Tim told the cat, "It is a switch, but my mom said not to touch it." The cat was curious but listened to Tim. In the end, Tim and the cat played together and had fun. They both resisted touching the switch and showed they were intelligent. Tim's mom was happy that they listened and gave them a treat.

Mother and son were having a picnic in the park. "Do you want to go and slide?" said mother. "Yes!" said son, who wanted to have some fun. They both went to the slide and mother was very serious. "Watch out and be careful," she said. Son understood mother's warning and slowly went down the slide. It was great fun. "Let's have a snack!" said mother. Son was very excited to hear that. He opened the bag and took out the snacks. They both ate the snacks and had a nice chat. After they finished, son asked mother if they could go and slide again. Mother smiled and said, "Yes, of course, but still be careful." Son nodded and they both went to have some more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a brave boy named Tim. Tim loved to celebrate with his friends. One day, Tim found a big dragon in the woods. The dragon was sad and alone. Tim wanted to help the dragon. He asked the dragon to come to his party. The dragon was scared, but Tim was brave and kind. They went to the party together. All the kids were happy to see the dragon. At the party, the dragon learned to be brave like Tim. The kids and the dragon had fun and celebrated. The dragon was not sad anymore. The moral of the story is to be brave and kind to others, even if they are different.

Once upon a time there was a candle made out of wax. One day the candle saw a crazy man walking around. It was really silly! The candle called out to the man, "What are you doing?" The man replied, "I'm looking for something - something special!" The candle asked, "What is it?" The man said, "I don't know. I've been searching a long time, but I can't find it." The candle thought for a moment and then said, "Maybe if you light a candle something special will happen." The man was really excited and lit the candle. At first nothing happened, but then something magical happened - a little genie popped out! The man was amazed. The genie said, "I was trapped in this candle. You saved me! Thank you. I will grant you three wishes." The man was so happy. He wished for lots of money, a beautiful house and a happy life. The genie granted him his wishes and they all lived happily ever after!

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Emma. Emma loved to play outside in the sun. One day, she saw a perfect, big tree with lots of roots. She thought, "I want to play near that tree!" Emma ran to the tree and started to dig in the dirt. She wanted to find a new friend, a little bug. As she dug, she saw a big, sleepy bug. Emma said, "Wake up, bug!" The bug slowly woke up and looked at Emma. "Hello, Emma," said the bug. Emma was so happy to have a new friend. But then, something unexpected happened! The bug started to grow bigger and bigger. Soon, it was as big as Emma! The big bug smiled and said, "Thank you for waking me up, Emma. I am a magic bug, and I can grant you one wish." Emma thought for a moment and said, "I wish for a day full of fun and play with my new friend!" The magic bug nodded, and they played all day under the perfect, big tree.
