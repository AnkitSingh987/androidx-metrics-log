Once upon a time, there was a persistent little dog named Poppy. Poppy loved to play with her toy ball. One day, Poppy was playing in the park when she saw a strip of cloth on the ground. Poppy picked up the strip and ran to her friend, Kitty the cat. "Look what I found!" said Poppy. "Let's use this strip to play a new game!" Kitty agreed, and they played a game of tug-of-war with the strip. They pulled and pulled, but neither Poppy nor Kitty would give up. They were both very persistent. Finally, the strip tore in half, and Poppy and Kitty laughed. They had so much fun playing with the strip that they forgot all about the toy ball. From that day on, Poppy and Kitty always played with the strip, and they became the best of friends.

Once there was a crane in the sky. It was so tall and it was very expensive. It was the most expensive crane anyone had ever seen! One day, a little girl was walking by the crane. She was only three years old so she didn't quite understand why a crane was so expensive. But she still thought it was really cool. Then all of a sudden the crane started to shut down. It became very quiet. Even though the little girl was very young, she knew it was bad that the crane had shut. The little girl was very sad that the crane was no longer working. She wished there was something she could do to help it. But she was too small to do anything. Eventually, people came and fixed the crane. But no matter how much they tried, the crane was still really expensive. The little girl knew that she couldn't do anything about it. But she was still glad that the crane was back in the sky.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She was very creative. She loved to draw and paint all day. Lucy had a favorite thumb that she liked to suck when she was sad or tired. One day, Lucy went to the park with her mom. They played on the swings and slides. Lucy was having so much fun that she didn't want to leave. But it was getting late, and her mom said it was time to go home. Lucy was sad to leave the park, so she started to suck her thumb. Her mom told her that they could come back to the park another day. Lucy felt better and stopped sucking her thumb. When they got home, she used her creative skills to draw a picture of the park. Lucy was happy because she had a fun day and made a beautiful picture to remember it.

One day, a good dog named Sam found a basket. He wanted to support his friend, Cat. Cat was sad because she lost her toy. Sam thought the basket could help find the toy. Sam said to Cat, "I will support you. Let's find your toy with this basket." Cat smiled and said, "Thank you, Sam. You are a good friend." They looked and looked for the toy. At last, they found it under a tree. Sam put the toy in the basket and gave it to Cat. Cat was happy and said, "You are the best friend, Sam." They played together and had a fun day.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there lived a dog named Bob. Bob was a happy dog with a tight, curly tail. One day, Bob heard people talking about a meeting. He wanted to go to the meeting too. At the meeting, Bob saw many people and animals. They were all talking and having fun. Bob saw a cat named Lily. Bob said, "Hi Lily, do you like the meeting?" Lily said, "Yes, Bob, it's fun. Let's play a game." Bob and Lily decided to play a game where they shoot a ball into a hoop. They took turns shooting the ball. Sometimes they missed, but sometimes they made it in. The other animals at the meeting joined them, and they all laughed and played together. In the end, Bob and Lily became good friends and had a great time at the meeting.
