Anna loved to bake cakes with her mom. She liked to mix the flour, the eggs, the sugar and the butter in a big bowl. She liked to pour the batter in a round pan and put it in the oven. She liked to smell the sweet aroma of the cake as it baked. But most of all, she liked to taste the cake when it was done. One day, Anna decided to bake a cake by herself. She wanted to surprise her mom and dad. She remembered the ingredients and the steps, but she did not measure them carefully. She put too much sugar, too little butter, and forgot the eggs. She also added some salt, pepper, and ketchup, because she thought they would make the cake more colorful and spicy. When the cake was ready, Anna took it out of the oven and put it on a plate. She was very proud of her work. She called her mom and dad to the kitchen and said, "Look, I made a cake for you! Do you want to taste it?" Her mom and dad smiled and thanked her for her effort. They each took a small bite of the cake and tried not to make a face. The cake was very sweet, very dry, and very weird. They did not like it at all, but they did not want to hurt Anna's feelings. They said, "Wow, Anna, this is a very... interesting cake. You are very creative and brave to try new things. But you know, sometimes it is better to follow a recipe and measure the ingredients. That way, the cake will be more delicious and more balanced. Do you want to learn how to bake a cake with us next time?" Anna nodded and said, "Yes, please. I want to learn how to bake a cake like you. I'm sorry, I did not mean to make a bad cake. I just wanted to make you happy." Her mom and dad hugged her and said, "We are happy, Anna, because you are our daughter and we love you. You are very intelligent and kind, and we are proud of you. But remember, not everything that looks good tastes good. And not everyone who tastes your cake will be as nice as us. Some people might be mean and judge you. So, be careful and listen to your mom and dad, okay?" Anna said, "Okay, mom and dad. I love you too. And I love cake. Can we bake a cake together now?" They said, "Of course, Anna. Let's bake a cake together. And let's taste it together too." The end.

Once there was a young boy named Jack. He wanted to go fishing in the harbor and see all the pretty fish. One day Jack and his Mom went fishing. Jack had a bucket but he was being careless and some of the fish were jumping out of the bucket. His Mom tried to catch the fish but she couldn't. Jack's Mom got angry and scolded him. She said, "You were careless and now all of the fish are gone! You must be more careful next time!" Jack felt guilty and started crying. His Mom wiped the tears from his face and said. "Don't feel guilty Jack. The best way to learn is from our mistakes. Just remember to be careful when we go fishing next time." Jack hugged his mom and said "OK, I will be more careful next time." The moral of this story is that we should learn from our mistakes. Even when we failed, we should not feel guilty and take away an important lesson.

Once upon a time, there was a big, sleepy dog named Max. Max liked to stay in the warm sun all day. He liked to run and play with his friends, too. One day, Max saw a little bird with a hurt arm. The bird could not fly. Max wanted to help the bird. He stayed with the bird and tried to make it feel better. After some time, the bird's arm got better. Max was happy to see the bird fly again. They became good friends and played together. Max was not sleepy anymore. He was happy to have a new friend.
