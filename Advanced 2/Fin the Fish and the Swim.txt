Once upon a time, there was a little fish named Fin. He loved to swim all day with his friends. One day, while they were swimming, they saw a big fish named Big Blue. Big Blue could swim very fast and far. Fin and his friends were jealous of Big Blue. Fin wanted to swim like Big Blue, so he asked his mom, "Mom, can you permit me to swim far and fast like Big Blue?" His mom smiled and said, "Fin, you are still a little fish. When you grow up, you will swim far and fast too." Fin was sad but he understood. He knew that one day he would be a big fish too. So, he kept swimming with his friends, waiting for the day when he could swim like Big Blue. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. She liked to design things with her crayons. One day, she found a big package in her room. It was wrapped with a pretty bow. Sue was very excited and wanted to open it. Sue pulled the bow and the package was open. Inside, she found a big box of crayons. She was very happy! Sue started to design a beautiful picture with her new crayons. She drew a big sun, a house, and some trees. But then, something unexpected happened! The crayons started to move! They jumped out of the box and began to design their own picture. The crayons drew a funny face on the sun, and the trees had big smiles. Sue laughed and clapped her hands. She was happy to have new friends who loved to design, too. And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a boy named Tim. He had a small camera that he loved. Tim liked to take pictures of harmless bugs in his garden. One day, Tim saw a big bug on a flower. He thought, "I will take a picture of the bug!" He ran to get his camera and went back to the garden. But the bug was gone! Tim was sad. Then, Tim heard a tiny voice say, "Hello!" He looked down and saw the bug. The bug said, "I can talk! Do you want to be friends?" Tim was very surprised but happy. They became best friends and took many pictures together.

One day, a little fly was flying in the sky. The fly was very tired. It saw a big dog and thought, "I will follow the dog and maybe find a place to rest." The dog saw the fly and said, "Hello, little fly! Why are you following me?" The fly answered, "I am tired. I need a place to rest." The dog was nice and wanted to help the tired fly. The dog took the fly to a pretty flower. The fly said, "Thank you, big dog!" The tired fly sat on the flower and took a nap. The dog and the fly became good friends and played together every day.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Sally. Sally loved to polish her shoes every day. One day, she went to the park to eat her salad. While Sally was eating, a rude boy came and took her salad. Sally was sad and didn't know what to do. Then, a big bird saw what happened. The bird wanted to help Sally. So, the bird flew to the rude boy and took the salad from him. The bird brought the salad back to Sally. Sally was very happy and thanked the bird. The rude boy saw this and felt bad. He said sorry to Sally and learned that it is not nice to take things from others. From that day on, the rude boy became a kind and helpful friend to Sally. They both played together and shared their salads. The moral of the story is to be kind and share with others.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She loved to play with her toys, and she was always ready for fun. One day, Lucy was playing in her room when she found a shiny penny on the floor. "Look, Mommy!" Lucy said, holding up the penny. "I found a penny!" "Wow!" her mommy said. "That's a lucky penny. Hold on to it, and it might bring you good luck." The next day, Lucy took her penny with her everywhere. At the park, Lucy saw a butterfly appear out of nowhere. It was so pretty! Lucy knew it was her lucky penny making the butterfly appear. Lucy showed her penny to her friend, Tommy. "Look, Tommy! I have a lucky penny. It made a butterfly appear!" "Can I see?" Tommy asked. Lucy let him hold the penny, and together they watched as more butterflies appeared. They laughed and played with the butterflies, knowing that Lucy's lucky penny made their day even more fun.
