Once upon a time, there was a brilliant kangaroo. He liked to jump and play all day. The kangaroo had many friends in the forest. They all loved to play with him. One day, the kangaroo found a big hill. He wanted to jump to the top. He asked his friends to come with him. Some of the friends said yes, but some of they said no. The kangaroo just shrugged and said, "That's okay, we can play another day." The kangaroo and his friends jumped up the hill. They had so much fun. They played and laughed all day. When they got to the top, they looked down and saw their home. It was a beautiful sight. They all felt very happy and proud.

One day, a little boy named Tim was playing in his room. He saw a big book with a pretty print on it. The print had a picture of a tree and a dog. Tim was curious, so he asked his mom to explain what the print meant. His mom said, "The print is a story about a dog who gets lost in the woods. The dog is scared, but he finds his way home." Tim wanted to hear the story, so his mom read it to him. As the story went on, the scared dog met a kind squirrel who helped him find his way home. The dog was so happy, and Tim was too. He loved the story and the pretty print. From that day on, Tim always asked his mom to explain things to him.

One day, a strong bear called Bob set off to find food in the forest. He had heard about a secret mine where he could find plenty. After searching for hours, Bob finally found it! He looked inside and said, “What a surprise! The mine is full of delicious fruit.” Bob wasted no time in filling his basket with all the fruit he could carry. On his way home, Bob ran into a rabbit. The rabbit said, “What do you have in your basket?” Bob proudly replied, “This mine is mine. I found a whole basket of delicious fruit!” The rabbit was very impressed and said, “That’s strong! You must be very proud.” Bob smiled and nodded, then walked on his way, happy that his strong discovery had made a new friend.

One day, a brave boy named Tim went to play in the park. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim thought there might be treasure inside the tree hole. He wanted to find the treasure, so he decided to try and look inside. As he got closer, he saw a little bird by the hole. The bird said, "This is my home. Please don't take my treasure." Tim felt sad because he didn't want to make the bird upset. He thought about what he could do to make both of them happy. Tim had an idea. He said, "Let's try to find a new treasure together! We can be brave and look all around the park." The bird agreed, and they both searched for a new treasure. In the end, they found a shiny rock and became good friends. They were both happy to share the new treasure they found.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big, scary calendar on the wall. Tim did not like scary things. He wanted to ask his mom about the calendar, so he ran to her. "Mom," Tim asked, "why is there a scary calendar on the wall?" Tim's mom smiled and said, "That calendar is not scary, Tim. It is a special calendar that helps us remember important days." Tim thought about it and said, "I want to learn about the calendar, but I am still scared." His mom hugged him and said, "Do not worry, Tim. I will be with you, and we can learn together." So, Tim and his mom looked at the calendar every day. They talked about the days and the special events. Tim was not scared anymore. He liked the calendar now, and he was happy to learn with his mom.

One day, a big bird and a small bird were in their nest. They saw an egg on the ground. The big bird said, "Let's discuss who will take care of the egg." The small bird said, "I will do it. I am dependable." The big bird agreed and went away. The small bird took care of the egg every day. One day, the egg started to move. The small bird was very excited. "What will come out?" the small bird thought. The egg cracked open, and a baby cat came out! The small bird was surprised but happy. The small bird and the baby cat became good friends. They learned that even if something unexpected happens, being dependable and kind can make everything okay.
