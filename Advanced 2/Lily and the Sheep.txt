Lily loved to bake. She baked cakes, pies, cookies and bread. She baked for her family, her friends and her toys. She baked every day in her play kitchen. One day, she wanted to bake something new. She looked at her books and saw a picture of a sheep. She liked sheep. They were soft, white and fluffy. She decided to bake a sheep cake. She took some flour, sugar, butter and eggs. She mixed them in a big bowl. She poured the batter into a round pan. She put the pan in the oven. She waited for the cake to bake. While she waited, she made some frosting. She took some cream, vanilla and powdered sugar. She whipped them in a small bowl. She added some food coloring. She made the frosting pink. When the cake was done, she took it out of the oven. She let it cool. She cut out a sheep shape from the cake. She covered the cake with the pink frosting. She used some marshmallows to make the sheep's wool. She used some chocolate chips to make the sheep's eyes and nose. She used some licorice to make the sheep's legs and tail. She was very proud of her sheep cake. She showed it to her mom and dad. They smiled and clapped. They said, "Wow, Lily, you are a famous baker! Your sheep cake is amazing!" She showed it to her teddy bear and her doll. They nodded and cheered. They said, "Yum, Lily, you are a famous baker! Your sheep cake is delicious!" She showed it to her dog and her cat. They wagged and purred. They said, "Woof, meow, Lily, you are a famous baker! Your sheep cake is tasty!" Lily was very happy. She shared her sheep cake with everyone. She said, "Thank you, thank you, I am a famous baker! I love to bake!"

Once upon a time, there was a deep blueberry. It was growing in a field behind a tall mountain. One day, a young girl came to the field. She had heard about the blueberry, but she had never seen it. The girl went to the blueberry and started eating it. As she ate it, the blueberry became smaller and smaller. The girl looked around and saw a rabbit. The rabbit said, "Why is the blueberry so small? You destroyed it!" The girl said, "I was so hungry that I ate it!" The rabbit sighed. "It's not nice to destroy things. Be more careful next time!" The girl nodded and thanked the rabbit. She never forgot what the rabbit said and she always tried to be careful with the things she found.

Once upon a time, there was a happy monkey named Momo. Momo loved to swing from tree to tree in the big jungle. He was very flexible and could reach the highest branches. Momo had many friends who liked to play with him. One day, Momo found a bottle of wine under a tree. He didn't know what it was, but he thought it smelled funny. Momo's friend, a wise old owl, told him that wine was not good for little monkeys. The owl said that it could make Momo feel sick and not able to swing and play. Momo listened to the wise owl and decided not to drink the wine. He knew that being strong and flexible was more important than trying something new that could harm him. Momo continued to swing and play with his friends, always remembering the wise words of the old owl. The moral of the story is to always listen to good advice and make smart choices to stay healthy and happy.

Once upon a time, there was a big farm. On the farm, there were many animals and plants. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the farm with her mom and dad. They saw many things, like big cows, little chicks, and tall corn. Lily and her mom and dad walked around the farm. They saw a fun game. The game was to see who could pick the most corn from the tall plants. Lily and her mom and dad wanted to play the game. They were very competitive and wanted to win. They started to pick the corn. Lily's mom and dad picked corn very fast. Lily noticed that she was not as fast as her mom and dad. But she kept trying. At the end of the game, Lily's mom and dad had picked more corn. But everyone was happy because they had fun playing the game together.
