Once upon a time, there was a little girl called Sally. She was very curious and always wanted to explore the world around her. One day she saw a fisherman in the river, she was so amazed and wondered what he was doing there. She followed the fisherman and watched him catch lots of fish. Then, Sally got really excited and decided to help the fisherman catch more fish. She grabbed a rod, found some bait, and casted out into the water. The fisherman was stunned that this little girl was helping him and smiled. Suddenly, the river started to get quiet. Sally hadn't noticed but the fisherman knew something was wrong. Suddenly, a giant wave came crashing down and knocked both Sally and the fisherman off their feet. Sally was terrified and ran away. The fisherman managed to climb back up the river bank, but Sally was too scared to go back. The moral of this story is to always listen to people around you who know more. Even though Sally wanted to help the fisherman, if she had listened to his advice she would have not been put in danger. It's important to respect the wisdom of those more experienced than us and not get too curious and adventurous.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. Lily loved to play in her garden. One day, she found a fragile lemon on the ground. She picked it up and looked at it. Lily saw her friend, Ben, and said, "Ben, look at my fragile lemon! It is yellow and smells nice. What should we do with it?" Ben thought for a moment and said, "Let's send it to our friend, Sam, as a gift!" Lily and Ben carefully wrapped the fragile lemon in a soft cloth. They put it in a small box and walked to Sam's house. They knocked on the door and Sam opened it with a big smile. "Hi Lily, hi Ben! What do you have there?" Sam asked. Lily said, "We found a fragile lemon and wanted to send it to you as a gift. We hope you like it!" Sam took the box and opened it. He saw the lemon and said, "Wow, thank you! I will make lemonade and we can all share it!" And so, Lily, Ben, and Sam enjoyed the lemonade together, and they all had a great time.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom liked to play with his old club. One day, Tom went to the park with his club. He was very happy. In the park, Tom saw a big tree. He wanted to bow to the tree. So, he bowed to the tree with a big smile. The tree seemed to smile back at Tom. This made Tom feel very special. Tom played with his club under the tree all day. He had so much fun. When it was time to go home, Tom bowed to the tree again. He said, "Thank you, tree. I will come back soon." And Tom went home with his old club, feeling happy and tired.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He was a very brave boy who loved to explore. One sunny day, Tom went on an adventure in his backyard. He found a small green plant that smelled nice. It was a mint plant. Tom's mom saw him playing near the mint plant. She came over and said, "Tom, be careful! I want to warn you that the mint plant can make your skin itch if you touch it too much." Tom listened to his mom and decided not to touch the mint plant. As Tom continued his adventure, he found a cute bug. He was excited and wanted to show his mom. He carefully picked up the bug and brought it to his mom. She smiled and said, "Wow, Tom! You are so brave for holding that bug. Good job listening to my warning about the mint plant." Tom felt proud of himself and continued to have fun exploring.

Once upon a time, there was a shy baby bird named Tim. Tim was too scared to fly. His mom and dad would say, "Tim, you can do it! Just try!" But Tim would just shake his head and say, "No, I can't." One day, Tim saw a tiny bug on a leaf. He wanted to eat it, but he was too scared to fly over to it. The tiny bug looked at Tim and said, "Hey, why don't you fly over here and eat me? All the other birds do it!" Tim thought for a moment and said, "Okay, I will try." Tim jumped and flapped his wings. He flew a little bit, then fell. He did not quit. He tried again and again. Finally, he flew all the way to the leaf and ate the tiny bug. But something unexpected happened. The tiny bug was magic! It made Tim grow big and strong. Now, Tim was not shy anymore. He could fly high and fast. All the other birds were amazed. Tim's mom and dad were very proud of him. And Tim was happy that he did not quit.
