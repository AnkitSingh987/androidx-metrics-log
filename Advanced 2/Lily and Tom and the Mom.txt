Lily and Tom are twins. They like to play in the kitchen. They pretend to cook and eat yummy food. They have a lot of fun. One day, they find a big jar of honey on the shelf. They want to taste it. They think it will be sweet and sticky. They climb on a chair and reach for the jar. But the jar is too heavy. It falls down and breaks. Honey spills everywhere. "Oh no!" Lily says. "We made a big mess. Mom will be angry." "What do we do?" Tom says. "We can't clean it. It's too much." They hear Mom coming. They are scared. They hide under the table. Mom comes in the kitchen. She sees the broken jar and the honey. She is surprised. She looks around. She sees two pairs of feet under the table. "Lily? Tom? Are you there?" Mom says. Lily and Tom peek out. They look at Mom. They are sorry. "Mom, we are sorry. We wanted to try the honey. We didn't mean to break the jar." Lily says. "Mom, we are sorry. We like honey. We thought it was sweet. We didn't know it was heavy." Tom says. Mom smiles. She is not angry. She loves Lily and Tom. She knows they are curious and hungry. "It's okay, my loves. I'm not mad. I'm glad you are safe. Honey is sweet, but it's also sticky and messy. Next time, ask me before you take something from the shelf. I will help you." Mom says. She hugs Lily and Tom. She gives them a spoon of honey each. They lick it. It is sweet and sticky. They smile. They help Mom clean the kitchen. They have a lot of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lazy bear. He didn’t like to play and he didn’t like to work. One day, the bear was feeling bored. He thought he needed some fun activities, so he decided to invite the other animals over to his house. He found some chalk and drew a big map on the ground. The bear invited all the animals to come and play. The animals were excited. They gathered all around the map and started to draw. Some animals drew mountains, and some drew lakes. The bear just watched and was impressed. He felt happy seeing all the animals cross paths and create art. The animals soon realized that by working together they could do much more than just draw a map. They could build bridges, or a playground! They all decided to work together and make a fun place out of their shared art project. The bear also joined in and worked, too. He found that by working together, everyone could be successful, even a lazy bear like him! He learned that everyone has something special to contribute, and that if you work together with others you can create something beautiful.

Once upon a time, there was a big calendar on the wall. This calendar was very special because it was modern. It had many colors and pictures of animals on it. One day, the calendar started to suffer. It was sad because it could not play with the other things in the room like the toys and books. It just hung on the wall and watched everyone have fun. Then, a kind girl named Lily saw the sad calendar. She wanted to help it feel better. So, she took the calendar off the wall and showed it to her friends. They all looked at the pretty pictures and talked about the animals. The calendar was happy again because it was not alone anymore.

One day, a little girl named Lucy was eager to make a pretty paper flower. She needed scissors to cut the paper. She asked her mom, "Can I use the scissors, please?" Her mom said, "Yes, but be careful and don't leave them on the floor." Lucy started to cut the paper. Her little brother, Tom, saw her and wanted to play too. He tried to take the scissors from her. Lucy said, "No, Tom! These are sharp. You can't play with them." Tom was sad and cried. Lucy did not want Tom to be sad. She had an idea. She gave Tom some paper and helped him tear it into pieces. They both made pretty paper flowers together. Now, Tom was happy, and they didn't leave the scissors on the floor.
