Once upon a time there was an honest fisherman. He woke up early in the morning to fish in the river. He poked his rod in the water to catch some fish. He sat in his boat and waited patiently. After a while, he felt a tug on his rod! He quickly pulled the rod and a big fish was on the end! He was so happy and excited. He put the fish in his bucket and decided to go home. When he arrived, he was so proud of his catch. Everyone in the village was so impressed by the honest fisherman and his huge fish. Everyone wanted to buy some of the fish to eat. The fisherman was so happy and he danced around with joy. He had made a lot of money and he was a very happy man.

Once there was a green frog. He wanted to settle somewhere and make a home. So, he hopped around looking for the perfect spot. He looked all around but he couldn't find the perfect place. Then one day he came across a special calendar. He hopped over to it and saw that it was made of green paper. He loved the color green! He looked at the calendar to see all of the days of the week, month and year. He thought that this would be the perfect place to settle. He hopped up onto the calendar and sat there happily. He settled onto the calendar and loaded it up with all of his belongings. This way he was sure he would never forget a special event. From then on, the frog was the master of that green calendar. He made sure to check it every day to see what fun things he could do. He had finally found the perfect place to settle and call home!

Sam and Lily were best friends. They liked to play in the garden and look for bugs. One day, they found a big potato under a plant. They were very happy and decided to share it for lunch. "Let's go to the kitchen and wash it," Sam said. "OK, but don't tell Mom. She will be angry if we eat her potato," Lily said. They ran to the kitchen and turned on the tap. They scrubbed the potato with a brush until it was clean and shiny. They cut it in half with a knife and put it on a plate. "Yum, this looks good," Sam said. "Let's put some butter and salt on it," Lily said. They opened the fridge and took out the butter. They sprinkled some salt from the shaker. They were about to eat the potato when they heard Mom's voice. "Sam, Lily, what are you doing in the kitchen?" Mom asked. She came into the room and saw the potato on the plate. She was very angry. "Did you take my potato from the garden?" she asked. Sam and Lily looked at each other. They were scared and did not know what to say. "Tell me the truth," Mom said. Sam decided to lie. He pointed at Lily and said, "She did it. She took the potato and made me eat it with her." Lily was shocked and hurt. She did not expect Sam to blame her. She said, "No, that's not true. We both found the potato and wanted to share it. He is lying." Mom did not believe Lily. She thought Sam was telling the truth. She said, "Lily, you are a naughty girl. You should not steal and lie. You are not allowed to play with Sam anymore. Go to your room and think about what you did." Lily cried and ran to her room. She felt betrayed by Sam. She wished she had a reliable friend who would not lie to her. Sam felt guilty and ashamed. He realized he had made a big mistake. He had lost his best friend and his Mom's trust. He wished he had told the truth. The moral of the story is: Honesty is the best policy. Lying can hurt yourself and others.

Once upon a time, there was a clean, little car. The car loved to drive around in the sun. One day, the car saw a pretty flower by the road. The car stopped to look at the flower. The flower was red and smelled nice. The car wanted to be friends with the flower. They talked and laughed together. Every day, the car would drive to see the flower. They were very happy. The car and the flower became best friends. And they lived happily ever after.
