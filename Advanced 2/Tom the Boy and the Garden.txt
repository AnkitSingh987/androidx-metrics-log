Once upon a time there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very cool. One day Tom was playing in the garden when he found an avocado. He was very excited because he had never seen an avocado before. Tom decided to have a competition. He would show his avocado to all of his friends and whoever thought the avocado was the coolest would win. So, Tom ran to the park to show his avocado to all his friends. Everyone said the avocado was so cool. They all wanted to win! Finally, Tom decided that his friend Sandy was the winner because she was the most creative with her ideas. She said it looked like a beautiful green egg! Tom was very happy with his decision. He knew he had made the right choice and was so proud of his cool avocado.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. She loved to visit her grandma's house. Her grandma lived in a big, red house with a beautiful garden. Amy always felt happy when she was with her grandma. One day, Amy went to her grandma's house when the sunshine was bright and warm. They decided to go outside and play in the garden. Amy saw many pretty flowers and butterflies. She felt rich with joy because she loved spending time with her grandma. After playing in the sunshine, Amy and her grandma went inside the big, red house. They made yummy cookies and drank cold milk. They laughed and talked until the sun went down. Amy knew that visiting her grandma's house was her favorite thing to do.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big tree with a hole in it. Tim looked inside the hole and found a small box. The box was gold and very pretty. Tim was very happy and wanted to show everyone his new gold box. Tim ran to his friend, Sally, and said, "Look! I found a gold box in the tree!" Sally looked at the box and said, "That's so cool! Let's open it!" Tim and Sally opened the box and found a little bird inside. The bird looked serious and said, "Thank you for saving me!" Tim and Sally were very surprised. They did not know that birds could speak. The bird told them that he was a magic bird. He said, "I will give you one wish each for helping me." Tim and Sally thought hard about what they wanted. They made their wishes, and the bird made them come true. They played in the park all day with their new magic friend.

Once upon a time, there was a big cat and a little mouse. They lived in a house with many colors. The cat and the mouse were friends. One day, they decide to draw a picture on the wall. The cat had ink and paper. The cat said, "Let's draw a big tree with many leaves." The mouse said, "Yes, let's make it fit on the wall." So, they started to draw the tree. The cat used the ink to make the tree trunk. The mouse used the ink to make the leaves. As they drew, they talked and laughed. They were happy to make the tree fit on the wall. When they finished, they stood back and looked at their work. The tree was big and beautiful. The cat and the mouse were very proud of their picture. They decided to draw more pictures together in the days to come. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw many toys to play with. He was troubled because he could not choose which toy to play with first. He looked at the slide, the swing, and the sandbox. Tim saw another kid named Sam at the park. Sam said, "Hi Tim! Do you want to play with me? Let's choose a toy together." Tim said, "Yes, let's play together. But I can't choose a toy. Can you help me?" Sam said, "Let's play on the swing first, then the slide, and last the sandbox." They played on the swing and the slide. They were very happy. But when they went to the sandbox, they saw a big dog. The dog was not nice. It barked at them. Tim and Sam were scared. They ran away from the dog. They did not get to play in the sandbox. They went home sad because the day had a bad ending. They wished they had more time to play without the big dog.
