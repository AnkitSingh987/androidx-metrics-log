Once upon a time there was a small dog. He liked to sit in his favourite chair. One day he heard a knock at the door. He ran to the door and saw a big brown box outside. He was so excited! Inside the box, the dog discovered something interesting. It was a long stick with a tip at the end. The dog picked up the stick and wanted to know what it was. He went to his human and said "What is this, human?" His human smiled and said, "That is a fishing rod. We can use it to catch fish in the pond. Let's go sit by the pond and try it out!" So the dog and his human went to the pond and sat down. They waited awhile and eventually, the dog felt a tug on the fishing rod! He pulled it and there was a big fish on the tip of the rod. The dog and his human were so excited! The human put the fish back into the pond and they went home feeling happy. The dog always remembered that wonderful day at the pond and never forgot how to use his long fishing rod.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy cat named Tom. Tom loved to wear his soft bathrobe all day. He would play in the house and take naps in his favorite spot. One day, Tom saw a little bird outside the window. The bird looked cold and lost. Tom wanted to care for the bird, so he went outside to help. He took off his bathrobe and put it around the bird to keep it warm. Together, Tom and the bird went back inside the house. They played and had fun all day. At night, they both put on their bathrobes and fell asleep, happy to have each other as friends.

Once there was a shy cat named Tim. Tim lived in a small house with his friend, Sam the dog. They liked to play catch with a red ball. One day, a big storm came with loud thunder. Tim said to Sam, "I am scared of the thunder. Can we play catch inside the house?" Sam said, "Yes, let's play catch and forget the scary thunder." So, they played catch inside the house with the red ball. But while they were playing, the thunder got louder and louder. Tim and Sam got more scared. Then, the big storm broke a tree near their house. The tree fell on their house and made a big hole. Tim and Sam were very sad because their house was broken.

Once upon a time, there was a little bunny named Bobo. Bobo was a very dizzy bunny. He liked to play and run around all day. One day, he was so tired from playing that he needed to take a nap. Bobo asked his friend, a big bear named Benny, if he could borrow a soft place to sleep. Benny was very kind and let Bobo borrow his bed. Bobo took a nice, long nap on Benny's bed. When he woke up, he felt much better and not dizzy anymore. Bobo thanked Benny for letting him borrow the bed. Benny was happy to help his friend. They played together for the rest of the day. The moral of the story is to always be kind and help your friends when they need it, just like Benny did for Bobo.

One day, a girl named Lily went to visit her grandma. Grandma lived in a small house near the wild woods. Lily liked the wild woods because she could see many animals and pretty flowers there. When Lily got to grandma's house, they looked at photos together. They saw photos of Lily when she was a baby, and photos of grandma when she was young. They laughed and smiled at the funny photos. After looking at the photos, Lily and grandma went for a walk in the wild woods. They saw a bunny and some birds. Then, they went back to grandma's house and had a fun day together.

Once upon a time, in a small house, there lived a kind mom and her little boy named Tim. Tim loved to drink milk every day. He would be very careful when he drank his milk, so he didn't spill it on the floor. One day, they ran out of milk. Tim was sad. His mom said, "Don't worry, Tim. I will go to the store to pay for more milk." Tim was happy to hear that. He couldn't wait to drink more milk. When his mom came back, she had a big jug of milk. Tim was so excited! He drank his milk very carefully, just like his mom taught him. He was a good boy, and his mom was very proud of him. They lived happily ever after, with lots of milk to drink.
