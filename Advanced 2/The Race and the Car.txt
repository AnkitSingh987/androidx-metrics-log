Once upon a time, there was a big race. Many people came to watch. In the race, there was a fast car, a big truck, and a funny clown on a bike. The people were excited to see who would win. The race started. The fast car and the big truck went very fast. The funny clown on the bike was slow. The people laughed at the clown. They thought the clown was not important in the race. But then, something unexpected happened! The fast car and the big truck got stuck in the mud. They could not move. The funny clown on the bike went around the mud and won the race! The people cheered for the clown. Now, they knew the clown was important too. The clown was very happy and so were the people.

Once upon a time there was a little bird named Lina. She liked to bounce around and chirp all day. Lina's dream was to soar like a big bird across the blue sky. One day, Lina was in the kitchen with her mom and saw the dishwasher. She asked her mom, "Mommy, what's that?" To which her mom replied, "That's a dishwasher, sweetie. It washes dishes so we can have clean dishes." Lina excitedly replied, "Mommy, can I soar like a big bird?! Please?!" Her mom smiled and said, "Sure, honey! Just flap your wings and soar around the kitchen. It'll be so much fun!" Lina flapped her little wings and soared around like a giant bird. She felt so light and free! She was able to touch the soft curtains and tickle her mom's face with her feathers. Lina was so happy that she could soar like a big bird! When she got tired, she landed back in her mom's arms for a hug. And so, Lina lived happily ever after with her mom, soaring around the kitchen and having lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a naughty princess. Every day, she cooked yummy treats in her castle. She made cookies and cakes, and caramel apples. Everyone in the kingdom loved these treats! One day, the naughty princess cooked a special surprise: a giant chocolate cake. Everyone cheered and clapped, it was so sweet and delicious! The princess smiled and said, "Cooking is a special way to make people happy!"

One day, a boy named Tim found a book. It was a big, dry book called a novel. Tim did not know what a novel was. So, he asked his mom. "Mom, what is a novel?" Tim said. Mom smiled and said, "A novel is a long story with many words. You can read it to have fun and learn." Tim wanted to read the novel. But, when he opened the book, the words were too hard. He felt sad. Then, something unexpected happened. The words in the book began to change. They became easy words that Tim could read! Tim was very happy. He read the novel and learned many new things. He knew that this was a magic book. And from that day on, Tim and the magic novel had many fun adventures together.

Once there was a little elephant named Sam. He was very bright and always looking for something fun to do. One day, Sam decided he wanted to try flying. He adopted a bright attitude and worked hard to make his dream happen. Sam tried hopping, jumping and even spinning around, but nothing seemed to work. He watched as the birds flew through the sky and wished he could join them. Then he had an idea. He saw a nearby mountain and thought that if he ran fast enough and jumped, maybe he could fly. So he ran up the mountain as fast as his legs could carry him, and when he reached the top, leaped into the sky. Although Sam didn’t end up flying, he was determined to keep trying. He was brave and never gave up. And one day, he finally achieved his dream.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly boy named Tim. He loved to play near the sea. One day, he went to the sea with his mom. They had a lot of fun. They built a big sand castle and splashed in the water. "Mom, can we go on the boat?" Tim asked. His mom said, "Yes, but you must stay close to me." They got on the boat and began to float on the sea. Tim was very happy. He liked the boat ride. Suddenly, the sky turned dark. The wind began to blow hard. The sea became rough. Tim's mom started to shout, "Hold on tight, Tim!" But the boat tipped over, and they fell into the cold sea. They tried to swim back to the shore, but it was too far away. Sadly, Tim and his mom never made it back home.
