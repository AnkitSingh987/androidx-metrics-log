Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Mia. Mia had a big, impressive bag. She liked to put her toys in the bag. Mia always wondered what she could find to put in her bag next. One day, Mia went to the park with her bag. She found a pretty rock and put it in her bag. Mia wondered what else she could find. She saw a big tree and found a small stick. Mia put the stick in her bag too. Then, something unexpected happened. Mia's bag started to move! She was scared at first, but then she saw that her toys were alive. They were playing with the rock and stick. Mia was happy and played with her toys, the rock, and the stick all day long.

Sam and Lily liked to go to the park with their grandma. Grandma was elderly, but she was very kind and fun. She would push them on the swings, help them slide, and tell them stories. One evening, they went to the park when the sun was going down. The sky was pink and purple, and the moon was big and bright. Sam and Lily saw the moon and pointed at it. "Look, grandma, the moon!" they said. "Yes, my dears, that's the moon," grandma said. "Do you know what the moon does?" "No, what does it do?" Sam and Lily asked. "Well, the moon is very special. It changes shape every night. Sometimes it is round, sometimes it is half, sometimes it is a sliver. And sometimes, it disappears completely. That's called a new moon," grandma explained. "Wow, that's amazing! How does it do that?" Sam and Lily wondered. Grandma smiled and said, "That's a mystery, my dears. But some people say that the moon has a secret friend, a rabbit, who lives on it. And the rabbit likes to play hide and seek with the moon. Sometimes he covers the moon with his ears, sometimes he nibbles on it, and sometimes he hides behind it. And when the moon is new, he is cuddling with the moon, so we can't see them." Sam and Lily liked this story very much. They imagined the rabbit and the moon playing together in the sky. They asked grandma if they could see the rabbit someday. "Maybe, my dears, maybe. But you have to be very lucky and very quiet. The rabbit is shy and doesn't like to be seen. But if you wish hard enough, and look carefully enough, you might catch a glimpse of him," grandma said. Sam and Lily nodded and said, "We will, grandma, we will. We want to see the rabbit and the moon." They hugged grandma and thanked her for the story. Then they went home, dreaming of the moon and the rabbit. And the next night, when they looked out of their window, they saw a small shape on the moon. It looked like a rabbit, with long ears and a fluffy tail. Sam and Lily gasped and smiled. They had seen the rabbit and the moon. They had been very lucky and very quiet.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Lucy. She was deaf, but she was very good at understanding what people said with their hands. Lucy loved to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to pack some food for a picnic. Lucy was very excited! Lucy and her mom started to pack sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. They also packed a small stove to cook some warm food. When they were done, they went to the park with their big basket. Lucy and her mom found a nice spot under a big tree to have their picnic. As they were eating their sandwiches, they saw a little cat come near them. The cat looked hungry. Lucy's mom turned on the stove to cook some food for the cat. But suddenly, the cat jumped on the stove! The cat was not a real cat, but a magic cat! The cat turned the stove into a big cake. Lucy and her mom were very surprised. They laughed and shared the big cake with the magic cat. From that day on, they became good friends with the magic cat and had many more fun picnics together.
