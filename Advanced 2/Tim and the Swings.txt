Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim had a lucky coin that his mom gave him. He believed that the coin made him happy and strong. One sunny day, Tim went to the park to play. He took his lucky coin with him. He played on the swings, the slide, and the sandbox. But then, something unexpected happened. Tim lost his lucky coin in the sandbox. He was very sad and scared. Tim looked and looked for his lucky coin, but he could not find it. Then, a little girl named Sara came to help him. They looked together, and Sara found the lucky coin! Tim was so happy and thanked Sara. From that day on, Tim and Sara became best friends. Tim learned that friends are even better than lucky coins.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a beautiful baseball in the park. He picked it up and saw a girl named Lisa standing nearby. Tim walked to Lisa and said, "I found this baseball. Do you want to play with me?" Lisa smiled and said, "Yes, I would love to play!" They took turns throwing and catching the baseball. They laughed and had fun. After a while, they saw another boy named Sam sitting alone. Tim and Lisa asked Sam if he wanted to play too. Sam's face lit up and he said, "Yes, thank you!" The three friends played together until the sun went down. They were tired but happy. When it was time to go home, they all smiled and said goodbye. They learned that playing together and sharing made them much happier than playing alone.

One day, a big turtle named Tim and a small rabbit named Ray were friends. They liked to move and play in the park. They were very happy. One day, Tim, the turtle, was slow. He had a worry. He said, "Ray, I am slow. I can't move fast like you. Will you still be my friend?" Ray, the rabbit, smiled and said, "Tim, I like you. You are my friend. We can move slow or fast. It is fun to play with you." Tim was happy. They moved and played together in the park every day. Tim and Ray were best friends. They knew that being slow or fast did not matter. They just liked to have fun together.

Alice and her mom were playing in the park. Alice saw the pond and wanted to go swimming in it. "Can I go swimming mommy?" asked Alice. "No sweetheart" replied mom, "it is too cold for swimming and you might get hurt". Alice was very sad. Just then, her dad called her from the nearby fireplace. "Alice! Come over here!" he said. Alice was so excited. She ran quickly to the fireplace and saw her dad had made a big, warm pool out of big stones around the fireplace. Alice jumped in and started swimming. She was so happy, she was laughing and splashing around the pool. She was having so much fun. Soon, the sun started to set, and Alice was getting tired. She started to swim more slowly and her dad noticed she was getting helpless. He reached in and grabbed Alice, holding her in his arms. Alice felt so safe in her dad's arms. It felt like she was swimming in a pool of love and happiness. She hugged him tightly and fell asleep in his arms.

Once upon a time, in a warm evening, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He had a toy gun that could shoot soft balls. He loved to play with it all day, shooting at trees and walls. As Tim was playing, he saw a big bird up in the sky. He thought it would be fun to shoot the soft ball at the bird. So, he aimed and shot. But something unexpected happened! The bird caught the ball in its beak and started flying towards Tim. Now, Tim was scared. He thought the bird would be angry. But, to his surprise, the bird dropped the ball in front of him and smiled. It wanted to play too! Tim and the bird played together, shooting the ball and catching it. They became best friends and played every warm evening, laughing and having fun.
