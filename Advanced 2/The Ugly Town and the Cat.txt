Once upon a time, there was an ugly town. One day, well in the night, a little boy called John decided to go and explore it. As he stepped in, he heard a dark laugh coming from a corner. Being somewhat scared, he leaned towards the wall to get a better look and he saw a cat. The cat was so bold it started to talk to John. The cat said "Do not be scared, John. Come closer and talk to me." John was hesitant at first, but then he leaned towards the cat. "I think this town is really ugly," John said. The cat just laughed and said "You bet it is! That's why I stay here. You should go before it gets too dark." John thanked the cat and started to walk away. As soon as he was turning away, the cat grabbed his leg and started to laugh. John struggled, but the cat was too strong! John was never seen again. Many people looked for him, but all in vain. No one ever knew what happened to him or why he went to the ugly town. John had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl who wanted to explore the world. One day, she saw a long gate with a keypad at the front. She put in the code and the gate opened. The little girl stepped through the gate and she saw a beautiful garden. She was amazed! She explored the garden and found many flowers, trees and other fun things. Then, the little girl heard someone shouting from the other side of the gate. She walked back and saw a woman who was looking for her. The little girl said, “I just wanted to go exploring. Please don’t be mad!” But the woman was very angry. She said, “I warned you not to leave the house and now you are lost! How could you have done this?” Unfortunately, the little girl had to go with the woman and the gate closed behind them. Although the little girl had a wonderful time, she never got the chance to explore the garden again.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily lived in a house with her mom, dad, and little brother Tom. Lily loved to play with her toys and go on adventures. One day, she wanted to find new toys to play with. She asked her mom where she could find them. Her mom told her there were old toys in the attic. Lily wanted to go to the attic, but her mom said it was dangerous for little kids. Lily thought about it and decided to choose a safe way to get the toys. She asked her dad to help her. Her dad said yes and they went to the attic together. In the attic, they found many old toys. Lily and her dad chose the best ones to bring back down. They played with the toys all day and had lots of fun. Lily was happy she chose the safe way to get the toys and did not go to the dangerous attic alone.

Once upon a time, there was a white fox. The white fox lived in a big forest. He had many friends, like the brown bear and the gray rabbit. They all played together every day. One day, the white fox did something bad. He took the gray rabbit's food. The brown bear saw this and said, "You must be punished!" The white fox felt sad and didn't want to be punished. He ran away very fast. But then, something unexpected happened. The white fox found a big pile of food in the forest. He thought, "I can give this food to the gray rabbit and say sorry." He took the food to his friends. The gray rabbit was happy, and the brown bear said, "You fixed your mistake. No more punishment!" They all played together again, and the white fox learned to be good.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable bird named Bobo. Bobo loved to fly high in the sky. One day, as he was flying, he saw a pretty creek below. Bobo flew down to the creek to play. He met a small fish named Finny. Finny said, "Hi Bobo! Do you want to play with me?" Bobo replied, "Yes, let's play together!" Bobo and Finny played by the creek all day. They splashed in the water and had lots of fun. When it was time to go home, Bobo said, "Bye Finny, I'll come back tomorrow!" And they became the best of friends.
