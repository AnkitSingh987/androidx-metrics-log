Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was excited to have a tasty snack. She picked up a cup of hot cocoa and began to walk outside. Suddenly, she stumbled and the cocoa started to drop. The cocoa spilled everywhere, making a big and dry mess. The little girl felt sad that her cocoa was gone. But, her mom was there to help. She put her hands together and squeezed a big, fluffy cloud. From the cloud, a sweet rain of cocoa began to drop and it filled the little girl's cup. The little girl smiled as she watched the dry cocoa turn into a tasty snack. The cocoa was yummy and she was so happy. She enjoyed her snack and never dropped her cocoa again!

Once upon a time, in an ordinary little house, there was a big fire. It was very hot and bright. The people who lived in the house were scared. One day, a magic bunny appeared. The bunny saw the fire and wanted to help the people. The bunny had a big, soft tail. It used its tail to put out the fire. The people were very happy. After the fire was gone, the house was ordinary again. The people thanked the magic bunny for its help. The bunny smiled and hopped away. The people lived happily in their ordinary house.

Once upon a time, there was a small bug who loved to crawl. One day, he saw a big structure made of sticks. He wanted to climb it, but he was scared. His mom said, "You can do it, just try!" The small bug was stubborn. He did not want to try. He sat and watched other bugs climb the structure. They were having so much fun. The small bug felt sad. One day, the small bug decided to try to crawl up the structure. He was still scared, but he tried anyway. It was hard, but he did it! The small bug learned that trying new things can be fun and good.

One day, a happy cat named Tom found a ball of yarn. He loved to play with yarn. Tom would roll it, chase it, and even shout, "Yay!" when he found it. He was very happy with his yarn. Tom's friend, a little girl named Ann, saw him playing. She asked, "Tom, can I play with you?" Tom nodded his head and shouted, "Yes!" Ann was happy too. They played with the yarn together, laughing and having fun. After a while, they got tired. Tom and Ann sat down and shared the yarn. They were both happy to have played together. They knew they would have more fun days with the yarn.

Once upon a time, there was a sweet rabbit. The rabbit lived in a small house near the woods. The rabbit had many friends, like birds and squirrels. They liked to play together every day. One day, a big cat came to the woods. The cat saw the sweet rabbit and wanted to play. But the rabbit was scared of the big cat. The rabbit tried to ignore the cat and hide in the house. The big cat felt sad and went away. The sweet rabbit's friends told the rabbit that the cat just wanted to play. The rabbit felt bad and went to find the cat. They played together and became friends. The sweet rabbit learned that sometimes, new friends can be big and scary, but still nice.

One day, a dog named Spot was very tired. He had been playing all day in the park with his friends. Spot walked home with his head down, feeling sleepy. When Spot got home, he saw a big tub of water. "I will soak in the water to feel better," he said. Spot jumped into the tub and splashed water all over. The water felt nice and cool on his tired head. Spot's friend, Kitty, came by and saw him in the tub. "Why are you in the water, Spot?" Kitty asked. "I am tired, so I soak in the water to feel better," Spot said. Kitty smiled and said, "You are funny, Spot!" Spot and Kitty laughed, and Spot felt better after his nice soak.
