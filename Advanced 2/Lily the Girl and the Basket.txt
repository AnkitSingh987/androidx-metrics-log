One day, a shy girl named Lily went to the store to buy food. She took a big basket to put all the things she needed in it. Lily felt happy because she was going to make a yummy dinner for her family. As she walked through the store, she put apples, bananas, and bread in her basket. She also picked up milk and some eggs. Lily was very careful not to drop anything. She was almost done when something unexpected happened. A small cat jumped out from behind a box and landed in Lily's basket! Lily was so surprised that she almost dropped her basket. But she didn't, and the cat looked up at her with big, cute eyes. Lily smiled and decided to take the cat home too. That night, Lily's family had a yummy dinner and a new friend to play with.

Once upon a time there was a purple scale. Nobody knew where it came from or what it was for. But one day, a little girl found it and decided to prepare it. She grabbed some paint and painted it all over until it was a shiny new purple. Next, she added some feathers and sparkles so it would be even more sparkly. Then she tugged on it and pulled it until it made a noise. To her delight, it was singing! The little girl loved it so much, she decided to use it every day. She would sit on it and it would make a happy sound. The little girl was so proud of her preparation of the purple scale and was sure she had never made anything so special before.

Once upon a time, there was a lively little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toy cars and watch films on TV. His favorite film was about a big red car that could go very fast. Tim would watch it every day and pretend to be the driver. One day, Tim's mom told him that they needed to value their time together. She said they should do something fun as a family. Tim thought about it and decided that they should all watch his favorite film together. So, Tim, his mom, and his dad sat down to watch the big red car film. They laughed and had a great time. Tim felt happy that they all enjoyed the film together. It was a special day for the family, and Tim learned to value the time they spent together.

Once upon a time, there was a big wall in a small town. The wall was very dull and not pretty. People in the town did not like the wall. They wanted to make it nice and colorful. One day, a little girl named Lily had an idea. She decided to paint the wall with her friends. They painted the wall with many colors. They painted flowers, trees, and animals. The wall became very pretty. The people in the town were very happy. But, there was a mean man who did not like the pretty wall. He wanted the wall to be dull again. He tried to make the wall dull, but he could not. He started to suffer because he could not change the wall. The people in the town saw the mean man suffer. They felt bad for him. They talked to him and showed him that the pretty wall was good for everyone. The mean man learned to love the pretty wall. The town became a happy place. The moral of the story is to be kind and accept changes that make everyone happy.

One day, a boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a girl named Sue. Sue had a toy shock. Tim wanted to play with it. He asked Sue, "Can I play with your shock?" Sue said, "No, it is mine!" Tim thought that was rude. He wanted to be nice, so he put a big smile on his face. He asked Sue again, "Please, can I play with your shock?" Sue saw Tim's smile and thought maybe it was okay. She said, "Okay, Tim. We can share my shock." Tim was very happy. They played with the shock and had lots of fun. After playing, Tim said, "Thank you, Sue. That was fun!" Sue smiled and said, "You are welcome, Tim. Sharing is fun!" They both learned that sharing and being nice is better than being rude. And they became good friends.
