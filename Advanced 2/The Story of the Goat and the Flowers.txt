Once there was a goat and she wanted to play. She hopped around the grassy meadow and stumbled through the trees. The goat was friendly and she wanted to make friends. All of a sudden she noticed a little boy at the edge of the clearing. She was so happy to have someone to play with - she pranced up to the boy and stood on her hind legs, bleating a friendly greeting. The little boy was scared of the goat, but he was friendly too. He waved his hand and said, "hello". The goat wagged her tail and stumbled closer. Soon, the two were running and playing all around the meadow. They talked about the trees, the flowers, and anything else that crossed their minds. The little boy and the friendly goat played the whole day and when the sun set they said goodbye, waving and smiling. They knew they would meet again to play another day.

Once upon a time, there was a rabbit named Bobo. Bobo loved to eat celery. One day, he found a big, green celery in the garden. He was very happy and wanted to share it with his friends. Bobo had a friend named Tina, who was a squirrel. Tina was good at performing tricks. She could jump high and run fast. Bobo asked Tina to perform a trick for their friends. She said, "Yes, I will be careful when I do my trick." Bobo and Tina went to the park with the big celery. All their friends came to watch Tina perform. Tina was very careful as she jumped high and ran fast. The friends clapped and cheered. After the show, they all ate the celery together. They were happy and had a fun day.

One day, a little girl named Lucy went to a big room with her mom. They went there to listen to a man talk about fun things. Lucy was very excited to hear the man talk. When they got there, they found a spot to sit. Lucy's mom said, "We will sit here and listen to the man talk." As the man started to talk, Lucy began to feel tired. She yawned and rubbed her eyes. Her mom noticed and said, "Are you tired, Lucy?" Lucy nodded and said, "Yes, Mommy. I want to go home and sleep." But Lucy's mom told her to wait a little longer. Then, something unexpected happened. A big, colorful bird flew into the room. Everyone was surprised and started to laugh. The bird landed on the man's head and started to dance. The man stopped talking and started to laugh too. Lucy wasn't tired anymore. She clapped her hands and laughed with everyone else. The bird made the day even more fun.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely little girl named Lily. She did not have any friends to play with. One day, she found a big box of paint in her room. Lily was very happy. Lily decided to paint a picture of a friend. She painted a big, round face with a big smile. She painted two eyes, a nose, and a mouth. When she was done, the painted face looked very friendly. "Hi, I am your new friend, Painty!" said the painted face. Lily was so surprised! "Hi, Painty! I am Lily. Let's play together," she said. From that day on, Lily and Painty played together every day, and Lily was not lonely anymore.

Lila and Ben were playing in the park. They liked to feed the pigeons with bread crumbs. They had a small bag of bread that they broke into pieces. "Look, Ben, there are so many pigeons!" Lila said. "They are hungry and want our bread." "Yes, they are. Let's give them some," Ben said. He threw a piece of bread on the ground. A pigeon flew down and ate it. "Wow, that pigeon is fast!" Lila said. She threw another piece of bread. Two pigeons came and fought for it. "Hey, stop fighting, pigeons! There is enough bread for everyone," Ben said. He threw more bread. More pigeons came. They made loud noises and flapped their wings. "Ben, I think we gave them too much bread. The pigeons are increasing!" Lila said. She looked around and saw pigeons everywhere. They covered the grass, the benches, the trees, and the fountain. "Uh-oh, you are right, Lila. The pigeons are too many. They are taking over the park!" Ben said. He grabbed Lila's hand and ran away. The pigeons followed them. "Help, help! The pigeons are chasing us!" Lila and Ben shouted. They ran to their mom, who was sitting on a bench with a book. "Mom, mom! The pigeons are increasing! They want our bread!" Lila and Ben said. "Wow, that's a lot of pigeons," mom said. She looked at the small bag of bread in Lila's hand. "Maybe you should share your bread with them. They are just small birds who are hungry." "OK, mom. We will share our bread with them. But can we go home after that?" Lila and Ben said. "Sure, we can go home after that. But first, let's say hello to the pigeons. They are our friends, too," mom said. Lila and Ben smiled. They went back to the pigeons and gave them the rest of the bread. The pigeons cooed and thanked them. Lila and Ben felt happy. They liked the pigeons, even if they were increasing.
