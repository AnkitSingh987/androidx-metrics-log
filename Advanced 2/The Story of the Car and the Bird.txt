One day, a big red bus was driving down the street. The bus was very hungry. It sighed as it looked for something to eat. It saw a little car and thought, "Maybe I can eat that car." The bus went up to the car and said, "Hi car, I am a hungry bus. Can I eat you?" The car was scared and said, "No, please don't eat me, Mr. Bus. I have a better idea. Let's go find some gas. Gas will make you feel full and not hungry." So, the bus and the car went to find gas. They went to the gas station and the bus started to drink the gas. But then, something unexpected happened. The bus turned into a big, happy bird! The bird said, "Thank you, little car. Now I am not a hungry bus, but a happy bird!" The car smiled and they both went on their way, happy and full of gas.

John and Sarah were best friends. One day they were playing in the garden when John saw some oil on the ground. It was silver and shiny! He thought it was so fascinating he just had to touch it. Sarah tried to tell him not to, but he was a naughty boy and couldn't resist. After John touched the oil he needed to sit down. Sarah wanted to help him so she brought a cloth and wiped the oil off his hands. It was a mess but she helped clean it up. John felt bad for being so naughty and apologised to Sarah. She hugged him and said it was ok. They both sat down on the grass and laughed about it afterwards. John never touched the oil again after that, but him and Sarah stayed the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a chubby bunny who lived in a big yard. The bunny loved hopping around and playing with all of the different bugs, flowers and grass. One day, the bunny was so happy that he forgot to stay in his yard and hopped away to explore. After hopping around for a while, he realized he was very far away from his yard and started feeling scared. He tried and tried to find his way back but couldn't. He missed his yard and started to regret his decision to explore. Finally, the bunny had a stroke of luck and found his way back to his yard. With the yard in sight, the bunny was filled with joy! From then on, whenever the bunny wanted to explore, he reminded himself to never forget where his home was and to never leave without checking twice.

Once upon a time, there was a big store. In this store, there were many toys. One day, a little girl named Lucy went to the store with her mom. Lucy wanted to find a toy that had the number zero on it. Lucy and her mom looked at all the toys. They saw cars, dolls, and balls. But they could not find any toy with a zero. Lucy felt sad. She asked her mom, "Why can't we find a toy with a zero?" Her mom said, "I don't know, Lucy. Let's ask the store man." They went to the store man and asked him about the toy with a zero. The store man smiled and said, "I have a big surprise for you!" He went to the back of the store and came back with a big box. Inside the box, there was a toy train with the number zero on it. Lucy was very happy. She said, "Thank you, store man!" And they all lived happily ever after.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Tiny. Tiny was very small and had a weak voice. He lived in a big house with his best friend, a big dog named Max. One day, Max wanted to introduce Tiny to his other dog friends. They went to the park where all the dogs played. Max said, "Tiny, I want you to meet my friends, Buddy and Daisy." "Hello, Buddy and Daisy," said Tiny with his weak voice. Buddy and Daisy were nice dogs, and they all played together. They ran, jumped, and barked happily. At the end of the day, Tiny, Max, Buddy, and Daisy were very tired. They all went back to their homes. Tiny was happy to have new friends, and his weak voice did not stop him from having fun.
