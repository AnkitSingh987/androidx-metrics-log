Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot lived in a small house with his family. He loved to play and run all day long. One day, Spot saw a different place. It was a big park with many trees and flowers. Spot wanted to go there and play. In the park, Spot met a little bird named Tweetie. Tweetie was sad because she was lost. She could not find her way back to her nest. Spot wanted to help Tweetie. He said, "I will rescue you and take you back to your nest." Spot and Tweetie walked through the park, looking for the nest. They passed a pond with ducks and a big tree with a swing. Finally, they found Tweetie's nest in a tall tree. Spot and Tweetie were very happy. They became good friends and played together in the different place every day. And they lived happily ever after.

One day, a little frog named Jan hopped over to a beautiful harbor. He saw a small boat and admired how colorful it was. He wanted to take a ride on the boat, but didn’t know how to get in it. A voice came from behind Jan, “You can hop on over here, but first you must solve a riddle.” Jan turned and saw a big fish. The fish said, “If you answer my riddle, then you can ride on my boat.” Jan thought for a minute then said, “Yes! I want to try!” The fish smiled and said, “What jumps and hops, is green and has four legs?” Suddenly, Jan realized that he was the answer! He hopped on board the boat and saw a world of colorful fish swimming beneath the sparkling waters. The conflict may have seemed difficult, but Jan was smart enough to solve it and have a great time at the harbor.

Once upon a time, there was a faucet in a big house. The faucet was not like the others. It was original. It could dance! One day, the faucet wanted to show its dance to the other faucets. So, it asked them, "Do you want to see me dance?" The other faucets laughed and said, "Faucets can't dance!" But the original faucet knew it could. It waited for the night when everyone was asleep. Then, it started to dance. It danced so well that the other faucets stopped laughing. They watched the original faucet dance and thought it was fun. But then, something unexpected happened. The big house started to shake! The faucets were scared. They did not know what was going on. The original faucet stopped dancing and the shaking stopped too. The faucets realized that the original faucet's dancing caused the shaking. They all agreed that the original faucet should not dance anymore to keep the big house safe. But they were happy to have seen the original faucet dance and knew that faucets could do special things too.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bob. Bob liked to play outside in the wind. One day, while he was playing, he felt a tickle in his nose. "Achoo!" Bob sneezed. His friend, a little bird named Sue, heard him and flew over. "Are you okay, Bob?" Sue asked. "Yes, I just had to sneeze," Bob said, rubbing his nose. "Let's play in the wind together, Sue!" So, Bob and Sue played in the wind. They laughed and had fun all day long. And whenever Bob had to sneeze, Sue would say, "Bless you, Bob!"

One day, a boy named Tom wanted to climb a big tree. He saw his friend, Sue, near the tree. Sue was doing a fun dance. Tom said, "I want to climb the tree and dance too!" Sue said, "That sounds fun, but be careful not to do a stupid dance up there!" Tom laughed and started to climb the tree. He climbed higher and higher. When he got to the top, he started to dance. Suddenly, a big bird came and joined Tom in his dance. The bird did not think Tom's dance was stupid. The bird thought it was fun! Tom and the bird danced together in the tree. Sue laughed and clapped from the ground. It was a very fun and unexpected day for Tom, Sue, and the big bird.
