Once upon a time, there was a girl named Daisy. Daisy liked to collect things. She had a big box full of toys, rocks, and pretty things she found outside. One day, Daisy met a wealthy man. He had a big house and many pretty things. The man said, "Daisy, I like to collect things too. Do you want to see my collection?" Daisy said, "Yes, please!" Daisy went to the wealthy man's house, and she saw a big room full of pretty things. But, the man had a secret. He was a magician! The man said, "Daisy, I have a surprise for you!" He waved his hand, and all the pretty things came to life! They danced and sang around the room. Daisy was so happy and laughed a lot. From that day on, Daisy and the wealthy man became good friends, and they collected pretty things together.

Mommy was in the kitchen, cooking. She chopped some beef and put it in a pan. "What are you cooking, Mommy?" asked little Johnny. "Beef," said Mommy. Johnny was so excited! He loved beef. Mommy added some sweet spices and melted in some butter. The smell was delicious. Johnny couldn't wait to eat it. So he asked again, "When can I eat the beef?" Mommy said, "Soon, sweetheart. Soon." But all of a sudden, something went wrong. The beef started to burn. Mommy said, "Oh, no!" Johnny was so upset. His favorite food was gone. Mommy said, "I'm sorry, little one. The beef has melted. We can't eat it now."

Once upon a time, there was a little bear named Ben. Ben lived in a big green forest. One day, Ben felt very hot. The sun was shining so bright and warm. Ben wanted to cool down and have fun. Ben had an idea! He would invite his friends to play in the water. He called his friends, the rabbit, the squirrel, and the bird. They were all very happy to come and play with Ben. They all met by the river, where the water was nice and cool. Ben and his friends splashed and played all day long. They were not hot anymore. They had so much fun together. And from that day on, they always played together when it was hot outside.

Once upon a time, in a friendly town, there was a big museum. The museum was full of fun things to look at. Many kids and their families loved to go there and see all the colorful things. One day, a little girl named Lily went to the museum with her mom and dad. They saw big animals, pretty pictures, and even a tall dinosaur. Lily was so happy to be there. She wanted to see everything. Lily's mom and dad wanted to eat, so they went to the museum's food place. They ordered yummy food for all of them. Lily got a tasty sandwich and a juice. After they ate, they went back to see more fun things in the museum. Lily had a great day with her family at the friendly museum.

One day, a brave boy named Tim went on a safari with his dad. They saw big animals like elephants and lions. Tim was very happy to see them. He knew that a big surprise was waiting for him at the end of the safari. As they walked, they heard a loud shout. Tim's dad said, "Do you hear that, Tim? I think someone needs help!" Tim and his dad ran to the shout. They found a little monkey stuck in a tree. The monkey was scared and shouted for help. Tim looked at the tree and knew he had to be brave to save the monkey. "I will help you, little monkey!" said Tim. He climbed the tree and saved the monkey. The monkey was very thankful and gave Tim a big hug. At the end of the safari, Tim's dad showed him the big surprise. It was a new toy monkey! Tim was so happy to have a toy that reminded him of the brave thing he did that day.

Once upon a time, in a small village, there was a brave girl named Lily. She loved to plan and make magic. One day, she heard about a big tree with magic fruit. Lily wanted to find the tree and bring the magic fruit back to her village. Lily asked her friend Tom to go with her. Tom said, "Yes, I will go with you. We will make a plan to find the magic tree." They planned and walked for a long time. They saw a big hill and knew the tree was on the other side. When they got to the tree, they saw the magic fruit. Lily said, "We did it! We found the magic fruit!" They picked the fruit and went back to their village. The village was very happy. Lily and Tom were brave and their plan worked. The magic fruit helped the village and everyone was happy.
