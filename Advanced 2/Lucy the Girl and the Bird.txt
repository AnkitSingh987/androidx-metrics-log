One day, a little girl named Lucy went for a walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to examine it. She found an amazing nest in the tree. The nest was full of pretty eggs. Lucy wanted to show her friends the amazing nest. She climbed the tree to get the nest. But a bird saw Lucy and was very mad. The bird wanted to protect its eggs. The bird and Lucy had a big fight. The bird pecked at Lucy and she fell from the tree. The nest fell too, and the pretty eggs broke. Lucy was sad, and the bird was sad too.

Tom and Lily wanted to decorate their bikes. They had stickers, ribbons, and bells. They put them on their bikes and made them look pretty and fun. They wanted to show their bikes to their friends. They rode to the park and saw Sam and Mia. Sam and Mia had bikes too, but they looked weird. They had license plates on them. "What are those?" Tom asked, pointing at the license plates. "They are license," Sam said. "They say our names and where we live. They make our bikes special and safe." "Why do you need license?" Lily asked. "We don't have license." "You need license if you want to ride on the road," Mia said. "Our moms and dads have license for their cars. They teach us how to ride on the road and follow the rules." Tom and Lily thought that was strange. They liked to ride on the sidewalk and the grass. They did not want to ride on the road with the cars. They thought the license plates were too big and ugly for their bikes. They said goodbye to Sam and Mia and rode back home. They liked their bikes better without license. They liked to decorate them with stickers, ribbons, and bells. They thought their bikes were pretty and fun.

Sam was so excited to receive a letter. He smiled and opened it. Inside, the letter said that he had been chosen for something special. Sam couldn't believe it and he wanted to tell his friends. He ran outside to tell them the news. "I got a letter!" he exclaimed. His friends were thrilled for him, but one of them didn't look that happy. Sam asked why, and the friend said it was because the letter was too deep for Sam to understand. Sam said he could believe it and he wanted the friend to explain it. The friend tried, but it was too complicated for Sam. He couldn't understand what was in the letter. In the end, Sam reluctantly accepted that he was not old enough to understand what was in the letter. He felt sad and disappointed that he didn't get the chance to do something special.

One day, a sad boy named Tim went to a big church with his mom and dad. The church was very big and had many people in it. Tim felt miserable because he wanted to play outside, but he had to stay inside the church. While Tim was sitting, he saw a big basket. The basket was empty, so he decided to fill it with his toys. He put his ball, his car, and his bear in the basket. He felt happy because he could play with his toys in the church. Suddenly, a big wind blew through the church. The wind picked up the basket and all the toys flew out. The toys went everywhere! The people in the church started to laugh and play with the toys. Tim was not miserable anymore. He had fun playing with everyone and made new friends. The church became a happy place for Tim.

One day, a little girl named Sue went on an adventurous walk. She saw a big tree and wanted to climb it. As she started to climb, she saw a long cord hanging from a branch. Sue was very curious and wanted to see what the cord was for. Sue leaned on the tree and pulled the cord. "What do you think will happen?" she asked her friend, Tim. "I don't know," Tim said, "but let's find out!" Sue pulled the cord harder, and something unexpected happened. The tree started to shake, and many colorful balloons came out from the top. Sue and Tim were so happy and surprised. They played with the balloons all day, laughing and having fun. They never knew that their adventurous walk would lead to such a wonderful surprise.
