Once upon a time, there was a human who was very lazy. He didn't like to do anything and he was always sleepy and slow. One day, he decided to search for something interesting to do. He went to different places and looked around, but he couldn't find anything that he liked. He went on and on and eventually he came upon a beautiful park with lots of flowers, trees and animals. He was so excited that he wanted to explore. He spent hours searching for exciting and new things in the park. He was so happy that he found a new place to have fun and be active. The lazy human then went home, with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who loved to skip. One day while skipping she saw a delicious looking cheese near a bush. She was very excited and ran to get it, but when she reached it, she found out it was already taken by a selfish rabbit. She was very sad and started to cry. The rabbit was mean and said, "If you want it, you must give me something first." The little girl was in a hurry and agreed to give him her shoelace. When the rabbit had the shoelace, he laughed and skipped away with the cheese. The little girl was very sad and started to cry again. She had no cheese and her shoelace was gone too. She learned a very important lesson that day: never be selfish like the rabbit was.

One day, two friends went on an adventure. As they looked around the landscape, they spotted a hill that looked like fun. Excitedly, they ran up the hill and stopped at the top. Smiling, they clapped together to celebrate. Suddenly, the sky grew dark. Lightning filled the landscape and the wind was cold. The friends looked at each other in fear. They tried to find shelter, but there was none. The wind grew stronger and the thunder grew louder. One of the friends screamed out over the storm, "Let's go back down the hill!" The other friend shook his head. "No!" he shouted. "Let's stay here and try to make it through!" The friends clapped again, trying to make the storm go away. But it didn't work. The storm only got worse, the lightning struck and one of the friends was struck by the lightning. The other friend frantically clapped for help, but no one came. Eventually, the storm stopped and the sky grew calm. The friend sadly looked down the hill and saw their best friend, lying still. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue had a yellow toy phone. She loved to play with her phone all day. One day, Sue saw a big bug. The bug was trying to get up. The bug was struggling. Sue wanted to help the bug. She picked up her yellow phone and said, "Hello, can you help the bug?" But no one answered. Sue was sad. She wanted to help the bug, but she didn't know how. Then, a kind boy named Tom came. He saw Sue and the bug. Tom said, "Sue, we can help the bug together!" Tom and Sue helped the bug up. The bug was happy. Sue learned that helping others is good, and friends can help too.

Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily went on a walk with her dog, Max. They walked and walked until they discovered a big, shiny rock. The rock was silver and sparkled in the sun. Lily was very happy and picked up the rock. Lily took the rock home and showed it to her mom. "Look, Mommy! I found a silver rock!" she said. Her mom looked at the rock and said, "Oh, that's very pretty, Lily. But we can't keep it. It might belong to someone else." Lily was upset. She didn't want to give the rock away. But she knew her mom was right. So, she and Max went back to where they found the rock. They waited and waited. Soon, a little boy came looking for his rock. He was very happy to see it. "Thank you for finding my rock!" he said. Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome!"
