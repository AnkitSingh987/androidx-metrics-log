Once upon a time there were two friends, Eric and Jack. They were different ages and they did different things. One day they decided to go to the park together. When they arrived at the park, they decided to swing first. Eric sat on the swing and started to swing. Jack watched and said, "Oh wow, that looks fun!" He also wanted to swing, so he sat next to Eric on the seat. Then they decided to get some juice. They ran to the nearest shop to buy juice. The shopkeeper gave them two different bottles of juice. They were different colours. Eric and Jack thought the juice was yummy and fun! The friends went back to the park and sat on the seat. They drank their juice and enjoyed their different flavours. They laughed and talked for hours until it was time to go home. Both Eric and Jack had a great day at the park and vowed to come again soon.

Once upon a time, a little dog named Spot felt very guilty. He had taken his friend's toy. He did not want his friend to worry. So, Spot thought of a way to fix it. Spot wanted to send the toy back to his friend. He put the toy in a box and went to the post office. But on the way, something unexpected happened. A big wind came and blew the box away! Spot was sad and worried. But then, the wind blew the box right back to his friend's house! Spot's friend found the toy and was happy. Spot felt much better and promised to never take a toy again.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Amy. Amy did not like to eat her broccoli. One day, her mom made a big plate of broccoli for dinner. Amy looked at the broccoli and said, "I don't want to eat that!" Her mom said, "Let's play a game. You have to guess what I am thinking. If you guess right, you don't have to eat the broccoli. But if you guess wrong, you have to eat it all." Amy thought for a moment and then said, "I guess you are thinking about a big, red ball." Her mom smiled and said, "No, I was thinking about a big, green tree. Now you have to eat the broccoli." Amy was not glad, but she ate the broccoli anyway. After she ate the broccoli, Amy felt strong and happy. She realized that eating broccoli wasn't so bad after all. From that day on, Amy was glad to eat her broccoli and became a very healthy and strong girl.

Once upon a time, there was a lonely little nail. It lived in a big box with other nails. One day, the nail heard a boy say, "I want to build a house for my toy dog." The lonely nail was excited and thought, "Maybe the boy will use me!" The boy started to build the house. He picked up the lonely nail and said, "You are just right!" The nail was so happy. But then, the boy dropped the nail on the ground. The nail started to panic. "Oh no! What if the boy can't find me?" Just then, the boy's mom came over. She saw the lonely nail and said, "Here you are! I found the nail!" The boy picked up the nail and said, "Thank you, Mom!" He used the nail to finish the toy dog's house. The lonely nail was not lonely anymore. It was now a part of a beautiful house for the toy dog. The nail felt proud and happy to have a new home and a new friend.

Once there was a little girl named True. She was three years old and the bossiest girl around. Everywhere she went, she was in charge. One day True imagined that she was a princess. She put on her mum’s old dress, grabbed a stick for a magic wand and went trotting round the garden. True watched as the flowers and leaves moved around without her touching them. She imagined that she could fly up in the sky and touch the clouds. True had such a great time imagining the impossible that the day flew by. As the sun began to set, True came back down to earth and said goodbye to her magical kingdom until tomorrow.
