Once upon a time, there was a green frog. The frog liked to hop and play all day. One day, he saw a band of bugs making music. The frog wanted to join the band and make music too. The frog tried to sing, but he could not manage to make a nice sound. The bugs did not want him to sing with them. The frog felt sad and sat by the water. Then, the frog had an idea. He could make music by splashing the water with his feet! The frog managed to make a nice sound. The bugs liked the sound and let the frog join the band. They all played music together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time there was a very adorable gorilla named Henry. He lived in the jungle with his family. Every day, he and his family went to the river to have a drink and play. One day, when Henry and his family were walking to the river, Henry stopped and said, "Wait, what's that?" He saw a bright red bird perched on a branch. As he looked at it he thought, "That is so pretty." His family looked at Henry curiously and said, "Henry, why do you want to remain here and look at the bird?" Henry smiled and said, "Because it's so adorable. I want to stay here to admire it." His family smiled and they all remained in the spot admiring the bird together. They had a wonderful time looking at the beautiful bird. After a while, Henry said goodbye to the bird and he and his family went on their way to the river. The end.

Once upon a time, Jack and Jill were playing outside. As they were running around, Jack managed to lose his toy iron. He screamed in despair. "Oh no! I lost my iron!" he cried. Jill said, "Don't worry, I'll help you find it." The two of them searched everywhere, but they couldn’t find the iron. It was getting late, so they decided to head back home. Jack sadly said, "My iron is gone." Jill replied, "Maybe someone found it. I'll check the empty place near the park to see if it's there." The two of them ran to the empty space by the park. To both of their surprise, Jack's toy iron was there. Joyful and relieved, Jack hugged his Iron and said, "Thank you Jill for helping me find my lost Iron!" Then, Jack and Jill happily played with Jack's toy iron for the rest of the evening.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was a lucky boy, because he had a magic tree in his yard. This tree had a special symbol on it. The symbol was a big, red star. One day, Tim went to the tree and saw the symbol. He thought it was very pretty. Tim wanted to see the symbol better, so he leaned on the tree. When he leaned on the tree, something magical happened! The tree started to shake, and lots of toys fell down from the branches. Tim was so happy! He played with the toys all day long. From that day on, Tim knew that he was a very lucky boy with a very special tree.

Once upon a time, there was a sleepy bunny named Bob. Bob liked to hop and play all day. One day, Bob was very sleepy, but he wanted to play with his friends. Bob's friends were at the park. They were playing on the swings and slides. Bob had to hurry to join them. So, he hopped and hopped as fast as he could. When he got to the park, he saw an empty seat on the swings. Bob hopped onto the seat and started to swing. He was still very sleepy, but he loved playing with his friends. They all laughed and had fun together. Soon, it was time for Bob to go home. Bob said goodbye to his friends and hopped back to his house. He was very tired from all the fun. When he got home, he went to his cozy bed and fell asleep. Bob had a big smile on his face, happy from his day at the park.
