Once upon a time, a little girl named Lily had a delicate basket. She loved her basket very much. One sunny day, Lily decided to take her basket outside to play. While she was playing, she met a friendly dog named Max. Max looked at Lily's basket and said, "Can I play too?" Lily thought for a moment and said, "Yes, but be careful. My basket is delicate." Together, they played with the basket. They took turns carrying it and being gentle. Lily and Max had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they were both very happy. And Lily's delicate basket was still safe and sound.

Billy and his dad were going on a walk around the park. "We have a surprise for you," said dad. Billy was excited. He liked surprises. They kept walking until they got to a huge tree. "Do you see something?" asked dad. Billy looked around. He saw a lunch bag sitting under the tree. Dad smiled. "Let's eat our lunch here," he said. Billy started to open the bag when a bright yellow bird flew out! Dad had a gun and shoot the bird. Billy was very surprised. Dad had been setting him up for a huge surprise. He had been preparing lunch so that they could go out and shoot the bird!

Once upon a time, there was a cute little dog named Spot. Spot liked to play with his friends in the park. One day, while playing, he saw a tiny bug. The bug was in a small cell on a leaf. "Hi, bug! Can I join you in your cell?" Spot asked. The bug looked up and said, "Yes, you can join me, but you are too big. Let's play outside instead." So, Spot and the bug played together outside the cell. They had a lot of fun and became best friends. And every day, Spot would visit the bug in the park and they would play together.

Once upon a time, there was a noisy cop who loved to hang around the town. Everyone knew him and he was quite popular. One day, all the kids were playing together in the park, when the noisy cop showed up. He came over to them and told them he wanted to play a game, but didn't say what kind. The kids were all very excited and eager to know what it was. The cop grabbed a rope and hung it up on a tree. He told the kids that if any were brave enough to jump onto the rope, he would give them a special prize. After some hesitation, one of the little boys stepped forward. He closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and then leaped onto the rope. The cop smiled, and told the other kids that he had really wanted to play soccer but nobody wanted to join him. But since the little boy had been brave enough to try something new, he gave him the special prize. The other kids all cheered, and it meant the noisy cop wasn't so noisy anymore!

Lily and Ben were friends. They liked to play in the park. One day, they saw a big truck with many lollipops. The truck man said, "Who wants a lollipop? Come and get one!" Lily and Ben wanted a lollipop. They ran to the truck. But they did not look where they were going. They were careless. They bumped into a dog. The dog barked. Lily and Ben were scared. They dropped their lollipops. "Oh no! Our lollipops!" Lily cried. "Bad dog! Go away!" Ben shouted. The dog did not go away. He picked up their lollipops with his mouth. He ran away with them. "Hey! Come back! Those are ours!" Lily and Ben chased the dog. But the dog was faster. He ran to his owner. He gave him the lollipops. "Good boy! You found some treats!" The owner said. He patted the dog. Lily and Ben were sad. They lost their lollipops. They went back to the park. They saw the truck man. He was still giving out lollipops. "Hey, kids. Do you want another lollipop?" He asked. "Yes, please!" Lily and Ben said. The truck man gave them each a new lollipop. They thanked him. "Be careful this time. Don't run into any dogs." He said. Lily and Ben nodded. They smiled. They licked their lollipops. They were happy. They learned to be more careful.
