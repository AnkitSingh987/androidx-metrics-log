It was a bright day and Sarah was so excited - it was time to go in the big van! She went out of the house and her mum made sure she was wearing her seatbelt. She waved good-bye to mum and settled down in the van. The engine purred and the tires rolled on the road. As they drove along, Sarah looked out of the window. She saw lots of people walking, playing and running. Sarah wanted to join in the fun and asked the driver to stop but her mum said to be careful. When the van finally arrived at their destination, Sarah saw a river. She was so excited and wanted to jump right in. But her mum said to resist. Instead, Sarah waited and looked on as the driver got out and put a boat in the river. Sarah sighed in disappointment, but she was very careful while they were out. As they got back in the van, she put on her seatbelt and began their journey home.

Once upon a time, there was a goat. The goat was very angry. One day, the goat met a boy. The boy said, "Why are you angry, goat?" The goat said, "I am angry because I do not feel value." The boy thought and thought. Then, he had an idea. "I will show you value, goat." The boy and the goat went to a big field. There were many flowers. The goat ate the flowers. The flowers made the goat happy. The boy said, "See, goat? You have value. You help the flowers grow by eating them." Now, the goat was not angry. The goat and the boy were friends. They learned that everyone has value, even when it is hard to see.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a new toy in his room. He was so happy and excited to play with it. The toy was a small car that could move on its own. Tim would push the car and watch it go. It was so much fun! Tim's friend, Sue, came over to play. Sue saw the new toy and said, "I want to play with it too!" Tim didn't want to share, but Sue was his friend. So, he let her play with the car. Sue pushed the car, and it went very fast. They both laughed and had a good time. After a while, the car stopped working. Tim and Sue were sad. They didn't know what to do. Tim's dad came into the room and saw the broken toy. He said, "I can fix it!" He took the car and worked on it. In a few minutes, the car was working again. Tim and Sue were so happy and surprised. They thanked Tim's dad and continued to play with the car, taking turns and sharing the fun.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. Tom was very excited for the big party tonight. There would be fireworks in the sky. He had to remember to take his fireworks with him. But Tom would sometimes forget things. Tom's mom told him, "Don't forget your fireworks, or you'll be frustrated." Tom nodded his head and said, "I won't forget, Mom!" He ran to play with his toys. But as he played, he thought about the bright lights and loud sounds. He was so excited! Later, it was time to go to the party. Tom and his mom went to the park. They saw lots of friends and yummy food. But when it was time for the fireworks, Tom felt sad. He forgot his fireworks at home. He was very frustrated, just like his mom said. The party was not fun for Tom anymore, and he wished he had remembered his fireworks.

Once upon a time there was a little boy who had lots of dreams. One day, he saw a novel hanging on his wall. He became very excited and decided to read the novel. Every day, he slowly read the novel, page by page. Although it was tiring, he never gave up and kept reading until he finished it. When he was done, he found that the novel was not only interesting but full of moral lessons. He learned that it’s important to try one’s best and never give up. He was very proud of himself for having read the novel and for learning something special from it. So he went to hang the novel back on the wall with a big smile on his face. The little boy now remembered always to hang onto his dreams and never give up.
