One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a wide tree with a big hole in it. He looked inside and found a big bag of candy. Tim was very happy and wanted to eat all the candy. Tim's mom saw him with the candy and said, "Tim, you can eat some candy, but you must stay near me. And remember, sharing is good." Tim nodded and started to eat the candy. He saw a girl named Sue, who was sad because she had no candy. Tim thought about what his mom said, and he gave Sue some of his candy. Sue was very happy and said, "Thank you, Tim!" Then, a big wind came and blew the bag of candy away! Tim and Sue were sad, but they still had the candy they were holding. They decided to share the candy with all the kids in the park. Everyone was happy and had fun. The wind came back and brought the bag of candy to Tim and Sue. They learned that sharing is good, and when you share, good things can happen.

One day, a boy named Tim went to play in the garden. He saw his friend, Sam, playing with a hose. Sam was having fun and spraying water everywhere. The garden was very wet and muddy. Tim wanted to play too, but he didn't want to get filthy. Tim said, "Sam, can I play with the hose too? But I don't want to get dirty." Sam thought for a moment and then said, "Okay, you can play, but be careful not to get muddy." Tim and Sam played with the hose, spraying water on the plants and flowers. But then, Tim accidentally sprayed water on Sam's face. Sam got angry and said, "You made me wet, Tim! Now I'm filthy too!" Tim felt bad and said, "I'm sorry, Sam. I didn't mean to." Sam was still upset, but he decided to forgive Tim. They continued to play with the hose, being more careful this time. They laughed and had fun, even though they were both filthy. In the end, they went inside to clean up and promised to play in the garden again soon.

Once upon a time, there was a patient fisherman named Tom. He lived near a big blue lake. Every day, he went to the lake to catch fish. One sunny day, Tom met a little boy named Tim. Tim asked, "Can I fish with you?" Tom smiled and said, "Yes, you can." So, they both sat by the lake with their fishing poles. Tom showed Tim how to pour little fish food into the water. They waited and waited. After a while, they caught some fish. They were both very happy. They went home and told everyone about their fun day at the lake.

Once upon a time, in a bright sunny garden, there was a long vine. The vine had many big, green leaves, and it lived near a tall tree. One day, a little bird came to the vine and said, "Hello, Vine! Can I sit on you?" The vine was happy and said, "Yes, little bird! I will let you sit on me." So, the little bird sat on the vine, and they became good friends. Every day, the little bird came to the vine, and they talked and played. They watched the sun go down and the moon come up. They were happy together in their bright, sunny garden. And they were the best of friends forever.

Once upon a time, there was a dependable dog named Buddy. Buddy loved to hurry and run around. One day, Buddy saw his friend Cat doing yoga in the park. Buddy wanted to try yoga too. Buddy and Cat did yoga together. They stretched and bent their bodies. Buddy felt happy and calm. After yoga, Buddy and Cat went to play. They ran and jumped together. Buddy was glad he tried yoga with his dependable friend. They had a fun day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim was sad and miserable because he lost his toy on the subway. He looked everywhere for it, but he could not find it. He was so sad that he started to cry. One day, Tim's friend, Sarah, saw him crying. She asked, "Why are you sad, Tim?" Tim told her about his lost toy on the subway. Sarah wanted to help her friend, so they went back to the subway to look for the toy together. As they were looking, Sarah saw something under a seat. She used her finger to point at it and said, "Tim, look! Is that your toy?" Tim looked and saw his toy! He was so happy that he hugged Sarah. From that day on, they became best friends. The moral of the story is that helping others can bring happiness and friendship.
