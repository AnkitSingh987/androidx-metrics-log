Once upon a time, there was a unique bird named Bob. Bob was very friendly and loved to share with others. One day, Bob met a big cat named Tom. Tom was very hungry and was looking for food. Bob said, "Hello, Tom! I can offer you some pepper to eat." Tom thought about it and said, "Okay, I will try the pepper." Bob gave Tom the pepper, and Tom took a bite. But Tom did not like the taste of the pepper. It was too spicy for him. Tom was still hungry, so he thought about eating Bob. Bob did not know this, and he was still being friendly. Then, Tom jumped and caught Bob in his big paws. Tom ate Bob, and Bob was gone. It was a sad day for the unique bird who just wanted to share.

Ben was excited when he saw the new toy he got for his birthday. It was a modern journal that he could write fun stories in. He had lots of ideas in his head. He got out his special markers and opened the journal. Then he started to draw a picture. He was focusing so hard he didn't realize how much time had passed. Soon it was time for lunch. His mom shouted, "Lunchtime!" Ben was so surprised he dropped his markers and journal. He quickly got up and ran to the kitchen. When lunch was over, he ran back to his room. He wanted to finish the picture he was drawing. He knew it was going to be something amazing. He couldn't wait to show his friends.

Anna likes to play mail. She has a big bag of letters and cards that she makes with paper and crayons. She wears a blue uniform with a hat and a badge. She pretends to be a mail person who delivers mail to her friends and family. One day, she decides to mail a card to her grandma. She writes "I love you" and draws a heart on the card. She puts it in an envelope and sticks a sticker on it. She puts the card in her bag and goes outside. She walks to her grandma's house, which is next door. She knocks on the door and says, "Mail for you, grandma!" Grandma opens the door and smiles. She sees Anna in her uniform and says, "What a brilliant mail person you are, Anna! Thank you for the mail!" Anna gives her the card and hugs her. Grandma opens the card and reads it. She says, "This is the most beautiful card I ever got, Anna! I love you too!" She kisses Anna and gives her a cookie. Anna is very happy. She says, "You're welcome, grandma! I like to play mail. It's fun!" Grandma says, "It is fun, Anna. And you're very good at it. Maybe you can be a real mail person when you grow up." Anna says, "Maybe I can, grandma. Maybe I can."

One day, a little girl named Emma found some pretty jewelry in her mom's room. She wanted to wear it, but she was worried. She did not know if it was okay to wear her mom's jewelry. So, Emma went to find her mom to ask her. "Mom," Emma said, "Can I wear your pretty jewelry?" Emma's mom looked at her and smiled. "I will explain," she said. "This jewelry is very special to me. It is not for playing, but I have an idea." Emma's mom took her to a store. They looked at all the pretty things. Suddenly, Emma's mom picked up a small box. Inside the box, there was a beautiful necklace just for Emma. Emma was so happy! She did not expect to get her own jewelry. Now, Emma had her own pretty necklace to wear. She was not worried anymore. She knew that her mom's jewelry was special, and now she had her own special jewelry too. Emma and her mom went home, both wearing their special necklaces, and they had a great day together.

Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot was a very happy dog. He loved to run and play in the park with his friends. One day, Spot saw a big dog with a beautiful, shiny coat. Spot started to admire the big dog. He wanted to be just like the big dog one day. He watched the big dog play and have fun with the other dogs in the park. Spot looked at his own paw and thought about how he could be like the big dog. He decided to practice running and jumping every day. As Spot grew, he became strong and fast like the big dog. All the other dogs admired Spot, and he was very happy.
