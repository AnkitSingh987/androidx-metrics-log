One day, a powerful prince was taking a walk in the forest. Suddenly, he heard a sound coming from behind a veil of leaves. The prince slowly stepped towards the sound. He slowly peered through the veil and saw a little girl, stirring a pot with a big stick. "What are you doing?" asked the prince. The girl smiled. "I'm making soup," she said. The prince smiled back. "It smells really nice," he said. The girl nodded. She was proud of her soup. The prince was very impressed. "You are very powerful," he said. The girl's eyes sparkled. "Thank you," she said. The prince laughed and said goodbye. He continued on his walk, with a big smile on his face.

Once upon a time there was a unique sack. It was a very special sack. Every day the sack made a strange sound. No one could understand what it meant. Every day, the sack seemed to know when it was time for lunch. Before lunch was ready, the sack would make a funny noise. But still, no one could understand what it meant. Then one day, a little three year old girl heard the noise. She was excited and curious. She decided she would investigate and find out what the sack meant. The little girl listened carefully and slowly figured out what the sack was trying to tell her. Its noise was a request to carry it to lunch with her! The girl smiled and happily grabbed the sack and took it with her to lunch. She had solved the mystery and understood the unique sack's message.

Once upon a time, there was a gray bunny named Bob. Bob liked to number his carrots. He counted one, two, three carrots every day. Bob had a friend named Tim, who was a competitive turtle. They liked to play together. One day, Bob and Tim had a race. They wanted to see who was faster. Bob hopped and Tim crawled. They both tried their best. But something unexpected happened. A big bird came and took Bob away! Tim was sad and worried about his friend Bob. But then, the big bird came back and put Bob down safely. It turned out, the bird just wanted to help Bob win the race. Now, Bob and Tim knew that winning wasn't the most important thing. They were just happy to be together and play.

One day, a nice boy named Tim and a nosy girl named Sue were playing in the park. Tim had a big glass of juice. Sue wanted some juice too, but she did not have any. Tim knew how to share, so he said, "Sue, let's share my juice!" Sue was very happy. They both drank from the big glass of juice and were having fun. But then, something unexpected happened. A small cat jumped up and knocked the glass over. The juice spilled all over the ground. Tim and Sue were sad, but they learned that sharing can be fun, even if it does not always go as planned. They laughed and played together in the park, now as good friends.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to dress in her favorite clothes. One day, she put on her pretty pink dress and went to the library with her mommy. Lily felt troubled because she could not find her favorite book. She looked and looked but it was not there. Then, she saw a little boy named Tim. He was also troubled because he could not find his favorite book. Lily said to Tim, "Let's help each other find our books!" Tim agreed and they looked together. Soon, they both found their favorite books and felt happy. They sat down and read their books with big smiles on their faces.

One day, a big fast car was going down the road. It was going too fast. A little bird saw the car and wanted to warn the car to slow down. The little bird was delicate and small, but it was also very brave. The little bird flew to the car and said, "Car, you are going too fast! Slow down or something bad will happen!" The car did not listen to the little bird. The car liked its speed and did not want to slow down. Then, something unexpected happened. The car saw a big wall in front of it. The car tried to stop, but it was going too fast. The car hit the wall and broke into pieces. The little bird was sad because it tried to warn the car, but the car did not listen.
