One day, a little boy named Tim went to the park. He saw a big alligator. The alligator was dizzy. Tim asked, "Mr. Alligator, why are you dizzy?" The alligator said, "I am dizzy because I spun around too much. I was trying to catch my tail." Tim laughed and said, "You should not spin so much. It will make you feel sick." Tim and the alligator played in the park. They went on the swings and down the slide. The alligator did not spin anymore. He felt better. Tim and the alligator became good friends. They had a fun day at the park.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lucy. She had a toy microphone that she loved to sing with. But she had a thin cat named Whiskers that she did not like. Lucy would say, "I hate Whiskers!" because he always knocked over her toys. One day, Lucy's mommy told her they were going to a big party. Lucy was very excited and wanted to bring her microphone to sing for everyone. She put the microphone in her bag and went to the party with her mommy. At the party, Lucy started to sing with her microphone, but she could not find it. She looked everywhere and started to cry. Then, she saw Whiskers with the microphone in his mouth. Whiskers had brought it to Lucy! She smiled and said, "Thank you, Whiskers! I don't hate you anymore!" From that day on, Lucy and Whiskers were the best of friends.

Once upon a time, there was a kind and generous bee. The bee wanted to help the animals in the forest, so it flew around looking for something special to share with them. It spotted a big, round mushroom. The bee flew over to it and carefully burried it in the ground. Then the bee flew away. All of the animals in the forest were very happy, because they now had something special to eat. They ate the mushroom and it made them feel better. The bee was happy too, knowing that it had done something generous for the animals. The kind bee flew off into the sunset, proud of its work. The end.

Anna and Ben are friends. They like to go to the museum with their moms. The museum has many things to see and learn. Anna likes the animals. Ben likes the cars. One day, they go to the museum again. They see a big sign that says "Do not touch". Anna and Ben are curious. They want to touch the things behind the sign. They sneak away from their moms and go to the sign. They see a shiny car. It looks like a toy. Ben wants to play with it. He reaches for the car. Anna says, "No, Ben, do not touch. The sign says so. We can get in trouble." But Ben does not listen. He touches the car. He pushes a button. The car makes a loud noise. It moves. It bumps into a wall. The wall breaks. The alarm goes off. Anna and Ben are scared. They run away. They lose their moms. They do not know where to go. They cry. A nice man sees them. He works at the museum. He asks them, "What is wrong? Why are you crying?" Anna and Ben tell him what happened. They say they are sorry. They say they want their moms. The nice man says, "It is okay. You made a mistake. But you told the truth. That is good. I will help you find your moms. Come with me." He takes them to the front desk. He calls their moms. Their moms come. They hug Anna and Ben. They are happy to see them. They are not angry. They say, "We are glad you are safe. We love you. But you should not touch things that are not yours. You should listen to the signs. You should stay with us. That is how you respect the museum and yourself." Anna and Ben say, "We are sorry, moms. We learned our lesson. We will not do it again. We will be loyal to you and the museum." The end. Moral value: The story teaches the value of honesty, respect, and loyalty. It shows that curiosity is good, but it should not lead to disobedience or harm. It also shows that mistakes can be forgiven if one admits them and learns from them.
