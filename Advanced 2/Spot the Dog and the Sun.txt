Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play in the park with his friend, a little girl named Sue. One day, Sue had to go away for a long time. Sue was sad, and Spot was sad too. Sue made a promise to Spot that she would come back soon. Spot said he would stay at the park and wait for her. Days went by, and Spot stayed at the park. He played with other kids, but he missed Sue. The sun was hot, and the rain was wet, but Spot did not leave. He wanted to be a good friend and keep his promise to stay. He thought it was only fair to wait for Sue. But one day, a big storm came. The wind blew hard, and the rain was very heavy. Spot tried to stay at the park, but the storm was too strong. He got scared and ran away. Spot could not keep his promise to stay. He felt very sad and lost. The storm took away his chance to see Sue again, and the story had a bad ending.

Once upon a time, there was a girl named Maria. Every day Maria liked to take her special box with her everywhere she went. Inside the box was a very special pin - it was Maria's favourite thing ever! One day, Maria was about to put her pin and box away when something strange happened. The pin started to shine and then it opened! Maria was very surprised and she looked inside. Inside was something so normal that it made Maria smile! Maria decided to keep the box closed and she asked her mom and dad if she could keep the pin forever. They said yes and Maria felt very happy. From then on, every day Maria would take out her pin and admire it. She loved it so much! She knew that if she kept the box closed she would always have her special pin with her - it made her feel really normal inside.

Once upon a time, there was a smart girl named Lily. She liked to help her mom in the kitchen. One day, her mom asked her to put the dishes in the dishwasher. Lily was happy to help. As Lily was putting the dishes in the dishwasher, she saw her favorite toy, a small bear. She wanted to play with the bear, but she knew she had to finish her job first. So, she put the bear on the counter. When Lily was done, she went to grab her bear. But, the bear was stuck under a cup. Lily tried to pull the bear out, but the bear's arm started to tear. She felt sad and cried. Her mom heard Lily cry and came to see what was wrong. Lily said, "My bear's arm is tearing!" Her mom hugged her and said, "Don't worry, I will fix it for you." Lily felt better and learned that it is important to be careful with her toys.

Once upon a time, in a big pink house, there lived a cat and a dog. The cat was very nice and had a big smile. The dog was not so nice and liked to scare the cat. They played together in the big pink house every day. One day, the cat and the dog saw a big box. They were very excited to see what was inside. The cat smiled and the dog stopped scaring the cat. They both opened the big box together. Inside the box, they found a big pink ball. The cat and the dog were very happy to play with the ball. But then, something unexpected happened. The big pink ball popped! The cat and the dog were very sad. They could not play with the big pink ball anymore.

Joey was playing basketball with his friends. He was trying to get the ball, but it was too heavy. His friends laughed at him, but Joey didn't give up. He bent down and tried with all his might to lift the basketball, but it wouldn't move. Just then, Joey's mom came out and said, "Don't worry, Joey. Let me help you." She picked up the basketball with one hand and said, "See, it's not too heavy for me." Everyone was amazed. Joey's mom then said, "Joey, if you never give up and don't get discouraged, you can do anything you put your mind to. You just have to try and keep a positive attitude." Joey smiled and nodded. He had learned an important lesson that day. With help from his mom, Joey was able to close the door on his fear of the heavy basketball.
