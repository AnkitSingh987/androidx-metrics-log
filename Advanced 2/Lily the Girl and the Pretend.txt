Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play in her chamber with her toy kitchen. One day, she decided to make a spicy pretend soup for her mommy and daddy. She mixed pretend peppers, pretend onions, and pretend tomatoes in her toy pot. When her mommy and daddy came home, they saw her playing in her chamber. Lily said, "I made a spicy soup for you!" Her mommy and daddy smiled and pretended to eat the soup. They said it was very yummy and they would pay her with a big hug. But then, something unexpected happened! When her mommy and daddy hugged her, they felt very warm. It was like the spicy soup was real! They all laughed and wondered how it happened. It was a fun and magical day for Lily and her family.

Once upon a time, there was a charming little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his ball. One day, Max saw a big hole in the ground. He thought it would be fun to play there. Max said to his friend, Cat, "Let's play in the big hole!" Cat replied, "Okay, Max! That sounds fun!" So Max and Cat went to play in the big hole. They played and played, having lots of fun. But then, something unexpected happened. Max tried to fit his ball into the hole, but it was too small. The ball got stuck! Max and Cat tried to get the ball out, but they couldn't. They were sad. Just then, a big friendly bird saw them and asked, "What's wrong?" Max and Cat told the bird about the stuck ball. The bird reached in with its long beak and got the ball out! Max and Cat were so happy, and they thanked the bird. From that day on, they all played together and had lots of fun.

Once upon a time, there was a big tree. It had many branches and leaves. It gave fair shade to all the animals in the forest. One day, a little squirrel named Sammy came to the tree to rest. Sammy said, "I like this tree. It's so cool and nice. I want to sleep here." So, he lay down under the tree and closed his eyes. But then, a loud noise disturbed him. He woke up and saw a big elephant named Ellie. Ellie said, "I'm sorry, Sammy. I didn't mean to disturb you." Sammy was not mad. He said, "It's okay, Ellie. We can both rest under the tree." So, they both lay down and closed their eyes. Just then, the tree started to shake! The leaves fell down, and the shade was gone. Sammy and Ellie opened their eyes and saw a big giraffe named Gerry. He was eating the leaves from the tree. Ellie said, "Gerry, you took our shade!" Gerry felt bad and said, "I'm sorry. I was hungry. Let's find another tree together." So, Sammy, Ellie, and Gerry went to find another tree with fair shade where they could all rest and be friends.

Once upon a time there was a little girl who was feeling very sick. She coughed, sneezed and felt very cold. Her mommy said it was time for the thermometer. The little girl said, "Please?" Her mommy said "Yes, please take this thermometer and put it under your tongue." The little girl did as she was told. After what felt like a very long time, the thermometer beeped. Her mommy looked at the thermometer and said, "Oh, I'm sorry sweetheart, but you have a fever. We need to make you feel better." Her mommy went to the kitchen and heated up some water. She brought a glass of warm water and a soft towel to bed with the little girl. The little girl rested and sipped the warm water while her mommy put the soft towel on her forehead. The little girl felt much better but still a bit icy. So her mommy put layers of blankets around her to keep her warm and cozy. After some more warm drinks and snuggles, the little girl eventually felt all better.

One day, a little boy named Tim found a big purple ball. He loved to kick it around the yard. His friend, Sue, came over to play. She saw the purple ball and wanted to kick it too. They took turns kicking the ball, laughing and having fun. But then, Tim accidentally kicked the ball too hard. It flew into the air and hit a mug on the table. The mug fell and broke into pieces. Tim and Sue looked at each other, both feeling sad and scared. Tim said, "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to break the mug." Sue said, "It's okay, we can tell your mom and help clean it up." They went inside to tell Tim's mom about the broken mug. She wasn't happy, but she knew it was an accident. Tim and Sue helped clean up the mess and promised to be more careful when they played.
