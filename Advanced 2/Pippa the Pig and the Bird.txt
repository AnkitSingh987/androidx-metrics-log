Once upon a time, there was a kind pig named Pippa. Pippa loved to travel and see new things. One day, Pippa met a little bird named Benny. Benny was very small and could not fly very well. Pippa said to Benny, "Do you want to travel with me? We can see new things together!" Benny was happy and said, "Yes, I would love to go with you!" So, Pippa and Benny went on a trip. They saw big trees, pretty flowers, and a big, blue lake. They played together and had a lot of fun. At the end of the day, they were very tired but happy. Pippa and Benny were the best of friends and they loved to travel together.

One day, a cat and a dog were in the kitchen. They saw a big freezer. They wanted to see what was inside. The cat said, "Let's be careful when we open it." The dog agreed and said, "Yes, we don't want to fight with the freezer." They both opened the freezer very carefully. They saw a lot of ice and some food. They were very happy to find food inside. But then, something unexpected happened. A small penguin jumped out of the freezer! The cat and the dog were very surprised. The penguin said, "Thank you for letting me out! I was stuck in there!" The cat, the dog, and the penguin all became good friends and played together.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tom. He loved to play with his toy cars. One day, Tom's mom bought him a new toy car. It was very quick and cheap. Tom was very happy. Tom played with his new car all day. He liked to race it with his other cars. He noticed that the new car was much quicker than the others. He thought it would be fun to find a match for his new car. Tom looked through his toy box and found a car that looked just like the new one. He was very excited. He raced the two cars together. The new car and the old car were a perfect match. They raced very fast, but the new car was still a little bit quicker. In the end, Tom learned that even though his new car was cheap, it was still very quick and fun to play with. He loved his new car and the matching old car. They became his favorite toys to play with every day.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue was 3-years-old, and she loved to explore. One day, she entered a strange room with a big bowl in the corner. The bowl was full of candy and Sue couldn’t resist trying some. “Yum, it’s very yummy!” said Sue. But then, after eating a few pieces of candy, Sue started to feel a bit dizzy. “What is happening?” asked Sue, feeling very confused. “That bowl is special!” cried a voice from behind. It was Sue’s mum! She was very upset. “You must never, ever eat from that bowl again!” Sue was very sorry, and she never entered that strange room again. Instead, she learnt to always listen to what mummy said.

Once upon a time, there was a big, bright sun that loved to shine. It would shine on a little house, with a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to read books. His favorite book was about a successful cat who could jump very high. One day, while Tim was reading his book, he heard a strange sound. He looked up and saw the successful cat from his book! The cat had jumped right out of the book and into Tim's room. Tim was so surprised and happy. The cat told Tim that it needed his help. It wanted to shine just like the big, bright sun. Tim had an idea. He found a shiny yellow hat and put it on the cat. Now, the cat could shine too! They played all day, and when it was time for the cat to go back into the book, it thanked Tim for helping it shine. From that day on, Tim knew that even the most unexpected things could happen if you just believe.
