One day, a boy named Tim went to the store with his mom. Tim was very tired, but he wanted to help his mom. At the store, he saw a big green celery. Tim thought it looked yummy, so he asked his mom, "Can we buy the celery?" His mom replied, "Yes, Tim. We can use it to make a tasty snack when we get home." Tim smiled and put the celery in the cart. They bought the celery and some other food, then went back home. At home, Tim's mom cut the celery into small pieces. Tim helped by putting the pieces on a plate. Then, they ate the celery together. It was a yummy snack, just like his mom had said. After eating, Tim felt less tired and was happy he had helped his mom.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. He wanted to help his mom with the grocery. He thought it would be perfect to carry a big bag of apples. But the bag was heavy, and Tim started to struggle. His mom saw him struggle and said, "Tim, let me help you with the bag." But Tim wanted to do it by himself. He tried and tried, but the bag was still too heavy. Finally, Tim's mom had an idea. She took some apples out of the bag and put them in another bag. Now, Tim had a bag that was not too heavy. He smiled and said, "Now it is perfect!" Tim and his mom went home with the grocery, happy that they worked together.

Once upon a time, in a small town, there was a big, heavy weight. This weight was available for everyone to see. It was in the middle of the park. Kids liked to play around it and try to push it, but it would never move. It was just too heavy. One day, a little boy named Tim wanted to stop the weight from being so heavy. He thought that if he could make it lighter, maybe his friends could play with it more. So, Tim asked his mom for help. She told him to try to use his mind to think of a way to make the weight lighter. Tim tried very hard to think, and then he had an idea. He asked his friends to bring all the balloons they could find. They tied the balloons to the weight, and as more and more balloons were added, the weight started to lift off the ground. The kids were so happy that they could finally move the weight. They played with it all day, and everyone in the town was amazed by what they did.

Once upon a time, there was a clever dog named Buddy. He loved to play with his toy motorcycle. He would push it and watch it go fast. Buddy was very happy when he played with his motorcycle. One day, Buddy's toy motorcycle broke. Buddy was sad and began to suffer. He missed playing with his toy. He didn't know what to do without it. But then, something unexpected happened. A little bird saw Buddy and wanted to help. The bird was also clever and knew how to fix things. The bird took the broken motorcycle and fixed it for Buddy. Now, Buddy could play with his toy motorcycle again. He was so happy and thankful to the little bird. From that day on, Buddy and the little bird became best friends, and they played together every day.

Ben and Lily were playing in the woods. They liked to find sticks and stones and make houses for the animals. They saw a big tree with a hole in it. They wanted to look inside. But when they got closer, they heard a loud roar. A big, brown bear came out of the hole. He was very hungry and angry. He saw Ben and Lily and ran towards them. Ben and Lily were very scared. They turned around and ran away. But the bear was faster. He was almost behind them. Then they saw an axe on the ground. It was near a pile of wood. Someone had left it there. Ben picked up the axe. He swung it at the bear. The bear was surprised. He stopped and backed away. He did not like the axe. It was sharp and hurt. He roared again and ran back to his hole. Ben and Lily were safe. They dropped the axe and hugged each other. They were still trembling. They decided to go home and tell their mom and dad what happened. They did not want to play in the woods anymore.
