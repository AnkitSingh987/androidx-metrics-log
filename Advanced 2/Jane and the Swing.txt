Jane was a smart girl. She liked to swing on the swing set every day. One day, while she was swinging, she thought of a spell. Jane could hardly believe it, but she knew it would work. Jane said the spell out loud. She said: "Wings, make me swing higher!" Suddenly, Jane started to swing a lot higher. She was amazed. Just then, Jane saw her best friend, Sally. Sally asked: "How did you do that?" Jane smiled and said: "I just said a spell. Want to try it too?" Sally said: "Sure! What do I say?" Jane said: "Just say: 'Wings, make me swing higher!' and you will swing higher too!" Sally was surprised, but tried it anyways. She said the spell, and she began to swing higher. Jane and Sally both laughed and enjoyed swinging higher together.

Once upon a time, in a big forest, a little bunny named Ben was playing with his friends. They were all having fun, jumping and running around. Ben was very fast, and he loved to race his friends. One day, a new friend, a deer named Daisy, came to play with them. Daisy had a big bow on her head. It was very pretty. Ben and Daisy started to race. They ran and ran, but Ben was troubled. He could not run as fast as he used to. He felt sad and did not want to play anymore. So, Ben sat under a tree and paused to think. He wondered why he could not run fast like before. Daisy saw Ben sitting under the tree and went to talk to him. She asked, "Ben, why are you not playing with us?" Ben said, "I am not fast anymore. I don't want to play." Daisy smiled and said, "It's okay, Ben. We don't have to race. We can do other fun things together!" Ben felt better and they all played different games. They played hide and seek, and tag. Ben was happy again, and he loved playing with his new friend Daisy and her pretty bow. They all had a great time together in the big forest.

One day, a creative bird named Tim was sitting on a branch. He liked to imagine fun things. He had a friend, a little girl named Lily. They loved to play together. "Imagine we can fly to the moon!" said Tim to Lily. Lily laughed and said, "That would be fun! Let's try!" So, they closed their eyes and imagined flying to the moon. Suddenly, they were on the moon! They were very surprised. "How did we get here?" asked Lily. Tim said, "I don't know, but it's fun!" They played on the moon, jumping high and laughing. Then, Tim said, "Let's go home now." They closed their eyes and imagined going home. But when they opened their eyes, they were still on the moon! "Oh no!" said Lily. "We are stuck!" Just then, a friendly moon creature came to help. "Don't be scared," it said. "I will help you get home." The creature showed them a secret moon door. They went through the door and were back home, safe and happy. They thanked the creature and promised to keep the secret.

Once upon a time there was a nurse who was busy at her job. Every day she had to stir something in her kitchen. It was always very messy, with flour and sugar all over the table. One day while she was stirring, something magical happened. Suddenly the ingredients started to move in the bowl! The nurse was so surprised that she dropped her spoon. As the ingredients kept stirring a cake was created! The nurse was so happy with her creation that she shared it with everyone in the hospital! Everyone was so delighted with the yummy cake. The nurse and everyone else enjoyed the sweet treat. The nurse was so pleased that she smiled from ear to ear. And ever since, the nurse has been known as the Cake Stirring Nurse!

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved ice-cream very much. One sunny day, he went to the park with his mom. At the park, Tim saw a big ice-cream truck. He was very happy. Tim's mom gave him some money. He went to the ice-cream truck and the nice man inside offered him a big ice-cream cone. Tim said thank you and took the ice-cream. It was so yummy! As Tim ate his ice-cream, a little girl named Lily walked by. She looked at Tim's ice-cream and wanted one too. Lily was envious. Tim saw Lily and offered her a bite of his ice-cream. Lily smiled and they both enjoyed the ice-cream together.
