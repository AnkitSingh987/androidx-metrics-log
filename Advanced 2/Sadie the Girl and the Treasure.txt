Once upon a time, there lived a young girl named Sadie. She had a terrible habit - she would not smile. Whenever someone tried to be kind to her, she wouldn't show them any kindness back. One day, Sadie stumbled upon a big drain near her house. Filled with curiosity, she took a peek inside. What she saw made her smile for the very first time. Inside the drain was a pile of coins! Sadie quickly took all the coins and ran off with her newfound treasure. But it turns out the coins were actually from the town's piggy bank, and when the people in town figured out that their money was gone, the mayor sent out a search party to find Sadie and get their money back. The search party quickly found her, and Sadie was forced to give back all the coins she had taken. The experience was terrible, and Sadie learned an important lesson - when someone tries to show you kindness, it's important to smile and be kind back. It may seem like a small gesture, but it can mean so much to the other person.

Once upon a time, there was a jolly little dog named Spot. Spot loved to play all day long. He would run, jump, and even crawl under things. Spot was a very happy dog. One day, Spot's owner got a new leash for him. The leash was red and shiny. Spot was very excited to go for a walk with his new leash. He wagged his tail and barked with joy. Spot and his owner went to the park. Spot crawled under a big bush to chase a butterfly. The butterfly flew away, but Spot was still happy. He got back on the leash and walked with his owner. They had a fun day at the park.

Lily and Ben are friends. They like to play at the park. They see a big slide. Lily wants to go first. She climbs up the steps and sits on the slide. "Ready, set, go!" she says and slides down fast. She laughs and feels happy. Ben waits for his turn. He climbs up the steps and sits on the slide. He looks at the sky. It is clear and blue. He sees a bird flying. "Look, a bird!" he says and points. He slides down slowly. He smiles and feels calm. Lily and Ben run to the slide again. They want to slide more. They see a sign. It says "Score". It has numbers and pictures. "What is that?" Lily asks. "It is a game," Ben says. "You slide and get points. The more points, the better. The pictures show what you get." Lily and Ben look at the pictures. They see a star, a candy, a toy, and a medal. "I want the candy!" Lily says. "I want the medal!" Ben says. They decide to play the game. They take turns to slide and see their score. They have fun and try to get the best score. They slide and slide until they are tired. They are happy with their prizes. They share the candy and the medal. They hug and say goodbye. The end.

Once upon a time, there was a pretty girl named Emma. Emma had a shiny penny. She loved her penny very much. One day, she met a boy named Tom. Tom saw the penny and said, "Wow, that's an attractive penny!" Emma said, "Thank you, Tom! I found it in my room. It's my favorite thing." Tom wanted to see if he could find a penny too. They looked around the park, but they could not find any pennies. Tom was sad. Then, Emma had an idea. She said, "Let's manage to find something else that's shiny and pretty." They looked and looked, and they found a shiny stone. Tom was happy. They played with the penny and the stone and had a great day.

Once upon a time, there was a brave young boy who wanted to make an original promise. He wanted to create an arrow that was special and unique. So the boy worked hard. He found a thin stick, some feathers and some string. He tied it all together and made an arrow. The boy was so proud and happy of his arrow. He looked deeply at it and he made a promise to himself. He said, “I promise to always believe in myself and to be brave.” The boy kept his promise and kept believing in himself. He was always brave, no matter what happened. He used the arrow to remind himself of his original promise.
