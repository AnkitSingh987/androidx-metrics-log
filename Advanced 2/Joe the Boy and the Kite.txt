Once upon a time there was a little boy named Joe. He was a very gifted boy and always enjoyed playing outside. One day, Joe was playing with a kite on the beach. The wind began to pick up and he had trouble holding onto the long line connected to the kite. Suddenly, the line snapped and the kite flew away. Joe was disappointed and upset. Joe chased after the kite but it was too far away. He started to feel hopeless and knew he wouldn't get the kite back. As he stood there watching the kite, he noticed a big gust of wind rolling in. Instinctively, he ran over to it. Suddenly, to Joe's amazement, the gust of wind brought the kite back to him. He was so excited, he jumped up and down with joy. Joe yelled with joy and thanked the wind for being so gifted and helping him get his kite back.

Once upon a time there was a strong bird. The bird wanted to take an object from a tree. He flew up to the tree and opened his beak wide. He tasted it and thought it was not good. The bird then heard a voice. He looked around but he could not see where it was coming from. The voice said "Do not take the object, it will not taste good". The bird was surprised, who was speaking? The bird saw a friendly face in the branches. It was a wise old owl. The owl said: "My friend, you should never take things that you have not tasted first. Strong creatures make wise choices". The bird thanked the owl and flew away. The bird was happy that he had listened to the wise old owl. From that day on, he always tasted before he took something. The moral of the story is that wise choices lead to happy endings.

Bob was playing outside. He looked to the west and saw that the sky was very interesting. He noticed a little bird that was flying in circles, and he wanted to follow it. However, every time he tried to get closer, the bird would ignore him and soar away. Bob was so determined that he ran after it, but it just kept going faster and further away. Bob stopped and sighed. He wanted to learn more about the bird and why it was so interested in the west. If only he could talk to it! Suddenly, Bob heard a voice coming from the bird. "Do not give up, Bob," it said. "Follow me and I will show you something special." Bob smiled and ran after the bird. And sure enough, the bird flew towards the west and soon Bob was able to discover something really interesting.

One day, a little boy named Tim went to play outside. He saw a black cricket jumping in the grass. Tim picked up the cricket and said, "Hi, little cricket! Do you want to play with me?" The black cricket looked at Tim and said, "Yes, I would like to play with you. But I am hungry. Do you have food to share?" Tim thought for a moment and said, "I have a cookie in my pocket. I can share it with you." So Tim took out the cookie and broke it in half. He gave one half to the cricket and ate the other half himself. As they ate their cookie, Tim and the black cricket played in the grass. They laughed and had a lot of fun. Soon, it was time for Tim to go home. He said goodbye to his new friend, the black cricket. "Bye, cricket! I had fun sharing my cookie and playing with you," said Tim. The black cricket waved goodbye and jumped back into the grass. Tim went home with a big smile on his face, happy to have made a new friend.

Once upon a time, there was a popular little dog named Max. Max loved to play with his friends and run around the park. He was very happy and everyone liked him. One day, Max found a jacket in the park. It was a little big for him, but he wore it anyway. Max felt very proud in his new jacket. He showed it to all of his friends and they liked it too. After playing all day, Max was very tired. He found a soft spot under a tree to relax. Max lay down and closed his eyes, feeling the warm sun on his face. He was happy and cozy in his new jacket, and soon fell asleep.
