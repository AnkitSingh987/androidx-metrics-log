Once there was an old man. He was very happy. One day he saw a pen standing on the table. He was very surprised. The old man picked up the pen and tried to write. But the pen was not working. He shook the pen but it still did not work. Then he remembered — it was an old pen. He got a new pen and wrote a letter. The old man was very happy. He put the old pen on the table. The old pen still stood there.

Lila and Ben liked to play in the park. They liked to run and jump and slide. They also liked to look at the trees. There were many trees in the park, but their favorite was the big oak. The oak was very old and tall and had many leaves. One day, they saw something new on the oak. It was a small, round, hairy thing. It was hanging from a branch. They wondered what it was. They asked their mom, who was sitting on a bench. "That is a cocoon," their mom said. "It is where a caterpillar lives. A caterpillar is a small, long, hairy bug that eats leaves. But soon, the caterpillar will change. It will become a butterfly. A butterfly is a beautiful bug that has wings and can fly." Lila and Ben were amazed. They wanted to see the caterpillar change. They decided to come back to the park every day and check on the cocoon. They hoped to see the butterfly. They waited and waited. The cocoon did not change. It stayed small and round and hairy. Lila and Ben were getting bored. They wondered if the caterpillar was still inside. They wondered if it was sleeping or dreaming. Then, one sunny morning, they saw something different. The cocoon had a small hole in it. And out of the hole, a colorful wing was poking. Lila and Ben were excited. They ran to the oak and watched. The hole got bigger and bigger. And then, out came the butterfly. It was orange and black and white. It was very pretty. It flapped its wings and flew away. Lila and Ben clapped and cheered. They had seen the caterpillar change. They had seen the butterfly. They were very happy. They thanked the oak for giving them a wonderful show. They said goodbye to the butterfly and hoped to see it again. They went back to their mom and told her what they had seen. Their mom smiled and hugged them. She was proud of them for being curious and patient. She said they had learned something new and amazing. She said they had seen a miracle.

Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She had many shoes. One day, she saw a crazy shoe. It was very big and funny. She wanted to study the shoe. Lily asked her mom, "Mom, can I study the crazy shoe?" Her mom said, "Yes, you can study the crazy shoe, but be careful." Lily studied the crazy shoe. She found that the shoe could talk. The shoe said, "Hi, I am a crazy shoe. I can help you learn new things." Lily and the crazy shoe became good friends. They studied and played together every day.

One day, a little boy named Tim saw a white cart. The cart was near a big tree. Tim liked the white cart, but he did not dare to touch it. He thought it belonged to someone else. Then, a girl named Sue came to the tree. She said, "Hi, Tim! Do you like my white cart?" Tim said, "Yes, I do. But I did not dare to touch it." Sue smiled and said, "You can play with it if you want." Tim and Sue played with the white cart. They put toys in the cart and pushed it around. Suddenly, the cart began to move on its own! Tim and Sue were surprised. They did not know the white cart was magic. They had fun playing with the magic white cart all day.

Once upon a time, there was a little boy named Tim. Tim loved to play with his toys and look at photos. One day, Tim found a big photo of his mom and dad. He wanted to keep the photo safe, so he thought of a plan. Tim found a long string and decided to tie the photo to his toy box. He was very careful not to rip the photo. He tied the string tight around the photo and then tied the other end to his toy box. Now, the photo was safe and would not get lost. When Tim's mom saw what he had done, she was very happy. She told Tim that he did a good job being careful with the photo. Tim felt proud and learned that it is important to take care of the things we love. And from that day on, Tim always took care of his things and the things he loved.
